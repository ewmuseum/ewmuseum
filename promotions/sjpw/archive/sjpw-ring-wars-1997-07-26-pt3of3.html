Ring Wars

26 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ring Announcer: This next match is one fall, 15 minute time limit, and is a first round match in the Super J Crown Tournament [crowd cheers]. Introducing first, from Hiroshima, weighing 215lbs, KEN-DO MAS-AO!! [crowd applauds as Masao heads to the ring, slapping hands with the fans on his way to the ring] AO: Masao has looked good training at the dojo, so I expect his first match will be a hard fought contest. Ring Announcer: His opponent, from Osaka, weighing 221lbs, he is one half of the NEW JAPAN CREW, THE GREAT O-NI!!!!!!! [crowd gives mixed cheers & boos as Oni & Ishikawa head to the ring] ER: Masao faces a tough opponent in Oni. These two have been on a tear since they got here, and Ishikawa is a top contendor in the Middleweight tournament! There's the bell; both men lock up, and Oni backs Masao to the ropes... Chop to the chest by Oni! [crowd "Whooos"] Oni whips Masao to the other side... spinwheel kick! AO: Oni snaps an elbow down onto Masao's chest, then runs to the ropes... senton splash. Cover...one...two..kickout! [crowd applauds] Oni stands Masao up, and runs to the ropes... jumping side kick! [crowd ooohs] ER: Oni drags Masao to his feet, and whips him to the ropes... Masao ducks the spinwheel kick... Oni goes for another spinwheel kick, but Masao catches his leg.. dragonscrew leg whip! [crowd ooohs] AO: Both men up, and Masao takes him down with a rana. He drags Oni to his feet...he lifts him up for a suplex, and drops him gut first onto the top rope! [crowd oooohs] Masao now runs to the corner, up to the top rope... somersault legdrop to the back of Oni's head as he hangs over the top rope! [crowd oooohs] What a move! ER: Masao snap mare's Oni into the ring, and drops a knee onto his head. Cover...one...two...kickout! [crowd applauds] Masao drags Oni to his feet... T-bone suplex! [crowd ooohs] AO: Ishikawa is on the apron, and he's arguing with the referee. Masao drags Oni back up, and Oni grabs Masao's tights, and pitches him forward, head first into the corner! [crowd ooohs] Oni locks Masao in a waistlock... German suplex! [crowd ooohs] ER: Ishikawa jumps off the apron, but the damage is done. Oni goes to the top rope... flying somersault splash! [crowd ooohs] Cover...one...two...Kickout! [crowd applauds] AO: Oni hoists Masao over his shoulders...gutbuster [crowd ooohs] He sets Masao up..Fisherman's suplex...one...two...th-kickout! [crowd applauds] ER: Masao definitely is giving it his all! Oni has him up again, and sets him up for a tombstone...Masao is fighting it... he's back on his feet... Masao reverses! [crowd ooohs, then applauds] AO: Masao has Oni up...hangman's neckbreaker. He drops an elbow, and heads to the top rope... Oni gets to his feet... Flying Bodypress! Oni rolls over...one...two..kickout! [crowd applauds] Both men up; Oni charges... spinning backbreaker by Masao! [crowd applauds] ER: Masao scoops Oni up, and slams him back down. Ishikawa gets on the apron again... Masao dropkicks Ishikawa off the apron! [crowd oooohs] Masao goes back to Oni, and takes him down with a DDT. He goes to the top rope... Shooting Star Press! Cover...one...two...THREE!!! [crowd cheers] Ring Announcer: Your winner, KENDO MASAO!!! [crowd cheers again, and streamers fly into the ring] AO: Great upset by Masao, in his debut match. Ishikawa is in the ring now, and he is stomping away on Masao! He drags Masao up... short arm lariat! [crowd ooohs] Oni is up, and he and Ishikawa set Masao up... Total Elimination!!! [crowd oooohs] ER: What a disgusting display by the New Japan Crew! I hope Mr. Yamashita fines them for this. Security is in the ring, and they are carting off the NJC, while the medics take care of Masao. While they take care of Masao, let's cut to the interview area, where Sharon Akiyama is waiting... [Camera cuts to Sharon Akiyama in the interview area...] Sharon Akiyama: Ladies and Gentlemen, here they are, back in action, the Badd Intentions....... (Donny "Ice" Berg walks out from the back first. After a few moments out walk Nick Patera and The Big Chill. The Big Chill is wearing a leather vest and black jeans. A new tattoo is seen on his bicep. It reads Reign of Terror - RoT for Life. The Big Chill looks bigger than before. Patera walks out wearing only a pair of black jeans and a pair of leather boots. The crowd boos them.) SA: Now, Mr. Berg.... (Donny "Ice" Berg grabs the mic from Sharon) Berg: Shut up. You're done here. You can leave now. (Donny has a smug look on his face) (Sharon looks confused.) Berg: Come one now, shoo.... (The Big Chill takes a step toward Sharon, and she quickly retreats) Berg: Have you missed us?? (Waits for a round of boos) I knew you did. We're back!! Nicky, show the people how much we've missed them. (Patera pulls a Japanese flag from his back pocket and blows his nose in it. He throws it to the ground and stomps it into the ground.) Berg: And Chill, don't you got a message for the great Japanese fans. (Chill walks over to the corner, climbs up the turnbuckle, and gives a double finger to the crowd. He climbs back down and as he walks by the announcers, he spits on them.) Berg: Before we go any furthur, Chill wants to clear up a little misunderstanding. (Hands the mic to Chill) The Big Chill: Listen up, you nips. It comes to our attention that some idiots think we're racist. Not mentioning any name, but NYCE people have been talking alot of trash. Well, the Baadd Intentions are going to dispell those horrible rumors right now. We'd like to welcome in our own special way, the newest memeber of the Reign of Terror, ALEX AKIRA! Come out here, A-dog! (Alex Akira and Mark Stone come to ringside. The Badd Intentions hug Alex Akira and shake hands with the Mark Stone.) The Big Chill: Not only is this man half Japanese, but he's got the bod, the talent, and the brains enough to live in the States! Tell em what's on your mind, A-dog! (Chill hands the mic to Alex Akira nd puts his hand on Akira's shoulder.) Alex Akira: So, now the people understand what a family is suppose to look like. I told you before that I came here for an understanding of you people and not for your approval. You ask yourselves why would I ally myself with men who disgrace a flag of a nation? That flag is not what embodies Japan and when they speak of the ill-mannered they speak of those who are inferior. That quota does not include me. Nik Nyce had a few comments about Badd Intentions being racist. Nik, we are all a bit prejudice or even a tad bit racist. No one is hate free and that includes yourself. You wish to talk of cheap heat, and I have explained this already but to hope that it sticks with you I shall do so agian. If you do not like the people who go after what you call cheap heat, then by all means, ignore them. If you do not do so, you are only helping to give them what they want and you gave Badd Intentions what they wanted...a reason to come after you. (Akira hands the mic to Berg.) Berg: Now, here's the deal. We were gone. Out for good. Done with this itty bitty little spec on the map that you idiots consider a country. We're were screwed with and we had enough. Nicky was never hurt. It was a big conspiracy to keep the big bad Americans out of the country. Now you must be wondering why the other American wrestlers aren't leaving? Well, with the exception of Ace Kilcannon, whose getting screwed too, they all suck! Why are we back? Simple. We're not going to let you guys get away that easy. The Badd Intentions are back. (Hands the mic to Nick Patera.) Patera: You think you can pull this kind of crap and get away with it? Do you think that we're going to be dictated to by nip bureaucrats? What doctors said I couldn't wrestle? They see me limping a little and all of a sudden, I got bad knees! But it didn't work! All this did was get me a little more angry, a little more determined and a little more nasty! Do you know who you're dealing with? A former World champion, former Hardcore champion, former Tag Team champion! (SCREAMING) I'VE DONE IT ALL!!!!! But besides that, do you know what I am? I am the baddest man to ever walk the face of God's green earth. Mothers hold their children a little closer when I walk by, fathers tell their daughters to get inside, old ladies cringe and nips wrestlers head for the hills! Toyota Brothers, the fed heads have elongated your title reigns. They have saved you idiots from the ass whoopings of your pathetic lives. We're going to see if the Toyota Brothers are like cue balls. We're going to see if we hit you hard enough, how much english we can get out of you! Now, there is no more running, there is no more hiding. The titles come home. The titles go RoT! Were the belong. Not to all you little men US the real men! (Hands the mic to the Big Chill) The Big Chill: Weeeeellllll....... The Big Man's talking and you better be listening because Nicky don't like repeating himself. The Toyota Brothers. Celica. Tercel. I'm shaking in my booties. Like Nicky said, the nips in the front office have protected those titles long enough. And who else do we have in the tag team ranks that we have to worry about? The Mulkey Brothers from Canada called the Brew Crew, the nips that we beat every time we get in the ring with, the Tokyo Depressed, Lisa and Bart, the Simpsons. And let's not forget the so-called number one contenders, Iwa Jawa and Yaka Moto. You two boys calling us out? I can understand that you nips are of limited intelligence, being genetically inferior, but even an idiot can read the bottom line. And don't even get me started on Prick Nyce. He can't even count. (SCREAMING)WE ARE UNDEFEATED AND WE WILL REMAIN UNDEFEATED FOR AS LONG AS WE WANT! And you know, Nicky, what's the worst part of this whole situation? Patera: I know, but tell these uneducated nips what the worst part is! Chill: Those damn nips took our first shot and gave it to someone else! Well, if you think for one minute that we're not getting what we deserve, you're nuts. Toyota Sisters, we will make sure you guys are champs when we wrestle! Count on it! We haven't even been challenged yet. But we understand. When you look this good, and fight as well as we do, can anything else be expected? But I digress, the real story is the Toyota Brothers. They got the belts and they got our attention. And that's more dangerous than having cancer, boys! Let me tell you what happened only this morning. I was walking down the street and I saw this little kid crying. I asked him what was wrong. He told me that his father beat him. I told him to go live with his mother. He cried even harder and told me that his mother beat him too. I asked him who did he want to live with. He told me he wanted to live with the Toyota Brothers because they don't beat no one. True story! Enough of the talk. The match is signed and so are you're death warrants. Toyota Brothers, we are the standard by which everyone else is measured. You may have the titles, and your God may have your fidelity, but your asses belong to us! Patera and Chill: We're coming at you with nothing but BADD INTENTIONS! (Hands the mic back to Donny "Ice" Berg) Berg: THE PLEASURE WAS ALL YOURS! AO: These two men are too confident. They won't win the titles. They think the Toyotas are a pushover? They think Asamiya & Asakino are too? The Reign of Terror are too cocky, and soon these gaijin, as well as the ignorant Alex Akira, will learn they are not so tough after all. ER: Akira, we all know how you feel about the RoT, and the Gaijin. Things are definitely coming to a head between Badd Intentions & The Toyota Brothers. That much is evident. We have to go to ringside for our main event. Ring Announcer: This next match is one fall, 15 minute time limit, and is a first round match in the Super J Crown Tournament [crowd cheers]. Introducing first, from Tokyo, weighing 232lbs, and representing the JAPAN AC, KY-U KA-BU-KI!!!! [crowd cheers wildly as Kyu & Hakurana head to the ring, Hakurana carrying a japanese flag] ER: It still amazes me how the Japan AC are so popular! AO: It's easy Eddie-san; Kyu, Hakurana & The Toyota Brothers are unlike the rest of the AC. They represent honor, patriotism, and warrior spirits. They are all champions! Ring Announcer: His opponent, from Kobe, weighing 233lbs, he is the SJPW CRUISERWEIGHT CHAMPION, he is SU-PER TEN-GU!!!!!!!!!!! [crowd goes even wilder as Super Tengu, wearing the SJPW Cruiserweight title, heads to the ring] ER: This man is more popular than ever! How can this be? AO: The fans respect his talent. He is one of the greatest cruiserweight wrestlers in the entire world! ER: Tengu gives the ref his title, and he hands it to the ring announcer. Ky and Tengu size each other up, and after the ref's instructions, Kabuki offers Tengu his hand... Tengu shakes it [crowd cheers, and chants of "Tengu!" and "Kyu!" start up] AO: They lock up, and Kabuki doubles over Tengu with a front facelock, and starts to hammer his back with forearm shots. Kabuki now with a knee to Tengu's forehead! [crowd oooohs] ER: Kabuki takes Tengu over with a fireman's carry, then drops an elbow to his chest. Kabuki drags Tengu up, and Tengu catches him with a European uppercut! Dropkick by Tengu! [crowd cheers] Kabuki staggers back... Tengu runs past him to the ropes... springboard leg lariat to the back of the head! [crowd ooohs, then applauds] AO: Tengu lifts Kabuki straight up in the air... Brainbuster! [crowd ooohs] Tengu runs to the corner... moonsault! [crowd applauds] He rolls Kabuki over...one...two..kickout! [crowd applauds] ER: Tengu Scoops Kabuki up, and slams him down. He flips onto him with a senton, gets back up, and drops a leg across his chest; cover...one...two...t-kickout! [crowd applauds] AO: Tengu cannot put him away! He drags Kabuki to his feet, and Kabuki catches him with a palm thrust to his throat! [crowd ooohs] Tengu staggers back... Kabuki kicks him in the gut, sets him up... slingshot powerbomb! [crowd ooohs] He flips over into a pinning position...one...two..kickout! [crowd applauds] ER: Kabuki sets Tengu up...piledriver! [crowd ooohs] Cover...one...two...th-kickout! [crowd applauds] He drags Tengu up..Tengu with a small package...one...two..kickout! [crowd applauds] AO: Both men up, and Kabuki catches Tengu with a high knee to the face. He whips Tengu to the ropes... Crucifix by Kabuki! One...two...t-kickout! [crowd applauds] Both men up...dropkick by Tengu! Kabuki staggers to the ropes... Powerslam by Tengu! One...two..kickout! [crowd applauds] Tengu whips Kabuki to the ropes... Side Suplex! He runs to the ropes... springboard back elbowdrop! [crowd applauds] ER: Tengu sets Kabuki up...snap suplex. He sets him up again... Northern Lights Suplex...one...two...kickout! [crowd applauds] Tengu sets Kabuki up... Powerbo-Kabuki flips over Tengu into a sunset flip! [crowd cheers] One...two...th-kickout! [crowd applauds] AO: Both men up, and Kabuki nails Tengu with a thrust kick! [crowd oooohs] He whips Tengu to the ropes... Flying Lariat by Kabuki! [crowd cheers] Kabuki slaps on an armbar, and lays into Tengu with a series of kicks. He twists the arm into a hammerlock, and takes him over with a belly to back suplex onto the hammerlocked arm! [crowd ooohs] ER: Kabuki has Tengu up, and sets him up for a bulldog... Tengu throws him off! [crowd cheers; some chant "Tengu! Tengu!"] Tengu drags Kabuki up... German suplex...one...two...th-kickout! [crowd applauds] Tengu drops a knee to Kabuki's forehead, then heads to the top rope. AO: Kabuki sits up now, and starts getting to his feet. Tengu is at the top now... Moonsault... Kabuki catches Tengu! [crowd oooohs] He lifts him up, placing him on his shoulders [crowd cheers].... airplane spin! Round and round and round... Death Valley Driver! [crowd oooohs] ER: Kabuki hoists Tengu up on his shoulders again, and goes into another airplane spin.... Deugau Drop! [crowd ooohs, then applauds] That was the Deugau Death Drop! Cover...one...two...THREE!!!! [crowd cheers] AO: I can't believe it! Kyu Kabuki has pinned Super Tengu!!! Ring Announcer: Your winner, KYU KABUKI!!!! [crowd cheers; streamers fly into the ring, and chants of "Kyu! Kyu!" start] ER: What an upset! First Hiro Kanemoto beats Akira Hashimoto, and now Kyu Kabuki pins Super Tengu! Two of the top seeds are defeated in round one! What an incredible night! AO: Tengu is up now, shaking his head... he goes over to Kabuki, and turns him around... Both men staring eye to eye [crowd goes silent]... Tengu offers Kabuki his hand... Kabuki shakes it! [crowd cheers again] Tengu now raises Kabuki's arm in victory! ER: What an incredible night it has been. The HCW tour starts tomorrow, so we hope to have some matches on Control Center. We are out of time; for Akira Ono, I'm Eddie Rose. We'll see you on the next Ring Wars!
