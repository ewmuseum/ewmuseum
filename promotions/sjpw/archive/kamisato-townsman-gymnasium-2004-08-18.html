SJPW House Show — Kamisato Townsman Gymnasium (8/18/04)

Aug 20, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wednesday, August 18th, 2004 Kamisato Townsman Gymnasium: 800 fans (sellout) The Saitama mini-tour continued, this time with a show at the Kamisato Townsman Gym. 1) Scott Starring def Dragon Mask (11:55) after a Shining Wizard kneestrike. 2) "Russian Superman" Petor Andresku & Vladimir Tarasov def Yatoro Hori & Kenichi Kubota (10:12) after Tarasov made Hori submit to the Iron Curtain. 3) "Dragon Fury" Minobu Tetsuhara def Botan Nakatoni (12:00) after hitting the Dragon Driver 1. 4) "Ecchi Kid" Kentaro "H-Bomb" Kawasaki, KANJI Sakaigama & "CRIMSON" Jin Hiroto def Tasogare no Prince, Takahiro KEOKI & "Firefly" Horo Atashi (17:54) after KANJI pinned TnP following the Violence Flash. 5) IICW Cruiserweight title match: "All-Star" Joey Darren (Champion) def Jiraiya (challenger) (10:23) following a flipping piledriver. - Starring again opened the show, taking on Team Tiger's Dragon Mask. Starring had a harder time with the mat oriented Dragon, who hit a sick Dragon Suplex on Starring about five minutes into the match. Starring miraculously kicked out, and made a valiant comeback. Dragon took him over with a sunset flip, but Starring rolled out of it, nailing Dragon with a Shining Wizard, and securing the three count. - The Red Army took on the SMASH Attack (as Hori & Kubota are calling themselves these days) in tag team competition. The match didn't last very long, as Andresku just mauled Hori with his superior power, before letting Tarasov make him tap out to the Iron Curtain crossface. - Team Tiger vs Disciples of Despair was the name of the game, and Team Tiger are 2-0 in Saitama. Tetsuhara was able to outwrestle the submission specialist in Nakatoni, and took him down with the Dragon Driver 1 for the win. - A wild six man that saw The Visual Spirits team with the GPH champion against the tandem of TnP, Team Tiger's KEOKI, and the young lion Atashi. TnP and his partners worked well together, but the Spirits are cagey veterans, and did a good job of keeping Atashi trapped on their side of the ring for a good seven or eight minutes. Kawasaki even seemed less bored than usual, taunting TnP while laying into Atashi with a series of chops. Kawasaki got too cocky, and Atashi nailed him with an enzugiri, making the tag to TnP. The crowd went wild as Kawasaki & TnP traded blows in the middle of the ring, until Kawasaki thumbed TnP in the eye, then hit a fisherman's buster for a two count. TnP came back from that, nailing Kawasaki with an Ace Crusher, following it up with a bridging German Suplex, but Hiroto broke the pinfall up. KEOKI tackled Hiroto, and both men went to the floor. Kawasaki made the tag to KANJI, then charged Atashi in the corner, sending him to the floor, and going out after him. KANJI stunned TnP with a release German Suplex of his own, then hit the Violence Flash as he was trying to get up, scoring the win for his team. - Darren made a second title defense in Saitama, this time against Jiraiya of Team Tiger. The masked youngster put up a good fight, almost pinning Darren following a Northern Lights Suplex. Darren proved why he is the champion though, pinning the challenger after hitting a flipping piledriver, which seems to be a new weapon in Darren's arsenal. Fans were talking about the wild trios match after the show, wondering when TnP would get his shot at Kawasaki's title? Many hoped that it would be soon, as they're itching to see the two men face each other one on one.
