Zenmen Sensou

31 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Zenmen Sensou continues...] [Eddie Rose climbs into the ring] ER: At this time, please welcome, "JIGOKU" TIGER EXTREME! [The fans give a warm welcome to the hardcore warrior. Extreme walks out and scans the applauding crowd. He smiles from under his Tiger Mask and finally rolls into the ring from under the bottom rope. He comes up on Eddie Rose quickly, shaking Rose's shoulders. Extreme then bows to the crowd and Rose.] ER: Extreme, what do you think of Kubota and "The Pyro"? JTE: Well... [Extreme scratches his head and contorts his lips as if trying to remember something. He finally smiles and shakes his head as if he remembers.] Ahhh, yes. I think that they are both great athletes. Very honorable fighters. ER: [In a surprised tone] You do? JTE: Umm, of course. ER: What have you been doing since your loss in the finals of the mini tournament? JTE: Master Matsuoko has been training me hard. We have been trying to make my personality more stable. ER: More stable? Can you elaborate? JTE: Master thinks that I cannot control myself. He also told me that a real warrior does not harbor ill feelings towards his opponents. ER: You mean Kubota and "The Pyro"? JTE: [He smiles] Yes. I dont like them. But, Master has told me not to say those things. I am to try to keep control of myself. I really want to be quite like my brother MLWO Japan Champion, Jensei Tenru. [The crowd begins to chant "TENRU! TENRU! TENRU!"] ER: It is funny that you mention Tenru. He is another product of the Matsuoko Dojo, like yourself. [Extreme nods] But, Tenru refuses to be cast into a mold by his very father! He shows his arrogant personality and seems to act like he really is. JTE: Ahh. You know as well as I do Rose that Tenru is actually that arrogant. So, you think that I can be as sucessful as Tenru if I am who I am truly then? ER: Exactly. [As if a huge light has just turned on inside Extreme's head, he smiles. He begins to laugh, easily at first and then like a raving madman. Eddie Rose now has a worried look on his face as Extreme continues to laugh.] JTE: HAHA! HAHA! [Extreme reaches into his baggy black pants and pulls out a large rusty metal file. Extreme opens his mouth wide and begins to file his canine teeth. Rose seems to be shocked and sickened. As he shakes his head in disbelief, Extreme takes the file out of his mouth and offers it to Rose to file his own teeth, he declines. Extreme grabs the mic suddenly and begins to scream and even looks to be foaming at the mouth.] JTE: KKUURRAASSHHAAA! PYRO! I hate you both! I want to play with you like a demented child plays with a hamster. I want to share my toys with you both and show you that I TOO CAN BE IT! [By this time, Rose has gotten out of the ring] JTE: DO YOU HEAR ME? DO YOU? I challenge the winner of your match to an Explodding Casket Match for the Battle Arts title! I CAN BE IT! [Extreme falls down to the mat, screaming like a madman. The shot closes with Extreme pulling out a fork from his pants and prodding himself with it in the arm, drawing blood] AO: This man is even crazier than "Demon Boy" Ishrinku is! [Rose makes it back to the broadcast table, while Extreme exits the ring shouting "I CAN BE IT!"] AO: Eddie, are you ok? ER: Fine. Remind me never to let Adonis talk me into doing another interview! [Ono chuckles, then nods] AO: He's harmless to you Eddie. His insanity is fixated on Kubota & Takada! ER: Thank God! [Rose looks are his notes...] ER: The Bonecrushers have proven to be worthy All-Asian tag team champions since winning those belts from The Genyosha. However, The Yakuza feel that they are unworthy champions, and seek to depose them. AO: Both teams are excellant, but I must agree with The Yakuza. ER: Why is that Akira? AO: Because they are more than likely the better team. ER: That remains to be seen... ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / All-Asian Tag Title Shimeijiai: / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / The Yakuza vs The Bonecrushers /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ Nishi: This next contest is for The All-Asian tag titles! It is one fall, fifteen minute time limit. Introducing first, the challengers... ["Edgecrusher" by Fear Factory blares over the PA] Nishi: They both hail from Tokyo, and are a combined weight of 479 pounds...GE-TO & TA-ZA-KA....THE YA-KU-ZA!!!!! [Geto leads his team into the ring, Tazaka following. They walk directly to the ring, Geto ignoring the fans, Tazaka occasionally exchanging 'pleasantries' with them. They both wear their usual ring attire. As they hit the ring, they remove their outerwear, and loosen up with stretching and martial arts katas as the ring intros are made] Nish: Their opponents are the current All-Asian tag team champions! ["Ballbreaker" by AC/DC blares over the PA] Nishi: Hailing from Scranton, Pennsylvania, USA, and weighing in at a combined weight of 530 pounds...AA-RON LANK-FORD & JAKE REED.... THE BONE-CRUSH-ERS!!!!! [Aaron and Jake walk to the ring slapping hands with the fans while keeping focused on the ring. When they get to the ring, they stand in opposite corners while the pyros go off. They take off the All-Asian tag titles, and hand them to the referee...] ER: Both teams in the ring now and.... here we go! Both teams lay into each other before the bell even sounds, and referee Jun Morisato is going to have a long night ahead of him! We've got Jake Reed and Geto trading blows, and Aaron Lankford matches up with Tazaka. Morisato is forced to call for the bell and now the match is officially underway. Lankford pushes Takaka against the ropes... short arm clothesline and both men go outside to the floor! AO: That leaves Reed and Geto in the ring, and they match up fairly well in size and weight. Reed connects with a few well-placed punches... and Geto retaliates with a knife edge chop! [the crowd "whooos" as they hear the impact of the karate chop against Reed's chest. Reed takes a step back and Geto follows up with another chop, and again the crowd "whooos" at the impact. Geto backs Reed against the ropes and then sends him to the opposite side of the ring with an irish whip. Reed rebounds off and manages to avoid a roundhouse kick by Geto. Geto turns around on his heel and is caught around the waist by Reed, who takes him over with a belly-to-back suplex.] AO: Both these teams are coming on very strong in the opening moments, and make no mistake about it... they know each others reputations. Finally Tazaka and Aaron Lankford have gotten back up on the ring apron, and they both managed to get in a few shots on the outside. Reed pulls Geto up off the mat and shoves him hard back into the Bonecrushers corner and makes the tag to Aaron Lankford. This is the key to good tag team wrestling, cutting the other man off from his partner. [Lankford steps between the ropes while Reed leans his back against Geto, preventing him from escaping the corner. Morisato begins to put a count on Reed, but he climbs out of the ring as Lankford fires away at Geto with several measured punches. Jun Morisato steps up and warns Lankford to move it out of the corner, and Lankford just nods and hits him with a headbutt, then quickly tags Reed back in. Lankford drops Geto to the mat with a drop toehold, and Reed follows it up with an elbow across the back of the head.] ER: The Bonecrushers have taken control for the moment, Akira... but we know the Yakuza are a team with a lot of credentials. The question is, can they handle a team who are just as accomplished as they are, and who some feel are even tougher? AO: I think the Yakuza can Eddie... Hold on, a nice piledriver by Reed on Geto and a cover... 1... 2... Kickout at 2 by Geto, just as Tazaka was about to enter the ring to make the save. [Reed pulls Geto back to his feet and whips him to the ropes. The Yakuza member bounces back and Reed goes to take him over with a Frankensteiner, but Geto catches him in the middle of his action and slams him hard into the mat with a sit-down powerbomb. The audience applauds the move as both men lay prone on the mat.] AO: Like I was saying, Eddie-san, I think The Yakuza have the abilities in the ring, like Jake Reed just learned courtesy of Geto. Aaron Lankford is holding out his hand, trying to get Reed to make the tag... Tazaka doing the same thing on the other end of the ring.... Geto is slowly crawling towards his corner..... Reed is almost there.... tag in to Aaron Lankford who comes rushing in after Geto.... but Geto rolls forward and makes the tag to Tazaka! [The crowd OOOHS as Tazaka slingshots over the top rope, catching the oncoming Lankford with a clothesline. Lankford drops to the mat as Tazaka nimbly springs back to his feet and drives a running kneedrop into the temple of the downed Bonecrusher. Tazaka quickly pulls Lankford to his feet and whips him hard into the turnbuckle, following him in with a dropkick. Lankford has nowhere to go as he 's crushed between the oncoming dropkick and the turnbuckles. The crowd applauds Tazaka politely.] ER: The Yakuza now beginning to come on stronger, as Tazaka pulls Lankford towards their corner... there's the tag to Geto... They have Lankford hooked up for.... DOUBLE FORWARD RUSSIAN LEGSWEEP! They just planted Lankford face first into the canvas... and into double spinning toe-holds! [The Yakuza continue their efforts with the double toe-holds, as referee Jun Morisato is distracted by trying to get Jake Reed to leave the ring as he comes in to try and help his partner. Finally Reed leaves the ring, and Morisato puts a fast five count on the Yakuza to force Tazaka out of the ring as well.] AO: Inexperience on the part of Jake Reed has just cost his partner. He wanted to help, but only ended up giving the Yakuza more time to do damage. Geto now has a chance to dish out some punishment as retribution for what he absorbed earlier. [Geto once again pulls Lankford up off the canvas and drives a knee to the midsection. Lankford doubles up and Geto attempts a Powerbomb, but Lankford manages to block it. Lankford grabs Geto around the legs and hoists him up and over his back, but Geto manages to land on his feet. Lankford turns around , still dazed, and walks right into a thrust kick to the stomach which Geto quickly follows up with a Double Arm DDT. Geto drops down for the cover as Morisato dives into position.... 1... 2... the crowd applauds as Lankford manages to get the shoulder up at two.] ER: A near fall on the part of the Yakuza, and here's another tag by Geto to Tazaka.... Geto now getting Lankford set up for a Death Valley Driver.... Tazaka up on the top rope.... SPIKE DEATH VALLEY DRIVER! Perfect timing on the part of the Yakuza, and a quick cover by Tazaka... 1... 2... Jake Reed makes the save! and in comes Geto again, and Geto and Jake Reed are really going at it now! Geto misses with a roundhouse kick... ENZIGUIRI BY REED! Geto goes over the top rope and out to the arena floor... and Tazaka catches Reed with a JUMPING SIDEKICK and it's Reed now taking a fall out between the ropes and down to the floor! AO: Tazaka now the one being unaware... Lankford is back up... T-BONE SUPLEX! Tazaka just got dumped right on his head! Lankford with the cover... 1... 2.... NO! Tazaka gets the shoulder up now... and he gets his legs around Lankford... bodyscissors being applied by Tazaka... and he's got hold of Lankford's wrists... combination submission holds being applied by Tazaka... Reed is trying to get into the ring to make the save, but Geto's got hold of his leg... Jun Morisato is asking Lankford if he wants to give up, but Lankford won't give up despite looking to be in a lot of pain and... [the crowd applauds wildly as the bell sounds. Tazaka releases Lankford as Geto climbs itno the ring, letting go of Jake Reed. The Yakuza hold their arms up in victory, but Jun Morisato pulls their arms back down. The Yakuza look furious as Morisato talk to them.] AO: Eddie-san, i'm not sure but I believe the time limit ran out! [Morisato consults with Nishi, who nods, then makes his announcement...] Nishi: The referee informs me that the time limit has expired. He therfore rules this bout....A DRAW!!! [crowd gives a mixed response] Nishi: Therefore, STILL All-Asian tag team champions...THE BONECRUSHERS!!!! [The crowd starts to applaud, then erupt into boos as Yamashita, Ishida & Usami walk to the ring. Yamashita enters the ring, smiling, and takes the microphone from Nishi...] Shinsaku Yamashita: Yakuza. You continually claim that you are more deserving of the All-Asian tag team titles, yet you are unable to wrest them from these gaijin in the alloted time limit! In America, the crowd would be chanting "Five more minutes!", but as this is Nippon, that is not the case... [The crowd boos even louder] Shinsaku Yamashita: It seems we have a dilemma. I *could* book a rematch at Wrestlefest, but it seems that you are still in the Frank A Marano Jr. memorial tag team tournament. Therefore, I cannot. [Crowd starts to throw trash at Yamashita] Shinsaku Yamashita: However, if you were to bow out of the tournament, I would gladly book another match, one with no time limit, so that you could prove your so-called "superiority" of the Bonecrushers to the world. The choice is yours Yakuza. Choose wisely. [Yamashita hands the microphone back to Nishi, then exits the ring, the crowd booing even louder] ER: What an announcement by Yamashita! AO: He is trying to stifle the Yakuza! That baka!
