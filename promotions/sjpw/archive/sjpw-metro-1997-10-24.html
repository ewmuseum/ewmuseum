We're here in this lovely arena tonight to bring you some of the best wrestling action that Super Japan Pro Wrestling has to offer! For you tonight, we have in action, the man who has become somewhat of a liability, Mr. Alex Akira will be in action tonight! As well as The Brew Crew in action and Akira Hashimoto will be here as well, and we have the first man announced to step in the Escape From Earth matchup! I just hope they have the guts to go through with it and not wimp out at the last minute. Goodwin: You never know. Let's go to the ring! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Alex Akira v. Doug Thomas (j) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Akira came to the ring with a mission to destroy. Thomas fought for all he was worth, but still managed to come up short and not win the match. Akira turned around on Thomas and used many submission moves to offset the similar arsenal of his opponent. Akira got the victory, not by using his normal finisher, but a cross between the Deep Freeze, finishing move of the Big Chill, and the Kill, finishing move of Ace Killcannon's.......and it was pretty much a slap to the face. Winner: Alex Akira (w/ The RoT Medley) Time: 3:58 [The camera cuts from Akira leaving back to the table.] Goodwin: An impressive win for the SJPW's insurance liability, Mr. Alex Akira. We wish him all the luck in the world in dealing with the man who makes Howard Stern look buff, Mr. Nik Nyce. Good luck to you. Mercker: Another chapter in the saga of crumbling pro wrestling supergroups. The Reign of Terror is almsot over with here in SJPW and we wish those left over from the RoT "B-team" all the luck in the world in trying to find work someplace else. I hear WCW is always on the lookout for Sting extras, so maybe you could find work there. I, for one, will not be worrying about it. Goodwin: What we have for you now is a pre-taped match featuring Masashi "Lightning" Lanther, SJPW's resident Lyger-kin, taking on Jason "Flash" Varga for the....[squints at the cue cards] CWO Lightheavyweight title and this was taped at the CWO Sunday Showcase, apparently. We didn't edit this thing, folks. It kinda picks up during an interview. Go to it. This shall hopefully be a confrontation that will test us both to our limits, and allow the fans tonight to see a match of the highest caliber. Tonight Jason Varga, I hope to take you to the limit, and I expect the same from you. Win or lose, you will know how dangerous an opponent I can be after tonight.
