State of tag teams in e-wrestling

3-4 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
----- State of tag teams in e-wrestling (Aug 3, 1997, 12:00:00 AM) ----- [name withheld] wrote: > > Is it just me or has tag team competion in the world of e-wrestling > gotten pretty weak. I remember last summer tag teams where in pretty > good shape. Teams like The Good Knights (Best tag team ever. IMO) Damage > Inc, The Cripplers, Devil's Desciples, Hardcore Heaven, Made out of > Steel, End Game, Menence 2 Soiciety, Superiority Complex, Zulu Nation, > L.A Style, and The Boston Battalion where there. Most of those teams > have since retired or barely active. This summer and some time before > that tag team competition in about EVERY fed is sub par, of course there > are some exceptions, but not many. I've been trying to find tag teams > for fed I'm VP for and it's almost imposible to find them out their. My > question is WHAT HAPPENED TO ALL THE TAG TEAMS! Opinions are > apperciated. [name withheld], I think you're right. I've been using tag teams in a few feds: The Tokyo Blades in Wild West Wrestling [and now also AAPW], The Panthers of War in RGW, and others. I always notice that there are few teams, and of those few teams, most don't really do much. I have that problem in SJPW, but many handlers who have more than one wrestler have them team, as well as wrestle singles [which I allow], so my tag team scene at least has a full roster. [name withheld] SJPW ----- State of tag teams in e-wrestling (Aug 4, 1997, 12:00:00 AM) ----- [name withheld] wrote: > I didn't say there where no good tag teams. Just not the abundence for a > STRONG tag team divison. Teams Like Crusher Tech (Gonna retire though) > Bigger Than Hollywood, Prophets of Rage, The Demons, Harlquins, > Rednecks, Extremly Elite and Anderson/Kauffman. But it's tough to get a > active tag team scene that can keep peoples intrest. Plus tag team > angles have decreased alot since 96, now it's just they fight and thats > it. Barely any heat behind tag team matches. I have been in many indy > leagues and it's all the same (I've been in SCW too:) Here is where I disagree with you [name withheld]. Both in my fed, and in one where I am a handler, we have some good tag team angles going. Angles are something the handler, as well as the fedhead, create. It's give and take. If tag teams want to make things interesting, they have to put in the effort to do so. One of my teams who are champs are in the middle of a feud with what was the only good team to oppose me. Now a new team is in that not only looks good, but has a connection to my team's cultural and ethnic background. So now my team is going through not one, but two angles. As for angles in the SJPW tag team scene, I think they speak for themselves, though I'm sure [name withheld] will agree that the feud between the Brew Crew & The Tokyo Express is only getting hotter! [name withheld] SJPW
