SJPW House Show — Tokyo Club ATOM (7/11/04)

Jul 12, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
July 11th, 2004 Tokyo Club ATOM: 230 Fans (Sellout) Tonight's show saw the start of the Grand Prix Heritage Tag Team League, as well as more matches in the Junior Struggle! Tournament. The show was concluded with a ten man scramble rules tag match: 1) GPH Tag League: "Hard" Knox Roberts & "Terrible" Tommy Bedford def Dynamite Boy & Eiji Kid Shimura (12:34) after Bedford pinned Dynamite Boy following a Cradle Piledriver. 2) Junior Struggle! Block A: "Firefly" Horo Atashi def Toramusha (9:09) via submission from a Fujiwara armbar. 3) GPH Tag League: KANJI Sakaigama & "Crimson" Jin Hiroto def Frankie "The Thumper" Douglas & Doug Thomas (14:54) after KANJI hit the Violence Flash on Douglas 4) Junior Struggle! Block B: Tsunami def Yatoro Hori (8:01) after the Tsunami Driver #2 5) "Russian Superman" Petor Andresku, Vladimir Tarasov, Van Wild, Scott Starring & Jet Jaguar III def Wolf Jager, Gregor Davinsky, Sergei Baranov, Despair & Super Bakemono (22:15) after Andresku pinned Bakemono following a Russian Sickle. - Roberts & Bedord showed a little ring rust as a tag team, as the Bulldogs scored nine near falls on them with their double teams. However, the Gaijin duo still had that ring savvy edge that saw Roberts distract the referee, allowing Bedford to low blow Dynamite Boy, then spiking his head into the mat with his Cradle Piledriver known as "Quittin' Time" - The rookie Atashi scores another victory in his quest to win the Sedei-X title, defeating Team Tiger's Toramusha. The match was fairly fast paced, with Atashi using his martial arts skills, and Toramusha hitting some suplexes. In the end though, Atashi scored the victory by making Toramusha tap out to the Fujiwara Armbar in the middle of the ring. - The Visual Spirts, rebounding from their recent loss of the Sapporo City Tag Titles, took out their frustration on The Maulers. While Douglas & Thomas are much bigger men, KANJI & Hiroto were continually in their face. After kicking out of a Mauler Plex, KANJI caught Douglas with the Violence Flash, and made the pin while Hiroto tackled Thomas to keep him from breaking up the pin. The crowd applauded the Visual Spirits, something they're not very used to. Both men looked a little perplexed at the crowd's support, and just shook their heads as they went to the back. - Tsunami finally pulled off a win, defeating Yatoro Hori. Yatoro, so far winless in SJPW in general, gave a good fight, but he seems to still be showing alot of ring rust. Tsunami easily beat him with his Tsunami Driver #2 in under nine minutes, giving him the win. - The fans at Club ATOM were thrilled to see this huge 10 man tag match, which was announced as a Scramble Rules match. Both sides did some wild spots, with Despair taking some very nasty bumps. First he was almost pinned by an Enzugiri/Shining Wizard combo by Wild & Starring, then almost fell to a Russian Sickle from Andresku. Starring took a bump as his missed a shooting star press on Jager to the floor, his legs hitting the security railing. He then got choke slammed by Davinsky on the floor, while Van Wild & Baranov traded suplexes in the ring. After almost pinning Tarasov following the Bakemono Bomb, Super Bakemono fell to Andresku's Red Square, which some consider to be the most devastating version of the Russian Sickle going today. Bakemono was pretty much out cold, and though Despair tried to make the save, he was stopped by Jaguar III, who caught him with a missile dropkick that pitched him out of the ring to the floor. Despair needed to be helped to the back, and some fans wondered if he would be ready to defend his title against Se-hong Kwan on the 13th.....
