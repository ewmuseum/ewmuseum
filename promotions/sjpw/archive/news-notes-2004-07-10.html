News & Notes 7/10/04

Jul 11, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CEO Yamashita held a press conference today to unveil the Grand Prix Heritage Tag Team Titles, which will be awarded to the winner of the GPH Tag League, which will start on Monday, July 12th. The following teams were currently scheduled to compete in the league: Team Raiden- Masashi "Lightning" Lanther & Jiraiya The Crush Crew- Tsutomu Kobayashi & Botan Nakatoni The Maulers- Frankie "The Thumper" Douglas & Doug Thomas The Nagasaki Bulldogs- Dynamite Boy & Eiji Kid Shimura The Red Army- "Russian Superman" Petor Andresku & Vladimir Tarasov The Visual Spirits- KANJI Sakaigama & "Crimson" Jin Hiroto "Hard" Knox Roberts & "Terrible" Tommy Bedford Van Wild & Scott Starring Yamashita informed the press that Junya Toroyama had requested a slot for him and a partner to be named for the tournament. Since Team Tiger has two teams in the tournament, Yamashita said that he had informed Tiger Kikuchi to choose one team to face Toroyama and his opponent fill the eighth slot in the league. Kikuchi was not pleased by that, but said he would decide prior to the next The Zone! taping. Yamashita said that although the Junior Struggle! tournament was ongoing, he felt having the tag league would draw more crowds, and allow the teams of Super Japan to share the spotlight. Yamashita also told the press that Super Japan was planning another big Super Show for the end of August, tentatively titled "Jisedei Tsunami". Yamashita said he hopes to crown the Sedei-X champion at this event, and possibly have GPH singles and tag title matches there. MAMMOTH Kagawa & Noboru "Raging Bull" Nishida were not happy at being excluded from the GPH tag league, and filed a complaint with the SJPW board of directors. EuroPact members Gregor Davinsky & Sergei Baranov likewise filed a complaint, stating they were being over looked in favor of the Red Army. Tsunami has expressed disappointment in his performance so far in the Junior Struggle! Tournament. His 0-3 record has left him very unhappy, and he feels his lackluster performance is tied to losing his IICW title to Joey Darren at Zenmen Sensou. Tsunami told reporters he is considering going back to America to compete for awhile, and regain his inner drive. El Muerte Rojo may be new to SJPW, but he is an experienced competitor, and one third of the SMLL National Trios Champions along with El Gato Loco and Sangre Guerrero. Recently, he expressed interest in challenging for either the IICW or NCWF cruiserweight titles. "It would be an honor to wrestle either Joey Darren or Masashi Lanther for their titles. Both men are very respected competitors, and the chance to compete against either man would be a golden opportunity. I hope one of them, even both of them, will consider my challenge"
