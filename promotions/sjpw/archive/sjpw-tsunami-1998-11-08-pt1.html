Tsunami, Part One

8 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Tiger Kikuchi & Akira Ono are seated at the broadcast table. Kikuchi is in his early 40's. He's has short black hair and is wearing a black SJPW blazer, white shirt, black tie and black pants. Ono is in his mid 30s with long black hair put into a pony tail and a goatee walks in from the right side. He's wearing a black suit with a white chinese shirt and black Oakley shades] Akira Ono: Greetings! I am Akira Ono, and this is Tiger Kikuchi. Welcome to SJPW Tsunami. Tonight we see the start of the All-Asian Juuryou tournament. This is an important tournament, as we need to crown a new champion to represent the promotion. Tiger Kikuchi: So we have organized a tournament of sixteen men, wo will battle it out to crown a new Juuryou, or heavyweight for our gaijin fans, champion. AO: There is one man who may not participate though: Super Tengu. After the story broke in PN Magazine, CEO Jumbo Kobayashi was on a rampage, and he demanded that Tengu come to his office. Tengu never showed for that meeting, and at this time, has yet to arrive here. Whether he does or not is something no one seems able to answer at this time. TK: I for one think he would be foolish not to participate. His own ego refuses to allow him to face the fact that someone may have stolen his followers, even his standing, in the eyes of SJPW! AO: I wouldn't go that far Kikuchi-sama. I do agree that Tengu seems...envious of Tenru somewhat. Then of course there is the stipulation for the other Tenru to officiate the match! TK: That was an unwise move on Jumbo's part. It stacks the odds in Tengu's favor, if he so chooses to wrestle tonight. AO: Most definitely. Our first match tonight pits Kagemusha of The Daiymo against Metamorphico, who is riding high after his victory over Scorpio. Some experts are saying that Metamorphico is the 'leader' of SJPW's small group of Luchadors, which includes Scorpio, Juventud Sagrada & El Gato Diablo. TK: Kagemusha also has to be on guard, for Shishi Fujiwara is lurking in the shadows, craving satisfaction for the stain on his honor. Kagemusha cost him that loss of face, and he intends on avenging it. AO: He also needs to regain his stature as a member of Chiimu Tora, ne? [Kikuchi glares at Ono, but says nothing] AO[chuckling]: You take things too seriously old friend. Let's go to ringside, and get things underway... ______ ___________________________________ /_ __/__ __ _____ ___ ___ _ (_)Juuryou tournament, Round One: / / / (_-</ // / _ \/ _ `/ ' \/ / Kagemusha vs Metamorphico /_/ /___/\_,_/_//_/\_,_/_/_/_/_/________________________________/ [The camera cuts to the ring, where Nishi Toju and Goto Kyoji stand. The crowd is cheering. Nishi waits for the crowd to die down, the prepares to announce the match...] Nishi: Greetings Osaka! [crowd cheers] This first contest is a round one match in the All-Asian Juuryou title tournament! Introducing first, from Pico de Orizaba, Mexico, and weighing 239 pounds....MET-A-MOR-PHI-CO!!! [Spanish Mariachi music begins to play on the PA, and Metamorphico steps out on the runway entrance. The crowd starts to applaud as he surveys them. He starts to jogs down the aisle, slapping fans' hands the whole way. He is wearing an AAA shirt along with his normal ring attire] TK: The crowd seems to like Metamorphico. AO: They appreciate his sportsmanship... ["Seasons In The Abyss" by Slayer cranks on the PA] Nishi: His opponent, representing THE DAIYMO! [mixed reaction from crowd] He hails from Osaka, and weighs 229 pounds...KA-GE-MU-SHA!!! [The crowd gives a standing ovation to their hometown son. The lights go out as Kagemusha steps out onto the runway, a serene look on his face. A red spotlight shines on him. His face is painted read, with the Kanji symbol for "Akuma" painted in white on his forehead. He is wearing black Karate pants, black belt and boots. He walks calmly to the ring, and the lights come back on as he steps between the ropes. Kagemusha spits green mist into the air before walking over to Goto to recieve instructions...] TK: Kagemusha is a strange one, is he not? AO: He reminds me a little of "Demon Boy" Ishrinku, if Ishrinuk was on valiums! [both men laugh at Ono's joke] AO: The bell sounds...And Kagemusha quickly on top of things with a kick to the gut, then a DDT. He takes NO time to mount an offensive. He quickly drops an elbow to the face of Metamorphico, and is back up again. TK: This man is fast!!! Kagemusha grabs Metamorphico, and picks him up off the ground. Only to plant him back into the mat with a vicious Powerbomb. Kagemusha climbs the turnbuckle. Metamorphico slowly starts to get back up, and is met with a Missle Dropkick. [Crowd Applauds] AO: We still have no word on Tengu-sama, do we? TK: I do not think that he has showed up Ono-san. I would hate to see a talent like that just throw his career away. Even though we have had our differences, I still respect the man. AO: I also respect Tengu...And hope that he has not done something rash. TK: Regardless, we need to get back to the match Ono-san AO: Yes...Have you noticed Metamorphico, is yet to mount an offensive of any sort. He could be in deep trouble here. Kagemusha might be going for the pin, and he does... 1... 2... 3... TK: NO...A KICKOUT!!! [crowd applauds] AO: Woah...He really did luck out on that one, Tiger. That was a near pinfall, Metamorphico better get some offense going quickly, or he may find himself the loser here. Kagemusha goes for an elbow, and Metamorphico moves out of the way. TK: Metamorphico showing SOME signs of life here. Both men are up, and Metamorphico irish whips Kagemusha into the ropes. Kagemusha coming back now, hops over the head of Metamorphico. Metamorphico turns around, and waits for Kagemusha to come back again. When he does, he nails him with a Hiptoss. [crowd applauds] AO: Nice!!! Kagemusha immediately back up though, and he charges Metamorphico, and another hiptoss. [crowd applauds] This time, Kagemusha isn't so fast about getting up. Metamorphico runs over, and twists the arm of Kagemusha. TK: Kagemusha with a grimacing look of pain on his face, and Metamorphico twists again. This makes Kagemusha dance around the ring in pain. Kagemusha taps his shoulder, and then seconds later reverses the hold. Kagemusha kicks Metamorphico in the stomach, and then NAILS a Doctor Bomb!!! [crowd "oooohs"] AO: What a move!!! Good job on the part of Kagemusha. [crowd applauds] Kagemusha back down for a pinning attempt.... 1... 2... TK: A KICKOUT!!! [crowd applauds] AO: Kagemusha cannot believe it. He thought that he had the young luchador there. Metamorphico trying to get back up. He is on all fours, and...GUILLOTINE LEGDROP!!! [crowd "oooohs"] TK:Now that was painful. AO: You can bet on it Tiger. Kagemusha picking him back up. He grabs him around the neck, and...SWINGING NECKBREAKER!!! [crowd applauds] Followed up, by another pinning attempt.... 1... 2... 3... TK: NO!!! [crowd applauds] Another kickout by the young Metamorphico. Kagemusha again cannot believe it. Ono-san, you have to admire the heart of this young wrestler here. He is getting dominated out there by Kagemusha, but he just WILL NOT give up. AO: Your right Kikuchi-san. This young man does have a lot of heart, but I don't know if his heart is gonna win the match for him tonight. Kagemusha picking up Metamorphico once again, and he goes to grab the arms of Metamorphico, but Metamorphico gets his arms under Kagemusha, and wraps him up in a small package... 1... 2... TK: KICKOUT!!! [crowd applauds] The crowd are firmly behind Kagemusha, which is fitting. as this is his home. Metamorphico and Kagemusha now both up. Metamorphico with the irish whip into the ropes on Kagemusha. Kagemusha now coming back and...HANDSPRING MOONSAULT by Metamorphico takes Kagemusha down. A GREAT MOVE on the part of the youngster. AO: Yes...that was a good move, but you do not have to go crazy out here. We do have a show to run after all, and your always the one to get after me about that kind of thing. Metamorphico goes to climb the ropes. He takes himself all the way up to the top turnbuckle, and jumps off...NAILING a Sommersault Legdrop from the top-turnbuckle. Metamorphico, now going for the pin... 1... 2... 3... TK: NO!!! [crowd applauds] Another KICKOUT, by Kagemusha. Neither one of these men will go down. [crowd applauds] Metamorphico picks up Kagemusha, and Snap Suplexes him back to the ground. He then knee's Kagemusha in the face. Metamorphico goes to climb the turnbuckle another time. Metamorphico picks up Kagemusha one more time, and plants his head deep into the mat with a Michinoku Driver. [crowd "oooohs" AO: Isn't that a setup move for his finsiher Kikuchi-san? TK: Yes...And I think you did not speak a moment to soon because Metamorphico is now climbing the ropes. He is going for his finsiher, the Shooting Star Senton Bomb, and he MISSES IT [crowd "ooohs"]...Kagemusha moved out of the way at the last second. CAN YOU BELIEVE IT? AO: I can believe it...I have seen it happen MANY times in my long wrestling career Tiger. TK: Wait a second! Who is that? AO: It looks to be Shishi Fujiwara, is it not? TK: He is here to settle the score... (Camera cuts over to a shot of Shishi Fujiwara walking down the isle. All the fans have started to make noise, and Kagemusha turns around to see what the fuss is about. He then see's Fujiwara advancing toward him. Fujiwara stops just a couple of feet away from the ring, and Kagemusha and him just stare each other down. This Gives Metamorphico enough time to get back up, and run up behind Kagemusha, and dropkick him to the back.) AO: SWEET REVENGE!!! TK: YES...I WOULD SAY SO... AO: As Kagemusha falls into the ropes, and Metamorphico decides to capitalize on the situation...He grabs Kagemusha from behind in a waistlock...GERMAN SUPLEX! [crowd "ooohs"] Metamorphico bridges back for the pin and Shishi Fujiwara grabs his ankles and holds them down. The referee does not notice. This could be the end... 1... 2... 3... **ding** **ding** **ding** [As Spanish Mariachi music plays, the crowd applauds, though some boo] Nishi: The winner of this match....METAMORPHICO!!! TK: This can only make the FIRE that has lit up between Fujiwara, and Kagemusha GROW even STRONGER. AO: I'll say. However, Kagemusha started it. So far, Chiimu Tora is ahead of The Daiymo in their matches. Three to one so far, is it not? TK: I haven't been keeping track. AO: Of course you haven't! That would be showing favoritism! [Kikuchi glares at Ono, who feigns a look of innocence] TK: Coming up next, what should be an interesting match pitting a high flyer against a submission expert. AO: Yes but what’s interesting is that in this case, the high flyer actually weighs more than the mat technician! TK: That’s true. It’ll be intriguing to see how this matchup goes. ______ ___________________________________ /_ __/__ __ _____ ___ ___ _ (_)Juuryou tournament, Round One: / / / (_-</ // / _ \/ _ `/ ' \/ / Scorpio vs. Kyle Lee /_/ /___/\_,_/_//_/\_,_/_/_/_/_/________________________________/ [The camera cuts to a shot of Nishi Toju standing in the ring with his microphone in hand.] Nishi: This contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, hailing from Sao Paolo, Brazil, weighing in at 246 pounds... SCOR-PI-O! ["Carry On" by Angra begins playing over the P.A. system as a handsome, muscular man with low-cut blond hair steps into view. He is wearing yellow pants and green boots, with the Brazilian flag marked on each boot The fans applaud as Scorpio walks quickly down to the ring where Scorpio grabs the top rope and executes a slingshot moonsault into the ring as green and yellow fireworks explode from each ringpost.] AO: An impressive entrance for the Brazilian superstar but he’s been a little shaken up since his match with Metamorphico. His valet is also missing again. I wonder why? TK: Yes but he is a consummate professional and from the expression on his face, you can see that he is very focused on this match. As for his valet, that is none of our concern! Nishi: And his opponent... hailing from Dallas, Texas... weighing in at 230 pounds... "THE DOOMSAYER" KYLE LEE! ["Perry Mason" by Ozzy Osbourne begins playing over the P.A. as a slender but athletic man with black hair steps through the entrance curtains. The fans cheer politely if somewhat less enthusiastically than they greeted Scorpio moments earlier. Lee is wearing blue jeans, a black "MINISTRY" T-shirt and a black trenchcoat. While his music grinds over the speakers, he walks to the ring staring straight at Scorpio, largely oblivious to the cheers of the fans.] AO: Scorpio will definitely have his work cut out for him as while he’s the high-flyer of these two, Lee may actually have a speed and agility advantage. TK: The fact that Lee is also significantly taller than Scorpio may help him in that his reach will be that much longer. But Scorpio has impressed me with his abilities and his power will be difficult for Lee to resist. AO: There’s the bell and the two men surge together in a collar-and-elbow lock up. Lee twists himself out of it, comes up beside Scorpio and goes for a quick Russian legsweep right off the start... no! Scorpio counters with an elbowsmash that catches "The Doomsayer" flush on the jaw. TK: Lee trying to establish his technical supremacy right off the bat but isn’t able to maintain the advantage. He grabs an arm and sends Scorpio into the ropes with an Irish whip but the Brazilian rebounds and smacks into Lee with a clothesline. AO: Hard-hitting action from the very beginning as Lee backs up a pace, circling around to look for an opening. Scorpio charges but Lee ducks underneath and comes up behind him, slapping on a full nelson... wait a moment... FULL NELSON SLAM! TK: Impressive power move from the Texan as he drives Scorpio down into the mat. A couple of quick stomps and "The Doomsayer" lays in some punishment, trying to wear the heavier man down. But Scorpio’s made of stronger stuff and manages to roll to his feet. Lee trying to stay on top of him... oh! AO: There’s Scorpio’s strength advantage coming into play as he grabs Lee and in a sort of combination hiptoss-bodyslam, he throws Lee over the top rope and out of the ring. Fortunately for Lee, he doesn’t come down too hard and he takes a moment to walk around on the outside. TK: Some early trading of the advantage in this match but Lee seems to be taking the more methodical approach. Having regained his bearings, he rolls back into the ring. Scorpio doesn’t cut him any slack, however, as he lays into Lee with a series of chops that knock the Texan onto his back. Scorpio bounces off the ropes and... RUNNING SOMERSAULT LEGDROP! AO: Exceptional mobility and you can see the lucha libre influence on Scorpio’s technique. Lee is dazed by that high-impact maneuver and Scorpio pulls him to his feet and whips him into the ropes. Lee tries to counter Scorpio’s momentum with a clothesline but he misses as the Brazilian ducks underneath it. Lee bounces off the ropes on the opposite side and rebounds back only to be taken down with a mighty chop across the chest. TK: Ordinarily I would have said that "The Doomsayer" was the better brawler but Scorpio is faring quite well so far. Lee is struggling to his feet as Scorpio steps out onto the ring apron. The Brazilian lines up his target... and hits Lee with a springboard shoulderblock! AO: He may be the heavier of the two but we’re certainly seeing Scorpio fly tonight! But Lee isn’t out of this yet as he rolls to his feet while Scorpio goes after him with a European uppercut... no! Lee blocks it and doubles him over with a kick to the gut. A kneelift sends Scorpio flying onto his back and it’s Lee with the advantage now. TK: Notice how Lee is constantly circling behind Scorpio whenever he gets the chance? He’s a very smart, technical wrestler as he slaps on a waistlock... BELLY-TO-BACK SUPLEX! AO: Great move! And Lee flings Scorpio right off his feet and stuns the Brazilian by hurling him down to the mat! Scorpio’s up quickly but Lee shows some of his infamous aggression as he’s right there to nail him with a series of headbutts to the head. TK: This is the brawling style Lee is so good at as he knocks the wind out of Scorpio with a fist to the midsection. Scorpio’s bent over and Lee hammers away on his shoulders with a number of punishing forearm smashes. He steps up beside Scorpio... GUTWRENCH SUPLEX! AO: Lee was in trouble earlier but he’s dominating now as he begins wearing Scorpio down, perhaps for one of his submission holds. And both men are getting a good reaction from the crowd as they appreciate the ability shown by the two of them.
