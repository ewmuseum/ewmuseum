Battlecade '97 Control Center Special

6 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
AO: El Perfecto would make an excellant addition to SJPW's roster. He does seem a bit arrogant though. ER: That's El Perfecto's way. We also have comments from Tiger Kikuchi... ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 6/5/97] [Tiger Kikuchi is seen sitting in his garden. He is wearing a kimono, and is sipping tea.] Tiger Kikuchi: A "Battle Of the Legends" they are calling it. A fitting name for my match with El Perfecto. This man has won numerous titles in the US, just as I have won numerous titles in Japan. We are much alike, El Perfecto and I. [pauses to sip his tea] TK: I wonder though, which of us has the better finishing move? You have your Perfecto-Plex, but can it compare to my Tiger Driver? Unlikely. Take this not as an insult Perfecto, for I have much respect for you. I only wish to point out the obvious proof that my finishing hold is more punishing than yours. In the end, that may mean the difference between victory and defeat. [pauses to sip tea again] TK: I have much to do at Battlecade. I must stand beside young JT Ruckus, as he wrestles the even younger Lance Smith, son of a man I have fought many times in the past. Then I must face El Perfecto. Two momentus tasks in one night. A lesser man would falter, but not I. [pauses to sip tea again] TK: Super Tengu, you and I have unfinished business. You see to think that you are the best cruiserweight in SJPW. That may be true. However, if you should somehow defeat Shinji Hamada, a man who I believe to be innocent of these foolish charges made against him, then I hope to pit my new protege against you. I feel my Black Tiger has what it takes to defeat you. [sips tea again] TK: Yes, Battlecade will be a momentus night for many of us. For others, it will be a tragedy. I for one plan on having it be momentus, so El Perfecto, prepare yourself to find only tragedy. [The camera slowly fades out as Tiger continues to sip his tea] ------------------------------------------------------------------- AO: Tiger Kikuchi is a talented man. Of the 3 times I wrestled him, I only defeated him once, and then just barely. El Perfecto may find himself in deep trouble tomorrow night. ER: It's quite possible, but we'll see, won't we? The next match will be the HCW tag team match. This promises to be exciting, as HCW wrestlers are experts at being extreme. We have comments from two men who are teaming together in this Match: Ben "Sexyboy" Toussi, and Michael "The Punisher" Kegonite... ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 6/3/97] [Camera opens on Ben "Sexyboy" Toussi, a youung man with brown hair and a "Extreme Wrestling" T-Shirt, standing in a white cement walled hallway, with an "HCW" banner in red and black behind him] Ben "Sexyboy" Toussi: Hello all! I am a wrestler from the Hardcore Championship Wrestling Promotion in the US! Actually, we travel all over the world! I hear that I have to team up with Punisher! Well, if you guys have ever watched HCW, you might have seen that we have been enemies in the past! BUT, if Punisher can get himself together, I KNOW that we can be a team and kick the (bleep) out of Enforcer and Hades! Enforcer and Hades, you guys are in for a fight!!!!! I know how good I am, and I know how good Punisher is ...believe me!!!! I know 'cause I felt the pain!!! Enforcer, and Hades.......you guys are going down!!!! End of discussion! [Camera fades out to HCW banner in the hallway] ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 6/4/97] [Scene opens on Michael "The Punisher" Kegonite against the HCW banner in the white cement hall.] Punisher: SJPW , get ready for some HardCore action. You guys are about to find out the true definition of Hardcore. I'm here cause I just love fighting in no DQ matches, how unlucky for you guys. But let me not bore you with myself and get right to the point. [name withheld], I have nothing much to say. You and me against Enforcer and Hades. We do have our differences and similarities Ben, I'm willing to forget all about it and get into the ring at your side. Enforcer, Hades, get your asses ready for some hard whipping. For the rest of you SJPW punks, watch out for me to make my mark in that little Battle Royal of yours. [AJ Black walks in, and stands in the shot next to Punisher] SJPW fans, you want to see what hardcore is all about? Well I look at the card, and I see many great EX HCW superstars, hey why don't you ask them? Anyhow what's this I see, a battlecade, a 20 men over the top slash pin slash submission battle royal with ropes of barbwire?? [Looks back at Punisher and turns to face you] Well if you boys want to see a fight, and the meaning of Hardcore, then just WATCH as the HCW win that event, and witness the way pro Wrasslin SHOULD be! [Both stare at the camera, fade out] ------------------------------------------------------------------- AO: These men should provide us with some excitement! ER: I'm sure they will. Following that will be the return match we've all been waiting for... ------------------------------------------------------------------- Akira Hashimoto vs "The Perfectionist" Nik Nyce ------------------------------------------------------------------- AO: This match will be one of the best matches on the entire card. Akira Hashimoto and Nik Nyce are master technicians. Akira won the first one, but Nyce has shown recently that he is a general in the ring when he downed Kenriki Ogami in that 8 man tag match. ER: We caught up with Nyce recently, and recorded these comments... ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 6/5/97] [Nik Nyce is shown wearing some black sweats and a black tall around his neck. He's headed in the locker room shower but notices a SJPW camera man] NN: Damn, I told you guys that I would send you an interview later. Why are you invading my privacy. CM: We really need your comments before you match with Akira Hashimoto. NN: Of course you do, its the only thing that puts life into your little "Trash Talk" show. NN: Well lets get some other stuff out of the way first before I talk about Akira Hashimoto. NN: Badd Intentions, I don't really care if you know who I am or not. I hope you don't, I like surprising people by beating them with pure ease. Badd Intentions you boys are lacking many things. Brains is on the top of the MIA list. NN: You know I've seen tag teams a whole lot badder than you boys. I head from the states that you boys still live at home with mommy and daddy. You guys don't even get to drive their car on weekends. You know I've seen your contracts and there's nothing holding you guys from leaving, I'm sure you won't disapoint the Japanese fans. Hell even the hardcore Japanese fans think your weak. NN: I don't want to face just one you little boys, I want both of you. I really don't care who I tag with. I'm sure I can find somebody able enough to stand in the corner and watch be beat your asses back to the states. NN: So after battlecade boys, we'll get a little match we'll see who's so damn Badd! NN: Another list on todays agenda is all these "special guests" from other feds. A few of the guys comming in are pretty average workers but I don't know how much the HCW guys bribed SJPW officials but it has to be an amazing amount of money. NN: Plain and simple, HCW is a joke. I seen a lot and fought against a lot of HCW "big draws" in various other organizations and have to say that they arn't very impressive. NN: For example, El Perfecto is a complete waste of wrestling tights. This guy just makes me laugh everytime he gets in the ring. When we were both in the same organization, he keep ducking me and wouldn't fight me. NN: "Hardcore" John Stillwater, this guy is all over place working in all kinds of places but the reason why is because the undercard doesn't pay too well and that's all he'll ever be outside of the HCW. He should be really be renamed "Mr. One-Dimensional" John Stillwater. Come on John, any drunk ass bumb can brawl. NN: Ohhhh and the great Champion of the HCW. The Heartbreaker. This guy had three matches with me and couldn't pin me. He proves that there is no real competion in the HCW or they would of kick his ass by now. It still makes me laughing thinking that this clown is the World Champ over there. NN: Ahhh.. the list could go on and on but the HCW is just a home for people that can't make it anywhere else. HCW can't afford to pay for the big name talent or they can't hold them for very long. For all you HCW boys comming over here to "visit", your going to get a quick reality check when you check out the competion here. NN: The competition here is a little less than average here but when compared to the HCW, SJPW looks like a who's who of champions. NN: Oh yeah, I was going to talk about my match coming up at Battlecade. NN: So I finally get my rematch against old man Hashimoto. NN: Hashimoto I know you were in the states for a while doing a few things and I've heard of a slow boat to China but COME ON. NN: Hashimoto I know your afraid and you should be. I had you beaten the last time we met but the refs here leave a lot to be desired. Hashimoto I wouldn't be surprised if you didn't even show up for this match. You just retire now before you get hurt in the ring. NN: Akira Ono, you can beg all you want but Hashimoto is very afraid and all your begging won't help him win a match against Nik Nyce. Once I beat him with ease, it will then be on to winning the SJPW Heavyweight title. [screen fades] ------------------------------------------------------------------- ER: I just want to point out that Nik Nyce's comments about HCW are his alone, and do not reflect the opinion of SJPW. We have the utmost respect for HCW and it's wrestlers; if we didn't, we wouldn't have worked put agreements for a talent exchange. AO: Don't forget that Nyce is one of the SJPW wrestlers who will go on the HCW tour too! ER: That is very strange. Maybe Nyce is gunning for a shot at Heartbreaker's title? Only time will tell. Following this match we will see a tag team war. Badd Intentions and the Tokyo Express have been at odds since our first event. Their war keeps flip flopping. At Battlecade, they meet once again in the ring... ------------------------------------------------------------------- Badd Intentions vs The Tokyo Express ------------------------------------------------------------------- AO: Badd Intentions have all the power, while the Express are faster and use more holds. Both teams are masters of tag team wrestling, so it comes down to one thing: Endurance. Whichever team can outlast the other will win. ER: With the no dq rules in effect, I have to give the edge to Badd Intentions. They used a brick during After Dark, and they could have been dq'd for it, Without the DQ rule, who knows what they will bring to the ring this time! AO: You have a point there Eddie-san. However, the Tokyo Express do have a mean streak, and we saw some of that during After Dark. Riki Kano can brawl too, and Ryu Taka, the weasel that he is, isn't above cheating to help his men, fan favorites or not! ER: It will definitely be an interesting matchup. Following this matchup, we will showcase two stars from PMW as Billy Hollywood battler "Demon Boy" Ishrinku in an exploding barbed wire ladder glass match! It will be a bloodbath, that's for sure. AO: It fits well with the concept of Battlecade though. ER: Which leads us to our main event: The Battlecade Brawl. 20 men in two rings surrounded by barbed wire laced with landmine bomblets. Over the top rope rules are in effect, as well as the standard rules for pinfall and submission. 16 SJPW, and 4 HCW wrestlers will be involved in this match. We have comments from some of those set to compete, starting with the newcomer, "Dragon" Hanashi Tanaka.... ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 6/5/97] (The camera opens onto a the base of a large mountain somewhere in Tibet. Nothing seems out of the ordinary about this scene, just another day in the Tibetan terrain. Slowly the camera pans up the mountain. It moves slowly up, catching glimpses of cracks in the rock, snow, and thousands of years in Natural history. About midway the scenery changes as a large stone and wood structure sits on a vast ledge, seemingly out of place. The camera remains focused on the struture and then it fades out to a second camera, this one situated directly in front of the large building. This huge building seems lifeless, yet full of activity. You can only wonder at the countless years it has existed, the things it has seen in it's life. It's purpose was at first a mystery, but now becomes clear, it is a buddhist monastary. It seems uterly lifeless. devoid of all mortality, it's existence forgotten. Just off to the side of the building, something seems out of the ordinary, out of place, not part of the picture. It moves to focus on the foreign presence and reveals a person, a being sitting patiently on a large, flat and uncomfortable rock. This person sits motionless, eyes closed and deep in thought. He is a mid sized Japanese looking man, early twenties, with short black hair. He is dressed in a full white and black Gi, and his face is painted in all white. on either side of his face are japanese characters which form his name. The camera zooms in to a tight closeup of the persons face. Seemingly oblivious to the world, he seems unable, or unwilling to recognize the modern technological presence. The camera remains locked on the person, and several minutes later, he decides to speak.) PERSON: Peacefull, is the way of the monk. Quiet is his life. Uneventful, and bathed in silence and prayer. The monk vows to uphold the ways of Buddha, to make sense of his works, and live his celebrated writings. That is what we are meant to do. We are expected to be the peacefull person personified in many Western books, plays, and Motion Pictures. I vowed to live such a life. I vowed to become what Buddha wanted. Many years I have been here. Born in Japan, I wanted nothing more than to do what I am doing now. Or so I thought. I tried to hide the other desire I had, the other dream I wanted to live, but found that I could not, it was impossible to control. It consumed all that I was untill I could no longer deny it's existence. I wanted to become a wrestler, much like those I had seen in my childhood, before I had commited my life to Buddha. I decided to live such a dream, to make it a reality. I found the greatest teachers I could, practiced many hours, in grueling, often unbearable conditions. I practiced until I felt I could compete against the worlds best, the best that the ring could offer. I would not rest untill I could wrestle well and bring honour to my country and my god. I found that time came quickly, and I set off for a career in the ring. I first made my way to the United states, making it the springboard to my career. There I would gain the experience necessary to compete in Japan. I found success there, although I have yet to gain a title. But long did the thought of Japans rings weigh on my mind. I wanted so desperately to wrestle at home, where I could gain the status I wanted, and truly complete myself. Now that Time has come, Now it is time to return to my homeland and it's wrestling rings and bring honour to my name. I am "The Dragon" Hanashi Tanaka, and I am here in the SJPW to become the wrestler I have dreamed about. Here I will make my name great, and I will bring honour to the ring. I am here to serve the ways of buddha, and make those without honour see the way of the great god. Good and evil alike will witness the wrath of the "Dragon", they will feel his fire, and will get burned in the process. If you wrestle without honour, you will soon feel the wrath of the DRAGON. If you beat the common person without mercy, you WILL meet the wrath of the dragon, and if you lack respect for life itself, you will feel the wrath of the DRAGON. (With those words the dragon retrurns to his silent prayer. The peacefull state he was found in. The sanctuary he goes to escape the world. He has come to the SJPW to bring honour to it, and he will not stop untill it happens.) ------------------------------------------------------------------- AO: Hanashi Tanaka is a competant wrestler, and has been a top title contendor in many federations. His appearance at Battlecade is a sign on the kind of quality competition SJPW recruits. Another man who will make his first appearance at Battlecade is Aguri Nagasawa, a man out to destroy the recovering Patty Babes Carter.. ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 6/1/97] [Scene opens on Aguri Nagasawa spluttering as he heres about Mr. Big, he starts to choke on a piece of his massive egg sandwhich and then recovers to say something.] AN: Ahem.... Right, Eddie-San, you are a baffoon, Mr, Big will win because of his size? Eddie my fine squirt, I am much bigger than he. And what else? Survival winning, as long as he has no help, he will be eliminated by yours truly at BattleCade. No matter what, I'll be wrestling soon, and want to wrestle the best that SJPW can throw at me. No boring down to Earth twits please. [He gobbles the rest of his egg sandwhich and splutters most of it on the floor, before rubbing his mouth.] AN: You know, it seems to me that Patty Babes Carter is scared, he hasn't been seen in the SJPW for a month now, yet I here he is back in the game. The bigger game. He is still signed as an SJPW wrestler, he will never escape from the Aguri Splash.... Again. Ahh, at BattleCade, i see a couple of TBA's. Let me see, will Carter step forward to fight then? I don't think so, if he does or doesn't, he'll have to, as I'm getting that slot RIGHT NOW!! Are you ready Carter? Survival and your other friends can't help you, can you help yourself? [Scene fades] ------------------------------------------------------------------- ER: That man is HUGE! Mr. Big would have trouble with this guy! Another man in the Brawl is the RoT's Ace Killcannon.... ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 6/3/97] [Fade into the Reign of Terror War Room, where everything is abuzz for some reason. Phones are ringing, people are coming in and out, and it seems rather chaotic. The camera finds Ace Kilcannon sitting in a corner chair, seemingly oblivious to all the camotion. As the camera comes closer Ace looks up at it. He speaks very softly and deep.] Ace Kilcannon: "Battlecade is right on the horizon now...an event of epic proportion in SJPW. It truly starts a new era for a fairly new promotion in Japan. Wrestlers will go for gold, revenge, and many other tangibles that go with a PPV. Along with people coming from federations all over the globe just to be a part of it. At this historic event, there is two paticular matches that I find interest in. First of all, there is the Cruiserweight title match between Super Tengu and Shinji Hamada..." [Ace seems to become angered at the the thought of the two men he just mentioned.] Ace Kilcannon: "The two men who screwed me out of MY chance at that oh so coveted gold belt that shall be decided this weekend. I should be wrestling the loud mouth Tengu...NOT poor Shinji...he took the cheap way to a victory over me. I had him primed for the kill with my plancha, but then Tengu knocks him outta the way and I bounce my head off the guardrail, which causes me to bleed like a stuck pig. Of course, Shinji takes FULL advantage of the situation by applying some little submission hold, which he KNEW I wouldn't be able to handle with all that blood loss. The ref raises his hand and another gaijin gets screwed. I will exact revenge for not only that, Shinji, but ALL the pain and suffering that you have caused me over the many years since out split...prepare." [Ace runs his hands through his hair and then looks back at the camera.] Ace Kilcannon: "And You Tengu...you will also pay for that before mentioned missed plancha...you have been the cause of a lot of pain I have felt lately....many nights I have stayed awake...thinking of how my DESTINY of taking that SJPW Cruiserweight title was snatched right from my hands by YOU and Shinji...that gold WILL eventualy reside right here in the Reign of Terror War Room..that's a guarantee. You best be watching your back at all time to, Tengu, because you never know when I'll decide to get my revenge on you! This is your final warning, Tengu, so you best leave that movie set and hide at home, because I am no longer responsible for my actions...I do WHATEVER it takes to punish you and Shinji for your pitiful existences...count on it." [Ace finally calms himself and goes abck to his soft and deep voice he started with.] Ace Kilcannon: "Then, the other match I hold particulat interest in is the actual Battlecade Brawl main even match...where twenty men will go through a sadistic match to crown a new kind if champion..one that I WILL become, since I have been bounced from the cruiserweight tournament by two unhonorable men. I will take every one of you son's of bitches and through your asses out of the ring and onto those mines that surrond that damn ring. All these outsiders that are coming in for this match have no idea for what kind of beating they are in store for...I plan on taking out all this built up anger and frustration all of those nips and outsiders that step in the ring with me. NONE of you can go to the extreme like I can...you hear me...NONE of you. I'm going to prove to all the sorry fans, wrestlers, and brass what Ace Kilcannon is all about....and that a gaijin CAN fight through all the bias around here to win a title. This is something which I now MUST do to put myself back into contention for that belt that either Tengu or Hamada will hold. To the other nineteen pieces of trash in the Battlecade Brawl...you best be ready for the ass kicking of a life time, because Ace is coming and you ALL will be ACED!" [Fade to black.] ------------------------------------------------------------------- ER: And to end things off, we have comments from the ever-controversial Survival... ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 6/4/97] [Scene opens to the outside building of the Renaissance Inc. building. A man storms out the front door, wearing a black Armani and smoking a cigarette. It doesn't take a genious to figure out who that man is...who is Survival, in case someone out there hasn't yet. <g> Survival's face bleeds with anger as he nearly topples the cameraman. He stops dead in his tracks and glares at the camera with his wide, piercing eyes. He speaks, loudly and angrily.> Survival: I'll bet that this is a real surprise to all of you,. hearing from me. Well, I'll tell ya, punks, I don't give a DAMN what you think!! I lost my enthusiasm for this Japanese federation when those jack asses over at the SPJW decided that the Japenese were by decree, better than us "Gaijins." I used to respect the Japanese, but when my good "friend" and partner, Kenriki Ogami, got his ass torn apart by Elijah...I drew the line! My purpose here in the SPJW is *not* to team up with these Japanese-san losers...it's to win titles!! I'm here to make a name for myself world wide! And if the SPJW has it in for me, calling me a barbaric Gaijin...then I ain't gonna put up with this bullshit!! I do what I do because it works for me. There's hundreds of other wrestlers who agree with me. But to fight with "honor"?? Japan isn't one to talk about "honor", when they spend their time cheating the world out of money and power! I saw Jarred Jones acting like a racist. Don't second guess me. I'm no racist. But I tell it like it is! You morons laugh at people like me because we're "barbaric." And yet, you happily count your money every time I sell out your crowds! Who's barbaric?? At Battlecade, I'm going to trash this barbaric Gaijin nonsense by throwing out each and every Japanese member out of the god damn ring! And that goes double for the HCW jack asses who crawled their way in!! <Survival pauses to take a drag from his cigarette.> Survival: Speaking of the HCW, what are they doing here?? They're just as "barbaric", if not *more* than me!! And they're all crooks, nobodies who don't belong in the same class as me! I'm making this statement, and those HCW "presidents" had better hear me out! You guys cheated me out of the World title by handing it to that second rate loser, Heartburn...er, Heartbreaker! But that's old news. The big news is that I'm here and I'm ready to take the SPJW by storm! And Ogami, you'd better watch your steps 'cause I'm not appreciative of your lack of effort lately. Get it? Got it?? GOOD!!! [Survival grabs the camera and extinguishes his cigarette on the lens. Scene fades.] ------------------------------------------------------------------- AO: Bold words from Survival. He talks tough, but in the end, he is just another Gaijin trying to be like Stan Hansen. He will soon learn that he made a mistake insulting all the japanese wrestlers like that. ER: He is one of the favorites to win Battlecade though! AO: Maybe so, but with the HCW wrestlers gunning for him, and many others, I think his chances of winning have gone down considerably. ER: You could be right Akira. That wraps up this special edition of Control Center. Tomorrow night is Battlecade '97; call your cable company for availability as soon as possible. Don't miss out on your chance to see the first SJPW Pay Per View! For Akira Ono, I'm Eddie Rose.
