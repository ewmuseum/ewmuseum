SJPW House Show — Chiba Bridge Arena Sub Arena (6/18/04)

Jun 19, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
June 18th, 2004 Chiba Bridge Arena Sub Arena: 400 fans (Sellout) 1) Wolf Jager & Sergei Baranov def Pramoj Ananda & Se-hong Kwan (9:00) after Baranov ko'd an Exploder '98 on Ananda. 2) Toramusha def Botan Nakatoni (10:09) after Toramusha hit the Tiger Suplex '85 with a bridge. 3) "GAIJIN Extreme" Jun Suzuki & "The Wolverine" Masuhiro Hayatashi def Frankie "The Thumper" Douglas & Doug Thomas (7:55) after Suzuki made Douglas tap to the Suzuki Deathlock. 4) "Dragon Fury" Minobu Tetsuhara def Mascara Negra (12:47) with the Dragon Fury Suplex. 5) Takahiro KEOKI, Dragon Mask & MAMMOTH Kagawa def Junya Toroyama, Yatoro Hori & El Gato Loco (22:17) after Kagawa pinned Toroyama following the MAMMOTH Charge! - The team of Jager & Baranov avenged their earlier tag loss to Ananda & Kwan when Ananda was knocked out after Baranov dumped him on his head with an Exploder '98 -Toramusha scored his first singles win over Crush Crew member Nakatoni, hitting the '85 version of the Tiger Suplex, and bridging it to pin him. - The Maulers took the fight to Suzuki & Hayatashi early in the match, but the monster and the man who wants to retire Brody Thunder were just too much for them, with Douglas tapping out after his back was worked over, and Suzuki put him in his version of the Sharpshooter. - Tetsuhara unleashed the Dragon Fury Suplex, which is a half-nelson, half Chicken Wing suplex, to take out the masked luchador newcomer. - Team Tiger scored a measure of revenge on Toroyama for their six man loss on The Zone! recently. KEOKI & Dragon mask wisely avoided much ring time with Toroyama, allowing the rookie powerhouse Kagawa to deal with him. After a successful double team by Dragon Mask & KEOKI on Toroyama, where Dragon Mask hit a falling backdrop suplex following by a top rope elbow by KEOKI, Kagawa was tagged in, and speared Toroyama halfway across the ring, his partners prevented Toroyama's team mates from breaking up the pin. After the match, Kagawa got on the microphone, and swore that at Zenmen Sensou, he'd beat Toroyama with the MAMMOTH Charge! again, and drive him out of Super Japan for good.
