There are two high back chairs with a large "V" shaped table in the middle of the set. The table has a large SJPW Logo on the front of it in Red Black and White. There are stacks of papers on top of the table. There is a video wall covering the entire back side of the set. Currently only a SJPW logo is shown on the screen. A caucasian man in his mid 20's walks in from the left. He's has red hair and is wearing blue jeans, a black T-Shirt with "SJPW" written in Red on it, and a black leather jacket. An asian man in his mid 30s with long black hair put into a pony tail and a goatee walks in from the right side. He's wearing a black suit with a white chinese shirt and black Oakley shades. They both sit in their respective chairs.] Eddie Rose: Good evening everyone. I'm Eddie Rose, and this is my partner, the legendary Akira Ono. Welcome to another addition of Super Japan Pro Wrestling Ring Wars, LIVE from the Tokyo Dome, in Tokyo, Japan. We'll see the debut of some new SJPW superstars: Steel Serpent, Baby Hotta, Rekka Sakura, and Jet Jaguar. Tonight's lineup change was made because Deja-Vu suddenly backed out of their contract with SJPW, and the fact that our mini-division wrestlers didn't make it to Japan in time to compete tonight. Akira Ono: And we have two round two cruiserweight matches, as Omega battles Kyu Kabuki, and Shinji Hamada faces his old partner, Ace Killcannon! ER: And our main event for the evening will pit 8 men against each other. On one side we have Kenriki Ogami, Survival, and the Tokyo Express, and on the other we have Badd Intentions, Elijah the Ultimate Physical Specimen, and "The Perfectionist" Nik Nyce. This is going to be one hell of a main event! AO: I feel sorry for Elijah and Nyce. ER: Why is that? AO: Because they are being forced to team with Badd Intentions. These two men will be the downfall of their side. I am sure of that. ER: If you say so. Let's go to ringside for our first matchup! [The camera pans to the Tokyo Dome, where thousands of japanese wrestling fans await the start of the show. As the announcement that the show is about to begin, the crowd erupts with loud hand clapping, and some throw streamers into the ring. The Ring Announcer steps into the ring, and waits for the clapping to die down] Ring Announcer: Honorable Greetings SJPW fans! (huge crowd pop) Welcome to SJPW Ring Wars! Our first contest is one fall, 30 minute time limit. Introducing first, from Nagasaki, weighing 230lbs, RY-U FUJI-MO-TO (Ryu Fujimoto walks down the runway. He looks like a younger version of his brother Takashi) ER: I hear this is his first professional match Akira. AO: Yes. His brother trained him, so I expect that given his youth, he will someday develop into a championship caliber wrestler. Ring Announcer: His opponent, from Bangkok, Thailand, weighing 240lbs, STEEL SER-PENT!!! (As "The Unforgiven" by Metallica plays, Steel Serpent and his bodyguard Victor Creed walk down the runway. Serpent is wearing black trunks, with tape going from his ankles down to his knees. He is barefoot, and has no elbow or kneepads. he is wearing a black mask with a metallic silver cobra on the front of the mask. The only facial features visible are his brown eyes. Victor Creed is large, and is wearing black army boots, jeans with a leather belt, a black, sleeveless t-shirt, and sunglasses. He has long, blonde hair tied back in a ponytail, and has a tattoo of barb wire around his right bicep) ER: This Victor Creed is massive! Wasn't he a pro wrestler at one time? AO: Yes, but he is only here as a bodyguard or manager for Serpent. I understand he helped teach Serpent to wrestle, and to apply his thai kickboxing skills to wrestling. I like this man. I saw him fight in a tournament, and he is very talented. ER: This is the man you'd like to see face Sung Kim? AO: Oh yes. I think these two men would have a classic battle! There's the bell... They lock up...Serpent with a series of 4 knees to Ryu's gut! Hiptoss now by Serpent; he reaches down to grab Ryu, who kicks him off, then kip ups to his feet. ER: Ryu takes a fighting stance...Serpent now with a series of jabs and kicks, but Ryu blocks them...backfist by Ryu staggers Serpent..Ryu moves in..hiptoss by Ryu. Ryu is a decent martial artist himself. (fans clap) AO: Oh yes. He and his brother have some skill in various techniques. Almost all japanese wrestlers do. But Serpent has much more skill in his art, and experience in the ring as a kickboxer. Ryu and Serpent lock up again, and Serpent takes him down with an armdrag, and goes into an armbar. Nice combination. ER: Ryu getting to his feet.. he's trying to find a way out of the hold... reversal by Ryu, but Serpent counters with a back elbow to the forehead. Serpent now with a wristlock, holding his arm up high...nice side kick by Serpent to the side of Ryu's head (fans clap) AO: Serpent now in with a series of forearm shivers to the side of his head, right where he kicked it before. Ryu looks punch drunk now! Serpent releases the wristlock, and grabs Ryu from behind... waistlock belly to back suplex (fans clap). I told you he knows some wrestling! ER: And he executes nicely too. I hear he'e entered Battlecade too, and is the 4th man to do so. Serpent has Ryu up..butterfly suplex. Serpent now dropping some elbows onto Ryu's chest... cover..one..two..kickout. AO: Serpent has him up again, and whips him to the ropes... Ryu ducks the thrust kick.. he comes off the ropes... Flying Forearm caught Serpent in the side of the head (fans clap)! Ryu now with a dropkick! He whips Serpent to the ropes... spinning elbowsmash sends Serpent to the mat! ER: Ryu now coming off the ropes... somersault senton splash... cover..one..two..t-kickout! (fans clap) Ryu has him up... Northern Lights Suplex! Cover..one..two..kickout! (fans clap) AO: Ryu has him up once more..he whips him to the ropes... Serpent ducks the cross bodyblock attempt, and Ryu hits the mat hard! (fans oooh) Serpent runs over to him, and nails him with a vicious kick to the ribs! Two! Three! Four! Five! Ryu is clutching his chest now! ER: Serpent now with a single leg Boston Crab on Ryu. He seems to be using the basic technical and submission holds, combined with his martial arts skill. Ryu is telling the ref he won't submit, but he is obviously in alot of pain after those kicks to the ribs. AO: Definitely. Serpent may be using the basic holds, but he executes them well, which is what counts! Ryu is now trying to get to the ropes...he's inching slowly towards them... he's almost there... Serpent breaks the hold before he gets to the ropes! (fans applaud) I wonder why? ER: Serpent drags Ryu to his feet, and wails on him with body punches on the ropes. The ref makes him back off, but the damage to the ribs is all ready done. He's definitely methodical, and is wearing the kid down piece by piece! AO: Serpent has him in the middle of the ring now, and is alternating between thai elbow and knee strikes on Ryu's body. The boy has no chance to defend himself! And look at how Serpent stands perfectly still, and balances on one leg as he executes those knee strikes! This man is tough! ER: Ryu looks out on his feet, but Serpent isn't done with him yet. He has Ryu by the hair, and nails him with a vicious headbutt..two..three..four..five! (fans oooh) Finally the ref makes him let go of the hair! Ryu's forehead is cut open now, and blood is trickling down his face. This is a massacre! AO: And Serpent knows it too! He hits Ryu with a spinning backfist, then goes behind him, and executes a Dragon Suplex (fans clap). He looks over at Creed who nods his head yes. This could finally be the end for Ryu! ER: Serpent is standing in a fighting stance, and is sizing Ryu up... Ryu is slowly getting to his feet... Serpent is pivoting his right foot... Ryu is almost to his feet.. Serpent is like a statue in the middle of the ring... Ryu now to his feet... he turns towards Serpent... Serpent just nailed him in the head with a vicious Shin Kick! (fans applaud) AO: That's the move he calls the Dragon's Tail! He covers Ryu...one..two..Three! Ring Announcer: Your winner, STEEL SERPENT!!!!!!!!!! (fans applaud, and some throw streamers into the ring) ER: Ryu had some moments, but Serpent outclassed him. AO: Definitely. Wait a minute! Serpent is asking for the microphone! Steel Serpent: "Sung Kim, you are an arrogant fool. You wish to prove your wrestling ability? Prove it here. Prove it to me. Your wrestling ability is a joke. Your martial arts is like something out of a movie: all flash, no force. I will face you anywhere, and start my dynasty by defeating you." (He hands the microphone back the ref, and then leaves with Creed right behind him) ER: It looks like you might get your wish Akira. Steel Serpent just challenged Sung Kim to a match. AO: I knew he would. Steel Serpent is a tough competitor, and Sung Kim feels he has yet to face a tough opponent, so now they both can have their wish! I wonder if they will face on Ring Wars, or maybe even at Battlecade 97! ER: That would be pretty good, wouldn't it? Let's go back to ringside, as the announcer is back in the ring. Ring Announcer: This next contest is an SJPW Woman's Division matchup. Introducing first, from Nagasaki, weighing 140lbs, KY-O-KO SHI-BAT-A!!! (Kyoko Shibata jogs down the runway, slapping hands with the fans. She has short black hair and brown eyes, and is wearing a one piece red and blue wrestling outfit. She is wearing white boots) ER: I understand Kyoko is a former All Asian Woman's tag team champion? AO: Yes, she was co-holder of the tag titles twice in her career, with her sister Fujiko. Ring Announcer: Her opponent, from Kyoto, weighing 127lbs, BA-BY HOT-TA!!! (Baby Hotta walks down the runway, taking her time with the fans. He hair is dyed an unnatural yellow color, and is kept up on her head in two coke buns on the top on either side, resembling animal ears. She is wearing a matching yellow bodysuit with black leopard spots. She has kickboxing style black boots, and black kneepads) ER (chuckling to himself): Interesting harido, eh Akira? AO (laughing): yes. What is with these young people today? Gaudy clothes, ridiculous hair... ER: Cheering Super Tengu... AO: I was not going to say that! ER: No, but it fits with the rest of it There's the bell, and Hotta starts quick with a series of palm strikes to Kyoko's face and chest! Hotta whips her to the ropes... spinwheel kick! (fans clap) What is she doing? She's talking to the crowd? Why doesn't she stay on Shibata? AO: Who knows. She looks like little girl, she acts like little girl. Now she goes back to Kyoko, who catches her with a dropkick to the face! (fans clap). Shibata now whips her to the ropes, and sends her up high with a backbody drop. Hotta is crying to the referee! (fans laugh; some chant "Hotta! Hotta!") ER: Now she looks mad! I guess she doesn't like the fans laughing at her! She got right in Shibata's face, and they are slapping each other in the face! Shibata looks to be winning though, and after one really (*SMACK* is heard in the background) hard one, Hotta is in shock! (fans clap, some laugh) AO: Shibata whips her to the ropes again... drop down by Shibata.. Hotta comes off the other side.. Leap..no! Hotta caught her in a powerbomb! (fans applaud). She's wasting time talking to the crowd again! What is her problem!? ER: Shibata is getting to her feet.. Hotta still dallying with the crowd... roll-up by Shibata..one..two..kickout! (fans clap). Hotta gets up..armdrag by Shibata! Hotta up again..hiptoss by Shibata! AO: Kyoko has her on the run now! Shibata now with a snap suplex..she runs to the ropes, and drops an elbow.. cover..one..two.kickout! (fans clap) ER: Shibata has her up...Hotta blocks the suplex..reversal by Hotta. She drags Shibata to her feet..Enzugiri to the side of the head sends Shibata staggering to the ropes. Hotta on the opposite side of the ring... She starts cartwheeling...Elbowsmash by Hotta right on Shibata's jaw! (fans applaud) AO: Hotta now with a series of rapid kikcs to the leg and stomach; reminds me of Ultimo Dragon. Hotta now with another spinwheel kick, sending Shibata over the top rope to the floor! (fans clap) ER: Hotta now on the apron..she sizes up Shibata... She goes for a hurricanrana off the apron, but Shibata catches her, and powerbombs her onto the floor! (fans clap). Shibata now dropping elbows on Hotta..one..two..three..four..five..six..seven..eight..nine..ten! AO: Shibata drags Hotta to her feet..Irish whip into the railing. Hotta is crying again (some fans start chanting "Hotta! Hotta!") Why are these fans chanting for her? She's crying! ER: I understand she has some very loyal fans who cheer her on when she acts like this. Shibata charges in, but Hotta ducks, and flips her over into the crowd! Security moves in, but Hotta is too busy complaining to the fans to followup on that move! AO: Hotta is now on the apron, as Shibata is back by ringside. Hotta is up on the second rope.. Asai moonsault sends Shibata back into the railing! (fans applaud) ER: Hotta is up pretty quick on that one, and rolls Shibata back into the ring. She rolls back in herself, and starts talking to the crowd again! Is she crazy? She has Shibata beat! AO: Hotta now with the cover..one..two..kickout! (fans clap). She would have had her is she didn't waste time with the crowd! Hotta runs to the ropes...big legdrop on Shibata's chest! Hotta runs to the ropes...another legdrop! Hotta is talking to the crowd again! ER: Yeah, but she's also going to the corner... she goes to the top rope...She's jawjacking the crowd again... She leaps off the top rope, and hits the mat as Shibata rolls out of the way! She had her again, and let it slip from her fingers! AO: Shibata now going after her arm.. she stomps on it a few times, then locks on an armbar. Hotta is screaming in pain, and tears are flushing down here cheeks! What a crybaby! ER: Hotta is gettint to her feet... and pokes Shibata in the eye! The ref admonishes her for that, but that won't help Shibata! Hotta grabs her from behind with a full nelson..Dragon Suplex! (fans clap) AO: Hotta now giggling to the crowd like a little child. She grabs Shibata, and lifts her up...Tiger Driver sends Shibata into the mat head first! (fans applaud). I hope she doesn't waste time now, when she can obviously pin her here. ER: Nope. She's wasting time with the crowd again (some fan says something obscene, and Hotta blushes, then laughs like a little girl). She seems to be motioning that it's all over... She drags Shibata up, and whips her to the ropes... she catches her, and hoists her over her shoulders; is she going for an airplane spin or Torture rack? No! She just drove Shibata head first into the mat again with a Death Valley Driver (fans applaud). She's bowing to the crowd! Why doesn't she pin her? AO: She is now, after bowing to all four sides of the ring... one...two..Three! Finally! (fans applaud) Ring Announcer: Your winner, BABY HOTTA!!!!!!!! (fans applaud, and throw streamers into the ring) ER: Baby Hotta with the win, but I have to say that she could've won the match sooner, and almost lost once or twice, because she allowed herself to be distracted with the fans. AO: That is true; she acted like a spoiled little girl. However, she does have alot of talent, and she did win. Still, she needs more self discipline, and less bantering with the fans if she wants to be a champion. Rekka Sakura and Dawn Rose are much more focused than Baby Hotta, and if she's not careful, she will lose the either one of them when it comes time to crown a woman's champion! ER: Speaking of Rekka Sakura, she's up next. This match was scheduled for next week, but with the recent no-shows, this match was set up. This is a rematch from After Dark, isn't it? AO: Yes it is Eddie-san. Rekka and Sumie had an excellant match, which we highlighted on After Dark. This rematch should be a good one! Ring Announcer: This next contest is a rematch, set for one fall, 30 minute time limit. Introducing first, from Tokyo, weighing 135lbs, SU-MIE O-DA-KA!!! (Sumie Odaka heads down the runway. She has short black hair, and is wearing a on piece yellow wrestling outfit, and white boots.) ER: She looks cute. AO: I'll tell her you said that later. Ring Announcer: Her opponent, also from Tokyo, weighing 125lbs, "BURNING CHERRY BLOSSOM" REK-KA SA-KU-RA!!!!!!! (As "Give A Reason" by Megumi Hayabashira plays, Rekka Sakura runs down the runway to the ring. She has long black hair with red highlights, and black eyes. She is wearing a black warmup jacket with "Rekka" scrawled on the back in red. She has on black tights with red and yellow fringes, black boots with red laces, and black elbow/kneepads. When she takes her jacket off, you can see a tattoo of Vejita on her right shoulder) ER: So you think she's one of the top contendors for the woman's title, eh? AO: Yes. Her and Dawn Rose both showed alot of promise when I reviewed their matches before After Dark. They are much more focused than Baby Hotta. ER: Of course, Hotta could simply be pretending to be unfocused, just to take us all of guard. AO: We'll see. There's the bell, and Sumie starts strong, catching Rekka with a series of kicks to her left leg and body! Sumie now whips her to the ropes, and catches her with a flying elbowsmash! ER: Sumie grabs Rekka again, and hiptosses her down. She drops the elbow..cover..one..tw-kickout. Sumie has her up again.. she whips her to the ropes... Dropkick sends Rekka through the ropes to the floor. She's really taking it to her! AO: Sumie comes off the ropes on the opposite side...she jumps up to the top rope...somersault plancha to the floor! (fans applaud) Ref: One! ER: Both of them are slowly trying to get up... Ref: Two! AO: Sumie to her feet first... Ref: Three! ER: Sumie tries to whip Rekka to the railing, but she reverses it... Ref: Four! AO: Rekka takes Sumie down with a russian legsweep on the floor (fans clap)... Ref: Five! ER: Rekka rolls Sumie in, then follows her in. Rekka now sith a slingshot suplex... She drags her to her feet again and... Belly to belly suplex. Cover..one..two..kickout! (fans clap) AO: Rekka going to the top rope... She waits as Sumie gets up... Missle Dropkick! (fans clap) Cover..one..two..thr-kickout! (fans clap) ER: Rekka has Sumie up again... she's going for another suplex.. Sumie drops back down, and counters with a small package! Cover..one..two..t-kickout! (fans clap) AO: Both of them up now... Sumie now chopping Rekka across the chest... Sumie goes for a kick, but Rekka catches her leg... Sumie nails Rekka with an enzuigiri (fans clap) ER: Sumie with a standing moonsault of Rekka..Cover..one..kickout! Sumie runs to the ropes...Somersault legdrop. She runs to the ropes again... springboard back elbowdrop! (fans clap) Cover.. one..two..thr-kickout! (fans clap; some start chanting "Rekka! Rekka!) AO: Sumie drags Rekka to her feet, and whips her to the ropes... Rekka ducks the clothesline, and grabs Sumie from behind... German Suplex! (fans clap) Rekka has turned this around with one move! ER: Rekka drags Sumie to her feet... and whips he to the corner. She follows in with a clothesline. Rekka sits Sumie on the top rope... Superplex! (fans applaud) Cover..one..two..thr-kickout! AO: Rekka now going to the top rope... Skytwister Press! (fans applaud). Rekka now with a Boston Crab! Why didn't she go for the cover? ER: I don't know, but Sumie's all ready on the ropes, so Rekka has to break the hold. Rekka has Sumie up, and locks on a sleeper.. She doesn't have it locked on well, cause Sumie just dropped down, and nailed her with a jawbreaker! (fans applaud) AO: Why did she go for the submission? She had her beat there! Sumie now getting to her feet, and drops the leg on Rekka. Cover..one..two..kickout! (fans clap) ER: Sumie now whips Rekka to the corner, and follows in with a handspring elbow (fans applaud). Sumie just throws Rekka to the mat, and starts heading for the top rope. AO: I think that was a big mistake, as Rekka is on her feet! She gets under Sumie, grabs her wrists, and falls backward with an Ocean Cyclone Suplex! (fans applaud) She bridges up into a cover... one..two..three!!!! (fans applaud again) Ring Announcer: Your winner, "BURNING CHERRY BLOSSOM" REKKA SAKURA!!! (fans applaud, and throw streamers into the ring) ER: That was a good debut by Sakura. AO: I told you she was good. I don't know why she went for submission holds when she had Sumie beaten, but she was able to turn it around again, and get the victory! ER: Very true. Let's go back to ringside for our next match. Ring Announcer: This next contest is one fall, 30 minute time limit. Introducing first, from Tokyo, weighing 235lbs, TSU-TO-MU KO-BAY-ASH-I!!!! (Tsutomu Kobayashi heads to the ring. He is tall and lanky, and looks like a younger version of his father) ER: This is Tsutomu's first appearance on Ring Wars. He's a second generation wrestler, isn't he? AO: Yes. His father, Jumbo Kobayashi, is one of the japanese legends. He was the All Asian champion 4 times, and retired a few years ago. His son is much like him, only younger. ER: He's only a teenager, isn't he? AO: Yes, he's 18. Other than Lance Smith, he's the youngest wrestler in SJPW! Ring Announcer: His opponent, from Nogoya, weighing 190lbs, JET JAG-UAR!!!! (As "More Human than Human" by White Zombie plays over the loudspeakers, Jet Jaguar comes running to the ring. He is wearing a full length silver outfit, with a balck sash. On the left leg it says "Jet Jaguar", and the right side is writing in japanese. He has on black boots, and kickboxing style shin guards. He has black and silver latex armbands that go from his wrists to just in front of his elbows. He is wearing a silver mask with black extensions forming around the sides, and ending as points on either side of his head, forming a 'V'. Black hair flows out from under the back of his mask. He slides under the ropes, and goes to the opposite corner from his opponent) ER: Know anything about this guy? AO: Other than he took his name from a robot in a Godzilla movie, no. ER: Hoo-kay. There's the bell. Both men lock up, and Tsutomu knees Jaguar in the gut, then slams an elbow into the back of his head as he doubles over. He whips Jaguar to the ropes... Clothesline taks Jaguar down. AO: Tsutomu drops the leg across his chest... two... three... four! Cover..one..two..th-kickout! (fans clap) Tsutomu drags him to his feet, and whips him to the ropes... leapfrog by Tsutomu... Dropkick by Jaguar takes him down. ER: Jaguar not wasting anytime, as he moves in on Kobayashi, and nails him with a series of chops. Jaguar whips him to the corner, follows him, and monkey flips him out of the corner! (fans clap) AO: Both men up, and Jaguar catches him with a jumping side kick. Tsutomu bounces off the ropes.. Spinning backbreaker by Jaguar! (fans clap) Jaguar runs to the ropes... Springboard into a diving headbutt! (fans clap) Cover..one..two..thr-kickout! ER: Jaguar drags Kobayashi to his feet, and whips him to the ropes... he goes for another spinning backbreaker, but Tsutomu counters with flying headscissors! (fans clap) Jaguar slides out of the ring! AO: Tsutomu goes to the top rope... Double Axe Handle to the floor onto Jaguar! (fans oooh) Jaguar hits the railing! Tsutomu now with a series of chops... He whips Jaguar into the railing! ER: Kobayashi with a knee to Jaguar's gut... he goes to whip him into the railing again, but Jagaur reverses! Jaguar runs in, and takes Kobayashi down with some headscissors! (fans clap) AO: Jaguar rolls him in the ring, and goes to the top rope... He measures Tsutomu... Sunset Flip off the top rope! He gets him over..one..two..thr-kickout! ER: Both men up, and Jaguar takes him down with a spinwheel kick. He drags him up...German Suplex!..Two..Three..Four..Five..Six! (fans applaud) Covcer..one..two..thr-kickout! AO: Jaguar has really taken it to Kobayashi, but he is hard to beat, just like his father. Jaguar whips him to the ropes again... he goes for a frankensteiner, but Kobayashi counters with a powerbomb! (fans clap) ER: Tsutomu drags him up..snap suplex. He sets him up; it looks like he's going for another powerbomb..Jaguar countered with a hurricanrana (fans applaud) AO: Jaguar runs to the ropes..Asai moonsault takes Kobayashi down. He drags him to his feet...He lifts him up high...and drops him down into a Tombstone! (fans applaud). Everyone seems to be using Tiger Kikuchi's move now! ER: Jaguar sense victory now! He sets Tsutomu up...powerbomb! This could be it! He's going to the tope rope... he backflips off the top rope, twists, and hits Tsutomu with a legdrop! (fans applaud)! That's the move he calls the Jaguar Special! He's going for the cover..one..two..THREE!!!! (fans applaud) Ring Announcer: Your winner, JET JAGUAR!!!!! (the fans applaud, and throw streamers into the ring. Jet Jaguar helps Tsutomu up, and shakes his hand, then raises both their arms in victory) ER: A sign of sportsmanship by Jaguar. This was a tough match for both men, but Jaguar pulled it off in the end. AO: His finishing move was very impressive. I think this man is almost as good as Super Tengu or Takao Nakami. ER: Really? That's high praise coming from you. AO: I know talent when I see it, and Jet Jaguar has it. He caters to the fans too much, but nobody is perfect. ER: I see. Well, we have 3 matches to go. The round two cruiserweight tournament, then our 8 man tag main event!
