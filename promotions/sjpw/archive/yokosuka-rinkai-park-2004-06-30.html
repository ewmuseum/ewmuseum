SJPW House Show — Yokosuka Rinkai Park (6/30/04)

Jul 1, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
June 30th, 2004 Yokosuka Rinkai Park: 489 fans 1) "Firefly" Horo Atashi def Se-hong Kwan (8:37) after a Frog Splash. 2) Frankie "The Thumper" Douglas & Doug Thomas def Masashi "Lightning" Lanther & Jiraiya (12:55) after a spiked powerbomb on Lanther. 3) "Dragon Fury" Minobu Tetsuhara & Junya Toroyama went to a 20:00 draw 4) Gregor Davinsky, Wolf Jager & Sergei Baranov def Dragon Mask, Jet Jaguar III & Toramusha (17:29) after Jager made Jaguar III submit to the Strangle Hold Beta. 5) Tasogare no Prince & Tsunami def "Ecchi Kid" Kentaro "H-Bomb" Kawasaki & "Living Legend" Tommy Fury (18:04) after TnP pinned Kawasaki following a Destiny Driver. - The veteran Kwan proved no match for the young "Firefly", falling to a spectacular Frog Splash in a short encounter. - The Maulers used their size and strength advantage to batter down Team Raiden, securing the win after a spike Powerbomb on the veteran Lanther. - Tetsuhara faced Toroyama, but this was not part of the Five Fight Series that Kikuchi demanded. Both men fought a very stiff match, and even though Toroyama was the stronger of the two, Tetsuhara did no back down one inch. Toroyama dominated things early on, but Tetsuhara was close to beating him near the end, scoring seven near falls before the time limit ran out. - In a rematch from the 28th, The masked men of Team Tiger again faced the EuroPack (A nickname given them by "smart marks" on the internet). Before the match began, Jager swore he'd make Jaguar III tap out for pinning him two days earlier, and spent most of the match working his arm. Baranov & Dragon Mask hit some high impact moves on each other, while Davinsky actually out wrestled Toramusha. In the end, Jager kept his word, making Jaguar III tap out to a Strangle Hold Beta. - In his first appearance since before Zenmen Sensou, Tasogare no Prince again proved why he feels he should be a contender for the new GPH title, pinning Kawasaki in a thrilling tag match. Kawasaki was as bored as ever coming to the ring, but became very animated when squaring off with TnP in the ring. Both men showed some great chain wrestling, maneuvering and out maneuvering each other. Fury also showed his impressive ring skills, coming close to pinning Tsunami on three occasions. In the end though, TnP showed Destiny was on his side, as he pinned Kawasaki following his Destiny Driver. Fans were buzzing after the event about TnP's beating Kawasaki twice now. While many feel Kawasaki's title reign may be short at the hands of TnP, a growing number of fans are starting to think the "Super Cultural Shock Trooper" is going to hold that title for awhile yet...
