SJPW Cards

Wrestlefest

21 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Wrestlefest Continues...] [The camera cuts back to the ring, showing Kenji Ishida & Shinji Usami entering the ring...] ER: It seems the Corporate Dragons are all ready in the ring. So far Chiimu Corporate is 1-1 for the evening. AO: And it will be 1-2 after Tetsuo and his mystery partner defeat these two thugs! ER: There is alot riding on this. If Tetsuo can win, he gets a non-title shot at Takada. If he wins that one, he gets a title shot for his belt! AO: Tetsuo can do it. I just wonder who his partner is... ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Matsumoto/TBA vs Corporate Dragons /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [The camera cuts to Nishi Toju...] Nishi: This next contest is a tag team challenge! In the ring at this time, representing CHIIMU CORPORATE [crowd boos], at a combined weight of 490 pounds....KEN-JI ISH-I-DA & SHIN-JI U-SA-MI...THE CORPORATE DRAGONS!!!! ["Painkiller" by Angra blares over the PA as Ishida & Usami stand up in opposite corners, and raise their hands in the air. The crowd boos loudly, and they look down at the fans with disgust on their faces...] Nishi: Their opponents... ["Get Wild" by TM Network replaces "Painkiller" on the PA] Nishi: First, from Kobe, weighing in at 253 pounds...."IRON MAN" TET-SU-O MAT-SU-MO-TO!!! [Tetsuo comes out to thunderous applause from the crowd. He walks slowly to the ring, slapping hands with his many fans. He never takes his eyes off his opponents, and enters the ring warily. He stands over in one corner, and takes up a defensive posture...] ER: Where is his partner? AO: SHH! Nishi isn't finished with the introductions! Nishi: His tag team partner...from Osaka, and weighing in at 235 pounds.... ["Phaedra" by Tangerine Dream replaces "Get Wild", and the crowd goes nuts..] ER: It can't be! AO: But it is! The LAST person anyone would expect him to team with! Nishi: He is a former All-Asian & Cruiserweight champion....SHIN-JI HA-MA-DA!!! [The crowd erupts with thunderous applause as Hamada steps out from the back. He soaks in the roar of the crowd, and a smile coming over his face, strides to the ring. He enters warily, keeping an eye on the Dragons. He walks over to Matsumoto, and the two men stare unemotionally at each other. The crowd suddenly goes silent, not sure how these two former enemies will react to each other. Slowly, both men bow to each other. The crowd applauds again, and Matsumoto pats Hamada on the back, then motions to Goto that he will start for his team...] ER: This is amazing! These two men were once the worst of enemies! I'd never expected to see them team together ever! AO: Ah, but their fathers were once close friends. And of course, they have one more thing in common... ER: Which is? AO: Rekka Sakura. Tetsuo's sister, and Shinji's beloved. I'm sure she had a hand in bringing these two former rivals together in commong cause.... [Ishida steps forward for his team, and Goto gives him and Matsumoto final instructions, then signals for the bell. The two men circle each other, then lock up. Ishida maneuvers behind Tetsuo, and twists over into a takedown. Tetsuo breaks free, and both men get to their feet. They lock up again, and Ishida applies a front facelock. Raising his arm above his head, he lands three stiff forearm shots to Tetsuo's back, then applies pressure on the facelock. Tetsuo tries to get out of the hold, but Ishida proves to be stronger than him, and starts to drive his knee up into Tetsuo's face! The crowd "ooohs" as Ishida breaks the hold, and Tetsuo falls backwards to the mat, blood seeping from his nose. Ishida flexes his muscles, and dares Tetsuo to come at him. Tetsuo crouches low, and dives at Ishida, taking him off his feet. He straddles his opponents, and starts to pummel him with stiff punches to the side of his head. The crowd applauds, then boo as Goto makes him break. Tetsuo backs off, and the camera shows Ishida bleeding from his nose. Ishida gets to his feet, his face a mask of rage. He charges Tetsuo, who catches him, and hits an Exploder suplex. Ishida lands hard on the mat, and Tetsuo runs towards him, landing a corkscrew elbowdrop. Usami enters the ring, and as Tetsuo makes it to his feet, nails him from behind with an enzugiri! The crowd "ooohs" as Tetsuo goes down, and Ishida gets to his feet. The Dragons start to put the boots to Tetsuo, and as Goto tries to get them off him, Hamada enters the ring, and nails Ishida with a running lariat that sends him into the corner. Usami attempts a spinwheel kick, but Hamada catches the leg, takes Usami off his feet, and drops his elbow on the inner thigh of his right leg. Goto makes Hamada go back to his corner, and Usami rolls out of the ring....] ER: So far, the Dragons have show superior team work, but Hamada & Matsumoto at least are getting along. AO: Their differences ended some months ago, so I'm sure they will work well together. [Tetsuo slowly gets to his feet, and Ishida bolts out of the corner, nailing him with a Yakuza kick to the face. Tetsuo goes down hard, and Ishida legdrops him across the chest. Ishida rises to his feet, dragging Tetsuo up with him. He drags him to his corner, and tags in Usami. Ishida hoists Tetsuo up, then drops down into a side suplex, then Usami lands on him with a senton splash. Usami makes a cover, and Hamada enters the ring, but Tetsuo kicks out at one. Usami gets to his feet, and stands Tetsuo up. He hiptosses him to the mat, then runs to the ropes, and hits a quebrada. He makes another cover, and as Hamada enters the ring, Tetsuo kicks out at one and a half. Usami drags Tetsuo to his feet, and whips him into the corner. He charges in behind him, and hits a dropkick to his face. Tetsuo sags in the corner, and Usami tags in Ishida. As Ishida enters the ring, Usami whips him to the ropes, and takes him down with drop toehold. Ishida goes to the ropes, and legdrops the back of his head! Tetsuo rolls around the mat in pain, and Ishida kicks him in the ribs. He drags Tetsuo to his feet, and headbutts him. Tetsuo staggers back, and Ishida lands a vicious chop across his chest. The crowd "Whooos" loudly, and Tetsup fires back with a chop of his own! The crowd "Whooos" again, and Tetsuo kicks Ishida in the gut, then hits an Ace Crusher! The crowd goes wild, and Hamada screams for Tetsuo to make a tag! Both men lay dazed on the mat, and Goto starts a count on them....] ER: Tetsuo has taken a beating out there, but he had enough in him to take Ishida down with that Ace Crusher! AO: If only he could have covered, this match would be over! [Tetsuo starts to move, and slowly crawls to Ishida. He drapes his arm over him, and Goto stops his count, and drops for the pinfall. He makes it to two before Ishida kicks out, and the crowd "awwwws". Ishida sits up, and shakes his head, while Tetsuo rolls to the ropes. Ishida starts to get to his feet, while Hamada reaches out to Tetsuo to make a tag. Tetsuo lays on the mat, unmoving, and the crowd starts to chant his name. Ishida makes it to his feet, and rushes towards Tetsuo, who raises his hand up, and tags Hamada! The crowd goes wild as Hamada ducks low as Ishida comes at him, drives his shoulder into Ishida's gut, then slingshots over the top rope, taking Ishida over into a sunset flip! Goto drops to count, but Usami breaks up the count at two. Both me roll to their feet, and Usami attempts an enzugiri, but Hamada ducks, and Usami crashes to the mat. Hamada gets to his feet, and Ishida slams into him with a shoulderblock! Hamada falls back to the ropes, bounces off, and Ishida catches him, lifts him up, spins around, and drives him to the mat with a spinebuster! The crowd "ooohs" as Hamada lays on the mat. Ishida flips Hamada over, and slaps on a camel clutch, and Usami runs to the ropes, and comes off with a baseball slide dropkick to Hamada's face! The crowd "oooohs" as Ishida breaks the camel clutch. Usami goes back to his corner, and Ishida makes the tag. Usami goes to the top rope, and Ishida grabs him, and rocket launches him off the top rope! Usami lands hard on top of Hamada, and Goto drops to count, but Hamada kicks out with authority at one! Hamada gets to his feet, and as Usami rises, slams into him with a football tackle! Usami flies into the ropes, and falls through them to the floor. Ishida charges Hamada, who catches him with a powerslam! Ishida rolls to the ring apron, and Hamada flexes for the crowd....] ER: Hamada really took it to the Dragons! They tried to take him out quickly, but he proved too much for them! AO: Hamada may be a cruiserweight, but he is incredibly strong! [Usami gets to his feet, and slides into the ring. Hamada attempts an elbowdrop, but Usami rolls out of the way, Usami kip-ups to his feet, and runs to the ropes. Hamada makes it to his feet, and Usami springboards off the ropes, and takes Hamada over with an armdrag. Hamada rolls to his feet, and Usami nails him with a flying forearm smash. Hamada backs to the ropes, and Usami attempts an irish whip, but Hamada reverses. Usami comes off the ropes, and attempts a high crossbody block, but Hamada catches him! The crowd "oooohs" as Hamada holds Usami in his arms, then hits a fallaway slam. Usami lays on the mat in pain. Hamada sits Usami up, and applies a surfboard, placing his knee into Usami's back. Usami's face contorts in pain, and Ishida enters the ring, and kicks Hamada in the back of the head. Hamada releases the hold, and Ishida starts to put the boots to him. Tetsuo enters the ring, and nails Ishida from behind with a running lariat! Ishida staggers forward to the ropes, and Tetsuo grabs him from behind in a full nelson, then falls back into a release dragon suplex! Tetsuo is forced back to his corner by Goto, and Ishida rolls out of the ring. Usami gets to his feet, and sets himself as Hamada rises. He runs towards him, and takes him over with a rana. Hamada rolls to his feet and charges, but Usami takes him over with a back bodydrop. Hamada lands hard, and Usami hits a somersault legdrop onto his chest. Usami makes a cover, but Tetsuo breaks the count at one. Usami stands Hamada up, and takes him over with a monkey flip. Both men get to their feet, and Usami leaps at Hamada, attempting a hurricanrana, but Hamada counters with a powerbomb! Usami sags on the mat, and Hamada grabs his leg, and turns him over into a single leg boston crab. Usami tries to grab the ropes, but Hamada applies pressure, and he hesitates. Ishida enters the ring, and kicks Hamada in the head, and he releases the hold. Tetsuo enters the ring, and he and Ishida start to brawl. Goto attempts to seperate them, while Hamada & Usami start to get to their feet...] ER: Hamada keeps getting the advantage, but Ishida keeps breaking his momentum! AO: And Tetsuo has had enough of his interference! [Goto tries to seperate Ishida & Matsumoto, but they keep rolling around, fighting, while Hamada & Usami start to exchange blows in the center of the ring. Goto finally gets Tetsuo back to his corner, and Ishida takes this opportunity to grab Hamada from behind, and hit a belly to back suplex. He drags Hamada to his feet, and whips him to the ropes. Hamada comes off the ropes, and the Dragons hit the Dragon Death Drop! Goto orders Ishida to his corner, and when he gets there, Usami tags him in. Ishida enters the ring, and they stand Hamada up, then hit the Dragon Strike on him! Usami goes to his corner, and Ishida makes a cover. Tetsuo enters the ring, but Hamada kicks out with authority at two and a half! The crowd goes wild as Hamada rises to his feet, his face a mask of rage! Ishida starts to pummel his face, but Hamada totally ignores the blows! Ishida backs off in fear, and Hamada grabs Ishida by the throat, and chokeslams him to the mat! The crowd goes wild, and Hamada staggers over to his corner, and tags Tetsuo! The crowd goes wild as Tetsuo enters the ring, and Usami enters. He charges Tetsuo, who catches him with a Shouda. Usami staggers back, and Tetsuo hits a jumping DDT on him! Tetsuo turns his attention to Ishida. Ishida attempts a Yakuza kick, but Tetsuo catches the leg, and hits a dragonscrew legwhip. He drags Ishida to his feet, hoists him over his shoulder, and sits him on the top rope. He climbs up with him, and hits The Depth Charge Fisherman's Buster! The crowd goes wild, and Usami gets to his feet, and nails Tetsuo from behind with a forearm smash. Hamada enters the ring, and he grabs Usami, and whips him to the ropes. He catches Usami coming off the ropes, and presses him over his head, then drops him down into a tombstone piledriver! The crowd goes wild, and Hamada points at Ishida, and Tetsuo nods. He drags the dazed Ishida to his feet, and slaps on a full nelson. He starts to snap back, releases Ishida, spins him inverted, and drops down into a sit out powerbomb! The crowd rises to it's feet as Goto drops down, and makes the three count!] ER: He did it! Tetsuo won! AO: As if it was ever in doubt... Nishi: The winners of this match....SHINJI HAMADA & TETSUO MATSUMOTO!!!! [The crowd starts to throw streamers in the ring. Matsumoto gets to his feet, and he looks at Hamada. They stand face to face, and Tetsuo offers Hamada his hand. Hamada looks down at his hand for a moment, then shakes it! The crowd erupts into cheers again, and more streamers fly into the ring...] ER: What a victory for Hamada & Matsumoto! Now Tetsuo goes on to face Takada! AO: And Chiimu Corporate are 1-2 for tonight! [Hamada & Matsumoto exit the ring, and head up the runway, waving at the fans. Ishida & Usami get to their feet, and help each other out of the ring, the fans surprisingly applauding their effort in the match...] ER: our next match was originally a tag team match, but due to unforseen events, it's now a singles match. PJW Grand Prix Senshuken The Banshee will face the man he defeated in the first round of the mini-tournament to crown that championship, Chandrasekhar Yoshida. AO: Yoshida was once the epitome of honor. Now, his so-called "honor" makes The Yakuza look like heroic samurai! ER: Akira... AO: I am not saying that as an insult to The Yakuza. They have their own code of honor. Yoshida's honor is supposedly that of the samurai, yet in many ways, it is not. I was once proud to know Yoshida, and now I am ashamed to have once endorsed him. His cousin Shigeru is still in seclusion over his shame... ER: Jonnie Adonis is backstage, waiting to get a few words with The Banshee... [The scene switches from ringside to a corridor backstage in the Tokyo Dome where Jonnie Adonis can be seen, standing with a microphone in one hand.] JA: You can feel the excitement backstage, folks, as Wrestlefest has really captured the imaginations of some of SJPW's finest. And speaking of which, here comes the PJW Grand Prix Champion! [The camera turns to catch The Banshee exiting a locker room with Jessica Starbird beside him. The two look up in surprise as Jonnie rushes over beside him.] JA: Banshee, can you give us a few words? [The Banshee grins as he stands with his hands on his hips.] BA: All right, Jonnie, although I didn't know we were scheduled for an interview. JA: Call this an impromptu session! And let me say that it's always an honor to meet you, Ms. Starbird! [Jessica Starbird nods her head and smiles as The Banshee speaks into the microphone.] BA: Well, all I can say is that Yoshida-san has heard The Banshee's Wail so he knows what to expect. When I step into the ring, I am determined to uphold the highest ideals of Chiimu Tora. JA: Will Tiger Kikuchi's recent injury have any effect on your mindset? [The Banshee's expression grows grim and serious.] BA: Kikuchi-kun represents everything that is good and fine about SJPW. For Dean to deliberately try and destroy that makes my blood boil and I look forward to the day when Takizawa-san teaches him a lesson in manners. But if you'll excuse me, Jonnie, Jessica and I would like to go over some last minute strategy. [The Banshee and Jessica Starbird walk away as Jonnie Adonis turns back to the camera.] JA: Well with the world's most highly-ranked women's wrestler helping him with tactics, The Banshee certainly looks like he's ready for action. Let's go back to the ring! ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / The Banshee vs Chandrasekhar Yoshida /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [The camera cuts to Nishi Toju in the ring...] Nishi: This next contest is one fall, with a thirty minute time limit. Introducing... (Suddenly, the lights in the arena dim. For a long moment, there is silence, only broken by the fans whistling and gasping in anticipation. Then, bright silver Japanese symbols representing the fifth element, the element of the void, highlight aisle and the ring. Over the PA comes a soft wailing sound, as if angels chanting, accompanied by slow, ancient flute and harp tunes. Then, with a loud bang, a sizzling lightning bolt comes crackling down within the arena, and the curtains part. A man steps through, a familiar man yet his appearance is unlike ever before. His long black hair is accented with silver highlights, and his face is painted with black and night blue accents surrounded by silver stars and sparkles. He is wearing a night blue kimono with the image of an oriental golden dragon on the front and twelve silver stars on the back in a circular pattern around a golden orchid. Around his waist is the Golden Orchid tsuihoonin title belt. His feet are in heavy black leather riding boots, and both of his wrists are heavily bandaged. For the first time since he made his appearance in professional wrestling, he is seen without the katana and wakizashi of his ancestors. This man is Chandrasekhar Yoshida) ER: Yoshida not wasting any time with his entrance! AO: The man's ego grows larger every day! (The boos are loud and almost deafening, yet the young warrior's gaze is fixed upon the ring, his concentration unbroken. His servant, Susan K'uang-yin, is nowhere to be seen. Yoshida slowly marches towards the ring, the dim arena lights giving the dark-and-silver clad samurai a very eerie and gloomy appearance. Yoshida enters the ring and asks for the microphone. Nishi hands it to him, and Yoshida calmly waits until the crowd has relented their boos) Yoshida: "Less than a moon ago, as I am sure you can all recall, the unthinkable has happened and I, the Golden Orchid Tsuihoonin champion, the Nobori Nin of the proud Yoshida bloodline, a worthy samurai who has never lost a duel before, suffered defeat at the hands of a masked gaijin." (Loud cheers as Yoshida mentions the Banshee! Team Tiger chants erupt throughout the arena, and Yoshida, an emotionless stare in his cold eyes, once again needs to wait for the fans to quiet down before continuing) Yoshida: "I withdrew to the temples of my ancestors, searching for answers. Did not the strength of a thousand great samurai flood through my veins? Am I now the successor of one of the proudest blood lines Japans? Is my heritage not pure and untainted, unlike the gaijin who defeated me? Is this not why I have been called 'The Ghost' in honor of my fathers and my fathers' fathers? Something went horribly wrong. But what?" (Yoshida pauses for a moment and chews on his bottom lip) Yoshida: "Not allowing my faith to sag, I traveled to the ruins of my family estate, to the battlefields where centuries ago my ancestors proved themselves in battle, consulted the wisest men and the most ancient writings about my blood. And finally, four nights ago, when the moon was covered by heavy storm clouds and the Gods of thunder relentlessly bombarded the land with bolts of lightning and thunder, I found the answer. And it was an answer which would change my life and my blood forever." (The fans have become quiet) "I have lived a lie. For twenty-one years, I have lived a lie. My ancestor, the great samurai and powerful warrior Shoju Yoshida, was but a simple peasant. An ashigaru. A rice farmer. He used forgery and deception to alter our family history but the truth is that the Yoshidas have been nothing more than rice farmers." ER: What? AO: Can this be true? (Silence) "Rice farmers. And I thought the strength of a thousand samurai was flowing through my veins, but it is a mere corrupt rice farmer and his breathen." (Yoshida sinks against one of the turnbuckles, then sits down on the mat and leans against the ringpost, his face bowed down in humbled shame) "So... what to do? All this was wrong? A lie? For nothing? What was the right way? What would a samurai of pride and honor do? Would he take the easy way? Abandon his name and hide behind a mask for the rest of his life like the Team Tiger bakas?" (Some boos can be heard again. Yoshida looks up, an angered yet determined expression in his eyes) "No. I cannot change who I am, but I can change who I will become. I am a Yoshida. If my ancestors were honorless, then I will be the first Yoshida to live the life of a samurai and to honor the traditions of bushido." (Yoshida stands back up and extends his arms, showing his heavily bandaged wrists to the camera) "I have cut my flesh and burned my blood. I have cleansed myself and purified my bloodline. I am no more 'The Ghost' who honors his family line; I am the progenitor of my own family line. Never again shall the Yoshida bloodline be ridiculed as corrupt farmers, never again shall the Yoshidas lose to masked gaijin. For I am not Chandrasekhar Yoshida anymore, the Nobori Nin of the Yoshida. For now I am the one and the many, the originator and the creator. No longer am I the son of my father, now I am the ancestor. From this day forward I shall be known as... SHIRYO NO YOSHIDA!" (Loud, uneasy crowd pop. Yoshida crosses his fists in grim determination) "The Yoshida shall be the most dominant, the most honorable and the proudest bloodline, and the Golden Orchid shall be our symbol. I am the ancestor, and the child formerly known as Susan K'uang-yin has been sent on a quest to prove herself worthy of the Yoshida heritage. Should she succeed, she will return to my side as my daughter and student. We are few, but we are strong, and we will grow. Yes, I have lived a mistake. But a fool is not the man who commits a mistake, a fool is the man who does not realize his mistakes." "Banshee, you have lived the same mistake. I believed my bloodline would give me strength. You believe your mask gives you strength and purpose. We are both wrong. Yet unlike myself, you fail to realize. And this will be your downfall. Your downfall at my hands, Banshee. At the hands of the new Yoshida." (With that, another loud lightning bolt fills the arena, and the mystical music begins again. The lights now dim completely, and to a loud crowd pop as Yoshida hands the microphone back to Nishi...) Nishi: And his opponent, representing CHIIMU TORA [crowd cheers], accompanied to the ring by Jessica Starbird, he hails from Los Angeles, California, and weighs in at 219 pounds, he is the PJW GRAND PRIX SENSHUKEN....THE BANSHEE!!!! ["Fly" by SUGAR RAY begins playing over the P.A. system as a pair of figures step through the entrance curtains greeted by a loud cheer from the crowd. Twin pillars of green flame explode on either side of them as The Banshee and Jessica Starbird both smile and wave at the fans. Around The Banshee's waist is the glittering PJW Grand Prix Championship belt and paper streamers descend around him into the aisle as he jogs down to the ring with Starbird by his side. Hopping up onto the ring apron, he vaults over the top rope as Starbird steps over to his corner and the theme music fades away.] ER: The Banshee looks ready for action, and you can see he isn't to happy with Yoshida's tirade before he came out! AO: No, he definitely looks upset... [Banshee & Yoshida are in each other's faces, talking tensely. Yoshida smiles at Banshee, who shoves him. The crowd "ooohs" as Yoshida shoves Banshee back, and Banshee kicks Yoshida in the gut, scoops him up, then slams him to the mat! Banshee attempts an elbowdrop, but Yoshida rolls out of the way, and gets to his feet. Banshee rises, and charges, but Yoshida stops him short with a standing dropkick. Banshee staggers back a step, and Yoshida takes him off his with with a legsweep. The back of Banshee's head hits the mat hard, and Yoshida gets to his feet, then stomps on Banshee's chest. Banshee folds up in pain, and Yoshida runs to the ropes, and comes off with a baseball slide dropkick to the side of Banshee's head...] ER: Banshee allowed his temper to get the better of him, and Yoshida is out-wrestling him! AO: He needs to focus! Otherwise he will lose! [Yoshida gets to his feet, and drags Banshee up. He whips him to the ropes, kicking him in the gut as he comes off. Banshee doubles over, and Yoshida steps back, then runs forward, sending Banshee to the mat with a high kneelift. Yoshida snaps an elbowdrop onto Banshee's chest, then makes a quick cover. Botan drops to count, but Banshee kicks out at one and a half. Yoshida runs to the ropes as Banshee rises, and lands a vicious axe kick to his shoulder! Banshee collapses to the mat, clutching his shoulder, and Jessica yells out encouragement to him...] ER: Banshee has had little to no offense so far. AO: This doesn't look good for him! [Yoshida drags Banshee to his feet, and sends him crashing into the corner turnbuckle with an Irish whip. He then charges in right afterward to hammer Banshee with a series of rapid-fire Muy Thai kicks. Grabbing Banshee's arm, Yoshida sends him across the ring with another Irish whip, charging right after him. But instead of crashing into the opposite corner, The Banshee vaults upward and performs a handstand on top of the turnbuckle, dropping his legs around Yoshida's head as he comes rushing in to perform a headscissors takedown that flips Yoshida over onto the top of his skull! As the crowd cheers, Banshee then clambers up onto the same turnbuckle, waiting for a dazed Yoshida to struggle to his feet. As Yoshida stands upright, Banshee leaps off the top rope and executes a hurricanrana that again dumps Yoshida onto the top of his head!] ER: Wow! What a comeback by Banshee! AO: This is why he is the Grand Prix Senshuken! [Suddenly, Hadi Moditan comes out from the back. He walks down the runway to ringside, the crowd booing him loudly. Inside the ring, Banshee drags Yoshida to his feet, and hits a fisherman's suplex. Botan drops to count, but Yoshida is able to kick out at two. The crowd applauds, and Banshee gets to his feet, and hits a standing moonsault. He covers again, but once more, Yoshida kicks out at two. Banshee drags Yoshida to his feet, and attempts an irish whip, but Yoshida reverses. Yoshida attempts a back bodydrop, but Banshee flips over his back, and lands on his feet. Yoshida turns around, and Banshee attempts a jumping side kick, but Yoshida ducks under it, and rolls to the ropes. Banshee turns, and Yoshida comes at him with spinwheel kick of his own, but Banshee ducks it. He rolls behind Yoshida, who turns, and Banshee attempts a cross bodyblock, but Yoshida counters with a standing dropkick that catches Banshee in the gut! The crowd "ooohs" as Banshee drops to the mat hard, clutching his chest....] ER: What a series of moves! Both men know each other pretty well, don't they? AO: It's uncanny how even they are matched. [Hadi Moditan applauds Yoshida, urging him to hurt Banshee even more. Yoshida stands Banshee up, and staggers him backward with a series of knife-edge chops. Bouncing off the ropes, Yoshida charges him with a flying cross body block but Banshee catches him, backs up and falls backward to hurl Yoshida over the top rope and out of the ring with a fallaway slam. Banshee gets to his feet, and immediately runs to the ropes on the opposite side, rebound back and jump up onto the top rope to executes a springboard corkscrew plancha down onto Yoshida! The crowd "oooohs" loudly, then applauds the suicidal move by Banshee! Botan starts a count on both men, and Moditan & Starbird urge their favorites to get up...] ER: Banshee pulling out all the stops now! And what is Moditan doing here? AO: Maybe he wants to manage Yoshida?
