SJPW House Show — Chiimu Tora Dojo (8/11/04)

Aug 14, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wednesday, August 11th, 2004 Chiimu Tora Dojo: 300 fans (sellout) Super Japan returned to Tokyo, and Team Tiger hosted a fan club show at Tiger Kikuchi's Team Tiger Dojo. The show featured Team Tiger members in matches against each other. 1) Takahiro KEOKI def Dynamite Boy (8:41) following the Uzumaki Toubida. 2) Jet Jaguar III & Toramusha def Dragon Mask & Jiraiya (10:39) after J3 & Toramusha hit Total Elimination on Dragon Mask. 3) Dragon Fury" Minobu Tetsuhara def Eiji Kid Shimura (9:22) after making Shimura submit to the Dragon Sleeper. 4) Noboru "Raging Bull" Nishida def Jun Kikuchi & MAMMOTH Kagawa (14:45) after hitting the Raging Bull Bomber on Kikuchi. 5) NCWF Cruiserweight title match: Masashi "Lightning" Lanther (champion) & Shoji Suzuki (challenger) went to a twenty minute time limit draw. - KEOKI started the show off against Dynamite Boy of the Nagaski Bulldogs in a fast paced match. Both men were high octane from start to finish, but Dynamite Boy was beaten following KEOKI's series of Fisherman's suplexes, topped off with a Fisherman's Buster. - J3 & Toramusha teamed together for the first time, taking on the duo of Dragon Mask & Jiraiya. J3 & Jiraiya tended to face off in the ring, while Dragon Mask & Toramusha faced off with each other. It was a great mix of aerial action and mat wrestling. The end came when Toramusha stunned Dragon Mask with a release Tiger Suplex, tagged in J3, and they hit Total Elimination on a dazed Dragon Mask, J3 scoring the pinfall for his side. - Tetsuhara faced the other half of the Bulldogs, and while Shimura was the bigger man, Tetsuhara prove the more technically adept. While Shimura took control with his power a few times, Tetsuhara superior mat skills enabled him to survive Shimura's pin attempts. After dumping Shimura on his head with an Exploder '98, Tetsuhara put Shimura into a Dragon Sleeper, and Shimura tapped immediately. - Nishida won a three way match against his tag partner Kagawa and Jun Kikuchi. All three men displayed some stiff striking moves and some hard impacting power moves, but Nishida's experience proved the deciding factor. After Kikuchi took out Kagawa with a Dangerous Backdrop Driver, Nishida nailed him with an enzulariato, then hit the Raging Bull Bomber for the pinfall. - To the delight of SJPW fans in attendance, Shoji Suzuki took some time off from his commitments in VSP to challenge Lanther for the NCWF cruiserweight title. Both Lanther & Suzuki pulled no punches, with Suzuki almost upsetting Lanther about ten minutes into the match after hitting his patented Spiral Bomb. Lanther kicked out, and about three minutes later hit the Lanther Bomb, but Suzuki kicked out. Both men then traded some stiff strikes for about two minutes, with Lanther scoring the upper hand with a koppo kick. Suzuki came back with an enzugiri, and went for the Spiral Bomb again, but Lanther countered it with a headscissors like move, knocking Suzuki out of the ring. Lanther hit a space flying tiger drop. Lanther then whipped Suzuki into the railing twice, before rolling him into the ring, going to the top rope, and hitting a diving headbutt for a two count. With time running out, Lanther went for a brainbuster, but Suzuki flipped behind Lanther, and hit a backdrop Driver. He rolled up, bringing Lanther with him, and hit a second Backdrop Driver. Suzuki then tried to set Lanther up for the Spiral Bomb again, but he flipped Suzuki over. As Suzuki got to his feet, Lanther nailed him with a lariat, then hit a fisherman's buster. Lanther was in the process of pinning Suzuki when the time ran out. Both men shook hands, and took time out after the event to sign autographs and take pictures with fans for about ninety minutes. Fans were buzzing the promotion's message board later that night over Suzuki's surprise appearance, and the fact that he and Lanther spent so much time with the fans right after their hotly contested match. Fans said they hope Suzuki gets his shot at Tora Waziname in VSP, as the fans have been wanting to see the two face off again for a very long time.
