SJPW Cards

Metro

27 December 1998

[Metro Concludes...] TK: Our next segment is the first interview conducted with our new All-Asian Senshuken, Ed Dean. AO: Dean is a traitor, and he's scum! He was scum when he was in the Gaijin, and he's worse now that he has allied himself with Hadi Moditan. TK: Regardless. he is our All-Asian Senshuken.... __ ___ _____________________________________________________ / |/ /__ / /________ Interview Segment: / / /|_/ / -_) __/ __/ _ \ All-Asian Senshuken Ed Dean /_/ /_/\__/\__/_/ \___/_______________________________________/ [Camera cuts to Jonnie Adonis standing to the side of the entrance way on a raised platform/stage.] Adonis: "Ladies and gentlemen at this time I would like to bring out a man who shocked all of SJPW when he tore through the All-Asian Juuryou Tournament and captured the title...I give you..ED DEAN!!!" [The fans react with a violent chorus of boos as the camera pans to the right as Ed Dean bursts through the entrance curtains, followed closely by Doug Thomas and Hadi Moditian. Dean and Thomas wear SHVF red and white jumpsuits while Moditian has on a tailored charcoal grey suit. Dean stares out at the crowd and snarls as he clasps the All-Asian title hanging on his right shoulder. He slaps it, and gives a grunting laugh and then proceeds over to Adonis.] Adonis: "Mr. Dean moments from now you will face Chiimu Tora member Shishi 'Lion' Fujiwara in your first defense of the All-Asian title, can you give us some insight on that match, and how it felt to win the title on Tsunami." [Dean looks at Adonis with a bit of contempt, and then growls into the microphone.] Dean: "Last week, I did exactly what I said I would do. I ripped through the 'best' SJPW has to offer, and I brought this title back to SHVF and Mr. Moditian." [Moditian smiles and nods his head behind Dean. Thomas looks out at the crowd with stoic indignation.] Dean: "I should also add that while Shinji Hamada wrestled three matches, I wrestled four." [Dean holds up four fingers in front of the belt.] Adonis: "Yes, I understand you had a shoot fight in the SHVF earlier in the day." Dean: "Yeah, against some untested young punk named Salazar, and you now what? He gave me a better fight than any of the SJPW 'stars'. First I dumped Onslaught on his ugly scared up head, and proved once and for all, that I own him in any kind of fight. Then Ace Kilcannon went down..Huh, Kilcannon is a cruiser, he should never have been in the ring against me in the first place. Then, you have the leader of Chiimu Tora himself." [Dean spits out the words "Chiimu Tora" with venom dripping from each syllable.] Dean: "You know, I would say Hamada let down the legacy of Tiger Kikuchi when he cried 'I quit!' like a gutless coward, but then again Kikuchi's legacy was never that great to begin with. Hell, seems like I have already beat the hell out of most of Chiimu Tora. Once I get done with the 'disciples' I may have to go after the 'messiah' himself." Adonis: "Tiger Kikuchi is retired, I think you should concentrate on the active members of Chiimu Tora..." Dean: "Well Adonis, you're retired too but maybe I'll slap some sense into you as well." [Dean glowers at Adonis, and Adonis doesn't step back, but returns the stare, a wicked smile tugging at the corners of his mouth. The fans begin to cheer on Adonis hoping he will go after Dean, but after a couple of tense seconds, Adonis shakes his head.] Adonis: "This is your time, you say what you want to, but you should be aware of the challange you face in Fujiwara tonight." Dean: "I've seen Fujiwara saying he wants this war between SJPW and SHVF to be over. Well, as far as I am concerned it is over, and we won!" [The fans begin to boo once more.] Dean: "Fujiwara, I know you and Moditian go way back, but you are standing in my way tonight and don't think I am going to take it easy on you." [Moditian steps forward.] Moditian: "Fujiwara...Business is business." [Moditian steps back and holds out his hands indicating he has no control of Dean's actions in the match.] Dean: "So bring them on SJPW. Chiimu Tora, Vladikoff, Matsumoto, Tokugawa, I'll take them all! No matter who it is, I am going to prove just like I did when I won this belt that...STRONG STYLE RULES!" [Dean holds the All-Asian title up in the air and Moditian claps and then bows to the crowd as Thomas yells out insults at a few fans.] Adonis: "Fans, Ed Dean and the SHVF contingent, ready to face the challenges of Chiimu Tora and all of SJPW." [camera cuts back to Kikuchi & Ono in the Control Center...] AO: That arrogant, no good... TK: Calm yourself Akira. AO: How can you stay calm after the things he said about you? TK: Because Ed Dean will have to put his Yen where his mouth is. AO: Eh? TK: I was going to save this announcement for the end of the show, but starting next week, Eddie Rose will be taking my place at your side. AO: Your leaving SJPW? TK: No Akira. After Dean won the All-Asian title, Jumbo signed a match between Dean & myself. We will see if he can truly handle me as easily as he says. AO: You're insane! Remember what happened earlier this year? What Hamada did to you? TK: Yes, and I have fully recovered. Hamada and I have been training, and Nishida, Onslaught & Takizawa, men who know Dean very well, have been coaching me on his strengths and weaknesses. This old man has a few fights left in him, and Ed Dean will be one of them. AO: This is the man who made Otto Verhoeven submit! He will break you in half! TK: I think not Ono, but I appreciate your concern. Now, let us go to our next match. The Mandarin faces off with Masashi Lanther, in what could be one of the best matches we've shown this year. [Ono says nothing, but continues to stare at Kikuchi in shock] TK: Since you have nothing to add Ono-sama, let us get this match underway... ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Masashi Lanther vs The Mandarin /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [The camera opens on referee Goto Kyoji, as he brings each man to center ring. He calls for a handshake, and Lanther chest bumps Mandarin instead to "ohhhs" from the crowd. Mandarin steps back, and gestures for Lanther to come to him. Goto signals for the bell, and as it rings, Lanther charges, only to have Mandarin deftly side-step him, and land a hard kick to the back of the leg.] AO: Mandarin dancing back away now as Lanther shaking out his leg. TK: Fans should not be fooled by Mandarin's thin frame and low weight. He strikes with accuracy and great speed, and thus great force. AO: Hai. [Lanther moves to lock up with Mandarin, but Mandarin again spins away, and connects with a kick to the leg. Lanther steps back to the ropes as Mandarin gestures to Lanther to come to him. Lanther shouts out in frustration, and then both men circle each other.] AO: Mandarin now taking a Thai Kickboxing stance, perhaps a message being sent to Steel Serpant? TK: Perhaps, Lanther moves in, and they lock up...OH! Mandarin immediately catches Lanther with a knee strike to the head, and now a roundhouse kick to the knee and a spinwheel kick to the head takes Lanther down. [Crowd applauds] AO: That is Mandarin's Cobra Twist, and he is not letting up as he stomps down on Lanther's right heel. Lanther rolling to the ropes to escape the attack. [Goto backs Mandarin off, and he turns to the crowd, and shakes his head as if to say "He is no competition. Lanther crys out and slaps the mat, and then pulls himself to his feet using the ropes. Mandarin turns to face Lanther, and now assumes a differant fighting stance. Lanther moves forward cautiously, limping slightly.] AO: Mandarin feints with a kick to the knee, and as Lanther raises his shin to block it, Mandarin catches him with a forearm strike, and now Mandarin unleases a flurry of elbows, forearms and kicks. TK: Mandarin is a whirlwind of motion as Lanther is staggered back under this relentless assault. [The fans "ohhh" and "ahhh" as Mandarin connects with strike after strike on Lanther. Goto backs Mandarin away from the corner, and Lanther slumps against the turnbuckles on weak legs. Mandarin moves back in, and Lanther surprises him with a knife-edge chop to the chest. Lanther hits another chop, and another and then sends Mandarin to the ropes.] AO: Tilt-A-Whirl Backbreaker! TK: NO! Mandarin flipped free and attempts a Spinning Heel Kick, but Lanther ducks under it. [Mandarin pivots on his left foot after missing the heel kick and immediately attempts a roundhouse kick to the body, but Lanther catches his leg. The fans buzz with anticipation and they are rewarded as Lanther hits a Dragonscrew Legwhip taking Mandarin down.] AO: Great counter by Lanther, and he is up now and clapping his hands over his head. TK: Mandarin spins on the mat and legsweeps the overconfident Lanther, and now he locks on a spinning toehold to complete his Quicksand manuver. [Lanther struggles to reach the ropes, but he does and Goto calls for the break. The fans applaud as Mandarin backs away, but they let out a surprised "ohhh" as Mandarin suddenly stomps away at Lanther's heel. Goto has to pull Mandarin away.] AO: Mandarin showing his aggressive nature, and Lanther now out on the apron, and he is up slowly, heavily favoring his right leg. TK: Mandarin moves past Goto and nails Lanther with an elbow. He fires another one, but Lanther blocks it. Headbutt by Lanther, that is surprising. AO: Hai. Lanther now locking a front facelock...He is going to Suplex Mandarin to the floor! [The fans "ohhh" and "ahhh" as Lanther attempts the dangerous move, but Mandarin blocks it by holding onto the ropes. Goto trying to break things up, but Lanther cries out and tries the move again, but Mandarin blocks it.] AO: Mandarin now with a knee to the midsection, and now he attempts to Suplex Lanther into the ring. TK: He has him up, but Lanther slips free, and lands behind him...GERMAN SUPLEX!! ...1... ...2... AO: Kickout by Mandarin [crowd applauds] [Lanther looks over to Goto, and holds up three fingers. Goto shakes his head, saying "Two, two." Lanther gets to his feet, and pulls Mandarin up. He whips him to the ropes and connects with a Tilt-A-Whirl Backbreaker and the fans applaud as he covers Mandarin, but only gains a two count.] AO: Lanther taking control now, but unable to put Mandarin away. TK: Lanther now has Mandarin up and he takes him over with a quick Belly-To-Belly Suplex and then immediately gets to his feet. [Lanther points to the top rope and surprisingly hears a few cheers as he makes his climb to the top. Mandarin rolls to his feet, looking stunned by Lanther's burst of offense. Lanther pauses for a moment, and then leaps off.] AO: Missle Kick...Mandarin side-steps it! TK: Lanther hits hard, but he rolls to his feet...Mandarin hits his Golden Thunder! [The fans applaud as Mandarin preforms his backflip into a double savate kick and Lanther falls back to the canvas. Mandarin gets to his feet, and motions for Lanther to get up. Lanther staggers to his feet, and Mandarin connects with a roundhouse kick to the side, and snap kick to the head, and then grabs Lanther's head and leaps up with a kneestrike.] AO: Brutal combo by Mandarin, and he holds Lanther up by his mask, preventing him from going down, and now steps back and launches a spinning kick... TK: Lanther was ready and he catches the leg...Dragonscrew ...ENZIGURI! [Mandarin leaps in the air catching Lanther with the kick to the back of his head and the fans "ohhh" and then applaud. Mandarin rises, and pulls Lanther up before connecting with a quick right/left combo strike to the midsection and then hitting a snap Piledriver on Lanther.] AO: Mandarin makes the cover. ...1... ...2... [Fans "ohhh" and applaud] AO: Lanther just able to get free. TK: Mandarin now standing, and he pauses for a moment before pulling Lanther to his feet. Double Kidney Strike from Mandarin and now he leaps up on Lanther's shoulders... [Lanther suddenly falls back slamming Mandarin to the canvas.] AO: OH! Lanther with a quick counter to the Cyclone Inverted Hurricanrana by Mandarin. Both men down on the mat now, trying to build back their strength. TK: Lanther is a skilled veteran, and he knew if Mandarin connected with the Cyclone, he would have been in serious trouble. [Lanther rolls to his knees, and then gets to his feet. Mandarin holds the back of his head as he sits up, and Lanther suddenly bolts across the ring landing a dropkick right to the face of Mandarin snapping him back to the mat and elicting "ohhhs" from the fans. Lanther gets to his feet, and claps his hands over his head. He pulls Mandarin up and whips into the corner, and then shouts out as he runs across the ring.] AO: ROLLING KOPPO KICK! [Fans "ohhhh"] TK: Lanther connects with one of his trademark moves, and now he has Mandarin in deep trouble. [Lanther rolls to his feet, and lifts the dazed Mandarin up to the toop turnbuckle, and climbs up after him. He sets for a Superplex, and suddenly Mandarin comes to life, firing in with elbows to the midsection and then to the head, and Lanther falls back to the canvas.] AO: Mandarin able to fight Lanther off, but Lanther back up. He nails Mandarin with forearms and now he attempts the Superplex once more. TK: Mandarin still fighting it, and he manages to push Lanther back down. [The fans "ohhh" as Lanther springs to his feet, and with a loud shout climbs up and connects with palm strikes to the face of Mandarin, and then standing on the top rope, cries out and hits a Frankensteiner.] AO: Lanther drives Mandarin down to the canvas, and now he staggers back and sits on Mandarin's chest and counts the pin with Goto and the crowd! ...1... ...2... ...3... AO: NO!! [Mandarin somehow wriggles out just before the count. Goto holds up two fingers, and Lanther is up quickly, and shoves Goto back to the corner, and yells out three, and shoves three fingers in Goto's face. Goto shakes his head, and showing great patience, demonstrates how Mandarin got his shoulder up.] AO: Lanther foolishly risking disqualification by laying his hands on Goto, band now he turns to Mandarin who is just finding his feet. TK: Lanther's temper is well known, he can be a terror. [Lanther charges at Mandarin, and cuts him down with a running lariat. Lanther waits for Mandarin to rise again, and runs at him connecting with a another lariat. He claps his hands over his head as he waits for Mandarin to rise yet again.] AO: Lanther charges...LARIA...OH! Mandarin connects with his Iron Arrow finger strike! TK: He stops Lanther in his tracks and now leaps up to the ropes...Springboard into a side kick to the head...RED IRON STRIKE!! [The fans "ohhh" as Mandarin connects with a brutally stiff kick to the side of Lanther's head dropping him to the mat. Mandarin makes the cvoer and Goto drops down.] ...1... ...2... ...3... **ding** **ding** **ding** [camera cuts back to Kikuchi & Ono in the Control Center...] TK: Mandarin showing his resilancy and resourcefulness and connects with a combination of moves that put Lanther down for the count of three. AO: Hai. Mandarin is a fine athlete, and while I don't agree with Lanther's attitude he is as gifted as any wrestler in SJPW. This is a big victory for Mandarin, but a good fight by Lanther as well. TK: We still have our main event ahead as Ed Dean will face Shishi Fujiwara for the All-Asian title. AO: I have high hopes for Fujiwara. He is young, but he has a warrior's heart, and I know he will give his all. I hope he beats Dean within an inch of his life! [An assistant comes out, and hands Ono a piece of paper] TK: What is it? AO: I'm told that we have Hadi Moditian and Carlos Liger Jr. standing by backstage. Moditian they're making me talk to you, just so you don't think I like you or something... TK: Akira! AO: Anyway... *MR.* Moditian, what is your rebuttal to the decision of the SJPW board of directors if Liger wishes to take part in the tournament, he'll have to beat Shishi Fujiwara first? [The shot cuts to Hadi Moditian and Carlos Liger Jr. standing backstage in front of a white cinder block wall...] Moditian: Thats fine with me, but it doesn't happen on an SJPW card, I don't trust the management here... I've seen Wrestling with Shadows and I won't chance a double cross on SJPW's part. The board of directors may have made the match and chosen the opponent, so I'm choosing the venue. There is a big independent show at Tokyo Korakuen Hall on January 7th, so the match takes place there on neutral ground. Lets go Carlos. [Moditian and Liger walk away as the shot cuts back to the annouce position.] TK: So the board of directors make another ploy to foil Moditan. and it backfires. AO: Not really. Fujiwara will face Liger Jr. Moditan got Fujiwara his start in this sport. It seems a fitting insult, does it not? TK: Akira... [Ono checks his headset, as if listening] AO: What? Oh, all right. TK: What now? AO: We have to cut to the ring... [The camera cuts to the runway entrance. Slowly, Juventud Sagrada makes his way from behind the curtains. He wears a black muscle shirt and his wrestling tights...and of course the crimson red mask. He makes his way to ringside slapping hands with a few fans.. before entering the ring.. and easking for the mic...for a few brief words.] SAGRADA: I must apologize for my lack of palabras..my lack of words over the past few months. For allthough my body has been here competing in SJPW, my mind has wandered back to mi querida homeland. Mi isla, Puerto Rico was bombarded in late october by very powerful hurricanes...mi familia, hiding themselves, worried for their well-being. [pause] Being here in Japan, has caused a great difficulty in communicating with them. But finally, today... I got through and all my worries were vanshied. Y por eso.. I can continue onwards with the SJPW. I thank those who have worried along with me.. and have worried about my state of mind. Juventud thanks you all... SJPW.. mi alma has returned....and I shall continue.. La Sagrada Mision. [Juventud shoots his fist into the air and acknowledges the fans. He rolls under the ropes and walks to the back. The camera then cuts back to Kikuchi & Ono in the Control Center...] TK: That was strange. AO: What do you expect from a masked luchador who claims to be on a "sacred" mission? TK: Akira... AO: It is now time for our main event! Shishi Fujiwara avenges the honor of SJPW, proving to Ed Dean, Hadi Moditan, and Shinsaku Yamashita, that SJPW is truly the one with STRONG STYLE! ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / All-Asian Juuryou Shimeijiai: / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ /Shishi "Lion" Fujiwara vs Ed Dean [CH] /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [The camera cuts to the runway entrance. Hissing and booing from the crowd greets the immense form of Dean as he paces through the entrance curtains with Hadi Moditian in his wake. No music plays and the arena is filled with the disapproving sounds of the fans as they express their displeasure. Dean holds the SJPW All-Asian Juuryou title over his head as he walks. Dean wears a red singlet, red shingaurd and kneepads pulled over blue Asics, a white SHVF "STRONG STYLE RULES" t-shirt and white tape on his wrists.] AO: The nerve of Ed Dean! He has the honor of being the SJPW champion, and instead continues to shill for that upstart SHVF! TK: And it looks as if Shishi Fujiwara doesn't approve as he gets a running start .... PLANCHA ONTO ED DEAN! The bell hasn't even sounded and already this one has begun to break down. Fujiwara grabs hold of Dean's singlet and rolls him into the ring, following him right back in. Fujiwara turns to the ropes... SPRINGBOARD MOONSAULT onto Dean... Botan drops down for the count, but Dean kicks out immediately. AO: Ha! The match has just started and already Dean is on the defensive. So much for his "Strong Style." TK: It's far too early to count Dean out, Akira, and you know it. He didn't become the Juuryou champion by luck, he got it by skill. You may not like his conduct or the other promotion he competes for, but you have to respect the man's skills. [Dean rolls backwards towards the corner, going into a defensive posture as Fujiwara warily moves in towards him. Dean suddenly lunges forward as Fujiwara takes another cautious step in, and the champion knocks the challenger to the mat. Dean begins to throw some closed right hands at the face of Fujiwara, but Owada Botan puts a fast 5-count on the champion and Dean relents, rolling off Fujiwara and again taking up a more defensive posture.] AO: Dean should be disqualified! Those were blatant closed fists! TK: He broke before the five-count, Akira... all Botan-san can do is warn the champion. It's possible that Dean has become more used to the "strong style" rules and forgotten that closed fists are illegal in SJPW. AO: Hmph. He knows the rules, Kikuchi-san. He just chooses to ignore them. [Fujiwara gets back to his feet, rubbing his jaw and trying to shakeoff the affects of the punches that Dean managed to land. Again Fujiwara moves in towards the champion and Dean lets the challenger come towards him. The two men hesitate for a moment then go to lock up. Dean shows his superior strength as he backs Fujiwara against the ropes and Botan demands a break. Dean slowly releases Fujiwara, but the crowd seems surprised as Fujiwara quickly pokes Dean in the eye.] AO: Well there's something we don't see from Shishi Fujiwara very often. Not only was it a great little shortcut, but he did it very well... Botan was more concerned with getting the clean break from Ed Dean and didn't even notice Fujiwara's eye gouge. TK: I hardly think that's something that deservers your praise, Akira! [Dean staggers backward, rubbing his eyes trying to clear them, while Fujiwara bounces off the ropes and catches Dean across the chest with a short lariat. The champion refuses to fall, however, staggering back more. Fujiwara suddenly stomps on Dean's right foot and as Dean's foot comes off the mat, Fujiwara drops down, taking the left leg out from under Dean with a legsweep, leaving the champion on his back. The crowd applauds as Fujiwara stomps on the right foot of Dean again and then applies an ankle-lock submission.] AO: I love it! Fujiwara is going to beat Dean at his own game! He's going to force Dean to submit! [Dean grimaces in pain as Fujiwara cinches down harder on the ankle, twisting it in directions it's just not meant to go. Inch by slow inch, Dean manages to pull himself towards the ropes, grabbing hold of the bottom one. Botan quickly demands Fujiwara release the hold, but a small murmur runs through the crowd as Fujiwara refuses. Botan begins to count to five, and at the count of four, Fujiwara finally breaks the hold. Botan warns Fujiwara, who seems not at all concerned.] TK: Shishi Fujiwara is coming dangerously close to getting disqualified, Akira. This is very unlike the way he normally competes. He gives Dean little time to breath as he moves back in on the champion and applies a side headlock, dragging Dean back towards the center of the ring. He turns away from the referee.. and again rakes Dean across the eyes! Dean is temporarilly blinded as Fujiwara runs to the ropes... FLYING ARMDRAG takes Dean over... Dean gets back to his feet quickly as Fujiwara goes to the ropes yet again.. ANOTHER FLYING ARMDRAG.... BUT DEAN HOLDS ONTO FUJIWARA'S ARM... AND TURNS IT INTO A FUJIWARA ARMBAR! Even if Dean can't force the submission from this hold, he's already started working towards the Cross Arm Breaker. AO: Fujiwara had everything going his way, but he had to go for one more aerial move. That's inexperience on the part of Fujiwara, and it's costing him now. TK: Fujiwara is obviously in a great deal of pain as Dean works over his arm and wrist... but Fujiwara refuses to quit. Like Dean earlier, Fujiwara is desperately searching for the ropes.... and he finds them, getting one leg underneath. That will force the Owada Botan to call for the break, and this time it's Ed Dean waiting til the four count to break the hold. [Dean reluctantly breaks the hold, but gives Fujiwara several quick stomps to the shoulder before backing off. Fujiwara slowly gets to his feet, holding his shoulder and trying to shake off some of the pain in his wrist. Dean moves in on his opponent, and the two men lock up... only to have Dean stun Fujiwara with a quick headbutt right to the bridge of the nose. The crowd "oooohs" as blood begins to flow from both Fujiwara's nostrils, but Dean shows no mercy, headbutting him in the same place again, then grabs him around the waist and drives him to the canvas with a belly-to-belly suplex.] TK: Devestating suplex by the champion and now Dean is demanding that Botan give Fujiwara a 10 count. I think Dean wants to be declared a winner by knockout. AO: He may very well be the winner that way. Those two headbutts could have broken Fujiwara's nose... he's bleeding profusely. Owada Botan IS putting the count on Fujiwara... [Botan starts to count, but Dean suddenly lifts Fujiwara's head up!] TK: What? AO: That bastard! [Dean hoists the dazed Fujiwara over his shoulder, then drops down into a shoulderbreaker. Not releasing the hold, he drops down for another one, and then a third. The crowd goes silent as Fujiwara screams in pain. Dean stands Fujiwara up, and starts to apply a wristlock, but Fujiwara lands a hard left to Dean's face! The crowd starts to cheer as Fujiwara pummels Dean with his fists. Dean starts to stagger backwards, then suddenly he blocks a punch, and and grabs Fujiwara's weakened arm, and hits an over the shoulder armbreaker! Fujiwara goes down, clutching his shoulder. Dean grabs the arm, and pulls him towards the center of the ring, and locks on the Cross Arm Breaker! The crowd "oooohs" loudly, as Fujiwara tries to find his way to the ropes...] TK: He has Fujiwara in the center of the ring. There is no way out for him now. AO: This can't be! [Suddenly, Hadi Moditan yells at Dean, and he looks over at him with a questioning look. Moditan says something else, and Dean nods, then breaks the hold! The crowd "oooohs", as they knew Dean had the match won. Botan checks on Fujiwara, while Dean stands in a neutral corner...] TK: What is Dean doing? AO: He's Moditan's lapdog! Moditan speaks, and Dean jumps! [Botan checks Fujiwara's arm, then calls for the bell. The other members of Chiimu Tora head to the ring from the back. Hamada & Nishida help Fujiwara to his feet, as Moditan steps into the ring. Moditan approaches Fujiwara, and throws a SHVF "Strong Style Rules" t-shirt at him. It lands on his shoulder, and Fujiwara looks at it, then looks at Moditan. Banshee grabs the shirt, and throws it in Moditan's face, while Hamada & Nishida helps Fujiwara out of the ring, and hand him over to Onslaught & Takizawa, who help him to the back. The fans applaud Fujiwara, while in the ring, Banshee & Dean are having a face off. Jet Jaguar II keeps his partner back. Dean just smiles at Banshee, and says "You'll get yours on January 9th punk. Don't be so eager for the punishment to come", then exits the ring with a smiling Moditan. The camera cuts back to the Control Center...] TK: It seems the referee decided Fujiwara could not continue, and awarded the match to Ed Dean. AO: This man has got to be stopped! He's making us all look bad! TK: He will be stopped Akira. No man is invincible. AO: I still think you're mad to take him on. TK: Then mad I am. We're out of time. For Akira Ono, I'm Tiger Kikuchi. Goodnight everyone. [The camera pans on the image of Dean heading to the back with Moditan, while an angry Banshee is restrained in the ring by Jet Jaguar II.
