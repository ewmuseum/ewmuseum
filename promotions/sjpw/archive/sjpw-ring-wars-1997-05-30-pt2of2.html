Ring Announcer: This next match is a first round match in the SJPW Woman's title tournament! Introducing first, from Red Hook, NY, weighing 190lbs TER-RA!!! (As "War Machine" by Kiss plays, Terra and Ryu Taka head to the ring. Terra is has a sqaut build, and her head is covered bu an orange towel. She is very muscular, but not as well defined as Chyna. She has an AC tatoo on her right shoulder. She tosses the staff she is carrying to Kyu, who leave the ring) ER: So the AC have a new member? AO: Of course! I knew about her a week ago. ER: I didn't! AO: That's because you are, how you say, "Out of the loop" ER: There's the bell. They lock up; Terra takes Hakurana down with a hiptoss. She gets back up, and they lock up again. Hakurana forces Terra to the ropes... ref makes them break... Hakurana nailed Terra with an kick to the side of her head, barely missing the ref! [fans applaud] AO: Hakurana not wasting any time; she nails Terra with more kicks to the legs and body... she whips Terra to the ropes... Terra ducks the spin kick.. she grabs Hakurana from behind... waistlock german Terraplex! [fans applaud] All impact, no bridge! ER: Terra wasting no time; she takes Hakurana down with a belly to belly release Terraplex. [fans applaud]. She sets herself up, waiting for Hakurana to get up... Hakurana runs over to her.. spinning backbreaker by Terra! [fans applaud] Hakurana slides out of the ring! AO: Terra goes out after her.. Hakurana runs around the ring... Terra follows... she the ref is following Hakurana... Terra turns the corner... Thrust kick by Kyu Kabuki! [fans oooh; some boo] He nailed her in the throat! ER: Hakurana now in the ring.. Ryu Taka is yelling at the ref, but Kyu is on the other side of the ring all ready. Hakurana goes to the outside... she nails Terra with an elbow off the apron. Hakurana now whipping Terra into the railing [fans oooh] AO: Kabuki is pushing the timekeeper away from his table; Hakurana whips Terra into the railing again, then rams her head into the steel. Kabuki calls her over, and she drags Terra with her... He's telling her something, and she smiles evilly... She grabs Terra by the throat... Chokeslam through the table! [fans oooh, then applaud] Ryu Taka is going nuts! ER: The ref is outside now, checking on Terra. She looks hurt bad! Hakurana pushes him away, and drags Terra to her feet. [fans applaud] She kicked out! AO: Hakurana doesn't need these shortcuts to win! What are she and Kabuki thinking! Hakurana looks mad; she goes to the top rope... legdrop! [fans applaud] Cover..one..two..thr-kickout! [fans applaud] Terra won't stay down! ER: Hakurana has her up, and whips her to the ropes... Terra ducks the spin kick, stops short, grabs Hakurana from behind.. T-bone Terraplex! [fans applaud] She grabs her... another T-bone Terraplex! [fans applaud] She snap mare's Hakurana into a sitting position... she comes off the ropes... dropkick to the back of the head! [fans applaud] Kabuki is on the apron, yelling at the referee! ER: The ref now telling Kabuki to get down... Terra is setting Harakuna up again, but she low blows her... DDT! Kabuki is still on the apron, but Ryu Taka pulls him down! Kabuki turns on Taka, and threatens him with the staff! AO: Taka better run! Hakurana now with a powerbomb on Terra. Cover..one..two..thre-kickout! [fans applaud] I can't believe Terra is still in this! Hakurana nails her with a Degau Drop, and signals for the kill! She whips Terra into the corner, and follows in with a clothesline! [fans applaud] ER: Hakurana now setting Terra on the top rope... she gets up on the bottom rope, and locks her arm around Terra around the shoulders... Terra grabs her arms! She's trying to fight it! [fans start chanting "Terra! Terra!"] AO: Both of these women are very strong... this is a true test of strength for both of them... Terra is slowly prying the grip loose... she almost has it... she broke the hold! She nails Hakurana with a back elbow! ER: Terra now with a backflip off the top rope.. she grabs Hakurana in a waistlock, but too low for Harakuna to hit her with a back elbow... She picks her up, and slams her forward, face first into the corner! [fans applaud] AO: Terra now ramming Hakurana's head into the corner; she backs away from her, the charges back in with a lariat to the back of her head! Terra is signalling the crowd [fans start chanting Terra's name again] Kabuki is trying to distract the referee, but Ryu Taka grabs him again! T-Bone Terraplex on Kabuki! [fans oooh, then applaud. Many start chanting Terra's name] ER: Terra just dumped Kyu Kabuki on his head! He'll never live that one down. Terra helps Taka in the ring, who looks unconcious. She is standing in the middle of the ring, victorious! AO: Impressive win by Terra. She has great skill with her suplexes! You know Akira... I heard that Reeking Sempura is from the shallow end of her gene pool." Can we get some medical personel to take Ms. Wu backstage. AO [looking flustered]: I think Rekka is in trouble. Before we go to our main event, we have these comments made by Shinji "Live" Hamada the other day. ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped May 30, 1997] [the camera opens inside the locker room of Shinji Hamada. Then I began to get into confrontations with Super Tengu... and I knew I had to double my efforts. Then came Dragon Special... now the rest of RoT... Kyu Kabuki... Takao Nakami... There is no order in SJPW... it is as out of control as ECW, and that is disappointing." "I find myself with few options. I will do what I must."
