The History of Super Tengu: The Feud Part 2 So here we were, [name withheld], [name withheld] and myself, putting together the angle that helped make SJPW so hot. Now all things considered, it is very naïve of me to think we were he reason people liked the fed. Honestly, we were one of many keys to success. The fact is we had a killer “Hardcore” division featuring guys like Demon Boy Ishinriku, Steel Serpent and Ed Dean. Gerry was busting his ass to get the Heavyweight title picture interesting and the tag team scene had the awesome Bonecrushers, the Down Boyz , the Tokyo Blades and the Tokyo Express (plus this little team featuring Kazuya Asakino and his cousin Keiji Asamiya). We were approaching summer and we had plans… The Set-up: To clear his name of conspiracy Shinji Hamada challenged Super Tengu to a tag match, each man choosing his own partner. Tengu nabbed Takao Nakami, a man Hamada disposed of in round 1 of the Cruiserweight Tournament, as his partner. Hamada got himself “Tsunami” Rhiyoto Makada to be his #1 man. Hamada was obsessed with Tengu, which is exactly how Scott wanted it to be. There were plans in the works for our babyface hero and Scott was being very careful about how Hamada was portrayed.
