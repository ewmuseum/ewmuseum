Tsunami, Part One

29 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Tiger Kikuchi & Akira Ono are seated at the broadcast table. Kikuchi is in his early 40's. He's has short black hair and is wearing a black SJPW blazer, white shirt, black tie and black pants. Ono is in his mid 30s with long black hair put into a pony tail and a goatee walks in from the right side. He's wearing a black suit with a white chinese shirt and black Oakley shades] Akira Ono: Greetings! I am Akira Ono, and this is Tiger Kikuchi. Tonight we bring you a special edition of Tsunami. Tonight, we have three of four matches for our Koubousen Geijutsu mini-tournament, loathe that I am to agree with such an event. Tiger Kikuchi: Now, now Ono-sama. It shouldn't be that bad. AO: Yes it will. Our main event for tonight has "Japanese Superstar" Jinsei Tenru his worthless MLWO Japan title against Frankie "The Thumper" Douglas. TK: A man who once defeated you for the Old Japan All-Japan title. AO: Don't remind me. Why have Tenru defend that worthless belt? MLWO is dead and buried. TK: There has been some discussions by the board of directors to make the title an official SJPW title, so they are allowing Tenru to defend his belt while they debate the issue. AO: Debate all they want; it's still a worthless title! TK: Ono! Enough of that! AO: Oh, all right. [someone approaches the broadcast position, and hands Ono a note] AO: What? Thank you. [scans paper]. It seems we have a change. El Gato Diablo is now going to face Tiger Extreme, and Kenichi Kubota will face Angel Taboo! TK: Why is that? AO: Who cares? It's all garbage matches anyway! Let's get to the ring, and get this over with! ______ ___________________________________ /_ __/__ __ _____ ___ ___ _ (_) Koubousen Geijutsu Tournament / / / (_-</ // / _ \/ _ `/ ' \/ / Angel Taboo vs Kenichi Kubota /_/ /___/\_,_/_//_/\_,_/_/_/_/_/________________________________/ [Camera cuts to the ring, where Nishi Toju and Owada Botan stand. Nishi waits for the crowd to die down, then speaks...] Nishi: Konnichi-wa! [crowd cheers]. This first match is round one in the Koubousen Geijutsu Tournament! Introducing first, from parts unknown, and weighing in at 190 pounds...AN-GEL TA-BOO!!! ["Her Black Wings" by Danzig cranks on the PA as Taboo walks out, his masked face betraying no emotion. He slaps hands with the fans, then enters the ring] Nishi: His opponent hails from Osaka, and weighs in at 245 pounds, he is...KEN-I-CHI "KURASSHAA" KU-BO-TA!!! ["Walk" by Pantera cranks on the PA as Kubota walks out, carrying a bucket of weapons. He walks to the ring, ignoring the fans. He plance the bucket on the ring apron, then enters the ring...] AO: See what I mean? The weapons are all ready here! TK: Ono! [Kubota enters the ring, and charges Taboo, who stops him dead in his tracks with a standing dropkick. Kubota staggers back, then charges again, only to be caught with a spinwheel kick. He staggers back again, and Taboo rushes him, and hits an inverted atomic drop. Kubota falls back to the ropes, and Taboo whips him across the ring, then leaps at him coming off with a spinning elbowsmash that sends Kubota to the mat. The crowd applauds as Taboo works the crowd...] AO: Good wrestling from Taboo so far. Why this talented Welterweight chose to compete in this tournament is beyond me. TK: Maybe he has something to prove Ono. AO: Prove what? He has talent, and is squandering it here! Taboo drags Kubota to his feet, and Kubota pokes him in the eye. Taboo drops to one knee, and Kubota grabs his mask, and headbutts him. Kubota now with an uppercut to Taboo's jaw! [crowd "ooohs"] This man has no talent! TK: Not true Ono. He does have some skill. Kubota with a kneedrop to the side of Taboo's head, making him sit up in pain. Kubota drags Taboo to his feet...Side Suplex...Kubota rolls to his feet, and runs to the ropes...elbowdrop to Taboo's chest...Kubota rolls to his feet, and turns Taboo over into a single leg boston crab... [Botan asks Taboo if he wants to submit, but he says no. Kubota pulls back on the leg, and the crowd starts to chant "Taboo! Taboo". Taboo reaches out and grabs the ropes. Botan orders Kubota to break, but he refuses. Botan starts a count, and Kubota breaks at four. He gets to his feet, and stomps Taboo in the back as Botan forces him to a neutral corner...] AO: This man has no honor! He is as bad as Ishrinku! TK: Ishrinku was a hero to many people Ono. AO: Not me. TK: Taboo slowly rises to his feet, and Kubota charges...Taboo backddrops him to the floor! [crowd "ooooohs"] Taboo goes to the rope and sets himself...Kubota starts to get to his feet...MOONSAULT TO THE FLOOR!!! [crowd "oooohs", then applauds] He nailed Kubota hard with that move! AO: Good! Crush the "Kurasshaa"! [Botan starts his count, while both men roll around on the floor. They both slowly get to their feet by four. Taboo attempts a spinwheel kick, but Kubota ducks under, and catches Taboo with a low blow. The crowd "awwws" as Taboo goes down. Kubota grabs Nishi's chair, folds it up, and smacks it across Taboo's back. The crowd "oooohs" at the blow. Kubota drops the chair on the floor, drags Taboo to his feet, scoops him up, then slams him onto the chair! The crowd "ooohs" as Taboo writhes in pain on the floor. Kubota drags Taboo back to his feet, rams his head onto the apron, then rolls him into the ring. He throws the chair in, then climbs up on the apron. He heads to the corner, and starts to climb to the top rope...] TK: What is Kubota doin? AO: Who cares? He sucks! TK: Kubota still heading to the top, and Taboo is getting up. He sees Kubota climbing to the top, and he charges him..Dropkick crothes Kubota! [crowd "Awwwws"] Taboo climbs up in the corner, and stands Kubota up...SUPERPLEX!! [crowd "ooohs", then applauds] AO: Taboo rolls to his feet, and runs to the corner. He stepladders to the top rope...MOONSAULT!!! Cover... ...1... ...2... KICKOUT!!! [crowd applauds] TK: Taboo runs to the corner again, stepladdering to the top... TABOO SPLASH!! [crowd "ooohs"] Cover... ...1... ...2... KICKOUT!!! [crowd applauds] AO: He can't keep this loser down! Taboo goes to the top rope again, and climbs up. He sets himself, then leaps...Kubota moves! [crowd "awwwws"] Taboo missed the diving headbutt! [Kubota gets to his feet, and staggers over to his bucket. He pulls out a beer bottle, and shows it to the crowd. He walks over to Taboo, who is sitting up, and breaks it over his head! The crowd "oooohs", and Kubota goes back to his bucket, and pulls out a frying pan. He walks over to Taboo, and drags him to his feet. Taboo looks dazed, and Kubota smiles before slamming the pan over his head! He holds Taboo up by his mask, and the crowd goes silent for a moment. Blood seeps from under his mask, and Kubota slams the pan over his head again, letting Taboo drop to the mat...] TK: Kubota is relentless! He throws the pan into the crowd, then grabs the chair. He lays it next to Taboo, then drags his dazed opponent up. He sticks his head between his legs...POWERBOMB onto the chair! [crowd "oooohs"] Cover... ...1... ...2... KICKOUT!!! [crowd applauds] AO: Get up Taboo! [Kubota goes to the corner, and climbs to the second rope. He makes a fist, then leaps off, fistdropping the side of Taboo's head. More blood oozes from under his mask, and Kubota smiles at the crowd. He gets to his feet, and grabs Taboo's legs. He splits them, then drops down, headbutting him in the groin. The crowd "oooohs", and Kubota gets to his feet, smiling...] AO: This is sick! End it all ready! TK: Kubota drags Taboo to his feet, and Taboo drives a knee into his gut! Taboo now hammering Kubota's back with forearm shots, and Kubota drops to one knee! [crowd "oooohs"] Taboo runs to the ropes...ROCKER DROPPER ONTO THE CHAIR! [crowd "ooohs", then applauds] AO: Good move! Taboo signals to the crowd...he drags Kubota up...hurrican-POWERBOMB by Kubota onto the chair! [crowd "ooooohs} He planted him again! TK: Kubota gets to his feet, and he draws his thumb across his throat. He drags Taboo to his feet...He applies a pumphandle...He pulls Taboo up over his shoulder...MICHINOKU DRIVER ONTO THE CHAIR!!! [crowd "oooohs"] That's the "Kurasshaa Driver"! AO: Taboo is finished! Kubota makes the cover... ...1... ...2... ...3...!!! **ding** **ding** **ding** [crowd applauds as "Walk" cranks on the PA] Nishi: The winner of this match...KENICHI "KURASSHAA" KUBOTA!!! [crowd applauds again] TK: So Kubota advances to round two of the tournament. AO: He got lucky, because Taboo got carried away. He shouldn't have gone to the top rope that third time. TK: Taboo is still young, and has to learn from experience. You made mistakes when you were young too. AO: Not as many as you. TK: That could easily be disputed... AO: All right all ready! I submit! [laughs] What is the next match? TK: Alan Sterling & Doug Thomas. AO: I don't know too much about Sterling. Do you? TK: Not really. We know Thomas is an accomplished shoot-fighter. AO: He also competes in SHVF. He's a traitor to SJPW! I hope Sterling beats him just because of that! TK: So much for being unbiased... AO: Bias has nothing to do with it. SHVF stole Ed Dean from SJPW, and they've signed Doug Thomas as well. I hear Douglas & Hori Yatoro may also be competing in that garbage fed! TK: SHVF is not a garbage fed. They have some excellant former SJPW superstars, such as Dean, as well as Keiji Asamiya, Chandrasekhar Yoshida & Sunjinski... AO: It is a garbage fed if I say it is! All of those "superstars" went to SHVF because they couldn't cut it in SJPW! Asamiya is the worst of the lot! He couldn't hack it as a tag team wrestler with his cousin Kazuya, so he fled. He is a disgrace to the Matsumoto clan! At least Tetsuo kept his obligations... TK: Ono, that's enough! You dishonor yourself with such a tirade. AO: I'm sorry Kikuchi-sama. Moditan is a weasel far worse than RAT ever could hope to be! Let's go to ringside, and get this next match started... ______ ___________________________________ /_ __/__ __ _____ ___ ___ _ (_) Koubousen Geijutsu Tournament / / / (_-</ // / _ \/ _ `/ ' \/ / Alan Sterling vs Doug Thomas /_/ /___/\_,_/_//_/\_,_/_/_/_/_/________________________________/ [The camera pans to the ring, where Nishi Toju & Owada Botan stand. "Ironhead" by Helmet cranks on the PA, as Nishi starts to speak..] Nishi: This next contest is round one of the Koubousen Geijutsu Tournament. Coming to the ring at this time, from Omaha, Nebraska, USA, weighing in at 278 pounds...AL-AN STER-LING!!! [Sterling is all ready on the way to the ring. He is muscular, with medium skin tone. He has alot of scars on his arms, chest & back. His hair is buzz cut, and he has the start of a mustache & goatee. He is wearing faded gret sweatpants, faded white Puma sneakers with green stripes, and each hand/wrist is heavily taped..] AO: He looks liked he needs this job badly! TK: Ono! ["Fall" by Click cranks on the PA] Nishi: His opponent, accompanied to the ring by his tag team partner Frankie "The Thumper" Douglas, from Chicago, Illinois, USA, and weighing 260 pounds...DOUG THO-MAS!!! [Douglas & Thomas come out from the back, and start jaw-jacking with the fans. The crowd booos them, as both men are wearing SHVF "Strong Style Evolution" T-shirts. They head to the ring, and as Thomas enters the ring, Sterling bumrushes him, catching him with a knee to the gut. Sterling sets Thomas up, then suplexes him into the ring. The crowd applauds loudly as Sterling gets to his feet, and starts to put the boots to Thomas...] AO: I like this man! TK: You like anyone who beats up someone from SHVF, don't you? AO: I'd even like Ishrinku if he did something like that! TK: You're incorrigible! [Sterling continues to kick & stomp away at Thomas, who vainly tries to block the blows with his arms. Botan convinces Sterling to back away, and he checks on Thomas. Thomas sits up, and shakes his head, and Botan keeps Sterling back while Thomas gets to his feet. Sterling shoves past Botan, and lands a double axe handle blow to Thomas' head, sending him staggering back to the corner. Sterling charges in with a clothesline, and as Thomas sags there, starts to lay into him with punches. The crowd continues to root Sterling on, and chants of "SHVF sucks!" start...] AO: The crowd love this man! TK: The crowd is very anti-SHVF tonight... AO: Douglas is entering the ring! These damn rules allow this! He charges Sterling from behind, and nails him with a double axe handle! [crowd boos] Sterling doubles over, and Douglas takes him over with a belly to back suplex! This is unfair! TK: Douglas gets to his feet, and starts to rain elbowdrops on Sterling's chest. Douglas gets to his feet, and he spits on Sterling! [crowd boos louder] [Douglas checks on Thomas in the corner, to see if he is ok. Thomas nods assent, and The Maulers drag Sterling to his feet, then take him over with a double suplex. Douglas drags Sterling to his feet, and presses him over his head, then drops him gut first onto Thomas' knee! The crowd "oooohs" loudly as Sterling rolls around the mat in pain. Thomas grabs Sterling by the legs, and turns him over into a boston crab, and Douglas runs to the ropes, and legdrops the back of Sterling's head. The crowd starts to throw things into the ring...] AO: The crowd looks like they might riot! TK: Look! Here comes Jinsei Tenru! [The crowd cheers as Jinsei Tenru storms the ring from the back. He blasts Douglas with his title belt, then nails Thomas. The crowd cheers, and starts a "Tenru! Tenru!" chant. Jinsei drops his belt, and grabs Thomas, and whips him to the ropes. Thomas comes off, and Tenru catches him with a series of palm blows. Thomas stands there dazed, and Tenru sends him to the mat with an enzugiri. Douglas gets to his feet, and he charges Tenru, who catches him with a spinning backfist. Douglas staggers back, and Tenru charges, nailing Douglas with a russian sickle lariat! Douglas goes over the top rope to the floor, and Tenru walks over to grab his belt. Sterling gets to his feet, and both men see each other. Sterling raises his fists, ready to fight, but Tenru points to The Maulers, then exits the ring. He raises his belt over his head, then leaps off the apron, and smashes it on Douglas's head...] AO: This has turned into a free-for all! Douglas & Tenru fighting on the outside, Thomas & Sterling on the inside... TK: Sterling grabs Thomas, and drags him to his feet...DDT! [crowd "oooohs"] He spiked him hard! He drags him back up...Swinging neckbreaker! [Tenru grabs Nishi's chair, and throws into into the ring, then gets nailed from behind by Douglas...] AO: Sterling whips Thomas to the ropes...Spinning backbreaker! [crowd "ooohs"] Sterling goes to the corner, and climbs to the ropes....DIVING HEADBUTT!!! [crowd "oooohs"] Cover... ...1... ...2... KICKOUT!!! [crowd applauds] TK: Sterling gets to his feet, and he drags Thomas up. He swings, and Thomas catches the arm, and drops down into an armbar! [crowd "ooohs"] He pulls back hard on the arm! [Botan asks Sterling if he submits, but he refuses. Tenru, after ramming Douglas head first to the post, puts Sterling's foot on the ropes, then points it out to Botan. Botan makes Thomas break...] AO: Tenru is playing an important part of this match. However, the way he and Douglas are brawling, will they be able to wresltle later? TK: Good question Akira. Sterling gets to his feet, and Thomas moves in, catching him with a european uppercut. Thomas grabs Sterling by the arm, and maneuvers him into a hammerlock. Sterling catches Thomas with a swift back elbow to the eye, and he breaks the hold. Sterling turns around, and levels Thomas with a roundhouse right hand! [crowd "oooohs"] [Security finally moves in on Douglas & Tenru, and force them apart. They start to drag Douglas backstage, while restraining Tenru...] AO: Sterling drags Thomas to his feet, and pulls him over by the chair...he sets him up, then lifts him up over his head...BRAINBUSTER ON THE CHAIR!!! [crowd "oooohs"] TK: Thomas looks out of it! Sterling drags him to his feet, and starts to pummel him with headbutts. Thomas looks dazed; he's putting up no fight at all! Sterling now with a punch to the midsection, and Thomas doubles over.... AO: Sterling goes to the corner, and climbs to the top rope. He sets himself, then comes off...KNEEDROP TO BACK OF THOMAS' HEAD, DRIVING HIM FACE FIRST INTO THE CHAIR!!! [crowd "oooohs" loudly] Cover... ...1... ...2... ...3...!!! **ding** **ding** **ding** [crowd applauds as "Ironhead" cranks on the PA] Nishi: The winner of this match...ALAN STERLING!!! [crowd applauds again] TK: Impressive win for the debuting Sterling. AO: He beat that SHVF scum! That's all the matters to me! TK: We have to take a short break, then we'll be back with more action!
