SJPW House Show — Tokyo Club Atom (6/6/04)

Jun 8, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
June 6th, 2004 Tokyo Club Atom: 230 fans (Sellout) 1) "Dragon Fury" Minobu Tetsuhara & "Firefly" Horo Atashi def Dragon Mask & Toramusha (7:42) when Tetsuhara made Dragon Mask submit to a Triangle Choke. 2) El Santo Azteca def Yatoro Hori (12:22) with the Aztec Suplex. 3) Dynamite Boy & Eiji Kid Shimura def Tsutomu Kobayashi & Botan Nakatoni (9:03) when Dynamite Boy pinned Nakatoni after the Burudoggu Attack. 4) Noboru "Raging Bull" Nishida def MAMMOTH Kagawa (5:15) after an Exploder. 5) Masashi "LIghtning" Lanther & Jiraiya def Despair & Super Bakemono (14:27) when Lanther pinned Bakemono after a Lanther Bomb. - The newcomers Dragon Mask & Toramusha made a good showing in their debut, but their inexperience cost them in the end against the Atashi & Tetsuhara. - Azteca's quest to earn a shot at Despair's Gunryo belt continued, as he battled former SMASH JHW champion Yatoro Hori in a fast paced match. Hori scored a half dozen near falls on the luchador, but in the end, Azteca's vast experience paid off. - Before their match with the debuting Nagasaki Bulldogs, Crush Crew member Botan Nakatoni stated that after they "Beat these rookie punks", they would petition CEO Yamashita to let them represent SJPW against the Visual Spirits. Needless to say, the Bulldogs too exception to that, and pinned Nakatoni after their version of the old Hart Attack double team move - In a battle of Team Tiger powerhouses, the rookie Kagawa faced off against the more experienced Nishida. The two had a *very* stiff match, trading elbows and lariats on more than one occasion. The end came when Nishida avoided the MAMMOTH Charge!, and Kagawa slammed into the corner, staggering out into an Exploder by Nishida. - Lanther scored a big upset over the man who took his NCWF title from him in a vicious fight. Team Raiden and the DoD fought tooth and nail, hitting at least a dozen high spots in and out of the ring. After stunning Bakemono with a variation of Total Elimination, Jiraiya dropkicked Despair to the floor, then dove over the ropes with a wicked corkscrew plancha. Lanther then pinned Bakemono with his patented Lanther Bomb. After the match, Lanther told Bakemono to shine the NCWF title, because he would take it back at Zenmen Sensou, no matter what the cost.
