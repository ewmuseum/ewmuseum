SJPW House Show — Tokyo Jiyugaoka Station Square Shoji Town SAKURA BANK Parking Lot (8/29/04)

Aug 31, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sunday, August 29th, 2004 Tokyo Jiyugaoka Station Square Shoji Town SAKURA BANK Parking Lot: 600 fans (Sellout) Super Japan held a big weekend show after The Zone! taping, as a way of pushing the big J*STAR event in America. The show was sold out, and the promotion booked extra matches just for the fans: 1) Tsunami def Mascara Negra (10:15) after a brainbuster. 2) Noboru "Raging Bull" Nishida & MAMMOTH Kagawa def Junya Toroyama & Super Bakemono (11:27) after Kagawa hit the MAMMOTH Charge! on Bakemono 3) "Black Wasp" Akira Saito def Fanta Boy (9:22) following his ''Will O' Wasp". 4) Kenichi Kubota & Yatoro Hori def Gregor Davinsky & Sergei Baranov (12:50) after Kubota pinned Baranov after the Kurashaa Driver. 5) "GAIJIN Extreme" Jun Suzuki def Jun Kikuchi (11:02) after the Aloha Extreme. 6) "Firefly" Horo Atashi & "All-Star" Joey Darren def Takahiro KEOKI & Masashi "Lightning" Lanther (13:35) after Atashi pinned KEOKI following a superkick. 7) Main Event: SMLL International Trios Title defense: El Gato Loco, El Muerte Rojo & Sangre Guererro def Despair, Tsutomu Kobayashi & Botan Nakatoni (15:06) after Loco pinned Despair after hitting the Tornillo Gato. - Tsunami continues his comeback trail to facing Darren for the IICW cruiserweight title, defeating Mascare Negra with a brainbuster in a good opening match. - Team Tiger's Nishida & Kagawa took on the outcast Toroyama & Bakemono. Even though Toroyama has asked for a reconciliation between Team Tiger & the Dokyu Dojo, Nishida & Kagawa showed no mercy to the outcasts. Nishida was extremely stiff with Toroyama, who gave as good as he got. Even though he was the smallest man in the ring, Bakemono showed his fighting spirit, taking the fight to both Nishida & Kagawa with various kicks, especially the koppo kick he learned from Lanther. Although he put up a good fight, Bakemono was no match for Kagawa's MAMMOTH Charge!, and was easily pinned after Kagawa caught him with that move. - Akira Saito (not to be confused with the Akira Saito of the NorthPac Coalition) made his debut against the flamboyant Fanta Boy. Though Fanta did his best to distract Saito, it didn't work, and soon enough, Fanta Boy fell victim to his ''Will O' Wasp", a variation of the Diamond Dust. - In a slight preview of the upcoming Gunryo Gauntlet tag match at J*STAR, SMASH Attack took on the EuroPack in tag team competition. At first, Davinsky & Baranov had the upper hand, beating Hori until he was almost unconscious. After a desperation superkick stunned Baranov, Hori tagged in Kubota, who unleashed a series of lariats on Baranov. Davinsky tried to make the save, only to fall prey to a Doctor Bomb. Kubota then hit his Kurashaa Driver, scoring the first win for SMASH Attack since they formed a team! - Originally scheduled to face Brody Thunder on this show prior to signing his J*STAR contest with Joe Petrow, CEO Yamashita replaced Thunder with Jun Kikuchi, who volunteered to take Thunders place. Suzuki was livid, and spent the first five minutes of the match mauling Kikuchi. Kikuchi made a comeback six minutes in, and nine minutes in, almost pinned Suzuki after hitting Thunder's Widowmaker on him! Suzuki went crazy, and after thumbing Kikuchi in the eye, merciless hammered him with a series of lariats before hitting the Aloha Extreme for the win. After the match, Suzuki tried to do more damage to Kikuchi, but was driven off by Nishida & Kagawa. - The two men who will face off to determine the first Sedei-X champion faced off in tag team competition. KEOKI & Lanther worked well together, both men being in Team Tiger. While Atashi & Darren didn't initially work as well together, they soon caught on after a miscue almost cost them the match. They then started tagging in and out more, and after Darren hit the All-Star Driver on KEOKI, tagged in Atashi, then knocked Lanther off the apron. While Atashi superkicked KEOKI to score the win, Darren went to the top rope, and hit a corkscrew plancha to the floor on Lanther. - The main event was a wild title defense by the SMLL Trios champions against the Disciples of Despair. The Blood Merchants have declared war against the DoD, and this was the first salvo in the conflict. All three luchadors did their best to keep Despair in the ring as much as possible, double and even triple teaming him. Things went against them when Despair was able to tag in Kobayashi, who was able to take out all three men with lariats. He tagged in Nakatoni, who attempted to out wrestle Muerte Rojo, but soon learned the luchador was a better mat wrestler, falling prey to the Red Death Driver #2. Despair came back in and hammered Rojo, taking him out with a Lanther Bomb. Sangre Guererro hit the ring, Missile Dropkicking the Gunryo Champion. Kobayashi tried to help Despair, only to catch Guererro's Red Mist to the face. Nakatoni also caught the red mist to the face, before Guererro took Despair down with a Tombstone Piledriver. He then tagged in Gato Loco, who hit a twisting moonsault, scoring the pin for his team. After the match Despair tried to take out the Blood Merchants with his patented chair, only for all three men to dropkick it into his face, Despair rolled out of the ring, cursing as his face bleed from numerous cuts. He swore that at J*STAR, he was going to make Muerte Rojo & Sangre Guererro pay for embarrassing him.
