Hour One =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [The camera opens on the Osaka Furitsu Gym. The crowd is cheering loudly. The camera pans across the crowd, then focuses on the broadcast position, where Eddie Rose & Akira Ono sit....]
Eddie Rose: Hello everyone! I'm Eddie Rose, and this is Akira Ono. We are now two weeks away from our one year anniversary, and one month away from the second Battlecade!
Akira Ono: A wonderful event to be sure. ER: You don't like Battlecade, or the Battle Arts division much, do you? AO: Not really. Battle Arts has some good men, like Steel Serpent,
Tiger Claw, Toshiaka Takada and Sung Kim, but the rest are scum.
ER: Then I guess you're not too happy with the Battlecade Brawl starting tonight, are you? AO: Not really. ER: What about the joint IIWF/SJPW King of the Cruisers tournament? AO: It's a good venture, but I daresay that the SJPW representatives will somehow be screwed over. I have this feeling...
ER: I think you're being paranoid. I guess you're more concerned about the Yakuza & Masa Juroda than I thought.
AO: I'M NOT CONCERNED WITH THOSE BAKA! The Bounty Hunters have assured me that they will deal with Juroda & Tazaka tonight, and I feel confident that the Bounty Hunters will prevail.
ER: Then why are you going to ringside with them? AO: To keep an eye on Geto. His interference is why the Genyosha lost in the first place! They had the Yakuza defeated! But no, they had to show how much they lack honor, and CHEAT!
ER: Calm down Akira! Don't get all riled up over what you can't change. AO: I'm fine Eddie-san. Hardin & Thunder will avenge the loss of face I, and the Genyosha lost last week. I've paid them very well to deal with these criminals, and they gave me their word that all will be well soon enough.
ER: Before we continue, I would like to wish everyone a Happy
Easter & Passover, especially those SJPW wrestlers who celebrate this holiday, but are still participating in tonight's event.
AO: How touching. Can we get to the first match all ready? ER: Certainly Akira. "Tiger" Tong Po faces your former partner,
Mitsunori Takizawa, in the first round of the Battlecade Brawl.
AO: Tong Po will hopefully beat Takizawa. He always had trouble with Martial Artists! ER: I'm sure Tong Po appreciates your support. Let's go to ringside.... [camera cuts to ringside] Ring Announcer: This first contest if a round one match in the
Battlecade Brawl! [crowd cheers] It is set for one fall, 20 minute time limit, and is to be fought under Battle Arts rules!
Introducing first, weighing in at 300 lbs...TAK-I-ZA-WA MIT-SU-NO-RI!!! ["Too Old To Rock & Roll, Too Young To Die" comes over the pa, and
Takizawa steps out on the runway, a sneer on his face. He slowly walks down the runway, his body scars glistening in the spotlight on him. He enters the ring, and faces one corner, where he smacks salt between his hands for luck, then turns to face the center of the ring...]
ER: Takizawa seems to prefer that ritual whenever he can. AO: He thinks it will bring him luck. It never worked against
Shinjisaki, so why should it work now?
Ring Announcer: His opponent, from Muang Nan, Thailand, weighing in at 228 lbs..."TI-GER" TONG PO!!!
["Demon Warriors/Final Kombat" blares over the pa, and the crowd cheers as Tong Po steps onto the runway. He slaps hands with the fans as he heads to the ring, but never takes his eyes off
Takizawa. He enters the ring, and proceeds to go to each corner, scaling them, then raising his arms in the air...]
ER: Tong Po seems pretty confident tonight. AO: And well he should. I gave him some tips on Takizawa's weak spots! [Both men face in the center of the ring, while the referee gives them final instructions. Both men nod to each other, then step back as the ref calls for the bell. Takizawa lunges forward, swinging his fist, but Po side steps, and takes Takizawa to the mat with a legsweep. Takizawa rolls to his feet, and Po nails him with an enzugiri, sending him staggering back to the ropes. Po moves on him, and hits a spinning backfist before attempting an irish whip. Takizawa reverses, and scoops Po up as he comes off the ropes, then slams him hard to the mat. Po sits up, holding his back, and Takizawa grabs him by the back of the head, then lands a punch to the side of his head. He drags Po to his feet, and rakes the face. Po staggers to the ropes, blinded, and Takizawa charges, sending Po over the top rope to the floor with a clothesline!]
ER: Po had a good start, but Takizawa's still stronger than him, even at his age. AO: Bah! He's an old buffoon, and soon enough, Tong Po will have him where he wants him! [Takizawa goes out to the apron, and comes off with a forearm shot to Po's back. Po leans over the railing, and Takizawa grabs his head, and smacks it on the railing. Po retaliates with a back elbow, then another. Takizawa doubles over, and Po grabs him by the head, and rams his head onto the railing! The crowd cheers, and
Po hops up onto the apron, and goes into the ring. He runs to the opposite ropes, charging towards Takizawa. As Takizawa turns. Po leaps into a Space Flying Tiger Drop, and both men go down. The crowd 'oooooohs' loudly, and some people start chanting Tong Po's name...]
ER: Risky suicidal move on Po's part. But he nailed Takizawa good with it! AO: Takizawa is in over his head here! [Both men rise to their feet, and Takizawa attempts a thrust kick, but Po catches the leg, and drives an elbow onto the knee. The crowd 'ooooohs' as Takizawa leans on the railing in pain, and Po, still holding the leg, takes Takizawa to the floor with a
Dragonscrew legwhip. The crowd 'ooooohs' again, and Takizawa rolls on the floor, clutching his leg...]
ER: Ouch! Po really did a job on the leg there! AO: Takizawa's joints just aren't what they used to be. Too bad for him [chuckles] [Po goes back to the apron, and climbs up to the top rope. He sets himself, and as Takizawa gets to his feet, Po comes off the top, and slams into Takizawa with a missle dropkick that sends him over the railing into the crowd! The crowd cheers loudly...]
ER: What a dropkick! He's got Takizawa beat for sure now! AO: Of course he does! [Po leans over the railing, and drags Takizawa to his feet. He pulls his stunned opponent over the railing, then rolls him into the ring. Po starts to climb up on the apron, when suddenly, someone hits him from behind with a bamboo stick! The crowd
'ooohs' as Po drops to the floor, and the man spins Po around, and
DDT's him to the floor! The crowd 'oooohs' again, which turn into boos as the man turns towards them, revealing Jinsei Shakanuzi!]
AO: NO! NO! NO! Damn Him! ER: What's worse is that he can get away with this during a Battle
Arts match! [Jinsei drags Po to his feet, and smacks him across the face. He rolls him into the ring, hops up on the apron, springs to the top rope, the comes off with a flying legdrop onto Po's chest! The crowd 'oooohs' again, and someone throws their drink into the ring. Jinsei drags Po up once more, then lifts him up in the air, before dropping down into a vicious brainbuster! The crowd boos louder, and Jinsei rolls out of the ring, yelling at the fans as he leaves...]
ER: This is sick. Po is out cold in the ring. He's finished. AO: Shakanuzi will pay for this! Po had the match won! [Takizawa, who was watching all of this, rises to his feet, slightly favoring his leg. He drags Po to his feet, and locks his arm around his throat. He lifts him up off the mat, then sends him crashing down with a chokeslam! The crowd 'oooohs' as Takizawa gets to his feet, and looks up at the broadcast position. He draws his thumb across his throat, then drags Po to his feet again. He whips Po to the ropes, catching him as he comes off, and hitting his patented Cradle DDT. The crowd 'oooohs' as Po's head is spiked to the mat, and Takizawa covers him easily for the three count]
ER: What a travesty. AO: Shakanuzi had better watch his back later tonight. Tong Po will not let this go unavenged!
Ring Announcer: The winner of this match...MITSUNORI TAKIZAWA!!! [crowd mainly boo, though some fans actually cheer] ER: Po has got to be injured after all that. AO: Definitely. The medical team is moving in right now! [As Takizawa exits the ring, sneering at the fans, the medical team hits the ring. Takizawa, who is still limping slightly, refuses medical assistance as he heads to the back. The medical team place a neck brace around Po's neck, then move him onto a gurney, and take him to the back, the fans cheering him all the way]
ER: Po could be out of it for awhile. AO: This is sick. Shakanuzi is jealous because the time ran out last week, when he almost beat Po. He doesn't want Po to win the
Brawl, because if he did, he could possibly beat Takada to become champion
ER: Hopefully, we can find out more about Tong Po's condition later in this program. AO: I hope he is all right. He didn't deserve this! ER: I know Akira. AO: Shakanuzi is becoming as bad as the Yakuza & Juroda! ER: This next match is a very special one Akira as it is a first round match in the IIWF King of the Cruisers Tournament. Sixteen of the best cruiserweights in the world, including SJPW's Kabuki Kid,
Middleweight champion Joey Rappaport and Tiger Claw who is in as an independant, will compete to find out who is the top cruiserweight is. We would like to thank the IIWF for allowing us to co-promote this great event.
AO: Ha! They should be thanking us. However, Kabuki Kid and Rappaport are solid choices, they should represent SJPW well. I am glad that the Syndicate scum Tiger Claw does not have an affliation with our great promotion.
ER: Never one to mince words are you Akira? AO: No Eddie-san. ER: Let's go to the ring. [Cut to ringside. An "IIWF KING OF THE CRUISERS TOURNAMENT" as the ring announcer stands in the middle of the ring.]

STE-VENS!! ["Dammit" by Blink 182 plays and the fans cheer as Xavier leads

Oakley sunglasses, and gives them to a young fan. He then gets up on the apron, and slingshots himself into the ring, and tests the ropes.]

Ronnie D once again rolling out of the ring, and he is right next to Xavier.
[Ronnie D turns to Xavier, looking stunned, and she hauls back, and slaps him hard across the face and he spins around as the fans cheer. Ronnie D holds his face in pain, and looks at Xavier. In the ring, Stevens leaps off the farside ropes, and the fans 'ohhhh' and

Ronnie D.]

Stevens, and Ronnie D is down and hurt.
