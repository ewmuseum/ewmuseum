Lineup for The Zone! 8/27/04

Aug 18, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With J*STAR on the horizon, SJPW is holding a short televised card. The following matches have been signed for this event: Junior Struggle! Block B: Takahiro KEOKI vs "Kyoto Tiger" Takayoshi Asakura KEOKI is on a roll, being one of the last three men in the Darwin Cup. He's also riding high on his big win against Wolf Jager. Asakura went from being the upset kid in the Baba Cup to going on a major losing streak since losing the finals of said Baba Cup to Kentaro Kawasaki. Will Asakura make a big comeback, or will KEOKI take Block B, and go on to face Block A winner & J*STAR rep "Firefly" Horo Atashi to determine our first Sedei-X champion? GPH Tag League: Van Wild & Scott Starring vs Junya Toroyama & Super Bakemono Right before his next title defense in Rumble Pro, Van Wild will team with his partner Scott Starring against the new tandem of Junya Toroyama & Super Bakemono. Van Wild & Starring are undeafeated in the GPH tag league, with only a draw against The Visual Spirits marring their record. Will Van Wild make it to his Rumble Pro title defense in one piece? Toroyama & Bakemono are unbeaten to date, but their last win over Knox Roberts & Tommy Bedford was tainted thanks to the interference of Gunryo Champion Despair. Will Despair interfere again? Will Roberts & Bedford seek to even the score? How does Toroyama feel about Despair's interference? And did Despair's DoD ally, Bakemono, know Despair was going to interfere? Gunryo Championship Match: El Santo Azteca (Challenger) vs Despair (Champion) The veteran luchador finally gets his shot at Despair. Azteca has worked his way up to contention, and now stands poised to take down Despair. He made Despair tap after he beat KEOKI at Zenmen Sensou, and then made him tap again on a later show. The champion is reeling from a major loss to Brody Thunder at TSTL 4, and his committments to NEO Pro, Rumble Pro, and Superslam. Will he be on his game, or will the aging Luchador capture gold once more? Main Event Trios Scramble Match: "Ecchi Kid" Kentaro "H-Bomb" Kawasaki, "GAIJIN Extreme" Jun Suzuki & "Living Legend" Tommy Fury vs "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder, Tasogare no Prince & "Dragon Fury" Minobu Tetsuhara This match promises to be an explosive one. Brody Thunder & Jun Suzuki hate each other with a passion. Suzuki wants to be the man to retire Thunder, but now Thunder could be retired by Joe Petrow at J*STAR, and Suzuki is fuming. Will he make sure Thunder can't win against Petrow by hurting him here, or will Thunder make it to J*STAR in one piece? And what of Joe Petrow? Will he appear again? In addition, Kentaro Kawasaki's new apathy and disrespect for all that Puroresu stands for has drawn the attention of Tasogare no Prince. TnP has beaten Kawasaki in a singles match before he won the title, but Kawasaki is riding high after beating Masashi "Lightning" Lanther on the last The Zone! The big question is: Will Kawasaki be motivated in this match, or just nap on the ring apron? Lastly, Tetsuhara has become the "shooter" of Team Tiger. He all ready holds a win over Tommy Fury in singles competition. Will Fury make Tetsuhara pay for his loss, or will Tetsuhara prove once again that he not only represents the spirit of the Burning Road, but that he deserves his spot on Team Tiger? For all this and more, be sure to catch SJPW's The Zone! on the International Channel, Time Warner channel 500, Friday night, August 27th, live from the Battle Sphere Tokyo, 11:30 pm EST!
