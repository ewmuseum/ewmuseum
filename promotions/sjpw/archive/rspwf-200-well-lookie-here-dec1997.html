----- [RSPWF 200] Well, lookie here... (Dec 14, 1997, 12:00:00 AM) ----- [name withheld] wrote: > Dan Kauffman: Where the hell have you disappeared to? The Syndicate > wants to rough you up a bit more... Come on... Please? [name withheld], Dan Kauffman is alive and well in SJPW. After foolishly ticking off the likes of Derek Mota with a recent post, and finding the time to start feuds with *THREE* men at the same time, and those men being 3 of SJPW's best [WWGP world champion Lance Smith, "The Perfectionist" Nik Nyce, and the soon returning Survival], Dan will be facing "Badboy" Randy Acorn tonight in Arena Mexico. I'll be posting that card to rspwf soon. Now if The Syndicate would like complimentary tickets to our next ppv in Madison Square Garden [the promotion is on a long US tour at the moment], just let me know, and we'll gladly accomodate the four of you :) As for Petrow, you're not the only one who wants a piece of the "Dark Saviour" He didn't suffer enough for interfering in a certain match between two men who shall remain nameless :) [name withheld] SJPW ----- [RSPWF 200] Well, lookie here... [name withheld] would never turn > down something that's free. Sure. Feel free to email me if you want about the appearance of the Syndicate then. Our current Battle Arts champ, Steel Serpent, is someone I'm sure Tiger Claw might find worthy of facing somewhere down the line :) > Hehe... Looks like we're all going to have to chase him about, huh? Too bad you're not still in IIWF [and I'm not in it either] or else we could rain on his and Triple M's good times right now :) [name withheld] SJPW
