News & Notes 7/8/04

Jul 11, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tiger Kikuchi was asked by reporters if he had anything to say regarding the Dokyu Dojo show in Osaka on July 7th "It is wonderful to see such more wrestling taking place in different parts of Japan. It's good for business, and good for the fans. It's a win-win situation." When asked about the comments made by Super Mask, Kikuchi said: "Super Mask? I remember the man well. He was once a top star in Japan, but he always held a grudge after he stopped main eventing when Lanther, Ono, Matsumoto, Takizawa and I became the next big stars in Japan. It still bothers him that I beat him in our best of five series, when I was the "Black Tiger". As for his comments about me starting a "war" with the Dokyu Dojo, that is utter nonsense. My issue is with Junya Toroyama, and Toroyama alone. He and Super Mask are the ones claiming this is an issue between Team Tiger and Dokyu, not I. That just goes to show you how far Toroyama has fallen from grace. He betrays the school that trained him, gave him employment in one of the best promotions in all of Japan, and he shows nothing but disrespect to those that helped him get started. And to include Super Mask, who always hated Team Tiger from day one, well, that shows you how little sense of honor Toroyama truly has" Kikuchi was also asked about KEOKI's return to the ring after being told to sit out three weeks to recuperate "KEOKI-san is very headstrong, feeling he needs to prove himself. He once more signed a waiver to face Tsunami, and needed help in the locker room area afterwards. I immediately went to Yamashita, and KEOKI will not wrestle again until the 17th. That means his other matches will take place later than the rest, but that is Yamashita-sama's fault for booking him into this tournament so soon after his injuries" When asked about the rumor that Team Tiger my leave Super Japan, Kikuchi said: "Yamashita-san and I are discussing Team Tiger's future involvement with Super Japan. I have been disappointed with some of his decisions, and we are in discussions to determine how to rectify my grievances. Yamashita-san has actually made a proposal I found most promising, and we may have an announcement in the very near future regarding that proposal. That is all I can say for now" Takahiro KEOKI taped an interview with Ken Otaku regarding his current health, the feud with Despair, and other subjects. We are broadcasting it here, as he is back home recuperating after his match with Tsunami. Get well soon KEOKI! [TAPED JULY 1, 2004] [Higashi-shi, Osaka, Japan.] OTAKU - "Good evening, SJPW fans. We come to you tonight with a very special interview segment. With his first words since Zenmen Sensou, Takahiro KEOKI will speak about the match against Despair -- and the brutal ending that caused him to receive multiple stitches on both sides of his mouth. KEOKI-san, thank you for allowing us here tonight." [The shot pans over to Takahiro KEOKI, who looks very _unlike_ his normal self. Numerous cuts and lacerations cover his body, dark blue and purple bruises are randomly on his arms and legs, and his mouth is _disgusting_. With two deep gashes on either side of it, there are anywhere from ten to twelve stitches holding them together. Though in considerable pain, KEOKI manages to smile and nod his head, wincing in pain a little as he shifts in his chair.] KEOKI - "Thanks for having me." OTAKU - "The first question, one that has to be on every fan's mind is: how are you recuperating?" KEOKI - "The injury gets better with each passing day. Though I don't look like I'm feeling better, the pain is a lot better than it was the night of Zenmen Sensou. The only thing that I don't like about this whole injury is the fact that I've been required to stay out of the ring for three weeks -- it's extremely hard to not get in the ring!" OTAKU - "Have you been allowed to train at all?" KEOKI - "No. Sadly, the doctors have told me any strain to my face might reaggravate the injury. I'm allowed to do some walking and light jogging, but other than that it's been a lot of rest and relaxation. As long as the injury is given the right amount of time to heal, I should be able to resume a normal training schedule around the 12th." OTAKU - "That's very promising." [KEOKI nods his head.] OTAKU - "So why go through all of the pain, KEOKI-san? Why did you decide to go after Despair with such disregard for your own body? What made you target a Gunryo competitor, instead of a competitor within the Sedei-X Division?" KEOKI - "I think it's been well documented, Otaku-sama. Despair thought he could utilize SJPW as his own personal playground. With Super Bakemono and his disciples behind him, Despair took what Kikuchi-sama worked so hard to create and destroyed it. As a member of Team Tiger and a graduate of Chiimu Tora Dojo, I could not allow that." OTAKU - "But why take it to such extremes?" KEOKI - "I was willing to do _whatever_ it took to get to Despair. When I asked Yamashita-sama about challenging Despair, he told me the only way I could do so was to earn my shot at him. So I did what I had to do. I fought a Gunryo Rules tag team match... I defeated Brody Thunder... and I accepted the Barbed Wire Match at Zenmen Sensou." OTAKI - "Looking back, do you regret your decisions?" [KEOKI shakes his head.] KEOKI - "Not in the least. If I were presented with the same situation, I would do it _again_. I don't back down when I'm disrespected, no matter if it favors me or not. There was no way that I was going to allow Despair's continued disrespect -- even if I was the _only_ one from Team Tiger to step up and fight the Disciples." OTAKU - "It sounds as if you've got some problems with your fellow allies." KEOKI - "Not at all! To be honest, I don't blame _any_ of Team Tiger for not stepping up to the challenge. This fight was _very_ brutal, and might have ended my career... I can't hold my friends responsible for not wanting to take that chance." OTAKU - "You are very loyal to Chiimu Tora Dojo and Team Tiger." KEOKI - "They've given me the chance to live out my dream... I owe them my loyalty." [KEOKI manages another smile.] OTAKU - "I know this isn't the best question to ask, but how do you feel about losing at Zenmen Sensou? You were very confident of your abilities, but ended up on the short end of the stick. Do you feel that you've failed Team Tiger?" KEOKI - "No. I feel as if I've won." OTAKU - "Could you elaborate?" KEOKI - "I stepped up to the threat known as the Disciples of Despair. I walked into _his_ kind of match. I did _not_ tap out like he guaranteed I would. I _survived_ the Gunryo Division. Whether he wants to admit it or not, Despair _does_ respect me. He knows that if he continues in his ways, I _will_ be back to fight him once again!" OTAKU - "You are truly a young lion, KEOKI-san. What's next for you?" KEOKI - "I've recently signed a contract with Pro Wrestling INFINITY, in the hopes of bringing the honor of Team Tiger to another Japanese promotion. I've also signed on to fight in the United States, as part of the Super 8 Tournament. But right here in SJPW is where I'll call home... and I'm going to work to become Sedei-X Champion." OTAKU - "There's a rough road ahead to attain that goal, KEOKI-san. The Junior Struggle! Tournament has many of SJPW's best competitors with the same goal in mind." [KEOKI nods.] KEOKI - "I'm well aware of the men involved. However, I think I've got what it takes to win. All of SJPW has seen what I'm capable of doing when I'm focused. No matter who stands in my way, I will give 100% effort in the ring." OTAKU - "We wish you the best of luck, KEOKI-san... and we thank you for the time tonight." KEOKI - "Thanks again." [The shot zooms back in on Ken Otaku.] OTAKU - "For SJPW, I'm Ken Otaku. We'll see you on The Zone!" [With that, we fade.]
