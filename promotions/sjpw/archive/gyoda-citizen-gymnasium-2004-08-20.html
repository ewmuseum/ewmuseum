SJPW House Show — Gyoda Citizen Gymnasium (8/20/04, evening)

Aug 22, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Friday, August 20th, 2004 Gyoda Citizen Gymnasium: 1,000 fans (sellout) Evening event Closing out the week long run to Saitama, Super Japan held a second event at the Citizen Gym for the fans. The gym was packed with fans, eager to see their favorite stars in action! 1) GPH Tag League: KANJI Sakaigama & "CRIMSON" Jin Hiroto def Tsutomu Kobayashi & Botan Nakatoni (12:35) after Hiroto pinned Nakatoni following a Yakuza kick. 2) Mr. Smiley Mask def Yatoro Hori (8:50) following Sh*t Happens. 3) GPH Tag League: Van Wild & Scott Starring def Dynamite Boy & Eiji Kid Shimura (11:48) following Van Wild superplexing Starring onto Dynamite Boy for the pinfall. 4) Jet Jaguar III def Super Bakemono (17:19) following a Spiral Bomb. 5) GPH Tag League: "Hard" Knox Roberts & "Terrible" Tommy Bedford def Frankie "The Thumper" Douglas & Doug Thomas (12:28) after Roberts pinned Douglas after a DDT. 6) IICW Cruiserweight Title Match: "All-Star" Joey Darren (champ) def Mascara Negra (challenger) (18:18) after the All-Star Driver. - In the first of 3 GPH Tag Team League events, the Visual Spirits took on the Crush Crew, and found themselves the fan favorites. As much as KANJI got pissed at the fan response, they continued to support the Spirits over the Crush Crew. KANJI took a bit of a beating at the hands of the larger Kobayashi, who stunned him with a sick snap powerbomb. He tagged in Nakatoni, who tried to wear KANJI down with some backbreakers, then put him in a texas cloverleaf. Hiroto made the save with a Yakuza kick, and KANJI was able to tag out. To thunderous applause, Hiroto hit the ring, and punished Nakatoni with a series of viciously stiff kicks to his body. Hiroto then hit the ropes, and rebounded off with a Yakuza kick that nearly took Nakatoni's head off! Hiroto then nailed Kobayashi with a Yakuza kick that sent him to the floor, allowing Hiroto to hit a second Yakzua kick on Nakatoni, Hiroto easily pinning him for the three count. - Mr. Smiley Mask brought the Smileymobile to Gyoda, and frustrating the luckless Hori with his antics. The crowd bellowed with laughter as Smiley repeatedly went into his heroic pose after hitting a move. Hori finally lost it, and attempted a superkick, but Smiley caught his leg, and hit a dragonscrew legwhip. He put Hori into a single leg crab, getting high elevation on his leg. Hori wouldn't give up, but he was pretty hurt after that. Smiley then hit an incredible enzugiri on Hori, went to the top, hit Sh*t Happens, and drove off in the Smileymobile, throwing out sesame candy to the children in the crowd. - In the second GPH Tag Team League match, Van Wild & Starring took on the two men they faced in singles competition earlier in the day, the Nagasaki Bulldogs. The Bulldogs were on their game early on, double teaming Van Wild with a series of moves, and scoring three near pinfalls on him. Finally, Dynamite Boy went for his top rope headbutt, and Van Wild moved, and tagged in Starring. Starring sprang to the top rope, leaping off and hitting a flying forearm on Dynamite. He drop kicked Shimura to the floor, and then he and Starring hit a variation of the Alabama Jam on Dynamite. Starring went to the top once more and Van Wild went up with him, superplexing his own partner on top of Dynamite for the three count! - J3 took on Bakemono in singles competition, re igniting the rivalry that was the talk of Super Japan before Bakemono joined forces with Despair. Both men were all over the place, exchanging aerial moves, stiff strikes, and even a few submission holds. After hitting the Jaguar Special, J3 chose to finish off Bakemono with the Spiral Bomb, the finisher preferred by the man who trained J3, Shoji Suzuki. In a surprising show of sportsmanship, Bakemono offered J3 his hand after the match. J3 shook it, and the crowd threw a torrent of streamers into the ring. - In the final GPH tag league match on the show, Roberts & Bedford took on The Maulers. The contest was very stiff, with Roberts & Douglas brawling alot, and Bedford & Thomas doing alot of chain wrestling. The Maulers did some double team moves on Bedford, but Roberts kept breaking up the pinfall attempts. After Bedford tagged out, Roberts took out Thomas with some stiff punches (a series of rights following by a devastating left hand), then hit a DDT out of nowhere, scoring the pinfall for his team! - Darren finished the week long tour by defending his IICW title for a third time, this time taking on SMLL superstar Mascara Negra. Both men did some great chain wrestling, and Negra had Darren on the run for awhile with a series of armdrags and hiptosses, eventually taking the match outside the ring following a dropkick. After a tope through the ropes, Negra tried to whip Darren into the railing, but Darren reversed it. He chopped Negra's chest raw, then rammed his head into the steel post. Taking the match back into the ring, Darren hit the All-Star Frog Splash, but Negra kicked out! Darren looked a little frustrated, and as he went for a hurricanrana, Negra countered it with a sitout powerbomb, almost pinning the champion. Negra then went for a sunset flip, but Darren rolled out of it, and hit a Shining Wizard. He dragged Negra to his feet, and hit the All-Star Driver (his version of the flipping piledriver), pinning Negra to retain his title. After the show, the crowd was buzzing about Darren's three title defenses this week. Many felt he was pushing himself too hard to prove himself after losing Block A of Junior Struggle!. Others were wondering about Bakemono's newfound sportsmanship, and wondered if his partnership with Toroyama had anything to do with it. All in all, it was a successful tour of Saitama, and Super Japan posted to their website that they would definitely come back again in the near future.
