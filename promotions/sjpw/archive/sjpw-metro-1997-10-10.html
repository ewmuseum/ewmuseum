Metro

10 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Surrounding the ring is a gray chain link fence. Sitting at ringside and all around the ring in the large room are young people dressed in "Beatnik" fashions in beanbag chairs, benches, picnic tables, and lawn chairs. The aisleway is just a clearing between the assembled crowd and the walkway. The enteranceway is two heavy doors which are still closed. The lights go down and a blue spotlight swirls around the room and focuses on center ring where the lights project the "Metro" logo on the ring mat in flashing strobe. The camera cuts away as the crowd cheers to the makeshift desk of 4 oil drums and a large piece of wood. Sitting at the desk are Tania Goodwin and William Mercker. Goodwin is wearing a black pantsuit with a blue pinstriped shirt with black riding boots and Mercker is wearing a blue windbreaker and brown courderoy pants with green Doc Martens.] Goodwin: Hello, SJPW boozers and welcome to the hottest new show in Japan, Super Japan Pro Wrestling's own.....Metro! Alongside me is my broadcast colleague William Mercker, the man who New Japan Pro Wrestling didn't want to have humiliate their "stars". Mercker: Truer words have never been spoken. Antonio Inoki thumbed his nose at me and it was the biggest mistake he ever made. Tell me this, those goofs on Control Center had better do some background work before they make smart ass remarks about this show, because all they do is sit around and talk. Goodwin: [pats him on the shoulder] Good Bill. And what do we do? Mercker: We sit around and talk, but WE make it look cool! [snickers] Goodwin: If you say so. Tonight on Metro, we have for you an exclusive interview with the Demon Boy Ishrinku LIVE from the hospital. Sharon Akiyama couldn't handle him, but I damn sure can I'll prove it tonight. I spoke with the Demon Boy from his hospital room earlier this week, and I'll share that with you here tonight. Sharon Akiyama. Ha! Mercker: You could take her. Goodwin: Of course I could. She hasn't been in too many barbed wire matches in her career, I'll bet. Also on the show, we're going to have the Sampson brothers in action, as well as Ace Killcannon and the big Aussie, Elijah as well. And as you've all heard, I'll be interviewing the one and only "Demon Boy" Ishrinku LIVE from his hospital bed! [several members of the audience let out a "Demon Boy" scream when the hear this. Goodwin looks behind her.] They're ready...are you? Bill Mercker, Jay Estes, and everyone else is here as well and we are ready to go! Let's go to the ring! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Patty Babes Carter v. Damaro Ramirez (j) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Carter brought his style to the ring against the Luchadore from Mexico City in a fast paced, exciting match-up. Ramirez used a bit of his training from Mexico to offset Carter early, but with the support of Cherry Wonderful, Carter regained his wind and got the high impact victory with the Baby Bombshell. Winner: Patty Babes Carter (w/ the Baby Bombshell) Time: 2:13 [The camera cuts from the exit of Carter and Wonderful back to the booth and Mercker shifts somewhat uneasily in his chair as Goodwin finishes a converstion with an offstage person. She sees Mercker and sighs with ex- asperation.] Goodwin: [to him] If you want to say it, go ahead. Mercker: No, thats not necessary. Goodwin: [turns to the camera] Hello! What a win for Patty Babes Carter and did he ever need it! He's English, but what can you do? Next up in the update center is Mr. Jay Estes. Go to it, Jay. [The camera cuts to the newsroom where Jay Estes is sitting behind a large desk. Jay is wearing a red studded leather jacket and he looks like he is trying to keep a straight face.] Estes: Thank you Tania. Tell Little Billy that they didn't mean anything personal by it. And to you SJPW fans, we have some exciting news. Coming up, as a part of the Super Japan Pro Wrestling, West Coast USA tour, it seems that a Mr. Jinsei Tsumaru has signed to bring a mega PPV to the Los Angeles Coloseium. The name of this Pay Per View will be none other than "Escape From Earth". And we've been told that the main event will be, for the first time ever in the history of this sport, a 3 cage, barbed wire, exploding ring, thunderdome matchup. We don't have the participants right now, but we will be sure to let you know when we do. [The camera cuts to a split screen between Estes and Goodwin. Tania Goodwin has an incredulous look on her face.] Goodwin: [with a hint of childish wonder] How many cages? 3? And barbed wire? EXPLODING RING? Estes: Whoa sweetie. Calm down. You won't be in the match. Goodwin: Oh my god! This is the best news I've ever heard! Who's going to call the match? Estes: It's Tsumaru territory, I guess you and Mercker will. Goodwin: YES! I love this job. They didn't say who is going to be in the match? Estes: No. All they said was that it was going to be Battle Arts rules with four of SJPW's most extreme going at it. Goodwin: What are the rules? Estes: They haven't said yet. I'm sure we'll know soon. Goodwin: [slaps her hand on the table in half-hearted frustration] Well darn. I guess we will have to wait and see. [the camera cuts to a full shot] Well fans, there you have it! Escape From Earth, live on PPV from Los Angeles, California. I can NOT wait! [she dances back and forth in her chair] Mercker: [who has been silent until now] No one cares about your stupid little Battlecade thing! I came here to say one thing and that is this: I came here to SJPW to WORK, not to be disrespected by my peers. Tania Goodwin came here to WORK, not to be disrespected. Jay Estes did the same. We won't have our work slandered. If it continues, I will take action upon myself and leave the legalities to Jinsei Tsumaru. Thats all I have to say on the matter. Goodwin: Cheap hoods, police, and gunmen couldn't lick him....perhaps you shouldn't either. Let's go back to the ring. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Elijah v. Quazar (j) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The mystery man from "Sector 5" took on the big Aussie, Elijah in a match- up that would determine higher rankings for the winner. Elijah overpowered his smaller opponent from the start and took him to task with power and brute force, slamming him 10 consecutive times to get the crowd into the contest. Elijah surpisingly took to the air with a flying shoulderblock and that just about put the match away. Elijah cracked Quazar with his version of the reverse neckbreaker/jawjacker/jawbreaker called "The Ultimate" in just over one minute. Winner: Elijah (w/ The Ultimate) Time: 1:04 [The camera pans away from the exit of the big man Elijah to the booth where Goodwin is still on a high from the match announcement and Mercker is sittng with a pissed off expression on his face. He tosses a pencil on the desk and shrugs as he looks off to the side with indifference.] Goodwin: Escape from Earth! How incredible. I wonder if I can get ringside seats? Mercker: You're going to be calling the freakin' match, of course you'll have ringside seats! For Christ's sake, think before you say something. Goodwin: [looks at him with distain] Listen, just because you can't be pro- fessional about jealousy doesn't mean that I have to sit here and listen to you badmouth me- From offstage: You're on. Goodwin: [with a look of shock] We're on! [slaps on a showbiz smile and turns to the camera] Hi! We're back on SJPW Metro and we still have no word from the front office about who is going to be competeing in the big Escape from Earth matchup, but we will have more info for you in the future. You can count on that. Say what you want about Jinsei Tsumaru, he shells out for the big event. [stacks the papers in front of her] Well fans, be sure to watch the other SJPW quality TV shows. After Dark and the show that has the honor to preceed us, SJPW Ring Wars. See lots of great SJPW action on Sundays, Wednesdays, and Fridays. Mercker: [with a snort] Control Center. Goodwin: [exassperated] Let's not start anything. Ace Killcannon is rumored to be on his way to Escape from Earth, or so Ive just been told to say, and what we have right now for you is one member of Reign of Terror on his way to the ring here on Metro to do battle with UFC expert Doug Thomas. Let's go down to ringside! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Ace Kilcannon v. Doug Thomas (j) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= This match turned out to be quite even as Killcannon went after Thomas with an arsenal of high impact, aerial maneuvers while Thomas tried his hardest to take Ace down to the mat so he could utilize his submission skills to the fullest extent. Kilcannon got the crowd involved after hitting a spinning leg lariat as well as a fishermanbuster and a top rope lariat. Kilcannon dominated the match thuroughly and won with a Skytwister corkscrew press that wowed the audience. Winner: Ace Kilcannon (w/ the Skytwister press) Time: 8:57 [The camera cuts from Kilcannon leaving the ring area to Goodwin and Mercker sitting behind the table. Goodwin is fooling with her microphone wire and looks somewhat annoyed with it. When she sees that the camera is back on her, she puts the cord down and flashes her smile.] Goodwin: A big win for Ace Kilcannon here on Metro. We wish him all the luck in the world, should he sign for a match at Escape from Earth....and we all hope that he does. Mercker: Will there be a loser must give the winner a blow....DRY match? [chuckles at his own joke] Goodwin: Thank you, Soupy Sales. What we are going to do right now is take you to an exclusive interview conducted with the Demon Boy Ishrinku. Roll it, Smokey. (Camera opens on Tania Goodwin standing in a hallway in front of a door. The door has a number 121 on the front of it. Tania is dressed in a black jacket and floral print skirt.) Tania: Hello, SJPW fans. It is my pleasure to bring you this exclusive Metro interview with one of the most controversial and surprising popular wrestlers in the SJPW. Former Battle Arts champion, "Demon Boy" Ishrinku. (Tania opens the door and enters the hospital room. Sitting up in a hospital bed is Ishrinku. He looks strange in this calm setting. His nose his bandaged and both his eyes are blackended. He is wearing a hospital gown and has a blanket covering his legs. The camera sweeps over to get a shot of several large bouquets of flowers. Tania sits down in a seat next to the bed.) Tania: Hello, Ishrinku. Thank you for allowing us to speak with you. Ishrinku: It is not a problem Tania. I am so bored here. It is nice to have someone to talk to. Tania: Well, the doctors aren't giving us much time, so I'll start in on the questions. First off, when do you think you'll be able to compete in SJPW again? Ishrinku: Well, my nose is healing fine, but..(He pulls back the blanket to reveal his heavily wrapped knee.) My knee is going to take longer. The doctors are talking in months, but I am thinking in weeks. They don't know the heart of the "Demon Boy". I will be back sooner than anyone thinks. Tania: Do you have any special plans for Steel Serpent once you return? Ishrinku(A smile stretching across his face): Of course I wish to face Serpant again. I understand he too is injured after our match. Good! He must remember that the referee stopped the match, I did not surrender. I would never have given up. Next time Serpant, you will have to kill me to beat me. Tania: Do you take pleasure in hurting your opponents? Ishrinku: Is it not obvious! To me that is the ultimate victory. To leave permanent injuries and deep scars on my opponents. To know that when they lean on that cane and hobble down the street, or a woman screams because of the scars on their body, they will think of "Demon Boy". HA! Tania: Speaking of women, are you currently in a relationship with a special woman? Ishrinku: No, I scare them away! Ha! Like most men though, I hope to meet someone special. Perhaps someday, after I have won many matches and caused many injures, I will settle down. One never knows in this life. Tania: Do you ever fear career ending injury? Ishrinku: No. If I let fears and worries creep into my head then I cannot be Ishrinku. I cannot wrestle how I want to. I cannot be the competitor I wish to be. If I head fear, I would not have leaped off the top of the cage and moonsaulted through a table. I cannot be "Demon Boy" and have fear. If it happens, it happens. Then I worry about it. Tania: What wrestling school did you attend? Ishrinku: Ha! I tried out with the SJPW dojo. They told me I was no good. No discipline. Lazy. They said I lacked the dedication to be a wrestler for the great Super Japan Pro-Wrestling Federation. So I got angry. I decided to prove them wrong. That is when "Demon Boy" was born. I joined the PMX and was trained by the great Volcano Jackson. Jackson taught me how to fight, how to maim, how to bleed! Ha! I loved it! Now I am in SJPW. They didn't want me, but they got me anyhow! Ha! Tania: How old were you when you started wrestling pro? Ishrinku: This is my first year. I am 24. I started late in life, but I have made up for it! I was training when I was 22, but I didn't wrestle my first match until this year in the PMW. Tania: What do you like to do away from the ring? Ishrinku: Recover from injuries(He smiles.) I do that and I continue to train. For pleasure though, I watch and collect movies. All kinds. It doesn't matter. I am particularly proud of my collections of American and European movies. I really don't have much time for anything else. Tania: Do you like children? Ishrinku: Yes, but I scare them too! I would like to have children of my own someday, but like I said it will have to wait. Tania: And finally, what is the most outrageous matchup you'd be willing to compete in? (Ishrinku smiles and then reaches up and rubs his chin and thinks about it for several minutes.) Ishrinku: I can't think of any I wouldn't agree too. Barbed wire, glass, explosions...I have done all of it. Hmmm...I never fought in a fire match. Or a scaffold. Maybe a scaffold match over pits of fire! Ha! An electrified cage! A burning coals matc! Beds of nails! I could on and on! It would take years for me to wrestle all of the matches I wish to! Ha! (Ishrinku reaches over and grabs a bouquet of flowers and proceeds to rip the flowers apart and then throw the glass vase against the wall shattering it.) Ishrinku: Double hell, exploding wire, landmines! Ha! (A doctor comes into the room and instructs Tania that she must leave. She looks somewhat dissapointed as she leaves.) Tania: It is time for me to go Ishrinku. Thank you for your time. Ishrinku(staring off into space): Barbedwire baseball bat, Taipai death match, exploding thumb-tack.... (Fade to black) [The camera cuts from the black screen to Goodwin and Mercker sitting in the booth. Mercker is hiding his face because he is laughing so hard.] Mercker: Very good, Miss Subtlety! Goodwin: Shut up! I was good. I did that better than anyone else in SJPW could have. Mercker: Probably. I would've smacked him after ripping up those [snickers] FIRST CLASS flowers. Sent from Jinsei Tsumaru, no doubt. Goodwin: I'll give you that much. Main event. Sampson brothers. Go to the ring. Right now. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The Sampson bros. v. The Wrecking Balls (j) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Peter "The Slamming" Sampson and his brother, Brad "The Suplexing" Sampson came to the ring and matched size with the two big men from "Metropolis". Brad Sampson impressed the crowd with a good tilt-a-whirl suplex and a Russian sickle style clothesline. Peter, not to be outdone, took it to one of the WB's with a sidewalk slam and a big legdrop combo. The match got out of hand for the WBs when Brad took out WB #1 with a northern lights suplex to win the match and close out the action on Metro. [The camera cuts back to the booth where Goodwin and Mercker are sitting. Mercker still looks somewhat pissed off, even though he has mellowed out. Goodwin still looks excited after hearing about the big Escape from Earth match.] Goodwin: Well thats about it, SJPWites. We will see you next week and we promise to have more for you concerning Escape From Earth. See you then.
