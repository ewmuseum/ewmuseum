Metro

27 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Metro Continues...] TK: We are back everyone. Up next is Heaven & Hell, a promising tag team, taking on The Golden Boys. AO: They haven't spoken since arriving in SJPW, but they are definitely a tag team to watch. ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Heaven & Hell vs The Golden Boys [J] /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ Soul Taker started on Keith with a scoop slam followed by an elbowdrop. He then hit a neckbreaker, and tagged in White Angel. They hit a double dropkick, then Jerry tagged in. White Angel caught him coming in with an enzugiri, then took him to the mat with a Russian Legsweep. Angel went to the top rope, and hit a flying elbowdrop for a two count. He tagged in Soul Taker, and they hit a double flapjack. Taker then atomic dropped Jerry, before bulldogging him to the mat. He charged Keith in the corner, and knocked him off with a forearm shot, then tagged in Angel, who charged over there, and dove through the ropes with a diving headspear that sent Keith to the railing. Angel went to the top rope, and nailed a missle dropkick on the rising Jerry. He tagged in Taker, then put Jerry in a scorpion deathlock. Angel went to the top rope, and hit a guillotine legdrop. Taker caught Keith, who was trying to make the save, with a clothesline, while Angel rolled Jerry over for the three count. Winners: Heaven & Hell by pinfall. TK: Another impressive win for this exciting tag team. AO: I'll say. I think they could win the tournament. if not for teams like The Yakuzaw & Outlaws! TK: Our next match pits Juventud Sagrada against Sorai Jinbo. AO: Ack! Another luchador! All this high flying makes me sick! TK: It does not. AO: It does so... ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Juventud Sagrada vs Sorai Jinbo [J] /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ Juvi started off strong with a victory roll, then caught Jinbo with a spinning backbreaker. He dragged Jinbo up, and went for a DDT, but Jinbo countered with a Northern Lights suplex for a two count. Jinbo then hammered Juvi with some forearm shots, then went for a superkick. Juvi caught the leg, spun Jinbo around, and took him to the mat with a short arm clothesline. From there, he hit a split legged moonsault. He went to the corner, and started to stomp his boot on the mat, causing the crowd to clap in unison with each stomp. Jinbo got to his feet, only for Juvi to hit La Sagrada Superkick right on his chin! Jinbo went down hard, and Juvi sat him up, executed La Magistral Cradle, and easily got the three count. Winner: Juventud Sagrada by pinfall. TK: An impressive win for the young Sagrada. AO: I will admit he looked good, and didn't really fly all over the place. TK: He's one of the top contendors for the Welterweight title. AO: Better him than Tasogare no Prince! He's so...effeminate. TK: Akira! AO: Well, he is. Plus, he's got to be an Ainu! That, or he's an Kyuuketsuki... TK: Akira, stop insulting Tasogare no Prince! He is the Welterweight Senshuken by virtue of his skill, not his ethnic background. AO: That is so, but I mean, how does it look to have an Ainu as a champion! His people must be ecstatic, that is if they have a television amongst their entire people to watch us with! [Kikuchi's face turns red with anger] TK: Akira, if you do not stop this tirade at once, I will be forced to do something we may both regret later. [Ono feigns an innocent look on his face] AO: Oh, all right. Spoilsport! TK: This next segment is an interview with The Mandarin. This man hasn't done too well of late, and he faces an old friend of ours, Masashi "Lightning" Lanther, later tonight. AO: You mean "former" old friend. Last I recall. Lanther hates both of us... __ ___ _____________________________________________________ / |/ /__ / /________ Interview Segment: / / /|_/ / -_) __/ __/ _ \ The Mandarin /_/ /_/\__/\__/_/ \___/_______________________________________/ [The camera pans to Jonnie Adonis, who is standing in the interview area. He waits for the crowd to quiet down, then starts to speak...] Jonnie Adonis: My guest at this time is a man whose reputation is known around the world. He's held numerous titles, and is a prominent member of both the Koubousen Geijutsu & Welterweight divisions. Please help me welcome....THE MANDARIN!!!! The Mandarin strides confidently into the interview area, wearing street clothes and a pair of Vuarnet shades. He waves to a small contingent of ethnic Chinese in the crowd, then shakes Johnny's hand. Jonnie Adonis: Mandarin, welcome. Let's get right to it: Things haven't been going to well, have they? First, the double countout with Super Tengu in the Welterweight tournament, and a double knockout with Steel Serpent in the Koubousen Geijutsu min-tournament. It must be very frustrating for you right now... Mandarin: I *am* frustrated Johnny. There is an obvious bias in the officiating here in SJPW. On several occaisions in the Tengu match I had won, cleanly and obviously--yet because Tengu is a franchise, because he sells merchandise he is protected. You may have noticed that he was more than a little frustrated when we faught, and for good reason. He couldn't beat me and I wouldn't lay down for him to make a cover on. When he decided to bring the match to the outside of the ring, I was ready and I showed it. But the officials protected him. [The Mandarin pauses] Mandarin: Of course, the double-knockout with Steel Serpent was something of a surprise to me...but such things happen. Shigata ga nai, neh? Jonnie Adonis: You recently confronted Steel Serpent when he faced Kensei Tokugawa. Why did you do that? Mandarin: I was making a statement to the powers that be here in SJPW that I will not be ignored. Steel Serpent is a former champion and almost as well loved here in SJPW as Tengu. He is also a great technical martial artist. Beating such men is my method of building my reputation here. Jonnie Adonis: The two of you have similar backgrounds. Do you consider him your equal? Mandarin: We *do* have similar backgrounds, and I respect the Steel Serpent's ability. But *equal*? The Steel Serpent has some good moves...that's it. My equal? Categorically *NOT*. Jonnie Adonis: Why not? The man has been the original Battlecade Champion, then when the title became Battle Arts, he held that as well. He's had wars with men like "Demon Boy" Ishrinku, Sung Kim, Jonnie Valentine & Toshiaki Takada. Some say he could be All-Asian champion if he wanted to. How is *NOT* your equal? Mandarin: Let's get a few things straight here, Jonnie. All the people you mentioned, Ishrinku, Valentine, Takada, are all good fighters. Ishrinku deals damage like no one else. Jonnie Valentine is master of the chair shot. Sung Kim and Toshiaki Takada are both solid, if flawed martial artists. But beating them is *not* the measure of greatness you seem to think it is. Now beating all these lesser foes makes Steel Serpent a better martial artist than they are, and he is! But does that make him *my* equal? Certainly NOT! Jonnie Adonis: I see. Later tonight, you face Masashi "Lightning" Lanther, another seasoned veteran who has recently been in a slump. How do you feel about him? Is he also beneath you? Mandarin: Lanther had a time when he was great. That time is past. Is he beneath me? Certainly not in his prime, but now that he is older and growing slower...I would have to say yes. Jonnie Adonis: Interesting. The last time you competed in SJPW, fellow Triad member Taifun was here as well. Will he be returning to SJPW, or will any other Triad members ever appear? Mandarin: Taifun is currently busy making life difficult for the Trifecta in AAPW and is thus far too busy to join me here. The Triad has no plans to bring more competition to SJPW, at least currently. As to whether or not they will *ever* appear here, that is hard to say. It is very possible. Jonnie Adonis: Is there anyone you consider worthy of Triad membership here in SJPW? Mandarin: As of now, the Triad is not recruiting here, although there are some worthies that our people are watching. We may be in a position to make some offers to the very best martial artists in SJPW when we are sure that the fighters in question are deserving of our consideration. Jonnie Adonis: You heard it right here folks! Good luck later tonight... Mandarin: Thanks Jonnie! It's always a pleasure having a chance to speak to the fans! (The Mandarin waves again to the ethnic Chinese in the crowd and heads backstage. The camera then cuts back to Kikuchi & Ono in the Control Center...) TK: The Mandarin feels he is the best martial artist in all of SJPW, and plans on defeating Steel Serpent to prove it. AO: Steel Serpent is one of the best overall competitors in SJPW. Mandarin is simply jealous of his success.... TK: Our next match features Kenji Ishida & Shinji Usami, Yamashita's henchmen, against The Chainmasters... AO: These two young men have become so despicable since allying with that honorless cur! ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Ishida/Usami vs The Chainmansters [J] /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ Before the match, Yamashita announced that Ishida & Usmai would now be known as the "Corporate Dragons". The crowd booed loudly, then actually cheered as The Chainmasters attacked the Dragons from behind. They threw Ishida out of the ring, then double teamed Usami in the corner. They whipped him across to the opposite corner, with Chainmaster #1 following in with a clothesline, then side stepping as Chainmaster #2 did the same. Chainmaster #2 put Usami on his shoulders, and Chainmaster #1 came off the top rope with a clothesline that sent Usami in a 360! They went for the pin, and as Chainmaster #2 intercepted Ishida, Usami kicked out at two and a half. Ishida poked Chainmaster #2 in the eyes, then threw him over the top rope. He then hammered Chainmaster #1 with forearm shots to the back. He helped Usami to his feet, and the Dragons hit the Dragon Strike on Chainmaster #2. Chainmaster #1 came into the ring, and they hit the Dragon Strike on him as well. The Dragons took turns stuff powerbombing both men, then Ishida threw Chainmaster #1 out of the ring again, then hoisted Chainmaster #2 onto his shoulders, while Usami went to the top rope. Usami came off the top, and executed a move similar to Bagwell's Blockbuster move, and as Ishida caught Chainmaster #1 coming into the ring with a Yakuza kick, Usami covered Chainmaster #2 for the win. Winners: The Corporate Dragons by pinfall. TK: Ishida & Usami are a powerful team. AO: They have some talent, but their attitude is all wrong. Corporate Dragons! What a joke! TK: Our next match was supposed to be Jiraiya vs Haruki Shimoi, but Jiraiya never arrived at the arena. Shimoi demanded to face anyone who had the courage to face him, so Noboru Nishida, Takizawa's new protege, came out and answered the challenge. AO: And Shiomi paid the price for his arrogance! ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Noboru Nishida vs Haruki Shiomi [J] /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ Shimoi caught Nishida as he entered the ring with a dropkick, but the big man bounced off the ropes, and leveled him with a stiff lariat. Nishida dragged Shiomi to his feet, applied a front facelock, then drove his knee into Shiomi's face three times. He then raised his forearm up, the crowd egging him on, and hammered Shiomi's back three times. Shiomi collapsed on the mat, his nose bleeding. Nishida locked his hand around Shiomi's throat, and lifted him off the mat to his feet, then up into the air, before spinning around, and driving him to the mat with a chokeslam that shook the ring. The crowd "ooooh'd" in amazement, before Nishida dragged Shiomi to his feet, and hit his patented Raging Bull Bomber (Doctor Bomb) for the three count. Winner: Noboru Nishida by pinfall. TK: Nishida looked very impressive AO: I've always thought Nishida had potential. Even when he was with Ed Dean. Now though, he has the right mindset. Takizawa has really fired this young man up!
