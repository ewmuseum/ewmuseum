SJPW House Show — Chiimu Tora Dojo (6/4/04)

Jun 4, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Before the festivities began, Tiger Kikuchi came out and introduced some recent graduates from the Dojo: MAMMOTH Kagawa, Dragon Mask, Toramusha, and the tag team known as the Nagasaki Bulldogs (Dynamite Boy & Eiji Kid Shimura). Kikuchi stated that in addition to the announced matches, some of these men would wrestle today as well, giving the 300 fans in attendance an even better free show. MAMMOTH Kagawa vs "Jackhammer" Vince Larson: After Larson was introduced, it was announced that Kagawa would take Kikuchi's place in this match, though Kikuchi would second him. Larson got on the microphone and claimed that Chiimu Tora was cheating, switching his opponent at the last minute. Kagawa then got on the microphone and told Larson that if he was so afraid of being Kagawa's first opponent, then he'd better leave early for his flight to America to compete in Nor-Cal! The crowd laughed, and Larson attacked Kagawa from behind as he was handing the microphone to Sorai Jinbo. Larson hammered Kagawa's back with forearm shots, then hit a side belly to back suplex. Larson went for a quick cover, only to get a two count. Larson brought Kagawa to his feet, and whipped him into the corner. As he charged in after him, Kagawa bolted out, and the two men hit each other with lariats. Both men staggered backwards, then Larson charged in for another lariat, but was met with a Yakuza kick by Kagawa. Larson staggered backwards again, only to be sent to the mat after Kagawa caught him with another running lariat. Kagawa covered, but only got a one and a half. Kagawa then whipped Larson to the ropes, hitting a high impact spinebuster that shook the mat. After landing three stiff elbowdrops, Kagawa hit a legdrop off the ropes, and got a two count. He brought Larson to his feet, and went for another irish whip, but Larson reversed it, and hit an overhead belly to belly suplex as Kagawa bounced off the ropes. Larson nailed him with a high elevation elbowdrop off the ropes, and got another two count after a cover. Larson went back to pummeling Kagawa with elbows to the side of his head, and Kagawa fought back with elbows of his own. Finally, Larson staggered back, and fell to the mat. The crowd went wild as Kagawa brought Larson to his feet, then hit an exploder suplex on him. Larson got right up and went for another Lariat, but Kagawa caught him by the throat, and drove him to the mat with a powerful Nodowa Otoshi. Kagawa then went to the corner and set himself, waiting for Larson to get up. Once Larson made it to his feet, Kagawa charged, hitting him with a spear. The crowd again went wild as Kagawa pinned Larson for the three count. Winner: MAMMOTH Kagawa via pinfall Otaku says: That MAMMOTH Charge! is a devastating move. Larson just crumbled under the impact! Ono says: Another great young prospect from Chiimu Tora has made his mark! After the match, Kagawa got on the microphone and said that he hopes to be on the team with Kikuchi & Tetsuhara next week against Super Bakemono & The Crush Crew. He swore that no matter who is the third man on their team, Chiimu Tora would stand triumphant once more! El Santo Azteca vs "Firefly" Horo Atashi: The luchador legend faced off with the hot young prospect in the second match of the day. Azteca has vowed to earn his way to facing Despair. The young Atashi did not appear to be intimidated by his opponent. As they locked up, Azteca took Atashi over with an armdrag, but he rolled to his feet and caught Azteca with a spinwheel kick. Azteca staggered back to the ropes, and Atashi charged in, pummeling him with a series of palm strikes to the face and chest. he whipped Azteca across the ring, sending him to the mat with a hurricanrana as he came off. Atashi hooked the leg, and got a two count. Atashi then went to work on Azteca with a series of stiff elbows and knees that sent the luchador back to the mat, and again Atashi got a two count. Atashi then brought Azteca to his feet, snapmared him over, and landed a viciously stiff kick to the back of his masked head, eliciting a loud "oooooh" from the audience. Atashi then hit a springboard moonsault, scoring another two count. Atashi then went to the top rope, and crouched, waiting for Azteca to regain his feet. When he did, Atashi attempted a crossbody block, but Azteca countered with a dropkick to the gut! The ref put the count to both men, and Azteca was able to make it to his feet by eight. When Atashi regained his feet, Azteca laid into him with a series of chops, followed by a European uppercut. Atashi attempted a roundhouse kick, but Azteca was able to duck it as he again caught Atashi with a dropkick to the gut. Atashi doubled over, and Azteca hit a butterfly suplex. Azteca then went to the top himself, and as Atashi gained his feet, caught him with a double axe handle blow. He covered, but only got a one and a half. Atashi was able to reverse an irish whip by Azteca, and he ran to the opposite ropes, springboarding off into a beautiful tornado DDT. He again covered for a two count. Atashi went for a whip of his own, but Azteca reversed it, and hit a spinning backbreaker. Azteca dragged Atashi back up, and hit his Aztec suplex, scoring a two count. Both men traded blows, with Atashi hitting chips, and Azteca hitting European uppercuts. Atashi then hit a spinwheel kick, sending Azteca back to the ropes. Atashi went for a superkick, but Azteca caught the leg, an took him over with a dragonscrew legwhip, holding the leg, and going into a single leg crab. Atashi refused to submit, and after three minutes, made it to the ropes. Azteca broke the hold, and the crowd applauded. With Atashi limping somewhat, Azteca started to target his leg with some kicks. Atashi was able to fire back with some kicks of his own, but he got cocky, and went for a dropkick, only for Azteca to side step, causing Atashi to fall to the mat. Azteca grabbed his leg, pulled him into the center of the ring, and went for the figure four leglock. Atashi tried to fight it, but after struggling for two minutes, was forced to submit from the pain. Winner: El Santo Azteca vs submission. Otaku says: Atashi should have won, but he got too cocky. Ono says: Azteca was the more experienced of the two, and it paid off. Beware Despair, for the Aztec Saint is headed your way! Before the next match, the crowd was show some highlights of "All Star" Joey Darren. Seeing him defeat Bobby Pherris, Haron Atkinson, and finally, winning the ICW Network Championship from Mike Johnson. "All Star" Joey Darren vs Tsunami: Darren came out with the ICW Network title around his waist. The crowd gave him a nice ovation, and a few young woman blushed as he made his way to ringside. The crowd's ovation for Tsunami was a bit louder, as he came out with his IICW Cruiserweight title. Both men stood face to face in the center of the ring, with Darren looking down on his smaller opponent. Tsunami offered him his hand, and Darren shook it, which got a nice ovation from the crowd. Both men started off slow, trading some armdrags. After one armdrag, Tsunami switched tactics, and caught Darren with a dropkick to the face as he got up. Darren fell back in the corner, rubbing his jaw and giving Tsunami a look of anger. Tsunami backed off, letting Darren check himself. Once Darren went back to the center of the ring, Tsunami moved in, but Darren caught him with a kick to the gut. As Tsunami doubled over, Darren hit a Rocker dropper, rolled Tsunami over, and got a one count. Darren brought Tsunami to his feet, and landed a stiff chop across his chest. Tsunami retaliated with one of his own, only to be sent to the mat by an enzugiri. Darren again covered, and got a one and a half. Darren hit a snap suplex on Tsunami, rolled, bringing Tsunami back up with him, and whipped Tsunami to the ropes, before hitting a spinning backbreaker. Darren hit a standing moonsault and covered, but got a two count. Darren then went to the top rope, and attempted a shooting star press, but Tsunami moved, and Darren hit nothing but mat. Tsunami went for La Magistral, but Darren kicked out at two. Tsunami brought Darren to his feet, hit an Exploder '98, and scored another two count. Tsunami went to the top rope, but Darren got up, charged into the corner, and nailed Tsunami with a dropkick. He then went up top with Tsunami, and hit a top rope Exploder! The crowd went wild at that, and Darren again went to the top, and hit his "All Star Frog Splash". Tsunami somehow kicked out before the three count, and Darren looked shocked. He dragged Tsunami to his feet, and whipped him to the corner. Tsunami used the momentum to stepladder to the top rope, moonsault off, catch Darren with his Tsunami Driver #1 DDT! Tsunami went for the cover, but Darren kicked out right before three, and Tsunami looked shocked! Tsunami went for a dragon suplex, but Darren slipped out of it, and went for one of his own. Tsunami slipped out, and went behind Darren, who caught him with a back elbow to the side of his head. Darren turned around, and drove Tsunami into the mat with a DDT. He went for a cover, but only got a two count. Darren then hit a sitout facebuster on Tsunami, went to the top rope, and hit the All Star Frog Splash a second time, finally scoring the victory! Winner: "All Star" Joey Darren via pinfall Otaku says: Big win for the newcomer from America! Ono says: This Darren is impressive. I hope we see more of him in the future! After the match, Tsunami got on the microphone, and told Darren he wanted a rematch. Darren took the microphone, and said he'd gladly face him again, but wanted to know if he could challenge for the IICW cruiserweight title. Tsunami too the microphone back, and told Darren he'd gladly put the belt on the line against such a worthy opponent. Both men shook hands, and streamers flooded the ring. CEO Yamashita came out, and told the crowd that he was pleased with Tasogare no Prince being in NEO's The Skys the Limit 4 tournament. He also said he didn't hesitate to book Brody Thunder vs Despair for that program when NEO offered them a match. He said that while the match was non-title, both men would not disappoint fans around the world with their performance. He also dropped a bombshell by announcing that the match would be contested in a barbed wire steel cage! Yamashita also stated that he was changing the main event for tonight's program. He said that because of the publicity over TSTL 4, he was going to allow TnP to face Kawasaki instead of Lanther! The crowd was stunned by this announcement! TnP came out, and told the fans he would do his best to represent Super Japan in TSTL 4, and that he looked forward to facing Kawasaki tonight. Main Event: "Ecchi Kid" Kentaro "H-Bomb" Kawasaki vs Tasogare no Prince: With TnP all ready in the ring, Kawasaki came out, accompanied by Mysterious Warrior. He got on the microphone and said that even though they had switched his opponents, he would prevail against TnP. As soon as they locked up, Kawasaki maneuvered TnP into a side headlock, and turned away from the referee's view. As he did, he thumbed TnP in the eye, then broke the headlock. TnP staggered around, blinded, only to be kicked in the gut, then sent to the mat with a slingshot suplex. Kawasaki covered, but only got a one count. He started to choke TnP on the mat, but broke by the five count. He choked him again, but broke at four this time. Kawasaki got to his feet, then snapped a quick elbowdrop across TnP's chest. He covered again, getting a two count. Kawasaki dragged TnP to his feet, and attempted an irish whip, but TnP reversed it. He kicked Kawasaki in the gut, and as he doubled over, hit a sitout face plant. TnP got back up, ran to the ropes, and hit a snap legdrop. He did it three more times, then rolled Kawasaki over for a cover. Kawasaki kicked out at two, and TnP stood up, and hit a standing shooting star press for another two count. TnP sent Kawasaki to the floor with a dropkick, then dove onto him with a no hands somersault plancha. He whipped Kawasaki into the railing, but Kawasaki reversed his second whip attempt, and TnP hit the railing hard. Kawasaki hit a swinging neckbreaker, then did a corkscrew legdrop off the railing onto TnP's chest. Kawasaki rolled TnP in before the ten count. Kawasaki did a slingshot into the ring, hitting another legdrop, and covered for two. After doing the Garvin Stomp to TnP, Kawasaki went to the top rope, and hit a guillotine legdrop. He did a lazy cover, but TnP kicked out at two and three quarters. Kawaski looked a little perturbed, and started raining elbow drops to TnP's chest. He went for the cover again, but again TnP kicked out at two. Kawasaki whipped TnP into the corner, and went for a handspring elbow, but TnP leaped as Kawasaki was coming in, and took him to the mat with a Bulldog. The crowd applauded, and TnP nailed Kawasaki in the face as he sat up with a dropkick, sending him back to the mat. TnP went to the top rope and crouched, waiting for Kawasaki. When he got to his feet, TnP caught him with a Missile dropkick that sent him back to the floor. TnP again went to the top, and attempted a flying bodypress, but Kawasaki sidestepped, and TnP hit the railing hard. Kawasaki choked TnP on the railing, rammed his head against it, then dropped him throat first onto it. With the ref laying the count on them, Kawasaki rolled TnP into the ring, and covered, but again only got a two count. Kawasaki went for the Ecchi Driver, but TnP flipped behind him, hitting a reverse DDT. He covered, but only scored a two count. He then whipped Kawasaki into the corner, then climbed the turnbuckle and hit a reverse Tornado DDT, again for a two count. TnP went to the top rope once more, and attempted his Nightfall legdrop, but Kawasaki rolled out of the way, and TnP hit nothing but mat. Kawasaki dragged TnP up again, and once more went for the Ecchi driver, but this time TnP countered it with a sunset flip. Kawasaki sat down on TnP, but he was able to flip Kawasaki over. Kawasaki reversed it, but TnP was able to get him over once, this time long enough to score a three count. Winner: Tasogare no Prince via pinfall. Otaku says: Major upset here! How will this affect Kawasaki going into next week's tag team match? Ono says: Kawasaki will be on his game next week. Still, TnP did impress today. With his renewed attitude, he is on the comeback trail! After the match, TnP got on the microphone, and thanked the fans for their support. He told them he had been very disappointed in himself after losing to Asakura in the Baba Cup, and that he hoped to prove to everyone that he was a worthy contender for any title in Super Japan. He said he would do his best to uphold the honor of Super Japan in The Sky's the Limit 4 tournament, and that next week, he and Asakura would defeat Kawasaki & Hayatashi in their tag team match up. As the fans left the Dojo, they were all discussing the big upset win by TnP. Chatter on the promotion's message board was abuzz about the match, as well as Darren's challenge to Tsunami (and some women endlessly discussing Darren's looks, his availability, etc). Everyone was excited about the main event for the next The Zone! taping, as well as the Thunder/KEOKI match, and whether or not MAMMOTH was the third man in the trios match, or whether it might be Junya Toroyama, who is rumored to be returning after leaving the promotion a few months ago....
