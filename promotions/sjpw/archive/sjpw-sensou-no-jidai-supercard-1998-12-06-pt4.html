Sensou no Jidai Supercard, Part Four

6 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / /Banshee/Jet Jaguar II/Shishi Fujiwara vs/ _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Kagemusha/Kabuki Kid/Kensei Tokugawa /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [The camera pans to Nishi Toju & Owada Botan in the ring. The crowd quiets down, so Nishi can make his announcement...] Nishi: This next contest is a special Six Man Challenge match! [crowd applauds]. It is set for one fall, thirty minute time limit. Introducing Team #1, representing CHIIMU TORA [crowd cheers], at a combined weight of 611 pounds...BAN-SHEE, JET JAG-UAR II & SHI-SHI "LION" FUJ-I-WAR-A!!! ["Eye Of The Tiger" by Survivor" blares over the PA as the Chiimu Tora team come out to a standing ovation. They head down the runway, slapping hands with the fans. Once woman hands Fujiwara flowers, which he graciously accepts. They head to the ring, and work the crowd...] TK: This team is so full of fire! AO: Well, you would say that, since they fight in your honor [Kikuchi glares at Ono] AO: Well, they do! [Kensei Tokugawa & Kagemusha come out, and stride to the ring. The crowd gives them a mixed pop. Nishi looks surprised as they slide into the ring. The Chiimu Tora team look ready to fight. but Tokugawa ignores them, and asks for the microphone...] Kensei Tokugawa: I must sadly inform everyone that Kabuki Kid is still unable to wrestle tonight [crowd "ooohs"] Therefore, neither will I! [crowd "ooohs" again, while the Chiimu Tora members give each other questioning looks] If my partner cannot be here, it would be wrong for me to compete. Therefore, I have found two men to replace us in this match tonight. TK: I wonder who? AO: Shut up so we can find out! Kensei Tokugawa: Kagemusha will still fight you tonight, as a true member of the Daimyo! His partners for tonight: First, from Osaka, and weighing 185 pounds...."YOUNG DRAGON" HI-DE-OH KAN-E-MER-A!!! [Kanemera comes running from the back, and hits the ring, while the crowd reacts in surprise. Kanemera attempts to lunge at Banshee, but Kagemusha restrains him...] TK: Kanemera? AO: I've heard that he idolizes Kabuki Kid. That would explain his anger towards Banshee... Kensei Tokugawa: Their partner is a man you know all too well. He left SJPW in disgrace, but tonight, TONIGHT! He makes his triumphant return, to regain that which SJPW STOLE from him! He is a man YOU [points at Jet II] should fear, he is..... CHAND-RAS-E-KHAR YO-SHI-DA!!! (The shocked crowd pops and gets to their feet. "Afterhours" by the Sisters of Mercy plays it's dark, eerie and gothic, yet melodic tunes over the public address system. The lights in the arena dim, and white katakana symbols around the aisle read "Cleansed in darkness". The ring is highlighted with small white stars) AO: "Buddha!! Dear Buddha, it cannot be..." (The fans suddenly get to their feet as the curtains part, and a man and a woman step through. The woman is obviously a geisha, clad in a midnight blue satin dress, ornamented with golden dragons, tigers, and orchids. Her black hair is tied up in traditional geisha-style, and her jade-green eyes reflect intelligence as well as obedience. The man next to her is tall, slender, athletic. His face proudly proclaims his Asian heritage, and if it wasn't for two scars, one across his temple and one beneath his neck, would almost appear aristocratic. His almond-shaped eyes carefully observe the screaming crowd, and his long, jet-black hair is hanging freely down to his shoulders. His face is painted white, with twelve black stars in a circular pattern and a golden shooting star on the forehead. He is wearing a black "Ride the Lightning" t-shirt and white, faded denim jeans as well as snakeskin leather boots. Over this is a dark green Japanese army jacket, bearing the applegreen katakana "5" symbols for Tai-Shu, the Japanese warlord. On his back are sheated katana and wakizashi, and his ears are pierced with two large golden hoops. This man is a former SJPW All-Asian tag team champion and one of the most controversial athletes in Japanese history. This man is Chandrasekhar Yoshida. The crowd seems utterly shocked at the sudden reappearance of the man, who left SJPW in disgrace just a few months ago. The geisha slowly ascends the ring stairs, then holds the ropes down for Yoshida to step through. He does and nods to the geisha and gives Jet Jaguar II an icy stare, then grabs a microphone. He patiently waits for the crowd to calm down, almost chuckling, as if amused at the impatiency of fans) Yoshida: "I am not a man of words. I am a man of deeds." (Crowd pops uneasily) Yoshida: "I am a man of deeds, as every samurai should be a man of deeds. And following the spirit of bushido and puroresu, everyone here in SJPW should be a samurai, or strive to be one. SJPW already died once because it could not uphold these traditions, and seemingly it did not learn." (The fans pop, and quite a number of boos can be heard) Yoshida: "Now let me tell you something. I left this promotion many moons ago, and I don't know if you are familiar with the path I traveled since, but let me fit you in before incompetent SJPW officials and puroresu kitsch magazines spread lies about me again and distort the truth. Based on the fact that I was clearly the most talented athlete to come from Japan... - " (The fans start to boo! Jaguar in the ring covers his ears with his hands. Yoshida sighs, then waits for the fans to somewhat quiet down and almost shouts over their negative response) Yoshida: "...and I don't care about Hamada, Tengu, Jaguar or even Kauffman's pathetic retirement run here in SJPW before going the way of the dinosaur, PJW offered me a contract with creative freedom, something SJPW never could, and the international manager of champions, Mr.Nontoonyt, offered _ME_ a contract worth over one million dollars per annum. ME. Not Hamada, not Tengu, and most definitely not Jaguar." (Loud boos! Yoshida walks around the ring, clearly annoyed at the fans) Yoshida: "Unfortunately, Mr.Nontoonyt died an untimely death this summer, and I inherited a large chunk of his empire. Money is not an issue any more for me, so I was able to pull a few strings and set a few things right. First of all, there was this man by the name of Jet Jaguar." (The fans immediately start to cheer! Many fans make the "Team Tiger" sign and cheer for the huge fan favorite. Jaguar II raises his fists, pumping up the crowd, and Yoshida rolls his eyes) Yoshida: "Yeah, that's right. Go and cheer him. Jaguar abandoned SJPW when the promotion folded like a rat a sinking ship and signed on with NWC, who lured him with an NWC World title shot and sold thousands of furry little Jaguar masks. Needless to say, Jaguar-baka enjoyed a healthy cut of those sales, while wrestling fans all over Japan were asking, praying, begging for his return. Unfortunately, no one could give an answer when the little girl with the Jet Jaguar foam finger and tears in her eyes asked where her idol went." (The crowd suddenly dies down in a stunned silence. Some fans start to boo, a lot of front row kids seem to be very agitated. Jaguar II seems to want to charge Yoshida but his teammates can barely hold him back) Yoshida: "So Jet Jaguar was invited to wrestle in a steel cage bunkhouse battle royal to wrestle for the NWC World Title and sell some merchandise. The other, the only other athlete from Japan invited to the event based on his undeniable talent, was me." (The fans boo. Yoshida doesn't seem to care, or even hear it) Yoshida: "But even Jaguar hat some ki, some soul and self-respect left and eventually started to realize that with hiding behind a mask and a cute name he would eventually earn him as much respect as something my cat barfed up he decided to drop the mask, drop the name, and wrestle under his real name - Shoji Suzuki." (The crowd cheers again upon the mentioning of Suzuki's name) Yoshida: "But since Shoji Suzuki masks don't sell as well as Jet Jaguar masks and his talent alone doesn't warrant well-paid contracts, his American federation informed Mr.Suzuki that he was not needed any more. So Mr.Suzuki decides to sign with that lousy rotten cesspole PJW to let his career run out and make a few Yen in the process, realizing that he does not have the skills or charisma to make it in the SJPW any more. And while I am here talking and you are here booing me, Suzuki watches us on TV, smoking a cigar in an expensive hotel suite and sipping champagne while a prostitute massages his genitals, and laughs until his ribs hurt, because he fooled you all and is soon to retire a millionaire." (The fans' boos are now deafening. Jet Jaguar II crosses his arms over his chest and stares at Yoshida, who looks at him and draws his thumb across his throat) Yoshida: "But since SJPW management understandably doesn't want to miss the income made my those Jet Jaguar masks and foam fingers and general Team Tiger merchandise, they decide to stick someone else in the mask and promote him as Jet Jaguar II. Now, come on! Jet Jaguar I was a carbon copy, a man using a mask and a false identity because he was too afraid to blemish his family name. Jet Jaguar II is... is... is a damn copy of a copy!" (The crowd boos and hisses!) Yoshida: "Now let me tell you one thing, Jaguar-go, I have returned to SJPW because I have a few things to set straight I could not do in my first tenure with this promotion. I unmasked Jaguar-ni, who was about ten times as talented as you are, and I will unmask you as well. I ran Jaguar-ni out of this federation, and you better pray to whatever diety you worship that your soul may find peace despite your failure when I bury the memory of Jet Jaguar once and for all..." (With that, Jaguar II, completely infuriated, charges at Yoshida and downs him with a high dropkick to the chest, under a tremendous face pop! The referee immediately rings for the bell to start this match) TK: This match is underway! Yoshida falls back to the ropes, bounces off, and Jet II with an armdrag takedown! He slaps an armbar on Yoshida, who winces in pain! AO: He shouldn't have provoked the young Jaguar; he has the ferocity of his namesake! [Jet II drags Yoshida to his feet, and lands a chop across his chest. The crowd "Whooos" as Yoshida recoils in pain. Yoshida backs to the ropes, and Owada orders a break. Jet II releases the armbar, and Yoshida explodes on him with a kneelift that doubles him over! Yoshida tags in Kagemusha, then charges Jet II, and lands an Axe kick to the back of his head, sending him to the mat! The crowd "oooohs" loudly...] TK: Jaguar made a dangerous mistake. AO: Well, he is still a rookie... [Kagemusha drags Jet II to his feet, double underhooks his arms, then takes him over with a butterfly suplex. He runs to the ropes, springboarding off into a moonsault. He hooks Jet II's legs, and Owada drops to count. The crowd applauds as Jaguar kicks out at two. Kagemusha drags Jet II to his feet, and attempts an irish whip, but Jaguar reverses. Kagemusha comes off the ropes, and Jaguar catches him, and hits a spinning backbreaker! He moves to his corner, and tags in Fujiwara, causing the crowd to "ooooh" Fujiwara grabs the ropes, and slingshots into the ring, catching ths sitting Kagemusha with a neck snap! The crowd pops for that move, and Shishi attempts a cover, but Yoshida breaks it up with a kick to the back of Shishi's head. Owada makes Yoshida go back to his corner, and Fujiwara drags Kagemusha to his feet, and hits a belly to belly suplex. He covers again, but this time Kanemera breaks the count. Fujiwara gets to his feet, and grabs Kagemusha by the leg. He drags him to his corner, and tags in Banshee. Banshee springs to the top rope, then comes off with a senton splash on Kagemusha. He makes a cover, but Kagemusha kicks out at one....] TK: Kagemusha has taken quite a beating, but he still can't be pinned! AO: Notice how Chiimu Tora is using teamwork, but the Daimyo team isn't? TK: I've noticed! Banshee now with a bodyslam, and he runs to the ropes...quebra-Kagemusha got the knees up! [crowd "oooohs"] AO: That saved him! Banshee is on the mat in pain now! Kagemusha gets to his feet, and he dives for his corner, tagging in...KANEMERA!!! [Kanemera goes right to the top rope, and sets himself. Banshee slowly makes it to his feet, still clutching his chest. He looks around the ring, and his partners point. He turns around, and Kanemera leaps connecting with a spectacular spinwheel kick off the top rope! The crowd "ooohs" loudly, as Banshee flies across the ring, and crashes to the mat. Kanemera kip ups to his feet, and charges, landing a stomp to Banshee's head. He drags the masked man to his feet, applies a side headlock, then runs forward, bulldogging Banshee to the mat. He rolls Banshee over for a cover, but Jaguar makes the save. Kanemera gets to his feet and shoves Jaguar, who catches him with a standing dropkick that sends him into the ropes. Kanemera uses the momentum to bounce off, and nail Jaguar with a flying forearm smash! Jaguar staggers back, and Kanemera catches him with a dropkick of his own, sending Jet II over the ropes to the floor! Kanemera celebrates, not knowing Banshee is back on his feet. Kanemera turns around, and Banshee rocks him with an elbowsmash to the forehead. Kanemera staggers back, and Banshee leaps at him, tackling him to the mat. He straddles the "Young Dragon", and starts to pummel him with punches. Owada tries to pull him off, but Yoshida enters the ring, and dropkicks Banshee in the back of the head. Banshee falls on top of Kanemera, who rolls him over. He heads to his corner, and tags in Yoshida!] TK: Now we really get to see how good Yoshida is now. AO: Who cares? He probably was sent here by Moditan! TK: Why do you say that? AO: He is in SHVF! Didn't you know that? TK: No! AO: Well, now you do! [Yoshida takes his time getting into the ring, making a spectacle of it. This allows Banshee to make it to his feet, holding the back of his head. Yoshida circles him, then charges, catching Banshee with a bulldog. He gets up, grabs Banshee by the waist, and takes him over with a wheelbarrow suplex. The crowd "oooohs" as Yoshida makes the cover, then applaud as Banshee kicks out at one and a half. Yoshida grabs Banshee's leg, and turns him over into a half crab. Banshee winces in pain, and Fujiwara attempts to break it up, but Owada intercepts him. Kanemera uses this distraction to slingshot into the ring, splashing Banshee's back! The crowd "ooohs" as Kanemera slides out of the ring, and Owada turns to see what happened. Banshee is trying to fight the hold, but Yoshida has it locked on too good. Owada asks Banshee if he wants to submit, but he refuses. He starts to reach for the ropes, and Yoshida pulls back harder. Banshee slowly pulls himself forward, and though Yoshida tries to keep him away, he makes it to the ropes. The crowd applauds as Yoshida breaks the hold. He stomps Banshee in the back, then drags him to his feet. He whips Banshee to the ropes, and Banshee makes a secret tag to Jet II before bouncing off. Banshee ducks the spinning leg lariat coming off, and Yoshida turns around, but Banshee stops short. He points behind him, and Yoshida ignores him. Jet II goes to the top rope, and leaps, nailing Yoshida from behind with a double axe handle. Yoshida staggers forward, and Banshee nails him with a european uppercut! The two men grab Yoshida, and whip him to the ropes, then double dropkick him coming off. He falls back into the corner, as Banshee exits the ring...] TK: Yoshida had Banhsee almost beat, but a quick tag saved the day! Jaguar now lying into Yoshida with a series of kicks to his gut! Is it me, or is Jaguar much more vicious tonight? AO: He must be very angry at what Yoshida said to him. He's yelling at Yoshida now, and slaps him across the face! [crowd "oooohs"] TK: Jet is starting to take his mask off! [crowd "Ohhhhs"] Why is he doing that???? AO: I don't know, but it's almost off. Get a camera on his face! Wait! That's...That's....SHOJI SUZUKI!!!! [The crowd erupts as Jet II unmasks, revealing Shoji Suzuki, the original Jet Jaguar! Yoshida puts his hands up, begging Suzuki off, but Suzuki lays into him with a vicious chop to rocks Yoshida. The crowd is still roaring, and Suzuki lands another chop across Yoshida's chest. Suzuki straddles Yoshida in the corner, and starts to pummel his face. The crowd counts to nine before Yoshida grabs Suzuki's legs, and moves forward, dropping him down into an inverted atomic drop! Suzuki's leg start to buckle, and Yoshida steps back, then lariat's his opponent to the mat! Yoshida leans on the ropes for a moment, catching his breath, then looks out at the crowd, a wicked smile on his face. He turns back towards Suzuki, and drags him to his feet. He lands a cross chop to Suzuki's throat, and Suzuki falls back into the Daimyo's corner. Yoshida charges him, landing a superkick to Suzuki's jaw. Suzuki sags in the corner, then tags in Kagemusha. Kagemusha enters the ring, and he lands a superkick on Suzuki's jaw, rocking him again! The crowd "oooohs" as Suzuki lays there in the corner, unmoving. Kagemusha tags in Kanemera, and he slingshots into the ring. He turns Suzuki around, gets up on the turnbuckle, then takes Suzuki out with a Tornado DDT! He rolls on top for a cover, but Suzuki kicks out at two and a half....] TK: I can't believe Shoji is here! Jet II must still be injured, and Shoji took his place! AO: Obviously. However, he is now paying for his actions... [Kanemera hits a neckbreaker, then tags Yoshida in. Yoshida enters the ring confidently, thinking his enemy beaten. Yoshida sits Suzuki up, then lands a vicious elbowsmash to the back of his neck. He smirks at Banshee & Fujiwara, then lands another elbowsmash. He applies a rear chinlock, placing his knee at the small of Suzuki's back. Pulling back with all his might, Yoshida screams at Suzuki to submit! Suzuki refuses, and the crowd starts to chant "SHOJI! SHOJI" at the top of their lungs. Suzuki tries to break out of the chinlock, and Yoshida releases the hold. He grabs Suzuki by the back of the head, and drags him to his feet. He doubles him over, then hits a kneelift. Suzuki staggers back, and Yoshida attempts a spinning heel kick, but Suzuki catches the leg! Yoshida's face goes wide as Suzuki glares at him, then takes him over with a dragonscrew legwhip! The crowd applauds, and Suzuki goes to his corner, and tags in Fujiwara! Fujiwara hits the ring, and Yoshida tries to back away from him, but Fujiwara grabs him, and takes him over with an armdrag. Yoshida gets to his feet, and charges, but Fujiwara armdrags him again! Yoshida makes it to his feet a third time, and Fujiwara charges, but Yoshida ducks under his lariat attempt, and dives to his corner, tagging in Kagemusha! The crowd "oooohs" as the two rivals face off in the ring. Kagemusha stares at Fujiwara sadly, while Fujiwara glares at his opponent. They lock up, and Fujiwara applies a wristlock, forcing Kagemusha to double over. Fujiwara hammers his arm with three swift elbowsmashes, then switches into an armbar. Kagemusha drops to one knee, then gets up, and drives a knee into Shishi's gut. Shishi loosens the armbar, and Kagemusha twists the arm over, reversing the hold. He doubles Fujiwara over, then hits a rocker dropper, spiking his face into the mat. Kagemusha gets to his feet, shaking his arm, and waits for Fujiwara to get up...] TK: This has been a very seesaw battle. Did you see the look on Yoshida's face when Suzuki unmasked? AO: It was classic! But not Kagemusha has Fujiwara in trouble! [Kagemusha sets Fujiwara up, and hits a snap powerbomb. He goes to the corner, and climbes to the top rope. He steadies himself, then flies off, landing a guillotine legdrop across Fujiwara's chest. The crowd "oooohs" loudly, and Kagemusha attempts a cover. Owada drops to count, but Fujiwara kicks out at two and a half. The crowd applauds, and Kagemusha gets to his feet, looking down at Fujiwara with a look of pity in his eyes. He sighs soflty, then grabs him by the head, and drags him up. He sets him up again, and hits The Ryoushi Doraibaa (Fisherman's Buster). The crowd "ooohs" as Fujiwara's head spikes to the mat. Kagemusha rolls on top for a cover, and the crowd applauds as Fujiwara kicks out once more at two and a half. Kagemusha gets to his feet, still looking sad, and drags Fujiwara to his feet. Fujiwara grabs his head, and drops down into a jawbreaker! The crowd applauds, and Fujiwara starts to crawl to his corner. Kagemusha sits up, shaking his head. Fujiwara inches closer to his corner, and Yoshida enters the ring to intercept him, but Suzuki enters also, and he catches Yoshida! He backs Yoshida to the ropes, then leaps, taking both of them over the top rope with a cross bodyblock! Kagemusha makes it to his feet, and he staggers over to his corner, and tags in Kanemera. Kanemera enters the ring, and charges Fujiwara, who finally makes it to his corner, and tags in Banshee! The crowd applauds as Banshee slingshots into the ring, catching Kanemera with a flying forearm smash,,,] TK: That was close! Fujiwara was almost done for! AO: And now Banshee faces the man who hates him almost as much as Kabuki Kid does! [Banshee and Kanemera stand toe to toe in the ring, exchanging punches. Banshee slowly backs Kanemera up to the ropes, then attempts an irish whip, but Kanemera reverses. Kanemera catches Banshee coming off the ropes with a thrust kick, and Banshee falls to the mat. Kanemera goes to the corner, and starts to climb the ropes. Banshee kip ups to his feet, and charges the corner, catching Kanemera with a dropkick. Kanemera dropkicks himself, and the crowd "oooohs" loudly. Kagemusha attempts to help Kanemera, but Fujiwara tackles him, and both men fall through the ropes to the floor. Outside, Suzuki & Yoshida still brawl. Banshee gets up on the second rope, and lifts Kanemera up for what appears to be a fireman's carry. He jumps backward off the turnbuckle, then twists in mid-air, spiking Kanemera's head into the mat! The crowd "ooooohs" loudly, then applauds. Banshee rolls on top of Kanemera, and Owada drops down to count, easily making the three count!] TK: He got him! Banshee pinned Kanemera! AO: Did you ever doubt Chiimu Tora would win? [Security forces the men outside apart from each other, and Fujiwara & Suzuki slide into the ring, and help Banshee to his feet...] Nishi: The winners of this match...BANSHEE, SHISHI FUJIWARA & SHOJI SUZUKI!!! [crowd applauds] [Chiimu Tora stands triumphant in the ring, while outside, Yoshida glares up at Suzuki, and Kagemusha pulls Kanemera out of the ring. Tokugawa, who stood impassive the whole match, walks over to Yoshida, and whispers in his ear. Yoshida nods, and together, they take Kanemera from Kagemusha, and help him to the back. Kagemusha lingers a moment, first staring at his team mates, then up at Chiimu Tora. He sighs once more, and with a look of reservation on his face, heads to the back with his fellow Daimyo members...] TK: Incredible match! Chiimu Tora stands supreme once more! AO: Did you see the way Kagemusha reacted? There is trouble in the Daimyo! TK: Maybe, maybe not. AO: I tell you there is! TK: We'll see. We have to clear the ringside area, then it's time for out main event! AO: Oh great! Two welterweights headlining a card! Buddha save me!
