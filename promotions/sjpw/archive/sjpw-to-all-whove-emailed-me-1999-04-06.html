To All Who've Emailed Me

6 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey everyone, For those of you who emailed me in regards to open slots in SJPW, please note the following:
1) It may take me a few days to respond. I'm catching up with work on the job 2) I don't know how many slots I have open yet, as I haven't sat down to figure that out 3) If you are inquiring about a gaijin (non-japanese/asian) wrestler for entry, your chances will probably be slim to none. I had 2 slots for those, and 1 was promised to someone on my mailing list who was waiting for entry.
I have all the emails, and once I've given a chance for those on my mailing list to submit applications, I will email you with a copy of the wrestler form and SJPW Faq. The best applications will get in, and I will inform everyone about whether they made it or not.
Thanks to all who contacted me [name withheld] SJPW -- "I admire your depravity, but you cannot carry on like this on a public thoroughfare" Ashe to Cassandra, Vampire Journals "There's always some motherfucker trying to iceskate up a hill" Blade "Sarcasm is the sign of a weak mind" Dr. Smith to Major West, Lost In Space
