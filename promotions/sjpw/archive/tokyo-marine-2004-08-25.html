SJPW House Show — Tokyo Marine (8/25/04)

Aug 26, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wednesday, August 25th, 2004 Tokyo Marine: 489 fans 1. Tsunami def Fanta Boy (8:59) after a brainbuster 2. Frankie "The Thumper" Douglas & Doug Thomas def Noboru "Raging Bull" Nishida & MAMMOTH Kagawa (12:32) after Thomas made Kagawa submit to the anklelock. 3. Super Bakemono def Scott Starring (13:04) following an inverted Tornado DDT out of the corner. 4. "All-Star" Joey Darren & Masashi "Lightning" Lanther def "Firefly" Horo Atashi & Mr. Smiley Mask (12:54) after Darren hit the All-Star Driver on Atashi. 5. Van Wild def Junya Toroyama (15:46) following a Dangerous Backdrop Driver. - Tsunami continues his comeback trail, defeating Fanta Boy after a sickening brainbuster. - The Maulers collided with the big men of Team Tiger, and pulled off a big victory. Nishida had a good showing, overpowering Douglas & Thomas for the most part. Kagawa on the other hand, proved once more to be reckless, going for the MAMMOTH Charge! early, and missing. The Maulers took turns working on his leg. Kagawa as able to tag in Nishida, who again took control of the match for awhile, until Thomas caught him with a Backdrop Driver. Nishida was almost pinned, and tagged in Kagawa who took Thomas down with a Lariat. He went for a second one, but Thomas ducked it, and took Kagawa down with a dropkick to the back of his knee. From there on out, Thomas was relentless working his leg, finally forcing Kagawa to submit to the anklelock. - In a pairing featuring members of opposing teams on this weeks The Zone!, Bakemono and Starring faced off in a high flying contest. Starring started off strong, keeping Bakemono off balance with armdrags & hiptosses, before sending him to the floor with a spinwheel kick. Starring went for a plancha, but Bakemono side stepped, and Starring hit the floor. Bakemono tore into Starring on the floor, whipping him into the railing three times before sending him back into the ring. Bakemono hit a guillotine legdrop for two, then Starring got his knees up on a springboard moonsault. Starring went back on the attack, stunning Bakemono with a pair of snap suplexes, followed by a fisherman's suplex for a two count of his own. Starring whipped Bakemono into the corner, but he avoided the follow in Avalanche Starring attempted, then nailed him from behind with an enzugiri. Bakemono went out onto the apron, climbed to the top rope, and hit an Inverted Tornado DDT on Starring, scoring the three count. - The IICW & NCWF Cruiserweight champions joined forces against Atashi & Smiley in tag team competition. Both teams went all out, with Smiley wowing the crowd with his aerial moves (such as an amazing springboard plancha to the floor he did on Lanther, and the split legged moonsault he did to Darren), and Atashi showcasing his stiff strikes. Lanther showed he could go toe to toe with Atashi when it came to strikes, and Darren showed Smiley he could keep up with his own aerial moves after he came off the top rope and literally flipped Smiley across the ring with a hurricanrana. The end came when as all four men were in the ring. Lanther nailed Atashi with a koppo kick, and he staggered towards Darren, who hit the All-Star Driver while Lanther superkicked Smiley, allowing Darren to score the three count. - The main event saw the other two members of the teams facing off this week on The Zone!, as the Rumble Pro champion took on Toroyama. It was a very stiff contest, with both men trading chops and elbows, and quite a few lariats in the early minutes of the match. Toroyama then took control after ducking a lariat, and catching Van Wild with a powerslam as he rebounded off the ropes. Toroyama then hit a press slam, and went for his high angle powerbomb, but Van Wild started to pummel him, forcing him to the mat. Van Wild then hit a Jackhammer, but Toroyama kicked out with authority, and levelled his opponent with a Yakuza kick. Van Wild kicked out at two, which got the crowd chanting his name. Toroyama went for another Yakuza kick, but Van Wild ducked it, kicked Toroyama in the gut, and drove him into the mat with a DDT for a two count of his own. Both men went back to stiff striking, until Toroyama ducked a lariat, grabbed Van Wild from behind in a waistlock, and hit a German Suplex for a two count. The crowd was torn between both men, cheering them both on. Toroyama attempted a second German Suplex, but Van Wild caught him with a back elbow, went behind Toroyama and hit a full nelson slam, also scoring a two count. Both men then went for simultaneous lariats, knocking each other to the mat. The referee put a count on both me, and Toroyama made it to his feet by eight, grabbing Van Wild, and trying to go for his high angle powerbomb again. Van Wild was able to block it, and countered with a back drop. Van Wild missed a lariat after that, then was caught by a Yakuza kick. Toroyama then went for a German suplex again, but again Van Wild countered with some back elbows, reversed the waistlock, and hit a Dangerous Backdrop Driver on Toroyama, stunning him long enough to pin him. The crowd gave both men a standing ovation, and flooded the ring with streamers after they shook hands. Both of their partners came out, and both teams stood together in the ring, showing respect for each other. Despair was seen scowling from the entranceway to the back, chugging a bottle of Generico sake, and shaking his head in disgust at the sign of sportsmanship. After the show, both teams, along with a few other wrestlers, took time to sign autographs with the fans. Despair again scowled as Bakemono signed autographs alongside Van Wild and Starring, and left after chucking his empty sake bottle against the side of the Tokyo Marine building. Toroyama gave Bakemono a look, but the masked man shrugged his shoulders, and went back to signing autographs. On the SJPW message board, fans were discussing Despair's actions, as well as Bakemono's seeming change in attitude. Many wondered if Bakemono was distancing himself from the DoD now that he was allied with another former Team Tiger Dojo graduate. Others wondered if this was all a swerve, and that Bakemono was setting Toroyama up for a fall. Some pointed out that the Crush Crew was formed to fight Toroyama and other Team Tiger members, and neither Crush Crew member has stated they like Toroyama now. Many felt the mach this Friday against Van Wild & Starring would prove to be very, very interesting....
