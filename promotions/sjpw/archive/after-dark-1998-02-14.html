Steel Serpent retained the Battle Arts title when defeated Sandman
Jr. by KO after the Dragon's Tail at 14:00 [Battle Arts match]
Black Sun Rising retained the All Asian tag titles when they defeated The Tokyo Terrors by submission after the Master
Submission at 17:04
Highlight: Akira Saito vs Tiger Kikuchi: Both men truly wrestled a technical match. Saito used various suplexes combined with high-flying aerial moves, while Kikuchi used submission wear down holds, and stiff martial arts blows. Saito got the upper hand after reversing a German suplex that dumped Kikuchi on his head.
Saito then nailed Kikuchi with a backbreaker, then locked Kikuchi in the STF to win by submission.
February 13th, Osaka Furitsu Gym, Osaka --------------------------------------- Greg "Millionaire" Miller def El Gato Diablo by pinfall after the
Millionaire Drop at 10:22
Takao Nakami def Blackline by pinfall after the shooting star press at 18:29 Tiger Claw/Hakiro Matsuoko & The Ronin battled to a double countout at 10:57 Dark Dragon def "Tiger" Tong Po by pinfall after a superplex at 13:09 Shigeru Yoshida def Dutch McNeely by pinfall after the Big Splash at 10:15 Asamiya & Asakino def The Bonecrushers by pinfall after the Rocket
Launcher at 13:17 Super Tengu def Ghost by pinfall after the Depth Charge at 11:12 Shinji Hamada & Sammy the Kid battled to a 20 minute time limit draw [Hamada retains the All Asian title]
The Tokyo Express def the Down Boys by countout at 17:32 [Down Boys retain the WWGP World Tag Team titles]
Highlight: Shinji Hamada vs Sammy the Kid: Sammy's first title shot in SJPW was a spectcular one, as he out-wrestled Shinji for the first five minutes of the match. Hamada came back with a vicious power attack. Sammy almost had the match when he surprised
Hamada with his patented superkick, but was too worn down to make a cover. When he finally did make a cover, Hamada kicked out. Both men wearily got to their feet, and Sammy attempted another superkick, but Hamada caught the leg, then lifts Sammy up, and slammed him to the mat with a modified spinebuster. He was too weak to cover, and the time expired as both men were attempting to once again get to their feet. Hamada shook Sammy's hand after the match, and the two men walked off together to the back.
February 14th, Tokyo Bay NK Hall, Tokyo --------------------------------------- The NorthPac & Japanese Dragons battled to a 20 minute time limit draw Sammy the Kid def Jinsei Shakanuzi by pinfall after It's All Over at 15:16 "Tiger" Tong Po def Dark Dragon by pinfall after the Flying
Tigerplex at 12:58 The Yakuza def the Bonecrushers by pinfall after a double flapjack 12:28 Masashi "Lightning" Lanther def Kyu by pinfall after the Lanther
Bomb at 14:54 Big Chill def Yaz by pinfall after the Deep Freeze at 11:02 The Ronin & Tiger Claw/Hakiro Matsuoko battled to a double countout at 13:21 Shinji Hamada & Alex Akira battled to a 20 minute time limit draw
[Hamada retains All Asian title]
Mr. Big retained the WWGP world title by def Joey Rappoport by pinfall after the chokeslam at 10:37
Highlight: Ronin vs Tiger Claw/Hakiro Matsuoko: This rematch from the night before was a wild brawl from start to finish. Both teams fought back and forth, with neither team maintaining control for too long a time. The end came when Kabuki Kid frog splashed Tiger
Claw through a table while Tokugawa held him there. Matsuoko blasted Tokugawa from behind with a chair, placed him on a table, went to the top rope, then put Tokugawa through the table with a spectacular Star Sault Press. The crowd was on it's feet, but all four men were just too battered to make the twenty count. All four men were carried out of the arena on gurneys, though Tiger Claw &
Tokugawa both got off their gurneys before they were carried to the back, and both men walked back to their dressing rooms under their own power, the fans giving both men a standing ovation.
