SJPW House Show — Kitazawa Town Hall (7/14/04)

Jul 15, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wednesday, July 14th, 2004 Kitazawa Town Hall: 250 fans (Sellout) 1) El Gato Loco def Fanta Boy (7:57) by submission with the Medio Cangrejo. 2) GPH Tag League: Van Wild & Scott Starring def Frankie "The Thumper" Douglas & Doug Thomas (14:41) following the Crossfire on Thomas. 3) "Dragon Fury" Minobu Tetsuhara def Junya Toroyama (13:28) following a top rope wrist clutch Exploder. 4) Masashi "Lightning" Lanther, Jiraiya & El Santo Azteca def Mascara Negra, Dragon Mask & "Kyoto Tiger" Takayoshi Asakura (12:09) after Lanther made Dragon Mask submit to an Octopus hold. 5) Gunryo Championship: Despair & Se-hong Kwan went to a double knockout (18:54) - El Gato Loco won his first singles victory in SJPW, defeated fellow Luchador Fanta Boy with a half crab submission. - The Grand Prix Heritage Tag League continued tonight, with Van Wild & Scott Starring having a hectic match with The Maulers. Douglas & Thomas had things well in hand for the first eight minutes of the match, keeping Starring in the ring for most of that time. Starring made the hot tag to Van Wild, who took the fight to Douglas & Thomas. In the end, they hit the Crossfire (Superplex by Van Wild, Moonsault by Starring) on Thomas to pick up the win. Both teams shook hands after the match, and the fans threw in plenty of streamers for both teams. - The next match was a crazy scramble rules trios match. Team Raiden & Azteca worked better as a unit, and scored more double teams. However, Mascara Negra & Dragon Mask did some double teams of their own, and Asakura proved he still has a strong fighting spirit when he almost pinned Lanther after a Skytwister moonsault. Lanther proved why he's the wily veteran though, making Dragon Mask submit to an Octopus hold. - No one expected Se-hong Kwan to beat Despair. He didn't, but he proved that he's a force to be reckoned with in the Gunryo Division. He took the fight to Despair early on, punishing him with stiff kicks to his body and head. Despair was almost pinned following an enzugiri, then a DDT. After Kwan missed a thrust kick from the top rope, Despair got down and dirty, using his barbed wire chair to batter Kwan down. Kwan came back for a short while, and bloodied Despair's back with his own chair. After Despair regained his chair, he blasted Kwan in the face, cutting him open badly. He fell back to the ropes, and as Despair was getting reach to blast him again, Kwan lunged off the ropes, and hit a Yakuza kick that sent the chair into Despair's face. Both men went down, and neither made the ten count. Despair retained his title, but both men were carried out on stretchers. The fans were very hyped after the show, especially due to the ending of the Gunryo match. Despair's fans were extremely concerned about their idol, and a few asked the staff at the Town Hall which hospital he had been taken to.
