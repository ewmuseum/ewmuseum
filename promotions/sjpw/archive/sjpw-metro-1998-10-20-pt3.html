SJPW Card Archive

Metro, 3/3

20 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ER: Our next event is going to be something special! We taped earlier the contract signing for one of our newest competitors. The man singing his contract is supposed to be a major superstar in Japan! JA: I wonder who it could be? ER: Well, let's the tape of the signing, and find out! ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Special Contract Signing! /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ (Camera comes up on a large office room. Sevral camera men stand by in the foreground as the president of SJPW Kobayashi stands in front of a wooden podium. Clearing his throat, he speaks.) Kobayashi: Thank you gentlemen. It is my utmost honor at this present time to introduce the reason for this Press Conference. After many hours of talks and dealings with former MLWO owner, Mark Cutter. I am deeply honored to introduce to you. The former and current holder of the now Defunct MLWO Japan title. "The Japanese Superstar" Jinsei Tenru. (There is a light smattering of confused applause as the group tries to come to terms with the name of a man they thought they knew already. From a nearbye door, a large man clad in a expensive looking suit walks. His eyes are covered by a pair of purple tinted sunglasses and the expensive blue material of his suit does little to hide the extensive musculature underneath. The man bows slightly to Kobayashi as he nears, then takes the podium. Lifting his left hand he places upon the podiums to a heavy loking leather and gold belt with the letters "MLWO" written in sapphire on its face. There is a collective rush of questions and exclamations as one of Japan's premiere film stars stands before them.) Tenru: Thank you gentlemen. First let me thank Mr. Kobayashi for his kindness and patience in this trying negotiation phase. It has been a long time in the coming. But I am glad to be yet again wrestling in my native Japan after a run with the MLWO. I would humbly ask that this be kept short as I am needed on the set of "Tokyo Titan" in thirty minutes. To cut off some of your more probable questions, this title is still being recognized by several boards of Wrestling in Asia, and I hope soon the SJPW will do the same. Now, questions? (The flashbulbs start going off as Tenru, his kind words belayed by the bored expression on his face, picks a man at random.) Tenru: Yes? (A heavyset japanese man stands up slowly) Reporter: Mr. Tenru... I, as I am sure I speak humbly for all of my companions. Our greatly honored to be in your presence here in Super Japan pro Wrestling. May I ask your thoughts on a man who already is within the halls of SJPW, a man whom obviously has some respect for you since he took your name, Jensei Tenru. (Tenru chuckles and removes his sunglasses slowly, raising his head with his trademark movie star smile. Looking directly into the camera, he answers.) Tenru: As a matter of fact. That was one of the major sticking points between Mr. Kobayashi and myself. (the crowd falls silent as Kobayashi looks up in suprise. not expecting the private business to be brought up.) Tenru: To be fair at this time. Your Tenru is allowed to retain his obvious title of affection to the Superstar. However, as part of the deal for my signature on the SJPW dotted line, I will be allowed to referee the expected match between Tenru and Tengu on the upcoming Tsunami. barring any major events of course. (Tenru chuckles slightly and points to another reporter.) Reporter: Ogawi Akibona, Rising Sun Times... Mr. Tenru, what is your current relationship with the former head of the MLWO, Mark Cutter? And what plans, if any. Do you have for defending the MLWO Japan title? (Tenru pauses and chews on the end of his sunglasses reflectively) Tenru: Cutter? Well, lets just say this. The money was good, but the laying down for the oldest piece of wrestling trash in the US, Marcus Renge, was not. Akibono: And the title? (Tenru leans against the podium and exhales slowly.) Tenru: As I said.. The title is still recognized and as a matter of fact. I intend to use this here as a way towards getting my admirer into the ring. And , if that doesnt work? Well, <chuckle> trust me, the Superstar has a trick or two up his sleeve. And now, I must break this up. I am needed on the set. (Tenru turns to leave as Kobayashi swiftly rises to meet him halfway.) Kobayashi: Thank you gentlemen... And mr. Tenru, I am sure that this display of uncouthness will not be the norm for such a defined Superstar.. (Tenru looks at Kobayashi and replaces his sunglasses carefully as he turns.. leaving without a word.) JA: Wow! This is a shocker! Jinsei Tenru signs to SJPW! I wonder how Jensei Tenru will react? I wonder if they are related at all? ER: I have no idea Jonnie! All I know is that this situation with him being the referee for the Tengu/Tenru match just made that match more volatile! JA: Speaking of volatile, The Yakuza are ready to maul the Maulers! Let's get to the ring! __ ___ _____________________________________________________ / |/ /__ / /________Frank A. Marano Tag Tournament, Round One:/ / /|_/ / -_) __/ __/ _ \ The Maulers vs The Yakuza /_/ /_/\__/\__/_/ \___/_______________________________________/ [The camera pans to the ring. Both teams are there, and the referee gives his instructions, then calls for the bell..] JA: Geto of the Yakuza, and Douglas of the Maulers are gonna start the match off for us tonight. Douglas, a long time veteran of the squared circles is calling for a test of strength. Geto, seems to be a bit reluctant. Douglas is a one-dimensionmal power wrestler. He is probably the stronger of the two men in the ring. ER: Well Jonnie...This is not true...Geto of the Yakuza is not exactly a weak man, if ya know what I mean. Douglas and Geto lockup their hands in a test of strength. Douglas quickly knees Geto to the gut, and then takes him over with a standing clothesline. [crowd applauds] JA: Douglas down quickly on the offensive. He stomps Geto in the head not once, not twice, BUT THREE TIMES!!! He then quickly drops his knee into the gut of Geto, and then he stands back up. He picks up Geto by the hair, and sets him up for a belly to belly suplex. HE NAILS IT!!! ER: The Maulers dominating the first part of the matchup here. The Yakuza have not had time to mount any kind of offense as of yet. The suplex does not hold down Geto for long as he quickly gets back up. It is as if all of the Mauler offensive was entirely useless against Geto. JA: Douglas with a quick kick to the gut of Geto, but Geto continues to get back up. Geto punches Douglas in the face [crowd "ohhhs"] Then a roundhouse kick to the face of Douglas takes him down on the mat. ER: Douglas gets back up, and is immediately jumped on by Geto, who double arm DDT's him face first into the canvas. Geto picks him back up, and throws him into the ropes......Douglas ducks under the clothesline attempt, and on the way back nails a clothesline of his own. JA: Mauler's coming back. They have not quit yet, as Douglas gets to his corner as fast as he can, and tags in Doug Thomas. Thomas quickly on top of things as he puts Geto, into an armbar submission. Geto screams and pain, but when asked to quit nods his head. ER: Thomas is a submission expert, but his lack of expierence in the ring could be the downfall of this tag team. As Thomas then let's go of the armbar, and allows Geto to get up. He throws Geto into the ropes, and Geto comes back.....drop toe hold by Thomas. JA: What a move...He really knows his way around the ring, does he not? Thomas then stands up, and looks into the crowd. [crowd applauds] Thomas is taking a little too much time getting back down on the attack. ER: Geto, starts to get back up. Thomas has not noticed as of yet, and Geto walks up behind Thomas, and lands a Samoan Backdrop. Good move on the part of Geto. He stands up, and is a bit wobbly. He makes it to his corner though to tag in his partner. JA: Tazaka, the High-Flyer is now in. Thomas stands up, and walks toward Tazaka, but is met with a standing Hurricanrana. Great Move!!! Tazaka, now has his opponent down. He lands an elbow to the face quickly before Thomas can have a chance to retaliate. He then picks Thomas back up, and swings him into the ropes. ER: Thomas on the rebound is met with a kneelift, which is then turned into a snap DDT. Tazaka really showing his moves, and his way around the ring out here tonight against the Mauler's. Tazaka then down on the ground executing the Temple of Doom. JA: A Devastating manuever!!! Thomas can not take much more of this. He seems to be pretty worn out. Tazaka up...He tags in his partner Geto, and they are gonna go for some double teaming. Geto and Tazaka whip their opponent Thomas into the ropes, and on his way back they nail a DOUBLE SPINNING HEEL KICK!!! ER: God!!! [crowd "ohhhhs"] The Yakuza now calling for their finisher the Sacred Blade. Tazaka steps out of the ring, and then Geto tags him back in. Geto hoists up Thomas onto his shoulders, and Tazaka then climbs to the top rope, and miraculously sommersaults over top of Thomas. On the way down he lands a snapover Diamond Cutter. JA: Well...I don't think Thomas is gonna be getting up after that one. As Tazaka rolls over and covers Thomas, Geto goes over to Douglas and plants a forearm to his face to assure that he will not break up the pin. 1...! 2...! 3....! [crowd applauds] Nishi: The winners of this match....THE YAKUZA!!! [crowd applauds again] ER: Once again, the Yakuza prove why they are one of the best teams in SJPW! The Bonecrushers better be watching this team closely! JA: That they will. Of course now, we come to our main event, which pits two teams who are currently rivals against each other. Chiimu Tora members Banshe & Jet Jaguar II, collectively known as Hiryuu Batsu, battle Daiymo members Kabuki Kid & Kensei Tokugawa, collectively known as The Ronin. ER: This is a war, plain and simple. Both teams have alot of animosity towards each other, and it's reflected in they way they treat each other in the ring. When these men face off, there's more than simply winning the match at stake! JA: I fully agree. Both teams are in the ring, so let's hook 'em up! __ ___ _____________________________________________________ / |/ /__ / /________Frank A. Marano Tag Tournament, Round One:/ / /|_/ / -_) __/ __/ _ \ Hiryuu Batsu vs The Ronin /_/ /_/\__/\__/_/ \___/_______________________________________/ [The camera pans to the ring, where both teams recieve last minute instructions from Goto Kyoji, who calls for the bell...] ER: Kensei Tokugawa starting off against Banshee tonight. They lock up, and Tokugawa forces Banshee to the corner. Goto calls for the break, and Tokugawa catches him with a chop across the chest! [crowd "whooos"] Tokugawa now with a knee to the gut, then a stiff back elbow to the side of Banshee's head! [crowd "ooohs"] JA: Tokugawa whips Banshee to the opposite corner, then charges after him...Banshee with a jumping sidekick out of the corner! [crowd "ooohs"] Tokugawa's on the mat! Banshee with a legdrop, then he gets to his feet, and tags in Jaguar II! Hiryuu Batsu drag Tokugawa to his feet, then take him over with a double suplex. ER: Jaguar II with a kneedrop to Tokugawa's chest...he covers... ...1... JA: Kickout! [crowd applauds] Jaguar II drags Tokugawa to his feet...Irish whip reversed by Tokugawa...Big Boot to the Face by Tokugawa! [crowd "ooohs"] Jaguar II went down hard! Tokugawa stomps Jaguar II across the chest, tags Kabuki Kid, then drives his elbow onto Jaguar II's chest. Kabuki Kid springs to the top rope... Diving Headbutt off the top! [crowd "ooohs"] ER: Kid drags Jaguar to his feet, and whips him to the ropes... spinning backbreaker! Whip to the ropes again...another spinning backbreaker! He whips him to the ropes a third time...ANOTHER Spinning Backbreaker! [crowd "ooohs"] JA: Tag to Tokugawa...The Ronin drags Jaguar II up, and ram him back first into the corner! Kid goes to the apron, while Tokugawa lays into him with kicks to his gut! Tag to Kid, who goes to the top rope, while Tokugawa turns Jaguar II around, and hoists him up on his shoulders...THESZ PRESS off the top rope! [crowd "ooohs"] Kid drove Jaguar II hard to the mat! Cover... ...1... ...2... ER: KICKOUT! [crowd applauds] Kabuki Kid can't believe it! He scoops Jaguar II up, and slams him to the mat. He rolls him over, then proceeds to rain elbowdrops to his back! How much more can Jaguar II take? [crowd starts chanting "Jaguar! Jaguar!"] JA: Tag to Tokugawa. He comes in, and Kid runs to the ropes...Tokugawa POWERSLAMS Kid on top or Jaguar II's back! [crowd "oooohs"] Jaguar II is screaming in pain! Kid rolls off Jaguar, and out of the ring to the floor. Tokugawa drags Jaguar II to his feet, sets him up, and lifts him up for a power...HURRICANRANA by Jaguar II! [crowd "oooohs", then applauds] ER: Tokugawa rolls to his feet, and charges, but Jaguar II back bodydrops him over! Jaguar II dives towards his corner, and tags Banshee! [crowd cheers] JA: Banshee springs to the top rope, and sets himself while Tokugawa gets to his feet...MISSLE DROPKICK!! [crowd "ooohs"] Banshee with a kip-up, and he runs to the ropes...QUEBRADA! Cover... ...1... ...2... ER: Kickout! [crowd applauds] Banshee drags Tokugawa to his feet, hooks the leg...FISHERMAN'S BUSTER!! [crowd "ooohs"] Kabuki Kid is up on the apron now, and Banshee leaps, catching him with a flying forearm! Kid stops dead in his tracks, and Banshee grabs his head, snapping his neck onto the top rope! [crowd "ooohs"] JA: Banshee turns, and leaps at Tokugawa...senton splash! Cover... ...1... ...2... ER: Kickout! [crowd applauds] Kabuki Kid is getting to his feet on the floor, and Jaguar II goes to the top rope, and leaps...Kabuki Moved! [crowd "ooohs"] Jaguar II slammed into the railing! Kabuki Kid drags him to his feet...SNAP SUPLEX on the floor! [crowd "ooohs"] JA: In the ring, Banshee has Tokugawa in a double chicken wing! Goto asks him, but Tokugawa refuses to submit! Outside the ring, Kabuki Kid just slammed Jaguar II onto the railing with a front-layout suplex! [crowd "ooooohs"] ER: Kabuki Kid peels Jaguar II off the railing, then side suplexes him onto the floor! [crowd "oooohs"] Banshee still has the double chicken wing on Tokugawa, who still refuses to give up! JA: Kabuki Kid hops onto the apron, and slingshots into the ring, kicking Banshee in the back of the head. He releases the hold on Tokugawa, and collapses to the mat. Kid rolls Tokugawa outside the ring, then drags Banshee to his feet, and takes him over with a snap suplex... ER: Kid gets up quickly, and hits a standing headbutt drop. He gets up again, and kneedrops Banshee in the side of the head! Banshee sits up in pain, and Kid flips over him into a jumping neck snap! [crowd "oooohs"] JA: Kabuki Kid with a senton splash, then a cover... ...1... ...2... ER: Kickout! [crowd applauds] Jaguar II is up now, and he's trying to get up to the apron! Kabuki Kid scoops Banshee up, and slams him to the mat. He goes out on the apron, and heads to the top rope. Jaguar II is on the apron now, and he's heading towards Kid! JA: Kid sets himself, while Banshee staggers to his feet. Kid leaps off with a high cross bodyblock, but Banshee catches him, and somehow keeps his feet! [crowd "oooohs"] Jaguar II goes to the top rope, while Banshee shifts Kid on his shoulders...Jaguar II leaps...Banshee Flapjacks Kid, while Jaguar II buttdrops the back of Kid's head! [crowd "ooooohs"] Kabuki Kid is out cold! Jaguar II rolls off him, and Banshee rolls Kid over, and grapevines the leg... ...1... ...2... ...3!!!! **ding** **ding** **ding** [crowd applauds] Nishi: The winners of this match...HIRYUU BATSU!!! [crowd applauds again] ER: What a match! Kabuki Kid is still laid out in the ring! JA: And we are out of time! We'll see you all next week on Metro!
