SJPW Ring Wars, 1 March 1998, Hour One

March 1, 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The crowd gives a heel pop as Brian Lau walks down the aisle alone. He appears slightly angry, but does his best to mask his feelings with a slick business-like savvy. Lau walks to the timekeeper's table, grabs a microphone, and enters the ring.]
BL: They said they wanted an interview with me, so I figured I'd conduct it myself. I'm sorry, but I just don't feel that there's an interviewer out there with the skills to be able to do the job right.
[The crowd boos Lau...] BL: Whatever... Now, down to business. When Tiger Claw and I first came to the SJPW, many thoughts sprang up in the minds of the atheletes here. Most of those thoughts can't actually be repeated without me getting in trouble, but there was one thought, one word, that I know everyone thought of... Syndicate. Yes, _The_ Syndicate.
As many people know, The Syndicate is an organisation that I founded in America, in which the greatest warriors in the world can come to and hone their craft. The potential is quite amazing, I assure you. Imagine, if you will, a group of fighters with the same goals, the same spirit, with slightly differing levels of skill, coming together and working with each other to raise their performance to a higher level. Wonderful, is it not?
Many Syndicate members have gone on to capture accolades worldwide. For example, Casey "Blackheart" James, one of the cornerstone members of the Syndicate, went on to set record title reigns in several organizations after working with and being trained by Tiger
Claw. Tiger Claw, in fact, has set several record title reigns as well, holding a certain title in a certain organisation on three separate occasions. Brody Thunder went on to become voted best wrestler in the entire world! Yes, Brody Thunder was once a
Syndicate member, and I guarantee you that he has never regretted it. Now, I can go on and on about all the members of the Syndicate through the years... I can talk about the men behind the scene... I could talk about our investors... I'd prefer to talk about the founding member of the Syndicate... Besides me, of course. That man is the one and only... TIGER CLAW!
[The crowd applauds for Tiger Claw, who comes down to the ring wearing his wrestling attire consisting of red and black Muay Thai shorts. No music plays... Claw enters the ring and stands beside his manager.]
BL: Yes... Claw was the first member of the Syndicate. Claw and I formed the Syndicate in the States, following the old ways of discipline and structure, and wreaked havoc. However, in the beginning, we were but two men... Just like we are standing before you right now. Two men hardly constitute an organisation... No...
But three men... Well, that's a start... Four men... Now that's getting somewhere... I can see by the looks on the faces of you fans that you're a bit confused. Let me make it easier for you...
We're building a new chapter of the organisation right here in the SJPW. [Mixed reaction from the crowd. Applause from the people believing
Lau's line, and boos from the people who have caught Syndicate matches in the past.]
BL: That's right! The Syndicate in Super Japan Pro Wrestling! Let me introduce you to our official second member... Friend, and once enemy of Tiger Claw, legendary wrestler and promoter, HAKIRO
MATSUOKO! [The pounding fury of kodo drums comes through the pa. The crowd pops pretty hard for Matsuoko, even if it is a mixed pop. Hakiro appears from the backstage area, wearing a charcoal three piece suit. He really doesnt take the time to shake hands as he heads down to the ring. Once in the ring, he shakes the hand of Lau, executes a slight bow to Claw and gently slaps him on the back.]
HM: Once again, I stand amongst friends. In the past, Claw and I forged a war that helped to make a promotion. We battled countless times for gold, we shed blood and bruises. No matter how much that
I felt hatred for Claw there was always the utmost respect for him.
He is the complete man, warrior and martial artist. In America, I was one of the charter members of The Syndicate. I stood proudly along side of Tiger Claw and Casey James. Both Claw and myself helped to train Mr. James. We helped him to hone his vaunted technical style. My days with The Syndicate were among my most proud. Now, working in my homeland and standing once again on the threshold of battle with my trusted friends, there can be no greater honor.
Today, we stand united. Against Ronin and Dark Dragon. Our enemies cannot know our bond. Our bond is not an alliance of convience. We unite as one, train and eat together. Ronin and Dark Dragon, I say to you, train with the utmost effort and diligence. That is the bare minimum is you are to even entertain thoughts of competing against myself and Claw.
BL: Say what you will, Ronin, of honour, of lineage. None of that compares to the raw skill of these two men. It begins here, with these two men. It goes on to include others. Tokugawa, it would have been better for you to accept your weakness, and to accept your punishment. Instead, you felt the need to make some sort of strike back. Tonight, along with the Big Chill, we will show you exactly what we mean. Even if the Big Chill were unconscious for the whole match, my men here could easily overcome you.
That is not to say that I do not respect the Big Chill, of course. I have come to appreciate the... how shall I put it? Bluntness... that TBC is known for. Chill, I would like to think that perhaps tonight will not mark the end of your working relationship with the
Syndicate. Please do not disappoint.
One last thing... Tokugawa, it has come to my attention that you wish to challenge Tiger Claw to a cage match with "I Quit" rules.
Tiger Claw is not one to back down from a challenge, and is eager to accept. It is your mistake, of course, but should be entertaining nonetheless. Tokugawa, bring your honour, bring your tradition, bring all of your dead relatives to the ring if you wish. None of it will help you. All it will serve to do is to sully that honour, break that tradition, and shame you in the eyes of the ghosts long dead that watch over you. Train yourself, Tokugawa...
Not for the athletic contest that lies before you, but for the shame of loss. That is all! [Hakiro Matsuoko's Kodo drum music begins to play once again, the thunder almost accentuated for the heel pop for Lau's disregard for tradition. Matsuoko, Claw and Lau leave the ring, and walk back up the aisle calmly.]
