Tsunami, Part Three

15 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
AO: This next match should be interesting, a true clash of styles as they say. TK: Hai. The power wrestling of Onslaught against the submission and striking skills of Akira Hashimoto. Hashimoto faces a much larger man in Onslaught, and he will have to compensate for that. AO: I believe he will. Onslaught is a monster, there is not doubt, but Hashimoto is a warrior. He has the training and experience while his opponent is raw and undisciplined. ______ ___________________________________ /_ __/__ __ _____ ___ ___ _ (_) All-Asian Juuryou Tournament/ / / (_-</ // / _ \/ _ `/ ' \/ / Onslaught vs. Akira Hashimoto /_/ /___/\_,_/_//_/\_,_/_/_/_/_/________________________________/ [The camera pans to the ring. Nishi Toju is standing in the middle of the ring holding the mic while referee Goto Kyoji stands in a corner.] Nishi: The following contest is one fall, 20 minute time limit. It is a first round match in the All-Asian Juuryou title tournament. Your referee for this match is Goto Kyoji! ["Hon-No-Fighter" starts up over the PA] Nishi: Introducing first, from Kanagawa, and weighing in at 260 pounds...HASH-I-MOTO A-KI-RA!!! [Pyros explode on either side of the entrance as Hashimoto walks out. The fans cheer as lights flash and Hashimoto strides to the ring wearing a red robe with a dragon illustration on the back. He slaps hands with some fans as he walks to the ring, and then he enters. He removes his robe to reveal black trunks, boots and shingaurds.] [A loud pyro explosion fires off at the entrance way, and then "Natural Born Killazw" by Dr. Dre and Ice Cube cranks on the PA] Nishi: His opponent, from Parts Unknown and weighing in at 350 pounds... ONSLAUGHT!!! [Onslaught stalks through the curtain, and then looks out to the crowd for a moment before walking slowly to the ring. He wears a black t-shirt, black t-shirt, black steel-toed boots, and his trademark black chromed hockey mask. He enters the ring, and stands across from Hashimoto ready for battle.] AO: That man has no business in the All-Asian tournament if you ask me Kikuchi. He is born and bred for the savage world of Koubousen Geijutsu. TK: Perhaps, but in this match, he must avoid the tactics used in those matches, and adhere to the rules of the ring. AO: In that case, he is lost. As I said, Hashimoto is the better pure werstler of the two. [Goto instructs each man forward, and he checks them for weapons, seemingly a bit wary of Onslaught as he does so. He then calls for a handshake. Hashimoto caustiously brings his hand out, and Onslaught looks around to the crowd, and then shakes Hashimoto's hand quickly, and then backs away to his corner as does Hashimoto. Goto signals for the bell.] TK: Onslaught charges out of the corner! AO: Hashimoto able to side-step, and he fires in with a stiff kick to the back of the leg, and now Hashimoto with a combination of kicks to the legs, and body. TK: Onslaught seems to be absorbing the kicks, and he simply shakes his head, and calls for more! [The fans "ohhh" and "ahhh" as Hashimoto backs off a bit, and then he attempts to shoot the legs of Onslaught, but he recieves a double-axhandle blow to the back of the head as he does so, and then repeated jackhammer blows driving him down to the mat. Onslaught then stomps away on Hashimoto, forcing him to cover up on the mat, and roll to the ropes. Goto backs Onslaught up as Hashimoto regains his feet.] TK: Onslaught taking up right where he left off in SJPW, he is a force of total destruction. AO: Onslaught moving back in with kicks to the midsection, but Hashimoto blocks the last on with his shin, and now he drives knees to the midsection, and now chops to the chest. TK: Onslaught doesn't step back, he absorbs the blows once again. [Hashimoto fires in with more knee strikes, and finally a jumping round kick to the chest sends Onslaught back to the ropes, but he comes right back with a lariat attempt, but Hashimoto catches his arm.] TK: WAKIGATAME! [fans "ohhh" and "ahhh"] AO: Onslaught blocks it, and he is able to shrug Hashimoto off! TK: Hashimoto went for one of his primary moves, but Onslaught with too much energy, and he fought it off. [Hashimoto moves back in with kicks to the body and legs, he backs Onslaught to the corner. He fires in with a back-hand chop, but Onslaught roars to life, and grabs Hashimoto and spins him into the corner. He kicks Hashimoto repeatedly, sending him sliding down the corner pads to the mat, where Onslaught continues to stomp away, as Goto attempts to get him to break.] TK: Goto finally able to move the massive man back and Hashimoto is in trouble early. AO: He must take the man off his feet. TK: That would be sound strategy, but it is a task easier said than done. [Onslaught moves back in, and pulls Hashimoto up, he grabs him around the throat with both hands, and then lifts him into the air in a choke. Goto immediately calling for the break, and as he counts to four, Onslaught drops Hashimoto, and he lands in the corner, the back of his head bouncing off the second turnbuckle.] AO: He is an unbridled savage. This is an outrage. TK: He is using tactics he always has Ono-san, and they do work, if they are not the most honorable. [Goto backs Onslaught off, and warns him about the illegal choke. Onslaught nods, and then moves back in on Hashimoto is rising to his feet, holding the back of his head. Onslaught with a kick to the midsection, and then pounding forearms to the back, sending Hashimoto down. Onslaught drags him back up, and scoops him up, and then holds the man for a few seconds before dropping him down across his knee in a back-breaker.] TK: Onslaught now has Hashimoto by the wrist, and he pulls him up ...Kick to the midsection followed by a Short-Arm Clothesline! AO: Onslaught pulls him off the mat by his wrist, and again connects with the short lariat. [The fans "ohhh" and "ahhh" as Onslaught pulls Hashimoto up once again, and attempts a third short clothesline, but Hashimoto ducks in, and catches Onslaught around the shoulder, and lifts him up before dropping down with a Urange as the fans cheer. Their cheers turn to "ohhhs" and "ahhs" as Onslaught quickly rolls to his feet. Hashimoto stands, and turns and gets leveled by a running fore-arm smash.] TK: Onslaught able to absorb any attack by Hashimoto thus far. AO: Hashimoto must find a way lock him up, and wear him down, or this match will not last very long I am afraid. [Onslaught pulls Hashimoto up yet again, and sends him to the ropes. As he comes off, Onslaught lifts him up high over his head with a Gorilla Press. He holds him several seconds, walking around the ring and the fans applaud. Finally, Onslaught drops Hashimoto hard to the canvas, and then the drops down with a hand on his chest for the cover.] ...1... TK: Hashimoto easily able to kick away as Onslaught with a very light cover, he seems to not care if the match ended that time. AO: He is in control, but he should still have gone for a strong press. The advantage can turn quickly inside that ring. [Onslaught pulls Hashimoto up once more, and again whips him to the ropes, and connects with a boot to the face, driving Hashimoto to the mat. Hashimoto holds his jaw, as Onslaught looks out to the fans. He reaches down and pulls Hashimoto up again.] TK: Onslaught with an irish whip, and a lariat attempted, but Hashimoto ducks it. Onslaught turns...Dropkick to the knee! [Onslaught staggers, but does not fall.] AO: Hashimoto back up, and another dropkick to the knee, and Onslaught falls to one knee. Hashimoto back up, and off the ropes...Spinkick to the side of the head drives Onslaught to all fours. As I said, momentum can turn quickly. [The fans cheer as Hashimoto scrambles to his feet, and then leaps up with a modified scissor kick to the back of Onslaught's head, and Onslaught finally goes down to the mat. Hashimoto scrambles up, and attempts to lock in a step-over neck submission.] TK: Strangle-Hold Gamma!! [The fans cheer, but Onslaught is able to fight the hold, and manages to shove Hashimoto away, and rolls to his feet. He still seems a bit dazed however, and Hashimoto runs toward him and hits a flipping heel kick.] AO: Rolling Koppou Kick!! [The fans applaud as the kick sends Onslaught reeling back to the corner. Hashimoto is up qucikly, and he pulls Onslaught out of the corner, and attempts a Belly-To-Belly Suplex, but Onslaught blocks the move. He breaks the front waistlock, and manages to lock on a bear-hug, but Hashimoto has one of his arms pulled in, keeping Onslaught from putting pressure on the ribs and driving the air out.] TK: Bearhug applied but, Hashimoto trying to fight out...Onslaught now lifts him up and spins...Belly-To-Belly Suplex. Onslaught covers... ...1... ...2... AO: Goto counts two only as Hashimoto gets his shoulder up. Onslaught pulls Hashimoto to his feet. Hashimoto with a weak kick to the back of the thigh, but Onslaught fires in with a forearm, and now grabs the throat. NODAWA SLAM! [Hashimoto's head bounces off the mat with extreme velocity as Onslaught drives him down, and then covers him. Goto drops, checks the shoulders, and counts.] ...1... ...2... [Fans "ohhhh" and applaud.] TK: Hashimoto escapes! Onslaught to his feet, and he backs to the corner, and climbs to the second rope. He does possess excellant agility for a man his size, and he stares down at Hashimoto who doesn't stir...FLYING LEGDROP! AO: NO!! Hashimoto rolls clear, and Onslaught clutches his leg. [Hashimoto rises, and sways on unsteady legs for a moment, and then attempts a Leg-Scissors on the right knee of Onslaught.] TK: Hazi-gatame applied, and Goto looking for the submission. [Onslaught shakes his head, but cries out in pain as Hashimoto applies the pressure. Onslaught drags himself to the ropes, and reaches out as Hashimoto sits back, pulling with all his strength. The fans let out a frustrated "ohhhhh" and then applaud as Onslaught reaches the ropes. Goto breaks up the hold, and Hashimoto rolls clear. Onslaught pulls himself up using the ropes, and Hashimoto moves in quickly with a waistlock.] TK: German Suplex!! Unbelievable strength by Hashimoto. AO: He is not down, he rolls Onslaught to his feet... [With some difficulty Hashimoto manages to get Onslaught back up, and switches for a waistlock to a full-nelson.] TK: He is going for his Rolling Triple Suplex...Dragon Suplex is next, followed by an Exploder...OH! [With a primal yell, Onslaught breaks free from the Full Nelson, and spins into one of his own. Hashimoto struggles for a moment, and then Onslaught lifts him up, and drives him into the mat with a Full-Nelson Slam. Onslaught rises, limping slightly, and pulls Hashimoto up, and he wraps his and around his throat.] TK: Onslaught attemtping yet another Nodawa, he lifts Hashimoto up...Hashimoto spins up and locks his legs around the arm, and sends Onslaught to the mat...JUJI-GATAME! AO: He locked on the hold, but Onslaught rolled into the ropes, and Goto must force the break. He had the arm extended, he had him. TK: Both men up now, and Hashimoto visible upset at the lost oppurtunity for the win, and now he flies at Onslaught and fires in with kneestrikes that double him over...He scissors the head, and he is attempting a Powerbomb on Onslaught. AO: No. He cannot play the power game with Onslaught. [Hashimoto cries out, and tries to lift Onslaught put falls. He tries it again, but then cries out, and staggers away, holding his back.] TK: Too much damage delt to the back early in the match, and Hashimoto can't lift the bulk of Onslaught. AO: Now he leaps back into the ropes and charges...Onslaught catches him...POWERSLAM!! [The fans "ohhh" and "ahhh" as Onslaught drives Hashimoto into the mat. Goto drops down ready to count, but Onslaught rises to his feet, instead of covering the man. He motions for the Onslaught Bomb.] TK: The fans rise to their feet as Onslaught lifts Hashimoto up... He has him set, and Hashimoto is unable to defend the move...Onslaught Bomb! [Onslaught drives Hashimoto into the mat with incredible power and force, and then drops down for the cover.] ...1... ...2... ...3!!! **ding** **ding** **ding** ["Natural Born Killaz" starts up.] Nishi: Your winnner...ONSLAUGHT!! [The fans applaud for Onslaught, as he rises, and has his hand rasied. Hashimoto remains on the mat, as ring attendants enter and check on him, applying ice spray to his neck.] AO: Hashimoto fought gamely, but in the end, he was overwhelmed by power. TK: Onslaught proving he has not lost a step in the ring, and looks to storm through the SJPW once more.
