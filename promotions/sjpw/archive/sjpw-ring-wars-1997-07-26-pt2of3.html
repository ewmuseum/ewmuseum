Ring Wars

26 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ring Announcer: This next match is one fall, 15 minute time limit, and is a first round match in the Super J Crown Tournament [crowd cheers]. Introducing first, from Osaka, weighing 261lbs, "THE MODERN DAY SAMURAI" HI-RO KA-NE-MO-TO!!! [crowd cheers as Hiro & Ryu Taka head to the ring] ER: Hiro really needs to break his losing streak, but he's up against the man many feel is the uncrowned All Asian champion! Ring Announcer: His opponent, from Kanagawa, and weighing 260lbs, A-KI-RA- HA-SHI-MO-TO!!! [crowd goes nuts as Akira heads to the ring, occasionaly stopping to high-five with the fans] AO: Akira is the best technician in SJPW; Hiro is much like Hashimoto, but less experienced, even though he's older. Both men shake as the bell sounds; that sign of sportsmanship tells us how this match will go now! ER: Both men are the same size, with Hiro outweighing Akira by 1lb. They lock up, and Hiro maneuvers Akira into a hammerlock. Akira's arm was injured at Battlecade '97, and some say it isn't 100% yet. Akira tries a back elbow, but Hiro ducks it, and cinches down on the hammerlock. Akira locks his other arm behind Hiro's head, and snap mares him over. Hiro gets to his feet, and Akira lays into him with a series of swift kicks, some to his chest, but most to his leg [crowd ooooohs] AO: Akira comes off the ropes... Axe Ki-Hiro catches the leg, and takes Akira down with a dragonscrew legwhip! [crowd applauds]. He gets up quickly, and catches Akira's shoulder with a elbowsmash! He locks on a top wristlock, and continues to land elbows on the shoulder! [crowd ooohs] ER: Akira flips over, reverses the wristlock, and executes a snap dropkick to Hiro's knee! [crowd oooohs] Hiro drops to one knee, and Akira lands a series of chops to Hiro's shoulder! AO: Both men seem to have similar gameplans. Hiro grabs Akira by the tights, and rams his head into Akira's gut...again...again. Akira doubles over, and Hiro grabs Akira's other arm, jumps up, and monkeyflips him over! [crowd applauds] ER: Both men up, and Hiro catches Akira as they lock up with a knee to the gut... T-bone suplex! [crowd ooohs] Akira landed on his shoulder! Hiro lays Akira's arm out, and drops a leg right on it. He rolls Akira over, and locks on another hammerlock. AO: Akira tries to get to his feet, but only gets as far as the sitting position. Hiro rams a knee on Hiro's hammerlocked arm, then drags him to his feet. He goes for a belly to back suplex, but Akira flips over, and dropkick's Hiro in the back of the knee! [crowd oooohs] ER: Hiro falls to one knee again, and Akira comes off the ropes, and lays a vicious Axe Kick to his shoulder! [crowd ooohs] Akira drags Hiro to his feet, and rams him shoulderfirst into the corner [crowd oooohs] AO: Akira goes to ram him into the corner again, but Hiro blocks it, stomps on Akira's foot, then catches him with a back elbow to the side of his head. He turns around, lifts Akira up, pressing him over his head [crowd oooohs], and drops him shoulder first onto the corner himself! [crowd oooohs] How did he get him up after taking that kind of damage? ER: I don't know, but you can see both men's arm & shoulders look hurt. You can see the brusises on their arms! Hiro drags Akira to his feet, and takes him down with a short arm lariat. He goes to the second rope, and leaps off with a stomp to Akira's shoulder! [crowd ooohs] AO: Hiro drags Akira to his feet once more, and lifts him up over his shoulder. Akira drops behind him...Saito Suplex! [crowd applauds] He dumped Hiro on his shoulder! Akira locks on an armbar, drags Hiro to his feet, and catches him with a hammerlock slam. He drags Hiro up again, and throws him through the ropes to the floor, and follows him out! [crowd oooohs] Ref: One! ER: Akira has him in that armbar again, but Hiro counters into a Hammerlock, and rams Akira shoulderfirst into the apron! [crowd ooohs] Ref: Two! AO: Hiro now landing forearms onto Akira's shoulder, but Akira lands a back elbow, staggering Hiro back. He turns around, and rams Hiro shoulderfirst into the ringpost! [crowd oooohs] Ref: Three! ER: Akira rolls Hiro back in, and goes in after him. He stands Hiro up, and starts to lay in a series of rapidfire kicks to the back of his leg and chest. Hiro stands staggered in the middle of the ring... Akira goes for another kick.. Hiro catches the leg, grabs Akira with his other arm, and takes him over into a modified fallaway slam! [crowd applauds] AO: Good move by Hiro; it was similar to the move Mr. Big did earlier to Goro Honda. Both men slow getting to their feet. They've taken alot of punishment tonight! Hiro kicks Akira in the gut, and sets him up... jumping piledriver! [crowd oooohs] ER: Hiro goes to the corner, jumps to the top rope... split legged moonsault to Akira's back! [crowd applauds] Hiro looks pretty wiped, but he looks like he's ready to end it. He drags Akira up, and whips him into the corner, following him in with a lariat. He sits Akira onto the top rope, and motions to the crowd [crowd cheers] AO: It looks like he's going for the Samurai Slam... He starts to set Akira up, but Akira headbutts him, and jumps over him off the top rope... Kataha-jime! [crowd ooohs, then cheers] He locked it on Hiro while he's on the turnbuckle! He moves up, and locks on the bodyscissors... Hiro falls backward; the ref goes to count... one...two...THREE!!! [crowd starts to cheer, then suddenly stop, not sure who won] ER: Who got pinned? The ref goes to talk to the ring announcer... Ring Announcer: Your winner, as a result of a pinfall, HIRO KANEMOTO! [crowd applauds; some streamers hit the ring] AO: I can't believe it! He beat Hashimoto! He's the #1 seed to win! This can't be! I didn't see both his shoulders on the mat! ER: Neither did I, but the ref obviously saw it different. Hashimoto looks totally stunned at this loss; He walks over to Kanemoto, and turns him around [crowd goes silent] Hiro and Akira are eye to ey, talking. Both men have an intense look on their faces, and security is starting to move closer to the ring... Akira steps back, and offers his hand to Hiro! Hiro looks at his hand, then the crowd [crowd chants "Hiro! Hiro!"] Hiro shakes his hand! [crowd cheers] AO: Good sportsmanship once again from these two men. They are so much alike! I'm glad that didn't get ugly, as I think both of these men are worthy of respect. Hiro has accomplished something incredible; he beat the best technician in SJPW! [camera shows Akira raising Hiro's arm in victory, then both men embrace. Hiro holds open the ropes for Akira, and both men head up the runway together, slapping hands with the fans] ER: Now that the ringside area is clear, we go to Sharon Akiyama in the interview area, with a special interview..... [camera cuts to Sharon Akiyama at the Interview stage...] [Sharon Akiyama is in the ring, dressed in a light green business suit. She takes the house mic, and gives the crowd a somewhat disgusted look.] Sharon: Hello fans, it's time for me to conduct an interview with a man I personally find to be... to the say the least... revolting. He is the number one ranked contender for the SJPW Cruiserweight title... SHINJI "BLOCKBUSTER" HAMADA! [The curtains part in the back and out comes Shinji Hamada. He's wearing torn jeans, a SJPW t-shirt, and a black leather jacket. He smokes his cigarette as he walks down the aisle, shaking his head in disgust as the fans cheer him. He climbs into the ring, blows a few rings of smoke upwards, then drops the cigarette to the mat and crushes it out with his boot.] Sharon: Let's get right down to business shall we. You requested this interview time and you got it. What do you have to say this time? Shinji Hamada: Sharon.. I have a few things to say tonight. First off, let me address something that Eddie Rose and Akira Ono brought up a few weeks ago. "Is Shinji Hamada a member of the Reign of Terror"? AO: Good! He's going to tell us! I hope he isn't being foolish like young Alex Akira... Sharon: Well, it's a good question now that you and Ace Kilcannon seem to have rekindled your friendship. Shinji: The answer is NO, I am NOT a member of RoT. Ace and I are friends again, and we may very well reform our tag team... but I am not a member of RoT and I have no plans on becoming a member of RoT. ER: You were right Akira; he's not in the RoT. Sharon: Well that's fairly straightforward. Now what about the challenge made to you by Tiger Kikuchi? He said he knows your family? Shinji: Yes, Tiger Kikuchi knows my family. I've known Kikuchi for a long time. And Kikuchi... it'll be my pleasure to DRIVE YOU THROUGH THE MAT WITH MY POWER BOMB! [crowd booos] I'll take your challenge old man, and after I cripple you... the way I crippled Meru the other day... I'll take apart the next person stupid enough to get in my path! Kikuchi... I hope you like being a wrestling teacher, because when I get through with you that will be the only thing you'll ever do in this sport again! AO: Now that is a boast I doubt he can back up. Kikuchi is too good a wrestler for him to take out. If he was talking about Ruckus, who is still a rookie, maybe, but Kikuchi? I doubt he can do this. Shinji: Now.. let's talk about that lying piece of garbage, Super Tengu. I saw him running his mouth again the other day, saying that my "ex-wife" works in a brothel. Well, as usual the fool called Super Tengu shows himself to be a liar, since I have *NEVER* been married, and I've never even *dated* someone named "Sasami". So I've just exposed Tengu as a liar on *that* count, and soon Yamashita will prove that Tengu's accusations of my bribing opponents are also lies. ER: Mr. Yamashita did say soon there would be an announcement concerning that investigation. Shinji: But let's examine his statement a bit closer shall we? Play the tape! [over the arena PA system we hear this quote from Super Tengu's interview: "I had a wonderful time giving her 'The Tengu Special' in that brothel she now works in [laughs maniacally]"] Shinji: Right there you have Tengu's admission that he has to PAY FOR IT! [crowd booos] He has to go to the brothels because that's the ONLY way a pathetic excuse for man such as Super Tengu can feel the warmth of a woman. Tell me something Sharon... we always hear Tengu has been sleeping with this woman one week, then another the next... never the same woman twice. And do you know why Sharon? Oh.. of course *you* know Sharon... because Super Tengu is an impotent man.. unable to please a woman! That is why he goes from one woman to another... because no woman will take him to her bed for a second time! Only *you* Sharon are compassionate enough to try and help Tengu overcome his .... shortcomings! That is why Tengu is so desperate to keep the title, to avoid facing Shinji Hamada... because the gold belt is the only thing near his waist that proves he is a man! [Shinji suddenly grabs Sharon, gives her a long hard kiss, then leaves the ring, lighting another cigarette as he walks down the aisle, laughing to himself.] AO: Hamada's new attitude is good for him, but he emulates Tengu too much. He allows himself to be fooled into thinking he is his own man, yet he still tries to be like Tengu! ER: His new attitude is disgusting Akira. He was once the second most popular wrestler in SJPW! AO: And now he is tied for most popular! ER: Up next: The Japanese wrestling debut of the IWF/WOW world heavyweight champion, Angel of Death! Ring Announcer: This next match is one fall, 15 minute time limit, and is a first round match in the Super J Crown Tournament [crowd cheers]. Introducing first, from Sapporo, weighing 237lbs, DE-VIL TSU-RU-TA!!! [crowd makes no sound as Tsuruta & Angel Mishima head to the ring] AO: This is his debut for SJPW, and he has a mighty task ahead of him! Ring Announcer: His opponent, from Toronto, Canada, he is the IWF/WOW World Heavyweight Champion, ANGEL OF DEATH!!! [crowd cheers wildly as AoD and his wife, Heather, head to the ring] ER: The fans seem to like AoD. AO: Of course; he outlasted all others after drawing #1 in the IWF/WOW ID4 rumble! He truly is a champion! ER: There's the bell, and Tsuruta starting off with a drop toehold on AoD, and turning into a half crab, but AoD grabs the ropes. He gets up, and Tsuruta nails him with a spinwheel kick, and follows it up with a reverse knife edge chop [crowd "Whoos"] AO: The Devil works fast, does he not? Devil now with a dropkick, sending AoD to the ropes; he comes off the ropes, and nail's Devil with a European uppercut! [crowd ooohs] He scoops Devil up, and slams him hard to the mat! ER: AoD drags Tsuruta up, whips him to the ropes, while running to the opposite ropes... flying spinning elbowsmash catches Tsuruta right on the jaw! [crowd oooohs] AoD comes off the ropes again... kneelift! AO: AoD quickly turned this around on the rookie. AoD now with a textbook vertical suplex on Devil, sending him crashing to the mat. AoD is slowly taking Devil apart. He drags Devil to his feet, and whips him into the corner. He follows in with a Stinger Splash, but Devil catches him in the face with his boots! [crowd ooohs] AoD staggers back... Devil charges him, and doubles him over with a kick to the gut... Running powerbomb! [crowd applauds] Cover...one...two..kickout! [crowd applauds] ER: Good comeback by the rookie. He starts to apply a figure four to AoD, but AoD counters with a thumb to the eye [crowd ooohs]. Tsuruta falls back, and the ref admonishes AoD for the illegal move. Both men on their feet, with Tsuruta still covering his eye. AoD grabs him from behind... Saito Suplex! [crowd applauds] AO: AoD lifts Devil up, and sits him on the top rope; he climbs on the second rope... belly to back superplex! [crowd ooohs] This could be it! Angel Mishima is on the apron, and starts arguing with the referee about something. One of the ring attendants is climbing in the ring; it's "TROUBLE MAN" MARTIN RICHARDS! What is he doing here? AoD is going to the top rope, and Richards shakes the ropes, crotching him! He goes up the turnbuckles... HURRICANRANA off the top rope by Richards! [crowd oooohs; then applauds] ER: Another ring attendant is in the ring now; it's PATTY BABES CARTER! He taps Richards on the shoulder..he turns..kick to the gut by Carter... BABY BOMBSHELL on Richards! [crowd applauds] PBC rolls Richards out of the ring, then rolls out himself! AO: Devil is on his feet now, and he sets AoD up as Angel gets off the apron... Fisherman's suplex; cover...one...two...thre-KICKOUT! [crowd applauds] Devil draws his thumb across his throat; he lifts AoD up... Kataha-jime! AoD immediately forces himself backwards, and Devil is crushed into the corner! [crowd ooooohs] ER: Devil calls his version of the Kataha-jime "The Broken Halo" AoD turns around, and nails him with a European uppercut, then picks him up, turns around and... Stun Gun! [crowd oooohs] AO: AoD is going to the top rope now... he sets himself up....DEATH FROM ABOVE! Cover...one...two...THREE!!! [crowd applauds] Ring Announcer: Your winner, ANGEL OF DEATH!!! [crowd applauds; streamers hit the ring as fans start chanting "AoD! AoD!"] ER: What an incredible match. AoD survives the attack by Richards, and pulls off a win over the impressive rookie, Devil Tsuruta. So now Angel of Death and Lord Robert D'Artois from IWF/WOW have advanced to round two. Why was Richards here though? AO: Richards has been seen at the SJPW corporate headquarters, lobbying for a return match with Patty Babes Carter. After tonight, I think Yamashita will give him that rematch. It's no secret that everyone in IWF/WOW are gunning for AoD's title, and Richards is no exception. If he had cost AoD the match, he could have goaded him into giving him a title shot. It was a good plan, but PBC spoiled it for him. First Nik Nyce, now Martin Richards; who can Carter call friend? ER: Zero Gravity is PBC's friend and ally, and he's up next against one of the hottest Cruiser & Middleweights we have, Jet Jaguar. Let's go back to ringside, and start this match! Ring Announcer: This next match is one fall, 15 minute time limit, and is a first round match in the Super J Crown Tournament [crowd cheers]. Introducing first, from the Dark Side of the Moon, weighing 270lbs, he is the IFW Bronze Heavyweight champion, ZE-RO GRAV-ITY!!! [crowd applauds as Zero Gravity heads to the ring. As he heads to the ring, he stops as AoD starts to walk up the runway. The two men stare at each other, drawing an 'ooooh' from the crowd. ZG nods to AoD, and moves out of his way. He high fives Patty Babes Carter as he enters the ring] AO: Interesting face off between AoD and Zero Gravity. Ring Announcer: His opponent, from Nogoya, weighing 190lbs, JET JA-GUAR!!! [crowd cheers as Jet Jaguar charges down the runway to the ring, he high fives AoD as he passes him...] ER: Zero Gravity just nailed Jaguar with a clothesline off the apron as he hit ringside! [crowd oooohs]. He drags him up in a headlock... Bulldog on the concrete floor! [crowd ooohs] AO: Gravity rolls Jaguar into the ring, and the match is officially underway now. This is disgusting, and I love it! That will teach Jaguar to pay too much attention to the fans! ER: Gravity now kicking away at Jaguar's ribs; He lifts Jaguar up, over his head [crowd oooohs].... Gutbuster! [crowd oooohs] Jaguar could have broken ribs from that! AO: Jaguar's head is bleeding from the bulldog on the floor. Gravity is going to the top rope; he sets himself as Jaguar gets to his feet... Flying clothesline from the top! [crowd oooohs] Jaguar does a 360 in mid-air! ER: Gravity now with a scoop slam on Jaguar... he's pointing to the top rope! He's going to the top again! He heads up to the top rope... it looks like he's going to try a moonsault... Jagaur MOVED! [crowd ooohs] Gravity landed on nothing but mat! AO: Jaguar runs to the ropes... springboard moonsault! He rolls Gravity over for a cover...one...two.kickout! [crowd applauds] Jaguar lifts Gravity into a setup position... Tiger Bomb! [crowd ooohs] One...two..kickout! [crowd applauds] ER: Jagaur has Gravity up once more... German Suplex! [crowd ooohs] Jaguar bridges back...one...two...th-kickout! Jaguar whips Gravity to the ropes... Spinning backbreaker! [crowd cheers] Jaguar runs to the ropes... somersault splash; cover...one...two.. kickout! [crowd applauds] He can't put Gravity away! AO: Jaguar is heading to the top rope; I think he's going for the Jaguar Special. If he hits it, it will be all over! He gets to the top, and looks to the crowd... Gravity MOVES! [crowd oooohs] Jaguar lands hard on his leg! ER: Gravity drags Jaguar up... Swinging neckbreaker! [crowd ooohs] He whips Jaguar to the ropes... Powerslam! [crowd oooohs] Gravity now with a stomp to Jaguar's bloody head, then drops an elbow on his chest. He gets up, lifts Jaguar into a set up position... GRAVITY BOMB!!! [crowd ooohs] Cover...one...two...THREE!!! [crowd cheers] Ring Announcer: Your winner, ZERO GRAVITY!!! [crowd cheers, and streamers fly into the ring] AO: Great win for Zero Gravity, who now stands at 2-2 on his SJPW record. He is finally making up for his early defeats since he got here. I expect big things for him. Jaguar could have won this match, but he caters to the fans too much, and Gravity took advantage of that, and that made all the difference. ER: I just hope Jaguar wasn't injured; he could possibly win the Middleweight title tournament! Speaking of tournaments, let's get back to ringside for the next match.
