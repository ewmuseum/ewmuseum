Zettai no Sentou

10 January 1999

The crowd is cheering in the background...] Eddie Rose: Hello everyone. I'm Eddie Rose, and this is the "Legend" Akira Ono. [Ono bows] Eddie Rose: We're proud to welcome you to the first SJPW televised event in 1999! This is the first of our new programs, titled Zettai no Sentou, or Total Combat for our english speaking fans. We've got a great show in store for you tonight! But first, let me say that it is a pleasure working with you again Akira. Akira Ono: I'm pleased to work with you as well Rose-san. I miss Kikuchi-sama though. Still, he has his destiny to face, and he must do so without me. ER: Tonight's show looks promising. We've got Ed Dean facing Banshee for the All-Asian strap, and Tetsuo Matsumoto faces both Tenru's in a three way dance for the MLWO Japan title. AO: With all the controversy surrounding Tetsuo, this match is one we will all keep a close eye. Will it be Tetsuo, or is Tengu returning? ER: Good question. As you saw by the footage at the top of the program, Shishi Fujiwara defeated Carlos Liger Jr. for the PJW Japan National Juniorheavyweight title, so he now goes on next week to face Kagemusha in the first round of the PJW mini-tournament to determine a new champion. AO: I am so glad he defeated one of Moditan's lackeys! What a tremdous comeback for him after being defeated by Ed Dean December 27th. Congratulations to Shishi Fujiwara. You have made all of Chiimu Tora, ne, all of Super Japan, proud by your actions. Your victory has restored some of the honor lost at the hands of Ed Dean! ER: Akira, you said a mouthfill! Let's get to the ring, and get this show underway! ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / "Vicious" Vladimir Vorshenko vs X-Treme /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ Vorshenko made his debut, and lived up to his title. At the bell, he locked up with X-Treme, and laid into him with a series of stiff European uppercuts. X-Treme backed up, and Vorshenko scooped him up, then dropped down into a backbreaker. He held X-Treme over his knee, bending him backwards. He let his opponent go, then grabbed his legs, and turned him over into a boston crab. X-Treme made it to the ropes, and Vorshenko broke the hold. Vorshenko brought X-Treme to his feet, and the masked man caught him with a kneelift, then sent Vorshenko to the mat with a short-arm clothesline. X-Treme started to stomp away at Vorshenko, but the wily russian caught the ankle, and was able to twist under X-Treme, and take him over with a modified legwhip. While X-Treme tried to get to his feet, Vorshenko nailed him with an elbowsmash, then whipped him to the ropes. Vorshenko crouched low, and as X-Treme came off the ropes, caught him with a stomach claw. He locked his other arm around his opponent, and lifted him off the mat, the masked X-Treme quickly submitting from the intense pain. Winner: "Vicious" Vladimir Vorshenko by submission ER: Vorshenko making an impressive debut. That Volga Spike is a nasty submission hold. AO: Agreed. I wonder if Vorshenko & Vladikoff know each other? Both men were in the military, and both served in the russian Spetznatz. ER: Interesting question. We'll have to look into that one sometime. AO: I think we should. ER: Our next match was going to pit The Great Kamikazee against Cham Linh, but Kamikazee decided not to sign with SJPW. So, Toshiaki Takada agreed to take the match. AO: He did? ER: Yes. Should be interesting, don't you think? ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ /Toshiaki "The Pyro" Takada vs Cham Linh /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ The Koubousen Geijutsu Senshuken, who has only wrestled once since SJPW reopened, decided to test himself against the Vietnamese martial arts expert. Linh started off hot by nailing Takada with a series of kicks to his leg & body, and Takada staggered back from the attack. Linh then hit a jumping thrust kick that sent Takada over the ropes to the floor. Linh grabbed the ropes, and slingshot over them, coming down on Takada's back with a stomp. Linh grabbed Takada, and stood him up. He went for a spinning roundhouse kick, but the champion caught his leg, dropped to his knees, and nailed Linh with a low blow. Takada then took a chair a fan handed him, and plastered Link with it. He dropped the chair, and DDT'd Linh onto it, and the crowd roared it's approval. Takada took out his trademark fork, and ripped Linh's forhead open. With Linh bleeding profusely, Takada hit a Fisherman's Buster on the chair, then rolled the dazed Linh into the ring. He grabbed a table from under the ring, and slid it into the ring. Takada entered the ring, and grabbed Linh. Link caught him with a ridgehand to the throat, and Takada doubled over, cluthing his throat. Linh hit an axe kick to the back of Takada's head. Linh set up the table, and tried to place Takada on it, but the champion raked Linh's eyes, then sent him to the mat with a stiff running lariat. He set the table on fire, which got the crowd excited, then sat Linh on the top rope. He climbed up with him, yelled to the crowd, then hit an Exploder Superplex through the burning table. The crowd went nuts as Takada draped an arm over Linh for the three count. Winner: Toshiaki Takada by pinfall. ER: That was an exciting match! AO: If you like burning tables & chairshots. ER: What do you have against Koubousen Geijutsu matches? AO: The ones like these are garbage matches. There is little skill involved. Just violence. Maybe in a gaijin country like the USA this is considered competative. but in Nippon, it is, and always will be, GARBAGE! Now when someone like Steel Serpent faces a man like The Mandarin, that's competition. That is the true spirit of what Koubousen Geijutsu is all about. ER: That's an interesting opinion Akira. AO: It's not an opinion. It's fact. ER: Akira, our next match should be a great one. Your old partner, Mitsunori Takizawa faces Frankie "The Thumper" Douglas. AO: Douglas? HA! Takizawa will wipe the mat with him! "The Thumper" is nothing, he betrayed Super Japan to SHVF, just like Ed Dean. It will be an honor to watch Takizawa steamroll on him! I hope Dean is watching as well, because he is next after Takizawa beat Douglas. ER: You think Takizawa can beat Ed Dean? AO: Of course! He should be wrestling Dean instead of Douglas. But that maniac Yamashita forced Takizawa into this match against such an unworthy opponent! ER: Unworthy? Douglas beat you for the All Japan title in Old Japan, didn't he? AO: Never mind that. Just know that Douglas is going to get beat and Dean will fall to Takizawa as well. ER: Takizawa is a great fighter but not even Onslaught or Hamada couldn't stop Dean. There is a line of thought that Takizawa was not forced into this match by Yamashita, but that he wanted this match so he wouldn't have to face Dean! AO: Nonsense! Takizawa can beat Dean becuse he can. By the way, I understand Misae is here tonight. How is she doing? ER: [trying to quickly change the subject] Okay, I understand that Douglas is about to make his way to the ring...thank God! ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Mitsunori Takizawa vs Frankie Douglas /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [The shot shifts to the ring, where annoucer Nishi Toju is making his introduction...] Nishi: This next match is one fall with a twenty minute time limit. First, making his way down the isle. At 6 foot 6 inches, 285 pounds, from San Antonio, Texas....FRAN-KIE "THE THUMPER" DOUG-LAS! ["Fall" by Click blares through the pa system. The crowd erupts into a heel pop as Douglas emerges from the backstage area. Douglas is wearing a long and flashy green robe with gold lettering. As he walks to the ring, he jawjacks with the fans a bit. They seem to be yelling at him about SHVF. Once Douglas gets to the ring, he opens his robe a bit to the ring attendant, trying to get a reaction out her. She says nothing, and Douglas takes off his robe showing his muscular body with bronze skin. He has red trunks, black boots, elbow and knee pads. He runs his hand through his blonde hair and walks into the ring.] Nishi: His opponent, hailing from Tokyo, Japan. He stands 6 feet tall and weighs 300 pounds...MIT-SU-NOR-I TAK-I-ZA-WA! [The crowd erupts as "Too Old to Rock and Roll, Too Young to Die" by Jethro Tull comes through the speakers. Takizawa emerges from the back to a big heel pop. Some fans are cheering him on, because of the conflict between SJPW and SHVF. Takizawa's body is bulky and scarred heavily. He is weaing baggy blue tights, black boots and blue elbow pads. He sneers at the fans as he walks down the aisle. Takizawa has long scraggily black hair with a long "Fu Manchu" mustache. He gets into the ring, turns to the corner and slaps salt in his hands as a token of "good luck"] ER: A battle of two vets here. Takizawa the twenty year pro and Douglas a ten year vet. AO: That's right, Takizawa has twenty years experience and that means that he is going to demolish Douglas! ER: Maybe so... [Rose is cut off as Douglas amubushes Takizawa with a double axe handle to the back as Takizawa still has his back turned, just having completed his "good luck" ceremony. Douglas hits the massive Takizawa in the back again and then pulls Takizawa around by the hair, hitting him with a few forearm shots to the face.] ER: Douglas with the early advantage. AO: HA! He got him from behind! See what "honor" these SHVF traitiors have? It will not work though, Takizawa will come back and beat him! [Takizawa staggers back from the forearm shots and Douglas whips him into the ropes and hits him with a nice powerslam sending Takizawa into the mat! Douglas is quick to get up and gives Takizawa a few kicks to the chest. Douglas pulls Takizawa up by the hair and gives him a few punches. Douglas then goes back to the ropes to get some momentum and looks to hit a running clothesline. As Douglas nears Takizawa to hit him with the clothesline, Takizawa quickly sidesteps and gives Douglas a knee to the gut which doubles over.] ER: Good quickness by Takizawa. AO: What would you expect? [Takizawa raises Douglas up and bit and hits him with a thrust kick to the gut which sends Douglas to the mat. Takizawa quickly follows up with a huge Avalanche Splash on Douglas! Takizawa then goes for an illegal choke to the throat. Douglas writhes in pain as referee Goto Kyoju begins to count on Takizawa. Takizawa lets go just before a count of 5. Douglas rolls over onto his stomach, grabbing his throat. The referee admonishes Takizawa for the choke but Takizawa pays no attention and kicks Douglas in the back. Douglas tries to get up but is met with a nerve hold on the trapezius by Takizawa. Douglas winches and the referee asks Douglas if he wants to quit. He screams out "NO!". Douglas stays in the hold for near a minute but finally edges his way to the ropes for the break. The referee once again begins to count Takizawa for the break. Again, he finally breaks near the count of 5. The referee again admonishes him. This allows Douglas to get to his feet. Takizawa tries to run in with a forearm shot but is met by a Douglas kick to the gut. Another kick to the gut and another. Takizawa staggers back and Douglas hits him with a stiff running clothesline that pushes Takizawa back more. Douglas quickly gouges the eyes and picks up Takizawa with a nice scoop slam. Douglas then hits an elbow drop and goes for the pin: 1 - KICKOUT!] ER: Takizawa out at one! AO: Please! [Douglas hits a knee drop on Takizawa and pulls up Takizawa. He sets up Takizawa for an impressive Snap Suplex. He follows that up with a leg drop and once again pulls Takizawa up. Douglas Irish Whips Takizawa into the corner and follows him in quickly with a huge clothesline in the corner! Douglas goes for a boot choke in the corner and breaks the hold at 4. He punches Takizawa and again Irish Whips him into the opposite corner. Takizawa counters and sends Douglas into the corner! He follows it up witha a huge Avalanche on Douglas that leaves him crushed in the corner. He pulls Douglas out of the corner and looks to go a belly to belly suplex. Douglas counters and belly to belly's Takizawa into the corner! The crowd responds with a huge "Ooh"] ER: What a move! AO: Takizawa is just playing possum. [Takizawa falls to the mat from the belly to belly and Douglas puts him in a Boston Crab! The referee asks Takizawa if he wants to give up and Takizawa responds "NO!". Takizawa tries to get to the ropes but cannot. He begins to push up to break the hold but falls. He then pushes up again and sends Douglas flying into the turnbuckle head first! Takizawa gets up slowly, holding his back and pulls Douglas back from the corner and goes for and inverted scoop into a spinning powerbomb! Takizawa then once again looks to hit another Avalanche Splash but Douglas moves out of the way! Takizawa plants himself face first in the mat. Douglas quickly gets up and hits an elbow to the back of Takizawa's head, puts the boots to him and pulls him up. He gets behind Takizawa and hits with a massive German Suplex! He then pulls Takizawa up by the hair, gives him a kick to the gut, which doubles him over and looks to ready himself to powerbomb the massive Takizawa!] ER: This could be the beginning of the end! AO: Douglas cant hit a powerbomb on Takizawa! He is too huge! [Douglas hoists Takizawa into the air and begins to run a few steps then plants Takizawa with a Running Power Bomb! The crowd responds with a huge pop! Douglas screams out "GET READY TO GET THUMPED!" and pulls up Takizawa by the hair. Douglas is setting up for his signature Heart Pucnch!] ER: This should be it! AO: NO! [Douglas rares back to hit punch. As he goes for it, Takizawa pokes him the eyes and hits a huge URANAGE ending Douglas down to the canvas. He pounds Douglas with some punches to the face and pulls him up. He grabs Douglas by the throat and lifts him up into the air, holding him for a moment and levels him with a monstrous CHOKESLAM! He kicks Douglas and then goes back to the corner. Takizawa bends over and goes into his tights and pulls out some sort of foriegn object.] ER: What the hell is that? AO: Takizawa was just adjusting his tights! [Takizawa hides the object in his hand and obscures it from the referees vision. By this time, Douglas has managed to get up and hits a few quick punches to Takizawa's gut. It seems not to fase Takizawa. Takizawa rares back and flattens Douglas with a shot from the foriegn object. Douglas goes down quick and hard. Takizawa quickly "adjusts" his tights again and puts the object back in his tights. Douglas gets up, a bit groggy and his met with a few palm thrusts by Takizawa that staggers Douglas back. He throws Douglas into the ropes and hits with a spinning back fist that sends Douglas down hard! Doulgas gets up yet again!] ER: These two guys are killing each other! AO: HAHA! Takizawa has him where he wants him! [Douglas tries to get some momentum off the ropes but is engulfed by Takizawa. Takizawa has him locked in a belly to belly position. Takizawa pauses for a moment, then lets loose a huge POWER HOIST that sees Douglas flying in the air and landing face first on the mat! The crowd gives a monster pop!] ER: WHAT A MOVE! [Takizawa pulls up Douglas, picks him up in the position for a Fallaway Slam. Takizawa bellows out: "BAKA YARO! ITAIME-NI AITAI NO KAI?" Takizawa then crushes Douglas's head into the mat with an unGodly CRADLE DDT! The crowd lets loose a huge "OOH!" Takizawa goes for the cover: ...1... ...2... ...3... **DING! DING! DING** Nishi: The winner of this match.... MITSUNORI TAKIZAWA! ER: What a match! ["Too Old to Rock and Roll, Too Young to Die" by Jethro Tull blares through the pa and the crowd gives Takizawa a huge ovation for his effort in the match. Takizawa seems unsympathetic to the crowd and sneers at them] AO: I told you, Douglas never had a chance! Ed Dean is next! ER: That would be a good match. If Tiger doesn't beat Dean, Takizawa might just do it. Although, this was not an easy match at all, Douglas put up a great fight and almmost has Takizawa. AO: Hey, isn't that Misae over there? [Rose's face turns red, and he struggles to find something to say...] ER: Er, um, I don't know. AO: Are you sure? ER: Po-po-positive! You're doing this on purpose, aren't you? AO: Me? On purpose? Why would I do such a thing? ER: Because you enjoy tormenting me. AO: I do not. ER: Yes. you do. AO: I do not, but feel free to continue behaving paranoid if you must. [Rose's face turns red]
