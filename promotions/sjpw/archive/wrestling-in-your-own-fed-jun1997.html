Wrestling in your own fed

20-30 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
----- Wrestling in your own fed (Jun 20, 1997, 12:00:00 AM) ----- [name withheld] wrote: > listen who cares ts your FED if they Whine kick them out and say Fuck you > its your you can do what you please So you think it's ok to have your own characters in an efed, and then let them win titles? If that's the case, I don't think I want to be in an efed you run. You can't just do what you please. Running an efed isn't just for your personal enjoyment. It's supposed to be fun for everyone. Not everyone will, but if most do, then you are actually accomplishing something. When I took over SJPW, I had 3 wrestlers entered under the old fedhead. I took them out, because I didn't want to use them that way. I do have a veteran wrestler, Tiger Kikuchi active, and he is used for plot purposes. He is now the mentor of one handler's wrestler, and he is helping to mold that wrestler into the kind of character the handler wants the wrestler to become. I have an upcoming angle with one handler who has an older wrestler who has been interfering in his son's matches. He's going to wrestle one of the two SJPW commentators, Akira Ono, who is a retired wrestler. I got the idea from the Steven Regal/Larry Zybysko mini-feud awhile back. The handler and I have the details worked out in advance, so no one gets screwed over. I don't see a problem with having wrestler of your own in an efed, as long as they are used properly. I like having some to help handlers who have yet to develop a strong angle going for themselves; it allows them to keep from floundering until something better comes along. It doesn't work in every efed, but on a limited basis, it works in mine. Plus, the handlers know these are wrestlers I control, and what their purpose is. I have handlers rooting for Akira Ono to kick Terry Smith's butt for his anti-Japan comments now! Anyway, just because it's your efed, doesn't mean you have total control over things. If a handler has a legitimate gripe over the way you control the wrestler, maybe you should listen first. If they are just bitching and moaning cause they lost one match, that's one thing. But if they present it in a rational manner, why kick them out? That's ridiculous, and soon you'll find yourself running a fed for no one but yourself ----- Wrestling in your own fed (Jun 21, 1997, 12:00:00 AM) ----- [name withheld] wrote: > > Quite a few PBM presidents have created their own wrestlers to play in > not only their games, but in each other's games. Within the past year, > it has lead to a war of words between the presidents, bad-mouthing in > their game bulletins, and the breakup of an overall NWA-type group, > where one true PBM champion was going to be crowned. (Another group has > taken it's place, but the bad-mouthing continues.) > If the players knew the full extent of these problems, I'm quite sure a > lot of them would have left their games, which would have meant bigger > problems. (Remember, PBM players pay for thier matches, so the PBM > presidents have a lot more to lose than you guys.) > Just another something to consider. Personally, I think there would be > too much of an opportunity to be biased towards your wrestler to the > detriment of all the other handlers. > > [name withheld], PBM player and E-Wrestling fan and observer [name withheld], Since I tried to restart a pbm game [The old UWA, which inspired Chris Devlin's Hardcore World], I know what you are talking about. However, I have to state that e-wrestling feds don't treat each other like the pbm feds do. Point in case. Chris Devlin, who I consider a friend, was the reason I decide to bring back the UWA. The person who ran it died a few years ago, and he was my closest friend. Chris even talked about us doing some stuff together. Then I don't hear from him for weeks, and when he finally does contact me, he tells me about all this stuff going down with other pbm feds, which has nothing to do with me or the fed I was doing, and tells me that I have to give him my word as a man that I won't screw him over. This from someone who has been my friend for years. I was so insulted by this, I said forget it. If he won't trust me after knowing me for years, and when I credited him for getting me to restart that game, I decided that the UWA wasn't worth it. Since I opened SJPW in April of this year, I joined with 3-4 other efeds to start the GWC, and I have deals with at least 2 other efeds [HCW and IWF/WOW]. I just got offered to do some stuff with another efed, and there are two more starting that have expressed an interest in working with me. Not one of these fedheads has ever showed me that they are suspicious of my motives. I'm in some efeds where the fedheads have their own wrestlers. Not one of them holds a title, and if they did, I would do my best to take the belt from them. The one wrestler I have in SJPW, Tiger Kikuchi, I don't even wrestle. He is there to help some handlers establish angles, and so far, he's done a good job. He's in the middle of a major war brewing, and is the voice of reason among the faces involved. In fact, the one handler whose wrestler has him as a mentor now rp's him more than I ever did. Pbm feds and efeds are different. You can't compare them. It's like Butter and Margarine.
