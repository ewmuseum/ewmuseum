SJPW House Show — Tokyo Oi Store (7/19/04)

Jul 20, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Monday, July 19th Tokyo Oi Store: 300 fans (sellout) 1) GPH Tag League: "Russian Superman" Petor Andresku & Vladimir Tarasov def Tsutomu Kobayashi & Botan Nakatoni (11:04) following a Doomsday Device. 2) El Muerte Rojo def Dragon Mask (9:47) following a Cristo del Muerte. 3) GPH Tag League: KANJI Sakaigama & "Crimson" Jin Hiroto def Dynamite Boy & Eiji Kid Shimura (12:50) following a Back Drop Suplex/Guillotine Leg drop double team. 4) "GAIJIN Extreme" Jun Suzuki def Gregor Davinsky (8:11) following a running Lariat. 5) GPH Title Match: "Ecchi Kid" Kentaro "H-Bomb" Kawasaki [C] & Noboru "Raging Bull" Nishida [CH] wrestled to a thirty minute time limit draw. - The Red Army scored their first tag team win, taking down the Crush Crew with a Doomsday Device clothesline. Both teams were very stiff with each other, with Andresku & Kobayashi trading some very stiff elbows/forearms at least three times during the match. The crowd was behind the Red Army, save for the DoD fans in attendance. - El Muerte Rojo wrestled a great match with Dragon Mask. Both men showed some fantastic chain wrestling and technical expertise. Rojo scored the win after putting Dragon Mask into a hanging Full Nelson, then dropping down into a sitout facebuster. - In a highly anticipated rematch from Zenmen Sensou, The Nagasaki Bulldogs & Visual Spirts collided once more. Both teams again went at it tooth and nail, but the Spirits again had the Bulldogs number. After taking out Shimura with a Violence Flash during an all out brawl between the four men, KANJI went to the top, and as Hiroto hit a Backdrop suplex on Dynamite Boy, KANJI hit a beautiful Guillotine legdrop off the top rope, scoring the pinfall for his team. - Jun Suzuki & Gregor Davinsky had a great match, and a very stiff one. The crowd was firmly behind Davinsky, as Suzuki seems almost universally hated. Suzuki hit a high impact Chokeslam on Davinksy, then nailed him with a very stiff Lariat when he got to his feet, and scored the pinfall. - Kawasaki's first title defence didn't go as planned for the champion. Nishida took control of the match early, using his power to keep the champion off balance. Nishida scored a two count after a series of 3 running lariats. Kawasaki fought back with some stiff kicks, but again Nishida used his power to take Kawasaki down. Kawasaki used a good old thumb to the eye to slow Nishida down, and went back to giving him some stiff kicks, as well as hitting a backdrop driver, which got him a two count on Nishida. Kawasaki went back to hitting Nishida with stiff kicks, until Nishida caught his leg, and hit a capture suplex. Nishida again tried to over power Kawasaki with his strength, pummeling him with a series of forearm shivers to the side of head, before hitting a ring jarring powerbomb. Nishida went for the cover, but again only scored a two count. Nishida then went for a superplex, but Kawasaki was able to counter it into a bodyblock, getting another two count on Nishida. Nishida went for another lariat, but Kawasaki ducked it, and nailed Nishida with a superkick. He tried to go for the Hentai Holocaust, but Nishida powered out of hit. He did another lariat then, but Kawasaki got his foot on the ropes. Nishida tried to go for another powerbomb, but Kawasaki pummeled him when he was lifted up, and brought Nishida down into a facebuster. Kawasaki went for the Hentai Holocaust and hit it, but somehow, Nishida got his foot on the ropes, breaking up the pinfall. Kawasaki was not pleased, and started laying into Nishida's prone form with soccer-like kicks. He was going for the Holocaust again, but Nishida powered out of it, and both men started exchanging stiff chops as the bell rang. Kawasaki wasn't pleased with the decision, but took his title and left the ring, a bored look on his face, ignoring Nishida challenging him to come back for five more minutes. The fans were a bit disappointed with the outcome of this match, but Nishida seemed very over with the crowd.
