SHIN-JI!!! [Hamada steps out on the runway to no music. The crowd cheers him, and he looks out at the crowd with a look of sadness on his face. He has the All Asian title over his left shoulder, and he walks to the ring slowly, almost resignedly. He enters the ring slowly, and hands the title to the referee...]
ER: Hamada looks ready to pack it in. AO: This is going to be sad Eddie-san. I don't know if I can call this match. [The referee gives both men their instructions, then signals for the bell. They lock up, and Hamada maneuvers Nyce into a side headlock. Nyce backs to the ropes, then whips Hamada across the ring. Hamada bounces off the ropes, and Nyce drops down. Hamada bounces off the opposite ropes, and Nyce catches him with an overhead belly to belly suplex! The crowd 'oooohs' as Hamada sits up, holding his back. Nyce drags Hamada to his feet, and chops him across the chest. The crowd 'whooos', and Hamada backs up. Nyce chops him again, and as the crowd 'whooos' again, Nyce attempts an irish whip, but Hamada reverses. Nyce comes off the ropes, and
Hamada sends him to the mat with a shoulderblock. He runs to the ropes, and Nyce leapfrogs as he comes off them. Hamada comes off the other side, and Nyce catches him with a sleeper! The crowd
'ooohs', then 'oooohs' again as Hamada reverses! Nyce drops down into a jawbreaker, and Hamada rolls on the mat, clutching his jaw...]
ER: Nyce is out-wrestling Hamada at every turn! He's got to get his act together! AO: He's lost his heart Eddie. He just doesn't care enough. [Nyce drags Hamada to his feet, then takes him down with a snap mare. He applies a reverse chinlock, driving his knee repeatedly into Hamada's back. Hamada's face contorts with pain, but he refuses to submit. Nyce drags Hamada to his feet, applying a side headlock. Hamada backs to the ropes, and whips Nyce across the ring. Nyce comes off the ropes, and Hamada scoops him up, then slams him to the mat. He attempts an elbowdrop, but Nyce rolls out of the way, and Hamada sits up, holding his elbow. Nyce grabs the arm, and applies an armbar. He drags Hamada to his feet with the armbar, then drives his shoulder into Hamada's shoulder. Hamada grimaces in pain, and Nyce backs Hamada into the corner. He drives his knee into Hamada's gut, then drapes his arm over the top rope, pulling down hard. Hamada screams in pain, and the ref puts the count on Nyce. He breaks at four, then drives his Knee into
Hamada's gut again. He attempts to whip Hamada to the opposite corner, but Hamada reverses, and Nyce slams chest first into the corner. He staggers backward, and Hamada grabs him, lifting him up, then planting him with a belly to back suplex. The crowd cheers, and Hamada gets to his feet, then legdrops Nyce across the chest. He makes a half-hearted cover, and Nyce kicks out at one...]
ER: Hamada isn't trying! He's wrestling like he doesn't care! AO: That's the point Eddie. At this point, he DOESN'T care! ER: Hamada has Nyce up, and he lifts him up high in the air, then falls backward into a suplex. Nyce sits up, holding his back in pain, and Hamada slowly rises to his feet.
AO: Oh no! Super Tengu is heading to ringside! [The camera shows Tengu walk to the ring, the crowd booing him. He gets to the ring, and yells at Hamada. Shinji turns towards Tengu, and his eyes brim with hatred. Tengu mocks Hamada, who turns on
Nyce, and drags him to his feet. He takes him to the mat with a snap suplex, then snaps an elbowdrop to his chest. Hamada gets back to his feet, dragging Nyce up with him. He whips Nyce to the ropes, catching him as he comes off, and presses him over is head.
The crowd 'oooohs' as Hamada holds Nyce over his head, and glowers at Tengu. Tengu simply smiles, and Hamada slams Nyce to the mat.
Hamada drags Nyce to his feet again, and takes him to the mat with a gutwrench suplex. He makes a cover, but Nyce kicks out at two...]
ER: Tengu's appearance at ringside has given Hamada inspiration! He's taking it to Nyce now! AO: Tengu came to mock Hamada, and only succeeded in inspiring him! He's trying to beat Nyce now to prove a point!
[Hamada drags Nyce to his feet, and whips him into the corner. He charges in after him with a clothesline, and Nyce sags in the corner. Hamada sits Nyce on the top rope, then climbs up after him. He tries to set Nyce up for a superplex, but Nyce punches him in the gut. As Hamada doubles over on the top rope, Nyce double underhooks his arms, then lifts him up, and comes off the top rope with a vicious gourdbuster! The crowd 'oooohs' loudly, and Tengu applauds Nyce's maneuver. Nyce rolls Hamada over for a cover, but
Hamada kicks out with authority at two. The crowd 'oooohs' as
Hamada miraculously rises to his feet as if nothing happened to him! Nyce backs away in fear, and Hamada kicks him in the gut,
Nyce doubles over, and Hamada hammers his back with forearm shots.
Hamada grabs Nyce around the waist, and lifts him up, then spikes him headfirst to the mat with a piledriver! The crowd cheers, but instead of covering Nyce, Hamada walks over to Tengu!]
ER: What's he doing? He needs to cover Nyce! AO: He's letting his hatred blind him! ER: Hamada & Tengu now talking trash to each other! Tengu's hopped up on the apron now! AO: Both men are arguing! Tengu spits on Hamada! [crowd oooohs] [Hamada shoves Tengu off the apron, and Tengu immediately hops back up on the apron, and shoves Hamada backwards, where the recovering Nyce catches him in a roll up! The crowd 'ooohs' as the ref drops to count, but Hamada kicks out at two! Both men roll to their feet, and Hamada goes for a lariat, but Nyce ducks it.
Hamada comes off the ropes, and slams into Nyce with a shoulderblock then sends him sprawling. The crowd cheers as Nyce crashes to the mat. Hamada drags Nyce to his feet, and whips him into the corner. He charges in after him with a clothesline, but
Nyce sidesteps, and Hamada crashes into the corner! The crowd
'oooohs', and Nyce grabs Hamada from behind in a waistlock, then takes him out of the corner with a release german suplex! The crowd 'oooohs' once more, and Tengu applauds Nyce. Nyce gets to his feet, and drags the stunned Hamada up. He takes him over to the corner, and sits him on the top rope. He turns towards the middle of the ring, grabs Hamda by the head, and nails the Nyce Finisher!
The crowd 'ooohs' as Hamada crashes face first to the mat. Tengu hops up on the apron again, and calls Nyce over. He whispers in his ear, and both men grin...]
ER: What is Tengu telling him? Nyce has the match won! What more is he going to do to Hamada?
AO: I don't know, but Tengu has gone too far! [Nyce walks back over to Hamada, and drags him to his feet. He takes him back over to the corner, and sits him on the top rope again, Nyce sets him up, then lifts him up, and plants him with a top rope fisherman's buster!]
ER: He nailed him with the Depth Charge! He used Tengu's move! AO: Adding insult to injury! ER: And he's still not done! AO: What now?
