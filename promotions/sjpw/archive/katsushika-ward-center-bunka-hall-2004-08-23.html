SJPW House Show — Katsushika Ward Center Bunka Hall (8/23/04)

Aug 25, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Monday, August 23rd, 2004 Katsushika Ward Center Bunka Hall: 350 fans (sellout) SJPW returned to Tokyo for the next sweep of shows leading to the next taping of The Zone! Hitting up the Bunka Hall, for the following set of matches: 1. Scott Starring def Jiraiya (12:25) after a top rope hurricanrana. 2. Junior Struggle! Block B: Super Bakemono def Yatoro Hori (9:09) following a springboard DDT. 3. Tsunami def El Gato Loco (13.40) following the Tsunami Driver #3. 4. Junior Struggle! Block A: "Firefly" Horo Atashi def Jet Jaguar III (16.05) following a Yakuza kick. 5. Scramble tag team Madness Main Event: Gregor Davinsky & Sergei Baranov def El Muerto Rojo & Sangre Guererro, Tsutomu Kobayashi & Botan Nakatoni, Frankie "The Thumper" Douglas & Doug Thomas (22:17) after Baranov pinned Guererro following a Tiger Suplex '95. - Starring continues to rack up wins in singles competition, this time defeating the other half of Team Raiden with a beautiful top rope hurricanrana. - Super Bakemono made sure that Yatoro Hori of SMASH Attack! remained winless in the Junior Struggle tournament, taking him out with a springboard into a DDT. - Tsunami started his comeback trail for the IICW cruiserweight title, defeated El Gato Loco with the Tsunami Driver #3. - Atashi cemented his Block A position by defeating J3 in a spirited bout. Both men traded stiff chops a few times, and J3 hit the Jaguar Special, but Atashi kicked out. After pummeling J3 with a series of stiff forearm shivers, Atashi went to the ropes, rebounded, and nearly decapitated J3 with a yakuza kick to secure the win. - The main event saw a great scramble match featuring two teams in the upcoming Gunryo tag team gauntlet. Davinsky and his nephew Baranov proved to be the dominant team, with Baranov suplexing the hell out of Nakatoni & Thomas respectively, while Davinsky chokeslammed Douglas, then did the same to Kobayashi. It came down to them and the Blood Merchants, with the BM's using their superior speed to befuddle their larger opponents with aerial moves and some mat wrestling from Rojo. Guererro went to the air one time too many, and after missing a frog splash, was pinned after Baranov hit the Tiger Suplex '95 on him. It was a great show, and the fans were pretty excited to see SJPW back in their home turf of Tokyo.
