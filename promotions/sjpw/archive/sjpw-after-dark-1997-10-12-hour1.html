After Dark, Hour 1

Jonnie Adonis is in the US wrestling in HCW, so we've taken over the show as kick off our last After Dark in Japan before we head to the US for the Invasion, USA tour! In this hour, we will see Masashi "Lightning" Lanther in singles competition, the debut of the Bonecrushers, Susan K'uang-yin making her debut against Misae Suzuki, and Mr. Big taking on the debuting Axl "Hammerhead" Yoto, who I understand is a protege of Goro Honda. Akira Ono; That is correct Eddie-San. I have also been informed that that match is Battle Arts rules, so there is no countout or disqualification. ER: Wow! That should be interesting! Let's go to our ring announcer, and get this show underway! [Camera cuts to the ring. The ring announcer, the referee, Masashi Lanther & Haruki Shiomi are standing there...] Ring Announcer: This first contest is one fall, 15 minute time limit. Introducing first, weighing 235 lbs, HA-RU-KI SHI-O-MI! [crowd applauds] AO Shiomi has no chance against Lanther! Ring Announcer: His opponent, weighing 233 lbs. MA-SA-SHI "LIGHTNING" LAN-THER!!! ["Shock Me" comes over the pa, and the crowd cheers as Lanther bows to all 4 sides of the ring] ER: This is Lanther's first singles match since his return from the now defunct MAWA. He had a bad start this past wednesday on Ring Wars when the new & improved Tokyo Express upset him and Tiger Kikuchi. AO: Cheated you mean. ER: You don't seem to have a problem with cheating. AO: Against my friends I do, and against anyone facing scum like the Gaijin or RoT! ER: The bell sounds, and Shiomi nails Lanther with a dropkick, sending him back to the ropes. He comes off, and Shiomi drops down, gets up, and takes Lanther over with a hiptoss as he comes off the other side! [crowd applauds] Shiomi looking good so far. AO: Lanther gets to his feet, and locks up; Shiomi takes Lanther over with an armdrag, but he rolls to his feet. Shiomi rushes him, and he sidesteps, pushing Shiomi towards the ropes. Shiomi comes off, and Lanther takes him over with a spinning backbreaker! [crowd cheers] ER: Lanther drags Shiomi to his feet, and executes a belly to belly suplex. He gets up again, runs to the ropes, and lands on Shiomi with a somersault splash! [crowd applauds] Lanther gets to his feet, and raises his fist in the air. [crowd cheers] AO: Lanther drags Shimoi up, then chops him across the chest [crowd "Whoooos"]; he whips Shiomi to the corner, and follows in with a running dropkick to his face! [crowd ooooohs] ER: Lanther hoists Shiomi up, and sits him on the top rope. He climbs to the top himself, and hooks Shiomi's tights....SUPERPLEX!! [Crowd oooohs, then applauds] Lanther rolls over Shiomi, gets to his feet, and runs to the corner...step ladder Moonsault! [crowd ooohs] AO: Lanther isn't done yet! He drags Shiomi to his feet, and locks his arms behind him...Double underhook Tiger Bomb! [crowd ooohs] 1...2...3!!! [crowd cheers!] He hit the Lanther Bomb! Ring Announcer: Your winner, MASASHI "LIGHTNING" LANTHER!!! [crowd cheers] ER: So Lanther scorse a win, and now moves on to face Blackline on Ring Wars. AO: Blackline is a great wrestler, but Lanther will beat him! ER: I don't know. After that match with Sampson, I think Blackline has his number! AO: Bah! That was a draw! [The camera shows Lanther helping Shiomi out of the ring, as The Bonecrushers and the Wild Jamaicans come down to the ring.] ER: Up next: The debut of the Bonecrushers. This team also competes in AAPW, and are a top team there. AO: One of them is related to "Dashing" Derek Lankford. I like that man. Too bad his brother is a goody two shoes. ER [shaking his head]: Let's go to our ring announcer... Ring Announcer: This contest is one fall, 15 minute time limit. Introducing first, team #1, at a combined weight of 600 lbs, SHA-KA & RA-STA-MAN, the WILD JAMAICANS!!! [crowd boooos] AO: These two men should be fired for their blatant drug use! Have you ever smelled their dressing room? Ring Announcer: Their opponents, making their SJPW debut, at a combined weight of 530 lbs, AA-RON LANK-FORD & JAKE REED, the BONECRUSHERS!!! ["Ballbreaker" plays, and the crowd applauds] ER: Here we go! The Jamaicans immediately attack the Bonecrushers! Shaka is hammering Aaron Lankford with forearms, while Rastaman is headbutting Jake Reed! [crowd oooohs] AO: Shaka now throwing Lankford through the ropes, and now he & Rastaman are double headbutting Reed! [crowd oooohs] They whip him to the ropes, and send him to the mat with a double clothesline! [crowd oooohs] ER: These Wild Jamaicans are just that; WILD! Shaka now goes to his corner as Rastaman scoops Reed up, and slams him to the mat. Rastaman now stomping away on Reed! These Jamaicans are relentless! AO: They smell bad too! Rastaman now dragging Reed to his feet, and whipping him to the ropes...Reed ducks the Yakuza kick, and goes to the other side...he tags Lankford, comes off the ropes...Clothesline by Reed sends Rastaman to the mat! [crowd applauds] ER: The Bonecrushers now drag Rastaman to his feet, and whip him to the ropes...Side Suplex by Reed, flying elbowdrop off the ropes by Lankford! [crowd applauds] Reed goes to his corer, while Lankford drags Rastaman up, then plants him with a belly to back suplex. AO: Lankford drags Rastaman up again, and locks his arms behind him...Buterfly suplex! He moves to cover...1...2...Shaka breaks it up! [crowd applauds] Reed comes in the ring, and he starts to punch awaya at Shaka! [crowd oooohs] ER: The ref is losing control! Lankford now attacking Shaka! The whip him to the ropes...Reed doubles him over with a punch to the gut, and Lankford comes off the ropes, nailing him with a vicious kneelift! [crowd applauds] AO: Reed goes back to his corner, while Lankford sends Shaka over the ropes after a vicious atomic drop. Lankford drags Rastaman to his feet, and the Jamaican rakes him in the eyes! [crowd booos] ER: Lankford staggers around blinded, and Rastaman grabs him by the back of the head, and rams him headfirst into the corner! He turns Lankford around, chops him across the chest [crowd "Whoooos"], then whips him to the opposite side. He charges after Lankford... Avalanche! [crowd oooohs] AO: Lankford staggers out of the corner, and falls to the mat. Rastaman tags in Shaka, then puts Lankford into a boston crab. Shaka goes to the top rope....Diving Headbutt! [crowd oooooohs] Lankford screamed in pain! Rastaman goes to his corner, and Shaka rolls him over for the pin...1...2...Reed makes the save! [crowd applauds] ER: The ref makes Reed go to his corner, while Shaka stomps Lankford's chest. He drags Aaron to his feet, and whips him to the ropes...Lankford ducks the clothesline, and stops short. Shaka turns around...SIDEWALK SLAM by Lankford! [crowd ooooohs] AO: Lankford jumps to his feet, and yells at the crowd [crowd cheers]. He tags in Reed, who goes to the top rope. Lankford drags Shaka to his feet, then lifts him up onto his shoulders....BULLDOG by Reed! [crowd oooohs, then applauds] He hit the Facesmasher! Lankford catches Rastaman coming in the ring, and takes him over with a belly to belly suplex as Reed covers...1...2...3!!! [crowd applauds] Ring Announcer: Your winners, AARON LANKFORD & JAKE REED, THE BONECRUSHERS!!! [crowd applauds, and streamers hit the ring] ER: Excellant win for the Bonecrushers here tonight. The Jamaicans gave them a fight, but they prevailed in the end. AO: If the Jamaiacans stopped smoking their pot, they would be a more dangerous team. ER [chuckling]: Then they wouldn't really be Wild Jamaicans now, would they? AO: You have a point. An immoral one, but a point nonetheless. ER: Up next, we have the debut of Susan K'uang-yin, as she faces Misae Suzuki, who is on a roll after her victory over Sake Edo at the ppv. Let's go to ringside, where these two competitors are waiting to lock up... Ring Announcer: This next contest is one fall, 15 minute time limit. Introducing first, weighing 105 lbs, MI-SAE SU-ZU-KI!! ["Parasitic People" by Super Junkey Monkey plays, and the crowd cheers] AO: I like Misae's new look. Did she do that for you Eddie-san? [Rose blushes] Ring Announcer: Her opponent, making her SJPW DEBUT, weighs in at 119 lbs, she is SU-SAN K-UANG-YIN!! ["Welcome To The Jungle" plays, and the crowd applauds] ER [clearing his throat]: The bell sounds, and Susan goes right for Misae, and nails her with a series of quick kicks to her ribcage! [crowd oooohs] Misae backs to the ropes, and Susan chops her across the chest [crowd "Whooos"]. Susan now whips Misae to the opposite side...flying clothesline! [crowd oooohs] AO: I like this woman! Agressive. Susan now scoops Misae up, then slams her to the mat. She runs to the ropes, and hits a snap legdrop. She gets to her feet, and hits another..another..another! [crowd oooohs] ER: Susan now dragging Misae to her feet, and chops her again [crowd "Whooos"], then takes her over with a snap mare. Susan now going to the corner, and hops up on the second rope...elbowdrop! [crowd ooooohs] She covers...1...2...kickout! [crowd applauds] AO: Susan gets up, then drags Misae to her feet. She grabs her in a waistlock, but Misae reverses...GERMAN SUPLEX! [crowd oooohs] Suzuki just dumped Susan on her head! Misae is still weak from Susan's early assault, and is leaning on the ropes, trying to catch her breath. ER: Susan sits up, and holds the back of her head. That suplex had alot of impact to it. Misae definitely has gotten more aggressive herself since Sake Edo worked with her. Susan now starts to get to her feet...Misae bolts off the ropes, and catches her with a high cross bodyblock; Susan crashes to the mat, and Misae hooks the leg...1..kickout! [crowd applauds] AO: Both women get to the their feet, and Misae catches Susan with a backfist to the face. She staggers back, and Misae sends her to the mat with a beautiful standing dropkick! ER: Misae now on her feet, and she charges Susan, who is getting to her feet...rana by Misae sends Susan across the ring, but she rolls to her feet. Misae charges her again...Superkick by Susan! [crowd applauds] AO: Misae staggers back, and Susan moves in, doubling Misae over with a kick to the gut. She runs to the ropes...Rocker Dropper! [crowd applauds] Susan rolls Misae over, and covers...1...2.. kickout! [crowd applauds] ER: Susan goes to the corner, and starts to climb the ropes. Misae sits up, and starts to rise. Susan gets to the top, and balances herself...Missle dropkick! [crowd applauds] Misae did a 360 in mid-air! AO: Susan covers again...1...2...kickout! [crowd applauds]. I can't believe this! Susan drags Misae to her feet, and chops her across the chest [crowd "Whooos} Misae fires back with a chop of her own [crowd "Whooos"]! ER: Susan now kicks Misae, who retaliates with a kick of her own. Both of them go for a dropkick, and crash onto the mat! [crowd oooohs] AO: Both of them are quick to get to their feet, and Misae goes for a spinwheel kick, but Susan ducks. Susan goes for a spinwheel kick of her own, but Misae ducks. They turn to face each other, and Susan leaps...Sunset Flip! [crowd applauds] Misae tries to fight it, but Susan gets her over...1...2..kickout! [crowd applauds] ER: Both of them get to their feet, and Misae catch Susan, slamming her to the mat. She hits a standing moonsault, then covers... 1...2..kickout! [crowd applauds] AO: This match is very even! Misae drags Susan to her feet, and whips her to the ropes...springboard hurricanaran by Susan! [crowd cheers] She moves to hook the legs, but Misae reverses! [crowd cheers] Susan reverses again, and hooks the leg! The referee starts to count...1...2... [*DING* *DING* *DING*] ER: The time ran out. AO: No! Susan was so close to winning! [the camera shows the referee talking to the ring announcer as both women go to seperate corners] Ring Announcer: The referee has informed me that the time limit has expired. He therefore rules this contest...A DRAW!!! [crowd applauds] ER: That was a very close match. Susan K'uang-yin may not have this match, but she definitely took Misae Suzuki to the limit! [the camera shows Misae & Susan shaking hands, which the crowd applauds. Streamers fly into the ring] AO: These two women are very alike. Young, fast, talented. They might make an interesting tag team. ER: Too bad we don't have tag titles for the women. Not enough teams. AO: It's just as well. Robin Blaze & Dawn Rose would win them anyway! [the camera shows Misae & Susan leave, and Mr. Big entering the ring] ER: Well, it's time for our last match for the hour. Mr. Big, who was a decisive factor in his teams victory over the Hot House on Ring Wars, faces the new protege of Goro Honda. AO: I hear this guy Yoto is pretty vicious. He has to be if Goro decided to manage him! I understand we have some pre-taped comments from him? ER: That's correct. Let's show them now, then cut to our ring announcer... ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 10/11/97] (Scene fades into a training gym in mainstream Japan where Axl Yoto is training for his match against Mr. Big. He has a black torn up golds gym t-shirt and black jeans and a pair of DC's. His hair is brushcutted and he has a goatee and huge muscles. He begins to speak.) AY: (Sweating speaking tirely) Hello everyone I'am Axl "Hammerhead" Yoto SJPW's newest competer. Well SJPW first of all let me tell you guys something big guy Axl Yoto chop down the competion and win all the belts and gain all the glory, because I'am the best looking and strongest. (AXl Yoto pauses for a sec flexes his giangatic pecs and does starts talking again.) AY: Mr. Big you think 7' something and 400lbs + your a tough guy. Well you got something else coming biggie. Your nothing, but an irregular sized dud. I'am gonna prove that to all of SJPW and world. On sunday After Dark everyone gonna findout who my manager is and lemme tell you something he's one big mean dude. (Axl Yoto laughs for a second and then starts to talk with an evil grin on his face.) AY: MR. BIG PREPARED TO BE FLEXED AND DOMINATED! ( Scene fades out with Axl Yoto going back to his normal training regiment.) ------------------------------------------------------------------- [the camera cuts to the ring..] Ring Announcer: This contest is one fall, 15 minute time limit, and will be fought under Battle Arts rules! Introducing first, weighing 400 lbs, MR. BIG!! [crowd applauds] AO: Mr. Big is so massive! Ring Announcer: His opponent, making his SJPW debut, AXL "HAMMERHEAD" YO-TO!! ["Wherever I may Roam" plays, and the crowd applauds] ER: The bell sounds, and Mr. Big immediately attacks the much smaller Yoto, hammering him with his massive forearm! [crowd ooooohs] He backs Axl into the corner, and starts to kick him in the gut! [crowd oooohs again] AO: Big now grabs Yoto around the waist, and takes him out of the corner with a belly to belly suplex! [crowd oooohs] Yoto landed hard! Big goes after him, and drags him to his feet. Yoto drops to his knees, and low blows Mr. Big! [crowd ooooohs] ER: Mr. Big falls to his knees, and Yoto starts to hammer the top of his head with elbowsmashes! This guys has guts! Mr. Big reels under this onslaught, and Yoto steps back, then nails Big with an enzugiri! [crowd oooohs] AO: I've never seen a small guy do this to Mr. Big before! Yoto goes to the apron, grabs the ropes, and slingshots into the ring, landing a legdrop on the back of Mr. Big's head. [crowd applauds] ER: Yoto now goes to the corner, and climbs to the top rope. He raises his arms in the air...FROG SPLASH! [crowd oooohs] He's taking Mr. Big apart! I can't believe it! Yoto gets to his feet, and raises his arms to the crowd [crowd cheers] AO: Yoto turns to the fallen Mr. Big, and starts to stomp away at his head. I just can't believe a cruiserweight is doing all this! This guy is vicious! He's almost as nasty as Ishrinku! ER: Yoto is going to the top rope again....SHOOTING STAR PRESS! [crowd oooohs] Mr. Big is still face down on the mat, and he isn't moving! Yoto looks over at Honda, who nods. He drags Big up to his knees, and locks his arms behind him in a surfboard! [crowd oooohs] AO: Yoto now sticking his foot on Big's back to give him more leverage! The referee is talking to Mr. Big, but he refuses to submit! He's trying to power out of the hold! [crowd cheers] If Yoto didn't have his foot on Big's back, he could do it! ER: Yoto releases the surfboard, and Big drops his arms. He looks weakened! Yoto runs to the ropes, comes off, and Bulldogs Mr. Big to the mat! [crowd ooooohs] This is unbelievable! AO: Yoto now goes to the corner again, and ascends to the top rope...Elbowdrop! [crowd oooohs] Not even Survival manhandled Mr. Big like this! ER: Yoto raises his arms to the crowd again [crowd cheers] He drags Mr. Big to his knees, and is now getting him to his feet. He doubles him over, and elbowsmashes him on the head. Yoto now setting Mr. Big up for...a SUPLEX!? AO: Yoto tries to get Big up, but it's too much weight! He tries again, but Big blocks it, then reverses! [crowd applauds] Did you see the height of that suplex! ER: Mr. Big staggers to his feet, and you can see the anger in his eyess! Yoto also gets to his feet, and Mr. Big sends him back to the mat with a huge clothesline! [crowd cheers] AO: Mr. Big drags Yoto to his feet, and presses him over his head...Big drops him face first to the mat! [crowd oooohs] Yoto is rolling around holding his face! ER: Big looks at the crowd, and raises his fist in the air [crowd starst chanting "Mr. Big! Mr. Big!"] Big stomps Yoto on the back, then runs to the ropes...Big splash! [crowd ooooohs] Big rolls to his feet, and locks Yoto in a camel clutch! [crowd oooohs] Yoto is screaming in pain! AO: The referee asks him, but Yoto doesn't want to submit! Big pulls back as hard as he can, then lets Yoto go. He grabs Yoto's leg, and locks him in a single leg boston crab! [crowd ooooohs] Big is tearing him apart now! ER: This match has been surprisingly clean for a Battle Arts match. Big releases the half crab, then legdrops Yoto's back with his massive leg [crowd oooohs] Mr. Big gets to his feet, and yells to the crowd [crowd cheers] AO: Big now drags Yoto to his feet, and hammers him with a massive forearm shot, but Yoto retaliates with a european uppercut! [crowd ooohs] Yoto hits another..another..another..another..another! [crowd oooohs] Big staggers back a bit, and Yoto executes a dropkick..another..another..another..another! [crowd ooohs] And still Mr. Big is on his feet! ER: Yoto runs at Big, and leaps up..hurrican-POWERBOMB by Mr. Big! [crowd oooooohs] Yoto looks out! Mr. Big gets to his feet, and drags the stunned Yoto up, and hoists him over his shoulder. He walks over to the corner, and sits him on the top rope. He headbutts him once, then climbs up on the second rope. He hooks Yoto by the tights, and Yoto gets to his feet...SUPERPLEX!!! [crowd ooooohs, then applauds] Mr. Big rolls on top of him to cover... 1...2...3!!! [crowd applauds] Ring Announcer: Your winner, MR. BIG!!! [crowd cheers] AO: That was an incredible match! Yoto took it to Mr. Big better than anyone I have ever seen! ER: That was an amazing match Akira. Yoto may have lost, but he showed everyone that he is no pushover. He was so close to beating Mr. Big too. That's it for hour #1. We're going to take a short break, then be back with Hour #2!
