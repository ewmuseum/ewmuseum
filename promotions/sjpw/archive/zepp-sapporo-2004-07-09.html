SJPW House Show — Zepp Sapporo (7/9/04)

Jul 10, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Friday, July 9th, 2004 Zepp Sapporo: 1,500 fans (Sellout) Closing out the three show Hokkaido leg of the Junior Struggle! tour, Super Japan put on a bigger show at the Zepp Sapporo, as a way of thanking them for selling out the venue. 1) Junior Struggle! Block A: Fanta Boy def Toramusha (8:37) after the Apple Sauce. 2) Van Wild & Scott Starring def El Gato Loco & Mascara Negra (12:02) following a combination Side Slam/Top Rope Legdrop on Negra. 3) Five Fight Challenge, Match Two: Junya Toroyama def Eiji Kid Shimura (7:28) after a High Angle Powerbomb. 4) "Russian Superman" Petor Andresku & Vladimir Tarasov def Se-hong Kwan & El Muerte Rojo (9:50) after a double Stun Gun on Kwan. 5) Junior Struggle! Block B: Wolf Jager def Tsunami (11:59) after an Exploder. 6) "GAIJIN Extreme" Jun Suzuki & "The Wolverine" Masuhiro Hayatashi def "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder & "Jackhammer" Vince Larson (13:04) after Suzuki blasted Thunder with his chair, then hit the Widowmaker onto it. - Fanta Boy scores his first Junior Struggle! victory, defeating Toramusha after a top rope Blue Thunder Driver. - A team entered in the upcoming GPH Tag League, Rumble Pro's Van Wild & Scott Starring wowed the crowd in their victory against El Gato Loco & Mascara Negra. While the luchadors put up a good fight, it was obvious that Van Wild & Starring gelled better as a team. The crowd threw many streamers into the ring, giving Van Wild & Starring a warm welcome to Japan! - The second match of the Five Fight series saw Nagasaki Bulldog Eiji Kid Shimura face Junya Toroyama. Shimura controlled the match early, keeping Toroyama off balance with a series of running lariats. He went for one lariat too many though, and got caught in an STO for his trouble, Toroyama stunned Shimura after that with a Nodowa Otashi, then finally hit a Last Ride style powerbomb to secure the win. After the match, Tiger Kikuch told Toroyama his next opponent would be his former tag team partner, Noboru "Raging Bull" Nishida! - The Red Army made their big SJPW debut, defeating the unlikely tandem of Kwan & Muerte Rojo. Although smaller than the two Russians, Kwan & Rojo wouldn't back down, and Muerte Rojo almost got a two count after hitting the Muerte Rojo Driver #1 on Tarasov. The Russians came back from the close defeat, eventually pinning Kwan following a double Stun Gun onto the top rope. - Tsunami has remained winless in the Junior Struggle! tournament, thanks to a loss at the hands of Wolf Jager. Much like his battle with KEOKI, Tsunami just couldn't put Jager away. Jager focused on Tsunami's left shoulder, and after a stiff exchange of forearm shivers, was able to hit an Exploder, dropping Tsunami on the injured shoulder, and getting the pinfall. After the match, Jager offered Tsunami his hand, and then raised his arm up after they shook hands. Tsunami got on the microphone and praised Jager, and said after facing both men, he looked forward to watching Jager & KEOKI square off in the tournament. -The main event was a nasty brawl that saw Suzuki & Thunder continually brawling in and out of the ring. Neither man would back down from the other, and the crowd was amazed at how stiff they were hitting each other. Hayatashi & Larson pulled no punches either, with Larson actually hitting the Jackhammer on the larger Hayatashi, but he kicked out at two. After a ref bump following a Hayatashi spear into Larson (taking an out of position referee down with him), Suzuki blasted Thunder from behind with his chair, then hit the Widowmaker onto it, pinning him as Hayatashi dragged the referee over to where Suzuki had Thunder covered. After the match, Thunder went after Suzuki as he headed to the back, and the entire locker room emptied to separate both men. They were able to beat down the men trying to restrain them, and went at it again, until finally Hayatashi & Larson were able to get in and separate both men. Thunder went back into the ring, and took a microphone. He made it clear to Suzuki that he had no intentions of retiring, and just like he would show Despair at TSTL 4, he would show Suzuki that he's just the next one in line to try and retire him, but that he'd be around for a long time. Larson got the crowd to start a "Thunder" chant, and Thunder stood there in the ring, bleeding, as fans flooded the ring with streamers. The crowd was very excited after the show. Many were discussing the Suzuki/Thunder rivalry, stating this one would be far bloodier than the one between Despair & KEOKI. Many of the young women in attendance were fawning over Van Wild & Starring, while a few men were commenting on how tough the Red Army look. Others discussing the upcoming Nishida/Toroyama bout, with many feeling Toroyama's luck would run out at the hands of the "Raging Bull"!
