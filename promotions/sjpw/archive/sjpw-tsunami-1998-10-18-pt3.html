Tsunami, Hour Two 1/2

18 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Let’s go now to the final first round welterweight tournament match Ono- san. This should be an exciting one for the fans. AO: Hai. This one pits the Kabuki Kid against the Banshee. Both men are high-impact, high-flying risk takers. I expect a match that will take our breathes away. TK: These men have a long history with each other, don’t they? AO: Hai. And it’s not a very friendly one either. They have squared off against each other in other federations before. They are also members of opposing stables, Chiimi Tora for the Banshee and the Daimyo for Kabuki Kid. You’re familiar with Chiimi Tora, aren’t you, Kikuchi-san? [Akira Ono smiles.] TK [laughs]: Hai, but I will attempt to maintain a level of impartiality in my broadcasting, Ono-san. Let’s go to the ring to announce our next match. ______ ___________________________________ /_ __/__ __ _____ ___ ___ _ (_) Welterweight Tournament / / / (_-</ // / _ \/ _ `/ ' \/ / Banshee vs Kabuki Kid /_/ /___/\_,_/_//_/\_,_/_/_/_/_/________________________________/ [The camera pans to the ring, where Nishi Toju & Goto Kyoji stand...] Nishi: This next contest tournament, is for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. It is a first round match in the Welterweight tournament! [crowd cheers] Your referee for this match is the inimitable Goto Kyoji! [crowd cheers as Goto bows] [The arena lights dim as "Fly" by SUGAR RAY begins playing over the P.A. system.] Nishi: Introducing first, representing Chiimu Tora, from Los Angeles, California, weighing in at 219 pounds...The BAN-SHEE! [A loud cheer rises from the audience as a pair of white spotlights begin playing over the crowd. Suddenly, they converge high in the bleachers where a slender but muscular figure can be seen. Dressed in a green-and-white wrestling mask and a green-and-white singlet, The Banshee tilts his head back, cups his hands over his mouth and issues his ululating warcry. The crowd cheers again as The Banshee begins jogging down the staircase toward the ring, slapping hands with fans along the way. Vaulting over the guard railing, he climbs up onto the outside of the ring, yells out his shrill warcry once more and then performs a front somersault off the top rope to land in the middle of the ring.] TK: The Banshee really knows how to play to the fans. AO: Hai, he is a very charismatic young man, no doubt about it. His particular style of wrestling, high-risk and very acrobatic, also endears him to the fans. ["Creatures of the Night" by KISS blares over the PA as the lights go out and plunge the arena into darkness.] # Searching in the Darkness Running from the day Hiding from tomorrow Nothing left to say. # Nishi: And his opponent, representing the Daimyo, from Nagoya, weighing in at 215 pounds...KA-BU-KI KID!! [The audience goes wild in anticipation for a home-town hero. A single spotlight shines on one of the turnbuckles. There shrouded in a mist is the figure of the compact and muscular physique Kabuki Kid. As the mist rises off his body and around him his face and wrestling gear can be seen. The light sparkles off his upper body from the moisture of the mist.] # Victims of the moment Future deep in doubt Living in the whisper 'Till we start to shout # [His face is painted white, not black like before, and this time the eagle is black not red and white. The wings cover his eyes and drape down his cheeks with the neck and head on his forehead. His pants, boots and kneepads are all black with thin red trim. The spotlight then goes out. The crowd cheers wildly.] # We're the creatures of the night We're the creatures of the night # [Red lasers form the Kabuki Kid face paint symbol throughout the crowd. This illicits more cheering from the Japanese fans.] # Breathing in the madness Spitting out the lies Searching for the answer Keep your alibis # [Slowly the two dozen or so Kabuki Kid symbols spin around the arena. They spin closer and closer to the ring until they all emerge into one big symbol in the middle of the ring. There kneeling down on one knee is the Kabuki Kid.] # Gathering up our courage Ready for the fight Howling in the darkness 'Till we start to bite # [Kabuki Kid slowly raises his left hand into the air above his head. He leans his head back, looking upwards, and suddenly spits out a red mist just past his outstretched hand. The lights then come back on. The crowd cheers loudly as the Kabuki Kid prepares for the match up.] TK: Very impressive indeed! And the fans of Nagoya continue to cheer! Kabuki Kid is a very popular wrestler in his own home city. AO: Hai. I’ve always wondered though how he manages to appear at the turnbuckle without even walking out from backstage? Do you know Kikuchi-san? TK: Some questions are better left unanswered, Ono-san. AO: Point taken. TK: Goto gives both men their instructions. He calls for the bell and this match is on! Both Kabuki Kid and the Banshee dance around each other, looking for an opening. The Banshee stretches out a hand and offers to shake Kabuki Kid’s hand, but the Kid is very suspicious of this. The Banshee looks towards the fans, then back at Kabuki Kid, his hand still outstretched as if trying to tell the Kid that this is a legitimate attempt at sportsmanship. The fans start cheering the Kabuki Kid’s name. AO: Kabuki Kid seems to listen to his fans pleadings as he shakes Banshee’s hand. Both men now return to stalking around each other in the ring. A lock- up and the Banshee has Kabuki Kid in a side-headlock. Kabuki Kid sends the Banshee to the ropes, attempts a clothesline but the Banshee ducks under the Kid’s extended arm, runs towards the reverse ropes, jummps up onto the top rope and leaps towards Kabuki Kid with a springboard shoulder block aimed directly at the Kid’s right thigh! TK: He connects and the Kid goes down, clutching his right thigh. This is very good strategy on the part of the Banshee, Ono-san. AO: Hai, the Banshee is attempting to ground Kabuki Kid and prevent him from performing his more deadly high-flying manuevers. TK: Yes, but Kabuki Kid has been known to wrestle with all sorts of crippling injuries. This man has no fear. The Kid is still on the mat. The Banshee lowers himself down onto the right thigh of the Kid with a devastating fist drop, followed up by an elbow drop on the same thigh, then a somersault leg drop also on the right thigh! [crowd "ooohs"] AO: Kabuki Kid is writhing in pain on the mat. You can tell that the Banshee has really done some damage to that right thigh. What a string of manuevers! TK: The Banshee picks Kabuki Kid up and delivers a chop to the chest. The Kid reels backwards. The Banshee continues the attack, whipping the Kid to the ropes and catches him with a flying dropkick! Look at the height the Banshee achieved with that dropkick! AO: Kabuki Kid drops down to the mat like a sack of potatoes. The Banshee with the cover... ...1... TK: And a kick-out! [crowd applauds] Kabuki Kid is a resilient wrestler. He won’t go down that easily. The Banshee can smell the victory. He walks around the ring, waving his arms frantically at the audience attempting to get them to cheer even louder than they now. [Audience responds to the Banshee’s attempts at pumping them up.] TK: The Banshee has a tendency to let-up on his offense and play to the audience, Ono-san. AO: Hai. That is his nature. With experience he will learn that once you step into the ring, anything can happen, and the great wrestlers only have one desire - to end the match as quickly as possible. TK: Well spoken, Ono-san. Kabuki Kid has taken this break in the action to gather his senses. He’s slowly raised himself up to his feet, his hand clutching at the ropes. The Banshee turns his attention back to Kabuki Kid. The Banshee delivers an European Uppercut to the Kid, then another. He then throws the dazed Kabuki Kid into the corner turnbuckle. The Kid hits the turnbuckle hard, grimacing in pain. The Banshee, with a head of steam, runs into the corner turnbuckle and lands a spinning back kick on the Kid! The Kid collapses to the mat, reeling from that powerful manuever! AO: The Banshee picks up Kabuki Kid, places him on the top turnbuckle. He then climbs onto the turnbuckle and again starts to pump his hands towards the fans. He is taking too much time, Kikuchi-san. [The fans cheer wildly.] TK: Hai. It looks like the Banshee is attempting a superplex, but like you said, he is taking too much time with this manuever. The Banshee finally tucks the Kid’s head under his arm and lifts him into the air...but the Kid falls forward onto the Banshee, sending the Banshee crashing on his back to the mat with the Kid landing flush on the Banshee’s chest! What a counter- move! AO: Hai, a move born of desperation. The Kid had enough battle sense in him to stave off what appeared to be a match ending move. TK: Kabuki Kid, now sitting on top of the hurt Banshee, begins to pummel the Banshee with a series of open hand punchs to the face. [Audience begins to count "1, 2, 3, 4..." all the way up to "10."] TK: Kabuki Kid rolls off the Banshee, hops to his feet, picks the Banshee up and delivers a super-fast snap suplex. Then with blinding speed, Kabuki Kid performs a headbutt drop from a standing position onto the Banshee’s forehead, then follows that up with a knee drop to the Banshee’s head! The Banshee sits up, clutching his face in obvious pain. Kabuki Kid doesn’t let up though! He delivers a a jumping neck snap, sending the Banshee to the mat. Now a cover... ...1... ...2... AO: And a kick-out! [crowd applauds] But it looks like the momentum has shifted. [Audience suddenly starts looking towards the ring entrance area. The camera pans toward the entrance to show that Kensei Tokugawa, Kabuki Kid’s tag partner has entered the arena. He slowly makes his way to ring-side. He starts slapping on the mat, giving advice to Kabuki Kid.] TK: Looks like Kensei Tokugawa is here to cheer on his tag-teammate. AO: The Security guards approach Tokugawa, but he waves them off, assuring them that he is only here as an observer. The guards back away. TK: Kabuki Kid nods at Tokugawa. He then stands the Banshee up and quickly places him in a front head lock and attempts to lift him into the air for a vertical suplex.Kabuki Kid lifts the Banshee slowly into the air, holds him up for the audience to see... [Audience starts getting rowdier, chanting Kabuki Kid’s name.] TK: ...but the Banshee folds up his legs forward giving him enough momentum to land back onto his feet on the mat. With super-human strength, he lifts Kabuki Kid into the air for a vertical suplex of his own...and then drops face forward for a vertical facebuster! AO: Tokugawa didn’t like that manuever! He pounds desparately on the mat, yelling at Kabuki Kid to get up and finish off the Banshee. TK: Both men are now on the mat, dazed and confused. The Banshee is the first one up. With unsteady feet, he lifts the Kid up and slams him down onto the mat. He then runs off the rope and performs a somersault senton onto the Kid, knocking the air out of Kabuki Kid! AO: The Banshee lifts the Kid onto the top turnbuckle, then climbs up behind him. The Banshee tucks the Kid’s head between his knees and...and I can’t believe he has the energy to perform this!...he jumps back off the turnbuckle and drives Kabuki Kid’s head into the mat in a modified spike pile driver! The Banshee calls this move "The Atomic Sky Spike" This match is over, Kikuchi-san. TK: The Banshee rolls on top of Kabuki Kid for the pin... 1... 2... TK: What’s this?!? Tokugawa pulls the Banshee off of Kabuki Kid, but the referee did not see this! The Banshee gets up and begins to shout at Tokugawa, who has now jumped up on the ring apron! Tokugawa and the Banshee are exchanging heated words with each other. [The audience is going crazy.] AO: Tokugawa slaps the Banshee in the face! The Banshee is beside himself with anger! He lunges at Tokugawa, but Tokugawa hops off the apron. Security guards have surrounded Tokugawa and are escorting him away from the ring, but both he and the Banshee are still jawing away at each other. TK: Look at this! Kabuki Kid suddenly comes up from behind the Banshee, grabs his legs and rolls him up in a cradle! The referee drops to the mat... ...1... ...2... ...3!!!! **ding** **ding** **ding** [crowd applauds] TK: I can’t believe what I just saw! Kabuki Kid, still reeling from the Atomic Sky Spike, managed to take advantage of the distraction Tokugawa performed on the Banshee! The Banshee is stunned, in disbelief. Goto Kyoji holds up Kabuki Kid’s hand. Nishi: The winner of this match...KABUKI KID!!!! [The audience is wildly enthusiastic that the home-town hero won. Kabuki Kid attempts to stagger out of the ring, but the Banshee runs up to him and starts shouting into his face. Security guards separate the two men, and usher them backstage. As they walk towards the entrance, the Banshee continues to yell at the dazed Kabuki Kid.] AO: This is a travesty of justice! TK: I agree. However, Kabuki Kid is the winner, and he will go on to face Jet Jaguar II on the October 20th Metro. Considering this tuesday Hiryuu Batsu, which is Jet & Banshee, face The Ronin, who are Kabuki Kid & Tokugawa, I expect it to be a heated match! AO: I agree. This whole situation between the Daiymo & Chiimu Tora is coming to a head. First Kagemusha costs Shishi Fujiwara his match, and now this. The Daimyo had best beware the sharp claws of the Tiger, lest it ensnare them, then crushes them between it's powerful jaws! TK: And you claim I have trouble remaining impartial? AO: Well, it's what you were going to say, were you not? TK: No, I wasn't. AO: Yes you were. I just saved you the trouble of doing so. If you had, then you wouldn't look very impartial. TK: You are incorrigible! AO: But I am right. TK: I guess you are. Now we come to our second tag team match of the evening. The Young Warriors will face Lady Mai Tai's mysterious Team Combattler. If there is one. AO: It would be amusing if there isn't one. TK: No, it wouldn't. AO: Yes it would [holds his headset]...It seems the camera crew backstage wants to show something... [The camera cuts to outside the Young Warriors' dressing room again just as the two men exit the room. Huan, the smaller of the two, is wearing a pair of black gi pants and a black vest-style gi top tied together at the front with a black belt. Now that there are no shirt sleeves covering his arms we can see a tattoo of a roaring lion's head on the right biceps. A bigger version of the same design can be seen on the back of the gi top. He is also wearing a pair of black martial arts shoes/slippers. Retsudo is wearing a somewhat similar looking outfit consisting of a pair of regular black wrestling tights, basic black wrestling boots and a vest style gi top like the one worn by Huan, this one too held together at the front with a black belt. On the back of the vest is the image of an angry bull's head snorting steam out of its nostrils] Retsudo: Is TIME, SJPW! Is time for all to see Young Warriors in action at last! Is time to prove ourselves on field of battle! We know we underdogs tonight. We know many think we just two rookies. That okay. We show everyone tonight! We make big mark in minds of fans... And make even bigger marks on bodies of Team Combattler!!! [Huan nods and stares at the camera with a serious, solemn look on his face] Huan: We may be young. We may be inexperienced. We may have the odds against us, but we are not here to fade into the background and provide punchingbags for the more experienced competitors. We are here for one purpose: To fight with honor! To prove that the warrior spirit lives on in the young people of today. We are at the start of our careers, and we have nothing to lose, and everything to win. Team Combattler... Mysterious Team Combattler. I am sure you are excellent competitors. That is to be expected in a place like the SJPW. I _would_ advice you not to take us lightly though. We will not back down from any challenge. We will not stand for being disrespected by anyone. We will not give up... _Ever_! [He pauses] Huan: Lady Mai Tai... You may have kept the identities of your combatants a secret, but this does only serve to even the field of battle somewhat. You know not more about us than we know about them, simply because there is next to no information to collect on Retsudo and myself. Sometimes being the unknown... The new kid... It has its advantages. Retsudo: HAI! Team Combattler! Is going to be _fight_! Young Warriors coming to win! Be ready or be defeated! Is that simple! [Both men start to head towards the runway entrance, and the camera cuts to the ring, where Nishi Toju & Owada Botan are standing...] ______ ___________________________________ /_ __/__ __ _____ ___ ___ _ (_) Young Warriors vs / / / (_-</ // / _ \/ _ `/ ' \/ / Team Combattler /_/ /___/\_,_/_//_/\_,_/_/_/_/_/________________________________/ Nishi: The following tag-team contest contest is a first round match in the Frank Marano Tag-Team Tournament!!! [crowd cheers] it is one fall with a twenty-minute time-limit. Your referee for this match is Owada Botan! [crowd cheers as Botan bows] ["Line Up" by Aerosmith cranks on the PA] Nishi: Introducing first, Team #1, at a combined weight of 438 pounds..."LITTLE LION" HU-AN FENG SHO & "RAGING BULL" RET-SU-DO YAG-YU....THE YOUNG WARRIORS!!! [The lights dim, the videoscreen goes dark..."Line Up" blares louder as the darkness of the videoscreen shatters in a pattern revealing red letters on a black background which read "YOUNG WARRIORS"! The Spotlights hit the entrance to the aisle where Huan Feng Sho and Yagyu Retsudo step out from behind the curtains. Huan does a few martial arts kicks and punches into the air, and Retsudo raises his arms into the air with a wide grin on his face, looking around at the crowd. The two men then jog to the ring, slapping hands and high fiving fans all the way. Once they enter the ring Huan does his routine from the center with four rapid high sidekicks, one towards each side of the ring, without letting his kicking foot touch the ground as he pivots on his other leg. Retsudo turns to the crowd, scrapes his foot on the mat like a bull about to charge and slaps himself across his massive chest with his hands while letting out a thunderous roar!] TK: This team is very motivated. But can they overcome Lady Mai Tai's team? ["Theme from Combattler V" by Animetal cranks on the PA] Nishi: Their opponents, led to the ring by Lady Mai Tai, at a combined weight of 475 pounds...COM-BATT-LER I & COM-BATT-LER II... TEAM COMBATTLER!!! [The arena lights shut off as a red spotlight searches the crowd. Suddenly it hits rampway, and both Combattler I and Combattler II run to the ring with Lady Mai Tai walking slowly behind them, almost swaggering as she smiles contemptuously to the fans. Both Combattlers wear Sky Blue long tights, black boots with blue laces. Wears a mask that covers his whole face with a silver mesh over the mouth and eyes. The mask itself is red save for a big blue "V" on the forehead] AO: So it seems Lady Mai Tai truly has a tag team after all! TK: Combattler I is bending over...what is he doing? AO: He's...taking off his mask? [Combattler I removes his mask, and stands up, revealing...] AO & TK: KAZUYA ASAKINO?!? [Kazuya throws the mask out to the crowd, who "oooooh" loudly] AO: Kazuya is Mai Tai & Tengu's cousin, so this makes sense. However, does that mean that Combattler II is...Keiji Asamiya? TK: I cannot say; he's still wearing his mask, and doesn't seem to want to remove his at this point. AO: This was an unexpected event! Kazuya is going to start, as will the smaller Huan Feng Sho. The referee gives both men their instructions, then calls for the bell! [both men circle each other warily...] TK: They lock up, and Kazuya overpowers Huan into a side headlock. He takes Huan over to the mat, releases the hold, and rolls to his feet. Huan with a kip-up, and Kazuya kicks him in the gut, doubling him over. Kazuya to the ropes...SWINGING NECKBREAKER! [crowd "ooohs"] AO: Kazuya to his feet, and he runs to the ropes again...SNAP LEGDROP across Huan's throat! [crowd "ooohs"] He runs to the ropes again...another legdrop! He goes to the ropes a third time...another legdrop! Huan is sitting up, holding his throat, and Kazuya with a standing dropkick to the back of his head! [crowd "ooohs"] TK: Kazuya tags in Combattler II, and stands Huan up...Double Dropkick sends Huan back to the mat! Combattler II drags Huan to his feet, sets him up, then lifts him high in the air...he holds him vertical, letting the blood rush to his head...BRAINBUSTER!! [crowd "oooohs"] Cover... ...1... ...2... AO: KICKOUT! [crowd applauds!] I thought Huan was done for there! Combattler II drags Huan to his feet, and grabs him from behind in a waistlock...Huan lifts his leg straight up, and nails Combattler II in the head! [crowd "oooohs"] Huan now with a backhand to Combatler II's face...he staggers back...Huan leaps into the air, and levels Combattler II with a spinning back roundhouse kick! [crowd "ooohs"] TK: Huan lands, and drops to one knee. He's taken alot of punishment so far. He rolls to the ropes, and starts to use them to get to his feet. Combattler II sits up, shaking his head. I bet he was totally surprised by Huan's limber leg! AO: Combattler II rolls over, and starts to rise, while Huan makes it to his feet, and tags in Retsudo! Retsudo in, and he charges Combattler II...Elbowdrop to Combattler II's back! Retsudo sits Combattler II up, and applies a surfboard! Kazuya in, and he kicks Retsudo in the head, breaking the hold! [crowd applauds] TK: Retsudo drags Combattler II to his feet, and whips him into his own corner. Tag to Kazuya, who springboards onto the top rope, and attempts a dropkick, but Retsudo steps back, and Kazuya crashes to the mat! [crowd "oooohs"] Retsudo grabs Kazuya by the leg, then lands an elbowdrop to his inner thigh! He stands up...another elbowdrop! He stands again...another elbowdrop! AO: Retsudo up again, and he applies a stepover toehold. Kazuya is wincing in pain, and Lady Mai Tai slams her hand on the apron in frustration! Retsudo re-applies the toehold, and Kazuya screams in agony! Combattler II in, and he clotheslines Retsudo! [crowd "oooohs"] TK: Huan is in as well, and he catches Combattler II with a front kick! [crowd "ooohs"] Combattler II staggers back, and Huan sends him to the mat with an enzugiri! [crowd "ooohs"] Huan now helping Retsudo to his feet, who shakes his head to clear it. They drag Kazuya to his feet...Retsudo runs to the ropes behind Kazuya as Huan nails him with a back kick...BULLDOG by Retsudo! [crowd "ooohs"] He rolls Kazuya over, and Botan drops to count... ...1... ...2... AO: Kickout! [crowd applauds] Retsudo now rolling Kazuya over into a single leg boston crab. Huan steps back, then dropkicks Kazuya's exposed knee! [crowd "ooohs"] Botan makes Huan go to his corner, and Combattler II is up, and he kicks Retsudo in the back of the head! [crowd "ooohs"] Retsudo breaks the half crab, and Combattler II stands him up...waistlock from behind....Release German Suplex! [crowd "oooohs"] TK: Combattler II drags Kazuya to his corner, then makes the tag. Combattler II drags Retsudo to his feet, then sends him back to the mat with a stiff lariat. He drags him up once more, then drives him to the mat with a Powerbomb! [crowd "oooohs"] Combattler II makes the cover... ...1... ...2... AO: Kickout! [crowd applauds] Combattler II now with an armbar, and he drags Retsudo to his feet...He twists on the arm, turning it over him, and turns it into a hammerlock. He spins Retsudo so he is facing him, then scoops him up, and slams him on his arm!
