Zenmen Sensou

31 January 1999

[Zenmen Sensou continues...] ER: Toshiaki Takada had some strong words for The Corporation, as well as Tetsuo Matsumoto. AO: Takada is an excellant athlete, but he prefers the violence and madness of garbage wrestling. Pity. He could be so much more. ER: What about his challenge? AO: Ha! Yamashita will never sign such a match. He'll put Takada in his place just like he has been doing to Matsumoto! The man cares only for himself, not these men! ER: Speaking of Tetsuo, he's up next against Se-hong Kwan. AO: Kwan is an accomplished martial artist from Korea. He may be able to defeat Matsumoto, but I doubt it... ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Tetsuo Matsumoto vs Se-hong Kwan [J] /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ The crowd gave Tetsuo a standing ovation as he came to ringside, and he acknowledged the fans by bowing to them. At the bell, both men locked up, and Kwan went behind Tetsuo, and nailed him with a spinning kick to the back of the head. He then started to stomp away on Tetsuo's head and back, until the referee made him break. Tetsuo got up, and shook his head, then glared at his opponent. Kwan moved in, and started to lay into Tetsuo with kicks, until Tetsuo caught his right leg, swept him off his feet, then dropped an elbow on Kwan's inner thigh. Tetsuo got back to his feet, still holding the leg, and drove his knee into Kwan's thigh three times, then applied a leglock grapevine. Kwan made it to the ropes, and Tetsuo broke the hold. Tetsuo dragged Kwan to his feet, and hit an Exploder suplex, Kwan's head landing hard on the mat. Tetsuo dragged Kwan to his feet, and went for a german suplex, but Kwan caught Tetsuo with two back elbows, then snap mared him to the mat. Kwan went to the ropes, springboarded off, and came down with a vicious stomp to Tetsuo's upper chest. Tetsuo coughed up some blood, and the ref stopped to check on him. Tetsuo shook him off, and dove at Kwan, tackling him to the mat. He straddled Kwan, and laid into him with stiff forearm shots to the side of his head. Kwan reeled from this assault, and Tetsuo dragged him to his feet, then hit a snap DDT. He dragged Kwan to his feet, and executed the Dragon Driver '99 for the pin. Winner: Tetsuo Matsumoto by pinfall. ER: Tetsuo looked very impressive tonight. AO: Kwan looked pretty good too. If Tetsuo keeps wrestling like that, Yamashita will have no choice on giving him some kind of title shot! ER: I totally agree. Our next match is the debut of Hashimoto "Seikyo" Asui, as he faces Yorozu Izeki. AO: Asui's reputation in America precedes him. This will be exciting! ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ /Hashimoto "Seikyo" Asui vs Yorozu Izeki [J] /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ Asui came out to a mixed reaction from the crowd. Once the bell rang though, he was all business. Asui applied a front facelock, then hammered Izeki's back with forearm shots. He then applied a waistlock, lifting Izeki up high, before falling back into a suplex, driving Izeki's head hard to the mat. He dragged Izeki to his feet, bent him over, then drove a stiff elbow to the back of his neck. Izeki dropped to one knee, and Asui drove his face into the mat with a rocker dropper. Asui dragged Izeki to his feet, then drove him to the mat with a Northern Lights Bomb. He stood Izeki up, and drapped him over the top rope, then climbed up in the corner. He pulled Izeki's head into the corner, and hit a Rocker Dropper off the top rope, rolled to his feet, and executed the Seikyo Driver, easily pinning Izeki for the win. Winner: Hashimoto "Seikyo" Asui by pinfall. ER: Asui is definitely a technician as well. That variation of the Tiger Driver is a deadly hold. AO: I'll say. Izeki will be lucky his neck isn't broken! ER: Coming up next, we have what's bound to be some great single action, Ono-san!! AO: I suppose anything is better than that Garbage style wrestling!! ER: Let's not get into that argument again! Both of the young men in this following match could very easily become contenders for any of the singles titles here in SJPW !! AO: Soo, ne? A win by either wrestler here this evening will go a long way towards that goal! ER: Well then, what do you say we go right on up to the ring for all the action? AO: You're the one who's still talking!! ER: [shaking his head...] Let's hear the introductions from announcer Nishi Toju... ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Jushin Kojima vs Scorpio /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ Nishi: Minna-san, our next match is scheduled for one fall with a 15 minute time limit!! Introducing first... ["Firestarter" by Prodigy blasts over the arena's loudspeakers...] Nishi From Yokohama, Japan... standing 5 feet 10 inches tall and weighing 223 pounds... "THE FLYING DRAGON" JUSHIN KOOOOOJJIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIMA!!!!!!!!!! [The arena lights go out as Jushin steps out from behind the curtain and holds his flaming wand up in the air. Black and green strobe lights begin to go off around the arena. He wears a green jacket with scales airbrushed on them, and on the back of the jacket the words "Flying Dragon" are airbrushed in gold. His wrestling attire consists of long wrestling tights that are black trimmed in green, on the left leg the word "Flying" is airbrushed in green, and on the right leg "Dragon" is airbrushed in green as well. Jushin wears knee length black boots with shin pads, on each shin pad the word "Dragon" is written. He wears a mask that covers his face only letting his hair flow out from the top. On his mask a picture of a dragon wraps itself around the entire mask.Jushin takes his time coming down to the ring, once he gets to the ring the strobe lights stop flashing, Jushin climbs the top rope and begins to spit fire...] ER: Another spectacular entrance by the Flying Dragon, eh Ono-san? AO: Is he a wrestler or a circus performer?? Nishi: And his opponent... ["Carry On" by Angra fills the arena...] Nishi: From Sao Paulo, Brazil... Standing 5 foot 8 inches tall and weighing 246 pounds... SCOOOOORRRPPPPIIIIOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!! [Scorpio comes down shaking all fans' hands that he can in his way to the ring. He wears yellow pants and green boots, with the Brazilian flag On each boot. Scorpio sometimes even stops a few times to pose for photos. Before entering the ring, Scorpio grabs the ropes and executes a slingshot moonsault to the delight of the fans. He then climbs on the top turnbuckle as green and yellow fireworks explode over the ring...] AO: Hmmm... ER: What is it Ono-san? AO: Doesn't Scorpio have a valet? ER: You mean Erika Lima? And she's his _manager_ Ono-san. AO: Whatever she is, she's not here. I wonder why? ER: I don't suppose it's really any of our business, anyway. Well, it looks like both men are ready to begin the match, Ono-san... AO: Then why don't they? *ding-ding-ding* ER: Perhaps they were waiting for the bell? Now both men lock up in the center of the ring with a COLLAR AND ELBOW TIE UP! AO: But the stronger Scorpio SHOVES Kojima back and down to the mat!!! ER: Scorpio is deceptively strong for his size, Ono-san! I don't think that Kojima will be able to match power with him!! AO: Kojima is not known as a power wrestler, anyway. Now he's back to his feet, and he and Scorpio are circling... ER: Kojima moves in close, but an awesome STANDING DROPKICK by Scorpio sends him back down to the canvas!!! AO: That was some drop kick by Scorpio!!! Very impressive... ER: Now Scorpio SCOOPS up Kojima... BODYSLAM with authority!!! AO: Again, Scorpio using his power against the lighter Jushin Kojima!! Very intelligent on his part!!! ER: Scorpio now helping Kojima up to his feet... and a savage EUROPEAN UPPERCUT to the jaw of Jushin Kojima!!! AO: I wonder what thay call that move in Europe? ER: What??? Er... anyway, another stiff EUROPEAN UPPERCUT by Scorpio and ANOTHER... He tops it off with another STANDING DROPKICK!!!! AO: That kick sent Kojima through the ropes out to the floor!!! ER: Jushin Kojima on the outside struggling to his feet... meanwhile, Scorpio is coming off the far ropes... SUICIDE DIVE to the outside... AO: But Kojima SIDESTEPPED him!!! ER: Not only that, but he managed to grab hold of Scorpio in mid-air and DRIVE him to the floor at ringside!!! That could be a fatal error by Scorpio... AO: Soo, ne? That landing seemed to take quite a bit out of Scorpio!! ER: And now Jushin Kojima answers with a well executed STANDING DROPKICK of his own!! Now he's picking Scorpio back up... AO: He's rolling Scorpio back up into the ring. Very intelligent. You can't win a match on the outside!! This is puroresu, not a streetfight!! ER: In the ring, Scorpio's a bit slow getting back up to his feet... AO: Who wouldn't be after the landing he made outside? ER: Very true Ono-san. Now Kojima back in the ring and delivers a BOOT to the midsection of his opponent!!! AO: Kojima drops to one knee... SNAPMARE takes Scorpio down to the mat!!! ER: Jushin Kojima quickly back up to his feet...He's coming off the far ring ropes... Scorpio is sitting up... OOH! DROPKICK right to the face of Scorpio!!! AO: But wait! Kojima back to his feet... STANDING MOONSAULT on Scorpio!!! This could be all over... ...1... ...2... ...3--KICKOUT!!! [crowd applauds] ER: Very near fall there by Jushin Kojima!! Fantastic chain wrestling by the Flying Dragon!! AO: Hai! I think Kojima is a bit frustrated that he didn't get the three count!! He rolls Scorpio over onto his stomach... CAMEL CLUTCH!!! ER: Look at the pain on the face of Scorpio!!! Jushin Kojima must really have that Camel Clutch cinched in!! He's pulling waaaay back on the head of Scorpio!!! Scorpio's flailing his arms, struggling to reach the ropes... AO: And he manages to GRAB the ropes, forcing the referee to break the hold!! [crowd applauds] Kojima quickly back to his feet as Scorpio uses the ring ropes to regain his footing... ER: Now Jushin Kojima grabs hold of the right arm of Scorpio... IRISH WHIP sends Scorpio hard into the corner!! Kojima in the opposite corner now... AO: Kojima runs in... HANDSPRING ELBOW!!! Great agility on the part of Jushin Kojima!!! ER: Scorpio falls to the mat!! He appears to be out of it!! Jushin Kojima senses victory... He's going up to the top turnbuckle!!! AO: Dame!! This is a mistake!! He should have covered his opponent!!! ER: It looks like Kojima's going for his "DRAGON DROP," a devastating SHOOTING STAR PRESS!!! He leaps... [The crowd simultanously "OOOFS!!"] AO: But Scorpio got his knees up!!! Jushin Kojima's midsection landing right across the top of Scorpio's knees!!! I knew it was a mistake for him to attempt that maneuver!!! ER: Kojima going for the high-risk move and it didn't pay off!! This could be Scorpio's chance to win this match if he can just capitalize on this!!! AO: It looks like he's going to!!! Now Scorpio climbing the ropes to the top turnbuckle... REVERSE MOONSAULT LEGDROP!!!! ER: He connected with the SCORPIO'S STING!!! Now Scorpio HOOKS the leg of Kojima... ...1... ...2... ...3...!!!!!! [crowd applauds] AO: Scorpio with the win!!! Very impressive!!! But the match was all Jushin Kojima until he made that one mistake!!! ER: Yes. It's a shame but one mistake is all it takes, Ono-san!!! But both men gave us a very exciting match here tonight!!! Nishi: Ladies and gentlemen your winner in 8 minutes, 29 seconds... SCORPIO!!!! AO: That was a good match!!! Much better than Garbage Style wrestling, ne? ER: sigh... AO: Well, it is! ER: If you say so Akira. AO: I do. [Rose shakes his head] ER: Let's go to Jonnie Adonis, who is standing by to do an interview... [The scene switches from ringside to the interview platform where Jonnie Adonis can be seen standing in a sports jacket featuring the SJPW logo on the breast pocket. He clears his throat as he brings the microphone up to his lips and begins speaking.] Jonnie Adonis: Just a short time ago, a mini-tournament was held which consolidated several titles together to form the PJW Grand Prix Championship. The winner of that tournament defeated Chandrasekhar Yoshida and Kagemusha, two of the best talents in this sport. He is undeniably one of the brightest lights shining in the Chiimu Tora constellation, ladies and gentlemen, the undisputed PJW Grand Prix Senshuken..... THE BANSHEE! ["Fly" by Sugar Ray begins playing over the P.A. system as a familiar looking figure appears in the arena entrance wearing a green-and-white wrestling mask. The crowd cheers loudly as The Banshee, dressed in jeans and a black T-shirt, waves and walks over to the interview stand. Climbing up next to Adonis, The Banshee tilts his head back, cups his hands around his mouth and issues his trademark ululating warcry which sparks another loud cheer. Around his waist, the newly minted PJW Grand Prix Championship belt can be seen.] JA: Congratulations, Banshee, on your tremendous victory. TB: Thank you, Jonnie. I must admit that I was a little dubious about surrendering the PJW International title but I knew that it was the right thing to do. And when I won this Grand Prix belt... the feeling was just indescribable. JA: What does this particular title mean to you? TB: An awful lot, Jonnie. This is my fifth title and it brings together a lot of wrestling history and heritage. I can't say that Hadi Moditian and I see eye to eye right now but when I was a part of PJW, I was proud to be the International Champion. And now, I'm even prouder of being the Grand Prix Senshuken! JA: What was the tournament like? Were you ever worried about losing? TB: Of course. No wrestler steps into the ring without accepting that there's always the potential to suffer a loss. But I got a tremendous amount of satisfaction from defeating Yoshida-san as I truly wanted to put him in his place. His actions and words are disgraceful and beating him was a sweet victory indeed. JA: What about Kagemusha? Did you enjoy defeating another member of The Daimyo? TB: Despite his associations, Kagemusha is a tremendous athlete and a warrior I respect. He demonstrated at the end of the match that not all of his instincts are dishonorable. The match could have gone either way and it means a lot to me that I won this belt by defeating one of the very best in SJPW. JA: I can't imagine your fellow Chiimu Tora member, Shishi "Lion" Fujiwara, would agree with you. TB: Fujiwara-san's actions of late have been highly disappointing. I am a proud member of Chiimu Tora because I believe in everything that it stands for. Fujiwara-san seems to have forgotten what those ideals are and I would welcome the opportunity to discuss this with him to clarify any confusion. He seems to be headed down a dangerous path and I want to warn him away from it. JA: Before we let you go, what about Ed Dean? Any thoughts about him? TB: Ed Dean is a ravaging animal, a brute with no honor or respect. I make no excuses, he knocked me out cold in the ring and I did not represent Chiimu Tora well. But when he injured my friend and partner, Jet Jaguar II, he ensured that The Banshee's Wail would be ringing in his ears very soon! JA: Thank you very much. Ladies and gentlemen... THE BANSHEE! ["Fly" begins playing over the P.A. once more as the crowd cheers and The Banshee waves, a broad smile visible under his mask. He steps down off the interview stand and heads back toward the entrance as the scene switches back to ringside.] ER: The Banshee had some interesting things to say about fellow Chiimu Tora member Shishi Fujiwara. AO: Of course he does! Oh yes, he made some brief comments about Ed Dean. However, he spoke more about his stablemate. Why? Because he's so afraid of Dean, he'd rather fight one of his own allies! ER: How can you say that Akira? AO: Because I've learned that Banshee confronted Fujiwara about his ties with Hadi Moditan. Few people trust Fujiwara because of it, and Banshee is the worst of the lot! ER: Well, I can understand their concerns... AO: Fujiwara is a man of honor! If he wasn't, would Kikuchi accept him into Chiimu Tora? ER: Well... AO: I rest my case.
