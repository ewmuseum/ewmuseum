SJPW House Show — Okinawa Fight Club Kamikaze (5/15/04)

May 26, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Friday, May 15th, Okinawa Fight Club Kamikaze (Cap. 1,450), Okinawa, Japan: SJPW made it's big return to Okinawa, skipping it's mid-week house show to prepare for this event. The Fight Club Kamikaze sold out, and the crowd eagerly anticipated this show, which was going to be longer than most house shows. Pramoj Ananda vs Gregor Davinsky: The Thai kickboxer and the ex-soviet soldier faced off in singles competition. Davinsky tried to out power Ananda with his slight size advantage, only to discover that the smaller Thai, who only weighs 10 pounds less than him, was actually stronger than him. Ananda quickly went from the initial lock up to a go behind, ducking the back elbow Davinsky sent his way, and took Davinsky to the mat, before applying a leglock. Davinsky was able to make it to the ropes, and Ananda broke the hold. Davinsky got up, angry, and thumbed Ananda in the eye when they went to lock up again. He then scoop slammed him, before choking him on the mat. The ref made him break after a five count, and Davinsky egged on the crowd, who booed him. Davinsky then started to punish Ananda, hitting a series of 3 rib breakers, then a side slam. He then started to stomp on his head and upper body, before dragging him to his feet, and whipping him hard to the corner. Davinsky then set Ananda up on the top rope, and tried to go for a superplex. Ananda blocked it, then hammered Davinsky with some stiff elbows to the side of the head. Davinsky fell off the top rope, but got right up. Ananda sprung off the corner, landed on the top rope, and springboarded off, nailed Davinsky with a spinning roundhouse kick. Davinsky went down like a ton of bricks, and the ref called for the bell after checking on him, giving Ananda the win. Winner: Pramoj Ananda by Knockout Otaku says: Ananda's kicks are very dangerous. I heard them say in the back Davinsky might have a concussion! Ono: Wolf Jager better be careful, because he's likely next on Ananda's hit list! "GAIJIN Extreme" Jun Suzuki vs Kenichi "Kurashaa" Kubota: In a rematch from the Okaziki City Gym show, Kubota challenge Suzuki to a Gunryo rules match. Suzuki accepted, only to get nailed by a fireball by Kubota! Kubota went for the rollup while Suzuki was blinded, but only got a one and a half count. Kubota then went insane, choking Suzuki on the mat, then pummeling him with a chain wrapped around his fist. Suzuki started to bleed, and Kubota rolled him outside, where he took a chair and blasted him with it. He rolled Suzuki back into the ring, went for a cover, but only got a two count. Kubota got flustered, and started to stomp away at Suzuki. He then dragged him to his feet, and went for a suplex, only for Suzuki to block it, then counter it into a brainbuster. Suzuki then drilled Kubota into the mat again with a DDT, and covered for a two count. Suzuki brought Kubota up to his feet again, and went for a whip, which Kubota reversed into a short-arm knee to the gut, the hit a piledriver. To Kubota's astonishment. Suzuki got right to his feet after the piledriver, and nailed him with a stiff lariat., sending him back to the ropes. Kubota bounced off, and Suzuki kicked him in the gut, then hit a swinging neckbreaker. Suzuki again got a two count. Suzuki again brought Kubota to his feet, who thumbed him in the eye. Kubota then applied a standing side headlock, and peppered Suzuki's face with right handed knuckle punches. Suzuki started to sag, then locked his arms around Kubota's waist, and with Kubota still punching him, fell backwards into a backdrop driver than stunned Kubota. Suzuki brought Kubota up, and hit a second backdrop driver, then put him in the Dragon Sleeper. Kubota tried to reach the ropes, but couldn't get his foot on them, and was forced to submit. Winner: "GAIJIN Extreme" Jun Suzuki via submission Otaku says: Two in a row for Suzuki! Kubota's just not in his league. Ono: This young lion is someone to watch out for. He's dangerous! Noboru "Raging Bull" Nishida vs "Jackhammer" Vince Larson: The returning Larson (who had been injured by Chiimu Tora member Junya Toroyama) took on the "Raging Bull". Both men are power wrestlers, so this match proved to be a stiff one. Both men traded stiff elbow shots, as well as a few lariats, with neither man going down in the early part of the match. Nishida hit a powerslam on Larson, then attempted a boston crab, but Larson was able to power out of it. Nishida then went for a series of backbreakers, scoring a one and a half count on Larson. Larson came back though, kicking Nishida, before driving him hard to the mat with a powerbomb for a one and a half count of his own. Larson then pressed Nishida over his head, before dropping him face first to the mat. He then hit a big legdrop off the ropes, and scored a two count. Larson then tried to whip Nishida into the corner, but Nishida reversed it, and Larson slammed hard into the buckle, followed by Nishida catching him with a stiff lariat to the chest. Nishida then hammered Larson with a series of knee strikes, before backing off at the ref's urging. He then went back in, this time with a series of shoulderblocks. He then tried to whip Larson to the opposite corner, but Larson stopped short, and pulled Nishida into a Manhattan Drop, holding on, then dumping him on his head with an overhead belly to belly suplex. Larson brought Nishida to his feet again, and planted him with a side slam. Larson got right up, ran to the ropes, and hit a high jumping elbowdrop to Nishida's chest. He covered for a two count, and started to argue with the ref. Nishida got to his feet, his face flushed with adrenalin. Larson turned around, only to find himself driven to the mat with a Chokeslam! The crowd started to chant for Nishida as he went to the top rope, and waited for Larson to get up. Larson got to his feet slowly, only for Nishida to hit a diving lariat that sent him back down. Nishida covered for a two count, then dragged Larson up, and hit his Raging Bull Bomber on him. Nishida went for the cover, but Larson managed to kick out before a three count. Nishida was stunned, and went for the RBB again, but Larson blocked it, and flipped Nishida over to his back. Nishida popped right back up, only to catch a boot to the face from Larson, knocking him back to the ropes. Larson whipped Nishida across the ring, then came off the opposite ropes with a flying shoulderblock that staggered Nishida. Larson once more pressed Nishida over his head, this time dropping him down into a spinebuster that shook the mat. Wasting no time, Larson dragged Nishida to his feet, and hit the Jackhammer, getting the three count. Winner: "Jackhammer" Vince Larson by Pinfall. Otaku says: Brutal, stiff match between these two powerhouses. Larson's improved since coming back from his injury. Ono says: Larson definitely has that inner fire going for him now. Nishida's no pushover either! Tasogare no Prince vs Jet Jaguar III: TnP's comeback from his loss during the Baba Cup continued with his confrontation with the current Jet Jaguar. Both men shook hands at the start of the match, then locked up. Jaguar took TnP over with an armdrag, then caught him with a hiptoss. TnP got back to his feet, bowed to Jaguar, and they again locked up. This time, TnP took Jaguar over with an armdrag, then hiptossed him as he got up. When they locked up a third time, Jaguar maneuvered TnP into a side headlock, but TnP backed to the ropes, and whipped him across the ring. Jaguar came off the ropes, and TnP dropped down, then did a leap frog when Jaguar came off the ropes again. As Jaguar bounced off the ropes a third time, TnP took him over with a hurricanrana. The crowd applauded loudly, and as Jaguar started to get up, got caught with a dropkick to the face, which sent him through the ropes to the floor. TnP went to the top rope, and as Jaguar recovered, TnP went for a shooting star press, but Jaguar moved, and TnP hit the padding surrounding the ring. The crowd "ooohed" as TnP crashed, while Jaguar leapt onto the security railing, and hit a moonsault onto TnP's back. Jaguar rolled to his feet, hopped up on the apron, sprang onto the middle rope, and came off, backflipping into an elbowdrop across TnP's chest. The crowd started to chant Jaguar's name, as he rolled TnP into the ring, then went for a springboard legdrop. He made a cover, and got a two count. Jaguar then went for a powerbomb, but TnP countered with a backdrop, then ran to the ropes, catching Jaguar with a dropkick to the gut as he started to get up. Jaguar doubled over, and TnP drove him to the mat with a Rocker Dropper, then ran to the ropes again, hitting a Lionsault. He made the cover, and got a two count. TnP started to bring Jaguar up, but Jaguar rocked him with a European uppercut, then went for a small package, getting a two count. Both men rolled to their feet, and Jaguar rushed TnP, who side stepped him, then grabbed him from behind as he hit the ropes, and rolled him up for a two count. Both men again got to their feet and stared at each other, while the crowd applauded. They circled each other, then locked up, with Jaguar going behind TnP, and hitting a backdrop suplex. Jaguar rolled to his feet, bringing TnP up with him, and hit a second backdrop, then rolled on top for another two count pin attempt. Jaguar brought TnP to his feet, and whipped him to the ropes. Jaguar went for a spinebuster, but TnP countered it with a Tornado DDT, driving Jaguar's head into the mat. He started to bring Jaguar up, then got under him, lifting Jaguar off the mat on his shoulders. Jaguar started to punch TnP on the head, until he dropped forward, driving Jaguar face first into the mat. With the crowd applauding loudly, TnP went to the top rope, and executed a beautiful 360 somersault into a legdrop from the top rope, which he calls Nightfall. He made the cover, and the ref counted to three. After the bell rang, TnP helped Jaguar to his feet, and both men shook hands. Winner: Tasogare no Prince by Pinfall. Otaku says: Lucky break for the Prince. Ono says: TnP is on a roll; title holders beware! Tsutomu Kobyashi vs "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder: The strong man of the Crush Crew took on the "Lone Wolf" in a spectacular brawl. Thunder hadn't forgotten the win the Crew and Despair got a few weeks ago, so he was ready for anything. Kobayashi tried to jump Thunder as he entered the ring, but Thunder clocked him with the handle of his whip, wrapped it around his throat, and whipped Kobayashi across the ring, his neck snapping when the whip stopped him short. Thunder threw the whip down, and started to put the boots to Kobayashi, who was still clutching his throat. Thunder then dragged Kobayashi to his feet, hoisted him up, and dropped him throat first onto the top rope. Kobayashi rolled on the mat, holding his throat in pain, while Thunder was given grief by the referee. The crowd started to chant Thunder's name, then started to boo as Botan Nakatoni made it to the ring, and hopped up on the apron. The ref told him to leave, but Thunder pushed him aside and took a swing at him, which Nakatoni ducked, before he snapped Thunder's neck on the top rope. The ref missed that, but ordered Nakatoni to leave ringside. He did so reluctantly, but smiled as he saw his partner start to get up. Kobayashi made it to his feet before Thunder did. Kobayashi caught Thunder with a knee to the gut, and ran to the ropes as Thunder doubled over. He came off and hit a swinging neckbreaker, got to his feet, then landed a stiff elbow to Thunder's chest. He went for a cover, but only scored a one and a half. Kobayashi stood Thunder up, ran to the ropes, and nailed him with a Yazkua kick. Thunder staggered back, blood seeping from the side of his mouth. He smiled malevolently at Kobayashi, then fired back with a European uppercut. Kobayashi staggered back from the blow, only to have Thunder grab him, and take him over with an Exploder suplex. Kobayashi landed hard, and as he got to his feet, Thunder sent him back down with a stiff lariat. Thunder then raked his eyes across his bootlaces, then drove his knee onto his head. The ref made Thunder back off, and the crowd was chanting his name loudly. The ref checked on Kobayashi, then allowed the match to continue. Thunder immediately went for a fistdrop, then another, and another, and finally a fourth one. Kobayashi was bleeding from his forehead after that. Thunder dragged Kobayashi to his feet, and went for a bulldog, but Kobayashi pushed Thunder off, and he landed hard on his back. Kobyashi set himself, and as Thunder got to his feet, Kobayashi caught him with a superkick, causing Thunder to spit blood from his mouth. He fell onto the ropes, and Kobayashi moved in, and landed a stiff chop to his chest. He whipped Thunder across the ring, and caught him in a sleeper as he came off. Thunder countered with a jawbreaker though, and Kobayashi fell to the mat, clutching his jaw. Thunder brought Kobayashi to his feet, and then nailed him with two short-arm lariats, followed by a lightning fast DDT, dropping him more on his face than on his head. Kobayashi rolled around again, holding his face, and Thunder just smiled as he spit blood out of his mouth. Thunder dragged Kobayashi up again, then drove his head into the mat with a piledriver, covering for a two and a half count. Thunder went for another Exploder, but Kobayashi countered with some elbows to the side of his head. Thunder broke the hold, and Kobayashi nailed him with a headbutt. Thunder fired back with one of his own, and the two traded headbutts, until finally Kobayashi staggered back, stunned. He checked his head, which was bleeding more profusely. Kobayashi charged Thunder, who stopped him short with a kick to the gut. He then hit the Widowmaker, spiking his head into the mat. However, Thunder didn't go for the pin. Instead, he dragged Kobayashi up, hit a second Widowmaker on him, then brought him up a third time, and went for a standing Dragon Sleeper, which is one of Koabayashi's finishing holds. With Kobayashi pretty much out cold from the two Widowmakers, the referee quickly called for the bell. Winner: "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder by submission. Otaku says: Thunder obviously sending a message to Despair and his allies that he's going to take them down! Ono: Thunder is a great competitor, and Despair has brought out his dark side. I pity those who get in his path while he seeks revenge! Main Event: NCWF Cruiserweight title match: El Santa Azteca (Challenger) vs Super Bakemono (Champion): After his non-title victory on 5/10, El Santa Azteca was granted a shot at the NCWF cruiserweight title by CEO Yamashita. The crowd was pumped to see the aging Luchador get a shot at winning another title. Azteca came out to a standing ovation, and was seconded by none other than Masashi "Lightning" Lanther, who had held the NCWF title for a very long time. Super Bakemonio came out to a decent ovation, seconded by Gunryo champion Despair. The match started off swiftly, with Bakemono catching Azteca with a knee to the gut as they locked up. Azteca doubled over, and Bakemono went to the ropes, and came off with a Rocker Dropper, driving Azteca to the mat. He went back to the ropes, and nailed Azteca with a baseball slide dropkick. Bakemono then started to stomp on Azteca's head, until the referee forced him to back off. The ref asked Azteca if he wanted to forfeit, and he refused. Bakemono then went back to work on Azteca, stomping him a few more times, before dragging him to his feet. He whipped him to the ropes, and bent over as Azteca came off, allowing Azteca to flip over his back and land on his feet behind him. Bakemono stood up, turned, and caught a stiff European uppercut for his trouble, Azteca then sent him to the mat with a hiptoss, then dropkicked him over the top rope as he got to his feet. Azteca ran to the opposite side, catching Bakemono with a baseball slide of his own as the champion got up, sending him crashing into the railing. Azteca went to the top rope, and came down on Bakemono with a double axe handle blow. As the referee started to count, Azteca slammed Bakemono's head onto the security railing, then whipped him parallel to the ring, slamming him into the railing. He charged the champion, but Bakemono nailed him with a rolling koppo kick. Bakemono then rammed Azteca's head into the railing, before rolling him back into the ring. Bakemono got up on the apron, and waited. As Azteca got to his feet, Bakemono sprung to the top rope, and came off with a missile dropkick that sent Azteca across the ring. Bakemono dragged Azteca to his feet, took him over with a snapmare, then landed a stiff chop to the back of his head. As Azteca recoiled in pain, Bakemono went to the ropes, and landed a dropkick to Azteca's face. Bakemono went for a cover, but only scored a one and a half count. Bakemono brought Azteca to his feet, and took him over with an armdrag. As Azteca rolled to his feet, the champion caught him with a spinwheel kick that sent him back to the ropes. Bakemono whipped him to the ropes, then leap frogged him as he came off, running to the opposite side. As Azteca came off the ropes again, Bakemono did a springboard moonsault, taking the challenger to the mat, and scored a two count. Bakemono brought Azteca to his feet again, and went for an enzugiri, but Azteca ducked, and Bakemono landed on the mat. As he got up, Azteca landed a stiff chop to his chest, then rocked him with a European uppercut. Azteca whipped the champion to the ropes, nailing him with a spinning backbreaker as he came off them. Azteca turned the champion over into a single leg crab, trying to force a submission. The crowd got excited, with fans chanting for both competitors. Bakemono almost gave up, as he had trouble reaching the ropes. After about four minutes, he finally was able to grab the bottom rope, and the referee ordered the hold broken. Azteca broke cleanly, and looked over at Lanther, who gave him a thumbs up. Azteca waited for Bakemono to make his feet, then landed another stiff chop across his chest, before whipping him to the ropes, and executing another spinning backbreaker. Azteca dragged the champion up, and went for an Abdominal Stretch, but Bakemono was able to counter it with a hiptoss before he could lock it on. The champion caught Azteca with a rolling koppo kick as he got to his feet, and drove him into the corner with two more. Bakemono sat Azteca on the top rope, mounted it, and took him to the mat with a stunning hurricanrana. Bakemono went to the opposite corner at a run, stepladdered to the top rope, and hit a moonsault. He went for the cover, but Azteca kicked out at two. Bakemono dragged Azteca to his feet, and hit a snap suplex on him. He rolled to his feet, bringing Azteca with him, and went for a second one, but Azteca blocked it, then countered with one of his own. He rolled to his feet, bringing Bakemono up with him, but instead of going for second suplex, went for a small package, getting a two count on the surprised champion. Both men rolled to their feet, and Azteca did a handspring, locking his legs around the champions neck, and took him over with a rana. He went for the cover again, but only got a two count. With the fans clearly behind him now, Azteca brought Bakemono to his feet again, and executed the Aztec Suplex. Bakemono landed hard, but got right to his feet, and charged Azteca. The challenger sidestepped him, and Bakemono went to the ropes, did a springboard moonsault, landing behind Azteca, and took him down with a DDT. The crowd were awed by this move, reminiscent of Tsunami, and chants for Bakemono started to get louder. Bakemono dragged Azteca up, hoisted him over his back, and hit the Bakemono Driver. The crowd went silent as Azteca's head was driven into the mat. Instead of pinning him though, Bakemono went to the top rope, and went for the Bakemono Bomb, crashing down on the challenger. He hooked the leg, and the referee counted to three. Winner, and still NCWF Cruiserweight Champion: Super Bakemono via pinfall Otaku says: Another great victory for the champion! Ono says: I hope Azteca wasn't hurt to badly by the Bakemono Driver! After the match, Despair entered the ring, and started to put the boots to Azteca. Lanther came in, and he attacked the Gunryo champion, even wresting his title belt from him, and bashing it over his head. Bakemono nailed Lanther from behind with the NCWF title though, then hit the Bakemono Driver on him as well. Brody Thunder hit the ring, and Bakemono charged him, only to get a kick in the gut, followed by the Widowmaker. Brody set himself, waiting for Despair to get up. When he did, Thunder caught him with a vicious lariat, sending him back to the mat. He dragged Despair back to his feet, and hit the Widowmaker on him, driving Despair's head into his own title belt! Wrestlers from the back stormed the ring, and separated the combatants. After the ring was cleared, Azteca was taken backstage on a stretcher, the fans applauding him as he went by them. Lanther was helped up by Thunder, and the two men went backstage together. On the SJPW message board, many fans were speculating that Brody Thunder was going to join Chiimu Tora, while others stated that he only aided Azteca and Lanther to get another crack at Despair. Everyone did agree though, that the tag team match on the May 29th taping of The Zone! was going to be one match they didn't want to miss...
