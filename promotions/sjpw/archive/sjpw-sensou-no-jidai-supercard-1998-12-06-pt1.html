The crowd is cheering wildly. The camera then pans to the broadcast position. Seated there are Tiger Kikuchi & Akira Ono. Both men are smiling...] Tiger Kikuchi: Konnichi-Wa! Welcome to Sensou no Jidai, the first supercard for the revamped SJPW! I am Tiger Kikuchi, and this is my partner, Akira Ono [Ono bows] TK: Tonight, we bring to you six great matches. We have two title matches, as Mitsunori Takizawa challenges Toshiaki Takada for the Koubousen Geijutsu title, and Yukio Murakami faces Tasogare no Prince to crown the first Welterweight champion! Akira Ono: We also have the last first round match in our Koubousen Geijutsu mini-tournament, as The Mandarin faces Steel Serpent! That should be a great match, as both men are accomplished martial arts experts! TK: Very true, Ono-sama. We also have an eight man tag match featuring All-Asian tag champions The Bonecrushers teaming with The Tokyo Express against The Yakuza & Kenji Ishida & Shinji Usami... AO: Then we have two six man matches, as Ace Kilcannon and The Outlaws face Rupan Loco and The Young Warriors. Our other match features Banshee, Jet Jaguar II and Shishi Fujiwara, representing Chiimu Tora, as they battle Kagemusha, Kabuki Kid & Kensei Tokguawa, representing The Daiymo. TK: This should prove to be an outstanding card. Let's go to the ring, and get our first match underway... ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / Six Man Tag Team Match: / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Kilcannon/Outlaws vs Loco/Young Warriors /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [The camera pans to the ring, where Nishi Toju & Owada Botan stand. Nishi waits for the crowd to die down, the proceeds to make the introdcutions...] Nishi Toju: This First contest is scheduled for one fall with a thirty minute time limit. It is a special 6-man attraction! Introducing first... hailing from parts unknown... weighing in at 261 pounds, this is RUPAN LOOOOOOCOOOOO! [Wolf Howls for anyone from 10-30 seconds before Rupan Loca runs down towards the ring. He's about 6'6" in very good shape He has a few scars on his back from previous matches, also a scar on his left shoulder from a bite from Tetnus in a match. Rupan Loco is dressed in a dark blue pair of long tights, and a brown furry boots] Nishi Toju: And his team mates... at a combined weight of 438 pounds.... from Hong Kong,... "Little Lion" Huan Feng Sho.... from Tokyo, Japan... "Raging Bull" Yagyu Retsudo....THE YOUNG WARRIOORRSSS!! [The lights dim, the videoscreen goes dark... And then "Line Up" by Aerosmith blares from the speakers as the darkness of the videoscreen shatters in a pattern revealing red letters on a black background which read "YOUNG WARRIORS"! The Spotlights hit the entrance to the aisle where Huan Feng Sho and Yagyu Retsudo step out from behind the curtains. Huan does a few martial arts kicks and punches into the air, and Retsudo raises his arms into the air with a wide grin on his face, looking around at the crowd. The two men then jog to the ring, slapping hands and high fiving fans all the way. Once they enter the ring Huan does his routine from the center with four rapid high sidekicks, one towards each side of the ring, without letting his kicking foot touch the ground as he pivots on his other leg. Retsudo turns to the crowd, scrapes his foot on the mat like a bull about to charge and slaps himself across his massive chest with his hands while letting out a thunderous roar!] Nishi Toju: And their opponents... ["This is the Life" by Weird Al Yankovic comes up on the PA system] ... introducing first, at a combined weight of 470 pounds... Brent Maverick... Doc Holliday... they are... THE OUTLLLLLAAAAWWWWWSSSSSSSS! [Maverick and Holliday make their way towards the ring, acompanied by their manager Tex James. Maverick and Holiday seem to be enjoying themselves as the walk to the ring, mocking the fans and revelling in the jeers. Brent is a well-built man with short wavy brown hair. Brent is usually possessed of a little stubble on his chin, with a small moustache. He is considered ruggedly handsome, but not overly so. Dresses with an Old West flair, with a black duster, red cowboy boots, black cowboy hat, and a gunbelt, complete with two six-shooters (presumably modified so they don't shoot...). Trunks are dark red, are knee-length, and have playing cards on the sides (four aces and the Twenty-One of Spades). Carries a metal Haliburton briefcase (presumably to carry gambling/wrestling winnings in). Doc is a well-built man with long wavy light brown hair. He is entirely clean-shaven, and is a very handsome man. Dresses with an Old West flair, with a black duster, red cowboy boots, a black cowboy hat, and a gunbelt, complete with two six-shooters (presumably modified so they don't shoot...). Trunks are dark red, are knee-length, and have playing cards on the sides (four aces and the Twenty-One of Spades). Doc has a very noticable limp. He uses a mahogany carved cane to get around. Their manager, Tex James is short, fat, has black hair and a big black handlebar mustache. His undershirt is white, with black suspenders that Tex likes to clutch in pride. Wears a grey business suit.] Nishi Toju: And their partner... weigh... [Toju is cut off as "Judgement Night" by Onyx and Biohazard rips out from the arena PA. Ace Kilcannon comes walking out from the back, with a cocky swagger to his step. He's in black combat boots, blue jeans, a black "Prepare to be ACED!!" t-shirt, and black Oakley sunglasses. He has a microphone in his hand, and heads down to the ring. He hops up onto the arpon and leans on the ropes, without getting in the ring. He spits into the crowd and then turns towards the ring.] Ace Kilcannon: "You know, things have just been ALL f<beep>ed up since SJPW re-opened it's doors. People have scattered all over the place with just the thought of having to face me. I know I'm a bad man, but I really didn't think these damn nips around here realized that fact yet. For once, though...I was wrong. Black Sun Rising ran like the yellow bastards they are...hell, Alex Akira and Kenji Takada ran before I even got a chance to plant my foot in their asses. Hiroyoshi Takada ran for the f<beep>in' hills after I caved his damn face in. Not to mention, Ed Dean and his slant eyed bitch partner took off before I could even sucker them into goin' after Black Sun Rising and then kicking their asses, too. So, I'm stuck in this no heat, worthless six-man match with five big f<beep>in' scrubs. What kinda bulls<beep>t is this? A damned main eventer is relegated to this crap because everyone is scared of me. Chicken s<beep>t mother f<beep>ers!" [Ace turns to the booing crowd.] Ace Kilcannon: "There isn't ONE single person who wants to step in the ring with me...not one, so I'm gonna do these c<beep>k smokers a favor..." [Ace turns back to the ring.] Ace Kilcannon: "Rupan...Warriors, I'll save you three the pain and suffering you'll have to endure facing off with me, and Outlaws... I'll save you two the embrassment of being CARRIED by me to the win and then having me beat the piss out of you. So, in case you all couldn't figure it out...go f<beep>k yourselves...I'm outta here..." [Ace flips the mic into the air and then gives everyone in the ring a double middle finger. He hops down off the apron and walks to the back, jawjacking with the fans the whole way, who are booing him lustily. The Outlaws both begin a heated debate with Tex James, who looks back and forth between his men and the retreating Ace Kilcannon.] AO: Unbelievable! Kilcannon is abandoning The Outlaws! The man is a coward! I think Kilcannon is scared to get in the ring with Rupan Loca and the Young Warriors. Not for a second to I believe ANYONE in Super Japan Pro is afraid of this yellow dog! TK: Calm yourself Akira. I'm sure Jumbo Kobayashi is already levelling fines against Kilcannon as we speak. I suppose the question now is: will this match happen? Or will the referee simply count out Kilcannon and give the win to The Young Warriors and Rupan Loco? [Owada Botan calls for the bell reluctantly and immediately both members of The Young Warriors charge the Outlaws who turn around a moment too late. A dropkick by Huan Feng Sho sends Doc Holliday through the ropes and out to the floor, while Yagyu Retsudo catches Brent Maverick with a clothesline, stunning him. Retsudo follows up with a series of quick punches, then sends Maverick towards the opposite side of the ring where Rupan Loco catches him and then drives him into the mat with a vicious Spinebuster Slam.] TK: It looks like the match will indeed go on, Akira, and the Young Warriors and Rupan Loco have seized on The Outlaws' distraction. They were obviously in discussion with Tex James about the Ace Kilcannon situation, and that momentary lapse has given their opponents the upper hand. AO: I can't believe Kilcannon is such a worthless cur. We knew he was scum to begin with, but he has gone to a whole new level tonight. [The Young Warriors leave the ring as Botan reaches a four-count, leaving Brent Maverick in the ring with Rupan Loco. Loco brings Maverick up off the mat and quickly goes behind, into a waistlock.. then up and over with a release German suplex that lands Maverick neck first on the canvas. The crowd OOOHS at the impact as Maverick clutches the back of his neck.] TK: Tremendous impact on that suplex, and Rupan Loco follows up with a legdrop across the back of the neck and now makes the tag in to the "Little Lion", Huan Feng Sho. Although Sho is a rookie in the sport, we know he possesses tremendous martial arts skills. AO: Hai, but placing well in martial arts tournaments and doing well inside the puroresu ring are two very different things. [Sho waits patiently for Maverick to get to his feet, shifting his weight from foot to foot.. moving in slow... circling around. As Maverick stands up, Sho begins to lay into him with a series of fast kicks, alternating between the chest and the thighs... a flurry of action that generates a lot of approval from the crowd. Maverick tries to cover up, but is forced back into the corner by a sudden thrust kick that catches him square in the jaw.] TK: Very impressive foot speed on the part of Huan Feng Sho! I don't think Brent Maverick knows how many times Sho hit him, and now Sho pulls Maverick out of the corner and Brent looks to be on wobbly legs. AO: After those kicks I'm not surprised. Sho now with a backfist to the jaw, and Maverick looks to be bleeding from the mouth and Sho goes for a Uraken .... but Maverick ducks the spinning backfist! LUNGING LARIAT right to the back of Sho's head and that looks to have stunned the Little Lion! Maverick may not have that bodybuilders physique, but he has a lot of power, as Huan Feng Sho just discovered. [Maverick shakes his head to clear the cobwebs as Sho slowly gets to his feet. Maverick picks his moment and as Sho turns around, Maverick dives into his mid-section with a Spear Tackle. Tex James begins pounding on the ring apron, pointing to Doc Holiday. Maverick nods and grabs hold of one of Sho's arms, dragging him towards the Outlaws corner where he makes the tag. Maverick twist up on Sho's left arm, holding it out, while Holliday springboards over the top rope and across the extended arm with a legdrop. Sho howls in pain as he drops to the mat clutching his arm as Holliday and Maverick high-five one another.] TK: A good double team move on the part of the Outlaws, and Huan Feng Sho is in pain... possibly with a dislocated shoulder... AO: Or broken arm. Doc Holliday didn't come down lightly on the extended arm, and there wasn't much give there. However the Outlaws really need to be watching out for his kicks rather than his punches. Sho's foot speed, as we noted, is amazing. [Holliday puts the boots to the fallen Sho for a few moments, then drops down for a lateral press... 1... 2...] AO: Holliday wasted too much time there and could only get the two count. Perhaps if he had gone directly for the cover... TK: I think Sho is far from being put away, Akira. He is in a lot of trouble though, as Doc Holliday brings him back to a vertical base... hooks the head and grabs a handful of tights and.... big vertical suplex lands Sho back on the canvas. Holliday wasting no time in heading up to the top rope.... very agile for a man with a limp, wouldn't you agree Akira? AO: Yes, very agile indeed, Kikuchi-san.... Holliday measuring his man and.... GUILLOTINE LEG DR... SHO MOVES! The Little Lion saw Holliday coming and managed to roll out of the way! If that had landed, this match would be over. [Holliday rolls on the mat holding his leg, while Sho slowly begins to crawl towards his corner. Tex James pounds on the mat again, screaming to his men. Brent Maverick climbs into the ring and rushes towards Sho, but Sho rolls forward and makes a tag and in comes "Raging Bull" Yagyu Retsudo, who tags the oncoming Maverick with a forearm smash that sends the illegal Outlaw staggering backwards. Owada Botan gets in Maverick's face, pointing to his corner and yelling at him to get out of the ring. Meanwhile, Doc Holliday has managed to get back to his feet and shakes out his leg.] TK: Well if that limp earlier was a psychological ploy, it will be hard to tell that now. After missing that guillotine legdrop, Holliday is lucky to be able to stand. AO: It will be interesting to see if Holliday can work his aerial style now, and against someone like Yagyu, who is a good mat wrestler, you don't want to have an easy target like that leg. Not that I care if The Outlaws lose, mind you... they are hardly honorable men... perhaps just a bit more worthy of respect than the coward, Ace Kilcannon. [Yagyu moves in on Holliday and the two men go to lock up... only to have Holliday back off at the last second. Holliday smirks at Yagyu and says something to Yagyu, who looks like he's been struck in the face. Yagyu suddenly lunges at Holliday, apparently taking Holliday by surprise. The two men fall to the mat, Yagyu on top, throwing punch after punch at his opponent.] AO: I don't know what Holliday said to Yagyu, but that _can't_ be the result he was looking for! Yagyu is just pummelling Holliday, who's trying to cover up... wisely. TK: We obviously couldn't hear exactly what Holliday said, but I believe Holliday made some kind of reference to Yagyu's father. As you said though Akira, whatever Holliday did say it clearly was not favorable. The ring official is warning Retsudo about the closed fists, and on the outside the Outlaws' manager, Tex James, is demanding the referee do something. AO: It's Holliday's own fault. Let him deal with it like a man. [Botan finally manages to get Yagyu off of Holliday, who rolls towards his corner but doesn't make the tag. He looks to Tex James, who nods and opens up Brent's haliburton and unrolls a poster of Yagyu Buntaro, Yagyu Retsudo's father. Buntaro is in the ring and although it's clearly been edited the poster depicts him on his knees grovelling. The japanese word for "COWARD" is spelled out across the poster in large red letters. Retsudo sees James holding the poster and immediately charges towards the Outlaws, only to have Holliday grab the front of his tights, sending him careening through the ring ropes and out onto the floor. Tex James brings the haliburton case up and then smashes it down over the head of Retsudo. The crowd begins to boo heavilly, then it turns to cheers as Sho and Rupan Loco come running over to the Outlaws corner. Brent Maverick meets Rupan Loco with Doc Holliday's walking stick, striking Loco in the chest, and following up with a jab to the throat. Holliday reaches between the ropes and takes Sho by surprise, grabbing him by the hair and pulling him into the ring, viciously choking him. Owada Botan wastes no time in calling for the bell, but Sho continues to be choked out by Holliday while Maverick clubs both Loco and Yagyu a few more times before climbing back into the ring. The crowd continues to boo heavilly as Holliday pulls Sho to his feet and twists and extends the arm they assaulted earlier while Maverick climbs onto the second turnbuckle and comes off ... smashing the cane across upraised elbow of Sho. Holliday lets Sho drop to the canvas and the two Outlaws begin kicking and stomping away on the injured arm as Tex James climbs into the ring, all smiles and patting the two men on the back as they continue to work over Sho. Finally the boos turn to cheers as Rupan Loco and Yagyu Retsudo both dive back into the ring, but it's too late... the Outlaws have seen them coming and quickly hightail it out of the ring while Retsudo and Loco turn their attentions to the fallen Sho. Nishi Toju enters the ring, and quickly makes his announcement...] Nishi: The winners of this match, as a result of a disqualification ...RUPAN LOCO & THE YOUNG WARRIORS!!!! [crowd applauds] TK: What a disgusting display by The Outlaws! AO: Those curs deserve all the fines they will gave leveled on them! [The camera pans to show Rupan Loco & Retsudo help Sho out of the ring. The crowd applauds again as they help him to the back...] TK: I hope Sho will be all right. AO: As do I. I look forward to the day the Outlaws must face The Young Warriors in the ring! TK: Our next match might resemble a martial arts bout more than a wrestling one as both men are very adept practictioners of unarmed combat. AO: Hai. The Mandarin with his Shaolin discipline mixed with Aikido, Jijutsu, Karate, and many other forms and Steel Serpent with his Muay Thai Kickboxing. This should be a great clash of two masters. TK: I agree Akira-san. Both wanting to prove they are the best in their chosen styles. ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / /Koubousen Geijutsu Mini-Tournament, RD 1: / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / The Mandarin vs Steel Serpent /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [Cut to the ring where Nishi stands in the center of the ring while referee Goto Kyoji stands in one of the corners.] Nishi: The following contest is set for one fall with a 20 minute time-limit and it is a first round match in the Koubousen Geijutsu tournament![fans cheer] ["Guilty" by Gravity Kills starts to play] Nishi: Introducing first...From Parts Unknown, weighing in at 189 pounds...THE MANDARIN!! [The fans give a mixed reaction as The Mandarin walks down the aisle, smiling wolfishly. He wears long black gi pants and black slippers. His hands and wrists are taped and as he hops up to the apron the camera gets a shot of an extensive four color tattoo of a tiger across his back, and brandings on the inside of both forearms. The raised image of a tiger on the right and dragon on the left. He stands in the corner, looking confident, but focused on the fight ahead. "Unforgivin" by Metallica replaces "Guilty" and the fans give a big pop.] Nishi: His opponent...From Bangkok, Thailand and weighing in at 240 pounds...STEEL SERPENT!! [The crowd continues to applaud and cheer as Steel Serpent walks down the ring, striding powerfully, his focus in the ring, his eyes trained on The Mandarin. He wears black trunks, his trademark black mask with a silver cobra emblem on the front, black padded gloves and tape on his ankles up to his knee. He walks up the steps, never taking his eyes off of Mandarin, and then steps through the ropes.] AO: Both men looking very focused on this match. I have said it before and I will say it again, I believe Steel Serpent is one of the most skilled men in SJPW, why he wastes his time in this "garbage divsion", I don't understand it. TK: I believe he choses it because it is greater test of his abilities. Besides, I believe the new era of Koubousen Geijutsu matches will have differant meanings. This is more like a full contact martial arts match than a "garbage" match. AO: I suppose, I suppose. [Goto brings both men together, and gives some last minute instruction. He then asks for them to shake hands. They instead bring their fists together, with Mandarin flaring a slight smile. Serpent's emotions are impossible to read as he breaks off and goes to his corner. Goto calls for the bell, and both men circle out of their corners, and continue circling until they meet in the center of the ring.] TK: Mandarin bouncing on the balls of his feet, letting his guard drop some, he will have to use his quickness to good effect here. He is giving up the edge in power. AO: Hai. Serpent taking up the basic fighting stance of Muay Thai, fists extended out, one knee slightly bent, ready to strike. [Mandarin makes the first move, firing a kick to the leg, but Serpent blocks the blow, taking it on the shin. Mandarin dances away, and then bops and weaves his way back in, and attempts some quick stabbing jabs and then a forearm blow, but Serpent is able to block them and he fires back with a knee strike, but Mandarin has already ducked away.] TK: Mandarin continuing to circle, and Serpent seems to be keeping his gaurd up, and waiting fo Mandarin to make a move, and leave himself open. AO: Good strategy. He should be able to absorb any strikes from the smaller man, and then bring him in so he can deliver his offense. [Mandarin snaps off a roundhouse, but Serpent is able to avoid it easily. The fans are quiet, waiting for either man to hit the first major blow. Goto stands back, looking in for any illegal action as the two combatants stand in the middle of the ring.] TK: Mandarin feints with a roundhouse, and now spins with a backheel kick that partially catches Serpent. [Fans applaud] AO: Steel Serpent is able to recover though, and he backs away, but Mandarin isn't content, and he fires in with a flurry of punches, elbows, and kicks and forearms! [The fans "ohhhh" and "ahhhh" at the speed of the strikes thrown by Mandarin, his arms and legs moving in a blur, striking Serpent,and driving him back to the corner.] Goto: Break! Break! TK: Goto backing Mandarin out, and he dances away, smiling and raising his hands. AO: I think he celebrates too soon. [Serpent lowers his arms which he used to deflect most of Mandarin's blows. He then calmly moves back to the center of the ring, and assumes his fighting stance once more. Mandarin scowls a bit, and begins to circle around his opponent once again.] TK: Mandarin now spins in the air...Spinning Kicks, and Serpent backs up to avoid the strikes. [Mandarin with several spin kicks sending him cartwheeling through the air like a ballerina, and Serpent backs to the corner to avoid them. Mandarin then does a couple of back flips out of the corner, and motions for Serpent to come to him. He gets some applause from the crowd.] AO: Mandarin attempting to show Serpent up, and get him angry. This strategy could end up hurting him. TK: Serpent seems unaffected by Mandarin's showmanship and he moves back to the center, with his gaurd up. [Mandarin shakes his head, and moves back in, attempting to lock up with Serpent, only to have Serpent land a stinging kick to the back of his leg. Serpent lunges forward, looking for a collar and elbow, but Mandarin squirms free, and spins away, rubbing the back of his leg. He shakes his head to Serpent.] TK: Serpent moves back in, and Mandarin snaps a kick to the leg, but Serpent blocks. Mandarin with a forearm, but again Serpent with his gaurd up. Mandarin attempts to lock-up, and fires in with knee strikes, but Serpent blocks those as well, and now Serpent comes to life with hard knee strikes of his own. [The fans cheer as Serpent connects with crushing knees to the chest and then rains elbows down on the back of Mandarin's neck, driving him to the mat, but Mandarin quickly spins, with a legsweep kick, bring Serpent down to the mat.] TK: Quicksand Takedown by Mandarin as he locks in a spinning toehold on Serpent. AO: Serpent looking to spin free, but Mandarin trying to turn it into an ankle-lock. Goto: Give up? Give up? [Serpent doesn't even acknowledge Goto, and easily drags himself to the ropes, causing the break and eliciting applause from the fans. Mandarin steps back, and raises his hands, looking for applause himself. As Serpent rises, Mandarin moves in connecting with a quick roundhouse to the head before Serpent can raise his gaurd. He then connects with a flurry of strikes as Serpent backs to the corner. Mandarin is once again a blur of motion, changing from kicks to punches to elbows to knees in seconds. Goto moves in to break things up, but Mandarin backflips out of the corner, and once again gestures for Serpent to come to him.] TK: This time Serpent does charge out! AO: Double-leg Takedown on Mandarin, and Serpent takes the mount. Elbow and forearm strikes, and Mandarin attempts to cover up! [The fans cheer the action as Serpent rains down blows. Mandarin manages to get his arms up, and Serpent immediately grabs the right arm, and spins into a Cross Armbreaker and the fans pop big time.] TK: JUJI GATAME!! [Mandarin frantically kicks his legs, managing to swing them around, and he snags the bottom rope with his foot. Goto breaks the hold, and the fans let out disappointed "ohhhh", but then applaud Mandarin's escape. Serpent gets to his feet, and Mandarin grips his arm, grimacing slightly, and the surprises Serpent by rolling across the mat to him, and attempting a legsweep.] TK: Serpent once again attempting the Quicksand Takedown, but Serpent jumps over the legsweep, and drops down for an armbar. AO: Mandarin rolls his body, attempting to escape the hold, but Serpent clamps it on tight. Mandarin struggles to his feet, but Serpent pulls him back down to the mat. [Mandarin lets out a cry of pain, and then drags himself across the mat, and once again hooks the ropes with his feet. Serpent seems a bit reluctant, but breaks the hold, and backs away. Mandarin rises, staring intentsely at Serpent as he shakes his arm out.] TK: Mandarin now moving in, Serpent with his gaurd up. Mandarin with a kick, blocked by Serpent. Mandarin with a forearm, and Serpent catches him with an armdrag. AO: Serpent going for the armbar again, but Mandarin is able to squirm free, and he kips up to his feet and stomps the heel of Serpent. [Serpent reaches down cluthing his heel in pain, and then rolls to his feet. Mandarin connects with a quick kick to the back of the knee, and then pivots on one leg and connects with a Spinwheel Kick to the side of the head, and Serpent goes down to the mat.] TK: Dragon Twist combo by Mandarin, and he once again stomps down on the ankle,and now with a spinning toehold, and he drops it into a Hiza Juji Gatame. AO: A very painful hold, and Serpent is able to turn his body, and now attempts to kick away at Mandarin with his free leg. [Mandarin twists with all his might on Serpent's ankle, but Serpent rains down heel blows to the shoulder, and Mandarin is forced to release the Cross Kneescissors. He rolls to his feet quickly, and Serpent is up, limping slightly. Mandarin is relentless with three snapping kicks to the right knee and ankle of Serpent, almost falling him.] TK: Mandarin has picked out the ankle, and is attempting to damage it. AO: Good strategy by Mandarin, and Serpent strikes out with an elbow, but Mandarin is just too quick. [Mandarin spins away, landing another kick to the ankle, and this time Serpent goes to the mat. As he rolls to his knees, Mandarin connects with a stiff kick right to the face causing the fans to shout "ohhhhhh".] TK: Mandarin landing a kick on a downed Serpent, and Goto moves in to check on him, and Mandarin dances away, and raises his hands to the fans. AO: He recieves nothing but jeers. The fans are firmly behind the former Battlearts champion. [Serpent pushes Goto away, and shakes out the cobwebs. He rises, and Mandarin quickly spins up and catches him with an Enziguri. Serpent's head snaps forward violently, and he drops to his knees and then collapses to the mat. Mandarin kips up, and then looks for and anklelock.] TK: Mandarin has the anklelock, and he is really applying the pressure. AO: It is not in Serpent to quit. I have never seen him surrender. [The camera moves in on a mat level close-up of Serpent. His mask may hide his face, but his eyes reveal his emotions. They do not show pain, but anger. Serpent grits his teeth, and drags himself to the ropes. Mandarin tries to pull him back to the center of the ring, but Serpent overpowers him, and makes the ropes as the fans applaud. Mandarin backs away, and as Serpent rises, he moves back in.] TK: Sidekick to the body, snap kick the knee, roundhouse right to the head! This is savage, it is brutal. AO: Serpent just hanging in the ropes as Mandarin connects with blows. Mandarin with a forearm shot, and now knees to the body and one to the head rocks Serpent back into the ropes. Goto needs to stop this. [Goto does indeed move in, but Mandarin moves him aside, and fires in with another kick to the side of Serpent's head.
