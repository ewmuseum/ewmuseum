Zenmen Sensou

31 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Zenmen Sensou concludes...] ER: We now have a match that pits a member of Team Corporate against the Koubousen Geijutsu Senshuken, Toshiaki Takada. AO: Takada, even though he wastes his talent in garbage matches, is a man of honor. Kubota, on the other hand, is a baka! ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / Koubousen Geijutsu Shimeijiai: / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Kenichi Kubota vs Toshiaki Takada /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [The camera cuts to Nishi Toju & Owada Botan in the ring...] Nishi: This next contest is a Koubousen Geijutsu Shimeijiai! It is set for one fall, twenty minute time limit. Introducing first, the challenger... ["Walk" by Pantera blares over the PA] Nishi: Representing CHIIMU CORPORATE! He hails from Osaka, and weighs in at 245 pounds....KEN-I-CHI "KURASSHAA" KU-BO-TA!!! [Kubota comes out from the back, pushing a wheelbarrow full of items in it. He pushes the wheelbarrow down to ringside, grabs a frying pan, then enters the ring...] Nishi: His opponent... ["The Hunted" by Kodo blares over the PA] Nishi: Hailing from Sapporo, and weighing in at 235 pounds, he is the current Koubousen Geijutsu Senshuken....TO-SHI-A-KI "THE PYRO" TA-KA-DA!!!! [The crowd cheers as Takada comes charging from the back, a wooden kendo sword in his hand. He storms into the ring, and charges Kubota...] ER: The champion charges Kubota! He swings the sword, but Kubota blocks it with the frying pan! Takada swings again, and Kubota blocks it again, kicks Takada in the gut...*WHACK!* and nails him with the pan! AO: You knew this was going to be the tone of the match! Takada staggers back, and Kubota charges forward...*WHACK!* and sends Takada down with another smash of the frying pan! [Takada falls to the mat, and Kubota starts to lay into him with kicks to the chest. Takada attempts to fend off the blows, but he is still dazed from being hit with the pan. Kubota grabs Takada by the hair, and drags him into a sitting position, then slams the frying pan on the top of his head. Takada collapses on the mat, and the crowd "oooohs" loudly. The camera zooms in on his face, which shows blood seeping down from his forehead. Kubota drops the pan, then kneedrops Takada across the forehead. Takada sits up, holding his head in pain, and Kubota lands an elbowsmash to the back of his neck. Kubota drags Takada to his feet, kicks him low in the gut, then elbowsmashes the back of his neck again when he doubles over....] ER: Kubota really taking it to Takada so far! Kubota now with a scoop slam, then elbowdropping the champion across the chest! AO: Kubota makes a cover... ...1... Kickout! [crowd applauds] ER: Kubota stands up, steps back, then drops forward, headbutting Takada's forehead! [crowd "oooohs"] Takada sits up in pain, and Kubota drives his knee into the back of Takada's neck! AO: Kubota is trying to prove his worth to Chiimu Corporate & Yamashita! He's become a victim of his own greed! ER: Kubota drags Takada to his feet...thumb to the eye by Takada... LARIAT sends Kubota to the mat! AO: Takada sits up, and checks the flow of blood from his forehead. He looks at the blood on his hands....and SMILES???? [Takada rises to his feet, an evil grin on his face. He drags Kubota to his feet. He slaps on a front facelock, then jumps up, then backwards, driving Kubota's head into the mat with a DDT! Takada sits up again, and rolls on top of Kubota, and starts to pummel him with his fists. Kubota tries to block the blows, but most of them get through. Takada ceases his onslaught, but remains on top of the dazed Kubota. He pulls his trademark fork from his tights, and the crowd cheers. He grabs Kubota by the back of the head, then jabs the fork into his forehead, grinding it in with all his might. The crowd "oooohs" as Kubota attempts to struggle, but cannot break free. Blood spurts from his head, splattering the mat....] ER: This match has been very brutal in a short period of time! AO: That's how these matches are! Garbage! ER: Takada now getting off of Kubota, who is rolling around the mat in pain! He's smearing blood everywhere! Takada puts his fork away, then drags Kubota to his feet...He slaps on a full nelson... RELEASE DRAGON SUPLEX!! [crowd "oooohs"] AO: Kubota landed hard on his head there! Takada has Kubota up again....FISHERMAN'S BUSTER!!! [crowd "oooohs"] [Takada gets to his feet, smiling malevolently, then raises his arms to the crowd. The crowd applauds him, and he turns towards his opponent. Kubota starts to rise, and Takada charges, sending him back to the mat with a big boot to his head. Takada starts to drag Kubota to his feet, and the crowd starts to boo. The camera view moves to the runway, showing The Maulers running to ringside. Frankie Douglas hits the ring first, and he lands a double axe handle to Takada's back. Takada lets go of Kubota, and drops to one knee. Douglas grabs Takada in a waistlock, lifts him up, then drops him down into a backbreaker! The crowd boos louder as Douglas chokes Takada on the mat, while Thomas helps Kubota to his feet. The Maulers drag Takada to his feet, and hold his arms, while Kubota grabs the wooden sword. Takada tries to struggle, but is unable to break free...] ER: This is unfair! AO: It's allowable by the rules, Eddie. You know that. [Kubota starts to lay into Takada with the wooden sword, causing welts on Takada's scarred chest. The crowd starts to cheer, and the camera shows "Jigoku" Tiger Extreme charging down the runway. He hits the ring, and nails Thomas with his bone. Thomas drops to the mat, and Takada is able to hip toss Douglas over. Kubota nails Takada with the sword again, and he drops to his knees. Kubota lands a blow to the top of Takada's head, and blood flies across the ring. Takada bends over, as if in prayer...] ER: It looks like Kubota is going to try and behead Takada with the wooden sword! AO: BUDDHA NO!!! [Kubota smiles down at Takada, then raises the sword up for a stroke. Tiger Extreme, who was out of the ring, slides back in, holding a window! He shatters the window over the back of Kubota's head, and Kubota goes down hard, bleeding. Extreme charges The Maulers, and catches both of them with a flying clothesline! The crowd goes wild as The Maulers go over the top rope to the floor. Takada starts to get to his feet, while Extreme goes out of the ring, and pulls a table from under the ring. He slides it into the ring, then attacks The Maulers again. Takada sets the table up, pulls out a small container of lighter fluid from his tights, and pours it over the table. He pulls out a lighter, and sets the table on fire....] ER: Man! Tiger Extreme saved Takada from losing his head! AO: Tiger Extreme is weird! He claims he wants to hurt Takada, then he goes and saves him! [Takada drags Kubota to his feet, then rams his head into the turnbuckle. Outside the ring, Extreme plants Thomas to the floor with a tombstone. Douglas grabs a bullrope from Kubota's wheelbarrow, and nails Extreme with the cowbell attatched to hit. In the ring, Takada sits Kubota on the top rope. He he turns around, then gets up on the top rope with Kubota. He stands Kubota up, hoisting him over his shoulders, then falls forward, driving Kubota through the burning table with a Plum Crusher off the top rope! The crowd goes nuts, standing on their feet...] ER: What a move! Takada even has the sense of mind to hook the legs... ...1... ...2... ...3...!!!! [crowd applauds] **ding**ding**ding** Nishi: The winner of this match, and STILL Koubousen Geijutsu Senshuken....TOSHIAKI "THE PYRO" TAKADA!!! [Takada gets to his feet, and falls back to the ropes as Botan raises his arm in victory. Outside the ring, Douglas is choking Tiger Extreme with the bullrope. Takada sees this, and he grabs a piece of the burning table, and climbs to the top rope. Douglas, seeing this out of the corner of his eye, turns, but Takada comes off the top, and nails him with a piece of burning wood! The crowd "oooohs" as Douglas collapses on the ground. Takada helps Extreme to his feet, and starts to drag him to the back, while Doug Thomas slides into the ring, and pulls Kubota out of the remains of the table...] ER: What a match! Takada again retains the title! AO: That Plum Crusher from the top rope was devestating! But could he do it to Tetsuo Matsumoto? [Kubota shoves Thomas off, and yells at Nishi to give him the microphone...] Kenichi Kubota: TAKADA! JIGOKU! [Takada & Extreme stop halfway up the runway, and look back at the ring] Kenichi Kubota: This isn't over! You two want a war? Then you've got one. I challenge the two of you to find a partner willing to team with you two lunatics, and face The Maulers & myself in a Koubousen Geijutsu Trios match! Do you have the courage, or are you afraid? [Takada & Extreme look at each other, then nod. Extreme loos at Kubota, and screams "YOU ARE IT!", and the crowd laughs. Takada & Extreme head to the back, the crowd cheering them all the way...] ER: It looks like we've got a Koubousen Geijutsu Trios match for Wrestlefest! Takada, Tiger Extreme, and a partner against Kubota & The Maulers. AO: Kubota & The Maulers are the insane ones to consider such a match. Buddha knows who Takada & Extreme will choose as a partner! [The camera shows the ring attendants putting out the burning table, then clearing it from the ring, while Kubota & The Maulers head to the back...] ER: Well Akira, it's time for our main event. This match has alot riding on it, doesn't it. AO: It does. I still think Kikuchi-sama is cray to challenge Ed Dean. He's not so young anymore, and Dean has defeated all of Chiimu Tora. How can Kikuchi hope to prevail??? ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / All-Asian Juuryou Shimeijiai: / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Tiger Kikuchi vs Ed Dean /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [The camera cuts to Nishi & Goto Kyoji in the ring...] Nishi: This is our MAIN EVENT for the evening! [crowd cheers] It is one fall, tv time limit remaining. It is for the All-Asian Juuryou title! ["Eye of the Tiger" by Survivor comes over the PA] Nishi: First, the challenger. He is the mentor of Chiimu Tora, and a legend known around the world. He weighs in tonight at 243 pounds...TI-GER KI-KU-CHI!!! [The crowd gives a standing ovation as Kikuchi comes out from the back, wearing his trademark Black Tiger Mask. He walks purposefully to the ring, and seemingly ignores the fans. He enters the ring, and fireworks go off in all four corners] Nishi: His opponent is the current All-Asian Juuryou Senshuken! He hails from New York City, New York, USA, and weighs in at 355 pounds, he is ED DEAN!!! [Dean comes out to no music, the All-Asian title around his waist. He stides to the ring, his eyes totally focused on Kikuchi in the ring. Dean enters the ring, takes off the title, and throws it to the referee, then charges Kikuchi...] ER: Dean charging Kikuchi...drop toehold by the challenger drops Dean...ANKLELOCK!!!! [crowd "oooohs"] Dean is struggling...he grabs the ropes! [crowd "awwws"] Kikuchi breaks the hold! AO: Dean is too cocky tonight! He thought he could attack Kikuchi like that? Kikuchi is no rookie! [Kikuchi backs away, and Dean slowly gets to his feet. He tests his leg, then nods to Goto, who signals for the match to continue. Both men circle each other, then they move to lock up. They tie up, and Kikuchi deftly goes behind Dean, applies a waistlock, and tries to german suplex the bigger man. Dean shifts his weight, preventing Kikuchi from taking him up, then lands a stiff back elbow to Kikuchi's masked face. He attempts another one, but Kikuchi drops down, and rolls Dean up! Goto drops to count, but Dean kicks out at one. Both men get to their feet, and Dean charges, but Kikuchi catches him with a dropkick to the knee. Dean stops short, favoring his knee, and Kikuchi sends him to the mat with a swinging neckbreaker...] ER: Dean can't seem to figure Kikuchi out... AO: Kikuchi is out-wrestling Dean! He's showing Dean what true puroresu is like. So much for Dean's "Strong Style" Ha! [Dean sits up, holding his neck, and Kikuchi slaps on a rear chinlock. He grinds down on Dean, who tries to grab Kikuchi's head. Unable to do so, Dean attempts to get to his feet. Kikuchi grinds even harder, and Dean stops for a moment, then continues to rise. Slowly, Dean makes it to his feet, and Kikuchi, still clinging to the chinlock, hovers over the mat on Dean's back. Dean positions himself, then runs backward, slamming Kikuchi into the corner. Kikuchi loosens the chinlock, and Dean drives him back into the corner again, then lands a stiff back elbow to the side of Kikuchi's head. Kikuchi sags in the corner, and Dean steps back, then charges in, landing a lariat that sends Kikuchi over the top rope to the floor! The crowd "ooohs" loudly, and Dean raises his arms over his head in victory...] ER: Dean is just too strong for Kikuchi! He can't handle this monster! AO: Don't say that Eddie! Dean may be stronger, but Kikuchi is smarter! [Dean steps out onto the apron as Kikuchi gets to his feet. He runs across the apron, then leaps off to land a double axe handle, but Kikuchi is able to catch Dean, and uses his momentum to hotshot him across the railing! The crowd "ooooohs" as Dean lands throat first onto the railing, then falls to the floor, clutching his throat in pain. Kikuchi drags Dean to his feet, then sends him back to the floor with a snap suplex. Kikuchi slides back into the ring, then goes to a neutral corner, and tries to catch his breath. Goto continues the count, and Dean gets to his feet, shakes his head, then climbs up on the apron, and re-enters the ring. He motions for Kikuchi to lock up, and Kikuchi exits the corner, and moves towards Dean. Dean catches Kikuchi with a kick to the gut, then double underhooks his arms as he doubles over. He looks out at the crowd, then lifts Kikuchi up into a vertical position. The crowd "ooohs" at this sign of strength, and Dean falls forward, driving Kikuchi's face into the mat with a gourdbuster! Dean sits Kikuchi up, and slaps on a full nelson. He drags Kikuchi to his feet with the full nelson, then hits a release dragon suplex. The crowd "oooohs" again as Kikuchi lands hard on the back of his head, then lays still on the mat. Dean gets up into a crouching position, and draws his thumb across his throat...] ER: Everytime Kikuchi gets the upperhand, Dean overpowers him! Kikuchi is in serious trouble! AO: Don't say that Eddie! Kikuchi can still defeat him. Kikuchi *must* defeat him! Dean has made a mockery of the All-Asian title for too long! [Dean gets to his feet, and stands over the fallen Kikuchi. He bends down, and grabs Kikuchi by the mask. He pulls Kikuchi to his feet, and Kikuchi drives his knee into Dean's gut. Dean lets out a surprised gasp of air, and Kikuchi drives his knee into Dean's gut again. Dean doubles over, sucking wind, and Kikuchi drives his head into the mat with a snap DDT. The crowd cheers wildly, and Kikuchi sits up, shaking his head. He slowly rises to his feet, and shockingly, so does Dean! Both men stand on unsteady feet. Dean attempts a lariat, and Kikuchi ducks under it. Dean turns around, and Kikuchi catches him, then hits a leverage Exploder Suplex. The crowd cheers as Dean's head hits the mat hard. Dean sits up, his face dazed, then slumps to the mat. Kikuchi falls on top of him for a cover, and Goto drops to count. He makes it to two and a half before Dean kicks out with authority! The crowd "ooohs" as Dean sits up, and slaps the side of his head to clear it. Both men get to their feet, and Kikuchi lays into Dean with a series of European Uppercuts, sending the champion staggering back into the corner. Kikuchi drives his knee into Dean's gut repeatedly, then lands a stiff chop across his chest. The crowd "whoos", and Dean suddenly grabs Kikuchi, and forces him into the corner! Dean lands a vicious chop across Kikuchi's chest, and the crowd "oooohs" as Kikuchi flies up, then falls down in the corner....] ER: I'm telling you Akira; Kikuchi is taking on more than he can handle! Dean is just too much for him! We've got to stop this match! AO: We can't Eddie; Kikuchi made it clear that he would see this match to it's finish, win or lose! [Dean lands another chop to Kikuchi's chest, and his golden skin turns red from the impact. Dean grabs Kikuchi by the arm, and whips him across the ring, sending him slamming into the opposite corner! Dean holds up his arm, signalling for a lariat, then charges across the ring. Kikuchi gets his foot up at the last second, and Dean's face slams into his boot. Dean staggers back for a moment, and he shakes his head, then charges forward again. Kikuchi's boot again catches Dean in the face, and he staggers back one more. Kikuchi hops up onto the second rope, then he leaps off, catching Dean with an elbowsmash to the forehead. Dean drops to one knee, his eyes looking dazed again. Kikuchi lands a second elbowsmash to Dean's forehead, then Kikuchi slaps on a front facelock. The crowd starts to "ooooh", sensing the Tiger Driver. Kikuchi starts to lift Dean up; at first it looks like he won't be able to do it, then the crowd "oooohs" louder, as Dean goes vertical. Kikuchi holds him for a second, then starts to prepare to drop him down, when he slips on some of the blood on the mat from the last match. Kikuchi falls backward, and Dean lands hard on top of him. The crowd "awwws" in dissapointment...] ER: Dammit! I thought Kikuchi could have won the match there! AO: So did I Eddie. So did I. [Dean gets to his feet, shaking his head. He sees Kikuchi laying dazed on the mat, and grins. He drags Kikuchi to his feet, and looks out at the crowd. He yells out at them "You want to see the Tiger Driver? Well here it is!", then lifts Kikuchi up in the air. He holds him vertical for what seems an eternity, then drops him down into a tombstone! The crowd "ooohs" as Kikuchi's head spikes into the mat. Dean gets to his feet, and drags Kikuchi up again. He sets him up a second time, and again lifts him up into a vertical position. Dean turns to face the camera, then drops Kikuchi down again into a tombstone, Kikuchi's head bouncing off the mat from the impact...] ER: OH MY GOD! He just broke Kikuchi's neck! AO: Buddha, I hope not! [Dean looks down at the fallen Kikuchi with a look of disgust on his face, then plants one foot over his chest. He yells at Goto to count, and Goto reluctantly drops down, easily making the three count...] Nishi: The winner of this match, and STILL All-Asian Juuryou Senshuken...ED DEAN!!! [The crowd boos loudly, while the members of Chiimu Tora head to the ring, as do The Maulers. Dean celebrates with Douglas & Thomas, while the Chiimu Tora members check on Kikuchi. Dean takes the All-Asian title, and holds it over his head. Takizawa looks on with a look of utter contempt on his face. Banshee & Jet Jaguar II look like they might attack Dean, but Takizawa holds up his arm to restrain them. Dean walks over to Nishi, and grabs the microphone from his hands...] Ed Dean: I told each and everyone of you that Kikuchi would fall. I told you all that Strong Style Rules! I've defeated all of Chiimu Tora, all of your so-called heroes! There is no one in SJPW who can stand against me! [Suddenly, Mitsunori Takizawa moves towards Dean, and grabs the microphone out of his hand. Dean glares at Takizawa, and moves to strike him, but Shinji Hamada & Doug Thomas get in between them...] Mitsunori Takizawa: Forgive me Dean-san, but it seems you have a faulty memory. Yes, you have defeated *MOST* of Chiimu Tora, but you have beaten *ALL* of us. You have not faced the most cunning member of Chiimu Tora, for you have not faced me! [Dean yells out "You had your chance, and you gave it away, coward!] Mitsunori Takizawa: Yes, I gave my tournament slot to Nishida. He pleaded with me for a chance to avenge himself on you, and I granted it to him. Call me coward if you will, but the only coward I see is YOU! [crowd cheers] If you are so mighty, then why not give me a title shot next week? [Dean says "I'll be more than happy to beat your old ass to death.." when Mr. Yamashita steps out onto the runway entrance, holding a microphone...] Shinsaku Yamashita: Hold on a moment, Takizawa-sama! You gave up your right to challenge for the All-Asian title when you gave Nishida your tournament slot. I made that clear when I was named acting CEO [crowd booos] On top of that, you are nowhere near ranked in the top ten contendors for *ANY* singles title! [crowd booos louder] However, I am not a fool. The fans wish to see such a match between you two, so I will give you *ONE*, I repeat *ONE* chance to earn a title shot. Next week, Dean & The Maulers will team with Kubota & The Corporate Dragons. Against them will be you, and five partners of your choosing [Takizawa smiles, and looks at his fellow Chiimu Tora members]. No, not them. Chiimu Tora have made fools enough of themselves with all their losses to Dean. You must choose five partners who are not Chiimu Tora members. Can you find five men daring enough to stand beside you? For your sake, I hope so. Otherwise, there will be a severly handicapped main event for next week! One last stipulation: You must pin Dean to get the title shot. Any other result, and you fail. You must prove able to defeat Dean, and thereby prove you deserve this one last chance. Seven Days Takizawa-sama. Make good use of them! [Yamashita heads backstage, the crowd booing loudly behind him] ER: Wow! Takizawa has one more chance at facing Dean? The odds are stacked against him though? How can he hope to prevail? AO: I don't know Eddie. I'm still concerned about Kikuchi though! [The credits start to roll, and as the camera fades out, The crowd is chanting Takizawa's name as he helps Kikuchi out of the ring...] ____ _____ ____ __ __ /\ _`\ /\___ \/\ _`\ /\ \ __/\ \ \ \,\L\_\\/__/\ \ \ \L\ \ \ \/\ \ \ \ \/_\__ \ _\ \ \ \ ,__/\ \ \ \ \ \ \ /\ \L\ \/\ \_\ \ \ \/ \ \ \_/ \_\ \ \ `\____\ \____/\ \_\ \ `\___x___/ \/_____/\/___/ \/_/ '\/__//__/ © 1999 http://www.[name withheld].com/sjpw/
