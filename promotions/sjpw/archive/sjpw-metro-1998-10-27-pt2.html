Metro, Hour One 2/2

JA: Kurasawa slowly rising to his feet, and he looks a bit shaken up, and who could blame him, he just jumped over the top rope, spun in the air, and crashed into his opponent, and then landed on the concrete floor. SENTON BOMB! 1....! 2....! JA: KICKOUT![crowd applauds]. Murakami manages to escape, and Kurasawa up to his feet, and off the ropes...Handspring into a kneedrop! He catches Murakami right across the throat with that knee, and he covers. 1....! 2....! ER: NO! Murakami refusing to be pinnned, and he kicks out again. JA: Goto says two count only, and Murakami rolls to his knees, and staggers up to his feet. He is still favoring his arm as Kurasawa rises. ER: OH! He didn't need his arm for that stiff Crescent Kick to the jaw of Kurasawa! Murakami quickly climbs to the second rope. Diving Headbutt connects! JA: Murakami rolls to a sitting position, and shakes his head as it looks like his bell was rung on that move. ER: He sacrificed his body to do damage to his opponent, much like Kurasawa did early. Kurasawa rolls to his feet, and Murakami hooks him for a Belly-to-back, but Kurasawa counters with quick back elbows, and now shoots off the ropes... JA: Right into a Tilt-A-Whirl Backbreaker from Murakami![crowd "ohhhs" and then applauds] Murakami now pulls Kurasawa up...He gets him in a standing headscissors, and hit looks like he is going for his Jumping Powerbomb.[crowd cheers] ER: Kurasawa is fighting the move...He blocks it again, and Murakami releases him, and runs back to the ropes...Jumping High Kick! JA: He attempts the Powerbomb again...Again Kurasawa blocks it. Murakami releases and jumps up...Enziguri![crowd applauds] ER: Murkami to his feet, and now he signals for the for the Divine Wind! [crowd cheers] JA: He pulls Kurasawa up, and grabs him in a front facelock. He drags him to the corner, and hops up to the second rope. ER: Here it comes...Swinging DDT...NO! Kurasawa is able to counter by shoving Murakami off, and Murakami stumbles back. Kurasawa explodes at him with a running clothesline, but Murakami ducks it. Murakami with a waistlock...German Suplex attempted, but Kurasawa counters once again with back elbows, and Murakami stumbles back. Kurasawa turns. JA: Murakami suddenly spins into a reverse rolling elbow...Oh! ER: Kurasawa got his arms up in defense, and Murakami drops to his knees in pain, cradling the arm Kurasawa worked over earlier in the match. Kurasawa pulls him up, and delivers a stiff forearm to the back of the head, and jumps up to the second rope, he hooks Murakami in a reverse facelock... JA: SWINGING REVERSE DDT!![crowd "ohhhhhs"] Incredible move from Kurasawa, and he has Murakami laid out. 1....! 2....! 3.... ER: NO! Goto points to Murakami's foot draped over the bottom rope.[crowd applauds] JA: Kurasawa up in the face of Goto, screaming at him, and Matsuo is up on the apron pleading his case. ER: Murakami is still down, and holding the back of his head, and he is hurting. JA: Kurasawa shoves Goto aside, and goes out to the apron. Murakami staggers to his feet, and Kurasawa leaps up...SPRINGBOARD HURACA....JUMPING POWERBOMB![crowd "ohhhhs"] ER: Great counter move by Murakami, as he drives Kurasawa into the canvas, and now stands, and he is fired up. JA: He signals for the Divine Wind yet again, and he pulls a limp Kurasawa to his feet, and has him hooked. He leaps off...Again Kurasawa shoves him off! ER: He knows how to escape his own move, as he should. Murakami charges in with a lariat...KURASAWA COUNTERS INTO THE FUJIWARA ARMBAR![crowd "ohhhs"] JA: Murakami frantically scrambles to the ropes, as Kurasawa was going to hyper extend the elbow, or dislocate his shoulder, or both. ER: Goto forcably pulls Kurasawa off of Murakami, showing the devastating reputation of that submsission hold. JA: Kurasawa is up, and he pulls Murakami up, he fires in with repeated forearm shots to the head, and he has Murakami staggered. ER: I think all the fight has gone out of the "Warbird". JA: He has taken a tremendous beating in this match and he came close, but Kurasawa now up to the second rope, and he looks to be ready for his version of the swinging DDT that he calls the Breath of Fire. ER: He leaps off....Murakami spins with him, and somehow stays on his feet, and now sends Kurasawa over...NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX!!! JA: How the hell did he do that?!!! 1....! 2....! 3...!!! [The bell rings and the fans cheer and applaud] Nishi: The winner of this match advancing in the tournament.... YUKIO "THE WARBIRD" MURAKAMI!!![fans applaud again as Goto raises the uninjured arm of the battle weary Murakami] JA: A great match and a great win for Murakami who pulled that Northern Lights Suplex out of his as... ER: Ahem, Johnnie... JA: Oh, sorry about that. He pulled that move out of no where. ER: Murakami advances in the tournament despite the efforts of Kurasawa, a great showing from both men. JA: So now Murakami faces the winner of our next match between Jet Jaguar II & Kabuki Kid. A shot of Kabuki Kid hitting a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker on Jet Jaguar II cuts to a shot of Tokugawa powerslaming Kabuki Kid down onto Jaguar's back. The camera zooms into a close-up on Jaguar. His mouth is agape with pain as his eyes are shut tight, his silver and black mask unable to hide the pain. Cut to a shot of Kabuki Kid hitting a snap suplex on the floor on Kabuki Kid. Cut to a shot of Jaguar crawling on the floor, pulling himself up to the apron, and then staggering painfully to the top rope. The clip ends as Jaguar II leaps off connecting with a modified flying legdrop as Banshee sends Kabuki Kid crashing to the mat with a flapjack. The scene fades out. Fade in on Jet Jaguar II standing in front of the SJPW banner. He is wearing his silver and black tights, and distincitive silver and black mask. He holds his fist in front of his chest, giving the Chiimu Tora sign.) Jaguar: "Last week on Metro, the Diamyo, Kabuki Kid and Kensei Tokugawa made me a target. They saw me as the weak link in the Hiryuu Batsu team. They attacked me viciously, and yes, I did feel great pain. No matter how hard they tried, though, they could not keep me down. I drew upon the strength of my spirit. The hours of training. The guidance and support of Banshee, Hamada, and all the memebers of Chiimu Tora. I knew I could not let my partner down. I had to fight the pain, and get back in the ring. In the end, Hiryuu Batsu stood victorious. Now, comes a new battle with the Diamyo. With Kabuki Kid." (Jaguar lets out a long breath and then continues.) Jaguar: "Kabuki Kid, your talent and ability are tremendous. However, you have allowed yourself to sink to the depths to gain victory. You had to use dishonorable distration from the outside in your match against Banshee. That has lead you to me. This match is unlike our tag match. This time, we can only rely on our own skills. No help will be provided. No one there to save us from defeat. It is a great challange to face someone with your ability Kabuki Kid. However, I will come into this match with the fire of youth. Do not take my calm tones as a sign of weakness or timidness. I have been training with the great warriors, and the spirit of the battle, the spirit of the Tiger roars within me." (Jaguar raises his right fist, and hammers it agaisnt his chest.) Jaguar: "Team Tiger does not have a weaklink Kabuki-san. You will find this out for yourself when we meet each other in the ring. Chiimu Tora!" (Jaguar raises his fist again giving the Team Tiger sign, his eyes burning with intensity as the scene fades to black.) ER: Jaguar II is much more intense than Shoji was. JA: Shoji was like a wide-eyed youth, surrounded by those he idolized. Jaguar II is a man on a tear! He beat his first round opponent, and along with Banshee beat The Ronin, one of the best tag teams going. That says alot for this kid. He has that killer instinct Shoji lacked at the time. ER: Whatever it is, it's working! But you’re right in that The Kabuki Kid is the more accomplished of the two. He’s got the pin as the referee counts... ...1... ...2... ...shoulder up!! [crowd applauds] ER: Both men back on their feet and Kabuki whips Jaguar into the ropes and flings him up into the air on the rebound with a back bodydrop. JA: He better not underestimate the rookie as Jet Jaguar II can surprise you when you least expect it. But the kid is taking a beating as Kabuki nails him with a European uppercut and then wraps him up in his own variation of an abdominal stretch. Jaguar is valiantly trying to break the hold and... he manages to reach the ropes. ER: The referee forces Kabuki to release the rookie but he has to yell at him a few times to get him to do it. Kabuki Kid looking for another title for his collection as he quickly climbs up onto the top turnbuckle... oh! Kabuki tried for a flying dropkick off the top rope but Jet Jaguar II countered it with a dropkick of his own that caught him in mid-air! [crowd "oooohs", then applauds] JA: What a collision! Kabuki looks seriously hurt as he took an incredible shot to the chest from the young Jet Jaguar II. Jaguar pulls him to his feet and smacks him with another Shotay palm strike. He then scoops Kabuki up and plants him on the mat with a bodyslam. ER: Jet Jaguar II definitely has the advantage as he slaps on a side headlock. Kabuki Kid is trapped and... nice move! Jaguar released the headlock and pivoted around to apply a punishing armbar submission hold! JA: Excellent technique from the young man and you can tell he’s been well-schooled by Shoji Suzuki. Hey! [crowd "oooohs"] JA: The youngster is definitely thinking in there as he hops up onto the second rope in the corner... and takes Kabuki down with a dropkick from the second turnbuckle. Jaguar is the first to recover and he pulls up a dazed Kabuki... and hits a tiger suplex! Jaguar runs into the ropes as Kabuki struggles to his feet. Flying cross body press... but Kabuki Kid ducks out of the way! Jaguar sits up in pain and Kabuki continues with his follow-through as he performs a front flip over top of Jaguar, snapping his neck forward as he flies overhead. JA: An incredible combination of moves and it’s certainly had an impact on the youngster! ER: But now Kabuki is focusing on Jaguar’s back as he catches him between the shoulder blades with a vicious kick! Jet Jaguar II is sitting on the mat in considerable pain as The Kabuki Kid drives his knee into his spine, trying to wear him down. A small "TORA" chant is being started amongst the crowd as Kabuki pulls Jaguar up... BACKBREAKER! JA: Kabuki’s going for the pin after punishing Jaguar’s back and here’s the referee’s count... ...1... ...kickout!! [crowd applauds] ER: Kabuki Kid is arguing with the referee as he drags Jet Jaguar II upright. He stuns him with a headbutt and then takes him off his feet with a legsweep. Jaguar’s back up quickly but Kabuki picks him up in... a bearhug? No! Kid falls backward, executing a guillotine drop right across the top rope! JA: That really hurt Jet Jaguar II as he’s rolling around on the ground, clutching his throat. Kabuki hits a kneedrop and now he’s headed up top. He’s perched up on the top turnbuckle and Jet Jaguar II is lying helpless on the mat. Here’s The Kamikaze Drop... OH! [crowd "oooohs"] ER: He missed it! Jet Jaguar II managed to roll out of the way at the last possible second! JA: And yet another reversal in this back and forth match as Kabuki struggles to his feet, clutching his injured head. Jaguar doesn’t give him any time to recover as he heaves him over with an armdrag takedown. Kabuki’s back up quickly but a standing dropkick knocks him down once more. ER: After nearly being pinned and taking a lot of damage to his back, Jet Jaguar II is coming back with a vengeance! And speaking of vengeance, here comes The Banshee! [The Banshee comes down to the ring and stands in Jet Jaguar II’s corner, slapping his hand on the ring apron to encourage his tag team partner.] JA: The Kabuki Kid achieved a rather dubious victory over The Banshee just previously to advance in this tournament and he’s likely here to make sure history doesn’t repeat itself. ER: Or perhaps for the first reason you mentioned, vengeance? JA: Could be. Meanwhile, Jet Jaguar II is really laying into The Kabuki Kid with a series of knife-edge chops. ER: A brilliant counter-move if not the most legitimate. Jaguar’s momentum is derailed as Kabuki gets a moment to recover. He notices The Banshee at ringside now and takes a moment to yell at him. JA: A lot of hard-hitting action as Kabuki sends Jaguar into the ropes with an Irish whip... HURRICANRANA! ER: Incredible move... but look! Kabuki is not going for a pin even though Jaguar is dazed! He’s going over to the edge of the ring and is yelling at The Banshee!! But The Kabuki Kid is committing a cardinal error here as he’s not paying attention to Jet Jaguar II who’s getting lots of time to recover. ER: Look at this!
