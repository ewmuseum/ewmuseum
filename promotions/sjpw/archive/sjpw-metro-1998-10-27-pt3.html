Metro, Hour Two 1/2

27 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The house lights begin to darken. Two large explosions burst at the entrance way. A balding man enters through the curtain and heads to the ring as the 2001 theme begins to blare over the loud speakers. He is carrying a large case with a cloth over it. The man grabs the microphone and enters the ring] MAN: Ladies and Gentlemen. Super Japan Pro Wrestling. My name is Dominick Barone. I am the president of the Unified Wrestling Federation Champion Tournament. For the past few weeks, I have been traveling the globe. Speaking in any wrestling venue that would listen to me. The man removes the cover to reveal a glass case. Inside the case is the most ornate trophy you have ever seen. BARONE: Wrestlers of the SJPW... The UWFCT is recruiting wrestlers to compete in an intra-federation tournament to crown the ultimate champion. Who among your ranks is good enough to enter this battle??? How does the SJPW stand against other federations throughout the world??? If you dare, enter the tournament and battle for the right to hold this prestigeous trophy. [Dominick Barone pulls the Championship Trophy out of the case and holds it in the air... The crowd is in awe and begins to chant... SJPW...SJPW...SJPW...] BARONE: Ladies and gentlemen... Thank you for your time. Up next, we have "Young Dragon" Hideoh Kanemera in action against Haruki Shiomi. What do you think of Kanemera Eddie? ER: He looked good in his first match, even if he came up on the losing end. I've heard rumors that The Daiymo have been scouting him of late. JA: Really? I didn't know that..... __ ___ _____________________________________________________ / |/ /__ / /________ / / /|_/ / -_) __/ __/ _ \ Hideoh Kanemera vs Haruki Shiomi /_/ /_/\__/\__/_/ \___/_______________________________________/ The "Young Dragon" surprised Shiomi with a somersault plancha to the floor before the bell, which got the crowd pumped. Kanemera then hit a neckbreaker, hopped up on the apron, and nailed Shiomi with an Asai moonsault. Kabuki Kid & Kensei Tokugawa came out to the aisle, and rooted Kanemera on as he russian legsweeped Shiomi on the floor, went to the top rope, and hit his patented Skytwister Press to the floor. The crowd was on their feet applauding as Kanemera rolled Shiomi into the ring, and covered him for the three count. Winner: Hideoh Kanemera by pinfall. ER: Wow. He destroyed Shiomi; the poor guy never even got into the ring until he was pinned! JA: I also saw The Ronin in the aisle. I guess that rumor has been proven true! ER: I'll say. I'm excited about our next match though. The debut of Heaven & Hell, a team with alot of credentials! JA: I'll say. These two men are dangerous... __ ___ _____________________________________________________ / |/ /__ / /________ / / /|_/ / -_) __/ __/ _ \ Heaven & Hell vs Fujimoto Brothers /_/ /_/\__/\__/_/ \___/_______________________________________/ White Angel started things off with Ryu, catching the younger Fujimoto with a dropkick. Ryu staggered back to the ropes, and tagged his brother, came off, and attempted a clothesline. White Angel ducked, and Takashi slingshot into the ring, nailing Angel with a bulldog as he stood up. Takasahi hit a scoop slam and an elbow drop, then covered for a one count. Angel came back with an enzugiri, then tagged in Soul Taker. Taker nailed Takashi with two backbreakers, then a side suplex. He tagged in Angel, who went to the top rope. Taker nailed Takashi with a powerbomb, while Angel hit an elbowdrop off the top rope. Taker dropkicked Ryu off the apron, while Angel nailed Takashi with a brainbuster, then hit his patented Angelsault for the win. Winners: Heaven & Hell by pinfall. ER: This team is awesome. I think they're the sleepers of the Marano tournament. JA: I'll say. They tore the Fujimoto Brothers apart in short order! Our next match should be pretty interesting. Angel Taboo, a man who came up short against Yukio Murakami, faces Se-hong Kwan in a Battle Arts match. ER: Kwan is a master of Tae Kwon Do, and is a deadly man. Taboo may have gotten in over his head... __ ___ _____________________________________________________ / |/ /__ / /________ Battle Arts Match: / / /|_/ / -_) __/ __/ _ \ Angel Taboo vs Se-hong Kwan /_/ /_/\__/\__/_/ \___/_______________________________________/ Kwan started off strong, nailing Taboo with a series of kicks to his ribs, then sent him to the mat with a jumping spinning roundhouse kick. Kwan went to the top rope, and nailed Taboo as he got to his feet with a thrust kick off the top, and covered for a two count. Kwan went for a whip to the ropes, but Taboo reversed, and hit a spinning heel kick of his own. Kwan doubled over, and Taboo hit an inverted atomic drop, went to the ropes, and sent Kwan to the mat with a flying shoulderblock. Taboo hit a rib breaker, then rolled Kwan outside the ring. Taboo went to the top rope, and waited for Kwan to rise. When he got to his feet, Taboo came off with a flying clothesline that sent Kwan over the railing into the crowd. Taboo went to the apron, and Asai moonsaulted over the railing onto Kwan in the crowd, and the fans went nuts. Taboo rammed Kwan's head into the railing, threw him over the railing, then rolled him into the ring. Taboo went for a moonsault, but Kwan rolled out of the way. Kwan layed into Taboo with some thrusts with his fist to Taboo's gut, then took him to the mat with a legsweep. Kwan leapt for a stomp, but Taboo rolled out of the way, did a kip-up, and attempted a lariat. Kwan ducked it, and attempted a thrust kick, but Taboo caught his leg, spun him in a 360, then hoisted him onto his shoulders, and drove him into the mat with the Death Valley Driver for the win. Winner: Angel Taboo by pinfall. JA: Angel Taboo really impressed me there. He seems to have decided to move into this division after his loss in the Welterweight division. ER: After his showing tonight, he could become a top contendor for the title soon. Takada needs to keep an eye on this man! JA: Our next match is one we've both been waiting for. Kagemusha & Shishi "Lion" Fujiwara have been heading for a confrontation since the first Tsunami. Now they meet one on one. This should prove to be an interesting match. ER: I'll say. Chiimu Tora vs Daiymo. This is one for the history books! __ ___ _____________________________________________________ / |/ /__ / /________ Grudge Match: / / /|_/ / -_) __/ __/ _ \ Shishi Fujiwara vs Kagemusha /_/ /_/\__/\__/_/ \___/_______________________________________/ [The referee pats both wrestlers down then gives them their final instructions. Kagemusha stares wordlessly at Fujiwara who still has a smile on his face. The referee then calls for the bell.] JA: Here we go! Fujiwara and Kagemusha circle each other like hungry lions. The two men lock up. Fujiwara manuevers Kagemusha into a side-headlock. Kagemusha shoves Fujiwara to the ropes, Fujiwara bounces off the ropes headlong towards Kagemusha who buckles him over with a vicious side kick to the stomach! Kagemusha then quickly takes a hold of Fujiwara and performs a devastating gutwrench suplex on the young man, sending him crashing to the mat! Fujiwara grabs his back in pain. ER: But Kagemusha is not finished. Moving rapidly, he delivers a legdrop across Fujiwara’s neck. He then picks up the dazed Fujiwara, locks his arms around Fujiwara’s waist and with a sudden flash of movement drops Fujiwara down on the mat in a Doctor Bomb! JA: This could be it! Kagemusha with a cover... ...1... ...2... ER: But Fujiwara somehow finds the energy to kick out! Kagemusha, not letting up on his pace, picks Fujiwara up, throws him to ropes, then catches him with an Asai moonsault off the top rope! Fujiwara drops to the mat, his breath knocked out of him. JA: It seems the brutality and mercilessness of Kagemusha has completely caught the young Lion off-guard. ER: I agree. Kagemusha back to the offensive. He levels a series of European uppercuts, then drops to his knee and punches Fujiwara in the gut. Fujiwara doubles over and Kagemusha grabs Fujiwara’s neck and performs a swinging neckbreaker on him! Fujiwara lies motionless on the mat. JA: But why isn’t Kagemusha going for the pin? After such a professional start, he is now walking around Fujiwara, staring at him disgustedly. [The audience begins to boo lustily.] ER: Kagemusha ignores the audience. He drops down on Fujiwara for the pin... ...1... ...2... ...3... JA: Wait! Kagemusha picks Fujiwara up from the mat! He just saved Fujiwara from a loss there! ER: This is disgusting Jonnie! Kagemusha is not satisfied with defeating Fujiwara. He must also humiliate the young man. JA: Kagemusha with a powerful chop across the young man’s chest. And another. Then an Irish whip to the ropes...Kagemusha with a dropkick...but Fujiwara somehow finds the sense to grab onto the ropes! Kagemusha crashes down to the mat, missing his target completely! But Kagemusha is not too badly hurt. He quickly hops to his feet, runs towards the exhausted Fujiwara and clotheslines him out of the ring! ER: Fujiwara hit the floor hard! He is writhing on the padding of the floor, holding his arm. JA: Kagemusha slowly walks to the corner turnbuckle, climbs on to the top rope. He measures Fujiwara, who is sitting on the mat, holding his arm. He then leaps into the air and comes down on Fujiwara with a devastating splash across the body! Fujiwara is laid out on the mat. Kagemusha, also dazed from his manuever, slowly stands up. He delivers a kick to Fujiwara’s head. Then picks him up and throws him back into the ring. ER: This young wrestler cannot possibly continue.
