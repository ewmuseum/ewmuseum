SJPW House Show — SAPPORO BREWERIES Factory Hall (7/5/04)

Jul 7, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
July 5th, 2004 SAPPORO BREWERIES Factory Hall: 972 fans Super Japan kicked off it's Junior Struggle! tour in Hokkaido. It was close to a sell out at the Factory Hall, which saw the start of the Junior Struggle! tournament: 1) Junior Struggle! Block A: Toramusha def Jet Jaguar III (9:45) with the Tiger Suplex '85. 2) Junior Struggle! Block B: "Kyoto Tiger" Takayoshi Asakura def Yatoro Hori (8:04) with the Firebird Splash. 3) Noboru "Raging Bull" Nishida & MAMMOTH Kagawa def Tsutomu Kobayashi & Botan Nakatoni (13:19) after a double Nodowa Otashi on Kobayashi 4) Junior Struggle! Block A: "Firefly" Horo Atashi def Fanta Boy (10:27) with a moonsault. 5) Junior Struggle! Block B: Super Bakemono def Tsunami (20:09) after a Bakemono Bomb. - Toruamusha surprised everyone by upsetting Jaguar III to score the first win in Block A. Both men shook hands before and after the match. - Asakura rebounded from his loss to Kawasaki at Zenmen Sensou, defeating Yatoro Hori in short fashion with his patented Firebird Splash - A new pairing from Team Tiger, Kagawa & Nishida defeated The Crush Crew like they did Toroyama, with a double chokelsam. After the match, Nishida told the crowd they'd repeat this victory in American when they face the Crush Crew at the debut show for Pro Wrestling Glory. - Horo Atashi scored his first Junior Struggle! win, defeating the flamboyant Fanta Boy in an exciting match. - In what some considered a major upset, Super Bakemono, reeling from his loss to Lanther at Zenmen Sensou, defeated Tsunami, who was also reeling from a loss to "All Star" Joey Darren. Both men pulled out all the stops, exchanging stiff chops and kicks, as well as going to the air. The end came after Tsunami went for his Tsunami Driver, which Bakemono was able to counter with a mule kick, then hitting a Michinoku Driver for a two count. Bakemono went to the top, hit the Bakemono Bomb, and was able to secure the win over Tsunami.
