It's like Hogan without Savage, Nash without Hall, Flair without Arn (or Sting), Tajiri without Torrie Wilson, The Rock without The people, Mick Foley without Mr. Socko...you get the picture! Messiah? Not me! Maybe Jonnie Adonis (who claimed to be the hardcore Messiah), but not me. Now Super Tengu, he was a messiah. > The last good Puro style feds I was in were JPA and TOP. TOP was run > by [name withheld] and featured Tetsuo Matsumoto, Shoji Suzuki, Ed Dean and > Kazuya Asakino Is Adam still around? I'd want to get in touch with him too. Ahh, all those wonderful names; some of the best SJPW ever had. > Gerry do you know how long I've waited for you to come back ot > e-wrestling? No, but feed my fragile ego, and tell me! :) > You can count Tetsuo/Super Tengu and Kazuya Asakino in. Do search in > Google Groups for Super Tengu and check out my old "History of Super > Tengu" articles plus my current JIWWA material. I will do that. > 2 feds were always tops in my book... IIWF, which I could never get > into since the wait list was the size of Orson Welles... and SJPW! I'd actually throw in NLWP (Ian was my main man for giving Soultaker a home), EMWC & SCRA were great feds, and I'm pleased to see IWF/WOW is still going; SJPW had a great working relationship with them for a short time. That fed was great. CWO (A sim fed) worked with SJPW, as did AAPW & Wild West Wrestling (Corey and Derek were great fedheads, even if unknown here on rspwf). Fletch and G-pro, Adam's feds. There was another fed, Silver City or something ([name withheld] would remember, since he kicked my ass there enough when I teamed with Deathbringer). HCW (the one I was in) was great too. It was the first efed I was in for any period of time, and my good buddy "Too Excess" was there (formerly El Perfecto). Wow, alot of old memories. I don't remember everyone and every fed, but it was all cool. Heck, I even remember Sabu424! Is that nutjob still around? > Even if it is just for one show SJPW being back will be great... I've > been trying to do a Tetsuo retirment match and now... well I can think > of no place better t do it than SJPW! I just can't say if I can commit to a full time fed. I just have a lowly laptop right now; I have a new motherboard with my old HD hooked up, so all my old files are intact, but no net access for that one :( A reunion show would be neat, then I can decided what to do from there. I had a bad habit of taking on too much in the way of handlers, and not giving enough of them a chance to shine. Watching the Alliance/WWF feud woke me up to the fact that it's too easy to squander good talent. If I do go back to an efed, it will probably be with HOW, and keep the roster *small*; I'm getting too old to run a fed the size SJPW got to be! :) > To know Tengu is to love tengu and loving Tengu is only natural! Tengu was the man. He was to SJPW what the Rock and Austin are to WWF; a wrestler with the far from typical attitude, but the fans just love the guy :)
