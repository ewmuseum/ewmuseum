SJPW House Show — Tokyo FM Hall (6/16/04)

Jun 17, 2004, 12:00:00 AM

June 16th, 2004 Tokyo FM Hall: 275 fans 1) Dynamite Boy & Eiji Kid Shimura def Yatoro Hori & Kenichi Kubota (10:34) after a top rope headbutt by Dynamite Boy on Hori. 2) Jet Jaguar III def Toramusha (9:17) after the Jaguar Special. 3) Dragon Mask & Jiraiya def The Crush Crew (13:11) after Kobayashi submitted to Dragon Mask's Dragon Sleeper. 4) El Santo Azteca def Se-hong Kwan (8:49) by making him submit to the Azteca Speciale. 5) "All Star" Joey Darren & "Living Legend" Tommy Fury def Tsunami & El Gato Loco (19:22) after Darren pinned Tsunami following The Falling Star DDT. - The Nagasaki Bulldogs took a big win by defeating the new Tandem of former SMASH Wrestling stars. The Bulldogs were on fire, using alot of double team moves on the two Gunryo competitors. After the match, they told the crowd that they would prevail over the Visual Spirits in their special tag team challenge match. - Jet Jaguar III, who has been very quite of late, had a great match with rookie Toramusha. The two men had a faced paced match, and shook hands before and after. There is talk that the two will form a tag team to enter the GPH Tag Team League after Zenmen Sensou. - The war between Team Tiger & The Disciples of Despair continued, with the rookie Dragon Mask forcing Kobayashi to tap out to his own finisher! Afterwards, Kobayashi swore he'd teach the rookie a lesson for that offence - El Santo Azteca scored another victory on his road to facing Despair. Se-hong Kwan, a member of the Gunryo division, took it to Azteca early, pummeling him with some vicious kicks. Azteca finally caught his leg, and hit a dragonscrew legwhipe. He kept up the offence on his leg, and though Kwan made a short comeback with a series of palm strikes, Azteca was able to get him down, and apply the Azteca Speciale to make him tap. out. - The rivalry between Joey Darren & Tsunami continues to heat up as Darren and Fury (a former ICW) star took on Tsunami & SMLL trios champ El Gato Loco. Loco had Darren in a few near pinfalls early on, even hitting the GT Martinette (Fire Thunder Driver) on Darren for a two and three quarters count. Darren was able to stun EGL with an enzugiri, and tag in Fury, who pummeled EGL with some stiff kicks. EGL was able to counter an irish whip, and hit a segradora that sent Fury to the floor. He tagged in Tsunami, then did a Tope Suicida on Fury outside the ring. Tsunami brought Fury back in the ring, and hit a few impact moves, but Fury refused to be pinned. He was able to catch Tsunami as he went for a sunset flip, and hit a sidewalk slam. He tagged in Darren, and the two hit a nice combination suplex/crossbody block for a two count. Darren and Tsunami then went on a tear, trading chops before Darren caught Tsunami with a dropkick, then took him over with a hurricanrana. Darren was then able to hit the Falling Star, pinning the IICW champion. After the match, he made motions of having the belt around his waist, which didn't sit well with Tsunami.
