SJPW Card Archive

Tsunami, Part Four

8 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
AO: Tokugawa now going up on the apron. He stands there, waiting. Diablo starts to rise... Goto: Five! TK: Tokugawa leaps off the apron, slamming into Diablo with a double axe handle that knocks him into the railing! Tokugawa now raining punches on Diablo! Goto: Six! AO: Tokugawa grabs Diablo by the back of the head, and rams his head into the ring apron. He rolls Diablo into the ring, and jumps up on the apron... Goto: Seven! TK: Tokugawa is going to the top rope; I don't think he's ever done this before! He looks a little lost up there. He starts to lose his balance, but is able to keep himself steady. He crouches low, waiting for Diablo... AO: Diablo starts to get to his feet. He looks dazed! He starts to stagger around the ring, looking for Tokugawa... TK: Diablo turns towards Tokugawa, who leaps...Flying Shoulderblock! [crowd applauds] Diablo went down hard! Tokugawa lifts Diablo up, and attempts an irish whip, but Diablo reverses...spinwheel kick by Diablo sends Tokugawa to the mat! AO: Diablo goes to the ropes, and hits a snap legdrop across Tokugawa's chest. He runs to the ropes again, and lands on him with a senton splash. Diablo hooks the leg... ...1... ...2... ...Kickout! [crowd applauds] TK: Diablo drags Tokugawa to his feet, sets him up, then takes him down with a russian legsweep. Diablo runs to the corner, stepladders to the top rope, and comes off with a moonsau-Tokugawa got his knees up! [crowd "ooohs"] AO: Tokugawa pulled that out of nowhere! He rolls to the ropes, and uses them to pull himself up. He leans over, watching. Diablo starts to rise, and Tokugawa charges, nailing him with a running lariat. He scoops Diablo up, and drops forward into a falling powerslam. TK: Tokugawa gets back to his feet, dragging Diablo up with him. He whips Diablo to the ropes, and nails him with a big boot to the face as he comes off. Diablo goes down hard, and Tokugawa again goes to the top rope! What is he trying to prove? AO: I don't know! He's almost up top, but Diablo is on his feet, and he runs to the ropes, and Tokugawa crotches himself! [crowd "oooohs"] Diablo runs to the corner, and catches Tokugawa with a dropkick to the face! [crowd "ooohs"] TK: Diablo climbs up the corner, and stands Tokugawa up. He starts to set him up for a superplex. but Tokugawa blocks it, and hammers Diablo's back with his forearm. He sticks Diablo's head between his legs....TOP ROPE POWERBOMB!!! [crowd "oooohs", then applauds] AO: What a move! Diablo looks unconcious! Tokugawa gets to his feet, and heads to the top rope again! What is he doing? He gets to the top, and Diablo is still lying there. He steadies himself... GUILLOTINE LEGDROP! [crowd "ooooohs"] Tokugawa with the cover... ...1... ...2... ...3... **ding** **ding** **ding** Nishi: The winner of this match..."THE SAMURAI" KENSEI TOKUGAWA!!! [crowd applauds] TK: A tremendous victory for Kensei Tokugawa. He used his size and strength advantage to defeat the smaller, quicker Diablo. AO: I think we're seeing a new Tokugawa tonight. He's out to prove that he's more than just Kabuki Kid's partner. Tonight he showed everyone why they are equals! ______ ___________________________________ /_ __/__ __ _____ ___ ___ _ (_)Juuryou tournament, Round One: / / / (_-</ // / _ \/ _ `/ ' \/ / Super Tengu vs Jensei Tenru /_/ /___/\_,_/_//_/\_,_/_/_/_/_/________________________________/ TK: Well, our main event should certainly be a classic, Akira. Super Tengu is set to take on Jensei Tenru, with "Japanese Superstar" Jinsei Tenru as the special referee. AO: At least that's what we're assuming. We've gotten mixed reports about whether Tengu is actually in the arena or not. Personally I can't see Tengu missing this match, after all the words that have passed between him and Jensei Tenru. In many ways it seems like the same way the feud with Tengu and Hamada started, but this time it's actually more personal in a way. Both men's egos are so huge, I'm not sure they'll be able to ever co-exist in SJPW. In fact,.... TK: Hold that thought, Akira.. something's happening in the ring. [In the ring, the Nishi Toju has taken the house mic and is unfolding a letter. He reads it over, shakes his head...] Nishi: Ladies and gentlemen, the special referee for this match, "Japanese Superstar" Jinsei Tenru has been unavoidably detained and therefore will not be able to fulfill his duties here tonight... [A slight murmur runs through the crowd.] Nishi: Therefore , the official for this match whill be SJPW senior referee, Owada Botan. [Owada Botan comes down the aisle, and enters the ring.] TK: So despite the rumors, it seems that it's Jinsei Tenru who is not here tonight and not Super Tengu. AO: So it seems, Kikuchi-san.. so it seems. Nishi: Ladies and gentlemen, this next bout is the main event for the evening! It is scheduled for one fall, with a thirty minute time limit. Introducing first... ["Kaimatafhi" by Sepultura begins to play over the arena sound system as a vocal part of the crowd begin to chant "TENRU"] Nishi: He hails from Tokyo, Japan... weighing 230 pounds... this is JEN-SEI TEN-RU! [Tenru is a Japanese male in mis early twenties. He has black hair down a pit past his shoulders with midlength sideburns. He's well built, but not overly muscular. He slowly makes his way down the aisle, followed closely by Hakiro Matuoko. Tenru looks at the cameras as he comes down the aisle, pointing to the fans and shouting, "Do you hear those voices? They are saying TENRU!"] AO: Tenru has developed a bit of a core following in his short time here, Kikuchi-san. In many ways, he's earning his following the same way that Super Tengu slowly developed his own fan following in his early days here. TK: Yet another reason why these two men can't co-exist. Each man's popularity takes fans from the other. Both men claim to be the "God of Professional Wrestling" in SJPW, and neither man is going to take a backseat to the other. AO: Silly really. Everyone knows that I'M the real God of Wrestling. [Kikuchi casts a withering glare at Akira, who merely grins.] Nishi: And his opponent.... [Sepultura is replaced by "White Reflection" by Two Mix, and is rewarded by a large heel pop from the crowd.] Nishi: .... hailing from Kobe, Japan... he weighs in at 221 pounds... this is... SU-PER TEN-GU! [Tengu makes his way down to the ring wearing a black singlet with black long tights. Everything is trimmed with gold lame lining. On his chest is an emblem of a burning phoenix. Tengu wears black ring boots with red laces. to the ring he wears a black cape adorned with black feathers and a mask shaped like a raven's head over his main wrestling mask. Tengu slowly emerges on the runway. Ignoring the fans he advances tio the ring, his arms hidden under his cape. Finally he climbs to the ring apron and raises his arms. In a sudden jump he walks the top rope strand before backflipping into the ring, arms raised in the air.] TK: Akira... this isn't right... AO: I know ... I know... and... wait a moment... that's Jumbo Kobayashi and Shinya Misawa coming down the aisle. TK: The CEO of SJPW and his head of security are making their way into the ring and Kobayashi is taking the microphone. This should be interesting. [Camera cuts back to the ring, where Kobayashi has the microphone. He points an accusing finger at Super Tengu as he begins to talk.] JK: I don't know what you are trying to do, but it's clear that you are NOT Super Tengu. Do you think I'm stupid? Do you think I'm senile? You're at least... what... 6'5.. 6'6.. I can tell that just from looking at you. Super Tengu is 6'2! Do you think I'm blind? Did you suddenly have a growth spurt overnight? Tengu weighs about 220... you look at least 50 pounds heavier. [Tengu begins to protest but Kobayashi cuts him off immediately] JK: I am DEMANDING that you take off the mask, RIGHT NOW... or Misawa will do it for you! [Tengu begins to protest again, but Misawa steps forward to remove the mask for him. Tengu backs off and stomps his foot on the mat in anger, but removes the mask to reveal...] TK: It's Jinsei Tenru! He tried to switch places with Super Tengu! [The crowd gives a mixed pop as Tenru's identity is revealed, and the pop subsides into a buzz as the fans talk amongst themselves.] AO: How did he hope to pull that off? A blind man could tell that he wasn't Super Tengu, as Kobayashi-san rightly pointed out. More to the point now, where IS Tengu? JK: Jinsei Tenru, I'll deal with you later. Right now I want Super Tengu out here IMMEDIATELY. If he's not in the ring by the time I count to ten, I will suspend him INDEFINITELY! One.... Two... Three... TK: I can't recall a time when I've seen Kobayashi as angry as he is right now. I wouldn't want to be in Tengu's place right now. He's definitely made an enemy of SJPW's CEO. [In the ring, Kobabayashi continues the count, and as he reaches 7, the real Super Tengu strides towards the ring. He looks up at Kobayashi and shakes an angry fist at him, and then climbs into the ring, facing Kobayashi face to face.] JK: Tengu, I don't know what you hoped to gain by this act of deception, and I'm not sure what Jinsei Tenru thought to gain by it. I do know, however, that you, Tengu, have been spreading wild rumors to PN Magazine. You've been saying that SJPW will not allow you to compete in the heavyweight division, but you seem to conveniently forget that you are in the heavyweight title tournament! Well then, since that's the way you want things, Tenru-san, you WILL compete in this match, and since Jinsei wants to get involved, HE can be in this match too, but it will NOT be a heavyweight tournament match! [The crowd seems a little bit upset at this news, but it's offset by the excitement of having the three-way match. Tengu begins to argue with Kobabayashi, but Jumbo merely ignores him... brushing him aside and climbing out of the ring. Misawa exits after him, making sure Tengu doesn't try anything.] AO: We were expecting a great main event tonight, and suddenly it's become even greater. Super Tengu, Jinsei Tenru, and Jensei Tenru facing off in a three-way dance.... and Jensei Tenru takes advantage of the distracted Tengu... TOKYO FIST! Tengu turned around right into the spinning elbow from Jensei Tenru, and Super Tengu is stunned! Tenru pulls him in... he's going for the Nodowa Bomb.... RUSSIAN SICKLE BY JINSEI TENRU ON JENSEI! At the risk of sounding cliche, "The Japanese Superstar" just about took Jensei's head off with that one. TK: Jensei Tenru has made enemies out of both Super Tengu and Jinsei Tenru, and the old addage "the enemy of my enemy is my friend" is clearly coming into play now. Tengu and Jinsei Tenru are both putting the boots to Jensei Tenru... just stomping away on him. [Tengu and Jinsei pulls Jensei up off the mat and whip him across the ring into the ropes. Jensei rebounds off and is met by dual spinning backfists that catch him square on the jaw, sending him tumbling to the canvas again, blood trickling from his bottom lip. The crowd OOOOHS at the impact.] AO: Double Uraken! Jensei Tenru will be lucky if he doesn't have a dislocated jaw after those vicious backfists. Some of the fans are trying to actually rally behind Jensei, and that only seems to making Tengu and Jinsei Tenru angrier! Tengu pulls Jensei up off the mat and sends him into the ropes... Tenru rebounds... high back bodydrop by Tengu... INTO A POWERBOMB BY JINSEI TENRU! [The crowd "oohs" at the impact as Jensei's body bounces off the mat as he's driven down by Jinsei Tenru. Jinsei stands over his fallen enemy, shouting at him, then points to his elbow and drops a series of snap elbowdrops to Jensei's chest. "How do you feel now, Sunshine?" Jinsei shouts , reaching down to slap Jensei's face.] TK: Jinsei Tenru now slapping the face of Jensei, trying to humiliate him. Tengu is just hanging back for now, but you can bet that Tengu is merely waiting on Tenru so they can continue this two-on-one .... assault. This isn't even a match right now.. it's a massacre. AO: Well, Jensei has only himself to blame really. He made enemies of two very dangerous men... two very egocentric men. They weren't going to let this youngster come in and make a name for himself off of their reputations. [Jinsei takes a step back as Tengu comes bouncing off the ropes and delivers a snap legdrop over Jensei's chest, followed by a second... then a third. While Tengu is assaulting Tenru in that manner, Jinsei begins stomping his foot down on Jensei's knee, over and over... just smashing it between his boot and the mat.] TK: Jinsei Tenru now pulling Jensei off the canvas again and here comes Tengu again... double irish whip sends Jensei into the ropes... Tengu and Jinsei set for a double backdrop... but Jinsei hooked his arms around the ropes! Tengu and Jinsei set too early and... DDT ON BOTH MEN BY JENSEI TENRU! [The crowd pops for Jensei as he drops both Tengu and Jinsei on their heads with the DDTs, and quickly scoops up Jinsei Tenru and tosses him out of the ring.] TK: Apparently Jensei wants to save Jinsei for last, tossing the other Tenru out of the ring and onto the arena floor. He's pulled Super Tengu up and it's Tengu who's sent for the ride this time.... Tengu bounces off the ropes.... and ducks a lariat by Tenru! Tenru spins around... Tengu with a kick to the stom.. NO, Tenru catches the leg and.... ENZUILARIATO BY SUPER TENGU! Tremendous impact to the back of young Tenru's head. AO: Tenru's problems haven't ended yet.... because... wait a minute... Jinsei Tenru is pushing a table into the ring! Owada is warning Tenru that if he uses the table he'll be disqualified, but Jinsei doesn't seem too concerned about that. He and Tengu are setting up the table, and now Jinsei is telling Tengu to go to the top rope. Tengu doesn't seem to like being told what to do.. but he's going up top anyway. Tengu set on the top rope and.... JINSEI SHAKES THE ROPES! [The crowd "ooohs" again as Tengu loses his balance and lands in an... *awkward*... position, straddling the top turnbuckle. Jinsei Tenru begins to throw punch after punch at the agonized Tengu, then climbs up onto the second turnbuckle. He hooks up Tengu and...] TK: NORTHERN LIGHTS BOMB THROUGH THE TABLE! [The crowd OOOHS again, louder... some fans popping for the move, but most jeering Jinsei Tenru as he stands over the fallen Tengu, stomping away at the back of Tengu's neck. The referee calls for the bell, which sounds over and over as Tenru continues his assault on Tengu. Finally Shinya Misawa hits the ring, pulling Tenru away and the two men begin to argue. It looks as if Jinsei is contemplating hitting Misawa, but thinks better of it... leaving the ring at Misawa's direction. Meanwhile the referee is checking on Tengu, who is writhing in pain, holding his neck.] TK: This doesn't look good, Akira. Everyone knows that Tengu's neck has never fully recovered being broken by Ace Kilcannon. Misawa and Botan are both looking over Tengu and.... Botan is telling the referee to ring the bell. AO: Apparently Misawa and Botan have declared Tengu unable to continue the match, so Jensei Tenru, who's just barely sitting up in the corner, is our winner. Truthfully though, the only person who came away this relatively unscathed was Jinsei Tenru. [Nishi enters the ring after Botan speaks to him. He waits for the corwd to die down, then starts to speak...] Nishi: Ladies & Gentlemen, Botan Owada has disqualified Jinsei Tenru for putting Super Tengu through a table. He has also ruled Super Tengu unable to continue. Therefore, the winner of this match, as a result of the referee's decision...JENSEI TENRU!!! [The crowd gives a mixed pop at Nishi's pronouncement. Tenru's fans start a "Tenru! Tenru!" chant, and throw streamers into the ring. Misawa and two other security personel escort Jinsei Tenru to the back, while a medical team hits the ring, and starts to place Tengu on a stretcher...] TK: This has been a short, but tragic main event. Super Tengu may not be the most loved man in SJPW, but he is one of it's most talented. If his career is over after Jinsei Tenru's actions, then SJPW has lost one of it's greatest competitors. AO: I hope he is all right Kikuchi-sama. This is too reminiscent of what happened to him at the hands of Ace Kilcannon! TK: I agree. We have to end the show now. Hopefully we will have an update on Super Tengu's condition next week. Until then, for Akira Ono, I am Tiger Kikuchi. Goodnight! [The camera pans back to the ring, where the medical team is taking Tengu backstage on a stretcher. The crowd is applauding him, while Tenru is celebrating his victory in the ring. The credits start to roll, and as the camera starts to fade out, Tenru looks into the camera, and says "I told you Super Tengu....I AM IT!"] _____ _____ _____ __ __ /\ _ `\ /\___ \ /\ _ `\ /\ \ __/\ \ \ \ \_\_\ \/__/\ \ \ \ \_\ \ \ \ \/\ \ \ \ \/` ____\ _\ \ \ \ \ ,__/ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ ` /\ \_\ \ /\ \_\ \ \ \ \/ \ \ \_/ \_\ \ \ \_____\ \ \____/ \ \_\ \ `\___ ___/ \/_____/ \/___/ \/_/ '\/__//__/ © 1998 http://www.calnan.com/sjpw/
