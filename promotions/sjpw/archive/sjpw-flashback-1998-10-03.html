Jonnie Adonis comes walking out, holding some papers. He sits down, shuffles his papers, and smiles into the camera...] Jonnie Adonis: Hello everyone. I'm Jonnie Adonis, and this is Flashback. We're showing matches from the early days of SJPW, starting with the first Ring Wars show. This time, we have two interesting matches. The first one features the "Icon" of SJPW, Super Tengu, in his debut match against the veteran Tiger Kikuchi. This was the start of Tengu's rise to dominance in SJPW's cruiserweight ranks, and the start of his cult-like following of fans. We take you now to this exciting match-up... Too bad he chose to follow his ego instead of his heart. [looks at his papers] JA: Our other match for the evening pits Elijah, The Ultimate Male, against Survival, a man I know very well. Both men were influential in the early days of SJPW, but both of them failed to achieve the greatness that could have been theirs. Survival left a more lasting legacy in SJPW by helping found The Gaijin, a coalition of american wrestlers who felt they were being used to put over the japanese talent. The Gaijin featured two men who became superstars in their own right: "Too Sexy" Lance Smith, who went on to win the WWGP World Title, and Ed Dean, who was coming into his own towards the time of the WWGP fiasco. Dean is back in SJPW, now teaming with Noboru Nishida. These two men can thank Survival for some of the attention they got in the old days, as he was as controversial as he was talented....
