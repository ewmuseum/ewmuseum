Metro, Hour One 1/2

Jonnie Adonis is wearing a leather jacket, white shirt, black tie, slacks and shoes. He has green eyes. Eddie Rose has red hair and is wearing blue jeans, a black T-Shirt with "SJPW" written in Red on it, and a black leather jacket. Both men smile at the camera..] Jonnie Adonis: Welcome to SJPW Metro! I'm Jonnie Adonis, and this is my partner, Eddie Rose. We have a big show tonight, as Metro expands to two hours! Tonight we'll see the debut of Heaven & Hell, as well as the teams of The Maulers & The Yakuza in action. Eddie Rose: We've also got Angel Taboo, Juventud Sagrada & "Young Dragon" Hideoh Kanemera in action as well. Plus we have four big matches for the fans: In the second round of the Welterweight tournament, Jet Jaguar II takes on Kabuki Kid of the Ronin, and "Black Dragon Masato Kurasawa takes on Yukio "The Warbird" Murakami! JA: We also have Kagemusha of the Daimyo battle Shishi "Lion" Fujiwara of Chiimu Tora, and in the main event, Tony Starks & Oleg Vladikoff battle Kenji Ishida & Shinji Usami! ER: That should be a great match, as both teams are highly talented! This is definitely going to be one hell of a show! Let's get to ringside, and get this show underway... __ ___ _____________________________________________________ / |/ /__ / /________ / / /|_/ / -_) __/ __/ _ \ The Yakuza vs Team Angel /_/ /_/\__/\__/_/ \___/_______________________________________/ The Yakuza, still angered that they have been overlooked by the booking committe to face the Bonecrushers, annhiliated Team Angel. As soon as they got to the ring, Geto & Tazaka exploded on the masked Angels, hitting them with various martial arts moves & suplexes. Within a minute, they hit the Sacred Blade on BOTH Angels, then forced them both to submit to Dragon Sleepers! Winnners: The Yakuza by submission. JA: The Yakuza are on the warpath! They want the All-Asian tag titles, and are going to obliterate everyone that stands in their way! ER: I agree. And this 8 man tag team match at the supercard is only going to anger them more. They want a title shot, and they want it now! JA: I hope Jumbo wises up, and gives it to them. If not, then more teams are going to get what Team Angel did! ER: Our next match features Juventud Sagrada in action. He's been silent since his loss in the Welterweight tournament. JA: He put up a good fight, but he fell short. I think the kid's got alot of potential. He's still young, so he's got plenty of time to come into his own. __ ___ _____________________________________________________ / |/ /__ / /________ / / /|_/ / -_) __/ __/ _ \ Juventud Sagrada vs Sorai Jinbo /_/ /_/\__/\__/_/ \___/_______________________________________/ Sagrada started the match with a series of armdrags, then hit a hiptoss. Jinbo was off balance, and Sagrada got a two count with a La Magistral cradle. He then went for La Sagrada Superkick, but Jinbo ducked under, and nailed Sagrada with a superkick of his own. Juvy sagged on the ropes, and as Jinbo charged him, Juvy sunset flipped him for another two count. Jinbo charged again, and Juvy hit a spinning backbreaker, then a springboard moonsault. He dragged Jinbo to his feet, inverted Atomic Dropped him, spun him over into a tombstone, and drove his head into the mat. Juvy hit a legdrop off the top rope, stood Jinbo up, and finished him off with La Sagrada Superkick for the win. Winner: Juventud Sagrada. ER: Impressive win for the young Sagrada. JA: I told you he has the talent. Jinbo had him for a moment, but Sagrada came right back at him. This kid is gonna be a major star someday. Mark my words. ER: Up next, we have The Maulers, who seem to think SHVF is the real deal, and SJPW is some kind of joke. JA: Yeah, they said that. Then they got their assess handed to them by The Yakuza! __ ___ _____________________________________________________ / |/ /__ / /________ / / /|_/ / -_) __/ __/ _ \ The Maulers vs Ozaki & Usami /_/ /_/\__/\__/_/ \___/_______________________________________/ Douglas & Thomas were in a foul mood after last week's loss to The Yakuza, and took it out on Ozaki & Usami. Douglas overpowered Ozaki, and just hammered him with 3 slams in a row. He tagged in Douglas, who nailed Ozaki with repeated backbreakers. Usami tried to save his partner, but Douglas caught him with a lariat, then hit his "Thumper" heart punch, sending Usami to the floor. The Maulers then lived up to their name, tagging in and out, and using various double team moves on Ozaki. They kept insulting the Yakuza while doing this, and to add insult to injury, then used the Sacred Blade to take Ozaki out! As Thomas made the pin, Douglas ragged on the Yakuza in the camera. After the three count, the Maulers attempted to use The Sacred Blade on Usami as well, but The Yakuza stormed the ring, and a brawl started between the two teams. Before security could stop them, The Yakuza hit the Sacred Blade on Thomas, and Douglas was able to nail Geto with his "Thumper" heartpunch after tagging Tazaka with a chair.. Winners: The Maulers by pinfall. ER: The Maulers are treading on thin ice insulting the Yakuza. JA: Definitely. I know alot of people don't like the Yakuza, but I do. I think they are the best tag team in SJPW, bar none. The Bonercrushers are marked men, and when they face in the ring, The Yakuza are taking the gold home! ER: I disagree, because I think The Bonecrushers are excellant champions. Plus, they have the edge in experience. We'll just have to wait and see though... JA: Next up is a second round match in the Welterweight tournament. Yukio Murakami takes on Masato Kurasawa. ER: This should be an excellant match. Both men have alot riding in this, and the winner gets to face the winner of the Jet Jaguar II/Kabuki Kid match that follows! __ ___ _____________________________________________________ / |/ /__ / /________ Welterweight Tournament, Round Two: / / /|_/ / -_) __/ __/ _ \ Yukio Murakami vs Masato Kurasawa /_/ /_/\__/\__/_/ \___/_______________________________________/ [The camera pans to the ring. Yukio "The Warbird" Murakami and "Black Dragon" Masato Kurasawa are in the ring as Goto Kyoji instructs them to shake hands. They shake, and then walk back to thier respective corners and Goto calls for the bell ..] JA: Two of the next generation of light heavyweights in the ring Eddie. ER: Indeed, both men have advanced to the second round of the tournament, and now look to move even further down the road to the gold. It is also the battle of finishers as both men use the same hold to win their matches primarily, the swinging DDT. JA: This could be a matter of whoever hits their DDT first, wins. Murakami circling around Kurasawa, and now he moves in...Collar and elbow tie-up, but a quick armdrag by Kurasawa. ER: Murakami quickly to his feet and Kurasawa charges...Japanese Armdrag by Murakami sends the "Black Dragon" crashing to the mat. Both men to their feet, and they stare each other down.[crowd applauds] JA: They come together again...Kurasawa spins into a wristlock, and now wrenches the arm of Murakami, causing him to double over in pain. ER: Kurasawa in control, and his mentor Hiro Matsuo is yelling to him...Kurasawa now drives an elbow down to the arm and shoulder of Murakami, and now positions him for an over the shoulder armbreaker. JA: Murakami frantically fights it, and he manages to break free, and now lands a hooking kick to the back of the leg of Kurasawa, and another...A third kick, followed by a Jumping Spinkick to the chest that sends Kurasawa down![crowd applauds] ER: Murakami runs off the ropes, and vaults over Kurasawa. Kurasawa to his feet as "The Warbird" fires off the opposite side. JA: YAKUZA KICK![crowd "ohhhhs"] Murakami with a kick to the face and Kurasawa goes down hard. Murakami runs off the ropes again as Kurasawa gets to his feet. Jumping kick, but Kurasawa blocks it. ER: Oh! Reverse rolling elbow by Murakami connects however. Kurasawa drops to one knee, and Murakami quickly hooks the arm and spins over...La Majistral Cradle![crowd "ohhhhs"] 1...! JA: Kurasawa quickly able to kickout [crowd applauds], and he rolls to his feet as Matsuo is livid on the outside, screaming to his man, the referee, the fans, us, anyone whithin earshot is getting a verbal barrage. ER: Murakami fires in with kicks to the legs and midsection, and Kurasawa is reeling against the ropes. Murakami with an irish whip sending Kurasawa shooting to the ropes. JA: Running Clothesline attempted by Murakami, but Kurasawa ducks under. Kurasawa comes back off the ropes with a clothesline of his own, but Murakami catches the arm...Full Nelson by Murakami and we could see a Dragon Suplex! ER: Kurasawa blocks the move, and breaks free from the hold with an impressive display of strength. He grabs Murakami's right arm and snaps it down across his shoulder! JA: Armbreaker by Kurasawa, and Murakami recoils back to the ropes, clutching his elbow. Vicious move by the "Black Dragon" and he isn't done. ER: Kurasawa grabs the arm, and drags Murakami to the center of the ring...Another armbreaker, and he maintains hold of the wrist, and does it again! JA: Murakami crying out in pain, and Kurasawa takes him down to the mat with an armbar, and Goto drops down, checking for a submission. ER: Kurasawa really doesn't have the hold locked in, and Murakami is able to turn, and reach for the ropes. JA: He is close, but he can't quite reach. Kurasawa now heading the words from his manager Matsuo, and he drags Murakami away from the ropes, and locks on a short-arm scissors! This could be it. ER: Goto checking, but Murakami won't submit, and the fans begin to clap and cheer, trying to get behind "The Warbird". JA: Both Kurasawa and Matsuo seem angered by this, and Murakami seems to be inspired as he drags himself to the ropes despite having Kurasawa clinging to his arm. Murakami reaches out with his leg...He got the ropes, and Goto calls for the break.[crowd applauds] ER: Kurasawa having to be told to break by Goto, and the referee lays in a five count, and Kurasawa breaks it just before the disqualification. Murakami cradling his arm, and he rolls out to the floor. Kurasawa backs away, and now he runs to the ropes.[crowd "ohhhhs"] JA: It looked like Kurasawa was going for a suicide dive, but Murakami saw it coming, and ran out of harms way. ER: Or so he thought...Kurasawa changing direction, and he leaps to the second rope and vaults over the top rope on the other side fo the ring post...SPRINGBOARD CORKSCREW PLANCHA!!![crowd "ohhhhs"] JA: He hit it! What a great move, and now both men down. Kurasawa known for his high-risk offense, and he shows why with that insane manuver. ER: Matsuo over to check on his man, and possibly to dish out more punishment to Murakami, but Goto is right there keeping the devious manager in check.
