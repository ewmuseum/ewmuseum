News & Notes 7/16/04

Jul 16, 2004, 12:00:00 AM

The Crush Crew upset MAMMOTH Kagawa & Noboru "Raging Bull" Nishida at "Legends Are Born", the debut event from Illinois based Pro Wrestling Glory. A summary of the event can be found here: http://www.geocities.com/pwglory/Shows/labresults.html The Crush Crew played it strictly by the rules, and even followed the regulation in PWG that opponents must shake hands before and after their match. Both teams got a good response from the crowd, and SJPW officials have stated that they hope to do more inter-promotional work with PWG in the future. CEO Yamashita told reporters today that he's very pleased with the response fans have given to both the GPH Tag League, and the Junior Struggle! tournament. "Our newer tournaments have been a bigger success than the Giant Baba Cup was. The competitors in this tournament are more motivated to entertain the fans, and showcase just how good they really are. With very few "established" stars competing for the promotion on a regular basis, we've built ourselves with many young lions. Add in the few veterans, along with some phenomenal touring talent, and we have the right mix to show the fans a great mixture of the old, the new, and the future of our sport." The Sky's The Limit 4 got underway. Tasogare no Prince faced Sushi Tsunami, but was unable to defeat the NEO Pro Cruiserweight champion, thanks to outside interference from Pablo O'Conner & Steph. TnP put up a hell of a fight though, and SJPW applaud's him for representing our promotion in this event. Despair & "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder will headline the second night, which will likely take place on Monday. As both men are facing off in a tag team match this Saturday, it should be interesting to see what kind of shape each man is entering and leaving their match at TSTL 4. More info as that becomes available. Se-hong Kwan is out for ten days thanks to the chairshot he took from Despair during their Gunryo title match on the 14th. Kwan plans on coming back strong, and has his eyes set on the Gunryo title. "Everyone saw that Despair had to resort to a chair to keep his belt. He's unable to rely on just his skills any longer. He's grown soft, and soft doesn't allow you to keep titles."
