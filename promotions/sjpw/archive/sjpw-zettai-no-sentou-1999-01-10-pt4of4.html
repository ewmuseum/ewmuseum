He cannot afford to lose his temper, that is the way of old, and it can be his undoing. [Tetsuo balances on top, and the stares out at the crowd as they "ohhh" and "ahhh" ready for a spectacular manuver. Tetsuo taking too long, and now Jensei pulls Tetsuo by the head and then drops...Hangmans Neckbreaker off the top rope!![fans "ohhh"] AO:..... [Jensei locks up Tetsuo looking for an Exploder, but Tetsuo escapes with back elbows, and then attempts one of his own, but Jensei manages to fire in with a couple knee strikes and then staggers back as Tetsuo releases the hold.] ER: Jensei now spins...Tokyo Fist! I can't believe it, this is incredible. Tetsuo suddenly comes to life hitting a forearm that staggers Jensei back. Right into another Tokyo Fist!
