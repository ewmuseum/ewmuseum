News & Notes 7/11/04

Jul 13, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Van Wild became the new Rumble Pro World Champion, defeating Justin Kell at the Rumble Pro Invitational. Our sincerest congratulations to Van Wild on his accomplishment. He has assured SJPW officials that this does not change his commitment to SJPW and the GPH Tag Team League, and SJPW officials will work with Van Wild to work around any title defenses and other contracts he needs to honor during the league's existence. Despair signed a waiver to wrestle on the 13th after Doctors wouldn't clear him to wrestle. The Doctors fear the wear and tear from wrestling in NEO, Rumble Pro and Super Japan, along with the heavy travelling, is starting to wear away at the Gunryo Champion. He took two nasty bumps in the ten man scramble match, and as he is also facing a very determined Brody Thunder at The Sky's The Limit 4, SJPW doctors fear Despair is not going to be able to fully heal from the heavy damage his body has been taking of late. Some insiders feel "Firefly" Horo Atashi is being overlooked by the other competitors in the Junior Struggle! tournament. The quiet, unassuming Atashi has gone 2-0 in Block A, and it hasn't been missed by CEO Yamashita. Yamashita is considering giving Atashi a shot at either "All Star" Joey Darren's IICW cruiserweight title, or Masashi "Lightning" Lanther's NCWF cruiserweight title. While Darren is in America preparing for a big match with Eric Payne in ICW, Lanther had the following to say about Atashi: "Atashi-san is very talented. He's definitely under rated so far, and I think both the booking committee and the other wrestlers tend to over look him. I've watched him wrestle, and I think he shows much promise. If SJPW will book it, I would gladly defend my title against him. Atashi-san is the future of this sport, and I love the competition that young lions give someone like me!" Rumor has it that GPH Champion Kentaro Kawasaki was relieved about not having to team with Lanther on the July 17th taping of The Zone! Kawasaki considers tag team contests to be boring, and has been known to actually take naps on the apron when he isn't in the ring! Noboru "Raging Bull" Nishida is elated to find himself the first challenger to Kawasaki's GPH title. "I never suspected that I would be the first to receive a title shot. Granted, I was likely a safe bet, since my record isn't the best currently. However, I intend on making sure that win or lose, Kawasaki-san is in for a fight to keep that title around his waist!"
