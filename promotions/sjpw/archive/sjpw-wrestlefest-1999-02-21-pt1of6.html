Prince slowly raising his own hand.... and now the other hands come up. ... both men straining to gain the advantage... and it looks like Murakami is winning... pressing back on Prince's wrists.... forcing Prince backward... and Tasogare no Prince displaying his athleticism as he arches back into a bridge! MONKEY FLIPS Murakami right over and Tasogare no Prince with a quick kip up, and both men back on their feet.
