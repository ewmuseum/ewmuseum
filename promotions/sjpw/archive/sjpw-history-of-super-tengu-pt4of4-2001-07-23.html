The History of Super Tengu: The Showdown & New Challenges The Showdown: It all came down to the finals. The SJPW All Asian Cruiserweight Title Tournament Finals were held at the first SJPW Pay-Per-View “Battlecade’97”. A week before “Battlecade’97” Super Tengu and Takao Nakami beat Shinji “LIVE” Hamada and Rhiyoto Makada. Tengu scored the pin on Hamada after knocking him out with a loaded boot superkick. Though a tainted win, Tengu was one up on Hamada. To put an even more interesting spin on things Hamada only won his semi-final with match Kyu Kabuki due to the interference of Dragon Special, who had been feuding with Kabuki at the time. In contrast Tengu scored a clean win over “Too Sexy’ Lance Smith. Going in Tengu seemed to have the stronger claim to the belt. [name withheld] had done a great job in writing matches building up to this big showdown. [name withheld] and I wanted this to be special and wrote very detailed match strategies. It was around this time that I started “calling spots” in my strats. I’d tell Gerry what I’d like to see happen to Tengu as far a high risk moves. But there was a twist to it… I also told Gerry what spots I’d like to see Tengu miss. By adding the effect of Tengu missing a spot or having a spot be reversed it made every match have a BIG moment, it also made writing transitions in the match that much easier for Gerry.
