SJPW Card Archive

Control Center, Part Four

5 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Control Center continues...] TK: We are back, and we have two matches to go! Our next match will feature Ed Dean facing Mitsunori Takizawa in the last first round match in the All-Asian Juuryou tournament. AO: Takizawa will at long last give Dean a beating that he so richly deserves! TK: We shall see Ono-sama. AO: Why do you say that? TK: Takizawa *is* past his prime, while Dean is well into his prime. AO: Takizawa is no more over the hill than you are. TK: He's much older, and has suffered many more injuries than I have! AO: He's not that much older than you.... TK: You are insufferable! Let's go to the ring... ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / /All-Asian Juuryou Tournament, Round One: / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Ed Dean vs Mitsunori Takizawa /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [The camera pans to the ring, where Nishi Toju & Goto Kyoji stand. Nishi waits for the crowd to die down, then raises his microphone to speak....] Nishi: This next contest is a first round match in the All-Asian Juuryou tournament! It is one fall, twenty minute time limit. Your referee for this match is Goto Kyoji! [crowd applauds as Goto bows] Nishi: Introducing first, accompanied to the ring by Hadi Moditan of SHVF, from New York, New York, he weighs in at 355 pounds....ED DEAN!!! [Dean & Moditan come out to a chorus of boos. No music plays. Dean is wearing an SHVF "Strong Style Rules" T-shirt. Moditan has a smile on his face as he watches the negative reaction from the crowd. Dean enters the ring, and goes to a neutral corner...] AO: How DARE that little weasel show his face tonight! TK: Ono-sama! Please! Moditan has a temporary manager's license! He has every right to be here! AO: Who gave him a license? TK: Kobayashi did. He had to as part of the compromise that brought Dean here tonight. AO: Bad move on Jumbo's part. ["Too Old To Rock & Roll, Too Young To Die" by Jethro Tull blares over the PA] Nishi: His opponent, from Tokyo, and weighing in at 300 pounds...MIT-SU-NOR-I TAK-I-ZA-WA!!!! [The crowd gives Takizawa a standing ovation as he comes out on the runway. He arches his eye, then walks to the ring. Standing outside the ring, he asks Nishi for the microphone...] TK: What is Takizawa doing? AO: I don't know! Shut up and listen! Mitsunori Takizawa (looking up at the ring and smirking at Ed Dean): Let me tell you something Dean.... nothing would give me greater pleasure than to get in the ring right now and run you out of Japan the same way I ran the MLWO out of this country.. BUT.. the fact is I have a match tomorrow night for the Battle Arts title so I'm saving my energy for something more worthwhile than YOU. Mitsunori Takizawa: HOWEVER.... I'm not about to let you advance in this tournament... a tournament you don't deserve to be in to start with. So I'm happy to announce that I have someone set to take you on, right here... right now... [The crowd murmurs as someone slides into the ring behind Dean & Moditan. He whirls Dean around, and levels him with a lariat! The man looks at Moditan, who exits the ring quickly. The man looks at Takizawa, who nods approvingly. He takes off his hood, revealing...] TK: NOBORU NISHIDA!?! AO: YES! Takizawa did bring him around! [Dean starts to get up, and Nishida slams into him with a shoulderblock that sends Dean back to the mat. Nishida drags Dean to his feet, and applies a front facelock. He looks askance the crowd, who roar their approval as he raises his forearm, and starts to pummel Deans back with resounding blows...] TK: Nishida fights like a man possessed! AO: That is because he has seen the light! Dean tricked him into hating Takizawa, and now he faces his real enemy! Dean is facing a monster of his own creation! [Dean drops to one knee, but Nishida pulls him back up. He releases the facelock, then hits a stiff european uppercut that sends Dean staggering back to the ropes. Nishida charges in with a knee to the gut, then whips Dean to the opposite side. Dean comes off the ropes, and Nishida takes him up and over with a high backbody drop. Dean sits up, holding his back, and Nishida kicks him stiffly in the back. The crowd "oooohs" at Nishida's obvious viciousness...] TK: Nishida is really taking it to his former mentor. As of yet, Dean has mounted no offense whatsoever! AO: He never expected to face "The Raging Bull". He was expecting Takizawa, but my old partner pulled one over on him! I love it! [Nishida drags Dean to his feet, sets him up, then lifts him up in the air. He holds him vertical, allowing the blood to rush to Dean's head. The crowd "oooohs" at this show of strenght, and then Nishida drops backwards, completing the suplex. Nishida rolls on top of Dean for a cover, but he kicks out at one. Nishida gets up, an angry look on his face. He drags Dean to his feet, and slaps on a headlock. Dean backs to the ropes, then whips Nishida across the ring. Nishida comes off the ropes, and both men slam into each other with shoulderblocks. Neither man moves much, and they glare at each other in the center of the ring. They both go back to the ropes, come off, and slam into each other again. They glare at each other again, and Nishida goes to the ropes a third time. He comes off, but Dean sidesteps him! Nishida bounces off the other side, and Dean catches him, picking him up off the mat, then slamming him down with a spinebuster! The crowd "oooohs" as Dean gets to his feet, breathing hard...] TK: Dean finally recovering from Nishida's early assault. This could be the deciding factor: who has the most stamina to survive such punishment? AO: Dean has more experience than Nishida, but the young man has heart! He has a fire in his soul! That makes up for his lack of experience! [Dean leans on the ropes, catching his breath, while Nishida sits up, shaking his head. He slowly rises to his feet, and Dean lunges, nailing him with a lariat that sends his former partner flying back into the corner. Dean charges, and starts to kick away at Nishida's gut. Goto tries to get Dean to back off, but he pushes the ref aside. Goto warns him, and Dean backs off. He waits a moment, then charges back in, driving his shoulder into Nishida's gut. He grabs Nishida by the arm, and attempts to whip him to the opposite corner, but Nishida reverese it, and sends Dean chest first to the corner! Nishida charges in behind him, nailing Dean from behind with a lariat! The crowd "oooohs" As Nishida backs away, then grabs Dean from behind in a waistlock, and takes him over with a German Suplex! He bridges back for the pin, and Goto counts to two before Dean kicks out. The crowd applauds both men...] TK: Dean still has alot of fight left in him. AO: He's a powerful man. Even I can't take that away from him... [Nishida drags Dean to his feet, then sends him to the mat with a vicious kneelift. He snaps an elbowdrop onto his chest, then makes a cover. Dean kicks out at two, and Nishida starts to look frustrated. Takizawa speaks to him, and he nods. He grabs Dean by the leg, and turns him over into a single leg boston crab. Dean winces in pain, and Nishida pulls back harder on the leg. Dean reaches out, and grabs the ropes, forcing the break. The crowd applauds as Nishida breaks clean. Nishida drags Dean to his feet, and Dean headbutts him, causing him to stagger back. Dean surprises everyone by nailing Nishida with an enzugiri, drawing loud "ooooohs" from the startled crowd!] TK: I can't believe Dean did that! Few men of his size can execute that move! AO: So he has one fancy move in his arsenal? Big deal! [Dean gets to his feet. He sits Nishida up, and slaps on a full nelson on him. Using his strength, he pulls Nishida to his feet, then falls back into a Dragon Suplex, dumping Nishida on his head! The crowd "oooohs" at Deans' feat of Strength, and he raises his fist to the crowd in response. Dean drags Nishida to his feet, scoops him up, then drops down into a shoulderbreaker. Nishida cries out in pain, while Dean holds onto him, stands up, and executes a *second* shoulderbreaker! The crowd "ooooohs" as Nishida lays on the mat, holding his arm. Dean stomps on the shoulder, berating his opponent while doing so. Outside, Moditan smiles, while Takizawa's face betrays his concern. Dean stops stomping on Nishida, and drags him to his feet. He slaps on a hammerlock then rams Nishida shoulder-first to the corner turnbuckle. He pulls Nishida back, then rams him shoulder-first to the corner again. He spins Nishida around, and applies a wristlock. He raises his arm above him, and slowly turns the arm, positioning himself under it. He pulls the arm down hard over his shoulder, the armbreaker causing Nishida to scream in pain. He slaps on a Fujiwara armbar, and Goto asks him if he wants to submit, but he refuses...] TK: This is where Dean excells. The man is an expert is various arm submssion holds. AO: Nishida could be in trouble now... [Dean smiles at his opponent. He holds the arm out, then drops down into an arm-DDT, causing more damage to Nishida's shoulder. Dean rolls him over, and applies a short armscissors, and Nishida struggles to break free. Goto asks him if he wants to submit, but he refuses again! The crowd starts to chant "Nishida! Nishida!", and the young man gets motivated. He starts to sit up, and Dean looks shocked. Slolwly, Nishida starts to get to his feet, while Dean maintains the armscissors on him. Nishida finally makes it to his feet, and in an amazing show of strength, actually gets Dean up off the mat, then falls down, slamming him hard! The crowd "oooohs" as Dean breaks the hold. Nishida rolls to the ropes, and slowly rises to his feet, favoring his injured arm...] TK: What a show of power by Nishida! He's much stronger than he looks! AO: But his arm has taken alot of punishment! If Dean applies the Juji Gatame, Nishida will be finished! [Dean rises to his feet, shaking his head. Nishida charges him, landing a big boot to the side of Dean's face. Dean staggers back to the ropes, bounces off, and Nishida catches him, lifts him off the mat, spins around, and drops Dean onto the ropes with a hotshot! The crowd "ooohs" as Dean rolls around the ring, holding his throat. Moditan looks concerned on the outside, while Takizawa nods approvingly. Nishida goes to the corner, and starts to climb to the top rope. Dean sits up, and Moditan yells at him to get up. Nishida makes it to the top rope, and steadies himself, while Dean gets to his feet. Dean staggers around, then turns toward Nishida. Nishida leaps off the top for a lariat, but Dean sidesteps, catching his arm as Nishida crashes to the mat. He locks his legs around the arm, drops to the mat, and starts to pull back as hard as he can, while the crowd "ooooohs"...] TK: Juji Gatame! He caught Nishida in the middle of the ring! AO: But the youngster refuses to submit! He'd rather let Dean break the arm! [Goto asks Nishida if he wants to submit, but he refuses. Although he is in serious pain, Nishida grits his teeth, and refuses to submit! Dean pulls back with all his might, and the crowd goes silent, expecting the arm to break. Suddenly, Takizawa runs to the timekeeper's table, and makes him ring the bell. Goto walks over, and asks why he rang the bell. Takizawa speaks to him, and Goto nods. He walks to Dean, and taps him on the shoulder, while Takizawa slides into the ring. Dean holds onto the Juji Gatame, so Goto starts a count. Finally, Dean breaks at four, and gets to his feet. He and Takizawa have a staredown, then Dean smiles, and says "Not today Old Man", and slides out of the ring. Goto and Nishi speak, and Nishi proceeds to make an announcement...] Nishi: The referee informs me that Takizawa has thrown in the towel for Nishida. Therefore, your winner, by decision....ED DEAN!!!! [crowd half-heartedly applauds] TK: Takizawa did the right thing. Nishida would've allowed Dean to break his arm rather than submit! AO: That would have been worse. His career would have been over! [Takizawa helps Nishida to his feet. The younger man's face is covered in tears. Onslaught comes to the ring, and the two men help Nishida out of the ring. Nishida holds his head down in shame, until the crowd starts chanting his name. He looks up, tears streaming down his face, then pushes his helpers off. Standing in the middle of the runway, he turns and bows to the audience, who give him a standing ovation. He bows again, then allows Takizawa & Onslaught to take him to the back...] TK: That man has nothing to be ashamed of. He proved that he is a force to be reckoned with. AO: He has a strong will, but too much pride. He almost lost his career if not for Takizawa. I hope his arm isn't seriously injured. TK: Hopefully we will know tomorrow night! But now, the ring is clear, and it's time for our main event! Oleg Vladikoff, making his singles debut after turning on his partner Tony Starks. The "Seven Second Killer" was supposed to face Akira Hashimoto, but Hashimoto has been detained by immigration officials in the United States. So now, Jumbo Kobayashi will announce his opponent. AO: I wonder who it could be? TK: We will find out soon. Let's go to ringside... ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / Main Event: / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Oleg Vladikoff vs ???? /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [The camera pans to the ring. where Nishi Toju, Goto Kyoji & Oleg Vladikoff stand. Nishi waits a moment, then makes his announcements...] Nishi: This is our main event for the evening! [crowd cheers]. Our referee is Goto Kyoji! [crowd cheers as Goto bows]. This match is one fall, with a twenty minute time limit. In the ring at this time, from Siberia, Russia, and weighing in at 230 pounds, he is the "SEVEN SECOND KILLER" O-LEG VLAD-I-KOFF!!! [Vladikoff bows as "Kashmir" by Led Zepplin plays over the pa] Nishi: Now, to introduce his opponent, the CEO of SJPW....JUMBO KOBAYASHI!!! [Crowd applauds as Kobayashi walks to ringside. He enters the ring, and takes the mike from Nishi...] Kobayashi: Dommo Nishi-sama. Vladikoff, as you know, Hashimoto cannot be here tonight. Rather than write this match off totally, I have found you another worthy opponent. Will you accept the match regardless? [Vladikoff nods yes, and the crowd applauds] Kobayashi: The let me present your opponent: from Kobe, weighing in at 253 pounds...."IRON MAN" TET-SU-O MAT-SU-MO-TO!!! ["Get Wild" by TM Network comes over the pa, and the crowd rises to it's feet as the house lights dim. Yellow spotlights scramble about the arena, until they all land on the rampway. Tetsuo emerges, and the crowd finally sees the man they once knew as Super Tengu. His black hair is now shoulder length, and his body has bulked somewhat. On his left arm is a tatoo of a Raven, and on his right is a black winged Angel. He is wearing black tights with yellow trim, yellow boots, elbow & kneepads. He has on a knee brace on his left leg. He walks to the ring, ignoring the reactions of the fans. He climbs through the ropes, and goes to his corner...] AO: I can't believe it! Tetsuo, here tonight? That pre-taped spot was a swerve! TK: He's bulked up somewhat. He looks more like his father now. AO: Will he still use his old high-flying style, or will he mat wrestle now that he's added twenty pounds? TK: I guess we'll find out! [Goto gives both men their instructions. Oleg & Tetsuo size each other up. Oleg offers Tetsuo his hand, and Tetsuo hesitantly shakes it. The crowd applauds, and Goto calls for the bell. The two men circle each other, then lock up. Oleg manuevers Tetsuo into a side headlock, but Tetsuo slips out, and applies a hammerlock. Oleg reverses it, and Tetsuo lands a back elbow. He lands a second one, and Oleg staggers back. Tetsuo turns around, and lands a stiff elbow to the top of Oleg's head. Oleg staggers back to the ropes, then comes off the ropes, and drops low, footsweeping Tetsuo off his feet. He grabs the leg and applies a stepover toehold, but Tetsuo grabs the ropes, forcing a break. Oleg backs off while Tetsuo gets to his feet. Both men nod to each other, then start to circle again...] TK: Tetsuo seems to be mat wrestling more now. AO: And it seems he and Vladikoff are fairly even matched! [Both men lock up again, and Tetsuo drives a knee into Oleg's gut. Oleg doubles over, and Tetsuo drives a stiff elbow to the back of his head. Oleg drops to one knee, and Tetsuo drives another elbow to the back of his head. He drags Oleg to his feet, sets him up, then takes him over with a snap suplex. Vladikoff sits up, and Tetsuo goes to the ropes, comes off and lands a sliding stiff lariat to Oleg, sending him back down to the mat! Tetsuo makes a cover, but Oleg kicks out at one and a half. The crowd applauds as Tetsuo drags Oleg to his feet. He attempts an irish whipe, but Oleg reverses, taking Tetsuo down with a drop toehold. He cranks on an anklelock submission, causing Tetsuo's face to contort with pain. He twists on the ankle, and Goto asks if Tetsuo wants to submit. He refuses, and Tetsuo starts to pull himself towards the ropes. Oleg ties to pull him back to the middle of the ring, but Tetsuo is able to make the ropes. The crowd applauds as Oleg breaks clean, and backs off...] TK: These two do seem evenly matched Ono-san. AO: Yes. It will be interesting to see how this goes.... [Tetsuo gets to his feet, and shakes his leg. He nods to Goto that he is ready to continue, and Goto motions for them to lock up. They do so, and Oleg takes Tetsuo over with a hip toss. He sits Tetsuo up, and locks on a rear chinlock. Goto asks if he wants to submit, but Tetsuo says no. Tetsuo starts to get to his feet, and Oleg releases the chinlock, and grabs his arms. He holds them behind him, and puts the sole of his foot on Tetsuo's spine, creating a form of standing surfboard. Tetsuo doubles over, relieving some of the pressure to his back, and slowly forces Oleg back to the ropes. They reach the ropes, and Goto orders the break. Oleg breaks clean, and Tetsuo takes the opportunity to nails Oleg in the side of the head with a stiff back elbow. The crowd "ooohs" as Oleg holds the side of his face, and Tetsuo turns around, and lands a vicious chop across Oleg's chest, causing the crowd to "Whooo". Tetsuo drives his knee into Oleg's gut, then sets him up, and hits an Exploder suplex. The crowd "oooohs" as Oleg lands on his head, and lays face down on the mat...] TK: Tetsuo is showing some of his mean streak here. AO: But he's wrestled fairly most of the match. This is a different man than the one who a few weeks ago wore the mask of Super Tengu... [Tetsuo stands Oleg up, and executes a Russian legsweep. He rolls on top for a cover, but Oleg kicks out at two. He drags Oleg to his feet, and sets him up for a suplex, but Oleg blocks the hold. He reverses the move, and rolls on top of Tetsuo. He mounts him, and starts to check him with forearm shots to the side of the head. The crowd "oooohs" as Tetsuo starts to bleed from his nose. Goto orders Oleg to break, and he does after a four count. Oleg gets to his feet, and backs off, allowing Tetsuo to get to his feet. Tetsuo rubs his nose, and looks at his blood. A grim look comes across his face, and he glares at Oleg. The two men moves to lock up, and Tetsuo catches Oleg with a kick to the gut. Oleg doubles over, and Tetsuo nails him with a stiff elbow to the back of his head. He then hits a european uppercut, and Oleg staggers back to the corner. Tetsuo moves in, but Oleg comes out of the corner with a Muay Thai roundhouse kick, sending Tetsuo to the mat. He drags Tetsuo to his feet, and rams him headfirst to the corner. He spins him around, and snap mares him to the mat. He jumps up on the second rope, then comes off with a jumping neck snap that compresses Tetsuo forward, then snaps him backwards. Oleg rolls to his feet, and mounts him again, and starts to check him with forearm shots! The crowd "oooohs" as again Goto calls for the break. Oleg continues to check Tetsuo until the four count, then breaks, and backs away. Tetsuo slowly gets to his feet, his nose bleeding more than before...] TK: This is a vicious match we're seeing. It started out friendly, but you can see bad feelings developing here... AO: This is a very stiff match. Tetsuo is wrestling more like his father did. Gone is the high-flying that was Tengu's trademark. Tetsuo has reinvented himself! [The two men circle each other, looking for an opening. Oleg attempts a Muay Thai kick, but Tetsuo catches the leg, and takes him over with a dragonscrew legwhip. Tetsuo gets to his feet, and sets himself, while Oleg starts to rise. As he gets to his feet, Tetsuo levels him with a stiff lariat! He drags Oleg to his feet, whips him to the ropes, then takes him over into a powerslam as he comes off. He hooks the leg for a cover, but Oleg kicks out at two. Tetsuo goes to the corner, and climbs to the top rope. The crowd "oooohs" in anticipation of what he might do, then Tetsuo flips off into a textbook moonsault! He makes another cover, but Oleg again kicks out at two...] TK: I see he still has some aerial moves in his arsenal... AO: Obviously. You can take the mask from him, but the skill remains... [Tetsuo drags Oleg to his feet, and Oleg gouges him in the eye. The crowd "Awwws" as Goto admonishes him. Oleg nods, then lays into Tetsuo with rapidfire knee strikes to his gut. Tetsuo sags over the top rope, and Oleg grabs him around the waist, then takes him over into a German suplex. He bridges back for a pin, but Tetsuo kicks out at two. Oleg rolls Tetsuo over, and grabs him around the waist again, lifting him up and over with a wheelbarrow suplex! He again bridges back for a pin, but Tetsuo again kicks out at two! Oleg gets to his feet, frustration setting into his face. He drags Tetsuo to his feet, and attempts an irish whip. Tetsuo reverses, and kicks him in the gut as he comes off. Tetsuo runs to the opposite ropes, and comes off, nailing Oleg with a neckbreaker. He stands Oleg up, goes to the ropes, and hits a second neckbreaker. He rolls on top for a cover, but Oleg kicks out at two and a half! The crowd applauds, while Tetsuo complains of a slow count...] TK: This has been a very even match. Vladikoff is a very talented man... AO: As well as very dangerous. Tetsuo must be careful... [Tetsuo drags Oleg to his feet, and lands a chop across his chest. The crowd "Whooos", and Oleg fires back with a european uppercut. Tetsuo comes back with an elbowsmash to Oleg's forehead, and Oleg staggers back, then responds with a Thai kick to the thigh. Tetsuo hits a backfist, while Oleg palm stikes Tetsuo in the face...] TK: This is starting to degenerate into a brawl... AO: I knew these two men couldn't keep their tempers in check! I can see where this is leading... [Oleg palm strikes Tetsuo in the face again, and he staggers back, then catches Oleg with a spinning backfist. Oleg staggers back, then ducks down, and tackles Tetsuo to the mat! He straddles Tetsuo, and starts to hammer his head/face with headbutts! The crowd starts to "oooh", then Tetsuo rolls Oleg over, and starts to hammer him with stiff forearm shots to the side of his head. Goto attempts to pull them apart when the bell sounds...] TK: The match is over? AO: I don't understand... [Goto pulls Tetsuo off Oleg, and orders him to a neutral corner. Oleg gets to his feet, and he orders him to a neutral corner as well. Nishi enters the ring, and he and Goto speak for a moment.. Goto then speaks to the timekeeper, nods, then speaks to Nishi again. Nishi steps to the center of the ring, and proceeds to make an announcement... ] Nishi: Ladies & Gentlemen, the time limit has expired. The referee therefore rules this bout....A TIME LIMIT DRAW!!! [The crowd reluctantly applaud, having hoped for a better outcome] TK: Well, Tetsuo is back, and he seems in top form. AO: Yes. I wonder how his return will affect things? TK: I don't know, but we'll see in the weeks to come. We are out of time. We hope you all enjoyed this preview show. We will see all of you tomorrow night for Sensou no Jidai. For Akira Ono, I'm Tiger Kikuchi. Goodnight!
