News & Notes 8/22/04

Aug 22, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Although he has secured Block A by going 4-0 in Junior Struggle!, "Firefly" Horo Atashi still has one last match in Block A, against Jet Jaguar III. That event will be booked very soon. Block B still has a few matches to go, with Wolf Jager needing to face Super Bakemono, KEOKI facing Asakura, and Hori needing to face Bakemono. At least two of those matches will take place on house shows this coming week, as will the Atashi/J3 match. Gregor Davinsky & Sergei Baranov of the EuroPack are unhappy that the Brute Squad was accepted into the Gunryo gauntlet match for J*STAR. They feel that as an outsider team, they don't deserve a chance to win the Gunryo tag titles. They are also eager to get their hands on Pramoj Ananda, who put Davinsky out for six weeks thanks to his Ananda Special. As mentioned above, Pramoj Ananda makes his return at J*STAR, teaming with Se-hong Kwan in the Gunryo tag Gauntlet. Ananda is eager to compete again, and looks forward to facing Davinsky & Baranov in the ring once more. Rumor has it that the SJPW booking committee are considering offering Scott Starring a title shot at either Lanther's NCWF title or against whomever wins the Sedei-X title at Jisedei: Tsunami. Officials have been impressed with his singles matches, and feel that even if it is a part time touring wrestler, he would make a fantastic addition to the Sedei-X talent roster. Following his dismal performance in Junior Struggle!, Tsunami is preparing a series of singles matches before asking for a rematch with Joey Darren for the IICW cruiserweight title. El Muerte Rojo and Mr. Smiley Mask are also possible challengers for that title. Darren has been looking impressive since losing to Atashi in Junior Struggle! He has been relying more on the flipping piledriver to win matches, which he has now dubbed the "All-Star Driver". Is there trouble in the DoD? Word has it that Despair is a little unhappy with Super Bakemono's recent attitude when dealing with members of Team Tiger. The last straw came when he shook hands with J3, his hated rival, after their match in Saitama. Bakemono supposedly is unhappy that Despair interfered on his behalf when he and Toroyama faced Bedford & Roberts, and has told Despair to stay away this week when they face Van Wild & Scott Starring. Jun Suzuki has filed a formal protest with the Super Japan board of directors over Yamashita's setting up Thunder vs Petrow for J*STAR, and for allowing it to have a retirement clause if Thunder loses. Suzuki, who has sworn to retire Thunder himself, is also upset that he is not booked to wrestle at J*STAR, and has bought himself a ticket for the event at his own expense. Takahiro KEOKI participated in the Darwin Cup, and was the second to last man eliminated. We congratulate KEOKI for being one of the final three men in the finals. The Rookie Sensation has come a long way since Zenmen Sensou, and has became a popular star in the United States of late. Tiger Kikuchi has announced that Hadi Moditan, formerly of SHVF and other sports promotions, is now affiliated with the Team Tiger Dojo. The first joint statement by both men was to announce that they have convinced Super Japan to create a new title, which will be based off the old SHVF heavyweight title. This new belt, the Hyper Visual Fighting championship, can only be won in matches where an opponent is knocked out or submits. Kikuchi & Moditan are scouting the SJPW roster for men to compete for this title. So far, Wolf Jager, "Dragon Fury" Minobu Tetsuhara, Botan Nakatoni, Doug Thomas, "Living Legend" Tommy Fury and Vladimir Tarasov have been mentioned. Moditan also stated that "CRIMSON" Jin Hiroto, one of the Visual Spirits, would be a perfect choice to compete for such a title. Once eight competitors are chosen, Team Tiger plans on hosting a "King of Strong Style" single elimination tournament, with the winner being awarded the HVF championship. No word on when such a tournament will take place, or even if those men listed will actually be involved in the competition for that title. Block A: "Firefly" Horo Atashi (4-0-0) Mr. Smiley Mask (4-1-0) "All Star" Joey Darren (3-1-0) Jet Jaguar III (1-3-0) Toramusha (1-4-0) Fanta Boy (1-4-0) Block B: Wolf Jager (3-1-0) "Kyoto Tiger" Takayoshi Asakura (3-2-0) Takahiro KEOKI (4-0-0) Tsunami (1-4-0) Yatoro Hori (0-4-0) Super Bakemono (2-1-0) GPH Tag Team League: Van Wild & Scott Starring (3-0-1) "Hard" Knox Roberts & "Terrible" Tommy Bedford (4-0-0) Junya Toroyama & Super Bakemono (4-0-0) The Red Army (4-1-0) The Visual Spirits (3-1-1) The Maulers (2-5-0) The Crush Crew (1-6-0) The Nagasaki Bulldogs (0-7-0)
