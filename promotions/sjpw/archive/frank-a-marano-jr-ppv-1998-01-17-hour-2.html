AO [crying]: I know Eddie-san. Buddha bless me, I know [The cage door opens, and the other members of Team Tiger enter the ring. Masao & Tanaka hoist Jaguar up onto their shoulders, while Kikuchi & Lanther shake his hands. Shinji Hamada enters the ring, holding the belt that once slipped from his grasp, and walks over to Jaguar. He hands Jaguar the belt, who unable to contain himself any longer, bursts into tears. The crowd cheers louder, and Kyu Kabuki, Asamiya & Asakino, The Down Boys, The NorthPac, the
Brew Crew, and Rekka Sakura all enter the ring to congratulate the new champion. Mr. Yamashita and the ring announcer enter the ring, and Yamashita hands Jaguar his trophy.]
Ring Announcer: Your winner, and NEW SJPW Cruiserweight Champion... JET JAGUAR!!!! [The crowd erupts into cheers, and more streamers flood the ring. Jaguar holds the title above his head, then straps it around his waist. He looks around the ring, then hops off his allies shoulders. He pushes past the other wrestlers, and walks over to Super Tengu, who is only now sitting up. Jaguar helps
Tengu to his feet, and the two men stare into each other. Jaguar offers Tengu his hand. He looks at Jaguar's offered hand, and motions for the ring announcer to hand him the microphone. He takes the microphone, and raises it to speak...]
Super Tengu: Jet Jaguar, I will not shake your hand! Yes, you defeated me today, something few men can lay claim to. Enjoy your title reign while you can young Jaguar, for it shall be a short one. NO ONE! No one, not even you Jet Jaguar, will keep me from what is RIGHTFULLY mine! If it is the last thing I do, I WILL take that title belt from you, and make you PAY for the indignity you have made me suffer this night! SO SWEARS SUPER TENGU!!!
[Tengu throws the microphone down, and exits the cage. The crowd erupts into boos, and some people actually shower him with trash as he walks up the runway to the back. Jaguar lowers his head in sadness, and Tiger Kikuchi pats him on the shoulder, whispering something in his ear. Jaguar nods, then takes the title off his waist, and exits the ring. He leaps over the railing into the crowd, and raises the title over his head! The crowd goes crazy, and they lift the new champion over their heads, and slowly pass him up the arena!]
ER: What an incredible night this was! Jet Jaguar had to have wrestler what is most likely one of the greatest matches we will ever see in SJPW.
AO [sadness still in his voice]: That may be true Eddie-san, but I predict that this great moment will be over shadowed by dark times to come. Super Tengu has sworn that he will make Jet Jaguar pay for defeating him tonight, and we all know Tengu keeps his word.
Tengu sinks even lower into darkness, and no one is safe from his wrath now! ER: I hope you are wrong Akira. We are out of time. Join us tomorrow night in Madison Square Garden as we have our third night in this most glorious event in SJPW history! For Akira Ono, I'm
Eddie Rose. We'll see all of you tomorrow night!
[The camera fades out on Jet Jaguar being passed to the top of back of the arena by the fans, holding the cruiserweight title above his head....]
