SJPW House Show — Chiba Central Plaza (6/20/04)

Jun 21, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
June 20th, 2004 Chiba Central Plaza: (421 fans) 1) Pramoj Ananda def Sergei Baranov (9:17) by KO after the Ananda Special 2) Dragon Mask & Toramusha def Mascara Negro & Jet Jaguar III (12:58) after Dragon Mask pinned Jaguar III following the Dragon Crush Drop. 3) "Firefly" Horo Atashi def El Gato Loco (7:20) after a Thunder Fire Driver. 4) Tsutomu Kobayashi & Botan Nakatoni def Masashi "Lightning" Lanther & Jiraiya (14:00) after Kobyashi pinned Jiraiya following a pumphandle Uranage. 5) Takahiro KEOKI def Despair, "All Star" Joey Darren & Tsunami (19:52) after a cross arm fisherman's buster. - Ananda gained a measure of revenge on Baranov for knocking him out on the 18th, knocking him senseless with his patented springboard spinning roundhouse kick. - The young duo of Dragon Mask & Toramusha looked good against Negra & Jaguar III, upsetting the more experienced duo in a fast paced match. - Horo Atashi has quietly racked up a few wins of late, proving that he will be a wild card in the upcoming Sedei-X tournament. His win over El Gato Loco tonight was just another one for the record books. - The Crush Crew & Team Raiden had one hell of a match, trading hard hitting impact moves with a mixture of aerial offense and submission holds. Lanther scored three near pinfalls on Nakatoni, each time with the Lanther Bomb, only for Kobayashi to break them up. Kobayashi later showed his contempt for Lanther by pinning his partner with a nasty Pumphandle in a Uranage! - As a special treat to the fans, a special four corners match was the main event. It was a wild affair, especially after Despair hit a belly to belly moonsault on KEOKI, only to fall to a Tsunami Driver #3 by Tsunami, whereupon Darren caught Tsunami with the Falling Star. KEOKI nailed Darren from behind with a running dropkick, sending him to the floor. With Tsunami down as well, he brought Despair to his feet, crossed his arms in front of him, holding both wrists with his left hand. He then hooked his leg, and took him up, dropping into a fisherman's buster, than hooked the leg for the three count. After the match, KEOKI got on the microphone and said his victory tonight was no fluke. He stated that at Zenmen Sensou, he would take the fight to Despair, and beat him at his own game!
