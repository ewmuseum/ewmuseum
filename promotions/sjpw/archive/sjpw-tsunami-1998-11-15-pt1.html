Tsunami, Part One

15 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Tiger Kikuchi & Akira Ono are seated at the broadcast table. Kikuchi is in his early 40's. He's has short black hair and is wearing a black SJPW blazer, white shirt, black tie and black pants. Ono is in his mid 30s with long black hair put into a pony tail and a goatee walks in from the right side. He's wearing a black suit with a white chinese shirt and black Oakley shades] TK: Greetings everyone. I am Tiger Kikuchi, and this is Akira Ono. We are getting ever closer to Sensou no Jidai, and tonight we bring you the semi-finals of the Welterweight tournament, as Jet Jaguar II of Chiimu Tora faces Tasogare no Prince, and Greg "Millionaire" Miller battles Yukio "The Warbird" Murakami! AO: Both matches should be intense affairs, as all four men strongly desire to be the first Welterweight champion!. We then have a tag team match in the Frank A Marano memorial tournament, as Heaven & Hell face Los Guererra de Pantera! TK: Then we have two matches in the All-Asian Juuryou tournament, as Akira Hashimoto faces the monstrous Onslaught, and Shinji Hamada, current leader of Chiimu Tora, battles Masashi "Lightning" Lanther, leader of Chiimu Lanther. AO: That match will be painful for us to watch, as Lanther was once our friend. More so for you, as Hamada is your protege. TK: Hai Ono-sama. But one must do what one must. TK: Our first match is one of our semi-final matches to determine the two men who will compete for the SJPW Welterweight Title at the Sensou no Jidai Supercard!! AO: I still don't believe that the main event of this so-called Super card's going to be a Welterweight match!! TK: Not just any Welterweight match, Ono. That match will determine who will be the SJPW Welterweight champion!! It is bound to be one of the most exciting and entertaining matches since SJPW's return!!! AO: I'd still rather watch Juuryou or tag team matches!! They're a lot more exciting!!! TK: This next match promises to be quite an exciting one as the American, Greg Miller, takes on "The Warbird" Yukio Murakami!!! AO: Ah! That is exciting! TK: I'm glad to see we agree on something... AO: It will be very exciting watching Murakami-san destroy that Amerikajin scum!! TK: Ono-sama!! It's true that Greg Miller has an attitude that can only be described as deplorable, but it's hardly fair to generalize all American wrestlers by the actions of one such as he! There are several American wrestlers competing in SJPW that are honarable and decent athletes. AO: This Greg Miller is neither though!! TK: But his ability cannot be denied, Ono. Let's go on up to Nishi Toju for the introduction of the match participants... ______ ___________________________________ /_ __/__ __ _____ ___ ___ _ (_)Welterweight tournament, Rd. 3 / / / (_-</ // / _ \/ _ `/ ' \/ / Greg Miller vs Yukio Murakami /_/ /___/\_,_/_//_/\_,_/_/_/_/_/________________________________/ [The camera pans to the ring, where Nishi Toju & Owada Botan wait...] Nishi: Our next contest is scheduled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit, and is a semi-final match in the SJPW WELTERWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP TOURNAMENT!!! [A hum of excitement rises up from the crowd...] NT: Introducing first... ["C.R.E.A.M. by the Wu-Tang clan plays through the PA] NT: From Sunset Boulevard, California in America... and weighing 215 pounds... GREG "MILLIONAIRE" MIL-LER! [The Millionaire struts cockily to the ring, as a murmur of disapproval goes up through the capacity crowd. Greg Miller wears a t-shirt which proudly proclaims, "I'm the Richest Man in the World!!" He climbs into the ring, and begins to strut around the ring, looking out at the crowd and mocking them...] NT: And his opponent... [The sound of flutes is heard, which is replaced by the sound of Japanese war drums...] NT: From Osaka, weighing 225 pounds...YU-KI-O "THE WARBIRD" MUR-A-KA-MI!!!! [Murakami walks determinedly to the ring, a look of concentration upon his face. He is followed by his usual contingent of his wrestling students, who carry a standard bearing his family crest and pound on war drums. As Murakami enters the ring, his entourage immediately goes to the back, leaving their teacher in the ring with his opponent...] AO: Referee Owada Botan is signalling for the bell to begin the match... TK: And this match is underway!!! And Miller RUSHES Murakami immediately!!!! AO: But Murakami DUCKS Miller's blow!!! TK: Miller stops short and turns to face his opponent... REVERSE ROLLING ELBOW by Murakami!!! AO: That sent Miller to the mat!!! TK: A devastating blow by the Warbird!! Now he PULLS Miller to his feet... WHIP into the far ropes... SPINNING KICK by Yukio Murakami!!!! AO: Miller goes down again!!! So far, he seems to be spending most of his time on the mat!!! [A buzz goes up throughout the crowd...] TK: What's this? It's Tasogare no Prince!! He's come out into the arena!! He's standing by the entrance to the locker rooms!!! AO: What's he doing here? TK: He's just standing there, holding that white orchid of his, and watching the match!!! AO: Hmph! I bet he's here to interfere!! He's wrestled a lot in America. They do that sort of thing all the time over there!!! TK: The Prince is a very honorable man, Ono. I'm sure he'll do nothing more than watch this exciting match-up. Murakami once again pulls Miller up... Goes for the WHIP... REVERSAL into a HAMMERLOCK by Greg Miller!!! AO: He's got the injured arm of Yukio Murakami!!!! I have to admit, that's very smart... TK: Greg Miller is a very intelligent wrestler, Ono. He continues to cinch in the Hammerlock, but Murakami is trying to reach the ropes! AO: A warrior like Murakami will never submit!!! TK: But if he is injured, it makes little difference!! Now Murakami makes it to the ropes and Miller RELEASES the hold!!! AO: But not without delivering a sharp BLOW to the shoulder of Murakami!!! TK: Murakami is still holding his arm in pain!!! Now Miller PLANTS Murakami with a DOUBLE ARM DDT!!!! AO: He covers Murakami!! Owada Botan makes the count... ...1... ...2... KICK OUT!!! [crowd applauds] TK: Almost a victory there for the Millionaire!!! AO: What a disgrace that would've been!! TK: Miller now with an ARMBAR on that wounded arm of Murakami's!!! AO: He's going to break the wing of the Warbird!!! TK: Again, Murakami appears to be in a great deal of pain, but his strength of will won't allow him to surrender!!! AO: Murakami's a true warrior, not like this... "Millionaire." TK: You may speak derisively of Greg Miller Ono, but he has been very effective here against Yukio Murakami!!! Miller back to his feet, maintaining his hold on Murakami's arm... and he WRENCHES the arm of Murakami!!! He's trying to destroy that injured arm!!! AO: Murakami's starting to stand... TK: But Miller TWISTS the Warbirds arm once more!!!! AO: Yes, but Murakami refuses to submit!! TK: Murakami agains grabs the ropes and FLIPS over!!!! Taking all the pressure off his arm!!! AO: And now, Murakami with a short arm clothesline on Miller!!! Miller goes to the mat!!! TK: Now the Warbird has Miller up... RUNNING POWERBOMB!!!!! He drove Miller into the canvas!!! AO: Greg Miller is getting back up to his feet... TK: And a YAKUZA KICK delivered to Greg Miller!!!! AO: Yukio Murakami has the definite upper hand now!!! TK: And he's going to take advantage of it!! He's taking Miller over to the corner... AO: It looks like he's going for the Divine Wind!!! TK: Indeed it does as Murakami sits on the top turnbuckle, he has a hold of Miller's head... AO: Forearm to the midsection of Murakami!!! The Warbird let's go of Miller's head now... TK: Miller grabs that injured arm of Murakami's... ARM DRAG takes the Warbird down from the top rope to the mat!!!!! AO: Smart wrestling by the American! TK: And now The Millionaire puts Murakami into his Bankruptcy STF!!!!! AO: That moves put an incredible amount of pressure onto the arm, and Miller has it on the injured arm of Yukio Murakami!!!! TK: Murakami's pride will never allow him to submit, but he may have no choice!!! If he doesn't submit, his arm may be permanently injured!!!! AO: Hmmph! A man like Murakami would rather be injured than submit!!! TK: That may be true Ono, but the referee will end the match before permanent injury could occur!!! AO: If Botan did that, he'd have to contend with the Warbird's wrath!!! TK: I'm sure that's one of the reasons why the referee is not ending the match yet!! Miller continues to put the pressure on Murakami's arm and leg!!!! AO: I think he may be trying to cripple the Warbird!!! TK: If Murakami doesn't find a way out of this hold immediately, he may well be!! He's trying to make his way towards the ropes!!!! AO: Miller's not letting go!! TK: But Yukio Murakami manages to grab the bottom rope!! What force of will by Murakami!!!! AO: I knew he wouldn't submit!!!! TK: Owada Botan is forcing Miller to release the hold!!!! Now Miller gets to his feet, and is yelling at the referee!! AO: Disgraceful!!! Typical American!!!! TK: Meanwhile, Yukio Murakami has gotten back to his feet!!! TK: He's coming up from behind Miller... He turns him around... KICK to the midsection of Greg Miller!!! Miller doubles over... Murakami has him... AO: THRONE OF BLOOD!!! TK: There's that RUNNING LIGER BOMB by Yukio Murakami!!! He rolls up Miller for the pin... ...1... ...2... ...3... **ding** **ding** **ding** Nishi: The winner of this match..."THE WARBIRD" YUKIO MURAKAMI!!! [crowd applauds] AO: THE WARBIRD WINS!!!! TK: Yukio Murakami advances to the final round of the SJPW Welterweight Tournament!!! But will he be able to compete at full capacity after all the damage inflicted to his arm by Greg Miller? AO: Meanwhile, Tasogare no Prince is just standing there, smiling! He sniffs that flower in his hand, and drops it before going off to the locker rooms!!! TK: "Millionaire" Greg Miller is already leaving, and now Yukio Murakami is slowly heading backstage as well, but wait! He stops, and picks up the flower left there by the Prince!! Now he continues on to the locker room with the flower in his hand... [camera shows Murakami head to the back, staring at the flower in his hand...] TK: Well our next match will definitely be an exciting one as it pits two of SJPW’s up-and-coming welterweights against each other. AO: Both men are young, both men are fast, and both men are hungry! The SJPW Welterweight Championship is turning out to be one of the most hotly-contested titles in recent memory! TK: Yes it is and while I must avoid any bias, I cannot deny enjoying watching Jet Jaguar II advance to this point. AO: But he is the last member of Chiimu Tora in this tournament! If he gets eliminated, none of your Team Tiger acolytes will have a chance at the belt! TK: Bah! We’ll see! ______ ___________________________________ /_ __/__ __ _____ ___ ___ _ (_)Welterweight Tournament, Rd 3 / / / (_-</ // / _ \/ _ `/ ' \/ /Jet Jaguar II vs Twilight Prince /_/ /___/\_,_/_//_/\_,_/_/_/_/_/________________________________/ [The camera cuts to a shot of Nishi Toju standing in the ring with his microphone in hand.] Nishi: This contest is scheduled for one fall and will produce one of the finalists to compete for the SJPW Welterweight Championship! Introducing first, fighting out of Kobe, Japan... weighing in at 190 pounds... JET JAGUAR II! ["Jet Jaguar" by Neon Bushido begins playing over the P.A. system and the crowd cheers enthusiastically as a cloud of dry ice fog forms around the entrance area. Suddenly, a slim figure comes running out with his hands raised in the air wearing full length black pants with a silver sash around his waist. The crowd applauds Jet Jaguar II loudly as he jogs quickly to the ring, slapping hands with fans, his silver mask unable to hide a brilliant smile as he hops up onto the ring apron. Grabbing the top rope, he slingshots himself over the top and into the ring where he quickly climbs up onto the second turnbuckle, raising his fist in front of his chest to give the "Team Tiger" salute.] AO: No wonder you like this boy so much! He practically idolizes you!! TK: Do you deny that he has talent? Heart? Courage?? AO: No... but I think your favoritism is cleverly camouflaged! TK: Hmph! Nishi: And his opponent... hailing from The Twilight... weighing in at 190 pounds... TASOGARE NO PRINCE! [The P.A. system begins reverberating with calm, somewhat somber cello music as the arena lights dim and purple, pink, and blue spotlights illuminate. The lights give the interior of the arena the appearance of an evening sky at sunset as a man strides through the entrance curtains with an air of grace and confidence. The crowd cheers politely as Tasogare no Prince pauses at the top of the ramp for a moment, smelling a white orchid in his right hand and smiling to himself. His eyes look to be closed as he walks slowly to the ring, enjoying the orchid's fragrance as he does. Climbing into the ring, the music fades as he walks to his corner where he removes his long purple trenchcoat to reveal a thin, yet well muscled frame. Hanging down his back between his shoulder blades, long, silvery hair waves back and forth as he moves.] AO: The fans not responding to Tasogare no Prince as warmly as they did your acolyte. TK: He’s not my acolyte! And Tasogare no Prince is a mysterious figure so it is little wonder the fans don’t know how to take him. AO: Regardless of his enigmatic nature, The Twilight Prince is every bit as good an aerialist as Jet Jaguar II so this promises to be an excellent bout. There’s the bell and the two men lock up... and the Prince pulls Jaguar in, picks him up and crunches his tailbone with an atomic drop. The Prince runs into the ropes but is caught on the rebound by a Shotay palm blow from Jet Jaguar II... oh! And a spinning leg lariat knocks Tasogare no Prince onto his back! [crowd "ooohhs"] TK: Tremendous agility on the part of Jet Jaguar II as he grabs an arm while Tasogare no Prince rises to his feet. Jaguar whips the Prince into the ropes... but gets hit with a springboard dropkick as the Twilight Prince bounces back off the top rope! Jaguar is down on the mat and the Prince hits him with a legdrop... and another!... and another! [crowd applauds] AO: Rapid-fire legdrops in quick succession as Tasogare no Prince really takes it to Jet Jaguar II. The Prince climbing up onto the top rope as Jaguar slowly rises to his feet. The crowd holds its breath as the Twilight Prince lines him up... MISSILE DROPKICK! [crowd "ooohhs" and applauds] TK: And the young Jet Jaguar II is sent flying with tremendous velocity from that impressive maneuver. The Prince quickly follows up with a pin attempt and referee Goto Kyoji is there to count: ...1... ...2... ...kickout!!! [crowd applauds] AO: Both men hitting high impact moves so far but it’s the Twilight Prince who has the advantage. He grabs a wrist and pulls Jet Jaguar II to his feet, going for an armdrag takedown... no! Jaguar counters with a spinning headscissors! TK: It’s too early in the match to count either of these two men out but Jet Jaguar II is coming back from an early disadvantage as he circles behind Tasogare no Prince, grabs him with a waistlock... and executes a German suplex! He’s going for the pin and Goto Kyoji is in position: ...1... ...shoulder up!!!! [crowd applauds] AO: He needs to wear the Twilight Prince down some more, I think. Jaguar runs into the ropes now, rebounding back... and bowling the Prince over with a running clothesline. The Prince is back up quickly as Jaguar climbs up in the corner... he leaps off... FLYING SPINNING LEG LARIAT! [crowd "ooohhs"] TK: One of the more devastating maneuvers in his arsenal and Tasogare no Prince took a very hard hit from that one. AO: Yes, but the young Jet Jaguar II isn’t following up as he climbs the ropes in the corner to give the fans the Team Tiger salute. That may win him fan support but it doesn’t score any pinfalls! TK: You’d be surprised how much adrenaline a loud cheer can give you. And Jaguar is still up in the corner as he turns around... and nails the Prince with a flying legdrop! He’s going for the cover: ...1... ...2... ...shoulder up!!! [crowd cheers] AO: A very near thing and the young kid almost won the match right there. And he’s keeping up the punishment as he hits a kneedrop... and then an elbowdrop... no! Tasogare no Prince rolled out of the way and Jaguar hits nothing but canvas! TK: Good evasion by the Twilight Prince and he needed to do something like that as he was starting to get in trouble there. Jaguar stills look to be a little fresher as he’s right on top of the Prince and sends him into the ropes with an Irish whip... no, reversal by the Prince. Jaguar rebounds off the ropes and comes back with a flying cross body press... oh no! the Prince caught him and shifts him up onto his shoulders... POWERSLAM! AO: Another show of good counter-wrestling and it seems to have done some damage as Jet Jaguar II is clutching his back. TK: Yes, that’s always been one of his weak points. He is rather prone to back injuries. AO: And Tasogare no Prince seems well aware of this fact as he pulls Jaguar up... and whips him into the turnbuckle with tremendous force! Jaguar is grimacing in pain as his back went crashing into the corner. The Prince now with the advantage as he flings Jaguar over and into the center of the ring with a snap mare takeover. TK: Jaguar sits up, his back still giving him some trouble as the Prince bounces off the ropes... oh! A devastating dropkick right into the face of Jet Jaguar II! AO: The Prince coming back with a vengeance as he pulls Jaguar upright and rocks him back on his heels with a knife-edge chop. The crowd is cheering on both men as the Prince works to wear down Jet Jaguar II. He sends him into the ropes with an Irish whip, springboard dropkick by Jet Jaguar II... no! The Prince steps out of the way and Jaguar crashes down on his back! TK: Perhaps a foolish move as he may have aggravated that injury again. He rolls onto his stomach and is groaning in pain as the Prince climbs up onto the second turnbuckle... and hits an elbowdrop right across the lower back! AO: Smart move! He’s targeting that injured back and it may well be the key to victory here. The Prince climbs up onto the top turnbuckle now and Jaguar is still face-down on the mat. The Prince leaps high into the air... SHOOTING STAR PRESS! [crowd "ooohhs"] TK: Oh no! And right across that injured back as well! You can see by the look on his face that Jaguar is in tremendous pain and the Prince rolls him over to make the pin: ...1... ...2... ...kickout!!! [crowd cheers]
