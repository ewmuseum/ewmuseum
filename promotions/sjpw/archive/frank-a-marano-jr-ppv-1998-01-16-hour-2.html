Kabuki made a big splash at Invasion, USA, when he wrestled Dan Kauffman to a draw. He's been inactive since then, and this match was put together at the last minute.
AO: It should be an excellant match, as both men are technical wrestling masters. Kabuki also has good aerial skills, but I hope this will stay mainly on the mat.
ER: Let's go to ringside, and see how it turns out then! [camera cuts to ringside] Ring Announcer: This next contest is one fall, 20 minute time limit. Introducing first, weighing in at 235 lbs, TIGER KIKUCHI!!!
["Eye Of The Tiger" plays, and Kikuchi, wearing the Black Tiger mask, comes out on the runway to a large pop from the fans. He walks slowly to the ring, slapping hands with fans as he does.]
ER: Kikuchi really hasn't done well in the last year. AO: No, his record has been lackluster, but he does give 100% every match! Ring Announcer: His opponent, from Tokyo, Japan, weighing 232 lbs...KYU KABUKI!!! ["Bad Boys" comes over the pa, and Kabuki comes out to a mixed pop. He starts to throw teddy bears into the crowd as he heads to the ring]
ER: Kabuki has a hard time with the US fans, since they only know him as an AC member. AO: Throwing teddy bears into the crowd isn't helping! [The ref gives both men last minute instructions, then calls for the bell. Kikuchi offers Kabuki his hand, and Kyu shakes it. Both men lock up, and Kabuki maneuvers Kikuchi into a hammerlock, only for Kikuchi to reverse it. Kabuki grabs the ropes, and Kikuchi breaks cleanly. They lock up again, and Kikuchi takes Kabuki over with an armdrag. Kabuki rolls to his feet, and Kikuchi rushes him.
Kabuki catches Kikuchi coming in, and takes him over with a hiptoss. Kikuchi lands hard to the mat, but is able to roll to his feet. Both men nod to each other]
ER: Both men slowly testing each other out here. AO: It will pick up speed soon enough. [Both men lock up again, and Kikuchi applies a side headlock. Kabuki whips him to the ropes, only to be sent to the mat as
Kikuchi comes off with a shoulderblock. Kikuchi runs to the ropes again, and Kabuki leap frops over him, and runs to the ropes opposite him. Both men come off the ropes, and Kabuki nails
Kikuchi with a spinwheel kick, sending the masked man to the mat!. Kabuki immediately rains elbowdrops on Kikuchi's chest, then attempts a cover. Kikuchi kicks out at one, and Kabuki drags him to his feet, then takes him over with a fireman's carry, slapping on an armbar as Kikuchi lands. He drags Kikuchi to his feet by the armbar, and applies pressure, causing Kikuchi to double over in pain]
ER: Kabuki in control now, and has Kikuchi trapped in a good old armbar! AO: This is a good wear down hold. He took Kikuchi to the mat with that spinwheel kick, then took the wind out of him with the elbowdrops. He kept him off balance with the fireman's carry, and now the armbar will wear down the arm, which will make it hard for him to execute the Tiger Driver.
[Kabuki starts to lay into Kikuchi with kicks to his chest, and
Kikuchi drops to one knee. Kabuki looks to the crowd for approval, but many people boo him instead. Kikuchi uses this distraction to grab Kabuki by the tights, and drive his head into his solar plexus. Kabuki grunts in pain, and Kikuchi drives his head into his gut three more times, causing Kabuki to release the armbar.
Kabuki doubles over in pain, and Kikuchi nails him with a european uppercut as he rises to his feet. Kabuki falls to the mat, and
Kikuchi drags him to his feet, then takes him over with a snap suplex]
ER: Kabuki let the fans distract him, and Kikuchi has taken control of the match from him! AO: Kabuki should just ignore the fans. He had the match well in his control, and he allowed the fans to distract him. Now he's paying for it!
[Kikuchi drags Kabuki to his feet, and backs him to the ropes. He drives a knee into his gut, then whips him across the ring. Kabuki comes off the ropes, and Kikuchi clotheslines him to the mat. He drags Kabuki up immediately, scoops him up, then slams him to the mat. Kikuchi runs to the ropes, then snaps an elbowdrop onto
Kabuki's chest. He grapevines the leg for a cover, but Kabuki kicks out at two. Kikuchi drags Kabuki to his feet, and starts to lay into him with european uppercuts. Kabuki staggers back from this assault, and after being hit with three uppercuts, fires back with a chop to Kikuchi's chest. Both men start to trade blows, their chests turning red from the onslaught]
ER: Neither man will give ground! And look at their chests! They're both red with welts! AO: This is a battle all right! Kikuchi is bringing out the killer instincts in Kabuki again!
[Both men continue to trade blows, then Kabuki switches tactics, and catches Kikuchi in the throat with a palm thrust. Kikuchi staggers back, clutching his throat, and Kabuki kicks him in the gut, then powerbombs him to the mat! He drags Kikuchi off the mat, then powerbombs him a second time. He attempts a cover, but
Kikuchi kicks out at two and a half. The crowd applauds this, but
Kabuki complains of a slow count. He drags Kikuchi to his feet, and sets him up for a third powerbomb, but Kikuchi slips out of his grasp, and catches Kabuki with a DDT as he comes down! The crowd cheers, and both men lie prone on the mat]
ER: What a counter by Kikuchi! He drove Kabuki's head into the mat almost as hard as he would with the Tiger Driver!
AO: Too bad that he can't take advantage of it! [The ref starts a count on both men, who look out of it. As the ref reaches ten, both men start to rise. He attempts a cover, but Kabuki kicks out at two. Kikuchi drags Kabuki to his feet, then sends him to the mat with a gutwrench suplex. Kikuchi drops the elbow, then covers, but again Kabuki kicks out at two. Kikuchi drags Kabuki off the mat again, and whips him into the corner, following behind him with a lariat. He whips Kabuki to the opposite corner, and charges after him, but Kabuki jumps up on the ropes, and sprinboards backwards, nailing Kikuchi with a leg lariat!]
ER: KYU KICK! He caught Kikuchi with the Kyu Kick! AO: But can he pin him now? [Kabuki slowly crawls over to Kikuchi, and covers, but he is able to kick out at two. Kabuki gets to his feet, a look of dissapointment on his face. He drags Kikuchi off the mat, then hammers his back with forearm shots. Kikuchi fires back with european uppercuts, and is able to force Kabuki back. Kabuki catches Kikuchi with a thrust kick, and he falls bak to the ropes.
He bounces off, and Kabuki catches him, locks his arms around him, and takes him over into a Northern Lights Suplex. The ref drops to the mat, and makes the three count]
ER: He got him! Kabuki beat Tiger Kikuchi! AO: That was a surprise ending! For a moment, it looked like
Kikuchi was going to stop Kabuki's momentum!
[Kabuki helps Kikuchi to his feet, and both men shake hands. He raises Kikuchi's arm in victory, and the crowd applauds both men]
ER: Another sign of sportsmanship tonight. AO: Kabuki respects Kikuchi. These men fought a fair, clean match, and there was no controversy about the ending. Both men can say they did their best, and didn't have to resort to shortcuts!
ER: Well, it's back to round two of the Marano tournament, as the
Brew Crew squares off with the Siberian Express.

Tournament [crowd cheers]. Introducing first, accompanied by Lady

Nyce sags again, and

ER: Hamada has really dominated this match so far. Nyce has yet to do anything! AO: And I hope it stays this way! Maybe Hamada can do what five other men could not! [Hamada drags Nyce to his feet, and attempts a lariat, but Nyce ducks under, and thumbs Hamada in the eye as he turns around. Nyce chops Hamada across the chest, causing the crowd to "Whoo". He slaps on a side headlock, and turns so he is out of the ref's view. He takes his thumb, and jabs it in Hamada's eye again, and
Hamada staggers around blinded. Nyce applies another side headlock, then takes Hamada down with a bulldog. Nyce keeps the headlock on, grinding Hamada's head with his arms]
ER: Nyce had to resort to underhanded tactics to stop Hamada's momentum. AO: Of course. Hamada, for all his faults, is a better wrestler! [Nyce drags Hamada to his feet with the headlock, then punches him in the face repeatedly, until the ref puts a count on him. Nyce breaks the headlock, snap mares Hamada down, then kneedrops his head. Hamada sits up, holding his face, and Nyce flips over him, executing a jumping neck snap. Hamada falls back to the mat, his face dazed]
ER: Nyce is working over his head. He must be preparing him for the Dreamkiller! AO: If he gets that hold on him, it will be all over. Get up Shinji! [Nyce elbowdrops Hamada repeatedly across his chest, then moves to cover. The ref counts to one and a half before Hamada kicks out, which cause the fans to cheer. Nyce drags Hamada to his feet, and whips him to the ropes. He applies a sleeper as he comes off, but
Hamada is able to shove him off to the ropes. Hamada applies a sleeper as Nyce comes off, but Nyce reverses. Hamada drops down into a jawbreaker, effectively breaking the hold]
ER: A desperation move on the part of Hamada. AO: Now he needs to regain his momentum. C'mon Shinji! [Both men lie on the mat as the ref starts his count. Nyce starts to move at 6; Hamada moves at 9. Both men make it to their feet at
13. Hamada moves towards Nyce, who kicks him in the gut, then chops him across the chest. The crowd "Whoos" again, and Hamada stands up straight, his muscles tightening. He glares at Nyce, who chops him again. Hamada seemingly ignores the blow, and grabs
Nyce, whipping him to the ropes. Hamada catches him coming off, and presses him over his head. Nyce's face betrays his shock before
Hamada drops him throat first onto the ropes. The crowd goes ballistic with their cheers. Hamada drags Nyce to his feet, then executes a gutwrench suplex. He elbowdrops him, then covers, but
Nyce kicks out at two and a quarter.]
ER: Hamada has control again. Can he keep it? AO: I hope so! He has the ability; if he can win this match, people will have to take him seriously!
[Hamada drags Nyce to his feet. He slaps on a full nelson, turning into a slam. Nyce arches on the mat, holding his back in pain, and
Hamada grabs Nyce by the legs, and turns him over into a boston crab. Nyce screams in pain, but refuses to submit when asked.
Hamada pulls back on his legs, and Nyce screams again. He reaches out for the ropes, inching towards them. Hamada pulls back again, and Nyce hesitates, then grabs the ropes, forcing Hamada to break the hold]
ER: Nyce was lucky there. Another minute or two, and I think he would have tapped out! AO: I know he would have! He's going down, and he knows it! [Hamada drags Nyce to his feet, then whips him to the ropes, powerslamming him as he comes off. He hooks the leg, but Nyce kicks out at two and a half. He gets to his feet, and lifts Nyce up on his shoulers for a torture rack. he starts to pull down on
Nyce, who somehow manages to rake Hamada across the eyes. Hamada stumbles, and Nyce slips off his shoulders, then takes him down from behind with an inverted DDT. Hamada lays on the mat, and Nyce goes to the corner, and wobbily climbs to the top rope. He looks down at Hamada, then leaps, crashing on top of him with a frog splash. The crowd 'oooohs' at this, and Nyce makes the cover, but
Hamada kicks out at two and a half. The crowd starts to chant
"Shinji! Shinji!"]
ER: Nyce has regained control of the match, but Hamada is still in it! AO: The frog splash is the prelude to some of Nyce's deadlier moves, so we may want to worry now!
[Nyce drags Hamada to his feet, then sends him back to the mat with a chop. The crowd continues to chant Hamada's name. Nyce drags
Hamada back to his feet, locks him in a double underhook, lifts him up, then drops him back down face first. He rolls Hamada over for a cover, but Hamada again kicks out at two and a half.]
ER: He kicked out of the Nyce Plex! AO: Maybe there is hope after all. [Nyce sets Hamada up, lifts him up high, then runs forward, dropping down into a Tiger Bomb. The ref drops to count, and once more Hamada kicks out at two and a half. Nyce rolls Hamada onto his chest, then starts to lock him in the Nyce Stretch, but Hamada grabs the ropes. Nyce breaks the hold, then drags Hamada to his feet. He drives a knee into his gut, then whips him into the corner. Nyce charges in after him, catching him with a clothesline.
Nyce sits Hamada on the top rope, then draws his thumb across his throat. He grabs Hamada around the head, and turns, attempting the
Nyce Finisher, but Hamada holds onto the top rope, and Nyce falls to the mat! The crowd cheers, and Hamada stands up on the top rope, steadying himself. Nyce gets to his feet, turns, and Hamada comes off the top rope, sending Nyce to the mat with a top rope lariat!]
ER: Hamada blocked the Nyce Finisher! He's taken everything Nyce gave him, and now he's got the advantage!
AO: Yes! Get him Shinji! [Hamada gets to his feet, then raises his fist to the crowd, who cheer. Hamada drags Nyce to his feet, then drops down into a DDT.
Hamada gets to his feet again, and drags Nyce to his feet. He sets him up, then hits the Blockbuster Fire Bomb. The crowd cheers again, sensing the end of the match.]
ER: He's got him! Hamada's got him! AO: Not yet! He's not finished yet! [Hamada drags Nyce to his feet again, and draws his thumb across his throat. The crowd starts to cheer, and Hamada lifts Nyce up in a vertical position, then drops down into a Tiger Driver! The crowd goes ballistic, and Hamada covers Nyce, the ref easily making the three count]
ER: He did it! We've got a new champion! AO: There is hope for him after all! Good job Hamada! Ring Announcer: Your winner, and NEW SJPW All Asian Heavyweight
Champion...SHINJI "TIGER" HAMADA!!! [The ref hands Hamada the belt, and he sinks to his knees, clutching the belt tightly. His partner, cruiserweight champion Ace Kilcannon, along with
Onslaught, come to the ring, and hoist Hamada up on their shoulders. Mr. Yamashita enters the ring, and hands Hamada his trophy. Hamada straps the belt around his waist, then takes the trophy as the crowd floods the ring with streamers]
ER: What a night this has been, and we have two more to go! AO: Tomorrow should be just as good, if not better! ER: That's all the time we have for Day One. Be sure to join us tomorrow, when we come to you LIVE from the Continental Airlines
Arena! For Akira Ono, I'm Eddie Rose. We'll see you tomorrow!
[The camera fades out on Shinji Hamada on Kilcannon & Onslaught's shoulders, holding his trophy high above his head...]
