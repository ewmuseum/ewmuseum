In trying to make a name for himself, he’s earned the wrath of not only myself, but Sung Kim as well. Akira: Have you and Sung Kim set your differences aside in dealing with Ishrinku? I’ve discovered that from our meetings. For now, our differences are secondary to dealing with Ishrinku. Make no mistake, "Demon Boy" WILL be dealt with. In fact, you’re still wearing the same bloody trunks and mask you wore that night. Steel Serpent: It is a reminder to myself. It is a reminder of my weakness. I let myself lose focus between my title match and the UWC Tournament. In the end, my temporary lack of discipline cost me both. Now, the Thai Warrior craves one thing: the Battle Arts Championship.
