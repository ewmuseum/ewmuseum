Tsunami, Hour One 1/2

18 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera cuts to the locker room area of the Nagoya Dome. Panning past the many doors with plates bearing names of SJPW stars like Shinji Hamada, Super Tengu, Tokyo Express, Ace Kilcannon and more. The area seems deserted until an excited voice breaks the silence..] [Voice]: WOW! [The camera turns towards where the voice came from, revealing the speaker and another man standing in front of a door leading into a dressing room, both of them looking at it with something approaching awe. They are both young. Very early twenties at most, and both are of Asian descent. The first man has a wiry, very well-muscled build similar to that of the late, great martial arts star Bruce Lee. His black hair is cut in a short style, and he is moderately handsome. He wears a pair of tan pants and a plain, white shirt with the sleeves rolled up. The other man is about the same height as his friend, but judging by the barrelchested, broadshouldered and burly build he is quite a bit heavier. For the most part it is a naturally powerful physique without the exaggerated muscledefinition of many powerwrestlers, although there is a good amount of definition in his arms and shoulders. His hair is also black, but long and tied back in a ponytail with a simple, short leather strap. He is wearing blue jeans and a white T-shirt with the word "Puroresu" on the back in red above a red, filled in circle (like the one on the Japanese flag) and the SJPW logo on the front. These two men are "Little Lion" Huan Feng Sho and "Raging Bull" Yagyu Retsudo, collectively referred to as the tag team "Young Warriors". Both men are carrying white and red sportsbags with SJPW logos on] Huan: Yeah.... Wow! [Retsudo breaks into a _huge_ grin and points at the door with the enthusiasm of a kid who just saw something really exciting] Retsudo: This is final proof, Huan-san! Is proof that we in bigtime now! Look! Our names on dressingroom door in Tokyo Dome! Is dream come true! [Huan looks at his burly partner with a grin and nods, then opens the door to the room and looks in] Retsudo: Well? Huan: Well what? Retsudo: You going to go in or we going to stand out in corridor looking like fools? [Retsudo grins widely at his partner and slaps him audibly on the back, drawing a grimace of pain from the smaller man as the meaty, heavy hand of the burly man impacts heavily with his back] Retsudo: Come! We get ready for Team Combattler! [With that he enters the room, shortly followed by Huan. Just before the door closes we hear Retsudo's voice again] Retsudo: WOW! We really _here_! Both of these men claim noble ancestry, and both claim they wish to purify SJPW of the foreign influence that taints it. AO: Both men are angry young men. I understand that well, as I was once like that. They need to look past their predjudices, and see everyone for what they truly are, and not judge just by appearance alone. TK: Eloquently put Ono-san. AO: Thank you Kikuchi-san. But let us not waste time in idle observation. We have a show to run!
