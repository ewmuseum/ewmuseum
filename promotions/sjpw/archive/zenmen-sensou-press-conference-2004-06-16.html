Zenmen Sensou Press Conference

Jun 17, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
June 16th, 2004 Tokyo FM Hall Super Japan CEO Yamashita held a press conference today at the Tokyo FM Hall, where SJPW was having one of it's house shows. The press conference had been advertised as regarding Zenmen Sensou, the Super Show scheduled for June 26th. Yamashita came out with Jumbo Kobayashi & Tiger Kikuchi. He greeted the press warmly, and thanked them for coming. He started off by saying that he was proud to have been named CEO of Super Japan, and that the wrestlers and staff have impressed him with their hard work ethic while he and the new management board reorganized the promotion. He also thanked the fans for their continued support of the promotion. He then went on to announce that the first Super show, Zenmen Sensou, would take place at the 5,500 seat Budokan Hall. A large video screen will be rented for use in the Hall, to aid people in viewing the matches. Yamashita then announced the matches that would take place at Zenmen Sensou: In the first of the Five Fight challenge series, Junya Toroyama will face newcomer MAMMOTH Kagawa. In the first of 3 title defenses, Tsunami will defend his IICW Cruiserweight title against "All Star" Joey Darren In a special challenge, Pramoj Ananda will face Wolf Jager in a KO vs Submission match. In a special tag team challenge match, The Nagasaki Bulldogs will face the Visual Spirits from Visual Spirt Pro. In a showcase match for the Pro Wrestling Glory promotion in the USA, "The American Champion" Chip Masters will face "Skyhigh" Bobby Skylark. "GAIJIN Extreme" Jun Suzuki will square off against Team Tiger's Noboru "Raging Bull" Nishida. Super Bakemono will defend the NCWF cruiserweight title against Masashi "Lightning" Lanther. In the finals of the Giant Baba Cup, "Ecchi Kid" Kentaro "H-Bomb" Kawasaki will face "Kyoto Tiger" Takayoshi Asakura to determine the first GPH heavyweight champion. In the main event, Despair will defend his Gunryo championship against rookie sensation Takahiro KEOKI. In a move that shocked the assembled press, Yamashita announced that the match would be a barbed wire death match! Tiger Kikuchi was shocked at this announcement, and gave Yamashita a dirty look from where he stood. Yamashita then announced that through SJPW superstar El Santo Azteca, Super Japan had inked a deal with Super Mexico Lucha Libre (SMLL), a promotion based in Tijuana, Mexico, that also caters to wrestling fans in Southern California. An exchange of talent was all ready taking place, and SMLL was working jointly with Super Japan to secure television rights in the United States. For now, the company would offer it's shows via subscription on it's website, and had compiled a half dozen matches for viewing at www.clickwrestle.com, an online site to view various indie wrestling from American and around the world. When Yamashita allowed the reporters to ask questions, the first question asked was why Suzuki would be facing Nishida, and not "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder. Yamashita replied "Due to the injuries Thunder sustained on June 12th, he will not be cleared to wrestle in time for this show. Since he is also facing Despair in America at The Skys The Limit 4, we need him in good health for his match there. It was with great sadness that we had to deny his request to face Suzuki at Zenmen Sensou. Nishida-san requested to face Suzuki in Thunder's place, and his request was granted. The next question regarded his decision to make the Gunryo title match a barbed wire match. Yamashita pointed out that the Gunryo title is for Jisatsu matches as well as mixed martial arts and wrestling, so having a specialty match as the main event seemed appropriate. When asked if this was unfair to KEOKI, Yamashita replied "I spoke with KEOKI in person regarding this match. He has assured me that he is willing to face Despair in any type of match chosen. He simply wants his opportunity to defeat Despair, and is content with the stipulations. The next question was directed towards Tiger Kikuchi, asking his thoughts on KEOKI's obsession with Despair. Kikuchi declined to comment, but it was obvious that he was very upset over this situation. Yamashita ended the Q&A session after that, and welcomed the press to stay and enjoy the event scheduled for that evening at the Hall. He left the podium, with Kikuchi & Kobayashi following him. The press were left still wondering what is going on between Kikuchi & Yamashita, as well as discussing KEOKI's chances of beating Despair in a barbed wire match. An update on the matches for Zenmen Sensou: The FMLL (Federacíon Mexicana de Lucha Libre) is sending over six competitors to amaze the crowd with their high flying style. The match will be De Dos A Tres Caidas (2/3 falls), and feature the following teams: Los Técnicos - El Arco Iris (Capitan), Cielos Azules, and Sol Brillante VS. Los Rudos - El Segundo(Capitan), Serpiente Leviatán, and Poderoso Buzz at the addition of this match saw close to 300 more tickets sold for the event within an hour of the announcement.
