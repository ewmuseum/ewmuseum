[Camera opens in the locker room of Shinji Hamada. Hamada is wearing his black wrestling tights and boots, and he's standing in front of a full length mirror, doing light reps with a curlbar. He sets the curlbar down and runs his hands through his buzzcut black hair.]
Shinji Hamada: Middleweight champion. All-Asian Champion. Yaz and myself, one on one. No titles are on the line tonight. Tonight it is about testing one another.
[Hamada sits down on the locker room bench and takes a towel out of the locker in front of him. He wipes the sweat off his forehead, then drapes the towel around his neck.]
SH: I've faced tests in the past. Super Tengu. Ace Kilcannon. Nik
Nyce. I won't make any apologies about the tests I've failed, and I won't sit here and brag about the tests I've passed. They are accomplishments in the past, and this is the present. I've changed over time, and the things I've done in the past are no longer matters which concern me. Shinji "Live" Hamada. Shinji
"Blockbuster" Hamada. The nicknames have changed, as has my attitude, as I continued to struggle to find myself. Right now it's simply Shinji Hamada, All-Asian champion.
[Shinji looks down at the floor for a long moment, arranging his thoughts, then looks back up at the camera.]
SH: I don't ask anyone to forget or forgive what I've done in my past. I am not ashamed of anything I've done. In my heart, I have always done what I felt was needed. Now I need to test myself yet again. Yaz, I respect your talents. Tonight, I will give you reason to respect * my * talents. Win or lose, you will know you have been in the toughest match of your career. The fans will get a match they will never forget....
...and Tiger Kikuchi will see what awaits him. Kikuchi, we still have matters to settle. Watch my match with Yaz carefully.
Eventually you and I * will * meet in the ring, and that day is approaching whether you realize it or not.
