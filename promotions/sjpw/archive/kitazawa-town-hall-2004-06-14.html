SJPW House Show — Kitazawa Town Hall (6/14/04)

Jun 15, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
June 14th, 2004 Kitazawa Town Hall: 250 Fans (Sellout) 1) "Firefly" Horo Atashi def El Gato Loco (5:44) after a superkick. 2) Tsunami & El Santa Azteca def "All Star" Joey Darren & Super Bakemono (13:02) after Tsunami pinned Darren following the Tsunami Driver #1. 3) "Living Legend" Tommy Fury def Frankie "The Thumper" Douglas (8:57) after a bridging Dragon Suplex. 4) Junya Toroyama & "The Wolverine" Masuhiro Hayatashi def Noboru "Raging Bull" Nishida & MAMMOTH Kagawa (11:29) after Toroyama pinned Nishida following a Jackknife Powerbomb. 5) Despair defended his Gunryo title, defeating Yatoro Hori (15:00) by making him tap out to The Human Torment. - Atashi initially had trouble with the masked luchador El Gato Loco, who seemed more of a Gunryo type competitor. Gato Loco went to use a chair, and Atashi superkicked it into his face, stunning him long enough to score the pinfall. - The rivalries of Tsunami/Darren & Azteca/DoD crossed paths in this interesting tag team bout. Darren & Bakemono shows surprisingly good team work, getting a half dozen near falls over Azteca. Azteca was able to avoid the All Star Frog Splash though, and after Tsunami was tagged in, he finished Darren off quickly with the Tsunami Driver #1, adding some more intensity to their bout at Zenmen Sensou. - Tommy Fury's first single match saw him outwrestle Douglas, easily pinning him following a Dragon Suplex with a bridge. - Toroyama, now on Team Tigers most wanted list, joined forces with the monster Hayatashi to battle the big men of Team Tiger, Toroyama's former partner (and trainer) Nishida, and the impressive rookie Kagawa. Kagawa actually took the fight to Hayatashi, showing that he could go toe to toe with the monster. After Toroyama tagged in, Kagawa tagged in Nishida, thinking the match would be won. The student showed up the teacher though, and after about five minutes of some hard hitting moves, nailed Nishida with his Jackknife Powerbomb, Hayatashi preventing Kagawa from breaking up the pin. After the match, Kagawa got on the microphone, and swore he would defeat Toroyama at Zenmen Sensou, and drive him out of Super Japan for good! - Despair made his second title defense since winning the Gunryo title, taking on former SMASH JHW champion Yatoro Hori. Hori took it to Despair in the opening minutes of the match, hitting a variety of stiff kicks on him. Despair fought back though, and did some damage with a singapore cane on the left arm & shoulder of Hori. He then worked over the arm with various armbars, causing more damage. Hori made a short comeback towards the end of the match, only to get caught in the Human Torment after Despair rammed his shoulder into the ringpost. Hori tried to fight it, but couldn't escape the hold, and was forced to tap out. After the match Despair told the crowd that he would retain his title against KEOKI at Zenmen Sensou, and proclaimed himself the *true* "Ugly American".
