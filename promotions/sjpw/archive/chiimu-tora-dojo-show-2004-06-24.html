SJPW House Show — Chiimu Tora Dojo (6/24/04)

Jun 26, 2004, 12:00:00 AM

June 24th, 2004 Chiimu Tora Dojo: 300 fans (sellout) 1) "Dragon Fury" Minobu Tetsuhara def El Gato Loco (8:29) with a Bridging German Suplex. 2) Dynamite Boy & Eiji Kid Shimura def Frankie "The Thumper" Douglas & Doug Thomas (12:12) after Shimura pinned Douglas after a tilt o'whirl driver. 3) "Living Legend" Tommy Fury def Jun Kikuchi (7:2 after a spinebuster. 4) MAMMOTH Kagawa & Noboru "Raging Bull" Nishida def Junya Toroyama & "GAIJIN Extreme" Jun Suzuki (13:47) after a double chokeslam on Toroyama. 5) El Santo Azteca, Mascara Negra & El Muerte Rojo def Super Bakemono, Tsutomou Kobayashi & Botan Nakatoni (18:00) after a Muerte Driver on Super Bakemono from the top rope. - Tetsuhara opened this last event before Zenmen Sensou with a quick and brutal match with El Gato Loco. Loco used all the dirty tactics he could, but Tetsuhara out wrestled him whenever he was able to get some advantage. In the end, a simple German Suplex with a bridge was enough to score the win. - The Team Tiger newcomers avenged their first loss, taking down the Maulers in tag team competition. After the match, Dynamite Boy told the fans in attendance they were going to show the Visual Spirits that they're not the best tag team going any longer.... - Tommy Fury continued to rack up single wins since he signed with Super Japan, this time over Team Tiger member Jun Kikuchi. - Kagawa & Nishida had their hands full with Toroyama & Suzuki, but showed much better teamwork. Toroyama and Suzuki didn't look like they trusted each other, and were sometimes hesitant to tag each other. They also didn't do many double teams, while Kagawa & Nishida did. In the end, after a MAMMOTH Charge! on Suzuki, Kagawa & Nishida gave Toroyama a double Choke Slam, Kagawa pinning Toroyama. After the match, Suzuki & Toroyama got into a shoving match that quickly turned into a brawl, with the locker room emptying to separate the two men! - The influx of SMLL talent into Super Japan has been paying off, with Azteca teaming with Mascara Negra & El Muerte Rojo (men who wouldn't normally team together in SMLL) to take on the Disciples of Despair. The Luchadors, much more accustomed to trios matches, kept the DoD off balance with fast attacks and quick tags. The DoD eventually gained the upper hand, with Kobayashi utilizing his superior size and strength to pummel his opponents. Following twin Tope Suicida's by Azteca & Negra on the Crush Crew outside of the ring, Rojo & Bakemono found themselves one on one in the ring. They exchanged blistering chops, until Bakemono kneed Rojo, and hit a Michinoku Driver. He went to go for the Bakemono Bomb, but Rojo got up, ran to the turnbuckle, and crotched Bakemono. He joined him up top, hoisted him over his shoulders, and did a top rope Muerte Driver #1 (Death Valley Driver) to secure the win for his team. After the match, Despair came out and attacked Azteca & Negra outside the ring with his Gunryo title belt, only to be hit by a corkscrew plancha by Muerte Rojo. The DoD took advantage of Despair's distraction to gang up on Rojo, until Takahiro KEOKI came out from the back, with Despair's own barbed wire covered Steel Chair, cracking it over the back of the DoD members, and even blasting Despair in the head with it, causing him to bleed profusely. As KEOKI was helping the luchadors to the back, Despair got on the microphone, and swore KEOKI's suffering at Zenmen Sensou would be something fans won't easily forget. After the event, the crowd was excited over KEOKI's new aggressive stance, and many felt the rookie sensation's chances of beating Despair at his own game had improved with his new found intensity.
