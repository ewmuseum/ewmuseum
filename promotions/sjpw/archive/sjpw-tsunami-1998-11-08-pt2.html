SJPW Card Archive

Tsunami, Part Two

8 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TK: No pin attempts so far as Lee pulls Scorpio to his feet and staggers him backward with an elbowsmash. Lee grabs an arm and sends Scorpio into the ropes with an Irish whip. Scorpio rebounds back... and surprises Lee with a charging axehandle bodyblock! AO: A punishing move that knocked Lee right off his feet. He’s back up again in a moment but Scorpio hits him with a standing dropkick and the Texan goes down once more. TK: Scorpio gaining a second wind of sorts as he grabs Lee, pulls him into the corner and stuns him with a headsmash into the turnbuckle. Scorpio pulls Lee back out of the corner and goes for... uh oh! I’m not sure what Scorpio tried to hit Lee with but "The Doomsayer" ducked it and counters with a backslide! ...1... ...2... ...kickout!!!! [crowd applauds] AO: And Lee very nearly wins the match by catching Scorpio off guard. Both men up quickly and Lee hits with a kick to the belly and then goes for a German suplex... no, Scorpio twists his way out of it and fires back with an elbowsmash. Scorpio ducks out onto the apron again as Lee shakes out the cobwebs and charges forward... SLINGSHOT BODYBLOCK! ...1... ...2... ...shoulder up!!!! [crowd applauds] TK: Another near-fall but this time for Scorpio. That took the wind out of Lee’s sails as he uses the ropes to pull himself upright... oh! And Scorpio nails him with a dropkick that knocks Lee between the ropes and down to the outside. AO: Scorpio definitely focused on winning this match as "The Doomsayer" rises on the arena floor. In the ring, Scorpio bounces off the ropes on the opposite side... races back... TWISTING CORCKSCREW PLANCHA! [crowd "ooohhs"] TK: Unbelievable! But that hurt Scorpio almost as much as it did Lee as both men are down on the floor in a heap! AO: Tremendous action as Scorpio demonstrates he may be the heavier man but that doesn’t mean he’s earthbound! He just sailed over the top rope, twisting in mid-air to come slamming down on top of Kyle Lee! TK: Believe it or not, I think Lee actually took less damage from that move as he’s the first one on his feet. I think Scorpio might have banged his head against the guard railing when he landed as he’s dazed while Lee pulls him up and rolls him back into the ring. AO: This match could still go either way as these two have really taken it to each other and are not ready to quit just yet. Lee picks up Scorpio in a fireman’s carry and places him on the top turnbuckle. He’s climbing up in front of him and... what’s he trying to do? TK: It looks like... a SUPERPLEX! [crowd "ooohhs" and applauds] AO: Again, both men took a hard shot from that move as Lee lifted Scorpio up into the air and dropped him from a tremendous height. They’re both just lying there as the referee checks on them... Lee’s rolling over onto his stomach now and is getting to his feet. TK: For a man who once used to work in the front office of a wrestling organization, I am very impressed by this man’s wrestling ability. Scorpio is also struggling to his feet but a little more slowly. Lee rocks him back on his heels with a headbutt and then kicks him in the stomach. Now he’s setting him up for a piledriver... AO: No! Scorpio grabs Lee’s legs, picks him up and slams him down with a spinebuster! The crowd is really cheering loudly now as Scorpio fights his way back from a dangerous position. TK: Scorpio with an elbowsmash and Lee staggers backward. A scoop and a powerful bodyslam plants "The Doomsayer" down onto the mat. Scorpio moving quickly now as he climbs up in the corner. He leaps off... FROG SPLASH! AO: He’s got the pin! ...1... ...2... ...3!!!! **ding** **ding** **ding** ["Carry On" by Angra begins playing over the P.A. as the crowd applauds.] Nishi: The winner of this contest match... SCORPIO!!!! [crowd applauds again, and streamers fly into the ring] TK: An exceptional bout as both men put on tremendous performances. Scorpio managed to pull off the victory but it was a very-near thing as Lee nearly put him away. AO: And "The Doomsayer" looked impressive himself, coming within inches of taking the victory in this contest. TK: Our next match features Ace Kilcannon facing off against the last remaining member of Black Sun Rising Hiroyoshi Takada. Rumor has it, this could be Takada's last match in SJPW Akira-san. AO: Hai. Black Sun Rising will be missed, but these things happen, and as they say, the show must go on. ______ ___________________________________ /_ __/__ __ _____ ___ ___ _ (_)Juuryou tournament, Round One: / / / (_-</ // / _ \/ _ `/ ' \/ /Ace Killcannon vs Hiroyoshi Takada /_/ /___/\_,_/_//_/\_,_/_/_/_/_/________________________________/ [The camera pans to the ring, where Nishi Toju is standing with referee Owada Botan...] Nishi: The following contest is one fall, 20 minute time limit. Your referee for this match is Owada Botan! [crowd cheers as Owada bows] ["Back in Black" by AC/DC starts up over the PA] Nishi: Introducing first, from Kyoto, and weighing in at 231 pounds... This is...TA-KA-DA HIRO-YO-SHI!!! [Takada walks throw the entrance way to a nice pop from the crowd. He is the study of intensity and concentration as he strides to the ring, his eyes focused straight ahead. He wears a long black leather coat that is open in the front to reveal simple black trunks and black boots. He steps into the ring, and removes the jacket, and stares down the aisle, waiting for his opponent.] ["Judgement Night" by Onyx and Biohazard blares over the PA] Nishi: His opponent, from Columbus, Ohio USA, and weighing in at 245 pounds... ACE KILCANNON!!! [Kilcannon emerges from the entrance way decked out in blue jeans, black combat boots, a thick black belt, a black SJPW t-shirt, black leather duster, black Oakleys with a red tint, and a black skull cap. He smiles and holds up his arms and soaks in a strong heel pop. He struts confidently to the ring, out at the fans, and then sneering at Takada in the ring. Takada just stares intently back at him.] TK: Kilcannon one of the most gifted athletes in the SJPW. AO: And one of the most disgusting! TK: Ono-san.... AO: No. I will not apologize, he is ill-mannered, dishonorable scum. I may have changed Kikuchi-san, but I have not changed that much. I still recognize him for the piece of garbage that he is. TK: Regardless, you must hold back those feelings, and comment without your personal beliefs interfering. AO:(accusingly) Like you do with Chiimu Tora? TK: Hai. AO: ....... [Kilcannon removes his duster, sunglasses, t-shirt and skull cap, and Owada checks him for any weapons, and then does the same to Takada. He orders them to come together in the center of the ring. He asks for a handshake, but Kilcannon shakes his head. Takada merely stares at him icely. Owada then has them go back to their corners, and then calls for the bell. Kilcannon circles around, keeping close to the ropes, as Takada bends down, keeping his center of balance low, and his arms up in a gaurd position. Kilcannon looks out to the fans, and yells at a few fans, and then moves in on Takada.] TK: They lock-up, and Takada immediately spins into an armbar, trying to force Kilcannon to the ground, but Kilcannon counters into a hammerlock. AO: And Takada spins into a hammerlock of his own. [Kilcannon winces in pain as Takada locks the hold in deep. Kilcannon slaps his shoulder, cursing under his breath, and then attempts to reverse the hold, but Takada has it locked in tight. Kilcannon looks for a back elbow, but Takada avoids it. Kilcannon attempts a heel trip, but Takada avoids that as well. Kilcannon crys out in pain and frustration as Takada cinches the hammerlock in tighter. Kilcannon is forced to get to the ropes, and Owada calls for the break. Takada breaks clean, and backs away, keeping his gaurd up. Kilcannon shakes out his arm, and stares Takada down.] TK: Takada working the arm, and that is a set-up for his Takada Lock, the Cross-Armbreaker. AO: Hai. Takada is as well rounded as Kilcannon, and I dare say he is better on the mat. TK: We shall see, we shall see. [Kilcannon circles around Takada, looking for an opening. He moves in, and they lock-up, and Kilcannon takes Takada in a side-headlock, and smiles confidently, but the smile quickly changes into a suprised grimace as Takada escapes and once again locks on the hammerlock. Kilcannon quickly scrambles to the ropes, and Owada calls for the break. Takada hesitates this time, but then breaks and backs away. Kilcannon kicks the bottom rope, and yells out, he turns to Takada, and points to him. Takada just gestures for Kilcannon to bring it on.] AO: Kilcannon is already losing his cool, he is frustrated and Takada is calm and calculated. TK: That should work into Takada's favor, but anger can fuel the fire of competition as well Ono-san. [Kilcannon moves forward again, and moves to lock up, but then switches gears in mid move, and instead drives a quick knee to the midsection of Takada, and then two more in quick secession, doubling Takada over. Kilcannon pushes Takada back to the ropes, and whips him to the farside, but Takada maintains hold of the arm with a wristlock, and quickly spins into a hammerlock, and Kilcannon crys out in pain as the fans applaud.] TK: Kilcannon once again going to the ropes...No, Takada has him and scoops him up and slams him down onto the arm. AO: He is really focusing on that one body part, and now he is going for it! [The fans buzz with excitment as Takada drops down, attempting the Takada Lock, but Kilcannon is quick to get to the ropes before he can extend the arm. Kilcannon rolls out to the floor, and the fans applaud Takada as he stands, and backs away waiting for Kilcannon to come back in.] TK: Takada showing great patience in this match while Kilcannon is yelling at the fans, and he has lost his temper quickly. AO: The sign of a warrior without proper training and proper mental attitude. He never had the first, and I don't believe he shall ever have the second. [Kilcannon steps back up to the apron, and shakes out his right arm, and then steps back into the ring. He suddenly charges across the ring at Takada, but Takada leapfrogs over him. Kilcannon springs off the ropes, and comes flying back, only to be taken over with a deep armdrag takedown, and then corraled into an armbar on the mat. Kilcannon stomps the mat in frustration as Takada locks the arm in tight.] TK: Takada once again controlling, and continues to work on the arm. [Kilcannon attempts to get to his feet, and Takada keeps the armbar locked in. Kilcannon pushes Takada back to the ropes, and then manuvers him into the corner. Owada moves in, and calls for the break. Takada cautiously releases the armbar, and Owada instructs Kilcannon to move out of the corner.] TK: Kilcannon backing away..OH! Driving shoulderblock to the midsection of Takada, and again...Repeated shoulders by Kilcannon, and now he stands upright, and smiles out to the crowd. AO: Takada springs to life, and spins Kilcannon into the corner...Blazing chops by Kilcannon in the corner! [The fans cheer as Takada fires in with stinging back hand chop after back hand chop to Kilcannon's chest. Takada then whips Kilcannon to the opposite corner, and he hits hard. Takada charges in, and leaps up, connecting with a brutal flying forearm that crushes Kilcannon in the corner as the fans cheer. Takada snapmares Kilcannon out of the corner, and attempts to lock in a Dragon Sleeper.] TK: Dragon Sleeper by Takada...Kilcannon escapes with kicks to the head. Kilcannon had the presence of mind to not get locked in the sleeper, and now he rolls to his feet, and Takada stands up, and holds his head in the corner. AO: He is briefly stunned from those kicks by Kilcannon, and now Kilcannon charges in with a running Dropkick to the face! [Kilcannon quickly gets to his feet, and whips Takada to the opposite corner, and charges in right on his heels and as Takada hits the buckles, Kilcannon catches him with a spinning leg lariat. Kilcannon snapmares Takada to the mat, and smiles before attempting a Dragon Sleeper of his own as the fans "ohhhh" and "ahhhh".] TK: Dragon Sleeper applied by Kilcannon! AO: But Takada is bridging up out of it...He gets to his feet... [The fans "ohhhh" as Kilcannon suddenly drops Takada down into a Reverse DDT.] TK: Quick move by Kilcannon, and he has Takada down. Kilcannon to his feet quickly, and he scales the ropes...He is on top...Split-Leg Moonsault! [The fans "ohhhh" and applaud as Kilcannon perfectly lands the Moonsault and then covers. Owada gets in position, but Takada kicks out at one only. Kilcannon gets to his feet, and stomps down on Takada and then pulls him up, looking out to the fans before fireing in with a forearm to the head. Kilcannon pulls back after the blow, however, wincing.] TK: Kilcannon feeling the pain of Takada's early assault on the arm, but now he maintains control with a knee to the midsection, and he whips Takada to the ropes. AO: He catches Takada...Tilt-A-Whirl Powerslam! [The fans applaud the high-impact manuver as Kilcannon jumps to his feet, and yells out at them. Takada arcs his back obviously feeling the pain of the move, but showing little of it on his face. Kilcannon grabs Takada's legs, steps through, and crosses Takada's leg around his, obviously looking for a Scorpion Deathlock. Takada fights the move, and fires in with a palm strike to Kilcannon's face. He hits another, and finally a third causes Kilcannon to release the hold, and stagger back a few paces.] TK: Takada showing his fighting ability and survival instinct, getting up off the mat, and Kilcannon runs off the ropes...Takada ducks under a lariat attempt and gets a waistlock... AO: German Suplex attempt, but Kilcannon with back elbows... TK: Kilcannon with a standing switch and now he has the waistlock, but Takada frees himself with elbows, and runs into the ropes...He comes back..Yakuza Kick! AO: Blocked by Kilcannon, he spins Takada around...Forearm to the back of the head...Waistlock..Release German Suplex. Takada lands hard, and Kilcannon quickly crawls to him, and makes the cover! ...1... ...2.... [The fans applaud as Takada kicks out before Owada counts three. Kilcannon rolls to his feet, and quickly pulls Takada to his feet. He attempts another waistlock, but Takada fires down elbows on Kilcannon's right arm, and he breaks the hold, Takada with a standing switch, and he attempts a German Suplex, but Kilcannon fires in with an elbow, and another, but Takada maintains hold. Kilcannon attempts a third back elbow, but Takada ducks under it, and locks on a front waistlock and snaps Kilcannon over into a Northern Lights Suplex and the fans applaud as Owada drops down to count.] ...1... ...2... TK: Two count only as Kilcannon escapes. Both men up, and Takada off the ropes...Yakuza Kick! [The fans cheer as Takada drops down for the pin.] ...1... ...2... AO: Kilcannon kicks out again...Takada immediately spins into the Takada Lock!![fans cheer] TK: Kilcannon has his hands locked, blocking the move, and he drags himself to the ropes, Takada levers back, trying to seperate Kilcannon's hands...Kilcannon flailing out with his legs... AO: Takada has the arm extended!!![fans cheer] TK: Kilcannon reached the ropes! [Owada immediately calls for the break, and the fans let out a disappointed "ohhhh" and then applaud Kilcannon's escape. Takada rolls to his feet, and waits for Kilcannon to stand. Kilcannon pulls himself up, favoring his right arm heavily. Takada runs back into the ropes and attempts a Yakuza Kick, and connects right to the arm and shoulder of Kilcannon and the fans let out an "ohhhh".] TK: That blow spins Kilcannon around, and he falls forward into the ropes as Takada targets the arm with that devastating running kick. AO: Takada now locks on a full nelson... [The fans buzz in anticipation of a Dragon Suplex,but Kilcannon blocks it, and drops down to his knees, falling into the ropes, and Owada steps in and seperates them.] TK: Takada backing away, as Kilcannon pulls himself up. Takada off the ropes...Flying Forearm...OH! Kilcannon side-stepped and pushed Takada to the ropes and he hit throat first across the top. AO: Baka! Blind luck for Kilcannon. [Takada lies on the mat, struggling for breath and clutching his throat as Kilcannon falls back to the ropes, shaking out his right arm. Takada staggers to his feet, and Kilcannon suddenly bursts for with a left armed Reverse Bulldog taking both men to the mat.] TK: Running Neckbreaker Drop from Kilcannon, and both men are down. AO: This match has taken it's toll, and Owada is checking on them. TK: Not to make light of this match Akira, but I am getting word that Super Tengu has still not arrived at the arena. AO: He will...I am sure he will be here. He maybe many things, but Tetsuo is not a coward. He will be here to face Tenru. TK: I hope you are right Akira-san. [Kilcannon gets to his feet, and Takada rises slowly as well, clutching the back of his head. Kilcannon moves in, and fires in with a knee to the midsection, and hooks Takada up for a Vertical Suplex. He lifts him up, and then drops him straight down into a Brainbuster illiciting "ohhhs" from the fans.] TK: Kilcannon has Takada down, and now he is heading to the top rope, where he excels. AO: Kilcannon may be scum, but he can fly with the best of them here in SJPW. [Kilcannon balances on the top, and yells out, and then leaps high into the air as cameras flash, and then he hits a pinpoint Frog Splash with such force he bounces off Takada and then drops back down for the pin.] ...1... ...2... ...3 AO: NO! Owada holds up two fingers only, Takada got the shoulder up. TK: Kilcannon getting in the face of Owada. [Takada rolls to the ropes, attempting pull himself to his feet as the crowd cheers him on. Kilcannon turns away from Takada in disgust, and fires in with kicks to the midsection of Takada and then whips him to the ropes. Kilcannon aims a superkick as Takada comes rushing back, but he ducks under it. Takada turns, and Kilcannon catches him, attempting a Choke Slam, but Takada fires in with elbows to the arm, forcing Kilcannon to release the hold.] TK: Takada now lifts Kilcannon up onto his shoulders... AO: Death Valley Driver... TK: No, Kilcannon is able to squirm free, and he drops down...Kilcannon runs off the ropes and charges as Takada turns... AO: OVERHEAD BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX!!! [The fans cheer as Takada sends Kilcannon flying up and over with an amazing belly-to-belly. Kilcannon almost landed on his head with the manuver, and Owada quickly drops down to check on him as he lays prone on the mat. Takada stirs, and rolls up to his knees, and stands on shaky legs. Kilcannon waves Owada away, and the match continues.] TK: Owada making sure that Kilcannon wasn't injured, and now Takada pulls him up, and Kilcannon is limp. AO: Takada summons his strength, and now locks Kilcannon...Gutwrench...Doctor Bomb!! [The fans cheer as Takada drives Kilcannon into the mat, and Owada drops down, checks his shoulders, and then counts.] ...1... ...2.... [The fans yell out "ohhhh" and then applaud as Kilcannon rolls his shoulder.] TK: Kilcannon escapes, and now Takada stands and attempts to pull Kilcannon back up...Kilcannon is dead weight, and Takada can't get him to his feet. [Takada lets out an angry yell, and pulls Kilcannon up right on his third try, and he attempts to scoop Kilcannon up but Kilcannon suddenly goes rigid and blocks it.] TK: Takada releases hold of Kilcannon,and now fires in with chops to the right arm of Kilcannon, driving him to one knee. [Takada pulls Kilcannon back up, and once again attempts to lift him up, but Kilcannon fights it again.] AO: It appears Takada is going for the Michinoku Driver II and Kilcannon is doing everything he can to prevent it. TK: Takada fires in with chops to the arm again, and now fires off the ropes...Yakuza kick to the shoulder of Kilcannon! [Takada looks down at Kilcannon, and as he rises, he runs to the ropes again, and again connects with a running kick to the shoulder. Kilcannon drops to one knee, and Takada looks out at the fans for a brief moment, and they cheer as he runs back to the ropes again.] TK: Yakuza Kick....Kilcannon side-steps! Takada turns and Kilcannon catches him...EXPLODER!! [The fans "ohhhh" as Kilcannon sends Takada crashing to the mat. Both men lay prone for a few seconds, and Owada checks on them.] TK: Kilcannon is fighting to get to his feet, he tries to push himself up, but his arm gives out. AO: Takada is dazed, and can only manage to get to one knee. [Kilcannon pushes himself up with his left arm, and then suddenly charges at Takada dropkicking him in the face as he is on one knee, sending him sprawling back to the mat. Kilcannon rolls to his feet, and makes his way out to the apron.] TK: Kilcannon going up to the top rope, trying another high risk move...He leaps off...Diving Headbutt... AO: TAKADA MOVED! [The fans cheer as Takada rolls clear, and Kilcannon hits nothing canvas and bounces up in the air before collapsing to the mat.] TK: Takada stands, and now he is going to the top. AO: No, he should go for the Takada Lock...Now is not the time for dangerous moves. [Takada slowly climbs up the ropes, facing out to the fans. They "ohhhh" and "ahhh" as he balances on top. Suddenly, Kilcannon surges to his feet, and charges the corner, pushing Takada's legs out from under him, and forcing him to straddle the turnbuckle.] TK: OH! Takada took too long, and Kilcannon was able to cut him off, and now Kilcannon climbs up to the second rope...He is giving the sign for the Kill! [The fans buzz with anticipation of Kilcannon's signature finisher, the Dragon Super-plex. Kilcannon reaches forward, and hooks Takada's arms, but Takada struggles and fights the hold.] AO: Takada is fighting it...He fires in with a back elbow, and Kilcannon teeters. TK: Another back elbow, and now a flurry and Kilcannon flys off the second rope, and crashes to the mat! [The fans cheer as Takada spins himself around on the top rope, and remains seated on the turnbuckle, looking drained as Kilcannon staggers to his feet.] AO: Kilcannon charges, and Takada catches him with a palm strike, and another...Takada locks on a front face-lock. [The fans cheer as Takada perpares to leap off with a swinging DDT.] TK: Kilcannon fires in with punches to the midsection, and forces Takada to release the hold. Takada with a palm strike, and Kilcannon with a forearm, but Takada fires right back, and they are exchanging blows. [The fans cheer as both men fire back and forth on each other with Takada seated on the turnbuckle, and Kilcannon standing in the ring. Kilcannon staggers back after three unawnsered blows from Takada, and now Takada grabs him, and looks for the Swinging DDT once again.] TK: Takada has him... AO: This is it... TK: Kilcannon spins free, and grabs Takada...He runs and pulls Takada off...ACER!!! [The fans "ohhh" as Kilcannon hits a Ace Crusher off the second rope on Takada. Kilcannon sits on the mat for a moment, cradling his arm, and then turns and covers Takada who is still.] ...1... ...2... ...3... **ding** **ding** **ding** ["Jugdement Night" by Onyx and Biohazard plays] Nishi: The winner of this match...ACE KILCANNON!!! [The fans applaud and several streamers are thrown into the ring as Kilcannon and Takada both remain down. Ring attendants enter the ring to check on both men, but Kilcannon pushes them away, and gets to his feet on his own. He yells down at Takada and then walks out of the ring, sneering at the fans, and cradling his right arm. Takada is helped up, but he drops to one knee, holding his jaw. The fans cheer him as he is helped out of the ring, and to the back.] TK: Well, Akira if that was Takada's last match, he went out with a great effort against one of the best in SJPW. AO: Hai, it was a fine performance, despite the fact he came up short in the end. Either man could have one the match.
