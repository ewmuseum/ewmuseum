Metro, 1/3

Eddie Rose has red hair and is wearing blue jeans, a black T-Shirt with "SJPW" written in Red on it, and a black leather jacket. Both men smile at the camera..] Eddie Rose: Hello everyone! I'm Eddie Rose, and this is Jonnie Adonis. Welcome to Metro! We've got another exciting show for you here in the Met Studio! Tonight we're going to see the debut of Daiymo member Kagemusha, as well as his current rival, Chiimu Tora member Shishi "Lion" Fujiwara! Jonnie Adonis: We'll also see the Tokyo Express in action, and their rivals, Kenji Ishida & Shinji Usami. In other action, Tasogare no Prince faces Xiamen in the second round of the Welterweight tournament, and the Yakuza face The Maulers in the tag team tournament! ER: And our main event is also a tag tournment match, as Hiryuu Batsu, comprised of Chiimu Tora members Banshee & Jet Jaguar II, face off against Daimyo members Kabuku Kid & "Samurai" Kensei Tokugawa, otherwise known as The Ronin! JA: That one should be a war! Our first match features Kagemusha, taking on Sho Nakatoni. We have these comments from the "Shadow Warrior"... ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Kagemusha /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [The screen is black. Out of the darkness, we hear...] tick tick tick tick tick "10" [A scene of Kagemusha blowing green mist into the air before a match flashes across the screen] tick tick tick tick "9" [We see Kagemusha with the STF locked onto a helpless opponent.] tick tick tick tick "8" [Kagemusha flies through the air in an Asai Moonsault] tick tick tick tick "7" [Kagemusha dives over the top rope onto an opponent on the floor] tick tick tick tick "6" [An opponent is blinded by a cloud of green mist being blown into his face by Kagemusha] tick tick tick tick "5" [Kagemusha takes an opponent to the mat with a wicked Dragon suplex.] tick tick tick tick "4" [Kagemusha and an opponent are on top of a table... the opponent is driven through the table with a Michinoku Driver.] tick tick tick tick "3" [A helpless wrestler lies motionless on the canvas as Kagemusha executes the 450 splash off the top rope] tick tick tick tick "2" [Kagemusha crowns an opponent with a brutal chairshot] tick tick tick tick "1" [A slow motion shot of the famous Jin'ei Nusumidasu shooting star press is shown.] tick tick tick tick [The screen goes black and silent.... suddenly, a disembodied voice with a Japanese accent speaks... could it be the never before heard Kagemusha?] "Get ready for the explosion, SJPW... it cannot be contained." __ ___ _____________________________________________________ / |/ /__ / /________ Debut Match: / / /|_/ / -_) __/ __/ _ \ Kagemusha vs Sho Nakatoni /_/ /_/\__/\__/_/ \___/_______________________________________/ Kagemusha made his "official" debut tonight, taking on veteran Sho Nakatoni. Nakatoni started off hot, nailing Kagemusha with a series of kicks to his body & leg, then snap mared him to the mat, where he hit a rolling senton splash for a two count. Nakatoni then attempted a German Suplex, but Kagemusha reversed the hold, driving Nakatoni's head into the mat. Kagemusha then hit a Quebrada, dragged Sho to his feet, and sent him back down with a Dragon Suplex. Kagemusha went to the top, nailed a Guillotine legdrop, then stopped as he saw Shishi Fujiwara come out from the back. Kagemusha simply smiled, hit a Doctor Bomb, went back to the top, and finished Nakatoni off with the Jin'ei Nusumidasu. Winner: Kagemusha by pinfall ER: This Kagemusha is an impressive wrestler. JA: Indeed. He has to be if he's a Daiymo member! Still, I'm wondering if he's up to the task of dealing with Fujiwara. That man is definitely chomping at the bit to get his hands on Kagemusha! ER: Very true. We're going to see Fujiwara a little later on the show. next up, we have The Tokyo Express, who were upset in the tag team tournament by The Outlaws. JA: The Tokyo Express also regained the respect of the fans it seems. They turned their backs on them awhile back, but it looks like the fans in Tokyo really warmed to them. ER: Definitely! The Express have always been favorites here! Plus, they have a grudge with Kenji Ishida & Shinji Usami, and seem to be allies of a sort with current All-Asian tag team champions The Bonecrushers. JA: Who also have a problem with The Yakuza. This title picture is very weird.... __ ___ _____________________________________________________ / |/ /__ / /________ / / /|_/ / -_) __/ __/ _ \ The Tokyo Express vs Team Angel /_/ /_/\__/\__/_/ \___/_______________________________________/ The Tokyo Express got a standing ovation as Ryu Taka led them to the ring. Team Angel jumped them at the bell, nailing Riki Kano with a double dropkick, sending him to the outside. The Angels then double-teamed Kokura in their corner, making quick tags in and out. Kokura finally got free when he was able to thumb Angel #2 in the eye, then threw him through the ropes to the floor. He tagged in Kano, then ran towards Angel #2, nailing him with a somersault plancha to the floor! He rolled Angel #2 in, and Kano went nuts on him, executing 3 backbreakers on him, then dropping down into a side suplex. Kano tagged in Kokura, and they did their camel clutch/dropkick combo, which got the fans cheering. Kokura hit a senton splash for a two count, as Angel #1 broke the count. Kano entered the ring, levelling Angel #1 with a lariat that sent him in a 360. The Express then hit a double flapjack on Angel #1, then executed a Rocket Launcher for the win. Winners: Tokyo Express by pinfall. ER: Once again, the Tokyo Express prove why they are one of the top tag teams in the world! JA: These guys are tag team specialists, and definitely one of the top contendors for the All-Asian tag titles, even if they did lose to The Outlaws. ER: Very true. I wonder how Kobayashi plans to deal with this four way situation between these teams? JA: I'm not sure. Up next, we have comments from Yukio "The Warbird" Murakami, who beat Angel Taboo this weekend to advance in the Welterweight tournament. This guy's attitude towards "gaijin" leaves a sore taste in my mouth Eddie. ER: He doesn't like us too much Jonnie, which is a shame. Still, the man is an excellant competitor, and he proved it this past sunday... ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Yukio "The Warbird" Murakami /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [The CAMERA fades in on a busy office building. Men and women in business suits move about the office performing various mundane tasks. The CAMERA tracks through the office until it reaches large oaken double-doors. The doors are opened by a large man in a suit and wearing sunglasses. The CAMERA enters the room. Inside the room, Yukio "The Warbird" Murakami sits behind a desk, talking on the telephone.] Yukio "The Warbird" Murakami: Yes, I understand. Yes, yes. It will be done. Don't worry, I will not fail you. Good-bye. [Yukio hangs up the phone. He smiles at the CAMERA.] Yukio: Konban-wa, SJPW. Welcome to Murakami, Inc. As you can see, I am not only the world's most skilled wrestler, but I am also a business man of the highest caliber. Murakami, Inc. has offices around the world. We provide our own specialized service to clients willing and able to pay our high prices. But that is none of your concern. [Yukio lights a cigar, sits back in his chair.] Yukio: On Tsunami, I showed the SJPW only a fraction of what I am capable of doing. Angel Taboo was, how should I say, an appetizer. "The Black Dragon" Masato Kurasawa shall be my first course. I don't need to remind Mr. Kurasawa that the welterweight belt will be mine. My match with him is just another warm-up. I'm not an arrogant man. I'm merely confident. I have spent my entire life honing my body into a living weapon. I will not be denied. [Yukio motions the CAMERA to look out the window at the Tokyo skyline shining in the night.] Yukio: That is the greatest city in the world. It would take a truly great man to become the king of that city. A man with vision, with dreams, with a mission. SJPW, I am that man. When I am finished with the SJPW, nothing will ever be the same. [The CAMERA fades out.] JA: Man is he cocky! Kurasawa is no pushover! ER: Murakami shouldn't overlook Kurasawa, or he could wind up on the wrong side of a three count! JA: Damn Straight!
