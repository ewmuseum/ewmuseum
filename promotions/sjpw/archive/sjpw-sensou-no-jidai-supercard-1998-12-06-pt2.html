Sensou no Jidai Supercard, Part Two

6 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Sensou no Jidai continues...] TK: Our next match will definitely be an exciting one as it puts four tag teams with a great deal of tension amongst them into the same ring. AO: The referee will have to keep a close eye on this match as it could easily get out of hand. ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / Bonecrushers/Tokyo Express vs / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Yakuza/Ishida & Usami /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [The camera cuts to a shot of Nishi Toju standing in the ring with his microphone in hand.] Nishi Toju: Ladies and gentlemen, this contest is an 8-man tag team match scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, hailing from Tokyo at a combined weight of 490 pounds... KENJI ISHIDA AND SHINJI USAMI! ["Painkiller" by Angra begins to play over the loudspeakers as two young Japanese men step through the entrance curtains wearing matching green wrestling pants with dragon emblems on them. One is short and stocky while the other is lithe and muscular and they are greeted with a polite cheer as they jog down to the ring. Pinwheels of bright green fireworks spin furiously, spitting sparks into the air as the two men climb up in opposite corners and hold their arms out to their sides.] TK: Although these two are rookies, they have demonstrated a great deal of potential as they have impressive ring skills. AO: Yes, but their disdain for The Bonecrushers might distract them from their objective... winning the match! Nishi: And their partners for this match... also hailing from Tokyo at a combined weight of 479 pounds... Geto, "The Silent Assassin" and Tazaka, "The Striking Serpent"... THE YAKUZA! [The air is filled by the sounds of "Edgecrusher" by Fear Factory pulsating over the P.A. as two Japanese men appear in the entrance, one muscular while the other is wiry. Geto, the stockier of the two, is wearing a loose black poncho-shirt with YAKUZA spelled out in blood-red kanji lettering on the back as well as a metal-plated balaclava-style mask. Tazaka is wearing a black leather jacket, also with blood-red kanji script spelling out YAKUZA on the back. Geto leads as the duo walks to the ring while the fans boo and jeer, Tazaka following in his wake. Entering the ring, they ignore Ishida and Usami as they remove their outerwear and loosen up with stretching.] TK: The Yakuza are the least popular of the four teams competing in this match and it makes you wonder how well they will be able to cooperate with Ishida and Usami. AO: From their perspective, all they want to do is get their hands on the champions. They won't be too concerned about playing 'nice'. Nishi: Introducing the first of their opponents... led to the ring by Ryu A. Taka... hailing from Tokyo at a combined weight of 513 pounds... Riki "Thunder" Kano and "Pretty Boy" Masa Kokura... TOKYO EXPRESS! ["Back In Black" by AC/DC begins thundering over the P.A. system as twin fountains of red sparks explode on either side of the entrance way. The crowd cheers enthusiastically and throws streamers into the aisle as three men step through the curtains. Ryu A. Taka leads the way wearing a gaudy golf outfit and with his Tiger Woods putter thrown over his shoulder. Behind him, the muscular Riki Kano follows closely while the tanned Masa Kokura takes his time, stopping along the way to pose and kiss a few girls. Taka remains as ringside as Tokyo Express steps into the ring, glaring over at their opponents as the arena lights dim. Golden strobe lights begin igniting throughout the arena as the sounds of a runaway train can be heard over the loudspeakers.] TK: Tokyo Express with a great deal of resentment built up against the rookies, Ishida and Usami. AO: But they have wrestled all around the world so they have the advantage in terms of experience. TK: Hai, that may play a decisive role in this bout. And we cannot forget that Rya A. Taka is at ringside and he has gotten himself involved in their matches as well. Nishi: And finally, the current reigning All Asian Tag Team Champions... hailing from Scranton, Pennsylvania, at a total combined weight of 530 pounds... Aaron Lankford and Jake Reed... THE BONECRUSHERS! [The P.A. system reverberates as "Ballbreaker" by AC/DC begins playing and two muscular men appear in the entrance. Twin beams of piercing white light shine down from the ceiling to illuminate Lankford and Reed who are greeted by a loud chorus of applause from the fans. With purposeful expressions on their faces, they walk down to the ring, slapping hands with a few enthusiastic fans while keeping a focused eye on the ring. Rolling underneath the bottom rope, they rise standing beside Tokyo Express, staring across at their opponents as a pair of roman candles fire off into the ceiling from the ringposts on their side of the ring.] TK: If The Yakuza are the least popular team in the ring right now, it is obvious that the fans have a lot of affection for the champions. AO: They may not be pretty to look at. They may not be the smoothest operators around. But The Bonecrushers are devastatingly effective. And you'll notice that Tokyo Express and The Bonecrushers have a noticeable advantage in terms of size and strength. TK: Not incredibly so. Geto and Ishida are both powerhouses in their own right but you are correct, the size difference is in favor of the champions and Tokyo Express. AO: It will be Geto starting out from The Yakuza for his team while Aaron Lankford from The Bonecrushers will be the first from his contingent into the ring. TK: There's the bell and the two men surge together in a collar-and-elbow tie-up. Geto manages to double Lankford over with a kick and then hammers him across the back of the neck with a punishing forearm. Geto wraps Lankford up... and takes him down with a T-Bone Tazplex! AO: A very impressive move and the Yakuza member starts off with the advantage. Lankford is down on the mat in a vulnerable position and Geto immediately pounces on him... and applies an STF! TK: A painful maneuver but it's doubtful Geto is going for a submission this early. I would say that he is trying to wear his opponent down and you can see Lankford expending a lot of energy as he is valiantly trying to break out of the hold. AO: He has a great deal of strength to draw on and he uses it to his advantage as he powers his way across the mat. Lankford is close to the edge... and he reaches the bottom rope, forcing Geto to break the hold. TK: And not a moment too soon as he needs to get back on his feet and turn the momentum around. Geto not giving him much chance to recover as he locks him up in a side headlock as Lankford climbs to his feet. Lankford pushing the two into the ropes... oh! And Geto drags Lankford's face across the top rope, blinding him with a ropeburn! [crowd boos] AO: The fans definitely did not like that blatant cheating as they are very vocal in their disapproval. The referee is warning Geto to behave but he ignores him as he goes for a kick to the midsection... no! Lankford blocks it, does a go-behind... and executes a waistlock takedown that puts Geto onto the mat! Lankford quickly tags out to Reed who rushes in and immediately slaps on a side headlock. TK: Largely mat wrestling thus far but we're seeing some double-teaming right now as Lankford is still in the ring. Geto is trapped as The Bonecrushers set him up, lifting him into the air... DOUBLE VERTICAL SUPLEX! [crowd "ooohhs" and applauds] AO: The momentum has shifted in favor of The Bonecrushers for the moment as Geto took a hard hit from that maneuver. The referee ushers Lankford out of the ring as Reed climbs up in one corner, leaps off... and crushes Geto with a flying splash! TK: You'll notice that all four of the teams feature one partner with greater strength while the other is a high flyer. That's an excellent combination to have as it rounds out a tag team's arsenal of potential maneuvers. AO: From an early advantage, Geto now finds himself in trouble. Reed pulls him to his feet and sends him into the turnbuckle with an Irish whip... no! Geto reverses it and it's Reed who goes crashing into the corner! TK: A lot of high-impact maneuvers being attempted as Geto somersaults forward into the corner... ROLLING CLOTHESLINE! [crowd "ooohhs"] AO: Geto being booed by the fans and here comes his partner, Tazaka, making it two-on-one. They send Reed into the ropes with an Irish whip... ow! Double spinning heel kicks! TK: Well-coordinated move as Tazaka hit a spinning heel kick to Reed's face while Geto hit a spinning heel kick to the back of his head. AO: Reed is down and he looks hurt as the referee forces Tazaka to leave the ring. Scoop and a bodyslam by Geto sends Reed back down into the canvas and now Geto makes an official tag out to Tazaka. TK: Reed is down and Tazaka sprints towards the ropes... and he bounces off the top rope to connect with a springboard guillotine legdrop! Tazaka is going for the pin and here's the count: ...1... ...2... ...shoulder up!!! [crowd applauds] AO: The Yakuza have been able to inflict a lot of damage on Jake Reed but he is far from finished. Tazaka pulls him to his feet and attempts a knifehand chop but Reed blocks it... and fires back with an enzuigiri! TK: That caught Tazaka completely by surprise and Reed makes a dive for his side of the ring where he tags out to Masa Kokura. Kokura in quickly and he grabs one of Tazaka's arms, whipping him into the ropes and hitting him with a back elbow to the jaw on the rebound. AO: Kokura full of energy as he lifts Tazaka up onto his shoulders... airplane spin as he begins to revolve around and around... wait, he's shifting him on his shoulders while he spins... DEATH VALLEY DRIVER! [crowd "ooohhs" and applauds] TK: A high impact maneuver as Kokura makes a pin attempt: ...1... ...2... ...kickout!!! [crowd applauds] AO: A very close call as Tazaka barely manages to escape in time. Kokura is getting a lot of cheers from the fans as he clambers up on top of the corner turnbuckle. He's waiting for Tazaka to stand... and sends him flying with a missile dropkick! TK: A bit of a miscalculation, however, as that kick sent Tazaka into his own corner. Battered as he is, the Yakuza member still has the wherewithal to reach up and tag in Kenji Ishida. AO: Definitely some bad blood between Ishida/Usami and Tokyo Express as Ishida rushes in only to get taken down by Kokura with a drop toehold. Kokura tags in Riki Kano and now both members of Tokyo Express are in the ring. TK: Kano the powerhouse of his team as he picks up Ishida while Kokura runs into the ropes. Ooh! And Kano slams Ishida's back into the canvas with a spinebuster slam as Kokura rebounds off the ropes... and executes a running somersault legdrop! AO: Good coordination being shown by the various teams so far but you can tell that Kano is enjoying dismantling Ishida piece by piece. He pulls him up and rocks him back on his heels with an open-hand chop. He lifts him onto his shoulders and leaps into the air... POWERSLAM! [crowd "ooohhs"] TK: Kano squashes Ishida into the mat and he's going for a pin: ...1... ...shoulder up! [crowd applauds] AO: Ishida is still too fresh to succumb this quickly. Kano still in control as he pulls Ishida upright and sends him for a ride with an Irish whip. Ishida comes rebounding back off the ropes... oh! Ishida nailed Kano with a flying shoulderblock but "Thunder" barely moved! TK: Riki Kano is the heaviest competitor of all eight wrestlers in this match and it will take some doing to knock him off his feet. AO: Ishida trying to make a comeback as he connects with a kick to the midsection on Kano. And here comes Geto into the ring to make it two-on-one! TK: A bit of double-teaming from two men who aren't ordinarily tag team partners and this answers your earlier question on how well The Yakuza will cooperate with their associates! AO: Ishida and Geto a little clumsy as they set Kano up... DOUBLE POWER BOMB! [crowd boos] TK: That took a lot out of Kano as the referee forces Geto to leave the ring. Ishida now with the advantage as he pulls the big man to his feet and whips him into the ropes... no! Kano reverses it... and hammers Ishida with a clothesline as he comes rebounding back off the ropes! AO: Kano now displaying his temper as he hammers away on Ishida with forearm smash after forearm smash. Ishida in trouble here... and he scrambles between Kano's legs, crawling quickly into his corner where he tags out to Usami. TK: Smart tactical thinking by Ishida as they needed to get a fresh man into the ring. Usami's giving up some weight and size to Kano but he's not backing off one bit as he charges forward, ducks a wild swing by Kano... ENZUIGIRI! AO: A vicious kick right to the head and Kano goes down. He's climbing back to his feet quickly but he's a little shaken as Usami grabs him in a front facelock... and takes him down with a spinning DDT! TK: He's going for some high impact moves to Kano's head, trying to take him out of the match. "Thunder" is reeling from the effects of those maneuvers but he's still fighting his way to a vertical stance. Usami sends him into the ropes with an Irish whip, Kano comes bouncing back as Usami leaps up... FRANKENSTEINER! [crowd "ooohhs" and applauds] AO: That may be the end for "Thunder" as Usami goes for the pin: ...1... ...2... ...3..no!...kickout!!! [crowd applauds] TK: So close! Usami is complaining to the referee about a slow count but Kano managed to escape the pin at the last possible second. Kokura and The Bonecrushers are out on the apron, cheering Kano on and the crowd is joining in chanting his name. Usami is distracted as he's still arguing with the referee while Kano pulls himself up in the corner. AO: Usami now seeing Kano back on his feet and he charges into the corner... ooh! And he connects with a handspring elbow right to Kano's jaw! Now Usami is signaling to his partner and here comes Kenji Ishida. TK: Kano has taken a lot of punishment but is surprising everyone with his tenacity and strength. Ishida and Usami showing some coordination as Ishida lifts Kano up while Usami climbs up in the corner. Ouch! Backbreaker by Ishida and he holds Kano over his knee as Usami leaps into the air... TOP ROPE ELBOWDROP! [crowd "ooohhs"] AO: Excellent coordination by the youngsters... but here comes Masa Kokura off the top rope! CORKSCREW PLANCHA! Kokura got tired of seeing his partner doubleteamed and flew into both Ishida and Usami! TK: We knew this match would get wild as all four men in the ring are down on the canvas. Riki Kano has taken a severe beating at the hands of Ishida and Usami but he's still struggling to get to his feet. AO: Usami is still the legal man for his team and he's crawling over into his corner, stretching out to tag in Tazaka. He gets it! TK: Tazaka charges across the ring to cut off Kano before he can make the tag... but he doesn't make it in time! Kano dives forward and tags in Aaron Lankford! AO: Six men are in the ring now as Lankford and Tazaka stand toe-to-toe, slugging it out as they trade knife-edge chops back and forth. Ishida and Usami are looking to get some revenge on Masa Kokura for that plancha earlier as they have pinned him against the ropes, punishing him with kicks and chops. TK: The referee needs to regain control of this match quickly... look at this! Riki Kano was still in the ring and despite all the punishment he's taken, he is coming to his partner's aid... AO: Oh! And Kano nails a running lariat on both Ishida and Usami that dumps all three men over the top rope and down to the floor! TK: Lankford, in the meanwhile, has caught Tazaka with a kneelift that doubles the Yakuza member over. Lankford picks him up... and crunches his tailbone with an atomic drop. AO: Tazaka being taken apart here as Lankford scoops him up and hurls him down to the canvas with a bodyslam. And the assault continues as the Bonecrusher pulls Tazaka back up to his feet right away, wraps his arms around his waist... BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX! [crowd applauds] TK: Lankford just dominating the smaller man as Tazaka is taking a pounding. On the outside, Ishida and Usami are pounding away on a bruised Kano as Kokura rolls out of the ring and rushes over to even the odds. AO: The Tokyo Express are fighting it out with Ishida and Usami on the outside as Aaron Lankford scoops up a dazed Tazaka and carries him into his corner where he makes the tag to Reed. TK: We may see the Facesmasher... yes! Lankford is hoisting Tazaka up onto his shoulders while Reed is climbing up in the corner. If he connects with that top-rope flying bulldog, this match will be over! AO: Oh! But Geto came charging in and he just kicked Lankford in the kneecap, forcing the Bonecrusher to stumble forward. Reed is caught off guard as he's up on the turnbuckle when Geto shakes the ropes... ooh! TK: That hurt! Reed slipped off the top rope and fell down, straddling the turnbuckle! Tazaka is starting to come to as he hammers away on the back of Lankford's head. AO: The referee has been distracted by the battle going on between Tokyo Express and the Ishida/Usami team on the outside so he missed Geto's interference. TK: Reed is helpless as Lankford is being double-teamed by The Yakuza. An Irish whip sends Lankford into the ropes as The Yakuza set him up for the rebound. And Geto hits a sweeping ankle kick just as Tazaka nails a spinning heel kick to the head! AO: Lankford goes down and now the referee is back to the action but he doesn't know who the legal man is. Tazaka rolls to the outside and stands on the apron long enough for Geto to tag him but Geto wasn't the legal man in the first place! TK: The Yakuza taking advantage of the wild melee going on outside the ring as I think they're going for the Sacred Blade. Geto has lifted a stunned Aaron Lankford onto his shoulders as he backs into the corner... AO: Here it comes! Tazaka somersaults over Lankford from the top turnbuckle, grabbing him around the throat on the way down... SNAPOVER DIAMOND CUTTER! [crowd "ooohhs"] TK: The Sacred Blade is one of the most devastating tag team finishers in this sport but The Yakuza had to cheat in order to hit it! AO: Here's the pin by Tazaka: ...1... ...2... ...3!!! [crowd applauds] **ding** **ding** **ding** ["Edgecrusher" by Fear Factory begins playing over the P.A. as the crowd boos loudly.] Nishi: The winners of this contest... KENJI ISHIDA, SHINJI USAMI AND THE YAKUZA!!!! [crowd boos again] TK: What an incredible match! The Yakuza score a win over the team they feel is undeserving of the tag team titles! AO: I think the Yakuza have proven themselves worthy of being number one contendors now! The Bonecrushers will have to face this team down the line for the belts... TK: We'll have to see what the championship committee has to say... AO: Please! Jumbo can make the match! They deserve it! TK: Not too long ago, you despised The Yakuza. AO: True, but I no longer judge them based on their past. What they do now is all that matters. They are skilled, and they just beat the champions. That is all that matters to me.
