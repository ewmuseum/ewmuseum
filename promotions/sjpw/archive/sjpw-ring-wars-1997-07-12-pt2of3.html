[The US National Anthem by Jimi Hendrix plays, and fans cheer as Nik Nyce enters the runway, and walks to the ring with a look of disgust on his face as he looks at Ishrinku] ER: I don't envy Nyce this match. This is right on the heels of hos attack on four men! Steel Serpent, Sung Kim, Zero Gravity and Sung Kim are no slouches! AO: Very true Eddie-san. Nyce with a chop [crowd "Whooos"], then whips Ishrinku into the railing! Ref: One! AO: Ishrinku leaps at Nyce, and catches him with a clothesline! [crowd oooohs]. Nyce is used to fighting extreme style matches, but Ishrinku is a master of them! Ishrinku now kicking and stomping away at Nyce! Ref: Two! Nyce drops an elbow on Ishrinku, then rolls him into the ring. Ishrinku backpeddles into a corner, and tries to beg off Nyce [crowd laughs] ER: Carter takes a seat at the timekeeper's table, and seems to be taking notes on Nyce's actions in the ring. Nyce meanwhile kicks Ishrinku in the gut, but Ishrinku grabs his tights, and pulls him forward, cracking his head on the top turnbuckle [crowd oooohs] AO: Carter shaking his head now, and says something to the ring announcer. Ishrinku now ramming Nyce's head into the turnbuckle repeatedly! [crowd ooooohs]. Carter looks to be dissapointed with this! Ishrinku backs away from Nyce, then charges in with a clothesline to the back of his head! [crowd ooohs] ER: Carter looks extremely dissapointed with Nyce's showing so far. Ishrinku takes Nyce down with a bodyslam, then drops a leg across his chest. Cover...one...two..kickout! [crowd applauds] AO: Carter nods his head in approval. Ishrinku drags Nyce up, and sets him up for a DDT... Nyce counters with a Northern Lights Suplex! [crowd applauds] One...two...kickout by Ishrinku! [crowd applauds] ER: Ishrinku looks in shock! He starts flailing away with punches and kicks to Nyce, but Nyce grabs his tights, and pitches him forward, sending him through the ropes to the floor! [crowd ooohs] AO: Carter nods approvingly, while Nyce gets up, and is trying to shake off the cobwebs. Ishrinku gets to his feet, and he slaps his hands on the apron. He jumps up on the apron, and starts arguing with the referee! [crowd laughs] ER: Ishrinku gets back into the ring, and charges Nyce in the corner. Nyce ducks, and Ishrinku hits the corner hard! [crowd ooohs] Nyce with a roll up...one...two...kickout! [crowd applauds] AO: Both men up, and Ishrinku swings at Nyce, who ducks the punch, and grabs him from behind...German Suplex! [crowd applauds] He dumped the "Demon Boy" on his head! Nyce drags Ishrinku to his feet, and whips him to the ropes.. he catches him coming off, and flips over into an overhead belly to belly suplex! [crowd applauds] ER: Carter now seems to be smiling, as if he approves at how Nyce is handling himself. He is still taking notes in that book of his. Nyce sets Ishrinku up... running powerbomb! [crowd oooohs] cover...one...two...thr-kickout! [crowd applauds] AO: Nyce goes to the top rope, and Carter is shaking his head 'no'. He sets himself up top... Frog Splash! [crowd ooohs] Cover...one...two...thre-kickout! [crowd applauds] ER: Nyce is really taking it to him, but Ishrinku won't fall that easily. He may be a lunatic, but he has heart, that's for sure! Nyce drags Ishrinku up, and he gouges Nyce in the eye.. Nyce staggers around blinded... Ishrinku catches him... Falling powerslam! [crowd ooohs] Carter now shaking his head. AO: Ishrinku screams at the crowd, and draws his thumb across his throat. He starts to go to the top rope, but you can see he is uncomfortable there. Nyce is getting up, and he falls onto the ropes, crotching Ishrinku! [crowd oooohs]. ER: Nyce is up now, and he heads over to the corner... NYCE FINISHER! [crowd applauds] Cover...one...two...THREE!!!!! [crowd applauds, and streamers fly into the ring] Ring Announcer: Your winner, "THE PERFECTIONIST" NIK NYCE!!! [crowd applauds again] AO: Quick thinking by Nyce turned defeat into victory. I must say that Ishrinku gave him a good fight, but he's still a lunatic who has no place in SJPW! Carter now walking up the runway, shaking his head. Nyce sees him, and grabs the microphone from the announcer. Nik Nyce: "Hey Carter. [Carter stops and turns around] Tutor this!" Take Ishrinku to the hospital, then to the mental ward! ER: Stop that Akira! The man was given a contract with SJPW, and even though we don't like him, he is still a competitor here. In America, he was the closest thing I had to a brother. He watched my back at all times. He protected me from lunatics, foresaking his own career, and you dare question his honor? Without him, I would have never won this.... ER: Thurmond is reaching into the duffle bag. He has pulled out a championship belt. AO: I recognize that as the NAEC US championship belt. Thurmond is the only US champ ever. I guess he kept the belt after the federation folded. BT: Without the Dragon, I'd be a washed up nobody, a vegetable in the hospital at the hands of a lunatic who thought he was a god. I will never forget what he did in America, EVER!!! That's why I am here, to pay him back. Once the NAEC folded, we went our separate ways. But I followed the Dragon's career in Japan. Once Vengence showed his ugly head, I jumped on the first plane to the land of the rising sun.... ER: Here comes Retribution.... AO: But where is Vengence? ER: I am sure that he cannot be far behind. BT: Oh, if it isn't the flunky. Where is your boss? AO: Retribution is yelling something. ER: He sure has the Dragon's attention, he is leaning over the ropes yelling back.... AO: NO!!!......Thurmond just clocked Tanaka with the NAEC belt!!!!!!......Retribution has climbed into the ring. He is holding Tanaka as Thurmond gives him a series of rights.....What is he doing?
