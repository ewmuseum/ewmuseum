Tsunami, Part Three

8 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Tsunami continues...] [As the referee and ring announcer hop into the ring, the CAMERA cuts back to Akira Ono and Tiger Kikuchi.] TK: Welcome back to Tsunami everyone. We still haven’t heard about Super Tengu’s appearance, Ono-san. AO: Yes, I wonder where he is. He does have a match tonight against Jensei Tenru. TK: Yes, but Super Tengu is a mysterious one. He knows how to make an impressive entrance indeed. Well, our next match pits two very large individuals with contrasting styles of wrestling, Ono-san. AO: Hai. "Mashiin" Chris K, although a hulk of an individual, is a surprisingly technical wrestler. Don’t count on him just using brute strength to win a match. The man he is wrestling, Rupan Loco, is a bruiser-style of wrestler who is slow of foot, but relies on an incredible array of high-impact suplexes and submission manuevers to put his opponent down. TK: Let’s go to the ring announcer for the introductions. ______ ___________________________________ /_ __/__ __ _____ ___ ___ _ (_)Juuryou tournament, Round One: / / / (_-</ // / _ \/ _ `/ ' \/ /Rupan Loco vs "Mashiin" Chris K /_/ /___/\_,_/_//_/\_,_/_/_/_/_/________________________________/ Nishi: Introducing first from Westchester, New York, weighing in at 235 pounds, "MASHIIN" CHRIS K!!! [The arena darkens as "Burning Heart" by Survivor begins to pump through the sound system. CHORUS In the burning Heart Just about to burst There's a quest for answers An unquenchable thirst In the darkest night Rising like a spire In the burning heart The unmistakable fire In the burning heart In the warrior's code There's no surrender Though his body says stop His spirit cries - never! Deep in our soul A quiet ember Know it's you against you It's the paradox That drives us on It's a battle of wills In the heat of attack It's the passion that kills The victory is yours alone ["Mashiin" Chris K enters the arena, walking slowly towards the ring. He looks very focused on the upcoming match, flexing his arm muscles and neck muscles, ignoring the cheers of the audience. He wears white trunks with royal blue stripes down the sides, and white boots with royal blue knee pads. Chris wears a white t-shirt to the ring which reads in black letters: Mashiin on the front and back. Also, the waist of his trunks sports Mashiin going horizontally across the front.] TK: Chris K looks very intense. No showboating from this professional wrestler. AO: Hai. Nishi: And now our next combatant, hailing from parts unknown and weighing in at 261 pounds, RU-PAN L0-CO!!! [The arena falls dark once again. After a moment of silence, the sound of howling wolves resounds through the auditorium. The audience oohs and ahhs and breaks into loud cheers as Rupan Loco, dressed in a wolf mask, body armor, a dark blue pair of long tights, and brown furry boots, comes sprinting out from the backstage area and runs down the runway, slapping hands with the fans. He slides into the ring then kneels down in the middle of the ring, holding his arms up in the arm. He then takes off his body armor and hands it to the ringside attendant.] AO: Rupan Loco takes his name from a very famous luchador who wrestled successfully in the 1970s. This Rupan Loco is a man who knows and respects the great tradition of professional wrestling. TK: Yes, certainly. Let’s wish this upstanding young man many future successes. AO: Hai. [The referee pats both wrestlers down then gives them their final instructions. Rupan Loco and Chris K shake hands. The referee then calls for the bell.] TK: Here we go! The Machine and Rupan Loco circle each other warily. Chris K holds his hand up in the air in a challenge of strength to Rupan Loco. Rupan Loco graciously accepts the hand and the two men lock both their hands in the air and begin to push down on each other. Chris K, much taller and stronger than Rupan Loco, is able to gain the advantage as he pushes Rupan Loco down to one knee. Rupan Loco looks like he is about to fall on his back...but he suddenly rolls onto his back, flipping the giant Chris K into the air and onto the mat! What a move! AO: Rupan Loco on the move now. He grabs Chris K’s right leg, wraps it around his own leg and falls onto the mat, wrenching it at a ninety degree angle! Chris K clutches his right knee in agony. TK: Rupan Loco hops back to his feet. He plants an elbow drop onto the injured right knee. Chris K slams his hand on the mat in pain. Rupan Loco, apparently following a pre-match strategy, drags Chris K by his injured leg into the middle of the ring and applies a devastating figure four leglock on Chris K’s knee! AO: Chris K looks to be in deep trouble now. He is clawing at his face in pain. The referee checks Chris K, but The Machine waves him away. Rupan Loco uses his size to apply leverage on that knee, but Chris K doesn’t seem to want to submit. What a fighter! TK: Rupan Loco releases the hold. He jumps up into the air and lands a legdrop onto the injured knee! I’m beginning to worry for the Machine’s knee. He can’t take much more of this! AO: Rupan Loco picks Chris K up, scoops him into his arms and delivers a massive release belly-to-belly suplex, sending Chris K flying across the ring! Rupan Loco keeping up the pressure, stalks over to Chris K, picks him up, throws him to the ropes...Chris K hobbles off the ropes and lands flush into a Rupan Loco’s arms! Rupan Loco, demonstrating his great strength, lifts Chris K up onto his back, and falls backwards with a fireman’s carry! [Audience oohs at the feat of strength.] TK: Rupan Loco rolls over for the pin... ...1... ...2... TK: But Chris K kicks up! He is in bad shape, but he refuses to go down! Rupan Loco goes back to work on the knee. He stomps on the knee, sending Chris K into a world of pain. He then picks up Chris K, lifts him up into the air and slams him down onto the mat in a power bomb followed up by a cradle...another pin... ...1... ...2... AO: Chris K manages to kick out of the pin! Rupan Loco doesn’t seem flustered. He adjusts his mask then moves back in to pick up Chris K. He sends Chris K back into the ropes...Chris K off the ropes, ducks a massive lariat from Rupan Loco! Chris K stops himself catching the confused Rupan Loco with his back turned! Chris K hooks his arms behind Rupan Loco’s neck in a full nelson then picks him up in the air and slams him to the mat! TK: What amazing strength! Rupan Loco clutches at his back and neck but Chris K seems too immobilized with his knee to follow up his incredible manuever. Chris K limps towards Rupan Loco, picks him up by the mask...an European uppercut! And another! Rupan Loco reels backwards into the ropes...and Chris K follows that up with a clothesline sending Rupan Loco out of the ring! AO: Very smart! Rupan Loco hits the outside hard and is squirming on the concrete. Chris K wisely doesn’t pursue Rupan Loco out of the ring and instead takes this break in the action to tend to his injured knee and to catch his breath. [The audience begins to scream for Rupan Loco to get back into the ring.] TK: The referee begins to count to ten. Rupan Loco stands uneasily and moves over to the ring. As he gets one foot onto the mat, Chris K grabs him by the mask and pulls him into the ring. Chris K throws Rupan Loco off the ropes and catches him with a power slam followed by a cover! ...1... TK: Rupan Loco still has a lot of fight left in him. He easily kicks out of the pin. AO: Yes, but you must also remember that with his injured knee, Chris K didn’t have enough strength to apply the proper leverage onto the pin. TK: Very good observation, Ono-san. Chris K once again hurls Rupan Loco to the ropes and drops the big man with a standing clothesline! He then follows that manuever with a leg drop onto the ches tof Rupan Loco. Chris K sensing that Rupan Loco is momentarily stunned, picks him back up, applies a few open hand punches to the face and chest, then cradles Rupan Loco in his arms and sends him down with an atomic drop! Rupan Loco falls to the floor, clutching his rear side! AO: Chris K easily picks Rupan Loco up and delivers a scoop slam, knocking the air out of Rupan Loco. TK: Chris K waves his hand exhaustedly in the air, signaling to the fans that he is about to apply his finisher, the Total Package. [Fans erupt in anticipation.] TK: Chris K wraps his arms around Rupan Loco’s abdomen and attempts to lift him up into the air...but he can’t find the leverage! His knee gives out from under him and he falls down to one knee! [Audience oohs in sympathy.] AO: This injury might be serious. Rupan Loco senses the opening and he elbows Chris K in the face...followed by another elbow...he then grabs Chris K’s head and wedges it between his legs and delivers a bruising piledriver into the mat! Chris K’s body lies limp on the floor! TK: Both men are completely exhausted. Rupan Loco falls down for the pin, locking Chris K’s good leg with his arms... AO: Very intelligent. Chris K cannot possibly kick up with his injured knee only... ...1... ...2... ...3... **ding** **ding** **ding** Nishi: And our winner, Rupan Loco! [Wolf howls resound through the auditorium as the audience begins to cheer loudly. The referee holds up Rupan Loco’s hand. Chris K continues to clutch painfully at his injured right knee. Rupan Loco bends down to check on Chris K then motions for the ringside attendants. Paramedics with a stretcher rush into the arena and head for the ring. Rupan Loco then helps the paramedics get Chris K onto the stretcher. The audience cheers.] TK: What a fine young man! We need more wrestlers like him in professional wrestling, Ono-san. AO: Hai, Kickuchi-san. I hope all the Daimyo is watching this. One need not be a trickster to win and gain respect in this federation. TK: I agree with you there, Ono-san. Speaking of the Daimyo, Kensei Tokugawa is in our next match, facing El Gato Diablo. AO: Diablo is a fine luchador, but he's never really found his niche in SJPW. Tonight, he could change all that. TK: Or will "The Samurai" score another win for The Daimyo? ______ ___________________________________ /_ __/__ __ _____ ___ ___ _ (_)Juuryou tournament, Round One: / / / (_-</ // / _ \/ _ `/ ' \/ /Kensei Tokugawa vs El Gato Diablo /_/ /___/\_,_/_//_/\_,_/_/_/_/_/________________________________/ [The camera pans to the ring, where Nishi Toju & Goto Kyoji wait...] Nishi: This next contest is one fall, 20 minute time limit. Introducing first, from Monterrey, Mexico, and weighing 224 pounds...EL GA-TO DI-A-BLO!!! ["For Whom The Bell Tolls" by Metallica plays over the PA, and El Gato Diablo comes out. His masked face betrays no expression, and he slowly walks to the ring, slapping hands with some fans...] AO: It looks like El Gato Diablo is taking this match very seriously... [Sammy Hagar’s "Marching to Mars" plays over the PA] Nishi: His opponent, representing THE DAIYMO, from Nagoya, and weighing in at 265 pounds..."THE SAMURAI" KEN-SEI TO-KU-GA-WA!!! [Kensei Tokugawa walks out from behind the entrance way curtains. He’s wearing Samurai armor with a kendo stick strapped to his back. The armor is worn and tattered from many years of battle. The crowd cheers for the Enforcer of the Daimyo as he enters the ring. He slides off his armor and hands it to the ring side attendant. He places his kendo stick outside the ring, leaning it between the ringsteps and the apron. With a stoic expression, Kensei awaits the match to begin] TK: Tokugawa is a man on a mission. He claims it is his time now to shine, and not to walk in Kabuki Kid's shadow. AO: Goto gives both men final instructions, then calls for the bell. They lock up, and Tokugawa forces Diablo to the ropes... reverse knife edge chop by Tokugawa! [crowd "Whooos"] Another [crowd "Whooos"]...Another! [crowd "Whooos"] Tokugawa attempts an irish whip, but Diablo reverses...Tokugawa ducks the spinwheel kick, goes to the ropes, and comes off...leap frog by Diablo, and Tokugawa goes to the ropes again..DROPKICK by Diablo stops Tokugawa dead in his tracks! TK: Tokugawa staggers back, and Diablo rolls forward, springing into a clothesline that sends Tokugawa over the ropes to the floor! [crowd "oooohs"] Diablo runs to the corner, and stepladders to the top rope...Tokugawa gets to his feet, looks for Diablo...Diablo leaps...HURRICANRANA!! [crowd "ooohs", then applauds] AO: Tokugawa is able to roll to his feet, and he charges Diablo, sending him to the floor with a vicious lariat! [crowd "oooohs"] Tokugawa now stomping away at Diablo's head! Goto: One! TK: Tokugawa drags Diablo to his feet, and attempts to whip him into the railing, but Diablo reverses! [crowd "oooohs"] Goto: Two! AO: Diablo charges Tokugawa and leaps, but Tokugawa catches him, and hotshots him onto the railing! [crowd "oooohs"] Goto: Three! TK: Tokugawa scoops Diablo into his arms, then runs towards the ringpost, ramming Diablo's back into it! [crowd "oooohs"] Tokugawa spins around, and slams Diablo hard to the floor! Goto: Four!
