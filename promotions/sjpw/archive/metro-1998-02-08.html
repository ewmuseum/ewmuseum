SJPW Metro, 8 February 1998

February 8, 1998

Suddenly, a loud whistle is followed by an explosion, with the footage of the intersection replaced by clips from various SJPW Ring Wars and Pay-Per-View events as "Too Close To Hate" by Sevendust plays in the background.

Jonnie Adonis & Akira
Ono are sitting there, headsets on...]
Jonnie Adonis: Hello everyone, and welcome to Metro! I'm Jonnie
Adonis, and I'm joined by the Japanese legend, Akira Ono!
[Ono nods his head] Akira Ono: Thank you Jonnie-san! I bet you are glad to have me here instead of that buffoon you had last week!
JA: Well, Jesse is an interesting character. AO: He's a moron! JA: Well, he's Jesse Reynolds! AO: And that explains it all? JA: Sort of. AO: I think he's insane. JA: Well, we could talk about Jesse all night, or we could get this show underway. AO: By all means, get this show underway. JA: Thank you Akira. AO: Your welcome. JA: Let's start off with a recap of this past week's Ring Wars.. [The results pop onto the screen] SJPW Ring Wars, 2/5/98, Osaka Dome, Osaka, Japan ------------------------------------------------------------------- Jinsei Shakanuzi def Kendo Masao via Submission Akira Hashimoto & Dutch McNeely battled to a double KO "The Perfectionist" Nik Nyce & Survival def Dan Kauffman & Randy Acorn All Asian tag title match: Black Sun Rising & The Brew Crew battled to a time limit draw N.P.A. women's match: Astrogirl def Susan K'uang-yin Ghost def Greg "Millionaire" Miller via Submission "Demon Boy" Ishrinku & "Hardkore" Jonnie Valentine def Battle Arts champion Steel Serpent & Toshiaki "The Pyro" Takada
Ed Dean & "Too Sexy" Lance Smith def WWGP World Champion Mr. Big &
Onslaught via Submission
------------------------------------------------------------------- [Screen fades for a second, then focuses in on the broadcast position...] JA: An exciting card, and you were there live! AO: I'm always there live Jonnie-san. It's my job! JA: Well, let's not slow things down. Let's go to our first matchup! Tiger Kikuchi vs Eizo Usami --------------------------- Usami started off strong with a series of dropkicks that took
Kikuchi off balance. Usami then scoop slammed Kikuchi, hit a springboard moonsault, then covered for a two count. Usami then went for a superplex, but Kikuchi blocked it, and gourdbusted
Usami off the top rope. He locked Usami in the STF, but Usami grabbed the ropes. Kikuchi then pummeled Usami with a series of forearm uppercuts, then whipped him to the ropes. Kikuchi hit a spinebuster on Usami, dragged his opponent back to his feet, hit the Tiger Driver, and pinned him for the win.
WINNER: Tiger Kikuchi via pinfall in 4:39 JA: Kikuchi showing why he's not too old to compete with the superstars of SJPW! AO: Tiger Kikuchi is a long time veteran in Japan. He may be older than many of these other wrestlers, but his has a vast amount of experience they lack.
JA: Very true. Our next match pits Takao Nakami against Haruki
Shiomi. AO: Nakami was impressive in his return to SJPW, but he failed to defeat El Gato Diablo. Shiomi may not be as tough an opponent, but he may be too much for him.
Takao Nakami vs Haruki Shiomi ----------------------------- Nakami totally dominated Shiomi from the start of the bell, armdragging him all around the ring. Shiomi raked Nakami in the eyes, then went for an irish whip. He went for a spinning backbreaker, but Nakami countered with a flying rana. He leveled
Shiomi with a lariat. powerbombed him, then hit a shooting star press to secure the win.
WINNER: Takao Nakami via pinfall in 3:57 JA: That was a quick win for Nakami. AO: I just hope he can keep this momentum up when he faces Lanther this week on Ring Wars. JA: Speaking of momentum, we have comments from a team that had alot of momentum going into the Frank A Marano Jr. tag team tournament.
AO: The NorthPac? JA: No, the Yakuza. AO: SCUM! CRIMINALS! JA: Calm down Akira. AO: I hate those two men (The scene is a traditional Japanese tea room. Quiet Japanese music plays in the background, as traditionally-clad geishas putter around quietly. As the camera pans around the room, we spy to large figures sitting in the back of the room. Both are wearing traditional black silk kimonos, with silver knji script on the back.... one which reads "honour", the other which reads "duty".
The rest of the people in the tea room are giving them a WIDE berth, casting furtive glances at them, and muttering quietly.
However, as the camera comes closer, we see Geto and Tazaka, sitting quietly sipping tea, oblivious to the commotion they are causing. As the camera finally closes in, Tazaka looks up, visibly annoyed.)
TAZAKA: Have you no respect for the tradition of TEMAE? Baka.... what should I expect by a promotion run by a GAIJIN. So, you wish another 'soundbite' for your pathetic little show? Fine. Down
Boys... it has been one week... and we have not had our challenge accepted. Continue to ignore us at your peril. It would be....
UNFORTUNATE.... if we had to take actions OUTSIDE the ring, which might have consequences on your performance INSIDE the ring.
(Geto looks up, smirking slightly, before returning to his tea.) TAZAKA: However, until we face you for OUR belts.... we must retain our edge. Genyosha..... why we would have to face you again is unknown to us. We have already BEATEN you. You are no challenge to us. Shigeru..... if you are such a great SUMOTORI.... then why are you not a YOKOZUNA? Obviously, the very fact that you must compete in the SJPW to get your victories demonstrates to us that you are already a beaten man. And the one they call Ghost..... you DARE to speak to us of dishonour? Bite your tongue, BOY, lest we remove it for your insolence!! It was YOUR rash impetuousness which cost you your last match... and you dishonour your partner by fighting as an idividual.... although I do not blame you. As a team, you are going nowhere....... except straight to hell, at our hands. Yes, we are forced to face you again, Genyosha..... last time, we beat you in order to achieve a greater goal.... this time, we will beat you for your insolence at challenging our honour, for your stupidity of thinking yourselves better than the Yakuza.... and simply because it will bring us great pleasure..... to cause you great pain.
[screen fades, then reopens on the broadcast position...] JA: The Yakuza seem to have accepted the challenge of the Genyosha for another match. AO: Good for them. These two men are ignorant fools. They claimed
SJPW is run by a Gaijin! Last time I checked, it was run by Mr.
Yamashita, who may be a senile worm, but he is Japanese! JA: The Yakuza are also still focused on the Down Boys, and the
WWGP world tag team titles.
AO: Those fools can make all the threats they want. They are not the number one contendors, so they must wait their turn. Besides, they lost to the Down Boys, and they can't beat Fatal Attraction in Wild West Wrestling. These men have LOSER stamped literally on their foreheads! [laughs]
JA: Aren't we testy tonight? AO: Testy? These men are scum, and as such, deserve NO RESPECT from anyone! JA: If you say so Akira. AO: I always do. JA: Up next we have Asamiya & Asakino taking on the Golden Boys. AO: Squash! Asamiya & Asakino vs The Golden Boys ------------------------------------ The cousins never gave the Golden Boys a chance. As soon as the bell rang, they double dropkicked Keith out of the ring, then proceeded to double team Jerry. After a spike piledriver they nailed Jerry with the Rocket Launcher, and made a quick three count
WINNERS: Asamiya & Asakino via pinfall in 2:26 JA: Talk about short work! AO: I told you it would be a squash! JA: And you were right. AO: I always am. JA: Uh-huh. Our next segment is the House Show Report for this weekend. We had alot of great action this past weekend.
AO: We always do! House Show Report Feb 6-Feb 8, 1998 ------------------------------------ 2/6 Sapporro Sports Complex, Sapporro ------------------------------------- Onslaught & Mitsunori Takizawa battled to a double KO at 12:27
[Battle Arts match] Big Chill def Joey Rappoport by pinfall after the Deep Freeze at 10:53 Black Sun Rising retained the All Asian tag titles after defeating the Japanese Dragons by pinfall after the Rising Star Driver at
12:15 Akira Hashimoto def Jinsei Shakanuzi by submission after the kata-ha-jime at 16:45 Akira Saito def El Gato Diablo by pinfall after the Fisherman's
Suplex at 9:16 The Down Boys & The Tokyo Express battled to a 45 minute time limit draw [Down Boys retain WWGP tag team titles]
Kyu def "Samurai" Kensei Tokugawa by submission after the Kyu
Killer at 28:04 Shinji Hamada retained the All Asian title by defeating Masashi
"Lightning" Lanther by pinfall after the Tiger Driver at 29:18
Highlight: Shinji Hamada vs Masashi Lanther: Lanther was on fire the whole match, using aerial moves to confound Hamada. His big mistake came when he went for a top rope hurricanran, and Hamada countered with the Blockbuster Fire Bomb. Not content to pin him there, Hamada sent a message to Tiger Kikuchi by nailing Lanther with the Tiger Driver, then pinning him.
2/7 Koruken Hall, Tokyo ----------------------- Alex Akira def Ed Dean by pinfall after the Tiger Superplex at 14:24 Sandman Jr. def Toshiaki "The Pyro" Takada by pinfall after a small package at 14:03 [Battle Arts match]
The NorthPac & Yakuza battled to a 30 minute time limit draw Tiger Claw def Dutch McNeely by pinfall after the Golden Tiger
Strike at 11:22 Survival & Dan Kauffman battled to a double countout at 10:15 The Ronin def Jet Jaguar & Takao Nakami by pinfall after a double flapjack at 15:07 Blackline & "Too Sexy" Lance Smith battled to a double DQ after Ed
Dean & Alex Akira interfered.
Steel Serpent def Dragon Special by pinfall after the Dragon's
Tail at 9:47 Highlight: NorthPac vs Yakuza: Both teams were giving 100% the whole match, and fans were on the feet many times. The NorthPac had the early advantage, scoring a dozen near falls. Towards the end of the match the Yakuza came back, and had just hit the Sacred
Blade on Saito when the bell sounded, ending the match in a time limit draw.
2/8 Nagoya Dome, Nagoya ----------------------- Dark Dragon def "Constable" Tom Turner by pinfall after a Northern
Lights Suplex at 12:24 Sammy the Kid def Big Chill by pinfall after It's All Over at 11:17 The Japanese Dragons def The Sampson Brothers by countout at 11:09 Ed Dean def Tiger Claw by pinfall after the Dragon Suplex at 15:00 Dan Kauffman def Joey Rappoport by pinfall after a top rope sunset flip at 18:34 The Bonecrushers def The Brew Crew by pinfall after the
Facesmasher at 13:49 Greg "Millionaire" Miller & Ghost battled to a 15 minute time limit draw Shinji Hamada & "The Perfectionist" Nik Nyce battled to a 45 minute draw [Hamada retains All Asian title]
Highlight: Ed Dean vs Tiger Claw: The "Ugly American" handed the Syndicate member his first loss in singles action after a grueling battle. Dean was obviously the stronger of the two, but Tiger Claw was the quicker. Both men constantly traded stiff kicks during the match. The end came when Claw was going for Golden Tiger Strike.
Dean rolled out of the way, and Claw's knee hit the mat hard. Dean locked Claw in a full nelson, lifted him to a standing position, then hit a Dragon Suplex, bridging back for the win. Dean attempted to beat on Tiger Claw after the match, but Blackline stormed the ring with a chair, and drove him away.
[screen fades for a moment, then reopens on the broadcast area...] JA: Once again, SJPW's house shows prove to be as exciting as our television shows. AO: We do have the best wrestling in all of Japan! JA: That is true Akira. AO: I told you: I always speak the truth. JA: If you say so. Up next, Survival takes on Frankie "The
Thumper" Douglas, a man he's met long ago.
AO: Douglas is a bum, Survival is a bum. Who cares? Survival vs Frankie "Thumper" Douglas ------------------------------------- Douglas actually dominated the match early when he eyeraked
Survival, then choked him on the ropes. He rammed his head into the turnbuckles repeatedly, then german suplexed him out of the corner for a two count. Douglas went for a second rope elbowdrop, but Survival rolled out of the way, then proceeded to take out
Douglas' arm with stomps, armbars, and elbowsmashes to the arm.
After he weakened Douglas enough, he sat him on the top rope, and hit his Atomic Bomb crucifix powerbomb from the second rope, and quickly pinned Douglas for the win.
WINNER: Survival by pinfall at 4:15 JA: Another short match. AO: Big deal. Both of them suck anyway! JA: Survival is an extremely talented wrestler Akira. AO: Really? And have you shined his shoes yet? JA[turning red]: You had to bring that up, didn't you? AO: Of course. [smiles] JA: Well, we can discuss that later. Up next was a press conference held by Hiro Shinjisaki. Hiro was once the major star in a rival promotion, but was recently let go from his contract. SJPW snatched him up immediately, and he will be making his debut soon.
AO: Hiro Shinjisaki is an over the hill, washed up loser! He's as bad as Hulk Hogan! JA: Your opinion wouldn't be biased due to the fact that he beat you shortly before you retired, would it?
AO: Of course not! I am not ashamed of any loss from my career. Hiro defeated me fairly, even if I *was* the better wrestler. JA: Uh-huh... [A decently tall Japanese male steps up onto a stage behind a podium as a polite crowd begin to clap. This man is Hiro
Shinjisaki, SJPW's latest signee. He looks up at the crowd and smiles once again. He wears a pair of black sunglasses and a black leather jacket. His hair is long and black, fixed in a pony tail directly on the top of his head. The jacket is partially open revealing his nicely cut chest. He grabs the microphone and makes a hand signal to the crowd attempting to get them to quiet up a bit and settle down. He taps the microphone to ensure it's quality and then hastily begins his speech.]
HIRO SHINJISAKI - The phones obnoxiously rang 10 at a time, the faxes poured in leaving a sea of papers on the floor and the e-mails were a plenty in my Japan On-Line mailbox. Yes, folks, the news was out, I had been released from my contract with a rival
Japanese wrestling organization, which will not be named. [few gasps, few boos] Apparently you find it as suprising as I did that these people could just ... could just dump off the man who single handedly carried their promotion on his back for ten years. Ten long and strenuous years. Well, for those of you who are suprised,
I've got something to tell you. Don't be. It's a dog eat dog
World out there, and these businessmen will stop at nothing to save a dollar or two. Even if it means stabbing their ICON in the back and degrading him in front of his people.
[Shinjisaki removes the microphone from the podium and slowly begins pacing around with his hands behind his back. He then puts the mic back up to his face and continues.]
HIRO SHINJISAKI - They told me I was getting too old. They also told me that their CruiserWeight division was their star attraction and there was no room on the top of the card for a 310 pound 36 year old man. [grin] Most people told me not to bother going to
SJPW for a contract because they'd be just as hesitant to have me under contract as the last organization. As a 36 year old professional wrestler, I'm commonly thought of as 'over the hill'.
And SJPW? Apparently they've got their share of young up and coming stars, over here in SJPW, as well. I'm talking about "Tiger"
Hamada, I'm talking about the Kabuki Kid, Even the Sammy the Kids of the World. Hell, Akira Hashimoto, and the list goes on and on.
But I will prove to you out there [looks deep into the crowd] and to everybody at home wondering what SJPW was thinking when they let me put my name in ink that I'm not washed up, I'm not out of shape,
I'm in the best condition of my life. And if you want me to display that, I will graciously do so because I'm hoping that my
SJPW debut will be against a man considerably younger than me.
[smile] In my SJPW debut I would like to take on a man who I have a considerable amount of respect for. Somebody who can give me a challenge, a run for my money. And until I find that man, you won't be hearing much from me. Now before my departure, I'd like to just let you know one thing ...
[Shinjisaki smiles.] HIRO SHINJISAKI - Maybe I can't do a moonsault. Maybe a shooting star press is out of the question, too. But what I can do, is be the cornerstone of the SJPW. You don't have to be a high flying lucha wizard to succeed in the wrestling World, contrary to many people's beleifs. Hell, my double axehandle could take Yazzy's
Asai any day. So I guess what I'm trying to say is, I'm looking for a chance to prove myself, because god know's I've got a lot to prove these days. I was let go once, I will not suffer the same fate twice. So I'm just itching for SJPW to give me some competition so I can _prove_ my worthiness. That will be all.
[The crowd claps as Shinjisaki removes his sunglasses, folds them up and hangs them from the front breast pocket of his shirt. He flexes for the crowd as they continue to cheer, the scene fades to black for a moment, then reappears at the broadcast position in the Met Studio...]
JA: Interesting words from Hiro! AO: Bah! He's playing up to the press again. Wait until he gets his jaw rattled from men like Onslaught or Ed Dean!
JA: Those would be some interesting matches Akira! AO: Of course they would. Men I can't stand beating each other's brains in! JA[shaking his head]: You never stop, do you? AO: Never. JA: Well, it's time for our main event. As per Mr. Yamashita's instructions, Black Sun Rising once again defend their All Asian
Tag titles against the Brew Crew.
AO: The Brew Crew impressed me this past week. However, Black Sun
Rising are a great team for a new team. I think my tutelage has helped them alot. Alex Akira is a rookie on the rise, and
Blackline is one of the most underestimated wrestlers in SJPW. JA: Of course, your opinion is biased, since you did help bring them together. AO: And I don't deny that. But their record speaks for itself. JA: Yes, it does. Black Sun Rising vs Brew Crew [All Asian Title Match] ----------------------------------------------------- Just like their last confrontation, both teams started to brawl as soon as the bell sounded. Flood target Blackline, while Haggarty matched up with Alex Akira. Akira soon got the upper hand over
Haggarty, and threw him out of the ring. He then went to
Blackline's aid, and the champions nailed Flood with a double clothesline. Akira had Blackline go to the top rope, and as Akira side suplexed him, Blackline flew off the top with a frog splash.
Haggarty made the save, but Akira came in, and the champs double-teamed him as well. Flood was able to make a comeback, and
DDT'd Akira, and both men barely made it to the 20 count. They tagged in their partners, and Blackline toyed with Haggarty by using armdrags and hiptosses on him. Blackline went for one hiptoss too many though, and Haggarty countered with a bulldog. The
Brew Crew then took it to Blackline, first with a double suplex, then with a double elbowdrop to his chest. Blackline kicked out at two, and Flood spiked him with a piledriver. Flood hit a fistrop off the second rope, and went for the pin, but Akira broke it up.
Haggarty attacked Akira, and Flood attempted to piledrive Blackline again, but he countered with a backdrop. Blackline nailed
Flood with a superkick out of desperation. Haggarty attempted to prevent him tagging out, but Blackline was able to reach Akira.
Alex went off on Flood, belly to belly suplexing him 3 times. He went for an abdominal stretch, but Flood hiptossed him over, and tagged in Haggarty. Haggarty and Akira brawled for about 3 minutes, until Haggarty poked Akira in the eyes. Haggarty spiked
Akira with a DDT, nailed a fistdrop to his head, and got a two count. He set Akira up for a superplex, but Akira was able to block it. He headbutted Haggarty, then double underhook powerbombed
Haggarty off the top rope. The ref started a count, and Akira made the tag at 12. Blackline was able to keep Haggarty in the ring, and after an inverted atomic drop, Blackline took Haggarty over with a sunset flip. Flood tried to make the save, but Akira caught him, and kept him off Blackline long enough for him to pin
Haggarty with some assist from his tights. WINNERS: Black Sun Rising via pinfall in 12:23 JA: Blackline got the win for Black Sun Rising, but he needed a little pull of the tights to help keep Haggarty down.
AO: Whatever works, works. JA: Did you teach him that move? AO: No. Blackline knew it long before I met him [snickers] JA: Well, we're just about out of time. We'll leave you with these comments from Greg Miller, directed at the man who defeated him this past week, Ghost....
(Scene opens with a picture of Greg Miller in the locker room after his bout with Ghost. Greg has a look of total disgust upon his face. He looks as if he is playing his match back and forth in his mind. Miller finally lifts his head up, and looks into the camera.)
There...There...Just can't be...Any...Other...No. I got it. Ghost you proved yourself a good wrestler tonite, and maybe I underestimated you, but I got here in Japan just a couple hours before my match. I was very tired. Plane rides are very hard on a man these days. You my friend have not seen the last of me. I guess you think you are some hot shot now don't you. Well Ghost you got another thing coming. Cause if Greg Miller was fully prepared for his bout you would not have beat him. I am a great technical wrestler. Ha...I just may even be the best. If you are the WARRIOR that you claim to be, and you have the HONOR that you claim to have you will face me in a REMATCH next week at Ring
Wars. You have embarrassed me in front of the Japanese, and now I shall embarass you in front of your whole country!
(Greg Miller get's up with a very angry look on his face. The look then turns into a smile. He starts walking towards the door, and turns around just long enough for the camera to get one last good look in of his face before he opens the door and leaves....Fade Out)
