[Metro Continues...] TK: This next segment is an interview conducted with Shishi Fujiwara, concerning his title match with Ed Dean later in our program. He is Chiima Tora member SHISHI FUJIWARA!!!! [The fans come to there feet and cheer as Shishi Fujiwara makes his way to the ring to "Even Flow" by Pearl Jam. He wears black jeans, a faded red Nike t- shirt, with a long black duster replacing his usual leather coat. He is sporting a very thin black goatee and mustache around his mouth. He wears his trademark black sunglasses that hide his eyes. His hair is gelled and spiked. He walks to the ring calmly, slapping hands occasionally with fans. He has an expression on his face that means business. He climbs into the ring and spins around with arms out gesturing to the crowd whose cheers increase. The music cuts and he comes to center ring and stands next to Adonis...] JA: Shishi Fujiwara you've got the biggest matc... [Fujiwara pauses and points to the crowd] ...all these people realize just what an important match tonights main event is, not only in my career, but in the future of Super Japan. [Fujiwara pauses and Adonis puts his hand on the mic and prepares to speak again but Fujiwara pulls it away and gently shoves him out of camera view, ignoring him.] Fujiwara: Ed Dean you may be the Ugly American, but when I'm through with you they'll call you the shit-faced american 'cause I'm gonna f*ck you up! [The crowd is taken back at Fujiwara's comments but gives him a huge pop due to his threat to the hated Dean. Adonis comes back into the picture covering up the microphone and presumably asking Fujiwara to watch his language. Fujiwara shoves him back out of the picture again.] Fujiwara: The reason Dean has the All-Asian championship right now is because the Japanese natives of SJPW are weak and meek, they speak softly and honor the rules... They speak in the press and on TV about morals, honor, respect, and decency. The problem is gaijin trash like Dean can't be treated with honor and respect because they have none and thus deserve none!!! [The crowd pops again. Fujiwara begins pacing the ring, appealing to the crowd as he speaks...] Fujiwara: Would you have men like my friend and fellow Chiima Tora member Shinji Hamada come to the ring and fight with honor and dignity even though they get their a**es kicked?!?! Is that what you would have, honorable Japanese warriors who a piece of shit like Dean turns away time and time again through underhanded tactics?!?! [Some in the crowd scream 'no' while others sit in stunned silence...] I say men who preach honor, respect, and decency and practice it in the ring make fine role models... but piss poor fighters! [The majority of the crowd sits in stunned silence while some show their approval through applause, cheers, and whistles...] Fujiwara: The suits in the back, they try and mold and shape me into a figure like the honorable Shinji Hamada or Shoji Suzuki, and truely both are fine men... But times have changed... No longer can honorable Japanese men like those in Chiima Tora cling to their fair and nobel ways when entering into battle, especially not against foriegners!!! Because no longer do our counterparts, our oppenents, practice the fair and honorable ways that we have so desperately tried to hold onto. Honor and respect is no longer a two way street! The nobel ways of the acient warriors are dead! [Fujiwara pauses and looks into the camera...] Fujiwara: I don't stand here proclaiming myself the savior of SJPW or promising a honorable and sportsman-like technical match... What I do promise is one hell of fight and victory at ALL cost, by ANY means necessary!!! [Some of the crowd comes to its feet cheering Fujiwara while others sit in there sit, silent, unsure of how to react. Fujiwara throws the mic to Adonis before hopping over the top rope to the floor, jumping the guardrail and exiting through the crowd do a mixed reaction. Some fans boo, others cheer, some even chant Fujiwara's name.
