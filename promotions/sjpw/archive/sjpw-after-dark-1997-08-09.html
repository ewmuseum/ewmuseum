After Dark

9 August 1997

Welcome to another edition of SJPW After Dark. This week, we have matches from a house show in Nagoya, at the Nagoya Dome. Tonight we will see Tiger Kikuchi vs Sho Nakatoni, as well as the debuts of Ryoko Kagura & The Tokyo Terrors. Cherry Wonderful will also be in singles action, and our main event pits SJPW Tag Team Champions, The Toyota Brothers, defend their titles against Keiji Asamiya & Kazuya Asakino. This match has been building for weeks, with the challengers continually berating the champs, claiming they have been ducking them. They've also claimed that SJPW CEO, Mr. Yamashita, have been shielding the Toyota's from defending their title. As the only team to defeat the Toyota Brothers, they feel they deserve this shot. This is more than a typical title defense; it's become a grudge match, and the Toyota Brothers are out to clear their good names. It's going to be a hell of a match. [pauses to look at his notes] JA: Tiger Kikuchi and Sho Nakatoni are no strangers to each other. Nakatoni was the Old Japan TV champion, and he and Kikuchi feuded over that title back in 1990. The title changed hands between the two a record 12 times! After Nakatoni lost the belt, he fell on hard times. He's never held a belt since then, while Kikuchi has won many titles over the years. Let's go to Eddie and Akira to get this underway. [The camer goes past Jonnie to focus on the big screen behind him] ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 8/8/97] Eddie Rose: Nakatoni starts of with a swift kick to Tiger's face. I understand Nakatoni was once a karate champion? Akira Ono: Yes. He won a tournament in 1988, before he went into wrestling. Nakatoni now with a a dropkick, sending Kikuchi back to the ropes. He chops him across the chest [crowd "whoooos"], then whips him to the ropes... spinwheel kick takes Kikuchi down! [crowd oooohs]. Nakatoni with goes to the ropes... snap elbow to the chest. Cover...one...two..kickout! [crowd applauds] ER: Nakatoni drags Kikuchi up, and plants him with a side backbreaker. He runs to the ropes...corkscrew elbowdrop! He gets up... standing moonsault! [crowd applauds] Cover...one...two...kickout! [crowd applauds] AO: Nakatoni is doing a very good job tonight! He drags Tiger up, who drops down into a jawbreaker [crowd oooohs] So much for momentum now! Kikuchi is leaning on the ropes, trying to catch his breath, while Nakatoni is still feeling the effects of the jawbreaker. ER: Nakatoni gets to his feet, and slowlt moves towards Kikuchi. Kikuchi catches him with a swift kick to the gut, then hammers the back of his neck with a jumping elbowsmash. He sets Nakatoni up.... running powerbomb! [crowd oooohs, then applauds] Kikuchi now laying in stomps to Nakatoni's head and chest! [crowd ooohs] AO: These two had a vicious feud in 1990. They still have ill will towards each other. Whenever he wrestles Nakatoni, Kikuchi seems to get a little meaner, more aggressive. Tiger goes to the second rope...legdrop! He drags Nakatoni to his feet, and holding the back of his head, rams him head first into the corner. ER: he definitely looks a little meaner. I hope he's like this when he and Ruckus face the Smiths. Kikuchi rams Nakatoni into the corner 2 more times, grabs him in a waistlock... German release suplex out of the corner! [crowd ooohs, then applauds] Did you see the 360 Nakatoni did in mid air? AO: That was a truly damaging hold. Tiger goes to the top rope this time.... elbowdrop to the back of Nakatoni's head! [crowd applauds] He's giving the signal for the Tiger Driver! [crowd cheers] He drags Nakatoni up... he lifts him high in the air... he hits it! [crowd ooooohs, then applauds] Cover...one...two...THREE!! Ring Announcer: Your winner, TIGER KIKUCHI!!! [crowd cheers, and streamers flood the ring] ER: Good win for Kikuchi leading into the tag match with the Smiths. AO: Absolutely. Kikuchi's version of the Square Driver is the most devestating I have ever seen! ------------------------------------------------------------------- [camera cuts back to Adonis in the Control Center] JA: Impressive win for the veteran Kikuchi. Now we'll see if he can hit the Tiger Driver on Lance or Terry Smith. [glances at his notes] JA: Up next, Ryoko Kagura makes here SJPW debut, as she faces off against Sumie Odaka in singles competition. [The camera cuts to the screen] ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 8/8/97] ER: Ryoko goes to lock up with Sumie, and catches her with a knee to the gut as she moves in. Kagura now with a headbutt, then takes Odaka down with an armdrag. Odaka up, and Kagura jumps up... monkey flip by Kagura sends Odak through the ropes to the floor! [crowd ooooohs] Ref: One! AO: Kagura goes to the top rope... she sets herself as Odaka gets to her feet... Ref: Two! ER: Spinwheel kick off the top rope to the floor! [crowd oooohs, then applauds] Odaka goes over the railing into the crowd! Ref: Three! AO: Kagura gets to her feet, and hops over the railing. She drags Odaka to her feet, and hiptosses her over the railing back to ringside. Ref: Four! ER: Kagura leaps over the railing, but Odaka moves, and Kagura lands face first on the floor! [crowd oooohs] Odaka drops an elbow on the back of Kagura's head! Ref: Five! AO: Odaka drags Kagura to her feet, and rams her back first into the apron [crowd oooohs] Ref: Six! ER: Odaka up on the apron, holding Kagura by the hair... Rocker Dropper! [crowd ooohs] Kagura driven face first to the floor again! Ref: Seven! AO: Odaka rolls Kagura in, and covers...one...two...kickout! [crowd applauds] Kagura's head is bleeding. She may have broken her nose. ER: Odaka drags Kagura to her feet, and Kagura rams a knee into her gut. Kagura sets Odaka up...powerbomb! [crowd oooohs] Kagura is one of the biggest women in SJPW! AO: Kagura lifts Odaku up over her shoulder, and sits her on the top rope. She lays into her with a punch, then gets up on the second rope. She sets Odaka up.... Superplex! [crowd applauds] Odaka hit the mat hard! ER: Kagura is working the crowd now. Why doesn't she cover Odaka? AO: Odaka is at her mercy; what is she planning to do? ER: Kagura stands Odaka up, who is wobbling. Kagura goes to the ropes.... OKINAWA SPIN KICK!!!! [crowd oooooohs, then applauds] cover...one...two...THREE!!!! [crowd applauds] Ring Announcer: Your winner, RYOKO KAGURA!!! [crowd applauds] AO: Excellant jumping roundhouse kick to the head by Kagura. That is a deadly move! ------------------------------------------------------------------- [camera cuts back to Adonis in the Control Center] JA: Outstanding debut for Ryoko Kagura. Cherry Wonderful makes her singles debut tonight. We saw here last week in our main event 6 woman tag team match. Tonight, she wrestles solo, taking on Kyoko Shibata. [camera cuts to the large screen] ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 8/8/97] ER: Shibata nails Cherry as she enters the ring! She hammers her with forearm shots to her back, then gives her a vicious knee lift to her face! [crowd ooooohs] AO: Cherry stumbles into the ring, and Shibata executes an axe kick to the back of her head! [crowd ooooohs] So far, Shibata has been totally in control! Shibata is going to the top rope... she sets herself.... Cherry rolls out of the way! [crowd oooohs] Shibata missed the frog splash! ER: Cherry staggers to her feet, then runs up and gives Shibata a wicked kick to her ribs! [crowd ooohs] She drags Shibata to her feet, and takes her back down with a bulldog! She gets up, and drops a leg across the back of Shibata's head! [crowd oooohs] AO: Cherry scoops Shibata up, and slams her to the mat. She runs to the ropes... corkscrew elbowdrop! Cherry goes to the top rope.... Skytwister Press! [crowd oooooohs, then applauds. Chants of "Cherry! Cherry!" start] ER: Cherry is going to the top rope again... she sets herself as Shibata gets to her feet.... She just crouches on the top until Shibata stands up... Double Elbowsmash to both of Shibata's shoulders from the top rope! [crowd oooohs] She calls that move "Wonderful Tonight" Cover...one...two...THREE!!!! [crowd cheers] Ring Announcer: Your winner, "RAINBOW GODDESS" CHERRY WONDERFUL!!! [Crowd cheers again, and streamers fly into the ring] AO: Strange move, but it got the win. ------------------------------------------------------------------- [camera cuts back to Adonis in the Control Center] JA: Quick win for the wife of my good friend, Patty Babes Carter. Cherry & Stephanie are two of a kind: talented wrestlers, and they make dinner too! [chuckles] Now we have the debut of the world reknowned Tokyo Terrors. These two men are an excellant tag team, and they make a great addition to SJPW's tag team division. They debut tonight, taking on the Fujimoto Brothers.... [camera cuts to the screen] ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 8/8/97] ER: It looks like Ryu Fujimoto is going to start against Green Jackal. These two men have held world tag titles in 3 feds, and they are still NWWF tag champs. They lock up, and Ryu whips Jackal to the ropes... duck down by Ryu... Leap frog by Ryu... flying clothesline by Jackal! [crowd oooohs] Ryu does a 360 in mid-air! AO: I like these Terrors. Agressive, just like I was. Jackal drags Ryu up... slingshot suplex. What momentum! He tags his partner, then snaps an elbow onto Ryu's chest. ER: Blue Scorpion in now, and he drags Ryu up... belly to belly suplex. Scorpion now locking on an armbar. Ryu gets to his feet, but can't reverse or counter the armbar. Scorpion now pulling him to the ropes... he wraps Ryu's arm around the top rope, and pulls! [crowd ooohs]. The ref makes him break at the 4 count, but he hurt that arm. AO: Scorpion goes after Ryu, who catches him with a knee coming in. Ryu rams him headfirst into the corner, then tags in his brother. Takashi goes right after Scorpion, and lays into him with kicks to his leg and chest [crowd ooohs]. Scorpion nails him with a rake of the eyes [crowd booos], then monkey flips him over! ER: Scorpion tags in his partner, and they take Takashi down with a double suplex. Jackal tags Scorpion back in, and whips Takashi to the ropes... double dropkick! Scorpion tags in Jackal, and whips Takashi to the ropes again... double running clothesline! [crowd applauds] Exellant teamwork from the Terrors. AO: Jackal is going to the top rope now.... he sets himself waiting... Takashi starts to get up.... Missle Dropkick from the top! [crowd applauds] Cover...one...two..Ryu breaks it up with a stomp! [crowd applauds] ER: Scorpion comes of the top with a cross body block onto Ryu! [crowd oooohs] Both men up, and Scorpion superkicks Ryu, and Jackal catches him, and belly to back suplexes him down! [crowd oooooohs] AO: Scorpion scoops Takashi up, and slams him to the mat. The referee makes him go back to his corner, as Jackal throws Ryu over the top rope. He tags Scorpion back in, and sets Takashi up. Scorpion goes to the top rope... Bakbreaker by Jackal, Elbowdrop by Scorpion! [crowd applauds] Scorpion covers...one...two...THREE!! [crowd applauds] Ring Announcer: Your winners, GREEN JACKAL & BLUE SCORPION, THE TOKYO TERRORS!!!! [crowd applauds] ER: This team is one to watch out for. ------------------------------------------------------------------- [camera cuts back to the Adonis in the Control Center] JA: The Tokyo Terrors using the old Demolition Decapitator for the win in their debut. This team is definitely a wild card in the SJPW tag team ranks. [pauses to look at his notes] JA: Which leads us to the main event. Keiji Asamiya & Kazuya Asakino are the only team to beat the Toyota Brothers. That win at Battlecade '97 has made them feel they deserve to be the #1 contendors, even though they were beaten in the first round of the tag tournament by the Tokyo Express. After accusing Mr. Yamashita of shielding them, the Toyota Brothers decided it was put up or shut up time, and accepted the challenge. Tonight, for the gold, we present Toyota Brothers vs Asamiya & Asakino 2.... [camera cuts to the screen] ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 8/8/97] ER: Listen to the ovation the crowd is giving the Toyota Brothers! The tag champs have really become fan favorites in short order, which is a far cry from the way american wrestling fans treat them. They even brought the Japanese flag to ringside with them, and the SJPW banner! The crowd loves them! AO: They also love Asamiya & Asakino. This is going to be an outstanding match! Asamiya is starting out with Kensauke. They lock up, and Asamiya with a go behind into a takedown. Kensauke gets free, and gets to his feet, only for Asamiya to take him down with a snap armdrag. Kensauke gets up once more, and Asamiya takes him down with a drop toehold, but Kensauke gets to his feet before he can lock on an leglock! [crowd applauds] ER: Asamiya is showing Kensauke how good of a grappler he is. Kensauke looks a little angry, and as Asamiya moves to lock up, Kensauke kicks him in the gut...again...again... now a jumpin elbowsmash to the back of Asamiya's head, sending him to his knees! [crowd applauds] AO: Kensauke sets him up... gutwrench suplex. He tags in Hiroshima [crowd cheers]. They set Asamiya up... double gutbuster! [crowd oooohs] Asamiya is flopping around the ring! Hiroshima drags Asamiya up... he presses him high over his head [crowd ooooohs]... he drops Asamiya into another gutbuster! [crowd ooohs, then applauds] If he didn't break something, he definitel bruised some ribs! ER: Hiroshima now working the crowd [crowd chants "AC! AC!"] He walks confidently over to Asamiya, and starts to drag him up... Asamiya rams a knee into Hiroshima's gut... Diamond Cutter! [crowd oooohs] Asamiya tries to get to his feet, but he falls back onto the ropes! AO: Kensauke yelling at his brother to get up, while Asamiya, with the ropes for leverage, gets to his feet. He staggers over to his corner, and tags in Kazuya! [crowd cheers] Asakino jumps right up to the top rope, and drops a diving headbutt onto the prone form of Hiroshima! [crowd oooohs, then applauds] He rolls him over into a cover...one...two...Kensauke breaks it up with an elbowdrop! [crowd applauds] ER: Asamiya charges in, and flying forearms Kensauke into his corner! The two start trading blows! The ref moves to seperate them, as Asakino gets up, and locks Hiroshima in a headlock. He signals for a bulldog, then starts to run... Hiroshima throws him off! [crowd cheers] Asakino crashes into the ropes! AO: The referee is still trying to seperate Asamiya & Kensauke. Hiroshima is up, and he goes to his corner, and hits Asamiya from behind with a double axe handle blow. Asamiya staggers forward, and Kensauke takes him down with a swinging neckbreaker! [crowd ooohs]. Kensauke goes to his corner, and Hiroshima tags him in. Hiroshima lifts Asamiya up as Kensauke goes to the top rope... Side Suplex by Hiroshima... legdrop by Kensauke! [crowd applauds] ER: Too bad it was the wrong man. Kensauke throws Asamiya through the ropes to the floor, then goes after Asakino, who is now on his feet. He kicks Asakino in the gut, and sets him up...power-hurricanrana by Asakino! [crowd applauds] Kensuake went through the ropes to the floor! AO: Asakino goes right to the top rope.... Corkscrew plancha to the floor! [crowd cheers] Both men go down hard! Ref: One! ER: Asakino gets up, and starts to kick Kensauke... Ref: Two! AO: Asakino now with a snap suplex on Kensauke... Ref: Three! ER: Asamiya over there now, and they double suplex Kensauke to the floor before he goes to his corner... Ref: Four! AO: Asakino now throws Kensauke into the ring, and goes to the apron... slingshot senton! [crowd oooohs] He stands Kensauke up, then tags in his partner. Asakino gets down on all fours as Asamiya goes to the second rope... Double Goozle from the second rope! Cover by Asamiya...one...two...Hiroshima with the save! [crowd applauds] ER: Both teams are really pulling out all stops! They want to beat each other so bad! Asamiya drags Kensauke up... small package by Kensauke...one...two..kickout! [crowd applauds] That came out of nowhere! Asamiya looks shocked! He sets Kensauke up for a belly to back suplex... Kensauke shifts on top of him...one...two...kickout! [crowd applauds] AO: Asamiya is once again shocked at Kensaukes' resourcefulness. He goes over to Kensauke, who grabs his tights, and pitches him over to his corner, where Hiroshima sticks his boot out, catching him in the head! [crowd oooohs] Asamiya staggers back... Oriental Sleeper [cobra clutch]! [crowd oooohs] ER: Asakino comes in, but Hiroshima intercepts him. The two start trading blows. Asamiya tries to fight the move, then Kensauke jerks him back into a german suplex... he bridges... one...two...THREE!!! [crowd cheers] He got him! AO: The referee is now trying to pull Hiroshima and Kazuya apart. Kensauke pulls his brother into their corner, and keeps himself between him and Kazuya. Ring Announcer: Your winners, and STILL SJPW Tag Team Champions, KENSAUKE & HIROSHIMA, THE TOYOTA BROTHERS!!!!! [crowd cheers, streamers flood the ring] ER: What a win for the Toyota Brothers. I guess they proved that that loss to Asamiya & Asakino hasn't slowed them down one bit. Kazuya is checking on his cousin, who is holding his neck. Wait a minute! The Toyotas walk over to the cousins! We have a staredown here! [crowd ooohs; "AC! AC!" chants start up] The Toyotas offer their hands to the cousins! They look to the crowd [crowd cheers]... they shake hands with them! Both teams now embracing! AO: Great show of sportsmanship by both teams. Look! Badd Intentions have hit the ring! They are yelling at the Toyota Brothers! [crowd chants "AC! AC!"] Kensauke & Hiroshima drop their titles in front of them, and motion for Chill & Patera to start something! [crowd oooohs] Asamiya & Asakino get between both teams as more referees and security hit the ring! ------------------------------------------------------------------- [camera cuts back to Adonis in the Control Center] JA: Luckily, security was able to remove Badd Intentions before a brawl broke out. These two teams are headed for an explosive confrontation at The Super J Crown ppv! We're out of time for now, so until next time, I'll see you After Dark!
