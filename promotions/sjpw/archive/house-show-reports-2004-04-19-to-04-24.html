SJPW House Show Reports (4/19/04-4/24/04)

Apr 29, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Monday, April 19th, Owase City Gymnasium (Cap 1,000), Mie, Japan: The Maulers vs The Crush Crew: The opening bout saw these two teams collide again. The Crew used every dirty tactic they could, but The Maulers, being veteran heels, out heeled them. The end came as the Crew went for their Assaiki on Thomas, only for Douglas to break it up by tripping Nakatoni. Kobayashi hit a baseball slide on Douglas, but ended up getting rolled up from behind by Thomas for the win. Otaku says: The Maulers pulled a fast one over the Crush Crew! Ono says: That's what they get for trying to out cheat two cheaters. Wolf Jager vs Tasogare no Prince: The newcomer Jager did very well against the well known Twilight Prince. While TnP went for an aerial attack early, Jager kept his actions to the mat, trying to take out TnP's arm. He did a number on him, but after applying the Strange Hold Beta, the time ran out, leading to a draw. Both men shook hands afterward. Otaku says: I'd like to see a rematch between these men, with a longer time limit Ono says: I agree. Their difference in styles makes for a good contest. American Enforcer vs Gregor Davinsky: Enforcer finally got Davinsky one on one, and took advantage of it. Enforcer out powered Davinsky at every turn, until Davinsky thumbed him in the eye. Davinsky hit the Russian Sickle, but Enforcer was able to kick out. Davinsky went for a second one, but Enforcer countered with an STO. He then hit a big chokeslam on Davinsky, and scored the pin. Otaku says: so-so match between two dinosaurs. Ono says: Not so! Enforcer has some years left to him. It was good to seem him finally get his hands on Davinsky! "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder & Takahiro KEOKI vs Despair & Super Bakemono: After what took place on The Zone! taping the night before, bookers set this match up for the next night's main event. The odd pairing of Thunder & KEOKI took it to their opponents. Thunder started off by throwing Bakemono all over the ring, hitting a few stiff lariats on him. Bakemono was able to nail Thunder with some koppo kicks, then tagged in Despair. With his section of fans cheering him on, the Gunryo champion hit a series of snap suplexes on Thunder, until he was able to block on, and hit the Widowmaker on the champion. Both men barely tagged in their partners before the ten count. KEOKI came in, and blasted Bakemono with a series of dropkicks, before hitting an impact DDT on him. He almost got the three count, but Despair broke it up. Bakemono snuck in a low blow as the ref was making Despair exit the ring, and then went for his Bakemono Bomb. KEOKI moved, and Bakemono hit the canvas hard. KEOKI tagged in Thunder, and as Despair tried to enter the ring, Thunder levelled him with a stiff lariat. He then hit the Widowmaker on Bakemono, scoring the pin for his team. Otaku says: Great match. Thunder really acted like he had something to prove in this match. Ono: And he proved it! Bakemono was knocked out! Wednesday, April 21st, Shirihama Hall (Cap 1,000), Wakayama, Japan "Dragon Fury" Minobu Tetsuhara vs El Santa Azteca: The young lion from Kobe took on the veteran luchador in a classic match. Both men traded armdrags, then went for some elbows before Tetsuhara took Azteca down with an STO. He then hit a rolling senton, and score a two count. Azteca was able to make a short comeback, reversing an Irish whip before hitting a hurricanrana, then a spinning backbreaker. Azteca's downfall was when he went to the top rope. Tetsuhara got up, nailed him in the gut with his head, then hit an overhead belly to belly off the top rope. With Azteca stunned, Tetsuara then hit the Dragon Driver 1 for the pinfall Otaku says: Great showing by the young Tetsuhara. He's making a nice comeback from his loss to Kawasaki. Ono: I agree. it will be interesting to see how he does in the Sedei-X tournament when it takes place. "Jigoku Battle Tiger" Kinjiro Matsuoko & Mr. Liar vs American Enforcer & Wolf Jager: The Matsuoko dojo team had little trouble with their opponents in this match. Jager started things out against Matsuoko, and the two attempted various submission holds, with Jager going for Matsuoko's arm, and Matsuoko going for Jager's leg. They tagged in their partners, and Enforcer took control over Liar, until he got raked across the eyes. The Matsuoko dojo team them doubled up on Enforcer, hitting a double suplex, a double elbowdrop, then a spike piledriver before the referee made Liar exit the ring. Enforcer tried to fight back, and Jager reached out for the tag. Liar snuck around the ring, and pulled Jager's legs out from under him, causing him to hit his chin on the ring apron. With Jager stunned, Matsuoko hit a Stun Gun on Enforcer, and rolled him up for the pinfall. After the match, Jager and Enforcer got into a shoving match, but the ring attendants separated them before they could get into a fight. Otaku says: Enforcer and Jager would make a good team, if they didn't dislike each other so much. Ono: True. Enforcer's power and technical skills, combined with Jager's submission skills, would make them a formidable team... Tasogare no Prince vs Jiraiya: TnP ended his losing streak with a win over the young Chiimu Tora member. Both men traded high flying moves, hitting dropkicks on each other, and diving outside the ring on at least four occasions. Both men ended up being whipped into the railing as well. Jiraiya took control for awhile, scoring a two count after hitting the Jinrai ("Thunderclap", a standing shooting star press). Jiraiya got a little cocky though, and after hitting Sliced Bread #2, went for a top rope headbutt. TnP rolled out of the way hit his Destiny Driver, and scored the pinfall. Otaku says: TnP is back to his winning ways! Ono says: That he is! "Ecchi Kid" Kentaro "H-Bomb" Kawasaki vs Tetsuo Matsumoto: This match was highly anticipated by the fans in Wakayama. They gave Matsumoto a standing ovation as he was introduced. Kawasaki was not impressed though. Matsumoto dominated early on, as he hit a few suplexes on the smaller Kawasaki. Kawasaki came back though, catching Matsumoto with a thrust kick to the throat, followed by an enzugiri. Kawasaki then hit a piledriver, and scored a two count. Kawasaki went for a few neckbreakers, and again scored a two count on Matsumoto. Matsmoto fired back with a series of elbows, stunning Kawasaki. Kawasaki was able to make one more pinfall attempt, after Tetsuo went for a powerbomb, and Kawasaki flipped out of it, and countered with a DDT. With the fans behind him, Matsumoto kicked out, got up, and nailed Kawasaki with a series of stiff lariats, before hitting his Dragon Driver for the pinfall. Otaku says: Fantastic main event. Ono says: Matsumoto comes back from his loss to Asakura with a great win over Kawasaki! Saturday, April 24th, Chiimu Tora Dojo (Cap 400), Tokyo, Japan Pramoj Ananda vs "Jackhammer" Vince Larson: Two young superstars faced off in a brutal match. Larson started off strong, landing some scoop slams on the smaller Ananda, before hitting a big powerbomb on the kickboxer. Ananda was able to kick out, and came back with some brutal kicks to Larson's body that say Larson actually bruise in the ring! Larson was almost pinned after one roundhouse kick to the side of his head, but he was able to get a foot on the ropes. Ananda went for his Ananda Special, but Larson caught him as he springboarded off the ropes, and took him over with a capture suplex. Larson then hit a second powerbomb, before going for the Jackhammer and the three count. Otaku says: Larson is one powerful dude! Ono says: He's still limited by his power attack, but I think he can go places if he expands his repertoire somewhat. "Kyoto Tiger" Takayoshi Asakura vs Masashi "Lightning" Lanther: A great match between two cruiserweights. Asakura was given strong applause when introduced, as he's been on a roll. Lanther was in no mood to be nice though, and started off by slapping Asakura in the face. Asakura wasted no time in pummeling Lanther with a series of elbows, before trying to whip him to the ropes. Lanther reversed it, nailed a koppo kick, then sent Asakura to the mat with a lariat. He went for his Lanther Bomb, but Asakura countered with a back body drop. Asakura went for an Implant DDT, but Lanther block, and countered with a snap suplex. Lanther hit a top rope headbutt, but Asakura kicked out at two. Lanther started to go for the Lanther Bomb again, but Asakura pulled his legs out from under him, and catapulted him into the corner. Lanther bounced off the top turnbuckle, and Asakura nailed him with the Asakura Crusher, scoring another upset victory! After the match, Asakura and Lanther shook hands. Otaku says: TnP, Matsumoto, now Lanther. Can anyone stop the "Kyoto Tiger"? Ono says: He's definitely on a roll. But he can get past American Enforcer? "Ecchi Kid" Kentaro "H-Bomb" Kawasaki vs Tetsuo Matsumoto: A rematch from the Wakayama show saw Kawasaki perform much better this time against his larger opponent. Though Matsumoto started off strong, nailed Kawasaki with a lariat, then hitting a release German suplex, Kawasaki kicked out at two. Matsumoto then went for a vertical suplex, but Kawasaki was able to flip behind him, and clip his knee. As Matsumoto went down to one knee, Kawasaki hit a stepladder enzugiri on him. He choked Matsumoto on the mat, and almost got disqualified as a result. Kawasaki went for a swinging neckbreaker, but Matsumoto blocked it, and nailed Kawasaki with a powerbomb. He got a two count, and then went for an Exploder suplex. He nailed it, but Kawasaki got right back up, and nailed Matsumoto with a lariat. Matsumoto got back up, and hit another Exploder, only to see Kawasaki get back, and lariat him again. Matsumoto got up again, hit another Exploder, only to see Kawasaki get back up, and nail him with a kick to the sternum. As Matsumoto doubled over, Kawasaki hit his Hentai Holocaust, but Matsumoto kicked out! Matsumoto fired back with a series of elbows, then tried to whip Kawasaki to the ropes. Kawasaki reversed it, caught Matsumoto in the gut with another kick, hit the Hentai Holocaust a second time, and scored the pinfall. Otaku says: Kawasaki evens the score... Ono says: That he did. Great matches between these two on these two shows. I'm impressed with both their in-ring performances. "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder, Kenichi "Kurassha" Kubota & Takahiro KEOKI vs Despair & The Crush Crew: This oddly booked six man saw Chiimu Tora's own KEOKI again joining forces with Thunder, with the added addition of Kubota. Normally a heel, Kubota saw the fans cheering or him early on as he went toe to toe with Tsutomou Kobayashi of the Crush Crew. The Crew eventually got the upper hand on Kubota, and actually hit the Assaiki on him, but Kubota kicked out, which got the fans to start chanting his name. Kubota finally tagged in KEOKI, who took apart Botan Nakatoni with a series of dropkicks, before sending him over the top rope to the floor with a spinwheel kick. With the referee keeping him and Despair from going at it. Kubota snuck around the ring, and blasted Nakatoni with a chair one of the fans handed him. Kobayashi chased him around the ring, only to find himself the victim of a lariat from Thunder. Nakatoni made it back into the ring, and almost got pinned after an Impact DDT. Despair broke up the pin, and nailed KEOKI on the temple with a closed fist, cutting open his eyebrow. Nakatoni finally tagged in Despair, who continued to attack KEOKI's eyebrow, until the young lion caught the Gunryo champ with a jawbreaker, and tagged in Thunder, who hammered Despair with a series of stiff lariats. The Crush Crew came in and attacked Thunder, only to see Kubota clear the ring with a garbage can lid he pulled from under the ring. As the referee was getting Kubota out of the ring, Despair low blowed Thunder, and the Crush Crew hit the Assaiki on him. Despair went for a cover, only to see Thunder kick out right before the ref counted three! Despair was beside himself, and tagged in Kobayashi. Thunder made a comeback though, and tagged in the bloody KEOKI, who nailed Kobayashi with a top rope dropkick. Nakatoni broke up the pinfall, and with the referee distracted, Despair nailed KEOKI with his Gunryo title belt. Kobayashi tagged in Nakatoni, and they hit the Assaiki on him, proving that the third truly is a charm as Nakatoni got the pinfall. After the match, Despair and the Crew went off on KEOKI, and even did a number on Thunder when he tried to help. Kubota stormed the ring again with his garbage can lid, and took out all three of his opponents, and stood over the fallen Despair, holding his lid up over his head, soaking in the crowd's applause. Otaku says: Big win for Despair and the Crush Crew. Ono says: True, but Kubota sent Despair a message tonight. I suspect these two will face off sooner rather than later
