Metro, Hour Two 2/2

27 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JA: Kagemusha seems to think so too. He falls on Fujiwara for the cover... ...1... ...2... ER: The referee stops his count! It seems Fujiwara’s leg is on the bottom rope! What determination by the Lion! Kagemusha is beginning to lose his cool. His calm exterior is beginning to crumble. JA: Kagemusha picks Fujiwara up, wraps his arms around his neck in a sleeper hold. He begins to apply pressure to the young man’s neck, cutting off the blood circulating to his head. Fujiwara, already weakened, is fastly fading. ER: The referee holds up a hand...it falls. The referee counts one. The referee holds up Fujiwara’s hand again...it falls. Count two. The referee holds up his hand a third time...this time it stays up! Fujiwara is drawing an inhuman strength from somewhere inside himself. JA: Fujiwara suddenly sits down on the mat, sending Kagemusha’s chin crashing into the top of his head in a modified chinbreaker! Kagemusha falls backwards in pain, holding onto his face. Fujiwara, still battered from his earlier beating, uses the ropes to slowly pick himself up. Taking a deep breath, he runs at Kagemusha, takes him by the hand, then Fujiwara runs up the turnbuckle, onto the ropes, and lands on the mat while delivering a spinning DDT to Kagemusha! What a maneuver!!! ER: Very intelligent move by the youngster! He realized that in the current condition he was in, he might only have one chance to end this match in his favor! Rather than execute a series of small moves which Kagemusha might have been able to counter, he went for the big move to end the match! JA: Fujiwara falls on Kagemusha for the pin... ...1... ...2... ER: But Kagemusha easily kicks up. I have a feeling the last man standing is going to win this match, Jonnie! JA: Better call the paramedics. ER: Fujiwara, still weak, picks Kagemusha up, then runs backwards towards the ropes, launches himself back towards the wobbly Kagemusha and nails him with a flying body cross! Kagemusha falls to the mat like a fallen tree. Fujiwara quickly gets back to his feet, picks up Kagemusha, grabs him by the legs, and lifts him over his head for a Lionplex! Then with lightning speed, another Lionplex! And another! Across the length of the ring! [Audience cheers and oohs!] JA: Fujiwara, not letting up, runs towards the turnbuckle, runs up the side, and twisting his body, lands on Kagemusha with an inside out moonsault! Fujiwara with the pin... ...1... ...2... ER: My goodness! Kagemusha has the presence of mind to lift up his shoulder! Fujiwara gets up, delivers a standing elbow drop. Picks Kagemusha back up, prepares to hook his arms...but Kagemusha suddenly spits his green mist into Fujiwara eyes! Fujiwara is reeling backwards, clutching at his eyes! The referee calls for the bell! **ding** **ding** **ding** JA: Kagemusha shoves the referee away as the referee tries to help the blinded Fujiwara. Kagemusha lifts Fujiwara into the air and sends him down to the mat with a devastating power bomb. [Audience boos!] ER: Kagemusha, taking his time, gets on the top turnbuckle. He then spits his green mist into the air, and leaps off the top turnbuckle and lands on Fujiwara with a shooting star press! He calls this the "Soul Stealer" JA: Unfortunately the match is already over and he lost. ER: Kagemusha exits the ring and makes his way backstage. Fujiwara, his face green with paint, lies unconscious on the ring. Nishi: The winner, as a result of a disqualification, Shishi "Lion" Fujiwara! [Audience cheers!] JA: The young man won fair and square. He should be proud of himself. ER: Yes. Now, your prediction was correct, Jonnie. The paramedics have made their way into the ring and are attending to Fujiwara. [Camera shows the medics taking Fujiwara to the back, the crowd applauding him, and chanting his name] JA: Our next match should prove to be as brutal as the last match! Tony Starks & Oleg Vladikoff are going to take on Kenji Ishida & Shinji Usami. Ishida & Usami want The Bonecrushers, and the Tokyo Express want them. ER: I think they'd better be focused, or else they won't get past Starks & Vladikoff. Vladikoff is a dangerous man by himself, and Starks is definitely no pushover! __ ___ _____________________________________________________ / |/ /__ / /________ Marano Tag Team Tournament, Round One / / /|_/ / -_) __/ __/ _ \ Ishida/Usami vs Starks/Vladikoff /_/ /_/\__/\__/_/ \___/_______________________________________/ JA: The bell rings, and Ishida and Starks are going to start off the match. Ishida locks up with Starks and is met with a kick to the gut. Starks swings Ishida into the ropes, and lands a vicious lariat. Starks quickly on the offensive as he puts on a Fujiwara armbar. ER: You can see the look of pain on Ishida's face. He is probably having second thoughts now about stepping into the ring with these two seasoned street fighters. Starks let's go of the armbar, and kicks Ishida in the face [crowd "ohhhhs"] JA: Starks over to his corner, he tags in the big russian. Vladikoff now in the ring. He goes to pick up Ishida, but is met with a punch to the gut. Vladikoff is a little suprised about that one. As he stumbles over. This gives Ishida enough time to run in, and tag his partner, Usami. He quickly gets into the ring, and irish whips Vladikoff into the ropes. ER: Attempted hiptoss by Usami, but he CANNOT get Vladikoff off the ground. He just is NOT strong enough. Vladikoff then goes for his own hiptoss, and throws over Usami like his is a rag doll. Vladikoff quickly down to capitilize on the mistake of Usami. As he pulls the hair of Usami to get him back up. He then takes him down with a footsweep. Vladikoff then quickly starts to kick Usami in both the side, and in the head. JA: This is vicious!!! Vladikoff figures that his opponent has had enough. He goes down for the pin. 1... 2... KICKOUT!!! [crowd applauds] ER: Nice kickout by Usami. The smaller man has not given up yet. Vladikoff up, and a bit disgusted goes over and tags in his partner, Tony Starks. Starks quickly into the ring. Picks up Usami, and irish wihips him into the turnbuckle of Starks corner. JA: I think that something is up here... ER: Definitely...Starks then runs over to plant an elbow in the face of Usami, and Usami backs out of the way. Starks NAILS VLADIKOFF!!! JA: Vladikoff looks at Starks...They stare each other down for a moment, and then Starks explains to the russian that it was not his fault. That it was a mistake. The russian looks a little reluctant to believe him though. ER: While Vladikoff and Starks continue to stare each other down Usami takes full advantage of it, and tags in Ishida. Ishida runs up behind Starks, and NAILS him with a shoulderblock. The shoulderblock knocks Starks into Vladikoff, and Vladikoff hits the ground HARD. Ishida quick to capitalize on his exploit. Turns around Starks and DDT's him. Ishida again quickly goes back on the offensive with an elbowsmash to the face of Starks. JA: Vladikoff starts to stir, and he has a look of total suprise on his face. He does not have a clue what just happened. All he knows, is that his own partner Tony Starks knocked him off the ring apron. One can only wonder if this will cause bad blood in between the two. ER: Ishida pulls Starks up, and goes for another DDT, but Starks uses all of his might to backdrop him back down to the floor. Starks tries to make it to his corner for a tag, and he cannot. As Ishida is up behind him, and he viciously NAILS him in the back of the head with a forearm. JA: Vladikoff has turned around, and is looking into the crowd. Ishida notices this, and quickly picks up Starks, and throws him into Vladikoff. Vladikoff is again knocked down, off the ring apron, but this time he recovers quickly. This may not be good Eddie!!! ER: Jonnie...You just may be right. As Vladikoff turns around, and he notices for the third time that Starks is standing there. Starks tries to tell Vladikoff that it was not him, but Vladikoff does not seem to be listening. Ishida backs away from Starks, and Vladikoff steps back into the ring. JA: Starks...Again trying to explain what happenecd, to his partner. Vladikoff does not believe him, and he nails him in the face with a closed fist. GOD!!!!!! [crowd "ohhhs"] ER: Somebody, get some security out there, NOW!!! JA: Vladikoff quickly ontop of things. He jumps down onto Starks, and he starts to pummel the face of Starks. Blood starts to fly, and Starks clearly, is not able to defend himself. Then Vladikoff let's go of him, and rolls him over. Vladikoff yells something, and then he slaps on the anklelock submission manuver. ER: A devastating manuver at that Jonnie...This submission hold, is used in many a Shoot fight in Japan. Vladikoff starts to twist, and Starks screams out in Pain. Vladikoff does not stop twisting though, and he keeps on going. [Security guards rush down the isle, and grab Vladikoff. Vladikoff let's go of Starks, and just pushes all the security guards away from him. Vladikoff steps away, and raises his hands into the air. He then steps out of the ring, and jumps down to the arena floor. Vladikoff does not look back one time as he makes his way back to the locker room. Meanwhile some paramedics come in with a stretcher to get Tony Starks.] JA: I think I can say for the both of us Eddie, that we hope Tony ends up ok. We never meant for this to happen, and I am sure that he never thought this was going to happen either. ER: Yes!!! You can Jonnie...I am sure this was a very unexpected attack, and Starks will try to get some kind of reprisal, I am SURE of it. [The paramedics have now succeded in loading up Starks, and they start to wheel him away on the stretcher. Many fans in the arena stand up, and start to cheer the name of Starks.] Nishi: The WINNER of this matchup, by a REFEREE'S DECISION...KENJI ISHI-DA, AND SHINJI US-SAM-I!!!! JA: What a disgusting display by Vladikoff. He turned on his own partner! ER: So Ishida & Usami advance to the next round because of it! Talk about lady luck shining on them! JA: We are out of time. Be sure to catch Tiger Kikuchi & Akira Ono on the next Tsunami, Sunday, November 8th! For Eddie Rose, I'm Jonnie Adonis. We'll see you next time on Metro! [The camera shows the medics putting Starks into an ambulence. As the credits roll, the ambulence leaves the arena, heading toward the hospital.
