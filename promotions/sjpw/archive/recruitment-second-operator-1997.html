SJPW recruitment under new ownership (late April-May 1997)

27 April - 19 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
----- SJPW Needs Cruiserweights & Tag teams (Apr 27, 1997, 12:00:00 AM) ----- I'm the owner of Super Japan Pro Wrestling, the only efed (that I know of) that is based in Japan, and is run like a real japanese federation. I need some more crusierweights, so I can hold a 16 man tournament. I also need more tag teams, as I currently only have 4 teams, and I need 8 for the tag team tournament. I'm short in the heavyweight division too, but expect that to be filled soon. Here's the deal: The wrestlers need to be Japanese, or from Asia (Korea, China, Phillipines, Vietnam etc). I have enough US wrestlers as is, so please don't try to submit a US wrestler. I need japanese wrestlers, to keep the feel of the fed like a traditional japanese fed. If anyone is interested, email me, and I will send you a copy of the Faq and Wrestler form. SJPW is a member fed of the Global Internet Wrestling Alliance (GIWA), and SJPW wrestlers will compete in the tournament for the vacant GIWA World Singles and Tag Team titles in September. I hope to hear from some of you soon! ----- [SJPW] Looking for a reliable booker/writer (May 18, 1997, 12:00:00 AM) ----- Super Japan Pro Wrestling is now a month old. It's growing so fast, that I am finding it harder to run the efed all by myself. What I need is someone to help me book and write matches for the cards. I'm primarily looking for someone to help with the fast growing Woman's Division, as I have 7 wrestlers now, and I've been asked to take over the roster of Cherry Pro, a woman's efed that was started from the original SJPW woman's division. Once I take in these wrestlers, that part of the fed will be closed! I'm also full with Cruiserweights, but still need 2-3 more tag teams, and I wouldn't mind another heavy weight or two (no Giant clones please! This is Japan, and not too many guys that big wrestle there! I have one big man, and that is more than enough for the fed!) *Warning* SJPW is doing it's BEST to be like the Japanese wrestling feds (ie All Japan/New Japan, not the hardcore feds like FMW or W*ing, though we do have a title for matches like they have), so you have to know a bit about Japanese/Mexican style wrestling. If you feel you can write this kind of stuff, email me, preferably with a writing sample. If I like what I see, I'll let you know. I hope some one out there is interested!
