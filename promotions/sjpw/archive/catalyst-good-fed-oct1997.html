What's the catalyst behind a good fed..

4 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mr. Friday Night wrote: > What is the one thing that takes a fed off the ground...It's like in pro > football you need a good offensive line and secondary to be competitive > before your get the big time playmakers...Same thing in e-wrestling, you > need one thing that the fed can grow around... > In my opinion a good Light-Heavyweight/Cruiserweight division is where it > begins...You have so many good rp'er that have character in the 220-250 > pound range...A solid cruiserweight division with good heat and balance is > where it starts.. SJPW had a solid start due to it's cruiserweight division, but in short order, both the tag team and women's divisions became hotbeds. The women of SJPW have proven to be vicious! Our Battle Arts division, while the smallest, draws mega heat, especially when Steel Serpent & "Demon Boy" Ishrinku are involved! It really depends on how the fed turns out when it starts out. The players will help make the angles start to work, and a fedhead can set up some matches which can lead into some more [name withheld] SJPW fedhead
