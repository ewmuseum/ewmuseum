News & Notes 9/1/04

Sep 1, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CEO Yamashita sent out a press release stating that "Living Legend" Tommy Fury & "Kyoto Tiger" Takayoshi Asakura have been released from their SJPW contracts. Fury was supposedly unhappy with how he was being booked, while Asakura, though showing some intensity in his last two appearances, had been on a major downward spiral since Zenmen Sensou. SJPW wishes both competitors the best of luck in any wrestling promotions they may compete for in the future. El Santo Azteca sustained a major concussion in his match with Despair on the 8/27 The Zone! He was taken to an undisclosed hospital, where x-rays showed he had fractures in his skull. Azteca will likely be out of action for some time due to this injury. We wish Azteca recovers from this injury. The Blood Merchants are fuming over Despair's treatment of Azteca, and have made it clear that they are now at war with the Disciples of Despair. So far, both sides are even in confrontations, but experts predict the real fireworks to begin at the J*STAR festival. Despair & Super Bakemono are entered into the Gunryo Gauntlet Tag title match, as is El Muerte Rojo & Sangre Guererro. Both sides are looking forward to facing each other, and predictions are that if anyone will shed blood at J*STAR, these four men will be the ones to do it! What people are wondering is if/when El Luchador Violento, the leader of the Blood Merchants, will make an appearance. Violento is Azteca's nephew and the SMLL Extremo Champion. More on this as it develops. Tiger Kikuchi was asked about Junya Toroyama's plea to end the feud that has developed between Team Tiger and the Dokyu Dojo. Kikuchi replied "I am willing to speak with Toroyama-san regarding this situation. However, some members of Team Tiger still feel slighted by his actions, and may seek satisfaction on their own" Leading the efforts to mend fences is NCWF Cruiserweight champion Masashi "Lightning" Lanther, who was very vocal in his opinion that Toroyama never betrayed Team Tiger by his aligning with the Dokyu Dojo. With Lanther supporting him, many feel Toroyama will be able to end this situation with Team Tiger without the need to continue the Five Fight Challenge. Noboru "Raging Bull" Nishida has petitioned SJPW officials for a rematch with GPH Champion Kentaro "H-Bomb" Kawasaki. Although Tasogare no Prince is being touted as the next challenger for the title, Nishida is quick to point out that he was Kawasaki's first challenger, and the two went to a time-limit draw. "Kawasaki may have retained his title, but it was a time limit draw. I feel I've proven myself worthy of a rematch". When asked about TnP being the llikely choice for #1 contender, Nishida said "I have the utmost respect for Tasogare no Prince. Like myself, he feels Kawasaki disgraces the title he holds. Whether he or I face Kawasaki, his days as champion are surely numbered" N Nishida has also expressed an interest in possibly challenging Van Wild for the Rumble Pro World Championship. "Van Wild is a great competitor, and I am sure we would have a great match. Both of us are very stiff, and use many high impact moves. The fans would surely enjoy such a contest between us" Despair is said to be disappointed in Super Bakemono's actions of late. Rumor has it the two men had an arguement in the DoD locker room on 8/27, and would have come to blows if not for the Crush Crew restraining them. Despair supposedly claimed that Bakemono is "going soft" on his opponents when he teams with Toroyama, while Bakemono told Despair that he crossed the line when he put Azteca in the hospital. Despair supposedly threw his title belt at Bakemono's feet, and challenged him to take it from him. Bakemono apparantly was going to do just that when Kobayashi and Nakatoni restrained him. Neither Bakemono or Despair would confirm or deny this story. It looks as if Saturday, October 9th, will be the date for the next supershow, Jisedei: Tsunami. The main event for that show will see "Firefly" Horo Atashi & Takahiro KEOKI face off in the finals of the Junior Struggle! tournament, with the winner being awarded the Sedei-X title. Also, "All-Star" Joey Darren is set to defend the IICW cruiser weight title, with former champion Tsunami as his likely challenger. Masashi "Lightning" Lanther will defend the NCWF cruiser weight title, but no specific opponent has been named. A few possible challengers include Scott Starring, Wolf Jager, and Mr. Smiley Mask. SJPW officials have stated they will be contacting Rumble Pro officials about possible setting up a title defense for the Rumble Pro world title at this event, though no challenger has yet been named to represent SJPW. CEO Yamashita has sent out a special notice that if Brody Thunder wins at J*STAR, he and Jun Suzuki will face off in a special best of three series of matches. Each match will have a different stipulation. The winner of the series will be granted a title shot at whomever is the Grand Prix Heritage champion at the time Block A: "Firefly" Horo Atashi (5-0-0) Mr. Smiley Mask (4-1-0) "All Star" Joey Darren (3-2-0) Jet Jaguar III (1-4-0) Toramusha (1-4-0) Fanta Boy (1-4-0) Block B: Wolf Jager (3-1-0) "Kyoto Tiger" Takayoshi Asakura (3-2-0) Takahiro KEOKI (5-0-0) Tsunami (1-4-0) Yatoro Hori (0-5-0) Super Bakemono (3-1-0) GPH Tag Team League: Van Wild & Scott Starring (4-0-1) "Hard" Knox Roberts & "Terrible" Tommy Bedford (4-0-0) Junya Toroyama & Super Bakemono (4-1-0) The Red Army (4-1-0) The Visual Spirits (3-1-1) The Maulers (2-5-0) The Crush Crew (1-6-0) The Nagasaki Bulldogs (0-7-0)
