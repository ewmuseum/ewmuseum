Tsunami, Part Three

29 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Tsunami continues...] TK: Welcome back everyone. We're just about set for our main event, but before we do, we have some comments from Jensei Tenru... AO: What does he want to say now? He's got a "title" to defend! TK: Wrong Tenru Akira. This is the other one. AO: Damn them both! They have the same name for buddha's sake! Just because they are spelled slightly different isn't enough! TK: Relax Akira. It isn't worth getting that upset over... [Scene: The busy metropolis that is Tokyo. It is near noon on Saturday. Tickets have just gone on sale for an upcoming Super Japan event. Fans are lined up from miles around it seems. Jensei Tenru is making an appearance at the on-sale. He is signing all types of Super Japan merchandise. He is joined by Hakiro Matsuoko and "Jigoku" Tiger Extreme at the autograph table. Tenru speaks first:] JT: Ah, my public! [Tenru stands on top of the table, in front of droves of fans.] WHO ARE YOU HERE TO SEE? [Yelling out to the crowd.] Crowd: "TENRU! TENRU! TENRU!" ["Jigoku" Tiger Extreme even joins in on the "TENRU!" chants. Tenru gets a roaring ovation from the crowd. He gets back to his seat and signs some more autographs.] JT: "Tokyo Titan", your little diatribe last week was about the most non-Japanese thing I have ever heard. You should know that Super Japan prides itself on the fact that we have have top level talent that can perform in Japan. Even the Gaijin, like Ace Kilcannon, Onslaught and Alan Sterling perfrom well here. You do not. I think you have been in America too long. You are a whore who smokes his cigars and makes movies that on their best day, I couldnt keep Tiger Extreme fascinated with. [A young boy asks Tiger Extreme for his autograph and he abliges by taking a bite out of the young mans program!] This should just go to show you: Just because you look Japanese and have a Japanese name doesnt mean you can perform Japanese! "Tokyo 'Roid Ripper" I know that when you look at me, you see what you wanted to be when you were a little boy. I know that you want so desperately to be me that you have sought out to sell your wares on television. But selling steroids? Good grief! How passe'! How very eighties of you... [Tenru is interrupted when he must sign the program that Tiger Extreme bit into.] ...Everyone in this crowd here today came to see ME! All the arenas that Super Japan sells out, I put those people in the seats. You can call me arogant, cocky...whatever. I AM _THE_ NUMBER ONE WRESTLER IN SUPER JAPAN PRO! These people come to the shows to see me. I have a superior work ethic, performance level, marketing scheme and I am just a better man. I dont say anything in my interviews that is not true. I am going to carry Super Japan on my back to the top position in the world of wrestling. Just like my father carried all his matches when he fought, so do I. I carry you, right now. I carried Hamada and Tengu as well. [In one quick motion, Tenru leaps on top of the table again.] And do you know why? Say it with me... [Tenru looks himself over as a blinding fury of flashbulbs go off as he poses. He smiles widely.] JT: [With the crowd and Tiger Extreme yelling as well] _I AM IT!!!_ [The crowd lets out a deafening cheer] I can only wish that my career was so hot and so on fire that I could make bad movies, sit on a beach and give myself lung cancer! HAHA! You are nothing here! You are not in demand...you are not on top of the world. I AM THE GOD OF THE PEOPLE! [Crowd cheers] AND I WOULD RATHER BE A 'FISHERMONGER' ANYDAY THAN TO BE THE MLWO WORLD CHAMP! [The crowd once again erupts into "TENRU! TENRU! TENRU!" as Jensei pumps his fists into the air with every chant.] AO: This man emulates Super Tengu more and more each day. TK: They are very similar, ne? Speaking of Tengu, how is he doing? AO: I don't know. He hasn't been seen since he left the hospital against the orders of his doctors. Tengu is Tengu. When he chooses to show himself, he will do so in grand fashion! TK: Very true. Now it is time for our main event. Jinsei Tenru is set to defend his MLWO Japan title against Frankie "The Thumper" Douglas of The Maulers. These two had quite an altercation earlier, when Doug Thomas faced Alan Sterling. AO: I don't understand why Tenru came to Sterling's aid... TK: More than likely, he did it to soften Douglas up for this match. AO: You're probably right. Tenru has no good reason to aid Sterling. I'm surprised he didn't hit him too! ______ ___________________________________ /_ __/__ __ _____ ___ ___ _ (_) MLWO Japan Shimeijiai / / / (_-</ // / _ \/ _ `/ ' \/ / Jinsei Tenru vs Frankie Douglas /_/ /___/\_,_/_//_/\_,_/_/_/_/_/________________________________/ [The camera pans to Nishi Toju & Owada Botan in the ring...] Nishi: This is our MAIN EVENT for the evening! [crowd cheers]. It is set for one fall, tv time limit remaining. It is for the MLWO Japan Juuryou Taitoru! Introducing first, the challenger. Accompanied to the ring by his tag team partner Doug Thomas, he hails from San Antonio, Texas, USA. and weighs in at 285 pounds... He is...FRAN-KIE "THE THUMPER" DOUG-LAS!!! ["Fall" by Click blares on the PA as The Maulers come out. Thomas has a sneer on his face, while Douglas betrays no emotion. Both men are wearing SHVF t-shirts, and Douglas has his head bandaged. The crowd boos both men as they head to the ring...] TK: It seems Douglas was injured by Tenru earlier... AO: Good! Traitors! They deserve all they get! Nishi: His opponent is the current MLWO Japan Juuryou Senshuken! He hails from Tokyo, and weighs in at 275 pounds....he is "THE JAPANESE SUPERSTAR" JIN-SEI TEN-RU!!! ["Carmina Burana" cranks on the PA, and Jinsei Tenru comes out, the MLWO Japan title around his waist. He is bathed in the light of two large spotlights. He pauses at the runway entrance to twirl around, showing of his armless, red sequined robe. He stops, and stares into the crowd. Expecting boos, he is caught off guard at the applause the crowd gives him, and raises his eyebrow in surprise. He smiles, then Fargo Struts to the ring....] TK: Tenru seemed surprised that the fans are behind him tonight. AO: Because he doesn't cater to them. However, they hate the SHVF traitors more than they do Tenru! [Tenru enters the ring, and twirls around again. He takes off his robe, and hands it to a ring attendant. he takes off his belt, and raises it over his head to the crowd. Douglas & Thomas charge Tenru, and before he can turn around, double clothesline him to the mat! The crowd boos loudly as The Maulers put the boots to Tenru. Owada Botan attempts to pull them off Tenru, but Thomas shoves him away. The Maulers continue to pummel Tenru with kicks...] TK: This is disgusting! Where is Misawa? AO: He's coming from the back now with his team! They'll take Thomas away in handcuffs! [As Misawa and his men head to the ring, one of the ring attendants slides into the ring, and pulls his hood off, revealing Alan Sterling! Sterling whirls Thomas around, and levels him with a lariat! The crowd cheers as Sterling drags Thomas to his feet, then whips him into the corner. Sterling grabs Douglas from behind, and headbutts the back of his head. Douglas tries to fight free, but Sterling applies a reverse facelock, then drops down into an inverted DDT! Sterling rolls to his feet, and charges Thomas, avalanching him in the corner! The crowd starts to chant Sterling's name as he throws Thomas through the ropes, then goes out after him. He holds Thomas down while Misawa handcuffs him, and the crowd cheers as Sterling hoists Thomas over his shoulders, and following Misawa, takes Thomas to the back...] TK: It seems Alan Sterling has repaid Tenru for his aid earlier... AO: Good for him! Misawa was sure happy he had Thomas down to be handcuffed! [Botan is counting both men out, as they slowly try to rise to their feet. Douglas makes it to his feet first, but Tenru is up shortly after. The crowd appplauds as both men lean on the ropes, catching their breath. Douglas gets to his feet first, and charges, but Tenru moves forward, catches him, and hits an overhead belly to belly suplex. The crowd cheers as Tenru covers, but Douglas kicks out at one...] TK: Tenru took quite a beating but he's not down yet! He stands Douglas up, then sends him back to the mat with an enzugiri! [crowd applauds] AO: Tenru back to his feet, and he legdrops the back of Douglas' head. He gets to his feet, and lands a second legdrop. Tenru back to his feet, and now he's playing to the crowd! [Tenru starts a mock "Tenru! Tenru!" chant, in obvious imitation of his namesake rival...] TK: Tenru needs to finish off Douglas, and stop antagonizing his nemesis... AO: Douglas is trying to get up, so he'd better hurry! [Tenru turns towards Douglas, and runs towards him, snapping an elbowdrop to his back. Douglas sags back to the mat, and Tenru gets to his feet, then grabs Douglas by the hair, and drags him to his feet. He attempts to whip him to the ropes, but Douglas reverses. Tenru comes off the ropes, and Douglas attempts a clothesline, but Tenru ducks under it, and runs to the opposite side. Douglas turns as Tenru comes off the ropes, and Tenru attempts a cross body block, but Douglas catches him! The crowd "ooohs' as Douglas turns to face all four sides of the ring before hitting a fallaway slam on Tenru. Douglas gets to his feet, charges Tenru, and catches him with a boot to the side of his head...] TK: Douglas seems to have turned things around now. AO: He's tenacious. I've fought him many times, and he's no pushover! [Douglas whips Tenru into the corner, following right behind him with a vicious clothesline that almost sends Tenru over the ropes to the floor! Douglas grabs Tenru by the hair, and starts to repeatedly ram his head into the turnbuckle. Botan orders him to break, and he backs off, then moves back in, kicking Tenru repeatedly in the gut with his boot. Tenru sags in the corner, and Douglas hoists him up onto the top rope. Douglas lands a right hand to Tenru's jaw, then climbs up after him. He stands Tenru up, and tries to hook him for a superplex, but Tenru blocks it, and starts to hammer Douglas' back with his forearm. Both men wobble on the top rope, but maintain their balance. Tenru sticks Douglas' head between his legs, then raises his arms up, enticing the crowd to start clapping their hands, and stomping their feet. He smiles when they do this, then locks his arms around his opponent's waist, lifts him up over his head, then leaps off the top rope, spiking Douglas to the mat with a vicious powerbomb! The crowd "oooohs" loudly, then applauds...] TK: Douglas has to be unconcious after that move! AO: It's got to be all over! Tenru hooks the leg... ...1... ...2... ...3...!!! **ding** **ding** **ding** [Crowd applauds as "Carmina Burana" cranks on the PA] Nishi: The winner of this match, and STILL MLWO Japan Senshuken... "THE JAPANESE SUPERSTAR" JINSEI TENRU!!! [crowd applauds again] TK: What a night this has been! We are now just one week from Sensou no Jidai. Up next, this saturday, Control Center! AO: That should be an interesting show, to say the least. TK: I'll say. For Akira Ono, I am Tiger Kikuchi. Goodnight! [Camera fades out on Jinsei Tenru standing in one corner, basking in the applause of the crowd...] _____ _____ _____ __ __ /\ _ `\ /\___ \ /\ _ `\ /\ \ __/\ \ \ \ \_\_\ \/__/\ \ \ \ \_\ \ \ \ \/\ \ \ \ \/` ____\ _\ \ \ \ \ ,__/ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ ` /\ \_\ \ /\ \_\ \ \ \ \/ \ \ \_/ \_\ \ \ \_____\ \ \____/ \ \_\ \ `\___ ___/ \/_____/ \/___/ \/_/ '\/__//__/ © 1998 http://www.calnan.com/sjpw/
