To SJPW handlers who monitor the ng! (the ISP-spam episode)

12-15 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
----- To SJPW handlers who monitor the ng! (Aug 12, 1997, 12:00:00 AM) ----- If this gets through, I need my handlers to do me a favor. Earthlink.net, in all it's infinite wisdom, claim that my mailing SJPW cards etc to my handlers is, of all things, SPAM, and therefore a violation of their account agreement. So, they have cut off my email. If you see this on the ng, please email: [detail withheld] and tell them that I wasn't spamming you. Customer Service won't help me, so I am taking my chances posting this here. If worse comes to worse, I may have to go back to aol after all.... [name withheld] SJPW ----- To SJPW handlers who monitor the ng! (Aug 15, 1997, 12:00:00 AM) ----- Just to let everyone know: After handlers, and at least one other fedhead [thanks Ian/NLWP] that I know of, earthlink turned my account back on late thursday afternoon. I just wanted to let everyone know, so that if you were planning on sending a message to my server on my behalf, you don't need to now. I am truly grateful to anyone not in SJPW who may have emailed them. I all ready thanked my handlers, so I wanted to make sure I didn't leave anyone out. I still find it amusing that they thought my SJPW mailing list [and another one I maintain for an email based music zine] were spam lists. I don't blame them though, but it is pretty funny. I just hope no other fedhead has to go through this; if you do, and you post something here, be sure that I at least will back you up if I know you run a fed. Thanks once more, [name withheld] SJPW president
