SJPW Cards

Zenmen Sensou

31 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[CAMERA fades in on Yukio "The Warbird" Murakami kneeling on a bamboo mat in the middle of his dojo. A large imperial Japanese flag hangs behind him. Murakami is dressed in his blue and white kimono. He stares coldly at the camera.] Yukio "The Warbird" Murakami: I have been silent for too long. My wounds have healed, but my spirit remains scarred. It is once again time for the Warbird to teach the SJPW a history lesson. [The Warbird stands motions to the flag behind him.] Murakami: In Japan, it is illegal for a man to display this flag. Our politicians feel that it will conjure up too many memories of the great war. Our politicians dishonor the courage and bravery of millions of fighting men, of the honorable citizens who remained at home, who all fought to prevent Japan from plunging into Western decadence. Before the generals took over power in Japan, the politicians had nearly bled the Japanese people dry. Farmers were being driven to starvation as a result of the politicians attempts to appease the West. Men of great military prowess were told that their time had passed, that the future was all about money and modernization. [Murakami closes his eyes. He is starting to get noticeably agitated.] Murakami: Following the war, that great crusade for independence from the influences of Western decadence, Japan crawled back into the belly of the beast. Our men were emasculated, our women were turned into toys of seduction, our children were hooked on pop music and flashy clothes. The memories of Japan, of samurai and bushido were all forgotten, thrown on the refuse heap of history. They no longer helped to maximize profits, to manufacture cars, to make electronic gadgets, so they were no longer needed. Now we are a nation of toy makers! When once we practiced the art of swordsmanship and marched across Asia, now we sell economy cars around the world! Japan is no longer a nation of men born of the blood of warriors! We are eunuchs to the whims of the gaijin. [Murakami suddenly stands up, grabs the imperial Japanese flag in his hand. His face is red with anger.] Murakami: Twilight Prince, do not tell me I am ignorant about Japan! I AM JAPAN! Japan flows through my veins! It is my destiny to lead Japan to a new period of spiritual glory! The Warbird decrees it! When next we wrestle, Tasogore no Prince, it will be the twilight of your reign as champion. A new, more worthy champion shall take your place. You are nothing but a weak-willed toady to the gaijin! [Murakami storms off camera. The crowd is cheering in the background...] Eddie Rose: Hello everyone. I'm Eddie Rose, and this is the "Legend" Akira Ono. [Ono bows] ER: We have an incredible show for you tonight on "Total Combat". We will see four title defenses, as the Welterweight, All-Asian tag team, Koubousen Geijutsu, and All-Asian Juuryou titles are defended on the show. Akira Ono: I'm not too pleased about Kikuchi facing Dean though. ER: It is probably the greatest challenge of Kikuchi's career. Even more so than when he faced Shinji Hamada! AO: Very true. I pray to Buddha that Kikuchi prevails! ER: In other action, Steel Serpent and The Mandarin join forces against Oleg Vladikoff and Vladimir Vorshenko, as well as Jushin Kojima facing Scorpio. AO: And Tetsuo Matsumoto faces Se-hong Kwan, a major step downward for the "Iron Man"! ER: In other events, and probably the only good thing Shinsaku Yamashita has done since being named acting CEO... AO: He's done something good? ER: As I was saying, Yamashita has granted airtime to Neo Japan Pro Wrestling, a new promotion in the land of the Rising Sun. SJPW & NJPW have signed a working agreement, and until NJPW fleshes itself out, we'll be seeing NJPW competitors on some of our shows over the next few weeks. AO: And this is a good thing? We give air time to some new, upstart promotion, instead of to our own competitors? ER: You always have to look at the negative side, don't you? AO: It's why they pay me the big yen. ER: Of course they do Akira. Our first match features Masato Kurasawa against Haruki Shiomi. Kurasawa is a pretty good welterweight, but as of yet, he hasn't found his niche. AO: Kurasawa is a good wrestler, but as of yet, he's been ignored. His challenge to Super Tengu has still gone unanswered, which makes me wonder if it is Tetsuo under that mask. ER: Good point. Tetsuo would never turn down any challenge from anyone! ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Masato Kurasawa vs Haruki Shiomi [J] /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ Shiomi started off hot with a series of armdrags, perplexing the smaller Kurasawa. Shimoi then executed a hiptoss, and Kurasawa kicked him off as he bent over to pick him up. Kurasawa got to his feet, then hit a standing dropkick. He dragged Shiomi to his feet, and hit a gutwrench powerbomb. He dragged Shiomi, took him to the corner, and sat him on the top rope. He climbed up with him, then hit a top rope superplex. Kurasawa ran to the ropes, and hit a quebrada for a two count. Kurasawa dragged Shiomi to his feet, and Shiomi hammered him with a series of forearm uppercuts. Shiomi attempted an irish whip into the corner, but Kurasawa reversed it, and followed in behind Shiomi with a lariat. Kurasawa hopped up on the top rope, and hit his Breath of Fire spinning DDT for the 3 count. Winner: Masato Kurasawa by pinfall. AO: Impressive win for the young Kurasawa. Hopefully now someone will pay attention to him! ER: One can hope! Our next match pits the returning Greg "Millionaire" Miller against Sorai Jinbo. Miller looked very impressiove in the TCW tournament to date. AO: That's because he wrestled mainly gaijin; if he runs into Hamada, he'll find himself overmatched! ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Greg Miller vs Sorai Jinbo [J] /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ Miller started off trash talking the crowd, so Jinbo took the opportunity to grab him from behind in a waistlock, then hit a german suplex with a bridge for a one count. Jinbo got to his feet, and landed a snap elbowdrop to Miller's chest, then started to choke him! The referee made Jinbo break, and he backed off. He then went back after Miller, dragging him to his feet, and throwing him into the corner. He started to lay into Miller with chops to his chest, but Miller was able to thumb him in the eye, then ram his head into the turnbuckle. Miller slapped on a full nelson, then hit a dragon suplex out of the corner. He ran to the ropes, and hit a quebrada, and covered for a two count. Miller dragged Jinbo to his feet, and hit a gutwrench suplex. He dragged Jinbo to his feet once more, hit his Millionaire Drop DDT, then applied the Bankruptcy STF for the submission win. Winner: Greg Miller by Submission. ER: Miller once again showing why he is one of the best technicians in SJPW. AO: Pfah! He is hardly that technical! A few suplexes and the STF is hardly the repetoire of a master technician. ER: Neither is the Ono Bomb. AO: Ah, but I had more than the Ono Bomb now, didn't I? Obviously you were sleeping during my interview with Kensei Tokugawa! ER: Not really. I still say Miller is a great technician. AO: Which is why you are paid scale. [Rose glares at Ono] ER: At this time, we go to Jonnie Adonis, who is set to interview the Welterweight champion... ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Tasogare no Prince Interview /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [The camera cuts to Jonnie Adonis standing in the middle of the ring...] Jonnie Adonis: Minna-san, would you please welcome at this time, the SJPW WELTERWEIGHT CHAMPION... TASOGARE NO PRINCE!!!!!! [The arena's lights dim as orange, pink and purple spotlights come on, filling the arena with the colors of the Twilight. Calm, sombre, yet soothing cello music plays over the arena's loudspeakers as white paper flower petals begin to float down from the ceiling. Tasogare no Prince steps out through the curtains leading to the backstage area, smelling the white orchid in his right hand. His purple trenchcoat is open, revealing the lavendar-colored SJPW Welterweight belt around his trim waist. As he enters, a loud cry goes up from a large group of young schoolgirls. As the Prince begins walking to the ring, the camera focuses on the girls, most of whom are seated together. Many of them grasp white orchids tightly in their fists, as they scream their adulation for the Welterweight champion. There are even a few girls dressed as the Prince among them. The Prince is about to enter the ring, when he suddenly turns and looks over towards the throng of admiring schoolgirls. He walks over to them, and the girls all reach out, offering him the flowers in their hands. The Prince smiles at them, but rather than accepting their gifts, instead gives the single white orchid in his hand to one girl at the very front, who accepts it tearfully. He touches her chin, lifts her face and smiles at her, eliciting yet another squeal from the schoolgirl mob. Then, he turns on his heel and climbs up into the ring, standing next Johnnie Adonis...] JA: Tasogare no Prince, welcome. TnP: Arigatoo, Adonis-san. It is a privilege to be here, as always. [He smiles...] JA: Prince, a lot of things have been happening in the SJPW as of late. Would you care to share with us your thoughts on some of them? TnP: I would be more than honored to do so. JA: First of all, I would be interested in knowing your thoughts on all the political upheaval that has plagued the SJPW lately? With Shinsaku Yamashita taking the reigns of SJPW from Jumbo Kobayashi... TnP: [frowns... Ah yes. A most unfortunate circumstance. I have never made a secret of my loyalty towards Kobayashi-san. After all, it was he who brought me to SJPW in the first place. Not to mention it was his faith in me that allowed me to be entered into the Welterweight tournament, and thus fulfill my destiny. Kobayashi-san, I look forward to your eventual, and rightful return, and I swear to you now, that you can expect the same loyalty from me as before. JA: What of Yamashita-san? TnP: Hmmph. I don't see how I can respect a man who has shown such disrespect to me. JA: What do you mean? TnP: I find the creation of another Welterweight title in SJPW unnecesarry and insulting. _I_ am the Welterweight champion. There need be no other. JA: But the new title is for the Koubousen Geijutsu division. It is completely separate from your title. TnP: Then it should be called something else. There is only _one_ Welterweight champion in SJPW, and that man is the Prince of Twilight. That was, and still is, my destiny. JA: And on that subject... Tonight, you will defend your Welterweight title against the man whom you defeated in the finals for that belt, "The Warbird" Yukio Murakami!! What are your thoughts going into this match? TnP: Murakami, it seems that this match has been put off and delayed again and again, but no longer. Tonight, in this very ring, we shall once again meet for my Welterweight title. At first, I was looking forward to facing you again. But now, I merely look forward to defeating you. Warbird, look out upon the faces of watashi no itoshi hana, my precious flowers... [the camera focuses once more on the group of young Japanese girls, all staring intently at the object of their adulation in the ring...] TnP: When you say that the Japanese people are weak, you say that _they_ are weak. When you insult modern day Japan, you insult that from which they sprang. Look upon the faces of those you have insulted Murakami. When you insult my dear, lovely flowers, you insult me, and I do not tolerate insults lightly. Tonight, you shall pay the price for your insolence. Tonight, I shall avenge the pride of each and every one of my flowers, and each and every man and woman in this arena. [A loud cheer goes up from the crowd...] TnP: Kore wa sakete toore me, anata no unmei. You cannot avoid your destiny. Murakami, tonight, you will enter once again into the Twilight, and again you will leave it _without_ the SJPW Welterweight title, and forever changed... [As the cello music begins again, Tasogare no Prince leaps nimbly over the top rope, exiting the ring. He once more goes over to the mob of young girls, taking a few of the white orchids they extend towards him, before heading down the aisle to the back...] JA: Ladies and Gentlemen, the SJPW Welterweight Champion... TASOGARE NO PRINCE!!!!
