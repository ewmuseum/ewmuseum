SJPW founding and recruitment drive (March 1997)

8-26 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
----- [SJPW] Hey, It's something different! (Mar 8, 1997, 12:00:00 AM) ----- Hello RSPWF, Sure this is another plug for a new e-fed, but since the subject line grabed your attention, might as well read what's so special about the SJPW no? What's so different about the SJPW compaired to other e-feds around the net? We're an e-fed that's based in JAPAN! I wanted to create something new and different from the usual "extreme" or "the hottest fed around", so I came up with an idea for a e-fed that's based in Japan. And you don't need to know Japanese either, since it's all written up in plain ol' english (I don't even know how to read Japanese, yet alone write it). We have one card a week, 4 titles in all, lot's of ideas and experirence behind the scenes. It will take me too long to explain all the details here on RSPWF about the SJPW, but all you have to do is go to the Super Japan Pro Wrestling web site and check us out. Trust me, it's WORTH IT! http://sjpw.home.ml.org Hope to hear from you! We still have room in all the divisions but spaces are filling up real fast, so don't miss out! I should point out that the SJPW is now a totally BOOKED e-fed! BTW, the SJPW has completely re-done it's web site with more features and links for both frames and non-frame browsers at: http://sjpw.home.ml.org Thanks, [name withheld] [detail withheld] ----- [SJPW] Looking for writters (Mar 26, 1997, 12:00:00 AM) ----- Like which e-fed today isn't looking for any writers eh? Well Super Japan is looking for some writers as well, so I might as well try my luck. The SJPW is looking for experienced writers to join it's staff. The work load isn't much, one match a week due by Saturday afternoon's at the latest. I do ask that the writers be on time and that they follow the strats which the handlers gave them. That's about it, if you interested please e-mail some past work so I can see your style. Remember, this federation is based in Japan. The wrestlers don't all use the same moves as they do in US style wrestling. But that shouldn't be problem to get used to. Hope to hear from you guys!
