SJPW House Show — Esashi Town Center Gymnasium (7/7/04)

Jul 8, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
July 7th, 2004 Esashi Town Center Gymnasium: 989 fans The second stop in the Junior Struggle! tour saw fans again almost selling out the venue! 1) Junior Struggle! Block A: "All Star" Joey Darren def Toramusha (9:17) after a top rope hurricanrana. 2) Junior Struggle! Block B: Wolf Jager def Yatoro Hori (10:09) after Hori submitted to the Strangle Hold Beta. 3) "Dragon Fury" Minobu Tetsuhara & El Santa Azteca def El Gato Loco & Fanta Boy (12:22) after Tetsuhara hit a wrist clutch Exploder on Fanta Boy. 4) Junior Struggle! Block A: Mr. Smiley Mask def Jet Jaguar III (12:48) after hitting "Shit Happens". 5) Junior Struggle: Block B: Takahiro KEOKI def Tsunami (15:00) after a High Impact Driver. - The new IICW cruiserweight champion defeated the up and coming Toramusha in an excellent opening bout that saw Darren use his aerial attack, while Toramusha mostly stuck to stiff kicks and suplexes. Darren had Toramusha set for a superplex, but he beat Darren down, and went for a top rope powerbomb, only for Darren to counter it with a hurricanrana, which stunned Toramusha long enough for the three count. - Yatoro Hori went 0-2 after Wolf Jager made him tap out. He started the match well, dominating Jager with stiff kicks, even getting a two and a half count after a superkick. However, Jager was able duck a second superkick attempt, and hit a high shining wizard to Hori's shoulder. From there on he worked the arm, and though Hori made a valiant comeback around 8:30, Jager was able to get him into the Strangle Hold Beta, and forced him to submit. - Tetsuhara & Azteca teamed up to take on two of SMLL's more bizarre competitors. Fanta Boy especially frustrated Tetsuhara by doing outrageous things like pinching his buttocks, and simulating humping his leg. Tetsuhara let his temper get the better of him, and nailed a wrist clutch Exploder, dumping Fanta Boy on his head, Azteca keeping Gato Loco from preventing his partner from being pinned. - In a battle of "super heroes", the debuting Mr. Smiley Mask made Jet Jaguar III go 0-2 himself after hitting his high angle shooting star press he calls "Shit Happens". Both men shook hands after the match, and the crowd was really into this bizarre, extremely large masked junior heavyweight. - In a highly anticipated match up, the rookie sensation KEOKI took on the veteran Tsunami. Both men spent a good portion of the match trading aerial moves, getting the crowd very excited. Tsunami actually hit the Tsunami Driver #1, and got a two and a half count on KEOKI. KEOKI fought is way back from that near pinfall, finally scoring the big upset win after his High Impact Driver.
