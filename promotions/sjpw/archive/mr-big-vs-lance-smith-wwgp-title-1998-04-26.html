SJPW: Mr. Big vs. Lance Smith, WWGP World Title Match, 26 April 1998

April 26, 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ER: Mr. Big hits the ring... and Big and Smith immediately start going at each other fist and fire! The bell hasn't even sounded yet, and Big and Lance Smith are trading lefts and rights in the middle of the ring! We knew there was bad blood between these two, going to when Mr. Big won the WWGP title from Smith, but time hasn't dampered that hatred. The bell finally sounds, though that's not really relevant at this point, and Smith and Big continue to trade blows.... Big catches Smith with a big right hand and Smith steps back and tries to retaliate... but he's blocked by
Big, and Big scores with another straight right hand. Smith punches... Big blocks... Big hits Smith... again Smith is blocked and again Big catches with his big right hand... Smith is staggering... and Big drops Smith t o the mat with a wild haymaker!
big pop from the crowd as Smith drops face first to the mat, then quickly rolls out of the ring trying to shake out the cobwebs]
AO: Smith is a fool if he thinks he can go blow for blow with a monster like Mr. Big. Big has close to 200 pounds on Smith, and an addition foot of height. Add to that equation the surprising speed of Mr. Big, and Smith had better start reconsidering his ring strategy if he wants to come out of this match alive, let alone with gold.
ER: Smith climbs back up onto the apron and tells referee Masa
Kobashi to get Mr. Big back. Big voluntarily steps back and Smith re-enters the ring. Smith rubs his jaw, and tells the official that
Big was using closed fists.
AO: So was Smith! ER: _I_ know that Akira, and so does Kobashi, who just ignores Smith's complaints. Both men circling each other now, preparing to lock up... and Smith backs off. Again they get set to lock up... and again Smith backs off. Big shrugs his shoulders to the fans, and... what's Mr. Big doing?
AO: Mr. Big is getting down on one knee, and putting one arm behind his back. He's daring Lance Smith to lock up with him now. I'm not sure how wise this is on the part of Mr. Big. Smith moves in on the champion... Big grabs Smith's tights and pulls him in, driving his own head into Smith's abdomen! Smith doubles over... EUROPEAN
UPPERCUT! Smith is on wobbly legs... Big is back on both feet and he grabs hold of Smith's neck! He's going for the chokesl....
Thumb to the eye by Smith... followed by a SINGLE ARM DDT! Smith with the cover.. 1... HUGE kickout by Big! He sent Smith about 2 feet backwards on that one. ER: Big slowly gets to a sitting position... and Smith comes up from behind and locks on a front facelock. Kobashi checks to make sure that forearm doesn't slide down into a choke.. but look at this! Big is pulling himself using the ropes, and Smith is still hanging on! Smith's feet are about a foot off the mat as he holds onto Mr. Big... Big turns around... and drives himself backward into the turnbuckle, crushing Smith! Big moves forward and then slams backward... and again Smith is caught between the champion and the turnbuckle. Smith releases the hold and is just keeping himself propped up in the corner. Mr. Big takes a few steps forward, rushes backwards... But Smith drops flat to the canvas and Big hits the turnbuckle.... Smith with a ... questionable...
blow that doubles Big o ver... DIAMOND CUTTER BY SMITH! AO: I can't believe Kobashi missed that blatant cheap shot by Lance
Smith! He's as bad as the officials who keep giving preferential treatment to everyone outside of SJPW in the King of the
Cruisers tournament. ER: Smith pulls Big up off the mat...irish whip... reversed by Mr. Big! Smith goes careening into the far turnbuckle... Big comes charging in with an Avalanche... but Smith catapults himself to the outside apron and Big eats turnbuckle! Smith leaps onto the top rope.... BULLDOG! Great a thleticism on the part of the former champion and Smith drops down for the cover...
1... 2... Only a two count as Big again kicks out. Smith pulls Big to a sitting position... bounces backwards into the ropes for momentum.... SOMERSAULT NECKBREAKER! I'm surprised Smith is targeting the neck of Big when his arm has already been injured on several occasions.
AO: Actually Eddie-san, as much as I hate to say it, it's a good strategy on the part of Smith. Despite the injuries to Big's arm,
Big still didn't submit to the Cross Arm Breaker of Ed Dean, and
Dean is much more cap able of forcing a submission with just the arm than Smith. Smith's best chance of forcing a submission is with his Too Sexy Submission version of a cross face chickenwing. Well, the arm is already primed... now he wants to weaken the neck. At least, that's what _I_ would do. I doubt Smith is smart enough to come up with that kind of strategy.
ER: You had me worried for a moment Akira. You almost got through that without insulting Smith. And it's Smith now in control of the match as he takes a few steps back, waiting on the champion. Mr.
Big is holding the back of his neck as he gets to his feet... but he's facing away from Smith and... MY LORD! SUPERKICK TO THE BACK
OF BIG'S HEAD! Big falls forward into the turnbuckle and Smith now moves to the top turnbuckle... SWINGING DD... NO! Big caught Smith under one arm... SIDEWALK SLAM! Only a seven foot, half-ton monster like Mr. Big could pull something like that off! Both men down on the mat, but listen to the fans pulling for Mr. Big!
AO: The fans began to take to Mr. Big just before his first encounter with Lance Smith, and they've only grown in their support for him. I have to admit, I never thought Mr. Big would succeed as champion, but I'm happy to admit I was wrong in this instance. He's proven to be an honorable and dedicated champion, the kind of champion SJPW needs.
ER: I agree with you there, Akira. Big has shown his courage in previous matches, and continues to show it here as he slowly gets back to his feet. Smith is also getting back up... Smith rushes in on Big... but Bigg ets his arm out and wraps that mammoth hand around Smith's neck! [big pop from the crowd] The fans know what's coming now as Big yanks Smith in towards him and... CHOKESLAM! What impact on that move, as Big drove him self down with Smith... that's close to half-a-ton just slamming you int the mat on the back of the head!
AO: And now the champion is drawing his thumb across his throat. He points to the top turnbuckle and the fans know what comes next
[the crowd is going into a small frenzy of excitement and a chant of "superslam" can be heard starting up]... the fans want to see
Lance Smith get the Superslam. Mr. Big pulls Smith off the canvas and sets him up on the top turnbuckle. The champion now gets on
the second rope... Smith with a quick poke to the eyes! Big stumbles off the ropes, but lands on his feet.... Smith comes off the top turnbuckle with a MISSLE DROPKICK! He caught Big square in the chest and both men are down on the canvas.
ER: The referee starts the twenty count, and right now neither man are moving. 1... 2... 3... 4... 5... Mr. Big begins to stir as Kobashi reaches five, and Lance Smith is already starting to drag himself towards the ring ropes...
6... 7... 8... Lance Smith reaches the ropes and has pulled himself up. That will stop the count. Smith now seems to be waiting on Mr. Big... what's
Smith going to try?
AO: Whatever it is, it had better be something effective. The longer this match lasts, the more I'd say it favors Big at this point. Usually it 's the smaller man who has more energy, but Big has shown he has a big reserve when the going gets tough. Big is getting to his feet.. Lance moves in.... SUPERK... BIG CAUGHT HIS
ANKLE! MR. BIG CAUGHT LANCE SMITH'S AN KLE... and he pulls up...
and then drives down, slamming Smith's leg knee-first into the mat... and again... and again... he's pulling Smith up and down like a top! Smith is howling in pain as Big continues to slam him knee first into the mat. Smith is in serious trouble now, because the majority of his offense lies in his ability to execute those aerial moves of his, and you can kiss mobility away if your knee gets blown out.
ER: That may not be a concern at this point Akira...Big pulls Smith in closer, dragging him along the mat... reaches down and picks him up around the waist... what's he... INVERTED POWER BOMB! I think
Smith may be unconcious after that! What impact! But Big doesn't go for the cover... instead... he's setting Smith on the top turnbuckle again. Big sets on the second rope... grabs Smith ...
and.......SUPERSLAM! Big hooks Lance Smith's leg as Kobashi drops down for the cover... 1... 2... 3!!! Mr. Big racks up yet another impressive victory, his second over Lance Smith.
