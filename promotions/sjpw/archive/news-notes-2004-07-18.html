News & Notes 7/18/04

Jul 19, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Gunryo Champion Despair, "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder, Akira Ono & Ken Otaku went immediately to the airport to catch a flight for North America after The Zone!, as they were all set to be part of TSTL 4, Night Two. Thunder had to have his face checked out before he could leave. Despair took a separate flight from the others, as he didn't want to be on the same plane with the man he was planning on facing in a barbed wire cage. The Super 8 Tournament's first round was held on the same night as the most recent The Zone! However, we have not gotten results regarding the match between GPH Champion Kentaro Kawasaki & Takahiro KEOKI. As soon as we get word, we will let you know. Super Japan has yet to pick someone to represent the promotion in the J*STAR tournament being sponsored by PWR. Rumor has it that CEO Yamashita might have Despair & Kawasaki square off to see who will represent the promotion in the tournament. El Santo Azteca is pushing Yamashita to book a Gunryo championship match between him and Despair. Azteca feels he has earned the right to face him by now, and after making Despair tap twice in a row on shows (even though it wasn't during a match), that he has the Gunryo champion right where he wants him. Yamashita however, contends that Se-hong Kwan deserves another shot, since he fought Despair to a double knockout this past week. More on this as it develops.
