(The camera opens on a room full of old men seated around a table. These men are the SJPW board of directors. At the head of the table stands CEO Jumbo Kobayashi, his head bowed down in shame. Jonnie Adonis stands behind him to his left....) Jumbo Kobayashi: I realize this looks bad, but I assure you that Dean's victory is only temporary. His next opponent, Shishi Fujiwara, can handle him.. Boardmember #1: That's what you said about Takizawa, and he let that stripling Nishida take his place, and the boy was beaten! Boardmember #2: Then you claimed Onslaught would stop him, and he failed. The same with Ace Kilcannon. Both of them came close, but Dean still made it to the finals. Boardmember #3: Finally, you claimed Hamada would stop him, and victory would be ours. Hamada submitted! He GAVE UP! That is worse than if Dean simply pinned him. He made him surrender! This is the worse humiliation we have seen since we made you CEO. Boardmember #1: This cannot go unpunished. You are the reason Dean was in the tournament in the first place. Therefore, the blame lies on your shoulders. You are the one who must be punished. Jumbo Kobayashi: I understand this. I will accept whatever fines the board levies on me... Boardmember #2: Fines? You think we would simply fine you for this? You are more arrogant than I thought! From this moment, until we decided whether or not to remove you from office, you are hereby suspended! [Kobayashi's eyes go cold, and he lowers his head] Jumbo Kobayahshi: Hai. I As Matsumoto is also suspended, I will make sure Adonis-sama is brought up to date on everything. Boardmember #3: That will not be necessary Kobayashi-san. [Kobayashi raises his head, his face covered by a questioning look] Boardmember #3: We have all ready chosen your temporary replacement [touches the intercom at his place] Kyoko, send them in please. And you not only allowed it to happen, but you condoned it! You are a disgrace! You couldn't keep K-Pro going, and you have disgraced Super Japan far worse than my infidelities ever did. Now Moditan laughs in our face, and PN Magazine will have a field day over Dean's victory! SHVF has won a great victory, and it is all your fault! You make me sick Kobayashi-sama, very, very sick. He has forfeited that right when he gave his tournament slot to Nishida. One by one, your precious little darlings will fall before Dean. Once they have fallen, sacrificing themselves to weaken him, then, and only then, I will choose a worthy opponent to face Dean, recapture the title for SJPW, and restore SJPW's honor! And when that day comes Kobayashi-sama, your days of running this company are OVER! [Kobayashi's eyes open wider at Yamashita's fanatical speech.
