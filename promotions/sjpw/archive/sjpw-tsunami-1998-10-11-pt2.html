Tsunami, Hour Two

11 October 1998

[Suddenly, the arena lights dim and heavy rhythmic thunder, the pounding of taiko drums fills the air with a deafening roar. A single white spotlight lights up and shines down into the center of the ring casting a circle of bright light which fades as it extends towards the ropes of the ring. Beyond it, little can be seen.] TK: What the..?!? What's going on??? AO: I don't know, this is completely unscheduled. [Abruptly, the taiko drums halt and in the breathless pause that follows, a loud male voice booms over the loudspeakers.] VOICE: Konnichiwa, Tora-san! This spectacle... is for you!! [A lone figure appears on the periphery of the light, dimly seen on the edge of the bright white circle which fills the ring. The man steps between the ropes and it can be seen that he is wearing a dark, business suit and is holding a microphone in one hand. Behind him, another figure steps onto the ring apron, his features still hidden in shadow. The man in the ring is Japanese and athletic of build and as he steps into the circle of light, his face can be seen and the crowd erupts in a cheer.] TK: Shoji-san!! I... I'm amazed... why is *HE* here?!? AO: Another mystery! He is not signed with SJPW but with Gunryo Pro!! And yet... that's Jumbo Kobayashi coming into the ring behind him! [Tiger Kikuchi is uncharacteristically speechless at ringside as his former adherent, Shoji Suzuki is joined in the center of the ring by SJPW's CEO. Kobayashi steps to one side as the man formerly known as the original Jet Jaguar, smiles down at him from the center of the ring.] SS: Hello, Tora-san, I hope you don't mind the surprise? And as you can see... [He indicates the tailored suit he is wearing.] SS: ...I am not here to wrestle. [Suzuki grins as a wave of laughter sweeps across the crowd. But he holds up his hand, gesturing for silence as his expression becomes more serious. He turns to Kobayashi who leans forward to speak into the microphone.] JK: Tora-san, if you please, we would be honoured if you would join us here in the ring. There's more to reveal than just a visit from an old friend. SS: Yes, sensei, I have some people who would like to meet you. [Kikuchi looks overwhelmed as a chant of "TORA! - TORA! - TORA!" rises from the audience. Standing, he removes his headset and climbs into the ring where he approaches Suzuki. Behind him, Eddie Rose steps out of the shadows to take his place at the commentary table. Suzuki bows deeply as Kikuchi steps closer who has a huge smile on his face as he embraces his young friend. Surprised and pleased, Suzuki steps back a pace as Kobayashi steps forward and shakes hands with Kikuchi.] ER: This has all the hallmarks of a very auspicious occasion! AO: Yes it does. There is a great deal of experience and tradition standing in that ring right now. [Applause continues to ring out for a moment and as it fades, Suzuki holds up his microphone to speak.] SS: Thank you, Tora-san, it is very good to see you. But... I'm afraid I did not come back to SJPW to compete. [At this, cries of denial and disappointment fill the air but again, Suzuki holds up his hand, asking for silence.] SS: No, my friend, I am here tonight for a very different reason. I am here... to introduce you to some friends of mine... [He turns to face the entranceway which is still hidden in the darkness, the only source of illumination in the arena being the lone spotlight shining down on top of Kikuchi and Suzuki. In the distance, just inside the entrance, another single spotlight comes on and shines straight down, illuminating a bright circle of light in the aisle. Over the P.A. system, the strains of "Eye Of The Tiger", familiar from the Rocky films, begins playing softly at first but then grows louder and louder.] AO: This has been planned for some time, I think. ER: I think you're right... these people are up to something! [Into the pool of light near the entrance step five men, all wearing identical tiger striped robes with hoods that hide their faces. The music grows louder as the men walk down the aisle toward the ring and as they leave each circle of light cast by a downward-pointing spotlight, another spotlight shines down in front of them so that a chain of bright pools of light lead them down to the ring. By the time the men climb into the ring, the music is quite loud and they form a loose semi-circle behind Suzuki. In the aisle, the line of lights shining down go out one by one so that the arena is again illuminated by only one spotlight shining down into the center of the ring.] AO: The rumours have been flying and they very well be coming true here tonight! [In the ring, Jumbo Kobayashi backs into a corner, watching the proceedings closely with an enigmatic smile on his face. Kikuchi looks puzzled as he tries to make out the men's faces but each lowers his gaze so that the hoods hide their identities. The music fades as Suzuki smiles once more and turns to face his mentor.] SS: Sensei, a while ago, you inspired a band of young warriors and I was honoured to count myself among them. But today, a new era dawns. Today, with your blessing, we will bring about a rejuvenation of a dream. Rising like a phoenix from the ashes, once more those who do evil will cower before..... TEAM TIGER! [A deafening roar of applause shakes the arena as the crowd leaps to its feet. The men in the tiger-striped robes remain still as Kikuchi gasps in surprise. Taking him gently by the elbow, Suzuki leads him over to the first man, stopping several feet in front of him.] SS: The first of the new Tigers, should you approve, is a worthy young man who I believe will become a shining light in our sport. He is young but he is fearless and devoted to the cause of ensuring that honour and respect are always defended. I give you... Shishi Fujiwara! [The first hooded man reaches up and pulls back his hood to reveal that he is the man formerly known as "Spirit Lion" in PJW. Fujiwara looks excited but keeps a serious expression on his face as he bows to Kikuchi. Nodding his approval, Kikuchi returns the bow as Suzuki holds the microphone for the young warrior to speak.] Fujiwara: With your approval Kikuchi-san it would be my honor to be one of the choosen five to carry on the tradition of Chiimu Tora. I can't promise a victory each time I step in the ring, but I can promise to uphold the standards set by yourself, and others whose tradition I follow, by displaying the same integrity, honor, and sportsmanship that have come to be recognized as the trademarks of Chiimu Tora. [Kikuchi smiles and nods his head. Suzuki then leads him to the next hooded man.] SS: Next, a man whom I am proud to call my friend. A warrior and a true samurai whose honour is unblemished and his courage unquestioned. As you taught me, I have taught him and he is called... Jet Jaguar II! [The second hooded man pulls back his hood to reveal the familiar Jet Jaguar mask. The fans cheer loudly as he bows to Kikuchi who smiles broadly and reaches forward to shake his hand in Western fashion. Jet Jaguar II is grinning broadly as Suzuki holds up the microphone so that he may address Kikuchi and the crowd in general.] Jet Jaguar II: I understand the importance of carrying on traditions. I have adopted the mask of Suzuki-san in the hopes of carrying on the legacy of Jet Jaguar and now I have the oppurtunity to help carry on the tradition of Team Tiger. I will do my best to fight the good fight, with your blessing. [The crowd cheers as Jet Jaguar II steps back and Suzuki leads Kikuchi to the next man in line.] SS: This next man is someone you may not know well, sensei. But I have fought not only against him in the squared circle but also side by side. I count him as a comrade, a man to be trusted and a valiant defender of truth. His name is... The Banshee! [The third hooded figure draws back his hood to reveal the green-and-white mask of The Banshee. Kikuchi gives him a friendly smile and a handshake but afterward, The Banshee bows deeply in respect.] Banshee: Though I am gaijin, Tora-san, your name has rung across the Pacific for many years and is legend in my homeland. I am honoured to meet you and hope that I may fulfill the legacy of integrity that you have built. [Suzuki grins at the masked man and then leads Kikuchi to the next man.] SS: The next candidate for the new Team Tiger is someone you are familiar with. And the finest elements of puroresuring flow through the very blood in his veins! He is definitely worthy of the honour of being a Tiger should you wish it so. He is... Mitsunori Takizawa! [The fourth man pulls back his hood to reveal the smiling face of the grizzled veteran, Takizawa. Kikuchi smiles warmly and embraces him, his expression one of immense satisfaction.] Takizawa: Who would have suspected this day would come, eh, Kikuchi? When you and I would stand in the ring and not come to blows. Make no mistake, Tora, I have not changed and will not change the way I wrestle. [smirks slightly] I'm a bit too old for that I think [smirks again]. Be that as it may, you can count on one thing, and that is that Mitsunori Takizawa will honor the tradition of Team Tiger as I have always honored the traditions of puroresu. [Finally, Suzuki leads Kikuchi to the final man and he chuckles as he figures out who it must be.] SS: And of course, Team Tiger would not be complete without a man who has walked through the very gates of hell to earn himself a place among its ranks. Show yourself... Shinji Hamada! [The fifth man draws back his hood and the crowd cheers as Hamada bows deeply to his former comrade and instructor. Kikuchi steps forward and draws him into an embrace and the two men slap each other's backs before stepping back to exchange handshakes.] Hamada: A path does not always go in a straight line, eh, Tora-san? How many twists and turns did our paths take before leading to this place. Through the darkest times I have had, you were always there for me... even when I seemed not to care. Even when I did things... things that I look on now with shame and regret... you were still working on my behalf. You exposed Super Tengu's collusion with Yamashita and paved the way for me to find redemtion in the eyes of my friends and family. You entrusted me with the honor of your mask and the traditions and honor it represents, and for that I salute you and promise that Chiimu Tora will uphold those values, now and always. [Suzuki steps back a pace and turns to face Kikuchi.] SS: Tora-san, my part in all of this is done. I am honoured to have been able to take part in the rebirth of the finest team the SJPW has ever seen. I may not be here in person but I will always be here in spirit and I will always walk by your side! [At a hidden signal, Suzuki and the five men simultaneously bow low to Kikuchi. Touched, he returns the bow and then looks up as Suzuki steps forward once more.] SS: But before I go, everyone would like to know your answer... do these men deserve to call themselves Team Tiger? Do they have your blessing to become the new Chiimu Tora? [Suzuki hands Kikuchi the microphone and steps back beside the line of wrestlers. Kikuchi pauses for a moment, taking a deep breath as he is obviously a little overwhelmed.] Kikuchi: Last year, I had a vision. That vision was an alliance of men who stood for all that is good in puroresu: Duty, Fair Play, and most of all, Honor. My first pupil, J.T. Ruckus, was a man of honor. Though he left SJPW in disgust, he exemplified all that I held dear. Though I failed with him, I never gave up hope. Shoji Suzuki [points to Suzuki, who bows] was next, a man who had so much promise. His ability to forgive a man like Takao Nakami showed the goodness in his heart, and it was that heart that redeemed Nakami, and made him worthy of Chiimu Tora. Kendo Masao was another worthy soul. [pauses for a moment, all choked up] Kikuchi: But Chiimu Tora also say dark days. Masashi Lanther, my oldest and dearest friend, turned his back on Chiimu Tora, and waged war against my pupils. He does so to this day. The blackness that eats away at his soul saddens me. Super Tengu once allied himself with Chiimu Tora, only to try and end Shoji's career for a title! And of course, all of you know how I almost lost not only my career, but my very life to bring Hamada-sama back to the light! [Hamada bows his head, tears streaming down his face. Takizawa puts a reassuring hand on his shoulder, and he stiffens, then raises his head once more] Kikuchi: Super Japan Pro Wrestling needs men like these. Super Tengu will never change, nor will Ace Kilcannon, or Jensei Tenru. Chiimu Lanther wants to destroy the legacy I once built. Shinsaku Yamashita, after poisoning the minds of Kenji Ishida & Shinji Usami, leads them to war against those who he claims betrayed him. [pauses to look at the crowd, who once more chant "TORA! TORA! TORA!"] Kikuchi: You ask if I give my blessing for these five men to become the new Chiimu Tora? The answer dear Shoji is.....YES!!! [The crowd erupts into applause, and the ring is flooded with streamers. Shoji and Kikuchi shake hands, then hug each other.
