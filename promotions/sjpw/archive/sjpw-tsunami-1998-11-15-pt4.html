Tsunami, Part Four

15 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera shows the ring attendants assisting Hashimoto out of the ring, as Onslaught heads to the back] AO: This next match is one we both regret having to call. Shinji Hamada, the current leader of Chiimu Tora, faces Masashi "Lightning" Lanther, one of the greatest competitors in all of Nippon. He's beaten Super Tengu, both of us, and won numerous titles around the world. Hamada also holds a victory over Tengu, and has held both the All-Asian and Cruiserweight titles. Still, this match weighs heavily on both our hearts. TK: I agree Ono-sama. Lanther was once my closest friend. Now, he hates everything I hold dear. It is his wish to destroy Hamada, as a way of striking at me... ______ ___________________________________ /_ __/__ __ _____ ___ ___ _ (_) All-Asian Juuryou Tournament / / / (_-</ // / _ \/ _ `/ ' \/ /Masashi Lanther vs Shinji Hamada /_/ /___/\_,_/_//_/\_,_/_/_/_/_/________________________________/ [The camera pans to the ring, where Nishi Toju & Owada Botan stand...] Nishi: This is a first round match in the All-Asian Juuryou tournament! [crowd cheers]. It is set for one fall, 20 minute time limit... ["Shock Me" by Ace Frehley blares over the PA] Nishi: Introducing First, weighing in at 233 pounds...MA-SA-SHI "LIGHTNING" LAN-THER!!! [Lanther steps out on the runway, holding his sword & shield, and wearing a long, flowing cloak. He raises his sword in the air, and the crowd applauds. Lanther strides to the ring, talking to the crowd. He enters the ring, then points the sword at Kikuchi & Ono..] AO: Why the nerve... TK: Calm down Ono! He's just trying to bait you! ["Phaedra" by Tangerine Dream comes over the PA] Nishi: His opponent is the current leader of CHIIMU TORA! [crowd cheers]. He weighs 235 pounds....SHIN-JI HA-MA-DA!!! [Hamada steps out onto the runway, and the crowd erupts into applause. Hamada soaks in the crowd's support, then strides to the ring, a look of confidence on his face] AO: It must make you proud to see Hamada-san walk in your footsteps. TK: Ono-san! Hamada walks his own path! (Owada Botan pats down both Hamada and Lanther, and checks their boots. He is convinced that neither of them have any weapons, and he signals for the timekeeper to ring the bell.) AO: Kikuchi-san, I know that calling this match is going to be hard for you. I know that Lanther betrayed your beloved Chiimu Tora. I also know that you can expect Hamada to try to get revenge for Team Tiger. I only hope that you can remain unbiased throughout the matchup. TK: I understand your thoughts Akira, and I can see why you might think this will upset me. What I think of Lanther does not much matter anymore. For now I am an announcer, and the TRUE leader of Chiimu Tora is in the ring right now. AO: The match starts off with a collar and elbow tie up, and Hamada brings Lanther down into a side headlock. Lanther immediately pushes Hamada off. Hamada rebounds off the ropes, and is met with a flying bodypress by Lanther. [crowd applauds] ...1... ...2... KICKOUT!!! [crowd applauds] TK: That was a nice move by Lanther. I may not like the man very much, but I have a respect for him. He is a great athlete. Hamada back up, and he charges at Lanther who hip-tosses him to the ground. Hamada is getting anxious out there. He is letting his emotions rule him, and that is BIG mistake. AO: You are right Kikuchi-san. Hamada again back up, and again taken back down by a hip-toss. Lanther quick to jump on Hamada this time as he plants a foot to the side of the head of Hamada. Lanther has had the advantage so far in this matchup. TK: Yes...You are right Ono-san. Lanther is really taking it to Hamada out there. As Lanther picks up Hamada, and German Suplexes him. [Crowd Applauds] ...1... ...2... ...3.NO KICKOUT!!! [crowd applauds] AO: BARELY...Hamada almost did not make it out of that bridge. Lanther goes to pick up Hamada again, but he is met with an uppercut to the jaw which totally amazes Lanther. TK: That was a desperation move on the part of Hamada. AO: You are right Kikuchi-san. Hamada felt that he had to come up with some kind of an offensive move, and he did. Now let's see if he can capatilize. Hamada DDT's Lanther to the ground, and then rebounds off the ropes, and drops a leg. TK: Hamada then goes into an armbar. He locks it on, and Lanther screams in pain, but will not submit. AO: Hamada is a MASTER of submission wrestling. There is NO WAY that Lanther is going to be able to equal him in that category. Lanther better do something fast. TK: Hamada let's go of the armbar, and kicks Lanther in the face. He then grabs a handful of hair, and picks Lanther up. He places Lanther in between his leg's and Powerbombs him to the floor. Hamada then goes down for the pin. (Owada Botan drops to count.) ...1... ...2... ...3.KICKOUT!!! AO: In the words of the Americans, Close but NO Cigar. [Smiles] TK: You hit the nail right on the head Ono-San. Hamada looks out at the fans, and asks if he should go for another one. [Huge Pop] Hamada grabs a handful of hair once more, and picks up Lanther. He places Lanther in between his legs, and hoists him up. AO: HURRINCANRANA!!! TK: Nice move. AO: That is was Kikuchi-san. Lanther back up to his feet. He irish whips Hamada into the ropes, and on the way back Hamada is met with a Spinning Heel Kick. Lanther jumps up to his feet, and drops an elbow to face of Hamada. TK: Lanther climbs up to the top rope, and waits for Hamada to stand up. Hamada stands up, and does not see Lanther. He then turns around, and is met with two feet across hi face as Lanther Dropkicks him from the top rope. I hope that Hamada is ok after that one. AO: Lanther going for the pin. (Owada Botan drops to count.) ...1... ...2... ...3.KICKOUT!!! TK: Another near fall, but Hamada managed to pull out of it. Lanther picks up Hamada, and and whips him into the turnbuckle. He then runs into the turnbuckle at full speed, and plants a knee to the gut of Hamada. AO: Lanther is REALLY laying it to him out there. He wants to get this match over with. Hamada is not looking too well. He may get beaten here tonight by one of sworn enemies. TK: Lanther picks up Hamda, and sets him on the top turnbuckle. Lanther climbs right up in front of him, and he then places Hamada's arm around his neck. AO: SUPER-PLEX!!! A DEVASTATING MANUVER!!! TK: Lanther slowly gets back up this time. Hamada has made no signs of motion at all. Lanther gets up and climbs to the top rope. He seems to be a little bit dazed himself, but regardless he Moonsault's off the top rope, and lands it on Hamada. AO: Kikuchi-san, I am sorry to say this, but I think Hamada might VERY WELL get BEATEN here tonight. TK: Ono-san, you can not win EVERY battle that fight. I have always treated Hamada like my son, and would hate to see him lose, but I told him that a LONG time ago. AO: Lanther gets back up, and picks up Hamada. He places Hamada in between his knee's and he hoists him up into the air. Lanther runs all the way across the ring, and drops then with as much force as he can drops down with a Powerbomb. TK: Ono-san, that is a devastating manuver. Made famous here in Japan by MANY young athletes. AO: Yes...It is a devastating move. A move, that can easily win you a match. Lanther get's up, and goes for the pin. He hooks the leg. (Owada Botan drops to count.) ...1... ...2... ...3.KICKOUT!!! [crowd applauds] TK: NICE...Hamada with the kickout. Lanther picks Hamada back up, and goes for a Suplex, but Hamada hooks his leg around Lanther's. Lanther can not get him off of the ground. A VERY SMART move on the part of Shinji Hamada. AO: DEFINITELY, Hamada then goes for his own Suplex, and with that TREMENDOUS amount of upper-body strength gets Lanther up off the ground, and over top of him. TK: Lanther is not worn down though, and is quick to get up. Hamada kicks Lanther in the gut though and DDT's him to the ground. Hamada is quick to get down on the ground, and he locks in the STF. AO: That is a VERY painful manuver, and it will quickly wear down Lanther. TK: The referee now asking Lanther if he wants to quit, and Lanther say's "NO" AO: Kikuchi-san, one could not expect Lanther to quit. He is hardly worn down at all, and he is not known to quit anyway. TK: This is VERY TRUE Ono-san, but the STF will quickly wear an opponent down. As you can see right now. The STF has been applied for about a minute, and now Hamada is wrenching back on it some more. The fight Lanther had in him, is quickly relinquishing. AO: You are right Kikuchi. TK: It has been WELL over two minutes now, and the fight Lanther had in him is almost completely gone. He is not even responding to the referee when the referee talks to him, but he has NOT give up. Hamada figures that he has had enough, and he stands up. AO: I KNOW WHAT IT'S TIME FOR!!! TK: So do I Akira, but don't get too excited as of yet. Hamada asks the fans should he finish off Lanther. [Huge Pop, and Chants of "YES" can be heard.] AO: Hamada grabs Lanther by the hair, and picks him up. He waits a couple of seconds, and then NAILS THE TIGER DRIVER 2000!!! TK: It is over with now, Akira. (Owada Botan drops to count.) ...1... ...2... ...3...!!! **ding** **ding** **ding** AO: It's over TK: Shinji Hamada with the win. Announcer: Ladies and Gentlemen. Here is your Winner...SHIN-JI HA-MA-DA!!!! [The crowd stands on it's feet, applauding] AO: What a match! Hamada has defeated one of the greatest wrestlers in all of Nippon! TK: Yes, he did. We are out of time for tonight, but we will see you soon, as we bring you another...TSUNAMI!!!
