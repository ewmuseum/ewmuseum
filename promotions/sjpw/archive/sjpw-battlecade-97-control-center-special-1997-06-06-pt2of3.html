Battlecade '97 Control Center Special

6 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ER: The next match is one of two matches featuring HCW wrestlers against an SJPW wrestler: ------------------------------------------------------------------- Poe vs Slash (Shredders) ------------------------------------------------------------------- AO: Slash is a tag team specialist, so who knows how well he can handle himself in singles competition. Of course, he will most likely have his tag team partner handy, so this could give him an advantage over his opponent. ER: We were sent a tape of these comments made by Poe about his appearance at Battlecade: ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 6/5/97] [Fade in to an exterior shot of the D.I.A. FORTRESS...it's megalithic features causing a twinge of fear, REAL fear, in the minds of those who view it against the black of night. As the iron gates swing wide to reveal the giant oak doors, "Misdirected Hostility" by 311 fades into audibility and the dorrs swing wide. QUICK through the dark and twisted labyrinth which leads to the spot where Lord DOOMSDAY and his loyal followers reside...the Room of DOOM. With the music now blaring, the camera moves to the wrestling ring in the center of this large room, and then on to the walls. Upon these walls are three murals and a giant video screen. One mural has Lord DOOMSDAY holding aloft the Hardcore Title belt against a stormy background, the second shows Damon ZeX applying the Spider Claw to a helpless opponent as hundreds of his fellow arachnids litter the web behind him, and the third is of Poe about to bring down his SingaPOE cane upon the head of a fallen dragon...the video screen is blank save for the large red letters--P, O, and E. The camera then moves back down to the ring where Poe awaits his time to speak] Poe: Hellooooooooooooooo S J P W!!! You are looking at the one and only Poe Daddy!--The Master of Mischief himself but put your money away cause I don't need your tips! So there I was one fine day, constructing plans to completely humiliate all the wrestlers I hate, and I get this call from the President of the HCW saying, "Poe, you're going to the SJPW for an exhibition"...So I say, "No, I'm going to Diamonds for some brew and naked women". And then he says, "You ARE going, and there's many GREEN reasons why". WELL I'M HERE!!! Roll out the red carpet because Poe Daddy has come to put the beat down on the first lucky fool that steps to him. And I hear say it's HCW rules apply so get ready for a no DQ, Poe's gonna bash your brains in circus of mayhem! I can't say much else right now because none of you jackasses has signed on the dotted line, so I bid you adieu...Bad 4 Life! Can you handle it?!!! [Fade out] ------------------------------------------------------------------- AO: This man talks a good fight, and from what I hear from my US sources, he can back it up too! ER: Slash will definitely have a task ahead of him during that match. Afterwards, his partner is involved in the following match: ------------------------------------------------------------------- Hawk the KillerFragger vs Slice (Shredders) ------------------------------------------------------------------- AO: Hawk is a decent wrestler, but again Slice has the edge because his partner will be nearby. ER: So will Hawk though. Hades is set to appear in the HCW tag team match, so he can negate Slash's presence. We have these pre-taped comments from Hawk.... ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 6/6/97] [Hawk the KillerFragger standing with Hades, and Enforcer who is on crutches] HAWK the K/F: Yeah, Hawk da KillerFragger here, wonderin' if any of you feebs in dose other feds wanna get it on wit da Main Man here in da HCW? Or are ya all yellah? . .[Cracks knuckles as the bWo smile] ------------------------------------------------------------------- AO: This man is a brawler. I am unsure of his abilities myself, but we shall see. Following this match, we will turn control over to the NACW broadcast team, who will do the play by play of the NACW tag team tournament finals between the Mafioso and the Space Warriors. It looks to be an exciting matchup. ER: I hear both teams are eager to get this over with, so they can claim those titles! Following that matchup, we will take control again, and get ready for the next event: ------------------------------------------------------------------- Sung "The Catman" Kim vs Steel Serpent [Death Match] ------------------------------------------------------------------- AO: Steel Serpent is a master of Thai kickboxing. He should have no trouble defeating Sung Kim. ER: Kim is an accomplished Martial artist himself! AO: True, but Thai Kickboxers are some of the best in the world! ER: Add in the presence of Victor Creed and Shaolin at ringside, and you have a volatile situation. Sharon Akiyama went to Steel Serpent's warehouse again, and conducted the following interview... ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 6/2/97] (Scene opens with Sharon Akiyama entering the warehouse training facility of Vic Creed. She walks towards the wrestling ring where Steel Serpent is sparring. The training partner is caught in a standing armbar. Steel Serpent armdrags him to the mat, where he rolls the opponent into an anklelock. The man taps the mat. Steel Serpent releases the hold, and both men stand again. Seeing Sharon Akiyama, Steel Serpent rolls out of the ring.) Steel Serpent: Yes? Sharon Akiyama: I was just in the neighborhood and I thought I’d stop by and see how your training was coming. (She eyes him hungrily.) By the way.. you look simply yummy today. Steel Serpent: Get to the point. Sharon Akiyama (slightly hurt): Yes, well, what do you think of your chances in the Battlecade Match? Steel Serpent: Despite what those ignorant announcers think, I feel I have an excellent chance to win the Battlecade Title. I have watched tapes on most of my opponents for the upcoming Battlecade match, and I’ve found flaws in their technique, their style. Flaws that I will take advantage of. I have not come here just to be a spectator and watch someone else win gold. I have been a champion before. I have been trained by one of the best wrestlers in the world. I know what it takes to be a champion, and I’ll do what it takes. And More. Sharon Akiyama: Wow! You seem ready. What about your Deathmatch against Sung "Catman" Kim? Steel Serpent: He claims to be training heavily. He’d better be. No one trains harder than I do. No one wants victory more than I do. Sung Kim, you may question my intelligence all you want, but in the end I WILL destroy you and continue to the Battlecade match, where I will become Battlecade Champion. You think you will defeat me twice in one night? Rest assured, you’ll be lucky to leave the ring unaided after our first match. The most destructive force in SJPW is Steel Serpent. I’m ready to prove it. Dragon Special, Survival, Goro Honda, the list goes on. I’ll prove my style is superior to any talent you have. In the end, Steel Serpent will stand victorious surrounded by broken, bloody bodies. ------------------------------------------------------------------- AO: This man is one of the most focused competitors I have ever seen. I predict that he will win his deathmatch, and advance to Battlecade as one of the top draws to win. ER: We'll see. That death match may take more out of him than he thinks it will. Both matches will surely test both men's stamina, as win or lose, they both advance to the Battlecade Brawl later that evening. AO: After this match, we have the two semi-final SJPW Woman's title tournament matches: ------------------------------------------------------------------- Baby Hotta vs "Heartbreaker" Robin Blaze ------------------------------------------------------------------- ER: Baby Hotta pulled off an upset over Dawn Rose; can she do the same to Robin Blaze? AO: Unlikely. Did you see how Robin defeated Rekka Sakura? She was one of my picks to advance to the finals. ER: So was Dawn Rose. AO: That was an upset. A fluke. Robin Blaze is a consumate wrestler. She will prevail over Baby Hotta. That I am sure of. ER: We have these comments from Robin Blaze... ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 6/3/97] [Robin Blaze is sitting at a table in the center of a toy store. She's surrounded by young children, who are waiting to get Robin's autograph on the Robin Blaze doll box. Beautia Bedlam is standing solemnly behind Robin.] Robin: "I'm so happy to see so many of my fans here today. You all love me, don't you?" [there are a number of different opinions voiced, but Robin takes no notice of any negative ones.] Robin: "Now... you all know that there are very few role models in the world today, but look how fortunate you all are. You can find at least THREE wonderful role models in Super Japan Pro-Wrestling. You have Super Tengu, who is a wonderful man, and perhaps someday he'll find a REAL woman. You have Mimi Honda, who is always trying to instill the proper values in your young minds. Naturally you have me, as well. You all want to be like me, right? Beautiful, strong, self-reliant..." small child: "um... if you're self-reliant, why do you have Chyna as a bodyguard?" Robin: "Silly girl... this isn't Chyna. Chyna is a man-woman... like Reeking Semura. Beautia Bedlam is 100% woman, and it's her job to make sure petty women like Reeking Sempura and Hanako Wu don't try and assault me. After all, those two slatterns are jealous of me, and for that I can't blame them.. but I need Beautia to look out for me while I'm in the ring destroying my opponent. For example, I have a match coming up with that mentally challenged Baby Hotta soon. Now, I have to concentrate on giving her the beating of her life, so Beautia will be making sure that Wu or Sempura don't interfere. You all love me more than you love Baby Hotta, right?" [again, mixed responses] Robin: "Hmm... I think a few of you like Baby Hotta more than me. That's ok. After I destroy her in our match, you'll have no choice but to love me more... since she'll have to retire due to the extreme nature of the beating she'll recieve. After that, I'll go on to become the SJPW Women's champion, because gold looks so good on me. Now I have to go, kids, but remember... the next time you pass by your parents bedroom and you hear them making strange noises... it's just your Father pretending he's wrestling ME." [Robin finishes signing a few more autographs, then she is escorted out of the store by Beautia as the camera fades out] ------------------------------------------------------------------- AO: Robin Blaze is a much better roll model than Baby Hotta! ER: I don't know if I want to have either of them be a role model! The second match in the semi-finals pits Pizzazz, whose arm was injured at the hands of Mimi Honda, battle the woman known only as Terra. We heard from Terra recently; let's see what Pizzazz has to say about her match with the mistress of suplexes... ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 6/4/97] [Fade in: The camera focuses past a bouquet of dead red roses to focus on Pizzazz combing through her hair as she gazes at herself in the mirror.] Pizzazz: Terra, I want to you to ask yourself quelquechose. Are you woman enough to defeat me? Non. Are you attractive enough to make a good championnat? Non. I am destine to be in ze finals wiz zis Robin Blaze. Pourquoi? Because we are ze true competiteurs in ze SJPW. [Pizzazz runs the brush through her hair lovingly.] Pizzazz: You, 'owever, are a big, bad 'oman, n'est ce pas? You zink you are big enough to defeat me? You zink you 'ave what it takes? Moi, je dis non. I do not zink you can even compare. I zaw what you did to zat big woman, but maintenant you are facing ze reine du monde. You are facing ze Queen. Terra, I 'ave studied you. You are not good enough on ze mat. You are not resourceful enough. And you do not 'ave ze killer instincte zat I 'ave. I am ze sauvage. I will zink no'zing of ending your carriere. I promise you zis. Zere will be 'ell to pay. Ze 'and of 'ate awaits you. And zen I am one step closer to ze championnat. One step closer to recreating ze world of the SJPW in mon image. Don't worry. Zis will be ze true mesure of your talent. Zis will be ze end of your aspirations. Salut ma belle. Soon we shall dance zat dance of death. Soon I shall lay you to rest. [Pizzazz blows a kiss into the camera and winks as the camera fades to black.] ------------------------------------------------------------------- AO: I like Pizzazz, but I am concerned about the damage Mimi Honda inflicted on her arm. Plus, Terra has mastered many forms of the suplex. She is truly formidable. ER: Of course, Pizzazz has Derek Rage by her side. This man is HUGE! I'd love to see him take on Mr. Big or Victor Creed in a one on one match! AO: Derek Rage has better things to do than wrestle in SJPW. He has the IIWF tag team titles to defend! ER: You know we don't recognize those belts Akira. AO: So? I do. A title is a title. Winning them is what makes a wrestler great. There is more to wrestling than the GIWA! ER: I know there is; we just don't recognize most of it! The next match at Battlecade has been dubbed the "Battle of the Legends"... ------------------------------------------------------------------- El Perfecto vs Tiger Kikuchi ------------------------------------------------------------------- AO: Tiger Kikuchi is one of the best wrestlers in Japan. He's been a champion many times in the past, both in singles and tag team competition. ER: And El Perfecto is also a former multi-title holder. Most recently, he was the HCW World Champion. He's on his way to MLW and WAR!, but agreed to a one time appearance at our PPV to wrestle a man many consider to be his japanese counterpart. We had a press conference when El Perfecto arrived in Japan... ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 6/5/97] [Scene opens with a large crowd of journalists and reporters in a large arena, awaiting the arrival of El Perfecto for his first press conference in Japan. They are speaking quietly amongst themselves, as two bodyguards and several SJPW promoters enter the arena, closely followed by El Perfecto, who's wearing black jeans, a dark green sports jacket, his trademark shades, and a black baseball cap. He's chewing some gum and wears a familiar arrogant smile, but seems a little jetlagged. He shakes hands with two of the promoters, before taking a seat in the middle of the table, between the various promoters, as a journalist in the front row hurriedly asks the first question.] Journalist: El Perfecto, welcome to Japan. It's good to see you competing in SJPW on the Battlecade card. I'd like to ask you about the actions of you and your student, Jonnie Adonis, in your most recent appearance on a wrestling card, in HCW, when you........ El Perfecto: No comment......I'll speak about that when the time is right. At the moment, I'm suffering from jetlag, I'm tired, and I'm not thinking strainght. Next question please. [A reporter near the back raises his hand, before asking his question, in broken English.] Reporter: El Perfecto, again welcome to Japan. Is it true that you are already familiar with some of the SJPW competitors? [Perfecto begins to answer the question in an almost disinterested tone.] El Perfecto: Yeah, I believe I've competed in the same federation as two of SJPW's finest. I competed in BAHW with "The Perfectionist" Nik Nyce, very briefly. In fact, I believe he sat in for color commentary during one of my matches......Nice guy, no pun intended. And of course, there's my old pal, Survival......a man I have a great deal of respect for......but a man I absolutely despise, and who despises me. Yeah, Survival and I know each other real well. It's just a shame I hate the man's guts. [Several reporters speak simultanaeously.] Reporters: Why? [Perfecto takes a drink of water, before smiling. He seems slightly amused.] El Perfecto: Let's just call it.......artistic differences. [A reporter in the third row raises his hand and begins to ask another question.] Reporter: How do you feel about competing in SJPW, and on it's first supercard, against one of wrestling's finest competitors, a true legend in the sport, Tiger Kikuchi? El Perfecto: Hey, it never made any difference to me if I was fighting Poindexter The Jobber, or one of wrestling's legends. I'm sure you all know El Perfecto's mantra...."The Legend.......The Legacy". It's not my calling card for nothing. You see, I've earned it. I've fought the biggest, the baddest and the most dangerous men on the planet, throughout my career, and believe me, I haven't ended up on the losing side too often. Tiger Kikuchi won't be the first person, with a great reputation I've knocked off his pedestal......Isn't that right Survival?.......and he sure won't be the last. And as for SJPW itself, it's an honour to compete here, and it's an honour to be able to show the people of this country the type of false idols, they've been worshipping. [A journalist in the front row raises his hand and asks the next question.] Journalist: Are you a fan of Japanese wrestling? [Perfecto takes a sip of water, before he begins to answer the question.] El Perfecto: You bet I am. I've always strived to be the best I can possibly be, and I've always considered myself to be one of the sport's great technicians, and hey, what other country has technicians of the quality of Japan? I've learned a lot from Japanese wrestling, but I've also advanced from the majority of wrestling styles, with a combination of the best aspects of US technical wrestling, and the the best aspects from Japan. Promoter: One more question. Mr. Perfecto needs to get some sleep. [A reporter in near the middle of the crowd raises his hand and hurriedly asks his question.] Reporter: Would you ever consider competing in Japan on a more regular basis? El Perfecto: Well, I never say never, and it may well be an option if things don't go as planned back home. I've already broken most of my ties with the United States, and in two weeks, I'll begin competing in Canada, in MLW. Sometimes, the US scene just gets you down, and you need to get away from it. But if things don't work out in Canada, I might just start working here full-time. Perfecto takes another sip of water, and slowly stands up.] El Perfecto: Actually, after my performance at Battlecade, I'll undoubtedly receive numerous offers from US federations, anxious to secure my services......so I guess we'll just have to see what happens. Thank you very much. [Perfecto leaves the arena, surrounded by bodyguards and promoters, before the journalists and reporters begin to leave, as screen fades to black.] -------------------------------------------------------------------
