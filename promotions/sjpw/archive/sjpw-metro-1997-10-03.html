Metro

3 October 1997

Surrounding the ring is a gray chain link fence. Sitting at ringside and all around the ring in the large room are young people dressed in "Beatnik" fashions in beanbag chairs, benches, picnic tables, and lawn chairs. The aisleway is just a clearing between the assembled crowd and the walkway. The enteranceway is two heavy doors which are still closed. The lights go down and a blue spotlight swirls around the room and focuses on center ring where the lights project the "Metro" logo on the ring mat in flashing strobe. The camera cuts away as the crowd cheers to the makeshift desk of 4 oil drums and a large piece of wood. Sitting at the desk are Tania Goodwin and William Mercker. Goodwin is wearing a white sleevless blouse and khaki long pants with black riding boots and Mercker is wearing a white polo T-shirt and black jeans with steel toed boots.] Goodwin: Hello there SJPW fans and welcome to the very first action packed edition of SJPW Metro! I'm Tania Goodwin. And sitting beside me is the man who made "Thunderkiss '65" a national anthem, William Mercker. Mercker: Thank you my child. We are here at the lovely hip Met Studio deep in the heart of Tokyo to bring you the very first show put together by new SJPW co-CEO Jinsei Tsumaru. We can only hope that he doesn't decide to have two different brands of toilet paper fight it out on the show. Thats all he's concerned with....buissness. Goodwin: I'm sure he has better things to do with his time. Tonight on the innaugural edition of Metro, we're going to have quite a main event for you when --- takes on --- in what is sure to be one hell of a battle! Mercker: Don't tell me, tell those losers who're watching New Japan Pro. Hey! Has Hiroyoshi Tenzan dropped one to a 40 year old yet this week? If he hasn't, then you're not getting true NJPW! Ah ha ha ha ha ha! Goodwin: Oh how charming you are. Let's go down to the ring for our very first match on Metro! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Alex Akira v. The Black Fog =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Alex Akira made the very first match on SJPW Metro something to remember. He battled the mysterious man from "The Deep Dea" and kept him off balance with various technical moves and submission-style tactics. The crowd got into this match early and several times, almost choked on their Euro-chic capuccinos after an Akira highspot. Akira strung together an impressive series of rolling reversals and tech- nical mastery in order to set up Black Fog for the Master Submission hold. Akira sinched in his version of the Stepover Toehold Facelock and within seconds, Black Fog gave in to the devestating hold. Winner: Alex Akira with the Master Submission (STF) Time of the fall: 4:16 [The camera cuts from Alex Akira leaving the ring to respectful applause and a smattering of boos as "Hate to be Nice" by the Scorpions plays inside the small building. He dismisses several fans as he walks through the large doors and back to the dressing room. The camera cuts to Mercker and Goodwin in the booth.] Goodwin: Well, there you have it. Alex Akira, the impressive young man from Los Angeles, California gets a win here on Metro and we're sure to see more from him in the very near future. Mercker: We'll see how cocky that little punk is after he takes on some real competition here in SJPW. Black Fog isn't the highest available opponent in SJPW and with each little match he wins, that leads to a bigger match and so on. Why don't we all just wait and see what Alex Akira can do when he has to get in the ring with some real talent. I wish him all the best, but we'll have to wait and see if he's worth the praise. Goodwin: Is he enough to make you want to get back in the ring? Mercker: Don't even get me started there! Goodwin: I won't even try. Let's go to Jay Estes in the Metro News Center and see what we have thats new on the fire. [The camera cuts away from the booth and cuts to Jay Estes sitting in an office chair behind a black wood glossy desk. Estes is a young man in his early 30's and he is wearing a black Planet Hollywood jacket with black jeans and a white T-shirt underneath. He is a handsome man with a "showbiz" smile, which he flashes when the camera cuts to him.] Estes: Hello there SJPW fans and boy, am I glad to be here on my Friday night rehashing the weeks news for all of you. But to be positive, we do have some somewhat interesting news that concerns the SJPW United States tour which is coming up in October, aptly named "Invasion USA." Estes: It seems that the co-main event for the Pay Per View will be a "Hell on Earth" matchup. A barbed-wire, time bomb ring surrounded by beds of nails. If you ask me, and nobody does, this match sounds like a Epidemi- ologist's dream come true. Better have the teatnus vaccine on hand for this one. [The camera goes to a slit screen between Goodwin/Mercker in the booth and Estes at the desk.] Goodwin: Jay? Estes: [looks to the ceiling] Grandma? Goodwin: Jay? Estes: Grandma? Is that you? How do you like Heaven? Just because I took the pillow and-- Goodwin: Har har har. Do you know who the participants in the "Hell on Earth" match are yet? Estes: I tried to find out, but Jinsei Tsumaru sent one of his Atari robots after me. They haven't told me anything, If I find out more, I'll let you guys know. Goodwin: Thanks, Jay. [The camera cuts back again to Goodwin and Mercker in the booth.] Goodwin: Well there you have it. A "Hell on Earth" match. That could be potentially interesting. Whomever they decide to pit against each other, we'll be sure to have plenty more news concerning this matchup in the weeks to come, here on SJPW Metro. Mercker: Is that it? Can we go get drunk now? Goodwin: Oh my dear, we are only beginning. If you want to get high, go have yourself a mocha latte. Mercker: [pretends to gag] Oh please! Don't mention that to me! I've only just begun to cope with the sorry excuse for beer that they have over here. Don't get me started on the coffee. Do we have any more matches to shove down the throats of these wonderful people? Goodwin: You've no idea, my dear. What we've got for you right now is tag team action with the Punjab Tigers taking on the Cagemasters. The Punjab Tigers coming at you all the way from India. Mercker: Do you think they drink the water? Goodwin: I assume they do. Maybe thats why they look so mad all the time... Let's go down to the ring! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The Punjab Tigers v. The Cage Masters =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= In hard hitting, tag team action, the Punjab Tigers used their technical knowledge to offset the brawling of the masked Cagemasters. Kesar Singh connected with several dropkicks and armdrags to entertain the crowd and his partner, the ever focused Ravan Singh took it to his opponents and wowed the audience with several good rolling reversals. The match kept up long enough for Kesar Singh to win the match with a choke slam on Cagemaster #2. Winners: The Punjab Tigers (Kesar Singh pins Cagemaster #2 w/ a choke slam) Time: 6:07. [The camera cuts from ringside as the Punjab Tigers are leaving the ringside area to a very sprase crowd response as "Misrilou" by Dick Dale plays over the PA system. The camera zooms around the room to show the fans, some of which wave to the camera, then cuts back to the broadcast booth and back to Goodwin and Mercker sitting behind the desk.] Goodwin: Well the big men from India get a win over the Cagemasters here at the Met Studio in Tokyo. The Punjab Tigers look to be on their last legs here in SJPW, and its a shame because they are two very talented athletes. Mercker: Talent is as talent does...in my book anyway. The Punjab Tigers have what it takes to make it big in this sport, they just need to dedicate themselves better and who knows what kind of sucess they could have? But if they continue to plod along against teams like the Cagemasters, then they can forget about being noticed by the matchmakers here at SJPW. You have to earn sucess......it doesn't come to you. Goodwin: Well said. Mercker: You should hear me after 4 or 5 shots of Jack Daniels! [laughs in a "sleazy" way] Goodwin: You are a real charm. Coming up next is the Great Oni in action taking on a fellow aerialist, Pancrease specialist Doug Thomas. But first, let's go to Jay Estes in the update center. Jay? [The camera cuts to Estes behind the desk in the center. He is wearing dark "Stevie Wonder" style sunglasses.] Estes: Thank you, Tania. Forgive me if I skip out on drinking tonight, I've just had a Hokkadio Iced Tea. 50 kinds of Sake and seaweed to taste! But any ways, I've been told that also on tonight's Metro, Black Ninja will be in action. I don't know how that qualifies as news, but it does. Back to you. [Camera cuts back to the booth.] Goodwin: Thank you, Jay. You're coming with us no matter what. Let's go to the ring. Great Oni v. Doug Thomas. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The Great Oni v. Doug Thomas =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Thomas brought his UFC/Pancrease experience to this match to go up against the aerial assault of the man from Osaka, The Great Oni. Both men matched up well early on but the Great Oni managed to pull away after about a minute and a half, overwhelming Thomas with a barrage of karate and flying kicks which was able to offset the submission arsenal of Mr. Thomas. Oni ended the match is spectacular fashion by hitting Thomas with a combo of a Yakuza kick to the stomach, a kick to the chest, and a jumping side kick. All that was left was for Oni to get the pin, which he did. Winner: The Great Oni (with the kick combination) Time: 2:39 [The camera cuts from The Great Oni leaving the ring to boos from the crowd, back to the booth where Goodwin and Mercker are sitting.] Goodwin: The Great Oni gets an impressive win over Doug Thomas here on Metro. Thomas had the submission ability, but it just wasn't enough to take out the aerial barrage of Oni. We'll be sure to see some good things from The Great One in the future...of SJPW. Mercker: For those of you who didn't catch the SJPW Super J Crown PPV, a young woman from Paris by the name of Pizzazz took the WWGP Womens title in a stunning win over Baby Hotta, who we'll go to in a second. Soon on Metro, we hope to have an interview with the new Womens champion, and as soon as we line it up, we'll present it to you. Goodwin: Thats right. Pizzazz the WWGP Womens champion. She's arrogant, she's self centered, and she thinks too highly of herself. I don't like her at all. Mercker: Really? With all that in common? Goodwin: [laughs to herself] Shut up. Before we go the the interview with Baby Hotta, We have one more match for you all here on the innaugural Metro broadcast. It will be the Black Ninja coming into your TV sets from Parts Unkown to take on the French sadist Van Mouton. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The Black Ninja v. Van Mouton =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Ninja neutralized the extreme brawling tactics of Mouton quite easily and early on in this contest by attacking him with a barrage of different aerial and agile moves that seemed to keep both the crowd and the wrestlers dazed and confused for much of the match. Ninja put the match out of range when Mouton, reverting back to his old style, tried to bring in a chair. Ninja dropkicked him in the stomach and hit him with a Enziguiri to win the match. Winner: The Black Ninja (with the Enziguiri) Time: 5:47 [The camera cuts from Ninja's exit to the booth where the two broadcasters are sitting. Mercker is caught swigging a beer and when he tries to put the bottle down, he drops it in his lap. Goodwin finds this amusing to say the least.] Goodwin: Cold beer, eh? Mercker: [wiping his mouth] Colder than you could imagine. Goodwin: [laughs so hard she has to wipe her eyes] Well anway, why don't we end the very first edition of Metro on a positive note. [breaks up laughing again] Mercker: Positive for YOU maybe! [aggresively wipes at his pants with a paper towel] Goodwin: All right. Let's go to Jay Estes in the studio who has Baby Hotta lined up for an interview. Go for it, Jay. [The camera cuts to Jay Estes sitting in the update center room.] Estes: What do you think of the "Fire Ninja" situation? Do you ever wonder whats going on over there? Baby Hotta: Is there a "Fire Ninja" situation? What's going on? I thought he was dead? Jay Estes: Someone made an appearance during the Super J match between Rekka Sakura and Robin Blaze. Some even think it was you... Baby Hotta: ME? Tell me, Mr. Estes. Do you think it was me? Jay Estes: No comment. Baby Hotta: Ditto. Next question. Jay Estes: What are your feelings on the WWGP Womens Champion Pizzazz? Baby Hotta: To Pizzazz? "Ich bin ein CHAMPION." Sorry, that's all the French I know... (Hotta giggles) Jay Estes: You're not going to make this easy, are you? Baby Hotta: Did you want "easy"? You should have interviewed Dawn Rose then. Next question. Jay Estes: Do you have anything to say in return to Rekka Sakura? Baby Hotta: Sort of. Rekka, you really want to make an enemy out of me. Well, good news. You have. Figuratively, you've lost face already. Literally, you'll lose your head. Jay Estes: What do you look for in a man? Baby Hotta: (blushing) Jay, are you coming on to me? Jay Estes: (awkwardly) No! I mean...ugh, just answer the question! Baby Hotta: A sugar-daddy. Someone who'll buy me all the toys and pets I want. He has to be cute, too. And not too old. A teddy bear with a big heart and bigger wallet. Know anyone who fits the bill? Jay Estes: I don't think I want to. Baby Hotta: Good, let's move on. Jay Estes: What wrestling school did you attend? Baby Hotta: For a brief time, I studied under Mr. Keiji Hokusai. You might remember him as the "Specialman" from the Sixties and Seventies. It's not a school per se, more like an apprenticeship. He's turned out quite a few wrestlers, both men and women. I only stayed for a short time because he showed lots of favoritism towards his own family in his lessons. Jay Estes: Do any of his family currently wrestle? Baby Hotta: (laughing) They do, but none are as accomplished as I am. His niece Daisy Firecracker wrestles abroad in the States, in the Grand Dragon Wrestling Alliance. She thinks she's real good, yet she's only held one belt and only for a week! Jinx Hokusai, Keiji's own daughter, is doing nothing in CherryBomb Pro. She's a little big woman with too much attitude and not enough aptitude. It's funny, if you ask me, all Mr. Hokusai knows how to do is inflate an ego. You can quote me on that. Jay Estes: So where did you pick up the rest of your wrestling skills? Baby Hotta: (leaning in and whispering) Would you believe...from outer space? Jay Estes: What? Come on now... Baby Hotta: No, no...I'm SERIOUS!! "Baby Hotta" is just my alter ego, like Clark Kent and Superman. My name is really Sailor Calypso and I'm at odds with the Negaverse... Jay Estes: (dryly) Ha, okay, I get it. Sailor Moon. Funny stuff... (Hotta giggles as Estes adjusts his notes and glares at her) Jay Estes: Is there anyone you plan to target in the near future? Baby Hotta: Target? Mr. Estes, a dart-board cannot throw darts. Jay Estes: Meaning? Baby Hotta: Meaning I'M the only target there is in SJPW. I've got to clean up every other mess I've got before me. Like Rekka Sakura and Dawn Rose. Jay Estes: What about Pizzazz? Baby Hotta: She's got a booby-prize. My belt is the only belt that matters, and I have to defend it. Pizzazz is a nobody, with or without the WWGP title. Jay Estes: What do you think of America as a country? Baby Hotta: America? S'okay, I guess. I like the blue jeans. America is just one part of a larger world, Mr. Estes. Culturally, they've got a lot to offer. Politically, they're a bit shallow. But, politics isn't my thing. Culture is. Jay Estes: What happened that caused you to adopt such a "unorthodox" persona? Baby Hotta: Unorthodox? Mr. Estes, I'm just being myself. That's a question better asked of other wrestlers; like Rekka Sakura. I wanna know what caused her to adopt such an unorthodox persona. Why she preaches one thing and practices another. Me, I'm exactly who you see. People might not like that, but it's the hard truth. Jay Estes: What do you think is the most effective finishing hold in SJPW? (Baby Hotta starts laughing hard) Jay Estes: What? Baby Hotta: You wanna know the most effective finisher in SJPW? I'll tell you. INTERVIEWING ME!! (Hotta laughs and Estes laughs along with her) Jay Estes: I hope not. I'd like to look forward to a career here. (They both laugh more) Jay Estes: And finally, where do you have your hair colored? Baby Hotta: (smiling seductively as she plays with her hair) You mean besides my head? Jay Estes: (blushing) Ummm, no. I meant where do you get it colored at? Baby Hotta: My house, hotels...anywhere I can get a bottle of peroxide. Depends if I'm on the road or not. Goodwin: We will see you next Friday with another action packed version of Super Japan Pro Wrestling Metro wrestling. See you then. [Goodwin looks at Mercker again and doubles over with laughter. Mercker throws the wet towel at her and she laughs even harder as the camera fades to black with the SJPW logo on the screen........] _____ _____ _____ __ __ /\ _ `\ /\___ \ /\ _ `\ /\ \ __/\ \ \ \ \_\_\ \/__/\ \ \ \ \_\ \ \ \ \/\ \ \ \ \/`____ \ _\ \ \ \ \ ,__/ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ ` /\ \_\ \ /\ \_\ \ \ \ \/ \ \ \_/ \_\ \ \ \_____\ \ \____/ \ \_\ \ `\___ ___/ \/_____/ \/___/ \/_/ '\/__//__/ © 1997 http://www.angelfire.com/ny/sjpw
