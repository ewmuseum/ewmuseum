(camera fades to the ring. Sharon Akiyama is standing in the center of the ring, holding a SJPW microphone. As the camera comes closer, she begins.)
SA: Ladies and gentleman, last week, we saw Kyu Kabuki lose his name after a loss to Kabuki Kid. However, it was the interference of a man in a suit and tie that cost him that match. At this time,
I would like to introduce the man who has lost his identity following that, and a man who is now known simply by one name.
Ladies and gentleman, this is KYU! ("Thunderkiss 65" starts up. Out from the curtains walks Kyu, to a pop which is smaller then he usually gets. He's wearing a Japan AC t-shirt, and track pants. He tosses out a couple teddy bears to the crowd, before just shaking his head, and stopping. He gets to the ring, and pushes the pouch in first. He then rolls in as well, and poses for the crowd a bit, but then stops because of a noticeable amount of boos from the crowd.)
SA: Well, Kyu, it seems that maybe the fans here aren't on your side as much as they once were. What do you have to say about that?
K: The people can think what they want, do what they want. But soon, I'm hoping that they'll be back on my side. I'm pretty sure they will be.
SA: Why's that? K: Next question. SA: (a bit shocked by the answer, but continues) Alright. Well, what about that man who came out here during your match with Kabuki
Kid? Who was that?
(Kyu sighs, and looks up towards the ceiling. He shakes his head, and looks back towards Sharon, and begins.)
K: That man was my father. SA: WHAT!?!? K: That man who cost me the match was my father. SA: You can't be serious. K: I am. For years now, he hasn't supported me in my goal to be a pro-wrestler. He's shot me down. He's yelled at me. He's dis-owned me. He's said I'm no longer his son. And after a while,
I accepted that. I accepted the fact that he hated me. And I forgot about him. But last week, when he came down that aisleway,
I thought he was coming to support me.
(shakes his head) K: But he didn't. Instead, he humiliated me in front of my fans, my peers, my family, and me. And he didn't care. So that's why I'm no longer going to use my real name. From now on, you people refer to me as Kyu. Not Kyu Kabuki. Just Kyu. That's all. If my family wants to dis-own me, then that's fine by me.
(Kyu points his finger around to the crowd) K: But as long as some of you like me, I'll always have a family. And next week, I want some more time. Because then, I'll have one more very important message for SJPW. And that message will be a
BOMBSHELL, that I doubt even these fans aren't ready for. Thank you.
SA: THAT WAS KYU, Ladies and gentleman! ("Thunderkiss 65" starts up again. Kyu waves to the crowd, and then leaves the ring, and walks to the back, slapping hands along the way.)
