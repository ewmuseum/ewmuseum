What brought around this change in your attitude? KK: Well, it's like this. When I came into e-wrestling, in a little fed called EWL, I was basically a mercenary. I came in for just one reason, and that's because of Chris Deugau. (boos from the crowd at the mention of Chris's name, getting a slight grin out of Kyu.) You see, Chris Deugau hired me to severly injure another man, named Damien Legend. I did that, but once it was done, I didn't know what else to do. After that, the guy started using his cast as a foreign object, and Deugau blamed ME for it! SA: Why's that? KK: He said it was MY fault the guy was in a cast, not his. And that pissed me off first. Then, later on, he brought me into a fed called MLWO, down in the states. There, I wrestled only once or twice, alongside a man known as Bubba Ellner. We were a team, until Chris said he didn't like what we were doing, and FIRED MY ASS! (more boos) AND THAT'S WHEN I LEFT! I came to my hometown, here in Japan. And when I came to Japan, and did what I normally did, the fans started to like it. (Kyu takes off his hat, and throws it into the crowd) KK: And then, I started to find out something I couldn't in the USA. I found out just how nice it is to be POPULAR! WHAT IT FEELS LIKE TO COME OUT TO CHEERS! And that's why I'm playing to these fans! That's why I've declared war on those stupid punks who come out here dissing the Japanesse. (HUGE crowd pop. Kyu starts grabbing stuffed animals from the wagon, and tossing them out) SA: Well, about your match tonight with Angel of Death. If you win, you move on to the Super J Crown PPV. How do you feel about that? KK: How do you think I feel? I FEEL GREAT! I feel great knowing that the little Kyu-ites out there are gonna have someone to look up to at the PPV. And that's where my pride comes from. Knowing that the fans get what they want when I wrestle. SA: Well, about the Crusierweight Champion, one Super Tengu. How do you feel about him? KK: I love him. I always love people I beat TIME, after TIME, after TIME. SA: Yes, you have proven you can beat Tengu. But can you do it with the title on the line? And that's the truth...whether you like it or not! ("Bad Boys" starts up again. Kyu finishes throwing out the merchandise, and then leaves with the flag raised.
