Control Center Flash Packet [5/3/04]

Jun 3, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1) "Firefly" Horo Atashi 2) "All Star" Joey Darren 3) Wolf Jager 4) MAMMOTH Kagawa 5) El Santo Azteca 6) Pramoj Ananda ---------------------------- "Firefly" Horo Atashi ---------------------------- " That was good young Horo. You were very sharp and wise in the ring. You had the look of a man beyond your years." [ Light clapping is heard.] " However it will get harder." [ Fade In.] [ We fade in on Horo Atashi dressed in street clothes setting on a small couch looking up at his Father who seems to be giving another one of life's many lessons to speech to his eager son. Young Horo has a somewhat puzzled look on his face currently as he trys to pay attention to every ounce of knowledge spilled out.] " They will become more prepared as your style becomes known. Not taking anything away from the man in which you did battle, for none should be looked over in this sport of warriors, but the combatants will become tougher. There will be consistantly better warriors sent your way in the near future." [ Horo nods.] " I am not sure El Santa Azteca is one of those for my research hasn't came up with many results, but we will treat him as if he was one of those warriors. We will treat him as if he was Japan's finest warrior doing battle with you for your last breathe." [ Horo nods, and even smiles.] " That is how we will look into this match at the Tora Dogo." [ Father Atashi motions to his own heart.] " It starts here." [ Then points to his head.] " And ends here my son. Both are of almost equal importance. Your skill level goes nearly unmatched, but we must make sure the other features also go nearly unmatched. If El Santa only equals you on one of those three levels then you should still be able to overcome him Horo. You can't be better than everybody at everything, but you can be better than everybody at most things my son. That is possible." [ Horo nods again, but Father reaches down putting his hand on the bottom of Horo's chin stopping him from finishing his nod.] " Don't let this go in one ear out the other Horo. If you nod I assume it is because you really hear me. Do not acknowledge every word I say,unless you truly comprehend every word I say." [ Father releases Horo's chin and he finishes the nod, no longer smiling.] " I don't want to see you get hurt. I want to see you have greater things on your plate than that which was put infront of me when I was your age, and trying to be involved in this sport." [ Horo stands up, standing slightly taller than his father, but of much smaller build.] " I don't try to be hard on you Horo to discourage you." [ Horo reaches out putting his hand on his Father's shoulder.] Horo Atashi : << In thick Japanese accent >> It's okay Father. I know you try to help me. [ The men both smile.] " Thank you son for understanding." [ Brief silence.] " Now back to work. Your kicks will not get stiffer on there own." [ Horo's smile widens as his father chuckles. Horo then takes off with a jog no longer in our screen leaving his father alone in the small room.] " If practice makes perfect as they say over in America. . . [ Father looks straight into the camera lens for the first time.] Then my son should be more than ready to make a perfect outing for his second showing SJPW at the Dojo." [ Father nods to the camera.] " You have yourself a diamond in the rough SJPW, and I just hope you realize it. He will die for your company if he has to in or out of the ring for his love is already that great for you just for giving him the chance to become the star he wants to become. His passion and desire that strong.. He truly will die for you . . " [ With that Father eyes the camera, and walks out of view leaving us to pan away.] [Fade.] ---------------------------- "All Star: Joey Darren ---------------------------- [We see a rather short, dark haired individual that obviously works out move away from the entrance to the airport. He hails a taxi and can tell he is obviously American. A taxi stops for him and the driver rushes out and takes his bags and puts them in the trunk. The American then gets into the taxi. The camera view shifts to the inside of the vehicle.] AMERICAN: Can you take me here? [He passes the driver a piece of paper. He looks at it and smiles.] DRIVER: Yes, I know this place. Pro-wrestling go on there. You pro wrestler? AMERICAN: [smiling, looking embarrassed.] Yes, yes I am. DRIVER: Who you are? I ever heard of you? AMERICAN: Maybe, I wrestle back in the states. I go by the name "All Star" Joey Darren. [The taxi swerves, the driver barely misses several vehicles. He then slams on the brakes and turns to face Joey Darren.] DRIVER: [VERY excited!] All Star Joey Darren in *MY* car?!? You leave America to come wrestler here? ALL STAR: No, I'm not leaving America, it is just time to....how do I say this....time to get out of my comfort zone. The president of Super Japan Pro Wrestling offered me a short contract to come over and see how I stack up. DRIVER: Ah, I see, I see. ALL STAR: For a long time, I've won titles in another federation in the states and now I want to try my luck here in Japan. [The driver turns around and starts the car. He pulls away from the curb still excited.] DRIVER: I predict luck here to be very good!! ALL STAR: Thank you very much, I hope so. In the states I have a nickname and I want to se.... DRIVER: You called "King of the Cruiserweights" in America. I see you on TV some. ALL STAR: [obviously getting embarrassed.]Well, I want to see just how I stand compared to some of the great cruisers here in Japan. [The drivers pulls the taxi over.] DRIVER: We here where you request. [Both men step out of the cab. The driver runs around to get JD's bags out of the trunk.] ALL STAR: So, so this is where it all begins over here. [Joey looks up in awe at the Team Tiger Wrestling Dojo. He then walks in the building as the driver gets in this taxi and drives away. We fade to black.] -------------- Wolf Jager -------------- [The camera fades in and there's Wolf Jager. He's perched on a stool glaring at a TV monitor, showing the end of the Gregor Davinsky/Pramoj Ananda match. As the match ending roundhouse kick is shown, Jager can be heard snarling.] Wolf: Ananda. I suppose you think you've accomplished something don't you? [As he speaks the emotion in his voice is quite clear, the barely contained anger coloring his words as he replies the final moments of the match. Watching his mentor get knocked senseless by the Thai Kickboxer's roundhouse.] Wolf: My mentor and tag team partner is in the hospital with a concussion, he's out of action for awhile. Quite possibly we'll have to miss the tag leagues because he's out with that injury. My victory over Thomas, turned to ashes in my mouth.... [Suddenly Wolf drops off the stool, whirling to face the camera as he does so. His face is a mask of intense rage, a look of intense hatred in his eyes as he looks into the camera. So intense is this look that the cameraman takes a few steps backward.] Wolf[shouting]: ....BECAUSE OF A VERDAMNTE HYPOCRITE LIKE YOU! [Wolf pauses a moment at this point, visibly calming himself down. He takes deep breaths, counts backwards from 20 in German. Once he regains his composure he resumes speaking, though his voice is still filled with anger.] Wolf: You were so enraged, so high and mighty when I injured Kwan. Your friend was hurt and you wanted vengeance, I had to be stopped you said. At the time I could almost empathize with your position, though to be honest I couldn't really care less. But then you did this. [Wolf looks like he might loose his temper again, white knuckled as he clenches his fists. The veins in his neck and forehead are standing out and he's actually trembling with his suppressed rage. But it is suppressed and he manages to continue.] Wolf: My partner, my mentor, my good friend is in the hospital because of you. Gregor Davinsky took me under his wing when I joined Super Japan, not because anybody asked him too but because he saw potential in me and wanted to bring it out. But your kick sent him to the hospital you hypocrite. Yes, I called you a hypocrite, because you berate me for injuring somebody after a match but not once have you shown any remorse yourself for injuring him. [Wolf snarls at that again, moving closer to the camera as he glares into it. His face soon fills the screen as the camera now has him on extreme close up. At this range it's quite easy to see the rage in his eyes.] Wolf: So I'll tell you what I'll do Ananda. Since you injured Gregor, I'm going to injure you. But I won't stop at a simple concussion, oh no. You hurt my friend so I'm going to hurt you worse, so that you might truly regret what you did to him. You will regret it, even if it means I have to tear your arm from it's socket, or injure Kwan again once I'm finished with you. You have done me harm, now I'll return that harm one hundredfold. [Camera fade to black] -------------------------- MAMMOTH Kagawa -------------------------- [Opening: An animated Wooly Mammoth comes charging towards the viewers, sunlight glinting off it's tusks. As it gets closer, it raises it's snout in the air, and bellows loudly. The Mammoth stops short, and slowly, the image changes into that of a man wearing a black mask with horns. The man is very muscular, his biceps flexing. The name "MAMMOTH Kagawa" appears on the screen in Red letters, followed by "Coming Soon", before fading out....] --------------------- El Santo Azteca --------------------- [The camera opens on El Santo Azteca in a suit, walking down the street in Tokyo. Some children come running up to him, and he smiles, pats them on the head, then signs autographs for them. He reaches into his pocket, and pulls out some sesame candy, and gives it to the children. They howl with joy, thank Azteca, then run off to play in the nearby park. Azteca continues walking, the camera now in front of him...] Azteca: So close. I was so close to defeating Super Bakemono for the NCWF cruiserweight title, a title he stole from Masashi "Lightning" Lanther. I made Bakemono tap out to the Azteca Speciale in our first encounter, but my luck did not last. [Azteca shakes his head and sighs] Azteca: Bakemono, you have tremendous talent. You remind me of my nephew. He too, felt the need to channel his inner darkness, and use it to propel his career. Such a waste. Your alliance with Despair is going to cost you dearly. Did you learn nothing when he you wrapped barbed wire around your leg, and legdropped KEOKI across the throat? You could have killed him! To see such a talented luchador sink so low disgusts me. [Azteca's eyes go wide, as if in anger] Azteca: However, I don't blame you for your madness. It is that Gringo Loco, Despair, who has set you on this path. I have always fought with honor and integrity. I have earned my reputation as a legend, both here and in Mexico. I have fought many Rudos in my day, but none of them could compare to the utter evil that is Despair. [Azteca shakes his head again] Azteca: Despair must be stopped. All ready he has turned KEOKI and Thunder at each other's throats. Their desire to be the only one to stop Despair will be their undoing. I know that my record to date is lackluster, but know this Despair: I will earn my chance to face you. I will make you submit to the Azteca Speciale, just like I made Bakemono submit. I will take your Gunryo title, and end your reign of terror. I cannot stand by while you destroy the reputation of one of the greatest promotions in all of Japan. You spit on it's history, and it's traditions. That must end. [Azteca stares solemnly into the camera] Azteca: Which leads me to Horo Atashi. Horo, you are an up and coming star. You have a bright future ahead of you. You have the ability to be a future champion. You follow tradition, and show honor not only upon Super Japan, but on your father. Which makes me sad that we must face now. I am on a mission, a crusade, to defeat Despair. To do that, I must defeat you on June 4th. I regret you were put in my path at this time. This match is critical for me, and I cannot lose. For that Horo, I am sorry that I must now show you no respite. I will come at you full force with the blood of my Aztec ancestors howling in him ears. Every match from this day forward is a battle in a war I cannot afford to lose. I hope you will not take your loss too hard Horo, for you will have many more victories to speak of. You just won't have one on June 4th. [Azteca shakes his head sadly once more, turns away from the camera, and calmly walks into the park, where once again the children surround him, calling out his name. The camera fades out as we once more see him give the children some candy from his pocket, the children's faced smiling with glee...] -------------------- Pramoj Ananda -------------------- [The camera opens on Ananda in the ring. He is locked up with an opponent, and quickly does a go behind, then a takedown to the mat. He pummels the man with some punches, gets to his feet, and drags the man up. He whips him to the ropes, and catches him with a kick to the gut, doubling him over. Ananda sets himself, then lands a hard knee strike the man's padded head. The man flies back to the ropes, bounces off, only to catch a roundhouse kick to the face. The man goes down hard, and ring attendants enter the ring, and check on the man. Ananda takes a water bottle from one of the men, takes a drink, then pours some water over his head. He takes a towel, wipes himself off, then heads towards the ropes, so that he is facing the camera...] Ananda: Wolf Jager. I find it amusing that you can injure Se-hong Kwan not once, but twice, then dare to call me a hypocrite for injuring Davinsky. You see, I didn't injure him intentionally. The move that gave him a concussion is the move I consider my specialty, hence it being called "The Ananda Special". Much like your famed Crossface Chicken Wing, the Ananda Special is how I defeat my opponents. What you did to Kwan, you did so purposely. You used Kwan to send a message to the rest of the competitors in Super Japan. I got your message Jager, so I sent you a reply. Spare me your insinuations. [Ananda throws the towel to the mat, and flips over the ropes, landing on the mat outside the ring] Ananda: You want to teach me a lesson Jager? Then I know the perfect place to do it. At Zenmen Sensou, on June 26th, I challenge you to face me in a match. The rules are simple. Knockout or Submission will determine the winner. So you can try to break my arm, and I'll try and give you a concussion, so Davinsky won't be lonely in the hospital. You see, I feel no remorse over what I did. Considering you felt no remorse over what you did to Kwan, I am sure you can empathize. Prepare yourself well Jager, for I plan on making you suffer for your misdeeds. Every kick, every knee, every elbow or punch will leave a mark on your body. You might suffer cracked or broken bones. Win or lose, you will know that you have been in a fight, and you will come out of this a different man. [Ananda moves closer to the camera, so that only his face can be seen] Ananda: Get ready Jager, for I am coming, and I plan on hurting you. And hurt you will. Can you handle that? [Ananda turns and walks away from the camera, and goes to check on his sparring partner. He pats the man on the back after the man tells him he is ok. The two men head to the locker room, as the camera fades out]
