Control Center- Flashes 7/16/04

Jul 16, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1) "All Star" Joey Darren 2) Van Wild & Scott Starring 3) Frankie "The Thumper" Douglas 4) "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder 5) The Red Army 6) Mr. Smiley Mask 7) "Dragon Fury" Minobu Tetsuhara 8) Despair 9) Super Bakemono ------------------------------- "All Star" Joey Darren: ------------------------------- [In front of a SJPW banner, we find "All Star" Joey Darren, IICW title on his right shoulder, with a mic in hand.] ALL STAR: Seems like business here is picking up for me some. I only have a moment so I'll be brief. First, Mr. Smiley Mask I want to wish you good luck in our tournament match coming up. I've been scouting you and know you are a very tough, happy-go-lucky competitor. You remind me of a taller version of myself. Just be reminded that when we climb in the ring, it's on. I will give no quarter and ask for none. It is all business and I plan to treat you accordingly. Good luck in our match, may the best man win. [Adjusting the IICW Cruiserweight title to his other shoulder.] ALL STAR: Then there is my title. A few guys have recently been 'rumored' to wanting a shot at it. That is fine, I have and always will be a fighting champion. So whether it is a rematch with Tsunami or a defense against newcomer El Muerte Rojo, just expect a fair fight and a double tough champion. You guys can have this title when you rip it from my beaten, bloodied, and battered hands. Time to go. [Joey drops the mic and walks off the set. We fade to black.] ---------------------------------- Van Wild & Scott Starring: ---------------------------------- Following their debut victory at the Zepp Sapporo last Friday, new arrivals Van Wild and Scott Starring had this to say about their first impressions of SJPW, and the upcoming GPW tag league: VAN WILD: "It was great to kick things off with a win, and we really want to say a big 'thank you' to Mr. Yamashita, Mr. Kikuchi and everyone at Super Japan, for the warm welcome you guys have given me and Scotty since we got here. Not to mention these great fans here tonight!" SCOTT STARRING: "Definitely. We're very excited and honored to be a part of this tournament. And although the competition seems very strong from what we've seen so far, we're looking forward to the challenge, and the chance to show Super Japan Pro Wrestling what we can do in the ring." ------------------------------------------ Frankie "The Thumper" Douglas: ------------------------------------------ [The camera opens on Frankie Douglas, pumping iron in the gym. He does a series of curls from the standing position, his face tense with concentration. After he finishes the set, he puts the weights down, and wipes his face with a towel. He then grabs a bottle of water, drinks from it, and sits down on a bench. The camera moves in closer, zooming in on his face...] Frankie Douglas: Van Wild, Scott Starring, I want to congratulate the two of you. Doug and I gave you everything we got, and you beat us. No hard feelings. We wanted to face the best, and we did. Maybe next time things will be different. Either way, continued success for you two in the GPH tag league. [Douglas sips some more water] Frankie Douglas: This weekend, I face a big test. "The Wolverine" Masuhiro Hayatashi. This guy is huge! He's more like a sumo guy! But I've got the task of facing him one on one. Can I beat him? Who knows. But I damn sure will do my best to see him down for the three count. I saw what his spear did to Asakura, so I know I have to avoid that. [Douglas sips some more water] Frankie Douglas: Hayatashi is bigger than me, and definitely stronger than me. But I've faced those odds before, and sometimes came out on top. Win or Lose, Hayatashi will know he's been in a fight. He's going to earn that victory, or go down in defeat. It's as simple as that. [Douglas sips some water again, and the camera fades out] ------------------------------------ "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder: ------------------------------------ [Blackness. A blackness with a fine texture.] VOICE: I wanna thank... Jun Suzuki. [The blackness is the top of a black cowboy hat. The hat slowly raises up to reveal the face of Brody Thunder.] THUNDER: Ya heard me right. I wanna THANK... Jun Suzuki right here in public. [The smile appears.] THUNDER: Y'see a great wrestler once said "adversity introduces a man to himself". [The camera pans out to a waist up shot of Brody Thunder. He's dressed in faded jeans, black Durango boots and cowboy hat and a black t-shirt with the words "EVIL, MEAN & NASTY" in red on the front. He's smiling ear-to-ear.] THUNDER: Well Suzuki, I rediscovered that lil fact the hard way. I rediscovered it when you rang my bell with that metal chair an' then spit in my face. Y'see, men like you, they tend ta fergit that there's men like me still around. Men who ain't about to let them run down a legacy o'bein' the best, night in an' night out in professional wrestlin'. Men who ain't gonna turn the other cheek an' walk away from a fight cuz they might get 'hurt'. Men who... when push comes ta shove... will do whatever it takes ta walk outta that ring with his hand in the air. I'll give ya credit, amigo... you drew first blood. The name Jun Suzuki ain't exactly a stranger ta me. I'd heard about this-this hardcore warrior who seemed ta take pride in sacrificin' his body fer a victory. I'd heard this man would walk through Hell itself if it meant gettin' his hand raised. But... I'd heard things like that... 'bout other men in our sport. Some I faced in the heat o'battle... an' left them face down. When I heard stories o' this man who'd spill a bucket o' his blood fer an ounce o'glory, I shook my head an' laughed. I ain't laughin' now. [Thunder tips his hat up abit, revealing the large white gauze bandages still adorning his forehead. A tinge of red can be seen in the middle of it.] THUNDER: Y'see.. I know you a lil bit _better_ now, Suzuki, a lil more... personally. I know you'll do whatever it takes to win. I know yer willin' ta sacrifice yer body an' yer blood fer the sweet taste o' victory. I know yer a good fighter an' a damn tough sunuvab[BLEEP]ch. I found out those things the hard way. But I also found out somethin' else, Suzuki. I found out... how to beat you. That's right, ace. Oh, I know I can't beat ya with a chair or with a table or with barbed wire. Hell, the world seen that it's jus' one way ta get the job done. I know that's _yer_ way. The 'Gaijin extreme' way. The way o' the Gunyro title. Well I've played by the Gunyro rules since I came ta this country an' in doin' so I lost track o'what made me what I am. The best there is in this business today bar none. It took you an' yer lil backjumpin' attack ta open my eyes, ta see you fer what you truly are. A yella-bellied coward. If ya wanted a fight, Suzuki, all ya had ta do was ask an' I'd have gladly obliged you. I ain't never backed down from a good scrap an' I sure wouldn't start with the likes o'a runt like you. But when you jumped me that night, you didn't want a fight, did ya. No, you didn't. What you wanted an' what you thought you HAD... was another victim. Another notch in yer belt. Another broken body ta step on on yer way up. [Thunder ignites the lighter and a flame appears. The smile widens.] THUNDER: Well how's that workin' out fer ya, ace? [Thunder slaps the cover back down on the lighter extinguishing it's flame.] THUNDER: An' now Suzuki, there's another lil wrinkle in our collision course; a tagteam match between Despair & El Santa Azteca... an' you an' I. [Thunder rubs his jaw in concern.] THUNDER: The suits in SJPW want me ta stand in the corner with a man who's backjumped, waffled me with a Texas Flyswatter, busted me open like a ripe melon an' then spit on me. [Thunder shakes his head in a combination of disbelief and disgust.] THUNDER: They say it's what the people want. They say it's for television ratin's. Hell, they say it's the biggest match they've had all year! Well, y'know what I say? I say I don't give a _damn_ about tv ratin's or what people wanna see! I'm goin' inta a match with a guy I'd love ta bury across the ring in front o'me... an' a no-good heap-o'-dung backjumper at my side. It don't take a flamin' rocket scientist to figger out that them odds ain't healthy fer yers truly. So here's what I'm gonna do. I'm gonna show up Saturday Night at the Battle Sphere. I'm gonna come down ta that ring fer that tagteam match. An' when that bell rings, I'm gonna kick the livin' beejeezis outta whoever's in front o'me. I don't care if it's Azteca... I don't care if it's that pile o' walkin' bullpucky Despair... an' I certainly don't give a tinker's damn if it's you, Suzuki. There's no reason ta pretend that this is jus' another tagteam match. It ain't. You know it. Despair & Azteca know it. I sure-as-hell know it so let's jus' cut to the chase. I got me a debt to pay. A debt to you, Suzuki. A debt I plan on seein' paid-in-full. A debt o'blood an' respect. That's first an' foremost on my mind. Despair! Don't think I've fergot or fergiven what we've been through, ace. Yer still wearin' the strap that should be around MY waist. Sooner or later yer gonna have ta put that shiny belt on the line against me, pal, an' when you do? You'll be leavin' the arena about ten pounds lighter, mark my words. Azteca! Yer a man in the wrong place at the wrong time, amigo. You get in that ring with me an' I promise ya... I'll shorten yer career right then an' there. I got no beef with ya other than yer on the other side o'that ring. That makes you a target an' if yer in my sights when that bell rings... I'm gonna drop ya like ten-point buck durin' huntin' season. An' Suzuki... yer in my corner, the SJPW says that's the way it's gonna be. [The camera tightens in slowly on Thunder's grizzled face, his eyes narrowing abit as he speaks through nearly-clenched teeth.] THUNDER: But if you get in my _way_ I'll send yer 'gaijin extreme' ass back ta whatever hole you call home... on a flamin' slab. Understand what I'm tellin' ya, Suzuki... I'm lettin' ya know up front... I don't trust ya, I don't like ya an' I damn sure don't want ya in my corner o'the ring an' if ya turn yer back... even fer a second... I can't promise ya that you'll see the end o'the match. Least ways, not on yer feet,ace. [Thunder stares for a moment, his gaze burning into the camera's eye. before speaking again.] THUNDER: An' when this cruel joke is over, Suzuki, ya best get ready fer the comin' storm. An' jus' remember... ya never hear the thunder.... ... till after the lightnin' strikes. [The camera tightens to just a shot of Thunder's squinting, bloodshot eyes as he stares intently.] [Fade to black...] ---------------------- The Red Army: ----------------- (the camera cuts backstage. Keiko Otaku is standing by with "Russian Superman" Petor Andresku and Vladimir Tarasov... The Red Army. Andresku and Tarasov are wearing matching Russian Wrestling Team workout suits) OTAKU: I'm backstage with recent Super Japan arrivals The Red Army. Vladimir Tarasov & "Russian Superman" Petor Andresku. Welcome to Super Japan Pro Wrestling and the GPH League. ANDRESKU: **in thick Russian Accent** Keiko Otaku... Red Army pleased to be out of sickening United States of America and in Super Japan Pro Wrestling. Vladimir and Petor looking forward to showing Japanese fan what we already show American fan... there no one who hold candle to Red Army. TARASOV: **also in thick Russian Accent** You look at Petor Andresku... Russian Superman... Olympic athlete... man who put fear into hearts of some of the greatest so-called champions America had to offer. He break the unbreakable submission hold. He humiliate American heroes with his wrestling skill. The only thing Petor and I not get was fair shake in America. They play politiks. We get railroaded time and time again. ICW loss is SJPW gain! ANDRESKU: That why we come to SJPW. That why when offer arrive to compete in league for Grand Prix titles, Red Army jump at chance. OTAKU: What are you impressions of the athletes you've seen so far in SJPW? TARASOV: Make no mistake... Japan athletes very impressive... but Red Army more impressive. ANDRESKU: Keiko Otaku, you stand here with two of greatest Russian Wrestling champion. I lead Russian National Team to Atlanta in 1996. I show the world what Russian machine can do. Vladimir robbed of chance by Western media. They plant seeds of doubt about truth of his abilities. Now Vladimir wrestle 10 times more passion than before. He here to show not just SJPW, but the whole world that he supreme Russian athlete. TARASOV: We no care who we face or when in GPH. We face Crush Crew first, but we no care if it Team Raiden... Nagasaki Bulldog... Maulers. Kobayashi and Nakatoni will fall just like all other team in league. There only one champion team, and Red Army will be the GPH Champions! ANDRESKU: And when get get chance to get... how you say... Gaijin team from America... we reintroduce them to Cold War. OTAKU: It sounds like you have a chip on your shoulders. TARASOV: Chip? This more than chip Keiko Otaku. This block of hatred that I will remove! When we finished... all of SJPW will know the name of Red Army!! (fade out) ---------------------- Mr. Smiley Mask: ---------------------- [Scene opens with an extreme close-up of a bright yellow smiley face mask...] Mr.SM: Is everyone happy?! I sure hope so! If not you will be! You're probably wondering just who the heck I am... Good question... Who the heck am I? Isn't it obvious? I'm Mr. Smiley Mask! [Stakes a hero pose... Chest out, fists on the hips, head held high... Looks like he's soaking in cheers...cept no one is there...] Mr.SM: Thank you... I was brought to SJPW to spice up the Sedei-X division... I know what you're thinking... Smiley you're 6'9"! You're too tall to be cruiser... You're not cruiser material... You can't fly! [Smiley starts flapping his arms like wings...] Mr.SM: Bah! I can fly... And I fly frequently... Heck I'm in the air so often I serve meals! Regular or kosher? Ha-ha... I will make you rise up out of your chairs and say......well I leave that part up to you... That is if you can still speak... I've been known to take people's breath's away... But you will take notice of Mr. Smiley Mask! [Again strikes his hero pose soaking in the cheers in his head...] Mr.SM: Thank you... To my worthy opponent Mr. Darren, I know you will be a tough challenge, you will prove to be a tough competitor... Certainly not a cake walk... You will test me, I will have to use all of my skills to win... I like tough competition because I am.....Mr. Smiley Mask! [Cripes... He's posing like a super hero again....] Mr.SM: Thank you... When it's all said and done Mr. Smiley Mask will be the king of the Sedei-X division... Take a long look at the first SJPW Sedei-X champion! [Here we go again... He's posing.... Again....] Mr.SM: I'm happy, I'm scrappy, I'm the king of the cruisers! Get ready to smile SJPW because.... [Strikes his hero pose......AGAIN!] Mr.SM: I AM MR. SMILEY MASK! [Another hero pose as we fade to black.....] ------------------------------------------- "Dragon Fury" Minobu Tetsuhara: ------------------------------------------- ["Dragon Fury" Minobu Tetsuhara is standing proudly in a white set room. Two flags hang behind him. On his left is the flag of Japan. On the right is a flag bearing the logo of Team Tiger. He is dressed in a black suit complete with black silk shirt and gold tie.] Minobu: July 17th, at Battle Sphere Tokyo, on SJPW's The Zone!, I will be facing a feared and proud gaijin warrior. His name is "Living Legend" Tommy Fury. This is a man who has done much in our sport. He has accomplished many things, and gained many accolades for himself. [Minobu inclines his head slightly in a sort of short bow, indicating his respect for his opponent.] Minobu: He comes into Super Japan with an ego as large as his legend. He is as prideful as he is strong and he is ready to prove how much better he is than all the others here in Super Japan. He has set himself up upon a pedestal, and unfortunately for Fury-san, that makes him vulnerable. [A smile of determination appears on Minobu's face.] Minobu: See, I fight for several reasons. First, I fight for myself. Duty and honor go hand in hand as I struggle to do my best each and every time I enter that ring. There can be no slackness in style, their can be no give in the steel of my soul. Every time I fight, my spirit is burning to do the best I can. Secondly, I fight for Team Tiger. The most prestigious army inside of Super Japan has seen fit to honor my skills by allowing me to join their ranks. Just a few days ago, I won glory for Team Tiger by defeating on of it's former students. Now I must win glory for the team once more in battle. Finally, I fight for my country. Japan is my homeland. This is my chance to prove that the fighting spirit here is the strongest in the world. Bring your best Tommy Fury. I will bring my best. I will show you that your gaijin ways are no match for the fire in my heart. [Scene fades.] ----------------------------------- Gunryo Champion Despair: ----------------------------------- *Scene opens to Despair in his Tokyo apartment. Currently the owner of said apartment is sitting on the sofa holding a large bag of ice up to his head. He puts down the bag to show his face is literally black and blue. Cuts and some stitches are all over his visible body, made all the more visible via his camo cargo shorts, black Flogging Molly wifebeater and bare feet. He turns his neck to the side and cracks it, grunting softly as he continues to do the same with his spine. There are a few empty GENERICO bottles on the floor and some scattered photos on the table in front of him. Despair looks like hell, more so than usual. He puts the bag on the floor and leans back. His eyes never look up at the camera and his tone of voice is very even as if he was controlling himself from breaking down in front of the camera.* Despair: Not a damn thing ever came easy to me. Not breaking into this sport, not becoming a FUCKING SUPERSTAR in it and not to mention enjoying the lifestyle that I now have. A year ago I was living out of my car wondering if I would be able to eat or have enough cash to buy my next fix. Now here I am living like a rockstar. Things looked like they were going up for me. Fame, fortune, people throwing money at me offering me endorsement deals, PPV appearances, you name it. For the past few months I've lived life fully and loved it. I've woken up almost every morning with thoughts of the sins I'd wage and the chaos I'd create all just for kicks. Getting paid to travel the world, doing about the only thing I know how for a great living finally. You've all seen this happen in front of you, most certainly if you've kept track of my career. But there's a hell of a lot that you haven't seen and have no clue is happening with me. I've done a pretty good job keeping matters in my private life that way. I've done a pretty good job of keeping my game face on all the time no matter what. Mom died. . . she was a bitch and deserved it. Got word my Dad died. . . he left me years ago so I couldn't mourn too much. I'm out of a job-again. Fine, I'll fine somewhere else to bleed and raise hell. But you see right now, right now there's too much shit hitting me at once. Too much ripping me, one of the proclaimed sickest men in the sport's history up inside. I don't have it in me right now to talk shit. I hardly have it in me to look at who's next and frankly I can't remember because this is the most sober I've been in the past two weeks and the only reason I'm not face down is because I managed to crawl onto the sofa without falling over. I've got guys swarming all around me, looking to make me the first FORMER Gunryo champion since SJPW reopened. I got guys around the world looking to take me out and take my spot in the sport. I have a personal life that's imploding around me and a work schedule I'd like to see any of you TRY and do without losing your fucking mind every now and then. So I'm gonna make this real clear. The happy-go-lucky Despair you all know pretty well is not here right now, leave a message with the answering service. The self loathing, bitter hate machine that on occasionally take his place is very much IN right now and will be more than happy to see you RIGHT FUCKING NOW. And maybe after some blood is spilled, another dozen or so chemicals are taken and some very carnal acts committed will your normal program resume. Til then, tell your ancestors that you'll be joining them very soon because I'm in no mood to play around right now. I'm not adding becoming the former gunryo champ, or being on the losing end of a tag match or having another part of my life FUCK OFF ON ME BE ADDED TO THE LIST OF FAILURES THAT IS MY LIFE. *Despair finally looks up at the camera.* Despair: I'm bringing all my persona hell with me boys, hope you've made peace with your makers. *fade out.* ----------------------- Super Bakemono: ----------------------- [Animation comes across the screen, showing a masked man battling some Ninja. The masked man has a demon faced mask, and he strikes down his opponents with precision martial arts strikes. Giant letters come across the screen, stating the name "Super Bakemono". After the masked man defeats all his Ninja opponents. The masked figure looks at the screen..] Animated Super Bakemono: Greetings! I am Super Bakemono, number one Disciple of Despair. I am the most reckless warrior in all of Super Japan. I destroy opponents in the ring... [a Ninja starts to rise up again, but Bakemono catches him with a back elbow to the face, sending him back down to the ground] Animated Super Bakemono: Or in the world of animation. I am the greatest villain since Super Tengu stalked tv sets around the world! This weekend, I crush another hero of the people. That's right. The "Kyoto Tiger". The man known as the "Upset Kid" for his great victories over such washed up has beens like Tasogare no Prince, Tetsuo Matsuomoto and the American Enforcer. Where are they know? Oh yes, I know Tasogare no Prince was wrestling in that big tournament, and he lost to a man whose first name is that of a dish of raw fish we love to eat! [Animated Bakemono laughs this loud, but distorted laugh] Animated Super Bakemono: I am your true hero. I am the man who strikes fear into the hearts of good men. I am the evil creature hiding under your bed at night. I am the monster in the closet. I am the thing you fear most after the lights are of. This weekend, I crush the "Kyoto Tiger", and end his future. He has no chance. He had no chance. Want to know why? Because in the end, he could not hope to contest a force of nature. Which is what I am. Pray to your ancestors Asakura-san, for soon, you may join them.... [Another pack of Ninjas drop down from the tree tops, and Animated Bakemono shakes his head] Animated Super Bakemono: *sigh* A villains work is never done. [Animated Super Bakemono turns his back to the screen, and starts to battle the Ninja. The scene slowly fades out as he kicks one in the throat, his opponent staggering back, and slamming into a tree....]
