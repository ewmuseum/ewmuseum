Control Center - Flash Packet 8/26/04

Aug 31, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

El Santo Azteca ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Grand Prix Heritage Champion Kentaro "H-Bomb" Kawasaki: ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ [Double K, "Ecchi Kid" Kentaro "H-Bomb" Kawasaki, walks into camera shot. He is wearing his superbike leather jacket unzipped exposing his "Damnation Alley" t-shirt, and has on his fingerless gloves. KK is wearing wrap around crimson sunglasses and has a half smoked cigarette in his mouth. A half finished bottle of Jim Beam is in his left hand. A few attendants yell at him, as his absence has been noted. Kentaro simply blows smoke in their faces. The GPH title is nowhere to be seen.] Shut up already! I don't care about you, your stupid job, or your stupid title. [KK sees the camera and blows more smoke. He has yet to remove the cigarette from his mouth. A 5 to 10 o'clock shadow is well represented on his jaw.] Funny how the big boss here gets hurt trying to pimp out SJPW even more. When your champion refuses to debase himself for an audience for a different promotion, after having some stupid tournament go nowhere, you'd think you'd be done with all this crap? No, of course not. He had to bleed in order to get a clue, though I doubt it worked. I got a good laugh out of it though. [Lowering his shades a bit, the camera catches Kentaro's bloodshot eyes, as if he has been up to a few less savory things.] Trios matches just aren't that much fun. Most of the match you sit on the apron, wondering when you're going to get into that ring. Some would kill their own first born to be in a match with Tasogare no Prince, Brody Thunder... but not that other guy. This little promotional thing is taking time away from my needed sleep. [Scratching his temple and taking a swig out of his precious bottle of booze, KK then spits the cigarette out of his mouth and yawns.] The outcome is secondary to the punishment I can lay on someone with my bear hands. Hopefully my idiot partners won't screw this up. I have little tolerance for imbiciles... and my tolerance for much of anything has already used up by this stupid intrusion on my personal space. [Kawasaki walks off camera, obviously no longer interested in speaking. ----------------------------------- Gunryo Champion Despair: ----------------------------------- *Open to Despair apartment. The current Gunryo champion is sitting on his sofa ripping off old bandages and inspecting the wounds. He still looks like hell from the TSTL match against Brody Thunder. Despair grimaces as he applies medication to some wounds that haven't finished healing yet. A shot of GENERICO Sake and he continues to patch himself up, not bothering to look at the camera when he begins to speak.* Despair: So Azteca, you think you got my number huh? Think cause you've managed to get a hold of me when I'm far less than one hundred percent that you can stand up to me? I'm gonna have to send your reality crashing down onto you very quickly and painfully old man. See this body? The scars, the disfigured hand, the fucked up face? I suffered all this for this sport. I gave my mind, body and soul to this sport in order to get to where I'm at now. I gave in the ways it hurt most and then gave more. And now you come along feeling high and mighty because you snuck in and got me to tap. I gotta admite the tactic, something like I would do. The claims you've been making about you can beat me got alot of ego in them as well. Just like the shit talkin' I do all the time. Difference is that I actually have a chance in hell of livin' up to my smack talk and then some. *Despair looks up and it's very clear he's not a happy camper.* Despair: I know your type Azteca. You're on your last wheels and trying to make one last big run for the money. One last run to show that your career was validated. That missing important things like birthdays and holidays was worth it. You want to know that you were that damn good. Most of all you want to stay relevant today. To not become a bloated joke or a hanger on. You poor bastard. To try and figure all this out you decide to fuck with me. That's not a good show of judgement Pops. I'm not going to go a little bit easier on you just cause you qualify for a senior's discount at most places. I don't care about your legendary battles or what you've done to lead up to this match. All I care about is making YOU tap out when it counts. And after that's done, I'm gonna piss on your legacy just for good measure. *A sick grin crosses Despair's face.* Despair: You can't hide forever Azteca and you can't keep living in the past. It's time you realized just how out of date you are and how weak your style truly is. Me, at least I can say I've hung with the best while the majority of the world only knows who you are because of your recent attacks on me. I brought you more fame lately than you've ever had and just like some bastard god I'm gonna steal it away from you. Hope you're ready to face up to your world going straight to hell. *fin.* ---------------------- Junya Toroyama: ---------------------- [He stands, his chest heaving as he breathes heavily. Perspiration furls down his wide chest, falling in rivulets against his thick musculature. He stands there, clad in nothing but dark shorts, bare feet digging into the hard soil. He stands there, on a stone cleft overlooking a steep fall, and he stands there with closed eyes. Hair falls over Junya Toroyama's eyes, hair grown somewhat long, worn shaggy and soaked through. His hands are clenched in fists at his side.] JT: No more Chiimu Tora. [He inhales deeply, exhaling with emotion.] JT: No more Dokyu Dojo. [Another inhalation. Another exhalation with purpose.] JT: No more wars with friends. No more wars with team mates. No more wars of words. No... more. [His eyes open slowly.] JT: I am tired. [His eyes drift closed once more, slowly coming back to life.] JT: I am very tired of the in fighting. Kikuchi-sensei. Super Mask-sensei... I apologize. Bakemono-san... I apologize. I apologize for the shift. I apologize for the change. I apologize for the dishonor which I have laid upon all around me. This is not me. This bickering and silly fighting. These arguments. These pointless contests. These are not me. These are not the ways Kikuchi-sensei trained me. These are not the ways my Grandfather, Super Mask to you, taught me as a child. I was taught to be strong. [His eyes snap to attention.] JT: I have come here, for yamagamorai. To cleanse my mind and body and spirit. To rid myself of the darkness I have caused. To clean up Super Japan Pro Wrestling from the filth which has followed me. No more Chiimu Tora. No more Dokyu Dojo. [He shakes his head. An act of silent defiance.] JT: No more fighting match trials. Please. [His head shakes again.] JT: No more words against each other. Please. [And again.] JT: Let there be peace. [He stops. He stares.] JT: No more Chiimu Tora. No more Dokyu Dojo. Now only... ....the strongest arm! [He slaps a bicep.] ....the strongest heart! [He slaps his chest.] JT: Now only Toroyama Junya! NOW ONLY TOROYAMA JUNYA! [His voice echoes off the cliffs. His eyes close. His face reddens. He smiles.] [Fade.] ---------------------- Mr. Smiley Mask: ---------------------- [Scene opens with Smiley in his hero pose.....] MrSM: Thank you! Today is a proud day for your favorite super hero... Today is the day I unveil the Smiley Signal! [The room goes black...........................] GIANT SMILEY FACE MOMENT!!!! [A giant smiley face shine's on the wall.....] MrSM: There it is... [Even though it's still dark Smiley still strikes his pose....] Thank you... You may bring the light's back up... [The light's come up to see Smiley in his most favorite of poses...] MrSM: Thank you! If there is trouble, if you're in need of help just give me a call.... For I am..... MR. SMILEY MASK! [Smiley strikes the pose yet again....] MrSM: Thank you! Your hero is but a phone call away... Just dial 1-800-SMILEY1, and I will be there! MrSM: It will not matter... If help is needed help will be given! I am here for the greater good of the people! [Oh brother here comes another super hero pose.....] MrSM: Thank you! The Smileymobile is gassed up and ready to go at anytime day or night.... [With that said the studio goes black and the Smiley signal flashes on the wall!] MrSM: Great Buddha! [Fade to black.......] -------------------------------------------------------------- Scott Starring/Knox Roberts & Tommy Bedford: -------------------------------------------------------------- [The scene opens some time before the latest episode of The Zone is due to hit the airwaves. Here, in a quiet corner of the arena parking lot, we find two men who aren't even booked for tonight's show - "Terrible" Tommy Bedford and "Hard" Knox Roberts. Bedford is sitting on the hood of a rental sedan, his flip-flops swinging lazily from his feet. Roberts stands in front of the car, leaning against the grill.] TB: Yeah, I hear ya -- he might _not_ show up. I don't know. . .But what do you think?? TOMMY~! ?? Or maybe SUPER Tommy?? You know, when in Rome -- [Bedford stops mid-sentence when Roberts nods in the direction of an approaching figure.] TB: Well, well. . .The Boy Wonder showed up after all. If an autograph is all you were after, son, we coulda handled this inside. [Scott Starring flashes a distinctly unamused look in Bedford's direction.] SS: You know why I'm here, so let's save the witty remarks, huh? [Bedford smirks and glances at Roberts, who nods curtly.] TB: Sure, kid. [Bedford shrugs.] We're all ears. SS: Okay. So look. . .last show, Despair cost you the match against Bakemono and Torayama, right? And you know, chances are, he's going to be looking to do the exact same thing tonight, when Jase and I face these guys. TB: Chances are. SS: Yeah. So here's the deal -- I want you two to watch our backs out there tonight. I want you to make sure that if Despair decides to show his face again, then he doesn't get the chance to affect the outcome of another match. How does that sound? [Bedford grins again. Roberts arches an eyebrow quizzically.] TB: Oh, _that's_ all you wanted? You want us to run interference on a certifiably insane suicidal maniac like Despair? I suppose you've got a reason why, too? At least I hope you do, since we don't exactly work outta the goodness of our hearts. So tell me...you got a reason why we should care, or are you as stupid as you look? [Starring shrugs, nonchalantly.] SS: Yeah, I'll tell you why you should care. For one thing, I know you're both mad right now. You're both pissed that Despair screwed you last week, and cost you ground in the tag league. TB: Maybe we are. And?!? SS: Well, I'm sure you'd like to even that score, right? You'd love to wipe that smile off Despair's face, and give him a taste of his own medicine. [Neither man is hiding his amusement at Starring now, who smiles in return.] SS: But more than that, I know why you really care what happens in this match tonight. See, I know a little about you guys. I've followed your career, I've watched your matches, and I think I know enough to have an idea how you operate. [Starring looks each man in the eye, in turn.] SS: You're businessmen. You're professionals. And more than anything, you're smart. You wan [Fade out... And back in again, to a locker room. Some time has passed, and now we find Scott Starring in the company of his tag-team partner, Van Wild. Both men are preparing for their match against Bakemono and Torayama, although Starring seems a little further along than Wild. The younger man is fully dressed in his ring attire, and is performing a stretching routine, while Van Wild still sits lacing up his white wrestling boots. His elbow pads lie on the bench beside him, along with a roll of wrist tape.] WILD: Okay, kid, so let's run through this one more time - with Bakemono, we need to be... [Wild pauses as he sees Starring look up at the clock in the room. His brow furrows, and he reaches into his nearby bag, pulling out a wrist watch and checking that too.] WILD: You holding out on me here, kid? [Starring turns to his partner, perhaps a little quickly.] STARRING: Huh? [Wild grins.] WILD: Well, this big hurry you seem to be in, and checking the time like every sixty seconds, I figured you must have a hot date lined up or something. STARRING: Oh...no, nothing like that. I...I just need to go find someone a second, okay? [Wild looks at Starring for a moment, his expression hard to read.] WILD: Yeah, sure. You need me to tag along? STARRING: Nah, it's okay, Jase. No big deal. I'll, uh...I guess I'll meet you at the curtain in a few minutes? WILD: You got it, kid. [Starring turns to leave, when Wild calls out after him.] WILD: Hey Scotty...you be sure and tell her 'hi' from me, okay? [Starring looks back, and nods distractedly, before disappearing around the corner. Wild chuckles to himself and returns to the task of lacing his boots. Fade out.] --------------- Takahiro KEOKI: --------------- [Chiimu Tora Dojo.] [The sounds of repeated strikes against an inanimate object echoes throughout the empty Chiimy Tora Dojo -- or almost empty, as we can hear. The strikes become more frequent, faster and faster until a _very_ loud strike is heard... and we find Takahiro KEOKI standing in front of a punching bag. Breathing heavily, KEOKI watches as the bag wavers from side to side -- an obvious side effect of the strikes upon it. Looking at the camera, KEOKI speaks...] KEOKI - "Have you ever been close to victory, only to have it snatched away?" [KEOKI bends down, hands on his knees, not taking his gaze off the camera.] KEOKI - "Seems like it's happening to me more and more as of late." [KEOKI lifts a hand into the air, his index finger extended.] KEOKI - "Zenmen Sensou. The Gunryo Title Match. Despair was at the edge of defeat, only a few more minutes of life left in his body. Though the victory _and_ the title were within my grasp, I managed to slip up... getting my mouth wrapped with barbed wire and passing out from the pain. I let my first chance at a championship slip away from me." [KEOKI extends his middle finger, making two fingers.] KEOKI - "The United States. The Darwin Cup. Winning a qualifying match made me one of only _five_ competitors in the final match... and two had been eliminated. The crowd was behind me, my opponents weren't able to keep up with me... but in the end, I _still_ lost. Though it was a great experience, it wasn't what I set out to do in the ring." [A third finger extends out.] KEOKI - "And finally... and perhaps the most disappointing... right here in SJPW. The rumors were swirling that I'd be Yamishita-san's selection to represent our promotion in the J*STAR Festival. Instead, the choice was made to let Horo Atashi take that spot. Though Atashi-san is a great selection, it still felt like a dagger had pierced me." [KEOKI gets back to an upright position.] KEOKI - "And now I'm once again poised for greatness... sitting atop Block B of the Junior Struggle! Tournament -- the tournament that will crown the first Sedei-X champion. I've beaten every competitor that's stood across from me inside the ring... except for _one_. If I win, I'm in the finals. If I lose, who knows what might happen to me. Will I continue on this path? Will I make a mistake? Am I destined for greatness, only to be beaten by failure?" [KEOKI bows his head for a moment... then brings it back up, confidently.] KEOKI - "Only time will tell... and only _I_ can change my direction. In a few short days, I'll get into the ring with Takayoshi Asakura. In a few short days, I'll get my chance to shake my streak of bad luck. In a few short days, I'll find out whether or not I have what it takes to become Sedei-X champion... and to _remain_ undefeated. Until then, I'm going to prepare for this fight. I'm going to shake off the jetlag from being in the United States, and focus solely on the challenge that lies ahead. Asakura-san, you are a formidable opponent, one that should not and _will not_ be taken lightly. I must become that much faster... that much stronger... that much more agile..." [KEOKI's face turns into a stare.] KEOKI - "...that much _better_ than you." [KEOKI begins to stretch, preparing for another round with the punching bag.] KEOKI - "I'm going to be ready, Asakura-san. No longer can I accept failure. No longer can I accept defeat. I'm at the point where these are not options -- only _victory_ is acceptable. And on The Zone!, Asakura-san, I _will_ get to the Sedei-X Title Match. And when I _do_ reach my goal... I _will_ bring home the Sedei-X Title back _home_..." [KEOKI motions towards the room.] KEOKI - "...right here in Chiimu Tora." [With that, we fade.] ------------------- Tasogare no Prince: ------------------- [Once again, we find SJPW's intrepid girl reporter and resident eye-candy -- Keiko Otaku -- standing on a stretch of Tokyo sidewalk in front of a nondescript looking building. She is dressed in a very short navy blue skirt, which shows off her shapely legs, and a white blouse which is buttoned all the way up to the collar, from which a short navy blue tie is suspended. She paces back and forth impatiently, as if barely able to contain her excitement. Finally, she explodes in excitement at something off screen and speaks to the camera...] KO: I think this is his limosine! Get ready!! [A black limosine pulls up to the curb next to the girl interviewer. A few moments later, the passenger door facing the sidewalk opens and Keiko rushes to greet whoever is exitting the car...] KO: I knew he was coming here!!! Tasogare no Prince? Can we have a brief word with you?? [Indeed it is Tasogare no Prince who steps up out of the long car and onto the sidewalk next to the overly excited journalist. He is dressed in khakis and a loose fitting black shirt, but has his ever-present purple trenchcoat on over them. His silver hair has been pulled back into a single thick ponytail which falls back past his waist and he wears a pair of violet lensed sunglasses -- which he now adjusts as he takes a good look at Keiko...] TnP: Ah, Keiko-chan, I must admit I'm surprised to see you here. You are truly a most resourceful girl to know to wait here for me. KO: Ah, gomen! [she takes a quick, short bow...] but I heard you were going to be coming here today to record some more voice-overs for your animated television show and wanted to get your thoughts on your Main Event match for this Week's Zone!! TnP: Soo? You are undoubtedly referring to the so-called "Trios Scramble Match" in which I will be teaming with Brody Thunder and Minobu Tetsuhara to face Kentaro Kawasaki, Jun Suzuki, and Tommy Fury, ne? KO: Hai! [she nods enthusiastically...] TnP: But of course I will speak to you about my match, Keiko-chan, seeing as how you've gone to all the trouble of tracking me down here. Firstly, I would like to say that the combination of talent I find myself allied with is an interesting one to say the least. You have the raw ability of Tetsuhara in stark contrast to the experience of Brody Thunder. KO: And what are you thoughts about teaming with Thunder-san in particular?? TnP: Well, I can honestly say that Thunder and I have little if anything in common. But I will also say that Brody Thunder is a true legend in our sport, and that is a word the Prince of Twilight does not use lightly. Likewise, I do not take lightly the opportunity to be in the same ring with Thunder, whether it be against him, or as in this case, on the same side -- to do so means that one has achieved a level of recognition in this sport that many others only dream of, and I am thankful for it. I assure Thunder-san and Tetsuhara-san that I will do my utmost as their partner to insure our victory! But, I must confess, victory is not necesarrily my main concern gong into this match... KO: Oh? Then, what is? TnP: Kentaro Kawasaki. KO: And what exactly are your feelings towards Kawasaki going into this match? TnP: Hmph! Some time ago Kawasaki made some reference to me "believing my own hype." or something equally ludicrous. Let me assure you, Kawasaki, that any "hype" surrounding me is not of my own doing. It is true that I intentionally project a certain image and surround myself with trappings to create that image, but it is not that image which has led to my success. It is my ability and talent in the ring which has made me what I am today, not some so-called "hype," a fact that Kentaro Kawasaki will learn to his regret all too soon. As for Kawasaki himself, his own actions belie a man who supposedly cares for nothing. A man who truly cared for nothing would not go to the lengths to which he has gone to make people believe just that. A man who truly cared for nothing would not feel the need to destroy the Baba cup trophy, or attempt to injure Masashi Lanther. Rather, these are the actions of a man desperate for attention. Truth be told, Kentaro Kawasaki lacks the character to be truly apathetic! Now, if you'll be so kind as to excuse me, Keiko-chan, I'm afraid I have an appointment I must keep. Suimasen. [The Prince bows politely and steps around the young interviewer to enter the building behind her. As the door closes behind him, Keiko looks into the camera...] KO: As usual, interesting comments from Tasogare no Prince!!! I know that I for one can't wait to see the big Trios Scramble Main Event on this week's edition of the Zone!! Don't miss it!!! This is Keiko signing off!! [The pretty girl reporter smiles broadly, beaming in self-satisfaction as the camera turns off and the scene fades to black...] -------------------------- "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder: -------------------------- [Fade in to a tight close-up of two hollow pipes. The camera's focus is on the end of the pipes with everything else out of focus. The low, gravelly voice of Brody Thunder cuts the unsteady silence.] BT: Yer lookin' at the business end o' a double-barreled 12 gauge shotgun. It can handle two seperate targets with the same deadly force. It's simply jus' a matter o'the shooter's focus. It's twice as deadly when it finds it's intended prey. * CLICK! CLICK! * Same as me. [The camera now shifts it's main focus beyond the barrels where we can see Brody Thunder is sighting the gun towards the camera. He lowers the gun and grins that wicked smile.] BT: I make that analogy fer one person. This person seems ta think that I'm .. 'distracted' cuz o'what happened on "The Zone". This person seems ta think that I've somehow lost focus or purpose with regards ta him an' our lil unsettled score. [Thunder cocks the shotgun open and lets it rest in the crook of his left elbow. His eyes stare into the camera with his familiar squint.] BT: So I'm here ta let him know ain't nuthin' further from the flamin' truth fer you... Jun Suzuki. [Thunder's grin widens.] BT: While it's true that there's more on my plate right now with Joey Petrow's lil pearl harbor job on me, I ain't fergotten yer sorry ass, runt. I still _owe_ you BIGtime. Y'see I make my bread-n-butter here in SJPW these days. That means if i don't win my paycheck's smaller an' if my paycheck's smaller my family suffers. An' _that_ jus' ain't acceptable. [The shot continues of Thunder from the mid-chest up. He seems a little distracted as he s looks downward, his body fidgeding abit as he continues talking.] BT: You seem to think that jus' cuz I got my squash busted open agin an' a lil blood got shed, that I've somehow jus' put you outta my mind. [He glances up into the camera with a steely stare and that wicked grin.] BT: Uh-uh, runt. You couldn't get _that_ lucky. [He fidget's some more looking back downwards.] BT: Y'see _yer_ the one who's gonna get my bank account fat. Yer the guy who's gonna provide me with the next step to gettin' the BIG prize, amigo... that Gunyro Champeenship. Yer the one... I'm gonna make an example o', Suzuki. I'm gonna show the boys in the back, the fans an' the entire wrestlin' world what it means ta cross swords with Brody Thunder. My focus has never been clearer, my friend. I _know_ my goal. An' I know that ta acheive that goal... I gotta put you down.. like the mangy dog you are. [Thunder chuckles to himself.] BT: All bark an' no bite, ain't that how the sayin' goes? [Still he continues fussing with something unseen by the camera.] BT: Well this _wolf_ bites an' bites hard, Suzuki. I _know_ I do. An' when we do collide inside that squared circle that canvas is gonna run red with our blood, you know it, I know it. One focus. The image zooms in on Despair's face, showing how much agony he is in. You hear Despair scream out "I QUIT" before the image freezes. The camera zooms back, showing the image is on a television. Pulling back farther, we see El Santo Azteca, in casual attire but still wearing his mask, seated on a couch. His hand holds a remote, and he stares at the screen. He calmly places the remote down, and stands up. He turns to face the camera, an intense look on the eyes behind the mask...] Azteca: Despair. You have made a mockery of a sport I love very, very much. Not with your actions in-ring. I do not like the Jisatsu style, but I understand it's place in our sport, and accept that it won't just go away. No, it is your actions outside the ring that offend me. The excessive drinking in public. The use of recreational drugs. The sexual escapades. These are the things that disgust me. Why? Because you make it seem that we are all this way. [Azteca shakes his head, looking down at the carpet] Azteca: In some ways, you remind me of my nephew. He too lives the way of Jisatsu in our beloved Mexico. He puts his body, sometimes his very life, on the line, in order to entertain the fans. I do not understand this fascination, no, this almost fanatical willingness to harm oneself in order to entertain our fans. It presents an image of insanity. Are you insane Despair? You so willingly put yourself on the line, then submit so readily to one of my armbars? [Azteca shakes his head again, sighing in apparent disgust] Azteca: You claim that you will make me tap out. I will warn you that will likely not happen. I am descended from the Aztec people Despair. My ancestors were a harsh people. They sacrificed human hearts in order to prevent the sun from going out. That is a fanaticism you will never be able to equal. It means that I will not submit so easily to a man such as you. It means that you can shed my blood, beat me within an inch of my life, and you will still not hear me say "I Quit". You can try though, but you will fail. As you failed to defeat Brody Thunder at The Skys The Limit 4, so too will you fail to make me submit. [Azteca walks closer to the camera, his eyes glaring into them] Azteca: You will not make me submit Despair. However, I can, and likely will, make you submit. So be prepared to say "I Quit" again, Despair. What is that saying you Americans have? "Third time's a charm?" Well, this will be the third time Despair, so when the end comes, and I take your title, remember that I warned you this would come to pass. [Azteca waves his hand in dismissal at the camera, and it slowly fades out as he goes back and sits down on the couch]
