The doorknob turns, the door opens, and standing there is the "Young Lion" himself, a look of relief upon his face. Closing the door behind him, KEOKI leans against the door, letting the rolling luggage in his hand to drop to the floor as he smiles widely and sighs.] KEOKI - "It's good to be home." [Walking into the living room, KEOKI plops down on his black leather couch.] KEOKI - "The United States was absolutely beautiful. The city of Las Vegas was amazing, with many things to see and do. But, as great as it was, there's just something about being back somewhere familiar... somewhere that you _know_ where everything is. It's been a long flight, a long day, but I've got little time to waste before wrestling again." [KEOKI flips on the television, SkyPerfect TV!, what else?] KEOKI - "August 7th. Tateyama Citizen Center. The Junior Struggle! Tournament. In the match that could very well determine the winner of Block B, I will step into the ring with an undefeated record facing off against the also undefeated Wolf Jager. With the prize being a shot at the Sedei-X Title at Zenmen Sensou, we'll both be ready. It doesn't matter how tired I am. It doesn't matter how many miles I've travelled. Wolf Jager will be waiting. Jager, I know you will not be an easy challenge. I know you're just as hungry as I am to win Block B. But I'll be there, and I'll be ready for whatever you've got. Though you might be a wolf, I am a young _lion_. I am filled with determination. I am filled with confidence. And I _will_ be the man to advance to the finals of Junior Struggle!" [KEOKI sits up from his prone position.] KEOKI - "Many have asked my thoughts on comments made by fellow Team Tiger members regarding Despair. I have been silent regarding this, mostly because of my flights between Japan and the United States. But right now, I'm going to address what has been said. Many have said I had no right to go after Despair. Many have said I should apologize." [KEOKI shakes his head, with fire in his eyes.] KEOKI - "But I will _not_ do so." [The shot pans in tightly on KEOKI's face.] KEOKI - "I have _nothing_ to apologize for. I meant no harm in my comments, I was simply stating that my actions of acting along in my fight with Despair was in _no way_ a reflection of Team Tiger backing down. You can take them as harmless or harmful. You can choose to accept my comments as they were intended, or try and _make_ me apologize. Either way, I am _not_ sorry for what I said. Despair was, is, and always will be a cancer in SJPW. Whether it's me, Tasogare no Prince, or anyone else in SJPW, we _will_ fight to see that he's eradicated from this promotion. Just know this -- I will _not_ back down from a fight. If you think I'm too brash for Team Tiger, so be it..." [The fire now _burns_ in KEOKI's eyes.] KEOKI - "...but _don't_ expect me to back down." [KEOKI stands, the shot panning back out.] KEOKI - "My goal is now to win Junior Struggle! and place the Sedei-X Title around my waist. I will take on _any_ challenger, Team Tiger or otherwise. I've already defeated three men, and Wolf Jager, prepare to become number _four_. I'm ready for the attack, Wolf... and when that bell rings, this "Young Lion" is going to _pounce_!" My guests at this time are Vladimir Tarasov and "Russian Superman" Petor Andresku... The Red Army. You currently have two wins and only one loss in the GPH Tag League. What is your impression of SJPW thus far? ANDRESKU: Keiko Otaku... you ask good question. What our impression of GPH so far. Red Army feel we get robbed last week on Zone. We feel that we should be perfect 3 and O. That represent true ability of Red Army and our dominance over SJPW Tag Team. TARASOV: No get us wrong. All are quality opponent, but none on level of Petor Andresku, Russian Superman. None on level of Vladimir Tarasov. That loss be only loss you see on Red Army record, and we avenge loss next time we meet. OTAKU: Coming up in your league bouts, you have matches against Doug Thomas and Frankie "The Thumper" Douglas as well as a match against KANJI Sakaigama and "Crimson" Jin Hiroto on the next SJPW: The Zone! taping. ANDRESKU: We look forward to next matches with teams you mention, but we confident that when chips down... Red Army prevails. TARASOV: We have more skill in little finger than Sakaigama & Hiroto have in entire bodies. ANDRESKU: And Thomas and Douglas... they just weak Americans and we already prove what we do in America. We look forward to giving both teams wrestling lesson they never forget. TARASOV: Make no mistake Keiko Otaku... we no take competition lightly... we just know that when come to wrestling... no one better than Red Army. No one get up from Sickle... no one escape Iron Curtain... they all tap to Red Square. ANDRESKU: They all lose... to Red Army. (fade out) -------------------------------------------------------- KANJI Sakaigama & "CRIMSON" Jin Hiroto: -------------------------------------------------------- [The camera opens to a backstage shot of the Battle Sphere Tokyo. Super Japan Pro Wrestling's 7/24 rally has just ended at the arena, and several wrestlers and office workers scurry about happily. A stark contrast to this are the Visual Spirits, who we see (still clad in their ring attire) rushing through the back of the arena, shoving various staff members away, their bags in hand. The camera pans behind them, as we see SJPW reporter Keiko Otaku trying to catch up to the pair. She eventually catches them, before speaking into a microphone she's been carrying] Keiko Otaku: "Hiroto-san, KANJI-san...do you have any post-match comments?" [KANJI Sakaigama, looking pissed off, turns to face her] KANJI Sakaigama: "Yeah, how's this? It was BULLSHIT. We had those baka beat, but your biased referee had to go and stop us from finishing them off!" Keiko Otaku: [Perplexed] "But KANJI-san...it was a double knockout..." [An angry Sakaigama sighs and begins to walk off, but Hiroto stops him] Jin Hiroto: "Just move on." Keiko Otaku: "All right...on the next episode of The Zone!, you'll be facing The Red Army of "Russian Superman" Petor Andresku & Vladimir Tarasov. Before that, you'll take on Gregor Davinsky & Sergei Baranov! Two tough matches coming up..." Jin Hiroto: "'Tough'? You really believe that?" KANJI Sakaigama: "Oi, I heard those Red Army guys running their mouths a couple weeks ago. Are they _trying_ to make themselves look gay, or does it just turn out that way...?" [Hiroto stifles a laugh, as Otaku looks disgusted] Jin Hiroto: "It's interesting to me how all of these teams come in here and automatically assume they're winning this tournament." Keiko Otaku: "But isn't that what YOU said...?" KANJI Sakaigama: "The difference is we're actually gonna fucking do it! These foreigners come to Japan, and think they're gonna run shit over here. But they weren't counting on us being around!" Jin Hiroto: "The Russians can't even form a coherent sentence. How do they expect to beat Japan's top tag team?" KANJI Sakaigama: [In a horribly done Russian accent] "Answer. is. they. lose. Run back to home land. Never come back." [KANJI, seemingly the only person who found that funny at all, cracks up as Otaku continues her interview] Keiko Otaku: "You're really not afraid of them at ALL? They're pretty strong, and undefeated here too!" KANJI Sakaigama: "Hey, so are we, you stupid bitch! Who do you think we are?" Keiko Otaku: "Well, I..." Jin Hiroto: [Interrupting] "We're done playing around. After tonight, we're not taking any more chances. You're about to see the REAL Visual Spirits in SJPW. I just hope these other guys are ready." Keiko Otaku: "What do you th--" KANJI Sakaigama: [Interrupting] "Interview's over. Now fuck off!" [KANJI and Hiroto both nearly knock Otaku over as they brush past her, exiting the arena as our camera fades out] ---------------------- Mr. Smiley Mask: ---------------------- [We catch up with Mr. Smiley Mask who is outside of his "estate." Alright it's not an estate, it's more like an apartment building somewhere in Chiba.... He's standing next to something covered by a large tarp...] MrSM: So glad you could make it! Your hero has an unveiling! [Smiley breaks into his Super Hero pose...] MrSM: Thank you... I'm excited to unveil to you MY fans.... [Another Super Hero pose....] MrSM: Thank you... My new Smileymobile! [Smiley whips the tarp off unveiling a VW bus! It's old, it's drab and it's a VW bus..... Smiley busts out another Super Hero pose...] MrSM: Thank you! Beauty isn't she? I know she's not much to look at now but with a few coat's of paint she'll be ready to roll.... Mr. Smiley Mask will now be better equipped to protect you people........my fans! [Yikes another Super Hero pose...] MrSM: Thank you! Now fitting with my "C'mon Get Happy" attitude I figure painting the Smileymobile in a multitude of colors... [Smiley takes another tarp off of several gallons of multi-colored paints...] MrSM: Now let's get painting shall we?! [Time lapse images fill the screen... Smiley is painting like a mad man... Cripes.....] MrSM: Thank you! Remember when danger is around and you fell unsafe don't hesitate to call on your friendly neighborhood Mr. Smiley Mask! [Alright go ahead.........One last Super Hero pose for the road....] --------------------------------------------------------------------- IICW Cruiserweight Champion "All Star" Joey Darren: --------------------------------------------------------------------- [In front of the SJPW banner, with his IICW Cruiserweight title over his shoulder, we see "All Star" Joey Darren.] ALL STAR: This Saturday, I face off with "Firefly" Horo Atashi. Atashi, you are a very formidable opponent and I look forward to our match. However, know that when I get in the ring, all bets are off. I will do whatever I have to do to get this victory...within the rules of course. So on Saturday Atashi, you will be left in the center of the mat, looking up seeing an All Star!!! [Joey walks off, stage left. We zoom in on the SJPW banner then fade to black.] ------------------- Scott Starring: ------------------- [Fade in to a sight very familiar to many of the stars of Super Japan – the interior of the Chiimu Tora Dojo. It's business as usual for the famed Puroresu school, with the dojo's instructors putting the latest batch of trainees through their strenuous paces. One new face to this place however, stands quietly at the back of the room, his attention focused on the class before him. A notepad hangs in his grasp, the page already covered with scrawled notes. His attention is diverted by a respectfully low voice nearby. VOICE: Excuse me, Starring-san – we are ready. [Scott Starring turns his attention to the source of the voice – SJPW interviewer, Keiko Otaku. She stands with microphone in hand, a camera man at her side. Starring nods and smiles, stepping closer to the camera. He watches until the cameraman signals the tape is running.] KEIKO: Scott Starring, what brings you to the Chiimu Tora Dojo this morning? STARRING: A couple of reasons, really. First of all, I guess, it's because you never stop learning in wrestling – there's always new moves, new counters, new techniques to be mastered, and a guy like me is always looking to improve my performances. That's one of the big reasons I came to Japan actually, why I've been wanting to do so for quite a while now. And when Mr. Kikuchi kindly gave me permission to visit Chiimu Tora, you can bet I jumped at the chance. KEIKO: So you are here to study the training methods of Chiimu Tora...and perhaps to study the techniques taught to Toramusha also? He is of course your opponent at The Zone this weekend. [Starring smiles.] STARRING: That's right. And yeah, I guess you could say I had that in the back of my mind too. I've spent the last couple of days holed up in my hotel room watching tapes of Toramusha's matches, so I feel I know his style pretty well now. But it's also nice to get away from that for a few hours and maybe get a different take on it too. KEIKO: So how do you think your match will go on The Zone? Are you confident of victory over Toramusha? STARRING: Well, from what I've seen, we're pretty well matched in speed and strength. His kicks of course are something I'll have to watch out for, but I think with the right game plan and my edge in experience, I should be able to pull out the win. And hopefully that will be the springboard I need to build some momentum going back into the Grand Prix tag league. Either way though, I'm looking forward to facing Toramusha. So stay tuned, because I think it'll be a good one. KEIKO: Yes, I am sure it will be. Good luck in your match, and thank you for speaking with me today. STARRING: No problem. [Fade out.] -------------------------- Tasogare no Prince: -------------------------- [The scene is a busy Japanese street at night. Numerous pedestrians hurry by along the sidewalks lit by a combination of street lamps and countless neon signs. Standing on the sidewalk, creating an obstacle for the many passers-by is SJPW interviewer Keiko Otaku, wearing a mustard yellow suit jacket with a SJPW logo cut to fit her feminine curves and a matching very short skirt. She is holding a microphone in her hand and smiles her overly enthusiastic smile as she looks into the camera...] Keiko: Keiko Otaku here! I'm standing on the street outside the back entrance to the Chiba Bridge Arena after the exciting sell out SJPW show!! The crowd has mostly gone, but I was told that some of the wrestlers are still here and may be exiting the arena through this back alley [she gestures to an alleyway behind her...] I can't wait to see who comes out!! [Keiko's annoyingly cute giggle is cute short as a door within the alleyway opens and the silhouette of someone is briefly seen before the door closes again, returning the alley to darkness. Footsteps echo in the shadows of the alley until finally a figure familiar to fans of SJPW steps out onto the sidewalk nearby the by now almost delirious interviewer...] Keiko: It's Tasogare no Prince! [Indeed it is the Prince of Twilight who has emerged from the darkness. He wears a long, lavender colored trench coat which is cinched tightly at the waist his hands in the coat's side pockets. His long silver hair hangs straight down his back past his waist and his eyes are covered by a pair of dark purple sunglasses made of a single molded piece of plastic. Upon hearing his name, the Prince stops suddenly and looks around tensely, but relaxes immediately upon seeing the young female interviewer...] Keiko: Prince-sama! It's me, Keiko Otaku... from SJPW?? [The Prince smiles charmingly at her and speaks in his distinctively soothing voice...] TnP: But of course I know who you are Otaku-chan. But pray tell me, what are you doing here? Waiting for me perhaps?? [Keiko's cheeks blush pink...] Keiko: Tasogare no Prince-sama, would it be possible to get a brief word with you? For our viewers at home of course! TnP: Most certainly, itoshii. How could I possibly refuse? [Keiko snaps out of her fawning schoolgirl mode into a pose that approximates that of a professional journalist...] Keiko: Tasogare no Prince, earlier here this evening you defeated SJPW star Yatoro Hori in what could only be described as a lopsided victory. Would you care to comment upon that match for our viewers? TnP: Of course. Firstly, I would like to say that I respect Hori-san as a fellow wrestler. Sadly, I may have been a little too... shall we say... abrupt in our contest earlier this evening. If any of my fans who came to watch me perform were disappointed I apologize. I'm afraid I may have been a bit over zealous since it was not Hori who was occupying my thoughts. Keiko: Oh? What exactly do you mean, Prince-sama? TnP: While I was wrestling my opponent tonight, I was not picturing myself against him, but rather against another individual, namely Kentaro Kawasaki. Keiko: The "Ecchi Kid?" TnP: Hai. A rather fitting name given his perverse nature. You see, Keiko-chan, in my match tonight, I imagined that it was Kawasaki I was facing and as a result, I may have been a little more determined to obtain a victory than I otherwise would be. Keiko: But why? Why this sudden interest in Kawasaki? TnP: Sudden? No, itoshii, it is hardly sudden. I have kept a close eye on Kawasaki-san's career for some time now. And for a long time, I knew that eventually our paths would cross. In fact, I have been hoping to face him for a while. However, thus far, fate and circumstance has kept us from meeting one another. But perhaps now, Destiny has finally seen clear to cause our respective paths to intersect one another... Keiko: I'm not sure I understand... TnP: No? Then I will make it very clear, not only for you dear Keiko but for Kawasaki-san should he be watching. When Kentaro Kawasaki won the Baba Cup, and chose to highlight that victory by destroying the trophy he was awarded for that victory, he insulted not only the man for whom the trophy was named, but all of puroresu as well. He also insulted each and every wrestler who participated in that tournament, of which I was one such man. I cannot speak for the other participants in the tournament, but I for one, do not lightly tolerate insults. Kawasaki, you have personally insulted me, and unlike others, I do not repay insults with mere words. I prefer to answer them in the ring. Kawasaki-san, sooner or later, you and I will meet in the ring, and whether you are still Grand Prix Heritage champion or not, I look forward to that day, for on that day, you will learn the price for insulting the Prince of Twilight! Now, if you will excuse me Keiko-chan, I must be on my way... Keiko: Oh! Sure!! Tasogare no Prince, thank you very much for talking with me... er, _us_! TnP: [The Prince of Twilight smiles at her...] It has been my pleasure... [With that he places a finger under her chin and lifts her face up towards his, lifting his dark glasses with the other hand as he does so. Her dark eyes meet with his deep violet eyes, seemingly hypnotizing her on the spot. He smiles as he lowers the glasses back over his eyes and walks away, leaving the girl interviewer standing there motionless, watching him walk away as the scene fades to black...]
