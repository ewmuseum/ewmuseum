Billy "The Regulator" James — UPW bio card

EWA ROSTER BIOS Wrestler's Name: Billy "The Regulator" James Height: 6'3" Weight: 241 lbs. Age: 32 Hometown: El Paso, Texas Orientation: Heel Wrestler Personality: Billy truly believes he is making his career based on being the kind of guy who is cleaning up wrestling. He wants to rid the wrestling world of those people who are think the flash should come before substance. he is a wrestling manand doesn't truly believe that angles should be run. He wants wrestling to revert back to the age old sports contest format rather than the entertainment one we have today. Although his is the minority view, he is somewhat popular with internet reporters, but highly hated amonst the younger crowd of viewers. Either way, Billy doesn't care what anyone thinks because he is on a mission. Primary Wrestling Style: Basic Match Worker Appearance and Ring Attire: He has a scruffy appearance. He is slightly tanned and he wears blue jeans and a white shirt while wrestling. He is a decent looking guy, but is brown hair and brown eyes are not emphasized by his attire. When he first enters he is wearing a black duster jacket over his attire and a cowboy hat as he walks to the ring nodding his head as if he knows something. Not very cocky, just always has a lok of determination. Entrance Music: "Blaze of Glory" by Bon Jovi 15-20 Favorite Wrestling Moves: 1) DDT 2) Piledriver 3) Juvi Driver 4) Drop Kick 5) Belly-to-Belly Suplex 6) German Suplex 7) Back Breaker 8) Fall-Away Slam 9) Verticle Suplex 10) Powerslam 11) Knee Lift 12) Snap Mare 13) Brainbuster 14) Trailor Park Slam 15) Short-Arm Lariat Finisher: "The 38 Special" Finisher Description: Inverted DDT, Slop Drop, Death Drop Set-up Move: Set of 3 chops in a row to daze his opponent. Strengths: 1) Technical 2) Toughness 3) Strength Weaknesses: 1) Overconfidence. Truly believes he is the man to "clean up wrestling". This attitude normally leads him to take matches he can't really handle. 2) Fan Problems. He can't understand why sometimes fans boo him when all he wants to do is make this sport worthwhile again...even if accomplishing this goal means taking down a few fan favorites as well. 3) Risk taker. He tends to try and do a little too much to win a match and this sometimes causes him to get caught in many precarious positions. Manager/Valet Name: None Manager/Valet Description: N/A Character Profile: Billy James has wanted to be a professional wrestler all of his life. When he was young he watched the days of "Rock-N-Wrestling" and always aspired to one day rub elbows with the true superstars of the business. As he grew up, he took an interest in football, amatuer wrestling, and other high school sports. He was quite athletic and enjoyed great success in all levels of high school sports. Once out of high school, Billy went to college at a small Junior College in Texas. He participated on their wrestling squad, which had meets against many top division I programs in Texas. Once out of college, he managed to catch on with a training group in Texas. The problem was Billy was mistreated an taken advantage of. He wasn't properly trained or prepared in how to perform as a pro wrestler and he was hurt many times over the first year. Billy wanted to break away and go somewhere else for training, but he had signed a contract that he would work for one year within this league as payment for the training he was supposed to receive. Upon the end of that contract, he tried to land several jobs with other independent wrestling groups, but was unsuccessful. Finally he caught on with a small group out of Indianapolis called EWA. Once he was hired, he performed matches for the staff and needless to say underwhelmed them with his ability inside the ring. However they loved the way he looked and EWA owner, Chris Hopper decided to make him a project. Over the next few months, he trained and worked with Billy and helped him grow into a decent worker. Chris also helped him develop a persona Billy wanted to perform as his gimmick. Billy saw the movie "Young Guns" and he loved the Billy the Kid portrayal in it. He figured he could call himself "The Regulator" and really get over as a cowboy. Chris thought it could work, especially considering Billy's background was easily usable for this gimmick. After a few weeks of work Billy "The Regulator" James was born and became an on-air staff wrestler for EWA. Billy was cut immediately after Hopper sold interest in EWA to Sundown Promotions and his career seemed dead until recently. Hopper called and offered him a spot as a roster wrestler within his new venture, EWA. Billy accepted and moved to the big city. Now he's here to perform for a grander stage than he has ever dreamed of. Wrestling History: Former Staff Wrestler in SCWE and EWA
