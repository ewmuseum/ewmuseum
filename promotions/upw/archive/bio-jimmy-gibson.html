Wrestler's Name: "Jammin'" Jimmy Gibson Height: 6'0" Weight: 235 lbs Age: 29 Hometown: Atlanta, GA Orientation: Face Wrestler Personality: Gibson has been in the business for a number of years now. He isn’t a young pup and knows how to work the crowd heel or face. He has charisma and he works over the crowd to get them really into the match. He is overprotective of his girlfriend / manager, Princess. (Not always his manager, but when she is...) Manager/Valet Name: Princess (CURRENTLY NOT USED) Manager/Valet Description: Jimmy’s girlfriend who is is super protective of. Think - Garvin/Precious or Savage/Elizabeth. This can distract him and motivate him. She is a drop dead gorgeous and classy blonde. She dresses classy no clad stripper look like women of wrestling today. Nice dresses or skirts. Usually glitz and glamour to go with the outgoing garb that Jimmy wears. She can’t wrestle or really protect herself versus other wrestlers. The best she has is a big booming slap or kick to the balls. However, she is feisty and will stand up for herself. Think old school wrestling couples. Character Profile: Jimmy and Princess had the classic Prom King and Prom Queen story. They were High School sweethearts that lived the Teenage dream. Jimmy was a sports athlete that spent his high school career scoring touchdowns and making game winning shots. In Chicago he was the man in SCW. He lived for the league and was sent on all fan gatherings. After the fed closed its doors, Jimmy spent some time away from the industry then toured Japan. Two years ago Princess gave birth to their first child. She hasn’t been touring with him. He is now ready to come back and find his new home and continue his climb as the top wrestler in the industry. Wrestling History: Former SCW Champion
