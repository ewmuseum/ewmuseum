UPW World Tag Team Title History

UPW World Tag Team Title History Champion Reign Date won Date Lost Event Notes Soldiers of the Sun 38 days 3/5/2017 Survival PPV Defeated The Warriors (c), Dominion, and The O'Malley Brothers in Fatal 4-way. The Warriors 166 days 9/20/2016 3/5/2017 Showdown TV Defeated The Hype 2 fals to 1 in a Best 2/3 falls match when Robertson pinned Nash. League closed down Nov, 30, 2015 The Wolverines 33 days 10/28/2015 11/30/2015 Battlezone TV Defeated The Order by pinfall when Jeff Cagney pinned The Overlord. The league closed at end of November, so reign ended there. The Order 79 days 8/9/2015 10/28/2015 Heatwave Defeated The Italian Stallions, The E.U., and The Wolverines in a fatal 4-way when The Overlord pinned Nicco Leone. The E.U. 84 days 5/17/2015 8/9/2015 Slamfest V Defeated Delta Force by pinfall when Henriech Kurchur pinned Danny Johnson. Delta Force 124 days 1/13/2015 5/17/2015 Showdown TV Defeated The Gladiators when Rick Snyder pinned Lucious. The Gladiators 58 days 11/16/2014 1/13/2015 Collision Course Defeated The Wolverines, The Mongolian Assasins, and Delta Force in a TLC match when they retrieved the belts first. The Wolverines 98 days 8/10/2014 11/16/2014 Heatwave Defeated Italian Stallions, The Gladiators, and The E.U. in a fatal 4 way match when Jeff Cagney pinned Pierre Goffinet. The E.U. 60 days 6/11/2014 8/10/2014 Battlezone TV Defeated Delta Force by pinfall when Heinreich Kurschur pinned Danny Johnson. This occurred on the first edition of Battlezone TV Delta Force 24 days 5/18/2014 6/11/2014 Slamfest IV Defeated The Order by pinfall when Rick Snyder pinned The Extremist. The Order 110 days 1/28/2014 5/18/2014 Showdown TV Defeated Fast & Furious by pinfall when The Overlord pinned Mac Diesel. Fast & Furious 156 days 8/25/2013 1/28/2014 Heatwave Defeated The Gladiators in a "Hell in the Cell" Match when Mac Diesel pinned Lucious. The Gladiators 68 days 6/18/2013 8/25/2013 Showdown TV Defeated Fast & Furious by pinfall when Crixus pinned Ricky Santana. Fast & Furious 30 days 5/19/2013 6/18/2013 Slamfest III Defeated Gladiators, Deathrow, and The Hackers in TLC match by being first to retrive the hanging belts. The Gladiators 124 days 1/17/2013 5/19/2013 Showdown TV Defeated Jones/Steele when Crixus pinned Apollo Jones. Jones was severely injured in this match and it was the last time he would wrestle. Jones/Steele 58 days 11/18/2012 1/17/2013 COllision Course Defeated the Gladiators when Damian Steele pinned Lucious. Also marked the first time titles held by a team not tag-centered. The Gladiators 189 days 5/13/2012 11/18/2012 Slamfest II Defeated The Hackers when Crixus pinned Crash Override. The Hackers 26 days 4/17/2012 5/13/2012 Showdown TV Defeated Killer Instinct when Crash Override pinned Fulgore. Killer Instinct 28 days 3/20/2012 4/17/2012 Showdown TV Defeated Italian Stallions, The Hackers, and Anarchy by pinfall when Fulgore pinned Acid Burn. Italian Stallions 30 days 2/19/2012 3/20/2012 Survival Defeated Anarchy in a cage match when Nicco Leone escaped the cage first. Anarchy 54 days 12/27/2011 2/19/2012 Showdown TV Defeated the Italian Stallions when Chaos pinned Nicco Leone. The Italian Stallions 30 days 11/27/2011 12/27/2011 Colision Course Defeated Anarchy, Killer Instinct, and Law & Order in a TLC Match by retrieving the titles that hung from the rafters. Anarchy 68 days 9/20/2011 11/27/2011 Showdown TV Defeated Law & Order on Showdown TV when Vincent Mariucci pinned Billy Law. Law & Order 128 days 5/15/2011 9/20/2011 Slamfest I Defeated Deathrow, The Hillbillies, and Anarchy in Fatal 4-way match when Billy Law pinned Redman.
