Tag Team Name: Thunder & Lightning Combined Weight: 500 lbs. Wrestler #1 Wrestler's Name: Thunder Height: 6'0" Weight: 270 lbs Hometown: South Bend, IN Orientation: Face Attitude Notes: Thunder is quiet and down to earth. Despite his appearence he is extremly intelligent. He graduated from collage with a 3.8 GPA with a Liberal Arts Degree and is currently working on his masters degree. Outside the ring Thunder spends alot of time outside the ring with family. He loves giving. He spends alot of time working with high school kids motivating them to get healthy physically, spiritually, and mentally. Believes in order to have a completly healthy you have fo feed all three. Wrestler #2 Wrestler's Name: Lightning Height: 6'4" Weight: 230 lbs Hometown: Toledo, OH Orientation: Face Attitude Notes: Lightning is a risk taker. He is one who takes life for everything it has to over. He spends life going from one risk to another. His previous adventures include sky diving, kayacking down waterfalls, and rock climbing, sometimes without the use of ropes. He isn't married because he can't settle down. He is an extremely faithful man and uses that faith to live. The fears of the earth don't scare him. He hasn't met a challenge that has scared. Lives by the motto "Show me risk and you'll get my attention." Tag Team Finisher: "All Clear" Finisher Description: Thunder lifts opponent up into stalling suplex position while Lightning goes to the top rope. Lightning then comes off the top rope hitting the opponent with a cross body block. Then they finish the move off by finishing the suplex while Lightning hits the cross body block. Sandwiching the opponent with the suplex and crossbody block. His legs and arms are musclular however is upper body looks like he is out of shape. Underneath the flab though is a powerhouse. He is built very much like Mark Henry. Thunder has buzzed brown hair and a short gootee mustace and beard. There is noticable three ince scare on Thunders right bicep where he tore it in college at a wieght lifting contest. Clothing ( Wrestler #1 ): Thunder wears long black wrestling tights with a yellow lightning bolt down the left side. He wears tall red wrestling boots with black strings. To the ring Thunder wears a baseball jersey featuring a local sports team, usually picked out by his tag team partner. Wrestler #2 Looks: Lightning Lightning is of average appearence and has a very athletic look to him. He is muscles are well defined but not huge or bulging. He has a short black hair with a short red mohawk spike down the middle. He has two tatoos on his upper arms and one on his left leg. The right side a cross and on the left side a picture of a lion's head. Around his lower left leg he has a strand of barbed wire. Clothing ( Wrestler #2 ): In the ring Lightning wears black cargo shorts that go just below his knees that have a two zipper pockets on the sides. He usually wears two different colored socks one red and one black. He wears midsized black ring boots that have been specially made for jumping and climbing. He usually wears basketball jerseys to the ring from the local sports teams of the city he is competing. Character Histories: The two meet while wrestling for a northern Indiana based wrestling league. At the time they were both vieing for individual titles. Then at a supercard Main Event they teamed uped for the first time and ended up in a high profile fued for the federation. During one of those matches the commentaters mentioned that the two reminded them of a football backfield where one running back was quick like lightning while the other was the bruiser. The two soon offically teamed up under the name Thunder and Lightning. They have been together as a team for the last two years. Before that "Lightning" Jake Miller was a member of the tag team Amish Extreme. The two parted ways in 2003 without any ill feelings when Jake wanted to drop the Amish part of the gimmick and focus more on the Extreme. Since then he has traveled all over the country honing his skills jumping off the highest parts of the arena he can find and diving off the top rope as far as he can. He likes to think of himself as the second best Extreme Aerilist in the country. On one of those stops he would up in the Northern Indiana Wrestling Federation, where the had just signed a brand new talent who just graduated from school named Tommy Johnson. Tommy Johnson had just started wrestling and was challenging anybody and everybody to beat him in submission matches. The two did meet one time in the ring in a elimination battle royal. They had thrown everybody out of the ring and were the only two left. It however was only for a couple of minutes before more compititors entered the ring. They were part of the final 5 when the last three entrants teamed up aganst the two eliminating them. As a result of that match a heated fued began which saw the two become friend and teamates. The two have had several offers to join the ranks of developmental leagues of some big time federations but don't want to get involved with the lack of morals and integrity that they have become known for. With the association of the University of Indianapolis the two are confident that the EWA can keep its integrity while at the same time create a product that is intense and enjoyable to watch. Thunder and Lightning are thrilled to bring thier air and ground attack to the UPW Wrestling History: Longtime journeyman tag team that has competed in numerous places, but never achieved any real success.
