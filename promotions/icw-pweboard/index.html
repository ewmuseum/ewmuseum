Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Everything held of ICW is an ending. Its own forum on the board that hosted it was described in one line — “Come inside to see the stars from ICW compete for one last time!” — and it sat first among that board's inter-promotional productions, then in its archives.

The card went up on 2 October 2005 for a show on 14 November, twenty names deep, in what its own poster said was the order people had accepted: Matt Stanton, Deuce Jackson, Tatewaki Kuno, Darkside, Carnage, Shane Helms, Hellraiser, Masked, Allen Miles, Nightcrawler, Demention X, Jonathon “Happy” Monroe, Stone Cold Steve Austin, “Eye Candy” Randy Walker, Halie, the Undertaker, Neta, Randy Orton, The Elite and Hawk. Underneath it ran thirty-three replies, one of them a reader telling the board to let it die and another answering that they were doing one more show.

Over the next six weeks the matches were announced and roleplayed: a thirty-man battle royal, a deadline thread, five singles matches, a proposed Royal Rumble, two wrestlers posting fresh bios for a promotion that had already closed. The last post on any of it is dated 5 December 2005.

No results were ever captured, and no championship of the promotion's own is named anywhere. What the record holds is the promise of a show and the twenty men who agreed to be on it.
