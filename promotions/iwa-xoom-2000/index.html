Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This IWA surfaces across five recruitment posts between 22 January and 14 March 2000, seeking cardwriters for cards it ran twice a week, Wednesdays and Saturdays, hosted at a site on Xoom.

Each post was signed by a vice president — different people at different times all carrying that title — a detail the promotion's own record never explains.

By 1 March it reported having just finished a pay-per-view and was taking a week off before the next card; a second post four days later announced new champions from that event and pointed to a sample card from it; and a third, on 14 March, opened a hunt for cruiserweights to challenge for its cruiserweight title, with new wrestlers barred from roleplaying until the promotion's own president had cleared them.

No document survives past that final posting. Which wrestlers actually held the titles the promotion crowned in March, or how it ended, is not recorded.
