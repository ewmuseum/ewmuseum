THE _W L W_ WORLD LEAGUE WRESTLING U P D A T E In this update, you will see... I. Results of 9/10 II. Line-up for 9/24 III. Due dagtes for 9/24 IV. Shout out for writers!! V. WLW Title Tournament ----------------------------------------------------------------- September 10th - TUESDAY NIGHT TITANS report Hour 1 ------- * In a Bracket A Title Tournament match, "The American Rage" Chris Page and Steve Douglas match was ruled a draw after a double pin. WLW Executives ruled that both Page and Douglas will advance to the Bracket A finals. (2 stars) * In a Bracket C Title Tournament match, Czon Yozon defeated "Hatchetman" Joey Terrifying with the use of the ropes. Czon Yozon advances to the Bracket C finals. (4 stars) * In the Hour 1 Feature Match, "Couch Potato" Carl Lumski defeated The Mercenary and "Redemption" Justin Slayne in a Tables Match that saw interference from Hightower. (3 & 1/2 stars) Hour 2 ------ * In a Bracket B Title Tournament match, Joshua Barnes defeated Jacob Josh Jordan via TKO when Jordan was knocked out after hitting his head on the canvas from a powerbomb. Jordan was taken to the hospital afterwards. Joshua Barnes advances to the Bracket B finals. (2 & 1/2 stars) * In a Bracket A Title Tournament match, "The Flyking" Will Geddings defeated "The real Untouchable" TrU after hitting his patented double arm DDT, known as "The Eagle Claw". Will Geddings advances to the Bracket A finals. (3 & 1/2 stars) * In the Tag team Gauntlet match, The final two teams, The American Dreams & the Deities made it to the Ladder Match, where Brett Young, from the Dreams, and The Suicide Kid, from The Deities, each grabbed one of the tag team championship belts. Mike Bilodeau ruled that Brett Young and The Suicide Kid are now the tag team champions!!
