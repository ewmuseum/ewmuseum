WLW Title Tournament - status ----------------------------------------------------------------- October 9th - TUESDAY NIGHT ACTION report Hour 1 ------- * In the opening match, "The Dominator" Ray Holt and Dr. Lester Jain defeated Big Daddy Captain Crunch and The Heartbreak Corn Flake when Lester Jain pinned Big Daddy Captain Crunch after Crunch was hit with "Termination" (1 &1/2 stars) * In the Bracket B Finals, Czon Yozon defeated Hightower via a forfeit when, after attacks by The Mercenary and Yozon, Hightower could not make it to the ring. Czon Yozon enters the Quadruple Threat Match for the WLW Title on October 22nd. (N/A) * In the Bracket A Finals, "The Flyking" Will Geddings defeated "The American Rage" Chris Page and Steve Douglas in an elimination match after pinning first Douglas, then Chris Page. Will Geddings enters the Quadruple Threat Match for the WLW Title on October 22nd. (4 stars) Hour 2 ------ * In the Bracket D Finals, "Revelation" Ryan Valtorro defeated "The Suburban Commando" Ryan Delaney in a very close match after getting the three count after both men exchanged a series of two counts. Ryan Valtorro enters the Quadruple Threat Match for the WLW Title on October 22nd. (4 1/2 stars) * In the Bracket B Title Tournament match, "Couch Potato" Carl Lumski defeated Joshua Barnes via Countout when Barnes was distracted on the floor by Jacob Josh Jordan. Carl Lumski enters the Quadruple Threat Match for the WLW Title on October 22nd. (3 stars) * In the Main Event, Wasted Youth and "Mr. Excitement" Dropkick defeated Brett Young and The Suicide Kid to become the new WLW Tag Team Champions in a Steel Cage Ladder Match when Dropkick captured both belts and Tag Team President Mike Bilodeau announced that, since Wasted Youth was Dropkick's partner, they both win the match.
