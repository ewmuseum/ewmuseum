Wrestlebowl V Press Conference

25 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
W.L.W. World League of Wrestling   PRESS CONFERENCE   [We open to the WLW Studios, where a podium is set up and waiting...for the WLW President, Mr. Mark Rosendorf. He steps out with a speech in hand. Rosendorf sets his speech on the podium and begins to speak into the microphone.] Mark Rosendorf: Good evening WLW fans and personnel. I must first begin this message with an apology. When someone, anyone, is hired to perform the services of a WLW official, it is with the trust that they do not involve themselves in the action. Brody Thunder betrayed this trust and attacked the WLW Champion, "Flyking" Will Geddings after a 43 minute grueling contest on October 22nd. Unfortunately, Will Geddings is unable to wrestle now, due to the unprovoked and, in my opinion, cowardly attack by Brody Thunder. Normally, there is a 30 day rule on a WLW title, meaning a champion must wrestle every 30 days. Because Brody Thunder was a WLW official when he initiated this attack, I am waiving this rule for the champion. Also, upon speaking to Will Geddings from his hospital bed and having conversed with...Thunder's "lawyers", I have come to a decision...one I hope I will not regret. [Rosendorf clears his throat as he flips over a page from the documents on his podium.] Mark Rosendorf: Although I normally do not believe in rewarding acts like the one committed on October 22nd, I have decided to give Brody Thunder a contract to wrestle here in the WLW. Furthermore, since it is Will Geddings' INSISTENCE that he wrestle Brody Thunder at Wrestlebowl V, and it is a WLW policy that the title be on the line during pay-per-views, I am making a match between "The Flyking" Will Geddings and "The Lonewolf" Brody Thunder at Wrestlebowl V...however... ...since Wrestlebowl V taks place from Chicago's United Center, and I want this "dispute" settled THAT night...I am making this match a CHICAGO STREET FIGHT!! May the best man win...and yes, the WLW title WILL be on the line! [Rosendorf pauses, looks down at his notes, and then back up towards the camera.] Mark Rosendorf: Ladies and Gentlemen, this match will be part of a DOUBLE Main Event for Wrestlebowl V...the second Main Event is a Number One Contender's Match for that very WLW World Title. This will be a 2 out of 3 fall match involving two men who each have a rightful claim towards being the number one contender to the World Championship based on the happenings of the October 22nd Quadruple Threat Match. At Wrestlebowl V, the 2 out of 3 falls match will be between "THE REVELATION" RYAN VALTORRO and CZON YOZON. In this match, Czon Yozon will choose the special stipulations for the first fall, just as Ryan Valtorro will choose the special stipulations for the second fall! Should this match make it to a third fall, the #1 Contender's spot will be determined in an "I Quit" match. The winner of this match will recieve a WLW World Title match shortly after the pay-per-view as part of a new season of Tuesday Night Action on UPN. Once again, I wish both men the best of luck. [Rosendorf flips a page from the podium, looks it over and picks his head up, continuing...] Mark Rosendorf: There is another issue I want settled, and that is the issue between HIGHTOWER and THE MERCENARY. At Wrestlebowl V, these two men will face oneanother...in a BARBED WIRE MATCH. [The President of the WLW pushes his papers together and straightens up, ready to leave the podium.] Mark Rosendorf: Ladies and Gentlemen, there will be a total of EIGHT matches at Wrestlebowl V. Between these three, as well as the two "Wrestlebowl Melee" matches, you now know five of those matches. On November 5th, I will announce the match that will determine the WLW's first NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION. As well, Mike Bilodeau will have more information on the WLW TAG TEAM Title defense at Wrestlebowl V. I thank you all for tuning in...as the Road to the WLW's Wrestlebowl V begins.
