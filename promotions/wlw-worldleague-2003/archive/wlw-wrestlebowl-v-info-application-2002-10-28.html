Wrestlebowl V Info & Application

28 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FOR THOSE OF YOU LOOKING TO TRY OUT THE WLW, THIS IS YOUR CHANCE!! Along with Wrestlebowl V being a WLW pay-per-view, there is also two matches based on the four Wrestlebowl open invitational tournament style of the past. The Wrestlebowl portions of the pay-per-view are as follows: ************************************************************************** WRESTLEBOWL MELEE!! ------------------- Participants draw numbers earlier in the night. The first four begin the match and participants, based on the order of the numbers, enter the ring every 30 seconds. 12 tables and two ladders surround the ring. Two Wrestlebowl Medals hang high above the ring, on opposite ends, hanging from cables hanging down from the ceiling. The two competitiors who can each climb a ladder and remove a medal from it's cable will be declared co-winners and move onto the three-way Elimination match for the Wrestlebowl title!! 3 WAY ELIMINATION MATCH FOR THE WRESTLEBOWL TITLE -------------------------------------------------- Joshua Barnes vs Winner of vs Winner of (Champion) Wrestlebowl Melee Wrestlebowl Melee *************************************************************************** While there will be WLWers added to this match, characters not on the roster will also be invited into Wrestlebowl V as part of this match...with no strings attached. This is like a trial period for us both. If you would like to remain on the roster, you can, but if you wish to keep this to a one-shot appearance, that will be respected as well. In order to join the Wrestlebowl Melee as part of the big Dec. 8th PPV - Wrestlebowl V, EITHER fill out the below application, OR send in another application that you have recently filled out. Roleplaying for the pay-per-view begins on November 20th for the fed and continues through Dec. 1st. As you know, we use an open roleplay system, you would simply send your roleplays to [detail withheld] along with those on the roster. Note - Preference will NOT be shown to wrestlers in the Melee simply because they are on the roster, this is a special match that anyone can win, which is how the WLW works...competition, best rps and strats win, no politics involved in the decision making. Also, handlers who already have characters in the WLW, that wish to test out a new character, this is a place you can do so. If you are interested, please let me know as soon as possible as only a limited amount will be accepted into the Wrestlebowl Melee...and it is a first come, first serve basis. Sincerely, [name withheld] WLW President Other matches at Wrestlebowl V: MAIN EVENT # 1 - NUMBER 1 CONTENDER'S MATCH 2 OUT OF 3 FALLS Yozon chooses stips for Fall #1, Valtorro chooses stips for Fall #2 If a Third Fall is necessary, it will be an "I QUIT" match!! -------------------- Czon Yozon vs "The Revelation" Ryan Valtorro MAIN EVENT #2 FOR THE WLW CHAMPIONSHIP - CHICAGO STREET FIGHT ----------------------------------------------------------- "The Flyking" Will Geddings vs "The Lonewolf" Brody Thunder BARBED WIRE MATCH Barbed Wire is wrapped around all of the ring ropes. ---------------------------------------------------- Hightower vs The Mercenary THE NORTH AMERICAN TITLE WILL BE DETERMINED IN A 4-WAY BRAWL Falls count anywhere in the building, anything goes, Eliminations do NOT count! The challenger who can beat ALL THREE of his opponents in this match will be declared the winner and be awarded the North American Championship!! AND THE TAG TEAM TITLES WILL BE ON THE LINE AS WELL!! All this and more on December 8th, from The United Center in Chicago!!!! ----------------------------------------------- Note - to join, you'd have to come onto the WLW mailing list for the time duration of when you join until the PPV. After the ppv on Dec 8th, it is easy to take your name off the mailing list...or you have the option of remaining. STRENGTHS AND WEAKNESSES: [Note: Try to come up with three of each. That's a good minimum.] Strengths are advantages your character has (ex: The Big Show's immense size, Kurt Angle's mat wrestling experience, Triple H's ability to sleep with the boss' daughter who also happens to be head writer, etc.) Things your guy does particularly well and perhaps better than anybody else, or curious physical attributes that are rare and special. Weaknesses are, of course, the opposite (ex: The Big Show's lack of speed and mobility, Hulk Hogan's advanced age, Jackie Gayda's everything, etc.) Things your guy doesn't do well, or physical limits and liabilities. Most apps will insist that your strengths and weaknesses even out (if you have three strengths you must have three weaknesses). I say bollocks. These are supposed to be realistic characters, and real people aren't products of simple mathmatical equations. Try your best to keep them equal, yes, but if you have one more of one than the other, I'm not going to get angry and tear it up. I've had my strengths and weaknesses be one off like that before, and spent way too much time trying to cut one out or split one up or make a new one to reach this magic equilibrium. The idea is to create balanced characters (and prevent people from sending in Invincible Bill Goldberg type apps, which are boring and WILL be sent into the void where junk emails go to die). I mean, I'm not going to throw out your app just because you have 5 strengths listed and only 4 weaknesses (as long as they balance each other). Though I might give preferential treatment to the app that had 4 strengths and 5 weaknesses. HISTORY: Your wrestler's historical story...er, hold on. Let me rephrase that. Your wrestler's historical story RELEVANT TO THE RING and to WLW. Give me a general idea of why he's here, or why we're supposed to believe he's here. Stuff like that for the broadcasters to talk about and use to hype / shill for your guy. Don't tell me about how his father left home when he was four and he learned early on to be tough in a small Texas town and fought his way to be captain of the football team but things went awry when the father of his cheerleader sweetheart made trouble blah blah blah blah blah, we don't care! Leave the "Call Me Ishmael by name" stuff at home. Keep it to why he's here in the WLW, and maybe how/where he learned to wrestle, stuff like that. Don't mention the high school sweetheart unless she's the valet now. MISC: if there's any info on your character that doesn't seem to fit in to one of my neat little categories yet you consider it important for the matchwriters and bookers to know about, put it in here.
