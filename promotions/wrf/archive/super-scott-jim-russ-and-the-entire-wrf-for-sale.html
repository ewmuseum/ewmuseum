Super Scott, Jim Russ, and the entire WRF....For Sale?

Mar 21, 1997, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I just got done reading Pamer's, or whatever the hell is fruity name is...anyways, I just got done reading his recent essay entitled, "What if?" ...In that essay, he talks about giving away wrestlers to keep them going. That gave me a great idea. You see, I don't wanna continue with e-wrestling. Its spring now which means I do _NOT_ wanna be behind a damn computer and I haven't had any interest in e-wrestling in quite some time now, but held on because...well... I actually don't know _WHY_ I held on. :) To make a long story short, my world reknowned characters, Super Scott, Jim Russ, Granny Russ, The Bisexual Man Called Imp, Granny's garage, the World RUSSling Federation, WRF Piss Champ Old Man Robertson, and yes...even The Flakes are up for grabs. Everyone of my characters could be yours if your interested and if I decide to give it to ya. Now, my characters are good/great. I'm not going to let just ANYONE have them. I have to be confident that you will handle the guys well, and all that crap. Now, I'm not going to be like a _CERTAIN_ person and give something of mine away and come back MONTHS later telling you what you are doing wrong with it and that you'll take it away. That's B.S. If I give these guys to you, they are YOURS to do whatever you want, but hopefully you kick some e-wrestling ass with them. All I ask is that if you decide that you've had enough, like I have now, that you talk with ME about what you are going to do with them. I don't want it to become that a scrub like Arcin's handler owns Super Scott, or some schmuck that has no sense of humor has Jim Russ. :) So, without further adieu... LET THE BIDDING BEGIN! -Scott
