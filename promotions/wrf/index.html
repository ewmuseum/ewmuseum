Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The World RUSSling Federation never ran a card of its own; its cast made its appearances as guest turns staged inside other federations' own broadcasts. Its one surviving document, dated 21 March 1997, isn't a card or a promo at all but its own creator putting the whole operation up for grabs, having lost interest in e-wrestling with spring underway: “I actually don't know why I held on.”

Up for the taking were the promotion's whole cast and premise: Super Scott, Jim Russ, Granny Russ, “The Bisexual Man Called Imp,” Granny's garage, the WRF Piss Champ Old Man Robertson, and The Flakes, offered outright to whoever wanted them — “If I give these guys to you, they are YOURS to do whatever you want” — on the one condition that the new owner check back in first: “I don't want it to become that a scrub's handler owns Super Scott, or some schmuck that has no sense of humor has Jim Russ.”

Whether anyone took the offer isn't in the record. Nothing survives dated after 21 March 1997.
