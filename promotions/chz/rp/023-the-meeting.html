The Meeting
Written: 6-17-02

Knock, knock, knock…

…

I knock on the door of my new, well, not so new boss, Richard Taylor. The first thing that he told me when we got on the phone after he informed me that I was coming to CHZ was, 'Mike, there are two things you MUST learn before you even step foot into one of the CHZ's ring, and those are to: One, respect those of higher authority than you are... Example, ME! Two, don’t fuck with people that you know who could kick your ass... That's for YOUR sake, not mine. I mean, if you fuck with someone like that, I could easily get the paramedics to haul your dead ass to the hospital, just like that.' WHAT?!? He actually told ME not to fuck with someone who could kick MY ASS?!? Who the fuck does this guy think he is?!? George W. Bush?!? Heh, heh... Yeah right. I hear footsteps coming from inside his office, and the doorknob starts to turn slowly. The door opens, revealing Richard, cell phone in hand, all spiffed up. 'Hey! He must be going to one of his Greek Orgies again!! I thought I warned him last time that he shouldn't be going to those, or else he’s gonna get them herpes... Oh well, his loss.' Well, anyways, he invites me into the office, and offers me a seat, which I cannot deny.

'So, Mike, what brings you here?' Richard says, reluctantly.

I reply with, 'I wanted to talk to you about some things that are bothering me here,' even though I wanted to reply with 'Richard, to tell you the truth, I just want to annoy the fuck outta' you, and ruin your day.'

Rich replies, 'What’s the matter, Mike...'

Oh my God, he’s starting to sound like my fucking mother! 'Boris... Boris Nakobi. He’s my problem.'

'Yes, yes. A lot of people have been complaining to me about his cockiness and unwillingness to work,' he says.

'So, basically I’m advising you to give me a match against him so I can legally beat his ass without getting fined by you,' I say, wanting to add, 'you fucking bastard' to the end of that phrase.

'Mike, you know I can't book you into a match with him for this Wild! He's already booked in one, and he'll be WAY overworked if I put him in two. And plus, I'd have to pay him DOUBLE for that!' Rich replies, with a stupid look on his face, much like the stupid clothes that he's wearing.

'Fine! You want it that way, you'll get it, buddy!' I reply, furious with the fact that he won't give me what I want!

I jump out of the chair, and storm out of his office, and slam the door behind me. I walk down the hall, when a bright idea hits me. I remember that inside my contract, it states, 'CHZ Superstars are not liable to any injuries or death that they bring upon other CHZ Superstars, as long as the tragedy occurs on ground that the CHZ is holding a show on.' And then it hit me... I would finally get my hands on Boris after all...
