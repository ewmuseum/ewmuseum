The Train of Thought
Written: 9.8.02

Persona Variable just sat there. He sat there wondering whether his match against DeJohn at Wild had been a success or a failure. He wondered if the two would meet again.
Persona sat and looked around the dimly lit room, the light struggling to escape from the lantern on the bedside table. He hated motels. There were no feelings inside of motels, no real history. Just temporary history that fades silently away like the stains on a kitchen cloth. Persona stood up and walked over to the window. He noticed various fingerprints of previous inhabitants on it. He decided to add his own. He knew that they would remain long after he had left. He looked out of the window at the street below. It was just ordinary, nothing spectacular, sure it had a few pieces of greenery, but it wasn't great. Much like Canada in general, PV thought.
Persona sat back down on the ageing sofa, allowing his back to get a much-needed rest. It had been one hell of a match, nobody could deny that. Hell, it had been Persona's first match in CHZ, and Persona Perfect Dark's first ever match. He was a bit sore, but he'd never felt happier with his life. He couldn't believe that he was actually getting paid to hurt people which he loved, and that it was 100% legal. Persona recapped upon the match, how he had withstood a hell of a lot of punishment and still managed to win the match, but he hated that he had indeed taken one hell of a lot of punishment. He knew why. He found it hard to combat Jman's basic flying ability, hell everybody has the ability to fly, but PV knew that he was just another Dodo.
Persona's face suddenly lit up as he revealed his pearly white teeth in a broad smile. He was remembering the later part of the match how he had made Jman bleed, bleed for his fans, the fans that adored Jman, gave him that little bit more confidence, a little more adrenaline. He showed the putrid fans who was superior, who was the more aggressive. He knew that Jman would come back at him hard, he knew that once Jeff realised that he had split him open, that Jeff would want to do the same to him… But PV didn't care, he has no fear, he shows no remorse, something that he emphasised further after the match. PV's smile widened even more. He was visualising the expression on Jman's face as he struck down on his back and ribs with that chair, it was one of both shock and scintillating pain. Persona Perfect Dark loved it.
Persona once again moved, opting to lay on the softish bed so reduce the stress put on his back further. He stared at the ceiling, noticing all the indents that it had, before continuing thinking about CHZ, and this time it was the upcoming Wild. He knew he was in a triple threat match, and not only a triple threat match, but a triple threat match in a cage. Persona knew that he had already shown that whole of CHZ that he was more than capable of competing in a cage match, and if you needed further evidence, ask Jeff 'Jman' DeJohn. As for his opponents, he knew that they disliked each other, in fact they hate each other. He also knew that they would be the main focus of the match, that the eyes of the world would be on them, and them alone. He knew that people will be intrigued to see how they feud develops, and if they can possibly hurt each more that they did last Wild. But Persona didn't care. He couldn't give a shit about either of the two about their feud and their rivalry, about the history that preceded the upcoming match. All PV knew that it was his job, his mission, his goal to ensure that by the end of the match he had left a never ending impression on the two, and that the two would hate him more than each other. Persona really didn't care about the outcome of the matchup, whether he wins or loses, all PV knew he wanted to do was hurt his opponents. Persona cracked his knuckles before continuing his train of thought. Persona knew for a fact that he couldn't give a flying fuck about the CHZ World Title that this match and a series of others could lead him to attain. Persona was securer than that. He didn't need a piece of gold with writing spewed across it connected to a leather strap to tell him that he was good. It may be important to some people, but not the Millennium Man, the Man in a Million Persona Variable. He knew that he was good, and if he happens to become the best of the best and win the gold, great, but if not, he wouldn't loose sleep over it. The train of thought had reached its station, and PV's back was beginning to seize up. He got up off the bed and, he had decided that a walk might do him, he switched of the lamp before walking out of the door.
