Reservations
Written: 6/14

The camera fades in on a small helicopter flying over the tops of the Canadian forest. Boris sits beside a white bearded man who looks to be about 20 years older then Boris. Boris is looking out of the small window at his home. He sees a few bears and a couple of dear running around in a small meadow. He looks up ahead and sees an eagle soaring in the sky. He then looks forward and sees his cabin. It’s just the way he left it. The door is shut tight. The fire that he cooked his last meal on before he left is still smoldering some how. Akita knows where to be seen. The helicopter slowly starts to descend to the ground and Boris gathers his belongings from behind him. Opens the door and gets out. He pays the man and the helicopter slowly rises to the air and is gone. He walks over to the house and unlocks the door. He then notices a trail of blood leading around back. His heat skips a beat. The blood is very dark. He walks around back slowly praying that it’s not what he thinks it is. As he rounds the corner his face lights up. Akita is there and she has brought supper. She has taken a small deer. Boris walks over to her and pats her on the head. He takes his knife out of his belt and cuts the deer’s belly open and he throws the stomach and the other internal organs out on the ground. He then cuts out the heart and cuts it into four pieces. He looks at Akita.

“Here girl. Eat this. It is very good for you.”


He walks over and puts them down in her bowl. He then hangs the deer by it’s feet and starts skinning it. This deer’s flesh looks to be very tender. It is freshly killed. It will make for a great stew or jerky. He looks over at Akita. Akita seems to be bigger then she was before and she seems to have lost all of her winter coat. She is a bright brown now and her eyes a dark green. Her nose red and black nose is very moist. She looks to have taken very good care of her self while he was gone. He finishes the dear and grabs a saw. He starts cutting the legs off and then moves to the ribs. He is very careful of the back strap. The back strap is the most delicious part of the deer. It’s the prime cut. Better then any steak. He cleaned all the meat of the bones and threw them in the woods along with the other organs. The wolves will take care of them. He then went inside and started cleaning the cabin. It needed a good cleaning. The floors were covered in mud and dirt and the bathroom was just horrible. He got down to work. He finished the floors right before sundown and decided to clean the rest in the morning. He hadn’t even cooked supper yet! He finished supper around 10:00 and he sat down to eat. He turned on the radio and listened to the Canadian wrestling station. He then heard the card for Wednesday wild being broadcasted in a promo for the CHZ. Boris listens for his name and it is called. He has a match with Theo Knox. He finishes the bowl of stew.

The 26 of June. Mark that in your calendar Theo. Because on that day you will be beaten with in an inch of your life. Theo its nothing-personal Theo. It’s just how I play this game. You come into a match with me you better be prepared to bleed. So before the match Theo I want you to call an ambulance and go ahead and make reservations with a hospital. After I get I am going to beat your ass so bad you won’t be able to walk for a week. You’re going to feel pain that you have never felt before and that is a promise.

Boris pours him another bowl of stew. He then looks down at Akita. She was lying on the floor under his feet. She had finished her deer heart and was waiting on some stew. Boris looked at her and said.

“No, Akita I said that you were going on a diet and that’s that.”

She looked at him with a sad look on her face. Boris smiled and laid some carrots on the floor. She looked up at him with a look that said, “What is this crap!” Boris knew that was what she was thinking.

“Akita it’s good for you just eat them.”

Akita started chewing on them. You could tell by her movement she was not very happy and did not like her diet. Boris noticed this and threw a couple pieces of venison on the floor. Akita in haled them. She then turned back to eating her carrots.

“Don’t worry, you will learn to love them.”

Akita was not even listening to him now and he knew it. He took up the carrots and put down some stew in a bowl. He just let her have the juice though. He didn’t let her have the meat. She lapped up the stew juice. He finished his second bowl and washed it in the small sink. He took off his boots and put them under the bed.

“Well good night Akita. Oh and Theo start reserving you a hospital bed. Your going to need it”

He lie down on the bed and fell asleep as soon as his head hit the pillow. Akita walked to the foot of the bed and lay down. She started dreaming about chewing on a huge t-bone sleep. Boris had a dream of his own. It was off him standing over a fallen wrestler. He held his hands high. The camera fades out on a freeze shot of him holding his hands over a fallen wrestler. The question is who is it?
