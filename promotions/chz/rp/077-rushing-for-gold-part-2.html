Rushing For Gold!Part 2
Written: 23/7/02

--Begin Feed--

The last few hours have been a blur. No cars in thirty minutes, the royal blue 1993 Pontiac Grand Prix cruises down a road, which can only be known as a deserted highway. Manny Garcia lies in the passenger seat as the seat is adjusted into a bed, his feet sprawled on the dashboard. Tiptoe in the driver seat, struggles to keep his eyes open, he then looks over towards his brother, Many snoring logs, as he develops a heap of drool on his seat. Tito reaches over to his Cola that is planted in the drink holder, and takes a sip. He views a sign he passes that says 500 Miles to Detroit, that being the next event. Tito the Garcia boy who will get his first chance in the ring, he has a main event match against Jim Surge, hopes to prove his talent and impress the world with his skill.

He then notices he is low on gas, and out of soda, as the blazing sun is seeps on the face of Tito. He then notices a gas station and pulls over and parks. He then stands aside his car as he fills the tank, he looks around the deserted area where he looks over to the station where he notices two old men on chairs outside of the gas station door, almost like in a movie. Then looks over to the road, notices another car pull in, An attractive mid-aged women steps out of the car, Tito begins a deep stare, the women looks over at him, she addresses Tito with a smile, and a hair brush back. He remains focused on him until one of the old men at the gas station yells over to Tito.

“ He buddy your leaking gas.”

Tito then gives his head a shake and turns off the gas lever, he then gives a quick stare over at the women, as she has a small laugh going as she knows he was to focused on her. Tito then feeling a bit embarrassed as he goes into the station to pay for the gas and get a soda. He pays for the gas, then makes his way back to his car, and gives the women a quick wave and climbs into his car. Before he takes off he stops the car, and makes his way back to the station, and asks the store clerk for the bathroom key. He is handed the key and leaves the station and makes his way to the shed type bathroom next to the station. He opens the door and looks on to, possibly the most disgusting bathroom he has ever seen. The stained white tile, the cob webs in every corner, the toilet that has a few patches of its original color and the rest rust. The walls all brown with some notes written on it. As he does his business he reads a few of the notes.

“ Everyone dies eventually, So why wait?”

“ Derek Carter was her, 1987.”

“ Why lose? When you can win!”

“ Life is like a hole, dig it.”

Not knowing what some of these people were thinking while writing this, he just flushes the toilet and gladly leaves and returns the key and makes his way back to his car, and takes off back onto the road. Manny still sounds asleep as he has been for at least a good four hours now. He continues to notice no cars in sight and every now and then looking at his map that is placed in the glove compartment. His dripping black shirt was beyond belief, so he quickly takes it off while steering with his knees. He then begins to get in deep thought, mostly about upcoming events.

Tito begins to think of his future in the wrestling world, is it the career for him, are the thoughts circling his head. Was football the path for him? He had always loved the game of football, always watching it on the small television they had. Always being the star on all his little league teams. Receiving awards for outstanding playing in high school. And finally his last draw in the line, college, where it ended his career. Not because of his football skill but lack of smarts in the book part of the scholarship he was granted after high school. He was given a few warnings about his grades, and never really took it serious, he believed being a starter for the football team was enough to keep him in the school. The coach was not happy to see him go, but the dean of the university, felt he is doing more harms then good to the school. Always throwing parties on campus, barely showing up for classes, disrupting the class when he was there, and all and all a total waste of a scholarship. The coach fought this with the dean for several weeks before he was let go. It was a downer for all the team and coaches, it was an emotional time, as they said there good byes to Tito, and watched him drive off back home. That is where he began his wrestling experience by joining the combat school in Oakview where his brother, Manny, was participating. Tito to this day still feels he should have been a football player, not a wrestler, but you must play with the cards your dealt with, and he was dealt wrestling, and he will put his all into wrestling as he did football. Manny has always loved this life, being a judo expert his youth life, so he has been waiting this moment for a long while. Tito feels him and his brother have a future in wrestling, because of their heart to win, they are skilled individuals that have winning on their minds. Manny as an individual he might not prevail, Tito on the other hand, is the more muscular one of the two, because of football. Strength is a big key in the one on one tactics to wrestling, Many will be key in tag team fighting, his stamina is unreal, and put the two together, definite trouble for the opponents will erupt.

Tito then gives his head a shake, as he was beginning to get into a groove of thoughts, as he then maintains control of the car again as he was driving on the off roads. He then notices a sign saying, next exit, Detroit. As he gives his brother a shove as Manny adjusts his seat back up, and rubs his eyes, and notices the tall buildings, homes, and enormous traffic. Eventually after site seeing they find the Joe Louis Arena and they pull into the parking lot. The two step out of the car, and make their way to the doors, as Tito looks over to Manny and replies.

“ This is it brother, the beginning of our lives!”

The two walk in the arena and get the directions to there dressing room, from the coordinator. As they step into the dressing room. They notice a red carpet room, with two leather couches in the middle of the room, with a television planted in front of it. The other side of the room is the weight set, where Tito quickly gets into his work out outfit and begins tackling the weights as Manny relaxes by kicking back to some television. Well there both set now its jut one more step, which comes tonight at Hell Bound.

--End Feed--
