Time To Shine
Written: 08/08/02

(As trucks are puling out of Canada and into the USA, nothing really exciting his happening. People are talking, trucks are going cross teh border and TYler Van Serio is fishing... Fishing?!?!?!?)

'Ah what a beautiful day it is to for a nice swin!' says Serio.

'Can't swim here,' says a man beside him.

'What ya talking about?' asks Serio.

'Read teh sign beside you,' says the guy pointing at the sign.

'Oh shit,' he says as he throws his bathing suit into teh water.

'At least I get to fish,' he says as he cast the line into the lake.

'Um, it's cought on that boat,' says teh man.

Serio looks at it then looks at the man, then looks at his rod flying out of his hand.

'Oh shit,' he says again.

'Not a good day? Bad Luck?' asks the man.

'I don't need luck, I'm too good for luck,' he says.

'Um,' the man says pointing at Serio's shirt.

Serio's shirt is bein pulled down by something.

'Shit,' Serio says yet again.

As Serio pulls his shirt, he finds that a fish was eating his shirt.

'Well, at least you didn't loose it,' says the man.

'Funny arn't you?' says Serio.

'Hey!' says teh guy, 'Arn't you Tyler Van Serio?'

Serio looks at him and smiles.

'Why yes I am,' says Serio.

'Ya, you suck!' the guy says throwing mud on him.

Serio looks at the mud.

'That was a $900.00 shirt jack ass!' says Serio.

The man begins to laugh as Serio laughs sarcastically and throws the guy over the bridge and into the water. People look at him

'What?' asks Serio.

The people look away again as teh guy climbs up the ladder and beside Serio.

'Dropped your wallet down there,' says Serio.

'Where?' asks the guy.

He bends over to look for the wallet as Serio pushes him over again.

'Sucker,' says Serio.

People look over again.

'What?' says Serio.

THis time they stay staring at him.

'He fell over,' Serio says.

Everyone says oh and go back to what there doing. Serio picks up a rock and throws it at the guy knocking him into the water again.

'Dumbass,' Serio says throwing another rock.

A lawyer walks over to Serio.

'WHat the hell do you want?' asks Serio.

'Can you sign this paper saying you agree to teh battle royal match?' asks teh lawyer.

'Dwan! I'm busy!' says Serio throwing another rock.

'Just sign teh damn form!' says Dwan.

'Fine,' says Serio shaking his head.
He takes Dwan's briefcase, takes out a pen and throws teh briefcase over. Dwan looks over and Serio kicks him over.

'Sucker,' says Serio signing teh paper.

He throws that over to. Dwan catches it.

'Thank you,' says Dwan.

'No prob,' says Serio walkign away.

Serio walks across teh border.

'Can I see I.D. please?' asks the Guard.

'Here' says Serio.

He reaches in his pocket and pulls out nothign and gives teh guard the middle finger.

'Hey!' says the guard.

'Screw off!' says Serio.

'I'll get you!' says teh guy that fell off the bridge.

'Ya right! Just like I'm gonna lose the battle royal to all those monkeys.'

Serio walks away. The guy trips. Dwan gets out of the water. Serio walks to his limo and jumps in. The driver drievs away as the scene fades to black.

End
