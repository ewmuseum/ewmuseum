Assassination Begins!
Written: 2/9/02

{{Begin Transmission}}

Good looks, natural-born athletic talent, well educated and being irresistible. Would if life was that easy for every individual? Well that means there would be less of the Robbie McMasters and Chris Starr’s in the world. Robbie and Chris have recently been scouted by the great new feeder fed called the Canuck Heat Zone. The federation saw the talent in the two with just a day into the wrestling schooling system. The two are very experienced in the ring, and only at the age of twenty-four and twenty-five. The two met in a Los Vegas federation at the age of nineteen. The two have been either stable partners, or a tag-team combination. The two clicked right away because of their similar personalities. Both men very good looking, and in fact they knew that, which resulted in friendship. The two have now made a name for themselves, Hollywood Assassins! The name is very self-explanatory.

With the small numbers of tag-teams in the CHZ, the Assassins plan to rise fast. Their skills will be put to the test, because at the next Wild they square off against another pair of newcomers, called Easton High. This is a big match for both of the two pairs, as first impressions are key here in feeder federations. Canuck Heat Zone has started there fed great, mostly because of their great roster in, Matt Hunt, Jake Shultz, Paul Harrington, Xander Krave, The Texan, Matt Blitz, Ryan Contra, Axl Young and Soul. And hopefully all the newcomers will be able to expand the ratings of CHZ, and make CHZ a place to watch.

The Hollywood Assassins are the kind of guys who feed off complements, the two can never hear enough about their good looks and great amount of skill. The two have always been crowd pleasers, the ladies love them for their looks, and the men respect their skill. In past federations they have always been able to put on a show, and they plan to do it here in CHZ.

Later today Canuck Heat Zone reporter Karl Malone caught up with the two after they were leaving the Canuck Heat Zone president’s office. They had just become official Cancun Heat Zone wrestlers and already an interview took place.

{{Interview}}

The interview took place at the Sky Dome where the Pay Per-View will be held. The two were strutting down the halls, when stopped by Karl. The interview began immediately after the microphones were placed on their dark blue and dark black dress Shirts.

“ This is Canuck Heat Zone reporter standing here live with the newest sensations of the federation, The Hollywood Assassins! The two have literally just came out of the president’s office and signed their lives away to the Cancun Heat Zone. Now boys are you feared of being in such a viewed federation, knowing all the big time federations are watching?”

The walls of the Sky Dome just recently painted, as the fumes of the hallway are seeping with the paint odor. The two contain themselves by ignoring the smell, as Robbie replies first.

“ Well Karl, being newcomers in a federation is no new thing for us two. We have been yanked around in federations, but we feel this federation will be a great hit to our careers, and get our names out there in the WT federation, seems like a great federation and plenty of talent is shown.”

After Robbie responds he turns around, and takes a long drink out of the water fountain, drying to get the dry paint air out of his mouth. Then Chris takes the spotlight.

“ We have never feared the first impressions, I realize the two guys from Easton High have high hopes, but we plan to begin our triumph of Assassinations with them. We are more then ready, as individuals we are great, but as a team we are unbelievable! We plan to succeed, and nothing else will be excepted in our minds. Our first impressions from the fans will be simply mixed. The guys will see us as pretty boys, but we plan to get their attention after the match, but I am sure we have the women’s attention from the start. But I assure you, they will have ours in return.”

Chris follows Robbie’s example by taking a drink from the water fountain. When this takes place Karl begin feeding the two with the next question. Robbie’s attention is at full, as Chris continues with his drink.
“ Now guys, confidence is great to have, but to much could eventually could hurt you.”

Chris takes a pause from drinking, and glances over at a smirking Robbie, the question obviously very easy to answer for the two, so Robbie takes over.”

“ Karl, you seem like a smart man, so I won’t toy with you. Confidence is key, if you don’t have confidence, most of the match you are thinking about losing. All you need is to think winning! Think winning, believe winning and winning will occur.”

Karl then is stumped, as he fumbles through his sheet of questions he had aligned for the two before the interview. The boys use this time scope the hallway out. The photos of the World Series Championships, the Blue Jays had one in previous series, also wit several closed canteens which are only used in the football and baseball season games. Finally Karl was set after twenty much wasted seconds.

“ What are you plan in Canuck Heat Zone? And if you plan to get promoted some day, which federation do you hope to wrestler in together?”

The two looks at each other, and signal that Chris could take this question. So Robbie goes back to scouting out the ballpark, While Chris answers Karl’s question.

“ Well Karl, that’s a great question. We had just recently discussed this matter on the way to the Sky Dome. We are not fussy, any of the top five federations in the WT will be acceptable. Our hopes will be for OSW or PTC, but like I said any will do. But for now I plan to be here for a long term, hopefully we are heard as Canuck Heat Zone superstars.”

The interview finished with the closing comments and pretty much died from there. It was a short interview, nearly over fifteen minutes is all it took. After the interview the two begin walking down the main hallway and out the front door. The camera stays on them until they reach the outdoors. The two young stars seem to be ready, focused and confident! So only time will tell, if their actions cancel out their words.

{{END TRANSMISSION}}
