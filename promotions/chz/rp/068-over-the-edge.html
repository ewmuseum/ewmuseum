Over The Edge
Written: 07/18/2002

[i]*Okay, okay – I’ll keep it down as Matt doesn’t know I’m down here.

For those of you that have just joined this performance, this is part five of my story “Matt Hunt: From The Bottom To The Top!” – although I’m wishing that I never turned this damn thing on.

Previously, Matt lost his job with the Professional Wrestling Alliance; knocked his wife into the middle of next week and Matt and I sat in his creepy cellar while he got time to himself. So then, I attempted to offer Matt a life line as I had a contact in a wrestling academy – a feeder organisation to the ‘majors’ or whatever. However, Sam – Matt’s wife – had already lined up a prestigious interview with a Mexican company, which offered perks of the job – much to Matt’s chagrin. This obviously started another confrontation (as Sam wanted to go Mexico), in which Matt totally snapped! He swung at Sam yet again and has now tied her up in their cellar!!! I wanted a story and I suppose I’ve got it!

I’m in the cellar now, but I crept in earlier while Matt went out. The surroundings are dark and murky – what else do you expect? Sam is straight ahead of me as she faces the wall, blindfolded and gagged as well as being tied up to the wooden chair, with her arms behind her back. The only sight of light available is a light bulb hanging from the low ceiling, there was a lampshade – but that got knocked off when Matt and Sam were in the struggle.

Even though the bulb is turned off, there’s a glow coming from the small crack as the door up and to my right is slightly ajar. I’ve been down here for a while, basically to see if Sam is okay but I don’t want to approach her as I might startle her and provoke Matt down here. Wait a minute, she’s groaning – she must have ‘woken up’. I’ll go over to see if I can talk to her. I’m slowly walking over to the chair and I can see that her head of golden blonde hair – which has been ruffled up – is tilted forward and her hands are indeed, tied behind her back.

Man, can’t believe that he’s done this! I’m quite close, so instead of just appearing in her face – I softly whisper, to notify her that I’m behind her*

Richard: “Sam!”

[i]*No reply, seems like my whisper was too soft. I repeat my whisper and with some bass in it*

Richard: “Sam!”

[i]*She heard me that time, because her head turned as far as it could go in my direction. I slowly approach the back of the chair and contemplate untying her, but what if Matt was to come down? He’ll have me as well!! So I ignore the shaking hands tied at the wrists with some thin grey string and I pan around cautiously – preparing myself for what state her face will be in*

Richard: “Sam, it’s Richard!”

[i]*She mumbles what I think was my name and I notice that a dark-coloured tie has been wrapped around her mouth – so tight, that it pulls her mouth right back and leaving indentations on the corners of her mouth. The blindfold seems to be a football sock, well the sock is long to reach all the way round a human head – so I presume that’s what it is!.

Her face isn’t as bad as I thought, but it’s messy nonetheless. There’s a huge bruise above her left eyebrow, as well as her cheek being inflamed red. I warily reach out and pull the sock down, so only one eye is exposed. She blinks a few times, to try and adjust her eyesight and focus. It’s obvious that she’s been crying as ran-down mascara is dried up around her eye – which also fills up again.

I hesitate about pulling the tie out from her mouth, but when I pull my hand back – she whimpers, so I have to! I reach inside and pull the sock out, hurting her some more. She coughs a little as she tries to get the feeling back into her lips with those weird mouth movements – can’t remember what they’re called though. I make it clear that we have to keep it quiet, so that Matt doesn’t hear us and I whisper as I talk*

Richard: “Be quiet, Matt doesn’t know I’m down here!”

[i]*Tears run down Sam’s face and my heart melts, I want to help her – but do I want to end up like this or worse? I know it sounds selfish, but come on!! With her eyes full of water, she pleads with me to release her*

Sam: “Richard…please…you gotta get me out of here…please!”

[i]*I pause as my heart and mind have a confrontation - obviously, my heart wanting to set her free.

I’m just about to answer as I hear the door creak open – as if it’s being opened wider! I can hear footsteps slowly coming down the steps and her eye widens in fear! I mumble an apology and attempt to replace the gag back in her mouth – but my hand is shaking and my heartbeat increases!! Thankfully there’s a good few collection of steps leading down here –so maybe that will save me some time.

I’m sweating bucket loads and controlling anything with one while you’ve got something in the other is hard enough anyway. Jesus Christ, my mouth has gone dry and I finally get the tie back into the mouth! My hand is all clammy and I can’t grip the damn sock to re-cover her eye!!! The footsteps are louder and closer!!!

My heartbeat is beating faster that I’ve ever felt before and as I stare at the bottom of the steps in anxious anticipation and fumble around with the sock!! Finally!! I manage to pull it back up and I bolt for the same darkened spot that I emerged from, almost falling over in the process!! Just as I recover my position and try to muffle my heavy breathing, Matt appears with a solemn expression on his face. He slowly approaches Sam – whose back is facing him.

I dread what is coming up next as he strolls towards the chair and immobile body of his beaten wife. When he turns around to face his wife, I can see that he face is white and his brow is wet…very strange. Anyway, due to the door being wide open – light sprays on the two and I can see everything…well, almost everything. Matt crouches down in front Sam who must be aware that he’s in the cellar area – otherwise I wouldn’t have disappeared so quickly.

He cautiously pulls down dark sock from around Sam’s eyes and takes it off completely. As Sam’s back is to me, I cannot see her facial expression so I slowly pan round while watching my step, making sure I don’t draw any attention to myself. I then place myself where I can get a good view of her face, while still in darkness. She hasn’t said anything and that’s probably because she’s silenced of the fear.

Her brown eyes swell up once again and tears come streaming down her cheeks. Matt doesn’t look to be enjoying this either as he just squats there, looking into Sam’s broken face with a sombre expression.

He then reaches up and tugs the tie down from around her mouth, pulling it off her face completely. Sam gulps as Matt then reaches around and unties her hands. She pulls her hands back while grimacing and then rubbing her wrists as Matt talks*

Matt: “I didn’t want to do this, you have to believe that. The last thing I wanted to do was to tie my wife up and leave her in darkness for a couple of hours. I know that my apologies will not make up for this terrorising ordeal, but I deeply and truly am sorry!”

*Matt takes her hand as tears run down his face!! I’ve never seen this side of him, but then again – there’s sides of Matt that I’ve never seen before in the last few hours*

Matt: “You mean everything to me, Sam and you always will. I don’t know what came over me, I just saw red after you…well, you know. I can only promise that I will never…ever, ever…pull this type of stunt again. I will never tie and lock you up as an animal. Although, I don’t blame you for not taking this in – I know I’ve said it before, but I really mean it. I just…”

*Matt breaks down!! He takes his hand away and wipes his brow!!*

Sam: “It’s okay…”

*What? Matt obviously thinks the same thing as he jerks his head up in surprise. I’m shocked myself*

Sam: “I…might have pushed you to do this. I’ve been selfish and…”

Matt (interrupting): “Hey, stop that…”

*Matt takes his wife’s hand again*

Matt: “Now, I’m going…WE’RE going to the US and I’m going to enroll in that wrestling academy…ok?”

*Sam gradually nods her head as Matt smiles*

Mat: “We’ll go to the US and set up a whole new life. Forget England and forget everything that’s happened, including today!”

Sam: “What about Richard? He’s the one who got you in…”

*Me? Why is she bringing me up? Matt pauses, but doesn’t seem to be bothered about it*

Matt: “Rich? Yeah, he can come along if he wants! He’s got that film thing going on so he might as well continue with it!”

*Wow, not what I was expecting. I perhaps thought that he may question why I should come along and the fact that Sam brought my name up at a time like this. Anyway, there’s a slight pause before Matt continues*

Matt: “Everything is going to be ok…I promise!”

*The two then embrace for a good few minutes and Matt closes his eyes. Mmmm, he seems genuine and he hasn’t been like this before, maybe Sam IS pushing him to do stupid things like this…I honestly haven’t got a clue! This might be a good time to get the hell out of here…*
