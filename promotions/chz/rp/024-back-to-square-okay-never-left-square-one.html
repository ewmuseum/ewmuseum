Back To Square.. Okay, Never Left. Square One
Written: 6/17/02

Vanilla Skillz busted into his training facility, a huge frown on his face, as he plodded up to his former female head trainer. The lovely Nubian, Maxine Larson. She blinked, as she saw him stomp in, and cocked her head to the side.

'Something wrong Mark?' She asked, rather surprised that a man with Vanilla Skillz usual cheerful demeanor, being so, well.. angry.

'Yo, my name is Vanilla Skillz.' He snapped, as he turned to her. Unfortunately, it was too late to stop, after he had realized who he had snapped at.

'No, your name is what I call you little man.' She growled, and immediately locked him into a side headlock, and floated him over with a headlock takedown, causing The Skilled One, to crash down, and swing his limps around wildly.

'I'M SORRY! I'M SORRY!' He screeched, then, started to smile.

'And just what are you smiling about?' She blinked, then the realization came to her, 'YOU PREV!'

Maxine let him go, and laughed, as Mark just laid there, and started frowning once more.

'What's up there, Ma..' He gave her an ugly look, and she sighed, 'Skillz? You seem rather down.'

'Yo, I just joined up with a fed..' He started.

'YOU?!' Maxine's eyes were wide with disbelief.

'What's that supposed ta mean, yo? You dissin' my incredible skillz?!'

'No, no.. go on.' Maxine waved him off, not wanting to get into that discussion again. Mark just went on, after clearing his throat and adjusting his New York Yankee's hat.

'Yeah, I joined CHZ, and everyone's all..' Skillz cleared his throat, and tried to mimic Cassius Deamont's voice, 'Hey.. look at that dude, he ain't worthy to be here, yo?' Obviously, his mimicry was, horrible, though Skillz didn't know that.

'Well, I must say ... with your unusual.. life style? Many people wouldn't take you seriously. I mean, doesn't Mark VanWinkle do rock now?'

'You.. NEVER say that about Ice!! He's still the bomb. Word.' Skillz growled.

'Okay.. okay, so what do you plan on doing to have CHZ take you seriously..' She sighed, 'Skillz?'

'I'm gonna train like a bitch. It's why I cam back here, yo?' Skillz nodded, and gave her a wide grin, and a wink, 'And for you, my Sista..'

'You never left here..' Another sigh, 'Skillz. You're still in training remember?'

Vanilla Skillz cocked his head to the side, trying to recall, all of his anger at Cassius Deamont, for underestimating him, had caused him to forget practically everything.

'She-iiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiit son, you're right!' He laughed, and bounced his head up and down in a nodding, as he stepped off to the side, and crossed his arms over his chest.

'All right, so you're back here to train for your first match in CHZ, any idea on where you wish to start?' She inquired, looking to Skillz, and then sighed, as she saw the complete blank look on his face. This was going to be a long few weeks for her. A very, very.. VERY long few weeks.
