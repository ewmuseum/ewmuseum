Diary of a tortured soul: Pt1:Vengeful Salvation
Written: 24/7/02

Today as I write this extract with my blood dripping against the crisp white paper I can feel a change occurring. They got me again today, and bust my head open with their clenched fists. What could have provoked them? What did I do?! I have been singled out and targeted. I have become the object of their punishment and amusement of the 'popular' people because of my 'freakish' looks. I have been outcasted even further by the society that inhabits the building at which I attend to obtain an education. This place however is slowly no longer becoming a place of new age education and learning, but a structure of anguish and torment.

*The vocals of Philip Anselmo of Pantera rang through his ears that day as he walked away from school. He wore a pair of blue baggy jeans and a black Metallica T-shirt. His hair was long and black, and seeing that on a male was just unheard of in this area*

* She was what could be described today as 'shagadelic'. She was the typical female teen dream, long blonde hair, breasts way too big and firm for her age. Yet sometimes Axl suspected she did use tissues for the extra added eye catching cleavage. Her boyfriend was the captain of the collage football team... blah blah blah you work it out, you know the story and you don't need me to tell you again. She was hot, popular, and out of his reach*

*He had his wars with Ritchie Anderson in high school, but now collage was a whole new deal. He had actually made some pretty solid and cool friends, along with some new enemies. Axl seemed mysterious and intriguing to some of the more popular females, and he wasn't exactly un-homely in looks, and he was a pretty attractive guy, although his looks didn't concern him. Yet they did with the ladies. He was the tall dark stranger who got their attention. Yes, someone who isn't popular was getting the attention of popular females, and it was generally a positive response. Yet he usually ignored them. However, ignoring her attention was like ignoring the jabbing stitch in your side when running a marathon, near impossible.*

'Hi Axl'

*She looked great today, and he smiled a little this time. Just a small 'hello, even though I'm not as popular as you I'd still have wild passionate sex with you' smile. Yet he didn't want to smile too much, wherever she was Eddy was usually within a 10 foot range, and if Axl was near her, that meant he was within the 10 foot range of Eddy, and that 10 foot was dangerous, for Axl*

'Hi Kelly'

*Today he felt in a surprisingly good mood, and decided to say hello to her as well. She smiled, almost a sly smile, but a smile all the same back at him.*

'There's my girl'

*Oh shit! Eddy was here. Maybe if he just walked a little faster he could....*

'See ya Axl baby'

*He then heard Kelly's lips clench and unclench making a soft wet sound as she blew him a kiss. What the hell was she doing!!*

'What are you talking to that freak for?!'

'Hey, he was looking me up and down really.....sexily'

'YOU LYING BITCH!'

*He shouted the words as he spun around to meet the voices he was hearing. if only he had thought about what the repercussions of his comment would have caused, but she was stirring shit so that Eddy would get enraged. Now however, Eddy was more enraged and was ready to give Axl, what Eddy liked to call 'A free pass to Eddy's house of pain'*

*Fist after fist, punch after punch, chant after chant, bruise after bruise. Make it fucking stop! For the love of God stop! Again and again, torment, torture, and pain oh the pain, the on going unrelenting pain! She caused it, that bitch! Now he was getting pulverised, beaten to a pulp. The crowd of collage students accumulating around him as his head was bashed into the white emulsion painted wall. Then, mercifully, the end came. It cased, the assault stopped. Yet the pain stil remained. They dispersed silently away from the scene of their vile crime. Leaving carnage and anarchy behind them. Three of them attacked him in the end. Sickening was the only word Axl could muster to describe them.*

'Axl, are you Ok...oh shit man. They beat the shit out of you good!'

*Randy, one of Axl's collage friends, was someone who Axl knew he could get an honest opinion from. However now was the not the time he needed them from him.*

'Shit man, you looked like something out of a Wes Craven film'

*Axl got to his knees and then to his feet. The bashing his head took against the wall seemed to cause the most damage, a nasty laceration across his forehead that was causing blood to spill down his face elegantly.*

'Thanks Randy '

'Dude, I got a baseball bat in the dorm. I say we go find.....'

*Randy's idea of violence suddenly seemed appealing to Axl, and he listened eagerly.'

'.... their cars and bash the shit out of them, windscreens , the full fucking works. But we'll have to go at night, if they catch us, woooo, boy, we'd get an ass kicking ten times worse then the one you just got, and the one you just got was mad bad!'

*Randy's idea seemed less appealing now, and his phrase of mad bad didn't seem so cool and funny. Yet as ........'

...My blood swirled around and blended with the saliva of my mouth a mournful vengeful untouchable emotion circulated throughout my body. This wasn't the bitter sweet taste of anger, and it wasn't the longing blemish of fury. It was........retribution. As I stood there still getting mocked for my unorthodox dress code I embraced the oh so sweet salvation of retribution.
The wound on my head is throbbing still though, it seems to hurt even more just to think about it. I fear for tomorrow. I fear that I’m going to snap and make a fucking fool of myself. What the fuck am I supposed to do!! DAMMIT! I DIDN’T ASK FOR THIS! FUCK YOU EDDY, FUCK YOUR WORLD! I hope this is what you wanted, God damn you, you worthless son of a bitch !I will get you , you sack of shit! DIE! Just die! Him and his worthless whore, she caused this, that bitch, for all I care they can both die! screw them both, screw them both to hell!

He placed the journal down slowly on his lap. He swallowed slowly, and took a deep breath. He was glad he found this after all these years, but he seemed afraid to turn and read the next entry in his journal of anguish. Yet slowly he did, he wanted to be reminded of the all the suffering and sorrow he endured. His face was filled with tears as he wrote that page, he remembered that. Oh how he feared that next day, that night it was like the eve of Armageddon.

It was time to go back in time again, to the time of vengeful salvation.
