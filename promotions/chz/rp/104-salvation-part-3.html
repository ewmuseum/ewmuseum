Salvation: Part 3
Written: 7/29/02

There I was... sitting in a closet with my case. Christina was still shaking, but she had more or less regained her composure after the experience with the demon, and that was comforting in itself. Mere minutes before, her eyes had literally changed color and the picture on her back had become animated. It was as if I were trapped in a horror movie and unable to escape, slowly suffocating on some deranged director's thoughts and plans. A part of me even wished for that all, because then... well, then all of this wouldn't be real.

No demons who have the face of evil; no unexplained cell phone calls from my superior just as the line goes dead; no images of beings of light decapitating demons with oversized swords... that would have made my day. Of course, this wasn't my day, this was a bad day, and it was one I wouldn't escape from.

The doors and windows were locked by a strange force, and I couldn't even think of escape let alone get the hell out. It was dark outside now, completely dark, and strangely enough, it almost seemed like the darkness was slowly seeping into the home. I noticed that the colors of the vibrant room had all but faded and the smiling faces on the pictures... they all had looks of hatred on them now. Worse than that, I could feel beings in the house with us, as if they were always there, but at the same time, out of reach. Sets of eyes sent chills down my spine, and the odd whisper that was sent coursing through my mind even made me shake as much as Christina was. I had never experienced this kind of fear, nor had I ever been in any kinds of situations such as this. When hearing the history of Joan of Arc, this wasn't my idea of what had happened to her.

This case was different though, and I could feel that the key was the picture of 'The War' on Christina's back. She refused to let me examine it now, perhaps afraid that it'd unlock another demon or cause her to go crazy again. In truth, I felt for her... but if the answers were there, I'd have to get them one way or another.

'You okay?' I asked her in a reassuring voice... unsure if it was for me or her. She just briefly nodded in reply. 'I know this is strange for you and I know you're not used to this... but hey, this is my job right? I'll take care of it Blitz style, just watch.

The grin she gave me was a nice change, but it was the sign that she saw right through me none the less. 'Thanks Matt... but I'm crazy, not stupid eh?'

I shook my head a little, recognizing that my ploy had been shot down. 'Well... we'll get through this. I mean, I have a match on Wednesday right? I can't miss that after all.'

I took the awkward pause the survey the situation. With no means of escape and even less means of contacting the outside world, it would be best to figure out what was going on. I decided to do just that, but not before saying a prayer. I knew God was with me, and I prayed to him for everything. Protection, strength and most of all, I prayed for Christina.

'A'ight, look. I'm going to go down stairs and see what's going on. I'll be right back,' I told her as I got up to leave, but like a viper, her hand shot out and grabbed the sleeve of my leather cloak. I looked back at her and took note of her pleading eyes.

'No... don't leave me here alone!' she urgently hissed. I wanted to stay with her, you know, tell her everything was going to work out fine... but I knew I had to go. So I did something perhaps even harder than staying with her... I turned my back to her and wrenched my arm from her grasp. Instantly, she curled up in the closet again and shut the door, whimpering from behind the wood as I slowly crept through the door of her room.

I was hit by a cold breeze that made my hair sway into my eyes. I took a moment to get it out of my face and made my way down the stairs, checking over my shoulder and around every dark corner as I did so. Sure enough, nothing in the home seemed real anymore, more like a dream, and I was desperately hoping to wake up any second. Course, I never did, and instead, stopped just outside the living room where I saw three of the creatures from before sitting, looking the same as the first, including the lack of emotions.

My heart leapt into my throat as I staggered back and pressed myself against the wall, hoping that they didn't see me. I knew better, they were looking right at me when I made my way down the stairs, and in the reflection on the mirror on the wall... I could now see the three of them smiling and bearing their fangs, representing the evil that had taken control of me and my companion Christina. Like the first time, I felt a chill run down my spine, and it looked as if they were staring at me, even though I was only seeing their reflections.

I reached inside my cloak and pulled out my golden lined dagger, and like before, I dropped it. I didn't bother to try and pick it up, and soon enough, it slid down the stairs and came to a rest in front of the closer demon. I again reached into my cloak and puled out a small hand crossbow, and loaded it with a silver arrow with The Disciple logo engraved on the head. I felt dizzy now, like I was going to throw up, and I knew the cause of the feeling was inside the living room. Without a moment of hesitation, I whipped around the wall and fired my shot, and it hit. It hit the wall, and the room... it was completely empty.

'Damn it,' I cursed to myself in a whisper as I darted down the stairs and frantically looked around the room. In truth, I was glad that I didn't find anything. Admittedly, I would have died for an extreme match right about then, even if it were against some of the most sadistic men in the sport. At least then I would know what was going on. In this home, with the unexplained events, I was just a lost soul in the dark.

'Lost in the maze, I can't find my own face; Lost for days, my soul's kept in the wrong place; Lost is life, yo, what's wrong is right; I've been lost in the night with the light to guide my sight,' I hummed to myself, a song called Lost Soul, by a rapper from Alberta named King Dylan. My entrance music comforted me a little and kept my head in reality, even if I was knee deep in Hell's feces.

I barged into the kitchen with my crossbow leading the way and found one of the emotionless demons sitting on the counter. I held my bow up and aimed, but it only smiled coldly at me, causing me to lower my weapon. I could already tell that it was futile.

'Disciple, Mr. Blitz, how do you fair this eve?' I heard in my head, guessing it came from the demon.

'You know,' I began, 'killing demons, helping people and making the world a better place. I'll probably sneak wrestling in there somewhere as well, eh?' I could tell my voice was a little shaken, but the words came out right.

The demon's smile grew wider and more sinister looking. 'Yes, we've been watching you for some time Matt. You've been a very good boy, ridding the world of my kind, sending us back to our domain. We are impressed with you Matt, very impressed. Yet, you work for God. Why is that so Matt?'

Its voice in my head sounded like the rustling of dead leaves on a cool wind, scratching against the forest floor. It made me uneasy, knowing that a demon was inside my head. I had seen my fair share of possessions to know that I'd never want to be so myself.

'God is the almighty demon,' I stated, making sure my voice sounded strong, 'and there are none above him. Who else to serve but he who is righteous?'

'Matt, what has God done for you? Yes, you are what you are today because of him, but does this look like eternal peace? When you lost your parents, who do you think played his hand in that? Has God helped your brother in his time of need, and in truth, is he helping you now?'

The questions, notably, shook my foundation and I lowered my eyes a little. They made sense, too much sense, and I could feel my faith seeping out of me as if I were some squeezed orange. I felt like I was about to fall from the grace I desperately needed, but like I always did when my faith faltered, I ran my hand over the bible that was attached to my belt and returned my gaze to the demon.

'God didn't take my parents from me demon, Satan did, through those that he had influence over. My brother... you cannot save someone who doesn't want to be saved demon, and that is my brother. He has damned himself and doesn't wish to change his path. And perhaps this is not paradise, and perhaps this a hard experience on me... but God tests us all demon. I am not in Hell, yet you are. Which of us has failed The Father's tests?'

The demon didn't stop smiling, but instead, faded from view as a black cloud enveloped the kitchen, driving me out. I decided it was best to get back to the room, hence, I staggered up the stairs, away from any demons, and reached for the handle of Christina's door. As if on cue, the phone on the floor began to ring, the very phone that hadn't been working since X hung up on me.

I picked the cell up and answered. 'Yes?'

'Who's this?' a familiar voice on the other end demanded.

'Matt,' I bluntly stated as I peered through the crack under the door. No demons.

'Geez kid!' X exclaimed from the other end, 'where have you been? I haven't been able to get a hold of you for three weeks!'

'What!?' I asked frantically, blinking at the news. 'It couldn't have been three weeks!'

'Kid, I don't know what's up with your current case, but The Disciples are planning to take her by tomorrow. They already have a new case lined up for you.'

'Whoa, hey... they can't do that. This is a little deeper than Confessors and Disciples man.'

X paused for a few seconds, which as always, made me feel uneasy. 'You'll want to take this case Matt. We had a psychic down at a wrestling event who detected a demonic presence, and she says it came right from someone you know.'

I hesitated as one thought coursed through my mind, eating away it it as it did so. 'Who?'

'You're brother, Spectre.'
