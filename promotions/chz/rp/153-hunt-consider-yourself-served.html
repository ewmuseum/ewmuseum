Hunt- consider yourself served
Written: 20/8/02

It looks corporate. But
looks can be deceiving, but in this case they're spot on. Persona Variable walked into the offices of Belloc and Co. offices in a very defiant manner. His shirt blustering in the false air inserted into the building in a futile attempt to keep it cool on this hot summers day. Persona's face was one of utter determination and looked as if it could've been carved out of granite. It would need to be...he was here to see a lawyer. In PV's mind, lawyers are a race of their own. They have the ability to talk their way out of the most precarious of situations, but equally talk themselves into one hell of alot of trouble as well. But Persona
Perfect Dark was determined, he wasn't about to get entangled into some posh talking lawyers sticky web, no, he was going in their for one reason and one reason only. To get a straight up, honest opinion on what he thought he already knew, whether or not he could sue Matt Hunt.


 


The lift arrived
with a ping, and immediately Persona Perfect Dark pressed the button that represented the 5th floor. Upon his arrival PV looked out for a door with the name Peter
Joyce plastered on it, for this is the person who the receptionist at the front desk had told him to see. It only took PV a few minutes to find it, and before long he was being invited in with by a small man in a sharp suit. Immediately the first thing that hit PV was just how spacious the room was compared to the rest of the floor, and why such a little man was in need of such a large room.


 


Peter: Please
sit (Peter Joyce at this point gestured for PV to sit in a rather dull looking chair)


PV: Delighted
to (this was said in a very sarcastic manner)


Peter: I understand
you want to talk to me about suing a fellow worker, a fellow wrestler to be exact?


PV: Yes, his
name is Hunt, Matt Hunt, he's a cocky little shit and he totaled my car


Peter: Can
you tell me exactly how it happened Mr. Malkovich


PV: Well, it
all started when...


 


And so it began, the retelling
of the scenario that led to Lexit Malkovich's car, a.k.a Persona Variable's car being destroyed. With every word that leaked out of PV's mouth, he began more angry and worked up at just how stupid Hunt had been, and just how gladly he'd hurt Hunt were it'd hurt most, his wallet


 


PV: ...So,
do you think I have a case?


Peter: Absolutely,
either way, this is a sure thing for you. If what you say is true, and I will look at the tape of Wild that you have just given me to be sure, than Hunt has no chance in hell of winning. I mean, that was unprovoked vandalism of your car. Hunt can easily be done with criminal damages if you charge him.


PV: Really...sure
thing...criminal damages (PV's face lights up like a light bold as a huge smirk derives across his face)


 


Persona variable left soon
after, requesting to speak with a representative of the CHZ camera crew, who were more than willing to speak with the normally mundane speaker.


As soon as the camera crew
showed up, PV wanted to get straight into it without any of the normal small talk that proceeds.


PV: We live?


Cameraman:
Yep


PV: Good, Hunt,
you may of thought that with as Wild went off the air, that I would just write of what you did to my car as an accident, a misunderstanding, but NO!NO!NO!
Hunt, I don't work like that. And listening to your little 'Park' chit chat,
I realised your even more of an asshole than I am, and believe me, I can be a real asshole. I don't just switch from Persona to Persona, hell, this is the first time I, Persona Perfect Dark, has surfaced in years. And do you know why? Because all the others Persona's are too scared of what
I might do to others in this big bad world. And yah know what? They're damn right. So far in both my CHZ matches I have succeeded in my prior goal, to make people bleed. In my first match it was Jman, in my second it was Schultz, and tomorrow on Wild, before I make you tap out, it will be you my friend. Hell,
I couldn't give a shit about the effing title, all I care about is making you suffer Hunt, and if that means it'll be my name, how did you put it 'below the main event slot on the DVD' then so be it. I have no remorse Hunt, I have no feelings, hell what did feelings ever do for anybody, all I care about is number one...ME! And if that means I have to be as cold as ice to get the job done and look out for myself, then so be it. 'Cos that's what it takes to succeed
Hunt, to be cold, and as evil as you think you are, as evil as you think you can possibly get, that doesn't even come close to what I'm capable of... Have
I made myself clear Hunt? I think I have...crystal.
