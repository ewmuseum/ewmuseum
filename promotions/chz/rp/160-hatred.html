Hatred
Written: 8/23/02

He sat there, just staring. He couldn't move his eyes from the light above him, and although his nose length red hair was completely in his face, he didn't so much as flinch to remove it. Instead, he scratched his finger nails against the cloth of the hospital bed and glared with emotionless, silver eyes at the lights.

The constant beeping of the monitors surrounding 'The Disciple' were beginning to get under his skin, though he didn't show it. His expression was one of anger, with his lips curled into a cold snarl, but his eyes remained expressionless, as if he had died looking the way he did. However, according to the heart monitor beside him... his organs were functioning fine.

Matt was laying in a hospital bed, under some white sheets, sitting in a stereotypical hospital room. He was alone, as his injuries were deemed to take special attention. In truth, the stitches and mild concussion presented nothing more serious than a checkup now and then, but where as the CHZ did not attribute too much money to his care, The Disciples did, and Matt was perhaps best described as a first class hospital patient.

Past the angered look, Matt more or less looked the same. His red hair hung in his face in tangled strands with some dried blood still stuck in clumps of hair. His face was dark, perhaps more so with his expression, and his eyes were modified silver by the group he worked for. Yet, underneath all the darkness and the punk looking features was a man who stuck to his faith and devoted his life to God, even the more violent aspects of it, such as wrestling. Yet, after a literal Wild night, perhaps some of the holy armor had been removed.

Slowly, the door to the room swung open on its clean hinges, not even making a slight sound as an old man stepped in. The man was wearing a leather jacket, jeans and shades, with his gray hair slicked back. Underneath the jacket was concealed countless weapons of his faith, and the man was known to Matt only as 'X'. He was Matt's only known superior.

X slowly walked forward and carefully circled around the bed. He took a moment to flatten out his jeans and gently sat in the chair beside the bed, examining Matt as he did so. Matt didn't even acknowledge that he was in the room, but only kept glaring at the lights.

X sighed and leaned forward so he could speak more quietly. 'You alright kid?' he asked in a soft tone, showing some genuine concern for his best field man.

Matt continued glaring at the lights, and didn't so much as flinch as the question was posed to him.

'You'll hurt your eyes doin' that kid,' X nervously joked. Matt had never known X to be nervous in the months he had worked for him, and although it struck him as odd, he still refused to acknowledge his superior. 'Look kid, I watched Wild. Tough break, really tough... but that happens. You've got to let that go. I can see the anger just by looking at you sport, and I don't like it one bit. Your beliefs, your life and your god are all based on anything but anger. I know that you're feeling it all kid, the hatred, the craving for revenge... I've been there. All you can do is let it go and think about what you got going for you man. I mean, after your battle with Nerezza last week, we've had no problems. No possessions, no hauntings, no demons... a clean month I'd suspect. You can focus on wrestling, and just wrestling. I'll keep the higher ups on your back... just relax and win some matches.'

Matt knew deep down that X was right. He knew somewhere in his confused and enraged thoughts that his superior spoke the truth. He did have matches to win, and perhaps the most important of his professional career coming up against Persona Variable on Wednesday. However, he could not so much as move. He didn't want to move, as if he were afraid what he'd do if he did. He could feel pure anger... pure hatred running through him. He pondered his dark feelings, and could find no answers. The thought of what happened on Wild ate at him like some sadistic plague. Summers turned her back to him, he was put through hell with Ryan Contra, and he was embarrassed in front of the world by an ass kicking from Young. He wanted to strike out, like a viper, and hurt anyone. He just didn't want it to be the wrong person.

'Well,' X trailed off, shaking his head in his failure to reach Matt, 'I have to get back to work. Take care Matt, and take in my words. We're with you kid, all you've got to do is see that, and realize that God is with you as well, and always will be.'

With one last look for any sign of a reaction, X groggily stood to his feet, stretched, and slowly made his way from whence he came. He stopped at the door, for just a second, opening his mouth to say something to Matt... but nothing came out. Instead, he opened the door and disappeared around the corner.

When Matt was sure X was gone, he closed his eyes and shook his head to himself. He slowly hoisted himself up on his bed and sat upright, shifting his angered gaze to the end of the bed as he did so. He could still feel the rage and hatred running through his veins, increasing with each beating of his heart. His mind was filled with nothing but anger towards his enemies, and he wanted to hurt them. He wanted to hurt Axl Young; he wanted to hurt Ryan Contra... he even wanted to hurt Angel Summers. The thoughts burned underneath his eyes, and he couldn't help but wonder if his brother whom he despised so much lived his life like this every day.

Almost on cue, the wound on his head reopened and a thick stream of blood slowly crept down his forehead. Matt felt it, and pulled the monitoring tools off his chest. He ripped off the sticking cords and turned off the constant humming of the heart monitor as it flat-lined. Matt threw the blanket off of him and stepped onto the cold floor, still wearing his bloodied wrestling attire from the night before.

He walked to the window and stuck his head out into the darkness of the Vancouver night, allowing the wind to blow the hair from his face, revealing the stream of blood that was now dripping off the end of his nose. In a sadistic thought amongst many, he moved his finger to his nose and ran it up to his forehead until his finger was coated with blood. He drew his hand away from him and held the crimson sight in front of him, and slowly, ran his tongue over the blood and savored the taste.

'I have a plane to catch,' he snarled under his breath as he slowly moved from the window and stepped back to admire the night sight. With one last longing look, he turned around and stepped through the doors to his room and disappeared down the hallway.
