The Big Game.Part 2
Written: 7/31/02

------Continued----------

{{{BEGIN FEED}}}

The sun is beating down hard now. The traffic is still heavy but moving along quite well. Angel and Robyn both have now entered the parking lot, which should be another ten minutes as the traffic is heavy in the eight floor dark lot. They go up and up, looking at the Blue Jay fans with there foamed fingers, they dyed blue hair, and Baseball caps and other artifacts sold nation wide. Finally on the seventh floor of the parking lot, they finally found an opening spot so they quickly claim it by speeding into it quickly. They climb out, as it is a tight fit due to a big van taking up 1 ½ parking spaces. They stretch for a bit, and begin walking towards the exit of the parking lot. They are starred at by all passing people, Robyn says its because of they beauty, but Angel believes its their cowgirl hats, but both are reasons to look.

They finally make their way out of the parking lot and directly into the stadium. They wait in a long line of fans, where the tickets are sold. About 15 minutes later they are at the front of the line and begin ordering their tickets. Considering its Angel’s treat she begins to order.

“ Hello, We will take the best available seats if possible, price is no concern.”

Robyn seems impressed in Angel, as she is finally living the big life, she is usually tight with her money, which is why she was able to buy her own bar back home, she always saved and finally she had enough for one of her dreams. She started off small with paper routs in the city, then moved on to winning money in the rodeo contests, and finally a local wrestler and sidekick in the HWF.

“ Well miss, we don’t have any available seats within reason. It is hard to get good tickets with only 10 minutes before game time, all we have in third deck in the middle row. And I know it is the highest deck but you can still experience the game from way up there, and it does bring you closer to god if that helps/”

The women obviously trying to make a sale, telling bad jokes and all. So she just quickly receives the tickets and pays the $30.00 dollars for the pair of tickets and begins making their way to their seats. First they stop for a hotdog and soda, in natural tradition of the game, and it was dinnertime. So with their hands full they steadily make their way to their seats with caution, finally they reach row R, deck 3. They settle in and look at the field, the view was crap, as all the players looked like small bugs. But the game could still be viewed within reason.

The two were enjoying themselves, never being to a professional game makes it ten times more fun. Still being starred at by close fans because of their cowgirl hats but the girls have begun to get used to it and continue on with watching the game. By the 5th inning the game was tied 3-3. Nothing really big happened like a home run, but plenty on singles and doubles were being met, this not really being a pitchers dual so much as a hitting dual.

By the time of the 7th inning stretch, the girls began talking while they awaited the game to re-start. Angel asking most of the questions, like what’s happening with the bar, what is everyone up to, and so on. Then finally Robyn asks her a few questions about her match again.

“ So how do you feel fighting three males in a four way dance?”

“ Wont be any different the HWF, just maybe a little more skill. Nothing I cannot handle!”

“ Well I have read some news reports from the CHZ. The bids are not high for you to win your match. It says a lot of votes for Matt Blitz and Ryan Contra. How do you feel about that.”

“ Well of course the voters will not vote for a woman in a match, especially when that woman squares off against four other men, but I will show them what I am about, just wait and see!”

The rest of the game was intense, with a few Toronto Blue Jays home runs, the cleaned the game up with a whopping 10-4 ball game. The fans went crazy because of the win, as it was a loud walk to their car with the screaming fans swarming the parking lot. The rest of the day was quite lazy for Robyn, as she settled into her new place, which she shared with Angel. And Angel hit the gym nearby to be prepared for her match at Wild. The odds are against her, but that only gives her more reason to fight harder to achieve stardom.
