Rest & a Hot Girl
Written: 6-18-02

*The cameras open to Don Rossi walking from a building, to a car. He’s in his normal attire but looks very tired, & weak almost. He carries his bag and throws it in the back seat next to him. He puts his head back and yells to the driver “Go to the Radisson Hotel. Its downtown if you don’t know exactly where it is.” Don puts his head back again and then rests his eyes as he has had a very long day, doing signings and things. He slips off to sleep and a few minutes later he hears the man yelling at him.*

Driver: Sir we are here at the hotel. You must have had a long day I been yelling at you for 20 minutes. Do you need any help with your bag?

Don~ *Still half drowsy* No I am fine thank you. Sorry for wasting so much of your time. I didn’t even feel myself get that tired. Well I will let you be on your way come by tomorrow and I will have you drive me to my next appearance. I like you. Keep up the good work.

*Don reaches over and grabs his bag and slides out of the back seat of the taxi. He closes the door and waves to the driver. He turns and walks back to the Hotel entrance and as he gets in the staff give him a message to call his wife. Don throws an arm into the air and continues on the way to the elevator. He gets inside and sees a young lady inside. She gives him a smile and he returns it. She chuckles a little as he hit’s the button he looks down to see what she is laughing about and he notices his shirt is un buttoned and his jacket is half off. He shakes his arm to fix the jacket but doesn’t worry about the shirt. She begins to talk.*

Lady: Must have been a long day. What do you do?

Don~ I am a professional wrestler and today I had a quest appearance at some hall to do. I didn’t want to be there at all and I think they saw it too. I don’t know I got in the taxi and I passed out I was so tired I couldn’t keep my eyes open anymore. I didn’t catch your name.

Lady: My name is Jessica. I take it your not used to coming here huh?

Don~ You kidding I used to come here often but the company shut down. I am with the CHZ. We are based not to far from here. I am not used to going to those meetings that’s all. I forgot how tiring they were and how boring. *the two laugh.* Well this is my floor I hope to see you tomorrow maybe.

Jessica: If you don’t mind maybe I could come by a little later? I know your tired and all. Maybe I could come by give you a massage and we could talk more of your profession? I don’t know we could talk about anything.

Don~ You do know I am a married man right? I don’t plan on cheating so whatever we do there will be no ass grabbing or anything like that. Just two people talking over a massage.

Jessica: *Smiling* Dang no ass grabbing? I can live with that. Lets go. I don’t care if your married I only wanna talk. This is my floor I will go to my room get changed into some looser cloths and I will be over in about 30 minutes ok?

Don~ Alright because I have to call my wife & take a shower. *Don without making it apparent looks her over and shakes his head some.* See you then.

He stretches and then pulls the blinds closed. Then he opens them again and walks over to the dresser and pulls out some shorts and a white t-shirt to wear, along with some boxers and socks. He goes into the bathroom and the camera hears the water running and after a few minutes it stops and Don walks out of the bathroom with his clothes on. He flops down on the bed and picks up the phone. He dials a number and talks.*

Don~ Hey Kelly how’s it going? Sorry its so late I know I told you I would call. I got stuck at some quest appearance and I couldn’t get out of it. It was crazy I was so tired from it that I fell asleep in the taxi on the way back here.

Kelly: So how is the hotel? I hope they are treating you right.

Don~ Yeah they are treating me great. They have me in some huge hotel its about $300 a night which isn’t bad but its very high class for being so cheap. They have everything here. Hot tubs in the rooms, golf course outside, pools in doors and out, I have requested room service a few times before in the hotels and finally I get it. El Diablo went above and beyond anything Petapaw ever did for us wrestlers. *There’s a knock at the door and Don smiles.* They even have masseuses here.

Kelly: Well who’s spoiled? Well since your getting it there then I am gonna go out tomorrow and treat myself. Maybe to a massage and dinner I don’t know. Maybe even go shopping.

Don~ Who you trying to kid if you go shopping you will just buy stuff we need for the house so stop trying to kid someone. You wanna go out then go ahead just do something you want to do and not shit for the house or me. But I gotta go Kelly I will call you tomorrow sometime. Love you.

Kelly: Love you too. Good Night sweetie.

*Don hangs up the phone and walks over to the door where a woman stands in her pajamas. Its some shorts and a t-shirt. Don does a double take and steps aside to let her in. She walks in and smirks. He walks over and throws his bag on the floor next to the bed. She sits down in the recliner and looks at him with her legs crossed. Don walks over to the edge of the bed and sits down.*

Jessica: So how’s the wife? Good I hope. Do you have any kids?

Don~ Nope just me and her back home in the huge house. That’s why I feel bad when I have to leave her.

Jessica: Why for all you know she is doing the same thing you are right now. There’s no way for you to tell who’s there in your bed or where she’s going to sleep tonight. What are you thinking Don? Whats going on in your head? Lets face it Don you want me. You love everything about me Don. You love that my legs are shaped just right. You love that my breasts are the perfect size. You love that when I walk my ass doesn’t shake all over the place. *getting closer to Don they are nearly face to face.* Give in Don. No one will find out.

Don~ I thought you came here with the same concept I invited you here for. That was to talk about the business and give a massage.

Jessica: Answer me Don. You ant this body tangled with yours. You want my legs wrapped around your body, you want my nails digging into your back. I came here to massage something but it wasn’t your back.

Don~ Oh. I will give you this much you are cute as hell but I cannot and will not cheat on Kelly. Now come over here to the bed and lay down I will massage you while we talk of the business I love more then anything in the world.

*Jessica gives Don an odd look and walks over to the bed and lays down with her back towards the ceiling. Don looks at her as she moves her hair and shakes his head. He kneels beside her and begins to rub her back and neck.*

Jessica: That feels so good. I can tell you do a lot with your hands. You must work out and train a lot. So what are you supposed to do this week?

Don~ Well this week I am teamed up with two other guys one I have worked with before and the other I have never heard of before. Though they are both very impressive I have to worry about their focus to the match. You see one named Matthews he is also in Rush and he was competing for the World Title there so I am worried he is not gonna be focused for our match. Then the Ice Cream Man Pat Dwyer he is off doing his own thing humping a damn ice cream cone. Who knows what the hell he’s thinking?

Jessica: Sounds to me your in more trouble then I am. So if those guys are your partners what do you have to go against? The odds don’t seem to favor you at all.

Don~ Nope they never do. But I am facing a rather easy hill to climb I think. There is this Mexican I am supposed to be facing, he is quick but that’s about all he is. I have seen some tape on him he’s not bad but he doesn’t have what it takes to defeat anyone on my team. I hope. Then a man I look forward to fighting, Luke Black. He seems to be the most impressive of them all. He is talented, well rounded. I worry of his threat the most. Then Davis Fury. I have seen this man wrestle and when hes on he is tough to stop but when he hit’s a rut in the road he is easily beatable.

Jessica: So who wins? It is predetermine inst It?

Don~ Don’t believe all you hear about the sport. The only thing that is predetermined is who we are gonna be fighting. The matches are real, the pain is real, the hate is real, we are performers but we are athletes as well. Yeah we get hurt and bruised and everything else but what it comes down to is who is the better man. Plain and simple.

*Jessica rolls over on her side. Now looking at Don.*

Jessica: Your turn get down. *Don lays down and takes off his shirt and Jessica is shocked and turned on by all the muscles in his back by the way he is laying.* Your very muscular, how much do you train?

Don~ Well I work out for 3-4 hours a day. Then I run in the mornings as well. Keeps me in shape and able to do this.
