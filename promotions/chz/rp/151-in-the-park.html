In the park
Written: 18th August 2002

*The shot slowly fades in and the beautiful scene of a hot Spring’s day comes into view. There blue skies with white puffy clouds fill the air and leaves that hang off the tall trees rustle in the soft cool breeze.

As the camera pans around, there are hundreds of large oak trees which sit in the glorious sunshine and as the camera pans down, there are acres and acres of neatly cut grass. The area is quiet as a dozen or so wooden benches are spontaneously scattered around the park, to which some eldery people can be seen relaxing in the sun. A few people can be seen walking the paths – children running around excitedly, while their parents walk the family dog, who also runs around like a headless chicken.

As the sun beats down on this sleepy Edmonton park area, the shot pans around again to see an individual in the distance. He’s wearing a black baseball cap with a small white tick on the front, a white polo type shirt – again with a tick emblazoned on the right breast, black shorts and white trainers. As the person sits back with his legs outstretched and relaxes in the heat, the camera slowly moves in and after a few moments, the identity of the individual is revealed as Matt Hunt who is also wearing a pair of sunglasses.

On the bench next to him is a bottle of water, to which he grabs and takes a few sips to quench his thirst. When the camera has got into a perfect enough position, The Postman starts to talk as the grin on his face beams bright*

“Ah, there’s nothing like relaxing in the sun as the soft cool breeze floats around you and silence is disturbed by the birds in the trees. It’s not often that I get time to myself these days, if I’m not on the road with the guys then I’m working out for a good few hours and if I’m not surrounded by a person of the media when I come home, then I’ve got the family around me. So what a better time to kick back, relax and go over what’s transpired - as far as the wrestling is concerned - in the last few days.”

*Hunt then sits up so that his legs are tucked in and his muscular knees stick out*

“Now, I could start off by saying how my chances of booking my spot at Mind Games and challenging for the CHZ Title are hanging by a thread. I could start off by saying that Jake Schultz deserves to be one step closer to becoming the top man and I could start off by saying that I am an alien from the planet Zorg, with my mission – being directly sent from General Zoidberg - to take over the world. May I draw to your attention that what you have just heard is a big pile of bollocks! It’s crap, bullshit, rubbish…whatever term you see fit to use in a situation like this.”

*Hunt pauses before clearing his throat*

“Title chances hanging by a thread? Do me a favour! All that Cage Match did was lead Jake Schultz into a false sense of security, it’s led him down a cul-de-sac, it’s led him down a road where flying pigs like to socialise while sipping on a dry Martini! Just because Schultz has one foot in the door, it doesn’t automatically mean that he’s a dead-cert to win the biggest prize in this company. Hell, I could sit here all bloody day and list off why Jake Schultz will not win the CHZ Title in two weeks…”

*Hunt glances around before continuing*

“But I won’t. You see, I am in a peaceful environment and I don’t think that these locals wouldn’t appreciate some ‘English thug’ shouting, effing and blinding about some ‘fake sport’. So, I am going to react to Wild and it’s consequences of that event in a quiet tone and respect the locals at the same time. It’s been a while since I addressed those concerned in such a peaceful manner, but I think it’s would be beneficial to my marriage and sex life if I dealt with the problems in my usual manner, when game time arrives!”

*Hunt pauses as an eldery couple slowly stroll past, walking their pet Golden Labrador, who runs up to Hunt sniffing and wagging his little tail*

Eldery Couple: “Hello”

Hunt: “Afternoon…”

*Hunt plays with the small puppy for a few seconds as the couple continue their stroll, Hunt then continues his speech*

Hunt: “For the first time since my arrival in CHZ, my attention has to be directed towards another camache whose name isn’t Jake Schultz. A camache who also has that slight knack of believing that he is a better wrestler and entertainer than I am. Jake Schultz, take a back seat because some schmuck called Persona Variable is all that stands between myself and that elusive title shot at Mind Games!”

*Hunt pauses again and then continues*

“Persona Variable…PV…Mr Different Personality Every Two Bloody Hours!! As a result of Schultz’s “victory” on Wednesday Night, it’s now down to you and I. It’s up to us to give the main event at Mind Games the honour and prestige that any main event should have. With Schultz already being guaranteed in that match, it means that the PPV buyrate is going to be low…so loooooooowwwwwwww! I know you’re a smart man, PV and I know that you care for this company. You care about the amount of mainstream publicity that the CHZ gets and the only drawing it’s going to get, it based on the calibre of the main event competitors. Now, we’re already screwed as Jake is already there – but it also means that either one of us could be there right with him, standing toe-to-toe in the CHZ’s biggest match since their “re-birth”. I know there’s no chance of both of us going to Mind Games with a hope of becoming Champion, but at least we can give the people what to expect in our match this coming Wild…”

*Hunt pauses and scowls, before glancing around and blurting out*

“What the bloody hell am I talking about? I’m basically sat here, spilling all this bollocks to a quince who wants MY title shot? I don’t think so. Persona Variable, enough with the preaching for the benefit of the CHZ, cos I don’t give a toss! All I care about is whether my name is below the ‘main event’ caption on the DVD release of the event. All I care about that I am the man who defies all odds and sneaks in at the last minute, after seemingly blowing the chance in that Cage Match. Do you get me, camache? Do you get the fact that it’s destiny that’s leading ME to this title?

*Hunt pauses and grabs the bottle of water, he then takes a few sips as he glances around the beautiful surroundings before him.*

“Heh, I could run my mouth all bloody day – but would it make a blind bit of difference? Would it really? I mean, would you take any notice of what I’m saying when you’ve got an important match to prepare for? Do you care what I say? I bet you’ve pressed “mute” on your remote control and all you can see is some guy waving his arms about! However, on the other hand I suppose it’s worth running your mouth, because then you just give the slightest indication to the ignorant streak of piss that you’re ready. That you’re stating your claim as to why you should be the man whose hand is raised in victory at the end of the match…you know, “when all the smoke has cleared”…”when it’s all said and done” etc, etc. All I can say to you, PV, is...

*At that point, the camera slowly pans away and Hunt sighs with a smile on his face as he stretches his legs out*

“Enough with the talking...let the games begin!!”

*He then puts his hands behind his head to motion that he’s relaxing and after a few moments, the picture slowly fades to black*
