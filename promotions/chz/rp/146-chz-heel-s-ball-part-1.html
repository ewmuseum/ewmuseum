CHZ Heel's Ball (Part 1)
Written: 16th August 2002

It’s a beautiful Friday night in Montreal on the famous Rue de Mal and it’s the night of the Canuck Heat Zone’s Heels Party, which has also turned into a dual celebration for Redemption Song’s CHZ Tag-Team Championship victory at Wednesday Wild. In attendance at the swinging shindig are the Carnival of Carnage - Matt Hunt, Igor Arefiev, Constantino Zergolov and Olga Zergolov, Watkins Group members Tom Core, Misty, Jim Surge and Ben Nighthorse Campbell, Stimpo (dressed as a princess), Texan and Axl Young with Roland the eagle!

“Welcome everyone to our beautiful building,” Tom Core says as he leans over a wooden railing at the top of the staircase. All of the CHZ superstars stand down on the second floor, which is a gigantic lounge area with red shag carpeting, 6 leather couches, a gigantic 20-foot screen projection television with dual film viewing area equipped with 25 bucket movie-theater style plush reclinable seats in stadium style for best viewing pleasure. Finally, topping off the room, the beautiful CHZ Tag-Team Championship maple leaf belts mounted on the wall in a special glass case.

There is also a long red oak table in the room upon which is a large platinum tray displaying a variety of delicious cooked meats such as hot dogs. Tom Core cooked all of the hot dogs himself using a secret recipe. Also on the table is a large punchbowl with a yummy St. Lucia-inspired rum fruit punch. Next to the punchbowl is Ben Nighthorse Campbell’s wood and ivory peacepipe and a leather pouch that gives off a slight lemon aroma.

Core practically glides down the stairs with one of the biggest smiles on a man’s face that a man could hope for, beaming with pride at his young tag-team’s accomplishments. First the #1 PWA ranking which they were able to achieve twice. Now, the CHZ Tag-Team Championship and a chance to really push for OSW contracts with the Most Extreme Team competition, where Redemption Song was one of the competition’s final four participants.

“This evening,” Core says as he begins to pour punch into glass goblets for everyone to enjoy, “is one for us to all come together and put our differences aside and watch Texan and Misty in the mud wrestling pit we had installed in the basement!”

There is loud cheering from all of the men in the room! Misty poses for the men in the room like the bad girl that she is and makes exaggerated angry faces and shakes both of his fists in the direction of Texan. Angel The Texan Summers, however, folds her arms a little disgusted.

“I didn’t know that this was going to be that kind of party!” the beautiful Texan stammers with a sweeping Texas drawl.

“Come on baby, you know you want it,” says Matt Hunt, putting two fingers up to his mouth and rapidly thrusting his tongue through the fingers repeatedly.

Axl Young nearly doubles over with laughter as Core glides by everyone and distributes the goblets of punch to all. Texan politely refuses and shows herself to the door.

“Awww... man, I thought we were going to have a little fun,” Stimpo says as he walks over to Tom Core, lifting his beautiful princess gown as he moves so as not to dirty the edges by dragging them.

“No fear my boy,” Core says in his really twisted high voice that is totally out of place on his physically toned frame. Even though his gray suit you can still see Core’s muscular frame. Having just retired from professional wrestling earlier this year with the 2XW Tag-Team title, Core was probably still in the best shape of his life.

“Now everyone, if you will,” Core says as he lifts his glass of punch, “to new homes, new federations, new championships and new beginnings!”

“Here! Here!” everyone replies in unison as all pound their punch. As soon as everyone is finished swallowing, they turn around and look at each other.

“That was delicious,” everyone says in unison again. This time everyone looks around at each other, completely bugged out that everyone was talking at the same time.

Suddenly, a very nasty, cold energy enters the room and each person can feel it. Ben Nighthorse Campbell immediately moves away from the assembled group. Olga, Igor and Constantino walk over to the punchbowl and each pour a pint of vodka into the mix. Igor and Constantino then jump up onto the lighting fixture and dangle from their legs like sleeping bats. Jim Surge walks up to Matt Hunt and gets straight in his face.

“Hey, hey.... hello in there?” Jim says as he pokes the side of Hunt’s head. “HELLLLOOOOO!!!!!!!” Surge screams at the top of his lungs, forcing Hunt to cringe, “Mr. Bombast, anyone home?”

“What is it camache?” Hunt answers in self-defense.

“I think I remember something about ‘worthless toolboxes.’” Surge says in a booming low voice. “Do you really think you’re in a position to speak in a negative way about the members of the Watkins Group?”

Stimpo walks over straight behind Jim, giving the impression that he might have the cowboy’s back or is identifying himself with the Watkins Group itself!! But something about the princess dress takes away from the intimidation factor. Tom Core immediately steps in.

“What in God’s green earth is wrong with you two?! This is a party!” Core puts his hands on his hips and his facial expression shows it all. He is not happy. His normally tanning-booth pink face is nearly flush red with anger.

“Drink some more punch the both of you this instant and let’s just have a fun time!”

Surge nods and puts both hands up in the air.

“Sorry mate,” Surge offers, “I didn’t mean to show such poor hospitality. The slimeball lawyer is right. Let’s just party here!”

“Now you’re talking,” says Ryan Contra, as he releases Roland the eagle who flies all around the room.

Contra comes back over with another round of punch. Ben Nighthorse Campbell then calls everyone over to sit down in a circle and smoke from the peacepipe.

“The peacepipe is very powerful medicine,” the Apache says in an excited voice like he was giving a lesson. “Medicine to my people is what you would call magic. The medicine of the peacepipe is that you make a wish for peace, such as you wish peace for the loss of a loved one, peace during times of war, peace during times of oppression.”

“What exactly is that stuff?” asks Stimpo.

“It’s just sweetgrass, that’s all,” winks Campbell. Everyone looks at the Native American, who just smiles and nods as if he was egging them on to take a hit from the pipe.

“Remember, think to yourself where you need peace in your life or in the cycle of life before you light the pipe. Then smoke to that peace.”

“I dunno about all of this...” Hunt says a conflicted expression crossing his face.

“Daaa.... comrade, drink one more of these and let’s try the pipe. It can’t kill us,” says Igor. Everyone else nods and another round of punch shots are distributed and devoured.

As everyone gets to a pretty high buzz due to the alcohol content in the punch, the peacepipe is passed around, and around, and around, and around, and around, and around, and around and around, being reloaded several times along the way until mostly everyone just lied there on their backs in on the red shag carpeting.

7 hours later the walls began to melt...
