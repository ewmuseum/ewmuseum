A Short Introduction Into The Ego
Written: 21st June 2002

(A green jaguar pulls up outside a large mansion in Calgary Alberta, Canada. The jade vehicle is shiny, and has been polished to perfection. Out of the car gets a chauffer whom opens the right hand back door, out gets a very attractive male. The guy has blonde hair just past his ears, wears a glittery jacket that is open to reveal his tanned muscular chest, and pectorals, he sports some rather fancy black jeans that are encrusted with genuine diamonds, and he wears some fashionable male boots. As the camera pans up his face is very clear, he smiles for the camera, and flicks his hair back; his eyes are hidden behind his Calvin Klein shades. The guy looks up at the mansion. The mansion is a rather spectacular site with a total of twenty-one windows from the front end of the house, seven on each floor. The man makes his way to the door of his house and a rather sassy housemaid lets him in. She has long blonde hair, blue sparkly eyes, voluptuous bosoms, and wears a traditional short black dress with a white pinafore. She smiles and let’s the guy in, her eyes are enough to welcome anyone in, she has a gaze that you could get lost in for hours, so beautiful…)

“Welcome home Jack, did you have a good business trip?” the maid enquired as the guy strolled through the hall with the rather attractive chandelier, and made a right turn into the living room collapsing in a leather chair.

“Yes thanks, Maria,” he said as the girl followed him through, “I have arranged terms with a Canadian based wrestling company, and they have given me a contract that I believe will last about a year before terms need to be renegotiated.”

“Excellent,” Maria said, standing up waiting for Jack’s command, “anything I can get you, sir?”

“Yeah there is, I would like you to crack open a nice bottle of champagne and pour me a glass of it, you can treat yourself too. After you have done that you can take a seat next to me if you wish? You have had a hard day working, I expect you want a rest, looking after a fourteen bedroom mansion cant be easy at the best of times.”

“Thank you Sir,” she said sauntering out of the room and into the kitchen to get a bottle of champagne.

Damn life is good; I just love being me – Jack “The Ego” Williams, a man that lives the dream, a man that lives a life of luxury. Looks like I will be participating in my first CHZ match soon, it is only a small tag team match but I am sure I will be able to show the world what I am capable of. I have looked at the roster and have came to the conclusion that most wrestlers are damn right envious of my position; I mean after all the only wrestlers that get anywhere near my calibre are Cassius Deamont, and Christ Matthews. Deamont has proved himself in RUSH but he hasn’t came up against me yet, as for Matthews, he is just a joke but I guess it doesn’t sound right to single out Deamont as the one piece of competition in this federation. Still…it isn’t as if you can really call Deamont competition; I mean let’s face it he’s no legend in this sport.

“There you go, Jack,” Maria said passing him a glass of champagne and sitting beside him awakening Jack from his thoughts.

“Thank you Maria,” Jack said quickly indulging in the sweet taste of champagne.

“So,” Maria said reluctantly, “what are your thoughts on this new federation you have entered, do you feel it is going to succeed?”

“A perfectly reasonable question to ask me, Maria, and one that I will answer just for you. I feel that this federation has potential, and the fact that I am on the roster has now guaranteed success. The man in charge of this federation is El Diablo, so he may not be any Jack Williams, but he is still a respected guy. The truth is thought Maria ratings are going to go through the roof, and it is down to one man, me – Jack Williams.”

“You seem to be very full of yourself today?”

“I am full of myself everyday and I am sure if you looked as good as me, and if you wrestled as well as me, and if you were me, you would be full of yourself too,” Williams put an arm around Maria consoling her, “but I am sorry to say Maria that you aren’t me, and you never will be me.”

(Williams removed his arm and stood up, Maria didn’t seem to take too kindly to Jack pointing out that she would never be him, and she didn’t like the way that Jack thought of himself as an almost godly figure. Williams paced around the room for a bit finishing the glass of champagne off.)

“Now I can see you look a little bit down Maria, but think of things in a positive way, you are Jack Williams maid; how privileged do you feel? Hell one day if you behave well enough I may let you wipe my ass, and then you can go around with a t-shirt on for the rest of your life saying, “I wiped Jack Williams ass”. I think Maria,” Williams said smiling, “as far as being lucky goes you are the luckiest maid alive so lighten up and enjoy yourself a bit, huh?”

(Maria forced a smile but deep down inside she was not happy, she was upset that Williams thought that it was a pleasure to serve him. She found it a rather frustrating experience, because the guy liked himself too much, no wonder he hadn’t got a girlfriend currently. She would keep her thoughts to herself though, besides the job paid well, as Williams was loaded with cash.)

(There was a pause of silence in the room, Maria didn’t have anything to really say to the arrogant Williams, and Williams didn’t wish to praise himself anymore to Maria. Williams still stood up and he seemed to be in deep thought.)

I guess it is just a matter of waiting now, waiting for things to happen in CHZ, waiting for matches to be booked, and waiting to see how feuds and friendships are made. As far as feuds and friendships go with me, there is something very simple, and that is that I look out for just me, I want to make that clear to everyone. My tag team match in CHZ this week is about me, not my tag partner, as a matter of fact I am so confident that I will prevail victorious that I haven’t even decided to find out who my opponents are, not that it will help me when training. I am not the sort of man that looks over tapes of his opponents, I am the sort of man that just goes down the gym now and again to stay in shape, and what shape I am in (no surprises there).

“I think I may go down to the local town in a minute, Maria,” Jack said.

“If there is anything you want, I can get it for you,” she said trying to be helpful.

“It’s ok Maria, I know you like to help but sadly you are not going to help in these circumstances, I am going down the town, to gloat, to see how the people react to me. It is something I like to do now and then, just walk down a street and get people screaming my name. Sadly Maria this will never happen to you, unless you were to become closer to me, perhaps a partner, or wife, then that would happen,” Jack said winking.

“I would love to…”

Jack interrupted Maria, as she seemed to be accepting his offer, “Sadly though Maria, that is something that will never happen, you are a little lower class for me, no offence, Maria.”

(Maria scowled, she got so angry with Jack, the guy could never compliment somebody, as soon as he started to compliment the person he just delivered a low blow, and flatter himself. Why couldn’t Jack just get a reality check, he wasn’t the most important man on the planet, some fans may think he is but the fact is that he is just an egotistical pig.)

“Why bother going down town Jack, it is late in the day as it is – can you really be bothered to introduce yourself just yet? Besides there was a letter in the post for you today inviting you to a dinner with Steven Spielberg tomorrow, he wants to speak to you about some sort of movie deal.”

“That is my point exactly, my dear Maria,” Jack said smiling, “even Spielberg wants Jack Williams. I see it now, Wrestler, Sex Icon, and Movie Star – Jack Williams, the real f’n deal.”

“Don’t get too carried away, it is early days yet,” Maria said.

“Maria you have a lack of knowledge of this world, I feel this is because you are bitter than you lost out when it came to well…just generally! You must remember this, Maria…Jack Williams is the hottest thing going, everyone wants him. If you remember that then next time you will understand that I can truly have anything, understand?” Williams said as Maria replied with a simple nod.

Jack Williams flexed his muscles and smiled at the cameraman, “I am Jack Williams, welcome to my idealistic dream life.”

(SCENE FADES)
