The Promotion
Written: 8/18/02

It was as if his spirits were to be lifted for a day. An important night for the CHZ upcoming star, and it would be hosted in the proverbial 'roots' of The Disciple, in Vancouver. After somewhat of an enthusiastic, but rough start in the CHZ, it was obvious that the religious wrestler could use the boost.

The thoughts ran through Matt's mind: racing, speeding, flying. His thoughts were a little clouded with the tournament as well, having dealt with another case, this one regarding a Kindred... and a PWA mentor. The match with Nerezza was grueling, and the NTICW superstar was more powerful than Matt had ever expected, but some how... his will to win, and his strength to resist giving up, it came through for him.

This day was a new day though. Matt had replaced his light wrestling attire for the heavy attire of his profession. A black, leather cloak hung around his knees, opened for easy access to his weapons, with some casual looking black armor underneath. As always, his nose length red hair hung in tangled strands in front of his face, which did a good job at concealing his modified, silver eyes.

'This thing is possessed!' Matt shouted in an aggravated sneer, smacking the small device with his hand. The camera rattled a little, but just continued staring at him blankly. 'Record! Damn you Satan, record!'

The door to the colorless room slowly creaked open and an old man walked in, rubbing his eyes a little as the light hit them. His squinting made the creases on his face appear all the more, and his slicked back gray hair did nothing to hide them. However, Matt's superior, known only as X, didn't care much for his looks. Every day, he wore the leather jacket and communication tools that all commanders would wear, and his grumpy demeanor was enough of a warning to not comment on the way he looked.

X crossed his arms and grumbled a little tiredly. 'What is the racket for kid? Tryin' to wake the dead?'

Matt looked up from the camera and grinned a little, trying to stifle a chuckle. 'You're up, I guess I already did eh?'

X muttered something under his breath and took a seat at the table. After taking a long drink of hot coffee, the old timer looked up and took note of the camera, shaking his head a little at the sight of it.

'You can't bring that in here kid,' X bluntly stated with a smirk.

Matt, who was again fiddling with the device, looked up and frowned. 'Why not?' he protested.

'Why not? Kid... this room here is more classified than the president's porno collection. You can't just bring a camera in here.'

Matt crossed his arms and looked down for a few seconds. 'Well,' he mumbled, 'nothing important is showing man. I mean... what can people get out of a cement wall?'

X opened his mouth to give an explanation about the rules, but stopped himself before any words came out. Matt grinned a little, realizing that X had no good explanation for Matt's proposal.

'C'mon X,' he urged, 'what's the harm? I mean, you almost got me killed by a vampire bro! You owe me!'

X sighed and tapped his fingers against the table for a few moments, then slowly shook his head. 'A'ight kid... but if you get in trouble, I never let you do this, ya' understand?'

Matt nodded a little, but paid almost no attention as he gave the camera on the tripod a hard smack. Immediately, the camera blinked to life, bringing about a small victory dance from The Disciple.

'Wooh! It works!' he exclaimed as he quickly pressed the record button on the back and stepped back to the wall.

Matt threw on a pair of shades and cracked his neck, giving the camera a cold glare before he began in an even colder tone. 'You know, the world is filled with a lot of things. Violence, manipulation, lies, cheating... all the sins of the world packed into one little box, packed in a crate stapled with those fragile tapes. See, that's the world we live in ladies and gentlemen, and it's people like me who don't want to live in that world! I don't want to lie, cheat and steal my way through life. Is it so hard to believe that one person in this corrupt society wants something better?'

Matt rubbed his chin a little in thought, and peered above the camera to X, who was watching closely. It made Matt a little nervous, as if he wasn't allowed to mess up... but he quickly returned his gaze to the camera and tried to ignore it. He had a promo to cut after all.

'So,' he continued in a little bit of a lighter tone, 'I'd be pretty damn selfish if I made the world better for only numbro uno, oui? So I step through those ropes not just for me, but for everyone like me. Everyone who wants to get away from the world for a day and watch that happens in the ring. Every man, woman and child who wants to take a break from all the stresses of our times and watch athletes perform in that ring! I don't just wrestle for me, I do it for them, the fans who made this sport what it is today. You can't forget them... the fifty thousand that blast the roof off the place every time Matt Blitz hits the stage! The millions across the world that sing in a synonymous cheer when The Disciple steps through those ropes! I do it every bit as much for them as I do for me, because I'm a wrestler, and I'm one of the few releases from the evil of the world.'

Matt, who was now grinning, slowly allowed his smile to fade as he tilted his head a little. He ran his hand through his hair, and took a moment to think about his next choice of words, but didn't look over the camera this time. X was a little too judgmental to help his nerves relax.

Matt continued in a lower tone, slowly leaning back into a more relaxed stance against the wall. 'Ryan Contra... I know what you're thinking brother. If I don't want to commit sin, if I don't want all the deception and lies... then what was that last week? Well, last week as living in the world I chose, Ryan. See, I serve God to the best of my abilities to the point where I won't even hit a woman in that ring... but the man upstairs and I have an understanding. See, I do what I have to do in that ring, and I do what I have to do out of that ring. After the night is over... maybe I did wrong. Maybe I deceived you, maybe I hid some brass knucks in my wrestling boot. Maybe I didn't follow the teaching that night, but I still did the best that I could, and through that... my faith strengthened. Later that night, I pray, for forgiveness and protection. I pray for me, you, Summers, Young... even the French Canadian announcer who sounds Swiss! I seek forgiveness for what I do in wrestling, and Ryan... God understands. He knows that fight evil in his name, I must first wrestle in mine. That's what's unique about me.'

Matt trailed off and took a second to stand up straight again. He creased the sleeves of his leather cloak by crossing his arms, and tapped his finger against his forearm.

'But Wednesday ain't about me, or my relationship with God,' he continued. 'It's about you, me and the chair that'll be in between us. See, the boys who run the show have seen fit to put you and me in a match with a chair to decide the victor. I can tell you right now Contra... the winner will be easily decided. Oh no my friend, it won't be about who has the better technical skill, and who is more innovative with their moves. High impact, high flying... it won't matter! The man who wins will simply be the man who wants it more Ryan, and no one wants it more than I do. Watch this past Wild... I refused to stay down no matter how much both of you beat me down, and I still came back to fight. Now, you're stepping into Vancouver, my home town, in front of my people! The fans will be chanting my name, cheering me on, giving me the strength to keep going! The American Hero can't compete with that, he just can't. So when you think of me, think of that chair Contra. Think about how it'll look wrapped around your head, because I want it more than you do, and I'll claw my way out of Hell itself to get my hands on you. Just remember, your soul might belong to God... but come Wild, your ass is 100% mine!'

Matt slowly walked over to the camera and hit the pause button, looking again to X who was looking on with a disgruntled look.

'What in God's holy name was that?' he asked in a confused tone, shaking his head a little.

'Huh?' Matt replied as he looked back to the camera, then back to X. 'That was a promo man. All us wrestlers gotta' do one once and a while.'

X sighed, blinked and took another long drink of coffee. 'I can't believe you woke me up so I could watch that crap...'
