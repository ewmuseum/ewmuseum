Buying and eating lunch
Written: June 27

(Note this a really crappy Rp so you don’t have to read)


Well do you like this one Angella? I’ve been picking wrestling attires for like a month now you haven’t said anything on every one. Well how about this one there is a red tank top black leather pants and high heels to kick people in the gut and maybe make them bleed.

Angella: WOW, that looks so cool.

Paige: Ya I know that’s why I’m taking it.

Angella: I want a suit like that because I do accompany you to the ring.

Paige: Pick which ever one you want

Angella: Ok are we going to go now my feet hurt.

Paige: Yes come one let’s get in the car.

Paige and Angella walk outside the store get into there car and drive off . The car arrives at there house it only took the 10 minutes to get there. Both of them get out of the car and walk into the house, they both take there shoes off and lay them on the rug. Then both of them walk into separate places, Paige goes into the kitchen and Angella goes upstairs.

In the kitchen Paige is getting stuff out for it looks like lunch because it is 12:00, Angella comes walking into the room with the new thing that she bought at the store.

Angella: Like what I bought, it’s cute isn’t it.

Paige: Yeah I guess so.

Then Paige goes upstairs and Angella puts a pan on the oven and gets some bread ham and mustard for sandwiches. Paige come’s running in.

Paige: Like my wrestling attire?

Angella: Holy mother of shit that looks even better than what it looked like at the store.

Angella: Lunch is ready we are having sandwiches, and spaghetti is that good we are running out of food.

Paige: Yes that is ok I love both stuff.

Paige and Angella sit down at the table and say a prayer and then they start eating.

Paige: This is really good where did you get the spaghetti?

Angella: At the store it said that it was the best in the world.

Paige: I can see why.

After they are done eating they both take there plates to the counter and Paige goes in the living room and turns on the TV.

Angella: Have you heard that you are against that Amanda Flaton on Wild, you are going to kick her ass.

Paige: Yeah I heard and you are right I am going to kick her ass.

Angella: I’m going upstairs to take a rest you can do whatever you want.

Paige: I’m taking a rest down here.

The scene closes with Paige and Angella both fall asleep and lay down.
