Case of the Missing Combatant
Written: 21/07/2002

Another day, another case, the story of my life and although at times it may not be the most rewarding one I still find a way to wake up in the morning and do it all over again.

The name is Durke Richter, Private Eye. Yes I am one of those guys also known as a gumshoe. In the world today the only places you would actually expect to find a real live detective is in the movies but just because we are not too popular in society doesn't mean we are not their.

I have had my share of cases some solved and some that I am still missing pieces of and have given up on. Don't get me wrong though, I know my stuff and I have my degree from Law School to prove it. It is just over the years I have never really took on a case that interested me enough to finish it. That is until one night I would receive a case that would not only catch my interest but in due time would change my life forever. This case, which by the way I am still in the midst of even as I speak, I call 'The Case of the Missing Combatant'.

It was a night like any other night in Manhattan, New York. The sky was dark and because Manhattan is such a big city the stars are not very clear, at least from where I am staring from my office which is conveniently over top of a bowling alley and underneath a clog dancing class. Needless to say I am always running low on aspirin. I was hoping to get a better place but you have to start somewhere and with little money I had this was the only place I could afford. The wallpaper was peeling off the walls and there were unusually big stains on the ceiling, which I would be happy not knowing how they got there or what they are. The floor underneath my feet is wooden and by the looks of the lumber it is not very well sanded, so I wouldn't suggest walking around bare foot in here. My desk was here when I bought the place and it was covered with a blanket of dust, which doesn't really help my allergies. I was glad however that I found a place and didn't end up lying in a pile of garbage behind a K-Mart with an empty bottle of vodka in my hand.

There are times though when I questioned my judgment of quitting the police force to start up my own Private Eye business, in a broken down old apartment, all taking place in a huge city unlike my small hometown of Sandusky, Ohio. I could have been the best rookie on the force but I traded it all for this. I knew though that I would probably make the same decision if I had to go back and do it all over again. Being on the force was fine but I had a feeling that I was being held back and that I was destined for much more then giving speeding tickets and checking the parking meters. So some nights I would doubt the decisions I made up to now and then other nights I would be glad that I am where I am. It is a reoccurring cycle but then this one fateful night I would have something else weighing on my mind.

She looked to be in her early twenties and had curves that would make a blind man stare. Her crimson red lipstick was calling out to me and her sky blue eyes were playing my heart like a harp. Her blonde hair soaking wet and slung over her shoulder went great with her purple bikini with a shiny silver lining. That is when I put down my latest issue of Sports Illustrated: Swimsuit Edition and took a look at my new customer who had sauntered in here while I was catching up on some bikini browsing.

Unlike the picture I was staring at in the magazine my customer was a male and I would expect he had all the necessary parts but no way was I going to check to make sure. He was a tall man, about 6'5'' and by the looks of his Goliath physique I would say he weighed in the neighborhood of 280lbs. An impressive sight he was in deed, not more impressive then the swimsuit model mind you but still pretty impressive.

'Take a seat' I said to him as I slid my magazine into the bottom shelf of my desk for future reference.

He looked to be pretty stressed out which I noticed by the huge vein popping out of his forehead. He had huge bags under his eyes that possibly carried a week load of groceries. This man was huge and looked like to be on the edge of sanity ready to plummet into the pits of insanity. If I didn't watch my words I could have easily been eating food from a straw. So I decided to be rather calm with him.

'Now how can I help you?' I asked hoping to allow this guy to get a load of his already Herculean chest.

He took a deep breathe and laid back in his chair so that he would be more comfortable and then he started to speak with an unusually soft voice for a man of his stature.

'He's missing,” the man said which didn't really help me at all.

'Who is missing?' I asked trying to get to the problem and not waste an hour of my time wondering whom the hell 'he' was.

The man then started to get uncomfortable and was fidgeting in his chair. He was looking more stressed out by the second and looked like he was ready to self-destruct or something of that nature.

'MY TAG TEAM PARTNER!' he blurted out which damn near made me jump out of seat and go head first through the ceiling to be stomped to death by a pack of clogs.

'Your tag team partner?' I asked a little confused about what he was on about.

The man at this point gets up out of the chair and started to pace back and forth nervously as he explained to me the details of the case I was to solve.

'Yeah,' he said confirming his earlier statement, 'I am Jack Murdock, one half of the Hawaiian Punches. My partner, Hugh Harley, has gone missing just days before are debut in a place called the PWA.'

This interested me as I had caught an article of the PWA in my magazine just before this guy walked in. The PWA or the Primetime Wrestling Academy is a wrestling school for future stars of its mother fed, Primetime Championship Wrestling or PCW for short. I have also caught some broadcasts of it when I went to the bars for a few drinks. I am not really a wrestling fan but I don't mind it either.

'This Hugh Harley, what is your relation to him?' I asked trying to get some back up info on the missing wrestler, which may help me solve the case in the end.

'He was one of my best friends from High School and we have always stayed in close touch.' Mr. Murdock stated nearly in tears, 'I just have no idea where he is.'

I leaned back on my chair and scratched my chin pondering the situation that was place before me.

'When did you last see him?' I asked still thinking to myself.

'The last time I saw him was two days ago.' Jack said in reply to my question, 'We went out for lunch at a place called Uncle Moe's Family Feedbag and then parted ways after that. Then that is when he went missing.'

'Hmmmmm. I thought to myself, what could have happened to this guy.

I put my feet up on my desk and went into deep thought of possible reasoning for the disappearance of this wrestling star but I kept coming up blank or when I thought I was getting somewhere it would just lead me nowhere.

'Is there any enemies of Hugh or yourself that you know of?' I asked thinking maybe that an old time rival could have been most likely responsible if it had been a kidnapping.

'No,' he said not even giving it a thought, 'Not any that we know of any ways.'

Now I was stumped and if I were going to find this guy I would need to do some major detective work that only a gumshoe like myself could do. I took my feet off the table and got up out of my chair. I walked around my desk and stood next to the much taller Jack Murdock who was about 5 inches taller then me.

'Do you have a picture of Hugh with you?' I asked trying to get as much info to get started on this case as possible.

Jack then reaches into his back pocket and produces a polaroid of him and his partner, Hugh Harley standing side by side in the ring over a pair of much smaller, knocked out opponents. Hugh was a little shorter and thicker of the two but his arms were gigantic. I took a long look at the photo and then grabbed my gray trench coat and my matching hat off the old hat coat rack by my door.

'Where are you going?' Jack asked thinking that maybe I am blowing him off or something.

What I was about to say would start what would be a major change in my life that may never leave me.

'To the Primetime Wrestling Academy.'
