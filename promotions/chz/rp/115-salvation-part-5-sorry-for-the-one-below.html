Salvation: Part 5 (Sorry For The One Below)
Written: 8/01/02

I was lost in time, sitting in a darkened closet, looking at nothing short of my fate. My fate ironically had dark red, leathery skin and emotionlessly black eyes without pupils. Fangs, hooked claws... the face of evil, magnified by the fact that we were in complete darkness and in an enclosed area. Every nerve in my body was screaming at me, telling me to be afraid or to run. One side of my mind was telling me to bolt out the door while another side begged me to keep myself between the creature and Christina beside me. I ignored them both, since they hadn't served me thus far. Instead, I found my hand slowly creeping across the carpeted floor towards that of the demon's.

'Take my hand,' I again heard the whispers in my head, 'take it and bind yourself to the only one who loves you.'

I could feel the beads of sweat trickling off the tip of my nose as my hand inched closer, my fingers outstretched, calling to the creature. It didn't move, but simply watched as it always did, intently, following my hand with its souless eyes as I was now mere inches away from taking hold of his hand. I was shaking a little, and I started to crack, unable to take the pressure. Screw God, screw my faith... were they here now? How much had they helped me? They didn't do anything for me when I needed them, and now more than ever, I did. This creature though... he accepted me and even called me brother. He wanted me to belong, be a part of what he was. He didn't spit out in spite against his god as I did mine, so how could his existence be any worse than mine? Quickly I lashed out and grabbed for the demon's hand.

Yet, I was stopped. A warm grip, shaking and sweating, grabbed me by my wrist and pulled my arm back. In rage, I glared back at her with my silver eyes flashing like embers in the dark closet. She looked scared, and didn't even slightly weaken her grip on my arm. What did she have to be scared of? She was possessed after all. Her plan was to keep my away from my salvation so that I wouldn't live the way I desperately needed to. Just like The Disciples, she wanted me in some damn cage, or as a pawn on a board she sees fit to move. Just like them all, she was against me, and she refused to let me turn to my only help in this dark world.

She looked at me, blinking away some previous tears, and frantically whispered. 'No... I told you... you can't leave me. They'll get me if you go!'

I glared coldly and tore my arm away from the witch. 'You'd say that demon! You want to keep my away from my salvation, just like them! Just like my brother, just like X, just like them all... why won't you just leave me be!'

My words frightened her... but whatever. She staggered back and pressed herself against the opposite wall, balling up in the corner. If I really wanted to save her, that little human ball wouldn't do much for her. Maybe she didn't deserve my compassion, but I couldn't just let her live the way she was. I knew that if I was to be saved, she would have to be saved as well. I'd take her from this harsh world when I left through my true savior, and she wouldn't have to cry anymore. It made me feel good, knowing how I'd be helping her like that.

I returned my gaze to where the demon was, but my salvation had left. I wanted to strangle that witch now, maybe leave her a bloody wreck in that closet. However, I had to find the demon again. I had to find my place.

'I'll be back,' I muttered as I opened the closet door and stepped out. She grabbed onto my leather cloak and was pulled half way out the door. Still trying to stop me? I didn't think so, and with a swift wrestling stomp to the back of her head, I was out there door. I heard the closet door close behind me, and I hoped I wouldn't have to deal with her again until I saved her.

I stepped over the shattered remains of my cell phone and calmly made my way down the stairs. I could feel them there, deep down, and I knew they still wanted me. They craved to call me brother more than I craved to call them such. I couldn't figure out why they wouldn't show themselves. They wanted me to join them... why wouldn't they show themselves?

'Where are you!?' I demanded, shouting to the corners of the room. Did they think I'd give up that easily? Did they think they could hide from me? I knew the demons were there.

I kicked the coffee table and knocked a few crystal decorations over. I wanted them to answer me. What was wrong? Why wouldn't they talk to me anymore? That witch ruined my once chance! By God it made me hate, and it boiled like hot lava inside my head. I clenched my fist and sneered to myself just thinking about it.

'I know you're here!' I again shouted, 'so show yourselves! You want me, brothers!? You want me to take your hands still!? Let me see them! Show me!'

I dropped to my knees and lowered my head into my hands. I wanted to cry... hell, I wanted to make someone else cry. But I knew... oh yes, they were testing me. I knew they didn't want no baby in their ranks, they wanted a strong brother, strong like Matt Blitz! I could hate, I could plague... I wouldn't drop the ball now that I had it. I stood up and cracked my neck, slowly pacing back and fourth as I did so.

I stopped and looked over my shoulder, seeing nothing, but whispering harshly anyways. 'You promised no tests brother. Why do you test me? I am perfect to join with the one who loves me, just give me the chance! What do I have to do to prove myself? What do I have to do to take your hand, brother?'

I sighed and shook my head when there was no answer. I sat on the couch and flipped on the television. The colors were altered, but I could easily make everything out. It was the news... I hated the news. It was boring, and since I was a Disciple, I knew everything already.

'The Turdo Church was the victim of arson mere days ago, July 30th, 2002. Police have released little facts, but strangely enough, this case seems to be wrestling related,' the anchor began, dragging a smile on my face. I was a wrestler, I wanted to hear. 'Apparently, the arsonist has something against CHZ superstars Angel, Saint and Matt Blitz. The three wrestlers are known for their religious belief in God, and it seems this was a hate related crime against the three wrestlers. The police currently have no suspects, but the investigation is ongoing. They ask that anyone with information to contact their local police department, or the RCMP stationed in Turdo. Constable Randy Cooper will be handling the case personally.'

I laughed, more or less at the irony. Didn't the arsonist understand? Burn all the churches, burn them all and watch as they become ash and cinder. I didn't follow him anymore, I followed another. He had forsaken me, but the one who loved me, I knew he wouldn't.

'Burn them, go ahead! You'll burn too, I'll make you burn!' I shouted at the television, throwing the changer at the anchor's image, shattering the changer. I looked out the window and saw nothing but the darkness of the eternal night that had been cast on the house. I noticed that the television as the only source of light in the room, but I didn't care. What was I to be afraid of?

'Lost in the maze, I can't find my own face. Lost for days, my soul's kept in the wrong place. Lost is life, yo, what's wrong, what's right? I've been lost in the night, with the light to guide my sight,' I hummed to myself, but it didn't take me back to reality this time. I had slipped too far into it all to escape. I knew it, but I just didn't care anymore. I had given up a long time ago.

'Or am I trapped in the light, with the night to guide my sight?' I asked myself with a cruel snicker. It was ironic... I loved it.

'In other news,' the anchor continued in a slightly altered voice. 'Christina McBorden, a young college student living in a suburban area of Toronto was found dead this morning. The Toronto RCMP haven't released any details, but she was last seen with CHZ wrestler Matt Blitz. He is a prime suspect in this case, and if he does complete his task... then he will prove himself to be our true brother.'

I eyed the anchor who was looking right at me. Something was deferent now... just something that made him look sinister. I knew it to be them.

'The message,' I exclaimed like a giddy school kid, 'that's the message!'

I jumped off the couch and bolted up the stairs. Before I reached the top, I slowed down. I couldn't seem 'ancy', oh no... she would know something is wrong. I had to act like the Blitz she wanted... understanding, comforting... I had to. My task was so simple, I would now take the hand of my brother! I was so excited, like I was in some angered ecstasy.

I slowly crept into the bedroom. You know when you're trying to be quiet and everything is against you? The door smacked against the wall and the floor began to creak as I walked. Why was it against me too!? I couldn't stand it. The world was closing in on me, trying to suffocate me! Didn't this world know that I am Matt Blitz? You don't just lock me in a cage, not now and not ever. He didn't learn that, and now, I had turned my back on him.

'Matt?' a frightened whisper came from the closet.

'Yeah,' I replied as softly as I could, glad that she couldn't see my grin. I wiped the expression off my face as I carefully slid the closet door open, stepped in and closed it behind me.

'You left me again,' she whimpered, still balled up like before. 'My head hurts..'

'I know,' I said, lowering my head in shame. 'I'm sorry Christina, I really am.' I put a reassuring hand on her shoulder, looking her straight in the eye. I wanted her to feel better. She didn't deserve this. I did... but not her.

'When are we going to get out of this?' she mumbled out, sounding scared of the answer.

I sighed and rubbed her arm. 'I don't know Christina... I really don't know. Don't worry though, I'm here with you. I won't let them take you... they'll have to kill me a thousand times first.'

She smiled, which almost seemed to brighten up the darkness we were sitting in. My spirit lifted a little when I saw that, and I smiled back. I was glad she was starting to be free of her hardships. That was the reason I was with her in the first place.

'Thanks,' she said as she gradually got out of her ball. I wiped some of the residual tears from her eyes and made sure she was Alright. She seemed fine... I guess it made it all worth while.

I circled around behind her and crossed my arms around her, holding her, making sure she felt safe. 'I'm here Christina... you're safe.'

She placed her hands over my arm and held on, resting her head back against my shoulder. I could tell that her spirit was being lifted as well.

I sighed and edged myself closer so that I could whisper in a comforting voice. 'You don't deserve this Christina. Remember that; all the hardships, all the pain and all the suffering... you deserved any of it. You're a good person.'

I listened for a moment, and heard her soft breaths. Her arms dropped to her stomach as she dozed off into a sleep. I don't know how long I held her for, but it was a while. Her breath slowed down, and she entered her slumber. I smiled... she looked so peaceful.

I reached into my leather cloak and pulled out a cross. I rested it to my side and pressed the hidden trigger on the side. From the bottom of the cross erupted a needle-like blade. I pulled it up and pressed the point of the blade against her throat, making sure not to wake her up. She didn't deserve that either.

'You're a good person,' I repeated in the same tone of voice. 'Let me save you.'
