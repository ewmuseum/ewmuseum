A mystery begins...
Written: 9th August 2002

Jim Surge and Ben Nighthorse Campbell look at each other in disgust. The members of Redemption Song have been bickering the entire flight home from Toronto to Newark Airport, from which a limo would take them to the headquarters of the Watkins Group in Greenwich Village, New York City. The half-brothers, while trying to keep it “professional” rather than personal, have shown a bit more hostility toward each other than usual following the beat-down they received in the locker room from a masked mystery opponent on the first Wednesday Wild. Not even the consolation of ending the careers of former CHZ tag-team Children of God, or their own victory over Gold Rush helped bring the mood down to something one could consider calm.

It seemed as if the half-brothers, the cowboy and Indian, had let their basic nature shine through and they were about to come to blows.

“Dude, I’m telling you for the last time, it was your fault,” Jim says, his western drawl steaming as the private jet the four Watkins Group members share hits a bit of unexpected turbulence. “You should have used your Apache superpowers or whatever to call on the Giant Eagle and warn us!”

“It’s GREAT SPIRIT!” Ben says, his eyes becoming narrow and his tone seriously angry. “Giant Eagle is a supermarket!”

Jim starts to laugh and turns his attention back to the first edition of the new CHZ magazine which ranks Redemption Song as the #1 tag-team in Canuck Heat Zone.

“Listen, I can’t just call on my Ghostrider medicine on a whim, especially to stop some cowardly sneak attack that really has no motive!” Ben says in a passionate, quick voice, his words dripping with sincerity.

Surge, sensing all of the anger gone from his half-brother’s words, sighs and eases up a bit as he slouches back in his seat.

“I understand that,” Jim says in a defeated tone, as if he felt a little guilty about ignorantly insulting his partner’s heritage and culture. “Hey, we still beat the hell out of Children of God so badly that they retired, and now that Gold Rush is out of the way we’re in the finals of the CHZ Tag-Team Championship tournament.

Ben nods, his handmade leather headband looking quite out of place inside the sleek Watkins Group private jet. Over at the plane bar, Tom Core and Misty shake their heads as if unfortunately witnessing a fight between a married couple.

“You two are pathetic,” Core says, unusually dressed down for the flight in tan dress pants, brown loafers and a gray polo shirt. “You sound like old ladies or an old married couple or something.”

“That’s real nice Core,” Jim says, taking off his Stetson hat and flinging it at Core’s head a lá the way Random Task threw his shoe in the original Austin Powers movie. “And you’re supposed to be looking out for us!”

Jim and Ben quickly glance toward each other, but they attempt to keep it cool in front of Core. As soon as they catch the other in their peripheral vision they focus their attention back towards their manager and lawyer.

“The thing for you guys to focus on now is this mystery team that jumped you. No matter who’s fault it is or whatever the hell you two are bitching about, you have to just say - hell, it happened, now let’s get the sonofabitches that did it.”

Jim is taken aback for a second as Core’s advice seems genuine and seems to exactly jive with what he’s been secretly thinking.

“You know Core, I think you’re absolutely right,” Jim says, his western accent much less noticeable now that his temper is calmed.

“Now we have to stay on guard,” the lawyer says in a commanding voice, as if he was delivering a highly convincing closing argument to a jury in a murder case, “not just for Flying Vergomovs who no doubt you will crush into oblivion on the next Wild to claim the CHZ Tag-Team Championship, but for this new threat!”

“So help me,” Ben begins, his own voice becoming much louder, “if they try and cost us the championship.”

“I think they’re a little too subtle for that,” Core replies, stroking his chin as he finally finishes pouring himself a Bombay Sapphire gin and tonic.

“A little too subtle?” Jim questions in a perplexed tone. “Subtle? You call attacking us in our locker room subtle?”

“Masks, Surge, maaaaaasks!” Core answers, emphasizing his point by protracting the second ‘masks’ utterance. “They don’t want you to know how it is. Taking the titles from you on Wild wouldn’t be good enough for these twisted fucks.”

Jim and Ben look at each other and nod. In unison, they both proclaim: “pay-per-view.”

Core smiles and nods affirmative.

“You boys are learning the business real quick. Before you know it, you’re going to add CHZ tag-team championships to that #1 PWA ranking we already have under our belts.”

The plane’s captain makes an announcement that the passengers should return to their seats for landing. Everyone does as they are told and Jim takes his seat right next to Ben.

“Can you at least try to sense trouble next time?” Jim asks his half-brother in a serious tone.

“Yeah,” Ben laughs, “I’ll at the very least try.”

Jim and Ben smile at each other as the plane descends into the beautiful Garden State of New Jersey and the eyesore that is Newark International Airport.
