Incognito Dreams
Written: 8/8/02

The scene opens up in the streets of Toronto. It is currently rain falling in a small city park. Suddenly a figure a shown wearing all black anda holloween Michael Myers mask swinging on the swing. He is swinging very slowly as the raindrops drinkle off his body.

'Pain...Anguish...A couple feelings to be felt very...very...soon.' The figure said as he creepily stops swinging.

'We are not playing games...the games are now finished...the fun stops here.' a voice says as another black figure walks in front of the camera wearing all black and a holloween Jason mask. They both are standing in the wet woodchucks as their hazel brown eyes stare into the camera.

'All members of CHZ...beware...fore there will be no telling what...we will do.' the first figure says.

'But you can count on one thing...when we come...things will never...ever...be the same...again.' the second figure says.

They both walk into the camera as the scene slowly fades.
