Disturbing Dreams
Written: 07.25.02

Blackness.

It is now snowing. We aren’t in Nevada anymore. That is obvious. The highway is snow covered from the snow drifting onto the road. The wind blows very strongly, making the Harrington’s Buick fight to stay in it’s lane. Everyone in the car is still happy, smiles still on there face. Something extra is in the car now, birthday balloons, and a wrapped gift. The man points out the window, and we then spot a bridge, with a sign on it that reads “Welcome to Illinois – You are now crossing the Mississippi River.” The car crosses the bridge without problem, and then gets back onto the normal highway. The car continues, for another half mile or so, until it gets to a few fields. Suddenly, the wind gusts, and blows the car towards the side of the road. The man corrects, and turns the wheel left, but this sends the car hurling towards the opposite lane. The man then looks ahead, and spots a huge semi-truck coming at them, looking to be doing seventy miles per hour. The man then slams on the brakes, and jerks the wheel back right, but nothing happens. The brakes are seemingly gone, and the steering wheel has broken. Finally, the inevitable happens, and the car collides with the semi at a forty-five degree angle, doing almost sixty miles per hour. The car gets launched into the air, and begins flipping, as the semi slides to a screeching halt. The car does one complete end-over-end in mid air, then noses down into the ground and launches into the air again. Not as high, but just as devastating. Finally, it lands, softer than the first, but still very hard, and begins to barrel roll until finally, it lands, on it’s side. The truck driver rushes over to see the injuries, and leans inside the back window. He quickly removes his head, and begins sprinting towards his truck, going to call for help.

Blackness.

A knock comes at the door of Paul Harrington’s office. Suddenly, the door opens, and his secretary walks in.

“Mr. Harrington?” Victoria asks, trying to wake him.

Finally, Paul lifts his head from his desk, with groggy eyes and looks at Victoria.

“I must have dozed off. What is it?” He asks.

“Michael Pearson is here for your meeting. Should I send him in?” Victoria asks.

“Not yet, let me get my head together. Then I’ll let you know.” He responds.

“Okay.” Victoria says, leaving the room.

The door closes, and Paul stands from his chair. He looks at the back wall of his office, that is all windows, and walks over to one. He peers outside, and sees the rain drops falling to the ground below him. He stretches a bit, then kind of goes into a stunned state.

“That was a crazy dream.” Paul says to himself. “Those people looked just like my family.” He ponders out loud. “Who knows?” He asks himself.

Paul leaves the window, and walks over to his filing cabinet. He opens a drawer and comes back out with the HIW folder. He sets it on his desk, and presses a button on his phone.

“Okay, go ahead and send him in.” Paul says.

A few seconds later, a knock comes at the door, and the door then opens. Michael Pearson from HIW walks in, carrying his briefcase, and wearing a very expensive looking suit. He walks over to Paul and extends his hand, offering a handshake. Paul accepts, and shakes firmly, before releasing, and sitting at his desk.

“Well, Mike, the time as come. I have the new contract for us to sign. And as soon as it’s done, you can legally air your show on TAN.” Paul says.

“Good deal, because I think we want to do a show tomorrow.” Michael says enthusiastically.

“It’ll be an open spot, so if you want to you can.” Paul says while pulling the contract out of the folder.

Paul sets the contract on the desk in front of Michael, who begins to look through it. He reaches the end and nods in approval. He then takes the pen from Paul’s hand, and begins to sign his name, one page at a name. Finally, after what seems like fourteen or fifteen signatures, he hands the pen back to Paul, who then begins to sign his name. He signs the same amount Michael did, and then looks up at his client.

“All right, that’s all there is to it. Congratulations.” Paul says with a smile.

“Thank you very much, Mr. Harrington. We won’t disappoint you.” Michael says, standing up.

Michael then walks to the door, and leaves, while another man walks in. Paul files the contract and all the other files away, and then sees this man. The man is about 6 foot three inches, and looks to be in his fifties. He is dressed in a suit almost identical to Paul’s, and has a pair of glasses on his face.

“Your secretary told me just to come in. I am your new Vice President of Public Relations.” The man says.

“Oh, welcome to TAN. I’ve heard quite a lot about you, but your name slips my mind at the moment.” Paul says.

“Richard Bell.” He replies.

“Richard, that’s right. So, how has your first day been so far?” Paul asks.

“Great. Everyone is real nice, and helpful too.” Richard replies.

“Yeah, we have very friendly employees here. Hey, just to ask, what did you do to your finger and face?” Paul asks.

By now, Paul has noticed a scar on Richards right cheek, and that the mans right index finger only reaches to the middle knuckle.

“Oh, nothing. Childhood accidents.” Richard replies.

“Same one?” Paul wonders.

“Yeah, I had a tractor tire fall onto me. The tire crushed my finger, and a rock lodged in the tread got my cheek. They had to amputate the finger, and I got seven staples in my cheek.” Richard explains.

“Ouch. Well, I am really sorry to rush you, but I’ve got just a few other things to do before the day is over.” Paul explains.

“Not a problem, I understand.” Richard says.

Paul shakes Richard’s hand, and walks him over to the door, and out with him. Richard gets onto the elevator, and Paul looks over at Victoria, who looks back. Suddenly, Paul shakes his head, and rubs his eyes.

“What’s wrong, Paul?” Victoria asks.

“I just had the strangest feeling of déjà vu.” He says.

“Oh, well maybe you should go back and lay down for a while longer.” She offers.

“Okay, I will. Hold all my calls. Thanks.” Paul replies.

Paul then walks back into his office, and lays on the couch. He stares at the ceiling, and closes his eyes. Not too long after, his snores are heard, as the rain begins to fall even harder, and hitting the windows.

Blackness.
