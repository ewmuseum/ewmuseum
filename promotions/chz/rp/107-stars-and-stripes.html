Stars and Stripes
'American Hero' Ryan Contra

The scene fades into a fairly busy hospital. Specifically the Emergency Room. Ryan, our newly acquired wrestling trainee along with one of the senior nurses were taking their break. All were in scrubs- this day was nothing. So far on Ryan's shift they had nothing serious. But the day was far from over. In any event Ryan wasn't head surgeon, he actually was supposed to watch and pay attention when there was a code blue. But regular chart checkups, and minor hazards were his free terrain. Ryan was allowed to handle many non severe cases, and many exams and such. Ryan had a pelvic exam in five minutes once he was off break and wasn't worried. There was nothing to it. Nurses and doctors all took their break on the thirteenth floor. The senior nurse had just taken a place with Ryan.

Ryan- Yeah I wonder how well the blood donation is doing? I was thinking of stopping down there during my break but I'm just not going to have time. My stomach had OTHER ideas.

Senior Nurse- I hear you there.*She said taking a large bite from her sandwich* But I heard their doing fairly well. You know this year's theme? Don't Be a Chicken, Donate!

Ryan Contra- Who the hell thinks of these catch phrases? Well I hope they didn't get paid!

Senior Nurse- Hahahaha. They got Marcus running around in an actually bird suit!

Ryan Contra- Poor unlucky bastard. With today's heat, he'll be fried chicken in no time. Though I actually heard we'll be getting rain this evening. IF.. you'll believe that!

Senior Nurse- I've heard that too. *Ryan get's up with his tray and starts to head for the door* We can only hope!!

They wave good bye as Ryan heads back to the ER. Punching in Ryan had already checked the charts. A female, teenager, had come in with a swollen ankle. It needed x-rays but O'rien was pretty sure it wasn't broken. Looking over at the Triage Desk an elderly women was trying to be placed in a wheelchair by paramedics. Walking over to help out Ryan noticed that the older women was in a drunken stupor.

Women- Damn, sons of bit-*Neither Ryan nor the paramedics can believe the words coming out of this women.*I isn't hurt!

Ryan Contra- *Facing the paramedic* So what's with her. Transfer from upstairs right?

Paramedics- Yeah, they're too busy upstairs. She was diagnosed with a dislocated shoulder

Just then the older women nearly collapsed, but was safely caught by the paramedics, and then sat in the wheelchair. Ryan eyed them, and then they nodded and left the room. But as they left the room the big yellow chicken walked in through the big double doors. Followed closely by administration and press. Ryan leaned against the triage desk and watched as the bird made his way to the elderly women. Ryan glanced over to the nurse and then back to the group. The ridiculous chicken costume- which looked like the bird from Sesame Street- along with the pack of people following made it's way to the women sitting in the wheel chair. The Bird than patted the drunk on the shoulder

Bird- Hi!

The women, in her drunken stupor, looked up in confusion. Ryan took a step forward, knowing darn well that nobody knew the state the women was in.

Women- I've waited two hours for an orthopedic surgeon, and all I get is a damn chicken!!

What followed next was utter, and total ackward silence. But was one or two seconds, seemed like minutes, one of the administration ushered them onto the next department. Ryan laughed then wheeled the women into the waiting room. The rest of the day was standard ER. Unpredictable. There were two cases of multiple shot wounds. very messy. Ryan's day at work came to an end, as it always has. After going to his locker, and getting into some clean clothes Ryan made his way outside. Almost to his car Ryan felt the coldness of a single drop of rain hit his shoulder. For a moment Ryan stopped, motionless, expressionless. Looking up for some kind of signal. A loud titanic roar was Ryan's answer. Then the rain came, almost like the thunder itself was a command to the single rain drops to fall to earth. The sky was still the same old plain gray.
It was raining like it had rained that day. In the ER, you could never look at the people, as living breathing people. You have to see them as patients. That's what they are anyway. But, when it hits close to home- all that bullshit goes right out the window.

Flashback (wheeeee)
Ryan's face was grave, and tired. Ryan was in a small room, which was occupied with small time furniture and snack machines. It over looked the great city of Chicago. As the rain started to trickle down the windows, his face neither wavered nor change in expression. The rain could be heard, almost as a few thuds here and there. But in a seconds time it sounded like someone had turned on a shower. Still, Ryan stayed the same. Without animation. As time goes by you wonder if Ryan is okay- alive even. Ryan blinked- showing a sign of life. This morning had been particularly rough. It was ryan's third month as a medical intern. A father had been driving his motorcycle with his two year old son strapped on back. The father, an alcoholic lost control going over a set of railroad tracks. The father died instantly hitting the pavement. Peeling the two year old boy off the cement the police found out that the son was still alive. So rolling into the ER, was a mush of flesh, but a heartbeat. Which left enough purpose for the rest of them. While Ryan watched. Watch their helpless efforts. Watch as the doctor told the remote mother, that of both deaths.

intercom: Ryan Contra please report to level one ER immediately.

Ryan's nonchalant daze was soon broken as he left the room and headed down to the ER. On break, what would they need a rookie intern for? Must be important Ryan thought as he entered the double doors. The atmosphere was trouble, medical technicians and nurses were all pacing about, doing their job. One of the nurses, seeing Ryan, walked over to him and laid out the situation.

Nurse- Ambulance, 123 is coming in, what've heard is this. The parents were skeptical over a cough this little girl had been having, and now she's stopped breathing all together. She's about two. You need to pay close attention.

Ryan knew the routine all too well. Just watch, that's how it always was. The crew were loading the girl into room thirteen from the ambulance ramp. O'brien was waiting was a full medical staff. Ryan walked in just as the little girl on the stretcher was being ushered in. Ryan stepped back to get a glimpse of the girl. Freezing up, ryan couldn't breath. This little girl was his neighbor's kid, they'd carpool with his little girl to day care.

Doctor- Ryan, get the anesthetic. Ryan- goddammit!

The nurse rushed past ryan, who was still in his frozen gaze, to get the needed anesthetic. Rushing back, everything to Ryan was so weird. Ryan watched in slow motion as the doctor, and nurses, lost the life of the little girl. Ryan wasn't alive for that moment- like in a coma. What if that had been his little girl? Ryan wanted to consult his neighbors, bill and mary. After crying with them Ryan was off work. Also suspended for three days for failure to cooperate in the Emergency Room. Once home, after picking up his daughter from day care, he put her to bed.

Ryan Contra- I love you, it will always be me and you, okay? I promise their won't be anyone to come between us.Before shutting the light Ryan fell to his knee, his face crushed with the thought of him losing her. Letting himself go, his tears flowed as the rain did early on this day. Ryan couldn't breath as he doubled over, weeping in his hands. He never felt so helpless. There's the 'american hero' Ryan Contra, emotionally torn and beaten as the scene fades.

Ryan snaps out of his emotional daydream only to find himself going down i-75 and nearly home. Getting off on his exit he noticed he was near empty. Pulling into a sloppy mobile, he creped up to pump 7. As Ryan walked into the gas station his recurring nostalgia, was shot to the back of his mind by the emptiness of his stomach. Stocking up on the 'quickie-mart' goods Ryan paid and made for a quick leave. Going for the exit, an equally impatient patron made her way through the exit into the store. Without warning the two made their inevitable collision. With mini hot dogs, cheesy nachos, and super slurpee's sent flying the two our left in disarray.

Ryan Contra- Jesus- what the hell?!

Lyndsey- oh my god! Im sorry?

Ryan contra- you did kind of came charging through the exit door.

Lyndsey- Geeze it looks like I ruined your three course meal. I don't know if I should, take you to dinner, or buy you a snickers bar.

The two talking still in front of the exit. But with the option of dinner coming up the two both started heading out of the mobile.

Ryan Contra- Dinner? Now, that's something I haven't had in some time. So will you be taking me out then?

Lyndsey was in capri pants, brown sandals, and a nike t-shirt. Ryan, in a silk t-shirt, and khaki pants, brown work boots. starter cap.

Lyndsey- Well, if your mommy will let you. IT will be my special treat okay? Did ruin your fine feast. Just follow me, I'll take you to one of my personal favorite Deli.

Ryan Contra- This is way unexpected. Of course though, in a good way I mean. But before I go, I gotta call someone.

Lyndsey- You have to call someone do you? I don't like the sounds of that. By the way, my name is Lyndsey. Your not, by any chance, calling your wife, are you?

Ryan Contra- Hahaha. My name is Ryan Contra. No I won't be calling my wife, nope, I am free. But I will be calling my daughter, Jaime. Will you wait here for me?

Lyndsey nods, and we aren't able to notice if she's effected by Ryan having a daughter or not. Though this is where we leave off, there will always be more to come. So what will be in store for our American Hero next? Time will tell. Will it no longer be just Ryan and Jaime, like he promised, or does fate have different ideas?
