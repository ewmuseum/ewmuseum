Living it Up!
Written: 8/12/02

The life of a girl that could not have life better then she has it. Angel summers are now living the life the way a superstar should. After winning her match last Wednesday she has opened up to the country of Canada. Angel is beginning to see artifacts with her picture on it, such as T-shirts, hats and posters. She has quickly become a star for both the male and female species.

The women love her because she is a role model to them; a girl then can compete with the men. Does not back down and speaks her mind. And the males love her due to her beautiful looks and few because of her talent, very few.

{-Begin Feed-}

The feed begins at the apartment of Angel Summers. A three-story brown bricked building, with a white wicket fence surrounding the apartment building. A beautiful listing indeed, with freshly cut grass, it looks like a place where every bird on the block would love to be. Three floors up is the home of Angel, as she is just retrieving her shoes as she is ready to leave for the Toronto mall as she has been offered a autograph signing to the Toronto citizens, she gladly excepted. Angel just needs to make an appearance, take a few pictures with some fans, sign some pictures and shirts, and leave. She figured it would be a quick day, but little she knew.

Angel gets out of her building and into her car and begins making her way to the mall. A quick ten-minute drive and she are pulling into the reserved parking spot she was given for doing this for the mall. A great highlight moment for the mall. Great advertisement to have a star a new young star to spend time with her fans. It is always good to have a star that loves her fans and takes time to show he/she cares.

Angel begins walking from her car, through the parking lot. Not being stopped by anyone, which was a first in a little while, well since the card. As she enters, she views about a thousand screaming fans, as she takes a few steps back as she is overwhelmed so many people that are fans. She was surprised that so many people viewed CHZ, and at that time her love for CHZ expanded.

Two casual dressed women that she was greeted by at the door guided her through the huge line-up to the front, where a large brown table with one seat, seated in front of it. Angel takes a seat, and then she brushes her hair back, which makes the men skip a breath. Angel then looks up and glances at the top of the line, as a ten year old little girl, with a large smile across her young beautiful face. The little girl holds a poster of Angel, which Angel was also, surprised all these souvenirs were sold, as the first card was only 4 days ago. The girl steps up as the red rope that held the line was removed. Angel politely greets her, and begins the signing, it seems it is going to be a long days work. With the little girls, she signs takes a picture and some she kisses on the cheek. And the young males she does the same. Older people generally got a picture, plus a handshake. So the day went by quick, she was having so much fun she lost track of time, it was closing time, so they wrapped up the last few fans and Angel then slowly made her way home to bed.

{-The Next Day-}

The day was still young, Angel is on her way to the gym where she does her normal work out. As it will be her last before she heads off to the airport, which will be her first airplane ride. She has about a 2 hours plane ride to Edmonton from her home in Toronto. Her boarding time was three o’clock, so being a organized person she is, she was there by two o’clock. By the time it was boarding time she was nearly asleep on the lobby chair she was so comfortably grooved into. The loud call over the P.A woke her up in a hurry.

“ Now boarding all flights to Edmonton at Gate 17.”

She quickly grabbed her gym bag and suitcase and began walking towards the gate, she dropped her bags and tagged them off and made her way to the stairs of the airplane. Her first experience was a good one, the airline played “Austin Powers” on the way which made the trip quicker then it really was. When she arrived she was once again greeted with praise, as she felt like she was being spoon-fed. These woman, carry her bags for her, call the cab for her and go over every minor detail possible. Finally she reaches her hotel room, the plane ride has made her exhausted so she has decided to go to the stadium in the morning to meet the press and get in tuned on the coming attractions on the next Wild.

{-End Feed-}
