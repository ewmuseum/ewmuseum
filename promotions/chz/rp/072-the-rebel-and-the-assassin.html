The rebel and the assassin
Written: 23/7/02

The small warehouse was rather small, smaller than you would imagine most warehouses to be. There were three big bulbs on the ceiling, all of which provided little to no light and a few wooden boxes stacked carelessly around the wooden dusty floorboards. The moonlight however was the strongest source of light, and he grinned at it through the small window , like the way you grin at the girl you have a crush on in school. It was a warm acknowledging welcoming grin, and one he didn't use often.


* ** * * * * * *

'I think its about time I introduced myself properly'

He turned to face the brunette who he had met in the small bar. Drunken middle aged unemployed men clustered around her like she was a chew toy. She stood out so much in there. Yet she seemed interested in Axl the most. She was very attractive to say the least, and he would have been foolish not to have gone with her to somewhere more quiet when she offered.

Pausing for a moment the tall brunette wiped her hand slowly across her mouth. 'My name's Roxanne. I'm an assassin.' She lowered her eyes shamefully, awaiting the response of the young man stood before her.

His long black hair fell loosely around his shoulders, and he grinned a little at the somewhat blunt and startling revelation.

'Well that's fascinating Roxanne, its really cool' He gushed enthusiastically at the lustful seductive specimen before him'

'No! No its not, don't you understand. I've been hired to kill you'

He laughed in disbelief and placed a strong firm hand on the shoulder of her black leather jacket. '

Oh, you nearly had me fooled then. I almost believed you. I mean, you sure as hell dress like an assassin. Look at you, all in black. Leather jacket and leather trousers!'

Bemused, Roxanne looked down at her attire. She didn't think her clothing was that obvious, but now that he mentioned it, she could see what he meant. She did look a bit stereotypical. Making a mental note to re-assess her wardrobe at a later date, she returned to the task at hand.

'No, no Axl I'm not joking'

'Is this some sort of kinky roleplay that........... how do you know my name, I didn't tell you it'

She looked at him square in the face, her face was so tender and calm it made this tale even more hard to believe, surely this woman could not be capable of harming a living thing, let alone killing it, let alone killing a human!

'Work it out Axl' She spoke slowly.

'Do you think I would spend my evenings in a seedy bar like that surrounded by perverts. NO! I only entered there because I followed you going in there. I've been following you even since you left Maine. I've been tracking you down for the past week!'

She took a slow deep breath.

'I really am a hired killer and I really have been hired to kill you.' She gently brushed his hand from her shoulder. 'I've been stalling, that's all. I should have done it days ago.'

Sighing heavily, she ran her fingers through her long dark hair. Spotting a wooden crate nearby, she sat down slowly. Never taking her eyes off him for an instant. She couldn't believe he hadn't suspected anything when she'd led him into the dilapidated warehouse. He probably thought he was going to get laid. He only looked about twenty. All full of hormones and urges.

'You see, this isn't as much fun as it sounds. This job has been getting real stale these past few months'

He slowly leant against the wall, now the realisation of what was occurring was slowly began to sink into his emotions, but he still needed some convincing, and he slowly sat down on the wooden floorboards.

'Track the body, kill it, dispose of it. Move on. Track the body, kill it, dispose of it. It's always the same old bullshit!'

'Then don't kill me. Who is it that wants me dead, anyway?'

He sounded almost annoyed now, not frightened. The reality of his situation wasn't sinking in, obviously.

'Axl, your a professional. You know I can't disclose that information, though I refuse to believe you don't know why people want you killed'

He sat there, looking around the room thinking.

'Ummm...my mind'

She nodded slowly and cautiously.

'Bingo! Your too dangerous. The mafia, the KGB, triad after triad would want you as their employee. Even the US government are likely to be at the stage of wanting you as an employee. Yet it's not even these organisations. FEDERATIONS! You'll be unique to say the least, and a ratings grabber for sure. Federations are cutthroat these days Axl, more cutthroat than you know. These days they don't take an approach of trying to sign you over. They take you out of the equation.'

Axl looked at her with shock and disbelief. This getting way too much for him it appeared.

'Yet..... the organisation that employs me sees you as a liability, and liability's are expendable. Therefore you are expendable. That's where I come in. I've been sent to dispose of you. You are a threat to them know, and if employed by another organisation you are an even bigger threat. They are merely playing the safe game and making sure you do not become a future threat. Your mind is one if not the most manipulative and dangerous one on the planer right now. You can manipulate just about anyone. You have to be eliminated.'

Beginning to regret having said anything she rose slowly from her seat. She dug her hand into her jacket and fished out a pistol.

'I'm not usually this cruel. It's just that I'm bored with the work'

He never moved but watched silently, eyes widening as she gesticulated wildly and waved her gun around.

'I just wanted a bit of variety.' She continued. 'I wanted to approach it differently for a change. I had a couple of idea's, but I couldn't get the rubber hose I needed for the first. As for the second, well, I won't go into details but it would have been far too bloody.'

'Stop. Please! Look you don't have to kill me!' Jason begged. The reality of the situation now came crashing home to him.

Roxanne felt a glimmer of pity for him. He looked so scared. This had been a bad idea. It wasn't healthy to fraternise with the victims. This would serve her right for wanting to make things more interesting.

'Couldn't you just pretend that you killed me.' He looked up at her. His eyes pleading with her. 'You know, fake it.'

'It's not that easy. You see, I have to take your head back with me.'

'What!'

'I know, it's not a standard request. It's a bit gruesome for my liking.'

Axl slumped down onto the floor. Roxanne figured it must be a bit of a shock to be told that someone wants your head. She felt a bit stupid for changing her routine. Decapitation was enough variety for anyone. Why did she have to go and make it more difficult.

'There must be some way out of this... Couldn't you make up some excuse. Tell them you weren't able to take my head with you?'

'Not really. Even if I claimed that your head was badly damaged I'd still have to take it back with me. I could probably convince them if I had another head, one with the face missing. But I would still have to find one that resembles you. That's not as easy as you'd think, and I have a strict policy of not killing anyone who doesn't have a contract out on them. So, no. I can't do that.'

Axl began sobbing.

'Please. There must be some way. Couldn't you take something else back instead. Like my ring.' He lifted his hand in desperation. Trying to show her his signet ring. 'It's got my initials on it.'

'No. I can't take back something that belongs to you. I have to take you. You know. A piece of you.'

She sat down again, trying to think. The gun still in her hands.

'Maybe I could take your hand. The one with the ring on it.'

'Oh God no! Please! There must be another way!'

He was almost wailing. Roxanne felt sorry for him now. This was degrading. He shouldn't be spending his last few minutes on his knees crying to his killer. She should put a stop to it.

Slowly she stood and motioned for him to do the same. He wouldn't get up off the floor so she had to pull him up by his jacket. He stood before her, shaking and crying into his hands.

'This just isn't working. I shouldn't have said anything. I'm sorry.'

She'd learnt her lesson. Keep things familiar. Don't complain. Just do the job. It made it much easier for everyone.

Sighing loudly, she smoothed his arm with as much compassion as she could muster while aiming the gun at his head.

'Noooo, p-p-pleaseee, I-I -I don't wanna die!!'

His sobs were torture to her ears. She finally realised she wanted out, Wanted out of this job period. After this mission, she would quit.

'Can I, can you just open the window for me. Please, just one final breath of fresh air, that's all I want'

Who was she to argue? She was going to quit after this, and wanted to make this last one as pain free as possible. She would probably even give him head if it would make him calm down.

'Ok, quick though'

He nodded whilst grinning slightly, and rushed over to the window. She watched as he climbed a few boxes and opened it. As he stuck his head out and took several deep breaths, she began to think about her future. She would quit after this one, for sure. Then maybe she would go back to school. Ben had already asked her to marry him, and she truly loved him. The two were both assassins, and they were both considering of quitting this job to get married soon anyway. Yes, after this, her life would be good again. Her life would be back to normal.

Then .......... it happened.

* * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * *

Just he always , Roland attacked quickly and suddenly, allowing hardly no time for reaction and no emotional room for reflex. He clawed at her face. Such a beautiful eagle he was, Axl's one true companion in life.

Roxanne stumbled around foolishly and clumsily, trying to thrust Roland off her face, which was a useless last act of defiance and defence. Axl watched grinning satisfyingly. He had spotted Roland perched on the windowsill outside, and he knew his last hope of escaping here would be by using the element of surprise and using Roland. He cunningly tricked Roxanne into letting him go to the window, allowing him to summon Roland and to attack

Eventually Roxanne fell to the floor, and Roland flew from her face and soared through the air and perched himself on Axl's shoulder. Roxanne lay on the wooden floor, blood streaming from the corners of her eyes and deep bloodied claw marks and scratches inhabiting her face. She sobbed a little as Axl came walking casually over to her.

'Oh my dear. How foolish you were to fall for my crocodile tears '

She looked up at him saddened and grief stricken. The hunter had know become the hunted.

'I knew from the moment I saw you, the moment you offered me to take part in a midnight walk with you to a secret rendezvous where you would try and make me believe we would indulge in sexual intercourse that you were after me, and yes dear, I knew it was because of my mind'

Through muffled pain filled sobs Roxanne tried to speak. Her voice was light and coarse, weak around the edges but strong enough to be understandable.

'What are you...'

'What am I going to do with you? Well let me see, the possibilities are endless. Killing you with my mind would be obvious and predictable, seeing as I am the master manipulator. However my dear my powerful and dark mind is not my main concern in life. I do not bother with it and take large amounts of interest in it, but obviously you do, as well as your organization'

'Axl....'

'You were going to kill me and it is only right now that I should return the favour'

'This was my last mission!'

He looked down at her with keen interest and intrigue.

'You were to be my last. I was going to quit after you. That is why I was so hesitant with you. Me... I was going to get married after this. Quit the agency and live a life empty of secrecy and assassination'

Axl seemed to calm down as she spoke softly to him with her sweet and tender voice.

'I just want to leave here and live. We can do what you said, I'll just give them your ring. I'll make up some excuse. If you let me live I can make sure no organisation will ever hunt for you again. Even if they haven't already they will. I can stop that, I can give you some security'

Axl seemed to agree with her statements and they did seem reasonable and logic to him. Letting her live would be wise, and he had grown quite fond of the young feisty vixen.

'OK.... I'll do what is wise'

He knelt before her and began to help her up. As he did he looked at Roland, He seemed to glare at him stupidly, and then shook his head from left to right. Was this just a twitch, or was he trying to tell him this was wrong. Wither way it didn't matter, Axl had already made his mind up on what he was going to do.

* * * * * * ** * * * ***

The sickening sound of her neck breaking made Axl grimace as he did it. He then dropped her body as it fell limp to the ground, making a faint thud as it did so. Roland then nodded once, almost approvingly at the act just committed.

'Shame, she seemed like a nice woman............. its always the nice ones'

Axl then left the warehouse with Roland perched firmly on his shoulder. The master manipulator who had a mind powerful enough to turn political members into his personal lapdog, escaped death here tonight. Some described his mind to the way it was portrayed by Anthony Hopkins in Silence Of The Lambs. The way he could talk people into killing themselves. However he was no Hannibal Lector. He was The Deep Dark Demented Rebel... Axl Young.
