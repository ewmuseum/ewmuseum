Rushing For Gold
Written: 23/7/02

Each athlete wants to leave the mark of his will, his signature, on important acts he touches. This is not the voice of ego but of the human spirit, rising up and declaring that it has something to contribute to the solution of the hardest problems, no matter how demanding!

Oakview, the home of the newest tag sensations to the Primetime Wrestling Academy, Is receiving a scorching hot day, with not a hint of a cloud in sight. The bachelor home that stands one story high, completely dark blue shutters, which stood out, as the house was a red brick home. The mailbox at the end of the driveway is gold, and in big black letter’s stating “Garcia”. As the feed then makes it’s way into the house throw the big red door and into the petite size living room that consisted of a plaid coach, A mid-sized television and a weight set, in the corner.

The two were both sprawled in the coach, in plaid pajama pants and Manny wearing a white muscle shirt as Tito without a shirt. They sit in front of the television, watching some NFL football. It’s usually an every Sunday thing that the two do together, Stay home all day and watch football. But on this particular day, they were to receive an interview, on their upcoming arrival in the PWA. So they awaited the reporter, and they continued watching football.

About an hour later, they were both in the kitchen that consisted of a glass table in the far corner and all of the kitchen appliances, They sit on their wooden stools and eat some cereal, as they have not lost there inner childhood eating habits. They both were now finishing their third bowl, the loud sound of the doorbell was erupting, and both then slowly got out of the wooden stools and made there way to the door.

Tito then opens the wooden door, they both look on to a six-foot, 150lbs man, wearing a blue sports jacket, and blue jeans. Tito invited the reporter in, they all then were seated in the living room. The reporter and Tito took a seat on the coach, Manny fetched a stool from the kitchen placed in along side them. Then the introductory came.

“Hello, my name is Ron, just like to thank you both, for accepting this interview. We will just start off with you both explaining yourselves and then I will move in on some question, of my own.”

Both Tito and Manny address the man with a nod.
__________________________________
-Begin Interview-

“ This is Ron Taylor, sitting here in a small town in Ohio, where the newest group of tag team wrestlers from the PWA grew up, As I am in the Garcia boys home hoping for some insight.”

Both men seem to look confident, they both adjust their positions in their seats and get settled in. Tito begins speaking first.

“ Glad to have entered the professional world of wrestling, I believe myself and my brother will fare well, We both are very aware of the skill needed to be here, And we will do all that is necessary to get where we need to go. Although we are quite happy where we are, we are very willing t travel with the PWA and be where we have to be, to win some matches and hopefully soon enough make our way up the federation chain.

Follows Manny.

“ Like my brother said, we are qualified to be here, we may be little, but a definite advantage is our speed, and agility to the sport. We plan to enter the wrestling world with a bang, We have not yet been able to scout the other tag teams here in the PWA, I am sure they are ready just as much as we are, to get in the ring and prove ourselves to the bigger federations. Because of our size we both had to work a little harder to get where we are today, I have to thank our teacher from Cyprus Creed for mentioning us to the federation, and we hope to make him proud, by achieving our goals.”

Ron quickly steps in.

“ Well it is said, that size is everything in the world of wrestling, In your case you need to work around other angles, like your said, your speed and agility will be your key advantage. My question is this, how do you believe you will fair against other opponents, your are both in-experienced to the world of wrestling.”

Tito replies.

“Perhaps the single most important element in mastering the techniques, and tactics of wrestling, is experience. But once you have the fundamentals, acquiring the experience is a matter of time. The quicker you get there, the shorter the pain. We already have the tactics it’s just experience we are to achieve which will all happen in due time. I definitely believe we will be the next to superstars that enter the tag team world, and by our standards, no match is out of the question, size and all.”

Ron then adds.

“ What goes through your heads, as you enter the world of wrestling, what do you believe you must achieve to win a match.”

Manny cuts in.

“To succeed...You need to find something to hold on to, something to motivate you, something to inspire you, all you need to do is, Think big, believe big, act big, and the results will be big. I just think to myself, Somebody has to be the hero. It might as well be me, or in our case, us.”

Ron then smiles and responds.

“ Nothing better then two little guys going into the wrestling world with a positive attitude, and an act to win, You both will be very liked because of this.”

Tito leans back and replies.

“We are not walking into this federation thinking were failing. We are walking in thinking I am confident that we can make it work. If we sat here, and listen to everyone else tell us that I cannot do it then, obviously, we wouldn't be here.”

Ron then closes with a final say.

“ Well I thank you both again for accepting this interview, Wish you both the best of luck in the future, And I am sure you will fair well, in the PWA.”

--Interview Ends—

Tito and Manny then both walk Ron to the door, Ron then puts his shoes on, then slowly makes his way to his car, and drives away. The two then turn back on the television and assume there position back on the plaid couch, and enjoy the rest of the day, in front of the television.
