A Night In The Bar
Written: July 10th

Skillz leaned back
slowly, in the booth, his
head cocked to the
side, as he looked at
his friend, mentor, and
OSW MET Tournament
Partner. The Masked
Mullet. Well, not really
The Masked Mullet, but
his alter ego: Clem
Jankins.

Clem: S'weird. Jesse
Ramey seems ta be
poppin' up more then a
Prairie Dog, in a
pasture.

Skillz nodded slowly,
taking a sip of his
coke-a-cola.

Skillz: I know, yo?
CCW, CHZ, and FSW?

Clem: CCW is closed now.

Clem sighed, and put his chin down on the bar top. The ring crew worker, wasn't really sure how he felt, CCW for the longest time was his home, even before he became a
wrestler.

Skillz: Yo, dude. You
aight?

Clem just gave a slow
nod.

Skillz: Yo, ya gotta get
stoked man. I gotta
match for CHZ here
soon, against that
Deamont prick again.
He's such a wigga.
Dude has two
girlfriends, you think
he's tryin' a bit too hard.
They do say the best
defense is a good
offense, the guy just
doesn't wanna admit
his homosexual
tendencies.

Clem, not really paying
attention, just gave
another nod.

Skillz glanced at his
friend, and sighs.
Slowly, he pulls his
hand back, and socks
Clem right on the arm.

Skillz: YO! PAY
ATTENTION!

Clem blinked, as he
rubbed his assaulted
limb, grumbling under
his breath.

Clem: Ah' was!

Skillz: Aight then, what did I say about Race
Alexander?

Clem: You said he..
was.. A jerk?

Skillz groaned, and
punches Clem in the
arm again.

Skillz Nice try, but no. I was talkin' bout....

A sultry young waitress waltz by, her hips in a little waggle on display for some of the other patrons of the bar. Of course, Skillz being in his own little world, slides from his seat, following after her with a strange grin.

'So, sweet thang, what's your sign?' He whispered into her ear as he got close enough.

She groaned, and shoved him away.

'Dag yo, why ya gotta hurt a brotha like that?!
You ain't that fly
anyways! YEAH! You
heard me!'

Of course, by the time
he said that, it had
been five minutes
since she left the bar,
and he had been
watching the sway of
her hips drooling
before he could think of
that snappy response.

Clem: Go well?

Skillz: Shut up.

Clem: So, who else ya got in this?

Skillz: Tyler Davidson..
he was in CCW, did ya
know the brotha?

Clem: Nah, he was
only around for a few
weeks, before he
disappeared to go
back to either Big
Money, or start
sompthan new. I think.
He ditched the New
Strangers, and his
buddy Scorpion.

Skillz: So, you don't know, nothin', Yo?

Clem nodded, then
shrugged, picking up
his glass and drinking
his milk.

Clem: Pretty much.

Skillz sighed, and lowered his head. He was going into yet another match, where he knew none of his opponents or partners, really. Let's hope he fares better then he did in his first match.

Skillz: So, dude, you're gonna help me train
right?

Clem: Sure. We gotta
train for OSW too. It
would do ya good to
spar against the last
CCW Lightweight
Champion!

Skillz: You're still
spoutin' that eh?

Clem: Yeah, I'm proud of mah accomplishment! It means a lot ta'h me!

Skillz: You beat up two girls for it.

Clem: Hey, those girls are tough..

Skillz: Like Lilith Eh?

Clem: ....I hate you.

Skillz: Good.

Clem glared at his compatriat, and
sighed.

Clem: Hurry up Skillz, e
got some super heroin'
ta do, tonight.

Skillz: Dude, ya sure?
Dag, you seem so
frickin' down, yo?

Clem: The world's safety, comes before any feelin's I may have, mah young friend.

Clem stands slowly, finishing the rest of his milk, he walks towards the bathroom, slowly pulling the Luchadore mask from under his shirt. Skillz nodded slowly, hanging outside the bathroom, so as not to look...
funny. Training on the
streets, was the best.
You learn your
reactions, you learn
what it takes to survive.
Despite the goofiness,
it was actually doing
him good. Though,
he'd never admit it to
Clem.

He stood, and waited,
wondering about his
match, about his life,
and completely
forgetting that he had to
pay the rent tommarow.
