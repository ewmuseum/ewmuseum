Moving To Canada Part 2
Written: 7/29/02

We join up with Jake again as he nears the border of the United States and Canada. His van is speeding down the highway followed closely by the moving van carrying all of his belongings. Inside the van Jake is listening to the radio via a small boom box he has running off of batteries due to the fact his car radio was stolen in that strange episode with the bar cleaning crew member, Bubba. He is now clad in a pair of black basketball shorts with a red Nike check mark by the left knee, a black t-shirt promoting the band Sum 41, and a pair of black flip-flops. The early morning fog is just lifting as Jake passes by the Buffalo Bison’s, a triple A team for the Cleveland Indians, stadium. He should be in Canada in about one hour. He continues on down the highway listening to an early morning talk show on the radio. The talk show begins to bore Jake and he turns the dial to check out all of his options. Nothing really appeals to him, so he just turns the radio off and cruises down the highway to only the sounds of traffic of many New Yorkers heading off to another day at work. Jake looks down at the watch he has strapped to his wrist; it’s eight thirty in the morning.

“Hmm, I should be in Canada by about ten,” says Jake aloud even though there is no one in the van with him to hear it. He continues on down the highway at about eighty miles per hour. He is flying past other drivers on the road that are obeying the speed limit. He travels like this for another half hour when he pulls off of the highway and onto smaller roads. He travels down these roads for about five minutes before pulling over at a gas station. He pulls the van up in front of the gas station and then comes to a stop. He puts the van in park and then climbs out. The moving van soon follows him into the gas station. The driver pulls up next to Jake and rolls down the window.

“What are you doing, you we lost or something?” asks the moving van driver with a semi-worried look on his chubby face.

“No, we aren’t lost, I just need to take a piss, that’s all,” says Jake as he turns from the driver and heads into the gas station to get the bathroom key from the gas station attendant. He then heads back out of the gas station with bathroom key in hand. He heads around to the back of the building where the bathroom is located, unlocks the door, and heads in. About ten minutes later he comes walking out.

“Whew, I feel five pounds lighter,” says Jake as he closes the bathroom door behind himself. He then walks back around to the entrance to the gas station and heads inside. He walks out a few moments later with a bag of Fritos and a two-liter of Mountain Dew. He opens the driver’s door, sets the food and drink on the passenger’s seat then gets in the driver’s seat. He starts up the van and pulls out of the gas station parking lot closely followed by the moving van. They continue on down these smaller roads for another half hour until they come to the Peace Bridge, an enormous bridge on which you can cross from the United States and into Canada or vise versa. They travel down the Peace Bridge until they come to the American customs officers. They are asked a few questions and are allowed to pass by without a problem. In between the United States border, which they just crossed, and the Canadian border, which they are soon to cross, Jake spots a store called the Duty Free. He pulls over into their parking lot, parks his van, and gets out. The moving van driver pulls up next to him

“No I don’t need to go to the bathroom again, I just want to go in this store. I remember going in here when I was a kid when me and my parents came up to Canada,” says Jake. He then turns around and walks towards the store. The man in the moving van can be heard muttering something under his breath as he opens up his door and gets out of the van. He then runs to catch up with Jake as Jake opens the door to the store and heads inside. The store boasts a huge selection of alcohol at extremely low prices. Jake and the driver of the moving van look around in the store for about twenty minutes. Jake ends up leaving with a bottle of vodka and a twenty-four pack of Labat Blue. They head back out to their vehicles and driver away towards the Canadian border. They reach the border in a matter of seconds, but have to wait for at least an hour in a long line of people heading to Canada. They finally get up to the booth where Canadian officials question them thoroughly.

“What is your name, sir?” asks a chubby Canadian official in a very rude tone of voice.

“Jake Schultz,” says Jake who is disgusted with the woman already.

“Where are you going to in Canada?” she questions in the same rude tone.

“I’m not sure yet, ma’am,” responds Jake as he looks impatiently at his watch.

“What do you mean you don’t know, you’re just going to Canada with a plan?” she asks in an increasingly annoying and snotty manner.

“Well, kind of says Jake. You see, I’m a professional wrestler and I’m moving to Canada since that’s where the federation holds most of its events. I don’t know where I’m going to live yet, but I’ll find a place,” says Jake who is starting to get really pissed off at this rude woman.

“Well, I don’t believe you, you’re going to have to pull your van over there,” says the woman as she point over to a large, tan building with a Canadian flag triumphantly flying above it.

“What the hell, what do you mean, all I’m doing is moving to Canada,” argues Jake. “I really don’t have time to mess around here lady,” he adds. It is very apparent that he is livid that this fat Canadian woman won’t let him pass through the border.

“Don’t argue with me young man, I can have you put in jail I’ll have you know,” she says. “Now pull your car over there before I have to exercise my power to do so,” she adds in a very serious manner. Jake mumbles something under his breath as he pulls over to the building she told him to go to. Immediately people in Canadian customs uniforms file out of the building and begin to tear Jake’s van looking for something that is not there. Then the chubby woman’s voice can be heard over someone’s walkie-talkie.

“Yeah, umm, make sure you search him too,” she says over the walkie-talkie. A man searching around in the back of the van then answers her.

“What should I do to him?” he inquires as he rips open a box of clothes Jake has packed up in the back of the van and begins to pull them out just tossing them all over the van.

“Give him a code red,” replies the fat woman over the walkie-talkie. The man looks surprised at this and unsurely responds.

“Are you sure that is really necessary?” he asks as he pulls out the last thing in the box. “We haven’t found anything at all in his van and the people looking through the moving van carrying his stuff haven’t found anything either,” he adds.

“I said give him a code red,” the woman yells. “Do it now!” she screams to the man in the back of the van.

“Okay, okay, I’ll do it, just calm down,” he says as he replaces the walkie-talkie in its resting place, a holster attached to his belt. “Sir, you’re going to have to come with me,” the man timidly says to Jake.

“What the hell, I didn’t do anything wrong, you guys didn’t find anything, why do I need to have a code red, whatever the hell that is?” Jake asks. He is furious at what they have done to all the things he had packed up, and what they have done to his van.

“Well, Bertha thinks you need a code red,” he says as he hops out of the large sliding door of the van and walks around to the driver’s side door. He opens the door and Jake climbs down out of the van.

“What is a code red anyways?” asks Jake as the two of them head towards the large tan building.

“Well, it’s a full…cavity…search,” says the man apprehensively as he reaches into his back pocket and pulls out a pair of white latex gloves. Jake walks out of the building moments later. He is walking kind of funny and is holding his ass while he walks. He walks over to his van and the man who searched him follows him.

I am so sorry about that sir, I didn’t think you had anything on you, but she insisted and I would lose my job if I didn’t do it,” says the man very apologetically.

“Get the hell away from me, and tell fat ass in the booth over there I said bye,” says Jake as he starts up his van and pulls away from the custom’s station. He drivers across the border and past a huge blue sign with the words “Welcome To Canada,” on it. Jake and the moving van travel down the Canadian highways at about ninety-five kilometers per hour. They travel like this for an hour or two until Jake sees a huge sign that reads “Storage Space Cheap.” Jake then pulls off the exit there, followed by the moving van, and they head to the storage building. Jake goes into the office and comes out moments later with a key. He pulls his van around to the back of the building and uses his new key to unlock the storage building he just rented. Him and the moving man unload most of the items from the moving van in about an hour. They leave Jake’s clothes and other necessities in the back of Jake’s van. Jake then pays the moving man the money he owes him for the trip up here.

“There is your money, and thank you so much for helping me out, I would have never gotten all of this stuff up here by myself,” says Jake as he hands over the last of the bills to the moving man.

“No problem, and I’m sorry about your misfortune back at the border, that had to suck,” says the moving man half serious and half making fun of Jake.

“Yeah, I’m never going to be the same again,” says Jake as he rubs his ass once again. Jake and the moving man then shake hands and say their good byes and the moving man gets back into his van and pulls away from the storage place to head back to the United States. Jake closes and locks up his new storage unit and then gets back into his van. He too pulls away and heads off to find a house, and to check out Canada.
