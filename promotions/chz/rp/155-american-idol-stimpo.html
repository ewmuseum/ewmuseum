American Idol Stimpo?
Written: 8/21/02

After my first tryout for a band, I had lost all hope, but then suddenly I was snatched up by two men in the shadows of my back alley, yes my back alley. My heart was beating quite fast as I stared in the eyes of these two men that I couldn't even see, but I knew were there. I tried to stop the sweat from dripping down my face and tried to breath slowly to calm my heart down. I took one last breath before I heard words being spoken in the dark of the night.

'Well, Stimpo, here we are.' The man to the right said.

He was at least 6 feet tall, maybe more. His muscles were as clear as snow in a black area even though we were in the dark area. I knew this man meant business.

'What do you mean here we are?' I asked back in curiousity.

'Your the singer right?' The man to the left replied with yet another question.

This man was shorter than the first man, almost like a sidekick. He seemed to be just as muscular though. These guys looked tough, so I thought to myself to keep for angering them.

'I am. What do you guys want?' I asked taking a large gulp down my throat.

'Friends you could say, fellow singers.' The big man to the right said flexing in the dark.

I was astonished. These huge men seemed to be some kind of body builders in their peak physical condition, were they really be singers? Perhaps they were part of a famous band, but what?

'What do you want with me?' I asked in both fear and the wanting to know.

'To be another singer with us!' The two men said in unison.

'But first, we need for you to perform the initiation.' The shorter man said.

'What's that?' I asked excitedly this time in hopes of perhaps soon joining the ranks of an unknown band!

'To perform on the great show on TV for all PoP icons, American Idol.' The left man said taking long breath in revealing the initiation

'How am I supposed to get on that?' I had so many questions to be answered.

'We already made your connections, your set to audition in the preliminaries.' Said the right man with authority.

'Then I guess I'm off then...?' I tried to sneak away, and successfully did.
I passed by my house and drove off in my car fast, far away on my way to become an American Idol!

******Scene Change - American Idol Auditions*******

“Here I am. That didn’t take long.” I said satisfied with the time travel ways of promo’s, I appeared before an amazingly long line of people in search for the same dream that I wanted and needed. I knew I had to prepare a song before this, but this was not what I had expected, I thought this would be a sinch, piece of cake job. The building was at least 30 stories tall and it seemed that all stories were buzzing with commotion.

“Excuse me, this is the tryouts for the American Idol correct?” I asked the sharp looking man in front of me wearing a nicely worn suit with his bushy goatee and short, stylish black hair gelled up with grease.

“That it is, looking to audition?” He replied with a question.

“Actually, yes and no, I’m here for an initiation for a band. But, why not become an American Idol on the way.” I told him in defense.

“American, eh? You look Mexican.” He questioned my appearance.

“I’m, I’m Mexican-American, yes, that’s it!” I replied with my great answer! I managed to fool the man, or at least confuse him enough to leave me alone.

A few hours had passed by as I stood there waiting in line as the line slowly moved. After what seemed like an eternity, I had finally gotten my way to the place where I could actually see the audition area. It was pretty much what I had expected, a big black stage with the three judges, Simon, Paula and the other guy who I forgot his name. I had just seen a woman with the most horrible voice I’ve ever heard attempt to sing Whitney Housten’s “Heartbreak Hotel.”

“Yes that was quite extraordinary singing.” Simon told the woman.

“Thank you.” The woman replied in thanks.

“Too bad it was extraordinarily bad.” Simon then bashed the woman down with his insult to her lack of abilities.

The woman shuddered a tear from her eye running down her cheek as it fell to the black stage along with all the others who did not make it as the American Idol.

“You know, your so mean to these people.” Paula complained to Simon.

“I could care less, get them some talent and I’ll stop.” Simon insulted them even more.

Paula let out a sigh as I then noticed that I was next. A man approached me and told me to walk down the aisle towards the stage. I did so as my heart started to pound faster and faster with fear and nervousness of this opportunity and at what was at stake. I climbed the little set of steps on the side of the stage as I then took the dreaded walk across the stage to the middle facing the three judges with nothing but non-emotion on their faces.

“State your name and what you’ll be singing for us.” Simon said using his UK accent just to annoy me.

“Well, my name is Brendon, but you can call me Stimpo. I’ll be performing, “Livin’ La Vida Loca” by Ricky Martin.

The judges seemed perplexed with my choice, but they played the music anyways. The trumpets sounded as crisp as ever as the words just came to me!

“She's into superstitions black cats and voodoo dolls. I feel a premonition that girl's gonna make me fall. She's into new sensations new kicks in the candle light. She's got a new addiction for every day and night.” I finished my first verse and the judges did not look happy.

“That’s quite enough.” Simon stopped the music at once.

“How did I do?” I asked curiously.

“Horrible, just flat out horrible.” Simon insulted me in his Ukish ways once again.

“Well, you know what?” I fired back, “This is a disgrace to all audition shows! This should not be American Idol, but Mexican-American Idol! You see I’m Mexican and I can shake my hips just as good as any American in here and I have the singing talent of any superstar out there! You are not going to tell me otherwise you old England prune!” I yelled in a fury.

“Prune?” Simon questioned my word judgment.

“That’s besides the point, the point is that I have more important things to worry about than this stupid showing of bad judging!” I lashed out at Simon while the other judges seemed to be asleep.

“Come my Mexican brothers! Rebel against this act of segregation of our Mexican singing abilities!” I screamed running out with the microphone down the aisle screaming, “Mexico! Mexico!” While I then realised I was all alone as I left the building in both a disgrace and in shame.

“Well, I think it’s safe to say that was the most bizarre audition I’ve seen in my life, we should of used him.” Simon said to himself as the camera blacked out.

What was I to do about the two men in my back alley, now knowing that my American Idol hopes are already faultered! Find out next time when Stimpo meets up with the men again, and their identities!
