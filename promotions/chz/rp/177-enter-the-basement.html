Enter The Basement...
Written: September 2, 2002

The Basement....

My home...

My sanctuary....

The one place my opponents have NO hope of defeating me in...

I revel in this thought as I lean against the cold wall of the basement, like I have many times after long grueling matches...

At first, the basement was just my place of rest....

Then as the weeks and weeks carried on...

It became my sanctuary...

My escape...

MY HOME.

It became my fortress, where I could rebuild my strength....collect my thoughts....become ever stronger in the battle against all those who wised to take what was mine...that being the CHZ National Title...

I could never get enough of that gleam...that brilliant shine that emitted off the thick center medallion of the title....

It entranced me...

It fed me...

It seduced me so, and it was for that reason that I could NEVER let this belt out of my grasp.

But there was someone who kept effecting that balance...

Cage...

The way he threatened to take my title was the way a rabid dog, loose on the streets, threatened a helpless child who just tripped and fell on the concrete...

I was not about to let my solace...

My love...

MY RELIGION slip between my fingers...

Not the National Title...

Cage will NEVER know the feeling of strapping this about his waist and calling himself 'CHAMPION'..I won't allow it.

This man is a monster...

First he tries to tear my beloved title from my grasp...

Then he DARES to try to invade my sanctum, so he can try yet again at that aforementioned task.

Has he not learned from the last few times?

Does he TRULY want to feel my wrath for a third time as he attempts to take what is mine yet again?

I won't allow it.

Because THIS TIME, I'm not going to trap the tiger just so I can release him back into the wild...

I'm going to trap this tiger and murder it...

Mutilate it...

Skin it...

Sever flesh from the bone...

END HIM in the cold fortress that is the basement.

He wants me?

He wants what I own?

He wants the 10 pounds of heavy gold I proudly brandish about my waist...

Then he obviously must be willing to DIE for it.

If that's what it takes for me to ensure that the belt stays about my waist, that is what must be done....

I will crucify him in the very bowels of this building and watch him bleed dry...

Watch the blood trickle from his body until he is just a battered and broken soul...

Then I can look upon him, look upon his beaten body...

Look upon the man that would be champion...

And leave him there to become a trophy upon my wall. The wall that will grow with more and more victims who dare to vy for my title...

The Basement is MY battleground Cage...

You are entering unknown territory little lamb...

You have strayed from the flock into the land of the wolves...

And last I checked...

The wolves are hungry for blood...

Cage, you will walk into the Basement....

But no one said ANYTHING about you walking out...
