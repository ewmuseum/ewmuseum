A Return of Sorts
Written: 6-13-02

'All the pieces are back in place, the roster is now full, the announcers have been selected, the front office is happy with what has happened. Now the first card back for the CHZ what will come of it? Well if you look at the card you cant help but think its gonna go far. Talent from top to bottom but what you need to be focusing in on is this, the main event where it pits 6 men in the ring for one thing. A chance at the World Title. One of those men who are involved is Don “The Game” Rossi. A man who in the past has always failed when it came down to the time for the one big match. The match that would put him over for sure as a legend in this business. He has won every title you could think of in his career but he has never won the title he wants most, the CHZ World Title. How does this affect him? Well if you ask him he says it doesn’t bother him one bit but if you ask me, it eats at his mind, body and soul. Everyone strives to be the top dog and every time he gets close and doesn’t pull out the win it kills him a little more inside. Given the chance yet again in this tourney I am curious as to how it will end as we look back at his previous attempts you can see he cant pull it out when he needs to. He can go the distance to get there but never close out. His first attempt was against a man named Kris Snyder AKA Chazy 2 Cool, where he got beat by his own finishing move to end the match. Next was just a few months back when he fought at Caged Fury against Matt Helms, Diablo, & the Count. A intense battle and again he couldn’t pull out the win. So I am not too anxious to see how this unfolds now that I think of it. But if any man can pull out a win from this bunch of competitors its Don. I know I am ready for the return of the CHZ but is the CHZ ready for The Game to Begin?”

BEGIN RP
Kelly: Where are you gonna be this week sweetie? I am not used to you being gone like this. *A woman comes around the corner of a door and into the room with Don Rossi. The two seem to have just woken up and Don is already getting ready to leave for his long trip to Canada.* I am so used to having you home with me now. Figures they would open up when I got used to having you with me again. Now I have to start all over again accepting you being gone on the road. *The woman is a short, petite woman who looks to be about 23 years of age. She has long dark hair and her eyes are a deep green. Dons days don’t get too good anymore but seeing her makes him smile no matter what happened that day.*

Don~ I am scheduled to be in Winnipeg, the road is gonna be great. I cant wait to see some of the guys again. Granted I will miss you dearly but you know my calling is in the ring not sitting at home. Some of us people have to work for a living you know. I wasn’t given a wealthy family but I was graced with good looks which keeps me on TV. So I will be back Thursday afternoon and we will go see a movie ok? *Don has a long sad look on his face as he looks over to Kelly who is nearly in tears. He gets up out of his chair and walks over to her and places an arm on her shoulder.* You know I would love to be here with you or have you with me but the office doesn’t like that. They expect us to work while we are there.

Kelly: Well at least they gave you your rightful spot this time. Your main eventing this week. You deserved it last time you worked for them but they thought Helms was more marketable and would be a better champ. I cant wait for you to prove them wrong. I know you can do it this time sweetie you have the chance this time. But I don’t know about this style of a tournament. Your match was booked like this Don Rossi, Chris Matthews, & Pat Dwyer against Luke Black, Davis Fury, & El Lobo De La Muerte in an elimination match.

Don~ Well Kelly I will be back Thursday to see you and I will call you later this week when I get done training and stuff but I gotta run before I miss the flight. I am not gonna get into your comments right now but thanks for telling me what my match was I must not have seen it on the fax machine this morning. What arena are we gonna be in so I know where to tell the driver when I get in the taxi when I get to the airport.

Kelly: Your going to Winnipeg Arena. Easy to remember, I swear you took one too many chair shots. I worry about you while your gone you know that right? Having to sit here and see you get hammered on with chairs and other objects by men of all sizes. I don’t like it Don.

Don~ You knew what you were getting into when you met me. So I am not hearing it from you its bad enough my mom doesn’t like what I do now I gotta hear it from you too? Bye sweetie.

*Don turns and walks out the door with his bag hanging on his right shoulder. He gets to the front door of the house and puts on some sun glasses that are sitting on a table by the door. He walks outside and is almost choked by the heat. A car pulls up to the door and he climbs in the back and takes off. Kelly can be seen at the door ready to burst into tears at any minute.*

Don~ I hate having to leave her. Since we met she has been there all the time when I needed her. Now that I am back to work I cannot be with her as much. Wish I had another few days to be home with her. You have any kids or anything Mike?

Mike: I have a baby girl, she’s turning 1 in a week. Best thing to happen to me Don. You will love it when you finally have a kid of your own. I am raising her by myself because her mom died during labor. Its been hard but I don’t regret it for one minute everyday I get home and see her waiting for me I know I did the right thing.

Don~ Damn, that must suck to be away from your only family, especially a baby girl. Whats her name?

Mike: Alexis, is her name. Where you headed to Don?

Don~ I am headed to Winnipeg, for a wrestling show next Wednesday. I just wanna get their and get my feet back into it all you know working out and training for my match.

Mike: yeah well I will get you there as soon as possible. Who are you fighting? Maybe I heard of them?

Don~ Some guy named Luke Black I havent seen much of him yet. So I really cant scout the guy or anything this first week is gonna be one hell of a week. Just feeling everyone out and getting used to it all again is gonna be weird. Then I am fighting some guy Davis fury. He looks impressive but nothing what I am used to fighting the only guys I really know will hang with me are is Diablo, Matthews, & Steve Brent.

Mike: I heard the CHZ picked up on a huge name, Cassius Deamont. Looks like he might try and be the top guy of the CHZ. Then you might have a problem because I have been driving you places for a little over a year and I know you arent one to sit back and watch other people take the spotlight.

Don~ Cassius Deamont huh? I wonder what this guy thinks hes doing? He must not realize that this is my yard and I will not let anyone get out of hand and try to oppose me because when they do people get hurt and I don’t wanna have to do that again. So I will just do what I normally do and that is entertain and if he gets in my way then so be it. I don’t back down from anyone Mike.

Mike: Well just watch yourself Don you’re a good guy I would hate to see you get hurt by some jackass because he is jealous of what you have.

*With that Don looks out the window and remains silent just thinking about the words Mike said “I would hate to see you get hurt by some jackass because he is jealous of what you have.”*
