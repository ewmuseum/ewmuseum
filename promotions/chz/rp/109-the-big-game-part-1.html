The Big Game.Part 1
Written: 7/31/02

{-BEGIN FEED-}

A brand new day has come. The Texan is now spending her full second day in her new home in Toronto, Ontario. The sky is once again clear and the sun is beating down hard, there has not been rain in at least a week, which has resulted to the grass fading to yellow. She knew a day like this could not be wasted inside so she decided to go to her first professional baseball game.

Angel had always followed the game in the sense of the citywide games they had back in the outskirts of Houston. The rules and fundamentals she knew quite well. She had looked and noticed the city team the Toronto Blue Jays were playing the New York Yankees in a league game today in an hour. She quickly got had something to eat, got dressed and then the door knocked from her big black apartment door. On the other side was her best friend from back home, Robin.

Robyn was an attractive brunette, being a friend of Angel she also worked at her bar at home along with her family. She was asked several times to come with Angel to Canada and be along side her. Just so she would not enter the Canadian atmosphere alone. She changed her mind as a few bags are dropped along side her ankles. The two share a hug and Angel invites her inside to her new home for the time being. Robyn explains that she changed her mind because she couldn’t imagine Angel going into a whole New World by herself. Angel told her she was ready to go to a Blue Jay game and Robyn decided to tag along as they got ready and hopped into the car and drove off into the dreadful traffic.

They near the Dome as the CN Tower parked right beside it was in clear vision from the heavy traffic streets. Angel happens to be one of the poor souls trapped behind about 10 thousand cars from the Dome. While they wait they catch up a little.

“ So how you like the CHZ life so far Angel? You met any of your co-workers or bosses?”

“ Well so far it has been a downer, some wrestlers are panting they would never fight a woman and that it is against their nature. I mean you witnessed me beating several men in the Houston federation back home. Some of these guys are half the skill of the ones back home. They do not realize how skilled I am, no push over that is for sure. I recently had a interview with one of CHZ interviewers, and announced to all the wrestlers I am ready for all challenges and for them to fight back because I will have no mercy on them. I have not met to many people just yet, interviewers a few times and some others from a distance, but still pretty new to the business in that sense.”

“ Now whom ever says they cannot fight a woman are either seeking attention, cannot cut it against you or just to afraid. But if I were a male in the CHZ, I would have no problem with fighting a woman, I would think she was a push over. But you will surprise them with your grit and talent, right girl.”

They both share a laugh as the traffic begins moving at a steady pace, as they begin seeing the people enter the stadium, the game begins in 35 minutes. The traffics begins to slow down again as Angel’s frustration begins to kick in as she slams her fist on the wheel. They continue waiting and chatting.
