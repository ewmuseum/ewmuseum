The Past Catching Up...
Written: 18/6/02

It had been almost a month since the fateful day he returned from his wrestling tour of Japan and found his wife’s lifeless body on the floor of his apartment. He had spent the month trying to track down the whereabouts of Tolkien Anderson, but he had no joy. From what he had gathered, Anderson had moved on to another city. He’d decided that New York was no longer a ‘profitable’ city to be in and simply moved on to greener pastures. With the likes of Spider-Man and the Avengers prowling the city who could blame him?

Davis had mixed feelings about this though. It was a good thing that Tolkien Anderson had left New York because it meant that Davis had a better chance of being the man to take him down. It was a bad thing because whichever city he would go to more innocent lives would be ruined as a resultant. The trail had indeed gone cold, but he knew of one resource he had not attempted to use yet, and was very reluctant to use, but that had to wait for the moment.

It had also been a month since the Canadian Wrestling promotion known as Cannuck Heat Zone, or CHZ had offered him a contract. That was main reason he returned from the tour of Japan so earlier. He had been putting the company off since the death of his wife and knew he could do so no longer. He could attribute the late reply to a period of mourning and accept the contract offer. However, his true goal would not be forgotten, not until Tolkien was either dead or in custody.

The bottom line was he hadn’t even begun to mourn his wife and probably never would until justice had been served, but he needed to get on with his life and more importantly fund this little mission of revenge. He needed the money not only to live but also to buy information from various dark and shady sources. Money was also needed to buy weapons and ammunition; the tools he needed to finish his work.

He had received a copy of the first CHZ card through the post earlier in the day and was surprised to find himself a part of the World Title match. The matched seemed to be tough to win to say the least, and to win the world title tournament would be nothing short of a miracle to him, but he sure as hell wasn’t going to give up! He was going to try his best and kick as much ass as he could whilst he was still in the tournament and hope for the best.

Content with his position upon the CHZ roster, his mind wandered to the night that this whole nightmare started. The catalyst that eventually led to the murder of his wife and upon extension ruined his whole life. It had taken Tolkien Anderson the best part of 18 months to catch up with him, 18 months to find the right moment to strike his revenge. The moment wasn’t as good as Tolkien had planned, obviously because if Davis had been home that night, he would be the one buried in the ground and not his wife.

That was the main reason this vow of justice was so important to him. He blamed himself for his wife’s death and rightly so whichever way he looked at it. He was the one responsible for pissing Tolkienn off in the first place and he was the initial target of Tolkien. His wife would still be alive if he’d have been home that night. Now he really didn’t care what it would take, he was going to even the score, even if it meant giving up his own life. At least then he would be with his wife once again, it whatever after-life awaited him.

He found himself at his original point, if only he hadn’t of killed Tolkien’s son, all of those months ago, the hell he currently found himself in would never have happened. He longed for some kind of time machine, so that he could go back and change what he had done, but he knew that the idea was hopeful at best. So he went back to working out strategies that potentially, could allow him to become victorious in the CHZ World Title match.

************

18 Months Ago…

Davis stepped out of the back of the police van and assembled with the rest of the SWAT team near the blockade of police cars, near the perimeter fence of the warehouse. The eight SWAT officers were assembled almost instantly and awaited the chief to fill them in on the details and the objectives of the mission at hand. Each and every one of the SWAT team was fully kited out with all of the necessary gear. The bullet-proof vest with the yellow acronym of SWAT printed across the chest, the black helmets with the transparent visors, and more importantly the very powerful automatic weapons. After a couple of seconds the Chief of Police casually strolls into view, absently placing his cinnamon coated donut on the bonnet of one of the police cars. The Chief then walks out in front of the SWAT team and coughs a little to gain their attention.

“Well” The Chief begins “As all you must know by now, we have a situation here. In one of those warehouses behind me…” The chief pauses to point in a vague direction. “The is a major drug deal going down at this very minute…

“Who exactly is our target, sir?” One of the SWAT team members from the front of the line up interrupts.

“As I was saying…” The Chief begins, pulling up his pants a little. “…In one of those warehouses behind me, there is a major drug deal going down. Led by none other than Seb Anderson!”

“Seb Anderson!” The same SWAT members blurts out.

“Yeah, that’s right The Crime Boss, Tolkien Anderson’s, son. We want the people involved in the drug deal alive if possible, but if they offer up any resistance, lethal force is authorised.” The Police Chief declares.

The SWAT team then rush inside the perimeter fence, in standard two by two formation, waiting a couple of seconds before another set of two enter the warehouses’ proximity. Davis and his friend Morris are the last two to enter the compound, as those ahead of them move from their cover, allowing them to take cover. Within a few movements the first two of the SWAT team enter the warehouse and gunfire soon followed. By the time Davis and Morris had entered the Warehouse, the place was a complete war zone. Davis noted, as he scrabbled to get behind the cover of very sturdy looking boxes, that two members of the SWAT team were down already. Whether they were dead was another matter; another matter that had to be answered after the battle was over. Davis briefly popped his head over the box and saw that more than three of the drug dealers were down, but the SWAT team was still out numbered.

Even though the drug dealers were not as well trained as the SWAT team, they were holding they’re own in this confrontation. Hastily hiding behind a combination of wooden boxes, machinery and walls. Davis popped his head over the boxes once more and fired a few shots at the drug dealer closest to him. The bullets hit the wooden box indifferently, as the drug dealer sticks his hand around the corner of the box and fires a few random shots, before running for the cover of machinery by the side of him. Morris lets a couple of shot’s go, one bullet hitting the man in the leg and the other in the back.

Davis nods at Morris in approval. “That’s what ya get for breakin’ cover, huh?” Davis jokes, absently popping up from behind the box and firing a few more shots at a number of targets. One hits a drug dealer in the shoulder, forcing him to the ground.

As Davis fires off a few more shots, Seb Anderson strolls into view, with two highly powerful automatic weapons in hand, firing them irresponsibly in all directions, forcing all of the SWAT teams back into cover, behind the boxes.

“Stop assing around and lets finish this now! Seb Anderson orders, as he finally ducks for cover himself.

Davis pops his head over the box one more time, intending to fire a couple more times, but then he sees it coming towards him. He sees the grenade fly towards his position, a few feet away from his own position. This is followed by about a half a dozen more grenades scattered about the place. Davis’ reactions were fast, as he instantly rises and rushes towards the exit of the warehouse. Davis makes it three meters past the exit of the warehouse before the series of explosions begin, propelling him at least ten meters forward. He wasn’t sure how much further he was propelled, because he lost consciousness soon after reaching the mark.

************

The day of his wife’s funeral was finally upon him. He had been so drawn into thinking of ways he could locate Tolkien Anderson and also planning tactics and different strategies that he could use during his world title match in CHZ. The day had crept up on him and he doubted that he could bring himself to go. In the end he forced himself to don, his black suit and get his ass to the crematorium, out of sheer will power.

At the funeral he didn’t expect so many people to be present and wasn’t convinced that he could face them all. They would undoubtedly try to console him and he really didn’t think he could stand that. So he decided to watch the whole ceremony from a distance. Leant on a tree, on a hill, high above the service. All those people there would undoubtedly notice that he wasn’t there and might think less of him, but he didn’t much care about what others thought of him. Not anymore at least.

Then he spotted a familiar face. A very familiar face indeed. It was his old superior Sgt Buckweiser. Davis hadn’t seen him since he stormed out of Buckweiser’s office all those months ago and was pleasantly surprised at his presence. Davis realised that Buckweiser might have access to certain files, which would allow Davis to finally track down Tolkien, but that would wait for another day and another place.
