Trainer from Hell: It's All Over
Written: June 18, 2002

Ross extends his hand to help Luke out of small pool of water he is lying in. Luke slaps his hand away in the huff and helps himself to his feet. Ross takes a towel of the rack beside him and tosses it Luke. “Dry off.” He says. Luke dries off and throws the towel in Ross’ face.

“Alright! Let’s finish the training!” Ross says excitedly taking off the towel from his face. “I’m going to have you do some chores as your next training method.”

“What?” Luke says shocked.

“That’s right, chores.” Ross assures Luke. “You know, cleaning toilets, cleaning windows, dusting, mowing, you know, chores stuff.”

“I’m going to do any chores!”

“If you want that title you will!”

“Maybe I don’t the title!”

“Oh but you do! I see it in your eyes!”

“You can see what I think?”

“Yes. Didn’t I tell you, I’m physic!”

“Your crazy! I’m leaving!” Luke tells Ross angrily.

“But we haven’t finished our training.” Ross says trying to get Luke back.

“I’ll train on my own time, in my own gym, by my self! Just get away from me!” Luke says as he enters the locker room.

Ross barges in the locker room just as Luke is getting his pants on. “You’re coming with me!” He demands, “You haven’t finished your training! You do want to be the CHZ World Heavyweight Champion, don’t you?”

Luke doesn’t say a thing as he finishes putting on his shirt. He storms past Ross and into the gym.

“What about my payment? You still have to pay me!” Ross reminds Luke.

Luke stops and pulls out his wallet. He takes out a few hundred-dollar bills, tosses them in the face of Ross and leaves out the front door. Ross picks up his money and shoves it into his pocket and hurries after Luke.

By the time he reaches Luke on the out side of the building, Luke is on his cell phone talking to one of his servants. “At least show me that you can beat them in the ring!” Demands Ross. Luke quickly turns around and hits Ross in the eye with a right hand. Luke hangs up his cell phone and puts it in his pocket. He gets down on his hands and knees and looks Ross straight in the face.

“You see me?” Luke yells, “I’m Luke Black! I’m the best the CHZ has to offer! I’m the future CHZ World Heavyweight Champion! I’m everything you want to be and more!”

Luke gets up and leaves Ross lying on the ground coving his eye. Soon enough a black Porsche pulls up and Luke Black’s limousine driver comes out and opens the passenger side door. Luke climbs in as Ross sits up and watches the driver get in the driver’s side and pull off.

“That’s right! Take the easy way out!” Ross shouts at Luke as he starts to get up. “You’ll regret it! And then you’ll come crawling back after you’ve lost the match! You’ll want my training! No! You’ll need my training! And so I’ll refuse to train you, and you’ll offer some much money I’ll be richer than Bill Gates!”

By this time Luke is far away and can’t even be seen, but Ross continues to shout, “And when I’m richer than Bill Gates I’ll use my power to make you my lackey! And when you’re my lackey I’ll make you do the most horrible things! Like clean the toilet when I’m using it! I’ll make you wear a dress! You’ll have no chouse to work for because you’ll be so poor that no other company will give you a job! I won’t allow you to take a shower SLASH bath! Then you’ll be stinky and sticky ‘cause I’ll make you jump in Kool-Aid! That stuff gets sticky when it gets dry you know! And…and…” Ross realizes how stupid he is and comes to his senses, “And who am I shouting at?”
