House of Klowns
Written: 7-23-02

It was one of those nights where the cool breeze was just cold enough to chill you to the bone yet the scattered leaves didn’t move with the gentle night air. As you stand at the bottom of the hill the sight before you makes that chill even colder as a tingling sensation travels up your spine. You try to shake it off knowing full well nothing is going to happen to you but yet there always is that small chance that something may happen, something may go wrong, and nobody will be there to here you scream…

Your friends dared you. They said you were chicken if you didn’t. They said you were a puss. They said many things and you couldn’t say anything to try and convince yourself and them that this really wasn’t a good idea after all. What could you say your all teenagers looking for a thrill, looking for a good time, just looking for some amusement. It just happens tonight their amusement came courtesy of you.

You slowly made your way up that long steep hill. It was hard to see anything as the trees surrounding the house made it hard to see very far in front of you. The moon was full and beamed down on you as if it were your protector trying to tell you once you entered those trees it could no longer be there to comfort you, to keep you in the sight of those who you called friends, and to give you something to hold onto as you accomplished this dare bestowed upon you by those laughing and giggling down the street safe and sound from what ever was inside that old spooky house which seemed to have a life of it’s own. They told you to go inside, to find something that proves you were inside, and then to come back down and show it to them. That was if HE didn’t get you first…

As you finally made your way to the edge of the yard you step up onto the curb being careful as to not touch the edges of the yard. It’s said that once you step onto the ground surrounding the house that he could feel you, that he would know you were there, and that once you were in his world there was no turning back… ever…

You turn back and look at your friends who are huddled together flapping their arms and clucking like chickens all in an attempt to get you to travel on. Instantly you regret your family ever moving here just over a year ago. You wish you were back at your old home where your friends wouldn’t provoke you into doing something so scary and crazy. Your old friends would be right here by your side every step of the way. But not these guys their not gonna be there for you. Their not gonna help you. If anything happens their going to run home and seek shelter from whatever it is that lives inside of this house. So then why are you doing this, why are you up here by yourself, why are you doing something which you know your so called friends wouldn’t do themselves? Acceptance is a hard thing to go without. Being part of a group, of being a part of a pack of friends is something every kid wants, needs, and craves.

You take your first step off the curb and onto the grass. You jump as the leaves beneath your feet crackle. Your heart begins to pound as you hear what you imagined as a wicked laugh from some ungodly creature of the night. Thoughts of turning back cross your mind. Thoughts of just running back down the street, running past your so called friends, running all the way home up your stairs and right into the arms of your mother and confused father. Sure your friends may laugh, they may cluck and call you chicken, but after all you’d be safe and unharmed. Isn’t that more important than being accepted by a group of hooligans anyway?

Some how you find the courage within to continue on. You find the courage to move forward. With each new step you take the woods become dark and the moon begins to fade within their thick branches. You realize that you didn’t bring a flashlight. Which you think could be your way out of this mess. Your friends didn’t really expect you to venture into THAT house without anything to see by did you. Sure their mean and at times cruel but their not heartless are they? How did they expect you to see anything? Better yet how did they expect you not to kill yourself falling over something or… somebody… Who are you kidding of course they are or at least one of them would be right here by your side.

As you glare up at the house you stop dead in your tracks and a single tear runs down your fear stricken cheek. In one of the darkened windows on the second floor you could have sworn you seen someone staring back at you. Bright green eyes and a grin so wicked that it seemed to invite you into the houses inner sanctum where you’d never be allowed to leave. You wipe your cheek with the back of your hand as you turn to look back towards your friends. But you’ve ventured to far into the wooded area and can barely even make out the street light their suppose to be by unless see if their still there. As you turn back towards the house you could have sworn you heard something running off to your left and the urge to turn and run had never been greater than it is now. Except you’re scared stiff and can’t move a muscle in your whole body.

Again you hear something this time to your right. You close your eyes tight wishing you were someplace else. Your bedroom, your father’s den, or even your grandparent’s house. Any place at the moment would be better than here. But yet you move forward ever so slowly.

You finally make your way to the foot of the stairs leading up to the porch. You jump at the hoot of an owl and begin to think that maybe you’ve been afraid for nothing. Everything you’ve seen or heard has been nothing but your imagination playing games on you. With this new found feeling you take your first step up the old stairs. The creaking sound causes you to slowly place your weight on the step hoping it holds beneath your full weight. With careful ease you make your way up each stair sighing relief after each new step as you realize that the creaks are only caused by the old age of the boards beneath your feet.

Once you reach the top of the stairs you let out a slight sigh of relief. You’ve finally made it to the porch and now all you had to do was go inside and take something then run like hell back to where you’re friends stood, simply? So you thought it would be until you hear a crashing sound from within the house. You quickly turn to jump down the steps but off in the distance you see a large figure slowly walking your way. You look to your left and then your right and in a panic you turn back towards the house thinking hoping that if you went inside that it would have a back door for you to escape by. You grab the door knob and in one twist your inside the house. Not having thought of what if someone else had been inside. Did you forget about the crashing noise you’d just heard moments before?

You stood pressed against the closed door huffing as you had been and still are consumed by the greatest fear you’ve ever known and will probably ever know if you don’t get out of this house soon… very soon. You jam you hand in your pocket as you remember having a pack of matches, smoking at a young age huh? You strike one of the matches bring glaring light to life inside the old house. You hold it out in front of you hoping nobody will be standing and staring directly back. You walk forward and jump as you hear the now common sound of creaking wood.

The room your in is rather huge and still holds the furniture of what you hope was that of someone that use to live here and not that of someone you currently resides in this old house. A fireplace sits off in one corner which you walk towards hoping that your luck might find you something to use as a torch. As you walk closer your stopped by that of a howling noise. You turn and look about but finally realize that it’s that of the wind blowing down the chimney.

You shake your hand as the match burns your thumb. You quickly strike another match letting light flood the room once again. Your luck pays off as you find a half used candle setting on the mantle above the fire place. You also find a giant picture of an devilish grinning clown hanging on the wall. As you turn you stop as you could have sworn the eyes had moved. You slowly turn your head back to find that they eyes were just as they had been. You begin walking forward towards a staircase when you hear that familiar creaking sound again. But this time it isn’t caused by the pressure of your weight upon the floor boards but sounds as though it’s that of the stairs on the porch. Something or someone was coming up the porch and in a moment would be standing inside the house and the very room that you now occupy.

You quickly blow out the candle as so who ever is outside can’t see you through the small cracks in the window shades. You quickly duck behind the banister of the stairs as someone slowly begins to turn the door handle. As the door slowly opens back you’re heart begins to pound and you just know that if you could see your reflection that you’d be ghostly white. You see the outline of a man or thing standing in the doorway as the door finally opens all the way. You close your eyes tight as the figure takes a step into the house. As you slowly open your eyes back up you hear a noise that reminds you of that of someone lighting a lighter.

In the red glow you see the face of… your father.

“Dad?” You call out questioning.

In that scruff voice of his he replies. “Junior is that you?”

You come out from hiding as you run towards and wrap your arms around his waist. Happy, so happy to see a familiar face and to feel the warmth of someone you know and love so dearly. You know that your in big trouble. Most likely grounded for two weeks, yard duty, and you’ll even half to wash dishes but at the moment the only thing you can think about is going home and hearing another long winded lecture from you father.

“You alright boy?” He ask in his fatherly voice.

“Yes dad, but can we go home now?” He grips your shoulder and confirms that he thinks it’s a good idea as well.

As the two of you turn you both see the outline of a massive man standing in the doorway. And with a flash of lighting behind him you see the gruesome face which you had thought you seen earlier in the upstairs window. Your father quickly pushes you behind him as the giant beast cackles and lunges forward. With a cry of pain your father howls out and something wet drops onto your face. You back away from the wicked looking thing which you first thought was a clown but now know it must be some sort of uncontrollable beast. Your father lays on the floor crying out in pain ten feet away as you stand there the beast rises and his cold hard eyes stare you into yours and you seem to be hypnotized by those green pearls of his.

You hear your father in his pain stricken voice telling you to run but the terrorizing fear of this monster has you frozen in place. Your father grabs the monster’s leg redirecting his attention giving you just enough time to run. As you run out onto the porch you hear what you think is the last gasping breath of your father. You jump down the stairs and begin running through the woods. With each few yards your chest and lungs feel as though their going to explode but the light looks to be as though it’s about to break through the darkness of the woods. You can begin to see your friends as they are yelling and begging you to hurry up. Just as your about to step off the edge of the yard your jerked from behind and the last thing you remember is that cackle of that beast which looks like that of a psycho looking clown………

The End

My heart was pounding with thunderous intend as I closed the cover of the book. I sat it down on the dust covered floor as I raised myself to my feet. I shown my flashlight across the empty room and just had to laugh to myself. My friends all waiting for me to make my return to where they had been waiting for me. In a strange turn of events the book they had me read was oddly familiar with that of my current surroundings. It was set in the same sort of house and in the same kind of circumstance as I’m currently in. I’ve just moved here just over a year ago, my friends had dared me to come up to this old vacated house and stay up here long enough to read this book. Oddly enough this house was on a hill as well as the one in the book.

I bent over and picked the book up. Flipping it over I read who the author was, Edgar Delacroix. The name sounded awfully familiar but I couldn’t place exactly where I had seen the name before. It says he had lived in Charleston South Carolina. I found this odd since this was where I had lived. Perhaps my friends had known this. The small paragraph giving information on the author had said he lived at 1315 South Evans St. A lump built up in my throat as I knew for a fact that this was the exact location of the house in which I currently stood. Then I remember where I had seen the name Delacroix at. On the side of the house was a small wooden sign and inscribed upon it was the name Delacroix.

I stood in the very house to which this book had been depicted. My stomach began to swarm as I recalled my friends telling me that around 1954 something strange had occurred in this house and both a father and a boy had disappeared. But if this was so then the book I had just read wasn’t fiction after all… I turned the book to the front cover and found that it was copyright 1955 a year after that gruesome story of which my friends had told me. I quickly decided that it was high time that I found my way out of this house and back to where my friends stood waiting on my return.

I walked out of the upstairs room and looked from side to side. I didn’t seen nor did I hear anything to my huge relief. I walked towards the staircase and began my decent down the creaky banister. With each step under my weight the stairs breathed heavily and I was afraid as I had been on the way up that they just may crumbled under my weight. Once I had made my way half way down the stairs my heart jumped into my throat as I heard that which I associated with breaking glass back up the stairs. I quickly began running down the stairs skipping two to three steps at a time. Once I was back on the first floor I heard another loud crashing sound this one even worse than the first. I stopped in the middle of the room and glanced towards the fireplace which had been depicted in the book and the sight had almost caused me to faint as there on the wall was a huge painting of the psychotic clown.

I didn’t wait around long as I began to hear the cackling sound which I imagined the young boy was hearing in the book I ran forward and swung open the door which I had entered through just half an hour before. There standing before me was a massive of a man with bright orange hair, orange eyebrows, nose, and lips. His eyes where a greenish color and decorated with blue stars and his teeth were razor sharp. My heart stopped as that terrorizing face grinned at me.

He then lunged forward and gripped my throat with powerful hands. I gasped for a breath as he simply laughed and said, “It’s not so funny when it’s not in a book is it?”

The sound of crushing bones and severed tendons can be heard as the sounds of someone gasping for their last breath echoes throughout the empty house.
