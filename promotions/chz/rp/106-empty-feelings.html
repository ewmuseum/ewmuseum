Empty Feelings
Written: 07.29.02

Blackness.

A cooler day in the city of Las Vegas. The sun is shining, but the warmth everyone knows just isn’t apparent on this February morning. Three people continuously walk in and out of the house, carrying suitcases out, and loading them into the back of the Buick that sits in the driveway. One of the people is a beautiful young girl, who looks to be around the age of sixteen. She has shoulder-length blonde hair, green eyes, and one of the prettiest smiles anyone has ever seen. She is wearing a pair of grey sweatpants, and a blue Old Navy t-shirt. The second person, is an older man. He looks to be about forty-five or six, but is in great physical condition. His hair is on the fringe of turning grey, and his face is clean shaven. He is wearing a pair of dress pants, and a dress shirt. The only thing casual about his attire are the Nikes that are on his feet. The third person, is an older woman, who looks to be the same age as the man. She has a skirt and blouse on, and is wearing a pair of sunglasses. Her hair is still a dark brown, but is cut short. At last, they seem to put the last of their luggage in the car, because the doors are closed, and everyone walks into the house, and shuts the front door.

Blackness.

Paul Harrington sits at his desk, staring blankly at the front wall. In the background, rain can be heard and seen falling outside the giant wall of windows. The rain looks to be falling hard, and flashes of lightning can also be seen. Suddenly, the audio breaks in, and Victoria’s voice is heard.

“Mr. Harrington?” She says.

Paul still sits, staring ahead.

“Mr. Harrington?!” She says a little louder.

Paul still stares.

“PAUL?!” Victoria yells.

Suddenly, a loud clap of thunder is heard, and this awakes Paul from his trance.

“I’m sorry Victoria, I was spacing out. I had another one of those ‘visions’.” Paul says.

“What was this one about?” She asks, with interest in her voice.

“I saw the same people, only this time, they were loading the car up. Then they went inside, and that was it.” He says, in an tone that says he doesn’t understand.

“Whoa, Paul. That’s weird. That’s really weird.” Victoria says, wide eyed.

“I know. I don’t really understand it.” Paul responds.

“Me either. I wish I could help.” She apologizes.

“Think I should go see someone about it?” He ponders.

“Wait a few more weeks. I they continue, then yes. It’s only been two. It happens to everyone sometimes. It’s called déjà vu.” She explains.

“Yeah, you’re right. But anyway, what is it you wanted?” Paul asks.

“Oh, nothing, just that you promised a building shutdown today at eleven o’clock. It’s eleven, and a lot of us are anxious for your announcement.” She says with anticipation in her voice.

“Oh, I get it now. You only care about the boss if he has to announce that you can leave?” Paul asks with a grin.

“Now you know that’s not true. I care when you give me raises too.” She replies with a smile on her face.

“Ha Ha, very funny. Yes, get your butt out of here. Go tell everyone else.” Paul says with a grin of his own.

Victoria rushes out of the room, as Paul gets out of his chair, and walks over to the window. The rain has subsided, but is still coming down hard. Paul looks to the street below, and watches all the people scramble to their cars to get out of the rain. He can’t help himself, and begins to chuckle at the site before his eyes. He turns away from the window, and walks back over to his desk where he picks up a remote control, and turns the television set hanging in the corner of the room on. The set comes to life, and begins to show PCW’s “Wrestle Vessel”. Paul watches for a second, then lets out a sigh as he sits back in his chair. He stares at the wall in front of him, and begins talking.

“August eighth. That’s when I can prove myself. That’s when everyone will find out what Paul Harrington is for real. They will find out that he’s not just some hard working, office type guy. No, he’s nothing like that.” Paul says, to who, it is unknown.

Paul then looks up at the tv, and sees Jason Snow. He begins smiling, and stands. He once again moves towards the window, and looks out, at the rain, and the people, still scrambling to their cars.

“Soon. Soon I will be fighting people like Jason Snow, or the new PCW World Heavyweight Champion, Xavier Cannon. Soon I will accomplish my life long dream of becoming a true superstar. Soon, very soon.” He says with a convincing tone.

Paul turns, and walks away from the window, and to his desk again. He shuts the TV off, and picks up his briefcase from the floor. He then walks to the door, and turns the knob, opening it. He exits the room ,and looks back in, checking things.

“Sometime soon, I might not have time for this, and I will miss it. But for now, I damn sure don’t wanna be here right now. Screw this, I’m going home.” Paul says, laughter in his voice.

He reaches inside the door with his free hand, and grabs onto the light switch. He starts to shut it off, but stops, looking out the window. The rain has suddenly stopped, and the sun is starting to break through. Paul smiles widely again, and flicks the switch to off. He walks out the door, and lets it close behind him.

Blackness.
