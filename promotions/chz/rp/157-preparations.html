Preparations...
Written: 23rd August 2002

--{{It’s cold, it’s miserable and it’s raining in Montreal. The camera has a wide-angle shot of the unmistakable Olympic Stadium in all its glory. Various streetlamps are lit-up surrounding the area as the “Summer” weather has darkened skies.

We see a small flock of fans at the ticket office to our left, at which point the camera pans in to next a large billboard that reads: “CHZ Wednesday Night Wild, Live! Wednesday August 28th”. Underneath the large Maple Leaf logo is a promotional picture for the scheduled Main Event Tag Match. On one side of the billboard is the smug, arrogant expression of Matt Hunt and his nemesis, the determined Jake Schultz. Filling up the other section are the other two rivals – a cocky Xander Crave, with his National Title draped on this right shoulder, and the focused Logan Cage.

The camera moves across to the ticket office, where the fans – dressed in different coloured waterproof gear - are trying to get the last few available tickets. The camera continues to film and a few moments later, it fades slowly to black

Immediately, the shot fades in again and we’re inside the arena. The camera pans around the magnificent setting as the workmen lay down some canvas. It’s a quiet setting, albeit the clanking of the workman some others cart in some of the stage and some equipment to set up the ring.

As the camera pans out again for a wide shot of the entranceway, the large video screen is operating and has a mysterious looking figure on the screen. Intrigued as we are, the cameraman decides to focus into the large structure and on to the figure. It’s hard to see who it is as the person is wearing a hooded jumper with the hood itself pulled over his head, so his face is hidden.

As we wait and watch for a glance at the face, the person slowly moves his arms up and pulls back the hood. The face Matt ‘The Postman’ Hunt is revealed, looking disgruntled as always. As the arena camera focuses out, the background of some blue seats comes in. Matt Hunt is in the stadium!.

With our camera still focused on the arena screen, Hunt can be seen looking to his left. Confused, cameraman slowly turns to his right to see Matt Hunt staring right at him!. Hunt is quite a few seats away and slowly stands, turns and then makes his way towards the camera. The cameraman stays on Hunt and finally, after what seems an eternity Hunt reaches the camera. He looks straight into the camera upon his arrival, without blinking his eyes or any movement, he starts to talk}}--

Hunt: “I knew you were watching me. I could feel it, burning into the back of my head. I can feel the sense of someone watching me.

Some people get a little uneasy, a tad bit uncomfortable when they finally realise that somebody is watching them. But me?. Doesn’t bother me one bit. Because I know that whoever is watching me, burns a desire for my blood, for my life maybe. That shows me that I am a targeted man, it shows…and pardon the pun here…that I am the hunted. And when that happens, I am more on my game than ever. I am always on my guard, at the top of my game, but when you’ve got someone after you, then you have to have that little extra edge.

Whenever you are a titleholder, which is all a matter of time, you are the hunted. Everybody wants a piece of you and they will stop at nothing to get what they want. I have to admit that it the roles were reversed, then I would do whatever it takes to get it and it wouldn’t end there. I would damn sure make the champion know that I am after them, that I am their maker and that they will eventually fall.

Wild is going to be an evening of unpredictability. So much is going on at the same time that it’s going to be chaos for the entire show and that’s what I like to see…chaos. People running backstage, looking for him, looking for her. Unpredictability is the name of the game as two pairs of hated rivals forced to tag with each other. Xander and Cage continue their rivalry, the same with that camache Schultz and yours truly

I have to say, it’s some great matchmaking…it really is. I mean, the last televised event before the big Pay-Per-View and here we are, tagged together.

Now some of you may not believe this, but I really have no problem whatsoever in teaming with the very man who stands between myself and my target. In fact, I’m looking forward to it because I want to see Jake Schultz squirm in that ring, contemplating whether or not to tag me, watching his shoulder every five minutes, etc, etc

--{{Hunt turns to unfold a seat and he brushes of any access water as he sits down. A few moments pass and he continues}}--

“I could attack you at any given moment, Schultz. I have done it before and you can be rest assured that I’ll damn well do it again. I could so easily hop over the top rope and knock you off your feet…but I won’t.

You see, Jake. I am a professional and I will compete in this match. Not to wind you up, not to stir shit between Cage and Xander, no. I am competing, because I love this business!!

Yeah, nothing hits me in the heart harder than hearing the thousands upon thousands of “people” who boo and hiss and jeer me, everytime I show my face. It brings a tear to my eye, when I lay out the fans biggest favourite with a good ol’ shot to head with a “foreign” object and all I can hear are the ‘asshole, asshole, asshole’ chants

I love it all, really…I do.”

--{{Hunt pauses as he leans forward, resting elbows on his knees and his hand holding up his chin. After a few moments of looking at the soaking wet workmen continue their jobs, he continues}}--

“Jake, I look forward to Wild. Let’s see who really has what it takes. Let’s see who been playing the Mind Games, eh? From Wild it’s on to the PPV and it’s game time. Anyway, you know I like to talk as much as the next guy – whoever he is – but I wanna get down to business. So, I’ll see you on Wednesday and camache…let the games begin…”

--{{Hunt stares at the lens for a few moments before standing up, turning and walking away, pulling up his hood in the process. The camera watches him walk away and after a few moments, turns around to get one last shot of the empty arena and slowly fading to black}}--
