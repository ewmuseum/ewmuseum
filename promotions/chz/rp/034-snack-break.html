Snack Break
Written: 6/23/02

Time continues passing everyone by with little change being shown to the world about them. Much the same can be said about the life and times of Tyler Davidson the past week. For a week now he has waited patiently for the arrival of CHZ Wild on Wednesday night. After nearly a week of waiting Tyler’s opponents had finally decided to make their presence felt. The team of Jack Williams and Amanda Flaton both appeared on the CHZ scene conducting promos of their own in recent days. Still not to be seen however is the supposed mystery partner which Tyler found himself unwillingly placed with. To say Tyler was displeased with the situation would be an understatement but he has so far managed to keep his cool. His patience however is another story.

The patience within him had begun to run thin and the change of scenery from Connecticut to Winnipeg, Manitoba had done little to help matters. He finds himself more and more waiting for the day of the show to arrive so he could remove all the questions and doubts from the minds of the people he is surrounded by in this new territory, a territory which Tyler finds to be quite hostile in his case. He now finds himself wandering a supermarket in the Winnipeg area not far from the arena where Wednesday nights show is set to take place. As normal at his side is manager and trainer Daniel [name withheld] still not showing much signs of happiness being in the cold Canadian weather.

“I still can’t believe I let you talk me into coming to this ass backwards place,” [name withheld] complained as Tyler pushed a nearly empty shopping cart down a store aisle.

“Hey if I have to be in this place then you might as well be here too,” Tyler replied. “You the one that suggested I agree to the damn thing.”

“Yeah but that was after I left the managing gig behind me,” [name withheld] retorted obviously just complaining for the sake of it. “Didn’t think I’d get suckered back into this thing and dragged to Canada of all places.”

“Well you’re here now so I guess you’ll just have to deal with it for a bit,” Tyler fired back as he grabbed a bag of ruffles potato chips off the shelf and tossed them into the cart on top of a bag of pretzels already in there. “We need anything else in this aisle?”

“Nope,” [name withheld] replied tossing a small can of onion dip into the cart. “How the hell long do we have to stay in Canada for anyway? When’s the next US show this CHZ is having?”

“Are you kidding?” Tyler asked looking at [name withheld] half amused at the notion.

“Why would I be kidding?” [name withheld] asked genuinely confused by the question.

“Did you not stop to think for one second just what the initials CHZ stood for?” Tyler asked suddenly realizing his friend was serious in his questioning.

“No I didn’t,” [name withheld] answered him. “Why?”

“It stands for Canuck Heat Zone,” Tyler responded to him shaking his head in disbelief. “By the way that is Canuck as in Canadian.”

“Ok so?” [name withheld] questioned him still not catching on.

“So they only run shows in Canada,” Tyler finally answered straight out. “There will be no US based shows anytime soon.”

“You’re joking right?” [name withheld] asked in disbelief mixed with horror.

“Nope,” Tyler replied amused at his friend’s expense. “Don’t worry though. The shows are every two weeks so we don’t have to be in Canada the whole time. I wouldn’t be able to stand that much myself.”

“You could have told me this before we left the states,” [name withheld] argued. “It would have saved me a lot of time.”

“Ah you’ll get used to it quickly enough,” Tyler responded. “The cold won’t even be noticeable after a few shows.”

“Yeah right,” [name withheld] said full of doubt. “How about on Wednesday you go into this thing, win your match, and we get the hell out of this country?”

“Sounds like a plan to me,” Tyler responded turning into another aisle of the store and nearly running into an elderly lady as she left her cart blocking the aisle. Tyler though pushes his way through still and continues down the aisle. “This whole week hasn’t been much enjoyment for me either. I’m still waiting to see if this damn mystery man will decide to show up or not.”

“Got to love mystery men partners don’t you?” [name withheld] asked sarcastically.

“Not one damn bit,” Tyler answered. “Of course it’s not like I’m going to need the guy’s help. Neither one of these two are showing anything at all impressive to me. That’s not anything new though really now is it?”

“Not at all,” [name withheld] replied. “You always thinking you can do things on your own. Whether or not you can is another story however.”

“Whatever,” Tyler responded. “I got a partner that I don’t even know exists and taking on two people in one match. Someone doesn’t like me around here already obviously. Probably some Canadian that’s still bitter at how much better us Americans are. They’ll be getting an up close and personal look at that in just a few days now.”

“Well don’t go too overboard with it now,” [name withheld] said. “No need to piss off a whole damn country while you’re still in it.”

“Good idea,” Tyler agreed. “Let’s finish this damn shopping and get out of here. Do we really need a lot of food? We are only here about two or three days as it is.”

“Not to much,” [name withheld] answered. “Just enough to get by and some snacks for me to munch on as I watch you get your ass kicked Wednesday.”

“Yeah you do that,” Tyler fired back. “In the meantime I’ll be sure to accidentally leave you behind when I head back to the US.”

Tyler walks off with the cart in front of him as [name withheld] tries to keep up with the suddenly quicker moving Tyler as the scene fades to black.
