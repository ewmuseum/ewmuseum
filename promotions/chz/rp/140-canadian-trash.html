Canadian Trash
Written: unknown

Canadian trash (yes...you)
'American Hero'Ryan Contra

With everything that's been going on Ryan hasn't had time to even take it all in. Canuck Heat Zone's first card went straight to hell, a women ended up winning. How could the weaker sex win do you ask? Count out- and a whole lot of staying out of the way. Don't take this the wrong way- Ryan in everyway has taken this personal. Now the next card he has to team up with a jackass, to face a weak women, and Mr. Bible. Now why would you think Ryan would be stuck with the trio of, cheap, cheesy, and retarded once more? The only reasonable explanation of course would be the bookers... are canadian! Not to fear, the canadian mass won't bring down our beloved American Hero.

We see our American Hero at the edge of a bed in a master bedroom hotel. Ryan was wearing a robe that was looking something like an American Flag. Ryan had Teddy bear slipper's on as well. Ryan's hair, jet black was all flared up- as usual. Jamie was staying with his mother. (Jamie's Mother's mother. Jamie's mother is deceased.) Ryan's Career in CHZ as well as being a medical intern- not to mention the oh-so valuable time spent with Jamie, has left Ryan the feeling of being lost in limbo. Insomina has engulfed Ryan in a shitty hell of Oprah re-runs.

' Ya' need to let it all go women. It's simple, you have to understand that YOUR NOT FAT!' Dr. phil said convincingly infront of the million's of poor canadian bastards tuned into this bullshit.

Canadians in Ryan's mind were all from the sixth layer of hell. All were hitler henchmen; pro's at beating their wive's and ruler's of the black and white television. With a crease on Ryan's forehead, his eye's twitched in extreme anger that neither you or I could understand. Ryan's head was spinning with ways he could ... erase.. Dr. Phil. Earlier he had learned that Phil was getting his own show- and had prayed for death. There was no telling the actually time, but were positive it's in the wee hours of the morning. As Ryan's seems to be slowly slipping into oblivion a knock at the door is heard. Ryan jolts awake from his fantasy of stabbing Phil to look over the door. Ryan's forehead had a white patch on, covering stiches he had got from an unknown attacker. The white patch was located over his right eye, where he must've been hit. Once more a knock at the door came. Ryan sat up and made his way over to the door. Opening up- A tired Wayne King came walking into the room.

'You've made it; good. Just setup in the corner of the room over there.' Ryan pointed to one of the rooms four corners.

The hotel room was larger than first thought. It had a Queen sized bed, with a golden canopy, there was a massive fridge on the other side of the room, big enough for twenty lumberjacks. Seriously. Wayne pulled up a love seat directly infront of the bed.

'Whooaa Kemosabie, Im going to have you leave your equipment. There's no way in hell your staying in here with me.'Ryan said motioning for the door.

Wayne King looked from the door, to Ryan, to his camera. Without so much as a question, Wayne had left. Besides it was too early anyway. Once Ryan had set up he pulled from the inside pocket of his robe- a pipe. Lighting it up Ryan sat on the love chair crossing his leg's.

'Before I begin- I'd like to refresh your memory with what happened at Wild Wednesday. Ryan motioned to the television in which previous Ophra was playing was now taken over by CHZ wild. The camera switches to just see what happened. (But isn't Ryan sitting on the chair away from both the television and the camera- how did this happen!? Who give's a damn freak)

Wild Wednesday

The camera instantly picks up The American Hero Ryan Contra charging from behind Blitz with a steel chair in hand, and before Axl Young can finish his insult on The Disciple, Contra smashes the steel chair across the back of Blitz, sending him reeling forwards clutching his back.

Byron: What the hell is the meaning of this!

Blitz then slowly turns as Axl and Angel move away and Contra taunts Blitz to hurry up and turn around. Then as he does, Contra slams the chair around The Disciples head with a bellowing sickening thud that sends him crashing to the cold floor and leaving a harsh dent in the chair.

'Yes- for your information, Im a badass' Ryan said in satisfaction. 'But let's continue!!'

Continuing of Wild

Contra then proceeds to smash the chair into the ribs of Blitz.

Ryan Contra: You...

WHACK!

Ryan Contra: DON'T...

WHACK!

Ryan Contra: MESS WITH...

WHACK!

As Contra then lifts the chair high above his head for the final devastating blow, Angel Summers drops to her knees behind Contra, and low blows him, sending The American Hero dropping to the floor slowly while clutching his manhood.

Byron: Low blow to Contra, and dammit he deserved it!

'Alright I'd like to note something here for the hopeless Canadians watching at home. Angel has deliberately touch- God's gift. Now- you might think that was to play dirty and to stop me from smashing Matt into little pieces- but I don't think so. Let me take you further into tonights little escapade.' Ryan said taking a puff from his pipe.

Again wild Wednesday

[This piece is fromt later in the night] An infuriated Contra leaps to his feet and drags Texan in by the hair, who was already on the apron. However, Texan levels a low blow to Contra much to some sympathetic groans from the crowd and the American Hero falls to the canvas reaching for his groin.

Jeff: Sheesh, low blow with impact!

The Camera turns slowly from the screen to Ryan Contra. Ryan meet's the camera's view with a slightly raised eyebrow, an upper lip curled and nearly closed eyes. Obviously disgusted 'Mere coincidence? No friends, I believe that this was a desperate attempt to touch ~The Miracle~ Ryan Contra. Yes, but will this weak charade stop and she'll admitt her love, or will a Angel Stalker Summers keep hitting me in the crotch for the slightest chance she'll get the feel she's looking for? Though your probably still not convinced are you? Let's show everyone my last segmeant from CHZ that I've prepared. Role it fella's' Ryan said, as he held his pipe, and tapping his nostrol with his index finger. The camera spins around to the television once more.

Wild Wednesday

However, Young has gotten back to his feet and returns to the ring, flying towards Texan and taking her down with a reverse spear. Texan lands on top of Contra, with Young falling on the pair of them.

The camera, then settles once more on contra. Who was now nodding his head. 'As you can see, she wanted me right then and there!! Though I have self-respect, the first female of Canuck heat zone... is a tramp!! My newly found tag partner also wanted in on it- but let's all just try to forget about this obessive nonsense.' Ryan said taking once more a hit from his curved oak pipe. Who's the person obessessed here?

There's a moment's silence as Ryan puts more tobacco and lets the every-so-growing fans stew on what Ryan has brought to their attention.

' Well Canadian trash, next up in my order of business, is the brutal attack made against me by someone who was well concealed. If you missed out on what happened, some ass munch knocked my head in with a chair. Giving me nine stiches; leaving a Cross necklace. Now I know alot of people had already started making speculations and pointing fingers. So- I want to too~!! So the number one guy who I KNOW knocked my American ass out is.... LEROY AVERY!! Yes that damn Gary Coleman referee! I know it was that shrimpy bastard! He wanted revenge for me calling him fat. Which I SO didn't mean!! But if he doesn't turn out to be the ~one... I'd have to go with Matt Blitz.' Ryan's dramatic accusations went well, or so he thought. Pleased that he got his point across Ryan smiled. Taking the final hits from his pipe the scene fades.
