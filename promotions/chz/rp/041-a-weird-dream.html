A weird dream
Written: 6/38/02

The scene fades in with Inferno sitting in his living room watching CHZ wild again he can’t believe at what an ovation he got from the old fans and start to wonder what is in store for him in the future of this great company. He stand up and goes to get a drink of water as he comes back he sees the part were he was making fun of Wayne King. Inferno just laughs and can’t believe that one guy can be such a wimp. Inferno turns the TV off and starts to head toward his room as he does so he begins to talk what he is thinking.

Inferno: Last night me and Tyler Davidson kicked some serious ass and after we are both done will are title tournaments that we are in I wonder if he would want to form an alliance of sorts a tag team were in the CHZ tag bracket we would be unstoppable. Last night as I watched my comrades in action I realized that this is not the old CHZ but a new one with great talent and young super starts clawing and fighting with every last inch of there life to obtain gold and that is what I like about the new CHZ it is going to be the best fed that there is going to be with the hunger of its superstars young old and some that haven’t even become stars yet. But I do know one thing after are next show I am going to be one step closer to my dream of getting gold around my waist I have to face this Summers guy and I really don’t give a damn about his name. But I do know that he made a impressive performance last night. I for one was impressed any way but none of that matters what matters to me is that I the Inferno get gold around my waist. I have done it all in this world except to win a title here. In four weeks when it comes down to me and whoever else is in that ring across from me I will achieve my gold but I am not going to be satisfied with just that belt know I want the big belt and I know it will take some time for me to climb that ladder but believe me it is closer then anyone thinks. Now on to more important business and that is in my bed room.

Inferno walks the rest of the way to his bed room without saying a word his wife is already asleep so he decides to go ahead and knock out for the night. As Inferno falls asleep a horrid and terrorizing nightmare begins starting with his past life and working its way forward to the present. Inferno’s dream starts with a flash back to when he was but a child. We pick up were his father drops him off at his aunts house for a few hours while he has to go and fight a raging fire that is heading to there town his mom is already at the scene but his dad had to pick young Larry up from pre school. We see Inferno kiss his dad good bye and ask if he would be home in time for the CHZ event his dad said sure son and head out to fight a fire but what no one knew at the time was that one fire was coming but that another was about to join it trapping the brave fire fighters in the middle of a big Inferno and making the start of are scene. Larry is sitting watching the TV CHZ is on and we can see DIABLO do a major body slam on STAZ then we see Diablo do his finisher and win the North American title. The little guys joins in with the people in the audience booing him. then there was a phone ring and his Aunt said ok I will Inferno just surged his shoulders and said mom and dad going to be late? See said yeah but we got to go and see them how is that? Inferno nods his head and as he goes outside he sees that everyone is waling out of there houses and heading toward the flames. Him and his Aunt get to his parents right as the fire encloses the little town of people they are all fighting vigorously but to no avail. All of the people go under covering the children days later the next town over finally comes in with a rescue squad they knew that there would be no survivors and were grateful to the small town for as the went down the fire lost all courage and died away. They were about to walk away when the saw some ashes start to move. The men with a look of shock on there faces rush over and see a small boy covered in ash and has a few burns on him but not many in one hand he has a yellow fire hat and in the other a gold necklace. The men take him and the two objects to there fire station and Inferno begins to tell them everything that has happened. The scene jumps ahead to Inferno getting adopted bye one of the men that found him and he starts to call him Inferno instead of Larry he didn’t mind and the name stuck throughout high school were he meet Heather. the scene jumps again and Inferno is getting married and about a few days later he gets his college degree and major in psychology. Then we see him helping a man called Porgies at the time the man was in finical dept because his Wrestling business was going under. He looked at Inferno and asked if he every thought about going into wrestling Inferno shock his head and the man after weeks of negotiation sings the Inferno to a small contract because he really couldn’t afford it. Well in the end the federation known as the OWA went under and Inferno was about to go back to his psychology when his old tag team Partner “The Reaper” Jaxon Green told him he was joining a place called the XWX and that he got him a try out. So Inferno went and made it with no problem the Prez there taught and molded Inferno and Jaxon into fighting machines the decided that they had what it took for the big time the went to the CHZ and Inferno and Jaxon took of like a rocket. Zero there prez however never made it in CHZ. Right at the time that Inferno was about to receive a title shot he got terrible new the CHZ was going under. Inferno stayed with them in till the end and even was part of a feeble group trying to keep it a float. But they too couldn’t do it so CHZ went under. Then Inferno disappeared and wasn’t scene for a long time in the wrestling world. That was because he had joined an organization that wasn’t a big name called 2XW there are flashed of victories defeats and mixture of a lot of competition. Then The scene jumps to El Diablo talking to Inferno about rebuilding the CHZ at the time Inferno wasn’t really interested but then joined after some tough negotiations the it goes to Inferno winning the match with Tyler Davidson. Then all of a sudden the scene fades.

Inferno sits up in a could sweet he was about near tears and his wife Heather touched his hand and told him it was ok. Inferno laid back down but couldn’t fall back to sleep it was 6:00 any way so he went and ate breakfast then went down to his work shop and started lifting some weights. Inferno is interrupted bye Jaxon who lives with him and want to talk to him about his upcoming match. so the sit down and start to talk. so they start to talk and Inferno sits up off the bench and they start to talk.

Jaxon: In two weeks time you have Shammon Smith how do you plan to get around this man?

Inferno: Well last week he was defeated quickly by Vega and I plan to do the same He has a bad arm and I plan to work on it there will be nothing to stop me from attacking attacking attacking I will beat this man to keep my CHZ streak alive and keep his losing streak strong. I will do a lot of big moves to make this man look like a small piece of pie. But seriously I am very quick for a big man and may even do some high flying maneuvers like cross body blocks and frog splashes moves like that. That is just a back up plan though for i plan to keep the match slow with slams and submission move to hurt him and so that he doesn’t forget that he got his ass burned down bye the INFERNO. He will never forget THE NEXT BIG THING. I will and I promise I will destroy this small fry. But for now I must train are you going to help me or not buddy?

Jaxon: YEAH. SURE.

The scene fades with Inferno and Jaxon lifting weights.
