Well Needed Advice
Written: 6/14/02

<

Pat Dwyer has recently has gave it all up for CHZ. He gave up his former federation just to wrestle in his home country of Canada. That was a decision he not only did for his fans but also for his country. Without the great country he has lived in for his whole life he probably wouldn't have the proper wrestling training. Sure he owes a lot to his former federation the XCWF but they only helped develop some of his many wrestling qualities. Now that he is only an indy star he can start worrying about what is important, his future in the world of wrestling of course. His burns from the ice after being locked in the ice cream cart have healed and he is back into activity. Pat has already started to work out and test his physical shape. The tests prove he is more then ready to step into the CHZ squared circle. This Wednesday he will be fighting in the opening round of the World Title Tournament.


The camera opens up in a gravel driveway. A red mustang convertible pulls into the driveway. The music is blaring on the large speakers placed in the back seats. The figure is a rather familiar man. He has a built figure. All of a sudden the music stops blaring and the engine is turned off. The man steps out of his mustang and closes the door. The camera zooms in on the man. He has a slight grin on his face. His eyes are hidden behind oakley'a sun glasses and his hair is flicked up. This man is our soon to be CHZ World Champion, Pat Dwyer! He is approaching this old farm building. The house has a beautiful look but it seems old fashioned. He knocks on the door and waits for someone to answer. After about a minute more of knocking the door is opened by a elderly woman. She gives Pat a hug and a peck on the cheek.


'Pat, It is so good to see you again. It's been almost a year since you have visited us!'


'I know that mother. I have been very busy since I am in the wrestling business. I barely have time for my girlfriend.' Pat replies with a saddened face.


'That's okay Dear, as long as you can visit us once in awhile since you are a busy man. Come in honey and come see your father.'


Pat enters the home that he had grown up in as a young child. He admires how well it has been taken care of over the years by his father and mother. This weekend just so happens to be Father's Day weekend so it is a perfect surprise for his Father. He owes it to him because of how well he has done in parenting him.


Pat follows his mother through the kitchen. For some reason it is the exact same way it was a year ago. I guess nothing has changed since he has left. His mother leads him into the living room. Pat's Dad is sitting on the couch watching the US Open on TSN. Pat realizes that his favorite golfer Mike Weir is currently shooting.


'Son, it's so good to see you!' His Father exclaims.


'It's good to see you also Dad! Have you been watching me wrestle as of late?'


'Of course I have. I am really proud of how well you have done in the ring and as an ice cream man. You have proved to me that you do belong in the business world.' His Dad says with pride. This is the first time he has praised his son like this. It feels good for Pat.


'I really need to put some thought into that Ice Cream Man thing as you may know.'


'Yeah, I heard about that sadly. I heard that you might be living the business for good.'


Pat frowns knowing that his Father must be ashamed of his recent antics. Even Pat knows that leaving the ice cream business would make his Dad ashamed and may even make the fans ashamed.


'I know that I need to stick with it Dad. You would be ashamed of me and disown me as your son if I was to drop the business that kept are family together during your horrible accident about 8 years ago.' Pat replies knowing that his Father is going to lecture him after this.


'You know Pat that I will be proud of you no matter what your decision is. I could careless what you do frankly. I was proud that you helped the family with it but you decided to extend it into your career. You may choose to decide whatever you want. Just follow whatever your heart desires. I just want to say whatever you choose I am happy with your accomplishments.'


Pat has a sigh of relief on his face. For once in his life his Father approves his decision and is content about it. Throughout most of his life his Father would reject any and all ideas that he came up with. Pat should be proud after hearing those words. He stares at his Father who is paying more attention to the television set then him.


'Dad, make sure you watch next weeks show for CHZ. I will have an important announcement about this whole ice cream man thing. I need some time to think about it and I think a week would be appropriate. Any ways can we please drop the subject?'


'Sure, I have had enough with this discussion any ways. I have told you all of my advice and now it is your decision to follow it or not. Just be sure to not let anyone else influence you. Make your own wise choices.'


'On a totally unrelated note I have a Father's Day present here for you. It will prove how grateful I am for all of your help. I will try to express my gratitude in the best ways possible.'


Pat reaches behind the couch and pulls out a package. It is wrapped in CHZ Wild wrapping paper. It has the logo all over the place with a black background. It is topped off with a red bow and a card that is set ion top neatly. He hands the package to his Dad.


'Go ahead Dad, open it right now so I can see how surprised you are!' Pat states anxiously.


His Dad quickly opens the card first and takes a quick glance at it. It reads...


To Dad,


Thanks for the wonderful things you have done in my life. The influences you have had on Collin and I. Also, the gifts that you graciously accepted by us even if they were horrible. I hope you enjoy it because it comes from the heart.


His Dad then opens the present to follow-up with the card. He quickly opens it. The box has a CHZ Logo on the front also. The present may seem to be CHZ related. Anything is possible though.


He finished opening the paper and pops up the box. His eyes light up as Pat smiles knowing that he will love the present. His Father picks up the present in both of his hands and shows it off to his mother. It is a Breyers ice cream shirt that says 'The Best Ice Cream Man and The Best Father!'


'Thank you so much Pat. This gift definitely comes from the heart!'


Pat just keeps smiling realizing he has done a good deed for his Dad today. He finally stops smiling as the camera fades to black...
