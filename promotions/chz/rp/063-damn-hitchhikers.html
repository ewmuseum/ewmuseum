Damn Hitchhikers
Written: 7/08/02

[The scene fades in to a small highway rest stop near the city of Canton, Ohio. The rest stop is near empty except for a large, blue station wagon with wood paneling on the sides and a huge, black full sized van. There is a man sitting in the station wagon looking over a map. There is also a young boy, around six years old, sitting in the back seat playing his Gameboy. Then a door in a nearby building swings open. A woman and her young daughter, five or so years old, coming walking out of the building. As the door swings closed the blue sign on it reveals it is the women’s restroom. The two of them walk over to the blue station wagon. The woman climbs into the passenger’s seat, and the girl in the back next to her brother. The man folds up the map and places it back in the glove box. He turns the key and the station wagon comes to life. He backs out of the parking spot they are in, turns left, and heads out of the rest stop. This leaves the parking lot all alone to the van. A few minutes later a door on the side of the van swings open. A man peers his head out of the door of the van, squinting his eyes to protect them from the bright sun. The man then steps out of the van. That man is Jake. He is clad in only a pair of smiley face boxers and a pair of sandals. He walks over to the restroom building and heads into the men’s bathroom. A minute or two later he comes strolling out again and heads over to his van. He climbs in and turns the key to see what time it is. The neon green numbers of the clock reveal it to be five thirty in the evening. Jake then turns the van off and heads back into the back. He puts on a pair of black mesh shorts with a white stripe down each side and a small And 1 logo on the front of the left leg. He then climbs back into the driver’s seat and starts up the van. He pulls out of the rest stop and onto the highway. He is heading north on the highway. About ten minutes later he drives up an exit ramp and pulls out onto a small city street. He makes a few turns and ends up at one of the houses he was at last night, Chris’ parents house. He pulls up in front of the house and honks the horn. Chris can be seen looking through the window to see who it is. He sees Jake’s van and then heads for the front door. He comes out onto the front porch clad in a pair of red shorts with a crazy flower design on them, and a pair of black and white Vans shoes. Jake rolls down the window and they begin to speak.

“Hey man, I still got your drums from last night, are you going to get them now?” asks Jake as he points into the back of his van where Chris’ drums are all stacked up.

“Sure, I’ll grab them, can you help me with them?” asks Chris as he heads back to the back doors of the van and pops them open. Inside the back of the van sits his drums, as well as Jake’s guitar. Along with that there are four empty Doritos bags; three empty twenty ounce bottles of pop. A half eaten Big Mac and a dirty pair of socks also accompany the instruments.

“Damn, this thing needs cleaned out dude,” says Chris as he looks inside the van. Just then Jake comes around the back to where Chris is standing.

“I know, I know, but that stuff can wait, you just need to get these drums out of here, they take up too much space in my house,” says Jake with a smirk on his face.

“Oh, that’s right, you live here now. So how was the first night in your new home?” asks Chris with a slight chuckle as he begins to unload one of the drums. He picks it up and carries it from the van towards the house. He manages to pull the front screen door open and head into the house. He heads down into the basement and sets the drum down. He then goes back upstairs and outside to get another drum. On the way he passes Jake who is carrying three small drums, a cymbal, and two pairs of drumsticks.

“You know were that stuff goes, right?” ask Chris as he passes Jake who is heading inside.

“Down in the basement, same place they sat for three years when we first started,” says Jake as he moves past Chris and into the house. He heads down the stairs and into the basement. He drops off his load and then heads back to the van to get more stuff. Chris is seen carrying four small drums and another cymbal. Jake grabs two of the drums from him and follows him in the house. They both head down into the basement where they drop off the rest of the stuff along side the things they already brought inside. Chris then heads over to a large, tan colored couch up against the far wall in the room. He sits down on the couch and pulls a small lever releasing the leg rest. Chris then props his legs up and begins to speak.

“Hey man, thanks for the help,” says Chris as he reaches for the TV remote control. “And good luck with the new house, hopefully rent isn’t too much and you can stay, because that is a nice place,” he adds with a joking smile as he presses the power button bringing the twenty seven inch Sony TV to life. He flips through the channels for a moment until he finally settles on A-Team reruns on TNN.

“Wow, why don’t you just quit the band and become a stand up comic or something, because let me tell you, you’re pretty damn funny,” says Jake in a joking, but also serious manner. “Nice choice of shows,” he adds when he sees what Chris has tuned into.

“You think so, I like it too. I’m thinking about getting some jewelry like that,” says Chris referring to all of Mr. T’s golden chains.

“Yeah, that would be a good idea if you weren’t so broke,” replies Jake in a sarcastic manner.

“Hey, at least I live in a house man,” says Chris as he admires all of Mr. T’s shining jewelry.

“That’s funny. If I had known she was going to cheat on me with that guy I would have never moved in with her, smart ass,” says Jake.

“And it’s not like she cheated on you with some rich guy, or some huge muscular dude, or some good looking guy. She cheated on you with some scrawny little computer geek, that has to suck,” adds Chris just trying to humiliate Jake some more.

“Alright, that’s enough out of your mouth,” says Jake as he darts across the basement towards the couch Chris is seated on. He runs over to where Chris is sitting and draws back his fist and blasts Chris in the shoulder.

“Ouch, damn, that hurt,” whines Chris as he begins to rub his shoulder.

“Well then, you should just learn to keep your big mouth shut,” says Jake as he turns around and heads back to where he was standing before. “I’m out of here, I have to go look for a place to live besides my van,” he adds as he walks over towards the stairs.

“See you later man,” says Chris as Jake starts up the steps. Jake ascends up the stairs until he reaches the top, he then pulls open the basement door and heads back to the main floor of the house pushing the basement door shut behind him. He then heads for the front door. Once outside he climbs back into the driver’s seat of his van, turns the key, and pulls away from the small house. He goes back the way he came, heading towards the highway. As he is about to make a right onto the main road he spots a man standing on the side of the road. The man is wearing an old pair of torn up jeans, a Nirvana t-shirt with the sleeves cut off of it, and a pair of tan work boots. The man is holding a white, cardboard sign with the word “Cleveland” written on it. Jake rolls the passenger’s window and begins speaking with the man.

“Hey, you need a ride to Cleveland or something man?” Jake asks the man standing on the side of the road. The man turns and looks at him.

“Yeah, I need to get up there to visit someone, why?” asks the man as he lays his sign down on the ground and walks over to Jake’s van.

“Because I’m heading up that way and I can give you a ride if you need one,” says Jake he pulls open the passenger’s door.

“That’d be great, just let me go grab my stuff,” says the man as he turns and heads back to where his sign is laying on the ground next to some papers and a few other miscellaneous items. He picks them up and heads back over to the van. He hops in the passenger’s seat and tosses his stuff in the back of the van. He pulls his door closed and then Jake drives away. They get on the highway and begin to talk to pass the time during their forty-five minute ride.

“So what’s you name?” inquires Jake as he takes his eyes off of the road and turns to look at the man.

“Jeff, Jeff Wilkins, and you are?” the man replies.

“I’m Jake, nice to meet you Jeff,” says Jake as he turns his focus back to the road. They go on for about another five minutes without talking. Just cruising down the highway at about seventy-five miles per hour. Then finally Jeff breaks the long, awkward silence.

“So Jake, what do you do for a living?” asks Jeff trying to make conversation.

“Nothing. I’m in a band and that makes me some money every now and then. But I’m in professional wrestling school right now, and that doesn’t pay. But if I get, no, no, no, when I get out of there, I’ll be making the big bucks, or at least I hope,” Jake says as he slows down as they pass through one of the many construction zones along the way.

“Oh, I see. I hate professional wrestling. It’s just a bunch of over-grown beasts pounding on each other. You should get a real job like me,” says Jeff as he digs in his pocket for something.

“Oh really, well what do you do for a living?” asks Jake, very curious to see what Jeff does since he thinks he so much better than Jake.

“I have a job any man would love to him…” Jake who is wide-eyed as if he is in shock at Jeff’s job cuts him off in mid-sentence.

“Dude, you’re a porn star?” asks Jake as he looks to see what Jeff has pulled out of his pocket, it’s his wallet.

“Do you want to see a picture of me at work?” asks Jeff as he opens up his wallet and flips through numerous pictures.

“No way, I don’t want to see you naked,” says Jake as he turns to look away from Jeff’s wallet.

“I’m not a damn porn star. I have a good job, a job many would love to have, but few have what it takes to fulfill, I carry on the tradition of the king, I am…an Elvis impersonator,” says Jeff in a very serious voice, but kind of goofy voice. Jake looks at him, then looks at the picture dressed up like Elvis, then begins to crack up laughing.

“Oh, you’re a…oh, damn, that’s hilarious, you’re an Elvis impersonator,” says Jake who can barely get it out due to him laughing so hard. Tears begin to form in his eyes as he pulls the van off to the side of the highway. He is now doubled over laughing, tears streaming down both his cheeks.

“I hardly see what you find so humorous,” says Jeff in a very official tone of voice.

“It’s just that you made it sound like you were so much better than me, because of your job, like you were the king of the world or something, not a damn Elvis impersonator,” says Jake as he begins laughing again. It takes a couple minutes, but Jake finally regains his composure and begins driving again.

“So, is that what you’re going up here for, some kind of Elvis impersonator convention?” asks Jake, wiping away a few stray tears from his eyes.

“As a matter of fact, no, it’s not. I’m going up here to visit my cousin who I haven’t seen in years,” says Jeff who is obviously pissed off at Jake for making fun of what he takes to be a very serious issue, his job. “So, about your stupid wrestling career, you wrestle, but you don’t get paid for it, what kind of crap is that?” asks Jeff who is puzzled by the fact someone would work without getting paid for it.

“It sounds bad, but it’s not, because once I make it out of there and into the big time, I’ll be getting paid good money for doing something I love, kicking ass,” says Jake as makes his into the right hand lane and then proceeds to head up and exit ramp.

“So do you have any battles coming up, or whatever you freaks call them?” asks Jeff as he points to the right instructing Jake to turn right onto the road.

“Matches, yes, as a matter off act, I do have a match coming up, this Saturday against some guy named The Fury,” says Jake as he makes the right and then follows more of Jeff’s directions.

“So, do you think you’re going to win against The Fury,” says Jeff as he makes the quotation marks with his fingers as he say The Fury.

“I don’t know, I hope so, I’m doing good so far and I’d like to keep it up. Are you sure this is the right place, man, there’s nothing here except for us,” says Jake as he looks suspiciously at Jeff.

“Yeah, I’m positive, now get out of the van,” instructs Jeff as he stands up to go over to the driver’s seat.

“What are you talking about, the van is mine, I gave you a ride up here,” says Jake as he looks at Jeff who is still standing waiting for Jake to move.

”What am I talking about? What am I talking about? This is what I’m talking about,” says Jeff as he reaches into a bag he had in his lap the whole time and pulls out a gun. He shoves the semi-automatic pistol right up against Jake’s mouth. The black metal tastes cold and sour against Jake’s lips. Jake doesn’t know what to do for a moment, but finally some words come trembling out of his mouth.

“Ok, but can I ge..get my guitar, please?” asks Jake, who is still seated with the gun in his face.

“Sure, take the damn guitar, then get out of my van,” says Jeff as Jake climbs out of the driver’s seat and into the back. Jeff then sits down in the driver’s seat. Jake heads into the back and grabs his guitar. As he turns to head out the large, sliding door on the side he notices Jeff isn’t paying attention. Jake springs into action. He lunges forward and wraps his arms around Jeff’s throat as tight as he can get them. Jeff’s arms flail backwards wildly trying to reach Jake. Jeff accidentally pulls the trigger sending six shots flying through the roof of the car. Jake then proceeds to wrestle the gun out of Jeff’s hand while still applying the chokehold to him. He grabs the gun and tosses it into the back. He holds the chokehold for another minute until Jeff’s finally stops fighting and his body goes limp and slumps over in the driver’s seat. Jake hops out of the van and yells for one of the people who came running over when they heard the shots to call nine-one-one. The woman pulls out her cell phone and calls. The cops are there in ten minutes. They handcuff Jeff who is now barely beginning to regain consciousness. Jake tells them all about what happened and then they allow him to go after he gives them the gun. He then hops back into the driver’s seat of his van, turn the key, and drives away. As he speeds off he says aloud, although no one is there with him, “Maybe I should try that move on The Fury this Saturday.”
