Anti Hero Pt:2 Conclusion
Written: 31/7/02

He towered over his limp cadaver. The alley was still silent and dark, except for the hookers wheezy choked cries. The blood seeped from the body and morphed into a small stream of crimson. He stabbed him furiously, attacking all the major arteries without hesitation, and then broke his neck with such ease and skill it was clear he had done it before. Now he just stood over the body, looking down at the whiteness in the mans eyes as they had rolled into the back of his head. He displayed no emotion in his hennas attack, and in the night all that was clear was the hypnotic blue shade of his eyes.

He turned and looked at her eventually, merely turning his head to glance at where the muffled cries of sadness and shock were originating from. He then took one final long glance at the dead pimp he had just savagely murdered, before walking over to the weeping hooker cowering in the corner between a wet brick wall and a murky smelly garbage can.

His long black leather jacket swung behind him with the wind as he walked to her. If it hadn't been for him that pimp would likely have raped and killed her and stolen what little money she had left on her. If it hadn't been for him the pimp would have killed her, yet there was no certainty that he now had the same objective and desire to do what the pimp did.

'T-Thank you, he was..... you saved my life'

She let out a cheerful yet still nervous smile. Her hair was long and purple but right now was damp and shapeless. She wore long black leather boots and a tight blue blouse and a short black mini skirt. What he did next however was totally unexpected and made her heart skip about 3 beats.

He grabbed her by the throat with his two hands and lifted her up off the ground and forced her into the wall back first. Her legs dangling several inches off the ground.

'DO NOT! I repeat DO NOT! ... mistake me for one of the superheroes you used to read in marvel comics that your brother used to own. Do not mistake me for a crime fighting saviour like the ones you seen in films'

His voice was calm, deep, but roared like a lion. She tried to scream but her voice had moved out of her body and vacated to parts unknown.

'Do not think of me as someone who watches over this city, do not go walking along the streets at night calmly thinking I will be watching you to make sure no harm will come to you. I am no hero and I am no saviour'

She began nodding furiously, and then ever so slowly he put her back down on her feet.

'I am not the man you who you dream of and think exists in the back of your deepest fantasies. I am not your knight in shining armour. I am the man who causes the damage to force the knight in shining armour to come. Then again, do they ever come? Does the knight in shining armour really exist, does he live on this earth or just in the deepest most exotic and unrealistic depths of your fantasies? I can't answer that question, but I know I am not a knight in shining armour.... I'm the knight who causes your deepest dark depressions'

She looked at him with utter disbelief and confusion. She didn't know whether she was dreaming or actually awake and witnessing this catastrophic unrealistic human before her. What was he telling her? Was there a reason for his comments? Does all what he just had to say mean something, something so deep you have search for the meaning? She didn't know, but his voice. Oh his voice, she couldn't resist hearing the words roll off his tongue calmly. She clung to his every word .

'You know, the typical hero would let you walk away and live. In the books and films after being saved you would stop this unholy profession and start a new. Begin a new life, get a descent job and right all your wrongs. BUT! That very, very rarely happens now doesn't it? Yes it does. I'll tell you something I don't think I've ever witnessed it happen before my eyes'

His eyes were intense as he looked into hers. Was he looking into her mind? Her thoughts? She didn't doubt it for a second.

'So child, let me ......... let me do you a favour. Let me help you right all the wrong you so deliberately and shamefully committed during your worthless and pathetic life. Let me help you..... do us all a favour. Let me help you make things reasonably right'

He slowly moved in close to her, his hands sliding up from her waist to her face slowly as if he was about to kiss her passionately. He slowly and gently gripped his hands underneath her chin. His touch was cold yet soothing. He then knelt his head in close and placed his lips next to her ear. Then he began to whisper calmly, whisper in that subtle yet strong voice. She was at his mercy again, this was better than any mad night of sex she ever had. Hearing his voice seemed erotic in a modified and controversial way. Her legs quivered slightly as his hot breath rang in her ear as he spoke, she almost felt like she was going to orgasm.

* * * * * * * * * * * ** * * ** * *

Some people would describe what Axl did when he manipulated people as .... twisted. He liked that. Someone described it as being exactly like in the movie, The Silence Of The Lambs. When Hannibal Lector whispered to one of his prisoners and made him swallow his own tongue. Now Axl liked that description very much, and took it as a compliment. He enjoyed abusing the human mind and how to manipulate it after studying psychology. It made him feel somewhat superior.

Now he used that manipulation once again on this pathetic useless hooker. He manipulated her and made her do what he wanted her to do. It gave him a sick pleasure when he did it.

Now she walked. Naked. Her clothes flung carelessly on the floor where she stood a few moments ago. He watched with sick amusement as she trod over broken pieces of glass which cut and sliced into her feet. Yet she kept walking, she was under his trance now. Her purple hair glided back as the wind blew against her face. Her teeth chattered slightly from the cold, yet on she walked. On and on through the alley she walked. His voice seduced and entranced her. She soon walked out of the alley, a few drunken middle aged men howled some wolf whistles at her as they seen her. She was oblivious to them. All she had to do was walk he said, so she did.

The bright beaming headlights from the truck shone in her face and eventually broke her out of her trance. She turned and looked in horror as the sound of the trucks horn invaded the night. It was too late. The truck mowed her down and she skidded under each tyre with disturbing force. Then she lay there, motionless and dead.

'I told you I would you put the wrong things right. Now you can do no more harm child. Your away from this world. Now your tribe of prostitution has one less sleazy tramp to worry about.'

He bared a grin as he spoke the words and walked out of the alley. He then threw his head back chucking some of his long black hair out of his face.

'Three other people will fall victim to a similar fate as this tramp did tonight, on August 7th, I will be the last soul standing amongst a set of worthless fallen scumbags.'

Another grin made its way up his right cheek as he spoke these words and left quickly in the night.


Fade to black.
