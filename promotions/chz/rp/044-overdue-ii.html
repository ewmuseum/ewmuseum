Overdue II
Written: July 4, 2002

We open up to a glare from the rising sun. We move out of the glare and find that we are in none other than Luke Black’s living room. There is a window above his black leather couch. The sunlight filters through it and reflects off of a mirror on the other side of the room. The light shines in Luke’s face and he begins to stir.

“Uh…my head…”Luke says groggily leaving his Old English accent behind, for now, as he sits up. Luke looks about the room realizing where he is. He rubs his eyes and shakes his head to wake him self up more. He looks to his left to see the time: 7:33.

It is at this time that he sees the blinking red light of the answering machine. “They never answer…” He mumbles to him self, obviously talking about his servants. He reaches over and presses the play button.

* Beeeep! *

“You have…three messages.” The programmed machine’s voice announces, “Wednesday, July 3rd, eleven fifty-six P.M.”

“Uh…yeah….” Chimes in Bob’s slow recorded voice, “This is Bob from Bob’s XXX Porno Rental Store, no relation to the owner. You have an overdue porno from our store. Uh…’Real’ Cock Fights? Oh no. My mistake; its Buffy: The Vampire Layer. That’s layer with an ‘L’ not an ‘S.’ It’s been overdue for about three weeks now. So if your hands are too ‘sticky’ you’ll have to return it by Saturday, before midnight, or we’ll have to charge you as if you bought it and then you’ll still have to return it. So it would be in your best interest to return the tape as soon as possible. If you have any questions please call us at 555-BobX. The store hours are four p.m. to six a.m.. Thank you.”

Luke looks surprised at what the message is. The second message begins as Luke looks around his living room looking for videotape. He finds it forgotten under the mantel on the fireplace. He looks at it for a bit, confused. “I didn’t rent this!” He says aloud, “Who would rent this crap?”

Just then, one of Luke’s servants walks into the room drying his hands off. He looks over at Luke and Luke looks over at him. The servant, whose name just happens to be Keith, turns around and walks out of the room after seeing what Luke has found. Luke rushes after him and stops him by grabbing his shirt.

“You rented this, didn’t you?” Luke shouts at Keith.

“Uh…” Keith begins nervously, “I don’t know what you’re talking about.”

In the background the third message begins.

“That’s what I’m talking about! The porn and using my name!”

“Oh…ha, ha. That. Well—“

“I don’t need your excuses! Just tell me where I can find this porno store.”

“Main Street! Big neon sign. You can’t miss it!”

Luke shoves Keith down and takes off towards the front door. As he opens it, a cute little girl scout stands there about to knock on the door.

“Would you like to buy some cookies, sir?” The girl scout asks politely.

“No, I don’t want to buy any of your expensive cookies!” Luke says as he pushes the little girl on her way. He leaves the mansion, shutting the door behind him.

He hurries off toward one of his cars: a dark blue Spectre. He unlocks the drivers’ side door, tosses the video into the passengers’ seat and gets in. He revs up the engine and backs out of the driveway. He begins his way to Bob’s XXX Porno Rental Store. Long name, huh?

As Luke leaves the neighborhood, as car waiting follows Luke. Luke looks back and thinks nothing of it. The car gets really close to Luke and follows his every turn. He starts to suspect something and pulls over. He gets out of the car and approaches the car that has been following him. A few men get out, quickly followed by a fat man. They are all dressed in black or navy blue suits. The fat one approaches Luke and introduces him self and the others.

“Hello.” He begins innocently enough, “We are the Mafia. I’m ‘Fat’ Joey. These are my men: Jeff, John and Frank. We’re here to stop you from getting that porn video in on time.”

“Wait? If you’re the Mafia, why are dealing with my little problem?” Luke asks confused.

“We aren’t your ordinary Mafia. We are Bob’s XXX Porno Rental Store Mafia.”

“Bob has his own Mafia?” Luke questions surprised.

“That’s right. And so far we’ve been able to keep you from returning that porno you have in your car. But this idiot over here lost the tape. So if you could so kindly return it to us, we would be ever so grateful and you could go on your way.”

Luke looks the men up and down and shouts, “NEVAH!” And rushes his way back to his car.

“Get him.” “Fat” Joey tells his men calmly and they rush up to Luke, guns drawn.

“Come with us.” Jeff says, “And take the video with you.”

Luke reaches over and grabs the video. He hands it over to Jeff who hands it to John. Jeff motions for Luke to get out with his gun and Luke follows. They grab Luke and push him over to the car. Frank goes into the trunk of their car and pulls out some rope. They tie Luke’s hands together and shove him into the backseat. They all pile into the car and drive off, leaving Luke’s car behind.
