Defeated?
Written: 8/09/02

The lights cut through the dark alley like a hot knife, scattering countless vermin in equally as countless directions. The head lights of the black car came to a stop on the back of a silver hummer parked next to the wall of the dark alley.

Matt struggled to open his eyes. The light was blinding, considering he was fast asleep in the back of his truck, but he managed to squint through the beams, as groggily as he could, and slowly sat up, wiping up some drool off the side of his face as he did so.

He shook his head and pulled his nose-length red hair out of his face, leaving it in tangled knots behind his ears. He was still in his wrestling attire and felt a little cold now. More than that, Matt felt as stiff as a board, like he was hit by a dump truck... twice. A hard fought wrestling match followed by a nap in the back of a truck will do that to a guy.

'Carol-Anne... come into the light?' Matt asked in a sleep-deprived, joking tone, rubbing his eyes, and inadvertently rubbing the dirt on his hands into them. He blinked a couple times as a small figure stepped out of the bright beams and off to the side where Matt could make out who he was.

'Kid, what the hell are you doin'?' X quietly demanded as he crossed his arms. He was still wearing his Disciple suit, provided to all the field superiors like him. Matt could see past his slicked back gray hair and wrinkled features to decipher that X had a slightly angered look on his face.

Matt blinked again and stretched and yawned loudly. 'Well, I'm wearing my ring attire... oh crap, I must be in a match! Inevitable Judgment, Inevitable Judgment!' he exclaimed as he jumped to his feet and frantically looked around his truck. Unable to contain himself, Matt broke out into a laugh and took a seat on the roof of the truck. 'I was sleeping you old dolt!'

X smirked and shook his head. 'I expected you to report back after the match Matt, and I find you here in an alley, sleeping in the back of your truck? Kid, what am I supposed to do? Sit at HQ and pretend our conversation never happened?'

Matt put his hands up into the air and arched away from X a little. 'Whoa! Holah senor, chill out bro! Man, I didn't want to head back to HQ. I was tired, I was sore and I felt like I could use a night away from those... uh. What do you call those deprived sardine cans we sleep in?'

'Beds,' X bluntly chimed in.

'Yeah! Beds!'

X sighted for a moment and shook his head, chuckling a little as he returned his gaze to his subordinate. 'You're chipper tonight for a man who got his ass royally kicked by a chick.'

Matt grinned and shrugged again. 'Hey, what can I say man? They all hit on me! I mean, I think Christina even had a thing for me. Oh, by the way, how is the lass doing?'

'She's doing fine, all considering. That was some report you filled out kid... if I wasn't who I was, I'd have you tossed into a padded room and then I'd throw away the key.'

Matt nodded and smiled again, rubbing some dirt off of his teeth with his thumb. 'Anyways, yeah, I got my ass kicked in that match, but what does it matter? I made the American Hero tap out, I didn't hit Summers and I took the most punishment in that match only to both win it unofficially, and then come within an inch of winning it officially. At least I lost by count out... left my ego nice and in tact eh?'

X rubbed his forehead with his hand and mumbled something to himself. 'Yeah, lord knows that's not big enough.'

Matt laughed and jumped off the side of the truck, landing on the paved surface beside X. After shaking out the remaining cob webs, Matt surveyed his surroundings. It looked like... an alley. Rodent ridden garbage, dirty ground littered with bottles and even the brick encasement created by the sides of the buildings.

'Please tell me that you didn't come here to give me an assignment,' Matt muttered, sounding like he was dreading the answer. In truth, the field man had a hard enough experience with his last case to not want another one. Besides... he could still feel the bumps and bruises atop his head from the demons that infested his previous assignment.

'Actually,' X said with a tone of enthusiasm, 'you don't get to mess anything up this time. You're free this weekend, hence, you can work completely on your wrestling.'

Matt instantly let out a Ric Flair 'whoo' and did more or less, the whitest looking dance anyone could have ever seen. Snapping fingers, swinging hips, closed eyes as he got into his rhythm... it was the cast of Wings all over again. X broke out into a laugh as Matt finished the dance off and cracked his neck.

'Man, that's the best news I've heard all week!' he exclaimed, slapping X hard on the shoulder. 'If you weren't so damn ugly, I could kiss you!'

'That would answer a lot of questions, wouldn't it?' X muttered to himself. The old man rubbed his chin a little, submerging into thought as Matt patiently waited with his own arms crossed. Matt hated it when X started to think... it usually meant something bad for Matt.

'So, anything I should know?' Matt asked in a less enthusiastic tone of voice. The wrestler never liked to hear any news from X... considering he only really ever heard bad news from the man.

'Well,' X began, still rubbing his chin intently, 'you have a match at the next Wild. It's in Edmonton and you're teaming with Summers to take on Axl Young and Ryan Contra.'

Matt smirked a little and twisted himself so that his back cracked. He still felt the repeated chair shots from Contra. He could also feel Axl beating away on him, even if he was a mile away from the ring.

'So... I gotta' work as a team with Summers and take down a couple people who I could do without? Last time I heard, she was a tad bit offended that I refused to hit her,' Matt said, crossing his arms and turning his eyes to the ground as he thought out his situation. 'I owe Contra one... that's for sure.'

X shook his head a little. 'I know we both believe in God, Matt... but you shoulda' clocked her one kid.'

'X!'

X casually shrugged again. 'It's true. Anyways, you are a follower of God. It is he who guides you, not the other way around. The Bible doesn't teach vengeance... and it's written that it belongs only to your lord and savior, son. Don't go doing something foolish in wrestling, 'cuz you'll pay for it as a Disciple.'

Matt sighed and nodded slowly, curling his mouth into an aggravated frown as he did so. 'Yeah, I gocha' man.'

X smiled a little and tapped Matt on the shoulder. 'God forgives Matt, and just between you and me... I'm sure he thinks Contra is a prick too. If vengeance belongs to God, then he'll get it through you. Do what you have to... God will understand, yeah?'

Matt grinned a little and nodded a little more enthusiastically. 'Yeah, he'll understand.'

Matt shook his head, spraying the dirt out of his hair, causing X to cover his face. The CHZ superstar closed the tail gate of his truck and leaned against the side of the hummer, lowering his head, then looking up again..

Matt paused for a moment, then looked X straight in the eye. 'Hey X. I got a question for ya'. I have a feeling people don't think I'm tough enough to be a wrestler. What do you think?'

'I don't think you're tough enough to be a man,' X muttered with a smirk, 'but, you've got the talent kid. A loss is a loss, and you're not exactly the highest ranked man in the federation.'

Matt shrugged as if the words didn't phase him, but deep down... it stung like a scorpion sting. 'Well, what do I do then? How do I make people notice Matt Blitz?'

X smiled, a somewhat dark smile for a man of God and patted Matt on the shoulder. 'Kid... do what you have to do.'
