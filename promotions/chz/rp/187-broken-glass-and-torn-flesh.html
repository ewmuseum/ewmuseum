Broken Glass and Torn Flesh
Written: 9/15/02

I look down at the small drops of blood fall from my hand. I softly pluck a few of the larger pieces of glass from deep within my flesh. The floor beneath my feet has slowly become stained with crimson and covered with glass. In a fit of rage I had put my fist threw the mirror just above my dresser. I'm not quite sure what came over me.

My emotions are shot. I have little control over them. Sometimes I'm happy, sometimes I'm angry. But always I am always afraid. Fearing for my life. Fearing for my own safety. It fucking sucks. Life fucking sucks. I wish my life would end. But alas... I am forever plagued. Banished. Thrust towards this Earth like a jagged rock towards a soft skull.

I will always live. Century after century will pass and still, I shall remain. Locked within myself. Bound by the strongest of metals, never to be free, all for the amusement of some sick fuck many would call 'God.' The single being whom has fucked me up the ass time and time again, and shows no signs of cessation. Some folks may call 'God' 'Our Father.' Others may go the other way calling it 'Mother.' Please... 'God' is neither paternal nor maternal. 'God' has no cock. No cunt. 'God' has power. The most disgusting power my eyes have ever had the misfortune of seeing. Fuck 'God.' I lead my own life.

I kill people... Mutilate, dismember. Ripping, tearing, bashing. That is my game. And I eat up every second of it... Some may call me sick... Others may call me a profit. I am neither. I am simply an addict.

My lover is trapped a small, darling body. But never let that fool you. She is my partner in crime. I drain the life from a body and she takes it apart, puts a few select pieces into an ice chest. She brings them home, cooks them and devours them. A cannibal. She may very well be the only human on this Earth even sicker then I... She is the Ying to my Yang, so to speak...

I make my way from my bedroom and into the living room. I plop myself down in a big black leather chair. Almost throne like in shape. I rest one arm on a rest while with the other I gently stroke my long goatee. A classic sign of being lost in thought. My fingers tap away against the armrest while my other fingers continue to stroke. Stroke, tap, stroke, tap, stroke. My finger tapping becomes quite violent of an action. I don't notice. The black peals from my nails and still, I pay no attention. Spirit and body separate. My mind is now on foreign territory. The sickening mixture of tissue and fluid I call my body is left behind. This beautiful separation is brought to a screeching halt as I feel my lap being weighed down. Weighed down with about 122 pounds.

Samantha!

Hello love.

We share a deep, passionate kiss. All my troubles float away like angels just earning their wings... Yes, the human body is indeed disgusting yet Samantha seems to have done it just right. Long, silky, black hair, big, beautiful blue eyes, full lips painted black of course, soft yet pale skin, and of course the perfect body. She has no faults what so ever. She is perfect, in every aspect. I never tire of hearing her voice, nor of seeing her face, for she is the one thing that has kept me from ending my life. I owe everything I have to her. I love her to death. Hmm. Funny I should use the word 'death' in such a warm and loving sentence. Death is what I give my victims. Death is what I have given most of my family. Death is what I give to whomever gets in my way.
