Ross Beckons Part 1: Abducted
Written: 09/02/02

Lasting images seeing Angel Summers hand raised in victory gave me a reality check and putting in perspective why I am really here, to win the World Title. While there are men before me that have dibs on it, in there minds they know there time is drawing near and that brings me to the one they call Blake Raines. Indeed, he is what they call a standout, but that means nothing when he meets with me and faces the fact that his world shall slowly crumble like the Berlin Wall.

It happened so quickly…

Police cars surrounded me and there was no fear just the question on why are they hear? Rain poured down on the street as everyone came out of their buildings looking at me as if I was some criminal like I had did something wrong. My body was warm from the thickness of my black coat and pants but there was a sudden chill shooting down my spine.

“This must be the end… I accept your proposal.” I said to myself pretty much insinuating my end.

There foots steps were slow and methodical; everything was going in slow motion even the reaction of the crowd was going slower than usual. A sudden tapping on my window did not startle me but let me know something horrible was coming. I opened the window with two police officers pulling to the ground beating me with there stick of brutality leaving me there paralyzed momentarily.

“Ross is going to be pleased, now we are going to get our bonuses!” One the police officers said with much excitement.

“Yeah, and they said he couldn’t be caught… what a joke.” Another said jokingly while slapping me.

Tears streamed down my cheeks knowing with no Kayla, I was truly alone, rain poured down making my once short and laid down spiky hair to looking something resembling a mushroom. They threw me inside the police car roughly with my hands behind my back I was rendered helpless as we drove away.

“Well if it isn’t Mr. Lawrence, it was a real pleasure killing your parents.” He said while laughing and driving the car at the same time.

“Yeah, there is nothing better than watching there insides show and blood starts to run like a river, it is so exhilarating.”

My eyes widened and anger filled my heart knowing that the murders I have long been looking for are in the same vehicle. As I looked out the window, rain slide down the window and there the voices that haunted me over the year has played in my head.

“Don’t do it!” My father voice cried

“Please have mercy!” My mother pleaded

Soon after the sound gunshots went off inside of my head insinuating there end and that is when my hatred began. There laughter echoed throughout the vehicle fueling the rage that filled my heart and there was only thought in my mind… kill.

“Not the talkative type eh?” Said the passenger trying to provoke an argument

“Have you ever thought of the repercussions of your sins?” I said in a low and demented tone.

There laughter stopped and they look at the rear view mirror at the twisted expressions that came across my face. One picked up the cell phone and started pressing numbers as the other kept a sharp eye on the road and at the same time looking concerned at my face.

“How does it feel to be the victim?”

“Who exactly is the victim in this situation me or you?”

He paused for a moment collecting his thoughts and did not try to talk anymore, the chill continued racing down my spine making me shiver. Finally, his friend hung up the phone and started to whisper something in his ear as they continued to ride faster and faster.

“Looks like your in luck; Ross wants us to bring you to him.” He said gleefully

“He’s not a fool; even Ross doesn’t want to face my wrath.”

I could hear there growling and moaning at my comments, and the rain poured down like a fist on a roof. There impatience was eminent as they did not agree on some things and they were playing right into my trap. Their arguing continued for five more minutes until a full-blown verbal fight ensued.

“It is your fault Chris!” The driver cried

“Bullshit bob! You know you had everything to do with this screw up!” Chris yelled at bob.

“Ha, looks like tensions are at an all time high.” I said to myself watching them argue.

They looked back, saw me looking down with my hair lying partial over my face, and went back to the blame game. I could not fill the chill running down my back and as we turned, I saw a huge castle in the distance that looked dark and gloomy. Suddenly they stopped the car and both me got out of the car, went into the side of the road, and started to fistfight.

“Idiots,” I said, “Do they not know I am still in the car?”

Continually they fought and I watched laughing to myself but at the same time trying to wiggle, my hands free from the cuffs but to no avail. Rain started to our down once more and they continued to fight but it was getting annoyed and my eyes were starting to close slowly.

Thirty minutes later

As I opened my eyes, a bright light shined in my face making me shield my eyes. Though it was dark, I could feel the surface that was cold and hard, my breathing was deep and my body was stripped of all its clothing except my white boxers.

“Welcome Samuel,” a cold dry voice called out “You are here to be put in way of righteousness.”

“Working for Ross is a pleasurable thing; you can go to higher heights in life.” Another voice cried.

Silently I sat there only listening to dry voices, which were obviously men. There slow and soft sinister laughs made me angry they can see it but they chose to hide behind the light that was blinding. Second after second my sanity was drawing the boiling point and the sudden tapping on a brown table echoed through the room and it somewhat calmed me down.

“Have you given up already?”

“No one can escape; being under Ross’s influences is something to be proud of.”

“Shut the fuck up!” I yelled and at that moment, I reached a point where enough was enough.

Silence came over the voices for a couple of minutes helping me to collect myself after the outburst but it was hard being in a darken with the only light is where my abductors hide like cowards.

“Your foolishness is only going to make it harder on you, Blake Raines, and Ross when he makes his way down here.” I screamed at the top of my lungs but no one seemed to answer.

Soon after their laughter became louder and louder almost startling me and they continued for a couple of more minutes as in my mind they saw the anger and frustration written all over my face.

“Mr. Lawrence, you are quite a riot and therefore tomorrow MR. Ross is going to be hear.”

“So do you have anything to say?”

I did not answer for a while but I can feel there impatience and agitation filling the room that made me smile.

“Answer the question!” They both screamed at the same time

“When this is over your blood and Ross’s blood shall be on my hands.” I said in a low and demonic voice.

“Interesting observation but that is unlikely to happen.”

With those words the light slowly dim and once again I was left in the dark room alone having my mind set on confronting Ross and on Wednesday Wild on the newcomer Blake Raines. Though things do not look good Raines, your blood shall be smeared all over the ring and in my hands.
