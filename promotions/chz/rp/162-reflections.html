Reflections
Written: 25/8/02

Nearly every time I looked at my reflection I felt so many mixed emotions I felt weak at the knees. They say a picture speaks a thousand words, well my reflection spawned a thousand emotions of sorrow and retribution. Yet looking at it in the water right now seemed to change things slightly, seemed to make things slightly more calmer, not much, but just a little, and sometimes just a little comfort being added to this painful emotions I beheld was welcomed and embraced with open arms by me. Yet I didn't want too much comfort. I needed these bitter emotions, they were a main cog in the twisted machine I had new become, they were a main factor in helping me become the deep dark demented human being I now was. These emotions were a remainder of why I detested everything negative in this world and why I wanted to desecrate it. It was because of these effects these emotions brought me that in my own sick twisted way, I enjoyed them, but you can never enjoy emotions like these too much.

The pond was a bright relaxing blue, mainly because of the bright sky reflecting off it. The nearby trees guarded the area like an army of soldiers standing at attention. They were guarding me as well. I felt like no one could get to me or hurt me here because of those trees, those trees that were protecting me proudly and daringly, I worshipped those trees right now. These trees were comforting but my suffering wasn't, why can't my suffering be comforting like these trees dammit!

This area was so bright and colourful, but I seemed to bring darkness to it. Why didn't I want to destroy this place, after al; everything that was good and cheerful in this world was only a another constant reminder to me of what I would likely never have again. So why wasn't I destroying this now, why wasn't I tearing it to pieces, burning it, dismantling it?! The only reason I could come up with was because I was too wrapped up in the happening in CHZ, the one place I was slowly beginning to hate with a passion. It was filled with people with hopes and reams and high expectations. That made me sick. I was only there to unleash the psychotic and twisted hate I had for most of this world out on opponents who were to wrapped up in their useless lives to realise what the world really was, a cesspool of vulgarity.

Angel escaped. That made me seethe. I was unable to sacrifice her. That made me furious. I now had the chance to decimate three other worthless souls in a double cage on Sunday. That made me fill up inside with a sick pleasure.

I ran my hand through my long black hair, it had grown considerably I noticed. I remember how much Tanya would play with my hair, how she loved to run her fingers through it. God I missed those days, God I missed her. I thrust my hand down and snatched some grass up in anger and tossed it violently to the side, not that it was going to make me feel any better or change anything. Sometimes thinking about the past can be both pleasing and painful. I would think about The Cadaver Dogz, my former rock band. The long months touring relentlessly, busting my ass off night after night, show after show with my band. The feel of my fingers slamming against the strings and threats of my guitar. I missed those days as well.

Yet I had no immediate need to think about the past right now, instead I wanted to focus on the future. Especially that double cage match. Good things come to those who wait, well I've done my fair share of waiting, and now it was time for me to enjoy the good things. The good things would be annihilating Ryan Contra, destroying Matt Blitz, and polluting the mind and desecrating the body of Angel Summers. The four of us would be confined and locked in a solid structure consisting of two double steel cages. Just like my emotions, the steel cage would entrap me, and just like my emotions, I would battle and fight through it. and emerge victorious

I slowly rose to my feet. I need to leave this place now, I've brought my darkness to it and I need to leave before it lingers and stays. But the worst darkness shall fall and linger in the souls of three unsuspecting victims at Mind Games.

Victims .......... aren't we all?

Fade
