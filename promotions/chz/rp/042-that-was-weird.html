That was Weird
Written: 7/02/02

On Wednesday I beat my first opponent and the repercussions are that press all around Canada will be hounding me making a big deal out of me being the first blind man to win a wrestling match. That was the last thing I wanted, all I wanted to do was go back home. But all that was at my home was nothing. Since my wife and kids death, it has been hell ever since. So wrestling is my escape goat from hold harsh reality that I face everyday in the states.

Vega, it is not just a catchy name it is a name that symbolizes the pain Willis James has gone through his whole life. He is not like any person you have met because he is blind; he has to deal with the idiocy that is said from the mouth of men and women who have been said to be fair with all. Now he has to face a bigger challenge then ever before, he has to deal with wrestling and the publicity that will obviously come from it.

It was a cold night as Vega sat on the ledge of a building bowing his head down in shame; he suddenly heard the voice of his child in his head. “Daddy Help!” she screamed out but there was nothing that he could do, he had to sit there listening to his daughter’s cry go in vain. He was wearing some black leather pants that went well with his white shirt and black leather jacket; his silky black hair blew in the wind as he gazed into the stars only to see darkness that was caused by his blindness. He reached in his pocket pulling out a picture of his daughter, he smiled before breaking down and crying his eyes out.

“I wish you could be here Donna,” he said softly “so I’ll keep your dream alive I will be the first blind champion in CHZ history.” He carefully placed the letter back into his pocket before standing up and dropping down to the roof top. Vega could here the sound of footsteps approaching; he continues walking until he got to shadow just beyond the boiler. He grabbed the air seemingly grabbing someone by the throat and he threw the mysterious stranger dressed in a black suit against the edge.

“Who are you?” The mysterious stranger pulled off her mask showing her black skin, green eyes and long black hair. She stood up in a fighting position and he stood there not saying a word. “What are you waiting for? Fight me you filthy piece of crap!” Vega does not answer; he turns his back on her and walks away. He could hear her seething at the mouth and heard her footsteps quickly approaching and as she got within a couple of feet, he gave a backhand to her jaw and she fell to the ground grabbing her jaw.

“I’m sorry for hitting you, but you had it coming.” She quickly rose to her feet as the blood was dripping from her mouth, she pulled out a small mirror looking at her bloody lip and she smiled sadistically. “I just want to know when John Wilson died and gave birth to Vega.”

“Very simple, when all that Willis James stood for was taking away from him.” He walked around the corner of the building exiting out of a door that led to a dark staircase. He cautiously walks down the step focusing on each sound his foot made when touching the hard steps. A door was heard opening with lights revealing the green walls and brown steps. It was the same girl he had backhand just on the roof top, she was trying to sneak up on him when he heard her with ease and stopped abruptly.

“What do you want girl,” he said with some impatience and she continued to walk down the steps but had a friendly grin that didn’t look sarcastic at all. “I wanted to fight you, I heard about your huge battle with Syndicate and wanted to see if you were as good as everyone said you were.”

He smiled at the woman and while scratching his head, he started to shakes his head in confusion. “I’m sorry, I do not know your name,” he said in some sort of confusion and she gave Vega a big smile and said, “It is Bethany Yates.” He nodded his head in approval as they both stepped down step by step until they got down to the brown platform.

He could hear a voice calling his name, “Willis, you will never lose me.” He quickly turned to the right and started walking down the hall. Bethany looked puzzled as he made his way down the hall cautiously. He could hear his footsteps on the creaking floor until he started to here laughing that seemed to get louder and louder by the minute.

“Please, do not forget me,” the voice said once more and Vega had a confused look on his face and he knew the voice was coming just ahead. He slowly walks to the door and as he placed his hand on the door it seemed to go straight through it. He then stepped through the door and it just disappeared as Bethany ran to the empty wall pounding on it for dear life.

While inside the room, he looked at the dusty room that was empty with its tattered and peeling walls with debris blowing through the room. Suddenly, through the wall, there were a couple of ghostly figures that came through the wall. They started to take shape as one of the ghost to a form of a young girl and the other as a beautiful woman. “Daddy, it has been awhile.”

His eyes got wider and his bottom lip started to tremble as tears started to roll down his eyes. “Donna... honey… you’re here?” She floated over to Vega and placed her hand on his shoulder and his shaking started to slowly settle down. The woman above started to float on his left side and put her hand on his left shoulder.

“Oh son, I missed you so.” He was speechless as he had heard that voice before when he was younger. “Mom, is that you?” We hear Vega say in an uncertain tone and she starts to cry but no tears come down her cheeks. They float around a little bit and they abruptly stop getting serious looks on their faces.

“Willis, were proud of you and we cannot tell you how much you have grown.” He smiled as he started to cry once more as his daughter Donna floated right in front of his face touching his face very gently. “Don’t worry daddy we will be together again.” He nodded his head in approval and cursed under his breathe.

“They took away time they belonged to us and I will never forgive them for that.” She nodded her head as they slowly started to dissolve but before the fully went away he heard is daughter say faintly, “No matter what, I will always still be your angel.” Vega was left in the room by himself with total silence as the door started appear Bethany came in looking very concerned.

“What did I miss,” and Vega replied with a smile, “just a visit from my guardian angel.”

As they started to walk down the lonely hall, Bethany started to shake her head in confusion. She turned to Vega and with a confused look on her face she said, “Who is your new manager?” He only shook his head and walked away from her, but she did not give up that easily as she turned him around pinning him to the right wall. “Tell me, please?” He dusts himself off and shakes his head no, this makes her furious until he laughs wile saying, “One of my manager’s is you, but the other will maybe have people shaking there heads.” She looked very shocked and finally shook her head in disbelief; he shook his head yes as she mouths the words “no”.

“Isn’t he considered Insane?” He shakes his head yes and immediately without hesitation she slaps her hand on her forehead still looking very confused. “Relax we have an agreement, plus look at it this way two managers are better than one.” Bethany placed her chin nodding her head in agreement. Vega then stopped, scratched his head and started to say, “Wild is like a week or so away, it looks like win number two for the blind insane luchadore… damn, when your this good it should be against the law. Oh well, looks as if Kazzuality, Rage, and the Mike guy will just be victims of the five core.”
