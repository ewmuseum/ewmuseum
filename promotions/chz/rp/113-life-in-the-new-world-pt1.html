Life In The New World.pt1
Written: 8/01/02

{{{BEGIN FEED}}}

The sound of silence is pure, like life could not be better at any given time. Usually the silence occurs around early morning, when the sun has yet to come out. The deep streets of Toronto are completely silent, focusing on the three-floored brick apartment building, the home of Angel Summers and now Robyn Stiles as well. The days have gone on quite slowly, Angel’s days consist of working on her tactics at the gym, and Robyn unpacking, and trying to settle in at her new home.

On this particular day Robyn was going to go on with her everyday procedure, such as unpacking, and exploring the city. Angel had a different plan today. On one of her late runs last night she passed a learning center, and with the big heart of Angel’s she stopped in, they were pleased that she had signed up to take in a child every other day. The work consisted of help reading, writing and whatever the child is lacking. Angel’s first day was today, still quite early, not yet awoken from her queen sized bed, the covers all pushed to the end of the bed, the heat from the rising sun beginning to seep in through the cracks in the blinds of her bedroom. Finally the bright sun peaks through the blinds and directly hits Angel in the eyes, which automadically is her alarm to wake up. She slowly gets out of bed, wearing normal summer pajamas, such as a tang top and underwear. She takes a long stretch letting out a moan to clear her throat. Then makes her way over to her closet, picking out her clothes for the day, once she decides she carries them over the bathroom, as she takes her morning shower. About 15 minutes later she was at the kitchen table enjoying a bowl of Special K for breakfast. Robyn is not up yet, and will not be for at least another 4-5 hours as it is still 7:00am. Angel enjoys two bowls of Special K, then proceeds by getting her shoes on, she puts her runners on because she has decided to run to the learning center to get some early morning exercise.

She arrives in good time. The learning center direct, Morgan Freeman was awaiting her at the front door with all smiles, she directed her two her individual child. The child was squared off in the corner away from the rest of the children. Waiting for Angel to arrive, as he sits with his needed books. Morgan directs her to the child, and takes off around the corner to contact the other children. The two were left alone, still without an introductory, Angel still glancing at the child from a far, as the little Asian boy is starring out the window of open field. Angel makes the first contact.

“ Hello there, I am Angel and I will be your tutor.”

The boy took a jump back at first, but then settled in and addressed Angel in a quiet voice.”

“ Hi”

(Pause)

“My name is Michael.”

Angel smiles and takes a seat on the two-seated couch next to Michael. Michael looked around nine, an age where they are beginning to read longer books, novels. A difficult age where many new things are taught to them. So Angel starts out with introductory about him. Like Family, recreation and anything that is happening in the boy’s life. Michael obviously being a shy child takes his time to think, and begins.

“ My name is Michael Stabile. My parents name are Pedro and Alice, I have no siblings and play no sports and have no hobbies.”

Angel clueing in why the child is withdrawn from the other children. Some only child children become withdrawn because of no siblings. The child is used to playing alone, therefore plans to keep it that way. Michael also explained that his parents work a lot and he is regularly alone in his home. He plays no sports because money is tight, and has no hobbies because he is usually the one cooking dinner and cleaning his home. He explained, he was just trying to help out around the house.

Angel then knew it was time to hit the books, so they started with reading a novel. It was a love story, which he picked out from the bookshelf, which was rare for a young boy to pick. He began reading, and the direct problem was his stuttering while he reads, generally by trailing some words. Angel told him to take a deep breath after each word, so that would decrease the stuttering. It worked with most words, still the odd few but that can always be worked on. Most of the day, they read. Some math and creative writing was slipped in also, but the main problem for the boy was speech, so reading was key. They finished up the day, by finishing the second chapter. Angel stayed around until his parents picked up Michael. Finally about thirty minutes late, a beat up brow and rusty truck pulled up, loud as could be, Michael waved good-bye to Angel and hopped into the truck and drove off. Angel not getting a good looks at the driver. Angel then feeling that may have been the most boring day of her life, but still feeling pretty good about helping a child in need. Angel is new to the city, so she has really not much to do besides work out, and prepare for her match. So she needed something to fill her time. The Baseball game way a great time and they plan to go another time before the end of the season. Angel then thought of getting a job as a bartender, as she would be over qualified because she has her own bar back home. These are all just ideas in a bored woman’s head. Hopefully something will happen soon in her life before she goes crazy.
