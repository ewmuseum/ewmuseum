I'm In Jail!
Written: 28/7/02

'And by the looks of it Mr. Serio, I will have to sentence you to prison for 10 days,' says the judge as Serio jazzes up at him.

'Oh s***!' Serio yells as he kicks the chair.

THe police come and grab him. THey put cuffs on him and drag him away.

'Come on guys!' says Serio as he's thrown into a cell, 'I can't beleive you listened to that cue ball!'

'Hey!' says the man beside Serio who is bald.

'No offense man,' says Serio as he jumps on his bunk.

'Oh well, at least it's not as bad as being in here for life like some people.'

Serio looks over at the guy.

'Hey,' Serio says, 'What's your name?

'My name's Jack,' he says.

'What are you in for?' asks Serio.

'Murder,' the guy says grabbing a knife. Serio gulps.

'Um...' says Serio.

'Don't worry,' he says, 'This knife is a fake.' He pokes his self.

'Thank god!' laughs Serio.

'Hey, arn't you that wrestler in that CHZ thingy? Tyler Van Serio?' Jack asks.

'Ya!' says Serio, 'How do you know me?'

'I see you on t.v.' says Jack.

'Well, whatever you see on t.v. isn't real. What I like to say is 'A real man fights his own battles.'' says Serio.

'True,' says Jack.

Serio looks around and sees weights.

'Are those yours?' asks Serio.

'No,' says Jack, 'They give them to us. Every few days, they give us 5 mor pounds.

'Hey,' says Serio, 'I bet I can do more curls.'

'In your dreams roookie,' says Jack, 'I've been doing those for 20 years.

'How many years did you get?' asks Serio.

'50,' he says as he picks up the first weightand begins.

About 10 minutes later, Jack was at 300. Serio grabs the other weight and begins. Jack begins to laugh as Serio drops the weight on his feet.

'Ow!' Serio yells.

'Man, you suck!' says Jack.'

'Thank you,' says Serio.

'Here,' says Jack handing Serio a weight, 'Try thus 50.'

Serio picks it up and does 100 in 5 minutes.

'And the challenge there was...' says Serio.

'Well, you mastered the easy step,' says Jack, 'Now try 100.'

Serio does 50 in 5 minutes.

'A little harder,' says Serio.

'Good work,' says Jack, 'Next, try the 150.'

Serio and Jack do this up to 400. Serio can't lift the 400. He drops it again and this time really hurts his toe.

'S*** s*** s***!' Serio yells as he jumps on his bed.

'Man,' says Jack.

'Do you have a band-ade or something?' asks Serio.

'Your funny,' says Jack.

'I'm not joking,' says Serio.

Jack looks down to see blood coming out.

'Um,' says Jack.

'Please!' says Serio.

'OK,' says Jack pulling a band-ade out of his pocket.

'Thank you!' says Serio.

Jack watches Serio take off his sock to see teh foot covered in blood.

'Do you need help?' asks Jack.

'No!' yells Serio.

'Sorry,' says Jack as he helps Serio any ways.

'There!' says Serio as he looks down.

'OK, you better get some rest,' says Jack.

'Your right,' says Serio as he gets on his bunk and lays down.

Serio falls asleep dreaming of what will happen next. The scene begins to fade away as Serio falls off his top bunk. Jack begins layghing but Serio doesn't look happy. He gets back up as teh scene ends.

End.
