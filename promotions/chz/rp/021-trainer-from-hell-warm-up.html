Trainer from Hell: Warm Up
Written: June 16th, 2002

A black pickup pulls up in an empty parking lot. From the passenger side a man dressed neatly in black steps out. It’s non-other than the future CHZ World Heavyweight Champion, Luke Black! From the driver’s side of the pickup comes Ross Youngblood, Luke Black’s unwanted personal trainer.

Luke walks over to Ross and pokes him in his chest. “I am NEVER going to ride in that smelly, hot, sticky peace of crap you call a car…AGAIN!” Luke angrily tells Ross, “It was the worst thing I have ever rode in!”

“Okay.” Replies Ross, “You don’t have to ride in it again. You’ll just have to find another way home.”

“Oh, I can find another way home, believe me you!”

“Okay then. We’ll just have to get you to change into something to train in.”

“The faster we this over with, the less time I have to deal with you.”

“Follow me.” Ross directs as he enters the building closely followed by Luke.

Once inside the training facility Ross takes Luke past many, many weight machines, a makeshift ring made of everyday rope, steel bars and blue mats. “That’s the worst ring I’ve ever seen!” Luke says commenting on the ring.

“Yep.” Ross calmly says.

“We’re not going to train in that ring, are we?”

“We sure are!” Ross says excitedly.

Ross leads Luke to the locker room and they enter. The stench of man-sweat is all over the place. Luke can barely stand to be in there. He is used to his own private gym. Now he is forced to train in this public gym. “Other people train in this gym, too, huh?” Luke asks.

“Sure do!” Answers Ross.

“Well, where is everybody else?”

“Not here. I got permission to use this place all day today!” Ross says as he walks over to a locker and unlocks it. He pulls out some sweat pants and tosses them to Luke. “Here, wear these.”

“You expect me to wear these dirty pear of sweat pants?”

“They’re brand new. I’ll be out side. Come out when you’re ready.” Says Ross as he walks out of the locker room and out the back door. He walks over to a little golf cart and hops in it. He starts the little thing up and speeds up and up a ramp to a walkway stretching down a long course of sand that leads into a pool of water. Then on to more sand into a wall you must climb over and more sand. Then leading on a mount of loose rocks witch at the top has a yet another wall you must climb over and splashing into a another pool which seems like the end. It isn’t. You have to swim to the bottom and open a door that leads into a small room which you must open yet another door.

Luke emerges from the back door dressed in his tennis shoes, the sweat pants Ross told him to wear and a black shirt. He looks around for Ross, but can’t find him. “Ross?” He shouts still looking for him. “Oh well, I’ll just leave then.” Luke says happily and begins to leave, when he hears Ross shout at him, “I’m up here!” Luke turns around to see Ross in the little golf cart up on the high walkway.

“What are you doing up there?” Luke shouts at Ross.

“I’ll be watching you while you do this course.” Ross says as he points down the course, “This is just a warm up!”

“A warm up? You must be crazy! This course must be at least a mile long!”

“A mile and a half to be exact! Anyway, when I say ‘Go!’ you’re going to start running!”

“No I’m not! I don’t need to! Have you seen what I look like?” Luke says as he takes off his shirt and tosses it aside. “I don’t need any training! Just look at this fabulous body!”

“Yeah that’s great and all, but there is always room for improvement!”

“Not when you look this good!”

“Go!”

“What?”

“Start running!”

“No!”

“Start running!” Demands Ross, “Start running or I’ll be force to release the dogs!”

“What dogs?”

Ross picks up a garage door opener and pushes the button. “I would start running if you where you.” Ross warns Luke. Soon enough the barks of several dogs can be heard coming around the corner. Luke takes one look at the dogs and starts to run down the course. Ross chuckles a bit before he starts moving his golf cart.

Luke reaches the sand and has some trouble running as fast as he was before. Luke trips a bit, but doesn’t fall. Ross seems to be really enjoying what is happening just by judging by the smile on his face. Luke eventually reaches the pool of water, dives right in and stops. He seems to have forgotten that dog can swim. “Hey Luke!” Ross shouts down at Ross. “Dog can swim!” Just as he says that the first of the dogs pounces into the water. Luke’s face turns to shock as he starts to swim as fast as he can. Ross laughs at the stupidity of Luke.

Luke seems to be leaving the dogs behind and is near the end of the pool. As soon as he reaches the end of the pool he climbs out and starts to make a break for the wall. After all, dogs can’t climb. His sweat pants are soaked with water and are weighing him down forcing him to run slower in the hard to run in sand. The dogs soon reach the end of the pool and make it out. They start to catch up with Luke. Luke finally reaches the wall and uses the rope to help him climb up and over. He falls off the top of the wall drenched and his long blonde hair in his face. Luke thinks that the dogs have stopped and can’t reach him with the wall in the way.

He couldn’t be more wrong. Luke stands up and starts to walk the rest of course when he hears a bark that isn’t muffled by the wall. Luke turns around to see a dog standing on his side of the wall. “But how? Dog’s can’t climb!” He thinks to him self. Another dog soon is there. There is a doggy door and the dogs are making their way though it. Luke turns and runs through the sand. The dogs are starting to nip at the heels of Luke forcing him to try to run faster. He soon reaches the hill of loose rocks and starts to make his way up. Slipping and tripping and falling on his way up. The dogs have just as much trouble reaching the top as he does.

Luke eventually reaches the top and starts to climb the second wall. Luke is obviously tired and is having trouble reaching the top. He falls off the wall and crushes a dog. He immediately gets up and starts to climb the wall. This time he reaches the top and topples over into the pool behind it. “I finished!” Luke shouts.

“Oh but you haven’t.” Ross says with a sly look on his face. “You still have to swim to the bottom on the pool and open a door. That will lead you into a room where you must open another door. Then you will be finished.”

Luke looks down in the bottom of the pool and sees the door. “Way down there?” Luke asks.

“Yep. Way down there.”

“Isn’t there another way out?”

“No! Now go!”

Luke is hesitant but goes anyway. Swimming down to the bottom he thinks to himself how he got into this. He opens the door and is sucked into the small room because there wasn’t any water in the room and it quickly filled with water. The door he opened closes and it soon becomes dark. Luke is running out of air and can’t find the other door.

Meanwhile, we see Ross on the other side of the door. There is a pool for the water to spill into when Luke opens the door. Ross has a worried look on his face when Luke doesn’t come out. But soon enough the door open and the water rushes out with Luke tumbling into the shallow pool, gasping for air. Ross walks over to Luke. “Need some water.” Ross jokingly asks as Luke looks up at him with a no laughing mater face.
