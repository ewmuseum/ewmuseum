Bleed for me
Written: 17/8/02

Four sounds. Four mixed rhythmic exact sounds. The first sound, the sound of his clenched fist being drilled into his various combat training equipment. His punch bag, his wooden figure of an opponent, etc. The second sound, the deep sighing and faint growling of him breathing deeply as he trained. The third sound, coming from a small hi fi system, Seek and Strike by Soulfly. A track on his work out compilation disc. The fourth sound, coming from a small stereo playing at the same time as Seek and Strike. The sound coming from the stereo was his voice. He recorded it shortly after discovering he would be facing Angel Summers on the upcoming Wild. He record it shortly after as to capture all the emotion he was feeling, he wanted his voice to remain fresh with the adrenaline rush of perverse pain and vengeance he was feeling at the time.

All these sounds blended together as the graceful moonlight glimmered against his toned defined body as he trained in the dark.

He drilled his first into the punch bag

'Ummmf!

::'Another day another strike!'::

'The whore of Babylon's time has finally come Axl'

He turned and executed a roundhouse kick into the wooden sculpture of an opponent.

'Heeeeyah!'

::'Another day another night!'

'Her destiny to be forced to accept her vengeful salvation has arrived'

He turned back to the punch bag and executed a fast and furious 1 2 continuous punch combo, his fists and arms nothing more than a blur as they hammered against the tight leather of the bag.

'Ummmmffhhh!'

::'Here we go, another day another strike!'::

' She will try to deceive you....'

He turned and hit a spinning superkick to the face of the wooden opponent.

'Arrggggggh!'

::' Seek and strike seek and strike ........ STRIKE!'::

'She will try and weave her pathetic yet tempting web around you.....!

He turned and attacked the punch bag again.

'HuuurrrrggHHHH!!!'

::'Seek and strike seek and strike.... STRIKE!!!::

'Yet you will not be fooled by IT!'

He turned and began punching furiously at the wooden opponent.

'AAAARrggHHHHHH!!!!'

::' Seek and strike seek and strike ... STRRRRIIIIIIIIIIKE!!!!!'::

'SHE WILL BE NOTHING MORE THAN A SACRFICE AXL!!!!'

'ARRRRRGGGGGGH!'

He yelled not in pain, not in agony, not of fatigue either, but of adrenaline and anticipation.

When he finally stopped punching, he slowly caught his breath, his compilation CD stopped and the tape of his voice finally finished, he walked to the corner of his secluded training area, and picked up a Bokken from the wall. A Bokken being a kendo stick type weapon, except it is shaped in the form of a Japanese sword and is deadlier and stronger than any normal kendo stick. He then stood in the centre of his training room, when Roland the eagle came swooping through the air and perched on his shoulder.

'Hello old friend' He said quietly. Roland merely rubbed the back of his head against his neck.

Axl then waited, he was sure he left something else on the end of that tape....

'The worthless whore will bleed Axl, she will bleed and feel the wrath of all that is deep dark and demented. She will bleed ......... she will bleed for you!'

He smirked as he heard the tape of his voice finally stop. Then with the moonlight the only source of light shining through the small window and Roland perched on his shoulder, Axl began his weapons training, with his Bokken.

Fade to black.
