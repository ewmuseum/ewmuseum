The Beginning
Written: 6/12/02

~Prologue~

Time can often have a funny effect on people. Throughout the course of time all that is around you is in a constant state of change and usually everything within a person is changing through time. In the life and times of Tyler Davidson that has become quite evident. To put things into perspective perhaps it is best for us to take a step back and look to the past to enlighten those who are unsure of the past events which have led us to the present reality.

~Begin Flashback~

You again see Tyler as he rolls over in the bed. The sun now shining in the sky comes through the curtains of the hotel room and shine into his eyes but the thing that wakes him is the ringing of his cell phone at the side of the bed on an end table. Tyler slowly wakes from his slumber and tosses an arm over to the table as he fumbles around for the phone before finally getting a hand on it and bringing it to his ear and speaking.

”Hello?” he muttered into the speaker barely able to open his eyes yet. He attempts to push himself up out of the bed but with little strength behind it as a voice comes back over the phone.

”T-ty?” a female voice said. At first sound it could have been mistaken for his girlfriend Heather though the sounds of sobs and tears from her makes it quite difficult to tell exactly who it was.

“Yeah, this is him. Is that you Jess?” he asked suddenly coming to his senses with a bit of alarm.

“Y-y-yes it is,” she slowly answered him. She was obviously quite distressed but what it was about he had no idea. Fearing the worse though he answered back to her.

”What’s wrong? Did you here something about Dan?” he asked with his alarm increasing further. At first he heard nothing but silence and the sound of Jess crying before she would answer again.

“N-no I haven’t,” she eventually answered. “It’s about Heather, Tyler.”

“What about her?” he asked unsure of what the problem could be.

”I’m so sorry,” was the only response she would give at first.

“Jess, what happened?” he asked again feeling an even greater sense of urgency.

“I’m sorry Tyler but….” Jess began before breaking off again in tears before being able to finish the sentence. “She’s dead.”

The phone falls from Tyler’s hand and comes crashing down onto the ground below him. Tyler in a state of complete shock sitting at the edge of the bed slowly slides off onto the floor his head in his hands as the tears slowly become to come forth and the only sounds left is the voice of Jess on the phone screaming for Tyler to answer her and the sounds of his own crying as the scene fades to black.

~End Flashback~

What would you do if the most important thing in your life was suddenly and violently taken from you? That is the question which for two months now Tyler Davidson has had to live with. That is the question which Tyler Davidson must somehow find an answer to. It is evident that thus far the answer has not been to the liking of the fans who once praised Tyler for his abilities in the ring and his character as well. As things always do over the course of time however Tyler had become a changed man. The question then becomes was it the events of his life which had changed him or has Tyler always had this darkness building inside of him? The truth of the answer can oftentimes be one of the most frightening realities a man is forced to face.

The future can indeed look quite dark from a certain point of view. Whether that is Tyler’s or not remains to be seen.

~The Beginning~

All you can see around you is the darkness of a stormy night in the Connecticut area. Bolts and flashes of lightning light the nighttime sky as the sounds of thunder rock the nearby houses. As the winds blows through the trees lining the area, another flash of lightning lights the area well enough for you to see the figure of a man standing out in the downpour of rain. Through the flashes of lightning you can see the figure of the man as he rushes through the area before coming up to a large mansion door. He grabs hold of the wooden door and turning the knob makes his way into the building and out of the downpour of rain. Immediately upon his entering the building the man, a young one with black hair drenched from the rain falling down in front of his face and a black goatee, is approached by an older man with short graying hair and clean shaven.

“It’s about time you came in from the rain Tyler,” the old man began as he looked Tyler up and down noticing the puddle of water forming at his feet. “You’re dripping water all over the place. Get your ass into the bathroom and dry yourself off.”

“Yeah, yeah I know pops,” Tyler replied as he walks by his father, Hunter, and into a side room of the hallway. After a few minutes he eventually reemerges and joins his father in the living room. Tyler crashes down onto the nearby couch across from his father and looks at the television to see the latest highlights from the World Cup action. As Tyler becomes seemingly entranced with the highlights his father looks over at him.

“What is this I hear about you going to Canada?” his father asked beginning the line of questioning he’s had on his mind all night long.

“I signed to work some shows with a Canadian independent promotion,” Tyler explained tearing his attention away from the highlights and looking at his father. “Not much of a big deal really. Figured I could head north of the border and show those Canadians what a real superstar is supposed to be. None of this Wayne Gretzsky or Bret Hart type crap anymore.”

“Oh yeah I can see you’re going to be a big hit over there,” Hunter replied rolling his eyes at the brash arrogance. “You never do learn do you?”

“Sure I do,” Tyler replied quickly. “I just don’t ever pay attention to what I learn most of the time. When you get to be as successful as me you wouldn’t either.”

“Riiight,” Hunter responded sarcastically. “I guess you got it all planned out then.”

“Mostly,” Tyler answered.

“Mostly?” his father asked giving Tyler a curious look.

“Yeah, mostly,” Tyler repeated again. “They got me booked in some tag match for the first show already.”

“And the problem with that?” his father again asked continuing his line of questioning.

“I don’t know who the hell my partner is,” Tyler responded. “They got rumors flying all over the place as to who it is but I’m the only one apparently that doesn’t know. It’s really quite annoying.”

“I bet,” Hunter agreed. “At least you know who you’re facing.”

“Yeah kind of,” Tyler again replied softly without looking back to his father. “I know their names but that’s about it. I've never heard of either one of them before.”

“You’re certainly off to a good start aren’t you?” his father commented sarcastically. “Maybe you should go and get some homework done on this people.”

“Hey I do what I can,” Tyler fired back. “When information is scarce it’s kind of hard to do that. It is Canada we’re in after all. I don’t think they have all the technology we do here in the States.”

“I’m sure they do Tyler,” Hunter admonished him knowing it would have little if any affect on his son. “You’ll just have to do things the hard way then and learn everything the hard way.”

“That’s been the way of my life lately,” Tyler replied looking away from his father again. “Anyway I’m tired and got an early wake up tomorrow so I’m hitting the sack.”

“Have a good night Ty,” his father called after him as Tyler quickly got to his feet and dashed from the room. The scene slowly fades to black one last time as Tyler disappears up the staircase.
