Facing Fears
Written: 08.25.02

Blackness.

A cloudy day outside of a small restraunt. The ground is noticeably wet from earlier rain that had fallen. A few people walk up to the restraunt and open the door as a few walk out. This nice, quiet diner is in good condition, and seems to be very clean. Suddenly, we turn to see a silver 2002 Honda Civic coming up the street. The car turns, and pulls into a parking spot right in front of the diner. The engine shuts off, and the door opens. After a few seconds of movement inside the car, Paul Harrington steps out of the driver side. He is dressed in a pair of khakis and a white, grey, and black camoflauge shirt. He shuts the door, and locks them with his key chain as he makes his way towards the entrance of the diner. He pulls the door open and walks inside, as a few rain drops fall in front of the camera.

Blackness.

Paul steps inside the diner and looks around, finding an open booth. He walks over to it, and scoots in, with his back against the wall. About a minute and a half passes, and a waitress walks up, setting a glass of water in front of Paul.

'Back again, Paul?' The waitress asks.

'Becca, do you think I'd go any other place than this diner?' Paul replies with question.

'Of course not. We know you're loyal to us.' Becca replies.

'Or so you think.' Paul says, laughing.

'Don't lie. So, you want you're usual?' She asks.

'Yeah, but I see you guys carry Gatorade now?' Paul observes.

'Yes we do. Just got permission. So you want one?' Becca asks.

'Yeah. Bring me a Glacier Ice.' He says with a smile.

'All right. I'll be right back.' Becca replies, walking towards the counter.

Paul closes his eyes for a minute, and a child walks up. The boy looks to be about twelve, and is dressed in a pair of jean shorts and a Tyler Nelson shirt. When Paul opens his eyes, he is startled to see the boy.

'Mr. Harrington?' The boy asks.

'That's me? What can I do for ya'?' Paul asks.

'Can I have you're autograph?' The boy asks.

'I've never turned it down before. Sure thing, kid.' Paul says with a smile.

The boy hands Paul a marker and a picture of Paul. Paul takes the marker and picture and sets it on the table in front of him.

'What's your name?' Paul aks.

'Kyle.' They boy replies.

Paul writes a small message on the picture, then signs and dates it. Below his signature he writes 'You have now met...perfection!' and hands the picture and marker back to Kyle.

'There ya' go.' Paul says with a smile.

'Thanks a lot Mr. Harrington!' Kyle says, taking the picture and running back to his table.

Paul smiles and leans back against the wall again, closing his eyes. Just as he closes them, Becca walks up with Paul's Gatorade.

'There's your Gatorade, Paul.' Becca says, setting it in front of him.

'Thanks a lot.' Paul says, opening his eyes.

He takes the Gatorade and opens it, taking a big drink. He puts the cap back on, and sets it on the table. He lets out a big sigh and leans against the wall again, closing his eyes once more.

'You're cheeseburger will be about another five minutes, all right?' Becca asks.

'Gotcha'.' Paul replies, not opening his eyes.

Becca walks away, as Paul leans his head against the back rest of the booth. A few seconds pass, and then Paul feels the booth shake as somebody sits down. The figure reaches over and taps Paul on the shoulder, but Paul doesn't open his eyes.

'Sorry. No more autographs. I need some rest.' Paul says.

'Trust me, I don't need your autograph. I'll get the only souvenier I need at Mind Games.' The figure says.

This provokes Paul to open his eyes. He looks across the table, and sitting there is his rival, Stimpo. Stimpo sits there, looking at Paul. Then he looks farther up, to gaze upon the cut on Paul's head.

'How's your head? Still hurt?' Stimpo asks.

'What in the hell are you doing here?' Paul asks, sitting up.

'I came out to eat, and you happened to be napping over here. I had to say 'hello'.' Stimpo says with a smile.

'What do you want, Stimson?' Paul asks, obviously angry.

'I just wanted to see how your head is feeling.' He says, trying to look concerned.

'That's bullshit.' Paul replies.

'I also wanted to remind you that I am the referee in your match with Ryan Contra.' Stimpo says, seemingly pleased.

'Trust me, Brendon, I haven't forgotten.' Paul says, glaring back at Stimpo.

'I hope you haven't forgotten about our match at Mind Games. The match that I am going to cripple you in? The match that you will bleed in?' Stimpo says, trying to intimidate his opponent.

'I haven't forgotten. Trust me. In no way have I forgotten. But don't let your gaurd down anytime soon now that you have some buddies. Strange things can still happen.' Paul fires back.

'Really? Do you actually think you have a shot at winning our match? You have ten stitches in your head.' Stimpo says, reminding Paul.

'I may have these stitches now, but remember, there's still time for me to open you up before Mind Games.' Paul says.

'Okay. But you better not forget about me. I'll always be around.' Stimpo says.

Stimpo stands and leaves, walking out the door. Paul leans back again, and closes his eyes. Just as he gets settled, Becca walks up with Paul's food.

'Food's up.' Becca says.

'Great.' Paul replies.

'Have a good lunch.' Becca says walking away.

Paul sits up and begins fixing his burger to eat it as the screen goes black.

Blackness.
