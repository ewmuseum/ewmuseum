To the T
Written: June 14, 2002

Scouting one's competition is always important. Not only from the normal 'how do I beat this guy' point of view, but its also important to know what a guy can and can't do in order to make sure you put on a well received, and smooth match for the fans and promoters.

In the independent feds, especially in the vast area north of the border, sometimes you are forced to work with people you not only are unfamilar with, but perhaps never even heard of.

Research is the key though. A well prepared, veteran grappler always learns about his opponents to ensure that he can call a match that helps put over both men, win or lose.

Race Alexander is a lot of things. He's charasmatic, outspoken and talented...but above all, he is a veteran.

...

Tap...tap...tap...

Fingers running across keys can be heard as the handsome features of Race Alexander come into view watching a computer screen intently. Behind Race stands his aging, yet still hot-looking and well tanned valet/girlfriend, Natalie Shea, who rubs her perfectly manicured fingers across the broad shoulders of Alexander.

'What have you found, babe?' queries Natalie, as she chews a bit of gum.

'I found Kazzulties fan site, on Geocities, nontheless,' responds Race, still reading intently. 'Well, he is a smallish guy, so it looks like we can get some good ring movement next week.'

Natalie nods and continues to rub Race's shoulders.

Suddenly, Race lets out a laugh.

'Well, hell, according to his fan site, there is apparently nothing this guy can not do,' chuckles Race. 'I mean, his supposed signature moves are a mile long, and more varied than the possibilities of which inbred cousin down in Myrtle Beach banged his mom on her twelth birthday, which resulted in this jackasses conception.'

Natalie, leans over Race's shoulders, which allows her blouse to fall hang low, giving viewers a gratuitou shot, of her ample, thoug obviously articifically enhanced breasts. She then also chuckles.

'Whoa,' she exclaims. 'Back up the milk truck!!'

Rhett nods.

'Yeah, its a joke, isn't it? I mean, this guy is claiming chokeslams, Last Ride Powerbombs, Crucifix Powerbombs, 450 splashes, stunners, tombstones, five star frog splashes, springboard legdrops AND Diving Hurricurana's as part of his arsenal?'

'Pfft,' responds, Natalie. 'Don't forget the crossface, sharpshooter and anklelock submission holds.'

Race scoffs.

'If he honestly thinks he is getting all this shut put over in the ring against me, he is sadly mistaken,' says Race with a scoff. 'I am all for letting guys get over with a couple of their sig sets, but this kid is ridiculous. Maybe this shit went over down in whatever backwater, hillbilly, bible-belt, carnival hopping promotion he was working in before, but up here, against a star like me, he best be prepared to run the match as I call it...'

Natalie nods, as Race's firm jaw tightens up.

'...to the T.'
