Road Trip...pt1
Written: 3/8/02

The best thing in the world is, not knowing your great. Only make’ s you want to be better. People who believe they could never get anymore great, eventually of the people who are lower then them, but at any given time anyone could be great. Have the strategy and improving will be routine.

At the age of 24, Angel Summers has completed a lifetime of memories. She has had a rough ride, but at times it has been worth it. All her rodeo awards, her University diploma, owning her own bar and becoming a professional wrestler. For some, this life would be considered hectic, but Angel has not regretted one time in her life, life could not be any better for the beautiful cowgirl. It is also rare for a woman to be in a male dominated sport such as wrestling. Angel feels there is no difference between a women and a male in the ring, many speculate a women cannot compete against a male, but Angel hopes to fix that problem at Wild. She plans to come out the winner, and become a known woman wrestler in the wrestling world and will truly show, she is no push over.

It has been a slow few days in the CHZ. Nothing really happening thus far. Last week there was a few interviews involving Matt Blitz stating he would not strike a woman, but hopefully that is cleared up and Matt has come to his senses. Angel feels she is the underdog in the match, she also believes the other 3 men she faces believe she is the underdog. Many woman state they can compete with men, some say in worldwide interviews, and few have even proved themselves. But this is not going to be a onetime thing, she plans to make it a weekly routine. This is the time in her life where proving yourself is key, CHZ is a development federation, the next step off the big times. If she is able to defeat 3 strong male individuals such as Matt Blitz, Ryan Contra and Axl Young, there is no doubt she is a true superstar.

A week now, since Angel has lived in the beautiful country of Canada, after spending a lifetime in the outskirts of Houston, Texas. The lifestyle has been dull thus far, except for the Baseball game, interviews and her tutoring every other day. Still not the life she is expecting, she is beginning to miss home, mostly her family and her bar. Still around a week until her debut in the CHZ, so Angel has decided to take a short trip back home, she notified her family of her return for a few days. They were pleased of the news, as they have never been separated from their only daughter for more then a few rodeo trip days. Her roommate Robyn, also a Houston hometown gal, has decided to stay in Toronto and explore some more. So on this Friday night will be her last in Toronto for a few nights as she leaves for Houston early the following morning. Already packed a suit case and the gas tank are full, no only one step left.

The day is not it’s normal sunny morning, in fact the sunlight is usually what wakes Angel up every morning. The sun has faded the grass to a brownish yellow. So as the rain beads down hard, the sudden blitz of lightning erupts and wakes Angel up, the bolt startled her and leaves her out has breathed. She finally realizing it is just the lightning, and slowly crawls out of her sheets. Again doing her morning routine. This took around an hour, with her shower, dressing and eating. She finally hit the road around 7 o’clock. She had nine hours of driving ahead of her, so she flips in a mixed country tape she had made the night before. Driving is a time to think, thinking is key to success. Think now! Achieve later.

The rain has continued city by city, growing harder the more south she drives. Not so bad that it is impossible to drive in but pretty close. Angel is the type of driver that would turn a 2-hour trip into an hour. She likes to get to where she wants to go in good time. As she passes several cars per minute, a million thoughts run through her head. Mostly of her life change, how she missed home. She also has been thinking of her recently boring life, but she realizes once the heat of the matches in CHZ has begun, her life will truly begin as a wrestler. Life has not been easy thus far, many doubts of a woman competing with three men, but she is ready, and she feels confident about achieving what she needs.

The whether finally becoming dreadful, she pulls over to a outskirts trucking restaurant, where she has her dinner, and waits for the whether to die down. It took around an hour for the wrestler to die down, so she passed time by reading the newspaper supplied at the restaurant.

Angel then continues her road trip, Angel filled her fuel tank up at the restaurant, so she is planning a long period of time before she makes another stop, unless the whether picks up once again. But as she continues driving, when she is doing her regular pass on the outside lane, she in cut off by the driver on the in-side lane and both cars are spun off the road due to the slippery roads. She is around 3 hours from home, and about 45 minutes until the next town or stop. The other car has tipped into the ditch, possibly a severe injury, and Angel’s car in barely hot, but also in the ditch, so possibly a second severe injury. A passing van stops, as a middle-aged man pulls over, and immediately goes to Angel’s car. And tries to make contact.

“ Hello there! Is everyone all right?”
