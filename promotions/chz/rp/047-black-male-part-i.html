Black Male Part I
Written: 7/07/02

Canadians, they are so ungrateful all they want is to turn their backs on you. I am from a rich wrestling family, but that doesn’t mean anything to anyone. My father has gotten me into the game and it is his fault that they hate me but it is not the end, not by a long shot. CHZ is a federation that I will rule and everyone is looking at the next National champion.

Today Shammon smith was lying on a hospital bed suffering from his arm injury that he suffered after the match on the first Wild. He squirmed and screamed in pain as his spokeswoman Shania Johnson kept poking at his arm. He reached for her arm pulling her into his body and gave her a menacing look that made her shake. He pushed her away as she slowly took a seat on the chair that lied by his bed.

“Are you still upset about Wild?”

“Of course I am, that stupid Mexican messed up my arm!” He said angrily while trying to keep his arm elevated.

They stood there in silence for a moment until Shammon slowly sat in his bed pointing to the sling that laid on the dresser right next to some roses. Shania nodded her head in agreement and quickly grabbed the sling tossing it over to her fallen client. He gazed at her with a dumb look and she shrugged her shoulders looking clueless.

“I cannot put it on with one arm!” he yelled obviously frustrated

“Calm down,” she said “I’ll help you just keep your shorts on.”

Carefully she placed his injured arm in the sling and his face cringed in pain but in a moment his pain was over with Shammon standing on his feet. His brown pants were exposed as he rips off his white gown to reveal his white shirt, the wrinkled, gray haired doctor steps in tapping his casual shoes on the floor.

“Where are you headed?” The doctor asks while scrunching his face at Shammon.

“Who the hell do you think you are? Your hospital system wouldn’t be as good if it wasn’t for me.” He said angrily.

He looked at the doctor with vengeance in his eyes and he moved closer striking fear into the doctor’s eyes. Thinking quickly; Shania stepped in front of Shammon before he could strike and the doctor stood firm with a cocky smile on his face. She turned to the doctor instantly being annoyed at the smile he was giving and punched his in his jaw. He fell like a sack of potatoes as he looked up at her she shook her head in anger.

“You’re such an idiot.” She said disappointedly

“What do you expect,” Shammon said “everyone else is just a petty attempted to copy my greatness.”

On there way to the door they heard screams from a patient and that soon followed the fast and furious wheels of the gurney zooming down the hospital halls. They opened the door almost being hit by a doctor that was rushing after the gurney that was half way up the hall. Shania looked in horror but Shammon smile devilishly almost enjoying everyone’s pain.

“These people are in pain… serves them right for being so stupid.” Shammon says with no remorse for the wounded.

“That is so heartless Shammon… it’s about time you got a mean streak.”

He smiles confidently as they walked by the nurses they jumped back in fear clinching on to the walls for dear life. He looked at the nurses scratching his head in frustration. As they walked further down the hall the screams of other patients became louder and louder as they walked by resulting in the two looking at each other in confusion.

'What is going on around here?”

'Just a couple of wimps crying nothing to worry about.' he said confidently.

When they continued there walk there was complete silence, they opened up the door and saw the men, women and children lying in a pool of blood. Shammon’s cocky and evil attitude suddenly changed and Shania shook violently with fear. Suddenly his phone rang; he reached in his pocket flipping it open.

“Like my style Shammon? I know something about your past that could ruin you” a dark voice said

“No, I may be a bitch, but this is more heartless than I could ever be.”

“Listen well, what place do they go before going to heaven or hell?”

The phone hung up on Shammon, he looked at Shania with a stumped and frustrated look.

'Besides having to teach The idotic Inferno a Lesson, now I have to find out what this weirdo wants from me.'
