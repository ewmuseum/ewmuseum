My Obsession...My Love..My Title...It's Gonna be All Mine! {Part 1}
Written: 8/17/02

'My obbsession for wrestling is high enough to get my ball rolling,' says Serio to his self as he walks down the street.

As Serio walks down he bumps into someone.

'Hello there,' says the young man.

'Hello' says Serio.

'I heard what you said over there, you know about your obbsession for wrestling and stuff?' says the man.

'Ya, so?' says Serio.

'Well, that would make a good poem, I'm a poet and I was wondering if I could use it?' asks the poet.

'Um, I guess so....' says Serio slowly.

The guy smiles and walks away. So does Serio.

'People these days don't have brains,' says Serio.

Serio is walking down the street and stops in front of a salon and sees alot of women.

'I need someone to love me, so I don't feel down, just feel like people that are loved,' says Serio.

'Excuse,' says the poet behind Serio.

Serio turns around quickly.

'Can I use that too?' asks the poet.

'Why not,' says Serio as he walks away.

Next Serio walks right up in front of the General Motor's Place Arena.

'The National Title, it will be mine Wednesday, because I will make it mine,' says Serio as he walks away again looking for teh poet.

'Good,' says Serio, 'He doesn't want that.'

As Serio continues walking down the street, he stops in front of a liquor stre and stares in.

'Beer, it killed my career...' he says as he walks away.

He continues walking and stops in front of a chair making factory,

'Chairs, paralyzed my arms, ended my career for 6 months,' he says and continues walking.

As he continues down teh street, he stares into a glass window bu can't see what is inside. He wipes the glass and stares in side to see a little boy playing with a toy train. The little boy is making choo-choo train sounds and is laughing. His dad comes and starts beating on his child. Serio backs away and continues walking.

'My dad, screwed my life, never let me do anything I wanted to do, never let me play with my friends, never let me date, no wonder why I had no love, I don't know how my mom could have taken him. I just know 1 thing that I will remember for teh rest of my life. My dad said it to me... 'Tyler, your a failure!''

Serio drops his head and continues walking. He isn't paying attention and he runs right into a women.

'Oh, sorry mame,' says Serio helping the lady pick up her stuff.

'OH, that's alright,' says the lady.

'Here,' says Serio handing the lady the stuff.

'Thank you very much Tyler,' says the lady as she walks away Serio begins walking away too.

Then all of a sudden he turns around and says,

'Hey how did you know my name....' his voice lowers throughout the sentence as he looks around for the lady.

Serio continues walking as he looks into a window filled with little toys.

'Toys, my dad never let me play with them,' says Serio.

Serio continues walking.

'The National Title will be mine, I know It will be mine because I beleive it can be mine. I want to prove my father wrong by winning the title, by showing him that I am not a failure, to show him, that I can succeed... To show him, I do not give up... To show him, that I am a real person, not a robot...' Serio continues walking down teh street as teh scene fades to black...
