Down To Business
Written: 07.20.02

Blackness.

A dark and dreary afternoon. Rain falls from the sky, and thunder claps in the distance. We look down, and spot Paul Harrington. He is walking along a sidewalk, trench coat on over his expensive, grey business suit. His head is bare, letting the rain fall onto the top of his crew cut. He continues walking, until he reaches a parking lot. It is almost full, with a few spots left open. Paul enters the parking lot, and walks to a space containing a silver 2002 Honda Civic. Paul takes a set of keys, and presses a button, unlocking the doors. He approaches the car, and opens the door. He climbs in and shuts the door, finally getting out of the rain. He starts the engine, and backs out, leaving the parking lot.

Blackness.

The same Honda Civic pulls into a parking lot, different than the one we were just at. The rain has stopped, and the sky is clearing a bit. The Civic parks, very close to the huge building, possibly in spot number one. The engine shuts off, and the driver’s side door opens, and Paul steps out. He locks the doors, and sets the alarm as he walks towards the building. As he reaches the doors, a sign containing the TAN logo, and office hours can be seen. Paul walks into the building, as the sky gets dark again, and it begins raining.

Blackness.

Paul walks past a desk, with a few younger ladies sitting behind it.

“Hey, Mr. Harrington.” One says with a smile.

“How are you doing, Katie?” He replies, smiling back.

“Great. Thanks for asking.” Katie answers, surprised at Paul’s happy mood.

Paul makes his way to an elevator, and steps inside. The elevator is empty, besides Paul. The elevator doors shut, and Paul stands, leaning against the back wall. He lets out a big sigh, and rubs his eyes, obviously tired. He then looks at his watch, as the elevator comes to a halt, and the doors open. He steps out, and walks past his secretary’s desk, towards his office. His secretary sits, reading a very thick book as he walks by. She spots him at a glance, and rushes to stop him.

“Mr. Harrington, Michael Pearson is in your office waiting.” Victoria says, showing little enthusiasm.

“All right, hold all my calls, and keep everyone from bothering me while I’m in there with him.” Paul says, with the leadership characteristics surfacing in him.

“Yes, sir.” Victoria says, acknowledging his orders very weakly.

Paul turns the door handle to his office, and pushes the door open. He walks in, and sees his guest sitting in a chair against the near wall reading a magazine. As soon as Paul’s door closes, Michael stands, and makes his way over to Paul.

“Hello, Mr. Harrington. How are you this afternoon?” Michael says, extending his hand.

“I’m tired as hell.” Paul replies, yawning, and accepting Michael’s hand, completing the firm business-type handshake.

“I know how that is.” Michael says, laughing a bit.

“Please, have a seat at my desk, and we’ll get down to business.” Paul offers.

Michael nods, and picks up his briefcase, following Paul over to his huge oak desk. Michael sits down in a big arm chair, as Paul walks to a filing cabinet and opens a drawer labeled “G-L”. He thumbs backwards, until he reaches the “H”s, and finds a file labeled “HIW”. He pulls it out, and sets it onto his desk. He then neatly bunches the other folders up, and closes the drawer. Paul then unbuttons his trench coat, and hangs it on a nearby coat rack to dry. He then sits down at his desk, and opens the folder, searching for a few important documents. He pulls a pen from his shirt pocket, and begins to look through the papers as he asks Michael a few questions.

“So, tell me, Mr. Pearson, why were you interested in taking us up on the offer of airing a wrestling promotion’s tv shows?” Paul asks, with seriousness in his voice.

“Well, we have been operating for about a year and a half now, and I thought it was time to go ahead, and attempt to get a television deal.” Michael answers with sincerity in his voice.

“Okay. That’s understandable. Now, why do you think you should receive this contract? In other words, what can High Impact Wrestling offer to us that other promotions cannot?” Paul asks, writing the previous answer onto a piece of paper.

“Let’s see. We at HIW have hard working wrestlers, and a great staff. We can bring in great crowds, and I expect we will be able to draw Television viewers just as easily.” Michael says, letting a sigh of relief out, knowing he has given a somewhat good answer.

“Well, Mr. Pearson, I think you and HIW are the best candidates, besides SIW, who will not receive the contract. Call me biased, but I still haven’t forgiven those bastards for not taking me back.” Paul says, his voice raising.

“I don’t blame you, not one bit man.” Michael says, knowing a little sucking up can’t hurt.

“Well, why don’t we talk about the contract?” Paul asks.

“Go ahead. What did you have in mind?” Michael says, happy to know he’s this far.

“Well, I was thinking possibly two years, at $70,000 a year. We might sign an extension at the end of three months, depending on how good your ratings are.” Paul offers.

“That sounds good. But, I was thinking four years, at $45,000 a year.” Michael says, in his best salesman type voice.

“You drive a hard bargain. How about three years, $55,000?” Paul says, putting the ball back in Michael’s hands.

“Three and a half years, $50,000?” Michael says.

“Well, that doesn’t sound half bad. You’ve got yourself a deal. Now, it’s one show a week, correct?” Paul asks.

“Yes, and it’s up to you to decide what night and time.” Michael explains.

“Okay then, the best opening I have, would be Friday nights, at eight o’clock central time. How’s that sound?” Paul offers.

“Great.” Michael replies with a smile.

“Okay then, we just need to set a meeting to sign the new papers.” Paul says. “How’s Monday?” Paul asks.

“Monday is fine, I’m open all day.” Michael says, still smirking from ear to ear.

“Great, just be here at two, and I’ll have everything we need.” Paul says, slowly getting to his feet.

“Okay, thank you Mr. Harrington.” Michael says, shooting up from his chair.

Michael extends his hand, and Paul accepts.

“Your very welcome. Have a great day.” Paul says, smiling with the feeling of accomplishment coming over him.

Michael picks up his briefcase, and walks to the door. He exits, as Paul sits, presses a button, and begins speaking.

“Victoria, were there any calls?” Paul asks his secretary.

“No, sir. There weren’t.” She answers, still a little unenthusiastic.

“You know what Victoria, why don’t you take the rest of the day off?” Paul says unexpectedly.

“Really? Are you serious Mr. Harrington?” She asks, finally coming to life.

“Yes, go out and do something.” He replies.

“Oh thank you!” She screams with joy.

Paul lets the button go, and then picks up the phone. He dials a few numbers, then pauses, waiting for an answer. Finally someone does.

“Mr. Cromwell’s Office of Law. How may I help you?” A voice from the other end of the phone answers.

“I need to speak with Mr. Cromwell, please?” Paul says.

“I’m sorry, he is in a very important meeting and cannot…” The voice is cut-off.

“This is Mr. Harrington from TAN.” He says.

“Okay then, let me get him.” The voice says, now understanding.

A few seconds go by, and another voice answers on the other line.

“What’s up, Paul?” Mr. Cromwell answers.

“Nothing much, man. Hey, I need you to draw up a new contract for HIW. We agreed on $50,000 a year at three and a half years.” Paul explains.

“Right away, Paul. I’ll have them on your desk tomorrow morning.” The Lawyer says.

“Okay, thanks a lot. Talk to you later.” Paul says, finishing his conversation.

Paul hangs up the phone, and gathers the papers from the HIW file. He sets them in the folder, and then stands, walking towards the filing cabinet. He files the folder, and closes the drawer. He then rubs his eyes, and looks at his watch. He yawns, and walks to the door. He reaches out, and turns something, locking the door. He then sits on a couch, and removes his shoes. He sets them off to the side, and lays down, preparing for a nap.

Blackness.
