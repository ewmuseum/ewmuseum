Cowgirl In Town!Part 1
Written: 07/22/02

{--BEGIN TRANSMISSION--}

Think of a champion. A person you respect as a person, a person you feel is your hero. Now tell me how many of you people literally thought of a male. It has been proven day in and day out, that male’s dominant, well now it is time for people to think of a women hero, A Texan cowgirl to be exact. The females have began to sprout up and express their opinions, and take control in most relationships, but there is still an area missing that women do not dominate. Athletics have always been a male thing, you think of athletics hero’s, and you think of, Michael Jordan, Tiger Woods, Kurt Warner, Barry Bonds and Patrick Roy. But one particular girl is doing what it takes, to think twice, when you think of your hero, Tex from CHZ is looking to make his way up and become a hero to some if possible.

The final good-byes are always the worst, when you say goodbye to your closest family members and friends. Having to leave everything you love so dear, to fight. Although it is nothing like leaving for a world war, but it always hurts to leave the one’s you love. Angel is leaving to Detroit, for her tag match at Saturday Hell Bound. She tags up with mob man Theo Eccentric, and squares off against Stomp and Jason Aaron. Although she has not got a clue about any of the wrestlers, she feels confident.

The transmission begins at Angel’s bar, The Texan. With her four brothers, mother, father and several friends saying their good-byes to the local upcoming superstar. Her family, and friends wave her off, as she gets into her newly bought car, which was a gift from her parents for graduating from University. It nears dark, as the skies become a dark gray, so she says her final good-byes and drives off. The big deal of her leaving is, she has never left town for more then a week, and she is uncertain how long she is gone, but as long as she is in the CHZ, she has decided to live in Toronto, in a cheap apartment, for the time being.

It is a long six-hour drive, so she stops at a Houston gas station, fuels up and begins her trip. The hours go by quick because of her mixed tapes, which she had made ahead of time before the trip. Mixed types of music such as, country, pop and classical. Not so much the classical part, with the exception of a few songs she had loved. With the constant singing, the time went reasonably quick because of it.

She had arrived to Detroit, at four in the morning. She parked at her apartment, where she will be living, depending on her success in the CHZ, it will be her home. She looked up at the six floored, red brick apartment building, which looked very newly built. With the rain beating down hard, she waits in her car, awaiting her landlord, who will give Angel her house key. With the parking lot reasonably empty, and the only sound that is heard is the rustling of the rain hitting the pavement. The loud Lincoln pulls into the parking lot, and upside her 1995 Cavalier. They both just roll down their windows, and exchange names, as the landlord hands over the key to her new place. Then drives off, looking very feisty to get home and out of the rain. So Angel pulls out her cowgirl hat, from the back seat, to cover her hair as she ran across the parking lot, and into her building.

There was no need for the staircase, as he apartment was on the first floor, where she requested. As she inserted the key into the slot, and walked into the dark bare room, she flipped on the light switch, was to the left of the door. And this wooden tiled floor, with beautiful blue and purple wallpaper seeps through the whole room, blending in beautifully with the wooden floor. The kitchen is to the left, not closed in but with half walls circling it, and the bedrooms directly in front of the door, which you would need to cut across the bare opened room, which where the living room will be. The bathroom is in the middle of the two rooms. Both rooms a fair size, but with only the needed supplies, such as food, kitchen table, bathroom essentials and her bed, she had really nothing to work with until the following day, when she will go on a shopping spree for her furniture and other needed essentials for her apartment. She had been saving up for this moment for awhile now, well since a few years ago when she opened her bar. So after a few hours of setting up her thousand-piece bed, and her large wooden kitchen table, she had went to bed as the sun was beginning to sprout through the shades.

After her long sleep, it nears noon. As she wakes up with ease, she gets ready for a big day of shopping. Not knowing the area, she asks a neighbor for directions to the nearest mall. Not being much of a shopper herself, she felt it was a longer day then it really was, as she bought all the essentials for her apartment, such as dishes, towels, couch, television, computer, groceries and other wooden desks. She slowly made her way home, several trips to be exact. And it took the rest of her Friday to arrange he furniture, to her perfection. Another thing she hated to do, but she felt, the quicker it is up, the quicker the time to work out, for her first match, this Saturday. After her dinner, she makes her way over to the Joe Louis Arena. Which she scouted while she was shopping, and enters into a loud of press, which were swarmed around certain superstars from the federation, she almost made her way through the hall until she was stopped by a young lady, obviously new to her job. Tex being the great girl she is, she tamed the women and answered her very interesting questions.

“ You must be The Texan, I’ll tell you from women to women, It’s good to see you female wrestlers sprouting up and being heard. How does it feel to represent your sex, in wrestling world?”

Tex releases her gym bag, to the floor, and gets comfortable in her standing position, and feels confident at anything the reporter hits her with.

“ Well I have never looked at it like that, but if that is the case. It feels great to be a female wrestler here in CHZ, and I plan to not let the fans down, as I plan to earnat my first match!'

The rookie reporter shuffles through her question categories, she had typed out to think ahead, so she would not get into a slump of questions. She clears her throat and speaks.

“ What you think of you chances in the higher talented federation then PWA, such as CHZ, considering you just re-joined the wrestling wold no more then a week ago?'

“ Well that is a great question, I do not really consider myself a rookie, yes I am to this federation, but I am very alert on what I need to do, to achieve the win. When you have the skill, the experience is not far off.”

Tex then waves off the reporter, and begins walking off down the hallway, as she is eager to work out and prepare for her match. She is not aware on what is expected her in the CHZ, on the outside it would appear she is very confident, but inside she is nervous and very impatient. She circles the building a few times, getting the feel of the atmosphere, also looking for the weight room. She finally reaches it, and gears up into her gym clothes, consisting of a one-piece, spandex black suit, with her black slush pants, along with her slush coat to match the pants. She notices the weight room is bare, so she feels for relaxed, as she flips on some music, and begins several reps of different equipment. She continues working out, as the transmission feeds cuts. Hopefully the stardom will not catch up to Tex, as her ego seems to be top on her mind. She has her gimmick outfit set, and her fitness has never looked better, only one more step, all she has to do is pave the road to stardom.

{--END TRANSMISSION--}
