American Poetry
'American Hero' Ryan Contra

The scene fades into a busy crowd people drinking in a poorly lit bar. As the camera creeps through the crowds, waitresses, pool tables it snakes itself to the back of the room. Looking up at the television which in this bar not a single patron took the time to look at the t.v. On television one of those wrestlers was cutting a promo. As the camera settles on the television and the wrestler the noise of everything seemed to be blocked out.

'American Hero' Ryan Contra - Well folks It's settled I'll be going head to head with the World Champion. You can bet your sorry ass it's going to be an ass kicking. There's going to be babies thrown, puppies kicked, and little tyke's candy stolen because I am going to roll in Memphis doing what I do dirty- or I mean best!

The man Ryan Contra on television smiles for a bit but the screen then turns to static- then off.

Funny how shit does, and doesn't work. I had gotten into wrestling but I knew this European Circuit bullshit wouldn't get me anywhere. So I packed up my shit and got the hell out of dodge. They say if you want the perfect gimmick, that you, yourself take your personality and times it by ten. Live larger than life and you have a chance. Fuck it doesn't work. So I blew off being a wrestler and went for a medical degree. Not exactly what you were expecting, but I had the knowledge. motivation. Now I am a medical Intern. But that's another story. Let me tell you a little childhood nightmare- okay?
The names Ryan Contra. The Professional World would know me by none other than 'American Hero' Ryan Contra. One of the biggest heels active today. Let me spill my heart out. A chance to explain why I hate people, why I just don't care. Alright, still here? Good. Let me take you back through time. Seven years, I am seventeen. Third Year of High school.

The scene fades in and we appear to be face to face with a pushed in cigarette lighter. After minutes of utter silence a slight noise can be heard. As it get's louder it's muffled- sounding like screaming. The lighter, with a sudden pop is ready for use. A hand reaches down and grabs the lighter, with it- the hand with the lighter leaves the screen. Following the hand now up close to the lighter against a cigarette. As it seems to be resting against the cigarette three smoke clouds form in quick secession. The hand as well as the lighter go back down but the camera slightly zooms out. The so thought screaming is actually laughter from a young man in the passenger side. The driver taking a hit from the cig put his hand on the steering wheel as he let's his other hand hang from the car window.

Well, that's me folks. Yeah I was a pretty boy from the get-go. The passenger there is Emmerich Nocie. My best friend. Right now we're heading to school.

Emmerich was wearing a Wife beater and a black Adidas visor. Regular blue jeans. Ryan was wearing Dark blue jeans and a Weightlifting Henston High school T-shirt. His build was okay, you could see ryan was a lifter just by the massive size of him compared to his friend and passenger. Ryan was quite tan, tanners? Maybe? His hair was short, thick and jet black. His face smooth and handsome. There vehicle interior was worn out. You could tell it was Ryan's first car. But whatever works.

Emmerich - Dude your gonna' go to Lincoln Park tonight right? I might need you tonight. Archie's been talking shit big time. Saying I am the reason we fucking lost our game the other night. You believe that shit?

Ryan - I don't think I'll make it there. I got Jamie and Annie. Well you didn't show up bro. You gotta show up, they need everyone, especially a pro like you.

Emmerich - Wasn't my fault. Always about you and your girlfriend and your little girl. Your such a bitch lately. Hey- you know what they've been saying lately right? A lot a people are disrespectin' you man. Well, your girl. They've been saying only reason Annie got pregnant was so she could keep you in line.

Ryan - Fuck em' let them say what they want. Want to know the truth? Jaime was my idea. I want a kid, and I love her.

Emmerich - Dude- I swear you've slept with like every chick ever. Now you pussying-out?! Ain't like you kid.

The conversation stops, as Ryan finishes his cig- looking out the the window, then over to Emmerich. Just as Ryan was going to turn around he noticed Emmerich sticking his head out of the window.

Emmerich - Hey ladies, looking for a dick to ride?! Hahahah

Ryan nods his head in disgust, though a month ago he would've pulled over for the ladies. Driving further down the rode, more and more kids could be seen. The one's close to school, walking. Emmerich froze when they passed two kids their age.

Emmerich - Man stop this fucking car- Stop this fucking car!!

Ryan quickly pulled to the curb as Emmerich got out and took off in the other direction. Looking through the back window Ryan saw who he was approaching-

Ryan - Fuck!

See, were I am from, we watch each others backs. There were two of them, two of us. I was expected with no thinking to keep pace and help Emmerich. Course that's exactly what I planned on doing too. Just the way things were. Were didn't live in a ghetto or anything, or 'capped' each other. Fists are our thing. We did it well too- fighting I mean.

Getting out of the car Ryan caught up with Emmerich just in time. Emmerich was yelling at the kid on the right. Emmerich wound up and grabbed the kid's shirt, but in the nick of time the crossing guarding separated the two.

Emmerich - Yeah you ain't shit boy! Boy! You don't mean shit to me kid! Tonight Lincoln's Park! You better be there you fucker!

Ryan eventually got Emmerich back into the car. Once back in the car they took off again- towards school. Turning up the radio on one of his favorite songs Ryan lit another cigarette.

I knew the two kids too. They were cool. But once Emmerich made up his mind, there was no changing it. Thing is Emmerich never learned to take responsibility. Nothing was his fault in his eyes and he just didn't fucking care. I grew up with the kid so it wasn't like I would just stop talking to him. In a place like Henston he saved my ass plenty of times. We didn't have too many in our school, but we had enough. It was closely nit, we all knew each other. But as the years have gone by, people's balls it seemed have shrunk. Gang fights were often. Like a bunch against a bunch, or sometimes a bunch against one. Didn't happen too often. But it happened enough.

The scene fades. A day of the past completed. The scene fades in for a moment to see Ryan pulling into a parking space, alone, but then fades out once again. Of course this is the next day. Ryan walked up into the school and opened the doors. Walking in everyone was standing around. Like always waiting for the first bell to ring. Hammering through the crowds Ryan, backpack slung over his shoulder headed to his locker. Once past the packs of teens Ryan opened his locker only to hear a pretty voice behind him.

Annie - Who's that handsome guy?

Ryan turned around, smile from ear to ear.

Ryan - And how is my lady this morning?

Annie was beautiful. Brunette, firmly built, mature young women. Though kids were all around trying to manage bullshitting, getting there books,and getting to class just in time there stood this beautiful girl. All for Ryan.

Annie - Jamie's fine. My mom's looking after her.

Ryan - Sweetheart I am talking about you. What's with that look on your face? What's the matter? Everything okay.

Annie - It's about Noice, Emmerich. It isn't good. Last night he was supposed to fight Archie you know, but it was weird, Archie didn't fight back. Just made sure he himself didn't get hit. Turns out he is a hell of a boxer. After Emmerich got tired out. Archie embarrassed him.

Ryan - Embarrassed?

Annie - Saying that he was stupid. All sorts of little stupid shit. It really got to him.

Ryan - Were's he now?

Annie - I don't know but I think he might want to hurt Archie. Everyone there was laughing at him. Well that's what April told me.

Archie - Hey Ryan.

Ryan Contra - Hey, what's up with Emmerich.

Archie - I just humiliated him a little- we're cool though, right?

A crowd was gathering, want to hear what happened with Emmerich, or share in the stories.

Ryan - Course' were cool Arch, no problem. Gotta' forgive Emmerich though alright?

The crowds were leaving knowing the bell was about to ring soon. Leaving Annie and Arch of course Ryan- talking about last nights events.

Ryan - Kind of worried about him. Stopped by his house earlier this morning to pick him up. He wasn't there. Which is odd, he's ALWAYS there hahahaha. Just be on the look out, he might not be thinking okay.

That was like the turning point. The day's from then on out were weird to say the least. Emmerich hardly showed up to school, and ignored the fuck outta' me. It's like he was pissed at everyone and me included for what had happen with Arch. But Emmerich is my best friend. You first have to understand that a best friend to me- well there is nobody like that person. They understand you like a brother. If you can understand that I'll continue. Shit just happened so quick.

The scene fades into a crowed classroom. Kids all at their seats, most paying attention to their teacher. A few here and there crashed out on there desk.

P.A System(front office) - Code red, Wing A- I repeat code red wing A- this isn't a drill.

What exactly did that mean. Code red, was- someone had a gun and was using it. Wing A, well there was wing B and wing A in Henston High school. This shooter was in wing A. As we had practiced so many times before we bunched up against the back wall, as far away from the door as possible. After a few of the people stopped asking what was going on it was completely silent. You know when your trying to fall asleep, and with complete silence you can hear everything. From the house groaning to the tree's outside- everything is magnified by ten. Well while we all sat there and listened all we could hear was a faint buzz, and a snap every ten seconds. Well after awhile we could make out the cry's and the gun shots.

Ryan - We just gonna wait here until he makes it over to us?! My girlfriend, the mother of my child is in wing A. Fuck this.

Ryan stood up and ran to the other side of the room- were the windows are. Taking a globe from the vent ryan smashed out the window. As kid's rushed over to get the hell out of there the teacher started up.

Teacher - What in the hell do you think your doing. Were staying right here! THIS IS SERIOUS.

Ryan - Yeah I understand that. Looks like YOUR staying right here. There getting to safety while the shooter isn't over here. If you have any sense you'll do the same!

The teacher looks from the door to the window, to ryan and back to the window again.

Teacher - Just stay behind me and hurry up.

Once everyone had gotten out of the window giving the exception of Ryan- ryan took the time to jar one of the foots of a desk. Giving him a long metal object, something he could use if he came in contact with the shooter.

Now I've lived with this secret long enough. When it was all said and done, I kept the truth to myself. But at that time I let people believe I was a hero. But I didn't pick up that weapon or whatever you want to call it, because I was going to go save everyone. Hardly- but if most were dead in A wing, all my friends, people I've lived with- Annie, I would wanna go too. I didn't want to be one of the kids who survived while all my friends died. It would tear me apart. It wasn't something I wanted to deal with that's for sure. I knew that right than too. I wasn't looking to save lives, I was looking for death. Anyway I had made past the cafeteria and into A wing with ease. Along the way I broke into each and every room to let them know to get the hell out of there! They did, they always asked me if I knew what had happened. Of course I said I was going to find out. Making it to A wing it wasn't what I expected.

The Hallway was empty, the gunshots it seemed have stopped as well. Walking down the hallway Ryan looked at the first door on his right- it hadn't been broken into yet. So logically the killer didn't get down that far yet. At the base of the A wing. Ryan thought for a moment that maybe he skipped that room. Continuing down most rooms were untouched. He thought of all the kids inside freaked as hell. Continuing Ryan started walking slowly. He could feel his heart beat, Ryan thought for a second that maybe the killer would hear his heart too and come out to the hallway. Walking up to the second to last room- it was wide open. Annie was having class there. Walking in there was blood everywhere. In the middle of it all, semi-automatic at his side- Emmerich.

Ryan - Emmerich what the FUCK?!

Emmerich didn't move, just looked at the dead bodies. Emmerich didn't even acknowledge him. After too long of a silence Emmerich spoke.

Emmerich - I am sorry. Will you die with me?

Emmerich turned around, raised his gun and shot Ryan right in his mid-section. With that- Emmerich put the gun to himself.

Ryan Contra - Yeah that's how that went.

Elder Patient - What, you didn't finish. What happened.

Ryan contra - The bullet nicked my ribs, nothing serious. Annie, and seventeen other died that day. But Now I am glad that I didn't. I have my girl Jamie, and this career of mine.

The elder patient smiled, and nodded. Dozing off to sleep, or at least letting Ryan know he was done talking. When a voice came across the room, a man blocked by a separating cover.

Ryan Contra- Ah, Reno. How's that broken arm?

Ryan went over to see Reno, seeing if he needed anything.

Reno - No Damn it I don't need a booster of meds goddammit! I listened in on your story!

Ryan Contra - Oh yeah?

Reno - What I am interested in- is the wrestling!! So you total given up on that? I tell you what doc, you feel the need to try your look at a professional school, you go right ahead and look in my coat pocket. It will have the name and address of a Wrestling Academy. If your interested.

Ryan thought for a moment and nodded. Two days later Reno was gone, and the everlasting thought of- what if, left as did the address. Coming in one night though, on his desk laid the card. Ryan thought of it for a week. Now juggling his wrestling career, and internship. Ryan has a new outlook. With his daughter Jamie, Ryan's looking forward to the future.
