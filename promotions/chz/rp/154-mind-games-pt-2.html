Mind Games.Pt 2
Written: 8/21/02

The life as a female wrestler is significant! Picture yourself going to the ring every week and not just fighting for yourself, but fighting for your sex considering you’re the only female in the federation. Life is good because of the attention of course, but in recent weeks she has proved she has more then looks to offer! The Texan is on a mission, so back up!

The days are growing short until Wild. With just two days before Wild, and all the CZ wrestlers are preparing for the worst! With just two weeks in CHZ, many feuds have begun! Hopefully this week’s Wild will settle them, and if not the first CHZ PPV will! The Texan and Axl Young have been feuding since the beginning of CHZ, but last week with the cheap shot with a weapon by Axl Young set the feud off. The Texan is eager to get in the ring and destroy Young for his antics in the past weeks, and Axl Young is demanding Angel Summer’s blood! Now both are serious with their remarks! There is only one more thing else to do, prove you’re a person of your word!

The feed begins in Toronto, at the home of The Texan. Three stories high, the apartment of The Texan and her roommate stands! Many adjustments have been made in the 3 weeks they have lived in the apartment! New oak wooden floors, black leather furniture and some walls knocked out, to open the main room up some more! And skylights have been added to bring more light to the rooms. The newly painted walls, many bright colors do bring out their enjoyment of life!

Angel is sleeping still, as it nears noon. As her roommate Robyn is settling in on the couch watching some her favorite soaps. Angel has been sleeping quite a bit the last few days, mostly do to drinking at the heels party, and staying up late with Robyn, going to the bars and such. As she lies in her bed, sheets and blanket pulled to the end of the bed, as she lies bare, with just her underwear and bra. Because of the heat she often sleeps with no covers over her.

Today’s agenda consisted of not much, but a light training session at her gym up the street, to give a few light workouts before her match! Around 1:30pm Angel woke up and slowly got dressed, and made her way to the gym. Her light workouts consisted of a 2-hour time period. Basically light weights, stretching, biking, rowing, running and aerobics, More then enough to break a sweat across her forehead.

Around 4:00pm, she set her workout to a rest, and decided to go home and veg for the rest of the day. As she was walking home, as the gym is just a few blocks away from her home, she was interupted by a CHZ’s reporter Wayne King. Wayne asks for an interview, and Angel gladly excepts, aslong as it is not her new enemy Alison Malone.

The interview takes place at a nearby back alley near the gym.

{-Begin Interview-}

“This is Wayne King, standing here live with young superstar of CHZ, Angel The Texan Summers! She is here with me today, to talk about her upcoming match against Axl Young, and her strategy to defeat him!“

“ I have no changes in my strategy, I am going to go out there this week at Wild, and do what I have done in previous weeks, give my all! I have a good feeling on this weeks outcome, my anger at Axl Young is growing by the second, and I am just itching to get in the ring!“

Wayne King itches his balding head, as he asks another question.

“ How do you feel about Axl Youg requesting for your blood, he seems determined!“

Angel lets out a slight laugh as she answers Wayne’s question.

“ Young is obviously not a smart man, to say the least! Anyone can demand anothers blood, but to go through with it is another story! I can come out week in and week out, demanding people’s blood, claiming I will win and demandng I am the best, but I let the fans decide who I truly am, and whatever happens, will happen! Yes, I plan to win, and not just win, but destroy, but I wont claim I am better the Axl Young, its all about strategy, and fortunitly he is not a mental fighter, but more a physical one. I will wiat him out, and then attack! I succeed with my mind, and the physical part comes later in the match.“

“ Well Angel you have proved yourself thus far, and the fans seem to love you! So keep up the great work, and I am sure I speak for everyone, Good Luck!“

{-End Interview-}

The interview cuts, and Wayne thanks Angel for the interview, then packs up and leaves. Angel then finishes her walk home, and settles in with Robyn on the couch, and sleeps her day away!

{-End Feed-}
