The Hit
Written: 07/29/02

“No Cage, fuck you.”

~~~A monstrous hulk of a man stands over a dark shape lying motionless on the hot asphalt surface. Despite the excruciating Nevada sun directly overhead, the man wears black dress pants, black alligator sandals, a black dress shirt and a black trench coat. Cracking his knuckles, which remain hidden within black leather gloves, the man stoops and kneels down on one knee as he removes his black Armani sunglasses with his right hand and wipes the omnipresent combination of sweat and dust with the handkerchief in his left hand. Looking closer, the shape on the ground appears to be a man, rather sizable himself. A pool of blood continues to form near his head as the man dressed all in black reaches down and jerks the beaten man up by the hair, raising his head a four or five inches above the scalding ground. This new viewpoint brings the man’s profile into view for the first time, and perhaps the last, as we see deep lacerations across his entire face from which dark crimson blood continues to flow. Crouching a little lower with a loud grunt, the large man stares menacingly into the barely conscious man’s face and begins to speak in a deep and guttural voice.~~~

“I said fuck you Cage. No one’s here to protect you now, because every ally of yours is now an accomplice of mine. Everyone you thought you could trust was with me the entire time. You stand alone, Cage. It’s really quite amazing how much things can change in a couple of hours isn’t it? Just yesterday you were on top of the world, sipping on fine wine with dozens of pretty and willing girls by your side? You had it all…but you threw it all away, you silly little prick. You can’t even begin to appreciate the wealth of riches you left behind, that you simply threw away when you betrayed me.”

~~~The monster ceases and glares up at the harsh midday sun, before spitting to his right and beginning again.~~~

“Sure Cage, the choice was ultimately yours. You knew the alternative; hell, you’re experiencing it right now. But the funny thing is, no matter what you say to justify your decision, no matter how great a lengths you go to right your wrongs, deep down you and I both know you’ll never be clean. You and I are kindred spirits Logan, we both knew what we had to do, but when it comes down to it, when morality and mercy and pain look you dead square in the eye, you don’t have what it takes. You’re weak, Cage, and I pity you for that. You’re going to die today, Logan Cage, and there’s nothing anyone can do to stop it, least of all you. So embrace it, because your life’s only going to get worse from here. Enjoy your time in hell, Cage, I’ll see you when I get there.”

~~~With that, the man slams the Cage’s head back down into the asphalt and reaches into his trench coat, pulling a pistol back out of it along with another handkerchief. Placing the handkerchief atop Cage’s head, the man stands back up to his two feet and points his glimmering silver gun. Suddenly, the man crouches back down to his knee and lowers his lead close to the bloodied man’s head once again. Craning his neck for the best possible angle from which to listen, we begin to hear a garbled noise from the victim. Moving his head ever so slightly, Cage suddenly turns to the right and coughs out a sickening amount of blood onto the attacker’s expensive alligator sandals. Smiling, the large man cocks his gun again and points it forward, only to again lower it as Cage now whispers ever so slightly.~~~

“Do it.”

“What the hell did you say?”

“Do it.”

“Cage, you’re begging me to kill you. Damn boy, I really am doing you a favor aren’t I? I swear, it can’t believe…”

~~~The monster of a man stops as Cage cuts him off.~~~

“Because if you don’t kill me now, I will kill you later.”

~~~Obviously shocked by the last comment, the big man stands completely still for a brief moment before beginning to brutally kick the beaten man in the ribs. After some thirty straight seconds of spiteful beating, the kicking stops and he begins to speak again.~~~

“I’ll tell you what Cage, I’m not going to make it that easy on you. I’m gonna let you live, if you can really call your current state living. You will never work again in this town, or his industry for that matter. I’ll make your life such a living hell you’ll beg pull the trigger yourself. I made you Cage, and I can sure as hell unmake you just as easily. You’ve always been expendable, but now you’re going to realize just how painful expendability can be. So congratulations Logan Cage, you will live, and you will learn—Mr. Matthias is not to be fucked with. Enjoy life as a cripple, bitch.”

~~~The big man stands to his feet again, holstering his gun and striding away. As the monstrous figure nears his awaiting black Escalade, he casts a final look toward Cage’s beaten body, barely visible amidst the swirling dust storms that litter the abandoned lot.~~~

“Sorry son of a bitch, you should have let me kill you.”

~~~Stepping into the driver’s seat, the man starts the engine and begins the long drive back to the casino, confident he will receive a glowing accommodation for his fine piece of work, eliminating the infamous Logan Cage.~~~

~~~The Escalade is just disappearing in the background when a slight movement from the victim’s right hand is noticed, then his left. And sure enough, as the scene begins to fade to black, Logan Cage’s hands are no longer limp at his side, they are instead two bloodied and scarred fists.~~~

~~~Fade to Black~~~
