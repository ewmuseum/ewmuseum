Next stop Moncton!
Written:

“Next stop Moncton,” the severely overweight train conductor pants as he makes his exit into the next car. “Moncton next stop!”

Tom Core clicks off his cell phone and is obviously pleased with what he has just heard. Still dressed in his new and perfectly pressed navy blue suit with a gray tie, Redemption Song’s manager and lawyer pulls off his black sunglasses which is always a signal that he wants his team’s attention. Ben Nighthorse Campbell is still seated next to Core and is awaiting whatever news there may be. Jim Surge and Misty are too wrapped up in each other in the seats across the aisle and it takes a throat clearing from Core to get their attention.

“That was my old tag-team partner Larry Lawrence,” Core says in his bizarre high-pitched voice, “he said he’ll think about joining the Watkins Group!”

Jim nods, knowing that Redemption Song is just the tip of the iceberg to help establish the Watkins Group in the wrestlingtattler universe and that the ultimate goal is the arrival of the legendary Doc Watkins himself. But those days were still a few months away. First would be a CHZ promotional appearance in Moncton. The train slows to a stop and the side door opens revealing a much healthier crowd than the crowd in Truro, at least 100 fans. The old man with the wrinkled nose helps set up the red, white and black podium that still lacks any official CHZ logo.

“Hey guys,” Core whispers to Jim and Ben, “Lawrence also said you guys are booked on OSW’s Slam! again this Saturday against NC-17!! This is our last stop for now and we’re flying back to Pennsylvania. There’s a helicopter on its way here for us.”

Jim’s eyes light up and a thin smile crosses his lips. NC-17 would be a great challenge but a great showcase for him. But then he would have to make a very important decision - would Redemption Song honor their CHZ booking against Gold Rush on Wednesday Wild even though they’re going to be damn tired and quite possibly hurt or would they skip it and risk being fired? Jim shakes his head a bit and walks over to the podium with Misty at his side. Ben suddenly becomes wild-eyed and grabs Jim by the arm.

“Be careful,” Ben says with a devilish smile that plays his pearly white teeth against his jet black hair perfectly, “I sense a rabid beaver.”

“You what?!” Jim laughs, taking off his tan Stetson cowboy hat and swatting his half-brother playfully. “Wait, are you talking about Misty or do you really sense an animal?” Jim gets a little more serious, remembering Ben’s senses as a trained Apache Ghostrider might just allow him to reach out into the Great Spirit and sense some great ‘disturbance.’

Ben nods and answers in his mock Native-American monotone: “Nope, I was talking about Misty.”

“Hey!” Misty exclaims, looking a little hurt.

Jim instantly lowers and shakes his head, a little disappointed that Misty had been clued in on the conversation.

Suddenly, a wild beaver with thick white froth dripping from it’s mouth jumps up through the side door and tries to bite Misty with its nasty buck teeth!!!

“AHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!” Misty screams and she puts her hands on her head and jogs in place sort of like a scared cartoon character!!! The beaver narrowly misses Misty and then turns around to eye up Surge.

The crowd of 100 CHZ fans start to panic and scream as well. Many people start bumping into each other and then, in the confusion, some asshole throws a punch. That’s all it took as people instantly took up chairs and started beating each other down in a full-out riot!!! Tom jumps onto his cell phone and checks to see the status of the helicopter almost at the same exact moment that it comes into view over the horizon. The wild beaver gets Jim Surge in it’s sights and charges straight at him, the animal’s teeth extended and dripping with sick venom. As it closes in it leaps into the air and from nowhere Ben Nighthorse Campbell grabs its tail and smacks it hard into the wall of the train car. Campbell doesn’t finish until he delivers 25 more punishing blows to the beaver, totally splitting the beaver open and seeing all of it’s vital juices flow out, utterly destroying it.

The buzz of the helicopter gets increasingly louder and the trees around the immediate area start to quiver from the force of the machine’s propellers. Tom Core grabs Misty by the arm and jumps out of the train side-door, doing his best to avoid the rioting hooligans but still getting thwacked several times on the side of his arms and legs. Surge looks in Campbell with sheer admiration for the way he took care of that beaver like a man, even if it was just a touch excessive. Campbell nods and the Redemption Song members jump down from the train and through the rioting crowd. In the middle of the crowd, Jim spots the old man with the wrinkled nose.

“Save yourselves!!” he says in his shaky old person voice as he thwacks someone with a Gold Rush t-shirt with his cane. “Don’t worry about me, I’ve led a full life! We’ll meet again!”

Jim just shakes his head in his “yeah, whatever” sort of way and catches up to Core and Misty, using his own body to shield the two smaller folks from any incidental blows from the riot. The helicopter comes to a near landing but does not touch the ground up on a small open area of train track just beyond the Moncton station. The four members of the Watkins Group jump into the helicopter and they take off back toward Pennsylvania.
