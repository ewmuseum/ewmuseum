Meeting her friends and Unanswered Questions
Written: June 19

Flaton? What kind of name is that?

It was a name that I had never liked all my life, that was sure but I had grown to like it....... not! The truth was that I tried to hide my name from my friends. They all said that the name wasn’t that bad but compared to their last names, mine was horrible! People always used to call me “flat” Flaton. Sure, my boobs didn’t grow as fast as some of the other girls, but they can be so bad!

I didn’t really like my first name either. Or I should really say that I didn’t like my FULL first name. I wasn’t very fond of Amanda for a name but I liked Mandy. It was so more sophisticated for an eighteen year, fun loving girl like me. Ever since grade three or four, to everyone my name was Mandy and I was happy.

My parents, where should I start with them. I guess you could say we were rich.... or I was rich. I was an only child so I got most of the attention and lots of stuff to play with which made all of the neighbourhood kids want to come over to my house. At first I lived in a trailer, just me, mom and dad. Then dad got a job at a local real estate/insurance broker office and everything got better. Not long after my dad got the job, we moved out of our trailer and into a three bedroom house. I was eight at the time when we got into our new house. Our house had three bedrooms, two upstairs and one downstairs. For the first few years until I was ten or so, I slept upstairs until one day I was moved downstairs and into the great downstairs as I called it. Right beside my room was a computer room and a bathroom for all of my teenage needs.

Then one day I turned fifteen and was more of a punk teenager. I had to admit, I was pretty hot, even the boys thought so. Lets just say, I had my share of boy asking me out!

I slowly turned sixteen and then seventeen and I was at the point that every teenage comes to in his or her life. I had to choose where I wanted to go in my life. What I wanted to major in. I decided that Edmonton was big enough, I wasn’t leaving home and it seemed that my friends felt the same way.

One day while on the internet, I stumbled on Canadian Heat Zone. Of course I had wrestled before but it was mostly play wrestling and not real. I didn’t think I would have a chance there! Then my friends, Renee, Jenna, and Celeste finally convinced me to at least apply! What was the harm in that?

I applied normally like any person did, not expecting to get it.

Over a week later, look what happened and look where I am now!

Now, as I stood in the entrance of my apartment that I shared with my three friends, I thought of what my job would hold? Would it be good or not? Certainly the pay was good but would the other wrestlers like me?

I walked into the apartment and took of my shoes. In the kitchen, after you walk from the door about four feet too the left was my friend, Jenna. She loved to cook and wanted to be a chef. She usually cooked all of our meals for us and we were all grateful. I greeted her as I walked by.

“Hey Jen, what are we having for supper tonight?”

“I was planning on Marinara Chicken Breasts with mashed potatoes and salad.”

Good Jenna. Her food was so good and tender and she loved to do it so we were all happy! It was just another good supper ----- as always!

“Sounds great!”

I didn’t want to disrupt her work so I walked away as she nodded. Straight ahead was our living room. In the living room were Celeste and Renee. They were playing Playstation. They loved how the men moved across the screen. Loveable Celeste and Renee. I walked by them and the cords to their controllers to get to the computer room, and right beside it, my room. I walked by them and smiled at them but they seemed to be in there own little world, for they mumbled at me.

“He Mandy”

“Hi girls!”

I said it more cheery then I thought. I walked over their cords and walked into the computer room.

What will CHZ hold for me?

Will it be good or bad?

Who will I like?

Who will I dislike?

Will I like my boss?

Will he like me?

Who will I face?

Will I be a good professional wrestler?

Are there other girls there?

Question.

Answered?

We’ll soon find out!
