Diary of a tortured soul: Conclusion.
Written: 28/7/02

Axl, if you are ever to read this in the future, remember this. As you sat here writing this entry into your journal of torment, with fresh blood seeping from the wound you endured yesterday and bruises surfacing on your face clearly already, remember that today you were massacred and savaged in a way beyond your imagination. They brutalised you in ways you thought they could not. Remember this day, for the love of GOD remember this fucking day because I'm not going through the agony of trying to write with a hand that has been stamped on repeatedly by combat boots. Remember this fucking day!!! Remember this day not just for the beating you took, but for........ the change. You felt it again today!

*He got the strange look and giggle at the cut brandished on his head from yesterday. It was a sickly sight now though, dried up blood and yellow pus seeped through the small slits and cracks where it hadn't fully scabbed over it yet. Not even his long black hair could cover it all up.*

*Music Technology was his first subject of the day, thankfully, at least there he could be filled with people who he could associate with. Once there he let out his aggression whilst thundering through riffs on a guitar while recording samples. Brian Roberts thought his cut was 'fucked up..... but in a totally good way', while Eve Patterson said the last time she seen something like that, she flushed it. So it was an overall mixed response.*

*As he left the studio he began to think where Randy was, he hadn't seen him all day, and he knew he would never miss music technology because Randy was certain that with a few more lessons allowing him to 'turn on the charm on Eve' would guarantee him to lose his virginity. However all these alarming thoughts were shoved out of his head by the recurring reminder that he had an appointment with the collage shrink. Axl didn't know the full title of the guys job or what it required him to do, but he was basically a shrink who couldn't make it to the big leagues. If only Axl knew he would be more qualified than this man in the somewhat same profession, and become a master manipulator of the human mind..*

********

'So, how have you been these last few days?'

Typical type of shrink. Too full of his own shit with a cocky accent in his early thirties to drum it into your head that he really one of your friends.

'I've been fine'

Axl was calm sitting in the chair opposite him, it wasn't one of those long reclining chairs, just an ordinary wooden armchair. The room was not exactly well furnished either to start with, not like the offices the shrinks in New York have.

'Hmm, I see. I heard you had a slight scuffle the other day. Is this true?'

'Yeah, yeah it's true'

'Right, soooooo. Care to tell me who it is?'

'Yeah, a fucking assho-'

'NOW! I thought we talked about this last time, there is no need for you to use such extreme profanity when talking to me!'

'Well it the truth!'

'Now, remember what I said last time. Sometimes it is the bigger man that walks away from the fight. Next time when you face Eddy, I am guessing it is Eddy again, just ignore him and calmly walk away.'

'What?!'

'Just walk...'

'Are you serious!?'

'Look now Axl, am I right in assuming this was Eddy again?'

'Yeah you are'

'Well... I thought so'

'Well congratulations, I can see why they hired you and cash your pay checks'

'Mr. Young!'

'No, screw you!'

*Axl rose up from his seat and grabbed his bad in the process. He couldn't get over that he told him to walk away from a fight. That was almost suicide, you walk away from a fight and you were pretty much signing your life away. Today was not exactly going to plan, today was going south of heaven fast. Something was wrong with today, something terribly wrong.*

*He ate his lunch in the cafeteria by himself that day, not that he hardly ate with dozens of popular chums everyday anyway. He sat in a corner away from everyone else, hoping he would see Randy from this view he had positioned himself in which enabled him to view all of the cafeteria. The food was of a murky saturated colour and contained a rubbery dry taste that sent your taste buds running for cover. He didn't even exactly know what he ordered. The uneasy feeling in his stomach had not healed or surpassed either, it still lingered and clung inside there. Where the hell was Randy. Then again...... where was Eddy?*

*He hurled out of his last class in collage, thinking anxiously. Eddy missing a day of school was not unusual, yet all of his entourage not being there and Axl not receiving a beating yet was just unheard of. Along with Randy missing, this could only lead to one conclusion , a vulgar ending.*

'AXL! AXL!'

He turned, it was Eve. She was running towards him like he had a Free Househusband sign on his back. She had a look of intense shock in her eyes and the sound of grief in her shuddering voice, and he could clearly see a small tear rolling down her eye.

'What's the matter!?'

Intense urgency was in his voice, but deep down he knew he didn't need to know the answer.

'Round the back...... the football field.......... Randy!'

** * * * * * * * ** * * *

He stormed through the campus grounds like his life was on the line. His long black hair waving behind him as the wind swept through it as he sprinted. The sight that greeted him was slightly sick to say the least.

'GET HIM DOWN FROM THERE!!!!'

Eddy and his goons were there, and about 10 people in the bandstand watching and laughing. Randy was hanging from the football posts upside down. A thick chunk of rope tied around his legs. His mouth was gagged by a piece of duck tape stuck over it.

Eddy turned and began to shout taunt Axl to come and get his friend. Why the hell would they go to these lengths just to get to him?! What the hell did he do?! The answer stood no more than 10 feet away from him watching from behind one of the walls adjoined to the cafeteria. Kelly had a clear swelled black eye that was almost as grotesque to look at as Axl's seeping wound. Eddy had obviously taken a disliking to the lie Kelly had told him about Axl coming onto her, maybe he had believed it too much. Either way she wasn't by his side so they had obviously broke up, and she had a black eye probably meaning he was the one who ended the relationship on his own terms. She looked at Axl with somewhat pity and sympathy. He felt no remorse for her, she got what she deserved in his opinion. However, now it was time for him to put the wring that Eddy had committed against Randy right.

He strode across the football field, feeling the intensity of the eyes gazing at him frm the bandstand. He tried to ignore the, and focused on the onjective in front of him. Eddy. The walk seemed like walking down The Green Mile to face your execution. Axl had a feeling it would be his execution, but if it meant getting Randy down from the footbalkl posts it would be soemwhat worth it.

'Lookie here, we got us.......'

'Cut him down!'

A chorus of 'Ooooos' echoed through the field.

'Ohhh, now.....'

'Cut him down now you worthless motherfucker!'

No 'Oooos'. Just silence. That was all he had to say for this battle to commence fully. Eddy stared at him intensely and gestured for his remaining goons to make the others in the band stand leave immediately. This didn't take long after some strong verbal persuasion. What did come as a surprise was when Eddy ordered his other goons to leave as well, saying he wanted Axl for himself. After more verbal persuasion and the muffled moans of Randy ringing through Axl's ears, they finally decided to leave.

'OK asshole, let's settle this right here and right now!'

Axl merely nodded, and was about to come out with some clever remark, but was cut off as Eddy's clenched fist smacked into his jaw. Axl fell to the floor like a sack of potatoes, and rolled up into a ball as Eddy proceeded to stomp him repeatedly. Axl finally managed to crawl away and get to his feet. This was only the start of the beating.

* * * * * * * * * *

Blood now gushed from the wound in his head, no longer seeping, and also poured from his lip. He was propped up against the football posts as Eddy has his way with punishing him.

'Mess with my girl!'

A right hand to the chest driving the air out of Axl's lungs.

'Call me a motherfucker!'

A right hand to the jaw of Axl making his head snap backwards and hit the back of the football post.

'Dare try and fight me!'

A leaping attack onto the back of Eddy by Kelly......... what?!

Kelly leaped onto the back of her ex boyfriend who abused her and clawed at his eyes. Axl watched as he yelled in pain and anguish, Kelly's grip deadly and seemingly unbreakable. Axl grinned a little, never before being so happy to see Kelly, and mustered up all his strength left in his battered body, and run and charged at Eddy, tackling him to the ground, sending Kelly down as well in the process.

'Get Randy!'

Axl pointed to Randy hanging from the football posts to Kelly, and she wasted no time in getting him down. Axl then didn't let Eddy catch his breath, and delivered a set of right hands to the face combined with some stinging left jabs. Eddy was reeling, Eddy was at his mercy.

** * * * * * * * * * * *

Sadistic seemed the appropriate word to describe the situation Eddy was in now. Randy to his left, Kelly to his right. Eddy dangling from the football posts from a piece of rope as they ignited into a blazing ball of flames. None of them felt no remorse, well, at least Axl didn't anyway. The sounds of the fire engines eventually snapped them out of their trance, and brought Axl back to life.

'Let the fucker burn!'

Randy shouted at the top of his voice as they began to walk away slowly. They said hardly anything as they walked away. Eddy was saved, unfortunately, though Axl still remembered his painful grief stricken scared screams. He still remembered them, and how much he enjoyed them.

* * * * * * * * * * * * *

Remember that today Axl, you took no more bullshit. He attacked you heinously and showed no remorse. But you stopped that fucker, you stopped him!!! You rebelled! You fought! You were the rebel!!!!!

* ** * * * *

He put the journal down. His memories of pain and sorrow refreshed. His demented drive recharged. The deep dark demented rebel was coming, and nobody could stop him.


((OOC Note: This sucked I know and half way through writing this series I realised that. So I just quickly wrapped this up as to move on because I really grew tired of this series early.))
