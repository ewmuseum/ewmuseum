The Mask is found!
Written: 7/28/02

Starting off, I was making my daring escape from the un-mounted Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs. The sound of maracas seemed to be getting ever so closer, so using the best possible part of my brain, I clicked my heels again, this time harder against the donkey's midsection. The message was sent clearly and quickly to the brain, or lack of on the donkey for it instantly got off it's lazy...ass and sped up almost like a cartoon character. The Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs did not give up just yet however, they acted as if they sped up their 'rides' and started to shake the maracas harder as the skipping became must more of a hastily run toward me. I looked back and saw the speeding men looking ridiculous as ever.

'I swear, everytime someone looks at these men they must lose at least thirty brain cells, just by looking at them!' I told myself my opinion, though no one cared for no one heard.

I heard faintly the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs screaming at me to halt, only mexican style.

'PARADA! PARADA! PARADA!' The Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs all yelled in unison to me. (Stop! Stop! Stop!) Expecting me to stop with their puny minds, they kept their chase going, slowing down for oncoming tumbleweeds then continuing again in the hot pursuit for two items that can be bought, or stolen in my case, from anywhere whether it be a store, a strip club or the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs' 'secret' hideout/club.

'These guys just don't get it, my journey's not stopping for some sad, strange little men sounding like they're on horses, or donkeys, or whatever they may be 'riding.' They can chase me all they want, I'm getting to my goal!' I pumped myself up as I was a few miles away from the Mexican border, the dream of the journey was in sight.

So I clicked my donkey repeatedly until it finally came up to the Mexican border exhausted and struggling with every ounce of his strength to take one step. Having my supplies from the Wegmans way back in Rochester, I pulled out a finely sharpened pair of (mexican) hedgeclippers. Covered with mexican designs such as sombreros and even the little dolls that go into eachother getting smaller and smaller.

'Steel, don't fail me now.' I prayed for cutting through a huge fence holding strong in front of me and with the fear that the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs may catch up to me. Pushing my sweating blonde hair back from my eyes, I glared with my blue eyes into the fence staring back at me almost laughing as I tried for the first time to cut through it. I kept looking back at the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs from behind me galloping toward me and shaking their maracas furiously as they came near. I tried once more and with a succesful flick of the wrist, the fence cut loose.

'Well, that was easy.' I told myself as I sighed in relief as I then saw many Mexicans then crossing the border into the USA. I was in shock, why were these true mexicans going the wrong way? 'STOP!! You're going the wrong way! Follow me mexican men! Follow me to freedom!!!' I screamed after them as they just continued to climb over and through the same fence I had just cutten through.

I tried with no succession of convicing them to stay but I had to continue on.

'Come on now, Jackass, we're in the heartland now.' I told the donkey with high spirits. I still saw the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs 'chasing' me so I mounted the donkey once more and set off into the country known to me as home, formely known as Mexico. I clicked my heels against it the darted off once again through the fence into Mexico while the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs chased me still with fire in their eyes as they drooled over retrieving their donkey and sombrero, they needed all the mojo they could get, desperate, putrid men, but who knows why they needed the donkey...

Never-the-less I passed by many torn apart houses until I was finally stopped by a mexican police guard in his car. The man was wearing a curly, black mustache and quite a nice sombrero also, it seems that a lot of Mexicans tend to wear sombreroes, imagine that? His black eyes were seen with the bright sun on that scawlding hot day. My blonde hair dripped with sweat from the covering sombrero sheltering my blue eyes. I came to a slow stop facing the Mexican copper as the man got out of his car. He then walked up to me putting his hands on his waist.

'The Federales...oh crap.' I whispered to myself in urgency.

'Señor, did you make that cut in the fence?' The cop asked me pointing to the fence. I started to sweat even heavier. I was nervous at what the condquences of what I had just done. 'Did you Señor?' The cop asked again. I looked around behind me, back at him, I started to breath heavier and heavier before I just exploded.

'YOU'LL NEVER CATCH ME ALIVE!!!' I screamed as I darted back off with my donkey passing the cop and his car.

'Well, that was unexpected, just a five pesos fine.' The cop said to himself as he put on his sombrero casually and got back into his car. 'Oh well, a little chase will scare him a little bit.'

The cop got into his car behind me and started it up, with a little flick of his wrist he screeched his tires but coming fast behind him were the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs. Already going, the man continued on with his driving not seeing the MMMM's until it was too late. Faint, mexican screams were heard behind me as I saw the cop car run over the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs. I turned around and watched the horrible sight.

'Well, at least Mexico's IQ just went up a few points.' I've always seen the glass half full in these incidents. My focus then went back to escaping from the cop car coming straight for me. I looked for an escape path but all I saw was water and a small little bridge. I dashed with the donkey over the bridge with the car honking a horn from the tune of 'la cucaracha!' within it.

'Only one way out of this one,' I said to myself, 'to hide!' I screamed as I jumped off the donkey and into the river and 'hid' underwater. Somehow, the cop drove over the bridge not seeing me in the CLEAR water. After the federale was gone I climbed out of the water and to my utter shock and disbelief, the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs were back on their feet 'riding' once agian after me!

'My God, you aren't human!' I yelled toward them.

'That's true,' The leader said in an evil smile to me, then in unison they all rose up and said, 'We're MEXICAN!'

'Wow, I'm beginning to think this journey was some sort of waste, of brain cells.' I told myself as I got back on the jackass and rode into the nearest town in Mexico.

*******

The town was buzzing with people just like in my flashback that started this journey. Kids all around are playing hopscotch and there's even the McDonalds store I first found my mask. Then it reminded me, I was here to find my true mask! I looked all around the entire place for someone or something that would remind me of my true home.

I looked right, left, right but nothing! I then turned towards the left again and saw a faint design of a hopscotch placement right outside a house with the line of sight to McDonalds...where my flashback exactly was!

'This is it! I'm home!' I said excitedly as I went right into the house and was greeted by the sound of a nice shotgun as I just walked back out with a smile, 'Well, maybe the next one.' I stepped over the hopscotch placement and went into the second house. Inside was a carpet with 'Stimson' printed on it.

'Well, this could be it...now to check upstairs.' I headed straight up the stairs into my humble abode of the past and examined the first bedroom I went into. I checked in the closet, under the toilet, then I realized it was a bathroom. I continued down the hall of my past upstairs and saw 'Stimpo' printed on one of the doors for one of the rooms. I took a big sigh then went inside only to find an empty room except for a lone mask laying in the center.

'My..mask?' I asked my self in amazement as I went right up to the mask and picked it up and under laid a nicely folded note just as I found in both Wegmans and after I was attacked from the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs, who were right outside the house. I picked up the note and read the title:

It begins here.

I opened the letter as I read it aloud to myself, 'Stimpo, Brendon, your journey to Mexico is complete, you've found your mask but now your true destiny begins. Wrestling Stimpo, I've made connections with many men and a few bribes to get you in but I did. Within this note is a ticket for the 5:00pm flight to Canada, you'll know more about it when you arrive there, Son. Best wishes,

-Dad.'

I ended the letter and stuck it in my pocket.

'Canada? But why would I want to go with the moose and beavers? Oh, and this is my father? Oh well, to Canada I go!' I said checking my watch, I had a few minutes to get to the airport of Mexico.

I jumped out of the window of the room and landed on my awaiting donkey.

'That was certainly not planned, thanks jackass. Man, I feel like I'm in a movie!' I said as I looked into the camera. Then away, I clicked the donkey's midsection as I finally was off to the races with a straight destination to Canada with the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs following me. I put the mask on, with it's beautiful designs on it and raced off as the camera view shutted off.
