My meal-ticket (Wild Rp #3)
Written: 5th August 2002

*The screen fades in and we’re in a dark-lit room, which has a bleak, creepy atmosphere about it. One singular light with a lampshade hangs quite low down and loose from the ceiling and as we look closer a large brown leather chair has it’s back to us. A few moments of silence pass, the brown leather chair being the only piece of furniture in the room.

Finally, the chair slowly spins round, knocking the electrical chord of the light in the process and this causes the light to sway left to right, making it difficult to make out who is in the chair. The chair continues to turn, with the person’s face in darkness. Finally, the features of the individual are revealed: Bright red curly hair, which sits on top of the head like a bird nest on a tree branch; a plain white facial colouring with a blue shade surrounding the eyes and eyebrows, a green shade covers the mouth – where stubble can slightly be seen – and a bright red nose, which appears to be a round object placed on the bridge of the nasal area.

With the light still rocking left to right, this individual calmly reaches up and grabs the lampshade, all of the light is now spraying over Hunt and the brown chair. The camera then pans to reveal this person wearing a mutli-coloured jacket, waistcoat and shirt as well as a pair of bright blue trousers, with different coloured patches scattered on the clothing.

The unknown man sits comfortable in the chair, leaning back into relaxation and lifting his left leg up to reveal a rather long red boot. As the camera moves back again, there’s another long red boot – obviously - and this man rests his left foot on his right thigh. After inching closer and closer to the screen and squinting your eyes, in order to identify the individual – it hits you. That smug grin, those piercing dark blues eyes, that stubble that can still be seen, despite the white and green make-up…it’s Matt Hunt, dressed up as a bloody clown. He still hasn’t said anything and just stares into the camera.

The vision of Hunt is that similar to a storyteller, about to start a horror tale. After some distant silence, Hunt sighs deeply and starts to talk*


Hunt: “Meal-ticket, a term that is used quite often in the wrestling world. Many use this expression in reguard to their own career and how they want to reach the next step, if there is one. What exactly does it mean? Who uses this saying more than anybody else? Where did it come from?

So, what does ‘meal-ticket’ actually mean? Does it involve a free coupon for a TV dinner? Does involve a competition to give a bum a decent meal in months? Well, like I said – this term is used more often in the wrestling industry, so obviously those suggestions I just made are completely useless. But it does symbolise efforts made in this competitive business.

Where did it come from? Buggered if I know, I’m not a walking talking history book.

Who would say it more than others? Take your pick. Managers/Agents could say it about their client – “this guy is my meal ticket”. Meaning the manager/agent will probably ride on the back of the wrestler, to push his own career into the spotlight or to further his own goals and basically grab all the money he can. For you North American viewers, take a look on UPN on Thursday nights for the best example.

Wrestlers themselves use the term for an up and coming match, which could either make them or break their careers. “This match is my meal-ticket” Meaning that the person concerned has to come out on top and succeed, in order to ascend the proverbial ladder and become a worldwide talent. Title matches or a match against a fellow aspiring superstar are the focal point of that phase. A young North Carolinian in the world’s biggest Sports Entertainment based company could well use it.

Of course, it’s not only the lesser-known performers that use the term. Those who are already in the spotlight and are regarded in the ‘best’ category, sometimes refer to their match as their meal-ticket. Why? To reach immortality, to win that match to be finally placed into the ‘legend’ category and will be forever be looked upon as a cornerstone of the company.


*Hunt gets even more comfortable, putting his feet up on a small stool, which he drags into shot and fully revealing those abnormally long red clown shoes!*


Hunt: “So with all these explanations of the term “meal-ticket” revealed, what does it mean to me. What picture is painted in my head the second I hear that expression? The scene that slowly illustrates in my mind is that of a man who needs to become a better person. He’s in the gutter, he’s down and out – but he hasn’t given up, he’s not ready to throw the towel in.

Of course, the word ‘meal’ is heavily featured to the point that this man is in dire need of some nourishment. Bang went his diet and the worry of cholesterol, hah! – fahgedaboutit! This man wants something to eat, so he’ll scratch and claw his way out of the gutter. He’ll do absolutely anything to get his hands on a meal, just one meal and silence the inner monster that bangs away at his empty stomach.

Does he become desperate? You bet. Will he sink to the lowest possible ebb in order to survive? Guaranteed. Will he survive? Only he will know.

But what happens when he finally gets his grubby mits on this meal, this feast that awaits him? Does suddenly decide that he’s not hungry and shove the huge plate of food off the table and thus ending the chance of the meal? Or does he gobble down the vegetables, the potatoes, the meat, and the side salad?

Does he shovel every single crumb of bread into his mouth? Or does he stare at the mangled selection, that was once well-presented, as it slides off the broken plate and onto the murky floor?”


*Hunt then pauses before continuing. He takes his feet off the small stool and leans forward, resting both elbows on both thighs with a thoughtful expression*

“I have the meal-ticket, people and this meal ticket comes my way in two days…Wednesday Night. What do I do? Do I just lay back and let that Jake Schultz take my ticket? Or do I stand up and scrap for my prize, do I sink to the lowest point in order to succeed?

It’s possible that whoever wins this match on Wild, will set himself up quite nicely in becoming the CHZ World Champion. I don’t know that for definite, but I’m treating it like that. Like this is my only opportunity to sprint out of the blocks first and charge towards the finishing line

Seeing as I have the drive and determination to re-install the lost honour, pride and prestige into this company and the World Title - I will be the one to scratch and claw my way to the top, not Jake Schutlz. I will be the one who step up one rung on the proverbial ladder, not Jake Schultz and you can all be rest a-damn-ssured that I will take the opportunity, the meal-ticket and cash the bugger in!

My last words before Wild? Simple – Let…the games begin…”

*Hunt sits back and folds his arms as the camera pans back. Hunt stares into the camera, as he moves further and further away. Slowly, the figure of Matt Hunt gets smaller and the screen fades to black*
