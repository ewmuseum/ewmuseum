Alpha | 1A | Mysterious Letter
Written: June 15th, 2002 A.D.

Two feet echo up and down red surface, thud they go. His feet, they move along the carpeted floor of his hotel room, situated several floors up above New York City, in America. Long black silky jeans snake down his legs, a dark red stripe flowing down in conjunction with them. His feet are uncovered, no shoes, no boots, just bare feet. The body of this man comes into visual view, his arms stick out from a grey t-shirt.

This man is Chris Matthews.

RUSH Wrestling Superstar, former CHZ Superstar; now returning to his home.

Matthews’s blonde hair is nearly tied back in a ponytail, snaking its way down toward the middle of his shoulder blades. Black streaks in his hair shine in the light, a few strands of hair dangle down his face.

A knock on the door.

“Ugh” Matthews comments as he walks toward the door. Violently swinging it open, Matthews’s looks around outside the door, nothing to be seen.

“What the fu..” he begins to say, eyes darting all over the place. “I could of swore someone at the door.”

Matthews turns around again and slowly walks over to the cabinet on the side, his feet scrapping on the carpet like a broom. He picks up his gold watch that lies underneath the opened flap of his wallet, Matthews holds the watch into the air as a smile comes across his face, reading the inscription.

“CHRISTIAN I LOVE YOU”

A smile spreads across his face as he reads it; he blinks several times. He sighs long and hard.

“I miss home.” He speaks through a sigh. “Montreal and Toronto, my two homes. Montreal is my childhood home, but Toronto my home in adult life.”

He sighs.

Again.

Again.

A fourth time.

“I do miss it.” he speaks solemnly again.

A shuffling of paper is heard shifting through the door, Matthews slowly turns his head around and watches. He watches as a large red and black envelope slides underneath the crack of large oak door.

“What the...?” he asks himself as the envelope slides further under the door. Matthews drops the watch back down onto the cabinet and slowly, yet cautiously walks over to the door to inspect the letter. He bends down and rips the letter out from underneath the door, the paper scrapping against the hard wood of the door.

“Wonder what this is?” Matthews asks himself; again.... seems to be doing a lot of that lately, could it be madness? No.

On the cover of the envelope is a large gold emblem surrounded by blue wraths of wire, or so it appears. Matthews looks all over the envelope for a return address, but nothing is found. No stamp, no nothing except the logo on the front.

“Hmmm..” he thinks out loud. “Well this certainly is odd. No return address, no stamp... very odd indeed.”

Matthews strokes his chin before he rips the top open, shards of paper fall gracefully to the floor of the hotel room. Matthews pulls the letter out, it’s white text written over a mixture of a red and black. The letter itself isn’t of great length, from what we can see it’s of roughly eight lines.

He begins to read out loud to himself.

“Dear Matthews” he begins to say, “We are happy to announce blah, blah, blah, blah, blah, blah that you have been chosen for blah, blah, blah, please meet me at the blah, blah, blah on Wednesday the 19th of June, 2002. Blah, blah, blah, blah.”

Matthews drops the envelope onto the bed and reads this mysterious letter once more, his eyes dart all over the white writing.

“Hmmm...” he thinks out loud once more; stroking his chin. “This is indeed an odd letter.”

Matthews walks over to the balcony of his New York City hotel room, looking over the city’s beautiful summer scenery a smile spreads across his face. He continues looking over the city of New York, holding the letter tightly in his left hand, scrunching the paper up.

“Sounds to me like another adventure!” Matthews proclaims, he suddenly pauses and scratches his head. “I sounded like I was from Scooby Doo for a second there...”

“Yes, yes you did.” A voice speaks into the room.

Matthews jumps around, slightly surprised and annoyed! The horror, indeed.

“Oh..” he speaks to his person. “Hey, Leah.”

Matthews turns around to see one of his best friends, Leah Fei, standing in the doorway. She wears a black jobbing suit, with a red jacket.

“What’s this about a letter?” she asks Matthews. His face screwing up the question of his Asian friend. “I was listening to you talking about it.”

“Here.” He speaks, passing the letter. Leah immediately begins to read the letter Chris Matthews hands to her. Her eyes grow wider as she continues reading, she makes sounds like “hmmm” and “Ahhh..” while reading the letter.

She finish’s reading the letter and looks at Matthews, “Oh my...” she speaks to him as he nods back.
