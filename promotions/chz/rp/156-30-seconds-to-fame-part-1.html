30 Seconds to Fame (Part 1)
Written: 8/23/02

Prolouge:

Everyone says we all have our 15 minutes of fame. Now, what I misunderstand is how many chances at these 15 minutes to we get? How often to they come to us? The answers to these questions can be answered by simply experiencing your own 15 minutes of fame. But, people may think that 15 minutes of fame truly means 15 minutes of being a famous person, but they have that meaning all wrong. The time can change at many times whether it be by a second or in this case, a mere 30 seconds. Now, by the power of game shows, anyone can appear on a show and attempt to obtain their very own 30 seconds of fame. Will I be able to obtain mine?

Start of Roleplay:

After my singing experience in American Idol, I knew I was in for a talking to from the two men who I thought were still waiting outside my house looking creepy in my very own dark, back alley. I walked down the sidewalk towards my house with my head hung in shame and my heart pounding and thudding in fear because what will the two men do to me when they find out I didn't make the American Idol cuts. Will they be furious? Probably. Will they attemtped to hurt me in anyway? Let's hope not. I passed through the equally spread sidewalk blocks, counting them as I went along with my head facing the ground. I then looked up and saw my house staring back at me and then I noticed a new car in my driveway. The car was a sleek black with a nice sunroof along with a remote key on the truck, which had some good space. The windows were tinted so I couldn't see anything inside as I feared that someone or something could be in there. Then I noticed that on top of the car, there was a letter. I jogged over to the car and reached on the top and took the letter and saw that the front of the letter read the following:

'Your fame begins now Brendon, open this letter at once!'

I wondered if I should follow these instructions, so I did. I ran my finger through the open part and cut the entire letter open and found where the writing was found. I read the letter aloud to myself, and for the camera.

'Well Stimpo, we found out that your American Idol experience did not go well. No worries, we will now enter you in the newest game show of fame, 30 Seconds to Fame. You have to perform anything for a total of thirty seconds before the crowd decides your no good at all and gets you right off the show. Good luck! We have programmed this car to take you directly to the destination.' I ended the letter.

'Now this is something you don't see everyday.' I told myself as I continued to stare at the car and I wondered if this car would actually take me to the place for 30 seconds to fame.

'Why the hell not, It's worth a try.' I said taking a gulp down my throat with a slight shake in my voice.

I opened the car door expecting some kind of explosion but then I heard the ignition start. I hurried inside and shut the door and sure enough, the car started on it's own and was backing up and was driving off in no time.

'Well this is something you don't see everyday. What an age!' I excitedly said as I put on my safety belt making sure I kept myself well within the margine of safety. The car zipped along the the road with ease as the speed gained from sixty, to seventy to eighty. For about thirty minutes, I sat there with my body being sucked back in the chair from the incredible speed that I wasn't used to at all. Then, the car abruptly stopped and it was perfectly parked at my destination no scratches or dents at all.

*******Scene Change from Car to Destination of 30 Seconds To Fame********

The building was even bigger than that of the American Idol station but the line was much more shorter this time and there were different groups of people. Freaks with some kind of metal weapons were jumping around doing flips and kicks and such while others were juggling flaming batons. This was obviously some kind of twisted talent show, then I figured out, it was. I thought to myself that I must have been in the wrong place before I saw a huge banner floating around, being carried by two men through the parking lot reading: Pretty Cool Guys. This must've been a sign, so I stayed in the short line and awaited what was soon to come.

'This should be fun to watch at least.' I said myself encouraglingly to keep the line moving, and it did. Passing the time by thinking of many naked women all over me, I found myself at the entrance to the building within seconds, and with an erected penis, which was an unexpected surprise.

'Here I am, now where are the tryouts?' I asked looking around with my hands on my hips. I looked left, right and center before I finally saw a crowd of people and gathered along with them I heard a muscular voice saying,

'Alright, everyone in another line behind the curtain here so you can start your auditions.' The man said clearing the crowd into lines behind huge blue curtains on a big black stage just as the American Idol show was.

I went along with his command as I followed a horde of people behind the right curtain as I listened to what was on stage at the moment. I also peeked my head out from behind the curtain, because I was the next in line. I saw a group of three up on stage right now and I saw them all carrying flaming batons. I knew that they were just going to juggle them. And sure enough, they did. Flipping them between eachother and even diving for catches, the man who gave instructions was pleased with them and allowed them to participate in teh show. I then took a gulp, swallowing my pride and calmed my beating heart and went out on stage. The place was extremely silent only for the echo of the man speaking to me.

'State your name and what you'll be doing for a talent.' The man ordered me.

'Brendon Stimson, I'll be doing my own interperative dance to the Mortal Kombat theme song, I have the CD right here. Track fifteen is the song.' I told him handing him the CD with a grin on my face.

He put the CD into the CD player as the high beat techno song played loud. I started off with my version of the robot which went quite wrong as I just looked like an old person trying to walk or who already broke all of his bones. The music got faster and I broke out into a breakdance routine that went horribly wrong. I started a worm backwards and then started to spin on the ground but my zipper got caught on a nail in the ground. I tried to move myself out of it with quick movements, but that only brought this hellacious scream.

'AHHHHHHIEEEE!!!!!' I screamed in amazing pain as my wang was just caught in my zipper. I felt like I was in the movie Something about Mary, but then I thought about the scene after and nearly cried my eyes out.

'Son, are you alright?' The man who gave orderes said to me with a consoling sort of tone.

'No.' I said in a whisper with all my pain I didn't have enough strength to speak.

'Well, we want you on the show! Be back here at three p.m. tomorrow.' He told me as I tried to smile.

After about three hours of screaming, I had finally lost my voice and all I heard the sound of paramedics rushing me to the hospital. That was surely one hell of a day, I got into a show and successfully caught my parts in my zipper. Another day of my life gone, another misery experienced. Will I have my thirty seconds of fame?
