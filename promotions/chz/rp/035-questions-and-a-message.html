Questions and a Message
Written: June 23

Last time with Paige she talked about being the second female in CHZ and when she is going to be in a match, but hey a couple of people in CHZ got released so she could be filling in for one of them. She was so lucky on getting in because out of lots of people she made it in, I wondered why El Diablo signed me out of all the women and I still got in.

I wondered if I was going to get to the tag Titles or even the National Title or even the WORLD title that would be hard to get. I breathed quickly thinking about lots of things, all the titles I thought about, and even who I was going to be against or anything to do with CHZ. I had lots of friends but they were away to Band camp the only place I hated, well not all of them were gone but still. There names were Angella (my sister of coarse), Sara, Nikki, and the rest were at band camp.

People have been asking me about how I’m going to do in CHZ am I going to get fired and lots and lots of other questions. I was kind of a tomboy I did hang around with boys but mostly girls because of coarse I am a girl. I had a little sister and her name was Emma she was 6 years old she was fun to play with she made me laugh she made me cry but she also made me happy.

I missed my friends at camp they had to stay there for the whole summer and that just started but I still had friends at home to so that was great. I had a brother that was 26 I think but he died in a car crash so I felt sad inside but it’s been a year now and I have gotten over it but whenever something bad happens I think about it and I feel that is what is going to happen to me.

CHZ was hard for me even though I just got in every time that I’m going into that ring will be hard or me. My mom and dad were still alive my dad is 37 and my mom is 32 so there is not a difference. I used to play a flute but when my brother was still alive he broke it I didn’t like band it was boring.

I promise that I will not miss any ring action and if I’m in a feud or an angle I will go with it. With my sister by my side no one can beat us well they could but still my sis will help when she wants and try to help me win.

I do sleep with a teddy bear that my grandma gave me when I was 3 years old but again she died of heart cancer I cryed my head off when I heard but eventually I got over it and I still keep the teddy as a reminder of my grandma. I’m going for the World title but I don’t think that I’m going to get it but you never know.

I had to stay at my Auntie’s for a week because my parents were at Las Vegas and I wasn’t aloud to go so I had to stay at my Auntie’s. my Uncle died of Brain Damage after a serious concussion so I don’t really have a family no more but that’s ok. My brother was a pain some time’s but other’s he helped me and cared for me and that’s what I like about him.

I have only been in CHZ for like a couple of days and I am happy with it. And that’s why I’m staying here until I get fired or retire or anything else but I’m staying! Ya know what I could replace someone like in the main event or in that other match so I better stay. If I’m in the main event I could get to the National Title if I win the tournament or if I’m in the tag match I could kick that other girls ass what was her name again Amanda Flaton. She is saying that I suck well let me tell you some thing your going to be FLAT HAHAHa.

With my sister by my side I’ll kick anyone in CHZ’ ass because they have to fear a lady that will kick there asses. And Amanda Flaton I have a message to tell you BITE ME.
