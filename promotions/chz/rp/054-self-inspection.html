Self Inspection
Written: 7-12-02

Theo Knox, a man steadily moving up within the ranks of the Canuck Heat Zone with the booking of his latest triple threat match against both Davis Fury and Luke Black on a special taping of the regularly taped Wednesday Night show, Wild. There were many unknown circumstances regarding the entire cards’ run down and Theo wasn't quite certain exactly what was going on himself, however he'd already given up on trying to play catch up with his match and focused on getting himself ready in the best possible way he could imagine, without worrying about what his opponents had and were doing. While he worked in a small gym inside of the arena where tonight’s show would be held later on, Theo told himself that both Fury and Black had already worked harder, and he pushed himself beyond his already high expectations and where his felt even more comfortable. If there is any sport where perfection isn’t achievable, it’s professional wrestling, and Theo kept himself on the boundaries of his so called perfection as often as possible so that a big ego wouldn’t cross the line.

On that note, our scene opens to show Theo after his workout at the gym, in front of full length mirror talking to himself. Maybe an aftereffect of the tranquilizers he’d been tricked into taking by Tyler Davidson on the previous Wild, maybe because his self esteem was too low as to share it with anyone else, whatever the cause Theo stood there, bare-chested with a pair of black, Nike athletic shorts on. And in all his glory, he did what every other man is embarrassed to admit that he does on occasion... He told the truth...to himself. Or maybe someone else?

July woke me up again
with indifferent sunshine angling in a little earlier every day.
The season is as familiar as it has always been
Even though the eyes that take it in are lighter, clearer, softer.
I must know lots of things that I didn't previously
Because every year I remember that I was a fool just the year before.
The more time passes the more I wait
To be taller, smarter, better.
I sit in the climbing tree that grew up by my side until it finally passed me, but it was still mine though now I am too old to climb
And my body is harder to pull up the branches.
My feet and palms are like leather to the bark, but they used to be soft and gentle, white and clean.
It's easier to sit in silence and love, now that I'm a little older
Even as I hear the children laughing and crashing around next door.

It's taken me years and here's all I've got.
I understand what it means to love someone else more then myself
(though maybe it's been long enough I've forgotten)
And I know that romance comes mostly from books and TV shows that promising glitter and stuff I want to believe in...
But instead I am slowly figuring out everything for myself
And at first I was surprised by how little it all resembled the popular idea
Of what life is supposed to be like

But then I feel in love with living all over again
I like the pain and blood
as much as the laughter ad the sunny days.

I don't believe in fitting in with all the beautiful people around me,
Though that's probably because I'm scared I can't.
They look so happy and put together in their charge card wardrobes,
Their careful, expensive haircuts,
And their lovely girls with flustered cheeks and dulcet voices,
And them, with their sharp three piece suits.

I guess I'm not current; my khakis are frayed.
and I can't bring myself to laugh at their jokes, but I know what it means to cry like I might be torn in pieces
(yeah...sometimes I just couldn't help it)
And to be so happy I could fall in love with everyone I see
And to sneak out at night
Just to feel the wind in my hair and kisses on my cheeks.

I haven't learned to stop caring about someone who doesn't love me or how to walk away from a fight, or how to swallow my pride,
Or how to stop looking in the mirror - I learned that once but I forgot that too.
I've learned to love my gray areas
and the distinct good and bad ones as well
And to not care as much if the other guys and girls approve of my broad shoulders or clean cut life.

I don't know how to hold on to someone who won't stay
Or how to get rid of someone who won't go.

I have learned to look beyond my own needs and how to take care of the friend who has been loyal instead of only living for my own pleasure.

I have not learned to control my temper, or why I should try.
I have learned the benefits of self-pity, sad music, and solitude.
I know mostly that there is a lot more that I don't know.
But I haven't learned to wait for it.
I haven't learned to wait for my heart to heal,
Or for things to get better, although everyone always says they will
And they're always right.

I haven't learned to wait for the days when I'll be smarter, taller, stronger,
But, whether I learn to wait or not,
I'm doing just that.
Summer will come again, just as love
And I will be here inevitably,
Waiting.

Theo ends his own self therapy/inspection session and leaves from in front of the mirror as the scene fades to black.
