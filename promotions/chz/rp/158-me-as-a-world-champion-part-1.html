Me as a World Champion?!?!? Part 1
Written: 8/23/02

(As Serio is walking he is always talkign to himself, but not now. He is talking to his sister. They have been chatting for the past hour just walking and walking and walking and walking..... As they continue walking, they see a man selling hot dogs. Serio asks Sara if she wants one.)

'Want a hot dog?' asks Serio.

'Why not,' replies Sara.

'What do you want? CHilli Dog, Hot Dog, Corn Dog?' asks Serio.

'Um, i guess a hot dog,' she replies.

'Same, I hate those cheap chilli and corn dogs.'

As Serio waits for his order he sees people walking up to him with pens and pieces of paper. Serio is about to grab ones paper when they all go over to Sara. Serio looks puzzled at the crowd and looks at Sara shakes her shoulders as not knowing as they all ask for her autograph. She says ok and gives each of them an autograph. Seiro looks happy as he takes out his pen but they don't go to him just walk away.)

'What the....' says Serio.

'Hey, people love me,' says Sara.

'Your upper body,' says Serio as he walks away with both hot dogs.

'Hey!' What about mine?' asks Sara.

'I have it,' says Serio as he turns and give it to Sara.

'Thank you,' says Sara.

'I can't beleive I get a World Title Number 1 Contendership shot! I mean, if I win this match, I actually get to face the champion!' says Serio excited.

'Ya, but first you have to win,' says Sara taking a bite out of her hot dog.

'Ya, like I'm gonna fall aand lose another match. My record is 2 and 1. I'm planning to make my record 3 and 1. Then 4 and 1,' says Serio.

'Well, good luck there,' says Sara.

'Thank you,' says Serio.

'Well, If you want a title that bad, why didn't you beat Xander?' asks Sara.

'I dunno, it feels like he cheated,' says Serio.

'No, he didn't cheat, he won fair and sqaure,' says Sara nodding.

'Um, ok, I thought you were gonna be at ringside?' asks Serio.

'I was, I was sitting front row,' says Sara.

'I didn't see you,' says Serio.

'You should of, I was hceering for about half an hour before your match and during your matcg,' says Sara.

'Ya but I still didn't hear you,' says Serio.

'Well, you must need a hearing aid,' says Sara.

'Very funny Sara,' says Serio.

'Hey, wanna go to a resturant?' asks Sara.

'I dunno,' says Serio.

'Come on! It'll be fun!' says Sara.

'OK, but first let me talk to all my viewers,' says Serio.

'Hello my fellow people, I am Tyler Van Serio and I plan to win that match at Mind Games because I amtaht good. I have some of the best skills. I have high flying skills I sure would of liked it if it would of been a hardcore match,' says Serio taking a bite of his hot dog.

'I really want a title though before I have to drop dead,' says Serio taking another bite.

'And I plan to do it before October,' says Serio.

'And to all those wrestler's watching, I'll be ready for any of you, because I have skill!' says Serio.

'Come on Tyler!' yells Sara about half a kilometre ahead of Serio.

'How did you get so far Sara?' asks Serio.

'What?' asks Sara.

'How did you get so far ahead?!?!?!?!?' asks Serio.

'Your a slow talker!!!!' yells Sara as she continues walking.

Serio begins spinting and catches up to her in less then 30 seconds.

'Wow, your pretty fast,' says Sara.

'I have a good speed,' says Serio panting for air.

'Well, you don't have that much andurance,' says Sara laughing. Serio alughs and hits the floor.

'Man, you need a doctor,' says Sara.

'NO, I need a a blankie,' says Serio.

'What?' asks Sara.

'*Sniff* I want my bwankie...' says Serio.

'Um,' says Sara.

People begin watching Serio. Serio's eyes go bald then go back.

'WHy am I laying down?' asks Serio.

'Um, You fell,' says Sara laughing.

'Why are you laughing?' asks Serio.

'I dunno,' says Sara laughing hard.

Everyone begins laughing at Serio and Serio looks around puzzled.

'What?' asks Serio.

More laughing.

'What? Is It funny when someone hits the ground?' asks Serio.

The crowd laughs harder.

Serio get real upset and walks overto a man and punches him down. Everyong stops laughing and Serio begins.


'Ha ha ha!!! That's so funny!... WHy isn't any one laughing?' asks Serio.

'Come on Tyler,' says Sara grabbing his arm.

'What did I do?' asks Serio.

As they walk away Serio turns and looks at the crowd.

'What happened?' asks Serio.

'Nothing,' says Sara trying to hold down her laughter.

'Tell me!' says Serio.

'No no, it's to funny,' says Sara.

'Please?' says Serio.

'No!' Says Sara and Serio shuts up and they continue walking.

'So what resturant are we gonna go to?' asks Serio.

'I don't know, where do you wanna go?' asks Sara.

'I dunno, you decide,' says Serio.

'No you!'

'No you!'

'No you!'

No me!'

No me!'

'Ok,' say Sara.

'S***' says Serio.

They walk and walk in circles when Sara finally stops Serio.

'I'll pick,' says Sara.

'Ok, that sounds good,' says Serio.

Sara looks at him.

'Sorry,' says Serio laughing.

'How about Mr...' 'No,' says Serio cutting her off.

'OK, how about Li....' 'NO,' says Serio.

'OK, how about.. um..' 'No,' says Serio again.

'I didn't say anything!' says Sara.

'Let's just go back to the hoetl and order in,' says Serio.

'OK,' says Sara.

'Does Swish Chalet sound good?' asks Serio.

'That sounds good to me,' says Sara.

'Ok, you gonna pay?' asks Serio.

'Your the one with the millions of dollars,' says Sara.

'I'm saving up!' says Serio.

'For what? A City?' asks Sara.

'Yes actually, I wanna name it Tylerland, where's it's summer all the time,' says Serio.

'Why don't you buy Iceland?' asks Saara.

'I don't want a land that it's always snowing at!' says Serio.

'But it doesn't,' says Sara.

'Yes it does! Read the name, Iceland!' says Serio, 'I wanna buy Greenland,' says Serio.

'Ok,' says Sara laughing again.

'What?' asks Serio.

'Let's just talk about your #1 Contendership for the World Title,' says Sara.

'Ok, Well, I wanna make this my best macth by kicking Persona's ass all over the place,' says Serio.

'YOu will,' says Sara.

'I know, because I am T-V-S!' says Serio.

'And I'm your manager!' says Sara.

'And I'm a good wrestler!' says Serio.

'And I get more attention!' says Sara.

'Actually...' says Serio.

'What?' asks Sara.

'Well,' says Serio.

'What?' asks Sara.

'Your chest gets most of the attention,' says Serio staring.

Sara grabs her purse and nails Serio. Serio hits the ground again and...

'I want my bwankie!' says Serio.

'Here we go again,' says Sara.

Serio continues saying all his stuff as the crowds begin to come. The scene begins fading away as the crowd starts laughing as Serio begins punching when he gets up.

End
