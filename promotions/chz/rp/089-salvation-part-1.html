Salvation: Part 1
Written: 7/27/02

The room was rather captivating, not as bland as some of the others he had seen. The walls were painted a mixture of red and purple, but could barely be seen with the countless posters that hung from the walls. Posters of bands mostly, and if one could describe the room as a stereotype, one could say it looked like the room of a heavy metal fanatic. However, the woman who lived in this room was hardly a fanatic, and it could be seen by the pictures of her family and sentimental objects that also lined her room. It almost seem that she lived in two worlds, yet neither of them held the answers she needed.

Matt was sitting in a chair across from the bed, thinking to himself. He wasn't wearing his Disciple uniform this time, but rather some casual 'dress clothes' and thin glasses that were there just to add certain pigments to turn his eyes a different color than silver. His red hair was combed back and tied into a tail and he was holding a small note pad with a pen, looking like a good reporter should.

The door behind Matt creaked open and in stepped a young woman carrying a tray of cups and snacks. She was about Matt's age, and nearly Matt's height. She could have probably been a model with her figure and features, but she obviously chose a different life, as Matt noted by seeing the small piercing holes in her nose and ears. Her hair was dyed black and was braided into thin strands that hung down by the sides of her face. She smiled at Matt as she stepped in, and the light notably shimmered off her blue eyes as she did so.

'I hope you like soda and chips?' she asked in a humorous tone. Matt chuckled a little as he picked up one of the cups and took a drink. Sure enough, it was soda.

'Mmm... I wish they served this stuff at work,' Matt answered as he took another long drink. He was of course speaking of The Disciples, and in the place where he worked, you could only drink water and coffee. It amazed him sometimes how he missed the small things in life.

'So,' the young woman said as she hopped onto the edge of her bed, 'James Stiles was it?'

Matt nodded. 'I can't say I caught your name though miss.'

'Oh, sorry! My name's Christina McBorden. Just call me Chris,' she exclaimed with another wide smile. She seemed very vibrant to Matt considering what she had been experiencing as of late.

Though it was his job as a Disciple to handle all the religious aspects no one else could handle, nor hear about, he had never seen a Class 3. In basic terms, a Class 3 is someone who experiences religion a little more deeply than anyone else. They speak with saints, or suffer the pain that Christ did before his death. It was extremely rare, with only three other reported cases in known history, one of them being Joan of Arc. Christina had been suffering from a little of them all, and Matt couldn't see how she could be so vibrant under the harsh circumstances.

'Chris is is,' Matt said with a casual smile of his own. 'Chris... the questions I wanted to ask you are about your experiences as of late, which I'm sure you've already guessed. Is that alright with you?'

Chris smirked a little and nodded. 'Sure.'

Matt flipped open his notepad and began to jot down some things. 'When did they start?'

'I'd say... a few months ago,' she muttered as she shifted her eyes to the floor.

Matt jotted down what she was doing rather than her answers, as he was expected to do a character profile with each case. 'Have you been experiencing the effects often?'

Christina frowned and shrugged a little without taking her eyes off the floor. 'I guess so.'

'Did anything memorable happen the day the experiences began?'

Christina, for a moment, closed her eyes and sighed. She tapped her finger nervously on her knee for a few moments, then out of nowhere, sprung up and gave Matt a big smile.

'I'm boring eh?' she giggled. 'So Mr. Stiles, or should I say Matt Blitz? Tell me about yourself!'

Matt blinked once and sat there stunned for a moment. 'Um, what? How'd you know who I was?'

Christina giggled again and rolled her eyes. 'Let's see, the Canuck Heat Zone which is having its first show here in Toronto is advertising like there's no tomorrow! I must have seen you face, like, ten times before you came to the door.'

Matt grinned and shook his head a little. 'Well... okay, I guess my name is Matt now. Nice to meat you,' he said with a smirk. He really didn't think he would have been caught.

'Yeah, well... the glasses only really work for Clark Kent ya' know? So, what's life like as a wrestler?'

Matt hesitated for a second before answering. He wanted to continue his questions, but he could tell they really bothered her. He sighed and figured she needed a break anyways.

'It's great,' he exclaimed as he closed his notepad and put it inside his pocket. 'I travel the world, see the sights, and when I stop, I stop for the sound of thousands of people cheering for me. There's nothing on God's green earth more powerful than knowing twenty thousand of your peers in one building want to see you win and maybe even a hundred times that many all over the world do as well.'

'Must not leave too much time for a social life eh?'

Matt shrugged a little. 'Every wrestler at one time or another has had to make sacrifices. I've made mine, without regrets.'

Christina smiled and bouncer her shoulders. 'Well, I know what you mean! I'm not a wrestler or anything, but I've had my fair share of sacrifices along the way.'

Matt raised an intrigued brow and scanned her face for a second. 'Like?'

'Nuh uh,' she chimed in with a playful frown, 'it's about you now, remember?'

Matt grinned and nodded once. 'You're the boss.'

Christina giggled at hearing him say that. 'So, excited about your first match Matt?'

'Damn straight,' he explained, 'and I'll win it too. I've waited a long time to get to where I am, out of the shadow of someone I wish I didn't know. You know, the guy who takes your fame or your recognition by just being there kind of deal?'

Christina nodded.

Matt rubbed his chin for a second before continuing. 'So, I went overseas and learned a new style of wrestling in Japan. The headmaster at the school told me that every wrestler lives by his own style, and I just had to find mine. Turns out, I can create offense off the top of my head, and he taught me how to best innovate moves into painful and unexpected combinations. I'm doing things I never thought I'd never do now.'

Christina seemed captivated by his story, resting her chin on her knee as Matt spoke. 'Cool!'

Matt grinned a little. 'Well, I'm back here now. I know the competition is tough, but I don't quit. This sport is in my blood, and if I gotta' claw and scratch my way to the top by a centimeter a day, then I'll do just that. I made a life decision, and I'll live it to its fullest,' he explained as he paused and noticed he was rambling a little too much. 'Well, that's all there is to me. A wrestler who lives for just that, wrestling.'

Christina raised her head and smiled again. 'That's the coolest story I think I ever heard! I'll be cheering for you on Wednesday.'

Matt grinned. 'Thanks.'

Christina looked at the wall for a second and gave Matt a quizzical look. 'Um, not to be rude since you're actually the first wrestler I ever met, but I was wondering. If you're not a reporter, then why are you here and how do you know about all the... stuff happening to me?'

Matt's grin faded as he crossed his arms and looked to the ceiling. He didn't know if he should tell her or not, and he stared as if the answered rested above him. After a moment's thought, he made his decision.

'Well,' he began with a little bit of a smirk, 'I'm about to lay some heavy stuff on you about who I am. You'll know eventually because I'll be handling your case, so just brace yourself, okay?'

Christina raised her eyebrows and blinked. 'My case?'

Matt ignored the question. 'I'm part of an elite religious organization known as The Disciples. We handle things like what you're experiencing, and things even worse than that. That's why I'm here.'

Christina burst out laughing, hard enough to fall over backwards and have trouble breathing. Matt's expression didn't change, but instead, he just crossed his arms and waited a few seconds.

'I know about the picture on your back,' he bluntly muttered. Instantly, Christina stopped laughing and sat up, looking at Matt in shock.

'How do you know that?' she demanded in a commanding voice. 'I couldn't have told more than one person about that, and he's a doctor!'

Matt shook his head. 'My group knows a lot. They figure these things out by their own means, but we're not here to do anything to you Chris, we just want to help.'

Christina shot to her feet and pointed her finger towards the door, fully glaring at Matt now. 'I want you out of my house! Go!' she shouted.

Matt slowly stood and removed his glasses, revealing his silver eyes. He placed his hands on Christina's shoulders and guided her back down to her bed. He didn't take his gaze away from her eyes as she slowly began to calm down.

'I don't mean you any harm,' he gently explained, 'but if we don't get to the bottom of this... it could kill you.'

Christina blinked a couple of times and gave it some thought. She lowered her head and sighed, shaking it a little.

'Alright,' she mumbled.

'Can I see the picture?'

Christina kept her head down and turned around. Matt pulled up the back of her T-shirt to her shoulders and held it there, examining the picture that had been engraved into her back. He knew that it would have hurt, a lot, and it may have hurt now. He took a mental note of some of the features of the picture, such as the angels with their shimmering swords coming down from Heaven, battling the demons below back down to Hell. It was a classic depiction of the oldest war in known history, and this one was finely detailed, nothing that a human hand could do given that it was literally carved into her back.

Matt gently ran his fingers over the picture, feeling the detail of even the bumps and grooves. The texture of everything seemed too real, and from what X had told him earlier, it was created inch by inch until completed.

Matt heard sniffling coming from Christina as he examined the painting, and he stopped. She was sobbing to herself with her head buried in her arms which rested on her knees. Matt lowered her shirt and only heard four words mumbled from underneath the tears.

'Please... make it stop.'
