Overdue I
Written: July 3-4, 2002

* Riiiiiiiiing! *

………

As we hear the ringing of a telephone we see that we are in the living room of none other than Canuck Heat Zone superstar, Luke Black!

His living room has lush blue carpeting and an array of priceless porcelain animals. Ranging from elephants to whales to crows. A beautiful fireplace is right below the porcelain animals and to the right is an 88-inch TV. Mainly for when Luke watches the tape of Wild.

………

* Riiiiiiiiing! *

………

The phone rings again as we recognize a black leather couch across from the big screen TV. A fan hangs from the ceiling in which the lights light up the room.

………

* Riiiiiiiiing! *

………

We search from where the ringing of the phone is coming from. We soon find the phone sitting on an end table by the couch. We focus in on the phone witch has an answering machine next to it. It seems that Luke Black has finally decided to get one, seeing as how his servants are too lazy to answer the phone.

………

* Riiiiiiiiing! *

………

The answering machine picks-up and Luke Black’s voice chimes in.

“’Tis I! Luke Black! I hath failed to hearken unto ye’ chimes as I hath gone off! Ye’ surly knows what unto speakth by thy end. If ye’ hath failed to recall, thou is a fool!”


* Beeeeeeep! *

“Uh…yeah….” Chimes in an unfamiliar slow voice, “This is Bob from Bob’s XXX Porno Rental Store, no relation to the owner. You have an overdue porno from our store. Uh…’Real’ Cock Fights? Oh no. My mistake; its Buffy: The Vampire Layer. That’s layer with an ‘L’ not an ‘S.’ It’s been overdue for about three weeks now. So if your hands are too ‘sticky’ you’ll have to return it by Saturday, before midnight, or we’ll have to charge you as if you bought it and then you’ll still have to return it. So it would be in your best interest to return the tape as soon as possible. If you have any questions please call us at 555-BobX. The store hours are four p.m. to six a.m.. Thank you.”

* Click *

We cut to the outside where a car is pulling up. It stops in front of the driveway. Don Rossi, Luke Black and in the drivers’ seat, Rage. Luke exits the car and we hear Rage mumble something about Luke and Don making him be the designated driver. Don and Rage speed off as Luke stumbles his way up the driveway with an empty bottle of whisky in his right hand. He makes it to the font door and opens it, stumbling his way inside. He bypasses the answering machine and pass out face down on his leather couch. He drops the bottle of whisky and it rolls around to the phone.

* Riiiiiiiiing! *

………

* Riiiiiiiiing! *

………

Luke rolls over onto his side as the phone is bothering him.

………

* Riiiiiiiiing! *

………

* Riiiiiiiiing! *

………

The answering machine picks-up and Luke Black’s voice chimes in.

“’Tis I! Luke Black! I hath failed to hearken unto ye’ chimes as I hath gone off! Ye’ surly knows what unto speakth by thy end. If ye’ hath failed to recall, thou is a fool!”


* Beeeeeeep! *

“Uh…yeah….” Says the now familiar slow voice of Bob from Bob’s XXX Porno Rental Store, “I know I just called you like, two minutes ago but I was hopping that maybe you’d be in by now. As you know you have an overdue porn from our store. Buffy: The Vampire Layer. That’s layer with an ‘L’ and not an ‘S.’ I always have to say that because people get it confused very easy. You’ll have to return that tape as soon as possible. Preferably some time after midnight on Saturday so we can make more money, but for your sake, before midnight on Saturday. If you have any questions please call 555-BobX, and the store hours are four p.m. to 6 a.m.. Thank you.”

* Click *

One of Luke’s servants enters the living room and finds Luke lying on the couch, passed out. He leaves and soon comes back with a blanket and covers Luke with it. He leaves and turns out the light. The only light left is the light of the blinking of the answering machine.

* Riiiiiiiiing! *

………

* Riiiiiiiiing! *

………

* Riiiiiiiiing! *

………

* Riiiiiiiiing! *

………


The answering machine picks-up and Luke Black’s voice chimes in.

“’Tis I! Luke Black! I hath failed to hearken unto ye’ chimes as I hath gone off! Ye’ surly knows what unto speakth by thy end. If ye’ hath failed to recall, thou is a fool!”


* Beeeeeeep! *

“Uh…yeah….” Says the now annoying slow voice of Bob from Bob’s XXX Porno Rental Store, “I know I’ve already called you twice before but it’s my job to call everybody with overdue videos just before the end of my shift. As you already know you have Buffy: The Vampire Layer overdue. That’s layer with an ‘L’ not an ‘S.’ So ‘Mr. Sticky Hands’ you need to turn that video in as soon as possible. Our number is 555-BobX. The store hours are 4 p.m. to 6 a.m.. Thank you.”

* Click *
