Image...Just As Important As It Sounds
Written: 7-25-2002

The press said he couldn't do it.....

How could some 'Manson wannabe' sell-out Proctor Warehouse on the east side of Los Angeles?

Proctor Warehouse was a place of broken dreams and bodies....it's original purpose for erection not even remembered by the general public...it wasn't until it was abandoned that it really started to gain it's reputation...

The rape of a 17 year old girl from Pacific Palisades out on her FIRST date...and the rape came AFTER her date, Bobby Nichols, was murdered in front of her before her vrignity was ripped from her like a newborn being taken from the womb....

Jason Jenkins, 11 years old....killed in the middle of the crossfire between a Crypt and Blood gang war...riddled by seven bullets...

The stories could go on and on, and they did, and the press CONTINUED to adamantly say that a performance there would never draw, even if Ozzy Osbourne himself opened the show with a reunion of Van Halen for the finale...

All of this was posed to him as the camera flashes bombarded a seated Xander Crave, sitting lacksidasically in the metal folding chair laid out for him behind a long white marble table also seating Xander's uptight record executives, simply embarassed in expression at how the media was eating Xander's concert idea alive at this press conference. Xander however, was brandishing an incredibly bored expression on his face for having to attend what he likes to call 'the media circus'. Finding more interest in wrapping the silver strand of his hair around his finger, he just stared into the space above him like a little child waiting to for his parent to say,'Okay you can play now.'

Seated to his left was Xander's closest confidant....and even that word for him should be used with care, James Dolby. Dolby cleared his throat, fixing the fit of his thin silver framed glasses on his face as he neared his mouth to the mini-mic stand set up for him on the table, his answer for the media hounds coming of in that usual 'prepared statement' style that only made Xander snicker everytime he would hear his weasely manager use it...feeling slightly sorry that he was a slave to the corporation that paid him.

'As stated in his contract, Xander has power to pick half of his tour location and he was quite serious about booking Proctor Warehouse before he left for Canada, where he would be performing on the 'Wasted Youth' tour in conjunction with Canadian Heat Zone wrestling. His intentions for WHY he booked the warehosue are beyond me, but we intend to stand behind him,' stated Dolby in his most professional demeanor as he locked his hands atop the table, internally holding in the urge to just lash out at the press and act on some homicidal tendencies that would make Jeffrey Dahmer cringe. Quite shortly after making this statement, one fo the reporters in the group stood tall in firm, the contorted look of disgust on his elderly face making it painfully obvious what he had to say was far from encouraging....

He fixed his brown suit jacket and spoke with a stern tone, suprisingly loud for his age as he addressed the table, Xander's eyes focusing on him strangely as he took a special interest in this reporters words,'This entire record company and that....'fake' up there is a disgrace to the music buisness...and just a pathetic role model to the youth of America and the world. The fact they woudl even buy into you is just...God-awful and this whole warehouse performance is innane, from an editorial stand point, I hope it falls flat on it's face...'

Those words sparked a fire that usually remains unlit within Xander....a fire that he would only feel when in the heat of action or during a performance...what that man said caused the Youth Rock God to sit upright, his baggy, no name brand cargo pants scrunhing against the metal seat of his folding chair as he slid the mic over his way fromin front of Dolby. Under his breath, Dolby let out a sigh and buring his head in his hands as he said,'Oh great...he's just ACHING for another 'Xander Is Satan' spread in People...it took me months to blame all those 'Fu** you's' into a temporary bout of tuortettes syndrome.'

The other record executives silently motioned to Dolby to stop Xander before he could do his speciality...say things he wouldn't regret, but Dolby was too busy sulking about the PR nightmare that was inevitable to stop Xander from replying to one of his mortal enemies....the blind media...a group of hounds who would murder respect for other human beings....and for what?

A headline on the Sunday news...

This made them inhuman in his eyes and Xander was quick to reply to the army of reporters ready to crucify him for his performance, the elegant sterness of his voice filling the room via the mic as he made his point heard...

'How dare you sit here and judge me for my acts....can you tell em the last GOOD thing you leeches have done for the world? Do you realize you MURDERED Princess Diana...a pure soul....and for what, your dumb questions?'

Xander's anger grew with each word he spoke, the press speaking quietly amongst themselves in shock of the emotion being conveyed by Xander, everyone taking notice and opening ear to something he never seems to do....speak on his feelings. He continued, not to be stopped,'F**k you for even daring to stand here and label me as Satan. Is that what you 'normal' people are like....you automatically crucify what you don't understand? Maybe because you'll NEVER have the capacity of being apart of something so beautiful? My freaks listen to me because I convey the truth...a message through anger. What, maybe because I'm not some clean cut teeny bopper selling soda, I'm not fit enough to be idolized? That's all you monsters have done...made your image of what YOU want. I am not what you want...never tried to be....and as long as I'm not that 'image'...I will be happy. Did you even look into WHY I booked this show?'

Everyone within the press conference grew quiet, Xander now getting comfortable as he laid back in the chair with mic in hand, looking around the room for an answer that would never come. He then shed light on the concert's purpose,'I had personal talks with the families of those who lost ones they loved at that warehouse...all the money will be split amongst them so they can lead more comfortable lives. See the difference between me and 'Mr.Image' is that I don't need to bring my good out in the public to sell records or for your approval....my records sell just fine and your approval is worth jacks**t to me anyways. Now if you'll excuse me, I have a sound check to attend...if any of you have a problem with my efforts....just find a private little corner....and f**k yourself liberally...I'll be too busy singing or wrestling to give a damn....love heals and kills...adieu..'

Xander drops the mic as if it were of no value to him, the feedback from the plummet not even phasing the crowd...in awe of his words, some even touched within by them. As he raised up from his seat, exiting left and leaving the suited executives he despised with a passion to finish the conference, Dolby simply wiped the sweat from his brow, picked the mic up off the floor, and cleared his throat before reverting back to his usual wise-ass demeanor and saying,'So....anyone interested in reserve tickets?'

'Never judge a man for murder if you were the one who handed him the knife.'
