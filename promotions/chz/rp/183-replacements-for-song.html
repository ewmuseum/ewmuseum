Replacements For Song?
Written: 04/09/02

==Begin Transmission==

The time is near, one day away from the big debut match. A debut match is often taken lightly. Many say that a debut match is just settling in. A match that will not damper much on a career. But in the Hollywood Assassins tag team eyes, they have no passion who think that way, they are hear on a mission. A mission that deserves credit for it’s being. They will take this match to their highest, their debut match in Canuck Heat Zone happens to be a big one, the Canuck Heat Zone president found some skill in the two young stars, and have placed them into a tag team title match against Easton High. A match that will decide who will be stars, a chance for a young tag team to get their name out into the spotlight. So both teams should be ready for battle.

Hollywood Assassin are ready to take over Redemption Songs roll as the most tag teams would be getting plenty of rest, and working out of constant pace, but the Hollywood Assassins are only doing half of what is suspected of them. They are working out at a combined eight hours a day, some may say this is extreme, but they have decided to do whatever it takes to advance in the Canuck Heat Zone and become champions. Canadian Assassins are ready over Redemption Songs spot at the top of the tag team pairs. The two spend every waking hour on preparing for the match, whether it be working out or studying delayed tapes of champions, and what they had to do to get the win. The two are not familiar with tag teams, mostly have experienced as individual matches, but now they are ready to combine their skills and be the number one tag team in the Canuck Heat Zone.

The day before Wild is usual hectic around the arena where the Canuck Heat Zone events are held. Always packed with reporters looking for final comments on their matches, agents making appearances discussing their clients thoughts to the press. The wrestlers are usually in the lobby taking interviews or working out in the stadium weight room. On this particular Tuesday morning, the two have avoided contact with the press thus far, as they have been set to the weight room for nearly three hours. Local Canuck Heat Zone reporter Alison Malone has been on the site for at least two hours outside Hollywood Assassins change rooms, hoping to catch a story or two.

Alison Malone has been passing time, by aligning her make-up, and preparing the questions she will provide too the young stars. Alison has had the chance on viewing two wrestlers bio’s and pictures, so she has herself to look her best for the two good looking stars. Alison beginning to look very impatient with the time she has wasted waiting for the two, but also impressed on their will to be tip-top for their match tomorrow. So after around 3 ½ hours of waiting, finally the two had exited their change rooms dressed in their blue dress shirts and black dress pants, with their hair slicked and sunglasses on top of their head.

Alison Malone had stopped the two, and asked in a kind matter for an interview, looking a little nervous herself when she had contact with the stars. Robbie and Chris gladly accepted mostly because of the beauty of Alison in the blinking lobby light. The lobby was becoming more and more empty, so they waited for about five minutes, and began without anyone else in the lobby.

==Begin Interview ==

“This is Alison Malone her live with young superstars Robbie McMasters and Chris Starr, the two had made some comments on their strategy to win their match, but now we are going to get their last minute thoughts on the match.. guys?”

The two then have steady standing position and focusing on the camera as Robbie takes the spotlight.

“ Well our strategy is to be physically and mentally ready for Easton High. This is the match that will prove to the fans they mean business. I am sure the fans will be disappointed with the leaving of Redemption Song, but we plan to take their role as the top tag team, and that is a big role, which we feel we are ready to receive. We are honored to be in the federation of this amount of talent. I just heard plenty more wrestlers have just been assigned from NTICW due to expanding, and now is ready to give it’s full potential, and attract new fans with the coming attractions on the roster. It should be a packed night on Wild, and we are ready for them and Easton High, you can count on it!”

Alison and Robbie never leaving eye contact through all of his interview, as right from the start they have locked eyes. They seem to have quite a connection. Chris Star noticing this constant eye contact and interrupts, and begins speaking.

“ Well sorry to interrupt you too.”

The two lose contact with each other instantly and begin focusing on Chris as he begins expressing his views. Which is very similar to Robbie’s.

“ Well I express the same views as my partner Robbie. We are more then ready to take Easton High out, we are mentally and physically fit, and we plan to Assassinate the two! We have tossed through federations like a bag of chips, and now we are ready to step up! We have always put on a good show, and a good portion of the time we had both won, Robbie more then me, but both equally good! We plan to Assassinate them, proving to the Canuck Heat Zone fans we are ready to be the champions!

Alison still fixated on Robbie again, but she decides to wrap up the interview, as she has lost all interest in interviewing at the moment. So she closes by saying.

“ Well there it is folks, the final thoughts before Wild from the new superstars from the Canuck Heat Zone, The Hollywood Assassins!”

==End Interview ==

The camera then fades to black, and Chris gives a quick hand shake to Alison and begins walking away, yelling to Robbie to hurry up. Robby meeting eye contact with Alison once again, but they wrap up with a small hug, and as Robbie turns his head Alison drops her phone number in his dress pant pocket. When Robbie is walking away, seeming a little down that nothing happened between the two, but just the he looks into his pocket and finds the number, and as he looks back, she was gone. But yet he had a smile on his face!

Now there is only one step left for the Hollywood Assassins, too prove their talent they talk about so much. I am sure the fans will be behind them, and they plan to Assassinate!

==End Transmission==
