CHZ — Roleplay

Nightmares RP 1 of 2

16 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys... This is with my Chris Night character... COuld someone plz gimme some feedback. Narrator: Always, something will come back to haunt you when your doing well. Something will tell you that is was your fault when it really wasn't. But you will believe it, you will clasp onto it, not letting it go. People will try to tell us, "It's not your fault, it isn't!" But you won't listen, and we will let it haunt you for who knows how long. No matter what you do or what you try, you will never be able.... To stop.... The Nightmare. The scene opens up with Chris and Amanda laying down asleep. Amanda is sleeping very comfortable, but Chris struggles around the bed. He tosses and turns, and his face begins to have a scared look on his face as we look into his nightmares. Chris sees his parents both binded in rope. He tries to help but all he can do is watch. Pain streams through every muscle in Chris' body, as he sees BTK torture his parents. Chris shakes his head, yelling out, "NO! NO! NO!" Tears stream down his cheeks as his parents shreaks ring through his ears. Chris does all he can do to get to his parents, but he can't get through. It's as if there is an invisible wall between him and his parents and he is unable to make contact to save them. He just watches in terror... Watches BTK hurt and torture his parents... BTK then grabs a knife, raises it up.... And..... Suddenly Chris pops up in his bed. Sweat is covering his body head to toe. His long hair is soaking wet, and he is breathing very heavily. Amanda lies beside him sound asleep. Chris wipes some of the dripping sweat off of his face, when suddenly, a blast comes through his bed room door, and BTK with his torture toys is standing there with the bodies of his parents. Chris begins to shake his head, yelling out, "NO! NO! NO!" As BTK begins to walk closer and closer. BTK then tells him, "It's your fault you parents are dead," and once BTK begins to strike... Chris suddenly sits up awake in his bed, and just like in his dream, his whole body is covered in sweat, and his hair soaked in it. And his breathing is very heavy. Chris now begins to think to himself. Chris: Am I really up this time? Or am I just dreaming again? Well, let's find out. Chris pinches his arm and realizes that he really is awake. Chris begins to shake his head as he stands up and walks to the bathroom, but before making it stumbles into the laundry basket lying on the floor. Chris turns the bathroom light on and sees his face in the mirror. It's soaking wet and his eyes are blood shot. His chest keeps moving up and down at a fast pace because of his unnormal breathing. Chris walks over to the sink and turns the handle for the cold water. Chris puts his hands down lets the water fall into them. Chris splashes the water into his face and begins to wash his face trying to wake him self up. Chris then splashes a lot of the water into his hair and he then puts it into a pony tail. Chris then grabs his big black baggy pants which are on the bathroom floor and puts them on. Chris then puts a white tank top on. Chris now thinks to himself some more. Chris: Man, I need to get out of this house for a minute and talk a walk. I'm feeling too fucked up to just sit around. Chris turns off the bathroom light and tries to figure out his way around the room, but still bumps into many things, yet Amanda is still sound asleep. Chris finally makes it to his dresser and he takes out a pair of socks. Chris then sits on his side of the bed on slips the socks on. Chris then grabs his Famous Stars and Straps DC shoes and puts them on. Chris now stands up and walks over to Amanda's side of the bed. As he whispers, "I love you" under his breath and he gives her a kiss on the lips. Chris then walks over to his closet and pulls out a black pull over jacket that says, "Ride The Lightning" on it, and says Metallica at the top of the pullover. Chris pulls the hood over his head as he walks out of the room slowly. Chris then walks towards the front door and he opens it. Chris then walks out of the house and shuts the door and locks it very quietly. Chris then begins to walk down the street as thoughts stream all through his mind. Chris: Why? Why does these things always happen to me. I'm suppose to be Chris Night, The Nightmare, but yet, I have these nightmares coming and haunting me. It's funny how things go in this world, how shit always fucks with us. I just don't understand it though, why am I suddenly having these dreams now. Is it really my fault that my parents are dead? Maybe if I never would've left, they wouldn't of been murdered, and they never would've moved from the old house. They never would've left that house to forget about me. I should of never ran away. Damn it! Chris has been walking for quite awhile now as he suddenly comes upon a stray dog with a collar on it. Chris takes a step back making sure the dog doesn't attack him, but the dog just stares at Chris, as Chris stares back into the dogs eyes. Chris sees himself in the German Sheperd which he is staring at. He sees a runaway dog, tired of all the bullshit that he has to go through at home, and tired of being locked up in a little room by yourself. Just wanting to be free and being able to run around as a stray is what anyone would want. Freedom, which is what the dog finally has. Some say that freedom is free, but it always comes with a cost. The dog's cost? No food, no shelter, no water. The dog has to scavenge for things. He has to prowl on things like a vulture and strike when the time is right before anyone else does. Which is why he and Chris are just alike. For Chris' freedom, he had to give up food, shelter, and water as well. Just like the dog had to watch for the dog catcher, Chris had to watch for the cops so he didn't get stuck in a foster home just like dogs get put in the pound. Just like Chris, the dog has to work hard for what it lives on, it has to scavage for it. The big dogs thing it can pick on the small young dog, but the young dog stays resilient and stays strong. The young dog has some rocky times and gets beat up here and there, but still stays resilient, and finally whips a dog twice it's size. Which is just like Chris Night.. At the start of Night's career in CHZ, everyone just called him a cocky punk kid, and they picked on him and used him as a punching bag, but Chris finally used all of his anger and fury, and finally lashed back by winning the Alpha Title in a hard fought battle against someone twice his size. Chris then beat the strong and resilient Vincent Liger, who put up a huge fight. Chris still plans to stay strong and be the resilient young kid that he is. He always holds his ground, just like now with the dog who is just like him. Chris holds his ground and stands there watching the dog, and finally the dog walks away with another dog that it was waiting for. Chris shakes his head as he continues his walking down the street towards nowhere. Chris finally makes it to the park and walks towards the basketball court and a ball is just lying there, left by someone, on the court. Chris picks it up and begins to shoot the ball as he begins to speak. Chris: The day is coming for me to defend my Alpha Title, and the moment of truth is coming. I face off against Chris Johnson, XWF Legend and and Hall of Famer... But that doesn't phase me. Does Chris Johnson really think that I care that he was the greatest Genesis Champion ever? Does he really? Does he think I care that he was US Champ also? Hell Chris, Robb Hardy never beat you, but he did more then you will ever have done in the XWF. He is about to be US Champion, he was XXX Champ, Genesis Champ, two time Tag Champ, and yet, your still better somehow? No Chris, no. Listen here man, don't compare me to Robb, were both different in ways. You see, Robb was never this successful at the age I am at now. Chris Johnson, I am the youngest Alpha Champion ever in CHZ. CHZ is the greatest federation around, and I am the youngest person to hold the second best title. And you see Chris, that challenge to Chris Helms, it isn't going to happen, because you won't he holding the Alpha Title after Sins of Summer V, and he won't be holding the US title either. Chris dribbles around some with the basketball and then takes a three point shot... Nothing but net. Chris: CJ, I do take advice here and there from Robb, and I respect him a lot, but I want you to know, I will never live in Robb Hardy's shadow or anyone else's. CJ, even though you think I am just some punk kid and you disrespect the hell out of me, I've got tons of respect for you. I've watched your career, and believe me Chris, I've always been impressed. It's going to be a joy for me to be in the same ring with you CJ. But you see, you say that your one of the people with the least challenging match, and there, I think your wrong. Just two weeks ago, you beat Chris Helms and I beat Hawke for the Alpha title... Now, if I didn't beat Hawke, things would probably be a little different this week. But the fact is, if you can beat Chris Helms, so can I buddy. I beat the seven foot tall, three hundred pounder Hawke. I beat a monster for the Alpha Championship CJ. I am 6'1 and 224 pounds... I'm a lightweight, and I beat a heavyweight monster. David always wins against Goliath. Chris dribbles the ball a little bit more and takes a corner three point shot and makes it in nothing but net once again. Chris: CJ, you don't understand that I strive for success. I strive to do good in every little thing I do. I love having challenges against people like you, because you see, people think I won't win, and the only reason I have this belt is because of a fluke. No CJ, it isn't because of a fluke, I won it on skill. Chris spits on the ground and takes another shot with the basketball. Once again, the ball goes through the net nothing but net and no rim. Chris now begins to speak some more again. Chris: CJ, your a funny guy, you thoughtlessly say that I am just a highflyer and your going to just wait until I make a mistake and then pounce on me like a lion and win. Well that won't happy Ceej, because there won't be any mistakes, my rhythm will be unstoppable and I won't miss a shot. By the way CJ, I am a technical wrestler as well, so when I want to, I will lock in the Goodnight Lock, and you will cry for mercy and tap out. Or I'll do to you what I did to Liger, and nail the Lights Out. But Chris, none of that will happen, because I will use the biggest move in my arsenal, and that's The Nightmare. Fear it forever Chris, because once I hit it, you'll be done with for the 1.......2.......3! Chris takes another shot with the basketball and hits it nothing but net once again. Chris: Perfection is what I strive for Chris, and at Sins of Summer V, it will be the perfect match, and I will get the perfect win. Chris takes one more shot with the basketball and another shot sinks in with perfection. Perfect form and perfect stroke. Chris then walks away as he throws the basketball into the park grass. Chris keeps walking as he just thinks about his parents and thinks about the past with them, and Chris thinks about all the stuff he missed with them and his sister Jade. Chris can still make things up with Jade, but he can't make anything up with his parents. They are gone, and yet, Chris still can't move on. Chris needed his freedom, no matter what the cost. Chris got his freedom, but at the cost of his parents death? Is that what the penalty was? Is that what the cost was? Is that the reason they died? Is it because of Chris they moved and ended up being killed by BTK in their new house? All of these thoughts run through Chris' mind as he continues to walk down the street. The night air blows by Chris with a cool breeze. The night sky is clear and beautiful with millions of stars showing and a full moon out as Chris walks towards who knows where. Chris then stops by a house and walks up to the door. He knocks on it and waits a bit for a response. The door opens up and a guy with his brown hair a mess, is standing at the door in a white tank and his boxers. Man at Door: Meh... What do you want Chris? Chris: Yo man, can you hook it up with a 40 ouncer. Man at Door: Sure Chris. The man walks away for a bit then comes back with a 40 ounce beer and hands it to Chris. Chris: Thanks Doug. Doug: No problem Chris, now go home and leave me be, I'm tired as fuck. Chris: Alright, peace out bro! Chris then walks off into the night with his beer and begins to take the way back he took to where he is. So many things are happening for Chris this week, the biggest event in wrestling and in his life, Sins of Summer V is happening, and Chris Night will be defending his Alpha Championship. In a couple of days he will be getting married. Horrible dreams are haunting him every night. So many great things happening, but the bad dreams keep coming. Almost as if the Nightmare giver is now recieving nightmares. The only thing Chris can do is try to wash the pain away by drinking. Chris drinks on the way back to where he came to wash away the pain and misery. Chris didn't know where he was going tonight until he decided he needed a beer. He walks to think about these major things. This week, the biggest week in his life, is being ruined by his parents death haunting him. People can tell him it isn't his fault, but he'll only pretend to listen, and keep it in the back of his mind that everything is his fault. But you can't blame him, were all human and we all do the same thing. We find ways to blame ourselves for something and we don't let it go. We let it eat away at us and kill us. No one can help us get it away either, we have to do it ourselves... But that's if we want to do it. That's if we want to take the blame off of our shoulders, because it never belonged there in the first place. Some people try to find people to blame for things like this, but Chris Night is a modest guy, and he sticks the blame on himself, he doesn't even blame BTK as much as he blames himself. Pain seeps through Chris Night's entire body because of this whole incident. Finally Chris makes it home and it's about 5 in the morning and a little light is coming outside with the sun beginning to rise up. Chris finishes off the beer and sets it down on the porch as he walks into the house. Amanda is sitting there on the couch, waiting for him. Chris looks into her eyes and he sees compassion and a worried mother's face on her. Amanda's hair is a mess and she is sitting on the couch in her bra and panties. Chris: Hey babe. Amanda: Chris, where have you been this whole time? Chris: Don't worry about it, I just took a little walk. Amanda: Chris, you had me worried sick. Chris: Babe, you know I can take care of myself. Amanda: But when I wake up in the middle of the night at 3 AM and I don't see you, I am suppose to worry Chris. Chris: Babe, I just needed to talk a walk, I'm having some problems. Amanda: Your having the dreams again aren't you? Chris: Yeah, with a little extra part that has got me to think a lot. Amanda has a worried look on her face as she talks. Amanda: Chris. what is it that made you think? Chris looks down with a sad look and begins to speak. Chris: Well, that it's my fault. Amanda gets a shocked look on her face as she begins to speak now. Amanda: Chris, honey, it is not your fault. Chris: Maybe it is, maybe it isn't, I must search about things to find out the truth. Amanda: Don't let things like this get to you Chris, this is our big week. Chris: I know babe, I know. No worries, I have everything under control. In the back of Chris' mind, he thinks, "Is something going to happen to Amanda too? Do I need to leave her so nothing happens to her? It's like I am bad luck or something, and I love Amanda so much.... I must make a decision by the wedding this week, I must." Amanda: Chris? Are you there? Chris: Heh, yeah... Just thinking about something. Amanda: Well please come to bed, you need to get some rest. Chris: No, I can't go to sleep Amanda. I don't want another dream to come now. It just keeps getting worse and worse. Amanda: Fine, but don't leave, I am going to go get some sleep, okay honey? Chris: Alright, I love you. Amanda: I love you too. Amanda and Chris kiss each other and Amanda goes back to the room to sleep as Chris grabs another beer out of the fridge and sits down on the couch and begins to drink away the pain. Chris: Well, it's decision time. I must figure out if I might hurt Amanda in some way, and the least I can do is leave her. So that's what I must thing on, and the wedding, and Sins of Summer V. Damn, this week is going to be hell. With that said, Chris begins to pound the beer he pulled out as the scene begins to fade away with Amanda sleeping in the room and Chris drinking the pain away in the living room. Scene Fades
