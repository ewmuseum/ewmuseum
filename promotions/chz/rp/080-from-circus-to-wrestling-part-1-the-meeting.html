From Circus To Wrestling (Part 1): The Meeting
Written: 24th July 2002

“Igor?” exclaims the cold, Russian voice of its owner. The black hair of is slicked back, Constantino Zergolov is about six foot tall, and has a very muscular build. Typical of most Russian athletes. He is standing wearing, a pair of white tights, and a red vest top. He reaches down and puts chalk on his hands.

Igor, with his brown hair, looks up. Arefiev is the younger of the two men. He has a narrower frame, but still is well built. He is wearing blue tights, but no top. His hair spirals around his chest. “Yes?” comes the reply.

The two men are standing in a locker room, style trailer.

“Does the circus not bore you sometimes?” asks the older, “wiser” Russian. He seems very well trained in English.

Igor shrugs. He holds no emotion on his face, as he ties at his laces.

“What does that shrug mean?” asks Constantino.

Again, the young Russian shrugs. “I am happy with my job,” snaps Igor, as his shoulders descend from the emotionless shrug.

“Just happy?” questions Constantino. “Would it not be better to be in love with your job?”

Igor again, shrugs. “You have a point,” admits Igor obviously bearing a grudge. Igor paces around the room with the chalk cloud following his palms.

“And don’t you want more money?” asks Constantino. In his head, he has a plan of where this conversation is going. Igor is bound to have noticed it. But where is he going?

Igor looks up form his right lace, straight into the beaming eyes of Constantino.

“You are a world class performer. We can earn so much more!”

Constantino stares into the yes of Igor. He is obviously making ground as Igor is now listening.

“What do you have in mind?” asks Igor. The young member of the Arefiev tradition seems interested as he peers towards Constantino.

Zergolov smiles and turns to his bag.

Arefiev peers onwards the sports bag, as Constantino removes a piece of paper. Igor is handed the page and looks at it. He is not aware of its contents.

“What is it?” asks Igor. He seems lost as he looks at Constantino. Igor is staring at a picture of one of OSW’s latest signee Griff Truxell. “Who is that?”

Igor looks towards the smiling face of Constantino. “That, my friend is one of the world’s richest paid athletes... Griff Truxell.”

Igor nods. “What does he do?”

Constantino smiles. “He is a professional wrestler...”

The smile on Igor’s face immediately drops.

“He works for a company in America called Ol....” Constantino fades off as he looks at Igor’s disgusted expression. “What’s wrong?”

Igor drops the page and turns from Constantino. “You expect me to give up my art-form for a fake industry, that doesn’t deserve to be called a sport!”

Igor stands looking out the window. The young Russian is disgusted.

“Igor... Igor... I am not asking you to give the art-form we both have lived our lives to show to the world!”

Igor seems confused and begins to turn round.

“What do you mean?”

“I am merely...” Constantino lifts Truxell’s picture off the floor. “I am merely asking you to help me make some more cash! Plus, spread our names!”

Igor slowly turns back to Constantino.

“The world of wrestling is a big money industry, and we, Russians, can show off our trapeze skills and agility in the ring, not only giving use big paycheques, but increasing the fans to the circus shows and helping out our heritage and also future.”

Constantino, now has the young “Russian Star” wrapped around his little finger.

“You see, Igor, if we put our minds to it. We can be the most entertaining tag team in the world. Each night, we will give the perfect performance of agility, speed and power. We will the biggest thing to hit the Wrestling Industry, and all for the good of the circus performers, we represent.”

Zergolov is painting the ideal picture for Igor in his head.

“Together, we can save the circus industry and make a little extra money as well.”

Constantino smiles, as Igor smiles back. Arefiev nods to the older Russian. The door of the trailer is then hit a number of times.

“IGOR! ZERGOLOV! YOU TWO ARE UP!”

The two men, chalk up one more time, as they both exit the trailer. Constantino stops Igor on the top step.

“Think about it!” exclaims Zergolov as the two men head out of the trailer.
