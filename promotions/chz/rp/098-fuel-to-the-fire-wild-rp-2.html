Fuel to the Fire (Wild RP #2)
Written: 07/29/2002


*A face appears on your screen. Two eyes, a nose and a mouth that surrounded by a brown goatee. At first you are startled, but as the camera slowly pans back you start to identify whose facial features they are. You notice two long and thick dark brown sideboards that hang off the face and you instantly recognize the individual as. Matt Hunt…and he has a grin for his expression. But this isn’t a cocky grin or an arrogant grin; it’s a happy grin. But as the seconds tick away, the smile slowly disappears and a scowl materializes as Hunt begins to talk*

“Are you finished, Schultz? Have you finished stuffing your face full of that fast-food crap? I had to watch and listen to your little speech about three or times, because I couldn’t quite understand what the hell you were going on about with all that spluttering and spitting. But I eventually got it. Did Mother not tell you that you shouldn’t talk while you’re eating? Mmm, I suppose not – you seem like a commoner and common folk can’t even talk properly, let alone eat in the polite manner. Anyway, I not a some British Blueblood from Blackpool and this isn’t a lesson in table manners. I gather that I confused you, Jack. Well, to be honest – I didn’t think that would be a hard task. But did I intentionally set out to contradict myself? Did I purposely want to confuse you with a sentence? Heh, I’ll let you be the judge of that, but in the meantime – don’t hurt your head.”

*Hunt pauses and glances off camera for a split second before continuing*

“I want you to impress the missus and bench press her and the bed – mmm, very confusing eh?. Basically, Jacques – I wanted you to feel good about yourself before I am forced to disappoint you next Wednesday and defeat you, etc, etc. So in order for you to do that, I simply advised you to impress the missus…you know, the wife, ‘er indoors – then you’d be full of yourself. Hell, you’ve just impressed the woman you love – so you’d be up on cloud nine. However, I said that without knowing that you are a potential fudge packer. The way you and that camache Chris discussed each others eating ‘skills’ and looked at each other with that little twinkle in the eye, not to mention how you described - whom I gathered was your previous ‘lover’ – as ‘psycho bitch’. Mmm, resentment towards the opposite sex, confiding to another ‘heterosexual’ male – do you see where I’m going here, John? I really didn’t want to bring this up, but hey – what can you do…”

*Hunt stops and starts to smile*

“So, with your fellow strudel eater out of the way and the fact that you’ve left the town you lived all your life in with Archie and the gang – it’s finally time to get down to business. Enough of the comment…snigger…about which way you hang, forget about…erm…everything else. Not only did you completely screw up, but you didn’t even answer my big question Jacob!! Now come on, sonny – don’t play that type of game with me. I know you’re not exactly smart, but don’t act like the bluntest blade in the drawer. Besides, if I had asked you three questions - the answers, they were far too short and you didn’t even seem to convince yourself, it’s like you just brushed it off. Oh and your boyfri…your mate called me a…moron. Hey, does he always let his wrists limply hang like that? Yes, you brushed them off with a question of your own. Good way to avoid the topic, Jackie – you answer a question with a question…something that my five-year old niece has gotten the hang of!”

*He pauses again and tilts his head forward, smirking after every few words*

“Anyway, think back to what I said, camache. I did think about asking you “Are you a failure” or whatever, but I didn’t. I also contemplated asking you “Do you feel lucky” – but hey, how many times have we seen that before and plus, you and camache Chris probably use that line in one of your sick roleplay games. No, I only asked the ONE question. Do you get me? ONE, UNO, EINS…etc, etc. Mmm, I can just imagine the confused expression on that face of yours. So I’ll make a sentence out of it to assist you… “Yes, Mr. Barman – just the ONE Shirley Temple tonight, I’ve got a manicure tomorrow and I don’t want to miss it, Livio would get sooooo mad!” Comprende? Comprende. Comprende, what a weird word. Compronde? Comprinde?…”

*Hunt pauses again and brings his hand up to his chin and rubs it, going over the word over and over again. Moments later he glances up at the camera, taking his hand away and continuing to talk*

“Ah, enough of this crap. Jake Schutzenfest, my clock is set – nine days and whatever hours, minutes and seconds to go until you and I stand eye-to-eye and finish the card. I look at you and I pity you, Jake. I pity you because I see a young man who has his little heart set of hitting the big-time and is only going to be heartbroken. You don’t want to sound cocky? Good call, but masking your feelings will only have an adverse effect on your career and I will be so happy in bringing you back down to earth. I will grab your pumping heart in one hand and your career in the other and….slowly but surely, I will steadily grip each at the same time. So as your career fades away, so does your dreams, your hopes and your life!”

*Hunt hesitates before continuing*

“This is all a new beginning, Jimmy and I want you to understand that new beginnings bring new pain. I want you to understand that I am going to push you and push you until you totally lose the plot – inside and outside of the ring. Can I achieve that? Heh, I think I can…I know I can. It’ll take a little time – but it’ll be worth the wait. Will this get personal? Will I wander into unwarranted territory? Do you think I’ll be crossing the line? Oh, I guarantee it because I want you to understand that I am no ordinary individual, camache. You’re in too deep, Jakey…you can only sit and suffer, while I make you disappear. Let the games…begin!”

*Instead of the screen fading to black, the footage cuts out immediately following Hunt’s last word. All that is left on the screen now is simply a fuzzy picture, with the accompanied uninterrupted continuous crashing sound*
