The Truth? You Can't Handle The Truth!!
Written: 07/18/2002

*Welcome Back! I’m Richard and this is instalment number six of “Matt Hunt: From The Bottom To The Top!”. Okay, let’s recap on this wonderful journey, eh? So, Matt had received some termination papers by his former wrestling company. He then had a…confrontation with his wife, which resulted in Sam developing a bruise on her chin. Matt and I sat in his creepy cellar in complete silence, as he wanted ‘time to himself’ To try and lift his sprits, I mentioned that I could give a contact of my contact a call with a view of getting Matt an interview with a wrestling academy. The interview went successfully and now Matt has to fly to the United States to live, but there was a problem: Sam had her sights set on a future in Mexico, where she set up an interview. Of course, this ended in a confrontation and promptly led Matt totally over the edge as he gagged, blindfolded and gagged his wife after dragging her violently into the cellar! So, the last time you had this story – Matt apologised (again) for his shameful actions and Sam (again) accepted, blaming herself in the process. Well, that’s that over and done with – and on with part six!!


We’re back in the living room of the estranged Mr and Mrs Hunt and I am sat on the two seater, facing Matt and Sam who sit on the three seater. I’m also underneath the large window, which is to the right as you enter the room. The window has some net curtains hanging and there’s a light blue drape hanging either side.

The room itself is of average size and there is a pair of wooden sliding doors straight in front of me – which are closed – with the dining room locked behind it. The medium-sized television – with a video recorder, etc, etc – is placed in the corner immediately to my right with a framed photo on the top. To the right of the TV set and whatnot is a fireplace, again medium sized.

The mantelpiece has several porcelain figures of animals as well as a large clock and some matches are placed on the corner, obviously to light the fire. The fire itself is slowly burning, with a metal protector in front of it. Between myself wooden sliding doors – and in the center of the room – there’s the thigh-high mahogany table, which has a small plant placed on a woollen doyley pattern in the center.

There’s also three-piece suite with the three-seater placed in the corner to my left, but the odd stain on the corner of one of the three cushions doesn’t distract me as I am in confused at the sight of the ‘happy’ couple cuddling on the couch before me. Considering that a few days before this picture, Sam’s face was full of bruises and cuts whilst being gagged, blindfolded and tied up downstairs in the dirty cellar.

They’re whispering between themselves as I try to work this sodding zoom out. Damn you Curry’s – new revolutionary technology my arse!! Anyway, I really don’t want to film these two canoodling all afternoon…so I make some conversation*

Richard: “So, what’s the plan now?”

*There’s no answer and not surprising either, I have to repeat myself to get their attention…or rather, Matt’s attention*

Richard: “Matt...”

*He finally looks up, after a long glance into his wife’s eyes – this is getting creepy now, I just have flashbacks of Sam sobbing from under a mask of dried-up mascara*

Matt: “Yes, mate?”

Richard: “Yeah…what happens now?”

*No answer, again and Matt motions to me to keep talking – expressing that he hasn’t got a clue as to what I’m on about*

Richard: “With the PWA…”

Matt: “Oh right. Well, I’ve booked the tickets and there’s a flight from Heathrow to Denver. There’ll be a representative from the PWA waiting…”

Sam (interrupting): “Oooh, with a black stretch limo!!”

*A small scowl appears on Matt brow for a few seconds as he glances at his wife, but he ignores Sam’s comment and continues*

Matt: “…who will either take us to instruct to the Academy HQ. From there, I’ll be given a list of temporary residences – probably some local cheap motels or something…”

Sam (interutping): “You’re joking aren’t you?! A decent hotel at least!!”

*This time Matt pauses and glances towards the floor, while exhaling deeply. He shakes his head and he continues…again. Sam doesn’t seem to realise that she’s provoking Matt again, although nobody can condone his recent actions*

Matt: “Then I have to report to the Academy on Saturday afternoon…urgent business or something like that!”

Sam: “Maybe they’re giving you a title or something!!”

*Matt takes his arm off Sam and turns towards her with a stare, she just glances back with a “who me?” expression*

Matt: “There are no titles in the academy, Sam – only gimmick matches. Now don’t start…”

*Sam jerks back, shocked by the comments. She meant every word she said, but she tries to cover it up with an uncomfortable tone while sitting upright*

Sam: “I was only joking…”

*There’s a slight pause and an awkward one at that, Matt stares down at the floor rubbing his knuckles and Sam daydreams into the orange glow of the fire. I – yet again - strike up the conversation*

Richard: “What time is the flight?”

*Matt answers, but still with his eyes firmly fixed on the blue carpet*

Matt: “7:30am”

Sam: “Half seven? No way, I don’t even get up that early for my favourite morning chat shows!!”

*Matt reacts in a calm tone and I’m getting a sense of déjà vu*

Matt: “Tough…you’ll be up!”

Sam: “Okay, okay…I’ll be up.”

*Phew, Sam managed to defuse that situation*

Sam: “So, who’s going to be your manager?”

*Matt slowly turns his head towards his wife and sighs as an irritated expression appears on his face*

Matt: “What?”

*Sam plays with her nails and doesn’t sense Matt’s lack of tolerance*

Sam: “Well, you need someone to handle your deals on the outside of the ring and you also need someone to watch your back while you’re in the ring!”

*Matt shakes his head in disbelief and sternly rubs his brow. He then glances back up and sighs again*

Matt: “A manager? What the hell are you on about? Firstly, the only ‘deal’ I’ll be doing is signing a contract to the PWA and I don’t need supervision for that. And second, I don’t need my wife looking out for me when I’m busting my arse in the ring – you won’t be coming anywhere near the damn ring…got that?”

*Sam adjusts her seating position, so she’s now facing Matt – with her back up against the armrest and her right leg tucked underneath her outstretched left leg. She holds up her hand in that female manner and…oh dear…*

Sam: “Woah! Hold on right there. What do you expect me to do while you’re wrestling? Sit in a cheap and dirty motel, where the only thing on TV will be some horrid blue movie?”

Richard: “Really? Like 24 hours? Is it free?”

*Sam glances at me and you know what’s coming next…*

Sam: “Shut up Richard!”

*Worth asking in my opinion. Anyway, Sam is about to continue – but Matt cuts her off*

Matt: “How about you get a job?”

*A look of shock appears on Sam’s face, as if Matt called her a tart or something…in fact…*

Sam: “Get a job? Are you having a laugh? No, I will be your manager – in and out of the ring. Thing is Matt, I know what’s best for you…only you can see that yourself!”

*Another short pause at Matt stares a hole through his wife. He stands, startling Sam - and myself for that matter – and slowly paces between the two-seater and the three-seater. He attempts to talk, but stops at the last minute…in fact he does this a few times and exhales deeply at the same time. Few moments pass as Sam looks on preparing for the worst*

Matt: “You know what’s best for me? YOU? Hell Sam, you don’t even know me!!”

*Sam is shocked by Matt’s comment and is lost for words in response – but manages to get it out*

Sam: “What? But…but we’ve…but we’ve been married for over eight years!!”

Matt: “So? That doesn’t mean anything! Fact is - you don’t know me…in fact you don’t know my background or my past…”

*What the hell is this? Sam is just as surprised as I am as she slowly rises to her feet*

Sam: “Matt, how can you say such a thing…”

Matt: “Well, tell me something that you think you know…”

*Sam pauses as she thinks of something*

Sam: “Erm…urr…OH!! Your Dad was in the Army and…”

Matt (interrupting): “WRONG!”

*Eh? I can’t believe this and neither can Sam!*

Matt: “Oh please…continue…”

Sam: “Um…you used to live in France?”

Matt: “INCORRECT!!”

*This is unbelievable!! Sam’s face turns into horror as tears slowly run down her face, ruining her ‘expensive’ mascara…not for the first time either*

Sam: “I don’t understand…”

Matt: “Exactly, all that about living in an Army Environment, my Dad being an Officer in the Army and everything else I’ve told you about my past is a cover-up!!”

*WHAT?! Sam is in tears now and this is surreal*

Sam: “Cover up?…I don’t…what was you covering up?”

Matt (interrupting): “You want to know, huh? I guess I better tell you before you nag me to my death…”

*Matt pauses as Sam stands a broken wreck. I have to admit that this is a big shock and I honestly don’t know what to say*

Matt: “When I was 2 years old, my ‘parents’ took me to a Circus. Yeah, I had a great time – I saw the Strongmen, the Clowns, the Fire-Thrower – the whole bloody thing. However, when the time came to go home – ‘Mommy and Daddy’ were nowhere to be seen, so I thought they were playing hide-and-seek again.

I sat and waited for hours and hours until I got scared…I mean I was only 2 years old for Christ’s Sake, what was I to think or do? I couldn’t even talk properly – oh yeah, I had a mouth on me but I couldn’t ‘construct sentences’ together.

Nobody could find my ‘Mom and Dad’ as I couldn’t say my own damn name correctly, hence the name search on the ol’ Police computer would be a pointless task. So there I was – a scared a frightening little boy, surrounded by these faces that I saw at the circus, I thought this was a holiday!!. So everybody at the circus took me in and they’ve all pitched in to teach me everything and everything that I know to this very day. They finally told me when I was 10 and suddenly, these people who I thought were my aunties and uncles – were complete strangers.

What kind of thing is that to say to a 10-year old? What was I to do? Well, I had nowhere to go so I stayed with them. I never went to a school or college and I never had a girlfriend up until I met you. Sheltered life, eh? I went to the University of Life and graduated with flying colours – what I got taught by those guys, is nothing compared to some fancy public school!

I covered it up all because I was ashamed. Can you imagine what the talk of the locker room in some Indy fed would be? “Matt Hunt’s a circus freak!” But not anymore, I’m proud that at least somebody CARED for me at my time of need and I’m not ashamed of my past ANYMORE!!”

*There’s a slight pause, but Matt continues*

Matt: “THAT’S my past and THAT’S my background. You don’t know shit and you don’t shit!”

*Jesus, he pointed at me that time! This is crazy!*

Matt: “I’ve had enough of you, Sam – I’ve put up with your crap for so long and I’ve had it up to here with it. This little money-grabbing scheme of yours is over…and so is our bloody marriage!!”

Sam: “Matt…NO!”

*Wow, he finally said it! So Matt storms out of the room as Sam sinks to her knees sobbing her heart out. However, moments later he returns and points at the broken Sam…*

Matt: “And don’t YOU…”

*Then at me, startling me shitless*

Matt: “…or YOU dare follow me to the United States because if you do – I’ll make you both wish you never got on the bloody plane!”

*He storms out and aggressively slams the door behind him. I’m sat here in shock – don’t follow him to the States? But what about the plane ticket, they’re not refundable! Sam is on her knees with her face in the carpet, rocking back and forth and crying her heart out. Is this the end of the “Matt Hunt: From The Bottom To The Top” story? Or is it?*
