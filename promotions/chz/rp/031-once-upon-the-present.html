Once upon the present
Written: 6-19-02

Hi there. My name's still Theo Knox and you're still deep in the Bayou of New Orleans, Louisiana in my home. I've told you once before why living in the Bayou is different from living in other cities. But today I plan on showing you. Come to think of it, maybe it's not even New Orleans that's different, maybe it's me. Whatever the case, living in the New Orleans for 21 years is the modern life epitome of experiencing the calm before the storm. And with my current transitions from Canada in the CHZ, to returning and furthering my knowledge in the Academy, on a daily bases, that just might be that edge that'll prove necessary for me.

Just facing Boris Nakobi in my debut match in the Canuck Heat Zone will be more than a necessity for my career however, winning is secondary. As long as I show up, and put up a good fight, then my job has already served it's purpose. Don't get me wrong, I plan on beating Boris until his useless face looks conveniently uglier than homemade soap. But it's not a priority of mine as it is to him. Supposedly I will be laying in a pool of my own blood come this time tomorrow, but I highly doubt it. See, Boris may have the brain that's wheel is still turning, but the hamster's long dead. And what Boris doesn't yet comprehend is a simple concept that came to me, through the ever quickening mind of Primetime Championship Wrestling's own, Jordan X Shabazz. And that is, pain is temporary, fame is forever. Meaning, no matter how many times you knock me down, Boris. I'll get up again and, hopefully, the same people that you demean by shouting your ignorant insults, will love me for it, simply because I'm not a prick like you.

The scene opens to show auburn flames leaping and tossing themselves at a single camera lens that remains still in front of it. The flames prove unruly and are moving left and right, then settle at once and are put out by an elongated slightly tan hand. We fade away from the shot, slowly, dramatically. Zooming around the house we find that it's windows are wrapped in white drapes which compliments the plan of the entire house. The white leather couch is hugged by white leopard spots. The chandelier is off-white with glass and tiny white light bulbs on the interior. The camera then catches sight of the tan arm that it spied near the beginning of the shot and unveils the figure's body, revealing more detail to the camera, such as mid length brown hair flipped at its edges and piercing, almost transparent hazel eyes. The man then stands to his feet and exposes to the camera a baby blue T-shirt with a large 'X' symbol with an arrow pointing down and then the words 'The Knox Spot'. He's also wearing white metallic mesh shorts with a small Nike check symbol in the corner and NIKE Air Turbulence sneakers incorporating both silver and blue. Theo sits back down to his white desk and looks up at the burnt out candle once again. he rereads what he's just written by hand, back to himself aloud:


'Two goblets rest on a window sill, each holding sweet nectar divine;
But one's is as clear as the mirror's reflection, while the other's is far less refined.

The transparent liquid delights like honey;
its favor attuned to all tastes.
One lonesome drop orders heartaches away, enshrouds worlds and transforms fates.

The flavor of the hazy beverage stings more often than soothes.
Its acid sharpness deepens with time,
Yet on occasion the taste improves.

All must peer through the window and sip from the goblets divine.
Though the murky draught lingers forever, hold the crystal in your mind.'

'Argh...something's missing.' Theo huffed to himself. Poetry wasn't a hobby of his, but really something to occupy his time while away from his sisters and not being able to hear their reoccurring advice. It was nice to be reminded of his mother from time to time, and have them laugh at his jokes and hug his sisters. So he wrote to vent his emotions instead. This particular piece about his upcoming match against Boris Nakobi. Everyone wanted a piece of Boris for one reason or another, but he'd be the first with the opportunity to kick his ass live. This maybe a life altering experience, but Theo wanted to take this for what it was, just a match. His first match, but just a match.

Theo laid down his pen and grabbed the Adidas Santiago 2002 sack pack in which he'd packed all the clothes he'd need for the next couple of days along with other valuables that he wouldn't be able to purchase in another country, another country like Canada. Such as his CD's..a refrigerator full of real American beer, you know, the essentials. After he'd assembled all his luggage he dashed out of the house with a bottled water in hand, absent minded leaving the door open behind him and jumped into the driver's seat of his 2002 Bentley Azure, paid for in part by his contract signing to the CHZ. Turning his key in the ignition he cruises down the road at about 65, seemingly waiting to be pulled over. As we curiously zoom into his face driving down the road, one can see that he wears a grave stare upon his face. Making it blatantly obvious that he is a daze and not focusing on his driving. The deep blue of the Bentley Azure turn pale, and even more pale...

The date is December 7, 1981. The cold winter air cut through the open window light a blade making the 10 month old Theodore Knox, shiver. The cold was average in New York City. Just like every other winter. Theo sat in his crib playing with his blocks and what not. His Mom- Patricia Knox- was playing too... Though it wasn't as innocent.

'A-Are you sure he wont be home?' Sean Taylor asked. He was the Knox family's next door neighbor and a very intimate friend of Patricia's if you know what I mean.

'You mean Joel? I told you... He's out of town Sean.' Patricia Knox replied. Her seductive grin begged Sean to remove her panties.

'Okay Pattie...' Sean replied as he ripped off her panties and began to make love to her. Not to get into particulars, but this was around their twentieth time doing this. They had even let little Theo stay in the room while they did it once. They were sick, very sick minded people.

Meanwhile Theo watched as the shadows of the two danced off the walls. Theo, although small, knew what they were doing. Theo was a smart child. He knew what was happening. Then came the turning point. The door slammed open and in marched Theo's father, Joel Knox.

'Oh! Oh! Oh! That's it Sean! Right there! Don't stop! Plea-'

'Well, ain't this just the cutest...' Joel huffed.

Patricia lets out a loud screech.

Sean looked up, blinking. Confused.

Joel Knox stood at the doorway, a shotgun hanging loose in his right hand.

Sean felt a warm gush as his bladder let go.

'So, it is true. I guess I owe Aaron McKane a Budweiser. Well, God damn...' Joel sighed aloud to the rest of the house.

Patricia found her voice first.

'Joel, listen. It isn't what you think. He broke in. He was like a crazy man. He-'

'Shut the f*ck up, c*nt.' Joel was smiling. It was a gentle smile, a smile that matched Theo's when he first saw his long lost Daddy in the doorstep. He was wearing a long trenchcoat, the same he wore when he left his house, on the job. The same he was wearing two hours earlier when Patricia kissed him goodbye.

'Listen,' Sean said weakly. 'Please don't kill me. Not even if I deserve it. You don't wanna go to jail for this. Beat me up, I got that coming, but--'

'Take your..self out of her, son.' Joel said. Still smiling his gentle smile.

'Listen, Mr. Knox-' Sean began to say but Joel cut him off.

'Please. Call me Joel. After all we're practically best friends. I've been getting your sloppy seconds, isn't that right?'

'Joel, this isn't what you think. He raped me.' Patricia said, whimpering like a child.

Joel looked at her, his smile was still gentle and benign. 'If you say one more word I'm gonna jam this up inside you and let you have some special airmail.'

Patricia began to cry. Her face had gone to the color of nonflavored yogurt.

'Mr. Knox... Joel...' Sean began again.

'Sean ... that's your name right?' Joel asked.

Sean nodded stupidly.

'All right, Sean. Why don't we step into the living room? There's something we need to discuss.' Joel smiled at Sean, showing that he meant him no harm, then flicked his eyes to Patricia. 'You stay right there, puss, or you ain't never gonna find out how 'Days Of Our Lives' turns out. C'mon Sean.'

They stepped into the living room where Theo was in his crib staring at the two, and Sean began to blubber. He tried not to, but couldn't help himself.

'Turn around and face me,' Joel said. Sean turned.

Now, you know what you been doin'?' Joel asked. His smile was gone, and his face was very grave.

Sean didn't answer. It was a stupid question. But, he did continue to blubber.

'You've been sleepin' with another man's wife.' Joel declared.

Sean nodded.

'Grab the barrel of this gun, Sean. Careful. It's got a five pound pull, and I've got about three on it now. So pretend....oh, pretend that you're grabbing onto my wife's breast.'

Sean reached out one shaking hand and put it around the barrel of the gun. The metal was cold against his flush palm.

'Put it in your mouth, Sean. Both barrels. Yes, that's right. Easy....that's okay. Yes, your mouth's big enough. Just slip it in. You know all about slipping it in, don't you Sean?' Joel mused.

Sean's jaws were open as wide as they could go. His terrified stomach was trying to retch. The steel was oily on his teeth.

Sean only stared at him, his eyes burning with tears.

'Close those baby blue eyes, Sean.'

Sean closed them.

Joel pulled both triggers. The bullets flew out piercing through Sean's skull. And then he fell to the ground. Blood pouring out.

Joel looked down on his for a moment, smiling gently. He then turned the shotgun so the butt end was in the air. He turned to the bedroom. 'Ready or not, Pat, here I come!'

Patricia screamed.

'No! Please don't Joel! Put the gun down! Please!'

The shadows danced off the wall yet again, and blood flew all over the room as Theo watched with innocent eyes. When it was over Joel walked out of the bedroom. The rooms were silent. He walked over to Theo's crib and kneeled down.

'C'mon champ ... get your teddy. I'm getting you outta this shit hole.' Joel whispered then kissed his son on the forehead.

BEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEP! WATCH WHERE YOU'RE GOIN A**HOLE!!!!! GET A LICENSE!

Theo stomped his foot down as hard as he could on the brake and the Bentley stopped dead in its tracks, almost colliding with other cars, crossing the intersection of the street. He'd had another one of his visions about his mother's death and almost paid the ultimate price for it. Theo laid his head on the steering wheel of the car in the middle of the road and then pounds on the wheel hard with his fists.

'Why can't I go one day without it? Fuck living like this all the damn time..I definitely gotta start takin it before all my matches so I won't be a lunatic.' Theo resolved. Having said this, Theo reaches into his bag and pulls out two capsules of pills and takes one from each capsule. Swallowing them down with the bottle of water resting in his cup holder. The scene then fades back the horizon in the setting distance and ominous clouds that are insidiously crawling through the sky.
