A Saint speaks.
Written: 7/25/02

The scene opens up and we are inside a similar looking room. The sun is shining throw a window off to the left, there is chair’s everywhere, and because it’s around three o’clock, there’s no need for lights. As we stay in ah over this very nice room, a man walks into the scene and takes a seat in one of the brown wooden chairs. This man also looks very familiar, as if we have seen him before. His dark black hair, and dark brown eyes seem to be etched in your minds. The man has a look of boredom on his face, and you can tell just by looking at him he doesn’t like to be bored. As this man sits there a woman comes up and hands him one of those microphones that go on your shirt. He takes the microphone, places it on his silk blue short sleeve shirt, and once again begins to wait. After a few minutes a very good-looking woman walks into the scene and sits across from this man. The man smiles because that means it’s time to start, and that means he’ll have something nice to look at during this interview.

“So what took you so long? I was just about to leave if you didn’t show up in the next few minutes.” Says this man lying out his teeth.

“Well, I had a few meeting I had to attend. And you should be thankful I let you reschedule this interview time. Lets not forget you were meant to be here with your partner Angel a few days ago.” Says the CHZ interviewer Alison Malone making her point well known.

It hits you, this is Saint on half of the CHZ tag team COG. You then begin to get mad at yourself because how could you forget who Saint was? Sure he has not been around that long, but come on, he’s on television. Saint then nods his head yes to what Alison said, and begins to say something back. Alison then cuts him off by going first with what she had to say.

“So Saint, I asked your partner this, and I’ll ask you this. How does it feel to be out of PWA and in CHZ?” Ask Alison giving you the sudden feeling of disavow.

Saint just shakes his head, letting Alison know that what she just asked was a dumb question.

“Of course it feels great. I mean to put in all that hard work in PWA, and be able to advance, it just doesn’t get any better then that. Well not until we make it to PCW and show all those tag teams up there that they are not as great as they think they are. But for now PCW is not a thought in my mind, right now CHZ is all I’m thinking about!” Saint adds once again reinforcing that fact that he doesn’t like PCW tag teams.

“Saint, the world has to know something.” Alison says a bit hesitant to ask this question.

“Yeah go on.” Saint replies wishing this female wouldn’t be so shy with her questions.

“Are you really out to help Angel spread the word of God, or are you just wanting to ride his tail coats so you can make the big bucks?” Alison says fearing a bad reaction from Saint.
Saint just looks at her. He can’t believe she just asked him that. But you the viewer have been wanting to know the same thing. And I bet if Angel is watching, he would also like to know the answer to this question. Saint just sits there, making you fear his answer will be a bad one. But then he smiles, and laughs a bit.

“That’s a good question. And the answer would have to be that I really do want to help Angel spread the word of God. You see I am a prime example of Angel’s teachings, and I’m so thankful he showed me the light. I will have to admit at first I was just wanting to become one of those high paid people, but now that I have taken the time to get to know Angel and fully understand what he was talking about, I see that he’s right. The world should know the true meaning of God.” Saint replies shocking the entire world with his answer.

Saint then nods and waits for Alison to respond. He Saint sees that Alison is in just as much shock as the rest of the world he continues.

“You see I was unable to attend the interview with Angel because I was back in my home town. I was helping a small church rebuild their building. And the money and time was something I had to give back to that church.” Saint adds letting the world know that he is a good guy.

Alison faces then lights up.

“So you really are a Saint.” Alison says ending the interview.
