A Good evening Gone bad - Part 4
Written: 8/7/02

The scene opens in what appears to be a small music store. Along the drywall wall guitars of many different make and brands lean, and stretching through the middle of the long room is a CD rack, all the CDs in it are carefully arranged in alphabetical order. In the front corner near the door is the counter, manned by a typical American teen. Baggy jeans and a white T-shirt with his name tag on it, “Phil.” He stands with his back to the large front glass windows and the door.

A bell ringing is heard as the door pushes open. Jman strolls in wearing his usual black jeans and a shameless self promoting black CHZ Jman T-shirt. He looks up at Phil the cashier as he enters then re focuses his attention on the CD racks. He stops as he looks at one of the letter tags then begins slowly searching down the racks, past the E’s then the F’s.

The rack ends and Jeff walks around the other side where it picks up at the G’s then the H’s. Jeff advances to the I section. He stops and leans in closer to skim the band titles. He moves slowly then stops at his beloved Iron Maiden. There are a few of their albums lined up there.

Jeff gleams and picks out one, “Somewhere in Time.” He glances down at the others and back up at “Somewhere in Time.” He begins walking back towards the counter, flipping over the case as he walks. He reads the back of the case, smiling in joy at the list of songs, noticing some of his favorites. He looks up when he reaches the counter and hands the CD to Phil.

“Is that all?” Phil inquires as Jeff looks at him.

“Yeah,” Jeff responds reaching down to his pocket.

Phil rings it up and the total appears on the little display thing in green characters. Jeff eyes the total then digs into his pocket for some money, he pulls out a handful of crumpled bills and a small white piece of paper. Jeff notices the paper and moves it into his left hand, as he sorts through and then hands Phil a 20.

Phil presses a button and the register shoots open, then he puts the 20 into it’s slot and makes proper change. He quickly tears off the receipt and hands Jeff the change with it.

“Thanks,” Jeff says coolly as he scoops up his CD and exits the store. He crams the CD into one of his cargo pockets and stuffs the change into another. Then he inspects the piece of paper he found. It is Tess’ phone number. Jeff stops smack in the middle of the sidewalk, causing a few people behind him to falter.

He thinks back to that night several weeks ago, he remembers the car crash(es) and that brutal beating he received. Then he remembers how Tess came and helped him. He looks up at the sky for a moment then begins a search for a payphone. He walks down the busy San Francisco street, numerous cars of different makes and models whiz by him in the street while a couple people share the sidewalk with him.

Jeff halts at the sight of a payphone. He approaches it and lifts the receiver to his ear, then he quickly slides in a quarter and punches in Tess’ number as he reads it off the paper. When he has punched in all seven digits he lowers the paper into his pocket and listens to the phone.

It rings once, twice, three times. Then it is interrupted.

“He- Hello?” the voice of a female answers.

“…Tess?” Jeff asks into the receiver quietly.

Tess seems to pause for a moment, then replies.

“It’s you! That guy from last week!” Tess exclaims.

“Yea…” Jeff begins.

“Why didn’t you call! I thought those guys killed you! You jackass! I was so worried!” Tess interrupts him screaming.

“This is why I didn’t call you! Your so…” Jeff trails off, then re starts. “You were worried about me?” He asks with a questioning tone.

“I was- You- Yes, I was worried about you.” Tess finally sighs out.

“Well I’ll be damned, psycho bitch doesn’t hate me!” Jeff laughs.

“Well… you looked so pathetic laying there, but you wanted to get up so bad, you had fight in ya,” Tess responds.

“Indeed,” Jeff answers.

“So why did they do that to you? What happened?” Tess begins pummeling him with questions.

“It’s a long story,” Jeff begins.

“I want to hear it.” Tess states.

“It all started nearly a year ago, I was just getting into the business…” Jeff starts.

“What business?” Tess asks.

“Pro wrestling… I’m a wrestler.” Jeff responds.

“Where do you wrestle?” Tess asks Jeff.

“Oh up in that Canadians place… CHZ.” Jeff replies, “But anyway I’ll tell you what happened, It all traces back to my first wrestling job ever. It was… I think the Hardcore Wrestling Federation.”

“I’ve never heard of it,” Tess states.

“Yea, it was a real crap o’ rama, but I needed work so bad and I was flat broke. But regardless, I was really energetic about it and tore into the business with such power, such ability, I flew through the ranks in no time at all, I was on good terms with the owner too, since I was his top star within a month.” Jeff continues. “Well anyway, I started giving him ideas to help better the federation, he tried some out and they went well. So I eventually became the vice president of the operation.” Jeff says.

“Oh wow…. What happened then?” Tess asks.

“Well, we weren’t always on the same page of things, we were arguing all the time, hell, once he punched me out over the color of the logo,” Jeff mutters. “So it wasn’t long until he fired me.”

“That asshole!” Tess exclaims.

“But I wasn’t gonna take it… hell no,” Jeff begins. “I came back, he didn’t see it coming. One dark… gloomy night, I infiltrated the headquarters. Yea real GI Joe like,” Jeff laughs. “But I went into the lobby and basically I… I…” Jeff trails off.

“I torched the building, I completely leveled it. All their important documents were in there… everything. Hell, the boss even kept all his money in a safe in there. It was all destroyed… they never recovered. They were all out of a job.” Jeff finishes grimly.

“Oh jeez…” Tess trails off.

“Yea it was insane.” Jeff states.

“Your sick!” Tess suddenly explodes. “I mean it’s one thing to have quit or something, but that’s a damn felony!” She screams.

“I!” Jeff starts.

“Do me a favor and never call me again!” Tess hollers in a rage, then slams the phone down. Jeff sighs and hangs up the phone.

He turns away and begins walking back down the road. Then he pauses and looks around, he is standing now infront of a sporting good store, outside a small display is arranged of different balls and sticks, footballs, baseballs, bats, hockey sticks and so on.

He turns and looks around, lots of big brick buildings along the sides of the roads, all different colors. Across the street in a chain link fenced in parking lot, several teens are playing a game of roller hockey. He watches the one with the ball he weaves in and out of the opposing player then nearly blindly slapshots the ball at the goalie. Who catches it in his glove, then he sets it down and hits it back into play. A few guys go for it and collide, the ball rolls out of play and through a small hole in the old rusted out fence it rolls through the road past numerous cars as if it were drawn to Jeff for some reason. Jeff stops it under his foot and scoops it up into his hand.

“Hey over here!” One of the guys yells. Jeff holds the ball and looks over at the tall fence, its across a 4 lane road, he couldn’t throw it over, then he sees the small hole it came out. He looks at it for a moment and then picks up a hockey stick from the display, he tosses the ball down and gives it a mighty slapshot from his stick, the ball sails across the road, nearly hitting several cars, then amazingly passes through the hole.

Jeff stands in awe at this feat then looks down at the stick he grips firmly in his hands. Then he sees the price tag, “Clearance! 75% off!” Jeff sighs and walks into the store, a few moments later he emerges, pocketing a receipt and some change, hockey stick still in hand…
