The Resurrection Part 1
Written: 4.8.02

The light from a nearby car beams brightly out of the headlights of an old, rusty in parts car. The car itself has no story, no tale, but the person who crashed it has, or should I said had. The cause of death was multiple fractures to the skull of the male, aged between 22 and 23. The man has been identified as Tom Samba, a student at a nearby college. His family has yet to be notified. Tom was said to have slid off of the road and into an old oak tree, which is still standing despite the ferocious impact of the car on it, but now has a huge indent on the side of it's trunk. The accident was said to have been an avoidable one.


The story goes as follows. Mr.Samba left from a pub at some time before midnight. No he was not drunk, nor did he fall asleep at the wheel. He left the pub and casually begun to drive, carrying a passenger in the back.

Tom: So Lexit, where are you heading? (This was said in a quizzical tone of voice)
Lexit: Do you know where Baker's Street is?
Tom: No
Lexit: Not to worry, I'll tell you when we get nearer

So the conversation between Lexit and Tom stopped there. The two had met in the bar not too long before, and Tom, noticing that Lexit was a little drunk, offered to give Lex a lift home, as the two had got on well during there brief encounter.
But Lexit may have only appeared a little drunk, but he was wasted, the world was a spinning whirlpool of colours and shapes, twirling around and around like clothes in a washing machine to him. This was the first time Lexit had been drunk in a long time, in fact probably ever. He doesn't usually drink to drown his sorrows, normally they just drown themselves naturally. But this wasn't the reason of Lex's visit to the small pub. From the outside it looked warm and inviting, and Lexit felt like celebrating. Hell, he'd just been accepted into the CHZ. He doesn't have any friends to speak of so for that night booze would accompany him.

Lexit fell asleep in the back of the car, using his crimson coloured shirt for a pillow. He was thankful he opted to wear his baby blue tracksuit bottoms over his jeans, as he felt these gave him a lot more freedom of the legs.
What he dreamt, only he knew, but one thing's for sure, when he woke, he wasn't the same person he was before he had drifted off. Amongst the swirling thoughts of his head, and the fact that he was majorly wasted, the personas of Lexit were subdued, and for the first time in two years vulnerable. Vulnerable to let the one persona surface that they all hated, all feared… Persona Perfect Dark (View Biography). The fears quickly turned into a possibility, and possibility into reality. Perfect Dark came to surface.
Dark's eyelids flickered open, and immediately he did what came naturally to him attack. Not only attack, but viciously assault anyone and thing that was in close proximity to him, in this case, it was Tom Samba.
Perfect Dark sat up and reached across the drivers seat, covering Tom's mouth and nose, starving him of much needed oxygen. Dark then began to look around, and it was only at that point he actually realised he was in a car. Tom couldn't keep a hold of the car, as he was more concerned with ensuring he didn't suffocate, which on the slippery surface slid of the side of the road. Almost simultaneously Tom a Persona Perfect Dark looked out of the windscreen…contact with the old oak tree was imminent.
