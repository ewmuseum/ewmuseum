Journey is halted by the MMMMs
Written: 7/20/02

On a dirt road near the country line of Mexico, the jackass and I rode out. With my sombrero covering my face, my supplies on the donkey sides, and pure determination in my heart, my journey had officially begun. Of course, the long trip will be full of hardships and crazy occurrences, but none that I expected that day. Seemingly alone, the jackass and I must've been traveling for at least 24 hours now. Surrounded by dry sands and the occasional scorpion, I rode on that jackass as skittish as a squirrel. The ride was almost silent, except for a few gusts of wind blowing sand up in both my donkey's eyes and my own. My throat was extremely arid and needed liquid desperately. I reached for a canteen that I thought to be filled with water, but to my surprise, it was empty. I was dying in the heat of the barren wasteland, with the sun beating down on me. I looked up and saw some tumbleweeds racing by, mocking me, I had almost lost hope.

'AYE DIOS MIO!' I screamed at the skies in a rage. (aye dios mio means oh my god in Spanish, I think.)

The donkey looked back at me thinking I was crazy.

'What the hell are you looking at, jackass!' I countered his look.

The donkey shrugged his head back and faced forward. Silence was kept for a few moments until a slight crackle was heard a few yards behind me. I quickly pulled the ropes on the donkey's neck forcing him to turn, making me turn to see what the noise was.

'What the hell?' I asked myself as I pondered what I saw.

A lone maraca laid on the dirt bed as the wind moved it slightly more once again making it's crackling sound.

'Now why would there be a maraca just laying here? Oh well, might as well take it along eh jackass?' I asked the donkey.

The donkey once again rolled it's eyes as if understanding me. I forced the donkey forward until we reached the still, well designed, Mexican maraca. I got off the donkey and walked over to the maraca with extreme caution as if I was expecting some kind of trap. I bent my knees along with my back as I went down to pick up the maraca and as soon as my hand touched it, another crackling sound was heard in my ear in front of my donkey. I instantly grabbed the maraca and stood back up in a panic. I went over to the second maraca while still looking around with an uneasy stomach.

'I got a bad feeling about this one, don't you jackass?' I asked the donkey as it nodded it's head up and down. The donkey then looked around, starting to back up slowly.

Just then, three very unsneaky looking characters dressed entirely in black made their way around on their tip toes, ducking under rocks or rolling around with the tumbleweeds. I wasn't sure what the deal was and assumed that they were just playing hide and seek with their sombreros and chiming little necklaces.

'Right.' I said look back to the donkey circling my index finger around my ear motioning that these men were crazy. With further inspection of their black attire, I soon discovered that they were bearing a type of red colored logo.

'Wait!' I shouted as I pointed to one of the men as they stood straight as a statue and stayed there as quiet as a mouse. 'I know that logo from somewhere.' I continued on pondering from where I might know it from.

Suddenly, the light clicked on in my brain.

'The Mexican Chile Hut! I went there every week when I was a kid, I used to get those little bowls of chili that came in those tiny sombreros. Boy, those were scrumptious! You guys have any of that stuff on you? Those sure do bring back memories.' I questioned the halted, crazy men.

Not one of them moved, instead, they all ran behind a nearby rock and took out maracas. Even though I could see their sombreros and obviously hear them, I decided to play along and act as if nothing had happened.

'At the right moment, I'll show 'em I see 'em.' I thought to myself. 'Alright jackass, let's move on out of here, there isn't anything to see, or hear.' I said motioning to the 'hiding' men behind the rock.

I then saw the three mens' sombreros move as I heard them murmuring to themselves in satisfaction. I laughed to myself as I went back to my donkey and mounted it. I patted it's neck and forced it to move forward. We moved slightly past the rock until one man had the courage to stand and reveal his spot. He stood tall, looking as though this was the first time he'd revealed his position, however, we all knew that was FAR from the case.

'Alto! Alto at once!' The man said putting his hand out with his wrist flexed. (Alto means both tall and stop, I'm using stop in this quote.)

Using my keen Mexican knowledge of my past time, I understood the man perfectly, or so I thought.

'There are no cats in my pants at once!' I shouted back at the man making a fool of myself. The man looked back at me with a peculiar face as if in confusion. I of course, did not understand why he was confused, for all I know, I could've stumped him with my great Mexican translation.

The man then pointed his arms out to each side of me as if he were signaling something. Then like clockwork, the two men beside him rolled in unison around me then stood up only to go to their tip toeing again. They both started to circle around my donkey as if it were their prey.

'Escuche, We are the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs! What we need, you have, señor Stimpo.' He said to me with authority. (Escuche is listen.)

I was shocked. How could these sorry excuses for Mexicans know me? Why were they calling themselves the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs?

'What do you need from me? I'm just a man looking for his past roots with this jackass and at the same time I have to worry about kicking some a** in the PWA along side Jason Arawn this coming Sunday! So lets get this straightened out so I can move on.' I demanded it to be solved now. My donkey rolled his eyes as I once again referred to him as my jackass.

'Well, that sombrero for instance. That sombrero is the sacred sombrero of sexiness.' He said explanatory bringing one of my eyebrows up in a confused fashion. The man continued on, 'The sombrero alone holds all the power of Mexican mojo therefore, us Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs must have it.'

I was left there stunned at the idiocy of these men. The man was not done showing his almighty dumbness that he even continued on about another precious item that these Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs must have.

'But, why do you need my sombrero?' I asked in defiance.

'We simply do you impotent Mexican man.' He said snapping his fingers.

'Also, that donkey of yours is also a precious item that us Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs must posses. That donkey is the dancing donkey of the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs.' He explained to me.

I stood there for a few more seconds before I finally grasped what the man had just said to me.

'You are one strange Mexican, and you can't have my dancing donkey or my sombrero of sexiness!' I said with force behind my voice.

'Very well, we shall take you by force. Men, arm your maracas!' The man said as if sounding a charge to his two companions. Before I knew it, one maraca cracked over the back of my head while two others hit simotaniously on my front and my left side, breaking my ear drums in half. I fell to the ground and heard the three men singing some type of Mexican song and also attempting to salsa, but to no avail.

I laid there for a least an hour before I started to awake. My head was in such a rush and maraca beads were all over the ground. My donkey was gone and so was my sexy sombrero. All that was left was myself, maraca beads and one folded up note, just like the two I found in the grocery store, Wegmans.

'Oh no, not again.' I said reaching for the lone paper. As I suspected, there was that same style of writing as was before written in a perfect blue:

It's not over Stimpo...

I had lost the man's donkey and lost the sombrero this man was to secretly know me by. I shrugged as I opened the letter up and read it out loud to myself.

'Stimpo, you have not yet failed, though you have lost both of my precious possessions, I still have faith in you. Quickly, retrieve these items and report to the Mexican country line where we shall meet as planned. Quickly Stimpo, now is your time, use it!' I stopped speaking, ending the letter.

I contemplated where these men could've gone and how to retrieve these items for this anonymous man. Then I though, why should I help this man?

'Why should I help this man?' I repeated to myself. 'This is MY journey, not his errand running type trip! Should I do this for the guy? Or should I just forget that piece of scum?' I asked myself over and over playing out events and results of what could go right and what could go wrong.

I then stood up from the dirty ground and shoved the third note into my pocket. I looked back down and there was my first clue, donkey prints.

'Thank you, jackass!' I said looking to the clouds. I then looked how far they went, miles and miles ahead of me was simply more wastelands. I looked then to the left where I saw only a few miles away, the country line of Mexico, where my roots were, my past was kept there.

'What to do, what to do.' I questioned myself once again. I stood there with two options, take a quick way to my past or risk my life against the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs. I let out a heavy sigh as I thought long and hard.

Which path did I take? Found out in the next part of Stimpo's journey to Mexico.
