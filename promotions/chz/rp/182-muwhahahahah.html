MUWHAHAHAHAH!!!!!!
Written: 2nd September 2002

*The screen fades in and we’re in a dark-lit room, which has a bleak, creepy atmosphere about it. One singular light with a lampshade hangs quite low down and loose from the ceiling and as we look closer a large brown leather chair has it’s back to us. A few moments of silence pass, the brown leather chair being the only piece of furniture in the room.

The chair slowly spins round, knocking the electrical chord of the light in the process and causing the light to sway left to right, making it difficult to make out who is in the chair. The chair continues to turn, with the person’s face in darkness.

Finally, the features of the individual are revealed.

Short brown hair in French-crop style, stubble around the mouth and on the chin with a smirk and a pair of blue eyes, one with a distinct bruise.

It’s Matt Hunt.

With the light still rocking left to right, Hunt calmly reaches up and grabs the lampshade, all of the light is now spraying over Hunt and the brown chair. Hunt sits comfortable in the leather seat and looks on into the camera, with his left leg nicely resting on his right knee.

The vision of Hunt is that similar to a storyteller, about to start a horror tale. After some distant silence, Hunt wipes his chin with his palm and starts to talk*

“Interesting night wasn’t it?. Questions that need to be answered and answers that will probably be questioned. One thing is for sure, when I said I was going to write a new chapter all those weeks ago, I damn well meant it.

Oh, you thought I was on about the gold?

How much of a fool must you look right now?

Course, the idea of being the top man in the company did appeal to me; but the prospect of being the individual of OWNS the top man and the whole damn company slightly edges it…just, just a tad

So why was I named a CHZ Commissioner? In fact, do any of you camaches believe me?

I doubt that

But does it surprise you? I mean I can imagine the instant reaction after I announced myself after my “loss” last night; but now that the developments have been able to sink in, who actually believes that I am the right man for the job?

When I questioned my own ability last week, the gold wasn’t the only thing that I saw in the distance…

I saw, an expensive paycheck being signed over to me every single month. I saw a few more zeros in that particular paycheck. I also saw the CHZ under a new regime…

I was named a CHZ Commissioner because El Diablo saw my ability and he knows that I’ll do the job in a proper and correct manner. I would pay my respect to El Diablo for giving me this opportunity, but the fact is that I deserved this…more so than the World Title, so I don’t owe anybody anything, jack!

Sure, I may have been preaching to the whole world and it’s uncle that I was the right man; that I was the man to restore the pride, honour and prestige into this company by winning the CHZ Title, that I was the man to take this company further than it had been before, to urinate and wipe away former CHZ “greats” like Don Rossi and Chris Matthews…double Champion, my arse

*Hunt pauses to get even more comfortable and continues*

I still am that man, winning the World Title will not change a damn thing as to the direction of the company. If only the ignorant know that.

As the commissioner, I am still that man to restore honour and…blah, blah, blah. I don’t need to repeat myself and I’m not on a preaching mission again, because it seems that my award winning speeches went on deaf ears before. Well, it won’t be my loss at the end of the day.

I wish I could sit here, knowing full well that the future of the CHZ is in my capable hands; safe in the knowledge that everybody answers to me and we’re now sailing on a ship with a new name…

But I can’t

No, similar to real life, there’s a meddling bird sticking her nose in; thinking she knows best.

You get that everywhere. Know-it-all women who you just want to push off a cliff

Blokes get dragged out to some shopping center on a Saturday afternoon against their will; women who just cannot shut up while the football is on; birds who complain when you don’t put the seat down; birds who feed you all this vegetarian crap when all you need after a hard days work is a succulent steak with those thick chips…Women who think they know it all.

Well, it seems the curse has hit the CHZ and THAT’S another reason why I will not thank El Diablo for this chance…as if the camache can do anything, I’ve got an iron-clad contract, etc, etc.

Yes, that Mexican Mug has poisoned the CHZ with the meddlesome ways of a female

We’re doing fine with Angel Summers, she does her job in the ring, she takes care of business out of the ring…no problem. Hell, even that bird reporter knew when to keep her mouth shut and when to open it.

But it seems that she’s suddenly the corporate type and now she’s in a position of authority.

I wish I knew the identity of the person in question, but that really doesn’t matter as all I need to know is it’s a female

So I’m not going to beat around the bush here. I hate this idea with a passion, I detest it, and I purposely stick my fingers down my own throat at the prospect of having a female in command.

I know this is the 21st Century and there’s this equal opportunity bollocks. Fair enough, women do deserve to work…but by that, it should be a receptionist, a librarian, a supervisor at some fast-food joint and so on and so and ariston.

Females should not be at the helm of a company, which will provide us with the future of wrestling!!

Why should I be complaining? Not as if I have to follow under her rules and regulations…and you’d be spot on.

That’s all I have to say on that matter, because quite frankly…I’m going to be sick

*He pauses again and glances around the room, before finishing off this little speech*

“Now on to our new World Champion, Jake Schultz…

I have some chosen words for you in person this Wednesday Night, but a slight mention won’t do harm.

I can imagine that you went through three different reactions last night, Jake…

Delight and glee as you skipped around the ring, cradling the World Title in your arms like it was a baby child…or the Optimus Prime action figure. Either way, you were overjoyed and I could see those tears…

But those tears changed to those of shock, anguish and fear as I ran my mouth and announced that I was the new CHZ Commissioner. Like I had grabbed your Transformers collectors mug, signed by the guy who did the voice-over for Deceptitron, and smashed the little bugger into a million pieces.

Then, as you ran to safety and cowardly hid behind the back of the person who you can “stand beside” in this little power struggle in a gleeful manner that made me want to trip myself up and land on a several million man-eating ants!

But that’s fine, if you two want to join forces and bring the Transformers storyline of good versus evil…so be it.

But I will destroy you Jake. I will destroy your career, the man inside that dopey looking head and your life. I will take your career and simply urinate on it, like my two-year old niece promptly gashes into her nappy thinking that it’s all a game.

And woman, I will rape you…of your authority powers. I will snatch them away like Christopher Lloyd did, when he grabbed that apple with his flick-knife off that little kid in the family film “Dennis”

I will achieve those two goals and the CHZ will be run…my way, by hook or by crook!

*Hunt pauses again, seemingly getting agitated*

So who is this uppity little arsehole that has attacked me twice?

El Diablo?

Mmmm, I know he’s intelligent…and I don’t think he’s got the ball to do so

Jake Schultz?

He is prime candidate, after all he was in the ring when it happened on Wild and at Mind Games, following the incident, he was nowhere to be seen

Woman?

Heh, I won’t even got there

Whoever you are, you’re a marked man. I’ll track you down camache and make you suffer the consequences of attacking a CHZ official…and not some dodgy zebra either.

You want to attack me again?

Go ahead punk, make my day…

But as I depart, I believe there’s only one thing left to say as we all rub our eyes and look upon the new horizon…

Let the games begin…oh, let them begin…

*Hunt sits back and folds his arms as the camera pans back. Hunt stares into the camera, as he moves further and further away. Slowly, the figure of Matt Hunt gets smaller and the screen fades to black*
