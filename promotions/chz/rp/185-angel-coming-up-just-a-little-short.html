Angel coming up, just a little short!
Written: 9/4/02

The scene opens and we see Angel and Saint for the first time on CHZ television for the first time in a few weeks. Angel, not looking the same, is the first person we see. His new acquired mask stands right out, he has on his ring gear, the long red and white wrestling tights, with the white being only in the cross on each side of his legs. You then start to realize that this may not be the same Angel, and that the rumor you have been hearing is true. You begin to study him, trying to think back to the last time you saw him, but because you really never got the full impact of Angel, you don’t remember that much about him. So to be honest, you find yourself not caring right off the bat. Saint then becomes visible in our scene, and he too is wearing his wrestling gear. His navy blue pants, and his white two shoulder tank top blends right into the tights. His spiked black hair his tinted with blond tips, and looks slicked back with gel.

“Angel, are you ready?” Saint asks as the scene becomes more visible, and we can now see that we are inside the locker room for this evenings Wild.

Angel nods his head, and pulls the strings tighter in the back of his mask, making the mask stretch across his entire head.

“It’s time to show CHZ who the big boys are!” Angel says as he punches his left hand with the fist he made with his right hand. He then stands and gets pumped up.

Saint then laughs and puts his right hand on Angel’s left shoulder.

“I’m sorry, but it’s funny hearing you say big boys when you are referring to yourself.” Saint says are he laughs.

Angel then Saint a look of confusion.

“Why?” Angel ask, really not knowing the answer.

Saint just shakes his head and laughs.

“Don’t worry about it man.” Saint just can’t stop laughing.

Angel shrugs his shoulders, and leaves the scene. Saint then laughs and motions at the height of Angel. He then puts his hands on his gut, and silently laughs. Angel comes back into the scene, he looks at Saint and gets in his face.

“I saw that, not funny!” Angel says with a hint of force behind his voice.

Angel then leaves the scene once again, and this time we hear a door shut, this must mean Angel has left the room. Saint points at door out of view and laughs out loud this time.

“Oh yes it is, god, I think I just pissed myself!” Saint says as he acts like he’s wiping off his pants.

The scene fades with Saint doing what Angel did before, and that’s leaving the scene. As the door shuts, the camera pans around and we see a sign on the back of the door. The camera zooms in and we are now able to read it.
“Hey Tyrome and Donald, just to let you know we will be watching you. So when you find this note on the back of your locker room door, don’t worry.”
