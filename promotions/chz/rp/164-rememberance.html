Rememberance
Written: 08/25/02

An image of my sister Kayla lying in a pool of her own blood on Wednesday Wild plays in my mind like a broken record. Who could do such a thing? She didn’t do anything to deserve this and I will find out who was the attacker. With Wild vastly approaching, will I ever find who did this Kayla?

Beep, Beep, Beep…

This was the sound of the life support keeping his sister Kayla alive at the moment and Soul sat at the head of the bed in a brown chair looking on with much concern. She was in a white hospital gown with a skinny little tube under her nose and her long blonde hair spread across the white pillow she was laying on. He could only look on at her lifeless face and shook his head in disappointment. His white skinny jacket rippled as he leaned back in the chair with his hands placed neatly on his white pants. Soul looked around to the green walls seeing the sunlight exit through the blinds touching it with a bright light.

“If only I could have been there.” He said sadly.

Flowers sat on the dresser to her right and behind Soul glimmering from the windows light. He sat there looking lost and only he licked his lipped, rubbed his head and sighed only getting deeper in depression.

“I could remember the days before are parents death were we would just talk.” He said smiling while tears rolled down his cheeks.

He arose from his chair and picked a red rose from one of the bouquets smelling its sweet fragrance and placing a smile on his face. He looked down at her with no facial expression but somehow she is still surviving and he walks to the window looking out at Vancouver and goes back to a place before he became who he was today.

Flashback

It was a wonderful night, tree blew from left and right in the fifty acre grass plains and a young Kayla and Samuel Oswald Unattus Lawrence, laid in the grass looking up at the stars in the night sky. They were both dressed in white t-shirts and black pants that exposed there bare white feet.

“Days like these make me glad to be a kid.” She said proudly.

“Yeah, this night cannot get any better than this.” He quickly replied.

It seemed the longer they looked up at the night sky; they seemed to be rotating and seeing the nights beauty for what it is. Kayla places her arms around her little brother and smiled messing around with his long black hair that was spread across the grass where he laid his head.

“Just think, when were older we will not have anything like this and you will have a girlfriend as well.” She said jokingly and watched her brother look in disgust.

“Ugh, girls have cuddies!” He said obviously unhappy at that image.

Kayla laughs hysterically at his comment and they swayed back and forth in the grass in rhythm smiling and humming tunes from a song that there mother sung to them every night.

“You still haven’t told me what you wanted to be when you get older?” she asked insisting on an answer.

“To the best wrestler the states and the world have ever seen.”

“Are you sure about that?” she said in a puzzled manor

“Yes I am.”

She smiled and hugged her brother tightly listening to the crickets croak about a couple of feet from where they were. Leaves whistled a lovely tune with cool breeze blew them slightly. Kayla was smiling and it made Samuel smile joyous as they continued to look at the stars.

“Have you ever thought about death?” Samuel said out of the blue.

“No, you shouldn’t either.” She said laughing as if it were a joke.

She saw the look in his eyes that were dead serious and her laughter stopped quickly and looked concerned over her little brother.

“I’m sorry I just feel something bad is going to happen to us.”

“No way, we will be together forever.” She said with a big smile reassuring Samuel.

They both sighed simultaneously pounding their heels against the floor and from the white mansion thirty feet away they could here there mother’s angelic voice calling out to them.

“Samuel, Kayla it is time for dinner!”

They looked at each other with much delight and hop up racing with each other trying to be the first to the house. They ran faster and faster until Kayla edged ahead of Samuel and looking back smiling at Samuel who smiles sarcastically.

{End Flashback}

Soul continued to look out at the busy streets of Vancouver and sighed, he looked back watching his sister making no progress and right when he is about to walk to the door he hears some moving on the bed to see his sister open here green eyes and smile. Tears of joy flowed down his cheeks, he ran to the side of her bed looking happy but at a lost of words.

“Damn, you seem shaken up did I scare you?” She said sarcastically.

“That’s not funny, you scared me.”

She slowly sat in the bed looking around the room and smiled, she looked towards a frightened and remorseful Soul as he seemed lost and alone. She scooted across the bed and placed a hand on his shoulder making grin a little.

“There is no need to e scared.” She said looking at a sad and depressed Samuel.

“I didn’t want to lose you.” He said softly and almost to a point of a whisper.

There was a brief moment of silence where Samuel continued to lay his head on the bed shaken and quivering. He looked scared of something and continued to cry and cry, like a little lost boy that he was long ago.

“Its ok, you have a match to worry about.”

“I know, but something’s are more important than wrestling.”

She smiled at her brother and placed her head next to his wiping the tears away from his eyes watching his shake and quiver state slowly dwindle. In his head he could the voices of his mother and father saying, “Don’t give up… please do not give up.” At that point he arose from the bed looking into the eyes of Kayla and nods his head.

“This Wednesday, shall be our day and no one shall take that away from us.”
