Written: 16/8/02

Mind Games.Pt 1

The weeks have past, two to be exact. Champions have been met, wars have begun and the only woman to step in a CHZ ring is yet to be defeated. Week one was a good week for Angel, as she won her match in a four-way dance against Ryan Contra, Al Young and Matt Blitz. In this past week she was in a tag match with Blitz, and up against Al Young and Ryan Contra. The match was considered a draw due to a double disqualification. This past week Al Young made the single most mistakes he will make in his life, messing with a cowgirl on a mission. By striking Angel cheaply with a hammer, sending Angel outside the ring damaged, it was like bells went off for Angel. These bells were the bells of hatred; it’s as if they went off in anger, her new mission was destroying Young! And now her wish has been granted, as the next wild will be no doubt a great one when she squares off one on one against Al Young.

The days were becoming more and more interesting for young superstar Angel Summers. Her first few weeks were vigil her in Toronto, Canada. No real excitement was brought to her until the matches had begun. Now that every Canadian is becoming aware of CHZ, Angel is being noticed on streets, signing a million autographs per day and walking by and seeing her picture on people shirts, coats and hats.

Angel has quickly become a fan favorite in the Canuck Heat Zone. Her will to win is surreal, most woman wrestlers with beautiful looks are all about attention, providing a show and yet having no skill to the natural mind. Angel prides everything a wrestler needs to succeed, skill, will and looks. Many women could never compete with fellow wrestlers so they focus on other woman. But Angel has no choice, but to compete against men, as she is the only female with courage to step in a ring against highly tuned male athletes. Angel has proved she has the skill to compete these past weeks, and next week will be no different.

The day was muggy, rare for a summer day. Thunderstorms and dry hot air consisted of the weather on this day. Angel has decided to spend this muggy Friday afternoon at the arena gym in British Columbia, where the next Wild will be held. The empty weight room was peaceful for Angel, as she needed time to clear her head of all the upcoming attractions that are mildly approaching. Rep after rep, pull after pull Angel works on her forearms. Not thinking of anything but being ready for her next match. Angel is not only fighting for herself, but to show all people women can and will compete as good or if not better then males some day!

Angel follows her long work out with a pool that is provided outside the gym, where she eased her stress for a few hours. After changing and into her natural clothes, as she is set to go home. Angel steps outside the gym door drying her wet hair with her towel. Alison Malone a CHZ interviewer is leaning against the cement wall obviously awaiting the showing of Angel. Alison is looking fairly beautiful as usual, as well as Angel. Angel was dreading the day when Alison interviewed her, as her good looks were no reason for her bitchy ways. Angel has always hated the bitchy good-looking girls. Angel has always been fair to all that were fair to her, never using her looks as a weapon.

Alison approaches Angel, begins setting up with microphones and such, without even asking for the interview, just assuming Angel would accept the interview. Angel just tosses and cooperates with her. The camera begins as Alison begins with her series of questions.

“ This is Alison Malone standing her with the popular Angel “The Texan” Summers. Angel has come out two weeks in a row with rage in the ring, trying to make a statement on women rights, proving to all mankind she is graceful, well now it is time to here her side of the story, Angel?

“ Well, I hope my matches were an eye catching blow to the CHZ wrestlers, showing that I am no prize but yet a competitor. I am not an assistant, nor will I be here in CHZ. I am her to fight, and fight I will.

Alison clears her throat and glances at her papers full of questions to ask Angel, while Angel is watching the ceiling fan spinning in boredom. Finally Alison is set.

“ Well I personally Angel, believe you have proved your point, but Axl is right, Matt Blitz is not there to protect you anymore, you are one on one now! Do you seriously believe you can defeat this highly tuned athletic machine?”
“ What kind of question is that? I would not be here if I were not able to compete! This past wee I was tagged with Blitz, but the week before that, it was everyone for themselves, tell me Ms. Malone who pulled out the winner?”

Alison shaking her head is disgust as Angel has a mild smirk across her face.

“ That’s besides the point Ms. Summers!”

The tension is building.

“ You are alone in a match now, Al will destroy your prissy self, and when he does, I will be first in line to congratulate him!”

Both women eye to eye now. Angel takes a deep breath.

“ Only time will tell, you will see.”

Angel says that while walking away out the front door leaving the interview. The camera stays focused on Angel until she leaves the building, then it fades to black.


This next week at Wild will answer several questions. Does Angel have what it take? We know Alison Malone’s prediction on the subject, what is yours?
