Southern Discomfort
Written: 6-18-02

Welcome to the Bayou, New Orleans, Louisiana. Like living in the 'city that never sleeps', you can live here for a while and not really experience it. The people here, are different. Their motivations, different. Their ambitions and dreams..yeah, I bet you get the notion. But I can't just describe to you the city and the state of mind. You gotta see it for yourself. But until then your eyes will be my eyes. You'll see what I see. But don't touch nothin' and try not to start any trouble. I ain't never say this was the safest place. Nor, the most friendliest.

'Er...'

'whoa'

'..ugh'

The scene opens to show thin, white, and almost transparent sheets jerking and creasing on a decent sized bed. We quickly back away from the figure, or figures in the bed [it's only right] and see the sun beginning to rise on the Louisiana skyline. The orange, reds, and purples pastel the sky and just as soon we saw them, the colors fade into white. This time we start to hear faint mumbling in the background and the voices are mildly audible as the screen remains on the white, still screen.

A young woman wearing a white dress, is running through a field of dandelions up to her knees. I'll give her twenty or so years, no older, no younger. The women stumbles a bit and looks as if she might fall right over. She smiles a wide smile that almost looks as if it hurts. She seems so innocent and careless while leaping, now at a quicker pace through the flowers. Her beautiful face begins to contort, seemingly you grow more worried and confused. What the hell are they paying the sound guy? The silence is literally deafening. The woman's face is clearly scared out of her wits now as what looks like her worst fears become reality and she falls to the ground dramatically. Exaggerating her gestures with her hand as though she can't stand the thought of making a mistake, of just falling on the ground.

The sound guy must have suddenly cashed in his paycheck and done his job, because the next thing we hear is..well, I'll let you hear for yourself.

'AAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!! OUCH! STOP IT YOU'RE HURTING ME!!! OOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOWWWWWWWWWWWWWW! STOP! PLEASE STOP! JUST....st-st-stop. You'll never get away..with..this, *gasp*'

The woman's face is shown on the screen once more. This time with no smile or symbol of happiness, but with her face splattered in blood. Her white dress soon is bathed in the red also, all of this stemming from the attack on her face. A man dressed in a black suit, then emerges from the tall field of lowers and trampling them along the way, he draws a piece of paper and makes a checking motion. He then gives a light laugh and straightens his tie as we zoom into his white shirt.

His shirt becomes the ruffled white bed sheets that we witnessed at the very opening of the scene.

BEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEPPPP!

An alarm clock sounds and almost in an automatic motion, an arm extends from the bed to shut it off. The same arm then uncovers the sheets from around his bed and reveals only himself, Theo Knox, in the bed. His slightly tanned skin and black boxers with white hearts seem to compliment each other as he lays there in bed. He runs his shaky hands through his hair then grabs his cell phone on the receiver near the bed. He dials maybe four numbers, then hangs up because of his hands still shaking. He then rubs his face ending by his chin and dials again, managing to keep the shaking to a minimum. The voice at the other end is dry, yet still sweet.

'Theo, what is it now?'

'Ashley, is that you?'

'Why must you continue to call me at all hours of the day? Have you looked at the time recently?'

Theo takes a quick glance towards the clock and reads 6:32 AM. 'Listen Ashley, that doesn't matter. Are you sure that all your doors are locked?'

'Yes, Theodore. I'm positive. Ever since you installed that security system yourself, I haven't been able to even leave my house until 10 minutes of convincing it that I'm me. You know these dreams you're having are ridiculous. They don't make any sense and Mom was always depressed so it wasn't a surprise when she killed herself. How else do you think you got the way you are?'

'Ashley how can you still, after all these years, let yourself believe that worn and torn story? I have dreams, Ashley. Real ones. I don't want to lose you and Elizabeth, you're the only things I have. The thing last thing I need is for you to belittle Mom, she was a good person and she didn't mean to hurt us.'

'Theo, I love you. Lizzie loves you. But when you continue to talk about these dreams that you think happened, I just get a little worried about you. Living alone shouldn't even be an option for you anymore. Have you taken your medicine yet?'

'Ash..you know what that stuff does to me. And I've got to start training today for the CHZ and being on the road, I won't have time to be lonely.'

'I hate this whole idea of the CHZ. When's the off season in that damn sport?'

'You know there's no such thing as in an off season in the CHZ, but I gotta work my way up to the big leagues. And you know we need the money, would you rather see your big brother, Ted flippin' hamburgers?'

'Well, you just might meet more girls with that cute little hat they wear at Burger King.'

'If you think that's good, wait 'til you see the spandex they've got me wearing.'

'Ha, ha, ha. Now you've sold me. Go get 'em today.'

'Can do. You haven't heard from Elizabeth today have you?'

'Go take the medicine Theodore.'

'Have you?'

'No, I haven't but, I'm sure she's okay wherever she is.'

'It's amazing to me how calm you are with a masked murderer on yours and your sister's ass, and how irritable you are when it's that time of the month. God forbid they coincide..'

'BYE, jackass.'

'Ha, ha. I'll see ya sis.'

Theo then hangs up his cell phone and contemplates the well being of his other sister. He picks up the phone to check his voice mail and is soothed by her having left a message.

'Hey Teddy, it's me Lizzie. I'm at my friend, Emily's house, if you didn't pick that up from me telling you all week. If you're having another dream, I'll save you the call to Ashley and tell you to just go take your medicine now. Everything's all right. I love you, buh bye.'

Theo smiles then hangs up the phone a second time. He then pulls himself out of bed and proceeds to the bathroom where a single orange capsule of pills sits on the desk. He splashes some water on his face and into a cup and then pops back a pill as he downs it with some water. He looks in the mirror again and laughs at himself for being so worked up about nothing. It wasn't an everyday thing. Normally his medicine calmed him right down. But lately it's been leaving side effects that aren't normal. The doctors' conclusions were that the side effects were only temporary and/or because of the stress that Theo's been inflicting on himself. So Theo tries to avoid the little things that could make him urk and focuses on the big picture. The little things like..the capsule from which his medicine came from reading:

'From: City of New Orleans medical department
To: Theodore Knox
Directions: Take every 7-9 hours. Additional usage, if needed is suggested but not advised. Should leave recipient feeling refreshed and ready to start a new day. If abused please contact your local poison center for help.
And don't forget to sleep well Theodore, even Mommy was never this stressed out.
