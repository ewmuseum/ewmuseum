The motel room massacre
Written: 26/8/02

::'Yippe kie ay mother fucker!'::

::'Jerry, my man has been screwing around with my dead sisters corpse for the past six....'::

::' Julio angleso aner perasato en Espanyol !'::

Axl flicked through the channels on the television with little hope of finding anything descent worth watching. Then motel room he was staying in was cheap and seedy, and reminded him of the set of a cheap amateur porn movie.

He wanted to sleep, not from tiredness or fatigue but the sheer fact that to him it was the best way to kill time. Yet he knew there was no chance of that right now, he wasn't tired and there was a constant thumping and banging sound coming from the room next to him, not leaving up to the imagination, there was obviously some serious hardcore sexual activity going on next door, or someone was trying their hand at some DIY, yet Axl suspected it wasn't the latter.

He ran his hand through his long black hair, as was a custom with him, and turned the television off. He rested his head back slowly against the soft pillows, and merely lay there staring at the white murky stained ceiling. Once upon a time that ceiling had been white, and how it was now stained in that murky depressing yellow was beyond his knowledge, and his care.

* * * * * *

10 minutes, probably 20, he didn't know how long he had dozed off for because he didn't bother checking the bedside clock. He slowly rose from the bed, noticing the banging from the room next door had ceased, and registered how that could not have been the noise that awoke him.

*Knock Knock*

There it was! Someone was knocking at the door. He got to his feet and stretched his arms slightly as the knocking proceeded once again. He then began to walk to the door, and ran his hand through his hair as usual.

'OK, chill the hell out.....'

He opened the door and took a deep breath as he did so.

'Look I don't want any towels........'

No maid was there to greet him, instead it was a clenched fist connecting with his jaw. He stumbled back hopelessly, it wasn't often that Axl was caught by surprise and off guard, but this was just one of those rare occasions.

'What the fu-'

Another solid punch, this time to his chest, followed by another right hook to the jaw. He flew backwards and onto his bed.

'Stay the fuck down and you just might live through this!'

He had yet to get a good look at his attackers, and he still hadn't got one as he used all his weight to muster and hoist himself to sit upright on the bed.

'You have no idea who you are messing with...'

'Axl Young. Former member of The Cadaver Dogz, currently employed by CHZ....'

He looked up in surprise but tried to hide it, but failed miserably in doing so. He could now see his attackers much more clearly. The man who attacked him now stood in the corner, he was pretty much bald and in his mid thirties. The owner of the voice that was talking to him was coming from a female. She was tall, with long brown hair, and reminded Axl of Sandra Bullock.

'......... and also an underground hit man.'

She smirked as she uttered the last comment. They may know who he is but now what he is capable of.

'Hmmmm, well, the autobiographies out next....'

The bald guy lunged forward and levelled him with a stiff right hand across the face, forcing Axl's head sideways and causing some blood to fly from his mouth, due to a cut in his lip from the blow.

'Enough of the wise cracks Axl'

He brought his head back up, tasting the corner of his mouth where the blood was running.

'OK, so what are you, feds? FBI? CIA?'

'Keep guessing cutie?'

'Rent a thug? Gangsters for hire?'

'Do you want Biggs to answer that question for you?'

Axl looked at Biggs standing in the corner of the room, he was much taller than Axl and he guessed just by looking at him he outweighed him by about 50 pounds.

'Maybe later, I bet old Biggs is a real cowboy once we get a few drinks in him, EH BIGGS!!?'

Biggs stormed forward but the Sandra Bullock look a like held up her hand and gestured for him to step back, which he thankfully did. Biggs then grabbed a chair from the corner of the room, and placed it down carefully for her to sit on at the opposite end of the bed.

'Now Axl, I'm only going to say this once so be sure to pay attention. You can either co-operate with us, and this entire experience will be pain free.'

Axl was now seething at the position he had been placed in, he didn't even know who these people were, and already was in the middle of being given an ultimatum by them.

'Or you can, which I expect you to, not co-operate. In which case, Biggs here will be forced to enforce himself upon you. So........ what's it to be?'

There was a long pause as Axl sat there, and he looked to the window. It was open, thankfully, he knew that window was going to prove useful soon.

'Well that all depends on what you want me to co-operate with?'

'Ha, negotiating as always. I admire that, especially seeing as it's coming from a man whos life fell apart when his girlfriend was murdered.'

'Fuck you whore!'

'Oh, now, I see we have struck a nerve somewhat haven't we Axl? Tanya, that was her name wasn't it?'

Axl took a deep breath and then spat a mixture of phlegm, blood and salvia right into the face of the young Sandra Bullock look a like. Her face cringed as she wiped it away immediately with her sleeve. She then gestured to Biggs with her other hand.

'Biggs!!!'

The tower of a stormed towards Axl , a sly grin on his face as he cowered of the deep dark demented rebel.

* * * * * * * * *

The scene fades out slightly, and fades back in, revealing all three people in the motel room positioned exactly where they were just before the scene faded. Biggs in the corner, the Sandra Bullock look a like sitting on a chair at the bottom of the bed, and Axl sitting on the bed. However this time, his face didn't bear the cool calm and collected appearance it usually did, it was now groggy and covered in small circular blood stains, obviously from someone's knuckles, and his face hung low to the right as he spat more blood from the now even bigger wound on his lip onto the brown motel room carpet. His long black hair hung loosely over his face he reared his head back against the bed board of the bed, and coughed slightly.

'Name!'

'I don't remember'

'Bullshit Axl'

'How about me and you hook up later in the week and discuss this over dinner'

'Haha, nice to see you didn't lose your sense of humour after that beating'

'Well, I do aim to please'

'Well Axl baby, please me, give me the name of the employer who hired you for that hit back in 99'

'What hit?'

'The politician from New Jersey!'

'..................... New Jersey has politicians?'

'BIGGS!'

'I don't remember!'

It was the truth. he remembered the job clearly, but not the employers name, in fact he didn't think he gave a name to Axl.

'Shall I get him Maria?'

Maria shook her head slowly while rubbing her temples with her fingers.

'No...... no.'

Biggs returned to the corner with a disappointed look on his face.

'Axl.......... we need that name, we reallllllly need it! I won't lie to you about that. However we can find the name from someone else, and if you refuse to give it to us we will kill you, I mean we don't want to leave any evidence behind that we contacted you now, would we?'

She smiled menacingly, her threats were empty however, and had no depth and real truth in them.

'Bullshit, only I know who the employer was and he didn't even give me a name'

'OK....'

Maria sighed and gestured for Biggs again, who this time produced a amply sized knife from inside his black suit.

'It's a shame it had to.....'

'NOW ROLAND!'

'What-'

Suddenly. within a split second, Roland the eagle came soaring through the open motel window. They were foolish enough to leave it open. Roland came soaring in and went straight for Biggs, flapping and clinging straight to the mans face as the behemoth of a man staggered around hopelessly punching at the air and dropping the knife.

Axl darted up from the bed and raced towards the fallen knife, the entire time Maria sat in the chair in shock, fright and amazement. Axl quickly retrieved the knife from the floor, and with Roland obscuring Biggs's vision, Axl drove the knife with force into the heart of Biggs. A faint scream then followed from the man as he dropped to his knees, Axl was careful to grip the knife with the sleeve of his black top. Roland then ceased attacking the face of Biggs, which was now severely scarred and bloodied, and perched himself on Axl's right shoulder.

Maria stared up at him, now seeing him at his full stature and the demonic grin that donned his face that made all your life's fears seem meaningless.

'No, n-now look right here..... d-dont y-you!'

Axl chuckled slightly at Maria's scared whimpers through her talking.

'Victims ... aren't we all?'

Roland then soared through the air once again towards Maria, her painful screams were worthless and meaningless as they rang through the night. Then soon her screaming ceased, and her pain on this earth ended as she fell limp on her stomach onto the floor. Her face, pecked away unmercifully by Roland, now made her look nothing like the Sandra Bullock look a like she once was to Axl.

Then as Axl stood, the last man amongst the sea of lost souls desecrated in the motel room, the scene fades to infamous........black.
