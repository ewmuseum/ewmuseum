Salvation: Part 6
Written: 8/03/02

I had the blade lightly pressed against her neck, and all I could think about was freeing her. It felt like a gut check to prove myself to my newly found brothers. I could hear her breaths... slow, steady... and unaware of the threat I was presenting. She looked peaceful for the first time since we met... had I the right to disturb that? I shook off my doubts. I had the right to disturb her sleep... after all, I was sending her to a better place, and proving that I was worthy to be accepted by the demons. I had no choice, and so I decided.

I ran the tip of the blade across her throat, but instantly, she gasped and grabbed my arm. A thin stream of blood ran down her neck, and her eyes bulged wide. She struggled to free herself, looking desperately at me... but I was a wrestler, used to throwing 200lb men across the ring, and she was rather weak compared to them. Though she was bleeding, it wouldn't be enough to kill her... and I knew that.

'Shh,' I urged her in a comforting whisper, 'you're going to a better place now.'

'No!' she screamed and struggled more desperately, but to no avail. I pulled the cross a little ways from her neck and was about to thrust the blade into her throat, but she got a hand up, catching me square in the face and knocking me back. I had underestimated her need for survival as she bolted from the closet. I pushed my hand off the floor to get up, but I felt something digging into my palm. Taking a moment from my chase, I looked down at whatever it was.

The Bible, plated in gold, was staring back at me. I ran my fingers over the emblem, looking at my reflection in the shimmering gold. I didn't see a Disciple, nor did I see myself... but I saw a demon, just like the ones who were calling to me. Could these creatures really be my brothers? Could they really hold me in the light that they said? It is written that they are liars... could I believe them? More doubts filled my head, piercing my thoughts, causing a war in my head much like the one pictured on Christina's back. I tried to make sense of it all... but my time away from reality, trapped in the house, had made that impossible. I was a senseless wrestler, unable to decipher what was happening around me. It was just like my entrance music... I was lost in a maze.

A bright flash illuminated the dark room, right in front of me, and sent me staggering back into the closet. I looked upon the face of a man wearing a golden crown, kneeling in front of me with a large blade in his hands. It was the same man I had seen days before, summoned by Christina.

'Michael,' I stuttered out in a shocked whisper. My system wasn't prepared to handle my current situation, let alone a saint right in front of me.

'Matthew,' he spoke in a soft, but stern voice, 'why do you defy your father?'

I bit my lip, trying to think of a good answer. 'I... I do not defy him,' was the best I could do.

Michael only slowly shook his head. 'Matthew, do you think that I cannot see through your deception? I am blessed with the holy spirit brother, your words fall on deaf ears. You have forsaken your father, yet you believe the fallen angel in that he has forsaken you? Did he not send his only son to this world to die upon the cross for your sins? Did his son not suffer a thousand times what you are now, for you? Yet, even through this, you say that your father has forsaken you?'

I had no answer, and the truth bit down like a viper. 'I... forgive me Michael,' I said, lowering my head a little. I had never felt like such an idiot as I did in that moment.

'Seek not my forgiveness Matthew, seek that of God's. Know that he is with you, even now. Know that you must help Christina, for she too is a child of God. Keep her safe, and know that only through faith can you hope to defeat this plague.'

With that, Michael faded from sight, and I was stuck pondering his words. I had regained my senses, that was for sure, and I couldn't understand what had gotten into me. I mean... ever feel like that? When you were extremely mad and said something, or drunk and did something? I felt about ten times as bad, and it made me feel weak. In an attempt to strengthen my faith a little, I prayed, and I prayed for a long time. I begged for forgiveness, asking God for guidance and protection. I praised him for the light he gave me, and I asked him to protect Christina until I could find her. When I was done, it felt as if the weight of the world had been lifted off my shoulders... and it probably was.

I scampered down the stairs with my leather cloak swaying behind me. I brushed my red hair out of my eyes and scanned the living room. Nothing. I turned the corner and scanned the kitchen. Nothing. Cursing a little, I pulled out my cross, which had its blade concealed, and turned another corner. There was a hallway, and I searched the various rooms. No spawns of Hell in the bathroom, guest room or computer room. Just as I was about to give up... I noticed an indentation in the wall which concealed another door. Without a moment's hesitation, I swung the thing open and made my way down the long stairs. In a few moments... I stood alone in the cold cellar, or so I thought.

I pulled out a flashlight and as soon as I flicked it on, I saw the face of one of the demons. My heart did a disco dance and jumped into my throat for a second, and I stumbled back. I shone the light down from its emotionless face and found an unconscious Christina laying beside it. She was still bleeding from the neck, because of me, and I felt pretty bad right there. I had to ignore it though... since I had to get the demon away from her, and being attacked by my conscience wouldn't help.

'You're here,' it stated in my mind, 'Good. Do what must be done and join us, brother.'

'Sure thing,' I muttered as I walked towards her with the cross. Before any of us could react, the blade shot out from the cross and I drove it into the demon's chest. Though it's expression didn't change, I could feel it's pain as my hand got too close to it. I could hear the tortured screams of its agony as it burst into flames and became nothing more than a pile of ashes. It was a quicker and easier death that I thought it would have been... but I was glad that it was.

I heard a scream beside me, and instantly, I knelt down beside Christina. She was kicking and whimpering, and her back was glowing. I pulled her up and lifted the back of her T-shirt up and noticed something remarkable. The demons in the picture were slowly being driven back, and Heaven, was advancing down her back. It only lasted a few moments, but when it was over, I knew what it all meant.

She looked at me in fear, as if she was about to frantically escape... but she only needed one look in my eyes. I wasn't going to hurt her... and she burst into tears. It made me wish that I had left her asleep, and if there was one thing I thought about that was true, when I was a tad bit insane earlier, it was that she didn't deserve this.

'Now what?' she asked, wincing in pain as the glowing on her back slowly faded. I gave her a reassuring smile and helped her to her feet.

'Well... I figure I'll kick some demon ass and go from there.'
