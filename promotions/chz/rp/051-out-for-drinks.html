Out For Drinks
Written: 7-9-02

*The cameras begin to roll outside a local bar in the streets of Saskatoon, Saskatchewan. There is a few people walking out holding onto the person next to them and finding a cab to drive home in. The cameras go inside to see the bar is not really full but its not dead either. Women walk around in their mini skirts and tight tops. The men all glance and point out the ones they intend on taking home. The men are mostly in casual dress a t-shirt & jeans. But one man sticks out, one man just doesn’t seem to fit the mold and that man is Don Rossi. He sits in the corner of the bar he has a drink in his hand resting on the table & in the other a hand full of his hair, propping up his head. Some people look at him and shake their heads but the one that catches Dons attention is the bartender. She had to have been there for 10 hours already and she seems dead on her feet. Her hair is still up in a ponytail neatly, but her face has become flush and her eyes seem to close more and more with every passing minute. She calls for one of the waitresses to go behind the bar as she walks out and sits at the table across from Don.*

Sunny~ Well honey whats it gonna be? You need a taxi or you plan on holding that same drink for another 5 hours? What happened did your wife die? Lose your job? It happens to us all sweetie don’t you worry about it. Your in great shape I am sure a lumber yard could use a man like you.

Don: Well to be honest I am not married, I have one of the best jobs a man could ever want. This drink, it sucks. Anyways I don’t drink, I figure you come to a bar to forget something and the best way to do it is alcohol. I don’t even drink so you can have it. I needed a place to kill some time, so I came here and the more I sat here the more I got to thinking. Maybe I should do this more often?

Sunny~ Well I guess you have it all figured out. Say what do you do? No job can be that great that you consider it the best. What do you bounce for those bums down the street?

Don: Sunny right? I think its time for you to get back behind the bar the alcohol is getting to you out here. Maybe even some sleep would do you good. Don’t you own this place? Why not close down for the night and get some sleep because I am no bouncer. I am a professional wrestler. I am in the CHZ the federation that kept Canada going right the last year.

Sunny~ If you have such a great job and its doing well then what you have to think about? Its not like any of it matters its all fake.

Don: I will let you take this time to leave before I blow up on you. I am a really nice guy but when people throw that fake shit in my face I want to hit someone. Sunny if you have the time on Wednesday tune in for the show and you will see just how fake it is ok?

*Sunny gets up from the table and walks back to the bar and begins to tend to some people. Don sits at the table and looks at the glass that is sitting on the table and as he takes it in his hand he shakes his head and drinks it really fast.*

Don: No point in paying for it if your not gonna drink it Don. Chris Matthews got what he needed last week. The old days he talked so much trash and now he got what he deserved. I was out looking for people to bring to the Neo Extremists and I got some of the best that I could find. Hell I will even tell ya now more are on the way. When will they come to the surface? Who knows. Hell it could be next week or a month from now. This week Chris I will take your already beaten body and break it down even more. The man that you once feared will give you even more of a reason to fear me after this week. I have been waiting for this moment Chris and now that I have the chance to make an example out of you one on one I will take it and show the world that you are nothing but a shadow here in the new CHZ. You were once on top of the world hell you came into the CHZ and within weeks you had claimed the TV Title, & the North American Title but now that is all about to change. Why? Because your not being pushed by the owners and I am getting the proper respect I deserved before. I am now in the main event slot yet again and I will stay there till the day I decide to retire. Last week I made an example out of Cassius Deamont for interrupting me and this week if he gets in my way I will do the same again. I am not a man to attack another for the sake of attacking him to cause problems but when another man steps in on my time you bet your ass he will have some problems to deal with. You see Cassius when I was out there making my return speech you had the audacity to interfere and when you did I saw that you were going to be the next Keyser Soze. You were going to be the man that would try to get in my way and oppose me. But I have that all figured out because like the last time I will be the man to survive the onslaught. Cassius you started a war you will never be able to finish and you can then run back to your damn RUSH and tell them of the man that kicked their champions ass all over the Canadian soil. I am not the kind to make false claims so Deamont take my words to heart your going to fail this mission and that is a promise.

*Rachel walks by the table and grabs Dons glass and as she walks away in her skirt Don looks at her behind shaking back and forth. As she gets to the bar she looks back at him and he signals for her to go outside. She walks to the back door and steps out after talking to Sunny. Don gets up from the table and follows her outside, the camera fades to black as Don begins to talk to her.*
