Case of the Missing Combatant Part 2
Written: 21/07/2002

Well, I found nothing out of the ordinary at the Primetime Wrestling Academy Headquarters, well, anything to do with the case anyway but I was hoping to solve it or my name wasn't Dick Tracy, which of course it isn't. I dropped the stressed out grappler at his hotel before I started my investigation at the Primetime Academy. For a guy of his size and muscle compacity I wouldn't expect him to be the emotional type but he was an absolute wreck when he walked into my office pleading for help. I took his case not only out of the goodness of my heart but a man has got to eat.

I currently had no leads or clues to help me solve this case so I decided to go down town to a little bar known as 'The Shocka'. When I am not working that is where I spend most of my time talking to the owner of the bar and my good friend, Joey Fingers. Not only is he good for a laugh once in a while but he also provides with me with all the hot details he picks up from the customers he serves. I owe a lot to Joey and he knows that always nagging me about each time I walk in.

After securing myself a parking spot right out front I left my car making sure I had the doors locked. It was a nice little bar but it was located in a bad part of town and if I counted the number of cars that have been broken into I would have an easier time counting the stars in the universe.

As I pushed open the big steel door a gust of smoke blew out into the cold night air. I stepped into the bar and was treated to the delights of some heavy metal music playing in the background, men playing pool while taking a sip from their beers and the sight of good old Joey Fingers tending the bar. Joey looks more like a biker for the Hell's Angels with a thick beard and a big expanding gut, which is covered by his white apron. He sees me walking over and flings his rag over his right shoulder as he extends his hand to shake mine.

'Hey buddy.' Joey says in a friendly fashion, 'Long time no see, what's been keeping you busy?'

'The usual Joey.' I said as I took a seat on one of the bar stools and removed my hat so to let my smooth brown hair get some oxygen, although that is rare in a place like this. 'I have been trying to land a good case and I finally got one last night.'

'Oh really?' Joey questions as he takes the rag from off his shoulder and begins to wipe the bar clean while still listening to me.

'Yeah,' I said, pouring myself a glass of vodka while doing so, 'I am working for a pro-wrestler whose tag team partner has gone missing. I checked out the Primetime Wrestling Academy where he and his partner were going to make their debut but nothing.'

Joey then stops wiping and raises his head looking as if he is thinking about something. I cannot help but notice this as I down my drink.

'The Primetime Wrestling Academy you say?' Joey asks as he thinks to himself, scratching his chin. 'I heard of them. A few students from their come over here for drinks now and then.'

I perk up after hearing this thinking that maybe Joey has some information that I do not know of yet. I decided to show him the picture of the missing Hugh Harley and see what he thinks. I grabbed the picture from my pocket and tossed it on to the counter. Joey picked it up and stared at it.

'The chubby with the big arms is the one who has gone missing.' I told him hoping he may have seen him or have information on him, 'His name is Hugh Harley and is teamed up with Jack Murdock, they are known as the Hawaiian Punches.'

Joey scratches his chin once again taking in the information I just gave to him.

'Yeah,' Joey says starting to remember something, 'I saw this guy and his partner come in here for a round of beers after they came in to town. Hugh Harley said that they just gotten into town and were going to be training at the PWA.'

Yes! Now I was starting to get somewhere but I decided to question Joey further for some more information.

'Can you tell me what happened the day those two came in?' I asked hoping maybe that whatever happened that day could lead me to my first clue or even solving the case.'

'Well these two came in here and had a couple of drinks.' Joey began as I listened carefully and finished my glass of vodka, 'They were having a good time and such drinking and laughing. After that they began a game with pool and.....'

Joey trailed off and we both sat in a silence for a couple of seconds.

'...And?' I chimed in trying to get Joey to spill the remainder of what he trailed off with.

'And he then got into a fight with one of my new assistants,' Joey said which narrowed down a suspect, 'Tony, I think his name is.'

'Why were they fighting?' I asked trying to get more info on my lead.

Joey then hands back the picture but keeps talking to me.

'I guess Tony said wrestling was fake and any one could do that crap and Hugh took exception to it.' Joey said, continuing to clean the bar. 'Hugh then shoved Tony down and everyone started to laugh at him including Hugh and Jack.'

I now had my very first suspect. This Tony guy who apparently was embarrassed in front of everybody by Hugh Harley himself. He could have kidnapped Hugh at of revenge for making him look like a fool. I poured myself another shot of Vodka and quickly guzzled that down. I then grabbed my hat and placed it on my head and threw my money on to the table for the two shots.

'Thanks for the insight Joey.' I said as I got from my stool ready to leave, 'You take care of yourself, all right?'

'You too Durke.' Joey said and then returned to his bar tending duties.

I now had some clue of what I was in for now and now I could get down to business. I knew who Tony was already as he use to work at the Bowling Alley underneath of my apartment. In fact he actually helped me move in and we always talked when he was working there. Bits and pieces of the puzzle were starting to come together but the question is.... Are they all from the same box?
