A Discovered Talent!
Written: 8/27/02

A big week is ahead in the likes of the Canuck Heat Zone federation. The ordinary Wild will be held on Wednesday. But the same week on Saturday, the very first Pay per view will be held. Angel “The Texan” has been scheduled in both events. This week will be the combination of her hard work. This week is a time for hard work and strategy to come in play.

At the moment Angel has Mind Games set to the back of her head, and focusing on her upcoming match on Wild. The big four-way match between Soul, Axl Young and Tyler Van Serio will be a great match. With Soul the newest member, Angel does not know what to except from the rookie. Also with her first match-up against Tyler Van Serio, she has studied some of his matches, but still will have her eyes open for anything that may come her way. Angel is very aware of Axl Young, a recent battle between the two have kept Angel on her toes. A draw between the two last weeks was the combination of their being. Two very skilled wrestlers that cannot seem to part, as the commissioner and the fans like the battle between the two young stars. This week should be known different, it will be a free for all, and who ever wants it the most should succeed.

A great sunny day in Montreal, PQ has erupted. A beautiful day, for the likes of Canadian Football, and this particular Monday night football, Angel was chosen too entertain the fans by singing the Canadian National Anthem. A newly discovered talent of Angel was announced, not only being a beautiful, athletic, and now a talented singer. In her younger years, Angel took both singing and dance lessons as a child, and now finally will come in handy. Angel had been awaiting the call to be sent to the center of the field, to sing the Canadian anthem, as she has been waiting in the back locker room. Finally after about 30 minutes got the call from the programming Coordinator.

The crowd rises to their feet, mostly males, holding a beer in one hand and a program in the other. As they hoot the sexy wrestling star the announcer introduces Angel.

“ And now, to sing our National anthem, she is known for being a female with attitude in the Canuck Heat Zone federation. She is beautiful and willing. She is the one and only, Angel “The Texan” Summers!”

The crowd roars at a fast pace, the announcer hands over the microphone to Angel, and begins stepping a side leaving Angel with some space. Then after clearing her throat, as well as raising the microphone, Angel begins the anthem.

“O Canada!
Our home and native land!
True patriot love in all thy sons command.
With glowing hearts we see thee rise,
The True North strong and free!
From far and wide,
O Canada, we stand on guard for thee.
God keep our land glorious and free!
O Canada, we stand on guard for thee.
O Canada, we stand on guard for thee.”
With all smiles, Angel yells after the anthem into the mic!
“Go Montreal!”

Which gets the crowd going, and the cheers continue several seconds after Angel disappears back into the locker room. A great moment for Angel, not being shy helps her confidence to be sky high.

Angel quickly dabbed her forehead with a nearby towel, and proceeds to the front row seats she was given for singing the anthem. She shared this moment with her friend Robyn. They enjoyed their first live experience in Football as the Montreal Alouettes won the game, and enhanced their record to 10-1.
After a great day of the field, and signing several autographs on her way out, she followed her football experience with heading to the gym. For the first time her friend Robyn tagged along in her work out as she stated.

“I am getting a little soft.”
They both had a long work out of 3 hours, mostly endurance work-out on the bike. They followed that with a quick dinner at a nearby restaurant. Then they slowly walked home, and edged off to bed.
