Salvation: Part 7 (Finale)
Written: 8/05/02

You think a lot when you're in complete darkness. Maybe it's because all your senses have no more functions... or maybe it's because it's our nature to be afraid of the dark, and we need something to occupy out time. In any case, in the stilled darkness of the cellar, all I could do was think. The images of the demons ran through my mind most of all. Their dark red skin, leathery and cracked... covering their entire body. The raven-black hair that looked like shining tenticles, draping behind their otherwise bald heads. Eyes without pupils, just completely black, always staring emotionlessly at you. Fangs, claws, bat-like wings... the whole hellish nine yards. In truth, they scared me a little, but I believed God was with me, and with his hand guiding mine, I could come to no harm.

So I felt around in the darkness, and found Christina's hand. I guided her grip to my leather cloak, and she clasped on, hard. I couldn't see any better than her... but if anyone was to be hurt, it should be me. Besides, after the experiences with the carving on her back and the demons... she could use a break.

I slowly cerpt up the stairs, hoping to make no sounds. I didn't so much as take a breath, nor did I brush my hair out of my face. I just bit my lower lip and took that first step on the aged, wooden stairs. It was fine, and I took a second step, a third... slowly making my way up the stairs. Suddenly, I heard it, shattering the silence like fragile glass. A loud creak... making me bite down on my lip harder. I closed my eyes and felt my heart do a jig, and lodge into my throat. I waited, and continued.

We made it to the top where everything was colored a pale blue because of the darkness and the full moon shining out the window. The hallway, in truth, was worse than the dark cellar. There were too many doors and too many rooms to hie in. The eyes you always felt on you... you just couldn't know where they came from in here. I wanted to get out, so I walked a little faster across her carpet, but came to an abrupt stop.

Christina ran into my back and remained close, breathing down the back of my neck. I heard the breathing... but it wasn't hears. No, it was slower, deeper... and I could hear the faind sound of a hiss in the breaths. I could feel it waiting, for me, for her... just sitting there with its emotionless face. The very thought sent a chill down my spine, but I ignored it and concentrated on the slow breaths.

With a loud slam, I drove the blade of my cross into the door. Images entered my mind, flashing, blinking, screaming like a symphony of tortures souls. It was intense and I frantically tried to release the cross, but I couldn't. I could feel the creature's pain, every moment of it... from the torture to the agony... it all entered my mind. How could I ever see such a thing as a brother? It amazed me how deluded I was, and as the flashes despersed and I fell back against the wall... I knew my place was not with them.

I realized that I was holding my breath and let out a long sigh, taking in lung-fulls of air. Christina was kneeling beside me, but I gave her a reasurring smile. From under the door game a great light as the demon behind it burst into flames.

For the first time... I saw Christina's poor decorating job. Her carpet had strange patterns on it and was colored purple, while her walls were painted red and black. The doors remained the color of the wood they were made from... and sensing the need for a little humor in the tense situation, I decided to take the moment to create some.

I looked over at her and gave her a mischievious grin. 'Someone got an F in decoration class eh?' I said with a sly laugh.

Christina blinked her blue eyes and smirked. 'What's wrong with the way I decorate my house?' she demanded.

My grin widened. 'Oh, nothing. The giant bruise look goes well with the family pictures,' I laughed and coiled up as she gave me a slap on the shoulder.

'Hey, that's mean!'

I laughed again and slowly got to my feet. It was nice to see her smile and giggle, which I hadn't seen since the first day we had met. I knew that we were in a dangerous situation, but still... her laugh lifted some weight off my shoulders. When I was a tad bit insane earlier, I thought about a lot of things, mostly things insane people would think of. Yet, the one thing I believed, which was correct, was that she didn't deserve this.

'Wait here,' I asked, placing my hand on her shoulder, 'I can feel one more of them, and it's close. You'll be safer if you stay here.'

Her eyes began to plead with my request, but she saw it would be no use. She slumped against the wall, but instantly began to scream. I darted back to where she was laying and saw that a faint glow was coming from her back. I turned her over and lifted up the back of her shirt to see, as I saw before, the picture moving. The detailed carvings on her back began depicting 'The War', and as before when I killed the last demon, Heaven was beating back Hell. It looked as if the battle was at the mouth of Hell now... and I could feel the end nearing.

As the glowing subsided and Christina breathed a sigh of relief, I again slowly got to my feet and made my way into the kitchen. The dim light coming through the window provided just enough shadows to keep me on my toes. My eyes had to adjust a little, but squinting did me just fine as I stepped towards the counter.

Instantly, a cold blare crossed my vision and my sight blurred. I staggered back and slammed my head against the oven door. I ignored the pain though and patted my cloak for my cross. I saw it down the hallway in the door and cursed. When I returned my gaze to the counter, there was standing one of the demons... and I could tell this one apart. It was the demon who had been in my head, tempting me, creating that lust to belong inside. I hated it, I wanted to send it back to Hell... but I knew I couldn't defeat it like that. Compassion, love, a fair judgment... that was the only way to defeat evil, and in truth, it sucked. It's hard to not hate something like that.

'Why have you given up on your quest?' it whispered inside my head, still sounding every bit like rustling leaes as it did before. 'I offered my hand to you, brother, and you refused it.'

I glared at the thing and clenched my fists. 'I saw a little light demon. Hell, I was blinded by it, and when the light cleared and the dust settled... I saw your offer for what it really was. So punk, I'll tell you once. You want some of this? I might be a man of God, but I'm also a wrestler of God... and I'll beat yo' as into the ground Blitz style! Bring it!'

The demon smiled... which wasn't what I wanted. As if on que, my surroundings morphed in front of me, and I found myself in an arena. I was standing in the middle of the ring, looking around at the fans. They were humming with excitment and I was a little confused about it all. Was this in my head? Was I dreaming or something? I was beyond confusion, but then one sound sent a chill down my spine, making me even shake a little.

Pyro exploded and the fans erupted. 'Southtown' by POD blared over the speakers. It pounded, and the arena nearly lost its roof. The lights began to flash and blink, and as I looked on the screen just above the ramp, I saw one word blare over the screen. 'Spectre'.

Instantly, the man who I feared as much as I hated stepped through and made his way down the ramp. He was exactly as I remembered him. Same attire, same shades, same hair and a golden belt around his waist. He slid into the ring and before even waiting for the bell, he drove a hard knee into my chest.

My wind left me as I felt a hard stomp to the back of my head. The bell sounded and my brother continued to stomp me down. I couldn't so much as think, let alone contemplate what was happening. He pulled me up and tossed me into corner, beating me down like I wsa a rag doll. Every shot hit its mark, and even through the ref warned him to watch his fists, Spectre didn't care. He nailed me with a few stomps and pulled me out of the corner, whipping me around and sending me through the ropes, right into the ring pole. I held my shoulder and fell to the outside, throbbing with pain, shuddering at what my brother had in store for me.

Spectre followed me out and I desperately tried to hit him with a sot of my own, but he blocked it and executed a perfect spine buster onto the thin mat. I could feel the cement through my back and the knee he drove into the side of my head didn't quell the pain any.

'You're nothing Matt! Look at you, just another name on the list... just another statistic on my record. I told you bro, I'm as good as it gets,' he decdlared as he continued to beat me down. I could feel my strength failing me as the world began to grow dark. He hiot me with move after move, hold after hold and shot after shot. I was having toruble breathing and seeing. I knew my face mush have been swollen shut by then.

Soon enough, the ref pushed too much and Spectre knocked him to the ground. He pulled me up and shoved me into the corner, wraping his cold grip around my neck and squeezing the life out of me. I knew my brother well enough to know that he wouldn't let go.

The ring and arena faded, and I found myself in the living room, pinned against the wall by the demon who's hands were around my neck. I could see myself losing to my brother again... as if fate had already written out what would happen to me. I forced my eyes open to look at the demon, who had that cold grin on its face.

'Where is your god now?' it hissed inside my head. My arms slumped to my side and I felt something move in the baggy sleeves of my cloak. I looked up at it, and glared right into its dark eyes.

'Nowhere near where you're going,' I choked out as I shot my arms out into the air. The objects on my sleeves erupted out of the opnenings, and revealing themsevles to be shimmering, golden lined daggers. I snatched the handles from the air before they sped away from me and before the red creature could react, I plunged the blades into its throat.
