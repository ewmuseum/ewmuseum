My Obsession...My Love..My Title...It's Gonna be All Mine! {Part 2}
Written: 8/19/02

We left off where Tyler was talking about all different things like his love, obsession and the National Title, Serio has gotten over his talking and has become normal again as he steps into his hotel room inn Britsh Columbia.

'Scary s*** these days in this world,' says Serio.

He jumps on his bed and is about to close his eyes as his phone rings.

'What the...' he says in a low voice as he walks over to the ringing phone.

He picks it up.

'Hello?' he asks.

'Hello, Tyler,' says the lady.

'Who is this?' asks Serio.

'Remember the lady you bumped into on the street?' asks the lady.

'Yes,' says Serio.

'Well, that person was me,' says the lady.

'Listen, I didn't mean too...' says Serio panicking.

'Don't worry, I'm just here to tell you something very important,' says the lady.

'Ya, what is it?' asks Serio.

'Well, I'm your sister,' says the lady.

'Um, I never had a sister,' Serio says puzzled.

'That's what you think. I was seperated from the family after about 4 months at a Carnival,' says Serio's sister.

'What? Just come up to my room,' Serio says as he hangs up.

About 2 minutes later, Serio here's a knock at his door. Serio opens the door and the lady walks in and sits on the bed.

'Hello Brother,' says Serio's sister.

'Why did you come to B.C.?' asks Serio.

'I leave here, I want to be at your match at Wild to cheer you on when you defeat that son of a bitch Xander Crave,' she says.

'This is some scary s***!' Serio says walkign over to the phone.

'What are you doing?' aks Serio's sister.

'I'm phoning Anthony, this is some scary s***!' Serio says.

'He's not in town, he's in Italy, training for his new wrestling federation,' she says.

'He is?' says Serio, 'I thought he retired?'

'No,' she says, 'Last month he moved to Italy with his midget.'

'He has a midget?' asks Serio.

'Ya, some 3 Feet Pete or something,' she says.

'OK,' says Serio.

'My name I will not tell, my age neither,' says the lady.

'Wait.. Wait wait...' says Serio.

Serio walks around.

'I'm starting to remember something, when I was young,' he says.

Serio takes a couple of more steps.

'I remember the carnival,' he says, 'I remember teh crowd, I remeber mom screaming, dad trying to come her down, me not knowing, Anthony not caring.'

Serio turns around the turns back and looks into his sister's eyes.

'Sara!' he says.

Sara just sits there.

'Sara, why didn't you ever go back to mom or dad? Why weren't you at there funeral's?' asks Serio, 'Did you not know?'

'I knew,' says Sara, 'I didn't care.

'What?' says Serio.

'I didn't care about mom or dad, I barely knew them,' she says.

'Don't you remember anything?' asks Serio.

'No, I don't even know my actual last name. I'm known as Sara Renoff,' Sara says.

'It's Serio!' says Serio.

'Really?' she asks.

'Yes!' Serio says.

Sara stares.

'What?' asks Serio.

'I don't know,' says Sara.

'Keep Renoff though,' says Serio.

'I was gonna,' she says.

Serio stares at her.

'What?' asks Sara.

'You look like...' Serio says.

'Like what?' asks Sara.

'You look like mom,' Serio says.

'I do?' asks Sara.

'Yes, like mom,' Serio says.

'I never knew what mom looked like,' Sara says.

'You never knew her,' says Serio, 'It's not your fault.

'I know,' she says.

'How did you end up in B.C.?' asks Serio.

'My family moved here,' Sara says.

'Why?' asks Serio.

'I don't know,' she says, 'I was only 3.'

'But when we lost you, you were 3,' says Serio.

'I must of moved after the carnival,' says Sara.

'Did you like your parents?' asks Serio.

'No, they always beat me, with belts, ropes name it, they beat me with it,' she says.

'Are you alright?' asks Serio.

'Yes,' says Sara.

'Good, because nothing is going to happen to my sister,' says Serio.

Serio stares at her.

'Hey, you know how you wanted to be at my match to cheer me on?' asks Serio.

'Ya,' says Sara.

'Well, How about becoming my manager?' asks Serio.

'I don't know,' says Sara.

'It's your choice,' says Serio.

'I'll think,' says Sara.

Sara gets up and leaves the room. Serio looks around and phones his brother in Italy.

The scene ends.
