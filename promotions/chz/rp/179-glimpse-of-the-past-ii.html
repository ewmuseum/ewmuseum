Glimpse Of The Past(II)
Written: 09/02/02

“I’ll show that n****rs family what it’s like to lose someone close to you.”

The last statement made by an angered filled teenager. After witnessing the horrific act made by one Earl Morris, Blake has vowed to himself to get revenge. After the young man exited his house he sprinted down the road to a phone booth and entered it. Ripping the phone book off the small coiled cord it was attached to, Blake begins searching for the name of his mother’s killer. He reaches the ‘M’ section of the phone book and begins looking for the name the culprit. He finally comes upon the list of names with the last being Morris.

“Where the hell is this Earl Morris…?” Blake states in rage as he slams his free fist into the keypad of the phone. His fist turns blood red and begins to give a slight burning sensation after hitting the phone.

The search continues on the second page of names. Blake runs his finger down the list and spots one Earl Morris whose domicile is on Portsmouth road. Blake doesn’t hesitate to drop the phone book and exits out of the glass phone booth. The young angered youth looks up at a street sign to see where he is at so he can get an idea of which way to head. The sign reads Florist drive.

“Portsmouth is only two blocks away.” Blake tells himself as he begins walking to his destination. His face is red, red as the face of Satan in the flaming dungeons of hell. His anger and taste for redemption is blocking out his sense of reasoning. Because of that this situation may end up more drastic then what it should have been

It is still fairly bright out on this humid summer day. People are still outside enjoying the day and many children are still out playing ball or some other activity. Blake pays attention to none of this. The thoughts of today’s happenings can’t help but repeat through his mind non stop. With each time the thought passes, more anger builds inside. The anger flowing through his veins right now should be enough for him to just spontaneously combust. He reaches the end of the street and crosses without thought, not realizing that this intersection has no stops signs. As he walks across an old Ford station wagon is forced to pull a complete stop. With the tires squealing, smoke coming from the tires and black skid marks being left on the road, Blake still pays no attention to the vehicle. The driver rolls down his window and sticks his head out. He is a wearing a John Deere baseball cap and has a scruffy looking brown beard. He begins to yell obscenities towards Blake. Young Raines turns his head and spits on the hood and continues along his path. He walks a few more feet then stops as if there was some object blocking his way. He pivots to his right and faces a small two story red brick house. His eyes narrow and blood vessels behind his eyes begin to pop, making them bloodshot.

“They say the initial blow hurts, but payback is always a bigger bitch.” Blake says to himself in a moderate tone of voice as he heads up the pathway to the front steps and up to the front door.

The windows of the house are open and some old soul music can be heard. From a window on the second story you can hear a young infant cry. Blake rings the doorbell and stands there waiting for it to be answered. The old wooden door swings open and a fairly overweight woman wearing an apron stands before him. She looks up at young Blake with concern in her eyes.

“Can I help you young man?” The woman asks as she places her hands on her hips awaiting a reply.

“Yes I believe you can. You see earlier today my cat ran away from home. He is an orange tabby and his name is Canton. Have you seen him?” Blake asks calmly, shadowing his anger and rage.

“I don’t know sugar we see lots of cats stroll around here. You have a picture?” She responds as Blake reaches into his back jeans pocket.

“Yes I do actually. I have the picture right here in my wallet.” Blake states as he pulls the knife out from his back pocket.

Before the woman can realize what is happening, Blake grabs her by the back of the neck and jams the butcher knife into her abdomen. The women’s eyes and mouth open wide. Blake forces her forward and into the house. He slams the door behind him with his foot and removes the knife. He then places the knife along her jugular vein and glides it across, making her bleed ferociously.

“Your bastard n****r of a husband killed my mother knocking off a corner store probably because your welfare cheque didn’t come in.” Blake whispers into her ear as she slowly dies.

He stands up and heads upstairs towards the crying child’s room. He walks down the narrow, blue carpeted hallway until he reaches a door at the end of the hall which is opened slightly. He pushes it open and walks towards a small white cradle where a baby who doesn’t even look to be six months old is lying down and crying. He takes the tip of the knife blade and gently lays it on the child’s stomach. He twirls it gently not want to inflict pain on the infant…yet.

“It’s ok little guy. Your welfare collecting parents are dead now. Those burdens on today’s society are going to where they belong, hell. Now it’s your turn to join them and the rest of you worthless n****rs.” Blake says as he lifts up the knife and places it along the infant’s throat.

Before anything can happen a loud crashing sound is heard. The front door was kicked down by three cops who were called to the scene when told what happened by a neighbour who saw the first killing. The quickly run upstairs as they hear the baby crying, thinking it’s in pain. They run down the hall and take aim at Blake who still has the knife to the child’s throat. Two of the officers kneel down while the other remains standing up.

“DROP THE WEAPON KID YOU DON’T WANT TO DO THIS!” The standing cop yells at Blake trying to persuade him to drop the knife. Blake doesn’t acknowledge.

“Your wrong, this little bastard’s father killed my mother today…he deserves to die just like his parents, so I am going to do what’s right.” Blake responds in a calm voice which has an eerie feeling to it.

In desperation one of the kneeling cops shoots the arm of Blake that has the knife. He immediately drops it and falls to the ground holding his arm. The officers move in to cuff him and succeed. They stand Blake up and escort him out to the cruiser waiting out front. They force him into the back with one of the officers and the other two take their seats in the front. The car then drives off down the road towards police headquarters. The scene slowly goes to white and we see Blake Raines as he is today, seven years later.

“That’s my story. I got seven years in prison for doing something that would help society out in the long run. You see Derek I wouldn’t be here right now if it wasn’t for my brother. If he testified I would have gotten life. I believe with the shit I went through, I can help your organization.” Blake exclaims to a figure sitting at a desk.

The figure at the desk is bald. He has a rather muscular build in his upper body and the swastika tattooed on the back of his head. He has cold blue eyes and no facial hair. He presents many scars on his let and right arms indicating he has been through a lot of battles and he probably has more on his body. Derek steps up from his chair and shakes Blake’s hand.

“The skinheads are always looking for new members and someone with your smarts and abilities would be greatly welcomed. Welcome aboard soldier.” Derek says with an arrogant smile on his face.

Both men exchange sadistic grins while shaking each others hand.

“Twenty-Five years old…and I am going to do something that will help further the advancement of the white race.” Blake states with passion and meaning.
