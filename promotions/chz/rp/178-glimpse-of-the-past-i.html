Glimpse Of The Past(I)
Written: 09/02/02

Have you ever felt alone? Of course you have, everyone has. Sometimes the loneliness is only a temporary thing but sometimes it can last a lifetime. Loneliness leaves a void in all of us, makes us feel like something important is missing. For Blake Raines this void has remained since that dreaded night in October of 1996. At eighteen years of age and coming from a broken home, things only got worse. Something like this will change anyone and most of the time it’s for the worst and not the better.

“Blake, I am just going out to pick up some stuff for dinner. You need anything while I am out?” A soft voice of a middle aged woman speaks at the bottom of an old wooden staircase of a two story house.

“No, just do your thing and get back here. I’m hungry.” A male in his late teen replies angered. He sits there on a torn lime green sofa watching television which right not is showing a Pepsi ad.

“What about Jeremy? Does he need anything?” The woman responds as she places her beige purse over her right shoulder.

“He’s asleep bitch! Just go get the shit you need for dinner.” Blake responds annoyed at his mothers continued barrage of questions.

“You wouldn’t talk to me like that if your father was still alive.” The mother yells to her soon who is upstairs. She turns around to the front door, turns the knob and steps out onto the front porch. Her jet black hair blows around as the cool fall breeze while she closes the door behind her and heads over to the car in the paved driveway.

It’s mid afternoon and the autumn breeze is blowing in full force. Blake could care less about the weather or anything that didn’t directly affect him. Blake lies back on the couch as he watches his wrestling program. The look on his face would give anyone the impression that right now he has no care in the world; he is just a simple teen who does his own thing. He does attend school, he does have homework but will he do it? No he won’t, that’s just the way he is and he doesn’t care what other people think of him.

Jeremy on the other hand was the exact opposite of his older brother. He was very well behaved, achieved good grades in school and was always willing to make a new friend or make someone feel welcome. This attitude drove Blake up the wall and he could never stand it. This person Jeremy is often got him beaten by his brother. The worst incident was a year ago when he was thrown into a bookcase that collapsed and broke his arm. Blake was always one to target the people who acted caring and friendly a good chunk of the time and no matter how often Jeremy has been hurt by his brother, he still continues to act the way he wants to.


A little over an hour ago Jeremy returned from his buddy William’s house. The two played baseball with some other kids for a couple hours which made him a little tired and that’s why he is taking a nap on the small futon in his room. He always has one of his afternoon naps around this time. Blake likes this time of day not because wrestling is on but because his annoying little brat of a brother is asleep and will not bother him.


“Fucking seven at night and she still isn’t home. She left at four and that bitch isn’t even back yet.” Blake comments in rage while looking at his watch. Blake keeps it on the channel he was watching wrestling on earlier. The intro to the evening news starts up and Blake just rolls his eyes.

A blue glow from the television lights up the wall behind the couch Blake is sitting on. The bookcase which was knocked over and broke Jeremy’s arm a year ago is resting against the same wall. A male’s voice is heard from the television introducing the show and its anchors. A blonde haired woman sits beside an older man who is balding in the front; he looks to be in his mid fifties.

“Hello I am Hugo Reynolds, Kyle Inman is off tonight. Our top story in local news today is a corner store robbery which killed two and left one wounded. Gerald’s Grocery was the scene for today’s crime. The gunmen took forty-eight year old Francine Raines hostage and demanded the clerk hand over the register and open the safe, when the clerk handed over the money the gunman shot the hostage, killing her instantly. The man then dropped the money and shot five bullets in every direction which wounded some shoppers. He then took his own life. Police are still investigating the scene but the killer has been identified as Earl Morris. Information on the victim is still being collected.” The male anchor reports in a calm voice.

The news has been heard; you think most children would be traumatized but not Blake. His eyes went from the color white to blood red. His veins bulged from his arms, he was breathing heavily and his fists were clenched. Blake stood up slowly and walked out of the room to the staircase. He reached the top of the old staircase and walked down, with each step a loud creak echoed throughout the domicile. Blake walked into the living room where the radio was left on. The very same news report was being played about his mother. He lost it at that very moment. Blake knocked over end tables, he smashed flower vases against the old, duty wooden floor. His anger had got the best of him as he finally threw the radio through the window sending little shards of glass every which way. Blake then turned to his right which led directly into the kitchen. He walked over to the marble top counter and removed a butcher knife from the magnetic holder on the wall. Young Raines placed the knife in his back pocket and headed out the front door of the house. He looked to his left and looked to his right. He didn't shut the door behind him and little did he know that his little brother was watching from the steps.

“I’ll show that n****rs family what it’s like to lose someone close to you.” Blake states to himself full of anger, rage and aggression. He walks down the steps of the front porch and to heads to the sidewalk.

What will happen next with young Blake?
