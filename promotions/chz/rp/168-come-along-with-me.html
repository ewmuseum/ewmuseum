Come Along With Me
Written: 8/26/02

People do not know me or they just don’t want to but my life has been revolved around pain and hatred overcoming my soul and hiding the man that should be. This Wednesday, my first match is a shot at the Tag Team gold after the PPV which is just fine with me. To Angel, Axl, and Tyler, be prepared this a night that soon will live in your mind like a live nightmare.

Just days after the first wild, my sister and I were walking along the banks of the river admiring the babbling brooks and trees that were surrounding us. Kayla was on crutches do to an accident earlier in week which I blame myself non stop but she thinks otherwise. We were dressed loosely in our matching baggy blue jean pants, white t-shirts and black boots which stepped lightly across the soft sand. Her blue eyes sparkled in the sunlight and her beautiful tanned skin so smooth making me proud of how far she had come.

“Samuel, are you still upset?” she asked

“Sort of, I didn’t expect to get a chance to get gold this quickly.” I said softly as if he were losing confidence.

She looked at me with a sarcastic but compassionate look as she messed around with her long blonde hair. I on the other hand, blinked dumfounded as my dark hair blew slightly in the smooth and slow breeze.

“I’m serious,” I said “till now I have been running away from something that I don’t even know is there.”

“Maybe it is you.” She said and hobbled over placing her hand on my shoulder.

“Yeah, maybe it is.”

As they continued to walk they cam across a tree which was oozing with blood that stopped them both in there tracks and made Soul go into a time where you have to check reality at the door.


Come along with me…

You want learn more about me? Maybe to understand why Soul is the way he is today? Then you have come to the right place. Pain Passion and Rage fill my heart and keep me going through the harsh days that have passed and yet to come. Some do not realize what I had to put up with and just label me as a broken home victim.

Imagine going through life with nothing but the thought of surviving to live your dream, do you know how it feels to be alone for most of your life? Most relatives didn’t want me and others just didn’t know I was alive. Can you actually look me in my eyes and tell me that I don’t deserve to be in CHZ?

No one knows what pain is until they have walked in my shoes. When you go from the love of a non dysfunctional family to seeing murders, stabbings, and raping it is so hard to adjust to. I am no hero, if that applied to me maybe my mother and father would still be alive. But there is one thing they can count on this Wednesday, a focused and dangerous Soul.

It was sick looking down at the blood that rushed from his head and it made me think how sick human beings can really be. There was no one on the street but myself and this man bleeding repulsively gasping for air. His white shirt and pants were soaking from his own blood but there was nothing I could do but sit there and watch him die. It was like I was visioning my parent’s death all over again. He opened up his eyes and with the last strength he had, there was something he said that made me think.

“Come along with me.”

After that, he fell limp and moved no more leaving me alone looking over the body of a man that I never knew. Sirens sounded from close by and to my surprise they surrounded me pulling out there gun pointing it directly at me. My heart wasn’t beating fast and my nerves were calm though I was looking at several gun barrels that can obliterate my existence.

“Sir, would you put the weapon down and step away from the body.” A police officer yelled at me through his megaphone.

I smiled and to me it was funny, seeing the serious look on there faces made me smile deep inside but they have the wrong person. Staring into their beady eyes I saw fear that was overwhelming and shook my head wondering if this could get any worse.

“I don’t have a weapon.” I said calmly not even caring if they would have shot me dead.

“This is no joke sir,” she said “you are the only one here and there is a dead body two feet away from you.”

My eyes slowly turned to the man’s body which lied in a pool of blood and there guns cocked back as if they were ready to shoot and my eyes closed awaiting for the worse. After a couple of seconds, there was nothing and when I opened my eyes a bearded man wearing a denim blue jean jacket and pants was wrestled to the ground screaming something over and over again.

“Come along with me! Come along with me!”

This is the second time I have heard this, it left me puzzled and shocked keeping me occupied from the police beating the crap out of the suspect. A strange wind came by swiftly, blowing my dark hair and clothing to the right only hearing the sounds of the fist and feet being placed in the victim and at that time I walked away feeling puzzled.

“What is happening here?” I said to myself not knowing if the answer was there or not.

What stood before me was an empty street surrounded by abandon buildings with the screams of insanity echoing throughout the night. There was horrific smell that couldn’t be unidentified and was along the lines of death, to the right was blood slowly dripping from the doors of the buildings and an eerie silence soon followed.

“Is this in my mind, is it all a dream?”

Something about the blood oozing out of the door that led me closer and closer wanting to know what was the deal. I looked inside seeing blood that was at least three inches deep and the walls painted red with blood of another. My nerves were shot, with the door slamming behind me rather quickly. Again, moans of pain filled the air making me feel a little uneasy but the curiosity kept me going.

“Come with me.” An angelic voice said.

To my surprise a young Indian girl with long beautiful dark hair stood before me with her face drenched in blood and her dress dies with her own blood. I shook with fear not knowing what to think and with the smooth touch of her hand she calmed my nerves and led me down to the hall to a door that had not been covered in the blood.

“Where are you taking me?”

“Shhh, I shall set you free.” She said quickly and gave me a big smile relinquishing my doubts.

Slowly the door opened showing a clean room with lights coming off of different spots on the white walls and standing before me smiling was something that I could not imagine, my mother and father. My mother was dressed in a nice white dress and flung her long blonde hair given the award winning smile that I have missed so much. As usual, my father stood back in his white casual shirt, black slacks and black shoes looking on grinning not showing any emotion.

“My son, it has been a while.”

“Son, we never thought this time will come.”

“But, your dead… how can this be?” I said completely put in a weird position.

They both approached me hugging me tightly and I could not return the emotion it was a weird situation that I just couldn’t grasp. They looked in my eyes nodding there heads for some reason and I held my head down lost in all of this.

“Life is what you make it, the decisions you made saved your sister.” My mother said with love and compassion in her tone

I stood there not saying a word; they saw the disbelief in my body language and sighed deeply.

“Samuel, be yourself and protect Kayla at all cost.

As I looked up at my parents the vision faded away to the vision of the tree and the blood was gone with Kayla nudging me. Finally I was able to snap out of it and look my sister in her eyes as she smiled and said something that shocked me.

“Come along with me, we need to get to Olympic Stadium.”
