Victims ... aren't we all?
Written: 3/8/02

The limo was comfortable and spacious and spacious, and so he made the most of it. He sunk comfortably into the black leather furnishing and interior of the seats and knocked down his second whiskey. He wore his usual long black leather jacket and black top, with long black leather pants and biker boots. His long black hair hung loosely around his shoulders.

'Care for another?'

Axl shook his head slowly as he stared out of the window of the limo looking at the blur of passing sights going past him as they drove on. He placed the glass down and sat back and got comfortable again. The man opposite him was young, maybe even a little younger than Axl, maybe about 2 years younger He had short blonde gelled and combed over in a slick come over. He bared an arrogant sneer as they drove. His eyes were a light hazel green.

'So Mr. Young, I take it you understand why I have brought you here?'

Axl remained looking out the window. He knew, he knew exactly why he had brought him here. It didn't take a rocket scientist to work it out.

'Yeah .... yeah I know why you brought me here'

'Good'

The man nodded to Axl, and then produced a small folder from inside his long black jacket. He looked at whilst grinning and then handed it to Axl. Axl leaned forward a little and then set it on his lap. He slowly slid his hand across the smoothness of the card material on the front, before carefully opening it and examining its contents.

'I think you will find everything suffice to your mission is in there. All the key facts and points'

Axl nodded slowly, not paying full attention to what he was saying. He was putting forth most of his concentration into studying the documents he had just been handed. Pictures, maps, blueprints, statistics.

'I also think your fee will be more than substantial'

'As long as it pays the bills'

'I'm sure they will'

'Who's the target?'

'.......My brother!'

Axl look up sharply.

'What? I have too many failed business ventures, I have failed more than I have succeeded in the business. He dies, I get his half of the will. However killing him is too risky. I want him locked up. Frame him if necessary, bah, I don't care. Just get him out the picture. Mother and father dearest would write him out of the will if he was a criminal'

Axl looked at him clutching the file with slight shock and disbelief before responding again.

'Like I said .......... whatever pays the bills'

'I'm glad we have an understanding'

He sneered even more as he uttered the last comment.

'Now, I understand you can ummmm. manipulate the ....... human mind?'

Axl folded up the folder and placed it by his side and sat back to a more comfortable position before nodding slowly.

'Yeah, its a speciality you could say'

'Excellent! I want it utilized on him! Use that to... I don't know, frame him!'

Axl merely sighed and nodded slowly before staring back out into the window. Roland his eagle was perched comfortably on his shoulder.

Slowly the man lurched forward and stretched his hand out towards Roland in a gesture that seemed like he was going to stroke him. Axl spotted this out of the corner of his eye and snatched his out sharply and gripped the man by the wrist.

'Wh-'

'I wouldn't advise you to do that'

The man looked up at him with somewhat embarrassment and confusion.'

'Do not touch the bird Mr. Colfax. Unless you want to leave this limo with your eyes still attached firmly into your head by their sockets'

'Is that a threat?'

'No. It's a fact.'

He gripped the man, now known as Mr. Colfax by the wrist firmly for an intense moment longer, before releasing it. Roland looked down upon him with a menacing daring yet wise look about him.

They travelled for a few minutes longer silently without uttering a word, before Mr. Colfax started wearing his sly grin again before speaking.

'You know if you were to make this job a success , I mean a real success. I could always use you as an official employee for me. There are plenty of other people I could use out of the way.'

Axl eventually turned his head to him again, this time with a slightly scornful look in his eyes.

'Who next. Mommy, daddy dearest? Maybe a sister here and there? Maybe a close friend or maybe your neighbours fucking dog?'

Mr. Colfax looked at him with surprise and confusion once again.

'I don't do this for damn thrills. Well.... maybe I do. But I do it mainly because I am good at it. I have a talent for it. I am good at what I do but just because I am doesn't make what I do anymore right. It doesn't mean that when my time is finally up I am not going to be sent south of heaven. Yet my conscience has eventually learnt to accept it. Not fully, but it has slowly accepted what I do. Yet you, your still going to have to go to sleep after what I do with this hanging ominously in your conscience. '

Mr. Colfax swallowed deeply, blinking furiously for a few seconds before taking in a deep breath through his nose. Axl then slowly knelt in close to him, the leather of the seats scrunching up as he did causing a screeching sound.

'Your going to have a voice after this. It's going to rent out a place in your head, maybe for a few days, weeks, months, years, maybe even forever. When your laying in your four poster bed with freshly washed white crisp sheet that your maid from Southern America did, this voice is going to decide to become active. 'Hey, Willy! Guess what, just in case you forgot, YOU HIRED A MAN TO KILL YOUR BROTHER! Yeah, that's right, he's dead and six feet under because of you.' Or 'Hey Willy, just here to remind you that your brother is rotting in a prison cell infested with maggots and cockroaches because of you!'

William Colfax looked on with an emotionally sickened disturbed face as Axl brought the truth and reality of what he was about to go through crashing down hard on him.

'Hope this is what you wanted William, hope this is what you had in mind! Because this is what your getting.'

His voice was cold and at the same time calm. Psychologists tend to have calm soothing voices, but then again Axl was not a psychologist. He was a man who studied the human mind and learnt how to abuse it and manipulate it to almost a manner beyond all belief.

After a long pause filled with one final moment of thought and consideration, William Colfax spoke once again. His voice was choked up slightly, but his words had certainty and his voice was sure.

'Yes ..... yes I am aware of the consequences. The plan is to go ahead.'

Axl nodded slowly. He was not a contract assassin. He was an underground hit man. Word got around he could force people to commit crimes without them fully being aware of it. He was the master manipulator, he was the hennas hit man.

'Very well then, I'm glad we came to this arrangement'

'Good, and remember. I want him to attack some innocent meaningless victim. No one who will attract too much attention.

Axl chuckled slightly.

'Victims ..... aren't we all'


Fade .. to......... black!
