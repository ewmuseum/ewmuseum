Road to Canada part 1
Written: 06/12/02

My first match in CHZ pits me up against Shammon Smith; it is kind of odd how my life has led me to this certain point in time. A couple of years ago, I was no where to be found but my shot at redemption starts on the 26th , I hope Winnipeg is ready for me, I’m look forward to show stopping like I usual do.

Today was rather cold and loud, as usual Vega woke up to the sound of his alarm clock that sat on a small table. He rolled over falling on the hard floor below and instantly grabbed his back grimacing in pain. He rose to his feet with anger in his eyes and the sweet smell of French toast. He rubbed his eyes and still held on to his back in obvious pain and began to walk to the closet on the right.

“God I hate mornings,” he said “hell, maybe people won’t actually treat me like I’m some charity case because I’m blind.”

He opened the closet door showing his dress clothes picking out a blue shirt with the anime character “Goku” on the front with his black baggy jeans and blue boots. He then walked to his dresser opening up the first drawer, pulling out a pair of white boxers and blue ankle high socks. He walks passed the TV into his bathroom closing the door behind him.

20 minutes later

Vega comes out of the bathroom full dressed and his hair, that was half blonde and half black, was tied in a pony tail with two sets of bangs coming over his forehead. He goes to his bed and sits there for a minute until his bedroom door opened with a derange man dressed only in some underwear pointing the gun at him. Vega hears the man shaking, slowly cocking back the trigger and she just smiles not saying a word.

“I was a member of the syndicate, but instead you had to destroy the group!” The man said violently as he shivered in fear.

“Indeed, you were killing innocent people and I could not have any of it.” He said calmly

He rose to his feet walking methodically towards him and he started to back up firing pistol barely missing Vega and it didn’t stop him at all. He kept shooting but he was missing badly and he finally ran out of bullets backing up into the corner shivering with fear. Vega stood over hi feeling the floor tremble and shook his head in disappointment.

“Are you going to kill me,” he said “that would make you feel better wouldn’t it!”

“No, you’re not worth it but, I have something much better.”

He helped the man up to his feet pulling out nine millimeter cocking it back placing straight on his forehead. He starts to shake violently as he starts to pull the trigger back slowly. He starts to cry before he pees on himself making Vega give a devious smile.

“This is how it feels when you are inches away from ending someone’s life.” He said with a smile on his face

“Please… do not kill me I am sorry!” He pleaded desperately to Vega.

He helps the man to his feet and sits on the bed listening to the urine drip from his pants. The tears really starts to pour in from the man’s eyes with Vega just sitting there staring at the ground. He points to the bathroom and he quickly runs in slamming the door behind him. He stood up adjusting his shirt and walk out of the bedroom, as he walked down the he could hear his golden retriever running towards and bent down to give him a hug.

“How’s my little man?” He says to the dog in a squeaky tone

His dog just barks as they walk down the upstairs hall and down the stairs, on the wall to his right were pictures of his sister when he was in his childhood. His dog started to bark and he heard the soft footsteps walking across the wooden floor. The around the corner by the black leather sofa, a woman dressed in a purple jacket and purple leather pants steps around looking at Vega and his dog on the stairs.

“Hi, are you Willis James?”

“Indeed I am, and you must be Janis Reed.”

He begins to jog down the steps and his dog stays on the step whining before walking into a bedroom. Vega and Janis walked into the living room and sat on the sofa; she then pulled out a picture and gave it to him. He examined the picture with his picture and handed it back to her.

“Who is this woman?” he said obviously looking confused

“That is your real mother,” she said hesitantly “she lives in Red Deer Canada if you want to go visit her.”

“It’s actually perfect timing, I was going that way today.”

She looked confused as Vega stood up looking down at the ground and shook his head in disappointment. He swung his arms back and fourth until the clock on the brick fireplace started to chime. He turned around and started to walk out the door with Janis following him.

“Can I come with you?” she asked

“Sure, hey maybe you could be my manager,” he says “it would be good to have someone I trust there to enjoy my success with me.”

She smiled and gladly accepted as the two walked down the hall. On the walls were a couple of great jazz musicians during the segregation days and they walked out the door. On the outside, Janis looked over the freshly cut grass and the red Ferrari passing by on the street. They walked across the grass and to the green convertible that was parked along the curb.

“We’re riding in my new convertible, I am so excited!”

“Uh-oh, looks like someone is moving on up.”

She punched him in arm and he hop in the passenger seat and instinctively leans his head back as Janis gets in the car and drives off. She turned on the radio as some country music starts to play and Vega shook his head closing his eyes as she made a sharp turn waking him up.

“If I wasn’t blind, I would be giving you a weird look.”

“Whatever, you know you love me,” she said sarcastically “so where we headed?”

“Airport, can’t wait till the 26th Shammon Smith is so going to be slapped around like my prison slave.”
