Overdue III
Written: July 6, 2002

One day.

One day has past. One whole day has past since Luke Black was kidnapped by Bob’s Mafia. Yesterday was the longest day of Luke’s life. Tethered and gagged to a chair only to eat scraps left over from Bob’s Mafia, which wasn’t much after “Fat” Joey got through eating. He could eat like he was going into hibernation the very next day.

We join Luke in a dark room dimly light by a single light swaying in the slight draft. Luke is tethered to a chair with his hands behind his behind his back and a cloth tied around his mouth. His long blonde hair has lost its sheen dangling in front of his hung head.

A door opens on the other side of the room. Luke lifts his head to see who it is. The bright light from out side makes it impossible to see tell. We can only see the figure’s outline. The door shuts and we can hear the steps get closer and closer to Luke. He steps into the dim light and reveals himself to be Frank, from Bob’s Mafia. “You’ll be needing this after midnight, ya’ know.” He says as he tosses the porno on Luke’s lap.

The door opens again and enters three more figures. Frank steps aside to allow then too, to enter the light. It’s the rest of Bob’s Mafia. Luke hangs his head back down. “You know, where not Italian, boy.” Says Jeff.

“Shut up you!” Snaps Joey, “He’s not supposed to know that!”

Joey backhands Jeff. “Now back to the matter at hand.” Joey begins, “Where going to let you go after midnight, at which time the video will be overdue and you’ll be forced to pay a large sum of money. But right know, we have other matters to attend.”

Bob’s Mafia leaves the light and exits the empty room. Luke can’t see past the dim light nor can see he see what’s behind him. He can’t escape from the ropes; they are tied to tight. Luke tries to hop forward in the chair but some how hops backward hits something metallic. He feels it and realizes that it’s pretty dull, but just maybe it’s sharp enough to cut the ropes.

Luke begins to rub the rope on the metal. It’s working! It’s cutting the ropes but it’s taking much longer than anticipated. Luke’s wrists are getting rope burn and begin to bleed. Finally, the ropes break and he takes off the gag. He picks up the porno on his lap and runs out of the small building. It turns out to be a shed and is filled with garden tools.

Once out side, Luke is blinded by the light and must adjust his eyes. Luke looks around. There are several ramshackle buildings, with windows busted out and doors broken in. There is a parking lot about a block away with about three old and rusted cars. Luke hurriedly makes his way over to them, thinking that they could help him to get to the store faster.

He tries all the car doors to see if any of them are open. None are. He looks around for anything to help him break in to one. He finds a pipe lying on the ground. He picks it up and smashes in a window. He reaches in, unlocks the door and sweeps out the glass with his hands. Lucky enough for him, the person who owns this car was stupid enough to leave his keys inside. Bob’s Mafia pulls up in their car and see that Luke is getting away.

The owner of the car has heard his window being bashed in and has come out side from the building. “Hey! Hey! That’s my car!” He shouts at Luke as he tries to start up the engine. It’s not starting up. The owner of the car runs over to Luke on the drivers’ side. Luke shuts the door trying to get away from him and start up the car.

The owner punches Luke and he immediately stops trying to steal the car. He opens the door and steps out. He hits the owner with a massive right fist and he falls to the ground. Luke picks him up and runs him into another car, breaking that car’s window.

Bob’s Mafia has stopped right behind the car Luke is trying to steal and step out. “Luke!” Jeff shouts, “You have nowhere to go! The car is dead and the cops will be here any minute for your attempted car theft!”

Luke flicks them the bird and hops in the car. He slams the door shut and it falls off. This car is shit! Luke tries one more time and it the engine starts up. He floors it, leaving Bob’s Mafia behind. They all climb back in their car and speed off after Luke.

We cut to a new traffic helicopter.

“And all is clear on the highway.” The new traffic guy says, “You should have no trouble getting home from—What the hell is this? There are two crazed drivers traveling at speeds faster than the speed limit! There seem to be no law enforcement officers chasing them. Wait, there are two police in his high speed pursuit, now.”

Bob’s Mafia stick their heads out the window and start shooting at Luke’s car.

“One of the cars is fired rounds of bullets are the other car! The car that is in the lead is…missing a door!”

Several more police join in the pursuit. They cross over a bridge making a beautiful seance with the sun setting and the police cars flashing their lights. Bob’s Mafia shoots out Luke’s stolen car’s back window.

“And that one car keeps falling apart! It’s missing its back window now.”

Bob’s Mafia gets closer to Luke and rams the back of his car.

“This is very dangerous. I would advise everyone on highway 25 to get off the road!”

We cut back to Luke in the car. Several bullet holes are in the front window.

“I wonder if there’s a gun in here somewhere.” Luke says to himself. He reaches over to the glove compartment and opens it. Sure enough a handgun is in there. He takes it and shoots back at Bob’s Mafia nearly missing a school bus.

We cut back up to the helicopter.

“And now the car that is falling apart is shooting back at the car that was shooting at him. I have just received word that the police are laying down a spike strip to stop the two speeders.”

Up ahead we can police have laid down the spikes and are waiting for Luke and Bob’s Mafia to run over it. Luke has realized what is happening and runs off the road. It is so rocky the car’s hood springs up and Luke can’t see anything. His muffler has fallen off and is dragging on the road behind him with a shower of sparks, now that he is back on the road.

Bob’s Mafia was a little more careful going around the spike strip.

Luke’s stolen car runs off the road and his right tires hit a rock. The car flips over, loosing its hood but landing back on its wheels and still goes.

“And he has just flipped! But, amazingly, he’s still going! I don’t believe this!”

Luke leads everyone off the highway onto the street where the Bob’s XXX Porno Rental Store is. Bob’s Mafia start firing franticly and manage to shoot out Luke’s two back tires. He drives the car out of control and it goes flying into some nearby woodland area down a big hill before hitting the bottom and exploding.

The police surround the area, enclosing Bob’s Mafia in. They take the group in and start investigating the crash.

We cut to where Luke is, hanging from a branch with the video in his mouth and the gun in his pocket. He is bleeding from his head, wrists and nose. Luke quietly watches the police pass him by to the crash. Luke drops down and lands on his feet. He turns around to start climbing the hill up. He stops when he sees a muscular man dressed in very expensive clothing, and dark sunglasses a gun pointed right at him. “Where do you think you’re going, Luke?” questions the mysterious man.

Luke huffs then ask, “Who are you?”

“It doesn’t matter! Hand the video over to me!”

“No!”

“Give me the video!”

“Not until…*huff*…you tell me your…*puff*…name.”

“Arg…It’s Bob! Okay, now hand over the video!”

“NEVAH!” Luke screams as he rolls out of the way behind a tree. Bob chases him behind it and Luke takes off up the hill. Bob fires a few rounds off before running up the hill after Luke.

Luke reaches the top of the hill and sees that the police are still there. He slides down the hill some and hides in a bush. Bob walks up and Luke takes out the gun he got. He goes to shoot Bob but there aren’t any more bullets. He drops the gun and makes a break for it. Bob blandly sees Luke, shoots at him, and runs out of bullets as well. He drops his gun and takes off after Luke.

Bob gets closer to Luke and tackles him. The video slides along the ground and is partially hidden by some leaves. Bob turns Luke over his back and hits him with a right hand. Luke flings Bob’s sunglasses off his face, pokes Bob’s eye and tries to take off for the video. Bob grabs a hold of Luke’s leg. Luke grabs a stick as he’s being pulled towards Bob and hit it across Bob’s eye. He makes a break for it but Bob pounces on Luke.

Luke sees that Bob has a knife and Luke takes it and slashes Bob’s right eye. Bob screams and allows Luke to get free. Luke drops the knife and grabs the video. He takes off towards the porno store.

He reaches the edge of the forest and tries to act like nothing has happened. The police see Luke and give chase. Luke finds the porn store and enters it. There is a man behind the counter. “Here! I returned the video!” Luke says, “How much do I owe you?”

“Um…” The man begins, “About $25.25.”

“What? How much would I have owed you if I didn’t return the video in on time?” Luke asks surprised.

“About ten bucks!”

“WHAT?!”

“Yeah. We make you think its going to be more, but it’s not. Pretty ironic, huh?”

Luke reaches into his pocket and hands over the amount owed. The police bust in and take drag Luke out.

“You have the right to remain silent. Anything you say can and will be used against you in the court of law…”
