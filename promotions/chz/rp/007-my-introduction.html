My Introduction
Written: 6/12/02

The scene opens at a house in Myrtle Beach, South Carolina. The front door is open, so the cameraman decides to go in. A voice is heard in the corner of the room, but the cameraman can't make out who is saying it, for the lights are off in the house.


Voice: What in the f@#k are you doing here? And what do you want you stupid looking b!@$h?


Cameraman: Well, sir, I came here to see a man by the name of, uh, Kazzualty. Do you know where I could find him?


Voice: Hell yeah I know where you can find him. He's standing right f@$#ing in front of you dumba$$!


Cameraman: Oh, so you're Kazzualty huh?


Kazzualty: No s#!t Sherlock!


Kazzualty emerges from the dark corner and walks over to the cameraman. He's wearing black baggy jeans, a black T-shirt, black and red sneakers, and a silver necklace around his neck. He has long black hair pulled back behind his shoulders.


Cameraman (While mumbling): Great, I get to interview a cussing redneck.


Kazzualty: What'd you say punk?


Cameraman: Nothing sir. Nothing at all.


Kazzualty: Don't get smart with me a$$ clown. I'll kick your teeth in right f@#$in' here!


Cameraman: Yes sir, it won't happen again. I promise you that.


Kazzualty: Yeah, you better be. You don't know how much s#!t you're getting into by doing this stuff.


Cameraman: I can only imagine.


Kazzualty: Anyways, what the f@#k are you doing here? You never did answer me that.


Cameraman: Well sir, I came here to see if you'd like to shoot a promo for your match on the 26th?


Kazzualty: I have a f@#$in' match? What is it, and who is the a$$h*!e I have to face?


Cameraman: Well, you're in a National Title Tournament Pool A Seeding Match against Race Alexander.


Kazzualty: Race f@#!in' Alexander? Who the f@#k is that?


Cameraman: All I know is that you have a match against him on the 26th.


Kazzualty: That's two weeks from today isn't it?


Cameraman: Yes I believe it is.


Kazzualty: You're damn right it is! So you want me to shoot a promo huh?


Cameraman: Yes sir, if you don't mind that is?


Kazzualty: What the hell, I'll go ahead and do one. Besides, I'm going to win the match anyways. Now, I've never heard of this Race Alexander guy, but whoever he is, he's going to get his a$$ kicked by me at Wednesday Night Wild. There's no f#@%in' doubt about that in anybody's mind. I'll speak more about him when I hear from him, or when I decide to speak again. Now, some of you all watching this don't know me. So, let me tell you about myself. I am Kazzualty. I stand in a 6 foot 3 and I'm 220 lbs of solid muscle. You will all be seeing a lot of me, because frankly, ya'll can't get enough of me. Everytime I turn around, I got fans running up to me like there's a f#@%in' fire or something, and they all want a picture or an autograph. But I tell each and every one of those bastards the same damn thing, and you know what that is Mr. Cameraman?


Cameraman: Well, no I don't. What do you tell them?


Kazzualty: I tell them all to F#@K OFF!


Cameraman: Hey, that's not very nice. All they want is an autograph.


Kazzualty: Does it look like I care what they want? Hell no I don't care! I could give a rat's a$$ what they want or need. I only do what I want. And right now, I want to kick somebody's a$$. I just have that feeling in my stomache. How about I kick your a$$? Huh cameraman?


Cameraman: No sir, please, please, don't do that. Why don't you wait until your match at Wild to do that?


Kazzualty: I don't think I can wait that f@#%ing long.


Cameraman: Why don't you just try then.


Kazzualty: Because I don't want to. I like inflicting pain on people. I love to hear them scream in agony as I continue to annihilate their stupid a$$es.


Cameraman: I'm sure you do. Oh, look at the time. I must be going then. Thank you for the promo Kazzualty.


Kazzualty: Ah, whatever. Now, as I tell all my loving fans, F#@ OFF!


Cameraman: Yes sir, right away sir.


Kazzualty (While turned around and walking away from the cameraman): Well, it looks like I couldn't wait until Wild.


Cameraman: What do you mean?


Suddenly, Kazzualty turns to the side and Kazz-Kick's the cameraman right in the chin. The cameraman flies back and falls to the floor, unconcsious. The camera flies out of his hands and shatters against the floor. The scene quickly cuts to static, then slowly fades to black. Scene ends.
