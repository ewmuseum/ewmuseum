Davis Fury RP #3: Past Misjudgements…

18 Months Ago…

The warmth of the darkness engulfed him, on any ordinary day he might have let the warmth take predominance, but this was ordinary day. Lives were at stake. The lives of his SWAT team colleagues and the civilians who potentially could be either killed or have they’re lives destroyed as a direct resultant of these drug dealers. He could not allow that to happen…so he fought the warmth of the darkness…fought to regain his consciousness.

The daylight reached him slowly at first, but the light battered through the darkness and Davis Fury was once again thrown back into the horrors of the real world. The first sensation to reach him, once again in the harsh reality, was unmistakably pain. More accurately, many different kinds of pain. The worst was the burning sensation in his back and arms. The smell of burnt flesh turned his stomach, as attempted to look at the damage caused by the warehouse explosion. Upon seeing his wound, he wished he hadn’t bothered to look. The sight of the burn almost sickening, but at least the blast had only hurt him on the top of his arms and little of his back. Basically, where his bullet proof wasn’t covering him.

Davis pushed himself up from the concrete floor and slowly got back to his feet. It was only then that the pain emanating from his ribs revealed itself to him. He took a second to regain his bearings, before looking back at the destroyed warehouse. He knew that not many of the SWAT team he was with could have got out of there before it blew, and he knew that some of his colleagues were dead. He also knew that the majority of the drug dealers were dead too, but it was his duty to check. His mission would not be complete until all of the drug dealers bodies were accounted for, regardless of his injuries. Davis bends down and picks up his gun. Then Davis notices the body.

The body was lay motionlessly, on the floor below him. The body was that of a SWAT team member that was obvious, but which one? Davis knew, even before turning the body over, the face that would inevitable face him. It was his friend Morris. Davis quickly placed his hand on the man’s neck and realised that his friend wasn’t unconscious at all…he was dead.

Davis shakes his head in disbelief, a tear rolling down his left cheek, holding the head of his dead friend up to his chest. He looked around some more and realised that none of the other members of the SWAT team had survived the firefight…he was the sole survivor. This revelation didn’t sink in with him immediately, but soon he realised that his entire squad, except him, had been taken out during a routine drug bust. He had to make sure that everyone of the drug dealers and their henchmen were dead, and make sure that the one’s that were not dead, were arrested. Davis Fury wasn’t in the mood for making any arrests today, not now.

Davis gently let Morris’ head down onto the floor, then defiantly stands up once more, this time bringing his gun with him. Ignoring standard SWAT tactics, Davis simply begins walking toward the half-destroyed warehouse, with a disturbed look upon his face. Only, at what could be loosely coined as a door of the devastated warehouse, did Davis raise the gun to shoulder level. He stood at the side of the door, and looked down the corridor leading to the devastated battle place, before taking a deep breath and rushing down the corridor, not knowing what was in store for him.

As Davis neared the end of the corridor the familiar smell of charred flesh and dead bodies, hung firmly in the air, once again urging him to be sick and once again he resisted. Davis quickly rounded the corner and eyed up the entire area. Bodies confronted him, some burnt without recognition some not. Davis swiftly past the group of crates that the SWAT team had previously taken cover behind and headed for enemy territory.

Behind the crates that the drug dealers had taken cover, Davis found himself staring at a familiar picture, except there were less charred bodies in this section. Then he heard a sound. Coming from the very rear of the warehouse, deep within enemy territory. It was possible that some could have survived the explosion that far back, so Davis entered the barrage of crates very carefully. After a couple of seconds of looking through all of the crates he was beginning to wonder whether he had imagined the sound, he actually questioned his sense of reality. After all he only regained consciousness mere minutes ago. Then he heard the noise again.

It was very difficult to determine what the sound actually was, but Davis figured that it was some kind of groan. Davis followed the noise, it was coming from right at the back of the warehouse, so Davis reluctantly followed the noise. Davis quickly rounded a crate to be confronted by a man dragging himself across the floor, a bloodied state. Davis aimed his gun at the man’s head.

Davis: Stay where you are!

The man crawling across the floor doesn’t turn around he simply slumps to the floor, finally giving up and leaving the mortal realm in favour of what the after life will bring, if anything at all. Davis sighs and lowers his gun. That’s when the arms grabbed him from behind. Davis couldn’t see who it was, but whoever they were, they were pretty strong. The arms latched around his neck, attempting to strangle Davis. Davis knew he was rapidly losing consciousness, he had to do something and fast.

Then he thought of his friend Morris, who was lying face down on the concrete floor, a couple of meters away from the warehouse itself. He thought about the massacre that had occurred here today, and all of the human life lost on this day. That’s where he drew his strength from, that is what got his adrenaline flowing through his body. Davis summoned all of his strength and forced him and who ever had hold of him backwards slamming the attacker into the wooden crates.

At first the blow is not strong enough to release the bear like grip, so her moves the both of them forwards again, then slams into the wooden crates once more. This time the bear like grip was effectively broken, and Davis managed to stumble out of the reach of his attacker. He took a minute to once again get his bearings, attempting to rub away the pain in his neck. He then turns to his attacker. It was none other than the man behind this drug deal. It was Sebastian Anderson. Anderson had cuts and bruises visible across his face and it appeared that he survived the explosion without any serious injuries. That can soon be rectified! Davis silently mused.

Seb Anderson manages to catch his breath and pull his self up using the wooden crates as leverage. Davis looks on as Seb develops a demented look upon his face and begins to lunge at him. Davis develops a smile upon his face.

Davis: I was hoping you’d do that!

As Seb Anderson lunges, Davis side-steps Anderson, punching him in the ribs as he goes past. Anderson instinctively moves his left arm to protect the now wounded ribs, allowing Davis with a free shot to the face, which he takes almost gratefully. The punch connects perfectly with Anderson’s jaw, forcing blood to spurt out of his mouth. Davis grinned at the sight before him and squatted down right before him, taunting him. He did not see Anderson’s hand reaching towards the inside of his boot.

Davis: You know what? You and all of your henchmen killed a lot of people today, and you my friend will be going to prison for a very long time!

Seb: I…I…don’t think so…

Davis: What makes you think that?

Seb Anderson does not answer, he simply smiles, Davis knew something was wrong, but he didn’t know what. Until it happened that was. Within a blink of an eye Sebastian Anderson draws the knife from his boot and lunges towards Davis for a second time, only this time with metal in his hand. Before the blade could impact, Davis manages to grab Seb Anderson’s hand, slowing the lunge to a complete stand still. The blade of the knife moved back and forth from pointing at both Davis and Seb. After a couple of minutes of the struggling, Davis manages to over power Seb forcing the knife into the man’s chest. Slowly, but surely, Davis watched both the blood and the life drain out of the man. The man responsible for his friends death. This was the reason Tolkien Anderson wanted revenge, this was the reason he turned to drink, this was the reason Tolkien Anderson tortured and killed his wife, this was the point where his life began to end.

Present Day…

Davis slammed the white receiver of his phone down in anger. Yet another false hope, yet another one of his sources used in his bid to locate the vacated Crime lord known as Tolkien Anderson. That was his last source too, he no longer had another sources he could turn to for information. All but one that was. The thing was, he really didn’t want to use the source…he didn’t want to call his old friend Sgt. Buckweiser for assistance, but he knew that if he was ever to find Tolkien, he would have to call upon his friends help.

Either way, he would have to put the call on the backburner for a couple of days or so because of his ‘high profile’ match against Theo Knox and Luke Black. Both were very good competitors, he knew that, but lately his wrestling career had been taking a back burn to his social life, which was understandable under the circumstances. The truth was he would be giving his wrestling career a break, to both mourn for the death of his wife and find the man responsible for the murder, but he needed the money to continue the search. The words to describe his situation was ‘Catch 22’.

He decided not to look at his situation in a negative light, he was sure that he could turn the situation around to make it positive for him. He didn’t know how to do this fully at the minute, but he had a temporary solution to say the least. From his unfruitful search for the crime lord known as Tolkien Anderson he ‘unwanted aggression’ to say the least, and hurting people was undoubtedly the only way to relieve this aggression – or so he hoped.

Davis placed last Wednesday’s wild videotape into his video recorder and began to watch the variety of matches that were placed before. He paid close attention to the matches involving both him and his competitors for this weeks match, for it was essential in order for him to over come his opponents. He pressed the ‘rewind’ button on his remote control, closely followed by the ‘play’ button, if he was going to fight in this match, he sure as hell wanted to be prepared for it.
