Trainer from Hell: The Beginning
Written: 6/14/02

Two cupped hands swoop through water and suds sloshing it everywhere in the sink. The hands bring up the cupped water and splash it on the man’s face. The water splashes everywhere and sparkles from the light of a near by window. When the hands slide down off the man’s face we realize that the man is non-other than CHZ superstar Luke Black! Luke turns to the towel and dries his face as one of his many servants walk in with the phone in hand. “It’s for you, sir.” Says the servant.

“Who is it?” asks Luke still drying his face.

“It’s a from the CHZ. He wishes to speak to you.” The servant informs Luke.

Luke hangs the towel on its rack and takes the phone from the servant. “Hello?” Luke asks answering the phone. Luke shoos his servant away.

“Hello there!” replies the man.

There is silence as neither man talks. Luke angrily breaks the silence, “Who is this and why did you call?”

“Oh, right! I’m your agent and…” He is cut off by Luke before he has a chance to finish.

“I don’t have an agent! Stop calling!”

“I have to tell you before it’s too late!”

“Tell me what?”

“I have to tell you that you have a match on the 26th!”

“I already knew that!”

“Oh, well, I sent over a trainer to help you train for your match!”

“Have you seen the shape I’m in! I don’t need your filthy trainer! And stop calling here!” Luke hangs up the phone and begins to walk out of his bedroom when the doorbell rings.

*Ding-a-dlong dong ding dune*

The sound of the door being opened by one of Luke’s servants can be heard. Luke rushes down the hall to see who it is. A man in his late 20’s stands there. He is very well built and his clothes are drenched in sweat. “Ah there you are Luke!” Says the man; “I am Ross Youngblood! Your personal trainer!”

“I don’t have a trainer!” Exclaims Luke; “I don’t need a trainer, now, in the past, in the future, NOT EVER!”

“I have word from your agent to train you today.” Ross tells Luke.

“That isn’t my agent! I don’t have an agent!” Luke informs Ross. He walks over to Ross and places his arm around him. “Listen junior….”

“Junior?” Ross cuts off Luke, “I’m older than you! I should be calling your junior!”

“Yeah whatever; now leave!”

“I’m not leaving until I get paid!”

“I’m not paying! You didn’t train me!”

“Then lets go train! It’ll be fun!”

Luke lets out a sigh of defeat, “If I train with you, will you leave me alone?”

“Absolutely! No question about!”

“Will it be for free?”

“Oh hell no! It’s going to coast you a few hundred dollars!”

“What? Get out of my house! Get out!” Demands Luke as he starts shoving Ross out.

“Fine! Don’t come crying to me when you loose your match and loose your chance to become Champion!” Ross says trying to persuade Luke into training.

Luke stops and thinks about it for a minute, “Fine! I’ll train with you! But you’re only getting half of what I have to pay you!”

“Then I’ll just have to raise my price then!” Ross jokes as he rushes out the door.

Luke has a confused look on his face before he decides to leave. Ross hops into his pickup and is soon joined by Luke. They speed off towards the training facility.
