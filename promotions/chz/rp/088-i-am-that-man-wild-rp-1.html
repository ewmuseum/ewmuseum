I am THAT man... (Wild RP#1)
Written: 07/27/2002


*It’s morning and we’re at the Plaza Hotel in the busy city center of Toronto Canada. The camera shot shows a clip of the hotel in all it’s glory, a five-star establishment and only the wealthy get the privilege to stay there. The camera pans up, the cameraman arching his back so he can get the very top of the building in shot and then follows it back down to face level. A doorman is seen opening the large double-doors to let the important guests in and out. A large canopy covers the entrance, as well as a dozen or so flags of certain worldwide countries that hang of the side of the building, the Red and White Canadian Maple Leaf flag stands out in the center of the other flags and hangs half-mast. Yellow taxis cabs and top of the range automobiles hog the road in front as the scene fades out. The next shot fades back in again within seconds.

We’re now inside a hotel room, one of the many excellent lodgings for the wealthy guests. As we pass through the large brown doors, with gold-coloured trimming, a large open spaced room stands before us. To our right is a passageway that leads to the bathroom; to our left is the bedroom and directly in front is another large room. The camera pans left and right to get a sense of occasion, the room and the hotel have some history. Polictians, Film Celebrities, Worldwide Royalty and Members of Congress are but to name a few of who have stayed in the expensive Plaza Hotel before. As we venture towards the room in front, we see “The Circus Freak” Matt Hunt sat on the bed watching some television. The maids look to have done their rounds, as the room is in excellent organized condition.

The television set is of average size and placed inside an antique looking oak cabinet, the bed is king-sized with a brown duvet tinted with floral trimmings. Different framed pictures hang on the walls, some are art, some are portraits, others are priced at $9.99 from the local hardware store, but nobody really notices them. Matt is dressed in a plain black T-shirt and a pair of green shorts. It appears that he has not long been awake as he glares at some US morning chat show.

His face looking tired and his hair all scruffy, Hunt glances at the camera and continues to watch television. The self-proclaimed Circus Freak is about to open his mouth and talk when there’s a knock at the door. Seconds later a young man, dressed a smart maroon and black suit walks in pushing a cart. On the cart is a average sized tray with a glass of Orange Juice, pot of tea, a cup turned over on it’s saucer, a round of toast – cut in half -, a bowl of Honey Nut Rice Krispies, small jug of milk and some sachets of sugar. Before the young servant can joyfully utter ‘Good Morning’, Hunt snaps in his broad English accent*

Hunt: “Did I say come in?”

*The young man jumps at Hunt’s sudden outburst, nearly pushing the trolley cart over. He mumbles something as he adjusts the trolley and himself. This is inaudible to us as well as Hunt, who continues his eruption*

Hunt: “Speak up!”

*The young worker clears his throat and repeats in his North American drone*

Servant: “I thought you’d like some breakfast.”

*Hunt grabs the remote control to the television set and aggressively presses the standby button and tosses the small black box on the bed*

Hunt: “If the Canadian Prime Minister was here, would you just barge in and utter…I thought you’d like some breakfast? No you wouldn’t. Why? Because you haven’t got the balls to show disrespect to Mr Chretien and to invade his privacy have you?”

*The young man stands head bowed, hands behind his back*

Hunt: “You’re damn right. I am a visitor to this bloody country as well as paying customer, now that signifies that I should deserve some respect around here. Now take that sodding cart, wheel it on outta here and don’t let the door hit you on the way out. Then, you will knock and wait until I say you can come in. When you come back in, take that sorry-look of your face and smile. Do you get me?”

*The tired worker nods his head and apologies before pulling the trolley and exiting the room, shutting the door behind him. Hunt shakes his head in disgust as the door quietly shuts, grabs the television remote and turns the television back on to continue watching. Seconds later, the knocking is heard. Hunt watches the television and acts as if he can’t hear anything. Some more few seconds pass and the knocking pattern is heard ‘rat-a-tat-tat’. Hunt looks up and says with a stern tone in his voice…*

Hunt: “Come in!”

*The worker re-enters the room, pushing the trolley with all the breakfast items on it. He uncomfortably smiles as he places the cart next to Hunt’s bed*

Servant: “Good Morning, Mr Hunt.”

Hunt: “Now that’s better. You know, you’re lucky that I’m in such a good mood, you muppet. I’m in a good mood for a reason and I don’t want anybody to screw it up...especially you! Instead of politely instructing you how to do your job, I could have jumped of this bed and gave you some serious slap.”

*The worker nods as he takes the medium-sized tray off the cart and places it on the bed. He is just about to pick it up and hand it to Hunt, but he stops him, motioning that he can do it*

Hunt: “Leave it! I’ll handle it from here. Don’t let the door hit you on the way out!”

*The worker looks as if he’s going to cry as he pulls the trolley out of the room and closes the door behind him. Hunt sits up, with his legs straighten out as he picks up the tray and places it on his lap, he inhales sharply as the cold metal of the tray touches his thighs*

Hunt: “Bloody kids, a good slap to today’s youth is order!”

*The Gifted One picks up his small jug of milk and pours it on to the cereal. He then looks around the tray*

Hunt: “Where’s the bloody spoon?”

*Hunt carefully lifts up the bowl of cereal and then replaces it on the tray. After a small search, he realises that there are no spoons, in fact no cutlery whatsoever*

Hunt: “What the hell is this?”

*He picks up the tray and places it on the floor, before grabbing the telephone receiver and barking down the phone*

Hunt: “Hello…Room Service?…This is Matt Hunt in…yes, good morning indeed…what can you do for me?…next time you decide to barge into my room with some unscheduled breakfast, make sure there’s some damn cutlery for Christ’s sake…yes, no cutlery…huh?, you’re damn right you’re sorry…okay.”

*Hunt slams the phone down and lies back on the bed, resting his head against the headboard*

Hunt: “What a nice start to the day this is! But, I could sit here and bitch about today’s youth and the incompetent hotel staff – but I’ve got other things on my mind, namely the CHZ Title and some camache called Jake.”

*Hunt leans over and picks up a half-cut slice of the toast, he takes average-sized bites out of it, while continuing to talk*

Hunt: “You know when you start a new job and how awkward it feels for the first few weeks…months even? You know the feeling when you enter a room for the first time on your first day and everybody stops talking…there’s complete silence and everybody directs their eyes on you. How small did that you feel, eh? How much of a nobody did you think you were, huh? How about when you got round to introducing yourself? There you are, you’re in the canteen and you advance towards this group of individuals who are laughing and joking, while puffing on their mid-morning cigarette. You cut in with a beaming smile and right before you mutter your name…the crowd ups and leaves, leaving you with a fake smile and an extended hand shaking thin air. How insignificant do you feel? Sure, you were just trying to be polite – you wanted to make a good start and introduce yourself to your new work…mates. But maybe they don’t feel like being all chatty to a bloke who has just taken their friends job…did you ever think about that? Instead of feeling sorry for yourself, just think of the poor bastard who you replaced. He was probably Mr Popular in your new department, he was the life and soul of every office party – he breathed fresh air into every working day and just because he gets caught shagging the Vice President’s daughter on the Persian Rug in the boardroom, you’re handed a new…career…”

*Hunt pauses with a scowl and shakes his head*

Hunt: “Woah, going overboard there. Anyway, I was trying to pass a comparison to starting a new job or starting a new school, to making yourself known in wrestling organisation. However, this isn’t quite the same thing. The CHZ has had a major shake-up and the whole roster was released. Why was this? Well, I personally couldn’t give a toss, nor am I going to. So there’s been a major drafting exercise by the new management – and he’s picked me up on the way. Not a bad move, if I do say so myself – but don’t worry, this isn’t a speech on why I was chosen as each and every single one of you will know why in the coming weeks.

However, as the new roster has been finalised and everybody has been put in their respective status within the roster – everybody starts off on the same foot. It’s like Big Brother – where nobody knows nobody and they have to work together to survive in the house…getting nominated and evicted, as well as those weekly tasks are very much irrelevant here. So who out of the roster will leap out of the starting blocks first? Who will catch the eye of the promoters, the fans and the public? Who will be the prominent role in the CHZ and will single-handedly restore the prestige and honour that has been missing from this company for so long. Will it be just one individual? Or will it take two?

Well, I am making it known that I intend on being that person…and I’m going to take the CHZ World Title in the process. I will re-establish the interest in CHZ and it’s World Title. I will bring back the pride and dignity that this flagging ship once had and I’m going to do it…in style. You see, I am an entertainer as well as being a gifted all-rounder – and in my book, those two qualities are important to pushing the CHZ back up to what it was – or at least, what it should be.”

*By this time, Hunt has munched through the toast and finished his Orange Juice. He starts to prepare his cup of tea and does so while continuing*

Hunt: “It would be so easy to walk up to the management and sell them my pitch, tell them my ambitions – and to demand the CHZ Title – but the simple fact is, it isn’t easy and I don’t expect it to be. As I look through the new roster, I notice different characters – a collection of people that could also have the same idea, that they want the World Title and that they would be the person to spearhead the CHX Re-birth. But after a considerable amount of time, nobody impressed me. Not one single name on that roster told me that they would be a potential threat, that I had to cautiously look over my shoulder and that I had to step up my game.

This may be conceived as arrogance and I might as well be patronising the whole list of guys – but at least I’m being honest. I’m speaking the truth and putting this message across to you, before you damage your own ambitions and your career is finished. But, I know that nobody will take any notice of what I’ve just said and I don’t blame him or her to either. I mean, if I heard some camache saying what I’ve just mentioned – I’d brush them off and start my own route to the top. That’s fine with me, because when I eventually do become the CHZ World Champion – don’t say I didn’t warn you!”

*Hunt takes a sip out of a cup of tea, which he started to drink while talking just now. After a slight pause, he continues*

Hunt: “Now, the first card has been announced and the name that was drawn from a roulette wheel was Ohio’s own Jake Schultz. I have to admit that I know absolutely nothing about this guy and I don’t know his wrestling ability either. I’ve never seen the bloke, nor have I spoken to him. So what could I say? What should I say to my first and next opponent? I could run my mouth endlessly and hype up our match. I could also tell him that “he’s the first on the list and he’ll get destroyed and that when all the smoke and dust has clear…” do me a favour! Jake, I have nothing particular to say to you – although, I’m guessing that will change from now until Wednesday. If I do have anything to say to you, it will be for you to be on top of your game – whatever game that may be. Tuesday night, I want to you to impress the missus and bench press her and the bed. I want you to impress the missus and rehearse the trash-talking display, which will no doubt be a part of your arsenal come fight time.

Finally Jake, I want you to stand in the mirror – a full, life-size, no extra cost, free in this week’s Bliss…life-size mirror. I want you to take a good look at yourself and ask yourself this question: “Do I feel lucky?” Heh, that’s too western. How about you ask yourself “Am I a failure?” Hah, that’s too Canadian – not to mention American. No, no – I think you should ask yourself “Will I be ready?” Notice how I didn’t say ‘get ready’? Anyway, will you indeed be ready Jake – when the circus comes to town and wipes…you…out! Let the games begin…”

*The camera pans away from Hunt as stares into the camera lens with a sinister smirk. Few seconds later, he pushes the tray away from him and reaches over to grabs the phone receiver*

Hunt: “Hello?…yes, I’m still waiting….hang on…”

*He then pauses to realise the camera is still recording, he turns and sternly says…*

Hunt: “Did you not get the hint? I’m done, get lost!”

*Hunt motions the cameraman to get out of his room as he impatiently shouts down the phone at room service. As Hunt argues with the person on the other end of the phone-line, the picture slowly fades to black*
