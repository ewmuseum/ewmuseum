From Circus To Wrestling (Part 2): Sacrifices - I
Written: 24th July 2002

“Dad, I have something to tell you.”

The words echo around the suddenly silent room that is the Arefiev household. Igor’s mother, father, two brothers and only sister are all seated in the room as the CHZ superstar walks into the room. He swallows hard, as every pair of eyes are tranfixed on the young Russian. He stands at the door.

“What is that son?” Mr. Arefiev seems very confident as he turns away from the newspaper he is reading. As he sees, the pale expression of his son, his face drops a little, just like the rest of the family.

“Can we talk in private?” question Igor, as he look nervously at all of his circus performing family.

Igor’s father, a small man with a fine head of jet black hair, but an aging body looks nervously at his wife and then back at the eldest Arefiev son.

“What’s wrong Igor?” questions his father. Nerves can be heard shake through the rough Russian voice of the former great trapeze artist.

“I would just like to talk to you.” Igor is trying to sound confident as he stands in front of the worried faces.

Each of the family looks nervously at Igor, as the father of the family slowly stands to his feet.

Thomas Arefiev is a shadow of the great trapeze artist that he once was. He slowly walks across the family room, that the Arefiev’s have descended upon. Igor’s mother and sister begin to talk among themselves as the two boys try to look like nothing is wrong. What are men like and putting on fronts?

Anyway, Thomas pushes slowly past Igor. The family all watch as Igor turns around follows his father.

Thomas walks down the hallway and into the kitchen.

The eldest Arefiev son follows. Along the wall, he can see numerous pictures of his father, mother, his brothers and even himself in action on the trapeze ropes.

In his head, his thoughts are spiraling out of control. He is thinking what he should do. The pictures are flooding back every memory possible to the Russian Star. Most of those pictures were either taken by him, or involve him. Is he ready to leave it all behind? He doesn’t seem to know.

As Igor steps across the threshold of the kitchen door, his father is already seated at the top of the kitchen table.

“Now Igor...”

Thomas Arefiev is only five foot six tall, and sitting down doesn’t seem like much of a threat to many of us. But at that moment Igor would rather have found himself one-on-one with Ivan Stanislav with a steel chair in a bad mood.

At least with Stanislav, there was somewhere to run to.

The young member of the Arefiev clan again swallows hard. He takes a seat, at the opposite end of the table. He runs his hand through his hair and then un-zips the top half of his tracksuit top. Somehow, relieving the pressure that he is feeling.

“Dad...” Igor leans onto the table. His two elbows look for support from the wood, but even that can’t disguise the shaking arms of Igor.

Thomas, follows suit and leans into his son.

“I have joined a wrestling company.”

Igor’s face is emotionless as Thomas stares at his son. His face looks as pale as that of his son.

Igor is silent and leans back onto the kitchen chair. A small amount of colour returns to the cheeks of the CHZ star.

Thomas Arefiev’s face however is whiter than white.

“You have what?”

The words slowly mutter form the legend’s mouth.

“I have a wrestling company contract.”

The second time around, Igor is alot more assertive as he talks to his father.

Thomas’ face drops as he looks at his son.

“You are leaving behind OUR business for that fake industry?” The words echo around the kitchen with a sense of disbelief on the part of Thomas Arefiev.

Igor’s head bows. “I am helping our industry survive.” He is muttering as he speaks towards his father.

“Pardon... What did you say?” Thomas leans onto the table once more to his son clearer.

Igor looks up.

“I am helping our industry survive.” The assertiveness that Igor possesed only moments ago has left him. He looks into the eyes of his father.

“Survive? We?”

Thomas’ eyes grow wider as he peers at his son.

“The circus industry is losing popularity with the fans. Myself and Constantino will bring back the glory days of circus by taking the wrestling fans from under their noses.” It is a good reason but somehow, Thomas doesn’t think so. He is still looking in disbelief.

Igor seems to have regained a little confidence as he leans into the chair.

“Constatino?” Thomas seems puzzled. “I don’t know any Constantino’s from around our circuits.”

“Zergolov.”

Thomas looks up from his train of thought as Igor echoes the surname of his tag team partner around the kitchen.

Thomas’ eyebrows close into the centre of his face.

“You are working with Constantino Zergolov? Who is he? Can you trust him?”

Thomas tilts his face, as he tries to make himself a part of Igor’s sight path.

“We both have the same ambiti...”

“DO YOU KNOW HIM WELL ENOUGH TO TRUST HIM?”

For the first time in this conservation, a side of the discussion raises their voice.

The question from Thomas causes the muttering in the family room to increase and obviously be centred around the conversation in the kitchen.

If Igor could run right now, he would be already across Europe and from Moscow to the other end of Europe is a good bit of running.

Igor does not move in any fashion in response to the question.

“WELL?”

Thomas is now red-faced. But then again, to hear your son verbally spit in the face of your life’s career and go side with a fake sport would cause anyone to be red-faced, wouldn’t it?

Igor nods.

“I can trust him. He wouldn’t have went through with this plan without being trustable and trusting me.”

Thomas can be heard cursing under his breath, as Igor gives his excuse.

“So you can’t trust him?”

Igor shakes his head.

“He is a member of the Brothers Zemganno with me. He is bound to be trustworthy.”

Igor’s last statement seems like the final straw for his father. Thomas slaps both his hands on the kitchen table and holds his head.

“Not only my son, but one of the finest atheltes, this industry has to offer!” The words leave his mouth full of pure disgust. But not just normal disgust, this is the type of disgust that wishes wrestling was never even invented. And boy is that said with disgust.

Igor also senses the disgust.

“I’m sorry dad!”

Igor motions forward, as a tear can be seen form in his eyes.

Thomas Arefiev looks up from his hands. He is already crying. “I never thought this day would come!”

Thomas shakes his head and motions his head back into his hands.

Igor drops his head, a single tear falling down his cheek.

“Get... Out!”

The line comes as shock to Igor. He looks over at his father.

“Get... Out!”

This time, Igor expects the line and can finally believe his eyes. He stands up as his father refuses to watch him leave.

“Goodbye!”

Igor looks down at his crying father, and bows his head to the floor. He then turns and exits the room slowly. As he passes the pictures on the wall, the emotions come flooding back to him.

Igor stops at a picture of himself. He touches the picture, remembering the time, place and what he was doing.

“I’m sorry...” He whispers to the picture. Igor then turns, and walks past the living room. Each of the family watch him. He nods, and walks out of the room, and down the hall.

As he reaches the front door, the tears can be held back no more.

He has left his family for wrestling. He has left behind his life-long trade.

He has a right to cry. But somehow the sacrifices he will make are not getting easier.
