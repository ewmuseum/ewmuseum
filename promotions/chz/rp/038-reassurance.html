Reassurance
Written: 6/24/02

People do not know how much the game means to me, it is where my father ran wild winning many prestige’s titles in his tenure. It is my duty to continue the legacy that he started and that is why I’m alone with myself separating myself from the hype that is surrounding Wednesday Wild.

Today was cold and in the distance on this thirty acre land Shammon was sitting on the porch of a small white cottage with his head bowed. It was very quiet to the point where you could only hear the flapping wings of an eagle fly overhead. He wasn’t dressed fancy, just some blue jeans, a white t-shirt and some white tennis shoes. When he lifted his head, an unusual fire and determination was easily seen through his hazel eyes.

He went to walk into the cottage when a black BMW screeched its way through the dirt. There was a moment of silence until his spokeswoman; Shania Johnson stepped out in her short pink flower skirt and a white blouse that fit tightly showing off her large breast. She cautiously walked across the dirt flinging her blonde hair to the side and Shammon shook his head in disappointment.

“So how did it go?” we hear Shammon ask

“It went great,” she said while sitting on the porch beside Shammon “are you ok, you seem very somber.”

He only smiled as he watched the tree leaves fall from the redwood trees in the distance; he looked to his right and saw a deer frolicking through the thirty acres of land that belonged to him. Shania rubbed her smooth brown cheeks and wrapped her arms around Shammon who seemed to be looking very depressed. He sighed looking up at the clouds moving along the blue sky he chuckled to himself as she looked on in confusion.

“You’re scaring me dear,”

“Yeah I’m scaring the heck out myself.” He said jokingly

She breathed a sigh of relief knowing that Shammon has not lost his sense of humor and she pulled out a small black box that seemed to have a ring inside. He looked at the box and his eyes widened; he looked at Shania as she gently hit him on his chin. He opened the box only to see a ring that had quite a big diamond that was in shape of two double S’s.

“This was my father’s ring that he always wore,” he says while looking at Shania “how did you get this?”

“From your Aunt, she said that it was the right time to give it to you.”

Tears rolled down his eyes as he slipped it on his ring finger and it glow was very radiant yet reassuring. He looked up at the sky once more and shook his in agreement as if he were acknowledging something. Shania put her arms around him once more as the gazed into the forest that was a couple of miles away. She gently wiped the tears from his eyes as he rubbed his hands together, he felt a weird warm wind crawling up his back blowing his long short frizzy hair in a circular motion.

“So, what are you thinking about right now?”

“Vega, he is unusual in so many ways, plus wrestling a blind man calls for strategy.” He said while stroking goatee.

As he gazed into the ring it seemed like he was mesmerized by its beauty and he could not hear anything but the howling of the wind. Slowly but surely he faded into a daydream and the empty thirty acre land that they were own turned into an all white room that he was the only person in. He also found that he was in a baggy cut off shirt, with blue khaki pants and white moccasins. Shammon looked to his left to see, his father in a white metallic suit leaning up against the wall.

“Father… is that you?” Shammon said hesitantly

“Yes it is,” he said while rubbing his bald head “you have grown so much and I wish my time was cut so short.”

He slowly rose to his feet approaching his father who was still leaning up against the wall. His father held out his hand for him to stop and he obeyed, he reached into his pocket pulling out a small crystal clear ball that was emitting a strange light. He placed on the ground and an image of Shania Johnson formed, but it was in holographic form. He went to touch her and his hands went straight through, and his father chuckled walking away from the wall and towards his son.

“She is quiet a beauty,” he said while standing next to Shammon “what are you waiting for?”

“What do you mean?”

He shook his head and slapped in the back of his head, as Shammon rubbed his head as it stung with his father shaking his head in shock. As Shammon walked a little closer to her his father stood back nodding his head in approval and as he looked back he saw his father crying tears of joy.

“I hate myself, instead of being with you I’m watching you grow up from here. Just remember, wrestling is a game treat it like that but that woman is your life.”

“It’s my fault you’re here; maybe if I wasn’t a prodigy everything would be well.”

“Things happened for a reason, you are the best son anyone could have and good luck on Wednesday.”

Suddenly as Shammon looks back a blinding light comes in his vision taking him back on his porch where he is still gazing into the ring. Shania is shaking his frantically and looks a little worried, and he finally gazes in her eyes given her some reassurance.

“Don’t scare me like that!” she scolded

“Sorry, what would you say if I take you out to dinner?”

“I would like that?”
