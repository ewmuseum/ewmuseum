Moving To Canada (Part 1)
Written: 7/29/02

The scene fades into the outside of a small yellow house at the end of a dead end street. There are twenty or thirty cars parked in the driveway, in the street, and a few in the front yard. People crowd the front yard talking and milling around. There is a large grill setup off to the right of the yard near the driveway where a man who appears to be in his mid forties or so stands flipping hamburgers, steaks, and chicken breasts as other people line up to get food. The inside of the house is not as alive. There are three people inside the house seated on a tan colored couch watching a small television. The three men are Jake Schultz, and his two band mates, Chris and Cory. Jake is clad in a pair of dark blue mesh shorts with a small white Adidas logo by his right knee, a white t-shirt with sleeves cut off it, on the front of the shirt is a big roman numeral II in the same dark blue color as his shorts. The back of the shirt boasts the saying “Chaos often breeds life, when order breeds habit.” Cory is wearing just a pair of blue jean shorts and black sandals. Chris is wearing a pair of bright orange swimming trunks, a white tank top, and a white pair of tennis shoes. They are watching someone on T.V. talk about something, but it can’t be heard. As the man finished up talking Jake reaches for the remote control and turns off the T.V. He sets the remote back down and then looks at Chris, blinks once, then starts laughing hysterically. The two others soon join him. After a few moments of laughing Jake regains his composure and starts to talk while trying to regain his breath at the same time.

“Whoa, what a moron,” says Jake in between gasps for air. “Did you hear him? ‘Will I be ready?’ ‘Do I feel lucky?’ ‘Am I a failure?’ Well Matt, let me answer your questions; yes, I don’t need to, and hell no. But let me ask you this, Matt. Will you just shut the hell up? I wasted all the time listen to you harass some kid, ask me questions that you already know the answer to, and talk about Big Brother, no one watches that show,” says Jake who had finally caught his breath and had stood up to walk over to the refrigerator.

“That guy is a joke,” says Cory as he too stands up, but just to stretch his legs. “Did you hear him say he wanted you to impress the missus and bench press her and the bed? What was that all about?” asks Cory as he sits back down on the couch next to Chris.

“I don’t know, he contradicted everything he said too. First he said no one on the roster impressed him, and then he says that he knows nothing about you or your wrestling ability, so of course you wouldn’t impress him. And he said that he could have spent that whole time running his mouth like he didn’t do it. Isn’t saying that he is going to end everyone’s career running his mouth?” asks Chris as he reaches over and grabs the remote and turns the T.V. back on and changes the channel to Nickelodeon. “Are you ready maties? Aye, aye Captain. I can’t hear you…” Chris goes on to sing the rest of the Sponge Bob Square Pants theme song with his eyes glued to the T.V.

“He is such a retard, Chris I mean. C’mon, he isn’t moving for another half-hour, let’s go outside and see what’s happening out there, this whole thing is for me after all,” says Jake as he heads over to the front door. Cory stands up from the couch and walks over to the door and heads out after Jake. Outside most of the commotion has calmed down; most people are seated at one of the many plastic picnic tables eating. Jake and Cory walk over to a long table with food lined up all down it and grab a paper plate. They head down the line filling their plates up with all kinds of food. They then head over to one of the only empty tables and sit down.

“So, do you think this Matt Hunt guy is going to be tough?” asks Cory as he cuts into his steak, stabs it with his fork, and pops a piece into his mouth and begins chewing.

“I don’t know. I don’t want to get too cocky, but if wrestles as poorly as talks trash, then no, it should be a piece of cake,” says Jake. He then takes a big bite out of his hamburger. “Your dad always was the best with a grill,” he adds as he takes another bite out of the burger.

“Yeah he was. Anyways, what was that deal about bench-pressing the missus and the mattress to impress her?” asks Cory who still has a confused look on his face about that.

“I don’t know, it has to be some stupid British thing, like Big Brother, no one in America watches that damn show,' says Jake as he swallows the last bite of his hamburger. He then takes a drink of his Pepsi then starts in on his baked beans.

“Wow, you must have been hungry,” Cory as he looks on amazed at how fast Jake at his hamburger. “So you’re really leaving Barberton, that’s weird,” adds Cory as he wipes up some rigatoni sauce that he spilled on his shirt.

“Yeah, it’s weird for me too. I’ve been here since I was born, but it makes more sense this way. With the majority of CHZ shows being in Canada it is a lot easier for to just move there instead of traveling from hotel to hotel, even though I’m sure I’ll end up doing that anyways,” says Jake who has just finished scraping up the last little bit of food off of his plate.

“You are the fastest eater I’ve ever seen in my life. So where do you plan to live at up north?” asks Cory who is then startled by someone tapping him on the shoulder. He spins his head around the see Chris standing behind him. “Ah, Chris, I see Sponge Bob is over, pull up a chair,” says Cory as he slides his own chair over a little bit to make room for Chris.

“Yeah, this was a great one, Squidward…” Jake interrupts Chris before he can even start he reenactment of Sponge Bob.

“Yeah, umm, we already saw that one, it was great,” says Jake, even though it’s easy to tell he is lying. Jake and Cory have both now finished their food and Cory grabs both the plates and walks away with them over towards the trash can.

“So what time is that guy supposed to be here?” asks Chris as he pushes Cory’s chair out of the way and scoots up to the table.

“I don’t know, he just said sometime this afternoon, so he could be here in a few seconds, or he might not be here for another couple of hours, who knows,” says Jake as he folds him arms on the table and lies his head down on them.

“Oh, I see. It sucks you have to go, but I guess it’s better for you. I’m sure you’ll be back though,” says Chris as he takes a drink of his pop that he brought outside with him. Jake’s head then pops back up from its resting place atop his folded arms.

“Yeah, I hope so. And by the way, thanks for helping me get the rest of my stuff out of psycho bitch’s apartment yesterday,” says Jake as he replaces his head on top of his arms but it is only for a second as he pops his head back up when he hears the sound of a large truck speeding down the street. He looks down the street to see a large green moving van coming down the street. The van pulls up in front of the house and comes to a noisy stop. A short, fat man in a blue jumpsuit hops out of the driver’s seat of the truck.

“Is there a Jake Schultz here,” shouts the driver over the noise of all the people talking now that they have finished eating.

“Yeah, I’m right here,” Jake yells back as he stands up and walks over to the man leaning against his truck. “We’ll have it loaded up there soon, feel free to go eat if you want to, “ says Jake as he calls Chris, Cory, and a few other people over to help him load up the moving van. They bring all of the stuff out of the house and load in into the moving van in about twenty minutes.

“Hey moving man, we’ve got it all loaded up!” yells Jake to the fat driver who is seated at one of the picnic tables stuffing his face full of food.

“Okay, I’ll be right over,” responds the driver as he stuffs half a hamburger in his mouth. He wipes his mouth with a white paper napkin then rises from the table. He tosses his empty plate into a trashcan as he walks by and then heads over to his van. He hops in the driver’s seat and starts up the truck. Jake then walks over to his van, which is parked on the side of the road. He opens the driver’s side door and gets into the van. As he does this all the people at the party stand up and look at him. They all begin to clap and cheer and yell their ad-lib congratulations to Jake as he gets into the van and closes the door. Cory and Chris walk over to the van.

“Well man, I’m going to miss you,” says Chris as he shakes Jake’s hand. They then look at each other and then hug.

“I’m going to miss you too, but I’ll be back eventually,” says Jake as Chris steps away from the van and Cory steps closer to it.

“Well, it sure is going to be different without you around. And I guess this is it for Tension II, huh?” asks Cory who is overcome with sadness.

“Yeah, for now. But Tension II will be back together again as soon as I get back,” says Jake as he reaches out and hugs Cory too.

“Good luck in CHZ,” says Cory as he too steps away from the van. Jake then turns the key and starts the van up. He waves one last time to everyone outside the house before stepping on the gas and pulling away from the house. The moving van soon follows Jake on his quest to Canada.
