Preparations
Written: 6/14/02

Things have already begun hot and heavy for the new CHZ organization. With the announcement of the roster and the premiere show all the stars have already begun popping up everywhere letting their feelings be known. Among the new group of CHZ stars is another wrestling sensation by the name of Tyler Davidson. Tyler finds himself paired with a mystery partner taking on Amanda Flaton and Jack Williams. More on the mind of Tyler at the moment is the looming presence of the CHZ title tournaments. He, like the rest of the roster, has the chance at not one but two separate title tournaments. One way or another he plans on making the most of his opportunities.

It’s been a few days since Tyler has last been seen at the home of his father in Danbury, Connecticut. Tyler has still remained at the Davidson Estate for a few more days as he waits for the time to draw closer for Wild. While waiting for the day to come Tyler sits in the living room much like where you had last seen him and again watching television. Across from him sits his father, Hunter, once again simply sitting there and staring down into the pages of a book paying little attention to television next to him. Tyler with an impatient look on his face continues flipping through channels searching for something to watch.

“Why is there never anything worth a damn to watch on television?” Tyler yelled flipping the remote control in front of him and down onto the couch.

“Try picking up a book once in a while,” his father replied without looking up and turning a page in his book. “They can be quite entertaining themselves, even when there’s nothing to watch on television.”

“Yeah I know,” Tyler responded half mockingly. “I’d still rather watch television, maybe a good baseball game or something.”

“Seems your sports are dwindling down now,” Hunter commented as he takes his glance up to his son on the couch. “Basketball and hockey are both done leaving you with baseball and the world cup now. Must suck for you now don’t it?”

“I’d say it does,” Tyler agreed not catching onto the sarcasm. “It’s bad enough the Lakers and Red Wings were the teams that won the championships already too. Inter-league play has started up at least now. Maybe that will make some of these games interesting again in baseball.”

“Have to pay close attention to that Mets-Yankees series of course,” Hunter put in showing at least a little interest before turning back to his book. Before he can read any further though the doorbell lets out a loud ring grabbing both his and Tyler’s attention. “A little early for visitors isn’t it?”

“Who cares?” Tyler replied. “At least this can be something interesting.”

Tyler sprang up from his seat and headed towards the door swinging it back and open to find a much larger man standing on the other side. The man stood about 6’8 and weighed 270lbs with short dark hair and a matching goatee. Longtime Tyler Davidson fans would of course instantly recognize his trainer and longtime friend, Daniel [name withheld] as the man standing on the other side of the door. [name withheld] stands there at the door with a cocky grin on his face as Tyler eyes him back.

“It’s about damn time you got here [name withheld],” Tyler yelled at him before extending a hand.

“It’s a bit of a drive from New York you know,” [name withheld] replied accepting the hand and returning a firm shake as he walked in the door. “I can’t just magically appear on your doorstep anytime I need too.”

“Excuses, excuses,” Tyler said as he closed the door behind [name withheld] and spotted his father coming out of the living room.

“About time you got here Dan,” Hunter said upon seeing [name withheld] while giving a grin to his son.

“Like father like son I guess,” [name withheld] responded sarcastically. “You two never change.”

“What brings you out here today anyway?” Hunter asked.

“Tyler wanted to discuss some plan of his with me,” [name withheld] answered looking back at Tyler. “He never did tell me what it was though really.”

“Probably has to do with that new organization he joined,” Hunter replied closing the book which he still carried in his hands. “I’ll leave you two to discuss that then.”

Hunter turns and walks up the stairs heading towards the library on the upper floor leaving [name withheld] and Tyler down below.

“Alright Tyler,” [name withheld] began. “What is this idea of yours?”

“Well….” Tyler began slowly before his voice dropped off as he was obviously not confident about what he had to say or ask.

“Oh just spit it out,” [name withheld] shouted at him giving him a soft shove in the process.

“Ok I signed with an independent promotion recently and I need my manager back there again,” Tyler managed to spit out so quickly that it was somewhat difficult to understand.

“What?” [name withheld] replied showing little if any shock. “It sounded like you said you wanted me to return as your manager yet again.”

“Well you are still my manager,” Tyler answered. “Now I just want you to extend that to another promotion.”

“And just where is this promotion?” [name withheld] questioned Tyler.

“Canada,” Tyler answered quietly with his head down.

“Canada?!” [name withheld] exclaimed. “You’ve got to be kidding me.”

“Nope,” Tyler replied.

“B-b-but its cold,” [name withheld] responded.

“So?” Tyler asked.

“It’s cold,” [name withheld] repeated again.

“What does that have to do with anything?” Tyler asked again.

“But it’s so damn cold there,” [name withheld] repeated yet again shaking his head in disbelief. “Why on earth would you want to go to Canada?”

“It’s a new opportunity for me,” Tyler answered him. “It looks like it could be a lot of fun, innovative and a lot of new faces to compete with.”

“Ok fair enough,” [name withheld] agreed. “Now why exactly do I have to come along?”

“I need someone I can trust to watch my back,” Tyler explained. “That would be you of course.”

“So because you don’t trust anyone I have to freeze?” [name withheld] asked.

“Yeah basically,” Tyler replied. “You’ll get used to it pretty quickly.”

“Great,” [name withheld] mumbled before continuing on filled with sarcasm. “Just what I always wanted, a trip to Canada. Yay.”

“So you’re in?” Tyler asked hopefully.

“Yeah,” [name withheld] answered sounding defeated. “I’m in, for now at least. I’m telling you though if it as cold as I think it is you might find yourself on your own.”

“Ok I’ll remember that,” Tyler said. “You’ll enjoy it there. You know it.”

“Oh yeah,” [name withheld] replied. “Last time I was in Canada, I left with third degree burns and a couple of buckets of blood missing. Real good time.”

“Quit your crying and let’s get out of here,” Tyler said. “Time to head to the gym.”

[name withheld] having nothing else to say slowly follows Tyler as they head down the hallway as the scene fades to black.
