The Tryout.
Written: 8/11/02

The Tryout-Part 1:

A bright Friday morning, two days after the great victory of mine at the first Wensday Wild in CHZ.. Knowing I clearly won the match boldly on my own, I knew this was a new stage of Stimpo’s wrestling career. Leaving the donkey for dead on the island, since the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs were now
gone as was their 'legacy.' The new legacy for me had begun though, I had noticed that my singing voice was actually a use for me now! All I needed
now was a band to start my new singing career. Now being in Canada, the home area to my newest federation, the CHZ. I sat there in my rental house, coming with a small pool in the back area and a balcony from my bedroom. The roof was a triangle shape and with shingling across the entire roof.
Healthy grass fills my front yard as the porch has a cuddling swing and two chairs set up with, of course, stairs walking up to the house. I stood outside the house sitting down on the swing, swinging and unfolding the paper
in search of perhaps, a tryout chance. Wearing my trademark mask and also wearing the casual wear of a regular CHZ T-shirt and some windpants from Adidas. Passing by the sports section and the businesses, I turned to the classifieds.

'Bands, bands, bands. There!' I got excited as I ran my finger down and found the first chance reading as follows:

Brand new band, eh? Yes, that's right, the Crazy Bit*ches are starting their group up for the second time! New members needed in the following positions:
Synthesizer man, Lead Singer, Bass player, Electric player and a conga player. Yes, we need every position! At 12:00 noon, Canada time, make your appearance at the Canada fair today! Prepare your own song for the tryout, we hope to see you there, eh? Girls only folks!

I folded the paper back up considering the offer.

'Well, I think their Canadian, I hate that, ‘eh?’ thing!' I threw the paper onto the ground. 'But, they do need a lead singer, and after handling those
crazy Mexican men. Who knows, maybe I could unleash my ‘Perfect Note.’' I knew I should try out, but what to prepare?' I contemplated my choices.

I went inside to find what I had in store of my record collection. I ran my finger through the choices of either Elvis remixes by Dr. Dre, John Denver sharing a country duet with Ozzy Osbourne, ‘O Canada’ by Yanni and finally, Mick Palmer’s greatest hits-including his hit single, 'Dildos, Dildos Everywhere!'

'Now how’d that get in there?!' I said tossing Harrington’s fake record out the window. 'Damn, these are hard choices! I could go with ‘Ice Ice Baby,’ I heard that’s popular, but then again, ‘Ghetto Superstar’ is calling to me!' I let out a sigh of frustration. 'So many choices, so little time!' I said to myself checking the clock, which read ‘9:00 a.m.’

A smile came across my face, 'Three hours, three hours to get my chance at perhaps becoming a newest member of the...' I grabbed the paper again to check the name once more, 'The Crazy Bitches! I mean, The Crazy Bit, astrec, ches? Oh well, it’s a band none the less.' I immediately started tossing records out in a flash, playing some preview songs before deciding to either throw it away or use it for the audition. I must have went through seventy different records before I found, the one. The one song that would soon propel me to the top! If not, just make the band.

***************(Scene Change to the Tryout area)******************

Extreme exhaust was sweeping all around the area for the tryouts for this great, new up and coming bands in the great country of U.S. Junior. At least three hundred different people were in a huge straight line, the line was so incredibly long, I couldn't even see the front of it at the beginning. I stood behind the last person in the long, curling line and took a look at my watch.

'Twelve exactly,' I encouraged myself, 'This shouldn't take any time at all.'

Many, many hours went by in the blazing sun as it seemed that the record I was ready to sing to was sticking to me! Sweat dripped down from my chin as not even the ants would move, scurrying around was their usual business. The time was soon clocked in at three hours past noon and my position in the line had not moved! Then, in a flash of an eye, I don't know if it was a hallucination or just plainly a miracle, everyone in the line left! One by one at the same time, as if they were brainwashed, the just all left the line completely. My choices were now narrowed down to follow them and risk crazy occurances, or try out for the band.

'Follow or audition....Choices.' I told myself in a peculair intrest.

I decided that it would be much better if I followed my dreams of perhaps, one day becoming a singing superstar, what way better than to go straight to the source, a band! I walked myself down to the entrance to the stage for the tryouts. Three girls were already standing in front of the stage in chairs, lounging with clipboards in their hands and pencils fully equipped. The stage was a small rectangle layout with a black layout behind it. Another girl is on the stage currently crying for some reason as the other girls off the stage are are telling security to get the poor lady off the stage!

'Off with you! Next!' The middle girl called as she turned and saw that I was next. I took a big gulp of pride and decided to take my chance in this band. I stood erect, (not that way!) and took a big, slow walk up to the stage and faced the girls who already had their jaws dropped.

'Is this some type of joke?' The middle girl asked me in surprise. I just looked around and shook my head at her. She seemed completely flabergasted by this appearance of mine and I just continued as regular. I took my knowledge of auditions and I thought the first they would do is ask me about myself, so I started without them asking me.

'Well, My full name is Brendon Stimson, but my amigos call me 'Stimpo,' I've been singing now for about twelve hours and ever since then, I wanted to become a singer!' I had completed my full resume.

'Right...well this is a girls band.' She told me in a mocking way.

'Are you gonna ask me to perform or what?' I still asked them in spite of it being a girls band.

'Whatever, eh?' She just shrugged to me as I nodded to the DJ thinking he had a record to play.

I had forgotten what I took from my house from my stack of records so I tried to sneak a peek at to what the DJ was ready to play. To my both surprise and horror, 'Ghetto Superstar' began to play! I knew I wasn't ready, but I had to continue for my singing career's sake. The introduction soon ended as I began my 'singing.'

(In a very military type voice)'Ghetto Superstar, (pause) That is what you are. (pause) Coming from far away, reaching for the stars. Run away with me, to another place. We can rely on eachother, (pause) uh huh.' I smiled as I ended the first verse, the chorus. The DJ immediatly pulled the record off the table making it stop playing. My heart was beating a mile a minute hoping that maybe I had just done it, maybe I've just become one of the Crazy Bit*ches!!

They sat there in their seats awestruck, I thought I had done too well to capture them. I awaited patiently while the three women huddled together for a few minutes. I looked around, bored and out of boredom, I threw the microphone at the DJ! Hitting him right in the side, he looked at me as I whistled and kicked my feet innocently. The girls then came out of their huddle and took a deep breath as I smiled wide.

'No.' She said directly to my face nearly crushing my entire self esteem. My heart sank into my stomach as I nearly started to cry. She then continued on,

'It's not because of your...talent, it's simply because this is a girls ba--' I cut her off before she finished by sticking my hand forward and face turning away from them.

I shielded my head from embarrestment as the girls laughed at me and the DJ pushed me out, most likely from the microphone blow. I ran out in shame from the tryout in a hurry to get back to my horrible yet, Canadian house. I ran inside crying my eyes out and sat down in my living room on my couch facing my TV with all the windows shut for privacy reasons.

“WHY?! I HAVE THE TALENT!! DAMN THE CRAZY BIT, ASTREC, CHES!!” I screamed with my sadness still flowing strong.

I had to get some fresh air now, after sitting inside crying for at least an hour. I whiped my face off, it was extremely red near the eyes but the mask hid it well. I tried to stand tall outside my nice suburban, Canadian rental home while still sniffeling slightly.

“It’s not all that bad, they’ll be other bands.” I reassured myself.

I walked up a few blocks from my home until I finally passed by a dark alley. **Note: never walk down dark alleys, especially in the day time!** I heard a quiet voice whispering to me as I kept reassuring myself of other bands.

“That’s right Brendon, there are other bands.” The voice spoke to me.

I instantly turned around and saw two dark yet, American idol type bodies. Before I could say a word, they snatched me from my walking position into the dark alley ending the promo at that very point.
