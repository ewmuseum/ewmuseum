Rememberance
Written: 6/23/02

He shouldn’t have died…

These are the four words that have been asked in my head and still there are no answers. All that is left is the horrible silence that follows afterwards. What was I thinking? Was there something that I could have done to stop this? No there wasn’t, I will have to live with the pain for the rest of my life.

Nothing could prepare me for death; even with my accomplishment of graduating Harvard at the age of seventeen there was nothing that could help me with his death. Some say that the best way to go out is to know your life was complete, but as he died he was in pain. It made me sink to a new level of guilt that I can never make amends for.

When will the pain stop…?

It can never stop; it can only get stronger as the years past. All you are left with is the memories that could never be taken away. With his dream gone there is no hope, just hatred for the one that caused his death. There is only revenge, an obsession to confront the killer and simply ask him “why?” You need closure to ease the pain and that closure eludes you with the pain becoming stronger.

Maybe, just maybe there was some explanation why he had to die, but none could be for a good reason. Every night I sit down hammering out the different scenarios that can result to his death and it just doesn’t add up.

It was early in the mourning and the sun was just coming up over the mountain tops, shining its light on the cemetery. Through the rows of the tombstones a young African American man, no older than eighteen, sat in front of a tombstone bowing his head in a moment of silence. His blue jeans were faded and he opened his green eyes gazing at the tombstone. His white collard shirt ruffled in the wind and his shoulder length black hair stood still as he gave a momentary smile before becoming serious once more.

“You didn’t have die,” he said “it shouldn’t have to end this way; you had so much to live for.”

His tears flowed down his cheeks and his sobbing echoed through the cemetery making a crow fly away from the tombstone he was perched on. He covered his face and just continued to cry until he felt someone tapping on his shoulder. He turned around and saw no one, but he heard a voice that sounded so familiar.

“It wasn’t your fault.”

He shook his head and let out a scream that made the birds scatter in the nearby tree, the voice he heard stopped and there was nothing but silence. He stood up looking down at the tombstone and saw the name Minoru Smith.

“Maybe one day you can forgive me, but I will never forgive myself.” He said softly.

He turned around and walked through the rows of tombstone kicking up the dead leaves that lay on the grass. He looked up at the gray sky and saw a cloud in a shape of donkey and started to smile. As he looked forward there was a young African American woman who was dressed in a black dress and held her sons hand. Her little boy was squirming around in his black suit and looked at the man with his hazel eyes.

“Hello mister, are you ok?” the boy asked with a smile on his face.

“Stay away son, he may be one of those thugs you see on the street.” She said while pulling him away

He shook his head as the woman pulled out a knife and started to swing wildly. He stood there continuing to shake his head and grabs her hand taking the knife away. He throws the knife down to the ground and his face was shaking with anger. The woman started to back away and tripped fearing for her life and he calmed down to help her up.

“Listen miss,” he said “my name is Shammon Smith and I am a Harvard graduate that teaches at a local high school.”

“I’m so sorry; I didn’t mean to offend you.”

He nods his head and continues walk through row of tombstones and she looks very confused. She grabs her son and jogs up to Shammon; she grabs his arm and looks him straight in his eyes. Her eyes started widen and she slowly steps back looking very scared. Her son looked at the man and showing no fear, she stepped up and hugged Shammon. He looked down at boy surprised and his mother slowly got back to her feet looking uneasy.

“You could have called,” she said “after the funeral it is like you disappeared.”

“I’m sorry… I did not know how to react to his death.” He said hesitantly

Shammon ripped the boy away from him and walked towards the black car that was parked along a bundle of roses. The woman looked confused and a tear rolled down her cheek and they walked towards the direction of the Minoru Smith grave. He looked back to see the two mourning over his deceased brother and wiped the tears from his eye managing to smile.

“Please forgive me.” He said softly.

He walked down the hill and to the black car that was parked. He looked to his right and saw a vision of his brother in a white robe. He backed up against the car and his was looking a little flushed as his brothers spirits started to slowly approach him.

“Brother is that really you?” he said

“Shammon, you need to forgive yourself and get on with your life.”

He looked up at him and shook his head trying to get in the car to find that it was locked. His brother placed his hand on his shoulder and Shammon looked at his reflection in the car which looks sad and alone. He looked at Minoru as he sat down on the ground and he sat up straight looking at his brother tap his fingers on his thighs.

“You are a prodigy and you were good at everything, hell I was even jealous at the time.”

“*sighs deeply* Minoru, I’m so sorry you would be alive if it wasn’t for me.”

Minoru shook his head in disappointment but then starts to laugh. Shammon looked at him with a weird look and he stood up patting his brother on the back.

“One thing I want you to do, that is to look after my wife and kid.”

“Consider it done.”

His brother spirit started to fade away and he looked at his brother’s family who were still kneeling at his grave. He sighed and leaned against his car banging his head against it and starts to remember the day his brother was murdered.

Flashback: Day of the Murder

It was exactly seven years ago and in the Smith household young Shammon Smith was dressed in some blue jean overalls looking at the mourning cartoons. He had his backpack sitting right next to him as his mother, who was in her pink robe, sat a plate that contained bacon, eggs, French toast, and pancakes, was sat in front of him. She bent down and he started to play with his mom’s curly brow hair, and in retaliation she started to tickle him all over his body as he squirmed around laughing hysterically.

“So, how is my smart man today?”

“Not too good mom, the shape of their heads and bodies look a bit abnormal, which could have been avoided by using the simplest cg program.” He said to his mother with a frustrated look on his face.

She looked at her son with a confused look on her face and went back to the kitchen. His older brother came walking in from the right and sat right next to Shammon looking at the cartoons with his head tilted to the left.

“Cartoon with no CG properties, never should happen”

“Yep, you would think people would get with the program.”

Minoru patted down his small afro and just then their there father dressed in a red wind breaker suit came in smiling. He was carrying a blue athletic bag and had his wrestling boots around his neck. Minoru and Shammon stood up as they father walked towards them given both a hug.

“How are my two smart guys?” he asked while his boots dangles around his neck.

“Yeah, I’m going to show my little brother the ropes.”

“I’m so scared,” Shammon said “I am the only thirteen year old on a college campus.”

Minoru and his father started to laugh but he was not. He picked up his backpack and the three left out of the door. While they were all outside a mysterious car drove up and three men dressed in all black, took Minoru and Shammon from their father driving off on the street. His father wasted little time hopping in his red Ferrari and chasing after the kidnappers.

Inside of the car, the two were sitting in the middle of two buff men that were on each side smoking a cigar. A smaller man in the passenger’s seat turned around showing his pale white face and he pointed at Shammon.

“Do you know why you two are here?”

“No, please tell us we are so interested.” They both said sarcastically

“There is a formula that only Shammon knows and if you do not deliver Minoru dies.”

Shammon’s eyes opened up widely and he looked at Minoru who showed no emotion. The mysterious man smiled as they turned into an abandon power plant. The two men threw Minoru and Shammon out of the car to the hot concrete outside. Shammon is the first to get up and is instantly put in a hammerlock and escorted into the power plant. His bother soon followed after as he is lifted up on one of the henchmen’s shoulders.

Inside of the plant it was dusty and the smell of rotten flesh was overwhelming as the henchmen threw the two brothers down on the concrete floor. Shammon was pushed up to a laptop as his father was ushered in by another henchmen and the mysterious man walked in dusting off his leather jacket and adjusting his black slacks. They began pushing Minoru and his father up the stairs wrapping a rope around their necks and gave thumbs up to everyone downstairs.

“Now this gets interesting,” he said “now if you don’t finish the formula your brother and father die before your eyes.”

Shammon looked up at his brother and father as they are shaking there head no. He turns around glancing at the lap top and he walks towards it. The three henchmen and the mysterious man are along beside him as there is a DNA chain that is set up on the screen. He looks up one last time and then steps away from the screen to the disapproval of the men standing behind him.

“This is not right you’re an evil man.”

“You don’t do this; two members of your family will die.” He says persuasively

He sighs and walks back up the screen and starts typing. Slowly the DNA strand starts to change, the henchmen started celebrating as the head boss held up his hand and they stopped immediately. He continues to type as the DNA looks very tightly spiraled and turns around to the mysterious man.

“Gees that was fast,” he said “maybe you should join me as a scientist.”

“Sorry, I don’t work for evil me now let my brother and father go.”

He gives a signal to the men upstairs and they drop his brother and father as he watches their necks break. Suddenly from the left, a huge group of police officers came in and arrested the men as Shammon looked up at to see his brother and father suspended up in air motionless. His mother can running through the entrance and stood in front of him with an evil look on her face.

“This is your fault, they be alive if it wasn’t for your stupid decision.”

“But, I did what I thought was the right thing to do.”

He looked at his mother and walked right passed her, she looks on and starts feel guilty as Shammon runs out of the door as it slowly fades into the picture of the present Shammon Smith still looking at his brother’s family and runs up the hill. He quickly walks up the row of tombstones and crouches down by the little boy.

“So, how long are you in town?”

“Well, someone bought our house and we were left homeless.”

He sighs and gives the young woman his house key and she looks shocked but happy. He scratches his head and reaches in his pocket and pulls out a map. Shammon hands the woman the map as she looks at the green outline that goes to the direct location to his house.

“I want you to stay with me until you can get on your feet.”

“Thanks Shammon, your brother was right about you.” She says as a tear roll down her cheek.

He nods his head as she gives him a kiss on his cheek and Shammon walks away down to his car. He looks back to see her little son jumping up and down for joy which brings as smile to his face. He hops in his car driving out of the cemetery and across the street to the huge burgundy high school. As he parked in the teachers parking space all the students looked at him very weird as he stepped out dressed like one of the high school kids. He pulls out a briefcase and begins to walk through the crowd of kids and into the school. He looked around and saw kids sitting on a couple of benches talking and giggling but they stop once he walks by.

As he walks through the campus, the student body seemed to become quite and Shammon could hear the sounds of his tennis shoes hitting the ground. He takes a right and enters the teacher lounge; the teachers stop what they are doing and stare at the newest member of the staff. An old woman in her late sixties adjusted her tight white dress starting to strut towards him.

“So we have fresh meat,” she said “well maybe we can do something later.”

She grabs his ass and he jumps back looking very disgusted as the principal just came walking through the door. She was dressed in a nice black pinstriped jacket and a tight skirt that showed her smooth brown legs. She stood right by Shammon and the rest of the teachers quickly shut their mouths as she pulled up a chair.

“This is Shammon Smith; he is our new History teacher.”

Everyone waves at Shammon as the principal goes to her office and Shammon made his way out of the lounge. Right when he takes one step the bell rings and he finds himself stuck in the middle of a thousand students. Sighing deeply, he weaves through the students jogging up the steps to room 209 and a group of students are there waiting for him. He opens the door and lets the group of students into the class. Inside of the room were four sets of long black tables that each had four outlets and a four gas faucets. As the students started to set in their seats, Shammon went up to his desk placing his briefcase down. He popped his briefcase open and pulled out a paper that seemed to be his first “a” when he first started school.

“Class, my name is Mr. Smith and welcome to History.”

Most of the class claps except for three men that have lettermen jackets on and he looks in the back to see the three talking. He turns around to the white board behind them and in red marker, he wrote the word love. The students were confused as he walked in front of his desk and raised his hands in the air.

“If this word was felt and expressed maybe this world would be a better place.”

The three students in the back stood up and started to walk towards the door; Shammon stepped in front of it and directed them to their seats. Suddenly he heard the bang on the door to see a young man fall to the floor as blood spilled on the floor. He looked out the door to see a drunken man chasing after him and stops him in his tracks.

“Who are you?” Shammon demands.

“Move out my way I have to teach that boy a lesson.” The drunken man slurs.

He threw him to the wall and he started to shake, Shammon threw him to the ground placing his foot in the man’s throat.

“Does that feel good,” He said while putting more pressure on the man’s neck “if he shows up to school with a scratch on his body I will hunt you down.”

He moves his foot away from the man’s throat and runs back to the classroom as everyone is scared. He looks at the boy spitting up blood and pulls out a cellular phone calling 911, he waits for a moment as he hears a ladies voice on the other line.

“What is your emergency?”

“I have a student who is suffering from internal bleeding that seems to be affecting his intestine and voice box.” Shammon said while keeping himself calm.

“Sir, we are on our way.”

He hung up the phone and sat the boy up right as he continued to cough up blood, shortly after the EMT came in securing the young boy. Shammon stood up and walked back to his desk, the room was rather quiet and some of the students had tears in their eyes.

“Let me tell you a little story,” he said “when I was thirteen and ready to go to college, my brother and I were kidnapped by some evil men. My father naturally gave chase and both my brother and Father were taken up stairs of the abandon power plant. They said if I didn’t give them the formula that they would die. So I had no choice but to give them the formula, but they killed them by breaking their necks in front of my eyes. Shortly after, my mother disowned me not wanting anything to do with me.”
