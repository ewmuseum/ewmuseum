Salvation: Part 2
Written: 7/28/02

I was sitting outside Christina's room now, and the first thing I noticed was that the hallway was a lot darker than her vibrant room. She wanted me to leave so that she could have some time to herself, but I didn't blame her. She had a vibrant personality, the kind that makes you feel like you've always known her, but she was suffering now. Her experiences were painful at best, and when I examined the picture on her back of 'The War', she broke down and started crying. She needed the time to herself so she could be alone, and I could still hear muffled sobs coming from behind the door. It was always things like that which made me ask myself if I was in the right line of work.

My cell rang off inside my leather cloak, breaking my chain of thought as I instantly answered it. 'Matt here.'

'How is she?'

I recognized the voice as that of my superior, only known to me as 'X'. That's the way The Disciples worked... you never really knew anything other than what you had to do. It was for their own security, or so I was told. In any case, X was sort of an old guy, and had a grumpy kind of commanding demeanor that I'd seen all too often. You could say that he was understanding underneath it all, but hey, he had a way of hiding it.

'Okay,' I answered in a whisper so she wouldn't hear, 'but she's a little distraught over it all.'

'You're surprised?' he asked as if I was a lunatic. Hell, after seeing some of things I'd seen in my line of work, I probably was.

'No,' I barked back, 'I'm just sayin', she's a little shaken by this all. I can't blame her though... if I had a divine picture carved into my back by some unknown force, I'd probably be crappin' myself too, eh?'

X stifled a laugh on the other end. 'You might smell better if you did. In any case, have you told her who you are yet?'

I paused for a second, wondering if I should tell him the truth or not. 'Well X... I didn't have to. She recognized me from some CHZ advertisements. So, yeah, I told her everything so she'd understand why I was there.'

X let out a somewhat exasperated sigh on the other end as if he didn't want to hear that. 'That makes things complicated kid.'

'How?'

'You gotta' get this case down and outa' the way soon now. If it lingers too long, The Disciples won't be too happy that she knows you're one of them.'

I shuddered to think what the group would do when pissed off about something. 'What would they do to her if I messed up?'

X paused for a second which made the hair on the back of my neck stand up. The guy spoke louder and more fluently than anyone I'd ever met, so when he stopped talking, it usually meant he didn't want to tell me something.

'They won't kill her,' he assured me, 'but they have surgical techniques of removing what our cases know. As for you... The Confessors would come down hard, and you know what that's like.

I bit my lower lip and gave some through to what X had said. I didn't want to know about the surgical techniques and I knew The Confessors well enough that 'coming down hard' really meant it'd hit the fan. They were kind of sadistic if you asked me, being the branch of the group that dealt with interrogation and torture. It almost seemed as if their personalities were removed and replaced with a lust to hurt someone.

'I never understood that,' I bluntly stated, releasing my sore lip. 'I mean, they make sure the people we drop the ball with never know what's happening, but when we succeed, it's all find and dandy?'

'The Disciples always worked like that Matt,' X explained in that tone he used that almost told you that he knew everything, 'and they always will. When they fail, the people who are having the hard time with their lives and situations often become angered and blame us for failing. So, they become unpredictable. They go running to the press, media... they shout it from the rooftops. However, when we succeed, they're grateful that it's over. You've seen the looks of relief on the faces of those possessed yourself. They'd kiss you if you weren't such an ugly lookin' person.'

'Haha...' I sarcastically muttered.

'As I was saying, why rat out the guys who saved your life and possibly your immortal soul? I mean, for most of these people, the belief in God never hit them, and they're rocketed into it. You can't help but be grateful when you realize you were just taken off the path to Hell. So these are the people that keep our secret and work with us. The doctors that tell us about unexplained situations, the police that speak of strange arrests and the billionaires that fund us. Just a little Disciple 101 for ya' kid.'

I grinned a little and was about to come back with something snappy, but I stopped myself. Something wasn't right, like that feeling you get in your stomach when you know something is happening that shouldn't. I rested the cell by my side and held my breath, ignoring my heartbeat completely as I just stood and listened.

'Matt, are you still there?' the faint voice called from the cell.

I let out a sound of urgency when I realized she had stopped crying. I plowed through the door, causing it to swing around and smash into the wall on the other side. I staggered into the vibrant room and let my vision dart around. The bed... no. Window... still closed. I began my frantic search, looking under the bed, behind the dresser, scattering her clothes across the floor. I could feel the color drain from my skin as the shock of literally losing her began to set in, but then... I heard it.

Faint whispering, words that I couldn't understand. I followed the trail and found myself standing in front of her closet. I swung open the door, and staggered back.

She was sitting there with her arms wrapped around her knees, rocking back and fourth. Her pupils were dilated and her blue eyes had turned a dull chestnut. She was looking wide-eyed at me, but yet, her gaze didn't change even slightly when I opened the door and staggered back. At a closer look, she was looking through me, so I stepped off to the side and looked to the wall. A Metallica poster... perfect.

'I can see, I can see, I can see,' she continually chanted in a harsh whisper, over and over again as a tear began to stream down her cheek. I knelt down and placed my hand on her shoulder.

'Chris?' I asked in a voice that I wished came out as strong, but was a little shaken after the frantic search I had just been through.

Christina slowly turned her eyes towards me, not even blinking or changing her gaze of fright. The look itself reflected nothing of the young woman I had been talking to earlier and sent a chill down my spine.

'Can you see it?' she stammered out, shaking a little.

'See what?'

'He's there Matt... he's there.'

I looked over to the wall and this time, didn't even see the poster. Sitting there was a creature with a dull and dark red pigmented skin. It had wings like a bat and black hair that looked like tentacles resting at the sides of its face. It's eyes were emotionless and hollow, filled completely black as if they were whole pupils that never blinked. He seemed kind of bony, but threatening with his hooked claws and fangs. Even I wasn't ready to see that.

'He's there, he's there, he's there,' Christina chanted, rocking once again as strands of her black hair began to cloud her face.

'What are you?' I demanded of the creature, but it didn't so much as react. 'I asked.. what are you!?'

I shook a little as I got up, and I had to grab the golden plated bible at my side to keep my composure. I kept reminding myself that God was with me, but when you're staring into the eyes of something that always seems to be staring at you with an emotionless glare, it's hard to keep faith. If it hissed, clawed, bit or jumped at me, I would have been fine. It didn't though... it just sat there, watching, waiting... and it ate at my mind as I pondered why.

'Demon,' I muttered, 'be gone from this place as God commands it.' My voice was shaken and I stuttered a little. Again, the creature didn't so much as move.

I began to falter. I reached into my cloak with a shaking hand and pulled out a dagger. Instantly, I dropped it. I went to pick it up, but the blade moved from my hand as I did so, reacting like the reflection in the mirror, always keeping just out of my reach. When I looked up, the creature looked as it did, except it was now smiling coldly, showing its fangs and making my heart jump into my throat. It was the first time I could say that I had seen evil in its purest form.

I lost my cool and dove for the 'demon'. As my hand touched its skin, I felt nothing but a blinding flash. Memories of pain, hurt and anguish began to enter my mind. Things about my past that should have stayed buried. My brother, Spectre, who I no less than hated. My dead parents who I no less than loved. Things of the past and visions of torment all entered my mind. I could hear Christina screaming, and just like that, there was another flash and I was on my back.

The screaming slowly began to die, and I realized it was me screaming. I was fully shaking now and noticed it just wasn't out of fear, but I could see my breath in the air. The room was completely dark, and I couldn't see much farther than a few feet in front of me. When I lifted my head, I was a mere foot away from the creature who was still silently smiling, as emotionless as it was motionless. In terror, I quickly clawed my way back to the closet and sat beside Christina.

'Please, make it stop. Please...' she begged me, but this time, I had no answers for her. I looked to my right and saw her face in a look of sheer pain, and she again asked me to make it stop. From under her black T-shirt, I saw a glowing, and I lifted the back of her shirt up. The still picture was now animated, all parts moving as the battle raged, demons against angels. The demons were battling back the angels, but the meaning eluded me.

I ran my hand over a couple of the pictures. The pictures of the angels sent a warm sense through my body where when I touched a demon, I saw the flash and the images again. It was too real, and I couldn't even begin to understand what was going on.

'Please Michael, please make it stop,' she continued, and it was then that I realized she wasn't talking to me. A blinding flash entered the room, sending spots in front of my eyes and causing me to raise my hand up as a shield. What I saw was a man, standing there with a grand crown on his head and a large swords in his right hand. With one swing, he drove the blade right through the red creature's neck, and it was done. An inferno erupted and engulfed only the body of the demon, sending it back to Hell... and like that, the figure of light was gone.

Christina began to giggle sadistically as the demon was gone. I tried to regain my bearings, even through it felt like my chest was going to explode with my heart going a thousand beats a second. I understood how the old priest got killed by Linda Blair now, I can tell you that.

I lifted up Christina's shirt again and noticed the picture had been changed. The demons had moved somewhat up her back and the forces of Heaven were being driven back. Still, I didn't even slightly understand it and thought it best to consult X on it. So I clawed my way across the floor, feeling weak and drained from the experience, like I had run three marathons without a breath. I made it out the door though and grabbed for the cell.

'Matt!' a voice snapped, still trying to get my attention from when I had dropped the phone moments before.

I took a breath and blinked a little. 'Yeah X... I'm here.'

X let out a sigh of relief from the other end. 'Where have you been kid, I've been on this line for hours!'

I looked out the window and sure enough, it was dark out. 'I dunno' man, this is some heavy stuff. I just saw a demon, some sort of angel that Christina was calling Michael, and you know that picture? It just became a home video man, rated PG13.'

'Michael?' X asked.

'Yeah,' I muttered through a cough, 'that's what she said.'

'Michael can't help you Matt, you're on your own.'

'Yeah, I know that, I'm the only field man here. Besides, I don't even know if that was Michael, but he did a number on that demon.'

'Matt, listen to me. Michael cannot help you!' X shouted, startling me a little.

'Chill man!' I exclaimed. 'You just said that, I got the picture. If it is Michael though... then why can't he help me? He is a saint after all.'

'Why?' X asked with an angered voice. 'We won't let him help you, that's why.'

I didn't even have time to respond as there was a click on the other end and my phone went dead. I peered through the crack in the door and saw Christina, still balled up in her closet, rocking and whispering. It was then that it hit me, and it hit me hard.

We were alone.
