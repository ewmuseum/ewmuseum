My Obsession...My Love....My Title...Take It If You Can....
Written: 8-12-2002

Do you know what it truly is to want something so bad you'd sell your soul for it's safety, pray tell?

Do you know what it is to hold something in your hands and never want to see it leave your grasp?

I knew the feeling of a mother holding her crying, helpless newborn after releasing her from the womb....

Hours and hours of intense pain....

Crimson blood, beads of glistening sweat, and rivers of salty tears...

And in the end, there in your hands is the greatest treasure you could ever behold.

I knew this feeling now as I sat in the dark underworld of the arena basement just mere hours after the match....

Logan Cage vs. I....Xander Crave. The action was intense, the crowd was jacked, and the battle was close and could have gone either way....

But the battle was not his that day, for when I leaped up and drove his head into the mat, his neck folding like a regularly used accordion....I placed my body atop of his....

I displayed my dominance...

I looked into the eyes of one of the earthbound, unenlightened in the front row....his face no longer glowing with entertained pleasure, but rather a paralyzing fear of what I had done to the man in the ring...

I stared into his eyes as the referee slammed his hand to the mat three times and the bell rang...

My music played....

My voice shrieked all over the arena as the heavy metal guitar added to the chaotic melody I loved to release upon the world....

And the reward for my destruction of another human being might you ask?

A wide, golden belt with a perfect mixture of white and red which created the Canadian flag upon both the leather strap and the medallion of the belt.

All it took was first sight and I was in love with my new prize....I grasped it in hand, no concern for the living, breathing person I just battered into submission for the prize....

No concern for my own health....

Just an overflow of lust for that belt...

A few hours later, I'm here....

Still in the dark....

Sealed away from all human life as I sat against the cold brick wall, the caressing breeze of the air conditioning bombarding my naked chest from the front while the stiff and chipped brick wall I sat against chilled my spine from the back.

I never thought to even go to my lockeroom after the win....

No call to Dolby about the next concert that was to take place...

Not even a chance to wipe my eyes, still suffering an annoying sting from the sweat invading them from the match...

All that was on my mind as I sat in the dubious darkness of the basement, no concern for the world in action around me....

Was this beautiful treasure I now hold in my hand with the utmost care...

The CHZ National motherfu**ing title...

The leather was smooth to the touch, making my loving grip upon my title an easy task in itself.

My eyes were fixated upon the wide medallion of the title, the darkness not enough to hinder the hypnotic gleam of the title shining through....the gleam I came to enjoy and crave...

This title proved my dominance over most of the other men on the roster...

It proved I was the 'chosen'...more worthy then they could ever DREAM to be...

To put it simply, I was better then them.

And that was a recurring melody within my head and it would stay that way as long as I held this belt by my side....

But then it hit me...it hit me like a gut punch in the 3rd round of a title fight with Lennox Lewis...

If I ever lost this belt....

I'd be...

Nothing.

Could I deal with being a virtual nobody once again if I ever lost this....my treasure...

The answer was short, simple, and easy to remember...

Hell no.

So what was the solution to this dilemma. How could I avoid EVER feeling like nothing in the ranks of CHZ and the eyes of those bound to the earth.....

NEVER lose it.

That's right.....

NEVER lose the belt....

NEVER let it leave my grasp.

And now that I think about it, my blissful happiness has now turned into something sinister...

A monster within me...

And this monster wants to see everyone destroyed...

All in an effort to hold on to my sacred gold....

Mutilation...

Just fine...

Violation...

No problem...

Murderous intent...

Comes with the territory...

I am willing to throw away that singing career of mine....the money....the reputation I've gained for myself...EVERYTHING...

Just so I can hold onto this belt yet another day longer.

I came down here to find peace, now I can't even do that....

All I found is a mission....

END ANYONE WHO DARES RIP THIS BELT FROM YOU!!

All my life, all I ever wanted was to ease my soul...

It was my whole reason for singing and wrestling in the first place...

Now all I've done is found a new demon in me...

And this demon wants all threats to what's mine destroyed.

J-Man....pity he will become the sacrificial lamb to prove this point....

I've gotta go do something...anyting...

Because if I sit here any longer in the dark with time to think...

I'm gonna find a new demon...

And it's always best to solve old problems before the new...

So is the life of a misfit in the spotlight...
