Anti Hero Pt:1
Written: 29/7/02

Techno dance music blared and darkness blended with dazzling instantaneous flashes of multi colour light submerged the surroundings. Teens who stuffed tissues down their bras for the extra bit of effort in helping convince the bouncers they were over the age limit to be allowed in groped and slithered across the bodies of middle aged men who were trying to look 22 again. The women were trying to look older, while the men look younger. He called this dance floor filled with perverts, hookers, paedophiles and drug addicts 'humanity's cesspool of inept scum'

He stood there at the edge of the dance floor wearing his usual attire. A long black leather jacket reaching just below his knees, a pair of leather trousers with black biker boots and a black sleeveless tank top. His long black hair hung loosely around his shoulders and encompassed most of his face, his deep hypnotic enchanting blue eyes the only clearly visible feature on his face in the club tonight.

He slowly began to walk across the edge of the dance floor, the smell of smoke from Class A illegal substances joined him as he walked past the drunk, horny, intoxicated and semi conscious people inhabiting the dance floor. He walked with a strong purpose, his strides indicating he knew his destination clearly and would be stopped, interrupted or distracted by any of the people prancing around him.

The steel steps leading upwards away from the dance floor made a 'clank' sound as his black biker boots made contact with them. The people seeing him made the wise and sensible decision of moving out of his way and letting him pass clearly. He took no time to thank them.

The small corridors on the second floor of the dance club would slowly lead the unsuspecting curious clubber or passer by into a maze of dark mind bending proportion. Fortunately Axl knew his way around here. He had been here before frequently, and found his way through the maze of corridors which all seemed to contain the same brown wooden worn down chipped away decayed look on the doors. Eventually he came to a corridor smaller and narrower than the rest. It was also smaller, and the dance music he detested with a passion had know faded away almost to a complete and empty mute, indicating how far he had travelled down these corridors . The door that faced him appeared to be more battered and decayed than all the others He walked towards it and knocked with moderate force on the door. There was a faint murmur of grunts from behind the door, before he heard a quirky voice.

'What's the password?'

'You have a cheap rotting decaying wooden door separating your domain from the corridor in which I stand. You need a rather large guard dog and a very big padlock to keep people from entering, not a password'

He could sense the logic of his words running through the owner of the quirky light voice's head. He slowly let out a small grin at the image.

'Or even a new door'

He added the last comment with a stern yet light hearted tone, before he heard a set of small cheap locks being unlocked and eventually the door was pulled open slowly. He was greeted by the sight of a middle aged man with long scruffy blonde hair and thick round spectacles staring at him with a small cheesy grin.

'Axl Young, long time and no damn see'

Axl rolled his eyes slightly and simply nodded before letting himself in. The room he entered was filled with computer monitors, all of which had their own quirky and mind boggling screen saver. There were electrical wires scattered across the cold floor and the walls were surrounded by humorous posters of cats dangling from trees with 'Oh shit' printed underneath. Each computer, printer scanner and monitor and keyboard was placed somewhat carelessly on steel desks which took up a large portion of the centre of the rather large room.

'I need some information Riley'

The young man nodded at Axls' request, and quickly jogged over to one of the nearest computers and sat down in a green swing chair before looking back up at Axl.

'So, what do you need. Bank passwords, criminal profiles. You know the FBI classified files have been updated'

Riley raised his eyebrow above the rim of his spectacles and nodded whilst grinning like a Cheshire cat. Axl merely shook his head whilst closing the rotted weak door behind him before coming closer to Riley.

'I need some information on some people. Three people to be exact. Two males, one female'

Riley immediately began typing away furiously at his keyboard whilst nodding and looking at his computer screen.

'Try CHZ personnel, employee and talent files. First name I want checked out is Matt Blitz, that's Bee Ell Eye Tee Zed

Riley merely continued nodding and thrashing away at his keyboard, only stopping occasionally to slide his spectacles back up onto his nose from whenever they slipped down the bridge of his nose.

'OK, Matt Blitz, 24 years of age, born on July 13th, 1978. Umm, that's about all there is to tell on this guy really, he's a mysterious guy from what I can tell from this profile, nicknamed The Disciple ....... and that's about it'

That was enough about Matt Blitz for now, and Axl nodded at what he had just been told by Riley.

'OK, second male, Ryan Contra, that's SEE OHH ENN TEE ARR AYY'

Riley again went back to thrashing away on his keyboard while Axl walked back and forth around the small space he had between the centre of the room surrounded by computer desks. His four way match was this Wednesday and he knew pretty much nothing about his opponents apart from their gender. He needed to get information on them immediately if he was going to have any chance in defeating them this Wednesday.

'OK, Ryan Contra, born 7th of April 1979. He calls himself The American Hero and hails from Collective Michigan'

That didn't help him much exactly either, but it would simply have to do for now. He nodded again and took a deep breath before speaking again.

'OK, Angel Summers .. that's'

'I know, I can spell Summers'

Axl chuckled slightly and ran his hand through his long black hair, brushing some of it out of his face as he again paced up and down the room. Eventually, Riley began speaking once again to the deep dark demented rebel.

'OK baby, now we got something. Angel Summers, calls herself The TeXan. Born the first of March 1978 and hails from Houston, Texas. Lets see what else do we have here.... ahhhh, keen horse rider and was picked up the academy of kickboxing, looks like she did well there, placed first a lot of times before breaking on into wrestling'

Axl looked at the computer screen as Randy turned along with his swivel chair to face him. Axl had finally obtained some solid info on one of his opponents. He now knew Angel Summers interests and part of her fighting style.

'Soo..... you think she's single?'

Axl looked at Randy with emotionless eyes, before shrugging his shoulders.

'I don't know, and I don't care'

'Well either way your gonna have your hands filled with this chick Axl'

Axl took into consideration what Riley just told him. All three of these opponents could pose as a threat to him, whether any of them would back up their reputation which seemed impressive only on paper still remained to be seen. However if the deep dark demented rebel was going to be there he would be the last man standing amidst a mass of fallen savaged souls which he had sent south of heaven.

'Anyway, I must leave you now. Thank you for the information'

'Hey anytime man, I just hope it proves to be some help'

Axl rubbed his hands together before looking at the rotting decayed door. He then slowly looked over his shoulder and pointed to the large window to the left of him and Riley.

'Do you mind if I use your window, the thought of that techno music invading my ears again doesn't appeal to me right now.'

'Yeah sure, your just lucky that you don't have a brother who owns this place like I do'

Axl let out a small grin, before walking over to the window and unlocking it, and sliding it open. He then stepped out onto the ledge, and closed the window behind him, and then dropped onto the fire escape.

It was quite a walk down, and it led into a seedy dark alleyway which was usually occupied by prostitutes, pimps, and drug dealers and drug addicts. However tonight it appeared to be empty and .... quiet, deathly quiet.

He made his way down further, looking cautiously at the alleyway. Listening out for the high pitched laughter of a local hooker, or the deep voice filled with slang talk of a pimp, or the sloppy tone and speech from a set of drug dealers and drug users. He heard none. However what he did hear ........ was a scream.

* * * * * * * * * * * * *

'Noooo!'

The sound of high heeled boots stamping into the cold damp concrete echoed through the alley. It made a fast paced tapping, like Morse code, indicating the female owner of the voice was running, or at least trying to. The bold echoing footsteps spurring not far behind the echoes of the females footsteps indicated she was being followed.

Axl made his way slowly down, all he could see was shadows dancing with the walls and cold floor, urging and pleading him to come and join them. He decided ....... he decided he would do just that.

'The money bitch, now!'

His voice was deep and booming. Full of anger and impatience and also a slow drawl between words.

'I haven't got it!'

Her voice was full of panic and hesitation. Axl could sense the tears swelling up in her eyes and her nerves coil around the underneath of her flesh.

'I'm going to ask one more time!'

He fell from the last few feet of the fire escape with such majesty and grace it was like almost like he was a breed apart from everyone and everything else inhabiting this earth. She seen IT, she didn't exactly know what she saw, but she seen it falling from the fire escape and landing on its feet like cat. The way she looked over the pimps shoulders caused him to stop in his own tracks and look over his shoulder cautiously. Slumped and smothered in the darkness several feet behind him was the deep dark demented rebel.


'What the......'

'Praying on the weak for egotistical purposes is mostly viewed as clear signs of pathetic ness and weakness'

His words echoed around the alley, and after uttering them he slowly rose from the slumped position he had thrown himself in. His long black hair covering his face, his blue eyes however still as enchanting as ever.

'Damn son, Halloween isn't even close yet'

The man, who Axl assumed was a pimp, let out a shrill cold laugh. Axl merely looked at him with his eyes, and grinned a demonic grin that slithered up the right side on his mouth. He then slowly arched his way forward towards him.

'Ohhh, you wanna play huh?'

Axl continued to stride forward as if ignoring the question asked to him, the hooker merely smothered herself into a ball of panic at the far end of the alley.

'OK then kiddo, lets play'

Slowly he drew a small knife from the inside of his long velvet jacket that glimmered in the night against the moons rays. Axl seemed to smile more as he produced the knife.

'Deadly weapons against an unarmed opponent, your vital signs of weakness are growing ever present and stronger'

The man was rapidly becoming irritated with Axl know, and any idea of torturing and having some sick and twisted painful fun with him using his knife were all thrown out of the window.

Slowly as Axl drew near the man grinned an impatient grin and lunged at him with the knife. Axl side stepped to his left as he did so, avoiding the attack. The man got to a fully vertical base again, and lunged at him again, however Axl side stepped him again, this time grabbing his free hand and twisting it sharply, forcing it to make a sick twisting sound as it fractured.

'Ahhhh, JESUS CHRIST!'

'Ah yes, Jesus Christ'

The man not furious and seemingly large amounts of pain lunged and swiped his blade at Axl, slicing across his chest, Axl stepped back but the blade sliced through his skin slightly drawing blood immediately. Axl flinched, but put the pushed the pain away and continued to toy with the man.

'Tell me if you've heard this one'

The man swiped at Axl again, cutting his right arm, this time making a clear protruding flesh cut.

'Arrrrrggggghh. Jesus Christ walks into an inn'

The man looked astonished at how he was still talking to him after sustaining to swipes through his skin from his knife.


'He hands the owner three nails'

This time he attempted to drive the knife through his throat, but only caught his shoulder he side stepped the man. He let out a pain stricken cry into the night. Yet still he talked.

'Then he asked......'

Axl kicked the knife out of the mans hand as he stood there looking at him with sick confusion. Axl lurched towards him with demonic intentions flowing through his mind and eyes. The man knew this, and began taking in deep breaths before screaming.

'Will you put me up for the night!'
