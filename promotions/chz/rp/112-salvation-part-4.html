Salvation: Part 4
Written: 7/31/02

I was sitting there with my jaw on the floor. I couldn't believe my ears, and if I did, I was sure I'd tear them right off my head. Of all the possessions I had handled, and of all the demons I had encountered... I never thought in a million years that my brother would become a case of mine.

Then the thoughts began to course through my head. The only person in this world that I could truly say I hated was my brother, Spectre, the man who had put me through so much. I mean, follower of God or not, I didn't think I could help my brother. I was human, just like anyone else out there, and I doubted that I could look into the eyes I no less than despised and try to free them with the best of my power. As far as I was concerned, Spectre deserved his fate, and didn't deserve my help.

'Spectre?' I asked again, hoping somewhere that the answer would change. I bit my lower lip and gnawed on it in thought. This was the last thing I needed to hear... or so I thought.

'Spectre,' X calmly repeated. 'Look Matt, like I said, The Disciples are out for blood now. Wrap this current case up. It's been three weeks, you even missed your first CHZ match... it's time you get this done and over with before something bad happens to that girl.'

I cursed under my breath and bit down hard, causing my lip to bleed. 'Damn it! I can't believe I missed my first match! What about my next one?'

There was a long pause on the other end, and coming from X, it make me feel a little sick. 'It's coming fast Matt, and I doubt you'll make it there either. The CHZ has been asking questions about you being AWOL, and we can't tell them the real reason. I think you might be gone by the time your case is over. Sorry kid.'

It was then that I saw my wrestling career flashing before my eyes. All the blood, all the sweat and all the tears for nothing. I knew the business would either make me or break me, and I was on the edge of being broken in half by the life I had chosen. It pissed me off, and I found myself clenching my fist and glaring at the floor. I had that feeling... you know... when you just need someone there to strangle, not even caring whose face it is. All I could feel was the anger, and I would have even taken it on X at that very moment.

'X, listen up man,' I stuttered out, shaking a little as the adrenaline created by my anger burned in the back of my throat. 'Look, I know you have connections, and I know you have them by the dozens. I don't care what you do, fake an injury, create a family crisis... but just do it. Forge some files, some reports... anything, but make sure I'm a wrestler by the time I come out of this, ya' dig?'

'I can't do that Matt,' he bluntly stated. I dug my finger nails into my palm and began to bleed from another spot on my body.

'What do you mean you can't!? This is my life you're messing with old man!'

X sighed on the other end. 'Matt... it's about time that you stop thinking about wrestling and start thinking about being a Disciple. Remember what I always told you kid? God comes before that ring, and no words have been spoken any truer. Forget wrestling Matt, you're a Disciple.'

Inside... I exploded, erupted like a damn volcano, and I couldn't control myself anymore. 'Listen to me old man, if I'm not a wrestler when I finish this case, then I'm not a Disciple either! Christ man, I've known you for a long time! What the hell is the matter with you!? You can't just wipe away what I've devoted my life to! This isn't...'

I trailed off and stopped myself. For a second, I stopped breathing my shaking breaths and I stopped clenching my fist. My lip ached, but I didn't stop biting; instead, I just sat there and listened to my own words.

'This isn't anything like you,' I said to myself in a tone of realization as a quiet snicker came from the other end of the phone. The snicker was dark and sounded like an orchestra of buzzing flies over the phone.

'That's why we have been watching you Matt. You have that hatred deep inside, just like your brother. Just like him, we have a place for you Matt. We know how you've felt Matt, as if you were alone, without a place in this life. Our master loves you, even though you serve the one who threw him from Heaven so many ages ago. He has a place for you Matt, and his hand reaches for you. Just take it and free yourself of all your burdens. Why lead a life of strife when you can lead one of your own desires? God gave you free will... do not become his slave, but become our brother.' The voice sounded like a harsh whisper inside of my head, and instantly, I plugged my ears. Of course, doing so had no effect.

I didn't like the way it felt, almost as if it made too much sense. I didn't want the demons in my head anymore, but... I couldn't get them out. The whispers, the hissing, the chanting... it as all there, there forever. Did I see God's slave in the reflection of the mirror? Did I see my brother when I saw myself? I couldn't believe it... I wouldn't. I wasn't my brother and I was no slave. I was Matt Blitz, a servant of God and a wrestler. That's all I needed to be.

In my confused rage, I smashed my cell phone against the wall. No more deception would come from it, and as far as I was concerned, the outside world didn't exist. I was in the home with a young woman who needed my help... and the demons who were slowly unraveling the bindings of my mind.

I pushed the bedroom door open and scooted across the floor. I was scared, confused... everything. I quickly pulled open the closet and I was met with a shrill scream of surprise that made me stagger back and hit my head on the end of Christina's bed.

'Christina!?' I called inside the closet and heard a few whimpers followed by soft sobbing.

'You left me,' she whispered in between the tears. 'You left me here with them.'

I lowered my head and slowly crept into the closet with her. 'I know,' I bluntly whispered back, 'but I had to. I can't fight them from inside here. I can't get rid of them cooped up in here with you. I'm sorry.'

She cried some more, and what was left of my sanity screamed at me to comfort her. I couldn't though... I just couldn't. It was as if she had a barrier around her, one I couldn't touch. I wanted to help her, but deep down inside, I didn't know if she was demon or person anymore. I didn't even know if I was.

She looked up at me and wiped her eyes. 'I don't want to be alone Matt,' she stuttered out in a harsh whisper.'They'll get me if I'm alone. I need you here, okay?'

I was going to nod. Maybe it wasn't the absolute truth, but it was the least I could do for her to comfort her. However, before I could do anything, my heart found its way into my throat as the closet doors whipped open, scratching hard against the floor as they did so. I think both Christina and I let out a startled sound as our gaze instantly turned that way. What I saw sent a crippling chill down my spine, and if I had a mirror, I'm sure my reflection would have been pitch white.

There was a cross, covered in blood, and crucified there was none other than my brother, Ryan Blitz, better known as Spectre. In my complete shock and horror, I missed the irony of it all, seeing as his submission was called 'The Crucifix', but irony had no place here. He was hanging there with nails driven through his hands and a crown of thorns on his head. He was still wearing those shades that didn't let you know what he was thinking, and that grin... the coldest and most inhumane grin I had ever seen. It was his grin, and when I saw it, the hair on the back of my neck stood to attention.

'Brother,' he called in that overconfident voice of his, 'why hast thou forsaken me?'

I yelled at the image, for how long... I don't know. It disappeared before my eyes, yet I still screamed at it. That picture had been burned into my mind now, and though I screamed, I couldn't get it out of my head. When I finally stopped, I realized I was shaking and I backed back into the closet, crossing my arms and lowering my head.

'Did you see it!?' Christina hissed at me, tears running down her face and her skin as chalk white as mine was. 'Was that Jesus?'

I lost all control, and jumped on her, pressing my hand against her mouth. Ever feel that? Those times when you just can't take it anymore and you just want to cover that mouth with your hand? I wanted to, and I gave in to the dark need. I pinned her to the floor, and I could tell by her look of fear that I had completely lost it.

'Does that look like our savior!?' I shouted in a crazed voice, squeezing harder as she tried to escape. I was going to shout again, but I realized that she couldn't breathe, and whatever was left of my humanity begged me to let her go. Slowly, I pulled my hand away from her mouth and backed away from her while she began to gulp in lung-fulls of air.

I shook, looking at my hands as if I were possessed. Was I? How would I know? Perhaps the demon in my head really had possessed me, and had briefly taken control of me right there. I'd be a danger to Christina, wouldn't I?

Christina curled up in her ball and began to rock again, singing some song to herself that I couldn't understand through my self-whispering. I noticed that her back lit up again, and she gritted her teeth in pain. She didn't even notice me crawl over and lift up the back of her shirt, which showed the picture again animated. I noticed the demons were again driving forward and beating back the angels... and I could feel something wrong with that, though I couldn't put my finger on it.

Suddenly, the picture stopped moving and Christina lashed out at me with a shrill scream. In shock, I stumbled back and 'crab walked' back to my corner of the closet. We stared at each other for some time, trying to decipher what one another was thinking. Was she possessed? Was she plotting something against me? She had the picture, maybe this was all her doing and I was lured here by The Disciples. I didn't even know if I was possessed, and it ate away at my very thoughts.

I could feel warm breath on the back of my neck, but I didn't turn around at first. I wanted it to do something to me... anything, as long as it would end this torture. However, it just breathed, and when I returned my gaze to Christina, she looked horrified. Shaking a little, I turned and was face to face with one of the emotionless demons, surrounded by darkness and the silent singing of my companion. A bright flash occurred in front of my eyes, accompanied by the painful memory of reading the paper and finding out that my parents were dead. Instantly, I staggered back beside Christina who was to scared to do anything.

'Where is your father now Matt? Where is he? Take my hand, and regain the loving father you once lost. We are brothers... you and I.'
