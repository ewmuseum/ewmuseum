The Greatest View
Written: 18/8/02

Everything seems so simple up here. Everything seems so calm. When you look at things from up here, and see how they develop and appear, it genuinely makes you think. It makes you ponder and consider why the hell is there so many wrong, corrupt and negative things this world? How?! Everything could be so simple, everything could be less ......less, what's the word? Vulgar! Yes, everything could be so much less vulgar than it already is.

I guarantee you look out of your window and you can spot at least 5 things you wish you could change, 5 things you wish could be better, 5 things that SHOULD be better, 5 things that shouldn't be the fucked up way they are now! Then again, I don't need to think about that right now, because I'm looking at the world from a point of view where everything is so much more clear.

I used to walk and climb all the time. Scale the mountains, explore the moors. That's where I am now. On top of some mountain. I don't know the name of it, I don't know where it is, nor do I even have the slightest care. I don't want to know, if I do it may spoil my mood. Then again I know it will be spoiled when I go back down, when I go back down and have to enter that decaying cesspool of waste known as humanity and society and life in general!

Nevertheless, it all seems more clear up here. It is now even more clear what I have to do to Angel Summers, the new ago whore of Babylon. She must be sacrificed. This is not about cleansing, sing, HA! How am I talk about sins? I've had more than enough committed to me and I've executed more than enough on others. The Saint Of Sinners, that's me! No, Angel Summers must be sacrificed because........ because it is time for her to wake up to the vulgar depression that is in this world. Why must she wake up to it? Because ........... because she has become to immersed and submerged in the small delicate sugar flavoured topping with a cherry on top that is the protective deceiving coating on the world. She will awaken to the sadistic, inhumane, unholy reality that is the real body of this world.

The air feels so fresh up here, it's been a while since I've inhaled real fresh air. The grass is soft, moist and pure. They haven't reached here yet. They haven't reached this place. They, they being them! Them being the society and mankind. They haven't polluted and destroyed this place yet.

God I truly hate that woman with a passion!

Angel Summers. Mankind may not have destroyed this piece of land, but I will join Angel Summers.

Just to hear the sound of her skin being pounded into that hard canvas. Hearing her skin being burnt against the hot mat and her helpless screams ring through my ears. Feeling her bones smash together as I dominate her. All those will be an energy to keep me driving and striving to desecrate her.

It seems almost shameful and sad to think about such morbid and twisted thought. Then again? Their about the only thing you can think about when the words life, existence, and earth popped up into the same sentence. Well, you could add Angel Summers to that selection of words now. For what I have planned for her, for what I will do to her, will be nothing more than morbid, sick and twisted.

However, to do those things I must come away from this view, where everything seems so simple and clear, and venture back to humanity where everything is distraught. Yet that is the only way to inflict the sickness of my pain into Angel Summers, and if I can do that, then that trip back into the cesspool of humanity will be a trip worth making.
