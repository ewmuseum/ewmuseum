He HAD the whole world in his hands..
Written: 28/8/02

'Do you get some sick fucking perverted pleasure from all of this!'

Today he was having a slight episode to say the least. He made a mistake, he even knew himself that he would regret doing it. He knew the consequences of his actions and knew the predictable violent repercussions that would ensue after he did what he did.......

...................... he bought a newspaper.

'I hope you like how sick and twisted your making your fucking precious pride and joy called the world!'

He began tossing various items across his small bed sit, then again there was nothing really worth throwing about in there, so right now he was tossing about his bed sheets.

'Urrrrgh! What do you hope to gain from this?!'

It was extremely rare seeing him in this mood, this hostile dangerous mood outside the ring. He was usually so calm cool and collected. He knew how to control himself and conduct himself. He has become so intimate with his emotions he knew almost how to unleash and contain nearly every one of them, even though most of his emotions nowadays were full of sorrow and vengeance. Yet he was not going to play by the traditional rules he had adopted for himself today, no, today he was letting it all out.

He picked up the newspaper from off the floor, and tore at the pages and tossed them across the room, shouting each headline and highlighted bold sentence as he did.

'Three teenagers murdered in woods, police yet to find killer'

He crumpled it up and tossed it onto the floor of his bed sit that was now covered in cushions, guitar tablature, CD's, clothes and bed sheets.

'Elderly couple savagely beaten and robbed in own home!'

He spat at the paper and crumpled it up, yelling obscenities harshly while breathing heavily and tossing it into the air and thrusting his right foot out, kicking it to the other side of the room and watching as it bounced off the opposite wall.

'Convicted paedophile released after serving only five years out f a 15 year sentence!'

He threw the paper down, this time effortlessly. He had used all his energy trashing his room in his anger and yelling. He shouldn't have bought that paper, he didn't need to become any more aware of the harshness of this world. He already knew how harsh it was, he knew that doing something as foolish as that would have sent him over the edge, and that was the last thing he needed right now. Then again, he himself was convinced he had dived off the edge of sanity a long time ago, and he didn't intend on ever trying to climb back up onto that ledge.

He didn't need these extra added distractions with the pay per view so close either, and his match at Wild.

'You fucking idiot!'

The words fell softly into the air as he uttered them in almost a harsh whisper under his breath.

'Then again this is all your fucking doing, isn't it God!'

Now he was shouting again as he slumped down and rested against the back of his small bed. His long black hair ruffled all over in several directions and pretty much completely smothering his handsome face.

'Your precious world is crumbling and you couldn't care less, I just hope that your happy that everything that represents your old nemesis Satan is roaming around and injecting it poisonous disease into it'

He leaned his head back and laughed slightly, it was sadistic in sound and cold in feeling. Nowadays it was his only laugh, because his sense of humour had dried up and frozen long ago. Now the only one he had was the dark sense of humour, the sense of humour we all have but won't let out too often. The sense of humour that laughs at the small boy who falls and grazes his knee and cries. The sense of humour that mocks those less fortunate than us, the sense of humour that points and bellows out hysterical cries of laughter at those less privileged than us. That was the sense of humour we all had and the sense of humour he had unlocked .

'They say you have the whole world in your hands, but somewhere along the line you dropped it. Dropped it and let the glass ball that is this world fall south of heaven and fall into the hands of something awful, maybe Lucifer himself caught it, and now he's shaking away at this world you once lost your might grip on'

He then dropped his head and held it in his hands, but he would not cry. He stopped wasting things like tears on this world a long time ago. Yet before he could even contemplate as of what to do next, his phone rung.

He looked up in amazement, nobody rung him, he didn't even get a wrong number. Who would it....

::' Leave a message'::

His own voice echoed from off the answering machine. It was followed by a voice of someone he hadn't heard from in a while, but it was one of the few voices he welcomed to hear.

'Hey Axl man, it's me Mick'

He smiled as he heard the strong proud deep growl of a voice from his mentor.

'Hey I've been watching you the last few weeks, glad to see my training paid off'

Axl smiled and nodded slightly as he heard the comment.

'But um, I seen your in a bit of a state. Now I knew you were, well, pissed at this world from the day I met you, and you get every reason to be don't get me wrong. But... but don't anything stupid. I know you got this agenda with that chick Angel, and you t to do your own thing I understand that...... I guess why I phoned you was just to say... be careful man, don't do something so stupid that it's going to cost you big time, you got nothing left to lose I know, but, just think about what you do, and think wisely. You'll always have my support, you know that. But, maybe think twice about going so harsh on this chick. I don't want you to desecrate her too much, she may have a sister I can hook up with.'

Axl smiled, and that was as close to true humour that you would get from him.

'Just, like I said. Be careful.......................... and ring some time you long haired gothic schmuck!'

The message ended with Mick laughing his usual jolly green giant laugh as the message ended. Mick was the wisest person he knew and his words always had a deep understandable meaning, but today he would defy him, and not for this first time.

'Can't do that Mick'

Axl stood up, his flight was leaving soon and he had to pack what little belongings he had.

'Our so called God abandoned the duty of making the sinners pay, so now it seems I'm the only one left with the morals to do it. Soon maybe our God will realise that..... Victims .... aren't we all!'
