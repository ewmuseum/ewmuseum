The Golden Moments....
Written: 29/07/02

--Begin Feed--

A beautiful day, sun is up, and not a cloud in the sky in Oakview, Ohio. It is a big day for the small town people as once a year, entertainers, bands, food and beer is distributed outside the city hall Park. The event usually racks up the whole town, with the exception of some. There are several different entertainers, doing their own thing, as they dress goofy, act goofy and entertain the locals with their goofy antics. Some with fire, blowing from their mouths, some juggling and some with 1-wheel bikes. This is actually a two time a year event, where this occurs in the winter as well, but in the winter there is polar swims, locals jump in the freezing water to entertain. Garcia boys usually participate in that event.

It took a day or so to settle the fight with the two boys, but it just took a mutual agreement of thinking as a team other them themselves. The two know they have to come together as they have a big tag match approaching against Redemption. The two have been down at City Hall Park for a few hours, watching the entertainers, drinking a few and a hot dog here and there. As they walk along the waters together, they take a trip down memory lane by talking about there past.

They look onto the still waters, where in a few months they will be jumping into at freezing temperatures. They share a few laughs with that as plenty line-up for the event but rarely people jump in. But as they boys always say, “You only live once!”

The boys also look over at the bar, where their parents would usually be getting drunk at this time. But had decided to stay at home this year, as the two were not feeling well to say the least. Then Tito is reminded of his football days, when he looks over to the city football field where a little league game is occurring. He does not say out loud of missing football, as that would probably make his brother Manny become hysterical. Tito remembers how he used to wake up at 5am every morning on game days, just to take a run to warm up for the noon game he had. He would plead to his father to play catch with him, but his father was usually not up to it as he often played with his brother, or his neighbor’s father or child. He was the football payer every football fan father dreamed of, but in his case, his father did not care much for the sport, unless it was on the television. He did miss the game, but he was beginning to feel comfortable about being a wrestling tag partner.

Then he memory lane shifts over to Manny, when he looks over at the outdoor ring, where he would kick box every Saturday, in front of the town. He had never lost a match, as the competition was in fact competitive for such a small town. But one match he remembers, when he was losing horribly, until the showing of his father, inspired him to impress him and eventually won the fight. His father was only really interested in leaving the house, if it involved beer or fighting. He went to the off fight of Manny’s which lead to Tito’s jealousy as Frank Garcia, the boys father never went to one football game of Tito’s. But they got over that stage as they knew their father did not mean a harm, just did not know any better.

The two were very competitive, which was triggered by schoolmates. Their similar style in school sports. Always debating were the schoolmates who were curious who was the better athlete. Never really solving anything, as they were both equals in such school sports as, soccer, hockey, volleyball and basketball. But the two only had one real love, for Tito it was football, for Manny it was judo. School days were a walk in the park for Manny and Tito, never really going to class but always passing barely, always being school stars in sports, plus plenty of friends because of there athletic ability. In fact this time in their lives, was probably the only time in their lives they were without a female partner, but they are still feeling good about one another. Tito is more the relationship guy, he was able to keep a girl for long periods of time, but Manny liked to play the field, with a different girl per week. Tito just came out of his longest relationship which stretched to 3 years until he just lost interest, as did Manny with his longest, which was 4 months.

Now being major wrestling stars, not finding time to do much of anything else but train, once they get settled into the wrestling world, they will buckle down and get back to their life. Back to partying, Social City events and of course, back to dating. The two have never really had a time in their lives where they did not have women on their minds, but for the first time they did not, all about wrestling. Which will result into good wrestling, without other things on their minds.

After a long walk along the shorelines, they walk back up to the park where plenty of people gather around the musical band, where they sing oldies, and begin to work up a big crowd. Tito and Manny not really liking the oldies so they just pass by the band, and make their way over to the bar, for a few drinks, and then head home. On their way home Manny is in a rarely good mood, as he feels like doing something crazy. He feels like he had to do something with his black hair, Tito not having any hair at all just laughs. Manny gets dropped off at the barber to get some gold spots in his hair, to support the team. And Tito drives to a local restaurant and grabs a little something to eat while waiting for his brother. The restaurant was pretty much empty, because of the City Wide event, which just had a young couple in the corner booth, and an old man reading a newspaper, on the bar table. The waitress then approached him.

“ So what you having on this beautiful day, Tito? The usual bacon cheddar melts burger, with fries…”

Tito then takes one final look at the menu, and shakes his head.

“Sounds good Mia, so how is the family? Your mother still taking those high schools classes to graduate? Or did she finish that already?”

She relaxes a little by putting her menu pad down, and grabbing a seat in the chair next to Tito, as she answers with a little excitement.

“ She finished last month, she is going to college now Tito. She is going to study law right here in Oakview with me. I was not all that happy when she first told me, having my mom in the same class and all, but it has been going pretty good, we both are doing well.”

Tito adjusts his seating, by lying back a little. Looking more relaxed around Mia.

“That great Mia, tell her I wish her the best of luck, as long with you! I am happy some people in this town are going to college. So how is that boyfriend your with, what is his name…Brian?”

She looks at Tito and smiles.

“ We broke up last night actually, we kind of lost interest I guess. Two years is a long time to be with a person that you cannot stand.”

Tito lets out a laugh and adjusts his watch.

“ I always thought he was a jerk, one of those rich boy attitudes. Always putting people down. Always knew you deserved better, good for you though by leaving. You have plenty more options then that guy.”

You can tell the flirting has both started, as they both keep adjusting their seats. Mia, brushing her dark brown hair, behind her ears. An attractive girl she was and always had Tito’s attention in school, but always was in a long relationship with someone. He was beginning to think maybe he should take a run at her, but it is such bad timing with the match coming up in a week.

“ Someone better… Like you? “

They both smile as Mia reaches into her pocket and pulls out a piece of paper. She takes the pen that she receives orders with, and writes her name and number on the piece of paper.

“ Here, whenever you get back from you match, give me a call! I will go get your food.”

Tito smiles and replies.

“ I will give you a call, but your going to have to cancel my order. I have to pick up my brother now.”

He gets up and gives Mia a hug, and leaves the restaurant with a smile as he has been waiting to get with her since High School. He then goes to pick up his brother from the barber, as he was waiting for him outside the barber with gold patches on his head. On the ride home, Tito jokes about it a little. Now both being in the spirit of the whole them, “Gold Rush.” As Tito has a big guy look, with his shaved head, with a dyed gold goatee, and his gold Nike slush pants, plus runners. And now Manny, with his new hairstyle, with gold spots. And also wearing the same as Tito. As they reach their house, the red brick home single floor home, with the big blue door. They begin to look at the red wooden front deck, which his father built 2 years ago when they bought this home. They had both earlier decided to build the deck from scratch again, make it stand out a little more. They already ought the wood for it, which it lies in the shed out back. They both realize today has to be the day, the weather perfect and still lots of daylight for the project.

They figure this would also be like a work out for the day, all the hammering they would be doing. They like doing this type of thing because it gives them time to talk about recent events. This particular time they talk about the match they have, and also about Tito getting Mia (s) number. The boys finished most that day and the rest the next morning. They seem to be focused on next week’s event, as they hope to win their first match as tag partners.


--End--
