The Debut
Written: 9/04/02

We arn't trying to be the next Redemption Song, or Mystery Assassians. We are going to be the first and only Easton High. Even though Redemption did have a good run, but that doesn't mean sh*t. It jsut sets a limit that we need to pass.

Tonight we are going to make our debut one to remember. One that will be impressive and give us respect from everyone in this place. To us, respect is more important then gold. We'll get the respect we deserve even if we have to make them hate us to do it.

Tonight is going to be a night for the record books.
