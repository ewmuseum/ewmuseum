Invading the Lair, beginning the Chase
Written: 7/27/02

Standing once again alone, I thought what I could do next in my journey now that it had been abruptly halted. These men, the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs must have had something to do with interfering in my journey but at the same time, why were they after my sombrero and donkey? Of course, the answer I had gotten was that they needed all the mojo they could get their dirty, sticky hands on. But that is another story, in fact, it's this story! I decided to think where the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs had gone, perhaps if I knew, I could follow them and continue my journey. My only clue were the hoof prints that looked like belonged to a donkey that lead to a far away cave.

'Well, I'm out of ideas.' I said to myself lost and hopeless.

I started to mope around following the donkey's tracks, brushing my feet against the sand covering the hoof prints.

'How could I be so dumb? Maracas knocking me out, Maracas! Just a minor detour I guess, but why do these guys have to interupt MY journey?' I asked myself in extreme confusion.

The heat was extremely exhausting as I myself was ready to lay down and die for the vultures. But something kept me going, it could've been my pure determination, or perhaps I wanted some of that mojo these men were after. Still, I thought of my roots, they do lead to Mexico is some way, that I intended to find out. To do this, I had to infiltrate the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs' lair and take back what was rightfully a man's, which became mine.

I must've drifted around thinking and moping for at least six hours before I finally bumped my head into a huge boulder.

'OW!!' I screamed rubbing my head in pain. 'Who would put a boulder here?' I questioned my mind.

Then, as if it was trying to be hidden, huge red neon lights lit up like magic perfectly spelling out:

Not the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs' lair.

I was absolutely awestruck at both the stupitidy, then the sense. I raised my hand as a lightbulb appeared over my head and lit up.

'They're right! This isn't the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs' lair.' I exclaimed as a long pause came over the atmosphere, I faced the camera and went into a whisper. 'It's their cave!' I gave the thumbs to the camera and nudged my head toward the cave as if I were guiding them to have a look see. 'Let's go have a gander.'

I went back into myself again and looked around the boulder for an entrance and sure enough, right where a large neon arrow was pointing, the entrance was to be found. I went up cautiosly to the side wall and peeked around the corner.

'What the (explitive deleted)?' What I had seen that day was not meant to be seen by any mortal's eyes.

The Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs seemed to have friends, in a nightclub business. The entire place was streaking in strobelights, all colors, purple, white, orange, green and even rainbow streaks. Loud Mexican music was blaring out of a huge speakers while a disc jockey was spinning the records with ease. In a crazy sort of way, this semi-resembled a nightclub of some sort. Every person attending the 'club' seems to be wearing the same type of sombrero with the same red sign as the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs were wearing the first time they had met me. The dance floor was packed while each tile lit up with a different color each time someone new stepped on it. Then I noticed the bouncer, one of the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs themselves, the leader. I casually stepped in front of him, then he pulled out a piece of paper and ran his finger down what seemed to be a list of names allowed into the club/lair/cave.

'Your not allowed in Señor Stimpo.' The man from MMMM said to me with authority. The leader was of good stature, with a curly mexican mustache and of course wearing what seemed to be the exact same sombrero I had received from Wegmans, the sombrero of sexiness. He once more wore the black cloak with the same red symbol (a chile bean surrounded by fire in a circle.) as he did in the desert where we first encountered eachother. His brown eyes twitched with every word he made and he always curled his mustache in a suave way.

'What do you mean I'm not allowed in? I'm the party animal and I'm Mexican, I could liven this club up even more!' I shouted at the man demanding to be let in. I then noticed above the man's head was a donkey on the dance floor being raised by all the people dancing.

'JACKASS!!' I screamed with great excitement.

'What did you say?' The man interrogated me.

'I said, damn Jack, look at that big Bass!' I told the man covering up and perhaps saving the donkey's life.

'Oh, right, well, I guess we could let you in the party for a little while.' The man sympathized me.

'Are you serious?' I asked in anticipation.

'What, are you calling me a liar?' The man questioned me.

'I just wanted the truth.' I replied, expecting a catchy Jack Nicholas line back.

'You want the truth? You CAN handle the truth!' The man screamed at me at the top of his lungs.

A long pause was kept throughout the club as it went silent until I finally spoke again.

'So, why don't you tell me?' I wanted to know.

'Right, your allowed in, but don't go near the sombrero of sexiness or that dancing donkey of the MMMM.' He said pointing to their locations. I chuckled as I passed by the man strutting as best as I could, which is as good as a slob trying to model swimwear. I entered the party busting a move as I referred to it, most people called it a seizure but what do they know? I decided that I should focus on what needed to be done, that being retrieving the two items I needed to continue the journey. The donkey still bouncing around on top of the dance floor, I came up from underneath him and lifted him as best as I could off the floor and behind the disc jockey.

'You alright jackass?' I asked the exhausted donkey. He nodded back. I let out a sigh of relief and turned my head and there I saw the disc jockey, it was one of the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs! He shook his maracas as he spun toward me and narrowed his eyes to me. He looked almost identicel to the leader man, but without the cool curly mustache.

'I believe that is our asna!' The Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniac shouted at me twisting around facing me showing his sombrero from the front.

'And, I believe that is my Sombrero of Sexiness!' I exhaled back at him in reply thrusting my arm foreward toward him and pointing my index finger straight at the sombrero a top the man's head. The man then pulled his sombrero harder on his head and started to scream at the top of his lungs at me.

'NO! The Sombero is mine! Yo tengo el mojo!' The man shouted at me at the top of his lungs (as I said before) and caused me to stutter my feet backwards from his screaming. When I was finally out of the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniac's screaming distance, I regained my manage and sense to think to speak again.

'Listen, why do you need the mojo, now...I can tell a man by the looks of you needs no mojo! Look at all the women surrounding you stripping with pleasure...and a few pesos.' The man looked around and smiled a crooked grin and stood up tall then faced back at me with the same Mexican, evil look with his eyebrowes twitching every which way. 'If you already have these women, why keep the extra mojo, it's just not the Mexican way!' The man looks around once more and shrugs as he responds to my expert offer of nothing from me and something from him.

'Well...I guess your right, I'll let you BORROW it, give it right back!' The Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniac said and started to lift his Sombrero off his head and slowly handed it to me. Quickly, and in an instant, I snatched the sombrero out of his hand and placed atop my oddly, egged shaped head.

'Now all that's missing is the true mask of Stimpo...from Mexico!' I said to myself thinking where to next...Mexico, duh!

'Que hable?' The Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniac said, sombrero-less and helplessly. (that means 'what did you say?' in spanish)

'I said, I was just leaving...right...leaving that'll do.' I said to myself once more. 'C'mon Jackass, your all partied out, let's get a move on.' I tugged at the donkey's collar and started to slowly pull out of the club with both the items successfully in my possesion.

Seemingly, not one of the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs bothered me on my way out, until...

'WAIT!' The leader man said as I was nearly clear out of his range of screaming...and mexiness. I turned around with both the items and standed tall letting out a small sigh. Expecting the worst, I called the man on.

'Give me your best shot, half mexi!' I yelled at him.

'I just wanted to let you know you got a little thing, on your lip, right there.' The man said pointing to the right side of his lip, meaning mine.

'Thanks...I guess...' I was completley purplexed by these men. I was simply waiting for them to chase me, plus it'd be a cool thing to add to my story when I tell my grandkids. But nonetheless, I continued on, clearing my lip and turning back around, mounting the donkey and trotting onward.

It took about three hours on the dirt road until I could finally hear the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs galloping toward me. I turned around to see what their cavalry was like...and once again, the men had out did themselves in stupitidy.

'My God...' I sighed at the horrid sight.

The men did not have horses, or donkeys or even camels, they were skipping, the man in the back seemed to be crackiling maracas together to make a galloping type noise. The men skipped with their arms out as if they were riding imaginary cavalry!

'Well, it's off to the races then!' I encouraged myself and clicked my feet together to start the donkey on the way to Mexico only to be followed by the enraged Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs, scolding and skipping with their maracas held high.
