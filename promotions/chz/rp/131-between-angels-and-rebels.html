Between Angels and rebels
Written: 8/8/02

Standing out from the crowd was something he had become painfully be eventually adoringly used to. He had even learnt to adore the attention it brought to him, be it good or bad. It made him feel ... unique. Let's face it, who wants to be ordinary? The ordinary die unnoticed, while the unique ones die with notice. It was simple, and very true when thought about logically.

The mall was buzzing with people, are you really surprised? It was the typical American mall, with the usual locals you would see in it. Hormone filled teenagers, young couples hand in hand in what they foolishly believed to be true love, gazing into each others eyes thinking about their feelings and the beautiful future they were planning for each other. Fools! Goths, grungers and punks patrolling the music stores, giving sharp and intimidating looks to anyone standing near the Pop section. He remembered being one of those metal kids. He missed those days sometimes. There were mothers with a cigarette dangling out of their mouth and one of those double pram contraptions shouting at her children to 'shut up!' over their piercing screams. The local Starbucks with filled with yuppies and 30 something businessmen and women all ready to get hostile if they weren't going to get their fix of caffeine within the next 30 seconds.

He was getting the expected attention he adored but didn't need or demand. People looked at him suspiciously and with interest as he stood there on the second floor leaning against one of the white painted walls with his right foot pressed up against the wall. He stood there observing and analysing the predictable regulars of the mall. He was still somewhat seething from the defeat on Wednesday.

'The damsel shall fall, fall to her fate!'

He spoke the words about Angel 'The TeXan' Summers under his breath calmly. He wasn't one of those men who would go out on a murderous rampage after a defeat ............... OK maybe he would, sometimes. However he didn't often get mad at opponents ........ he didn't even get even ..... he got rebellious.

Ryan Contra and Matt Blitz were expendable in his eyes. They were nothing more than pawns thrown to the feet of Axl for exposure. That was why he focused so much of his attention to Angel on Wednesday. He reminisced with a slight grin at how demonically satisfying it felt to drive his foot into her throat when he hit her with Twisted Intentions. How he marvelled at the destruction of his work as he watched her fall from the turnbuckle, clutching her throat, her lungs compressing as she grasped for that so needed oxygen that kept dodging her deep breaths. He took a slow deep breath, and went back to observing the people at the mall, until he found what he had so hoped he would discover.

She was a teenager, about 15. She had what he had hoped he would discover. An Angel 'The TeXan' Summers T-shirt on. The way he saw it had succumbed to the false lies that damsel had led the female population to believe. That she was some women of morals. He needed to let her see the light.

The young girl stopped as he approached her. She was with two other female friends, and they all looked with amazement at him.

'Like, oh my GOD! It's .................. you!'

Axl sighed and rolled his eyes slightly before forcing a smile to the girl with the T-shirt who uttered the comment.

'You like ......... so lost to Angel!'

The girl laughed and her, along with her friends laughed. They didn't seem as star struck as he thought they would be at seeing him, but then again he didn't class himself as a superstar.

'Yes .... well, I noticed you were a fan of that pesky little tramps!'

'Hey, she's like, so not a tramp ..................... do you know how long she must have spent getting that complexion!'

'I can only imagine'

Axl sighed again as he spoke. He came to the conclusion that the girls had a combined IQ of less than his shoe size.

'So like, what are you doing here. IS ANGEL HERE!' If she is you have got to get me her autograph. Oh wait ... are you two seeing each other now?'

He shuddered at the thought, and shook his head furiously. Then raised his hands to silence the two girls.

'No, look ........ I'm not here to bother you'

It was true. He wasn't like the usual people you see around who wear 'dark clothes' in wrestling. They were portrayed as people who were nothing more than people out for revenge on society. he was like that, but he wasn't like they portrayed them to be on television. Believe it or not Axl does have a heart and a conscience.

'Damn right your not, my boyfriend is around here somewhere and he'll kick your ass, JUST LIKE ANGEL DID!'

Axl paused for a moment, before nodding in sarcastic agreement. This seemingly confused the girl even more.

'What the hell is this?'

Axl looked over the girls shoulder at the owner of the voice, noticing it belonged to a strapping young men, probably a jock. He walked with a strut and put his arm around the young girl as he approached her, Axl assumed this was her boyfriend.

'Who's this circus freak!?'

Axl wondered if he would have talked differently about him if he was aware that he was an underground hit man with the power to manipulate the human mind at the drop of a hat.

'A guy who got his ass kicked by Ang-'

'I advise you to watch your mouth young.....'

'Hey circus freak! Nobody talks to my women like that you worthless piece of shit!'

Axl smiled and stroked his chin goatee slightly. It seemed as though he was delaying the inevitable outcome of violence about to occur. What he was really doing was analysing the surrounding area. No security guards near, security camera was looking the other way. The busy stores nearby were now closed. Would killing this man be too much? Possibly, even though it was getting quiet here it was still a bit too public.

'Very well my misguided intellectual'

'What!?'

With that the Angel Summers T-shirt began to grab his attention more than ever. Then with one quick movement, he reached both hands forth and ripped the shirt off the young female, leaving her wearing nothing more than her bra. She gasped in horror and covered herself with her arms immediately. Axl then quickly tossed the shirt into her boyfriends face. He staggered backwards, as he did Axl delivered his Twisted Intentions kick to the throat. He pulled the shirt back off the boys face as he fell to his knees clutching for the oxygen he was currently being starved of.

'Victims .......'

The boy looked up at Axl as security came jogging up the escalator stairs and his girlfriend screamed in high pitched screams.

'....aren't we all!'

With that Axl took off, running towards the nearby exit with the Angel Summers T-shirt in his hand.

* * * * * * * * * *

Darkness encompassed his room. Right now it was encompassing him. At this point in time he deeply embraced the deeply untrue feeling it brought to him. It helped him forget that how negative and pathetic this world can be, how this tormenting effect called life can grip us and squeeze the energy out of us.

Three candles strategically placed in his room provided the only source of positive light in his room. He didn't wish to use the light bulb he had. His bed sit was small but cosy. It had everything he needed.

He breathed heavily and grunted slightly as he attacked his punch bag in the corner. The sweat on his muscular toned body glimmered as the candles reflected on it amidst the darkness. He unleashed his unorthodox style of martial arts onto the punch bag. Kick after kick, punch after punch.

'On next weeks Wednesday Wild folks, we're gonna see four bitter enemies divided and forced to unite for one night only.'

He stopped as he heard these words. The sound from the television had only been a faint hum to him until now. However it had now grabbed his utmost attention.

'It's gonna be Matt Blitz and Ryan Contra Vs Axl Young and Angel Summers!'

The television fell back to a mere hum as he turned his attention away at the thought of this match. He was going to be taming with the very women he had spawned to hate with a passion.

'So, the deep dark demented rebel, and the conniving sly bitch from Texas! Forced to team!'

Axl grabbed something from up off the couch which lay opposite the television. He brought it up to his face slowly and smiled as he did. On closer inspection, it was clear the garment was the Angel Summers T-shirt he took from the young teenage girl earlier. He smothered his face in it and wiped the sweat away gently, breathing in deeply as the material covered his face. He eventually brought it down and away slowly, with the scene fading to black on the demonic smiling face of Axl.
