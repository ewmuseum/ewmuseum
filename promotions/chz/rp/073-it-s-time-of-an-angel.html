It's time of an Angel!
Written: 7/22/02

The scene opens and we are shown a brown haired, light green eye man. He’s facing the camera, but he’s not looking at it. He’s staring at something out of the scene, something we have no idea of yet. The man blinks and finally looks at the camera. He smiles and proceeds to look deep into the camera, as if he’s looking at everyone that is watching him. His light green eyes give you the feeling of happiness, warmness, and they make you feel safe. The man runs his right hand through his short brown hair and rubs the back of his neck.

“I guess this is the part where I tell you a little about myself?” He says as he stops rubbing his neck.

The man smirks and begins to laugh lightly, while doing so he begins to shake his head. He stops and looks back at the camera.

“Well my name is Angel, and I’m from the PWA.” Angel finally announces, reminding us just who he is.

When Angel finishes a voice with no face cuts in. A voice that is lovely, sexy, and very attractive.

“So Angel, how does it feel to be here in CHZ?” Asks the attractive voice.

Angel once again stops and smirks, still very much over whelmed by the woman’s voice.

“Ah man, to be given a chance like this is great. These type of chances only come once and awhile, so I feel pretty great.” Angel proclaims still retaining the smirk on his face.

The camera then pans out and we can finally see all of Angel. He’s wearing a white button up long sleeve shirt, with the sleeves rolled half way up, a pair of khaki pants, and a pair of light brown dress shoes. Angel’s looking very good, and very business like.

“So, what are some of your goals now that you are out of PWA and on your way to becoming a huge talent?” The attractive voice asks with a hint of flirting wrapped with her words.

Angel sits up and takes a long hard look down at his hands. He then looks back to the woman giving the interview, and proceeds to tell her his goals.

“Well first off I want to make myself known. I want to go out there each and every night and put on a good show; then after the world learns my name, I will start to do what I was meant to do, and that’s spread the meaning of God. See that’s why I choice this type of business, it gives me a big audiences.” Angel says with a look of pure confidence on his face.

“Well….” The voice stops, trying to figure out why a man would be so driven about spreading the meaning of God. “Don’t you care about titles, or winning?”
Angel then gives the interviewer a stupid look. He shakes his head in disgust and looks back down at his hands. He then looks back to the woman and regains his smirk.

“I can tell you will be one of the people I preach to the most. Well besides my partner Saint.” Angel testifies as he looks at the woman.

“Speaking of Saint, where is he?” Ponders the woman giving the interview.

“He had to go back to Ohio for a few days to help his sister. I believe he scheduled an interview with someone on a later date.” Angel says putting to rest anyone questions the interviewer had about Saint.

“So is there anything you would like to say to the world, and to your fellow roster members?” Asks the sexy hot voice of the woman giving the interview.

Angel then rubs his chin and thinks. He then nodes his head signaling he does in fact have something to say.

“To the world, and to the boys in the back. If you don’t believe in god now, you will soon!” Angel says while looking deep into the camera.
