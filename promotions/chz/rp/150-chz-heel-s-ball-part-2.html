CHZ Heel's Ball (part 2)
Written: 18th August 2002

Suddenly Texan bursts back into the room.

“Hey, I heard there was going to be a mud wrestling match here!” she says in a very cute southern accent.

Matt Hunt, the Flying Vergomovs, Stimpo, Axl Young, Tom Core and Redemption Song all cheer as they grab for another glass of punch.

“Damn, all empty,” Igor grunts. Olga quickly walks over to the punch bowl and opens up a galon jug of vodka that she’s had hidden underneath her dress.

“You go vatch da mud wrestling,” Olga says to Igor, draping over his shoulder like a weak-legged lush. “I’ll make more puuuuunch,” she slurs with a smile.

“Okay,” Igor replies, leaving Olga who immediately collapses to the ground without Igor as support and starts giggling uncontrollably.

Meanwhile, all the men plus Roland the eagle go downstairs into the basement of the building to see Misty battle Texan in a wild drunken mud wrestling match! The men make a complete circle around the pit and Matt Hunt stands up to act as the ring announcer:

“Ladies and gentlemens,” he slurs as he laughs while gulping down a little more punch, “its time for the main event. Oh man! Its two gorgeous women in the middle of a mud pit wrestling for nothing but pride! First its that sexy, messed up in the head, maybe a turncoat, maybe not valet for the CHZ Tag-Team Champions - MISTY!”

Misty walks out and all of the men start howling and hooting at the black-haired beauty wearing a black corset and black lace stockings with straps up to the bottom of the corset. Stimpo, in his princess dress, slaps his hand against the ground while panting like a dog. In fact, he crawls over to Misty on all fours and licks her leg!

“Watch it buster,” Misty growls, “I got a lot of fight in me!”

The gallery of CHZ heels let out more howls at Misty’s boldness, more to mock Stimpo than anything else! Stimpo gets right up and plants a long kiss right onto Misty to the outrageous cheers of the men! Misty finally pulls herself away and brushes off her mouth with both of her hands as if trying to wipe Stimpo cooties away. All the men continue to laugh as Misty lowers herself into the mudput and rubs some mub on her upper thighs and between her fingers.

“And her opponent, also gorgeous and the only babyface we’ve allowed in here tonight because she’s just that special,” Hunt says really laying it on thick, “again, the blonde beauty, the master of the groin shot, the people’s womens champion - ANGEL TEXAN SUMMERS!

Angel walks out to just the same sort of wild ovation from the drunked CHZ heels in the crowd, dressed in skimpy bluejean shorts and a practically unbuttoned white blouse. As Texan makes her way over to the mudpit, Axl Young moves out into her way and grabs her as if he was going to try a kiss! But Texan pulled her face away at the last second!

“No sir!” Texan emphatically barks at Young. “I don't associate with men who have eagles for a pet.”

“Whatever, psycho” Young mutters under his breath in reply.

Texan lowers herself into the mudpit but Misty grabs Texan’s legs and drops her straight into the mud!! Texan’s head is totally covered in the mud and Misty jumps on top of Texan’s back and continues to rub her head in the mud! The men above the pit laugh and cheer with delight before starting a mock “Texan” chant to get Angel fired up!

But it works! Texan immediately springs up and thrust kicks Misty in the gut, doubling Misty over. Texan walks up to Misty confidently and DDTs her straight down into the mud!! The men go wild and Texan, her own body completely covered in mud, raises both hands and poses for the men, allowing them to take in the sheer beauty of her form.

Misty isn’t even moving down in the mud but Texan jumps up and drops a big knee drop to the back of Misty’s head. Texan rolls around a bit in the mud, enjoying herself to the fullest before getting back up. Matt Hunt and Constantino Zergolov help Texan up out of the mudpit and Stimpo throws the Houston native a yellow towel to start cleaning off.

Back upstairs, Tom Core walks in on Igor with the Russian’s nose pressed up against the glass where the CHZ Tag-Team Championship belts are being displayed.

“Oh, you, shooo,” Core says while rolling up a newspaper on one of the leather couches in the room and walking over to the Carnival of Carnage member. “Those are our belts!”

“Ha!” Igor responds, smiling and falling down to the ground into a log roll.

“Okay,” Tom says, a little freaked out. “This party is officially getting strange.”

Soon, everyone notices that 7 hours have past and the group all decides to split up for the evening. Tom Core doesn’t explain anything about what happened during the 7 missing hours that everyone feel asleep after they had smoked from Ben Nighthorse Campbell’s “peacepipe.” It’s something Core wants to hold onto, until the time is right.

As everyone leaves, the most tearful farewell is that of Stimpo, who it seems will be announced as the newest member of the Watkins Group - and serving notice to the Carnival of Carnage that one one messes with the eternal Song of Redemption.
