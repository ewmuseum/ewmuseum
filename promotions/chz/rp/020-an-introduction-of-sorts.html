An Introduction, Of Sorts
Written: 15th June

Canuck Heat Zone. A Federation that, while small, has an illustrious history. One of the most popular independent feds in the world, ever. The old home of such big names as Don Rossi, Chris Matthews, Diablo, Thor, JT Fringe and Matt Helms. CHZ truly has a long and storied history, and every single person who steps into this proud Federation should respect and embrace that history.

Cassius Deamont doesn't.

Cassius Deamont doesn't really care about CHZ. He doesn't give a damn about it's past, about it's legacy. Cassius Deamont just cares about money, and making as much of it as he can. Cassius doesn't care about the people in CHZ, Cassius doesn't care about the history of CHZ, as long as payday comes round he doesn't care what happens to the CHZ. Nothing in this federation matters to him.

Oh, except gold. The CHZ World Title would be nice.

Oh yes, there is one reason he is here; besides the small amounts of money he's receiving. That World Title. For, just one day before he competes for the RUSH World Heavyweight Title at the Federation's debut Pay Per View, Ignition, Cassius has already set his sights on another goal. Not merely to become the first CHZ Champion since the federation re opened, but also to achieve something else.

To be a Double World Champion. Possibly the first.

After all, what's gonna get you more publicity, more interest - and, in turn, more money from interviews and advertising - than being World Champion of not one but two major wrestling promotions? Add that to the controversy Cassius had already caused in RUSH and was sure to continue doing in CHZ and there can be no doubting that Cassius is sure to make headlines.

Well, that's the plan.

But, for the moment at least, Cassius isn't going to need to focus on the CHZ World Title. Because, for the Federation's first show back, it would seem that they have given him a different challenge. A one on one match with none other than...

'Vanilla Skillz? What sort of name is that?' Cassius Deamont exclaims as he sits on his private jet en route to the RUSH Pay Per View, reading a sheet of paper that features the card for CHZ's first edition of Wednesday Night Wild since its restart.

'Apparently a Vanilla Ice wannabe,' The voice of one of Cassius' two lovers, Kathryn, comes from her seat to his right as she looks through some notes she has been given on Cassius opponent for the show.

'Yeah, check this out,' Comes the voice of Cassius other lover, Erica, from his left hand side, 'Mark Kiggle was born in Canada, and was pretty plain growing up. Listened to everything his friends listened to, music wise, at least until he heard a break through artist, that sent him spiralling down his current path. That artist, the one, the only, Vanilla Ice.'

'Vanilla Ice? You're telling me I'm facing a Vanilla Ice wannabe?' Cassius said, seeming completely outraged by this, 'I've fought some of the best there are - Jonathan Drachen, Skye Stone, Tex Kelley, Maxx Crimson - and the best that this god forsaken Federation can throw at me is a failed rap artist? This is absurd!'

'A disgrace!' Kathryn agreed.

'A travesty!' Erica added.

'Well, if they've done this because they feel they need to find out how talented I am - as if that was an issue - then I can tell you now, I will not disappoint! Because if they're going to insult me by putting me in a match with such a lowly nobody then the least I can do is return the favour by putting one of their so-called 'superstars' out of commission! And if they take the Jeremy Howard approach, and try to screw with me, I'll just do to them what I'm in the process of doing to Jeremy - make their lives a living hell! Now, enough of this CHZ nonsense, I'm sure there are far more... Enjoyable things we could do with our time.'

And with that closing statement Cassius' eyes meet, in turn, with Kathryn and Erica's, and a smile can be seen on his lips as our cameras fade to black.
