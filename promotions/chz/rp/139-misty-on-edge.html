Misty on edge!
Written: 12th August 2002

Tom Core looks down impatiently at his watch, tapping his shined black leather shoes against the leg of his sleek metal desk. It has been a quiet day at the Watkins Group Headquarters in New York City, the home base for Redemption Song, as Core has shut down media access to the powerful tag-team that has been turning heads ever since their arrival in CHZ. Core's gaze shifts to his window and he sees just a sampling of the millions of New Yorkers going about their regular hum-drum dog days of summer. After what seems like an eternity, Misty, Redemption Song's beautiful valet, opens the door and enters the office.

'It's about time,' Core says, tapping his watch.

'I just wanted to check in on the boys to see how the training was going,' Misty replies, her face dropping down and reddening as if she had just been caught with her hand in the cookie jar.

'You know we have a big match coming up on Wednesday, right?' Tom asks.

Misty looks up and her face makes a confused expression.

'I just told you I was down there watching training,' Misty answers, her voice just the slightest bit shaky. 'Don't you think I know the CHZ Tag-Team Championship is a big deal?'

'Are you okay Mist?' Tom asks as he gets up from his desk and moves over to place a comforting arm around Misty's shoulder. 'You've been acting very strangely ever since we took that train ride....'

'I'm FINE!' Misty says in an aggravated tone as she slinks away from Core's arm and walks to the other side of the office. 'Jeez, I wish I could just go one day without someone calling me a freak or a stalker or accusing me of destroying down churches....'

'No one mentioned churches,' Core says, his eyebrows raised. 'I was just asking if you were okay.'

'Well, I'm fine,' Misty says, 'and I didn't find anything else out about that mystery team that jumped Jim and Benif that's what you're going to ask me.'

Core takes a thoughtful step back, strokes his chin and nods.

'Misty, when we go up to Edmonton for Wild, I want you to come with me to find a place that we can make our home base in Canada. All this flying is going to catch up with us sometime and we're not going to be ready inside the ring.'

Misty stammers back toward the door, as if Core's suggestion threw a wrench into plans that she had already made for her return trip to Canada.

'Uh... sure, okay,' Misty says as she opens the door. 'I'll go and start some online research now.'

As Misty exits, Tom shakes his head and slumps back down into his chair. After a long moment, he picks up the telephone.

'Hello... is this the Royal Canadian Mounted Police?' Core asks, his own voice not sounding entire sure of himself. 'I think I might have something for you...'
