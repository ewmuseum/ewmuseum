Merely A Flesh Wound
Written: 8/8/02

The scene fades into the backstage area of Maple Leaf Gardens in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. It is nearly empty, as CHZ Wild ended about two hours ago. The only people left are the crewmembers cleaning up the camera equipment, taking down the ring, and other things like that. The mood backstage is very upbeat, as tonight’s Wild was a huge success. The backstage is mostly silent, a few crewmembers talking here and there, but nothing very loud, until the sound of a door swinging open and men shouting is heard.

“Wait, Mr. Schultz, we’re not done yet,” yells a man after the door is heard swinging open.

“Ahh, who cares,” responds the familiar voice of Jake Schultz. Jake is then seen staggering down the hallway and around a corner. He is wearing only a pair of black mesh shorts. He has his ribs taped up; he has three large bandages on his head, as well as stitches starting above his right eyebrow and going all the way down his right check to where his lips are. He is barely dragging himself down the hallway. He is stumbling and swerving almost falling over just trying to make his way out of the arena with two medics chasing after him.

“Wait, you’re in no condition to drive Jake,” yells a short, skinny trainer with glasses, as he chases Jake down the hallway.

“Please stop, we still have work to do on you,” yells the other trainer, he is a little taller and a lot fatter than the other. They chase Jake all the way down the hall and out into the parking garage of Maple Leaf Gardens. They finally catch him and grab him.

“Come on, you’re coming with us,” says the skinny trainer as he tugs on Jake’s arm to try to pull him back to the training room.

“I’m not going anywhere, now get the hell away from me!” shouts Jake as he tugs his arms away from the trainers sending them sprawling out onto the concrete floor. They pick themselves up and brush themselves off.

“Fine, it’s your choice, but don’t say we didn’t offer,” says the fat trainer as he turns and walks away from the parking garage and back into the arena. The thin trainer then shakes his head and turns to follow him. They head into the arena and leave Jake in the parking garage alone. Jake is heading over to his van when he spots one of the crewmembers walking towards one of the production trucks.

“Hey, cameraman, get over here,” shouts Jake. The noise echoes throughout the garage and the crewmember turns to look at him.

“What do you want?” inquires the crewmember as he continues over to the production truck.

“I said get over here, and bring that camera with you,” says Jake as he groans in pain as he continues to limp through the parking garage.

“Yes sir,” says the crewmember. He makes a ninety-degree turn and sprints over to where Schultz is hunched over in agony.

“That’s good, now get that camera going and turn it on, I have something to say,” says Jake as he fiddles with the bandages on his head. The crewmember then sets the large, black, plastic case on the floor and pops in open revealing the camera. He picks up the camera and rests it on is shoulder. He plays around with some buttons on the camera and then a red light on the front comes on. The crewmember turns and points the camera at Jake. Jake clears his throat and begins to speak.

“Well Matt, I hope you’re happy with what you and your circus freak friends have done to me. Look at me! Three cracked ribs, countless cuts and scrapes on my head, a severe concussion, and two-hundred and eighty eight, yes that’s what I said, two-hundred and eighty eight stitches in my head. Not to mention many other injuries I just didn’t have treated. So Matt, Igor, Constantino, and Olga, was it fun? Did you guys enjoy doing this to me? I sure as hell hope you enjoyed, because let me guarantee you, it will never, NEVER, happen again. Enjoy the taste of victory this week, because when I get a hold of you next week all you’ll taste is your own blood,” says Jake. He adjusts himself and places all his weight on his right leg, he then starts speaking again.

“Hunt, you’re a piece of trash, a cheating piece of trash that is. You know damn well I would have won that match if you hadn’t used the ropes when you pinned me. If you fought a fair fight like me you would have been in this position, not me. And even when your circus freak friends ran in I fought all three of punks off. If it weren’t for that bitch Olga you all three would look like me. That bitch is lucky you woke up when you did Hunt, or I would have snapped her damn neck. But then you attacked me from behind like the coward that you are and then the beating ensued. And here we are, I should be in a hospital, and you four, the ‘Carnival of Carnage’ are probably off somewhere celebrating. But listen here you freaks, and mark my words, I will get a hold of you all next week, and not one of you will walk away from that arena,” says Jake as he puts his face right up into the camera. He finishes his sentence and just stares into the camera with a crazed look in his eyes. He then slowly pulls his head away from the camera. He then walks a few feet over to his van and opens up the driver’s side door and gets in. The cameraman follows him over. Jake is about to close the door, but he notices the camera and begins to speak again.

“Matt, Igor, Constantino, and yes, even you Olga, you all better watch your backs next Wednesday. Every strange noise, every time you feel someone following you, you better be ready to fight just incase, because it’s not a question of if I will get my revenge, it’s a question of when. My advice to all of you is to travel together. Don’t get caught by yourself, because that’s when I’m going to get you. Well I think I’m off to the hospital now to get these wounds checked out. Hunt, just do one thing for me next week, be ready, camache,” says Jake. He then slams the door to his van closed and starts it up. He backs up out of the parking spot and then speeds away from where the cameraman stands. He pulls out of the parking garage, turns the corner, and drives off.
