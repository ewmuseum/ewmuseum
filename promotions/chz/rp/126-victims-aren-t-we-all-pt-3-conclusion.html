Victims ... aren't we all? Pt:3 -Conclusion
Written: 6/8/02

Edward sighed as he stretched his arms out. He was laying softly in his four poster bed at his estate. His head ached terribly, a sign that that Axl's manipulative grip had faded and ceased. He slowly rubbed the palms of his hand across his forehead as means to try and soothe it. He then yawned and placed him right arm out to the side of him, expecting to feel the calm smooth body of Natasha, his wife. however all he felt was the cool clean crisp white sheets meet his touch. He then looked to his right, and noticed the quilt was thrown off carelessly. That appeared strange, Natasha was Miss. Neat to say the least, and for her to stumble out of bed carelessly and not fold the sheets back up neatly was unheard of. It looked like she was in some urgent hurry to get out of bed.

Edward thought nothing more of the idea, and agreed with the thought in his head the postman must have come early with a package, for her no doubt, something large and expensive ordered from one of her many catalogues where all the prices had no less than three digits. So Natasha, excited, threw the sheets off and ran down to collect her parcel. Yes, that seemed logical, that must have been what had happened.

Edward then rolled on his side and let his eyes encompass his vision and close shut. Leaving him in darkness. He had way too much to drink that night, and he blamed it on that Alex fellow.

'Hmmm, haha, yes Alex, fine chap'

He spoke the words carelessly and sluggishly as he dozed back to sleep. He blamed Alex for buying him all those drinks. He should have known better than to accept them all and not buy Alex one, and he should have known his limit. How he managed to make it home at all last night after all those drinks defied logic to him. Then he remembered they went for that walk, and spotted that beggar, well , a money box in the form of a beggar.

'Natasha, bring me some coffee sweetheart!'

The words barely escaped his throat from his dazedness, but he assumed Natasha would hear him. He then thought of that beggar some more. His devilish menacing grin, his dull emotionless eyes. His head, how deathly cold it was. His repetitive voice and the ever present dull tone the words he spoke were uttered in.

'Natasha! Some coffee PLEASE!'

A slight improvement on his last shout to her octane wise, that time she should have definitely heard him. He needed some coffee urgently if he planned on sobering up at all today. So again he thought back to last night. How he felt the mans heartbeat, but waved it off as some mechanical cog inside him, it was noting more than a machine after all. A simple yet oversized moneybox, probably used in an old arcade in that area. He thought about how he placed the coin into the thick black groove in the mans head. The sound of the coin hitting and mixing with the other coins in his head, the CLINK and CLANG noise they made on impact.

'COFFE NOW NATASHA, PLEASE!!!'

He was getting impatient now, and when he got impatient he got migraines. Not that didn't have one already, he did, but the thing he needed was one of his impatient migraines on top of the one he already had. Natasha was just being downright rude and blatantly ignorant now. So once again he thought back to lasts nights festivities. He thought of the brick that he found on the desecrated cold floor. He remembered having that urge to break dismantle and destroy something. He remembered hoisting the brick up with his two hands and bring it crashing down into the face of the beggar shaped money box, causing it to cave in. He recalled repeating doing it over and over again, eventually smashing the beggars face into pieces and sending numerous amounts of coins scattering all over the cold floor.

'Oh come on Natasha! This behaviour is simply uncalled for!'

He slowly hoisted himself up from the bed as he remembered how he ran across the cold floor picking up the coins. How foolish he felt, a wealthy man like him running around picking up coins. It didn't bear thinking about, and the thought of him picking up coins off the floor made him realise how drunk he must have been last night. Slowly he threw the sheets off himself. If Natasha wasn't going to tidy the sheets after she got up then neither was he. He looked down at himself in disgust, he was sleeping in his clothes from last night. He sighed in disappointment and shame. Natasha had obviously seen him in his clothes and he hoped she hadn't phoned her friends and told them of this utter embarrassment. Edward coming home in the early hours of the morning drunk out of his mind and falling asleep in his clothes. Yet he knew the first thing Natasha did every morning was phone her close friends after her coffee and croissants. So this realisation didn't help his mood or migraine, knowing that Natasha had probably told half of the tennis and golf club of Edwards escapades.

He got up from the bed and felt something wet and moist inside his trouser pockets. He stopped as he took a few steps towards the door, and looked down at his trousers. There were stains seeping through from his pockets leaving damp murky patches on his trousers. As he slid his hand in his pockets he remembered something else about last night. Axl had grabbed hold of him, rather forcefully and started whispering to him. Edwards head hurt even more at thinking of this. he slid his hand into his pocket. Axl whispered into his ear and Edward remembered feeling a a loss of control after that. He remembered feeling like Axl was in control of his every action. Like Axl was a puppet master and Edward was his puppet.

He brought his hand out of his pocket slowly. In his hand ................. were lumps of brain tissue! Edward gasped, he sound found himself struggling for air. His bed was full of blood and more brain tissue. Natasha had run once she woke up and saw it all over him.

'What in....what?'

Suddenly everything fell into place. Axl had manipulated Edward, made him believe that beggar was some money box. When he caved in his skull it wasn't really coins that came pouring out, it was..... it was his brain, blood, tissue. Edward had run around picking it all up and shoved it in his pockets.

'Oh GOD!'

'Yes, he's just upstairs'

That was Natasha's voice, and it was followed by a set of footsteps. He screamed. He screamed as he looked at his hands covered in brain tissue. he screamed as they came upstairs. he screamed as they put him in the straight jacket. He screamed as they found him guilty. They screamed as they put him in the white padded room. He screamed.

** * * * * * * * * * * * * * *

'There's a little extra in there, for a job very well done!'

Axl took the brown parcel from William and said nothing after it. He placed the parcel inside his long black leather jacket and then looked back at William, who's grin was broad, and Axl guessed it had been since his brother had been locked up in the nuthouse.

'So your mother and father have taken him out of the will?'

'Oh of course. Their not going to leave their estate and whatnot to a fruit loop of a son. They think he's insane, just like I wanted them too, just like I told you to make it out to be, and by gosh you did it.'

Axl merely nodded as he uttered the words before looking around the living room of the mansion William lived in. Plush comfortable furniture, fine works of art on the walls, a big cackling fire. A typical mansion really. William stood there like a blithering idiot, smiling as usual. It was here that Axl became certain that he truly hated William with a passion.

'You know I'd like to make you an employee of mine. Like I said there's plenty of people I want out of the way. I could make life very comfortable for you, money, even power.'

Axl chuckled and shook his from left to right gently.

'No William, in case you hadn't noticed the best things in life aren't for me'

William nodded slowly. Little did he know Axl had one final trick up his sleeve for old William.

'Anyway I'm no longer going to beat about the bush William. I am a hired killer and the way I see it I have not killed anyone.'

Williams face suddenly dropped, there was no arguing he didn't know what was about to happen. Axl edged right to the side and behind him within almost the blink of an eye.

'Good bye William'

'You-no.....!'

Axl broke his neck with ease as usual, and let his limp body fall to the ground. He fell face first to the carpet and Axl sighed gently.

'Victims ... aren't we all!'

Fade to black.
