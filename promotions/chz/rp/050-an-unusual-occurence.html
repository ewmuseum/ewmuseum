An Unusual Occurence
Written: 8th July 2002

(The scene opens in a public house, it is a fairly small room and there is a distinct smell of stale cigarettes, and alcohol – something that locals of the pub are undoubtedly used to. Sitting down at a small table is Jack Williams. Usually the guy is seen wearing elaborate sparkly tops, and glittering jackets with his long golden locks neatly style, today however, is a little different. William’s wears a black leather jacket, a rock t-shirt showing his support for Pantera, and blue Tommy Jeans, on his feet are some casual shoes with a fancy sterling silver buckle, the man has taste but he also has money. Williams is sitting on his own sipping at a Carling beer, occasionally he will tweak his thumbs indicating that he is waiting for somebody, but whom? The only people we know whom are in his life are his tag-team partner Amanda, and his housemaid Maria, both incidentally are women. Williams seems to get on well with women, some have put it down to his charm, others his good looks, and the jealous have suggested he is gay. Williams certainly isn’t gay just fashionable, something that most straight heterosexual men like himself seem to lack. As Williams takes another sip of his cold beer someone or something seems to catch his attention. His eyes stay fixated on something momentarily and it is only when he is joined by a young man that it is clear whom he was looking at. The man wears a black jacket, black trousers, black shoes, black tie, and white shirt his hair is brown and slicked back neatly. The man seats himself next to Jack brandishing two pints of beer in his hand, one for Jack, and one for himself. The man gives the beer to Jack, and Jack smiles finishing off the last of his previous beverage.)

“Good evening Mr. Williams, and how are you today?” the man said in a stereotypical Canadian accent.

“I’m good thanks, just glad you could make it, how was the traffic?” Jack said making conversation.

“So, so,” the man replied, “how are you finding the whole CHZ experience at the moment?”

“It seems ok to me, the staff are friendly enough, as are the wrestlers but I can’t help but feel that there is a lot of heat backstage, I guess that is expected though when you are as good as me, you are bound to run into jealous wrestlers who wish they could be you, jealous wrestlers that can only dream of touching “The Ego” Jack Williams, let alone wrestling with the guy,” Jack said arrogantly grinning.

“Seems you haven’t lost your confidence,” he said pausing briefly, “nor your ego come to think about it.”

Williams shook his head and again his face was filled with that infamous egotistical smile, “Gene, when you are as good as Jack Williams, confidence, and egos never fade. I am quite simply “the man” in this federation, nothing less, but a lot more.”

(There was a brief silence as the two men looked at each other, the silence was short-lived however when Gene began to speak.)

“I have a proposition for you Williams,” he said seriously, “a proposition that I think you are going to find it hard to refuse, the proposition of propositions.”

“Just get on with it, and cut the crap,” Williams said smiling.

“I know you aren’t the kind of man that likes getting his hands dirty but that is exactly what I want you to do, as you know I have worked as an undercover detective myself for a good few years, and I want you to follow my lead,” Gene said smiling.

“What the hell? I am a wrestler not a detective, is this permanent or one off?”

“We will discuss that after the first operation, but I do feel that you will want to strongly consider working full time with me,” Gene said passing Williams a brown envelope.

Williams gazed down at it, a thin smirk spread across his face, “What is this for?” Williams asked.

“It is your cash upfront for the operation, $250,000, enjoy it,” Gene then began to lower his voice, “I know the money is a lot but this undercover work is going to require a lot, we are going to be raiding the giant villa in London as it is reported that illegal activities are going on there.”

“You mean THE villa?” Williams said a little surprised.

“The very one,” Gene said.

“God damn…now I know why you needed to give me so much money, what equipment are we taking with us?”

“Everything, we will be taking guns, medical kits, and rations,” Gene said.

“I think we better talk somewhere else about this in more detail…”

(The scene fades as the two men walk off about to discuss negotiations further.)
