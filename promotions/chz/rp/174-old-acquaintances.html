Old acquaintances
Written: 31/8/02

The rain swirled down from the heavens in numerous misguided directions, yet each direction it slithered off to eventually led to the same direction. Downwards, but more importantly, down onto him. The rain slithered and hammered down onto his shoulders, his face, his trousers, his entire clothing in general. it fell onto his hair, which was now nothing more than a clingy mess upon his handsome yet mysterious and intriguing face. Now the blackness of his hair covered his face, like the blackness of sorrow covered his emotions and heart.

Beneath him cars soared past. Horns were beeped furiously and violently and the screeching of tires squealed throughout the caliginous night that watched intimidating over all the proceedings. People walking beneath him, 25 floors beneath him to be exact, were all running whilst getting drenched. The only thing that instantly and obviously marginally separated these people from Axl tonight was that they were running from the rain and avoiding it, whereas he was immobile and embracing it.

He looked down as he stood on the balcony of the apartment, the bright reflection of neon lights on the wet morbid puddles on the roads and pavements from 7-11's were blurred and unclear, yet they helped built up the predictable and expected seen on a night...well, on a night like this.

Prostitution was clear and evident on the streets below. The prostitutes were the only people not running from the rain and the only people in vibrant red latex skirts and tops which almost matched the eye blistering shades of the neon signs on the 7-11's 50 yards away from the. Drug addicts were also among the assumingly usual residents of the lonely night, huddled in dark alleyways and at the side of quiet shops or business buildings that had closed up for the night except for the usual thirty something year old male who worked late to avoid the obvious yet bitter harsh and depressing fact that he had nobody to go home to.

'He HAD the whole world in his hands' were the only words he uttered into the long lonely dark wet night.

Suddenly behind him the lights switched on, and he turned calmly and slowly. After about five seconds of the lights having been turned on a young brunette female walks into the main living room of the apartment, and walks straight to the kitchen, followed by a small cat. Moments later she can be seen setting down a bowl in the corner to which the cat hurriedly scurries over to and begins to ravage and digest at.

The young female then walks to the living room and stretches her arms out, followed by moving her neck from right and then left........ then she sees him.

'HOLY SHIT!'

Her surprised and shocked and probably fearful reaction comes out in a short violent wisp as her breath was too afraid to eject from her lungs. She stands there momentarily, sold as a stone gargoyle statue that would sit on top of a derelict sacred building, mostly in shock and panic. Then she suddenly relaxes, not much, but slightly, her brain, nerves and mind all simultaneously recognize the figure standing on the balcony.

'A...Axl?' She says almost in a whisper, but her voice is more stable than it was when it uttered the horrified cry a few moments ago.

The deep dark demented rebel merely standing there motionless with Roland the eagle simply turning his head from left to right, and then directly back forward whilst arching his wings slightly.

'Yes' He says ever so softly, the words brushing against his lips as he utters them whilst nodding his head back and forth slowly.

She lets out a relieved grin, before hurriedly walking to the balcony windows and opening them up. The Rebellious One then calmly smiles slightly as he walks in, his clothes darker than their original supposed shade from the rain having been beating on it for 2 solid straight hours. Yet the female seems not to notice nor care.

'Wha-Why...?'

He turned slowly to look at her as she closed the balcony windows. She was now much more calmer and relaxed, he didn't think she would have coped this well with the sudden and startling greeting he knew he would bring her.

'What were you doing out there, how did you get up there?'

'The fire escape' He answered simply.

'W-what are you doing here?' Her voice was ringing with amazement.

'I was in the neighbourhood'

'What?'

'I have some events that I will be attending in the area, so I thought I'd, ya'know, drop by'

'Drop by?! On my fucking balcony ledge!'

He shrugged his shoulders while letting out a small grin while Roland soared off his shoulder and onto the arm of an armchair positioned to the right of a wide screen television.

'What the-'

'It's OK, that's Roland, he'll be fine there he won't harm you Francine'

The woman, now identified as Francine looks at Roland the eagle slightly hesitantly, before turning her head and walking to the kitchen area of the dulex apartment and opening the fridge.

'You want a drink, a beet or something?'

Axl merely shook his head. Francine however, calm as she had now become, still needed the sweet taste and the intoxicating feeling of alcohol to run and pass through her body to try and make her nerves gather themselves and reform back to normal. She brought out the bottle of Budweiser, hardly the drink he expected her to have in stock and the hardly the drink he assumed that would calm her nerves properly. She flipped the top off and proceeded to knock back the alcoholic liquid substance without stopping for breath. She eventually drew the bottle away, now half empty, and took a deep breath.

'Sorry, it's just.....'

'I scared you, I know I understand. I apologise I didn't intentionally mean to scare you like that'

She laughed a little before finishing off the beer and tossing it in the garbage.

'That's OK, Jesus how long has it been anyway?'

'Since we last seen each other? Nearly three years'

'Holy shit, of course the last time we seen ach other was at Tanya's....'

She stopped and became hesitant in her speech.

'Tanya's funeral, yeah that's right'

'God, it's been that long already

The dark rebel merely nodded.

'You look... you look.....'

'The complete opposite of the false compliment you were about to pay me, I know'

'Oh come on, don't be like that'

'Don't be like what? Honest? Truthful? Are you surprised that I am, are truth and honesty really that alienated in society these days?'

She looked to the floor nervously, avoiding his face.

'I - I'm sorry Francine, I didn't mean that. Just you know, I've got a lot of things on my mind lately'

She looked back up and made eye contact with him again and nodded while smiling.

'I know, I understand. I watch you sometimes, on television, that federation CHZ right?'

'That hellhole and comedy act of an organisation, yes'

'Oh right. How are things going there, I watched you about three weeks ago but haven't had much time to follow it since'

'Not bad, has both it's positive and negative points'

He didn't want to tell her about his plans of using Angel Summers for the brunt of his preaching and sadistic amusement by using her as a human sacrifice for the entire world to see. Yet he still had not overlooked the threat of Matt Blitz and Ryan Contra in that match either, but Angel was still his main focus in the match.

'Look, you want to sit down?'

The dark rebel sighed and nodded gently, before taking off his long black leather jacket and folding it up and placing it onto the arm chair of where Roland was sitting. He himself, then sat down in the arm chair and brushed some of the long black damp hair out of his face, as Francine sat down on the couch to the right of him.

'So, how has life been treating you?'

He looked up with an expression that clearly read 'Are you serious?'

'Was that a stupid question?' She asked while chuckling slightly.

He simply nodded slowly, still a little taken back by how stupid the question was.

'Look, I'm not bothering you am I?'

'Oh no, I come home from a hard days work, I find you standing on my balcony to greet me only to find to find your still in the same state, if not worse than when I last saw you nearly three years ago at Tanya's funeral'

The words came out sarcastically, but he knew she meant every word she uttered. The harsh but obvious fact was that she was right. For three years he had been in the same state, and to be honest, he was surprised he had let himself live this long.

'Seriously Axl, what brought you here tonight?'

He rested back comfortably in the arm chair and sighed through his nose. He closed his and rubbed them with his hands before opening them again. He was tired, exhausted and in need of sleep, yet sleep rarely came to him these days.

'I wanted to see you. You were Tanya's best friend. You moved away 2 months before she was murdered and the last time we saw each other face to face, was, like you said, at Tanya's funeral'

A predictable deep mournful silence followed, both of them searching their minds and uncovering the memories past of the beautiful love of Axl's life, Tanya.

'I miss her' He spoke softly.

'I miss waking up next to her. I miss the way she would scream at seeing just the smallest insects, and come begging for me to come and kill them. I miss the way she would cook breakfast every Saturday morning for us, and how we would sit in bed eating it. I miss the night we would spend together in each others arms watching some romance film that she would rent out and that I complained about. I overlooked these small things Tanya was alive. Now I want her and those memories to come back and become my reality, I want the more then the will to live. I miss her'

'I know, me too kiddo'

She always called him kiddo, mainly due to the age difference between them, which was all of a year, but she still enjoyed to call him it. He suspected it was mostly down to force of habit.

'You know I thought you were, or would have....'

It's amazing sometimes how you know what people are trying to say.

'Suicidal, going to kill myself?'

'Well, yeah. Sorry if that hurts you, me talking about that, but you were'

'Oh I know'

He nodded as he spoke the comment with such coolness and conviction.

'There are only two things keeping me alive on this earth right now. Getting rid of this pent up frustration and hatred for the world I have, and finding Tanya's killers. I need no oxygen to live, I need not food nor water, I only need these feelings of sorrow and vengeance to continue to pour their sadistic fuel into the burning fire in the pit of my soul. It is because of those emotions that I have the will to get out of bed in the morning. It is because of those emotions that I can resist the urge and temptation to pick up a blade and slice away at my wrists. Those emotions are the reason that I don't swallow a handful of pills and drift off into my eternal deep sleep'

Silence fell between the two once again, both again looking and searching in tiers minds of fond memories of past of Tanya.

'Anyway.....'

He stood and unfolded his jacket and slipped it on.

'I must be going. Sorry for frightening you, I just wanted to see you again. I missed Tanya, maybe in some small way seeing you would ease the pain, I suppose it did slightly, but the sadistic drive is still inside my soul. thankfully.'

'Well, um-I...........call again'

He merely smiled, and did not answer, although he had a feeling that he might. With that, he strolled towards the front door of Francine's apartment.

'Roland' He uttered calmly.

Roland then soared off the arm of the armchair and perched himself on Axl's shoulder.

'Farewell' He said to Francine.

Then as the dark rebel left, leaving Tanya still somewhat surprised and slightly confused, the scene fades to the shade that encompasses Axl's soul................. darkness.
