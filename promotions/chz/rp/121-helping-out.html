Helping Out!
Written: 8/4/02

The scene opens and we are shown both Angel and Saint. They both are wearing a pair of khaki pants, Angel has on a light blue button up shirt, and Saint has on a white button up shirt. Angel has his short brown hair spiked up, and has his light green eyes covered up by a pair of Fossil sunglasses. Saint has his should length black hair pulled back in a tail, and his dark brown eyes are very much seen. His eyes capture you and immediately you start watching his eyes. He then begins to lightly blink his eyes, and for the first time we see a sign of emotion from him. Saint looks to be a little sad. The scene then pans out and we see that Angel and Saint are on a stage. They are both sitting on black stools, and they both have microphones in front of them. We then see a very large group of people in the audience. Music then begins to play and Angel puts his mouth to the mic.

Angel: “Wish I was too dead to cry, the self-affliction fades, stones to throw at my creator,
Masochist to which I cater.”

Saint then joins in.

Angel/Saint: “You don’t need to bother
I don’t need to be
I’ll keep slipping farther
But once I hold on I won’t let go ‘til it bleeds”

Saint then captures you again with his eyes and begins to sing a solo.

Saint: “Wish I was too dead to care
If indeed I cared at all
Never had a voice to protest
So you fed me shit to digest
I wish I had a reason
My flaws are open season
For this I gave up trying
One good turn deserves my dying”
Angel joins back in.
Saint/Angel: “You don’t need to bother
I don’t need to be
I’ll keep slipping farther
But once I hold on I won’t let go ‘til it bleeds”
Both Angel and Saint then take deep breaths. As the music plays the look out into the crowed as well as you do. You see that the crowed is very much into what they are hearing. Saint then gets ready to sing again.

Saint: “Wish I died instead of lived
The zombie hides my face
Shelf forgotten with its memories
Diaries left with cryptic entries”
Angel then gets ready to join Saint.

Angel/Saint: “You don’t need to bother
I don’t need to be
I’ll keep slipping farther
But once I hold on I won’t let go ‘til it bleeds

You don’t need to bother
I don’t need to be”
Angel then finishes the song by himself and Saint lowers his head trying to hide the emotions he’s feeling.

Angel: “I’ll keep slipping farther
But once I hold on I’ll never look down my deceit”

As the music finishes, Angel lowers his head. After a few seconds Angel and Saint look up and see that the audience is on their feet cheering them on. Angel then puts his mouth back to the mic.

Angel: “That song was for the people that felt the lose of those churches burned down. All our love and prayers go out to the families that attended the Truro Church and the Moncton Church. And believe me, we will find out who did this. Because god as already forgiven us if we kick their asses!”

Saint and Angel then stand up from black stools and leave the stage as another woman walks on. She most likely is also there to help raise funds for the churches.
