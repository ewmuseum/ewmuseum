Manipulation: Myth or legend?
Written: 11/8/02

'New methods are being discovered everyday, new techniques are being created via the minds and brains of the top people in this field of expertise'

The room was large, larger than he first thought when he entered. The carpet was a deep navy with oak furnished and varnished walls. There were about 60 people in the room at least, all seated in rows that were in a half circle form. The rows ascended upwards and faced down on small stage. Most of the people there were either very young, in their early twenties, students he guessed, or people in their mid thirties. He guessed the latter where professionals in the topic under discussion today. Down on the small stage where all the seats were facing, a tall man in his mid thirties, with a white beard and a bald head. He talked in a light but professional voice.

'Gathered here today we have the veterans of this field, the men and women who laid the illustrious path for the new blood, the younger generation with us here today as well, to follow down. These are the men and women, of both old and new, who will lead us into the future.'

Up at the back row, sitting by himself in the corner, sat the deep dark demented rebel Axl Young. As usual he looked distinctly out of place in his clothing attire.

'These are the men and women, who will lead Psychology into the next generation!!!'

A loud round of applause then ensued from everybody in the room, everyone except Axl. The small but stout bald man lapped all the positive attention up and smiled a wide smile revealing a wide set of teeth, probably false Axl thought. He then waved his hand in a gesture for the applause to stop.

'Thank you, thank you..... now, are there any questions'

An immediate number of hands were thrust into the air, like a number of Nazis doing the heil Hitler salut. However Axl was not ready to wait his turn for his question to be answered, so he simply stood up.

'Professor Willard'

The professor looked to the owner of the voice, yet he did not smile he was clearly not amused that Axl had taken matters into his own hands to get himself noticed here.

'Um ye sir, can you please wit your turn?'

'........ no!'

A thick deep silence followed, and all the hands fell back down and in their replacement, a set of heads turned to face Axl.

'I have a question, or somewhat of a revelation if you will'

The professor now seemed to be intrigued by Axl's comment, and now rested his arms against the stand in front of him where he had his notes.

'Very well, ask away'

'I shall. I came here to ask on your theory on manipulation in your book. Psychology: The art and works by, Professor Nathan Willard'

The professor smiled at the sound of the name of his book.

'Yes, I did do a small theory on manipulation in the art of psychology'

'Yes, you associated it as nothing more than an urban legend, and nothing more than amateur hypnotism'

'Yes, that is correct'

Axl slowly began walking down the small set of steps leading down to the circular area where the seats were assembled.

'Well ... I beg to differ.'

The professor laughed aloud, accompanied by numerous students and other professionals in the room.

'W-what do you....'

'I believe!...'

He shouted the two words which then caused the rest of the people in attendance to fall silent.

'Thank you. I believe that the art of manipulation does strongly exist in modern day society. I believe that psychology has played a part in spawning it, and I believe that you have been to dumb, foolish and ignorant to acknowledge this or even write about it in your worthless 432 pages of mindless bullshit!'

'I must protest professor!!!'

One of the other professors shouted and rose to his feet while Professor Mandrake stood there seething, his face turning red.

'Let me tell you something you vulgar cretin!'

Axl stood there grinning sadistically as usual with his hands behind his back.

'You are nothing more than a worthless piece of scum! I have no idea what you are doing in attendance here today, but I can immediately tell that you are not qualified as a human being! To even dream of being a involved in the fine and complex arts of psychology!'

Axl's grin faded slightly as he sighed and took a deep breath.

'Wrong again my misguided fool. I already am involved. Involved in manipulation that is, and whether you choose to adopt and come to terms with the fact that your precious little art of psychology helped spawn it or not, it is true nonetheless.'

'SECURITY!!!'

One of the other professors ran out f the room and within a matter of seconds three security guards came storming into the auditorium and followed the Professors finger which was pointing straight at Axl.

'Escort this man from this institute at once!'

Axl rolled his eyes as the guards came and escorted him swiftly away from the room, two of them gripping him by his shoulders, the other leading the way in front. However Axl quickly turned an looked at the professor.

'Professor .......... you should be careful. You may wake up one day and find that you've become a puppet, and the puppet master may just be the man who stands before you right now ..........me!'

'GET HIM OUT OF HERE!!!!!'

With that the guards used even more force escorting him away from the building.

Ax's sole purpose for attending that lecture was to let it be known that the art of manipulation did exist. The best way to do that was to tell the one man who doubted it the most bluntly to his face. He felt he did that, however he would have to put everything else on hold, manipulation wise until he took care of that pesky damsel Angel Summers and that wannabe Judas Iscariot Matt Blitz. There time would come this Wednesday. He could feel it.
