The scene fades in to reveal a slim, bejeweled, and over-tanned ladies hand holding up a facsimile sheet so the back of the paper itself so it dominates the shot...for a moment at least.

Throwing down the fax page in disgust, a woman comes into view. Perhaps incredibly attractive when she was younger, the still attractive, yet whethered, well-tanned, and well endowed brunette puffs angrily exhales smoke and smashes her cigarette into an ash tray.

The woman stands up, revealing herself to be wearing little more than a tank top, and a pair of black lace panties, giving us a full view of her. Her breasts are massive...too massive and firm looking to be real. He figure is shapely elsewhere, indicating despite her smoking habit, she keeps her body in good shape, because after all, her body is her meal ticket. Without it, she is just another has-been hussie, hoping to keep her man's dick from entering the next young and hungry whore who may have her eyes set on simply being the woman of attractive professional wrestler

That was not going to happen to Natalie Shea. No 20 year old, with real breasts and a tight snatch was going to steal her man. It wasn't only about love and commitment, but about investment.

That's right, investment.

Natatlie had invested the best 13 years of her life in Race Alexander. During these thirteen years, she could have been using here once youthful features and naturally large and firm breasts to be seducing a doctor or blowing a lawyer to get by. Instead, she spent her time on driving thousands of miles, working in countless small venues, and giving up her womanly fruits in low budget motels to him, so in her mind, she invested her youth, and in turn her future in him. She wouldn't let him fail.

13 years hadn't lessened her drive or resolve. She he had what it took to make it to the big time, and when he did, she would be there with him.

But now this...

'Race, how in the HELL did this come about,' said Natalie, lighting another cigarette. 'You should be headlining the opening card, not entrenched in the midcard, battling in secondary title qualifiers, against nobodies like...what the hell is his name?'

Natalie casts her glance across the room, to where longtime indy legend, her investment, Race Alexander, was reading the sports page. Without lowering the page, the handsome Alexander, brushed his blond locks aside.

'I think his name is Kazzualty,' quips Alexander. 'Aptly named at that, because he is sure to be one.'

Natalie frowns.

'Cut the macho bravado,' she scolds. 'You know this is bullshit. YOU should be at the top of the card. YOU should be getting the phone calls for preference. YOU are the star of this promotion.

Race lowers his paper.

'Don't worry babe, I got it covered. We've been treated like shit before, but in the end, I always rise to the top. You know that.'

Natalie nervously puffs on her cigarette amd scowls.

'I am not willing to settle for the top of oblivion or public access cable,' she says, exhaling smoke out her nose. 'We need to be noticed. We need to headline. We need to be in the spotlight. If we aren't, we might as well do all the indy dates we can, or go back to Japan, if its just about money.'

Natalie gets up from her chair and saunters over to Race, plopping down on his lap.

'Now its about the future, baby and our security,' she says, while straddling him and rubbing his shoulders. 'We need to look good. You need to shine every chance you can get. You need time on camera. You need to get your heat. You need time on the stick.'

Race nods as Natalie continues.

'We can not let anyone or anything hold us back from the main goal. We can...'

Suddenly Natalie's stops midsentence and lets out a gasp, as sly smile crosses Race's face.

'If the crotch of your panties can't hold me back what makes you think some huckleberry booker can?' Race queries, as Natalie lets out another gasp, followed by a slight groan...as the scene fades to black.
