Servant Problems
Written: 6/12/02

*Ding-a-dlong dong ding dune*

The sound of the musical doorbell rings throughout Luke Black’s mansion. One of his servants unlocks and opens the double doors. There stands Luke Black on his porch.

“Why the heck where the doors locked?” Yells an enraged Luke Black, “Didn’t I tell you to keep them unlocked?”

“It wasn’t me, sir.” Apologizes the servant; “It was…your dog.”

Luke pushes his way inside and the servant shuts the door behind him.

“I don’t have a dog! How stupid are you?”

“I’m so—“

“No!” Luke yells cutting the servant off, “You’re fired!”

“But—“

“Get out!”

The servant’s head hangs low as he begins to leave the mansion, but is stopped.

“No, I have a better job for you.” Luke tells the servant, “You’re going to be my janitor.”

“But I already did janitor stuff, not to mention answering the phone and door, and serving you your food.” Says the servant.

“Nope. I fired you, remember? Now get to cleaning my toilets, if you know what’s good for you!”

“Yes, sir…”

The newly hired janitor hurries off as Luke snickers to himself.

“I just love…” Luke pauses for a second to think, then continues, “me!” He says raising his arms extravagantly with a huge grin on his face.

Luke turns around to admire a portrait of him self on a wall.

“I’m so beautiful!” Luke thinks to himself.

Luke begins to walk to the kitchen when the phone rings.

*Ring-a-ling! Ring-a-ling! *

Luke stops and watches the phone ring.

*Ring-a-ling! Ring-a-ling! *

He turns to look for his servants to answer the phone. No one is coming, nor has anyone answered the phone elsewhere in the mansion.

“Where are my servants?” Luke questions himself; “Must I do everything my self?”

Luke reaches into his pocket and pulls out a cell phone. He dials a number to one of his servants that is elsewhere in his mansion.

“Hello?” We hear the servant on the phone ask.

“Why haven’t you answered the phone?” Luke yells at the servant.

“I didn’t know it was ringing. I just picked it up.”

“Not your dang cell phone! The phone to the mansion, darn it!”

“Oh! I’m sorry, sir. I’ll answer it right away. I just—“

Luke hangs up his cell phone and puts it back in his pocket. He turns to watch the phone stop ringing, which is does.

“Finally!” Luke says out loud.

A servant runs down the stairs and over to Luke.

“Sir…the phone is…for you. It’s…your agent.” Says the out of breath servant.

“Oh, ok.” Says Luke turning to pick up the phone, but he then remembers that he doesn’t have an agent, “I don’t have an agent you moron!”

“This man claims to be your agent, sir.” The servant tells Luke after regaining his breath.

“Tell him that he’s an moron, just like you, and to never call back here!”

“Yes, sir! Right away, sir! I’ll get right on that, sir!”

“Some time today!”

The servant picks up the phone behind Luke and tells the man on the phone exactly what Luke told him to say.

Luke walks into his kitchen. His cook is standing there with his arms behind his back.

“Hey, cook!” Luke says to his cook, “Make me a sandwich.”

“What kind would you like, sir?” Asks his cook.

“Do I need to tell you every time? It’s always the same! Ham on white bread!”

“Yes, sir. I just thought that you might want something different this time.”

“No…now get to making it!”

The cook walks over to the refrigerator and takes out ham and bread. Luke starts to hum a little song while the cook is making his sandwich. The cook walks over to Luke with his finished sandwich. Luke takes and takes a bite of it.

“Hmmm…This is the best! You are the best!” Complements Luke to his cook.

“You say that every time.” Says the cook.

“It’s true. I love it.”

Luke walks over to an intercom on the side of the wall. He pushes the button.

“I want all my staff to meat me in the kitchen!” Luke says into the intercom.

Soon enough servant after servant show up until all of them there. There are about ten: three maids, four servants and three janitors.

“See this cook behind me?” Luke says pointing to his cook behind him, “He is the best darn cook ever! If he had any of your jobs he could do it ten times better! Now get back to work!”

“F*ck that! He is full of sh*t! He’s just sucking up to you! I see brown all over his damn nose!” yells out one of the servants.

“Do my ears deceive me? Do I hear fowl, evil language in my mansion?” Questions Luke, “You’re freaking fired! Get the heck out of here!”

“F*ck! Sh*t! You’re a bitch!” Replies the angry the servant as he leaves.

Luke rushes up behind the servant and picks up vase. He raises the vase high above the servant’s head and smashes it over him. The servant falls to the ground. Luke turns the servant over on his back and beats him with his fists. The servant begins to bleed from his mouth.

“Mother f*cker! You’re a little bastard! If I hear one more damn swear word from your hellish mouth I’ll f*cking kill you!” Screams the enraged Luke allowing swear words to pass through his lips, “I need some soap!”

A janitor walks over and hands Luke a bar of soap. He shoves the soap in the servant’s mouth. Luke picks up the servant and tosses him out of his mansion. He turns around to a shocked group of servants.

“Oops! Did I swear?” Questions Luke sarcastically, “Janitor! Clean up this mess!”

The janitors rush over and hurriedly clean up the broken vase.

“Ah…That’s more like it!” Luke says relaxed.

Luke turns and walks upstairs.
