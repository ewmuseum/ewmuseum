Victims ... aren't we all? Pt 2
Written: 5/8/02

'Your one of a damn kind man!!!'

His words burst out with hysterical laughter as he pointed at Axl, tears almost running down his eyes from the sheer energetic bellows of laughter he was letting out.

Axl nodded and smiled as he sat down at the upper class bar with Edward Colfax. Axl stood out thoroughly, but it made no difference now or to him. He had told Edward Colfax that he used to attend the same school as him. Edward was unsure of this but after Axl bought him a few rounds of double scotch he suddenly 'remembered' Axl very well.

As of right now Edward was drunk as a skunk and inherited beyond belief. He was laughing hysterically at everything Axl would say, even how he thought he was losing hair rapidly and thought he spoke like a self centred stuck up prick.

'Another double scotch for the gentleman please bartended'

The bartender nodded while Edward tried to pull a straight face and spurted 'Than-Thank you!' through breaths that stunk of Cuban cigars and caviar, followed by droplets of spit shooting from his mouth over the bar.

The bartender eventually came back with the double scotch for Edward, and placed it on a coaster with the bars logo. Edward smiled caringly at it like it was a long lost lover, before knocking it back carelessly, with most of the drink flowing out and around his moth and dripping onto his sweater. He then slammed it back down onto the table with authority and wiped his mouth with his sleeve.

'So Andrew.....'

'It's Axl'

Edward stopped, nodded with his eyes closed and opened them pointing his finger at Axl clumsily.

'Right you are, right you are Ax...Ax...'

'AXL'

'YES! Axl. So Axl, what business are you in these days. I'm in stocks myself as I already told you. Got a big fat inheritance to my name as well when mother and father decide to pass the toll booth up in the sky.'

'Not by the end of tonight you won't'

'Excuse me?'

'Nothing. Anyway, I'm a professional wrestler Edward'

There was a long pause between the two before Edward broke the silence with another booming hysterical laughter that rang through the air in the upper class bar. Heads turned and expressed shocked and un-amused glances, Axl didn't know whether they were because of Edwards inappropriate laughter or Axls' s dress sense.

'No really Axl, all jokes aside. What line of business are you in?'

'Well really Edward ... I really am a professional wrestler but I am also an underground hit man who uses his experience of psychology and who to abuse the human mind to kill the people I have contracts on. By the way, tonight your lucky night Edward, because tonight I'm gonna kill you'

Axl thought Edward was going to piss his pants from the way he was laughing. His eyes were now filled with tears and his face was a bright red.

Axl slowly helped Edward off his stool, and began to lead him out of the bar slowly with one of Edwards arms draped over his shoulder.

'W-w0whwre are we going Alan?'

'We're going to go for a little walk, get you some fresh air and sober you up'

'Right you are Alex!'

* * * * * * * * * * * * *

The night air was calm and cool, and glided across their faces. It also actually did seem to sober Edward up because now he was walking on his own two feet by himself.

'Walking Alex, is like riding a bike... you never forg-'

Edward then stumbled over ..... well nothing, just his own damn feet. This to no surprise led huge bellows of laughter from the wealthy young man. Axl merely grinned. He had been silent for most of their walk, that was because he was listening out for something particular. Then he heard it!

'What's that noise?'

Edward asked while still giggling slightly. Looking up into the sky trying to find where the noise was coming from.

'That is the sound of an eagle my dear Edward'

Axl said the comment just as he spotted Roland flying through the air and perching himself on the roof of a somewhat derelict building about 200 yards away from where they currently were.

Axl had trained Roland well. Tonight Axl had instructed Roland as to what to look for. A victim.

'Come on Edward, lets follow it'

'Uhhh, I'm not sure we should, this is a rough area of the city, I make it an objective not to venture down here'

'Come on Edward!'

Axl grabbed Edward with his arm and hoisted him up forcefully, with Edward eventually following, still hints of laughter running through his head.

* * * * * * * * *

Soon the two were at the derelict decaying building. Edward proceeded with caution while Axl simply walked ahead with confidence, tugging Edward to follow him ever now and then. Roland sat perched tall and proud upon the rooftop of the building, looking down at the.

'Spare change please Sir?'

The two of them stopped.

'Well done Roland. Well done!'

Axl beamed as he said the comment and heard the sound of the beggar

'What was that sound Alex?'

'I don't know Edward, lets go and see, it came from the building'

'Oh I don't know....'

'Oh but wait, before we do'

Axl then thrust Edward in close to him and wrapped his arms around his neck.

'WHAT IN THE BLAZES!!!'

'Shhhhhh now, listen... LISTEN!'

Soon Edward stopped struggling as Axl applied his grip tighter. Then ever so quietly and slowly Axl whispered. Whispered into his ear, manipulating his mind , manipulating him. Axl slowly dragged Edward into the darkness as he did so to avoid any attention, the area was scarce and dead anyway though. Then ever predictably .... Edward became manipulated. Axl was so precise and efficient in doing it these days it could be executed at a rapid rate. Edward was now nothing more than a puppet to the puppet master Axl!

* * * * * * * * * * * * * * * ** * *

'Spare change please sir?'

Edward followed the voice with no more caution in his pace. The voice was coming from somewhere behind a small derelict wall no more than 6 foot high. Edward continued to walk towards it, his eyes not blinking while Axl followed calmly. The puppet master controlling and leading his puppet onward.

'Spare change please sir?'

The voice was ragged and old, full of aching but hope. Axl admired that. He had respect for that. Here was a male, from what he guessed by the voice, who was living on the streets in what could only be described as a shit hole. Yet he still didn't give up, and he still bared that ever so small faint shining glimmer of hope and prosperity in what he must think is a never ending meaningless life. Yes, Axl admired and respected and admired that a lot.

Soon they turned the corner to where the small derelict destroyed deceased wall once stood. The darkness of the night was thicker in here. Deadly silent, and the air was thin and sharp. It was almost painful to inhale. Axl was unsure but he almost felt like eh could still taste the lingering dust particles of the decayed buildings, almost like they were only destroyed a short time ago, almost like they were destroyed for this special occasion tonight. He liked the idea of that thought.

The man who was the owner of the pleading voice and the bearer of the faint glimmer of hope he poured into it sat there with a small cotton blanket wrapped around him. His teeth chattered slowly in the night.

However ........ Edward saw things differently. He was manipulated .... he was just a puppet.

* * * * * * * * *

Edward looked at the man. His bald head was almost certainly too large for his body, and the clear fact that he was bald only contributed to the fact that he could pass as Humpty Dumpty , the man in the nursery rhyme.

He looked up at Edward, his eyes scarce and relaxed, yet menacing somewhat. He bared a broad grin across his face as he looked at the wealthy man.

'Spare change please sir?'

Edward ignored the kind request from the beggar, and knelt down closer to the man. The man followed him down and his broad smile didn't wither an inch. His eyes met Edwards and Edward suddenly noticed something strangely peculiar about the mans head.

'What is that?'

Axl knelt down for closer inspection as Edward asked him, and then slowly nodded and smiled.

'Some sort of black line on the top of his head I'm assuming'

Edward looked up at Axl as he stood back to a full vertical base, and then turned his attention back to the beggar.

'What's that mark on your forehead Sir?'

Edward suddenly felt strange addressing someone of a much lower class than he himself as Sir. He suddenly remembered he had no recollection of anything that happened earlier that night. How did he get here? However all questions were banished out of his head as the beggar replied to Edward in the same tone of voice which he had maintained all night since they discovered him.

'Spare change please sir!'

'Dammit!'

Edward rose to his feet and went to lunge at the man out of pure frustration and anger. However Axl spotted the attack boiling before Edward even considered doing it, and grabbed Edward around the wrist as he rose and lunged at the man.

'Easy Edward, easy, no need to unleash violence on out poor helpless gentleman here'

'I demand an answer from you, you worthless scum sucking peasant!'

'.......spare change please sir?'

'............ARGGGHHHH.... DIE!!!!!'

Edwards attempt at breaking from Axl's grip around his waist were hopeless. Axl was about an inch taller than him and at least 70 pounds heavier. So Axl merely rolled his eyes and he tried to squirm out of it. However eventually Edward stopped squirming, probably out of exhaustion, and Axl let him go and watched as Edward dropped to his knees breathing deeply.

'Spare change please sir?'

This time Edward only had the energy to look at him and flip him the bird. Yet upon doing so, he noticed that black line across his head. The man maintained his ever present menacing grin as Edward leaned in closer for a further more detailed inspection. He eventually stood up right and cowered over the man and looked at the thick black line across the mans head. Then slowly he touched the top of his head. He pulled back instantly clutching his index finger as he did so.

'Good grief!'

'What?'

'The top of his head, it's absolutely freezing. I mean that must be at below sub zero temperatures!'

Axl merely grinned and urged Edward to take another look. This time Edward protested slightly, but the temptation of seeing what that thick black line was eventually and predictably overcame his hesitation. Edward slowly brought some gloves out of his long jacket, and placed then on quickly using his teeth to hold each one as he placed them on his cold now trembling fingers.

He edged closer to the man again, and ran his index finger across his forehead. It was still deathly cold even through the gloves Edward was wearing, but he could feel the mans head was incredibly smooth as he slid his index finger across it. All the time the man bared the same grin. Then eventually Edward slid his index finger across the thick black line on the top of his forehead.

'Sweet Jesus Mary and Joseph!'

Axl came in closer, not surprised at what Edward was seeing at all. After all, he was the puppet master. He was controlling that his puppet Edward would do ... and see. Edward escaped his manipulative grasp when he lunged at the man and when the temperature of his head shocked Edward, but after it all ceased Edward fell back into the grasp.

'What is it Edward?'

'It's his head Alex!'

'What about it?'

Edward gestured for Axl to come closer and ran his fingers across the thick black line on his forehead. Sure enough it was something to be alarmed over. It was a thick groove, right at the top his head, and from closer inspection it was extremely deep.

'Yes, most peculiar'

Axl said the comment nonchalantly. It was what he was making Edward see after all. Edward remained there marvelling at the groove. It was warm around it, which alarmed Edward even more. This made him reach and pull the blanket off the man and feel under the torn and tattered shirt he was wearing. The mans skin was not as cold as his forehead, but cold. The man all the time, as always it seemed, maintained his menacing grin as Edward checked for his heartbeat. A he did, his heart almost stopped.

'Good grief! He's alive! His heart is beating!'

'Spare change please sir!'

'AAAAGHHHHHHHH!!!'

Edward jumped and flew back on his rear end as the man startled him, to say the least. Axl chuckled lightly, but not loud enough for Edward to hear, he didn't want him to get suspicious. However Edward was back to his feet almost instantly. This man had captured his attention with a force, a force almost as strong as the manipulative one Axl had on him.

'Maybe you should .... put some money in the groove?'

Edward looked at Axl with a confused look, and almost as a cue, the mans head titled sideways slightly, and the clear sound of change rattling in his head rang through the night as he did so.

'Oh this is beyond belief!'

'Maybe he's just a machine Edward. Used in some old arcade, dropped off her a while ago.'

Edward pondered over the thought for some time, it when thought of logically it did seem like a reasonable notion. Maybe the heartbeat was nothing more than a mechanical cog working. It would explain a lot of things indeed. Edward then soon found himself picking a 50 cense coin out of his pocket and edging close to the man. He titled his head back to an upright position, and then stood over him and dropped the coin down into the groove. There was a pause, before an inevitable CLINK as the coin hit and mixed with all the other coins down there. The beggar looked up and his smile grew so big it almost touched the corners of his eyes.

'Thank you kindly sir'

Edward smiled with relieved yet sick satisfaction. So did Axl, because he knew what was running through his head.

Edward, without talking or consulting Axl then grabbed a nearby brick. It was heavy and large, and Edward had to carry it with two hands, but he was able to walk with it reasonably well. H edged closer to the beggar smiling demonically, the beggar merely maintaining his broad menacing grin.

Edward then lifted the brick high above his head while Axl watched on. Edward then brought the brick crashing down into the head of the man. It made a sickening crack. Edward lifted it up again, and brought it crashing down again, this time coins flying everywhere. Edward then again brought the brick crashing down upon the mans head on last final time with all the strength he could muster.

Axl then watched with amusement as Edward ran around picking up the coins and stuffing them into his pockets, giggling like a small schoolgirl. It was strange to see a wealthy man like him run around picking up coins, but Axl had dearly hoped he would. Then when Edward was done, the two ran away from the derelict building as they heard sirens, Edward still laughing hysterically. As they did Axl signalled for Roland to follow them, which he responded to immediately.

There it was, Axls's manipulative powers in all their glory. Edward would realise how deadly they were when he woke up tomorrow morning.
