Arena Appearance
Written: 6/25/02

The scene opens at a Canuck Heat Zone House Show Arena in Winnipeg Arena in Winnipeg Manitoba, Canada. In the ring, two house show wrestlers are facing each other in a one-on-one hardcore match. One is a medium height wrestler, a powerhouse kind, while the other one is short, a luchadore. The bell rings to start the match. Ding! Ding! Ding! The two men circle around each other. Then, they lock up. The powerhouse wrestler whips the lucahdore into the ropes, and gives him a Clothesline from Hell when he comes back. The Powerhouse picks the luchadore up and gives him a big back drop. He covers him. 1-2-kickout. The luchadore and powerhouse get back up and the luchadore hits a standing hurricanrana onto the powerhouse. The powerhouse wrestler gets back up and hits another hurricanrana.


The luchadore runs into the ropes and does a front flip senton onto the powerhouse wrestler on the mat. The luchadore picks up the powerhouse wrestler. He whips him into the turnbuckle. He sits him down on the top turnbuckle. The luchadore stands in front of him, going for a hurricanrana, but the powerhouse wrestler holds on and pulls him back up onto his shoulders, stands up on the top rope, and jumps off the top, hitting a Super Sit-down Powerbomb to the luchadore. The powerhouse wrestler goes for the pin, but he pulls his shoulders off the mat after the count of 2. The powerhouse wrestler picks up the luchadore and plants him with an Implant DDT. The powerhouse wrestler goes to the outside and looks under the ring. He pulls out a steel chair, a table, and a trash container. He rolls the steel chair and trash container into the ring, and sets the table up on the outside floor, right next to the ring apron.


The powerhouse wrestler rolls back into the ring. The luchadore suddenly springs to his feet and grabs the steel chair, and smacks the powerhouse wrestler in the head with it, knocking him down. The powerhouse wrestler gets back up, only to be knocked down again with a shot to the skull with a steel chair. The luchadore sets the chair up in the center of the ring, and whips the powerhouse wrestler into the turnbuckle. The luchadore runs at the powerhouse wrestler and jumps onto the chair with one leg and leaps off, going for a hurricanrana, but the powerhouse wrestler reverses it and Powerbombs the luchadore onto the set-up chair. The powerhouse wrestler gets back up and picks up the luchadore wrestler. He signals for the end of the match as he flips the luchadore wrestler upsidedown in front of him. He then jumps up into the air and drives the luchadore's head into the mat with his finishing maneuver, the Tombstone Piledriver. He goes for the pin. The referee slides down to count the pinfall. 1-2-suddenly, the lights go out, and the ref stops the count.


Smoke begins to fill the entranceway as red strobe lights cut on all around the arena. As 'My Plague' by Slipknot hits, flames explode out of the entranceway as the fans all erupt in roars of cheers. The powerhouse wrestler stands up, wondering what's going on, and who's coming out. The luchadore wrestler starts to stir, and tries to make it to his feet as well. The strobe lights cut off, and the lights cut back on, and there's Kazzualty standing behind the two wrestlers, watching them as they look at the entranceway, having no clue of what's about to happen to them! The luchadore turns around first and is caught, picked up, and slammed down to the mat with a Rock Bottom. The powerhouse wrestler turns around and is decked in the chin with the Kazz-Kick. Kazzualty picks up the luchadore wrestler and sticks his head between his legs. He lifts him up into the air, and slams him down with a Jacknife Powerbomb. He picks up the powerhouse wrestler and kicks him in the gut. He hooks his arms up, and plants him flat on his face with the Death Drop onto the steel chair.


The luchadore gets back up, and Kazzualty whips him into the ropes. When he comes back, Kazzualty bounces off the ropes on the opposite side of the ring, charges at him, and takes him down with a Battle Scar. Kazzualty picks up the trash container and lays it down on its side in the center of the ring. He grabs the lifeless luchadore by the throat on the mat, and pulls him to his feet. Kazzualty then lifts him high into the air and holds him up in the air with one arm for a few seconds, then, he puts his left hand against his back, and falls forward, slamming the luchadore flat on his back onto the trash container with the Death Toll. The trash container crumbles beneath the luchadore from the force emitted from Kazzualty. The luchadore lies there, motionless. Kazzualty picks him up, and tosses him over the top rope to the outside.


He walks over to the powerhouse wrestler and picks him up. Blood drips down the face of the powerhouse wrestler, from where he was busted wide open with the Death Drop onto the steel chair a few moments ago. Kazzualty sticks the powerhouse wrestler's head underneath his left arm. Kazzualty sticks his right arm out to the side, then quickly spins around and grabs the wrestler's neck with his right arm, and drops him down on his stomach and neck with a Twist of Fate. Kazzualty picks up the powerhouse wrestler and latches onto his throat with his right hand. He wraps his hand around the powerhouse wrestler's neck, and stares into his eyes. He lifts him high into the air and holds him there for a few seconds with one arm while walking towards the edge of the ring. Then, he sticks his left hand back up against his back, and slams him over the top rope through the table set up on the outside. The table shatters to hundreds of pieces, like it wasn't even there. A sickening 'Thud' is heard from the powerhouse wrestler's body bouncing off the outside floor.


The fans cheer their heads off, showing their appriciation for the little 'Show' Kazzualty has just done for them. Kazzualty walks over to the ring announcer and takes his microphone from him. Then, he raises it slowly up to his mouth to speak.


Kazzualty: Thank you Winnipeg. Thank you. What a warm reception. Now, that display of athletecism that I just showed you great people, is nothing compared to what I can really do. Now, if I wanted to, I could have used every move in the book on these poor, worthless jobbers, and they wouldn't have done anything about it, except cry and whine and bitch and moan and beg, beg for mercy, and beg for me to stop. Which I wouldn't have. I just would have kept on annihilating their pathetic carcasses. Now, let me get down to what I really came out here to do. What I just did to these jobbers, was to merely get them out of the ring so that I could speak.


The fans cheer a little, then settle back down so that Kazzualty can continue speaking.


Kazzualty: Alright now. Knowing that loser of an opponent of mine, Race Alexander, when he sees this promo, he's gonna respond to it by saying stupid crap like, 'Hey, beating up those jobbers don't show much of anything. I can do that. They're nothing compared to me,' or something like that. Well, I know it doesn't show much of anything. Sure, it's fun for me, and it sucks to be the jobbers, but I'm not trying intimidate anyone with that, except those jobbers. But I guarantee you those two losers will run their asses away from me next time they see me. Now, Tomarrow, I must face Race Alexander one-on-one in the ring to determine who will go into Pool A for the National Title Tournament. Well, I'll be the first to tell you, I my friends, will be that man to go into Pool A. I will be the first man to enter Pool A. I can guarantee you that.


The fans cheer in response to Kazzualty's words. They then die down once again so that Kazzualty can speak again.


Kazzualty: Now, I saw both of Race's promos that he cut, and frankly, I wasn't impressed. First, he's doing absolutely nothing, but listen to some bitch of a girl complain about you not being higher in the card. To me, it doesn't really matter. Yeah, I think I should be higher in the card as well, but, wherever I am put, is where I'm put. You sit there and say that I will be a 'Casualty', much like my name. Not true my friend. You see, although my name is that, just spelled differently, doesn't mean that's what I am, or become. Oh no Race, I have that name, because I am famous in different federations for making people them. Casualties that is. Almost everyone I face in the ring feel my wrath, and there's nothing they can do about it. Race, what you said about one of your cousins banging your mom, is something I didn't need to know. That's personal business, and frankly, nobody wants to hear about one of your cousins f@#king your ugly ass mom. So please, keep that s#%t to yourself.


The fans begin to chant 'Kazz-ual-ty! Kazz-ual-ty! Kazz-ual-ty!' over and over again. Kazzualty smiles and chuckles, and nods his head to the fans, thanking them. He then speaks back into the microphone.


Kazzualty: Thank you fans, thank you. You're too kind. Now, it seems to me that Mr. Alexander doesn't seem to be too concentrated on this match. Hell, that's all I can think about right now. Race is gonna say something like, 'Of course he's been concentrating on it, because he's wondering how I'm going to kick his butt in the ring. Blah Blah Blah Blah Blah!' Race, nobody gives a damn about what you have to say Race. If I were you, I'd take my eyes off that bitchy hooker's tits for a day or two, so that you can concentrate and think of how you want to be destroyed by me, because that's exactly what I'm going to do to you. I'm going to run your lousy ass straight into the ground, and there's not a damn thing you can do about it. Race you seem to doubt my abilities in the ring. Yeah, so I have a wide arsenal of moves, which is a hell of a lot better than your arsenal. You have what, 6 or 7 different moves or so. That's just sorry. The reason I have all these moves in my arsenal, is because I train, I practice these moves out, and I use them against people, and they are very effective and very devastating.


The fans begin to cheer once again, making Kazzualty stop speaking again. Kazzualty laughs, and tries to calm the crowd down, but they won't have it. They continue to chant Kazzualty's name, over and over again. Finally, after about 3 minutes of chanting, the fans finally die down and allow Kazzualty to finish speaking.


Kazzualty: Race, I will tell you this, out of that arsenal, I will use a lot of moves against you. I don't know whether I should try to make you tap out, or just knock your ass out, plain and simple. These moves you say I won't do to you, I may just pull them out of my bag and put you in them without you even knowing. Maybe after the ass kicking I'm going to give you tomorrow night at Wednesday Night Wild, maybe it will knock some sense into your stupid ass, because trust me, you really need some. Race, there shall be no E-Racers in this match between you and me. Because there won't be time for any, and you won't be awake to put me in it, because I'm going to knock your ass down with the Death Drop, pick you up, and knock you back down on your ass with the Kazz-Kick. I may pin you with that, I may not. You never know. Hell, I may even pick you back up and latch onto your throat, and choke you. Lift you high into the air, hold you there for a few seconds so that you can't breathe, and then slam you down flat on your back against the canvas, making you the first ever CHZ Television Wrestler to feel the wrath, of the Death Toll!


Kazzualty drops the microphone as the fans scream and cheer again. His music, 'My Plague' by Slipknot, hits again. The fans continue to cheer as Kazzualty climbs out of the ring and slowly makes his way up the ramp. He taunts to the fans at the top of the ramp, then continues on and disappears behind the curtains leading to the backstage area. The camera then slowly fades to black. Scene ends.
