One Bad Night!
Written: 09/03/02

==Begin Transmission ==

Champions are not made in gyms. Champions are made from something they have deep inside them. A desire, a dream, a vision. They have to have last-minute stamina, they have to be a little faster, and they have to have the skill and the will. But the will must be stronger than the skill. Hollywood Assassins both have the will to get the skill, the correct attitude is very positive to have. Why think of losing, when you can think of winning? A question people have to ask their selves, losing must not be in your mind, think positive, then the results will be positive.

Midnight is near and the room is rocking. Orlando Florida has streets full of tourists and locals, rather usual for a Holiday. Main Street is rocking with drunks, as the hottest bar in Orlando has a line-up that lasts nearly a block. All the celebrities and V.I.P people would park right up front, and automadically get on the other side of the red rope. Only a few of them tonight. Like “ Ben Stiller, Viktor Kozlov and David Cone.”

And on this day Robbie McMasters and Chris Starr have a visit to the club, they are not celebrities yet, but in fact they are local heroes to these people, so they also park out front. Robbie coming from a rich family has a new BMW, so automadically people were impressed. All the locals applaud the men for getting into the Canuck Heat Zone, the two were shocked, they did not think they would get greeting until they get into a bigger federation, but they enjoyed the applause. They automadically went inside the bar. The place was full, the abr had two visible seats available so the two took their seats there. They ordered their drinks from the bar, and swung the bar seats around and scoped the women with structure. Robbie and Chris were the kind of people who enjoy the one nightstands, still have not grown up, but they always say, “If you still have it, use it!”

The two were often approached by women that were interested, but the two were very picky, so they would as nice as they could, then send them in the opposite direction. But every time they were at the bar, they would always find the girl of the night. This girl would need to be higher then all the rest, even better then the previous ones they found at the bar, so it was hard, but they always found, that special one night stand.

Only selected songs they would go out on the dance floor and kick it, most times Robbie got the hottest girl in the club, and Chris Starr being the second, for obvious reasons. Many famous people pick this particular bar because of its special features, like a car looking like it’s smashed through the wall, the bar walls and roofs are all made of glass, which is also a great affect. Finally around 2:30am Robbie and Chris found their women, and Robbie usually takes his partner home, and Chris to a hotel, well often they rotate.

==The Next Morning ==

The usual routine for the guy who has the girl in the hotel, gives her number, and then says he will cll and throws her money for a cab. The one that takes the girl to their house has the tough job. They have to break it right down for them that they were just looking for a good time, which usually leaves the girl smacking either Robbie or Chris, and storming out of the room. But on this particular morning, Robbie did not plan to send her packing, he actually felt a strong connection between her and him. But when he woke up she was gone, it’s like he was used this was very shocking to him. He finally felt like he was on the opposite end, and it just made him feel more attracted to her. But now that she had gone, there was nothing he could do now that she was gone. Usually around twelve, the one who was at the hotel, arrives, and they talk about their night, and how the morning went with sending the girl off.

When Chris arrived home, Robbie is usually on the leather sofa, relaxing with a victory cigar. But on this day, Chris notices Robbie not on the sofa, but yet still in bed. Chris is suspicious but just ignores, and settles to the couch by himself, watching a movie. Just before the movie was ending, Robbie enters the room, all showered up, with him gym bag in his hand.

“ Hey Robbie, where the hell have you been? We have not even talked about our nights yet! What up? She take it bad?”

Chris smirking at Robbie when he says this, expecting a great story on how he got rid of her. But Robbie does not smile back, in fact he just ignores, and continues searching through the shelves looking for a power bar.

“ Robbie would you just stop, what wrong? What happened with that girl last night?”

“ Her name was Sara!”

Chris stopping what he was doing, and looking at Robbie with disgust. As he replies.

“ The one night stand has a name now? What the hell happened last night?”

Robbie again ignoring, as he puts his running shoes on, and picks up his gym bag”

“ Well you coming? I don’t feel like talking about last night today, I just feel like blowing off a little steam, and we have a match tomorrow, so lets go!”

Within thirty second Chris is in his gym clothes, and they are off to their local gym, A great five-star gym.
On this day, Robbie was fast paced in everything, driving, walking, dressing and lifting weights. Chris was not able to keep up with Robbie like usual, well he was never as quick as him, but usually within the same ballpark. After a good four hours of hard working out, Chris and Robbie head back home, and not like the usual night, having a few drinks. Robbie just hit the bed, while Chris had a few by himself, then went to bed a little depressed. A day they both wished did not happen.

But the question is, will they be able to re-group and pull off the win tomorrow for the titles. If Robbie keeps this mood up, I pity the opponent, he seems more locked on kicking the crap out of his opponent, he has the motivation, now hopefully all night stands will be like Sara.

==End==
