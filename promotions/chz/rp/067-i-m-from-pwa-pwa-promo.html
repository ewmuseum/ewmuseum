I'm From PWA (PWA Promo)
Written: 07.21.02

Our scene begins in the middle of a building as spectators are passing in every direction. Some by them selves, some with other people. As they continue to walk, Tyler Van Serio is noticed. The camera begins to follow TVS. TVS doesn't notice anything. He keeps on walking not knowing he is being followed. He stops and the cameraman bumps into him. Serio turns around and smile. He looks at the guy’s shirt (it says PTA: Primetime Wrestling Academy) and looks at the camera (also says PTA: Primetime Wrestling Academy). Serio just laughs.

'You know it's just funny,' Serio says to himself, 'I've always been followed by people, cameras, kids etc. But I've never been followed by some camera from Primetime Wrestling!' He begins to laugh.

'I never thought I would see this day when I was being followed by PTA. I thought I wasn't good enough. But I have found my place and I am ready for it! I found Primetime Wrestling Academy. It's an amazing place for wrestling although it has no titles or no pay per views. But I don't really mind, I'm not really a Title person. And not going for a title means no pay per view. But I'm used to it. It isn't that bad. But I know I can do anything here.'

Serio walks around for a little then looks back

'I was walking yesterday threw a park. I was also thinking that it would be so nice to actual begin wrestling like The Rock and Chris Jericho. I always wanted to make it big. So I decided to phone up Mr. Tyler Nelson. He's a real nice guy and all and said that I should try out at Primetime Wrestling Academy. I thought.... 'Hey! I will wrestle and make it big!' SO I came to the arena, signed a contract and badda bomb badda Bing! I didn't think it would be so easy.'

Serio keeps on walking right into a restaurant. He sits down.

'Come join me!' he says to the cameraman.

The cameraman sits down and Serio orders 2 Ice Teas.

'You know,' he says as he takes a sip of his drink, 'I think I can really do good here.'

'What do you mean?' asks the Cameraman.

'Well, I thought if I do real good here, I would be able to go far. I thought if I succeeded the necessary requirement, I would go for something new. Something bigger, better. I know that I am a beginner in the big feds but I have a lot of experience in wrestling. I fought against people all over the world. Chine, Italy, Germany etc. but I never thought that I would make it to PTA. I was watching 'Cops' a few days ago and noticed that those cops have to take a lot of punishment all threw their job. It's almost the same as wrestling, but wrestling is safer. You can't get shot or stuff like that. You know, pepper spray in the eyes and shit like that. But you can get very seriously injured and not wrestle, but my body is as fit as a... Can you think of anything?'

'No,' says the cameraman.

'Not many people can,' he says taking another drink.

'Can I get you anything?' asks the waiter, 'Because I have been standing here for about 5 minutes listening to you talk about PTA and wrestling and Cops. So, I have final...'

'Did I tell you the time I broke my arm?' he asks the cameraman ignoring the waiter.

'Uh, I don't think so....' he responds.

'Excuse me!' yells the waiter, 'But I would like to get your order and get back to my job!'

'Then go!' replies Serio signaling him to leave.

'Fine...' the waiter says and walks away.

'Anyways,' says Tyler, 'I broke my arm while wrestling against 5 other men. I got a chair shot so hard that it paralyzed my arm for a little while. I didn't know what to do so I let someone cover me.'

'Pretty serious,' the cameraman says finishing his Ice Tea.

'Ya, what ever,' Serio says.

The waiter comes, takes the money and TVS and the cameraman walk to a store. 'Stupify' by Disturbed is playing and Serio is smiling.

'This is my favorite song!' says Tyler, 'I love it!'

'Then make it your theme,' suggests the cameraman.'

'You know....' Serio thinks...

Serio walks into the store. He comes walking out with a cd.

'What cd?' asks the cameraman?

'What do you think?' says Serio puzzled, 'It's Disturbed!'

'Oh, I forgot...' the cameraman says.

Serio and the cameraman walk away as the scene fades to black Serio singing the lyrics to 'Stupify.' The both leave the place.

End
