A Trip To The Park
Written: 8/26/02

The scene fades in to a small park in a suburb of Montreal. It’s a bright sunny day; the temperature is a comfortable seventy-six degrees, not unbearably hot, not bone chilling cold, just perfect. Over on a playground area twenty or so small children are running and playing and laughing, having a great time. Then off in a grassy area a young couple is having a picnic, while an elderly couple is talking their afternoon walk. But we don’t care about that, our attention is directed over to a small green bench. The bench is isolated from the rest of the park. There is a man on the, just sitting there alone staring up at the sky. As we get closer to him it can be seen that it is Jake Schultz seated there all alone.

He is sitting there just thinking. Thinking about tagging with Matt Hunt. Thinking about his barbed wire table match with Matt Hunt for the CHZ World Title. He is thinking about everything that Matt Hunt and the rest of the Carnival of Carnage have put him through since the first Wild. He is trying to think of a way to keep his focus and not be blinded by revenge next week. He knows he will have every opportunity to attack Matt Hunt and make him suffer, the same way Hunt has made him suffer. He doesn’t want to attack him, he would love to just win this match, then get his pay back at Mind Games, but he’s not sure whether he can or not. It’s going to be tough. When he sees Hunt struggling to make the tag it’s going to be so tempting to turn his back and let Logan Cage and Xander Crave do what they please with him. It would be just as easy to hit him when he isn’t looking, even if it does cost him the match. But his record in CHZ so far is one win, one loss, and one draw, that’s not good enough for someone like Jake, someone who strives for perfection.

He has to cooperate with Hunt on Wild. It will only be for one night, and really only for maybe a half hour or so, not that long. Then next week he gets Hunt, one on one, for the World Title. Actually, he doesn’t even have to wait that long. He could always take Hunt out after the match is over. He can’t wait to take Hunt out; he’s been waiting for this since the CHZ first opened. Now, with the first pay per view coming up so soon the desire to make him suffer burns more deeply within him to the point he can barely control himself at the mention of the name Matt Hunt.

Jake slowly lowers his head and is now staring at the playground where the children are. He takes a deep breath, inhaling the fresh Canadian air, as he smiles slightly seeing the children having such a good time. He then remembers his own troubles and the slime slowly gives way to a look of despair. He sighs as he stands up from his seat on the small, green park bench. He stands up and stretches his arms out, as well as his legs, and unleashes a big yawn from deep within. He then picks up a small black bag that was next to him the whole time he was on the bench. He throws the bag over his shoulder and slowly starts down one of the cement paths that go through the entire park. He is looking around, taking in the beautiful scenery as he walks.

“Sometimes, I like to come here, well not here particularly, but somewhere peaceful like this to escape. Sometimes you just need to escape the chaos of the real world. This professional wrestling stuff is much harder than I ever dreamed it would be. A new city each week, constantly getting pounded and pummeled, only to show up next week for the same exact thing. It takes a tough person to do what we do. Not just anyone can be a wrestler; you have to really want to do it. This is where I began to question just how great Matt Hunt is. Sure, he’s beaten me into a bloody pulp before, but that was always from some kind of sneak attack or a three on one assault. He has never proved to me he is better than me in the ring. Any moron can run around and beat people with chairs and have all their buddies beat them up, wow Matt, real big accomplishment there. In the ring, what have you done? Well, we have had two matches. You won the first one because you cheated, as usual. Then the next time we met up, when you actually played fair, I gave you the beating of your life. Do you see a pattern here? When you can cheat you are one of the best there is. However, when you can’t have your friends interfere you are no more than the average jobber. And now your little circus freak friends are in the hospital due to their little accident; that just about takes you out of the match at Mind Games, doesn’t it?” says Jake. He then stops in the middle of the path and looks ahead at something in the woods. It’s a doe, a female deer. She is munching on some plants on the ground. She can feel Jake staring at her; she looks up; then takes off running back into the woods.

“See, you need things like this too keep you from going insane. And trust me Matt, me going insane is the very last thing you want. I’ve beaten you pretty badly before, but trust me, you haven’t seen a fraction of what I’m capable of yet. Wait until Mind Games when the World Title is on the line, an incentive like that raises everyone’s game, it’s just too bad for you that no matter how much you raise your game I will always be better than you. And being a barbed wire table match, that makes it even easier for me to cause you a monumental amount of pain and suffering. Oh, Matthew, Matthew, Matthew, I just can’t wait until Mind Games. I can’t wait until I see you bleed at my hands, as you’ve done to me. I can’t wait to see you in an inhumane amount of pain and agony, as you’ve done to me,” says Jake. He reaches the end of the cement path through the park and stops. He stops, takes another large breath of fresh air, and then walks out into the street.

“But I guess that will all have to wait a bit, won’t it? Yeah, because I have the pleasure of being on a tag team with you. This ought to be fun. Well, at least it will be a short match, seeing as you have the best wrestler in CHZ on your team, me. So we should make quick work of those bums, then I can get down to the important business, you,” says Jake. He then heads over to a big Ford Excursion parked across the street. As he walks across the street he pulls out his keys and unlocks his doors with his electronic key chain.

“Nice car huh? A lot better than that old van I had. The CHZ paychecks come in very handy. Oh, and Matt, all that stuff you said about worrying about everyone wanting a shot at you when you’re a title holder, you don’t need to worry yourself about that. Seeing as you aren’t going to be holding any titles anytime soon. Now me on the other hand, I may need to worry about that, since I am going to be the World Champion here soon. So Mat, just don’t screw up Wednesday, you’ll get your shot at me on Sunday, camache,” says Jake as he opens up the driver’s side door. He hops in the car and starts it up. “Girl All The Bad Guys Want” by Bowling For Soup starts to blast from the CD player when the car starts up. He closes the door, rolls down the windows, and speeds away. The scene slowly fades to black.
