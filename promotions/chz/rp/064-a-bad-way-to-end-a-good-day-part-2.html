A Bad Way To End A Good Day (Part 2)
Written: 7/01/01

[…We return where we last left Jake, in his apartment in Barberton, Ohio. He had just arrived home with his band mates when they heard a female voice shouting from a room in the back. Jake had just throw his keys onto to the floor and dashed across the living room and through an open door.]

[He runs into a small room with red carpet and red painted walls. The lights in the room are shut off, however, there are candles lit and sitting on a table near a bed. There is soft music coming from a CD player over in the corner of the room. There is a queen size bed sitting in the middle of the room with the top of it pushed up against the wall. On the bed there is a man and a woman. The man is on top of the woman, they are having sex.]

“What in the hell is going on here?” asks Jake as he comes bursting into the room.

[Both the man and the woman look up as Jake yells this at them. The man is off of the woman in a split second. The woman rolls over on to her stomach and then gets up off of the bed. She approaches Jake.]

“Please baby, it’s not what it looks like,” says the woman, now in tears, as she walks over to Jake. She is about five foot seven inches tall; she has long blonde hair and blue eyes. She stops in front of Jake and wraps her arms around him.

“Get your ass away from me!” screams Jake as he pushes the woman away from his. She stumbles backwards and falls onto the bed. About this same time the man whom she was having sex with has finished dressing himself and is trying to sneak out of the bedroom. Jake sees him right as he gets to the bedroom doorway. The man knows that Jake saw him so he makes a break for the front door.

“Chris, Cory, stop that bitch,” instructs Jake as the man darts out of the room. Out in the living room Chris and Cory both tackle the guy as he races for the front door. They pin him to the floor and hold him there until Jake walks over to them.

“You wait here bitch,” says Jake as he walks out of the bedroom towards where Cory and Chris have the man pinned to the floor. He walks over and looks down at the man who is being held down by them. The man is terrified of what is going to become of him; you can see it in his eyes.

“Stand up asshole,” says Jake as Chris and Cory get off of him. The man just lies there for a moment looking up at the three men.

“Are you deaf, I said stand up!” yells Jake at the man. The man pops up from the floor and stands facing Jake. He has short, curly brown hair and green eyes that are hidden behind a pair of large bifocals.

“What do you think you were doing in there?” yells Jake right in the man’s face. He is spitting all over the man as he screams at him.

“I..I’m sorry, I di…di…didn’t know that she was taken,” the man answers very timidly. He then closes his eyes and lowers his head as if he is expecting Jake to hit him. After a moment without Jake punching him the man peeks up at Jake who is still standing there staring at him.

“What the hell do you mean you didn’t know, did you not look around, I mean look at all the damn pictu…” Jake stops in mid-sentence. All of the pictures he is looking for are gone.

“Oh Jessica, get out here,” yells Jake back to the bedroom. The woman walks out into the living room covering herself with a sheet. She walks up to Jake and looks him very seductively as she begins speaking.

“Yes, baby, what do you need?” questions the woman as she runs her hands down Jake’s chest.

“Get your damn hands off of me you slut,” says Jake as he garbs her wrists and the pulls her hands off of his chest. She puts them down at her sides.

“Just where did all of our pictures go?” Jake asks the woman. She looks around obviously stalling for time to think of an excuse.

“I, umm, I took them down to clean them, the frames so dirty, you know,” says the woman. She then looks at Jake to see if he believes her.

“You liar, where are they?” asks Jake. The woman then points over to a desk sitting in the corner of the living room. Jake walks over to the desk and opens up one of the drawers and peers inside. The drawer is full to the top with pictures of Jake and the woman. He opens up a few other drawers; they too are over-flowing with pictures. Jake shuts all of the drawers and heads back to where Jessica and the man are standing.

“So you hid all of our pictures so this geek wouldn’t know we were together,” says Jake to Jessica. He then turns to the man.

“So, what’s your name?” Jake asks the man who was sleeping with his girlfriend.

“My name, oh, umm, Russell,” stammers the man. He then extends his hand to Jake. Jake just looks at him and then begins speaking again.

“I don’t want to shake your damn hand. Just because you didn’t know doesn’t mean I’m not pissed off at you,” says Jake as Russell pulls his hand away and puts it back in his pocket.

“But are we cool?” Russell asks Jake who has just turned around to face Jessica.

“Are we cool? Are we cool? Hell no we aren’t cool,” says Jake as he turns around to face the man again.

“You were just in there banging my girl, do you think I’m just going to forget about that?” Jake asks the man. The man has a stupid look on his face as he thinks about what Jake said.

“Well I just thought that…” Jake interrupts Russell in mid-sentence.

“Well I just thought, well I just thought,” says Jake mimicking the guy in a really whiny voice.

“Maybe you should just stop thinking then asshole,” says Jake as he stares at the man. He is now in the Russell’s face as he is talking to him.

“Hey, I don’t have to stay here and take this shit from you, I’m out,” says Russell as he turns around as heads for the door. He turns the knob and begins to open the door when suddenly his heads slams against the door, going right through the glass window in it.

“Damn Russ, you have a hard head,” says Jake as he stands there rubbing his knuckles as he looks at Russell lying on the floor. Jake then walks over to where Russell is lying and picks him up by his shirt collar.

“You see Russ, that’s where you’re wrong, you do have to stay here and take this shit,” says Jake as he drags a bloody Russell over to the couch and throws him down on it. Russell is bleeding all over the couch and himself. There are shards of glass sticking out all over his face. He is now begging Jake for his life.

“Please man, I didn’t know she had a boyfriend or I wouldn’t have done this, just please, don’t hurt me,” whimpers Russell as he bleeds all over the place.

“Jake stop this, it’s my fault, just let him go,” begs Jessica to Jake. Jake just looks down at Jessica how is down on her knees in front of him.

“Shut the hell up, I know whose fault it is,” says Jake as he pushes Jessica over with his foot. She falls back on the floor. She then picks herself up and begins begging again.

“Please baby, just let him go and I’ll do whatever you want,” says Jessica looking into Jake’s eyes.

“Well, I’m not letting him go just yet, but there is something you can do for me, get the hell out of my face!” shouts Jake right in Jessica’s face as he pushes her away. She begins bawling her eyes out as she runs into the bedroom. Jake then turns back to Russell.

“Well Russ, it’s just us four now, no witnesses except for them and I know that they won’t rat me out, as a matter of fact, they’ll probably help me kick your ass,” says Jake pointing over to Chris and Cory who are sitting on another couch watching TV.

“Oh for sure,” says Chris turning to look at Jake.

“I’d be honored to help you,” says Cory with a smirk on his face. The two of them then stand up and walk over to Jake.

“Wow, three on one, that doesn’t seem very fair does it?” says Jake as he is cracking his knuckles.

“Who the hell cares?” says Chris as he bends over and picks up Russell by his shirt collar.

“You want to go first?” asks Cory as he turns and looks at Jake.

“Why yes I would,” says Jake. Chris now has Russell’s arms hooked in with his so he can’t move. Jake makes a fist and pulls it back. He then throws it forward with all his might slamming it into Russell’s stomach. Russell yells in pain as Jake’s fist smashed his stomach. Russell begins coughing, because the impact knocked the wind out of him.

“What Russ, too much for you?” asks Cory. “Well you got a lot more where that came from,” says Cory as he pulls his leg back. He then swings it forward kicking the doubled over Russell square in the jaw. The sound of a bone breaking can be heard upon impact.

“Shit!” screams Russell as Cory pulls his foot away from his face. “You just broke my jaw you bitch,” says Russell who his moaning in pain.

“You aren’t very smart are you Russ? Calling me a bitch right now,” says Cory as he makes a fist and then pounding it into Russell’s kidney. Russell screams as Cory blasts him in the lower back. Russell falls to his knees and hangs his head down. Chris then releases his arms and he falls onto his face.

“C’mon Russ, get up, I haven’t gotten my turn yet,” says Chris as he steps over Russell and walks over to where Jake and Cory stand. Russell begins to pull himself up off of the ground. As he gets to his knees he opens his eyes right in time to see Chris’s size thirteen shoe smack into the side of his head. He then falls to the ground again. He is bleeding all over the carpet.

“Russ, get up man, you’re messing up my carpet,” says Jake chuckling at his little joke. He then bends down and rolls Russell over and gets down on top of him.

“Sorry Russ, but you screwed the wrong whore,” whispers Jake right before he smashes his fist into Russell’s right eye, then the left. Jake continues to assault Russell for a good five minutes. As Jake finally gets off of him all of the damage can be seen. Russell has two black eyes, a fat lip, bruises all over his face, and he is bleeding profusely. Jake then walks back over to where Chris and Cory are standing.

“Well guys, I think he has had enough,” says Jake as he looks down at Russell.

“Oh Jessica, where are you?” says Jake loudly as he turns towards the bedroom. He walks to the door and goes to turn the knob, it’s locked.

“Jessica, open the door,” says Jake through the door.

“Fuck you,” screams Jessica from within the bedroom.

“Well then, I guess I’ll just have to take the door down,” says Jake as he backs away from the door. He then gets into a sprinter’s position on the floor,

“On your mark, get set, go!” screams Jake as he takes off running towards the door. He hits the door with awesome force sending the weak door flying off of the hinges and into the bedroom.

“Please Jake, don’t hurt me,” begs Jessica who begins crying again.

“I hurt my shoulder, you should have just opened the damn door,” says Jake as he approaches Jessica.

“I’m sorry baby, but please don’t hurt me,” begs Jessica who his seated on the floor.

“I’m not going to hurt you, just get out of the house,” says Jake as he walks over and sits down on a chair in the room.

“It’s my apartment, you moved in with me,” says Jessica as she stands up from her seat on the floor.

“Damn, you’re right,” says Jake as he stands up from his chair and heads over to a closet. He pulls out a large white duffle bag and then heads over to a large oak dresser.

“Well then, I guess I’ll be leaving, won’t I,” says Jake as he empties out the dresser into the duffle bag. He packs up all of his clothes and then throws a few other things of his into the bag.

“Bye Jess,” says Jake as he leaves the room.

“No, wait baby, don’t go, we can work this out,” begs Jessica as she runs after him.

“No, we can’t work this out you slut, goodbye,” says Jake as he turns towards Cory and Chris who are seated on the couch watching TV again.

“Lets go guys,” says Jake to the two of them. Cory turns off the TV and then throws the remote on the floor as the two of them stand up and head over to the door.

“Bye Jessica,” says Jake one last time as he heads out the door slamming it shut behind him. Jessica then falls down on the couch and breaks down crying. About this time Russell is moving again and goes over to comfort her.

[Back out in the parking lot Jake throws his stuff in his van then hops in the driver’s seat with Chris getting in the passenger’s seat and Cory in the back. Jake starts up the van and pulls away from the apartments.]

“So, who’s letting me stay at their place?” asks Jake as he pulls onto the highway.

“Sorry man, there’s a bunch of family over because of the holiday and we don’t have any room,” says Chris as he turns to look at Jake.

“Well we wouldn’t have that problem if you didn’t live with your damn parents,” says Jake as he looks in the rear view mirror at Cory.

“How about you Cory, you going to let me stay?” asks Jake as he turns his focus back to the road.

“Sorry, my place is being fumigated, there was a ton of ants trying to clean up food from that party and I couldn’t take it. I’m staying at my mom’s house and she doesn’t have any extra room either,” says Cory as he looks away from Jake.

“Well, I guess I’ll be sleeping in here tonight. Oh well, it won’t be the first time says Jake as he makes a turn onto a small side street. He then pulls up in front of a small brick house.

“Thanks man and I’ll get my drums out of here tomorrow,” says Chris as he hops out of the passenger’s seat. “Sorry that you don’t have anywhere to stay,” adds Chris as he walks away from the van and towards the house. He opens the front door and heads in the house. Jake then pulls away from the house and gets back onto the highway. About ten minutes later he gets off at an exit. He goes down a few side streets until he comes to a very small, white house.

“Thanks,” says Cory as he slides open the van’s door and hops out with his bass in his hand. “See you later dude,” he adds as he slams the door shut and heads towards the house, he opens the front screen door and goes inside. Jake then pulls away from the house and gets back on the highway. About twenty minutes later he gets off at one of those rest stops. He parks his van and gets out to go to the bathroom. He returns from the bathroom and gets back into his van. He locks the doors, reclines his seat, and slowly drifts off to sleep.
