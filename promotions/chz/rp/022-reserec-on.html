Reserecíon
Written: 6-16-02

{The scene opens within a small confined room. A single light bulb is seen as the filaments are working their hardest to light the room. A medium sized figure steps up to the light bulb and places his head next to it. His hair long and multi colored as the light replenishes its true nature. As the camera pans in on his eyes they grow wide and begin to catch a milky film over them.}

El Lobo: Sí estoy aqui gente. El Lobo está aqui.

{Soon a female appears in a tight pair of jeans a tight black shirt with Angel in diamond studs along the breast cavity. As she walks her shirt rids up a bit exposing her tanned stomach and belly button ring.}

Angél: Yes he is here people. The Wolf is here.

{El Lobo looks at her as she walks across the plain wooden floor. With each step she takes the creaking eerie mass disturbs the peaceful night. As the camera pans around the room an open window with a single white curtain flapping from the wind is seen. EL Lobo begins to stand as he and his lovely Angél walk to the open window. As her brown hair is whipping in the wind she walks to the window with one leg in front of the other looking very lady like. She looks out the window and pleads El Lobo to watch the moon with her.}

Angél: Tomás ven acá and watch the night with me. So perfect reminds me of home.

{El Lobo takes a few steps toward her and gently sits on the window sill. He pulls his hair behind his head as the shimmering is seen off his piercings on his face. He lifts his head up and his face is seen then he speaks.}

El Lobo: Yes reminds me very much of home. Sit on beach at night watch sunset contigo and hear the waves come in. This country is disgusting. Canada, mierda es que esto es. I come here because of Mexican brother El Diablo. He ask me to help company. He need a Mexican brother to make company best can be.

{Angél walks towards him and sits upon his lap. As he holds her he continues to speak.}

El Lobo: A new company, a new era, and a new victim every single night. I will continue to climb the ladder until El Lobo will be a house hold name in this excuse for a country.

Angél: Tomás I don’t like it here. It’s too cold. I want to go home.

El Lobo: I’m sorry we cannot leave. I promised Raul that I wouldn’t let him down. He needed a Mexican there to make it the best company ever.

{Angél looks back at EL Lobo and caresses his cheek. Trying to pursuade him to return back to Mexico.}

Angél: El Diablo is you’re friend he would understand if you left to return home. I know that he doesn’t want to be there. But he has too it’s his job.

El Lobo: This is my job now too. Remember he pay me to make this company the best company ever. I am here for one reason and one reason only. I came to become el campéon del mundo. I will stop at nothing to attain that goal. Mi amigo El Diablo brought me from the depths of Hell to represent Mexíco. I was given the power of el dragón y el lobo. When the time comes I will fly over all of the competition like the dragon over the village. I will destroy each one of them one by one. I will stalk them, and tear apart their world. I will eat off their fear and in the end I will eat them like the wolf eats its prey.

{Angél gets up and walks toward the door. As she passes through the light from the bulb a shadow fills a dark void. Then the light returns as she pulls the light string turning the bare light off. As she stands in the dark a cold wind blows through the darkness sublime as the pan back on El Lobo with the sounds of foot steps in the room.}

El Lobo: Fue un tiempo cuando estaba hablado con un viejo. Y cuando fui saliendo el viejo me dijó «El fin de un libro es el primer capitulo de un otro.»…

{Angél enters back into the scene with El Lobo still on the windowsill. As the moonlight shines on him a black shirt with “Roach” on the front is visible. As Angél pears out of the window she translates what he had just said.}

Angél: El Lobo De La Muerta has just said that there was a time when he was talking with an old man. And when he was leaving the old man told him “The end of one book is the first chapter of another.” This is so true. When the darkness overcomes these stupid Canadians and ignorant Americans the Mexican will stand strong in victory. The ending of the El Lobo’s reign of terror in another federation becomes the new plague in CHZ.

El Lobo: …Reminiscing of Darwin’s Theory “Only the strong shall survive.” The strong shall truly survive but all those that are weak shall not be spared. Within the manada de lobos only the strongest shall be worth to walk in the presence of the leader of the pack. All shall bear witness to the rebirth of a dynasty. The strong and pure Mexicans are at the point of impact. The seriousness of this title tournament is not known by the likes of any other people involved. I am forced to team with the very people with whom I shall destroy to obtain that prized possession. Ahhh but there are no true possessions here it’s only a single obsession and the one with the strongest desire will prevail and return to sublimity. So I leave you with a warning…

{El Lobo takes a step outside and begins to climb to the lonely streets below. As he is crawling down Angél steps to the window and puts one leg over and through.}

Angél: He may not obtain the title at first but refrain from disturbing the facts of life. Refrain from angering the demons inside of him. Refrain from awakening the beasts inside…

{She steps out of the window and follows El Lobo to their darkness sublime. The camera shifts over to the new moon out. As it peers into its soul it begins to rain as the scene fades and comes to an end.}
