Other Side of That Mountain
'American Hero' Ryan
Contra

The scene fades into a busy hotel, and the main attraction is Ryan Contra, and his young daughter jamie who are currently seated in the Hotel's lobby furniture. Jamie's wearing a cute flower dress, standing on the couch playing 'peek-a-boo' with Ryan. While Ryan wearing an American Eagle shirt and blue jeans, laughs when the sudden removal
of his hands from his face excites Jamie. The desk clerk takes a moment from
looking at his clip board to feel brief enjoyment, at the father daughter play.
Only just getting a taste with a slight chuckle the clerk go's back to work.
Time passes, and Ryan intelligently keeps Jamie occupied. But what's their reason for staying at this fancy whoopy-do hotel? Though there seems to be no answer in sight, the attitude quickly changes as Canuck Heat zone make their way into the lobby. Earlier Ryan had gotten permission with the manager to shoot an interview here.

Ryan Contra- uh-oh, looks like daddy's gonna have to get some work done. hahah.

Jamie quickly puts her hands over her face, but spreading her fingers to see her dad.
Obviously showing her resentment to the best of her ability. Ryan laughed again. Ryan learned to appreciate every detail of his world with his daughter
Jamie. Most times people don't know what they have until they lose it. But not Ryan, he knew what he had. A beautiful life, Ryan's own goddess. But he knew though he'll have to play dad, and tell her no. Show her right from wrong, protect her from danger, and to trust her when she's old enough to experience life. Always thinking Ryan had to laugh when he thought of- when
Jamie's old enough- she WILL argue with him, over any and everything, but he'll be the one to remember days like this were peek-a-boo brought to her so much love. That's his little girl yelling at him for not understanding her.
Ryan tried to imagine the arguments they might get into, and how hard it would be to just hug her and to tell her that he loved her. There would be nothing she could do to make him mad he figured. But that's far down the road, things may change. When the crew were finally set up, one of the sound directors, took Jamie away to go get some french fries. Her favorite. The interviewer sat down on a chair placed across from the couch Ryan was sitting at.

>Wayne King- So before we start do you know what this whole thing is
aboot?

Ryan Contra- Well... yeah. Your french Canadian eh?
Get this thing filming nancy.

Wayne King- Where here at
Henston Plaza with the one and only 'American Hero' Ryan Contra. We finally caught up with him after some time. Finally the one-on-one we've all waited for.

'American Hero' Ryan Contra- I doubt you damn Canadians been sitting on the edge of your seat waiting for this interview, but hell whatever. I'll never understand you damn Canucks- my arch enemies.

>Wayne King- Are you saying you hate Canadians?! Where your employed
IS called Canuck Heat Zone.

'American Hero'Ryan Contra-
So..... Hell finally has a name. If the john elton lovers would so kindly put on hold the child beating and tune into their 'American Hero' Ryan contra, they'll see something they've never witnessed before.

Wayne
King- Your sweet disposition is concerning. You-

'American
Hero' Ryan contra- Dammit Bruce, here's an idea why don't you get a hair piece, grow five feet and lose some fat. Mr. king. Like I was saying, they'll see class, something unfamiliar to the dregs of society, aka Canada. No longer we'll they have hockey and beer to rely on for their social activities. Tv dinners will be put on hold all to watch their American Hero, the man they'll grow to love. Why I sit back and explain in detail why I hate you and everyone else.

Wayne King- Uncanny!!

'American
Hero' Ryan Contra- Fat!!

Ryan's quick replies and rude comments make for a long hang time for a moment. Fatty bruce King stumble's through cards- losing his composer completely. Starting to sweat.

> 'American Hero' Ryan- Looks like you need a twinkie frenchy.

Wayne King- Let's continue. I am ready.

> 'american Hero'Ryan Contra- Maybe you outta lay down first.

>Wayne King- *dismissing the comment* Now, your an up and coming
superstar in the Canadian Heat Zone you must be getting celebrity status around here. What a fabulous life you lead Ryan, and to top it off I hear you work at an ER in Chicago. Between your two life's where does your most excitement come in?! Do tell!

'American Hero'Ryan Contra- Well, the most excitement comes when I am bed with your sister and donkey punch her. BAM!!
Right in the back of the head!. But besides that Bruce, the time I have with my daughter is always most enjoyable. I wish I could spend every waking moment with her truly, but I've always gotta do the promotional bullshit. Never know when some bald headed fat guy wants to chit chat. Wayne
Bruce- Har har har*fake laugh* Your quite the jokestar Ryan, really. So how have you been handling the recent increase of fans? Are you ready for Wednesday wild and your first debut match?

>'American Hero' Ryan Contra- Name Calling usually works. I have a
match?

Wayne King- Your in the first starting match with Axl
Young vs Matt Blitz vs Angel 'The Texan' Summers. How do you plan to handle them?

'American Hero'ryan Contra- Routine four-way match. I just might have to get some cheap shots in. I am not implying that I'll be attacking them, like in the back or anything... or anything. Just.. yeah.
Anyway Axl young is a chump wanna-be canadian. Matt's his side kick. Now,
Angel' the texan' Summers. She interests me! Aside from the fact that'd I'd like to ride her like a bull, she seems to think women are the wave of the future and what not. Superior or some crazy shit. All I know is she better get in my damn kitchen where she belongs and cook me food. That's what there good for anyway. So here's my outline, I'll grope her, rope her, then pin her.

Wayne king- Wow alot's been said. This is Bruce King signing off, live with Ryan Contra, your american Hero!

>With the camera stopped filming- their seemed to be some social
unrest in the lobby as crew's silently packed up.

Ryan
Contra- This was my first interview Wayne, what'd ya think? Be honest bro. >Wayne looked up from his microphone equipment in utter disbelief.
He slowly nodded his head back and forth in a I-don't-know fashion as nothing came out of his mouth. Ryan's eyebrow creased as he waited for a reply.
Obviously very offened by Ryan.

Wayne King- Good- good. I think you've got it. Hehe

Ryan nodded in improvement when
Jamie came running to front dive onto Ryan himself.

>Jamie- Daddy your silly!

Ryan Contra-
No YOUR silly Jamie Sweety.

Jamie- I am funny, your silly!

Ryan laughed, as did Jamie. The camera crew lefted as did Ryan and Jamie,
