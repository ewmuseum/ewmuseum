A interveiw?
Written: 6/11/02

'Well I wonder who that could be'

I thought out loud while I walked to the small motel room door. I opened the door to see a drop dead gorgeous blonde standing in the crisp Kansas City air. She was wearing a small fur coat. The fur coat looked to be made out of mink, Though it was very hard to tell. She had a small hint of charcole gray eye shadow above her eyes and her lip stick was red, the red lip stick made her blue eyes more noticable. It was very hard to tell though. She was shaking. I looked at her for a few more seconds and said.

'Well just don't stand there. Get out of the cold.'

She nodded and walked in. Her dress was white with red trim. she sat down on the small bed. I went to close the door when she said.

'Wait. My camera man is coming.'

Camera? I thought. What the hell is going on.Then it hit me. I remember talking to a agent on the phone at. He said he was interested in seeing me in action. She was from the CHZ. I took a long look at her. They had picked a winner. She was beatiful. She wasn't trashy like most of the women that the Big leagues signed. As I stood there looking at her beauty I noticed that a young man was standing by me. He looked to be around 19. His hear was dyed green and he had a nose ring. His skin was a dark tan. His eyes where brown and he looked like he had just been in a car wreck. He held the camera up as the blonde said to me.

'I am Alisyon Malone from the CHZ. I was sent to do a interveiw with you Mr. Nakobi.'

Mr. Nakobi? That was something new. I have never been called that before. I don't like being called by my last name.

'Just Call me Boris, Ms. Malone. Now what can I do for you.'

'I am here to interveiw you as you know by now. I would like to ask you a few questions if it's not any trouble'

'For a Schön lady like you I will do anything.'

She took off her coat and set it on the floor. She then stood up and walked over to the table where to chairs where. She sat down in one, whilest I sat down in the other. She began the interveiw.

'Boris? Where are you from and how did you get to the USA?'

'Well if you can't tell my accent is German. I came from Germany when I was very little. My mother brought me her. My Fath-'

I stopped and thought for a second. Should I tell her about my father or should I just not mention him. It was a question that many interveiwers had asked me. I had never told anyone the truth about my father. I decided to tell her. I had to get it off my chest. This might be the best way to do it. The only problem is if I did tell her and the German mafia saw the interveiw they might come after me. Ah hell if they wanted me by now then they would have come for me.

'My Father was a member in the German Mafia. He was shut down by his best friend. Turns out his best friend had accepted the contract on his head. The bastard shut him down. Right infront of my mother and me. The next day we got on a plane and came here to the USA. My mother and I were not on good terms when I turned 18. I was starting to go down the path of my father. She then told me that if I became what my father once was that she would kill me herself. I decided not to piss her off. I moved away as soon as I got the chance. I was offered a Lumberjacking job in Canada and that is where I now live.'

I finished the story and then looked at my watch.

'Oh shit.'

'What?'

'I must go. I am going to be late for my plane. If you don't mind take this money and Key and go down to the main office and pay them for the room. I got a plane to catch.'

I gave her the key and grabed my luggage. I ran out the door fast and hopped into the rental car. I quickly drove to the Enterprise office and droped the car off. I had a taxi come pick me up and I made it to the air port just in time for my flight.
