Ending the Journey, becoming a singer?
Written: 8/7/02

On my way, mounted upon the now famous jackass, I was ready for whatever Canada had in store for me. The jackass was pounding hard that day through the sand, dodging sombreros and people everywhere it turned. Also, the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs were on their last chance of catching me, which I planned on escaping them of course. Almost out of breath, and acting as if we were in a high speed chase (2mph) in a rush to get to the destination ahead, the Mexican airport. A few bystanders had tables set up handing out free samples of what looked like urine, so being what I thought was hardcore mexican, I took a small glass and glugged it down. I hurled the hideous taste straight out of my mouth landing it all on my donkey's face!

'Sorry about that Jackass, I just wanted to fit in for my last time in Mexico.' I shedded a tear as I thought about leaving my home country. I let out a sigh as I finally decided to get off my donkey as I arrived at the aeropuerto of Mexico.

'Well this is goodbye old friend.' I looked the donkey in his eyes as slow mexican sad music played in the background. It abruptly ended as I turned the table around. 'You'll always be a jackass!' The donkey hood it's head in shame as I just continued on whisteling a happy tune to myself. I entered the airport of Mexico and went straight up to the desk with no bags of clothing or any luggage. Only wearing my beloved mask covering his face, the sombrero of sexiness and also with me, the dacing donkey of the infamous Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs, who were currently 'creeping' behind me.

The lady behind the desk at the airport was not elderly but not young, but somehow still living. Her short blonde hair was seen clearly in the gray airport with no ceiling. Her blue eyes glittered in the scene as I stood there, silent at her beauty. She swung her hair back with her blue airport uniform as my view of her went into slow motion as elegant, happy music played in my head as my focus centered upon this incredible woman. The music once again came to a halt as the woman spoke.

'Excuse me?' The woman asked me. 'Excuse me, Sir?'

I came out of the trance from the woman’s radience as her words struck me. I shook my head as I took in a deep breath as I got the courage to take the steps up to the counter where the lady stood awaiting my arrival.

'Hello, and welcome to Mexican Airlines, if they fall apart, don’t blame the mexicans!' She told me with an innocent looking smile. I took a look at her chest and saw her name tag reading clearly: 'Michelle Stimson.' If I had the knowledge to put two and two together, I would’ve known that she might have been related to me, and I did.

'Excuse me, your name tag reads Michelle Stimson, are you related to the Stimson family?' I asked her in curiosity.

'Oh no, that’s just a fly.' She told me brushing off the fly from the name tag, 'It’s Michelle Stims. Were you looking to fly Mexican Airlines?'

I was shocked that I thought a fly was some letters, but I continued on. 'Yes, I’m looking to get out of Mexico, even though I took quite a long journey here. Can I bring animals on board?' I wondered.

'Sure, as long as it’s a mexican, but you can’t bring it back to the U.S. you’ll have to ditch it half way.' She told me knowingly.

I thought long and hard about possibilities of this situation. I looked back to my possible cargo as it looked back at me with a cocked head and it’s poor dried up eyes. I looked back at the beautiful lady behind the desk.

'Alright then, I’m bringing one jackass on the plane.' I told her with a nod.

She chuckled as she complied. She mashed her keyboard looking like she was doing work as she asked me more questions.

'Will this be a one way ticket?' The lady asked.

'Yes, how much will that cost?' I asked back.

'Free of charge for your first flight, for it may be your last.' She told me with an evil look.

'Great! Tu eres mui fea!' I told her in the spanish languenge. I had thought I complimented her by saying she was attractive but instead, she slapped me claiming she was not ugly. I thought this was a way to compliement in Mexico so I innocently slapped her back full force knocking her unconcious.

'Your welcome!' I told the unconcious body. 'Come on, Jackass, it’s time to go!' The donkey looked back at me and came to my side as I slapped my thigh. I took some tickets from below the counter and went straight to the gateway, the only gateway in the airport. I passed by the high security of a chuwawa (sp?) by scaring it with the donkey. I continued on to the boarding platform and entered the 'plane.'

The plane was rusty all over and the frontal was torn up as the glass was broken and the wings were ready to fall off the rust piece of shi—wait this is Canada, we can’t swear! I got on the plane and went into the first seat I found was empty, being that the whole plane was empty and the pilot seemed to be watching a mexican porno film and trying to masturbate, but out of breath as it seemed.

'HEY! Get this thing off the ground, get to the US! andale!' I demanded the active pilot. (andale=quick (like that little mouse in the cartoons)

'Si!' The pilot replied to me as I went back to my seat(s) in the plane. I let out an enormous sigh as the plane started up with it’s one and only engine. I then saw four men crawling on the ground on the plane 'sneaking' past me covered by sombreros and black clothing. I thought to myself about the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs but then it occured to me, there's four here, the MMMM's have only three. I ignored the fact that they resembled the MMMM group identically as they sat down behind me.

******About 20.23 minutes later******

Havoc was the word to describe the ride but havoc is small to describe the havoc on the plane! The right wing was falling fast to the ground while the left engine already busted off into the atmosphere. The tail was on fire while the front was shattering in a flash. My only hope was a dream of mine in airplanes, to slide off the yellow blow up slide.

The pilot already had gone down the slide, which was released out of the broken emergency door to the left.

'The time is now jackass, our journey begins here!' The donkey looked back at me, 'Alright fine, our journey continues, happy?' The donkey nodded to me. I nodded back sarcastically. I then took a deep breath and took the slide down the sky.

'WEEEEEEEEE!' The sound echoed through the sky as I kept falling. The donkey quickly followed sleeping the way down.

******A few minutes later******

Still falling, I was gasping for air for further screams until finally the donkey and I both safely landed in a deep pool of what looked like water but was really heavily polluted water of a lake. The donkey and I surfaced from the sludge and I took a huge gasp of breath.

'Well this was surely unexpected.' I said as I then looked up to the sky and saw three men holding sombreroes to their heads I quickly recognized them as the Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs.

'I thought they left us, oh well, let’s get a move on jackass. I have a feeling these men are going to be chasing for a long time.' I sighed as I came to reality with myself.

As the donkey and I finally moved from our sludgy position, the MMMM’s were close to the surface of the ocean and were snatched out of the air by a huge shark. The shark came up from the green water and opened it’s wide mouth with his teeth already bloody from a past meal. The camera blacked out for a few moments as a new screen came up.

****This scene is not sutibale for children, we will return in a moment.****

Screaming and pain was seen through the edited screen until it was silet. When the screaming finally came to a cease as the camera came back into view and three sombrero were floating alone in the ocean as a red liquid settled below them. I took my keen sense of commen sense of dying and got myself moving by swimming with full force, well my donkey was doing the work with myself on top of him giving him morale support.

'Well, Jackass, I haven’t got the slightest clue where we are, any bright ideas?' I asked the donkey hoping it might answer back. I looked all around and there was nothing but water all around me. The camera zoomed out to show that acres around me was water, water and even more H2O!! Just then, Stimpo zipped his head back to see that the MMMM's were standing up right with mexican smiles on their faces and maracas in their hands while a dead shark lays beside them.

Their smiles quickly turned to angry faces as they slowly started to swim/walk toward me. A long time passed (about 3 or 4 days) before the stand off finally reached the shore. It seemed that I was surely done for. Their maracas would crush me within a shake of my life!

'Well, this is it. It's been nice knowing you jackass!' I cringed as the MMMM's closed in on me. They raised their maracas, I shut my eyes and all I heard were four screams, from the four new MMMM's. I did what I thought any true mexican man would do, scream. Well, I thought I was screaming but it came out like a tone, as if I were singing something. I opened my eyes while still singing and I saw that the MMMM's were enjoying it. But, they soon did not, they fell over dead from some aura of the tone that I had exherted from my voice box. All four Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs fell to the wrath of, the perfect note.

'Now that, was unexpected.' I told myself in utter confusion. The scene cut off as I stood up from the fallen Mexican Maraca Mojo Maniacs and looked high to the sky. 'The new Stimpo is here,' I said to the world throwing off my sombrero and throwing the donkey into the ocean, 'Singing is my calling and singing I will do! Now, to look for a band.' I contemplated to myself as I stood there alone on the mesely little island as the camera blacked out ending my journey.
