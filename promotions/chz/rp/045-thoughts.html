Thoughts
Written: 7-4-02

A tormented soul is one that you don’t want to mess with or come into contact with, a person who is twisted around in circles from their bodies to their minds. These are the people you never want to associate with, because their minds are so warped, but then again you can’t really tell when you’re around one. It takes a much-needed drastic measure to force these demons down within a person out. You’ve all seen what I mean and you know the cause of it all. Just take a look around you and you’ll see that the person sitting next to you could very easily be one of these people. The one that we speak of in our tale is one that you are currently familiar with and associate with on many occasions, but only recently did you find his dark side. Only is this a small piece in this twisted jigsaw puzzle, where as, there are many more pieces that are left out and some of too odd of shapes to fit in. A word of wisdom when surrounding yourself with these people, think not what they may be thinking, think what you are saying. Do not pay mind to how these people may feel, for they will only turn for the opposite in the end. Deep in thought are they mostly, where as you are left wondering to yourself about their next move. Think not as they wish for you to do, think, as you would ponder your next move. They turn life into a gigantic maze or chess game. Trying to twist the maze into something you’ll never be able to make your way out of or pinning you into a corner where the only words you’ll hear are Check Mate!

Their twisted minds aren’t necessarily missing as many links as they want you to think. In retrospect, they are some of the most brilliant minds the world has to offer and thusly the reason why most of them are in power now. They seize control and power and do not wish to take orders from others that come in and demand as much as a finger lifted of them. Beware as you are now stepping into the mind of one of these people. Sick and twisted is he, but so brilliant is his mind. Do you plan to follow the path he has carved for you or are you merely going to turn back now and return to your corner cowering in fear. Never to fully understand where he is coming from or his plots for domination. Think not what the outcome could be from this all for you, but think how beneficial it would be for the future and your untimely demise from what you hold so dear.


I grasp my hands around the top of the bar, feeling the metal begin to plunge itself into my skin. I pull myself up as so many thoughts begin to run through my mind and how much this shit really pisses me off. How much I can’t stand the sight of not being able to make my way into an arena unguarded and not being placed on a list that doesn’t mean shit to me. If I weren’t as smart as I am, I would have most likely just beat the shit out of all of them and been on my way, or I could have just told my driver to take them out with the limo? None the less, I can’t really force myself to think about these types of things at a time like this. I have my things in order and I have some things here in CHZ that I need to keep my eyes on. El Lobo isn’t going to be much of a challenge, whether he is, was, or ever will be El Diablo’s friend, but then again, when I think about it. I’m El Diablo’s friend as well. I lower myself down from my pull up bar and make my way through my little apartment floor. Making my way to the refrigerator. I open the door and grab a soda as I slowly make my way back over to the glass window that looks down upon the city.

“These people remind me so much of the Canadians. Just like little ants crawling around on the ground it is so easy for me to bring my foot upon them and crush them like the bugs they are.” To my amazement, a thought pops into my head, “Where as,” I state as I turn and make my way through the living area and into my bedroom, “I am of genius levels and these people are nothing more than mere peons in my plot for total CHZ control. Making things possible on this next Wild by continuing into the National Title hunt. I hold no grudge with a man from Mexico, but I do understand that he is several screws loose up in the head.” I begin to ponder and think for another moment, “It must have been the water, poor thing was made to drink public water as a child.”

Which gets me to thinking once again, “What if the person running our company was to be the same as this man? What if he to, did in fact, drink the water from his home country? Would this be a problem or has he been around us civilized people in America long enough to understand the proper way of running a decent wrestling promotion?” I begin making my way around my bed as I slip into a pair of jogging shorts and I sit down on my bed. Put on a pair of socks and slide my running shoes over next to me.

“Obviously he knows nothing about running a wrestling promotion, because if he did, then I’d be the one main eventing this whole damn thing. My talent surpasses all of the others in CHZ and I guess I’m just going to have to prove this at Wild.”

I slip both sets of shoes onto my feet and I make my way out of the apartment, leaving nothing, but the beautiful view of the outside world in sight.

~End~
