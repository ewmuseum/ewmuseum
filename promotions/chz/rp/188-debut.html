Debut
Written: 9.15.02

Sometimes all it takes for one man to succeed is determination. For another man, it might be money. Yet another man could care less if he succeeded. But in this business? Every man in sports entertainment has the urge to succeed but at what price? Luke Lawless so far has not had the chance to succeed, not even to compete in a CHZ match, or nationally televised match at that.

The biggest crowd that the 22 year old just out of PWA has competed in was a mere 500 people in a dinky little one-time thing performed at a talent show at his high-school just about four years ago. Now, then, he wasn’t determined to make it big, let alone train to be a wrestler. But he had the talent, or so a PWA talent scout said.

It paid off, his first big step at getting closer, and closer to that place where every wrestler wants to be…a champion. Not just any champion but a champion of a globally known fed. PCW or OSW for example. It may be a long and tough road ahead of him but hey, its worth it…right?

As he makes his way down the four flights of stairs of his apartment, he thinks to himself what possibly will be waiting at CHZ for him. Will it be a long night on September 19 where he and three other promising CHZ rookies square off in a fatal four-way match?

'This is just great, my first match and I don’t have a fifty-percent chance at winning. Just a measly twenty-five. Pfff…'

He climbs down his second set of stair cases and continues to map out his strategy.

'Who should I go for first? Mike Dipyatic? What a name, that dip head. Oh, I know, Ben Haynes. Hmmm…he sounds interesting, maybe he will be the first victim of Luke Lawless…rookie phenomenon.'

Wrestling, to Lawless, is not a job, but a privilege. Its not a chore, but an honor. Wrestling is all about speed, agility and strength. The fan’s could care less if you are a good natured man outside of the ring. They like the fierce, aggressive kind.

His third set of stairs, almost to the bottom of his apartment complex and he is already thinking of a way to take Ben Haynes out and using him as a stepping stone in the wild world of CHZ.

'Go for the lower back, then for sure he won’t somehow get up after the Slam Heard ‘Round the World and try to rack me on the top rope when I get ready to jump on him with the Rude Awakening. This guy has no chance.' A small chuckle is heard from Lawless as he nears the end of the last set of stairs and pushing through the glass double doors out to a warm, sunny day in Chicago, Illinois.

Cars are buzzing by as Luke reaches into his pockets and pulls out a ring of about four keys and one keyless entry control. He points the control to a nearby BMW convertible and presses a button. A quick two beeps sound from the car and the lights flash and the car unlocks. But what’s the need for the keyless control when the top is down and the windows are rolled down? Luke can be a slight dumbass sometimes as he climbs into his shiny like new BMW and he puts the keys into the ignition.

The car has nice, tan Italian leather interior and chrome hubcaps and a silver gear shift. He puts the car in reverse and backs out of his parking space and speeds off out of the parking lot of the apartment complex and into the busy rush hour streets of urban Chicago.

'Cripple him, its that simple…cripple the bitch and win the match…'

END
