A Lonely Tear he Cried
Written: 6/23/02

Some things happen for a reason and that leads me to this upcoming wild, the last before the big PPV Mind Games. A four corners match to determine who shall get that shot at the tag team gold and could be a start to something special. My mind has gone through a lot since my PWA days and this could be the big pay off but first there is somewhere I need to visit.

It was dark and gloomy night where the crows perched on the top of the dead trees and Soul stood in front of two gravestones dripping wet from the rain pouring down on the section of the cemetery. He looked through the light blanket of rain gazing at headstones placed neatly in perfect rows throughout the holy grounds. His black leather jacket and black pants were soaked, with his wet hair draped over his pale face and green eyes.

“I had to come see you both one last time.” He said softly almost in a sweet whisper.

He flung back his hair back and the rain covered up the tears that were falling down his cheeks. From behind, soft but heavy footsteps crept closer and closer making his fingers twitch and keeping him on his toes. Soul’s heart was beating quickly with his breathing becoming harder and quicker.

“You were always the silent and compassionate type.” A husky voice said echoing through the night.

He was a husky fellow dressed in a yellow rain suit that covered his black tuxedo that was opened just enough to see his red clip on tie attacked his white collar shirt. Though the man looked pleasant, Soul glanced over at him with hesitation and caution.

“We haven’t seen you in a long time Samuel, why is that?” He says sarcastically and almost as if he was trying to get under his skin.

There was no emotion coming from Soul and as he walked away he grabbed his arm firmly not to harm him but to stop him.

“If it wasn’t for me you would be dead by now Samuel.”

“Do you want a medal for that? You weren’t my reason for surviving, Ross was and I plan on taking him out sooner or later.”

Stan’s face filled with anger and started to seethe at the mouth squeezing harder and harder until Soul delivers a backhand that made him stagger back in surprise. There was a momentary staredown between the two with Stan starting to laugh sinisterly wiping the blood from the corner of his mouth.

“I have to admit you have guts coming back here.” He said full of confidence and started to close the five foot gap that was between them.

“My mission was to bid my mother and father adieu but it seems old wounds still linger.” He said bowing his head with disappointment and looked deeply depressed.

Stan looked confused and watched Soul take a white rose from his pocket and lay it on his mother and father’s tombstone. He reached in his pocket once more pulling out a handkerchief whipping the wetness from his face and walked away saying nothing.

“Damn you Samuel! You owe me more than you think.” He yelled at the fleeing Soul.

His shook his fist to no avail until he felt a weird warm wind through the cold and dreary weather which made him stop and turn back around. Stan looked highly upset and Soul gazed at him looking puzzled yet confident.

“You heard me, your nothing without us.”

“Hmmm…” he mused “This was the same warm wind I felt right before my parents were killed in front of my eyes.”

Bullets streamed through the air hitting Stan in his head, chest and neck as he fell down to the ground lying motionless. His blood flowed like a river of wine dying the grass a bright red and Soul was stiff still smelling the gun smoke in the air.

“Why does death follow me?” He said to himself and started to move quickly through the trees as a bullet barely misses his arm.

At this point he was breathing hard and short once again with crows flocking away at the sound of the second shot that seemed to be shot straight up in the air. There was a sense of excitement that kept his heart pumping quickly and a shot fired once more missing him once again only to pierce through a couple trees.

“Stupid move Soul, you should have stayed away from here.” A demonic yet angelic famine voice cried out to him.

“Aria…? But why is she trying to kill me?” he said softly so his voice wouldn’t echo through the cemetery.

Looking ahead was a brown barn with its paint slowly pealing off mainly to the rain pouring down non stop. Soft and steady footsteps stepped across the muddy grass and passed a couple of grave stones spotting Soul behind a barn.

“Gotcha little punk, you won’t escape me this time.”

Her thick black hair was thinned out by the rain with her beautiful brown eyes fixated on his body. Her tight leather suit showed off her picture perfect butt right along with a good set of boobs but in the same instance she point nine millimeter silencer at his head.

“Guess Wild will have to wait eh?” She said egotistically

“Maybe and then again anything can happen in a blink of an eye.”

As she click on the trigger it seemed the bullet was caught in the chamber and Soul ran through the rows of tombstones without any hesitation. He could hear the howling on the wind passing through in blazing speed. Meanwhile Aria finally got the trigger working and looked ahead noticing he had vanished. She chased him through the tombstones only hearing the rain drop down from the sky.

“This is just like you,” she said “you’re a coward and always will be!”

Behind a tombstone about one hundred feet away, Soul lied on the ground trying to catch his breath but he heard her footsteps getting closer and closer which made him scoot from tombstone to tombstone trying not to be spotted.

“When will this woman give up?” he said to himself.

He couldn’t take it anymore and stood up letting her see him between the rows of tombstone. She held her gun up but she was shaken and looked spooked, she tried to hide it with an evil grin but tears started to flow down her eyes.

“Why did you have to leave?” She said looking frustrated and angry. “We were supposed to spend the rest of our life together.”

“I’m sorry Aria, but he would have killed me and the one I loved if I didn’t leave.”

She slowly lowered the gun and walked slowly but cautiously to Soul crying and sobbing looking to be in so much pain. Her makeup was running down her face and her eyes widened seeing the mysterious man in a black robe pointing a gun at the back of his head.

“No!” She screamed

Soul turned around to see the man pointing a silver pistol at his forehead smiling and enjoying the moment. Just when he pulled the trigger, Aria stepped in the way taking the bullet in her chest and falling to the ground in pain while the gunmen ran away.

“Samuel, please come here.” She said faintly as blood sprouted out of her mouth.

He looked down in anger and sorrow, he once tan face was getting paler by the minute. Soul finally kneeled in the grass slowly watching her eyes dilated and she looked at him once last time before saying her last words.

“Don’t lose, please don’t lose.”

“I am so sorry… so sorry.” He said sadly closing his eyes in total disappointment and shame.

Her finally tears rolled down her cheeks hitting the wet grass and she lay motionless in his arms. He sat there not moving an inch and looked at the motionless Aria sitting in his arms that woman he was suppose to marry but know shall be looking on from heaven. At that point, a lonely tear dropped down his cheek and bowed his head in shame only listen the rain drizzle on this dismal and horrifying night.
