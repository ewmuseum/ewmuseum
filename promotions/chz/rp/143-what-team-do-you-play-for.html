What Team do You Play for?
Written: 8/14/02

A scene opens upon our beloved Jman rollerbladeing down a busy Edmonton road. Many would suspect to see a snowy or slushy surrounding in America’s northern neighbor, Canada, but alas mid August means sunny days. Jman weaves through people on the sidewalk on his sleek new rollerblades with little blue wheels. His black jeans are tucked neatly into the top of his blades. Dangling down over the top of his jeans is an Edmonton Oilers Jersey. Slung across his back with a strap across his chest holding it in place is his new hockey stick.

The surrounding area is a typical town area. He is going down what seems to be the main road of the town, four lanes, dividing the cars into their respective places. To his right numerous buildings are lined along the extra wide sidewalk. He goes soaring by a deli, then the fire station, big red fire truck parked in the garage, ready for action. Then Jman turns down a side street. Still relatively commercial, more shops and businesses.

He continues along this road nearly sending about twenty people to the sidewalk in twenty separate “Boom! Oh shit let me help you with that, sorry, bye’s.”

Jman finally halts his quest infront of everyone’s favorite fast food place, McDonalds. The Big glass walled dining room is littered with customers, chomping down their over priced Mcwhatevers. Jman skates long the side of the building then pushes open the door. He begins to walk in but his hockey stick, which is partially sideways, catches both sides of the door, stopping him from entering the small glass door room place. Jman fails to realize this and tries again, and again, and again.

“HELP! THERES A FORCEFIELD HERE!” He screams in horror, “DAMN YOU RONALD MCDONALD!” he finishes, with a shake of the fist to the sky. Finally male employees dressed in the typical blue McDonald’s uniform and spiffy black visor approaches the spectacle and helps him move the hockey stick so its rightways up.

“Thanks pal,” Jman quickly says as he skates past him and into the line area.

“Sir you can’t skate in here, it’s a McDonalds!” The employee yells back to him.

“Nonsense! This is Canada!” Jman yells up to the air as he skates around in circles, “This is Hockey country!”

“Sir take off the skates or leave!” The employee states, approaching him.

“Where are you from?” Jman asks.

“Sir-“ He starts again.

“WHERE?” Jman asks again.

“Winnipeg, now sir you have to-“ He answers, then starts again.

“Then you should understand fool!” Jman says as he skates a wide turn and starts moving along the wall.

“Sir this is the last time I will ask you, take off the skates or we will have you restrained!” He barks out, folding his arms.

“Restrained! You mean two minutes for delay of game! I’ll be back!” Jman laughs.

The employee calls some fellow workers out from the back, and together they rush Jman and push him up against the wall!

“BOARDING! BOARDING!!! THAT’S TWO MINUTES!!! HELP!!!” Jman starts yelling, kicking, and screaming. Everyone in the restaurant is watching now, as the men drag him out of the building and toss him out into the parking lot.

“Oh great, now I’m being ejected!” Jman sighs. “This will not look good on my stats!” He picks himself up and inspects himself for injuries. He finds a small scrape on his hand. “Uh oh…” He moans “This could have been avoided with the proper equipment… I need some gloves.”

Jman begins skating down the sidewalk once more, now in search of a sporting goods store. He passes by numerous businesses and people, re creating the situations experienced minutes before.

After about ten minutes he stops outside an old white building, paint chipping and peeling everywhere. Above the two front windows an old blue painted sign reads “Doug’s Hockey Shop!” and in the front windows numerous hockey gear and memorabilia are strewn about in a makeshift display. Jeff decides to check it out. He skates up and pushes into the door. It gives way and he skates into the building.

His skates make loud thuds as he passes over each floorboard of the old wooden floor, and his stick scrapes along the top of the long counter to the right. All along the walls many small shelves are attached, with numerous hockey products arranged neatly on them. At the counter near the front corner is middle aged man dressed in a Dallas Stars Jersey. What little hair sits atop his head in a desperate comb over is brown. Jman eyes him out and then begins browsing the shelves for gloves, but the man at the counter quickly stops him with a yell.

“Uh hey!” He calls.

“Look I don’t have my damn shoes! I’ll be out in a-“ Jeff starts, but he is cut off.

“Ah, your welcome to skate in here, we live in Canada right?” He chuckles, “I just wanted to comment on… how bad we beat you last year!” He laughs.

Jman looks puzzled, then he notices the man’s jersey and then his jersey. “Oh… yea… haha,” Jeff replies. “Well the Oilers did alright…” Jeff trails off.

“Like hell they did!” The man barks. “Out four to two in round one, that’s laughable!” He says.

“Hey 3 OT wins is also… luck?” Jeff replies aggressively.

“Ha ha… down tiger” The man replies. “So what are you looking for?” He changes the subject.

“I need some good gloves, I got some nasty cuts from not having any,” Jeff replies.

“Here lemme see your stick, I can recommend some good ones for the right stick.” The man replies. So Jman walks over to him and hands him the stick. “Damn son! What the hell is this?” He barks looking at the crappy stick purchased at 75% clearance last week. “Any real hokey player would buy something better!”

“Hey I’m a real hockey player!” Jeff replies.

“No you’re not! You don’t event own a pair of gloves!” He responds, “What team do you play for?”

Jeff pauses in silence. Then replies “Just give me the gloves!”

The man reaches down and pulls out a nice looking pair of hockey gloves, then hands them to Jman.

“20 dollars.” He states.

“I’ll take em” Jeff replies, then tosses him a twenty from his pocket. He scoops up his new gloves and carries them out to the road, he stops out by the sidewalk, thinking about what that man asked him.

“What team do you play for?”
