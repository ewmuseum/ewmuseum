He then performs a quick splash off the top rope and goes for the pin. Stable History: N/A Bio: Dave McReadie grew up in Hammersmith, one of the less luxurious parts of South London. It was an up-hill struggle to survive in the environment Dave was brought up in. If a riot didn't break out on a Friday, then it was seen by all to be a bad night. Parents would argue over who would get the child benefit to spend on beer that week, and if someone wanted to make a blood donation, their specimen went to the brewery and not the hospital. In the middle of all this apathy was Dave, and he was sinking fast. He had no money, had been thrown out of his house, and was sleeping in the back alley behind the bookies. One night, Dave found some money, and proceeded to the nearest pub to drown his sorrows. It was there that the simple answer to his problems hit him. The saying goes "if you can't beat them, join them". Dave did more. He surpassed them. A system of hard training and exercise, combined with a few brutal bar fights earned Dave a reputation as the most feared man around practically over-night. People would practically run to get him whatever he wanted, out of fear for the inventor of the "pure violence philosophy". Dave eventually realised he could make his talents even more profitable if he turned to the world of pro-wrestling, after seeing his hero, "Hacksaw" Butch Reed wrestle with Ron Simmons against The Steiner Brothers in 1990. He went to a training camp, and was picked out as the most promising trainee there by Davey-Boy Smith, who was on tour with the WWF at the time. Dave went to Japan, perhaps the country with the most brutal wrestling reputaion at the time. He won fight after fight against people who were well established, and turned the heads of many bookers in his direction. Then he decided to move on.........
