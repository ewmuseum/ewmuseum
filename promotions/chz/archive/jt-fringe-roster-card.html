What this is A CHZ roster card from the fed’s March 2002 site layer, as it stood in the operator’s own site backup.

name : 'The New York Assasin' JT Fringe height : 5' 11" weight : 240 lbs. age : 36 hometown : New York, NY finisher : East River Side finisher descritption : Running Lyger Bomb entrance music : 'Dragula' (NY ASSASSIN Remix) by Rob Zombie bio : Jason Bartowski is the son of a former wrestler "Iron Man" Adam Bartowski. From the age of 12 Jason's life was a happy, loving childhood. Although he never saw his father much, his mother Jean Bartowski gave every inch of her heart and patience to her only child. Jason, in order to spend more time with his father, decided to learn the ways of wrestling at the age of 16 during the summer.

Under the name of High Flying Jason Steel, he tagged with his father on numerous occasion throught Japan and the Far East. It was here that he would learn the moves that he would master in the ring. He also embraced the Chinese mentality and heritage as his own. Things took a turn for the worse, however, after receivinbg a scholarship from Siena college for Baseball. A highly touted college amateur, he was scouted by the Mets during his senior year...with a nice bonus to sign after college. Life was great for Jason Bartowski, he had one game left...The NY State Collegiate Championships. With his beloved mother in attendence Jason Bartowski hit the game winning Home Run. Against the wishes of his Mother, who wanted to drive back to NYC as soon as possible. Bartowski drank way too much and lied to his mother on the ride home. He disguised his breath with Altoids. His deception proved to be fatal, he wrapped his SUV around a tree in the Catskill mountains, his mother died on impact. and Jason Bartowski would earn the scar from his right temple to his neck...that has become synanmous with evil.

It was at this point that Jason Bartowski would die and JT Fringe would be born. After many months of mourning, Bartowski went with his father on the pro circuit once again. As much as Adam Bartowski would plead with his son, Jason would take his anger in the ring. Taking drastic measures his father refused to tag him in a All Japan wrestling match, foricng Jason to be mutilated with a barbed wire baseball bat. Jason Bartowski then changed his name to JT Fringe and went one on one against his father in 1990. It is still regarded as the bloodiest matches and disasters in all of pro wrestling. After 90 minutes of Hardcore battle, with exploding barb wire and heavy weapons, Adam Bartowski...the Iron Man...collapsed of an apparent heart attack, he would never wrestle again and would die one year later of the same ailment.

JT Fringe stayed in New Japan for the next 8 years. earning his now famous moniker of The NY Assassin from the way he decimated his opponents in a hitman like fashion...getting his matches over quickly and as painfully as possible. His last match in New Japan was against a young up and comer named Ichina Susiki. Fringe did not heed the referee's wishes in part becuase Susiki would not submit to numerous submission holds. The referee called for a KO, but Fringe would have nothing to do with a 3rd party determining his match. He applied a inverted Texas Cloverleaf named The Assassin's Guild and snapped the lower vertebrae of the young 18 year old. Ichina Susiki would never walk again and the Japanese Pro Wrestling council banished Fringe from ever competing in Japan again.

Unemployed and destitude...Fringe headed for Alaska to get work on an oil rig, however a young promoter by the name of Mike Steele called from the Alaskan fed of HCWF part of the National Wrestling Alliance. Fringe signed a contract immediately. He also signed with a small independent fed called the Canunk Heat Zone based out of Canada. These two feds would be the push to make JT Fringe one of the most accomnlished and hated men of the decade.

While HCWF closed for a bit Fringe went full on with the CHZ, although he did not like it at the time and things would end up differently, he played the part of a cocky face. the CHZ fans loved his brass way and he wound up with his first ever Heavyweight title in late 1999. Unfourtenelty the CHZ closed down after that...but was reborn the Fire Wrestling Federation a week later. Fringe would win the HW Title there in a 10 man Battle Royal.

For the HCWF and NEWA...Friinge counted the moments and it came to realization in late 1999. After many hard fought battles with his nemesis Seth Creed, including an ingenoius Fringe plot of dressing up as Santa and handing Creed an exploding Xmas gift, then ripping off the Santa suit, proclaiming he was the 1st Jewish Santa, Fringe interrupted the 2000 New Year's ball drop by cutting power in NYC. Seth Creed pulled one over on Fringe this time testing Fringe in Alaska.

Fringe could no longer wrestle in the US until his name was cleared so he went full tilt in defending his FWF title. But the FWF was in financial ruin from it's German owner Rick Gereadts so Rick sold it to NY entrapeneur Shawn Smith...becoming the SWF...Spawn Wrestling Federation...Fringe lost his HW title because of the merger.

Pissed at the loss of his title Fringe was handed an oppurtunity he would later regret making...he made a deal with NEWA official JT Banks in order to get out of prison...bring down Bank's oldest foe and owner of the NEWA Proving grounds...Don Owens. Fringe would do just that. Fringe found allies in the Reign of Terror in HCWF...with members Dreamaker, Brian Hale and Brian Walters. They held HCWF hostage for 3 months...also making rising the HCWF from the ashes. However, JT Banks...now wormed his way into the NEWA Presidency and declared that in May of 2000 there would be an over the top battle royal costing one fed to close. Fringe needed this to be on top, to get power...but he needed a ladder. The ladder was none other than Christian Light, a newcomer to NEWA but one with a glaring weakness...a fiancee for whom he cared for more than anything.

Fringe started the mind games early and often...tempting Light to come to him...and Light did. As Fringe won the HCWF's spot at WrestleClassic 2000. He made sure Light won his from the IWA. With Banks influence of course. Then the great plan unfolded. Fringe kidnapped Light's fiancee with clever ways of getting past her numerous bodyguards and sent Light an ultimatum...Mike Bell gets thrown out first...PNW is done...and you will help me throw out everybody until there is just you and I...then jump over the top rope yourself.

Everything went as planned until it was Fringe and Light...but Light got the last laugh as he jumped but did not let go...he then flipped Fringe over the top. PNW was destroyed but Fringe would not be getting his title shot at the NEWA World Champion. As a consolation Brian Hale brought Sara Mullen out and piledrived her on the entrance ramp...slipping her into a coma. She would later be declared dead.

From that point on Fringe hit one snag after another with Banks pulling the strings. He would later at the threats of more jailtime be told by Banks that he would have to take the 'dive' against Mike Bell. The same man Fringe destroyed PNW with. Fringe would reluctently accept and leave the NEWA after the match, never to return again.

Fringe happy at his accomlishments at NEWA would enter SWF in late 2000, earning the IC title in one week and later becoming the SWF Heavyweight Champion. He would hold the belt until June of 2001 when Steven Petapaw former wrestler at CHZ and SWF would buy the SWF and change the name to the NEW CHZ. Fringe would be a key character from the get go...kidnapping Matt Helms children and causing havoc to multiple superstars. He would later once again have his actions paid back...this time his girlfriend Sasha Edwards would turn her back on him...and sided with the Helms family, causing his almost death at the hands of a deranged Mad Max. After four months of healing Fringe is now in the position of scapegoat for CHZ President Cellrock...a former foe in the ealry CHZ days.
