Shammon Smith Real Name: Shammon Smith Birthdate: July 6, 1983 Hometown: Red Deer, Canada Height: 6'2' Weight: 235 lbs. Nicknames: Canadian Genius Status: Heel Manager(s): Current Teams: Entrance Music: control by puddle of mudd Wrestling Style: Technical Signature Moves: Superkick, fisherman's suplex, running powerbomb, dragon suplex, half crab, missle dropkick, spear, reverse figure four leg lock, swinging neckbreaker, float over DDT, brain buster Set Up Move: Redemption (Swinging DDT ) Finisher: Brainflow (Elevated STF) CHZ Title History: CHZ Award History: Other Federations: Other Federation Awards: Bio Shammon Smith is an Canadian Native that was bought up on the principles of wrestling by his father Ragan Smith. Though he was a prodigy, wrestling was something that he could relate with his father and there relationship flourished. But with his mother they never saw eye to eye. They would always argue and usually he would be right, but it was like his mother just picked a fight with him every minute of the day. When he mother mysteriously dissappered for a couple of days everyone in the family was very concerned. Shammon could relate to his brother because he was just like him... a prodigy. Suddenly, his mothers attitude became better and everyone thought it was for the better. One day he and his brother were kidnapped and they wanted his formula that he had created years ago. With this he ws given a ultamatum that he would give them the formula or his father and brother would die before his eyes. He gave them a fake formula and they took it killing his father and brother in front of his eyes.
