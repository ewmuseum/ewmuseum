CHZ Card Archive — News

CHZ News, August–September 2002

5 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The news page of the CHZ’s 2002 site as its news script held it in the operator’s own site backup: nine items from 1 to 5 September 2002, newest first. The page showed the newest eight; the ninth, of 1 September, is in the same news file.
Hunt Sends Warning To Mystery Attacker At the conclusion of the Main Event Tag Match last week on Wild, Matt Hunt was the victim of a savage attack. During the time when the arena was blacked-out to when the lights were restored, “The Postman” was seen lying unconscious covered in a pool of his own blood and in the middle of several broken tables.

The incident repeated itself at Mind Games. After Hunt lost the brutal Barbed Wire Tables Match and the CHZ World Title in the process, the arena again went into darkness and only to reappear to see Hunt again a bloody mess, but also covered in barbed wire.

As Matt Hunt concreted his claim to being one of the Commissioners last night on Wild, he guarantees that he will dig this perpetrator out and use his full authorative powers to punish the individual concerned.

“You may think you have the upper hand, but you’re making a big mistake” snarled Hunt.

“If you’re a employee of this company, I will make your stay here in CHZ a living nightmare. If you’re some nobody off the street, I’ll strike back in ways that you can never imagine and you’ll wish that you never attacked me in the first place” he concluded

So who is behind these mystery attacks and what is their motive?

We’ll keep you updated on this as the news breaks
Match Cancelled The match between Paul Harrington and Mr Powerful scheduled for Wild has been cancelled.

No explanation has been given as of yet.
Post PPV Personnel Report The Flying Vergomovs are in a critical condition after Matt Hunt destroyed them prior to Mind Games.

"They ate expected to be out for at least six months," stated Personnel Director Richard Taylor.

The Mystery Assailants Bert and Ernie have been released from CHZ and were warned never to return again, much to Byron Calver's disappointment.

And Redemption Song are moving on from CHZ.

"CHZ wishes Redemption Song good luck in their future endeavours," said Taylor.

More information when given.
Tag Team Titles After Redemption Song and Mystery Assailants (Bert and Ernie) battled to a no contest at Mind Games it was declared the Tag Team Titles would become vacant.

"With Redemption Song moving on from CHZ it made sense to have the Titles vacated," said Personnel Director El Diablo.

Angel Summers' match for the Tag Team Titles on Wild has been cancelled, but she will still gain her shot sometime in the near future.

Instead, the Tag Titles will be contested between Hollywood Assassins and Easton High, rather than their match being a #1 Contenders Match.

More when available.
Mind Games a Success With buy-rates just coming in and the crowds reaction at the event, it is reported that CHZ's first pay-per view under El Diablo ownership was a complete success.

"The figures don't lie," was all Personnel Director Richard Taylor could say of the news.

Further information will be given when available.
Wild Card Finalised With recent hirings and firings the Wild card for after Mind Games is up in it's complete form.

Go to the "Card" link to see all of the matches.
Two More Teams Signed The CHZ Tag Division is continuing to grow as two more teams were signed to contracts in CHZ!

Day and Night were signed and Children of God were re-signed to CHZ!

"We are pleased to have COG back," said Personnel Director El Diablo, "And are pleased to have Day and Night on board with what we are building here," he finished.

Further information on Personnel will be given when available.
Who Will Be Picked? After Angel "The Texan" Summers was victorious on Wild, questions were quickly going around on who she will pick as her partner for next weeks Wild against Redemption Song for the Tag Team Titles (assuming they retain at Mind Games)

Angel has refused to answer yet, but we will keep you updated on this on-going situation.
Good Luck to..... CHZ would like to wish Brendon "Stimpo" Stimson and Tyler Van Serio the best of luck in their future promotions.

Stimpo is heading to RUSH after the Mind Games pay-per view and Van Serio is heading to NTICW!

CHZ wishes them both good luck in their future endeavours and were glad to help them in their short time here.
