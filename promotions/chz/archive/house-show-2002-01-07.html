CHZ House Show — Burlington, Ontario

CHZ House Show Burlington, Ontario Jan 7, 2002

Brent (The Freight Train) Brewer Vs M-PaKt Darren Tyler - Off a impressive week in the CHZ, Brent (The Freight Train) Brewer, dominated from the beginning of this match, Darren Tyler couldn't match up to brewer from the ring of the bell. Brewer wins by pinfall.

Chazy 2 Cool Vs Kid Thunder - Kid Thunder suprises C2C with a domination of various moves, and even gets off his trademark move the "THUNDER BOLT" but with his experience and a little help from an old friend Matt Helms Chazy takes Kid Thunder out with a chair shot and then the "The Sweet Check"

The Count Vs High Flyer Vs The Tank - High Flyer and Tank battle in the outside of the ring as The Count watch in the ring, High Flyer dominates The Tank getting him all bloodied, The Tank is outside of the ring and The Count gets involved. The Count tries to take out High Flyer with numerous headshots, but High Flyer pics up his first win in the CHZ with a comeback win by pinfall.

Diablo Vs Gazz "TNT" Maybury - A old school matchup as Gazz gets some mic work in, talking trash about what he has been doing in the ChZ lately. Diablo comes down and tries to shut Gazz TNT up, but after only a few short moments, Gazz takes Diablo into a Dynamite Driver. It's over in minutes.

Violent Dave Vs Double J Josh Jados - Violent Dave wins by submission. Double J looked very rusty.

Extreme Chancelz Vs The Franchise Matt Helms - Extreme on his way to the ring gets attacked by Chazy 2 Cool, then Matt Helms comes in and it's 2 men against 1. Extreme battles against these 2 until Brent Brewer comes down to give him a hand, Brewer and C2C take it into the crowd, and Extreme and The Franchise go into the ring. A very entertaining match from then on. The Franchise looking very sharp with some impressive moves, but Extreme who seems to be on some kind of high from his recent success in the WWS and now in the CHZ, dominates to the end and picks up the win.
