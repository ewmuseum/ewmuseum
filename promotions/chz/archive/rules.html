Contract & Rules

April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OFFICIAL CANUCK HEAT ZONE CONTRACT SECTION I : RULES This is a original wrestler, wrestling federation. A : This means no "Stone Cold Steve Austins." Be creative and think of an original name. This goes for persona as well. We don't want a roster flooded with Undertaker knockoffs You can only manage two wrestlers. A : This may take the form of a tag team, or two independent wrestlers. There is no set limit for role-plays. A : You must RP at least one time for any given match. B : There is no limit on how many times you can role-play. C : Role-plays are based on quality not quantity. It is recommended that you submit a strat for every match that you are involved in, excluding House Shows. A : Strats are not mandatory. B : Strats are based on quality. All printed results are final. Role-plays are not allowed to contain wrestlers not handled by the RP submitter, without the express writen consent of the given party. Racism is not tolerated in the Canuck Heat Zone. Title shots are earned. A : The Intercontinental, North American, Television, and Tag Team title holders are required to defend their title twice a month. B : The World Champion is required to defend his title once a month. Enjoy your Stay at the Canuck Heat Zone SECTION II : DEADLINES All strats are due by Monday night, prior to a given Wednesday Night Wild, at 10:00 PM, EST. All role-plays are due by Tuesday night, prior to a given Wednesday Night Wild, at 10:00 PM, EST. If you miss a deadline on a consistent basis you will be contacted. If you fail to meet the next deadline you will be dropped from the roster. SECTION III : MISC The CHZ is a very relaxed wrestling federation. It is required that you keep this atmosphere throughout your stay here. If you agree to abide by these rules and regulations, please click here.
