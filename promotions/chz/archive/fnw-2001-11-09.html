Helm takes Santos down with a head and arm throw into a pin, which Santos counters by scissoring Helm's head with his legs. Santos tries for an arm bar, but Helm is able to block it, grab his opponent's legs and go for a Boston Crab, but Santos is able to fight him off with stiff punches to the head.
