Strat Submission Form

March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Below is a 'strat' it helps us write handwritten matches to a maximum enjoyment for everyone involved. We EXPECT everyone to fill this out or don't bitch about the results. A strat is the BEST way to win a match...roleplays are essential but strats put you over the top. IF you want to increase you chances in Winning your match copy and paste this information, fill it out and send it to Petapaw [ TABLE OF CONTENTS ] 1. Heading 2. Match Overview 3. Mindset 4. Match Strategy 5. Moves 6. Ring Entrance 7. Angling 8. Interference 9. After Match 10. Interviews 11. Sneak Attacks 12. Spots 13. OOC Statements [ 1. HEADING ] Wrestler's Name: Match Involved In: Stipulations: Height: Weight: Finisher (and description): [ 2. MATCH OVERVIEW ] A match overview is basically any information the writer needs to know. Such as the past between the wresters in the match. [ 3. MINDSET ] This will describe the type of mindset your character is in. It will say how he feels about the match, and what he believes his odds are. [ 4. MATCH STRATEGY ] This explains to the writer what your character will try to do throughout the match up, to get him on top. Whether it be mental or physical. [ 5. MOVES ] Under moves you list 10-20 wrestling moves your wrestler will do to help him win this match. [ 6. RING ENTRANCE ] This just may be the most important thing to include in your strategy. Right here, you create a ring entrance using the event's commentators. YOU MUST put it in a form in which we can copy/paste it onto the card. [ 7. ANGLING ] This will include any sort of angling or storylines between the wrestlers. Try to explain in detail. [ 8. INTERFERENCE ] This will include how your wrestler will react to certain interferences. Whether it be from fans, security, or a wrestler. [ 9. AFTER MATCH ] This will include what your wrestler will do after the match if he wins, after the match if he loses, and after the match if it's a draw etc. [ 10. INTERVIEWS ] Under this subject you should put any sort of interviews your wrestler has prior or after his match which you want to be added to the card. It could be in the locker room, in the ring, in the crowd, taped earlier in the day, whatever. [11. SNEAK ATTACKS ] If you were going to sneak attack another wrestler, you would include in here. You must make it in copy/paste form including the names of the commentators for the card. [ 12. SPOTS ] This is probably the second most important thing in a strat. Spots are used for you to get your wrestler to do certain actions, like moves, or brawling. In spots you must also abide by the copy/paste law. As usual you use the commentators names, and make sure the writer can copy/paste it onto the match. Spots mean a lot in your strat. You may have anywhere from 2-10 spots. [ 13. OOC Statements ] You will include how you felt about your rp's during the week, and if you think you should win or lose the match. You can also include any complaints or ideas you may have. Remember IF you want to increase you chances in Winning your match copy and paste this information, fill it out and send it to Petapaw
