What this is An out-of-character post on the CHZ’s 2002 roleplay board, from the operator’s own site backup: a roster member’s complaint, signed as Don Rossi, that only five or six wrestlers had roleplayed for the show. It is served as the board’s own discourse about the federation, not as a roleplay.
YOU GUYS SUCK OOC
Written: today

I swear if the damn original owners of this place saw what happened to the name of this fed they would be rolling over in their CHZ graves. This is pure ignorance of the roster. I mean damn a whole 5-6 of us RPed for the show. What happened to the guy who were supposed to be huge in the new CHZ? I have tried to get this going but when you have nothing to work off of you cannot work. I am shocked and almost embarrassed to be here. This fed was once at the top now I dont think we are even beating the CCW in ratings. El Diablo busts his ass and for what? Us to sit here picking our nose doing nothing but waiting for something new to happen? Well I tell ya what if you dont do anything your not gonna see anything new because there is nothing to go on. You want this place to work we need huge angles, huge stars and huge effort. So far I am sickened by what I have seen. I would ask for my esignation if I were anyone else I would have asked for my resignation but I am the kind of guy to stick it out so I will be here but you guys really piss me off.


__________________
A CHZ Legend, Proud of it too.
