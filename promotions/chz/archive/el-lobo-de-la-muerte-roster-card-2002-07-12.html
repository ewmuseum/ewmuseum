El Lobo De La Muerte Real Name: Tomás Lobo Birthdate: November 1, 1980 Hometown: Nueva Ciudad de Mexíco, Mexíco Height: 6'3'' Weight: 247 lbs. Nicknames: Lobo Status: Neutral Manager(s): Angél Current Teams: Entrance Music: Wolfs howl then Break Stuff by Limp Bizkit Wrestling Style: brawler with lucha libre Signature Moves: cobra clutch, cobra clutch bomb, shooting star press, flying splash, side four leg lock, face crusher bulldog, hangman's noose neckbreaker, repeated punch to face, mudhole stomping in corner, mudhole punches in corner, front face DDT,half crab, deathlock Set Up Move: Fear Factor (vertabreaker ) Finisher: Dragon In Flight or El Fin Del Lobo (Swantan bomb or a release verticle suplex) CHZ Title History: CHZ Award History: Other Federations: OCW, ROW Other Federation Awards: OCW heavyweight champion tag team champion hardcore champion intercontinental champion Euro champion ROW hardcore tough man cruiser and tag champ Bio As a child the young Tomás Lobo lived on the streets of New Mexico City. He would often fight to eat and survive day in and day out. His parents left him at an early age due to the poverty that they were in. Thinking that he would die they left crying. He was found by a bum and raised till he could survive on his own. At the age of about 13 he would sneak into arenas to watch his favorite wrestler defend his title. One night it was announced that El Vampiro Canadiense was going to retire. During much of his promos he stated that he was going to retire with the title. This was a match that young Tom couldnt miss. He snuck in to see El Vampiro defeat Rey Mago. Later that night as he was scrounging for food he was picked up by a young American couple who couldn't stand to see him the way he was. As the returned to Grand Rapids, Michigan they had a hard time understanding him. THe only one they knew that could speak Spanish was a neighbor who had lived in Tiajuana for a few years. They sent Tomás with their neighbor on many occasions so he could learn English. One night they had went to a street fight where many latinos spent their time. There he entered and was destryoed by the other latinos in the first round. He then decided to train and return at a later time. Summer time rolled around and Tom had much free time on his hand so he decided to save up money to return to his home country when he was eighteen. He entered many tournaments knowing enough english to say hello, what's up, and good bye. During the next few years he concentrated on his inner being and his fighting ability. Although he wasnt the strongest he was one of the quickest and craziest. It was right before his bierthday that he returned home. On the streets of Mexico City there were many people celebrating Día de los Muertos. It was there where he met this girl who was born in Mexico but spent much of her life in the U.S. After she was lost looking for her hotel he offered to help her. As it turned out she was staying at the same hotel and was a few rooms away. They had agreed upon that he would protect her as a personal body guard for English lessons. So during the next year they traveled all through out the US. On a luck night during a street fight Tom was among a group of hardcore latinos who wouldn't settle for any loss in the trounament. He had won it and the manada grew angry. They attempted to steal his freind but he fought them off like a wolf to its cub. As one ran away they called him EL Lobo De La Muerte and it had stuck with him. WHat he didnt know was that a wrestling promoter was there trying to find talent. He appraoched them later that night and offered them a chance to wrestle in Canada. Tom was a bit leary of the idea but Angél decided it was worth it. AS they stayed in Quebec for a while Angél picked up french. After about a year or so they were sought out by many of the lower feds. After being approached by El Diablo himself El Lobo De La Muerte accepted his invitation to become a star for CHZ. Promo Archives Reserecíon Quotes "El Lobo y el dragón está viviendo en mi corazon."
