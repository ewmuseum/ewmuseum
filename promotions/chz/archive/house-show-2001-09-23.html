CHZ House Show — Hartford, CT

What this is The results of a CHZ house show in Hartford, Connecticut, from the fed’s older site layer as it stood in the operator’s own site backup. The page heads itself “Sept 23” with no year; the fed’s own old-shows index dates its house show to 09/23/01, two days after the Friday Night Wild of 21 September 2001.
Old Shows

Sept 23 - Hartford, CT

Don Rossi def Merlin- Merlin had the early lead, however Don Rossi with his experience and Team Extreme posure, Don Rossi wins.

Tane def Fire & Diablo - Fire and Diablo seemed to be in complete control of this one fron the start. Even with interference from Matt "The Franchise" Helm, However when Diablo was going for a Chair shot on Tane he caught Fire, and Tane wins.

Gazz "TNT" Maybury def Violent Dave - Gazz totally took Violent Dave to the cleaners. Gazz was in total dominance from the begining. Gazz wins, - Gazz challanges Santos to a Rematch for the NA Title, Match Signed and will take place Oct 5 in Jersey City, NJ

Kris Snyder def HardKore - In a nightcap as Team Extreme interfered again. This Time Don Rossi sent the message out that Team Extreme is here to stand.

*The scene opens to Don Rossi sitting in middle of the members of Team Extreme, Snyder, Helms, SOT, Hells Fury, Darryl Rossi, Don stands up out of his chair as the men all look up to him from their seats*

Don: You boys have stood behind me for long enough now I know I pride myself on being the leader of this operation and I will always take pride in being here for you guys but I called this meeting for 3 reasons. !, I am making a co leader of Team extreme a man to watch out for us when I am making the wrong calls and the only other man here that really deserves it is Matt elms man great job the last few weeks watching out for Team Extreme and I want to let you say a few words before I go onto the next topic.

Matt: Don I want to say thank you but first and foremost I cannot except this offer I mean there is only one man we want to run things around here no matter what I have said in the past I cant do this. Don you know that we only stand behind you and that is because we are a family and you have brought us all together but if you ask me to do this I am sorry but I cant. You have to ride solo on this one my friend.

Don~ Well I guess I am gonna be the only leader around here but I want to dsay this if any of you see me slacking off a bit like Matt did let me know so I can make the life for Team Extreme better. Now I am gonna go on to the next subject and that is where everyone stands in the Team Extreme camp. Now let me see a show of hands on where everyone is at on this. If you are still in with the Team Extreme raise em high.

*All members in the room raise their hands, the door opens as a man walks in with a note and he hands it to Fire, Fire stands up and reads it*

Fire: Don if you dont mind I am gonna read this. "The day is growing closer Don as you and me get in the ring things are going to heat up as you and me dance our way to greatness to the PPV. Can you show your values of love & family or will you stick with your greed?" Thats all it says Don whats that mean?

Don~ I have no clue but I know it is from the Count. As I am facing him in the Main Event on Friday. Forget him but on the same topic I am in a war for not only my life but for the life of my ex-wife Genelle. Yeah she has caused me problems in the past but I am not gonna hold that against her. She needs my help and I will be damned if I am gonna let her go through anymore pain if I can prevent it. Guys who is with me in this fight besides Matt?

Fire: hey me and Diablo has said it before we are with you my firned we will fight with you til the end if you need us. Coutn and his goons dont scare us and we will be damned if they are gonna desimate you on the way to taking Genelle down.

*the rest of Team extreme stand up in cheer as the house show ends*
