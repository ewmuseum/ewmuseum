CHZ House Show Jan 20, 2002 Montreal Forum, Montreal, Que

The CHZ House show opens up with Flap Jacks and Rex talking about the upcoming PPV on January 27th "Caged Fury "

FJ - Rex i can't believe that we are only 1 week away until THE CHZ will unreveil the CHZ World Champion.

RL - It's going to be a busy week, being here in Montreal tonight and back in Toronto Wednesday, alot of things will come in place around here too. Will Helms blame all the attacks on JT FRINGE??

FJ - I know what you mean Rex, there is alot of unanswered questions. But The PPV is going to be exciting as Well. All our current champions will be involved, by my question is WHO are the 8 men for the Caged Fury Heavy Weight Championship ??

RL - That's Easy ..

FJ- oh Ya Rex, then name them ?

RL - I will tell you at Wild Flap, geeze you expect me to tell you everything. I just know you couldn't keep a secret.

High Flyer Vs Tai Mai Shus - as a opening match to the CHZ House show, this match was not that bad. High Flyer coming off a loss to the the North American Champion Violent Dave was the fan favorite in this match. High Flyer got a few of his signature moves off, and won by Pinfall.

"American Stud" Anson kane Vs Black Dragon- This Match started off with Black dragon and "The American Stud" exchanging blows. The match went back and forth, but Black Dragon seemed to be getting the upper hand, until BoneSmasher came down to ringside, BoneSmasher attacked Black Dragon, and tried to break his leg. The match was called for DQ.

Diablo Vs Brent "The Freight Train" Brewer - Brent Brewer wins by Submission.

Lucifer Vs Chris Chaos - A Battle both CHZ rookies showed 100 % into their first match. Lucifer started punishing Chaos with Suplex after Suplex. But Chaos battled back to almost win with a close 2 count. The Match went to the outside where Psycho Cody Hawke Lucifer's Manager helps lucifer get Chris chaos with a low blow. Lucifer wins by Pinfall.

"Main Event"Shane Andrews Vs Don Rossi - Don Rossi's return to the CHZ was a short lived one, and The Main Event was sure to make him famous. Rossi and Andrews were very close at first, but the new Heel Rossi used his Ring experience and a unexpecting Ref to gain the win over Andrews.

Matt Helms and Chazy 2 Cool Vs Mid-night Wonders - Matt Helms and Chazy -Before the match Helms and Chazy were in the ring discussing a brotherhood. A union was formed with a handshake. Mad Max was at ring side for support. As the newly formed brotherhood took on The CHZ new TagTeam Mid-Night Wonders. Helms and Chazy dominated for most of the match, taking advantage of the weaker team. isolating the weaker man, until Bone Smasher was tagged in, Black Dragon can out of the crowd and attacked him, and Helms and Chazy just stood back and watched as Black Dragon finished off BoneSmasher.
