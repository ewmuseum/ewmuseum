Tyrome James was born out of Compton California in 1971. Youngest of 6 brothers, most of the time at dinner table there wasn't enough food to even go around. And when there was, his older brothers would steal it. The only school Tyrome ever gradutated from was the School of Hard Knocks. To survive out on the mean streets of Compton he had to get involved in stealing, fighting, and drugs. When Tyrome was only in the 4th grade he burnt down his elementary school and had to serve 3 years in the Juvenile Detention Center. When he got out, he continued doing illegal activity. He got arrested again years later for armed robbery at a licker store. This time he served five years in the big prison. When Tyrome came out he had an idea. He'd been living on the streets of Compton all his life and had to fight to survive. He then realized he can fight and make money the legal way. He was going to become a wrestler. For the next year he saved every penny he had and only spent money on food. He purchased a Greyhound bus ticket to Decaytur, Illinois to train at famous wrestling school ran by indy legend, Technically Inferno. Upon arriving he realized he didn't save enough money to pay the $1000 a month fee. Spending the money he had he could only last for 4 weeks. At the school he met a man he couldn't even stand. His name was Donald Rudaneyer. Donald had the completely opposite life that Tryome had. He was white for one, and Tyrome was black. Tyrome was raised in Compton. Donald was raised in Decaytur. Donald had graduated from MIT and had a degree in rocket science. He actually helped build newest fusion reactor for a NASA space ship. The minute Donald saw Tyrome training he wanted to make a tag team. Tyrome rejected the offer several times until the day he ran out of school money. The only favor Tyrome ever asked Donald was for a job so he can graduate from the wrestling trainig camp. Donald was more then happy to give Tyrome a job. On one condition though. When done with the training Tyrome had to form a tag-team with Donald. Tyrome desperate at that point accepted the offer. When he finally finished the wrestling school he kept his end of the bargin. Together they are known as the team of 'Day & Night'. After working two years in the indies, they recently moved to Canada to work in the CHZ promotion to test their abilities in a higher level organization.
