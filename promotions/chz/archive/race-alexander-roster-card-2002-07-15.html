Race Alexander Real Name: Race Alexander Birthdate: July 15, 1969 Hometown: Calgary, Alberta, Canada Height: 6'1 Weight: 236 lbs. Nicknames: None Status: Heel Manager(s): Natalie Shea Current Teams: None Entrance Music: Young Man Blues by The Who Wrestling Style: Technical, old school Signature Moves: Russian Leg Sweep, Knife Edged chop, a variety of suplexes and DDT's, low blow, eye rake Set Up Move: None, though usually opponent is moving towards Race (N/A ) Finisher: The E-Racer (Drop toe hold into ankle lock submission) CHZ Title History: None CHZ Award History: None Other Federations: None of consequence Other Federation Awards: None of consequence Bio Race Alexander is a second generation wrestler, who unlike his father, has all the physical tooks and charasmatic style needed to make it not only to the big time, but to be 'the man' in any major wrestling promotion. Unfortunately for Race, who has been wrestling professionally since he was 16 years of age, it hasn't shaken out that way. Injuries, high profile feuds with nobodies-now-turned-somebodies, allegations of substance abuse, and a whole line of other allegations have kept Race Alexander right where he started: The Canadian Indy Circuits. Race was 20 years old when he met the then 22 year old Natalie Shea. Natalie saw a lot of talent in Race, and felt she was her ticket to the top. Thousands of miles later, a set of breast implants, followed by a set of replacement implants, followed by a face lift, hasn't changed anything for Natalie. She is still exactly where she started. Still beautiful, but a little older, a little more seasoned, and a lot more jaded. Race is so close to the big time, he can taste it. Natalie can smell it. Can the regionally televised Canuck Heat Zone be the stepping stone they need to break into the big time? Only time will tell. Promo Archives To the T Yawn Yawn Quotes
