What this is A CHZ roster card from the fed’s March 2002 site layer, as it stood in the operator’s own site backup.

name : 'The Game' Don Rossi height : 6' 9" weight : 285 lbs. age : 26 hometown : Detroit, MI finisher : Game Over finisher descritption : DVD into a Mitchunoku Driver. entrance music : 'The Game' by Disturbed bio : Don was born in Detroit, he moved to Tennessee for 3 years to take care of his grandfather. After his grandfather died he moved back to Michigan.

He finished school doing very well. He got a scholarship for college but decided not to go because of family problems at home. Three years later his father died.

As he wondered what to do next Don remembered him and his father watching wrestling when he was little and decided thats what he would do. He went to school and trained with some of the best. when he got a call from Shawn Smith to go work for the SWF. He won the Hardcore belt there from Havoc and after a few matches with Count he was recognized by Steven Petapaw and got a contract to go work for the CHZ.
