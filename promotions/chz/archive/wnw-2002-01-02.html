CHZ Wednesday Night Wild — Ottawa, Ontario

Jan 2, 2002 Ottawa Palladium Ottawa,Ontario The show began with The Count walking into the arena and another car tried to run him over But he escapes injury. After he shakes his self off, he looks towards the back door to see Cellrock Standing there with a surprised look on his face. Rumpke vs Lil Nikki The first match of the night was very entertaining as 2 new CHZ stars made their debut. Lil Nikki had the early advantage using a array of high flying moves to work over Rumpke. But Rumpke did not give up as he fought back as he got the crowd behind him. Rumpke chants Started as he hit lil Nikki with the Hangover to win the match. High Flyer vs Kid Kruel Another high Flying match but this was over rather quick as Kid Kruel showed he has some Skills and could be force in the CHZ. It ended with Kid Kruel hitting his sig move the Seeing Stars. Chazy 2 Cool ran into Extreme Chancelz and gave him congrads on his WWS tourney success and Told him don't take his ass kicking tonight personal. but Extreme Chancelz asked him why was he telling Him that when he should be telling himself that. Slasher vs Double J Josh Jados Back and forth match as these two did not want to lose.Double J pins Slasher several times but the man From New York would escape each one.Double J got mad at the ref and while he was in the ref's face pleading his case,Slasher regained his energy and hit the Fear for the win. Mad Max vs The Xtremist Max offered his hand in frienship to The Xtremist who slaped it as the match began.The Xtremist pulled out all the moves early trying to wear down Max.several times he tried for the pin but Max always kicked out.The Xtremist climbed the top rope to try a high impact manuever but Sasha pushed him off the ropes. The Xtremist furious grabed here by the hair and was about to slap her when Max put him in the sleeper and the Xtremist passed out. Kid Thunder vs Diablo vs John Reed All three men stared at each other as the bell rung.Suddenly John Reed charged Diablo and the match was on.these three big men all went toe to toe,each trying to get the upper hand.the momentum swung Diablo's way as he threw both Reed and Thunder over the top rope.Diablo played with Estella Storm and Rachel Reed telling them to come kiss him.while he did that.Kid Thunder came from behind with a stunner. And won the match.after the match Estella told Diablo to kiss her ass.as her and Thunder walkes up the ramp Diablo started smiling. In the back Violent Dave attacked the Tank with a bat and told him to stay in the back for their match Because it would be worse in the ring. Gazz "TNT" Maybury vs Brent "The Freight Train Brewer This was total domination by The Freight Train. He destroyed Gazz seemly playing with him at times thru out the match till he finally hit the 50 ton Fury for the win. He then grabbed a mic and told the crowd that this was just a taste of what the CHZ can expected from him. "M-PaKt" Darren Tyler vs Maniac This was a quick one as M-PaKt made quick work of Maniac and then hit him with several submission moves finally ending it with the Crippler crossface after the match he asked for a mic and told Chazy 2 Cool, and Matt Helms their time would come soon enough so they better be on the look out. The Tank vs Violent Dave Violent Dave came to the ring with a mic.he announced to the crowd that The Tank told him backstage he was not coming out for their match.he said The Tank was scared.as he worked the crowd The Tank came from out of the crowd and hit Dave with a gore then he hit with a Tankbomb for the win. The Count walked into Cellrock's room and told him he knows he is behind the plots of trying to run him over.Cellrock denies it and tells him to get the hell out of his room.The Count askes why did he see him right after the runover attempts.Cellrock says it was just luck to see someone try and take him out.The Count tells him he better call it down the middle tonight or he might be next to get run over. Chazy 2 Cool vs Extreme Chancelz-IC Title match Very entertaining match as these two starts went back and forth.each man coming close for the victory. Chazy knocked the ref out by accident with a forearm and Extreme took him outside the ring pounding him With chairs,he set him up on the announcers table and tried to powerbomb him but Chazy reverse it.and clothsline Extreme.Chazy then grabed a monitor and hit Extreme in the head and pushed in the ring.he then woke up the ref and layed on top for the win. As he posed after the match "M-PaKt walked out to the ramp and stared him down. The Count vs The Franchise Matt Helms NA Title match (special ref Cellrock) Best match of the night as the Count used his size advantage early in the match to try and wear down The Franchise. He tried for the pin severals times but Matt kicked out everytime. The Count looking at Cellrock complaining about a slow count. Matt soon worked the match into his advantage using a array Of moves to counter The Counts power.soon both men were bleeding from the head as a classic was building.The Count tries for a pin after a power bomb from the top rope.some how Helms kicks out Count grabs Cellrock by the throat and hits a chokeslam when someone comes from under the ring with a mask on and a sledgehammer and clocks The Count in the head knocking him out.he then pulls Helms and lays him over The Count. He shakes Cellrock who then starts the 1..2..3... As the show ends The Titantron shows a promo for the first PPV of the new year. Caged Fury -Jan 27,2002 Pengrowth Saddledome in Calgary,Alberta
