CHZ House Show — Halifax, NS

What this is The results of a CHZ house show in Halifax, Nova Scotia, from the fed’s older site layer as it stood in the operator’s own site backup. The page heads itself “Nov 14” with no year; it sits in the fed’s autumn 2001 run, five days after the Friday Night Wild of 9 November 2001, and the fed’s own Hardcore title history dates this Barbed Wire match to 14 November 2001.
Old Shows

Nov 14 - Halifax, NS

Merlin def Darryl Rossi( Non Title ) - a great match to start the evening off. Both men got off alot of moves, and battled outside of the ring, Merlin uses a chair to his advantage again catching The Devastator Darryl Rossi of guard along with the referee.

Son's of Thunder Darryl Rossi and Don Rossi NO DECISION- The New Tag-Team Title holders Sons of Thunder were put to the test when the Rossi Brothers came together. From the start of this match the Rossi's looked like they may take this match,even getting a close 2 count. But fate wasn't ment to be as the teams ended up outside of the ring, and The Canadian Blackheart Brian Nash was there to pay a little visit.

Extreme Chancelz def Kris Snyder and Diablo and Fire (4 way Hardcore Title BarbedWire Match) - The first ever BarbedWire Match in CHZ History did not dissapoint many fans in Atlantic Canada. Snyder with his experience had the early lead using garbage cans, baseball bats, and wood tables to his advantage. Diablo the Hardcore champion seemed to be off in another world, as Fire and him battled it out. Extreme Chancelz was the first one to draw blood, when he bounced off a barbedwire turne buckle, but that didn't seem to effect him as he came back and took out Fire and then Diablo with massive blows with a steel chair, Then it was a bloody massacure as Snyder and Extreme Chancelz exchanged numerous career ending shots, until the final shot was taken by Extreme Chancelz as he piledrived Kris Snyder former World Champion through a garbage can and picked up the pin.

Steve Santos and Cat def Matt Helms and Sasha - at the begining of this match Steven Petapaw came down to ringside to an ovation of boos, and chuckles. Helms confronted him about this match and about his suspisions, but Petapaw just sat down at the announcers table. Helms got in the ring with Santos as they battled it out. Helms wouldn't tag in Sasha , as Petapaw saw this , he began to get very angry and dissapointed, he stormed away stoping at ringside for a long stare down with The Franchise, With Helms distracted by Petapaw's actions Santos capitalized, taking Helms down and out with the The Dragon Slayer. Santos and Cat got a very large Pop as the show ended. Helms was furious, and him and Sasha took off after the match.
