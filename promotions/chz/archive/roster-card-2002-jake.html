singles Jake Schultz Barberton, Ohio 6'5' 257 'Degenerate' by Blink 182 Hell Raiser Same setup as a powerbomb, only instead of slamming his opponent on their back, Jake drives them head first into the mat Face None Jacob Adam Schultz Feb. 23 1980 Jake None Brawler/Power Kick to gut Forcefully places his foot into opponents abdomen...in case you didn't know this already Born Jacob Adam Schultz to parents Kelly and Roger Schultz on February 23, 1980 in the small Ohio town of Barberton. Jake spent all of his years in elementary school without any true friends; he was picked on a bullied daily due to his size. He was always the shortest and skinniest kid in class and an easy target for bullies. Things didn't change much for him as he entered middle school. He still remained friendless. This was due to the fact he listened to punk rock in a city largely dominated by rap music and white people who like to believe they are black. He was constantly picked on not only for his small size, but also for the music he listened to and the clothes that he wore. It wasn't until his freshman year at Barberton High School that Jake began to make some friends. At BHS he found other people who shared the same interests as him, in particular two guys, Chris Knight and Cory Knox. With his newfound friends the bullying due to his dress and taste and music soon ceased. But that wasn't the only bullying that stopped. Between his eight and ninth grade years Jake had gotten much bigger and was no longer the runt of his class. During his time at Barberton High School Jake spent every free hour he had in the weight room, he figured if he added muscle to his newly gained size he could get revenge on everyone who had picked on him since he was a kid. By the time he was a junior Jake was one of the biggest kids in his class standing at 6'4' tall and weighing in around 235 pounds. He then set out to gain his long-awaited revenge. Jake got in three fights at school within a month's time and was expelled. Even though he was expelled he remained friends with Cory and Chris. During what should have been Jake's senior year the three of them started a band. The band was known as Tension II. They started out just playing tiny gigs in Barberton with only a few people who liked them. However as time progressed they got more and more of a fan base in Barberton and word began to spread about them. Eventually they became very popular in the area where they lived. However that all changed during a performance at The Glass House in Akron, Ohio. For some reason, still unknown, the crowd did not like the performance at all and Tension II was booed off of the stage. Outside of the building one of the people who from inside started to torment Jake about the bad performance. Jake, a person who is usually calm and avoids confrontations, snapped. He lost it and attacked the man. The cops finally drug Jake off, but no until he had done some serious damage to the man. Jake was taken to jail that night, but was later bailed out by his friends. However, before they came to get him they stopped off to get something to eat. While eating they happened to see a flyer for a wrestling school called the Primetime Wresting Academy. They got the flyer to give to Jake as a joke, not knowing just what this simple flyer would do for Jake. After being bailed out Jake took the flyer and thought nothing of it. It wasn’t until later that night when he returned home that he actually thought about the flyer. He thought it over and eventually found himself at the PWA recruiting offices. He joined the PWA and had a rather successful stay there, losing only one match. CHZ Personnel Director Richard Taylor then contacted him about coming to the Canuck Heat Zone. Jake accepted and is now a member of the CHZ… "Chaos often breeds life, when order breeds habit."
