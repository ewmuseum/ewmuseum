WILD DOG VERSE MAD MAXX

[The camera switches to Tiffany Tate in the ring.]

TT:"The following match up is for one fall, with a 30 minute time limit."

[The camera looks up a huge poll attached to one of the ring posts. On top is a cattle prod. About one and a half feet in length, and black.]

["Fight Music" By D12 begins to play over the PA system, white lights begin to flicker on and off. He is flanked by two dogs on either side. He holds the leashes as the dogs bark at the audience.]

TT:"Coming to the ring, hailing from Sheffield, England...and weighing in at 205 pounds...WILD...DOOOOG!!!"

[Wild Dog pulls the dogs to one side of the ring, and ties the leashes to the ring post. He then slides into the ring and takes off his ripped T Shirt. TT:"And his opponent..."

[The lights dim as Mad Maxx steps out onto the entrance way. Within ten seconds a dozen or so security guards run out and surround Mad Maxx.]

FJ:"It's for your own good Maxx...just go quietly."

[The guards approach cautiously. Three from behind grab Maxx. He swings around, two fly off, one still grabs hold. Two more charge in for his legs. He kicks upward, landing some clean shots. From the ring Wild Dog charges up the ramp and dives onto the screen, pulling down three guards with him.]

FJ:"What the hell is he doing."

RL:"He wants to fight, and it's his right...to party."

[Max breaks loose and begins swinging wildly, laying out three more guards. Wild Dog begins throwing haymakers, laying out four more. The two back into each other, then quickly turn and begin wailing away.]

DING*DING

FJ:"I guess we are going to have this match."

[Wild Dog gains the advantage by drilling his knee into Max's stomach, then tossing him head first down the entrance ramp. Max tumbles, head over heels, towards the ring. Wild Dog grabs a dropped can of mace, and a Billy club, then walks towards the ring.]

FJ:"What's he going to do with that stuff?"

[Max gets to his feet and charges Dog as he is walking down the ramp. Max wraps his arms around Dog's waist and lifts him into the air, trying to squeeze the life out of him. FJ:"Max in a world of trouble right now...HERE COMES DOG..."

[Wild Dog charges at Max, leaping in the air, and smashing the club across the top of Max's head. Max stumbles backward, and then falls to his back. Wild Dog jumps on top of him, and then begins driving the club into Max's throat. Wild Dog grabs the can of mace and shakes it up. Before he can spray, Max rolls to his side, forcing Dog off of him. The two get to their feet. Wild Dog swings the club over his head, but Max blocks it, and then boots him in the mid-section. He then grabs him in a front face lock.]

FJ:"DDT!!! Max, now...to his feet. He lifts Dog to his feet...Stiff right hand...and again. Max rolls Dog into the ring now. He's going up top."

[Max gets to the middle rope, when Dog jumps to his feet, pulling him down from behind.]

RL:"OOH it looked like Max's head bounced off the mat. Maybe it reassembled his brain."

[Dog aids Max to his feet, then drops to his knees, delivering a low blow. Max stumbles around, and then walks into a vicious clothesline.]

FJ:"Wild Dog has looked good here today. Damn NBK."

[Wild Dog slides to the outside of the ring, and grabs a table from underneath the ring. He opens it, and positions it outside the ring.]

FJ:"Wild Dog back into the ring now...He's got Max in a standing head scissors."

RL:"He's going to POWER BOMB MAX THROUGH THE TABLE!!"

FJ:"NO!!! MAX HANGS ON....HURICANRANA!!!!"

[Max flips Wild Dog out of the ring with a huricanranna. The move propels Wild Dog over the table.]

FJ:"MY GOD I DON'T THINK I'VE EVER SEEN SOMEONES BACK HIT THAT HARD ON THE CONCRETE!!!"

[Wild Dog slowly gets to his feet as Max waits to pounce inside the ring. As Wild Dog gets onto the apron Max charges forward, spearing Dog. The two fly off the apron and through the table below.]

RL:"MY GOD!!!"

FJ:"These two are tearing each other apart, and the match is barely eight minutes old."

[The two slowly pull themselves from the wreckage, Max gaining an advantage with two stiff punches. He reaches into the crowd and pulls out a steel chair. He swings it wildly over his head.]

*CRACK*

FJ:"What a chair shot! And again...and a third time."

RL:"How is Wild dog still standing after three chair shots?"

[Wild Dog motions for Max to keep hitting him with the chair. Max swings again, crashing across the top of Dog's head...he then hands him the chair and tells Wild Dog to hit him.]

RL:"What the hell is wrong with these two?"

FJ:"OUCH...what a shot by Wild Dog now."

FJ:"Max doubles over....OOOH!!! Chair shot to the back knocks Max to his knees. OOOOHHH! Right across the face with that chair shot."

RL:"I think Dog just smashed his nose into a thousand pieces."

FJ:"There is twisted steel and broken bodies scattered in front of us folks."

RL:"You love to stretch the truth don't you?"

FJ:"What?"

FJ:"Front face lock by Dog...no, vertical suplex...and...OH he caught him hard with that one. Dog, to his feet...stomps away at Max. He picks him up to his feet...body slam! Wild Dog, off the ropes...drops the leg. He's going for it."

FJ:"NINE FOOT DROP BACK INTO THE RING! NOW MAX IS GOING TO THE TOP."

FJ:"HE'S GOT THE TAZOR!"

FJ:"Max, in behind...GERMAN SUPLEX! The tazor just went flying out of the ring! Max drops a quick elbow...then another one. Max lifts Dog to his feet...standing head scissors...POWERBOMB! Max slides out of the ring, he's searching for tazor."

FJ:"That was not a good idea!"

[Max grabs the fan by his throat, and picks up the tazor. He jabs it into the breast of the fan, causing him to convulse. Security begins filtering through the crowd, en route to Max.]

FJ:"Max has gone completely insane. He's throwing wild lefts and rights, and tazing the whole front row!!!"

RL:"Here comes Wild Dog!"

FJ:"He's over the railing...double axe handle on Max. Max stumbles...he grabs another fan and tosses him at Wild Dog...Dog scoops the fan...body slam! What the hell is going on?!?"

FJ:"MAX IS CUT OFF AT THE PASS!!!"

FJ:"BELLY TO BACK SUPLEX!! The tazor flies towards the ring!"

FJ:"Here comes Max...no wait, he's grabbed by the officers...He's kicking free! He's made it into the ring....OOOHHH WILD DOG TAZED HIM!!!"

[Mad Maxx convulses in the ring, then falls to his knees. The armed police officers storm the ring, and gang tackle the two men. Wild Dog begins tazing guards left and right. Max fights valiantly to free himself from the officers' grasp.]

FJ:"NOOO...FANS ARE POURING INTO THE RING...THIS IS INSANE!! THIS IS UNPRESEDENTED!!!"

RL:"This is LA revisited!"

[More officers pour out of the back, pulling rabid fans from the ring and quickly cuffing them. Even more guards filter through the crowd, preventing more fans from entering the ring area. In the ring Max fights on, finally tossing out a few guards. He charges at Wild Dog, who is being held by officers, and drops him with two stiff punches.]

FJ:"THIS IS PANDEMONIUM!!!"

[Max grabs the tazor and begins buzzing the remaining officers. He then shoves the tazor into Wild Dog's mouth. Dog convulses, then flips over the top rope.]

FJ:"MAX IS THE ONLY MAN STANDING!!"

[The crowd is going ballistic.]

FJ:"THERE HE GOES!!"

[Max dives out of the ring and jumps over the guard railing. He forces his way through the fans, and out of site.]

RL:"I guess this one is over."

KAANEN THORNE VERSE STAZZ
