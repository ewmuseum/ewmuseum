Damian — CHZ roster card, 2002

16 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A roster card of the CHZ’s 2002 site, from the operator’s own site backup: the card’s own data file, field by field, in the site’s own order, followed by its biography and quote files where they exist. The site’s own display script supplied the field labels and is not reproduced; the card’s out-of-character handler block is left out whole. The date is the day its data file was last written.
singles Damian Philadelphia, Pennsylvania 6 foot 5 inches 241 'Scorched Earth Erotica' by Cradle of Filth Welcome to Hell Immediately after landing the 'God is Dead,' Damian lifts them to their feet and places on the top rope he climbs up with them and lifts them in a crucifix powerbomb position. He leaps high into the air, and throws them forward landing a crucifix piledriver Heel Samantha Damian Allen Wesley January 22, 1982 None None Innovative/Sadistic See Below None None PWA, NTICW None God is Dead Damian whips the victim into the ropes, he runs at them at full force as they come back and lands a huge double footed handstand kick to their chin Samantha Full Name: Samantha Sarah Jezebel Birthdate: January 22, 1982 Hometown: Philadelphia, Pennsylvania Height: 5'8' Weight: 122 lbs. Entrance Music: 'ATTAK' by KMFDM Fall From Grace (The opponent is laying on the mat. (it does not really matter whether they are on their back or not but usually will be) Damian will climb to the top rope and leap into the air doing a shooting star press. Once the SSP is completed Damian will quickly do a frog splash.) Death From Below (Usually used as a counter. Damian will be lying on his back. The victim will be standing and facing Damian. Damian will tilt backwards so that he is on his shoulders with his legs in the air. He will spring into the air, wrapping his legs around the victim's neck as if going for a hurricanrana, spin so that he is now facing the same direction as the victim and fall backwards slamming the victim onto the top of their head.) The Prayer (Damian will be facing the victim. He will run to the ropes which are behind the victim, perform a second rope springboard moonsault and land a DDT onto the victim.) The Crucifixion (Damian's victim will be laying face down on the mat. Damian will hook his opponent's right leg with his left arm, then turn over into a single leg Boston crab, rotate into a reverse leg lock (like an S.T.F.), pull his victim's arms back, and raise them off the mat as if they are hung on a cross.) Alter of Sacrifice (Damian will lock the victim's arms between the top and bottom ropes so that they are trapped and facing the crowd. Damian will vault to the top rope and perform a rocker dropper which wrenchs their neck and shoulders.) Decadence (Damian climbs to the top rope while the victim is laying back down on the mat. Damian will perform a moonsault with a 360 degree corkscrew so that he lands stomach first onto the victim.) Despair (Damian lifts the victim into a crucifix powerbomb. Damian slings them forward and catches them with a DDT.) Dream to Nightmare (Damian climbs to some elevated surface and performs a 720 degree corkscrew into an inverted bulldog.) Face Crusher (Damian will be handed his hammer by Samantha. He will hook the victim in a sleeper while he places the hammer under their nose. He will lean himself backwards and the victim forwards before thrusting forward with all his might, shattering the victim's nose.) Demise (Damian will lift the victim in a fireman's carry. He will then release their arms and swing the around so that it looks as if he is going to land a diamond cutter but instead whips them forward into a fall forward inverted DDT. Damian will then stand, not releasing the victim's head and flip forward into a neck wrench.) Principle of Evil (Damian will pull the victim to the top rope while he is up there as well. He will lock their head under his arm and perform a 360 Degree Tornado DDT landing on the floor outside the ring.) Hand Made Flesh (Simple yet deadly. Damian will wrap his hand around the victim's face and pull his fingers down slowly ripping and tearing the flesh as he goes.) Dusk Arrives (Damian will wrap his arms around the victim's throat before spinning around and slamming them to the mat with full force.) The Black Goddess Rises (Damian will unleash a series of kicks and punches which push the victim to the turnbuckle. Once they reach it Samantha (whom is standing on the apron waiting) will begin to gnaw on their neck.) Hell Awaits (Damian will lift the victim onto his shoulders in a fireman's carry. He will position himself on one side of the ring before running towards the other. He will leap over the top rope, and flip forward landing the victim in a samoan drop. Usually threw a table.) The Tortured End (Damian locks in a full nelson. He sits himself and the victim down before locking on a legs scissors and pushing forward with all his might.) Unholy Ground (Damian throws the victim from the ring. He climbs to the top rope and leaps into the air performing a 900 cross body or splash suicida.) Morning Star (Damian throws the victim from the ring before performing a springboard shooting star suicida.) God Can't Save You Now (Damian runs at the standing opponent, does a forward roll, and leaps at them with a fall forward lariat. Insomnia (Damian climbs to the top rope and performs a corkscrew body press.) Mark of the Beast (Damian slings the victim stomach down over his shoulder with their head facing the same direction as him. He bounces off the ropes, and releases them dropping in a diamond cutter.) "'Are you afraid?', 'Welcome to my own personal Hell.'"
