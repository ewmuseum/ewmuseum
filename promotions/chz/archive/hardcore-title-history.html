CHZ Hardcore Title History

March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is One of the per-title reign histories the fed’s own Belt History page links to, as it stood in the operator’s own site backup (the March 2002 layer). The ledger runs through the fed’s predecessor and merger years — FWF and SWF — exactly as the fed itself kept it.

The Count (1) 8/1/99 - 12/13/99 4M, 12D CHZ PPV Caged Fury 1 Defeated 3 opponents in a Hell in the Cell, casket, burning ropes, buried alive match which is now known as "The Translyvania Hardcore Match". (1) Staz 12/13/99 - 12/17/99 14D

SWF Smackdown Staz defeated the "King of Transylvania Hardcore" to become the first ever SWF HardKore Champion. Freddy Landstrom 12/17/99 - 1/24/00 1M, 7D SWF Raw "The Butcher" defeated Staz in one of his first SWF matches. The Butcher retired soon afterwards. Prodigy 1/24/00 - 1/29/00 5D SWF Smackdown Prodigy became the second Outlawz member to wear the SWF Hardkore Title Belt. He defeated "The Butcher" in what can only be called a woodshed match. T-Money (1) 1/29/00 - 2/21/00 23D SWF Raw T-Money, a high flying member of BlaQ Inc., defeated the insane one to bring Gold back to the BlaQ Inc. family. T-Money (2) 2/21/00 - 3/13/00 20D SWF PPV Chaos After taking Country Killer to a no decision, effectively vacating the title, T-Money easily defeated the "Big Bad Redneck" at Chaos, becoming the first 2 time Hardkore Champion. Ren O’ The Blade 3/13/00 - 5/26/00 2M, 13D SWF Raw Ren O' The Blade became the third Outlawz member to become Hardkore Champion by defeating T-Money in one of the final SWF events. VACANT 5/26/00 - 11/26/00 ??? ??? SWF closed for 6 months. The Count (2) 11/26/00 - 1/28/01 2M, 2D SWF Inferno The legendary Count captured his first SWF gold by destroying T-Money in a Transylvania Hardkore match. JT Fringe 1/28/01 - 3/5/01 2M, 7D SWF PPV Psycho Circus The "NY Assassin" JT Fringe challenged The Count to a Transylvania Hardkore Match. After a blast from a fire extinguisher, Fringe escaped with the title. (2) Don Rossi 3/5/01 - 4/1/01 26D SWF Inferno A gruessome triple threat match betwen Rossi, Barbwire, and Havoc, ended when Rossi was able to execute the Game Over on Barbwire and pick up the pin. The Count (3) 4/1/01 - 9/21/01 5M, 20D SWF PPV Total Chaos SWF Total Chaos saw a tremendous triple threat Hardkore Championship Match. The Count managed to escape with the title after some unexpected help from Thor. (3) Diablo 9/21/01 - 11/14/01 1M, 23D CHZ WILD Diablo beat friend and partner Fire to pick up the CHZ Hardcore Title. Extreme Chancelz 11/14/01 - 1/24/02 2M, 10D CHZ WILD Extreme Chancelz beat fellow Team Extreme member Diablo to get his 1st CHZ title. Black Dragon 1/24/02 - 2/27/02 1M, 3D CHZ WILD Black Dragon returned after a 6 month absence and in his second match upset EC for the Hardcore strap. Lucifer 2/27/02 - 3/06/02 7D CHZ PPV No Luv Lucifer bested The Count and Black Dragon at the CHZ's February PPV. Madd Maxx 3/06/02 - PRESENT ??? CHZ WILD Madd Maxx wins a killer match. For his first CHZ Hardcore Title.

FOOTNOTES 1 : The Count was allowed to carry his Hardcore Title reign from the CHZ, FWF mergers. 2 : Fringe relinquished the Hardcore Title upon winning the SWF HW Title 3 : The Count was kept as a non-fighting title holder until a Finals could be held in the new CHZ
