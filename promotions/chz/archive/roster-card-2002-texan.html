singles Angel The Texan Summers Houston,Texas 5'11 145 Bloodbrothers by Papa Roach 'The Texan Surprise' Back Flip from top ropes on to dazed opponent Neutral Angel Summers 03/01/78 Tex,The Texan,The Texan Cowgirl High Flyer Top Rope Jump, Leg Drop,Low Blow, Ankle Lock None None PWA None Spinning Side Walk Slam A original side walk slam, but with a spin {{--History Bio On Angel “The Texan” Summers--}} On 1978, February 25th, The life of Angle Summers was taken into place at the Houston local church. Angel was the third of five children that were all conceived by Ron and Cathy Summers, and Angel was the only girl of them all. So being the only girl, she was edged by her parents to act like a proper women, which included, no sports, no fighting and no swearing. Angel spent most of her summers selling lemonade on the street corner, while her brothers were off playing sports and hanging out. Angel had her best friend always along side her on at the lemonade stands; they were best friends since they were both infants. This life became a bore to Angel at the age of ten; she became more in tuned with her brother’s style of life. Meaning she wanted to be athletic, and live more like a man. So she pleaded to her parents to let her play sports, after weeks of her father discrete about the subject, she was allowed every weekend, going over to her grandfathers ranch to participate in horseback riding. She loved this, as most young girls should. She took these lessons all the way up to her teens, at this time era she became interested in competition other then lessons, so she began participating in some events. She had a particular horse she loved at her grandfather’s ranch named Meadow, a complete black shade, but around his right eye; he had a white shade. Angel developed her talent from this horse, so by the time she was sixteen she had bought the horse, so she can legally say she owns Meadow. She always placed first in the women competitions, so she advanced to the men’s competitions and always placed top three, beating some of her brothers on some occasions, which stirred up problems in the summers home. She was always able to hold her end with her brothers, as they loved to push her around, but later in life it was much harder. She was a beautiful blonde haired beauty, slim fit and athletic, which usually means trouble with the other males loving her, but she was always divided her from the others from her school, as she spent most of her free time at the ranch with her grandparents. But she always felt all right with that, as she never really spent much attention on popularity since her best friend Laura moved at the age of thirteen. When she was sixteen it became a rough time for her, the beginning of that year her grandmother passed, and at the end of that year her grandfather passed, both from long cancer, as they both smoked heavily since they were young. The only good that came out of this was she and her family was given the ranch in the will, which forced them to move from the city of Houston to the outskirts of Houston. She had always loved the ranch, as it was like a ranch you would see in a movie. Everything circled the house; the house was a beautiful gray brick home, with a red tiled roof. And directly in front of the house was the fields where the horses were rode, and the barn, where the horses slept in the evening was to the left of the house. Behind the house was the shed where the supplies were stored for the horses, such as food and grains. And to the left was the tool shed. The two older brothers, Chris and Matt, were already moved out years ago. The other two younger brothers were not thrilled with the move as they were moving farther away from there friends from the city, which now would be a half hour drive to get to school. Angel’s parents Ron and Cathy were thrilled with the move along with Angel. The whole town knew Angel Summers as “Tex” as she was a true Texan, loving the country Texan life, and riding her horse’s day in and day out. At the age of eighteen, Tex discovered a talent in kickboxing, which she signed up for at school. She was always feisty being the only girl in the family, and being in a very slim, but yet muscular fit she developed very easily in the competitions, in fact she placed second in her first meet. She was scouted early by the academy for boxing, which was the step below big times in women boxing, although it was a different type of fighting, she developed quite well in the event always placing top three at first and eventually becoming first on a steady basis. She also graduated from high school that year. She was always a smart girl, so she moved on to University at Lincoln University in Houston. Which worked out good because Lincoln was more in the middle of the city and the ranch. While majoring in business management at Lincoln, she continued her goals in boxing, and riding her horses daily. Houston only had one wrestling federation in the town, which was a pretty big federation called World Extreme Wrestling (WEW). Tex dropped the boxing bit as it was becoming easy, and she had joined WEW originally to be sidekick of a big time wrestler named Biff Briggs. Her duties were just to look cute, walk down the ring, and interfere the off time when Biff was losing. Tex finally got her break when she was asked to participate in a match of her own, which was against the women’s champion of the federation, Sid. The original match was called a draw, due to interference of other wrestlers, but the follow up match, Tex was the victor of the match. She started to become known as an individual other then Biffs sidekick. Many people became to love this woman. As many nick names were developed, such as, “The Texan, Tex and the Texan cowgirl. As she was given that gimmick, coming to the ring with black leather pants, silver belt, with cowgirl boots, a brown cowgirl vest, with just a black bra underneath, and of course her black cowgirl hat. Plus she entered the ring with brown rope around her shoulder for the gimmick, but never really used the rope in any matches. Her beauty was the main attraction; many have not seen beauty and skill in the same category for a long time. She participated in this federation up to the age of twenty-two. This was the last year of her University career, so she wanted to focus on school for a year. She graduated at the age twenty-three, and opened a bar called The Texan. It was a success from the start. Tex and her two younger brothers Roger and Ted, were the owners of the bar and their father was the bartender along with their mother. The older brothers were the bouncers and guard type workers, as this developed into a family business. Although it was 25 minutes out of town many people went out of their way to come to the bar. She focused on the bar for a year, then recently stepped down to resume her career in wrestling, as her agent, Jim Walker, contacted her, and got her a job in the Prime Time Wrestling Academy, the federation before the big times. This federation will teach her what she needs to do to achieve a roll in the big time federations. She plans to go by the name of “The Texan” as in the previous federation. And with the same gimmick, being the Beautiful Cowgirl. She has decided to put her all into her body, working out and doing what she has to do, to be a star. Her family is behind her 100%, and will look after her bar for the time being. "If it ain't broke.....Dont fix it!" "'Why be defeated? When you can defeat!'"
