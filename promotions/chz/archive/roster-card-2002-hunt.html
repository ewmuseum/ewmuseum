When the beat of the song kicks in, pyros and fireworks are set off from the top of the video screen and on either side of the entracne way and after a few minutes, Matt Hunt appears through the cloud of smoke. The different colours whizz around the arena as The Postman makes his slow approach to the ring, while glancing around the arena cautiously and disgruntled. He ascends up the steel steps and walks across the apron scanning the crowd, after which he marches towards the nearest turnbuckle and sits on the top of it, waiting for his opponent to enter the squared circle. Character/Gimmick Matt isn't a real postman (duh), nor does he dress like one. It's basically an old school nickname, which he has developed. However, this is an obvious tongue-in-cheek swipe at the British Postal Service, which is often critized for it's poor standard of providing a service to the British Public. In Matt's words, he is the opposite to the British Postal Service; efficent, straight to the point and precise - as he displays in his performances He's also arrogant, proving that he may have the biggest mouth the CHZ! Matt also has a comedic side. He'll often poke fun at opponents/foes by embarassing them, purposely getting their name wrong and making a mockery of something they said. He'll deliberately get under his opponents skin and take the short cut to presses their buttons. With Matt Hunt also one of the Commissioners, who knows what type of new aura he will bring in...there goes the federation? "I'm not a REAL Postman..." "Hey camache..." "Let the games begin..." "I'm unique cos I'm a freak, camache!"
