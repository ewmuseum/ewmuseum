tagteam Hollywood Assassins Robbie McMasters Miami, Florida 6'4 239 Chris Starr Miami, Florida 6'7 255 Thunderstruck-AC/DC 'Final Assassination' 2-man Powerslam Neutral None Hollywood Assassins Ankle Lock,Leg drop,Choke Slam,Bodyslam,Powerbomb,Backbreaker,Reverse Piledriver, High Flying Leaps None None PWA None Shot to mid-section kick to the stomach History Lesson On Robbie McMasters and Chris Starr {-Begin Feed-} Childhood On March 1st, 1975 in Miami’s St. Mary’s hospital a star was born. Robert McMasters entered the world. He was a smart boy as he started kindergarten at the age 3 because of the progress he was having. The years went on and Robbie at a young age became very self involved. It was because he got everything he wanted, His parents always told him he was the most important in the world, He was given a lot of encouragement as a boy and grew into a self involved person. In High School he was oh so popular with the guys and especially with the ladies. Was top in his school in grades. He was involved in a few sports such as Rugby, hockey and of course wrestling. He placed first in North America under high school boards, plus he wrestled under middle-weight division and graduated at the age of 18 from Ridge Monte High. As he was off to college to major in law, mainly to have a secondary job if wrestling had failed him. (College and RUF Days) Robbie McMasters started at a college called Birmingham University, six months after he had graduated from High School and begins to peruse his law degree so he can become a cop someday. He did not forget his dreams of becoming a Pro-Wrestler, but he did want to think of things in the positive direction. If he did not succeed as a Pro-Wrestler, he could always have a law degree to fall back on. Robbie made a 91% average his first semester of the college year, plus he also had his job on the side, working as a wrestler for an Inter State Federation called the . Robbie made it halfway through college, and he had made a lot of fans through the RUF. He wrestled with ease at this rate of competition. The promoter of the RUF also told Robbie, that he was top quality and he did not deserve to wrestle for the likes of an organization as the RUF, but Robbie would only shake his head and laugh at the sound of what was coming out of the promoter’s mouth. The time had come for the day of Robbie’s college graduation and the promoter of the RUF had a little of a graduation gift in order for Robbie. He had gotten Robbie a contract to a largely known wrestling organization in Las Vegas. Robbie was so thrilled that he didn't even pay any mind to what was enclosed in the contract and just signed to the deal. What Robbie didn't know was that he had just signed a contract for three years of wrestling at only one hundred dollars a match. After reading the whole contract Robbie was seriously pissed and wanted to just rip the contract into a million pieces, but he also learned that it was an ironclad contract and that there was no negotiating on him leaving. Orlando Federation (VWF) On the day of July 30, 1998, Robbie got on a plane at the Charles Yeager Memorial Airport in Miami, Florida. He had already started his career as a VWF wrestler when he set foot on that plane. As soon as Robbie got seated on the plane he found out that he had been seated next to a man that had also fallen into the same trick that Robbie had been. The man went by the name of Chris Starr. Robbie and Chris hit it off from the start and decided that they would rather team together in the VWF than they would want the bookers to just place them with some rookie wrestler. The two were on their journey to the land of the rising sun and there was nothing much they could do about it. They were stuck in Vegas for three years of worthless wrestling. Robbie and Chris arrived in Japan the next day and went to meet the promoter for VWF. Robbie and Chris regretted signing those contracts without reading, since the moment they arrived in Vegas, but regretted it even more after their first match. After about a year and a half Robbie and Chris were finally getting the hang of the native tongue and were actually winning a lot of matches. At times even two or three per night. At the time, they were enjoying the little that they had. But on December 27, 1998 a promoter for the UWL (Ultimate Wrestling League) from America had been in the crowd for a couple of Robbie and Chris’s matches, and had talked the VWF scouts into letting him take them back to America with him to wrestle for the UWL. Robbie and Chris were both very happy about this. UWL One the day of their arrival into the UWL the two had learned that the UWL was a very big major federation in the states and that they had two televised shows a week and a monthly pay-per-view. They had given Chris the gimmick of being the wane-be African-American named FUBU and they let him start wrestling the night after his contract signing. Robbie on the other hand was given a while off and in the process of this time off they were using him as a human genie pig on some new muscle enhancing pills that were supposed to be safe and non-addictive. The pills on the other hand did what they said, but they also made Robbie huge. He was even more pumped then what he was. He may have been huge, but he was only in the Lightweight Division. Robbie was given the gimmick of local show off and an American Beauty. Which still sticks with him today. Robbie had finally been given a chance in the UWL and he was booked in a Television Title match right off the bat. What the bookers didn't know was that Robbie was to win the match and that he would become the Television champion the first day he was on the show. Robbie went on to obtain the UWL Television Title, on two separate occasions. The Tag Team Titles on one occasion, with his former stable mate Black heart. And he held the number one contender ship of the World Heavyweight Title, but on the night of his Title match the UWL had went bankrupt, and could not afford to do anything, but cancel the show and close down the federation. Robbie was left with billions and no job. Free Agent and CWA During most of 1999 Robbie was left without a job and had to stay in Penthouse in Miami. Robbie was stuck in his home with no job, his friend Chris and plenty of women, no family and a little bit of a mind reaming. He then was more determined then every to get back into the ring and found a home in the CWA (Championship Wrestling Alliance). There he stayed until the day that it closed. This brought him in even more millions. And also helped him achieve the CWA Extreme Title on two separate occasions, brought him together with his friend John again and him and Chris held the Tag Team titles on one occasion, and then Robbie went solo to claim his first World Heavyweight Title reign ever. After the night of Robbie winning the title the federation went under and shut down. It officially shut down on the day of October 30, 2000. He was a Millionaire without a job yet again. On the day of December 17, 2000 Robbie makes his debut on live television, once again. After being off of air and out of wrestling for almost 3 months, he returns to wrestling and joins up with a new wrestling federation called Federal World Order, Feared Wrestling Organization. Not only did he make a big bang back into the sport, but it was at a Pay-Per-View and it was the Feared Wrestling Organization’s first. Robbie was signed under a six-month contract with the Feared Wrestling Organization. And plans to claim his own stable in it. In a short time there now he has formed a dominating group with long time friend Chris Starr, and a new comer Heat, he has also made connections with Rims G, and has plans on taking out the Feared Wrestling Organization President, and his White Blood goons. Robbie also won the Feared Wrestling Organization King of Extreme 2001 title, at the January Pay-Per-View on the 21st. Time slowly goes by for Robbie here in the Feared Wrestling Organization, but it is also a prosperous time, he has gained the Middleweight Title in a match against Heat. And he also won the United States Heavyweight Title in the Over the Top Rope Elimination match. Plus the unthinkable happened, Robbie and Dan turned on his loyal friends to go and become one of the President's lackeys, as a member of the White Bloods. On the night of February 13, 2001, Robbie tosses down both of his titles. In hope of a shot at the Feared Wrestling Organization World Heavyweight Title, somewhere down the road. The days of Robbie McMasters begin in the Feared Wrestling Organization were finally becoming numbered, for this talented young star. On the site of the Feared Wrestling Organization third Pay-Per-View. The Great American Nightmare, Robbie’s friend Todd 'The 1 Man Army' Stratton made his debut and Robbie and Dan. And Todd started to take control, during Todd's first Feared Wrestling Organization match he captured the Hardcore Title, but things started to go down hill from there. Todd was placed in another match where he was forced to job to Psycho, and lose the Hardcore Title. Afterwards the Feared Wrestling Organization creative team decided that Robbie, Dan and Todd were a bit to over rated and were not getting over with the fans. So they were pretty much just placed on a shelf until the day of March 29, 2001. On that day, the two were seen in New York City, N.Y. where they both ripped up their contracts, and became Free Agents once again. DWF Up To Now! Then as time pasted, Robbie stayed in touch with longtime friend Chris Starr, but lost touch with new friend Todd Stratton. This is where he once again became self involved, felt no one was good enough to be his friend except another self involved individual named Chris Starr. They both managed to be scouted into a great fed, called the DWA. The DWA was at the top of the charts in the g-federation world. After this they would step into e-federation waters. They were successful as a tag team again, creating their own gimmick, self centered, good-looking pricks, Hollywood types. But somehow they were still loved by the fans, because of their skill and his respect for them. They became well known when they won their first titles, which was the tag team titles. Which was first of many for the young stars in DWA. Around 3 months into the DWA, they were called to the PWA for a try-out. To enter a school, which will help you progress, it was a chance to show what you got to the bigger and better. Only time will tell what Young Robbie McMasters and Chris Starr have in store for them. {-End-} "' NOw before we begin the match..We will accept a forfeit to save time!'"
