tagteam Flying Vergomovs 'The Russian Son' Igor Arefiev Moscow, Russia 5' 7 120 'The Russian Star' Constantino Zergolov Moscow, Russia 5' 8 128 'When The Circus Comes To Town' by Phish Zemganno Legend 450 spalsh ending in a leg drop by both men. Heel Olga Zergolov Everything high-flying... None.. yet! None.. OSW OSW Most Extreme Team Finalists Doubel Superkick Double Superkick Constantino Zergolov was born on May 26th, 1976 to the parents of Ivan and Maria. Constantino, from an early age, was an adventerous child. He was not amused by simple things, like other Russian children. He wanted more. He was a showman from a young age. Two years after that birth, came Igor Areifev, on January 23rd 1978. He was a small child and very shy about himself. His parents, Thomas and Olga were both circus performers and wanted their son to follow suit. Igor was an ealry entrant into the Moscow School of Circus and Variety Arts, joining two years earlier than normal and meeting a young Constantino Zergolov. The two never really knew each other. They trained together on a number of occasions, but both were destined to be successes and friendship’s in school were of no importance. Both young men, graduated with flying colours literally and soon joined a prestige outfits. Igor worked in Amercia, travelling throughout cities, were as Constantino was far more travelled. He worked with the Wallenda and Vargo families, two of the world’s finest names. Igor then joined The Medinkov, a popular high wire act, and he toured the world as Constantino stayed with the Vargo’s as his interest in a certain female grew. Constantino finally, married that female, Olga Vargo, soon to be named Olga Zergolov. Igor continued to impress with his own act, “La Nouba.” Constantino performed on numerous occasions in front of many impotant people, but then the two crossed paths again. Igor and Constantino met at a charity event, with many other of the world’s top names. At this event, both men were instated to the elite group of circus performers, known as “The Brothers Zemganno.” In this group, Igor and Constantino learned more of each other, but still never became close. At a second event, where they combined together for their first public appearance as a team, the two Russian’s thinking met. Both Igor and Constantino loved the circus life, but wanted more. The wanted the interest to retun to circus, they wanted the money of the top athletes of the country.... The two men wanted to be stars! They decided among themselves to enter the wrestling ring. The Flying Vergomovs were born as a wrestling team. The pair went straight to the Old School Wrestling Most Extreme Team Competition. After two weeks in the competition however, the pair were elimanated after not submiting a match plan soon enough. They then decided to head to PWA, the academy of the Wrestlingtattler federations. Almost immediately, the pair were sent to CHZ, the development federation. The place they finds themselves today... "The glory days of circus is coming!" "Can you do this?"
