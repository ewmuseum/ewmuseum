Theo Knox Real Name: Theodore Knox Birthdate: February 14th, 1981 Hometown: New Orleans, Lousiana Height: 6'4 Weight: 230 lbs. Nicknames: Theo, Ted, Knox, 'Cream of the Crop', 'The Ragin Cajun' Status: Neutral Manager(s): N/A Current Teams: N/A Entrance Music: 'Southern Hopitality' Ludacris Wrestling Style: All round amatuer with advanced submissibve skills Signature Moves: Southern Discomfort {Body press slam into a falling neck breaker and keeps the hold around the opponent's neck where he locks in a rear chin lock.} Dead End {360 degree Death Valley Driver into turnbuckle} Stroke of Brilliance {Reverse flapjack, leaving the opponent on his stomach, Theo then applies a figure four pin} Bayou Bomb {Sit-down scoop slam} Set Up Move: Proper procedure (A rolling leg lock after irish whipping the opponent into the ropes ) Finisher: 'Tell me about it' (An extended lion tamer submission where Theo stands on the head of his opponent for extra leverage.) CHZ Title History: N/A CHZ Award History: N/A Other Federations: (Handler) ICWA, WAWF, VWF Other Federation Awards: (ICWA) Extreme Title, Tag Team Titles (by himself), First and last Intercontinental Champion, World Champion (WAWF) Cruiserweight Champion, World Champion Bio Born on February 14th, 1981 Theodore Knox {now 21} the son of Patricia and Joel, endured a lifetime of anything but love. While born in New Orleans, Louisiana and being of distant Afrikaner decent, Theo and his two younger sisters, Ashley {18} and Elizabeth {16} were raised in a single parent home by their mom, who as a result of her husband leaving them induced upon herself the blame and guilt. Because Theo was the oldest, he became the butt of most of her anger and now suffers severe depression because of this. He also takes anti-depressants now as a result. When his mother died in 1997, at age 16 he and his sisters were devastated, him more than the his sisters and they were forced to move into their local homeless shelter. These were when the dreams started. During the seemingless endless nights on the cold cots of the shelter Theo would visualize various things, most commonly the reenactment of his mother's death. The organizers of the shelter prescribed Theo severe hallucinogenic medicine that calms and soothes all parts of the brain until he is running on only his will. This makes him able to perform in the ring. {Presently} Either for going long periods of time without the medicine or directly after. After living with nothing and witnessing his sisters torture at being poor and homeless. At age 19, Theo had a total of 3 odd jobs which finally provided him with enough money to leave the shelter and into their own house, and allow his sisters to finish up school like he did. Though his only exposure to professional wrestling were on the select nights that the men chose what they all were to watch on their sole television screen, with their shelter's loans and extra cash Theo saved up enough money for a plane ticket and was on his way to Canada to try out for the CHZ. And was accepted. His in-ring is shaky, but his ring presence and charisma is always strong. Submissive skills were gained from older men around the shelter who knew the ride that he was in for within his lifetime. If anything, Theo looks to make the crowd laugh and reach that one little kid in the homeless shelter who doesn't have anything else to admire or hold dear. His motivation and source of drive are his family, friends, and fans.
