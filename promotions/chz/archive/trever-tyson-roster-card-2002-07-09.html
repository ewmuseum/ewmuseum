Trever Tyson Real Name: Trever 'Eskimo' Tyson Birthdate: January 15 1975 Hometown: Old Crow ,Canada in the Yukon Height: 6'0 Weight: 234 lbs. Nicknames: ESKIMO, Mr cool beans Status: Neutral Manager(s): Gimp The sled dog Current Teams: Eskimo &Sled Dog Gimp (Eskimo Pie!) Entrance Music: 'I'm the only gay Eskimo' By corky and the juice pigs Wrestling Style: Technical, Signature Moves: side Canadian leg sweep, Ice Pick(Scoop tombstone piledriver, Cold Shoulder(Diving shoulder block) Canadian back breaker submission, Eskimo drop(Manhatten drop,), Pedelum backbreaker Set Up Move: Eskimo Kiss (Blue mist spit into opponents face ) Finisher: Frost Bite (Crippler Cross face) CHZ Title History: None CHZ Award History: None Other Federations: None Other Federation Awards: None Bio As a little boy of the age of 6 Trever Tysons parents dumped him off in the woods of the Yukon leaving him for dead. Few days passed and Trever relized he was alone, he knew his name and that was neough for him. He learned to fend for himself living off the land and evntually becoming an ESKIMO! One day while he was out fishing for food around the age of 27 the ice under him broke and he fell to his death. Amazingly enough he was able to escape but was permanetly altered by the cold. His hair froze and his skin turned a pal blue. His eyes turned a deep ice blue and his mentality became that of a beast. He sharpened his teeth into razors and began picking fights with such animals as bears and wolves in which he killed with his hands and teeth. At the age of 28 he made his way back into civilization in which he read a flyer offering try outs for CHZ. He knew how tuff he was so he applied and they excepted him from just the way he looked. He has come here to reek havoc on the wrestling world but be introduced to the civilized world as well. Promo Archives Gimp Molested by Brent and Diablo WHAT! Quotes "Eskimo Kiss Your Ass Good Bye!"
