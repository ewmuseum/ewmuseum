Heels and Faces alike respect Big Boy, not because of his past experience but because even at 60. he might still knock them on their ass. Sasha Edwards Position: -Interview Reporter / PR Agent Age: 24 Fresh out of college this 5'8 113lb blonde bombshell not only got her job in June 2001 with her good looks but also because she was dating CHZ wrestler JT Fringe. However, JT Fringe's infamous attack on Mad Max and kidnapping of Matt Helms' children were too much for Sasha to take. She turned on Fringe despite her fear and caused his downfall, ending in Mad Max almost killing Fringe at a CHZ taping. Her gentile attitude and love for all things, has formed a friendship with the slightly crazy Mad Max...although could it be more than friendship? Sasha not only handles extensive personal interviews seen in the CHZ magazine and on CHZ Weds. Night Wild. She also works hand in hand with Rick Dudley on CHZ merchandise. Sasha also sets up press releases before CHZ PPVS. She truly is the elbow grease that keeps CHZ churning. Melvin "FLAP JACKS" Murphy Position: Play by Play Announcer Age: 30 Murphy has been a wrestling fan for many years, his earliest memory was being a teenager in Upstate NY watching Andre The Giant come out in the RPI Fieldhouse in Troy, NY. He was on Mid-Mezzanine and Andre came out from the concourse and was looking him in the eye. Every since then Murphy has been enarmored with the square circle. A Communications Major from Siena College jump started him in the career of sports. He bounced from Minor League Hockey and Baseball until finally getting his shot in June 2001 as Play by Play Announcer for the CHZ. He respects admirable sportsmanship so therefore he usually sides with faces and tweeners who do not break the rules. Rex Likely - Color Commentary George Wilson Position: Head Referee Age: 50 A veteran of the squared circle. Wilson has been with CHZ since it's founding in 1999. He is usually the one called upon during comlicated matches, since his knack for avoiding wrestlers and being at the right spot at the right time is unmatched in pro wrestling. Although he is consistent, Wilson will let some things go based on the match's importance. Will not DQ at the drop of a hat. Ty Finkle Position: Mid-Card referee Age: 28 Started in June 2001 with the CHZ's new opening. Hired on part of George Wilson's recommendation. Before the CHZ was trying to become a NFL official. Misses some things in the ring but for the most part, knows the rules and plays by the book more than Wilson, so will be more prone to DQ with only one warning. His specialty are Hardcore Matches and matches that move from one area to another, since he is younger, more agile and has more stamina than the older Wilson. Jeff Marsupial Position: Open Card Referee Age: 22 A former CHZ wrestler by the name of SteelJaw who was partner to Noble (now Cellrock's chaffeur). Jeff wasn't in the league of the CHZ's superstars and begged for another position in the company. However, his piss poor attitude since then greatly affects his refeering. He sides with heels and his 3 count is based on personal feelings, using his position as a source of a small power trip. He would have been fired by now, but somehow Noble keeps him employed by begging Cellrock when Jeff screws up.
