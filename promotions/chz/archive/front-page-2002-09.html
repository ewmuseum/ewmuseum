CHZ Front Page, September 2002

11 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The front page of the CHZ’s 2002 site as its news script held it in the operator’s own site backup: the 11 September 2002 announcement of the fed’s sale, then the teasers the fed ran for Mind Games and the Wilds of 28 and 21 August. Each item’s date is the fed’s own news script’s.
CHZ SOLD!!! That's right, CHZ CEO, El Diablo has hereby sold CHZ to former NTICW President, Mick Palmer! When CHZ reporters caught up with Mick Palmer after the signing over of the federation was complete and the money transactions happened, he had this to say...

"El Diablo wanted to get out of CHZ for some reason. He's in PCW wrestling and I guess he found it hard to run a developmental federation to PCW. Oh well, what's one man's trash is another's treasure. Expect things to change around here with myself in charge, now I don't have to answer to anyone like I did back in NTICW to God AKA Evan Levine. Yep, I'm revealing his name to the world, God was Evan Levine!"

After that shocking announcement, he had more to say:

"My first order of business is to announce that the President of CHZ is now Alex Mann, who worked with myself on NTICW. And my second order of business is to strip all champions of their titles. We'll have a fresh start."

When asked about when the first show under the new regime would start Mick had this to say...

"The first show will be within a week on next Wednesday, the show name and all will stay the same, so not much is changing around here under my control."

What will happen in the Palmer era? Who knows!?
Everything Gets Revealed- Really?

Toronto, Ontario- Mind Games was to be the first CHZ PPV in a long while and the first PPV under it's developmental status, would the PPV be a success? All reports say yes, but you be the judge!

Did the expected arrivals of the dual Commissioners play any part in Mind Games? Who would bleed first between Stimpo and Harrington? How did Sesame Streets Bert and Ernie appear on CHZ television?

Catastrophes occurred in more ways that one this night, but where, when and who was involved in them?

And finally who would be crowned the first CHZ World Heavyweight Champion? And who would have the last laugh by the nights end?

Of course this is just a teaser, click the "PPV Results" link to find out what happened.
The Road Home

Montreal, Quebec- Only one word could sum up this night in Montreal- "Zany"

Wild was anything but ordinary tonight, with only four matches on the card and rumors of dual Commissioners arriving on the scene. Would the matches live up to the billing? And would the rumored Commissioners arrive tonight?

To add to that word "Zany" CHZ Owner El Diablo appeared on Wednesday Night Wild for the very first time, and perhaps the last?

Also, who would gain a shot at the CHZ Tag Team Titles? Who would Soul destroy? And just what the hell happened during and after the Tag Team Table Main Event?

For all the answers just click the "Results" link.
Brutal Decision

Vancouver, British Columbia- With a heavy schedule of three shows in seven days approaching, would the CHZ stars be relaxed and tame tonight? Of course not!

Wild became brutal with attack after attack and vengeance as well as redemption.

Who would attack who? What challenges were made? How seductive is Angel Summers? And just what is the stipulation for the CHZ World Championship Match at Mind Games? Or most important, who will be facing Schultz for that honor?

For the results go to the link on your left or at the top of the page.
