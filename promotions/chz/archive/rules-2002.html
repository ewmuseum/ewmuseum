CHZ Rules, 2002

CHZ Rules Statement

We have little tolerence for troublemakers, complainers and sore losers. We will fire you if you make a complaint, so the option is to not do it. This is an Angle based fed, and if you feel that you unjustly lost, then expect there to be some sort of Angle based reason.

If you are warned against a course of action from a member of CHZ's staff, refrain from said action. We do not warn people twice about the same infraction. A second infraction is likely to result in termination of your contract.

No Profanity, NC-17 Stuff or Racism

The RP Board here does not allow profanity. If you must, do it like this, (s&*t, f@#k, etc.). No sexual harassment or racism is allowed. You will be warned on your first violation. Continued violations may result in you being fired if someone takes offence to it. For some reason if a CHZ wrestler uses slang or "ghetto speak" then just treat the harder stuff as if it was profanity.

Regarding Wrestlers

This is a FAKE wrestler e-fed. No REAL wrestlers will be accepted. If you send in an application for a real wrestler, don't expect a response. Also, do not use any catchphrases from real wrestlers. We stress originality here.

If you want to make a tag team, fill out a singles application for each wrestler first, and state at the top of the bio portion that you wish to tag with the other application.

Roleplaying

Your success in the CHZ is based on your roleplays. Yes this is an Angle fed, but however the better your roleplays are, the more your angle will be pushed to the front of the card. While the quantity of your roleplays is important, it is not as important as the quality of your roleplays. A wrestler that has one great RP will almost always beat a wrestler who posted five mediocre RP's.

No PIC Bases in your RP's.

Period. This means no pictures of real wrestlers.

Your roleplays should be a minimum of 50 lines long, if not longer. You should also have a purpose for your RP's, whether it be to challenge another wrestler to a match, or simply to entertain. You must roleplay once a week, and you also must roleplay before every card you are scheduled to wrestle in. If you are unable for some reason to RP for an extended period of time, contact us. If you do not RP for an extended period of time, and do not contact us, you will be removed from CHZ.

It is in your best interests to read all the RP's on the RP Board, especially the posts from CHZ staff. You never know when you'll be challenged or when you might find an opportunity to get into an interesting angle. Plus, a lot of these will be pretty damn funny, so read if for no other reason than that.

You can not attack other or use other CHZ wrestlers in your RP's without their consent. If you do, you will job to that wrestler on the next card. You can attack reporters, announcers, and cameramen in your RP's, but make sure you're willing to face the consequences that could arise from such acts if you injure them.

Kidnapping, torturing, sexually abusing or the like to CHZ staff in your RP's is a violation of the rules and may carry serious penalties.

Cards and PPV's

There is one card every two weeks called Wild. Results usually appear the following morning/afternoon. If we take longer than expected for any reason, we will give you updates on the OOC Board. Do not ask staff "when the results will be up!" as you WILL BE IGNORED.

You must send in all your run-ins, RP's, interviews, etc., 11am. EST on the day of the card. RP deadlines are typically 12 midnight the day of the card, but sometimes are extended. Check the RP board for updates. There will be one Pay-Per-View every two months.

Spamming/Advertising

You may not advertise other efeds or interfeds on using our resources. If you do so, you will be fired immediately.
