What this is A roster card of the CHZ’s 2002 site, from the operator’s own site backup: the card’s own data file, field by field, in the site’s own order, followed by its biography and quote files where they exist. The site’s own display script supplied the field labels and is not reproduced; the card’s out-of-character handler block is left out whole. The date is the day its data file was last written.
singles Logan Cage Orlando, Florida 6'4' 254 'The Beginning is the End is the Beginning' by The Smashing Pumpkins Lethal Lockdown Guillotine Choke with Leg Scissors Face Logan Cage February 27, 1976 Submission/Shootfighting Leg Grapevine, Triangle Choke, Gargoyle Suplex, Head and Arm Suplex, Spear, Mounted Punches, Forearm Smash, Bow and Arrow Submission, Full Nelson Suplex, Short Arm Clothesline, Belly-to-Belly Suplex Lost Wages Release German Suplex While little is known about his upbringing or childhood background, Logan Cage's name has grown to become synonymous with the criminal underworkings of Las Vegas, Nevada. Hired in 1997 as a 'debt collector' for one of the Strip's premiere crime bosses, Cage was notorious for his unmerciful dealings with overdrawn debtors and any feeble excuses thrown his way. Supplimenting his income with earnings on the blacklisted pitfighting circuit, Cage is well-versed in the art of shootfighting and honed his craft against both his opponents and his boss's targets. In late February of 2002, after a dramatic realization of the moral implications of his actions, Cage attempted to break ties with his boss, only to endure a brutal firing that left him recovering in the hospital until May. Now determined to earn his money legally, and right many of his past wrongs, Cage has taken to the world of professional wrestling, with the hopes of using his skills for good......and one day extracting revenge on the men who created the monster he used to be.
