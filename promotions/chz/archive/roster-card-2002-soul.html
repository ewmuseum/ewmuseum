What this is A roster card of the CHZ’s 2002 site, from the operator’s own site backup: the card’s own data file, field by field, in the site’s own order, followed by its biography and quote files where they exist. The site’s own display script supplied the field labels and is not reproduced; the card’s out-of-character handler block is left out whole. The date is the day its data file was last written.
singles Soul Gary Indiana 6'3' 300 Aerial by system of a down Five Niner he gets them in a full nelson slam and as he has them in the air he flips over hitting a reverse powerbomb Face Kayla Samuel Oswald Unattus Lawrence 09-23-1979 Powerhouse, Technical Fallaway slam, spinebuster, huge powerbomb, gorilla bress slam, spinning powerbomb, top rope belly to belly suplex, German suplex, Inverted DDT, short arm clothesline, STF, superkick, Reverse DVD PWA Cryption Reverse brainbuster Samuel Oswald Untattus Lawrnce or as Soul which everyone calls him, was born In gary Indiana during time where gang warfare were at its heights. Though he lived by bad influences, he was not brought up in that kind of an manor. His mother and father were very loving in every way showing him unconditional love which was rare in Gary. Though corruption and drugs were around he was taught right from wrong at an early age and was quite smarter than other little boys at his age. At a very young age he had a dream of becoming a professonal wrestler and it was far off from the profession of Marine Biologist that his parents were. It seemed like nothing couldnt go wrong and his world was perfect but one day something terrible that changed him forever. It was during a blackout and they were having a family gathering when a couple of gun men busted into there home kill his parents in front of his eyes leaving him alone and scared. During the funeral, he found out that the people of the city did not think foundly of his parents and made fun him, his sister and parents espically a man that call Dr. Ross. From that point he had an intense hatred from that man and vowed that if they meet again his life will end. Over the years he had to survive on the streets seeing things that kids at his age should not see and at times the future did not look great. By seeing the hatred and no remorse his hatred was fueled off of it and it consumed his heart for a while until a fateful day he found his sister Kayla that made his heart soften and opens his eyes to the truth. With the two together once again, Soul looks to take CHZ by storm with his brutal style in the ring.
