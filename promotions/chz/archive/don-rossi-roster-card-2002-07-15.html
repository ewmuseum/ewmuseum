Don Rossi Real Name: Donald Richard Rossi 3 Birthdate: September 21, 1977 Hometown: Detroit, MI Height: 6'9' Weight: 280 lbs. Nicknames: The Game Status: Face Manager(s): none Current Teams: Neo Extremists Entrance Music: 'The Game' by Drowning Pool Wrestling Style: Submission- High Flyer Signature Moves: Set Up Move: No Continue (Superkick ) Finisher: Game Over (DVD into a Tombstone Piledriver) CHZ Title History: IC Title, North American Title(2) CHZ Award History: Other Federations: WCWF World Champ(4), WCWF Tag (1), AWA TV (2), AWA Tag (3) Other Federation Awards: Bio Don Rossi a normal guy living just outside Detroit with his parents and three siblings grew up like anyone else did in Detroit. Loving the Red Wings and spending alot of his time by himself in thought. He always knew he wanted to be in front of millions of people doing something he just didnt know what. When he was young he was into hockey and his idol was Dominik Hasek he vowed to be like him when he got older but when he hurt is back in a game it took him out of the sport. So Don moved on to his next love which he shared with his brother, pro wrestling. The two boys would constantly wrestle and try different moves on each other to test their stamina and creativity. Well after high school he decided thats what he was gonna do and he went after it. He began wrestling for the now defunct SWF against legends like JT Fringe, The Count, Staz, Thor, & Jaguar-F. Steven Petapaw broke away from the SWF and opened the CHZ, he looked into bright talent from the SWF and found Don Rossi. They began talks and a week later don appeared the first man from the SWF to be on the CHZ air waves. Don went on to do alot of things in the CHZ. He formed the most elite group in CHZ history 'Team Extreme', he was a 2 time North American Champion & the IC Champion for 6 months without a loss. The CHZ closed down and now its back and Don looks to start right where he left off dominating the CHZ ranks. Promo Archives Out For Drinks Rest & a Hot Girl A Return of Sorts Quotes
