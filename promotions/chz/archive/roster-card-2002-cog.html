What this is A roster card of the CHZ’s 2002 site, from the operator’s own site backup: the card’s own data file, field by field, in the site’s own order, followed by its biography and quote files where they exist. The site’s own display script supplied the field labels and is not reproduced; the card’s out-of-character handler block is left out whole. The date is the day its data file was last written.

2.)Heaven Sent(Saint puts a single opponent on his shoulders, Angel then delivers a top rope flipping neck breaker)
3.)Eye Opener(Double Super Kick). PWA No Name. Saint tags Angel. Angel was born on January 23, 1975 in Cleveland, Ohio. His real name is Mark Allen O’Kane, he is the son of Patty O’Kane and Michael O’Kane. At the age of 4 Angel and his family moved to the city of Dayton, Ohio where Angel lived for seven years. When Angel was eight, his father Mike got drunk and murdered his mother. Angel was then taken away from his father, because his father went to jail, and was moved back to Cleveland to live with his aunt and her family. Since his mother’s death, Angel has not been the same. He’s taken a life of god, and has valued to teach everyone the true meaning. When Angel was seventeen he started getting his body in shape, knowing that god would ask him to teach by physical actions. But Angel never thought it would be through wrestling, he always thought it would be through police work, or some other form of protection. When Angel was twenty-two he embarked on a journey like non other. He started spreading the word of god. After a few years working as a bar tinder, Angel was approached about becoming an Indy Circuit wrestler. Knowing that this stage of the business was not the best, Angel turned it down thinking he was too good for that stage. But later he found out that you had to start somewhere, and that’s the place you had to start. So Angel called a booker by the name of Chris Haley, and he and Angel set up a meeting between themselves. After a few hours of discussion Angel was a proud member of the ICW, a small local wrestler federation. From there Angel started his teachings, preaching to everyone that would listen. Then when Angel was twenty-four, a man named Eric Simmons made contact with Angel. Eric wanted Angel to be a part of his company called ESW, a small federation located in the Boston area. Eric told Angel that if he joined the ESW Angel would be able to preach in front of a larger audience. Angel liking the idea decided to take Eric up on his offer, and became a member of the ESW. But two months into the job, Angel suffered a tragic elbow injury and was unable to compete. Eric, being the money hungry businessman, fired Angel do to his inability to work and make him money. From there Angel returned to his home in Cleveland. There he met a girl by the name of Page and started dating this woman. After two years of dating, Angel asked Page to marry him. She said yes and they two got married on the third of July the following year. And at the age of twenty-seven Angel is now back on his feet, and fully recovered from the tragic elbow injury. Which brings us to the present and the CHZ. After a few months in a academy called PWA, Angel is now with a company called CHZ along side his tag team partner Saint. Will these two men be able to co-exists and become the next big thing, or will they simply become another team wanting to make it big? Not much is known about the man named Saint. The only thing we know is that he’s driven by the want to become big. That becoming famous is his soul purpose in life, and he will stop at nothing to become just that. Saint formed the tag team of C.O.G, which is short for Children of God, with Angel on June 23, 2002. He appeared in a church Angel was at, and he and Angel proceeded to have a conversation about the wrestling business. And at the end of their conversation, Angel was no longer a singles competitor, but a tag team competitor. Since then Saint has seemed to be the evil of the two, trying to get Angel to stop thinking about his purpose of teaching the meaning of God, but wanting him to focus on becoming a great wrestler. Saint says he is there to help Angel spread the word, but only time will tell if he’s speaking the truth. "'The world should know the true meaning of God.'" "“To the world, and to the boys in the back. If you don’t believe in god now, you will soon"
