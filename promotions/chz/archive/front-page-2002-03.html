26 March 2002

Breaking News....... Posted Tuesday, March 26, 2002 by Cellrock In CHZ Breaking News. The Candaian secret service has Alexander Sebastian Saunders III aka ASSIII in custody and escorting him back to the UK.UK officals believe ASSIII could be involved in a possible drug ring using his "Welsh Academy of Noble Knowledge as a cover up.as he was being drag off in handcuffs he had this to say. "This is a bloody conspiracy...This a plot to stop me from following in the footsteps of my idol William Regal and riding the wrestling world of Goths". CHZ Staff had no comments on this matter at this time, but then say that Brent"The Freight Train"Brewer will take his place in match against Knyghtmare at NBK Pride. NBK PRIDE Posted Tuesday, March 26, 2002 by Cellrock NBK PRIDE WILL CHANGE THE CHZ FOREVER.... Card is final...Check the card here. Posted Monday, March 25, 2002 by petapaw From Toronto Ontario, Friday Night Wild results have been posted. Check out all the action here. Results in .doc form here. Online version here. New CHZ Talent. Posted Saturday, March 23, 2002 by Cellrock The CHZ have signed some new talent to the roster.The CHZ has signed the WWA Champion Steven Brent.Cellrock had this to say about him. "Major talent,we were trying to get him before he signed with the WWA,but he slipped tru the cracks,not this time" Also Signed to a contract was Slaine 'BIGDADDY' Rodrik.Cellrock had this to say."This awesome big man will be a force in our fed,guys like Diablo and Galuda better look out" And also signed was Teddy Rafter."He is from my hometown of Houston,TX so you know he has talent"said Cellrock. Also word is that there are ongoing talks with others as this is being printed.As always with the CHZ with additions always comes some subtraction so expect some releases this week. CHZ Superstars Promote PRIDE Posted Wednesday, March 20, 2002 by petapaw CHZ Superstars have been assigned to Tour of Durty Brent 'the freight train' Brewer, Kaanen Thorne, The Count, and JT Fringe will be on the road promoting CHZ's biggest PPV 'PRIDE'. "The CHZ is sending a few guys for a promo tour of the Northern States as there has been a huge demand from venues south of the border for the CHZ to appear in places like Buffalo, Boston, Portland, Hartford" Thorne the CHZ's IC Champion, Brewer, The Count, and Fringe will be appearing at local radio stations doing interviews and giving autographs. They will be available to the media through a press confrence to be held later on in the week.
