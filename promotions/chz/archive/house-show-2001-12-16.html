What this is The results of a CHZ house show at the Pacific Coliseum in Vancouver, dated by the page itself, from the older site layer as it stood in the operator’s own site backup.

House Show - Pacific Coliseum Vancouver,BC Sun Dec16,2001

John Reed vs Darrly Rossi - In a Debut match John Reed showed everyone in attendance what he was made of. Reed also suprised Rossi with a fleury of moves, however Darryl Rossi capitalizes on Reed's ignorance, Rossi wins by pinfall.

Kid Thunder vs Exteme Chancelz - Another Debut Match for a CHZ new comer Kid Thunder, Kid Thunder suprises all with a massive upset win over Exteme Chancelz. However another new comerBrent "The Freight Train" Brewer makes an appearance as he attacks Thunder asfter his match.

Cellrock vs Gazz "Tnt" Maybury - As soon as this match took off, Cellrock dominated from the get go. Gazz had no hope, Cellrock wins by Submission.

Matt Helms vs The Count - The Franchise and Count square off in the Main event of the night, but only 5 minutes into this historic battle of the Old School and the new School .. Crazy 2 Cool comes down to the ring and attacks The Franchise but The Count doesn't stand for this and attacks C2C and Helms. The 3 of them battle to the outside of the ring, and into the crowd. But the Count gets the last laugh as he piledrives C2C into the concrete floor as the CHZ goes off the air.
