Luke Black Real Name: Lucas Jason Killingham Birthdate: 3/21/78 Hometown: Toronto, Ontario, Canada Height: 6’1” Weight: 240lbs lbs. Nicknames: Status: Heel Manager(s): Current Teams: Entrance Music: “I Am Hated” by Slipknot Wrestling Style: Unorthodox with a twist of high flying and speed Signature Moves: Blood of Skulls (180 into a 450),Powerbomb to Facebuster. Death Valley Driver, Frog Splash, Sharpshooter, Flying Kick, German Suplex, Superplex, Flipping Leg Drop, Reverse Suplex Set Up Move: Pressure Drop (Rolling Thunder ) Finisher: End of DayZ (Gets him in a reverse DDT then spins around really fast hitting a diamond cutter) CHZ Title History: CHZ Award History: Other Federations: Other Federation Awards: Bio Lucas Jason Killingham (now known as Luke Black) was born on a sunny day in Toronto, Ontario, Canada on March 21, 1978. He grew up with two loving parents and an older brother. He and his brother would fight a lot and Luke would never win. He took up an interest in hockey and loved every bit of it. He loved it so much that he thought he would grow up and be a pro hockey player. That all changed when he first saw wrestling on TV. He was instantly hooked and he stopped playing hockey and started his own backyard wrestling federation. He and his brother would fight in backyard wrestling and he would never win. Around when Luke turned 16, he finally beat his older brother. It made Luke very happy, but it crushed his brother. It was due to out side interference by his friend’s steel chair. His brother wouldn’t wrestle again until he joined a wrestling school. Luke was forced to join a college by his parents, but he really wanted to wrestle. He found a wrestling school but didn’t have the money to enroll, so he took out a student loan and joined. He excelled quickly and graduated with honors. He joined a few Indy federations before making it to the CHZ. The rest is CHZ history. Promo Archives Quotes
