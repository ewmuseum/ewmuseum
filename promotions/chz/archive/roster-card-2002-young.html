PWA No.2 in PWA The Height Of Callousness 5 Star Frogsplash Axl was born and raised in Bangor, Maine and was the proud son of Michael and Jane Young. Axl had an average happy upbringing as a young child, he enjoyed the benefits of being a young only child as his father was a popular and well known journalist, and he was often taken care of by his mother who was a house wife. By the time it came for Axl to attend high school, he was what you could call a typical teenage kid. Yet to his classmates and the school he was invincible, he was not one of the popular members of the school, he didn't play for the football team and he didn't date the head cheerleader, like I said, he wasn't Mr. Popular, nor did he want to be. His first year of high school moved along smoothly. He was doing well and obtaining more than satisfactory grades, but it was in his second year that the shit began to hit the fan. He didn't exactly know the reason why, there usually never is. He became one of the many victims of bullying from Ritchie Anderson. I don't think I need to bore you with all the details. He was physically and sometimes emotionally abused by this boy. Not everyday, Ritchie had an imaginary timetable of victims and what day he would makes their lives miserable on. As this went through into his third year, Axl confessed what had been happening to his parents, and he told the headmaster of his school. Yeah, you guessed it. Predictable and more intense beatings followed for Axl after this courageous confession. Ritchie Anderson and his goons barely got a slap on the wrist after Axl finally managed to muster up the courage to confess what they had been putting him through. The school system eh, you gotta love it! During Axl's final year in high school, he began to learn to play guitar. It was an instrument that had fascinated him for years and he finally decided he wanted to learn to play the fine instrument. While the beatings and taunting from Ritchie Anderson finally decreased to nothing more than petty name calling, Axl excelled rapidly as a guitarist ... as well as in looks! By his final year in high school Axl had evolved from 'the guy who listens to those, metal bands!' to 'damn how I haven't I noticed that piece of fine meat' guy. He wont deny it, he liked the attention for a short while, for about a month or so, what red blooded male wouldn't? Yet he was not blinded and fooled by it. Many females made advances on him, and he rejected them as ...... well, as rudely and obnoxiously as he could. Why should he be polite to them? They were all part of the 'popular' society that tormented him, he wasn't going to succumb to their false advances now. Yet it seemed Ritchie Anderson wasn't done yet. He didn't take kindly to Axl's new found good looks, and after hearing his own girlfriend had made advances on Axl, which she had, Ritchie, well, Ritchie took offence to it to say the least. So yes, the obvious happened, he delivered a beating to Axl, but more vicious and brutal than any other he had delivered. It would be the last beating in high school Ritchie Anderson would deliver to Axl, but it would be the one that left the biggest mark on him not physically wise, but emotionally wise. By the time Axl graduated from high school, with honours, he had formed a very talented metal band, called The Cadaver Dogz . Upon graduating and attending collage, the band practiced relentlessly, striving to become a success. Soon they began touring across the local area in small bars and opening for other major bands who were playing locally. Eventually, upon graduating from collage with a degree in Psychology, English language, Graphic Design and Music technology, Axl and the rest of The Cadaver Dogz had signed to a small independent record label. Their big break had finally arrived. The Cadaver Dogz then took time out from their lives, and determined to hit it big, they toured relentlessly while working on songs for an upcoming album. The band were making a big name for themselves, and had even been hailed to be one of the future leaders in the new generation of heavy metal music. Remarks like these only fed their drive to put all their effort and attention into becoming a major heavy metal band. The one night while they played a gig in The Cadaver Dogz hometown of Maine, Axl's life changed. After the concert, Axl decided to grab some food from the local fast food joint for the others and himself. Upon leaving the arena, he spotted an attractive young female just outside the arena. She near perfect look wise, and Axl immediately became entranced by her. His hopes of even talking to the girl were dashed when he saw she was with another man. However his hopes were lifted once more , in a somewhat terrible way, to find out the man was doing nothing more than trying to, well, rob her! Axl Young to the rescue. Or at least sort of. Upon touring and throughout high school collage Axl had taken up weight training. While on tour one needs something to pass the time away from practicing and writing new songs. It is a common fact also you had to stay in shape on the road, so it seemed logical for Axl to take up weight training. Since he had, gone were the days of the boy who wore 'dark clothes' and would weigh 80 pounds soaking wet. Now we had the man who, still wore 'dark clothes' and weighed at a nice200 lbs. So Axl made his knight in shining armour appearance. It wasn't hard, he approached the fiend from behind without either him or the girl noticing and simply overpowered him, and proceeded to pummel the man until he was satisfied he was immobile. Now bear in mind Axl was not famous for chatting up and making the first move with the opposite sex, thankfully on this occasion he did not have to. The young girl ran and embraced him tightly and hugged him. Axl put most of this down to shock, and simply hugged her back. They did not utter a single word for the 5 solid minutes they stood there, and by the end Axl was convinced that for the majority of the time she embraced him tightly it wasn't just down to fear. Do you believe in love at first sight? That night he 110% sure as hell did. That was it. The night Axl Young and Tanya Rose met. The love of his love was everything he could have hoped for. Axl had toured for 2 years straight the night he met Tanya, and after just one month of her touring with him and the band, he decided it was time for them to take a break. The band sure as hell needed one, and Axl desperately wanted to spend some time with Tanya. So after 2 long hard years of relentless touring the band settled down and took a long well deserved break, all returning to Bangor Maine, their home town to recuperate and recharge. After 3 months of seeing each other Tanya moved in with Axl. Some might have said it was too soon, and that they were moving too fast. Then again when your in love time doesn't mean jack shit, time doesn't exist. All that matters is the time you spend with your significant other, your loved one, and that was all that mattered to Axl and Tanya. Once she moved in with him, everything seemed perfect. Every morning he got to wake up to the sun shining down on her fiery enchanting red hair. He got to see her bright blue eyes gaze up at him when she woke up. Not many people will admit or know this but watching the sun rise at 5am every morning with the person you love laying in your arms is the closes thing to heaven you will ever come to on this earth. He was truly happy..................... but those bastards took it all away!!! One night, he didn't remember when, nor did he want to. He went out to collect some groceries. He came back. He went to open the living room door but found it was already open. Her screams greeted him. He threw the groceries down and ran into the living room of where the screams were coming from. Three of them. Three of them stood there over her fallen body. She was bloody battered and bruised and crying and screaming before him. You see something like you don't move. No matter what anybody says you don't! Tons of emotions hit you at the same time and try to unscramble themselves into the right order, while your just registering the horrific sight that stands before you. When that happens, you don't move! The pummelled him and beat him, slashed his chest, nothing fatal, merely a flesh wound. Tanya however. She did. Plain and simple, they stabber and beat her and she died on the way to the hospital. For Axl, his true love and happiness had ended. It was just as simple as that. He loved her. They killed her. They loved each other. They killed her. He was going to ask her to marry him. They killed her. They were going to get married, and have children. They killed her. They were going to live happily ever after just like in the fucking fairy tales!!! They fucking killed her!!! So it began ... so it spawned .... so it was unleashed. Deep ... dark ... demented. That was what he became... that is what he is. That night, it was unleashed. * * * * * * * * * * He had nothing left to live for. The Cadaver Dogz had split up. Why shouldn't they? I mean he had nothing left to live for. The police never caught the criminals who killed her, the worthless sacks of shit were still out there! She became his life and they took her away, his reason to live was gone. 6 lonely months passed. He sat in a lonely bar in Maine. On that night he finally decided he could be without Tanya no longer. That night he was going to end it. He was going to end his life and join Tanya. Until one sentence changed all that. 'Your in that band my nephew plays all the time when he stays over and keeps me awake all frickin night!' Axl turned slowly and locked eyes with the owner of the voice. It belonged to a man who would change his life in a dramatic way, and is one of the main factors that he is still alive and has the will to live today. The man was Mick Hammett. That night, Mick sat to Axl. Surprisingly Axl didn't want to 'step outside'. Instead he listened to Mick. Mick told him he recognized him, not from his music career though, but from seeing his picture in the local paper, he recalled it as being about the article involving Tanya's murder. For 3 long hours Axl confided in Mick and told him what happened. Axl said how the killers were never caught. Mick said that Axl should get his own justice upon them. Axl was intrigued to say the least by this. Mick then went on to explain how he had been a professional wrestler. Briefly, but he had been one. He told Axl he had been banned from most states for violent misconduct. The thing was Mick was a black belt, professional, master, grand master and sensei in just about every fighting technique there was today! Axl was glad to hear this, mainly because it was putting the daunting thought of mustering up the courage to actually end his life later on. Then Mick said how he lived alone. No immediate family. No one knocked on his door very often. At this point he had Axl hanging off his every word. By closing time, Mick had given Axl an ultimatum. He would make Axl his protégé. He would train Axl, give him a place to stay. He vowed to make Axl the ultimate fighting machine for one reason. Mick's wife was murdered 10 years ago. He felt the same way as Axl. That was why he dedicated so much time to training in the arts of fighting. He told Axl the police shot the murdered of his wife. Mick said that was only a small consolation, as he wanted to unleash his own justice on those scum bags, but he never got the chance. He said he would give Axl that chance. He said he would give him two valid reasons to live. 1. He would train him, and take him to professional wrestling to unleash the new born anger and anarchy filled within him. 2. To find and unleash his own justice on Tanya's killers. It sounded like one hell of a deal to him. * * * * * * * * * * For 2 solid years Axl pretty much disappeared from the world. He submerged and dedicated himself to Mick's tutoring. After 2 solid years, 730 days of non stop training, Axl emerged from Bangor Maine with an extended knowledge of nearly every fighting art there was in existence today. While Axl trained with Mick he studied Psychology once again. In his own time. This time however, Axl learnt the darker meanings. He learnt the stuff psychologists didn't tell you. He learnt how to abuse the human mind and manipulate it to his own sick twisted pleasure. When Axl finally emerged from Mick's training, Mick said they had one final stop to make. Japan! Mick said this would be the last chapter of his training, and the most brutal. The two stayed in Japan for 6 months, and during that time Axl made a defiant and valiant name for himself. Mick thought taking Axl to Japan would be a wise move as to get him away from America and away from where the memories of Tanya lingered. He worked his way through the ranking of Japans toughest federations, displaying his unorthodox style to the world. After 6 months of becoming a prominent feature Axl developed a bitter and violent feud with Yoshi Shinzaku. Yoshi Shinzaku was one of Japans top wrestlers and investments when the two crossed paths. Yoshi was a fully experienced martial artist with second to none high flying skills. Axl was a guy who got his ass kicked on a nightly basis, but bled like there was no tomorrow and absorb more punishment than most Japanese pros, making him tougher than Japanese algebra. Oh yeah, and he had some fascination with barbed wire for some reason after watching countless barbed wire death matches . The two had numerous standard and stipulation matches, and when it was coming to the end of Axls stay in Japan, they were pretty much tied in victories over each other, and Axl would have rather dies than leave Japan on a major loss to Yoshi Shinzaku. Axl made the challenge, and Yoshi accepted. A barbed wire death match, one Axl wanted more than anything. The date was set for it, no turning back for either man, it was shit or bust. Brutality would be a brutal understatement to describe the match. Blood was shed, skin was ripped, bodies were savaged, and after 30 minutes of this deadly warfare the two men were still standing. Then Axl's finishing move was born. He nailed Yoshi Shinzaku with a devastating double arm piledriver, and before he executed the move Axl let out a ear piercing yell. It seemed only fitting that the move should therefore be called The Rebel Yell. It was the move that enabled Axl to pin Yoshi Shinzakus shoulders to the mat for the 1...2...3! It ended. Axl's stay in Japan was over, and he left little short of a legend and nothing more than a hero. Upon returning Axl got two barbed wire tattoos on his right and left arms to represent the war he went through with Yoshi Shinzaku. While Axl was there the thought of Tanya stayed with him. While there he learnt to fuel the memories of her as a positive yet violent energy to use while unleashing his hew found despise of the world. So, Axl decided to make a personal stop of his own. Ritchie Anderson. The boy who tormented Axl at school would meet a cruel fate at the hands of Axls' new found friend. Roland the eagle. Axl became attached to the eagle whilst in Japan, and brought it back with him to America. Ritchie Anderson would come home. He would see Axl in his living room. He would sense revenge soaring through Axl's body. He would feel pain as Roland the eagle pecked his eyes out. The last thing Axl heard as he left that house after extracting revenge was the sound of Mr. Anderson tripping over his sons fallen body in the dark and landing on the shotgun he was carrying, thus blowing his head off. Killed two birds with one stone? .......... Try killing to humans with one eagle. Now, with personal and evolving issues aside. Axl has been moulded into an ultimate fighting innovative machine. Now Mick has brought him here to professional wrestling. Axl is dangerous for one simple reason. He has no will to live. he will put his body on the line every single night, with no regard for his own health. Why should he have any? He plans on ending his life anyway soon, when he hunts down and kills the murderers of Tanya, and unleashes his new wrath of the world out in the wrestling ring, he plans on ending his life on this earth and joining Tanya once again. He has no other reason to live, giving new definition 'to putting your body on the line'. Deep ... dark ...demented. That is Axl Young. That is what he has become. "Victims..... aren't we all?" "Every passing second is just another chance to turn it all around, and every passing minute is full of regrets as to why we didn't" "Who wants to be a hero? Being a hero brings responsibility, with responsibility comes stress and with stress comes anxiety, then with anxiety comes violence which leads to destruction. then the hero becomes the villian, and we're back where we started. F**k being that!" "I'm way too unique to live, and too damn rare to die"
