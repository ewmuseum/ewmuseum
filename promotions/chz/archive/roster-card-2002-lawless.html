singles Luke Lawless Chicago, Illinois 6'4' 245 'Papercut' by Linkin Park Rude Awakening 450 splash from top rope Neutral none Jett Blake June 10, 1980 none High-Flyer/Hardcore Lawless-sault (springboard moonsault) Fire Within (Mysterio's 169) Paranoid (720 Tornado DDT) N/A N/A PWA PWA #7 Ranked Wrestler, #5 Ranked Wrestler Malfunction D'Lo's Sudden Impact It was a kind of cold for June day in 1980 where screams were heard down the hallway in the Chicago Hospital. It was 12:01 AM as Janice Marie Blake was in labor with a baby boy on the way. After 5 hours of pushing, the 8 pound 6 ounce bundle of joy later named 'Jett' squeezed out of Janice's...well...ahem...birth canal with a loud scream and 5 minutes of crying. Jett grew up to be a very loving boy, very kind growing up. His parent worked their asses off to support the family of 7 in downtown Chicago, Illinois. His father a partime wrestling manager in a udnerground independant fed in Chicago that traveled to various warehouses in the suburbs and inner-city Chicago. His mother, taxi driver. Jett fell in love with professional wrestling and could barely live through waiting a week to watch wrestling with his father. He loved it so much that his father's 'client' start to train Jett at 13. Just minor stuff though, submissions, body slams and headlocks for the most part. When he grew older, around 15 or 16, he actually started to wrestle for the fed earning around 25 dollars a match. He started to get noticed at 19 by talent scouts for WCW, WWF, ECW, etc. until he fell upon PWA. He wrestled there and trained there under the name 'Lawless.' He thought the name fit his type of wrestling, kind of daredevilish, hardcore, edgy. He was promoted to Canuck Heat Zone after 1 month of training in PWA now under the name Luke Lawless. As most would expect, he just hopes the CHZ gets him to where he belongs... "Cripple the bitch and win the match!"
