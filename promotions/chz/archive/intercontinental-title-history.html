CHZ Intercontinental Title History

March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is One of the per-title reign histories the fed’s own Belt History page links to, as it stood in the operator’s own site backup (the March 2002 layer). The ledger runs through the fed’s predecessor and merger years — FWF and SWF — exactly as the fed itself kept it.

Cellrock

8/1/99 - 11/2/99

3M, 1D

CHZ PPV Caged Fury 1

Defeated Triple D, in a Last Man Standing Match, to become the CHZ’s first IC Champion. Had a CHZ record 15 wins in a row during his reign (1)

Jaguar-F (1)

11/2/99 - 12/9/99

1M, 7D

FWF PPV Total Chaos

Beat Rick Martell for the FWF IC Title (2)

Kryptonic Sonic

12/9/99 - 2/2/00

1M, 24D

SWF PPV

KryptonicSonic pinned new comer Road Holly to win the SWF Intercontinental Title. It would prove to be Road Holly's final match in the SWF.

JT Fringe

2/2/00 - 2/12/00

10D

SWF Raw

"The NY Assassin" won a tournament for the Intercontinental Title which had been vacated by KryptonicSonic upon his retirement.

Jaguar-F (2)

2/12/00 - 3/13/00

29D

SWF Smackdown

Jaguar-F defeated his old rival JT Fringe to capture his final title as a professional wrestler.

Staz

3/13/00 - 5/26/00

2M, 13D

SWF Smackdown

Staz left Jaguar-F bloody inside of a steel cage on his way to capturing the SWF Intercontinental Title in a Loser Leaves Town Match.

VACANT

5/26/00 - 11/26/00

6M

???

SWF closed for 6 months.

Rage

12/17/00 - 2/25/01

2M, 8D

SWF PPV Survivor

Rage survived the first ever SWF Survior PPV by pinning Greave to capture the Intercontinental Championship.

Devastation

2/25/01 - 5/27/01

3M, 2D

SWF PPV Broken Hearts

Devastation battled Rage inside a steel cage with a hole cut in the roof. On the roof a ladder led to the belt habging 30 feet above the ring. He held the title until his retirement.

Black Dragon

5/27/01 - 6/6/01

9D

CHZ PPV

Defeated Crimson Dagger and Staz in a 3 way dance for the title.

Don Rossi

6/6/01 - 11/9/01

5M, 3D

CHZ WILD

"The Game" cleaned Dragon’s clock to win his 1st title.

Merlin

11/9/01 - 12/12/01

1M, 3D

CHZ WILD

Merlin beat Rossi when Extreme Chancelz, Rossi’s "Team Extreme" stablemate hit Rossi accidentally with a steel chair.

Chazy 2 Cool

12/12/01 - 1/9/02

28D

CHZ WILD

The artist formerly known as Kris Snyder defeats Merlin to earn his second CHZ Title and 1st IC.

Brent Brewer

01/9/02 - 3/18/02

2M, 9D

CHZ WILD

"The Freight Train" blew past the competition on arrival and quickly started a rivalry with Chazy 2 Cool cumulating with Brewer winning the IC Title. Kaanen Thorne 3/18/02 - PRESENT ??? CHZ WILD Kaanen 'disects' Brewer to win his first CHZ gold.

FOOTNOTES 1 : Cellrock lost his HW and IC Title when CHZ closed and FWF took its place 2 : Jaguar-F lost his title when FWF merged with SWF
