singles Durke Richter Manhattan, New York 6'0'' 240 'Smooth Operator' by Sade Case Closed Frog Splash Face This lone wolf runs in his own pack Durke Richter June 20, 1977 Durke, Gumshoe, Flatfoot N/A All Rounder - Durke has learned plenty tricks of the trade over the last couple years and specializes in brawling and technical. DDT, Falling Suplex, Superplex, Neckbreaker, Reverse Figure Four Leglock, German Suplex, Abdominal Stretch, Rolling Hammerlock, Boston Crab, Spinning Backdrop, Flying Elbow, Sunset Flip, Flying Clothesline, Dragon Screw, Snap Mare into Chin Lock, Catapult, Sitout Powerbomb Pin, Sambo Suplex, Spinning Neckbreaker None Yet None Yet PWA PWA #3, #4 The Richta Scale Hanging vertical suplex into a reverse falcon driver in which instead of driving them back first Durke drives them down to the mat face first. Durke Richter had a happy childhood growing up in the small town of Sandusky, Ohio the son of two loving parents. His mother was a homemaker and always kept her eye out for young Richter. His mother always showed him a lot of affection back when he was little and wouldn't hesitate to help out her little one when he needed help. Durke's father was a local police officer and a good one at that. Durke's father had been employee of the month so much and has been awarded so much that they needed an extra room to put all his trophies and medals. Durke looked up to his father and saw him not only as a caring loving dad but as also his biggest hero and role model. Durke always knew what he wanted to be when he grew up, a police officer just like his dad. As Durke grew he turned into a very smart young man and proved his intelligence by getting extremely good grades all through elementary and high school. Durke had not lost his main goal in life though and that was to become a police officer and make his dad proud. So after he graduated from high school with honors he went to law school in Manhattan, New York. His stay there only enhanced his knowledge of the police force and law. Durke graduated from law school after four years of hard work and he was well on his way to becoming a cop. When Durke returned home to Sandusky, Ohio he was accepted into the Police Training Program or the PTP for short. Here he and a lot of other cops to be were trained to react and think like any good police officer. Durke went through the PTP without a hitch and soon was brought on as a new member of the force. Durke's father shortly retired from the force after Durke's call up. Durke's father had been on the force for a good thirty years. Shortly after his retirement though Durke's father suffered a stroke and soon after died in the hospital. Durke took a few weeks off from the force to comfort his mother until she decided to go and live with her sister in Ottawa, Ontario, Canada. Durke returned to the force saddened by his fathers passing but not letting that slow him down from doing his job. However after about a year and a half on the force Durke no longer felt that he was very much use anymore. When his father was around everyone was always praising Durke and given the good assignments because he was the son of one of the best officers in the history of that station. When Durke's father passed on though Durke was no longer taken very seriously. Instead of the great assignments he was handed meter man duty and was often sat behind a desk. Durke hated this and it wasn't long before he cleaned off his desk and left the station and Sandusky, Ohio. Durke moved back to Manhattan, New York where he bought himself a small apartment, which was conveniently located above a bowling alley and under a clog dancing class. Needless to say he was always running out of aspirin. It would have to do however until Durke made enough money with his new business: Durke Richter: Private Eye. Those years at law school really paid off and Durke was so experienced that he had more then what it took to be a successful detective. Using his apartment as his office and an ad he placed in the paper he was hoping to get some business and work his way up. Now forward about a year and a half later and he is still in the same old beaten up apartment and still running low on aspirin. He has had quite a few cases but most of them were duds and those that were the real deal left a lot to be interested in. Durke was at crossroads now in his life. Should he return to Sandusky and go back to his police duties or would he tough it out as a detective in the big city. Then on that fateful night Durke would get his answer. It seems that a local wrestler approached Durke and help finding his tag team partner who had gone missing just days before their big debut in a federation. He had not heard from him in quite a while and when he went to his house he found it trashed. This captured Durke's interest a bit as he never took on a case like this before. Durke of course took the case but had to go undercover as a wrestler in order to get some information. So under the fake alias of Bobby Rage, Durke Richter teamed with his client and while participating in wrestling matches he questioned everyone in the fed and was always snooping around, looking for clues. Durke did not find a single thing though but his passion for wrestling was beginning to get the best of him. Durke didn't really think much of the case after he had been wrestling for a while. Wrestling had sucked Durke in and god only knows what this wrestling sleuth will have in store for the wrestling world. "Bits and pieces of the puzzle were starting to come together but the question is.... Are they all from the same box?" "Another day, another case, the story of my life and although at times it may not be the most rewarding one I still find a way to wake up in the morning and do it all over again."
