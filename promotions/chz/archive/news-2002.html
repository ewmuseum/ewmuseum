O'Reilly leaks Rumors Posted Saturday, February 23, 2002 by petapaw Thursday Night on 'The O'Reilly Factor' with Bill O'Reilly some intresting facts were layed out on the table. O'Reilly had special guest and CHZ World Champion Matt "The Franchise" Helms sit down for a few questions. Their discussion led to the questions reguarding to the suspisions of Steven Petapaw the old owner of the Canuck Heat Zone who recently came back into the picture. Petapaw who's orignal idea of the Canuck Heat Zone started in late 1999 has been at the head of the business for a long time, going through many changes. Petapaw was the sole owner of the CHZ up until 8 months ago when the CHZ decided to invade a California based federation for ratings, at that point Petapaw gave up on the Heat Zone and jumped ship. Thus leaving the door open for current owner Cellrock. The Canuck Heat Zone has changed for the better in the hands of Cellrock striving into a successful Federation rated among the top 10. But on 'The O'Reilly Factor' Bill O'Reilly reviewed some very important facts about what has happened to Helms. Less than 2 months ago The Franchise's prize possession car a 1979 Chev Cuba was blown up in a CHZ parkling lot, also just a few weeks ago the CHZ champion's gym in Pittsburgh was torched, and together with the Cage at Caged Fury falling apart and the arrival of Helm's most hated villian JT Fringe , O'Reilly pointed the finger to Steven Petapaw. O'Reilly: "Petapaw. You guessed it. According to my sources, he currently has two plans on the table. One is to cause the CHZ to falter just enough to where he can buy it back from Cellrock. At the same time, he's trying to confuse you to the point that you'll be an easy target for JT Fringe to take your title. I may not be a wresting insider, but it doesn't take a rocket scientist to prove that Steve Petapaw wants full control of the company back. That means sole ownership and control of the CHZ Heavyweight title. Does that sound far fetched to you? " WHAT WILL COME OF THIS ? WILL NO LUV go on without a hitch ? Stable War ?
