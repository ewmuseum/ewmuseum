What this is A roster card of the CHZ’s 2002 site, from the operator’s own site backup: the card’s own data file, field by field, in the site’s own order, followed by its biography and quote files where they exist. The site’s own display script supplied the field labels and is not reproduced; the card’s out-of-character handler block is left out whole. The date is the day its data file was last written.
singles Persona Variable London 6'2 225 Last Resort by Papa Roach The Darkness Within As his opponent stumbles to their feet Persona grips their head under his arm and squeezes. Whilst squeezing PV lifts up his foe with his other arm so that they are horizontal, before sharply ramming their head into the canvas. Heel None Lexit Malkovich 1.1.1976 Persona, PV None Technical/Hardcore Human Torture Rack, Hangman's Neck Breaker, Brain Buster, Spine Buster (Slam), Suplex, Belly2Back Waistlock Slingshot, Wristlock into Quarter-Nelson, Sleeper into Cross-face Half-nelson w/ Russian Leg Sweep None None Only PWA with current Persona Variable None with Persona Variable Moonshine Persona gets his foe and lifts him above his head in a Gorilla Press stance for as long as he can (fatigue willing), before dropping them behind himself as he steps forward Biography: Lexit Malkovich was born in a London based tenant block on the 1.1.1976. For years he had been living under the false facade that his father had died whilst he was still a baby, only to discover that this was all a lie. He began to search for his Father, but to no avail. This sent Lexit into a whirlpool of emotions, which ultimately sent him over the edge. He began to become his father or at least what he depicted of him. This worried his mother who feared for her son's sanity. Lexit wanted nothing to do with her, claiming that she conspired with everybody around her to take his father away from him. Of course this was not true, as in reality his father was a drug addict, who recently O.D'ed on heroin, which nobody knows of to this day. Lexit's mother tried to tell him that his father was a drug addict, but Lexit was having none of it. He dissolved into himself, talking to himself, rambling. His mother was worried about her son, but what could she do? She didn't want him committed into some loony bin. This behaviour continued from Lexit, until one of his many newly formed personas's reached the surface, a persona named by the others as 'Perfect Dark'. Normally the personas have no remembrance of the other personas, but Perfect Dark is different. Perfect Dark has no remorse, no fear and no regret. He constantly tries to surface, tries to force the other personas out of the way, and on the 22nd of July 2000, he finally surfaced. His first act was against the person he regarded as the Devil's advocate, the person who lied to and deceived him for all those years, his mother. He strolled into the house he had lived for many years using the spare key, and immediately headed to the kitchen where his mother was chopping onions, unsuspecting of what lied ahead…BLAM…she was knocked to the floor. Perfect Dark began to choke her, watching carefully for the glint to fade away from her eye. But he was unsuccessful, before he could complete his mission; the other persona's managed to drag him back down into the shadows of himself. Lexit was shocked at what had just happened, he knew that no matter how much he hated his mother at this period in his life, he still loved her, and must leave her to stop Perfect Dark surfacing again and completing his what he had started. So Lexit flew to America, Chicago to be exact, why Chicago? Why not. For two years he tried to establish a normal or to as near to normal a life as he could manage. But he wasn't normal, his persona was variable. Most of the time he was ok, but every so often a strange persona would surface, a persona that for one reason or another wasn't accepted by the public. So he saw an ad in the local newspaper, about a wrestling academy, the PWA. It was as if fate was handing him a note. Only in wrestling could somebody like him be accepted. So he signed up for PWA as Persona Variable, trained hard, and before long was accepted into the Canadian Heat Zone. Here he is to this present day. What lies ahead for Persona? Hell knows…but he is a man with nothing to lose, and those are the most dangerous type. "NO!NO!NO!" "I think I made myself clear........crystal." "I am the Best of the Best" "Hmm.......Interesting"
