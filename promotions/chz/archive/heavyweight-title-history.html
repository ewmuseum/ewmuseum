Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is One of the per-title reign histories the fed’s own Belt History page links to, as it stood in the operator’s own site backup (the March 2002 layer). The ledger runs through the fed’s predecessor and merger years — FWF and SWF — exactly as the fed itself kept it.

Millennium 8/1/99 - 9/5/99 1M, 4D CHZ PPV Caged Fury 1 Beat 4 men in a Elimination Battle Royal. Ricky Jackson 8/5/99 - 10/30/99 2M, 24D CHZ PPV Halloween Havoc "The Rattlesnake" won his only title against an unmotivated Millennium. Cellrock 10/30/99 - 11/2/99 3D CHZ Friday Heat In a controversial decision Cellrock pinned the unbeatable Jackson, forcing Jackson to retire. (1) JT Fringe (1)

11/2/99 - 12/9/99 1M, 7D FWF PPV Total Chaos Upon FWF’s opening "The NY Assassin" defeated ten men in a Battle Royal for the FWF Title. (2) Rufus BlaQ (1) 12/9/99 - 2/12/00 2M, 3D SWF PPV The extreme entrepreneur defeated Staz to become the first ever SWF World Champion. Devastation 2/12/00 - 2/21/00 9D SWF Smackdown Devastation defeated Rufus BlaQ in what could be the biggest upset in SWF history. This was the SWF's first Candian show, taking blace in Devastation's home town of Vancouver, BC. Rufus BlaQ (2) 2/21/00 - 3/1/00 8D SWF Chaos Rufus BlaQ recaptured his SWF World Title at SWF Chaos, becoming the first ever 2 time SWF Champion, and ending Devastation's Career in the process. Thor 3/1/00 - 5/26/00

2M, 25D SWF Raw "Big Mighty" climbed the SWF ladder faster than anybody. The leader of the Outlawz defeated Rufus BlaqQ, capturing both the SWF Gold and a majority of BlaQ Inc. stock. VACANT 5/26/00 - 11/26/00 6M ??? SWF closed for a period of 6 months. Staz (1) 11/26/00 - 1/28/01 2M, 2D SWF PPV Tattered & Torn Staz won the SWF World Title tournament by defeating Rage in the semi-finals and edgeing Greave 4-3 in a one hour iron man match to capture the gold. Steven Petapaw 1/28/01 - 2/25/01 27D SWF PPV Psycho Circus Steven Petapaw outlasted 30 of the SWF's top superstars in an over the top rope battle royal to walk away SWF World Champion. JT Fringe (2) 2/25/01 - 5/27/01 3M, 2D SWF PPV Broken Hearts JT Fringe defeated CellRock, Champion Steven Petapaw, and his arch enemy Jaguar-F in a Fatal Four Way to capture the World Title. (3) Kris Snyder 5/27/01 - 7/4/01 1M, 7D CHZ PPV Defeated Don Rossi, Thor and Ricky Jackson in a 4 Way elimination match Staz (2) 7/4/01 - 8/31/01 1M, 26D CHZ WILD Staz defeated Kris Snyder with the help of old ally The Count. (4) VACANT 8/31/01 - 01/30/02 4M, 29D ??? Staz left the country taking CHZ’s belt with him. Matt Helms 1/30/02 - PRESENT ??? CHZ PPV Caged Fury 2 "The Franchise" overcame a enormous amount of adversity to beat Diablo in cage #1 where you must handcuff your opponent and leave the cage then the three other finalists, Don Rossi, The Count, Chazy 2 Cool in the Center Cage. FOOTNOTES 1 : Cellrock semi-retired, relinquishing the HW title when CHZ closed and FWF took its place.
2 : Fringe lost his title when the FWF merged with SWF. 3 : Fringe lost the title again when SWF closed and the New CHZ opened. 4 : The title was declared vacant when Staz left North America. Cellrock hunted Staz down and retrieved the belt but it stayed vacant for 5 months.
