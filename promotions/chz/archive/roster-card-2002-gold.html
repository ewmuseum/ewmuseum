tagteam Gold Rush Tito Garcia Oakview,Ohio 5'11 205 Manny Garcia Oakview,Ohio 6'0 195 Golden Moment by Eurocase Golden Moment Two torpedo drops from top ropes Face None Ankle Lock, Leg Drop, DDT, Top Rope leaps, spear, low blow, big boot, back kick None None PWA None Golden Double Double Team Clothes Line 'History Bio On GoldRush' The Garcia boys have never really had it easy, but yet never really had it tough. They have had they ups and there downs like the rest of us, but everything happens for a reason. Manny and Tito were born into a family which consisted of drug abuser parents, which really never intended to have any children, do to lack of maturity, and also lack of funds. Lola and Franko, the parents of Manny and Tito, never really spend time, nor were involved with there twin boys, but were always able to scrap up the money for a sport for each of them which was decided by the child. At the age of ten the boys each picked a sport, Manny being the smaller of the two, decided to side with the tactic type of sports, by joining judo, And Tito, the bigger out of the two, chose Little League Football. They both progressed great in there sports, Manny was an All American Judo champ for his age group, And Tito was amongst top in ranking for rushing yards. By the time high school hit they were both really known for their athletic ability, in the town of Oakview, which was a small town, and consisted of twenty thousand, which sat just inside of Ohio. They were weekly entries in the local newspaper, by being the finest athletes that came out of Oakview. With Manny being top in school’s in the United States, as Tito was raising eye brows to college scouts, things were looking up for the two boys. And when the parents were interviewed on the success of the boys, all they had to say to the reporter was. 'We getting paid for this?' Then the day came that the two boys graduated high school, they both squeaked by with low D’s, but a pass, is a pass. The boys both went there separate ways, Tito moved to Carolina, to continue with his football career, he played for the school, and received a full scholarship to play for a great University. As Manny made his way to the top martial artist schools in America, where he was taught several tactics of combat, being the small indivitual he was, it was mostly speed, and aggression. About half way in Tito’s season with the Carolina Bulls University, he was released from his school, due to lack of marks in class, and missing several practices. So he then went to live with his brother in Boston, where Manny went to school. About a month into that, Tito expressed an interest in Manny’s profession, and also began schooling at Cyprus Creed. Tito was a little more built due to football, but still a small indivitual, so he was taught the same tactics as his twin brother. Because of the them both being 5’11 even and 200lbs they progressed well in the speed of there motions, and also the advantage against opponents in the opposite corner. They went to Cyprus Creed School for 3 years, and at the age of 20 the two were graduated from the school, top of there class, as athletic wise, there was never a problem with the boys, it was just school itself that was the problem. But schooling was never a needed thing in the boy’s lives. Instructor Markus from Cyprus Creed, their teacher, put in some good words for them to a nearby wrestling academy, called Primetime Wrestling Academy. Markus described the two as, the little Ninja’s, which as a duo, they could prevail. Because as a team they show there finest qualities. So now they will proceed to the CHZ as a tag team duo, and were given the name of 'Gold Rush' as the two tanned skinned Asians both had dyed there shortly trimmed hair gold and Manny with his goatee also dyed gold as Tito has no facial hair. And the rush stands for their main talent, which is speed which then was turned into 'Gold Rush'. "' Two heads are better then one'"
