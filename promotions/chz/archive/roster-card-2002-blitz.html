Matt Blitz — CHZ roster card, 2002

singles Matt Blitz Vancouver, BC 6'4' 247 'Lost Soul' by King Dylan Inevitable Judgment Modified Cobra Clutch Sleeper Hold Neutral N/A Matt Blitz July 13th, 1978 The Disciple N/A Innovation Blitz IV (knee drop-kick into DDT) - Blitz III (high drop-kick into floatover pin) - Blitz II (release vertical suplex/leg-drop combo) - Blitz Original (inverted sidewalk slam) None None Unknown Unknown Blitz V Sidestep Spear Features & Attire: Matt has dark red hair, dyed, that rests in tangled strands down to his nose. His features are mostly dark, except for his eyes which are modifed so that they are silver in color. He is as built as any wrestler, and some would say that he looks like a modern day rock star. Out of the ring, Matt wears his Disiple suit, which is basically a black leather cloak that reaches down to the back of his knees. The cloak as a silver cross embedded on the back, and loose fitting sleeves. Under the cloak, Matt simply wears a black shirt and black pants, also assigned by The Disciples. On his belt, a golden plated bible can always be found, and his cloak always conceals the weapons of his faith. In the ring, Matt wears black wrestling tights with 'Blitz' colored in silver runnind down the legs. He wears the elboe pads and the knee pads, and his hands are always taped with black tape. Even in the ring, he wears a silver cross around his neck, and refuses to take it off. History: Official I-Spy Industries Report: To Mr. Ryan Blitz, As asked, Spectre, I got a hold of your brother after he disappeared last year. This report might be a little bit unbelievable and maybe even a bit radical sounding, but I swear to you that everything you read in it has been seen first by my very own eyes, and speaking unprofessionally, your brother is neck deep man. I tracked him to a small city in Japan where he was training to further his wrestling skills. Last time I flipped on the television and watched him go at it, he was pretty good, but I dare say he's better now. It appears that they know a few things about wrestler down there and after speaking with the headmaster at the wrestling school, Matt's changed his style a little bit. According to the headmaster, every personality and attitude has a style that works well with it and Matt is one of the few people who can create moves and combinations off the top of his head. I guess you have a creative brother, because he was doing some amazing moves down there I've never seen before. Anyways, as you asked, you're now updated on how he is as a wrestler. From there though, it all gets a bit perplexing man. Your brother hit Europe and started to frequent, of all things, the churches. It seemed that he turned over a new leaf and became religious, which I found odd seeing as his job is to beat up his 'brothers'. In any case, I took a closer look and entered the church myself during one of his visits and well, it was 100% legit. Praying, bible in hand, head lowered... the whole religion nine yards. I admit though, he didn't look anything religious before this point... more like some rock star or something, and it completely caught me off guard. As if it wasn't strange enough, one day, he stopped going to the churches. I checked the apartments where he was staying and turned up nothing except that they had never heard of a Matt Blitz. Checking his room name, I caught the name of a man named William Xent, and decided to pay him a visit. According to the old guy, he had been staying there the entire time and was getting ready to leave tomorrow for Toronto even though I had seen Matt ever that very same building for weeks. When I tried to dig, it was like cement, and he just told he to take a hike for my own good. So I did, but not before asking to use his phone and hitting redial. Much to my delight, the phone had different tones for the different numbers, and it was all I needed to dig some more. So I dug, and came to a place that was supposedly shut down a decade before, because whatever the factory was producing had no more business in Belfast (Europe). What I found there was the 'you know what' and it hit the fan. Documents, reports, videos and tapes of things you'd never believe even if you saw it. I'm talking possessions, hauntings, miracles, signs of the Apocalypse, biblical predictions and sightings of saints. I mean, I'd think it was a hoax, but everything was professionally documented, and I had a feeling your brother was involved in whoever dealt and reported these things. The group is called 'The Disciples' and their origins are unknown. What I do know is that they have their hands all over the world, and in deep pockets which I also don't know the origins of. Basically, they handle everything that the churches of the world aren't prepared for and they keep that lid on the jar of the public nice and sealed. They handle all these modern possessions and hauntings... which is already strange enough knowing that this stuff might even exist. To make it more interesting, your brother was recruited by The Disciples a month before he left Belfast and had been handling these cases in Europe right up to the day when he stopped going to the churches. Now, Matt Blitz never existed in Europe and he's been transferred to North America to continue his wrestling career there and to handle the tougher cases down there. The group is religious to the core. They're like a whole other church under the table and I'd best describe your brother as some strange version of a modern day crusader, though I don't know what that means in this day in age. All I do know is that this is some strange stuff you're brother has gotten himself into, and I have no clue on what the future holds for you and him. That being said, I'd watch what you said on the phones from now on, and be careful about who you talk to. I don't know how deep into out little social structure these boys go, but if I were you, I'd burn this report and forget you knew me. You know my unmarked account, so you can transfer the amount discussed there. I know you're bad, dangerous and everything else, but these guys aren't lightweights. Be cautious and watch your back, because this is far beyond wrestling. Good luck man and take care. It's been a pleasure, but in my best interests, I don't know who you are beyond this report. Sincerely, 'The Eye' "Your soul belongs to someone else, but your ass is 100% mine!"
