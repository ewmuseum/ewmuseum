CHZ House Show — Thunder Bay, Ontario

11 January 2002

What this is The results of a CHZ house show at the Thunder Bay Arena, dated by the page itself, from the older site layer as it stood in the operator’s own site backup. It opens with the fed’s own anniversary tribute.

CHZ House Show Thunder Bay Arena, Thunder Bay, Ont Jan 11, 2002

The Show opens with a tribute to the CHZ for the last 3 years. January 11 1998 the CHZ opened it's doors to the likes of Millenium, JT Fringe, Cellrock, Steven Petapaw, Jaguar-F, The Big FO, Rickey "The Rattle Snake" Jackson, Canon, and the Outlawz. Images are shown on the jumbrotron of classic matches held in the CHZ. The Busted Steel Cage match, the 20 foot Scafold match and others. JT Fringe and Steven Petapaw decking it out on the entrance ramp.

The Scene ends with the screen showing "CHZ FOREVER 3 Years and Counting...."

The Count Vs Diablo - The Capacity crowd in Thunder Bay were treated to a great one to start the night off. The Count coming off a recent victoy over the Hardcore Champion, takes on back to the ring in months without his Team Extreme to lean on Diablo. Both Big Men get their fair amount of shots in. This match was definatlly a crowd pleaser, The count at one time, selling the match. Diablo goes for his finisher but gets distracted by the Appearance of DON ROSSI in the crowd. The Count knowing he wasn't looking connects wih a set of Brass Knuckles. The Count Wins by Pinfall.

JT Fringe Vs Matt "The Franchise" Helms (revenge strap match) - The Franchise Was Pissed after Wednesday night when his pride and glory was taken from him in a explosion that rocked all through the CHZ his 1971 Dodge Cuda. The Franchise came out and demanded to know who it was that was responsible for this, but before he could drill the suspected any more, JT Fringe attacked him from behind, the ref quickly attached the strap and the match was on. JT FRINGE dominated for the whole match throwing The Franchise to turnbuckle to turnbuckle pounding on him at every chance. JT Wins by submission.

High Flyer Vs Gazz "TNT" Maybury - High Flyer , high in his once sence, off his recent victory in the "Your Fired" Battle Royal took that to the ring, and suprised TNT Maybury again, for a quick and painful win. High Flyer with a winning streak.

Brent "The Freight Train" Brewer Vs Thor - The CHZ's Newest IC Champion took to the ring against the most feared of all CHZ superstars THOR. But Thor was no match for Brewer, as The Freight train kept on rolling past Thor. Thor looks very rusty in the ring, with almost 4 months of no action. The youngster showed the veteren Thor how things are done here in the CHZ.

Interview from Extreme Chancelz Extreme- You ask about the WWS. What exactly can I say? (laughs slightly to himself) The WWS was another subject proving my point to be correct. I'm Greatness! I was even to great for the WWS. Although the WWS is full of talented wrestlers, they are all still nothing compared to The Extreme One. My opponents were embarassments to the wrestling world. Insomniac- a minute thirty match. Absolutely no competition there. He didn't even fight back. Smoke- a five minute match. He fought back but his offense was short lived. Kid X was just that in the ring, an immature little kid. His rookie mistakes showed his amateur abilities. Plain and simple, he got lucky and I got screwed! After he hit the top rope hurincanranna on me and went for the pin, the ref counted 1...2...3! THE STUPID SON OF A BITCH DIDN'T SEE THAT MY FOOT WAS ON THE BOTTOM ROPE AND AWARDED THE DAMN MATCH TO X! Kid X needs to cherish that victory deeply because that was the only time he will ever beat Greatness!

Interviewer- How do you feel about Pete Anselmo and Kid X moving on to the finals for the WWS Extreme Title and who do you think will win?

Extreme- What do you mean how do I feel? The whole world should know how I feel! The finals should be EXTREME CHANCELZ BEATING THE SHIT OUT OF PETE ANSELMO! I don't care who wins the match cuz neither man deserves to be the first WWS Extreme Champion. But know this, my sights on the WWS Extreme Championship have been blurred but not blinded. The First Champion, whether it be Pete Anselmo or Kid X, will not experience glory long. This message goes out directly to Pete and X. Cherish your moment in the sptolight while you can. I'm coming for you! And before you know it, one of you will be being escorted out of the ring without the Extreme Championship around your waist! (laughs)
