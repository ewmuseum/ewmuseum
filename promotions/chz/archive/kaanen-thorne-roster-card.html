What this is A CHZ roster card from the fed’s March 2002 site layer, as it stood in the operator’s own site backup.
name : 'The Cowboy From Hell' Kaanen Thorne height : 6' 6" weight : 283 lbs. age : 31 hometown : Calgary, Alberta finisher : 6-6 Crush finisher descritption : Headfirst powerslam entrance music : 'Cowboys From Hell' by Pantera bio : Kaanen was an all around athlete in high school. His favorite sports were football, hockey, rugby and amateur wrestling. Kaanen has long been an individual of good size and enjoyed the roughest positions in his school sports. In football, he was a big, fast linebacker. In hockey, he was a big bruising defenseman. As an amateur wrestler, Kaanen won gold in many provincial meets and several national titles in his weight class.

He attended Rutgers university on an athletic scholarship and joined their football program as a linebacker. With his schedule filled with class and practice time, Kaanen would drop the other sports he grew up loving. After leading his team and his conference in tackles for two straight years, Kaanen returned home to Calgary on a family emergency. After that time, he would not return to Rutgers and so decided to work closer to home to take care of his mother and ailing father. He worked mostly heavy construction but his love for sport quickly returned and he soon joined the legendary Stu Hart's "Dungeon" wrestling school. Here Kaanen excelled and quickly learned and was thus given a contract with Stu Hart's Stampede Wrestling Circuit. He wrestled with several fantastic wrestlers like Owen Hart, Chris Benoit, and others and was quick to make a name for himself. He played the roles of face or heel very well, depending what was demanded of him.

Kaanen first toured the country and northern parts of the US before joining several national American promotions. His noteriety grew rapidly as his skills were of high calibre and so Kaanen became a maineventer and a title holder evereywhere he went. He did not only depend on his natural skills, but also on his strong work ethic and this the fans recognized and showed their appreciation of The Cowboy From Hell. His merchandise sales were among the best in every promotion where he wrestled. He even landed an Adidas endorsement deal at one time which proved financially beneficial to both parties.

Kaanen now runs his own successful gym and fitness center in Calgary Alberta, teaching the art of wrestling. He keeps himself in great shape and now looks towards the CHZ and their organizations for an opportunity to return to the spotlight.
