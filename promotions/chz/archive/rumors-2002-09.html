CHZ Rumors, August–September 2002

5 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The rumors page of the CHZ’s 2002 site as its news script held it in the operator’s own site backup: nine in-fiction rumors from 15 August to 5 September 2002, newest first. The page showed the newest eight; the ninth, of 15 August, is in the same news file.
Soul Is A Father? There are rumors running ramped all over Canada that CHZ’s Superstar Soul is a Father. This shocked everyone in the world since Soul is definitely not the social type but we happened to get a word with his sister Kayla.

“This is a total shock to me,” she said is surprise “If that is true, this going to be a situation that everyone shall drop there mouths and at the same time laugh at.”

This is strictly rumor now but there is word that his so called “kid” could be showing up at Wild.
Will She Be Revealed? After being evasive on both last weeks Wild and at Mind Games, it is heavily reported that the female Commissioner will be at this weeks Wild and will reveal herself to the world.

Who will it be? And why did she leave with Jake Schultz?

Tune in to Wild on Wednesday!
Is He Really Commissioner? After Matt Hunt's outburst that he is one of the 50/50 Commissioners at Mind Games close, it has yet to be proven whether his claim is true or not.

Hunt nor El Diablo were available for comment.

Tune into Wild to find out if Matt Hunt really is a Commissioner.
El Diablo Cover Up? News around internet wrestling sites is that CHZ Owner El Diablo is the mastermind behind a large cover-up of the recent attacks on Matt Hunt.

Rumor also has it that Matt Hunt has started this story.

More on this when available.
C.O.G Only One? With the re-signing of Children of God, the CHZ tag team division has picked up. But also, COG brings with them a lot of heat.

The last time we saw C.O.G., they were getting their butts kicked by Redemption Song. They then asked for their release from CHZ. At this time we do not know what C.O.G. did with their time off, but sources say they spent a lot of time in the hospital recovering from the beating.

A source also says that Angel, Mark Allen O’Kane, never fully covered from the beating and is still in rehab.

"Mr. O’Kane, or Angel, has been in and will be in rehab for close to another 6 months." Claimed a reliable source.

If this is true, how can C.O.G. be signed once again with CHZ?
Who are the Commissioners? After the twists and signings of two Commissioners on Wild, the question still lingering in the air is "Who are the dual Commissioners?"

The only clue is that one could be female, but who could it be?

As this goes on rumor has it that both Commissioners will be revealed at Mind Games.

Tune in to find out.
Hired? Rumor has it that CHZ Owner El Diablo has already hired a Commissioner for CHZ!

He or she is stated to appear at CHZ's first pay-per view Mind Games.

Who will it be?

We'll keep you updated.
Commissioner to be named at Mind Games? It is rumored that at Mind Games, CHZ owner El Diablo will be introducing a Commissioner into CHZ, to install law and order to what have been some very "Wild" shows.

"It might be true, it might not be," commented El Diablo when asked.

More information on this rumor as it develops.
Stimpo joining a band? Rumors are flying that Brendon "Stimpo" Stimson has got himself into a band. Which band? No one knows for sure. People are believing that Stimpo may have himself in a country band while others say his new singing talent will bring hardcore rap back to the radio. Many fans of Stimpo, or lack of, have had something to say. We went to the streets to find a women with this to say:

"Where Stimpo goes, I'll follow. But, couldn't we have a little hint?"

After hearing this, Stimpo responded with his own reply:

"Here's a hint, it has singers, and it has a band, good luck!"

Apparently, Stimpo dares not to shudder the name as of yet.

We will give you further news on this rumor when available.
