name : The Count height : 7' 0" weight : 385 lbs. age : ??? hometown : Transylvania finisher : The Impaler finisher descritption : The Impaler is an inverted tombstone. entrance music : Bad Religion by Godsmack bio : Vlaad Dracuul, better known as The Count, was born in to a royal Transylvanian family in the latter half of the twenieth century. While the stories of his ancestory have become common knowledge in the wrestling community, a columnist for one wrestling magazine claims that there is no record of him ever being born.

In 1989, Vlaad moved from his beloved Transylvania to rurla Connecticut where he was enrolled at Yale, and graduated with degrees in business and economics. He returned home to Transylvania only to find that a peasant uprising had occured and his parents had been slain. Fearing for his life and his crown, Vlaad hastily fled back to the United States.

He began wrestling for the fledgling KSCW, located in southern California, after the owner, the enigmatic KryptonicSonic, promised to help him become a warrior and reclaim his family's throne. After the KSCW closed it's doors, Vlaad, now calling himself The Count, found his way to the CHZ.

It was here that he first crossed paths with Thor, and his future partners the Crow and Dirk Diggler. After a brutal match with Jaguar-F's younger brother, the Prophet, "The New York Assassin" JT Fringe coined the term, "The King of Transylvania Hardkore". The name has been with him ever since. The Count bounced around after the CHZ closed eventually ending up in the New York based SWF.

He enjoyed a great deal of success under his mentor, Shawn Smith, and truely began to turn a corner in his career.

He has returned to the place that turned a tall Romanian kid in to the most feared and revered monster in the business, and this time, he has his eyes on the gold.
