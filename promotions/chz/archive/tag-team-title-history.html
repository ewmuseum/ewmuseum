Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is One of the per-title reign histories the fed’s own Belt History page links to, as it stood in the operator’s own site backup (the March 2002 layer). The ledger runs through the fed’s predecessor and merger years — FWF and SWF — exactly as the fed itself kept it.

Millennium & Canon

8/1/99 - 9/1/99

1M

CHZ PPV Caged Fury 1

The original "Outlawz" defeated Sex & Violence to become the first Tag Team Champions.

Sex & Violence (1)

9/1/99 - 9/20/99

19D

CHZ Friday Heat

Sex & Violence got their revenge on "The Outlawz " and won their first of many Tag Titles.

Detroit Boyz (1)

9/20/99 - 10/3/99

13D

CHZ Friday Heat

What would turn out to be a great rivalry between the two. The Detroit Boyz beat S & V for their first reign.

Sex & Violence (2)

10/3/99 - 10/15/99

12D

CHZ Friday Heat

Dirk Diggler & The Crow won a vicious Table match to become the first ever two time CHZ Champions.

Detroit Boyz (2)

10/15/99 - 10/30/99

15D

CHZ Friday Heat

Shaggy and 2 Dope jumped Sex & Violence before the match, on route to their 2nd title reign.

Sex & Violence (3)

10/30/99 - 12/2/99

1M, 3D

CHZ PPV Halloween Havoc

Diggler & Crow beat the Detroit Boyz for good in a buried alive match to become the 1st CHZ 3x Champions. (1)

NY2K

12/2/99 - 12/4/99

2D

FWF PPV Total Chaos

JT Fringe & Jaguar-F beat 3 other tag teams in a wild Tornado tag match to win the titles.

Sex & Violence (4)

12/4/99 - 12/9/99

5D

FWF Massacre

Diggler and Crow beat Jaguar-F in a handicap match when Fringe turned on Jaguar-F. (2)

Cell Block 11

12/9/99 - 12/17/99

8D

SWF Smackdown

Cell Block 11 defeated the Front Page to capture the SWF Tag Team and Hardkore Tag Team Titles.

Devastation & Invertion

12/17/99 - 2/21/00

4D

SWF RAW

Devastation and Invertion pulled off a huge upset defeating Cell Block 11 to capture The Tag Team Gold.

Sex & Violence (5)

2/21/00 - 5/26/00

3M, 5D

SWF PPV Chaos

Dirk Diggler made his triumphant return to the ring teaming with his old partner and Legion member, Crow. Together they overpowered Devastation and Invertion to become the FIVE TIME Tag Team Champions. Sex and Violence would retire after this reign.

VACANT

5/26/00 - 11/26/00

6M

SWF closed for 6 months.

Boylan’s Boys

11/26/0 - 5/27/01

6M, 1D

SWF PPV Inferno

Boylan's Boys defeated Sex and Violence at the November 26 Inferno. Despite attempted interference by Cell Block 11, Tony and Ryan pulled off the huge upset victory. They still hold the longest reign by any CHZ champion.
