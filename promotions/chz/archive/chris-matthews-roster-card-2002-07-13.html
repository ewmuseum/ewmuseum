First Ever CHZ Television Champion, Last Ever Intercontinental Champion Other Federations: RUSH Wrestling | (Old) Canuck Heat Zone Other Federation Awards: Bio The story of Christian Daniel Alexander Matthews began at 9:13 pm, the date was March 29 and the year was 1978. Young Christian was the 3rd of 4 kids to be born from Karen’s stomach and from Peter’s loins (lol). Weighed in at 7 lbs Christian was a bright young baby boy, one of several born at Montreal General Hospital that day. The first three or so years of his life were wonderful. Attention and praise was lavished on young Christian. Everything he did and accomplished was considered to be of high importance by his mother and father. For instance, at age 10 ½ months he spoke his first words of “dada”. Learning to walk soon after that Christian had enormous potential and a bright future. But at age four Christian received an un-welcome gift, a baby brother in young Andrew Nicholas Matthews. Christian was used to receiving whatever he wanted whenever he asked. His two older sisters, Danielle and Jade grew up beyond this. Christian was still several years younger then both. With young Andrew around Christian wasn’t noticed. He entered elementary school at age five and immediately became a troubled kid. He wasn’t much of a learner in the first few years of school either. He had his group of friends, among them his closer personal friends Damien and Scott who remain friends with Christian to this day. At age nine when his younger brother Andrew entered school did only then Christian show his full potential grades began to pick up. No longer was considered stupid, and the reason why. Andrew was nothing special either. This made Christian happy. The two youngest of the Matthews family have never liked each other. Right to this very day. Andrew and Christian Matthews have never seen eye to eye on anything. They have more then a sibling rivalry. At age 10, Christian now called Chris began to play amateur Ice Hockey for a local team. We’ll just call them the ‘Hawks’ (Mighty Ducks!!). Chris played for several years across the domestic Montreal scene, earning both a good and bad reputation. Good because he was a team player and had an excellent slap shot and bad because he was known to start fights on the ice. Playing hockey till he was 16 kept Chris fit and strong. He wasn’t the strongest person in the world mind you, he may have made fun of and teased kids at school but was no bully. He was a good kid at heart. But at age 17 everything when wrong. His father lost his job as a high priced officer in a major Montreal Electrical Company. We’ll call it the MEC. Money sauce began to grow thing and the bulk of the money came from Welfare and his mothers fairly low payed job. His father began to be the most hated man alive. Venting his anger out on Chris’s mother a lot of the time. One day when 17 year old Chris came home from school he heard yelling at his loved mother screaming. He immediately raced inside and saw his father with his fist clenched and raised. This is all Chris could take. Defending his mother like any loving son would he raced up to his father and pushed him off his mother. Stunned at his son’s actions his father threw Chris out of the house. Straight out onto the street with no where to go Chris began to fend for himself, after awhile getting sick of living in dingy ‘drop in centers’ Chris left the city of Montreal which he loved. He began a several day hitchhike to the city of Toronto in Ontario. Upon arrival in the bigger city Chris found refuge in a safe house on the cities edge. But after awhile of the dingy life he fell into the wrong crowd once more. Stealing food, breaking the law and once or twice taking drugs. Of course Chris was never as stupid to go to far with anything, he would wreck things. Get in trouble but he had his limits. Living on the streets was tough; Chris needed a way to protect himself. A way to have some art of self-defence. He found it in Martial Arts. Chris picked up his life again once the training at the Toronto Center of Martial Arts began. Training for nearly 15 hours ado. Rising at 6 am for jogs Chris became one of the premier students. At age nineteen his left couldn’t get any better, he had a solid girlfriend as well. Learning almost as much as he could in the dojo. What was left? Chris wanted to keep up with Martial Arts but something was lacking. He wanted to get into a more contact and aggressive style of fighting. Pro-Wrestling was the answer. His current sensei knew little of the wrestling arts but out of love for Chris he sought out a trainer, and brought him into the dojo. Training began swift and quickly with his wrestling coach. Month after month went by. Studying the fine art of professional wrestling both physically and mentally Chris had one of the fastest training periods his teacher had ever seen. Incorporating his Martial Arts background and training into it Chris was a legend in the making. He needed somewhere to start, but where? He toured the independent leagues across Southern California, upstate New York and in Florida. After spending close to 2 years in the “indies” Chris returned to Toronto, Canada and once again went back into training. Two months passed after some solid training Chris returned to the ring in the Canuck Heat Zone. Running rampant throughout the promotion for over 3 months it soon shut down, leaving Chris without answers and without pay. Chris signed up with up and coming Rush Wrestling promotion ran by PCW Legend; Jeremy Howard: After a good few weeks in Rush, CHZ was announced to be reopening and Matthews jumped at the chance to wrestle in the bi-weekly run promotion. Chris Matthews returns to CHZ with one thing on his mind and that is World Title Gold... Martial Arts Training- Karate: Shotokan Karate- Black Belt. Ju Jitsu: Ikkyu Shodan Kyu- Brown First Dan.
