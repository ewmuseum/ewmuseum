Misfit's Teardrop Diving Headbutt from the top rope after calling out to his freaks The Look Of A Youth God-Xander has scruffy, untamed locks of blonde hair with a silver streak in his front bang. His eyes are ice blue, housing a soft intensity that can be quite intimidating. His body is well built, well defined and sculpted abs and arms from years of work with personal trainers and martial arts instructors. His face is devilishly handsome to compliment his body and he makes it a point to always ALWAYS have his arms and body painted before his matches. No one knows where this ritual came from, but no one ever argues. He can have anything from religious to fantasy markings painted on his lightly tanned body and they come in all colors, the markings VERY detailed. He wants his body doused in the art. He has a tattoo of a flaming sword impaling a heart on his back, the sword parallel with his spine perfectly. He wears baggy leather pants with burning swords on each leg and black boots, along with black RVD-style fist wraps for when he fights. On his way down to the ring, he wears a platinum replica of Egyptian neckwear, removed immediately before his battle. Outside of the ring, he wears baggy, comfortable clothes reminiscent of a common man, preferring not to get swept up into all the fancy clothing and attire.

Behind The Misfit...Xander's Known History...

Xander is a disenchanted rockstar looking for a new challenge, something exciting to turn up the heat in his seemingly bland lifestyle. Cars, money, drugs, women aren't enough for him...he needs to know the taste of his enemy's blood on his lips, the cold sweat before a punch lands across his face, the melody of bones snapping under soft flesh. Xander is a poetic loner who often speaks as if he was telling you a riddle and is obsessed with the tie-in's between love and destruction. The people aka 'the freaks' or 'the misfits' love him, but he doesn't wish to be viewed as their hero....how did this all begin...well...

Xander Crave is an enigma to those who have met him, for it is unclear what he truly is. Is he a rockstar who has intoxicated millions across the globe with his sultry voice and addictive lyrics set to a hard melody? Is he a hard-edged fighter who shows no remorse within the confines of a ring until his opponent cannot even rise to take a violent step towards him? Is he a poetic lover who is searching for a heart to share himself with? Or is he a manipulative game player with a knack for finding that perfect way to crawl under your skin....delightfully or otherwise? Xander is a strange mixture of all of these, a virtual unknown when he was signed to a recording contract 1 year ago for Underground Euphoria Records after being discovered in underground rave's and parties entrancing legions with his soft as silk, yet tough as nails delivery of a song. Xander was an immediate hit with millions, breezing past cult following to a youth rock legend in little to no time. To this day, his songs still top international charts, earning over 20 hit singles and 2 chart busting albums with his hard rock love sonnets and tales of human struggle set to music. Soon came the awards....the money...the fame...the legions of devoted fans....but to Xander...they didn't satisfy him. It was obvious when he won his first Grammy for 'Best New Artist' in 2001....his acceptance speech in front of millions of tuned in viewers, the hottest networks and news stations covering this event as he went on to say with a somber look in his ice blue eyes, 'Thanks for this piece of shit...I'm sure it'll go great in the bathroom next to my toothbrush. Love heals and kills.....good night.' With those words, the world officially realized....Xander Crave was unlike ANYTHING they'd ever experienced before...and the more and more he displayed this one-of-kind behavior, the stronger his following grew. All the while, when Xander was not trapped within the studio where he would unleash his caged feelings, he was learning more and more forms of martial arts and grappling, finding the thrills, pain, and adrenaline of locking up man to man was as healing as his singing. Xander even won a few local tournaments, thriving off the feeling of each win he achieved over his opponents. With each win, he knew what he needed to be whole...and he set out after it...he set out to become a warrior for fufillment and best every last man he could until he could fill that void of satisfaction that he could never seem to please. He could already feel the inspiration for new music as he was aching to climb within the confines of the squared circle and battle mono-a-mono until one man stood and the other fell. Will he fight till he's bored? Or until he creates a river of blood? Or until he has claimed every accolade their is possible in this stage? Even he knows not the answers to that question...but the chorus to his song 'Satisfaction' say it best.... 'I will sacrifice the innocent and the guilty to feed it, My nightmares invade the day, I ache for it, I need it, Love, peace, glory, death, destruction, drugs, sex, action, Which one of these things will give me what I need.....satisfaction...' And so, Xander begins his quest to discover is this is what will satisfy that insatiable urge that has kept him from being at peace for so many years. "Where are all my freaks out there??" "I'd rather not learn your name....when you learn a name, you gain attachment....and the last thing I want to do is feel attached to you when I sacrifice your body to feed my satisfaction." "And the media wonders why rockstars are bad influences..." "I don't like repeat performances..." "It takes a man who has experienced pain to truly know love....with all the pain you will feel, you could write a bible on it."
