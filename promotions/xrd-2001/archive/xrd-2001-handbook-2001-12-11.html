I have decided to once again try and make a successful E-wrestling Federation. This time though I'm making up my own rules on how I've always wanted a fed to be run. How is that you might ask? Well I'll tell you, I've decided to make a
E-fed thats perfect for those people who love the game but don't have enough time to be online roleplaying every second. Thats right this is a fed for those people that have lives and can't dedicate it entirely to E-wrestling like some of those freaks out there. I like E-wrestling a lot, I've been involved in it since 1997, but since I've gotten older my time has grown shorter. I was always having to quit feds because of lack of time because my LIFE comes FIRST. I want to be involved in it some way so I thought I'd start my own fed again but this time, think out and plan everything before jumping the gun and opening to early. Making a fed for those who have the same problem as me. I would also like to note that I will ALWAYS be updating the site. I always hated feds that never updated, so I'll be updating all the time. I'm also going to keep the design fairly simple and easy but appealing. Below is the list of rules and descriptions for all the answers to your questions. If you like what you see fill out an application.
01.) Roleplaying rules and regulations: Like I said the EWF is for those that love the game but don't have enough time to be online countering your opponents rp all the time. You are required to rp at least once in two weeks. That means if you don't have a match scheduled for two weeks you're still required to do one rp to let us know you're still alive.
However when you are scheduled a match you are required to rp the minimum of 1 time. I'm not looking for quantity here, I'm looking for QUALITY. There is no limit, but let me be the first to say, 20 lines just won't cut it. Lets put it this way whosever's rp's are the most realistic and well written will be very successful here in EWF. Your Wrestlers success is determined on your ability to become that wrestler when writing up a roleplay. There is no using another persons character in your rp unless you've had the consent of that characters handler. If you look at the staff page you'll see a list of all the staff and weather they can be allowed in your rps. Rp's are not done on a message board or guest book. You send your rps to me, and I will include them in the card, I will also send everyone's opponent's rp to them, example: if Kane were fighting
Rakishi, and Kane rp'ed I would send his rp to Kane's handler. 02.) Roleplay, Strat & Match Deadlines: You are only required to do one roleplay for each match your scheduled. If you want your interview posted on Thursday Carnage, you have to have the roleplay emailed to me by 12:00am Tuesday. Roleplays for Monday Night Warfare must be emailed to me by 12:00am EST time Saturday Night. No exceptions to these deadlines unless I find you have a seriously good reason for them being late.
Also, we will only have one card until we get at least 14 wrestlers. Strat deadlines are the same as roleplay deadlines. Remember when writing up a strat if you're using someone else's character it must be alright with them.
Strats are to be written out in full detail as to what you want to have happen. Strats range from, Suprise commentary, Sneak Attack, Surprise Interview, and so on and so on. These will help you a lot in winning and also show me that you are putting some effort behind your wrestler and his career.
Match writers will be required to have their assigned match or matches emailed to me by 12:00am the day before the card. All matches will be assigned to the match writers by me on the day the card is send out. When writing a match do not put in any Strats that were not sent to you by ME. In other words, do not put in a Strat if it was sent to you by a person in that match. You are to only write what I assign to you, if you didn't hear the ok from me don't do it. Not sending in a match on time can result in termination of your match writing contract. If for some reason you can't write any matches for a week, you must let me know at least 4 to 5 days beforehand so I can find someone else. I enforce these rules strictly because I want to have cards that are on time and well done.
03.) Events: EWF has 2 cards held each week ( 1 until we get 14 members). The Thursday Carnage, which is a strictly news/rumor/highlights/small matches show airing every Thursday at 8:00pm EST time. I will have an interview usually with the wrestler of the week.
EWF's 2nd card is the actual event where the matches take place. It's called Monday Night Warfare, which airs every Monday at 9:00pm EST Time. I will usually schedule 3 to 7 matches each week for Warfare depending on the roster size. Roleplays for this card should be taken place somewhere in the EWF Arena, unless you're not scheduled to be fighting and then it's ok to have your RP take place anywhere you choose. I've made the deadline for this card Saturday at 12:00am EST Time because I want to be able to make good judgements and have time to put together a nice card each Monday. Remember cards can be late due to the Real World so no complaining.
EWF will also have a supercard every month. The supercards will be held in place of Monday Night Warfare and all roleplays will be due on the Friday prior to the event, at 12:00am EST Time. So I will have enough time to make a great pay-per view. Every title must be defended at each pay-per view.
List of Supercards: • January - Hell 2 Come • February - Love is Lost • March - • April - CHAOS • May - • June - Meltdown • July - FireWorx • August - Shadows Fall • September - • October - All Hallows Eve • November - Thanksgiving Feast • December - Holiday Hell To get a match on Warfare you must send me an email with who you want the match with. Remember I will only be having 3 to 7 matches on Warfare so I don't get in something I can't handle. So the best matches that I think will make a good warfare will be chosen from the requests. So if you're having an angle with someone let me know because I'll put angle based matches on before a "just because" match. I'll be hand writing all the events no simulators will be used.
I'm going to have people apply for match writing jobs and hopefully have good match writers on the staff.
04.) How to win in EWF All matches are decided by ME and only ME, so what I say goes. I judge matches on your ability to out roleplay your opponent. Meaning, If you wrote 2 really good rps better then your opponents 1 really good rp you win. If you wrote 1 really good rp better then your opponents 2 alright rps you win. If you write 2 really good rps better then your opponents 2 alright rps you win, as simple as that. Basically whoever shows the most talent and effort win. I judge roleplays on your ability to become your wrestler, staying in character, staying on the subject and doing it better then your opponent. Remember if you want to reach
Superstar status you'll need to do just that. Bringing yourself up to Superstar status is probably one of the most important things and biggest goal you should have when writing up a flash and angles, it won't happen over night though.
Angles are a big part of the EWF as well. You won't win every single match you have. You will eventually loose for the sake of an angle. I want all angles to be thought up by you guys, the wrestlers. But if your wrestler is hurting for some heat and an angle I will make some up. In order for angles to become official I need all the wrestlers or staff members written consents. In other words I want emails from each wrestlers own email letting me know you are ok with it.
Another good way to get yourself in the spot light and get noticed is by becoming the Wrestler of the Week. I'm going to pick one wrestler of the week each week. How I will choose that wrestler is, I'll be looking at who had the best Roleplays and Participation for that current week. And the wrestler of the week will be announced on Tuesday after Monday Night Warfare. After being choosen to be Wrestler of the Week your salary will be raised. Becoming wrestler of the week can really help you out a lot with your status and salary.
It's also possible to be wrestler of the week two times in a row. If that should happen it is an automatic increase in status.
05.) Wrestlers Salary and Status: I've seen this in a few feds before and liked it. I think it brings more of a realistic feel to the whole thing. Your wrestler will be given a salary depending on your skill level. If you're a main eventer you'll be making the big bucks and getting more opportunities then the lower ranked wrestlers. The higher ranked you are the more money you'll be making. Wrestlers will be wrestling wrestlers with similar salaries. How do you get ranked as a main eventer? Read the part above about how to win in EWF. Dedication and Effort are what will bring you to main eventer status. When you join you are automatically started at Rookie status. Yes maybe you're not a rookie to E-wrestling but you are a Rookie to EWF. Even if I know you should be at superstar status you'll start at Rookie. To sum it up, you start at Rookie status NO MATTER WHAT, you've got to work you're way up to Superstar status. Some will do it faster then others depending on their talent. There is however one exception to that rule. If you left EWF on good terms for whatever reason and you decide you'd like to come back you would be started at one level lower then the status you were when you left. Your salary is determined on what I think you deserve when first joining. Then after it's all up to you to bring your status and salary up by doing exactly what it says in the "How to Win" section of the rules.
Salaries and Status will be adjusted as I see fit, show me you deserve to be a Mid-Carder and I'll make you one. If you can't show dedication and lack of heat/angles will result in a decrease in salary and eventually a decrease in your status. Here are the current statuses...
Superstar (Highest status you can reach) - Income: $100,000 to $500,000 Mid-Carder (Second highest status you can reach) - Income: $50,000 to $95,000
Rookie (Lowest status you can reach) - Income: $10,000 to $45,000
06.) EWF Championships: EWF is going to have 4 singles titles and 1 tag team title. All titles must be defended at every Supercard. I don't want more then 3 singles titles because where would the competition be if there was 5 or 6 titles? I think with only 3 titles there will be good feuds and keep things interesting. I always see feds with a title for everything they can think of and that just makes things boring because everyone has a freckin title. Here is a list of the championships...
EWF World Title Championship Title - Most prominent and important title Title Defenses: At least twice a month on Warfare and at Every Supercard EWF Intercontinental Championship Title - Second best title Title Defenses: At least three times a month on Warfare and at Every Supercard
EWF Hardcore Champion Title - Third best title
Title Defenses: Every Week on Warfare and at Every Supercard EWF Television Championship Title - Fourth best title Title Defenses: At least three times a month on Carnage, and Once on Warfare and at Every Supercard
EWF Tag Team Championship Titles - Belts for the best tag team Title Defenses: At least twice a month on Warfare and at Every Supercard 07.) EWF Roster: The EWF roster size is going to be about 20-30. If things go well for EWF and people keep sending in apps after the roster is full to be put on the waiting list, I'm going to have a contract review. A contract review will be held every month. You will be notified of it as soon as I know the date its scheduled. Now a contract review is when the roster is at its maximum number of wrestlers, and there is a bunch of people that sent in applications to be on the waiting list.
Once a month has passed I'll be looking through all the applications to see if there is any new talent possibly talent to replace a person on the roster that isn't as good. Therefore in order to keep your spot on the roster you need to keep up the good work and stay up to our standards. If you can't stay up to standards you will be released from EWF and replaced with someone who can.
There will also be a contract review when a wrestler resigns or retires from
EWF. I will choose the best one out of them all and contact you by email telling you you're welcome to fill the empty spot. If you don't want it anymore
I will move onto the next best, and so on. These rules might change I still have to work out everything.
You are allowed to have 2 single wrestlers or 1 tag team. If you join up as singles and become friends with another wrestler on the roster you can form a tag team as well as being a singles wrestler. If you join up as a tag team you must wrestle tag matches and occasionally wrestle singles. You can't break the tag team up so they can be singles wrestlers until you can prove you can handle both as singles.
When joining remember only Orginal wrestlers allowed. No real wrestlers, and no real wrestler rip offs. I look for orginality and someone that can keep that persona in their roleplays. Also when writing out your wrestlers appearance description, make sure that you are perfectly clear on what you want your
wrestler to look like.
