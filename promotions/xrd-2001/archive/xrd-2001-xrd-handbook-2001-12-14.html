__________________ |___XRD Handbook___| Welcome to the XRD, the eXtreme wRestling feDeration. This is an RP based e-fed. We use a money system to help us keep track of who wrestles who. Everyone starts out with a rookie paycheck, which is $100,000 a year. As your wrestler progresses your pay check gets bigger. This fed is owned by me, [name withheld]. I have been into the e-wrestling scene for about 3 years now, I have attempted to run a few feds, but none yet have been successful. What makes this fed different than all the others he has tried to run, you may be asking yourself. Well, I have been planning this fed out for about a few months now, before I always jumped the gun, created a website and immediately started recruiting. However this time, I took my time and wrote the rules.
If you have any questions, you can email me at [detail withheld] , I will gladly answer any and all questions. And I will also respond back to you within 24 hours! _____________________________________________ I. Introduction II. Titles III. Cards IV. Roleplaying V. Commentators and Interviewers _______________ |___Introduction___| eXteme wRestling feDeration is a brand new E-fed thought up by me [name withheld]. I have decided to once again try and make a successful E-wrestling Federation. This time though I'm making up my own rules on how I've always wanted a fed to be run. How is that you might ask? Well I'll tell you, I've decided to make a E-fed thats perfect for those people who love the game but don't have enough time to be online roleplaying every second. Thats right this is a fed for those people that have lives and can't dedicate it entirely to E-wrestling like some of those freaks out there. I like
E-wrestling a lot, I've been involved in it since 1997, but since I've gotten older my time has grown shorter. I was always having to quit feds because of lack of time because my LIFE comes FIRST. I want to be involved in it some way so I thought
I'd start my own fed again but this time, think out and plan everything before jumping the gun and opening to early. Making a fed for those who have the same problem as me. I would also like to note that I will ALWAYS be updating the site. I always hated feds that never updated, so I'll be updating all the time. I'm also going to keep the design fairly simple and easy but appealing. Below is the list of rules and descriptions for all the answers to your questions. If you like what you see fill out an application.
__________ |___Titles___| The XRD has 4 single title and One tag title. All titles will be defended at every supercard
Some titles may be defended on Warfare and Carnage. XRD World Title Championship Title - Most prominent and important title Title Defenses: At least twice a month on Warfare and at Every Supercard XRD Intercontinental Championship Title - Second best title Title Defenses: At least three times a month on Warfare and at Every Supercard XRD Hardcore Champion Title - Third best title Title Defenses: Every Week on Warfare and at Every Supercard XRD Television Championship Title - Fourth best title Title Defenses: At least three times a month on Carnage, and Once on Warfare and at Every Supercard
XRD Tag Team Championship Titles - Belts for the best tag team Title Defenses: At least twice a month on Warfare and at Every Supercard ___________ |___Cards___| The XRD has 2 cards a week( One until we get 14 members ). There is the Monday Night Warfare Card, which is our biggest weekly event, All titles can be defended but wrestlers are not required to defend their titles.
Titles can be defended in any type of match, the title holder has the right-of-choice about what type of match to defend his title in.
At Carnge, titles can be defended, but they will not be defended in special matches, (ie: cage, ladder, table). All titles will be defended in a regular match, unless instructed by me or the president.
Monday Night warfare Our biggest weekly card. Title defenses take place, special matches. Few interviews, Few debuts, etc.
Thursday Carnage Smaller card, more interviews, few title defenses, no special matches, wrestler of the week interview.
_______________ |___Roleplaying___| There are not many basic rules to Roleplaying. There is no limit, and there is no minimum, but based on what I have seen so far, 20 lines isn't going to cut it. You can curse as much as you would like in your rp's. But do not say anything racial, things like calling a wrestler gay,homo, queer, etc. is ok, but don't say anything that referrs to a persons skin color. If you do, you will be fired on the spot. You are also required to
RP at least twice in two weeks time. So that means if you don't have a match on either Carnage or Warfare, you still are required to Rp twice to let us know you still are alive. We also accept strats (strategies). Strats are things like Interviews, sneak attacks, or interfearences. All strats have the same deadline as the card. The RP deadline for the card is as followed
Warfare: Sunday Midnite Carnage: Wednesday Midnite PPV: Friday Midnite( takes place on Sunday ) __________________ |___Commentators___| Ringside Commentator: Doug Lynard Rindside Commentator: Chris Conover Interviewer: Angel Interviewer: Michael Davidson Interviewer: Richard Collins Interviewer: Johnny Sylo Ring Announcer: Angel ____________________________________________________ © 2001 [name withheld], Inc.
