XRD: Full Contact: 26 Man Tournament!!

19 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The XRD is currently hosting a 26 man tournament. To visit the site for the tournament, <a href="
http://xrdfc.cjb.net ">Click Here</a>. Get your fed noticed! Join Today! We will start when we have 26 members. Think you can handle it? Think you got the skill to survive? Find Out!!!
****************************************************** First off let me explain how this tournament works. We are taking in 26 applications, so that means 26 wrestlers. We will have 13 matches to start off.. then the thirteen that win will go on and face in the second round. We will have 6 matches there, one match will be a triple threat. Then we will have three matches..and the winners of those matches will face in a triple threat match. For the Full Contact championship. I hope to make this a yearly event.
So come on, show your skill, and be a part of the first Full Contact Tournament!! Rules - The rules for this tournament are going to be similar to the rules of the XRD. Roleplaying - Wrestlers are required to rp at least once for every match that they compete in. There is no maximum number of times you can RP, but we do limit the count to one RP per day. The Roleplaying is done on a closed email list, so there is no spam mail involved. Also there are no one line rp's allowed.. we require a minimum of twenty lines per RP. If you lose your match in the tournament, you are not allowed back in.. but you do have the choice of staying on the mailing list. We do allow cursing in your rp's, but we do not allowed Racial Comments.. If I see you have written a racial comment, I will kick you out of the tournament on the spot. I don't care if it is the finals..
we do not put up with racism. Events - Events will take place on a Sunday, which means RPs will be due on
Friday. Like I explained above, there are a total of 23 matches.. the matches will be placed over a period of 4 weeks. A card every week. I will send out the new card, following the results every Sunday.
Winning - All winners are based on the quantity of their rps.. nothing else. You might be asking.. well, if I were to join this tournament, what will I get if I win? Well the answer is simple.. The winner of the tournament not only gets the title, first ever Full Contact Champion, but they will also receive a full contract with the XRD, and if they decide to go with the XRD. They will participate in a match to be the first XRD Champion at the first XRD Supercard.
