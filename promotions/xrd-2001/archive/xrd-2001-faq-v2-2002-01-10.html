Roleplaying 5. Strats 6. XRD TV 7. Supercards 8. Unhandled Staff 9. Application ***** 1. What is XRD and who runs it? Well, first off I'll explain what XRD means. It's actually pretty simple. XDR stands for the eXtreme wRestling feDeration. The XRD is a new e-fed run by me, [name withheld]. This is my third attempt at running a successful fed.
And so far, so good! The XRD will bring you great match-ups between two or more great wrestlers. Matches can vary from Standard Matches to Death Matches. Like
I said before, I own this federation, my job is to keep it up and running, although I have a few staff members to help out with writing cards and things, I am in charge of everything. The XRD is based on the east coast, in the city of Orlando,
Florida. Although, unlike most e-feds, we will not stay in the Orlando area. We will have cards in all of the great cities of the United States.
2. Rules - First off, although cursing is allowed in this federation, Racial language isn't tolerated. The first time someone uses racial language, they will be fired, they will not be issued a warning, they will be fired on the spot.
-Secondly, attitude. For most of the days I am in a good mood. But when someone tries to cross my boundaries, I can become like a rotweiler guarding his territory. If someone tries to second base my judgement, I will not put up with it, if someone thinks they can walk all over the fed and these rules, you can guarantee you will never see them in this federation again.
-Third, participation. Participation is the key to a good fed, without participators, your fed will just plain out suck. That's why everyone in the XRD is required to participate, everyone is required to RP at least once a week, even if you don't have a match, you are required to do a strat, just to let us know you are still alive and with this federaion. If someone does not RP, they will be issued a warning, and after the second Warning, well... Three
Strikes and your out.. -And last, but definitely not least. Have Fun, that is the point of these e-feds, to give you something to do when your friends are still doing homework, or when your in a fight with some friends, or when your grounded. We are always here to fall back on, and we can give you something to do, always.
3. Titles XRD is going to have 4 singles titles and 1 tag team title. All titles must be defended at every Supercard. I don't want more then 3 singles titles because where would the competition be if there was 5 or 6 titles? I think with only 3 titles there will be good feuds and keep things interesting. I always see feds with a title for everything they can think of and that just makes things boring because everyone has a freckin title.
Here is a list of the championships... XRD World Title Championship Title - Most prominent and important title Title Defenses: At least twice a month on Breakdown and at Every Supercard This is our major title. This is the title every XRD superstar craves. If you can get your hands on this title, you better be prepared to do some major RP'ing because you will be involved in some of the most halacious angles and matches the XRD has ever put on.
XRD Intercontinental Championship Title - Second best title Title Defenses: At least three times a month on Breakdown and at Every Supercard This is our second major title. Matches for this title will not be big, but they will have stipulations. Matches can vary for this title, they can be almost anykind of match you can think of except a Japanese Death Match.
XRD Hardcore Champion Title - Third best title Title Defenses: Every Week on Breakdown and at Every Supercard This is my favorite title. This title can take place anytime! all matches for this title are harcore, so there are no table matches or ladder matches or whatever.
XRD Television Championship Title - Fourth best title Title Defenses: Every Breakdown and at Every Supercard This is our smaller title, this is for the wrestler who looks best on TV, the wrestler who looks best on TV is determined by the wrestlers attitude, gimmick, and the wrestlers record.
XRD Tag Team Championship Titles - Belts for the best tag team Title Defenses: At least twice a month on Breakdown and at Every Supercard Right now this is the only title for a tag team, pretty soon, once we get more tag teams we will create a Hardcore Tag Team Title. But this title can take place between at least two teams, and it can be anykind of match.
4. Roleplaying I mentioned earlier that this is an RP fed, RP means Roleplay. So in other words, Roleplaying is a must! If you do not roleplay you will be issued up to 2 warnings. After that, well 3 strikes and your out, You get the picture. There is no minimum for roleplaying, and there is no maximum, we let you take care of that. With no minimum and no Maximum, let your character get carried away, get caught up in the moment, these are all suggested ideas for roleplaying. Try your best to stay in character, and you will be rewarded by fighting in a title bout.
5. Strats Strats (strategies) are a good way to introduce your wrestler, tell about your wrestler, introduce a tag partner, talk about a match, and create a good angle. Strat's can be anything from interviews to interfearences. If you would like to create strat, then all you have to do is email me and put Strat in the Subject and tell me how you want your strat to be. And I will include it in the card.
6. XRD TV XRD TV is on Monday and Thursday. We are on our own Channel, it's called XRD TV ( Figures huh? ). We are on from 8-10 on those nights. Those are when our card's are. 7. Supercards Every month, XRD has a Supercard ( aka a PPV ). These will be our biggest cards, they will take place according to my schedule. If you have a request as of when the
Supercard should be, email me, and I will gladly try to see if it works out. 8. Unhandled Staff Unhandled Staff are people like the referees, ring announcers, ringside commentators, and interviewers.

Michael Kaline - Also known as Mickey Kaline, Michael usually speaks his mind, no matter what circumstances he and Jay don't get along to well, but Michael isn't afraid to say something to anyones' face.
