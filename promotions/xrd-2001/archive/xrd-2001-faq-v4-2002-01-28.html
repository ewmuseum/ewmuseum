Commissioner 2. Talent Coordinator 3. Enforcer C. Wrestlers -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------- 1. What is the XRD? A. E-feds The XRD is an email federation. Which takes place over the internet, and allows almost anyone to join, as long as they can roleplay with a fake wrestler.
The XRD uses an open email list, which means that all email address can be viewed by anyone in the federation. B. Roleplay Like most e-feds on the internet, this fed runs souly on the roleplays we expect to see from our wrestlers. Without roleplaying... there would be no point in having an e-fed. This is where you guys out there come in.. We need people like you to help keep this federation alive... we need active roleplayers.. we can only do so
much to get you to RP.. and we do that much. 2. Who runs the XRD? A. About [name withheld] The XRD is run by one person, and one person only.. [name withheld]. Although I
Will have some help with writing cards.. no other person will run the XRD. I am 15 years old and I have had 4 years experience in e-fedding. Since I was
Eleven years old.. I have ran a few feds as well as handled such characters as Justin Tyme and Spyder. Although none of my feds have made it into the big leagues.. most of my feds were open for quite a while..I did all I possibly could do to influence the wrestlers to rp.. and much to my amazement.. they did! I had one fed that ran for quite a while.. it was called the NCW.. I ran it back in 1999-2000.. I'm not exactly sure for how long it stayed open.. but I know that I had alot of good rp'ers..
and now here I am trying to run another fed.. and hopefully it will be succesful.. Because I can tell you one thing.. I am going to put 110% into this fed.. No matter how many RPs are sent out.
3. How does the XRD Work? A. Events The XRD has 1 event weekly as well as a flash show. Breakdown, the weekly event, takes place on Mondays. That means the Results and the following Breakdown will be sent out on Monday. On Breakdown, you can expect to see such matches as title matches, special matches, pretty much anything you can think of can happen on Breakdown. Trashtalk is our weekly flash show. It will not begin until about a week or two into the fed. Trashtalk will have one or two matches, a few interviews, and a few rumors will be addressed. Once a month we will have a supercard/PPV. All Titles will be on the line during these events.. angles will begin, angles will be resolved, and angles will be boosted. You can also expect a match
which makes the Supercard what it is(ie: Royal Rumble) B. Winners The Winners of the matches are based souly on the QUALITY of Roleplays. That means you can send
out as many roleplays as you would like, but that doesn't mean you will win. When I receive and review your application, I get a good idea who your character is, and how your character is.
Whether he is cocky or he is a kiss-ass. This is what I base the winners on.. if you stay in good character, you have a good chance of winning. Also, if I believe that if a certain person
wins, an angle will be enhanced.. I might make that person win.. There is always a good excuse
why the winner wins.. and if you want to know, please.. email me or instant message me and I will
be more than happy to explain it to you to where you understand. 4. What positions are available in the XRD? A. Writers Although not the most important spot that needs to be filled, it is certainly a necessity. Until
I get a few talented Card Writers on the staff, Matches will be shorter than normal.. But they still
will be PBP(Play-By-Play). Card writers are usually asked to writer 2-3 matches per card, depending
on how big the card is. Cards will be written by hand, and not some crummy simulator. Matches will
go into small detail, but until I get some writers, they will not go into major detail. B. Front Office As the XRD progresses, I plan on getting 2-3 people to help me with making matches, creating angles,
etc. I plan on getting a Commissioner, a Talent-Coordinator, and an Enforcer. 1. Commissioner The Commissioner of the XRD will be my right hand man, he will be in charge of helping me create matches on cards, as well as give me ideas for angles. He is also the guy that is is in charge of the other two positions, the enforcer and the
talent-coordinator. If they have any problems, they need to come to him. He is always there to help out anyone. He is also the person who will continue to run the fed when I am on vacation, or for some reason
I can't be online.
2. Talent Coordinator The Talent Coordinator is in charge of making sure that we have a good wrestler. He is the guy that goes out and spreads the word about the XRD. He also makes sure that wrestlers who don't roleplay are taken off of the roster. He makes sure that the roster is full at all times.
3. Enforcer The Enforcer is the man who makes sure that when wrestlers roleplay, they keep within the rules. If he believes that a wrestler has broken a rule, he has the right to whatever is necessary to punish the wrestler as well as make sure it doesn't happen again.
He can fire someone if he feels it is needed. But he has to run it through me first, and I will have a meeting seperately and together with both the wrestler and the enforcer.
C. Wrestlers This is a definite Must! We Need Wrestlers more than we need anything! The More wrestlers the better.
But don't close this out that quick. I plan on having a small roster of at the maximum 30 wrestlers.
I want to maintain a roster of at least 15 wrestlers at ALL times. We need wrestlers badly. So Come On!
