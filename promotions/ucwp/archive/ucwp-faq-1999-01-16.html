Introduction 2. Roleplays 3. Cards 4. Push System 5. Titles 6. Staff 7. Application 8. Closing ________________________________________________________________ 1. Introduction The UCWP is being developed by myself, [name withheld], in the winter of 1998. I have got a vast experience of running an EWrestling federation, as I ran the SPWO for two years. I have also written for federations with the calibre of the SCRA, TOP and the HHWO. The UCWP is being developed as a light-hearted federation, purely aimed as fun in the beginning, although it is also looking to be fairly sucessful.
Therefore, it will be a bi-weekly federation, meaning less participation from all will be needed than in a normal federation.
It will be based in Canada, and shall hold shows every week from there. Simon [name withheld] himself, former SPWO president and EWrestler will be on the fictional staff, along with some other EWrestling creations.
________________________________________________________________ 2. Roleplays It is well known that a roleplay is what makes your character good. However, while many federations look for _quantity_ in a roleplay, the UCWP is looking for _quality_ and quality only. Moreover, character development is the essential doing, in a roleplay, and that is what shall make you an UCWP World Heavyweight champion. Going out of character won't get you far in the UCWP.
There are certain words which the UCWP will not permit, so that we can get the ''realness'' into the federation. These words are, ''Face, Heel, Jobber, Roleplay, Angle.'' Replacements for these words _must_ be used, otherwise you will be fined [see money section] for breaking kayfebe, so to speak.
Another important part of a roleplay is cursing. Cursing is allowed in the UCWP, so long as it is fully [CENSORED] or [BLEEPED] out. Also, to make the roleplay seem more realistic, try to stick to words that you would hear on WWF television.
Please don't go over the edge. It's very unlikely that it will impress me. Scenes are another part of your roleplay, where your wrestler is. Descriptive scenes help to get the reader involved and willing to read the roleplay. These should be done in great detail, and can also play a large part in developing your wrestlers character. A scene such as the following. . .
[Mr. X stands in his locker room then starts to talk.] . . .is not nearly as intresting as. . . [Fade in.] [The camera opens up on a beautiful setting. The scene is obviously in the countryside as we see hills and trees and animals. The sun is out and shines down on the hills. The camera then starts to move and it seems as though the camera man is beginning to walk forward. He seems to be joined by another camera man as we can hear low mumbling. ]
[After a bit, the pair arrive at the large gates in front of a large, impressive looking mansion. The gates are large, too high to climb and they are also locked. One of the camera man is seen moving nervously forward and then pressing the button which is on the gates. Instantly, a door is opened from the mansion and out steps an elegantly dressed butler. He comes over to the gates and asks the man what they want. The other camera man replys that they would like to speak to the Slave. The butler gives them a filthy look, but allows them to come through the gates.]
Get the point? Anyway, everyone has different styles of roleplaying and we'll leave it at that.
________________________________________________________________ 3. Cards [UCWP Turmoil] Held every week from Canada, UCWP Turmoil is the main show of the week for the federation. On this show, we will see around six matches, and we will also see angles and in-ring interviews. The show will be written out in traditional style
[style used by SCRA, IIWF, etc.] and will be in excellent quality. The federation surrounds this show.
[UCWP Re-Cap] This show gives house shows results, develops angles, brings news, rankings, Pay-Per-View updates, etc. ________________________________________________________________ 4. Pushes ''How do I get to the top?'' The ECWP works by the push system. That means that however well you have been working, you get that push going into your match. It is a complex system which means that if someone is unlucky after working really hard one week, they may win easily the following week. It will be explained more clearly to you at a late date. Bonus pushes can be gotten though if you use the following. . .
[In-ring interviews] These are always nice to see on cards. They provide the writer and reader a welcome break from the card, and gives your wrestler the chance to talk freely about a current UCWP topic.
[Angle Segments] These are great. An example was the Steve Austin/Goldust toilet incident before the Royal Rumble or the Undertaker dressing up as
Kane, etc. Either give me your ideas and I'll develop them for you or simply write them yourself. Either way, this will _really_ help you in the UCWP.
Anyway, you are all good roleplays, and I am sure you know what is required from you. ________________________________________________________________ 5.
