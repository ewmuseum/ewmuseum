Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Ultimate Canadian Wrestling Promotion introduced itself in a founding FAQ posted 16 January 1999, describing development over the winter of 1998 by an operator with several years already behind them running and writing for other e-feds. UCWP pitched itself as light-hearted and quality-over-quantity: roleplays that leaned on real wrestling words like “face,” “heel,” or “angle” were fined for “breaking kayfebe,” and the FAQ asked handlers to write for character development over sheer volume.

Based in Canada and run on a bi-weekly rather than weekly rhythm, UCWP staged two regular programs: “UCWP Turmoil,” its traditional-style main card of roughly six matches, angles, and in-ring interviews, and “UCWP Re-Cap,” carrying house-show results, developing angles, and rankings. A push system decided outcomes, rewarding handlers whose roleplays had earned momentum going into a match. Four championships anchored the roster: the UCWP World Heavyweight, Canadian Heritage, Cruiserweight, and Tag Team titles.

The last surviving document runs through early March 1999, still producing weekly cards on schedule. No closure notice survives, and how UCWP's own run ended isn't known.
