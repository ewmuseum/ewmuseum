Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The NEW AGE Wrestling Federation kept its front door on one GeoCities account and everything behind it on another. The door survives, all three pieces of it: a bare two-frame shell, the navigation column that filled the left frame, and a splash page that filled the right, promising "Wrestling For The New Age" and a place to sign up. Nothing behind the door does.

The navigation is the real record, and it is unsparing. Beside the roster link it says 10/31. Beside the stables, the belts, the belt history, the shows and the schedule it says n/a. On the day that page was last written — 31 October 2000, the same date it puts against the roster — this fed had one roster and five empty rooms, and the roster is among the thirteen pages it linked that no archive anywhere still holds. What survives is a federation caught in the act of getting ready.

This is a distinct promotion from the held NAW (New Age Wrestling, naw.type-dfx.com, a Primetime Central member fed that opened at the Cow Palace in March 2003) — a different, longer name and a different address, with nothing in either record connecting the two; not merged.

Storylines

Nothing here ever got as far as a story Written by AI, 2026

Not a single wrestler, title, card, stable or match date is named anywhere in the three documents this fed left behind. What survives is a front door and a signpost: a frameset, a navigation column and a welcome page inviting people to sign up. The angles page, the chat page, the roster, the belts, the belt history, the cards and the schedule are all either marked "n/a" by the fed itself or simply gone, and the roleplays that would have carried a story went to an off-site message board that this collection does not hold. There is no angle, no turn and no feud to recount — only the invitation.
