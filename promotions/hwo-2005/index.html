Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The hWo relaunched on 8 July 2005, by its owner's own count its third version, with a roster of five or six players and a Los Angeles arena that existed only on its site. Its first pay-per-view, Scarred For Life, aired on 17 July and crowned a National Champion; a series called Vendetta followed. The owner's own verdict on his opening show was that the matches pleased him more than the segments.

The record is two weeks of forum threads, the fed's home page of August 2005 and a Vendetta 6 report from Albany on 27 September 2005 in which the former owner walks back in. None of its results pages is held.
