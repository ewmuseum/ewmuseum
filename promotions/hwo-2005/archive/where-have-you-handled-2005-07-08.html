Jesse Jamester — 8 July 2005 I almost joined NTICW when I was in PWA and LOW at the same time. Before they started the rule of you could only be in PWA if you had intentions on staying. A guy in NTICW that I knew, believe he went by Biohazard, offered me a spot on the roster. I said no cause I was loyal to LOW and knew I'd have more fun their, plus I didn't want to restart. Geez, was that a good idea.
All in all, NTICW was a decent fed. Surely ruined in it's name as the fed continued to grow and die over periods of time, but it was definitely a top fed in the WO circuit, and had great roleplayers and angles.

The 'indy fed' image really was part of its charm, but once Ron bought it, it lost it's image, and then it's reputation. A few of us original NTICWers clubbed together and attempted to recreate it's image, but most of the original guys either left the EW circuit for good or had other commitments.
