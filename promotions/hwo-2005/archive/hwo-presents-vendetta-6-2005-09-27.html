hWo Presents Vendetta 6

27 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
hWo Presents Vendetta 6 Live From Albany, New York September 27th, 2005 The Shocker As the live Internet Broadcasting of Vendetta got under way, Jack Foley and his goons stood in the ring. The live crowd began through their garbage at the four men. Ron White and “Madd” Miller swatted the garbage away before it hit Jack Foley.
Jack Foley kept a smile going the whole time. Tonight, Fosters had to make his decision and Jack Foley wanted the answer right now. “It’s Tuesday Fosters, deadline day. I hope you’ve made the correct choice. Cause for the sake of your health, I hope you have.”
“So Payne…come on down.” Jack Foley waved for Payne Foster to come to the ring. No music came up on the stereo system, instead a tired look Payne Fosters came walking down to the ring. He walked up the steel steps and entered into the ring. The Fans gave Payne Fosters a Standing Ovation.
Fosters walked right up to Jack Foley. “So Foster what will it be…Put the title into my hands and walk away, or have you career ended come born to bleed?” Jack Foley said.
He waited for an answer, as Payne Fosters looked out to the fans. Jack looked at his watch. “Let’s hear it…we don’t have all night.” “…” Payne Fosters mumbled something under his breath. Jack leaned up putting his ear near Fosters mouth. “What? I couldn’t hear you.” Foster grabbed a firm grip on his title… CRACK! The title hit Jack Foley right in the side of the head, knocking him right out. “It just isn’t worth it.” Payne Foster stood over Jack Foley with a smile on his face. He lift the title up over his head for everybody to see, then just let it drop. It fell right on to Jack Foley face, as Foster turned around and held his hands out. “Madd”
Miller, Ron White, And Stitches all rush Payne Fosters, taking him down.
The beating began. One after one they took turns beating on Payne Foster. Ron White hit his finisher, so did Stitches, and “Madd” Miller. The crowd booed, as the assault continued until Payne Fosters lay in a pool of his own blood.
Ron White helped Jack Foley up and out of the ring. As “Madd” Miller picked up the title and exited the ring.
The crowd was in shock, as EMTS ran to Fosters aid. The garbage tossing never stopped, as the four of them tried to make it backstage safely. It wasn’t going to be that easy, as a very familiar man stepped out from behind the black curtain, bring all four men to a stop.
Jack Foley had a look of shock and terror on his face. Stitches and “Madd”
Miller stood in front of Jack Foley, to protect him from this man.
The garbage came to a stop, as the crowd grew silent. Waiting to hear him speak, to listen to what he was going to say, it was...Ex. how owner Trevor Echo. “You know, in an honest opinion I could give two shits about the hWo. I didn’t even want to come and show my face here…but my Ex. Wife called in all my favors I owed, so as you can see I’m here right now.”
Echo said.
He took a step closer to Jack Foley, but Stitches and “Madd” Miller made it impossible for Echo to come face to face with Foley.
Echo smirked. “Jack Foley me and you both have something in common. Where both business men, we have are tricks, and I’ve seen your tricks…but now see my tricks.”
Ten unidentified men came threw the crowd, and grabbing onto the four men. They began leading them into the backstage area of the arena, but Trevor Echo grabbed one by the shoulder.
He turned to the man and mumbled. “Teach them lesson, and then get rid of them.” He waited for them to disappear behind the black curtain. “The Internet broadcasting will end after I walk backstage, but you people here live will bare witness to some of the newest hWo talent brought in by me.
Born to Bleed will still go down, and Davis Echo will resume ownership once again…” Trevor Echo dropped the microphone and made his exit.
For the first time ever, a hWo crowd had cheered Trevor Echo. Internet Broadcasting Ended. Catch Born To Bleed This Saturday. Just A Test
