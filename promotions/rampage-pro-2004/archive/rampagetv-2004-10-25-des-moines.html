Date: Monday October 25th Location: Des Moines, Iowa Venue: Veterans Auditorium You're television screen is black. Then, the countdown appears, starting at ten. Its that time again. Time for some pure wrestling action like only Rampage Pro can bring with with RampageTV. Will it be as good as last week? Will it live up to the feedback from the first show? Time will tell. Five...four...three...two...its time...one! Instead of the video package to "So Cold" by Breaking Benjamin, the image the cameras reveal is that of the parking lot. It is dark outside, and standing in the parking lot is Michael Cole. He looks at the camera and begins speaking.
Michael Cole: Good evening ladies and gentlemen, I'm Michael Cole and I am standing here in the parking lot of the Veterans Auditorium, where we are awaiting the arrival of Rampage Pro's Commissioner, Terry Funk. I am told he will arrive any second now and...wait just a second...
Michael Cole turns around and a large whit limousine pulls up, the license plate reads "FUNKR1" and a bull's skull is on the hood. Clearly, this has to be Terry Funk. The limousine comes to a complete stop and Cole opens the door. Out step a pair of cowboy boots covering gray sweat pants. Terry Funk steps out of the limo and looks around smiling. He then looks towards Michael Cole.
Michael Cole: Well, Terry Funk has arrived! Terry, I just want to ask you one question. Terry Funk: Why certainly Michael, anything. Michael Cole: Why did the Rampage Pro executives hire you to be the Commissioner of this company?
Terry Funk: Well Michael, the answer is quite simple. I know wrestling. Who better to book matches, and run things in the squared circle than me---
Suddenly, out of nowhere, Dusty Rhodes comes running into the camera's view and charges at Funk. The two collide and smash into the side of the limousine. Michael Cole runs away and the cameras stay to catch the brawl between the Vice President and Commissioner. The two exchange many lefts and rights. Referees, security, and even Vince Russo rush into the picture trying to separate the few. A couple referees get punched out by Funk, while Rhodes shoves down security guards. Funk and Dusty find each other once again and begin slugging it out. Vince Russo gets in the middle of it and both Terry and Rhodes shove him down. Russo gets to his feet in anger and storms off. Security comes back into the picture with more men and finally separate the two. The cameras switch to blackness. "So Cold" plays on the speaker system and video highlights of last week are shown. The cameras come back to live action to the jam packed, sold out, Veteran's Auditorium as pyro shoots off, with the song still playing. The cameras settle in on Mike Tenay and Gene Okerland sitting at their announce table.
Mike Tenay: Well, ladies and gentlemen, I don't know what to say. This situation between Terry Funk, the new Commissioner, and Dusty Rhodes, our Vice President of Operations, its certainly getting worse Gene.
"Mean" Gene Okerland: You can say that again Mike. These two have hated each other for years, certainly, the RP Executives knew what they were getting themselves into.
"Mean" Gene is interrupted by the instrumentals to Black Sabbath's song, "Iron Man". The crowd gives a mixed reaction, as Vince Russo comes down to the ring, with aggressive stomps. A microphone is in his hand. He climbs the steel steps quickly and gets into the ring. He raises the microphone to his lips and begins speaking.
Vince Russo: THAT'S IT! I've had it up to here with this crap! All I have been hearing from BOTH Dusty and Terry is that they want to kill one another. Its got to end. We can not run Rampage Pro with these fights happening all the time. So, Dusty, please come out here now, we have to talk.
The familiar theme of the American Dream plays throughout the arena and the crowd cheers. Dusty comes marching out and heads down to the ring. When Dusty gets about halfway down the aisle, however, Terry Funk comes running down from behind Dusty. Terry clubs Dusty in the back and the two begin to fight around the ring, while Russo screams and yells in anger.
Vince Russo: STOP IT! STOP IT! Russo drops the microphone and calls for people to come from out back. Referees and security come running down, but, as soon as they get to the fight, nothing happens. Nothing happens because Funk and Rhodes eliminate them before they can separate the brawl. Once all the referees and security guards are taken out, Dusty and Terry exchange blows again. Russo continues calling for someone to come down. And, low and behold, someone with authority comes out from behind the curtain. Shawn Michaels runs out and heads directly to the fight. He is standing behind Dusty Rhodes and taps him on the shoulder. Dusty turns around and when he does, he is met by some Sweet Chin Music! The crowd boos the instant Shawn kicks Dusty in the face. Shawn looks at the crowd in disgust and checks to see if Funk is alright. Shawn and Funk walk up the ramp together, side by side, leaving Dusty Rhodes laid out, and Vince Russo confused. The show goes to a promotional break.

Taz The show returns from its promotional break. "The Game" by Motorhead hits the speaker systems of the Veterans Auditorium and the crowd erupts into a chorus of boos. Triple H walks out onto the entrance stage and the boos get even louder. Hunter stops in the middle of the stage and looks down. He sips his water and throws the bottle into the crowd. He then looks to his left, and then to his right. He looks right in the camera and then spits his water. He gets into the ring and climbs to the second turnbuckle of the farthest ring post. He raises his arms and taunts the crowd.
Taz's theme hits the speaker system and the crowd gives a mixed reaction. Bill Alfonso runs out onto the stage blowing his whistle, like always. Taz then appears and doesn't take time to taunt the crowd or anything. He simply walks down the aisle, with a serious look on his face. Taz slides into the ring and stands in the closest corner. He crosses his arms and stares across the ring at Triple H. Alfonso gets out of the ring, and continues to blow his whistle. The crowd goes quiet as Taz's music fades. The referee calls for the bell and the match begins.

Taz doesn't budge and then shoves Hunter back. Triple H falls back and smacks down onto the canvas and gets up quickly only to get punched in the face by Taz. Hunter falls down again and gets up quickly and Taz kicks Triple H in the mid-section and gives him a vertical suplex. Taz gets up quickly, as does Triple H. Taz locks Hunter in a quick headlock but Hunter pushes him into the ropes. Taz bounces back and ducks a clothesline. Taz then grabs Hunter by the hair and throws him out of the ring. Taz paces back and forth, looking at Hunter with a serious expression. Hunter stands up outside the ring and waits a couple seconds to collect his thoughts. Taz waits patiently in the ring. Hunter climbs the steel steps and gets on the ring apron. Taz charges at Hunter but before he hits him, Hunter jumps down and Taz simply runs into the corner. Taz jolts back a little and Hunter slides into the ring quickly and charges at Taz. Hunter then gives him a clothesline. Taz gets back up and Hunter gives him a scoop slam. Triple H then hits Taz with a quick elbow drop. Hunter pins Taz but the human suplex machine kicks out at two. Hunter looks at the referee in disgust and then tells him to count faster.<P>
[- Mid-Match Notes -] Hunter grabs Taz's head and gets him to his feet, he locks Taz in the suplex position and goes to give him a snap suplex but Taz blocks it using his weight. Triple H tries a couple more times but Taz won't allow the Game to lift him. Taz then, seeing the chance, reverses it and hits Hunter with a suplex of his own. Taz floats over with the suplex and begins punching Triple H in the face. The referee starts counting to five. Taz gets as many shots in as he can, and gets off of Triple H at four, barely getting disqualified. Taz intimidates the referee by simply walking up to him. The referee gets out of Taz's way and Taz chuckles. He turns back to Triple H and walks over to him. Taz gets Hunter to his feet and hooks him into a full nelson. Taz then delivers a full nelson back suplex to Triple H. Hunter hits the canvas and is clearly in an intense amount of pain. Taz gets right back on the offensive, grabbing Hunter's hair and getting him to his feet. Taz locks Triple H in position and hits him with a T-Bone Suplex. Taz pins Hunter, hooking the leg, but Triple H gets a shoulder up at two. Taz gets a pissed expression on his face and looks around in anger. He gets up and signals that the match is soon to be over by slamming his arms down. He walks over to Triple H and slowly gets him to his feet. Taz goes to hook Hunter in the Tazmission but Triple H quickly fights it and elbows Taz in the stomach. Hunter then takes Taz and ddts him to the mat, Taz bounces off the canvas and lays on the mats motionless. Triple H looks down at his fallen opponent.
[- The Big Finish -] Hunter starts laughing. He grabs Taz by the head and gets him into position for the Pedigree. Hunter looks around the crowd and puts his thumb down. Hunter goes to give Taz the Pedigree but Taz reverses it and gives Hunter a back body drop. Taz slowly gets to his feet, as does Triple H. The two look at each other and begin exchanging blows. Taz runs into the ropes and bounces off, he comes running back at Hunter and goes for a clothesline. Triple H ducks it, kicks Taz in the mid-section and gives him the Pedigree! Hunter hooks the leg on the pin. 1...2...3!
Winner: Triple H "The Game" hits the public address system and Triple H gets his arm raised in victory. Hunter looks around and then he raises his arms, in celebration. Triple H walks past a motionless Taz and gets out of the ring. He then heads up the entrance aisle and looks back at his motionless victim. The cameras then cut and the show goes to a scene in the back.
