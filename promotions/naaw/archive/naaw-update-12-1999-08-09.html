First of all, the federation is generally becoming more recognized among people and we have been getting a lot of positive talk. Still some negative comments but even I can't please everyone :) Also, angles are starting to come together, I am getting help from writers and participation is picking up. As Sizzler was out a day late, Madness shall be pushed back a day, so it will now be out on Thursday rather than Wednesday, although I don't really think this makes a great difference as I'm sure you'll agree. I am also adding a new feature to Madness, stay tuned :) and in the coming weeks, I will be looking at ways to jazz up our shows. Already, this should have been evident in the way that Preview was presented. This is basically the pre-card roleplays but I have done it in a way that makes it more interesting to read. It might not please everyone, but I think it's an improvement, and I will try to improve the standard of all NAAW related work in the near future. Despite our recent improvement, I have to warn you that following the next Sizzler show, things may become a bit rocky for a short time. This is due to me returning to school, and basically, this is a massive year :) My entire career depends on this year, so naturally, I will have to make some adjustments, although I can still run the NAAW. All it means is Madness may be cancelled from time to time, and roleplays may just be sent like a flash packet. This period though is when I really need your help, and if I get it, I am 100% rather than 99.9% sure the NAAW shall survive. Further down the line, perhaps Feburary and again in May, we shall be going on short hiatus due to exams, but lets not worry about that yet :) Just to let you know :) Enjoy the present time, and keep up the good work. Adios. ________________________ News - WHAT HAPPENED TO THE CHAMP? Towards the end of Sizzler's Main Event, Andy Thomas was taken under the ring by a mystery man and was counted out, losing the match to Doomsday. Homeless Joe then emerged from underneath the ring, but there was no sign of the NAAW Heavyweight Champion! - GOTHAM HERITAGE CHAMP! Last week on Sizzler, president John Martin proclaimed a new title to the NAAW; the Heritage Title. Chris Gotham then came out and took the title, and he hit Martin on the head with it, proclaiming himself NAAW Heritage Champ. Gotham will be on Sizzler this week to explain his actions. - NEW TWIST IN LIGHTWEIGHT SAGA! Last week on Sizzler, the current holder of the Lightheavyweight Title Ryan Walters and Alain Fiscus attacked rightful champion "Spotlight" Stuart Taylor in the ring. However, as this was taking place, Tommy Brooks came through the crowd and took off with the title. Tune in next week to view the latest developments in this saga. - WEALTH OF NEW TALENT! NAAW President John Martin has recently been busy - securing a number of top stars to the federation! Head of the list of new recruits was former SCRA star "Legacy" Arthur Gybertson who shall soon be making his debut. He follows APWI star Brian Storm, Levi Ash, Kenneth Morlock, Mitch Gatlin and "Highlight" Dylan Ryder into the federation. However, the downside of this is that Martin has warned several of the federation's under-achieving stars to 'get their act together or face the boot'. He has hinted that roster space is precious and those not earning their pay will quickly be told to pack their bags and make way for new talent to the NAAW. - NAAW ON THE WWW! New Age American Wrestling has now been officially launced on the world wide web. The site, http://members.tripod.co.uk/naawwebmaster/index.html will feature updates, profiles, articles, news, information and much, much more. Interactive features shall hopefully soon be made available. - #NAAW now available! Moving with technology, the NAAW has launced it's own mIRC chatroom. Fans, officials and superstars of the federation are encouraged to meet in this room to discuss federation matters, and also become acquainted with fellow federation members. To get mIRC, go to www.mirc.com , download and enjoy. The #NAAW chatroom is available on stargate.startchat.com . ________________________ Roster OFFICIAL NAAW ROSTER ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ "True MC" Mark Bivins "Cherry Poppin" Ryan Walters Chris Williams Headhunter "Protector" Damon Robertson "Devastating" Damon Knight "Spotlight" Stuart Taylor "Firepower" Andy Thomas "Homeless" Joe Boxcar "Hot Stuff" Scott Richards King Agony Johnothan Hammer Engima "Lord of Darkness" Doomsday "Goldenboy" Jeff Keenan "Mr. Attitude" Kenny Kopecky Guru Jimmy "Doomsday" Wallace Tommy Graves "Superstar" Mark Johnson Adam Morley Aaron "Wildfire" Farner Chris Gotham "Hardcore Prodigy" Alain Fiscus "Sadistic" Shane Alexander "Ladies Man" Vince Taylor "Disciple" Eric Wayne "Showstopper" Scott Jamison Scott "Fop" Rogers Dan Debacle Devon Case "Malevolent" Dennis Lasombra Victor.
