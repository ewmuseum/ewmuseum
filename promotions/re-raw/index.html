A promotion whose own opening broadcast narrated itself as the rebirth of an earlier, closed federation of legends, running two full hardcore-flavored television broadcasts and a fan-voted championship tournament toward a debut pay-per-view in August 2005. Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Re-RAW's own opening broadcast, “Re-RAW At War,” aired 1 August 2005 and staged its own founding on-air: an in-fiction retrospective declared its predecessor, the RAW Wrestling Federation, dead, immediately followed by the words “Re-RAW LIVES!” and a new owner's own ring introduction. Broadcasters Sean O'Brien and RAW veteran Tommy Creighton called a card that opened with a tag match between developmental hopefuls Brett Read and Charles Paul, backed by David Rheaume and El Savaje; when Read tapped out without tagging in his partner, an enraged Rheaume turned a steel chair on his own man.

A second broadcast, “Re-RAW At War #2” (19 August 2005), unveiled the promotion's own top prize, the Re-RAW World Heavyweight Championship, as a fan-voted tournament ahead of its first pay-per-view, and ran under an “All Chairs Legal” stipulation after a backstage ambush the week before: Orin “The Lynx” LeBlanc had jumped Jay Ruby from behind mid-interview, and the broadcast opened by tracing that violence forward into the night's own card, ending on Rum Barton, Nick Demola and Doug McCray standing triumphant over a battered LeBlanc.

A third document, “Re-RAW In Retrospect #3” (27 August 2005), a studio recap show, carried a backstage confrontation between industrialist Werewolf Gregorson and an emissary, Casey Cool, sent by the promotion's own owner: Gregorson, having lost his own spot in the “Pick It Raw” rankings to LeBlanc, dispatched Cool back with a threat aimed at an upcoming six-man match pitting himself, Lucifer Magnusson and Cardinal Synn against Arron Iron, Ric Beauty and Lark Fenriz.

No result for the Re-RAW World Heavyweight Championship's own tournament survives; the promotion's last held broadcast previews a further show and pay-per-view that aren't themselves in the surviving record, and how the title, or the promotion, was ultimately settled isn't in the surviving record either.
