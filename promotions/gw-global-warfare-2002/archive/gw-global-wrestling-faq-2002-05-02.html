INTRODUCTION <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> Welcome to Global Wrestling! 1.1 What is GW? Global Wrestling is a fantasy based e-wrestling federation. Global Wrestling is a recently opened promotion ran accross the southern states in the US. It's not quite global yet, but give it some time. 1.2 Who runs GW? GW is run by me, [name withheld]. I handle "Your True Life Fantasy" [name withheld] and Takisuma Ogama. I've run a few federations before, my previous attempts were unsucessful, my goal for GW will be a long lasting one. <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> 2. TELEVISION <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> 2.1 Weekly Shows 2.1.1 Global Report This is your basic weekly update. It comes out once a week right after Warfare. It features post show results, rankings, news, schedules, etc; 2.1.2 Global Warfare This is our weekly card, where everything goes down. It features two hours, one hour with three dark matches airing and a second hour with three live matches airing. It is in PBP format. 2.3 Supercards Once every two months GW will hold a supercard which will have every title defended and about 6-8 live matches. <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> 3. CHAMPIONSHIPS <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> 3.1 Global Championship This is the big belt, the one you dream about growing up. You hold this belt and your on top of GW. 3.2 Skyweight Championship This is for those lightweight's out there. You must be under 230 lbs. to compete for this title. 3.3 Tag Team Championship The name says enough, two on two tag team matches are how the belts are defended. <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> 4. CHARACTERS <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> 4.1 Owner/CEO - Devon Titan Devon looks like a guy off of "Life styles of the rich and famous." Only alittle older. Imagine Hugh Hefner and you've got Devon Titan. Devon remains off camera, except on special occasions. 4.2 President - David Kilso This is the man doing the dirty work for Devon. He's an honest guy, trying to do the right thing at the right time. He's a stickler for the rules and hates when others break them. 4.3 Interviewers 4.3.1 Tim Mackoney Tim Mackoney knows his way around the block. He's worked various promotions and always asks the most daring questions. Which is often why he gets pushed around. 4.3.2 Sarah Kristin Blonde, big breats, high skirts, need I say more? Well, her IQ fits her description she's a blonde ditz. 4.4 Ring Announcer - Juan Havier Juan's mouth is loud and his voice is extravagent. Which is why he's got his job. 4.5 Commentators 4.5.1 Louis Eckle Louis Eckle used to work for CEW, then it closed and now he's found himself a job with GW. Louis calls it the way it is, he does our PBP. 4.5.2 Sean Primer He's a retired pro wrestler who's career never went anywhere, yet he feels the need to bring up how great he is when given the chance. He does our color commentating. <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> 5. ROLEPLAYING / ANGLES <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> This is what determines the winners in Global Wrestling. If you don't roleplay you don't win. It's as simple as that. I strive over quality than quantity. I'm looking for at the most one flash per week, I'd rather you put all your effort into one roleplay rather than three. Another thing, don't make your roleplay a novel. Length doesn't matter to me, as long as you get your point accross. Like I said match outcomes are determined by roleplaying, except if the outcome is angled, where the winner has been determined before the match by the handlers. Don't make any mention of real wrestlers in GW. Or real feds. I'd rather you keep your mentioning of other federations to a minimum. Don't use other people's characters in your roleplay without their permission. <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> 6. STRATEGIES <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> These things are a life saver when it comes to writing matches. Too bad most people tend to not do them. If you come to me bitching about how your character was portrayed wrong or didn't do the right moves in his match and you didn't send in a strat you can blame yourself. If you want your character portrayed perfect, send one of these in. In it should include... Offense, Defense, Several Spots, Win/Lose Situation, Ring Entrance, and anything else you feel necessary. <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> 7.
