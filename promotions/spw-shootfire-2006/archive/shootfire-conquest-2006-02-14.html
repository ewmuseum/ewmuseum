SPW Presents: Conquest!

14 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The brand new season of SPW Conquest now available through the RSPWF! Join us as we kick off a brand new season fresh from our award-winning series off the heels of our wildly successful Shootfire: Violent Ascension Tour. All the titles in SPW are on the line as we begin our new season gearing up towards Shootfire's next Pay Per View event, Culture Shock on 12.26.06. On this edition of Conquest, the match fans voted on our egroup to see, Andrew Davis takes on Larry Gionet to establish a number one contender, David Rheaume makes an open invitation to Lazz Sterling on X-Rated TV. The RAW Deal is formed with the debuts of Ric Beauty and Chris Scarlet, The Shootfire Army names their wishes earned at Charity Carnage from their tremendous Deathwish Cage Match win over the Founding Fathers of Extreme. Two of the most talented wrestlers in SPW history put their best on the line in our big main event match; AsH "The Cruiserweight Icon" defends the SPW World Title against Adam "FRIGGIN" Cage! Plus we celebrate our success and thank the IGA for their support in 2005! We emanate live from the IGA Arena, the Lucas Arts Entertainment Complex! All this and so much more as we blast off with a Championship Night to rock in the new season debut from Shootfire Pro Wrestling, now on free broadcast thanks to the RAW Sports Network and the RSPWF, this is SPW Conquest!
