Blackout

3 February 1999 (posted 6 February 1999)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JDMJrs Ultimate Wrestling Federation presents BLACKOUT http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Arena/7773/index.html Wednesday, February 3rd Pure Pain (5-4) -d.- B.E.E.R. (1-0) by pinfall Pure Pain reinforces his reputation with an annihilating victory over B.E.E.R... Pain put all resistance to rest with a Pure Piledriver through a table outside the ring... Fans shocked as much as B.E.E.R., who never landed a single shot...
Aahz (0-1) -d.- Catman Michael Scythe (8-14) by pinfall Aahz put his career into an upswing with a victory over fallen former Lightweight Champion Michael Scythe. A stunning put away/heart punch move shocks the life out of Scythe, who fell pale, giving up the pin...
Steel Machine (debut) -d.- Stuart Tyson (9-14) by pinfall Has Steel Machine left the grips of FATE, or does he roam stalking in their name? Going out on his own, the Machine starts his heavyweight career on a positive note with three methodical minutes that put Tyson away...
Armageddon (1-1) -d.- The Winner (15-11) by pinfall Armageddon has returned with an attitude heavily recognized in the UWF Press as of late. But his absence from the ring almost cost him this match against a veteran talent like The Winner. Persistance and raw power saved this match for Armageddon. Nothing pretty, but victory nonetheless...
King Nothing (1-0) -d.- Duke of New York (9-7) by pinfall King Nothing, looking to make a name for himself that can stand out amongst the rest of LEGION of ELITE and the Arena division, absolutely destroys a formidable opponent, Duke of New York. Nothing's use of the floating figure four tangled and strangled the Duke in the corner of the cage until he was limp and unconscious...
JJ Rock (2-3) -d.- The People's White Boy (1-0) by disqualification JJ Rock expects a lot more of himself, because he is the founder and leader of LEGION of ELITE, and is quite simply capable of much better showings.
People's White Boy got the best of him inside the ring with reversals and unique oppurtunity - eventually crushing Rock under the ringsteps outside the ring. Referee Bob Callahan, looking to keep the Heavyweight Division from turning Arena, called for the DQ. JJ Rock won the match, but was hurt, and red with furious disappointment...
RANKINGS CHECK Top Dawg (9-8) -vs.- Cruisin Issac (2-1) no contest Cruisin Issac doesn't show, and Top Dawg successfully earns his rank without contest. If you can find a wrestler in the top ten ranks whom you think doesn't come around to the UWF anymore, challenge him to a RANKINGS CHECK like Dawg did, and you'll win their ranking via no contest. Although no wins or losses go on the record, its an excellent way to advance...
Berube (2-0) -d.- Snake Zurlock (4-0) by pinfall Snake Zurlock had the strength advantage, but Berube's crafty ringwork wins the day. In the end, two Shotguns (a suplex dropped into a Diamond Cutter)
followed by a DDT put the finishing touches on Zurlock's undefeated run. As a result, WWF's Berube has stolen the Heavyweight Gatekeeper rank, and now it is Berube that stands one win away from Elitedom...
Doomsday Dan (0-3) -d.- UWF Rookie (0-2) by pinfall In standard superheavyweight fashion, COLD WAR II finalist Doomsday Dan makes simple work of a UWF Rookie, putting win #1 on his career record at last...
Rangon (2-0) -d.- Guchi Guaro (3-2) by pinfall A heavyweight masterpiece for the 99 highlight reel. Guaro and Rangon fought
40 minutes, tooth and nail, both men taking the other far beyond the limits of pain. Guaro could have won the match, but he waited too long to go for the pin. Rangon caught a wave near the end, and rode it on home to victory.
An outstanding battle between two major heavyweight players. Stinging factor is that Guaro submits his top ten rank to Rangon by losing to an unranked wrestler of the Abyss...
Kamakazi (6-8) -d.- UWF Rookie (0-3) by pinfall Kamakazi looks to get back on the right track and does, absolutely destroying some poor UWF Rookie in two minutes with a Kamakick that had no mercy - nearly took the young man's head off...
The Executioner (8-4) -d.- 187 (2-1) by pinfall The Executioner might be second only to Powerhouse when it comes to Arena violence. He rose to the occasion and went toe to toe with 187 every step of the way. Both men were bathed in blood from their opponent's frequent use of barbwire - but it was The Executioner who prevailed when he lifted the Arena cage with superhuman strength and dropped it across 187's abdomen.
Executioner covered his shoulders, got the three count, and secures his challengership to Powerhouse and the UWF ARENA CHAMPIONSHIP TITLE at the
IDES of FEBRUARY SUPERCARD coming February 15th...
PRIME CUTS (5-1) Razor Blade Arber & Midnight Domination -d.- APOCALYPSE (6-3) by submission PRIME CUTS have the edge in ring experience, and are as dirty and aggressive as ever. There was nothing wrestling wise Omega's Glory could do when a husky Razor Blade Arber blocked his way from getting to Saint Necrodamn, who was strangled dim by Midnight Domination's cobra clutch. It was a good fight, but the two wanna-be pirates came out on top once again. Aside from
Powerhouse, they are the most successful force in the UWF right now...
DAWG POUND (debut) Top Dawg & Soldier Grunge -d.- UWF ROOKIES (0-4) by pinfall It didn't go as smoothly as they would have liked, but it was successful and impressive debut by the latest tag team to emerge from UWF GANG WARFARE
CHAMPION GREAT NORTHERN ARMY. These two rookies didn't go down without a fight. Top Dawg performed the most devastating Dawg Drop witnessed in a while to end the match...
FAIR FIGHT RULES Blizzard (0-2) -d.- Captain Macchu Peru (2-3) by pinfall Peru had much more respected days when he was leading the UWF Riot Squad. Since entering the ring, he has lost a lot of his scare. Blizzard has no problem outfighting the master bouncer, strangling him numb with an Icelock cobra clutch, and planting him flat with the Blizzard Powerplex, a
Jackhammer that names Blizzard the victor of this confrontation... NO DQ/NON TITLE SUPERHEAVYWEIGHT MATCH UWF SUPERHEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION ToNiK (2-1) -d.- Myst (6-7) by pinfall Myst took it right to ToNiK's face, and beat the shit out of him, but the
Superheavyweight Champion wouldn't stay still long enough to collect a pin.
He dragged the match on and on by leaving the ring area a few times, until he caught a frustrated and off guard Myst with an attack that capped things off with a Drink Mixer - ToNiK's patended put away that claims him his third match as a UWF wrestler. Myst recovered after the match, took a steel chair, and bent it like tin foil over ToNiK's head, who was laid out flat and left in the isle...
Thor (12-8) -d.- Vogel (debut) by pinfall After the match, you could hear the fans muttering under their breath,
"Why'd he have to go and take on big 'ol Thor?"... The newcomer Vogel showed promise by almost taking the match against the massively strong and well endured Thor, but a bodyslam got reversed and delivered outside the ring, and the tides were turned... well... not in his favor. Thor shuts the match down with his Flying Hammer off the ropes...
FAIR FIGHT RULES Darksider (debut) -d.- The Jojo Train (debut) by pinfall Two brand new UWF talents locked it up and one of them ran away with the match. Jojo Train started things off with some fists and chops like any other wrestler looking for an offensive opening, but Darksider would have no part of it. He beat Jojo down and came at him heavily off the ropes with clotheslines and elbows. A Darkside Devastator, Darksider's adapted pedigree put out a fatiguing Jojo's lights in the tenth minute... An impressive debut by Darksider - we have yet to see what Jojo can do...
FAIR FIGHT RULES The Umber Hulk (16-9) -d.- Chaos (0-2) by count out Hang in there Chaos. You just met ZOMBIES UNLIMITED's silent killer, The
Umber Hulk. He's survived the 97 Can Games, and has shown that he can beat any man without a belt. Now at 0-3, many feel frustrated for Chaos...
Perhaps he'd better start setting his sights on some better known figures, instead of challenging wildly in the dark - or pick up a manager. Umber Hulk bear hug splashed Chaos off the ring apron and left him on the cement for the win.
Lord Igsel (debut) -d.- UWF Rookie (0-5) by disqualification ...And the sixth UWF Rookie did useth the ringside bell to knockout'th Lord
Igsel, causing him to bleed and spill his cranial crimson onto the canvas mat. A shocked crowd watched the rookie place his foot on Igsel's chest, but no three count came forth from thy referee's mouth - instead, a disqualification, for the rookie had overstepped his boundaries, and the rules of the lightweight division. Two paramedics came and they did lift'th
Lord Igsel from the mat, victor nonetheless...
ULTIMO'S RULES Ultimo Inferno (1-1) -d.- Butcherboy Maurice Simms (7-10) by pinfall Butcherboy Maurice Simms thought he was ready for Ultimo, when he came down to the ring in a suit of armor. Ultimo Inferno dragged a garbage can of various items down to ringside and went to work on an embarrassingly slow and bumbling Simms. Ultimo jackhammered Simms atop his head and pinned him in less than four minutes. But after the match, while his hand was being raised the victor, someone shot down to ringside and hit Inferno from behind. It was Mark XIII! The Population Control Device! He clotheslined
Inferno from behind and pinched his neck with a metallic claw hand, that folded Inferno's collapsing body to the canvas. Ray Anson, XIII's manager, took a microphone... "THIS is hardcore Ultimo Inferno? Who the hell do you think you are? Coming into my UWF, and changing the rules. This is bullshit.
JDMJR, you know damn well this is bullshit! The Lightweight is a no-weapon division! This little suckas too scared to join Arena, where he belongs. And until something is done, Inferno! You can expect me to hide in the shadows and never sign a match, and keep attacking you from behind after your matches, until you play fair!" Mark XIII then lists his cyborg arm and flaunts the needles under his fingernails, dripping with tranquilizer, and they walk from the ring, leaving both men lying on the mat...
Johnny Reb (debut) -d.- Holy Terror (1-1) by ref's decision Just as Holy Terror emerged, he now exits the Heavyweight Top Ten. Johnny
Reb, debuting unranked against the recent dethroner of the UWF Heavyweight
Championship Holy Terror, and took the match, earning the number one challengership from him and LEGION OF ELITE, and ousting him from the ranks.
Nothing being taken away from a spectacular performance by Reb in his long awaited debut - who basically submitted Terror into limpness with the Dixie
Land Delight - but the heavyweight championship and top ten ranks are now in utter turmoil - the power has been removed from office, and now the unranked are in charge. There is no better time for a realignment of the rankings, and the naming of a definitive champion, for the upcoming Heavyweight BATTLE
FOR THE BELT... Vengeance "The Master of Mayhem" (debut) -d.- The Reaper (5-2) by pinfall Arena competitors, look out. A madman is on the loose. Many thought this UWF newcomer crazy to step up and challenge the leader of DEATH'S CLAW, but apparently, those press releases were no sets. This man is barbaric. As strong as he is, however, The Reaper is still able to hold his own. For the majority of the match, The Reaper maintained the upper hand - but he just couldn't put this monstrosity away. Vengeance claimed the day with a thundering superheavyweight powerslam that knocked The Reaper out cold for the pin... Vengeance disgusted the crowd when he knelt down and broke and peeled off The Reaper's left pinky as a souveneir after the decision. The pain brought The Reaper to, and Psychopath, Bill Pax, and Devastator out from the back. The UWF Riot Squad swarmed in, and no confrontations occured.
A moving victory by Vengeance that puts him right in the thick of the Arena
Top Ten! HEAVYWEIGHT CAGE MATCH Padre Muerte (4-1) -d.- Biohazard (6-4) by pinfall What an INSANE all out brawl for it all... Just three minutes before the one hour time limit, Padre Muerte managed a corkscrew jackhammer that put
Biohazard down and out for the last time. Muerte blocked his way out of two
Atomic Hazes - Biohazard escaped three Muerte Death Drops!!! These men took it to the limits with each other - both were busted open - either man could have taken it - but it was Father Death himself who maintains his grip on his morbid reputation and puts the kill on Biohazard inside the cage. A great performance by two outstanding athletes.
UWF ARENA CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH Powerhouse (24-1) -d.- Razor (debut) by pinfall Nice try, but no cigar for the newcomer who couldn't help but bite at Powerhouse's open contract for title matches. No cigar, but he did receive three broken ribs and blood filled lungs from the belly to belly suplex that pancaked him to the rock solid floor. It was a brave effort, but Razor came up way short of threatening Powerhouse's miraculous Arena Championship reign...
