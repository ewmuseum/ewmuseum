Rage for Order

8 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JDMJr's Ultimate Wrestling Federation http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Arena/7773/index.html UWF presents RAGE FOR ORDER Sign your contracts for the Ultimate Wrestling Federation's RAGE FOR ORDER event - we've set up a ring in an old Federal Courthouse in Washington
D.C.... In a cement temple where right and wrong was written for centuries,
RAGE FOR ORDER will pitt the bitterest grudges held in the federation against one another in a final showdown to settle the score. Drag your worst enemy in for absolute judgement in the UWF's RAGE FOR ORDER!!!!
FOR THE NUMBER ONE TAG TEAM CHALLENGERSHIP PRIME CUTS (3-1) Razor Blade Arber & Midnight Domination d. OLD SCHOOL (2-1) The Winner & Mighty Quinn by submission With current number one challengers PRIME CUTS waiting in the ring, OLD
SCHOOL came barreling down to ringside and attacked before the bell. The
Winner took Razor Blade Arber outside early and worked him over, but not enough. The Winner, on the verge of his recently announced long awaited run for gold, has dropped the ball on two occasions. Once against Dr. Undead and now against PRIME CUTS. While The Mighty Quinn hung in strong and housed both Midnight Domination and Razor Blade, avoiding The Plank twice; The
Winner got wrapped up in the turnbuckle and bent into The Plank, and submitted the match after spending about ten seconds in the hold. Quinn got knocked outside the ring by Arber and failed to save his partner in time.
Nevertheless, The Winner spoils OLD SCHOOL's chance to face the UWF TAG TEAM
CHAMPIONS DEATH'S CLAW. That honor instead goes to PRIME CUTS, who hold tight onto the number one challengership with a shining record of 4-1. Oddly however, after the match, Razor Blade Arber was not able to leave the ring area without the leaning aid of Midnight Domination. It seems that some sort of foot injury occured. We will wait to hear from Arber about his condition, and whether or not PRIME CUTS will be ready by the IDES to perform their task...
THE WAR TO SETTLE THE SCORE LIGHTS OUT DEATH MATCH JJ Rock (1-2) d. Myst (6-6) by ref's decision JJ Rock and Myst have been having it out over a number of weeks since the end of the summer. Each superheavyweight has respect for the other, but also a deep competitive hatred and desire to dominate the other. Perhaps this fire burns hotter inside JJ Rock. He was obviously the aggressor for the most part of this bout. There was no need for a cage in this match. JJ Rock put Myst's lights out with the use of brute force and massive strength, which lifted Myst off the ground several times, inside and outside the ring, only to plant his superheavyweight body hard to the surface. When Myst attempted to mount offensive attacks, he was limited by the massiveness of
JJ Rock's 7 foot+, 500+ pound girth, and had little else but ground attacks to work with. He might have brought him outside the ring if Rock wasn't already doing that to Myst himself. powermoves to the canvas and gravitized body slams to the concrete worked Myst over and into eventual unconsciousness. The ref moved in swiftly and called the match, as well as the paramedics. JJ Rock's hand was lifted in victory. Myst fought a strong war singlehandedly against the entire LEGION OF ELITE, without the aid of his former UNTOUCHABLE allies. I hold great respect for Myst in his efforts, as did JJ Rock who made sure Myst still had a career at the end of the match. Rock levels off a 2-2, while Myst takes an aching 6-7 trip to the hospital. Upon further inspection, Myst was released with pain killers and sent home.
UWF 1998 YEAR END AWARDS 1998 WRESTLER OF THE YEAR: POWERHOUSE It was no contest, was it, really? Undefeated for the entire year, increasing his jaw dropping record to 22-0. Successfully held the UWF Arena
Championship without a blemish. Put Pagan out of wrestling. Cut off then UWF
HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION Wizard's hand. Collected the ears of Dr. Undead and
Frenzy. Hung Hangman by his own noose. Took on Nighthawk and Acid at the same time in the Arena cage, and was victorious. An amazing year that set the standard for all future champions.
1998 ROOKIE OF THE YEAR: BIOHAZARD It was close in the end, but postal votes pulled Biohazard over the hump built by Pure Pain, who marked a close second. Although many of the internet voters picked JJ Rock before controversy broke out, Biohazard's dominant and chaotic year won out over JJ Rock's manslaughter of Sabre and Pure Pain's capture of the gold. No doubt, a future force that all with have to reckon with.
1998 TAG TEAM OF THE YEAR: APOCALYPSE Omega's Glory & St. Necrodamn It wasn't even close. This superheavyweight and lightweight covered all aspects of tag team wrestling during their climb to stardom. Winning the
1998 Tag Team Battle For the Belt, and regaining the championship for the second time against then UWF TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS NEW AGE SOLDIERS, APOCALYSPE brought their holy war to the UWF and dominated in the name of our Lord
Jesus Christ. Last word is that the team has been put on the shelf after their second consecutive loss to current UWF TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS DEATH'S
CLAW. Shame.
1998 STABLE OF THE YEAR: FATES WARNING This was difficult to award, with so many top notch stables taking root in
1998. ZOMBIES UNLIMITED, DEATH MILITIA, and THE GREAT NORTHERN ARMY were all close in the running and each collected a lot of votes. But edging out all others was FATES WARNING, the first ever UWF GANG WARFARE CHAMPIONS. Their organized efforts this year infiltrated and destroyed THE UNTOUCHALES, captured and defended the UWF GANG WARFARE CHAMPIONSHIP TITLE, and gave birth to a very strong and dominant subchapter, DEATH MILITIA. It is apparent there will be a great battle for GANG WARFARE dominance in 1999 - and GREAT NORTHERN ARMY, current UWF GANG WARAFRE CHAMPIONS look up to the task. But none will be able to go back and do what FATES WARNING did in
1998. Congratulations to all that were affiliated, and to Kamakazi, who's leadership guided them all.
1998 FAN FAVORITE OF THE YEAR: BRAD NAPALM No other man could lead a stable of superheavyweights and not see mutiny. This proud and noble leader of the current UWF GANG WARFARE CHAMPIONS GREAT
NORTHERN ARMY has forcefully and righteously led a stable of future champions down the path of success. He has single handedly stepped up to the task to deliver the Death Vallety Driver when called upon and was a top contender for the UWF Superheavyweight Championship. But moreso than anything else, his stablemates and the fans admired his leadership and grace under fire more than any other wrestler this year.
1998 MOST HATED WRESTLER OF THE YEAR: WIZARD Although men like the President of the UWF would have said it was Andros/Nemesis (who was without a doubt the worst handler of 1998) widespread voting across the federation told that Wizard was without a doubt
THE most hated man around. Holding the UWF HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP about 11 months, and defending it only when mandatory, Wizard took on all top contenders eventually and scored wins against most of them. Wizard's resiliency to give out title matches and his magic use in the ring frustrated almost every opponent to the point of madness, especially when
"The Untouchable" spoke in the press. He was without a doubt, the master of getting under your skin, in 1998.
1998 MATCH OF THE YEAR: WIZARD vs. POWERHOUSE It was held on UWF LIVE, and everyone there couldn't believe what they saw, when Powerhouse not only took the match, but Wizard's right hand as well!
Wizard had won just about every war with every opponent leading up to this megabout, but fell short against the UWF ARENA CHAMPION. The man you love to hate had his spell casting hand chopped off by the Japanese Killing Machine, and it burned a wonderful image in so many fans mind's, that it won match of the year for 1998. Without a doubt, a classic we'll all remember.
1998 ROLE PLAYER OF THE YEAR: OMEGA'S GLORY The father of wisdom and the preacher of sin and salvation, 7' plus Omega's
Glory laid down the law for many heavyweights in 1998, and his words in the press releases spoke volumes. Each and every post a quality one, Omega's
Glory fought not only the battles of might and power in the ring, but the war of words as well in the press. And more times than naught, regardless of whether or not Omega's Glory had won, he left you filled with stinging lessons, undenyable and universal judgement, and plenty to think about.
Vividly literate, Omega's Glory takes this one hands down. CONGRATULATIONS UWF's 1998 AWARD WINNERS CAGED HEAT - BURNED Venom (6-4) vs. Guchi Guaro (3-1) no contest It was Venom's return to the ring after a considerable hiatus. He was dressed in revealing leather garb, and hyped up for the match. Alongisde him was the amazingly more repulsing Gramma Bizarro, who was donning her own S&M getup. She teasingly whipped Venom into the Arena cage, while his mouth worked on a tightly wrapped burrito. Inside the cage, he hungrily awaited his confrontation with Guchi Guaro.
Soon Guchi Guaro appeared, leader of LOS LOBOS, current number one challenger to the UWF HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP, garbed in full Cuban colors.
Aside him was the beautiful Carmela, who had a thorn rose bitten between her pearly teeth. The fans roared as he approached the ring, when suddenly, a wrestler ran down from the locker rooms.
It was none other than Biohazard, who stepped in front of an angered Guaro with a finger in his face. Without a mic, Biohazard spat, "You dont need to be worryin about this fag! You need to quit duckin the real case at hand!
Biohazard! And until you quit duckin me you wetback, I'm gonna-" Guchi Guaro was enraged and about to attack. It was then that another wrestler came barrelling down the isle. Guaro's face red and blaring, he went to strike at
Biohazard, but someone grabbed his fist and held him back. Guaro spun around angrily to find it was Dazzling Dustin Jenkins. Jenkins kept Guaro at bay when he grabbed a microphone brought over to the conflict. "Dont worry about this chump Guaro. He's mine. He's been ducking ME for a long, long time..."
Suddenly Dazzling Dustin Jenkins and Biohazrd break out into a shoving match. Guaro tries to get toward the ring but is blocked, and does not want to leave Carmela alone near this rucous. Biohazard is shoved over some cables running along the floor, and DDJ moves in when two more wrestlers appear from the back.
Johnny Reb and Hawk show up to form a wall bewteen Dazzling Dustin Jenkins and Biohazard. It looks like an ugly three on one is gonna break out, so
Guchi Guaro takes a couple steps back toward the locker room with his manager. As he backsteps a few feet, from the mists of the locker room comes none other than the UWF HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION Pure Pain, wearing the gold around his waist. He is looking bluntly into the eyes of Guaro, who notices him and turns around. When Pure Pain emerges from the mists, he and Guaro get increasingly closer to a prematch brawl in the isle, when the rest of
LOS LOBOS comes out to surround their stable captain. Aahz, Rangon, The
Demon, and Kampfer circle the founder of LOS LOBOS and begin to lead him to the ring through the collected wrestlers.
Rangon then takes a confused Carmela, and gently begins to pull her aside. Rangon guides an unsure Carmela towards the railings, until Guchi Guaro notices and doesn't like whats going on. He sees Carmela being pulled away by Rangon, while suddenly Dazzling Dustin Jenkins, Biohazard, Pure Pain,
Aahz, Johnny Reb, Hawk, The Demon, and Kampfer gather around. Guaro almost goes pale realizing what is at hand. He does the only thing he can do... He attacks Aahz immediately and breaks him hard onto the isle floor with a
Cuban Cigar. Aahz goes unconscious and is flapping on the isle floor, as the crowd of gathered wrestlers attack. A kick fest of record proportions begins, battering Guaro flat and helpless down to the floor. Kampfer and
Johnny Reb help pull his beaten body off the floor while the rest of the wrestlers begin to try and fight off the UWF RIOT SQUAD, who are moving in.
The UWF HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION gladly grabs Guaro in his grasp and sets him up for his Pure Piledriver. The crowd cant believe it. Guchi Guaro is raised, and plowed down to the hard isle floor. Soon after, he is flapping in seizure next to his mutinous stablemate Aahz.
With the UWF RIOT SQUAD moving in fast and netting Hawk and Biohazard and
DDJ, the Demon blocks the isle while Pain, Kampfer, and Rangon drag Guchi to the Arena cage. With the alert help of Venom, they do their best to force the cage to the floor. The safety mechanisms built in forbid its complete lowering, but it was lowered enough to do the job. Inside the ring, as Riot
Squad trickled in and were fought off by Pain and Rangon, the only two not yet neutralized, Venom went to work on the unconscious Guaro. He pulled his
Cuban tights down, exposing his bare ass, and signaled to the crowd! Venom took the burrito from his lips and pushed it up into Guaro's crack, until it ruptured and beans were smeared across the LOS LOBOS leader's rear end.
The UWF Riot Squad cleared the area, but not before the damage was done. Carmela was crumpled outside the ring in tears, while Guaro and Aahz were tended to by medical personell. A sheet was put over Guaro, whose rear was exposed and violated by Venom's food. The match was called no contest by the building officials, and footage was censored immediately for the outside viewers. A horrible night for Guchi Guaro. He looked hurt. How bad? We'll have to wait and see. But if his body is all right, surely his pride and self esteem are not. A horrible, horrible mutinous feast on a generous leader and professional wrestler - Guchi Guaro...
CASKET MATCH - NON TITLE UWF HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION Pure Pain (4-2) d. Padre "Father Death" Muerte (4-0) via stipulation The UWF HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION Pure Pain has climbed out of his brief lived championship slump. After dropping a match via pinfall against Guchi Guaro and succumbing to the Cuban Cigar, he returned to the UWF Arenadome tonight to reclaim the top of the mountain. Pure Pain mauled Guchi Guaro with the aid of some turncoats before the match, and now stepped into the ring to fight Padre Muerte at his own game - a casket match - a match that Father
Death is undefeated at since joining the UWF. But history had no weight in the ring tonight. Referee Bob Callahan was all over JJ Rock's every suspiscious move, as he paced outside the ring in support of his LEGION OF
ELITE stablemate. Padre Muerte was attacked early in the match, and had trouble turning it around against a fired up Heavyweight Champion. Muerte turned things around halfway through the match with some spectacular moves - including a suplex over the ropes, directly into the casket. Muerte dove outside the ring and tried to close the lid, but Pain popped it up into his jaw and escaped. Later in the match, Pure Pain took Muerte past the point of no return with the Pure Piledriver, bouncing the undefeated Muerte off his head and into his first loss. The UWF HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION bulldogged Muerte through the ropes and onto the steel casket, busting him open, and afterwards, sealing the fate of the match. Muerte tastes the dark despair of his own suffocating realm, locked inside the casket. Pure Pain regains his composure with a commanding win over a top heavyweight force. He must now look foward to the IDES, where an angry Guchi Guaro will no doubt be waiting - and on January 15th, the title WILL be on the line...
HEAVYWEIGHTS SEEK POWERHOUSE - NON TITLE Biohazard (5-3) d. UWF ARENA CHAMPION Powerhouse (22-0) by pinfall The bubble had to burst one day, and tonight it did. The 22-0 wonder of the world came crashing down with a thunder onto the cement of his own Arena floor - a realm Powerhouse has wandered throughout as its King, undefeated and undenyably the most powerful force in the entire federation. Until now.
Wrestling perhaps the most energized and pumped up match of his life, Biohazard came forth and did what no other man could do. With the goriest use of the Arena saw wired cage we've seen in months, Biohazard literally bled the 500 pound beast and lasted long enough until Powerhouse came off his feet. Biohazard single handedly shredded Powerhouse the best he could early in the match, and his cuts came deep. Powerhouse seemed an enraged
Japanese demon of myth, his oversized head dripping dark blood, his left eyelid hanging by a sliver of skin, his eye bulging outward. Powerhouse was beaten, and then Biohazard, with an amazing show of strength, actually lifted the UWF ARENA CHAMPION off the ground and into the air, dumping him over his shoulders and onto the back of his neck. Powerhouse's own body crushed himself between 500 pounds of gravity and the cement floor, and before 20,000 roaring fans in the UWF Arenadome, Biohazard pinned the unpinable. Before the match, Biohazard had psychotically cut off his own ears. Many said the Biohazard feared this to be his last match, and he wasn't going to let Powerhouse add to his growing collection of appendages he had collected over the past year. So, earless and freakish, Biohazard has found fame - but Powerhouse remains Arena Champion. For Biohazard to get a title shot, he would have to switch his contract over to Arena division, where he would then be ranked as the number one challenger, just above The
Reaper. Such a contract signing, however, would mean that Biohazard would be spending 1999 in a ringless enclosed cage of steel, where regardless of whether or not he wins the title, countless growing Arena forces would be gunning for HIS head - including what must now be a very irate and reawakened Powerhouse. So now, we will wait and see what the UWF's 1998
Rookie of the Year will do. Will he remain a heavyweight, and add this record breaking victory to his repitoire, or will he take the oppurtunity to become the UWF ARENA CHAMPION, by signing into Arena competition, and facing this monster one more time. An amazing victory that may bring with it some very dark clouds...
HOMICIDE FOUND GUILTY Homicide (debut) d. Omega's Glory (11-7) by disqualification Omega's Glory has changed his appearance slightly, but his beliefs are strong and the same. In old leather war uniform of the US Army chaplains,
Omega's Glory beamed into the ring and dished out such a beating that the ref had no choice but to disqualify the two time former Tag champ. Homicide had rustled up Omega's Glory without relent - performing mock rituals of
Satanic proportions - mocking everything he stood for, all after APOCALYPSE had spoiled redemption against DEATH'S CLAW. Omega's Glory had punishment to serve, and Homicide, as he had asked for it, did receivith. Glory lumbered into the ring and immediately attacked a cocky Homicide. From there, the ring shook with each towering blow from the heavens, and the three successive powerbombs he slammed Homicide with almost put him through the ring. After laying him flat, OG dragged him up, sent him into the ropes and caught him with a flying knee that almost broke Homicide's jaw. One more trip into the ropes was Homicide's last - Omega's Glory showed no pause or remorse in using his 6' Staff of Glory, which he used to hammer Homicide down once and for good. Omega's Glory stood above his motionless body, and began to take out his tunic and bible, from which he read scriptures. He blessed the body of his victim, Omega's Glory did, all while the bell rang from the cries of disqualification, said forth by the ref. The win goes on
Homicide's record, while the ring's true victor bears the numerical scar of loss. Homicide was revived and assisted to the back by personnel. Battle on
Omega. Battle on...
CLASH AT THE LIGHTWEIGHT GATE El Diablo (2-0) d. Kamakazi (6-5) by pinfall Its hard to tell after this match if Kamakazi is losing grip or if El Diablo is just the most recent lightweight to dominate out of the gate. There were no dramatics in this bout. It see-saw'd back and forth, and each time El
Diablo brought Kamakazi down to the mat, the FATES WARNING frontman was much worse for wear. With no interferences lurking at ringside, El Diablo got the pinfall victory after ten minutes and a superplex finale. Capturing the Gate of the Lightweight Top 10 is none other than El Diablo, who has taken this division by storm. His victory over Kamakazi gives him birthrite into the ranks as one worthy to be among them. The FATES WARNING captain may be in a slump, but he is by far, no slouch in the ring. However, Kamakazi will hereforth have to look up the ranks to men like El Diablo and Cloud Strife, and regain his composure before slipping out the teens and into the Abyss.
LORDS OF PAIN AND HURT 187 (debut) d. Dr. Undead (11-6) by pinfall 187 joined the UWF a little less than a year ago, and has been limited to
FATES WARNING bodyguard status and Gang Warfare assistant until now. 187 has entered the cage in a fit of steroid pumped fury, claiming his chunk of the
Arenae pie. Dr. Undead, a lightheavyweight by class, agreed to step into the ring against this newcomer, in hopes of crushing FATES' biggest man and latest hopes with the Zombie Clutch. Perhaps they should have sent in a bigger man to do the job, because the manager of ZOMBIES UNLIMITED could have been killed. 187 tore him to shreds at will, crushing Undead's body against the stone floor repeatedly until blood flowed from his facial orifices. He had ample oppurtunity to injure Dr. Undead badly, but instead, he took him deep into the woes of pain and hurt and loss. A successful and dare we say it, considerably merciful, debut for 187. Dr. Undead had best stick amongst the heavyweights from here forward and concentrate on protecting the ranking he is so delicately clinging onto.
ARENAE HARVEST (part one) UWF TAG TEAM CHAMPION The Reaper (4-1) vs. Arena's #1 Challenger Jack the Ripper (5-1) Reaper awarded number one challengership via forfeit The Reaper had come calling for the number one challengership to Powerhouse's UWF ARENA CHAMPIONSHIP TITLE and found that nobody was home. Jack the Ripper, the Arena ranked top challenger to the title is missing in action, and was forced to forfeit over the top rank due to his abandonment of his UWF contract and venue obligations. The immense Reaper, UWF TAG TEAM
CHAMPIONSHIP belt in hand, stood alone amongst the cold, barren stone floor of the Arena cage until he was announced the winner via forfeit.
It was then that the spotlights shifted over to the locker room entrance. Out of the mists came Top Dawg. In one hand was a microphone. In the other was some sort of document. His voice rang throughout the Arenadome as he walked towards the ring...
TOP DAWG: "You know what!? Romper Ross has a point. This damned division is barren now! All you had to do Reaper was show up to get that rank, cause there aint nobody out there that a challenge for it... That got me to thinkin. All throughout the beginning of my career here in the UWF, I have been one of the most hardcore mother fuckers you've had to deal with! FA and
I have carved a path of destruction through the heavyweight division at will! It took only the Dawg Drop to put away that lame ass Blizzard! So I said to myself, Dawg, its time you took your ass over to where you belong. I have in my hand a contract. As of midnight last night, I am no longer a heavyweight. I am now and will be for the rest of 1999, an Arena division wrestler!"
Top Dawg reaches the Arena cage and is allowed into its perimeter. The
Reaper stands silent, listening to his words, until they stand nearly face to face...
TOP DAWG: "So the only thing left to know is this! Since Jack the Ripper didn't have the guts to show up here tonight, what do you say you prove you're worth that rank by facing me here tonight - right here, right now..."
The fans went into an uproar hoping The Reaper would accept. He pondered it for a moment, and then slowly lifted his hand, asking for possession of the microphone. He holds it to his lips, but says nothing. The Reaper thinks about it some more, and then begins to shake his head, lowering the microphone to his side. He turns and begins to walk away.
Just as Top Dawg raises his hands in disappointment, The Reaper spun around and clocked him upside the cheek with the microphone, splitting it into pieces and putting a lump on Top Dawg's face! With his concurrance, the cage was dropped, and the match allowed to go on.
It was a bloodbath. The Reaper was churned up into bloody chunks in the saw wire by Top Dawg, who was merciless and quick to slice open the TAG TEAM
CHAMPION's flesh without remorse. It looked to be along the lines of a
Powerhouse strategy, used by Biohazard earlier in the evening. Deep into the match, The Reaper had done very little offensively. He lost a lot of blood having to tear himself loose from the saw wire in order to continue. But as shocking as that was, to see The Reaper pull his flesh away in bloody strings that tore from the tangled razor steel of the arena cage, it paled in comparison to the comeback that followed. Perhaps numb from the pain and driven by endorphines, The Reaper struggled to get hold of Top Dawg. When he did, there was revenge in store. Powerful, gut wreching moves from the pit of The Reaper's last breaths struck hard on their mark against Top Dawg.
Reaper concentrated on Dawg's head, smacking it like a coconut against the stone floor - entangling it in the wire of the cage until it bled. And then, with a reversed neckbreaker to the cement - The Reaper suddenly was on top of a cover. Top Dawg kicked out a moment too late from a stunning blow that left him dizzy and disoriented after the match. The Reaper, barely able to get past the ring perimeter area before passing out cold, won by pinfall, and proved his worth. He, as the UWF's Arena divisional number one challenger, will face Powerhouse for the UWF Arena Championship Title at the
IDES OF JANUARY SUPERCARD, coming January 15th. Top Dawg showed tremendous
Arena promise, and should be able to survive the year. An outstanding match by both men.
ARENAE HARVEST (part two) UWF TAG TEAM CHAMPION The Reaper (4-1) d. Top Dawg (8-6) by pinfall The remaining matches on the card were blacked out due to time constraints. The UWF apologizes for the missing write-ups. Results, however, are better than a backed up fed that ends up closing down.
Here are the matches and some brief notes as to any notables that occcured... RAGE FOR ORDER BLACKOUT MATCHES Kampfer (1-0) d. Catman Michael Scythe (8-12) by pinfall Kampfer goes 2-0 by defeating the once flash in the pan Lightweight Champion
Scythe, who seems to be very little of a threat as of late.
Psychopath (0-1) d. Minnion (2-0) by pinfall In the Arena cage, ten minutes into this bitter brawl, Psychopath clamps on the figure four leg lock and grinds away mercilessly. Minnion, the outcast of DEATH'S CLAW, defiantly passed out before crying submission.
Loco (debut) d. Jackhammer Colt Mason (3-13) by pinfall Loco feasted on Colt Mason inside the Arena cage. Jackhammer was beaten, bled, and put away in standard fashion. Victorious debut for the unknown

Craving bounced Cloud Strife off the mat with several piledrivers before the
UWF Riot Squad swarmed in. Strife was hospitalized, tested, and later released with minor injuries.
