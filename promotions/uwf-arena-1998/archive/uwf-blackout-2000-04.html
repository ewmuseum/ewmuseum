JDMJr's Ultimate Wrestling Federation http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Arena/7773/index.html BLACKOUT - April 2000 UWF LIGHTWEIGHT BOUTS: D-DAY MATCH: (1-0) DeadBolt vs. Dark Eron (debut) "winner earns contract power over loser's next three matches" WINNER: Dark Eron DECISION: pinfall TIME OF THE MATCH: 6:05 ENDURANCE FACTORS: Dark Eron: started (300) ended (-1129) DeadBolt: started (560) ended (-742) MATCH NOTES: With so much on the line in this bout, outside interference was rampant - Mack attacked Dark Eron as soon as he emerged from the locker rooms, and put on a bruising beatdown to uneven the score from the start -
Overlord appeared and threw Mack into the audience from off the ramp so
DeadBolt wouldn't get DQ'd - match was all DeadBolt from the starting bell, inside and outside of the ring - Dark Eron avoided pinfall with escape tactics (ie. foot on the ropes, kickouts, etc) and began to fight back -
Diabolus appeared from under the ring, took out Overlord, and nailed
DeadBolt with the Fallen Angel, a shooting star press from the top turnbuckle - DeadBolt never regained control - Dark Eron makes worthy comeback to win match with the help of the UWF Lightweight Champion,
Diabolus... UWF HEAVYWEIGHT BOUTS: (3-2) 187 vs. UWF Rookie WINNER: UWF Rookie DECISION: ref's decision - disqualification TIME OF THE MATCH: 2:35 ENDURANCE FACTORS: UWF Rookie: started (100) ended (-2359) 187: started (720) ended (168) MATCH NOTES: Somebody tell 187 he's in the Heavyweight division, not Arena - came out, dominated, took some shots, laughed them off, kicked serious ass, too much ass - 187 takes ringside bell from timekeeper and nearly kills UWF
Rookie with devastating blow to the head - Bob Callahan feels compelled to enforce heavyweight rules and calls the match, and is tossed over the top rope as a result of the call...
(13-11) Dr. Undead vs. UWF Rookie WINNER: Dr. Undead DECISION: submission TIME OF THE MATCH: 2:20 ENDURANCE FACTORS: Dr. Undead: started (750) ended (744) UWF Rookie: started (100) ended (-1258) MATCH NOTES: Dr. Undead humiliated another UWF Rookie by dressing him up as
Snake Zurlock of all people, bitch slapping him until the rookie's lip trickled blood, and Zombie Clutching him into absolute useless submission -
Dr. Undead beat the Snake Zurlock looking rookie out of the ring when finsihed, exclaiming "Zurlock! You wouldn't fare any better in a one on one, you piece of ex-FATE shit!" to the cameras before exiting the ring...
BETWEEN MATCHES: As Dr. Undead walks up the isle to the locker room, Gargantuan begins his descent to the ring down the same isle. Gargantuan's massive leg is in a specially built titanium brace, after suffering a broken knee to ZOMBIES UNLIMITED's hit in the garage at the Can Games. Dr. Undead stopped right in front of Gargantuan, and began to laugh his ass off.
Gargantuan pummelled Undead with massive fists, lifted him onto his shoulder in a painful grunt, and over-the-shoulder slammed him to the isle carpet, leaving him twitching and collected by UWF Riot Squad personnel...
(4-2) Gargantuan vs. UWF Rookie WINNER: Gargantuan DECISION: pinfall TIME OF THE MATCH: 4:10 ENDURANCE FACTORS: Gargantuan: started (645) ended (243) UWF Rookie: started (100) ended (-1119) MATCH NOTES: Gargantuan was jumped by the rookie before he entered the ring, and was slow to recover. The no-name rookie almost made a name for himself, by wearing down the injured Gargantuan with hit and run moves - but a glob of spit to Gar's face was more than he could take, and it angered him enough to fight thru the swelling pain in his knee, pound the rookie into the canvas, and finish him off with a titanium-braced big leg drop off the ropes - Gargantuan collects a win against doctor's orders, and is obviously still a few cards away from returning to full health...
(15-10) Omega's Glory vs. Dazzling Dustin Jenkins (8-3) WINNER: Omega's Glory DECISION: pinfall via main move TIME OF THE MATCH: 6:25 ENDURANCE FACTORS: Omega's Glory: started (760) ended (-351) DDJ: started (670) ended (-891) MATCH NOTES: Someone never identified watched over this match from the back locker room entrance, clouded from vision by the dry-ice mists... Dazzling
Dustin Jenkins came out strong and knocked around Glory fairly well, but his attempt to get things dirty and dangerous outside the ring brought forth the fury from Omega's Glory, and DDJ was put away in the end by the omnisuppressant Finger of God...
UWF ARENA BOUTS: (1-1) Overlord vs. Timebomb (1-0) WINNER: Overlord DECISION: pinfall via main move TIME OF THE MATCH: 5:50 ENDURANCE FACTORS: Overlord: started (555) ended (410) Time Bomb: started (460) ended (-1293) MATCH NOTES: Someone got their ass kicked in the Arena cage, and it wasn't
Overlord. Time Bomb's attempts to thwart Overlord's onslaught is to no effect, as Overlord overcomes and dominates in the ringless cage, endling things dramatically when he doused the stone floor with lighter fluid, lit it aflame, and sent Time Bomb to the locker rooms a victim of his final move of the match, a hellfire chokeslam to the rock hard floor...
(1-0) Eruption vs. 'Big Time' Brian Howlett (debut) WINNER: Eruption DECISION: pinfall TIME OF THE MATCH: 1:55 ENDURANCE FACTORS: Eruption: started (490) ended (490) 'Big Time' Brian Howlett: started (530) ended (-268) MATCH NOTES: Biohazard was outside the ring holding some sort of weapon, onguard for outside interference. There was none. Inside the Arena cage,
Eruption worked fast and furious - barrelling over Big Time Brian Howlett like a truck, offering no mercy or a chance to retort, he collected the pin with authority... No After Shock necessary to gain the victory - just backbreakers and horrible collisions with the Arena stone... After the match, Biohazard instructed Eruption to add insult to injury by delivering an AfterShock to Howlett as the Riot Squad moved in to clear the post match overkill...
(9-2) Padre Muerte vs. Psychopath (1-1) WINNER: Padre Muerte DECISION: pinfall TIME OF THE MATCH: 7:05 ENDURANCE FACTORS: Padre Muerte: started (655) ended (375) Psychopath: started (510) ended (-1213) MATCH NOTES: Can anyone stop the rising master of destruction, Padre Muerte? Even the terrible and violent Psychopath could not stop the power of Father
Death, who seems at home in the Arena cage. Seven minutes of fighting off the collector of souls was all Psychopath could manage in his return, as
Padre Muerte overpowered and bled his opponent to victory, wrapping the match with a nasty backsmacking powerslam and a thudding DDT to the cement...
I QUIT MATCHES: (5-1) Rangon vs. Sorrow (0-1) WINNER: Sorrow DECISION: submission TIME OF THE MATCH: 8:45 ENDURANCE FACTORS: Sorrow: started (595) ended (-452) Rangon: started (680) ended (-1033) MATCH NOTES: In the back before the match, Sorrow had to be scraped up off the floor because of an attack - Rangon hit early in the locker rooms with a saw-wire baseball bat, that left referees having to pull him back to consciousness so he could get to the ring... While Rangon waited inside the heavyweight cage, Frenzy came down the isle and sat in the middle of the passage dressed up as Sorrow. He set up a WWF play ring and took out some
WWF action figures, and began to play - cameras picking up his Sorrow mimicked voice saying, "I wanna play hell in a cell!!!" Sorrow came down the isle pissed to all hell and began to kickfest Frenzy to the floor from behind - Rangon shot out of the cage and attacked Sorrow, dragging him into the cage match he asked for... Both wrestlers weapons were taken from them before they came down the isle, referee Bob Callahan not allowing the slaughter - wanting a one on one submission match bewteen the two as signed... The match was longer than anticipated, Sorrow putting in much more work than expected... Late in the bout, Sorrow twisted Rangon's ankle until it broke, and worked on the hold until Rangon had no option but to submit...
Barring further injury, Rangon should be able to return to the ring immediately with a special foot cast. This match is considered an upset victory by the fans - none the less, kudos must go to Sorrow for pulling off the surprise victory...
(11-6) Pure Pain vs. The Hangman (debut) WINNER: Pure Pain DECISION: submission TIME OF THE MATCH: 5:50 ENDURANCE FACTORS: Pure Pain: started (660) ended (660) The Hangman: started (460) ended (-783) MATCH NOTES: Sorry Hangman - you decided to pick on one of the big dogs -
Pure Pain is a former UWF Heavyweight Champion and Can Games survivor, and while that doesn't guarantee him a victory, that sure as hell meant the odds were stacked high against you. Pure Pain used hit and run tactics, moving fast and striking with roundhouse kicks and elbows to the jugular - juggernaughting from off the ropes with powerful force, keeping his man stunned and/or on his heels... a submission clutch around Hangman's neck spelled defeat. Pure Pain clobbered the rookie Hangman with a Pure
Piledriver after the bell, calling in Riot Squad members to ensure safe exit for The Hangman. Fans of old, this is not the old Hangman who used to carry the bloody noose - this is a new talent who simply bit off more than he could chew. Perhaps someone with UWF experience will guide this promising athlete down the right path, and train him until he is ready for proper revenge against the personification of his rookie error...
UWF ARENA NON TITLE MATCH: (11-10) Top Dawg vs. UWF ARENA CHAMPION (7-4) The Reaper WINNER: UWF ARENA CHAMPION The Reaper DECISION: pinfall TIME OF THE MATCH: 6:25 ENDURANCE FACTORS: UWF ARENA CHAMPION The Reaper: started (1005) ended (165) Top Dawg: started (755) ended (-1992) MATCH NOTES: You want hardcore, you want Top Dawg. In wrestling terms, this man is a sick mother fucker, plain and simple. Even though he lost this non title match, wrestlers with brains must fear him. He sacrificed his body in ways rarely witnessed in the Arena cage as of late. While most wrestlers approach Arena as a weapons division, Top Dawg sees it as an extreme wrestling zone. If not for the opening move by the UWF Arena Champion - The
Reaper, which was a simple but highly crippling open palm to the nose, Top
Dawg may have fared better. But the champion was quick to attack, and left no scraps for the Dawg - breaking his nose in the opening move, pushing nose cartilage under the skin of his face... Reaper held his own throughout Top
Dawg's arsenal, which in the end went in the champion's favor, when Top Dawg went for a chokeslam and had it reversed to his dismay. Dawg was hard to kill, kicking out of three pinfall covers already battered and bruised into semi-consciousness - and oddly enough, he put himself out of the match, after climbing the saw-wire cage to the ceiling and missing a full body splash he had set up after putting The Reaper down on the stone floor with a gut wrenching stomachbreaker - Dawg missed his target and splashed the cement with his rib cage. Reaper collected his carcass for the pin and took the match. Top Dawg did not care, and took himself to great heights in an attempt to win the match - if his splash had hit its mark, this match might have turned out different. Another loss for the gold hungry Dawg. Champions beware...

Thus, regardless of what the UWF threw at this man, the UWF LIGHTWEIGHT
CHAMPION Diabolus, on this night, earned his right to call himself the undisputed UWF Lightweight Champion. Overcoming all odds thrown before him by John E. Holmes, a potent #1 contender, Diabolus manages the victory after a come from behind turnaround and breathtaking sitting powerbomb that shocked all the fans and wrestlers alike. But Diabolus' night was far from over. A group of wrestlers stormed the ring for an attack, and the UWF Riot
Squad was not effective enough in their response. Out of the locker rooms came DeadBolt, Overlord, and Sorrow... they swarmed the ring and put a hell of a beatdown on the UWF Lightweight Champion, who had nobody there to help him. Before the UWF Riot Squad could make it to the ring, Dark Eron barrelled down the isle to try and make a save, in return for Diabolus'
favor earlier in the evening - but to no avail - he was beaten down to the canvas right alongside Diabolus. Then another man came down to the ring....
It was Kamakazi! Kamakazi, dressed in street clothes, walked down to ringside. Overlord, DeadBolt and Sorrow, kept the two battered men down with kicks as they watched, surprised as everyone else. Kamakazi gave a menacing stare to the three men, and perhaps out of respect, they backed off slightly, for it seemed that Kamakazi had something to say to the new UWF
Lightweight Champion... Kamakazi grabbed a microphone, took the UWF
Lightweight Championship belt from the ringside attendant, and entered the ring to kneel down beside Diabolus' beaten and unconscious face... He said,
"Hey Kid...You're nothing but a scrub that got a lucky win, and I'm better that you'll ever be!!! Just be glad that I'm retired or I'd Kick your ass and take the belt from around your waist!!" With that, he helped Diabolus on to his feet! The UWF Lightweight Champion could barely stand on legs that were like noodles, his arm draped around Kamakazi for support. Overlord,
Sorrow, and DeadBolt looked unhappy with this, and were about to attack - but it wasn't necessary. Kamakazi kissed the UWF gold belt, and swung it roundhouse, full force into Diabolus' unprotected face. Blood splashed in droplets all around his body, which clumped to the mat like a sack of potatoes... The UWF Riot Squad finally appeared and cleared the ring with nets...
And that's all for this UWF Blackout... Watch for official TOP TEN standings to hit the UWF menubar in the next couple of days, followed by the long awaited UWF ROYAL RUMBLE to decide the vacant UWF HEAVYWEIGHT
CHAMPIONSHIP... So long everybody, from the UWF Arenadome.... JDMJr's Ultimate Wrestling Federation http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Arena/7773/index.html
