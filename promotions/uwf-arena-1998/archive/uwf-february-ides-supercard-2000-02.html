FOR CONTROL OF FATES WARNING: (4-5) Mack vs. Snake Zurlock (5-3) Nobody's quite sure what Mack was thinking getting in the ring against a powerhouse of a heavyweight named Snake Zurlock. Perhaps it was pride in defense of his love for FATE that overtook his logical thinking. Mack was the first one into the ring, followed closely by a familiar female figure of the past named Sidney, who had returned to his side. Dressed in black and coming down through the audience to Great Malinco by ICP, Mack looked more confident than he has in months. Snake entered the Arenadome with Blizzard by his side, who was brandishing a softball bat in case anyone wanted to get involved. Nobody did, and nobody needed to. Mack was alone in this fight, and sad to say, he was outweighed and outmatched by Snake Zurlock, who obviously took this match to repair his bruised ego. It was a slaughter, and the crowd showed no appreciation for his tactics. Fans booed Snake for his overkill methods throughout the match, which lasted only five minutes - a barrage of powermoves that slung Mack's body all over the ring like a rag doll. Snake took the pinfall victory easily, and Mack fans (yes there are quite a few) as well as Sidney were horrified when after the needless conflict, Snake pointed to the announcer's table, and proceeded to powerbomb
Mack's lightweight body onto the keyboards, microphones, and monitors. The blow jarred Mack's body, crushed equipment, but did not break the thick table. Snake Zurlock was declared the winner and the rightful controller of
FATES WARNING from here on out, and Mack was helped from the table and to the back by paramedics and a saddended Sidney, all the while fans chanted
Mack's name, and Snake was splattered with cokes as he returned to the locker room...
SNAKE ZURLOCK by pinfall (8-9) Kamakazi vs. Ishahn Van Drack (1-1) Kamakazi and Ishahn Van Drack. A veteran lightweight versus the new up and coming lightweight superstar. No lengthy write-up will make this pill any easier to swallow for Kamakazi, who dominated most of the match. But throughout the event, about once every couple of minutes, somebody from
ZOMBIES UNLIMITED would make an appearance. At first, UWF Lightweight
Champion The Scissor simply came down from the locker room and walked by the ring, never taking a moment to even acknowledge the match - walking by the ring and exiting the other locker room isle with his UWF Lightweight
Championship belt raised above his head for the fans. It distracted Kamakazi just enough to sway momentum, allowing Ishahn to capture the break in his concentration. Two minutes later, when Kamakazi had regained control, UWF
Arena Champion Frenzy walked down the isle, by the ring, and across the
Arenadome, barbedwire belt raised high above his head. It was ineffective, and Kamakazi looked prepared to put the final touches on the wrestler formerly known as Metal Man. But Ishahn Van Drack is no fluke, and he hung tough. Just long enough, in fact. For in the final minutes, somebody else appeared. The immense Powerhouse exited the locker room doors, and walked down to ringside. Kamakazi attempted to ignore his massive presence, assuming he would walk by like the others. However, Powerhouse stepped up to the ring apron, outside the ropes, and stomped on the ring. Kamakazi spun around as would anyone on guard, and Ishahn took advantage, nailing Kama with a jumping DDT off the turnbuckle and upsetting him for the victory.
Exhausted and beaten worse for wear, there was not much left of Ishahn, who after the three-count, fell to the mat. Powerhouse left the scene without contact, while an infuriated Kamakazi scrambled down to ringside, collected a steel chair, and re-entered the squared circle. He beat Ishahn over the head neck and shoulders with the steel chair until it was a twisted, unrecognizable ball of metal. Blood trickled from beneath Ishahn's covered face, as the UWF Riot Squad swarmed the ring and swept Kamakazi away.
Kamakazi grabbed a microphone and was bellowing for anyone from ZU to show their face, but nobody answered his call. Another painful blemish on Kama's win-loss record, as ZU sways the outcome of an otherwise powerful showing by the former member of FATE'S WARNING. Ishahn made it back to the locker room on his feet with ringside assistance, and improved his record to an impressive 2-1... ...
ISHAHN VAN DRACK by pinfall (5-1) Rangon vs. Hellbent (0-1) There seemed to be some confusion at the opening bell of this matchup. When
Hellbent met Rangon within the ropes, Hellbent demanded that the arena enclosed cage be lowered, but it never was. Apparently UWF executives had this match contracted as an interdivisional brawl to be fought under standard heavyweight rules. Rangon had no problem with this of course, being of the heavyweight division, but Hellbent obviously did. He grabbed a microphone and demanded that the cage be lowered, and while he waited in vain, the bell was rung and Rangon attacked. UWF fans and wrestlers alike all know that Rangon is a respected force - whether you like him or not - he is a major power to be reckoned with. So is the sickness of steroid inhumanity known as Hellbent. Even with his early attack, Rangon found it very difficult to put Hellbent into any type of submissive hold. Hellbent was just that, a maniacal, uncontrollable freak of an athlete who paid no heed to Rangon's size and strength. With each exchange of blows between the two, Hellbent was constantly being chased around the arenadome, from ringside to the locker rooms, and eventually out into the streets - whereafter Rangon did all he could to bring the match back inside the building. Hellbent just about refused to "wrestle" - instead he did everything he could to humiliate Rangon out of spite - at one point taking mustard and ketchup from the concession stands and squirting them up
Rangon's nostrils - and at his most putrid moment, when he spewed black bile from his rotten insides and dumped his rancid puke all over Rangon's head.
Each time Rangon got to his feet to retaliate, Hellbent scattered away, grabbing microphones whenever he could demanding that the arena cage be lowered. He even went so far as to climb into the rafters by himself, leaving Rangon on the floor below, strangling a technician into unconsciousness, trying to force him to lower the cage. The UWF never complied, as it was never agreed to in contract - and referee Bob Callahan had no choice but to call this match a no contest. Fans enjoyed watching
Hellbent break every rule he could all over the building, but were jipped of the high caliber violent fight they were expecting. Perhaps if Rangon will agree to take this fight under Arena rules, we will get to see a most interesting rematch at the Can Games Pre Card next week...
DOUBLE COUNT-OUT - NO CONTEST PURE PILEDRIVER vs. ZOMBIE CLUTCH: (10-5) Pure Pain vs. Dr. Undead (11-10) No interferences. No foul play. Dr. Undead and Pure Pain took moments before this matchup to stare evil intentions across the ring at each other, the hatred and rivalry having been built up to an intensity over the past three weeks. But once they clashed in the center of the ring, the former UWF
Heavyweight Champion was taken by surprise by an equally furious Dr. Undead, who matched Pure Pain's attacks blow for blow. Neither man was really beaten off his feet until just three minutes into the match, when Dr. Undead tossed
Pain into the ropes after a flurry of fists and kicks, and caught him with a chokeslam that spun him up and down hard to the canvas. It shook the wind from Pain just long enough for Dr. Undead to prove what has been well known for quite some time. It doesn't take very long to apply the Zombie Clutch -
Pain was turned onto his stomach, Dr. Undead perched himself over his back, crossed Pain's arms in front of him, held his wrists, and propped him up into the Clutch, rearing back on his lower spine with no mercy. It took less than five minutes to get Pain into the Zombie Clutch, and once he was in it, as usual, there was no escape. Pain held on an extra few seconds, but the spine is EVERY man's weak point, and Pure Pain had no choice but to submit.
Dr. Undead let go immediately - reveling in his victory instead of making an attempt to injure Pure Pain. If this is to be considered one of Kamakazi's
Triad matches for the evening, things have gotten off to a rotten start for fans of the former FATES WARNING...
ZOMBIE CLUTCH wins STANDARD HEAVYWEIGHT MATCH: (12-10) Dr. Undead vs. Pure Pain (10-6) Dr. Undead had won the first match, and began to walk back to the locker rooms in celebration of his legendary Zombie Clutch. Pain straightened his body out with a grunt, popping vertebrae into place like knuckles as he stretched and finally wobbled to his feet. John E. Holmes was announced over the PA system, and the debuting lightweight began to come down to the ring.
Pure Pain grabbed a microphone and told the puny man to get back to the locker rooms, cause his time had not come, and if he dared step into the ring, he'd know the chaos of the UWF and never see his match against
L'Amour. Calling out Dr. Undead for an immediate segway into their next match, John E. Holmes stood in the isle protesting Pure Pain's selfish war call - which paid no regard to the scheduled card line-up. John E. Holmes wouldn't leave the isle while Pure Pain waited in the ring. Soon enough, a blur was seen dashing down the isle toward Holmes. It was The Scissor - UWF
Lightweight Champion - who barrelled down the isle and whalloped Holmes over the head with his Lightweight Championship belt. Holmes was KO'd and fell like a sack of potatoes, and The Scissor motioned for Dr. Undead to re-emerge from the locker rooms. Undead quick stepped it back to the ring apron, while The Scissor dragged John E. Holmes into the back. Once Dr.
Undead was in range, Pure Pain wasted no time and leapt through the ropes like a missle, plowing them into the steel railings as the bell for the second match rang. Dr. Undead found himelf constantly reeling on his heels as Pure Pain brought on an onslaught reminiscent from his championship reign. Adrenalized and refusing to lose, Pain scrambled Dr. Undead's eggs and splattered him all over the ring perimeter - after a bodyslam to the concrete, Pure Pain darted up the apron with a steel chair and dropped a steel enforced knee onto Undead's head that left him twitching and defenseless. It was only a set-up for the end. Pure Pain dragged Dr. Undead up the ring apron, and up to the third turnbuckle. Fans couldn't believe it, but Pure Pain set Dr. Undead up for a Pure Piledriver off the top turnbuckle, and delivered to the cheers of ten thousand fans, plowing him through the announcers table!!! Dr. Undead's broken and limp body slumped over itself and lay upside down amidst broken monitors and table fragments!
Pure Pain spit on his carcass, and dragged his shattered opponent into the ring for the one-two-three. Perhaps Dr. Undead should have never let go of that Zombie Clutch while he had the chance, cause Pure Pain rose to his feet, and absolutely beat the mentor of ZOMBIES UNLIMITED about as bad as anybody ever has in a straight one-on-one matchup in over ten years. Pure victory for Pure Pain in their second and final match of the evening - bringing Kamakazi's Triad count even at 1-1. The fate of Kamakazi's self made sentence lies in the hands of Top Dawg, when he'll face the abominable
Powerhouse in the arena cage later tonight...
PURE PAIN by pinfall (11-9) Top Dawg vs. Powerhouse (25-3) Top Dawg looked to become the first wrestler in modern UWF history to beat the Japanese Killing Machine two meetings in a row. He entered the ring fired up as ever, with FA close by - the very manager that indirectly caused the death of Powerhouse's long time mentor, The Seer. As the arena cage was lowered, Powerhouse stormed into the cemented zone and began to pummel Top
Dawg down through his ankles with tree like forearms, one afetr the other.
nothing fancy. Just 500 pounds of force, blow by blow. One thousand sentences couldn't better explain what this next one will. Top Dawg barely got a shot in, and Powerhouse absolutely kicked his ass and regained his name. Piledrivers, ddt's to the cement, and his patented belly to belly suplex to the stone floor under his 500 pound frame put an exclaimation mark on this rematch. It was about no match whatsoever. Not to take away from Top
Dawg, one of the Arena's best competitors. Powerhouse some nights just can't be stopped. And this was one of those nights. Four quick back breaking minutes, and Powerhouse walked out of the arena cage the undisputed victor of this bout...
POWERHOUSE by pinfall ROMPER ROSS RECAP: Have you forgot whats going on? here's a recap of FEBRUARY IDES as it is about to be complete...
In the opening bout, Snake Zurlock bullied and beat up Mack over rights to the now defunct FATES WARNING. As far as I know, the UWF is seeing him as the rightful controller of that stable until someone else steps forward and does something about it besides bitch - cause to tell ya the truth - figurin that one out otherwise would take a group of lawyers, and I aint one!
In the meantime, Snake Zurlock has announced the birth of a new championship belt - stay tuned for the UWF to make an official ruling on that one...
Kamakazi is distracted and eventually upset by the new rising lightweight superstar, Ishahn Van Drack, due to the virtual interference of members of
ZOMBIES UNLIMITED. A post match beating left Ishahn sore and victimized due to the fact Kamakazi couldn't let his anger out on anyone responsible.
Kamakazi looks headed towards a violent date with Frenzy in the nearby future - a match that may be as much as a bloodbath as the Can Games main event just down the road...
Hellbent was hellbent on an arena match against prominent heavyweight Rangon, yet found himself in a heavyweight match instead. Hellbent did all he could to fuck up the show, and ended up strangling UWF technicians instead. Rangon has accepted a rematch to be fought in the Arena cage at the next event. It should be an explosive clash of two intense forces looking to establish high rank in their respective divisions...
Pure Pain and Dr. Undead beat the living shit out of each other. The Zombie
Clutch proved to be the more lethal put away move, but Dr. Undead not holding onto his victim for further damage blew up in his face when Pure
Pain returned for an immediate rematch and crushed the veteran with a Pure
Piledriver from the top turnbuckle and through a table outside the ring!
What a feud! After two losses in a row, the Japanese Killing machine Powerhouse regained his stature and absolutely mauled Top Dawg in the arena cage, all but splitting him in two on the stone floor. I was cringing when I saw Top Dawg take the rematch after Powerhouse's recent stumbling losing streak. I've been around since Powerhouse began, and if nobody else knew it, I had a damn good feeling he was gonna make an example of the next man he fought. Top
Dawg - too much balls! UWF - beware - Powerhouse should NEVER be taken lightly. HE is the UWF franchise, and Biohazard - one battle does not a war win - although I myself have never been able to outbleed that bastard...

(15-5) Wizard vs. Omega's Glory (14-9) WINNER: Omega's Glory DECISION: pinfall TIME OF THE MATCH: 5:55 ENDURANCE FACTORS: Omega's Glory: started (755) ended (579) Wizard: started (725) ended (-3059) MATCH NOTES: Wizard looked rusty against the more recently experienced
Omega's Glory. Omega's Glory maintained control 80% of the match and surprised fans when he used his staff to spear Wizard in the mouth outside the ring, after mauling him on the concrete. Omega's Glory goes hardcore and takes over this long time rivalry...
(0-1) Overlord vs. Genesis (0-1) WINNER: Overlord DECISION: pinfall TIME OF THE MATCH: 1:45 ENDURANCE FACTORS: Overlord: started (545) ended (545) Genesis: started (445) ended (-600) MATCH NOTES: Much hatred and talk between these two hardcore individuals in the press had fans expecting a long drawn out fight to the death. Instead,
Overlord runs the show, absolutely owning Genesis this time in the arena cage, jawbreaking and powerslamming him to the stone floor among other powermoves to dominate and attain victory. On the way back up the victory isle, a man dressed in black appeared out of the crowd and whalloped
Overlord over the head with a slapjack, sending him stumbling to his knees. The man in black disappeared through the crowd - and nobody has a clue who the hell it was...
(2-2) Bitchin Remy L'Amour vs. John E. Holmes (debut) WINNER: John E. Holmes DECISION: submission TIME OF THE MATCH: 4:25 ENDURANCE FACTORS: John E. Holmes: started (530) ended (-949) Bitchin Remy L'Amour: started (665) ended (-232) MATCH NOTES: John E. Holmes survives Bitchin Remy L'Amour's early Bloodlust put-away for an outstanding come from behind win. Holmes was slightly injured going into the match, and was beaten worse for wear, but outlasted
L'Amour long enough to connect with his put away, the Jimmy Hat O'Doom, a condom covered fist into the mouth of L'Amour that choked him out for the
"upset" victory...
UWF ARENA CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH: (4-2) Darkson vs. UWF ARENA CHAMPION Frenzy (17-12) WINNER: Darkson - new *2 TIME* UWF ARENA CHAMPION DECISION: pinfall TIME OF THE MATCH: 3:50 ENDURANCE FACTORS: Darkson: started (640) ended (297) Frenzy: started (925) ended (-530) MATCH NOTES: Frenzy was worked over quickly by Darkson, who's head was taped up heavily for the match due to their first encounter. Nothing extraordinarily violent was done to beat Frenzy - Darkson's use of bodyslams and crushing boot blows to the throat and face knocked the champion out.
Darkson captures his second UWF Arena Championship title from the man who beat him just one month ago...
UWF LIGHTWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH: (2-1) Ishahn Van Drack vs. The Scissor (21-6) WINNER: UWF LIGHTWEIGHT CHAMPION The Scissor DECISION: pinfall TIME OF THE MATCH: 2:45 ENDURANCE FACTORS: The Scissor: started (1125) ended (97) Ishahn Van Drack: started (575) ended (-430) MATCH NOTES: The Scissor had his arm in a specially made brace so that he could defend his title, when he drew a random name from the Lightweight barrel. He drew forth Ishahn Van Drack, who had ample time to recover from his beatdown suffered at the hands of Kamakazi. Or perhaps not. Ishahn, on dizzying pain killers at the time, came to the ring only to be beaten to shreds by a pearl-harbor attack unleashed from a blindside before he ever got into the ring. The UWF LIGHTWEIGHT CHAMPION used the early advantage, ring railings and cement, to instigate an early victory. Whether or not
Ishahn could have performed better under more normal circumstances remains to be seen. Fans booed the Lightweight Champion for defending the championship against a man who had wrestled earlier in the card - not to mention being under medical supervision when the match was announced. After the match, much to everyone's delight, the UWF Riot Squad appeared and began to disperse the men in the ring. It was then that a man dressed as UWF Riot
Squad fully attacked The Scissor and brainbusted him onto a front row steel chair before real Riot Squad members could gain control. The man's disguise was removed, only to reveal that 187 - the Arena titan - had done the job...
UWF HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH: T.B.A. vs. JJ Rock (7-4) WINNER: none DECISION: JJ Rock stripped of UWF Heavyweight Championship TIME OF THE MATCH: nil MATCH NOTES: After winning the UWF Heavyweight Championship against all odds in a 16 man elimination tournament - JJ Rock disappears from the face of the earth and fails to defend his title at the Supercard - thus, with ample warning, he is stripped of the UWF Heavyweight Championship. A 16 man Royal
Rumble will take place immediately following the Can Games to decide the new champion. An absolute shame considering the work JJ Rock had put in to capture the belt...
