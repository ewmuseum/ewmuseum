Mighty Quinn Wins UWF Heavyweight Championship

May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WINNER of HEAVYWEIGHT ROYAL RUMBLE & NEW UWF HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION!!!: MIGHTY QUINN!!! After four long years in the UWF, a career that should have been retired refused to. After 8 years in the AWF, Mighty Quinn finds new life in the
Ultimate Wrestling federation, lurking amongst the background, a force to be reckoned with. All that have crossed his path have been taught - although this thick, gigantic Funk type brawler has remained in the shadows, his accomplishments and abilities are absolutely prime time. After dragging
Snake Zurlock into the corner by a merciless neck grip, propping his choking body up on the top turnbuckle, and clotheslining him out of the ring, onto, and through the announcers table, Mighty Quinn brings the gold home. Dr.
Undead was furious, but stood clear, as Mighty Quinn has always had his number. Handed the belt, Mighty Quinn stood tall and tireless in the center of the ring, and its quite possible that the belt has found a sturdy new home amongst a division of top talents.
And before the victory could be releshed, as Snake Zurlock was being helped onto a stretcher against his foggy will, the music began to blare. But it was not Mighty Quinn's. It was Pure Pain's! Pure Pain, the number one challenger to the title, who's ribs were broken by The Scourge upon emission from this event, ribs taped and all, began to limp down the isle - making his way to the ring. Pain grabbed a microphone and made it up onto the apron with a grimace of agony on his sweaty face. The new UWF HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION did not relinquish his position in the center of the ring, and simply held the belt at his side, waiting and watching what the number one challenger might have to say...
PURE PAIN: "Quinn! This event was a fucking sham, and you know it! I earned my spot atop this division, and had to fight a ring full of people to collect the title that belongs to me!?!? I dont fucking think so!" --pain grunts as he grabs his side-- "So get this old man! No way in hell, are you gonna sit in your rocking chair, and lumber out here to take what is rightfully mine! May IDES, I'm putting my match with Holy Terror on hold, and you're gonna have to step up to the plate, get in the ring with me, broken ribs or no broken ribs, and wrestle me for that belt! You got me you fat hillbilly fuck!?"
Mighty Quinn has a look of disgust on his face, and says nothing. Instead, he looks at the gold, looks at Pure Pain, and coughs up a glob of spit, which he launches in the number one challenger's direction... It lands gooily, smack in the center of Pure Pain's face!
Pure Pain steps up to the champion, and kicks him in the nuts! Mighty Quinn buckles over, and drops the belt! Pure Pain picks it up, lifts his own hand into the air as if to motion himself the new UWF HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION, and cracks the gold over Quinn's skull. Instead of falling to the canvas, Mighty
Quinn slowly stands erect, his face red and beaming. Pure Pain shouts some nasty remarks to the new champion, and Mighty Quinn attacks - booting Pure
Pain in his ribs, buckling him over! UWF HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION Mighty Quinn puts Pain's head between his legs, scoops him up from the waist, HIGH over his head, and powers Pure Pain down to the canvas in one of the most violent one-on-one power bombs witnessed this year! The force is so dramatic, that it leaves a dent in the ring floor, Pure Pain's body slumped into the hole... he passes out from agony, blood trickling from his mouth!!! Mighty
Quinn then picks up the UWF Heavyweight Championship belt, and drops it atop
Pure Pain's body. Quinn picks up the microphone...
and decides to say nothing... he simply leaves the ring, leaving Pure Pain to the busy paramedics, leaving the UWF Heavyweight Championship belt atop his unconscious broken body...
ROMPER ROSS: "Holy fucking crap! If thats not a challenge, I dont know what is! Mighty Quinn captures the UWF Heavyweight Championship title, and leaves the belt with the number one challenger Pure Pain, as if to say, 'you want the belt? enjoy it now while you can, cause at the MAY IDES SUPERCARD, its going right back around Quinn's waist!!' What a silent, spiteful bastard!
You know fans, for those of you who never witnessed the days of the AWF,
Mighty Quinn was a champion's champ. He defeated men like Powerhouse back when Powerhouse was undefeatable! But these are different times! Mighty
Quinn is much, much older - in his sunset years! And men like Rangon, Snake
Zurlock, Omega's Glory, Pure Pain, all in their prime, all await their chances at that title... how in God's name will this belt remain in the hands of one man for more than a month with the caliber of talent that fills the heavyweight division? Remembering the days of Great God Wizard, when a
250 pound man held the belt for over eight months - I can now give that
Untouchable respect. Same goes to Powerhouse. And Scissor. Because before
The Seer died or disappeared, he made one last true prediction - that the belt would change hands more this year than all the years of the past combined... One man must rise... will that man be Mighty Quinn? We'll know more after the MAY IDES SUPERCARD, coming up May 19th - when the fate of these titles will be set. More info to come at the Supercard - Im Romper fucking Ross, and youre not! See you all at the IDES...."
