Another sick ass match from JDMJRS UWF IDES OF JANUARY http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Arena/7773/index.html *************************************************************** ************HEAVYWEIGHT MATCH - MANAGERS BARRED FROM RINGSIDE ************Omega's Glory (11-8) ************vs. ************Venom (6-4) *************************************************************** Who in their right minds would get in the ring against Venom nowadays? Granted, he is one wrestler many people would gain pleasure from seeing pounded into the canvas, but the risk. Oh, the risk. Omega's Glory saw an odd and distasteful irony in relation to his last match, when he kicked ass and was DQ'd. For tonight, Venom lasted toe to toe against the holy warrior. But he, as usual, had clever tricks up his sleeve. Out from the locker rooms came a young teenage boy, halfway through the match. This was no manager - it was recognized by one of our reporters as Yuri, a burn victim that Venom spent time with in his off time. He was carrying a cross to the ring - the boy and the crucifix were dripping. When he came to ringside, both Venom and Omega's Glory seperated and kept an eye on the young man. He stepped onto the ring apron, and held the cross high. The name Venom was written across the horizontal slab. Grabbing the microphone,
Yuri took a zippo and lit the flame... "Venom is God..." he softly speaks, touching the flame to himself! His entire body goes up in flames! The UWF
Riot Squad clammered in a frenzy, not knowing how to deal with the situation. Beofre anyone could do anything in their awe, the boy stumbled in through the ropes and ran towards Omega's Glory, who could not get out of the way in time. Yuri smashed the cross over Omega's Glory, and they both fell to the mat in a pile of smokey flames! Security soon got to ringside to distinguish the burning, all while Venom gave a kickfest to Omega's Glory down on the mat. The referee in shocked disgust, called the match via disqualification in favor of the fallen Omega's Glory. Avengelyne came screaming down to ringside as paramedics scattered around the boy and the superheavyweight. St. Necrodamn also came down to ringside. But instead of being taken out on a stretcher, Omega's Glory rose from the ashes, covered in soot and heat blisters, his eyes enraged and beaming towards the unknowing Venom, who was boating his way back to the locker room. Omega's
Glory shot up from the crowd of paramedics, and barreled toward Venom until his hands were around his throat. The UWF Riot Squad soon neutralized the scene and seperated the men. The young boy Yuri was brought to the hospital and is currently in critical condition. The fire of hatred between Venom and Omega's Glory obviously still burns very strong.
Omega's Glory by disqualification ************************************************************** Superheavyweight slaughterhouse - JDMJRS UWF IDES OF JANUARY http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Arena/7773/index.html *************************************************************** ************SUPERHEAVYWEIGHT MATCH - FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE IN ARENADOME ************JJ Rock (2-2) ************vs. ************Northwatch (4-1) ************************************************************** This was a hardcore match in nature. These gigantic men were actually sanctioned to fight it out anywhere in the Arenadome, starting in the center of the ring. The match was slow paced, both of these men exceeding 400 pounds by a few meals. It reached distances as far as the concession stand, where Northwatch was amazingly powerbombed through the glass and shelving.
The GANG WARFARE CHAMPION of GREAT NORTHERN ARMY had shards of glass imbedded in his back, as he fought Rock back toward the ring through the crowd. The crowd attempted to scatter around them, but at one point, JJ
Rock and Northwatch tumbled over a family in their seats - the seats were flatted, and two small young boys wearing UWF T-Shirts and foams hands were taken to the hospital with boroken legs - crushed beneath the bohemoeths.
The match ironically ended right at ringside, when Northwatch bulldogged JJ
Rock from the ring apron and down onto the announcers table. Everything was demolished, and as a result, JJ Rock found hiself knocked unconscious just long enough for the gore streaked Northwatch to get the win on the ringside concrete. Northwatch was attended to by medical personnel and had three relativelty large pieces of glass removed from under the skin of his back.
Northwatch by pinfall ***************************************************************
