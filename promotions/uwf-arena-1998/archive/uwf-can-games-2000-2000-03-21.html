JDMJr's Ultimate Wrestling Federation presents..... http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Arena/7773/index.html UWF CAN GAMES 2000 Supercard MAIN EVENT BARE HANDED DECAPITATION MATCH (11-6) PURE PAIN vs. HELLBENT (0-1) ONLY ONE MAN WILL SURVIVE WINNER: Pure Pain DECISION: decapitation TIME OF THE MATCH: 13:45 ENDURANCE FACTORS: Pure Pain: started (660) ended (-2092) Hellbent: started (535) ended (-13553) MATCH NOTES: Hellbent. UWF newcomer. Madman from the infernal home of Satan himself. An embodiment of evil in a mortal shell, here on earth. Pure Pain.
UWF superstar. Former UWF Heavyweight Champion. A man schooled in the extremes of pain, violence, and the conquoring of countless UWF opponents.
His veterancy under the UWF lights proved to have worth when he fought for his life against Hellbent in the 2000 Can Games Arena death match.
Hellbent started the feud with a full frontal attack of fists and elbows, forcing Pure Pain's back up agianst the saw wire cage. Pain fought back to avoid entanglement and knocked Hellbent to the floor. Down on the cement,
Pain applied a jaw hold and tried to pry the mandible bone from Hellbent's face. Screams ensued, but Hellbent escaped the hold... A toe take down puts
Hellbent on his stomach - Pure Pain stomps on the back of his skull and spins around to hellbent's front side - Pain lifts his head off the stone and puts him directly into his put-away, the Pure Piledriver - the same move we saw him demnolish Dr. Undead with just a few weeks ago... Up and down, to the screaming of blood ravenous fans, and Hellbent soon found himself clutching his skull, busted open on the arena cage floor...
This was only the beginning. For the first five minutes into the bout, Pure
Pain unleashed a storm that gave him a hell of an advantage heading into the meat of the match - and sorely enough, Hellbent found very few moments of clarity to turn anything around. His face was pummelled and his head was smacked to the cement like an egg, blood streaming from wounds atop and around his head...
In fact, ladies and gentlemen, lets not fluff this up with dramatic descriptions of powermoves and motives and intensified staredowns - in a fight for your life there is no strategy, no plan of atack - only an animalistic, gut-wrenched attempt at survival through offense - and Pure
Pain was the king of offense in this match. Pain was relentless, and left almost nothing for Hellbent to chew on. We saw everything that could happen to Hellbent happen - his head battered against the stone via piledrivers and skull stompings - his arm entangled into the steel mesch of the cage, leveraged, and subsequently broken from an elbow smash, the limb left snapped and dangling from skin only as Hellbent lumbered, fighting for consciousness around the ring. There a segment when both men entangled in a blur of fists and twisted limbs, when Pure Pain rolled over stop his opponent and latched onto Hellbent's upper lip. Pure Pain yanked back several times, blood rivering down over Hellbent's teeth and chin, as Pure
Pain peeled back Hellbent's upper lip and nose with his fingers! The demonstration of survival showed clearly the difference between standard arena matches and the fight for life in the arena cage. There was no tact, little sway in the battle. Hellbent was mauled by a professional of the ring, and any further descriptions of what was done to Hellbent would be monotonous...
In the final five minutes of the match, Pure Pain could sense victory. After a one-sided onslaught enhanced by the early domination of Pain, the former
Heavyweight Champion began to take Hellbent apart. Over the course of seven minutes, Hellbent sustained damage from three Pure Piledrivers and two brainbusters onto the solid stone floor! Amidst this dismantling, Hellbent must have sensed his own demise, for in a fury of raving anger and hunger for life, he launched his bloody and broken self at Pure Pain in one of the most disgusting attempts at survival the arena cage has ever witnessed. With
Hellbent's broken forearm dangling purple and clearly snapped in half at the bone, Hellbent screamed, echoing throughout the Arenadome, his eyes drooling red, his mouth agape, missing teeth, his upper lip swollen and falling off his face - Hellbent screamed, held his broken forearm to his mouth, and
CHEWED the dangling, broken piece of limb and hand off his body with skin shredding teeth, tossing the useless appendage to the Arena floor in a deathcry! What was left was a forearm broken just past the elbow, a jutting splintered bone emerging from purpling, gore dripping tissue - as sharp as anything else in that cage. Pure Pain had never faced such an attack in his career, a demented Hellbent knocking him back against the saw wire perimeter, stabbing him over and over in the chest and neck with his broken forearm!!! Holes opened up one by one all over Pain's upper body, his eyes rolling back in his head from blinding, stabbing pains, and gore dribbling holes being forced into his neck and chest by the splintered forearm he relentless jabbed with!! Pain began to slump to the floor after one lash of bone stabbed him in the cheek and nearly took off what was there from the side of his face. That stabbing shocked Pain back to his senses, that second wind of panic that so fired up Hellbent took Pure Pain back to the fight - only he had much more left! Pain front face locked Hellbent into his grips, lifted him and splashed him face first down onto the stone, choking him out, putting his entire weight on clamping Hellbent's neck closed. Hellbent's legs thrashed and kicked until he slowly grew still. When it was obvious that Hellbent was neither conscious or unconscious, Pure Pain got to his feet, his face swelling from the traumatic injury, and lifted Hellbent up for yet another Pure Piledriver. One by one - Pure Piledriver, brainbuster, jackhammer, jackhammer, Pure Piledriver... Hellbent's skull was softened into a bloody sac, barely maintaining its shape on the top of Hellbent's body. Pure Pain sat Hellbent up and grabbed around his neck and chin as tight as he could with a headlock, twisting and pulling at his head in an attempt to pull his head clean off his body. Hellbent was already as good as dead, his body limp and flopping defenselessly to every demand of Pure
Pain's grip. But the blood was too much - the grip was slippery, and Pain could not pull the skull off for victory...
Pure Pain looked wearily and unfocused to the raging crowd that screamed for death. Collecting his thoughts, he lifted Hellbent one last time into a brainbuster and landed him smack down to the cement for the umpteenth time!
Hellbent's head seemed to crack open, a split opening up atop his skull! Pure Pain motioned to the crowd for a final Pure Piledriver! Fans cringed as it his full force, denting the top of Hellbent's skull, flattening it and warping its shape. Hellbent's head was all but off - splintered into a hundred useless pieces... Pure Pain decided it was time to end this slaughter - carefully climbed the corner of the cage, hovering over
Hellbent's motionless body on the stone... Pure Pain clenched his fist, and dropped an eight foot gravity fist right to the center of Hellbent's face!
Pain's fist went clean through Hellbent's globe of broken skull and facial bones and to the floor, actually breaking Pure Pain's right fist!! He withdrew his hand, and within his grips was Hellbent's scrambled brain, and a section of his medulla, which snapped off as it pulled it out. What was left on the floor resembled no man's head - it was more like a pile of rotten vegetables and meat (pulp and brain tissue), clumped together with some sections of skin and hair, with an open eyeball on top. Pure Pain held what was left of Hellbent's head up for the crowd to see, teetering from nausea, pain, and injury, almost beckoning for the officials to tell him if he had done enough or not... "Is this good!?!" he innocently asked as he toppled and grasped the side of the cage... The cage raised a sliver, and in came a medical official, slipping awkwardly on the gore and froth on the arena cage floor. With a stethiscope in his hand, he checked Hellbent for any signs of life... With an indication with his left hand, the official motioned that Hellbent was certainly dead, and the match had been won...
As the PA system announced Pure Pain as the victor by death, the crowd roared, but no wrestlers came from the locker room to congradulate him as one would have suspected. Instead, LEGION OF ELITE members began to walk from the back and they came to the Arena cage to witness their fallen soldier, one by one paying respects to one of their own. Perhaps the Pure
Pain supporters were simply in shock from the sight on the monitors - Pure
Pain stumbling out of the Arena cage, his hand raised, and in that hand, the remains of Hellbent's helldriven brain - pieces of it landing on the floor and along the railings as Pain stumbled by the front row of fans... One little girl picked up a chuck of brain to show her mommy that she had gotten a true life UWF souveneir - but she was smacked and it fell to the ground useless. No doggie bags allowed - UWF custodians moved into the ring area immediately and began the clean-up... a body bag was draped over Hellbent's body - Pure Pain stumbled back to the locker rooms the victor, assisted by countless UWF Riot Squad pioneers, and the fans began to stream out the
Arenadome doors... Witnesses to yet another historic, gut twisting barf-fest that has become so well known around the wrestling world as the Ultimate
Wrestling Federation's CAN GAMES... Congradulations Pure Pain... Rest in peace Hellbent.... and see you next year - so long from the UWF Can Games

His open contract left for much speculation when his opponent decided to make a mystery appearance - leaving OG with no specifics to prepare for prior to the bout. Omega's Glory came to the ring with Avengelyne, bearing his great staff which he has been known to use on occasion - waiting amidst the square circle for the announcement of the man to face him... He was surprised to hear the old music of FATES WARNING from when they first banded together.
The crowd cheered, and out from the locker room mists came none other than
Gargantuan! Not seen since his early departure in January's Battle for the
Belt against Destroyer in the second round, many thought this to be a repeat of OG/Wizard - a fortified Omega taking on a superstar but rusty talent. It began that way entirely. Omegas Glory fought within the rules and used his 7 foot plus frame to batter and bash the everlarge Gargantuan down to the mat, turning Gargantuan's mass against himself. OG rode a wave of assault for nearly six straight minutes, picking apart Gargantuan to the point of limpness. After a powerbomb, things looked certainly finsihed for
Gargantuan - but for reasons unbeknownst to the wrestling public, Omega's
Glory never went for the cover... He instead took Gargantuan to the concrete, and that was Omega's biggest mistake of the night. For it was at that point, after Gargantuan scrambled away and around the ringside area, that he managed to ambush the giant Omega, and nail him in the knee with a chair. It bought him enough time to gather a breath or two, and focus his blurred vision, take OG back into the ring, and work on the damaged leg on the mat. OG could not get up sturdy enough to redeliver powermoves, and he was at the mercy of an air-gasping Gargantuan. The great Gar worked on
Omega's legs at first, and then went for a succession of DDT's. Three of them could not put OG away. Gargantuan returned to work with a jumping DDT off the second turnbuckle, with barely any energy left to continue. Lucky for the former member of FATES WARNING, it was enough to secure the victory.
Omega's Glory and Gargantuan - two long time legends of the UWF Heavyweight ring - put on the wrestling show of the night thus far. With Gargantuan appearing so rarely for matches (six in two years), this shouldn't hinder
Omega's recent establishment at the top of the heavyweight rankings...
THE WAR TO SETTLE THE SCORE HOSPITALIZATION MATCH IN THE ARENA ENCLOSED CAGE Kamakazi (8-10) VS. Frenzy (17-13) If Kamakazi wins, he earns a hospitalization match against UWF Lightweight
Champion THE SCISSOR
If Frenzy wins, Kamakazi will retire from the UWF WINNER: Frenzy DECISION: ref's decision TIME OF THE MATCH: 26:15 ENDURANCE FACTORS: Frenzy: started (720) ended (-2778) Kamakazi: started (710) ended (-4638) MATCH NOTES: So much was on the line in this match that the UWF executive committee tried to do everything they could to book this match, 'cause nobody wanted Kamakazi to leave the federation. He is an icon of UWF
Lightweight wrestling. It is sad and ironic that Kamakazi let his UWF Lightweight career ride on a final Arena match against his career long rival
Frenzy. This match was without a ring, and any spill could have spelled certain doom for either wrestler. Such was the case between these two superstar contenders. A back and forth battle from the start - both Frenzy and Kamakazi rode waves of destruction that brought the other man down.
However this match differed from any other Arena match. This match meant a lot to both men - it was to settle the year long score, it was a chance to put an extreme hurt on the other... it had one man's career on the line.
Thus, they fought beyond the capactities of normal wrestlers. Kamakazi took control early after a spinning kick locked Frenzy in the arena barbwire -
Kama yanked him free, and Frenzy fell to the stone suffering a blow to the forehead. A blood knot grew to a lump on his head, and before the darkness of unconsciousness set in, Frenzy leapt forward like an absolute psycho, laughing, and clawing at Kamakazi's legs! Down the line of suffering, Frenzy managed a devastating nut shot that brought Kamakazi to his knees. Frenzy grabbed Kama by the hair, and headbutt him six or seven times in the nose, splattering it open, breaking its bones, leaving Kamakazi sucking on blood drenched air. During one point in the match, Frenzy was able to slip out of a headlock by Kamakazi due to his missing ear - another point during the match Kamakazi managed to wring a bar loose from the Arena cage frame and spike it into Frenzy's earhole with all his weight behind him! But not one single blow of visciousness would put out the other's lights. Referee Bob
Callahan stayed back from the melee as they teared each other apart.
Statistically, they went blow for blow, equally delivering the assault to the other, until Frenzy got brainbusted and subsequently DDT'd onto the stone floor! Kamakazi took a cover, and Bob Callahan went along and counted
Frenzy for the three. Three times, Kamakazi took Frenzy off the floor and spiked him headfirst into the cement, followed by a moot three-count. Frenzy was in desperate trouble, barely clinging to consciousness, his face ballooned and busted worse than any encounter they'd ever had. But Frenzy wouldn't die and fall beyond the point of no return. He clung to the edge of what was left, and found his way over to the pipe Kamakazi had wrung off the cage earlier, left forgotten and stumbled upon in the corner. Kamakazi came in for what could have been the kill, and Frenzy blindly swung and hit
Kamakazi in the side of his face, crushing his zygomatic bone and jaw into splinters - for this was no WWF prop - no WCW rubber 2x4 - this was 100% real UWF Arena steel, and it nearly took Kamakazi's head apart. Frenzy bellowed like a madman fighting for his life in one last battlecry, and delivered three blows to Kamakazi's face - the last of which left him motionless. Frenzy fell atop his foe, barely moving, but moving just enough.
Kama was checked several times for consciousness, and failed every time. Referee Bob Callahan panicked, called for the cage to be risen immediately, and paramedics swarmed in as an ambulance was backed into the Arenadome.
Fans were nearly silent, their mouths agape, as Frenzy was announced the winner of the match, and Kamakazi was announced retired from the UWF. Frenzy was rolled unwillingly onto a stretcher in time and strapped to a board and moved out, but the concern was with Kamakazi, who barely looked himself after suffering the injury. Medical attendants moved frantically around
Kamakazi, and as they were preparing to drive him away via ambulance, a chopper could be heard landing in the parking lot. He was to be emergency lifted to the nearest trauma center. During the stillness and concern, Dr.
Undead found his way over to the microphone as footage in homage of Kamakazi was played over the monitors. He tested it for sound and then spoke on behalf of ZOMBIES UNLIMITED... "Ladies and gents - there goes the best thing that ever came out of FATES WARNING! In fact, that man was the heart and soul ofFATES WARNING as far as we're all concerned! Pricks in the back should be on your knees! Kamakazi had more fighting spirit that all of you other FATE schmucks put together!" Dr. Undead dropped the mic and the ring attendants went about preparing the ring for the next match.....
UWF ARENA CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH The Reaper (6-4) VS. TWO TIME UWF ARENA CHAMPION Darkson (5-2) WINNER: new UWF Arena Champion - The Reaper DECISION: pinfall TIME OF THE MATCH: 7:45 ENDURANCE FACTORS: The Reaper: started (795) ended (-767) UWF 2x Arena Champion Darkson: started (850) ended (-1004) MATCH NOTES: Darkson is a man who takes on all challenges to the fullest extreme. He never looked to cushion his championship reign with numerous fluffed up wins. He is simply determined to take on his greatest foes, til death do they part, gold or no gold. The Reaper has been Darkson's wall in the Ultimate Wrestling Federation - these two men faced off in Darkson's debut match here in the UWF. The Reaper was victorious after reversing a pinfall back then. Tonight, The Reaper pulled off another come from behind victory. The 2 time UWF Arena Champion Darkson entered this match after rebrewing an intense hatred between himself and The Reaper. Words were said that could not be taken back, and that bitterness flowed over into the Arena cage. They took it to each other toe to toe, face to face, blow for blow.
Darkson is not a man of extreme mass as is The Reaper - therefore his gravity powermoves worked well - The Reaper's skull thudding every time it connected with the stone floor. Darkson spent a good deal of his offense grinding the massive Reaper into the barbwired perimeter. Shredded lacerations opened up all over The Reaper - wounds that seeped blubber and blood. But the UWF Arena Champion seemed more bent on the distribution of suffering than he was defending his barbwire belt. His eyes filled with fire - the Arenadome pyrotechnics exploding with flames each time Darkson performed what looked to be a put away powermove. But no cover ever ensued, and The Reaper was left small openings for redemption. Unlike Powerhouse earlier in the night, Darkson's attempt to put The Reaper into a permanent grave fell short - these two men were too well matched, and it was only a matter of time before The Reaper caught Darkson in the barbwire. At one point, The Reaper had a strand of sawwire between Darkson's legs, and he saw'd away until blood streamed down Darkson's thighs. The UWF Arena
Champion managed a retaliation that nearly took The Reaper's nose off his face when he managed to get his finger up one of his nostrils for an outward yank. It was a viscious battle that The Reaper would not succumb to, even if he was the worse for wear. Many minutes into what is usually finsihed in just a few, The Reaper caught Darkson in his grip and powerbombed him three times to the cement - the last of which smacked Darkson's head to the floor and knocked him out. Blood trickled from all his facial orifices as The
Reaper defended his life and career and ends up capturing the UWF Arena
Championship title! Reaper has strived for such heights for quite a while, facing off against monsters like Powerhouse and Arena legends like Top Dawg in the past - only today has he finally received the recognition he has deserved for so long. The Reaper had faded away from the UWF after a series of losses late last year, only to return and dethrone what is becoming his primary rival here in the UWF Arena division. Whether or not The Reaper will carry his championship in the open as Darkson had will remain to be seen. As for the former two time UWF Arena Champion Darkson - its only a matter of time before he comes calling for his third barbwired reign. Congradulations to The Reaper - new UWF Arena Champion...

FLASHBACKS ONE YEAR AGO - JANUARY IDES SUPERCARD, 1999 ************************************** ************LIGHTWEIGHT STREET MATCH ************Kamakazi (6-6) ************vs. ************Frenzy (14-11) ************************************** Its tough to report this to what has become a fan majority in favor of FATES
WARNING, because in all honesty, this reporter could give a flying fuck whether or not ZOMBIES UNLIMITED ((OR)) FATES WARNING wins this great war of the ages. It could have gone either way - hell - the match only lasted about a minute. Both wrestlers HATE each other to the hilt, and signed this match with the incentive of taking it to the streets. Both men seemed to be prepared for the other - the fear and anger factors brought the violence level to such a high peak that they slaughtered each other before anyone waiting in the wings could interfere. The wrestlers were kept in seperate taxi's just a quarter mile from the Arenadome, alongside a busy Main Street.
When they were let out of their cabs, Kamakazi stormed across the street weilding a Kendo Stick. Frenzy immediately retreated and opened the driver's door of his car. He reached in and wrung the steering wheel off with iron arms, and turned to meet Kama, who was right upon him. They clashed, and battered the shit out of each other with their weapons. Each blow made your stomach turn - the reality of the violence being dished out between them.
Frenzy's forehead opened like an envelope with Kama's last strike... The shock of the injury caused Frenzy to lunge in close. Kama could not get in good shots on the raving maniac who was squirting blood from his forehead.
Frenzy screamed and thrashed down on Kamakazi's head and shoulders and chest with the steering wheel! Kama went down after six hard blows - one of which caught him injurously hard in the nose. With a suffocating choke hold over the fallen Kamakazi, Frenzy's dark red blood streaking his skin, Kamakazi soon went dark under the "frenzied" grips of a severly injured man. Frenzy passed out seconds after being declared the winner. It seemed that several other wrestlers were stirring in the area ready to strike to the favor of their stable, but their was an air of concern instead. Kamakazi was choking on gore from his broken nose, spitting out teeth. Frenzy was out cold, the wound on his forehead open wide like a mouth, and he wasn't moving at all.
Ambulances cleared the scene. Later at the hospital, in seperate wings of course, Kamakazi went under the knife to repair a broken upper pallette and nose. He also lost six front teeth. Frenzy underwent brain surgery to relieve the swelling of his brain, nevermind the 34 stitches to close his brow and forehead. Surgery with Frenzy was tense but successful. He was closed up and sent to recovery. He's expected to be sidelined well into
February. Kamakazi will be in no shape to compete in the upcoming COLD WAR
II - but could be day to day around Feb 1st. He also has some serious healing to do. Neither man wins this battle in my opinion. And ever still, the FATES WARNING / ZOMBIES UNLIMTED wars continue...
