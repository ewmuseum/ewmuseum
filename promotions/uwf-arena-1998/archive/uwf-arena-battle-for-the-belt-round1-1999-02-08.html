NO MORE ENTRIES BEING ACCEPTED!!! ALL TOURNIES FILLED... In each division, the Champion will be excluded from competition - the winner of each Battle for the Belt will face off against their division's champion in the final match...

TAG TEAM BATTLE FOR THE BELT ROUND I: APOCALYPSE (6-4) Omega's Glory & Saint Necrodamn -d.- EXPENDABLE YOUTH (1-0) Johnny Reb & Hawk Two excellently matched teams grind it out for 20 minutes - quick tags - multiple pin attempts - including a dramatic comeback by Johnny Reb that almost took the match, but failed. Hawk gives up the pin after being gashed wide open out on the floor. He took 22 stitches to the forehead after the match. APOCALYPSE, Omega's Glory and Saint Necrodamn advance to the final four...
PRIME CUTS (6-1) Razor Blade Arber & Midnight Domination -d.- DAWG POUND (1-0) Top Dawg & Soldier Grunge PRIME CUTS stands up to UWF GANG WARFARE CHAMPIONS Top Dawg and Soldier
Grunge, a very formidable combination. Ten minutes into the bout, Razor
Blade Arber tangled up Soldier Grunge in a small package out of nowhere and took the match. It has become obvious to the world now, that Razor Blade
Arber and Midnight Domination simply aint no fluke. PRIME CUTS Razor Blade
Arber and Midnight Domination advance to the final four...
BLACK SANITY Snake Zurlock & Darkson -vs.- OLD SCHOOL The Winner & Mighty Quinn BAD MEDICINE Mike "STRYCHNINE" Sullivan & Steve "CYANIDE" Emery -vs.- BIRD'S NEST Soldier Buzz & The People's White Boy Results to be posted throughout February... LIGHTWEIGHT BATTLE FOR THE BELT ROUND I: Inferno (2-1) -d.- The Lesson Man (13-5) The Lesson Man was one of the few favored to go all the way, but the wrestler formerly known as Ultimo Inferno took over with authority. Inferno connected with two Ultimatums (a 450 splash) from the top turnbuckle, securing the win. After the match, Inferno put on a FATE t-shirt, and announced on the microphone that he would like to speak with Kamakazi in person and fulfill his destiny. Inferno is an impressive Lightweight wrestler, presently ranked high amongst the best of all time. Inferno advances to the next round...
Mack (3-5) -d.- Kampfer (1-1) Mack fought the match of his young career, and pulled it off with a Mack
Attack/putaway series of uppercuts and roundhouses to Kampfer's jaw. Bookies and fans were shocked to see the overlooked link of FATE succeed over the strong newcomer Kampfer, but Mack earned it. Mack advances to the next round...
Saint Necrodamn (7-3) -d.- Lord Igsel (1-0) Saint Necrodamn has been wrestling with the big boys too long. The former two time UWF TAG TEAM CHAMPION St. Necrodamn absolutely devoured Lord Igsel, and upon such sight, looks to be a shoe-in for the divisional final.
Necrodamn won by ref's decision after Igsel passed out from a locked on crucifix submission hold. He never submit - that was Igsel's personal victory. Saint Necrodamn advances to the next round...
Aahz (1-1) -d.- El Diablo (4-0) Another upset according to some people. The silver eyed devil Aahz looks stronger than ever and on top of his game, ending El Diablo's hardcore undefeated streak at 4-0, where most of the Elite are leveled. Aahz's elevated DDT upset El Diablo. Aahz looking to make a respected name for himself here at BFTB. Aahz advances to the next round...
The Designated Hitter: Gordie Frier -vs.- Soldier of Execution Kamakazi -vs.- Lightning Vogel -vs.- The Mercenary Stealth -vs.- Sinosphere Results to be posted throughout February... SUPERHEAVYWEIGHT BATTLE FOR THE BELT ROUND I: Hyena Cornwall (17-5) -d.- Steven Pierce (17-10) Two major superpowers of the superheavyweight division met early in this tournament, possibly giving other talent a chance to advance further.
Cornwall visciously Animal Attacked Pierce twice, biting chunks from his left trapeziod muscle and left latissimus dorsi. Pierce fought long and hard, but weight like these are exhausting to move for long periods of time.
Hyena Cornwall gets the pin, and advances to the final four... Doomsday Dan (1-3) -d.- Myst (6-8) This was a great even fight that lasted a long time, both Myst and Doomsday
Dan giving it their all. No put aways or fancy drama - just a good 'ol
American fist fight that had both men weak on their feet in the end.
Doomsday Dan pulls off the pin after a side splash late in the match, further damaging the record of one of the UWF's only exsisting good sports.
Doomsday Dan advances to the final four... JJ Rock -vs.- Mighty Quinn Omega's Glory -vs.- The People's White Boy Results to be posted throughout February... ARENA BATTLE FOR THE BELT ROUND I: Hangman (6-4) -d.- The Executioner (9-4) What is it about Hangman that has The Executioner's number? For the second time in two meetings in the past few months, The Executioner's rise to fame has been snuffed short of its destination by the nooseful antics of the superheavyweight Hangman. Hangman captures the number one seed to challenge
Powerhouse for his title, and ZOMBIES UNLIMITED will have to reroute their plans to topple Powerhouse. Hangman advances to the next round...
Darkson (0-1) -d.- Duke of New York (9-8) No rivers of blood flowed, but Darkson came out and delivered a message to the entire Arena division with one of the quickest Arena victories witnessed in quite a while. Darkson came in and destroyed Duke of New York with unescapable powermoves to the cement that left him squirming unconscious after only two minutes - and against the endurable Duke. Darkson advances to the next round...
Top Dawg (9-8) -d.- The Reaper (5-3) Top Dawg got some revenge in the Arena cage against the man that beat him in his debuting divisional match a few weeks ago. The Reaper obviously didn't have full use of his left hand - injured from the non-surgical removal of his pinky from a loss suffered against Vengeance. Top Dawg was just too fast this time, using his speed to overcome a sore and slower Reaper. Top Dawg advances to the next round...
187 (2-2) -d.- King Nothing (2-0) King Nothing was met in the Arena cage by a very angry 187 who was determined to put the fear back in his reputation. After suffering losses to
Top Dawg and The Executioner, 187 puts his career back in an upswing toward the championship after taking apart King Nothing in just six minutes.
Nothing was outmatched in size, and that edge gave 187 the room to execute many key powermoves, such as a powerbomb, and finally a disgustingly violent brainbuster to the rock for the pin. 187 advances to the next round...
Phantom -vs.- Devastator Jack Rabid -vs.- The Demon Apocalypse -vs.- Minnion Psychopath -vs.- "a surprise wrestler" Results to be posted throughout February... HEAVYWEIGHT BATTLE FOR THE BELT ROUND I: Pure Pain (6-4) -d.- Venom (7-5) An outstanding match between two long time UWF Heavyweights. Venom wrestled probably the best technical match of his career, but lost by the skin of his teeth by a gutsy performance by Pure Pain. Both wrestlers avoided the other's put away move - and kicked out of numerous pin attempts. No miraculous moments - just good mat wrestling and plenty of intestinal fortitude - and a pinfall in the 25th minute. Pure Pain advances to the next round...
Chaos (0-3) -d.- The Winner (15-12) You haven't heard the UWF Arenadome roar with cheers like this in a long time. Chaos puts an end to his losing streak and pulls out all the stops against The Winner, a veteran and formidable opponent. Chaos was uncomfortable with the fans routing for him in large numbers, but it drove him through tough times and helped push for the victory via Total Chaos, his put away. Chaos advances to the next round...
Rangon (3-0) -d.- Armageddon (2-1) This was a close battle all the way to the finish, that included the red rivers of blood it got so intense. Two very intense heavyweights took this match to the time limit, and both agreed to continue the match if the referee would allow it. He did. Rangon, in the 40th minute(!!!) won one of the longest UWF heavyweight matches in recent history - a match that either blood crusted man could have taken - pinning Armageddon after an exhausting
Perfectplex. Rangon becomes ELITE the hard way, over a very tough opponent in Armageddon. Rangon advances to the next round...
Blade (1-0) -d.- Blizzard (1-2) Blizzard and Blade shook hands before the opening bell, neither man very friendly at heart, but both having respect for the other stablemate. Snake
Zurlock was at ringside to hopefully coach Blizzard out of his sub .500 record, and never interfered, but did possibly cost Blizzard the match.
Snake Zurlock got a little too involved, and distracted Blizzard with advice at the wrong time, giving the larger Blade too much of an opening - catching
Blizzard from behind, Scorpion Death Dropping him for the pin. Blade assited
Blizzard up from the mat after the decision, raised his hand for his efforts, and the three men went back to the locker room without incident.
Blade advances to the next round... Snake Zurlock (4-1) -d.- Holy Terror (1-2) Holy Terror is slipping. After taking on and defeating Pure Pain to earn the number one challengership, Terror has been sought after by some of the toughest talent in the heavyweight division, and it is costing him on his record. Snake Zurlock is proving to be one hell of a wrestler - absolutely murdering Holy Terror in the ring with powermoves, crushing him limp and collecting the pin in just two and a half minutes. Holy Terror obviously has the ability to win, as seen against former UWF Heavyweight Champion Pure
Pain - but Snake Zurlock is a damned serious contender for the title - and on this night, he took the match. Snake Zurlock advances to the next round...
