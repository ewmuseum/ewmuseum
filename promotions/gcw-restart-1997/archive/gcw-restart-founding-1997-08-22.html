Hello All The GCW needs wrestlers so we can hurry up and start things. All you need to do is Email me at [detail withheld] , and I will get you in if at all possible. The last time the GCW was open everyone was saying we were on our way to the top, but it folded and now it is back. The REAL reason it folded before was because 1 person took it upon themselves to run the whole FED, and now we have 3 so the FED is almost FOLD-PROOF. Just send the App enclosed with this message back to me to join. Female wrestler's are also welcome. Here is the APP Your Name (Not Wrestler's Name) - Your Email - Wrestler's Name - Wrestler's Height (Be reasonable) - Wrestler's Weight (Be reasonable) - Wrestler's Appearence (clothing both in and out of the ring along with a description) - Hometown - Background - Entrance Music - What does your wrestler do while coming to the ring? - Will your wrestler "work the crowd"? - If so, How? Will your wrestler use Forgeing Objects? - If so What? Is your wrestler a HEEL/FACE/NEUTRAL ? Please distribute 250 points between the following catagories (100 points max. per catagory) - How good of a work is he/she How much push does he/she get- How much Stamina does he/she have- Name of Finisher- Description of Finisher (if needed)- What is your style- List up to 31 moves or at least 10 moves in your wrestler's profile- Name of Boyguard/Valet/Manager if you have one- Description of the above mentioned- Sex of M/B/V- Will he/she interfere?- Does he/she carry forgein objects?- Any other comments? Fill out two of these for a Tag Team. There is no women's Tag Team division yet so do not even send Apps for them.
