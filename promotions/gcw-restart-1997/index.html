An August-October 1997 GCW restart run by a self-described three-president team meant to be "almost FOLD-PROOF" after a prior single-operator GCW's own collapse, one of at least five distinct 1997 federations sharing the bare acronym GCW. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Global Championship Wrestling relaunched itself on 22 August 1997, telling its own story of why the previous attempt had folded: “1 person took it upon themselves to run the whole FED, and now we have 3 so the FED is almost FOLD-PROOF.”

Its own application built a wrestler from a 250-point split across work rate, push and stamina, asked for up to 31 signature moves, and welcomed female wrestlers in singles competition, though it explicitly turned away tag applications for them: “There is no women's Tag Team division yet so do not even send Apps for them.”

A second attempt by the same three-president office, 14 October 1997, admits the August relaunch “just would not happen” before trying again. No card, roster or result of any kind survives, and nothing records whether that second attempt succeeded.
