XWW if you please fill out a application.
2.) XWW INFORMATION XWW stands for X-World Wrestling and will be a indy fed located in the small city of Denton , Texas . We will be a farm league for the great IWC and we will be ready to take you through years of excitement. Which in all means if you become among the great here in XWW you could be boosted up to IWC according to if IWC is needing men on their rosters or not , so that is no promise.
A.) RULES 1.) NO PYROS 2.) ROLE PLAYS MUST BE ATLEST 3K 3.) ALL ROLE PLAYS MUST BE TURNED IN ON TIME 4.) THERE WILL BE ONLY 2 IN - RINGS PURE CARD 5.) IF YOU DO NOT ROLE PLAY 2 TIMES IN A ROLE YOU WILL BE FIRED 6.) NO PUTTING ANY STAFF MEMBERS IN ROLE PLAYS OR IN - RINGS WITHOUT [name withheld] OR MY PERMISSION 7.) ALL SRATS MUST BE TURNED IN THE DAY YOU TURN IN YOUR ROLE PLAYS 8.) NO BIG FANCY THINGS FOR YOU CHARACTER BECUSE REMEMBER WE ARE A SMALL INDY FED FOR NOW. 3.) SHOWS A.) HOUSE SHOW This will be displayed on the MONDAY X-POSED right before the televised show begins. All Role Plays will be due the Friday before the card.
B.) MONDAY X-POSED This will be a card that happens once a week every Monday . This Show will be only showed on a local channel in Denton, Texas and will only be a hour long until we get more people on the roster. All Role plays will be due the
Friday before the Show. C.) SUNDAY AGRESSION This will be XWW's big show and will only occur the last Sunday of every month. This Show will be Two hours long . All Role Plays will be the
