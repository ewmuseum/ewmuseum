====================================================== 10) "The New Legend" Kenneth Morlock (Debut) [[The XWW camera begins to roll. The scene is a very dark office inside Kenneth
Morlock's townhouse in Memphis Tennessee. Inside the office you can see a huge oak desk. On top of the dek there are basic desk items, such as a stapler, pens, pencils, a few files are also laying on top of the desk. Behind the desk is a huge leather chair. All you can see of the chair at this moment is the back of it. Behind the chair is a huge window that is covered up by huge blinds. The room is all but slient until a voice starts coming from behind the chair. The voice is none other than "The New Legend" Kenneth Morlock.]]
[["TNL" KM]] A new day, a new era, a new circumstance. For some people all these new things would be nothing but a burden for them. For most people a new day would mean that they have to leave the things they have seen and done, where they belong in the past. For most people a new era would mean that there was a change like no other coming and that they would have to either adjust or get out. And for most people a new circumstance would mean that what they have done and what they wanted to do are now out the window because a new inevitable has come into play.
[[The chair turns around and there he is sitting none other than "The New
Legend" Kenneth Morlock. Kenneth Morlock is wearing a pair of light colored khakie pants, a brown on light brown, button up shirt, and a pair of Doc Martin boots. Kenneth has his short, light brown hair in a perfect part to the right side of his head. In the camera view you can see his hazel eyes almost glowing for everyone to see. He looks up at the camera with an almost deadly serious look.]]
[["TNL" KM]] Like I said these are all things for most people. That's because these people aren't a "New Legend". You see I am the one that is starting this new day here in XWW. From now on in XWW, the day will start and end for these wrestlers in
XWW, when I say so. The new era for XWW, would also be none other than "The New
Legend" Kenneth Morlock. This promotion itself may not be that old, but even still change has come and this change will stick for as long as I see fit for it to stick. XWW wrestlers will now come to realize that Kenneth Morlock decides whether they win or lose, sleep or stay awake, and hell even live or die! The new circumstance here in XWW, would also be Kenneth Morlock. You see I am the inevitable here in XWW. It is inevitable that I will walk right in here and do what I please when I please and damn sure how I please. It is inevitable that I will walk in here and beat down who I see fit, exactly how I see fit, and it is also inevitable that I will walk in here and take a title of my choosing.
[["The New Legend" Kenneth Morlock shakes his head in disgust as he sits up in his chair and pulls it closer to his desk and closer to the camera. ]]
[["TNL" KM]] Now the first event that will take place in XWW with me on the roster will be on Christmas day, and it will be none other than Monday X-Posed. Trust me, XWW, on Christmas day someone will be getting some hellified gifts when I show up. I will have a bag full of tricks to use on anyone and everyone if I need to. Let me go on and run down the card for all of you so you can see exactly how I feel about it. You know you should want to know my feelings since I am now your domain ruler over you all. The first match would be a three way dance, Jagged
Edge, Bad Bonz, and Dr. Feelgood. A battle of the new comers in XWW. What a site to see, blown moves, suck ass action and rookies galour. I can honestly say I am going to be taping this one for the blooper real. You all can come right on out with these hard ass names and try to act like you know whats going on but when it all comes right down to it, you dont know a damn thing and if you try and run your mouth, you wont be going anywhere except into a coma either. You are going to have to realize that Kenneth Morlock makes no empty promises.
[[Kenneth Morlock chuckles to himself as he kicks back and puts his feet up on the desk as he puts both his hands interlocked bhind his head.]]
[["TNL" KM]] Next match we have is "The German Destroyer " Fritz Von Reutchen, damn just go ahead and try to say that three times...[[Morlock pauses for a second]].. now whoever just tried that needs a beat down, it isnt a tongue twister it is just a stupid ass Russian name. But anyways, his opponent is "The Silent Storm" Cody
Townsend. You know they usually say that the quiet people are the ones to watch out for. I say to hell with what "they" say. What I say is if either of these two members of the mediocre mafia try and get in my way, then I will have no choice but to get very loud and very violent proving what people say is wrong when I beat the living crap out of both of them...[[Morlock whispers for a moment]]...then you will see..[[Morlock hollars]].. I AM THE ONE TO WATCH OUT
FOR!! [[Morlock retains the angry look on his faces as he kicks his feet off the desk and moves in close to the camera again with a deadly look on his face.]]
[["TNL" KM]] Next is none other than a man that I have met personally. So has a match and some gasoline with me and my tag team partner. You see Bridges, I have come to realize you arent very much in this wrestling game we have here. Hell in fact you arent much in life itself. Maybe you should have just let those firemen stand there because extinguising you was a waste of precious water as I see it.
Hell, your name is even a figment of your own imagination. You may think you are the big bad monster but I just want to make it aware, that "Cookie" was a monster also! Now as for your opponent. I have heard nothing but good things about your skill and your moves. But you know. Hear say doesnt mean a damn thing anymore in the world or in this wrestling biz. You are gonna have to drop those money bags, and bring your high priced ass over here for the ass whoopen that I know you defintly need.
[[At this point you can see swaet starting to bead off of the forehead of
Kenneth Morlock, it looks as though he is going to explode. All of the sudden he pushes everything off of the desk and goes over the desk to where he is right in front of the camera you can here him stepping on most of the stuff that he just pushed off. ]]
[["TNL" KM]] Now the Main Event, if that is what you want to call it is more a match for me. Just the name Main Event means that real soon Kenneth Morlock will be involved in it. You see I am a major headliner wherever I go. Whether you like me or not, something you cannot do is doubt me. That is true and right to the point. But anyways, the wrestlers in this match, "Brutal" Brutus Bombastic and
"The New Age Icon" Chris Collins. Now this match may be one that I really need to watch in the case that it is a number one contendor match. Now this could be intresting because this might just be one that you see Kenneth Morlock throw himself into. You see I am one that is greedy I see a way to catapult my way to the top I am going to take it. You may hate me you may love I really dont give a damn. Just be sure to know that both of you are in dangerous waters when "The
New Legend" Kenneth Morlock is around. And for the special guest referee, champion himself, Simon Troy. I would definitly be watching my back. Word to the wise! Here the words when I say this,...\
PRETTY... PLUS POWER... EQUALS... ME! [[The camera begins to fade out to black as "The New Legend" Kenneth Morlock pushes the camera man out of the way and leaves the room.]]
============================================================= 11) ???????????????? [Hometown, sweet hometown. A special place at a special time. Childhood. Hometown is always special] [Even for Big Time Stars.] [Even though he never quite fit in or felt well-liked by the kids his age. Even though he always could feel his personality and outlook and attitudes toward life and certain groups of people were a bit different than everyone else around him. Even though it was the heart of Cardinal country, and he was a
49ers fan.. Even though he'd like nothing more to move away from Devils Post,
Arizona, and get the hell out of his mother's house...and even though he's scarcely shown his face in it in the past 8 months. Ever since the "local boy made a fool" hysteria that broke out when some fool of a promoter decided to let a fool of a wrestler almost end his career. Ever since that terrifying period when he had to basically live in exile after that for a while, layed up in bed.. In spite of all those things, his hometown is still special.]
[So, it's only natural that Denton, Texas is special to the fools that run rampant around here. The same fools that love Indy Fed wrestling, watching sub-par wrestlers putting on poor examples of wrestling in a poorly contructed ring. Where the hell is the sports entertainment around these parts? There's more to wrestling than just a drop toe hold, and a fireman's carry slam. Where the hell is all the glitz and glamor? The big stars, with the big wallets, and the big bank accounts?]
[This is a story of one said man. A man from a small Arizona town, who doesn't even claim it as his rightful home. Tuscon, is where this boy claims to have grown up and gotten his roots. Quaint huh? This is a story of a guy, who at one time was on top of the world in a large wrestling federation, but now finds himself residing in the small, everyone knows everyone, and everyone knows your business town of Denton, Texas.]
[Fame and fortune don't mean much around here. A totally different mindset, and atmosphere around these parts. But, hell, that may very well change soon]
Voice - Dammit, where the hell are we? Cattlebrush on this side. Desert on the other. Hell, I always knew I was a demon in disguise, but even I was hoping hell would have a better doormat!
[Fade to the inside of a car. SUV rather. That same man, who is from a small town much like this, who once started out in a place much like this, who reached fame and fortune, now sits in a car going down US Interstate 35. His destination? Denton, Texas...home of the XWW.]
Voice - Hard to believe that in all my years of excellence, this is where I'd wind up for my last hurrah.
[As the camera gets a clear shot of the inside of the car. We see a rather young man, late 20's at the max. He has a neckbrace strapped to his neck, and is sitting in the passenger side seat, with his head resting on the window. In the back seat, we see an infant child buckled in the car seat. Just a cute, little giggly thing. More interested in pulling the sock off it's foot, than where exactly they are, and the hardships his mom and dad has encountered. And driving, is a lovely blonde haired woman. Both the man and the woman look strikingly familar.]
Voice - I've went this route before. Got my big break in a fed much like this in a town much like this. But what are the chances lightning will strike twice?
[The woman who is driving looks over the her husband, who looks to be in deep concern right about now. She puts her hand on his thigh to comfort him, and he just continues to look out the window]
Woman - It will be alright, Steve. Really. It will. We'll find a way. [The man looks down at the woman's hand, and then at her face. The two look almost bonded by the moment. They both then look at the giggling child in the backseat. Then look forward again. The woman goes back to driving, and the man rests his head back on the window.]
Voice - I certainly hope so. [As the camera pans down, it shows the newpaper article that was forementioned in the opening. "Local Boy made a Fool" is the headline, and below it rests a picture of IWC legend, Loaded.]
[Fade to Black]
