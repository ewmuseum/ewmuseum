This undersized, under-funded establishment has helped many up and coming boxers out when they were in a bind and nowhere to work out. It has everything one would need to get into shape. The place is pretty dark and depressing. Water pipes are dripping and the smell of sweaty men is pungent in the moist air. The camera pans over this place. Dozens of men are lifting various weights, grunting and groaning...struggling to just get one one curl in. In the ring is a couple of young, aspiring boxers duking it out, perfecting their style. Christian is in the office of his late Uncle Pete, behind a desk doing paperwork. He's had to pick up on a lot of slack ever since his Uncle Pete died. Christian couldn't let this place shut down. It meant too much to his Uncle
Pete for that to happen. So Christian decided to take up the administrative work to keep this place running.
He looks up from behind the desk with a pile of paperwork before him. Christian is wearing a tight black tee-shirt, blue jeans, hiking boots, and black rimmed sunglasses. He looks at the camera with his glasses hanging slightly down on his nose, allowing him to look over the top rim of his glasses. With a quiet voice, he speaks...]
Christian: Can I do something for you? [The cameraman is heard muttering something. But it can't be heard by the microphone that's mounted on the camera. It sounds like he's looking for a soundbyte or two though. Christian nods and removes his glasses, folding them up on the table.]
Christian: I did hear that I was set to fight "The Greatest" Mark Williams next card. Can't claim that I've seen anything from the guy, but I assume he can't be all that bad. The XWW doesn't let just anybody come in off the streets and wrestle.. But there's something that does bug me about him. It's his nickname.
THE GREATEST Do you realize what it means to dare call yourself that? To dare have the guts to take a look at hundreds of people who came before you and still have the arrogance to put yourself above everyone else?
What makes you so great Mark? Really, what does? [Christian rolls the chair back and gets to his feet. He still stands a full 7'0", one of the tallest men in the industry today, no matter what federation. His hulking muscular figure is still one of the most intimidating, no matter where you look. His long straight blond hair falls over his shoulders and down his back. It goes just beyond his shoulders, the length of the hair that is. Christian walks out from the back of the office and towards the door. The cameraman backs up and turns around as Christian walks past him and into the main area of the gym. Slowly,
Christian walks away from the office and past the various men working out. Each of them pauses slightly to nod towards Christian in a sign of respect.
Christian makes eye contact with them, smiling politely and nods slightly. Christian continues doing this while he talks...]
Christian: Mark, how could you claim to be the best? I can't fathom how you could make that kind of claim to begin with. Then again, from where you are sitting at, maybe you're convinced that you are the best. You think that because you pinned a jobber or two once or twice in your insignificant existence that you're somehow justified in declaring your blatant superiority to the entirety of the human face. Why don't you just take a step out of your world and take a look into mine.
[Christian stops and looks around where he's at. The cameraman does the same and sees the entire gym stopping what they are doing and just staring into the camera. Each one of these men are chisled athletes.
They look mean and vicious. Their eyes cuts into the camera with a cold, glassy stare. Some of them were lifting weights and others were working on their boxing form. It's obvious why they are here. They have something to prove. It might be to themselves or to a loved one. But it's burning within them. This desire is all consuming.]
Christain: [his voice is heard, but the camera keeps panning around the gym, only seeing these men standing there] Right now, I'm surrounded by men who would gladly go 15+ rounds with God himself if it would put bread on the table. These men have heart and desire.
They are willing to do whatever it takes in order to achieve what they want. They know they aren't the best. But that doesn't mean they will ever stop trying. [Christian pauses briefly] Here's an old piece of wisdom left by Uncle Pete. I'm sure you've heard it.
"There's always someone bigger, badder, and meaner than you." I'm a lot bigger than you Mark I'm a lot badder than you And I'm one HELL of a lot meaner, stronger, and experienced than you. I've been wrestling since you've been in diapers. And I'll be wrestling long after you retire. You say you're the greatest? I say you're just another bump in the road.
[The camera fades out. But Christian's voice is still heard] Christian: Try to show me why you are the greatest.
