Power Surge, 15 October 2003 (Part 1)

Oct 15, 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
["Face To Face" plays through the speakers as the camera shows the hot crowd waiting for the start of Power Surge! The camera then goes to our announcers Abe Martin and Beah Dahl.] Abe Martin: Hello wrestling fans and welcome to SSGC Power Surge! We have a action packed show for you tonight. Gregory Brians takes on Jesse Bollinger, AsH takes on the debuting ArchAngel! In our tv title match Hayden Grayson takes on Adam Cage and in the main event its the rematch between The Pretender Eli Bridger and The Jackal Bryan Vincent! Beah Dahl: That is looking to be a stacked main event! Coming up in just a few minutes we will have Toxic taking on the man we will hear from now Vik Ventressa! [Fade in on the shiny white wingtips of Vik Ventressa as he walks around the back dressing room hallway of the GrappleVision Studios in Memphis Tennessee. Weaing a white three piece suit, fine linen and leather, alligator and hide, the Dead Legend looks distorted with a smile of pissed off vengeance. A half sober smile blazing from one side of his grizzled mouth, Vik snorts as he heaves a thick cane over the left shoulder, water dripping down from his mangled hair, his bloodshot eyes paranoid with rage] Vik: What's the problem, what the problem, what the problem IS. I'll tell you fans what the problem is. This particular offense is a high treasonous of the worst friggin' kind. Can't be that bad Vik, can it? I bet you're thinking what could this talented and sexy ass son of a gun have to worry his head about. I mean you the fans are going, "Compared to my shitty life you're the macaroni AND the cheese!" And well, I hear your sympathy and say SHUT UP DUDE, you don't know me! You can't live in MY world!! I says -hold that camera still. I says I come all the way to the south and they stick me in a curtain jerker blasphemerzing my good name! You know it's not enough that I almost break out in a rash from breathing your air. It's not enough I sit and grow stupider and boringer by the minute every blasted second I stand on the wrong end of the Mason Dixie line. It's not enough for you great lactating cow abusers to bring me, VIK, into the funk that is whatever that damn smell IS in these back hallways! It's gonna get in my hair man, and that's not cool! [Vik composes himself and stances all boys II men for a second before looking up in a creepy smile, heading for the foreground. Remembering his newfound Southern Charm, Vik reaches into his coat pocket and pulls out a dainty white kercheif. Er, a manly white kercheif. Right] Vik: Sweating like a white boy in Jamaica. Queens that is. Queens that be like this fruitcake teabagger Toxic -this guy who is stepping into the ring WITH ME tonight and I guess that's management being cute. Being REAAAAAAAL CUTE for me taking up their TV time last week. Conspirulous? You better believe it's conspirulous and if you're looking for an apology you stuffed suit morons, you aint' gonna get it! SSGC can southern states grapple my ASS for all I care and wipe it with a Toxic Handkercheif! [A smile] Vik: That made me feel better. That made me feel- [From off camera] ???: Whoa whoa. Settle down there, superstar. [In walks Gregory Brians, cocky smile in place, casually twirling a yard stick in his hand] GB: I mean, ever since I met you, it's just blah blah blah. If that short term's not shot, why don't you get to the point already. You're keeping valuable time away from the _real_ attraction around here. Vik: And where the hell was the Real attraction when I was working The Wolf last week?! I mean not like I needed any help but you know it'd be nice to see someone I came here with show up and I don't know, support the team and make sure I WIN? GB: Quite frankly, I thought you had more in you than to call it quits to the mighty vice of the freakin' Bear Hug. I didn't have much trouble showing Hayden Grayson the new pecking order in SGCC all by my lonesome. Vik: Zuh? Didn't you lose your tag team match last week?! Didn't Hayden Grayson Donkey Punch you outta the ring or something? The Ticket Sella, the Money Maka, the Jabba to tha Stars!!! GB: THAT WASN'T ... my fault. The powers that be saw fit to team me up with some dead weight, it was all I could do to keep that one at four and a half stars instead of my usual five straight. But hey, better a "Jabba" to the Stars than a "Jabba" the Hutt. Packing down those pizzas pretty well, eh Vik? You could learn something from the lean, mean, neck breakin, title stealin, lady pleasin machine. You better just start holding up your end. C'ya lata, sucker. And you can count on that. [Brians walks off down the hallways with a superior smirk as Vik stares after him, open mouthed] Vik: LEARN SOMETHING!?! Better Start-? I used- Mothafuc... I used to school you boy! I used to beat you around and don't you forget it. Snap at me. You're lucky I don't kick, yeah KEEP WALKING! Hold up MY end. I'm gonna kill that Toxic punk! [Vik stares at GB's back as it gets smaller and goes around the corner. Eyes blinking in confusion, Ventressa spits out a curse and turns on his heels, hair whirling as he stomps towards the ring. The camera comes back to Abe Martin and Beah Dahl.] AM: What was that all about? BD: I really can't answer that question. Your guess is as good as mine Abe. [RA is in the ring ready to make the introductions as a ref makes his way to the ring. "Bring Me To Life" by Evanescence plays over the loud speakers as RA begins to speak.] RA: Our opening contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, the challenger from Corpus Christi, Texas. Weighing in at 239 pounds.. TOXIC!!! [Toxic parts the curtains wearing black combat pants with an olive vest. His face is covered by a leather mask. As he climbs into the ring, the light shines off of numerous scars located on his arms.] Abe: I wonder how many of those scars he made himself. Beah: You could always ask him. Abe: I'm not going to ask him!!! Beah: Then I wouldn't be worrying about it if I were you. ["All About The Benjamins" begins to play.] RA: And his opponent from New York City. Weighing in at 235 pounds... VIK VENTRESSA!!! [Vik Ventressa parts the curtains wearing black jeans with holes in the knees and ragged leg ends. His shirt is just as ripped from wear, and covered with more lint than your pockets could probably hold.] Abe: I think Ventressa looks a little filthier each time we see him. Beah: Well he is rich enough to afford mud baths, maybe he took one. [Vik Ventressa climbs into the ring and over to his corner. The ref checks them both over for weapons and calls for the bell to start the match. They both lock up. Ventressa begins to power Toxic back into the corner, Toxic then shoves Ventressa out of the lock. Ventressa runs back at Toxic as if to go for a spear, but Toxic jumps out of the way as Ventressa continues forward, shoulder first into the cornerpost. Ventressa pulls himself out to be grabbed in a front facelock and spun around as Toxic runs up the turnbuckle and jumping back toward the middle of the ring, flipping Ventressa over onto his back with a DDT.] ***DING***DING***DING*** Abe: Toxic and Ventressa are locking up. Toxic showing his strength by shoving Ventressa backwards. Ventressa races in for a spear and finds the steel post with his shoulder!!! Beah: And after that tornado DDT, Ventressa is suffering from both head and shoulder pain. Someone get the man an aspirin!!! [Toxic lifts Vik Ventressa up onto his shoulders before dropping him with an fireman carry brainbuster.] Abe: DEATH VALLEY DRIVER!!! Beah: And Toxic says hes not through yet!!! [Toxic puts Vik Ventressa onto his shoulder like a sack of potatoes before sitting him down on the top turnbuckle. Toxic then climbs onto the 2nd turnbuckle and pulls Ventressa over him from behind, lowering him into an upside down belly to belly. Toxic then falls forward piledrivig Ventressa into the canvas. Toxic places a hand on Ventressa's chest for the pin.] Abe: That was a Tombstone!!! This could be it, cover.. 1... 2... Abe: Ventressa got a shoulder up!!! Beah: I thought this match was over before it started. Glad to see Ventressa is still hanging in there. [Toxic begins to lift Vik Ventressa to his feet to do some more damage. As he does, Ventressa drives elbows into Toxic's midsection. Toxic and Ventressa brawl for only a moment before Ventressa gets the upper hand and lays a hard right on Toxic launching blood from his chops.] Abe: Ventressa and Toxic are trading out punch for punch. Beah: I don't know if you'd want to do that against Ventressa's well known boxing abilities. Abe: And those abilities are showing here tonight as Ventressa lands a hard right to Toxic's face that sends everything less of a mouthpiece out onto the canvas. Beah: My goodness. Look at Toxic's face. It looks like he got it caved in with a shovel. [Toxic and Vik Ventressa begin to brawl again. This time Toxic takes Ventressa to the mat with a swinging neckbreaker before applying a dragon sleeper.] Abe: What a turn of events for Toxic!!! Beah: Toxic isn't known for his submission holds as much as his high flying ability. I don't see Ventressa tapping out from this. Abe: I don't think hes going for the tap out right now as much as he just weakening the body part for later. [Vik Ventressa begins throwing punches with his free arm that connects with Toxic's face. Toxic loosens the hold just slightly but enough for Ventressa to grab Toxic's head and spin each other around into respectively opposite positions. Ventressa then takes Toxic to the mat with an inverted DDT.] Abe: I can't believe what I just saw. Ventressa just spun each of them around and dropped Toxic with an inverted DDT!!! Beah: It looked like Toxic's superb balance worked against him a little there because I'm pretty sure Ventressa couldn't do that on his own. [Vik Ventressa helps Toxic to his feet before irish whipping him out and back into a clothesline. Ventressa then lays the boots down on Toxic's.] Abe: Ventressa with a shortarm clothesline sending Toxic to the mat. Beah: And look at those vicious kicks to Toxic's ribs. It's almost like Ventressa wants Toxic to choke from internal bleeding, not just the blood in his mouth. Abe: Either way its limiting the amount of air Toxic is able to breathe in at any time. [Vik Ventressa lifts Toxic to his feet before lifting him vertically into the air and dropping him on the back of his neck with a brainbuster. Ventressa goes for a pin.] Abe: A brainbuster by Ventressa and Toxic appears motionless on the mat. Cover... 1... 2... Abe: 3!!! NO!!! The ref says Toxic got his shoulder up just in time!!! Beah: That was so close to a three count that wasn't even funny. Abe: Niether was the brainbuster Ventressa put on Toxic. Beah: You can say that again. Ventressa is definately working the wind pipes tonight and slowly taking Toxic completely out of this matchup. [Vik Ventressa is helping the seemingly lifeless body of Toxic to his feet and as he does so Toxic shoves him backwards. Ventressa is staggering off balance as Toxic plants him in the jaw with a savate kick so hard it knocks him back into the ropes and then as he falls forward he lands in a crossface chickenwing in the middle of the ring.] Abe: Toxic with a superkick to Ventressa's chops. Beah: He just flatlined him into the ropes. Abe: And Toxic now has a submission hold applied on the already hurt neck of Ventressa. Will he tap out?! Beah: Once again I don't see it happening. Abe: Even with the superkick? Beah: Especially with the superkick. He nearly knocked him out with it. [The ref lifts the hand of Vik Ventressa and drops it.] Ref: 1!!! Abe: It looks like Toxic could win this with a different 3 count that we normally see in a wrestling event. Beah: Maybe, lets see how the next one goes. [The ref lifts the hand of Vik Ventressa and drops it again.] Ref: 2!!! Abe: Ventressa is certainly not in a position to go anywhere. Beah: And with his arm still hitting the mat, this one could be over in a heartbeat. [The ref lifts the hand of Vik Ventressa and drops it again for the final time. It stops barely above the mat shaking furiously. He begins crawling to the ropes making slow but steady progress.] Abe: And Ventressa shows he still has life left in him. Beah: That he does, but I believe the Energizer bunnies in each of their hearts is about to play out. [Vik Ventressa grabs the ropes and the ref forces the hold released. Ventressa and Toxic fight each other to their feet where they continue to brawl. Ventressa eventually slips in a chop to Toxic's chest just long enough for him superkick Toxic.] Abe: Ventressa and Toxic are brawling again, perhaps for the last time this evening. And Ventressa throws off the mix with a chop into Toxic's chest. Beah: FLYING SUPERKICK!!! Abe: Ventressa is going for the cover... 1... 2... Abe: 3!!! ***DING***DING***DING*** ["All About The Benjamins" plays over the speakers as Vik Ventressa celebrates in the ring to a mixed reaction from the crowd.] Abe: And Vik Ventressa has defied the odds and pulled out a victory here tonight. Beah: This match was going in so many directions I never knew who was going to pull it out. Abe: This was definately a great start for an even greater show coming to you later tonight here in SSGC TV. Beah: You can say that again, now can someone please get the man some aspirin!!! Abe: In just a few moments we will see a high flying out between ArchAngel and AsH!
