Send the app to [detail withheld] <INTENSE WRESTLING ALLIANCE> | Application and Info | ________________________________________ Info The IWA is a FAKE wrestling fed. No Real wrestlers. You can have up to 3 wrestlers. The IWA has 2 divisions. World Division, and the Cruiserweight Division. The Cruiserweight Division is 220 lbs and under. And The World Division is 221 lbs and over. The IWA not ran by a simulator. This is not a Simulated fed. This fed is judged by what I think of your roleplays. Remember quality not quanity. If you put out a 200 line roleplay every just repeating the same thing. And the other guy you are fighting is putting out unique 60 line roleplays every other day. The 60 line roleplayer will win. I must be impressed with your roleplays for you to be excepted into the IWA. And also your roleplay must be at least 4 paragraphs or 25 lines. Or you will not be accepted. If you are on AOL you cannot TOS me for profanity in the roleplays I send out in an IWA Flash Session I send you. If you do you will be fired immediately from the IWA. And there is 1 weekly card and that card is Sunday Night Storm.And there is a House show every so often. Please try to limit profanity in roleplays to a minimum. Because a roleplay with a lot of swearing does not mean it is a good roleplay. There are four titles in the IWA. The World Title, The Cruiserweight Title, The Tag Team Titles, and the Television Title. Remember to send all roleplays to [detail withheld] . I will send them back to you everyday or every other day in what I call an IWA FlashSession. The IWA has a weekly newsletter. Which I call simply the IWA Newsletter. It will have all of the cards and PPV info in it. PPV's come out every month here in the IWA. There is only one Tournament here in the IWA. The Ringmaster Tournament. Winning this Tournament will give you an automatic World title shot. And it will give you the right of going by the title of "Ringmaster" ________________________________________ Application
