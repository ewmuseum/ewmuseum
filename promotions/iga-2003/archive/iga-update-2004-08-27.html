The International Grappling Association heads to it's PPV this coming Sunday, August 29th, 2004! Catch up on last weeks action here first! Under Pressure (08/22/04) [url]http://p220.ezboard.com/fewrestlingroundtablefrm13.showMessage?topicID=13.topic[/url] ****************************************************** * International Grappling Association Update. * * Thursday: August 22nd, 2004 * ****************************************************** In this release: - Notes From The Director Of Talent Relations - Roster - Champions - Rankings - Coming and Going - Schedule - Upcoming Cards ********************************************** * From the Director of Talent Relations * ********************************************** Welcome back wrestling fans! It's been a long summer so far for a lot of us. It's been a tough road in spots and I gotta tell ya, I've been a bit disappointed in my efforts to help Jon keep this federation timely. You guys have had great patience with us and that has helped out. We thank you. Consider these full updates returned to a regular weekly deal so we can keep you apprised of everything. On to the notes! UNDER PRESSURE: Is over! What a turn of events. Two titles changed hands as Kasady picked up the Phoenix Title. Roland Dean upset Damian Payne to win the TV title. Tori Vale shocked the wrestling world by defeated Tawny Blake, again, this time in a rematch for the womens title. Vale retains and continues to rise in the IGA! The Monster Factory had to face a tough task in Code Red in a rematch for the world tag team titles and walked out retaining those titles after a wild match, yet they walked out without the titles as Forever Unchained came to ringside and stole them! Buddy Drifter was stunned in a loss to new comer David Crisp and as of the end of Under Pressure, Buddy Drifter is on the injured reserve list. Tommy Danger also faced Steve Greedy in the main event for the world title in a classic battle that can't be missed. Be sure to check that out as well as the entire Under Pressure card! CHANCE ENCOUNTERS: It's the PPV that ends the summer rotation. This year it's in Memphis, TN at the The Pyramid and it follows very closely on the heels of next weeks Under Pressure! Live on PPV it is Chance Encounters on August 29th, 2004. Chance Encounters always has had an interesting concept. For those new to the IGA, here is how it works. All the wrestlers in the IGA were out into a hat. Names were drawn and names were paired up. The pairings will team with each other and try to win a tag team match. If they do, they move on to the Chance Encounters Battle Royal where they will become enemies and battle for a shot at the world title at Requiem For Pain, the October PPV! Also, the world champion, which will be either Tommy Danger or Steve Greedy, (Tommy Danger defeated Steve Greedy at Under Pressure to ratain) will have to face a contender that will be randomly drawn at the show! This can't be missed as this tests the mettle of every wrestler on the roster! IGA WEB PAGE: It's been a while since the IGA site has been updated. Again, it's been a tough summer but as director of talent relations, I do plan on getting that updated as soon as possible. The fall is coming and my work schedule will be settling down, giving me a little more time for my hobby, and that includes web page design. MATCH WRITERS: Like all E-Feds, we do have regular match writers but the next few weeks we need some guys and gals to step up. If you can do a match for either Under Pressure or Chance Encounters (two major shows where I will be limited), we need you to offer. It is greatly appreciated in advance. ****************************************** * IGA MEN'S ROSTER/RECORDS * ****************************************** Listed by Winning Percentage: (Inactive
