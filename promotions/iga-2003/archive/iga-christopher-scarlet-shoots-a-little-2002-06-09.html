International Grappling Association — Roleplay

Christopher Scarlet "Shoots a Little", 9 June 2002

9 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Before you read the following "shoot" please realize this was written in character, and it's not meant to offend anyone. I also mention the website RSWPF.com in this rp but I don't break character by saying anything about fantasy wrestling, and I treat the website as a legitimate rankings website for the sport. Also I'd like to take this opportunity to let everyone know they should follow a great e-fed called the (International Grappling Association) IGA. [detail withheld] =========================== IGA - International Grappling Association "The Goldenboy" Christopher Scarlet =========================== [Scene opens to a black logo with IGA written in gold lettering. Sitting in front of the sign on one of those metal folding chairs is none other than recent IGA signee, "The Goldenboy" Christopher Scarlet.] CS: It's funny how things turn out, five years ago I never thought that I would be in this position. I started out in this business jumping from promotion to promotion, all of them folding within months of starting up. I looked and looked for any promotion that would be around long enough for me to make a name for myself. Over the years I've found a few that have been around for a while, but they too eventually folded for various reasons. [Chris pauses before beginning again.] CS: I remember back when I first started wrestling that I was looking for some where to compete and there was this one promotion that was becoming huge. It was a place that was gaining a huge following, it was called EMWC. Everyone loved it, everyone wanted to be a part of it, and even today a lot of people feel the same way about them. There were however just as many people who did not like EMWC, and to this day that group of people remain. The reason? A lot of people feel as if the people associated with EMWC give off the impression of elitists, that they are better than everyone else. A lot of people believe that EMWC is the best and everyone else are merely pretenders to their throne, this is what fuels a lot of the ill feelings towards EMWC. [Slight pause.] CS: Do I think the people associated with EMWC are elitists and think they are better than everyone else? No, but I will freely admit that I am one of those people that does not feel as if EMWC is the best thing since sliced bread. Now I know what a lot of people are probably thinking, here is another jackass that is going to try and put EMWC down to make himself or the promotion he is in look better. You couldn't be more wrong. Do I think that EMWC is one of, if not _the_ best promotion ever? By all means yes. I think they are a great promotion that puts out a great product. I mean looking at it objectively, how could you not? They are one of the few promotions that have withstood the test of time, they continue to stay up and running when other promotions fail and disappear. You have to give them respect for doing that. Do they also happen to have some of the best wrestlers ever in their promotion? Yes on that account too, those guys are some of the most dedicated people I have ever seen. [Chris looks to side as to gather his thoughts, then begins again.] CS: So I guess the big question is, if I think that EMWC is so great then why don't I mark out for them like a lot of other people do? The reason for that is simple, I've seen equally talented people elsewhere out there. Despite how good Chris Blue and his people are, I'm sure even he would admit that there has to be someone out there that is better. No one gets better unless they are constantly trying to improve, and why do people try to improve? It's because there has to be someone out there that is better than them at what they do. There are three wrestlers in EMWC that I think are the best wrestlers in the sport right now, those guys are Chris Courtade, Alex Martinez and my personal favorite, The Gremlin. Those guys are great at what they do in the ring, and they are great in their interviews outside of the ring. When I watch them I am thoroughly entertained. Despite the respect that I have for their talent, I know there are people out there that are better than them. Just because I haven't seen them doesn't mean they don't exist. I just recently joined an up and coming promotion called International Grappling Association, this is a promotion that I sincerely hope is around long enough so that I can fully enjoy it. There is a wrestler there named Maxime St. Jean, he and his dog Speck are right up there with The Gremlin in my opinion. I find Maxime to be one of the most entertaining wrestlers I have ever seen, and if you take him for granted in the ring he will make you pay. There are many good promotions out there, and an equally amount of talented wrestlers out there all waiting to be discovered. The problem with this sport however, is that everyone is blinded by EMWC's greatness. Back when I was looking for somewhere to go and wrestle I started doing my research. I wanted to join the right promotion for me, and for me that is a place where I would have a chance to shine. I wanted to find a promotion that put out a good quality show, had good competition, and more importantly, had a future. I didn't want to join yet another promotion that would fold within a few months time. So when I started my research I went to the one place I never thought I would go, the internet. I got on the internet and I went to various websites because I wanted to see what the fans were saying. The good thing about the fans is that they know what they want and what they like, they also find the obscure talents in the world and bring them to others attention. One of the websites I kept hearing about was RSPWF.com, so I decided to visit the site and see what they were all about. Once I got there I saw a link that would take you to their rankings of both wrestlers and different promotions. I thought to myself great, maybe here I can find out what promotions other than EMWC have great competitors in them. I thought that maybe they would help some of the younger and promising upstart promotions get a little recognition. Imagine my surprise when I realized that this site must be bought and paid for by some fan that is blinded by their love of EMWC. [Chris shakes his head in disbelief.] CS: Who ever runs that site, or I should say does their rankings, it's so obvious that he or they are just another fan of EMWC. I'm not saying EMWC isn't great and that they don't deserve all the recognition they get, but give me a break. I went to the section of their website where they do their very own wrestler rankings, and you know what I saw? Thirteen out of their top twenty wrestlers were from EMWC, the top nine out of ten were EMWC people. Now not to take anything away from any of those guys or EMWC, but come on fellas. You mean to tell me that all of the other promotions out there, that all of them combined might only have one or two guys that are good enough to be ranked? Not only that but if you are going to be a fan of EMWC you should have Alex Martinez ranked above a lot of those other guys as far as _talent_ goes. I mean seriously, it looks like who ever does those rankings only follows two or three promotions. Or if that is not the case it looks like they are just throwing in a few guys from various promotions to make their rankings look legitimate. I guess what I'm trying to say is that if you are going to run a site that promotes EMWC then by all means focus on them and them alone! However, if you are going to promote yourself as a website that promotes competition between promotions in an effort to help lend credibility to our great sport, then give some of the other promotions or wrestlers out there some exposure. [Chris stops to think about what he just said and decides to add one last thing.] CS: There is one last thing I would like to say, and that's to all the great wrestlers out there. If you are in a promotion that seems to be doing good and appears to be stable, stick with it and show a little loyalty. Loyalty seems to be something this business really needs. I know a lot of guys out there want to evetually make it to EMWC but you know how I look at it? Why go to another promotion where you will be just another star in a long line of stars to grace that promotion? Why not stay where you are and try to help the promotion you are already in make it big? Why not try to become a legend in that promotion? EMWC is a great place with a great history, but anyone that goes there now is not helping to make it a Hall of Fame type promotion. They have already done that in most people's opinions so you won't be breaking any new ground. What people fail to realize is that if they help a different promotion make it big, their pay off is going to be much better. You will help the _competition_ in the sport, you will help _create_ history. Last but certainly not least, if you are looking for a promotion to compete in or if you are someone looking for a new promotion to follow, then give IGA a look, you won't be sorry.
