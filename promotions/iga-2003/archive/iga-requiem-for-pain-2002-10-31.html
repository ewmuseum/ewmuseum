ONE.....! TWO.....! THR....NOOOOO! DeLucia: Shoulder is up! The shoulder is up! [HUGE FACE POP!] Streamline: Wow! There is no way this is happening! This is a dream! [Tommy sunk back down after rolling up, his body had had enough. His eyes closed and he fell silent, save the rise and fall of his chest. The fans hushed. Corey lay in a slowly growing pool of blood that seeped from his forehead. Unmoving. Anderson had gotten to his knees and motioned for the
EMT's. But he held them at bay when Corey muttered...] Corey Irons: No.... [Corey got to his feet, wobbly, and he headed for Danger, grabbing at his left foot and tucking it up and under his arm, then he turned the most dangerous man in sports entertainment over. He settled one foot back and stepped over and in, locking on the Web of Death!]
DeLucia: Web of Death! Corey Irons locks on a submission hold! Streamline: And Danger is in no shape to fight it! [The place went nuts as Irons locked on his version of the sharp shooter, wrenching it in tight and really putting the power to the move. Tommy's eyes went wide once as he felt the pain rocket through his body, he then fell face first to the mat, head slightly turned as he tried to fight that pain.]
DeLucia: Irons has it locked in! Danger is fading! Danger is fading! [Tommy closed his eyes, slowly, hearing the voice of Paul as things seemed to slow down for him. His vision was misted as he blinked his eyes back open, seeing the fans rise to their feet, urging him on, but his eyes closed again as he faded. There was a sudden roar that touched his ear as the fans all came to life once more. Up on the ramp, the curtain had swung open and there, sitting in his wheel chair, was Dirk O'Malley. Behind him, half hidden still by the curtain was Katrina Marshana.]
[OH MY GOD POP!] DeLucia: It's Dirk O'Malley! Streamline: What's he doin here!? [Dirk set his chair in motion and started to head towards the ring, the electric whir of the chair at full speed. Katrina walked behind him a look of rising concern on her face. The reached ringside and Dirk set himself up so he could see Tommy. Tommy blinked open an eye and saw Dirk. He saw Katrina.
Then his eye closed as he heard Bull Anderson's voice.] Bull Anderson: Do...you...give? [To Tommy, it sounded slurred, but he shook his head and grit his teeth. No. No. No. He thought to himself and the motion brought a cheer once more from the fans. Across the ring, Exotica and Devante began to pound the mat for
Corey, who now really leaned back and made the hold worse. A shot of pain flew through the blood stream and made Tommy growl out loud. He reached out with questing fingers towards Katrina and Dirk, and fingers caught ropes.]
[HUGE POP!] DeLucia: MY GOD!!! He's broke the hold! [Tommy hung on for dear life until Bull could get Corey unraveled from him. Once Corey let go, Corey ran out of steam and fell forward, drained. Devante and Exotica still urged him on. Tommy lay there, listening to Dirk O'Malley raise his voice.]
Dirk O'Malley: Get up! Come on Tommy! Fight the pain. Fight the fatigue. Stand up! Stand up! [Tommy fell to his chest and face, his arm out in front of him. The fans were on their feet, emotions running high, many screaming, many hushed, tears in their eyes.]
Tommy Danger: I can't. [Tommy's head fell to the mat, staining it more with the blood from his chin. Bull Anderson had once more started counting the 10 count as both Tommy and Corey lay on the mat.] Dirk O'Malley: Yes you CAN! Stand up, Tommy! Stand up! Stand up! GOD DAMNIT....STAND UP! [It was at this point that the fans exploded, for they watched as Dirk O'Malley, crippled and bound to a chair fight to push himself up, gritting his teeth and shaking in effort. He grabbed the ring apron and yanked hard and soon, Dirk O'Malley stood....leaning against the ring.]
[OH MY FRICKING GOD HUGE POP!] DeLucia: OH MY GOD!!! Did I just see.... Streamline: Dirk O'Malley....JUST STOOD UP!!!! [Tears form in Dirk's eyes as he fought to stay up right. Tommy blinked open his eyes and saw this, heard the crowd, felt the rush of emotion and he settled his arms under him.]
Dirk O'Malley: Stand up, Tommy. [With all his heart, all his strength, all he had left, Tommy pushed up to all fours, the crowd roaring...and then he stood. The place exploded. Dirk could not last long, he fell back into his chair.]
DeLucia: HE'S UP!! Streamline: HE'S UP! [But so was Corey Irons. A man with perhaps even more guts. More determination. No fear. Corey stood and faced his Nightmare when Tommy turned. He stepped in, grabbed him up to finish the match with an Extreme
Smasher, but with a shift, Tommy moved to Corey's left hip, grabbed at the shoulder and suddenly rolled down and under, pinning Irons to the mat with a textbook inside cradle. Anderson had to move fast.]
ONE.....! TWO.....! [Corey wriggled under that pin attempt and rolled out, reversing the pin and holding Tommy down.]
ONE.....! TWO.....! THR....NOOOOO! DeLucia: Kick out by Danger! How the hell are they even standing! [Irons grit his teeth when he got to a stand and ran to the ropes. Bouncing off, he came at Danger with a head of steam. Danger shifted, grabbed at the mans torso and twisted, angled down and drove the body of Irons to the mats with a power slam.]
Crowd: Ooooooooo! [Danger popped right back to his feet, seethed out a breath and headed to a corner. Climbing up, he settled himself on the top turnbuckle and measured
Corey, waiting for him to get to his feet. Corey did. Dazed. Bloody. Tommy then leapt when Corey turned around and sailed out high and far. Camera's flashes flickered, filling the arena as Tommy came down on Irons with a bone crushing high flying cross body block.]
Crowd:OOOOOOO! [They hit the mats with tremendous force, air being forced from the two of them and they bounced once. When they settled, Tommy hooked a leg.]
ONE.....! TWO.....! THREEEEEEEE!! [HUGE INSANE POP!!!] [The crowd lost it. The place filled with a roar unlike any other as the three count came down. Bull turned and motioned for the bell.]
*DING DING DING DING* DeLucia: HE DID IT! TOMMY DANGER HAS DONE IT!! *DING DING DING DING* [Tommy rolled off Corey and to his back, eyes blinking as he stared up into the arena ceiling. Corey held his ribs, trying to fight for his breath back as he too lay there. The place was bedlam. The fans were on their feet. Stomping.
Chanting. Applauding both men. It was over.] Giovanni Corlone: Ladies and gentlemen.... the winner of the match.... and NEWWWW IGA WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION..... TOMMYYYYY DANGERRRR! [Paul and Danny could not talk over the noise and the pop seemed to get louder, or rise again as the curtain up at the top of the stage was shoved open. There was Todd Bishop. Battered. Bruised. Bloodied from his match with Anatole Lee and despite the pain that riddled his body, Todd made his way to the ring. He reached the ringside area and caught Bull before Bull could slide in with the title. Todd said something to him and Bull handled over the title, letting
Todd roll gingerly into the ring. Exotica was now at Corey;s side, checking on him, while Katrina was in the ring, trying to get Tommy to sit up. Todd made his way over and helped his partner to rise up...then draped the title over his shoulder.]
[HUGE POP!] DeLucia: OH MY GOD! What a night! What a week end! Devils Night and Requiem For Pain have come to an end and the IGA landscape has most certainly changed! Tommy Danger is the new IGA World Champion! [Danger held the title high over his head, rears in his eyes, as Todd Bishop and Katrina Marshana held him up, but he soon wriggled free and despite his woozy stumble, he made his way to Irons, who was being held up by Exotica and
Devante Anderson. Corey pushed himself away from them and met Danger in the middle of the ring.]
Streamline: Oh oh. [There, in the middle of the ring, two men that had spilled each others blood, had out wrestled each other to the point they could hardly stand, leaned against each other, touching forehead to forehead, holding each other up.
Words were exchanged, but no one would ever know what was said between the two as the camera angle was pulled back, the scene soon faded and then to black.]
