Steve Greedy's Challenge, 30 March 2002

30 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ Fade In ] [ The view pans in to see Steve Greedy sitting behind a wooden table.The room has nothing in it, except for the table, The chair Greedy sit's in and a small light on the ceiling of course lighting the room. Steve seem's to be counting money in neat stacks across the table of Ten's, Twenty's and Fiftys.]
"The Rich One" Steve Greedy : " Nice little payday the IGA gave me for doing pretty much nothing at all."
[ Greedy continues to count money as each stack get's higher.] "The Rich One" Steve Greedy : " If I gave you Fifty Dollars and said produce me
One Hundred dollars. How would you do it ? Some people would invest, Some people would buy a nice lil stock, But most people would bet. They would bet on a sporting event. Which just happens to be what wrestling is. As someone was looking down the line up for next week's IGA's shows, They would see lots of things, But mostly close matches. Most of the time, You don't want to bet on a close match up, The thing could go ethier way, Thats why its called a CLOSE match up. If they looked hard on the line up though they would see Steve Greedy
Versus Ash."
[ Greedy finally finishes counting all the money, and looks up to the camera with his normal trademark smirk.]
"The Rich One" Steve Greedy : " This would be the point in which most people start pulling out there highlighter to highlight the match, Or if they only had a pen. This would be the time in which they circle a particular match. Reason behind that of course being, Steve Greedy is an easy pick over Ash. Sure,
Sometimes you want to go with the upset pick, So if he does win you can get more money out of it, But sometimes it's safe to take the favorite cause you know it's safe money coming your way. Espically when your talking about a
WRESTLING match between Steve Greedy and Ash. If it was a weight lifting contest, Or a drawing contest maybe the money wouldn't be placed on me in vast majority,but it's a wrestling match. Which means, Your looking at the favorite on the betting lines in Vegas."
[ Greedy stands up from the table and walks around infront of it. We now see a close view of Greedy and his attire which consist of finely made Italian Shoes,
A nice brown Armoni suit, A very nice rolex watch and An expensive gold necklace placed around his neck.]
"The Rich One" Steve Greedy : " That might not matter to you Ash,but our match is gonna be in Vegas. Not to frighten you, But all those people who bet money on me. Which is the majority, Might be a lil angry if you pull off the upset. I wander what they would do to you outside the arena when you were trying to collect your money, or even celebrate your victory ? Might not be a pretty sight."
[ Pauses ] "The Rich One" Steve Greedy : " But keep that out of your mind my man. That should be the least of your concerns, Cause you beating me would be about as likely as The Montreal Expos winning the world championship in Baseball."
[ Greedy chuckles and reaches behind him grabbing some of the money on the table.] "The Rich One" Steve Greedy : " This is what were fighting for, or should I say what your fighting for."
[ He tosses the money in the direction of the camera.] "The Rich One" Steve Greedy : " Me on the other hand, I have plenty of that, what I want is a lil bit of power here in the IGA. Which starts by what I do inside the ring, which flows over to what I can do outside the ring. Outside the ring, I have tested the waters of the IGA, and I am not really dealing with smart people here. Won't be a problem, and In the ring I haven't been around long enough to get the propper respect I deserve, but I am off to a good start.
A start in which I would like to keep going the right direction instead of getting a set back by losing to someone like Ash."
" I am not trying to say Ash isn't a good wrestler << Coughs >> Okay, Maybe I am, But he does have something. That something is heart. A heart can take you along ways, But in the end you do need some talent. Which is what your lacking there Ash. Your lacking ACTUAL talent my man. Which leads to you going
_BANKRUPT_ Next week when we meet. Don't feel bad though, You won't be the first, nor the last to go Bankrupt. Just chalk it up in the "L" Column and move on Ash. That's all you can really do."
[ Greedy steps towards the camera ] "The Rich One" Steve Greedy : " You know I'm right....." [ He get's right up infront of the camera giving us a close up view.] "The Rich One" Steve Greedy : " I know you know, I am right Ash.." [ While chuckling Greedy puts his right hand over the camera lens, and we go to a sudden darkness.]
[ Fade Out ]
