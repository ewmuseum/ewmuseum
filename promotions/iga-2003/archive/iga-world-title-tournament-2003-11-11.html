World Title Tournament Announcement

11 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
************************************************************************** ************************ IMPORTANT ANNOUNCEMENT (11/10/03) ************************************************************************** ************************ From the Director of Talent Relations; The IGA World Title Tournement: Shocking to say the least!  Devante Anderson suffered a serious neck injury and will have to have surgery!  The IGA world title is now up for grabs.  But wait!  Devils Night 3 and Requiem have taken a toll.  Tommy Danger, Todd Bishop, even Maxime St, Jean, are out of action!  AsH is rumored to be suffering back problems as well! With that news, Jazz Madison (at Combat Zone) announced a tournament would be held to determine a new champion.  But that's not all!  This world title tournament is open to the E-Wrestling world.  Anyone can sign up for the tourney and once they do, they will be paired up against a random opponent.  It is the hope of the IGA tournament organizers that the over all tournament count reaches 32. The deadline for signing up is November 16th, 2003. To make your intentions known, do not reply to this post and think you are in. E-Mail your intentions, your character and where we can get in touch with you (mail or messenger options) and will will add you to the list and then send an information sheet to be filled out (for match writings.) This tournement will run as part of the IGA regular programing, with matches taking place every Sunday and the world title match between the top two, taking place at the December PPV, Tournament of Warriors! The organizers of this event ask one stipulation of the winner, should he be a non contracted IGA employee; they must sign a three month deal and defend the title in the IGA! ************************************************************************** ************************ IMPORTANT ANNOUNCEMENT (11/10/03) ************************************************************************** ************************ From the Director of Talent Relations; The IGA World Title Tournement: Shocking to say the least!  Devante Anderson suffered a serious neck injury and will have to have surgery!  The IGA world title is now up for grabs.  But wait!  Devils Night 3 and Requiem have taken a toll.  Tommy Danger, Todd Bishop, even Maxime St, Jean, are out of action!  AsH is rumored to be suffering back problems as well!
