As Mikel waited at the top of the ramp, his video switched to shots of him doing some of his most famous moves such as the Guillotine Legdrop, and the C-M-D. The first line of the song was then sung.]
"This ain't a song for the broken-hearted" [As soon as the drum hits and the electric guitars began, Mikel took a step forward and the lights around the arena lightened up again. Mikel could be seen walking down the rampway with a cocky smile in his face.]
"No silent prayer for the faith-departed" [Mikel continued the middle of the aisleway avoiding all hands trying to touch him. The fans continued their booing spree as "Mr. Magnificent" made his way to the ring. He walked towards the stairway to the ring, then slid a leg into the ring, then continued to bring in the rest of his body to the ring. He walked to the middle of the ring and waited for his next move through the song.]
"I ain't gonna be just a face in the crowd You're gonna hear my voice When I shout it out loud" [After the word "loud" was sung, two big drum beats are sounded. Mikel each arm into the sky, shut his eyes, and tilted his head to the sky. The chorus begun as Mikel stood there in the pose, and fireworks from the top of the building began to burst.]
