His feet were exaggerated by the black boots he wore, with kicking pads up to his knees, and black knee pads over them. The only color on his outfit was a single red line along the side of the pads over his shins.]
"Shallow skin I can paint with pain I mark the trails on my arms with your disdain Every day it's the same - I love, you hate But I guess I don't care anymore I'll fix my problems with the blade While my eyes turn from blue to gray God, the worst thing happened to me today But I guess I don't care anymore!" [Once Anatole and Maxime reached the ring, Lee rolled in quickly and hopped up to his feet. With a chip on his shoulder and something to prove, the European
Champion looked back to his partner, giving a nod across the ring to indicate his intentions to start things off. He turned to face the man across the ring, cracking his knuckles under the white tape wrapping his fist, and then kicking in the air a couple of times.]
Giovanni Corlone: Ladies and gentlemen...this is the main event! [HUGE POP] Giovanni Corlone: This match, scheduled for one fall, is for the IGA World Tag Team Championships! Introducing first, alreay in the ring, the team consisting of MAXIME ST. JEAN and ANATOLE LEEEEE!
DeLucia: A chance here for these two guys to pick up some more gold here, Danny. Streamline: If they walk away with the titles tonight, we truly will have an Empire on our hands, Paul and there will be no room for the likes of guys like the tag team champions!
Giovanni Corlone: Their opponents, at a total combined weight of 578 pounds... they are the IGA Tag Team Champions...Donovan Keltrane and RAAAGEEE! [The lights dimmed to just spots that swirled around the fans until that all centered on the ramp entrance as Black Sabbith's "Into The Void" began to drift from the PA system. The fans came to their feet as the tag team champions stepped out from behind the IGA logo'd curtain, tag belts displayed over shoulders, intent looks on their faces. The champions, they made their way down the ramp amid the cheering fans and tossing their belts into the ring and climbing inside glaring at Maxime & Anatole.]
DeLucia: The tag team champions look ready for this one tonight, Danny. *DING DING* Streamline: And it looks like it's gonna be Keltrane and Lee kicking things off in this match.
[Anatole charged into Keltrane, and began pounding his fists furiously into the slightly smaller man. The champion though, wasn't about to let his challenger's blows go unanswered. As they pounded back and forth, fists meeting heads with resounding thumps, Keltrane began to get the upper hand, blocking Anatole's fist and firing back. He pounded the muscular member of the Empire back into the ropes, then fired him out across the ring. Lee came off of the ropes, only to leap over a ducking Keltrane, who had been hoping for a Back Body Drop.]
DeLucia: Keltrane telegraphed the move there, Lee bounds over... [Coming off of the ropes again, this time, Anatole had to jump over Keltrane, who had dropped down. Donovan hopped up to his feet this time though, and hooked an arm under Lee's, flipping him over in an attempt at a hip toss. The bigger man showed his agility, though, and landed on his feet, keeping hold of the more experienced tag team wrestler's arm. He twisted under it once, wrenching on the limb, then a second time, adding even more pressure to the hold.]
Streamline: A block by Lee and now he has the advantage. Really twisting in that arm lock.
[Deftly, the smaller man twisted back the other way, reversing the hold and making Anatole yell out in pain. Keltrane then yanked that arm backwards, pushing it into a hammerlock, and adding even more pain to the mix. Lee was a gifted technician, though, and slipped down and behind Keltrane, reversing the hold, then he twisted it around and up, locking the champion's arm in a Top
Wrist Lock. With a snarl, Anatole pressed on the hold with all of his might, forcing Keltrane to bend backwards.]
DeLucia: Nice out by the European champion there. [Just as Donovan was beginning to stir, to try to fight his way out of the hold, the challenger slipped behind him, locking an arm around his neck. Just as suddenly, Anatole dropped, driving Keltrane's head into the canvas in a
Reverse DDT.] Crowd: Ooooooo! Streamline: Nice move by Lee! [Slithering around, Lee went to hook a leg and Bull Anderson dropped to make the count.] ONE.....! TWO.....! [Rage had slipped under the ropes and was about to drop and elbow to break up the count, but the right arm of Keltrane shot out and stopped the three from coming down.]
DeLucia: Keltrane with a shoulder up! [Keltrane rolled to his knees and went to tag in Rage, but Rage had his back turned as he was being forced out by Anderson. Lee took advantage and grabbed
Keltrane's left leg and pulled him back into Lee's and St. Jeans corner. Leaning back, he reached out and slapped at St. Jeans hand for the tag.] Streamline: Keltrane could not tag in there, Paul...and he really needs to get out of there.
DeLucia: Here comes the TV champion. [Maxime stepped through the ropes and took a hold of the leg that Lee had hold of, then lifted the other. Looking out at all his Max-Tremists, he then lifted a foot and planted it right in the groin of Keltrane.]
Crowd: Ohhhh.... DeLucia: Maxime going downstairs there. [Max strutted a bit and even went to taunt Rage who was about to step in, but Anderson ran over to make sure he stayed out, letting Max stomp away at Keltrane before lifting him up by the hair. Pushing him into a corner, Max followed up with a run in European uppercut that rocked the tag team co holder.]
DeLucia: Good grief, what a shot by Maxime St. Jean! [Keltrane staggered out of the corner but Maxime shoved him back in and pointed a finger at him. The fans boo'd as Max let loose with a good ole French blow, but all that did was serve to piss off Keltrane. He ran out and lunged, driving an arm across the chest of Maxime, sending him down with a run through clothesline.]
[HUGE POP] DeLucia: Keltrane looking to make a tag here! [Keltrane was crawling towards his corner on hands and knees as the fans got behind him to make the tag. Max rolled and grabbed onto Keltrane's leg, trying to hold on, like a child does a teddy bear.]
Streamline: Maxime trying to prevent the tag here! [Keltrane rolled to his arse and kicked at Maxime, forcing him off and then he re rolled and lunged for the tag.]
[SLAP] DeLucia: Keltrane makes the tag! [Rage stepped through and instantly went after Max and caught him with another clothesline as Max stood. Lee came in and was scooped up and slammed to the mat with a big body slam. Max got up again and came in at Rage, who surprisingly dropped and wrapped his feet about that of Max's, dropping the TV champion with a drop toe hold. In the fall, Max's cranium caught that of Lee's as he lay on the mat after the body slam.]
Crowd: Ooooooo! DeLucia: Maxime with a headbutt on Lee! [Lee rolled out and held his head, but quickly got to the corner again, shaking his head. Rage grabbed at Max's singlet and forced him to rise before he creased his chest with an open hand chop.]
Crowd: Woooooo! [Maxime flinched and tried to cover up, but Rage came across the mans chest with another open hand snap.]
Crowd: Wooooo! DeLucia: Good God! The 346 pounder laying in those chops like he was choppin wood! [Rage held Maxime up, who by now, really wanted to fall down. But Rage just held him in the corner and suddenly lashed forward, slamming his head into that of Max's with a big headbutt.]
Streamline: Owww! Big headbutt by the tag champ! [Maxime came out of the corner as Rage shook his head and tried to wrap his arm up and across the bigger mans chest, looking for a one arm side slam. Rage smirked and planted his feet, making Max's eye grow wide as he could not lift him up. Rage reversed the hold and ended up slamming Max into the mat with an echoing thud.]
Crowd: Oooooo! [Rage let out a roar and flexed a bit, then snapped a look over at Keltrane who had gotten to a stand in his corner. A look of sudden foreloning washed across his face, but he sniffed back once and went to get Max again. But Max was scurrying to his corner and he reached out to tag in Lee. Rage snorted and leaned back to tag in Keltrane, who came in with a dart.]
DeLucia: Two fresh men in and looking to clash here! [As Maxime finally made his way over to the ropes and slapped Anatole's hand, the European Champion leaped up onto the top rope deftly, pausing for only an instant before leaping off. He stretched himself out, and both of his boots crashed into the center of Donovan Keltrane's chest, flooring both men out of the Missile Dropkick. Both men got up to their feet rather quickly though, with
Anatole in the lead. He broke away and bolted for the ropes, coming off of them and dropping again, this time to plant his feet in Keltrane's face with a
Seated Dropkick.] Crowd: Ooooo! Streamline: Nice seated drop kick there! DeLucia: A classic and punishing shot, reminiscent of Davey Boy Smith! [As the challenger for Keltrane and Rage's belts got up to his feet this time, he yanked the half of the champion's he was battling up with him. He pounded him in the face with a couple of stiff looking forearms, which staggered him back into the ropes. Grabbing onto Keltrane's wrist, Lee whipped him across the ring and to the ropes. On his rebound, Anatole was there to meet him with a lariat, only to be ducked. He kept running, and as both men came off of the ropes again, Anatole dove onto Keltrane, locking a taped hand around his throat, and tackling him to the canvas. Instead of some complicated hold, Lee strangled Donovan barbarically, while pounding his free hand into his face. The referee counted at him, threatening to disqualify his team.]
[HUGE HEEL POP] DeLucia: Now there is no need for that! Lee getting frustrated that he can't put Keltrane down and resorts to choking!
[After waiting for the firm count of four to come, Anatole stood up, and dragged Keltrane up to his feet, by the throat, as well. Then in one smooth movement, he hooked the champion up and threw him over his head, and let his back crash sideways into the canvas in a beautiful, if unexpected, T-Bone
Suplex. Never one to rest on his laurels, Anatole kipped up immediately, only to flip over backwards, landing on Donovan in a Standing Moonsault. He hooked up a leg, trying to take the win right there.]
DeLucia: Wow! Nice move by Le and look! He's goin for the cover! ONE.....! TWO.....! [Rage never made a move out of the corner this time, but again, Keltrane kicked out just before the third slap came down to ring across the mat.]
[HUGE FACE POP] DeLucia: And again, Keltrane kicks out! Streamline: How in the world is he able to do that!? [Lee pounded the mat then looked at Anderson, slapping his hands together in a One, Two, Three count before he whipped back his oddly done up hair. Rolling off his shoulder, he tagged in Max, who stepped through the ropes and grabbed at Keltranes hair.]
DeLucia: Lee and St. Jean working like a well oiled machine here. Showing lots of continuity.
[Max walked a groggy Keltrane around the ring and then leveled him with a balled up fist. Keltrane lay on the mats as Max taunted Rage again, but Rage didn't bite this time and just glared at Max before looking down at Keltrane and shaking his head.]
DeLucia: What is up with Rage tonight? Streamline: Nothing. He knows Anderson calls a straight match and he'll get DQ'd if he steps in there. [Max kicked at Keltrane, who rolled over and began to craw towards Rage and he reached up, extending his fingers. Rage urged him on and the two hands grew close, the fans once more cheering the tag team champions on.]
Streamline: Keltrane with the tag! [SLAP] [HUGE POP] DeLucia: And here comes Rage! [Rage stepped through as Max's eyes went wide again.] Maxime St. Jean: Oh oh.... [Rage stepped in and flexed, making Max hold up his hands, but suddenly, Rage turned away from Max and grabbed Keltrane by the shoulder. He looked at his tag team partner and got a forelone look across his face and sighed, then he slapped his massive paw around the neck of Keltrane.]
[SURPRISE MIXED POP] DeLucia: What the hell is he doin!? Streamline: Holy (beep)! Rage is gonna.... [HUGE MOVE POP] Crowd: OOOOOOO! DeLucia: Chokeslam! Chokeslam! Chokeslam on Keltrane by his own partner! What the hell is going on! [As the fans boo's, Rage dropped and rolled under the ropes and started to make his way up the ramp. Max was frozen in the ring, just blinking at what happened. Keltrane was laid out and Rage was walking up the aisle.]
DeLucia: Rage is leaving! He just choked slammed Keltrane in the ring and is leaving! [Max was still blinking and watched as Rage walked away shaking his head and looking like he felt bad for doing what he did, Lee stood on the apron and was hopping up and down, screaming at Max to make a tag. Then suddenly a form came shooting out of from behind the curtain and went after Rage.]
[HUGE POP] DeLucia: My God! It's..... Streamline: SCOTT SEXTON!!! [The fans ooo'd as Rage and Sexton began to trade blows.] DeLucia: My God! Scott Sexton returns after being put out of action by the very man he is going after right now!
[Rage and Sexton began to battle towards the back and Max finally snapped out of his surprise shock and saw Lee hopping up and down. He blinked once more and then hopped over a downed Keltrane to make the tag. The fans stirred as
Lee easily bound to the top ropes and set his arms out wide. He then leapt, flipped over once and landed a camera flash inducing senton bomb.]
Crowd: Ooooooo! [Lee rolled back, his back pressed to Keltrane's chest and he hooked a leg. Anderson dropped to make the count.] DeLucia: That's it! Senton bomb by Lee! ONE.....! TWO.....! THREEEEE!!!! DeLucia: New Tag Team Champions! Streamline: Yesss! The Empire has just gotten stronger! *DING DING DING DING* Giovanni Corlone: Ladies and gentlemen, the winners of the match and Newwwww IGA Tag Team Champions..... MAXIME ST. JEAN and ANATOLE LEEE!! DeLucia: My God! What a shock! Rage choklelams his own partner, allowing the Empire to walk away with the titles! They have indeed, just gotten alot stronger! The question is, why! Why did he do it!
Streamline: And I just got word that Sexton and Rage are still going at it backstage, but folks, we are out of time!
[Leaving this weeks Combat Zone, it showed Max and Anatole celebrating with titles held in the air, Keltrane suffering on the mats on his side, holding his ribs and the camera soon faded.]
