Both the Nightstalker and the referee went sprawling onto the floor, with the grappler atop the zebra. As Mark was climbing up to his feet, though, a figure that dwarfed even The Nightstalker slid into the ring behind him.]
Delucia: "WHO THE HELL IS THAT?!" Streamline: "I have no idea, but he's got his sights on Mark Davenport! I like him already!"
[An absolute monster of a man, standing around seven feet tall and with a rather muscular build rose up to his full height. His red hair was wetted down, hanging over his face and well down his back, though it did nothing to hide the snarl that escaped him. As the massive man growled, Davenport turned around and his eyes went wide. On instinct and adrenaline, he charged the huge man, only to be caught with both hands around his neck. The growling man lifted Mark into the air easily, only to release his neck and catch both legs over his shoulder, catching him out of the Choke Lift, only to drive him down into the canvas in a
VICIOUS Powerbomb!!] [HOLY GOD, HE JUST KILLED HIM POP!!] Delucia: "Good God!! This man is a monster!! He just powerbombed the life right out of the World Champion!!"
Streamline: [Laughing in delight!] "I love it! DO IT AGAIN!" [Mark's head and shoulders bounced off of the mat with a sickening thud that send groans throughout the arena, and he wasn't even given an instant to recover before he was ripped right back up to his feet. The seven foot monster manhandled Davenport over into the corner with the exposed turnbuckle, and practically roared as he began to pound the World Champion's head into the metal. Over, and over, and over again, until blood was pouring from Mark's forehead, and even then, the monster wasn't satisfied. He kept beating
Davenport's head into the steel until he collapsed downward, and even then, he kept pounding his head into the turnbuckles, though this time, against the second, padded 'buckle, which was quickly marked by Mark's blood.
As if that wasn't enough, the beast of a man yanked Davenport right back up to his feet and lifted him up to sit on the top turnbuckle, with blood pouring down his face in the quintessential crimson mask. After shoving each of Mark's legs onto his shoulders, in position for a powerbomb, the monster turned around and ran full tilt out of that corner, only to actually leap into the air and kick his legs forward. The impact of the Running Powerbomb was deafening and poor Mark fell away limply, twitching and with his blood dripping onto the canvas!!]
Streamline: "Davenport's bleeding! This is over!! The Nightstalker wins!" Delucia: "Who the hell is this monster!? Where is security!? Get him out of there before he kills Davenport!! GOOD GOD!! What a powerbomb!!!"
[The monster clad only in black jeans and a pair of boots stood up, dragging the bleeding, battered, and unconscious champion up to his feet as well. With another growl, he left Mark standing there, dazed in the middle of the ring. The seven footer walked almost casually back toward the corner made a mess with Davenport's life's blood, and then broke into a full run as a roar echoed out through the arena. Just before he hit Mark, the beast thrust his legs out and dove, absolutely FLATTENING his victim with a Gore! The impact was so intense that even the huge man tumbled away from it, head over heels, after bulldozing Davenport. He ended up upside-down, with his legs resting against the rope, and a wince on his face as he held onto the shoulder he used as a weapon!]
Streamline: "GORE!! Did someone get the license plate number of that truck!?" Delucia: "Davenport has to be dead!! This is terrible! Someone put a stop to this!!" [As he got up to his feet, he growled and shook out his head, flexing that pained shoulder a few times. Mark wasn't anywhere close to moving now, looking more like roadkill than the IGA champion. Outside of the ring, though The
Nightstalker was getting up to his feet, and he turned around in time to climb into the ring. He ended up staring up at the snarling beast for a moment, before the man taller than the massive superstar by at least five or six inches dropped backwards and rolled out of the ring. The Nightstalker looked as confused as anyone, as referee as well rolled back into the ring, though slowly and looking more than a little pained.]
Delucia: "What, he doesn't want anything to do with someone his own size?" Streamline: "It looks to me like he did what he came here to do, Paul! Whoever that was made one hell of an impact!"
[With the crowd still awed by the display of brutality and power by the huge man, The Nightstalker went about picking up the pieces. He dragged the bleeding and unmoving Davenport up to his feet, and managed to leave him standing, mostly doubled over in the center of the ring. Then he easily hopped over to the ropes and came off of them, leaping into the air and extending a powerful leg to cut the already broken man down to size with a devastating Axe
Kick! After the onslaught of the mystery man, and The Nightstalker's own intense offense, there was no way in hell Mark was doing anything but kissing canvas.]
Delucia: "Oh come on! Davenport was already out!" [The referee finally got up to his feet, and almost immediately, he spotted the bloodbath that had taken place inside of the ring. Hardly a spot of it had touched the Nightstalker, and with Davenport laying face down with blood pooling around his head, the obvious conclusion was made. The official waved for the bell, and stepped over, raising The Nightstalker's hand, declaring him the victory, even if he was left confused as to whom had handed him the win.]
[ABSOLUTELY HUUUGE THE FACE WAS ROBBED HEEL HEAT!!] Giovanni Corlone: "Your winner... THE NIGHTSTALKER!!" Delucia: "Davenport may still be champion, but he's had a lot more than his pride wounded at the hands of The Nightstalker and his mystery monster!"
Streamline: "This was beautiful!" DeLuica: Nightstalker now has a title shot! And we're out of time! 1 year down and an eternity left to go! See you next week for Under Pressure!!!
[The Nightstalker stands over Davenport and looks down at him shaking his head slowly as the IGA logo pops up on the bottom of the screen before the camera fades to black!]
