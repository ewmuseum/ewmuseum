KAWF VS IGA Federations clash Why would they do such ? Ego's Fame Dream oppurtunitys Hatred and most of all _RESPECT_ KAWF vs IGA supercard " Darkness Visibile." September 9th, 2004 Kansas City, Kansas Hosted By - Danny Delucia, Paul Streamline, Frankie Tha Pimp, and Russell Cunningham. *Opening Bout* *Cruserweights meet* Kassady[IGA] Versus Tyler Rose[KAWF] *Second Bout* *Tag Team Action* Tommy Rowlands & Damian Payne[IGA] Versus LezKanic & Tommy Flames[KAWF] Ronnie Jamieson has been banned from the ringside area by both Presidents. *Third Bout* * Texas Deathmatch* Todd Bishop[IGA] Versus Karl Ferguson[KAWF] *Fourth Bout* *Superstars meet* Kirk MaClean[IGA] Versus Riley Young[KAWF] *Fifth Bout* *Elimination Style* Cyril Lesley[IGA] Versus Kai Lewis[KAWF] Versus Terrance Cleveland[IGA] Versus Lark Fenriz[KAWF] *Sixth Bout* *DOUBLE TAG TEAM TITLE MATCH* Monster Factory[IGA] Versus True All Stars[KAWF] One of these teams will walk out of this supercard with both federations belts. *CO MAIN EVENT* *KAWF GLOBAL TITLE MATCH* *Last Man Standing* Vile Vince Viper[IGA] Versus Hayden Grayson[KAWF] *CO MAIN EVENT* *IGA WORLD TITLE MATCH* *STEEL CAGE MATCH* Tommy Danger[IGA] Versus Garrett Jax[KAWF] This match was booked cause of Jax pinning Danger on IGA Television when KAWF invaded the IGA In response to Jazz Madison showing up to degrade the KAWF. Deadline - Septmeber 8th at 11PM Central Time. That's for On cards, Flahes, Strats, and everything. Please send everything to [detail withheld], and [detail withheld]. Thanks for being apart of this event fellas, from top to bottom this should be an interesting show, and build into some very interesting situations.
