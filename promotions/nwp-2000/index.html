A January 2000 e-mail-booked e-fed whose own rules document sets out a kayfabe-strict, original-wrestlers-only house style. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

New Power Wrestling introduced itself on 4 January 2000 as a booked, email-run federation for “TALENTED and DEDICATED role-players,” promising, in its own words, never to hand out title reigns on reputation: “If you consistently perform, you will win.”

Its rules asked for original characters rather than reheated ripoffs — naming, as its own cautionary examples, a “‘The Complete Set’ Flex Fluger” and an “‘Ice Cold’ Steve Boston” as exactly what it didn't want — barred real-life celebrities and kayfabe-breaking from roleplays, and named its broadcast team: face commentator Todd Akers, heel commentator Joey Glance, ring announcer James Crowsby, and flash distributor Rusty Baker.

Its flagship show, Friday Night Free-for-All, ran on volunteer match-writing, and its rules post closes with a sample commentary exchange built entirely around two placeholder wrestlers, Whatshisname and Joe Blow, walking new writers through the format before asking them to use their own.

No roster, card, or later post survives; whether New Power Wrestling ever ran a show beyond this rules packet is not part of the record.
