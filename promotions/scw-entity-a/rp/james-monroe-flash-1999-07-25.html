SCW (Aug 1996) — Roleplay

James Monroe Flash

25 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene fades into a red car.The model is early 90's.The car has came to a hault and is parked at the corner of the Local street known for drugs and other illegal things..As the camera gets closer we see there is a man in the car slouched down.As we get right up on the car, He raises up and we see it is
James Monroe.He has a cigarette in his hand.He must of been reaching down for it...He has a smile on his face which is unusual for someone who just lost there belt...He lights the cigarette, takes one drag and speaks for the first time since the second Scw pay per view he has took part in.
"The Bayou Assassin" JM : Ya know these things will kill you, but I am starting to think so will the Southern Championship Wrestling world.. Man I know I just recently got my first title around here and all, but life around here is becoming one tough cookie...I mean Root of all evil has put mental and physical wounds to me.My wars with Dan Starr are adding up.Now I got to wrestle one angry man in Ric Studd.This is getting hard, even for an Assassin like myself.My schedule is filled, and there aint no moving it around to make time.Scw, is really strenous right now. But ya know, it does pay the checks at my house.Which is something that must be done.Nough bout me though, Back to Mr
Studd....He is all complaining bout his Pay per view match, but the funny thing is, he don't want the guy who beat him at the pay per view for revenge..He wants the top dawg, the man whom holds the biggest belf of them all Rick
Showboat..I doubt he will be able to wrestle, but even if he could...Why would he fight you Studd ? You lossed at the pay per view.That usually knocks you down in the rankings don't it ? I might be wrong, but I thought thats how things work. I mean don't get me wrong, I think you are one good wrestler, but
There is a pecking order.One in which I don't think you are on the top of.So you might want to slow down with all the commands and threats, so you can worry bout me the Assassin from the Bayou's of Louisiana...Cause I have been told I am pretty good by quiet alot of people round here <<<Smirks>>> One being Dan
Starr, A man in which I respect, and a man in which I wish would change his name.Cause I will never beleive he is the FINEST citizen in the city of nawlins.....No matter what he says or does...By the way Starr.I got a win over you <<<Chuckles>>> A tainted win thanks to Merc, but it is a win....What do you two guys say to a rematch...DAN STAR VS MERCENARY VS JAMES MONROE one more time...This time I will be ready for everything, and I do mean everything.
Maybe a Brass Knux title match on a Scaffold...Pepper spray won't even stop me this time Merc.I will walk away with MY STRAP....
( He pauses for a moment and takes another drag of his cigarette...He holds it with one hand and with the other he turns the radio on faintly.The song Outlaw by 2pac Shakur is playing for the first time in ages...Monroe smiles, Listens to the song for a moment, then continues to speak.
Ha, the only thing that's gonna stop me is these things..I never thought I would get addicted...Bad for the lungs, make you get tired quicker than you should..Woah, Sounds like Listening to Merc....Sleep seems to be the only thing that comes to mind when that guy horrifys us with his presence. Merc, you got me with pepper spray once, but can you honestly do it again ? Starr things the belt is his, I think the belt is mine, but the thing is you got it...The one who least deserves it has it...Is that fair ? Nah, but life aint fair....That's why I am asking, Unlike Studd who demands stuff.I am asking for a Triple threat
Scaffold match next week..Let's finish this once and for all.Get the past behind us and Look to the future. I know yall up for that, or at Least I know
Starr has the guts for that. <<Smirk>> Still wandering bout good ol Merc.
Friday Night, My leg is back to one hundred percent for the first time in a
Month and I get to fight a man who most over look round here, but I think is one of the better in this orginization.We have been on opposite teams in a tag match before Studd and in a rumble with each other...I know you are good, but I am gonna prove I am better.See, a wise man once told me " An angry man is a man in which I will fight anyday of the week, For he will have a weak mind and make a flaw in which you can captalize on"....Well Studd to be blunt, you seem kinda angry, which means your mind is elsewhere. I know you said people are gonna start getting hurt, but are you sure bout that one ? I mean you do seem to be a lil bit out of it lately...I hope you calm down a bit, cause I would like a challenge. I mean a tune up would be good for my Big rematch....
( He throws the cigarette out the window and turns the music down again.He rolls up the passenger side window, which we do not even know why was down, then looks back towards the camera.
Ya Know ( He looks away from the camera and finishes his sentance People are really doubting me round here, and it is starting to make me a tad bit frustrated.What's a guy got to do to get some respect ? Wrestle God ? Make a deal with the devil ? Go down to Georgia ? Man, Whatever it takes I will do.
Studd you are in the wrong place at the wrong time.You told me that once and I didn't back them words up....I will back the words up boy.Cause you are the agenda for the week, and I don't miss targets.
( The scene fades as Monroe Rolls up his window, turns the music up and pulls away from the camera.
