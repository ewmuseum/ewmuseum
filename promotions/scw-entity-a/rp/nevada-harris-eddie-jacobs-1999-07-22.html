SCW (Aug 1996) — Roleplay

Nevada Harris & Eddie Jacobs

22 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade in] <<The camera opens up on a winding dirt road. It pans up to see a house about twenty yards away. Or perhaps a 'shack' would be more appropriate. Two men are seated on a back porch in equally weatherbeaten wooden rocking chairs.>>
<<As the camera starts to pan closer to the house, the identities of the men are revealed. They are the newest team sensation to hit Southern Championship Wrestling . . . Eddie Jacobs and
'Rodeo' Nevada Harris.>>
<<The camera passes an old mailbox with the name 'HARRIS'
written in faded paint. It's obvious where they are. The men are talking to each other, and each has a glass in his hand.>>
<<The side table between them sports a pitcher of lemonade and a pint of Jack Daniels. The men quiet down and exchange a formality of 'cheers' in the air across the porch as the camera man reaches the steps.>>
<<After downing some of his drink, Eddie Jacobs makes a slight gesture to the camera. Sighing in deep satisfaction, Eddie begins to talk first, per norm, in his low, Southern drawl..>>
[JACOBS:] "Well lookie what we got here, Nev. Looks like one 'a them reporter types from th' SCW. Reckon Shay Peters is with
'em?" <<The camera shakes in a 'no' motion, and Eddie frowns slightly. Undaunted, he continues..>> "Well, bein' that Ah'm company, Ah figger it'd jus' be better if Ah said my piece right now so we don't have ta blast this sucker offa yer property, eh?"
<<Eddie chuckles again, takes another drink, and then sits up. The humor in his face drains out quickly as he begins again.>>
"Look fella, Ah don't need ya comin' up here while we're discussin' our plan fer Friday Night. We told ya ta be out here yesterday, an' ya didn't show. Ah guess SCW has a thing about puttin' its rookies last, huh?" "No matter." " 'Cause from what Ah here, y'all treat yer champs nice. Real nice. So in about two weeks, yer camera men, yer reporters, yer announcers . . . " "Are all gonna be signin' the praises of Eddie Jacobs an' Nevada Harris." "But Ah'll be honest wit' ya. Ah've fergotten tha names 'a those l'il fellas they have us up against this week. Somethin' dumb, ah know.
But Ah don't mind. 'Cause we're lookin' past it. We're lookin' ta spring like a bullet straight towards McShane an' Lebaron . . . "
" . . . An' hit 'em right 'tween the eyes. Nev?" [Nevada grins as he takes a cigar out from his pocket. He smells the cigar and the grin turns to a smile.]
[HARRIS:] "Well, Eddie. Are run in EsSeeDubbya is lahk this cigar heah. See, nothin' beats it . . . an' nobody beats us.
Nah ah was jus' heah admirin' this heah cigar an ah was jus' wonderin' jus' who said that you can come up onta my lan' without me sayin' so." [Harris shows an annoyed look. Eddie sits up.] [JACOBS:] "Nah, Nevada, settle down." [HARRIS:] "Nah, ah'ma wonderin' what in the hell he's doin' heah. But hell, ah'ma fair enough man. Ah'll tell ya what ah think. Ah think that the EsSeeDubbya is jerkin' us aroun' by throwin' some no-named talent at us. We don't need nor want warm-up. What we want is the big guns. Howeva, if the EsSeeDubbya wants some sacrificial lambs for the slaughter at the Harris
Ranch, then we'll jus' hafta oblige." [Eddie Jacobs looks on with his eyes bugging out. Nevada gets up and walks over to his window.]
[HARRIS:] "Nah ah thought ah'd only use this tuh hunt wolves, but since you'se heah, ah may have me uh practice session."
[Harris chuckles as he reaches into the window and pulls a shotgun out from underneath the windowsill. He stands back up and cocks the gun. The cameraman starts backing away.]
[HARRIS:] "Git! Git off mah property!" <<With that, Eddie, who apparently knew this was coming, stands up. He lifts the glass up and tilts his head far back. Eddie downs as much as he can while some dribbles off onto his neck, leaving a few colored streaks in his undershirt.>>
<<Eddie tears his flannel off and tosses it back into the chair. With the glass near empty, he throws it hard and fast to the camera man's feet. The camera man jumps back, and the image blurs for a moment.>>
[JACOBS]: "Look what ya did here! Ah'm doin' my best ta keep this fella's powder dry 'till our match, and ya gone an' set a fire under him! This is jus' great . . . "
<<Eddie snatches the gun from Harris's hands. He breaks the barrel and peeks inside. He nods to Harris and gives it back. Looking to the camera man, Eddie starts to yell..>>
"Look at this! He's got a double barrel sawed off right there, Ace. An' Ah c'n vouch fer it bein' loaded. So gimme the camera an' get th' hell offa his property 'fore ya wind up wit' another hole in yer head." "Gimme th' flamin' camera, Ah said. We'll mail th' film in, you jus' get goin' while th' goin' is good." <<The camera darts over to Harris, still pointing the gun at the lens. Harris never breaks eye contact as he jerks the shotgun up the the roof of the porch and fires.>>
<<With that, the camera man turns and starts to run off. Eddie shakily films him tripping over himself in panic while trying to escape the unkept territory of 'Rodeo' Nevada Harris.>>
<<Harris starts to yell..>> [HARRIS]: "You dumb sumbitch! Dun eva come back heah!" <<With that, Eddie turns the camera to Harris, still grinning. Harris breaks open the barrell, and two shells fall onto the rotting wood porch.>>
<<The two men start to laugh quite loudly as the camera turns out to the mess that is the Harris Ranch.>>
[Fade] -[name withheld] Handler of: "Rodeo" Nevada Harris [SCW, MLW, ACW] Uncle Istvan [MCW]
