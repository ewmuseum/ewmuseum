SCW: Coastal Wrestling from Florida 8-9-97 PART I

11 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
======================================================================================== ++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ ======================================================================================== ****Southern Championship Wrestling**** Proudly Presents August 9, 1997 /-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/ COASTAL WRESTLING from Florida /-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/ ======================================================================================== ++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ ======================================================================================== [The scene cuts in from commercial on a moderate sized space. Located in an old middle school gymnasium, two-thousand fans standing. Screaming and shouting they anxiously await the arrival of the first match. The lights begin to dim and spotlights go on from above and below the outside of the ring, lighting up the canvas and the aisles that surround. The crowd begins to cheer wildly as Casey Blain is standing by in the CWF interview area awaiting the start of the monumental occassion] BLAIN: Hello FLORIDA! [The crowd cheers as the recognize the sight and voice of the SCW's ring announcer, sometimes commentator and interviewer] BLAIN: LIVE, from the old Sarasota Middle School Gym this week, we're hosting the first edition of Coastal Wrestling from Florida. Brought to you straight from the offices of Southern Championship Wrestling. I'd like to welcome to the commentators table at this time, the unpredictable..."Georiga Stud" TULLY MANTEL!!!!! [HUGE HEEL POP as Mantel walks out from the back area. Dressed in blue jeans with black cowboy boots, white button down shirt and gray cowboy hat. He casually walks down the aisle, extends a hand to Casey Blain and then picking up a microphone to speak...] MANTEL: Thank ya vary much Mistah Blain. Always a pleashah ta be in ya company. BLAIN: Even *you* must be excited about this first showing. MANTEL: One uh the best things Southern Championship Wrestling ever decided was starting this focus on a particular group of wrestlers in Florida. And ah think we got tremendous talent already. Billy Joe Pherris [Huge Pop], Adrian "Tiger" Lee [another cheer from the crowd], David Smith [The fans go crazy booing and hissing]. Guess ah touched uh nerve on that one... [Blain Chuckles a bit as Mantel smiles] BLAIN: Not forgetting to mention other greats as the Coastal Champion, the Serial Killer, Joe Clapp, The Newcomers...Vic Garibaldi, Frankie "The Flash" Cash and many others. Some of which we will recognize and other beginning their careers with us as well.... MANTEL: Another thing that we want ta remind y'all is that the Coastal Wrestling from Florida is part of the SCW. All championships are Southern Championship Wrestling titles. As for example...tonight we'll be seeing Billy Joe Pherris take on Adrian "Tiger" Lee for the vacant SCW Florida Heavyweight Title. BLAIN: Well said there "Stud" MANTEL: Mahty kind uh you there partner. Ah do believe they're tellin us it's tahm tah take uh seat at the commentators table cause the action is about ta git undah way. But first lets' go to these pre-recorded comments...from the "Iceman" himself... The camera fades out and cuts back into The Iceman's gym but no one is present. The camera zooms over to a TV where the Iceman is on.] TV: "Pherris, you can see I am not here to berate you like usuall and I have a reason. I am down in Florida right now preparing to whip "Mr. I don't know what I am getting myself into" Von Johnson. After the match with Johnson, I will be back in the gym with some more words for you Pherris and Chains and Cages comes closer by the day. Live in fear Billy Joe because you are about to feel it!(Fade to black) [The scene fades out and returns to the ring where Von Johnson is already inside the square circle waiting for the match with referee Chet Blake] /-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/ David Smith vs. Von Johnson /-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/ "Iceman" David Smith comes out to "Brain Damage" over the speaker system. Fans all around the gym are booing him. His manager Jessica, leading the way towards the ring. He takes his time examining the fans and his opponent. As Smith climbs into the ring, he continues to stare down Johnson who is looking at him with the same ugly glare... BLAIN: It appears the "Iceman" is ready to take on the "Mighty" Von Johnson... MANTEL: Ah think ah'm ready tah take on Jessica! Have merseh mama. Ah got's ta have uh talk with Davey Boy aftah the match. She shaw is a fine lookin' lil' lady... BLAIN: I see what conversation will be revolving around... Chet Blake calls for the bell and the two wrestlers circle one another. They lock up and Johnson gets hold of Smith. Picking him up and bodyslamming him back down to the mat. Smith stands right back on his feet and stretches his back out. Johnson flexes for the crowd [fans cheer]. They begin to circle again and the two lock up once more. Johnson gets Smith in a bearhug...but lifts him up and turns it into a belly-to-belly suplex. Again Johnson is up on his feet, flexing for the crowd. With his back turned towards Smith, the "Iceman" leaps towards Johnson and comes crashing into the back of The Cajun's knee, sending him down to the mat. Smith gets up, taking hold of the leg and dropping several elbow smashes to the inner thigh. "Iceman" takes hold of the leg again and twists it around into a leg lock. Johnson yells out in pain but refuses to give up as referee Chet Blake comes to check on him. Smith twists around the leglock again and this time Johnson reaches upward, hooking the head of Smith and bring him down for a small package. The referee makes the count... ONE... TWO!!! "Iceman" kicks out. Johnson gets up off the mat and Smith doesn't seem to be moving at all. Johnson looks around and leans forward to pick him up. As he reaches in Smith EXPLODES with a thumb to the throat of Johnson sending him sprawling across the ring. Smith springs up onto his feet and rushes towards Johnson. He throws him into the corner and begins to unleash with lefts and rights. "Iceman" takes Johnson out of the corner and bodyslams him back down to the mat. He climbs up to the top rope and faces out towards the crowd... BLAIN: It appears thar Smith is going for one of his patented moves, the corkscrew moonsault... MANTEL: Appears....he jist...BAM...NAILS him with it. This could be it...but NO Smith lifts his head off the mat and forgets the pin attempt. Smith lifts Johnson off the mat and brings him back into the corner. He turns him upside down and hooks his legs under the top rope, locking him in position. He lands a shot to the mid-section for good measure and then hurries to the opposite corner. He winds up and RUNS into Johnson, with a knee to the mid-section again. Johnson falls off the turnbuckle and slumps to the ground. Smith quickly picks him up, taking him a few steps out of the corner and hooking the leg and the head. Lifting him high in the air...he comes down with a fisherman's suplex...holding him in the pinning combination. Blake drops down to make the count... ONE... TWO!!! THREE!!!! BLAIN: Your winner...in his debut match on Coast Wrestling from Florida...the "Iceman" David Smith. A pinfall win in six minutes and forty-four seconds. Impressive... MANTEL: Clearly tryin' tah make uh point that Florida is his domain. [Smith kicks Johnson out of the ring and grabs the house mic] SMITH: PHERRIS! You're lucky my crazy side didn't come out tonight. Otherwise I would've had to come into the dressing area and kick your carcas from Tallahassee to Tim-buck-too. You better pray you don't win that title tonight boy. It'll only lead to more punishment at the hands of yours truly. [with that he throws the mic down to the mat and heads over to the ropes, climbing through and hoping down onto the concrete floor. He motions Jessica back towards the dressing room and the two walk out to the sounds of fans booing and screaming] MANTEL: Ah think that boy there better jist settle down. O'l Billy Joe ain't gonna be taken lightly bah no one. Especially no rookie. What ta do...what ta do. Ah guess we'll find out laytah in the program. Right now fans we've got one of our two main events tonight...as the WARLORDS trah their hand at takin the SCW Florida Tag-Team Titles from Matt Moyer and Rex Stone. Casey...ah like these fellah...ah really do.. BLAIN: And apparently so do the fans of the SCW and especially down in Florida. We're going to find out if these rumors though are true as we head back up to the ring for this event. /-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/ SCW Florida Tag-Team Title Match Champions Matt Moyer & Rex Stone vs. The WARLORDS /-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/-/ "Electric Head" by White Zombie begins to play and the massive team of Taurus and Irish Pride come out of the back [Fans begin to boo]. Irish pride carrying the flag of Ireland to the ring. They taunt the fans as the quickly walk down the ring aisle to the main part of the squared circle. They climb up the steps and through the ropes. "Zero Zero Zero One" - by Hovercraft takes over the sound system and the curtain swings open as "The Crippler" Rex Stone and "The Menace" Matt Moyer storm out of the back and rush down the aisle towards the ring. Both men slide under the bottom rope and dive at Taurus and Irish Pride. All four men brawling around the squared circle. Stone taking Taurus and whipping him into the ropes. Taurus coming back off and Stone catching him and executing a belly-to-belly suplex. Taurus down and Stone levels him again with a boot to the face. Moyer grabbing hold of the equally big Irish Pride and sending him out and over the top rope. Both Moyer and Stone working on Taurus now. Stone grabs hold of Taurus and whips him into the ropes. Taurus comes back and Stone taks him down with a leg lock. Holding him there, Moyer goes back into the ropes and comes FLYING off with a devestating elbow smash onto the sternum of Taurus. MANTEL: The Florida Tag-Team Champions showing why they are in fact, wearing those tag-team titles. TCB BABY! Takin' care uh business tonight. Ah jist love this kindah thing. Seein' people that don' belong in the ring gittin' they're tails whooped. BLAIN: I must admit that Irish Pride and Taurus are not fairing well to say the least Irish Pride tries to re-enter the ring but Stone, already on his feet clotheslines Irish Pride back onto the concrete floor, sending him flying into the guardrail. Pride, falling over the rail and into the crowd. Moyer looking around. He points to Stone and "The Crippler" nods his head. Moyer GRABS hold of Taurus and gets in a double underhook. Stone gets moves into position. Moyer lifts him up and has him in the air with a powerbomb. Stone, on the way down takes hold of Taurus' head and simultaeously executes a neckbreaker. Stone turns around and covers him for the pin while Matt Moyer goes to the ropes to hammer away at a dazed Irish Pride... ONE... TWO!!! THREE!!!! BLAIN: WOW [the crowd goes crazy. Everyone is one their feet as the announcer gives word of the winners. The referee brings both men to the center of the ring, handing them their tag-team titles and raising their arms in the air for vicotry] MANTEL: Talk about uh impressive win. That right there... could very well be the beginning of reign for the Hearts of Darkness. Moyer and Stone certainly a for-mid-able tag-team here in the SCW. They right now are the dominating force of florida's tag-team scene.... BLAIN: Without question Tully. Right now though we're getting ready for the final match of the evening. The bout to fill the vacant SCW Florida Heavywieght title. Two newcomers to the SCW vying for the championship. Billy Joe Pherris and Adrian "Tiger" Lee... MANTEL: Lee's a silent one ain't he. Ah mean this gahz only been here one month or so and he wrestles one major match and gets in the finals for the belt. What kin ya say ta that. NOTHIN' cause the man is pure talent. That's whah. He's gonna be a force ta reckon with no matter who he faces. On the other hand...ya got a good ol' southern boy lahk Billy Joe Pherris. He ain't good in the sense of following rules, but he sure as anything knows how ta wrestle. On the ground, in the air. That boy kin do it all BABY! BLAIN: Well put my friend. We're going to take a quick commerical break. When we come back we'll be seeing the SCW Florida Heavyweight Title Match between Billy Joe Pherris and Adrian "Tiger" Lee... MANTEL: Ah can't wait ta see that one! [The scene fades out as MANTEL flashes that wide eyed grin causing BLAIN to chuckle] ======================================================================================== SCW e-league (Coastal Wrestling from Florida) copyright 1997 ======================================================================================== HOMEPAGE:
