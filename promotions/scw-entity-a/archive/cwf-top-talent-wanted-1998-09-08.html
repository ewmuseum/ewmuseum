Hey, I am the Booker of the CWF (Coast Wrestling from Florida), which is the Florida Region of the SCW ( http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/7876/SCWhome.html ). The CWF has been closed for a couple of months now, but we are starting up again, and it's gonna be bigger and better than ever... We are working on a brand new website format, and it should be ready by tonight... We are looking for the best talent around, and if your wrestler fits that category, then we want him aboard... Please Reply ASAP if you are interrested, and include an old sample RP. Now, a little bit about us... The CWF is the Florida Region of the SCW (Southern Championship Wrestling). The SCW is like the old NWA of the '80's in that it is one promotion that is made up of seperate feds all over the South. The CWF is the newest region, with the potential to be the best. For more information, visit the SCW website at........... http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/7876/SCWhome.html . I hope that you seriously consider becoming a part of this great organization... The website will be up by this weekend, but if you want to get a headstart, you can fill out the app that follows this letter... Thank you for your time, [name withheld] SCW Florida Region Booker ============================================ Rules & Regulations 1. You may have one unit. A unit is defined as a singles wrestler or a tag team. 2. The C.W.F. is an entirely booked e-fed. Results are based on your roleplaying and activity. 3. There is NO use of PROFANITY in ANY ROLEPLAYS. Only the word Ass will be tolerated 4. NO RACIAL, ETHNIC, SATANIC, or SEXUAL COMMENTS/SLURS. ORIGINAL wrestlers only...THERE ARE NO EXCEPTIONS. Do not even create a wrestler that is a copy of another 6. Champions MUST defend their titles, once every three weeks. 7. STABLES- There can only be FIVE wrestlers to a stable and two managers 11. ALL Roleplays should be mailed to the President to be forwarded to all players. ============================================ CWF APPLICATION: You may create one unit, either a singles wrestler or one tag team. Tag teams need to send in a separate application for each member. Remember to be origional!!! Wrestler's sizes must be realistic or else I will not approve of them. That means no 7'10'' 700lbs. guys. Also, the heights and weights must be realistically compatible.
