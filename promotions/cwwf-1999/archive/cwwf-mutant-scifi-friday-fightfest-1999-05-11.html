Hello CWWF Mutant Wrestling fans this is Jonathan Wasilewski, and welcome to another exciting night of Friday Night Fightfest! What a tourney we had to get a new champ! And right now I would like to congradulate the Scarlet Surfer on winning the belt! We have great matches tonight, we have a return match between Satan and Spawn, we also have two ring veterans making their first apperance as Mutants!
So lets get down to the ring where we will see one of the new Mutants in action! It is former CWWF USA Champion The Atomic Punk agaist the giant caveman
Ogre, and they will be fighting in an old space ship materials hanger!
So let's get it on! The Atomic Punk is down in the hanger allready, and now Ogre is making his way! Both of these men have superstrength so this should be a good one! Ogre,with the appearance of a classic text book neanderthall only 8 foot something runs into the hanger and is immediately nailed with a fist from an equally massive Atomic Punk that knocks him back about ten feet!Atomic Punk looks like Bruce Banner's Hulk with a purple body and his face looks like Sid Viscious with a purple mowhawk!
Atomic Punk now showing his superstrengeth by picking up Ogre and throwing him across the hanger!
Punk now goes for the pin! 1- Ogre kicks out. That was a an uncharachteristic rookie mistake by Punk who is no stranger to the squared circle trying to put Ogre away that early!
Punk now picking up Ogre by the hair but Ogre gives him a hard blow to the gut, and another, and another, and yet another!
Ogre now runs and nails Punk with clothesline knocking him about 20 feet! Ogre is now going for what looks like an old space ship engine and, Oh! He just nailed Punk in the head with that 300 pound engine! Ogre now picks up the Atomic one and hits him with a massive headbutt sending him through the floor a couple feet!
Ogre now picks up Punk and heaves him through a spaceship wall! What a move! Ogre picks up a huge pipe that looks like it could way about a ton and throws is on top of the Atomic Punk!
He goes for the cover, 1-2- Punk kicks out! What energy this new mutant has! Ogre now picking up Punk again, but Punk hits him with a low blow! I think that one really got the Ogre in pain! The Punk now seizing up the Ogre and nails him with an uppercut that sends him flying! Punk is now going into one of the spaceships and turning on a forcfield,
I wonder what the point of this is?

Punk with the pipe!
What a smart move! Punk now sends Ogre into the field again but Ogre reverses it and gets Punk into a powerslam, but Punk reverses it back, and powerslammed Ogre through a titanium flying saucer!  
Both men are lying down the ref starts to count, 1-2-3-4-5-6, Punk is up! He goes over and lifts up a 50 foot concrete wall, and Oh my god! He just splashed on Ogre with that wall and it crumbles everywhere! Punk goes for the cover!
1-2-3! Atomic Punk wins! What a debut by the veteran grappler turned rookie mutant! We need to watch him for being a contender for the world title!
Our next match is a match of the shapeshifters, and will be a hell in the cell match pitting T-4 versus Lucky 2! That should be a duzy!

He picks him up again and gives him a huge paw to the face! That one hit the mark! Lucky now sends T-4 into the ropes and he shifts into a hawk! T-4 comes down and claws Lucky in the face! He is now tearing away at Lucky who has now shifted back to his normal self! T-4 now shifts into a gorrilla! He picks up Lucky and gives him a major gorrilla press slam!

He gets into the ring with it and, oh! He just clobered T-4 over the head with it! He now just shifted into none other than Waz the Crazy Polish-Man! And he just Polish Powerbombed T-4!
He goes for the cover, 1-2- oh, T-4 kicks out in the knick of time! He now gets back to his normal self and sends T-4 into the ropes but T-4 comes off with a clotheline of his own!

Nitro goes crashing into the turnbuckle with great force!Nitro still learning how to control his great speed!

He now lays Nitro on the table but Nitro keeps on roling off before
Cyber can make it to the top rope!
Nitro now picks up the chair and trys to hit Cyber with it but he disapears and reapears behind Nitro, Cyber goes for another savate kick but Nitro blocks it and nails Cyber with about 85 savate kicks of his own in a second!
He now picks up Cyber, now Nitro goes to the second rope and nails Cyber with the heart attack! He goes for the cover, 1-2-3!
Nitro wins! Fans we just found out that when Nitro repeatedly nailed
