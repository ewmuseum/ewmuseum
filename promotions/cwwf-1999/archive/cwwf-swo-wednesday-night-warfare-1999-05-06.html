CWWF/SWO Wednesday Night Warfare

6 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GET READY TO BRING THE BIG GUNS TO BARE! Good Evening wrestling fans and welcome to another edition of CWWF's Wednesday Night Wars. I'm your host, Doug DeVore bringing you the finest wrestling action the CWWF can offer. We're ready for our first bout so let's go to the ring.
In our first match this evening we feature the team of Whole Lotta Rosie and Welfare Mamma challenging the ladies tag champions, the Hood Rats in a non title event. There's the bell and it's the Hood rats that start things off with a double drop kick that sends Rosie onto the floor. A double team on Mamma and down she goes with a double back elbow. The referee forcing Shadiba outside and that leaves Shaquanda in the ring with Mamma. An arm bar with a twist and a quick tag to Shadiba who enters with a kick to those exposed ribs. A fireman's carry take over by
Shadiba and another quick tag to her partner who comes off the ropes with a guillotine leg drop. Both Hood Rats stomping away at Mamma before the ref can force Shadiba back outside leaving Shaquanda in control on the inside. Shaquanda allowing Welfare Mamma to get back to her feet before giving her a knee lift to the mid section. A waist lock into a suplex, no it's a drop across the throat over that top rope and another quick tag. Once again the ladies champions taking advantage of the five count to get out of the ring, double teaming Welfare Mamma in their corner, but Mamma's not done yet!
  An elbow from Mamma and a quick right hand stops the Hood Rats. A shoulder roll to her corner and a tag to Whole Lotta Rosie! Rosie to the top rope and it's the dog eat dog on both the Rats as they chase after
Mamma. Referee in position, 1-2-3. Wow! Rosie didn't have a lot to do with that match but I'll tell ya what she did was a Whole Lot to say the least. Your winners, Welfare Mamma and Whole Lotta Rosie.
In our second bout we feature the return of Revelations, Atomic Punk and
Wandering Soul against the SOS's Beast and Riff Raff. Hold on here… I am being informed from the truck that there has been a change…
  Oh this has to be a mistake! Apparently, Atomic Punk will not be here this evening and his replacement will be Alexi Pizsoff, the Crafty
Commie! An unlikely duo to say the least and yes, there they are in the ring. Beast and Riff Raff hit the ring like a runaway train and here we go! Riff Raff and Pizsoff to start things off and there is very little wrestling to speak of here. A right hand across the jaw of Pizsoff and a knee to the belly in reply. A snap mare by Pizsoff and then repeated rights to the top of Riff's head. A tag to Wandering soul but Riff with a low blow stops him dead in his tracks and here comes Pizsoff, but an atomic drop from Riff sends Soul headlong into the Crafty Commie and now both men go down. A tag to the Beast and he's like a house afire. A right to Soul a left to Pizsoff and a double nogg'n knocker has both men on rubber legged street. A quick tag to Riff who charges in but takes a boot to the face from Wandering Soul. Again Riff lunges but a second boot from Soul and a quick tag to Pizsoff. The Crafty Commie with a rake of the eyes and then drags his face across the top rope. Riff is blinded and falls prey to a belly to back suplex. Riff raff is down on the mat face first and Pizsoff is going for a Boston Crab, or is that Kiev? In any case, he's got it locked in while Riff is desperately reaching for the ropes. Satan now down to ring side, yelling encouragement to his charge and now the Beast has joined him at ring side. The referee ordering Beast back to his corner while Satan sneaks up on the apron with a baseball bat. Wow! A shot to the back of the head that would have made Mark Maguire proud! Pizsoff is down and Satan pulls the unconscious
Riff Raff on top of him before exiting the ring. The referee back in position for the count, 1-2-3. One up and one out as the SOS takes home the 'W'. Your winners Riff Raff and the Beast.
Our next bout is for the USA tag team titles between the champs, the
Mississippi Mauler and the Exciter, meeting the challenge of the SWO's
Ring Lord and Waz. A little out of the ordinary as the champions are the first to the ring, entering to the music of Molly Hatchet's "Flirt'n
With Disaster", they await their challengers and.. Oh here they are with an attack from behind!   A no dq barb wire match and we are off and running. Waz tosses the Exciter into the barbed wire and begins working him over while Ring Lord and the Mauler duke it out in the center of the ring. Ring Lord with a handful of hair, drags the Mauler's face across that wire, opening up that heavily scarred forehead. Oh and here comes trouble! Former CWWF champion Napoleon Bonsay has come to ring side and this doesn't bode well for the champions. Back in the ring, Waz and the ring lord set up for a train wreck but the Mauler reverses and the
Exciter recovers with a flying shoulder block the puts Ring Lord down.
Bonsay, outside of the ring seems to be enjoying this, taunting Waz who is distracted long enough for the Mauler to hit him from behind with a double axe handle blow. Exciter with a snap suplex on Ring Lord and the
Mauler has Waz tied up in the ropes with that wire ripping away at his flesh but he tears free only to take a heart punch that sends him to his knees. Exciter goes for the small package but it's a reversal from the
Ring-Lord, 1-2- and a kick out from the Exciter.   Napoleon now turning his taunts to the Ring-Lord, it appears the former champ has a different agenda than the two in the ring. Ring-Lord with a boot to the mid section on the Exciter and a perfectly executed gourd buster from
Ring-Lord. Mauler coming in to save his partner but Ring-Lord with a shoulder block to the knees takes the Mauler down. Oh my! I think that shot did a lot more damage then I originally thought as the Mississippi
Mauler is clutching his knee in the center of the ring. Ring-Lord signals to the crowd it's time for the Texas-cloverleaf as Waz makes his move on the Exciter. Ring-Lord approaching in for the move but a heart punch from the Mauler stops him in his tracks. Ring-Lord down to his knees as the Mauler applies that kneeling abdominal stretch 'the Biloxi
Stretch". This could go as a pin or a submission depending on how long
Ring-Lord can fight the pain. Waz sees his partner in trouble and moves to help, but Bonsay at ring side has hold of his leg! Referee checking in and yes! That's it, your winners and still USA tag team champions, the Mississippi Mauler and the Exciter.
Mauler now calling for the mic," Waz! Ring-Lord! You too Bonsay and all the SWO goons! Take a good long look at this here. This is what they and all the rest of ya'll are gonna get when you start FLIRT'N WITH
DISASTER! Disaster indeed wrestling fans! We move on to another tag team bout for the CWWF titles as 2Xtreme, Dave Deline and Antwan James take on the champions the Matinee Idols, with Gorgous Greg and Ravishing Rob. Waz and Ring-Lord have left the ring but not the ringside area as 2Xtreme makes their way down the aisle. Oh and a sneak attack by Waz and the
Ring-Lord on the unsuspecting 2Xtreme. Deline and James doing battle with the Ring-Lord and Waz while the Matinee Idols make their way to the ring. Waz with a power bomb drops James through a table at ring side and
Ring-Lord with a chair shot to Big Daddy. Oh that may have been a mistake as Deline snatches the chair from Ring-Lord and drives him down like a stake. In the ring, Gordon is demanding the ref sound the bell and start counting 2xtreme out. Waz with a crack back block takes down
Big Daddy but James is up from behind and delivers the Zulu Dawn to Waz on the concrete. James now with the chair and Ring-Lord drags his partner away from ring side as the bell sounds. Deline and James are wondering just what has happened as the Matinee Idols get the win without even removing their ring robes. Your winners by count out, the
Matinee Idols.
Two more title match ups yet to go as we feature next, Little Waz challenging Tiny Terror for the CWWF midget title. Both men are in the ring and the cage is being lowered as the two have already begun. A snap suplex from Waz but Terror quickly to his feet but a leg sweep from Waz puts him down again. An Irish whip from Waz is reversed by Terror and
Waz hits the turn buckle hard. A bronco buster from Terror but Waz rolls free and Oh my God! The cage is still lowering and Tiny Terrors legs have slid beneath it on the ring apron. The arena crew got it stopped before any damage could be done but they are having trouble reversing it. Meanwhile Tiny Terror is trapped there. Waz is showing no mercy, dropping knees and elbows on the defenseless Tiny Terror. There is no dq in a Hell in the Cell match so Waz is within his rights but this is anything but fair!   Waz continues to pummel away at Tiny Terror and
I'm sure he is out cold. Finally the arena crew gets the cell to start rising but it's a mute point now as Waz makes the cover on the unconscious Tiny Terror. A reluctant count by the referee, 1-2-3. With the help of a steel cage and some bad timing, the new CWWF Midget champion, Little Waz!
Our Main event folks! A UWP title defense for champion the Phoenix against the APWA champion S.O.B. and it promises to be a barn burner.
Both men in the ring and the bell sounds. A collar to elbow hook up and
SOB forces Phoenix into the corner and the ref calls for the break, oh and SOB with a knife edge chop on the break. Phoenix, shaking it off back to the center of the ring and once again they tie it up. SOB using his size to force Phoenix into the ropes and the ref calls for the break and it's a carbon copy of the last break with a vicious knife edge chop.
Oh but Phoenix with a handful of hair tosses SOB into the corner and unleashes a lightning fast chop of his own. A hip toss brings SOB out of the corner and a rolling neck breaker by Phoenix and a cover, 1-2 and a kick out from the APWA champ. Both men to their feet and SOB with a fore arm shot to the jaw. Sob to the ropes and a shoulder block puts the
Phoenix down. SOB once again to the ropes, measuring an elbow drop that catches Phoenix right between the eyes. SOB drags Phoenix to his feet and stands him straight up with a European uppercut before heading to the ropes again but Phoenix catches him with a back body drop that puts the APWA champ down hard on his tail bone. SOB slow to get up and
Phoenix wastes no time with an Atomic drop that sends SOB into the turnbuckle. Phoenix to the opposite corner and heads in for the splash but SOB rolls clear and Phoenix takes the turnbuckle in the chest. SOB with a roll up but only a one count as Phoenix kicks out. The APWA champ brings the UWP champ to his feet again and sends him down with a side
Russian leg sweep before diving down for an ankle lock submission.
Phoenix is in obvious pain, battling up to apply a reverse chin lock forcing SOB to break his hold. SOB reaching back as he fights to his feet then drops down for a jaw breaker on Phoenix. A quick clothesline from SOB puts Phoenix down and another cover, 1-2 and Phoenix just barely gets the shoulder up that time. Both men slow to their feet as
SOS strikes first with a head butt to the mid section then a reverse head butt to the chin, Phoenix is staggered. SOB with a front waist lock goes for the belly to belly but Phoenix blocks it. He blocks it again then picks SOB up and delivers a gourd buster in the center of the ring.
Phoenix for the cover, 1-2 and a kick out from SOB. What a match! Phoenix with a back elbow drop but SOB sees it coming and clears out. A roll up from behind by SOB nets him another one count and now he climbs on top and starts raining down lefts and rights. The ref admonishing SOB who stops to tell the ref where to go when Phoenix with an elevator reverses the hold and unleashes some heavy leather of his own. Finally the ref has restored some order with both men back on their feet as they begin to bounce of the ropes, criss crossing the ring and it's a double clothesline and both men go down. The ref begins the count as SOB crawls slowly over to make the cover on Phoenix, 1-2 and a kick out from
Phoenix. Both men appear exhausted in the ring. SOB back to his feet as
Phoenix is a bit slower and it's SOB with a reverse waist lock into a side body slam and again a cover, 1-2 and once again Phoenix has enough to lick out, but how much longer can he go on? SOB is up and waiting on
Phoenix going for the clothesline but a duck under from Phoenix. SOB off the ropes and a boot from Phoenix but SOB catches the leg. Oh and an
Insuguri kick from Phoenix sends SOB end over end. Phoenix to the top rope, oh he's taking way too much time! SOB down in the center of the ring as Phoenix takes flight with a summersault guillotine leg drop.
That one took as much out of Phoenix as it did SOB but Phoenix gets the cover, 1-2-3. Phoenix wins! What an incredible match up between these two champions.
And what an incredible evening of CWWF wrestling. We hope you've enjoyed this weeks show and hope to see you again next week for more Wednesday
Night Wars. Until then, for all of us at the CWWF this is Doug DeVore saying, see you next time.
