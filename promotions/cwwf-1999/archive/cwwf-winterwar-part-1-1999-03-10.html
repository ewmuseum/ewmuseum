I speak of course about the UWP World-held Grand Unified Championship. Ok now on to our first match for the first <li>CWWF Hardcore Championship lots were drawn and the two combattants will be The Rebel and Big Daddy Dave Deline! The theme music for The Matinee Idols plays and Georgeus Greg Gordon walks out and towering over him at 7ft3 ,a Big
Daddy Dave Deline is in tow. Fans greet thier arrival with a hail of boos and flung popcorn!
Big Daddy grabs the mic and steps over the top ropes into the ring. "Hahahahah aha The Big Man is here hide your women and hold back your daughters !I am more man than any of you little wimpy 9to5'ers Hahahahah
As my little buddy Troy Martin brought the World Cruiserweight belt to us Matinee Idols I am gonna pop that little redneck like a bad zit and bring The Hardcore belt home where it belongs!Nobody can stop Big Daddy
NOoooBody!"
The fans boo and jeer drowning out the last part of Big Daddy Deline's proclaimation. The boos turn to cheers as The Rebel streaks to the ring ,bypasses the metal detector device as his concealed objects set off the alarms,and hits the ring .
Big Daddy's back is to him as Rebel does a flyng dropkick to Big daddy's knees. Big Daddy turns around ,angry looking, as the Rebel attempts a nother flyig dropkick. Big Daddy catches him and powerslams the Rebel. The
Rebel rolls out of the ring as Big Daddy tried to drop an elbow on him.
The Rebel grabs a chair ,he whacks Big daddy hard 5 times and the fifth time Big Daddy rasised his size 17' boot to the chair ,Rebel goes flying back.
Big daddy picks up the dazed Rebel and takes him out side the ring . Big
Daddy powerslams him on the concrete! Now the Rebel grabs 2 table legs and drives them hard into Big daddy's mid section.
The Rebel chokes Big Daddy with them. Big Daddy stands up with the Rebel on his back choking him. WOW Big
Daddy's face is turning red and blue!! Big Daddy shakes but the Rebel holds on. Big Daddy with wobbly knees falls back against the side of the ring squashing the Rebel. Big Daddy shakes away the stars of oxygen deprivation ,picks up the Rebel ,lifts him high in the air and KA_POW!
The Rebel whacked his head with a pair of brass knux! The Rebel is outweighed by about 200pounds and is about 2 and a half feet shorter but he is like a Swiss watch !He takes a lickin' and comes back tickin'!
Big Daddy drops him and falls back hard on the concrete. The Rebel lands on Big Daddy's chest and opens up his head with a series of hard ,fast and continous brass knux poundings! The Rebel grabs a folding chair ,climbs up the ring to the top turnbuckles and,OH! Big Daddy rolled out of the way !!The Rebel had the chair upside down and was going to do a bombs away Ray Stevens style driving the chair into Big daddy's throat!
The Rebel is stunned as he hits nothing but concrete and Big Daddy scoops him up and gorilla presses the Rebel through the Popcorn Shop's plate glass window!
Oh my Lord the Rebel is bleedeing from his head,shoulder ,arms and hands from the broken glass!!!
A big piece is sticking out of his right forearm! The bleedng Rebel staggers up,pulls out that huge piece of glass and lunges at Big daddy with that piece of bloody glass! Big Daddy blocks it and his arm is lacerated in the process! Big Daddy ,I don't believe it !!Big Daddy surprized The Rebel with a flyig dropkick and the Rebel is sent through a plywood partition head first hitting a brick wall!
Big Daddy goes for the pin and pulls Rebel's tights for good measure! 1---2---3!!! The winner and NEW CWWF Hardcore Champion BIG Daddy Deline!!! Big Daddy takes the belt and Gorgeous Greg,The Franchise and Ravishing Rob Wade douse him in champaign .They hoop and hollar all the way back to the dressing room.As the fans boo and jeer. The bleeding and tired Rebel climbs to the ring and grabs the mic away from the announcer.
"Hey Deline ,boy if y'all think this is over bet again you done started a war !The fans cheer .
Son next time we meet all your F#$%^ bets are out the door!The fans cheer again. Y'all probably reckon on a nice long title reign,huh?Well that dog don't hunt boy! That Hardcore title's a gonna be mine! If y'all reckon I'm scared of a little bleedin' y'all don't know the
Rebel! The fans cheer a little louder. I'm American by Birth and Southern by the Grace of God ! At that the fans nearly raise the roof.
The Rebel limps back to the lockers one of the young female fans runs out gives him a tall mug of beer and helps him back to the dressing room.
The Rebel leaves the Stadium arm in arm with the beautifull young lady and a big smile on his blood stained face.

The cage is wrapped around the floor,half way up and on the top by razor wire! The first to climb out of this cage and both feet hit the floor will be the CWWF World Champion! Allready in the Cage is the current CWWF World
Chapion,Billy B.Badd.
Fans boo and jeer as this once popular CWWF superstar turned rulebreaker straddles the top of the cage and plays an off key rockabilly tune.
Terry Funk ,his manager ,dispite the jeering and boos ,claps and smiles at BBB like he enjoys it.
CWWF officials open the cage and the boos get much louder as Stalin from the SWO leads The Crafty Commie to it.
Stalin snatches away a seat out from an elderly fan and watches from ringside. The Crafty Commie looks alot like the fat Gistapo General on the old
60's comedy tv show Hogan's Heroes only considerably taller with muscular arms.
Crafty Commie busts a hidden Vodka bottle over BBB's head. BBB falls back and CC clotheslines him into the cage. CC drags BBB's face hard along the razor wire. BBB shakes his head,flinging blood from side to side and kicks CC in the nads!
Terry Funk starts to argue with Stalin as BBB quickly and consecutively ddts,then piledrives,then gordbusts,then powrslams CC! BBB shoots like rocket up to the top of the cage. He grabs the guitar from the corner and leaps off back into the cage busting CC in the head with it.
BBB climbs to the top again as Terry Funk and Stalin brawl. BBB leaps off the cage dropping a double axe handle on Stalin from 15 feet up!
The winner and STILL CWWF Champion of the World Billy B. Badd! <P>_------------------------------------------- <li>Ok fans The APWA's first World TV Champion "C" will take on the

He stands 6foot8 and is 295Lb a big muscular brute of a man and quiter dangerous I've been told.

ER.
Napolean is 7foot3 and weighs 255Lb,the tall and wirey frenchman is a master of power moves.
