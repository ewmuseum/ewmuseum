Roleplaying The UWF is Strictly Elite Roleplayers. Matches Will Be Judged 50% Quality and
50% Length. But please realise that Quality will always have the edge on
Length. For Example we do not want you to come out with 200 lines of pure cussing or 200 lines of pure " Im gonna beat you up. Oh Hey You know next card im gonna beat your but. " Grammer Is Very Needed. If you do not have good grammer it will be counted against you.
To Be qualified as a member of a UWF you must rp for every match you have. After the second time of a failure of Roleplaying Your UWF Acount Will Be
Terminated Without Notice. You Must Also Roleplay Within Your Character as we do not want any of the following :
DeathNight : And Thats The Bottom line Cause DeathNight Said so !! And cause
DeathNight 3:16 said so. No We Do Not Want Any of that garbage. We Want originality in our roleplay fed and Originality Only.
Each Roleplay Must Have UWF somewhere in the subject Line or it will simply be deleted and not counted.Each and every roleplay must be 40 Lines in 8 print no acceptions. Try To Keep Macros Down...We dont want macros all over roleplays as one...it takes way too long for us to open and two it just adds length to your roleplay. Disclamiers are needed for cussing. If you cuss and are tossed without a disclaimer then tough tit's because we will do nothing. But if anyone is tossed and is a current member of the UWF. The person who tossed him will be fired imeddiatley from his UWF Acount. When Rping Never Talk about rping in your matches. If you do it will count against you and not for you. What is the point of roleplaying when you talk about it ? Nothing at all so do not talk about roleplaying in your rps.
As Many Feds Do not do we will carefully read each and every roleplay to assure you that your hard work is being read. We will not open it say " Oh nice rp "
and then close it. No we will read it. How in the hell do you think quality is judged if a rp isnt read ?
When Rping is done Keep Racial Slurs completley out of it. If any racial slurs are made your UWF acount wil imeddiatley be terminated. Keep Cussing to a minimum.
