MPW Rulebook and Application

MIDGET PRO WRESLTING THE BIGGEST "LITTLE" FED ON THE NET Tired of the same old, same old in Fantasy Wreslting? Sick of seeing TEN THOUSAND messages on the RSPWF, broadcasting a "new" extreme fed, when in the end they all just turn out to be the same? Well, the MPW hopes to change all that, and SO much more! WHAT IS THE MPW?!? Simple, the MPW was started by fantasy wrestling veteran [name withheld] in hopes to add a little MORE fun to the e-wrestling experience and give handlers something DIFFERENT from the every day grind. And MPW IS different, to say the least! The premise behind the MPW is to create a NEW and ORIGINAL fed and it is just that. It's set up like so many other countless feds out there, with one "LITTLE" difference, the MPW is for MIDGET wrestlers only! HOWEVER, I would like to stress that this IS NOT a parody fed. Or some sort of joke. This is for ORIGINAL wrestlers, and for serious roleplayers. Our hope is to bring you the action of the regular wrestling just in a new way. And you may be thinking that it'll never work, but that's probably what they said about the first E-Fed, and it's probably what they said about the first Women's e-fed! So why should this be any different, but with the help of great, and dedicated roleplayers, and some friends that might pitch in! We could just make this work!!! I would also like to take this time to clear up some confusion that's bound to come up. NO YOU DO NOT HAVE TO ACTUALLY BE A MIDGET TO ENTER INTO THE FED!!!!!! :-) And if your wrestler has a manager, he doesn't have to be one either if you don't want. The only MUST in this fed is that ALL wreslters have to be MALE midgets. And now...the following is a rulebook on MPW! Or Midget Pro as I like to call it! Please read up and send in the application attached at the bottom if you are interested. And if you have ANY questions. E-mail them to [detail withheld] Thanks and once again I stress!!! THIS IS NOT A PARODY FED!!!! THIS IS AS SERIOUS AS THE FIRST E-FED WAS, AND AS THE FIRST WOMEN'S E-FED WAS!!!! I hope to move past the "comedy" aspect that midget wrestling has today, and get some great things going! And of course, HAVE FUN!!! That's the whole idea behind it!! :-) MPW RULEBOOK MPW RULES AND REGULATIONS Midget Pro is a booked fed, where matches are decided by Flashes and strats. Flashes are broadcast once a week on MPW All-Stars, and strats will be sent to me, a week before your match takes place! For those not familiar with e-wrestling, flashes are interview type roleplays sent in with your wrestler! They are to be done IN character!! And should feature your wrestler with manager if he has one and any other people affiliated with him! You have the choice of using an interviewer or not! Flashes aren't that hard and if you've never seen one, let me know and I'll send you a sample to use as a reference. Strats, are detailed descriptions of what your wrestler will do in his match. Their name is short for strategy and they are just that! A strategy of what your wreslter will do in the ring. They can be as long and detailed as you like! But remember, the more detail the better chance you'll have of winning. As with flashes if you've never seen or done one, let me know and I'll send you a sample! Also, in your flashes there should be NO heavy swearing. And if it is impossible to avoid please edit it out! (i.e. $#it, or @$$, or even f**k) Another key rule is your wrestler HAS to be original! No Mini Vader's in here folks! I'm sorry! However I have decided to accept SOME mini versions of prominent E-WRESTLERS!! (i.e. Mini Robski, or Little Quickstrike or Mini Starfox) BUT, the handlers of the BIGGER versions of these little wrestlers have to give the okay! (i.e. the handler of "The Starfox" Curt Crane would have to tell me that it was alright for someone else to turn in a Mini Starfox) I'm not letting someone turn in a version of someone elses creation, and have them get pissed off at the fed! And I will only accept a few of these MINI wrestlers. Like 1 for every 4-5 original wrestlers I get. I wouldn't impliment this rule but I want to keep this from becoming the home of the Little versions of every e-wrestler! Since this is a VERY ORIGINAL idea, I'd like to have some ORIGINAL creations as well! :-) I think that's about it for the rules! I DO however want to say this! It's important to stay active and flash a lot. I know that everyone doesn't have the pleasure of being on-line ALL the time. We ALL have personal lives! So I'm not making anyone flash every week, but the MORE active you are, the better you'll do! Thanks! MPW CARDS AND EVENTS WEEKLY CARDS: MPW Prime Time: This is the FEATURE show of the week. It is held each Saturday and has anywhere from 3-5 matches! Has 3 commentators and interviews and things! MPW ALL STARS: The weekly flash show! Will be out on Wednsday Nights. 2 hosts and features interviews, flashes, indepth rumors, and other little tidbits. MPW IN THE HOUSE: Monthly House Show report! Listed results of matches! And list of MPW tour dates and sites. MPW PPVS!!! Midget Pro will have Pay Per Views from time to time and here is the tentative schedule of those as of right now. This IS subject to change at ANY time. MPW SLAMFEST: January. Not your ordinary event in your not so ordinary e-fed. This is an event reminicent of the Japanese style. It will take place on one weekend in January. Beginning on Friday Night and ending the following Sunday it will house not only matches from Midget Pro but also feature matches booked by other feds. MPW FUN IN THE SUN: Spring Break. Will be held outside on a yet to be named beach. MPW MAY DAY: May. A regular card, with yet to be determined number of matches. Regular PPV. MPW STARS AND STRIPES: July 4th. An Independence Day Extravaganza where the guys and girls of Midget Pro pay homage to the RED, WHITE, AND BLUE!! MPW FRIGHT NIGHT: Halloween. A Halloween blow out, where SPECIALTY matches are the main attraction. For each match a stipulation will be drawn from a pot and that stip will be implemented into the match. MPW WINTER WAR: December. This card is for a yet to be determined number of wrestlers. And it's premise is as follows. The wrestlers involved will be paired into randomly drawn "WILD" card teams! These teams will have to try to work together to compete through so many rounds of mayhem. Then after they've made it through the "WILD"card tag tourney, they must face each other in a match with a special stip to be named THAT night! MPW PERSONALITIES COMMENTATORS Lil' Lucky: Former Mexican Midget Heavyweight Champion. Is a FACE commentaror on Prime Time. Joe Williams: Unbiased commentator on Prime Time. Tends to lean toward the faces though. Kinda like Jim Ross. "SEXY" Sally McClane: Women's wrestler in the GDWA, and manager in CCW. HEEL commentator for Prime Time. HOSTS AND INTERVIEWERS: Sam Malone: FACE host for MPW All Stars. Also conducts interviews. Randall "Big Dawg" Hawkins: Wealthy stock holder for the fed. HEEL host for MVP All Stars. Not a very likable guy! Soni McCraig: Interviewer who is mostly face but usually on the side of whoever he's interviewing to save his skin. :-) SECURTIY PERSONNEL Mohammed Stone and Trey Hawkes: Served as body guard for Sally McClane in the GDWA and CCW. Came on board as Chiefs of Security here. Not bad guys, just do what they're told NO MATTER what it is. FRONT OFFICE JOBS MPW Executive "Figurehead" President: [name withheld] MPW Executive Vice President: (yet to be announced) MPW TITLES Midget Pro will have three titles. 2 singles and one tag the singles are as follows. MPW World Heavyweight Champion: Holder of this title is automatically named the number one ranked in the league. The holder of this belt is considered to be the best of the best, in singles competition. Must be defended once a month against anyone in the TOP 5!!! And against anyone else if the champion so wishes or the Championship Commitee demands. MPW All-American Champion: The holder of this title is ranked #1. Beneath the World Champ but above everyone else. This title must be defended once a month by ANYone in the TOP 10!! And against anyone else if the champ wishes, or if the Championship Comittee demands. And the tag belts are... MPW World Tag Team Champion: This is the same honor bestowed to a tag team that the MPW World Heavyweight title is to a singles wrestler. This places that team above everyone else in the tag team division. The belts must be defended once a month by anyone in the tag team division TOP 5!!! And against anyone else if the champ wishes, or the Championship Comittee demands. MPW CLOSING WORDS!!! That's about it I think! I'd like to take this time to say a few more words. First off I'd like to thank the CCW, the GDWA, and the NIFW and their respective fed heads and associates. I got a LOT of influence from them, and I hope to run the fed in the same GREAT fashion that they have for so long. Or in the NIFW's case as it hopes to in the future. ALSO, I'd like to remind everyone, that this IS just a game! Please don't take anything that's said by anyone in flashes too personally! Trust me I've had problems where both of us got personal and in the end it doesn't work out good! JUST TRUST ME!!! It makes everyone in the fed uncomfortable and it takes the fun from fantasy wrestling. And keep in mind. Just because this isn't a parody fed doesn't mean you can't have fun!! That's the point in this. You see, there are so many handlers out there that have been roleplaying for the same guy for years or if not the SAME, someone similar, well wiht the MPW, they get something COMPLETELY new! I'd like to see the same action as any regular fed, with the same heat and angles, just with midgets. And GUYS, if we pull this off we'll be making history as the FIRST Midget E-fed! (to my knowledge :-) But I guess what I'm trying to say is HAVE AS MUCH FUN AS YOU CAN!!!!!! And if you don't get titles right away, no sweat, that just means it's going to be all the sweeter when you do get them. I know from experience! So let's make the MPW the newest, most original, and most of all FUNNEST e-federation around! And thanks to EVERYone that's signed up!
