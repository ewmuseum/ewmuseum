IWF Rules & Regulations

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
IWF Rules & Regulations You will wrestle at about once a week, barring injuries. You must post at least one 'flash' (interview) per card that you are scheduled to wrestle. Failure to do so will result in termination (after numerous warnings) . Remember, this federation is based on roleplaying. If you do not roleplay for a match, you will get jobbed, period . If both competitors do not roleplay, the executive committee may do whatever they choose with the outcome. If you can't roleplay for a certain match (or matches), and have a legitimate excuse (i.e. vacation, college exams, etc.), email one of us (addresses below) and we will work something out with you. The wrestlers that roleplay more will get a greater push (Card appearances, title shots, and such) than those who do not. Cursing in the guestbook is permitted, in a limited way. Basically, if you hear it on TV, (ass, hell, damn, etc) then it's okay. If you have a question about what words can/can't be used, please get in contact with me. If you want to use one of the so-called 'bad' words...just censor out the middle of the word, like s**t, or whatever. (like a censor on a TV show does). If you do curse in the guestbook, the following penalties apply: First Offense: Warning. Second Offense: One week suspension. Final Offense: President's Discretion (possible termination). Racial slurs directed at other wrestlers are possible grounds for immediate dismissal. Challenges -- either make the challenges in your flashes, or over email (send me the challenge) No IWF wrestler shall hold more than two titles at a time. Maximum Height for your wrestler: 7'4". Maximum Weight: 500 lbs. In special cases, (Yokozuna or the Giant, for instance) the limits can be waived. You are allowed up to 4 wrestlers. Although we PREFER original wrestlers, we may possibly allow you to use a real wrestler. Check with us first and we'll decide if it is permissible. Check the roster for taken wrestlers. If you are a constant roleplayer, you may be allowed a 5th wrestler. Please e-mail the Executive Committee for 5th wrestler requests. Managers are optional. (Check the roster for taken managers) This federation is a roleplaying federation. In other words, if you roleplay well, you win. If not, you lose. (Barring very rare circumstances where a match has to have a set outcome to start/further a feud. If this is the case, you will be notified in advance.) The standard deadline for roleplaying for Wednesday Wartime  is 11:59 pm CST (Central Standard Time), the Night prior to the  event. For Sunday Massacre, the deadline is 11:59 PM (Central Standard Time). If this deadline is extended or shortened, you will know in advance. Interference during a match is allowed, (to an extent -- run-ins, etc) providing there is a good reason for doing so. (feud-related, etc.) E-mail me and tell me if you want to interfere. Impersonation of another wrestler in the guestbook, unless I (The President) have the other person's express permission for you to do so, and am notified before time, is forbidden, and grounds for termination of all of the wrestlers you control. Attacking somebody in the guestbook is NOT permitted, unless you have cleared it with the other wrestler first, and I receive word from that wrestler. If an attack does take place without my receiving an e-mail, you will be terminated. For flashes, you are required to submit your email address. If you do not, then the flash will not count toward your wrestler's match. The guestbook is meant STRICTLY for roleplays. No material promoting another e-federation shall be posted. Save it for personal e-mails. On the same token, the guestbook is not a ground to personally bash the Executives or any other member of the IWF. Save it for the personal e-mail or ICQ. At no time is breaking "Kayfabe" allowed. For those of you who don't know what "Kayfabe" is, a brief definition follows. Kayfabe is the 'line' drawn between 'fake' and 'real' in wrestling... for example, take the infamous WWF 'Madison Square Garden' incident, right before Scott Hall and Kevin Nash left. In the WWF, Shawn Michaels and Kevin Nash were feuding, and Michaels had just gotten done beating Nash. After the match, Triple-H (a 'bad guy') and Scott Hall (a 'good guy') came out, and they all celebrated in the ring. This was a break in kayfabe... breaking the 'illusion' that the bookers promote. An attack on a wrestler's gimmick, like claiming that Kane is really Glenn Jakobs, not the Undertaker's brother, is not acceptable, and any posts like that will be deleted immediately. Breaking kayfabe will also incur the following penalty: ALL of your wrestlers will be removed from the IWF, no if's, and's, or but's. A wrestler's gimmick is what makes him/her a success, and without it, the results could be disasterous. Please check back to this page from time to time, as it could be updated and/or changed. All member will be notified via e-mail about changes/additions, regardless. Remember... This is the IWF. It's all in fun. Don't wreck it for others, OK?
