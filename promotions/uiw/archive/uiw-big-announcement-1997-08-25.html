UIW BIG ANNOUNCEMENT

25 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ULTIMATE INTERNET WRESTLING EMERGENCY ANNOUNCEMENT... We have recieved a huge number of applicants to UIW. We have some ver important announcements to follow up today's Press Conference. - UIW will be opening a chat room and a homepage within a week - UIW is still allowing members to send in wrestlers, but only those that are on this mailing list as of NOW will be able to have their wrestler in the title tournaments - The tag tournament is taking shape - The UIW announcing team has been anounced, as follows: Play By Play -- [name withheld] Play By Play -- [name withheld] Factual Color -- [name withheld] Color -- [name withheld] Interviews -- [name withheld] Their duties should be announced on September 1st at the huge dbut show. At the show, you should see all the matches, the duties as mentioned above, an official roster of 'The U', along with personalities and interviews, and the tag team situation. That will be a huge show. The first Countdown Weekly show will be the following Monday, and will have Thursday THUNDER matches, Saturday Night Showdown matches, plus some VERY interesting news about MegaEvents and IRC. Please look for both of them on RSPWF, along with UIW Championship Weekend, coming out September 5, 6 and 7, in 1 hour installments. They should be very interesting. We will see you then. This just in, after the above was written: Many UIW personalities have taken on wrestling names that have upset some other federations. The following UIW wrestlers will be forced to change their name by this Friday or will be fired: Big Daddy Cool Ticking Time Bomb Smash Warrior They will all be e-mailed personally concerning the matter. More announcements will follow. If you want to join this league, you will not be in the tournaments, but your wrestler will debut a week after on our first newsgroup programs. [name withheld] UIW President
