Ultimate Internet Wrestling Application

12 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
***************************** ULTIMATE INTERNET WRESTLING ***************************** IF YOU WANT TO BE INVOLVED IN AN E-WRESTLING LEAGUE THAT TRULY IS GOING TO BE GREAT, READ THE FOLLOWING.... UIW, Ultimate Internet Wrestling will be the most competitive, most interactive and most fun league to participate in on the internet EVER!!!! This league offers something to everybody. If you enjoy wrestling, or just enjoy role-playing, or BOTH, this league is for you. Please read the following before filling out an application, which is located below. ***************************** THE LEAGUE ***************************** NOTE: All are possible to change as league grows larger. TITLES: Two (2) singles, one (1) tag team EVENTS: One (1) megacard [ppv] bi-monthly, two (2) weekly, and possibly some house shows. BASED IN: North America The league will have a lot of events in the US and Canada to open up. As house shows start, the league will go overseas to Europe and Asia. ***************************** WRESTLERS ***************************** NOTE: All are possible to change as league grows larger. MAXIMUM IN LEAGUE: Thirty (30) singles wrestlers, ten (10) tag teams MAXIMUM PER HANDLER: One (1) singles wrestler and (1) tag team MINIMUM IN LEAGUE: Twenty - Four (24) MINIMUM PER HANDLER: One (1) singles wrestler or one (1) tag team (all executives are allowed 2 singles and a tag team) The first twelve applicants to send in an application with a tag team will get a team while the rest will be placed on the waiting list. As you participation in the league grows, you will be allowed more wrestlers, but if you show you can't handle that many, you will lose one. ***************************** ROLE-PLAYING ***************************** In UIW, role-playing has nothing to do with who wins matches. As you read above, you can only enter ONE wrestler. Role-playing can easily help you get another. If you have good role-playing, just like in a real fed, you may even get a push! If we like your role-plays, and your participation, you will be granted an extra manager. There is even talk of a role-playing tournament, where the best role-player wins a huge push!!! More details when you sign-up. ***************************** THE APPLICATION ***************************** Ultimate Internet Wrestling is going to be THE best Internet Wrestling federation out there. I strongly believe that the only way to do this is to put time and dedication itno your federation. That's why UIW has assumed a copy of TNM6.2 and will be using it to decide matches. No simulator can come even close to what TNM can do, and with the release of TNM 7.0 in the near future, you better believe that TNM will more than adequately provide UIW with it's needs. Please fill out this application carefully, as UIW wants to be such a great fed that we want good gimmicks and good wrestler ideas. You can only enter ONE original wrestler, so make it good! *ABOUT YOU* Here is some information about yourself. This has nothing to do with the wrestler, only YOU the handler. Name: E-mail Address: Hometown: Some of your favorite arenas from all over the world: Would you like to handle a tag team also?: *WRESTLER STATS* Name: Weight: Height: Face/Heel: *WRESTLER ATTRIBUTES* Select ONE of the following. NOTE: If you want your wrestler ULTRA customized, please skip this, and at after the wrestler types, put 30 moves you want your wrestler to perform. 1. Amateur Wrestler 2. Top-Notch Brawler 3. All-Rounder 4. Stiff Martial Artist/Grappler 5. Basic High Flyer 6. Basic Brawler 7. Luchador 8. Submission Wrestler 9. High Flyer/Good Technician 10. US Standard Worker 11. Brawler/Aerially Skilled 12. Technically Skilled Brawler 13. Technician/Brawling 14. Daredevil Flyer If you want ULTRA customizing, put your moves here: We are giving you 100 points to start with. Please divide them up into: Worker: Stamina: *MANAGER* If your wrestler has a manager, please fill this out. If not, just put 'no manager' for manager's name. Manager Name (of course, if any): Manager Characteristics: Does he interfere: *THEME MUSIC* UIW is the only e-fed that is going to attempt to add music to their cards. Please select the music you would like your wrestler to have, and if possible, send a 30 second demo of it to use. If you are unable to send us music, we will try and get the song. SONG NAME: ARTIST: You may send a song YOU have created or any song you want. Please cut down on songs with massive profanity. ***************************** PUSH POINTS ***************************** UIW has a very unique system of pushes. Every wrestler will start with 20 points. Title match points are in brackets. WIN by pin/sub -- gain 3 points (5 points) LOSE by pin/sub -- lose 2 points (4 points) WIN by dq -- gain 2 points (4 points) LOSE by dq -- lose 3 points (5 points) DRAW -- both receive 2 points (4 points) *extra point not rewarded goes to winner of rematch The top ten wrestlers are in the World title runnings, while the next ten are in the North American title runnings. ***************************** THE START ***************************** September 1 -- "This is...UIW!" Intro Show debuting the wrestlers *Championship Weekend* September 5 -- UIW Preliminary Round First Half of Matches September 6 -- UIW Preliminary Round Second Half of Matches September 7 -- UIW Championship Battle Royal (winners for World title/ loser for North American title) Once you join you should receive a package concerning UIW. Read it carefully, because it reveals how to be a successful wrestler in UIW! Thank-you, and the revolution of e-wrestling is finally upon us!
