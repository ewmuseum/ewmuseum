ULTIMATE INTERNET WRESTLING SPECIAL REPORT After a huge month of August for UIW, the Championship Weekend did not go on as planned. September is going to be a "re-building and re-set to go" month, as October 1st, Wednesday Night, UIW presents UIW Championship Coronation. The line-up is as follows: October 1 -- UIW Championship Coronation -- Madison Square Garden, New York City WORLD TITLE: 30 Man Battle Royal WORLD TAG TITLE: First Round Matches This event will last an hour and a half (750 lines). UIW will present Thursday THUNDER debut on October 2, which will also eminate from the Garden. Promised for that show is a World Champion interview, as well as tag title matches and North American title tournament matches. There have been many falling outs in UIW. 5 wrestlers from the original roster have quit/left without notice. By October 1st, here are some amazing things UIW will bring, as best they can be done: The Official UIW Homepage The Official UIW Chat Room 30 of the Best E-Wrestlers in E-Wrestling Many Special Effects/ Features If you would like to join UIW, please e-mail [name withheld] at: [detail withheld] and ask for an application. [name withheld] UIW President
