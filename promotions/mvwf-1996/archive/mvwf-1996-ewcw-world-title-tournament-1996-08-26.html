EWCW World Title Tournament Results

26 August 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As many of you well know, the EWCW World Title Tournament was heald last night, and the results are in.
The night begin with Steven Grimm defeating Diablo after 2:07, with an easy pinfall. A surprising result considering neither Steven or Shawn
Grimm, better known as the tag-team Brothers Grimm of the Suicide Squad, had ever wrestled in singles matches before tonight. Steven Grimm advanced.
The second match of the first round had the second of the two brothers,
Shawn Grimm, facing off against Sky Flyer. S.F. was disqualified after
16:42, allowing the second Grimm to advance, but creating an odd situation, as the two tag partners and twin brothers would be forced to meet in the second round.
It the third match of the first round, Cole 'The Grim Reaper' Grimm, older brother of the already advancing Brothers Grimm, took on The
Overtaker. It was a brutal match which ended in an Overtaker DQ at 11:58. So a third member of the Suicide Squad advanced. When the fourth match rolled around, it only took for the crowd to hear the opening bars of 'Painkiller' by Judas Priest before the chant of
'ZEROBURN! ZEROBURN! ZEROBURN!' began. The big man, led to the ring by the beautiful Tracy, took on Juan Guerrero. As soon as the Zeroburner, the vicious face-first choke slam, was executed at 13:21, this one was one for the books. Zeroburn advanced.
In the fifth match of the first round, Executioner won by disqualification over The Malkavian at 15:54, when Dr.No, the manager of the Suicide Squad, threw The Malk's Lecter Mask into the ring and the Malk attempted to bash the Executioner with it. Thus, the first elimination of an SS stable member, and the advancement of Executioner.
In the final match of the first round, the seven foot tall Nightside
Warrior was up against his own teamate in the Suicide Squad, the masked
Thunderstrike. It was an action packed match, with the Warrior's streetfighting style slowly overpowering Thunderstrike's Japaneese acrobatics. Nightside Warrior advances after a pinfall at 7:45
The first match of the second round was a brutal match. Becaust the twins, Shawn & Steven, are esentally the same build, wear the same black ring attire, and both were the skull facepaint, it was nearly impossible to tell the two apart at times during this match. At 16:54, Steven got his brother in position atop the 3rd turnbuckle, and was able to execute the Grimm Doom Drop, a belly-to-belly superplax, which we have usually seen in the past as a precurser to the Grimm Doom Blade, Shawn's flying legdrop from the opposing turnbuckle. Not tonight. Steven Grimm advances to the Semi-Finals.
Unbeliveable, is the only way to describe the results of the second match of the Quarter-Finals. 33 seconds into the match, to the roar of the crowd, Zeroburn hit The Grim Reaper with the Zeroburner. As of yet, no one has gotten up from this menuver, and tonight was no exception.
Zeroburn will face Steven Grimm in the Semi-Final match. It had been decided that the winner of the 3rd & final match of the Quarter-Finals would get a one way ticket to the Final round to battle for the EWCW World Title. It was obvious that any match between the
Executioner & The Warrior wold be a drag out affair. But at 11:20, after applying the Guillotine, Executioner got the win and advanced to the Final match.
Well, the crowd knew that the winner of the next match would go to meet the Executioner for the title. There could be no doubt who the fans were pulling for. The crowd seemed almost surprised when the match wasn't over after 5:00, but it didn't take much longer than that. At 9:01, Zeroburn hit with the Zeroburner, and the final match was set.
The Final was a brutal affair. Both Zeroburn and Executioner pulled out all the stops in a desperate battle to become EWCW's first World Title holder. Back and forth the match swayed, until, at 14:47, Zeroburn hit withthat vicious choke slam, winning the title.
Ladies & Gentlmen, your NEW EWCW WORLD CHAMPION: Zeroburn!!!!!!!!! Injury Report: Executioner---l. bicep---6 cards. Brought to you by ~K~ & DR.No, Leaders of the Suicide Squad, and Corey Collins, EWCW President. ALL THOSE WHO WISH TO JOIN SHOULD E-MAIL COREY AT [detail withheld]
