Really Awesome Wrestling Info/Application I. Introduction- Hello RPer, my name is [name withheld], some of you may know me as Jax Jordan and I am the president of RAW..Really Awesome Wrestling. If you are receiving this, that means you are in some way interested in E-Wrestling. I would like to offer you the chance to join one of the newest and best that the e-wrestling world has to offer....RAW Really Awesome Wrestling will be a "Closed" fed meaning you send your roleplays to me and only me and I send them out in daily or bi-daily RP packets. This federation is also "booked" meaning I and my staff judge who wins matches by roleplays, strategy, and angles. By joining you are indeed joining one of the most fun, orignal, and action packed feds around. II. Roleplaying, Stratagies, Angles- A. Roleplaying: This is what is the most important factor when deciding who wins and who loses. YOU NEED TO ROLEPLAY HERE IN THE RAW. I judge roleplays by the quality of it. I want you to be as descriptive as possible when writing you roleplays. Setting, Grammar, Creativity, Language, and all sorts of other stuff come into effect when deciding the victor. B. Stratagies: If a roleplaying battle is so close that it must be decided by another matter, these will be it. They're optional for your match-ups, but I suggest sending one in, just in case. Strategies are something you send to me, and me only, and they will say what you want to do during a match-up. For example, if you want your wrestler to go to the limit to win, you would write to me, saying this, only in a little more detail. These don't have to be near as good as your roleplays, but remember, they could decide your match. And hell, they make things more interesting and fun. C: Angles: Here is another thing that is not manditory, but it makes thing more interesting and it helps you in your matches. Again, it is not as important as roleplaying, but it is important that youtry to work angles with me and my staff. If you want a specific angle to take place, just email me and me only, and I will try to make it go down, I can't guarantee it always will, but if you RP, it will most likely go down, 9 out of 10 angles always go through. III. Misc. Information- A: The RAW WeekEnd Rap, Smack Talking, and Update ": These are weekly newsletters and Roleplay packets that will be coming out. It will have all sorts of neat stuff, including polls, contests, trivia, and current news and rumors going on in the RAW. B: House Shows: We will have one house shows a week which will take place on Sunday Night. This show will be written out in summary form, and usually every wrestler in the RAW is involved in some way. This show will be titled And Justice for Brawl C: Weekly Card: There will be one weekly card and it will be held on a Wednesday it will be called "Wednesday Warfare" It will include interviews, 8-9 matches, and it is the place where all angles and everything builds up towards the monthly Pay-Per-Views. Most of the matches for this show will be written out in a play-by-play method. D: Pay-Per-Views: Pay-Per-Views will be extremely action packed. Many fueds and battles will end here and much more will start. Look for almost every title to be defended at every pay-per-view. Dates and times of these pay-perview will be determined as time goes by. E. Titles: RAW Heavyweight Title: This is the pride and joy of the RAW. Whoever wins this must be top notch, and on top of there game. Must be defended every 30 days. RAW Unitied States Title : This is the 2nd ranked title here in the RAW. Top notch athletes hold this title. RAW TV Title: The hardest title to hold on to as it will be on line line every week on Warfare. RAW Tag Team Titles: This is given to the best "TEAM" in the RAW. Must be defended every 15 days. F.
