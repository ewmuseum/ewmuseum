A newly opened closed-roleplay fed's own November 1998 handbook and application, naming a four-title belt structure ahead of its first announced card. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Really Awesome Wrestling introduced itself on 20 November 1998, calling itself “the newest e-fed to hit the internet” and setting its goal plainly: “not to be one of the biggest just one of the best.” Its own president, wrestling under the name Jax Jordan, ran it as a closed, booked federation judged on roleplay quality, with optional strategies and angles submitted to him alone.

Its own handbook laid out a four-title structure — a Heavyweight Championship defended every 30 days, a United States Championship as the second-ranked prize, a Television Championship on the line every week on its flagship card, Wednesday Warfare, and a Tag Team Championship defended every 15 days — alongside a Sunday house show, And Justice for Brawl. The promotion announced its opening event for 6 December 1998: a sixteen-man tournament to crown its first Heavyweight Champion, billed as “A NIGHT TO REMEMBER.”

No document from that tournament, or from any card after it, survives in this record. Whether the promotion's opening night ever aired isn't known from what remains.
