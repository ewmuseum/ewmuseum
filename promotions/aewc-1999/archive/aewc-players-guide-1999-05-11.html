What are the main rules of AeWC? 12.1 Roleplaying 12.2 Angles 13. Application ------------------------------------------------------------------- 1. What is the AeWC? AeWC simply stands for American e-Wrestling Council, which is one of the many federations that's held and found on the internet. The AeWC is here to provide fulfillment of fun and for wrestling fans to enjoy throughout character of choice. You see the played out angles on television and if you don't like them then you can provide your own guide towards the World title...this is one of the many ways to yourself with enjoyment. The emphasis of AeWC is carried on by the involvement of the wrestlers' handlers. And how much participation is implied by each and every handler; and quite frankly it's up to you as an handler to schedule the matches, to create angles, post match interferences and anything else need be to keep things successful and interesting. You may not have control over who wins the matches but the quality, effort, and dedication put into your flashes determines all of that in the long run. Presided by Carl Grant, AeWC could give good and solid competition towards it's enemies. Not mentioning a confident but worthy group of writers which will lead the AeWC into the next millenium.
AeWC is not alone in this genre of sport and wishes to become the best and with the goal set in mind aiming highly of course with the coherent, creative handlers' it will suceed. As such, the league has evolved over the period of its existence, and so too have evolved particular traits which make the league unique. For example, there is an established group of AeWC employees who each have their own roles and their own personalities in this internet profession. It is essential to become familiar with these figures as quickly as possible. in which is portrayed by the wrestlers' handlers.

Only if you're sure to come up with an original character which whom you are capable of flashing and operate with, and only then may your application be considered into the main stream of being accepted. So that's why the roster will be somewhere in the area of 20 at the minimum and 30 at the most. Also, there will be a waiting list for those of who wants into the AeWC but at the moment it was at it's limit of the roster. Those will be placed there. And there you'll remain until a slot on the roster opens due to an handler leaving or being put out of the league. It is hard to predict how long the wait will be once your character is accepted onto the waiting list. Admission is dependent on a number of factors, including the opening up of a spot on the roster due to another character leaving, and so on. The President does not appreciate constant email from handlers on the waiting list concerning the entry of their wrestlers.

5. What's AeWC Weekly Cards There are two constructive cards to be held in AeWC. One being during the mid-week and the other, a morning show on the weekend.
5.1 Thursday Night Turbulence Thursday Night Turbulence is the main stream, the WCW Nitro of AeWC. This card will be some what of an live format, including flashes, in-rings, and most of all...matches! John Grant, Leslie
Jackson, and Stacy Miller are all commentating on this mega event as they flood the announcers booth both with news breaking news from backstage as well as action. Turbulence will definately live up to it's meaningful name.
5.2 AeWC Backlash Blacklash is an event similar towards the desk commentary from WCW Worldwide, Control Center or whatever you want to call it. This is the smaller of the two cards as this will consist of a mere four matches along with continuous footage from the past week of Turbulence. Also, comments on upcoming matches and news.
AeWC is sometimes live due to certain time restraints, such as; Pay Per Views and Special Events. Flash are permitted on the show Blacklash is also somewhat of an debate campaign with talks of matches to come and on the next cards.

Grant brings to the table respect, organization, and most of all...experience to lead away the team. John is a pretty good looking guy. You can just say he's the JR of the AeWC.

Dean is practically known for amusing comments devoted towards the wrestlers, he only likes who he think's the man around here, which also brings grief in between mostly with himself and Grant. Also being the experienced professional that he is, he knows when to stop and commentate a great match. He's co-host of Turbulence.

Belle, outstandingly beat all odds when he was faced with sickle cell anemia a year and a half back...he had to leave the squared circle to seek treatment and has faught his way into arms of fans, co-workers, and most of all, into the squared circle where he once dreamed of actually becoming a major superstar in the sport of which he loved so much. He pretty much calls it down the line, fair and square!
10. Championships 10.1 World Championship It certainly doesn't take a brain surgeon to figure out that this is the most pretigious title in all titles. Not just any one can claim the World title...not only will you have to flash, but be great at it. This title is considered for the top dogs that'll give it all they have to claim it. The World title represents to everyone around and watching that you're found your home and your World Champion.
10.1 Television Championship A secondary title which was brought in to compete with the
World title. We felt it wasn't right to just have one champion and for right now this is the only secondary title until other wise notified. This title will most likely be defended every two weeks and of course, at Pay Per View, Supercards. This title will only be defended on televised events, (taped also), and/or unless specifically authorized and notified.

World and Television titles! 10.2 World Tag Team Championship The tag division, this is pretty much a specialty. Teams of two combined and with determination and at the top of their game will only be able to capture these "puppies".
This division is especially produced to have emjoyment other then the single competition. In some cases, tag teams can square off in one on one competition but that's only occasionally. Alot of sweat, blood, and tears go into being tag team champs as you strive on your partner as a friendly companion.

Right now there are no Pay Per Views nor Supercards, but occasionally they will follow every 4-6 weeks pending on various situations and etc.
12. What are the main rules of AeWC? 12.1 Flashes In order to win matches, roleplaying is a must, and I do insist on you doing so. Without flashing there is no other way to build heat around your character nor determine a winner of an match up. Flashes are a _must_. If you are unable to flash then please contact me in ample time so
I'll know...and also there is a flashing limits, I think a limit of _three_ flashes per match is enough, it wouldn't be fair if your opponent did lets say 7 flashes while you on the other hand sent 2. It's based on quality not quanity so if you do happen to send the three flashes and your opponent only sent two, it wont be judged on that, I couldn't care how many you sent as long as it has a point to get a cross and good quality as well.
12.2 Angles Without them there would only be a mere bore. Angles play a huge part. Angles provide interesting facts/storylines/ and most important adventure. Angles bring in heat towards your character which makes it better for your character to get over. Don't expect anything drastic but a bit on the abnormal side. Angles are primary the heart and soul of a federation yet to mention a character.
