VCW — Weekly Card

VCW Online #50

8 April 1996

This is the trademark music of ...
V V CCCCC W W W V V C W W W W V V C W W W W V CCCCC W W irtual hampionship restling ===================================================================== Cameras swing wildly around the Globe Sports Theatre as the theme music plays. Signs abound, and fans wave and cheer and act like loons. The camera slowly fades in on Misty Mountainhop and
Big Bill Shakespeare in the booth. MM: Welcome to the Ides of March! We have a wild card for you tonight, with the I-C and Lightheavy Titles on the Line, as well as a bunch of specialty matches. Of course, one of the main events of the evening is the Ballotbowl. Twelve entrants will randomly be paired off, the winners of each tag team match advance and the losers are eliminated. Almost. After the first round, two eliminated wrestlers get a Second Chance! Selected randomly, they are added back to the pool, to bring it up to 8 so that the rest of the rounds work out. Let's get going with the
Ballot bowl! Commentary as usual from Vile Vince Vendetta and Rocky Baroque. The names of the entrants spin around on the video screen then begin to fall into place: Hoss Tumbleweed and Rasputin vs. Skumm and Delicious Dave Sands RB: A poor draw for Hoss and Rasputin, their recent run-ins may make it hard for them to work together.
Krudd and Officer Max vs. Big John Law and Special Dark Ikea Perry VVV: The Precinct are the wild cards tonight, so I'd give the edge to Max and Krudd, although Law and Perry have the experience.
Blind Melon Chitlin and Officer Buddy vs. Warfare and Marcelle Keller VVV: What a ripoff! Pitting two members of BMCE against each other! RB: This one is hard to call. Chitlin is always tough, but Warfare is as fine a wrestler as you can find...before we go to the ring though, let's roll this tape.
[roll tape] (The Posse, Hoss Tumbleweed, Sheriff Big John Law, and Brute, are hard at work building a new doghouse for Spot at the Rattlesnake
Dude Ranch. Actually, Hoss and Big John are hard at work while Judge Roy Bean is keeping Brute busy ferrying supplies over to them and out of trouble. The new doghouse is quite large with an impressive space left for a run and surrounded by a five-foot high chain-link fence.)
Judge: No Brute, just take those posts over to Hoss (turns to camera) Ivan Miller, Mr. Nails, you two gentlemen made a large mistake. Brute is quite upset with your ruining his "managers" last home and only the
Ranger's promise to build Spot an even better home kept him from coming after the two of you with a full head of steam. And you've seen what the results are first hand when Brute...SMASH...As you can also see behind me the Ranger's are keeping their promise to their new amigo.
And they have some promises to make to y'all as well. Break time, boys! (All three men wander over to the water cooler next to the Judge who begins to pass out cold drinks as they address the camera.)
Law: Miller, you have got to be one of the DUMBEST men I have ever had the displeasure of meeting. You piss off three of the toughest men in the VCW and then you enter a match where all three of those men might have a shot at you. Did your Mama drop you on your head when you was a baby or what?
Hoss: I reckon his mammy dressed him in those purty dresses with the frills on 'em too! Miller, Nails when you reduced Deputy Spot's house to splinters, Marshall Brute an' me challenged you cowards to a
Texas Tornado match. You ain't even had the guts to face us in the ring, now I see where you runned to the Battle Bowl instead. Well, we done cornered you now, ain't that right Brute?
Brute: Ivan and Nails BAD men! Law: I wanna apologise again for not being around when those two low-lifes showed up Brute, but I just had to head over to Amarillo when my sister called. Her oldest boy, my nephew, was in a car accident and family duty called.
Hoss: Cain't nobody fault you none fer that Big John, family is family. Although I figger the tanning you gave his hide will pale next to the one Miller is a fixin' to get.
Brute: Brute SMASH BAD MEN!!! Judge: But Miller isn't the only malefactor who is due for a warning boys. Law: That's right, Judge. Precinct, I don't know where you fellers come from, but in the VCW, we already got us some law enforcement, and y'all are looking at it. This ain't L.A., or Indianapolis, or even, as ashamed as
I am to admit a Texas city is in the same class as those other two, Houston.
Leave the nightsticks behind and follow the rules, or you boys will have to answer to the Law.
Hoss: Now I'm the first one to admit that I ain't always been on the right side of the law, but you two donut eatin' Barney Fifes don't know nuthin' about law and order. You two are stickin' your coyote noses into a rattlesnake's nest. If'n you Rent-a-Cop, Mall Security types try to come in to VCW then...
(Hoss gets wound up as his face reddens, and it appears that he's going to blow a gasket; fortunately Brute steps in...)
Brute: Brute not understand...They police...Police not good guys? (Hoss quickly calms down and both he and Big John get worried looks on their faces. How in the hell are they going to explain THIS to Brute?)
Judge: People change, Brute. Sometimes they turn good. But sometimes they turn bad. Officers Buddy and Max may have started out good guys, but they have turned bad. Trust me on this Brute, even cops can be the bad guys sometimes.
Law: Fortunately, there are a few other good men entered in this event. Men like our current World Champion, Warfare. Champ, wether we wind up on the same side of the ring or as opponents, good luck and may the best men win.
Hoss: An' Big John, I knows it can happen that you, or Brute there can end up on the opposite side of me and vice versa. Well, anything that happens is strictly perfessional. Following Battle Bowl, the steaks and redeye are on me!
Judge: That's enough boys, time to get back to work. [fade to black] Law: (sotto voice) Hey Hoss, ain't this spread just a little oversize for a pup as small as Spot?
Hoss: Hail, Big John, Spot's a Texas dawg now, and he's a gonna grow up ta be a real Texas-size dawg, too. Ya gotta have faith there, Big John.
Law: I suppose... [end tape] Hoss Tumbleweed and Rasputin vs. Skumm (of Devastation) and Delicious Dave Sands (of Desire) Hoss and Rasputin almost came to blows before the match started, and Skumm and Sands took advantage of this acrimony.
They managed to double team Hoss the whole match as he wasn't much interested in tagging out, and Rasputin was much interested in letting him. The end was barely two minutes in when Skumm whipped Hoss into a Sands crucifix.
Skumm/Sands in 2:03 An interesting sidelight was watching the Dominatrix and
Heather N. Lace eye each other and pace around. Half the audience was chanting "Cat fight" rather than watching the match.
RB: As suspected the lack of cooperation between Hoss and Rasputin was a telling feature of this match.
Krudd (of Devastation) and Officer Max (of the Precinct) vs. Big John Law and Ikea Perry (of Desire) Law and Max began in the ring and Law gave Max a lecture on how the law ought to be enforced, Max decided he'd had enough and opened the match with a choke that almost got him DQ'ed right there. A pair of tags saw Ikea Perry firing martial kicks at Krudd before a tag to Law took Krudd for a ride into the ropes and up in a back body drop. Max broke the momentum with a drop kick off the top rope. Nothing much was done for another minute or two, but finally Law was in position to whip Krudd hard into the buckle and follow with an avalanche. Repeat on the opposite buckle. Tag to Perry who simply hopped in and dragged Krudd to the center of the ring before covering. As he did, Heather N. Lace got in Law's face - apparently complaining about him not holding up his half of the match. Krudd, meanwhile, had kicked out. Krudd made a diving tag, and Max came in fast - only to have Perry hit a flying kick that laid him out. Perry covered, no count as Max kicked out. Perry rolled him up again. One count.
Perry complained about a slow count, and Max rolled him up from behind. Law and Lace were so into their argument, that it took
Judge Roy Bean to remind them that Perry was in danger of being pinned. They turned around, just as Perry stuck out a hand.
Law made the tag and chased Max across the ring - too late, he tagged and Krudd came in - only to be simply tossed across the ring into the enemy corner. A quick thinking Ikea Perry got a boot up, and Krudd staggered. Law ran across the ring, clotheslined him down and tagged. Perry came in over the rope with a somersault leg drop and covered.
Law and Perry in 12:11 Blind Melon Chitlin and Officer Buddy vs. Warfare and Marcelle Keller An interesting match, with Keller and BMC on one side, and Warfare in the ring with a bunch of people he didn't particularly like. The match started out slowly, the only interesting point occuring when Keller and BMC found themselves facing each other.
They engaged in a "test of strength" for a moment with neither one gaining an advantage (big surprise) before both tagging out.
Finally, Officer Buddy who was in a test of strength with Keller decided to opt out with a kick to the gut and a quick atomic drop.
A tag to BMC broke the momentum as BMC simply waited for Keller to stumble over and tag out to Warfare. A single forearm from Warfare sent BMC scurrying over to tag out to Buddy. Warfare must not have seen the tag, because Buddy simply blindsided him with a knee smasher and a cover. Warfare tossed him off. Buddy tried a standing splash and a cover. Warfare tossed him off. Buddy decided to tag out. This was followed by another couple of minutes of nothing in particular. Then BMC and Warfare were facing off, and BMC received, in rapid succession, an ICBM and the Sucking Chest Wound.
Warfare made the cover and that was that. Warfare and Keller in 9:27 RB: So the Second Chance entrants are Hoss Tumbleweed, Blind Melon Chitlin, Officers Max and Buddy, Rasputin, and Krudd. VVV: Right, but first we've got an elimination match between the Klingons and the Team Supreme, a grudge that goes back years!
The Klingons vs. The Team Supreme With much fanfare, and accompanied by sparkly "transporter" effects, Admiral Chekov, The Klingon Disrupter and the Klingon Phaser made their way ringside. The crowd cheered like madmen, obviously some old timers remembered the Klingons. The cheers turned to boos when Mr. Touchdown and Magnus Bane appeared with Muffy and Buffy, the Cheerleaders. Bane waved his I-C belt around at the crowd which just riled them up further.
Both teams were looking to Pearl Harbor the other, so nothing quite happened until the bell rang and Bane was facing the
Disrupter. Quick tags set up a double Irish whip by the Klingons, but Bane came off with a double clothesline! He grabbed the smaller Klingon, the Phaser who was now the legal man, and ran him out between the ring ropes...following him down to the ground. As expected, Mr. Touchdown and The Klingon
Disruptor got involved in the festivities. Also as expected, Chekov cut off one of the cheerleaders, Muffy, when she looked like she might be getting frisky with a pom-pom. Slightly less expected, a man in a trenchcoat and sunglasses hopped the ring rail and grabbed Buffy at about the same time and took off towards the lockers. About this time, the bell rang, and the referee declared a double countout. Magnus Bane and the Klingon Phaser were both eliminated, from the smirk on Bane's face that seemed like it might have been the idea.
VVV: Clever! Even the odds up,and avoid injury since he's got a title shot later. RB: You know, there was something familiar about that man in the trench- coat. If it's who I think it is, Mr. TD is going to in for a nasty surprise. The smirk vanished when he realized that Buffy was missing and he took off towards the lockers at high speed. That left Touchdown and the Disruptor in the ring. They locked up, Touchdown got a head lock, but the Disruptor turned it into a side suplex. The
Disruptor went for a powerslam but Touchdown slipped out and locked on a sleeper. Well, at least after he'd been DQ'ed for a blatant choke, he claimed it was a sleeper. By then, though, it was too late and the Klingons were victorious.
The Klingons in 3:47 RB: We've received word that Magnus Bane found Buffy locked in a broom closet backstage, and there was no sign of the mysterious "Trenchcoat". VVV: Interesting development there, I suppose. Now a quick commercial and then back for the second round of the Ballotbowl.
===================================================================== It is dawn and a rough looking cowpoke is seen cooking by a trail wagon, holding an iron skillet over a roaring fire, he calls out
"Come an' git it!" Big John Law comes out of the house as Hoss Tumbleweed stumbles from the barn and they sit across from each other at a wooden table. Between them sits a huge bowl.
[Hoss] (looking in the bowl, as he wrinkles his nose) You reckon that there stuff is edible? [BJL] I'm not quite sure, but you better not let Wishbone hear you. [Hoss] (pushing the bowl towards BJL) Well, I ain't gonna eat it. [BJL] (pushing the bowl back toward Hoss) I'm not going to eat it. [Hoss] Hey! I know, let's get Brute. [BJL] Even he isn't going to eat this! [Hoss] Pshaw! Brute'll eat anything. [BJL] (calling out) BRUTE! (Brute comes running to the table and immediately grabs the bowl and begins to wolf down the bowl's contents)
[BJL and Hoss] (in unison) He likes it! He likes it! *** LIFE cereal from Quaker, You'll Like IT! *** ===================================================================== We return to the spinning video wall and names begin to fall into place: Warfare and Big John Law vs. Rasputin and Ikea Perry VVV: So Rasputin is one of the lucky ones to get a second chance! Skum and Marcelle Keller vs. Dave Sands and Officer Max RB: And Officer Max is the other. Warfare and Big John Law vs. Rasputin and Ikea Perry Warfare and Law seemed pleased with their draw - which didn't stop
Rasputin, by far the freshest of the four, from cleaning house at the bell and driving both members around the ring with forearms and kicks and punches. He tagged out and Perry slipped in, swept Law's leg and covered for a 1. He then put on a short clinic of martial arts strikes and holds, before letting Rasputin back in to show off a few power moves.
Again Heather N. Lace and the Dominatrix were catting it all over each other outside the ring and drawing whistles and calls of "catfight". Their conflict distracted Rasputin long enough to allow Law to tag out. As Warfare charged over, the
Dominatrix looked up long enough to warn her man. Rasputin ducked and Warfare nearly brained himself on the ring post. An impromptu double team set up a Rasputin MONSTER DDT.
Rasputin and Perry in 7:32 Skum and Marcelle Keller vs. Dave Sands and Officer Max Sands flying drop kick at the bell sent Keller staggering - but Keller recovered enough to grab an over-eager Officer Max when he was tagged in and hit a Tombstone piledriver out of nowhere.
No count on the cover, but it put the fear of God into the other team. Some decent tags back and forth saw no one gaining an advantage until Sands hit Keller with a flying cross body block. It actually knocked the giant down, a one count followed before Keller kicked out. A pair of tags set Skumm and
Max up for a battle of technical skill vs. agility and high flying moves. In this case, it seemed that Skumm and his high speed attack had the advantage with drop kicks and leaping forearms and so on. So it seemed until Max finally got a hand on him. Then a headbutt set up an armbar that could have caused Skumm a real problem. Skumm broke it at the ropes, hopped up, hopped to the ropes, hit a drop kick and followed it up with another hop up and a dragging clothesline - cover for 1.
Max and Sands then began to make some faster tags, and a moment later caught Keller in their neck of the woods and promptly rang his chimes over and over again in the corner. They pounded him silly (1 pt END injury). Skumm came in to break it up and the ensuing brawl saw chaos raging across the ring. When it was settled, though, Keller was still in the ring and still being thrashed silly. He was a sucker for Max'es reverse figure four.
Officer Max and Dave Sands in 16:11 RB: So both members of Desire, Special Dark Ikea Perry and Delicious Dave Sands, as well as Officer Max and Rasputin advance to the next round of the Ballotbowl.
VVV: But before that The Reaper and the Metallibaby face off in a coffin match. A coffin will be placed ringside and the match ends when one wrestler is placed in the coffin AND the lid is closed.
Metallibaby vs. The Reaper, Coffin Match At the bell, the Reaper hit a drop kick that drove the 'Baby back into the ropes. He followed with a powerslam and a cover for a 1. A series of kicks and punches set up a whip and a small package - reversed by the 'Baby, but no count. The
'Baby kept moving, though, and hit a whip and hip toss towards the coffin. The Reaper landed on both feet in the coffin, and the two traded blows over the ropes for a moment, until the grabbed the ropes, hopped up and head-scissored the Reaper back into the ring. The Reaper kept rolling and staggered back to his feet. The 'Baby followed and set him on the buckle. He climbed up for a superplex - blocked and reversed! The Reaper superplexed the 'Baby out of the ring before collapsing into the ring.
The Reaper recovered first and met the 'Baby as he was reentering the ring. A few punches and so forth took us past the ten minute mark. A vicious chop to the throat drew a warning but looked like it might have seriously damaged the 'Baby (1 pt END injury).
The Reaper then went to work methodically, bouncing the 'Baby all over the ring. Finally, at about fifteen minutes, he scooped the
'Baby up and powerbombed him over the ropes (so it didn't have a lot of extra impact...) right into the coffin. He slipped out to slam the lid - and caught it square in the face as the 'Baby kicked it wide open. The 'Baby rolled back into the ring, the Reaper followed and now the 'Baby bounced the Reaper around for a moment or two before hitting the "Hit the Lights". The Reaper was out of it enough after that shot that the 'Baby dragged him all the way to the ropes and started to push him over - no go! The two exhausted and battered men traded punches until the Reaper saw the chance to scoop the 'Baby up and toss him over into the coffin. Before the
'Baby could get out, the Reaper vaulted the ropes and landed feet first on him then jumped to the floor. He flipped the lid over, and the 'Baby caught it with bare inches to spare. The
Reaper leapt to the apron and thence to the top of the coffin which slammed firmly shut! The Reaper in 21:40 RB: Which brings us to the final TAG round of the Ballotbowl. The winners of this round will face each other as singles wrestlers in the last round.
VVV: And these men have already wrestled two matches tonight and are starting to show the strain.
Again the names swirl on the video ... Ikea Perry and Officer Max vs. Rasputin and Dave Sands This match was punctuated at various points by Sands and Perry finding themselves in the ring facing one other and pausing to pose to the crowd before tagging out. Sometimes one would pose while the other pointed, and so on. The crowd didn't seem to like it. After Perry took a stiff forearm from Rasputin at the bell, a pair of tags set Sands and recent partner Officer Max against each other. The highlight of their couple of minutes was when Sands grabbed Max'es arm and leapt over the ropes to the ground dragging Max'es arm over the edge of the buckle (1 pt. STR injury). Of course, he was out of the ring, so Max had plenty of time to tag. So, of course, so did Sands. Perry took Rasputin to dojo - reverse karate chops setting up a judo throw and cover for 1. A tag put
Max back in, perhaps a poor choice since Max still smarting from his arm wound. Rasputin promptly body-slammed him once, then twice, then slapped on an armbar. Max squirmed but showed remarkable intestinal fortitude (another 1 pt. STR injury). Rasputin tagged out, Sands dropped a knee to the arm. Quick tags had
Max in bad shape, until he decided that a finger to the eye might buy him time to tag out. He was right. Both teams battled back and forth tagging left and right, until Max was in again against
Rasputin. He just had nothing left. Rasputin hit a shoulder breaker and covered.
Rasputin and Sands in 14:38 MM: So it will be Rasputin the Mad Monk and Delicious Dave Sands in the final round. But before we do that, we're going to see The Blood Dragon, as the Demon has renamed himself defend his title against Brother Sunshine, as Sexy Sam Shaft renamed himself a while back. First, though, Rocky has a very special interview in the ring.
RB: Thanks Misty. Ladies and Gentlemen, will you please welcome the VCW World Heavyweight Champion,
THE PUUUURRRRPPLLLLLLLE HHAAAAAAZZZZZZEE!!! (Purple smoke flows from the entrance as the chords to "Purple Haze" echoes throughout the arena. The Haze walks out with the belt over his left shoulder. He slaps the hands of the fans as he makes his way to the ring.)
Rocky: Well Purple, congratulations on winning the title again. Haze: Thanks Rock. Warfare and I had three tremendous matches. He has been a great champion and has held the title with tenacity and class. His reign as VCW Heavyweight Champion has been the greatest this league has seen. And it will be hard to duplicate.
Rocky: No sooner had you won the title than you were challenged by Wilhelm Faust. Will you accept his challenge?
Haze: Well Rock, when I first won the title, I told all the fans that I would defend it against all worthy opponents. Now Faust comes along and demands a title match offering his career as an enticement. Herr Faust, I don't give a rip about your career. In fact, your *career* isn't worth a plate of sauerkraut. Your so called *victories* have come against opponents who are winless in VCW. Frankly, there are many other VCW superstars who deserve a title shot more than you.
Rocky: So you're saying that you are not going to give him a title shot? Haze: He'll get a title shot. When he shows me that he is worthy of it by beating me. That's right, Faust. If you beat me here next week, you'll get your title shot.
Rocky: Spoken like a true champion. Haze: One more thing, Rock. I'd like to thank the fans for standing behind me and I hope to make them proud as their new champion. ("Purple Haze" blasts through the speakers as the Haze exit the ring and walks around shaking the hands of the ringside fans. Rocky slips out of the ring and returns to the announcing table as we take a short commercial break.
There, that wasn't long at all was it?) Lightheavyweight Title Match, Brother Sunshine challenges Blood Dragon VVV: Hey, maybe we should call Blood Dragon "The Wrestler Formerly Known as the Demon", eh? Whatever we call him, he was accompanied by Christopher Andrews to the ring, while Brother Sunshine simply posed his way down alone. We hadn't seen Brother Sunshine in a while, and when the bell rang, we saw why - he'd been working hard in the gym.
His opening headlock was a little like a choke, and drew a warning, and the Blood Dragon used that chance to dance away and recover his breath. Then Sunshine took him to school.
The Blood Dragon had grabbed Sunshine's arm and scaled the buckle. Sunshine pulled him over in something like an armdrag, then bounced back up as the Blood Dragon rolled a little slowly to his feet.
A leg sweep put him back down and a cover scored a 1. Sunshine then pounded Dragon around the head and shoulders with closed fists until the ref chased him off, then set up the "Bad Trip" piledriver.
The Dragon hit hard, but not quit hard enough for Sunshine, who declined to cover. Instead, he got the Dragon on his feet in a hammerlock and ran him shoulder first into the ringpost a few times, before pulling him back and hefting him up in a gorilla press - impressive even though the Dragon is one of the lighter of the light-heavies. The drop took the wind out of the Dragon, and Sunshine had no trouble slapping on a reverse figure four.
The Dragon looked like he might be losing his will, until Andrews got into his face. With that help, he made it to the ropes.
Sunshine shrugged and went for the torture rack instead. Things were looking very grim for the Dragon as the ten minute mark went by. Finally he worked a hand loose, and fired chop with the leading edge of his hand into Sunshine's throat. Sunshine coughed and dropped him. The Dragon crawled slowly for the outside but could barely move. Sunshine recovered well before he did, and was back on the job - arm bar this time. A few moments of that, then he stood and executed a respectable body slam.
VVV: Sunshine is just taking him apart in there. RB: Looks like Andrew's beating last broadcast may have rattled the Dem..err, Blood Dragon. Actually, the Blood Dragon was rallying, barely, and a fist strike to the temple bought him a second to breathe. But not much more.
Sunshine was back with a sleeper attempt, and the Dragon wildly thrashed to the ropes to avoid getting caught in it. Sunshine paused for a moment to yell at the ref for being in the way, and the Dragon hopped to the buckle, leapt off and hit a side kick that drove Sunshine across the ring. It also was his last gasp of energy; he collapsed in the ring as well. Again Sunshine was up first, and a whip set up a cross body block. Not satisfied yet, he helped the Dragon up and then gouged his eyes before hooking on a bear hug. Dropping the hug, he whipped the Dragon and dropped back to the ropes himself, and was tripped up by Mr. Andrews. He staggered forward as the ref turned ... and plowed right into the ref. The ref DQ'ed him on the spot. Chants of "screwjob" began to rise from the crowd.
The Blood Dragon by DQ in 18:11 RB: I have to admit, Brother Sunshine looked like the better man in there this evening. VVV: Yep. Now the last match of the Ballotbowl. Rasputin vs. Delicious Dave Sands Again the fans were treated the Dominatrix and Heather N. Lace, but this time they didn't have to co-operate. Rasputin won the lock up and drove forearms onto Sands before trying for a piledriver.
Sands blocked it, but couldn't block the follow-up suplex. Rasputin was up and whipped Sands into the ropes and bent over for a back drop - bad plan. Sands went straight over into a sunset flip, he got a one on the surprised Rasputin. Rasputin then spent the next three or four minutes trying hard to work up enough momentum to get off some kind of high impact move, but all that really happened was that he kept Sands off the ropes and the match in a rest hold. Finally he just took Sands down for an armbar. Sands broke it at the ropes and rolled to his feet first. He hit a flying cross-body block as Rasputin rose, then rolled over to try for a cover. But Rasputin was nowhere near worn down enough, he grabbed Sands arm and twisted - Oriental wristlock. Sands again rolled into the ropes. Back to the resthold while Rasputin tried to build some momentum. Finally it looked as though he might be able to hook on a full nelson, but Sands squirmed out and went up behind him for a crucifix! Rasputin staggered, almost went down, but then recovered and ran backwards into the buckle. Sands was knocked loose and slid down outside.
Heather N. Lace and the Dominatrix raced to the scene where they faced off as Sands recovered. He slid back in carefully, and
Rasputin went back to work with forearms and so on before putting him back down and locking on the armbar again. Sands made a last valiant effort, but to no avail. Rasputin shifted to the
Oriental wristlock and used it to lift Sands up. DDT! Then a pile- driver. Bing bang boom.
Rasputin in 20:34 RB: A strong showing by Rasputin in there. VVV: Who cares? I want to see the Dominatrix and Heather N. Lace in a Mazola match. RB: You are a sick man, Vendetta. So Rasputin is sitting pretty now and has his pick of title shots. And we have one more match, the I-C title rematch between Magnus Bane and Brandon Welsh.
VVV: And maybe a little blood and mayhem, too. I-C Title Match, Brandon Welsh challenges Magnus Bane RB: Well fans, we have the wheel with the following six stipulations on it. Lumberjack match! No DQ match! I Quit Match! Cage Match, you can see the quick-set-up cage is placed on the floor here and is ready to be lifted into position. Pure Science Match! And, if you will direct your attention to the center of the ring, you will see the belt suspended there, because the final choice is a ladder match. Our guest celebrity, Ron Nasty of the Rutles is here to spin the wheel.
[Clickaclikaclik...] RB: Pure Science, a match that has to favor Brandon Welsh. And favor him it did. Bane must have been distracted from the Buffy-napping, or more beat up from the first match than he looked, or worried about "Trenchcoat", because he simply let Welsh take him down, lock on an armbar for a moment, then chain it to a sleeper. It wasn't until the sleeper was on that he realized his danger, but by then it was too late. By the 2 minute mark, he was unconscious. The fans booed, feeling that they had been robbed of a good match, especially considering the matches these two had had in the past.
Brandon Welsh in 2:03, new I-C champ RB: Somehow, I doubt this is over, though. VVV: Baroque just get ready to run. The crowd is not too pleased with this outcome and is starting to turn ugly.
RB: Until next time, then...
