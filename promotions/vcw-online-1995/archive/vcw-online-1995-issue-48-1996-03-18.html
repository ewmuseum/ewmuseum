MM: Welcome everyone to a special edition of VCW Online. This week we come to you 100% not-live! That's right, each of the matches you are about to see was taped earlier in the week and has been editing for your convenience. Next week we'll be alive and kicking again! But for now, well, enjoy.
[Editor's note: all these matches are a bit more abbreviated than usual. My dire predictions of lack of time during the run of the show have come to partial fruition, and this week was very busy. Enjoy the short(er) reports.]
Ivan Miller vs. Hoss Tumbleweed The first near fall occurred hardly a minute in, when Miller managed to scoop up Hoss and drop him with a powerslam. He got no count, but dominated the match for the next few minutes, working Hoss over with a variety of moves. Then six minutes in, another powerslam and another cover. Again no count, and Hoss showed signs of rallying. A blown powerdriver by Miller let
Hoss break free and grab Miller's nose. He dragged him around the ring waving a fist and playing to the crowd. When the fans yelled back loud enough to suit him, he plowed his fist in hard and laid
Miller out on the canvas. Then he began to stomp away at the leg and knee. Miller managed to roll free, and slowly went back on the offense. He finally worked his way around to a figure-four on Hoss, which looked like it might end the match, but Hoss turned it over and Miller broke it quickly. Miller kept on going until Hoss blocked another try at a powerdriver and took him down with an elbow. A moment later, Hoss had "Tied
Him to the Tree of Woe" upside in a corner and was pounding on the leg. He kept right on pounding until the ref almost
DQ'ed him, then he let Miller out and promptly slapped on a Texas Cloverleaf. Miller had used all his energy on offense and submitted. Hoss Tumbleweed at 18:11 Styx and Stone vs. Demolition Fast and furious was the name of this game. Crash and Smash put together a quick powerslam - elbow drop combo about 2 minutes in on Styx but got no count. They then took a beating singly and in groups from both Styx and Stone, culminating in a back body drop of Smash, an elbow drop by Styx and a cover. Again no count.
Styx tagged in Stone, who walked right into a leaping DDT from Smash and a cover for 2. Styx broke the count and was tagged in a second later to hit with a top rope elbow. He made a slow tag, and as Stone came in, Smash made a desperate tag to Crash who hit a massive flying clothesline as he came in, scoring a 1 on the cover.
Styx and Stone then spent a few minutes tagging in and out and working on Crash. He tagged and Smash came in and he walked right into an armdrag and a series of knees to the shoulder from Styx.
More quick tags as Styx and Stone began to settle into a rhythm. Finally, Stone whipped Smash to the ropes and made the tag. Smash came off right into a Styx top rope dropkick. Bang, bang...bang. Styx and Stone in 14:11 Brute vs. Ivan Miller Brute smashed for a couple of minutes, then Ivan took control with a chokeslam and a cover. He kept up the pressure, scoring a two count after a major elbow drop at the four minute mark.
Suddenly, Nails appeared ringside and tried to put the snatch on Spot. Bad move. Instead of distracting Brute, it pissed him off mightily. As Judge Roy Bean, who was also ringside (advising Spot said Brute) ducked around to avoid Nails, Brute dealt with the fact that Ivan was keeping him from Spot's side by powerbombing Ivan nearly through the canvas and covering him for 3. Then he was after
Nails like a bat out of hell. Nails bailed the back with Ivan as the Texas Rangers started down to ringside to help out Brute.
Brute in 6:04 The Purple Haze vs. Brute Brute was off for some reason and let the Haze control the pace. The Haze kept it quick and on the mat. Brute never really got much going but he's a hard man to keep down. The Haze softened him up for about twelve minutes with armdrags and bars and technical type moves, mixing in the odd power move, before kicking into high gear. A knee lift staggered Brute, and a snap suplex and cover scored the pin.
The Purple Haze in 13:39 I-C Title Match Magnus Bane challenges Brandon Welsh These guys REALLY do not get along. Bane was wearing a shoulderbrace because of his injury in the cage, and used it to cheapshot
Welsh's injured shoulder before the bell. Welsh was in good form, though, and the fans and everyone else at VCW was reminded that he is a fine technical wrestler when he chooses to be. Bane, on the other hand, is a brutal madman. Sometimes, though, that works.
He shifted from the arms to the legs a few moments in when he wriggled out of a german suplex attempt by Welsh. He went for step- over crucifix on a staggered Welsh and nearly got a submission.
But Welsh worked free and then put on a clinic of armbars, twists, grapevines and so on. Finally, he chained his way back to a german suplex, this time he hit it solidly, but Bane kicked out before the bridge could be completed.
Again Welsh went to work, still working the arms, though now the wrist was getting some work, and he also tossing in some shoulder breakers and old fashioned punches and stomps. A hammerlock suplex set up the next pin attempt - no count, though.
Welsh was now beginning to tire, after fifteen minutes of hard wrestling, and for the next five or six minutes neither man got a strong advantage, although both men got their licks in.
Then Welsh was back in control, with high impact moves and general weardown mat moves. A small package got a 1 count at 24 minutes, and Welsh looked good. He took Bane up for a high vertical suplex and ... the arm gave out. Bane's body was dropped right across Welsh's head and both men crashed to the ground.
Bane was up at the ref's four count and crawled over to the stunned
Welsh. He grabbed arms and legs and painfully rolled into a surfboard crucifix. The extra strain on the arm was too much.
Bane by submission at 25:51, new I-C champ MM: And that's it for this Online, we leave you with this. And again, like clockwork, each of these self-appointed saviors of the VCW falls victim to their own misguided folly. shalt not lie' and 'Thou shalt not steal'? And you'd be LYING to yourselves and everyone else if you deny STEALING the belts from the Gladiators. That's why it was necessary to destroy the belts;
considering that you run a very real risk of death by entering into your manager's little crusade against BMCE, I wouldn't want the fate of your eternal souls hinging on the false idol worship you're engaged in right now by deluding yourselves into thinking you're actually WORTHY of calling yourselves tag team champions. It was for your own good. By the time they finish making the new belts I'll be paying for, we'll have sent you two little altar boys back to the rectory where you can reflect on your transgressions."
