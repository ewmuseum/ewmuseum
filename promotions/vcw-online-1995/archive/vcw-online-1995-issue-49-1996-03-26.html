VCW Online #49

26 March 1996

===================================================================== [Hoss] Rasputin?! What the Hail kind of name is that?! Don't make no difference to me. Can't say that I have a whole lot of respect for a man that sides with that saloon reject....Dom-MOO-natrix!
I hate ta tell ya, but the dang cow looked a whole heap better in that leather than you. Course it is nice that you kept the flies! HAW HAW HAW. Rasputin! I don't know where you came from, but you ain't the Mad Monk I'm familiar with. Don't make no nevermind to me! Just you keep that milk machine outta my way, or there will be steaks til
Tuesday! MMMMOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! ===================================================================== Liszt's "Mephisto Waltz" swells to a crescendo and is then buried under the opening riff of "Burn" by Deep Purple. This is the trademark music of ...
V V CCCCC W W W V V C W W W W V V C W W W W V CCCCC W W irtual hampionship restling ===================================================================== [Cut to Misty Mountainhop in the booth.] MM: Welcome to VCW Online. We have that match coming up soon, Hoss Tumbleweed vs. Rasputin, as well as a number of other exciting matches and a World Title tilt between the Purple Haze and Warfare! So let's get right into the action. As always, ringside commentary comes from Rocky Baroque and Vile Vince Vendetta.
Hoss Tumbleweed vs. Rasputin At the lock up, Rasputin shifted quickly to an armdrag takedown and planted a knee in Hoss'es shoulder. Hoss retaliated by popping back up, blocking a punch and tripping Rasputin so he fell with his throat over the rope. As Rasputin slumped, Hoss criss-crossed for a running leg drop, Rasputin rolled out of the way, but just a moment too soon...Hoss was able to abort the leg drop. Instead he took it to Rasputin with punches, a choke that the ref broke, and finally a big knee lift. Rasputin landed hard and Hoss covered for 2. Hoss complained about a slow count, and when he turned around it was to be on the receiving end of a Rasputin splash into the ropes. He couldn't get a roll started, though, and Hoss pummeled him with piston punches before lifting him up for an atomic drop and a short arm clothesline out of the ring. Perhaps not surprisingly, when Hoss followed it was the
Dominatrix with her whip who blindsided him as he lifted up Rasputin. Rasputin took advantage of the cheap shot to roll Hoss back in and go for a spinning toehold. He tried to cover to a figure four, but Hoss kicked him off. Back he came, a sharpshooter this time - still no luck as Hoss kicked him off again. He called for his finisher, and set up for a DDT, only to find himself being whipped across the ring and catching a big boot in the face. Hoss grabbed him as he staggered and sent him into the ropes again, catching him as he came off in a mighty bearhug.
Rasputin almost submitted, but the Dominatrix hopped onto the apron and caught his eye at the last moment and he refused.
Obligingly, Hoss dropped him, grabbed his chin and spat tobacco juice into his eye as the ref was getting the Dominatrix off the apron. A powerslam set up the cover, and the ref turned around in time to count it.
Hoss Tumbleweed in 11:38 MM: Earlier in the week, Hoss Tumbleweed had another match, and we have that for you on tape as well as comments from both the man and his opponent pre-match.
[Roll tape: [Hoss] Steve Slanders, you're a mighty big man. But it ain't the size of the man in the fight, but the fight in size, er...the size of the man, er...Dagnabbit! Can't we start this here interview over....What!? We're live?
Don't matter none, you ride in the same outfit as that moneybags Brandon Welsh and that alone makes you a no good revenuer! With your fancy cars, and expensive dinners, and personal trainers! Pshaw! That ain't worth a hill of beans when you step in that ring.
One thing I can't quite understand! While the Hail do you people pronounce that street RO-DAY-O, any dang fool can see that it is
RO-DEE-O like the ropin' and ridin' event!!! Get ready rich boy! It is time to hook it up!!! [Cross cut to: Brandon Welsh, Steve Slanders, Brenda and Valerie are standing backstage prior to Steve's match with Hoss Tumbleweed.
BW: Tonight I will unleash on the VCW one of the most powerful forces to ever exist in California, Mr. Steve Slanders. The unfortunate first opponent of Mr. Slanders will be Hoss Tumbleweed.
But what I really want to talk about is Magnus Bane, and MY Intercontinental Belt. You are correct Mr. Bane, I do want another match and I don't care what type it is, just as long as after I pound you into a little pile of pulp that I get MY title back. So here it is...
Brandon holds up a contract that is signed at the bottom I will be turning this contract into the VCW offices and they can decide what type of match we will have. My only stipulation is that it is for
MY title. And I will be bringing my friends here along to make sure that everything is even. See you at the supercard, and as for you Mr.
Tumbleweed, get ready to get rolled!! Hoss Tumbleweed vs. Steve Slanders Slanders is, without a doubt, a monster. He is one of the biggest men in the federation, up there with John Law and
Marcelle Keller. Hoss Tumbleweed is no small man, but Slanders dwarfed him. Slanders was also moving pretty quickly for a big man. He took control early and began to pound on
Hoss with forearms and punches - no style, no technique, just a big guy pounding on a guy not quite as big. A whip sent Hoss into the ropes, where Brandon Welsh tripped him. Slanders covered but got no count. Judge Roy Bean was immediately complaining about both Welsh and Valerie being ringside, but to no avail.
Slanders, meanwhile, was running Hoss all over the ring. Hoss couldn't seem to get anything going, hitting with maybe a couple of forearms in the next four minutes. Valerie then hopped up on the apron and as the ref and Roy Bean chased her off, Welsh hopped up and stuck a boot over the rope and Slanders whipped Hoss into it. The ref caught the tail end of that out of the corner of his eye, and sent Welsh back to the lockers before dropping to count the cover - a one count. Slanders was angry about both of these developments and set up for a powerbomb. It scored and the cover scored a 2. A short while later he almost got himself DQ'ed for a choke, and it was while arguing about that the Hoss got off almost his first offense of the match. He hit Slanders with a couple of forearms and then whipped him to the ropes. As
Slanders came off, he went for the big boot, but Slanders caught it. Slanders took a moment to sneer at the crowd, then everyone's jaw dropped as Hoss Tumbleweed took off in an enziguri! It's your guess as to who was more surprised - Judge Roy, the audience, the announcers or Steve Slanders. On second thought, it was probably
Slanders, since he was shocked he let Hoss cover him for the 3.
Hoss Tumbleweed in 11:29 VVV: Robbery! Highway robbery! Slanders dominated that match. RB: As much as I hate to agree with you, Vince, I think that Hoss did get a little lucky - Slanders sure seemed to be in control of that match. [Click: end of taped segment.] MM: Tag team action is heating up as well, the same night that Hoss faced Slanders, at a benefit for Cable-Deficient TV Viewers, the team of Desire faced Demolition. Before that match, we had these words... [Roll more tape: Heather N. Lace stands in the center of the screen with Perry and Sands on either side of her.
Sands: "Heather, what can I say? You're the greatest manager in the VCW!" HNL: "Undoubtedly." Perry: "Not only the smartest, but also the most beautiful!" HNL: "I couldn't agree more." Sands: "Special Dark, are we not the luckiest two sons of guns in the entire sport of e-wrestling?"
Perry: "Delicious One, of course we are!" HNL: "You're not getting my Perrier, boys. But feel free to beat enough loose gold fillings out of Demolition's heads to buy yourselves a Diet Coke..." All 3: "See you in the ring!" Desire vs. Demolition Crash started out with a cheap shot clothesline on Ikia Perry at the bell. Perry staggered back into Sands who decided that was a tag and drove Crash off with a standing drop kick. The ref decided that Crash and Sands were legal, and forced the others out of the ring. Crash slowed Sands high speed attack by a kick to the knee, then the pair played tag for a minute before they both tagged out. Smash caught a surprise sleeper on Perry who broke free by tagging out. The next few minutes were all Demolition, though,
Crash swept the leg and hit a spinning toehold. He tried once or twice to go for a figure-four, but Sands kicked him off of that, so he settled instead for holding the toehold and tagging. Smash came in and picked up Sands out of the figure four for a powerbomb.
As he lifted him for another move, Sands elbowed free and dove for the tag - missing by a foot as Crash jumped in and caught him.
The ref forced Crash out, but Smash tagged him back in immediately, and they began to set up the Wrecking Ball. The business with the ref had messed up their timing, though, and the decided against trying it as Sand staggered up. In fact, as Crash was climbing down from the buckle, Sands took a quick run and hit a dropkick that sent him spinning to the apron. That was all Sands had energy for and he slumped to the mat again. By the time Crash had recovered,
Sands had responded to the exhortations of Heather N. Lace on the outside, and was again staggering towards Perry. Crash tagged in
Smash, who charged in shoulder blocked Sands - right into Perry.
Who called that a tag and jumped in. He chased Smash all over the ring, firing kicks and punches, and eventually sweeping the leg and covering. No count. But it gave Sands a breather. They set up for another double team, but Sands' leg drop missed, and
Demolition was back in control, but time was running out. They were working over Sands again, but in their enthusiasm to get the match won before the time ran out, they got too close to Desire's corner. Sands made a blind tag just before Smash whipped him across into Crash's raised foot. Sands fell to the mat, out like a light.
Perry on the other hand was in a great position to hop to the top of the buckle and land both knees on Smash's neck/shoulders.
Smash was driven right down into the mat, Perry hooked the legs, and by the time Crash realized who the legal man was, Desire had clocked up the win.
Desire in 19:11 [Click: End of that tape.] MM: Which brings us up to Demolition and their match this evening, Styx and Stone... Let's go back ringside. Styx and Stone vs. Demolition This one was almost over before it began - Krudd and Skumm hit S&S with a double top rope dropkick as soon as they hit the ring, and Krudd covered Stone. Stone was surprised but tossed him off.
Devastation were tagging quickly and moving well, but Skumm got a little too close to Stone who took him down with a quick belly to belly and then stomped on his arm. He turned then to go tag, but Skumm was quicker with his tag and Krudd clipped Stone from behind with a martial arts leg sweep chained to a hamstring stretch. He worked Stone back over to Devastation territory before tagging out. The tag was sloppy and Styx and Stone got a quick couple of tags in finishing with a spiked shoulder breaker on Skumm that looked like it might have really hurt him (1 pt STR injury). Skumm made a desperate tag out, and Styx and Stone kept up the double teams for another minute or two, as
Krudd tried to hold them both off to let Skumm recover. He did a good job, too. A martial arts combo setting up a clothesline to take down Stone and getting a cover for no count. Styx broke the count and they worked some double teams. They ended up setting up for something that required Stone to gorilla press Krudd. Krudd squirmed around, though, and a nerve strike to Stone's neck took the strength out of his left arm. Krudd was dropped and slipped down behind to roll up Stone. He managed, barely, to hold him down for the 3.
Devastation in 7:58 MM: Desire is also back on track this evening, facing former World Champions, the Gladiators! Desire vs. The Gladiators The first few minutes were back and forth with some good double teaming from all the wrestlers, it looked like the Glads were getting slightly the better of the match, then Ikia Perry locked an armtwist on Massacre and Dave Sands came off the top with a knee to the twisted arm - Massacre flopped down, a cover was made, but no count. Another double team had Massacre whipped by Perry into a Sands crucifix - again no count. Then it was back to Gladiator territory for a slowdown and some mat wrestling from Massacre, and as the ten minute mark rolled by, he had Sands in a figure four and held it as Vengeance hit a standing splash. The Glads scored a two count for that. Vengeance tagged back to Massacre, but in the switch, Sands made a diving tag to Perry. Perry charged across and a leaping spin kick drove Vengeance to the floor. Massacre was on the receiving end of a number of martial arts type kicks and punches which drove him back to the Desire corner, where Sands was scaling the buckle. Tag, leg sweep and hold by Perry, elbow drop by Sands off the top buckle and that, my friends, was that.
Desire in 10:38 MM: As you can see, the tag team scene is getting complicated. But we like that sort of complications, don't we? Our last non-title match is next!
Blind Melon Chitlin vs. The Demon The match had potential - two of the best VCW has to offer going at with nothing on the line but pride.
The Demon leapt up with a crescent kick at the bell, as Chitlin ducked and charged. Both men went piling into the corner, where the Demon's martial arts and speed quickly put him in control.
He couldn't quite break free from the grip of BMC, though, until he rammed a spear finger to the throat that dropped the bigger man to the mat. He tried for the nerve pinch sleeper, but BMC ducked into the ropes. As BMC stood, the Demon hit with a jumping spin kick and went for the sleeper again. Still too early as
BMC easily backed away. For the next couple of minutes, the Demon played keep away and tagged Chitlin at range while avoiding his attempts to close. He couldn't keep it up forever, though, and a whip to the corner gave BMC the chance he needed. The Demon was coming in after the whip to put a high knee into the Chitlin, and the Chitlin ducked. The Demon practically crippled himself, and ended up tangled in the ropes and buckles.. His costume seemed to be caught on the buckle as well and he couldn't quite get loose.
This gave Chitlin a much needed moment to recover. He got to his feet, grabbed the Demon and yanked him loose, ripping a huge tear in his suit across the chest and back, revealing a massive tattoo of some kind underneath, and hiptossed him out of the corner.
After a few stomps, the Chitlin hit a rushed "Pedigree". In control for the first time, he lifted up the Demon for a long vertical suplex Harley Race style. A cover got a two count, before the Demon broke free and both men rolled slowly to their feet.
Things got a bit confused as Chitlin grabbed the Demon and whipped him across the ring, as he did so two things happened.
The first was that as he grabbed the Demon, the rip in the Demon's outfit became wider, showing a black oriental dragon with red diamond markings coiling around his whole upper body. The second was that the ref didn't quite get out of the way and was sent spilling into a corner in a daze.
Chaos erupted. Three very large men, and one smaller business suit types stormed the ring, where two of the big men hustled BMC out of the ring as the other two men jumped the Demon. The big one grabbed the Demon as a camera zoomed in on the businessman.
VVV: You know I think I recognize those guys. The pair are the Storm Lords, and the other big guy is Theseus Devlin, all from the GIWF.
RB: That would make the other guy Christopher Andrews, their manager. I think we've got a mike there now.
[Christopher Andrews] Tanaka, you have about ten seconds to give me a *REAL* good explanation for all of this! [the Demon] I hold great respect for you, Andrews-san, but this is not your concern. I have been in the VCW since before you hired me to compete in the GIWF. [CA] Not my business, eh? Well, I've done a little research into your VCW history. Before you started workin' with me, you were a *nobody* in the
VCW! When I took over your training; groomin' you for the GIWF
Light-Heavyweight Championship, you all of a sudden become the *VCW* Light-Heavyweight Champion! It was *MY* training program that got you there! *I* was responsible for making you a champ, and you kept it secret from me! You'd better *belive* this is my business! Do you realize how much *money* I could be making as the manager of the VCW
Light-Heavyweight Champion?! Not to mention the kind of pull that would give you in the GIWF?! THIS IS DAMN WELL MY BUSINESS! NO ONE MAKES A
FOOL OF MR. CHRISTOPHER ANDREWS! [CA turns to Theseus Devlin and the Storm Lords] SHOW THIS PUNK WHAT HAPPENS TO PEOPLE WHO TRY TO FOOL CHRISTOPHER ANDREWS! The Storm Lords and Devlin began to work the Demon over, carefully since he's valuable property, but it sure looked like it hurt.
During all of the above, unnoticed by everyone, The Inquisition, Rasputin and the Metallibaby had been working up behind Chitlin. Taking the
GIWF visitors jump of the Demon as a cue, they plowed into Chitlin from behind and began to work him over - The Inquisition holding him as Rasputin went after him with a chair and the 'Baby smacked him with his bass. This, of course, brought the Gladiators, BMX and Marcelle Keller spilling out of the back. It was right about this point that the referee staggered to his feet, looked around and called for the bell. The match ended in a double DQ, and several brawls erupting all over the place.
Andrews and his men dragged the Demon off towards the lockers as the Iron Maiden and BMCE continued to slug it out. We break for commercial here. MM: What a wild ride that last match was, we've got one more match for you folks - and here it is!
World Title Match, The Purple Haze challenges Warfare Again these two juggernauts clashed. Warfare won the initial test of strength, then traded forearms with the Haze until the
Haze reversed a whip and sent him to the mat with the clothesline after. A wimpy looking powerslam had Warfare bouncing back up immediately, only to catch a knee lift. The Haze followed the staggering Warfare around and introduced him to the buckle a few times, until Warfare blocked one, and gave the Haze a reverse headbutt that laid him out. A couple of punches set up a quick
Northern Lights Suplex by Warfare, but he also landed hard and couldn't quite capitalize on it. Still he kept up the mixed assault and around 11 minutes in, hit the Sucking Chest Wound for a 1 count. The Haze was showing amazing resilience, and when
Warfare hit the ICBM a couple of minutes later tossed a leg on the ropes. Warfare hit another Sucking Chest Wound but only got a 1, then another ICBM for no count. As the twenty minute mark loomed, the Haze rallied, using an armbar to set himself up to ram Warfare's arm into the buckle a few times. Warfare went for another Sucking Chest Wound - one too many it seems. The Haze reversed the whip, hit a powerslam as Warfare came out and made the cover.
Winner and new Champion, The Purple Haze. RB: And once again The Purple Haze is VCW World Heavyweight Champion. We're already over time limit, so we'll see you soon. ================================================================== Don't worry - Postview, Rankings and so on will be out tomorrow (Tuesday), we've got a Supercard next so we're on a two week schedule anyway...
