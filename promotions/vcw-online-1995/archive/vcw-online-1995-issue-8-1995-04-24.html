Virtual Championship Wrestling — Roleplay

"VCW Online #8, Apr 24 1995"

24 April 1995
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Liszt's "Mephisto Waltz" swells to a crescendo and is then buried under the opening riff of "Burn" by Deep Purple. This signifies ........ V V CCCCC W W W V V C W W W W V V C W W W W V CCCCC W W irtual hampionship restling
(Camera opens to an upper torso shot of Blind Melon Chitlin tapping down a deserted street that looks like South-Central L.A. after the riots. The camera follows him on his journey...)
BMC: After Marcelle "told" me about your magnificent display of technical wrestling at last week's card, I sat back and thought about your alleged lineage and its influence on your wrestling. It's quite awe-inspiring when you think about it...grapplers with chiseled bodies competing to the death in the purest, most scientific of all wrestling styles. The visual images are just spectacular...spectacular... Then I thought about the stark contrasts between your pristine and my, let's say, 'less than pristine,' backgrounds. The greatest influence on my wrestling style was pure survival. When you're left to fend for yourself on the streets of TJ because your momma didn't want to deal with a blind baby you learn real quick how to protect yourself and take what you want, no matter what it takes. That's how I survived then, and that's how I wrestle today. But after last week, Actagon, I thought to myself, "Self, it's never too late to change." You're my new hero, Actagon. I used to think Dick the Bruiser was a god, but now I know that you and all of your ancient Greek homeboys are the icons I should model my wrestling career after. We're getting close now...I want you to see how sincere I am about this.
(Blind Melon Chitlin quickly ducks around a corner. The cameraman lags behind for a few seconds, then pans to a wider shot. Marcelle Keller is pounding a sledgehammer into a contraption that seems to be a home-size fiberglass trash dumpster on wheels, with two battered aluminum crutches attached to each side. The wider angle also shows that BMC is wearing a homemade toga made from a sheet that apparently hasn't been washed in quite a while.)
BMC: Actagon, as an act of respect and homage to you and your ancestors, I have instructed Marcelle Keller to construct this chariot (he makes a swooping, pointing motion with both arms, but he points in the wrong direction. Marcelle grabs his hands and points him in the direction of the "chariot"). I knew that, Marcelle!!! (cracks him across the head with his cane). Just like all the ancient Greek myths, this chariot carries with it great symbolism.... The dumpster is symbolic of my past - a past that transported and transformed me into the wrestler I have been up to this point. It represents the trials and tribulations that I was able to overcome during my life. Remember, those who forget their past are doomed to relive it...or something like that. Like the great winged horse, Pegasus, it will carry me into future battles. And you may be curious why the crutches are on my chariot. You know what they symbolize, chinga? THEY SYMBOLIZE WHAT YOU'RE GONNA' NEED TO WALK WITH AFTER I BEAT THE CRAP OUT OF YOU!!! Did you actually think you had become my idol, Actagon? You must be loco!!! I think I'm gonna' call up the Psycho Ward and see if they have any extra Prozac for you, nina. You humiliated mi amigo, Marcelle, last week in a last second fluke pin. I don't think you know what you're getting yourself into, chinga. Marcelle! What the hell are you doing to the chariot?
(Marcelle is banging on the dumpster portion angrily with the sledgehammer)
BMC: Stop it!!! (Chitlin clangs his cup over Marcelle's head) See what you've done to him, Actagon? He can sense that our match is approaching, and he's like an elephant - he's got a long memory. Don't worry, I'll do my best to keep him out of our match. I promised him he could ride in the chariot if he didn't get me disqualified. You still wanna' ride in the chariot Marcelle?
(BMC signs, Marcelle nods) Then stay out of the ring! (Clangs his cup over Marcelle's head once again) Actagon, be ready. Just like Darius kicked the Greeks' ass to form the Persian Empire, I'm gonna' do the same to you to start the Chitlin Empire. Except I'm not gonna' stop at Athens like Darius did. I'm gonna' roll straight through you and then onto the Human Rockslide, Raymond Hardy the next week. I grow weary. Marcelle! My chariot awaits!
(Marcelle pulls the chariot up to BMC who hops into the dumpster, and they leave the area.)
[Pan across the crowd, one slender young thing in leather holds a sign saying "Enforce your Will on Me", another group holds a sign saying "The Chitlin is a Chicken", yet another saying "RSP-W.F Loves VCW". Finally, the camera whips back around to the booth where Misty Mountainhop is waiting.]
MM: Welcome to another exciting VCW On-line! As you can see Blind Melon Chitlin is more than ready to take on Actagon, so let's get down to it!
[Cut ringside, with Rocky Baroque and Vile Vince Vendetta. Actagon is again warming up in the ring, to the cheers of the fans. Solaris stands watchfully nearby.]
VVV: It's like I've been saying all along, The Chitlin is a scholar, Darius, and all that - he knows what he's talking about.
RB: It was a surprisingly literate interview...wait there go the lights.
[The arena lights go out and twin spotlights beam in on Marcelle Keller pulling the "chariot" containing Blind Melon Chitlin down the aisle. Chitlin is wearing a crown made from dried up old weeds (because he couldn't find any olive branches), and the music from '2001 - A Space Odyssey' plays Marcelle makes a couple of circuits around the ring, finally stopping at his corner, where Chitlin makes as regal an entrance as he can into the ring, considering he's got the cane, cup, sunglasses and all...] Actagon vs. Blind Melon Chitlin Whether or not Actagon is really his idol, BMC certainly spent the last week brushing up his Greco-Roman style. That and his weight advantage looked like it might give him the overall advantage in the match. BMC began fast with a Fireman's carry, followed by a series of amateur riding moves, and ending in a Turk. A surprised Actagon kicked out at the two count, but was obviously rattled. BMC followed up with a side-headlock, then a whip to the ropes into a "Tito Santana Flying Burrito", two knees to the head and BMC scored another two count. The next minutes were frustrating, as BMC chased Actagon all over the ring, and inflicted damage on him left and right, but could never quite get him in a pinning predicament. BMC hit his trademark Frankensteiner about 6 minutes in, and went for a cover, but Actagon laid a foot on the ropes immediately. BMC took a moment to argue with the ref, and Actagon made good use of it, he grabbed BMC from behind and executed the Protean Slam Piledriver Suplex. The Slam connected solidly, but it had been a desperation move by Actagon since BMC was relatively fresh. He got a one count but that was all. BMC argued with the ref over this one, and Marcelle Keller took the opportunity to tag Actagon with a pair of brass knuckles from the apron. It wasn't a great shot, though, since Solaris dragged the huge deaf-mute from the apron as he was throwing the punch. Keller then began to chase Solaris around the ring. This distracted Actagon enough for BMC to rest a moment, then schoolboy him from behind but no count. We crossed the 10 minute mark, and Actagon was looking much the worse for the beating he had taken. BMC slapped on a sleeper, but Actagon quickly slipped out - he may be beaten up, but he still knows his counter-wrestling. A bit later, Solaris tripped up BMC as he was about to go to town on Actagon, and as Actagon stopped to tell Solaris that he didn't approve, BMC set up the Blind Man's Bluff. He came off the ropes, and just barely tagged Actagon, but stumbled into Keller who had wrenched a crutch off the "chariot" and climbed onto the apron. When they got sorted out, the time had run out. Timeout
RB: So that feud has no resolution yet.
VVV: It's got highway robbery, though, BMC ran that boy around the ring like he was, I don't know, the bird in the cartoon.
RB: "The Bird in the Cartoon." Sigh. Now fans get a chance to see Bad Karma in the ring for the first time, although they've seen them in action against D&D and the Stone Chizzlers already. The Gladiators vs. Bad Karma Both teams wanted to hurt each other, and the match was almost declared a no-contest from the start as they both jumped each other before the bell. Finally, though things got sorted out a little bit, and the match actually began. Brutal. That's the word for this match. Four big men beating living hell out of each other. Bad Karma showed a little more ring savvy, making smoother tags and in general better teamwork. The Gladiators tried to make up for it with sheer bad temper. After about three minutes of this, the expected Pier Six erupted as all four men just went to town on each other. It was here that Doom and Disaster put in an appearance. They came down to the ring (and the apron, and the ringside area, and the first few rows of seats, and, well, you get the idea) and started to get in on the action. Moments after that, a strange man in armor and accompanied by four huge men cloaked in black came out as well. D&D looked surprised to see him. Bad Karma took advantage of the moment to jump D&D. So did the Gladiators for that matter. At that, the armored man laughed and left. The referee threw up his hands and declared it a Double DQ. Just as BK finished destroying Doom and were about to jump either the Gladiators or Disaster
(it was hard to tell which), security finally arrived and started to break things up. Double DQ
RB: What a mess. The Commisioner had better do something about that D&D and Bad Karma situation.
VVV: I hear that he has a plan to announce something at The Bedlam Breakout.
RB: Speaking of the Breakout, here's Misty with an update.
[Cut to Misty in the Booth.]
MM: The entries keep rolling in, remember, this Friday is the last date for entries. Here's whose already entered. Achmed Fuga, Actagon, Anarchy-X, Blind Melon Chitlin, Brandon Welsh, Dylan Mackey, Jose Delvalle, Landmine Lavin, Madman Schmidt, Marble Stone, Marcelle Keller, Raymond Hardy, Sand Stone, The Cajun, The Priest, Ugly Jim Studly, and Warfare Rumour has it that there may also be some surprise entries. Stay with us!
RB: Next up, The Human Rockslide Raymond Hardy still looking for his first victory, and Anarchy-X. The Human Rockslide Raymond Hardy vs. Anarchy-X


Stone Man and Suite Sister Mary maintained an uneasy detente during this whole match, and neither was a factor due to the unceasing vigilance of the other. Anarchy-X plan was clear, use his speed and agility to stay at range and avoid the bigger and stronger Hardy. He began with Kicks and chops, trying to move in, hit, then move away. Hardy just took it all, A-X couldn't move him. Or rather, couldn't move him for a minute or two, then A-X stepped in and threw a German suplex, and locked on the Scorpion Deathlock. Hardy powered out of it quickly. A-X grabbed him as he stood with a neckbreaker and then an inside cradle. Hardy powered out immediately. Then Hardy began to go to town. Forearms, irish whip and shoulder block - really using his weight to it's best advantage. He hefted A-X up and hit with kind of sloppy second rope piledriver, which rattled A-X's cage pretty well. One superplex later, and A-X was stretched out in the middle of the ring. Hardy hooked the leg and got the pin. Raymond Hardy in 5:43 Scarcely had Hardy had his hands raised, but The Gladiators came racing out of the back and jumped him. Anarchy-X was being helped to his feet by Suite Sister Mary and looked for a moment like he wanted to do something, but then let himself be led out. The Gladiators went to work on Hardy like they meant it, after taking a quick blindside shot or two at Stone Man with a chair. Following them out was a man in robes with a hood covering his face
(more manager sized than wrestler sized). He was carrying paint, rope and a stencil. The Gladiators slammed Hardy into the ring post and started to tie him down (with Vince Vendetta cackling in the background the whole time). The manager slapped the stencil on his back and slapped the paint over it. Then all three made a quick exit as security finally got itself motivated to start getting out there.
RB: What a disgrace! What did they paint on Hardy's back?
[The camera tracks in and in runny letters we can read "a present from the Gladiators"]
VVV: The Gladiators just want to show that they shouldn't be trifled with. And I think they did. That's a nasty working over they gave that right leg.
[Cut to security helping Hardy limp towards the back.]
RB: We'll see if we can have more information about Hardy's condition later on for you. But now, we have Ugly Jim Studly and former world champion, the Priest! Ugly Jim Studly vs. The Priest This one turned into a contest of pure endurance, how much damage could the two men inflict on one another before someone gave out. The match started with a test of strength, Studly got the upper hand, then threw a knee into the Priest's stomach and pulled him over for a 1 count only about 15 seconds in. Then the match turned into an exchange of injuries. The Priest with a suplex, Studly with a shoulder block, the Priest with a snap-mare, Studly with a forearm, the Priest with punch combos, and finally almost eight minutes in, Studly with a series of powerful axe-handles to the Priest's back and a suplex and a cover. This time for a two count. Slowly but surely, now, Studly seemed to be gaining the upper hand. The question seemed to be "Will he get the Priest pinned before time runs out?" Several times he tried to get the Priest out of the ring, but the wily veteran managed to go into the ropes rather than over them. A concerned Suite Sister Mary got closer and closer. After a nasty Slingshot Suplex on the Priest, Studly slipped out of the ring and gave her a big sloppy kiss
[close-up of Studly shouting something like, "Feel the Studlieness!] The Priest came out after him angry and caught a steel chair for his trouble, but the referee got in Studly's face and both men returned to the ring without too much difficulty. The Priest looked mad, and a short flurry ended with Studly in a Boston Crab. He made it to the ropes, but it looked like a near thing. The Priest tried to follow up his advantage, but Studly managed to sidestep and slap his neck onto the ropes. He used his body like an expert to keep the ref from spotting the fact that between the rope and his hands he had the Priest in a choke for a solid thirty seconds. After that, the Priest dropped to the mat and Studly hooked the leg. The Studliettes kept Mary from getting involved and that was that. Ugly Jim Studly at 13:38
MM: I've caught up with Ugly Jim Studly after his impressive match with the Priest. Jim?
[Jim leers at Misty for a moment, then speaks]
UJS: All right, all you geeky Studly-wannabes, listen up! I just destroyed the number one contender to the heavyweight title. The way I see it, that makes ME the man to beat! Enforcer, I don't want to hear any whining about how some lightweight jobber is the only one one worthy of a shot - put up the gold against a REAL man, and we'll see how long your title reign lasts!
[Sara and Tiffany giggle and wave, then follow Studly as he stalks off.]
MM: Why am I not surprised, back to you Rocky.
RB: And back for the main event! Our first ever three man tag team event. The Psycho Ward and a mystery partner against The Regulators - the Enforcer and the Equalizers. First, though, this from the Ward.
(The camera opens to Brandon Welsh and Dylan Mackey standing on the interview platform with Nurse Kimberly)
Kimberly: Sorry that Brenda couldn't be with us here, but she is preparing to make her debut in the NCW.
Brandon: As for the statements made previously by John E. Gillette, I have only this to say. We didn't like the way that The Stone Chizzlers were jumped at the end of our match, but we haven't lost any sleep over it. As for you not wanting to accept our challenge, that's cool, I understand the force that self preservation puts on people.
Dylan: Exactly, but even though we may not get a match with the Dang Foreigners, don't think that there won't be a little payback for Mr. Gillette. Gillette, they make razors don't they, hmmmm.....
Kimberly: But for now, onwards and upwards, we are looking forward to participating in the first VCW six-man tag match.
Brandon: And with the help of our mystery partner, we will take care of the Equalizers, and the current VCW chump the Enforcer.
Dylan: And once that is out of the way, it's back to pursuing the tag team titles that would match so well to our white hospital outfits.
Brandon: And taking part in the Bedlam Breakout, and coming away with the new I-C title! The first in a long line of titles that we will hold.
Dylan: Also, I have one other comment. Actagon, you look awful familiar to me, weren't you in the room across the hall from me at the institute a few months back. That guy was crazier than any of us. He thought he was some sort of greek god. Ha!
Brandon: And as for you Warfare, how about another go round now that the VCW has extended the match time, so that I can finish you off for good!
Kimberly: Until next time...
(The camera fades out) Psycho Ward + 1 vs. The Regulators (The Enforcer/The Equalizers) Six man action the way it was meant to be! The mystery partner of the Psycho Ward was a masked man, who looked vaguely familiar. The Ward looked like they just wanted to put a hurting on someone, the Regulators looked efficient, and the mystery man looked like he had something to prove. The Regulators obviously had had more practice as a team, starting out early a side-headlock and then a second rope forearm drop which scored a two count on a surprised Mystery Man. Not to be out done, Nurse Kimberly tripped up Mike Lavin allowing Dylan Mackey came in with several powerslams followed by a small package from Brandon Welsh for a two count on Mike "Landmine" Lavin. All this in the first two minutes. Then came three minutes of see-saw action, then Mike "Madman" Schmidt locked on the Stand (a scorpion deathlock). Things could have been very ugly for the Ward, but while one Nurse distracted the ref, Mackey came in and broke the hold. Several solid double-team shots were delivered by the Ward before the ref turned around. As the ten minute mark approached the Regulators began to hit with some combinations such as an Enforcer press slam and a Landmine top rope splash. Despite their moves, the endurance of the trio
(and the work of the Nurses) kept them from getting near a pinning predicament until almost thirteen minutes in. Then they set for Double Jeopardy on a stunned Dylan Mackey. The Enforcer hoisted him onto his shoulders, as both Equalizers came off the ropes with flying shoulder blocks. This would probably have ended the match if the Mystery Man and Brandon Welsh hadn't come in at almost the same moment and rolled the Equalizers out of the ring for a few quick cheap shots. The Enforcer, no longer the legal man, came out fairly quickly to save his partners, giving the Nurses a chance to roll Mackey back to his corner. "Madman" Schmidt finally made it back into the ring and Mackey tagged out to the Mystery Man who quickly locked on the figure four. Schmidt was in agony, and things looked bad, there's the bell! Timeout! Lots of boos over the inconclusive result. Timeout
VVV: Got it!
RB: Got what?
VVV: The man in the mask, I know who he is.
RB: Who is he?
VVV: I'm not going to tell you, if he wanted you to know he'd tell you himself. But I figured it out.
RB: Vince..never mind. We have received word that Raymond Hardy has been injured but it is not critical, his Doctor has advised him to rest but is not forbidding him to wrestle. Good night everyone, see you next week.
[After the credits roll, the following plays:
(Cut to the throne room of Apocalypse, the armored man who appeared earlier tonight... he sits silently, pondering, thinking... he turns his helmeted head slowly towards the camera and the eyes glow an eerie blood red.) "For those of you who have wondered why there has been not a whisper from these halls since the conception of VCW... I have been sitting, waiting, planning for the day when Apocalypse would rise up from his hidden lair and send forth his henchmen to destroy all those who opposed him in the e-wrestling world. But something unexpected happened... the return of a great team... the return of a team that could aid my cause greatly... the return of Doom and Disaster!"
(A flash of lightning crackles, illuminating the throne room for a brief second. Apocalypse flinches not a bit and continues speaking, his tone somewhat annoyed, as if the very weather itself should grant him the utmost respect.) "Doom and Disaster... you know not what you do now. You fight for the cause of good... the cause of justice... think of what could be done, how much better to use your abilities... for complete and utter domination! You have it in your blood... you showed it during your early days in EWC... you SHALL join Apocalypse and his horsemen! You SHALL be my war dogs in the VCW!"
(He has risen a bit out of his throne, realizes this, and promptly seats himself.) "Or you shall be destroyed. Bad Karma shall no longer annoy you, shall no longer pester you... if you were to join... but refuse my offer and you shall be terminated... this Apocalypse swears!"
(Fade to black.)
