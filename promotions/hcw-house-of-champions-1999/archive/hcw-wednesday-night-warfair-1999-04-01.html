Wednesday Night WarFair

1 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Morrin: Back to the action. GreatOne vs. Traveler Knight: Our next match is scheduled for one fall. Coming to the ring first accompanied by Stan "Sex Machine" Vickers, weighing in at 266 pounds, George "The Greatone" Goldstein. (Goldstein and Vickers make their way to the ring as "Nobody Likes Me" by the Dwarves plays over the PA.) (As Dueling Banjos begins playing) Knight: And his opponent, from the Backwoods of West Virginia, weighing in at 350 pounds, accompanied by his brother Dale, Jerry "The Traveler" Travis Morrin: Stan Vickers is holding a briefcase, I wonder what that's all about. Goldstein: Traveler, I'd like to make a deal with you. There is $25,000 in this briefcase, and you can have every last cent of it if you do one thing for me. Mr. Vickers here is going to take that briefcase into the back and you are going to follow him and take a countout. When the ref reaches 10 and youíre not in the ring, you get all 25 grand. What do you say? (The Travis brothers whisper to each other) Inman: He can't possibly be considering this offer? (Traveler nods his approval and follows Vickers to the back. The ref starts his count and reaches 10.) Knight: Here is your winner as a result of a countout, George "The Greatone" Goldstein! Morrin: I can't believe somebody would stoop to such a level. Knox: There goes the commercial light, we'll be right back. Are you a free agent looking for a fed? Are you a fed looking for some free agents? Would you like your Rping ability critiqued? Do you just want to talk wrestling? If you answered yes to any of the above, then you need to check out the 2 hottest newsgroups on the web. Check out rec.sport.pro-wrestling for all your latest news, rumors, and pro wrestling gossip. If it's fantasy wrestling you like then check out rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy where you can find dozens of feds looking for members and plenty of free agents and Rpers looking for feds. This message brought to you by the House of Champions Family. Morrin: We're back here in the sold out Louisiana Superdome in New Orleans, Louisiana where we've just witnessed two of the best matches the web has to offer and we aren't through yet. Still to come, for the first time anywhere, a Lights Out Double Hell Exploding Barbed Wire Match between Ah'riman and Cherokee, two of the hardestcore extreme wrestlers I've ever seen. Knox: This Cherokee chump claims to have held every Hardcore and Extreme belt he's ever challenged for so you know he must be willing to take a fall through most anything. Inman: Well I've seen this Ah'riman guy practicing and for a smaller wrestler he sure does back quite a wallup. This won't be any easy match for either man here tonight in the Big Easy.I'm sure the Heavyweight title is on both of their minds but they do both seem set on that Extreme Title. Morrin: If they want it then they both will have to wait until the May supercard where President Gary has some outrageous plan concocted to determine the Extreme champ but in the meantime we have a Supercard coming up in just under a month from now. (Camera fades off) Clear your calendars. (clinking sound) Call your local service provider and make sure your internet charges are paid up (clinking sound) because the Lord is coming! Sunday Night April 25th live from the Houston Astrodome, House of Champions Wrestling and When Worlds Collide proudly present: HCW brings to you not one but two rings of high flying butt kicking action where a special Royal Rumble will decide the first Heavyweight Champion of HCW. Be there or else! Inman: That's Lord of the Rings, sure to be one of the greatest SuperCards on the net Youíd better be there or Gabe here will have to enroll you into the Colossus School of Hard Knox. Knox: Thatís right Kyle. It's in less than 4 weeks, and I know where each and every one of you are. Morrin: That's enough you 2 let's get back to the action. Shadow vs. Swamp Thing ("Devil Goes Down to Georgia" comes on as Swamp Thing appears from the tunnel.) Knight: Coming down the aisle weighing in at 280 pounds from the Louisiana Swamps, Swamp Thing! (Moderate pop for Swamp Thing who comes out in his dingy overalls and toothless grin.) (The lights go out and the blue light comes up on the ring and another on the tunnel as the eerie Batman music plays and a spotlight shoots around the arena. A cloud of smoke crawls up around the ring and when it clears the lights come back up to reveal Shadow standing in the ring. Swamp Thing cowers in the corner and pulls the ref down with him not letting him up. Inman: Look Gabe, it's your good buddy Shadow. Knox: It sure is and if you'll excuse me gentlemen, I have business to attend to. (Knox jumps out of his chair and takes it into the ring with him. He sneaks up behind Shadow and levels him with a chair shot across the back. Colossus picks him up and whips him into the ropes, catches him on the run into a Gorilla Press and delivers a Gorilla Bomb to Shadow on to the chair. He runs back out of the ring and takes his seat) Morrin: What the hell was that? Inman: Swamp Thing out of the corner and applying a crippler crossface to Shadow. Knox: He calls it the Crocodile Smile. Inman: Whatever Morrin:Whatever it is Shadow isn't answering the ref and this one is over already. Shadow is out cold Knight: Here is your winner, Swaaammmp Thiiinnng! Inman: I hope your happy. We were on a roll of great matches and you had to go and ruin that one didn't you? Knox: What do you mean ruin it? I thought it was a great match. Swamp Thing clearly dominated from start to finish. Morrin: I can't believe you. We've got to go to commercial.
