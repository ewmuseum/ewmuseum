House of Champions Wrestling: Everything You Need to Know

29 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here is all the info you could possibly need about House of Champions
Wrestling I. Wrestlers- Mostly original, we do accept real wrestlers but none have joined as of yet. Every handler can manage up to 3 wrestlers if they like. II. Cards- ARE NOT SIMMED, they are all hand written by the card writing staff and will be pretty amazing. Our weekly card is Wednesday Night
WarFair which premieres this week. We will also have one monthly supercard starting on April 25th with Lord of the Rings. III. Matches- We are open to any kind of match you could possibly want. If your wrestler wants a special match all you have to do is ask and clarify the details and the match is yours. No match is too extreme. IV. Roleplaying- RPing is the key to your success in HCW. We aren't looking for quantity so much as we are looking for good quality RPs. Impress me with your words and you can receive a bigger push. RPing is done on our messageboard hosted by InsideTheWeb,com V. Titles- We have 6 belts up for grabs. Heavyweight, North American , International, Extreme, Cruiserweight, and Tag Team. The first 3 will all be decided at Lord of the Rings and the Extreme title is set to be decided at the Supercard in May. VI. PPV- Since this is the internet and not TV I will not call them PPVs they will be called what they are and that is SuperCards. VII. Character Limits- The only limits we have on your wrestlers is that they cannot be more than 7' tall or more than 500 lbs. VIII. Post applying requirements- After accepting your application you will be asked to submit a bachground of your wrestler and their entrance. This way you have your own personalized entrance for your wrestler into the ring. IX. Owner INvolvement- I do not intend to be a McMahon or Bischoff and get involved in the fed beyond controlling it. There will only be a
Corporation if you form your own stable and name it that. I have a
Commissioner who will be the closest thing to that happening. X. Miscellaneous- That's all I can think of. Any other questions can be directed to our website at http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Pressbox/6783 or emailed to the HCW mailbox at [name withheld] Gary
