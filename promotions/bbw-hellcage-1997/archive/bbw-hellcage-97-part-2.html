BBW Hellcage '97 part 2

Apr 23, 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<<camera fades back into Waterfront park and at the commentating table.>> RJ: OK fans lets get to our next match. Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, accompanied to the ring by Arch Angel, please welcome, Tom B. Stone!!!!! <<crowd cheers>>. His opponent, accompanied to the ring by Havoc, Wild! A fairly even match between these two. Arch Angel stays at ringside throughout the match, and argues with Wild on several occasions. As Tom whips Wild into the ropes, Arch trips Wild, who falls crashing to the mat. Tom then picks up James and nails a swinging neckbreaker. Three seconds later, Tom is your winner. After the match Havoc tells Wild that his stay as a 4 demon is over. Wild accepts in great dignity and walks back to the dressing room. Havoc proceeds back also. TE: Well I guess Wild is no more a 4 Demon. I wonder who will be the next demons. RJ: Lets get back to the ring. Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen our next match is scheduled for 1 fall and is for the BBW World Tag chamionship. The challengers, accompanied to the ring by Arch Angel, The Darkriders, Hurricane and Tusk. There opponents, the BBW Tag Champions, The Darkside, Grimm and Reaper!!!!!! Rj: Now joining us for commentary is Josh Davis JD: Grimm & Reaper bum rush the ring! TE: They what? JD: You heard me! <<bell rings>> TE: The Darkriders stand their ground and exchange blows with the arriving Force. RJ:Grimm plows Tusk into the corner, and Reaper does likewise to Hurricane in the opposite corner. Reaper & Grimm both Irish whip The Darkside out of their respective corners and straight towards each other. JD: Hurricane leap-frogs Tusk, and the darkriders both hit the The darkside with a flying corner splash on the opposite sides of the ring. The Darkside now whip Grimm & reaper into each other. RJ: They crash into each other, full speed, in the center of the ring! They both fall to the mat and roll out of opposite sides of the ring! The crowd pops for the Darkriders! JD: Tusk charges, flies over the top, and catches Grimm by surprise with a cross body-block! Tusk stomps on Grimm for good measure, then picks him up and rolls him back into the ring for the waiting Hurricane to deal with. RJ: Terry presses the discombobulated Grimm against the ropes, hits a standing knee to the gut, and whips Grimm into the opposite ropes. TE:Hurricane catches Grimm with a dropkick on the rebound. The darkside has not had a good start in this match-up! RJ: Certainly not, Tim! JD: You just wait and see, non-believer! TE: That I will, JD, that I will. JD: In the ring, Grimm gets to his feet, only to get caught up in the side headlock of Hurricane. RJ: Grimm points towards the back of Hurricanes wrestling tights, claiming to the ref that there is a foreign object. As the referee checks up on this, Grimm reaches his arm up to the face of Hurricane and presses back hard on the nose. TE: The noses goes, as they say! Hurricanes head shoots back and Grimm uses this change in leverage to clasp around the back-leaning Hurricanes head and hit a reverse DDT!Grimm covers. RJ: 1... 2.... Quick kickout at two by Hurricane. Grimm drags him up and pulls him into the Darksides corner. Grimm tags out to Reaper, but holds Hurricane in a front facelock as reaper climbs the ropes. Reaper comes off with a double axehandle blow to the back of Hurricane, who crumples to the mat. Both members of theDarkside start stomping on Hurricane, and Tusk rushes in to make a save. The referee turns to stop the charging Tusk. I can't believe this! Get the darkside out of there! JD: Hurricane kicks Grimm in the knee from the mat. As Grimm hollers, the referee turns, and Tusk uses the opportunity to brush by him and rush Grimm with a flying clothesline that takes them both over the top rope and out onto the floor. RJ: HUrricane, meanwhile, gets to his feet and goes blow-for-blow with reaper. Hurricane ducks a swing from reaper, shoots in, and scoops Reaper up in a fireman's carry. TE: Tusk and Grimm both hobble to their feet at ringside as Hurricane slams reaper from the fireman's carry. RJ:Grimm rushes Tusk with a knee to the gut and follows up with an elbow to the back of the neck. Grimm grabs Tusk by his hair and trucks and shoots him into the ring steps. JD: While Grimm dominates on the outside, Hurricane has the advantage over reaper inside, currently holding him in a chinlock. RJ: grimm looks at the fallen Tusk, who is motionless on the outside, and starts to return to his corner, seeing his work is done. Grimm raises his chair above his head to take a shot on Tusk. Hurricane sees this about to happen and releases his hold on Reaper to grab the chair out of grimms hands from inside the ring. RJ: Grimm, meanwhile, has returned to his corner. Reaper makes the tag, then lifts Hurricane up and holds him arms back. The referee turns to tell Reaper to get out of there. TE: Whoa! Out of nowhere, Tusk appeared behind Grimm and got a clear chair shot to his head. A limp grimm rolls through the ropes and falls into the ring. RJ: reaper sees this and goes totally bonkers, trying to tell the referee, but the referee just continues harassing him. A frustrated Reaper exits the ring, and Hurricane goes for a cover. 1... 2.... No! Reaper pulled Grimm's foot onto the bottom rope, breaking the count. TE: Tusk jerks Reaper's feet off of the apron, and reaper sails face-first into the ring apron. Tusk dives into the ring to... Uh oh. RJ: What? JD: Here come the Stars RJ: You're right! BIlly Evans, and Bart Star are all here, making their way towards ringside! The Darkriders ignore Grimm in the ring and start a verbal spat with the Stars. JD:Grimm is trying to regain his senses. He rises and slowly wobbles towards the Darkriders, who don't see him coming. RJ: Tusk turns just in the nick of time and gives Grimm a boot to the gut. Tusk, the illegal man, then steps out of the ring as Hurricane hooks him up for a German suplex. TE: The dazed Grimm manages to hit a go-behind reversal, and hits a German suplex of his own. The referee counts. RJ: 1... 2.... 3? Yes, Hurricane kicks out just a second too late, and it's over just like that! JD: I was right, still champs, the Darkside! RJ: What a match! the best tag match ever in the BBW!
