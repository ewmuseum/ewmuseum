Just as Amp is about to enter the ring, Sid grabs him and pummels him outside the ring. Meanwhile, Sn goes after MIC. Finally, the referee restores order, and KN and Amp go to their respective corners. The Kevin hall and SCott Nach retain power until Amp gets the ring bell and clobbers KH. MIC proceeds to do a number on SH. Amp wants to be tagged in, but MIC wants to stay in. He wants to prove to everybody that he can defeat SN. He goes up for a moonsault, SN sits up and MIC eats canvas. SN slowly makes his way to the corner to tag KH, but Amp runs in and attacks him. As the referee escorts AMP back to the apron, Amp gives MIC a look, a "don't interfere" look. Amp grabs SN and nails him with a vicious power bomb. He goes for the cover, and Mic runs in and pulls him off! Mic and Amp start arguing, and Mic nails Amp with a DDT. SN Tages in KH. KH climbs into the ring, and grabs Mic by the throat. Chokeslam!
