Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This Bad Boys of Wrestling's entire surviving record is a single night: Hellcage '97, staged 23 April 1997 before a sold-out Waterfront Park crowd of more than 20,000. The broadcast opened on an angle rather than a match — Ric Payne returning to reform the stable MOD, bringing in a tag team called New Breed, one of them introduced on air as the son of a former BBW co-president.

The card's own centerpiece was its first-ever HellCage match: 25 men locked in a 20-foot steel cage, weapons allowed, fighting to be the last one thrown over the top to become the new Intercontinental Champion. A mystery entrant announced only as Scorpion won it, edging out Silver Bullet in the final elimination by lifting him gently over the top rope instead of finishing him off with the bat still in his hand, then walking straight to the back without celebrating.

The main event, a three-way dance for the World Heavyweight Championship, brought the BBW Internet Champion, “Farmdawg” Brian Farmer, into the ring against champion Havoc and a challenger billed as the World Warrior, who entered by zip line from the scoreboard. Havoc let go of a clean pin on a knocked-out World Warrior rather than take a win he judged cheap, and the World Warrior answered by rolling Havoc up for the title instead. Scorpion returned to attack all three men with a chair before being run off, and the night closed with Havoc handing the World Heavyweight belt to the new champion and inviting him, Farmer, and Silver Bullet to join his own stable, the 4 Demons, all four raising their hands together to end the broadcast.

No further document survives past that one night in Trenton, New Jersey.
