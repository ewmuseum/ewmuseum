"VWF Ring Report 17/07/95"

17 July 1995
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello everybody and welcome to the first installment of VWF Ring Report. I'm your host [name withheld] and every once in a while I will be doing these Ring
Reports to keep both VWF fans and competitors up to date on the happenings in the VWF. This Ring Report covers a card that happened last night at the
Boston Garden. Here we go... VWF HOUSE SHOW BOSTON GARDEN 16/07/95 Hooligan def Jerry "Goon" Gordan Hooligan made his VWF debut here against a man who was successful in his VWF debut on the first tournament. But Jerry Gordan was defeated by an awesome newcomer who showed that he may be ready to climb the ladder to the championship in a hurry.
Hawk "The KillerFragger" def "The Sniper" Jack Terom Hawk was recently eliminated out of the VWF championship tournament by Nightstick but he bounced back in a big way against this newcomer. Hawk made him submit in his awesome "Alienator".
The Wu-Tang Clan def The Lazer Warriors In the first ever VWF Tag-team matchup the Wu-Tang Clan consisting of RZA and Gza defeated the Lazer Warriors consisting of the GIANT Q-Zar and his partner the Atomic Warrior by a pinfall.
Tommy Angle def "Black Night" Eric Knight Tommy Angle a newcomer who considers himself to be a very lucky individual defeated another newcomer "Black Knight" Eric Knight with his finisher "The
Even Money" which is basically a cobra clutch. Eric Knight did put on an impressive display however Tommy Angle proved to be too much for him in the end.
Crimson Skull def Wild Redneck Wild Redneck became the first VWF wrestler to go 0-2 when he was defeated by an impressive newcomer named Crimson Skull. Crimson Skull made Wild Redneck submit in his awesome SKULL CRUSHER.
Gemini def The Yakuza And in the final match of the evening, The identical masked duo known as Gemini defeated The Yakuza (Kendo and Kitana) by a pinfall after a switch while the referee had his back turned.
I hope you enjoyed the first installment of VWF Ring Report and i'll be seeing you later in the week with either another installment of Ring Report or the next tournament. So long everybody!
[name withheld]
