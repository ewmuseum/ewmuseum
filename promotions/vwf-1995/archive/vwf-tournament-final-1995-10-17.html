"VWF Tournament Final"

17 October 1995
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scott:Welcome back once again to the VWF tournament! Last time out Captain
Flagg scored an amazing pin over Akira Hashimoto but was attacked by Kendo and Kitana-The YAKUZA!!! Is Captain Flagg ready to face a well rested Jean
Demort?
Jacobs:I spoke with Captain Fag and he said that he is okay to fight tonight. However, I fully expect Jean Demort to be crowned the first VWF champion tonight.
Scott:We'll see about that, tonight the Captain has The Ultimate Soldier and
The Patriot at his side to prevent any interference!
Jacobs:Jean Demort isn't going to need interference. He's going to outWRESTLE the big dumb Captain.
"The French Fury" Jean Demort vs. Captain Flagg w/Uncle Sam,Ultimate Soldier,The Patriot
Jean Demort went to work right away on Captain Flagg and he NAILED him with his finisher, the cradle suplex, at the 3 minute mark. Captain Flagg somehow managed to kick out. It became obvious that Captain Flagg couldn't possibly win this match. The Captain tried desperately to fight back, but he was too tired and just too sore to match Jean Demort. Demort finally finished the
Captain with a cradle suplex to capture the VWF title.
WINNER AND NEW VWF HEAVWEIGHT CHAMPION: "THE FRENCH FURY" JEAN DEMORT (8:43) Scott:I can't believe the Captain had to wrestle this match! He could've beaten Demort! Jacobs:Way to be impartial, you stupid PUTZ! Jean Demort is going to make a
GREAT champion.
Scott:We'll be seeing you later everybody! ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
