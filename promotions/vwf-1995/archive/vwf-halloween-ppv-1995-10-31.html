"VWF Halloween PPV part1"

31 October 1995
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scott:Welcome everybody to the much anticipated VWF Halloween PPV. Joining me in the commentary booth are "Fabulous" Fred Jacobs and VWF President Mark
Rose. Welcome Mark, to your first VWF broadcast. Rose:Thanks Matt. I have two changes to make on tonight's card, first of all, Freak o' Nature has refused the loser leaves town stipulation. And second of all he has chosen what the second fall will be against Akira
Hashimoto. He calls it the match of immortality. Both men will be in a steel cage for 60 minutes. The man who scores the most falls in this time will win the 2nd fall of the matchup.
Jacobs:Can I say something here? Rose:Go ahead. Jacobs:What about the third fall? Rose:I will decide the third fall if it is necessary. Scott:There you have it folks, let's go to the action. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Royal Rumble match for a world title shot featuring: Ravage Morman Faith Jose "Raging Bull" Hernandez John Winddancer "Thunderstrike" Chris Blaze Super American T.C. Cannon Youth Blazer "Hitman" Nick Jerman [name withheld] Masked Sentinel Cactus Kid Soultaker Shermon "The Preacher" Morse Harry "The Hammer" Holmes The first two would be Shermon Morse and Chris Blaze. Morse started quickly pounding Chris Blaze and then he locked him in the cross face chicken wing.
The Chicken wing stayed on until wrestler #3 Super American came down and broke the hold. Blaze and Super American double teamed Shermon Morse but they could not toss him out. The next man in would be Cactus Kid. Cactus Kid came to the Preacher's aid and He took on Super American. Morse locked the
Chicken Wing back on Chris Blaze. Super American eliminated Cactus Kid and the next man in would be Ravage. Ravage jumped the Super American and the
Super American found himself in a heap of trouble being triple teamed. They could not eliminate the big guy and the next man to hit the ring would be
Jose "Raging Bull" Hernandez. The crazy Hernandez came to the ring with brass knuckles on each hand. He KO'ed Ravage, Morse, and Blaze. Super
American thought Hernandez came to his aid but Jose KOed him as well. Hernandez jumped out over the top rope and left the building. All four men lay unconcious as Soultaker hit the ring. Soultaker threw out Chris Blaze and Ravage. Shermon Morse procured the Chicken Wing on Soultaker but Super
American broke it and pounded on Morse. Morman Faith came to Shermon Morse's aid and it became a tag team match of sorts. The next guy in would be John
Winddancer and he came to Super American's aid. Winddancer threw out Shermon Morse and he and Super American double teamed Morman Faith. Youth Blazer entered next and he was tossed out quickly by the Super American. The next guy would be T.C. Cannon and he attacked Super American and John Winddancer.
He threw out Super American and and began battling John Winddancer. Morman Faith attacked T.C. Cannon and threw him out. Morman Faith and John Winddancer went toe-to-toe until the Masked Sentinel came to the ring and helped Winddancer. The two tossed out Morman Faith and began battling each other. Harry Holmes entered next and he was content to watch the battle.
Winddancer didn't like that idea so he jumped Harry Holmes and Masked Sentinel helped him. [name withheld] hit the ring next and he tossed out the
Masked Sentinel. Winddancer grabbed [name withheld] and he attempted to throw him out but he could not. Finally the last man, Hitman Nick Jerman, came to the ring and he battled Harry Holmes. Jerman threw Holmes out and helped
Winddancer against [name withheld]. Sherrick somehow managed to toss out Nick
Jerman. That left just two men, [name withheld] and John Winddancer.
Winddancer started with the upper hand and he tossed Sherrick over the top. Winddancer thought he had won but Sherrick did not hit the floor. Winddancer saw this in time and when Sherrick ran at him, he powerslammed [name withheld]
HARD to the mat. Winddancer got overconfident however and Sherrick took the advantage after he caught Winddancer on an attempted tomahawk chop. Sherrick surprised Winddancer and threw him out over the top rope to claim the victory and title against the VWF World Champion.
Winner-[name withheld] (45:36) Freak o' Nature vs. Akira Hashimoto 1st fall Taipei Death Match Both men had their hands wrapped in tape, dipped in glue and rolled in broken glass for this one. Both men went absolutely nuts and bloodied each other quickly with the glass. Hashimoto took the upper hand and locked an asian nerve hold for a while. Freak broke the hold and nailed Hashimoto with a clothesline. Freak o' Nature power bombed Hashimoto and Hashimoto managed to get up at the count of eight. Freak jumped out of the ring and grabbed a chair. He nailed Akira with the chair and then power bombed him on the chair. Hashimoto failed to answer the ten count and he lost the first fall of this marathon 2 out of 3 fall match.
Winner of first fall--Freak o' Nature (15:27) "The Matmaster" Matt Masters vs. Jackson Savage Masters frustrated Savage early on with several armdrag takedowns. Masters then locked on a chinlock and he kneed Savage several times in the back.
Masters broke the chinlock and walked on Jackson Savage's back. Masters then threw Jackson Savage off the ropes and hit him with an awesome elbow to the head. Masters clamped on an indian deathlock and Savage looked to be in serious trouble. Savage reached the ropes and while the ref broke the hold,
Savage took a cheap shot on Masters. Savage then moonsaulted onto Masters from the second rope. He picked Masters up off of the mat and threw him into the corner for a handspring elbow. Masters rolled out of the ring to regain his senses and Savage ran off of the ropes and leaped out on top of him.
Savage threw Masters back into the ring. Masters surprised Savage and rolled him up but only managed to get a two count. Masters locked Savage in a cross face chicken wing. Savage broke the hold with a kick below the belt. Savage threw Masters off of the ropes again and nailed him with a spinning wheel kick. He rolled Masters onto his back and then moonsaulted onto his back. He covered Masters but only got a two count. He then put Masters on top rope and appeared to be going for a superplex. But instead he jumped on his shoulders and rolled Masters back to the mat into a victory roll for the pin.
Winner---Jackson Savage (35:12) Original New Jersey Boys vs. US Invasion for the Tag Team titles On their way to the ring, the US invasion was attacked by Elemental Force. Elemental Force pounded the US Invasion while the Original New Jersey Boys merely watched and cheered. They splashed the Patriot's leg several times until Captain Flagg and Super American came to the rescue. As a result of the attack, The US Invasion forfeited the match and the titles were given to
DWA superstars Vinnie and Tony, the Original New Jersey Boys. THE WINNERS BY FORFEIT AND VWF WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS: VINNIE AND TONY THE ORIGINAL NEW JERSEY BOYS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Rose: As a result of their actions on the first part of this card,Elemental Force will be suspended for two weeks and fined $10,000 dollars EACH. Jose "Raging Bull" Hernandez will be suspended for 1 week and fined $5000. This also means that Super American will face a new opponent on Thursday.
