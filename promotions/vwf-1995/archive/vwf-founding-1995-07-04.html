I'm starting a new fed called the VWF. Outcomes will be decided by Wrestling Simulator 5.0 and write-ups will be done by co-fedheads (myself and my brother Christian). The first eight entries will be entered into the tournament for the heavyweight title!
All you have to do to join is e-mail the following application to me at: [detail withheld] Name: E-mail address: Wrestler's name: Wrestler's Hometown: Wrestler's Height: Wrestler's Weight: Theme music (optional): Finishing move (describe if necessary): Brief description of wrestler (just put his attitude, the types of moves he uses, face/heel etc.):
Finally, your wrestler will have 11 attributes. Out of the eleven attributes, you can choose a primary and secondary specialty which will be graded 10 and 8 respectively. Then with your last nine attributes, you can divide thirty points among them as you please. BUT, you canNOT add these thirty points to your primary or secondary specialtys. The attributes are as follows:
1)Strength 2)Toughness 3)Conditioning 4)Intelligence 5)Cheating 6)Brawling 7)Technical 8)Mat Wrestling 9)Aerial 10)Quickness 11)Teamwork (Tag Teams can formed as soon I get a few wrestler's into the fed) Here is an Example of how I want a wrestler to be created: Name: Joe Blow E-mail:[detail withheld] Wresler's name: Beavis Height:5'5" Weight:98 lbs Theme Music:The Beavis and Butthead Theme Finishing Move:The Buttmunch (Same as Doink's Whoopee cushion) 1)Strength 10- primary 2)Toughness 8- secondary 3)Conditioning 5 4)Intelligence 1 5)Cheating 3 6)Brawling 7 7)Technical 4 8)Mat Wrestling 3 9)Aerial 2 10)Quickness 2 11)Teamwork 3 Simple enough! Send Your entries now! Right now there is a limit of two wrestler's per person.
