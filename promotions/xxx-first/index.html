The independent, pre-acquisition life of "the 1st XXX" — Bobby Williams' own promotion, which he took by winning the rights to it and sold on to Pres Burns to be reopened as a farm efed; a wing with three wrestlers, one dated event, no document of its own, and two of the fed's own bios that cannot agree on what year it changed hands. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Not to be confused with the CWF-Pro-era “XXX” farm efed (2006-2007, already held as part of the CWF-Pro wing's own developmental-league title system) — this is the earlier, genuinely independent XXX, owned by Bobby Williams himself before Pres Burns ever bought it. Williams' own bio states it plainly, out of kayfabe: he wrestled there from 2000, the promotion closed in November 2002, and he “won the rights to the company” at that point — “the XXX is now his and his alone.” The Flash Brothers' own bio corroborates the same shape from the outside: “The 1st XXX was not own by Pres Burns. Untill he bought it... after it had shut down. He reopened it as a farm efed for the cwf.pro.”

Exactly when Burns bought it is a real, unresolved disagreement between two of the acquiring fed's own served bios. The Flash Brothers' own account dates the sale to 2003, right on the heels of the 2002 closure. Williams' own account instead dates it to 2005. Both are recorded here; neither is preferred. Whichever year is right, the outcome both bios agree on is the same: Burns reopened the promotion as CWF-Pro's own farm efed, which ran 2006-2007 under his own name before he closed it again — a run that already belongs to the CWF-Pro wing, not this one.

No independent-era document of the 1st XXX survives on its own terms — no site, no roster page, and no title ledger of its own (the five XXX-branded ledgers already held under CWF-Pro are all dated uniformly to the 2006-2007 farm era and carry none of the CSW wing's own acquisition-pivot language). This wing exists entirely as three career fragments inside CWF-Pro's own served bios — Bobby Williams, his brother Bryan Williams, and the Flash Brothers — the only three names the record places inside the 1st XXX by name.

One dated event

It is the whole of this wing's event record, and it comes from a line the accession pass did not carry. The Flash Brothers' own Earlier Career section: “They started out in 1997 as rookies in a few efeds winning minor titles in the PWE, JJP, GWC, & RFP. They left the XXX efed after talking with R@ven in 2001.” R@ven was the buyer's own talent scout, and the next section of the same page says what the talking was for — they were set to sign in 2001, and the deal died when the buyer's own promotion closed instead. So the independent XXX was running, with a main-event tag team worth poaching, two years before the earlier of the two years its sale is dated to. Nothing else in the surviving record carries a date inside this wing at all.

Who owned it: three accounts, two eras

Bobby Williams says he did — he took the rights when the company folded in November 2002, and “the XXX is now his and his alone.” Bryan Williams' own bio names someone else entirely: “Blain Williams was the ower of the XXX and ran the company for Pres Burns as a farm efed” — the brothers' own father, who also trained them both. That second passage is explicitly about the era after the sale, which is why the two can both be true. The reading is named here and not adopted, because neither page says it, and Blain Williams is recorded rather than given a place on this roster: his only stated role belongs to the farm era, on the other side of the buyout.

What does not survive — and what that was checked against

This wing holds no document of its own. That is now a checked statement rather than an assumed one. The vault was read directly on disk: the corpus contains exactly five XXX-named HTML files, all five are the farm-era ledgers, all five are already served, and there is no sixth.

The stronger test is a count. Every XXX championship credit any of these three entities carries was matched, one at a time, against those farm-era ledgers — Bobby Williams' three XXX Championships, five XXX 2nd Championships and one tag title; Bryan Williams' four and two; the Flash Brothers' fifteen World Tag Team reigns and two 2nd Tag Team reigns. All twelve credits reconcile exactly, several against the ledgers' own summary tables. Nothing is left over. Whatever championships the independent XXX crowned, not one of them survives anywhere in the record — which is a different and more useful finding than simply not having looked.

A fourth name goes unresolved on purpose. The Flash Brothers name “the main tag team in the XXX with Bryan Williams, Bobby Williams and Star” — and Jason Star's own bio dates his entire XXX involvement to a 2006 developmental contract, after the sale. The loose “Star” is left standing rather than read onto him.
