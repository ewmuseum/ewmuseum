A fantasy-roster federation on the fwrestling.com hub whose own title histories record its PWX Championship, Hybrid Championship and Tag Team Championship from September 2005 to January 2010, through a closure it announced in December 2007 and a return in the autumn of 2009. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Pro Wrestling X called its front page “The Revolution Reborn” and counted its visitors “Since August 2005”. When its hub-hosted site was read in September 2026 it held only the news, an event schedule and three title pages; its roster, forum, information and application pages sat on an Invision Free board that the site links to and that is not held. Captures of the same site from 2007 and 2008 also carry its show results and a show archive, read here only in part.

What is served here is those pages. The three title histories name every change from 2 September 2005, when John Pariah won the Hybrid Championship at an Adrenaline show, to January 2010: 19 reigns of the PWX Championship, 24 of the Hybrid Championship and 21 numbered tag reigns, with events from Adrenaline 14 to Adrenaline 83, Do Or Die: The 100th Show on 29 July 2007 and Civil War on 26 August 2007. All three record the same stop: PWX announced its closure in December 2007, the titles were vacated, and nothing in the histories changes hands again until Never Say Die 2009 on 18 October 2009. The schedule lists an Adrenaline taping for September 2009, and the last dated item is a news post of 25 January 2010 welcoming visitors to a new PWXOnline.net.
