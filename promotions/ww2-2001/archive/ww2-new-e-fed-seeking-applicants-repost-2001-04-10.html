If you want to sign up, fill out the application for you wrestler or tag team below and send it to: [detail withheld] To find out more about whether you'd want to sign up for WW2 either check out the website at http://www.ww2wrestling.com/ or read on for a brief summary of the fed. The website is pretty basic graphics wise, but I feel it does it's job well. WW2 is a booked, email based federation for original e-wrestlers and tag teams. Wins and losses are decided on the following: 1. Your commitment to the league. If you stay with the fed for ages and lack the promo skills of some of your fellow wrestlers, your loyalty will be rewarded with wins over newer people who might normally beat you due to better flashes. This also prevents some new guy waltzing in and taking the World title, regardless if he's better than the rest of the fed. 2. The quality of your flashes/interviews. WW2 is primarily booked on the quality of the flashes/interviews you send in for your wrestler, the more entertaining your promo the more likely you are to win. You need send only one in per match, but if you choose to send in more than one, I will use the best one as a guide to see how well you do. Quantity does not matter, you can send in hundreds of promos, but if the all suck you won't win, but people who get into a back and forth feud with their opponents will be rewarded with better card placement in writing and title shots. So the more you get involved the better your career in WW2. Of course if you don't send in any flashes/interviews you won't win either. 3. If you have a strat/angle. If you've got an idea for an angle for your wrestler, be it a simple run in or something more complicated, then send in a strat to let me know. I can give you the win if it furthers the WW2 storylines. If you start sending in pointless ones they won't help you and might not get used. For example running in on the final match of a feud you have nothing to do with. 4. If I feel a feud will be forwarded by a win I might give someone a win even if the shouldn't have got it for storyline purposes. WW2 will have one card a week. The card will be totally hand written, with most of the matches in summary format, although of the matches will be fully written. If you want to see the writing style for the fed, there is a sample match at http://www.ww2wrestling.com/samplematch.html The fed will be fairly slow paced. We'll have one summary card a week with a full match on it and every two months I'll have a Supercard where I'll write up every match of the card and the big Title matches will take place. This is to ensure the workload on me is of a level that won't burn me out, we don 't want the fed closing because I get fed up with writing do we? All Flashes are sent to the federation and are delivered to the players in the fed by email in packets. This is to prevent spamming of any kind. Hopefully that all sounds good.
