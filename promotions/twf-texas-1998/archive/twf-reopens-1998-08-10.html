Texas Wrestling Federation Reopens, 10 August 1998

10 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RSPWF, That's right the Texas Wrestling Federation has reopened. Check out the homepage at http://members.aol.com/Tex1123/index.html I hope you like it and fill out an app. Thanks. 1.Only original wrestlers. In other words, no "Hitmans" or "Stone Colds", and nobody saying "And that's the bottom line" or "Rest in peace". 2.The matches are done on a simulator, so I don't have a say in the outcome. Therefore, don't complain to me about losing any matches. The only thing that I can do is give one wrestler a push for the match if they clearly out roleplay their opponent. The Simulator I use is Zues. I will not hear any complaints. 3.I don't do the rankings either, the simulator does that also. So, again, don't complain to me if you think that you should be higher than you are. 4.There will be one card a week called Saturday Night Sizzler. There can be as many matches as needed. The Week of the PPV, there well also be a Wednesday card called War. 5.There will be 2 singles belts at first: TWF World Title and TWF Texas Heavyweigh Title. There will be 1 tag team belt at first. 6.All roleplays will be sent to me, and I will mail them out to everyone in the league. They should be entitled TWF Rants and Raves or something like that. 7.If you want to challenge someone or answer a challenge, do it in roleplay form. If I see one wrestler challenge another, and the other then accepts, I'll schedule the match for the first show where both of them are free. When you send in a challage or roleplay for the Subject write who you Challage or accept. 8.Swearing is allowed in roleplays, but no sexual connotations, please. If you start to push it, I'll first give you a warning, and if you don't tone it down you'll be suspended for a week. If it still continues, you'll be kicked out of the league. And you also don't have to swear every other word, either. I don't see how it makes a roleplay any better, but that's just my opinion. A few swears can add some emphasis, but too many clog it down. 9.If you want to interfere in a match, e-mail me in advance. As far in advance as possible, please. And don't just say, "I want to interfere in this match." Tell me exactly what you want to do, like "I want to interfere in (for example)the Shawn Michaels/Bret Hart match. I want to come down to ringside with a steel chair and attack Bret Hart and then run off into the crowd before the ref can see me." And interference does not guarantee that the wrestler who you attack will lose. They could win by DQ, or just win. The simulator determines it all. 10.In order to challenge for the World Title, you must be in the top 5, and for the Texas Heavyweight Title in the top 10 For the Tag Team Titles, you have to be top 5. 11.Each title must be defended at every Pay-Per View, unless the champion is challenging for another belt. Same goes for the Tag Team Titles. The Texas Heavyweight Title is to be defended at every PPV. 12. Each person is allow to control one Single and one Tag Team. Tag Team Wrestlers can interact in the singles division. Thanks [name withheld] Prez! of the TWF! http://members.aol.com/Tex1123/index.html JR Kidd
