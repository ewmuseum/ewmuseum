UJP — Card

Fighting Spirits, Part One

14 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The opening graphics, consisting of the UJP logo spinning in circles, are overlaid to the scene, which is literally superimposed behind it.
Spectators are filled to the brim of the arena, packed against eachother, madly cheering as fireworks explode from the ringposts at ringside. The camera pans upwards, giving an overhead shot of the ring. Emblazoned in the center of the ring is the UJP logo, surrounded by a white canvas. The red ropes are sufficiently tighten to the turnbuckles. Another blast of fireworks explodes from the ringposts, flying right towards the camera, which cuts away at the last second. The scene is then filled of the announcer's table, with
"The Great" Baba Matsouko sitting behind the desk. He's quite a rotund man, mainly due to the years out of the sport. His face his round and slightly pudgy, with a single scar running the length of his cheek. His overall appearance is very professional as he spreads his arms wide and addresses the camera.]
BM: Konnichiwa puroresu otakus! It's 8pm, we're at Korkuen hall, and this is... U J P FIGHTING SPIRITS!! All-Asian Title Grand Prix Round #1: Road to Victory Ladies and gentlemen, this is the very first card for none other than
Ultra Japan Pro! Tonight, here on Fighting Spirits on Fighting TV
54!, we have an absolutely great half-hour, jam packed of Puroresu!
You might have noticed that my planned colleague is not here tonight. Terry Roberts, the announcer whom we had hired, has now quit and gone back to America. Therefore, we have a great surprised for all the fans out there! Let's cut to ringside where our President is standing now.
[The camera shot moves from the image of Baba Matsouko to a long shot of the wrestling ring. It begins zooming in and then quickly snaps to a horizontal, standard wrestling view. The President of UJP stands in the center, with the ring announcer, Jin Hayate, off to the left.]
JH: Ladies and gentlemen, it is my pleasure to give the microphone over to the President of the UJP, none other than _Akira Mushashi_!
[The crowd gives Musashi a standing ovation as the microphone is placed in his lands.] AM: All of you wrestling fans, are you ready for the shock of your life! Tonight, due to certain technical and financial difficulties, the announcing partner to none other than "The Great" Baba Matsouko, has left the Ultra Japan Pro!
[A moderate applause goes up for Matsouko, who still has a cult following.] AM: So, without further ado, I'd like to bring out his replacement. [The arena goes dark, and the megatron screen explodes with Kanji lettering, subtitled with English, that says 'Feed the Fear,' then shatters. Another phrase 'The Master of the Tiger Driver 99,'
followed no later by another rapid fire string of explosions 'He' 'Is' 'Your' 'Worst' 'Nightmare.' Suddenly, a beat of ancient drums followed with a screeching heavy metal guitar cuts into the entrance.
"Eternal Warrior" by Asian Lightning begins playing as fireworks explode at the entranceway. The Iron Tiger appears amidst the fireworks display. He then bolts down the aisle, the fireworks following him as tracers, as he finally reaches the ring amidst a chorus of furious cheering. The Iron Tiger wears his trademark mask, alongside his crimson and black attire. His corded muscles enrapture his body like snakes. His momentum carries him between the first and second rope with a flying leap. He rolls quickly across his shoulder blades and leaps to his feet. Pulling his shirt across his body, he lifts it up to show the crowd, with "The Iron Tiger" written across the back. Big face pop! The Iron Tiger then throws his tanktop into the crowd, and they just don't stop cheering.]
AM: Fans of the UJP... [The fans don't stop cheering for the enormously popular high-flyer. Akira can barely be heard over the microphone.] AM: He's the former CLLL champion... [Akira's voice is now nearly totally drowned out by the noise of the crowd.] AM: He's made history in Mexico and America, and now he's right back here in Japan... [Another enormous face pop which practically raises the room off of
Korkuen Hall.] AM: This is possibly the greatest wrestler to grace the ring, he is
_THE IRON TIGER_! [The microphone on the camera crackles from the pure sound echoing off the walls in Korkuen Hall. The microphone is given to The Iron
Tiger.] IT: Ladies and gentlemen, I've hidden behind this mask for a long, long time. It's time for you to meet the man behind the mystery. My name...
[The Iron Tiger reaches up, and loosens the strap on his mask, and slowly pulls it off. His long black hair falls downwards towards his spine, and his perfectly clean-, cut face, bares an energetic youthfulness that couldn't be seen behind the mask. At the site of this, the unbridled fury of 2,150 fans, screaming at one time, drown out every other sound as The Iron Tiger cordially bows.]
IT: ... is Hakiro Sakazaki! [Cut back to Baba Matsouko.] BM: Ladies and gentlemen, this crowd is going absolutely nuts! We're going to take a quick commercial break, we'll be back for more of UJP
Fighting Spirits!
