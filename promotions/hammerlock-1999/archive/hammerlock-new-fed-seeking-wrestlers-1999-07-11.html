Hammerlock — Recruitment

New Fed Seeking Single/Tag Team Wrestlers for Championship Tournament

11 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hammerlock is a regional, booked federation for original e-wrestlers. We're currently recruiting for our championship tournament. It is an e-mail based fed.
If you're interested in signing up, you can find out more about the fed by checking it's webpage at:
http://www.users.globalnet.co.uk/~freeze/ If you're interested fill out the application below and you'll be entered into the tournament to crown the first champions if you're quick.
