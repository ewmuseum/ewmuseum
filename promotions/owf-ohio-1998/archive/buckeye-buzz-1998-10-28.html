He's a true champion, and this match is going to be one for the ages. FJ: I'm still looking at Michael Robinson taking it to a whole other level in this match. Robinson is so ready to get back into the squared circle. He wants this match and he wants the Akron Championship. TS: True. All of this is true. The next two matches are the semi-finals of the OWF Lightweight Title Tournament. The first match will have Chris Forrest taking on Shane Matthews in a great match. Many people see "Sudden Death" Shane Matthews as the crown jewel of the OWF's future. FJ: And you wanna know why? TS: Why? FJ: Because he is. He's a great wrestler and he's a great newcomer. Shane Matthews is one of the best newcomers that the OWF has without a doubt. TS: Well, with all this talk surrounding Shane Matthews, I think that it's making it a whole lot easier on Chris Forrest. Seeing that he's the more than likely underdog, he should have the attitude of having nothing to lose when wrestling. FJ: And that's a great attitude to have, too, Tommy, because if you have nothing lose, nobody will care if you lose the match, anyway. So it's going to be tough for Shane Matthews to counterattack the strategy and the attitude of Chris Forrest. TS: Who wins this match? FJ: Shane Matthews wins the match but in a close match. TS: Okay, Mad Goat will be action as he looks to forget his haunted Goat past by locking up with "the Headliner" Scott Wright. FJ: Well, we all know about the struggling Mad Goat in the EMWO, but he's looking to erase that win/loss column by snatching a victory from "the Headliner" Scott Wright. TS: What do you like about this match, Freddy? FJ: Well, I like the matchup, Tommy. I mean, both of these guys are great wrestlers -- not to mention great high-flyers -- so it's going to be a great treat to watch these two duke it out. TS: Who wins this matchup and why? FJ: Mad Goat steals a victory from Scott Wright, and I do mean steal. It's going to be a great match, but the crowd becomes an important face in the end, making Scott Wright make a mistake, giving Mad Goat the clear path to victory. TS: So you're thinking that Mad Goat gets off to a clean start here in the Ohio Wrestling Federation, eh? FJ: Yep. If he loses this match, he will be scrutinized for a long time about not being able to cut it in Ohio. It would really have a detrimental effect on Mad Goat's confidence and self-esteem. It just might send in him into a downward spiral. TS: Though it seems as if you just might be exaggerating a little bit there, Freddy, I'll take your word for it and say that your opinion counts, just like everyone elses. FJ: I don't think I exaggerated any bit right there. All that I said could become true one day, and that's a fact. TS: Anyway, the next match is a big one. It's the first ever King of Inferno match. And due to the lack of wrestlers, this week's battle royal will only be consisted of seven men and not ten like originally scheduled. Let's go down the list of competitors, Freddy. FJ: Okay, I can do that. TS: "the Natural" Justin Miller. FJ: Yeah, he's okay, but he sucks up to the crowd too much. That's just not my type of wrestler. He's a great wrestler, though, and that's what's going to give him a better at winning this match. It should be interesting to see what he's able to do inside the ring with a whole bunch of great wrestlers. TS: Jack Vars. FJ: Not him again! He's back? I hope he still doesn't have that little tag along Alex Fury because if he does, he's not going to win this match. TS: Strong comments, Freddy; however, I don't think Alex Fury is competing in the Ohio Wrestling Federation. I seriously doubt that he was even invited to this federation. FJ: Well, in the case, Jack Vars is a great wrestler, and I feel as if he should go far in this league. This battle royal should be a great test to see where Jack Vars goes in the OWF. I mean, there are six other great wrestlers in that ring, so he has to be at his best on Sunday, October 31, 1998. TS: Ramacees. FJ: Two words: Suck up. He's another sucker upper, and that's something that I don't like. You just don't suck up to a bunch of guys that will backstab you in a heartbeat. You can suck up to the Furiosos, but that is about it. TS: What about his chances in the battle royal, Freddy? FJ: Well, I'm thinking that he's going to be one of the last men inside the ring but he won't be able to pull out. It'll be a great match, Tommy. TS: "Sadistic" Shane Alexander. FJ: This is a weird dude. I don't know what's up with him. One day he wants to be good, one day he doesn't. All I know is, is that he'd better fine tune those wrestling skills of his if he plans on defeating guys like Ramacees and James Pillow. TS: Do you see him winning. FJ: Though anything's possible, I think that there's an exception in this case. TS: Okay: the HeadHunter. FJ: Another mystical feller. I don't know what's up with him but I do know that he's a great wrestler. He's one hell of a wrestler to be truthful. This man get up in the air and he can get down on the mat. He just might win this whole thing and become the first ever King of Inferno. TS: High hopes for HeadHunter, eh? FJ: Yeah. I don't know much about him but I do know that he has chances that any man would _die_ for. TS: Cyrus Elison. FJ: Also known as Cyrus Greene, this punk is going down. TS: You seem to have strong feelings from this Cyrus Elison. FJ: Yeah, he's internationally-renowned for his rather harsh views upon minorities. I don't like the punk, and when Freddy doesn't like somebody that the crowd doesn't like, you know that this person has done something offensive. TS: So, you think that he's not going to go anywhere in this battle royal. FJ: No... I know that he's not going to go anywhere in this battle royal. He's a chump, and he's going to lose. TS: Great point there, Freddy. "Simply the Best" James Pillow. FJ: Great wrestler but a little bit overrated. I mean, when Shawn Walsh beat 'em, it was like he'd just defeated J.W. Hardin. What was that? Pillow isn't the best nor is he near it. He's a great wrestler, but there are always wrestlers better than you. TS: It seems as if you've gotten a new attitude, Freddy. FJ: Please, Tommy. You know that damned Rushing made us have cue cards. TS: Yeah, but it was worth a shot, you know? FJ: I see ya'. TS: "Da' Greatest Ever" Shawn Walsh. FJ: Another kid with a great attitude. He's got a great personality, so that just might carry him to the victory. He's a new wrestler, but he's a great one, too. TS: I must say, even though he has an attitude that smells like manure, he's a great wrestler. FJ: Tommy, you've got to stop saying that. TS: What? FJ: Saying that just because somebody has an attitude that smells like "manure." That ain't right. They're not cocky, they're just honest. Honesty is a great virtue, Tommy. TS: To a certain extent. FJ: Please. If you're honest, you don't have to worry about anything. TS: Oh yes you do. If you're arrogant, rather honest, you're going to take a big fall when and if you lose. That's why some guys aren't trying to act so tough and cocky, so that when they fall, they don't fall far. FJ: Shut up. TS: Anyway, the last wrestler in the battle royal, Tim "Nugget" Beck. FJ: Oh yeah! I couldn't wait until you said his name. TS: Wow. You sound like you're really into Tim Beck. He really is a great-- FJ: No. He's a punk. He plays up to the fans like they're God or something. He's a little kid trying to suck up to the fans so that Daniel Rushing thinks he's over. He's nothing, and I can see right through him. TS: Funny, because Mr. Rushing went to great lengths to sign this young, up and coming superstar. Believe me, Tim "Nugget" Beck has a great chance to win this battle royal. FJ: Please. TS: Well, folks, the King of Inferno battle royal will kick off the first second hour _ever_ in the history of the Ohio Wrestling Federation. FJ: And you know that the second hour is _always_ two times better than the first one. And if the first hour is any indication, then the second hour will be straight pay-per-view quality. TS: That's true enough. The second match of the second hour will have the newcomer, "Kill Devil" Hakisho Shiniko taking on "Too Perfect" Craig Hayes. This is going to be a great match, regardless. FJ: Great attitude versus great wrestling. Craig Hayes, of course, having the great attitude. Craig Hayes hasn't been seen since leaving the EMWO Towers for the last time, so many people are wondering about his status with the Ohio Wrestling Federation. TS: Yeah, the type of bond that includes extracurricular object throwing into the ring. FJ: Yeah, they're just being generous. They want to see Craig Hayes survive in this world, and if it takes them throwing beer cups into the ring when he's thirsty to do it, then damnit, they're going to do it! TS: I gather that we've run out of cue cards. FJ: And loving every minute of it!! TS: Who do you see winning this match? FJ: I see "Kill Devil" Hakisho Shiniko winning this match. This is going to be a great match, though, seeing that both men really want to get off on the right foot early on in the OWF. It's going to be a battle, nonetheless. TS: It sure is. Another great match will be "the Beast" Hannibal Stone taking on Destroyer. Now this match is going to be a battle of the titans. These two men have been itching to get into the ring against each other, and now that they have it, one must wonder: What's going to happen? FJ: I'll tell you what's going to happen. Rushing's going to change this match to a No Disqualifications match. These two men are two of the most explosive men in this sport, and to have them in a regular match is asinine. TS: Well, I can see where you're coming from, but, unfortunately, Rushing makes the matches. FJ: Yes, it sure is unfortunate, because these two men could make the night _that_ much better if it were no disqualifications. But no! TS: Regardless of what the match is, however, this is going to be a great match. It's going to be filled with bumps and bruises and possibly blood. If there's blood, the fans will like it that much more. FJ: Sure will. TS: We then get a break from the regular matches of the evening. Yes, it's the first ever no disqualifications match, and this one is going to be a biggy. Yes, "Flamboyant" Paul Dare and Destroyer will finally step into the ring. FJ: And this time, there ain't no rules. There's going to be a lot of juice flowing in this matchup, believe me. TS: These men have had their problems in other leagues, and they're going to try and solve the problem right in the OWF Civic Arena for the last time. This is really going to get good. FJ: It sure is. My main man, Destroyer, is going to tear the flesh from the bones of Paul Dare. Dare, watch out. TS: We're not done yet, folks. We then get another treat as "Sick and Twisted" Zack Sharp takes on "the Word" Joey Jackson. What about this one, Freddy? FJ: I don't know too much about this Zack Sharp fellow, but I hear that he's a great wrestler but a little deranged at the same time. I mean, did you hear that guy blasphemizing and totally disrespecting God? Man, I don't think God's on his side at all. TS: And it's due to my religious beliefs that I must say that Zack Sharp will not win. He doesn't even have a chance of winning. God doesn't allow evil in prominent positions. FJ: Um... who was the president of the last federation? TS: Even still, "the Word" Joey Jackson shall destroy Zack Sharp inside that ring on the first of November. Though Jackson will totally annihilate Sharp from the face of this Earth, I'm sure that it will be a hard-core and extreme matchup. Trust me. FJ: Okay, I will. TS: Well, that brings us to the last match of the evening. The first ever main event of Sunday Inferno and, more importantly, the Ohio Wrestling Federation. It's for the Buckeye Championship. It's "the Disciple" Eric Wayne taking on the Buckeye Champion, "Mr. Charisma" Christian York. Freddy? FJ: Just a few words: Christian York is going to beat the devil out of Eric Wayne. Eric Wayne is fooling everyone but me. I know that he's evil and I hope that Reverend Sin comes back and gives him a trashing. TS: Well, I doubt that you'll be seeing any of Reverend Sin but nonetheless, this is going to be a great match! Eric Wayne has won a title in the past, and Christian York is the current champion. Who wins? FJ: Christian York, without a doubt. York just came off the victory of his life by beating Hannibal Stone, and he's ready to roll on through Eric Wayne. He's going to be the champion _forever_, Tommy! TS: Well, I think, barring interference, that we'll have a new OWF Buckeye Champion on Sunday, November 1, 1998! FJ: I don't think so. Now that Hannibal Stone has left, I think that Christian York is the best wrestler that Rushing has. He won't have York lose this match, trust me! TS: Well, folks, we've got to take a commercial break. When we come back, we'll have the news and rumors from around the world of the Ohio Wrestling Federation! Turning... [With another lightning flash, we see that a small buckeye has been placed on the top of the tombstone.] VO: Turning to Ohio. You see, many have been in Ohio, and many _are_ in Ohio, but they could not and _can_ not measure up to what is brewing in Akron. [More thunder rolls, and lightning strikes the OWF logo, lighting it on fire.] VO: You've seen the rest...now come and join the best. Sunday, November 1, 1998, is rapidly approaching.
