OWF — News

Closure Notice

8 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OWF: Well, I didn't think it would come to this, but it has. I'm closing the Ohio Wrestling Federation, and there is no other option for me. Just to let you all know, when I got onto the computer about an hour ago, I didn't know I was going to close it. I guess it's just a feeling that pops up, and it's also one that you can't avoid.
So you're wondering why I'm closing the OWF, eh? Well, the computer crashing had a lot to do with it. It wasn't that I didn't feel like redoing everything that I had lost; it was more a sign than anything. I thought it was a sign that was letting me know that the OWF was slipping. OWF started to slip the day that I created it.
So you're wondering how that is when the first three cards came out on time, eh? Easy: the first two were biweekly cards in which I had two weeks to write most of the matches--which were mostly dealing with angles, so I could write them ahead of time.
You see, whenever I ran an organization such as the OWF, I never really had any help. When I ran the NAWA, I only had one match writer in [name withheld], and an occasional one in [name withheld]. So I did most of the writing, angle- planning, and other factors by myself. At fifteen-years-old, you have to understand that that is a tremendous burden of pressure. I mean, at times I was thinking that running the EMWO/OWF was an obligation, when it should not have been.
And I'm realizing that right now. To tell you, there are several reasons why I could never run a federation on a consistent basis: for one, I have absolutely no self-discipline. For some unknown reason, I can not make myself do something OWF-related once I'm on the computer. Whether or not this is a good thing, I don't know--but I do know that it played a huge factor in the downfall of this federation.
Another reason is that I'm just not consistent. I'm one of those people that can't have the same thing for so long, and usually when I start running a federation, I get some wacky idea that I like, and I want to change it.
That is the reason for the voluminous number of federations that I have run in the past--I can't stay consistent.
And this raises another question: will I give the OWF to another president who's willing to run the bad boy.
The answer: no. I have thought long and hard about what would happen if I decided to shut this thing down, and at the time I thought that giving it over to another qualified president would be the most wise thing to do. But there are two factors that won't let me do that: firstly, my ego. I have decided that if
I'm not running the OWF, it's not going to be running. It was my federation from the beginning, and if I can't do the job, I'm not letting any other person have it. Sure, it sounds conceited, but I'm sure a lot of presidents feel that way. The other factor is that I don't think that the next president would handle all of you guys' characters like I did. The styles would definitely be different, and you guys have become so accustomed to me that it would take a while to get used to the next president.
So now that you know why the federation is closing, I'll tell you *when* the federation will close.
The E-mail Wrestling Organization's first card was held on 13 June 1998, and the Ohio Wrestling Federation's last card will be February 21, 1999.
That's right: SnowScraps will be the last card. No fancy World Title battle royal--just the card that we had planned before this came about. I'm not going to even bothering writing that last Sunday Inferno all over again, because right now, I don't have the motivation. But if you want the results,
I'll give them to you. But I'll be sending you guys the official SnowScraps lineup sometime this evening.
Wondering where the thanks are? Well, they will come out after the supercard has been sent out. I will hope to include everyone, so don't bug me to put you in the thank you's letter, because if you do, I'll ignore you. :)
And for those of you who are trying to think of some schemes to keep the OWF running... Stop. It won't work. My mind is made up. I have no more obligation to the OWF after the 21st of February. I'm sorry, but this has to happen. And you know what they say: resistance is futile. There's no sense in trying to get me to keep the OWF open; I'm closing it.
I enjoyed the six-month reign, gentlemen. It's been real. I'll talk to you guys later. :( Kind regards, ~ Daniel Rushing president, OWF
