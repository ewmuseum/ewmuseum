The Application . . .

14 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, it seems as if the members, and a couple of non-members, wanted it, so I'm bringing the Ohio Wrestling Federation back. But since I know that there are going to be former members that don't want to join, I decided to post this to RSPWF, letting you all know that the OWF is looking for new wrestlers--up to 25 this time.
Currently, I'm trying to work on recreating the Player Handbook, because there are *a lot* of things that I need to change, but for right now, if you want to join, fill out the all-new, detailed-to-the-ass
Handbook.
And remember: first come first served. Thanks.
