OWF Christmas Carnage: Holiday Hellraiser (Promo)

2 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The preceding commercial has just gone off the air, and the following logo, while spinning, slowly descends from the top of your screen.] OHIO WRESTLING FEDERATION HTTP://OWFWRESTLING.HYPERMART.NET [Suddenly your screen turns to sable. Nothing can be seen, but the sounds of a guitar lightly strumming in the background can be heard. A voice-over of longtime announcer, Tommy Sessons, is then heard.] TS: It started with the end of a short era. [Clips of the famous Last Brawl at the EMWO's last pay-per-view, The Grand Finale, are shown. Included in the clips are "The Beast" Hannibal Stone clotheslining "Mr. Charisma" Christian York over the top rope.] TS: Although the end came, a beginning was born. [Clips of Daniel Rushing signing the contract that gave him full ownership of the Ohio Wrestling Federation are shown.] TS: So it continued with the dream of a perfect resurrection. A dream of a continuation of what was once a great thing. [Clips of Daniel Rushing speaking to a throng of reporters at the OWF's inaugural press conference are shown. A very serious Daniel Rushing is explaining the plan that would soon take the OWF straight to the top.] TS: But little did they know that the dream would become a reality... A reality filled with action-packed cards. A reality filled with bone-crushing moves. A reality filled with mind-boggling angles. [Clips of "The Disciple" Eric Wayne hitting "Mr. Charisma" Christian York with the Teardrop Powerbomb on a chair are shown. They are quickly turned into clips of "The Onslaught" Jakob Andrews cocking back to his Nick "Bulldog" Demola in the back of the head with a chair. These are turned into the clips of Alex Holy double-crossing "The Word" Joey Jackson during OWF's first card, Sunday Inferno: Resurrection.] TS: Through the action-packed matches... the jam-packed school gyms... Through the tumultuous matches... the sometimes ominous happenings... Through the earth-shattering explosions from the crowd -- whether good or bad... the exciting endings to great main event matches... And through heartbreaking losses from the fan favorites... the sadistic triumphs of the bad guys... Through all of these things, the Ohio Wrestling Federation has stood strong. [The scene now turns dark again, the sounds of thunder the only audible features. As the thunder rolls, the lightning strikes, revealing that we're in a graveyard.] TS: For over two months, we've held our ground with the best in the business. We've backed up our claim as the best federation in the state of Ohio. We've showed you why we're what the world of wrestling is paying attention. We've showed you all of it. [The lightning flashes again, revealing a shot of a glaucous tombstone. The lightning continues to flash, and it seems as if the actual grave has been dug up, as unpacked dirt surrounds the tombstone.] TS: But what's next? What lies ahead for the Ohio Wrestling Federation? Is it fame? Is it dejection? Or will it be the same old Ohio Wrestling Federation? Only time can tell. Only time will tell. Whichever one of those options comes true, only one thing remains evident: You can watch it all fall into place on pay-per-view only. [A huge bolt of lightning flashes, and slowly rising from out of the grave is an electrified OWF logo, illuminating all that is around it.] TS: We have already emerged from our ashes... Now watch us take our first step forward. You can watch us do that on pay-per-view. At Christmas Carnage... A Holiday Hellraiser. [The OWF logo is then set ablaze, with everything around it turning black, so that it's the only object on your screen. And as the fiery OWF logo continues to burn, slowly fading-in is the Christmas Carnage logo.] CHRISTMAS CARNAGE SUNDAY, 20 DEC 1998 OWF CIVIC ARENA
