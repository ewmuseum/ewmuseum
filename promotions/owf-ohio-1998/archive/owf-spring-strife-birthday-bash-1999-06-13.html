Spring Strife: Birthday Bash

13 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RSPWFers, On Saturday, June 13, 1998, the E-mail Wrestling Organization was created. On Sunday, September 27, 1998, the E-mail Wrestling Organization closed down but only to reopen and change its name to the Ohio Wrestling Federation. On Sunday, June 13, 1999, the Ohio Wrestling Federation will celebrate its one-year anniversary with a big-ass card. To get on the mailing list, e-mail me at [detail withheld] or instant message me.. whatever.. Here's the line-up. Enjoy. Dan.
