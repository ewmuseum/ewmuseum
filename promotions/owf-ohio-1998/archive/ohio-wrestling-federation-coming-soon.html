OWF: Ohio Wrestling Federation Coming Soon

20 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The color of black is all that is seen on your screen. All of a sudden, a roll of thunder is heard, and a flash of lightning illuminates the screen, showing us a graveyard filled with tombstones and old trees. A chilling voice-over is then heard.] VO: An old African proverb states that to stumble is not to fall but is to go forward. Yes...something has stumbled, but it has _not_ fallen. [As the voice-over is heard saying that, a flash of lightning reveals an old tombstone entitled, "EM--," the rest not being able to be seen due to it being worn away.] VO: Something has taken an unfortunate turn for the worse, but as I speak to you now, it's turning for the better. Turning... [With another lightning flash, we see that a small buckeye has been placed on the top of the tombstone.] VO: Turning to Ohio. You see, many have been in Ohio, and many _are_ in Ohio, but they could not and _can_ not measure up to what is brewing in Akron. [We fade-out from the old graveyard, and we fade through black to a small, archaic gym, which only has wrestling ring. The camera slowly pans around the ring, catching its every detail. From the red turnbuckles, and the red, white, and blue ropes, we see it all.] VO: What's brewing in Akron, Ohio is incomparable to anything that you've ever seen. What's brewing in Akron, Ohio is preeminence taken to a whole other level. What's brewing in Akron is Ohio wrestling... [The screen fades to black, but the OWF logo quickly appears in the center of your screen.] VO: Ohio wrestling at its best. [More thunder rolls, and lightning strikes the OWF logo, lighting it on fire.] VO: You've seen the rest...now come and join the best. [And with that, everything fades out except for the following date that flashes across your screen.] SUNDAY, OCTOBER 31, 1998 To join the Ohio Wrestling Federation mailing list, mail its president at [detail withheld].
