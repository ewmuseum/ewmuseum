What to send where 1. NEWS Once again, I'm sorry for the late card. I can't make any excuses. But I will say that the next week will also be hectic; it's exam week, so there will be a lot of studying. But I'm going to try as hard as I can to get the card out on time. I have changed my mind -- I'm going ahead with SnowScraps. There will be a free week before the actual supercard so I can get all the stuff written down, so that should enable the card's timing to be a bit better than this week's fiasco. If nobody quits before the SnowScraps deadline, I plan to have 10 matches, so I'm going to start asking people if they can write a match for the big supercard. Instead of having OWF Clamor LIVE! every week, and instead of totally scrapping OWF Clamor LIVE!, I'm going to make OWF Clamor LIVE! a bi-weekly event. This should help out a lot. Thanks. I want to tell you members of the OWF to actually read this update. In the past couple weeks, I have been approached with questions that were easily answered, but the answers were also in the updates that I sent out. That's why I send the updates -- to inform you of what's going on. Please read the updates all the time to see if you have a match -- you might be surprised! I'm going to need some writers this week. If you can write a match (the assignments come out Wednesday), please let me know as soon as possible. I'm also making a new rule: if you have a sneak attack, angle request or a role play for a match that's on that week's Sunday Inferno, your stuff *must* be in on Wednesday. I will stop accepting anything after Thursday, because I start writing matches on Wednesday, and if I'm getting angle stuff on Thursday for that match... You can do the match. So once again: get all angles, sneak attacks and role plays in before or *on* Wednesday for it to count on that week's Sunday Inferno. Thank you. 2.
