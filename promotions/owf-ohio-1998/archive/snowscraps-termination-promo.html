SnowScraps: Termination (Promo)

17 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
____ _ ________ / __ \ | / / ____/ / / / / | /| / / /_ / /_/ /| |/ |/ / __/ \____/ |__/|__/_/ VO: For over six months... In five known countries... The Ohio Wrestling Federation... Ohio wrestling at its best! [The OWF logo slowly fades out, and a silhouetted image of Ohio Wrestling Federation logo is shown. After a few moments of the camera's shot of the silhouette, a voice-over of Tommy Sessons is heard.] VO: The Ohio Wrestling Federation. A small independent wrestling promotion that stood among the best professional wrestling organizations for over six months. Those six months were filled tumultuous and strenuous matches. Those six months were filled with blood, sweat, and tears of victory, anger, and defeat. And through the blood, sweat, and tears of victory, anger, and defeat, there was always one thing in common about all of the wrestlers and the fans of the Ohio Wrestling Federation - all of them shared an equal amount of passion for the Ohio Wrestling Federation as an organization. [Clips of "Sick & Twisted" Zack Sharp's bloodied face during his match against the Mystery Man are shown. The broken nose of Sharp emanates blood profusely, not looking as it would ever stop.] VO: Through the blood... [Clips of "Sudden Death" Shane Matthews stomping away at the body of "El Diablo" Eddie Deegan in the corner are shown. A look of pure intensity is emblazoned on the face of Shane Matthews, and the sweat begins flying off his hair.] VO: Through the sweat... [Clips of "Mr. Charisma" Christian York after the match where he dropped the title to "The Beast" Hannibal Stone are shown. York is kneeled over in a corner with his hands covering his face.] VO: Through the tears... All of these men have given it their all for the Ohio Wrestling Federation. [Clips of "The Beast" Hannibal Stone holding up the OWF Buckeye Title in the air, while standing on the middle turnbuckle, are shown. A shot of pure excitement and happiness fills the face of Hannibal Stone, and the fans respond with huge pops.] VO: Through the excitement of victory... [Clips of Klonis throwing Ramacees' head through a glass window at one of the Sunday Infernos are shown. Klonis' face resembles that of an insane man as he drives Ramacees' head through the glass.] VO: Through the intensity of anger... [Clips of "The Word" Joey Jackson on his back after losing the OWF Akron City Title match to "Flashy" Michael Robinson are shown. A predictable look of disappointment is on the face of Joey Jackson, because he knows he'd never get another shot at the title.] VO: Through the pain of defeat... _All_ of these men continuously went out night after night putting on a show for the fans, knowing that their lives were at risk _every_ time that they stepped into the squared circle. [The silhouette of the OWF logo reappears.] VO: But they're lacing their OWF boots up for one last time. They're going out to step into an OWF squared circle for one last time. They're going out there to please the crowd... to give the fans a breathtaking event... _One last time._ [Superimposable images of all the competitors in the 26-man King of Ohio battle royal begin flashing by. Shots of "Pharisaic" Jack Vars, "Mr. Charisma" Christian York, "The Beast" Hannibal Stone, Klonis, James Warnham, "El Diablo" Eddie Deegan, Jakob, "Darkside" J. W. Brimstone, and many others are caught.] VO: And as a token of appreciation, OWF President Daniel Rushing is giving them the opportunity to become the King of the Ohio Wrestling Federation... forever. [Clips of "The Beast" Hannibal Stone hitting "Mr. Charisma" Christian York with a Hannibal Cannonball through a table are shown; shots of Mystery Man and "Sick & Twisted" Zack Sharp falling off the second deck of the OWF Civic Arena are shown; and shots of "The Disciple" Eric Wayne hitting Nick "Bulldogg" Demola with the Teardrop Powerbomb are all shown.] VO: But, of course... They're going to have to work for it... _One last time._ Twenty-six men go head-to-head in one squared circle. The possibilities are endless... But there is only one winner... And at the end of this four-hour long evening of a tribute to one of the greatest professional wrestling organizations ever created... We will crown the King of Ohio...
