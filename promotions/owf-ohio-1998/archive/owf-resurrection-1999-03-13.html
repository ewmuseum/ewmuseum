Resurrection???

13 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
To all of those with an interest in the OWF, It is with mixed emotions that I announce my ambivalence towards the closure of the Ohio Wrestling Federation. As most of you know, the Ohio Wrestling
Federation officially closed down just a few short days ago after I realized that there was no way I could recover from a fatal computer crash that left the
Ohio Wrestling Federation crippled in more ways than one.
For those of you that care, this is the first time that I have ever had to close down a federation due to the lack of interest, ability, or strength. All of the other times, I had to close the federation down because of the restrictions to the computer.
And then the computer crash came - and it hit me harder than the Holy Ghost. There was no true recovery from it, and for those handlers who really didn't want to be in the federation, it was their perfect escape; for the others, they had to be pulled backwards just so we could have advanced forward. I felt as if that four months into a federation this was just too much, and I did the first thing that came to mind: I closed the federation.
But after a good two weeks of nothing to do, mind relaxation, and finger resting, I decided that I just needed a honest break - and during the two-week period that was supposed to be there for me to write the final OWF card,
Termination, I simply rested. Rested my fingers; rested my mind; rested my patience. Simply rested.
And that rest has felt damned good. I really want to keep the Ohio Wrestling
Federation, because let's face it: a lot of good federations are dropping like flies, and I don't want to be in that category of federations. But it's up to the people who were, or who weren't, in the Ohio Wrestling Federation. If you want me to continue this Ohio Wrestling Federation thing, let me know about it
- e-mail me; and if you want me to let the Ohio Wrestling Federation just die, let me know about it - e-mail me. Either way, I'm not going to make you do anything. If anyone leaves the OWF after all of this sporadic updating and other stuff, I would not blame him one bit.
So it's up to you guys. Whatever you want. Kind regards, Dan. president, OWF? http://OWF2000.webjump.com
