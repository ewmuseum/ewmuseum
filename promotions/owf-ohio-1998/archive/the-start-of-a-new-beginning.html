OWF: The Start of a New Beginning

25 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[As the screen fades in, you're taken to a completely black screen, where October 31, 1998, is centered on your screen in red text. Inside the text, clips of various wrestling fighting are seen. Then, a voice-over is heard.] VO: October thirty-first, nineteen ninety-eight. A date that will revolutionize the way you see Ohio wrestling. [We now change locations, and you're taken inside an old gym, where the camera begins panning around a small wrestling ring, catching the ring's every detail -- including the ropes which are caked with dust and the worn away logo in the center of the ring that is entitled, "E---."] VO: October thirty-first, nineteen ninety-eight. The start . . . [From atop the gym in which the camera looms, the camera rockets down towards the ground, its destination the center of the ring. The camera continues speeding down the gym, the ring becoming bigger and bigger inside the screen. And right when the camera should crash right into the ring, we explode into clips of wrestlers fighting inside a ring set ablaze with pyrotechnics and bursts of flames on the ropes. With all the pandemonium inside the ring, "Fuel" by Metallica begins to play, making the view seem even more surreal.] VO: The start of a new beginning. [Each of the four ring posts then ignite with four blasts of rutilant flames, towering high in the air for only a few moments. The flames then evaporate, and the wrestlers continue to brawl, as if there is no fire on the ropes.] VO: From blasts of flames that tower twenty feet in the air, a new generation of professional wrestling superstars shall walk through the curtains. From ropes set ablaze, the dawn of a new era shall be cast upon us. [The camera continues to pan around the ring, catching all of the brawling that continues to go on, "Fuel" continuing to pump in the background.] VO: You just might not believe the hype. You just might not believe what your eyes have yet to see. We are in no position to see yes or no. But one things remain evident: [The camera then quickly zooms in on the ring, and rapid-fire clips of wrestlers powerbombing, moonsaulting through tables, piledriving, and DDTing other wrestlers are shown. And with one last surge of guitar and the ring exploding into bursts of sparks and flames, "Fuel" by Metallica comes to a close, and we return to an all black screen.] VO: Ohio wrestling will _never_ be the same. Be there to witness the dawn of a new era. Be there to witness . . . [The date of October 31, 1998, comes back onto your screen in red lettering.] VO: Ohio wrestling at its best. [Fade.] To get on the Ohio Wrestling Federation mailing list, e-mail its president at [detail withheld]. -OWF Staff
