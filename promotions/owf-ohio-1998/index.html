Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Ohio Wrestling Federation launched on Halloween 1998 out of the storyline wreckage of its own predecessor, the E-mail Wrestling Organization, whose own first card had run 13 June 1998. Its launch promo staged the transition as a graveyard scene, a worn tombstone lit by lightning: “Something has taken an unfortunate turn for the worse, but as I speak to you now, it's turning for the better... turning to Ohio. You see, many have been in Ohio, and many are in Ohio, but they could not and can not measure up to what is brewing in Akron.”

It ran on a Sunday Inferno card line backed by two newsletters, Clamor and Buckeye Buzz, under four championships: a flagship Buckeye title, a hometown Akron City title, a tournament Lightweight title and a Cincinnati-based Tag Team title. Its own first card, 1 November 1998, billed the Akron City title match between “Flashy” Michael Robinson and Red Raider as a clash of former EMWO champions, while “Mr. Charisma” Christian York retained the Buckeye Championship over “The Disciple” Eric Wayne in the main event.

It closed on 8 February 1999, and its own president explained why in unusually candid terms: a computer crash had wiped its own records, and he had been running the promotion's writing and booking alone, at fifteen years old, since it opened. “OWF started to slip the day that I created it,” he wrote, naming its planned final card, SnowScraps: Termination, as the last one he would write.

Five weeks later he reconsidered. A 13 March 1999 post left the promotion's fate to whoever still wanted it — “If you want me to continue this Ohio Wrestling Federation thing, let me know... if you want me to let it just die, let me know” — and by 28 March 1999 it relaunched under a new card, the Second Coming, opening on a fire-and-glass video package scored to Metallica's “Fuel.” Christian York carried the Buckeye Championship into the new run.

It continued through the summer of 1999, reopening its tag division in May and running a Spring Strife: Birthday Bash card in June. Its own last dated document, 17 July 1999, still bills the promotion as active; no further closure notice survives.

Affiliations

E-Mail Wrestling Organization (EMWO)

Predecessor to OWF (Ohio, 1998)
The promotion's own operator lists the E-Mail Wrestling Organization among his own prior federations in his closure post, and its own one-year-anniversary card states outright that the E-Mail Wrestling Organization (founded 13 June 1998) closed on 27 September 1998 and reopened under the Ohio Wrestling Federation name -- the same 13 June 1998 date the predecessor's own held record already carries as its founding date.
