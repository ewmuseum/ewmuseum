"Advanced Role Players Wanted!!!"

19 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello all! This is [name withheld], president of a fairly new e-fed, USIW and we are currently undergoing a little cosmetic surgery so to speak. I'm flinging the deadweight and trying to bring in some fresh new talent. I try and write some good quality cards and make everything as interesting as possible. We hold the following cards on the following days: Monday Night Dynamite! (Monday): Live card with 10 matches Tuesday: "Inside USIW"- magazine type update show Wednesday: house show card with results only Thursday Night Thunder! (Thursday): Live card with 10 matches Friday: "Inside USIW"- magazine type update show Saturday: house show card with results only Sunday: house show card with results only, Interview of the Week, and updated rankings We also have a PPV every month. This is a role playing fed that started in early November and our champions are as follows: USIW World Heavyweight Champion: Survival USIW United States Heavyweight Champion: Jim Mongol USIW Internet Champion: Curmudgeon USIW World Heavyweight Tag Team Champions: Power and Pain USIW TV Tag Team Champions: The Pack I judge who wins or loses by role playing. I prefer QUALITY to QUANTITY, but several good flashes could easily outmatch one GREAT one. Anyhow, I also judge the strategies sent by each man for his matches. In case of some conflicting plans, I'll either: use them both to counteract each other, or judge which is better and makes more sense and use it. I try and do everything I can for the people who try and role play well. I HATE three line insults that people send and claim it's a flash. I DO NOT WANT THAT EVER! So, if you'd like to join a young and up coming fed that has a lot of potential to be really great, just e-mail me and let me know. I'll send you more details and an application. Thank you for your time. Cheers! -Chris
