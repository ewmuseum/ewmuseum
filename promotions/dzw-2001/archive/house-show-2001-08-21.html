House Show — Tuesday, August 21, 2001

21 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
oooooooooooooooooooooooo Danger Zone Wrestling: Indy Challenge Tour Tuesday, August 21, 2001 oooooooooooooooooooooooo oooooooooooooooooooooooo Rochester War Memorial Rochester, New York oooooooooooooooooooooooo The DZW show in Rochester featured Danger Zone wrestlers against a bunch of local indy workers. It's strange the way DZW does this, but I liked it a lot.
It was nice to see our indy talent step in the ring with some nationally known grapplers (although they jobbed). It was _definitely_ interesting and worth attending. Here's the layout of what went down. An [i] indicates an independant talent.
--> Demon Deathwish v. the Duke [i] Demon Deathwish opened up the show, to my suprise, and stepped in the ring with a much smaller wrestler by the name of 'the Duke'. The Duke put up a fight at the beginning of the match, but eventually faltered and was destroyed.
He was hit with a MASSIVE big boot that we all thought removed the Duke's head. After a side suplex and a nice sit out powerbomb, Deathwish signalled for the 'Deathwish' double underhook piledriver. He hit it and put a finger on the chest for the three count. This was a nice opening match to get the crowd going, but Deathwish got a _lot_ of heat. Mostly because he's responsible for
Devon Tracy winning the US Title.
--> the Undead Warrior [i] v. 'Blue Dragon' Ichiro Hamada It's absolutely rediculous how _over_ Ichiro Hamada is. The match got off to a great start with an astounding number of reversals and re-reversals by the two. Undead Warrior showcased some of his abilities with a few aerial moves, including a nice split legged moonsault. Before long, however, Hamada took control and hit his cartwheel elbow in the corner, and followed up with a springboard bulldog. He hit a split legged moonsault of his own for a nearfall. Warrior started coming back with some crude offense, but Hamada ducked a clothesline and spit the blue mist in his face, setting up an implant
DDT for the pinfall win. The crowd went NUTS for Hamada. He's a truly talented and graceful wrestler.
--> Keith Stone v. Phat Clyde [i] The absurdly fat Rochester indy talent, Phat Clyde, put together very little offense as Keith Stone dominated him with a technical ground attack for the first five minutes of the contest. Stone locked on a figure four about eight minutes into the match. Clyde passed out and his shoulders rested on the mat for the three count, as Stone celebrated in the ring. The crowd wasn't much into this one.
--> 'Madman' Mike Falcone v. Hank [i] 'Madman' Mike Falcone was treated to a handful of 'meatball' chants during this one. The contest itself was a pretty good one. Hank put together some nice offense in the beginning, much to the delight of the crowd. Hank hit some nice knife edges in the corner, followed by a tornado DDT. At one point Hank even piledrove Falcone to a _close_ nearfall. Shortly after, though, Falcone came back and dominated him with an ace crusher, reverse ddt, and finally his
'Jawbuster' second rope jawbreaker for the three. He argued with a number of fans on his way out of the ring area.
--> Al Keeler v. Pete the Plumber [i] Sleeper. Keeler isn't much a wrestler, but he seems to draw pops anyway. This one went back and forth for a little while, with both men putting together some offense. Keeler got the victory with an elbowsmash off the top rope.
Nice seeing an old finish brought back. --> UNITED STATES TITLE MATCH --> Mega Invader [i] v. 'Dreamboat' Donovan Tracy [c] Tracy got on the mic before this one drawing more heat than I've ever seen before. He berated the muscular Mega Invader calling him all sorts of names, before this contest began. He stated that Invader is no challenge and argued that this should not be a championship match. Invader shut him up by hammering him with a lariat and beating him from pillar to post for the next seven or eight minutes. Invader hit a number of high impact moves, including a sick jumping piledriver and a well executed superplex.
Just to give you an idea of how badly Invader was beating him, some of the fans I was next to were actually convinced that the local talent was going to snatch the US Title off Tracy. Invader truly gave him a believable beating.
He eventually, however, made a key mistake by trying to use an aerial attack. He missed a top rope splash, and from then on, Tracy was on him like a vulture. Invader tried to come back but Demon Deathwish came out and distracted him. By the time he turned around, Tracy hit a nice superkick, you could hear the pop in the cheap seats. Finally, after his patented jumping flatliner, Tracy covered for the three. The boos were deafening.
At the end of the show, Tracy payed off Demon Deathwish (again) in front of everybody. Deathwish patted him on the back and returned to the backstage area as Tracy played to the crowd, holding up his US title, and he almost got booed out of the building. A huge 'Sanders' chant went out to Steve Sanders, but he wasn't onhand. I heard his jaw injury is legit.
ooooooooooooo FINAL NOTES - ooooooooooooo Biggest Pops: 1. Ichiro Hamada breaking out the blue mist on the Undead Warrior. 2. Local talent Super Invader for giving Donovan Tracy a brutal lariat. 3. Steve Sanders, and he wasn't even onhand. Most Heat: 1. 'Dreamboat' Donovan Tracy playing to the crowd with the US Title. 2. Demon Deathwish for ... well ... being Demon Deathwish. 3. Local talent Phat Clyde for being absurdly fat. Best Signs: 1. Tracy Sucks D*ck (maybe I'm immature, but it was funny) 2. Phat Clyde (because it was sarcasm) 3. Falcone - Meatballs for Sale Anyway, if you haven't gotten a chance to catch a DZW show, yet, you should really check one out. I had a good time, and I still think it's a cool idea to have them go around the world facing indy talent. If you want to see them all face eachother, go see Danger Zone, but this is different. Definitely unique.
