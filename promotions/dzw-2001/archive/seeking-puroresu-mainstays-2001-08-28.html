It's is not to be taken terribly seriously, and is not meant to be a job. This is just a group of e-wrestlers coming together for an organization based on fun. Our wrestlers are expected to roleplay at least once a week (length isn't important) and to regularly be involved in some sort of feud or rivalry.
-> In Character <- Danger Zone Wrestling is an organization started by billionaire promotor Mr. Steven C. Charleston. Charleston has spent several years in the business helping backstage in his father's promotion, the World Wrestling League. He is experienced enough, now, to run his own promotion, in leui of his father's promotion folding (TV fell through).
DZW is run on WVTV, a nationally syndicated television station. It has timeslots for two shows, and is able to promote one Pay Per View per month.
With the excessive funding DZW is entitled to, wrestlers have pyrotechnic displays, a Danger-tron video, etc. This is a worldwide promotion.
oooooooooooooooooooooo What We Expect oooooooooooooooooooooo -> Roleplays <- You don't have to be the greatest writer in the world to pump out a decent roleplay each week. All that we ask, here, is that our handlers stay active.
We don't want anybody lurking around and not flashing. You have to put things out. If you don't feel your roleplaying is up to par, go to e-wrestling university and _read_ the player essays involving roleplaying. This is imperative.
http://ewu.efeduniverse.com/ If you follow the link, on the upper left hand menu, you will find a bunch of very helpful tips and useful ideas for roleplaying. It will get you a good start.
-> Angles & Feuds <- This is one of the most important aspects of DZW. You are expected to try and stay involved in something at all times. Follow the link above and find information on quality angles and feuds, and this will give you an idea of what
I'm talking about here.
It is expected that you regularly put out backstage segments for the cards, and try to come up with creative stuff for your character to do on the show. We want the cards to boast our activity. =)
Picture RAW, but with a _tad_ less talking and a _tad_ more action. 'Nuff said. -> No Bitching <- Nope, none of that. oooooooooooooooooooooo Shows oooooooooooooooooooooo -> Danger Zone <- DZW Danger Zone is the flagship program for DZW. It takes place every Monday
Night, and is a two hour long program. The first hour is usually summary matches, with the second hour being all Play by Play. The two hours are treated seperately, with most of the 'major' matches taking place on hour two.
-> Warriors of Wrestling <- DZW Warriors of Wrestling is a one hour secondary program, which is all summary matches (jobber matches). It is used to hype up the major feuds and angles through in ring interviews, etc. Currently, Warriors of Wrestling is on hold. We haven't yet held the first, but we will introduce the show once the roster fills up.
-> House Shows <- House Shows will usually take place once or twice a week, with the most active DZW wrestlers showing up and wrestling against local indy talent. These are not major cards, but those who are involved in angles and roleplay consistantly will be rewarded with house show matches.
-> Pay Per Views <- Once a month, DZW will have a massive Pay Per View spectactular. These shows will have full play by play, and every title is required to be defended. The
Pay Per Views will be used to settle feuds and start new ones, and will be
_major_ in determining the leaders in rankings, ect.
oooooooooooooooooooooo Titles oooooooooooooooooooooo -> DZW World Title <- The DZW World Title speaks for itself. Only the best wrestler in DZW is fit to wear this title, and he will be forced to defend it on a semi-regular basis against any of the top five contenders.
-> DZW United States Title <- The US Title is the secondary title in DZW. It's prestige is just slightly less than that of the DZW World Title. It will also be defended regularly on
DZW programming, and at every Pay Per View.
-> DZW Tag Team Titles <- The Tag Titles will be the trophies for the greatest Tag Team in DZW at any given time. ooooooooooooooooooooooooo Non Player Characters ooooooooooooooooooooooooo -> Commentators <- The commentators for the DZW broadcasts are Andy Hollister and Robbie Ward. Hollister is one of the best play by play men in the business, while Ward sticks to doing color. They have worked together in many other promotions, but are finally ready to settle down in DZW for a nice paycheck. They are both young men, mid twenties, and have a lot of energy.
-> Interviewer <- Devon Michaels is the resident Interviewer in DZW. He calls things straight down the middle, as he sees them, and will not pull any punches when interviewing a DZW superstar. He usually catches comments from wrestlers in the back before their matches, and is quick witted and provocative with his statements.
-> Ring Announcer <- David Jacobs is the ring announcer, in DZW. He is an elderly, balding man, with a very loud voice. =)
-> President <- Mr. Steven C. Charleston is the president of DZW. He has the final say on
_everything_ that occurs in the DZW rings. He reserves his right to override any decision by the officials, championship committe, etc. He runs things, and his word is what counts on any matter. If you have a problem, take it up with him, he'll be in his office.
oooooooooooooooooooooo Other Stuff oooooooooooooooooooooo -> Updates <- Updates will appear in your mailbox after every card. They will review some of the happenings from DZW TV, list injury reports and give you the 411 on the upcoming cards. They will be essential to your stay in DZW, and you _will_ learn to like'em. =)
-> Rankings <- Rankings will be handled as a seperate mail in DZW. The criteria for the rankings is still 'to be determined'.
-> Roleplay Packets <- Roleplay packets will appear semi-consistantly in your mailbox. I will send one out once for every five or six roleplays I get, which means you could plausibly end up with three in a week. You are each allowed a minimum _and_ maximum of 1 roleplay per week, so make it good.
