Informer Newsletter

19 August 2001

ooooooooooooooooooooooooo Danger Zone Wrestling ___ ____ _ _ | \ |__ || | | | | |\ \ / / | |/\| | | |/ / / /_ | | |___/ |____| \_/\_/ The Informer Newsletter ooooooooooooooooooooooooo oooooooooooooooooooooo Quick Headlines oooooooooooooooooooooo --> A new United States Champion was crowned on the first DZW Danger Zone on August 20, 2001! 'DREAMBOAT' DEVON TRACY duped us all. He defeated JASON
JACOBS in round one, STEVE SANDERS in round two (with help from DEMON
DEATHWISH) and finally captured the title from DEMON DEATHWISH in the finals with an elbowdrop! DEATHWISH was paid to lay down, handing TRACY his first singles title.
--> Danger Zone University Graduate KEITH STONE has laid out an open challenge to _any wrestler_ in the world to make up for his first round loss to DEMON DEATHWISH. --> STEVE SANDERS has vowed to gain his revenge on both DEVON TRACY _and_ DEMON DEATHWISH once he recovers from his broken jaw. He feels that he rightfully should be the holder of the DZW US Title.
oooooooooooooooooooooo Recent Results oooooooooooooooooooooo DZW Danger Zone Pespsi Arena, Albany, NY Monday, August 20, 2001 Hour One DEMON DEATHWISH defeated AL KEELER with the 'Deathwish' double underhook piledriver in 7:32
'BLUE DRAGON' ICHIRO HAMADA defeated KEITH STONE after a vertebreaker in 5:34
STEVE SANDERS defeated JASON JACOBS after a 'Burn Out' fisherman's brainbuster in 12:14
DREAMBOAT' DEVON TRACY defeated 'MADMAN' MIKE FALCONE after a jumping
Flatliner in 10:34
Hour Two
DEMON DEATHWISH defeated 'BLUE DRAGON' ICHIRO HAMADA with a 'Deathwish'
double underhook piledriver following a kick to the groin in 13:45 'DREAMBOAT' DEVON TRACY defeated STEVE SANDERS after a jumping flatliner following a shot with a foreign object in 15:36
'DREAMBOAT' DEVON TRACY defeated DEMON DEATHWISH with an elbowdrop in 0:14 to capture the DZW United States Championship
oooooooooooooooooooooo Injury Report oooooooooooooooooooooo --> 'BLUE DRAGON' ICHIRO HAMADA suffered a stinger at the hands of DEMON DEATHWISH from the 'Deathwish' double underhook piledriver. He will work through his injury and miss no action. --> STEVE SANDERS suffered a broken jaw at the hands of DEVON TRACY following a shot with a foreign object. He will miss 3-4 weeks of action. oooooooooooooooooooooo Champions oooooooooooooooooooooo --> DZW World Heavyweight Champion --> Vacant --> DZW United States Champion --> "Dreamboat" Devon Tracy --> DZW World Tag Team Champions --> Vacant oooooooooooooooooooooo Roster oooooooooooooooooooooo --> Al Keeler (face) --> 'Blue Dragon' Ichiro Hamada (face) --> Steve Sanders (face) --> Jason Jacobs (face) --> Demon Deathwish (heel) --> Keith Stone (heel) --> 'Madman' Mike Falcone (heel) --> 'Dreamboat' Donovan Tracy (heel) oooooooooooooooooooooo Upcoming Cards oooooooooooooooooooooo DZW Danger Zone Binghampton War Memorial in Binghampton NY Date: Soon! We are looking to add more talent to DZW. As soon as we grab a few more athletes, we will schedule the second televised DZW event. House shows, however, will continue rolling. Once we get a couple more wrestlers we will preceed with Danger Zone, and the fed will continue full force ahead! This does not really mean we're on hiatus, we are just going on a recruiting spree. =)
