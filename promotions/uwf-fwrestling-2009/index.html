A fantasy-roster federation on the fwrestling.com hub whose two surviving home pages, from about March 2009 and January 2010, carry its news, top five, champions and a Hall of Fame induction, under the tagline Legends Will be Made. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Universal Wrestling Federation signed its home page 'Legends Will be Made', and the two copies of it that survive, ten months apart, are a news board more than a history. The first, from about March 2009, has Blair Kivisto, the Universal Champion, defending against Gage Gannon at Numbered Days and being saved from the Horsemen by Glenn Legend; it also reports one wrestler released from his contract, another back, and a Hall of Fame induction for one called Spade. The second, from about January 2010, has Tyler Havoc as champion, the three-time champion Ricky Stanton and the twice-champion Andy Titsuhiro each after the belt, and the Lethal Brawl of 31 December 2009 won for the first time by a woman, Serena, who picked the only other woman, Zoey Swan, as the eighth entrant of an Annihilator match for the Universal title.

Every section the pages name is missing: the application and rules, an Adrenaline staff page, rosters, the weekly cards and results and the forums. The cards, which the menu implies were weekly, were not collected, so the record is two snapshots of five belts and two top fives. The site's own images are dated by the hub's directory listing from 2005 to 2010, which shows the address was in use for years before the pages that survive; no page of that earlier period is held.
