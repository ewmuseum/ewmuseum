Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The World Grappling Council posted a bare recruitment call to rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy on 26 November 1997, pointing anyone interested to a Tripod-hosted application form. Two days later it published a full first-round bracket for its own World Title Tournament — Kahn vs. Mean Mike Massacre, Johny Blade vs. Max Slash, Kill Razor vs. Prototype, The Ultimatum vs. Extremely Deadly, Ric Lacy vs. Jose Ramon, Rick Stephens vs. “Too Sweet” Tyrone Mackay, Karl Wimberly vs. “G-Money” Greg Allocca, and Apocalypse vs. Icicle — eight entrants named for a tournament with no surviving second round.

Its own hype peaked on 6 December 1997 with a promise that “NOTHING CAN STOP IT... THE NEXT GENERATION OF E-WRESTLING WILL ARRIVE JAN. 1 1998,” naming Vince McMahon among the forces it claimed couldn't touch it. It drew an immediate, mocking reply on the same board, matching its grandiosity line for line: “OH GOD! I BETTER HURRY AND SIGN UP!... Oh MY! It's amazing! This fed must rule! You've been addressed.”

Nothing dated later survives — weeks still short of the 1 January 1998 launch it had promised. No card, roster, or result confirms the World Grappling Council ever actually opened.
