EUWC Hiatus in 1998.  The titles remained vacant until October 1999. Titles vacated due to controversial finish at Head Bash 2000, when the Dark Angels wrestled the Chaos Factor to a double pin. This was a six Pack Elimination match including three teams.  The Wannabes, The Kings and the Alliance of Violence.  The final two remaining men would be World Tag Team Champions. EUWC hiatus in 2000. The titles would remain vacant until September, 2001. EUWC hiatus in June 2002.  The titles remained vacant until April of 2003. Titles vacated when Paul Doom suffers an injury.  A tournament would be held to crown the new Champions. 240 days deducted due to EUWC hiatus.
