Harris Michael Lennox made "Classy" Mike C tap out Samuel Roundtree pinned Angel of Death Semi Final Round Rocko Daymon pinned Malik Johnson Michael Lennox defeated Samuel Roundtree Final Round Rocko Daymon defeated Michael Lennox October 15, 2006 University Arena - Albuquerque, New Mexico 3rd Annual Chad Dupree Memorial Cup Pay Per View Results Chad Dupree Memorial Cup - Quarter Final Round Match: "The Great & Almighty" Nero defeated Daymon via disqualification. Chad Dupree Memorial Cup - Quarter Final Round Match: Cobris Grayson defeated "Classy" Mike C with a rollup. Chad Dupree Memorial Cup - Quarter Final Round Match: "The Hatchet Man" Violent K defeated "The Tiger" Sanket Desai with the Unveiling. Chad Dupree Memorial Cup - Quarter Final Round Match: Sevyn defeated "The Hazard" Lynch Garrison with the Rage. EUWC North American Champion Newfy Jack and EUWC World Tag Team Champions Diabolic Forces defeated EUWC Tag Team Champions Black Death Gauntlet and EUWC European Champion Chris Comet in a six man tag team match when Molloch pinned Chris Comet after Newfy Jack hit Comet with a title belt. Chad Dupree Memorial Cup - Semi Final Round Match: "The Great & Almighty" Nero defeated Cobris Grayson with the Nero Kick. Chad Dupree Memorial Cup - Semi Final Round Match: "The Hatchet Man" Violent K defeated Sevyn with a German Suplex. EUWC United States Champion Draco defeated EUWC International Champion Hawkeye with a roll up. Chad Dupree Memorial Cup - Final Round Match: "The Great and Almighty" Nero defeated "The Hatchet Man' Violent K with the Nero Kick.  Nero won the 2006 Chad Dupree Memorial Cup Tournament. MAIN EVENT: EUWC Champion "The Hound of Ulster" Cuchulain defeated EUWC World Heavyweight Champion "The Disease" Jay Smash when he pinned him with the Gae Bulga. October 28, 2007 United Center - Chicago, Illinois 3rd Annual Chad Dupree Memorial Cup Pay Per View Results Chad Dupree Memorial Cup - First Round Match: "The Great & Almighty" Nero defeated Hawkeye with the Nero Kick. Chad Dupree Memorial Cup - First Round Match: "The Disease" Jay Smash made "The Signal" Sanket Desai tap out to the Jay Locke. Chad Dupree Memorial Cup - First Round Match: STEALTH pinned "The Crippler" Joe Boyer after the Stealth Drop Inverted DDT. Chad Dupree Memorial Cup - First Round Match: "The Ironman" Buck Naked defeated "The Ego Buster" Dan Ryan with the Five Star Butt Splash. Chad Dupree Memorial Cup - First Round Match: The Phoenix defeated Sevyn via disqualification. Chad Dupree Memorial Cup - First Round Match: Angel of Death pinned Chris Comet with a school boy pin. Chad Dupree Memorial Cup - First Round Match: Allister Crowley defeated "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin with the Blood Line. Chad Dupree Memorial Cup - First Round Match: Rocko Daymon pinned "Commander" T. Waring with the Daymonplex. Diabolic Forces defeated Team Fusion when Mammon pinned Fred Fusion after the Damnation.. Chad Dupree Memorial Cup - Quarter Final Round Match: "The Great & Almighty" Nero defeated "The Disease" Jay Smash with the Buzz Cutter. Chad Dupree Memorial Cup - Quarter Final Round Match: STEALTH pinned "The Ironman" Buck Naked with a reversed superplex. Chad Dupree Memorial Cup - Quarter Final Round Match: Angel of Death pinned The Phoenix with the Six Feet Under. Chad Dupree Memorial Cup - Quarter Final Round Match: Rocko Daymon pinned Allister Crowley with a spear. EUWC North American Championship Match: "The Thriller" Braden Kincaid defeated Kenny with the Thrill Ride.  Braden Kincaid retained the EUWC North American Championship. Chad Dupree Memorial Cup - Semi Final Round Match: STEALTH pinned "The Great & Almighty" Nero with the Stealth Bomber. Chad Dupree Memorial Cup - Semi Final Round Match: Rocko Daymon defeated Angel of Death with the Brain Rocker after Texas Lightning interfered attacking AOD.. EUWC International Championship Match: Stanley Leash defeated Jonathan Darkstar by disqualification when the Darkstar Brothers interfered.  Jonathan Darkstar retained the EUWC International Championship. Chad Dupree Memorial Cup - Final Round Match - EUWC World Heavyweight Championship Match: STEALTH pinned EUWC World Tag Team Champion Rocko Daymon with a roll up reversal.  STEALTH won his seventh EUWC World Heavyweight Championship.
