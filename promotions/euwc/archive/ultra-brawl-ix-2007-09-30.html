Ultra Brawl IX — Results

EUWC Ultra Brawl IX Results! September 30, 2007 Reliant Stadium Houston, Texas Show Intro A war has been waged.  A battleground set.  Two armies will enter the battleground and do whatever it takes to destroy the other.  Each army with their set of leaders (an image of Hawkeye, Nero, Benjamin, Kincaid, Comet, Violent K, Ryan & Daymon and Diabolic Forces flash on the screen).  Each army with their own Generals.... (Pickstock and Silver).... Each army with their own Captains.... (Smash and STEALTH) And each army waging war to prove which will be able to raise their flag at the end of it all... (Main Frame and Blackout logos)  Tonight the war will come to an end.  One will win, one army will lose.  In the end nobody will survive the ULTRA BRAWL! [“Razorblade" by Blue October continues as the arena goes of with pyro exploding off the stage and around the ring.  The crowd is seen going nuts with signs high in the air throughout Reliant Stadium.  The music continues as we join Scott Marchman and John Shaft ringside...] Scott: 75,000 strong fans welcome you to Ultra Brawl IX! John: The war is about to explode here Scott!  And I for one cannot wait!. Scott: What a night it is going to be.  of course we will see that huge Elimination Tag team Turf War between Main Frame and Blackout! John: That is going to be huge, but even bigger will be the Main Event....  The Unification Match between EUWC Champion Hawkeye and the World Heavyweight Champion Nero!  At the end of the night, only one man will be crowned Champion! Scott: Let’s not waste anymore time, let the first match commence!  Let's throw it over to Roger & Keith! [The screen switches to Keith & Roger at the Blackout announcer's table.] Keith: What a night we have from Blackout as our Superstars get ready to lay waste to Main Frame! Roger: And I for one cannot wait to see our Tag team Champions of the World, Diabolic Forces, completely obliterate Daymon and Dan Ryan! Keith: But before that, let's throw it up to Shelley for our opening bout! EUWC International Championship Match Champion - "The Hatchet Man" Violent K vs Jonathan Darkstar - Challenger (Iron Man" by Black Sabbath starts playing, and the arena goes dark. As the song reaches a crescendo, the arena lights slowly go up to reveal Darkstar in the ring, standing behind his opponent.) Kandi: The following contest is set for one fall and is for the EUWC International Championship!  Introducing first is the challenger, fom Dallas, Texas. Weighing in at 295 lbs, Jonathon Darkstar!! [("Get Ya Wicked On" Thunder noises are heard as we are given an earthquake type of feeling around the arena. The lights totally cut to black and the crowd scream and get extremely hyped when a purple flashlights shine towards the entrance way with mist beginning to emerge. The purple spotlight fades out and we are in darkness once again. With a moment of silence, fire blast from either side of the entrance way and dies quickly as Insane Clown Posse's Murder Rap begins to play through the speakers. The lights flash with a purple and white effect when Violent K walks out and begins walking down towards the ring while being given an enormous pop from the crowd. Fans reach out to touch him and to clap his hand but he is to focused and carries on staring at the ring. He slides into the ring and makes his way to the top rope where he throws his arms up into the air.) Shelley: And his opponent, from Detroit, Michigan. Weighing in at 247lbs. Representing EUWC Blackout, he is the current EUWC International Champion.... "The Hatchet Man" Violent K! [The scene opens up in the ring where the ref holds the International Championship high into the air and walks around the ring. Violent K slaps his face getting himself pumped up for the match as Johnathan Darkstar glares from the other side of the ring straight into the eyes of Violent K evilly. The two of them both smirk at one another looking forward for the match to commence. The ref hands the title to the outside official and then calls for the bell to begin. Ding Ding!! Violent K begins bouncing around on his feet to pump himself up as Johnathan Darkstar runs straight at him and takes him down with a vicious clothesline!] Scott: The match has begun and Darkstar has been it known that he wants that International Championship and is now taking it to Violent K! Roger: The champ didn’t expect that one; he didn’t know what hit him! [Darkstar starts off with the advantage as he throws Violent K into the turnbuckle and then begins kicking him into the gut until he slowly slides down onto the floor. Darkstar bends down to pick him up but gets a vicious punch from Violent K sending him back and almost off his feet. Violent K tries to get back to his feet but Darkstar doesn’t give him chance as he drives a knee straight into his stomach to send him back down in pain. Darkstar continues his dominance and continues to look impressive as he drags Violent K away and then locks in a boston crab. Violent K screams in pain and tries to reach for a rope but struggles.] Scott: Wow I never thought I’d see Darkstar take control of Violent K quite like this. His thoughts must have been towards the International Champion for a long time as you can see the desire in his eyes as he sends his opponent through the pain! Roger: Darkstar has been after this title for a long time now… a year in fact and the desire just continues to grow and grow and grow. [The champion finally makes a brave attempt as he spins himself around and kicks Darkstar away. Darkstar immediately comes running back at Violent K but Violent K uses his legs to push Darkstar over his head and make him go flipping to the mat! Darkstar holds his back as Violent K hops to his feet. Darkstar begins to get to his feet but a shining wizard kick connects to Darkstar to make it known that the champion is very much back in this match. A scissor kick to Darkstar sends him to the canvas face first as the adrenaline inside Violent K begins to grow.] Scott: Darkstar needs to stop Violent K right now if he wants to win this match! Roger: That is true; once the adrenaline begins to flow in Violent K he can become unstoppable. He needs to find a way to slow his momentum down. [With a bounce into the rope, Violent K takes down Darkstar with a shoulder tackle and then bounces into the ropes again. Darkstar gets to his feet quickly and swings for him on the rebound but Violent K ducks and grabs Darkstar by the neck hitting a devastating neckbreaker. Violent K goes for a cover: 1…2…NO! Darkstar got a shoulder up and Violent K right back to work on him.] Scott: Violent K is in complete control here and like he was at the start, it’s now Darkstar who finds himself in this situation. Roger: Whoa! Who’s that huge guy with the mask just coming out of the entrance?!? Scott: I’m not sure, but holy hell this guy is a big man and he does not look very happy for whatever reason. [As Violent K plants his opponent with a bulldog, he then spots the huge being walking down the ramp and acts surprised as if he knows him. It distracts him for a couple of minutes, but then he gets straight back into the match and picks shoves Darkstar between his legs looking to go for a piledriver. Darkstar escapes and throws Violent K over his head making him crash to the floor. The man on the outside is now at ringside and bends over looking under the ring for something.] Scott: Darkstar has turned the match around, and is looking very impressive but what the hell is that strange being on the outside doing looking under the ring. Roger: He looks like a deranged freak; I don’t think its good having him at ringside at all. If he comes over here then I know for sure I won’t be feeling too safe. He looks a little confused! [The masked man comes out and holds a sledgehammer high into the air as the crowd cheer when seeing the weaponry. Back in the ring Darkstar throws Violent K into the turnbuckle which he smashes into it hard, and to make the impact worse, Darkstar then spears him in the corner taking the wind straight out of the champion. Darkstar then lifts him up on the top rope before getting up himself. He looks around to the crowd with a sadistic grin on his face as he picks Violent K up and hits the… DARKSTAR RISING! The masked man on the outside jumps up and down in excitement as Darkstar feels hurt from connecting the move, however managed to get an arm over Violent K for the 1…2….3!! Ding Ding!! We have a new champion!] Kandi: Your winner by pinfall, and new EUWC International Champion… JOHNATHAN DARKSTAR! Scott: It’s been a long time coming but it’s finally come true… Darkstar has finally gotten himself the International Championship! Roger: He has done it in a fashion… and looked impressive ever since the bell rung at the beginning. [The masked man then gets into the ring with the sledgehammer as Darkstar slowly rolls out with the title in hand. The man raises the sledgehammer into the air as he turns his back on Violent K and glares evilly at Darkstar on the outside.] Scott: I think he’s gunning for Darkstar! Roger: No he isn’t!!! [As people think he is gunning for Darkstar, he then turns around and smashes the sledgehammer straight down towards Violent K! The lights in the arena cut out so people do not see the aftermath of the sledgehammer shot into the cranium. A strange noise is heard and then the lights reappear in the arena with an empty ring and both the masked man and Violent K vanished. Darkstar looks around confused wondering where they have both disappeared as the crowd look stunned.] Scott: What the hell! Where have they both gone!?! One moment the man smashed a sledgehammer towards his head then the next they were gone! Roger: I can’t believe what I have just witnessed! [Darkstar’s music hits the speaker as he walks up the ramp with the championship in hand.] Backstage Segment Matt Pickstock & Buck Naked Staci -- Matt, tonight's the big night team Main Frame vs team Blackout in the 10 man tag team elimination bout the entire crowd is waiting for... Matt -- Could you be any more of a cliche?  Seriously Stacy, could you look more like trash right now?  I've got four members of my team and I can't find my fifth man anywhere.  Have you seen Buck? Staci -- Yeah, he's over there... (Staci points to a 2007 Dodge Viper sitting in the lot with Buck Naked and the French Tickler getting out of the car.) Matt -- OK...you can go...GO!!!! (Stacy runs off whimpering.) Buck, the show started 20 minutes ago, where.... Buck -- Where the hell was I?  Up yours Pickstock... Matt -- What?  IN case you didn't know you twit, there's 72,000 people out there waiting to see what's going to happen. Buck -- That's the best you've got you little bastard.  You really don't think I know what's going on out there?  You don't think I hear the snickering from the guys in the back?  "Buck's back...yay...what a joke..."  I know what the guys are saying. And the best part of my return this time... I just don't care.  Tickler and I are going to the locker room to 'relax'.  I'm going to go out there and lay a beating on anything that moves and then, pray that I get the one on one match I came for.  I spent 8 years looking for this little bastard, and you guaranteed me that he's mine this time.  Right?  Our deal is still on right? Matt -- Of course it is, you do for me and take out Blackout and you'll get him tonight, tomorrow, he will be yours until the day you are done with him. Trust me. Buck -- Trust you.  That's funny.  See ya out there boss. (Buck piefaces Matt out of the way, slings the French Tickler over his shoulder and walks to the dressing room.) Texas Bullrope Match Allister Crowley vs Kenny (The arena goes red and smoke fills the entry way as "Ecstasy of Gold" by Metallica plays. Strobe lights flash and out walks Crowley. He struts to the ring. He looks around the arena and at the crowd as he rolls in the ring. He climbs the second rope and raises his arms in the air and he awaits his opponent. ) Kandi: The following contest is the Texas Bullrope Match!  Both entrants will be tied together at the wrists using the bull rope.  To be victorious, one man must hit all four turnbuckles in succession without interruption.... Introducing first from San Diego, California weighing in at 240 pounds here is Allister Crowley! The Time is now by John Cena hits the P.A. System and the crowd stands to their feet and cheers as they wait for Kenny to step out. Kenny comes out spinning his spinner necklace and flexes his muscles at the beginning of the ramp. As Kenny walks down the ramp he stops and slaps the ground with both hands .As he raises them up to the top he quickly throws them down and pyro comes down the side of the ramp and Kenny continues to walk to the end of the ramp. At the end of the ramp Kenny throw up the Texas Longhorn sign as he rolls into the ring. Kenny gets up and gets on a turnbuckle and does his pose the Diamond. Kenny gets off the turnbuckle and takes off his necklace and gives it to the ref. Kenny takes off his shirt and throws it into the crowd and waits his opponent to come out. Kandi: And his opponent, from Killeen, Texas, weighing in at 247 pounds, here is Kenny! John: This is going to be good! Scott: I can't say for sure, but I think this is the first time the EUWC has ever had a Texas Bullrope Match! Both men were connected to the bull rope. Kenny kicked Crowley in the gut and nailed him with right hands and went for a corner but Crowley pulled him back. Crowley nailed him with a clothesline and then a right hand. Crowley wrapped the bull rope around Kenny's neck and then Crowley touched one of the turnbuckles, but Kenny pulled Crowley back and it broke the streak. Scott: Crowley trying to end this one early here! John: He doesn't get paid by the hour, and besides, he's already beaten Kenny once before. Crowley hit some knees to the head and then nailed another right hand, and touched the turnbuckle but Kenny stopped him from going to the second. Crowley knocked Kenny down with a hard right hand and touched the second turnbuckle. For good measure, Crowley used the cowbell and cracked it across the skull of Kenny, and managed to get three lights on. Crowley reached for the fourth turnbuckle, but Kenny pulled on the bullrope forcing Crowley toward him, and Kenny connected with a hard lariat. Kenny touches the first turnbuckle, and then quickly dashes to the second one. Crowley started to stir and Kenny leaped for the third turnbuckle but before he could get there, Crowley hit a drop toe hold breaking the streak. Scott: Quick thinking by Crowley there! Roger: I can't believe Kenny would even attempt that after seeing Crowley up. Kenny hit a thumb to the eye and then a big boot and then hit some right hands in the corner. Kenny nailed Crowley with the cowbell and then touched three corners but Crowley tripped him with the rope before he could touch the final buckle. Crowley nailed Kenny with the cowbell and then touched three corners but Kenny rolled to the outside so Crowley couldn't reach the third. Keith: Smart move by Kenny. John: Crowley needs to get Kenny back into the ring in order to finish this. Crowley pulls hard on the bullrope, and it yanks Kenny into the ringpost. Crowley pulls Kenny into the ring and bodyslams him. Crowley has Kenny on his back and touched one corner, and then a second and a third but then Kenny nailed him in the back of the head with the cowbell and we are back to square one for both men. Kenny nailed a clothesline and then went for another but Crowley hit him with the cowbell, and then whipped Kenny in the back with it. Scott: What a hard shot with that cowbell! Roger: Kenny is busted open! I don't believe this! Crowley hit the Blood Line on Kenny. Crowley wrapped the rope around Kenny's neck and then dragged him to the first corner, and tapped it, but Kenny hit it too. The same happened with the second corner, and Crowley isn't realizing it. Kenny hits a low blow on Crowley! Kenny then went for the final one and he is inches away from winning this, but Crowley pulled him in and then hit a spear and touched the final buckle for the win! Kandi: Here is your winner, Allister Crowley!! The ref unties the arm, of Crowley and he raises his hand. Crowley celebrates as Kenny lay on the mat. Crowley leaves the ring as Kenny staggers to his feet. Suddenly a masked man hops the barrier and enters the ring.  He grabs the cowbell and levels Kenny with a hard shot to the skull.  The masked man wraps the rope around Kenny's neck and then swings him over the top rope. Holding Kenny high enough that he is hanging him, the masked man pulls on the rope as hard as he can.  Kenny's arms are flying all over the place and he is desperately trying to escape.  After a few minutes of hanging, Kenny's arms go limp and he stops struggling.  The masked man releases the rope, and Kenny falls hard to the outside of the ring where officials and EMT's flood the scene.  The masked man grabs the microphone.... “Well, I don’t know about you, but it’s nice to be back in this ring…doing what is best to me….kicking ass, forgetting to take the time to record names.   In case you have forgotten about me, I am the original ICON of the EUWC, the Southwestern Legend, the former NWA, LCW and WWA World Heavyweight Champion, the Ultimate Wrestler, the absolute brawler….I am….” The man’s phone begins to ring loudly. “I am….” Phone continues to ring. “I…..” Another ring…the crowd begins to chant “Answer It!!” He picks up the phone. “Hello?” “You don’t know who I am, but let’s get this clear, I know you and right now, since you opened the envelope in front of that courthouse…I OWN YOU!!” The crowd gives a mixed reaction. Scott: “Who owns the man in the ring?” John: “Who IS the man in the ring?” “Nobody owns me.  I have been and always been my own man. You see, I am ….” "You are mine now…and so is your lady.” The man in the ring looks confused. “Now listen and do as I say.  Go to the back…you will receive your instructions later.” “Nobody tells ….” “I do now.  Now, GO TO THE BACK!” The connection is ended.  The man complies and slowly exits the ring and walks to the back. Keith: “What just happened here?” Roger: “I wish I knew.” EUWC United States Championship Match Champion - "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin vs Damien Dante Stone - Challenger ("Click Click Boom" by Saliva begins to play as smoke shoots up from the entrance of the stage. The fog stops as Damian Stone stands there on the stage, the crowd going ballsitic as he stands there, with a smile on his face. He then begins to walk down the isle, stopping on the rampway, slapping hands with the fans. He then walks to the edge of the ring. He points to the fans and then slides into the ring. Going over to hard-camera side, he raises his right hand as pyro explodes over his head.) Shelley: The following contest is set for one fall and is for the EUWC United States Championship!!  Introducing first - the Challenger, from St. Louis, Mossouri. Weighing in at 350lbs. Damien Dante Stone! ["The Final Countdown" By Europe begins to play As "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin emerges though the entrance way. Slowly he makes his way towards the ring soaking up the reaction from the crowd. As he approaches the ring he demands the referee to hold the ropes open as he enters the ring.] Kandi: And his opponent, representing Main Frame, from the United Kingdom now residing in Tampa, Florida, weighing in at 243 pounds.. He is the EUWC United States Champion - “Yours Truly” Adam Benjamin!!! Benjamin and DDS stare each other down from their corners, while the referee displays the United States Championship. The bell rings as both men are still staring and Benjamin has a smile on his face. DDS smirks as well as both men are about to tie up but Benjamin backs out and goes flexing on the ropes. Just then, Damien Stone does a taunt that takes the smile off Benjamin’s face. Benjamin hurriedly ties up with DDS but DDS kicks him in the gut and starts elbowing Benjamin to the back till he’s his down. DDS hammers away at Benjamin while he’s down and then starts slapping at Benjamin’s head. The referee pulls him off of Benjamin. DDS pulls Benjamin up to his feet and starts nailing him with right hands and Benjamin seems dazed. DDS goes for an Irish whip but Benjamin reverses, only to have DDS hit him with a powerslam! DDS goes for a quick cover but Benjamin gets the shoulder up. Roger: Looks like DDS may have the champ’s number here. Scott: Well, a Blackout guy would say that. Keith: We have two former World Champions battling it out here, and I can't wait to see who comes out on top. Benjamin tries to retaliate with punches to the gut and bounces off the ropes for a clothesline, but DDS ducks and hits Benjamin with a neckbreaker! DDS goes for a sleeper hold but Benjamin scurries his way out of it and gets his feet on the ropes. DDS has Benjamin up and goes for a vertical suplex but Benjamin grabs the top rope and holds on. DDS goes for one again but Benjamin picks him up and tosses him over the top rope but DDS manages to stand on the apron. DDS grabs Benjamin again and suplexes Benjamin of the ring, while still standing on the ring apron! Benjamin is out on the floor outside as DDS dives at him with an elbow drop! The referee, who was previously counting them out, has now come outside to check on both men. DDS gets up first and picks Benjamin up. DDS whips Benjamin towards the steel steps but Benjamin goes for a reversal, only to have DDS kick Benjamin in the gut and take him down with a clothesline! DDS heads over to the announce team and grabs a TV monitor. He is about to us it on Benjamin when the referee takes it away from him. DDS is arguing with the referee which gives Benjamin enough time to recuperate and Benjamin spears DDS into the barricade! Keith: Benjamin saves himself from that monitor and may have hit DDS bad. John: That’s classic Main Frame for you right there. Benjamin picks up DDS and hits him face first into the barricade. Benjamin throws DDS over the barricade and into the crowd and hops over as well. Benjamin hits DDS face first into the front row seats as the fans nearby are on their feet. Benjamin takes DDS away into the crowd where he repeatedly hits him in chairs. The referee follows them as Benjamin is taking DDS all the way to the stage. Benjamin goes to whip DDS into the barricade but DDS reverses and rakes Benjamin’s eyes. Benjamin staggers right into DDS who picks him up and tosses him over and into a nearby pop-corn stand! DDS picks up backs of popcorn and drops all of it on Benjamin’s face. DDS picks Benjamin up and lifts him up in a stalling suplex position and drops Benjamin with a brainbuster on the floor! DDS takes Benjamin towards the stage. DDS rams Benjamin’s head on the stage floor and climbs up himself and hits Benjamin with a dragonrana off the stage! Scott: MY GOD! Benjamin must be dead by all rights at this point! John: That’s where we have to see if Mr. United States can survive this. Scott: Mr. United States? The referee orders to take this fight back into the ring as DDS brings Benjamin back into the ring. DDS gets in and goes for a quick cover but it takes the ref some time to get into the ring and count just a near fall. DDS gets angry at the ref about this and both start arguing. Benjamin slowly gets up and hits a shoulder block to DDS from behind, with DDS inadvertently knocking down the ref as well. DDS goes to Benjamin but Benjamin hits DDS with a low blow and hits him with a DDT! Benjamin goes for a cover but the referee slowly recovers and goes for a count 1..2..DDS kicks out! Scott: Benjamin nearly had him there Roger: Are you kidding? DDS isn't about to give up that easily. Both men slowly get back up on their feet as DDS hits Benjamin with a right hand but Benjamin retaliates with a right hand of his own. Both men exchange rights until Benjamin gets control and hits him with a few rights and goes for an Irish whip, but DDS reverses and whip Benjamin across the ropes. DDS goes for a clothesline but Benjamin ducks and comes back with a flying forearm smash! Benjamin bounces off the ropes and lands with a quick leg drop! Benjamin picks up DDS and hits him with a snap suplex and rolls onto DDS and mount punches him. The referee gets Benjamin off DDS as DDS tries to get up but Benjamin bounces off the ropes and hits him with a modified bulldog! Benjamin climbs up the top rope and raises his arms into the crowd and flies off with a moonsault but DDS gets his knees up and hits Benjamin in the ribs. Benjamin rolls around the ring clutching his rib. Benjamin tries to get back on his feet but from behind DDS grabs him and hits a high impact release German Suplex! DDS then climbs up the top rope and flies off on Benjamin but Benjamin rolls out of the way, but surprisingly, DDS lands on his feet. DDS waits for Benjamin to turn around and hits him with the Cyclone Slam! DDS goes for the cover 1..2..Benjamin kicks out! Roger: DAMN! Almost had a new Champion right there! DDS cannot believe that Benjamin kicked out of the Cyclone Slam and is angry. DDS signals the JJE again as he waits for Benjamin to get back up on his feet. DDS goes for the JJE but Benjamin shoves him away and DDS knocks down the referee again. DDS turns around and Benjamin goes for a stunner but DDS reverses it into a reverse DDT! DDS puts Benjamin into a corner and sits him on the top turnbuckle. DDS climbs up for a superplex attempt but Benjamin hits DDS with a few punches till DDS loses his footing and Benjamin climbs on the top turnbuckle. Holding DDS’s head, Benjamin looks for a tornado DDT and leaps in the air but he turns his body in mid-air and hits a stunner off the top rope! Scott: HOLY CRAP! THAT WAS UNBELIEVABLE! Roger: I’m speechless! How the f*** did he pull that?! Both DDS and Benjamin are down on the mat. The referee is trying to recover as Benjamin slowly, slowly, rolls onto DDS and puts his arm over him. The referee crawls over to them and slowly makes a count 1..2..DDS gets the shoulder up! The referee now starts to count out the two as both men attempt to get back up. Benjamin hits a groggy DDS with a right hand but DDS replies the same. Benjamin kicks DDS in the gut and goes for a DDT but DDS holds onto Benjamin and bounces him off the ropes. DDS goes for a Southern Cross but Benjamin does exactly the same and bounces him off the ropes, Benjamin hits DDS with a devastating Shining Wizard! DDS drops down like a brick wall and Benjamin hooks the leg and the referee counts 1..2..THREE! Kandi: Here is your winner, and STILL EUWC United States Champion.... "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin!!!! Scott: He did it! Benjamin retained the title in this classic contest! John: And Main Frame gets another victory over Blackout! That's 3 for 3 boys! Roger: Shut up! Keith: Still to come, we have more Championship matches and that all important Elimination Tag Team Match, not too mention the two Unification Main Events.
