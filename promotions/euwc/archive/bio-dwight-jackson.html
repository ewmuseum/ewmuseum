Roster Bio: DWIGHT JACKSON

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DWIGHT JACKSON Height: 6'1" Weight: 229 lbs. Hometown: Long Island, New York Entrance Theme: "Bring Me To Life" - Evanescence Finishing Move(s): - Slow and Painful - Tear Jerker - The Apology Style: Brawling / Grappler EUWC Accomplishments: - EUWC 52 Wild Champion BIO: 10 years ago, Dwight Jackson was a successful lawyer in a Manhattan firm. He had a wife, two kids, a seven figure salary, and he was moving up the corporate ladder at an extremely fast pace. Some of his co-workers became extremely jealous of him and decided to frame him for a murder he did not commit. Over the course of the next 2 years, Dwight poured every cent he saved into his defense. Lucky for him, the jury found him not guilty. The problem was he was broke, his wife and kids left him, and he did not have a job to go back to. So he went into seclusion and began searching out ways to gain revenge on those who had wronged him. He began going to wrestling matches as a way to try and relive some of his stress. Gradually he decided that this would be the best way for him to try and re-build his shattered past. He went to Japan, and learned from some of the legends over there, now he's back in North America looking to make a name for himself. Dwight Jackson has arrived in the EUWC. He's made some serious remarks and its obvious from them that not only is he here to make a name for himself, but also he is here to ensure that he will take away whatever the superstars hold dear, just like he lost his.
Source
Common Crawl capture, 14 May 2008
