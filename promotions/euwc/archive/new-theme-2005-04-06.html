Damian D Stone — 6 April 2005 Hey. Now that EUWC is opening, I thought about changing Stone's theme to a new one. This is what it is w/entrance:
Theme: Go to Sleep by Eminem, DMX, and Obie Trice [[The lights dim as smoke begins to fill the main stage as we begin to see scenes from Damian's wrestling career in the EUWC. As Go to Sleep by Obie Trice, Eminem and DMX begins to blast over the PA, the crowd begins to anticipate the arrival of Damian Stone. As soon as the main beat kicks in, a light shines out underneath the stage and we see Damian Stone w/Echo standing there, looking over the crowd as the smoke continues to pour out from the stage. Both begin to walk down the isle toward the ring, looking ahead, not turning toward the fans. Damian slides into the ring and walks up to a turnbuckle. Hopping up, he raises his hands in the air, taunting the crowd and getting a major pop from his actions. He hops down and does it from the other side as well. He then hops down and takes off his duster. He then lays it down in his corner and stretches, waiting for his opponet to enter the ring.]]
