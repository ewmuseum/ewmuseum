Eye to eye and maybe even nose to nose, with this much of nothingness around us we can not tell no matter how much we dare to try and tell. Where there once was colour and all sorts of life buzzing before us, there is now tons upon tons of black. No way around it, no matter where you look, you see nothing but black. We are all in the now. None of us knowing what to expect next. What begins to happen next has a faint feeling of being seen before but not by anyone for quite a few years. And when it happened back then, it resulted in there being quite an impact by its creator. More precisely what begins to unfold before the public's eyes is a small, yet very sharp, white crack can been seen protruding through the black. A small crack soon grows to a more noticeable size, and then grows a bit more still. It is at this point when something new pushes forward - a sound not heard of last time. A screeching siren starts to push its way through the black in time with the ever growing white cracks. Making this now not only an ambush on the eyes but also an raid on the ears. As the siren's scream grow forth and multiple from nearly every direction, the wash of white is close behind it. And as the white cracks are nearly engulfing what once was darkness, that very same darkness seems to shattered away - leaving us bare to the new sight and sound. But then, as suddenly as all this started, the sound cuts itself off which is only to hieghten our attention to another matter. That being a few choice words in bold, red lettering, which take up the screen by storm leaving just enough room around each word to keep the public's eyes squinted slightly. Those words are: A New Age Is Upon Us! Almost a full minute later everything is gone again and our sets are returned to the regularly scheduled programs. Cuchulain grabs Hawkeye’s leg and pulls him down. Hawkeye wraps his legs around the shoulder Cuchulain and puts him in a cross arm breaker. Cuchulain quickly makes it to the ropes. Keith: Hawkeye might be thinking right, ground the quicker man. Hawkeye lets go and but Cuchulain gets back to his feet before Hawkeye can stand. Cuchulain hits a dropkick that sends Cuchulain to the floor. Hawkeye quickly gets to his feet. Cuchulain backs up and runs towards Hawkeye. Hawkeye ducks and Cuchulain fakes the suicide dive and stands in the ring. The crowd cheers a bit for Cuchulain. Hawkeye stands up and Cuchulain goes for the plancha. Hawkeye slides in as Cuchulain lands on the apron. Hawkeye gets to his feet and turns towards Cuchulain.. Cuchulain slides between Hawkeye’s legs and reaches up for a sunset flip, Hawkeye falls back 1...2...kickout. Keith: Cuchulain almost pulled a fast one Roger: There’s the speed factor. Cuchulain gets up and hits a forearm to Hawkeye. Cuchulain runs to the ropes. He tries a clothesline, but Hawkeye hits a kick to the gut. Hawkeye goes behind and hits a backbreaker. Hawkeye holds on and hits another backbreaker then cradles Cuchulain, 1...2....kickout by Cuchulain. Hawkeye goes for a belly-to-belly suplex, but Cuchulain blocks it. He hits Hawkeye with a headbutt. Hawkeye staggers back and Cuchulain quickly hits him a kick to the gut and a DDT. Cuchulain runs off the ropes. He nails Hawkeye with a high cross body block, Hawkeye catches him and hits a Fallaway slam. Hawkeye looks out to the crowd then picks up Cuchulain. He signals for the Starmaker. Hawkeye goes for the Starmaker, but Cuchulain reverses it into a Victory Roll….1….2….thr..kickout by Cuchulain. Keith: WOW!! Cuchulain almost pulled the rug out from under Hawkeye again. Hawkeye gets up and Cuchulain hits a European uppercut. A second uppercut Hawkeye stumbles into the ropes. Cuchulain whips Hawkeye into the ropes. Cuchulain goes a backdrop, but Hawkeye nails him with a swinging neckbreaker. The ref is knocked down by the flailing legs of Cuchulain. Keith: Ref Bump! Roger: Cuchulain isn't moving either, Keith. Celtic Pride runs out with a chair in his hand. Keith: This could get interesting. Hawkeye lets go of Cuchulain and walks towards the ropes. Tommy MacKeegan gets into the ring and swings the chair but misses. Hawkeye throws Tommy out of the ring then turns around. Woad charges Hawkeye and Hawkeye leaps over him. Woad crashes into the ringpost and Hawkeye dumps him to the outside. Hawkeye turns and walks into a Gae Bulga from Cuchulain. Cuchulain makes the cover and the ref staggers over and makes the count… 1………………2………………3. Shelley: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner and STILL EUWC North American Champion, Cuchulain! Keith: Cuchulain gets the win over Hawkeye! And here comes Leo Silver! ("Cold" by Evan's Blue blares and Leo Silver walks out onto the stage holding a microphone.) Leo: Cuchulain, Celtic Pride, you three seem to like interfering in matches lately. Next week on TakeDown, I've got you three lined up in a six man tag match against Jakob Mclean and The Salvation. And since you like bringing weapons into a match, let's make this . . . oh I don't know . . . a hardcore rules match. (Cuchulain and Celtic Pride start to protest but Leo holds up his hand.) Leo: And Hawkeye, since you're never going to get a fair fight with these three around the United States Title, the EUWC Championship Committee has authorized another match next week. You get first crack before eXile to make an impact. On the next TakeDown, it's you versus Rocko Daymon. And the EUWC International Title will be on the line. Keith: Whoa! A six man tag team hardcore match AND Rocko defends the International Title against Hawkeye on the next Takedown! Roger: Leo Silver is drunk with power. He can't do that to Cuchulain and Celtic Pride! Keith: He can and he did. Ladies and gentlemen, we're out of time. See you next time on EUWC TakeDown!
