Blood Bath (Moscow) — Card Preview

10 February 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
February 10, 2008 Luzhniki Stadion - Moscow, Russia Theme Song: TBA THE CARD Champion - STEALTH vs Rocko Daymon - Challenger At the 4th Annual Chad Dupree Memorial Cup, STEALTH did what nobody believed he could do when he outlasted 15 other superstars in four rounds of hellacious action to become the EUWC World Heavyweight Champion for an unprecedented seventh time.  However, with the EUWC at the height it is currently at, this seventh reign may be his toughest to date.  Immediately following the Dupree Memorial, Matt Pickstock announced that there would be a number one contenders match with the three men who managed to make the Final Four.  Those men were Daymon, Nero & Angel of Death.  When the Triple Threat match reached it's end, Rocko Daymon emerged as the victor, setting up the rematch from the Championship Tournament Finals at the Dupree Cup. That match is being heralded as one of the most amazing Championship matches in quite some time. Both Daymon and STEALTH were fatigued and traded close falls back and forth.  In the end, STEALTH managed to secure the victory with a roll up and won his seventh EUWC World Championship.  Now Rocko Daymon and STEALTH will meet in only their second encounter ever.  Can STEALTH once again defeat Rocko Daymon and retain his Championship or will Rocko Daymon finally win the big one after four years of coming close to finally securing the World Title? 20 Man Steel Cage Blood Bath Match Once again, twenty EUWC Superstars will step into the steel cage to do battle.  With the winner earning an EUWC World Heavyweight Championship match at any time within the next 365 days, the stakes could not be higher.  Twenty EUWC Superstars will do battle.  All 20 Men will draw numbers randomly from 1 to 20 to determine entry into the match. Two men start out.  Every two minutes another man enters the cage. Elimination occurs via pinfall, submission or failure to continue. The final man standing after 19 others have been eliminated will win the match and the Money in the Bank title shot.  And to top it all off, every man in the match is allowed to bring any weapon of their choice into the battle. In previous years men like Adam Benjamin, Stephen Greer, and HICAL have won the match and gone on to become Champion. This year, each of these 20 men look to recreate that success. With 20 men in the ring it shall be a huge mountain to climb.  At the end of the night, which one man will stand tall over 19 others, with the title shot?! Sean Taylor vs Sevyn When Sean Taylor secured a new EUWC contract and was once again able to grace our rings, critics and fans alike proclaimed that it was only a matter of time until he regained his EUWC World Championship.  However for Taylor his return has not been what one would call a successful one.  Sean Taylor made his in-ring return on Monday Night Brawl to face Sevyn in a No Disqualification match.  That stipulation was added by Sevyn at the last minute and it proved useful as Sevyn pulled off an amazing upset over Taylor. At this point critics didn't jump down Taylor's throat, but instead claimed that it was simply ring rust after being away from the ring for over a year.  A week later these two would battle in a tag team match, but the result would wind up the same as Sevyn managed to roll up Taylor for yet another impressive upset victory. Refusing to admit that Sevyn is the better man, Taylor has secured a rematch with the leader of Synn at Blood Bath, in a match that will be held under a stipulation to be named by Sean Taylor.  However many believe that the deviant mind of Sevyn is just too much for the former two time EUWC World Champion.  Sevyn's mind games include using the seven deadly sins to gain the upper hand. And while usually Sevyn would be backed by the likes of his Synn stable mates; Diabolic Forces and Dwight Jackson; he has not needed them in this situation.  Fans have been shocked that both victories have come cleanly over Taylor.  At Blood Bath, will Sevyn's mind games be too much for Taylor even in a match of his choosing, or will Taylor's stipulation alone be enough to throw the former International champion off his game? Angel of Death vs "The Ironman" Buck Naked Angel of Death had just completed an exhausting triple threat match... He was dejected that he failed to secure the victory and the Championship match with STEALTH.  However a vicious assault from Buck Naked shocked the world and forced AOD to forget about the Championship match at Blood Bath. Since returning to the EUWC several months ago, Buck Naked has been on a one way street with his only thought being to take out Angel of Death one on one.  These two men have long been on the same roster and many times passed each other on the way to the top and back... At Monday Night Brawl, Buck Naked assaulted AOD and injured his ankle after wrapping it in a steel chair and leaping off of the top rope onto it, not once... but twice!  AOD was carted out on a stretcher and the match was announce d soon after.  Being that Pickstock owed Buck a favor, he granted Buck his wish and has made this match a No Disqualification Street Fight. Can Buck Naked fulfill his mission of destroying AOD?  Or will AOD destroy Buck on his way back to the top?  And what will AOD's former friend Texas Lightning think of being bypassed for this match? "The Disease" Jay Smash vs "The Ego Buster" Dan Ryan In what is being described as another Dream Match, Jay Smash will meet Dan Ryan. This all started at Superiority when Ryan, Daymon and Benjamin interfered in the triple threat match involving Nero, Alucard and Jay Smash.  After Ryan's crew destroyed all three men, Ryan and company declared war on the rest of the EUWC.  Since then, the war has basically gone unanswered by the EUWC Superstars.  That is until Monday Night Brawl a few weeks ago.  That night, Ryan renewed his vow to destroy the others when he singled out Jay Smash in a match against Hawkeye.  The Ego Buster certainly busted Smash's ego allowing Hawkeye to gain what could be the biggest win of his career.  Now these two will finally lock horns for the very first time at Blood Bath. The question is, who will win the first battle in this all-out war?! Unification Match for the EUWC International Championship United States Champion "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin vs International Champion Jonathan Darkstar vs European Champion Cobris Grayson vs North American Champion "The Thriller" Braden Kincaid With the restart firmly in place, four Champions will become one at Blood Bath.  With perhaps more on the line than any other match, these four Champions will enter the ring to determine which one will be the lone International Champion when all is said and done.  United States Champion Adam Benjamin knows what it's like to be the best superstar in the ring, having held the World Heavyweight Title once before.  Jonathan Darkstar seems to be the rising star as he upset Sevyn for the International Gold.  Cobris Grayson ended one of the longest reigns in EUWC History when he beat Chris Comet for the European Championship.  But the real threat has to be the man who remains undefeated Braden Kincaid.  Not only has the Thriller managed an undefeated record for over a year, he even ended the career of Violent K...  Who can stop him?  Who will leave Blood Bath as the Champion? **CARD SUBJECT TO CHANGE**
