Letters to Chad

13 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MattPickstock — 13 August 2005 Organizing the upcoming Chad Dupree Memorial Show has brought back many memories and feelings of Chad. This thread is dedicated to the memory of a good friend. If you would like, please write a "Letter to Chad". I KNOW that Chad is up in heaven and he is reading everything we have to say. Chad is a great man, and while this thread, or the the Memorial Show may not do him justice, it is our way of saying thank you to an incredible man.
Please please please post a letter to Chad below... I am working on mine, and I want it to be perfect, before I post it.
For those who knew Chad, and loved him.... this thread is your chance to say your peace.... But ultimately, this thread is for Chad!
At this point, all I have to say is thank you Chad, and I love you. I will elaborate more in my letter when I finish it. I encourage anyone who knew Chad to respond to this thread.
It is my belief that Chad will be able to read these letters. And I am sure that up in heaven, he will shed a tear and more than likely .... he will smile!
I miss you Chad.... TD_Walker — 14 August 2005 It will take me a little while, but I will write something. James - Notta easy task for me, prolly many tears
