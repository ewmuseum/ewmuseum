EUWC Hall of Fame

30 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MattPickstock — 30 July 2005 I am currently in the process of re-designing the Hall of Fame section for the EUWC website...
When it is finished several superstars will be added / inducted for 2005. The list of this year's inductees is as follows... - "Mr. Main Event" Rob Sampson - Chad Dupree - Lord Alucard - The Apocalypse Also, I am going to incorporate the opposite of the Hall of Fame... The EUWC Hall of Shame...
2005 Inductees are: - Sledgehammer - Warrior II (and the Warriors) - The Elektrik Force I am open to other nominees for both categories so if anyone has any suggestions for either the Hall of Fame or the newly created Hall of Shame, please nominate them below, and give a brief explanation for why they should be inducted to either...
Thanks... The Moundfields — 30 July 2005 Hall of Shame Nominees: Trent Blackwell, Razorblade, D.N.A., Lee Satima, Tyrant, Dreadnaught, The Jackal: What do these 7 have in common? Well along with myself (Classy Mike C) they made up the contestants in the terrible Round Robin tournament. 8 rookies battling it out for a chance to win a shot at an EUWC title, hell it even made up four matches of the Excessive Force pay-per-view. Why do I escape nomination? Because I was the only one who made an EUWC career after it and i seemed like the only one who wanted to win the thing!
"The King Of Queens" Adam Anderson: For looking like he was going to single-handedly save the hardcore division, then walking out while being reigning champion.
Kris Crowley and Chris Puller: Two "superstars" whose claim to EUWC fame is putting in terrible performances in the 2004 Royal Rumble.
Troy Martin: Teamed with Jay K to lose against Kai Lewis and Havoc at Uprising. Jay K: Teamed with Troy Martin to lose against Kai Lewis and Havoc at Uprising AND had a horrible hip-hop gimmick.
Joe Johnson: The first man eliminated in the 20 Man Battle Royal at Superiority '04. Suffered the unenviable feat of being outlasted by BOTH Parker Brothers.
The Chill Pills: Clyde "Be On My Side" Mayonni and Chris "Don't Get Him Pissed" Roberts, perhaps the worst tag team in EUWC history.
Jay_Smash — 31 July 2005 Hall of SHAME: The SphinX: After a run of being probably the largest man that ever disgraced the EUWC ring, he was able to fight, not sure if he actually ever won a match, but certainly rped once or twice.... and even had matches when he was no longer taking action as his character. Also... I know this, as a friend of mine was this character.... He really didn't try.... and my goodness... the surprise when we watched how long SphinX was able to stick around for... What a Shame.... what a big Shame....
Paul Doom — 1 August 2005 I am both shocked and appauled that Doom is in neither of those catagories! Jay_Smash — 1 August 2005 Hall of Doom: Paul Doom-He is the perfect person for this category. AOD — 6 August 2005 Doom should be in the Hall of Shame ... he's the greatest black hole of momentum in this industry today! He'd suck the heat out of a Rocky Maivia return to TV!
