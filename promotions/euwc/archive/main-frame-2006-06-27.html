A very much angered Smash stares down at Shaft with sweat dripping from his face and the World Title hanging on his shoulder. Smash slowly reaches out at Shaft, then grabs the mic from his hand and waits for silence.] Jay Smash: Where do you think you are? You may think you have found yourself in a wrestling ring, surrounded with thousands of fans in attendance here to agree with every word you’ve got to say. You might even think everything that is coming out of your mouth is making any sense whatsoever. But here are the facts you’ve been so impatiently waiting for all night Shaft... You want to know how I won this title at Malicious Intent? You think I cheated some how? Maybe I drugged Mike’s water before the match… Or maybe I stuck metal in by boots or stuck a brass knuckle under the tape wrapped around my hands. Maybe I paid the referee off to somehow count every pin so perfectly that no referee could have done any better John you’re a moron! You see, the fact is, Mike C is no longer the champion. He pissed me off, and I proved everything I said I was going to prove. That I was no one hit wonder. My first title shot was no fluke! Mike C entered that ring knowing exactly what it was going to take to face any man trying to take away the World Heavyweight title from him. And I entered that ring, and I spread the disease! I took the lifeline of Pickstock’s Army away! You see, there was no cheating… there was no lame excuse for Mike C losing Sunday night. It was plain and simple victory, and now I… Am the EUWC World Heavyweight Champion! So John… f*ck you… [Jay Smash stomps John Shaft in the mid section and delivers The Disease from out of nowhere! Smash leaves Shaft down and lifeless in the ring as a few referees and medics arrive to check on John Shafts condition. John is placed on a stretcher with a neck brace on, while “Now Is the Time” by Dope hits once again, with Jay Smash on the turnbuckle, raising the World Heavyweight title in the air for the entire world to see.]
