A single spotlight appears at the top of the stage as a video reel of The Living Highlight's feats. After several highlights, "The Living Highlight" Johnny Dymondz appears at the top of the ramp, revelling in the boos showered on him.] Kandi: Hailing from Montreal, Quebec, Canada... [He raises both fists into the air, and white pyro shoot out up into the air for several seconds. He extends his middle fingers, which only infuriates the fans before bringing his arms down, inviting the fans to keep booing him. ] Kandi:... weighing in at 224 pounds... [He casually strolls down the ramp, talking trash to every fan that he lays his eyes on. He jogs the rest of the way, sliding into the ring from under the bottom rope. He kips up to his feet, then jumps onto the top turnbuckle and raises a fist into the air in front of a blinding surge of camera flashes. ] Kandi: ... he is "THE LIVING HIGHLIGHT" JOHNNY DYMONDZ!!!! John:  At Blood Bath this man came very close to United State gold, but fell short to Matt Falcons impressive victory. Scott:  Well what a hop and a skip from United States gold to World Heavyweight title. Kandi Reed:  And his opponent… [The sounds of whispering can be heard through the speakers as the lights dim and a blue spotlight shines on the stage. Water sprinkles down like rain across the stage while several strobe lights flash into the air. A flag props over the large screen on the stage with the letters "J.S." marked on the cloth. Several moments later, the whispering stops and "Riot" by Three Days Grace blasts through the PA system. Kandi Reed: Now making his way to the ring from Long Island, New York, weighing in at 265 pounds, he is the EUWC World Heavyweight Champion, Jay Smash!!! The lights stream across the arena, until Jay Smash pops through the curtain. Smash makes his way down as the rain continues to fall from above onto the ramp. Smash slides into the ring and rises to his feet. When he stands, he moves towards the turnbuckle, and climbs to the top with little effort. His body stands tall and his arms stretch to the sky with his hands in fists while the flashes from cameras take him away. Smash leaps back off the turnbuckle and lands on his feet to stand in the center of the ring.] John:  This is it!  World title on the line on Mainframe! [Both men have stepped to their corners with the referee in the center of the ring, holding up the World Heavyweight Championship for the fans to see.  The referee sends the title to the outside of the ring then motions for the sound of the bell.  Before the bell can sound, “Down With the Sickness” by Disturbed hits and Matt Pickstock makes his way down to the ring with his hands flaring and a powerful strut towards the bottom. Just as Pickstock reaches the bottom of the ramp, he turns and walks around the ring, then to the announcer table where he stands staring at the ring, his back turned to the commentators Scott Marchman and John Shaft. Jay Smash stares back outside the ring while Johnny Dymondz watches both men from behind Smash and the ref inside the ring.  Pickstock pulls out a pair of brass knuckles in a clear view for Smash to see, but before Smash can realize what is going on, Pickstock turns around and strikes John Shaft in the side of the head!  Shaft pauses in his seat as his eyes grow heavy.  As Shaft begins to slip away, Pickstock grabs Shaft by the side of the head and throws Shaft out of his announcer chair.  Pickstock throws the headset on and sits down as Smash looks on enraged.] Scott:  What in god’s name!?!? [At this time, Smash begins to turn when Dymondz rolls Smash up from behind and goes for the first pin in the match. 1…. 2…. Smash kicks out and both men head back to there feet where Smash quickly races to the ropes and back with a huge clothesline!] Pickstock:  What’s wrong there Scott, your partner looks a little out of it tonight.  Perhaps it was that sandwich he just ate…  That was a joke Scott, I just made it up myself. Scott:  Well forgive me Mr. Pickstock if I’m not playing as if I’m in a good mood at the moment. Pickstock:  Oh boy Scott, shut up. [Pickstock stands up and cracks the brass knuckles against Scott Marchman’s skull, causing him to lose control of his body and fall to the floor next to his chair!  Pickstock fixes his jacket then takes a seat while the match continues.] Pickstock:  And what a shot by Pickstock! [Smash watches on as Pickstock laughs to himself sitting in Shaft’s chair.  Pickstock waves back at Smash while Smash turns and finds himself thrown against the ropes with a dropkick by Dymondz! [Smash begins to climb to the top with one arm while the other is in a fist punching the back of Johnny Dymondz’ head! Smash makes it to the top and begins to stand Dymondz up to his feet.  When Dymondz stands he elbows Smash in the gut to counter the top turnbuckle predicament.  Dymondz successfully turns around and tries to toss Smash away, but Smash holds on.  Dymondz pushes harder and Smash pulls back, taking Dymondz with him off the top and into a lock up in midair!  Smash sends Dymondz crashing to the canvas with a superplex that shakes the entire ring!  Dymondz rolls a couple times till his body is next to the ropes, then he begins to keep himself still as the sting in his back grows worse.] Pickstock:  Well this looks like the perfect time to tell you about the new upcoming EUWC Mainframe vs. Blackout 2007.  Now with a huge new list of superstars to play as, you have the option to use all your old favorites as well as legends in the EUWC.  Singles matches with normal rules to Ring of Fire matches to burn your very own Jay Smash alive. Well, it looks like things are getting good again, so let’s take a look. [While Pickstock was speaking, Smash and Dymondz had climbed back to their feet and Smash began to throw continuous punches into Dymondz.  Smash whipped Dymondz from one side to the other then took a chance of running forward only to find himself planted into the turnbuckle chest first!  Dymondz now delivering shoulder thrusts to Smash’s back as Pickstock smiles once again. Dymondz pulls Smash away from the corner and turns around to deliver a neckbreaker that takes Smash to the mat!  Dymondz goes for a cover! 1…. 2…. Smash kicks out!  Dymondz picks Smash back up off the mat and delivers a snap suplex.  Dymondz goes for another pin. 1… 2… Smash kicks out once again!  Dymondz grabs Smash by the back of the head and begins to slaps him in the face a few times till Smash pushes Dymondz away!  As Dymondz backs away, Smash grabs Dymondz by the wrist and pulls him back and into a headbutt!  The crowd makes an “ohhh” as Dymondz falls to his knees after the headbutt connects hard.  Smash holds on to Dymondz and delivers a second headbutt!  Dymondz begins to lose his balance as he tries to keep himself up, but Smash pulls Dymondz back into a third headbutt! Smash releases Dymondz and watches him drop to the mat while Pickstock stares at the announcer booth in disgust.] Pickstock:  This is just sick.  What a sick bastard.  What a sick monster this dumb ape Jay Smash!  He’s lost so many damn brain cells that he doesn’t care what he bashes his head into!  He’s a damn moron!  And if he finds himself unable to continue to wrestle due to sever brain damage, I guess he will have to just forfeit the title!  So go ahead Smash, do all the damn headbutts you want, you’re digging your own grave! [Smash watches as Dymondz begin to stand, but as Smash moves forward, Dymondz rolls out of the ring and stands on the opposite side of the announcer table with his back turned to Pickstock. Pickstock begins to yell out at Dymondz, then pulls off the head set and walks around the table trying to tell Dymondz to get back in the ring.  While Pickstock is pointing at the ring, Smash springboards off the top rope and lands on both Pickstock and Dymondz with a cross body! Pickstock begins to smile, rooting Dymondz on for what he is planning to do. The crowd continues to boo as Dymondz raises Smash up slowly and stares back at the announcer table.  Dymondz pulls Smash closer and closer to the table until Smash’s weight becomes too much. Dymondz tries to pull, but Smash does not budge.  The crowd begins to cheer as Dymondz has a hard time, and Matt Pickstock once again looks furious.  Before Dymondz can release Smash’s arm, Smash pulls his own arm back and sends Dymondz forward.  Smash picks Dymondz off the ground and delivers a spin buster to Dymondz through the announcer table!!! Pickstock throws his hands to his head as he watches Dymondz go through.  The table shatters across ring side with Dymondz lying in the rubble.  Smash slides back into the ring as the referee makes it to a seven count.  The referee continues as Dymondz lies motionless in the destruction of the announcer table outside the ring.  Pickstock now stares at Smash, no longer with any interest in Dymondz as the referee makes the final call: Ref: 10!!! The referee calls for the bell!] Kandi Reed:  Ladies and gentlemen, the following match has ended as a result of a count out.  Therefore, here is your winner and STILL the EUWC World Heavyweight Champion, Jay Smash!!! [Pickstock watches as Smash celebrates once again with his arm raised and the title held in his hands.  Pickstock walks around the ring and begins to stare back as he heads up the ramp disappointed once more.  “Riot” by Three Days Grace hits as the fans cheer and Smash celebrates another championship victory on Mainframe.]
