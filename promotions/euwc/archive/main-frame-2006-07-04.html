Jay is looking around at them till Samuel Roundtree tries to enter but Jay is ready for him. Samuel gets out of the ring but from behind Smash, Texas Lightning has a steel chair in hand and goes to attack Jay but the referee snatches it away from TL. TL and the referee argue but Jay grabs TL from behind and knocks him out of the ring. Nero is up by this time and looks at the situation. He looks at Jay when Buck Naked and Samuel Roundtree enter the ring to attack them but Nero and Jay take them down with right hands but from behind Mike C hits Jay Smash with a chair and TL does so too to Nero! Scott: DAMMIT! Pickstock’s Army has ruined this match! John: No wait, they were obviously offering Nero and Smash chairs to sit down and rest. The referee calls for the bell as Pickstock’s Army is beating on the two men but the crowd erupts as John Doe enters the ring to even the odds! John Doe goes to attack Mike C but from behind Doe Samuel hits him with a low blow and Mike C takes him out with a spinebuster! Pickstock grabs a chair and gets ready to hit Nero with it from behind but Nero turns around and grabs the chair before Pickstock can hit. Pickstock looks scared as Nero snatches the chair from him and goes to hit but Buck Naked hits Nero with a low blow from behind and Nero drops the chair. Buck grabs Nero and hits him with a Starmaker Powerbomb on the steel chair! By this time Alucard has entered the ring and looks at his fallen team members. Alucard then looks around him to see Pickstock’s Army circling around him. Scott: This is not good. John: Alucard’s wishing he was with the Army now! Before Pickstock’s Army can attack, Alucard starts laughing maniacally. Pickstock looks surprised at this as no one can figure what’s wrong. Suddenly the lights go out and the arena is completely dark. In a spine-chilling moment, the sound of a boy is heard who begins to sing the word "halleluja". With one final word, the song breaks into guitar as "Halleluja" by Rammstein kicks in. The entrance lights flash different colours as Doom appears at the entrance way in his classic trench coat. The crowd goes electric as Pickstock and his army cannot believe it! John: WHAT THE HELL! Scott: IT’S PAUL DOOM! PAUL DOOM IS HERE ON MAINFRAME!!! Doom takes off his trench coat on the entrance and looks intently towards the ring. Pickstock sends TL and Samuel to get him but Doom takes down Samuel with a big boot and hits TL with an uppercut to the jaw and whips him into the barricade! Doom enters the ring and stands next to Alucard as they face Buck and Mike C. Doom starts to hammer away on Buck while Alucard attacks Mike with right hands! Alucard gets Mike over the rope and out of the ring while Doom kicks Buck Naked in the gut and delivers a Doomsday Powerbomb to Buck out of the ring! Pickstock runs away from the ring and the Army is all knocked outside. Alucard and Doom look at each other with uneasy tension till Alucard offers Doom a hand. Doom looks at it and looks around the crowd till he finally accepts the handshake. Nero and Doe get up and look at Doom as Alucard grabs a mic. Alucard: Matt, when I said you would meet your ‘doom’, yeah, I DID mean it literally! And you’ll be seeing it again and so will your Army. They will see Nero, John Doe, a yet to be named partner and Paul Doom, at SUMMER SIZZLER! Pickstock’s Army is regrouping outside and looks into the ring as John Doe, Nero, Paul Doom and Lord Alucard are standing tall in the ring with each other’s hands in the air as Mainframe comes to a close.
