MattPickstock — 13 August 2005 As of this date and time, the EUWC has learned that the Odinist has turned down the challenge of Commander T. Waring for Summer Sizzler. RBW's the Odinist has been upset since the change of ownership in Red Branch Wrestling, and since that change, has decided to abandon the RBW and the EUWC European Championship. As of this report, there is no replacement for the European Title Match at Summer Sizzler. However rumours state that an announcement will be made at Main Frame concerning this PPV match!
