This was a tournament final. On 09.28.97, the International Title was unified with the North American Title.  HICAL defeated Chet Jenkins to unify the belts. EUWC went on hiatus from April 1998.  The title remained vacant until August, 1999. This was a match for the vacant Championship. This was a Six Pack match.  Other participants included Dan Sokolov, STEALTH, Erik kelly & Neighborhood Sniper. Ray Santana forfeited the title when he was awarded a World Title match with Dan Sokolov. This was a tournament final. The EUWC went on hiatus in November, 2000.  The title remained vacant until September, 2001. This was fatal four way match for the vacant title. This was a two fall triple threat match for the International & United States Championships, which also included Larry Serson. This was a four man Hell in a Cell match for the International & U.S. Titles.  Other participants were Gavin Glass & Simon. Serson pinned Glass in the deciding fall to win both titles. EUWC went on hiatus.  The title remained vacant until April, 2003. This was a tournament final. Nick Marshall was involved in a serious car accident which ended his career, and thus vacated the title. This was a tournament final. Sean Taylor was the Special Guest Referee.
