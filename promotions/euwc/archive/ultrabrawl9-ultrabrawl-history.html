Ultra Brawl — Event History

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ultra Brawl Event History August 25, 1996 Joe Louis Arena - Detroit, Michigan EUWC Count Down 3 Towel Match: The Hitman defeated "Wonderful" Walter Williamson with a sharpshooter in 10:13. 4-Way Tag Team Match: The Outsiders defeated the Shadow Warriors, the Gang Bangas, and the Twin Cobras in 11:42. EUWC Ultra Brawl Pay Per View Results Big Daddy defeated the Bad Guy with a jack-knife power bomb in 3:21. Street Fight: Emilio & Insane defeated Generation X in 10:43. Warror Armageddon pinned Dr. Who after a choke slam in 1:19. EUWC JuniorWeight Championship Match: The Perfect Pugilest defeated Sweet Daddy Bad Ass with a Stone Cold Stunner in 17:52 to regain the JuniorWeight Championship. The Darksider pinned John Shaft after the RIP Pile Driver in 7:52. Retirement Match #1: Team PSD defeated Team Wolf in 17:13 forcing Team Wolf to retire. Retirement Match #2: The Black Panthers defeated the High Flying Vegetables in 0:56 forcing the Veggies to retire. EUWC International Championship Match: X.Plosion defeated the Crippler by disqualification in 16:24 to retain the International Championship. Locker Room Brawl: "Cruel" Kevin Gray and Ben "the Body" Lane battled to a draw. No Holds Barred: Ahmed the PowerHouse pinned No Feelings after a Pearl River Plunge in 2:13. No Pain & The Lawman defeated No Fear via forfeit in 0:00. I Respect You Match: Warrior II defeated Warrior in 10:05. EUWC International Tag Team Championship Match: Killers Inc defeated the Missouri Connection when Killer #1 pinned Phantom X in 1:04 to retain the International Tag Team Championship. EUWC World Tag Team Championship Match, No Holds Barred: The Beantown Bruisers defeated the Specialists when Duke Barrett powerbombed and pinned Roger Thorpe in 16:43 to win the World Tag Team Titles. EUWC World Championship Match No Holds Barred: STEALTH pinned StreetFighter after a Stealth Bomber in 19:42 to retain the EUWC World Championship. August 31, 1997 Reunion Arena - Dallas, Texas EUWC Ultra Brawl II Pay Per View Results John Shaft pinned "The Russian Bear" Dan Sokolov in 11:58 with a small package. EUWC World Juniorweight Championship Match: Michael Foreman defeated "Ice Man" Brent Morris with a roll-up 12:11 to become the NEW Juniorweight Champion! EUWC United States Championship Match: "Dangerous" Don Hall beat YAHOO with an STF in 32:42 to become the NEW United States Champion. Unification Match: The Midnight Massacre defeated Cause & Effect when Knightquest pinned Effect after a falling splash, in 6:12 to become the EUWC Unified International Tag Team Champions. EUWC North American Championship Match: Chet "Stardust" Jenkins pinned "All American" Dave the Rave after the Stardrop in 8:17 to retain the North American Title. EUWC World Tag Team Championship Match: The Titans defeated the Black Panthers via disqualification due to outside interference from the Chaos Factor. No Holds Barred Match 1997 Champion of Champions, Sweet Daddy Bad Ass pinned Beast after Thunderbird hit Beast inadvertantly in 10:25. Falls Count Anywhere Match: "Cruel" Kevin Gray pinned Ben "The Body" Lane in 9:14. EUWC International Championship Match: HICAL pinned WarHawk with the HICAL Bomb in 12:31 to win the title. Steel Cage Six Man Tag Team Match: The New Apocalypse (YAHOO, Static and "Superninja" Chris Michaels) defeated KAOS (Kain, Genesis Taylor & HICAL) when Michaels pinned Kain after a Dragon Suplex from the top of the cage in 13:26. HICAL turned on KAOS and joined the Apocalypse. EUWC World Championship Street Fight: STEALTH pinned X.Plosion with the Stealth Bomber in 17:26, to become the EUWC World Champion for a third time! October 24, 1999 Sky Dome - Toronto, Ontario, Canada EUWC Ultra Brawl III Pay Per View Results EUWC United States Championship Match: Angel of Death pinned "The Degenerate" Kurt Atlas with a Shooting Star Press to become the NEW EUWC United States Champion. MIR defeated Mythological with a chokeslam. EUWC Hardcore Championship Ladder Match: Neighborhood Sniper defeated Bibyl to become the NEW EUWC Hardcore Champion. Savior made Freak submit to a Figure Four Leglock. Saul and The Chameleon battled to a double count-out. EUWC World Tag Team Championship Four Corners Match: Team PSD defeated the Midnight Massacre, the Blood Brothers & The American Mafia when the third member of Team PSD - T-38, illegally entered the ring and scored the pinfall on E.J. Lilly. Team PSD became the NEW EUWC World Tag Team Champions. Big Country pinned "G" Funk with the Perfection. EUWC International Championship Match: EUWC International Champion "The Man" Fred Rogers defeated Triple T by disqualification to retain the EUWC International Championship. MAIN EVENT - Six Pack Match for the EUWC World Heavyweight Championship **Special Referee: STEALTH** HICAL defeated Apocalypse, Chet Jenkins, Sean Fein, Ray Santana & Dan Sokolov when HICAL pinned Apocalypse after having Fred Rogers help him beat up Apocalypse, Chet Jenkins, Sokolov and STEALTH. Rogers hit STEALTH's hand to the mat for the three as HICAL was crowned the NEW EUWC World Heavyweight Champion. August 27, 2000 First Union Center - Philadelphia, Pennsylvania EUWC Ultra Brawl IV Pay Per View Results Johnny Wannabe won a 20 man Battle Royale, last eliminating Vladimire Dracos. EUWC World Tag Team Championship Match: The American Mafia (E.J. Lilly & Timmy Hoffa) defeated Extreme Lightning (Kritical Kondition & Mark Thunder) when Hoffa pinned Kritical Kondition with the Knock Off! The American Mafia became the new EUWC World Tag Team Champions. The Hollywood Heroes defeated Armagedon when The Specialist pinned Higginson with a Swanton bomb. EUWC North American Championship Match: The Alaskan Assassin pinned "Red Hot" Robert Keller with the Alaskan Snow Plow to win the EUWC North American Championship! EUWC United States Championship - Ladder Match: "The Terrorist" David Harten defeated Bibyl to retain the United States Championship. World Title - #1 Contenders Match: Cuchulain and "The Eradicator" Erik Kelly wrestled to a double count out. EUWC International Championship Match: Kritical Kondition defeated the Chameleon with the Pull the Plug. Kritical Kondition became the New EUWC International Champion. Bow To The Masters Match - Sledgehammer on a Pole: Ray Santana defeated Awesome after Santana knocked Awesome out with a sledgehammer shot to the face. The referee stopped the match. EUWC World Heavyweight Championship - Iron Man Match: Neighborhood Sniper defeated Triple T. After 60 minutes, the two men were tied at five falls a piece. In overtime, Sniper pinned TTT with the Sniper Shot to get the sixth and deciding fall. Neighborhood Sniper won the EUWC World Heavyweight Championship! August 31, 2003 Corel Center - Ottawa, Ontario, Canada EUWC Ultra Brawl V Pay Per View Results EUWC World Tag Team Championship Match: Nero & Big G defeated The Action Faction when Big G pinned Max Action after Nero hit Max with one of the tag team belts. Nero and Big G won the EUWC World Tag Team Championship. International Title - #1 Contender: Cole Justice defeated Apocalypse when he forced Apocalypse to pass out to the Final Justice. EUWC Hardcore Championship - Canadian Ladder Match: Marshall Snow defeated Virus to win the vacant EUWC Hardcore Championship! World Title - #1 Contender: Damian Dante Stone defeated Buck Naked when DDS pinned Buck with the Judge, Jury, Execution! Wreckage won a 20 Man Battle Royale, last eliminating Chibi Devil. Order of Elimination: Ryan Vierra by Johnny Vandal... Milton Parker by Rage... Rage by Bradley Parker... El Nutso by Lee Satima... Jim Roberts by Lee Satima... Tony DeLucci by Tyrant... Tyrant by Noxious Reign, Johnny Vandal, Fate & Wreckage... Johnny Vandal by Tom Armstrong... Mike C by Noxious Reign & Shane Saunders... Black Widow by Chibi Devil... Assassin by Chibi Devil... Noxious Reign by Chibi Devil... Tom Armstrong by Chibi Devil... Fate by Chibi Devil... Sevyn by Chibi Devil... Shane Saunders by Wreckage... Bradley Parker by Lee Satima... Lee Satima by Chibi Devil... Chibi Devil by Wreckage... EUWC United States Championship Match: Scott Samson defeated T-Charger with a small package to win the EUWC United States Championship. EUWC International Championship - Stairway to Hell Match: Sean Taylor defeated Samuel Roundtree to retain the EUWC International Championship. Last Man Standing Match: Commissioner Matt Pickstock defeated Angel of Death in a Last Man Standing Match. Pickstock was able to get the win due to some heavy interference from the Regime. EUWC World Heavyweight Championship - Hell in a Cell Match: Lord Alucard defeated Paul Doom with the TKO. Lord Alucard became the New EUWC World Heavyweight Champion. September 12, 2004 Sky Dome - Toronto, Ontario, Canada EUWC Ultra Brawl VI Pay Per View Results 10 Man Battle Royale: The Monster won a 10 Man Battle royale last eliminating Apocalypse. Order of Elimination: Myles Jordan by Kerry Wayne... Violent K by The Monster... Gunner Larensson by Volcano.... CasH by Brad Freeman... Kerry Wayne by Volcano... Mikey Cook by The Monster... Brad Freeman by Volcano... Volcano by Apocalypse... Apocalypse by The Monster... Loser Leaves EUWC Match: Evol pinned Apocalypse with the Extinction. Apocalypse was forced to leave the EUWC. EUWC 52 Wild Championship - Casket Match: "Classy" Mike C defeated "The Sandman" Marcus Slayton after shoving him in the casket. Mike C retained the EUWC 52 Wild Title. Falls Count Anywhere Match: Tyrone Walker defeated Stephon Kardell with the TKO2K. EUWC United States Championship - I Quit Match: Sevyn defeated Donny Collins after throwing Collins through a table. Sevyn retained the EUWC United States Championship. Dominion's Circle Match: Havoc defeated Malice after hitting the Shooting Star Press off of the Ultra Brawl set. Malice was forced to un-mask due to the loss, while Havoc won his freedom from the Dominion. EUWC World Tag Team Championship - Tournament Finals: Diabolic Forces defeated the Moundfields when Molloch pinned Jaz Moundfield with the triple powerbomb. Diabolic Forces won the EUWC World Tag Team Championship. EUWC World Tag Team Championship Match: Nero & Big G defeated Diabolic Forces when Lynch Garrisson pinned Mammon with the Showstopper Chokeslam. Nero & Big G won the EUWC World Tag Team Championship. EUWC European Championship Match: The Monster defeated Jack "The Ripper" with the Bucharest Blast to win the EUWC European Championship. EUWC International Championship Match: Sean Taylor defeated Lord Alucard with the Blood Rush to retain the EUWC International Title. Angel of Death & Texas Lightning defeated Ray Santana and Triple T when AOD pinned Santana with the Going Home. Hell in a Cell Match: "The Definitive" Danny Collins defeated STEALTH with the Definition. Due to prematch stipulation, STEALTH was forced to resign as EUWC Director of Authority. EUWC World Heavyweight Championship Match: Holocaust defeated Damian Stone with the Ragnarok to win the EUWC World Heavyweight Championship for a second time. September 25, 2005 Ralph Wilson Stadium - Orchard Park, Buffalo Hills, New York EUWC Ultra Brawl VII Pay Per View Results Tag Team Street Fight: The Moundfields defeated the Noblemen when Jaz Moundfield pinned King Buxton after the Monster interfered tossing Buxton off of the stage and through several stacked tables. WWC Continental Championship Match: Nero defeated "Vagabond" RJ Harris by disqualification when Angel of Death interfered.  Harris retained the WWC Continental Championship. EUWC European Championship - Tables, Ladders & Chairs Triple Threat Match: "Commander" T. Waring defeated Lt. Hawkeye & TT McGraw when Waring secured the title.  T. Waring retained the European Championship. EUWC World Tag Team Championship Match: Fire & Ice defeated STEALTH & Jordan Lockhart by disqualification when the Moundfields interfered attacking Fire & Ice.  STEALTH & Lockhart retained the World Tag Team Championship. EUWC United States Championship - Empty Arena Ladder Match: Held at Madison Square Garden... "Classy" Mike C defeated Samuel Roundtree when he was able to retrieve the belt after a discus heart punch.  Mike C became the New EUWC United States Champion. EUWC International Championship Match: Rocko Daymon defeated "The Black Jesus" Tyrone Walker with the Brain Rocker Redux to win the International Championship. EUWC World Heavyweight Championship - Hell in the Cell Gauntlet Match #1: "The King of Pain" Stephen Greer defeated "The Definitive" Danny Collins with a rollup reversal to retain the title. EUWC World Heavyweight Championship - Hell in the Cell Gauntlet Match #2: "The King of Pain" Stephen Greer defeated Angel of Death when Nero interfered and hit AOD with the Nero Kick.  Greer retained the World Heavyweight Title. EUWC World Heavyweight Championship - Hell in the Cell Gauntlet Match #3: "The King of Pain" Stephen Greer wrestled "The Disease" Jay Smash to a double knock out.  Stephen Greer retained the EUWC World Heavyweight Championship. September 24, 2006 Staples Center - Los Angeles, California EUWC Ultra Brawl VIII Pay Per View Results EUWC North American Championship Match: Newfy Jack defeated "Commander" T. Waring when Newfy Jack pinned Waring after a Last Call off the top rope.  Newfy Jack retained the North American Title. EUWC United States Championship Match: Draco defeated Matt Falcon with a pin reversal to win the EUWC United States Championship. "The Hazard" Lynch Garrison defeated Damien Dante Stone with the Terrarizer. Cobris Grayson defeated Sanket Desai with an inside cradle roll-up. EUWC World Tag Team Championship - Triple Threat Match: Diabolic Forces defeated The Skyscrappers and JD Infinity & Distributor of Pain when Mammon pinned Iggy Romanov after Molloch hit him with a title belt. Diabolic Forces retained the World Tag Team Championship. EUWC European Championship - Inter-Gender Handicap Tables, Ladders & Chairs Match: Chris Comet and Cindy Youngblood defeated "The Icon" Jared McAllister when Comet secured the title belt. Chris Comet retained the European Championship. Celtic Pride & Violent K defeated Tony DeLucci & The Clique when Violent K pinned DeLucci with a roundhouse kick. EUWC Tag Team Championship - Tag Team Turmoil Match: The Four Star Pimps defeated the Ninja Stars... Black Death Gauntlet defeated the Four Star Pimps.... Black Death Gauntlet defeated Steve Stone & Kobra... Black Death Gauntlet defeated Pickstock's Army... Black death Gauntlet won the EUWC Tag Team Championship. EUWC International Championship Match: Hawkeye defeated Sevyn with the Hawkeye Driver to retain the International Championship. "Classy" Mike C defeated Lord Alucard with a flying elbow drop off of a ladder. EUWC Championship - Barbed Wire Ultimate X Match: "The Hound of Ulster" Cuchulain and Angel of Death battled to a No Contest when both men retrieved the title belt at the exact same time.  Cuchulain retained the EUWC Championship. EUWC World Heavyweight Championship - Elimination Chamber Match: Jay Smash & Adam Benjamin started the match... Texas Lightning entered third... Nero entered fourth...  Nero eliminated Texas Lightning with the Nero Kick... Daymon entered fifth... Paul Doom entered sixth... Benjamin pinned Doom with a rollup and a handful of tights... Daymon pinned Adam Benjamin after Angel of Death interfered... Daymon pinned Nero with a Daymonplex off the top of a chamber.... Jay Smash pinned Daymon with a small package to win the match and become the New EUWC World Heavyweight Champion. September 30, 2007 Reliant Stadium - Houston, Texas EUWC Ultra Brawl IX Pay Per View Results EUWC International Championship Match: Jonathan Darkstar defeated "The Hatchet" Man" Violent K with the Darkstar Rising to become the New EUWC International Champion. Texas Bullrope Match: Allister Crowley defeated Kenny. EUWC United States Championship Match: "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin defeated Damien Dante Stone with the Shining Wizard to retain the United States Title. EUWC North American Championship Match: "The Thriller" Braden Kincaid defeated "Commander" T. Waring with the Thrill Ride.  Braden Kincaid retained the North American Title. EUWC European Championship Match: Cobris Grayson defeated Chris Comet with teh Snake Pitr to win the EUWC European Championship. Team Blackout defeated Team Main Frame in a Ten man Tag Team Elimination Bout. Jay Smash pinned Newfy Jack with the Smash Attack. Sevyn made Buck Naked submit to the Lazy Boy. Farris Fusion pinned Angel of Death after Farris & Fred Fusion hit AOD with the Fusion. STEALTH pinned Lord Alucard with the Stealth Bomber. The Phoenix pinned Farris Fusion with the Flight of the Phoenix. The Phoenix pinned Fred Fusion with the Ground Zero. Jay Smash pinned Sevyn with the Smash Attack. Jay Smash pinned The Phoenix with the Smash Attack. STEALTH pinned Jay Smash after three consecutive Stealth Bombers to win the match and be the sole survivor for his team. EUWC Tag Team Championship - EUWC World Tag Team Championship Unification Match: "The Ego Buster" Dan Ryan and Rocko Daymon defeated Diabolic Forces when Daymon pinned Molloch with the Daymonplex.  Daymon & Dan Ryan became the EUWC World Tag Team Champions and retained the EUWC Tag Team Titles; unifying both titles.. EUWC World Heavyweight Championship - EUWC Championship Unification Match: EUWC World Heavyweight Champion "The Great & Almighty" Nero battled EUWC Champion Hawkeye to a draw when members of both the Blackout and Main Frame rosters interfered stopping the match.  After the match, Matt Pickstock emerged claiming that both titles had been unified and that one Unified Championship was vacant.  A new Champion would be crowned at the Chad Dupree Memorial Cup.
