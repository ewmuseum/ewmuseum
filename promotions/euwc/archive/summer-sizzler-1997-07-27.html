He even brought his own little posse with him- all to no avail. He was trying, but he knew deep down in his heart that he couldn't beat me. He knew he'd met the man who was going to lead this sport into the 21st century. He knew he was in the ring with future of wrestling. Stealth is the PAST, and me and KAOS are the present AND future. I've got my bodyguard (points at KAIN) I've got my title (taps the EUWC world title belt) I've got KAOS (Screen shows a group photo of KAOS for a few seconds), and KAOS rules professional wrestling the way it should be." SA: This match is a Triple Threat Match for the EUWC World Championship! ("Wipeout" by Surfaris blares and the fans continue to go NUTS!) From Boston Massachusetts, weighing 243 pounds, here is the EUWC United States Champion, YAHOO! ("Simply The Best" by Tina Turner blares and the fans boos drown it out.) And finally, from Three Mile Island, weighing 292 pounds, and guided to the ring by Kain, here is the EUWC World Heavyweight Champion, X.Plosion!!! Match #10 No Time Limit EUWC World Heavyweight Championship Triple Threat Match 1997 Champion of Champions Sweet Daddy Bad Ass -vs.- United States Champion YAHOO -vs.- EUWC World Heavyweight Champion X.Plosion All three men circled eachother as the match began. Sweet daddy came forward and locked up with XP. Sweet Daddy shoved XP back and after doing so was blindsided by YAHOO with a clothesline. YAHOO then went after XP and stomped on him but XP threw several fists into the gut of YAHOO. Sweet Daddy got up and hammered on the back of YAHOO and then pounded on XP's skull. Sweet Daddy pulled XP up and slammed him. Sweet Daddy whipped XP off the ropes and clotheslined him down. YAHOO rolled up Sweet Daddy, 1... 2... kick out! XP stomped on Sweet Daddy and YAHOO swept the legs out from under the World Champion. YAHOO pounded the head of XP while Sweet Daddy got up and forcefuly removed the US Champ off of XP. Sweet Daddy whipped YAHOO off the ropes and nailed him with a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker and then dropped an elbow. XP nailed Sweet Daddy with a boot to the skull. XP pulled Sweet Daddy up and gorilla press slammed him to the outside of the ring. Out from the crowd came Beast who tore into Sweet Daddy with awesome force. He whipped him into the guard-rail and then dropped him with a spinebuster slam. Inside the ring YAHOO whipped XP off the ropes and caught him in a hurricarana. YAHOO covered, 1... 2... kick out! YAHOO picked up XP and whipped him into the corner and followed in with a Stinger Splash. He then executed a hiptoss out of the corner and dropped an elbow to the skull. YAHOO pulled XP up and nailed him with a suplex followed by a kneedrop. YAHOO bounced off the ropes and attempted a legdrop but XP rolled out of the way. XP got up and pounded on YAHOO with stomps and kicks and then dropped a knee to the gut. He whipped YAHOO off the ropes and caught him with a powerslam, 1... 2... kick out! Sweet Daddy tried to get back into the ring after the attack from Beast, but agains out from nowhere, came Thunderbird and he pounded on Sweet Daddy with chairs and rammed him into the ringpost. Thunderbird pounded on Sweet Daddy and then whipped him into the ring apron. Inside the ring, XP whipped YAHOO off the ropes and attempted a clothesline but YAHOO countered with a crucifix, 1... 2... kick out! YAHOO got up and threw an awesome forearm at XP. He whipped XP off the ropes and dropkicked him. XP fell through the ropes and YAHOO was about to follow when Genesis Taylor flew into the ring and pounded on YAHOO from behind and nailed him with his patented lariat and then applied the Revelation. On the outside, Tom Leonard came out and joined the attack on Sweet Daddy and made sure he couldn't continue. Leonard got into the ring and Taylor held YAHOO. Leonard grabbed a chair and bounced off the ropes and attempted to hit YAHOO but YAHOO ducked and Leonard hit Taylor. YAHOO grabbed Leonard and pounded him. XP snuck up on YAHOO and spun him around and kicked him in the gut. XP executed the Mushroom Cloud onto the chair that Leonard brought into the ring, 1... 2... 3! SA: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner by pinfall in 17:34 and STILL EUWC World Heavyweight Champion, is X.Plosion! X.Plosion continued to pound on YAHOO with repeated stomps. Kain, The Outsiders and Taylor joined in until STEALTH, Static and the Black Panthers bombarded the ring and got into an awesome brawl with KAOS. STEALTH and XP got into it trading blows and battling their hearts out. Static and Kain hooked it up while the Panthers battled the Outsiders and Taylor! The fans were going nuts! Don Hall emerged from the dressing room and made his way to ringside. From the crowd, we saw USSR (Sokolov, Equalizer and Pride & Honour) emerge. They all got into it except Hall who stood back and watched the melee as EUWC Summer Sizzler 1997 went off the air.
