[Suddenly, the lights go out in the arena as “Judith” begins to play. The crowd goes wild, while Kandi announces inside the ring.] Kandi: And their opponents. First, making his way to the ring, from Tacoma, Washington, weighing in at 243 pounds, Rocko Daymon!!! Rocko Daymon storms to the ring and pounces on TL but Mike C attacked Rocko from behind and now both TL and Mike are stomping away on a lone Rocko. Mike is ordering the referee to bring the cage down but the referee is denying it. Just then “Now is the Time” by Dope hits the speakers as Mike C looks alert. Jay Smash runs down to the ring and starts going at it with Texas Lightning & Mike till Jay whips Mike across the ropes and takes him down with a spinebuster! TL attacks Jay with right hands and goes for an Irish whip but Jay reverses it and hits TL with a belly to belly suplex! TL rolls out of the ring while Jay is calling for the cage to come down. Mike C gets up on his feet to see the cage being lowered and then sees Jay Smash with a smile on his face waiting impatiently. Scott: Jay Smash wants to go face to face with the Champ! John: But this is supposed to be a tag team match! They need to get the other two in the ring! The referee outside locks the door as Mike C is trying to get his partner in the ring while outside TL is yelling at the referee outside to open the door but Rocko Daymon gets TL and rams him into the cage! Rocko throws TL over the barricade and Rocko jumps over and starts punching at him. Both men fight into the crowd while Mike C and Jay Smash are still in the ring and circle around till they both tie up and Jay gets Mike C in a headlock but Mike C pushes him across the ropes and sends him across the ropes for an inverted atomic drop! As Jay is staggering, Mike throws him onto the cage wall! Jay rebounds back and Mike hits him with a German Suplex! Mike then goes to the leg and it looks like he’s going for a Classyleaf but instead Mike monkey-tosses Jay face-first into the cage wall! As Jay is dangling on the ropes, Mike brings the middle rope over Jay’s neck and starts choking Jay with the ropes! The referee finally gets Mike off of Jay but Mike then pulls Jay out of the ropes and then throws him head first between the opposite ropes and into the cage! Jay is hanging on the ropes for support but Mike comes over and starts choking Jay with his knee to the neck till the referee gets Mike off of him. John: This is just a taste of what Jay Smash will get at Malicious Intent! Scott: This is far from over just yet! Jay Smash is crawling for recovery while Mike C is smiling and mockingly slaps the head of Jay. Mike slaps Jay’s head again and bounces off the ropes to hit a sliding dropkick to the face! Mike yells at Jay “You’re too pathetic to take MY belt!” Mike bounces off the ropes again but Jay hits a crouching shoulder back toss! Mike gets up and goes after Jay but Jay gets him first and hits a snap suplex! Jay whips Mike across the ropes and hits a high shoulder back toss right into the cage wall! Mike C is now lying in the ropes as Jay Smash measures him up and bounces off the rope to hit a sliding dropkick of his own and Mike hits the cage hard! Jay picks up Mike and hoists him up in the air for a suplex and holds him up in the air for a while till he brings him down hard! Jay sits up smiling and gets up on his feet and gets the crowd going as he waits for Mike to get up. Meanwhile cameras show backstage where Rocko Daymon and Texas Lighting are still fighting at the concession stand. Scott: Those two are brawling all over this arena! John: TL is just giving him a preview of Hell in a Cell! Back in the ring, Jay gets Mike set for The Smash Attack but then he looks distracted as outside the cage Samuel Roundtree & Chet Jenkins are arguing with the referee. While Samuel is arguing with the referee, Chet tries to cut the cage lock with lock-cutters. Meanwhile Jay goes for the Smash Attack but Classy reverses it and hits Jay with a low blow behind the referee’s back! Mike then hoists Jay up and hits him with a Death Valley Driver! Mike goes for a cover but the referee is busy trying to stop Chet. Mike pulls the referee away till Chet finally cuts the lock open and then knocks out the referee outside. In the ring Classy shoves the referee away and Samuel enters the ring and clotheslines the referee! Jay Smash gets up and looks at Pickstock’s Army and Mike C standing together and waiting like sharks. Jay Smash makes the first attack at Chet but Mike and Samuel pull him off and Samuel hits Jay with a full-nelson slam! Samuel and Chet hold Jay up and Mike hits Jay with straight right hands to the face. Samuel and Chet hold Jay up higher as Mike bounces off the ropes and Samuel & Chet let Jay go as Mike hits him with a flipping neckbreaker! Scott: Get them outta here! John: Anything goes in a tag match! Mike tells Chet to get a chair as Chet goes outside to get a steel chair from the announce table. Chet brings in the chair and puts in down in the ring as Mike sets up Jay for a powerbomb till the fans start booing the Pickstock’s Army. Chet & Samuel face off into the crowd yelling at them as Mike goes for the powerbomb but Jay reverses and hits Mike with a DDT head-first into the steel chair! Jay hits a clothesline on Chet and Samuel goes for an Irish whip on Jay but Jay reverses the whip and hits Samuel with The Disease! Chet goes outside and closes the door with another lock. Jay notices the door locked and goes to climb up the cage wall. Samuel goes after him and both men are fighting on the cage wall. Chet then unlocks the door and pulls Mike out of the ring and revives the referee outside to look at Mike outside. The referee calls for the bell as the announcement is made Scott: This is a travesty of justice! John: Shut up Wuss! Kandi: Here are your winners, the team of Texas Lightning and Classy Mike C! Samuel runs out of the cage and Jay Smash chases after him but Chet quickly locks the door before Jay can get out and Jay is locked inside the cage. Mike gets his title and Pickstock’s Army take him up the ramp as Mike is clutching his title belt and laughing at Jay. Jay fumes inside the cage as Mainframe goes off the air.
