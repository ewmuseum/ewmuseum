This was the first ever 20 man Blood Bath match.  STEALTH pinned Don Hall in the final fall for the vacant title. The North American Title was unified with the International Title at And The Winner Is… Part V: War Games!  HICAL defeated Chet Jenkins to unify the belts.  8 years later, the belt would be resurrected thanks to the roster split. This was for the vacant title. Due to a short hiatus the reign actually begins on Feb. 9 2006.
