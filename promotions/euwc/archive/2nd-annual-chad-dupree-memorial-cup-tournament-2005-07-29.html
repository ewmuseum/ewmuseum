2nd Annual Chad Dupree Memorial Cup Tournament

29 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MattPickstock — 29 July 2005 Hey guys... I know it is a bit early, but I am already planning for the 2nd Annual Chad Dupree Memorial Cup Tournament.
This year we will be introducing a small twist. The tournament will be a one-night, eight man tournament, currently set to take place on September 25, 2005.
There will be only two representatives from the EUWC in this tournament. The other spots will be awarded to Superstars from fellow feds in the WWC.
2 to ROW, 2 to WCCW and 2 to BWA. If you would like to be considered for a spot in this tournament, please reply below as the qualifying will begin shortly for that tourny.
In addition to the tournament on Sept 25, there will also be a special Double Main Event. I can confirm that there will be an EUWC World Heavyweight Title Match on the show. I am currently also trying to book a WWC Undisputed Championship Match for the show. For those who are unaware of the WWC at this point, the current WWC Undisputed Heavyweight Champion is the EUWC's own Tyrone Walker.
I am looking to make this one great card, and remember it is all for Chad... Thanks... SamuelRoundtree — 29 July 2005 Matt, you can include Samuel in your choices if you want. TD_Walker — 29 July 2005 Does the *WWC World Champion Dance* N'yah!! hehe 8) James - I rule! RStrawsma — 29 July 2005 I'd like to announce my interest in the tournament. I knew Chad pretty well OOC... he was a phenomenal roleplayer, a dedicated federation owner, and a good friend. He would stick with me and give me advice when it seemed as though nobody in the FW circuit was on my side.
It would be an honor to compete in his memory, if nobody else has a greater interest, or if the spots haven't already been filled.

JS MattPickstock — 6 August 2005 On September 25, the EUWC will be hosting the Chad Dupree Memorial Cup... This will now be a sixteen man tournament with teh first round held on EUWC Main Frame in the weeks leading up to the show.
As it stands right now, several superstars are already confirmed for the tournament. They are as follows....
- Rocko Daymon (EUWC) - Malik Johnson (BWA) *BWA World Champion* - Dreaded Ed (SOC) *SOC World Champion* - Ricky Chambers (SOC) Plus 12 other Superstars! Also it has been confirmed that two other matches will take place on the September 25 card... In one of the encounters, the WWC Undisputed Heavyweight Champion Tyrone Walker will defend the title against "The Natural" Mike Bell (representing WCCW)
And in another match, the EUWC World Heavyweight Championship will be on the line. As it stands current EUWC World Champion "The King of Pain" Stephen Greer will defend the title against an opponent to be announced. However should Greer lose the title between now and then, whoever is Champion at the time will defend at the Chad Dupree Memorial.
MattPickstock — 10 August 2005 New Superstars Added to Tournament In addition to the following four superstars: Rocko Daymon (EUWC) Dreaded Ed (SOC) Ricky Chambers (SOC) Malik Johnson (BWA) Three more names have been added to the tournament: Mike Bell (WCCW) Jason Jousma (WCCW) Michael Lennox (BWA) More names will be announced as they become avilable, and Saturday the full tournament brackets will be announced Stay tuned...
