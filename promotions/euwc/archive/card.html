16 November 2008

Onslaught III - November 16, 2008 ScotiaBank Place - Ottawa, Ontario A Champion Without A Challenger! STEALTH defied all his critics at Blood Bath when he emerged victorious over Rocko Daymon, retaining the EUWC World Heavyweight Championship in dominant fashion. Now, proving his recent Title win was no fluke, STEALTH looks ahead to Ultra Brawl X.  The question remains, who will he defend his Championship against when he enters Ford Field?  Which EUWC Superstar will step up to the plate and try to knock the Champion off his pedestal?  Will Rocko Daymon be in search of yet another rematch?  Will a former World Champion like Nero, Jay Smash or Paul Doom step forward?  Will a young star leap out of the shadows to issue a challenge?  Or will it be a Money in the Bank Contract that determines STEALTH's upcoming opponent?  Tune in this week to find out! W.A.R. is Declared! When Onslaught last went off the air, Black Widow stood tall with her new foursome she announced to the world as W.A.R.  The faction consisting of the New World Tag Team Champions has threatened to destroy the EUWC.  They began with Rocko Daymon, but what carnage will they pull off at Onslaught?!  What is Beast doing back in the EUWC?  And what will become of the Tag Team Titles now that Jay Smash has abandoned his partner? Main Event EUWC World Heavyweight Championship Match (c) STEALTH vs "Commander" T. Waring For the first time in months, the EUWC World Heavyweight Championship will be up for grabs when STEALTH enters the ring to defend the title against T. Waring.  The Commander has had some time to heal his wounds from his match with Mike C at Blood Bath, but is he 100% concentrated on his Championship encounter?  The world saw STEALTH pin Rocko Daymon at Blood Bath and he is definitely at an all time career high at this point.  Can he continue his amazing streak at Onslaught? Blood Bath Rematch Sean Taylor vs Sevyn At Blood Bath, Sean Taylor was once again defeated by his new nemesis Sevyn. Taylor even stacked the odds in his favor by choosing the stipulation to the match, but it was all for naught as Sevyn managed to pull out a victory. However, later that night, an enraged Taylor attacked Sevyn as he was on his way to the ring to enter the Blood Bath match.  The attack forced Sevyn out of the Blood Bath match altogether.  Since these two men cannot seem to co-exist in a locker room, Pickstock has ordered that they face each other in a rematch at Onslaught this week.  Can Taylor finally get the monkey off of his back that has plagued him since his return?  Or will Sevyn continue to dominate the Hall of Famer? International Championship Qualifier Battle Royale Angel of Death, Beast, "Classy" Mike C, Nero, Jonathan Darkstar, Choker, "The Crippler" Joe Boyer & a Mystery Entrant At Onslaught, eight men will enter the ring to battle in an over the top rope challenge to determine who will earn a spot at Ultra Brawl to battle for the vacant EUWC International Championship. All of the eight men are top tier superstars looking to gain another taste of EUWC gold, but what superstar will be able to throw out seven others and advance to Ultra Brawl?  Tune into Onslaught to find out. Tag Team #1 Contenders Match Hawkeye & Braden Kincaid vs Diabolic Forces vs Cold Blooded vs The Darkstars Even with the Tag Team scene up in the air, there will be new number one contenders determined this week at Onslaught!  The unlikely duo of Braden Kincaid & Hawkeye have been on a shocking win streak as of late.  However standing across from them are three veteran tag teams; each looking to gain their own title shot at Ultra Brawl.  Diabolic Forces are the four time former Tag Champions, while Cold Blooded and the Darkstars look to gain their first taste of gold in the EUWC.  Which team will emerge victorious to challenge WAR at Ultra Brawl X? Sgt. Mayhem vs Kenny Kenny demanded to be put in a match on this weeks show, so he has been given the opportunity to break in another new member to the EUWC roster, Sgt. Mayhem. With so little known about the newcomer, and Kenny still looking for his first EUWC victory, one of these two men will earn their first notch in the win column this week at Onslaught!
