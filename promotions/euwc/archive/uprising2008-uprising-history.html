Uprising — Event History

EUWC Uprising Event History February 22, 2003 The Pyramid Memphis, Tennessee Lethal Lottery Match: Kai A. Lewis & Havoc defeated Troy Martin & Jay K when Havoc pinned Jay K with a shooting star press. Lethal Lottery Match: Holocaust & King Buxton defeated Abe Smash & HICAL when King Buxton pinned HICAL after holocaust hit HICAL with the Ragnarok. EUWC World Tag Team Championship - Triple Threat Match: The Dream Team defeated the Parker Bros and Insane Fury when Jaz Moundfield pinned Krimzon Fury with the Jaz Bash Splash.  The Dream Team retained the EUWC World Tag Team Titles. Lethal Lottery Match: Havoc & Kai A. Lewis defeated Holocaust & King Buxton when Havoc pinned Buxton after Holocaust turned on his partner and nailed him with the Ragnarok. Harder They Fall Match: Damien Dante Stone defeated Antoine Wright. Tag Team Turmoil: The Good Ol Boys defeated the U.S. Express, Bibyl & DeLucci, Havoc & Malice, The Chill Pills & Shock Therapy to earn a future Tag Team Title Shot. EUWC 52 Wild Championship - Barbed Wire Ladder Match: "Brooklyn's Finest" Kai A. Lewis defeated Havoc to become the first ever 52 Wild Champion. EUWC International Championship - London Bridge Street Fight: "The Definitive" Danny Collins defeated Apocalypse when he retrieved the belt. Danny Collins became the New EUWC International Champion. Main Event - Eight Man Elimination Tag Team Match: The Regime defeated The Apocalypse in an Eight Man Elimination Tag Team Match. Order of Elimination - Nero pinned Major Action with the Nero Kick.... Sean Taylor pinned Nero with a rollup.... T-Charger defeated Lynch Garrison with a school boy.... Jay Smash pinned Sean Taylor with the Smash Attack.... Jay Smash made T-Charger tap out to a Death lock.... Angel of Death pinned Lord Alucard with the Going Home.... Jay Smash pinned Angel of Death after the Dominion interfered attacking both men. Jay Smash was the sole survivor. March 25, 2007 Continental Airlines Arena East Rutherford, New Jersey Hardcore Match: Newfy Jack defeated Sanket Desai after a powerbomb onto the guard rail. The Skyscrappers defeated the Four Star Pimps when Viktor Cruz pinned Sean Mack with the Eliminator. "The Almighty Dollar" Jordan Turner defeated General Bromhead with the Bankruptcy. EUWC International Championship Match: Sevyn defeated Jonathan Darkstar after a frog splash through a table to retain the title. EUWC World Tag Team Championship Match: Diabolic Forces defeated Damien Dante Stone & Newfy Jack when Molloch pinned DDS after hitting him with a title belt. Diabolic Forces retained the World Tag Team Titles. EUWC North American Championship Match: "The Thriller" Braden Kincaid defeated "The Hatchet Man" Violent K with the Thrill Ride to become the New North American Champion. Main Event - EUWC Championship - Hades Dungeon Match: Hawkeye defeated Angel of Death, STEALTH & Lynch Garrison to retain the EUWC Championship.
