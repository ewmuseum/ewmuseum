EUWC Summer Sizzler Results! August 6, 2006 Pepsi Center Denver, Colorado Show Intro Sometimes heat can cause men to do crazy things. Some can't handle the humidity and lose all energy. Some get what is called heat stroke. Others begin to lose their mind. In the EUWC several men seem to match with the latter. Lord Alucard and Matt Pickstock... Two warring General Managers, both of which will stop at nothing to ensure their personal survival. Tonight their eight hand-picked Superstars will do battle against each other inside a Steel Cage Ultimate War Match! When the dust settles, one GM will show their true dominance. Jay Smash should be on cloud nine. The new World Heavyweight Champion finally achieved his dream, but instead of celebrating, he has gone mad. Concocting a special challenge for the Money in the Bank Winner Adam Benjamin... A match so brutal that few would dare to enter it. The Ring of Fire... Sure to drive these men into heat exhaustion... One match which is sure to be a Summer Sizzler! "It's All Over" by Three Days Grace blares and the EUWC logo fades into a barrage of action shots including Jay Smash standing triumphant in the ring... Adam Benjamin coming to the ring, The Four Star Pimps brawling with Diabolic Forces... Johnny Dymondz attacking Matt Falcon, Newfy Jack and Angel of Death trading punches, Lynch Garrison, Cuchulain & Hawkeye at the contract signing, Jared McCallister hitting Chris Comet with the European Title, Pickstock's Army brawling with Team Alucard and finally Jay Smash standing face to face with Adam Benjamin. The scene then explodes into the Summer Sizzler logo as EUWC Summer Sizzler opens up to a jam-packed Pepsi Center, in Denver, Colorado... 21,000 fans are on their feet in anticipation of the PPV event.
