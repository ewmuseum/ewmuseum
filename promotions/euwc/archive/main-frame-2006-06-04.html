Apocalypse tags in Mike C and Apoc taunts Jay Smash in his corner, prompting him to enter the ring but the referee is trying to stop him. While the ref is distracted, Apoc lifts up Doe on his shoulders and Mike flies off the corner with a neckbreaker on Doe! Apoc quickly gets out of the ring while Mike twists the leg of Doe, inflicting pain and forcing Doe to scream. Mike then stomps at the leg of Doe and tags in Nero who pulls up Doe by the hair and kicks him in the gut and goes for a powerbomb but Doe rebounds and hits Nero with a tornado DDT! Doe quickly makes a tag to Matt Falcon who climbs up the top and waits for Nero to get up and flies off with a cross body off the top! Nero charges at Falcon but Falcon takes him down with an armdrag! Nero charges at him again but this time Falcon hits him with a northern-lights suplex and pins 1..2..Nero kicks out! Nero gets up and chops at Falcon and goes for an Irish whip but Falcon reverses and hits a German suplex! Falcon goes for a cover but Nero gets the shoulder up. Falcon goes for a submission but Nero kicks him into a corner and charges at him but Falcon moves out of the way and Nero hits the corner. Falcon places Nero up on the corner and goes for a superplex but Nero kicks him off the ropes and Nero flies off with a high leg drop! Nero laughs at Falcon and mockingly kicks him in his face. Nero bounces off the ropes but Falcon takes Nero down with a swinging neckbreaker! Scott: Falcon is mounting an offence now! John: Only for the moment Scott! Both men slowly head for their corners as the crowd goes electric. Nero tags in Mike C while Falcon tags in Jay Smash! The fans are on their feet as Jay Smash enters and ducks a clothesline from Mike C and knocks Apoc off the apron. Nero goes to attack him but Jay hits him with a flurry of right hands and gets him out of the ring. Mike goes for a right hand but Jay turns around to spot him. Both men look at each other with uneasy tension. The fans are in anticipation for those two to get it going as both Jay & Mike are exchanging right hands till Jay gets the upper hand whips Mike across the ropes and takes him down with a flipping neckbreaker! Mike gets up but Jay whips him again and hits him with a front dropkick! Mike rebounds off the ropes and Jay goes behind Mike to hit a JS Jaw Breaker! Jay yells out into the crowd and climbs up the top and waits for Mike to get up and when he does, Jay flies off and hits a top-rope neckbreaker! Jay covers Mike and the ref counts 1..2..Nero pulls the referee out of the ring! Scott: Come on! This match was over! John: You just don't know when to shut up, huh Scott? While the referee and Nero are arguing outside, Apoc enters the ring and takes Jay down with a bearhug front slam! As Apoc turns around, John Doe flies at him with a moonsault while Apoc is standing! Meanwhile, Nero and Falcon get into a fight outside and Falcon whips Nero towards the steel steps but Nero reverses and sends Falcon crashing into the steel steps. In the ring, John Doe gets up and hits Mike C with The Muzzle but he turns around to get a Nero Kick to the face! Nero then goes to the corner and measures up Jay Smash, teasing a Nero Kick while Jay Smash slowly gets up. Jay gets up and turns around when Nero strikes but Jay grabs him and hoists him on his shoulders to hit The Disease! Nero rolls out of the ring while Jay turns to be rolled up by Mike C who locks in the Texas Classyleaf! Mike builds up the pressure on the hold as Jay tries to get to the ropes but can’t get there. Just then Falcon gets on the top rope and hits Mike with a double axe handle! Mike kicks Falcon in the gut and goes for a powerbomb but Smash attacks him from behind and takes him out with a Smash Attack! Jay goes for the cover and the ref counts 1..2..THREE! Scott: It's over! Jay Smash has just pinned the World Champion! John: WHAT?! HE CHEATED! Jay Smash stands up in victory but just then Apocalypse takes him down with a big boot! Falcon tries to take on the big man but Apoc whips him across the ropes and takes him down with a shoulder back toss! The bell continues to ring as both Mike and Apoc start beating on Jay Smash till Matt Pickstock comes down to the ring. Matt is cheering on to Mike till John Doe enters the fray and takes Apoc down with a low blow and ties to hit a powerbomb but Matt Pickstock attacks him from behind. Doe is unfazed and goes to hit a scared Pickstock but Mike C hits Doe with a low blow! Matt Falcon tries to enter the ring to help but Nero catches him outside and takes him out with a Buzzcutter! At last Alucard marches down to the ring and stands facing Apocalypse, Pickstock & Mike C. Alucard tells them to clear the ring or else. Apocalypse walks to the back and Pickstock backs off. Mike C just stands where he is and stares at Alucard. Alucard tells him to leave but Mike shakes his head. Mike then gets a smile on his face and looks behind Alucard. Alucard turns around only to get a chairshot to the head courtesy of Texas Lightning. Nero just watches from the outside as Matt Pickstock, Texas Lightning and Mike C stand over Jay Smash and Alucard. Apocalypse is laughing from the outside while Pickstock is all smiles. Just then they notice Nero walking up the ramp with his title belt and Pickstock gets a mic. Pickstock: Hey Nero, where ya goin? Don’t you wanna join in? C’mon, this is a festive time. You see, Alucard made a huge mistake of putting you three in the same ring against the likes of Falcon, Doe and Smash. Look at them now. Oh and Alucard, how did that chairshot feel? You want to be high and mighty on my show? You picked the wrong show! As of tonight, I’m taking back what is mine. I’m going to be rid of every one that dares stand against my army. At Malicious Intent, my army will be victorious; first Roundtree and Jenkins will win the tag titles. Then, Apocalypse will take out this worthless piece of crap John Doe. And hey, check this out. (Pickstock points to the titantron which shows backstage where Rocko Daymon is laid out with glass shards lying around him and medics are tending to him) That’s nothing; imagine what Texas Lightning will do to him at Hell in the Cell. And of course, yours truly will get rid of the biggest pain in my @$$ ever! And of course, Jay Smash will learn that he’s nothing without me, when Mike C retains the title. And Alucard, all of this will be on your hands. Just like tonight. Cameras show a fallen Matt Falcon, John Doe, Alucard and Jay Smash as Apocalypse is still laughing outside the ring while Pickstock, Texas Lighting and Mike C are standing triumphant in the ring as Mainframe goes off the air.
