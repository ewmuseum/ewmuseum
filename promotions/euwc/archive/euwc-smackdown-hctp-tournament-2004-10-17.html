EUWC - Smackdown HCTP Tournament

17 October 2004

MattPickstock — 17 October 2004 Hey guys... I decided to run a Smack Down : HCTP Tourny on PS2... The first round matches look like this.... Ray Santana vs Paul Doom Damian Stone vs "Classy" Mike C Tyrone Walker vs Sevyn Stephen Greer vs Holocaust Texas Lightning vs Evol Rocko Daymon vs Sean Taylor Danny Collins vs Lord Alucard Triple T vs Angel of Death First Round Results: 1. Ray Santana d. Paul Doom with the Santana Slam. (Velocity) 2. Damian Dante Stone d. Mike C with the JJE. (Raw) 3. Tyrone Walker d. Sevyn with the Super Freak. (Smack Down) 4. Stephen Greer d. Holocaust with the Decapitator Lariat. (No Mercy) 5. Evol d. Texas Lightning with a fallaway slam. (Backlash) 6. In the closest match of the first round, Sean Taylor d. Rocko Daymon with the Blood Rush. (Raw)
7. In another close match, Danny Collins d. Lord Alucard with the Definition. (Summer Slam)
8. TTT d. Angel of Death with the Thunderlock. (Armageddon) Quarter Final Matches 1. Ray Santana vs Damian Stone 2. Tyrone Walker vs Stephen Greer 3. Evol vs Sean Taylor 4. Danny Collins vs TTT MattPickstock — 19 October 2004 Quarter Final results Quarter Final Round 1. DDS d. Ray Santana with the JJE. (Judgement Day) 2. Stephen Greer d. Tyrone Walker after reversing a frankensteiner into a DDT. (Bad Blood) 3. Evol d. Sean Taylor via a rollup. (Backlash) 4. Triple T d. Evol with a leglock. (Royal Rumble) Semi Final Round Matches 1. Damian Stone vs "King of Pain" Stephen Greer 2. Evol vs Triple T The Semi-Finals will be 3 Stages of Hell. First Fall = Singles, Second Fall = Submission, Third (If Necessary) = Cage
MattPickstock — 19 October 2004 Semi Final results Semi Final Round 1. (1st Fall - Singles) Damian Stone d. Stephen Greer after repeated back breakers. (2nd Fall - Submission) Stephen Greer d. Damian Stone with an armbar submission. (3rd Fall - Cage) Stephen Greer d. Damian Stone via escaping the cage after a fisherman DDT.
2. (1st Fall - Single) Evol d. TTT with a powerslam. (2nd Fall - Submission) Evol d. TTT with an STF. Final Round Match Up 1. Stephen Greer vs Evol The Final Round will be a Ten Minute Iron Man Match. MattPickstock — 19 October 2004 Finals!!! Final Round - Iron Man Match Stephen Greer vs Evol - Evol d. Stephen Greer with the Extinction at 5:55. - Stephen Greer d. Evol with the Godstopper at 7:07. - Evol d. Stephen Greer with a jumping DDT at 9:37. - Time Expired at 10:00 Evol d. Stephen Greer 2 falls to 1. EUWC Smack Down : Here Comes The Pain Champion - EVOL
