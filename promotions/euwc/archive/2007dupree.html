4th Annual Chad Dupree Memorial Cup

28 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
October 28, 2007 United Center - Chicago, Illinois Theme Song: "The Memory Will Never Die" - Default THE CARD 4th Annual Chad Dupree Memorial Cup 16 Man Single Elimination Tournament For The Vacant EUWC World Heavyweight Championship This year's Chad Dupree Memorial Cup is for more than just the trophy itself, and some much desired fame.  For the first time since 2003, the EUWC World Heavyweight Championship will be held up in a tournament to decide the new rightful owner to the coveted gold.  The top sixteen Superstars in the EUWC today will do battle in four rounds of elimination action until one man is left standing.  Only then will he be able to call himself the Undisputed EUWC World Heavyweight Champion.  This all started at Ultra Brawl, when in the Unification Match, Hawkeye and Nero battled to a Draw which led to a locker room clearing brawl that featured each and every member of the EUWC roster. Since that time, the separate brands in the EUWC have been abandoned and have been combined to form one of the most star-filled rosters in EUWC History. Sixteen men from that roster were chosen to battle it out inside this year's tournament to determine who is the true top tier in the EUWC.  Those men are (in alphabetical order, and as pictured above) "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin, Allister Crowley, Angel of Death, "The Ironman" Buck Naked, Chris Comet, "The Ego Buster" Dan Ryan, Daymon, Hawkeye, "The Disease" Jay Smash, "The Crippler" Joe Boyer, "The Great & Almighty" Nero, "The Signal" Sanket Desai, Sevyn, STEALTH, "Commander" T. Waring, and The Phoenix.  When the dust settles at this year's Chad Dupree Memorial Cup, which man will be left standing with his head; and newly won EUWC World Heavyweight Championship; held high?! Champion - "The Thriller" Braden Kincaid vs Kenny - Challenger The Undefeated North American champion Braden Kincaid has been looking for a challenge.  He has been so upset with the recent opposition that he wanted to hand pick his opponent at the Chad Dupree Memorial Cup.  Kincaid announced that his opponent at the PPV would be none other than EUWC upstart Kenny.  And while, on the outside, Kenny may look like a pushover, don't let the rookie deceive you.  Kenny is one tough son of a bitch who will do whatever it takes to claim his very first taste of EUWC gold.  Meanwhile The Thriller will be out to prove that once again he is the most dominant star in EUWC history and extend his Undefeated streak to one full year.  Who will be the North American Champion when the Chad Dupree Memorial Cup comes to a close? Champion - Jonathan Darkstar vs Stanley Leash - Challenger At Ultra Brawl IX, Jonathan Darkstar shocked the world when he upset Violent K to become the new EUWC International Champion.  Darkstar now joins a long list of legends who at one time held that title.  Among those names, he joins alumni like Sean Taylor, STEALTH, X.Plosion, Don Hall and Daymon as Champion.  However the road as Champion is not an easy one.  Darkstar is sure to find that out first-hand when he steps into the ring at the 4th Annual Chad Dupree Memorial Cup.  His opponent is the monstrous and unpredictable Stanley Leash.  Led by Dean Fanrong, Leash is looking to tear Darkstar apart in an effort to gain EUWC Gold.  Can Darkstar emerge with a successful title defense?  Or will the rabid Leash sink his teeth into the International gold? Team Fusion vs Diabolic Forces For the first time in over a year, Diabolic Forces go into battle on a Pay Per View without the Tag team titles, and without them on the line. This time around their opponents will be the up and coming team of Team fusion. Fred & Farris Fusion have been slowly working their way up the EUWC ladder and one day wish to challenge for the Titles, but the Four Time World Tag Team Champions are not willing to let this up-start team surpass them just yet. At the 4th Annual Chad Dupree Memorial Cup, which team will emerge as the victors and take another step towards Tag Team Gold? **CARD SUBJECT TO CHANGE**
