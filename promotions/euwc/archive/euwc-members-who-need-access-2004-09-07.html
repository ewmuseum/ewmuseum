Chad — 7 September 2004 My apologies on a foul-up that I forgot about when I was setting up the EUWC forums. In the middle of expanding the FW Central forums, there were some concerns that some new folks might interfere with some of the ongoing league activity. In order to prevent that, I created two distinct usergroups.
Basically, new users log-in and are assigned "Registered User" status. However, to access the "League Central" forums (including the EUWC ones), you need "League Member" access.
Here's how to get it: Once you've set up your account go to the "User CP" link up top. In your User Control Panel, click on the "Group Memberships" link in the left-hand sidebar (third from the bottom).
In "Group Memberships", click "Join Group" for the "League Member" access. Please note that your access will be updated as soon as I go in and approve you. You may need to log out and back in once you're approved for the full change to take effect.
Once again, my apologies on forgetting about that extra 'security feature' I put in place. Please send this to any of your EUWC folks who may have missed it.
-Chad
