Match #1 Unification Match: Unified Simulator Champion Tower (w/ Desire) - vs. - Television Champion Cold Blooded Tower and Cold Blooded engaged in a stare down and exchanged last minute comments. Cold Blooded and Tower circled each other until they locked up. Tower showed his power by shoving Cold Blooded back into the corner. Cold Blooded bounced back onto his feet instantly. He charged at Tower and attempted a shoulder-block, but it backfired sending CB down. He got up and locked up again with Tower but this time hammered the big man in the gut with his knee. JT Ruckus came down to ringside and set a chair up in the aisle-way to watch the bout. CB then raked the eyes of Tower and body slammed him. CB bounced off the ropes and executed a fist-drop, followed by a knee across the chest. Cold Blooded then climbed to the top rope and executed a flying elbowdrop onto Tower and attempted a cover, 1... 2... KICK OUT! Cold Blooded picked Tower up and whipped him off the ropes and dropkicked him down. He then dropped an elbow, and proceeded to climb the ropes again. This time Tower got to his feet and tossed CB off the turnbuckle and to the mat. Tower then waited as his opponent got up and then delivered a thunderous lariat followed by a powerslam. Tower then charged at CB and clotheslined him over the top rope and to the floor. Tower then bounced off the ropes and executed a somersault plancha onto Cold Blooded. He then picked up Cold Blooded and executed a Tombstone pile driver onto the concrete. Tower threw Cold Blooded back into the ring, and then re-entered it himself. He whipped CB off the ropes and attempted a clothesline but CB ducked and countered with a cross-body block. 1... 2... KICK OUT! Cold Blooded staggered to his feet and Tower grabbed him and whipped him into the corner. Tower charged in but CB raised his knee into Tower's face. Tower staggered backwards and Cold Blooded moved in. He executed the Chaos Breaker onto Tower. He began to laugh and then pulled Tower to his feet and executed a second Chaos Breaker, 1.... 2... KICK OUT!!! Cold Blooded pulled Tower to his feet and was about to attempt a third Chaos Breaker, but Tower countered it with an atomic drop. He whipped CB off the ropes and caught him in a choke slam. Tower dragged him over to the corner and stood on the second turnbuckle. He raised his fist and hauled Cold Blooded into the air and executed the 7-Storey Drop, 1... 2... 3! JT stood up and applauded Tower, and returned to the dressing room. Stardust then dragged Freak to the corner and climbed to the top rope and executed a Shooting Star Press, which he calls the Stardrop, 1... 2... 3!
