Roster Bio: "HOT SHOT" DOUG BLAZE

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"HOT SHOT" DOUG BLAZE Height: 6'6" Weight: 250 lbs. Hometown: Miami, Florida Entrance Theme: "Hot Blooded" - Foreigner Finishing Move(s): - Flaming Death (Stone Cold Stunner) - 3rd Degree (Modified Camel Clutch) - The Scorcher (Jackhammer) Style: Old School, Rough, Technical, Dirty EUWC Accomplishments: - None BIO: When one thinks of legends, they think Hulk Hogan, Bruno Sammartino and Ric Flair. But there is another man in their ranks. "Hotshot" Doug Blaze. Hotshot began his career in the wrestling boom of the 1980's. Although he never competed in the big leagues of the NWA or WWE (then known as WWF), he was an icon and a visionary in the wrestling community. Hotshot disposed of any and all opponents in his way, claiming a plethora of title belt gold along the way. Now, his hall of fame career as bright as ever, he seeks a new horizon. The horizon of EUWC.

Common Crawl capture, 9 May 2008
