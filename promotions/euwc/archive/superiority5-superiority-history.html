Superiority — Event History

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EUWC Superiority Event History March 28, 2004 The Alamodome San Antonio, Texas 20 Man Battle Royale: Woad won a twenty man over the top rope battle royale last eliminating Dwight Jackson and Lt. Hawkeye.. EUWC 52 Wild Championship - Dumpster Match: "Brooklyn's Finest" Kai A. Lewis defeated King Buxton to retain the 52 Wild Championship. EUWC World Tag Team Championship - Triple Threat Tables Ladders & Chairs Match: The Dream Team defeated the Dominion & the U.S. Express when Jaz Moundfield retrieved the Championship belts.  The Dream Team retained the EUWC World Tag Team Titles. EUWC United States Championship Match: "The Show" Ryan Baker defeated Sevyn with the Big Picture to become the new EUWC United States Champion. Handicap Inferno Match: Luther McLean defeated Cold Blooded when he set Tony DeLucci ablaze as per the rules.. Grudge Match - Special Referee STEALTH: The Phoenix defeated Matt Pickstock with the Judge, Jury, Executioner.. EUWC International Championship Match: Lynch Garrison defeated Apocalypse & "The Definitive" Danny Collins when Lynch pinned Danny Collins with the Terrarizer as Nero interfered and took out Apocalypse with the Nero Kick. Lynch Garrison won the EUWC International Championship. 30 Minute Rulebook Match: Sean Taylor defeated John Shaft. #1 Contender Mini Tournament - Semi-Final Round Match: Holocaust pinned Jordan Lockhart after Lord Alucard interfered attacking Lockhart with a steel chair. #1 Contender Mini-Tournament - Semi-Final Round Match:  T-Charger defeated Lord Alucard with the Re-Charge to advance to the finals. #1 Contender Mini-Tournament - Final Round: Holocaust defeated T-Charger with the Ragnarok to become the #1 Contender to the EUWC World Heavyweight Championship. EUWC World Heavyweight Championship Match: "The New Disease" Jay Smash defeated Angel of Death when Paul Doom interfered and hit AOD with the Doomstone, allowing Smash to get the pin.  Jay Smash became the New EUWC World Heavyweight Champion. July 10, 2005 Wachovia Center Philadelphia, Pennsylvania EUWC International Championship Tournament - First Round Match: "Classy" Mike C defeated Paul Doom with a small package. EUWC International Championship Tournament - First Round Match: Rocko Daymon pinned Evol with the Brain Rocker. EUWC International Championship Tournament - First Round Match: Sevyn defeated "Commander" T. Waring with the Covet. EUWC International Championship Tournament - First Round Match: Lord Antharg defeated "The Show" Ryan Baker with a choke slam. EUWC International Championship Tournament - First Round Match: Jay Smash defeated Damien Dante Stone with the Disease. EUWC International Championship Tournament - First Round Match: "xXx" Xavier Marshal defeated "Man of the Hour" Nick Elliot with the X-Bomb. EUWC International Championship Tournament - First Round Match: "The Black Jesus" Tyrone Walker made Lieutenant Hawkeye submit to the Texas Cloverleaf. EUWC International Championship Tournament - First Round Match: Angel of Death defeated "The Definitive" Danny Collins with the Going Home. EUWC International Championship Tournament - Quarter Final Round Match: Rocko Daymon defeated "Classy" Mike C with a roll up. EUWC International Championship Tournament - Quarter Final Round Match: Sevyn defeated Lord Antharg with a top rope fisherman's buster. EUWC International Championship Tournament - Quarter Final Round Match: "The New Disease" Jay Smash defeated "xXx" Xavier Marshal with the Smash Attack. EUWC International Championship Tournament - Quarter Final Round Match: "The Black Jesus" Tyrone Walker defeated Angel of Death with an Implant DDT. EUWC United States Championship Match: Samuel Roundtree defeated Texas Lightning with the Godstopper to retain the United States Title. EUWC International Championship Tournament - Semi Final Round Match: "The Black Jesus" Tyrone Walker defeated "The New Disease" Jay Smash with a small package. EUWC International Championship Tournament - Semi Final Round Match: Rocko Daymon pinned Sevyn after he hit him with a Daymonplex. EUWC World Tag Team Championship Match: Team Danger and the Moundfields battled to a double disqualification.  Team danger retained the EUWC World Tag Team Titles. EUWC International Championship Tournament - Final Round Match: "The Black Jesus" Tyrone Walker defeated Rocko Daymon with the Everlasting Godstopper to win the EUWC International Championship. Main Event - EUWC World Heavyweight Championship Match: "The King of Pain" Stephen Greer defeated Sean Taylor with the Broken Wingz. Stephen Greer became the New EUWC World Heavyweight Champion. April 2, 2006 The Alamodome San Antonio, Texas EUWC International Championship - Blood Draw Match: Rocko Daymon and Texas Lightning battled to a draw. Rocko Daymon retained the International Title. EUWC United States Championship Match: "The Great & Almighty" Nero defeated "The Dragon" Nick Frost with the Nero Kick to retain the U.S. Title. EUWC European Championship Match: "The Mental Rapist" Sean Jackson defeated John Doe by disqualification when Doe hit Jackson with the title belt. Since Championships cannot change hands via count out or DQ, John Doe retained the European Championship. "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin defeated Angel of Death with the Shining Wizard. EUWC North American Championship Match: "The Hound of Ulster" Cuchulain defeated Lt. Hawkeye with the Gae Bulga to become the New North American Champion. EUWC World Tag Team Championship - Four Team Elimination Match: The Glamour Boys defeated The Salvation when Barry Silvers pinned Ray Santana with the Silverfish... The Glamour Boys defeated Celtic Pride when Barry Silvers pinned Woad with the Silverplex.... The Texas Outlaws defeated the Glamour Boys when Terry Norris defeated Barry Silvers with the Outsider's Edge.  The Texas Outlaws retained the EUWC World Tag Team Titles. Falls Count Anywhere Match: Samuel Roundtree and Jared McAllister fought to a no contest. Money in the Bank Ladder Match: Lynch Garrison defeated Apocalypse, T. Waring, Matt Falcon, Evol & Jakob McLean when he retrieved the briefcase. EUWC World Heavyweight Championship Match: "Classy" Mike C defeated Sean Taylor with Taylor's own finisher, The Blood Rush.  "Classy" Mike C won the EUWC World Heavyweight Championship. May 1, 2007 Madison Square Garden New York, New York "The Almighty Dollar" Jordan Turner defeated Paris LeClair with the Bankruptcy. Team Fusion defeated Cold Blooded when Farris Fusion pinned Jack "The Ripper" with a flying legdrop. Tables Match: Darksyde defeated Cobris Grayson. EUWC United States Championship - Triple Threat Match: Enigma defeated "The Ego Buster" Dan Ryan & Daymon when Enigma made Ryan submit to an armbar.  Enigma became the New EUWC United States Champion. **Enigma would later be revealed as "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin.** "The Hurricane" Sanket Desai defeated Draco with the Hurricane. United States #1 Contender: "Commander" T. Waring defeated Matt Falcon with the ankle lock. EUWC World Tag Team Championship Match: Diabolic Forces defeated Damien Dante Stone & Newfy Jack when Molloch pinned Newfy Jack after a choke slam.  Diabolic Forces retained the World Tag Team Titles. Blood Letting Match: Jeremy Oliver defeated "Dynamite" Dan Thorn. EUWC International Championship Match: "The Hatchet Man" Violent K defeated Sevyn with the Unveiling to become the New International Champion. EUWC European Championship Match: Chris Comet defeated Raven Cyarm with a roll up to retain the European Title. EUWC North American Championship Match: "The Thriller" Braden Kincaid defeated Angel of Death with the Thrill Ride to retain the North American Title. EUWC Tag Team Championship - Triple Threat Tables, Ladders & Chairs Match: Daymon & "The Ego Buster" Dan Ryan defeated the Dream Team & The Clique to retain the Tag Team Titles. EUWC Championship Match: Hawkeye defeated STEALTH with the Hawkeye Driver to retain the EUWC Title. EUWC World Heavyweight Championship - Triple Terror Match: "The Great & Almighty" Nero, "The Disease" Jay Smash and Lord Alucard battled to a No Contest when Dan Ryan, Daymon & Adam Benjamin interfered, stopping the match.  Nero retained the EUWC World Heavyweight Championship.
