Sean Taylor walks into the arena proudly carrying the EUWC Rulebook. Kandi : And his partner, from St. Paul Minnesota, weighing in at 225 pounds, here is …. SEAN TAYLOR! Sean slides into the ring and hands Kandi the rulebook. He raises his arms into the air for a moment, before coming back down and looking at Jay Smash. "Begging You" by The Stone Roses plays and strobe lighting flashes at the top of the ramp. Classy Mike C steps out onto the stage with the EUWC World Title belt around his waist. Following him are his partners Chet Jenkins and Samuel Roundtree and EUWC co-GM Matt Pickstock. Mike C holds his arms out to his side, prompting the majority of fans to boo and jeer him before walking down the ramp and rolling under the bottom rope into the ring as do Samuel and Chet. Classy stands in the centre of the ring and removes the World title belt before holding it aloft, triggering fireworks to shoot up from behind him. After a few seconds, the fireworks stop and the music starts to fade as Classy goes and hands the belt to a ringside assistant before the three go to their corner. Meanwhile Matt Pickstock joins the announce table for color commentary. Kandi: And their opponents, at a combined weight of 730 pounds, the team of Samuel Roundtree, Chet Jenkins and the EUWC World Heavyweight Champion….. “CLASSY” MIKE C!!!!! Sean Taylor is in the ring as Jay heads to the corner. Samuel Roundtree is the first from the opposition as the bell rings. Both men tie up and Samuel gets in a headlock and hits his fist in Taylor’s forehead! Samuel gets behind Taylor and goes for a back drop but Taylor reverses and dropkicks Samuel to the back. Samuel bounces off the ropes and Taylor goes for a roll-up but Samuel kicks out of it. Samuel quickly gets back up and nails Sean Taylor with a vicious spear! Samuel picks up Taylor and goes for an Irish whip and hits Taylor with a powerslam! Samuel goes for the cover but only a near fall. Scott: Taylor obviously reeling from Aftershock. Matt: Yeah and that was what, a WEEK AGO! Samuel picks up Taylor and takes him to his corner and tags in Chet Jenkins. Samuel then mocks Smash and Smash tries to come in the ring but the referee is holding him back. Behind the ref’s back, Mike C is choking Taylor from behind the corner while Chet and Samuel are kicking Taylor in the gut! Mike C lets go and Samuel leaves the ring while Chet hits an Irish whip to Taylor and takes him out with a rear-naked choke drop! Chet bounces off the ropes and hits a front elbow drop on Taylor’s throat! Chet goes for a cover but another two count. Chet gets in a sleeper but Taylor manages to get up on his feet and elbows his way out of it. Taylor goes for a whip but Chet reverses, only for Taylor to hit a front neckbreaker! Taylor gets up and waits for Chet to get up and bounces off the ropes but from behind, Mike C knees him in the back and lets Chet hit Taylor with a DDT! Matt: That’s it CHET! TAKE HIM OUT! Scott: How can you say that?! Mike C hit Taylor in the back! John: Are you trying to argue with the boss? Chet makes a tag to Samuel and both men walks towards but the referee is stopping Chet and the three argue. This allows enough time for Taylor to recover and hits a superkick to Chet! Samuel goes for a clothesline but Taylor ducks and takes out Samuel with a hurricarana! Chet rolls out of the ring while Taylor mount-punches away on Samuel! Taylor goes bounces off the ropes but Samuel grabs him and goes for a spinebuster, but Taylor reverses it in mid-air into a spike DDT! Taylor picks up Samuel and sets him up for a powerbomb but Mike C and Chet distract the referee long enough for Samuel to hit a low blow on Taylor and hits him with a belly to belly suplex! Roundtree goes for a cover 1...2...Smash comes in and breaks up the count! While the referee is forcing Smash out of the ring, the trio of Mike C, Chet and Samuel are stomping away on Taylor! Scott: And now the triple team on Taylor. Matt: I don’t see it. John: Neither do I boss. Matt: Good for you Johnny. The referee looks back to see Samuel tag in Classy Mike C. Mike C picks up Taylor and chops at him in the ropes. Mike whips Taylor across the ropes and takes him down with a thunderous chop to the chest! Mike C looks down at Taylor and smiles at Smash. Mike C yells in Taylor’s face “HE CANT HELP YOU, NO BODY CAN!” Mike C picks up Taylor but Taylor bursts open with right hands to the face of Mike C and the crowd goes “OOH” on every punch! Mike C looks dazed and Taylor goes for another right hand but Mike C ducks and delivers a big back drop! Mike C signals for the end and locks in the Classyleaf. Taylor tries to get to the ropes but Mike C pulls him away from the bottom rope. Taylor is in immense pain and gets his body on his arms. Smash gets the crowd going for Taylor as Taylor turns and twists his body and in one burst of energy, manages to get Mike C off his feet! While still in the submission position, Taylor gets up and locks in a sharpshooter! Scott: MY GOD! Taylor just reversed the Classyleaf into a Sharpshooter! John: Here, have some water boss. (gives a glass of water to a sweating Matt Pickstock who promptly drinks it.) Mike C takes a while but manages to get his hand on the bottom rope and the referee breaks up the hold. Both men try to stand up and run towards each other and take themselves down with clotheslines! The referee is counting both men as Taylor is crawling towards his corner. Mike C grabs Taylor’s leg just before he gets to the corner. Taylor is standing on one leg and hits Classy with a Roundhouse Dropkick to the head! Taylor tags in Smash and Smash hits a clothesline on an incoming Samuel Roundtree and another one to Chet Jenkins. Samuel runs at him but Smash takes him out of the ring with a shoulder back toss and clothesline’s Chet out of the ring! Mike C stands up to see Jay Smash waiting and calling for him. Mike C goes for the first punch and Smash retaliates with a punch! Both men exchange right hands as the fight turns into a slugfest till Smash gets the upper hand and continues with more right hands and whips Mike C across the ropes and hits him with a sidewalk slam! Smash whips Mike C to the ropes and bounces off the ropes himself and hits Mike C with a running DDT! Smash goes for a cover 1...2...Samuel and Chet break up the pin! Scott: Once again, the numbers game taking its toll on Smash. John: This wouldn’t have happened if Taylor would back up Smash properly. Scott: What are you… Matt: See this is why you never move up on the job Scotty. Scott: But, he’s still doing the same job as I am. Samuel and Chet double team on Smash in a corner. Samuel turns around and sees Taylor run at him and hit him with a spear through the ropes and out of the ring! Smash hits Chet in the face with a right hand and hits a belly to back suplex to get Chet out of the ring! Smash looks at Chet outside and turns around to have Mike C kick him in the gut and hit him with a double arm DDT! Mike C goes for a cover 1...2...Smash kicks out! Mike C doesn’t believe it and notices Samuel Roundtree recovering in his corner. Mike C tags in Samuel who looks surprised but goes in the ring anyway. Mike C goes to Chet Jenkins and tells him to help Samuel while Samuel goes for a Size Doesn’t Matter. But Smash reverses it and sets up Samuel for a powerbomb but Chet attacks him with a double axe handle to the back. Meanwhile Mike C heads for the announce table and grabs the World Heavyweight Title Belt. Mike C climbs on the apron and sees Chet & Samuel set up Smash for a double vertical suplex but Chet lets go and heads for an incoming Taylor who flies off the top rope with a clothesline! Meanwhile Smash reverses the suplex on Samuel and hits him with a suplex of his own. Taylor has Chet on his knees as does Smash and both men are calling the Champion in the ring but Mike C gets off the apron and heads for the ramp. Mike C looks into the ring as Smash & Taylor give each other a look and nod before they simultaneously hit their finishers; Taylor hits the Bloodrush on Chet Jenkins and Smash hits the Smash Attack on Samuel Roundtree! Mike C watches as both men cover their victims and the referee counts 1...2...THREE!!! The music hits as the announcement is made… Kandi Reed: Here are your winners, Sean Taylor and Jay Smash!!! The referee raises Smash and Taylor’s arms in victory as Mike C walks backwards up the ramp and he holds up the EUWC World Heavyweight Championship as Mainframe goes off the air.
