Monday Night Brawl — 2007-11-12

12 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EUWC Main Frame Results! November 12, 2007 Joe Louis Arena Detroit, Michigan Show Intro CHAD DUPREE MEMORIAL CUP.... Daymon starts to stir first and crawls over toward STEALTH. He tries to cover STEALTH but STEALTH rolls over on his stomach and starts to get up. He grabs STEALTH and pulls him to his feet, looking for a reverse DDT but STEALTH holds on to the ropes. Daymon lands hard on the back of his head and STEALTH drops a quick leg drop across Daymon's neck. STEALTH goes to the top rope and leaps off going for a frog splash. Daymon moves but STEALTH manages to roll through the move. Daymon gets up and kicks STEALTH in the midsection. He goes to whip STEALTH into the corer but STEALTH reverses it sending Daymon Chest first and then nails the Stealth Bomber!! Once again both men go down neither able to move. Scott: And now STEALTH has a chance to win but can't capitalize on the move... John: Lucky Break for Daymon. Scott: Looks like both men have used up their second chance for this match. It's anyone's ball game now!! STEALTH struggles and crawls over covering Daymon but Daymon rolls over on his side. He rolls over to the ring apron and pulls himself up, waiting for Daymon to get up. STEALTH catapults himself over going for a hurriacanrana but Daymon turns it into a modified powerbomb. Daymon goes to the top rope slowly as STEALTH starts to stir. STEALTH groggily gets to his feet and Daymon walks along the ropes and leaps off taking STEALTH down with a missile dropkick. Daymon pulls STEALTH up, signaling for a third Brain Rocker, but STEALTH reverses it into a roll up. Daymon rolls through STEALTH's reversal into a small package 1... 2... Thr... STEALTH reverses it into his own, the roll up this time: 1... 2... THREE! Scott: IT'S OVER!!!! John: I DON'T BELIEVE IT!!!! Both men slump to the mat exhausted after that hellacious match. Kandi Reed makes the official announcement... Kandi: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner and the NEW UNDISPUTED EUWC World Heavyweight CHAMPION........ STEALTH!!!!!!! "The Unforgiven II" by Metallica blares as fireworks engulf the arena. The fans are on their feet as the celebrations begin. Both competitors still lay on the mat, completely drained from the encounter. Daymon is the first to sit up, slapping the mat in frustration and shaking his head. STEALTH uses the ropes to get to a crouching position as the referee awards him the Championship. Daymon looks over, shaking his head again. He slides out of the ring and takes a moment to compose himself. STEALTH stands up and the referee raises his hand as the winner. The crowd explodes into cheers. Scott; What an amazing match! What an amazing night! John: I can't believe it, but he has done it! STEALTH is the Seven Time EUWC World Champion! Scott: And Daymon has nothing to be ashamed about. That match was one of the closest I have had to call in my entire career! John: You know that one day soon those two men will cross paths again, and if tonight is any indication, the result could go either way. Scott: I agree. But for now lets just sit back and watch this celebration! On the outside, Daymon is applauding STEALTH now, but just for the moment. STEALTH stops and looks out at Daymon, who points to STEALTH and then back to himself and shouts the words "One More Time!" STEALTH nods in agreement and respect. Daymon smiles and walks toward the back. STEALTH turns to continue his celebration. The referee hands STEALTH the Chad Dupree Memorial Cup Trophy. STEALTH holds the trophy high in one hand, and the World Championship Belt in the other as confetti falls from the rafters and the Chad Dupree Memorial Cup goes off the air "Ladies & Gentlemen" by Saliva blares and the EUWC logo fades into a barrage of action shots including Hawkeye walking out to the stage with his EUWC Championship, Jay Smash delivering the Smash Attack to an opponent, Lord Alucard coming to the ring... Braden Kincaid defeating DDS with the Thrill Ride... Chris Comet with Cindy Youngblood after winning the EUWC European Title, Nero on the set of Sunday Nero Live, Dan Ryan & Daymon in front of an EUWC Monday Night Brawl backdrop holding the Tag Team Titles... Angel of Death and Cuchulain going at it towards backstage... Diabolic Forces entering... A flash of more tag team action... then a shot of a Nero Kick just before the scene explodes into the Monday Night Brawl logo as EUWC Monday Night Brawl opens up to the American Airlines Arena in Dallas, Texas with thousands in attendance for EUWC Monday Night Brawl. John: Ladies and gentlemen good evening! Scott: We are here live from the Joe Louis Arena in Detroit Michigan! John: We are one week removed from the Chad Dupree Memorial Cup and we have a New EUWC undisputed World Heavyweight Champion! Scott: And I for one, cannot believe it, but the man has done it again.
