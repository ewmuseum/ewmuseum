This was a tournament final. EUWC went on hiatus in April 1998.  The title remained vacant until October 1999. This was a match with the two top contenders for the vacant title. The match was originally TTT vs Santana.  In the middle of the match DeLucci entered to attack both men and pinned Ray Santana to win the title. Title vacated when Harten targeted the World Heavyweight title on October 30.  The EUWC subsequently went on hiatus and the title remained vacant until September 2001. This was a Six Pack Ladder match for the vacant title.  Other participants were Simon, Jakob McLean, Gavin Glass, Grizzlie & Fred Rogers. This was the first ever Canadian Ladder Match. The EUWC went on hiatus.  The title remained vacant until March 2003. This was a four-way elimination match for the vacant title. AOD forfeits the title when he discovers he is entered in an Elimination Chamber match for the World Title at Only The Strong III. This was a tournament final. 230 days deducted due to EUWC hiatus in 2004/2005. This was a fatal four way Unification match for the United States and 52 Wild Titles. This was an Empty Arena Ladder match, held in Madison Square Garden. Due to a short hiatus, the reign actually begins on Feb. 9, 2006.
