Episode 2 May 19, 2008 Globen Arena Stockholm, Sweden Earlier Tonight "The Hitman" Tony DeLucci "The Hitman" walks into the arena, dressed in a suit and tie of blue and steel gray. His hair is still wet from the shower he had no more than twenty-five minutes ago. But, in a subtle attempt to save some grace and prestige, he does have it brushed straight back. Tony and his fine-fashion attire are not alone though, his long time friend and in-ring partner of many years is of course along with him tonight. "The Ripper" is currently wearing a finely hemmed and pleated suit of the finest materials, more red than white of course, with top of the line shoes of a solid artic white and matching laces. The only outright difference between the two men right at this moment, other than their choice in attire, is that Jack's hair is bone dry. They walk past a group of wayward reporters and general labourers who are standing near the main entrance to the building, a doorway which is several meters from the door that Tony and Jack just used. Neither man go out their collective way to avoid nor make their presence known to the reporters just yet. However their efforts, or lack thereof, are soon no longer of a concern as the reporters soon spark to life and quickly buzz their way full tilt. Jack and Tony soon find themselves surrounded by all makes and models of microphones, recorders, and video cameras; each one just desperately warranting the first words. A collage of reporters' voices ask aloud, "Excuse me! Gentlemen, excuse me! Just a couple of questions...please." Both men are prepared to speak their individual and team minds on several sparse subjects, but neither man show sign of wishing to oblige anyone other than themselves at this time and make such very clear to all quickly. Tony speaks first. "Each and every single one of you hypocritic vultures of the media world best shut those holes you call mouths, start using those peanuts that are supposed to be your brains, and listen to what we are about to say because we will NOT be repeating any of this to any of you..." Jack continues with, "Before tonight, none of you wished to fill any of your t.v. schedules with what either of us wanted to say or show you and the general public. But now, now you all want us to throw attention your way. Well, Tony and I have decided that from here on out, for the most part...we don't want you. More importantly, and to put it all in simplier terms for you to understand...we don't need you - any of you!" Tony smiles with, "If and when either one of us, or both of us, have something we want any of you or the anyone else to see or hear...we will make it happen in our own way. Not your way. OUR way!" "Cause you see, keeping in line with us getting back to basics...we realised that we have no use for you - for any of you. We realised that you people, or even the mere thought of people like you, was a true waste of time...time we will now be using preparing ourselves for bigger and better things." Jack interupts with, "And speaking of bigger and better things, instead of allowing ourselves to carry on with you media gophers any longer...I am going to get myself and, more importantly, my partner away from you and continue on with our preparations for his match tonight against Krueger and Choker." Jack and Tony grin at the mere mention of leaving all these reporters and such behind them as Jack comfortably pushes Tony further into the depths of the arena and away from their present environment of electronic-driven hell. Both men roll their eyes and give a sigh of relief, grateful that this part of their night is now over and done with - they can return all their efforts on the real matters of business for them tonight. Backstage Segment "The Hitman" Tony DeLucci The door gets kicked open as "The Hitman" Tony DeLucci makes his way through and down the joining hall. We do not see any signs of pent up anger, fenced in frustration, or barrels of determination waiting to be unleashed coming from DeLucci at this point. At the same time, however, no one is dare foolish enough to take that at full face value. We all know that it is there, just waiting for the right place and time to be set free among the masses. Luckily, this is neither the precise time nor the correct place. But it will be soon. In less than one full hour's time Tony DeLucci will finally find himself in the squared-circle with two other competitors - all three aiming for some of the same goals. In less than 60 minutes the thousands in attendance and the millions upon millions watching from the comfort of their homes and neighbourhood bars witness the extreme talent and superiority that is "The Hitman". And within the very same token of time, "The Hitman" Tony DeLucci will be authorized and empowered by his own god given nature to put in practice what he and his tag team partner have been planning and training for all week long - for a golden victory to be brought back to Cold Blooded's side of the world. Tony brings himself to a halt and begins to slowly tilt his head around. It tilts just enough to enable him to look back down the stretch of hallway he had just travelled. He smirks almost silently as he confirms his pseudo-solitude for the time being prior to going back on his way, now with a more casual and head strong pace. "Have you ever had the feeling as though you were about to accomplish something great, but you were not sure what exactly that something was?" he questions aloud. "Don't get me wrong, I have been able to get many great things done inside and outside...and even around, the ring...but even nowadays, from time to time, I still tend to get that feeling again." Another doorway crosses Tony's path, an obstacle which is solved this time with a cooler and calmer predisposition. He swings the double doors open and glides through them. It is becoming blantantly apparent to us now that Tony is building up in confidence and esteem the closer he gets to the ring and the closer the time gets for his match this evening. "I guess I could just say I am feeling this way tonight because tonight is going to be special...special for more than just one lonely reason mind you. We all know that tonight is going to be the first step towards myself and Jack stepping ever closer to greatness and godliness here in the EUWC. Tonight will also be the night that I will be able to punch my exclusive ticket to being an active member at Blood Bath, a ticket which "The Hitman" will surely cash in to its fullest extent." Tony comes to a stop again, this time he pivets himself as to allow himself to rest his back against the wall softly. He folds his arms across his chest and sighs twice before continuing to speak. "But before truly looking forward to tonight, I am going to take a moment or two and go over what recently has led us here in the first place. That being the actions and words from my two opponents tonight, Choker and Krueger." "Earlier this week, both Choker and Krueger stated supposed facts that they had a sense of wrath, a sense of aggression, a sense of hatred in store for me for tonight. They both carried themselves as being the ones who were going to knotch another loss on my side of things. As though one of them was going to walk away tonight with their hand raised instead of my own. They said all this thinking, damn near hoping, that some of it would unsettle or unnerve me enough as that I would let any of it actually happen. But they forgot about a few things..." Tony uncrosses his arms and has them fall halfway near his waist bringing them to life by using each hand to aide in pointing out the following, "They forgot that I have been around the EUWC too long for either of them to keep me away from a win. I have tasted championship gold before tonight, and I will have it again after tonight - neither of them will stand in the way of that, especially not tonight. In simplest terms...those two men have said far too much and have done far too little to warrant any more of my free time here and now on them." "It's time for me to return my concentration back to tonight, and what it is that will be making tonight so special." Tony pushes himself from resting against the wall. His march down the hallway, towards the ring, is on again. "The best part about what will be making tonight special is that I will be returning myself and Jack to greatness and godliness while locking in my ticket to Blood Bath by soundly beating, pummeling, and outright outwrestling Choker and Krueger. I am going to make those two men scream in pain...all sorts of pain. They may think that they know what they are getting themselves into tonight...and on some other night, they may have been right, but not tonight." "Not on a night where 'The Hitman' and 'The Ripper' have so much going in their favour," states Tony as a more obvious grin crawls across his face. A few moments of emptiness passes us before "The Hitman" is once again joined by his tag team partner, "The Ripper". And now, together again, Cold Blooded hold their heads up high and push on through the last doorway before stepping onto the entrance way to the ring.
