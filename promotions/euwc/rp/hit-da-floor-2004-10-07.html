Hit Da Floor

7 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Damian D Stone — 7 October 2004 **OOC- No parodies here...(hinting to ST's video), this one is gonna be fun** Linkin Park feat. Damian Stone & Echo Hit the Floor Meteoria Warner Bros. [[Slow fade into Damian's Stone's lockerroom during a house show. We see Damian sitting down, next to a locker. He is dressed in his ring gear, looking down. The door opens from the side]]
Man: Five minutes Damian...Five minutes. [[Damian, not raising his head, slams his right hand into the locker. He then slowly stands up and stretches. Picking up his new leather coat, he places it over the black DDS "Thou Shalt Be Crucified" Shirt. He then walks over to the door and opens it.
The camera quickly shows us Damian exiting the lockerroom, and the harsh sounding guitar opening of the song. As Damian walks down the hallway, we see people lined up along the wall. We see Damian look at them all as he walks down the lit walkway. Around the corner, he runs into Echo. Not even looking at him, she matches his pace as they begin to walk to the curtain. Mike Shindoa speaks]]
There are just too many Times that people Have tried to look inside of me Wondering what I think of you And I protect you out of courtesy Too many times that I've Held on when I needed to push away Afraid to say what was on my mind Afraid to say what I need to say [[We see them approach the curtain, as Damian drops his head, preparing himself for the crowd response for his entrance. Echo grips his right hand]]
Too many Things that you've said about me When I'm not around You think having the upper hand Means you've got to keep putting me down But I've had too many stand-offs with you It's just about as much as I can stand Just wait until the upper hand Is mine [[They walk out as the bright lights of the arena flash down on the camera. We cut to a camera on the other side of the curtain where we see Echo and Damian posing for the fans]]
One minute you're on top The next you're not Watch it drop Making your heart stop Just before you hit the floor One minute you're on top The next you're not Missed your shot Making your heart stop You think you won. And then it's all gone [[Cut back to the hallway after the match. We see Damian and Echo smiling, holding hands as they walk down it. During this, we see Damian quickly being fuzzed in and out, with Chester Bennington standing in his place]]
So many people like me Put so much trust in all your lies So concerned with what you think To just say what we feel inside So many people like me Walk on eggshells all day long All I know is that all I want Is to feel like I'm not stepped on [[Damian reaches the lockerroom. He turns around and kisses Echo and then steps inside.]] There are so many things you say That make me feel like you've crossed the line What goes up will surely fall And I'm counting down the time [[Damian walks over to the sink and turns on the cold water. He then reaches down and splashes water in his face. He then looks up at the mirror and his face is in shock]]
'Cause I've had so many stand-offs with you It's about as much as I can stand So I'm waiting until the upper hand Is mine [[Damian sees Danny Collins, then Holocaust, then Chester Bennington looking back at him. Cut to Chester on stage at a live LP concert. He begins to scream with Mike on back-ups]]
One minute you're on top The next you're not Watch it drop Making your heart stop Just before you hit the floor One minute you're on top The next you're not Missed your shot Making your heart stop You think you won. [[Damian punches the mirror, and his arm goes completly through it, making the mirror now looking like the molten metal from a T-1000 from Terminator 2.]]
And then it's all gone And then it's all gone And then it's all gone And then it's all gone [[Damian looks at the mirror as someone else reaches out and grabs Damian by the right arm. Damian looks down in shock as he is pulled into the mirror.]]
I know I'll never trust a single thing you say You knew your lies would divide us But you lied anyway And all the lies have got you floating Up above us all But what goes up has got to fall [[Echo walks into the lockerroom, looking around as she sees the mess of clothes that Damian has thrown around in his wake. As she begins to clean up, she looks at the mirror, then she smiles.]]
One minute you're on top The next you're not Watch it drop Making your heart stop Just before you hit the floor One minute you're on top The next you're not Missed your shot Making your heart stop You think you won. And then it's all gone [[We see Damian standing on LP's stage, rocking with the band, by the drumset as Chester, Mike, Hahn and Phoenix are grooving along to the beat beside him]]
And then it's all gone And then it's all gone And then it's all gone Now it's all gone. [[As the song finishes up, Damian is thrown back through the mirror, landing hard on the floor. As he pulls himself up, he notices Echo. He then gets up, and walks over to the mirror. In the mirror, he see again, Holocaust, Sean Taylor, and Danny Collins. Damian, in another fit of rage, punches the mirror, this time shattering it. As it shatters, we hear Mike say:
"Told you everything loud and clear..." Damian bows his head. He then looks up, with a smile on his face]] D: Nobody's listening...
