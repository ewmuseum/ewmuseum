EUWC — Roleplay

T. Waring vs Sevyn

24 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MattPickstock — 24 July 2005 Post all promos for the above match below... Roleplay deadline is Saturday July 30 at 12:00 a.m. Saturday morning July 30. Any and all in-card promos and match strats are to be sent to [detail withheld]
T. Waring — 24 July 2005 *The scene opens in a bedroom. The room is decorated with pictures of famous title belts from past federations. The old WWF title is hanging on the wall and the WCW world title is hanging on the foot of the fourpost bed. The room also has wrestling figures scattered all around. The figures have been aranged to show finishing moves of some WWE, WCW, ECW and EUWC wrestlers. The door soon opens and in walks "Commander" T. Waring and Grace. Waring has a bright gold suit on. The gold tie and the red shirt clash horribly but Waring seems to like it. Grace has nothing but a towel on.*
Grace: Lets see. You have had three matches in EUWC. You faced Sevyn, Lt. Hawkeye and Lord Anthrag and you fought in a 10 man over the top battle royal match. You have had three matches and you have won.....
Waring: None! I have won none. None! None! None! Grace: Well, no need to get so worked up about it. Why havent you been winning? Waring: Its because I am being cheated on. That Matt Prickstock or Pickstock or whatever his name is has been putting me in hard matches on purpose. Its cause I was Red Branch High champ for a month before I left that pathetic company and used someone to fake my death and now he tries to put me in matches against these Rookies and Hasbeens. Im not mentally prepared to fight jobbers. Im here to fight Champions and i should be on MainFrame but who i have i got instead.
Grace: Sevyn. Waring: Who? Grace: Sevyn. The guy you tried to take out. Waring: Oh him. He is nothing more than a worthless pile of horse S**T. Maybe when I ge the job done and tke him out, then pickstock might give me the title match i deserve.
*Waring takes his top off and a massive bruise on his back is visible almost immediatly. Grace takes her towel off and goes into the other room which joins on the bedroom. Waring sits down and begins rubbing his back*
Waring: (wispering) I hope my back holds on Sunday. Grace depends on this win. I cant let her down again. I took too many spinebusters from Anthrag. My back was knackered otherwise i would be facing Rondtree.
Grace: What yu talking about? Waring: Nothing Grace. We ready for our poker guests. Grace: Yeah, what do you think. *Grace comes out in a silver satin dress. The dress is very long and goes with her hair perfectly. Waring sits there jaw-dropped. He eyes looking all over Graces body.*
Waring: You....look.....lovely. Grace: Thanks, come on lets go. *After a moment of black, the scene reopens. Waring is seated with around $300 worth of chips. There are five more men seated at the table. Its on Waring to act*
Waring: Ill see you $20 and i raise $40 Man 1: Ok I call. *Waring looks down to see an Ace of diamonds and 6 of spades. He thinks while the others call around him*
Waring: So, Sevyn accepts my challenge. It seems we will fight on a sledgehammer on a pole match. A first in any wrestling event. It cant be as bad as the guitar on a pole match I lost back in 98'. I have fought in many matches, some more brutal that others and some just down right stupid but this one, this one could make my career. If i defeat the leader of Synn then i could be in line to face Greer, Walker or Roundtree for the title.
*The poker action continues while Waring thinks. He calls another $20 and the flop shows Ace of Spades, Seven of Spades and King of Hearts.*
Waring: What if sevyn wins? Then I will be at a point in my career that I have never been at. Either I continue going down the losing path. Or, maybe I should call it a day. So many decisions. Maybe, I cant take on EUWC without the help of a few guys. Maybe join a tag team, start making friends. Even, join Pickstock. So many decesions.
*The Turn comes up as the two of spades and after everyone checks, the river has a nine of spades. Waring raises all in and goes back to thinking*
Waring: So, If i join Pickstock then I could own this company. He might not want someone of my class. He might want some other T. Waring wanna-be like Lt. Hawkeye. Maybe even Team Danger might take me on. So many choices. But firstly I need to prove I am man enough to be part of the EUWC. Maybe the resurection of the Hardcore belt might prove this. Maybe I can get it to resurface and then take it to hell and back with me. Anyway enough about wrestling i need to win this.
*Waring waits for the showdown and shows the Ace high flash. He rakes all the chips as the other players moan.. The scene then turns to black*
T. Waring — 29 July 2005 *The scene opens in a deserted street down Orlando, Florida. The street has trash cans down it and the passing of cop sirens from far away is distinctly heard. There camera moves further down to reveal a man. The man has a grey trench coat on and huge black jeans. He has steel top cap work boots on too. Standing next to him is a woman dressed in a black suit. Her silvery hair stands out in the little moonlight. The man pulls a lighter to his face so we can see it is "The Commander" T. Waring. He uses the lighter and goes further down the path. He comes up to the door and knocks three times. The door opens like no-one is there. Waring and Grace walk in to meet a rather old man. He has a dinner jacket on. His beard looks to have not been shaven for months. He smiles as Waring and Grace walk down the corridor to him.*
Old Man: Waring! What a pleasure it is to see you again. I trust you havent forgotten me? Waring: Not a chance Archy. I have been looking foward to meeting you again. This however is no social visit. I need access to my old stuff.
Old Man: Ok, let me go fetch the keys. *The Old man leaves to fetch the keys. Waring and Grace sit on the shabby looking wooden chairs and wait. Grace takes her coat off.*
Grace: Why did we have to wait in the rain? We could have just used a taxi. Waring: It is better to walk. I cannot let a taxi driver witness what I am about to pull. We will be living here for a few days too. Its better for the 'work' you see.
Grace: What are we even doing here? Waring: You will find out. However, I must keep the building secret until Archy returns. Ah, here he comes now.
*The Old man returns as Waring and Grace stand up. Archy comes back with a key in his hand. He places the key in Waring's black glove, smiles, nods and walks off humming "coming round the mountain". Waring walks down the same corridor Archy came down and walks up to a door. He unlocks but he does not open it.*
Grace: Why you not going inside? Waring: Well, I want you to stay out here. Things could get nasty. I havent been here for a while. There could be violence involved. Stay here.
Grace: Ok, but be careful. *Grace gives Waring a kiss before she walks back to the seating area. Waring opens the door. Inside are a few men and a wrestling ring. They all stop looking when Waring walks in.*
Waring: My brothers. I have returned Brother 1: You return to us Waring. The question is why? Why do you come back after leaving us?
Waring: I had other things. I needed to clear my head. I needed to get away from it. Brother 2: But you broke the ritual Waring. You sold us out when you left. You are nothing here.
Waring: I seek help, and answers that is all. Brother 1: Why should we help you? You went to become the superstar you are today. You won the RBW high title. You are on EUWC. Why do you need us?
Waring: I wish to know if I made the right choice. If I really should be on EUWC? Brother 1: Well, to us you have homed your skills perfectly. However, you left us and we can offer no answers.
Waring: I really need to know. My career depend on it. Please? Brother 2: No, and you will not get away this time. *The men who were wrestling suddenly begin to move in on Waring. Waring takes off his coat and begins to defend himself. However, suddenly a man takesa out Waring by the knee. The men then begin to stomp on Waring and punch him. Waring breaks out of the hold and runs to the door. The men run after him. Waring whitles. However the men get him and pin him to the floor. One of the brothers comes onto Waring with a knife. He attempts to stab Waring put the door suddenly bursts open and the Old man comes in wielding Waring s sledgehammer. He tosses it to Waring who catches it and drives into into the gut of one of the men. He swings and takes out another. The men back off. They look at Waring in fear. Waring swings and connects with the man on the floor. The shot drills him in the heart. Waring then walks over to the brother with the knife. He picks him up and out of rage he slams the sledgehammer into his gut and drops the brother.*
Waring: Hahahaha! You see Sevyn. I am so deadly with the sledgehammer you dont stand a chance against me. I just proved I can take numerous assaults and I can dominate anything. You will be in the hospital next to Dwight on Sunday night and you will say to him, "WHy did i chose the match?" He will say nothing and then you will see me on Mainframe with your blood on my hands and me as the winner. I will then go onto the crush anyone who gets in my way and then there will be one man left. Greer you better shine that title up real nice, give it a spit shine and then get ready to hand it to the commander. You see I dont need to earn respect. I COMMAND it!
*With that Waring walks out with the old man. They lock the door on the way back out and Grace runs over to Waring and hugs him. She notices the huge gash on Waring head.*
Grace: What happened? Waring: Nothing, it was just another day in the office. Cyah later Archy! Old man: Good day to you sir. *Waring and Grace walk back up the corridor and into the pouring rain. THey walk up the street as the scene fades to black.*

Synn — 30 July 2005 Sevyn gets out of the passenger side of a shiny red BMW. He motions for DSwight who is driving to go and he will call him when he is ready to be picked up. He walks up to the door of a shop which has a sign reading "Tell me the future". He knocks on the door and a lady answers.
The lady has thick heavy rimmed glasses and a very messed up shoulder length hair. Her multi-coloured shawl covers her upper body.
Lady: Come in Sevyn I have been expecting you. Sevyn: Thank you Madam Raven. Raven: What is is that brings you here my son? What is it you are seeking? The two walk into a dimly lit room and sit on opposing sides of a round table covered in a red tablecloth.
Sevyn: What I seek is not truly for myself. What I truly seek is for you to look into what a certain 5 playing cards may mean.
Raven: Ah, you seek your fortune through the cards. I can definitely see what is in store for you. What is the first card?
Sevyn: The six of spades. Raven: The six of spades. Ooooh, that is a good card. That means gradual improvement. For a man in the wrestling business that will mean great things.
Sevyn: Damn, maybe his winning hand will prove to be good enough. Raven: What do you mean my son? Who's fortune is it you seek? Sevyn: My opponent on Sunday. Commander T. Waring. He was playing some Texas Hold'em and he showed himself winning a hand. I am sure that it must mean something. It has to have some deeper meaning. He must be telling me something other than he can win at poker.
Raven: What is the next card? Sevyn: The Ace of spades. That must be bad. Raven: Bad news, death, endings. Definitely a bad omen. Maybe you should not wrestle this man.
Sevyn: These are his winning cards. I will be fine. Next is the Seven of Spades. Raven: The Sevyn do you say. Let me think here. That card means A BATTLE NOT WORTH FIGHTING. Sevyn do not fight this man. It is truly not worht it.
Sevyn: Wait, wait, wait. What about the Two of Spades. Raven: Deception and scandal surround this card. Not good omens for you. Do not fight this man.
Sevyn: Hold on here so we have gradual improvement, bad news, a battle not worth fighting, deception and scandal. The fifth and final card if the Four of Spades.
Raven: Well this seems to favour you. Delays, illness, emotional problems. Those can't be good for Waring.
Sevyn: None of this is good for a man who conciders this to be what would take him to the top. He is only gradually improving and as of now he can barely hold my jock. The man is nothing. His collection of wrestling items include silly trinkets. Mine includes real pieces of history. It is a shrine to wrestling not some childrens playroom. The man has lost to me when everything was down. There is not a chance that gradual improvement will be enough.
Raven: You see things differently then I. I am seeing that this is bad for you. Sevyn: You forget Madam Raven this is not my fortune you are reading. This is the fortune of my opponent. He is the man who will be entering a battle not worth fighting. The man who will receive bad news and death. The man who will become delayed or ill. This man will receive division and scandal as I send him back to the minor leagues of Red Branch where he was so great before.
Raven: The man did hold the high championship there. Sevyn: So did Jack The Ripper and well he is one of the most rediculous excuses for a human being I have ever seen. Anyways I have a little card up my sleeve. A card which would have ensured my victory had I been at that same table.
Raven: What is this card you speak of? Sevyn: The eight of spades. I don't worry (pause) I have nothing to fear. Raven: You must leave at once. But be warned that the eight of spades does not bring good things. It brings a secret enemy and dissapointments.
Sevyn gets up and walks toward the door. He turns and looks at Raven. Sevyn: No worries Madam Raven I have Dwight at my side to watch for those secret enemies. Raven: Beware the rival pretending to be a friend. Sevyn walks out the door. He pulls out his cell phone and makes a call. Sevyn: Dwight you can come pick me up now. She warns me to watch out for secret enemies and that a rival may be posing as a friend. I am not worrying because damn was Waring ever screwed with his cards.
The car pulls up and the BMW drives away as we focus on the liscence plate reading SYNN.
