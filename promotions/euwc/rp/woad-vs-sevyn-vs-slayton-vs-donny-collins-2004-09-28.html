Marcus: Done but I'm not going to do that until after my match because to put it even more simply, I've already got a full plate on my hands for Main Frame ranging from beating the hell out of three other kids to paying a certain mister C back for what he did at Ultra Brawl. Other than that, I won't stop at Main Frame until the game is ended pure and simple. The game ladies and gentlemen is life and while I don't intend to kill anyone, I do intend to severally hurt someone who I won't say his name right now to keep it a secret for the time being but I do have one comment to pass onto you Holocaust. Holocaust, myu issues with you are merely on hold until I feel that you're ready again for me to start kicking your ass again. pchammer — 1 October 2004 The wolf and the Sandman (We fade into the familiar to some as the workout area for the Reaver's Dojo. The man formerly known as the "Reaver" is currently working out on a heavy bag wearing a pair of black workout pants and a white tank top. The ever present tattoos and scars from years of wrestling is evident as he works out. The person holding the heavy bag is one of his former tag team partners in the form of Eric Helms, the man once known to the wrestling community as "Slash".)
Eric: Damn Marc, you've been at this for the past six hours. Shouldn't you be thinking about taking a break by now?
Marcus: No, like I told those kids before in my last promo that the gloves are off and if that means that I have to train twice as hard in order to get the job done then so be it. I'm through playing nice with these punks ranging from DONNY Collins to Holocaust to Mikey C, if they feel that I haven't been giving it all that I've got in these past few matches are going to get a real surprise when they step into the ring against me next because I'm absoutly through being nice.
Eric: Yeah, but you're going to overwork yourself? I mean there is proving people wrong and then there is being so overworked that you are too ****ing tired when you climb into the ring and give less than you're best?
(Marcus actually stops and looks at his friend and former teamate) Marcus: That's a good point my friend. There is such a thing as over doing it even though some people like Donny Collins who's apparentally not giving this match the full attention that it deserves and he automatically thinks that he'll be able to get out of the match and everyone will just lay down for him but that's not happening this time because putting plain and simple-I'm not gonna stop until I've shown him the shortest eight second ride of his whole career to date and I'm not going to stop until I've got my hand raised high and my music playing over the speakers. You see Donny, you think that I've got my attention focused elsewhere when in fact it's focused manly on our match for Main Frame and I promise you that it will be a dozey especially since you've just volunteered to be victim number one in this match by accusing me of being Prince, I'm sorry but I'm not "Sugar" Kevin Renzor who uses a move called the "Purple Rain", so to compare me to that rookie is nothing and it'll get you no where because very soon you're going to see what it means to be shown what a little something that my friend Eric hear calls the "Path of Rage" where it's basically an overridding urge to completely and utterly wipe out an opponent from the match. So, I want you to bring the best that you've got to the ring and show me just how tough all three of your are and how much you three truly want this title match other wise, you'll just be another victim...qouthe the Sandman...Nevermore.
