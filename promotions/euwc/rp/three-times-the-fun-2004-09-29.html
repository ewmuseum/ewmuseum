EUWC — Roleplay

Three times the ... fun ...

29 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
AOD — 29 September 2004 {Suddenly, the screen jumps as if it has just been turned on. As it clears, only a blank black screen can be seen. A few seconds pass, and white lettering begins to scroll up the screen, much like the openings of the Star Wars movies.}
Three Stages of Hell ... Three matches, one night ... One man standing at the end ... {Fade to a shot of Angel of Death walking through an iron refinery. The heat from the machines creates the familiar wavy effect in the air as the occasional spark flies from the iron being processed. AOD stops in an open area, and turns to face the camera.}
AOD: {smirking} So Ray, can you feel the heat? Blood Bath approaches, and Pickstock has ordered us to settle our differences in Three Stages of Hell. Frankly, I think it's a brilliant idea. Since we've taken each other to hell and back on more than one occasion, why not visit many levels of hell? After all is said and done, I say we finally bury the hatchet. This series of matches is to finally settle the score. What do you say? I also propose that we each choose one of the first two stages, and if there is a tie after two matches, the winner of the second match chooses the type of match for the third stage. Again, let me know what you think. Are you willing to put all of your pride on the line? I guess I'll just have to wait to find out.
{He turns, and walks off into the shadows of the refinery as we fade to black.}
