EUWC — Roleplay

[SS 2005] TTT & Ray Santana vs Antharg & Buxton

6 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MattPickstock — 6 August 2005 Post any promos for the above match below. RP Deadline is Friday August 19 at Midnight (Eastern Standard Time). All in-card promos and strats are to be sent to [detail withheld] by the RP Deadline.
Ray Santana — 17 August 2005 (The Salvation - Ray Santana and TTT - are sitting in a bar knocking back a few.) Ray: Well, we got the match we asked for. The Noblemen at Summer Sizzler. TTT: 'Bout time we got back into the ring. Ray: Tell me about it. It's been a while since we've champs. Now Pickstock says we have to start at the bottom of the ladder.
TTT: Fine by me. I could use a warm up before challenging Team Danger. Ray: Intense ****ers, aren't they? TTT: At least Greer. Although I heard he staged that riot at Superiority. Ray: Yeah? TTT: Yeah. I heard he hired actors to rush the ring. Even heard that there were blanks in his gun.
Ray: Well, before we get to Team Danger, we're going to have to go through the rest of the teams, starting with the Noblemen.
TTT: Who are these guys again? Ray: King Buxton and Lord Antharg. TTT: Lord what? Ray: Antharg. I don't know. He's new. Far as I can tell, this match should be cake walk. TTT: Good. That way we can be on the plane home early. (A drunk walks over to them.) Drunk: Hey! Aren't you guys wrestlers? Ray: Yes. Drunk: You don't look so tough. TTT: (laughing) We get by. Drunk: You two look like pussies. I could kick your asses. TTT: Hey Ray, I think he called you a *****. Ray: Yeah, I heard. Drunk: C'mon ya sissy boy. TTT: Sissy boy? Ooooooohhhhh. Ray: It's ok. I've been called worse. (The drunk takes Ray's beer and dumps it onto the floor.) Ray: (standing) Ok look. You can call me a ***** and you can call me a sissy but the one thing you can't do is spill a perfectly good beer.
Drunk: Ya? Sho whattaya you do abit it? (Ray quickly lifts his fist and stops. The drunk cowers and takes a step back. He walks into a chair and trips over it. he falls onto a table spilling drinks everywhere. The rest of the bar starts laughing hysterically.)
TTT: Well done. Ray: (smiles) I thought so. (Fade to black.)
