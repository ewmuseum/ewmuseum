EUWC — Roleplay

Jay Smash vs Danny Collins

24 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MattPickstock — 24 July 2005 Post all promos for the above match below... Roleplay deadline is Saturday July 30 at 12:00 a.m. Saturday morning July 30. Any and all in-card promos and match strats are to be sent to [detail withheld]
Jay_Smash — 27 July 2005 (To this very day, people have questioned how this man has become who he is today. Millions of wrestling fans watched as a man went from giving pain, to wanting pain. Now he sits in a chair, thinking to himself. he sits with the look of tears in his eyes, his face looks as if he has not shaved in weeks, and his hair drooping down. He sweats in the enclosed room as the tempurature rises outside. The heat seems to be nothing to him, as he sits there, thinking. What is he thinking about?)
Jay Smash: They said, I shouldn't feel so bad. That I should remember the good times, and try to believe they are in a better place. I have to make peace with God, and understand that it was their time. They left me, because it was meant to happen. Do you know what happens when someone's heart stops? When the heart stops beating, at first, you can't really take much of a breath, it begins to sting, and then, not much happens after that. You fall, and you die. You can't live without the heart. I think I can live without though. Yeah... I think it's broken enough, to pull out, and feel a bit better. And why not; why can't I feel better? Is there something wrong with feeling better?
(He crouches down where his arms meet his thighs, then pulls out a small knife from his boot. He begins to stare at it, staring at his reflection through the blade, then places the knife close to his heart.)
Jay Smash: Should I? Maybe it won't be so bad. After all, this is exactly what is causing all the pain! She kept me feeling alive. She was going to help me to believe. I was going to sit there with her and learn the answers. She motivated me, and now she is gone. My wife, and my child, my young child that had barely learned about the world. She has left me for God. What god? What kind of God would do this? The all mighty himself works in mysterious ways. The creator of all things, good... and evil... has taken a good soul, and punctured a whole straight in his heart!
(He begins to carve his chest with the knife, not really showing what he is doing, but drops of blood fall from his chest and to the ground. He continues to carve, while crouched down, then he drops the knife and shows his hands to the world, dripping with blood)
Jay Smash: Now I remember... I know what the plan is... We have it all planned out... Every detail... Every move that must be made... We have everything ready, to take EUWC beneath us. Is this what they want? Is this what they are waiting for? Are they waiting to see me at the top again? They won't see me alone... We are going to find pain.... and we will look at... everyone. I began, with Angel of Death. Danny Collins is next.... Wait and see... just how I work is mysterious ways.... the "disease" spreads once more.....
(After his words are spoken, Jay Smash stands up, spreading the blood from his hands against the lens of the camera he had taping him, now showing only the blood, and nothing else...)

Danny Collins — 27 July 2005 ((Fade In to a park in Central London, nothing big, nothing fancy, just a same piece of green grass, surrounded by bushes and a few benches. On one of those benches, sits a young man, his coat collar covers the lower part of his face as he sits in the fine rain on a muggy evening in London. The camera moves in slowly to reveal the Young man to be Danny Collins. His face is stern, and he barely notices the rain. Slowly he looks up and toward the camera, before speaking.))
Collins: I've been quiet lately, too quiet. But that's about to change. See, since I lost to Sean Taylor at Blood Bath, I've been kind of depressed, but can you blame me?
I mean, here I was, standing at the top of the pedestal, the world title finally in my grasp, and then, just like that, its gone. Then to make matters worse, I lose to Angel of Death... ANGEL OF DEATH! Holy crap.... I don't know what the hell is going on with me, I was once this determined force, that couldn't be stopped from getting what I want, but that has stopped dead, just like my title reign. I no longer feel the determination I once did, I no longer feel the want, the lust for the title that I once did. I was once the guy who would do what was right, stopping the Dominion despite the fact it put me in tremendous danger, I know I didn't do it alone, but I spoke out, I didn't like it, thing is, its all happening again....
Team Danger..... The New Dominion. Isn't it obvious? Terror and fear is once again rife in the EUWC, because one threat has moved on, and new one has emerged, so who will stand up? Who will rebel against them? Certainly not me, to be honest, and I'm going to be, I couldn't give two ****s about Team Danger, and what they do. Nor could I give two ****s about Mike C and his group of rebels to Team Dangers menace, as far as I am concerned you lot stay out of my way, and I'll stay out of yours, and we'll be sweet.
((Danny sits up a bit, and turns down his collar. He then reaches into his pocket and produces a box of cigarettes and a lighter. Danny then puts the cigarette in his mouth and lights it, puffing smoothly on the stick and exhaling the grey smoke, before speaking once more.))
Collins: So this week, I'm back, and I am facing someone who I haven't seen in a while, Mr. Jay Smash. Well, I must say this is a present suprise, when I first came here, Jay Smash was the guy to be. It was obvious Jay was going to become great, and his momentous rise was only matched by his equally large downfall. Pity really, because since my first day here in EUWC I really admired you, because your just like me in so many ways Jay, and if we wasn't booked to fight this week, I would have loved to had team with you. But I think somehow that a Jay Smash/Danny Collins match will be just as bloody good. Although I am quite concerned for Jay's mental health, he seems a little, too depressed. I mean, all the dark things he said happened to him, and then he cuts himself?! Hmmm, Jay Smash is a strange individual indeed these days. This could possibly be one of the toughest matches I have had in my career, and I am excited, because it will be good to be back, for different reasons anyway.
See this time, its not about what's right, its about one thing, me. I look out for me now, Donny can look after himself, but he knows I'll always be there for him. And Cheryl, well Cheryl's here in London, and that's where she will stay till she's had the baby. Hence me being here.
Jay, I look forward to facing you this week, I'd rather it be a wrestling match rather then a fight, but we all know that you and I just won't be able to do that, we will fight, and I'm sure that whoever wins, will definitely earn it. But when its all said and done, I'm gonna look right at you, regardless of the outcome, and show my respect for you by offering a handshake, whether you take it or not, is up to you.
See you soon. ((Danny puts the cigarette in his mouth and then rises from his seat, before walking down the concrete path and out of the park as the scene slowly fades out....))
