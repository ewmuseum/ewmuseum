T. Waring — 8 July 2005 *The halls are dark. There seems to be no sign of life. The wind screams in the distance and the lightning outside is fierce. The rain on the roof is defening to anyone who enters. The lights flick on almosy automatically. They flick back off again. Again there is only darkness. The lights again flick on and a shadow is seen in the distance. They flick back off. The sound of footsteps are heard. "Clop" "Clop". The lights flick back on and the face of a man stares blankly into the camera. His red hair blowing in the breeze. He has a old raincoat on. His hair is all wet. His trousers are cut and skin is visable. The man is known as "Commander" T. Waring. He continues to stare into the camera. Suddenly....*
Voice: Waring!!!! *Waring jumps as his name is heard. He turns around to see Grace in the corridor. He walks up to meet her*

My skull has taken so many shots it doesnt even feel pain.
Grace: Still didnt stop you losing on Thursday. Waring: Evol, Evol, Evol. The man who eliminated me. The man who cheap shotted me from behind. The man who had to take me from behind to eliminate me. You had just better hope that after i have dealt with this loser Sevyn that you dont end up facing me because you will face at my feet. Your face will be a crimson mass. You will be hurt and you will fall onto the canvass. You will try to get up and i will kick you back down. You will lose to me.
*Waring walks out of the corridor and into a red velvet door. This door leads to the film room. Here the film of when Waring was eliminated is showing.*
Waring: You see. I dominated the match. I showed Team Danger just how to play. Walker was effectively finshed thanks to me. But you, Evol, You had to eliminate me while i was entertaining the fans. Something you seem unable to do. You have to take the cowards way out.
Grace: Erm.... Waring: And you, Rocko Daymon. You only won that match because i wasnt the last man. You only won because i softened up Walker for you to eliminate him. You took the victory which was mine. You had also better hope you dont face me because i will snap you like a twig. You will fall at the feet of the mighty comannder. You will beg for mercy. You will beg me to forgive you. You will Beg me not to end your career. Then I will look down on you Daymon. I will smile. Then I will pick you up and put you into the commander slam. Then when your body is crippled i will pin you and win the intercontinetal title. Then when i am victorious you will be in hospital with Evol and Sevyn. You will be looking at your flowers that i sent you and you will be retired!!! You hear me. RETIRED!!!!
*Waring turns to face the camera. He looks into the lens and then he speaks more* Waring: Now we come to Paul Doom. Paul Doom. Umm...Ill call you Paul. Paul when you got into the ring on Thursday I had heard that you were the toughest SOB. You were the best. What i saw on Thursday was a lost child. You lost. you were the best and you was eliminated first. To me thats just poor. Ryan Baker! You are nothing. If you fluke your match and face me I will crush you like a twigg. You will fall in the feet of greatness.
Grace: Waring!!! Waring: And you Tyrone Walker. Where would you be without Greer or roundtree licking ure a**. You would be just another guy. Sevyn: Where the hell are you Dwight? I was supposed to sit down with you before the tournament and scout out each of the competitors. I know who I face first round but from there the rest is a mystery. T. Waring, the man who claims to be great. The man who breaks men like twiggs. The man who thinks the only reason Rocko Daymon won the match is because T. Waring wasn't the second last man. Maybe you were not the second last man Waring because other men were better than you. You are but a lowly rookie here. You have no right to claim who is and is not great. You need to learn when shooting your mouth off matters and when you shoot it off make sure what you are saying is FACT. Yes I have held the Hardcore title and was very likely the last man to hold it with any honour. I defended it and left many men brutally beaten. That much will never change. You do however need a wrestling history lesson and as the local historian I am just the man to bring it to you. I have held another title here in the EUWC. I have held the US Championship and fought through everything to win it after 216 days of fighting. Although it took me some time I fought through a serious concussion and was not actually cleared to wrestle. My matches with former US and World Champion Jay Smash are legendary. My fights in the Seven Stages of Sin were great. My matches with Tony DeLucci were
The door opens and in walks Dwight Jackson with a duffle bag. He sets the bag down on a table and pulls out a portable DVD player. He plugs it into the wall and puts a DVD in the player.
Sevyn: About time you got here. I want to go over the footage of the other men in the tournament. This truly means alot to me. You know I have set my goal at winning the International title and then moving on to challenge Sean Taylor for the EUWC World Title.
Dwight: I know what your goal is. That is why I have the footage of all the competitors in the tournament here on DVD. We can see what each and every one of these guys brings to the ring and look for possible weaknesses.
Sevyn: I just wish Mammon and Molloch were going to be there tonight. I could use there help keeping Donnie Collins out of my business. That's right Collins I haven't forgotten what you and your brother did to me on Main Frame. I will have my revenge in time. Tonight I focus on the task at hand and that is winning gold once more.
Dwight stops the disk and points at something. Sevyn: What is it Dwight? What did you see? Dwight: I just saw a weakness in Waring's offence. I see where he made a mistake. I see where he has shown a small flaw.
Sevyn: Sure, how are we going to get him to repeat exactly the same move again. Dwight: You aren't seeing the big picture. Sevyn: So what you are telling me is I should let him get me in the very same spot and then focus on the flaw where his current opponent missed the chance.
Dwight: No what I am trying to tell you is Waring doesn't stand a chance. You have me in your corner and that is Waring's flaw. He is one man and we are two. No matter what happens Collins, Waring, whomever it happens to be next we are ready and we are heading to the finals and our ultimate goal of International Gold.
Sevyn: I appreciate your enthusiasm but Dwight I am going o be champion again and you will still just be Dwight Jackson.
Dwight: What do you mean by that Sevyn? What do you mean I will just be Dwight Jackson? What are you saying?
Sevyn: I am saying you are my friend. You are my best friend but when it comes down to it I am the champ and you are just another member of Synn.
Dwight: I meant we in the royale kind of way. You hold the gold and I help out where I am needed.
Sevyn: Okay, I knew you had the right ideas. Just checking with you though. Let's continue to watch this footage and maybe we can find a true weakness.
Sevyn pauses the DVD again as we fade to black T. Waring — 9 July 2005 *The scene opens in an office. The office has black walls and the curtains look like they were used in the 1950's. We see an old chair in the office. The chair is rocking back and forth. In the chair is "Commander" T. Waring. Waring is watching some footage of sevyn in action. He is not impressed.*
Waring: Look at this joke. The last man to hold the hardcore title with honor. The US champion for 200 plus days. Were the rest of the roster on holiday Sevyn. You fight with no honor. All you do is get in there, make $50 and get home to your pathetic family and feed them for the week. Me, I wrestle to win. I wrestle not for the huge cash sum. I wrestle to entertain.

Then you will wake up in a hospital knowing you were beaten by the better man. Then you will watch the DVD and see Waring holding high his intercontinental title. You will watch me entertain, something which many of EUWCs roster cannot do. I will point to the intercontinetal title and when you are at home a broken has-been, you will just simply applaude greatness.
*Waring picks up the sledgh hammer and smashes it into the TV. He laughd manically then turns back to the camera*
Waring: That TV will be you Sevyn. You will be broken into thousands of pieces. You will be crushed and then you little friend will be crying over you. Then I will pick him up and finish him. You say I cannot say who is great or not around here. Well I say you might want to rethink that when i beat 16 other men and get my first EUWC title within 3 days of being here. Then I will find you Sevyn. You will be on a bed in a amulance. I will get a microphone and you will announce to the world just who is great. I command respect. I have paid my dues. I wasnt the Red Branch high champion because I got it handed to me. you will feel the full force of me Sevyn.
