A fantasy federation on the fwrestling.com hub that ran from mid-2007 to the spring of 2008 under two owners' billing, with a weekly eXposed, a second show called Violence, pay-per-views such as GoldRush and Halloween Massacre, five championships and nine World reigns; the next company's staff page says its founder closed it after eleven months. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Barely Legal Wrestling Alliance, written Barley Legal on its own pages, ran in 2007 and the first months of 2008 with Johnny Hall and Tyler Sparks opening the recap of every captured show and a co-owner called Ryder E behind them. Its championship page opens at GoldRush on 16 June 2007, where Celeste LaRue became the first World champion, Saigon the first Grand Prix champion, Desperado the only TVX champion and Team Vendetta the first Tag Team champions; its weekly show was eXposed and its second show Violence.

Seven eXposed shows survive in captures of November 2007 to June 2008, each billed 'BLWA Presents: eXposed!'. The first follows Halloween Massacre on 31 October 2007, where Barelai Von'Vicious won the World title and, by the fed's own telling, was destroyed and died after the return of Johnny Stylez; the show went on without him. Hallow Wicked won a tournament for the vacant title on 12 November 2007, lost it to Mastermind at Violence on 30 March 2008 and won it back on eXposed on 6 April.

Its championship history ends with every belt vacant 'due to BLW Closer', its last dated entry is 30 April 2008, and a capture of 8 May 2008 carries only an under-construction line. The next company's staff page says Johnny Hall closed it after eleven months and that Jayson Burrows, who had worked under him, bought its rights and ran it into the ground as BLW before founding Wrestling Unplugged.
