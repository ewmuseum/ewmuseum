Jaden Lee Name: Jaden Lee Nickname N/A Height 5'6 Weight 123 lbs Hometown New Jersey Manager N/A Alignment Tweener Physical Appearance: Jaden, of Korean and American decent has the “girl next door” look. With long brown hair but her hair color will change from time to time and brown eyes. Outside of the ring, Jaden would be caught in a cloud of hairspray and makeup. Most of the time when she’s out and about, she’s dressed in a tube tops and skirts. She is one girl who isnt shy about what she has.
Gimmick: The IT girl, The one everyone wants, but none can have Wrestler's History: Jaden was born in Singapore, while her family was emigrating from Vietnam after the Vietnam War. A few years later, they moved again to a small neighborhood in Jersey, where they lived in rundown housing for most of Jaden's childhood until they were admitted to a gated community run by a strict Buddhist temple. The family left the community when Jaden was eight.
As soon as she entered middle school, Jaden developed a tomboy attitude and got in fights. Her behavior eventually got her sent to a boarding school for six months before being transferred to another school. As soon as she reached high school, she used her sister's identification card to enter nightclubs, where she began recreational drug use and joined a gang. In an interview with Wrestling Today, Jaden said she had been searching for a sense of identity: "I was really confused then, 'cause at first I thought I was black, then I thought I was Hispanic and joined a cholo gang". Later she made some friends outside the gang who briefly helped turn her life around, however her past caught up with her, and she fled to Queens, New York at the age of sixteen. She then began to focus on her studies and jobs. And her anger as she joined Big Apple Wrestling Federation where she was at first just a ring girl, but it wasnt long before she was inside the ring, and not long after that when she Captured the Women's championship. The promotion has since closed down, leaving Jaden a free agent
