Stoner (wrestler profile)

28 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Stoner Name: Stoner Nickname The Twisted Thriller Height 6'05" Weight 275 lbs Hometown Texas City, TX Manager N/A Alignment Heel Physical Appearance: Blonde hair cut into a mohawk, although he keeps it brushed down to the sides. A pair of black leather pants with various blue and silver designs.
Gimmick: A pot Head who likes to wrestle. He is now on Parole and must be kept under survailence by Bill Sampson.
Wrestler's History: Started his career at the ag of sixteen when he got to see a local wrestling group. Got a gig with JWF and after a backstage conflict of interests, left with a few other members to join the BLWA. Has had success and failuer along the way, Stoner is ever vigilant in his desire to make wrestling into the sport he believes it can be. In the process, he earned himself a speeding ticket, which gained him a sidekick in the form of William H. Sampson III, his Parole Officer.
Top 15 moves: Dropkick Suicide Dive Hero Neckbreaker Hero Clothesline Head Scissors Takedown Elbow Strikes Muay Thai Grapple Drop Toe Hold DDT Snap Suplex "Chain Wrestling" Jumping Elbow Strike Arm Drag Arm Bar Leg Drop Signature Move(s) "Colon Close Parenthesis"- Tosses the opponent in the air for a flapjack, brings them down by pulling hard on the left arm and falling to the mat with them. Once they land, Stoner locks in a Crippler Crossface, opting instead to grab the mouth of the opponent and stretching it out while pulling back on the neck and stretching the shoulder with his legs.
"Expletive Deleted"- Moonsault into Leg Drop "Gravity Bong"- Holds the opponent on his shoulder in a Fireman's Carry position, then throws the opponent in the air as if about to perform an FU. He then turns and catches the opponent slamming them to the mat "Jackhammer" style. Basically, FU into jackhammer.
"Dropkick of Doom"- Top Rope Dropkick to the Knee "The Knee"- Muay Thai Knee Strike to the Chest/Head area "The Knee 2.0"- Shining Wizard "Rolling Stones"- Rolling Thunder "Wolf Fang Fist"- The quick jabs with his right hand, before going for a fourth he yells the name of the move, then instead of punching the person, he hits them in the jaw (quite strongly I might add) with his elbow.
"Kamehameha"- Michinoku Driver Finisher: "The Roachclip"- Vertabreaker "The Bong Hit"- Triangle Choke Theme Song: Shadows Fall- "The Light that Blinds" Entrance: The opening notes of "The Light that Blinds" begin to pour out through the loud speaker as a mediums dense fog descends on the crowd. From the backstage curtain emerges Stoner, with a big ol' smile emblazoned on his most likely doob-ed up face. Bill Sampson walks through the curtain a few moments later, looking around at the crowd for potential threats to his Parolee. The two begin the trek to the ring, as they go down the ramp Stoner walks on the left side and Bill walks on the right, the two men clapping hands with the fans the whole way down. Stoner rolls in under the bottom rope, as Bill opts to take the stairs instead, stepping over the ropes to enter the ring itself.
