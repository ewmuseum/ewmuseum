A Test The scene opens to a very dark room with what appears to be an individual who is extremely groggy and not exactly sure where he is. The mans face looks up and you can tell it is Bobby Azula. He looks very tired and out of sorts. He is in his wrestling gear and is blinking wildly trying to get his composure, and figure out what is going on. The lights start painfully slowly kicking on. Bobby Azula can see only what is in his general area. He finally gets his bearings back and turns to his left to see a TV with a DVD play connected to it. The TV comes alive with a pop. On the screen when it comes on is the masked man from the week before. You can tell he is smiling underneath his mask. His mask is red with black teardrops under the eyes and he is wearing a suit jacket, shirt and tie. All his articles of clothing are black.
“Mr. Azula time to wake up. You wouldn’t want to miss what is about to happen to you. Wakey wakey now.”
Bobby Azula looks over at the television and starts to mutter something when the masked man cuts him off.
“I really don’t want to hear anything you want to say right now. I just want you to listen. You know those stories from that precious bible of yours where the hero has to prove his faith. Like the three men and the really hot furnace. Oh and my favorite Daniel in the lion’s den. By now the light is slowly coming to be in this room and you are starting to see just how big this room is. Soon you will see that there is no doorknob on the door, and the door can only be opened from the outside. The really bad news is that I have put a rather unique lion in the room with you. He is a monster that has been wanting to hurt someone since she who shall remain nameless killed his brother. Hmmm I can just smell the fear in there. You will see very soon what I am talking about. Your challenge is to keep alive long enough for your faith in your God to kick in and you can be saved. Or maybe the faith you have in the goodness of people. Maybe you would like someone to not take advantage of a situation like you did last week, and just let you get the hell beat out of you.”
Bobby Azula looks around the room and notices it isn’t a very big room. The lights kick on by the door and he sees that it appears to be true that you can’t leave the room from the inside. He turns and looks in the corner opposite him and there he sees Mad Kato strapped in a straight jacket, and looking very angry. He looks at Mad Kato’s chest and notices that there is a timer on the locks of the straight jacket. He can tell he has two minutes before Mad Kato gets lose, and more than likely tries to kill him.
Bobby Azula quickly looks at the television in awe and shock, and then starts to silently pray to himself.
“Pray you poor weakling. I am going to test your metal this week. If you pass, and the other part of this test comes through. I will allow you a chance to prove yourself to me, and maybe even get a shot at some gold around here. We both know that is what you want, maybe even more than your faith to be proven right. But you are only part of my game. The other part should be learning about their spot in the game in just a few seconds. If she can make it to you in enough time you will be able to compete in your match tonight. If she can’t well you will have to be eating out of a tube more than likely at the hospital for a while. I think you have about a minute left. Are you sweating yet? Are you afraid? Can you make yourself stand up to a beast and only cling to your faith that you can come out on top? We shall see.”
The masked man starts laughing an evil and maniacal laugh. Then the screen changes to where you can see the inside of Akemi’s dressing room. She has just walked in to start getting ready when she sees a video camera lying on her counter top. She walks over to the camera and notices the note that says press play. She thinks for a moment and then hits play on the camera.
Static clicks on for a few minutes and then the familiar masked man appears on the screen. “Akemi sweet Akemi. I have something that is very dear to you. This something is a mountain of a man for such a little woman as you. But I know he holds a big place in your heart. But he is about to do something really nasty to someone and then suffer the repercussions for his actions. You might see him again in 5 to 10 years. If you want to keep that from happening I want you to show me that you aren’t afraid of pain any more, and that you will do whatever it takes for a friend, and to save someone. Because you truly wish to be good don’t you. I will give you one clue it is downstairs, and be prepared to hurt, a very searing hurt.”
The masked man stops talking and starts laughing his maniacal laugh. Akemi darts out the door and you can hear her as she runs down the hall and down a flight of stairs.
The television changes back to the masked man and he is enjoying himself very much. “OOOH I wish I had me some popcorn. The fear and the drama is so much better than going to a movie. By the way Azula you now have 10 seconds. I hope you can take some of his punches for a bit.”
Azula turns and sees the time click to zero and watches with some fear and awe as the straight jackets locks come undone, and Mad Kato just rips the jacket to shreds. Then grins at Azula as he approaches. Azula backs up into the far corner this time praying out loud. Mad Kato stalks him silently but slowly.
Outside the door there is a loud banging sound and then a scream of unadulterated pain as the door comes flying open. Akemi walks in holding her hand that is starting to blister some. She is grimacing as she calls Mad Kato to her. Mad Kato stops in his tracks and walks over to her.
“Bravo, bravo. The hero saved the day. Akemi meet Azula. Bobby Azula meet Akemi. You both have need of each other, and you both have needs that only I can fulfill. So this was a test to see what you would both do in a dangerous and stressful situation. Akemi predictably would have tried to save Mad Kato from a prison term even at the cost of a searing pain that could cost her, her match tonight. Azula didn’t stop trusting in his faith in a moment. Sort of like making up for what you did, and didn’t do last week. So I propose this as a way for you both to start paying off your debt to me. You will team up, and face a team of all stars next week. If you win, I will help you get a shot at the tag team gold. You lose, and I will end both of your careers. If you say now then you will also see your careers ended. Because there are always people willing to do anything to get the deepest darkest desires of their heart. Have a nice day.”
The television goes blank leaving Akemi silently cussing to herself, and Bobby Azula looking very worried. Mad Kato picks up Akemi and starts to walk away from Azula at the same time giving Bobby a very nasty look. Bobby Azula follows them at a distance and heads back up to the arena.
