Kid Styles (wrestler profile)

28 November 2007

Kid Styles Name: Kid Styles Nickname The K.I.D Height 5'6 Weight 168 lbs Hometown Orlando, Florida Manager None Alignment Face Physical Appearance: Hair Color: Light Blue with purple tips Hair Length: Near medium length, hair sometimes hangs over his eyes. Eye Color: Blue Ring Attire: Ring Attire: Once dawning the colors of white and purple similar to the colors of Section 8, a group he had been in for a long time. Kid now dresses in white and blue, similar to these pants http://www.eztights.com/highimpact.jpg with the letters 'K.I.D' on the left side and a image of a dragon on the right side. The T-shirt he wears is black and has the image of a dragon on the back and on the front the words "K.I.D - Living Life To The Fullest" is shown. The wrist bands he wears are blue with white trimming and the boots he wears are solid white.
Outside Attire: Casual clothing, cackey pants, T-shirts; Nike or something to do with anime, Nike shoes and a nike hat which often matches his clothing in some way
Gimmick: Often quiet and shy around certain people, Kid Styles has been known by his fellow wrestlers in the past to cut promo's in places that many of these guys who try to act tough would be embarrassed to even be seen in. His attitude can be described as that of a young Teenager. His motto has always been to 'Life life to it's fullest'. Meaning he loves to have fun and even do things that many would consider childish. He also has a martial arts background and sometimes uses that when competing in the ring.
Despite having a childish attitude he does have a bite that is worse than his bark if pushed to that extent and will push his body to the limit when in the ring to give the fan's and television viewers a heck of a match and hopefully a memorable one as well. He had always been the guy that wanted to earn what he accomplished, never the one to get involved in the politics because in his words; "If you have the heart, you can accomplish anything, it's better to accomplish what you strive for rather than have it handed to you"
Wrestler's History: KWA: Began the debute of Kid Styles, he remained with the promotion for 6 months, even capturing the KWA Grand Prix title which was basically a crusierweight championship.
SCW: Was the personal favorite promotion of Kid Styles, he made his debute there after leaving KWA. He remained with the promotion up until it closed it's doors, he even managed to capture the protions top title, the SCW Cup Championship. During his time there he only suffered one loss and that was when he lost the championship near the end of the promotion. The SCW was the only federation he hated to see close. He managed to earn the respect of his fellow peers in SCW, an accomplishment that was bigger than any title in his eyes.
XWA: The shortest federation to ever come and go, he was there during the opening and there during the closing of the federation which only lasted about four months but during that four months he managed to capture Tag gold, Crusierweight gold and the top title itself.
TKOW/HPWA: When SCW Closed down, TKOW Moved and was taken over by a new fed head, Kid lasted there for about a year and managed to win a Prince of Hell invitational. His career there was very rocky and he never even came close to achieving the success that he had in SCW.
Hardkore World: 1 month. Nuff said. Top 15 moves: Pile driver snap suplex 360 off top rope diving elbow drop Bulldog Russian Leg Sweep Baseball Slide Hurricanrana Moonsault DDT Reverse Neck breaker West Coast Pop Corkscrew (Over the top rope onto opponent below) Crossface chicken wing Running clothesline Signature Move(s) One Leg boston crab Frog Splash Corkscrew moonsault Finisher: 1. (For smaller people) "Playtime's Over" Description - Petey Williams move, the canadian destroyer which is a flip piledriver
2. (For basically anyone but mostly the bigger guys) "School's Out" Description - 619 into a one leg boston crab, what happens is, Kid Styles will perform the 619 and as the opponent flips over he'll quickly grab the leg and pull back, thus performing the one leg boston crab.
Theme Song: "Behind the mask" by Anarchy Club Entrance: The lights in the arena go dim as Behind the mask by Anarchy Club begins to play to signal the arrival of Kid Styles. Soon after he emerges from behind the curtains and walks out onto the stage area. He stops at the top of the ramp and looks out at the fans throughout the arena. His lips curve into a smile and he lifts his right arm up with all fingers closed into a fist but his thumb. He gives the fans a thumbs up then makes his way down the aisle and toward the ring.
Along the way he tags the hands of those with outstretched arms, his smile never fading. Once he arrives to the ring, he slides in under the bottom rope and climbs to his feet. He then makes his way over to the corner of the ring and climbs the turnbuckle, once he reaches the middle turnbuckle he looks out to the fans on that side then gives them a thumbs up and lifts his hand into the air to give God above a thumbs up. Kid then hops off the turnbuckle then turns around and leans against the turnbuckles, waiting for the match to begin.
