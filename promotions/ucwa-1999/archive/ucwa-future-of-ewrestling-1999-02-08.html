UCWA: The Future of E-Wrestling

8 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
======================================================= U.C.W.A. Ultimate Championship Wrestling Alliance "The Future of Professional Wrestling" ======================================================= Welcome to the Ultimate Championship Wrestling Alliance! This is a very promising online roleplay wrestling federation run by Co-Presidents [name withheld]
[[detail withheld]] and [[detail withheld]] In this package, we explain indepth just exactly what our federation is about, as well as how to join up in the ranks of the Ultimate Championship Wrestling Alliance. Now then, onto quality roleplaying.
A lot of people do not know what a quality roleplay is. Some people think that if you make one long, it is better. Wrong. Some people think it is better if they are around five lines. Wrong. I want all flashes to be atleast 20 lines.
Also, it really helps if you spellcheck your flashes. If you have a one hundred line flash that it makes no sense, you repeat yourself, and there are a lot of mispelled words, that is not a good flash. Be original, be daring!
Don't be afraid to piss people off. Now then, to be considered active in the federation, you must send at least one flash a week. Although, if you want title shots and want to move up in popularity divisions, you should flash about once every two days. There is no real limit on the daily number of roleplays, however don't over do it. If you have a title match, whether you are the champion or not, your flashes should reflect those of your competitor! The least you should do is one every two days, if your competitor is sending out great flashes everyday and you are only sending out a flash once every two or three days, don't expect to win! And don't complain when you lose!
This is my rule, if you type in all caps I will beat you silly. It hurts my eyes and it makes it hard to read. Also, please do flashes in normal colors. By that, I mean something readable. Please no blue backgrounds and red text... or yellow background and bright green text. The only background colors I would like for you to use are black, white, dark purple, grey, navy, teal, and dark green. As long as you use one of those as your background, I don't really care what text color you use. As long as I can read it clearly. I read a lot of this stuff at around 8:00 a.m., so my eyes are already hurting from staying open that long. Please do not make it worse with all the bright backgrounds [the backgrounds are really what hurts my eyes, not the text].
Here in the Ultimate Championship Wrestling Alliance we are hoping we can get the most talent possible. We want to see if you can be the best you can, and take the UCWA to a level which very few federations reach. This is an original-wrestler federation only. This means we only accept fake wrestlers, because now on America Online, the majority of the people are original wrestlers. I have had my share of real federations, but fake federations are what makes e-wrestling popular. We allow one wrestler per person.
In the Ultimate Championship Wrestling Alliance I give you full freedom of your angle. Although if I think that your character can be big, but you have a gimmick that is struggling I will take you aside and make suggestions on a new gimmick for your wrestler. You do not have to agree with me. I will not fire you if you don't like it. I am just here to help you out in any way possible.
If you do come up with a new angle, please e-mail me and get my approval before going ahead with it. If you don't follow that procedure, I will suspend you.
As things stand right now, we will be one main card every week... with the possibility of a additional card every week as well. In the future this may change, but I intend to keep what social life I have intact. I may be bringing on extra writers in the near future so that this can change! [Note: all cards are written out in play by play format unless specified]. If you have experience in writing please send me an example of your card writing with the words "Resume - Writing Staff" to [detail withheld] or [detail withheld].
The main weekly card in the Ultimate Championship Wrestling Alliance is "Saturday Night Slash" and is broadcast on Saturday Nights. This card is always a live [that is, in play by play format] broadcast from all over the country, and maybe even another part of the World. It comes out to the Members at around
12:00 a.m. EST. With usually a total of five to seven matches every week. There may also be an interview.
Our Pay Per View events are second to none! All major feuds and angles come to an end on this night.
[----------------] Pay [----------------] Per [----------------] Views [----------------] Here The Co-Presidents reserve the right to require that a champion defend their title on any given show! A real champion will defend their title regularly, and the President will not have to enforce this rule. Anyway, here is the list of Championships available in the Ultimate Championship Wrestling Alliance...
World Heavyweight Championship: The most prestigious Championship belt. Is defended Once a month atleast. If the Champion wants to defend more, that's fine. Is defended at all Ultimate Championship Wrestling Alliance
Pay-Per-Views, unless decided differently. Intercontential Heavyweight Championship: Holder of this Championship is the Number One contender for the Ultimate Championship Wrestling Alliance Heavyweight Title. Is defended atleast 2 times a month, or more if the Champion so chooses.... Always defended at Pay-Per-Views, unless decided differently.
Extreme Heavyweight Championship: Holder of this Championship is the Number One contender for the Ultimate Championship Wrestling Alliance Intercontinental Title. Is defended atleast 2 times a month, or more if the Champion so chooses.... Always defended at Pay-Per-Views, unless decided differently.
Light Heavyweight Championship: This person is first in line for a shot at the Ultimate Championship Wrestling Alliance Canadian Title. This belt is defended at Sunday Night Revenge, as well as all the Pay-Per-Views, unless decided differently.
Tag Team Heavyweight Championship: The most prestigious Championship belt in Tag Team wrestling. Is defended Once a month atleast. If the Champion wants to defend more, that's fine. Is defended at all Ultimate Championship Wrestling
Alliance Pay-Per-Views, unless decided differently. Now, let me tell you why you should join up with the Ultimate Championship
Wrestling Alliance. We are not an "extreme" federation. Although there are going to be matches such as No Disqualification, or Pinfalls Count Anywhere, ands we will have matches where chairs, tables, and stuff like that are used, although these are not every-card kinda things. If you see it in the World
Wrestling Federation or in World Championship Wrestling, then you can see it here. Lets make e-wrestling as much like the sport of wrestling we like, and not worrying about killing each other. I will tell you right now, the way I have the Ultimate Championship Wrestling Alliance set up, it will be a great federation and one well talked about if you do what you need to do as a roleplayer.
Many thanks to you, the wrestler for the interest in the Ultimate Championship
Wrestling Alliance. Please take the time to read through the Information and inform me of any suggestions you may have! Please, only original wrestlers are allowed. Thank you for you time...
