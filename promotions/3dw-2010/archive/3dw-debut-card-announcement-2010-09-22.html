3DW — Debut Card Announcement

Debut card announcement and primer

22 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NOTE: You COULD join one of those feds that have been putting out garbage for 10 years. You COULD watch your characters turned into caricatures. Or you could read this bomb ass card announcement, write to 3DWrestling-subscribe@yahoogroups.com and get in on the ground floor of the best new thing in e-w. Your choice. [Black.] [Thundering guitar riff from Motorhead's "Shoot You in the Back" blares.] [Lemmy Kilmister growls...] # Western movies # [The 3DW block logo in purple and gold breaks in half as images fly from it.] [Menagerie du Diablerie clowning around, throwing a flaming wig at the screen as Sara Mouche whips them.] # The riders ride # # Into the night # ["Deathdealer" Tommy Ganz wrenching on a Dragon Sleeper as his opponent is in surfboard position in the Inverted Kabel Naria.] # Into the west # # To see who's gun's the best # [Rob Nolan twisting his neck as he walks to the ring, cracking his knuckles.] # They're all fools # # To live by rules # [Na Neun Park hunched over while sitting on a bench in front of a locker, eyes focused on the ground.] # The rider wearing black # # You know he's gonna # # Shoot you in the back # ["The Immaculate Conception" Sebastian Madison looking at himself in a mirror, his long blonde hair whipping around as he moves.] # The horseman turns # # The wound that burns # ["The New Legend" Kenneth Morlock hitting a superkick and a backflip in what seems to be one motion.] # The awful pain # # The crimson rain # [The Disciple being ushered to the ring by Joseph Savior, both men glaring ahead with total focus.] # Got realize # # Before he dies # [Loco Fausto sloppily laying in punches as his opponent crumbles.] # The rider wearing black # # You know he's gonna # # Shoot you in the back # [Randal Bishop snapping a DDT and sitting up, shaking his head.] # It's suicide # # To live on pride # ["Shadow Stalker" Chance Holiday drops an opponent onto a chair with a drop toe hold, then immediately repeatedly slams his face onto the chair.] # You claim you own # # Your skin your bone # [Jaime Roberts shouting at the crowd as he jogs to the ring, a smirk on his face.] # Your own life # # Cuts you like a knife # ["The Street Samurai" Spade hits a running WildKard DDT then kips up to his feet and shrugs as the crowd pops.] # The rider wearing black # # He's gonna, he's gonna # # Shoot you in the back # [Larry Gionet nailing an opponent with Darkness Falls, an inverted tombstone piledriver.] # In the western movies # ["The Celtic Crippler" Caleb Foley hitting Irish Eyes Are Smiling, a 450 splash from the top rope, his red hair flying.] [Fade to the 3DW logo with the riff playing lightly in the background.] [...] [The camera cuts to a youthful man with short blonde hair. He wears a wifebeater and black jeans. He'd be good looking, except for the scars on his forehead. He speaks in a high tenor.] Man: Welcome to 3D WRESTLING! I am your host, Brandon Spears. Formerly known as Jeremy Locke, Flash Andrews, The Hardcore Pop Star... and whatever other bullshit gimmicks [name withheld] has thrown me into ov-. [Brandon stops mid-sentence and holds his finger to his ear.] BS: What? Not supposed to mention him? Promised the fans [name withheld] wouldn't be mentioned? [Brandon smirks.] BS: Well, fuck! There went that! Two lines in! [Brandon looks back at the camera.] BS: Look, the man behind the curtain ain't showing up. This isn't about him, or me. This is about giving you all the wrestling you've wanted to see. No commissioner, president, board member, CEO angles. No sports entertainment. No old-style grappling. We've got fifteen guys who are ready to do whatever it takes to make a name for themselves. Fifteen guys who are tired of getting shit on by the establishment! To start their own way. This is their promotion! This is their show! 3DW is the vehicle, but this is a product of what they come up with for your viewing pleasure. [Brandon chuckles.] BS: But we do have some... er... surprises that we as a promotion bring to the party. The disqualification rules are, well, not really used. The rules violation is going to have to be completely out of line for a disqualification to be used. Like if someone brings a fucking gun into the ring, he might get disqualified. The countout rule is simple. There is none. Countouts are for pussies. Here's how you win. Pin. Submission. Knockout. If a guy can't get up, and the referee feels his life is in danger, the ref can stop the bout. But they'll be reluctant to do so. And we have this... [A picture of an absolutely enormous masked man appears. He looks to be about seven feet tall and is chiseled. He wears all black from head to toe with a black mask.] BS: This is Project X. You won't see him much. We have our own security team, but this is the head of security. [The camera shows Project X lifting up two men, one in each hand, in a chokehold.] BS: If shit gets out of hand, PX will take care of it. And you don't want him to take care of it. [The camera shows Project X throwing a wrestler about ten rows deep into the crowd.] BS: If someone doesn't... uh... perform up to the standards of 3DW, Project X will take offense and deal with it in his own way. [The camera cuts back to Brandon.] BS: Trust me, not good. When the lights go out and "The Thing That Should Not Be" hits... run. Run fast. [The camera cuts back to Brandon.] BS: And I, Brandon Spears, will be your commentator. Your only commentator. I have to fill two hours weekly by myself. [Brandon looks off camera.] BS: Great fucking idea... [Brandon smirks at the camera.] BS: But again, none of that is the point. The wrestlers didn't come from literally all around the world to settle on Sacramento and the southwest United States to hear me talk anymore. So without further delay... here is the first card lineup, set for the Sacramento Memorial Auditorium on October 6th... [A graphic pops up on the screen: An athletically-built Korean man dressed as a pop star with the name Na Neun Park below it. Next to a shrugging, dark haired, athletically built man with the name "The Street Samurai" Shade below it.] NA NEUN PARK VS. "THE STREET SAMURAI" SHADE BS: The first match in 3DW, the return after 10 years of DWF-style action, will be two men who look to overcome past obstacles. Na Neun Park was a successful businessman and mega-star in Korean circles. He was saddled with the hopes and dreams of his people and watched it all collapse under him. Now he's looking for redemption in the most physical way possible... "The Street Samurai" Shade is one of the most exciting, determined men in the game. But everywhere he's been, he's found himself relegated to the bottom of the card. Because the guys at the top know that if he gets his shot, they won't be at the top anymore. This isn't the curtain jerker match. 3DW believes in showcasing top talent in every show opener, and this is no different. It's the tone- setter for the entire show, hell, the entire federation! [A graphic shows an athletically-built older man with a scar over his left eyebrow and Larry Gionet below it. Next to him, a man with an attractive face that's showing some age and the name Rob Nolan below it. On the other side of the graphic is a lean man with a baby face and the name "Deathdealer" Tommy Ganz below it. Next to him is a man with a clenched fist and a stern look and the name "The New Legend" Kenneth Morlock under it.] LARRY GIONET & ROB NOLAN VS. "DEATHDEALER" TOMMY GANZ & "THE NEW LEGEND" KENNETH MORLOCK BS: We at 3DW are going to make some waves with our insane wrestling, with our sometimes warped approach to brawling... but this match is going to highlight the other side of the coin. Four physical men who know how to get in a ring and fuck some people up old-school style. Larry Gionet is a veteran of the mat wars. If there's a town that's hosted a wrestling show, he's been in that town wrestling. Everywhere around the world. Rob Nolan used to be a pretty boy. One of those wrestlers we really have no room for in this place except to shit on. But now he's coming back after a horrible accident and two years away to show he's still got it. "Deathdealer" Tommy Ganz breaks people for a living. He's from Boston, so you know he's got a chip on his shoulder. And what he lacks in size he makes up for in focus. "The New Legend" Kenneth Morlock has been up and down the game. He's known for his athleticism, and his ability to get out of any situation. The winners of this match will be able to say that they are the cream of the crop of the pure wrestlers in 3DW. [Graphic: A short, flabby man with greasy hair that covers his face, barely showing black eyes with the name Loco Fausto. Next to him, a perfect athletic specimen with long blonde hair and tan skin with the name "Immaculate Conception" Sebastian Madison. Next to him a good looking man whose face has aged since the last time we saw him, with the name Jaime Roberts under him.] LOCO FAUSTO VS. "IMMACULATE CONCEPTION" SEBASTIAN MADISON VS. JAIME ROBERTS BS: Now here's a clash of styles... Loco Fausto has already gotten a name for himself. People who have seen him, the few who have, talk about how it looks like he hasn't been outside in years. And he's from Yuma, Arizona. Who's actually from Yuma? The guy has no soul, and he's just looking to get off by destroying people. Sebastian Madison has no designs on being destroyed. He thinks he's the greatest wrestler... no... the greatest MAN ever created. He's got the looks, he's got the pedigree, and he can do it all. Fly, wrestle, make you tap out. He's a prick, but you'll want to watch him. Jaime Roberts. The Sex Pistol. One of the most brazen guys in the history of wrestling, and hated the world over. The rumor is that he's toned down his ways and he's trying to reform. But we'll see about that. Three totally opposing ways of seeing the world. And themselves. [Graphic: One half shows one rotund man in a dark orange jumpsuit, a red afro, and clown makeup... a small man with horns of hair rising from his head and covering his face and a wool sweater... a hugely buff man in a fake mustache and a gray jumpsuit, and a hot woman with a bullwhip... the names Marime the Mischevious and Zyppo D. Klown - Menagerie du Diablerie appear under the chaotic picture. On the other side is the largest man we've seen yet, dressed in a leather vest with a white-shirted small man behind him and the name The Disciple below. Next to him is a picture of a well-built man with a handlebar mustache, a mean looking scar leading to what appears to be the lower half of the ear in view missing, and under the picture is the name Randal Bishop.] MENAGERIE DU DIABLERIE (Marime the Mischievious and Zyppo D. Klown) VS. THE DISCIPLE & RANDAL BISHOP BS: So who hasn't heard about the clowns by now? The Menagerie isn't here to amuse you. They're here to scare the shit out of you. If you didn't have a fear of clowns before, you will now. They have a hot chick and a strongman coming to the ring with them, and they just don't give a fuck about anything but amusing themselves through pain. The Disciple is led by Joseph Savior, the former El Savaje. He is here to do the bidding of Savior, who thinks we're all a bunch of fucked up crackheads and can't believe 3DW is promoting the kind of insanity that we're out here promoting. I say fuck him. Even if he is right sometimes. And Randal Bishop... I tried to talk to this guy the other day and I couldn't figure him out. He just doesn't give anything away. And he kept making me call him Mister. And he quoted the Bible, but I don't know if he knew what he was saying. You know, I have no idea how this one will end up. But it'll be physical and violent. That much is sure. [Graphic: MAIN EVENT! A toned man a big red beard, red hair, wild eyes and tattoos everywhere with the name "The Celtic Crippler" Caleb Foley. Next to him a picture of a muscular man with dark stringy hair covering eyes that just go into the distance with the name "Shadow Stalker" Chance Holiday under it.] "THE CELTIC CRIPPLER" CALEB FOLEY VS. "SHADOW STALKER" CHANCE HOLIDAY BS: Some of the guys in 3DW have a name. But none are bigger than the name Caleb Foley. It's a coup to have him here. Caleb Foley has earned his moniker through match of the year candidates around the world. He's one of the most popular wrestlers in the entire world right now because of his straight ahead style and his propensity to fuck people up. Chance Holiday? A lot of hype surrounds his debut, but not a lot is known about him. Someone in 3DW must think very highly of him to have him debut in the main event against the biggest star in the federation. [The camera shows Brandon Spears standing in front of the camera with .] BS: That's it! We're going to have a bunch of interviews a week from now on our Talk is Cheap show, then the following week, the DEBUT of 3DTV with yours truly calling the action all by himself! What a good idea! Not like it won't get redundant hearing my voice for two fucking hours! Thanks for being on board, and we'll see you in a week for Talk is Cheap! [Fade to the 3DW logo.]
