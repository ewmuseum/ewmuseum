DWA — Pay-Per-View

DWA Memorial Day Massacre! (Part 2 of 3)

9 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Garrett Montgomery: Welcome back to MEMORIAL DAY MASSACRE! Julie, your assessment thus far of the proceedings.
Julie Cooper: In one word- INTENSE! We’ve crowned two new champions already tonight, and we’re set to unify the tag team titles next! Let’s go to the ring!
Scott Meridian: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is for... excuse me,
I’ve just been told that there is some commotion in the locker rooms. Can we get the DWA Wildfeed Camera on the main screen?
The huge video screen in the Kingdome lights up with a slightly blurry image from the DWA Wildfeeds Camera. The cameraman wipes steam from the lens, and Lord Byron appears, clad only in a towel. He rushes past the cameraman, and is followed by Suna “Lovely to Touch” Gib, who cries, “I know what you’re up to, you bad boy!” Byron shrieks as he slips on the wet floor, and Suna pounces on him and begins slapping his face. Byron breaks free, and Suna grabs for him. As the feed fades we see an elated
Suna holding Byron’s towel as the nude Lord Byron streaks out of the locker room.
Julie: My, my- I don’t think that I’ve ever seen Lord Byron looking so... exposed! Garrett: Suna appears to have a plan for his royal jewels, that’s for sure! Scott, are you still there?
Scott: Yes, Garrett, I’m just a little underwhelmed! Anyway, ladies and gentlemen, this next match is a unification match for the DWA Internet
Tag Team Championship and the DWA World Tag Team Championship.
Introducing first, accompanied to the ring by their manager, the Godfather, at a combined weight of 530 pounds, here are the DWA Internet
Tag Team Champions- the Big Town Bully and the Hitman- 911!
911 enter the arena to a mixed reaction, but when the Godfather comes out the place erupts. The Godfather appears confounded by his popularity, and a wry smile works it’s way across his normally stoic face. He stops to kiss a baby as his team climbs into the ring.
Scott: And their opponents, accompanied by their manager, “Neon” Leon
Parker, weighing in at a total of 507 pounds, here are the DWA World Tag
Team Champions- “Acid” Andrew Downey and Alex “the Hammer” Simpson- PAIN
FACTORY! Parker leads the way as Pain Factory emerge from the locker room area. Downey and Simpson appear confident, and pay no attention to the jeers from the fans. As his team is entering the ring, Parker confronts the
Godfather, and after a few tense moments the two are separated by DWA officials. The referee calls for the bell and the match begins.
Downey takes down the Hitman with the flying head scissors, but the
Hitman regains his feet and drives Downey into the ropes. The Hitman attempts to choke Downey, but gets a knee in the stomach for his efforts.
Downey connects with a knee lift to the head that sends the Hitman sprawling. Downey tags in Simpson, and the two begin a double team effort on the
Hitman. The Bully tries to intervene, but the official escorts him from the ring as Downey and Simpson flail away on the Hitman. The referee escorts Downey to the apron, but the damage has been done. The Hitman staggers towards his corner, but Simpson catches him with a bulldog. He covers for the two count, then the Hitman kicks out.
As the Hitman rises, Simpson tags in Downey, who is perched on the top turnbuckle. Downey launches, but the Hitman catches him with a drop kick in mid air. The Hitman tags in the Bully, who drags Downey to his feet and slams him to the canvas. The Bully covers, but Downey kicks out after one. The Bully pulls Downey to his feet and slings him into the ropes, where he becomes entangled. The Hitman enters the ring, and he and the
Bully begin hammering Downey with elbows and knees.
Simpson leaps over the ropes, and nails 911 from behind with a double clothesline, sending them over the ropes. He frees Downey, then propels himself over the ropes, knocking the Bully to the floor. Downey follows and lands on the Hitman, and a brawl ensues. Downey rams the Hitman into the ring post, opening a gash above his eyes. Simpson powerbombs the
Bully onto the concrete floor, knocking him out cold. He joins Downey, and they execute a double suplex on the Hitman. Simpson pushes the Bully back into the ring as Downey climbs the turnbuckles. The Bully rises and is met by a bone crushing moonsault from Andrew Downey. He covers the
Bully for the three count.
Scott: Your winners, and DWA Unified Tag Team Champions- PAIN FACTORY! Garrett: What a match! These two teams are without a doubt the best in the DWA! Julie: Pain Factory may just be the best tag team in WRESTLING! Just ask
911, once they recover!
Garrett: Speaking of recovering, we’ll find out next if the Serial KiLLa has recovered from that 6 man battle royal and can defend his title against the biggest man in the DWA! Let’s go to the ring for the Internet
Championship bout!
Scott: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is for the DWA Internet Championship belt! Introducing first, the challenger. Accompanied to the ring by his manager, Hurly Burly, from the Windy City, standing 7’ 2” tall and weighing over 555 pounds, please welcome EDDY GONZALEZ!
The huge man steps into the arena to polite applause. He waves to the crowd as he climbs into the ring, and bounces his mighty bulk from rope to rope.
Scott: And his opponent, from San Antonio, Texas, weighing 298 pounds and standing 6’ 9” tall, the current DWA Internet Champion- THE SERIAL KILLA!
The crowd goes wild for the enormously popular KiLLa as he races down the aisle. He flips into the ring, and climbs the turnbuckles, raising the
Internet belt high above his head. The crowd explodes again, and after the applause subsides the referee calls for the bell.
The Serial KiLLa and Eddy Gonzalez square off, each anticipating the others first move. Gonzalez charges, and the KiLLa takes him down with a leg whip. The KiLLa tries to work on his ankle, but Gonzalez kicks free.
Gonzalez slings the KiLLa into the ropes, but misses a clothesline on the rebound. The KiLLa take him down with a drop kick to the back of the head and rolls him over for the cover. Gonzalez kicks out at two.
The KiLLa rebounds off the ropes and goes for the cross body block, but
Gonzalez catches him in mid air. The KiLLa struggles as Gonzalez drops him with a back breaker. Gonzalez climbs the turnbuckles and the KiLLa tries to catch his breath. Gonzalez launches from the top, but misses his splash as the KiLLa rolls aside. The ring collapses as Gonzalez hits, and both men go tumbling into the crater. The referee leaps in after them, and a three count is heard. The referee climbs out of the pit, and reaches in to assist the Serial KiLLa up to what remains of the ring. He raises the KiLLa’s hand in victory.
Scott: Your winner, and still DWA Internet Champion- THE SERIAL KILLA! Julie: I’ve never seen a ring do that before! Garrett: It looks like an atomic bomb was dropped in the ring! They’re calling for a stretcher for Gonzalez.
Julie: It better be a big stretcher! And what are they going to do about the ring? We’ve still got one more match to go!
Garrett: Scott Meridian is on his way back to the locker room to meet with DWA president Shelton Colby. We’ll have his comments after this brief intermission.
