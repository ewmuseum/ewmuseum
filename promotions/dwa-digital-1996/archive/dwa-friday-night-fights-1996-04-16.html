Friday Night Fights! (4/16)

16 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Digital Wrestling Alliance presents FRIDAY NIGHT FIGHTS! Taped at the Dallas Sportatorium, home of the DWA! Garrett Montgomery: Welcome to another edition of Friday Night Fights! Tonight we'll see three more matches in the DWA Tag Team Championship
Tournament, along with several singles battles, including a no DQ grudge match between Misery and the Bounty Hunter. But first, some comments from
Class War taped during last week’s Friday Night Fights, and a word from
Misery.
-----Roll Tape: Class War----- [Class War -- Monster Lucas Masai and Kuwasi "Black Fist" Shakur -- walk though the dressing room. Friday Night Fights is on the monitor....]
"Garrett: Class War is out of control!" Kuwasi "Black Fist" Shakur: You've got that right! Thunder and Firewalker got what they deserved. Thunder and his punk partner and manager tried to jump us before our tag match because they knew Class War is the team to beat. You wanna go to war, we'll take you to war! You tragic slobs will get
yours come the tag tourney! For now, better be looking over your shoulder, because your cheesy-ass pre-match attack is gonna get payback on each of you.
Monster Lucas Masai: And Firewalker, you want to start something with
Class War, my man, and we'll finish it! Congrats to the *new* DWA
Internet Champ. Your Flavor of the Month status is up, Firewalker. And before you even start whining, don't blame WarHawk for the beating the
Doc got -- blame
yourself! Black Fist: And I'm not done with Damage. You got a lucky win, kid, and we'll meet down the line. To the tag teams, get ready for the tournament.
Class War will win. -----End Tape----- -----Roll Tape: Misery----- Dr. Insane: WELCOME TO THE CLINIC! MY GUEST THIS WEEK IS THE MAN THAT
WILL BRING A LOT OF SUFFERING TO A WHOLE LOT OF PEOPLE - MISERY!
Misery: BEFORE WE BEGIN I MUST READ THIS ANNOUNCEMENT FROM FIREWALKER ---I CHALLENGE THE WINNER OF THE INTERNET CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH TO A NO-DQ MATCH FOR THE TITLE! I WANT MY TITLE BACK. Now, it is my turn to speak,
Bounty Hunter, your little joke will not go unpunished and when I get my hands around your scrawny little neck I intend to inflict a lot of pain an your body. But before that, I have an appointment with Firewalker as we challenge some team known as Class War. They are just another stepping stone in our drive for the top. And for that fool who cost
Firewalker the title, I announce that I would like to challenge him in the future to a falls-count-anywhere match. He loves to run like a little schoolgirl so that type of match should be one that even he can agree to. We aren't going anywhere just yet!
Dr. Insane: THE CLINIC IS CLOSED! -----End Tape---- Garrett: Class War is STILL OUT OF CONTROL, but the good doctor and his team have plans to straighten them up! Let's go to the DWA Commando
Center for highlights of the match between the Nomads and Class War.
-----DWA Commando Center----- Class War vs. The Nomads (w/ Dr. Insane) The Nomads were shocked by their first round loss, as well as the attack on Dr. Insane. Class War drew first blood when Masai powerbombed
Firewalker on the steel steps next to the ring. Unfortunately for Masai,
Misery was the legal man in the ring. Misery flew from the top rope, catching Masai from behind and driving him face first into the barricade.
Shakur tried to assist his fallen partner, but Dr. Insane blocked his path. Misery dragged the woozy Masai into the ring for the pin.
Your winners- the Nomads 911 (w/ the Godfather) vs. The Burly Bunch (w/ Hurly Burly) The Burly Bunch became the first team eliminated from the tournament when the Hitman nailed Eddy Gonzalez over the head with a wooden axhandle while the Godfather distracted the official. Hurly Burly snatched the weapon away from the Hitman, but the damage was done as the huge Gonzalez dropped to the map. The Hitman covered for the pin as Hurly Burly wailed away at the Godfather's knees. DWA officials escorted Hurly Burly to the locker room as the Godfather was loaded on to a stretcher.
Your winners- 911 Pain Factory (w/ "Neon" Leon Parker) vs. Thunder and Lightning (w/ Tommy J. Dynomite) Thunder and Lightning followed the Burly Bunch out of the tournament following a stunning victory by Pain Factory. Thunder and Lightning attempted to double team "Acid" Andrew Downey, but Alex "the Hammer"
Simpson intervened, driving Thunder out of the ring as Downey dropped
Lightning with a tremendous suplex slam.
Your winners- Pain Factory ----- Garrett: Only four teams remain in the quest for the gold, but the two that have been eliminated still have a chance at a belt in the Internet
Tag Team Tournament starting next week. Speaking of Internet titles, let's go to the ring for the match between Dr. Joe Slasher, M.D. and the current Internet Champion, WarHawk!
Scott Meridian: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is for the DWA Internet
Championship belt! Introducing first, the challenger. From Norfolk,
Virginia, weighing in at 295 pounds and standing 6' 9" tall, this is DR.
JOE SLASHER, M.D! Slasher enters the arena slowly, and is greeted by an avalanche of disdain from the fans. He pulls a large, rusty syringe from his medical kit, and jabs it at the fans. A putrid yellow liquid squirts from the syringe, arcs through the air, and lands on a man wearing a white Vinnie t-shirt, ruining the shirt. The Doctor laughs hysterically as he enters the ring.
Scott Meridian: And his opponent, from Talequah, Oklahoma, standing 6' 3" tall and weighing 235 pounds, your DWA INTERNET CHAMPION----- WARHAWK! WarHawk rushes into the arena in full warpaint and vaults into the ring. The crowd goes wild as he slowly approaches the Doctor, waving a tomahawk. The referee steps between the two wrestlers, directs them to their corners, and calls for the bell.
WarHawk attacks with a vengeance, dropping the Doctor with a kick to the knees. WarHawk climbs to the top turnbuckle and executes a perfect backflip, nailing the Doctor in the face. WarHawk mounts the turnbuckles again, but the Doctor shakes the ropes and WarHawk plunges to the arena floor.
Slasher steps to the apron and drops a knee into the gut of WarHawk. Slasher grabs a chair, but Vinnie races from the locker room and snatches it away. Vinnie nails Slasher with the chair, and WarHawk tags the Doctor with a drop kick. As Slasher falls to the floor, the referee calls for the bell.
Scott Meridian: The referee has stopped the match and has disqualified
WarHawk. Your winner- DR. JOE SLASHER, M.D!
Vinnie climbs into the ring and grabs the microphone as the referee explains his decision to Slasher.
Vinnie: Slasher, I'm not through with you yet! I want you in the Texas
Death Match at April Anarchy! You and me, one on one, no way out...
except through the electric chair! GET UP, YOU PUNK, AND ACCEPT MY CHALLENGE! Slasher starts into the ring, but DWA officials restrain him. Vinnie drops the microphone and joins WarHawk at ringside. They shake hands and head to the locker room.
Garrett: Slasher wins by DQ, so the title remains with WarHawk. Will Dr. Joe accept Vinnie's challenge? I’ll let you know as soon as we find out. Let's go to the... STOP THE PRESSES! Here comes Apollo and Thantos Polopoulos! Apollo: I'm getting real annoyed with all the outside interference here in the DWA. In both of my fights there has been a sick amount of interference and I'm downright mad.
Polopoulos: That's right folks. We came here from Greece to wrestle, not to referee other people's disputes.
Apollo: Be forewarned! Anyone that interferes in any of my upcoming matches will be challenged to a steel cage match. There will be one hitch. The door will be padlocked so no one else gets in. The only way to win is to climb out. STAY OUT OF MY BUSINESS!!!!!!!
Garrett: Strong words from Apollo. DWA President Shelton Colby is looking into the interference problem, but so far no fines or penalties have been levied. I have heard that he's looking for an enforcer to oversee the matches, but there's been no word on who that person will be. Let's go back to the DWA Commando Center for the rest of tonight's results.
-----DWA Commando Center----- Monster Lucas Masai vs. The Serial KiLLa Masai was still upset over the loss to the Nomads, and the Serial KiLLa took advantage of the situation with a horrific choke slam into the turnbuckle. The Serial KiLLa got the win via submission with a scorpion deathlock.
Your winner- The Serial KiLLa Vinnie, the Original Jersey Boy vs. Coyote Coyote and Vinnie were on their way to a time limit draw, but Vinnie turned the tide with his Original Jersey Finisher, a reverse choke slam.
After the match, Vinnie extended his hand to Coyote, and assisted his opponent to the locker room.
Your winner- Vinnie Apollo (w/ Thantos Polopoulos) vs. Jason Andrews In yet another marathon match, Jason Andrews appeared to gain momentum with spinning back kick to Apollo's stomach. Apollo was dazed, but managed to reverse the pin into a small package.
Your winner- Apollo Misery (w/ Dr. Insane) vs. The Bounty Hunter The Bounty Hunter threw everything he had at Misery, including two chairs, a table, and ring rope. Misery was battered and bruised, but kept coming after the Bounty Hunter, and finally stopped him with a series of back suplexes on the arena floor.
Your winner- Misery ----- Garrett: Well, that does it for another edition of Friday Night Fights! I'll be back on Thursday for a preview of April Anarchy and the semifinal matches of the DWA Tag Team Tournament!
