Friday Night Fights! (6/9)

9 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Digital Wrestling Alliance presents FRIDAY NIGHT FIGHTS! LIVE from the Sportatorium in Dallas, Texas! Garrett Montgomery: Welcome back to FRIDAY NIGHT FIGHTS! I’m Garrett
Montgomery, and I’ll be bringing you the latest from the Digital
Wrestling Alliance. Joining my tonight, as always, is my lovely co-host,
Julie Cooper. Julie Cooper: Thanks, Garrett! Tonight we have another three title matches, including a first defense of the DWA Intercontinental
Championship belt. Vinnie, the Original jersey Boy, is really pumped for the chance to get a new championship belt!
Garrett: That’s right, Julie, but the current champ Lightning isn’t about to hand the belt over! Or is he? Rumors have been circulating all week about Lightning and the loss of his tag team partner Thunder. Thunder suffered a career threatening injury to his arm at Memorial day Massacre.
Julie: Their manager, Tommy J. Dynomite, announced this week that Thunder would be taking a leave of absence to recuperate and evaluate his commitment to wrestling. Lightning disappeared after the announcement.
Dynomite insists that he will appear for the championship match tonight, but as of now Lightning has not been seen.
Garrett: We’ve got two more title matches tonight, including this one. Let’s go to the ring for the DWA Internet Tag Team Championship bout between the Nomads and Pain Factory!
Scott Meridian: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is for the DWA Internet
Tag Team Championship! Introducing first, the challengers. Accompanied to the ring by their manager Dr. Insane, weighing a combined 635 pounds, here are FIREWALKER and MISERY- THE NOMADS!
The Nomads enter the arena to a standing ovation. They look determined and confident. Dr. Insane follows, frantically searching the crowd for a possible ambush from Class War.
Scott: And now please welcome the DWA World and Internet Tag Team Champions! Accompanied to the ring by their manager “Neon” Leon Parker, at a combined weight of 507 pounds, here are “ACID” ANDREW DOWNEY and
ALEX “THE HAMMER” SIMPSON- PAIN FACTORY!
Parker steps into the arena, but Pain Factory are nowhere to be seen. Parker is laughing as the Nomads glare at him. Pain Factory suddenly attack from behind the Nomads, dropping Firewalker and Misery with double drop kicks. The referee calls for the bell as the mayhem continues.
Firewalker tosses Simpson over the ropes and follows him to the floor. Misery and Downey square off in the ring. Misery drops Downey with a spinning back kick and goes for the cover. Downey kicks out at two, and gouges Misery’s eyes. He floors Misery with a snap suplex, but Misery bridges out.
Firewalker and Simpson are still exchanging blows on the arena floor as
Misery flips Downey with a flying clothesline. Downey backs into the corner and Misery charges, but Downey slips to the side and Misery hits the turnbuckle hard. Downey whips Misery into a neck breaker, and goes for the kill with a giant splash. Misery manages to get one leg over the ropes and the ref breaks the pin.
The managers finally separate Simpson and Firewalker, and both wrestlers are tagged in. Simpson charges into a huge right cross from Firewalker, then gets hit with a Rainmaker that sends him tumbling over the top rope.
Firewalker drags the stunned Simpson back into the ring, and tags Misery, who flies from the top turnbuckle and nails Simpson with a double ax handle. Misery goes for the pin, but Downey intervenes with a kick to the temple, stunning Misery.
Downey raises Misery into a powerplex as Simpson climbs the ropes for a moonsault. Simpson nails Misery as Downey powerplexes him. Misery struggles to his feet, but Simpson pins him before he can tag in
Firewalker. Scott: Your winners, and still DWA Internet Tag Team Champions- PAIN FACTORY! Garrett: Pain Factory looks invincible! Julie: Yes, but they haven’t met the new team that Shelton Colby is trying to sign! He has stated that he’ll be bringing some familiar faces back into the DWA, and if my sources are correct Pain Factory better be watching their backs!
Garrett: We’re sure to find out more at the press conference next Tuesday with Colby. While their clearing the ring for the DWA Intercontinental
Championship match, let’s go to the DWA Commando Center for an update of tonight’s early matches.
-----DWA Commando Center----- Apollo (w/ Thantos Polopolous) versus Monster Lucas Masai Apollo had this match well in hand until Masai nailed him with a set of brass knuckles while the official looked away. Apollo became enraged and snatched the knuckles from Masai. The referee saw the brass knuckles in
Apollo’s hand and disqualified him.
Your winner by disqualification- Monster Lucas Masai The Masked Ninja (w/ “Golden” Johney Pathos) versus Dr. Joe Slasher, MD Slasher was near defeat at the hands of the high flying newcomer, but salvaged a victory when the Masked Ninja fell right into a sleeper hold.
Slasher kicked the unconscious Ninja out of the ring, and tried to attack
Pathos. Your winner- Dr. Joe Slasher, MD Poseidon (w/ Thantos Polopolous) versus The Hitman (w/ the Godfather) These two wrestlers fought for nearly one hour before the Hitman finally stopped Poseidon with the Assassination, a top rope DDT. In an uncharacteristic gesture, the Hitman offered his hand to Poseidon and assisted him to his feet. The Godfather and Polopolous joined their wrestlers in the ring for a standing ovation from the crowd.
Your winner- The Hitman WarHawk versus Lord Byron (w/ King Benny II) Byron’s attention was on the court case against Colby, and WarHawk quickly caught him in a figure four, resulting in a submission from
Byron. WarHawk released the hold, and Byron attacked, piledriving him over the ropes and on to the concrete floor. The Nomads came out and chased Byron away from the shaken WarHawk.
Your winner by submission- WarHawk ----- Julie: WarHawk stunned Byron with the quick victory, but suffered Byron’s revenge after the match! Rules seem to mean absolutely nothing to Lord
Byron!
Garrett: That’s true, Julie. Hopefully this sham of a court case will backfire on him and teach him a lesson! And what do you make of the after match display of respect from the Godfather and the Hitman?
Julie: It’s obvious that the Hitman wants the tag belts back, and it appears that he and the Godfather are looking to gain allies in their battle to dethrone Pain Factory. Polopolous is a very ring savvy manager and would not join any alliances that could backfire on his stable. We’ll have to wait and see what surprises the coming weeks bring to the DWA!
Garrett: Our next match is set to begin- a battle for the DWA Intercontinental Championship belt between the Original Jersey Boy,
Vinnie, and the current champ Lightning! Let’s go to the ring!
Scott: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is for the DWA Intercontinental
Championship belt! Introducing first the challenger. Accompanied to the ring by his manager, “Golden” Johney Pathos, weighing in at 315 pounds and standing 6’ 11” tall, please welcome the pride of Basking Ridge, New
Jersey and the former DWA World Champion- the ORIGINAL JERSEY BOY,
VINNIE! The crowd goes wild as the most popular champion in DWA history bursts into the Sportatorium. Vinnie runs down the aisle, leaps into the ring, and climbs each corner to wave at the fans. Pathos takes up position on the apron.
Scott: And his opponent, accompanied to the ring by his manager, Tommy J. Dynomite, for Osaka, Japan, standing 6’ 0” tall and weighing 222 pounds, here is the DWA Intercontinental Champion- LIGHTNING!
Lightning enters the arena looking disheveled. As he slowly walks up the aisle, the crowd begins to rise and clap. His pace quickens as the response from the crowd turns into a roar. He raises the DWA
Intercontinental belt high above his head and the crowd explodes. Dynomite is surprised by the great response, and waves to the crowd. Vinnie and Lightning meet at center ring and shake hands. The referee calls for the bell and the match begins.
The two men lock up in the center of the ring, and Vinnie takes command with a chin lock. Lightning breaks free and slings Vinnie into the ropes, catching him on the rebound with a forearm smash. Vinnie rolls away from a leg drop, and pulls Lightning to his feet for a snap suplex. Vinnie tries the sleeper, but Lightning drags him to the ropes and the referee calls for the hold to be released.
Lightning drops Vinnie with a flying head scissors and goes for the pin, but Vinnie kicks out at two. Lightning drags Vinnie to his feet and introduces his head to the turnbuckle. Vinnie retaliates by slamming
Lightning’s head into the turnbuckle, then slingshots him across the ring into the opposite turnbuckle. Lightning hits hard, and Vinnie follows with a splash that shakes the ring.
Lightning is dazed as Vinnie drags him to the center of the ring. The crowd rises in anticipation as Vinnie lines up Lightning for the Original
Jersey Finisher, a face first choke slam. Lightning slips the hold, and nails Vinnie with a reverse atomic drop. Lightning whips the stunned
Vinnie into the ropes, but misses on a flying drop kick. Vinnie drops an elbow across Lightning’s windpipe and goes for the pin. Lightning kicks out at two and one half.
Lightning pops Vinnie with a series of spinning back kicks, then climbs the turnbuckle for the Lightning Strike. He moonsaults high into the air, but Vinnie catches him and drops him into a back breaker. Vinnie pulls
Lightning to his feet, and this time he nails the Original Jersey
Finisher. Vinnie covers for the three count. Scott: Your winner, and new DWA Intercontinental Champion, the Original
Jersey Boy- VINNIE!
Vinnie pulls Lightning to his feet, and raises his hand in the air. The crowd goes wild as the two champions shake hands, then Lightning calls for the ring microphone.
Lightning: I’d like to thank you all for your support, and I congratulate the new champion, Vinnie. He’s a great competitor deserving of the title.
I would also like to take this opportunity to announce my departure from the DWA. While my partner Thunder heals, I will return to my homeland and my family. There I will continue to train and wait, and if... WHEN
Thunder heals and is ready to return to the ring, I will be there! Thank you once again. Julie: Wow! I expected Lightning to make an announcement tonight, but I didn’t expect him to leave the DWA!
Garrett: He’s obviously upset over the injury to his tag team partner
Thunder. He’s a great competitor, and I fully expect him to return to the
DWA someday soon, with or without Thunder!
Julie: We wish him the best of luck. It looks like they’re removing the ring! What going on here- we’ve still got the DWA Internet Championship bout coming up?
Garrett: That’s right, Julie, but this will be a special stipulation match. Kuwasi Shakur requested it, and the Serial KiLLa agreed, and we’ll be having the first ever Octagon Shoot Match!
Julie: An Octagon Shoot Match! What are the rules? Will the title be on the line? Garrett: The Internet title will be on the line as it always is at Friday
Night Fights. The rules are simple- ANYTHING GOES! The two men will battle until one submits and taps out!
Julie: Someone could get hurt in there! Garrett: I think that’s what Shakur has in mind! Let’s go to the OCTAGON! Scott: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is the first ever DWA Octagon
Shoot Match! At stake- the DWA Internet belt! Introducing first, the challenger. From the South Bronx, New York, standing 6’ 9” tall and weighing 300 pounds, one half of the tag team Class War, here is KUWASI
“BLACK FIST” SHAKUR!
Shakur enters the Octagon to the chant of “shoot, Fist, shoot”. He punches his fist above his head and stalks the Octagon, awaiting the champion.
Scott: And his opponent, from San Antonio, Texas, weighing 298 pounds and standing 6’ 9” tall, the current DWA INTERNET CHAMPION- THE SERIAL KILLA!
The crowd goes wild as the popular champion enters the Octagon. The KiLLa carefully avoids Shakur as he makes his rounds of the Octagon. The referee calls both competitors to the center of the Octagon and explains the rules one last time. He calls for the bell and the shoot match begins.
The two wrestlers stalk each other, and after several feints they lock up. Shakur gets the KiLLa in a headlock and rams him into the chain link mesh. The Serial KiLLa breaks free with a kick to the abdomen, and takes
Shakur down with a leg sweep. He leaps on Shakur and begins hammering away to his head. The referee steps closer as a large gash opens on
Shaker’s forehead, but he does not submit!
Shakur pulls one arm free and knocks the KiLLa away with a roundhouse to the temple. Shakur tries to crawl away, but the KiLLa catches him with a kick in the gut, flipping him on his back. The KiLLa goes for a head stomp, but Shakur grabs his foot and takes him down with a toe hold.
Shakur drives one foot under the KiLLa’s chin and begins twisting his ankle. The KiLLa screams in pain but does not submit!
After several minutes of excruciating pain, the KiLLa manages to break free with a punch to the knee. Both men rise, and hobble around the ring, looking for the advantage. The Serial KiLLa charges, but Shakur trips him and he hits the chain link mesh face first. Shakur drives a knee into the back of the neck, forcing the KiLLa hard against the chain link. The
KiLLa twists free and tags Shakur with a right to the ribs.
The KiLLa get Shakur in a choke hold and pulls him to the canvas. Shakur begins to lose consciousness, and the referee steps in, breaking the hold.
Scott: The referee has stopped the bout! Your winner, and still DWA
Internet Champion, the Serial KiLLa!
Julie: Wait a second! I thought that the match could only be decided by submission! Garrett: The official must have seen something that we didn’t. He’s calling for the paramedics. Shakur is still down!
The paramedics arrive and strap an oxygen mask to Kuwasi Shakur. He rips the mask free and begins screaming at the official.
Shakur: I DIDN’T SUBMIT! I DIDN’T SUBMIT! Shakur continues yelling as DWA officials escort him from the Octagon. The crowd is stunned by the stoppage, and begins pelting the official with garbage. Garrett: I don’t think that this one’s over, folks! Shakur is still hot, but the KiLLa has the belt! He’ll face his next challenge next Friday night against Lord Byron.
Julie: And the DWA Internet tag team belts will be on the line in a rematch between Pain Factory and 911! See you next week!
