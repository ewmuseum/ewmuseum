DWA — Pay-Per-View

DWA April Anarchy! (Part 4 of 4)

23 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Garrett: Welcome back once again to APRIL ANARCHY! Crimson Skull: It truly has been anarchy tonight here at the Superdome! Is there any word on Vinnie or WarHawk? Garrett: According to the medics, Vinnie will be fine. He's being treated for lacerations at a nearby hospital. As for WarHawk, nobody knows anything! It's as if Venom removed him from the face of the earth!
Crimson Skull: Speaking of faces, we've got some truly ugly ones headed to the ring. Let's return to ringside for the DWA World Tag Team
Championship match! Scott Meridian: Ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls, this next match is for the vacant DWA World Tag Team Championship belt! Introducing first, accompanied to the ring by their manager "Neon" Leon Parker, here are
Alex "the Hammer" Simpson and "Acid" Andrew Downey- PAIN FACTORY!
Neon Leon leads his team to the ring, talking trash. Pain Factory oozes confidence, as if the outcome of the match was already determined.
Scott Meridian: And their opponents, escorted to ringside by the Godfather, here are the Hitman and the Big Town Bully- 911! The Hitman and Bully stroll down to ringside, followed by the Godfather. They look tired after the eight man battle royale. The Godfather approaches Leon, and a few heated words are exchanged before the officials can separate them. The bell rings and the match begins.
Downey and the Hitman trade face slaps, then begin brawling. Simpson tries to intervene, but the Bully tags him with a roundhouse right to the jaw. The Bully joins the Hitman in a double team on Downey as Simpson rolls to the apron. They miss on a face first slam and end up eating canvas. Downey and Simpson drag 911 to their feet and then drop them with dual DDTs.
Simpson drags the Hitman out of the ring and slams him on the concrete floor. Downey sets up the Bully for another DDT, but the Bully reverses it into a devastating back body drop. The Bully climbs to the top turnbuckle, and somersaults on to Downey. The Bully gets a two count before Simpson pulls him off of Downey.
The Hitman crawls back into the ring as Downey rises. A boot to the face staggers the Hitman, and Downey lifts him for the powerplex moonsault.
Simpson climbs to the top turnbuckle and launches into a moonsault, nailing the Hitman, who is powerplexed by Downey. Downey covers the
Hitman and gets the three count.
Scott: Your winners, and new DWA WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS- PAIN FACTORY! Crimson Skull: Pain Factory was incredible, although their teamwork might have bent the rules a bit!
Garrett: That's an understatement, my friend! Wait... this just in. DWA
President Shelton Colby has ordered the uninjured wrestlers to ringside for the DWA World Championship match. He has declared it a lumberjack match!
Crimson: And here they come! Let's go to ringside! Scott: Are you ready for the final match of the night- for the DWA WORLD
CHAMPIONSHIP BELT? President Colby has declared the next match a lumberjack match! Introducing first, accompanied to the ring by his manager Jon Robinson, from Toronto, standing 7' 2" tall and weighing 315 pounds, this is BANE!
Bane rushes down the aisle to a chorus of boos, and floors the lumberjacks Shakur and Masai before he enters the ring. Class War tries to follow, but get held back by the other lumberjacks.
Scott: And his opponent, here tonight with his manager Thantos Polopolous, from Greece, standing 6' 7" tall and weighing 315 pounds, this is APOLLO! Apollo enters the arena and brings the crowd to it's feet. His hands and arms are still stained with Vinnie's blood, which he wears like a badge of honor. The lumberjacks part and he climbs into the ring. The bell rings and the match begins.
Bane charges Apollo, who ducks as Bane flies over the ropes and into
Class War. Shakur and Masai soften him up with a few low blows, then roll
Bane back into the ring. Apollo drops Bane with an arm drag takedown and begins working on his arm.
Bane kicks out of the hold, and takes Apollo down by the hair. Apollo sweeps Bane's legs, and tags him with a chop to the throat. Bane retaliates by kicking Apollo out of the ring, where Dr. Joe Slasher pounces on him. Jason Andrews and Brian Damage pull Slasher off Apollo, and Coyote tosses him back into the ring.
Apollo and Bane square off again, trading blows until Apollo catches Bane with a spinning back kick. Bane is stunned, and Apollo lines him up for the Gyro. The twisting piledriver stuns Bane, but he kicks out at two.
Apollo nails him with the Gyro again, and again Bane kicks out at two. Apollo tries a third Gyro, but Bane hooks his legs around Apollo's neck and whips him with the neckbreaker.
Bane raises the stunned Apollo for the Death Drop, but Apollo reverses it and flips Bane out of the ring. As Bane rises from the arena floor,
Apollo nails him with an over the top splash, knocking him into 911. 911 assist Bane back into the ring as Apollo climbs the turnbuckle. Apollo nails Bane with a flying bulldog, knocking him out cold. Apollo covers
Bane for the three count.
Scott: Your winner, and new DWA WORLD CHAMPION- APOLLO! Garrett: What a battle! Either man could have won the belt! Crimson Skull: I'm really impressed with the DWA! APRIL ANARCHY was AWESOME! Garrett: Thanks for joining me once again. I'll see you next week at
