DWA — News

DWA Wildfeeds!

26 March 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Digital Wrestling Alliance presents DWA WILDFEEDS! News and unedited comments from the superstars of the DWA! ----- DR. JOE'S ON A ROLL!----- (Camera goes into the locker area where Dr. Joe Slasher is throwing chairs at walls:: Dr. Joe Slasher, M.D.: Vinnie??? Who cares about that rotten idiot!! Tonight I proved what a real Iron Man was all about when I went through that tourney like a freight train!!! How many matches did you wrestle tonight Vinnie?? That's right, only once!
(Gets closer to the camera) Dr. Joe: Don't worry because that title will come to the Doctor, and YOU VINNIE!!! (smiles) Dr. Joe: Will fall under the knife ONCE AND FOR ALL!!!!!!!!!!! HA, HA, HA, HA, HA, HA, HA, HA, HA, HA, HA!!! (shoves the cameraman against the wall and leaves) ----- DWA ANNOUNCES MORE SIGNINGS ----- The Digital Wrestling Alliance announced on Monday the signing of a wrestling superstar and a promising young tag team.
Managed by Jon Robinson, veteran champion Bane will enter the singles division. This monster of a man will make his appearance this Friday night against the popular Johnny "Sidewinder" Missle.
Thunder and Lightning will strike the DWA tag ranks this weekend. The newcomers will face Class War in the first tag match of the year. Both teams anticipate this tuneup for the DWA World Tag Championship tournament.
----- WARHAWK'S ON THE WARPATH! ----- (scene opens up on WarHawk pacing furiously.. blood mingling with blue and white warpaint on his face..)
WarHawk: Slasher....why? Are you not a good enough wrestler to pin me on my back? Guess not...apparently, you also are not good enough to be champion. Too bad. It is now coming out that you are not a true warrior by any means. I am challenging you to a "NO SUBMISSIONS" match on the next card. It is time for you to feel the pain of the TomaHawk backbreaker...you must be punished for your trickery and deceit...
WarHawk: Firewalker....it is good to see another real warrior in the DWA. It is unfortunate that I could not win the belt I was wrestling for, but
Vinnie is a good wrestler. Congratulations on your win and I wish you the best in your defenses. I will be watching your back....
WarHawk: Byron....you are most pathetic.... I thought you might be a warrior. You are nothing more than a whining woman who ate too much and got sick...maybe you should try fasting before matches like I do...your out of shape white body was no match for my conditioned hard native muscle...as always, a native is unstoppable one-on-one.. or as the case may be here (pointing to stitches in his left eye) two-on-one...if you want a rematch Lord and Thing, let me know...I will be glad to punish you again and prove to you that I am the wrestler and you are a talker...whatta ya say? Another round?
(WarHawk starts to walk off, then stops and turns...) Lord Byron....it must be rough to be defeated by two native warriors in the same night....again, congratulations Firewalker!
(scene fades as WarHawk gives out a warcry and walks into the evening...) ----- DWA Current Singles Rankings ----- DWA World Champion- Vinnie, the Original Jersey Boy (1-0) DWA Internet Champion- Firewalker (3-0) 1. Dr. Joe Slasher, M.D. (3-1) 2. WarHawk (2-1) 3. Lord Byron (2-2) 4. Coyote (1-1) The Serial KiLLa (1-1) 6. Johnny Missle (1-2) Misery (1-2) 8. Jason Andrews (0-1) Eddy Gonzalez (0-1) The Bounty Hunter (0-1) 11. Brian Damage (0-2) Not ranked- Apollo, Monster Lucas Masai, Kuwasi "Black Fist" Shakur,
Thunder, Lightning, Bane
----- BYRON ILL, OR FAKING? ----- (The scene opens in the dressing rooms during the final match of the tournament...the match between Vinnie and Dr. Joe can be heard outside, and the interview team are waiting outside Lord Byron's dressing room door. Crashing sounds can be heard from inside.)
Interviewer: Well, here we are outside an obviously disappointed Lord
Byron's dressing room, hopefully we will be able to get a few comments from the man on tonight's events...
(The interviewer knocks on the door. King Benny II answers.) King Benny: Go away. One is not giving any interviews at this time. ( King Benny puts his hand over the camera) Interviewer: Could you just give us a few words on your man's performance tonight..? King Benny: No comment. Please leave. ( The dressing room door is thrown open, and Lord Byron emerges, his fists bleeding and blood dripping from his injured jaw. He fixes the interviewer in a deathly stare.)
Lord Byron: Hold on your highness, I have a few words for these working class maggots... (King Benny hurries over to Byron and whispers something in his ear. Byron waves him away.) Lord Byron: I have one thing to say. I was robbed. That petty, insignificant little oik WarHawk had used some native concoction to drug my food. There was no way he could have beaten me, so he resorted to cheating.
Interviewer: That's a pretty big accusation, sir, do you have any evidence to back it up? Lord Byron: The evidence was in the ring!! I was hardly my usual self,
I was disorientated, weakened and if it was not for my good friend King
Benny, I probably would have passed out before I got in the ring!! I have to ask.....I must...(Byron's eyes glaze over)
Interviewer: Sir, are you all right sir? (Byron collapses to the floor) King Benny (to the camera crew): Get out of here!!!! Someone get a medic!!! ----- This week on FRIDAY NIGHT FIGHTS! For the Internet Championship belt- The DWA Internet Champion, Firewalker (w/Dr. Insane) vs. The Bounty Hunter In a non-title bout- The DWA World Champion, Vinnie vs. Apollo (w/Thantos Polopoulos) Jason Andrews vs. Brian Damage (w/Hurly Burly) Eddy Gonzalez (w/Hurly Burly) vs. Coyote Johnny "Sidewinder" Missle vs. Bane (w/Jon Robinson) In a special NO SUBMISSION challenge match- Dr. Joe Slasher, M.D. vs. WarHawk Hot Tag Action- Class War vs. Thunder and Lightning (w/ Tommy J. Dynomite) ----- UPDATE- BYRON POISONED!----- ( The scene opens outside the medical facilities in the back of Reunion
Arena. The interview team is standing outside, with the ring physician.)
Interviewer: Here we are with the chief medical practitioner in the arena, and the man who has been treating Lord Byron, Dr. Samuel Matthews.
Dr. Matthews, what is the latest report on Lord Byron's condition? Doctor (with a grave expression): Quite serious, I'm afraid. Lord Byron has indeed been subjected to a dangerous toxin.
Interviewer: You are saying he has been drugged? Doctor: It's more serious than that I'm afraid. The drug has been identified as a medical toxin used to treat the nervous system. Lord
Byron is suffering from a massive lactic acid overdose and severe muscle spasms. I'm afraid Lord Byron will be out of action for quite some time.
Interviewer: Is it really that serious? Doctor: The toxin, combined with the physical exertion, has caused extensive muscle damage, and on the treatments he needs, it would be safer if he stayed out of competition for at least three weeks.
Interviewer: Can you identify how long the toxin has been in his system? Doctor: It appears to have been his bloodstream for at least sixteen hours. I am amazed Byron actually made it to ringside, let alone fought in the match-ups.
Interviewer: Do you have any idea who could have done such a thing? Doctor: Well, for a start I can write off Byron's idea that it was the
WarHawk. The toxin was delivered in an extremely accurate dosage. This could only have been done by someone with both knowledge of the toxin and acute medical experience.
Interviewer: Can we speak with Lord Byron? Doctor: He is too weak to talk at the moment, and I have prescribed plenty of rest for him. The toxin is still present in his bloodstream, although it's effects have now been greatly reduced by antibiotics.
Interviewer: Thank you Doctor..(turning to the camera) well, that is indeed grave news...especially happening on Byron's debut. Somebody has obviously gone to a lot of trouble to keep him out of competition. We will have an update on Lord Byron's condition as soon as new news arrives....(The scene fades out.)
