DWA — Pay-Per-View

DWA April Anarchy! (Part 3 of 4)

23 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Garrett Montgomery: Welcome back to APRIL ANARCHY! As the technicians work on the ring for the Texas Death Chamber matchup between Vinnie and
Dr. Joe Slasher, M.D., let's go to some prerecorded comments from Lord
Byron.
(The scene opens on a squash court. Lord Byron is there, playing against
King Benny II. Both men have been playing for some time, and are drenched in sweat. Byron serves.)
King Benny II: Well struck, my dear sir. One is so glad to have you back. Lord Byron: Thank you Benny, I have never felt better...physically, at least... (Benny returns, Byron smashes it at a corner, and Benny runs forward to volley...) Benny: Your talking about not holding a belt, I presume? Byron: Indeed. It seems I underestimated our (Byron sneers) "two little Indian boys". Benny (laughing, misses Byron's return): They can cheat, can't they? I have never before witnessed such underhand tactics. It's shameful!
Byron (grinning as he serves an winning ace): That's in the past, Benny. Forgive and forget. That's what I say. (Byron's hand runs across the scar on his jaw, a permanent result of Firewalker's kick, and his eyes glint dangerously.).
Benny (shaking Byrons hand): Good game, dear chap. (The scene changes, and Byron and King Benny are both in a sauna. Byron is sipping wine and relaxing, showing off his tremendous physique, to the two beautiful ladies who are feeding him grapes, while King Benny is more modestly wrapped in a towel.).
Benny: So Byron, do you have anything in particular in mind? Byron: Oh, I'm sure some of these pathetic, jumped up little peons will be challenging me soon. It's only a matter of time before I return to the title track. (Byron smirks).
Benny: And? Byron: And to all would - be glory hounds, think twice before you cross my path. And as for the champions themselves....(Byron snaps his fingers) All in due course. I don't forget my defeats quickly, rare as they are. There should be few nervous people out there.
(The scene fades out, with one of the ladies giving Byron a backrub.) Crimson Skull: That Byron is one strange character! He seems awfully refined for someone who can be so brutal in the ring.
Garrett: It's good to have him back, I guess. If I were him, I would stay in that sauna, well away from the DWA. This place is full of snakes waiting to strike! And now there's Venom to contend with!
Crimson Skull: It looks like they've assembled a torture chamber where the ring was. What is that glass box?
Garrett: That's the Texas Death Chamber, and it's time for the challenge between the Original Jersey Boy and the evil Doctor! Let's go to the ring!
Scott Meridian: Security, please escort all remaining minors to the concourse area. Anyone who is pregnant, has a pacemaker, or has a weak stomach- please join the minors on the concourse. It's time for the TEXAS
DEATH CHAMBER CHALLENGE! The lights dim, leaving only the Texas Death Chamber lighted. The chamber is a glass cube enclosing the ring and apron area. Two glass doors are positioned on opposite sides. On the ringposts are various tools of the trade, a whip, a billy club, an electric cattle prod, and wrist and ankle cuffs. At the top of one ringpost is a switch labeled DANGER. In the center of the ring is the electric chair, and a large power cable runs from it to the ceiling of the Superdome. A thick fog bellows from under the chair and cascades on to the arena floor.
Scott: Introducing first, from Basking Ridge, New Jersey, standing 6' 11" tall and weighing 315 pounds, the former DWA WORLD CHAMPION, the ORIGINAL JERSEY BOY- VINNIE! Vinnie enters the arena to a standing ovation. He waves confidently at the crowd, and takes up position next to one of the glass doors.
Scott: And his opponent, from Norfolk, Virginia, weighing 295 pounds and standing 6' 9" tall, the scourge of the AMA, DR. JOE SLASHER, M.D!
Slasher strolls into the arena, oblivious to the fans dissent. The official meets him at the other door of the cage, and checks him for weapons. DWA President Shelton Colby takes the microphone from Scott.
Shelton Colby: This match is not sanctioned by the DWA. Both wrestlers have signed releases stating that the DWA will not be held accountable for any injuries that may occur during this match. Do you, Vinnie, understand the release and accept the challenge?
Vinnie: Yes, I do. Shelton: And do you, Doctor Joe Slasher, understand the release and accept the challenge? Slasher: Of course I do! Shelton: Officials, open the cage doors and let the match begin! The glass doors open and the wrestlers enter. The doors are padlocked. Slasher grabs the cattle prod, and Vinnie acquires the billy club. Vinnie climbs into the ring and confidently stands on the chair, waiting for Dr.
Joe. Slasher crawls into the ring, and charges Vinnie. Vinnie leaps aside, and the cattle prod makes contact with the electric chair, causing a flash of sparks. Dr. Joe screams in agony as Vinnie smashes the club into his lower back.
Slasher spins and thrusts the prod at Vinnie, catching him in the ribcage. Vinnie is catapulted across the ring, into the ringpost. Slasher advances slowly, but Vinnie does not move. Slasher gets closer, and
Vinnie comes to life, kicking him in the groin. Slasher falls on the cattle prod, and is blasted into the air. The prod rolls into the metal ringpost and shorts out.
Vinnie gets the ankle cuffs and locks Slasher's legs to the ringpost. Dr. Joe struggles against the cuffs as Vinnie gets the whip. Vinnie lashes out at Slasher's back, raising huge welts. Slasher screams again and rolls out of the ring, still cuffed to the ringpost. The chain between the cuffs breaks, and Slasher struggles to his feet. Vinnie moonsaults out of the ring and on to Slasher, knocking him headfirst into the glass wall. Blood splatters on the glass as a huge gash opens over Slasher's left eye.
Slasher and Vinnie struggle for the whip, and a tug of war ensues. Slasher releases unexpectedly, causing Vinnie to somersault into glass enclosure. The glass wall shatters but does not buckle. Slasher grabs the handcuffs and chains the stunned Vinnie to the ringpost.
Dr. Joe grabs the whip, and twirls it high above his head before striking
Vinnie in the back. The blow rips Vinnie's skin, and blood squirts on to the shattered wall. Slasher begins choking Vinnie with the whip, but
Vinnie kicks him into the shattered wall. It collapses, spraying the crowd with glass. Vinnie tugs at the cuffs, and yanks the ringpost loose as Slasher rises. Vinnie knocks Slasher back into the ring with the post.
Vinnie climbs into the ring, still cuffed, and is met with a blow from the billy club. Slasher jackknifes Vinnie into the electric chair, and runs for the switch. Vinnie rolls out of the chair, and Slasher returns, piledriving him back into the seat. Slasher takes the whip and binds
Vinnie's neck to the back of the chair. Vinnie struggles, and Slasher stops to taunt him.
The crowd is screaming at Slasher, and he seems animated by the response. He struts around the ring, periodically nailing Vinnie with the billy club. Vinnie continues to struggle as blood begins to ooze from around his neck. Slasher slowly starts toward the switch, stopping every step to taunt the crowd.
Suddenly Apollo appears in the rafters, and slides down the power cable to the chair. Slasher rushes for the switch, but Apollo yanks the whip loose and pulls Vinnie from the chair. The chair explodes with white flames as Slasher pulls the switch. Smoke fills the ring, and when it clears Slasher is left alone on the canvas. Apollo is seen carrying
Vinnie to the locker room.
Scott: Your winner- DR. JOE SLASHER, M.D! Garrett: Slasher wins, but doesn't get what he wanted! Crimson Skull: Apollo took a huge chance coming in there! He's still got a championship match tonight!
Garrett: Let's hope that Vinnie is all right. We'll take a break as the technicians prepare the ring for tonight's championship matches.
