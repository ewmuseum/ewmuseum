DWA — Pay-Per-View

DWA Championship Tournament (Part 3 of 4)

25 March 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Garrett Montgomery: We're back with more exciting action from the DWA
Championship Tournament!
Johnny X: Do we get to see Byron now? Garrett: Yes, Lord Byron returns, resurrected in the Internet Tournament. The Command Center has the results from the first round. ----- Results of the first round action in the DWA Internet Championship Tournament ----- Eddy Gonzalez vs. Johnny "Sidewinder" Missle The 555 pound Gonzalez should have crushed the smaller Missle, but Johnny attacked aggressively, nailing the big man with a savate kick that knocked him out cold.
Winner- Johnny "Sidewinder" Missle ----- Jason Andrews vs. Lord Byron (w/King Benny II) Byron was hot after his first round loss to WarHawk, and attacked the unsuspecting Andrews with King Benny's gold cane. Andrews fought back, but Byron caught him with the painful Aristo-Clutch for the submission.
Winner by Submission- Lord Byron ----- Firewalker (w/Dr. Insane) vs. Brian Damage Damage was still suffering the effects of his match with Dr. Slasher and was no match for the fresh Firewalker. Firewalker got the pin after a series of suplexes drained the remaining energy from Brian.
Winner- Firewalker ----- The Bounty Hunter vs. Misery (w/Dr. Insane) The Bounty Hunter was loaded for bear, but ran right into Misery's Roadblock and a quick pin. The Bounty Hunter attacked Misery after the match, severely injuring Misery's right knee.
Winner- Misery ----- Johnny: Man, that Bounty Hunter is another mean dude! Maybe I was wrong about the DWA. Garrett: Cowardly is more like it. Misery's in misery after that unnecessary attack. Hopefully he can continue into the second round.
Johnny: Lord Byron will be there! Byron, Byron, Byron! Garrett: Let's go back to the Command Center before this idiot explodes! ----- Results of the second round action in the DWA Internet Championship
Tournament ----- Johnny "Sidewinder" Missle vs. Lord Byron (w/King Benny II) Missle nailed Lord Byron with a explosive DDT, but was pulled off the prone Byron by the King. An altercation ensued between Johnny and King
Benny, giving Lord Byron time to recuperate. Byron attacked the
Sidewinder from behind, knocking him over the ropes and on to the hard arena floor. As Missle struggled to his feet, Lord Byron launched from the top rope and connected with the back of Missle's head. He dragged
Johnny back into the ring, and added insult to injury with repeated backbreakers prior to the pin.
Winner- Lord Byron ----- Firewalker (w/Dr. Insane) vs. Misery (w/Dr. Insane) Firewalker did not anticipate this matchup with his teammate, but was prepared to go at it when Dr. Insane announced that Misery could not continue due to the injuries suffered in the previous bout.
Winner by Forfeit- Firewalker ----- Johnny: Byron made it! He's gonna be champ! Garrett: Not so fast, monkey boy! He's still got to beat Firewalker, who has dedicated this match to his injured teammate Misery. We'll be back with the finals of the DWA Championship Tournament!
----- Stay Tuned for the Conclusion of the DWA Championship Tournament!
