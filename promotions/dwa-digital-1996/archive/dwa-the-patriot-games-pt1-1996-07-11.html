DWA — Pay-Per-View

The Patriot Games! (Part 1 of 3)

11 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Digital Wrestling Alliance proudly presents LIVE, from the lawn of the Whitehouse in Washington, DC! THE PATRIOT GAMES! Garrett Montgomery: Hello again, wrestling fans, and welcome to the PATRIOT GAMES! I’m your host for this DWA
Challenge Event, and with me tonight is the sweetheart of the Digital Wrestling Alliance, Miss Julie Cooper!
Mr. Head: Hey, what about me! Garrett: And, of course, the intellectually challenged Mr. Head! Mr. Head: Look who’s talking- lover boy- the man who wouldn’t know a wrestling genius if he sat next to one,
WHICH HE DOES! Julie Cooper: Why, thank you, Mr. Head, but I’m sure that Garrett is fully aware of my expertise in the sport of
grappling! Mr. Head: I MEANT ME, YOU BIMBO! Julie: Watch it, buddy, before I slap that stupid grin off your huge NOGGIN! Garrett: It looks like it will be a barn burner tonight, both in the ring and in the booth! We’ve got all of the titles on the
line, along with two challenge matches between Class War and the teams of the Dark One, Total Destruction and
Annihilation, Inc.! Julie: And what about that special stipulation World Tag Team Championship match between Pain Factory and 911!
Mr. Head: "Losers leave the DWA"- I love it! Finally Colby has done something good! 911 and that loser, the
Godfather, is out of here! Garrett: You had better watch what you say, because here he comes now! Let’s go to the ring!
Scott Meridian: Ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls, welcome to the Patriot Games! Tonight’s first bout will be a
"losers leave the DWA" match for the World Tag Team Championship belts! Introducing first, the challengers…
Accompanied to the ring by their manager, the Godfather, weighing in at a combined 530 pounds, here are THE
HITMAN and THE BIG TOWN BULLY- 911! 911 step into a spotlight on the steps of the Whitehouse, and sprint to the ring, trailed by the Godfather. The crowd
seems infatuated with the Godfather, tossing roses and waving to him. He stops along the way to sign a few
autographs, then joins his team in the ring. Scott: And now their opponents. Escorted to the ring by their manager, "Neon" Leon Parker, weighing in at a
combined 507 pounds, the reigning DWA World Tag Team Champions, ALEX "THE HAMMER" SIMPSON AND "ACID"
ANDREW DOWNEY- PAIN FACTORY! The crowd screams obscenities at the two champions as "Neon" Leon blasts his Pain Factory rap from a boom box.
Pain Factory slowly strolls to the ring, polishing their belts with tattered 911 tee-shirts. The Godfather huddles with
his men as the referee calls for the bell. Simpson and the Hitman square off in the center of the ring. Simpson makes the first move, lunging for the legs of
the Hitman, but the Hitman side steps him and drop a knee across the small of his back. Simpson groans in agony
as the Hitman applies the camel clutch. The referee checks for a submission, but Downey kicks the Hitman off of
Simpson before he can concede. Downey and Simpson double team the Hitman, slinging him into the ropes and dropping him with a double knee lift.
The referee escorts Downey to the apron as Simpson begins choking the Hitman. The Bully steps in and separates
the two with a fist to the back of Simpson’s head. The Hitman tags in the Bully, who immediately takes down
Simpson with a snap suplex. The Bully tags the Hitman, and he climbs to the top turnbuckle. He launches, but
Simpson raises a knee and catches the Hitman in the groin. The Hitman struggles to his feet as Simpson tags in Downey. Downey drags the Hitman to his corner and slams his
head into Simpson’s waiting knee. The Hitman collapses to the canvas, and Downey jumps to the floor, dragging
the Hitman to the ringpost. He slams the Hitman’s knee into the ringpost time and time again as "Neon" Leon
cheers him on. The Hitman begins crawling back to the Bully as Downey climbs into the ring and tags in Simpson.
Simpson and Downey both drop elbows on the Hitman’s knee, and the Hitman screams in pain. Simpson drags the Hitman to his feet and slings him into the ropes, but the Hitman holds tight and Simpson misses
a flying drop kick. The Hitman tags in the Big Town Bully, and the Bully flies over the ropes, nailing Simpson with a
series of slaps. The Bully slams Simpson into the turnbuckle, then drives a shoulder into his gut. The Bully locks
Simpson’s head between his knees, and lines him up for the powerbomb. Downey attempts to intervene, but is met
by a revitalized Hitman and driven over the top rope. The Bully executes a perfect powerbomb, but "Neon" Leon
interferes during the cover, nailing the Bully with the boom box. The Godfather steps into the ring, and "Neon" Leon
runs for the locker rooms. The Bully tags in the Hitman as Simpson tags Downey. The Hitman nails Downey with a forearm smash, then tags
Simpson with one, knocking him off the apron. The Hitman drags the dazed Downey to the corner and lifts him to the
top turnbuckle. The crowd goes wild as the Hitman climbs to the top and sets up for the superplex. The Hitman
executes a perfect superplex, then tags in the Bully, who drops onto Downey with a giant splash. The referee
counts to three and calls for the bell. Scott: Your winners, and new DWA World Tag Team Champions- 911! The crowd explodes as Simpson, Downey, and Leon are escorted from the grounds of the Whitehouse by DWA
security. The new champions raise the belts high above their heads, and are greeted with a standing ovation.
Garrett: WOW! The long time champs, Pain Factory, are out of the DWA! Julie: What a match! 911 finally beat the one team that they said could not be beaten! Mr. Head: Pain Factory deserves a rematch! The Godfather interfered! Garrett: It’s too late for that now! Pain Factory is gone- eliminated from the ranks of the DWA!
Julie: And our next match is about to get under way! Let’s go to the ring for the DWA Internet Championship match!
Scott: Ladies and gentlemen, this bout is for the DWA Internet Championship belt! Introducing first, the challenger!
Accompanied to the ring by his manager, King Benny II, standing 6’ 2’’ tall and weighing 265 pounds, the former
DWA Internet Champion- LORD BYRON! A sleek, black limousine pulls up at the grandstand entrance and King Benny steps out, carrying a red carpet. He
rolls the carpet onto the ground in front of the limo door, and Byron steps out, followed by a half dozen beautiful
women wearing "Byron’s Babes" tee-shirts. The women escort Lord Byron to the ring, and he rewards each with a
kiss before climbing over the ropes. Scott: And now his opponent, accompanied to the ring by his manager, Thantos Polopolous, from Athens, Greece,
standing 6’ 6" tall and weighing 295 pounds, here is your DWA Internet Champion- POSEIDON! Poseidon appears at the top of the grandstand, and works his way through the crowd, shaking hands and signing
autographs. He meets Polopolous at ringside, waves the Internet Championship belt to the fans, and climbs into the
ring. The referee calls for the bell and the match begins. Poseidon grabs Byron for a belly to belly suplex, but Byron slips the hold and brings Poseidon to the mat with an
arm drag takedown. Byron drives a knee into Poseidon’s shoulder, then begins exerting pressure with an arm bar.
Poseidon pulls himself to his feet and breaks the hold with a head slap. Poseidon misses on an elbow drop, and
Byron takes the offensive, rebounding off the ropes and onto Poseidon with a cannonball splash. Byron goes for the
cover, but Poseidon kicks out at two. Byron drags Poseidon to his feet and attempts to introduce his face to the turnbuckle. Poseidon resists, and
reverses the face slam, stunning Byron. Poseidon slings Byron into the ropes and catches him with a vicious
clothesline on the rebound. Byron wisely rolls to the apron, where he is tended to by King Benny.
The referee urges Byron to return to the ring, but Byron feigns injury. Poseidon comes over to take a look and is
rewarded with a rake of the eyes. Byron vaults the ropes and drops Poseidon with a flying DDT. Poseidon kicks out
at one and tries to pull himself up with the ropes. Byron assists him by tossing him over the top rope and onto the
ground. Benny hands Byron a small object as he climbs to the top turnbuckle. Poseidon rises, and Byron leaps,
nailing him with the foreign object. Poseidon falls to the ground unconscious, and Byron climbs into the ring,
proclaiming his victory. The referee goes to raise Byron’s hand and a set of brass knuckles fall out of Byron’s
trunks. King Benny dives for the knuckles, but the referee sees them first and disqualifies Lord Byron.
Scott: Ladies and gentlemen, the referee has disqualified Lord Byron. Your winner, and still DWA Internet
Champion- POSEIDON! Julie: Byron is desperate to regain the title! I’ve never seem him resort to tactics like that before!
Mr. Head: It’s obvious that those brass knuckles were planted on Byron! There’s no way his lordship would ever
employ such an object! Garrett: Yeah, right! Although the ladies love him, Byron is a ruthless weasel who would do anything for a win! Just
like this next tag team- the irrepressible Class War! We’ll be right back with their back to back challenge matches
against the forces of the Dark One!
