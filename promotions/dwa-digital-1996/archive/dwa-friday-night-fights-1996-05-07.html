Friday Night Fights! (5/7)

7 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Digital Wrestling Alliance presents FRIDAY NIGHT FIGHTS! Live from 3Com Park in San Francisco, California! Garrett Montgomery: Welcome back to FRIDAY NIGHT FIGHTS! I'm your host,
Garrett Montgomery, and I'll be joined here tonight by the DWA President and my friend, Shelton Colby! Welcome to the program, Shelton!
Shelton Colby: Thanks, Garrett! It's great to be here, and it's great to be outdoors in beautiful San Francisco!
Garrett: This is certainly a unique setting for tonight's matches. Before we get started, tell us- who are these DWA Enforcers?
Shelton: I really can't say due to contractual obligations. But I can tell you this- the DWA Enforcers are here and will be deployed as I feel necessary!
Garrett: Does that mean you would use them again to control Lord Byron? Even with his threats of legal action? Shelton: Without a doubt. Byron is subject to the same code of conduct as all the other superstars. Personally, I think Lord Byron is just frustrated with his inability to get a title shot.
Garrett: If his performance tonight is any indication of his abilities, it will be a long time before he gets that shot. Let's go to the DWA
Commando Center to see the highlights of tonight's early matches.
-----DWA Commando Center----- Mayhem, Inc. (w/ "Legend" Josh Brown) vs. Thunder and Lightning (w/ Tommy J. Dynomite) There were no holds barred in this matchup after the beating and kidnapping on last week's Friday Night Fights. Dynomite attacked Brown at ringside, and after a brief altercation the two managers were lead away by the DWA Enforcers. Thunder got the pin on Masked Mayhem 1 after repeated body slams to the canvas. The Mayhems tried to continue the brawl after the pin, but Thunder and Lightning pulled out the baseball bats and chased them from the ballpark.
Your winners- Thunder and Lightning Pain Factory (w/ "Neon" Leon Parker) vs. Burly Bunch (w/ Hurly Burly) Pain Factory proved that they were still the team to beat in this non-title matchup. Brian Damage was hurt early, and Downey prevented him from tagging Gonzalez while Simpson launched himself off the top rope.
Simpson pinned Damage after a somersault splash. Your winners- Pain Factory Wasp Man (w/ Roscoe Law) vs. Dr. Joe Slasher, M.D. In this battle of good versus evil, it appeared that Wasp Man would triumph, as he came close to pinning the Doctor six times. Slasher is smart as well as tough, and tricked Wasp Man by feigning injury. Wasp Man released his hold, and Slasher hooked him with his 911, a variation of the figure four leglock. Slasher refused to release the hold after Wasp
Man submitted, and had to be pried away from the popular wrestler.
Your winner by submission- Dr. Joe Slasher, M.D. Bane (w/ Jon Robinson) vs. Vinnie (w/ "Golden" Johney Pathos) Bane and Vinnie went toe to toe for over an hour, neither man showing the ability to take the other out. Robinson was the deciding factor when he clipped Vinnie's knee with a chair. Bane stopped Vinnie with his Death
Drop, the added insult to injury by applying the potentially fatal move on Pathos. Robinson and Bane exited the stadium before the Enforcers could be dispatched.
Your winner- Bane Venom (w/ Dark) vs. Jason Andrews Venom and Dark attacked Andrews as he entered the ring, ignoring the official's pleas to stop. Andrews collapsed after numerous blows to the back of the head, and Venom dropped him across the top turnbuckle several times before retreating to the locker rooms. The referee disqualified
Venom and awarded the match to Andrews.
Your winner by disqualification- Jason Andrews Lord Byron (w/ King Benny II) vs. Monster Lucas Masai (w/ Shelton Colby) Masai continued to improve under the supervision of Colby, executing several highly technical reversals of Byron's holds. Masai got the win with a small package when Byron began focusing on the presence of Colby at ringside. After the match, Byron and Benny confronted Colby, but Masai intervened before the confrontation got out of hand.
Your winner- Monster Lucas Masai Coyote vs. The Bounty Hunter In the most bizarre match of the night, the Bounty Hunter got the win by countout after Coyote left the ring, apparently distracted by the full moon. After the decision, the Bounty Hunter tracked him to the roof over the grandstands, but Coyote fled and disappeared into the crowd.
Your winner by countout- the Bounty Hunter ----- Garrett: Coyote flaked out! I've never seen a wrestler leave the ring to chase the moon! Shelton: He's a very mysterious character. Since signing with the DWA, I haven't spoken with him. He definitely marches to the beat of a different drummer!
Garrett: Speaking of drumming, have you seen the beatings that Firewalker has suffered over the past few weeks? It's rumored that he may not be able to appear tonight to defend the Internet Tag Team belt.
Shelton: He's one tough cookie! But I've been assured by Dr. Insane that he'll be here. We'll see soon enough, since the match is about to begin.
Let's go to the ring! Scott Meridian: Ladies and gentlemen, this next match is for the DWA
Internet Tag Team Championship belts! Introducing first, the challengers.
Accompanied to the ring by their manager, the Godfather, weighing a combined 530 pounds, here are the Hitman and the Big Town Bully, 911!
911 enters the stadium to an overwhelmingly negative response. They spit at the fans as they enter the ring.
Scott: And their opponents, accompanied to the ring by their manager Dr. Insane, weighing a combined 635 pounds, here are the DWA Internet Tag
Team Champions- FIREWALKER AND MISERY- THE NOMADS!
The Nomads come out to a standing ovation. As they make their way to the ring, it's obvious that Firewalker is still not fully recovered. After a brief warm-up, the referee calls for the bell and the match begins.
Misery and the Hitman lock up. Misery breaks the hold with a hip toss, and the Hitman responds with a chop to the throat. The Hitman nails
Misery with a running clothesline, sending the bigger man out of the ring and on to the wooden apron.
Misery regains his senses and crawls back into the ring, only to be doubleteamed by the Hitman and Bully. They hammer him with a series of drop kicks, knocking him into the turnbuckle. The Hitman crawls out of the ring as the Big Town Bully choke slams Misery and drops a knee into his windpipe. The Bully gets the figure four on Misery as he tries to crawl to the tag.
The Bully increases the pressure as Misery tries to make the tag. Firewalker stretches, and as Misery reaches to tag him a masked Lord
Byron appears from beneath the ring and yanks Firewalker from the apron.
Misery finally submits as the masked Byron retreats to the locker room. Scott: Your winners by submission, and new DWA INTERNET TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS- 911! Shelton: SEND THE ENFORCERS AFTER BYRON! Excuse me, I've got a problem to handle! Garrett: What an ending! Byron gets his revenge on Firewalker, causing him to lose his second title in two weeks! This is an outrage! I'm sure that Dr. Insane will be demanding a rematch! Wait... I have word that
Scott Meridian is following the DWA Enforcers into the locker room.
Scott? Garrett: Yes, Garrett, we're right behind the Enforcers, who are following Shelton Colby through the locker room area. Wait, it looks like they致e found him!
The DWA Enforcers drag a half dressed Lord Byron out of the locker room and into the adjoining breezeway. Fans stop to gawk at his royal jewels as the handcuffs are applied. Byron protests loudly as he is escorted from the stadium.
Shelton: I want him off the premises NOW! BYRON, YOU'RE SUSPENDED! Lord Byron: YOU HAVEN'T HEARD THE END OF THIS, COLBY! Scott: It looks like Shelton Colby has had enough of Lord Byron's antics. Back to you, Garrett. Garrett: Thanks, Scott. Well, as president Colby prepares to escort to the ring another DWA troublemaker, Kuwasi "Black Fist" Shakur, let's take a moment to look at next Tuesday's preliminary matches in the DWA
Intercontinental Championship Tournament! -----DWA Commando Center----- Preliminary Round Matches for Tuesday, May 7th. The Serial KiLLa vs. The Bounty Hunter Alex "the Hammer" Simpson (w/ "Neon" Leon Parker) vs. Masked Mayhem 2 (w/ "Legend" Josh Brown) Apollo (w/ Thantos Polopolous) vs. The Hitman (w/ The Godfather) Firewalker (w/ Dr. Insane) vs. Jason Andrews Lord Byron (w/ King Benny II) vs. Thunder (w/ Tommy J. Dynomite) Coyote vs. Kuwasi "Black Fist" Shakur (w/ Shelton Colby) Misery (w/ Dr. Insane) vs. "Acid" Andrew Downey (w/ "Neon" Leon Parker) Bane (w/ Jon Robinson) vs. The Big Town Bully (w/ The Godfather) Venom (w/ Dark) vs. Lightning (w/ Tommy J. Dynomite) Dr. Joe Slasher, M.D. vs. Masked Mayhem 1 (w/ "Legend" Josh Brown) Vinnie (w/ "Golden" Johney Pathos) vs. Brian Damage (w/ Hurly Burly) Wasp Man (w/ Roscoe Law) vs. Monster Lucas Masai (w/ Shelton Colby) Eddy Gonzalez draws a bye in this round. ----- Garrett: These preliminary matches will not be televised, but I'll be bringing you a tournament update with complete results on Tuesday night.
This is a double elimination tournament, so all the participants get a second chance for success. It looks like their ready at ringside, take it away, Scott!
Scott: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is for the DWA Internet Championship belt! Introducing first, the challenger. From San Antonio,
Texas, weighing 298 pounds and standing 6' 9" tall, please welcome THE
SERIAL KILLA! The Serial KiLLa saunters to the ring, accompanied by cheers from his small but loyal fan base. He climbs into the ring, bounces off the ropes, and waves at the crowd.
Scott: And his opponent, accompanied to the ring by DWA President Shelton
Colby, from the South Bronx of New York, standing 6' 9" tall and weighing
300 rock solid pounds, here is your DWA INTERNET CHAMPION- KUWASI "BLACK
FIST" SHAKUR! The crowd reaction is mixed as Shakur enters the stadium. A small but noisy group of fanatics start up the "shoot, Fist, shoot" chant, and the crowd joins in. Shakur raises his handcuffed hands in response. He enters the ring and Colby removes the cuffs. The referee calls for the bell and the match begins.
The two men size each other up, and Shakur steps forward, taking the
KiLLa to the mat with an arm drag takedown. The KiLLa kicks out, and knees Shakur in the gut. He then climbs the ropes and executes a back flip on to Shakur's back, knocking him out of the ring.
Skakur hits the flooring hard, but manages to roll away as the Serial
KiLLa leaps from the apron. The KiLLa careens into the barricade and collapses to the ground. Shakur pulls him to his feet and slams his head into the barricade. Shelton Colby intervenes and directs the two wrestlers back into the ring.
They tie up in the center of the ring. The Serial KiLLa pushes Shakur into a corner and drives his elbow into Shakur's head. Shakur no sells the move, and the "shoot, Fist, shoot" chant begins again. Shakur responds by slinging the Serial KiLLa into the ropes, and nails him on the rebound with the South Bronx Bomb. The KiLLa is dazed and drops to the apron.
Shakur crawls under the ropes and on to the arena floor. He drags the
Serial KiLLa to the ringpost and whips his knees into it. The KiLLa kicks free, sending Shakur sprawling into the seats. As Shakur is crawling back over the barricade, the KiLLa leaps from the top turnbuckle and connects with a flying drop kick. Shakur drops, and the KiLLa mounts the turnbuckle again, only to drop an elbow on the dazed Shakur.
The Serial KiLLa drags Shakur back into the ring, and slams him to the mat. The Serial KiLLa covers for the win.
Scott: Your winner, and NEW DWA INTERNET CHAMPION- THE SERIAL KILLA! Garrett: Wow, what an upset! And what a night- both Internet titles change hands in the city by the bay! Be sure to join us next week for more exciting action on Friday Night Fights when the new Internet Tag
Team Champions 911 face Thunder and Lightning and the new Internet
Champion the Serial KiLLa faces "Acid" Andrew Downey of Pain Factory!
