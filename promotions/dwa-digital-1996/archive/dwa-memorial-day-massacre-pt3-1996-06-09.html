DWA — Pay-Per-View

DWA Memorial Day Massacre! (Part 3 of 3)

9 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Julie Cooper: Welcome back to MEMORIAL DAY MASSACRE! We’ve got Scott
Meridian standing by with DWA President Shelton Colby. Scott, what’s the status on the final match?
Scott: Good question, Julie! President Colby, the ring is obviously destroyed. Will the match between the Nomads with WarHawk and Thunder and
Lightning with the mystery partner be rescheduled?
Shelton Colby: Absolutely not! The show must go on! The wrestlers have agreed to battle on the floor of the Kingdome. We’ll have the debris cleared away in a matter of minutes and the show will go on!
Scott: Great! Now can you tell us who the mystery partner will be? Shelton: Sure. He’s got a grudge against Firewalker and WarHawk, as well as me! The mystery partner of Thunder and Lightning will be--- LORD
BYRON! Scott: There you have it, Julie! The match will go on, and Lord Byron is the mystery partner!
Julie: Thanks, Scott. Well, this match has just gotten really interesting! Garrett Montgomery: That’s right! And don’t forget that the DWA Enforcers will be the special guest referees for the match! We’ll have a total of eight men in the ring... uh, on the floor at the same time. Let’s go back to Scott for the introductions.
Scott: Ladies and gentlemen, our final match of MEMORIAL DAY MASSACRE! This is a six man, no DQ Challenge Match ON THE FLOOR! Our special referees for this bout will be the DWA Enforcers. Introducing first, accompanied to the ring by their manager, Tommy J. Dynomite, THUNDER and
LIGHTNING! And their mystery partner for tonight, accompanied by his manager King Benny II, LORD BYRON!
Dynomite and Benny lead the way as their wrestlers enter the Kingdome. The contingent known as “Byron’s Babes” is in full attendance and is elated to see their heartthrob up close and dressed. Byron seems a little embarrassed, and scans the crowd for Suna Gib.
Scott: And their opponents, accompanied to the ring by their manager, Dr. Insane, here are Firewalker and Misery- THE NOMADS, and their special partner, WARHAWK! The Nomads and WarHawk come rushing out of the locker rooms, and run head on into an attack from their opponents. The DWA Enforcers ring the bell as the carnage begins.
WarHawk and Byron lock up, and WarHawk flips him to the floor with a hip toss. Firewalker nails the new DWA Intercontinental Champion Lightning with a forearm to the mouth. Thunder choke slams Misery to the floor and begins kicking him.
Lightning rams Firewalker’s head into the barricade, stunning him. He lifts Firewalker over his head and slams him to the floor. Lightning misses with an elbow drop as Firewalker rolls aside. Firewalker chops
Lightning’s throat, then pins him as he gasps for air. Lightning kicks out at the two count.
WarHawk and Byron exchange slaps, then Byron drops him with a right hook. Byron pulls Firewalker away from Lightning, and Lightning begins pelting
Firewalker with blows to the head. Misery nails Thunder with a drop kick, then clips Byron from behind, freeing Firewalker. WarHawk drops a leg on the prone Byron.
Thunder pulls a chair from the crowd and smacks Firewalker, knocking him to the ground. He goes for the cover, but WarHawk nails him with the chair. Lightning DDTs Misery, then tries the same on WarHawk. WarHawk slips out of the DDT, and punishes Lightning with an atomic drop.
Firewalker drags Thunder to his feet and slams him into the barricade. Thunder’s left arm becomes entangled in the metal railing, and Firewalker drops a leg on it. The arm snaps horrifically, and Thunder screams in agony. The action stops as Thunder submits.
Scott: Your winners- the Nomads and WarHawk! Firewalker tries to assist the paramedics with Thunder, but Lightning attacks from behind. WarHawk tries to pull him off of Firewalker, but the
DWA Enforcers attack WarHawk. Byron nails Misery with the chair and he collapses to the floor. Lightning kicks Firewalker repeatedly, then joins the Enforcers as they plummel WarHawk. Byron lifts Misery and slams him into the barricade.
WarHawk collapses to the floor, and the Enforcers begin powerbombing
Firewalker. Dr. Insane jumps the barricade, and Lightning drops him with a hard right. Vinnie rushes out of the locker room and clotheslines
Lightning into the crowd. They begin to brawl as Poseidon and Apollo join the fray. Byron and Lightning make a quick exit, leaving the DWA
Enforcers surrounded by the Nomads, WarHawk, Apollo, and Poseidon. President Colby steps in to end the confrontation. The DWA Enforcers back away toward the locker rooms. The DWA Enforcers pause, and flip off
Colby. They remove their masked, revealing their true identities, and begin laughing. Masai and Shakur toss the Enforcer masks into the stunned crowd and exit the arena.
Julie: Class War are the DWA Enforcers? The DWA Enforcers are Class War? I’m confused! Garrett: It looks like Class War is, or was, employed as the DWA Enforcers! Colby’s project with Masai and Shakur has failed. Class War is, once again, OUT OF CONTROL!
Julie: This whole night was OUT OF CONTROL! Two new champions, a unified tag team champion, and to top it off a brawl on the floor of the
Kingdome! The repercussions from tonight will undoubtedly affect the future of the DWA. Garrett: That’s true, and we’ll see soon enough this week on Friday Night
Fights! See you then!
