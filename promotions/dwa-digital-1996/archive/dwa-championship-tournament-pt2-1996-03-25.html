DWA Championship Tournament (Part 2 of 4)

25 March 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Garrett Montgomery: Welcome back to the DWA Championship Tournament! We'll get on to the second round highlights after a message from the
Bounty Hunter. ----- Standing in a dark ally. Bounty Hunter: In the DWA, the Bounty Hunter will be the primary force to reckon with. Anyone who wants to make a name for themselves will be
looking for a chance to face me. But much to their disappointment, they will come out of the match up a loser, cause that's what all you are. I challenge anyone, anytime, to any matchup, and I will come out victorious.
----- Johnny X: That big boy means business! Garrett: And we'll be seeing him in the first round of the Internet
Championship tournament. Now to the Command Center for updates on the second round of the World Championship tournament!
----- Results of the second round action in the DWA World Championship Tournament ----- Coyote vs. WarHawk These talented competitors went toe to toe for almost thirty minutes before WarHawk gained the advantage with a hip toss that sent Coyote flying into the crowd. Dazed and confused, Coyote stumbled away from the ring and was counted out. After the bell, WarHawk assisted Coyote to the locker room, where he received treatment for a concussion.
Winner by Countout- WarHawk ----- Dr. Joe Slasher, M.D. vs. The Serial KiLLa Slasher continued his questionable tactics, choking the Serial KiLLa with a piece of surgical tubing. The referee failed to see the infraction, and
Dr. Joe pinned the gasping KiLLa for the victory.
Winner- Dr. Joe Slasher, M.D. ----- Johnny: Byron, Byron, Byron! Garrett: Shut up, you moron! We'll be getting back to Lord Byron soon. But first, we've got to determine who will meet the DWA World Champion
Vinnie. Will it be the noble WarHawk, or the nefarious Dr. Joe Slasher,
M.D.? Johnny: My money's on the good doctor! ----- Results of the final round action in the DWA World Championship Tournament ----- WarHawk vs. Dr. Joe Slasher, M.D. WarHawk hammered Slasher from the bell, getting a near pin with a tremendous powerbomb. The Doctor recuperated, and nailed WarHawk with a forearm smash that sent him crashing into the referee. Slasher pounced upon WarHawk, choking him with the same surgical tubing that he used against the Serial KiLLa. As the referee regained his composure, Dr. Joe placed the tube in WarHawk's hand, and pretended to be choking. The referee disqualified WarHawk for the foreign object, awarding the match to Dr. Joe Slasher, M.D.
----- Johnny: I told you the Doctor would win! Now he gets to perform surgery on that jerky boy, Vinnie!
Garrett: I'm sure WarHawk will have something to say about that match! And the Original JERSEY Boy is well aware of Dr. Slasher's antics. We'll have the title match live later tonight when we return.
----- Stay tuned for Part 3 of the DWA Championship Tournament!
