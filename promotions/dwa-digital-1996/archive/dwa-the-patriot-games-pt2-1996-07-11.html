DWA — Pay-Per-View

The Patriot Games! (Part 2 of 3)

11 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Julie: We’re back, and Scott Meridian is standing by in the ring for the introductions for this next challenge match!
Scott: Ladies and gentlemen, the next match is a special back to back challenge match! Introducing first, weighing a
combined 577 pounds, Kuwasi "Black Fist" Shakur and Monster Lucas Masai- CLASS WAR! Class War strut confidently to the ring amidst shouts of "shoot, Fist, shoot" from the hard-core fans. Despite their
sometimes questionable tactics, Shakur and Masai have gained a small but loyal following among the DWA fans.
Scott: And now their opponents! Accompanied to the ring by their manager, the Dark One, weighing in at a combined
540 pounds, here are the Masked Warrior and Ken Lee- ANNIHILATION, INC.! The crowd applauds politely for Annihilation, still wary of the Dark One. The Dark One waves to the crowd and the
applause builds. Scott: Win or lose, Class War will face Total Destruction at the conclusion of this match! Masai climbs into the ring to face the Masked Warrior as the referee rings the bell. The Masked Warrior charges
Masai, driving him into the ropes. Masai retaliates with a forearm smash, followed by a drop kick that sends the
Warrior flying. Masai tags in Shakur, who leaps from the top turnbuckle and nails the rising Warrior with another
drop kick. Shakur pulls the Warrior to his feet and drags him to the corner, tagging in Masai. Masai and Shakur
sling the Warrior into the ropes, but they miss with the double drop kick as the Warrior hangs onto the ropes. Ken
Lee is tagged in and immediately drops Masai and Shakur is succession with spinning back kicks.
Shakur rolls to the apron as Lee drags Masai to his feet. He lifts the bigger man for a backbreaker, but when he
connects a loud pop is heard and Masai collapses to the canvas. Shakur rushes into the ring and pushes Ken Lee
aside. Masai is writhing in pain as Shakur waves for a stretcher. The referee calls for the bell as Masai is carried
out of the ring. Scott: Ladies and gentlemen, the referee has stopped this bout due to a severe injury to Monster Lucas Masai.
Your winners, Ken Lee and the Masked Warrior- ANNIHILATION, INC.! Garrett: Masai was really hurt by that backbreaker! Julie: That popping sound almost made me sick! Let’s hope he’s all right. Mr. Head: Hey, that’s real action in there, and if you can’t handle the injuries, you had better just get back to that
Commando Center where it’s nice and cozy! Garrett: Oh, no, we’re not getting into that again! Let’s go back to the ring for an announcement from Scott Meridian.
Scott: Ladies and gentlemen, DWA President Colby, in light of the injury to Monster Lucas Masai, has canceled the
match between Class War and Total Destruction. Kuwasi "Black Fist" Shakur comes running from the medical trailer and leaps into the ring. A conversion ensues
between Scott meridian and Shakur, and Scott motions to DWA President Colby. Colby climbs into the ring, and after
a brief consultation with Shakur and Scott motions to the Dark One. The Dark One steps over the ropes, and begins
laughing as Colby speaks to him. Shakur seems agitated, and moves toward the Dark One, but Scott holds him
back. President Colby takes the house microphone from Scott. Shelton Colby: Ladies and gentlemen, Kuwasi "Black Fist" Shakur has requested that the next match become a two
on one handicap match. Shakur will face Total Destruction alone! The crowd goes wild at the announcement. Colby and the Dark One exit the ring as Scott begins the introductions.
Scott: As President Colby just stated, this next bout is a two on one handicap match. Introducing first, one half of
Class War, weighing 300 pounds and standing 6’ 9" tall, here is KUWASI "BLACK FIST" SHAKUR!
The crowd continues to cheer for Shakur as he intently awaits the arrival of his opponents.
Scott: And his opponents, joined at ringside by their manager, the Dark One, at a combined weight of 695 pounds,
here are Vanquish and Conquer- TOTAL DESTRUCTION! Total Destruction walk slowly to ringside, wary of Shakur. The crowd is on edge as Conquer climbs over the ropes,
but Shakur does not attack. The referee calls for the bell, and Shakur offers his hand to Conquer. Shakur shakes
with Conquer, then snaps him to the canvas with a hip toss. Shakur drives a knee into his throat and Vanquish
attacks from behind. Vanquish connects with a kick to the back of the head and Shakur retreats to the apron.
The referee escorts Vanquish to the apron as Conquer rises to his feet. Shakur leaps the ropes and locks up with
Conquer. The two struggle against the ropes, then both flip over and onto the ground. Both men rise and begin
exchanging tremendous blows. Shakur takes the advantage and rams Conquer into the barricade, which collapses
onto the first row. Fans scatter as Shakur picks up a chair and nails Conquer in the back. Conquer drops to the
ground as Vanquish charges Shakur. Shakur spins and pops Vanquish with the chair, dropping him. Shakur climbs
into the ring as the Dark One assists Conquer to his feet. Conquer climbs into the ring, carrying a section of the barricade. The referee blocks his way, and as the ref tries to
wrestle the barricade away from Conquer, Shakur charges, driving both men into the turnbuckle. The referee
collapses to the canvas, and Shakur and Conquer begin struggling over the section of barricade. Shakur drives
Conquer back into the corner and he falls to the canvas. Shakur places the barricade on top of the down Conquer
and climbs to the top rope. Shakur launches and drops all of his weight on the barricade, crushing Conquer. Shakur
rises and is attacked by Vanquish. Vanquish gets control, and drops Shakur with a swinging neckbreaker. Vanquish
pulls Conquer to his feet, and they drop the section of barricade on Shakur. Both men jump on the barricade, driving
it into Shakur’s ribs. The Dark One pulls the segment of barricade from the ring as the referee regains
consciousness. Conquer goes for the cover, but Shakur manages to kick out at two and one half. Conquer drags Shakur to his feet
and tags in Vanquish, who hammers Shakur with a series of punches to the gut. Vanquish pulls Shakur to the center
of the ring and sets him up for the powerbomb. Shakur slips the move by locking his ankles around Vanquish’s
neck, and snaps him to the canvas with a variation of the Frankensteiner. Shakur goes for the cover, but Vanquish gets his foot on the ropes and the referee breaks the hold. Shakur pulls
Vanquish to his feet and sets him up for the powerbomb. Vanquish struggles and flips Shakur over the ropes.
Shakur hits the ground hard, and Conquer begins pounding him with a chair. Conquer pulls Shakur on to the
announcer’s table, and hold his arms down as Vanquish moonsaults from the top turnbuckle. Vanquish drives
Shakur through the table, and he and Conquer begin stomping on Shakur. The Dark One pulls them away and
directs Vanquish into the ring. Shakur is out, and the referee awards the match to Total Destruction.
Scott: Your winners by count out- TOTAL DESTRUCTION! Julie: Shakur put up an incredible fight! Garrett: He was obviously exhausted, and Total Destruction took advantage of the situation. I’ve got a feeling that
this isn’t over yet! Mr. Head: That’s right! Once Masai is back, the Dark One won’t have enough men to protect him from Class War!
Garrett: The Dark One has his eyes set on the gold, and his two teams will be demanding title shots after their
performances today. Annihilation, Inc. will get their shot against the winner of this next match. Let’s go to the ring!
Scott: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is for the DWA Internet Tag Team Championship belts! Introducing first, the
challengers! Accompanied to the ring by their manager, Dr. Insane, weighing in at a combined 635 pounds, here are
the former DWA Internet Tag Team Champions, Firewalker and Misery- THE NOMADS! The Nomads run to the ring amidst thunderous applause. Dr. Insane joins the popular team on the apron as they
wave to the crowd. Scott: And their opponents, accompanied to the ring by "Legend" Josh Brown, at a combined weight of 690 pounds,
here are Masked Mayhem 1 and Masked Mayhem 2- MAYHEM, INC.! The masked men slowly walk to the ring, led by Brown, who waves the two belts high above his head. The crowd is
annoyed by his arrogance, and overwhelm him with a barrage of insults. Mayhem, Inc. seems oblivious to the
crowd reaction. Firewalker and Masked Mayhem 1 climb into the ring and the referee calls for the bell.
Firewalker and Mayhem 1 meet in the center of the ring in a test of strength, but Mayhem 1 pulls free when
Firewalker gains the advantage. He tags in Masked Mayhem 2, who is immediately assaulted with a volley of
punches from Firewalker. Mayhem 2 steps through the ropes, and the referee sends Firewalker to his corner.
Firewalker tags in Misery. Misery takes down Mayhem 2 with a leg sweep, then drops an elbow to the gut. He goes for the cover, but Mayhem
2 kicks out at the count of one. Misery drags him to his feet and bashes his head into the turnbuckle. Mayhem 2
responds with a heart punch that sends Misery flying across the ring. Mayhem 2 tags in Mayhem 1, who chases
Misery out of the ring. Misery climbs back to the apron and is met with a flying clothesline from Mayhem 1 that sends both men to the
ground. Firewalker leaps from the apron and tags Mayhem 1 with a double ax handle to the head. Mayhem 2 rushes
across the ring and somersaults over the ropes, landing on Firewalker. Mayhem 2 drags Misery into the ring and
sets him up for the piledriver, but the official intervenes and escorts him to the apron. Firewalker pushes Mayhem 1
under the ropes and the referee starts the count. Misery tries to get to his feet, but Mayhem 2 stomps him back to the canvas. The referee pushes Mayhem 2 back to
the apron as Firewalker grabs Mayhem 1’s legs and pulls him to the corner. Misery crawls to the corner and tags in
Firewalker, who immediately begins slamming Mayhem 1’s leg into the ringpost. Misery climbs the turnbuckle and
Firewalker tags him, then Misery squashes Mayhem 1 with the banzai drop. As Misery is dragging Mayhem 1 to the
center of the ring, Mayhem 2 attempts to interfere, but Firewalker meets him head on and both men tumble over the
ropes. Misery goes for the pin and gets the three count. Scott: Your winners, and once again DWA Internet Tag Team Champions- THE NOMADS! Julie: Both tag team champions lose their titles today! Anything can happen in the DWA! Garrett: That’s an understatement! We may have more titles change hands yet! Mr. Head: Yes, Vinnie, the Original Jerky Boy, will be losing his next to Dr. Joe Slasher, MD.!
Julie: We’ll find out next after this short break!
