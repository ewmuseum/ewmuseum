Friday Night Fights! (5/22)

22 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Digital Wrestling Alliance presents FRIDAY NIGHT FIGHTS! Live from the Georgia Dome in Atlanta, Georgia! Garrett Montgomery: Welcome back to Friday Night Fights! And welcome back, once again, my new co-host, the lovely Julie Cooper!
Julie Cooper: Thank you, Garrett! It’s great to be back! Tonight is an extra special night for Friday Night Fights, because we’ve added the round four matches from the DWA Intercontinental Championship Tournament.
Garrett: And we’ll have two title defenses! The Serial KiLLa puts his
Internet Title on the line against Bane, and the Internet Tag Team champions, 911, defend against Class War!
Julie: 911 is standing by in the DWA Commando Center with some comments on their upcoming matches.
-----DWA COMMANDO CENTER----- The Hitman: So after a successful title Defense the Champs get to face
Class War then we get a unification match with Pain Factory. Well, first off, Class War, you’re way out of your "class"! Then we get a piece of the only team we have lost to- Pain Factory! Well, boys, we are more experienced, and pumped up to dethrone you chumps and show the world who the best tag team is!
The Big Town Bully: Yeah, I can't wait to stomp all these teams that think they’re better than us! Everyone that faces us better remember our name ‘cause after they face us they need to call that number!!
The Godfather: Well, my boys are ready to dominate the DWA! We will unify the Tag Belts then Hitman will take the Intercontinental Belt!!! Anybody that steps in our way will just end up a casualty of 911!!!!!
----- Garrett: Strong words from 911! Julie: Yes, but look at what Class War did this past week to the Serial
KiLLa’s car! Let’s roll that replay!
-----REPLAY- CLASS WAR PRESS CONFERENCE----- (Monster Lucas Masai and Kuwasi "Black Fist" Shakur, in the DWA parking lot surrounded by members of the wrestling press, carry crowbars and accept questions.)
Reporter: You guys have been pretty quiet as of late, but the shoot was a pretty shocking way to come back. What's in store for Class War?
Masai: Class War has the DWA standard for extreme. We've destroyed our opponents and done more than our share to make this place synonymous with action. The DWA begs for us, the fans beg for us, because we're no-bullshit wrestlers. We don't need cute dance moves, valets, one name or cheesy gimmicks or rip-offs of other wrestlers or musicians to get over. Class War gets a name by being insane in the ring and taking no prisoners. Our goals right now are to make people's lives' hell and continue the DWA-wide Class War art tour by painting misery on people's faces and mats with blood. Long-term is to get what's ours.
Black Fist: When it gets right down to kill or be killed, no one can match Class War's intensity. All wrestlers have come to respect and many more fear this team. They know who we are and they shuffle their feet when we step in the place. Class War has no focus but to f*#$ people up.
We have no remorse and no regard. Reporter: Black Fist, you briefly held the DWA Internet belt and lost it quickly. Any plans to regain it?
Black Fist: Serial KiLLa's typical of the lame-ass wrestlers around here. Half the grapplers here were only smart enough to come up with one name. Others had to steal a name from people -- so far, there are three other wrestlers in other feds who stole names from Wu Tang Clan: Raekwon, GZA, and RZA. Now this chump steps up and runs a crock of how he's the best.
It's truly sad. To answer your question, though, Colby will tell you after tonight if I get a chance to get my belt.
Reporter: Hey, I thought you guys were supposed to be accompanied on all
DWA premises by President Colby?
Monster: We are, but we jumped him and tied him up in his office with a pen taped in his mouth and a contract on his desk. It's for the DWA's first shootfight with Black Fist against Serial KiLLa for the Internet belt. It'll be in a UFC-style shoot cage and winner can only be decided by submission or if the referee stops the fight. Colby found out tonight how serious we are and Serial KiLLa will too. That's why we're here.
Black Fist: We stand in front of Serial KiLLa's auto. And we've decided to do him a favor and redecorate this hooptie.
(Monster and Black Fist smash out Serial KiLLa's windshield and grille. Reporters back away in shock as the pair punch holes in the car's body with the tips of the crowbars. Laughing, Monster and Black Fist sit back and wave the reporters closer...)
Monster: Now, I'm sure clowns like Firewalker are gonna whine that we're doing some sleazy stunts. Too bad. Class War means business and everyone here is finding that out. Serial KiLLa's method of showing what a hard guy he is has been bragging on a wrestling newsgroup. We've proven it in the ring. This little stunt was just to get his attention and to remind
Colby we ain't his kids.
Black Fist: We're out. ----- Julie: The Serial KiLLa has to be furious! Garrett: Not quite, as you’ll see with his response! -----ROLL TAPE: SERIAL KILLA----- Serial KiLLa: Class War I ain't complainin' about that broken windshield besides I was going to get me a brand new car anyway. But Class War you seem to have a major problem up your a**. So since you think that I'm not as good as I claim to be how'd you like me too prove it to you in the ring.????
----- Garrett: The submission shoot match has been signed, and Kuwasi Shakur will get his chance against the Serial KiLLa at Friday Night Fights on
May 31st! The Internet Title will be on the line if the Serial KiLLa can defeat Bane tonight and defeat Eddy Gonzalez at the MEMORIAL DAY
MASSACRE. Julie: That will be a challenge for the KiLLa! Gonzalez is HUGE! Garrett: And don’t forget Bane, perhaps the most ruthless wrestler in the
DWA! They’ll lock up tonight in our feature match.
Julie: But first, let’s go to the DWA Commando Center for results from the matches held earlier tonight.
-----DWA COMMANDO CENTER----- Round Four Matches from the DWA Intercontinental Championship Tournament
Lightning (w/ Tommy J. Dynomite)
versus Eddy Gonzalez (w/ Hurly Burly) The big man, Eddy Gonzalez, was favored in this match that pitted strength against speed. But his previous defeat to the Bounty Hunter still clouded his mind, and Lightning dropped him with a flying clothesline.
Your winner- Lightning Eddy Gonzalez is eliminated from the tournament. Lord Byron (w/ King Benny II) versus Firewalker (w/ Dr. Insane) Lord Byron tried every trick in the book to stop Firewalker, but this time the native American would not be denied. Firewalker pinned Byron after a tremendous Frankensteiner.
Your winner- Firewalker Lord Byron has been eliminated from the tournament. Vinnie (w/ John Pathos) versus The Bounty Hunter Vinnie continues on in the tournament with a stunningly swift victory over the Bounty Hunter. A hard right to the jaw dropped the Hunter as he came out of his corner, and Vinnie tied him up with the figure four for the submission.
Your winner by submission- Vinnie The Bounty Hunter has been eliminated from the tournament. “Acid” Andrew Downey (w/ “Neon” Leon Parker) versus Bane (w/ Jon Robinson) Prior to the match, Parker and Robinson got into a scuffle at ringside, which resulted in both managers being escorted to the showers by the DWA
Enforcers. Bane seemed lost without his manager, and Downey took advantage of the situation by viciously attacking Bane’s lower back with a chair. Bane was pinned after a horrendous backbreaker, and had to be carried from the ring by the medical staff.
Your winner- “Acid” Andrew Downey Bane has been eliminated from the tournament. Thunder (w/ Tommy J. Dynomite) versus Masked Mayhem 1 (w/ "Legend" Josh Brown) Masked Mayhem 1 fell to the power of Thunder in short order, as Thunder pounded the masked man’s body with a series of body slams. Thunder pinned
Mayhem 1 after a show stopping elevated suplex from the second turnbuckle.
Your winner- Thunder Masked Mayhem 1 has been eliminated from the tournament. In Non-tournament Singles Action- Poseidon (w/ Thantos Polopolous) versus Dr. Joe Slasher, M.D. Poseidon was ambushed by Slasher in the locker room, and hobbled to the ring with a sore left knee. Slasher continued his welcome of the newcomer by working on the knee, slamming it into the ringpost several times.
Poseidon fell to the arena floor and was counted out. Your winner- Dr. Joe Slasher, M.D. Vinnie (w/ John Pathos) versus Lightning (w/ Tommy J. Dynomite) In a preview of a possible Intercontinental Tournament match, Vinnie took the early lead and was on the verge of pinning Lightning when Thunder interfered. Thunder and Lightning plummeled Vinnie at ringside until a mysterious masked newcomer appeared and dropped Thunder with a high flying savate kick. Vinnie was still groggy when he climbed back into the ring, and Lightning dropped him with the DDT.
Your winner- Lightning ----- Julie: Who was that masked newcomer? He appeared from nowhere, and disappeared just as fast!
Garrett: I don’t know, but it seemed like Vinnie knew him! It’s a good thing that Vinnie won the match that counted, since round four eliminated a lot of DWA Superstars!
Julie: That’s right, but now it gets even more exciting, as the remaining wrestlers have two rounds to eliminate their opponents. If after the sixth round more than two wrestlers remain, the final match becomes a battle royal!
Garrett: The remaining participants are “Acid” Andrew Downey, Firewalker,
Lightning, the Serial KiLLa, Thunder, and Vinnie. I’ll put my money on the former DWA World Champion, the Original Jersey Boy!
Julie: He didn’t do so well in his head to head matchup with Lightning, but I suspect that the champ will be back in form for his next tournament match!
Garrett: Speaking of champs, let’s go to the ring for tonight’s DWA
Internet Tag Team Title match!
Scott Meridian: Ladies and gentlemen, this next match is for the DWA
Internet Tag Team Championship belts! Introducing first, the challengers.
Accompanied to the ring by DWA President Shelton Colby, weighing a combined 577 pounds, here are Kuwasi “Black Fist” Shakur and Monster
Lucas Masai- CLASS WAR!
Class War enters the arena to the chant of “shoot, Fist, shoot”. Colby tries to calm the crowd, but Shakur shoves him aside and raises his fists in the air, which causes the crowd to explode! Colby takes up position at ringside as Class War leaps into the ring.
Scott: And now the reigning DWA Internet Tag Team Champions! Accompanied to the ring by the Godfather, and weighing a combined 530 pounds, here are the Big Town Bully and the Hitman- 911!
911 is greeted by the “shoot, Fist, shoot” chant, and an altercation ensues between the Hitman and a fan wearing a “Black Fist in Yo Face”
T-shirt. The Godfather steps in, pulling the Hitman off of the bloodied fan, who throws his tattered, bloody shirt into the ring. Shakur grabs the shirt and twirls it around his head as he flips off the Hitman. The crowd goes wild!
The bell rings, and the Hitman charges Shakur, driving him into the ringpost. Shakur retaliates with a knee to the groin that leaves the
Hitman writhing in pain. Colby attempts to intercede, but Masai holds him back as Shakur repeatedly kicks the Hitman in the groin. The Big Town
Bully rushes into the ring, but misses Shakur with a flying clothesline and flies headlong into Colby, knocking him out.
The Godfather attempts to enter the ring, but he’s pulled from the apron by Masai. Masai nails him with a heart punch that sends him flying over the barricade.
Masai climbs the top turnbuckle and drops an elbow on the Hitman’s head as Shakur holds him down. The two begin plummeling the Hitman, oblivious to the demands of the referee. The Nomads rush out of the locker room and chase Class War from the ring as referee calls for the bell.
Scott: The referee has disqualified Class War! Your winners, and still
DWA Internet Tag Team Champions- 911!
Julie: Well, it looks like Class War is out of control again! Garrett: I really doubted that he could control them for long! It’s up to him now what sanctions are levied against those two. Here he comes now!
President Colby, welcome to the booth! Shelton Colby: Thanks, Garrett. I’m still a little groggy, so please be patient. Garrett: So what is going on with Class War? I thought that you had them under control. Colby: So did I, but now I see that simple discipline won’t work on these wrestlers. So I’ll hit them where it hurts, in the bank account. Class
War will not be allowed to wrestle in the upcoming DWA Challenge Event-
Memorial Day Massacre!
Julie: Will Shakur still get a title shot against the Serial KiLLa at
Friday Night Fights?
Colby: That contract is signed, and I will honor it. But after that I can not guarantee the Class War will ever wrestle in the DWA again!
Garrett: Thanks, president Colby. Speaking of the Serial KiLLa, his title defense is about to begin! Let’s go back to the ring!
Scott: Ladies and gentlemen, this next bout is for the DWA Internet
Championship belt! First up, the challenger. Accompanied to the ring by his manager, Jon Robinson, from Toronto, standing 7’ 2” tall and weighing
378 pounds, this is BANE!
The Sportatorium crowd goes silent with fear as Bane enters. Robinson leads the way to the ring, talking trash about the “next DWA World
Champion”. Scott: And now the champion! From San Antonio, Texas, standing 6’ 9” tall and weighing 298 pounds, the reigning DWA Internet Champion- THE SERIAL
KILLA! The Serial KiLLa enters the arena to a standing ovation, and rushes down the aisle to the ring. He grabs a Texas flag from a nearby fan, climbs to the top turnbuckle, and waves the flag to the crowd. Bane begins sneaking up on him, but the KiLLa flips over Bane’s head and on to the canvas. The official calls for the bell and the match begins.
Bane takes an early advantage, dropping the KiLLa with a flying fist. The
KiLLa recovers and retaliates with an arm drag takedown into an arm bar.
Bane gouges his eyes and breaks the hold. The referee steps in and issues a warning to Bane.
The Serial KiLLa, still partially blinded by the gouge, charges Bane. Bane steps aside, and the KiLLa hits the ringpost hard. Bane dives at him from behind, and nails him in the back of the knees. The KiLLa collapses in pain and rolls to the arena floor.
Jon Robinson kicks the KiLLa several times as Bane climbs to the top rope. Bane dives from the top rope, but misses the KiLLa and hits the floor hard. The KiLLa drags Bane to his feet, and DDTs him on to a ringside table. Robinson attacks the KiLLa with a chair, knocking him away from Bane.
The Serial KiLLa climbs into the ring as Robinson assists Bane to his feet. The KiLLa catapults himself over the top rope and comes crashing down on Bane, driving him back into and through the table. A huge gash opens over Bane’s left eye as he hits the table.
Both wrestlers stagger back into the ring and lock up. The KiLLa pushes
Bane into the corner and begins pounding on his head. Robinson slips Bane a set of brass knuckles, and Bane nails the KiLLa with them, busting the
KiLLa’s lip.
The official sees the brass knuckles, and calls for the bell. Bane tries to hit the KiLLa again, but the referee separates them. Robinson drags
Bane from the ring.
Scott: Your winner by disqualification, and still DWA Internet Champion- the Serial KiLLa! Garrett: Another disqualification! It looks like the DWA has finally gotten serious about the rules!
Julie: Colby had to take control of the situation after that Texas Death
Chamber match between Vinnie and Dr. Joe Slasher, otherwise someone would have eventually been maimed, or worse!
Garrett: Hopefully he can keep it under control at the Memorial Day
Massacre, our next show! Until then, this is Garrett Montgomery...
Julie: And Julie Cooper saying “good-bye” from the Sportatorium. We’ll close with a special interview with Suna “Lovely to Touch” Gib! See you next week!
-----ROLL TAPE: SUNA GIB----- Julie Cooper: I'm here with Suna "Lovely to Touch" Gib outside the WAR! HQ. Mr. Gib, may I call you Suna? Suna: Suna yes, sauna no. I am Suna Gib. You can call me Suna. Yes. Julie: Suna, you apparently have some history with Lord Byron. Could you give us a brief description of your relationship?
Suna: Jealousy. He was jealous of my... friendship... with Bane. He feels that Bane was getting in the way of a... friendship... between me and him. I can understand his feelings. But I told him he had to wait.
He didn’t like that at all. Julie: How does Lord Byron feel about you? Suna: I think deep down he still loves me. He tries to hide it. I know that it is me doing the running now, but I hope he will see reason and be my... friend...
Julie: You recently left a surprise for Byron in the DWA locker room. Do you have any other surprises up your sleeve?
Suna: Love is full of surprises Julie: Any last comments? Suna: Please, Byron, answer my calls. You know it is for the best. Julie: Thank you for your time, Suna. copyright 1996, the Digital Wrestling Alliance, all rights reserved
