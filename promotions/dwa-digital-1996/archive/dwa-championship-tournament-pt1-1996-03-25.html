DWA — Pay-Per-View

DWA Championship Tournament (Part 1 of 4)

25 March 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The darkness in the arena is punctuated by the light of ten thousand lighters as "Right Now" by Van Halen begins. A huge video screen comes to life, replaying scenes from the history of the DWA- Vinnie's triumphant victory in the first World Championship tournament- Sinister Sam smashing his guitar over the head of Storm- Richard Cranium getting slammed, and slammed, and slammed again!
The house lights burst on as the video screen explodes, revealing a flaming DWA logo! Garrett Montgomery: The Digital Wrestling Alliance is back and rocking
Reunion Arena in Dallas, Texas! I'm your host, Garrett Montgomery, and with me tonight is none other than Johnny X from the Street Punks of the
ESWP. Welcome to the DWA Championship Tournament, Johnny!
Johnny X: That's Mr. X to you, dipstick! I'm just here to check out some of the jobbers in this second rate fed.
Garrett: Well, if the preliminary action we've seen tonight is any indication of the talent level in the DWA, there aren't any jobbers here.
This federation is chock full of talented, determined wrestlers, as you'll see in my first interview with the notorious Lord Byron. Let's go to the tape.
----- Garrett: At this time, I have a newcomer to the DWA with me, alongside his manager King Benny, Lord Byron.
Byron enters the set, dressed in his ring attire and carrying his brass topped cane. King Benny arrives, dressed in an Armani suit.
Garrett: Excuse me, er... King Benny: You may address me as your Highness, and Lord Byron as sir. Garrett: Very well, er, sir, what are your plans against the WarHawk? Lord Byron: I'm here to win the championship tonight. WarHawk does not interest me, it's Vinnie who should be looking over his shoulder.
Garrett: You still have to get through the tournament, though... Byron gives the interviewer an irritated glance. King Benny: One knows that Lord Byron will have to face some ignorant peons before Vinnie, but one is also confident of Byron's stamina and technical skill. He is more than prepared for this tournament, and for the WarHawk!
Lord Byron: WarHawk, you may have come here with some jumped up little notion that you can beat me and progress to the next round, but let me tell you this: No-one, and I mean no-one, is going to stop me from taking the world championship tonight. Not you, not Vinnie, not any other of the peons involved. Ciao.
Byron and King Benny leave the dressing room area. Garrett: Well, Lord Byron is obviously psyched up for this tournament, but only time will tell if he truly has what it takes to be DWA World
Champion.
WarHawk walks on to the set. Garrett: WarHawk, what a surprise! Do you have any comments for Lord
Byron? WarHawk: Lord Byron...are you a real warrior? Or are you another RedCoat? I am a warrior and I'm here to fight other warriors. Will you fight me one-on-one or will you bring your "manager" into the arena as well? I come alone and will fight you. I have conditioned myself for many fights...my muscles cry out for hard fought wrestling... I look forward to our match and the others who I will fight in this tournament...
WarHawk walks off the set. Garrett: There you have it. The challenge has been issued. This one will be settled tonight!
----- Johnny X: That Lord Byron is my kind of wrestler! Confident and... Garrett: Pompous! Let's go to the DWA Command Center for highlights from the first round. ----- Results of the first round action in the DWA World Championship Tournament ----- Johnny "Sidewinder" Missle vs. Coyote Missle took an early lead in this matchup, but missed a flying drop kick from the top rope and hit the mat hard. Coyote took advantage of the mistake and pinned the disoriented Sidewinder.
Winner- Coyote ----- Lord Byron (w/King Benny II) vs. WarHawk King Benny II nailed WarHawk as he entered the ring, opening a huge gash above his left eye. WarHawk appeared to gain strength from the bloodflow and was able to counter all of Lord Byron's moves. WarHawk finally stopped Byron with the TomaHawk, a submission backbreaker hold.
Winner by Submission- WarHawk ----- Brian Damage vs. Dr. Joe Slasher, M.D. The evil Dr. Slasher eliminated Brian with surgical precision and questionable tactics. Slasher got the pin while grabbing the trunks of the crowd favorite Damage.
Winner- Dr. Joe Slasher, M.D. ----- Misery (w/Dr. Insane) vs. The Serial KiLLa Misery looked to be the favorite in this match, but the Serial KiLLa kept it close for the first fifteen minutes. The breakthrough for the KiLLa came when Misery missed on a flying clothesline and became tangled in the ropes. The KiLLa capitalized on Misery's misery by hammering the trapped wrestler with a series of elbow smashes. The Serial KiLLa got the pin with a powerbomb of the weakened Misery.
Winner- The Serial KiLLa ----- Garrett: The Serial KiLLa moves on to the second round to face Dr. Joe
Slasher, M.D., and Coyote will mix it up with WarHawk.
Johnny X: Lord Byron was robbed! WarHawk cheated! I demand a recount! Garrett: Actually it was Byron who cheated, and recounts are only used in elections! Lord Byron will get a second chance at a title in the first round of the Internet Championships, coming up next!
Johnny X: Byron, Byron, Byron! ----- Stay tuned for Part 2 of the DWA Championship Tournament!
