Tuesday Night Terrors

11 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Digital Wrestling Alliance presents A Friday Night Fights Special Edition- TUESDAY NIGHT TERRORS! Taped at the DWA Headquarters, the Sportatorium in Dallas, Texas. Garrett Montgomery: Hello, wrestling fans, and welcome to a special edition of Friday Night Fights, Tuesday Night Terrors! Tonight we'll see highlights of the three first round matches in the DWA Tag Team
Tournament, as well as four challenge matches. But first, the news with our man in the ring correspondent, referee Chuck "Slow Count" Worthy.
Chuck? Chuck "Slow Count" Worthy: Thanks, Garrett. Cover-up, or just an oversight? Although denied by DWA officials, the following news release appears to have been covered up in an attempt to shield the public from negative publicity about the young federation.
-----Cue Teleprompter---- By Eric McMahon Associated Press Writer Police are appealing to the public for leads in locating those responsible in a case of aggravated assault on an area man whose knees were apparently broken with baseball bats.
The man, whose name authorities refused to release, is reported to be the father of professional wrestler Vinnie The Original Jersey Boy, who is currently Digital Wrestling Alliance world champion.
Area police have issued warrants for two other professional wrestlers,
Lucas Masai and Kuwasi Shakur, who wrestle under the names Monster Lucas
Masai and Kuwasi "Black Fist" Shakur respectively. The men were seen in the vicinity moments before the attack. No witnesses have come forward to identify the pair as the attackers.
-----End Teleprompter---- Chuck: It was apparent to me that Vinnie was not himself last Friday night when he lost his cherished title to the less than spectacular
Johnny Missle. Could this be the reason for his loss? I spoke with Class
War after last Friday’s matches, and here’s what they had to say.
-----Roll Tape: Chuck with Class War---- Chuck: I'm here in the DWA to talk with Kuwasi "Black Fist" Shakur and
Monster Lucas Masai about some recent controversies, the upcoming tag tournament and a few other items. First off guys, a lot of people -- particularly the police -- want to know your involvement in the attack on Vinnie, the Original Jersey Boy's father?
Monster Lucas Masai: Other than that our lawyers are negotiating extradition, we can't really comment on that whole thing....
Kuwasi "Black Fist" Shakur: Except to say that Jersey Girl and anyone who steps to us can expect the same thing!
Monster: Hey, shut up! Black Fist: Oops. Chuck: You two will be competing in the upcoming tag team tournament as
Class War, but you also wanted to address some of the singles wrestlers here?
Black Fist: I've got one punk in mind. He and I go back a long way and I haven't forgotten you either, squirrel nuts! Don't even go ducking behind that manager of yours because I see you -- and when I get you in the ring, your ass in mine again! Who is it? BANE!!! Did you really think you'd be able to bail the GWF and escape me? I've got news for you, son. No more hiding behind your old crew, the Death
Unit. No more squabbling about how you'll kick my ass but never facing me. You and I will meet again, and it'll end up like it did last time-- with you on your back and cross-eyed from my fist in your face!
Monster: Chuck, we've made a lot of enemies over our careers, but we're not gonna let that distract us from the tag team tournament. We'll be wrestling as singles but our goal here is to win the straps here in the DWA. What makes us unbeatable is our experience as singles athletes and as a team. Class War is a machine no one can stop and, come this tag title tournament, you have no doubt who will end up on top.
Chuck: What does the future hold for Class War? Black Fist: Confrontation. And chaos. Monster: Victory. -----End Tape---- Chuck: Hear more about this alleged cover-up on the Slow Count Hotline- dial 1-900-12THREE. It'll cost you one slim dollar a minute- kids, get your parents permission before calling. Back to you, Garrett!
Garrett: Thanks, Chuck. Class War will be featured in both singles and tag action tonight, and Vinnie is rumored to be in the Sportatorium looking for them. Dr. Joe Slasher, M.D is here tonight as well, to respond to the challenge of Apollo.
-----Roll Tape: Apollo/Slasher----- Apollo: Slasher!!! I want a piece of you. You ruined my debut so now you will pay!! You are truly pathetic. You disrupt Vinnie during our match and then go on to get pummeled in your own. Let me tell you what's up doc. You and me, steel cage, anytime, anyplace!!!
Dr. Joe Slasher, M.D.: (laughs) Well, fool, if this is your wish, then how about at the DWA's earliest convenience?? It will be a pleasure to destroy you inside of a cage as well as show that idiot Vinnie what's in store for him!!!
-----End Tape----- Garrett: Wow! The first DWA cage match, but not the only one tonight! Bane and Kuwasi "Black Fist" Shakur will also mix it up in the cage. Let's see what Bane’s manager has to say in this prerecorded interview. -----Roll Tape: Bane----- (Bane is working in the cage over two jobbers. Jon is nearby he holds
Bane's World RAW title.)
Jon Roberson: So, Black head zit thing...or what ever you call your self! You want to challenge the mighty Bane to a cage match? Well that's really beneath Bane, perhaps we up the ante?????? Maybe you herd of the
Lights out match???? Where the only way too win is to knock your opponent out?? Of course not, because you don't carry the caliber of Bane to participate in such a fight!!! OR MAYBE IT'S BECAUSE YOU
SUUUUUCCCCKKKKKKKK!!!!! Oh, sorry, did I lower myself to your standards.......well, yes I did lower myself beyond scum such as yourself, but if you feel you have the balls to step into a career ending match....THEN LET'S SEE HOW BIG THOSE BALLS ARE BABY!!!!!!! THEN YOU WILL
SEE YOURSELF LOSE TO THE REVOLUTIONARY FORCE IN THE DWA!!!! And to the
DWA officials, the winner gets a shot at that pretty little belt.....now what's it called??? umm ah yes the world title, Bane wants to add another trophy to his collection. HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HAAAAAAA!!!!
(Bane knocks out the jobber in the cage with ease, he then takes Dark
Destroyers mask which is always tied around his wrist and lifts up his
mask just long enough to wipe his face with it. He draws his thumb from one end of his neck to the other.) -----End Tape----- Garrett: Bane raises the stakes with a Lights Out match! Let's go to the
DWA Commando Center for highlights of this match and others.
-----DWA Commando Center----- In a special Lights Out Cage Match- Kuwasi "Black Fist" Shakur (w/ Monster Lucas Masai) vs. Bane (w/ Jon Roberson) Before the match started, both wrestlers were required to sign injury waivers by the DWA President, Shelton Colby. Shakur entered the cage first, and slammed the cage door in Bane's face, which opened a gash over his left eye. Bane seemed excited by the sight of his own blood, and promptly slammed his bloody forehead into Shakur's. Masai attempted to crawl over the top of the cage, but Roberson grabbed his ankle and held on. Shakur whipped Bane into the corner of the cage, but Bane rebounded with a tremendous uppercut that drove Shakur into the opposite corner.
Bane tossed the dazzled Shakur over his shoulder and climbed to the top of the cage. Holding on with one hand, Bane executed a modified vertical suplex, and Shakur crashed hard down to the mat, out cold.
Your winner- Bane The Serial KiLLa vs. Wasp Man (replaced Brian Damage) Damage failed to show up for this match, so Wasp Man stepped in and gave the Serial KiLLa a lesson in technical wrestling. After a series of press slams, Wasp Man caught the KiLLa in a figure four leglock for the submission. After the match Brian Damage was found naked, handcuffed to a toilet in the ladies restroom. An investigation is forthcoming.
Your winner by submission- Wasp Man Monster Lucas Masai vs. The Bounty Hunter In the strangest match of the night, the Bounty Hunter entered the arena and was immediately confronted by what appeared to be a very large woman.
The Bounty Hunter was smitten by the "woman", and offered to escort her to a ringside seat. As they approached the arena, the "woman" rammed the
Bounty Hunter into the ringpost, knocking him out. After a brief tease, the dress came off, revealing Monster Lucas Masai, who dragged the Bounty
Hunter into the ring for the pin.
Your winner- Monster Lucas Masai In a cage match- Apollo (w/ Thantos Polopoulos) vs. Dr. Joe Slasher, M.D. Apollo caught the Doctor sleeping, and wrapped him up in a small package for the quick victory. Slasher was incensed, and attacked Apollo from behind with a chair. Vinnie raced from the locker rooms and nailed
Slasher with a kick to the head, and Slasher retreated. Vinnie and Thantos assisted the dazed Apollo from the arena. Your winner- Apollo ----- Garrett: Things are really heating up on the road to April Anarchy! We've got three more matches to come, but first some comments from Jason
Andrews and Coyote. -----Roll Tape: Andrews----- (From a tape sent to the DWA office) The scene: An empty wrestling ring in Sportatorium. A tall, well-built brown haired man in a gray suit walks towards the ring, then turns to smile into the camera. He hops up onto the ring apron, then steps through the ropes and walks to the middle of the ring.
An unseen hand off to his right hands him a microphone. The man takes it with a nod of thanks and looks into the camera.
Jason Andrews: Some of you will know who I am, and some of you won't. My name is Jason Andrews, and I'm new to the DWA. I've been in wrestling for several years, but this is my first opportunity to wrestle here.
Jason: My goal is, of course, to win titles. I'm not a flashy wrestler, and I don't wear flashy costumes or call myself by some silly name. I just like to get in the ring, get the job done, win the titles, and go home.
Jason: Whoever I have to wrestle, I'll wrestle. It doesn't matter, because I'm going to win, and then move on up the ladder. Whoever it is will be stepping stone number one on my way to the world heavyweight championship.
Jason: DWA, I'm here. Even if you don't yet know the name Jason Andrews, you will soon. And you'll never forget it, and that I guarantee.
-----Roll Tape: Coyote----- (From a tape anonymously sent to the offices of DWA.) As the tape begins to roll, you see a lonely country road somewhere, bordering on the edge of the woods. Birds chirp and the sound of a nearby stream rolls on in the distance. Branches begin to move in the woods, as something comes closer and closer. Finally, the branches part way and someone steps through.
The camera pans up from his black wrestling boots past his green trunks, past his muscular yet agile looking frame, to rest on the visage of a
6'0" tall man wearing black warpaint. The man smiles, then walks towards the camera. He takes a microphone from whoever is behind the camera and begins to speak.
Coyote: Hello, DWA. My name is Coyote, and I'm coming to your federation, and I'm going to take your titles.
His steely gaze, confident and calm but with a hidden fire burning just beneath the surface, adds credence to his words.
Coyote: You may or may not have heard of me. I don't care. I don't have any friends on either side of the fence, and I'm not looking forward to making any anytime soon. What I want - and what I'll get - is plain and simple; the DWA World Championship. That's why I'm coming to the DWA.
You may not know me now but rest assured, you'll never again forget me. With those words, Coyote drops the microphone on the ground, turns back towards the woods, and is gone. The tape continues to roll for another
30 seconds, showing the woods, and then fades to black.
-----End Tape----- Garrett: Andrews and Coyote, two more superstars joining the DWA! Speaking of superstars, the DWA will crown it's World Tag Team Champions at April Anarchy. A double elimination tournament will determine the final two teams, and that tournament begins today! Let's go to the
Commando Center for highlights from the three first round matches. -----DWA Commando Center----- First Round Action- DWA Tag Team Championship Tournament Burly Bunch (w/ Hurly Burly) vs. Class War The Burly Bunch didn't last long once Class War came out swinging. Masai pinned Damage after a backbreaker, then added insult to injury by handcuffing him to the ringpost.
Your winners- Class War 911 (w/ the Godfather) vs. Thunder and Lightning (w/ Tommy J. Dynomite) The Godfather and Dynomite got into it before the match and were escorted to the locker room. Newcomers 911 got their first win after Thunder missed a giant splash and hit the mat hard. Big Town Bully covered him for the victory.
Your winners- 911 Pain Factory (w/ "Neon" Leon Parker) vs. The Nomads (w/ Dr. Insane) In the longest match of the night, newcomers Pain Factory pull off the upset when "Acid" Andrew Downey dropped Misery with a solid right hook.
In a sign of mutual respect, both teams shook hands after this ironman bout. Your winners- Pain Factory ----- Garrett: So Pain Factory, 911, and Class War all get first round victories. But the other teams aren't out of luck yet, since it's a double elimination tournament! The second round matches will take place on this week's Friday Night Fights- see you then!
