Friday Night Fights! (4/9)

9 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Digital Wrestling Alliance presents FRIDAY NIGHT FIGHTS! Live from the Gulf Coast Coliseum in Biloxi, Mississippi! Garrett Montgomery: Welcome back to FRIDAY NIGHT FIGHTS! I'm your host,
Garrett Montgomery, and I'll be bringing you the action tonight along with my co-host and ESWP World Champion, the Delinquent! Welcome to the
DWA! The Delinquent: Uh, thanks. When can I pick up my check? Garrett: I'll let you know. Tonight we'll see the battle between two native Americans, as WarHawk challenges Firewalker for the Internet
Championship. And by contractual obligation, Johnny "Sidewinder" Missle will challenge the reigning DWA World Champion Vinnie for his belt.
The Delinquent: Wait, there's only one World Champion, me! Garrett: Wrong, my academically challenged friend. Your title won't get you a cup of coffee at a DWA match! Stop the presses- Dr. Insane and his boys are in the DWA press room. Let's go to the Nomads...
----- DWA Press Room ----- Firewalker: I THANK THOSE THAT HAVE COME BEFORE ME FOR THE OPPORTUNITIES
THAT I HAVE HAD. (DISPLAYS THE CHAMPIONSHIP BELT) NOW, I MUST DEFEND
THIS BELT AGAINST ONE WHO HAS THE SAME BLOOD AS I. TO MY FANS I WILL SAY THIS, I BELIEVE THAT THE GODS WILL DETERMINE HOW LONG I WILL HAVE THIS BELT. MY DESTINY AT THE MOMENT IS TO BE THE BEST THAT I CAN BE! Misery: MANY MEN HAVE COME HERE HOPING TO BE THE BEST! BOUNTY HUNTER, I
CAN ASSURE YOU THAT THERE WILL BE ONE BOUNTY THAT YOU WILL NOT BE ABLE TO
COLLECT ON AND THAT IS THE ONE THAT MAY BE ON MY HEAD! Dr. Insane: My men have come to ensure that justice is served here! ----- Garrett: Strong words from Firewalker. Let's roll those prerecorded comments from WarHawk and Dr. Slasher.
----- DWA Command Center ----- WarHawk: You are a powerful warrior Firewalker! I look forward to our match on Friday night. May the strongest warrior win! I promise you. I will give you a fair fight, but it will be a long match! See you on
Friday my friend!
----- Dr. Joe Slasher, M.D.: What is this garbage?? a 1-3 record gets a title shot at this idiot so called champion we have?? Vinnie you can duck the
Doctor only so long by defending to these unworthy fools for I'm a patient man but WHEN I get you in the ring one more time you'll truly see where my patience runs out!!!
----- The Delinquent: That Slasher guy is really hacked about something! Garrett: He seems to think that Vinnie is ducking him, but in reality tonight's match with Johnny Missle is based upon contractual obligations, not rankings. We'll get to the match soon, but first let's go back to the
DWA Command Center for highlights of tonight's earlier action.
-----DWA Command Center----- Monster Lucas Masai vs. Dr. Joe Slasher, M.D. Dr. Joe operated on Masai early, dropping him with a DDT. Slasher got greedy, and was climbing the ropes for the kill when Masai's partner,
Kuwasi "Black Fist" Shakur, shook the ropes. Slasher fell to the apron, where Shakur began pounding him with a chair. Masai recovered, and dragged the collapsed Slasher into the ring for the pin.
Your winner- Monster Lucas Masai Kuwasi "Black Fist" Shakur vs. Brian Damage (w/ Hurly Burly) Shakur was overconfident after the beating he gave Dr. Joe Slasher, and stepped right into Damage's Death Sentence, a tremendous flying clothesline that flipped the Fist into the air. Shakur was knocked unconscious upon impact with the mat, and Damage got the easy pin.
Your winner- Brian Damage Thunder (w/ Tommy J. Dynomite) vs. Apollo Thunder, in his first singles appearance, was attacked by Class War on the way to the ring. Shakur and Masai attempted to ignite Thunder with a flaming hairspray can, but the fire marshall intervened and escort the tag team from the building. The attempt enraged Thunder, and he took revenge on Apollo, dropping him for the pin with a Thunder Crash, a variation of the superplex.
Your winner- Thunder Lightning (w/ Tommy J. Dynomite) vs. The Serial KiLLa Thunder's partner was equally impressive in singles competition, as he punished the Serial KiLLa with repeated choke slams. The Serial KiLLa attempted to mount an offense, but Lightning retaliated with a flying fist off the top rope, dropping the KiLLa.
Your winner- Lightning The Bounty Hunter vs. Misery Misery came out like a wild man, dropping the Bounty Hunter with successive roundhouse rights to the temple. The Bounty Hunter, fearing yet another loss, resorted to questionable tactics, and connected with a kick to the groin that left Misery unable to continue. The official was swayed by the apologies of the Bounty Hunter, and awarded him the win.
Your winner- the Bounty Hunter Bane (w/ Jon Robinson) vs. Wasp Man These two wrestlers had a score to settle from last week, and battled for almost one hour before Bane got the upper hand with a tremendous suplex slam. Bane finished Wasp Man with his Death Drop, a painful jackknife powerslam. Jon Robinson entered the ring after the victory and encouraged
Bane to continue beating Wasp Man. Vinnie emerged from the locker room area and chased Bane out of the ring.
Your winner- Bane ----- Garrett: Class War is out of control! I would expect some kind of suspension is in order after the attacks on Vinnie's dad and Thunder.
The Delinquent: Yeah, those guys are bad news! I've seen them up close and personal in the ESWP, and I was impressed!
Garrett: Impressed! Please... If you want to see impressive, look down at the ring, where Firewalker and WarHawk are set to battle for the DWA
Internet Championship!
Scott Meridian: Ladies and gentlemen, the following bout is for the DWA
Internet Championship! Introducing first, the challenger, from Talequah,
Oklahoma, standing 6' 3" tall and tipping the scales at a lean 235 pounds, WARHAWK!
The crowd responds wildly to the popular native American as he wardances his way to the ring.
Scott: And now, accompanied to the ring by his manager Dr. Insane, from the plains of Wyoming, weighing in at 285 pounds and standing 6' 8" tall, your DWA Internet Champion, FIREWALKER!
The crowd bursts into cheers as the reigning champion enters the arena, followed closely by Dr. Insane. His look is intense, and as he enters the ring WarHawk approaches. The two men eye each other carefully, and
Firewalker extends his hand. After a brief handshake, the bell rings and the match begins. Firewalker starts an offensive, slinging WarHawk into the ropes and catching him with a knee to the midsection. Firewalker attempts a DDT, but WarHawk reverses it into a snap suplex. Firewalker tries to roll up
WarHawk into a small package, but WarHawk bridges out of it. WarHawk tries to engage a figure four leglock, but Firewalker manages to reach the ropes and the hold is broken.
Just as both men are rising to their feet, Monster Lucas Masai emerges from under the ring and begins plummeling Dr. Insane with a baseball bat.
Firewalker leaps from the ring, but misses Masai and crashes into the barricade. Masai turns the bat on Firewalker, but WarHawk stops him from behind. Masai drops the bat as WarHawk applies a full nelson. Firewalker grabs the bat, and nails Masai in the gut. Masai falls to the floor as both men climb back into the ring. Security arrives and escorts the breathless Monster out of the building.
WarHawk and Firewalker lock up again. WarHawk breaks the stalemate with a hip toss, then drops an elbow on Firewalker's skull. Firewalker arises disoriented, and WarHawk drops him with a flying drop kick. WarHawk pins the prone Firewalker for the victory.
Scott: Your winner, and new DWA Internet Champion- WARHAWK! Garrett: What a match! Aside from the interference from Masai, this was a battle for the ages!
The Delinquent: Interference? He was just a spectator! Those guys just beat up an innocent man!
Garrett: Innocent is not a word I'd ever use to describe Monster Lucas
Masai! In any event, I suspect the disagreement between Masai and
Firewalker will continue to simmer until they meet in the tag team tournament. The Delinquent: Oh, no, here comes the pretend champion... Garrett: Let's go back to the ring for our final match of the night- Johnny "Sidewinder" Missle vs. the Original Jersey Boy, Vinnie! Scott: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is for all the marbles- the
Digital Wrestling Alliance WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP! Introducing first, the challenger. Standing 6' 5" tall, weighing 275 pounds, this wrestler is from the Great White North, Winnepeg, Manitoba, CANADA! Please welcome to the ring, the Canadian daredevil, JOHNNY "SIDEWINDER" MISSLE!!!
Johnny Missle enters the arena to a lukewarm crowd response, which begins to build as he shakes with the fans. By the time he hits the ring, the response has built to a respectable level for the relatively unknown wrestler.
Scott: And his opponent, from Basking Ridge, New Jersey. Weighing 315 pounds and standing 6' 11" tall, the DWA World Champion, THE ORIGINAL
JERSEY BOY- VINNIE!!! The lights dim one, two, three times as the crowd explodes into a frenzy. Vinnie enters the arena, surrounded by security. Fans reach for him, but the security team keeps them away from the obviously distracted wrestler.
Vinnie enters the squared circle and the match begins. Johnny and Vinnie lock up, and Johnny breaks the lock with an arm drag takedown. He begins working on Vinnie's arm, pinning it hard behind
Vinnie's back. Vinnie twists out and nails the Missle with a heart punch that sends him to the mat. Vinnie bounces off the ropes, and drops a knee on Johnny's throat. Missle rolls out of the ring to recuperate.
Vinnie connects with Johnny's throat again, sending him flying over the top rope. Vinnie climbs to the top turnbuckle and launches downward on
Missle, but Johnny catches him in mid air and slams him to the hard floor.
The lights go out as Johnny is climbing back into the ring. A confrontation occurs in the darkness, and when the lights come up, Vinnie is found draped over the barricade, blood oozing from his mouth. A masked assailant runs from the arena. Johnny Missle drags the unconscious Vinnie back into the ring and pins him for the three count.
Scott: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner, and NEW DWA WORLD CHAMPION, JOHNNY "SIDEWINDER" MISSLE!!! Garrett: What a shocker! Missle was definitely the underdog in this bout, and without a contractual obligation he would have never even been in the ring with Vinnie.
The Delinquent: Vinnie was a marked man from the day he first put on that belt- believe me, I know! Garrett: You're finally making sense... Anyway, I would like to thank my guest co-host, the ESWP World Champion, the Delinquent, for stopping in.
The Delinquent: Now if you'd just sign the check I'll be going.... Garrett: Be sure to tune in next Tuesday for a special edition of Friday
Night Fights- the Road to April Anarchy!
