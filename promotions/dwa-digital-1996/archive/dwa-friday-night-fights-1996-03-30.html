Friday Night Fights! (3/30)

30 March 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Digital Wrestling Alliance presents FRIDAY NIGHT FIGHTS! Live from the Pasadena Civic Center in Pasadena, California! Garrett Montgomery: Welcome, wrestling fans! Welcome back to FRIDAY NIGHT
FIGHTS! I'm your host, Garrett Montgomery, and I'll be bringing you all the action tonight. But before we get started, I'd like to introduce my guest co-host tonight, the wrestler without a fed, the man from Marfa,
Crimson Skull! Welcome back to Friday Night Fights!
Crimson Skull: Thanks, Garrett. It's great to be back. I see that Vinnie still retains the World title. I guess the Original Jersey Boy hasn't lost his touch since I was last here.
Garrett: We'll see soon enough, since Vinnie is up next against the newcomer Apollo in a non-title bout. Let's hear a few comments from the newcomer Apollo, followed by a flash from Doctor Joe Slasher, M.D.
----- (Apollo stands with his manager Thanatos Polopoulos in front of the
Parthenon in Athens) Polopoulos: We can not be any more excited about the upcoming match with the DWA champ Vinnie than we are right now. Apollo has been training for months to make his debut in the league and I can truthfully say that he is ready.
Apollo: First, I want to say thank you to the DWA and Vinnie for allowing me the chance to come in against the best wrestler in the league. Vinnie,
I can promise you that this will be a great fight. I have never been more prepared both mentally and physically. I know that I can trust you to have a clean and fair fight, and like I always say MAY THE BEST MAN WIN!
----- (Dr. Joe Slasher laughs at the camera.) Dr. Joe Slasher: WarHawk?? Well since you seem so eager and overconfident then you shall go under the knife this Friday night!!!
HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA ----- Crimson Skull: That Slasher looks like trouble! Garrett: He's the definition of trouble. But more on that later. It's time for Vinnie versus Apollo. Let's go to the ring!
----- Ring Announcer Scott Meridian: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is a non title bout. Introducing first, in his DWA debut, accompanied to the ring by his manager Thantos Polopoulos, standing 6' 7" tall, weighing 315 pounds, from Athens, Greece-- APOLLO!
Apollo enters the ring, receiving polite applause. Then the crowd erupts as the lights dim one, two, three times.
Scott: And his opponent, the pride of Basking Ridge, New Jersey, standing
6' 11" and also weighing 315 pounds, the reigning DWA World Champion, the
ORIGINAL JERSEY BOY, VINNIE!!! Vinnie appears at the edge of the first balcony and motions to the fans below to raise their hands. He steps off, and lands on a sea of hands that transport him to the ring. The bell rings, and action begins.
Vinnie takes charge early, and appears to be teaching the newcomer Apollo a lesson in DWA style wrestling with a series of quick rights to the chin. Apollo bounces off the ropes and into a clothesline from the champ.
Apollo is dazed, but escapes a chin lock and counters with a heart punch that send Vinnie over the top rope and on to the apron.
As Thantos Polopoulos is assisting Vinnie back into the ring, Dr. Joe
Slasher comes running to ringside, knocking Polopoulos away. As Apollo watches from the ring, Slasher rams Vinnie headfirst into the ringpost, then slams him to the hard arena floor. Slasher grabs a chair and takes aim on Vinnie's dazzled skull, but is stopped by Apollo, who pulls the chair away.
Slasher and Apollo struggle for the chair, but the Doctor has the leverage and pulls Apollo from the ring. Slasher drops Apollo with the chair, then turns and nails the rising Vinnie on the head. Apollo crawls back into the ring on the count of eight, while Slasher hammers the prone
Vinnie with the chair.
Polopoulos steps in and gets nailed before security appears and Slasher retreats to the locker room.
Scott: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner by count-out, APOLLO! ----- Garrett: Wow, Slasher unloaded on Vinnie! I had heard that the bad Doctor was out to get the Jersey Boy, but I expected it to happen in the ring!
Crimson Skull: Slasher cheated Apollo out of an excellent opportunity to work with one of the best in the DWA, Vinnie. Apollo gets the win, but
I'm sure it's a hollow victory to him.
Garrett: Let's go to the DWA Command Center for highlights of the early matches tonight. ----- DWA Command Center Jason Andrews vs. Brian Damage (w/ Hurly Burly) In a spirited battle between two fan favorites, Damage took the early lead with an aerial show, dropping Andrews with several flying side kicks. Damage lined up Jason for his Death Sentence, but Andrews ducked the flying clothesline and Brian hit hard. As Damage arose, Andrews stopped him with a high flying move of his own, the top rope drop kick, and covered him for the pin.
Your winner- Jason Andrews Eddy Gonzalez (w/ Hurly Burly) vs. Coyote Damage's tag partner Gonzalez had better luck tonight, and quickly pinned
Coyote after he walked in to Eddy's Lights Out, a vicious choke slam.
After the pin Coyote angrily attacked Gonzalez when he offered a handshake, and the two hammered each other all the way back to the locker room.
Dr. Joe Slasher, M.D. vs. WarHawk Slasher attacked WarHawk prior to the bell, but WarHawk retaliated, slamming the bad Doctor into the barricade. WarHawk dragged Dr. Joe into the ring and attempted a giant splash off the top turnbuckle, but missed when Slasher rolled aside. Slasher went for the 911, his figure four submission hold, but WarHawk reversed it and Slasher submitted.
Your winner by Submission- WarHawk Johnny "Sidewinder" Missle vs. Bane (w/ Jon Robinson) Newcomer Bane weathered a tremendous series of savate kicks from Missle, and responded with his Death Drop, a modified DDT, that left Missle dazed and defenseless. Bane began beating Missle after the bell until fellow newcomer Wasp Man appeared. Wasp Man attacked Bane, driving him over the top rope and through the timekeeper's table. Bane retreated, and Wasp Man assisted Missle to the locker room.
Your winner- Bane ----- Crimson Skull: What a brutal series of matches! That Bane looks like trouble! Garrett: Hopefully Johnny Missle wasn't too seriously injured. He's signed to meet Vinnie next week for the DWA World Championship belt.
Crimson Skull: At this point I'd bet on Vinnie, unless Slasher is in the house. Garrett: Our next match will a preview of the upcoming World Tag Championship tournament, as Class War faces the newcomers Thunder and
Lightning. Earlier this week I met with Class War, and here's what they had to say.
----- Garrett: With me at this time, are two guys making their debut tonight and look to be a future force here. I am of course referring to these men, Monster Lucas Masai and Kuwasi "Black Fist" Shakur, who are the tag team
Class War. Gentlemen, what's your assessment of your debut competition? Monster Lucas Masai: Looks like it's time for war with the Gimmicks From
Hell. Thunder? Lightning? What are you guys called? The Rainclouds? One thing is certain -- Class War won't be the ones storming off in stretchers!
Kuwasi "Black Fist" Shakur: I always enjoy a first match. It's our chance to set an example and warn the rest of this place who the team to beat is! So many people ask 'What make Class War *the* team here?' Simple.
Killer instinct. Experience. Determination. My man here and I are as battle-scarred as the streets of New York and hard as steel. We weren't born to wrestle -- we were born to fight. How do I think these jobbers will do against us? I predict a stormy night for the Rainclouds!
Garrett: And your assessment of the talent in general? Monster Lucas Masai: The Rainclouds HAVE NO TALENT. I'm so sick of one-name guys with a cheese gimmick and attempt at skill. You can be a
Thunder, an Executioner, a Hammer, a Madonna for all I care -- they're all the same and all will hit the dirt.
Kuwasi "Black Fist" Shakur: Class War will rise to the top of the ranks -- guaranteed! Same for our singles work -- we are as good as they come and we will outlive the pack! ----- Crimson Skull: Class War looks tough, but I've heard that Thunder and
Lightning are bad news!
Garrett: Let's go to the Command Center and see the results! ----- DWA Command Center Class War vs. Thunder and Lightning (w/ Tommy J. Dynomite) Class War was attacked by three masked assailants in the way to the civic center, and appeared uneasy when they entered the ring. Dynomite made his presence known early by stomping Masai's head as he lay on the apron.
Shakur mounted an offensive, slinging Lightning from turnbuckle to turnbuckle. Dynomite distracted the official as Thunder nailed Shakur with a load elbow pad. The official turned in time to see Lightning pin the disoriented Shakur.
Your winners- Thunder and Lightning ----- Crimson Skull: Oh, man, I don't think that Class War is going to let that match slide! Garrett: I suspect you're right! Their descriptions of the masked assailants seemed awfully close to Thunder and Lightning and their manager Tommy J. Dynomite. Let's go back to the ring, where are feature match is about to begin!
----- Scott Meridian: Ladies and gentlemen, this next match is for the DWA
Internet Championship! Introducing first, from Syracuse, New York, the 6'
4", 304 pound challenger, THE BOUNTY HUNTER! The crowd reacts negatively as the Bounty Hunter enters the ring. He responds by jumping to the apron and snatching a Firewalker poster from a young fan, only to shred it and toss it back in his face.
Scott: And now, accompanied to the ring by his manager Dr. Insane, from the great state of Wyoming, standing 6' 8" tall and weighing 285 pounds, the undefeated DWA Internet Champion- FIREWALKER!
The crowd explodes as Firewalker rushes to the ring. Dr. Insane takes up a position at ringside, posing for pictures and signing autographs.
The match begins with both men trying various grappling moves. The Bounty
Hunter gains the advantage with a full nelson that drops Firewalker to his knees. Firewalker retaliates with am arm drag takedown and begins working on the Bounty Hunter's left arm. The Bounty Hunter breaks free by propelling Firewalker into the ropes.
The Bounty Hunter misses a clothesline as Firewalker comes off the ropes, and is greeted by a knee to the gut. Firewalker chops the Hunter's throat, and climbs to the top turnbuckle. The Bounty Hunter rises just as
Firewalker leaps, and catches him with a drop kick.
The Bounty Hunter scales the ropes, dragging Firewalker by the head. Firewalker regains control and avoids the Bounty, a deadly top rope DDT. The Bounty Hunter hits the mat hard and bounces up, still stunned, when he is stopped with the Rainmaker. The submission hold is too much for the
Bounty Hunter and the official calls for the bell.
Scott: Ladies and gentleman, your winner by submission, and still DWA
Internet Champion, FIREWALKER!
----- Garrett: Firewalker continues his undefeated streak, but as Internet champion he'll have to defend that title again next week against fellow native American WarHawk!
Crimson Skull: That will be a match! And you've got Vinnie defending his
World title against Johnny Missle!
Garrett: Thanks again, Crimson Skull, for sitting in with me. I'll be back next week with another exciting chapter of FRIDAY NIGHT FIGHTS!
