DWA — Preview

DWA April Anarchy - the Preview Show

18 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Digital Wrestling Alliance presents APRIL ANARCHY!- The Preview Show Live from the Superdome in New Orleans, Louisiana... Garrett Montgomery: Hello, DWA fans, and welcome to the Preview Show for tomorrow night's DWA Challenge Event- APRIL ANARCHY! Tonight we'll see the semi-final matches in the DWA Tag Team Tournament, and check in on the preparations for tomorrow night. But first, let's go to Scott
Meridian, standing by with Bane! ----- Scott Meridian: I'm here with the man who could be champion, Bane, and his manager Jon Robinson. Bane, what is your game plan for your fight against Apollo at April Anarchy?
Jon Robinson: What plan? Ohhhhh, wait, I get it, you think that Bane is gonna lose!!! Well here's our plan WE'RE GONNA KICK HIS ASS!
Scott: Do you anticipate any interference from outsiders such as Class
War during the match?
Jon: Outside interference?...NO ONE CAN BEAT ME IN THE OUTSIDE INTERFERENCE AREA....NOOOOOOO ONNEEEEE!!! Scott: DWA President Shelton Colby is seeking an enforcer to eliminate outside interference. If asked, would your man accept the role of DWA enforcer?
Jon: Bane is nobody's fool, if he wants to enforce.. HE WILL ENFORCE. AS for Mr. President, you don't go bossing around Bane, or you find yourself in a bed next to Mr. Monsoon!!!
Scott: And finally, Bane, a question from Melissa Bond of Jacksonville,
Arkansas. She writes- "Bane, I am your biggest fan. I love what you do in the ring, but what do you do when you're not wrassling?"
Jon: Bane responded to that letter with the following comments.. Ahem
"Dear Melissa, thank God your not a Bondo!!! As Jon is writing this for me, I don't speak to anyone except Jon, hell, I don't think you even deserve this letter. But to answer your question.. I KICK THE %#@* OUT
OF ANNOYING BRATS LIKE YOU BOTHERING ME WITH POINTLESS QUESTIONS!!!!"
End of letter. Gentlemen, I assure you the World RAW champion will soon be the DWA Champ.. AND THAT'S A FACT!!
Scott: Thanks, fellows... Jon: No, you have been honored with the presence of Bane! Scott: Back to you, Garrett. ----- Garrett: Bane is obviously determined to win the belt, but he's not winning over any fans with that manager Jon Robinson. We'll have comments from his challenger, Apollo, later tonight. But first, let's go to the
DWA Commando Center to view highlights from tonight's tag team action.
-----DWA Commando Center----- Pain Factory (w/ "Neon" Leon Parker) vs. Class War Class War attacked Pain Factory in the locker room area, and the brawl continued all the way to the ring. Downey plummeled Shakur with a trash can, while Masai hammered away at Simpson's knees with a piece of lead pipe. DWA officials separated the teams and lead them to the ring, where the brawling resumed. Downey powerbombed Shakur and pinned him while
Masai sought submission from Simpson with an inverted figure four leglock. The ring official declared Downey and Shakur the legal participants, and awarded the victory to Pain Factory.
Your winners- Pain Factory 911 (w/ the Godfather) vs. The Nomads (w/ Dr. Insane) The Hitman started off on the wrong foot against Firewalker, stepping into a Rainmaker that left him begging for mercy. The Big Town Bully entered the ring and broke the submission hold with a kick to
Firewalker's temple. Misery responded by attacking the Bully, and a free for all ensued. The Godfather and Dr. Insane entered the ring, pulling their wrestlers apart. The match resumed with the Hitman dropping
Firewalker with a roundhouse right. He pulled the groggy Firewalker to his feet, and climbed the ropes for the Assassination. The Hitman executed the top rope DDT perfectly, and pinned Firewalker for the win.
Your winners- 911 ----- Garrett: So it will be Pain Factory versus 911 for the DWA World Tag Team
Championship! The Nomads and Class War resume their quests for gold tomorrow night in the DWA Internet Tag Team Tournament. Speaking of tomorrow night, here's Scott Meridian with the complete lineup for APRIL
ANARCHY! Scott: Thanks, Garrett. In a match that will determine the next DWA World
Champion, the heavily favored Bane takes on the man from Greece, Apollo.
Garrett: I'll be speaking with Apollo later tonight about that match. Scott: And another title will be on the line when the DWA Internet Champion WarHawk encounters Firewalker in a rematch. Garrett: Another classic battle between the two popular native Americans. Firewalker is determined to regain his title. Scott: As decided earlier tonight, Pain Factory will face 911 for the DWA
World Tag Team Championship. This promises to be a brutal encounter between two of the nastiest teams in the DWA.
Garrett: Speaking of nasty, what about that fiend, Dr. Joe Slasher, M.D.? Scott: The bad doctor will operate on the former DWA World Champion
Vinnie in the Texas Death Chamber. This glass enclosed ring is equipped with an original electric chair from the Texas Department of Corrections, as well as the various tools of the trade in the prison business- leg irons, a whip, a billy club, an electric cattle prod, handcuffs, and a tazer gun.
Garrett: Somebody's gonna get hurt in there! Scott: That is certain, since the winner must get his opponent into the chair, manacle him, and throw the switch! Children are required to be removed from the arena during this potentially lethal matchup.
Garrett: Is that it? Scott: No, there's more! In the eight man Best of the Rest Battle Royale, the lowest ranked wrestlers battle for a chance to move up and face the
DWA Internet Champion on Friday Night Fights! Kuwasi "Black Fist" Shakur,
Jason Andrews, Big Town Bully, the Bounty Hunter, the Hitman, Wasp Man,
Brian Damage, and Coyote will compete in this over the top elimination bout.
Garrett: And we've added two tag team matches from the DWA Internet Tag
Team Tournament! Scott: That's right! Class War will face Thunder and Lightning, and the
Nomads will face the Burly Bunch. The winners will meet on next week’s
Friday Night Fights in the title match.
Garrett: So many matches on just one night, only at APRIL ANARCHY! Thanks for the update, Scott! And now I would like to introduce an up and coming superstar of the DWA. Accompanied by his manager, Thantos
Polopolous, here is the man from Greece, APOLLO! Apollo, any thoughts on your upcoming World Championship match with Bane?
Apollo: Garrett, I am hyped!! The competition here in the DWA has been heating up lately and I'm, glad to be in the thick of it. There's one reason I became a wrestler, I WANT THE CHAMPIONSHIP! Bane, I'm sorry to say that you are the next in a line of many to be defeated by Apollo.
Garrett: You've suffered interference in most of your matches, and it's gotten to the point that DWA President Shelton Colby is seeking an enforcer to prevent future incursions. How do you feel about this interference?
Apollo: Until my last match, it had gotten to the point where I was ashamed to be a part of the league. Garrett, you know that I am a straight up kind of guy. I will fight anybody, anywhere as long as its a fair match. I must forewarn anyone who tries to use illegal tactics or interfere against me. Remember, when you mess with the gods, you shall perish!
Garrett: If asked by President Colby, would you let your man be the enforcer, Thantos? Thantos: That's an interesting possibility, Garrett. I know that Apollo will do what is best for the league. If the DWA asks him to become the enforcer, I can't see how we could refuse. There is one hitch, don't expect Apollo to enforce alone. When Apollo becomes involved, there are many more forces to be reckoned with.
Garrett: One last question- if you win, you become a marked man in the
DWA. Do you intend to seek out alliances with other wrestlers, or will you remain independent?
Apollo: I have made only one friend in the league. He was the first wrestler I fought. I want to offer a public challenge right now. Vinnie, if you are with me, I challenge Fist and Shakur to a tag-team match after
I win the championship. Vinnie, I will defend your honor and that of your father's if you accept to join me!
Garrett: An interesting proposition, and one that I'm sure Vinnie will consider. Thanks, gentlemen.
Apollo and Thantos: Our pleasure. Garrett: There you have it- Apollo, definitely one of the classiest wrestlers in the DWA. And that will wrap it up from the Preview Show.
I'll see you tomorrow night at APRIL ANARCHY! copyright 1996, the Digital Wrestling Alliance. All rights reserved.
