DWA Tonight! (5/16)

16 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Digital Wrestling Alliance presents Live- from the Commando Center, it’s DWA Tonight! with your hostess, Julie Cooper! Julie Cooper: Welcome to this week’s DWA Tonight! I’ll have a special report on the house matches from the DWA Intercontinental Championship
Tournament, as well as some new hot rumors and a preliminary lineup for
MEMORIAL DAY MASSACRE! But first, let’s take a look at the top ten singles contenders in the DWA.
-----DWA TOP TEN LIST----- 1. Thunder 2. “Acid” Andrew Downey 3. Eddy Gonzalez 4. Alex “the Hammer” Simpson 5. Poseidon 6. Bane 7. Vinnie 8. Firewalker 9. The Bounty Hunter 10. The Hitman ----- Julie: Poseidon was impressive in his first match, but we’ll see if he can hold that number 5 position this Friday when he challenges Dr. Joe
Slasher, M.D. Thunder leapfrogged Downey after his loss to the DWA
Internet Champion, the Serial KiLLa. And the former DWA World Champion
Vinnie is quietly working his way back into title contention. His next opponent is Lightning. Speaking of Lightning, a little bird told me that he and his tag team partner Thunder are under investigation by the DWA for substance abuse! This investigation could affect the outcome of the
DWA Intercontinental Championship tournament. Speaking of which, let’s look at more results from the preliminary round matches held tonight.
-----Results of Round Three----- Lord Byron defeated the Serial KiLLa. Firewalker lost to Alex “the Hammer” Simpson. Lightning eliminated Jason Andrews. The Bounty Hunter pinned Eddy Gonzalez. Dr. Joe Slasher was eliminated by Vinnie. “Acid” Andrew Downey eliminated Wasp Man. Masked Mayhem 2 fell to Bane and is eliminated. The Hitman sent Monster Lucas Masai to the showers. Kuwasi “Black Fist” Shakur was eliminated by Masked Mayhem 1. ----- Julie: Quite a few DWA Superstars are gone, and the pressure is building on the remaining eleven. Here’s the lineup for Thursday’s tournament matches:
Lord Byron versus Firewalker. Lightning versus Eddy Gonzalez. Vinnie versus the Bounty Hunter. “Acid” Andrew Downey versus Bane. Thunder versus Masked Mayhem 1. Julie: I’ll have the results of those matches at this week’s Friday Night
Fights. Remember, those wrestlers that remain after the end of round six will meet at the next DWA Challenge Event- MEMORIAL DAY MASSACRE! The lineup for that night has been filling up, but there’s still room for more matches. Here’s what’s been signed:
For the DWA World Championship Belt- A “Last Chance” Match Bane (w/ Jon Robinson) versus The Defending DWA World Champion- Apollo (w/ Thantos Polopolous) Bane gets one last chance at Apollo. If he fails to win, Apollo will not have to defend his title against Bane again!
For the DWA Internet Championship Belt- Eddy Gonzalez (w/ Hurly Burly) versus The Serial KiLLa A DWA Tag Team Title Unification Match- The DWA Internet Tag Team Champions- 911 (w/ the Godfather) versus The DWA World Tag Team Champions- Pain Factory (w/ “Neon” Leon Parker) A No DQ, Six Man Tag Team Challenge Match- The Nomads (w/ Dr. Insane) and WarHawk versus Thunder and Lightning and a yet to be named partner. The DWA Enforcers will be the special referees in this match. DWA President Shelton Colby will draw the name of the partner that night from a list of volunteers.
Julie: What a lineup! The two top tag teams will battle it out for all the belts, and Bane will get his last chance against the DWA World
Champion Apollo! I can’t wait! This is Julie Cooper for the Digital
Wrestling Alliance- see you next time!
