DWA — Pay-Per-View

DWA April Anarchy! (Part 1 of 4)

23 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Digital Wrestling Alliance presents APRIL ANARCHY! Live from the Superdome in New Orleans, Louisiana! Garrett Montgomery: Hello, DWA fans, and welcome to APRIL ANARCHY! All of the superstars of the Digital Wrestling Alliance are here tonight.
Joining me in the booth as guest analyst is the man without a fed, the awesome Crimson Skull! Welcome back to the DWA!
Crimson Skull: Thanks, Garrett, it's great to be here! I'm really pumped over the DWA World Championship match! Bane is the favorite, but I'm putting my money on Apollo.
Garrett Montgomery: We'll see that match later tonight, along with title matches for the remaining DWA championship belts. But for now, let's go to ring announcer Scott Meridian for tonight's first match, a semifinal bout in the Internet Tag Team Tournament between Class War and Thunder and Lightning!
Scott Meridian: Ladies and gentlemen, the Digital Wrestling Alliance welcomes you to APRIL ANARCHY! Tonight's first bout is a semifinal match in the Internet Tag Team Tournament. Introducing first, accompanied to the ring by Louisiana State Troopers, here are Kuwasi "Black Fist" Shakur and Monster Lucas Masai- CLASS WAR!
Class War enters the arena in handcuffs, escorted by six large state troopers. The crowd begins to chant "shoot, shoot", and Shakur responds by raising his manacled wrists above his head. As they reach ringside, the cuffs are removed.
Scott: And their opponents, accompanied to the ring by their manager
Tommy J. Dynomite, here are THUNDER AND LIGHTNING!
The lights go out, and brilliant flashes of light burst off the rafters of the Superdome. Thunder crashes at a deafening level. The lights come up, and Thunder and Lightning are in the ring! The referee calls for the bell.
Thunder and Masai square off as their partners take up positions in the corners. Thunder charges Masai and is taken down with a knee to the gut.
Masai drops an elbow across Thunder's throat, the attempts to pin him, but Thunder kicks out at the one count.
Masai drags Thunder to his feet, and tags Shakur. Masai slings Thunder into the ropes, and he and Shakur nail him on the rebound with a double flying clothesline. Shakur grabs Thunder from behind, and attempts a sleeper. Thunder makes it to the ropes and the official orders the hold released.
Thunder tags Lightning, but Shakur meets him at the ropes with a tremendous drop kick that sends him flying off the apron and on to the floor. Lightning rises, but is crushed by a splash from Shakur. Both men hit the concrete hard. Dynomite rushes to aid Lightning as Thunder executes a perfect sunset flip off the top turnbuckle on to Shakur. Masai rushes across the ring, vaults the ropes, and crashes down on Thunder.
The teams begin brawling at ringside, undeterred by the count of the referee. Masai pounds Thunder's head into the ringpost, and Lightning connects with a heart punch that sends Shakur over the barricade and into the crowd. Lightning crawls back into the ring, beating the ten count.
Shakur can't make it, and the official calls for the bell. Scott: Your winners by countout- THUNDER AND LIGHTNING! Skull: What a turnaround! Class War was dominating that match, but Lightning kept his head in the game and pulled out the victory. Garrett: Sometimes that's all it takes. Thunder and Lightning advance to the finals of the Internet Championships, to face either the Nomads or the Burly Bunch. They're up next, let's go to the ring!
Scott: Are you ready for another matchup in the DWA Internet Tag Team
Tournament? Here we go! Introducing first, accompanied to the ring by their manager Hurly Burly, please welcome Brian Damage and Eddy Gonzalez,
THE BURLY BUNCH! The fan favorites Damage and Gonzalez dash down the aisle, waving at the fans. They enter the ring and perform simultaneous backflips, much to the surprise of the crowd.
Scott: And their opponents, accompanied to the ring by Dr. Insane, former
DWA Internet Champion Firewalker and his partner Misery, THE NOMADS!
The crowd explodes as Firewalker and Misery enter the arena. Dr. Insane leads his team to the ring, where they meet and shake hands with the
Burly Bunch. The official calls for the bell.
Firewalker and Brian Damage start the match with a rapid fire succession of moves and counters. Damage gains the advantage with a arm drag takedown, and transitions into an arm bar. Firewalker reverses out of the arm bar, flipping Damage to his feet.
Firewalker slings Damage into the ropes, but misses on a clothesline. Damage rebounds and nails Firewalker with an elbow to the back of the head. Firewalker is stunned, and tags in Misery.
Misery rushes Damage, driving him into the turnbuckle. Misery climbs to the second turnbuckle and begins pounding on Damage's head. Damage steps through the ropes and the referee calls for the break. Damage tags in
Gonzalez.
Misery sizes up the huge Gonzalez, and attacks with a dropkick that has no effect. Misery tries coming off the ropes with a cross body block, but
Gonzalez catches him, and drops him with a backbreaker. Gonzalez launches himself into the air and drops a leg across Misery's throat. He tags in
Damage, and tosses him from the top turnbuckle on to Misery.
Damage tries to roll up Misery for the pin, and gets the two count before
Misery kicks free. Misery tries to crawl to Firewalker, but Damage catches him and drags him to the corner. Damage tags Gonzalez, and
Gonzalez climbs to the top turnbuckle for the super banzai drop. As
Gonzalez drops, Misery rolls aside, and Gonzalez hits hard. Misery tags in Firewalker.
Firewalker hits Gonzalez with a series of knees to the gut, then slings the big man into the turnbuckle. Firewalker launches off the ropes and connects with the Rainmaker, dropping Gonzalez to the canvas. He tags
Misery, who executes a backflip from the top rope, dropping on Gonzalez for the pin.
Scott: Your winners- THE NOMADS! Skull: That was a well executed match from both teams! Outstanding athleticism! Garrett: And what about that backflip from Gonzalez? The man weighs over
500 pounds! I thought that the ring was going to collapse!
Skull: So the Nomads will meet Thunder and Lightning next week for the
DWA Internet Tag Team Championship!
Garrett: That's right, and up next we've got an eight man battle royale to determine next week's challenger for the Internet belt. Stay tuned for the Best of the Rest Battle Royale!
