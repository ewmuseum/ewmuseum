DWA — Pay-Per-View

DWA April Anarchy! (Part 2 of 4)

23 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Garrett Montgomery: We're back with more DWA action! Next up is the Best of the Rest Battle Royale, featuring eight of the DWA's toughest competitors.
Crimson Skull: The winner of this over the top match gets a shot at the
Internet belt next week on Friday Night Fights. Let's go to the ring!
Scott Meridian: Are you ready for more APRIL ANARCHY! It's time for the
Best of the Rest Battle Royale. Introducing our first competitor, one half of Class War, from South Bronx, New York, standing 6' 9" tall and weighing an even 300 pounds, here is KUWASI "BLACK FIST" SHAKUR!
The crowd explodes negatively as Shakur is escorted to the ring by Louisiana State Troopers, once again handcuffed. A small group of fans scream "shoot, Fist, shoot" as Shakur vaults into the ring with a look of angry determination.
Scott: Our next competitor hails from the hometown of the DWA, Dallas,
Texas! He weighs 236 pounds, and stands an even 6 feet tall. Please welcome COYOTE!
The crowd seems indifferent to this wrestler, but Coyote doesn't seem to mind. He enters the ring and takes up position across from Shakur.
Scott: Next to enter the ring, hailing from St. Louis, Missouri, standing
6' 4" tall and weighing 252 pounds, the exciting JASON ANDREWS!
Andrews enters the Superdome to a tremendous crowd response, especially from the female spectators. He poses for pictures as he enters the ring, then takes his position in the third corner.
Scott: Escorted to the ring by his manager, the Godfather, our next wrestler hails from the Big Apple, New York, New York. Please welcome one half of 911, the 6' 6", 300 pound, BIG TOWN BULLY!
The Bully strides confidently to the ring, trailed by the Godfather. He approaches Shakur, and a shoving match begins between the two wrestlers.
Andrews and Coyote step in to break it up. Scott: Our fifth competitor is from the windy city, Chicago. Accompanied to the ring by his manager, Hurly Burly, he's a member of the Burly
Bunch. Give it up for the 6' 3", 243 pound, BRIAN DAMAGE!
Damage bursts into the arena, and is greeted by a roar of approval from the crowd. He enters the ring and climbs to the top turnbuckle. The crowd explodes once again as Damage executes a perfect backflip into the ring.
Scott: Next up, from Syracuse, New York, the 6' 4", 304 pound BOUNTY HUNTER! Moving slowly to the ring, the Bounty Hunter sizes up the competition. The crowd tosses debris at him, but he is not affected. He crawls under the bottom rope and takes up position in the center of the ring, carefully observing the other wrestlers.
Scott: Our seventh competitor is from Japan. Escorted to the ring by his manager, Roscoe Law, this wrestler stands 6' 3" tall and weighs a solid
220 pounds. Please welcome WASP MAN!
Wasp Man races down the aisle to the ring, and executes a series of twisting backflips on the apron. He vaults into the ring and launches himself into the ropes as the other wrestlers scatter. The crowd goes wild!
Scott: And now our final participant. The other half of 911, from the Big
Apple, weighing 230 pounds and standing 6' 4" tall- THE HITMAN!
The Hitman strolls down the aisle, taking time to taunt the fans. He huddles briefly with the Godfather before entering the ring and joining the Big Town Bully. The bell rings and the mayhem begins.
The Hitman and Big Town Bully team up on the Bounty Hunter, and begin hammering away. Wasp Man and Andrews begin battling Shakur. Damage and
Coyote tie up, but Coyote gains the upper hand with a piledriver. The
Bounty Hunter goes over the top rope courtesy of the Hitman and Big Town
Bully.
Scott: The Bounty Hunter has been eliminated! Shakur slams Wasp Man into the corner, but Andrews charges and crushed
Shakur between them. Shakur takes the offensive and drops Andrews with a hard right, followed by a splash. Wasp Man goes after the Hitman, dropkicking him into the turnbuckle. Coyote hoists the stunned Damage, and tosses him to the floor.
Scott: Brian Damage has been eliminated! Shakur lifts Andrews on to the top turnbuckle, the clotheslines him out of the ring. Wasp Man knocks the Hitman over the top with a flying drop kick.
Scott: Andrews and the Hitman have been eliminated! Shakur and the Bully team up and attempt to lift Wasp Man over the top, but he snags the ropes and pulls the Bully over instead. Coyote nails
Wasp Man and Shakur from behind, breaking Wasp Man's grip on the ropes.
Scott: The Big Town Bully and Wasp Man have been eliminated! Coyote and Shakur square off in the center of the ring. Shakur tries a headlock, but Coyote slings him into the ropes. Coyote misses a drop kick and hits the mat hard. Shakur, sensing victory, climbs to the top turnbuckle and somersaults on to Coyote. Shakur drags Coyote to his feet, then drops him again with a DDT. Coyote struggles to his feet, only to be met with a snap suplex. Shakur whips Coyote into the ropes, and nails him on the rebound with a heart punch. Shakur hoists the shaken Coyote over his head and drops him on to the floor.
Scott: The winner of the Best of the Rest Battle Royale- KUWASI "BLACK FIST" SHAKUR! Crimson Skull: What a battle! Shakur prevails, and will face the Internet champion next week!
Garrett: This is the opportunity Shakur has been waiting for, a chance at a title! He'll be facing one of the next wrestlers at Friday Night
Fights. Let's go to the ring for the introductions. Scott: This match is for the DWA Internet Championship belt! Introducing first, the challenger. From the plains of Wyoming, accompanied to the ring by his manager Dr. Insane, standing 6' 8" tall and weighing over 285 pounds, please welcome the former DWA Internet Champion- FIREWALKER!
Firewalker enters the arena slowly, carrying a peace pipe. The crowd cheers wildly, but he seems distant and determined.
Scott: And now his opponent, from Telequah, Oklahoma, standing 6' 3" tall and weighing 235 pounds, the reigning DWA INTERNET CHAMPION- WARHAWK!
WarHawk rushes to the ring, carrying the DWA Internet Championship belt high above his head. He places it in the center of the ring, and is met by Firewalker, who offers his peace pipe. WarHawk admires the pipe, and the two native Americans shake hands as the bell rings.
WarHawk starts the battle with a full nelson, but Firewalker reverses it with a hip toss. WarHawk charges, but is flipped with an arm drag take down. Firewalker begins to twist his arm, but WarHawk breaks free with a chop to the gut. He follows with a knee lift that sends Firewalker flying.
WarHawk continues his assault with a series of elbow drops to Firewalker’s chest. Firewalker rolls to the apron to regain his composure. As Firewalker climbs into the ring, WarHawk explodes off the ropes, but misses with a clothesline. Firewalker flips WarHawk on to the floor.
Firewalker and WarHawk crawl into the ring and square off again. A test of strength ensues, and Firewalker twists WarHawk into a sleeper hold.
WarHawk struggles valiantly, but loses conscience and falls to the mat. Firewalker covers him for the pin. Scott: Your winner, and once again DWA Internet Champion- FIREWALKER! As Firewalker is trying to revive WarHawk, the sound of a thousand snakes blasts through the PA system. The lights go out, and a tremendous crash is heard. When the lights come up, Firewalker has been smashed through the timekeeper's table, and a large man stands in the center of the ring with WarHawk held high above his head. The man slips WarHawk into a torture rack until he once again loses conscience, then drops him. Scott
Meridian enters the ring.
Scott: What do you think you're doing? Who are you? Crimson Skull: Meridian better watch out, or he'll get... Oh, no! Garrett: Get security out there! Get Scott some help! The big man drops Scott Meridian with a blow to the throat, then kneels on his head. He grabs the microphone, and with a hissing voice says "I am
VENOM!" As security approaches, Venom rips through the canvas, grabs
WarHawk, and disappears under the ring.
Garrett: We've lost control here! I'm not sure what happened there... Let's take a break while we sort things out. copyright 1996, the Digital Wrestling Alliance, all rights reserved
