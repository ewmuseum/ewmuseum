The Digital Wrestling Alliance is still looking for a few high quality tag teams. Experienced and novice handlers are welcome to try their luck in the World Tag Team Championship Tournament, starting next week. Tag team members will be eligible to wrestle in singles matches also. If interested, please complete the following form for each wrestler.
