DWA — Pay-Per-View

The Patriot Games! (Part 3 of 3)

11 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Garrett: We’re back, and Scott Meridian is standing by for the introductions in our Intercontinental Championship
match! Scott: Ladies and gentlemen, this bout is for the DWA Intercontinental Championship! Introducing first, the
challenger! From Norfolk, Virginia, standing 6’ 9" tall and weighing 295 pounds, here is DR. JOE SLASHER, MD!
Slasher steps into the ring arena to a barrage of anger from the fans, who remember the brutal Texas Death
Chamber match against Vinnie. The bad doctor reaches in his medical bag and retrieves the cattle prod from that
match, but the referee relieves him of the object as he enters the ring. Scott: And his opponent, accompanied to the ring by "Golden" Johney Pathos, the pride of Basking Ridge, New
Jersey, weighing 315 pounds and standing 6’ 11" tall, here is the former DWA World Champion and current DWA
Intercontinental Champion, the Original Jersey Boy- VINNIE! Vinnie runs out to the ring, greeted by a standing ovation. Pathos follows, waving to the crowd. The referee calls the
two wrestlers to the center of the ring, and after checking each for foreign objects, calls for the bell.
Vinnie and Slasher jockey for position, then Vinnie takes the offensive and drops Slasher with a snap suplex. Vinnie
attempts to apply the sleeper, but Slasher responds with a thumb to the eye. Slasher slings the partially blinded
Vinnie into the ropes, and drops him with a power slam. Vinnie kicks out at two. Slasher pulls Vinnie by the hair to his feet, and slaps him hard back down to the canvas. Vinnie bounces up and
returns the slap, driving Slasher into the ropes. Slasher gets tangled in the ropes, and Vinnie hammers away with
several kicks before Slasher breaks free. Slasher drops to the apron to regain his senses. The referee tries to direct Vinnie to a neutral corner, but Vinnie somersaults over the top rope and crashes down on
Slasher. Slasher is stunned, and Vinnie lines him up for the Original Jersey Finisher. The crowd goes wild as Vinnie
drops the doctor with the modified face first choke slam. Vinnie pulls Slasher back into the ring and sets him up for
another Original Jersey Finisher. Slasher comes to only to see the mat coming at his face again, and Vinnie covers
for the pin. Scott: Your winner, and still DWA Intercontinental Champion- VINNIE! Julie: Vinnie gets his revenge, and retains his title! Mr. Head: Please! It was obvious that Dr. Joe was less than one hundred percent for the match! Vinnie owes him a
rematch… Garrett: When he recovers! Scott Meridian is standing by with Vinnie, Johney Pathos, and the Masked Ninja. Scott?
Scott: Thanks, Garrett. First off, congratulations on your victory over Dr. Joe Slasher. Vinnie: Thanks, Scott. It felt great! Scott: Gentlemen, the big news around the DWA concerns the return of the Dark One and his stable known as the
Theater of Pain. I understand that you have some strong feelings on the return of your former foe.
Vinnie: Well, it's no secret that the Dark One has caused me some very tense moments. Scott: Vinnie, your previous partner, Tony, had a near fatal run-in with the forces of the Dark One. Will we see Tony
returning in the near future? Vinnie: Funny you should ask that, I just recently talked to Tony, who has been wrestling over in Japan, where he is
currently the IWWGP world champ, but he doesn’t see the DWA in his future. Scott: Johney, as a manager in the DWA, you've seen many other managers come and go. Do you have any advice
for the Dark One? Johney: Look Dark One, you stay away from my wrestlers! We want nothing to do with you or your trash you
manage! Ninja: Wait, Mr. Johney, I wish to speak for myself. Mr. Dark One has offered a very generous offer to me to become
a wrestler under his control. I wish to accept this offer. Vinnie: What are you talking about, the Dark One is evil. You don't want his dirty money! Johney: Yeah, you don't want him as a manager! Ninja: But I have already signed this contract. He said he would take care of the rest. Johney: Let me see that. Do you know what you signed, this makes you his personal slave, and we can't go within
200 ft. of you? Ninja: That is something we will have to accept I'm off to serve my master, the Dark One. Vinnie and Johney follow the Masked Ninja as he leaves the ringside area. Scott: Disturbing news from the camp of "Golden" Johney Pathos. Back to you, Garrett. Garrett: Well, the times are a changing in the DWA. The Dark One has apparently gone from being a manager that
wrestlers avoid to a manager that wrestlers adore! Julie: Something doesn’t seem right about the Masked Ninja’s defection, but I can’t put my finger on it.
Mr. Head: It seems to me that he just dumped one loser of a manager for another loser of a manager! Someday
these DWA "Superstars" will get smart and come knocking on the door of the genius of the DWA!
Julie: And who might that be? Mr. Head: Me, of course! I practically invented wrestling! Garrett: I would love to respond to that, but our final bout is about to get under way. Let’s go to thing ring!
Scott: Ladies and gentlemen, this is our final bout of the Patriot Games! This match is for the DWA World
Championship belt! Introducing first, from San Antonio, Texas, weighing in at 298 pounds and standing 6’ 9" tall,
here is the former DWA Internet Champion- THE SERIAL KILLA! The crowd goes wild as the popular former champion races to the ring. The Serial KiLLa climbs all four ring posts
and waves to his fans. Scott: And his opponent, from Toronto, Ontario, standing 7’ 2" tall and weighing 378 pounds, the reigning DWA
World Champion- BANE! Bane strolls to the ring, minus his long time manager Jon Robinson, whose whereabouts are unknown. The crowd
takes a step back as the huge wrestler passes, and a low snarl emerges from his lips. He enters the ring and
immediately begins attacking the Serial KiLLa. The referee calls for the bell and runs for cover.
Bane drives the KiLLa into the corner and begins pounding away with forearms to the head. The KiLLa responds
with a knee lift to the groin that stuns Bane enough for him to escape. Bane spins around, and the KiLLa tags him
with a drop kick that drives him into the turnbuckles. Bane charges, and the Serial KiLLa elevates him with a back
body drop. The KiLLa climbs the turnbuckles and launches himself at the rising Bane, but Bane catches him and
drops him with a backbreaker. Bane goes for the cover, but pulls up before the three count when he hears a
commotion at ringside. He turns and there stands the former champion, Apollo, flanked by Poseidon and Thantos
Polopolous. Bane charges to the edge of the ring as Apollo beacons him to come down. The Serial KiLLa sees the
opportunity and locks Bane up with the sleeper. The last thing Bane sees as he passes out is the face of the
laughing Apollo. Scott: Your winner, and new DWA World Champion- THE SERIAL KILLA! Garrett: Another champion falls! Julie: Without Robinson to guide him, Bane fell for the oldest trick in the book- distraction!
Mr. Head: I admire the quick thinking of the Serial KiLLa, but he got a cheap victory over a great champion.
Garrett: The Serial KiLLa continues to be successful in the DWA, and here are two new wrestlers that hope to
emulate his success. Let’s go to President Shelton Colby who is standing by in the Oval Office.
Colby: Thanks, Garrett. As the fans of the Digital Wrestling Alliance know, the past month has been a season of
change for our federation. Gone are the old champions- WarHawk, Pain Factory, Johnny Missle. Last week I
announced the signing of another great prospect, Dave "The Destroyer" Schulz, and tonight I’m pleased to
announce the signing of two more sensations. Without further ado, let me introduce the latest additions to the DWA
family- "Superstar" Scott Hazard and Danny Victory! Two men step into the Oval Office- one with the look of a champion, and the other with the look of trouble. Colby
offers his hand to both. Danny Victory shakes it vigorously, but Scott Hazard declines. Bane burst through the Oval
Office door and begins threatening Colby, but Danny Victory pulls Bane away. A tense moment ensues as
Whitehouse security rushes into the Oval Office, followed by DWA security. Bane is surrounded and backs off, but
punches a hole in the office wall as he exits. Scott Hazard is laughing hysterically. Garrett: Whoa! Bane is furious! He came close to ending up in jail! Julie: Robinson managed to keep him under control, but now that he’s gone- look out! Mr. Head: That Scott Hazard looks like my kind of wrestler! He stays out of the way and minds his own business,
unlike that pretty boy Victory! Garrett: We’ll see how both newcomers perform at the next Friday Night Fights. Until then, this is Garrett
Montgomery… Julie: And Julie Cooper… Mr. Head: And the wrestling genius, Mr. Head… Garrett: Bidding you goodnight from the Patriot Games! Copyright 1996, the Digital Wrestling Alliance, all rights reserved
