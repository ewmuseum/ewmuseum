Friday Night Fights! (tape delayed, 5/16)

16 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The following is a tape delayed broadcast of Friday Night Fights!) ----- The Digital Wrestling Alliance presents FRIDAY NIGHT FIGHTS! LIVE, from the Sportatorium in Dallas, Texas! Garrett Montgomery: Welcome back to FRIDAY NIGHT FIGHTS! I’m your host,
Garrett Montgomery, and with me tonight is the one and only star of DWA
Tonight- Julie Cooper! Welcome to FRIDAY NIGHT FIGHTS, Julie!
Julie Cooper: It’s great to be here! We’ve got another exciting card tonight, as once again two titles will be one the line! The Serial KiLLa will defend his Internet Championship belt in a battle with “Acid” Andrew
Downey, and the Internet Tag Team Champions, 911, will defend against
Thunder and Lightning.
Garrett: And we’ve got a special challenge match between the Nomads and the tag team of Lord Byron and the mystery wrestler! What have you heard about this mystery man, Julie?
Julie: All I can say is the tag team should be called the “Mega-Heels” if what I’ve heard is correct!
Garrett: The “Mega-Heels”? Sounds like trouble for the Nomads! We’ll see soon enough, but first let’s go to the DWA Commando Center for updates on tonight’s earlier action.
-----DWA COMMANDO CENTER----- Class War (w/ Shelton Colby) vs. Mayhem, Inc. (w/ “Legend” Josh Brown) Class War devastated Mayhem, Inc. in what some observers labeled the first “shoot” in the DWA. Masked Mayhem 1 had Masai in trouble earlier, but failed to capitalize. A brawl broke out between the two teams, and after the officials separated them Shakur nailed Mayhem 2 with an elbow to the face that left him out cold with a broken nose. Brown was furious with the officials, and left the building screaming “it wasn’t in the script!”
Your winners- Class War Poseidon (w/ Thantos Polopolous) vs. Venom (w/ Dark) Venom had no chance to establish an offense, as the newcomer Poseidon attacked him furiously. Poseidon blew away the crowd with his tremendous trademark move, the Whirlpool, which left Venom dazed and easily pinned.
Your winner- Poseidon Wasp Man (w/ Roscoe Law) vs. Brian Damage (w/ Hurly Burly) In a tough fought “battle of the good guys”, Wasp Man took an early lead with several high flying maneuvers that left Damage damaged. But Wasp Man went to the top one too many times, and Damage got the pin with a horrific Frankensteiner. After the match, Roscoe Law and Hurly Burly met at ringside and assisted Wasp Man to the locker rooms.
Your winner- Brian Damage Coyote vs. Eddy Gonzalez (w/ Hurly Burly) Hurly Burly’s men got back to back victories tonight when the huge Gonzalez defeated the smaller Coyote. Coyote tried to use his speed to circumvent the power of the 555 pound Gonzalez, but Gonzalez cornered him early and dropped him with a ring shaking giant splash.
Your winner- Eddy Gonzalez ----- Julie: I can’t get over how huge that Eddy Gonzalez is! Coyote is lucky to get out of there in 3D after that splash!
Garrett: He’s big all right, but not any bigger than this next challenge match between the Nomads and the “Mega-Heels”! Let’s go to the ring!
Scott Meridian: Ladies and gentlemen, the following match is a no disqualification challenge bout. Introducing first, accompanied to the ring by their manager Dr. Insane, at a combined weight of 635 pounds, here are the former DWA Internet Tag Team Champions- FIREWALKER AND
MISERY- THE NOMADS! The crowd goes wild as the two popular wrestlers enter the Sportatorium. The Nomads still appear furious, and stalk to the ring without stopping to acknowledge the fans.
Scott: And their opponents, accompanied to the ring by... hey, wait a minute! King Benny II: Excuse me, you commoner! Ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls, would you please rise and pay tribute to the future champion and the personification of DWA wrestling itself, my Lord BYRON!
Lord Byron enters the arena to a barrage of displeasure, punctuated by squeals from the small contingent of admirers known as “Byron’s Babes”.
He pauses to plant a kiss on one female fan, but pulls away as she closes her eyes, leaving her frozen in anticipation.
Benny: And it’s my pleasure to introduce his Lordship’s special partner, a man of superior breeding and impeccable taste, accompanied by his manager, Jon Robinson, please welcome the one and only BANE!
Bane bursts into the arena, following closely by the mouthy Jon Robinson. The crowd is stunned by Byron’s new partner, and the Nomads step to the apron as Bane climbs into the ring. Bane and Byron come face to face, and after a brief tense moment shake hands. The official calls for the bell.
Firewalker enters against Bane and is immediately dropped with a superkick to the chin. Bane goes for the pin, but Robinson calls him off and sends in Byron, who immediately applies the Aristo-Clutch. Firewalker shakes from the pain, but does not submit. As Robinson and Bane distract the referee, Byron increases the pressure by using the ropes for leverage. Misery enters the ring, but the official intercepts him. As
Misery is ushered out of the ring, Bane assists Byron with the
Aristo-Clutch. Firewalker submits from the pain, but Byron refuses to release the hold. Robinson and Benny jump Dr. Insane as Bane attacks
Misery.
The lights dim slightly, and the sound of an Indian tom-tom blasts from the PA system. WarHawk rushes through the Sportatorium doors and knocks
Byron off of Firewalker. Byron rises, and is nailed with a tomahawk chop that sends him flying out of the ring. The Mega-Heels and their managers retreat from the arena.
Scott: Ladies and gentlemen, your winners by submission, Lord Byron and
Bane? Garrett: Wow! Byron and Bane- a new tag team? Julie: And WarHawk is back just in time! Byron was intent on finishing
Firewalker for good! I’ve got a feeling that this feud is only going to escalate into a full blown firestorm!
Garrett: That’s what it looks like! Wait, I’ve just received word that our wild feed camera has caught up with Lord Byron in the locker room.
Let’s cut to the wild feed! -----WILD FEED CAMERA----- Lord Byron is obviously elated, and opens his locker to grab a towel. He steps back as he looks inside, and his face grows pale. The camera swings around, and inside Byron’s locker is what appears to be an offering of affection. A red rose stands in a crystal vase next to a box of chocolates and a gold framed autographed photo. The camera zooms in, and the image in the photo becomes clearly. The inscription reads “I’m coming for you, big boy”, and the man in the picture is Suna "Lovely to Touch"
Gib from WAR. Byron slams the locker as the camera fades out. ----- Julie: Who was that in the picture? I’ve never seen anything like him before! Garrett: That was an old friend of Lord Byron’s, Suna “Lovely to Touch”
Gib, from the federation called WAR! Apparently Mr. Gib has decided to follow Byron wherever he wrestles.
Julie: Sounds kind of kinky to me! I’m sure Byron will have something to say about him. Garrett: We’ll have to wait and see, because the combatants for our next match are entering the arena!
Scott: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is for the DWA Internet Tag Team
Championship belts! Introducing first, the challengers. Accompanied to the ring by their manager Tommy J. Dynomite, at a combined weight of 530 pounds, here are THUNDER AND LIGHTNING!
Thunder and Lightning enter the Sportatorium to a mixed reaction. They virtually ignore the fans as they make their way to the ring. Dynomite follows talking trash about the “greatest tag team in the world”.
Scott: And now the reigning DWA Internet Tag Team Champions! Accompanied to the ring by their manager, the Godfather, also at a combined weight of
530 pounds, here are the HITMAN and the BIG TOWN BULLY- 911!
The Hitman and the Bully stride arrogantly into the arena, and are surprised by a standing ovation. The Godfather looks bewildered as a young boy in a 911 t-shirt requests an autograph. He complies, then smiles as he poses for a picture.
The Hitman and Lightning square off as the bell rings. Lightning hits the canvas after a leg sweep by the Hitman, but rolls aside as the Hitman tries to drop an elbow on his face. Lightning drags the Hitman to his feet and slings him into the ropes, catching him on the rebound with a drop kick that sends him flying into the neutral corner. Lightning attempts a spinning back kick, but the Hitman counters with a punch to the groin that leaves Lightning and the crowd breathless. Lightning tags out.
Thunder rushes into the ring and into a snap suplex by the Hitman. The tag is made, and the Big Town Bully climbs the turnbuckles. With the help of the Hitman, the Bully catapults high into the air and comes crashing down on the head of Thunder. The Bully goes for the pin, but Thunder kicks out in two. Thunder tags in Lightning.
Lightning enters the ring and is met with a ferocious blow to the head that leaves him dazed. The Bully lifts Lightning out of the ring with a clothesline, then nails him on the floor with a spectacular Lights Out.
Thunder attacks the Bully, piledriving him into the arena floor. The
Hitman retaliates by bulldogging Thunder into the barricade. Lightning drags the Bully into the ring and covers for a count of one before the
Bully kicks out.
Both men tag out, and their partners meet at the center of the ring in an earth shattering collision. The Hitman hammers away at Thunder’s stomach, driving him back into the corner. The Hitman whips Thunder into the turnbuckle, and follows with a flying elbow that drops the big man. The
Hitman covers for the win.
Scott: Your winners, and still DWA Internet Tag Team Champions- 911! Julie: Great match! Both teams had their chances to win, but the champs prevail! It looks like the only team that can beat 911 may be the World
Champions- Pain Factory! Garrett: That showdown is coming real soon, and it’s definitely a match that you don’t want to miss! Speaking of mandatory matches, our next title bout is about to begin! Let’s go to the ring!
Scott: Ladies and gentlemen, our final match of the night is for the DWA
Internet Championship belt! Introducing first, the challenger.
Accompanied to the ring by his manager “Neon” Leon Parker, from the Motor
City, weighing 256 pounds and standing 6’ 4” tall, here is “ACID” ANDREW
DOWNEY! Downey enters the arena wearing his DWA World Tag Team Championship belt. A small chant of “911” rises from the crowd and soon becomes a roar. Downey covers his ears as Parker screams at the crowd to “shut up”. Scott: And now the DWA INTERNET CHAMPION! From San Antonio, Texas, standing 6’ 9” tall and weighing 298 pounds- THE SERIAL KILLA!
The Serial KiLLa, with the DWA Internet Championship belt hanging over his shoulder, enters the Sportatorium to the cheers of his Texas fans. He rushes to the ring, and bounds over the top rope. Downey retreats to his corner as the KiLLa bounces off the ropes. The official calls for the bell and the match begins.
Downey rushes the KiLLa, and is dropped with a hip toss. The KiLLa gets
Downey in an arm bar and begins applying pressure to his elbow. Andrew rakes the KiLLa’s eyes and he breaks the hold.
The Serial KiLLa is disoriented, and Downey nails him with a DDT. The
KiLLa kicks out at the two count, and rolls off the apron. As he tries to clear the cobwebs, “Neon” Leon nails him with a chair in the back. Downey leaps over the top rope, but misses the KiLLa and hits the barricade hard. The KiLLa pulls him to his feet and rams him headfirst into the steel ring post. Downey drops to the concrete floor and the KiLLa pulls the chair away from Leon.
The KiLLa lines up Downey with the chair, but changes his mind and pops
Leon instead, driving him to the floor. The KiLLa drags Downey back into the ring and sets him up for a powerbomb. Downey struggles but is unable to get free and is slammed into the canvas. The Serial KiLLa covers for the win.
Scott: Your winner, and still DWA Internet Champion- THE SERIAL KILLA! Garrett: No surprises tonight as both titles remain in the champions hands. Julie: We’ll really see what the champs are made of next week, as the
Serial KiLLa defends against Bane and 911 faces Class War!
Garrett: You won’t want to miss next week’s Friday Night Fights! See you then! copyright 1996, the Digital Wrestling Alliance, all rights reserved
