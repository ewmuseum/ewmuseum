DWA — Pay-Per-View

DWA Memorial Day Massacre! (Part 1 of 3)

9 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Digital Wrestling Alliance presents LIVE from the Kingdome in Seattle, Washington! MEMORIAL DAY MASSACRE! Garrett Montgomery: Hello, wrestling fans, and welcome to MEMORIAL DAY
MASSACRE! I’m Garrett Montgomery, and with me tonight is none other than the sweetheart of the DWA, Julie Cooper!
Julie Cooper: Thank you, Garrett! We’ve got some exciting action in store for you tonight, including four title matches!
Garrett: Just four- I thought there were five titles in the DWA? Julie: You’re right, but tonight we’ll find out just who the best tag team is in a title unification match between the DWA World Tag Team
Champions, Pain Factory, and the DWA Internet Tag Team Champions, 911!
Garrett: But first, let’s go to the ring for the DWA Intercontinental
Championship Battle Royale, and our special guest announcer, the current
ESWP World Champion, the Delinquent, who’s standing center ring with
Scott Meridian.
The Delinquent: O.k., listen up, you jerks! This match is a battle ro... roy... what’s that word? Scott Meridian: Royal... The Delinquent: I knew that! This match is an over-the-top battle royal for the DWA Int... Inter... uh, what’s that word?
Scott: Intercontinental... The Delinquent: I KNEW THAT! Ahem... This match is a battle royal for the
DWA Intercontinental Championship! How’s that?
Scott: Great... Now let me finish! Introducing first, from Backing Ridge,
New Jersey, the former DWA World Champion, the Original Jersey Boy,
VINNIE! Vinnie rushes into the Kingdome to a huge roar, and runs down the aisle to the ring. He climbs the ropes and waves to the crowd, then takes up position in a corner.
Scott: And introducing our second competitor, from New York City, one half of the DWA World Tag Team Champions- Pain Factory, this is “ACID”
ANDREW DOWNEY! Downey and his manager “Neon” Leon Parker enter the Kingdome to a barrage of negative reactions. Andrew struts confidently to the ring, ignoring the crowd, and takes up position opposite Vinnie as Parker aggravates the ringside fans.
Scott: Our third and fourth competitors hail from New York City and
Osaka, Japan, respectively. They are the tag team partners THUNDER and
LIGHTNING! Thunder and Lightning stride into the arena, followed closely by their manager, Tommy J. Dynomite. As they approach the ring, Parker walks up and begins whispering to Dynomite. The men shake as if an agreement has been made. Thunder and Lightning climb into the ring and join Downey.
Scott: Our fifth competitor, from San Antonio, Texas, the current DWA
Internet Champion- THE SERIAL KILLA!
The Serial KiLLa enters the arena to a standing ovation, and shakes hands with the fans as he makes his way to the ring. He slips into the ring, and after a moment of indecision, joins Vinnie in his corner.
Scott: And our final competitor, a two time DWA Internet Champion, from the plains of Wyoming- FIREWALKER!
Firewalker bursts onto the floor, and the fans roar with approval. Dr. Insane follows his wrestler to the ring, and instructs him to join Vinnie and the Serial KiLLa. The three shake hands as the referee calls for the bell.
Thunder, Lightning, and Downey rush the three fan favorites, and are greeted with three drop kicks that send them flying. Vinnie catapults himself off the ropes and splashes Downey. Firewalker drags Thunder to his feet, but Lightning strikes him from behind, breaking the hold.
Lightning holds Firewalker as Thunder begins kicking him in the right knee. The Serial KiLLa launches himself from the top turnbuckle, and drops
Lightning with an ax handle to the head. Firewalker and the Serial KiLLa begin team up on Thunder, and knock him over the top rope with a double clothesline.
Scott: Thunder has been eliminated! Vinnie and Downey exchange slaps to the chest, then Downey slings Vinnie into Firewalker, and both former champs go over the tope rope.
Scott: Vinnie and Firewalker have been eliminated! Downey and Lightning attempt to team up on the Serial KiLLa, but the
KiLLa eludes their grasp and nails them both from behind with a mule kick. Both men are stunned, and the KiLLa slams their heads together.
Lightning drops to the canvas as the Serial KiLLa locks up with Downey. Downey slings the KiLLa into the ropes, but misses a clothesline on the rebound. The KiLLa bounces off the opposite ropes and catches Downey from behind with a DDT. The KiLLa drags Downey to his feet and tosses him over the top rope.
Scott: “Acid” Andrew Downey is eliminated! Lightning has staggered to his feet and leans in the corner as the KiLLa charges him. The KiLLa goes for the splash, but Lightning ducks and he hits the ringpost. Lightning lifts the KiLLa to the top turnbuckle and climbs up. The Serial KiLLa begins struggling as Lightning raises him for a superplex. Both men tumble over the side of the ring and drop to the floor. The crowd is confused as the referee walks over to the broadcast table to review the tape. After a few moments, he grabs Lightning’s hand and raises it in victory.
Scott: The referee has determined that the Serial KiLLa hit the floor first, and thus Lightning is the new DWA Intercontinental Champion!
Julie: What a finish! It looked like the Serial KiLLa was going to get his second belt, but the replay showed that he hit the floor just nanoseconds before Lightning!
Garrett: It doesn’t get any closer than that! Speaking of close matches, our next title bout will feature two wrestlers that have had very close matches before.
Julie: That’s right, Garrett, but this time will be the last chance for
Bane to challenge for the DWA World Championship belt- at least while
Apollo holds it! It’s “put up or shut up” time for Bane! Let’s go to the ring!
Scott: Ladies and gentlemen, this next bout is for the DWA World Championship belt! Introducing first, the challenger, accompanied to the ring by his manager Jon Robinson, from Toronto, standing 7’ 2” tall and weighing 378 pounds, this is BANE!
Bane stalks into the Kingdome, followed closely by the load mouth Robinson. The crowd steps back from Bane, fearful that this unpredictable monster might attack. Bane climbs into the ring and Robinson follows.
Scott: And now the DWA World Champion! Accompanied to the ring by his manager Thantos Polopolous, from Athens, Greece, standing 6’ 7” tall and weighing 315 pounds, please welcome APOLLO!
Apollo and Polopolous step into a tremendous ovation, and slowly make their way up the aisle, pausing for pictures. Apollo climbs into the ring and Robinson gets in his face. A brief argument ends in Robinson getting ejected from the arena floor by the official. Thantos Polopolous escorts
Robinson to the locker room as the bell rings.
Apollo and Bane lock up in the center of the ring. Apollo drives Bane into the turnbuckle and is forced to break the hold when Bane steps through the ropes. Apollo steps back, and is rewarded with a punch to the face. Bane pounces on the stunned Apollo and begins slamming his head into the mat.
Apollo breaks free and scurries out of the ring to gather his thoughts. Bane is directed to a neutral corner as the referee starts the count. Apollo tries to climb back into the ring, but Bane greets him with a boot to the jaw. Ignoring the referee’s pleas, Bane climbs to the top turnbuckle and drops on Apollo, driving him into the broadcast table. A brawl ensues, and both men become dangerously close to being counted out.
Bane drags Apollo into the ring, and lines him up for the Death Drop. Apollo reverses it into the Gyro, and piledrives Bane into the canvas. Bane kicks out at the two count, and Apollo drags him to his feet. Apollo tries another Gyro, but this time Bane reverses it into the Death Drop, powerbombing Apollo into the canvas. Apollo kicks out at the two and one half count.
Both men struggle to their feet and lock up. Bane pushes Apollo into the corner and begins choking him. Apollo breaks free with a knee to the gut, but as he’s gathering his breath, Bane nails him with a neck breaker.
Apollo is down, but Bane climbs to the top rope, intent on doing more damage to the champ. Bane somersaults onto the prone Apollo’s stomach and knocks the wind out of him. As Apollo struggles to breath, Bane drops a leg across his throat and pins him.
Scott: Your winner, and NEW DWA WORLD CHAMPION- BANE! Jon Robinson rushes out of the locker room as Bane stands over the fallen
Apollo. At Robinson’s urging, Bane pulls Apollo to his feet, and executes another Death Drop. Poseidon and Thantos Polopolous come rushing out of the locker room as Robinson urges Bane to execute yet another Death Drop.
Poseidon pulls Apollo free from the new champ, and nails him with a heart punch. Lord Byron rushes out to the ring and attacks Poseidon with a chair. Apollo and Bane lock up again, and both go tumbling over the ropes and through the announcer’s table. The DWA Enforcers emerge from the locker rooms and pull the wrestlers apart. The Enforcers drag Apollo and
Poseidon to the showers as Bane, Robinson, and Byron celebrate.
Julie: We have a new champion, with an attitude called Jon Robinson! Garrett: That guy has been trouble from day one! And NOW he’s got a World
Champion to protect him! I’ve got a feeling that bad times are coming to the DWA!
Julie: This next bout will do nothing to belay those feelings, as two of the most notorious tag teams are due to lock up and unify the tag team title! We’ll have the DWA World and Internet Tag Team Championship match next after this break!
