DWA — Pay-Per-View

DWA Championship Tournament (Part 4 of 4 — Finals)

25 March 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Garrett Montgomery: We're back with the final matches of the tournament! Johnny X: And my man Byron is in one! Garrett: Yes, but it's not the one he was counting on! Vinnie will defend his title against Dr. Joe Slasher, M.D., and your Lord Byron will attempt to win the Internet Championship belt in a match against
Firewalker. And here he comes, accompanied by his manager, Dr. Insane, from Wyoming, the 6' 8", 285 pound native American, FIREWALKER!
The arena erupts with the sounds of his tribal drums as Firewalker rushes to the ring, attired in buckskin and warpaint.
Garrett: Firewalker has but one thing on his mind, beating Lord Byron for the new Internet Championship belt. Wait, what's this, King Benny II is joining us. King Benny: Now I would like all you petty, working class peons to stand up and show some respect for the man who WILL become the DWA Internet
Champion tonight, probably the most gifted technical wrestler in the world today, from Oxford, England, weighing in at 265 pounds-
Looooooooorrrd Byyyyyyyrrrooooooon!!! Byron walks out of the entrance, treating the crowd to a look of total disdain before making his way down to the ring. He enters the ring, turns and performs a French bow to the cameras before preparing for the match.
Johnny: Byron, Byron, Byron! Firewalker starts to attack, but Lord Byron steps through the ropes. As
Firewalker retreats, Byron rushes him and knocks him to the apron. Byron attempts to flip over the ropes on to Firewalker, but Firewalker catches him and rams him into the ring post.
Firewalker climbs back into the ring and the King is assisting Byron. Byron crawls onto the mat and is greated by a tremendous leg drop across the back of the neck. Firewalker drags Bryon to the center of the ring, but Benny stops the pin by pulling Firewalker's leg.
Firewalker climbs to the top turnbuckle as Lord Byron begins to rise. Firewalker flys off the turnbuckle, but Byron caughts him with a heart punch. As Firewalker tried to regain his breath, Lord Byron launches from the top rope and nails him with giant splash.
Instead of going for the cover, Lord Byron returns to the top rope, but this time he's met in mid air by Firewalker and is slammed to the canvas.
Firewalker presses Byron about his head, preparing to slam him again, when King Benny trips Firewalker with his cane. Firewalker slips, and falls backwards into the ropes, dropping Byron on to Benny.
Benny attempts to catch Byron, but fails and Byron hits hard, opening a gash on his chin. Byron struggles up to the ropes and is pulled in by
Firewalker. He struggles to his feet, and is caught right under the chin by a spinning back kick from Firewalker. Byron drops, and Firewalker covers for the pin.
Garrett: We've got a new DWA Internet Champion, FIREWALKER! Johnny: Rematch, rematch, rematch! Garrett: Not so soon, I'm afraid. Due to contractual obligations, Firewalker's first title defense will be against the Bounty Hunter next week on Friday Night Fights. Lord Byron will have to work his way back into title contention.
Johnny: Lord Byron will be back! Lord Byron rules! Garrett: Thanks for that mind boggling insight, Mr. X. Whoa, here we go! The arena explodes with the sounds of maniacal laughter and screams as the evil Dr. Joe Slasher, M.D. saunters up to the ring.
Garrett: It's time for our main event! Introducing first, from Norfolk,
Virginia, weighing in at 295 pounds and standing 6' 9" tall, the challenger, DOCTOR JOE SLASHER, M. D!!!
The arena lights dim once, twice, three times, and a familiar voice thunders out of the PA system-
VINNIE'S IN THE HOUSE! Vinnie steps out into the arena and is mobbed. The DWA World Champion makes his way to the squared circle on the shoulders of his adoring fans.
Garrett: And introducing next, standing 6' 11" tall and weighing 315 pounds, our DWA World Champion, the Original Jersey Boy, VINNIE!
Johnny: I think I'm going to be sick... As Vinnie removes his leather jacket, Slasher attacks with a series of roundhouses to the jaw. Vinnie is staggered, but slips out of an attempted sleeper and slings the Doctor into the ropes. Slasher careens off the ropes and misses a clothesline. Vinnie counters with a choke slam that rocks the arena.
The Doctor regains his feet, only to be dropped again with an elbow to the face. Vinnie climbs to the top rope and drops a leg on the windpipe of the prone Slasher. Slasher rolls out on to the apron to recover.
After what seems like an eternity, Dr. Slasher crawls back into the ring, and is met by a clothesline from Vinnie, which sends both wrestlers on to the apron. Slasher recovers first, and drags Vinnie to his feet. The
Doctor slams the Jersey Boy's head into the barricade, and Vinnie collapses to the arena floor.
Slasher climbs back into the ring, pulling Vinnie with him. He attempts a cover but Vinnie kicks out. Slasher begins to get frustrated, and reaches for his surgical tubing. Vinnie snatches the tubing away, and begins choking Slasher with it, much to the crowd's delight.
Slasher scratches blindly at Vinnie's eyes, and Vinnie releases the choke. Dr. Joe slings Vinnie from turnbuckle to turnbuckle, but when he goes in for the splash he's met with a right under the chin. Slasher is dazed, and Vinnie attacks, plummeling him with a series of slaps to the head. Slasher drops to his knees, and is met by a knee to the jaw that sends him sprawling.
Vinnie pulls Dr. Joe to his knees, and raises his fists in the air. The crowd erupts as Vinnie drops Dr. Slasher with his trademark move, the
Original Jersey Finisher. Vinnie drags the disoriented Slasher to his feet and tosses him over his shoulder. He backs up the turnbuckles until he reaches the top, then slams the Doctor to the mat again with the
Original Jersey Finisher. Slasher is out, and Vinnie covers for the three count.
Garrett: Ladies and Gentlemen, the DWA World Champion, VINNIE!
