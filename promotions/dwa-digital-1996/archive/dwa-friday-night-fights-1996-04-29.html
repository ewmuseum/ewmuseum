Friday Night Fights! (4/29)

29 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Digital Wrestling Alliance presents FRIDAY NIGHT FIGHTS! Live from the Sportatorium in Dallas, Texas! Garrett Montgomery: Here we go again! Welcome back to FRIDAY NIGHT FIGHTS! I'm your host, Garrett Montgomery, and tonight I'll be bringing you the hottest action in wrestling. Two titles are on the line tonight, and we'll see a rematch of the DWA Championship bout between Apollo and
Bane. And to top it all off, Lord Byron returns! Let's go to ringside for the final match in the DWA Internet Championship Tournament- The Nomads versus Thunder and Lightning!
Scott Meridian: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is for the DWA Internet
Tag Team Championship belts! Introducing first, accompanied to the ring by their manager, Tommy J. Dynomite, at a combined weight of 532 pounds,
THUNDER AND LIGHTNING! The crowd reacts with indifference to Thunder and Lightning, irritating
Tommy J. Dynomite. He lashes out at some nearby spectators, and gets drenched with beer and soda. The crowd explodes with laughter.
Scott: And introducing their opponents, at a combined weight of 635 pounds, here is your current DWA Internet Champion FIREWALKER and his partner MISERY- THE NOMADS!
The Nomads enter the Sportatorium to a standing ovation. Firewalker holds the DWA Internet Championship belt high above his head as he walks to the ring. Misery seems uncomfortable without their manager and longtime mentor Dr. Insane.
Thunder and Lightning attack the Nomads before the bell rings and steal
Firewalker's belt. Misery is held at bay by Lightning and Tommy J.
Dynomite as Thunder hammers away at Firewalker's head with the belt. Firewalker collapses, and Thunder drags him out of the ring and on to the announcers table at ringside. As Thunder climbs to the top turnbuckle,
Misery breaks free and nails him with a drop kick in the back. Thunder wobbles, then crashes down to the mat. Misery turns on Lightning and
Dynomite, and clears them from the ring with successive drop kicks.
Misery begins to work on Thunder, driving his knee deep into the smaller man's back. Thunder screeches in agony as Misery applies a cobra clutch.
Lightning enters the ring and breaks the hold with a kick to Misery's head. Misery is dazed, and Thunder and Lightning begin working him over.
A double team clothesline sends him over the top rope and on to Firewalker. The table collapses under the weight of both men as the referee calls for the bell.
Scott: The referee has disqualified Thunder and Lightning! Your winners, and new DWA Internet Tag Team Champions- THE NOMADS!
Thunder and Lightning are angry with the ruling, and surround the referee, backing him into a corner. Dynomite is in his face, screaming obscenities. Without warning gray fog begins pouring from the rafters, engulfing the ring. Mayhem, Inc. emerge from the smoke and begin attacking Thunder and Lightning with barbed wire baseball bats. Thunder and Lightning retreat from the ring, bloodied and bruised, leaving Tommy
J. Dynomite surrounded by Mayhem. "Legend" Josh Brown emerges from the growing fog and drops Dynomite with a set of brass knuckles. He laughs wildly and drags the limp Dynomite into the fog. The fog dissipates, leaving only two bloody baseball bats in the ring.
Garrett: What in the...? Mayhem just made an unscheduled appearance in the DWA, and apparently they have a score to settle with Thunder and
Lightning! We'll try to get more information on the status of Tommy J.
Dynomite as soon as it's available. Whoa, another surprise, here comes the Original Jersey Boy, Vinnie! Welcome to Friday Night Fights, Vinnie!
I understand that you have an announcement to make concerning your future in the DWA? Vinnie: That's right Garrett, with April Anarchy in the past I need some support in my corner. So I enlisted as my manager, the legend Golden
Johney Pathos!
Garrett: Welcome to the DWA, Johney! As Vinnie's new manager, what is your first order of business?
Johney: Well, first I would like to thank Mr. Apollo for his help, on behalf of Vinnie. Garrett: Are there any plans for a rematch with Dr. Joe Slasher? Johney: Slasher, mark my word- you will be paid in full for your actions, paid in full! Garrett: Vinnie, you may not want to discuss this, but I have to ask. What happened between you and your former partner Tony? Vinnie: Tony and I decided that we should both go back to singles competition and I haven't heard from him in a few months.
Garrett: Any last words for your legion of fans, Vinnie? Vinnie: Just keep believing in me and I will regain my place in the DWA. Garrett: Thanks, gentlemen, for stopping by. It's good to see that Vinnie has recovered from the Texas Death Chamber match, and with Johney Pathos on his side he should be back at the top of the DWA in no time. As they clean up the ring, let's go to the DWA Commando Center for highlights of the earlier matches.
----- DWA Commando Center ----- Apollo (w/ Thantos Polopolous) vs. Bane (w/ Jon Robinson) In this rematch of the DWA World Championship match from APRIL ANARCHY,
Bane attacked Apollo like a man possessed. After a couple of close pins,
Bane got frustrated and pulled a chair into the ring. The referee immediately disqualified him, awarding the victory to Apollo. Polopolous and Robinson got into it at ringside after the match, with Robinson begging for mercy after Polopolous applied the full nelson.
Your winner- Apollo Jason Andrews vs. Monster Lucas Masai (w/ Shelton Colby, DWA President) Masai seemed distracted in his first match supervised by Colby, and
Andrews took the advantage, nailing him with successive top rope drop kicks. Andrews got the pin after a tremendous vertical suplex.
Your winner- Jason Andrews Alex "the Hammer" Simpson (w/ "Neon" Leon Parker) vs. Coyote Coyote's superior conditioning prevailed in this marathon match. Coyote got the win with a top rope DDT after almost one hour of brutal wrestling. After the match, Coyote was jumped in the parking lot by
Simpson and his partner from Pain Factory, "Acid" Andrew Downey. Coyote escaped with a slight concussion.
Your winner- Coyote Big Town Bully (w/ the Godfather) vs. The Serial KiLLa The Serial KiLLa looked to be outmanned in this match, as the Godfather assisted the Bully from ringside. After a near pinfall, the Bully went for broke with a top rope splash and missed. The Serial KiLLa press slammed the staggered Bully for the pin.
Your winner- the Serial KiLLa "Acid" Andrew Downey (w/ "Neon" Leon Parker) vs. The Bounty Hunter Still fired up after the beating of Coyote in the parking lot, Downey wasted no time in dispatching the Bounty Hunter. The pinfall came after a perfect moonsault buried the Bounty Hunter in the mat.
Your winner- "Acid" Andrew Downey Venom (w/ Dark) vs. The Hitman (w/ the Godfather) The Godfather's boys went 0 for 2 tonight when newcomer Venom stopped the
Hitman with the Final Bite, a powerslam off the top rope. Venom continued to plummel the Hitman after the bell until Dark managed to pull him away.
Your winner- Venom Mayhem, Inc. (w/ "Legend" Josh Brown) vs. The Burly Bunch (w/ Hurly Burly) Hurly Burly and the boys welcomed the newcomers Mayhem, Inc. to the DWA with an out of control brawl. The end came when the 555 pound big man
Eddy Gonzalez pinned Masked Mayhem 1 after a flying splash.
Your winners- the Burly Bunch ----- Garrett: Gonzalez and Damage seem to be improving weekly- I would expect the Burly Bunch to be challenging for the Internet title soon! And the
World Champions Pain Factory seem to work well as a team both in and out of the ring. But they had better watch their backs, because Coyote is not going to forget that parking lot attack! We're just about set for the return of Lord Byron, but first let's go to some prerecorded comments from Roscoe Law, the manager of Wasp Man.
----- Roll Tape- Wasp Man ----- (Wasp Man and Roscoe Law are in the gym sparring in the ring. They take a break as Roscoe speaks.)
Roscoe Law: Well, Waspy, looks like you got a reeeeal winner this week. Good ol' Lord Byron, some putz making himself to be something he ain't. Well, Little Lord Fauntleroy, here's an analogy that even you can comprehend. You can take a pile of crap, put a robe on it, stick a pipe in it, and have women stand around it, however, all you're left with is a ruined robe, a waste of a pipe, and a waste of some fine women. And when a piece of crap like you gets in the ring with Wasp Man, all you'll do is make a mess of the mat. Somebody bring the damn squeegee.
----- Garrett: Well, it's obvious that Roscoe Law isn’t buying Byron’s snake oil. Let's head to the ring and sort this one out.
Scott: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first... King Benny II: Give me the mike, you peon. Your not worthy to speak
Byron's name. Ladies and gentlemen, you are honored to witness the return of one of the world's finest technical wrestlers. Hailing from
Oxford, England, and weighing in at 265 pounds, would you all get up on your feet and show some respect for Looooooord Byyyyyrrrrron!!
Intermezzo from Karelia Suite plays over the P.A., and Lord Byron makes his way down to ringside, sneering at the crowd. He gets into the ring and performs a French bow to the camera.
Scott: And his opponent, accompanied to the ring by his manager Roscoe
Law, from Japan, standing 6' 3" tall and weighing a solid 220 pounds, this is WASP MAN!
The crowd goes crazy as the popular Wasp Man enters the arena. He stops to sign a few autographs, then propels himself into the ring, driving
Byron and Benny on to the apron. The referee allows Lord Byron back into the ring and calls for the bell.
Wasp Man attempts to drop-kick Lord Byron, but Byron catches his foot and twists him down to the mat. Byron begins working on the foot and leg, applying pressure with a vicious toe hold. Wasp Man pulls himself to the ropes and the referee calls for the break.
Byron releases the hold, then drags Wasp Man to the ringpost, where he begins slamming the knee into the post. Wasp Man kicks out and hobbles to the center of the ring, beckoning Byron to join him. Byron reenters the ring and is met with a flurry of heart punches, driving him to the mat.
Wasp Man gets the two count before Byron reverses the pin. Wasp Man kicks out at two. Wasp Man is hobbling, and Byron takes him down with a leg sweep. He slips
Wasp Man into the figure four leglock, and twists it into the sharpshooter. After what seems to be an eternity, Wasp Man submits. Byron releases the hold and leaps from the ring victorious, leaving Wasp Man clutching his knee.
Scott: Your winner by submission- Lord Byron! Garrett: An impressive return by Byron... Wait, Byron's back in the ring! He's asking for the mike!
Someone outside the ring passes Byron the mike, and with a look of complete disdain at Wasp Man, who is clutching his leg in agony, addresses the crowd with a French bow.
Lord Byron: FIREWALKER!! I haven't forgotten how you defeated me, you peon! I want that belt! Put it on the line, my friend, I cannot be reduced to fighting fools like these!
Byron throws down the mike and kicks Wasp man hard. He grabs Wasp Man's leg, and puts him in the Aristoclutch. Wasp Man is screaming in pain.
Garrett: Oh come on, someone get down there! Byron is out of control!! The referee gets back in the ring, and tries to make Byron let go with a warning count, Byron does, but pushes the ref away and hits Wasp Man with a kneebreaker, before throwing him out of the ring. Byron picks up the mike again.
Lord Byron: Get down here Firewalker, or have you no courage?? This is just a taste of what you'll get next time we meet!!!
Byron leaps from the apron, dropping a knee on Wasp Man's injured leg. Roscoe Law tries to stop him, but gets thrown into the barricade. Suddenly two masked men wearing DWA emblazoned black leather jackets burst into the arena and subdue Lord Byron. King Benny attempts to intervene as they are handcuffing Byron, and he too is handcuffed. The masked men drag the struggling Byron and Benny from the arena.
Garrett: Whoa! Another surprise from the DWA! Who were those guys, and what are they going to do to Byron and Benny? Let's go back to Scott
Meridian, who is standing by in the locker room. Scott?
Scott: Thanks, Garrett. I'm here in the locker room, tracking the two masked men and their captives. It appears that Lord Byron and King Benny are being treated to cold showers courtesy of the DWA. Wait, there's
President Colby! Shelton, could we have a word?
Shelton Colby: Hello, Scott. As you can tell, I have instituted a new enforcement policy in the DWA, and my two enforcers will be dispatched to quell the rising violence.
Scott: Who are the masked men, and why are they masked? Shelton: The masks allow them to remain anonymous, which is essential to enforcing the rules. As far as their identities are concerned, that information is confidential.
Scott: When will we see the DWA Enforcers again? Shelton: Whenever there's a serious problem, the DWA Enforcers will be dispatched. Scott: Thanks, president Colby. I've got to get back to the ring. Garrett? Garrett: Thanks for the update, Scott. We're almost ready for tonight's final match, Firewalker versus Kuwasi "Black Fist" Shakur for the DWA
Internet Championship. Let’s go the ring!
Scott: Ladies and gentlemen, tonight's final bout is for the DWA Internet
Championship belt! Introducing first, the challenger. Accompanied to the ring by DWA President Shelton Colby and his partner Monster Lucas Masai, from the South Bronx in New York, standing 6' 9" tall and weighing an even 300 pounds, here is KUWASI "BLACK FIST" SHAKUR!
Shakur and Masai enter the arena, followed closely by Shelton Colby. The crowd reacts negatively, but the hard-core fans of Shakur scream "shoot,
Fist, shoot!" Shakur is calm and focused, and acknowledges their cries by slowly raising his fists above his head.
Scott: And introducing next, accompanied to the ring by his tag team partner Misery, from Wyoming, weighing 285 pounds and standing 6' 8"
tall, the current DWA Internet Tag Team Champion and your DWA INTERNET CHAMPION- FIREWALKER! Firewalker and Misery enter the arena to a standing ovation, but both are still suffering from the beating administered by Thunder and Lightning.
Shelton Colby approaches Firewalker, and after a brief conference allows him to enter the ring. The official calls for the bell.
Shakur and Firewalker lock up in the middle of the ring. Firewalker takes down Shakur with a belly to belly suplex, but Shakur kicks free of the pin. Shakur slings Firewalker into the turnbuckle, and follows with a flying elbow to the head.
Firewalker staggers out of the corner and collapses in the center of the ring. Shakur, puzzled by his opponent's collapse, slowly rolls him over for the pin. Firewalker remains on the mat as Misery and Shelton Colby call for the medics.
Scott: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner and NEW DWA INTERNET CHAMPION- KUWASI "BLACK FIST" SHAKUR! Garrett: What a shocker! Firewalker was out before the match really got started. Obviously the attack from Thunder and Lightning affected him dramatically! Hopefully he'll recover before the Internet Tag Team title defense against 911. And Shakur will defend his newly acquired Internet belt against the Serial KiLLa on next week's Friday Night Fights! See you then!
