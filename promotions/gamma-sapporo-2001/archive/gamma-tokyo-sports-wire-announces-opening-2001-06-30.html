TOKYO SPORTS WIRE GAMMA Announces Opening By: Homare Ota (6.30.01) Wealthy businessman Ryota Kazama held a press conference toady announcing the official opening of his new puroresu promotion Goukai Alpha Mixed Martial Arts (GAMMA). The press conference was held outside a Sapporo office building whose ninth floor houses GAMMA's headquarters. On display were the Combative Tag-Team Title belts, which were purchased by Kazama after G-Pro closed its doors. The Combative Tag-Team Championship is tentatively planned to be awarded during GAMMA's second tour, the Gemini Battle Festival. Kazama opened the press conference thanking everyone for attending and gave a short speech on how GAMMA will bring a unique style of puroresu throughout Japan that will hopefully catch the attention of the fans. GAMMA president/owner Ryota Kazama promised to stay loyal to Sapporo, running at least one show of each tour in the area. The first show, in fact, will be held in the 1,200-seat Teisen Hall, only a few blocks away from the GAMMA offices. Before Kazama announced the line-up of GAMMA's inaugural rally, a reporter joking asked if he'd be participating on the card, referring to Kazama's days competing in Brazilian vale tudo events. The 52-year-old laughed the question off saying that he's a little old to make a comeback. He pretended to rethink his statement when someone mentioned the booking of 43-year-old Noboru Kuwabara. Ryota Kazama the started announcing the card, which will take place on July 10, giving short profiles on each fighter as we went along. The opening contest will pit Dutch kickboxer Ashwin Seko against former J-Fight star Hisashi Kuroyama. The second match still isn’t finalized, but another former J-Fight combatant, Noboru Kuwabara, will be in action. The semi-main event will have TOP star Hayato Ikeda against IKAJIN. The main event brought the most excitement from the crowd and will probably be responsible to draw the fans. Two former JPA stars will face off in a tag-team contest, Keiji Kobe and Kaijiro Kuroyama will fight Eiji Yatsu and Takumi Make. To help huild up to this match, Kobe and Yatsu will have a special 10-minute expedition mach on the TOP T-3 tour ender, two days before GAMMA’s initial show. After the card line-up was announced, Kazama was somewhat criticized for the fact only three fighters, Yatsu, Kobe, and Ikeda, have committed to be a long term part of GAMMA. Kazama quickly claimed that the permanent roster would fill up quickly and in the mean time GAMMA would go on using freelancers until that happens. While Kazama brushed it off, if GAMMA can't find a group of wrestlers that are wiling to work for the company, its future may be in peril. - Peter [detail withheld] http://groups.yahoo.com/group/gammapuoresu AIM: [detail withheld]
