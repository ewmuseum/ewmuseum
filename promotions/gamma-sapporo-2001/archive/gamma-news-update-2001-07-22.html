T O K Y O S P O R T S W I R E =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= GAMMA News Update 7.22.01 By: Homare Ota (7.22.01) [x] GAMMA did fairly well drawing against the CLLL Japan yesterday. While GAMMA didn’t sell out Differ Ariake, CLLL Japan faired much less. No one can really predict the future of either company, as fans are just starting to warm up to them, but it will be interesting to see how things work out in the Tokyo market with TOP, CLLL Japan, and now Kitty! Visual operating in the area. TOP, however, does have the advantage of starting to expand to a national scale, so the smaller promotions may have an easier time. GAMMA is a good position as their home base is Sapporo, a distance away from Tokyo, plus President Kazama is willing to run rallies in other major cities besides Tokyo. [x] After the very good Suzuki/Ikeda v. Yatsu/Kubo match, it is unknown how the GAMMA-TOP relationship will go. Both GAMMA and TOP seem willing to allow a Yatsu-Suzuki one on one match, the problem is neither company will likely allow the other to promote the show alone. GAMMA looks like they may try to run without TOP assistance, rather use other independent talent, but with combatants such as Daijiro Kuroyama and other CLLL Japan’ers signing contracts, it will be much more expensive to use them on GAMMA rallies. Kitty! Visual seems the likely partner for GAMMA currently, as President Kazama is wants to use Akitoshi Kubo on a semi-regular basis, and would like to use other Kitty! Visual talent at the next rally, besides the CLLL Mexican Tag-Team match. [x] The next show for GAMMA is slated to be on July 30 at the Kobe Sambo Hall (2000 seats). This will be a taping of a Fighting Samurai TV Battle Station that will air on August 3. The main event will be Yamada and Mako defending their newly won CLLL Mexican Tag-Team titles against a GAMMA team. It’s unknown who this team will be comprised of, but in all likelihood it will be the team of Eiji Yatsu and Akitoshi Kubo. Yatsu and Kubo told reporters today that they will be forming a hopefully regular tag-team to be called Boujakubujin, a Japanese word meaning defiance and insolence. The official card will be release on the 26th of this month. [x] Until the card is released, President Kazama will be searching for additional combatants to add to the GAMMA roster. Kazama will look a native independent talent, and review tapes and information sent in by gaijin fighters. If Kazama is able to sign two to four combatants in the next few days, the future of GAMMA will be more solidified. If he isn’t, the company may be in danger, since without a roster that can compete regularly, without interruption of other booking dates, a tour will not be able to occur. Any combatants interested in joining the company should contact Kazama at [detail withheld]
