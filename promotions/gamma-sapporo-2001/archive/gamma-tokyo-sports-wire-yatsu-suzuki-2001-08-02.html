Tokyo Sports Wire: Yatsu-Suzuki Will Happen

2 August 2001

T O K Y O S P O R T S W I R E =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Yatsu-Suzuki Will Happen By: Homare Ota (8.2.01) Today at the press conference to announce the line-up for GAMMA’s first Battle Station taping, GAMMA owner and president Ryota Kazama announced that a deal had been made to run a TOP Jr. Heavyweight Title match between current champion Shoji Suzuki and GAMMA combatant Eiji Yatsu. Yatsu will receive the first shot at the title in exchange for Suzuki being named as Yatsu’s partner in the match for the CLLL Mexican Tag-Team Titles. Though it is a somewhat unusual request, it is obvious that Fugiwara of TOP wants to make his biggest draw even bigger as one half of the CLLL Mexican Tag Titles. The same can be said for Kazama in the case of Yatsu, who is currently the biggest star under contract with GAMMA. The exact date for the title match is still up in the air, but TOP officials assured Kazama that the match will occur in the near future. The main event will have the CLLL Mexican Tag-Team Champions Kyohei Yamada and Takumi Mako defend their titles against Eiji Yatsu and Shoji Suzuki. CLLL Japan, whom the champions work primarily for, has been under equally harsh conditions that GAMMA has been under, but as GAMMA appeared to improve, CLLL Japan recently announced the cancellation of their first tour and that all contracts have been dropped. The Sex Dog team will likely continue to work for CLLL Japan, as well as freelance for other companies. The semi-main event will feature the debut of American Chris Micheals against Kentaro Satake. Micheals is most known for his tenure in the Elite Shoot Wrestling Promotion, and has told media that he is living a dream doing a tour of Japan. Satake is coming of a no-contest from the last GAMMA show. That result really killed any momentum Satake had, as well as turning the fans against him. The second bout on the card will feature the debut of recently signed Yoshashi Sato is a cross-promotions tag team contest. Sato will team with Sex Dogs fighter IKAJIN against the Kitty! Visual team of Akitoshi Kubo and Hello Kitty! Mask III. The opening bout might as well be made a kickboxing match as Kitty! Visual fight Yoshiji Yamanaki will face Ashwin Seko. Both men possess considerable height (Yamanaki is 6’7 and Seko is 6’5) and have a heavy background in the kickboxing sport. August 6, 2001 @ Kobe Sambo Hall To be broadcasted on Samurai TV on August 10, 2001 CLLL Mexican Tag-Team Titles: Kyohei Yamada & Takumi Mako (Champions) [CLLL Japan Team] v. Shoji Suzuki [TOP] & Eiji Yatsu Chris Micheals v. Kentaro Satake Yoshashi Sato/IKAJIN [CLLL Japan] v. Akitoshi Kubo/Hello Kitty! Mask IIII [K!V Team] Yoshiji Yamanaki [K!V] v.
