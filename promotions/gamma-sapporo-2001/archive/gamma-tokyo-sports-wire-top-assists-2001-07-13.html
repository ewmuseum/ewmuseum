Tokyo Sports Wire: TOP Assists in Second GAMMA Rally

T O K Y O S P O R T S W I R E =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= TOP Assists in Second GAMMA Rally By: Homare Ota (7.13.01) While the show has already been announced by the Tokyo Organization of Puroresu, and advertising for it began the day after the first GAMMA rally, President Ryota Kazama officially gave word that GAMMA would run Differ Ariake a week from tomorrow, July 21. The show would see plenty of help from outside talent, including two wrestlers from CLLL Japan and three from TOP. Kazama and TOP headman Fujiwara seem to have forged a working relationship, mainly focusing on the junior heavyweights. The main event of the card will feature Shoji Suzuki, the TOP Jr. Heavyweight Champion, and Hayato Ikeda against Eiji Yatsu and Takumi Mako. Many Tokyo puroresu fans remember the early TOP contest between Yatsu and Suzuki, and after Yatsu tag-team victory over Keiji Kobe (Though he didn’t defeat the man himself) Eiji is positioning himself as one of the top combatants in GAMMA. Besides the possibilities of the Yatsu-Suzuki lock up, the Ikeda-Yatsu confrontations will be more important in the long run in GAMMA as if Ikeda can score a KO or submission over Yatsu, he’d be immediately elevated to the top. The semi-main event will have TOP wrestler Mike Johnson face-off against Kentaro Satake. Satake brutalized Noboru Kuwabara in their fight at the debut rally. With a victory over Johnson, Satake could be setting himself up for the main event in GAMMA, but some say his checkered past may hurt him in the short term as Kazama may not want Satake to represent GAMMA. However, there is word of Satake and another GAMMA wrestler competing at the next TOP card in a six-man tag match. In the other two matches on the card, Takao Kimura, TORA Wanizame Memorial Tournament semi-finalist while meet Hisashi Kuroyama, and Ashwin Seko and IKAJIN will fight the team of Noboru Kuwabara and Daijiro Kuroyama. IKAJIN and Kuroyama represent the new CLLL Japan company. At their debut rally, IKAJIN forced Kuroyama to submit during the course of a six-main tag match. As mentioned before, Satake and another GAMMA combatant will be the first to compete at a TOP card as part of this Fujiwara-Kazama deal. There have been rumors that Suzuki may defend his title against either Eiji Yatsu or Keiji Kobe. The latter is conspicuously absent from this show. GAMMA can ill afford to lose one of its top draws. The Kobe camp hasn’t been in contact with the GAMMA offices, save one note, so Kobe’s future in the company is uncertain. Again the thought arises that no new combatants have been added to the GAMMA roster, which again shows the danger the promotion is in. The TOP talent deal will be very important for GAMMA until it creates a solid group of its own workers. Arena Show, Differ Ariake, Tokyo July 21, 2001 Ashwin Seko/IKAJIN v. Noboru Kuwabara/Daijiro Kuroyama Takao Kimura v. Hisashi Kuroyama Kentaro Satake v. Mike Johson Shoji Suzuki/Hayato Ikeda v.
