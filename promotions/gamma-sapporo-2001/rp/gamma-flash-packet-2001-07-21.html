GAMMA (Sapporo, 2001) — Roleplay

GAMMA Flash Packet 7.21.01

21 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GAMMA Flash Packet 7.21.01 1) Eiji Yatsu & Akitoshi Kubo 2) Shoji Suzuki EIJI YATSU & AKITOSHI KUBO: [The camera fades into a gym. The camera appears to be sitting on the ground, being propped up on something. Akitoshi Kubo is kneeling in front of it, occupying much of the screen. Over his shoulder and under Kubo’s arm, Eiji Yatsu can be seen in the background delivering various kicks to a heavy bag. Both Yatsu and Kubo are wearing red adidas basketball shoes, and dark blue pants with red stripes. Kubo is in a black t-shirt with “I will not instigate revolution” written six times on it in white, while Yatsu isn’t wearing a top. Kubo has short hair and side burns that go almost all the way down his face, both of which are pure white in color.] Kubo: Very few people know my face, but I was going to be the next big thing. The top pankratist in the welterweight division, I was being trained in the Steel Wind Dojo to wrestle in JPA. We all can guess how that ended. Right now I’m searching for a home, and was asked to come here to wrestle in GAMMA as a last minute replacement for Mako to face Shoji Suzuki and Ikeda. I saw the raw handheld footage of the Ikeda-IKAJIN contest. I saw the complete lack of intensity that Ikeda brought, but somehow he was able to defeat the CLLL Japan squid boy. You have no excuse, you aren’t coming off a big loss; this time you better bring your best to deal with my fist [Kubo brings his hand up in a fist] and Yatsu’s knee. [On cue, Eiji grips the bag and starts laying in knee strikes.] Suzuki, you are one of the few combatants in the sport that actually has the history to back up what everyone says about him. You’re easily recognizable by the fans and all the combatants everywhere. You’ve accomplished much in your career, but the past was yesterday and our match is tomorrow. When I joined the Defy dojo I learned to do just that, defy the past and create the future. Suzuki, you may be the TOP Jr. Heavyweight Champion, but you didn’t defeat me and you definitely didn’t beat Yatsu. We’re going to give you a test no TOP wrestler ever has. [At this point, Eiji comes over and takes a knee by Akitoshi.] Yatsu: Remember, Suzuki, I’m the guy that was able to take you down. Some say that the flash pin was a fluke; well here in GAMMA there are no pins. This time I’ll show that it’s no fluke when you get knocked out or just can’t take it anymore and submit. [The camera fades out.] SHOJI SUZUKI [Open on a muscular young Japanese man standing against a black background. His long jet black hair is pulled back into a ponytail. He wears long silver and black tights, black kneepads and shingaurds pulled over black Asics, a black elbowpad on his right arm and black tape on his wrists and hands. He is also wearing a championship belt around his waist. He smiles and executes a small bow towards the camera.] Man: "Greetings fans of GAMMA. My name is Shoji Suzuki and I am the newly crowned TOP Junior Heavyweight champion." [Suzuki runs his hand across the belt around his waist.] Suzuki: "When I was invited to wrestle on the next GAMMA card, I jumped at the chance for many reasons. First, to team with my friend and ally from TOP Hayato Ikeda. Ikeda I feel is the future of the TOP junior division and could very well lead the way in GAMMA as well. Second, another opportunity to wrestle Eiji Yatsu. Not in some foolish desire to 'revenge' my loss thelast time we fought in TOP. No." [Suzuki shakes his head and smiles wider.] Suzuki: "No, this is an opportunity to test myself against one of the best juniors in Japan. Our last encounter has been called a classic by some, and I would agree. I hope to live up to the standard we set and perhaps surpass it this time." [Suzuki looks down for a moment. He lets out a breath and continues.] Suzuki: "The final reason I quickly agreed to wrestle here in GAMMA was to proudly represent TOP outside of the promotion. As a champion I should seek out the best and GAMMA qualifies. I was greatly impressed by the Keiji Kobe/Eiji Yatsu exhibition match in TOP. I will be forced to adapt to the GAMMA style, another challange I greatly look forward to facing. Thank you for the opportunity." [Suzuki executes a curt bow and then raises his fist in front of his chest in the classic Chiimu Tora(Team Tiger) salute. Fade to black.]
