GAMMA (Sapporo, 2001) — Roleplay

GAMMA Flash Packet 7.10.01

10 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GAMMA Flash Packet 7.10.01 1) Eiji Yatsu 2) Kentaro Satake EIJI YATSU: [The camera fades into Eiji Yatsu standing on the steps outside Teisen Hall. He’s wearing a GAMMA t-shirt and dark blue adidas pants with red stripes. His face-long black dreadlocks are being held together by red rubber bands. Yatsu has a look of calm intensity on his face.] Yatsu: So here it is, the inaugural GAMMA rally. When the Prez contacted me and told of what GAMMA would have to offer, I knew I must be part of this. If people look back at my early career here in Dai Nippon, they’ll see that my style would fit perfectly for this environment. For plants, they say that give the perfect environment they’ll thrive beyond anything you imagine. I suppose we’ll find out if the same is true for the simple creature we call man. [Yatsu pauses a second, looking away from the camera, then starts staring almost through it towards someone on opposite end.] Kobe, you must remember my hatred for you, our arguments over the issues of Pure Fight and lucha libre, and our consistent petty insults. After our draw I believe we were moving away from that, but the words of Inoue clouded my mind. While Inoue is a strong competitor and good teacher, his philosophy of taking away much of what I was able to do in that ring was what that brought me down. The man you faced in the Junior Struggle League isn’t the man you faced at the TOP rally, and isn’t the man you’ll face at the GAMMA card. As you’ve seen, I am a much more complete fighter. My offense isn’t limited to what Inoue taught me. I’m also not the disciple I once was. I don’t look towards Inoue for every movement. Kobe, my days of believing you are my enemy are over. You are nearly my rival. Hatred doesn’t pump through my veins, but the desire to be victorious over you does. Ever chance I’ve had to beat you we’ve gone the distance. At the Teisen Hall, we have others fighting with us, but a win over you is what I will look for and what I shall get. [The camera fades out.] KENTARO SATAKE: [Some strong rythmic Japanese drums begin to pound mercilessly throughout your television speakers. A dark figure is seen in an abandoned boxing gym, shadowboxing in the emptiness of a worn down ring. His fast fists seemingly defy logic, while his kicks are no less slow. The camera closes in as the drums pick up in tempo.] MAN: HAAAAAAAAIIIIIICH---!!! [The shot suddenly changes to the man at speedbag, with one fast overhand hook, and a "HAICH" to boot, the rickety old speed bag explodes upon impact. The man looks at its remnants in disgust. The man is now noticeably of Asian descent, bald, and a goatee covers his menacing scowl. ] [CRAAAAAAAAAAACK!!!] [The scene now changes to an empty, more well lit portion of the gym, the intimidating man and another (presumably a training partner) both have kendo sticks in hand and attack each other furiously. Each swift swing is met with an expert block. The partner swings low and the bigger man jumps over, swinging straight down upon his descent. His opponent blocks, and swings, and connects directly on his opponents shin. The man simply stands there, another scowl forming on his face... The training partner, realizing his mistake, begins to slowly back pedal.] MAN: HAAAAAAAAAAICH! [A sonic speed swing of the kendo stick connects directly to the chest of the poor man, sending him flying, and skidding on the floor. The shot returns to the man in a boxing ring, shadowboxing again, as a professionally deep voice over is heard.] VO: Kentaro Satake, "The Japanese Assassin", has arrived... [A close up shot of Satake is seen, with Satake attacking with a fast right hand.] VO: GAMMA will never be the same... [The scene fades to black as one last "HAAAAAAAAAICH!!!" is heard.]
