WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION THURSDAY KNIGHTS 4/10/97 >From Wellington, New Zealand -----Main Event----- =World Title Match= Shelly Rage v. Blood =Can-Am Title Match= "Nasty Girl" Amber Jane v. Janet Strong The Posse Report Sweet Sue v. Liberty "Psycho" Vicki V v. Bodacious Janet Muniz (Best of 11 Match 6) Christy Cooper v. Mary Williams Freedom v. The Assassin (Cage Match) *Cage Match* -------------------- Freedom (w/ Shannon Stevens) v. The Assassin (w/ Li'l Cheetah) -------------------- Both women lock up. The Assassin drives Freedom back to the ropes, then spins her around and scrapes her face along the cage wall. She spins Freedom back around and executes a snap mare, then pulls Freedom back to the center of the ring and applies a leg hammerlock, starting her offense. After a vicious 4 minutes Freedom blocks a double chickenwing. She spins around and hits the Assassin with a forearm to the back, then sends her to a corner. Freedom runs in and executes a somersault splash, only to nail the turnbuckles as the Assassin lunges out of the way. The Assassin bends down and rewards Freedom's effort with a facerake, resuming her attack. After 3 minutes the Assassin concludes her super-offense with the Sharpshooter. Freedom submits after 20 seconds. (7:05) <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.> <Fireworks explode as the camera shows the crimson WCA logo on the white ring canvas. The camera descends rapidly, then levels off facing the aisle. The camera swerves left, panning out over the capacity crowd and circling the ring, ending its focus on the iridescent three-dimensional WCA letters bracketing the entrance to the backstage area. The referee for the first match heads to the ring.> -------------------- Christy Cooper (w/ Dr. Insane and "Psycho" Vicki V) v. Mary Williams (w/ Crimson and Red) -------------------- Both women lock up. Williams blocks a back suplex; Cooper counters a legsweep faceslam with her own faceslam. She goes down and applies an armlock leglock, taking control of the match. She soon runs into the ropes; Williams misses with a clothesline, then glances back and decks Cooper with an elbow to the jaw. Cooper pops back up and reverses a whip to a corner. Williams blocks a charge in; Cooper staggers back and around. Williams uses the opportunity to execute a crucifix, but only gets a two count. Cooper gets up and rewards Williams with a European uppercut, resuming her offense. Williams soon ducks a lariat, then takes Cooper down with a fireman's carry, turning the match around. She shortly sends Cooper to a corner; Cooper reverses a whip to the opposite corner, then charges in, smashing into Williams and retaking control of the match. Williams soon sidesteps a kneelift, then throws Cooper out of the ring. The ref keeps her from following; Cooper confers with Dr. Insane briefly, then reenters the ring. Williams spins her around; Cooper blocks a back suplex and turns around; Williams turns a European uppercut into a backslide. Cooper rolls it over in two; Williams' momentum rolls her out of the pin. Cooper gets up and blocks a fireman's carry, instead executing an Irish whip to the ropes. She decks Williams with a spinning leg lariat, taking charge. Williams shortly reverses an Irish whip, then jumps a kick, running to the ropes. She jumps another kick, rebounds again, and takes Cooper down with a high cross body; Cooper kicks out in two. She gets up and reverses a whip to a corner, then charges in and smashes into Williams, putting her back on the defensive again. After ninety seconds Williams sidesteps a kneelift. Cooper lands on her back, giving Williams a chance to a corner and scale the turnbuckles. She keeps Cooper on the mat via a flying shoulderblock, turning the match around. Cooper shortly reverses a whip to the corner, then runs in and jumps onto the middle rope, straddling Williams. She grabs Williams by the hair, then raises her fist up and starts punching. The crowd counts along; Williams stops the sixth punch and shoves Cooper off the ropes. Cooper quickly gets up, then dodges a tackle and runs to the ropes. Williams stops her with an ipponzei, then covers; one, two, Cooper throws Williams off her. Williams blocks headscissors, then gets up and meets a standing Cooper with a kick to the midsection, taking charge again. Cooper soon reverses another whip to the corner, then again goes in and starts a series of punches. Williams again stops Cooper at the sixth one; Cooper hangs onto Williams' hair, pulling her out of the corner. Both women get up; Williams takes Cooper back down with an armdrag, resuming her assault. After a minute she turns a European uppercut into a backslide, but only gets a two count. Cooper punches Williams in the stomach, then walks past her to the corner and goes to the top. Williams runs in and returns the punch to the midsection; Cooper falls onto the turnbuckle. Williams climbs up and goes to the top, setting Cooper up. Williams executes the Big Dipper, then covers with a lateral press. Cooper barely kicks out in time. Williams waits for her to get up, taking the opportunity to rest. Cooper slowly gets up; Williams takes her down with a takeover suplex, starting a new offense. After a minute she whips Cooper to the ropes; Cooper ducks a clothesline, then nails Williams with a kick, starting a new, slower assault. After 2 minutes Williams blocks a double reverse chinlock with a facerake, ending Cooper's offense. Cooper very shortly reverses an Irish whip, dodges one shoulderblock, dodges another, then stops Williams with a leaping shoulderblock, capitalizing on an opportunity to retake control. Williams shortly ducks a spinning leg lariat. Cooper slowly gets back up, then blocks a headbutt. She executes a belly-to-back suplex, hurting herself more than Williams. Both women lie motionless on the mat. The ref begins counting; one .. two ... three .. four .. five .. six .. seven .. eight .. nine .. ten. The match is ruled a draw.
