WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION TUESDAY KNIGHTS 12/3/96 Live from Athens, Greece -----Main Event----- <Tag Team Titles Submission Match> Darkness, Inc. v. "Psycho" Vicki V / Christy Cooper "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln v. Janet Strong (non-title match) Double D (Debbie Diamond) v. The Vinnie Babe Double J v. Angel Hunter She-Devil v. Jane Gordon End of Rampage's "Psycho" Vicki V / Christy Cooper v. The Scandalous Ones <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.> <Fireworks explode as the camera shows a top-down view of the ring, highlighting the maroon WCA logo on the canvas. The camera descends, panning to the right and leveling off, showing the capacity crowd. It focuses on the end of the aisle, where 'WCA' frames the entrance to the locker room. Several WCA officials walk down the aisle.> Birmingham: "Welcome fans to Tuesday Knights! Tonight we'll see newcomers She-Devil and Jane Gordon in action, Angel Hunter takes on Double J, the Vinnie Babe goes head-to-head with Delicious Doll Double D, Janet Strong meets Can-Am champion *and* Television champion 'the Intimidator' Amber Axeln in a completely non-title match at the Intimidator's request, and in our main event the new team of "Psycho" Vicki V and Christy Cooper challenges Tag Team champions Darkness, Inc. for the titles. But first, as we promised on Saturday we'll show you the end of the intra-stable match between the Psychotic One and Christy v. The Scandalous Ones. "When we left, Vicki and Black were engaged in a vicious floor battle, with Vicki reentering the ring. Rage reenters the ring; V catches her in a drop-kick. She nails Rage with a bulldog. Rage tags out to Black; they send Vicki to the ropes, then Rage nails her with a clothesline. She leaves the ring as Black executes a backflip cradle; V easily kicks out. Both women stand up. Black slaps Vicki; Vicki returns the same. Black slaps her again, then heads to the top rope. Vicki throws her to the mat! She applies a Boston crab. A wrestling hold? From the Psychotic One?! Good Lord, the world is ending! Vicki's not on an all-out assault tonight, no doubt to seriously avoid injuring her friends. She lets Black go. "Black slowly gets up. V rocks her with a punch, then hammers her in a front slam. Cooper enters the ring - Rage enters and knocks Christy back out, then nails V with an enzuilariato. She leaves the ring as Black climbs the turnbuckles. She nails Vicki with a flying spinning leg lariat. Both women stand up; V ducks a spin kick. Black blocks a DDT with a backdrop. Vicki blocks a full nelson and tags out to Cooper. She reconsiders leaving the ring and catches Black in a leaping clothesline. She rests inside the ring as Cooper follows up with a tiger suplex; one, two, shoulder up. "Black gets back up and ducks V's thrust kick to the head. V leaves the ring as Black runs into the ropes. She backdrops Cooper, then follows up with a monkey flip. Both women stand up; Cooper decks her with a jumping side kick. Black blocks a bow-and-arrow and scrambles to tag out to Rage. Cooper blocks a mule kick. She executes a tiger suplex; one, two, kickout. Rage blocks a suplex; Cooper ducks a short clothesline. Rage blocks an armbar and hits Christy with a double-axhandle chop. She follows up with an enzuigiri. "Cooper counters a double leg cradle with a double leg-hook; Black makes the save! Cooper follows up with a tiger suplex; Rage kicks out in two. Christy with a Northern Lights suplex; one, two, shoulder up. Cooper heads to the top rope. Rage counters a flying karate chop with a fist to the midsection! She takes Cooper down with an enzuigiri. Here comes Black! She keeps Christy down with a spinning mule kick. Cooper slowly gets up; Rage nails her with an enzuilariato. "V enters the ring - Turner cuts her off! Rage and Black pull Christy up, then send her to the ropes. They deck her with a double clothesline, then help her up again. They whip her to the ropes, then stop her with a double fist to the midsection. Black follows up with a diving shoulderblock. Cooper gets back on her feet; Rage executes an enzuigiri. Black leaves the ring as Cooper slowly gets up. Rage sends her to a corner and charges in - to a boot to the face! Rage staggers, then reverses a whip to the ropes. She nails Christy with a kick to the midsection and hits her with a double-axhandle chop. She follows up with an enzuilariato. Cooper gets back up; Rage bodyslams her. Cooper slowly picks herself up. Rage sends he to the ropes, then nails her with a clothesline. "Cooper staggers back. Rage sends her to a corner, then runs in shoulder-first. She nails Christy, then tags in Jenny. They send Cooper to the ropes, then take her down with a double enzuilariato. Cooper slowly gets back up; Black nails her with a double-axhandle chop. Christy lunges at V - and makes the tag! Vicki sends Black to the ropes. She drop-kicks Black. Cooper gets up; she and V send Black to the ropes, then double-backdrop her. Cooper keeps Black down with a lariat. "Vicki follows up with a bridging back suplex, but only gets a two count. Cooper leaves the ring as Black gets up. Black picks Vicki up and sets her on a turnbuckle. She climbs up, then executes a belly-to-belly superplex! Black returns to the top rope, then hammers V with the Superfly Splash; one, two, three! The Scandalous Ones win!" RA: "Ladies and gentlemen, the winners of the match: in 15 minutes, 53 seconds, the Scandalous Ones!" <Cooper and Rage enter the ring. They help Vicki up, then all five women raise their arms together to a decidedly hostile crowd reaction as the house lights rise.> Birmingham: "The Scandalous Ones pull out the victory, proving themselves to be the stronger of the two teams. Is this a fluke victory? Vicki and Christy take on the Tag Team champions tonight; they'll have to pull out all the stops to win the belts. All four women and Darkness, Inc. will be at the WarGames match this coming Sunday on the highly-anticipated No Sanctuary." ===== ---------- She-Devil (w/ Vinnie Gambino) v. Jane Gordon (w/ Samantha Jones and Joanne Watson) ---------- Both women lock up, then She-Devil counters a snap suplex with a vertical one, taking control of the match. After 2 minutes Gordon sends She-Devil to the ropes. She-Devil ducks a clohtesline, then rebounds and rakes Gordon's face. Gordon pulls her hands away, then quickly executes a piledriver, turning the match around. She soon hammers She-Devil with an elbowdrop from the second turnbuckle, then stands up. She-Devil sits up slowly. She gets on her feet, then blocks the Crimson Sunset. Gordon executes a Frankensteiner, regaining her momentum. After 3 minutes She-Devil blocks a back heel kick. She puts Gordon in a full nelson, turning the match around. A minute later Gambino jumps up on the apron as Sara Bryant runs down the aisle. The ref tries to send him back down; Watson tries to warn the ref as Jones moves to intercept Bryant. Bryant ducks a kick to the head; Jones nails Watson instead. Bryant sneaks into the ring and nails Gordon with her In Memory of Jacky, then leaves as Gambino jumps back down. She-Devil covers Gordon for the pin. (8:04) ---------- Double J v. Angel Hunter ---------- Both women lock up, then Hunter reverses a small package. Double J kicks out in two; both women stnad up. Hunter counters a powerslam with a lariat, taking control of the match. After 2 1/2 minutes Double J blocks a slap. She punches Hunter in the midsection, taking control of the match. After another 2 1/2 minutes Hunter blocks a front-layout suplex. Double J backdlrops out of a front facelock, then sends Hunter to the ropes. Hunter slows down and slaps Double J. Double J reverses a whip to the ropes, then stops Hunter with a shoulderblock. Hunter reverses a small package; Double J kicks out in two. Hunter beats Dobule J to her feet; Double J dodges a kneelift. She applies a half Boston, resuming her assault. After ninety seconds Hunter counters a slingshot facebuster with a vertical suplex, turning the match back around. After 3 1/2 minutes Double J counters an ipponzei with a larait. She throws Hunter out of the ring, then nails her with a legsweep faceslam. She follows up with a belly-to-belly suplex, then reenters the ring. Hunter follows her back in; Double J sends her to the far ropes. She backdrops Hunter, then nails her with an over-the-shoulder stomachbreaker, turning the match back around. A minute later she runs into the ropes. She attempts to counter an abdominal strech with a hiptoss, but Hunter re-reverses it into a backslide, pinning Double J. (11:24) ---------- Double D (Debbie Diamond) (w/ Tammi Cooper) v. The Vinnie Babe (w/ Vinnie Gambino) ---------- Both women lock up, then Diamond kicks the Vinnie Babe in the head, going on the offensive. The Vinnie Babe shortly counters a backspin DDT with a belly-to-belly suplex, then throws Diamond out of the ring. Diamond reenters the ring quickly; the Vinnie Babe shrugs off a bulldog, then naisl Diamond wiht a Hotshot, gaining the advantage. Dimond soon blocks a facerake. The Vinnie Babe jumps a legsweep; Diamond counters a bodyslam with a small package. The Vinnie Babe reverses it in two; Diamond kicks out int wo. Both women stand up. The Vinnie Babe punches Diamodn; Diamond responds with a kick to the head, retaking control fo the match. A minute later she sends the Vinnie Babe to the ropes. The Vinnie Babe comes back with a Hotshot, turning things back around. After ninety seconds she throws Diamond out of the ring and joins her on the floor. Diamond knocks the Vinnie Babe into the ringsteps, then keeps her down with a dropkick. Gambino distracts Diamond; the Vinnie Babe grabs her by the head and slams her face into the guardrail, busting her open. She gets up on the apron and distracts the ref as Gambino nails Diamond with the Gold Digger on the concrete floor. Cooper gets up on the apron and physically points the ref at the incident, then explains what happened. The ref disqualifies the Vinnie Babe. (5:46) (Non-title Match) ---------- "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln (w/ Valkyrie) v. Janet Strong ---------- Both women lock up, then Strong executes a belly-to-back suplex, taking charge. Axeln shortly reverses a whip to a corner. Strong comes back out; Axeln spins her around and executes a German suplex. Strong kicks out in two and gets back up. Axeln slaps her, then takes her down with neckscissors, going on the attack. After 3 1/2 minutes Strong ducks an enzuilariato. She puts Axeln in a front facelock, turning the match around. After 2 1/2 minutes Axeln blocks short-arm scissors. Both women stnad up; Strong reverses a whip to the ropes. She ducks an elbow, then jumps a shoulderblock, jumps another. Axeln rebounds again and nails Strong with a chop, turning the match back around. Axeln soon reverses a whip to a corner. Strong charges back out; axlen meets her with a leaping forearm. Strong gets back up and counters an abdominal stretch with a hiptoss. Axeln gets up and reverses a whip to a corner. She charges in; Strong moves out of the way. Strong shortly blocks a side suplex and puts Axeln in a front facelock, ending the stalemate. A minute later she sends Axeln to the ropes. Axeln comes back with a kick, turning things around. Strong shortly blocks a full nelson; Axeln ducks a jumping side kick. She puts Strong in an abdominal stretch. Strong breaks free in 8 seconds. Axeln executes a German suplex, pinning Strong.
