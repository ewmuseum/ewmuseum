WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION (http.// kessel.simplenet.com/WCA/ ) RAMPAGE 7/12/97 Live from the Lake Arena in Springfield, Missouri -----Main Event----- Li'l Cheetah v. Sweet Sue (World Title Match) Janet Strong v. Samantha Strong "Mistress of Death" Krista Stevens / Shannon Stevens v. The Scandalous Ones (Non-title Match) Christy Cooper v. Nadia Williams Liberty v. Red [Henchmen of Hades] (Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.) (Fireworks explode as the camera shows the crimson WCA logo on the white ring canvas. The camera descends rapidly, then levels off facing the aisle. The camera swerves left, panning out over the capacity crowd and circling the ring, ending its focus on the iridescent three-dimensional WCA letters bracketing the entrance to the backstage area. The referee for the first match heads to the ring.) ---------- Liberty (w/ Bodacious Janet Muniz, "the Intimidator" Amber Axeln, "Mistress of Death" Krista Stevens, and Shannon Stevens) v. Red Referee: Esther Siler ---------- Both women lock up. Red drop-kicks Liberty. Liberty gets back up in time to duck neck-scissors. Red pops back up; Liberty executes spinning headscissors into a cradle, but Red immediately kicks out. Liberty gets back up and takes Red down with an enzuigiri, taking control of the match. After a minute she throws Red out of the ring and joins her on the floor. Liberty executes a front-layout suplex. Red picks herself up; Liberty nails her with an enzuigiri. Red struggles back to her feet, then she grabs Liberty's head and slams it on the guardrail. She throws Liberty back into the ring, then she gets up on the apron and goes to a corner. Liberty rolls away from a flying elbowdrop, then she covers Red; Red kicks out in two. Liberty gets up and waits on Red, then she executes a savate kick, retaking control of the match. After 2 minutes Red blocks a Boston crab. She gets up, then she runs to the ropes and backdrops Liberty, turning the match around. She soon whips Liberty to the ropes; Liberty rebounds and misses with a clothesline, rebounds again and puts her into a headlock, slowing the match down again. Red soon puts her into a front facelock. Liberty breaks the hold in 5 seconds; Red goes to a corner and gets on the second turnbuckle, then she executes a flying forearm and covers; one, two, Liberty kicks out. Red goes back to the corner. She executes the Hook, Line, and Sinker, pinning Liberty. (6:50) ---------- Christy Cooper (w/ Dr. Insane, "the Tax Collector" John Blair, "Psycho Queen" Vicki V and Valkyrie) v. Nadia Williams (w/ Red) Referee: Julian Pace ---------- Both women lock up. Williams executes an inside cradle; Cooper kicks out in two. She throws Williams out of the ring and joins her on the floor. She hurls Williams head-first into the ringsteps. Williams picks herself up, then she turns around and blocks a blow from Valkyrie. She turns around and ducks a lariat, then she grabs Cooper's head and slams it on the ringsteps. Valkyrie distracts her as Cooper straightens up again. Cooper grabs Williams' head and bangs it against the guardrail. Williams breaks free, then she blocks another blow from Dr. Insane. Cooper grabs Williams' head and bangs it repeatedly on the guardrail. Williams elbows her way free, then Vicki distracts her. Cooper whips Williams into the ringpost. Williams staggers back, then she blocks a forearm smash. She breaks the count; Cooper shoves her back into the ring, then she reenters. Cooper runs to the ropes; Williams nails her with a clothesline, turning things around. She shortly takes Cooper down with an inside cradle, pinning her. (1:44) ---------- "Mistress of Death" Krista Stevens / Shannon Stevens (w/ Bodacious Janet Muniz, "the Intimidator" Amber Axeln, and Liberty) v. The Scandalous Ones (Shelly Rage and Jenny Black) (w/ Dr. Insane, "the Tax Collector" John Blair, and Valkyrie) Referee: Esther Siler ---------- Krista and Jenny lock up. Krista breaks off, then she executes a lariat, taking control of the match. She shortly tags in Shannon, who climbs to their top turnbuckles and executes a flying sunset flip; Jenny kicks out in two. Shannon runs into a corner, then she rebounds off the second turnbuckle and executes a flying cross body press; Jenny again kicks out in two. Shannon rolls out to the apron and gets up; Jenny rolls away from a slingshot somersault splash. Shannon rolls away from jenny's slingshot splash, then she puts Black into a full nelson, taking charge. Black soon rolls away from a slingshot legdrop and tags in Rage; Krista reenters the ring and throws Black out of the ring, then she and Shannon send Rage to the ropes. They nail her with a double clothesline, then Krista leaves the ring as Rage blocks a bow-and-arrow. Both women get up; Rage takes Shannon down with a clothesline. Shannon sits up; Rage applies a chinlock deathlock. Shannon uses the cheers to build up her energy, then she breaks free in 12 seconds. Shannon gets up and runs to the ropes; Valkyrie trips her. Shannon stumbles, then she takes Rage down with a belly-to-back suplex, retaking control of the match. After a minute Rage blocks a pick-up, then she tags out to Black. Black applies a standing wristlock, turning the match around. After 2 minutes, Shannon reverses an Irish whip, then she puts Black into an abdominal stretch, turning the match back around. Black shortly counters another abdominal stretch with a hiptoss. She runs to a corner and climbs the turnbuckles, then she executes a flying headbutt, retaking control of the match. Shannon soon ducks a flying spinning leg lariat. She runs to the ropes, rebounds and jumps Black, rebounds and jumps Black, rebounds and jumps a rising Black, rebounds and nails Black with a clothesline. Shannon covers her; one, two, kickout. Shannon gets up and tags in Krista; Rage enters the ring and throws Shannon out of the ring. Rage takes Krista down with a clothesline as Black picks herself up. Black hammers Krista with a somersault legdrop, retaking control of the match. Krista soon reverses an Irish whip, then she backdrops Black. Krista tags her sister back in. They send Black to the ropes and take her down with a double clothesline. Shannon grabs under Black's arms and hauls her back up. She holds Black for Krista; Black slips down, letting Krista nail Shannon with a spinning leg lariat. Black throws Krista out of the ring, then she goes into a corner and scales the turnbuckles. She executes a moonsault bodyblock; Shannon kicks out in two. Black gets up before Shannon, then she reverses an Irish whip. Shannon misses with a clothesline, then she stops and sends Black to the ropes. She scoops Black up and hammers her in a side suplex, retaking control of the match. Black soon blocks a full nelson, then she backs up a step. She nails Black with a clothesline, going back on the offensive. Shannon soon sidesteps a cartwheel kick, then she tags in Krista. Shannon takes Black down with a waistlock suplex. Krista follows up with a flying cross body press; Black kicks out in two. Rage enters the ring; Siler cuts her off, distracted. The Stevens send Black to the ropes, then they take her down with a double spinning leg lariat. Rage and Siler get into a yelling match as Black get back up. Shannon takes her down with a belly-to-back suplex. She returns to her corner; Rage sees her leave and storms back to hers. Black gets back on her feet, then she rolls a flying cross body over; Krista kicks out in two. Both women get back up, then Krista reverses a whip to the corner. Black lunges at Rage, tagging her in. Rage enters the ring via a springboard shoulderblock, taking control of the match. She shortly sends Krista to the ropes; Dr. Insane clocks Krista with the ring bell in front of the ref, who disqualifies the Scandalous Ones. (11:15) ---------- Janet Strong (w/ Angel Hunter) v. Samantha Strong (w/ Precious) Referee: Tina Nolke ---------- Janet sends Samantha to the ropes; Samantha rebounds and misses with a shoulderblock, rebounds again and ducks neck-scissors. She waits for Janet to get back up, then she executes a lariat. She applies a leg hammerlock; Janet reaches the ropes after 5 seconds. She gets back up, then she counters Samantha's back suplex with a cross-body block; Samantha kicks out in two. Janet complains about a slow count; Samantha uses the opportunity to catch her in an inside cradle; Janet kicks out forcefully in two. Janet puts her into legscissors, taking control of the match. Samantha shortly ducks a jumping side kick. She goes to the apron, then Janet rolls a slingshot bodyblock over; Samantha kicks out in two. Both women get up; Samantha ducks an enzuilariato, then she goes back to the apron and reenters via another slingshot bodyblock, only to get a two count. Samantha puts Janet into a leg hammerlock, taking control of the match. She soon sends Janet to the ropes; Janet rebounds and misses with a kick. She turns to Samantha and rocks her with a swinging punch, going back on the offensive. Samantha shortly reverses a whip to a corner. Janet charges out; Samantha takes her down with a clothesline, retaking control of the match. Janet soon sends Samantha to a corner, then she runs in shoulder-first, ramming into Samantha and going back on the offensive. After ninety seconds she sidesteps a kneelift; Samantha lands on her back. She picks herself up, then Janet sends her to the ropes. Janet misses with a shoulderblock, spins around, and rakes Samantha's face. She follows up with a kick to the midsection, resuming her onslaught. After ninety seconds Janet runs to the ropes; Samantha ducks a clothesline, turns around, and nails Janet with her own, turning the match around. Janet soon blocks an armdrag takedown with a lariat. Both women get up, then Janet again blocks another armdrag with a lariat. Janet gets up and goes to the apron, heading for a corner. She climbs the turnbuckles, then she executes a flying lariat. Janet stands up and returns to the corner as Samantha gets back up. Samantha counters another flying lariat with a fist to the midsection. She runs to the ropes; Janet misses with a clothesline and turns around in time to duck Samantha's clothesline, then she turns back around and decks Samantha with a jumping side kick, going back on the attack. Samantha shortly counters a flying lariat with a fist to the midsection. She backs up, then she takes Janet down with a lariat, retaking control. Janet shortly backs away from a kneelift, letting Samantha hit the mat. Janet leaves the ring and goes to a corner as Samantha picks herself up. Janet comes off the top with a flying lariat, renewing her gameplan. She shortly throws Samantha out of the ring; Samantha lands on her feet. Janet comes through the ropes and hops to the floor, then Samantha hiptosses her. Janet gets up; Samantha executes a vertical suplex on the floor. Both women rest a moment. Samantha gets up ahead of Janet; Janet charges her, staggering her with a clothesline. Janet turns around, then she tackles Samantha. Both women slug it out on the floor as Nolke continues the count. Janet gets up. The Bounty Hunters come out of the crowds with garbage cans full of objects. They climb over the guardrail; Nolke sees them and steps out of the ring. Black sees her coming and pulls out her baseball bat. She slugs Nolke hard with it, causing her nose to bleed. Black immediately turns to Janet and nails her across the knee with her bat as Red pulls out a pair of brass knuckles from her can. Red slugs Samantha with them; Debbie Angel and Vicky Armstrong run in from the back, followed by security. Black pulls out a slab of marble and brings it down on Janet's back; Red pulls her bat out of her can and nails Angel with a spike into her chest. Red backs up, then she uppercuts Armstrong with her bat. Security comes around the ringpost; one guard helps Samantha get up as the others rush Red. Red takes two of them down before the last two reach her. Black continues beating a prone Janet with several objects from her can. The two hit guards pick themselves up. Black sees them and empties her can onto Janet's back, then she throws the can itself down on Janet's head. She picks up her bat and backs into the ring. She nails one of Red's restraining guards, then the one by Samantha Strong rolls in into the ring and grabs her bat. He throws it back down the aisle, then he ducks a punch and tackles Black. Red's guards escort her out of the ring and walk her back to the locker room, followed by the other three with Black. Halfway down the aisle, Panthra comes out of one side and takes one of the guards down with a springboard drop-kick to the head. She quickly gets up as one with Black turns to her; Panthra takes him down with a chop to the neck. Another of Black's guards comes at her; Black and Red see their opportunity and break free of the remaining ones. Panthra disappears into the stands as the Bounty Hunters go over the guardrail and vanish into the crowd. (11:14) ===== MIKE SHARPE: "The Bounty Hunters make their presence known tonight, taking on both Strongs. Those two women are on a path of destruction, and they won't let anyone stand in their way. They're royally angry right now because they were sanctioned for their actions at Summer-sault. Still, tonight's situation might not have happened if Janet hadn't throws Samantha out of the ring the last time. That's one thing that I've noticed about Janet. If she feels threatened in the ring, she'll let her emotions take over and forget all about strategy. And sometimes that costs her the match, as it did tonight. On the other hand, she worked very well against Samantha Strong. It's clear her time off hasn't hurt her ring ability. "Fans, up next is our main event. The World champion Li'l Cheetah still holds the belt since the Bounty Hunters interfered in her rematch with the former champ Femme Fatale. Cheetah won the Can-Am belt Sunday, but this match is for the World title. This is also Sweet Sue's first match since her injury - an injury caused, I might add, by not keeping in shape when she went on strike to protest - well, to protest sanity. This is a big comeback match for her, and I'm sure everyone is watching to see how she does. Let's go to the ring." (In the ring.)
