WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION ( http://www.inet-images.com/skessler/WCA/ ) THEATRE OF WAR 5/19/97 Live from Chicago, Illinois -----Main Event----- "Psycho" Vicki V v. Anaconda The Mistress of Death v. Asano Otani Interview with The Mistress of Death "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln v. Femme Fatale (Best of 5 Gimmick Match 2) Magic Mountain Warriors v. Shannon Stevens / Freedom Bodacious Janet Muniz v. The Assassin (Best of 11 Series: Match 8) (Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.) (Fireworks explode as the camera shows the crimson WCA logo on the white ring canvas. The camera descends rapidly, then levels off facing the aisle. The camera swerves left, panning out over the capacity crowd and circling the ring, ending its focus on the iridescent three-dimensional WCA letters bracketing the entrance to the backstage area. The referee for the first match heads to the ring.) *Best of 11: Match 8* -------------------- Bodacious Janet Muniz (w/ "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln, Liberty, and the Mistress of Death) v. The Assassin (w/ Li'l Cheetah) Referee: Esther Siler -------------------- Muniz kicks the Assassin in the leg, then she bites the Assassin's cheek. The Assassin slips behind Muniz and takes her down with a belly-to-back suplex. Muniz sits up quickly, then she gets up; the Assassin whips her to a corner and charges in, leading with her elbow. She pulls Muniz out of the corner and spins her around; Muniz blocks a tiger suplex, then she turns back around and puts the Assassin in a bearhug. The Assassin breaks the hold in 5 seconds, then Muniz reverses an Irish whip; the Assassin ducks a clothesline, rebounds, and blocks a kick to the midsection. She goes behind Muniz and executes a German suplex; one, two, kickout. The Assassin follows up with an Asai moonsault, then she puts Muniz in the Sharpshooter. Muniz submits after 8 seconds. The series is now Muniz-5, Assassin-3. (1:17) ===== Mike Sharpe: "The Bodacious One submits to the Sharpshooter in 8 seconds, forfeiting her chance to wrap the series up. The series is now 5-3, giving the Assassin a break in this series of matches. The Assassin still needs to win the next three matches, but if Janet continues to perform at this level, that won't be too much of a problem. "Later tonight, fans, you'll see the television debut of one of the newest stars of the WCA, Anaconda. Right now Shane Templeton is standing by with her backstage. Shane?" (Cut to backstage interview area.) Templeton: "Thank you, Mike. Joining me at this time is one of the newest wrestlers to the WCA, Anaconda. Ma'am, thank you for joining me. Tonight you'll be making your television debut against one of the top wrestlers of the league. You've seen the Psycho Queen in action, I assume. What do you think of her as a wrestler?" Anaconda: "Thanks, it's a pleasure for me to be here... Answering your question, I already saw Ms. V in the ring, and I can say that she is a very powerful wrestler... But so am I, and she can expect that I'll not make things easier for her tonight." Templeton: "Could you tell us a little bit about yourself? I mean, where your from and all that." Anaconda: "Well, I was born in Manaus, a big city in the middle of the Amazon Rain Forest... Since I was a girl, I was trained for hard work, cause my parents were poor, and the only thing that we could do is carry wood from the Forest to the city... And you don't know how strong you must be to do it. When I finally became a woman, I went to Colombia to try my luck... I was noticed by a wrestling coach and started to learn wrestling... Quickly I became Colombian champion, but I was stripped of the belt cause I am Brazilian... So now I received the contract for WCA and I'm here since." Templeton: "Thank you for joining me and good luck to you!" Anaconda: "Thanks for the opportunity and Vicky... don't expect a good time, girl! Nobody beats the strength of my hug!" (Cut to Sharpe.) Sharpe: "Anaconda is a big woman, to be sure, and she's determined to rise to the top. With those two factors, she more than likely will achieve her goals. All the wrestlers of the WCA should keep an eye on her. "Up next, fans, is a return of sorts for the Magic Mountain Warriors. After returning to the WCA at the Quake II pay-per-view, they've fought only in one match, challenging the Bounty Hunters for the Tag Team titles. This is their first active tag team match, and their opponents, Freedom and Shannon Stevens, will afford them the opportunity to see how much their time off has affected them. Let's go to the ring." ===== -------------------- Magic Mountain Warriors (Mai and Mariko) v. Shannon Stevens / Freedom (w/ the Dream Master) Referee: Bob Jordan -------------------- Mai and Stevens lock up. Stevens blocks a Japanese armdrag with an elbowjab, then she goes into a corner and leaps to the top rope. She executes a flying sunset flip, bumping into the ref and putting him out of position. Stevens gets back up, then she executes a handspring moonsault. She gets up, then Mai blocks another handspring moonsault by pulling up her knees. Mai gets back on her feet, then she catches a rising Stevens with a Japanese armdrag takedown, taking control of the match. She soon tags in Mariko, then they whip Stevens to the ropes and deck her with a double clothesline. Stevens gets back up; Mai nails her with a thrust kick to the head and leaves the ring. Stevens gets back up and blocks a legsweep faceslam, then she tags in Freedom. Jordan cuts Mai off, allowing Stevens to nail Mariko with a Western lariat. Freedom follows up with a flying elbowdrop, then she goes back to their corner. Freedom comes off the top with the Showstopper and covers; one, two, Mariko throws Freedom off of her. Both women get up; Mariko ducks a lariat, then she nails Freedom with a drop-kick to the knee, taking control of the match. She very shortly tags in Mai; both women whip Freedom to the ropes, then they nail her with a double fist to the midsection. Stevens enters the ring and throws Mariko out, then she and Freedom send Mai to the ropes and nail her with a double kick to the midsection. Freedom goes to a corner, then she executes a flying karate chop. Freedom tags in Stevens; Mariko enters the ring and throws her out, then she returns to her corner as Mai executes an Irish whip. Stevens reverses a rolling reverse cradle; Mai kicks out in two. Stevens follows up with a waistlock suplex, but Mai easily kicks out. Mai gets up and ducks a spinning leg lariat, then she rushes to her corner and tags out to Mariko, who walks down the apron and enters the ring via a springboard drop-kick, taking control of the match. She shortly tags Mai back in, then they whip Stevens to the ropes and nail her with a double fist to the midsection. Mariko leaves the ring as Stevens blocks a back heel kick. Mai attempts to blocks an abdominal stretch with a hiptoss, but Stevens in turn turns it into a backslide; one, two, shoulder up. Stevens follows up with a waistlock suplex; Mai kicks out in two again. Stevens complains about a slow count, then she tags in Freedom. They whip Mai to the ropes and stop her with a double kick to the midsection. Mariko enters the ring and throws Stevens out, then she nails Freedom with a savate kick, giving control of the match to Mai. Mai shortly tags in Mariko, then Freedom ducks her spin kick. Mai leaves as Freedom goes into a corner, then she executes a flying drop-kick, retaking control of the match. Mariko shortly dodges a springboard drop-kick, then she executes a standing moonsault, but she only gets a one count. Mariko applies a standing wristlock, taking charge. Freedom shortly tags in Stevens, then they whip Mariko to the ropes and double-backdrop her. Mai enters and throws Freedom out of the ring, then she nails Stevens with a drop-kick to the knee as Mariko gets back up, giving control to her partner. Stevens shortly rolls away from a moonsault, then both women get back up. Stevens executes a sunset backslide; Mariko kicks out in two. Stevens nails her with a spinning leg lariat, taking control of the match. Mariko shortly nails her with a drop-kick to the knee, going back on the attack. After 2 minutes Stevens ducks a flying forearm, then she executes a Western lariat, turning the match back around. Mariko shortly runs to the ropes, then she nails Stevens with an elbow. She follows up with a hiptoss, retaking control. She soon tags in Mai, then she goes to their top turnbuckle and takes Stevens down with a flying forearm. Mai covers; one, two, shoulder up. Mai follows up with a rolling reverse cradle; one, two, thr-kickout! Both women get up, then Stevens catches Mai in a belly-to-back suplex, retaking control of the match. Mai soon manages to escape the punishment and tags in Mariko; Freedom rushes into the ring and throws Mai out of the ring, then she and Stevens send Mariko to the ropes and double-punch her in the midsection. Freedom returns to her corner as Stevens runs to the ropes. She blocks a faceslam and scoops Mariko up, then she puts her on the mat via a side suplex, continuing her assault. Mariko shortly blocks a Western lariat, then Stevens blocks a pick-up with an elbowsmash. Mariko blocks a belly-to-back, then she whips Stevens to the ropes and backs up. She backdrops Stevens out of the ring; Stevens lands on her feet. Mariko turns and goes through the ropes. Stevens goes over the barrier, then she reenters ringside via a springboard shoulderblock. She picks Mariko up and throws her into the ring; Freedom enters the ring and both women pick Mariko up, then they whip her to the far ropes and double-kick her in the stomach, starting a series of double-teams. The ref shortly forces Freedom out of the ring as Stevens whips Mariko to the ropes; Mariko returns with an elbow to the face, going back on the attack. After ninety seconds Stevens rolls away from a moonsault and scrambles to tag out to Freedom. Mariko blocks a standing wristlock and scrambles to tag in Mai; Freedom ducks Mai's flying headscissors. Stevens reenters the ring and scoops Mai up, then she executes a side suplex, giving control to Freedom. Mai shortly ducks a lariat, then Mariko enters the ring and takes Freedom down with a faceslam. Mai goes to a corner, then she catches a rising Freedom with a flying bulldog. Stevens enters the ring and throws Mariko out of the ring, then she nails Mai with a spinning leg lariat as Freedom struggles back up. Freedom rests while Mai gets back on her feet, then she executes a lariat, resuming her offense. She shortly runs to the ropes; Mai ducks an elbow, ducks a clothesline, and backdrops Freedom. Freedom gets back up in time to duck a superkick; Stevens enters the ring and takes Mai down with a spinning leg lariat. Mai gets back on her feet; Freedom executes a lariat. Mai scrambles to tag in Mariko, who enters the ring and takes Freedom down with a faceslam, turning the match around. She soon executes the Skytwister Press and pins Freedom. (20:17) ===== Sharpe: "The Magic Mountain Warriors win the match, with Mariko pinning Freedom with the Skytwister Press. That was a very good match, with all four women giving it their all. Let's go to the ring." ===== *Best of 5 Series: Random Gimmick Match 2* -------------------- "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln (w/ Bodacious Janet Muniz, Liberty, and the Mistress of Death) v. Femme Fatale (w/ Snakebite) Referee: Tina Nolke -------------------- Both women step up to the Wheel of Fate. Fatale spins the wheel. It stops on "Submission Match;" Axeln spins Fatale around and nails her with a swinging punch, causing Fatale to fall back and knock the Wheel of Fate down into the aisle. Axeln follows up with an enzuigiri, taking control of the match. Fatale shortly executes a fireman's carry, taking charge. She takes it to Axeln for the next 6 1/2 minutes, not giving the Intimidator a chance to get a move in edgewise. At the seven-minute mark, Axeln dodges a drop-kick. She rests and recovers while Fatale pops back up, then she slaps Fatale. Fatale executes an armdrag takedown. Axeln gets back on her feet, then she executes neck-scissors, turning the match around. Fatale shortly ducks neck-scissors. She goes into a corner as Axeln gets up, then she takes Axeln back down with a flying lariat. Axeln sits up painfully, then falls back to the mat. She rolls over and raises back on her knees, then gets up. Fatale sends her into a corner and charges in; Axeln moves out of the way, grabbing Fatale's arm in the process and sending her to the far corner. Fatale charges back out and ducks a clothesline. She sets up Axeln for a suplex; Axeln blocks it and nails Fatale with a chop, resuming her offense. After ninety seconds she pulls Fatale into a corner, then she gets on the second turnbuckle and executes the Doombomb, pinning Fatale. (11:46) ===== Sharpe: "Amber Axeln salvages a win, managing to defeat the much stronger Femme Fatale with her patented Doombomb, a move that's put away many an opponent. Axeln is one of the finalists in the Television Tournament matches, and she'll have a much bigger opponent in Big Norma Hudson to take on. It'd be wrestling history if the Intimidator can pin Hudson with her Doombomb. That match will happen Thursday. "Fans, right now Shane Templeton ready in the ring to talk to one of the WCA's biggest superstars, the Mistress of Death. Take it, Shane." ( Cut to the ring.) Templeton: "Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome the former WCA World champion the Mistress of Death!" (The Mistress walks to the ring and enters.) Templeton: "Good evening, Mistress. Let me first ask you what you think about you're crusade against the Posse of Punishment so far. You've defeated the Geisya Girls, Christy Cooper, 'Psycho' Vicki V, but lost to the Scandalous Ones in individual matches." Mistress of Death: I never said that I would be able to win them all. In fact, it is kind of ironic that I beat two of the tougher members of the Posse, but have lost to the two 'scientific' wrestlers. I think that I am proving to everyone around the world that I am as good as the reputation. Templeton: "You'll also be facing Valkyrie, Asano Otani in just a few minutes, and both members of Hardcore. Who do you think'll be the toughest opponent?" Mistress of Death: I am not going to answer this because I don't want to give anyone that little extra. Every opponent is tough because you never know what someone will do to get the victory. I respect every opponent's abilities. Templeton: "Why did you decide to go through all the members of the Posse? It certainly seemed suicidal at first glance, what with the Posse's tendency to gang up on people and all." Mistress of Death: I had to in order to prove a point. I lost the World title and seemed like I was on a slump right after. I needed this to prove to myself that I still had it. If I wouldn't have won the matches I did, I would have stepped aside for a while. Templeton: "Changing the subject, are you ready for your match against your sister Shannon Stevens this Saturday at Meltdown?" Mistress of Death: You never want to fight family. I hate the fact that I will be facing my sister, but I look at it as another opponent. I am ready to face her, but after the match, it will hurt me that it came down to this. Templeton: "Shannon started wrestling so promisingly in this league, but she seems to have fallen into the mid-level status. What's your take on that?" Mistress of Death: She is a fantastic wrestler who just happens to be misguided. I am going to go out on a limb here and say what is exactly on my mind. Shannon dedicates herself 100% to whatever she does. I respect her and know her skills. She asked for a stipulation that almost everyone has agreed to. I am going on record and will say that I agree as well. Janet, I know you are the lone holdout. Shannon will dedicate herself to us. I think this is a major step in her turning her life around. She has to be thinking about the fact that she will lose and will have to join me. Consider even a trial period to see if she changes. Templeton: "One last question; we've heard Liberty and the Bodacious One speak out on the Helena Knightsbridge controversy, but we haven't heard too much from you about it. What's your opinion?" Mistress of Death: Controversy abounds tonight during this interview. I don't know what to say. I always didn't want to believe that she did it because I got to know her so well. I want to see the tests that the WCA has just to make sure. I am torn on this and I don't want to say the wrong thing without the facts. Templeton: "Thank you, Mistress. Ladies and gentlemen, the former World champion the Mistress of Death!" (Cut to Sharpe.) Sharpe: "The Mistress, giving her opinions on some of her and the Click's biggest issues. She's right when she said that controversy dominated the interview, but controversy always surrounds the Posse of Punishment and the Click II. The Posse always bends, breaks, and ignores the rules, and the Click II always does what they think is right, regardless of what others think. I don't imagine either trait changing anytime soon, either.
