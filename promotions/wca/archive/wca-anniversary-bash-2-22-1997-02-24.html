WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION ANNIVERSARY BASH 2/22/96 Live from Nairobi, Kenya -----Main Event----- =Tag Team Titles Match= Hooked on Destruction v. Magic Mountain Warriors Semiramis v. Monique (non-title match) The Mistress of Death / Freedom v. Linda Belle / Dangerous Renee Lake The Scandal Sheet Bodacious Janet Muniz v. Li'l Cheetah (Leather Strap / Detroit Street Fight / I Quit Match) Femme Fatale v. Flower Power Helena "High Kick" Knightsbridge v. "Nasty Girl" Amber Jane Mary Williams v. Black [Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.] [Fireworks explode as the camera shows the crimson WCA logo on the white ring canvas. The camera descends rapidly, then levels off facing the aisle. The camera swerves left, panning out over the capacity crowd and circling the ring, ending its focus on the iridescent three-dimensional WCA letters bracketing the entrance to the backstage area. The referee for the first match heads to the ring. The view cuts to commentator Mike Sharpie, setting at a crimson-tableclothed table with two monitors before him.] Sharpe: "Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to the World CyberWrestling Association's birthday party! I'm Mike Sharpe, emcee for the festivities. Now, as long-time fans of the WCA will point out, the origninal WCA was bought out by ICA back on June 30th, and new management was put in place. However, most fans of the WCA see the league as starting on February 22, 1996, as do I. So, tonight, we'll be handing out awards for Best Match of the Year, Pay-per-View of the Year, which is an admittedly self-praising concept, Feud of the Year, Manager of the Year, Stable of the Year, Hardcore Wrestler, Tag Team, and Wrestler of the Year in between all of the matches. Speaking of which, tonight you'll see Hooked on Destruction defend the Tag Team titles against the Magic Mountain Warriors, plus World champion Semiramis faces Monique in a non-title match, the Mistress of Death and Freedom will be in action, the highly-anticipated Bodacious Janet Muniz return as she takes on Li'l Cheetah, Femme Fatale will attempt to get revenge on Flower Power, and Helena 'High Kick' Knightsbridge will face 'Nasty Girl' Amber Jane. But first, Mary Williams takes on Black. Both of these women represent the two top tag teams of the WCA, the Psycho Crushers and the Bounty Hunters respectively. Both of these women are also rather low on the singles scale, so this match is doubly important for them. Let's go to the ring." ===== -------------------- Mary Williams (w/ Crimson and Red) v. Black (w/ Panthra, Red, and Semiramis) -------------------- Both women lock up. Black blocks a back suplex, then executes an ipponzei and covers; one, kickout. Black keeps Williams down with an earringer, taking control of the match. After ninety seconds Williams executes a sunset backslide; one, two, shoulder up. Both women get up; Black counters a swinging neckbreaker with a side suplex. Williams rolls onto her stomach, then raises back on her knees. She backdrops out of a front facelock, then executes a double leg cradle; one, two, thr-kickout! Williams gets up and yanks Black to her feet, then sends her to the ropes. She misses with a clothesline, then stops Black with a back heel kick. Black falls into the ropes, then straightens up and executes an enzuigiri, resuming her assault. After 2 minutes Williams backdrops out of a front facelock. She follows up with a takeover suplex, turning the match around. She shortly runs into the ropes; Black stops her with a shoulderblock, retaking control. Williams shortly blocks an armbar, then takes Black down with a DDT, going back on the offensive. After ninety seconds Black blocks a sleeper, then takes her down with an earringer. She executes another; Williams gets back up. Black nails her with an enzuigiri. She follows up with a belly-to-belly; one, two, three. Williams complains about a fast count. (8:12) ===== Sharpe: "Mary Williams didn't like that call, but the history books will show that was a clear pin. Speaking of history books, let's go to John Birmingham, who's standing by in the ring with the first of tonight's awards. John?" Birmingham: "Thank you, Mike. Our first award tonight is for the Match of the Year. Since February 22, 1996, countless matches have been presented. Some of them have been spectacular, while others have been lacking in both length and quality. This award would normally be presented to the involved parties to recognize their skills in the ring, but the elected match had over twenty participants, many of whom are no longer associated with World CyberWrestling Association. The Match of the Year, as voted by the fans of the WCA, is the Three-Ring Battle Royale presented at the Lords of the Ring pay-per-view. This battle royale exemplified the WCA's best stars, and capped off a stellar night. This plaque <holding it up> will be hung in the display case in the hallway outside the Commissioner's office for everyone who visits the WCA's headquarters to see." Sharpe: "Thank you, John. Fans, we need to take a time-out. When we return, Helena 'High Kick' Knightsbridge and 'Nasty Girl' Amber Jane will face off in a return match." -----Commercial Break----- Sharpe: "As fans will recall, on February 3rd Helena Knightsbridge and the Nasty Girl met in the ring for the first time. Jane shortly counters a belly-to-back with an elbowsmash, then executes a single-leg takedown, retaking control again.
