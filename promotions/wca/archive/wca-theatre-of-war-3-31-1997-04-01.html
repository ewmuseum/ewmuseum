WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION THEATRE OF WAR 3/31/97 From Katmandu, Nepal *Special 30-Minute Edition* -----Main Event----- "Psycho" Vicki V v. Bodacious Janet Muniz (Best of 11 Match 4) "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln v. Asano Otani Freedom v. Karen "Bugs" Huffman Hardcore v. Innocent Blood <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.> <Fireworks explode as the camera shows the crimson WCA logo on the white ring canvas. The camera descends rapidly, then levels off facing the aisle. The camera swerves left, panning out over the capacity crowd and circling the ring, ending its focus on the iridescent three-dimensional WCA letters bracketing the entrance to the backstage area. The referee for the first match heads to the ring.> -------------------- Hardcore ("Nasty Girl" Amber Jane and "Dominatress" Kathy Lynn) (w/ The Dream Master) v. Innocent Blood (Blood and Red) -------------------- Jane and Red lock up, then Red executes a ropeflip hiptoss, going on the offensive. She soon sends Jane to the ropes; Jane misses with a clothesline, then misses with an elbow, then takes Red down with a leaping forearm and tags in Lynn. They hammer Red with a double elbowdrop, then Jane leaves as Lynn goes to a neutral corner. Lynn hammers Red with a flying karate chop. Red staggers back, then executes an armdrag takedown, resuming her assault. She soon throws Lynn out of the ring, then joins her on the floor. Lynn whips Red into the ringsteps, then takes her down with a snap mare. Lynn prevents a pick-up and reenters the ring, tagging in Jane as Red reenters. Blood rushes into the ring and throws Lynn out, then she and Red send Jane to the ropes. Jane rebounds and slows, then counters the obvious double backdrop with a double headsmash. Blood staggers back to her corner as Jane hits Red with a palm blow, then executes a handspring elbow. Red gets back up and blocks a hiptoss, then goes to the apron and executes a springboard lariat, retaking control of the match. Blood shortly enters the ring; she and Red send Jane to the ropes, then nails her with a double kick tot he midsection. Jane counters Blood's bodyslam with a small package; Blood reverses it in two. Jane kicks out in two; Blood rolls away as Red rewards Jane with a flying kneedrop. Blood meets an incoming Lynn with an elbow the face as Jane picks herself up. Blood throws Lynn back out of the ring, then she and Red execute a double drop-kick. The ref forces Blood back to her corner. Red nails a standing Jane with a cartwheel kick. She executes a drop-kick; Jane falls into the ropes. She stands upright, then nails Red with a palm blow, starting her own offensive. Red soon dodges a springboard drop-kick, then tags in Blood. The ref cuts Lynn off; Red uses the distraction to climb her turnbuckles and take Jane down with a flying forearm. Blood covers; one, two, kickout. Jane gets up rather slowly, then blocks a belly-to-back with an elbowjab. She goes to the apron; Blood moves away from a springboard drop-kick, then goes to the far apron. She reenters the ring via a springboard lariat, taking charge. Jane shortly tags in Lynn; they whip Blood to the ropes, then stop her with a double kick to the midsection. Lynn follows up with a backslide; one, two, kickout. Lynn gets up and goes into the corner, leaping to the top turnbuckles. Blood meets her flying karate chop with a fist to the midsection, then tags in Red. They send her to the ropes; Lynn ducks a double clothesline, rebounds, and executes her own. Blood rolls out of the ring as Red reverses an Irish whip, then knocks Lynn down with a shoulderblock, retaking control of the match. She shortly fights a backslide, pulling Lynn down and pinning her. (6:30) -------------------- Freedom (w/ Shannon Stevens) v. Karen "Bugs" Huffman (w/ Sweet Sue and Lovely Liz Strohlheim) -------------------- Both women lock up, then Huffman puts Freedom into a Sammartino backbreaker, taking control of the match. Freedom shortly blocks an ipponzei with a lariat. She nails Huffman with a jumping elbow thrust, going on the offensive. She soon runs into the ropes; Huffman nails her with a clothesline, turning things back around. Freedom soon blocks an atomic drop, then goes to the apron and takes Huffman down with a springboard drop-kick, going back on the attack. After a minute Huffman turns spinning headscissors into a Hotshot. Freedom picks herself up, then Freedom reverses a whip to the ropes. She blocks a sleeper with a jawbreaker, sending Huffman to her knees. Freedom sets her up, then puts her on the mat via a front-layout suplex, resuming her assault. Huffman shortly blocks another suplex, then rocks Freedom with a European uppercut. She follows up with an atomic drop, turning things back around. After 5 intense minutes Huffman sends Freedom to the ropes. Freedom misses with a shoulderblock, rebounds and nails Huffman with a kick, turning the match around. Huffman soon ducks neck-scissors. Freedom blocks a Sammartino backbreaker; Huffman turns a suplex into a vertical suplex, going back on the offensive. She shortly pins Freedom with a bridging back suplex. (9:11) ------------------- "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln (w/ Bodacious Janet Muniz, The Mistress of Death, and Liberty) v. Asano Otani (w/ The Dream Master and the Geisya Girls) -------------------- Otani backdrops out of a front facelock; Axeln hangs on for a sunset flip; one, two, kickout. Axeln gets up and complains about a slow count, enabling Otani to roll her up with an inside cradle; Axeln kicks out in two. Otani puts Axeln into a bearhug, taking control of the match. Axeln soon blocks a forearm smash; Otani blocks a roundhouse punch. Axeln ducks a kick to the head; Otani sends her to the ropes. Axeln returns with a kick, going on the attack. Otani soon ducks a lariat, then runs into a corner and rebounds off the turnbuckles with a flying cross body press, only to get a two count. Otani sends Axeln to the ropes; Axeln returns with a kick, taking Otani down. Axeln puts her into a figure-four leglock, resuming her offensive. After 2 minutes, Otani rolls away from a stomp and gets up. She whips Axeln to a corner, then walks in and whips her to the far ropes. Axeln returns with a roundhouse punch, resuming her assault. Otani shortly turns a headlock takedown into a back suplex, turning the match back around. Axeln shortly takes her down with a lariat, retaking control. Otani soon whips Axeln to the ropes, then decks her with a clothesline, going back on the attack. After 2 minutes Axeln catches Otani in a German suplex; Otani kicks out at the last second. Axeln follows up with a roundhouse punch, turning the match around once again. After 2 more minutes she puts Otani back into a figure-four. Otani submits immediately. (9:39) ===== Sharpe: "Amber Axeln racks up another win for the Click over the Posse of Punishment, defeating Asano Otani with a figure-four leglock. Speaking of Click v. Posse matches, our next bout features two of the biggest stars in both stables and in the entire league for that matter, 'Psycho' Vicki V and Bodacious Janet Muniz in Match 4 of their Best of 11 series. Right now the series is Muniz-2, V-1. The Bodacious One is very confident that despite V's first win this past Saturday, she'll come out on top in this series. But at this stage in the game, anything is possible. Let's go to the ring." Ring Announcer: "Ladies and gentlemen, tonight's main event is set for one fall! Introducing first: representing the Click II and accompanied by her stablemates the Mistress of Death, Liberty, and 'the Intimidator' Amber Axeln, from Rock Springs, Wyoming, weighing in at 128 pounds, Bodacious Janet Muniz!" <The Click II walks down the aisle, the non-combatants wearing the familiar black high-heels. They enters the ring, then take up all four corners and shout out "Long live the Click!" to the delight of the cheering arena.> "Her opponent: representing the Posse of Punishment and accompanied to the ring by her tag team partner Christy Cooper and her manager Dr. Insane, from Brooklyn, New York, weighing in at 150 pounds, 'Psycho' Vicki V!" <The leather-clad Vicki is lead down the aisle by Dr. Insane on a leash followed by Cooper, to a booing audience. Vicki wears her spiked glove. They ignore the fans and enter the ring. Cooper faces the back of the arena and raises her arms up, drawing the fans' ire.> Sharpe: "Dr. Insane unleashes Vicki; she gives Christy her spiked glove. Muniz pulls on the top rope, doing some last-minute stretching. The other Click members encourage her, then step out of the ring. Cooper and Dr. Insane give Muniz dirty looks, then step out. Mark White will referee this match. There's the bell - and V takes Muniz down with a spinning DDT! I've rarely seen anyone catch the Bodacious One off guard at the start. V yanks Muniz up by the arm and sends her to a corner. Muniz straightens up, then charges back out and nails her with a kick to the midsection. She slips behind V and executes an atomic drop. Muniz grabs V and throws her out of the ring, then goes through the ropes. She takes V down with a faceslam to the concrete! Cooper comes up behind Muniz - she sees her and nails her with a swinging punch. V shoves Muniz into the guardrail, then slams her face onto the steel. V picks Janet up and drops her into a stomachbreaker, then reenters the ring. Janet is really off her game today. She follows V in, then blocks a kick to the midsection. "She decks V with a thrust kick to the head. Maybe she was just lying in wait for the right moment. Muniz picks V up, then slams her back onto the mat in a fallaway slam. V picks herself up; Muniz executes a kneebreaker. V falls into the ropes, using them to stay on her feet. She reverses a whip to a corner, then walks in. She chokes Muniz; one, two, three, four, Vicki lets go and insults the ref. Muniz blocks another chokehold, shoving V back into the ring. V counters an atomic drop with a bulldog. Muniz rolls onto her back and sits up. One wonders if Muniz has something on her mind. She's up; V takes her down with a reverse neckbreaker. Muniz quickly gets up again. V takes her back down with an earringer, irritated. She scoops Janet up, then plants her with a running powerslam; one, two, the ref sees Vicki's foot on the bottom rope. "Both women get up; V kicks Muniz in the midsection to no effect. She whips Muniz to the ropes; Muniz rebounds, then chokes Vicki! One, two, three, four, she lets go and yells at the ref. V uses the distraction to throw her out of the ring, then she steps through the ropes and hops to the floor. Muniz executes a legsweep faceslam. White begins the count. Muniz whips Vicki into the ringsteps. She rolls V over and rakes her face, then yanks her up and throws her back into the ring. Muniz rolls in and covers; one, two, shoulder up. Vicki sits up; Muniz pulls her up, then sends her to the ropes. Both women clothesline the other! "Muniz gets up first, then scoops V up and bodyslams her. She's got to gain the upper hand. V's back up; Muniz grabs her by the throat and choke slams her! She presses the heel of her boot into V's eye! Cooper hurls an insult at her; Muniz goes to the apron and insults her back. V stirs on the mat; Muniz sees it and runs to the ropes. She misses with an elbow, rebounds, and nails V with a kick to the head! Muniz sets V up - for a Tombstone! And bam! Listen to that crowd cheer! V rolls over on the mat - then slowly begins to get up! What does it take to put the Psychotic One down? Vicki punches Muniz in the stomach to no effect. Muniz kicks her in the leg, sending Vicki back to the mat. It seems like Vicki can take more punishment than the Bodacious One. She's back up and kicks Muniz in themidsection, doubling her over. Vicki hooks the arms - for a double-underhook piledriver! What goes around comes around, although that seemed to really damage Janet. She turns over, shaking her head to clear it. V jeers the other Click members, then hooks Muniz's arms, setting her up and taking too long. Muniz backdrops her - V hangs on for a sunset flip; one, two, kickout. "Muniz grits her teeth, getting on her feet. She throws Vicki out of the ring, then rests a moment. She goes through the ropes and hops to the floor. She decks V with a short clothesline as White counts 2. They'd better watch themselves; White reaches 4. V hiptosses Janet, who gets back up quickly. V knocks Muniz into the ringsteps. Janet picks herself up, then rushes V, biting her face! She drew blood! Yuck. Muniz hooks V's arms for her own double underhook piledriver - there's the bell! White's counted both women out!" RA: "Ladies and gentlemen, referee Mark White has ruled this match a double countout!" <White leaves the ring as Cooper jumps into the fray on the floor. The Click members immediately join in the fight, quickly putting the Posse members on the defensive. Hardcore and Shelly Rage race down the aisle, quickly followed by an army of security guards. Muniz and V take their fight to the ring, punching each others' heads furiously. The guards pull them apart. Both women struggle to continue the fight as the other guards and referees try to halt the floor battle between the Posse and the Click.> Sharpe: "The war has reached a new level! It looks like there'll be at least eight matches in the Muniz / V series, with the double countout. Both women definitely despise each other, but this match confirms my suspicions that both wrestlers, although they say differently, have very similar abilities. It all boils down to desire. Fans, we are out of time. Join us for Thursday Knights. I'll see ya'll then!" <Closing credits appear.>
