WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION RAMPAGE 4/5/97 From Beijing, China -----Main Event----- "Psycho" Vicki V v. Bodacious Janet Muniz (Best of 11 Match 5) Valkyrie v. Shannon Stevens The Psycho Crushers v. Geisya Girls Angel Hunter v. "Nasty Girl" Amber Jane Blood v. Asano Otani <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.> <Fireworks explode as the camera shows the crimson WCA logo on the white ring canvas. The camera descends rapidly, then levels off facing the aisle. The camera swerves left, panning out over the capacity crowd and circling the ring, ending its focus on the iridescent three-dimensional WCA letters bracketing the entrance to the backstage area. The referee for the first match heads to the ring.> -------------------- Blood (w/ Red) v. Asano Otani (w/ Dr. Insane) -------------------- Both women lock up. Otani breaks off and nails Blood with a cartwheel kick, then knocks her down with a short clothesline. Blood gets back up, then counters a back suplex with a cross-body block; one, two, thr-shoulder up. Otani blocks a ropeflip hiptoss, then scoops Blood up and takes her down with a backbreaker, taking control of the match. Blood very shortly nails her with a drop-kick, taking charge. Otani soon ducks a flying clothesline. She sidesteps a cartwheel kick, then puts Blood back on the mat via a vertical suplex, going back on the attack. After ninety seconds Blood reverses a whip to a corner. She meets Otani's charge out with an elbow, then takes her down with a belly-to-back suplex, turning things back around. Otani shortly rolls away from a flying elbowdrop. She scoops Blood up and powerslams her; one, two, shoulder up. Otani executes a monkey flip, retaking control of the match. After 3 minutes Blood backdrops out of a jumping DDT. She turns and picks Otani up, then bodyslams her, turning the match around. Otani shortly sends Blood to a corner, then rushes in and nails her with a savate kick. She sends Blood to the far ropes; Blood rebounds and blocks a hiptoss. She nails Otani with a spinning leg lariat, resuming her offensive. Otani soon blocks a ropeflip hiptoss, then nails Blood with a short clothesline, turning the match back around. Blood shortly reverses an Irish whip, misses with a clothesline, then knocks Otani down with a shoulderblock. Blood puts her in a grapevine, retaking control. Otani soon ducks a spinning leg lariat. Blood picks herself up and blocks a standing wristlock, then Otani turns spinning headscissors into a Hotshot. She goes to a corner and climbs to the top; Blood throws her to the mat. Otani dodges a kneelift as she gets back up; Blood whips her to the ropes, then moves away from a kick, then nails Otani with a cartwheel kick. Otani responds with a kick to the head, taking control of the match. She soon takes Blood down via the Chokeslam and covers; one, two, Red pulls the ref out of the ring. Otani gets up and walks over to the ropes; Blood gets up slowly, then sends her to a far corner. She runs in shoulder-first; Otani raises her knees up for the block. Otani takes Blood down with a hiptoss, resuming her assault. Blood shortly rolls out of the ring and grabs a chair, then brings it back into the ring with her. She decks Otani with it, dazing her. Blood disposes of the chair, then takes a stunned Otani down with the Gutwrench Suplex and covers her. Red sees it and quickly ends her argument with the ref, who rolls back into the ring and counts; one, two, three. (15:56) -------------------- Angel Hunter (w/ Janet Strong) v. "Nasty Girl" Amber Jane (w/ The Dream Master and "Dominatress" Kathy Lynn) -------------------- Hunter takes Jane down with a belly-to-back, assuming control of the match. Jane shortly rolls away from an elbowdrop, then throws Hunter out of the ring. One .. two .. three .. four .. five .. six .. seven .. Hunter reenters the ring. Jane runs to the far ropes, misses with a clothesline, then Hunter blocks a facerake and runs to the side ropes. Jane takes her down with neck-scissors; Hunter pops back up and nails her with a chop, retaking control of the match. After 2 minutes Jane nails Hunter with a knee to the back. Hunter whips Jane to a corner, then runs in. Jane lunges away from the Stinger Splash, then slips through the ropes and returns to the corner, going to the top. She takes Hunter down with flying headscissors, turning the match around. She shortly runs to the ropes; Hunter follows her in, but gets backdropped over the top. Jane goes through the ropes, then keeps Hunter down via a flying spinning leg lariat from the apron. Strong helps Hunter get back up, then Hunter throws Jane back into the ring. She rolls in and hooks a leg; Jane kicks out in two. Hunter yanks her up, then sends her to the ropes and nails her with a kick to the leg. She sends Jane to the side ropes; Jane returns with a shoulderblock, then she drives her knee into Hunter's back, retaking control of the match. After 2 minutes Hunter dodges a handspring elbow. Jane picks herself up; Hunter applies a standing wristlock, turning the match back around. After another 2 minutes she sends Jane to the ropes; Jane rebounds and rakes her face. She hits Hunter with a knee to the back, then dodges a kneelift. Hunter reverses a whip to a corner, then throws her out of the ring. The ref keeps Hunter from following. Jane reenters the ring; Hunter sends her to the far ropes. Jane rebounds and takes her down with neck-scissors. Hunter gets back on her feet, then blocks a hiptoss, instead applying another standing wristlock, retaking control. After ninety seconds Hunter nails Jane with a forearm smash. Jane leaves the ring, then motions for Lynn and the Dream Master and walks out, taking the countout. (12:07) ------------------- The Psycho Crushers (Mary Williams and Monique) (w/ Crimson and Red) v. Geisya Girls (Aya Ichijou and Kazumi Hayakawa) (w/ Dr. Insane) -------------------- Williams and Ichijou lock up, then Williams slips behind her and executes a belly-to-back, taking control of the match. Ichijou shortly blocks a legsweep faceslam as Hayakawa enters the ring; Monique enters and throws Hayakawa out, then she and Williams send Ichijou to the ropes. Ichijou returns with a double clothesline as Hayakawa reenters the ring and throws Monique out. She and Ichijou send Williams to the ropes, then take her down with a double clothesline. Monique reenters the ring and tackles Hayakawa, driving her through the ropes to the floor and starting their own brawl. Williams takes Ichijou down with an armdrag as the ref leaves the ring to break up Hayakawa and Monique. Ichijou shortly catches a high cross body and turns it into a backbreaker, retaking control of the match. Williams very shortly blocks a standing wristlock and tags in Monique, then Ichijou counters her belly-to-back with an elbowjab. Monique blocks her facerake; Ichijou reverses an Irish whip. Monique rebounds, then takes control of the match with a spinning mule kick. Ichijou shortly catches another high cross body, turning it into a backbreaker. She goes into a corner and leaps to the top turnbuckle, then hits Monique with a flying splash; Monique kicks out in one. Both women get up; Ichijou blocks a pick-up, then Monique ducks a lariat. She attempts another high cross body, but Ichijou catches and drops her into a backbreaker. Ichijou tags in Hayakawa, then Monique ducks her thrust kick to the head. Ichijou goes to her corner as Hayakawa reverses an Irish whip, then nails Monique with a shoudlerblock. She tags Ichijou back in, then the Girls send Monique to the ropes and double-backdrop her. Ichijou follows up with a kick to the midsection, continuing the assault. She shortly tags in Hayakawa, then both women whip Monique to the ropes and deck her with a double thrust kick to the head. Ichijou goes to her corner. Hayakawa slips through the ropes, then takes control of the match with a springboard drop-kick. After ninety seconds she bodyslams Monique, then tags out to Ichijou, who continues the onslaught with a palm blow. Monique shortly blocks a Japanese armdrag with an elbowjab, then she tags in Williams. They nail Ichijou with a double punch to the midsection, then Ichijou reverses Williams'' whip to a corner. She tags in Hayakawa; Monique gets cut off as the Girls send Williams to the ropes and nail her with a double elbowsmash. They follow up with a double fist to the midsection, then Ichijou returns to her corner as Williams ducks an enzuilariato. Williams executes a kick to the midsection, gaining the advantage. Hayakawa shortly counters a takeover suplex with a belly-to-belly; one, two, shoulder up. Hayakawa executes a front-layout suplex, retaking control of the match. After 2 minutes Williams tags out to Monique, who picks Hayakawa up and drops her into a stomachbreaker, turning the match around. Ichijou shortly enters the ring; she and Hayakawa send Monique to the ropes, then nail her with a double fist to the midsection. Hayakawa puts Monique into an abdominal stretch, retaking control. She shortly tags out to Ichijou; Monique throws the latter out of the ring. One .. two .. three . Ichijou reenters the ring; Monique decks her with a short clothesline. Ichijou picks herself up, then blocks a kick to the leg. She puts Monique into a cobra clutch, taking charge. Crimson very shortly throws Monique a chair; Ichijou catches it and slugs Monique with it right in front of the ref, causing her to disqualify the Geisya Girls. (10:43) ------------------- Valkyrie (w/ The Dream Master and Jenny Black) v. Shannon Stevens (w/ Freedom) -------------------- Both women circle each other warily. They lock up briefly, then Stevens executes a sunset backslide; one, two, kickout. Stevens follows up with a rolling reverse cradle, only to get a one count. Valkyrie ducks a spinning leg lariat, then executes a backflip cradle; one, tow, in the ropes. Stevens executes another rolling reverse cradle; Valkyrie kicks out in two. Stevens plants Valkyrie on the mat with a side suplex, changing her offense. Valkyrie shortly turns spinning headscissors into a Hotshot, taking charge. She soon whips Stevens to the ropes; the Dream Master nails her in the back with Valkyrie's steel-tipped bamboostick. The ref disqualifies Valkyrie.
