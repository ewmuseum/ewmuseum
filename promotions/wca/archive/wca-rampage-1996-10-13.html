Christy Cooper (Submission match) "Psycho" Vicki V v. Karen "Bugs" Huffman "Lethal" Lori Michaels / Bodacious Janet Muniz v. Thunder and Lightning Lovely Liz Strohlheim v. Dawn <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA wrestling action> <Wide angle view of the inside of the arena shows a capacity crowd. The ring announcer, timekeepers, and other officials are walking down the aisle to get ready for the show.> ---------- Lovely Liz Strohlheim (w/ Karen "Bugs" Huffman and Sweet Sue) v. Dawn ---------- Both women lock up, then Strohlheim nails Dawn with a kneelift, taking control of the match. After almost 3 minutes she attempts a savate kick, but Dawn ducks it. She climbs the turnbuckles, then nails Strohlheim with a flying spinning leg lariat. She applies the Scorpion Deathlock; Strohlheim submits after 6 seconds. She played the role that destiny had for her -- second fiddle to me. So I'll give that loser that much. She played a very, very minor role in the LOR hype. It was because I was the only Unified champion this fed ever had. And I was the one who led the WCA on to it's greatest event ever. Without me, the LOR would have been, well, it would have been like Hollehammer might turn out to be. "Right now, I am not scheduled to be at Hollehammer. That may be a surprise to Darkness Inc., but Lori has a big singles match coming up at Hollehammer and I don't know if it would be right for me to even ask her to step aside for Darkness Inc. anyway. I think Lori probably has more to gain in that singles match than the Click has in fighting Darkness Inc. at Hollehammer anyway. You girls can run around with your meaningless belts all you want. But you know that you won't get close to greatness until you get into the same ring with The Click. You see, Darkness Inc., Blunder and Fizzling, Scandalous Ones, Locklears and everybody else, this isn't about tag team belts. This is about leadership. It's about bringing back the tag team division from its doldrums. It's about making that division worth something again. And to do that, you've got to have the Click involved for that to happen. We are involved, so that's happening. It wasn't happening with Darkness Inc. champion. They were messing around with The Racist Wussy group and that was about it. They weren't leaders. They still aren't. The Click is. And we will lead the WCA tag team division to greatness with or without the tag titles and with or without Darkness Inc.'s involvement. Like I've said many times, when my destiny and the Click's has been revealed, all the rest of you are just bit players in the fulfillment of my destiny.
