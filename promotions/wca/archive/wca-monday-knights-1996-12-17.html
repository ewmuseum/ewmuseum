WCA Monday Knights 12/16

17 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION MONDAY KNIGHTS 12/16/96 Live from the Lake Arena in Springfield, Missouri -----Main Event----- <Television Match> "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln v. Christy Cooper Semiramis v. Angel Hunter Valkyrie / Freedom v. Flower Power / Nasty Tracy Turner (No-Countouts / No-Disqualifications / No-Time-Limit) Janet Strong v. Vogue Springfield Succulents v. Bounty Hunters (Baseball Bat Best of 7 Series: Match 1) The Scandalous Ones v. Hardcore <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.> <Fireworks explode as the camera shows a top-down view of the ring, highlighting the maroon WCA logo on the canvas. The camera descends, panning to the right and leveling off, showing the capacity crowd. It focuses on the end of the aisle, where 'WCA' frames the entrance to the locker room. Several WCA officials walk down the aisle.> ---------- The Scandalous Ones (Shelly Rage and Jenny Black) (w/ Dr. Insane and the Dream Master) v. Hardcore ("Nasty Girl" Amber Jane and "Dominatress" Kathy Lynn) (w/ Jonathan Schiz) ---------- Rage and Jane lock up, then rage bodyslams Jane. She executes a monkey flip, then gets up and runs into the ropes. Jane takes her down with a drop-kick to the knee, taking control of the match. She soon sends Rage to the ropes; Rage comes back with a clothesline. She tags in Black, then turns to Jane. Jane counters her brain buster with a vertical suplex; Rage rolls out of the ring as Jane executes a single-leg takedown. She tags in Lynn, then both rake Black's face. Jane follows up with an enzuigiri, then Lynn applies a sleeper, continuing the assault. Black shortly reverses a whip to the ropes. She stops Lynn with a kick to the midsection, then takes her down with a springboard drop-kick. She tags out to Rage, then reconsiders and stays in the ring. Jane enters and knocks Black over the top rope. Rage ducks her enzuigiri to the face, then throws her out of the ring and plants Lynn on the mat with a jack-knife powerbomb. She drops down and covers; one, kickout. Rage pulls Lynn up, then sends her to the ropes. She backdrops Lynn, then runs into the ropes and keeps her down with a shoulderblock, taking charge. Lynn shortly catches her with an enzuigiri to the face, retaking control. A minute later she tags out to Jane. Jane hammers Rage with a flying headbutt, continuing to pummel Rage. She soon runs into the ropes. Rage backs up, then backdrops Jane out of the ring. Jane gets up and pulls Rage out; Rage kicks her back into the guardrail. She whips Jane into a ringpost. Jane whips Rage into the ringsteps, then slams her face onto the. She reenters the ring, followed by Rage. Jane runs into the ropes, then executes a Thesz press and covers; one, two, kickout. Rage gets up and executes an avalanche, then heads to the top rope. Jane dodges a Frankensteiner from the top rope, then prances suggestively. She runs into the ropes as Rage gets back up; Rage takes her down with a bodyslam, gaining the advantage. Jane shortly catches her in flying headscissors, then tags in Lynn. She takes Rage down with an enzuigiri to the face before leaving the ring. Lynn follows up with a dragon suplex and covers; Rage kicks out in one. Lynn slaps her, then hammers her with a flying legdrop, taking charge. Rage shortly bodyslams her, turning things around. Lynn shortly dodges a Frankensteiner from the top rope. She nails Rage with a kneeling headbutt twice, then covers her; one, two, three. (8:08) *Babeball Match Best of 7 Series: Match 1* ---------- Springfield Succulents (Sweet Sue and Karen "Bugs" Huffman)_v. Bounty Hunters (Black and Red) (w/ Panthra) ---------- Sue and Black lock up, then Sue blocks a bat-thrust. Black reverses a whip to the ropes; Sue ducks a bunt. She clotheslines Black with her bat, taking control of the match. Mr. Canuck followed by Thunder and Lightning walk down the aisle. They take up seats at ringside to watch the match. Sue soon drops Black into an over-the-shoulder stomachbreaker, then picks up her bat and tags in Huffman. Huffman runs into the ropes, then jabs Black in the stomach with the end of her bat. She runs into the ropes; Black nails her in the stomach with a hard swing. Huffman falls to her knees; Black pulls her up by the throat and executes a belly-to-belly suplex. She tags out to Red, then hands gives her her bat. She sends Huffman to the ropes as Red sets the bats down, then backdrops Huffman onto them. Red kicks Huffman in the head, then grabs her pulls her up. She picks up her bat, then sends Huffman to the ropes and knocks her down with a bat-thrust to the head, taking control of the match. She soon nails Huffman in the back with her bat, then tags out to Black. Red slugs Huffman across her jaw before leaving. Huffman staggers back, then grabs Black's bat and pulls it forward, then executes an armdrag takedown. She spikes her bat onto Black's chest, then covers her. Black kicks out at the last second. Both women stand up; Huffman executes a bulldog, taking control of the match. After a minute Black sends Huffman to the ropes. Huffman treats Black's head as a softball. Red enters the ring, distracting Huffman to allow Black to execute a takeover suplex. She puts Huffman in a grapevine. Huffman grabs her bat and throws it at Black. Black lets go; Huffman retrieves her bat and makes the tag to Sue. Black sends Sue to the ropes, then decks her with a clothesline with her bat. Sue gets up and blocks an armbar. She whips Black to a corner, then picks her bat up and runs in. She kicks Black in the leg, then slugs her in the head. She goes to the apron and climbs the turnbuckles; Black turns around and throws her to the mat, then tags out to Red. Sue gets up in time to duck Red's jumping side kick. Red jabs Sue's jaw with her bat, then bodyslams her, taking control of the match. Sue shortly backdrops out of a front facelock, then keeps Red down with neck-scissors. Red gets up again; Sue uppercuts her with her bat, retaking control. Black shortly enter the ring and takes Sue down with a blow to the stomach, then Red pounds on Sue's shoulderblades with her bat, retaking control. Sue very shortly nails Red in the shin, then jabs her kneecap, regaining her momentum. After 3 minutes Sue backdrops Red out of the ring, then turns and joins her on the floor. Red knocks Sue into the ringsteps, then brings her bat down on Sue's abdomen. She pulls Sue up, then sends her to a ringpost. Panthra moves toward Sue; Sue nails her with a kick to the midsection. Red nails Sue with a bat-spike to the head, then sends Sue back into the ringsteps. Sue gets back up, then blocks the Snare. Red headbutts her, then takes her down with a jumping side kick. Sue scrambles past her and retrieves her bat. Red ducks a swing with it, then throws Sue back into the ring. She enters and tags out to Black, then slugs Sue before leaving. Black applies a cobra clutch. Huffman enters the ring and jabs Black in the head with her bat. She leaves the ring as Black ducks a bunt. She runs into the ropes, then nails Sue with a blow to the neck, taking charge. A minute later she nails Sue with a blow to the base of her skull. Sue falls to the mat, stunned. Black spikes her bat into Sue's neck, then props her up. She pulls Sue to her feet, then applies the Swinging Full Nelson. Sue bites her lip, but 10 seconds later submits. Thunder and Lightning leave the arena. After the bell, Huffman and Red enter the ring. They trade blows while Black continues the Swinging Full Nelson. Semiramis, dressed in a military outfit, comes racing down the aisle to a cheering audience. She sizes up the situation, then grabs Black's bat. She gets in front of Black and Sue, then nails Sue in the stomach. Red takes out Huffman's knee, gaining the advantage in their brawl. The Mistress runs down the aisle and picks up Huffman's bat. She decks Red, then turns and confronts Semiramis. Semiramis throws the bat at Sue, then chokeslams the Mistress. She picks her up again and chokeslams her onto Sue, then the Bounty Hunters with Semiramis and Panthra leave them sprawled out in the ring. (Match: 14:52) ---------- Janet Strong (w/ Angel Hunter) v. Vogue (w/ the Prince) ---------- Strong catches Vogue in a belly-to-belly suplex; Vogue gets her shoulder up in two. Strong puts her in a short arm scissors. She lets go in 6 seconds and applies a Boston crab. Vogue submits after 5 seconds. (0:32) (No Countouts / No Disqualifications / No-Time-Limit) ---------- Valkyrie / Freedom v. Flower Power / Nasty Tracy Turner (w/ Dr. Insane and the Dream Master) ---------- Valkyrie and Power start. Valkyrie catches Power in a backflip cradle; Power easily kicks out. She leaves the ring and retrieves her rattan cane. She swings it at Valkyrie, who grabs it and takes it away. She nails Power with it, then sends Power to the ropes. She executes a reverse neckbreaker, taking control of the match. She soon tags in Freedom. They send Power to the ropes, then double-backdrop her. Turner enters the ring and throws Valkyrie out, then she and Power double-backdrop Freedom. Turner leaves as Power executes a high cross body. Freedom kicks out in two. She gets up as Power scales the turnbuckles. Power comes down with a flying clothesline. She tags out to Turner. Turner runs into the ropes. Freedom ducks a clothesline, then knocks Turner down with a shoulderblock, taking control of the match. After a minute she runs into the ropes. Turner ducks a clothesline, then takes Freedom down with a leg lariat, turning things around. Freedom very shortly tags out to Valkyrie. Turner reverses a whip to the ropes. Valkyrie ducks a clothesline, then ducks another, then backdrops Turner, taking charge. Turner shortly catches her in a reverse cradle. Valkyrie kicks out in two; both women stand up. Turner nails her with a spin kick, taking control of the match. She shortly sends Valkyrie to the ropes; Valkyrie comes back with an elbow to the face, retaking control. After 2 minutes Freedom enters the ring. She and Valkyrie send Turner to the ropes; Turner ducks a double elbowsmash, then executes a double clothesline. Freedom leaves as Turner nails Valkyrie with a leaping forearm, turning things around. She soon tags in Power, who continues the assault with a flying clothesline. After almost 2 minutes Valkyrie counters a flying karate chop with a fist to the midsection. Power falls to her knees and begs off; Valkyrie knocks her back with a kick, turning the match back around. After another 2 minutes she caps her onslaught with the Valkyrian Storm and pins Power. (11:11) ---------- Semiramis v. Angel Hunter (w/ Janet Strong) ---------- Both women lock up, then Semiramis chops Hunter. She applies a bearhug, taking control of the match. After 2 minutes Hunter blocks a faceslam. She sends Hunter to ropes, then misses with a clothesline. She blocks a clawhold, then kicks Semiramis in the midsection. She applies a full nelson, turning the match around. After 2 minutes she runs into the ropes. Semiramis stops her with a kick, then hammers her in a side suplex, turning the match back around. After 3 1/2 minutes Hunter counters a Guerrilla Press with a rolling reverse cradle. Semiramis kicks out in two. She throws Semiramis out of the ring; the ref keeps her from following. Semiramis reenters; Hunter sends her to a corner. She charges in shoulder-first; Semiramis moves out of the way. Hunter rams the ringpost; Semirmais pulls her out and puts her in a bearhug, resuming her assault. After 3 minutes Hunter sends Semiramis to the ropes. She stops her with a kick, then executes a back suplex, turning the match around again. After 2 minutes Semiramis dodges a kneelift. She executes a Hotshot, going back on the attack. After 5 minutes of pummeling she takes Hunter down with a short lariat and pins her. (18:01) ===== Birmingham: "Semiramis, former Tag Team co-champion turned backstabber, defeats Angel Hunter with a harder-edged assault than we've seen from her in quite a while. Angel Hunter continues to improve as a wrestler, though, and will undoubtedly overcome this setback. Up next is our main event, a match for the Television titles. 'The Intimidator' Amber Axeln, herself no stranger to betrayal, faces former Can-Am champion Christy Cooper. The Posse has seen some hard times, but won the Tag Team titles on Saturday, albeit using questionable tactics leading up to the match. Are their misfortunes over? Let's go to the ring." Ring Announcer: "Ladies and gentlemen, tonight's main event is for the Tag Team championships! Introducing the challenger: representing the Posse of Punishment and accompanied to the ring by Dr. Insane, from Boston, Massachusetts, weighing in at 278 pounds, Christy Cooper!" <Fans boo cooper as she and Dr. Insane walk down the aisle. They enter the ring; Cooper raises her hands up to further incite the crowd.> "Her opponent and current Television and World champion: accompanied to the ring by her tag team partner Valkyrie, from Richmond, Virginia, weighing in at 120 pounds, 'the Intimidator' Amber Axeln!" <Axeln carries her World and Television titles slung over her shoulders as she walks alongside Valkyrie to the ring. Valkyrie slaps the fans hands, then both enter the ring.> Birmingham's voice: "The rest of the Posse are supposedly preparing videotaped apologies to show to Bodacious Janet Muniz; we'll follow Cooper out of the ring after the match to see if that's actually happening. Axeln gives Valkyrie her World belt, then hands the Television title to referee Esther Siler. dr. Insane leaves the ring; Valkyrie steps out as well. Siler checks both wrestlers, then calls for the bell. Collar-and-elbow lock-up! Cooper throws Axeln out of the ring and joins her on the floor. Dr. Insane distracts Axeln; Cooper kicks her into the guardrail! She whips Axeln into the ringpost. Axeln backdrops out of an earringer. Cooper gets back up and shoves Amber into the ringsteps. Axeln uses them to reenter the ring; Cooper follows her in. She blocks a full nelson; Axeln reverses a whip to a corner. "She charges in - Cooper moves out of the way! She spins Amber around and rocks her with a European uppercut. Cooper takes her out of the corner via an armdrag takedown, then executes a tiger driver; one, two, three! We have a new champion!" RA: "Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the match and NEW Television champion: in 1 minute 5 seconds, Christy Cooper!" <Cooper accepts the Television title, then slings it over her shoulder and raises the opposing arm up as Axeln leaves the ring.> Birmingham: "Christy Cooper becomes the new Television champion! The Intimidator seemed almost distracted during the match; she may still be occupied with Muniz's vicious turn on her. Cooper's now leaving the ring; let's see where she goes. It's interesting to note that the Television title has gone to wrestlers who have also earned the Tag Team titles, with the exception of Amber. Cooper enters the backstage area and heads to the dock. Walking past the Posse's limo, outside the arena. There's our broadcast trailer. She walks around that to another trailer - with the Dream Master waiting by the door! Cooper enters, followed by Dr. Insane. The Dream Master enters, then our cameraman." <Camera shows Cooper getting behind Rage, Black, and Turner. Turner stands beside a cameraman, focusing the camera on "Psycho" Vicki V. The Dream Master and Dr. Insane take up positions beside her.> "Can we get audio? No? Well, fans, it appears the Posse are preparing the apologies for display at the upcoming Stone Cold. And that ends this edition of Monday Knights. Coming up this Theatre of War, Janet Strong takes on Valkyrie, Big Norma Hudson goes up against 'Psycho' Vicki V, Nasty Tracy Turner meets 'Destroyer' Dara Holt, Jenny Black takes on Angel Hunter, Freedom meets Black, Blood makes her WCA debut against the Vinnie Babe, and Sarah Bryant takes on Flower Power! I'm John Birmingham; have a good evening!" <Closing credits appear.>
