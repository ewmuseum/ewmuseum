WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION RAMPAGE Live from Minneapolis, Minnesota 6/22/96 Bad Company and X-Men make their debuts tonight. -----Main Event----- <Women's Television Title Match> The Mistress of Death v. "The Flawless One" Sydney Sheppard Russian Ring Masters v. New Masters of Violence "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln v. "Hot Stuff" Erica Linley Diego v. Bob "Crusher" Jones Lovely Liz Strohlheim v. Karen "Bugs" Huffman Count Darkon v. Drake "the Eliminator" Davis Chris Cliff v. White Tiger =X= v. Bad Company Johnny Vicious v. Thunderlips Sultans of Swing v. X-Men Double J v. Cindy Simmons ------------------------- Double J v. Cindy Simmons (w/ Ghetto Blaster, Jeff "Heartbreak Boy" Blackwell, and Chris Cliff) ------------------------- Simmons goes on the offensive with an enzuigiri. She shortly goes for a hiptoss, but Double J counters with a backslide. Blaster distracts the ref; Simmons doesn't notice and reverses the pin. The ref doesn't see it; Double J kicks out and tries to stand up. Simmons nails her with a legsweep faceslam and resumes her offensive. After almost three minutes she whips Double J into the ropes. Doulbe J comes back with a shoulderblock, evening the match up. Simmons climbs to the top turnbuckle, but Double J throws her to the mat, then waits until Simmons tries to stand. She hammers Simmons with a swinging neckbreaker, then tries to follow up with a reverse neckbreaker. Simmons blocks it and nails her with a double underhook piledriver, taking control of the match again. After two minutes Double J evens the match up again with a belly-to-back suplex. She shortly runs into the ropes; Simmons misses her with a clothesline, then Double J misses with a kick. She rebounds a third time and misses with a shoulderblock, then rebounds again and clotheslines Simmons, sending her stumbling over the top rope to the floor. Double J goes through the ropes, then whips Simmons into Cliff. Simmons rebounds off Cliff's ample girth and almost falls to the floor, but Cliff grabs her arm and pulls her upright. Double J reenters the ring, followed by Simmons. Double J kicks Simmons in the head as the latter comes through the ropes, turning the match around. After two minutes she goes for a reverse neckbreaker, but Simmons blocks it and tries to nail her with a spin kick. Double J ducks down, then stands back up. Simmons slaps her, then takes her down with a legsweep faceslam, turning the match back around. She shortly throws Double J out of the ring, then follows her out. Double J walks around the ring corner; Simmons chases after her. Double J slides back into the ring, then runs into the far ropes. Simmons enters the ring and gets hit with a shoulderblock as Double J goes back on the offensive. Simmons shorlty runs into the ropes, then turns the match back around with a takeover suplex. She shortly nails Doulbe J with the FrankenSteiner and pins her. (10:27) ------------------------- Sultans of Swing (Rodgers and Hammerstein) (MGR: Frankie) v. X-Men (Executioner and Ethan Hawk) (w/ Drake "the Eliminator" Davis) ------------------------- Rodgers and Hawk start. Hawk goes on the offensive with a backspin DDT. He shortly tags out to Executioner, then goes for a back suplex. Rodgers counters with a cross-body block, going for the pin. Hawk kicks out in two, then rolls out of the ring as Hammerstein enters and nails Executioner with a thrust kick to the head. Rodgers goes for a flying elbowdrop, but Execuioner rolls out of the way and goes on the offensive with a spinning elbowdrop. He shortly tags out to Hawk; Hawk nails Rodgers with a spin kick, continuing the onslaught. He shortly tries to send Rodgers into the ropes, but Rodgers reverses it, then hits him with an elbow. Rodgers tags out to Hammerstein, then nails Hawk with a belly-to-back suplex. Hammerstein follows up with an elbowdrop from the second turnbuckle, turning the match around. After a minute Hawk evens the match up by whipping Hammerstein into the ropes. Hammerstein goes for a high cross body, but Hawk catches him and hammers him with a backbreaker, turning the match back around. Hammerstin shortly catches Hawk in a backslide; Hawk kicks out in two. Hammerstein nails him with an inverted atomic drop, retaking control of the match. Hawk tags out to the Executioner. They take Hammerstein down with a double backdrop, then Hawk nails him with a spinning leg lariat before leaving the ring. Executioenr goes for a back suplex, but Hammerstein counters with a cross-body block, and goes for the pin. Eecugioner gets his shoulder up in two; Hammerstein rewards him with a kick to the thigh, takign control. He tags out to Rodgers, then Hawk enters the ring and helps him out by booting him through the ropes. Executioner runs into the ropes; Rodgers hits him with an elbow, continuing the offensive. He soon goes for a legsweep faceslam, but Executioenr blocks it. Rodgers whips him into the corner, then walks in. He goes for headscissors, but Executioner ducks them. Hammerstein enters the ring and together he and Rodgers whip Executioner into the ropes. They nail him with a double kick to the thigh, then Rodgers follows up with a reverse flying elbowdrop. Executioner tags out to Hawk; Rodgers greets him with a thrust kick to the head, continuing thier offensive. Hammerstein soon enters the ring and together he and Rodgers nail Hawk with the Crooner. Rodgers goes for the pin; the ref counts one .. two .. three. (11:01) ------------------------- Johnny Vicious (MGR: Tall Troll) v. Thunderlips ------------------------- Vicious goes on the offenisve with a Soviet suplex. After 75 seconds Thunderlips begins to even the match up. Vicious shortly after chokes him. The ref reaches the five count and warns Vicious, who yells back at the ref, then reapplies the choke out of spite. He lets go at the three count, flips the ref off, then whips Thunderlips into the ropes. Thunderlips comes back with an enzigiri, turning the match around. He soon goes for a headbutt, but Vicious blocks it and attempts to send Thunderlips into the ropes. Thunderlips reverses it, then ducks a clothesline. He misses with a shoulderblock; Vicious rebounds for the thrd time and kicks Thunderlips in the butt, turning the match back around. He shortly goes for a piledriver, but Thunderlips counters with a backdrop. Vicious re-reverses it into a sunset flip; Thunderlips kicks out in two, then stands up. He goes for a side suplex, but Vicious blocks it and nails him with a DDT, regaining his momentum. Thunderlips immediately begins to make a comeback. Vicious whips him into the ropes; he comes back with a kick, then runs into the adjacent ropes. Troll pulls the top rope down, sending him crashing to the floor. Vicious goes through the ropes, and goes for a flying axhandle from the apron, but Thunderlips catches him and powerslams on the ringsteps. He throws Vicious back into the ring, then rolls back in. He goes for a figure four, but Vicious counters with a small package, pinning him. (8:35) ------------------------- =X= (Condor and Scorpion) (MGR: Harvey "the Mouth" Lippman) v. Bad Company (Dustin Bones and "Deadly" Dylan Vaughn) (MGRS: Jason Hate and Randall Chase) ------------------------- Scorpion and Vaughn start. Scorpion goes on the offensive with a fireman's carry. He shortly goes for a lariat, but Vaughn ducks it and dropkicks him twice. He takes Scorpion down on the second one, then goes for the pin. Scorpion kicks out in two and gets back up. Vaughn goes for a European uppercut, but Scorpion counters with a backslide. Vaughn kicks out in two and reverses it; Scorpion kicks out in two. Vaughn punishes him with a European uppoercut, taking control of the match. Scorpion shortly tags out to Condor. He goes for a Samoan Drop, but Vaughn counters with an elbowsmash. Scorpion leaves the ring as Vaughn continues his offensive with a crucifix; Condor kicks out in two. Vaughn soon nails Condor with a flying clothesline, then executes the Stun Gun and pins Condor. (2:59) ------------------------- Chris Cliff (w/ Ghetto Blaster, Cindy Simmons, and Jeff "Heartbreak Boy" Blackwell) v. White Tiger ------------------------- White Tiger assumes control of the match with a flying axhandle. He shortly goes for a slingshot bodyblock, but Cliff catches him and hammers him with a powerslam. Cliff covers White Tiger, but can only get a two count. He complains about a slow count, enabling White Tiger to catch him in an inside cradle. Cliff kicks out at the last second; White Tiger punishes him with an elbowsmash and resumes his offensive. He soon goes for a flying headbutt, but Cliff rolls out of the way, evening up the match. Both men begin trading blows in the ring. White Tiger shortly gains an advantage with a shoulderblock. Cliff shortly turns the match around with a legsweep faceslam. White Tiger soon evens the match up with a handspring moonsault. Both men stand toe-to-toe with each other. Cliff shortly breaks the stalemate with a backspin DDT. He picks Whtie Tiger up, then pins him with the Running Powerslam. (6:36) ------------------------- Count Darkon v. Drake "the Eliminator" Davis (w/ Executioner and Ethan Hawk) ------------------------- Davis assumes control of the match with spinning mule kick. He shorlty goes for a flyng karate chop, but Darkon counters with a fist to the midsection, turning the match around. After 90 seconds he puts Davis in a camel clutch. Davis breaks the hold after 7 seconds, then turns the match back around with a belly-to-back suplex. He soon climbs to the top turnbuckle, but Darkon punches him hard in the stomach. Davis loses his footing and straddles the turnbuckle. Darkon dropkicks him out of the ring, then joins him on the floor. Executioner attempts to distract him, but Darkon sees him coming and reenters the ring. Davis reenters at the nine count; Darkon punches him, turning the match back around. Davis soon nails him with a lariat, turning the match around again. After a minute Darkon evens the match up with a stomachbreaker. Both men stand toe-to-toe. Davis shortly takes control agian with a handspring bodyblock. He shortly executes a belly-to-belly suplex and pins Darkon. Darkon complains about a fast count. (8:36) ------------------------- Lovely Liz Strohlheim v. Karen "Bugs" Huffman ------------------------- Strohlheim goes on the offensive with a back suplex. She goes for a cobra clutch, but Huffman counters with an armdrag takedown. Strohlheim in turn re-reverses it into a lariat and continues. She shorlty goes for a kneelift, but Huffman sidesteps it, evening up the match. Strohlheim shortly goes for a back suplex, but Huffman counters with a cross-body block and goes for the pin. Strohlheim gets her shoulder up in two; Huffman rewards her witn a kneeling headbutt, taking control of the match. She soon whips Strohlheim into the ropes. Strohlheim comes back and rocks her with an elbow, turning the match back around. After three-and-a-half minutes Huffman nails her with a Guerilla Press, turning the match around again. After two minutes she clothesliens Strohlheim to the floor, then joins her. Huffman whips Strohlheim into the ringsteps, then bodyslams her before reentering the ring. Strohlheim follows her back in, then nails her with a back heel kick, going on the offensive. Huffman shortly retakes control with a swinging punch. Strohlheim just as quickly turns the match back around with an elbowsmash. Huffman begins to even the match up. She shorlty kicks Strohlheim in the head, sending the latter spinning. Huffman hammers her with an inverted powerbomb and goes for the pin. Strohlheim gets her foot on the bottom rope in two. Huffman rewards her with a stomp in the stomach, resuming her offensive. Strohlheim soon evens the match up with an abdominal stretch. Huffman breaks the hold after 7 seconds and stands toe-to-toe with Strohlheim. Strohlheim shorlty takes control of the match with a double-axhandle chop. Huffman quickly tries to turn it around with a front-layout suplex, but Strohlheim counters with a vertical suplex. She pulls Huffman to her feet by the hair, then hammers her with a brain buster. She covers Huffman with the Schoolgirl Pin and gets the pinfall. (15:16) ------------------------- Diego (MGR: Tyrone Jones) v. Bob "Crusher" Jones ------------------------- Diego hits Jones with a knee to the back before the bell, taking control of the match. Jones shortly ends his assault with a thrust kick to the head. After 90 seconds Diego blocks a slap and returns the same, then chokes Jones. He lets go at the four count and goes for a short lariat. Jones ducks it, then takes him down with an elbowsmash. Diego gets back up and takes Jones down with a chop, turning the match back around. Jones soon retakes control with a jumping DDT. He shortly goes for a kneelift, but Diego sidesteps it and applies a bearhug. Jones reaches the ropes behind him after 7 seconds. Diego runs into the opposing ropes, then both men clothesline each other. Diego gets up first and nails Jones with a kneedrop, taking control of the match. He shortly punches Jones in the gut; Jones returns the same, then nails him with a kneelift, turning the match back around. He soon goes for neck-scissors, but Diego ducks them, evening up the match. Both men stand toe-to-toe. Dieglo shortly goes for a bodyslam, but Jones counters with a small package. Diego kicks out in two; Jones applies a standing wristlock, taking control of the match again. Diego soon throws him out of the ring to end his onslaught. Jones reenters immediately and goes for a kick to the thigh, but Diego blocks him and his him with a chop. Jones comes back with a front facelock, takign control again. After three minutes he goes for a kick to the head, but Diego ducks down, then nails him with a Hotshot, turning the match around. After two minutes he goes for a short lariat, but Jones blocks it and takes him down with neck-scissors. Diego gets back up and blocks a back heel kick, then punches Jones in the abdomen, regaining his momentum. Jones shortly puts him in a cobra clutch, taking control of the match. Diego quickly ends it with a fist to the midsection, then he nails Joens with an enzuilariato and pins him. (17:59) ------------------------- "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln (MGR: The Prince) v. "Hot Stuff" Erica Linley (MGR: Fred Scuttle) ------------------------- Linley whips Axeln into the ropes; Axeln comes back with a backdrop, takign control of the match. After a minute she hammers Linley with a backbreaker, then follows up with the Doombomb and goes for the pin. Linley kicks otu at the last second; Axeln punishes her with a standing wristlock, beginning a series of submission holds. She shortly backdrops Linley out of the ring, then goes through the ropes. Linley leads her on a chase around the ring, then reenters. Axeln comes in through the ropes; Linley meets her with a DDT, turning the match around. She shortly tries to send Axeln into the ropes, but Axeln reverses it. The Prince trips Linley when she hits the ropes; the ref warns him as Axlen keeps Linley on the mat with a legsweep. She shortly covers Linley with a standing moonsault; Linley kicks out in two. Axeln complains about a slow count. She attempts to demonstrate a three count to the ref, but before she's done Linley catches her in an inside cradle. Axlen kicks out in two, then kicks Linley in the head in retaliation, resuming her offensive. She soon clotheslines Linley out of the ring. Linley reenters at the seven count; Axeln dropkicks her, sendiing her stumbling into the ref. Axeln shortly goes for aother dropkick, but Linley moves out of the way, then nails Axeln with an elbowdrop, turning the match around. She soon whips Axeln into a corner. Axeln comes back and takes Linley down with a clothesline, turning the match back around. She soon hammers Linley wiht a backbreaker, then executes the Doombomb and pins her. (9:24) ------------------------- Russian Ring Masters (Knikita Van Vadar and Ruben Von Nazck) (MGR: Jason Hate) v. New Masters of Violence (Demolisher and Brian Simpson) (MGR: Tyrone Jones) ------------------------- Van Vadar and Demolisher start. Demolisher takes control with an elbowsmash. He soon tags otu to Simpson, who continues the offensive with a facebite. Van Vadar shortly ends it by throwing Simpson out of th ring. He tags out to Von Nazck, then lays on the mat. Simpson enters and looks at Van Vadar, distracted long enough for Von Nazck to take him down with a forearm to the back of the head, turning the match around. Simpson shortly runs into the ropes, then shoulderblocks Von Nazck, going back on the offensive. After a minute he goes for a rope burn, but Von Nazck blocks it and tags out to Van Vadar. Demolisher enters the ring and helps Von Nazck out with a shove that sends the Russian to the floor, then he and Simpson nail Van Vadar with a double elbowsmash. Simpson continues his assault with a stomachbreaker. Van Vadar shortly turns the match back around with a kneelift. He soon goes for the Vadar Bomb, but Simpson counters with a small package. Van Vadar kicks out in two; Simpson hits him with an elbow, retaking control. He shortly tags out to Demolisher. Demolisher goes for a short lariat, but Van Vadar blocks it, evening up the match. The two men stand toe-to-toe. Demolisher shortly breaks the stalemate with a chokehold. He lets go at the four count and slaps Van Vadar, beginning a series of high-impact blows. After two minutes he goes for a flying headbutt, but Van Vadar rolls away, turnign the match back around. After two minutes he executes a sunset flip; Demolisher kicks out in two, then bites Van Vadar's face. Van Vadar takes him down with a short clothesline, regaining his momentum. Demolisher soon tags out to Simpson, who catches Van Vadar in a back suplex, turning the match back around. Van Vadar shortly tags out to Von Nazck; Simpson greets the latter with a Hotshot. He shortly goes for a forearm to the back, but Von Nazck moves out of the way, then nails Simpson with a back heel kick, taking control. Demolisher shortly enters the ring to make it two-on-one. they whip Von Nazck into the ropes, then nail him with a double back heel kick. Van Vadar enters the ring, but gets cut off, enabling the New Masters of Violence to continue double teaming Von Nazck. They shortly goe for a double backdrop, but Von Nazck counters with a double headsmash, retaking control. He soon tags out to Van Vadar; Simpson counters by tagging out to Demolisher. Van Vadar tags back out to Von Nazck, Demolisher responds by tagging back out to Simpson. Von Nazck applies an armbar; Simpson breaks out in 10 seconds and nails the Russian with a reverse neckbreaker. He climbs to the top turnbuclke as Von Nazck stands up; Simpson goes for the timber Cutter, but Von Nazck counters with a fist to the midsection, turning the match around. He goes for the Figure Four, but Simpson counters with a small package. The ref counts one .. two .. Hate pulls him out of the ring. Von Nazck punishes Simpson with a snap suplex as Hate and the ref argue on the floor. Simpson shortly puts Von Nazck in a figure four of his own; the ref reenters the ring five seconds later to ask Von Nazck, who submits after 17 seconds. (20:22) <Women's Television Title Match> ------------------------- The Mistress of Death (MGR: Lu Chin) v. "The Flawless One" Sydney Sheppard (w/ "Heat Wave" Heather Hetfield and MGR Jason Hate) ------------------------- Sheppard goes on the offensive with a swinging punch. She shortly goes for a snap suplex, but the Mistress counters with a vertical suplex, turning hte match around. She shortly attempts to send Sheppard into the ropes, but Sheppard reverses it. The Mistress goes for a back heel kick, but Sheppard blocks it, evening up the match. Both women stand toe-to-toe. After a minute Sheppard goes for a spinebuster. The Mistress counters with a swinging neckbreaker, takign control again. After two minutes she goes for a headlock takedown, but Sheppard counters with a back suplex. She tries to follow up with a scorpion deathlock, but the Mistress kicks her off. The Mistress goes for a snap suplex, but Sheppard counters with a brain buster, turning the match around. She shortly goes for a double underhook backbreaker, but the Mistress blocks it and goes on the offensive with a series of submission holds, starting with a full nelson. After 45 seconds Sheppard ends the assault by backdropping her out of the ring. The Mistress rolls back in at the three count; Sheppard goes for the pin. The Mistress kicks out at the last second; Sheppard rewards her with a flying legdrop, taking firm control of the match. She soon climbs to the top turnbuckle, but hte Mistress nails her in the stomach. Sheppard falls onto the apron, then to the floor. The Mistress joins her, but Sheppard tackles her and runs her back to the guardrail. Both women begin trading blows while the ref counts. Hate tries to get Sheppard back into the ring, but she ignores him. The ref reaches ten and counts both women out. The Mistress of Death remains the Women's Television champion.
