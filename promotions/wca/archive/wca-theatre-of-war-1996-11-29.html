WCA Theatre of War 11/28

29 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION THEATRE OF WAR >From Vienna, Austria 11/28/96 -----Main Event----- <Television Title Match> The Mistress of Death v. "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln Sweet Sue v. Karen "Bugs" Huffman (I Quit match) The Assassin v. Janet Strong Flower Power v. Freedom Helena "High Kick" Knightsbridge v. Tammi Cooper Battle Kats v. "Psycho" Vicki V / Christy Cooper ---------- Battle Kats (Tygra and Panthra) (w/ Lu Chin) v. "Psycho" Vicki V / Christy Cooper (w/ Dr. Insane and the Dream Master) ---------- Tygra nails Cooper with an enzuigiri, assuming control of the match. She shortly sends Cooper to the ropes; Cooper rebounds and ducks an elbow, rebounds again and clotheslines Tygra. She keeps Tygra down with a jumping side kick and raises her hands up, then tags in Vicki. Vicki catches Tygra in a bridging back suplex; Tygra easily kicks out. She blocks Cooper's back suplex; Cooper leaves as Tygra executes a legsweep faceslam and tags out to Panthra. Panthra executes a Doctor bomb, taking charge. Vicki shortly blocks a side suplex and nails Panthra with an enzuigiri, turning things around. After 2 minutes Panthra backdrops out of a DDT, then tags in Tygra. Cooper enters the ring and throws Panthra out, then she and Vicki send Tygra to the ropes and double-backdrop her. Tygra picks herself up as Cooper leaves the ring. Vicki punches her and tags in Cooper. Panthra enters and knocks Vicki out of the ring, then nails Cooper with a spinning mule kick. Tygra follows up with a bridging back suplex; Cooper kicks out in two. She rolls away from a slingshot somersault splash and tags out to Vicki. Vicki falls onto Tygra, then heads for the top rope. Tygra rolls away from a flying elbowdrop, then hammers Vicki with a legdrop, going back on the offensive. Vicki soon ducks a diving elbowsmash, then Cooper enters the ring. The ref cuts Panthra off before she can intervene; Cooper nails Tygra with a lariat. Vicki follows up with a back suplex, turning things around. She shortly sends Tygra to the ropes; Tygra comes back with a reverse neckbreaker. Vicki gets back on her feet; Tygra sends her to a corner and charges in. Vicki raises her leg up, then tags in Cooper. Both women rake Vicki's face, then send Tygra to the ropes. Tygra rebounds and ducks a double elbowsmash, rebounds again and dual-clotheslines them. Vicki rolls out of the ring as Cooper counters a fisherman suplex with a small package. Tygra kicks out in two; Cooper executes a backbreaker, taking charge. Tygra soon counters a back suplex with a cross-body block; Cooper kicks out in two. Tygra tags out to Panthra; Panthra ducks a thrust kick to the head, then returns the same. She follows up with a kick to the midsection, retaking control. Cooper soon counters a double reverse chinlock with a facerake, then tags out to Vicki. Vicki pulls Panthra up, then whips her into the ropes. Panthra comes back with an enzuilariato. She attempts to execute a backslide, but Vicki fights it. Vicki wins; Panthra kicks out in two. Vicki pulls Panthra up, then whips her to the ropes and stops her with a drop-kick to the knee, taking control. She soon runs into the ropes. Panthra ducks a clothesline, spins around and ducks an elbow, then glances back and rocks Vicki with an elbow to the face. She climbs the turnbuckles, then executes a Frankensteiner from the top rope, turning things back around. She shortly runs into the ropes. Vicki knocks her down with a shoulderblock and covers her; Panthra kicks out in two. Vicki tags in Cooper as Panthra gets back up. Panthra counters Vicki's Samoan Drop with an elbowsmash, then hiptosses Cooper as Vicki leaves the ring. Panthra follows up with a short clothesline, retaking control. Cooper shortly sidesteps a Frankensteiner from the top rope. She takes Panthra down with a swinging neckbreaker, then puts her in Christy's Clutch. Panthra submits after 5 seconds. (13:25) ---------- Helena "High Kick" Knightsbridge v. Tammi Cooper ---------- Knightsbridge catches Cooper in a belly-to-belly suplex; Cooper kicks out in two. Knightsbridge keeps her off-balance with a kneelift, then bodyslams her, going on the offensive. Cooper soon reverses a whip to a corner. She runs in shoulder-first; Knightsbridge blocks her with a knee. Cooper staggers back into the ring, then blocks a standing wristlock. She executes a short lariat, turning things around. After 2 1/2 minutes Knightsbridge ducks a short lariat. Cooper ducks a jumping side kick; Knightsbridge settles for an elbowsmash. She applies a standing wristlock, turning the match around. Cooper shortly reverses a whip to a corner and nails Knightsbridge with a drop-kick to the knee, retaking control. After 2 minutes Knightsbridge blocks a spinning toe hold. She gets back up and takes Cooper down with neck-scissors, turning the match back around. Cooper shortly ducks a lariat, then nails Knightsbridge with a spinning mule kick, retaking control. Knightsbridge soon turns things around with a kick to the head. Cooper soon retakes control with a spin kick. She soon runs into the ropes. Knightsbridge shrugs off the Bulldog; Cooper settles for an elbowsmash. She ducks a kick to the head; Knightsbridge reverses a whip to the ropes. She hits Knightsbridge with an elbow and executes a German suplex, regaining her momentum. Knightsbridge shortly ducks a jumping side kick. She taps her thigh, then decks Cooper with the Running Superkick. Knightsbridge pins her. (12:47) ---------- Flower Power v. Freedom ---------- Both women lock up, then Freedom punches Power across the jaw, then in the midsection. Power backs up to the ropes, then rushes Freedom; Freedom hits her with a shoulderblock. Power reverses a whip to the ropes, then clotheslines Freedom, taking control of the match. Freedom soon punches her in the midsection, retaking control. Power shortly counters a headlock takedown with a back suplex, retaking control. Freedom shortly reverses a whip to the ropes, then nails Power with a bulldog, going back on the offensive. Power shortly counters a bodyslam with a small package. Freedom kicks out in two; Power rewards her with an enzuilaraito, retaking control. Freedom shortly nails her with a chop, going back on the attack. After 2 minutes Freedom hammers Power with an elbowdrop from the second rope. She follows up with the 4th of July, pinning Power. (6:31) ---------- The Assassin (w/ Li'l Cheetah) v. Janet Strong ---------- The Assassin throws Strong out of the ring. Strong reenters at the seven count; the Assassin runs her face along the top rope, assuming control of the match. Strong shortly blocks a kick to the leg and executes an enzuigiri, taking charge. She soon runs into the ropes, the puts the Assassin in the Bearhug. The Assassin submits after 17 seconds. (1:22) ===== Birmingham: "Janet Strong continues her recent winning streak with a victory over the Assasssin, taking her out in less than ninety seconds! Janet Strong has the potential to become one of the greats in the WCA; time will tell if she does. Up next is an I-Quit match between Sweet Sue and Karen "Bugs" Huffman, two members of the TCA and together the tag team the Springfield Succulents. Both women have a long, not-always-friendly history together. Sue has defeated Huffman at least twice before; will tonight be any different? Let's go now to the ring." Ring Announcer: "Ladies and gentlemen, the following match is an I-Quit match! To win, a wrestler must make her opponent say the words 'I quit' into the microphone. Introducing first: representing the Top Catfighters' Alliance, from Springfield, Ohio, weighing in at 123 pounds, Karen 'Bugs' Huffman!" <Huffman walks down the aisle, slapping hands. She enters the ring and starts skipping around, waving at the crowd.> "Her opponent: also representing the Top Catfighters' Alliance, from Medford, New Jersey, weighing in at 117 pounds, Sweet Sue!" <Sue walks down the aisle, ignoring the fans. She enters the ring.> Birmingham: "Referee Mark White checks both wrestlers. Liz walks down the aisle to observe her friends' match. The bell sounds, and we have a collar-and-elbow lockup! Sue breaks off and kicks Huffman in the head. She follows up with a brain buster! Huffman slowly raises up. Sue applies a double reverse chinlock. Karen quickly breaks the hold. She rubs her head, then picks herself up and runs into the ropes. She ducks an elbow, rebounds and takes Sue down with a kick. Sue blocks a gutwrench suplex. She executes anoth - Huffman reverses the move into a vertical suplex! She runs into the ropes, then applies an armbar. Sue quickly breaks the hold. Karen heads to the ropes, then rocks Sue with an elbow to the face. "She skips around the ring, waving at the crowd as Sue gets back on her feet. Sue sidesteps a drop-kick to the knee. She catches Huffman in a sleeper! Karen stumbles around the ring. An idea strikes her; she struggles toward a corner and falls backward, hammering Sue against the turnbuckles and breaking the hold! Huffman turns around - and ducks a leaping forearm! She hits Sue with an elbowsmash. Sue gets back on her feet and ducks a thrust kick to the head. She scoops Huffman up, then drops her into an over-the-shoulder stomachbreaker and applies an armlock leglock. It's unusual to see Sweet Sue this passive; usually she's very agressive in a match. Sue lets go. Huffman gets on her feet, a bit wobbly. She kicks Sue in the head. "Karen whips her to a corner, then runs in shoulder-fi - Sue raises her knee up as a shield! She sends Huffman to the ropes. Huffman comes back with a clothesline. Sue blocks a gutwrench suplex, then nail Karen with a rolling kick. She runs into the ropes, jumps a rising Huffman, rebounds, and gets decked by a thrust kick to the head! Sue raises up on her knees. She blocks an armbar and catches Huffman in a belly-to-belly! Sue raises her friend up and puts her in a chinlock. Liz looks on interestedly. I wonder if either Sue or Karen thought about the possibility of injuries during this match. Cheetah and the Assassin would go after any taped area like sharks to blood. Huffman elbows her way free! Sue hits her with a double-axhandle chop. Karen's eyes are tearing up; she rakes Sue's face to block a double reverse chinlock. "Both women get back on their feet. Sue ducks a short clothesline; Huffman ducks neck-scissors. She quickly executes a Doctor bomb. Sue rather slowly gets back on her feet. Huffman reverses a whip to the ropes; Sue comes back with a backdrop. Huffman gets back up; Sue sends her to a corner. She walks in, then whips Huffman to the ropes. Huffman comes back with a lariat. She skips around the ring again as Sue gets up. Huffman sees her and runs into the ropes. Sue backdrops her. She waits for Huffman to get up, then puts her in a standing wristlock. Huffman begins to work on breaking the hold. Liz looks on. Huffman frees herself; Sue runs into the ropes. "She punches Karen in the stomach, then pummels her with an over-the-shoulder stomachbreaker. Huffman sits up, starting to cry. Sue drops down and applies a sleeper! Huffman can't use her height advantage on the mat! She manages to hook the bottom rope with her foot, though! Sue pulls her back to her feet and sends her into the ropes - and follows her in, clotheslining her to the floor! Sue goes through the ropes, then hammers Huffman with a flying kneedrop! She pulls Sue up, then sends her head-first into a ringpost. Sue pulls Huffman into the guardrail. Huffman rests on it. Sue blocks a back heel kick; Huffman blocks a kick to the thigh. She throws Sue back into the ring and follows her in. "Huffman nails Sue with a spin kick. Sue picks herself up and sends her to the ropes. Huffman comes back with an elbow. Sue staggers back, then ducks another spin kick. She whips Karen to the ropes; Huffman comes back with a drop-kick. She runs into the ropes, jumps a rising Sue, rebounds - Sue takes her down with neck-scissors! She follows up with a jack-knife. Huffman slowly gets back up. Sue applies a slee - Karen counters with a jawbreaker! She runs into the ropes, then hits Sue with an elbow to the face. She follows up with an elbowsmash, then takes her down with a lariat. Sue slowly gets back up. She kicks Huffman in the head. "Huffman responds with a spin kick! She hammers Sue with an inverted powerbomb! Sue's motionless - no, she stirs! Sue slowly gets back on her feet. Huffman sends her to a corner and run in shoulder-first - Sue blocks with her knee. She throws Huffman out of the ring and joins her on the floor. She slams Karen's face on the guardrail, then catches her in a belly-to-belly. Huffman blocks a sleeper and executes a snap mare. "Sue gets back up and hits her with a double-axhandle chop. She knocks Huffman over the ringsteps. Huffman gets back up and shoves Sue into the guardrail. She throws Sue back into the ring, then reenters herself. She sends Sue to the ropes, then stops her with a slap and runs into the ropes. She takes Sue down with a kick t the head. Sue gets back on her feet; Huffman executes a snap mare. Sue gets back up and runs into the ropes. Huffman decks her with a kick to the head. Sue gets on one knee; Huffman knocks her off-balance with a drop-kick to the other knee! She applies an armbar. Sue quickly breaks the hold. Huffman hits her with a double-axhandle chop. "She follows up with an elbowsmash, then sends Sue to the ropes. Sue ducks a spin kick, then turns around and hits Karen with a double-axhandle chop. Huffman blocks a kick to the leg and throws Sue out of the ring. She joins her on the floor. Sue ducks a spin kick and shoves Huffman into the ringsteps. Karen stands up; Sue takes her down with a short clothesline. She pulls Huffman up - Huffman counters a back suplex with a cross-body block! She pulls Sue up, then slams her face on the ringsteps. She throws Sue back into the ring and follows her in. "She hits Sue with a double-axhandle chop, then decks her with a spin kick. She heads to the turnbuckles nearest Sue and climbs up them. Sue gets on her feet; Huffman nails her with a flying bulldog! Sue blinks several times, then rolls over and gets on her knees. She blocks an armbar and stands up. She applies a sle - Huffman counters again with a jawbreaker! She picks Sue up and hammers her with a Samoan Drop, then puts her in a kneelock. White extends the mic - and Sue gives up!" RA: "Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the match: in 15 minutes, 36 seconds, Karen 'Bugs' Huffman!" <Strohlheim enters the ring. She and Huffman help Sue up. Sue shrugs away from both of them, then heads back to the back with Huffman and Strohlheim following. The Battle Kats charge out of the back, knocking the TCA down like bowling pins.> Birmingham: "Tygra and Panthra take out the TCA. They turn back and begin pounding on Sue and Karen! Liz pulls Tygra off of Sue; Tygra nails her with an elbowsmash and executes a fisherman suplex, dropping her onto Sue! Liz gets up and tackles Tygra. Sue gets up and jumps on Panthra; Huffman lies on the floor, crying! The crowd stirs - two women in camoflagued-singlets come out of the stands with baseball bats! The one with the red mask uses the butt end on Strohlheim's head, then repeats with Tygra! The other one with a black mask nearly decapitates Panthra! She jabs Sue in the midsection with it. Security is on the ball - so are the two mysterious women; they leave as quickly as they came! Tygra snags Red's bat, then hits Sue with it. Security takes the bat away from her, then escorts her, Liz, and a sobbing Karen back to the back. Stretchers come out for Panthra and Sue. Fans, we'll bring you more information when we have it. Up next is our main event after this commercial time-out. Stay tuned!" --------------------COMMERCIAL BREAK-------------------- Birmingham: "We're back! Up next is a match between the Mistress of Death and 'the Intimidator' Amber Axeln. This match was signed before Amber won the Can-Am title, so only the Television title will be up for grabs tonight. I expect to see a very agressive Mistress, as she has nothing to gain and everything to lose. Let's go to the ring." RA: "Ladies and gentlemen, tonight's main event is for the Television championship! Introducing the challenger: accompanied to the ring by her tag team partner Valkyrie, from Richmond, Virginia, weighing in at 122 pounds, WCA Can-Am champion 'the Intimidator' Amber Axeln!" <Axeln and Valkyrie walk down the aisles, slapping the fans hands. They enter the ring.> "Her opponent and current Television champion: accompanied to the ring by her tag team partner Semiramis, from Buffalo, New York, weighing 122 pounds, the Mistress of Death!" <Darkness, Inc. walk down the aisle. Both women have the Tag belts slung over their shoulders. Semiramis carries the Mistress' Singapore cane; she occasionally slaps the fans hands. The Mistress wears her Television belt, also occasionally slapping hands. They enter the ring.> "The Mistress hands Semiramis the tag belt, then displays the Television title before handing it to referee Esther Siler. Siler checks her and Axeln, then Valkyrie and Semiramis leaves the ring. The bell rings, and both women lock up! The Mistress takes Axeln down with a spinning leg lariat. She waits, then executes a high cross body; one, two, shoulder up. The Mistress heads for the top rope - Axeln gets up and throws her to the mat! She runs into the ropes, then backdrops the Mistress. The Mistress quickly gets up and ducks a kick to the head. She kicks Amber in the leg, then executes a single-leg takedown. Axeln picks herself and blocks a back heel kick. She punches the Mistress, then sends her to the ropes. She stops the Mistress with a slap; the Mistress reverses a whip to the ropes. "The Mistress misses with a kick, but spins around and decks the Intimidator with a thrust kick to the head. She applies a spinning toe hold. Valkyrie encourages Amber on. Many of the fans are rallying behind the Intimidator! The Mistress' fans respond, cheering *her* on. I'd say this crowd is evenly split! The Mistress lets Axeln free. Amber slowly gets on her feet. She hits the Mistress with a forearm to the back, then sends her to the ropes. She reverses a small package; the Mistress re-reverses in two. Axeln kicks out at the last second! Both women stand up. Axeln counters a Japanese armdrag takedown with an elbowsmash, then kicks her in the head. She throws the Mistress out of the ring! She rests while Siler begins the count. One, two, three, four, five, six, the Mistress enters the ring. "She dodges a kneelift. Axeln gets back on her feet and hammers the Mistress in a side suplex. The Mistress gets on her knees; Axeln kicks her in the head! The Mistress falls back on the mat, then blocks headscissors. She gets up, then kicks Axeln in the leg. Axeln gets up; the Mistress sends her to a corner. Axeln walks back out and whips the Mistress to the ropes. The Mistress executes a small package; one, two, thr-kickout! So close! Both women get up; the Mistress kicks Axeln in the leg. Axeln counters a cobra clutch with an armdrag takedown, then puts the Mistress in a front facelock. The arena erupts again as fans choose sides. This seems to be a very even match so far. Axeln lets go. "She beats the Mistress to her feet, then takes her down with a kneelift. The Mistress gets back up; Axeln runs into the ropes. She misses with a clothesline, rebounds, and connects with a clothesline. The Mistress stumbles back, then reverses a whip to the ropes. She misses with a shouilderblock, misses with a kick, jumps a shoulderblock, misses with a clothesline, nails Axeln with a spinning leg lariat. She applies her own front facelock. Axeln fights to break free. She backdrops the Mistress! The Mistress gets back up; Axeln blocks her legsweep faceslam. She executes an enzuigiri, then pulls the Mistress into a corner and gets up on the second turnbuckle. The Mistress blocks the Doombomb! "Axeln hops down and throws the Mistress out of the ring! One, two, three, four, five, six, the Mistress slides back in. Axeln flashes the V-sign to her fans, then resets for the Doombomb and executes it. She drops down and covers; one, two, three! Amber has unified the Can-Am and Television titles!" RA: "Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the match and NEW Television champion: in 5 minutes, 44 seconds, 'the Intimidator' Amber Axeln!" <Axeln receives the Television title, then raises it up in one hand as Valkyrie raises the other. The Mistress rolls out of the ring and picks up her Tag belt, then she and Semiramis raise them up for an equal crowd reaction.> Birmingham: "The Mistress of Death may have lost the Television title, but she still has one-half of the Tag Team championships. Axeln now holds two of the three singles belts; this may put a whole new spin on her match with the World champion 'R' at No Sanctuary. Fans, we are - what? Fans, I'm being told that Tygra has either left or been expelled from the WCA, and Panthra is no longer able to compete! Apparently WCA officials have decided that the two masked wrestlers with the bats will assume all of their matches. There will be more on this development, I'm sure. Anyway, we are almost out of time. Here's what's coming up this Saturday on Rampage: Joanne Watson takes on Janet Strong, Freedom will *not* take on Tygra, but will face either Black or Red instead, the Scandalous Ones face 'Psycho' Vicki V and Christy Cooper in an intra-stable match, and lots more! I'm John Birmingham; have a great evening!" <Closing credits appear.>
