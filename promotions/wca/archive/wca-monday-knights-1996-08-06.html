WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION MONDAY KNIGHTS Live from New York, NY 8/5/96 -----Main Event----- Lovely Liz Strohlheim v. Sweet Sue v. Karen "Bugs" Huffman Battle Kats v. Thunder and Lightning Darkness, Inc. v. Delicious Dolls Amanda v. Wildcat <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA wrestling action> <Wide angle view of the inside of the arena shows a capacity crowd. The ring announcer, timekeepers, and other officials are walking down the aisle to get ready for the show.> ------------------------- Amanda v. Wildcat ------------------------- Both women lock up. Wildcat breaks off and runs into the ropes. Amanda takes her down with an elbow on the rebound and goes for the pin; one, two, kickout. Wildcat blocks a choke against the ropes; Amanda settles for a springboard rana. She pulls Wildcat up and sends her to the ropes. Wildcat sidesteps Amanda's Whiplash, then nails her with an elbowdrop, going on the offensive. She soon attempts an Asai moonsault, but Amanda moves out of the way. She nails Wildcat with a Frankensteiner and covers her; one, kickout. Amanda sends Wildcat into the ropes. She misses with an elbow, then misses with a shoulderblock. Wildcat rebounds again and misses with a clothesline, then nails Amanda with a kick, regaining her momentum. She shortly sends Amanda into the ropes. Amanda reboudns and takes her down with spinning headscissors, evening the match up. Amanda gets up and runs into the ropes. Wildcat kicks her; Amanda comes back with a choke against the ropes. The ref reaches four; Amanda lets go. Wildcat nails her with neck-scissors, going back on the offensive. She shortly attempts a cobra clutch, but Amanda counters with an armdrag takedown, then nails her with a Frankensteiner from the top rope, turning the match around. Wildcat quickly retakes control with a knee to the back. Amanda soon ends her offensive with a flying bulldog, turning the match back around. Wildcat again quickly goes back on the offensive with neck-scissors. She soon goes for a swinging neckbreaker, but Amanda counters with a side suplex, taking control of the match. She soon goes for a front facelock, but Wildcat backdrops her instead. She follows up with an atomic drop, going back on the offensive. After a minute Amanda ends it with a Frankensteiner. She covers Wildcat; one, two, kickout. Amanda pulls Wildcat up and sends her into the ropes; Wildcat rebounds and backdrops her, then climbs the turnbuckles. She executes a flying sunset flip, pinning Amanda. (10:48) ------------------------- Darkness, Inc. (The Mistress of Death and Semiramis) (w/ Lu Chin) v. Delicious Dolls (Donna Star and Double D - Debbie Diamond) ------------------------- The Mistress and Diamond start. Both women lock up, then the Mistress applies a front facelock, taking control. She shortly goes for a kick to the head, but Diamond ducks it and returns the same. She tags out to Star; the Mistress puts Star in a cobra clutch, resuming her attack. Star quickly turns the match around with a legsweep. She shortly goes for an armlock hammerlock, but the Mistress blocks it, then tags out to Semiramis. They whip Star into the ropes, then nail her with a double elbowsmash. The ref keeps Diamond in hte ring as the Mistress nails Star with a thrust kick to the head. Semiramis attmpts a crucifix, but Star counters with a Samoan Drop, regaining her momentum. Semiramis soon reverses a whip into a corner. Star charges back out and runs into an elbow as Semiramis evens the match up. Both women stand toe-to-toe with each other. Semiramis quickly takes control of the match with a crucifix; one, two, thr-kickout. She soon goes for an earringer, but Star backdrops her. She throws Semiramis through the ropes, then joins her on the floor. Semiramis attempts a facebite, but Star counters with a kneelift. Semiramis bodyslams Star on the guardrail, then reenters the ring. Star follows her back in; Semiramis nails her with a flying clothesline, then tags out to the Mistress. They nail Star with a double kneeling headbutt, then the Mistress follows up with a spinning leg lariat, continuing their offensive. Star shortly ends it with a choke against the ropes. She tags out to Diamond; the Mistress greets her with a lariat and resumes her onslaught. She shortly tags out to Semiramis; Diamond applies a chokehold to turn the match around. Semiramis soon turns the match back around with a kneelift. She tags out to the Mistress; Star enters the ring and nails the Mistress with an enzuilariato. Diamond follows up with an elbowdrop from the second turnbuckle, going back on the offensive. The Mistress shortly turns the match back around with a swinging punch. Diamond quickly evens the match up with a short lariat and tags out to Star. They whip the Mistress into the ropes, then double backdrop her. Star follows up with a lariat. The Mistress blocks a figure four sleeper, then tags out to Semiramis. Semiramis takes control of the match with a DDT. Star shortly turns the match around with a full nelson. Semiramis just as quickly retakes control with a clothesline. Star shortly retakes control with a small package; one, two, kickout. Semiramis shortly counters a German suplex with an elbowsmash, then nails Star with a kneelift, going back on the offensive. After two minutes she throws Star out of the ring and tags out to the Mistress. Star reenters at the seven count and is met with a leaping forearm. Star shortly gets a forearm smash in and tags out to Diamond. Diamond whips the Mistress into a corner, then runs in shoulder-first. The Mistress moves out of the way, then legsweeps Diamond. She follows up with an elbowdrop from the second turnbuckle and covers Diamond, pinning her.
