WCA Rampage 10/5

6 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION RAMPAGE Live from Kelowna, British Columbia 10/5/96 -----Main Event----- Sweet Sue v. "The Flawless One" Sydney Sheppard Mistress of Death v. Tygra (Weapons-filled Cage Match) "Lethal" Lori Michaels / Bodacious Janet Muniz v. Wildcat / Sierra The Assassin v. Dawn The Scandalous Ones v. Adora Locklear / Kelly Locklear <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA wrestling action> <Wide angle view of the inside of the arena shows a capacity crowd. The ring announcer, timekeepers, and other officials are walking down the aisle to get ready for the show.> ---------- Adora Locklear / Kelly Locklear (w/ Wildcat and Sierra) v. The Scandalous Ones (Shelley Rage and Jenny Black) (w/ the Dream Master) ---------- Kelly and Rage start. Kelly goes for a headlock takedown, but Rage counters with a back suplex, gaining the advantage. She shortly climbs to the top turnbuckle, but Kelly nails her in the stomach. Rage falls onto the turnbuckle; Kelly climbs up and takes her down with a belly-to-belly superplex, going on the offensive. She shortly tags out to Adora; they whip Rage into the ropes and double-backdrop her, starting a series of double-teams. The ref shortly sends Kelly out of the ring as Rage catches Adora in a dragon suplex, turning the match around. She makes the tag to Black; they whip Adora into the ropes, then double-backdrop her. Black climbs the turnbuckles and hammers Adora with a flying somersault splash, starting her assault. Adora shortly ducks a flying spinning leg lariat and tags out to Kelly. Kelly puts Black on a turnbuckle and takes her down with a belly-to-back superplex, retaking control. Black shortly nails her with a spin kick, resuming her attack. She soon goes for a full nelson, but Kelly blocks it and takes her down with a short clothesline. She tags out to Adora, who keeps the pressure on with a belly-to-belly suplex. She soon nails Black with a spinning leg lariat. Black comes back with an earringer, going back on the attack. She soon attempts a tiger driver, but Adora counters with a backdrop. She bodyslams Black, turning the match back around. Rage shortly enters the ring and catches her in a powerbomb; Black follows up with a moonsault. Adora kicks out at the last second; Black tags out to Rage. Rage executes a springboard legdrop, continuing their offensive. Adora shortly turns the match around again with a sunset flip. She shortly tags out to Kelly. Rage catches her in a jack-knife powerbomb, continuing her assault. After two-and-a-half minutes she tags out to Black. Kelly sidesteps a backflip cradle and executes a crucifix, turning the match around. Rage shortly enters the ring and nails Kelly with a leaping clothesline. Black follows up with an enzuilariato, retaking control. Kelly shortly puts her back on the defensive with a roundhouse punch. Almost two minutes later she tags out to Adora. They whip Black into the ropes, then nail her with a double elbowsmash, starting a series of double teams. The ref forces Kelly out; Black runs intot he ropes, then takes Adora down with a spin kick. She tags out to Rage; Rage takes Adora down with a flying cross body press and pins her. (15:27) ---------- The Assassin (w/ Li'l Cheetah) v. Dawn ---------- Both women lock up, then the Assassin assumes control of the match with a kick to the head. Dawn quickly turns the match around with a faceslam. The Assassin soon retakes control with a rolling kick. She goes through every move in her arsenal, pounding on Dawn. Dawn is able to get a few blows in, but cannot seem to be able to slow the Assassin down. Almost 8 minutes into the match, Dawn dodges a forearm to the back and climbs the turnbuckles. The Assassin nails her in the stomach. Dawn falls onto the top turnbuckle; the Assassin climbs up and takes her down with a belly-to-belly suplex, then pins her. (7:43) ---------- "Lethal" Lori Michaels / Bodacious Janet Muniz v. Wildcat / Sierra (w/ Adora Locklear and Kelly Locklear) ---------- Michaels and Wildcat start. Both women lock up. Wildcat blocks a headbutt and takes Michaels down with a swinging neckbreaker, assuming control of the match. She soon tags out to Sierra. Muniz enters the ring and throws Wildcat out as Michaels bodyslams Sierra. She and Muniz double-stomp on her, then Michaels makes the tag to Muniz. Sierra nails Muniz with a doube-axhandle chop; Muniz responds with an elbow, taking control of the match. Sierra soon tags out to Wildcat. Wildcat executes a backflip cradle, taking control. She soon tags out to Sierra, then rocks Muniz with a thrust kick to the head. Sierra follows up with a spinning mule kick. Muniz shortly retakes control with an elbow to the face. She shortly goes for a flying headbutt, but Sierra rolls out of the way, retaking control of the match. Muniz shortly puts her back on the defensive with a slap. Sierra shortly catches her in a crucifix. Muniz kicks out in two; Sierra tags out to Wildcat. They whip Muniz into the ropes; Muniz comes back with a double clothesline. She bodyslams Wildcat, retaking control of the match. Wildcat shortly nails her with a kick to the head and tags out to Sierra. They whip Muniz into the ropes, then nail her with a double elbowsmash. Muniz throws Wildcat out of the ring, then rocks Sierra with a European uppercut, regaining her momentum. Sierra shortly turns the match around with a spinning leg lariat. After a minute Muniz makes the tag to Michaels. Wildcat enters the ring and shows Muniz the way out, then returns to her corner as Sierra puts Michaels in a full nelson, continuing her attack. She soon goes for an armlock leglock, but Michaels blocks it and picks her up. Michaels sets her on a turnbuckle, then climbs up and executes a belly-to-back superplex, turnign the match back around. Muniz shortly enters the ring; Wildcat enters and throws her back out, then leaves the ring. Muniz shortly enters the ring again. She and Michaels send Sierra into the ropes, then stop her with a double facerake. Wildcat enters the ring and throws Muniz out, then nails Michaels with a brain buster, ending her offensive. Sierra shortly tags out to Wildcat. Michaels meets her with a dropkick to the knee, turning the match back around. Muniz soon enters the ring; she and Michaels double-bodyslam Wildcat, starting a series of double teams. Sierra attempts to enter the ring, but the ref keeps her out. He turns around and forces Muniz out. Wildcat shortly ends Michaels assault by throwing her out of the ring. Michaels reenters at the seven count; Wildcat puts her in an abdominal stretch, starting a new offensive. Michaels shortly legsweeps her and tags out to Muniz; Muniz hammers Wildcat with a Doctor bomb, taking firm control. A minute later she caps her assault with the Latino Leg Split; Wildcat submits after 16 seconds. (15:05) *Weapons-filled Cage Match* (Pinfall-only) ---------- Mistress of Death (w/ Semiramis) v. Tygra (w/ Panthra and Lu Chin) ---------- Both women lock up, then the Mistress applies a front facelock, assuming control of the match. After two minutes Tygra whips her into the ropes. She nails the Mistress with a reverse neckbreaker, taking control of the match. The Mistress shortly begins to even up the match. Tygra soon rakes the Mistress' face across the cage. She goes for a handspring bodyblock, but the Mistress moves out of the way. She picks up a metal tray from the apron, then backdrops Tygra into the cage. She pounds on Tygra's back with the tray, then goes for the pin. Tygra kicks out in two. The Mistress attempts a kneeling headbutt, but Tygra rolls out of the way. She goes for a elbowdrop, but the Mistress rolls out of the way. She pulls a brick from the apron into the ring, then executes a piledriver onto the brick, turning the match around. Tygra very slowly attempts to build a comeback over the next several minutes, with the Mistress using alternately the brick, the tray, a fire extinguisher, a cheese grater, a 2-by-four, and a chair to further her assault. After the 8-minute mark, the Mistress attempts a piledriver, but Tygra backdrops her onto the chair. She follows up with an enzuigiri, turning the match back around. She soon applies a sleeper. The Mistress manages to escape the hold after 19 seconds, then nails Tygra with a spinning leg lariat, going back on the offensive. She shortly goes for a cobra clutch, but tygra counters with an armdrag takedown. Tygra picks up the chair and folds it up. She attempts to slug the Mistress with it, but the Mistress blocks the blow and grinds Tygra's face across the cage, busting her wide open. Tygra whips the Mistress into the ropes; the Mistress attempts a lariat, but Tygra ducks it. The Mistress rebounds again; Tygra stops her with a brick to the midsection. The Mistress comes back with a shoulderblock. She clotheslines Tygra, knocking her into the cage. The Mistress picks Tgyra up, then bodyslams her onto the grater. She sets up the chair, then grabs Tygra's head. The Mistress executes the Kiss of Death onto the chair, knocking Tygra out. The Mistress pins her. (14:51) ===== Birmingham: "A solid victory over Tygra caps a one-sided hardcore cage match, and solidly proves Darkness, Inc.'s superior ring ability over the Battle Kats. Tygra and Panthra have been in a slide ever since the Lords of the Ring; they'll have to find another way to come out of their slump. Up next is our main event. Speaking of slumps, Sweet Sue is coming out of one, and wants a win over her opponent tonight, the woman known as 'the Flawless One' Sydney Sheppard. Sheppard has been rather quiet as of late, and despite a strong showing against the Assassin earlier this week seems to be headed into a slump of her own. Let's go now to the ring." She executes a flying kneedro - Sue rolls out of the way! She sets Sheppard up and jack-knifes her. One, two, and a forceful kickout! Sheppard goes to the top turnbuckle again and executes a flying cross body press. Sue kicks out in two and gets up; Sheppard nails her with a thrust kick. She sends Sue itno the ropes; Sue comes back with a backdrop - and covers her; one, two, kickout. Sue accuses the ref of a sloww cou - Sheppard rolls her up; one, two, kickout. Sheppard picks Sue up; Sue won't be put on a turnbuckle! Sheppard drops her and runs into the ropes. Sue ducks a clothesline; Sheppard rebounds and knocks her down with a shoulderblock.
