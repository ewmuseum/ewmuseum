WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION THEATRE OF WAR 3/6/97 Live from the Lake Arena in Springfield, Missouri -----Main Event----- =Tag Team Titles Match= Magic Mountain Warriors v. Bounty Hunters Muniz's Musings Janet Strong v. "Dominatress" Kathy Lynn The Psycho Crushers v. Delicious Dolls Christy Cooper v. Helena "High Kick" Knightsbridge Femme Fatale v. Liberty The Scandalous Ones v. New Dawn -------------------- The Scandalous Ones (Shelly Rage and Jenny Black) (w/ The Dream Master and Dr. Insane) v. New Dawn ("The Intimidator" Amber Axeln and Valkyrie) -------------------- Rage and Valkyrie lock up. Valkyrie rocks Rage with a back heel kick, then attempts a sunset flip. Rage blocks it with a fist to the face. Valkyrie gets up and reverses a whip to a corner; Rage charges back out with a clothesline, taking control of the match. Valkyrie soon counters an abdominal stretch with a hiptoss. Rage begs off; Valkyrie tags Axeln in. Black enters the ring and boots Valkyrie out, then she returns to her corner as Axeln nails Rage with a chop. She chops Rage again; Rage ducks a kick to the head as Black enters the ring. They send Axeln to the ropes, then double-backdrop her. Black and Rage head to the apron; Axeln turns Rage's springboard lariat into a powerslam, turning the match around. Rage shortly blocks a roundhouse punch, then tags in Black. Valkyrie enters and throws Rage out, then nails Black with a spinning leg lariat. Axeln follows up with an enzuilariato, resuming her offensive. After 2 minutes Axeln puts Black into a front facelock. Black breaks the hold after 5 seconds and tags out to Rage, who ducks Axeln's enzuilariato. Axeln settles for a forearm to the back, then executes a German suplex; one, two, thr-kickout! Rage blocks an armlock leglock and scrambles to make the tag to Black. Valkyrie enters the ring and nails Black with a spinning leg lariat. Black gets back up and counters Axeln's cobra clutch with an armdrag takedown. Rage keeps Axeln down with a clothesline, then Black pummels her with a moonsault, taking charge. She soon whips Axeln to a corner and charges in; Axeln raises her leg up to block her, then runs to the far ropes. She spins Black around with an elbow to the face, then tags in Valkyrie. Axeln catches Black with an enzuilariato before leaving the ring. Valkyrie follows up with a flying sunset flip; Black kicks out in two. Both women get up; Black dodges a cartwheel kick. Valkyrie sits up, then reverses a whip to the ropes. Black rebounds and nails Valkyrie with a kick, retaking control. She shortly tags in Rage; Axeln gets cut off, distracting the ref from seeing Black and Rage send Valkyrie to the ropes and decking her with a double clothesline. Valkyrie picks herself up; the Scandalous Ones take her down with a double kneelift. Black leaves the ring as Rage heads to a neutral corner. Rage executes a flying forearm, taking control of the match. Valkyrie shortly tags in Axeln, then both women deck Rage with a double back heel kick. Black feints past the ref and throws Valkyrie out of the ring, then sends Axeln to the ropes. Black and Rage double-punch her in the stomach, then Black returns to her corner. Rage runs into a neutral corner and executes a flying cross body press; Axeln kicks out in two. Rage returns to the corner, then takes Axeln back down with a flying somersault bodyblock, going back on the offensive. She shortly whips Axeln to the ropes; Axeln misses with a shoulderblock, rebounds, and nails Rage with a lariat. She tags in Valkyrie, then both women nail Rage with a double back heel kick. Black gets cut off, enabling New Dawn to sneak another double back heel kick in. Axeln leaves the ring as Rage picks herself off the mat. Rage reverses a whip to the ropes; Valkyrie ducks a clothesline, ducks another, rebounds again and backdrops Rage, taking charge. Rage shortly blocks another sunset flip with her fist. Black enters the ring and keeps Valkyrie down with a clothesline. Valkyrie gets back up; Rage runs into a corner and rebounds with a flying cross body press. Valkyrie rolls it over, but only gets a two count. Valkyrie vents her frustration, then puts Rage into a Boston crab, retaking control. After 2 1/2 minutes Valkyrie clotheslines Rage to the floor, then slips through the ropes and hops down. Rage blocks a back heel kick, then whips Valkyrie into the ringsteps. Rage goes over the guardrail into the stands, then shoves a fan out of her seat and turns the chair around. Rage clears the chairs behind it out, then uses the chair and guardrail in a springboard lariat, knocking Valkyrie back into the ringsteps. A few fans in the crowd start chanting 'W-C-A, W-C-A;' the rest look at them skeptically. Rage heads to the apron as Valkyrie gets back on her feet. Rage takes Valkyrie down with a flying forearm. Valkyrie raises back onto her knees, then gets up before Rage. Valkyrie throws Rage into the ringsteps, then reenters the ring. Rage rolls back in as well; Valkyrie heads back to the apron, then pummels her with a slingshot somersault splash, retaking control of the match. Rage very shortly makes the tag to Rage, then both women send Valkyrie to the ropes. Valkyrie returns with a double clothesline. Rage rolls out of the ring as Valkyrie executes a sunset flip; Black kicks out in two. Valkyrie heads to the top rope, then takes Black back down with a flying bulldog. Valkyrie tags in Axeln, then both women whip Black to the ropes and deck her with a double back heel kick. Rage reenters the ring and throws Valkyrie out, then nails Axeln with a drop-kick to the knee. Rage leaves as Black executes a ropeflip hiptoss, going back on the attack. She soon sends Axeln to a corner, then charges in. Axeln raises her legs up to block her, then takes her down with a kneelift, turning things back around. After 2 minutes Black ducks a kick to the head. Axeln backs away from a drop-kick to the knee, then walks around Black and grabs the back of her singlet. She pulls Black into a corner, then gets on the second turnbuckle. Axeln executes the Doombomb and pins Black. (17:53) -------------------- Femme Fatale (w/ Snakebite) v. Liberty -------------------- Both women circle each other, then lock up. Liberty breaks off and heads to a corner, then executes flying headscissors, going on the offensive. Fatale soon ducks an enzuigiri to the face, then takes Liberty down with a spinning leg lariat, taking charge. Liberty shortly blocks a facerake, then sends Fatale to the ropes. Fatale misses with a clothesline, misses with a shoulderblock, jumps a shoulderblock, then executes a high cross body; Liberty gets her shoulder up in two. Both women get up; Fatale shoves Liberty to the ropes, then grabs her head and runs her face along the top cable. Liberty blocks an inside cradle; Fatale gets back up in time to duck a short clothesline. Liberty blocks a fireman's carry and puts Fatale into an abdominal stretch, retaking control of the match. After 2 minutes Fatale reverses an Irish whip, then stops Liberty with a facerake, turning the match back around. After 3 1/2 minutes Liberty blocks another fireman's carry, then puts Fatale into a bearhug, turning the match around again. Fatale soon blocks an armdrag takedown with a lariat. She follows up with a European uppercut, going back on the offensive. After ninety seconds Liberty blocks a ropeburn. Fatale counters an abdominal stretch with a hiptoss. Liberty gets up and takes her down with a rolling kick, going back on the attack. After a minute Fatale ducks a flying clothesline, then heads to the same corner. She hammers Liberty with a flying karate chop, retaking control. Liberty soon blocks standing short-arm scissors, then picks herself up and throws Fatale out of the ring. Liberty goes out after her; Snakebite distracts Liberty as Fatale gets off the concrete. Liberty turns around and ducks a spinning leg lariat. She reenters the ring, followed by Fatale. Liberty runs to the far ropes; Fatale decks her with an elbow, then drops and pins her. Knightsbridge reaches the ropes after 5 seconds, then blocks a snap mare. Cooper attempts to counter an abdominal stretch with a hiptoss, but Knightsbridge re-reverses with a backslide again, pinning her. (3:51) ===== Sharpe: "Janet Strong takes out 'Dominatress' Kathy Lynn, who was recently dismissed from the group she helped form, the Henchmen of Hades. This has been a very tough week for the Henchmen and anyone associated with them. The future certainly looks bleak for them, but I have no doubts that the Henchmen of Hades will rise from the ashes like a phoenix. Well, fans, tonight marks the first time Bodacious Janet Muniz's show Muniz's Musings will be seen in a month-and-a-half. Tonight her guest is the Mistress of Death." <Dissolve to tape; the words "Muniz's Musings" appears in small white sans-serif letters at the bottom left.> The Bodacious Janet Muniz is shown in her usual attire and is sitting at the newspaper's conference room desk. She says, "Hello, and welcome to yet another return edition of the `Muniz's Musings' show! You know, this show is very important to me, perhaps more important to me than ever, because I had to literally fight to get it back. When you earn something like that, it means so much more. And across the table from me today I have someone whom knows what it means to fight for what you get. I have had a few run-ins with over time myself outside the ring but not many inside it. However, as I watched her recently I noticed some interesting things about her that made me think and that is why I asked her to come onto my first show. Mistress of Death, welcome to yet another premiere edition of `Muniz's Musings!' Mistress of Death: Janet, it is an honor to be on your show. The fans find this show both interesting and informative. "First of all, Mistress of Death is an interesting moniker. Why do you call yourself that and what is your real name?" Mistress of Death: When I first broke into the business, the bookers thought that I needed some sort of a gimmick. I chose this name because I thought that I could make it work as a manager. When I started wrestling, I just kept the name. As to my real name, I guess I might as well tell everyone. My real name is Krista Stevens. "There are a lot of new wrestlers in the WCA since you began. In fact, I think you've been around here almost as long as I have in one form or another. I recall that you first started out as a manager of some men's team before the whole division got kicked out. Would you tell the fans watching us how you got into the WCA and also tell us about your involvement in the early days of the fed, how you gradually became a wrestler and some of your accomplishments, which I understand are numerous." Mistress of Death: I was contacted about my wrestlers joining the fed and theydid wrestle at the very first show. At the time, I was training to wrestle, but had some setbacks. I didn't start actively wrestling until May. I won the first Television title, surviving the submission gauntlet. I also won that belt again, along with winning the Can-Am title twice. Semiramis held the tag titles for three months, winning the tag team of the month honors each month. "I understand that you were involved in The Feud of the Year and were rewarded for that. Could you please describe the feelings you had about that feud and what you feel resulted from that battle?" Mistress of Death: I still am resentful towards the Posse because I think that the feud took its toll on my friendship with Semiramis. We were involved in something that caused her to blame me for losing the belts. There were some great matches during the feud, but nothing good ever came out of it. I still am having matches with them that have turned out to be vicious. "Looking back on your career, what do you see have been the most shining moments and the most depressing moments?" Mistress of Death: The best moment I have had so far was regaining the Can-Am title, after I had promised everyone that I would. The worst moment was when Semiramis took our feud to a new level by attacking my mother. "I understand you have had some words with Big Cheater and that TCA group as well as some other stables and wrestlers. Could you please describe how you got into a battle of words with some of these people?" Mistress of Death: I ended up in a battle of words with Cheetah after I broke my wrist and was not allowed to compete against her. She said I was a coward that hid from her. Yet, I have seen her use even worse excuses to avoid people. I have never ducked a match that I was physically able to wrestle in. The TCA has been upset with me ever since it was revealed that I was wrestling pregnant. My daughter is perfectly healthy, yet they insist that I was abusing her. "What are your feeling about the Magic Mountain Warriors holding the tag team titles, the same titles you held for so long at one point? Incidentally, how long did you hold the belts and do you plan on getting them back with a partner?" Mistress of Death: The Magic Mountain Warriors have had some good matches and some bad matches. They could make good champions, but they will face some tough competition. Semiramis and I held those titles for three months, so I know what the tag team competition is like. I would like to eventually get them back and I think that Freedom is the perfect choice of a partner that is skilled enough to win them. "Who do you see as your toughest opponent ever in the WCA?" Mistress of Death: Without a doubt, it is Amber Axeln. We have had a few matches, but nothing has ever been truly settled as to which of us is better. "What groups and wrestlers in the WCA do you most admire and which do you have no respect for?" Mistress of Death: I admire wrestlers that have wrestled for the fans. Freedom and Thunder and Lightning come to mind. I also admire wrestlers that work hard, such as yourself and Amber Axeln. I do not respect Panthra's Army, because they do not know where to draw the line. "What do you like to do outside of the wrestling ring?" Mistress of Death: I like spending some quality time with my family. I don't nearly get to spend as much time with them as I would like. I also play guitar a little bit and I like to cook. "Now I'd like to ask you some questions that have been on my mind for many days now. First of all, I noticed you at my match against Big Cheater. I was wondering why you were looking on so intently." Mistress of Death: That match was one of the biggest that has happened here in the WCA so far. I wanted to get a first hand look at this match because I wanted to make sure that the match was allowed to finish to its proper conclusion. "Now tell me the story about your daughter. As you know, my daughter Laura and I have a new lease on life. A brand new relationship. I imagine you feel just as strong about your daughter as I do. But I understand that before she was born, you continued wrestling. Could you give us the whole story behind that? Wasn't that really dangerous to do?" Mistress of Death: I was being a little selfish at the time. I wanted to be able to give her the things that I never had as a child. I wanted to be the perfect mother. I thought that I would need all the money that I could do to do that. Therefore, I continued wrestling. I did see a doctor a lot, in order to make sure that everything was okay. It wasn't the smartest thing that I have ever done, but I can't change the past. "Looking back at it now, what are your feelings about wrestling while pregnant? If one of the lady wrestlers confided in you that she was pregnant, would you advise her to do the same thing you did or take a sabbatical" Mistress of Death: Looking back on it, I realise that I made a mistake that I would not repeat. I would explain to them what I did and advise them not to do the same thing that I did. I took a big risk and luckily nothing happened. They might not be as lucky. "Tell me what your daughter and family means to you." Mistress of Death: My family means everything to me. My husband, my daughter, and my mother are the most important things in my life right now. If I thought that it would be best for any of them if I stopped wrestling, I would quit without a second thought. "You know, I was shocked and flabbergasted at what that monster Semiramis did to your mom. The thing that amazes me is that she was once your tag team partner. Describe to us your past relationship with her in the WCA and before, how you became one of the greatest tag teams ever and why you and her parted ways." Mistress of Death: We became one of the greatest teams because we both are different. She is one of the strongest wrestlers here. I am a better technical wrestler. We combined those two strengths to be a dominant force. "Why do you think Semiramis went to the extreme measure of attacking your mother." Mistress of Death: Semiramis was resentful of the fact that I was getting the credit for the team. I also had the success individually that she didn't have. This is her way of dragging me into a series of matches that she hopes will end my career and put her in my place. "Do you think this war you are having with Semiramis will ever end? I see she ha linked up with The Bounty Hunters. What do you think she has in mind with them? And if your feud ever does end, do you think there is a remote chance at reuniting with her?" Mistress of Death: They are her way of getting at me even more. I know them and they will never end this feud until one side or the other is completely defeated in their eyes. Their end of the feud is for one side to no longer be competing here. Semiramis and I will never team again. "I see you have been tagging with Freedom recently. What do you think of her and what do you think of the team you and her make? Do you see yourselves as future champions?" Mistress of Death: She is a great competitor and a true friend. I think that we make a great team that could possibly be the champions of the future here. "Speaking of futures, what do you see in the future of the WCA and who do you think have bright futures in this fed?" Mistress of Death: I see a lot of changes in the future of the WCA. Everyone seems to be heading in new directions. It is hard to tell who has a bright future here. "What role, if any, do you feel male managers play in this fed? You have a unique perspective on this since you were a manager at one time. Do you think they play a role or are they all like Tall Troll -- leeches who just suck the life's blood out of you and would be nothing if it weren't for your hard work and accomplishments that they have little to do with?" Mistress of Death: I also had a male manager here as well. I think that they can be good behind the scenes, but I don't think they have any business at ringside. "What do you think of the leadership in the WCA and what things do you feel could be changed to make this fed even better than it is?" Mistress of Death: I think the only changes I would make would be for some sort of punishment system for attacks. The Bounty Hunters can attack people with baseball bats and not be punished. Semiramis can beat my mother up and nothing happens. You were attacked several times and most of the times nothing was ever done. "One more thing. What do you think of Click II? Do you think you would fit into a group like this?" Mistress of Death: I think that this form of the Click can do a lot of things to make up for what the last Click did. I know that you have changed, as have your other members. I think that I could fit into the group, but I would have to think about joining if you asked me to. "Thank you, Mistress of Death, for appearing with me tonight. Before I sign off, let me say hello to my daughter, Laura! Are you in bed yet, girl? Well, time to hit the sackeroo! I'll be home in a few minutes to tuck you in. And a special thanks to Carla and mom. Hey, without you guys, I'd be nowhere. And lastly, let me just say this -- Long Live the Click!!"
