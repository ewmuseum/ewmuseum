WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION (http.// kessel.simplenet.com/WCA/ ) RAMPAGE 6/14/97 >From Sydney, Australia -----Main Event----- Femme Fatale v. Widow Maker (World Title Match) "Mistress of Death" Krista Stevens / Shannon Stevens v. Innocent Blood Interview with the TCA Freedom v. Jenny Black (Best of 9 Series Match 2) Hired Help v. Geisya Girls Rebecca Grace v. Dangerous Renee Lake (Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.) (Fireworks explode as the camera shows the crimson WCA logo on the white ring canvas. The camera descends rapidly, then levels off facing the aisle. The camera swerves left, panning out over the capacity crowd and circling the ring, ending its focus on the iridescent three-dimensional WCA letters bracketing the entrance to the backstage area. The referee for the first match heads to the ring.) ---------- Rebecca Grace v. Dangerous Renee Lake Referee: Mark White ---------- Both women lock up, then Lake puts Grace into an abdominal stretch. She lets go in 18 seconds, then Grace quickly moves away from a forearm to the back, running to the ropes. She rebounds and nails Lake with a kick, then she applies a standing wristlock, taking control of the match. Lake soon reverses a whip to a corner. Grace rebounds off the turnbuckles, then Lake puts her into a full nelson. Grace breaks the hold in 11 seconds; Lake sends her to the ropes. Grace returns with a kick, resuming her offense. Lake very shortly backdrops out of a front facelock. She turns around and sets Grace up, then she executes a piledriver, turning things around. Grace shortly counters a forearm to the back with an Irish whip to a corner, then she runs in and nails Lake with a spin kick, retaking control. She shortly whips Lake to the ropes; Lake rebounds and takes Grace down with a clothesline, renewing her gameplan. Lake soon reverses an Irish whip, then she ducks a clothesline; Grace rebounds again and hits her with a shoulderblock. She sends Lake to a corner; Lake bumps the ref out of the ring on her way in. She moves away from Grace's incoming spin kick, then she executes a Doctor bomb, but without a ref for the cover. Lake stands up; Grace gets up and punches Lake in the stomach, then she follows up with neck-scissors, turning things back around. She very shortly puts Lake in short-arm scissors. Lake breaks the hold after 5 seconds, then she gets up and takes Grace down with an earringer, retaking control. Grace shortly reverses a whip to a corner, then she takes Lake down with a spinning leg lariat, starting a new gameplan. Lake shortly blocks a pick-up with a lariat. Both women rest a second on the mat. Lake gets up first, then she rocks Grace with a swinging punch. Lake runs to the ropes, then Grace takes her down with a Frankensteiner and covers; one, two, kickout. Grace keeps Lake down with neck-scissors, taking charge. Lake soon blocks a pick-up, then she staggers Grace with a swinging punch, retaking control. She shortly whips Grace to the ropes; Grace rebounds and takes her down with a spinning leg lariat, retaking control. After ninety seconds Grace attempts a suplex, but Lake reverses the lift into a vertical suplex. She gets up and follows up with a gutwrench suplex. Grace slowly gets back to her feet. She reverses an Irish whip, then both women get decked in a dual clothesline. One .. two .. three .. four .. Lake begins to stand. One .. two .. three .. four .. five .. six .. seven ., Grace gets back to her feet. She grabs Lake by the head and pulls her to the ropes; Lake prevents a choke against them, then she puts Grace into a full nelson, slowing the match down. She shortly whips Grace to the ropes; Grace rebounds and kicks Lake, then she sends her to the side ropes and stops her with another kick. Grace follows up with an enzuilariato, taking control of the match again. After ninety seconds she executes the Amazing Grace, then she covers Lake and pins her. (11:14) ---------- Hired Help (Samantha Jones and Jane Gordon) (w/ Joanne Watson) v. Geisya Girls (Aya Ichijou and Kazumi Hayakawa) (w/ Dr. Insane, Asano Otani, and Valkyrie) Referee: Tina Nolke ---------- Jones and Ichijou lock up, then Ichijou executes a tiger suplex, but she only gets a two count. Ichijou executes an Irish whip, ducks a clothesline, then she drop-kicks Jones. Jones gets up and turns a rana into a powerbomb; one, kickout. Jones keeps Ichijou down with a bulldog, taking control of the match. Ichijou shortly reverses an Irish whip, then she blocks a faceslam. Hayakawa enters the ring, and both Girls send Jones to the ropes. They take her down with a double rolling kick. Jones picks herself up and ducks Hayakawa's thrust kick to the head. Ichijou takes her down with an enzuigiri, taking charge. Jones shortly counters another rana with a powerbomb; one, two, kickout. Jones gets up and tags in Gordon; Nolke cuts off Hayakawa as Jones rocks Ichijou with a spinning mule kick. Gordon follows up with a backflip cradle; one, two, thr-Hayakawa makes the save! She throws Jones out of the ring, then she whips Gordon to the ropes and takes her down with a rolling kick. Hayakawa returns to her corner. Ichijou takes a rising Gordon down with a leg lariat, retaking control. Gordon soon executes another backflip cradle; Ichijou kicks out at the last second. Gordon gets up and turns a rana into a powerbomb; one, two, shoulder up. Gordon rewards Ichijou with a kneedrop, turning the match around. Jones shortly enters the ring. Both women whip Ichijou to the ropes; Ichijou rebounds and ducks a double elbowsmash, hits the far ropes and decks the Help with a double clothesline. Jones rolls out of the ring as Ichijou keeps Gordon down with a rana; one, two, kickout. Ichijou goes to a corner as Gordon gets back up. Ichijou comes off the top ropes with a flying somersault bodyblock, retaking control of the match. Gordon shortly ducks a lariat, then she chokes Ichijou; one, two, three, four, she lets go and tags in Jones. Ichijou moves away from her drop-kick, then Gordon leaves the ring as Jones blocks a back heel kick. Jones takes Ichijou down with a spinning leg lariat. Ichijou sits up, then she stands. Jones sends her to a corner; Ichijou charges out and throws her out of the ring. She leaves the ring and whips Jones into a guardrail, then Jones turns around and shoves Ichijou into the ringsteps. Valkyrie comes up on her; Jones turns and kicks her in the stomach. Ichijou uses the distraction to take her down in a fallaway slam. Ichijou gets back up and breaks the ref's count. She slides back to the floor to find Jones waiting for her; Jones takes her down in a bulldog. Jones gets up and breaks the ref's count, then she rolls back to her feet and gets nailed from behind by Valkyrie. Watson comes over and clips Valkyrie in the knee. Jones turns back to Ichijou and gets kicked in the midsection. Ichijou prevents a pick-up and reenters the ring. Jones follows her in; Ichijou sends her to the far ropes. Jones rebounds and executes a leaping shoulderblock. Ichijou falls into the ropes, then she executes spinning headscissors into a cradle; Jones kicks out in two. Both women get back up, then Ichijou blocks a standing wristlock and tags in Hayakawa. Gordon enters the ring; Nolke cuts her off as Jones ducks Ichijou's thrust kick to the head. Ichijou leaves the ring as Jones sends Hayakawa to the ropes; Hayakawa returns with a cartwheel kick, taking charge. She soon tags in Ichijou, then both Girls send Jones to the ropes; Jones returns with a double clothesline. Hayakawa rolls out of the ring as Ichijou blocks a Japanese armdrag takedown with an elbowsmash. She goes to a corner as Jones staggers back, glassy-eyed. Jones sidesteps a flying drop-kick. Ichijou gets back up, then Jones puts her into a bearhug. Ichijou breaks the hold in 7 seconds, then she kicks Jones in the stomach, renewing her offense. She soon executes a tiger suplex, but only gets a one count. Ichijou accuses Nolke of bias; Jones uses the distraction to catch her in an inside cradle, but she only gets a one count. Jones follows up with a single-leg takedown, turning things back around. Ichijou shortly blocks a bulldog, then she takes Jones down with a spinning leg lariat, retaking control. She shortly applies a standing wristlock. Jones breaks the hold in 8 seconds, then she tags in Gordon. Ichijou counters her pick-up with a small package, but Jones reverses it in two; Ichijou kicks out in two. Jones yells at the ref over the "slow count" as she returns to her corner; Ichijou rolls away from Gordon's flying headbutt. She gets up first, then she ducks Gordon's jumping side kick. Ichijou executes a rana; one, two, thr-shoulder up! Ichijou gets up and waits on Gordon, then she executes a rolling kick, retaking control. Gordon very shortly takes her down with a jumping side kick. Ichijou sits up in surprise, then she gets up. Gordon whips her to a corner, then she meets Ichijou's charge out with a drop-kick. Ichijou gets up and executes a rana; one, two, three. (9:52) ---------- Freedom (w/ the Dream Master) v. Jenny Black (w/ Dr. Insane, "the Tax Collector" John Blair, Shelly Rage, and Valkyrie) Referee: Esther Siler ---------- Both women lock up. Black breaks off and backs up, then she executes a clothesline. Black runs into a corner, then she takes Freedom down with a flying spinning leg lariat. Black gets up and returns to the corner; Freedom rolls away from a flying headbutt. She goes to the opposite corner as Black gets back up, palm to her forehead. Black sidesteps a flying drop-kick. She shakes her head to clear it as Freedom picks herself up. Black whips her to a corner, then she drop-kicks her in the knee, taking control of the match. Freedom very shortly executes a springboard drop-kick, taking charge. Black shortly executes a kneelift, retaking control. After ninety seconds Freedom whips her to the ropes, then she executes a hiptoss, turning things back around. Black soon counters a back suplex with a headlock takedown. She nails a rising Freedom with a drop-kick to the knee, then she decks her with a palm blow. Freedom gets up and executes a sunset backslide; one, two, kickout. Freedom follows up with an ipponzei; one, two, shoulder up. Freedom executes a sunset flip; one, two, kickout. Black gets up and turns spinning headscissors into a Hotshot. She hammers Freedom with a somersault legdrop. Freedom blocks leg scissors and rolls out of the ring to the apron. She slowly gets back up, then she reenters and blocks another Hotshot with a lariat. Black sits up; Freedom puts her into a double reverse chinlock, slowing the match down. Black soon executes a clothesline, turning things around. Freedom soon sidesteps a kneelift. She throws Black out of the ring and rests while the count begins; one .. two .. three .. four .. five ., Black reenters the ring. Freedom meets her with a sunset flip; Black kicks out in two. Black gets up and counters an ipponzei with a lariat, going back on the offensive. After 2 1/2 minutes Freedom dodges a flying bulldog; Black lands hard. She slowly gets up, then Freedom takes her down with neck-scissors. Black gets up a bit faster, then Freedom hits her with a palm blow. Black ducks neck-scissors. Freedom gets up in time to move away from a flying bulldog. Black counters a ropeflip moonsault by pulling up her knees. One .. two .. three .. four ., Freedom picks herself up. Black slowly gets up; Freedom nails her with a drop-kick to the knee. She goes into a corner and leaps onto the second turnbuckle, then Black rolls a flying cross body over; Freedom kicks out at the last second. Black gets up and runs to the ropes, then she nails Freedom with a clothesline, taking control of the match. Freedom shortly takes her down with spinning headscissors into a cradle, but Black kicks out in two. Freedom follows up with a flying elbowdrop. She returns to the corner, then she pummels Black with the Showstopper; one, two, thr-kickout! Freedom stands and runs to the ropes, jumps over Black, rebounds, jumps over a rising Black, rebounds and keeps Black down with a lariat, taking charge. Black shortly dodges a backflip cradle. Freedom gets back up; Black decks her with a clothesline, then she drops and hooks a leg. Freedom kicks out in two, then she gets up and nails Black with a forearm to the back, retaking control of the match. Black shortly blocks a monkey flip, then Freedom dodges a cartwheel kick. Black moves away from a forearm to the back, taking Freedom down with a double-axhandle chop. She goes to a corner and climbs the turnbuckles. Black hammers Freedom with a flying headbutt and covers; one, two, kickout. Black complains about a slow count as she puts Freedom into a grapevine. Black lets go in 16 seconds and returns to the corner. She executes the Superfly Splash and pins Freedom. The series is now Black-2, Freedom-0. (13:12) ===== Mike Sharpe: "Jenny Black makes it 2-0 in her series against Freedom, the woman who stole her fiance away from her - in her mind, at least. Both women put a lot of effort into this match, and should be commended for such a display. Fans, we need to take a commercial break now; when we come back, we'll be talking with the TCA." -----Commercial Break----- (In the ring.) Shane Templeton: "Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome the Top Catfighters' Alliance!" (Sweet Sue, Lovely Liz Strohlheim, and Karen "Bugs" Huffman enter, walking to the ring to a mix of cheers and boos. They ignore the crowd and enter the ring.) Templeton: "Thank you for joining me, ladies. Let's begin with your recent acceptance of Kessler's new contract. You said earlier that this was victory even though you didn't get everything you wanted. Can you explain what you mean by that?" Sue grabs the mike and frowns at Templeton. She says, "You obviously know nothing about labor negotiations, nor have you been listening for the past two months. First of all, TCA won because we brought the whole union issue to the forefront of this fed. No one ever did that in any wrestling organization before. It took three women to do something like that. No, we didn't get a union in the end and, no, we didn't get our $1.5 million a minute. But that demand was just a negotiating ploy. Ultimately what we did get out of it was not only a raise, but a health insurance and life insurance policy for everybody! Wouldn't you call that victory, you dumb cluck?" Templeton: "I'd say it is a victory of sorts. But in the end, you are still at the mercy of Kessler and his cronies, are you not?" Sue: "I would say he is at our mercy, wouldn't you? He sat on his duff for almost two months, refusing to negotiate, until he saw what was happening to his fed. And he saw me, the WCA Heat Machine, stirring up the only interest this fed had going for it and he finally realized that TCA was the real boss of this outfit! So he finally answered my phone calls and agreed to a deal. No, we aren't at his mercy. He's at our mercy! And you just remember that, Kessler! Atlantic City's still just a plain flight away!" Templeton: "How disappointed are you about the WCA's apparent apathy toward the union vote? Could it be that when you left the WCA, most wrestlers thought the issue was a dead one?" Sue: "Nah, I'm not disappointed. The thing had run its course. They had already voted once anyway. You look at any election. You vote once, and if somebody doesn't win a majority, you get a runoff. The turnout for that runoff election is always awful. The bottom line is this. We brought the issue to the forefront, we made history, and while doing all that we got a raise and benefits! Do you think Kessler would have given the WCA that if TCA didn't face him down? Ha! No way! Now it's on to WCA business. We've got a lot of scabs to pound on here, such as Widow Maker, Lisa and DannyBoy. We've already won the first round against Lisa and DannyBoy since they lost to Karen and Liz respectively. But we're going to give those skanks another shot to get at me, which we've already outlined. Also, we've got the corporate hogs, the Henchmen, to deal with. If they can come up with a real woman to fight us, which I doubt. And let's not forget Femme Fatale, who I beat up before and will beat up again." Templeton: "Let's change the subject. The three of you, and especially you, Sue, have earned some enemies from the newer wrestlers, namely Luscious Lisa and Danni Moonstar. I heard your offer earlier, about the two six-woman tag matches. How does that differ from a handicap match? After all, you have the possibility of 3-on-1 situations in these type of tag matches?" Karen looks at Templeton with a quizzical look on her face. Liz puts her hands on her hips, looks at Templeton and says, "Do you know anything about wrestling at all?" Karen: "Yeah! How did you get your job in the first place?" Sue: "You're as dumb as a scab! Comparing a six-woman tag match to a handicap match because there is a *possibility* of a three on one! Sheesh, there's a possibility of three-on-one in singles matches, if you look at it, due to potential outside interference! This is three on three, moron. If you don't know the difference between three on three and three on one, then I suggest you go back to kindergarten." Templeton (irked): "I happen to know a lot about wrestling, thank you very much. [Sighs.] Changing the subject again, Sue, you've got a shotcoming up at the reigning World champion when you heal from your injury. Now, Janet Strong also has a shot coming up, and I know you won't face her if she's got the belt. What will you do in that situation, go for another belt?" "We'll have to see who holds the belts at that particular situation. If an honest-to-gosh woman holds a title, I'll go after her. If some hippo or man dressed like a woman or a man who wishes he was a woman is holding a title, we of course will skip him. You know, I always thought that Kessler got rid of the men in this fed a year or so ago. But they have sneaked right back in here, wearing wigs and 5 o'clock shadow. Half of these so-called `women' use the men's restroom, if you catch my drift." Templeton: "Ladies, thank you for joining me tonight. Ladies and gentlemen, the TCA!" (TCA throws their arms up in the air and in unison shout, "Down with the scabs!" they leave.) (Close-up of Sharpe.) Sharpe: "That was the TCA, talking about their victory to win more money from the workers. Ya know, they didn't have a victory. Both sides just realized that the issue had deadlocked, especially since the union vote fell through. They just came up with something to end the deadlock. Ah, no matter. The TCA are returning to full-time wrestling, and all wrestlers have higher compensation as a result of this issue. "Fans, up next is a non-title match between the Tag Team champions the Stevens sisters and Innocent Blood. The Stevens sisters are proving themselves to be a formidable force in the ring together, but Innocent Blood are not a team to be taken lightly. Let's go to the ring." ===== (Non-title Match) ---------- "Mistress of Death" Krista Stevens / Shannon Stevens (w/ Bodacious Janet Muniz, "the Intimidator" Amber Axeln, and Liberty) v. Innocent Blood (Blood and Red) Referee: Esther Siler ---------- Krista and Red lock up. Krista drives Red to the ropes; Red steps through them, then she executes a springboard drop-kick, taking control of the match. Krista shortly takes her down with an enzuigiri, taking charge. After ninety seconds Red reverses a whip to a corner, then she runs in shoulder-first; Krista raises her knees up to block her. She turns and gets up on the second turnbuckle, then Red rolls a flying cross body over; one, two, shoulder up. Red gets up and tags in Blood; Shannon enters the ring and throws Red out, then she returns to her corner as Blood blocks a suplex. Blood runs to a neutral corner and quickly scales the turnbuckles, then Krista ducks a flying forearm. Krista goes into the corner and climbs the turnbuckles, then she takes Blood down with a flying cross body press; Blood kicks out in two. Krista puts her into a spinning toe hold, slowing the match down. Blood shortly runs to the ropes, then she nails Krista with a rolling kick, turning things around again. She soon throws Krista out of the ring, then she joins her on the floor. Blood whips her into the guardrail, then she grabs Krista's head and slams her face on the ringsteps. Blood gets up on the apron, then she takes Krista down with a flying forearm. Krista rolls onto her stomach, then she raises back on her knees and stands back up. She throws Blood back into the ring, then she rolls in and puts her into a bow-and-arrow, slowing the match down. After ninety seconds Krista tags in Shannon, then both women send Blood to the ropes. They stop her with a double fist to the midsection; Red enters the ring and gets cut off as Krista picks Blood up. Blood prevents being set on a turnbuckle; Krista gives Blood to Shannon, who takes her down with a side suplex as Krista returns to her corner. Shannon gets up and executes a flying kneedrop, continuing the assault. After ninety seconds Shannon takes Blood down with a belly-to-back suplex. Panthra comes down to ringside and gets on the apron. All eyes turn toward her, including the ref's. Shannon turns back to Blood; Blood blocks a pick-up. She picks Shannon up as the Bounty Hunters rush into the ring from the sides behind Siler. They raise their bats up and bring them down on Blood, causing her drop Shannon. Red turns around and stops her namesake's charge with a jab from the end of her bat. She brings the bat down on Red as Black spikes her bat into Blood's back. Red lays her bat on the mat, then she snap-suplexes the Henchman Red onto it. Black lies her bat down, then she retrieves Red's bat and puts it next to her own. Both Hunters lift Blood up, then they execute the Spiked Piledriver onto the bats. They pick the bats up and leave the ring, then Panthra lets the ref go and leaves as Shannon picks Blood up and takes her down with a side suplex. After a minute Shannon caps her assault with the Shooting Star Press, pinning Blood. (9:25) ===== Sharpe: "The Tag Team champions 'Mistress of Death' Krista Stevens and Shannon Stevens defeat Innocent Blood in a non-title match, with more than a little help from the Bounty Hunters. This is the second time the Bounty Hunters have interfered against the opponent of a Click II member; one wonders if there isn't a hidden agenda on behalf of both groups. Given the history between Panthra and Krista Stevens, you might think that that would be unlikely, but the Hunters had a clear shot against her tonight and didn't' take it. You've never fought for a belt before, and some people might say that you don't deserve this chance. What is your response to that?" I am still undefeated in the WCA and those people who say that I don't deserve it should get in line because after tonight those people will regret their words they so speak! Templeton: "I know this was touched upon in your interview on Muniz's Musings, but what are your long-range goals in the WCA?" Templeton: "Thanks again for joining me!" WM: You better be grateful............I'll see you soon.....I'll see you soon (Widow Maker leaves as her theme starts to play over the P.A.) It must be very hard to prepare against a wrestler whom you know little about; my question is, how does a person do that - prepare for the unknown?" All I can say is that I didn't get this belt by fluke, I earned it, so like hell if some "Mysterious" Woman is going to take it away from me, because I worked damn hard for this belt (slaps it onto her shoulders) so Widow Maker or Window Washer or whatever you want to be called be prepared because my determination and the newly found support from my few "fans" is going to prevent you and that "so-called" Motorcycle you ride from running over my toes, you got that!!!!! Templeton: "I've noticed that some fans are starting to cheer for you. Has this changed your attitude in any way, or do you think the fans are cheering for you only because of whom you've been fighting?" FF: (looking through the curtains) Do you think I am stupid or something I know I have some following now, and as for my attitude listen honey my attitude is fine just the way it is, why do you ask? Do you need an attitude adjustment Shane? Templeton: "Uh no ma'am, no I don't." FF: Well good I didn't want to hit a helpless "boy" like yourself HEH HEH. Like I said before I don't care who I fight because when I won this belt just like the Can-Am title I said I would not hide behind my belt, and up to now I have not backed down from anybody big, small, or just plain freaky!!!!!! Templeton: "Thank you for joining me." FF: Good now get out of my face before I reconsider your facial structure!!! FF: (Walking through the curtains as Snakebite by Alice Cooper begins) Come on Snakebite let's go "explore" the unknown!!!!!! FF: See Ya! Wouldn't Wanna Be Ya!! (In the ring.) Ring Announcer: "Her opponent and current World champion: accompanied to the ring by Snakebite, from parts unknown, weighing in at 132 pounds, Femme Fatale!" (The fans mostly boo Fatale as she walks down the aisle, wearing the belt over her skintight black bodysuit, followed by Snakebite. Fatale enters the ring.) Sharpe's voice: "Femme takes off her belt and gives it to senior referee Mark White. He turns and calls for the bell - Fatale with a back-bridging back suplex; two, three! It's over before it began! This has to be some kind of record!" RA: "Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the match: in 3 seconds, Femme Fatale!" It also turned what could have been Widow Maker's opportunity to earn her biggest victory into her biggest disaster. The end result is that Femme Fatale will go on to face the woman who held the WCA World title the longest, Li'l Cheetah, this Saturday. "Fans, we're almost out of time. Coming up this Thursday, the Tag Team champions the Stevens sisters take on the Street Assassins, Big Norma Hudson will defend her belt against Helena 'High Kick' Knightsbridge, Hooked on Destruction will return to action against Innocent Blood, and more!
