Haley "Comet" Hanson (w/ Big Norma Hudson and Mr. Canuck) ---------- Both women lock up, then Hanson takes Bobbet down with neck-scissors, taking control of the match. Bobbet shortly puts her on the defensive with a elbow to the face. After 3 1/2 minutes Hanson applies a standing wristlock, turning the match back around. After 90 seconds Bobbet rolls away from a flying headbutt. She throws Hanson out of the ring and joins her on the floor. Hanson slams Bobbet's face on the guardrail. Bobbet ducks a short lariat; Hanson settles for neck-scissors. Bobbet gets back up and staggers Hanson with a vicious kick to the midsection. She reenters the ring, followed by Hanson. Bobbet hammers her with a side suplex, going back on the offensive. After 2 minutes Hanson turns things around again with a spinning leg lariat. After 2 more minutes Bobbet throws her out of the ring. Hanson reenters at the six count; Bobbet grabs her by the head and runs her face along the top rope. She stops and chokes Hanson against the ropes. Hanson breaks the hold and whips Bobbet to the far ropes. Both women nail each other in a double clothesline. Bobbet gets up first and puts Hanson in the Abdominal Stretch. Hanson hiptosses her way out; Bobbet rewards her with a bulldog, retaking control. Hanson soon blocks a ropeburn. Bobbet blocks a standing wristlock; Hanson shrugs off a bulldog. Bobbet continues her run into the ropes. She rebounds and nails Hanson with a clohtesline, taking charge. After 3 minutes Hanson turns the match back around with a spinning mule kick. Bobbet soon ducks a savate kick and chokes Hanson. She lets go at the five count. The ref warns her; she shrugs him off and stretches her arms into the air, then puts Hanson in the Abdominal Stretch. Hanson breaks the hold after 21 seconds. Bobbet whips her into the ropes, then ducks an oncoming clothesline. Hanson rebounds and ducks an enzuigiri to the face, rebounds and takes Bobbet down with a moonsault bodyblock. Bobbet kicks out in two; Hanson keeps her on the mat with a double underhook backbreaker, going back on the attack. After 3 minutes she takes Bobbet down with a short lariat and covers her. Bobbet kicks out in two. The crowd starts calling for the Crashing Comet; Hanson listens to the chant, then climbs the turnbuckles and executes the Crashing Comet, pinning Bobbet.
