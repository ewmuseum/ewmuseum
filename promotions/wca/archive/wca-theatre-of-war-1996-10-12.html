WCA Theatre of War 10/10

12 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION THEATRE OF WAR Live from Edmonton, Alberta 10/10/96 -----Main Event----- Semiramis v. Adora Locklear "Psycho" Vicki V v. Panthra Sweet Sue v. Nasty Tracy Turner (No-Disqualification Catfight) Christy Cooper v. Janet Strong (Lumberjack match) Double J v. Shelley Rage <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA wrestling action> <Wide angle view of the inside of the arena shows a capacity crowd. The ring announcer, timekeepers, and other officials are walking down the aisle to get ready for the show.> ---------- Double J v. Shelley Rage ---------- Both women lock up, then Rage goes for a jack-knife. Double J backdrops her instead; Rage hangs on for a sunset flip. Double J kicks out in two; Rage climbs the turnbuckles. She attempts a flying dropkick, but Double J sidesteps it. Rage catches her in a jack-knife, taking control of the match. She soon sends Double J into the ropes. Double J rebounds and misses with an elbow, rebounds again, and nails Rage with a knee to the back, going on the offensive. Rage quickly ends it with a facerake. She soon runs into the ropes. Double J surprises her with a short lariat and covers her; Rage kicks out in two. She stands up; Double J punches Rage in the midsection, resuming her assault. After two miutes she executes a double leg cradle; Rage kicks out at the last second. She quickly executes a jack-knife; Double J kicks out in two. She goes for a powerslam, but Rage counters with a lariat, turning the match back around. She soon goes for a double chickenwing, but Double J blocks it and rolls her up in a small package. Rage kicks out in two; Double J follows up with a dragon suplex, retaking control of the match. After two-and-a-half minutes she whips Rage into the ropes. Rage attempts a leaping clothesline, but Double J ducks it. She goes for a short lariat, but Rage ducks it and goes to the top turnbuckle. She attempts a flying cross body press, but Double J rolls it over. Rage kicks out in two. She attempts a monkey flip, but Double J blocks it. She takes Rage down in a crucifix; Rage kicks out in two. Double J hits her with a forearm in the back, regaining her momentum. After two minutes she whips Rage into the ropes. Both women get hit with a double clothesline. Rage gets up first and attempts a bodyslam, but Double J counters with a small package. Rage kicks out in two. Both women get up; Double J attempts to whip Rage to the ropes, but Rage reverses it. She nails Double J with an elbow, turning the match around. Double J shortly nails her with a front-layout suplex. She goes for a powerslam, but Rage counters with a lariat and heads to the turnbuckles. She goes for a flying clothesline, but Double J ducks down instead. She attempts a reverse neckbreaker, but Rage blocks it and takes her down with a chop, regaining her momentum. She shortly pins Double J with the Superfly Splash. (13:20) ---------- Janet Strong (Lumberjacks: Sweet Sue, Lovely Liz Strohlheim, Karen "Bugs" Huffman, Wildcat, Sierra, Adora Locklear, Kelly Locklear) v. Christy Cooper (Lumberjacks: Nasty Tracy Turner, Jenny Black, Shelley Rage, "Psycho" Vicki V, Double D - Debbie Diamond, Donna Star, Tammi Cooper) ---------- Both women lock up, then Christy rakes Strong's face, taking control of the match. She soon attempts another facerake, but Strong blocks it and nails Christy with a back heel kick, going on the attack. Cooper shortly throws Strong out of the ring near her lumberjacks. Strong nails an advancing Turner with a thrust kick to the head; Tammi distracts Strong, allowing Star to throw her back into the ring. Christy attempts an earringer, but Strong backdrops her instead, regaining her momentum. Christy quickly ends her assault with an armbar. She shortly rocks Strong with a double axhandle chop. Strong responds with a back suplex, retaking control. She soon nails Christy with a swinging punch. Christy comes back with an armlock hammerlock, turning the match back around. She shortly attempts to send Strong into a corner, but Strong reverses it. She runs in shoulder-first, nailing Christy and retaking control. She soon attempts a back suplex, but Christy counters with a cross-body block. Strong kicks out in two and stands up. Christy punches her hard, going back on the attack. She shortly goes for a facerake, but Strong blocks it and applies a front facelock, starting a series of submission holds. She shortly whips Christy into the ropes. Christy comes back with a lariat, ending Strong's offensive. Over the next two minutes she gradually gains more control of the match. She then runs into the ropes. Strong uses her speed to throw her out of the ring. Christy lands among her lumberjacks, who help her up. The ref orders the lumberjacks to throw Christy back in, but they pretend deafness. Strong goes to the floor after Christy. Black comes up on Strong, but she nails her. Christy slams Strong's face on the guardrail; the Rebellion and the TCA come up on the Posse and the Dolls, starting a full-scale war. Strong backdrops Christy and nails an advancing Diamond with a kick to the head. She reenters the ring, followed by Christy. The war continues on the outside of the ring; the ref heads to the floor to restore order. Strong continues her assault with a front facelock. The ref shortly returns the lumberjacks to their respective sides and reenters the ring. Strong notices and applies the Bearhug. Christy submits after 5 seconds. (14:21) *No-Disqualification Catfight* ---------- Sweet Sue (w/ Tall Troll) v. Nasty Tracy Turner (w/ "Psycho" Vicki V and the Dream Master) ---------- Sue goes for a standing wristlock, but Turner blocks it and takes her down with a leaping forearm. She covers Sue, but can only get a two count. Turner continues her assault with an over-the-shoulder stomachbreaker. Sweet Sue shortly uses the same move to retake control of the match. After a minute she attempts a Soviet suplex, but Turner blocks it and sends her into a corner. She runs in shoulder-first, nailing Sue and going back on the attack. She shortly goes for a swinginng neckbreaker, but Sue counters with a side suplex. She throws Turner out of the ring. Turner slaps her hard, then whips her into the guardrail. She reenters the ring, followed by Sue. Turner continues her assault with an elbowsmash. After a minute she goes for a spin kick, but Sue ducks it. She takes Turner down with a rolling kick, turning the match around. Turner shortly attempts a Doctor bomb, but Sue counters with a backdrop. Turner gets back up and pulls Sue's hair, then executes a knee to the midsection, retaking control. Sue shortly takes her down with an enzuilariato, retaking control of the match. Almost two minutes later Turner ducks a leaping forearm. She slaps Sue hard, then rakes her face. Sue responds with a facerake, then whips Turner to the ropes. Turner comes back with an elbow, taking control of the match. After a minute she throws Sue out of the ring and heads to the apron. She takes Sue down with a flying forearm. Sue gets back up and rushes Turner, prompting a full-scale catfight. Both women fall into the guardrail, then Turner whips Sue over the ringsteps by the hair. She slams Sue's face on the ringsteps again by the hair, then plants Sue on the concrete with a jack-knife. She reenters the ring, followed by Sue. Sue rushes Turner, raking the face. Turner responds with a facebite; Sue executes a belly-to-belly suplex, pinning Turner. (12:57) ---------- "Psycho" Vicki V (w/ Nasty Tracy Turner and the Dream Master) v. Panthra (w/ Tygra and Lu Chin) ---------- Vicki attempts a brain buster, but Panthra counters with a vertical suplex, taking control of the match. She shortly goes for a hiptoss, but Vicki counters with a backslide. Panthra kicks out in two and quickly executes her own backslide, pinning Vicki. (0:50) ===== Birmingham: "Whoa!
