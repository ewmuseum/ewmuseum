WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION (http.// kessel.simplenet.com/WCA/ ) CRUCIBLE Live from Moscow, Russia 8/13/97 -----Main Event----- Bodacious Janet Muniz v. Luscious Lisa (World Title Match) Valkyrie v. Red [Henchmen of Hades] "Mistress of Death" Krista Stevens / Shannon Stevens v. Bounty Hunters (Double Tables Match) Widow Maker v. Samantha Strong "Psycho Queen" Vicki V v. Freedom (Best of 13 Series: Match 4) Conclusion of Li’l Cheetah v. Shannon Stevens (Street Fight) (Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.) (Fireworks explode as the camera shows the crimson WCA logo on the white ring canvas. The camera descends rapidly, then levels off facing the aisle. The camera swerves left, panning out over the capacity crowd and circling the ring, ending its focus on the iridescent three-dimensional WCA letters bracketing the entrance to the backstage area. The referee for the first match heads to the ring. The camera focuses on commentator Mike Sharpe.) MIKE SHARPE: “Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to Crucible! Tonight in our main event Bodacious Janet Muniz will defend her World championship against Luscious Lisa of the Dollhouse, plus Valkyrie takes on Red of the Henchmen of Hades, the Tag Team champions the Stevens sisters face the Bounty Hunters in a highly-anticipated non-title Double Tables match, Widow Maker faces Samantha Strong, and ‘the Psycho Queen’ Vicki V takes on Freedom in Match 4 of their Best of 13 series. But first, we’ll show you the conclusion of Monday night’s Theatre of War main event between Li’l Cheetah and Shannon Stevens. When we left, Shannon was firmly in control of the match and had just thrown Cheetah out of the ring.” (Dissolve to tape. The words “Theatre of War 8/11” appears at the bottom right briefly.) SHARPE’S VOICE: “Cheetah nails Stevens with a chair - and another blow. Jordan comes through the ropes; he’s moving a bit slowly tonight. Cheetah throws Stevens into the stands. Cheetah falls backward; Shannon drops and hooks a leg; one, two, shoulder up. “Shannon stands up. Her sister comes up to her and whispers something in her ear. Cheetah pushes him away, then takes her arm away from the ref. The Assassin enters the ring as Stevens rolls out. The Assassin raises Cheetah’s arm as Stevens joins her sister and Axeln.) (Dissolve to live close-up of Sharpe.) SHARPE: “Li’l Cheetah defeated Shannon, although with a generous help from Tall Troll. Shannon was apparently setting up for the Shooting Star Press, but with Cheetah getting up that move would have backfired on her big-time. Cheetah will go on to face WCA newcomer Queen Amanda at Dog Daze in a Falls-Count-Anywhere No-Disqualifications match. I fully expect that one to be brutal as well, and Shannon will be competing with her sister later tonight in the Double Tables match against the Bounty Hunters. “Up next, though, fans, is the fourth match of 13 between ‘Psycho Queen’ Vicki V and Freedom. The Psycho Queen has won every match in this series, but has had to fight each time to win. Freedom needs to begin worrying about a win; with each show her chances at victory are slipping away. Let’ s go to the ring.” ===== *Best of 13 Series: Match 4* ---------- “Psycho Queen” Vicki V (w/ Dr. Insane, “the Tax Collector” John Blair, Christy Cooper, and Valkyrie) v. Freedom (w/ the Dream Master) Referee: Julian Pace ---------- Both women lock up. Vicki punches Freedom in the stomach, then she goes to a corner and slips through the ropes before going to the top. Freedom runs in and throws Vicki to the mat, then Vicki kicks off a grapevine. She gets up and hiptosses Freedom, taking control of the match. Freedom soon ducks a flying clothesline. Vicki gets back on her feet; Freedom takes her down with a front-layout suplex, going on the offensive. Vicki shortly dodges a handspring elbow, then she goes to a corner. Freedom picks herself up; Vicki takes her down with a flying sunset flip; Freedom kicks out at the last second. Both women get back up, then Freedom blocks a kick to the midsection and applies a front facelock, retaking control. Vicki soon blocks a monkey flip. She takes Freedom down in a reverse neckbreaker, going back on the attack. Freedom shortly sidesteps a springboard drop-kick. She taunts the audience, drawing some flak as Vicki gets up. Freedom whips her to a corner and charges in, hammering her with a somersault splash. Freedom gets up and whips Vicki to the opposite corner. She runs in; Vicki lunges away from the somersault splash. She steps on Freedom’s neck; one, two, three, four, she steps off and scrapes Freedom’s face with the bottom of her boot, taking control again. Freedom shortly nails her with a kick to the midsection, retaking control. Vicki shortly blocks a front facelock, then she punches Freedom in the stomach, going back on the attack. Freedom soon blocks a swinging punch, then she nails Vicki with a series of chops, starting a new gameplan. After 2 minutes Vicki blocks a double reverse chinlock with a facerake, then she gets up and kicks Freedom, turning the match back around. After a minute Freedom reverses an Irish whip, then she ducks an elbow to the face and stops Vicki with a back heel kick, turning things back around. She shortly puts Vicki into a front facelock. Vicki breaks the hold after 11 seconds, then she goes to a corner and climbs the turnbuckles. She comes off the top with a flying clothesline, retaking control. After ninety seconds Freedom blocks a reverse neckbreaker. Vicki blocks a legdrag takedown and goes to a corner, steps through the ropes, and scales the turnbuckles. Freedom watches her every move, then she ducks a flying clothesline. Vicki lands hard, then she blocks standing short-arm scissors and picks herself up. She picks Freedom up and executes a running pwoerslam; one, two, shoulder up. Vicki lets Freedom get back on her feet, then she punches her, taking control of the match. After 2 minutes she takes Freedom down in a spinebuster, pinning her. (15:18) ---------- Widow Maker v. Samantha Strong (w/ Precious, Ashley Hamilton, Debbie Angel, Ginger Thompson, Luscious Lisa, Vicky Armstrong, and Janet Strong) Referee: Julian Pace ---------- Both women lock up. Maker runs to the ropes; Strong backdrops her. Maker pops back up and reverses a whip to a corner, then she runs in shoulder-first; Strong raises a knee up to block her, then she legsweeps Maker, taking control of the match. Maker shortly punches Strong, taking control of the match. Strong soon blocks a kick to the thigh, then she takes Maker down with an armdrag takedown, going back on the offensive. After 2 minutes Maker blocks a fisherman suplex, then she takes Strong down in a lariat, turning the match back around. Strong shortly executes an armdrag takedown, retaking control. As her offense progresses, the crowd gets distracted by the Bounty Hunters moving along the rafters. They cross the beam over the ring and halt midway, pushing a shopping cart. As Strong takes Maker down with a springboard drop-kick and makes a cover, the Hunters drop a bagful of wet concrete onto her, covering her and splashing onto Pace, Maker, and the ring. Pace disqualifies Maker as Strong quickly leaves the ring with Janet and the Dollhouse, followed by Maker and Pace. The Hunters disappear in the rafters as a cleaning crew comes down to the ring to wipe the concrete up before it sets. (6:10) *Double Tables Match* (Non-title Match) ---------- “Mistress of Death” Krista Stevens / Shannon Stevens (w/ Bodacious Janet Muniz, “the Intimidator” Amber Axeln, and Liberty) v. Bounty Hunters (Red and Black) (w/ Panthra) Referees: Julian Pace ---------- Shannon and Black lock up. Red enters the ring and takes Shannon down in a lariat. She rolls out of the ring as Shannon pops back up. Shannon blocks a suplex with a facerake, then she executes a belly-to-back suplex, taking control of the match. Black soon takes her down with a spinning leg lariat, then she tags in Red and keeps Shannon down with another spinning leg lariat. Black argues with Pace as Shannon gets up; Red taps her partner on the shoulder, then they whip her to the ropes and double-punch her in the stomach. Black goes to her corner as Red throws Shannon out of the ring. Shannon lands on her feet, then she quickly sidesteps Red’s slingshot bodyblock to the floor. Krista comes over; Black hops off the apron and walks runs over, brushing past Shannon and taking Krista down with a forearm smash. She turns back to Shannon and holds her as Red gets up and executes a spinning leg lariat. Black returns to her corner as Shannon picks herself up, rubbing her jaw. She blocks a Russian legsweep with an elbowjab, then she reenters the ring. Red puts the first table into the ring, then Black hops back to the floor and puts the second table in. Red whips Shannon to the ropes, then she takes her down with a belly-to-belly suplex, taking control of the match. Shannon soon reverses an Irish whip, then she blocks a belly-to-belly with a punch. She kicks Red in the knee, turning things back around. Red shortly nails Shannon with a chop, retaking control. She shortly runs to the ropes; Shannon stops her with a shoulderblock, then she puts her into a full nelson. Krista enters the ring and rushes across it, knocking Black off the apron with a running forearm. Krista turns and sets the tables up. Black reenters the ring; Krista sets her up for a suplex as Shannon goes to the top rope and turns around. They signal to each other, then Black reverses the suplex into a front-layout suplex through the table as Red catches Shannon and bodyslams her through hers. The Bounty Hunters win the match. (3:47) ---------- Valkyrie (w/ Dr. Insane, “the Tax Collector” John Blair, and Hardcore) v. Red (w/ Blood) Referee: Julian Pace ---------- Both women lock up. Red takes Valkyrie down with an armdrag takedown, taking control of the match. Valkyrie soon reverses a whip to a corner, then she meets Red’s charge out with a punch. She chops Red; Red responds with her own chop, then she drop-kicks Valkyrie, retaking control of the match. Valkyrie shortly takes her down with a handspring elbow, retaking control of the match. After 2 minutes Red ducks a back heel kick, then she takes Valkyrie down with an armdrag, turning the match back around. Valkyrie soon reverses an Irish whip, then she ducks a clothesline, turns around, and stops Red with a shoulderblock. She follows up with a back heel kick, going back on the offensive. Red soon counters the Valkyrian Storm with a front-layout suplex. She rolls out of the ring, then she reenters via a springboard drop-kick, going back on the offensive. Valkyrie shortly reverses an Irish whip, then she takes Red down with neck-scissors, retaking control of the match. Red soon blocks and reverses a hiptoss, turning things around again. After 2 minutes Valkyrie blocks a front facelock, then she backs up and takes Red down with a leaping clothesline, turning the match back around. She soon executes the Valkyrian Storm and covers Red, pinning her. (10:06) ===== MIKE SHARPE: “Valkyrie defeats Red, pinning her with the patented Valkyrian Storm.
