The referee for the first match heads to the ring.) *Best of 11: Match 9* -------------------- Bodacious Janet Muniz (w/ "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln, Liberty, and the Mistress of Death) v. The Assassin (w/ Li'l Cheetah) Referee: Esther Siler -------------------- Both women circle each other, teasing a test of strength. They lock up briefly, then the Assassin blocks and returns a slap. She follows up with a chop, taking control of the match. Muniz shortly blocks a hangman, then she applies a chickenwing, taking charge. The Assassin breaks the hold after 14 seconds, then she ducks a kick to the head and blocks a kick to the midsection. She runs to the ropes, then she ducks another kick to the head. Muniz blocks a sleeper with a jawbreaker, putting the Assassin back on the defensive. She shortly whips the Assassin to the ropes; the Assassin returns with a shoulderblock, then she whips Muniz to the ropes and follows her in, choking her against the top cable. One, two, three, four, five, Siler warns the Assassin, who lets Muniz go. Muniz falls to the mat as the Assassin yells at Siler; the Assassin drops and hooks a leg. One, two, shoulder up. The Assassin hauls Muniz to her feet and sends her to a corner, then she charges in and nails Muniz with an elbowsmash, retaking control of the match. Muniz shortly blocks another choke against the ropes, then she decks the Assassin with a punch, going back on the offensive. After 2 minutes the Assassin quickly executes a brain buster, turning the match back around. A minute later Muniz stumbles back from a kneelift. She goes to one knee, then the Assassin blocks a full nelson and gets back up. Muniz executes a single-leg takedown, rebuilding her offense. After ninety seconds she whips the Assassin to the ropes; the Assassin rebounds and decks Muniz with a clothesline, then she covers her; one, two, shoulder up. The Assassin beats Muniz back to her feet, then she puts Muniz back on the mat with a kneelift, going back on the attack. Muniz shortly reverses a small package; one, two, kickout. Both women get back up; Muniz tackles the Assassin, retaking control. The Assassin soon blocks a kick to the midsection, then she puts Muniz on the mat with a front-layout suplex, renewing her offense. Muniz shortly blocks a snap mare, then she whips the Assassin to the ropes and stops her with a bearhug, slowing the match down. After 2 minutes the Assassin blocks a chickenwing, then she puts Muniz into a front facelock, turning the match back around. She shortly whips Muniz to the ropes; Muniz rebounds and backdrops her, then she puts her in the Latino Leg Split. The Assassin submits immediately. Bodacious Janet Muniz wins the series 6-to-3.
