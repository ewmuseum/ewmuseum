"Sideshow" Bob Tuwilliger =====CALL TO ARMS: Sweat and Blood Pre-Show===== -------------------- The Crown Jewels (Nigel Steele and Ian Stone) (MGR: The Bristol Brawler) v. The Dictators (The Czar and the Kaiser) (MGR: Jason Hate) -------------------- The Dictators are introduced first, then the music changes to "God Save the Queen" and the Crown Jewels make their way down the aisle. The Russian Ring Masters come over the guardrail halfway down the aisle and begin pummeling the Crown Jewels. Van Vadar nearly dislocates Steele's arm before the Bristol Brawler runs them back into the crowd. The Crown Jewels make their way into the ring. Stone and the Czar start. The Czar grabs Stone and forces him to tag out to Steele. Steele reluctantly enters the ring, then takes the Czar down with a legsweep faceslam. He runs into the ropes, but the Czar catches him in a small package. Steele kicks out at the last second; the Czar rewards him with an armbar on his bad arm. Steele submits after 5 seconds of agony. The Bristol Brawler and Stone enter the ring and attack the Dictators, leading to an all-out brawl between the six men. The fight moves to the floor of the arena as Stone clotheslines Hate and the Kaiser to the floor. The Czar punches Steele over the top rope to the floor, then the Bristol Brawler busts him open with a television camera. The Kaiser moves the fight to the stands as he clotheslines Stone over the guardrail. The Russian Ring Masters come down to ringside through the fans and get in on the brawl. Two WCA sparring opponents run from the back to help the Crown Jewels out. The Bristol Brawler sends the Czar into the stands, completing the transition. The fight continues in mid-arena; the arena security comes out to halt the carnage. Fans begin handing their chairs to the wrestlers for use in the war; one fan even takes the liberty of punching Hate before being arrested and taken away. Hate tries to stop the Dictators from doing any more damage, but they turn around and knock him out with a double punch. The Russian Ring Masters leave to get ready for tonight's main event. The fight continues out the front door; fans in the parking lot start crowding around it. WCA officials try to end the fight; the Bristol Brawler groin-shots one of them, then turns his attention to the Dictators. The officials confer for a moment, then reenter the building. President Kessler walks down to ringside and talks with them. Kessler talks to the announcer, then gives him a statement to read. The announcer reads aloud to the audience the statement indefinitely banning the Crown Jewels and the Dictators from competition in the WCA for their actions tonight. (Match: 0:38) *Iron Man Match* -30-minute Time Limit- -------------------- Rick Showboat (MGR: Dr. Love escorted by Ms. Ronda) v. "Deadly" Dylan Vaughn (MGR: Randall Chase) -------------------- Showboat goes on the offensive before the bell with a belly-to-back suplex. He shortly clotheslines Vaughn to the floor, then follows him out. Dr. Love distracts Vaughn, allowing Showboat to nail him with a lariat. Both men reenter the ring, then Vaughn applies a leg hammerlock on Showboat. Showboat reaches the ropes after 15 seconds. Vaughn runs into the ropes; Dr. Love trips him. The ref warns him as Vaughn gets up and whips Showboat into the ropes. He goes on the offensive with a side suplex. Showboat quickly retakes control of the match with a front facelock. Vaughn just as quickly evens the match up by kicking Showboat. The two men stand toe-to-toe with each other. Showboat shortly goes for a DDT, but Vaughn counters with a backdrop. Showboat in turn re-reverses it into a sunset flip; Vaughn kicks out in two. Showboat ends the stalemate with an earringer. He shortly goes for a thrust kick to the head, but Vaughn ducks it and takes him down with a back suplex. Showboat evens the match up with a short lariat. He covers Vaughn, but can't get a one count. Again the two men stand toe-to-toe with each other. Showboat shortly goes for the Love Lock, but Vaughn counters with a kick him in the midsection and catches him in a German suplex. Showboat kicks out in two; Vaughn takes control of the match with a kick to the thigh. Showboat very shortly stops his momentum by backdropping him to the floor. Showboat follows Vaughn out and whips him into the guardrail. Vaughn hits it with his back and slide to a sitting position against it on the floor. Showboat runs at him and lands a vicious kick to the head, then reenters the ring. Chase helps Vaughn reenter as well, then rolls out of the way of a kneedrop. Showboat executes a lariat as Vaughn gets to his feet, retaking control of the match. Vaughn soon turns the match around with a back heel kick. He shortly applies a scorpion deathlock, then lets go after 27 seconds. He puts Showboat in an STF; Showboat submits immediately. (11:15 - Showboat 0; Vaughn 1) Vaughn continues his offensive with a chop. He soon goes for a side suplex, but Showboat blocks it and nails him with a German suplex. He puts Showboat in the STF; Dr. Love jumps onto the apron, distracting the ref. Vaughn releases Showboat, then goes for an elbowdrop. Showboat rolls out fo the way and stands up. Showboat runs into the ropes, but Vaughn stands up and clotheslines him, evening the match up. The two men stand toe-to-toe. Dr. Love shortly enters the ring with a chair and attempts to deck Vaughn. Vaughn sees him coming and pulls Showboat in front of him. Dr. Love nails Showboat, then Vaughn throws him over the top rope onto Ms. Ronda and pins Showboat. Showboat kicks out in two. Vaughn, his right side darkening as a bruise develops where Showboat kicked him, pulls Showboat up and attempts to whip him into the ropes. Showboat reverses it; Dr. Love trips Vaughn. The ref warns him again as Showboat goes for a short lariat. Vaughn ducks it and catches him in a belly-to-back suplex. He nails Showboat with the Stun Gun and pins Showboat. (20:23 - Showboat 0; Vaughn 3) Showboat goes for a short lariat, but Vaughn blocks it and returns the same. He covers Showboat, but can only get a two count. Showboat comes back with a swinging punch. Vaughn kicks him in the thigh, taking control of the match. He very shortly nails Showboat with the Stun Gun and pins him. (21:40 - Showboat 0; Vaughn 4) Showboat turns the match around by whipping Vaughn into a corner and nailing him with a shoulder to the midsection. He shortly runs into the ropes, but Vaughn catches him in a belly-to-belly suplex and covers him. Showboat kicks out in two; Vaughn rewards him with a half-Boston, turning the match back around. He shortly applies an STF; Showboat submits after 5 seconds. (23:06 - Showboat 0; Vaughn 5) Showboat applies a sleeper; Vaughn stumbles into the ropes to break it. He goes for an enzuilariato, but Showboat ducks it and turns the match back around with a kneelift. He shortly nails Vaughn with a fist to the midsection, then Vaughn comes back and pins him with a German suplex. (26:42 - Showboat 0; Vaughn 7) Showboat executes his own German suplex, but Vaughn kicks out in two. Showboat stomps on his back, then goes for a jumping DDT as Vaughn gets up. Vaughn counters with a backdrop, then turns the match back around with a back suplex. He puts Showboat in a figure four; Showboat refuses to submit, but the pain causes him to be pinned in the leglock.
