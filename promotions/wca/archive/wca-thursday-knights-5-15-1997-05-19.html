WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION ( http://www.inet-images.com/skessler/WCA/ ) THURSDAY KNIGHTS 5/15/97 Live from St. Paul, Minnesota -----Main Event----- Bounty Hunters v. The Scandalous Ones (Tag Team Titles Match) Janet Strong v. Big Norma Hudson (Television Title Tournament Match) Muniz's Musings The Mistress of Death v. Kazumi Hayakawa Valkyrie v. Freedom v. Liberty Bodacious Janet Muniz v. The Assassin (Best of 11 Series: Match 6) (Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.) (Fireworks explode as the camera shows the crimson WCA logo on the white ring canvas. The camera descends rapidly, then levels off facing the aisle. The camera swerves left, panning out over the capacity crowd and circling the ring, ending its focus on the iridescent three-dimensional WCA letters bracketing the entrance to the backstage area. The referee for the first match heads to the ring.) *Best of 11: Match 6* -------------------- Bodacious Janet Muniz (w/ "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln, Liberty, and the Mistress of Death) v. The Assassin (w/ Li'l Cheetah) Referee: Esther Siler -------------------- Both women lock up. Muniz reverses a whip to the ropes, then she ducks a clothesline. Muniz glances back and misses with an elbow to the face, then charges forward and stops the Assassin with a kick. She puts the Assassin into a full nelson. the Assassin breaks free in 8 seconds; Muniz grabs her by the head and pulls her to the ropes. The Assassin blocks a ropeburn and spins around, poking Muniz in the eye. She whips Muniz to the far ropes; Muniz ducks a clothesline, rebounds, and chokes the Assassin with both hands; one, two, three, she lets go briefly and chokes again; one, two, three, she lets go and warns the ref. She chokes the Assassin with one hand; one, two, three, four, she lets go. Muniz whips the Assassin to a corner and charges in; the Assassin moves out of the way. Muniz stops herself in time, then the Assassin puts her into a sleeper. Muniz turns away from the corner and executes a jawbreaker. She gets up and puts the Assassin face-first on the mat with a double-axhandle chop to the head, taking control of the match. After a minute the Assassin counters a legsweep faceslam with her own faceslam. Muniz picks herself up, surprised; the Assassin grabs her head and walks her to the ropes, choking her against them. One, two, three, four, the Assassin yanks Muniz around and executes a snap mare, putting Muniz on the defensive. Muniz shortly blocks a ropeburn; the Assassin ducks a kick to the head, then she executes a Russian legsweep. Muniz gets back up quickly; the Assassin takes her back down with a belly-to-back suplex. Muniz sits up, then she gets back on her feet. She reverses a whip to a corner, then she charges in and nails the Assassin, retaking control. The Assassin shortly catches her in another Russian legsweep, going back on the offensive. Muniz soon blocks a front-facelock with a backdrop, then she puts the Assassin into body scissors. The Assassin grits her teeth. Muniz steadily tightens the hold; the Assassin submits after 28 seconds. (5:20) -------------------- Valkyrie (w/ Dr. Insane and Hardcore) v. Freedom (w/ Shannon Stevens and the Dream Master) v. Liberty (w/ Bodacious Janet Muniz, "the Intimidator" Amber Axeln, and the Mistress of Death) Referee: Mark White -------------------- Liberty enters the ring last; she gets on the apron and climbs the turnbuckles, then she hammers Freedom with a flying karate chop. She throws Valkyrie out of the ring and puts Freedom into a Boston crab, taking control of the match. She relatively quickly lets go and screams out "Posse's going down!" Freedom reverses a whip to a corner, then she meets Liberty's charge out with an elbow. Freedom goes to the apron and executes a springboard rana, taking charge. Liberty shortly blocks a somersault splash by pulling up her knees. She gets up and hammers Freedom with an elbowdrop, retaking control. She shortly tags out to Valkyrie, grinning evilly at her. Valkyrie enters the ring and blocks an armdrag takedown with a lariat. She puts Freedom into a grapevine, taking control. Freedom shortly tags out to Liberty, who takes Valkyrie down with a front-layout suplex, retaking control. Valkyrie soon blocks a standing wristlock and chokes Liberty; one, two, three, four, a moment's release, one, two, three, Valkyrie switches hands, one, two, three, four, she lets go. Liberty blocks a back heel kick while gasping for breath, then Valkyrie dodges a drop-kick to the knee. She puts Liberty into a cobra clutch, going back on the offensive. Liberty shortly blocks a ropeburn, then she turns around and takes Valkyrie down in a front-layout suplex. She goes to a corner as Valkyrie rolls onto her back. Valkyrie sits up, then she gets back up; Liberty takes her down with a flying shoulderblock. Liberty gets up ahead of Valkyrie; the latter reverses an Irish whip, then she ducks a clothesline. She ducks an elbow to the face; Liberty rebounds again and blocks a facerake. She rocks Valkyrie with a jumping side kick. Valkyrie staggers back to the ropes, then she charges Liberty and nails her with a clothesline, taking control of the match. Liberty very shortly reverses an Irish whip, then she blocks a facerake and decks Valkyrie with a jumping side kick, taking control of the match. Valkyrie shortly reverses a suplex into a vertical one, putting Liberty back on the defensive. Liberty shortly blocks a facerake, then she executes a back suplex, retaking control of the match. After an intense 5 minutes Liberty tags out to Freedom. Valkyrie ducks Freedom's neck-scissors. She meets a standing Freedom with a spinning leg lariat, turning the match around. She shortly runs to the ropes; both women get hit with a dual clothesline. Freedom gets up before Valkyrie. She grabs Valkyrie's arm as the latter gets back up and sends her to the ropes, then she executes a drop-kick to the knee, starting a new offense. She soon whips Valkyrie to a corner and runs in; Valkyrie raises her leg up to block her. She takes Freedom down with the Valkyrian Storm and covers her; one, two, three.
