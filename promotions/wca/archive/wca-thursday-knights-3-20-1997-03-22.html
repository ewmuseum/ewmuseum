WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION THURSDAY KNIGHTS 3/20/97 *Special 30-Minute Edition* From Istanbul, Turkey -----Main Event----- =Tag Team Titles Match= Magic Mountain Warriors v. Delicious Dolls Muniz's Musings =World Title match= The Mistress of Death v. Jenny Black Interview with Magic Mountain Warriors Helena "High Kick" Knightsbridge v. "Nasty Girl" Amber Jane "Psycho" Vicki V v. Liberty <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.> <Fireworks explode as the camera shows the crimson WCA logo on the white ring canvas. The camera descends rapidly, then levels off facing the aisle. The camera swerves left, panning out over the capacity crowd and circling the ring, ending its focus on the iridescent three-dimensional WCA letters bracketing the entrance to the backstage area. The referee for the first match heads to the ring.> -------------------- "Psycho" Vicki V (w/ Dr. Insane and Christy Cooper) v. Liberty (w/ Bodacious Janet Muniz) -------------------- Both women circle each other. They lock up briefly, then Liberty breaks off. She goes to a corner and leaps to the top turnbuckle, then takes Vicki down with flying headscissors, taking control of the match. She shortly runs to the ropes; Vicki misses with a kick, then spins around and hits Liberty with a shoulderblock. She punches Liberty in the stomach, taking charge. After a minute she gets on a top turnbuckle; Liberty throws her to the mat. Vicki gets up; Liberty executes a bridging back suplex. Vicki kicks out in two, then sits up. She gets back up; Liberty sends her to a corner. Vicki rebounds off the turnbuckles, then Liberty nails her with an enzuigiri to the face, going back on the offensive. Vicki shortly ducks another enzuigiri to the face, then she runs to the side ropes and ducks a clothesline. She sidesteps a drop-kick to the knee, then goes to a corner and scales the turnbuckles. Vicki catches Liberty in a flying sunset flip; Liberty gets her shoulder up in two. She gets up and runs to the ropes; Vicki jumps a shoulderblock, then spins around and clotheslines Liberty, retaking control of the match. Liberty shortly sidesteps a springboard drop-kick. Vicki gets back up in time to duck a short clothesline. Liberty blocks a fireman's carry; Vicki blocks an armdrag takedown with a lariat. Liberty rolls away from a kneedrop, then she gets back up and screams "Posse's going down!", which draws a round of cheers from the English-speaking members of the crowd, followed by a second wave after the translation. Vicki rolls away from a kneedrop. She sets Liberty up; Liberty turns a spinning powerbomb into spinning headscissors. Vicki sits up; Liberty applies an armlock hammerlock, taking control once again. After 2 minutes Cooper gets up on the apron, distracting Liberty. Dr. Insane hands Vicki her spiked glove, then Cooper hops back down. Vicki decks Liberty from a punch with the glove, turning the match back around. She soon swings at Liberty with the glove again; Liberty moves to the left, then grabs Vicki's arm and takes the glove off. She puts it on, then nails Vicki with it. Liberty throws Vicki out of the ring, then throws the glove out after her. Vicki reenters the ring at the seven count; Liberty sends her to a far corner. Vicki lunges away from a shoulder-first run-in, then pulls Liberty out of the corner and applies the Claw. Liberty instinctively makes her way to the ropes. Vicki, irritated, scoops her up and drops her into a stomachbreaker. Liberty rolls away from a diving headbutt and gets back up. She rests a moment, then screams out "Posse's going down" again. She puts Vicki into an abdominal stretch, turning things back around. Vicki shortly reverses an Irish whip, then blocks a shoulderblock, instead lifting Liberty up and pummeling her in a spinning powerbomb, retaking control again. She soon runs into the ropes; Liberty nails her with a kick, followed by a crucifix. Vicki kicks out in two; Liberty puts her into a full nelson, resetting the pace of the match. After ninety seconds Vicki ducks flying headscissors. She picks Liberty up and drops her into a backbreaker, turning things around again. After a minute she laughs maniacally, then applies the Claw. Liberty submits immediately. (11:25) -------------------- Helena "High Kick" Knightsbridge (w/ Bodacious Janet Muniz) v. "Nasty Girl" Amber Jane (w/ "Dominatress" Kathy Lynn) -------------------- Both women lock up, then Jane reverses an Irish whip. Knightsbridge returns with a bodyslam. She sends Jane to the ropes; Jane returns with a kick, taking Knightsbridge down. Knightsbridge rolls away from an elbowdrop. Jane gets up and blocks an abdominal stretch with a hiptoss. Knightsbridge gets back up, then counters a headlock with a back suplex. Both women get back up; Jane dodges a kneelift. She applies standing short-arm scissors, taking control of the match. After an intense 2 minutes Knightsbridge attempts a high cross body; Jane catches and drops her into a backbreaker. Knightsbridge gets back up in time to duck a flying forearm. She grabs Jane's arm and yanks her to her feet; Jane reverses an Irish whip. Lynn pulls the top rope down; the ref sees it and goes by the book, disqualifying Jane.
