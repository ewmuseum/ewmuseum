WCA Rampage 9/14

15 September 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION RAMPAGE Live from Seattle, Washington 9/14/96 -----Main Event----- "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln v. "Too Sweet" Tracy Turner (Lumberjack I Quit Match) Shadow Warriors v. Top Guns Janet Strong v. Thunder Lolita The Scandalous Ones v. Flair's Girls "Psycho" Vicki V v. Warhead <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA wrestling action> <Wide angle view of the inside of the arena shows a capacity crowd. The ring announcer, timekeepers, and other officials are walking down the aisle to get ready for the show.> ------------------------- "Psycho" Vicki V (w/ the Dream Master) v. Warhead (w/ Snakebite) ------------------------- Both women lock up, then Warhead runs into the ropes. Vicki jumps a kick; Warhead rebounds again and puts her in an abdominal stretch, taking control. Vicki quickly gains the upper hand with a backdrop. She shortly rocks Warhead with a European uppercut. Warhead reels back, then picks Vicki up and bodyslams her, retaking control. After a minute she throws Vicki out of the ring. Vicki quickly reenters. Warhead attempts an Irish whip to the ropes, but Vicki reverses it. She nails Warhead with a dropkick to the knee, going back on the attack. Two minutes later Warhead catches her in a double underhook backbreaker, turning the match back around. She shortly attempts to put Vicki on a turnbuckle, but Vicki blocks it. She blocks a double underhook backbreaker, then attempts a facerake. Warhead blocks it and sends her into the ropes. She jumps a kick; Vicki rebounds again and nails her with a thrust kick to the head, taking control of the match. She shortly nails Warhead with a back heel kick, then applies the Claw. Warhead submits after 6 seconds. (6:07) ------------------------- The Scandalous Ones (Shelley Rage and Jenny Black) (w/ the Dream Master) v. Flair's Girls (Woman and Elizabeth) (w/ the Nature Boy) ------------------------- Rage and Elizabeth start. Rage attempts a monkey flip, but Elizabeth blocks it and drives her knee into Rage's back. She goes for a roundhouse punch, but Rage blocks it and attempts an Irish whip to a corner. Elizabeth reverses it instead; Rage charges back out and doubles her over with a kick to the midsection, taking control. She shortly goes for an avalanche, but Elizabeth counters with a kick to the head and tags out to Woman. Woman takes control of the match with a monkey flip. A minute later Rage makes the tag to Black. They nail Woman with a double enzuilariato, then Rage takes her down with a leaping clothesline. Black covers her, but can only get a two count. The Scandalous Ones send Woman into the ropes, then attempt a double clothesline. Woman ducks it, then returns with her own double clothesline. She shortly tags out to Elizabeth. They nail Black with a double elbowsmash, taking control of the match. Elizabeth soon tags out to Woman, then nails Black with a savate kick. Woman executes a German suplex; Black kicks out in two. Woman rewards her with a fist to the midsection, resuming her onslaught. She shortly goes for a legsweep faceslam, but Black blocks it and nails her with a monkey flip, turning the match around. She shortly tags out to Rage, then nails Woman with an enzuigiri. Rage attempts to follow up with a dragon suplex, but Woman blocks it and kicks her. She tags out to Elziabeth; Elizabeth retakes control with a figure-four sleeper. After 90 seconds she goes for a front facelock, but Rage backdrops her, then follows up with a legdrop. She throws Elizabeth out of the ring and goes to the floor. Elizabeth smashes Rage's face on the guardrail, then hammers her in a side suplex. Rage reenters the ring, followed closely by Elizabeth. Rage turns around and nails her with a brain buster, turning the match back around. She soon executes a flying elbowdrop and covers Elizabeth; Elizabeth kicks out with force, then hammers her in a side suplex. Rage absorbs the blow, then nails Elizabeth with a dropkick, regaining her momentum. After 90 seconds she tags out to Black. Black blocks a side suplex and executes a monkey flip. She goes for a kneelock, but Elizabeth blocks it. She nails Black with a lariat, turning the match back around. Black shortly blocks a side suplex. She executes a tiger driver, pinning Elizabeth. (13:07) ------------------------- Janet Strong v. Thunder Lolita (w/ Cactus Jack) ------------------------- Strong executes a belly-to-belly suplex before the bell. Lolita kicks out forcefully, then goes for a powerslam. Strong counters with a lariat and runs into the ropes. She applies a figure-four sleeper, taking control of the match. Lolita soon attempts a Samoan Drop, but Strong counters with an elbowsmash. She whips Lolita into the ropes. Lolita comes back with a short lariat, going on the offensive. Strong shortly hits Lolita with a turnbuckle and charges in. She attempts a splash, but Lolita moves out of the way, letting Strong splash the turnbuckles. Lolita goes for a jack-knife powerbomb, but Strong backdrops her. She collapses; Lolita picks her up and puts her in an abdominal stretch, resuming her offensive. She soon runs into the ropes. Strong kicks her, retaking control of the match. She soon whips Loltia into the ropes. Both women get hit with a double clothesline. Lolita gets up first and sends Strong into a corner. She walks in and slaps her, then drives her knee into Strong's gut. Lolita pulls her out with a snap mare, taking control of the match. A minute alter she throws Strong out of the ring and goes to the floor. Strong knocks her over the ringsteps and attempts a facerake. Lolita blocks it and powerbombs her on the floor. She throws Strong back into the ring and follows her in. She attempts a powerslam, but Strong counters with a lariat. She runs into the ropes, then DDT's Lolita. She rolls Lolita over, then pins her. (8:46) ------------------------- Shadow Warriors (Valkyrie and Vonya) (w/ the Prince and Night Crawler) v. Top Guns (Warthog and Warhead) (w/ Snakebite) ------------------------- Vonya and Warthog start. Both women lock up, then Warthog puts Vonya in a double chickenwing, starting a series of submission holds. Vonya shortly escapes from her and tags out to Valkyrie. She nails Vonya with a swinging neckbreaker; Valkyrie attemtps a follow-up elbowdrop, but Warthog rolsl out of the way and gets back up. She sends Valkyrie into the ropes and clotheslines her, resuming her offensive. A minute later Valkyrie ends it with a chokehold. She shortly goes for a springboard splash, but Warthog rolls out of the way and tags out to Warhead. They nail Valkyrie with a double leaping clothesline. Valkyrie gets right back up and throws Warthog out, then punches Warhead, retaking control. Vonya soon enters the ring; she and Valkyrie repeatedly hammer Warhead with a series of fistdrops. Warhead throws Vonya out of the ring, then rolls out as well. Valkyrie chases her out and nails her with a rolling kick. She smashes Warhead's face on the guardrail. Warhead reenters the ring and tags out to Warthog. Valkyrie greets her with an enzuigiri to the face, continuing her attack. She soon goes for a leaping forearm, but Warthog ducks it and throws her out of the ring. She goes to the floor and knocks her over the ringsteps. Valkyrie blocks the Crash n Burn; Warthog settles for a tiger suplex. Valkyrie gets back up and climbs to the apron. Warthog grabs her and throws her onto the floor. She rolls Valkyrie back into the ring and climbs up the turnbuckles. The Prince shoves her off into the ring; Valkyrie applies an armlock hammerlock. She shortly tags out to Vonya. Vonya puts Warthog on a turnbuckle, then takes her down via a belly-to-belly superplex, continuing the assault. She soon goes for a swinging neckbreaker, but Warthog counters with a side suplex and tags out to Warhead. They hit Vonya with a double kick to the midsection, then Warthog leaves the ring. Vonya bites Warhead, then picks her up. Warhead blocks being put on a turnbuckle, then nails Vonya with a thrust kick to the head. She runs into the ropes; Night Crawler pulls down the top rope. Warhead quickly reenters the ring; Vonya hits her with a shoulderblock, going back ont he offensive. Warhead soon retakes control with a spinning backbreaker. Vonya soon regains the upper hand with a reverse neckbreaker. She shortly runs into the ropes. Warhead catches her in a small package on the rebound, turning the match back around. After three minutes she whips Vonya into the ropes. Vonya hits her with a clothesline off the rebound, turning the match around again. She quickly tags out to Valkyrie. Warhead greets her with a Soviet suplex, retaking control. Valkyrie shortly ends her onslaught with a rolling kick. Warhead quickly regains her edge by throwing Valkyrie out of the ring. She goes to the floor, then nails Valkyrie in a double underhook backbreaker. She throws Valkyrie into the ringpost, then executes the Bombs Away. Valkyrie is dazed; Warhead follows up with a spinning backbreaker and reenters the ring. Valkyrie barely rolls back in the ring in time. Warhead executes a flying cross body block, but her momentum reverses the pin. She kicks out in two, then slaps Valkyrie. Valkyrie shortly regains enough momentum to kick Warhead and tag out to Vonya. Warthog enters the ring; she and Warhead nail Vonya with a double kick to the midsection, regaing the advantage. After two-and-a-half minutes Warhead runs into the ropes. Vonya hits her with an elbow and tags out to Valkyrie. Warhead blocks being put on a turnbuckle, then sidesteps a backflip cradle. She rolls Valkyrie up; Valkyrie kicks out in two. She grabs the Prince's cane, then mistakes Warhead's head for a baseball. Warhead crumples to the mat; Valkyrie covers her. Warhead kicks out after three and accuses the ref of making a fast count. Axeln counter-challenged Turner to an I-Quit match. Turner in turn proposed a lumberjack stipulation. Axeln agreed, and in a few moments that match will happen. Let's go now to the ring."
