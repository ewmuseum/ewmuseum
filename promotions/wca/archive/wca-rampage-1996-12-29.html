WCA Rampage 12/28

29 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION RAMPAGE 12/28/96 Stone Cold Tour Live from Port-au-Prince, Haiti -----Main Event----- <Tag Team Titles Match> "Psycho" Vicki V / Christy Cooper v. Bounty Hunters Valkyrie v. Lovely Liz Strohlheim Semiramis v. Freedom Angel Hunter v. Joanne Watson Femme Fatale v. Tammi Cooper <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.> <Fireworks explode as the camera shows a top-down view of the ring, highlighting the maroon WCA logo on the canvas. The camera descends, panning to the right and leveling off, showing the capacity crowd. It focuses on the end of the aisle, where 'WCA' frames the entrance to the locker room. Several WCA officials walk down the aisle.> ---------- Femme Fatale (w/ Snakebite) v. Tammi Cooper (w/ Donna Star and Double D - Debbie Diamond) ---------- Both women lock up, then Fatale executes an ipponzei. Cooper kicks out in two. She rolls away from an elbowdrop. Fatale sits up, then gets up. Cooper sends her to a corner and runs in shoulder-first, nailing Fatale. Fatale absorbs the blow and shoves Cooper back, then takes her down with a rolling kick, taking control of the match. She shortly takes Cooper down via a double underhook suplex. Cooper sits up, then picks herself up. She catches Fatale in a short lariat and covers; one, two, kickout. Cooper stands up and executes a spinning elbowdrop, taking charge. She shortly throws Fatale out of the ring, then joins her on the floor. Fatale grabs her neck and nails her with a chop. She reenters the ring, followed by Cooper. Fatale sends her to the far ropes, then ducks a clothesline. Cooper rebounds and clotheslines Fatale from the back, resuming her offensive. After an intense 5 minutes Cooper runs into the ropes. Fatale knocks her down with an elbow to the face, surprising her. Cooper sits up slowly, then gets up. Fatale sends her to a corner, then runs in shoulder-first, turning the match around. She soon sends Cooper to another corner. Cooper charges back out with an elbow to the face. She stops Fatale's spin, then takes her down in a double underhook piledriver, turning things back around. After another 5 minutes Fatale blocks a powerbomb. She executes an ipponzei; Cooper kicks out in two. Cooper prevents a pick-up; Fatale settles for a leg lariat, turning the match around. Cooper shortly ducks a diving elbowsmash. She sends Fatale to the ropes, then knocks her down with an elbow. Cooper drops down for the cover and pins Fatale. (11:00) ---------- Angel Hunter (w/ Janet Strong) v. Joanne Watson (w/ Samantha Jones and Jane Gordon) ---------- Both women lock up, then Watson pulls Hunter to the ropes and chokes her against them. She switches hands at the first four count, then lets go at the second and confronts the ref. Hunter kicks her in the head, then gets the crowd to cheer for her. She runs into the ropes; Watson decks her with a clothesline, taking charge. She soon sends Hunter to the ropes. Hunter comes back with a backdrop. She drop-kicks Watson in the knee, turning things around. Watson shortly reverses a whip to the ropes. She nails Hunter with an elbow, then pulls her to the ropes and runs her face along the top cable. Watson lets go. Hunter counters a reverse cradle with a sit-down; Watson kicks out in two and gets up. She reverses a whip to the ropes, then dodges a drop-kick to the knee. She keeps Hunter down with a lariat. Hunter gets up and executes an enzuilariato. She puts Watson in leg scissors, going back on the offensive. After 3 minutes she sends Watson to a corner, then runs in shoulder-first. Watson moves out of the way; Hunter stops herself. Watson nails her with a kick to the head, then runs into the far ropes. She backdrops Hunter. Hunter picks herself up; Watson pulls her to the side ropes. Hunter blocks a choke against them; Watson blocks a forearm smash. She sends Hunter to the far ropes; Hunter returns with a clothesline, dumping Watson out of the ring. Hunter steps back as the ref begins the count; one, two, three, Watson reenters the ring. Hunter takes her down with a jumping side kick, then goes for the pin; one, two, kickout. Both women get back on their feet. Hunter slaps Watson; Watson takes her down with a kick to the head. She heads to the top rope, then hammers Hunter with a flying elbowdrop, taking control of the match. Hunter soon blocks an armlock leglock. She gets back on her feet, then rocks Watson with a kick to the head. She follows up with a legsweep. Watson kicks Hunter's feet out from under her, then gets up and executes a double underhook suplex, resuming her assault. Hunter soon reverses a whip to the ropes. She takes Watson down with an enzuilairato, turning the match around. Watson soon moves away from a forearm to the back, then goes to the top ropes. She nails Hunter with a flying spinning leg lariat, turning things back around. Hunter shortly reverses a slingshot facebuster into a vertical suplex. She gets up first, then waits on Watson. Hunter keeps her down with neck-scissors, retaking control of the match. Watson soon backdrops out of a front facelock. She applies a spinning toe hold; Hunter reaches back and grabs the bottom rope. Watson lets go reluctantly. She pulls Hunter further into the ring, then applies an armbar, turning things around again. Watson soon nails her with a kick to the head. She follows up with a jumping side kick, going back on the offensive. After 2 minutes Watson blocks an abdominal stretch with a hiptoss. She applies a spinning toe hold. Watson lets go after 23 seconds. Hunter slowly gets back up; Watson suddenly heads to the apron. Hunter sidesteps her slingshot. Watson picks herself up; Hunter nails her with an enzuigiri, resuming her assault. After 2 minutes Hunter applies an armlock hammerlock. Watson quickly breaks the hold and slips behind her, applying the Full Nelson. Hunter quickly breaks the hold; Watson applies an armbar, turning the match around. Gordon shortly hands her golf club to her, then distracts the ref as Watson uses Hunter's head as a golf ball. Watson slides the club out of the ring, then covers Hunter, grabbing a handful of tights. Gordon lets the ref go; he turns and drops down; one, two, three. (20:02) ---------- Semiramis (w/ Red, Black, and Panthra) v. Freedom ---------- Freedom runs into the ropes, then misses with a clothesline. Semiramis catches her in a Guerrilla Press, then applies a grapevine. Freedom manages to reach the ropes; Semiramis reluctantly lets go. Freedom gets back up, then runs into the side ropes. Semiramis ducks a clothesline, then rocks Freedom with a shoulderblock. She follows up with a faceslam, taking control of the match. Freedom shortly catches her in a rolling reverse cradle; one, two, Panthra punches Freedom in the head. Freedom gets back up, then nails Semiramis with a kneelift, taking charge. Semiramis shortly reverses a whip to a corner. Freedom rebounds off the turnbuckles; Semiramis nails her with a double-axhandle chop, retaking control. She soon runs into the ropes. Freedom ducks a clothesline, then turns around and nails Semiramis with a savate kick. She follows up with a chop to the neck, turning things back around. After 3 minutes Semiramis blocks a gutwrench suplex. She executes a crucifix, pinning Freedom. (5:26) ---------- Valkyrie (w/ "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln) v. Lovely Liz Strohlheim (w/ Karen "Bugs" Huffman and Sweet Sue) ---------- Both women lock up, then Strohlheim executes a snap mare. Valkyrie gets up and reverses a whip to a corner. She nails Strohlheim with a mule kick, taking control of the match. After ninety seconds Strohlheim blocks the Valkyrian Storm. She ducks a diving elbowsmash, then nails Valkyrie with a spin kick. She follows up with another, turning the match around. Valkyrie shortly dodges a flying bulldog. She taunts Strohlheim as the latter picks herself up. Valkyrie nails her with an enzuigiri to the face, retaking control of the match. Strohlhiem shortly blocks a dragon suplex, then throws Valkyrie out of the ring. She joins her on the floor, then sends her into the guardrail. Valkyrie shrugs off a bulldog, then slams Strohlheim's face onto the guardrail twice. Valkyrie gets on the apron, then takes Strohlheim down with flying headscissors. She throws Strohlheim back into the ring, then rolls herself back in. Strohlheim reverses a whip to the ropes, then nails her with an enzuigiri to the face, retaking control of the match. Valkyrie shortly reverses a whip to a corner, then runs in shoulder-first. She nails Strohlheim hard, going back on the offensive. Strohlheim soon nails her with a back heel kick. Valkyrie blocks a side suplex; Strohlheim reverses a small package. Valkyrie kicks out in two. She dodges a kneelift; Strohlheim lands on her back. She sits up, then gets on her feet. Valkyrie meets her with an enzuigiri to the face, resuming her assault. Strohlheim very shortly blocks a reverse neckbreaker. She executes an enzuigiri to the face, going back on the offensive. After 2 minutes she sends Valkyrie to the ropes. Valkyrie comes back with a thrust kick to the head. She applies a half Boston, turning the match around. Strohlheim shortly blocks a dragon suplex, then runs into the ropes. She nails Valkyrie with a kick, retaking control. Valkyrie shortly dodges a flying bulldog. She hammers Strohlheim with a handspring moonsault; one, two, thr-kickout. Valkyrie follows up with an elbowdrop from the second turnbuckle, going back on the attack. She shortly sends Strohlheim to the ropes. Strohlheim returns with a hard clothesline, then goes for the pin. Valkyrie kicks out in two and gets back on her feet. Strohlheim takes her down with a snap suplex, retaking control. After a minute she plants Valkyrie with a brain buster, then pins her with the Schoolgirl Pin. (13:01) ===== Birmingham: "Lovely Liz Strohlheim slowly gains the edge in her match with Valkyrie, then plants her on the mat in a variation of the Valkyrian Storm and covers with the simple-yet-effective Schoolgirl Pin, winning the match. Up next is tonight's main event, a match for the Tag Team titles between the champions 'Psycho' Vicki V and Christy Cooper, and the challengers the Bounty Hunters. The latter team originally wanted to face Darkness, Inc., but the Psychotic One and Cooper nixed that idea with a stunning upset on December 14th. They accepted the defense against the Bounty Hunters nonetheless, and now have their first challenge. Let's go to the ring." Ring Announcer: "Ladies and gentlemen, tonight's main event is for the Tag Team championships! Introducing the challengers: representing Panthra's Army and accompanied to the ring by Semiramis and their manager Panthra, with a combined weight of 313 pounds, the Bounty Hunters!" <Panthra's Army marches down the aisle to the ring, ignoring the booing fans. Black and Red wear their customary camouflage wresting singlets with masks of their respective colors. They casually swing their aluminum ball bats. They enter the ring.> "Their opponents and current Tag Team champions: representing the Posse of Punishment and accompanied to the ring by Dr. Insane and the Dream Master, with a combined weight of 428 pounds, 'Psycho' Vicki V and Christy Cooper!" <The Posse members walk down the aisle, ignoring the booing fans. Dr. Insane pulls the leather-clad Vicki to the ring on a leash. They enter the ring. Cooper raises her hands up, inciting the crowd further.> Birmingham's voice: "Panthra and Semiramis glare at the Posse members, both having wrestled them exhaustively. Dr. Insane unleashes the Psychotic One, then quickly leaves the ring. Cooper and Vicki hand over the Tag belts as Panthra and Semiramis bows out. Referee Tina Nolke displays the belts, then hands them to the ring attendant as the Dream Master leaves. The Bounty Hunters gives Panthra their bats, then Black goes to the Bounty Hunters' corner; Cooper takes up the opposite one. Vicki and Red circle each other - and there's the bell! Both women lock up! Red chops V, then runs into the ropes. She ducks V's clothesline, then blocks a back heel kick. V counters an ipponzei with a lariat. She catches Red in a back - bridging back suplex, but can only get a two count. V gets up and nails her with a thrust kick to the head. Red falls into the ropes; V nails her with a spinning mule kick. She sends Red to the far ropes, then - ducks a clothesline, spins around, and clotheslines Red. She catches her fall, then sends her to the side ropes. Red comes back with a slap! V ducks her lariat, though. "Red reverses a whip to a corner - and meets V's run out with an elbow! She decks V with a punch, then tags in Black. They send V to the ropes, then take her down with a double clothesline. Vicki gets back up - and gets hit with another double clothesline! Red kicks her in the head. V gets back up, angry as heck. Black takes her down with an enzuigiri as Nolke forces red out. Black applies a cobra clutch. Cooper enters the ring and makes the save! Black ducks her lariat; Cooper leaves as Black executes an ipponzei. V kicks out in two. "She gets up and blocks an armbar, then runs into the ropes. Black backdrops her! V gets up; Black chops her and - V reverses a whip to the ropes! She blocks a full nelson; Black settles for a kick to the leg. She chops V, then sends her to the ropes. Black executes a headlock takedown - and turns it into a front facelock! V kicks her leg, hoping to - and does! - reach a rope. Black shoves V out of the ring! Temper, temper. Nolke begins the count; one, two, three, four, five, Vicki reenters the ring. She executes a belly-to-belly - but only gets a one count. "Both women stand up. V executes - Black reverses it into a vertical suplex! I think that took more out of her than Vicki! V gets back up. Black gets on her feet - and backdrops out of a - wait, V's going for a sunset flip! Black falls back; one, two, kickout. Vicki picks Black up and hammers her in a front slam. She gets up, then tags in Cooper as Black gets back up. They surround Black - Black ducks V's thrust kick, letting V nail Cooper! Red enters the ring and tackles V as Black catches Cooper in an ipponzei; one, two, shoulder up. V suplexes Red onto the guardrail, then reenters the ring as Cooper sends Black to the ropes. V nails her with an elbowsmash - here comes Red! She throws Vicki out of the ring, then returns to her corner. "Black tags her in, then they send Cooper to the ropes. They nail her with a double elbowsmash, then Black leaves as Red applies a grapevine. Cooper reaches back and grabs the bottom rope. Red reluctantly lets go. Christy pulls herself up by the ropes. Red runs into the ropes, then charges Cooper and drives her knee into the small of her back! And again! Red sends Cooper to the ropes. She ducks a clothesline and spins around, then stops Cooper with a kick. She applies a front facelock. Cooper backdrops out of it! "Black enters - Nolke cuts V off as the Bounty Hunters nail Cooper with adouble elbowsmash! They follow up with a double kick to the midsection, then Black leaves as Red executes an ipponzei. Nolke turns and sees the pin; one, two, shoulder up. Red stands up and stomps on Cooper, then pulls her up. She sends her to a corner and charges in - into a boot to the face! Cooper takes Red down with a lariat. Red gets back up - Cooper executes a tiger driver; one, two, thr-kickout! Red blocks short arm scissors and stands up. Cooper gets up and blocks a standing wristlock. "She applies a double chickenwing. Red slowly advances to the ropes, then reaches one leg out and snags one! She blocks a pickup - and tags out to Black! Black runs into the ropes - Cooper stops her gently, then applies an armbar. Black quickly breaks the hold. Cooper tags out to V, who nails Black with a swinging punch! She follows up with a bulldog! Black sits up. She gets up - here comes Cooper! Red tries to enter, but gets cut off. Black ducks Cooper's lari - Cooper gets Vicki instead! Mistakes like that are costly! Black takes V down with a short lariat; one, two, shoulder up. "Both women stand back up. Black grabs V's shoulder and chops the other side of the neck - and again! She follows up with an earringer. Vicki slowly gets back up. She executes a Soviet suplex. Black gets up - and ducks a thrust kick to the head! She counters a fisherman with a small package, but only gets a two count. Both women stand up; Black sends V to the ropes. Vicki comes back with an elbow to the face! Black lands hard; Vicki heads to the top rope. She comes down with a flying elbowdrop, then hooks a leg; one, two, kickout. "V turns Black over and hooks the arms. She nails Black with a double underhook piledriver! V throws Black out of the ring! She leaves goes through the side ropes as Black slowly gets up. Black blocks a front-layout suplex, then executes an ipponzei! She keeps V down with neck-scissors. V gets back up and blocks a kick to the leg. Black responds with a jumping side kick! V kicks Black back into the guardrail. Black circles her, then - V backdrops out of an earringer! Black reenters the ring and tags in Red as V enters. Cooper enters and throws Black out! Fans, we are out of time. We'll present the conclusion of this match on Knights Monday night. I'm John Birmingham; have a good evening!" <Closing credits appear.>
