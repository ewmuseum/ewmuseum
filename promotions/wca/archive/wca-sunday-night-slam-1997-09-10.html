WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION (http.// kessel.simplenet.com/WCA/ ) SUNDAY NIGHT SLAM Live from Toronto, Quebec 9/7/97 -----Main Event----- "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln v. Joanne Watson (Best of 7 Match 2) Megan van Drake v. Debbie Angel Queen Amanda v. Liberty Samantha Strong v. Sellyna Shriek Ashley Hamilton v. Blood (Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.) (Fireworks explode as the camera shows the crimson WCA logo on the white ring canvas. The camera descends rapidly, then levels off facing the aisle. The camera swerves left, panning out over the capacity crowd and circling the ring, ending its focus on the iridescent three-dimensional WCA letters bracketing the entrance to the backstage area. The referee for the first match heads to the ring. The camera focuses on commentators Anthony Blake and Mike Sharpe.) SHARPE: "Good evening, ladies and gentlemen, and welcome to Sunday Night Slam! Tonight in our main event 'the Intimidator' Amber Axeln takes on Joanne Watson in Match 2 of their Best of 7 series. The Intimidator won the first match by disqualification, something that will add fuel to the fire in tonight's match. Plus, Megan van Drake takes on Debbie Angel, Queen Amanda faces Liberty, and Samantha Strong takes on Sellyna Shriek. BLAKE: "Let's go to the ring for the first match-up, between Ashley Hamilton, and Blood of the Henchmen of Hades." -------------------------------------------------- Ashley Hamilton (w/ Precious) vs. Blood (w/ Red) -------------------------------------------------- [The bell rings] BLAKE: "And we're off! And Blood immediately hits Hamilton with a dropkick; Hamilton back onto her feet. Not for long, though; Blood takes her down again, this time with a chop, and locks her into a front facelock. The Henchmen haven't had much success lately here in the WCA, but maybe Blood is the woman to redress that fact; she's a sound wrestler, and has a army of friends to help her out. Hamilton back on her feet, but Blood shifts hold to a short arm scissors. Hamilto trying to get out, but a poke in the eye soon stops her momentum, and Blood clamps on another hold, this time a bear hug; a bit too early for that though, and Hamilton makes it to the ropes with relative ease. She swings at Blood, but Blood makes the block and boom! She takes down Hamilton with a Belly-to-back suplex. This is a good period for Blood, and she continues it by nailing a ropeflip moonsault! This could be it! No, only got a two count." "And Blood's enraged by that, and hurls Hamilton out of the ring, shouts at referee Esther Siler, and follows her out, only to be met by a succession of rights from Hamilton. Hamilton slings Blood back into the ring, and gets back in herself. She quickly nails Blood in the stomach , locks the arms.....well executed double-underhook suplex. Blood back up, but not for long! Nasty clothesline there from Hamilton, and she clamps on a chinlock submission hold. Blood is crawling towards the ropes, and just about makes it. Hamilton hauls her up, but receives a rake to the eyes for her efforts. Blood pushes her away, but misses with the dropkick after Hamilton steps to one side. Hamilton whips Blood into the ropes...big shoulderblock from Hamilton! And the crowd are starting to get warmed up here! Hamilton with another whip, this time it's reversed, and Blood hurls her opponent out of the ring for a second time!" "And we've got a war on the floor here! Hamilton and Blood trading blow, but Blood's winning the battle. She boots Hamilton in the stomach, and then whips her into the rails! Blood laughing at her opponent, runs at her, and...oh my! Blood went for a flying forearm, but Hamilton ducked, and Blood came down hard on the concrete behind the barrier. That may be it for this encounter! Hamilton retrieves Blood, and rolls her bak into the ring." "Hamilton now stomping away on the prone Blood; she goes to the ropes and executes a picture-perfect springboard splash! That might be it! No, the ref only got to two. Hamilton looking frustrated at her inability to put away Blood; she get up and runs to the ropes again, where's she's been tripped up by Red! The Blood's partner couldn't keep her hands off the match, and the ref saw it; she's disqualifying Blood; Hamilton wins by DQ." "Red retrieves Blood and makes her way to the backstage area, obviously pleased with herself...I think the Henchmen are counting this as something of a moral victory. Hamilton, however, is enraged in the ring, even though she won the match. Still, when you fight the Henchmen, you've got to be prepared to fight them all." "Next up: Hamilton's stablemate Samantha Strong takes on the vicious Sellyna Shriek, after this commercial break." [screen fades to commercial] BLAKE: "Samantha Strong better be on her guard here, as everyone is well aware of the joy Sellyna Shriek takes at seeing her opponents suffer; she's not only got to be worried about winning the match, but also with leaving the ring with her health. Let's go to the ring. --------------------------------------- Samantha Strong (w/Precious) vs. Sellyna Shriek (w/Snakebite) --------------------------------------- BLAKE: "The bell has rung, and we're underway! And immediately Shriek goes on the offensive, punching away at Strong! Shriek takes her down with the headscissors, but Strong immediately sits up! The crowd appreciated that one, but Shriek certainly didn't, whipping Strong into the corner! Shriek runs in....but Strong ducks, and Shriek clatters into the turnbuckles! She stagger out of the corner, dazed." "Strong now setting up Shriek; goodness, there's the Body Drop! Strong nails the Body Drop early on, and this one is over! Tina Nolke counts...one, two, and Shriek kicks out! Unbelievable enduarance on the part of Sellyna Shriek there! Strong hauls her back up onto her feet, and run to the ropes. Strong goes for a clothesline, but Shriek ducks and clamps on a sleeperhold! Strong attempting to get to the ropes, but Shriek has this hold on good.....however, Strong's power is telling and she's almost there...got them, and Shriek breaks the hold surprisingly quickly." "And now Shriek starts waling away on Strong with kicks and punches, howling at her in the process; Shriek is prone to these 'wild moods', and when it happens, get out of her way. And now Shriek hurls Strong to the floor, and follws her out there. She slugs Strong a couple of times, and tries to whip her into the ringposts, but it's reversed! Shriek collides with the solid stell of the ringpost with a resounding thud! And now, what's Strong up to? Oh no, she's getting rid of the padded mats at ringside; looks like Strong is determined to show her vicious side as well. She hauls Shriek up...woah! Body Drop on the concrete floor! Shriek has been busted wide open, there's blood everywhere!" "Strong throws her back in the ring, and goes straight for the top turnbuckle. This is a mistake, she should be covering Sellyna Shriek, not taking another high-risk move; this could backfire easily on Samantha, and it does! Shriek gets back up, and hurls her off the top buckle! The ref asks Shriek if she wants to continue, and Shriek's reply is unrepeatable on a family show like this. Ugh, there's blood all over the ring. Strong getting back on to her feet, only to be met by boot to the face! And Strong...oh no, Strong rakes Shriek's face, opening her wounds even further! What a brutal encounter this is....Strong whips her to the ropes, and is going for a backdrop; Shriek reverses with a DDT! And there's the Psychic Venom! Shriek's got it clamped on in the middle of the ring, and it looks like there's no escape for Strong; she's trying to fight the hold, but the pain must be intense; Tina Nolke raises her arm once, and it falls. She raises it a second time...again, Strong's arm drops. One more time and she's out of there; Nolke raises the arm, and it drops! Shriek wins this gruelling encounter, but at what price? She's still bleeding profusely from that Body Drop on the concrete, and we're going to have to get some people out here to clean up the ring." [The screen suddenly goes blank, then about half an hour later, it comes to life again, and a flustered Anthony Blake can be seen.] BLAKE: "Fans, I've got to apologise; our satellite link went down there and I'm afraid to say we've missed the broadcast of two exciting matches, for which we apologise profoundly. He's a re-cap of what you missed." ------------------------------------- Liberty (w/Mistress of Death) vs. Queen Amanda (w/Ratt Fink) ------------------------------------- A closely fought battle, between the young upstart Queen Amanda and the wily veteran, Liberty. Liberty began with an early volley of devastating offence, and with the crowd clearly behind her, almost pinned Amamnda on several occasions; however, a reversed irish whip to the corner slowed Liberty just enough for Amanda to get some offence of her own in, pounding Liberty with a tiger suplex, and a slingshot splash. This did not last, however, with Liberty coming back strong with a succession of dropkicks, suplexs and a splash; but the veteran got a big surprise when she charged into the corner and was met with a hard knee. Amanda followed this up with a hard elbow, and got the surprise win to the dismay of the crowd. ------------------------------------------ Megan Von Drake vs. Debbie Angel (w/ Precious) ------------------------------------------ Another see-saw battle ensued here between one member of the Doll's House, and impressive newcomer Magan Von Drake. The action was to and fro until Drake hit a combination of moves and moved the action to the outside, where she pummeled Angel into the ringpost and guard rail. This assualt proved too much for Angel, who succumbed to Drake finisher, the Canadian Maple Leaf after being in the hold for 40 seconds. BLAKE: "Yet again, we aplogise for the interruption of our programme; but we must move on, to the highlight of our programme, the main event between Joanne Watson, and Amber Axeln; both women have had a lot to say about each other in the past couple of weeks, and none of it pleasant. The bookmakers favour Axeln fairly heavily in this one, Mike: do you agree?" SHARPE: "My Vegas contacts tell me they expect to see the Intimidator sweep the series. Axeln is said to have better ring skills and supposedly more dedication in her favour. But her recent losses to both the Psycho Queen and to Watson herself lessens her chances of a total sweep. Besides, what Watson may lack in skill she makes up in both attitude and size. This series will probably go to the seventh match. BLAKE: "Both opponent are in the ring, so let's get the action under way!" -------------------------------------- "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln (w/ Click II) vs. Joanne Watson (w/ Jane Gordon) -------------------------------------- BLAKE: "And we're off! Both women circling each other warily; they may not like each othe, but both respect the other's wrestling skills. They hook up, and immediately Watson goes to her sizable bag of rule-bending manauvers, poking Axeln in the eye. She follows this up with a couple of hard rights, the nails her with a leg lariat! Axeln back on her feet; not for long, though, as Watson takes her off her feet, and over the ropes with a powerful clothesline. Watson goes outside, and lands another huge lariat on Axeln; if Watson keeps this up, it could be over very soon. Hang on a moment, here comes Liberty from around the corner, taunting Watson. The pair shout abuse at each other, allowing Axeln time to recover; Axeln with a punch to the kidneys, and whips Watson into the railings, then slams her head into the steel steps! And now, she rolls Watson back into the ring. Mike, even though the crowd is certainly behind the Click every time thay wrestle, they are quite apt at bending the rules themselves, don't you think?" SHARPE: "Well, the Click's background certainly isn't that of Girl Scouts. Each and every one of them, from Muniz to Shannon Stevens, started their professional careers as rulebreakers, and it just comes naturally. I imagine the crowd still baacks them because rulebreaking is a relative thing nowadays. It's the double standard the Assassin was talking about the other day. BLAKE: "We're back in the ring now; Axeln slings Watson into the corner, and follows her in with a brutal clothesline; Watson is left slumping on the floor after that one. Axeln slings her into the ropes, and comes back with a back heel kick, and again Watson finds herself on the mat. The Intimidator runs to the ropes, and executes a good-looking legdrop; that could be it right there...no, the ref only gets to two. Axeln hauls up Watson, slings her to the ropes; it's reversed, and Amber gets thrown outside again, landing nastily on her shoulder! Watson taking her time to recover, then jumps outside again. Watson keen to take it to the outside in this match." SHARPE: "Watson may be counting on the Hired Help to, er, help her out in this match, no pun intended." "And now they're both going toe-to-toe on the floor, and Watson's getting the upper hand! Watson, with a vicious kneelift, and whips Axeln into the ringpost, regaining the advantage. Not for long though, Janet Muniz having words with Watson this time, and again this is giving Axeln time to recover. Smart action here by the Click" SHARPE: "That's one of the biggest strenghts of the Click II. They've stuck by each other through thick and thin, although Helena Knightsbridge and Shannon Stevens might question that. But the core group of Muniz, Axeln, et. al. always show up in these matches together to help balance out the odds, especially with the Posse or the Henchmen around." "Axeln nails Watson with a blow to the back, and slings her into the ring. Axeln comes back in with a springboard clothesline, and Watson's in trouble! Again Watson gets thrown to the ropes, but this time she ducks under Axeln's clothesline, and nails her with a hard right on return. Watson to the ropes, but she's been tripped by Liberty! And Watson is enraged! From behind....Axeln with a punch, then a kick....and a marvelous fisherman's suplex! This could be it! One, Two, Three, Axeln wins! And Watson is on her feet, complaining to the ref about a fast count as the Intimidator leaves to the cheers of the fans. Mike, did that look fast to you?" SHARPE: "It certainly did, Anthony, and that leads me to ask, what the heck is going on here? I mean, Saturday night you've got Julian Pace showing blatant bias in the Shannon SStevens / Shelly Rage match, and now this apparent lightning fast count. I think Mark White has some explaining to do regarding his referees." BLAKE: "Well, that's all we've got time for here; coming up on Theatre of War, we've got Liberty going up against a member of her former stable, 'Nasty Girl' Amber Jane, a TV title match between Monique and Jenny Black, and much, much more. I'm Anthony Blake, thanks for watching!"
