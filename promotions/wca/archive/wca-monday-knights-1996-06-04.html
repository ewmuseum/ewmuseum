WCA Monday Knights 6/3

4 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION MONDAY KNIGHTS Live from the Lake Arena in Springfield, Missouri 6/3/96 -----Main Event----- <Mixed Tag Team Titles Match> Li'l Cheetah / WarHawk v. Zero Tolerance <North American Tag Team Titles Match> Japanese Dragons v. Hardcore Heaven Richie Rich v. CrUsHa - non-title "The Destroyer" Shawn Mancubus v. Platinum Powers- non-title "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln v. Double J "The Blade" Larry Brown v. Lucky Joe Lovely Liz Strohlheim v. "Heat Wave" Heather Hetfield New Masters of Violence v. Fatal Justice Thunderlips v. Jeff "Hearbreak Boy" Blackwell Sweet Sue v. Samantha Spencer-Lane -------------------- Sweet Sue (MGR: Tall Troll) v. Samantha Spencer-Lane (MGR: Fred Scuttle) -------------------- Sue assumes control of the match with a Guerila Press. Spencer-Lane shortly ends her offensive with a reverse neckbreaker. She begins her own with a brain buster. Sue quickly ends it by throwing her out of the ring. Spencere-Lane reenters at the six count; she stands toe-to-toe with Sue, then Sue takes control of the match with a double chickenwing. Spencer-Lane shortly turns the match around with a flying somersault bodyblock. Sue gradually makes a comeback, then takes control of the match at the seven minute mark with a choke against the ropes. Spencer-Lane ends her offensive a minute later with another brain buster. Sue remains on the defensive for two minutes, then turns the match back around with a side suplex. She pummels Spencer-Lane for five minutes, then nails her with the Jersey Jaw Jacker and pins her. (15:00) -------------------- Thunderlips v. Jeff "Heartbreak Boy" Blackwell (w/ Ghetto Blaster, Cindy Simmons, and Chris Cliff) -------------------- Thunderlips goes on the offensive with a belly-to-back suplex. Blackwell abruptly turns the match around after 3-and -a-half minutes with a legdrop. He soon goes for an armbar, but Thunderlips blocks it and turns the match around with an avalanche. Blackwell soon turns the match back around with a clothesline. He goes on the offensive for almost three minutes, then nails Thunderlips with a flying forearm and pins him. (7:52) -------------------- New Masters of Violence (Demolisher and Brian Simpson) (MGR: Mistress of Death w/ Diego) v. Fatal Justice (Justice and the Executioner) -------------------- Demolisher and the Executioner start. The Executioner goes on the offensive with a hiptoss. He shortly tags out ot Justice, who conitnues their offensive with a huge kneelift. Demolisher evens the match up after four minutes with a swinging neckbreaker. Both men stand toe-to-toe with each other. Demolisher shortly tags out to Simpson. The stand-off continues with Fatal Justice making frequent tags. At the ten minute mark, The Executioner breaks the stalemate with a fist to the midsection. Justice enters the ring and they both double team Simpson. Demolisher enters the ring and a four-way brawl ensues. The ref shortly gets the two illegal men out; the Executioner and Simpson trade blows in the ring. At the thirtenn minute mark, the Executioner takes control of the match with a superkick. Simpson very shortly tags out to Demolisher. The Executioner greets him with an elbow to the back of the head, but Demolisher evens the match up with a single-leg takedown. He shortly tags out to Justice, who takes control of the match with a shoulderblock. Fatal Justice makes another tag duirng their offensive; Demolisher tags out at almost the same time to Simpson. Sixteen minutes into the match, the Mistress of Death hands Simpson her Singapore cane. He nails the Executioner with it and goes for the pin. Justice makes the save; Demolisher enters the ring and together they nail the Executioner with the Superbomb. Simpson goes for the pin and wins the match. (16:56) -------------------- Lovely Liz Strohlheim v. "Heat Wave" Heather Hetfield (MGR: Jason Hate) -------------------- Strohlheim goes on the offensive with an enzuigiri. Hetfield shorlty turns hte match around with a fistdrop. She remains in control for three minutes, then Strohlheim tunrs the match around with an elbow to the throat. Hetfield begins to even the match up almost a minute later. She whips Strohlheim into the ropes, then takes her down with an elbow. She goes for the cover and pins Strohlheim. (5:34) -------------------- "The Blade" Larry Brown v. Lucky Joe -------------------- Borwn goes on the offensive with a lariat. He controls the match for over a minute before Joe counters a back suplex with a cross-body block and pins him. Brown complains about a fast count. (1:26) -------------------- "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln (MGR: The Prince) v. Double J -------------------- Axeln goes on the offensive with a ropeflip hiptoss. Double J almost immediatly starts to even the match up. She does a minute later with an over-the-shoulder stomachbreaker. She takes control of the match with a backdrop. Axeln begins to turn the match around 30 seconds later. Both women try to make the other submit with various holds in a highly scientific match. 5-and-a-half minutes into the match Double J goes on a high-impact-move offensive and solidifies her control. In desperation Axeln applies an octopus hold a minute later. She lets go after 14 seconds and nails Double J with the Doombomb, then pins her. (7:01) -Non-title Match- -------------------- Television champion "The Destroyer" Shawn Mancubus (MGR: Jason the Terribly Naughty One) v. Platinum Powers -------------------- Both Mancubus and "Sideshow" Bob Tuwilliger come to the ring when introduced. Jason grabs the mike from the announcer and demands President Kessler come to the ring. Kessler does so, and listens to Jason as he says the Cripplers are retiring from pro-wrestling to pursue "other sh*t." Jason continues to inform the audience that they'll give up thier titles; the Cripplers give the ref the belts. All thre men leave as Kessler stares at them, stunned. He then grabs the mic and announces that Christian Kingsworth will face "The Blade" Larry Brown for the Television title, and will schedule a tournament between the top four tag teams for the World Tag Team titles. This match with Powers will be thrown out. -Non-title- -------------------- North American Champion Richie Rich v. CrUsHa -------------------- Rich assumes control of the match with a snap suplex. A minute later CrUsHa turns the match around with a bulldog. Rich gradually evens the match up for the next three-and-a-half minutes, then both men stand toe-to-toe with each other. A minute later Rich takes control of the match with a slingshot. He assaults CrUsHa with a variety of high-impact blows, then three minutes later nails him wiht the Blank Check and pins him. (8:53) <North American Tag Team Titles Match> -------------------- Japanese Dragons (Super White Samurai and Super Blue Ninja) (MGR: "Duke" David Jones) v. Hardcore Heaven (Hardcore and X-Treme) -------------------- Ninja and Hardcore start. Ninja assumes control of the match with a side suplex. Hardcore shortly turns the match around with a lariat. He shortly tags out to X-Treme. Samurai enters the ring; together he and Ninja whip X-Treme into the ropes, then double backdrop him. Ninja nails him with a fist to the midsection, turning the match back around. Ninja soon tags out to Samurai; X-Treme uses the opportunity to take control of the match with an enzuigiri. He tags out to Hardcore a minute later. Samurai responds by tagging out to Ninja, who turns the match back around with an elbowsmash. Hardcore shortly retakes control with a headlock takedown. He shorlty thorws Ninja to the floor, then bodyslams him on the ringsteps. He throws Ninja back in the ring, then goes for the pin. Ninja kicks out at the last second and retakes control of the match with a back heel kick. He puts Hardcore in the Osaka Oasis, but X-Treme makes the save. He and Hardcore nail Ninja with the Decapitator, then Hardcore pins him. Hardcore Heaven are the new North American Tag Team champions. (6:51) <Mixed Tag Team Titles Match> -------------------- Li'l Cheetah / WarHawk (MGR: Tall Troll) v. Zero Tolerance ("Heat Wave" Heather Hetfield / Ruben Von Nazck) (MGR: Jason Hate) -------------------- Warhawk and Von Nazck start. The two men stand toe-to-toe with each other briefly, then Von Nazck goes on the offensive with a bulldog. WarHawk shortly turns the match around with a slingshot bodyblock. Von Nazck soon retakes control of the match with a snap suplex. WarHawk very shortly evens the match up with a vertical suplex. Both men stand toe-to-toe with each other again. Von Nazck breaks the stalemate with a fist to the midsection. He tags out to Hetfield; WarHawk tags out to Cheetah. Cheetah runs into the ropes, then Hetfield takes control of the match with a jumping DDT. She chokes Cheetah; the ref reaches five disqualifies Zero Tolerance. Li'l Cheetah and WarHawk remain the Mixed Tag Team champions. (3:34)
