"Nasty Girl" Amber Jane (w/ "Dominatress" Kathy Lynn) ---------- Jane carries her OWL Tag belt, while Blood has her OWL Women's belt. The ref starts the match; both women circle each other warily. They lock up briefly, then Blood whips Jane to the ropes. Jane rebounds and jumps a kick, then misses with a clothesline, rebounds again and backdrops Blood. She runs into the ropes; Blood stops her with a clothesline. She hammers Jane onto the mat with a piledriver, taking charge. She shortly runs into the ropes. Jane misses with a clohtesline, then spins around and executes a reverse cradle; one, two, kickout. Jane heads to the top rope, then executs a flying spinning leg lariat, going on the attack. Blood shortly whips her to a corner, then charges in. She smashes into Jane, then nails her with a double-axhandle chop, retaking control of the match. She soon attempts a high cross body; Jane catches her, then drops her into a backbreaker. Blood gets back on her feet; Jane sends her to the ropes. Blood rebounds and quickly executes the Gutwrench Suplex, then pins Jane. (2:58) ---------- "Destroyer" Dara Holt (w/ the Prince) v. "Dominatress" Kathy Lynn (w/ Jonathan Schiz) ---------- Both women lock up, then Lynn ducks neck-scissors. Holt ducks a savate kick and puts Lynn in an abdominal stretch, taking charge. Lynn shortly chokes her, then lets go in four. Lynn blocks Holt's chokehold, then sends her to the ropes. Holt returns with a clothesline, regaining her momentum. After two minutes Lynn reverses a whip to the ropes. She misses with a clothesline, then spins around, taking Holt down with a kick. Lynn covers her; Holt gets her shoulder up easily. Both women stand up; Holt staggers Lynn with a kneelift. She executes an inside cradle, putting her feet on the ropes. The ref sees it and disqualifies Holt. (2:57) (Non-Scheduled match) ---------- Sweet Sue v. Lovely Liz Strohlheim ---------- Both women lock up, then Strohlheim throws Sue out of the ring and follows her out. She takes Sue down in a back suplex. She follows up with an armdrag takedown and reenters the ring. Sue follows her in; Strohlheim puts her in a full nelson, going on the offensive. She very shortly reverses a whip to the ropes; Sue bumps the ref out of the ring, then returns with an elbow, turning things around. Strohlheim soon sends her to the ropes. She ducks a clothesline, then catches Sue with a high cross body. Sue kicks out in two; Strohlhiem quickly applies a full nelson, turning things back around. She soon attempts a backslide; Sue fights it, then wins; one, two, kickout. Sue puts Strohlheim in a cobra clutch, retaking control. Strohlheim shortly shortly catches Sue with a rolling kick. She follows up with another, then puts Sue back in a full nelson. Sue quickly breaks the hold, then executes a takeover suplex. She stomps on Sue, going back on the attack. She soon catches Strohlheim in a rolling reverse cradle, pinning her.
