WCA Theatre of War 12/5

6 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION THEATRE OF WAR 12/5/96 Live from St. Petersburg, Russia -----Main Event----- The Mistress of Death v. Christy Cooper Valkyrie v. The Assassin Delicious Dolls v. Bounty Hunters Donna Star v. Freedom Angel Hunter v. Haley "Comet" Hanson Double J v. Kassandra Hired Help v. Doomsday ---------- Hired Help (Samantha Jones and Jane Gordon) (w/ Joanne Watson) v. Doomsday ("Destroyer" Dara Holt and Vogue) (w/ the Prince) ---------- Jones and Vogue lock up, then Jones headbutts Vogue, taking control of the match. She soon tags out to Gordon. They send Vogue to the ropes, then deck her with a double clothesline. Vogue gets back up and gets sent back to the ropes; Jones and Gordon stop her with a double elbowsmash. Jones leaves as Gordon executes a German suplex; Vogue easily kicks out. Gordon executes a Frankensteiner, continuing the assault. Vogue shortly blocks a ropeburn. She hits Gordon with her forearm, then tags in Holt. They send Gordon to the ropes; Gordon ducks a double clothesline, then returns with a dual clothesline. Vogue rolls out of the ring as Gordon rakes Holt's face. She tags out to Jones. Holt rolls Jones up in an inside cradle; Jones kicks out in two. Jones counters a double leg-cradle with a double leg-hook, but only gets a two count. Jones sends Holt to the ropes, then decks her with a back heel kick, taking control. Holt very shortly counters a swinging neckbreaker with a side suplex, turning things around. She soon puts Jones in an abdominal stretch. Gordon makes the save; Jones tags out to her, then both send Holt to the ropes and double-backdrop her. They keep Holt down with a double back heel kick as the ref keeps Vogue out. Vogue feints past him and throws Jones out of the ring. Gordon ducks her spin kick, then sends her to the ropes. She backdrops Vogue; Vogue rolls out as Holt punches Gordon. She executes a crucifix; one, two, kickout. Both women stand up; Gordon blocks a legsweep faceslam and nails Holt with a spinning leg lariat, turning things back around. Holt shortly blocks a slap. Gordon ducks neck-scissors; Holt gets back up and plants her with a tiger driver. Gordon kicks out in two; Holt follows up with a Soviet suplex, going on the attack. After ninety seconds she tags out to Vogue. Vogue executes a swinging neckbreaker. Gordon slowly gets back up, then blocks a forearm smash. She tags out to Jones; Vogue nails her with a forearm smash, continuing the onslaught. Jones soon sends Vogue to the ropes. Watson trips Vogue; the ref warns her as Jones rakes Vogue's face. She follows up with a bulldog, turning the match back around. Vogue shortly tags out to Holt. Holt takes Jones down with a jack-knife powerbomb. She follows up with a tiger driver; one, two, shoulder up. Holt pulls Jones up, then sends her to the ropes and decks her with a clothesline, retaking control. Jones soon nails her with a lariat. She sends Holt to the ropes; Holt ducks a clothesline, then returns with the same. She covers Jones; one, two, thr-shoulder up. Jones counters a Guerrilla Press with a rolling reverse cradle, pinning Holt. (12:16) ---------- Double J v. Kassandra (w/ Miguel) ---------- Both women lock up, then Double J hiptosses Kassandra. Kassandra pops back up and reverses a whip to the ropes, then misses with an elbow. She ducks an elbow, then misses with a shoulderblock. She turns and misses with a clothesline; Double J stops and drives her knee into Kassandra's back. Kassandra blocks a pick-up with an elbowsmash and sends her to the ropes. Double J ducks a spin kick, then executes a slingshot facebuster. She runs into the ropes; Kassandra gets back up and hits her with a clothesline. She follows up with a dragon suplex, gaining the advantage. A minute later double J nails her in an over-the-shoulder stomachbreaker, turning things around. Kassandra shortly decks her with a spin kick, retaking control. Double J soon rolls away from a springboard legdrop. She gets up and does the Texas Two-Step, then throws Kassandra out of the ring. She goes through the ropes, then hops to the floor. Kassandra nails her with a double axhandle chop. double J responds with a piledriver. She reenters the ring. Kassandra reenters at the eight count. She reverses a small package; Double J kicks out in two. Kassandra head to the top rope. Double J counters a flying lariat with a fist to the midsection, going on the offensive. After 3 minutes she sends Kassandra to the ropes. Kassandra misses with a clothesline, misses again, misses again, and finally connects. She follows up with an earringer, retaking control. She shortly climbs to the top turnbuckle. Double J punches her in the stomach; Kassandra slips and falls onto the turnbuckle. Double J climbs up and takes her down via a superplex. She covers her; one, two, thr-shoulder up. Double J puts Kassandra in an STF. She quickly lets go, then applies the Texas Cruncher. Kassandra submits in 5 seconds. (9:56) <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.> <Fireworks explode as the camera shows a top-down view of the ring, highlighting the maroon WCA logo on the canvas. The camera descends, panning to the right and leveling off, showing the capacity crowd. It focuses on the end of the aisle, where 'WCA' frames the entrance to the locker room. Several WCA officials walk down the aisle.> ---------- Angel Hunter v. Haley "Comet" Hanson (w/ Big Norma Hudson and Mr. Canuck) ---------- Both women lock up, then Hanson executes a slingshot facebuster, going on the offensive. She shortly sends Hunter to the ropes; Hunter returns with a back heel kick, taking charge. Hanson shortly blocks short arm scissors. Both women stand up; Hanson executes a slingshot facebuster, retaking control. Hunter very shortly puts her back on the defensive with a drop-kick to the knee. She shortly sends Hanson to the ropes; Hanson comes back with a lariat, retaking control. The Dream Master walks down an aisle in the stands and takes a seat at ringside as Hunter ducks a short-lariat. She puts Hanson in an abdominal stretch, gaining the advantage. Hanson shortly counters a takeover suplex with a belly-to-belly; Hunter kicks out in two. Hanson applies a standing wristlock, turning things back around. After 2 minutes Hunter rolls away from a flying headbutt. She waits for Hanson to get back up, then sends her to the ropes. Hunter nails Hanson with a kick, turning the match around. After 2 1/2 minutes Hanson takes Hunter down with neck-scissors. She follows up with a backbreaker, turning the match back around. Nasty Tracy Turner comes to ringside carrying her kendo stick. She walks around the ring, avoiding Hudson. Hanson shortly hammers Hunter with a flying splash, then heads back to the top rope. Turner clips Hanson's knee with the stick; Hanson falls backward to the mat. Hunter rolls out of the way, then rolls back and pins Hanson. (11:07) ---------- Donna Star (w/ Tammi Cooper) v. Freedom ---------- Both women lock up. Star ducks a spinning leg lariat; Freedom blocks a back heel kick. Star reverses a whip to the ropes, then bodyslams Freedom. Freedom backdrops out of a front facelock; Star blocks a sleeper. Freedom counters an ipponzei with a lariat. She follows up with a flying fistdrop, gaining the advantage. After 2 minutes Star ducks a savate kick. She puts Freedom in a cobra clutch, turning the match around. Freedom shortly reverses a whip to the ropes. She jumps a shoulderblock; both women clothesline the other. Freedom gets up first. She plants Star with a tiger driver; Star kicks out with force in two. Freedom heads to the second turnbuckle, then comes down with an elbowdrop. She follows up with the 4th of July; Star kicks out at the last second. Star slowly gets to her feet; Freedom sends her to the ropes. Star comes back with a drop-kick. She rests on the floor, then gets back up. Freedom counters the Figure Four with a small package; Star easily kicks out. Both women stand up. Freedom punches Star in the abdomen, going on the attack. After 3 minutes Freedom runs into the ropes. Star ducks a clothesline, ducks an elbow, then takes her down with neck-scissors. She follows up with a lariat, turning the match around. Freedom shortly reverses a backdrop into a sunset flip; Star kicks out in two. Freedom follows up with a chop, retaking control. A minute later Star reverses a whip to a corner, then charges in. She nails Freedom, then follows up with a drop-kick to the knee, turning things back around. After 2 minutes she sends Freedom to a corner. Freedom charges back out with an elbow, turning the match around again. A minute later she sends Star to a corner. Star staggers back out, then chops Freedom. She follows up with a takeover suplex, turning the match back around. After a hard 4 minutes Star runs into the ropes. Freedom backdrops her out of the ring. Star reenters at the five count; Freedom sends her to a corner, then leaps in with a somersault splash, turning the match around. After 2 minutes Freedom backdrops Star out of the ring again. Freedom joins her on the floor. Star sends Freedom to the guardrail, then reenters the ring. Freedom reenters; Star sends her to the far ropes. Both women get hit with a dual clothesline. Star gets up first and runs into the ropes. She takes Freedom down with a drop-kick to the knee, then applies the Figure Four. Star realizes Freedom won't submit and reluctantly lets go in 26 seconds. She punishes Freedom with a knee to the back, starting a new assault. Freedom shortly blocks a figure-four sleeper. She slightly limps into the ropes; Cooper clips Freedom in the back of her knee. The ref disqualifies Star. (17:06) ---------- Delicious Dolls (Double D - Debbie Diamond and Tammi Cooper) (w/ Donna Star) v. Bounty Hunters (Black and Red) (w/ Panthra) ---------- Black ducks Cooper's short lariat; Cooper blocks an armbar. She executes a double underhook suplex, gaining the advantage. Black soon nails Cooper with an enzuigiri, turning things around. Cooper shortly counters a cobra clutch with an armdrag takedown and tags out to Diamond. Black takes Diamond down with a belly-to-belly suplex, continuing her assault. She shortly tags out to Red. Diamond backdrops out of a front facelock; Red gets up and reverses a whip to a corner. She runs in shoulder-first; Diamond moves out of the way. She kicks Red in the head, gaining the advantage. Red shortly retakes control with a boot to the face. Diamond shortly runs into the ropes. She nails Red with an elbow, then nails her with a short lariat, retaking control of the match. A minute later Red sends Diamond to the ropes. She jumps a kick; Diamond begs off. Red knocks her back, then hammers her with an avalanche. Diamond rolls back onto her knees and begs off; Red throws her out of the ring. She goes through the ropes and jumps to the floor. She ducks a spinning mule kick and knocks Diamond into the ringsteps. Diamond ducks a short lariat and reenters the ring. Red follows her in; Diamond tags out to Cooper. They pull Red up, then deck her with a double spin kick. Cooper follows up with a powerbomb, taking charge. Black soon enters the ring; she decks Cooper with a clothesline, then leaves as Red follows up with a standing wristlock, turning things back around. Cooper shortly retakes control with an enzuigiri. She soon tags out to Diamond. Red reverses a whip to the ropes, then knocks Diamond down with a shoulderblock, going back on the attack. After 2 minutes Red tags out to Black. They nail Diamond with a double elbowsmash, then Red leaves the ring. Black follows up with an elbowsmash, continuing the assault. She shortly tags in Red. They plant Diamond on the mat with the Double Spiked Piledriver, then Red pins Diamond. (12:16) ---------- Valkyrie (w/ "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln) v. The Assassin (w/ Li'l Cheetah) ---------- Both women lock up, then the Assassin reverses a whip to the ropes. Valkyrie stops the Assassin, then hammers her with the Valkyrian Storm and covers her; one, two, thr-kickout. Valkyrie stands up and waits on the Assassin. The Assassin gets up at the eight count. She bites Valkyrie's face, then kicks her in the leg, taking control. Valkyrie very shortly decks her with a back heel kick, resuming her attack. During the assault several fans in the audience begin chanting "Posse! Posse! Posse!" After 2 1/2 minutes Valkyrie executes a handspring moonsault, pinning the Assassin. After the match Turner and Cooper walk down the aisle. They enter the ring and confront the Assassin and Cheetah. The Street Assassin's bristle, then attempt to leave. Turner and Cooper stop them; the Assassin and Cheetah punch them, prompting a brawl. Vicki and the Scandalous Ones run down the aisle and enter the ring to help the Street Assassins. Vicki puts the Assassin in the Claw as the other four pummel Cheetah. A flood of WCA officials run down the aisle and enter the ring. They try to stop the carnage. One of the officials threatens to take Vicki's potential title shot away; she reluctantly lets go and calls off the others. They walk back to the locker room with their heads held high; Troll comes out and helps the Assaassin and Cheetah back to the back. (Match: 3:08) ===== Birmingham: "A vicious attack by the Posse of Punishment leaves the Street Assassins bruised and battered, hampering their chances at Sunday's No Sanctuary. The only thing that stopped them tonight was the threat of a lost opportunity for the World title, but fans can bet the bank that the Posse will continue to hound the Assassin and Li'l Cheetah. Speaking of which, I wonder if the fans demanding the Posse during the match were planted there by the Dream Master or Dr. Insane? "And now it's time for the official card rundown of Sunday's upcoming No Sanctuary. In the first match of the evening, 'the Intimidator' Amber Axeln meets the Mistress of Death in a match for the Can-Am championship. This match is the result of the mysterious 'R''s interference against Axeln in her first title match against the Mistress. Then newcomers Joanne Watson and Flower Power meet in the ring; fans should look foward to some impressive moves and tactics from both wrestlers. Then a grudge match between Double J and Tammi Cooper. Double J feels Cooper wronged her, and now she wants revenge. The next match is a tag team match between the new teams of Joanne Watson's Hired Help and the weird . . . very weird Magic Mountain Warriors. This match should be . . . interesting, to say the least. After that, the Street Assassins meet the Springfield Succulents in a Home v. ESWP Tag belt Atlantic City Street Fight, broadcast live from Atlantic City. Both of these teams want the belts, for different purposes. Which league's former champs are better? We'll find out. After that, the alliance of Darkness, Inc., Thunder and Lightning, and Janet Strong take on the Posse of Punishment in a WarGames match with the Intimidator as the referee. This match is sure to be a great one, with both teams fairly even. One thing's for sure; these wrestlers will take it to the extreme. The following match returns us to singles action, as Valkyrie's open contract has been fulfilled by newcomer Freedom, who initially didn't feel worthy to be in this spectacular event. Both wrestlers work hard, and this should be one of the classic matches. And in our main event, 'R,' 'the Intimidator' Amber Axeln, and Lovely Liz Strohlheim meet for the WCA World and Television titles *and* the former ESWP World title in an elimination triangle match. 'R' wants Axeln's Click membership if she loses, and will unmask if she wins. Who is she? We may finally find out. Coming to you from Kiev, Ukraine, and Atlantic City, New Jersey, it's No Sanctuary! Call your cable company today and don't miss this great event!" "Now it's time for our main event tonight. Originally this match was to be for the Television title, but the Mistress of Death dropped the belt to the Intimidator. But this match would take place regardless, as the Mistress wants the entire Posse to pay for what they've done to her in Paris earlier on the No Sanctuary tour. Let's go to the ring." Ring Announcer: "Ladies and gentlemen, tonight's main event is set for one fall. Introducing first: representing the Posse of Punishment and accompanied to the ring by the Dream Master and Dr. Insane, from Boston, Massachusetts, weighing in at 278 pounds, Christy Cooper!" <The Dream Master leads Cooper and Dr. Insane down the aisle. The Mistress carries her Singapore cane; both women slap hands on the way. They enter the ring.> Birmingham's voice: "The Mistress hands her belt and cane to Semiramis. Semiramis wishes her luck and leaves the ring. Dr. Insane and the Dream Master pat Cooper on the back before leaving. Referee Bob Jordan checks both wrestlers, then calls for the bell. Collar-and-elbow lock-up! Cooper switches to a full nelson. The Mistress hooks the ropes to break the hold. Cooper executes a Northern Lights suplex and covers; one, two, kickout. Cooper keeps the Mistress down with a swinging neckbreaker. She follows up with a diving headbutt. The Mistress gets back on her feet. She blocks a DDT with a backdrop, then puts Christy in an STF! Ooops - Cooper's too close to the ropes. The Mistress settles for short-arm scissors. I smell a trap; the Psychotic One, the Nasty One, and the Scandalous Ones aren't here, yet we've seen them earlier tonight. I smell a trap, and if I were Semiramis, I'd be looking more toward the locker room and the audience than the match. On the other hand, Semiramis is encouraging her friend. The Mistress lets go finally. She puts Christy in a front facelock. This is a very different Mistress from the rough brawler at the Lords of the Ring, when DI captured the Tag Team belts. Three submission holds in a row. I remember when it was three kicks ina row, or three punches in a row. The technician style is better suited to her, though. The Mistress lets Cooper go. She executes a single-leg takedown. "The Mistress executes a leaping forearm, then covers; one, two, kickout. The Mistress suspects a slow cou - Cooper rolls her up! One, two, kickout. Christy gets up and runs into the ropes. She jumps the Mistress, then returns with a rolling kick! The Mistress picks herself up; Cooper takes her down with a jumping side kick. The Mistress gets up slower. She blocks a Euro upper and executes a lariat. Cooper gets up and blocks a Japanese armdrag with an elbowsmash, then heads to the top rope. The Mistress counters the flying chop with a fist to the midsection. She sends Cooper - into the ref! She executes a high cross body - but Jordan's on the concrete! The Mistress gets back up. "Cooper sits up. She gets off the mat - and reverses a whip to a corner. She charges in - the Mistress moves out of the way! Cooper remembers to reverse a small packag - the Mistress re-reverses it, to no avail, as Jordan slowly reenters the ring. Both women stand up - ahh, good old Bob's back on the job! The Mistress ducks a short clothesline and sends Cooper to the ropes. She executes an Asai moonsault; one, two, kickout. Cooper sits up.
