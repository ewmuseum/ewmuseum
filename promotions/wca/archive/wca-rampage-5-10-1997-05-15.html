WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION ( http://www.inet-images.com/skessler/WCA/ ) RAMPAGE 5/10/97 Live from Houston, Texas -----Main Event----- Tag Team Titles Match: Bounty Hunters v. Freedom / Shannon Stevens The Mistress of Death v. Aya Ichijou The Scandal Sheet Christy Cooper v. Angel Hunter (Television Title Tournament Match) Innocent Blood v. Hooked on Destruction Bodacious Janet Muniz v. The Assassin (Best of 11 Series: Match 4) (Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.) (Fireworks explode as the camera shows the crimson WCA logo on the white ring canvas. The camera descends rapidly, then levels off facing the aisle. The camera swerves left, panning out over the capacity crowd and circling the ring, ending its focus on the iridescent three-dimensional WCA letters bracketing the entrance to the backstage area. The referee for the first match heads to the ring. The camera focuses on commentator Mike Sharpe, seated at a ringside table holding two monitors.) *Best of 11: Match 4* -------------------- Bodacious Janet Muniz (w/ "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln, Liberty, and the Mistress of Death) v. The Assassin (w/ Li'l Cheetah) Referee: Julian Pace -------------------- Both women lock up, neither one giving any ground. Muniz changes to a bearhug, assuming control of the match. After ninety seconds she runs to the ropes; the Assassin stops her with a shoulderblock. Muniz reverses another Irish whip; the Assassin rebounds and puts her into a sleeper, taking control of the match. Muniz soon blocks a tiger suplex, then she takes the Assassin down with a giant swing, going back on the attack. The Assassin shortly ducks another, then she takes Muniz down with a chop to the base of the neck, retaking control. She shortly puts Muniz on a turnbuckle and climbs up. Muniz blocks the Assassin from hooking her arms and throws her back to the mat. She hops down as the Assassin quickly gets back up. The Assassin blocks an eye gouge and throws Muniz out of the ring. She feints past the ref and leaves the ring, then she whips Muniz into the guardrail. Muniz blocks a chokehold, then she fends off Cheetah. The Assassin rewards her with an Asai moonsault, then she reenters the ring. Muniz follows her in as the Assassin runs to the far ropes; Muniz takes the Assassin down with an elbow to the face, retaking control again. She soon whips the Assassin to the ropes; the Assassin returns with a kick. She scoops Muniz up and puts her on a turnbuckle again, then she climbs up and takes Muniz down with a double underhook suplex. The Assassin covers her; one, two, shoulder up. The Assassin picks Muniz up again and puts her back on the turnbuckle, then goes to the top and executes another double underhook suplex. She covers Muniz; the ref sees her put her feet on the middle rope for leverage and stops the count. The Assassin and Pace exchange heated words, then the Assassin pulls Muniz further into the ring and puts her into an Indian deathlock, changing her strategy. Muniz soon takes the Assassin down with a tackle. She gets back up and runs to the ropes as the Assassin gets up; the latter misses with an elbow. Muniz rebounds again and nails her with a clothesline, beginning a new offense. She shortly sends the Assassin to the ropes; the Assassin rebounds and stops just behind Muniz. She executes a Russian legsweep, going back on the offensive. Muniz shortly blocks a facerake. She puts the Assassin into a bearhug, retaking control again. After a minute the Assassin counters a legsweep faceslam with her own faceslam. Muniz slowly gets back up; the Assassin whips her to the ropes, then she ducks a clothesline. She turns around and nails Muniz on the second attempted clothesline. Muniz gets back up and blocks a belly-to-back with an elbowjab. The Assassin blocks a kick to the leg, then Muniz reverses a small package. The Assassin kicks out in two and gets back up. She reverses an Irish whip and nails Muniz with an elbow to the face, taking charge. Muniz shortly blocks a hangman, then she takes the Assassin down with a double-axhandle chop and puts her into the Latino Leg Split. The Assassin submits after 6 seconds. (10:13) -------------------- Innocent Blood (Blood and Red) v. Hooked on Destruction (Christy Cooper and "Psycho" Vicki V) Referee: Esther Siler -------------------- Blood and Cooper lock up. Blood drives the bigger woman to the ropes, then Cooper reverses an Irish whip. Both women clothesline the other, but only Blood goes down. Cooper keeps Blood down with an armdrag takedown. Blood kicks Cooper's leg out and rolls away from the resulting fall. She covers; one, two, shoulder up. Blood gets up, then catches a rising Cooper with a kick to the head, taking control of the match. She shortly tags out to Red, then both women hammer Cooper with a double jumping elbowdrop. Blood and Red grab Cooper's arms and pull her up, then Blood bodyslams the big woman. Cooper rolls away from Red's flying senton as Blood leaves the ring. Cooper gets up and decks a standing Red with a clothesline. She tags in Vicki as Red gets back up, then Red blocks her bell-to-back suplex. Cooper goes to her corner as Red puts Vicki into a front facelock. Vicki quickly backdrops out of the hold, then she blocks a fireman's carry and kicks Red in the stomach, taking charge. Red very shortly catches a high cross body and turns it into a backbreaker, then she tags out to Blood. Blood runs to the far ropes; she jumps a rising Vicki, rebounds again, ducks an elbow, rebounds, and backdrops Vicki, taking control of the match. She very shortly tags in Red, then Vicki blocks her belly-to-back with an elbowjab. Blood leaves as Red blocks a reverse neckbreaker. Red takes Vicki down with neck-scissors, taking control of the match. She shortly tags in Blood, then both women send Vicki to the ropes and double-backdrop her. Cooper enters the ring and throws Red out, then she takes Blood down with a lariat. She rolls out of the ring as Blood gets back up. Blood blocks Vicki's inside cradle, then Vicki blocks standing short-arm scissors. She scrambles to her corner and tags in Cooper. Red enters the ring and throws Vicki out, then she returns to her corner as Cooper blocks a hiptoss. Blood ducks a clothesline, then she takes Cooper down with an inside cradle; one, two, kickout. Blood applies standing short-arm scissors, taking control of the match. Cooper shortly dodges a flying drop-kick. Blood gets back on her feet; Cooper takes her back down with a belly-to-back suplex. She gets back up and tags in Vicki, then both women hammer Blood with a double fistdrop. Cooper rolls out of the ring as Vicki goes to a neutral corner. She waits for Blood to get back up, then she hammers her with a flying karate chop, going on the offensive. Blood very shortly blocks a ropeburn, then she takes Vicki down with a spinning leg lariat. Blood tags in Red, then both women climb their turnbuckles. Cooper attempts to enter the ring, but gets cut off as Innocent Blood pummel Vicki with a double flying elbowdrop. Cooper feints past the ref and throws Blood out of the ring. She whips Red to the ropes and doubles her over with a kick to the midsection. She helps Vicki up, then she returns to her corner as Vicki tackles Red, taking control of the match. She shortly tags in Cooper; Blood enters the ring and "helps" Vicki leave, then she and Red send Cooper to the ropes and nail her with a double drop-kick. Blood returns to her corner as Red takes Cooper down with a legsweep, turning things around. Cooper shortly reverses a whip to a corner, then she meets Red's charge out with a kick to the midsection. Cooper tags in Vicki, then Red ducks her clothesline. Cooper leaves the ring as Vicki executes a sunset flip; one, tow, thr-shoulder up! Vicki gets up, then she bodyslams Red, retaking control of the match. Red shortly turns a spinning powerbomb into spinning headscissors, then she scrambles to her corner and tags in Blood. Cooper on cue enters the ring and throws Red out, then she climbs their turnbuckles and takes Blood down with a flying shoulderblock. Cooper rolls out of the ring as Vicki executes a fistdrop, resuming her assault. Blood shortly tags in Red; Cooper gets cut off as Blood executes a belly-to-back suplex. Red climbs to the top rope; Valkyrie gets on the apron and shoves her off. Vicki picks herself up, then she meets a standing Red with a fireman's carry. She goes to a neutral corner and scales the turnbuckles. Vicki keeps Red down with a flying clothesline. She gets up first, then she sends Red to the ropes; Red rebounds and nails her with a shoulderblock, then she executes a Japanese armdrag, turning the match around. After 2 1/2 minutes Vicki rolls away from a slingshot splash. She gets up and rests, hands on her knees. Red gets up; Vicki picks her up and drops her into a stomachbreaker, turning the match back around. She shortly tags in Cooper, then she takes Red down with a backbreaker as Cooper comes off the turnbuckles with a flying splash; one, two, thr-kickout! Cooper puts Red into a half Boston, taking control of the match. Red soon counters an armdrag takedown with a lariat. She struggles back to her feet, then she tags out to Blood. Cooper blocks a pick-up, then she takes Blood down with a belly-to-back suplex, continuing her offensive. She shortly tags out to Vicki, who enters the ring via a springboard drop-kick, continuing the assault. Blood soon blocks an earringer with a backdrop. She goes to the apron as Vicki picks herself up. Blood reenters via her own springboard drop-kick, turning the match back around. After a minute Vicki dodges a cartwheel kick. She tags out to Cooper, who keeps Blood down with a clothesline, putting her back on the defensive. Cooper shortly puts Blood into a sleeper; Blood goes under in 15 seconds. (16:44) *Television Title Tournament Match* -------------------- Christy Cooper (w/ Dr. Insane, "Psycho" Vicki V, and Valkyrie) v. Angel Hunter (w/ Janet Strong) Referee: Bob Jordan -------------------- Both women lock up. Hunter displays her strength with a vertical suplex. She keeps Cooper down with an armdrag takedown. Cooper gets back up and sidesteps a drop-kick to the knee. She executes an armdrag takedown, taking control of the match. Hunter shortly catches Cooper in a backbreaker, turning things around. Cooper soon throws her out of the ring. Hunter confers with her cousin as the ref counts; one .. two .. three .. four .. five .. six .. seven .. eight .. nine ., Hunter reenters the ring. Cooper whips her to the far ropes; Hunter rebounds and catches her in a hiptoss, retaking control of the match. After ninety seconds Cooper sidesteps a drop-kick to the knee. She raises her hands up to the crowd, enjoying the boos and insults hurled her way. She turns back around and whips Hunter to the ropes; Dr. Insane pulls the top rope down. One .. two .. three .. four .. five .., Hunter reenters the ring. Cooper meets her with a kick to the head, turning the match back around. Hunter shortly puts her into a front facelock, changing the pace of the match. She shortly turns a European uppercut into a backslide; one, two, Cooper rolls it over. One, two, shoulder up. Cooper gets back on her feet, then she kicks Hunter in the leg, going back on the attack. After a minute she catches Hunter in an inside cradle; one, two, three. Christy Cooper advances in the tournament.
