WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION (http.// kessel.simplenet.com/WCA/ ) MONDAY KNIGHTS Live from the Lake Arena in Springfield, Missouri 12/22/97 -----Main Event----- The Assassin v. Super Assassin (Super Assassin Must Change Her Name if She Loses) "Mistress of Death" Krista Stevens / Shannon Stevens v. Nadia Williams / Blood (non-title match) "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln v. (Fireworks explode as the camera shows the crimson WCA logo on the white ring canvas. The camera descends rapidly, then levels off facing the aisle. The camera swerves left, panning out over the capacity crowd and circling the ring, ending its focus on the iridescent three-dimensional WCA letters bracketing the entrance to the backstage area. The referee for the first match heads to the ring.) -------- Joanne Watson (w/ Jane Gordon and Samantha Jones) v. Steele Dragon (w/ Steele Justice, Snakebite, Femme Fatale, and Widow Maker) Referee: Julian Pace ---------- After Dragon is introduced, Watson's music begins to play. Watson never appears; Gordon shortly comes out in street clothes and enters the ring. She demands the mic, then she tells the arena that she's got a message from Watson. Gordon then turns to Dragon and kicks her in the stomach as Watson races through the crowd waving her golf club. Fatale and Maker enter the ring; Watson flies over the guardrail and enters as all three wrestlers gang up on Gordon. Watson levels Maker with her golf club and swings it at Fatale, who backs off. Gordon uses the moment to slap Dragon, then she and Watson take off for the lockers, with the Snake Pit in pursuit. ---------- Monique (w/ Mary Williams and Red) v. Mai Referee: Tina Nolke ---------- Monique sends Mai to the ropes, then she nails her with a facerake, taking control of the match. Mai shortly blocks a spinning mule kick, then she puts Monique into a front facelock, taking charge. She soon puts Monique onto the top ropes and climbs up onto the second turnbuckle; Monique throws her back to the mat. She puts Mai into a Sammartino backbreaker, going back on the offensive. Mai shortly reverses a whip to a corner and charges in shoulder-first, ramming into Monique and retaking control. After a minute Monique reverses an Irish whip and backdrops Mai; the latter lands on her feet and stumbles forward, falling over the ropes. Monique leaves the ring and throws Mai back in, then she reenters and runs past Mai to the far ropes. She comes back with a shoulderblock; Mai stumbles back, then she blocks a spinning mule kick and throws Monique out of the ring. Mai turns and leaves, then she sends Monique to the guardrail and slams her face down. She leads Monique by the head to the ringsteps and smashes her face on the metal. Monique stands up and hits Mai with her forearm, then Mai nails her with a jumping side kick. Monique busts her head open on the ringpost, then she throws Mai into the ringsteps. Mai gets up; Monique executes a back suplex on the floor. Mai scrapes her leg open on the edge of the ring attendants' table. Mai rolls away from a kneedrop and reenters the ring. Monique follows her in; Mai pummels her with a flying somersault splash, taking control of the match. After a minute Monique ducks a jumping side kick, then she decks Mai with a swinging punch, going back on the offensive. After 2 minutes she climbs to the top ropes; Mai dropkicks her in the stomach, causing her to lose her balance and slip on the turnbuckle. Mai steps onto the second turnbuckle; Mai shoves her back to the mat. Mai gets up as Monique hops down to the canvas, then she reverses an Irish whip. Mai sidesteps a kick, then she backdrops Monique, starting a new gameplan. Monique shortly hiptosses her, retaking control. After 3 minutes Mai takes Monique down with a fallaway slam, turning the match around. Monique shortly sidesteps a cartwheel kick, letting Mai nail Nolke. Monique uses the moment to take Mai down with a double-axhandle chop, retaking control. She shortly runs to the ropes; Mai nails her with a clothesline, then she gathers her energy and picks Monique up, quickly putting her on the top ropes. Mai climbs up and executes the Top-Rope Frankensteiner, then she covers Monique. Nolke recovers from the earlier blow and sees the pin; she gets in position and counts, one, two, three as Monique gets a shoulder up. Monique complains about the count, but Nolke refuses to reverse her decision. (13:06) ---------- "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln v. "Nasty Girl" Amber Jane (w/ "Dominatress" Kathy Lynn and Valkyrie) Referee: Bob Jordan ---------- Both women lock up. Jane takes Axeln down with a hiptoss, taking control of the match. After almost 2 minutes Axeln reverses an Irish whip; Jane backdrops her to the floor. Axeln lands on her feet and turns around as Jane joins her. Axeln plants her on the concrete with the Doombomb, dazing her. Axeln breaks the count at nine as Jane struggles back up. Axeln nails her with an enzuilariato, then she throws her back into the ring. Axeln rolls in and covers her; one, two, Jane puts her foot on the bottom ropes. Axeln grabs both feet and pulls Jane into the center of the ring, then she applies a leg hammerlock, turning the match around. After ninety seconds Jane blocks a fisherman suplex, then she takes Axeln down with a knee to the back, turning things back around. Axeln shortly rolls away from a flying splash and gets back up. She keeps behind Jane as the latter gets up, then she executes an enzuilariato, retaking control. Jane shortly blocks a full nelson, then she nails Axeln with a palm blow, putting her back on the defensive. She shortly attempts a sunset flip; Axeln fights her, then she sits down; Jane quickly kicks out. Axeln lets Jane get up, then she nails her with a back heel kick, retaking control. After 2 minutes Jane blocks another back heel kick. Axeln sidesteps a handspring elbow, then Jane turns a headlock into a back suplex. Axeln sits up as Jane goes out to the apron. Axeln gets back up, then she lunges away from a springboard dropkick. Jane picks herself up; Axeln nails her with an enzuigiri, taking her back down. She puts Jane into an armlock leglock. Jane manages to make it to the ropes rather quickly. She uses them to pull herself up, then she runs to the far ropes. She rebounds and ducks a clothesline, rebounds and ducks another, rebounds again and nails Axeln with her own clothesline, taking charge. She shortly throws Axeln out of the ring and joins her on the floor; Axeln hits her with a chop, then she goes behind and executes a German suplex without the bridge. Axeln reenters the ring. Jane struggles to her feet, then she rolls back in; Axeln covers and pins her.
