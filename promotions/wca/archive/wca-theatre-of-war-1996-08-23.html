WCA Theatree of War 8/22

23 August 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION THEATRE OF WAR (Special 30-Minute Edition) Live from Indianapolis, Indiana 8/22/96 -----Main Event----- Li'l Cheetah v. Lovely Liz Strohlheim (Princess of the WCA title) Darkness, Inc. v. Top Guns Woman v. "The Flawless One" Sydney Sheppard ------------------------- Woman v. "The Flawless One" Sydney Sheppard (w/ Jason Hate and Dustin Bones) ------------------------- Both women lock up, the Sheppard takes control of the match with a kneelift. After 3 minutes she attempts a belly-to-back, but Woman counters with an elbowsmash. She goes for a superkick, but Sheppard ducks it and stands toe-to-toe with her. Woman shortly ends the stalemate with an atomic drop. A minute later Sheppard evens the match up with a flying bulldog. She quickly takes control with a tiger driver. Woman soon retakes control with a superkick. After 2 minutes Sheppard begins to build a comeback. She shortly retakes control with a front slam. Woman soon evens the match with a hiptoss. Both women begin trading blows. Sheppard shortly gains the edge by throwing Woman to the floor. Woman nails her with a belly-to-back suplex on the concrete and reenters the ring. Sheppard follows her in; Woman whips her into the ropes. Sheppard comes back with a bulldog. She follows up with the Flawless Finish and pins Woman. (11:22) ------------------------- Darkness, Inc. (The Mistress of Death and Semiramis) (w/ Lu Chin) v. Top Guns (Warthog and Warhead) (w/ Snakebite) ------------------------- The Mistress and Warhead start. Both women lock up, then Warhead goes on the offensive with a forearm to the back. She shortly goes for a diving elbowsmash, but the Mistress ducks it and tags out to Semiramis. Warhead nails Semiramis with an elbowsmash, continuing her offensive. The Mistress reenters the ring; she and Semiramis whip Warhead into the ropes. They nail her with a double kick to the midsection, then the Mistress leaves the ring as Semiramis follows up with a bodyslam, going on the attack. Warhead shortly ends it with a forearm to the back followed by a roundhouse punch. A minute later Semiramis evens the match with an elbowsmash. She tags out to the Mistress. The Mistress executes a small package; one, two, kickout. She follows up with a flying forearm, taking control of the match. Warhead shortly evens the match up with a backdrop. The Mistress struggles to keep control, but Warhead shortly puts her on the defensive with a jumping side kick. The Mistress shortly ends the onslaught with a belly-to-belly suplex, but can only get a two count. She tags out to Semiramis. Warthog grabs her flight helmet and enters the ring. She nails Semiramis with it, then brings it down on the Mistress' head. The ref disqualifies the Top Guns. (5:55) ===== Birmingham: "The Top Guns resort to using foreign objects to win their match, prompting a disqualification. Up next is our main event, originally scheduled for Monday. Li'l Cheetah is offering up her Princess of the WCA T&T to two of the TCA women, Lovely Liz Strohlheim tonight and Karen 'Bugs' Huffman on Sunday. You probably already know the story behind the T&T, so I won't go into its history. Suffice it to say that it goes a long ways back in WCA history, and tonight another chapter in its legacy will be written. Let's go to the ring." Ring Announcer: "Ladies and gentlemen, the following match is for the Princess of the WCA T&T. Introducing first: accompanied to the ring by Sweet Sue and Karen 'Bugs' Huffman, from Cinnaminson, New Jersey, weighing in at 115 pounds, Lovely Liz Strohlheim!" <The TCA walk down the aisle, ignoring the fans. They enter the ring.> "Her opponent and the holder of the T&T: accompanied to the ring by her tag team partner the Assassin, from Detroit, Michigan, weighing in at 143 pounds, Li'l Cheetah!" <Cheetah and the Assassin walk down the aisle, also ignoring the fans. They enter the ring as Sue and Huffman leave.> Birmingham's voice: "Wonder why Sue and Karen left as the Street Assassins entered. The Assassin pats her friend on the shoulder, then leaves as well. Where's Troll? Mark White is the referee for this match. The bell rings; collar-and-elbow lock-up. Strohlheim blocks a roundhouse right. She picks Cheetah up, but Cheetah won't be put on a turnbuckle. Liz sets Cheetah down; Cheetah reverses a whip to the ropes. Strohlheim comes back with an elbow. She slaps Cheetah - don't get her mad! Liz runs to the ropes. She jumps over Cheetah, then puts her in a full nelson. It's interesting that the Assassin came out with Cheetah, considering both are going to focus on their singles careers right now. Then again, maybe not. These two have dominated the tag team scene for a long time. Strohlheim lets go and runs into the ropes. Cheetah jumps a shoulderblock. Liz reboudns and knocks Cheetah down with an elbow - going for the pin; one, two, kickout. Liz complains about a slow count. "She keeps Cheetah prone with a legsweep faceslam. Cheetah gets back up; Liz attempts a snap suplex - Cheetah reverses it into a vertical suplex! She runs into the ropes as Liz gets up; she nails Cheetah with a faceslam. She pulls Cheetah up and sends her to the ropes. She ducks a clothesline. Cheetah reboudns again, then stops and puts Liz in an abdominal stretch. There's the Assassin displaying the T&T for the camera, two things that once belonged to Janet Muniz. Strohlheim hiptosses her way out. She blocks a chokehold and nails Cheetah with a front slam. Strohlheim follows up with a snap suplex. She climbs the turnbuckles - Cheetah rolls away from a flying kneedrop! She kicks Liz out of the ring, then goes to the floor. "The Assassin distracts Liz - Cheetah grabs Liz's head and smashes the face onto the guardrail! What an opportunist. Liz bodyslams Cheetah onto the concrete. She rams Cheetah's head into the ringpost. The Assassin comes up behind her - Sue nails the Assassin! Liz whips Cheetah into the guardrail, then into the cornering barrier! Cheetah reenters the ring, followed by Strohlheim. She reverses a whip to the ropes. "Strohlheim rocks Cheetah with an elbow, then executes a double underhook suplex. She stomps on Cheetah, then goes for the pin; one, two, kickout. Strohlheim pulls Cheetah up and sends her into the ropes. Cheetah comes back with a shoulderblock! She nails Strohlheim with a reverse neckbreaker. Strohlheim raises up; Cheetah kicks her in the back! Strohlheim gets up and nails Cheetah with a bulldog. She throws Cheetah out of the ring. Cheetah slaps the apron in anger, then reenters. Liz greets her with a kneelift. Cheetah reverses a whip to a corner. "She charges in - Strohlheim moves out of the way! She hammers Cheetah with a front slam, then puts her in a double chinlock. Cheetah quickly elbowsmashes her way out. Strohlheim keeps her down with an earringer. And another! Cheetah comes back with a full nelson. Huffman looks on; she may be facing Cheetah real soon, and after what Cheetah did to her she oughtta be worried. Cheetah lets go of Strohlheim; Liz reverses a whip to the ropes. Cheetah comes back with an abdominaa stret - Strohlheim hiptosses Cheetah. She backdrops out of a DDT, then climbs the turnbuckles from the inside. She nails Cheetah with a flying bulldog, then keeps her down with a legsweep facelsam. "Cheetah gets back up and blocks a snap suplex. She dropkicks Liz, then puts her in a Sammartino backbreaker. STrohlheim gets to the ropes; Cheetah curses and pulls her into the ring more, then reapplies the hold. Did Strohlheim submit? No, she didn't. The Assassin's trying to get her to, though. Cheetah breaks the hold. She nails Liz with a backspin DDT, then drives her knee into Strohlheim's back. Cheetah pulls Strohlheim up and sends her into the corner. She charges in shoulder-first, ramming into Strohlheim. Cheetah sends her into the ropes. The Assassin trips her! White warns the Assassin as Cheetah nails her with a knee to the back. She follows up with a fistdrop. "Strohlheim gets back up slowly. Cheetah kicks her in the head. Strohlheim reverses a whip to the ropes, then jumps a shoulderblock. Cheetah reboudns again, then blocks a slap and returns the same. Strohlheim slaps her back - Cheetah puts her in an abdominal stretch. And grabs the ropes for leverage! White checks - yeah, Cheetah, 'who, me?' alright. The Assassin vouches for her, uh, integrity. Cheetah lets go and chokes Strohlheim! One, two, three, four, Cheetah applies a cobra clutch. Strohlheim struggles for the ropes . . . and makes it! Cheetah blocks a back heel kick, then nails her with an atomic drop. "She avoids Strohlheim's dropkick, then kicks her in the back. She runs into the ropes. Strohlheim nails her with a back heel kick and follows up with an enzuigiri to the face. Cheetah staggers into the ropes. She blocks a slap - and throws Strohlheim out of the ring! Cheetah goes to the floor. Strohlheim walks around the corner and reenters the ring. Cheetah chases her - Strohlheim takes her down with a snap mare. "Cheetah gets back up and rakes her face. She nails Strohlheim with the Heart Punch! Liz crumples to the mat; Cheetah arrogantly covers her; one, two, three!" RA: "Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the match and still holder of the Princess of the WCA T&T: in 12 minutes, 4 seconds, Li'l Cheetah!" <Cheetah and the Assassin raise their arms in victory, then head back to the back. Cheetah winks at Karen on the way, grinning evilly.> Birmingham: "There you have it fans, Li'l Cheetah retains the Princess of the WCA T&T and will face Karen "Bugs" Huffman for it on Rampage. As you know, the WCA President has a prior commitment this weekend, but since he wants to see the Title v. Title Ladder match he's decided to hold a special edition of Rampage this Sunday. Other matches include the Mistress versus Amanda, Vonya takes on 'the Intimdidator' Amber Axeln, and more! I'm John Birmingham; have a good evening!" -- ------------------------- [name withheld] [detail withheld] "Make it idiot-proof and someone will make a better idiot."
