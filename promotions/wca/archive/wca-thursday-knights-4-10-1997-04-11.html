WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION THURSDAY KNIGHTS 4/10/97 From Sydney, Australia -----Main Event----- =World Title Cage Match= The Mistress of Death v. Shelly Rage =Television Title Match= Sweet Sue v. Li'l Cheetah Muniz's Musings "Nasty Girl" Amber Jane v. "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln (Submission Match) Femme Fatale v. The Assassin <Fade in to tape:> Red: What decision has been made about the belts? We wrestle for them one week ago and get screwed out of them. I thought they wanted a team to be wearing them as quickly as possible. Black: We actually had to fight two matches for the belts, unlike Hardcore who only had to wrestle once. We won our first match in the tournament and should be the tag team champions. Panthra: A decision was promised and there hasn't been any talk of what is going to happen with the belts. I thought we were going to get a quick decision here, not have to wait until no one cares any more about the belts. <Dissolve to tape:> Kessler <reading from a statement>: "Ladies and gentlemen, the Championship Committee has come up with a solution regarding the Tag Team titles situation. We had hoped there would be a quick decision, but some suggestions were hotly debated this past week. One option was to have all teams in a tournament for the belts. Another decision was to have the Mini-Tournament Final Round refought, with belts being able to go to the winners of a countout or disqualification. However, those suggestions were not adopted. "The Committee has decided to hold a match between the Bounty Hunters, who won the right to be in the final match, and indeed won that match, and the team the last champions left empty-handed, the Delicious Dolls. Thus, in one week the Bounty Hunters and the Delicious Dolls will face off in a no-countouts / no-disqualifications match for the belts. Thank you." <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.> <Fireworks explode as the camera shows the crimson WCA logo on the white ring canvas. The camera descends rapidly, then levels off facing the aisle. The camera swerves left, panning out over the capacity crowd and circling the ring, ending its focus on the iridescent three-dimensional WCA letters bracketing the entrance to the backstage area. The referee for the first match heads to the ring.> -------------------- Femme Fatale (w/ Snakebite) v. The Assassin (w/ Li'l Cheetah) -------------------- Both women circle each other warily. They lock up; the Assassin takes Fatale down with a front-layout suplex, taking control of the match. Fatale shortly counters a flying sunset flip with a sit-down; the Assassin kicks out in two. Fatale follows up with a Soviet suplex, taking charge. After 2 1/2 minutes the Assassin ducks a spinning leg lariat. She lets Fatale stand back up, then takes her down with a Russian legsweep, turning the match back around. Fatale very shortly blocks a double chickenwing submission, then sends the Assassin to the ropes. Snakebite trips the Assassin up; Nolke gives him a warning, then turns back around as Fatale executes a bridging back suplex; one, two, thr-kickout! Fatale rewards the Assassin with an enzuigiri, retaking control. After a minute the Assassin blocks standing short-arm scissors. She reaches up and yanks Fatale through the ropes to the floor. The Assassin rests a moment, then rolls out after her. She blocks the Perfect Plex; Fatale settles for an atomic drop. She nails a threatening Cheetah with a kick to the midsection, then heads back into the ring. The Assassin mirrors her; Fatale changes her mind and attempts a leaping clothesline; the Assassin drops down, letting Fatale bust her head open on the ringsteps. The Assassin picks herself up, then slams Fatale's head on the ringsteps. She turns Fatale over and rakes her face, then grabs an arm and sends Fatale into the guardrail. Fatale angrily punches a taunting Cheetah; the Assassin turns her around and gouges out the eyes. She whips Fatale back over the ringsteps, then whips her back to the guardrail. The Assassin puts Fatale back into the ring, then rolls in and stands up. She picks Fatale up and puts her on a turnbuckle, then climbs up and executes a double underhook superplex, continuing her offense. After a minute Fatale reverses a suplex into a vertical suplex. Both women rest a moment on the mat, then Fatale gets up first, clutching her back. She executes a rolling reverse cradle; the Assassin easily kick out. Fatale runs into the ropes, jumps a standing Assassin, rebounds and executes a side suplex, going back on the attack. After ninety seconds the Assassin ducks a flying clothesline. She chokes Fatale; one, two, three, the Assassin lets go and confronts the ref as Fatale gets back on her feet. The Assassin chokes her again; one, two, three, she lets go. Snakebite throws his snake-headed cane to Fatale; the Assassin intercepts it, then spins around and decks Fatale with the cane, then she drops and covers; one, two, Fatale throws the Assassin off of her. She gets back up, then puts the Assassin in a sleeper, retaking control of the match. After 2 minutes she runs to the ropes; the Assassin catches her in her own sleeper, starting a new offense. Fatale soon blocks a tiger suplex, then she runs to the ropes, ducks a clothesline, rebounds and backdrops the Assassin, putting her back on the defensive. After ninety seconds she blocks a sleeper. The Assassin blocks a back suplex; Fatale blocks a chokehold. The Assassin blocks a pick-up; Fatale blocks an eye gouge. The Assassin blocks a fireman's carry, then she slips behind Fatale and takes her down with a Russian legsweep. The Assassin applies the Sharpshooter. Fatale submits immediately. (14:28) ===== Sharpe <sitting at a ringside table with a crimson tablecloth and two monitors>: "The Assassin returns to fine form tonight, pulling through Fatale's strong challenge and forcing the former two-time Can-Am champ to submit to the Sharpshooter. Time will tell if this is the beginning of the Assassin's ascent back to the top of the WCA. Time will also tell if this is the beginning of Femme's slide back down the rankings ladder. She went into this match having lost the Can-Am belt on Monday for a second time, and with this loss needs a victory in her next match to prove these two were flukes. "Fans, we need to let our sponsors have their way with you; when we come back, we'll introduce the newest member of the WCA broadcasting family!" -----Commercial Break----- Sharpe: "John Birmingham's mouth may have gotten him into trouble and forced his resignation from the WCA, but he left some pretty important shoes to fill. We are pleased to announce that they've been filled by his friend and fellow former wrestler Shane Templeton. Mr. Templeton is standing by right now backstage with Li'l Cheetah." <Backstage in front of a blue curtain:> Templeton: "Li'l Cheetah, you've been a one-time Television champion, but you've also been a World champion, a Tag Team co-champion. Why go after the TV belt again?" Cheetah: "Because it's there! There aren't too many people who fall into my requirements for matches. Most of them are either too big, like Norma Hudson, or they are too chicken, like Mistress of Death. Sue isn't too big. And she isn't too chicken. She's just real, real stupid and dumb. She should have just handed over that TV belt to me and avoid having her heart punched out. Now she'll have to accept the beating of her life and a sixth straight defeat at my hands. And the final result will be the same as if she just gave me the belt in the first place." Templeton <shaking his head in disbelief>: "I've noticed you've been pretty busy lately regarding the titles scene, having unsuccessfully pursued the Can-Am belt and also the World title, potentially, this Sunday at Quake II. Feeling a little gold-hungry?" Cheetah: "To be honest, not at all! I am not impressed with any of the contenders for the world title right now. To be honest, they are all either too chicken or too big. The only one I really want to get at if she wins the title is this Blood character. I saw her get her butt kicked by Loser Liz a while back. Talk about a battle of losers! Let me tell you what I'd like to see happen. I'd like to take that World title at Quake II, if a worthy opponent has that title. Then, with the TV title I'll take from Sue tonight -- and that's a foregone conclusion -- I just may ask for a unification match against whoever has the Can-Am title! Everything on the line at one time! Now that would be the biggest match ever in the history of the WCA, wouldn't it? Even bigger than Muniz vs. Valkyrie last August! Hmmm? See what I'm after? If you think I'm gold hungry, well, read between the lines!" Templeton <sarcastically>: "Thanks for joining me." Cheetah: "Yeah, yeah, yeah. Incidentally, aren't you related to some guy who used to play ball for the Atlanta Braves? What was his name -- Perry Templeton?" <Templeton just shakes his head.> <Cut to Sharpe:> Sharpe: "I guess Cheetah doesn't watch the show backstage. With concentration like that, it's no wonder she blew her chance at the Can-Am title. "Fans, up next is the highly-anticipated battle between the two Ambers, the Nasty Girl known as Amber Jane and the Intimidator, also known as Amber Axeln. These two met once before, with the Nasty Girl winning the match early. They settled on a return match, submission style. Axeln's been training pretty hard lately. Let's find out if it's enough to vindicate her loss." ===== *Submission Match* -------------------- "Nasty Girl" Amber Jane (w/ The Dream Master and "Dominatress" Kathy Lynn) v. "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln (w/ Bodacious Janet Muniz, Liberty, and the Mistress of Death) -------------------- Both women lock up. Jane nails Axeln with a palm blow, then blocks a side suplex. Axeln backdrops out of a front facelock, then puts Jane into a half Boston. Jane pulls herself to the ropes to break the hold. She uses them to stand up, then Axeln blocks a hiptoss and runs to the far ropes. Jane executes an armdrag takedown; Axeln blocks standing short-arm scissors, then pops back up and runs to the side ropes. She nails Jane with an enzuilariato, taking charge. After a minute Jane reverses an Irish whip, then takes Axeln down with an armdrag. She drops her knee into Axeln's back, going on the offensive. Axeln shortly sidesteps a handspring elbow. Jane gets up in time to duck neck-scissors. Axeln backdrops out of a front facelock as she stands back up; Jane gets up and reverses an Irish whip, then knocks Axeln down with a shoulderblock. Axeln gets back up; Jane sends her to a corner, then runs in shoulder-first. Axeln lunges out of the way, letting Jane ram the ringpost. Axeln pulls her out of the corner, then sends her to the far ropes and decks her with a clothesline, taking charge. Jane shortly moves away from a forearm to the back, then she hits Axeln with her palm, going back on the attack. Axeln soon nails her with a lariat, starting a new offense. She shortly runs to the ropes; Jane follows her in and drives her knee into Axeln's back, causing Axeln to go to her knees and retaking control. After ninety seconds Axeln blocks a ropeburn, then she executes neck-scissors, turning things back around. Jane shortly dodges a forearm to the back, then she goes to the apron and reenters the ring via a springboard shoulderblock, retaking control. After 2 minutes she runs to the ropes; Axeln stops her with a kick, then she rocks her with a swinging punch, turning the match back around. Jane soon blocks a back heel kick, then she goes to a corner and takes Axeln down with a flying forearm, going back on the attack. After an intense 6 minutes Jane throws Axeln out of the ring, then goes through the ropes and joins her on the floor. She shoves Axeln into the ringsteps, then walks up her back to the apron. The Mistress grabs her leg and yanks her back to the floor; Jane lands hard. Axeln grabs Jane and sends her head-first into the guardrail, then spins her around and sends her back into the ring. Axeln rolls back in, then puts Jane into a front facelock. Jane hooks the bottom rope with her foot; Axeln stands up, then executes the Doombomb, turning the match around. After 3 minutes she puts Jane into a cobra clutch; Jane submits immediately.
