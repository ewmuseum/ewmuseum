WCA Theatre of War 12/20

20 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION THEATRE OF WAR 12/20/96 Live from the Lake Arena in Springfield, Missouri -----Main Event----- Valkyrie v. Janet Strong "Psycho" Vicki V v. Big Norma Hudson "Destroyer" Dara Holt v. Nasty Tracy Turner Angel Hunter v. Jenny Black Black v. Freedom <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.> <Fireworks explode as the camera shows a top-down view of the ring, highlighting the maroon WCA logo on the canvas. The camera descends, panning to the right and leveling off, showing the capacity crowd. It focuses on the end of the aisle, where 'WCA' frames the entrance to the locker room. Several WCA officials walk down the aisle.> ---------- Black (w/ Red, Semiramis, and Panthra) v. Freedom (w/ the Mistress of Death) ---------- Black sends Freedom to the ropes, then misses with a shoulderblock. She stops Freedom with a punch to the stomach, taking control of the match. She shortly runs into the ropes; Freedom stops her with an enzuigiri. She follows up with a gutwrench suplex, taking control of the match. After ninety seconds she catches Black in a rolling reverse cradle, pinning her. (2:01) ---------- Angel Hunter (w/ Janet Strong)_v. Jenny Black (w/ "Psycho" Vicki V, Christy Cooper, the Dream Master, and Dr. Insane) ---------- Both women lock up, then Black nails Hunter with a spinning mule kick, going on the offensive. Hunter quickly puts her on the defensive with a takeover suplex. She soon sends Black to the ropes. Black comes back with a kick. She sends Hunter to the ropes, then blocks a swinging punch. She executes neck-scissors, turning the match back around. Hunter shortly blocks a full nelson, then runs into the ropes. She nails Black with a kick, retaking control. After 2 minutes Black counters a figure-four leglock with a small package; Hunter kicks out in two and gets back up, then runs into the ropes. Black pops up and takes her down with an elbow to the face, then covers her again. Hunter kicks out in two; Black keeps her on the mat with a bulldog, turning the match around again. She shortly runs into the ropes. Hunter ducks a clothesline, then another, then misses with a clothesline, then nails Black with an elbow. Black ducks a kick to the head; Hunter puts her in an abdominal stretch, going back on the offensive. After 3 1/2 minutes she runs into the ropes. Black jumps a shoulderblock, jumps another and follows Hunter in, clotheslining her over the top rope. Hunter reenters the ring at the eight count; Black sends her to a corner. She charges in and smashes into Hunter, turning the match around. Hunter shortly ducks a flying shoulderblock. She applies an abdominal stretch, resuming her offensive. After 3 minutes she sends Black to a corner, then runs in shoulder-first. Black moves out of the way, then slaps Hunter. She throws her out of the ring, then joins her on the floor. Hunter knocks Black back into the ringsteps. Black gets up and nails Hunter with a spin kick. She throws Hunter back into the ring, then slides in. Both women stand up; Black sends Hunter to the ropes. She stops her with a monkey flip. Hunter gets back up, then reverses a whip to the ropes. She nails Black with a lariat, going back on the offensive. After 3 minutes she runs into the ropes. Black nails her with a shoulderblock, then heads to the top rope. She hammers Hunter with a flying somersault splash, turning the match around. Hunter shortly puts Black in an abdominal stretch. Black submits after 5 seconds. (17:48) ---------- "Destroyer" Dara Holt (w/ the Prince) v. Nasty Tracy Turner (w/ Shelly Rage, Flower Power, the Dream Master, and Dr. Insane) ---------- Both women lock up, then Turner takes Holt down with a leaping forearm, going on the attack. She shortly attempts a sunset backslide, but Holt reverses it; one, two, kickout. Holt picks Turner up; Turner blocks being set on a turnbuckle. Holt settles for a back suplex. Turner gets up and blocks a Soviet suplex, then plants Holt with a Doctor bomb and covers; Holt kicks out in two. Turner follows up with a jack-knife powerbomb, going on the offensive. Holt soon catches Turner in a bridging back suplex; Turner kicks out in two. Holt hammers her in a Samoan Drop, turning things around. She soon sends Turner to a corner, then runs in shoulder-first. Turner moves out of the way, then runs into the ropes. She rocks Holt with a clothesline, going on the attack. Holt soon reverses a whip to a corner. Turner rebounds off the turnbuckles; Holt nails her with a short clothesline. She pulls Turner out of the corner, then plants her on the canvas with a jack-knife powerbomb, turning things around again. After a brutal 5 1/2 minutes Turner nails Holt with a faceslam. She follows up with a front-layout suplex, turning the match around. After 3 minutes she takes Holt down with a jumping elbow thrust. She puts Holt in an STF; Holt submits immediately. (13:05) ---------- "Psycho" Vicki V (w/ Christy Cooper, the Dream Master, and Dr. Insane) v. Big Norma Hudson (w/ Haley "Comet" Hanson and Mr. Canuck) ---------- Vicki surprises Hudson with a bulldog. Hudson slowly gets up; Vicki sends her to a corner. She runs in shoulder-first; Hudson moves out of the way. Vicki rams the ringpost; Hudson nails her with a forearm to the back. She pulls Vicki back; Vicki falls backward to the mat. Hudson hammers her with a kneeling headbutt, going on the offensive. She soon bodyslams Vicki, then drops down and covers her. Vicki kicks out in two. Hudson inquires about a suspected slow count; Vicki uses the opportunity to execute an inside cradle. Hudson's momentum keeps her from being pinned. Vicki stands up and runs into the ropes. She jumps a rising Hudson and rebounds, then nails Hudson with a clothesline, going on the offensive. After 4 minutes Hudson counters a European uppercut with a backslide. Vicki kicks out in two, then both women stand up. Vicki nails Hudson with a thrust kick to the head, taking her down. Hudson rolls away from a falling splash. Vicki blocks a powerbomb, then nails Hudson with a back heel kick. She sends Hudson to the ropes; Hudson comes back with a shoulderblock, knocking Vicki down. She runs into the ropes, then hammers Vicki with the Hudson Bay Splash, pinning her. (6:31) ===== Birmingham: "Big Norma Hudson continues her winning streak with a victory over Tag Team co-champion 'Psycho' Vicki V. Up next is our main event, a match between two fan favorites, Valkyrie and Janet Strong. Both of these women want to climb that ladder to the top, and both are highly-skilled wrestlers. Tonight we'll see who the better wrestler is. Let's go to the ring." Ring Announcer: "Ladies and gentlemen, tonight's main event is set for one fall! Introducing first: accompanied to the ring by her cousin Angel Hunter, from Seattle, Washington, weighing in at 175 pounds, Janet Strong!" <The fans cheer Strong and Hunter as they walk down the aisle. Both women acknowledge them with smiles and waves, then they enter the ring.> "Her opponent: accompanied to the ring by her tag team partner 'the Intimidator' Amber Axeln, from Des Moines, Iowa, weighing in at 129 pounds, Valkyrie!" <New Dawn walk down the aisle to an approving crowd, slapping hands. Axeln carries her World title belt slung over her shoulder. Both wrestlers enter the ring.> Birmingham's voice: "Amber wishes Valkyrie luck, then leaves the ring. Angel slaps Janet on the back before bowing out as well. Referee Bob Jordan begins the match, and both women lock up! Valkyrie takes Strong down with a kick! Strong gets back up and executes a takeover suplex. Valkyrie gets back up. Strong blocks a back heel kick; Valkyrie reverses a whip to the ropes. Strong comes back with an elbow. Valkyrie blocks an earringer and throws Janet out of the ring! She goes through the ropes and joins her on the floor. Strong blocks a reverse neckbreaker. Valkyrie settles for a single-leg takedown. Janet gets back on her feet and executes a takeover suplex. Valkyrie gets up and gets on the apron. She nails Strong with a diving elbowsmash! "Valkyrie reenters the ring. Strong follows her in - Valkyrie throws her out the other side! Jordan counts; one, two, Strong reenters the ring. Valkyrie meets her with a rolling kick. Both women stand up; Strong legsweeps Valkyrie. Valkyrie backdrops out of a front facelock! Strong picks herself up and runs into the ropes. She backdrops Valkyrie. Valkyrie gets up; Strong sends her to the ropes. She ducks a clothesline, then spins around and ducks a thrust kick to the head. Strong rakes Valkyrie's face as we go to commercial! Stay tuned!" --------------------COMMERCIAL BREAK-------------------- Birmingham's voice: "Welcome back! Just after we left Valkyrie took control of the match with a belly-to-belly superplex. She's controlled the match since. Going back to live action, Valkyrie executes a backflip cradle; one, two, kickout. Both women stand up; Valkyrie with a single-leg takedown. Strong gets back up; Valkyrie blocks a cobra clutch with an armdrag takedown. Strong gets back up; Valkyrie nails her with a back heel kick. Strong staggers back, then reverses a whip to a corner. She runs in shoulder-first, ramming Valkyrie! Strong nails her with a thrust kick to the head; Valkyrie falls face-first to the mat! Strong puts her in a Boston crab. Both Strong and Angel Hunter are appreciated by the fans, but Strong has a bit of a rule-breaking streak in her. She may use some questionable tactics if pushed. Strong lets go. She pulls Valkyrie up, then sends her to the ropes. Valkyrie returns with spinning headscissors! "Both women stand up. Valkyrie nails Janet with an enzuigiri to the face, then sends her to the ropes. She ducks a clothesline, ducks an elbow, then misses with an elbow, misses with a shoulderblock, then rocks Strong with a back heel kick. Strong recovers and applies an armbar. She shakes her head to clear it. Valkyrie and Amber are both very good singles wrestlers. It still remains to be seen if they'll make a good tag team, though. Teamwork is of the utmost importance, and many singles wrestlers need time to learn when to make tags and when to double-team an opponent. Strong lets Valkyrie go! Valkyrie blocks a kick to the midsection, then runs into the ropes. She misses with an elbow, ducks a clothesline, then takes Strong down with a rolling kick. She's going for the pin; one, two, shoulder up. Both women stand up; Strong reverses a whip to a corner. "She charges in, nailing Valkyrie with a Stinger splash! Valkyrie reverses a whip to the opposing corner. Strong comes back out; Valkyrie nails her with an elbowsmash. She ducks a short lariat. Strong settles for a jumping side kick, taking Valkyrie down. Strong throws her out of the ring! She joins her on the floor, then rakes Valkyrie's face! Strong sends Valkyrie into the ringsteps. Valkyrie scoops Strong up and drives her back into the ringpost! Strong escapes back into the ring. Valkyrie follows her in - Strong nails her with an enzuigiri. "She applies a figure-four sleeper! Axeln rallies the fans behind Valkyrie! Strong hears the cheers and lets go. Both women stand up; Valkyrie blocks a belly-to-back and throws Strong out of the ring! She joins her on the floor. Strong goes around the corner, then reenters the ring. Valkyrie chases after her, reentering herself. She counters a back suplex with a cross-body, but only gets a one count. Valkyrie tries a backslide, and gets a two count. Strong dodges a backflip cradle. "She runs into the ropes, then staggers Valkyrie with a swinging punch - then decks her with a thrust kick to the head! Valkyrie sits up! She gets back up, then reverses a whip to a corner. Strong heads back into the ring, then dodges a backflip cradle. Valkyrie sits up, then blocks an armbar. She gets on her feet, then takes Strong down with a leaping forearm and covers her; one, two, shoulder up. Both women stand up; Strong blocks a back heel kick. She executes a German suplex; one, two, thr-kickout! Almost won the match! Valkyrie picks herself up; Strong nails her with a kick to the midsection. "She applies an armbar. Axeln again rallies the fans behind Valkyrie! Valkyrie breaks the hold. Strong rams her knee into Valkyrie's back; Valkyrie sends her to the ropes. She avoids a kick - then plants Strong on the mat with the Valkyrian Storm and covers her; one, two - whoa! Strong kicked out with AUTHORITY! Both women stand up. Valkyrie blocks a belly-to-back suplex. She executes a single-leg takedown. Strong picks herself up. Valkyrie rolls her up in a small package; Strong kicks out in two. Valkyrie keeps her down with a single-leg takedown. She climbs the turnbuckles, then hammers Janet with a diving elbowsmash. Strong slowly gets back on her feet. She ducks a leaping forearm! "Valkyrie gets back up. Strong nails her with a knee to the back, then sends her to the ropes. Valkyrie jumps a kick, ducks a clothesline, then legsweeps Strong! Strong gets back up in time to duck a leaping forearm! Valkyrie rolls over, then raises up on her knees. Strong takes her down with an earringer! Valkyrie sits up! She blocks an armbar and gets up, then sends Strong to the ropes. She takes Janet down with a kick and goes for the pin; one, two, in the ropes. Both women stand up. Strong nails Valkyrie with a kick. She grabs Valkyrie by the back of the head, then sets her face against the top rope - uh, oh. Strong runs it along the cable; ouch! "Valkyrie blocks another ropeburn. She rolls Strong up in a small package; one, two, kickout. Both women stand up; Strong sends Valkyrie to the ropes. Strong comes back with a swinging punch! She applies a cobra clutch. Valkyrie breaks the hold; Strong takes her down with an earringer! Valkyrie gets back up. Strong counters an armdrag takedown with a lariat. She punches Valkyrie, then nails her with a knee to the back. Strong legsweeps her. Valkyrie picks herself up. Strong runs into the ropes; Valkyrie takes her down with headscissors! "Strong gets back on her feet. Valkyrie takes her down with a reverse neckbreaker, then puts her in a surfboard! Strong's too close to the ropes! She grabs one to break the hold. Valkyrie runs into the ropes, then misses with an elbow. Strong misses with a shoulderblock, then stops Valkyrie with a jumping side kick. Valkyrie pulls Strong's feet out from under her! Strong puts Valkyrie in a figure-four sleeper! Now Valkyrie's too close to the ropes not to use them! Both women stand up. Strong executes a German suplex; one, two, three! Janet Strong defeats Valkyrie!" RA:: "Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the match: in 17 minutes, 2 seconds, Janet Strong!" <Both wrestlers stare at each other, then shake hands. Hunter and Axeln enter the ring and raise their respective partners' arms up to a delighted crowd.> Birmingham: "Janet Strong and Valkyrie struggle for 17 minutes, with Janet gaining the pinfall! Fans, we are out of time. Coming up this Rampage, we'll see World champion Amber Axeln take on Flower Power in a non-title match, Janet Strong goes up against Double D, Sellyna Shriek makes her comeback, but not against Artic Blast. She'll meet Double J instead. Artic Blast and the Gravediggaz have left the WCA for personal reasons. And also on Rampage, Nasty Tracy Turner will go through the Posse of Punishment's gauntlet. I'm John Birmingham; have a good evening!" <Closing credits appear.>
