WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION RAMPAGE 2/15/96 Consecration Tour Live from Luanda, Angola -----Main Event----- =Television Title Texas Death Match= Double J v. The Assassin The Psycho Crushers v. Hired Help The Scandal Sheet Magic Mountain Warriors v. Bounty Hunters Christy Cooper v. Angel Hunter (Best of 3 Submission match) Hardcore v. Delicious Dolls Blood v. Joanne Watson Sarah Bryant v. Nadia Williams -------------------- Sarah Bryant (w/ Vinnie Gambino) v. Nadia Williams (w/ Crimson and Kim) -------------------- Both women lock up, then Bryant whips Williams to the ropes. Williams returns with an enzuilariato. Bryant pops back up, then throws her out of the ring. She goes through the ropes; Williams takes her down with a belly-to-back suplex. Gambino distracts Williams as Bryant reenters the ring. Williams follows her in; Bryant puts her in a sleeper. Williams uses the ropes to break the hold; Bryant reluctantly lets go, then runs to the far ropes. Williams stops her with an Asai moonsault; one, two, kickout. Williams executes a belly-to-back suplex. Bryant blocks an armlock leglock, then gets up and executes her own belly-to-back. She puts Williams in a scorpion deathlock, taking control of the match. Williams shortly counters a back suplex with a cross-body block; one, two, kickout. Williams executes a double underhook backbreaker, going on the offensive. Bryant soon puts her in another sleeper, retaking control. After 3 minutes she throws Williams out of the ring, then joins her on the floor. Williams executes an Asai moonsault, then nails a standing Bryant with a thrust kick to the head. She throws Bryant back into the ring, then rolls back in and gets up. She nails Bryant with a roundhouse punch, turning the match around. After 2 1/2 minutes Bryant blocks a double reverse chinlock with a facerake. She executes neck-scissors, then drives her knee into Williams' back, turning the match back around. After ninety seconds Williams drives her knee into Bryant's back. She moves away from a forearm to the back, then sends Bryant to the ropes. Bryant returns with a clothesline, knocking Williams out of the ring. Bryant goes to the floor and throws Williams back in, then reenters and applies a grapevine, taking control of the match again. Williams soon counters a pick-up with a small package, but only gets a two count. Williams dodges a forearm to the back, then sends Bryant to the ropes. Bryant returns with a swinging punch. Williams blocks a forearm smash, then runs to the side ropes. She ducks a clothesline, rebounds, and executes a high cross body; Bryant easily kicks out. Williams rewards her with a double reverse chinlock, taking control of the match. She shortly whips Bryant to the ropes; Bryant returns with a kick to the midsection, going back on the offensive. Williams shortly catches Bryant with an enzuilariato, retaking control of the match. Williams shortly runs into the ropes; Bryant ducks a clothesline, then glances back and nails Williams with an elbow to the face, retaking control. Williams shortly nails her with a headbutt, going back on the attack. She shortly runs to the ropes; Williams ducks a clothesline, then catches her in a belly-to-belly; one, two, in the ropes. Both women stand up; Williams sends Bryant to the far ropes, then knocks her down with a shoulderblock, retaking control. She soon counters a pick-up with a small package; one, two, thr-kickout. Both women get back up; Bryant blocks a headbutt. Williams reverses a whip to a corner, then runs in with a Stinger Splash, pummeling Bryant and resuming her offensive. Bryant very shortly grabs Williams by the hair and throws her out of the ring. Williams reenters the ring; Bryant executes the In Memory of Jacky, then covers; one, two, three. Bryant gets up and yanks Williams up, then sends her to the far ropes. She meets Williams with a clothesline, taking control of the match. She shortly throws Williams out of the ring, then joins her on the floor. Bryant executes a belly-to-belly, then keeps Kim at bay. Williams gets up and shoves Bryant into the guardrail, then slams her face on the steel. She backs Bryant back to the apron, then snap suplexes her onto the barrier. Crimson taunts Bryant as Williams breaks the count. She reconsiders and slides into the ring. Bryant enters at the eight count; Williams meets her with an enzuilariato, going back on the offensive. Bryant soon blocks a ropeflip hiptoss, then executes a belly-to-back suplex, turning things around again. Williams soon ducks neck-scissors. Bryant slowly gets up, then reverses an Irish whip. She catches a high cross body and drops Williams into a backbreaker. Bryant gets up slowly, then puts Williams into a Boston crab, regaining her momentum. She soon blocks a ropeburn; Williams settles for a snap mare. She hammers Bryant with the Guillotine and covers; one, two, Gambino pulls the ref out of the ring. The ref disqualifies Bryant. (20:40) -------------------- Blood (w/ Crimson and Kim) v. Joanne Watson -------------------- Watson counters a suplex with a vertical one. Blood gets up and puts her in an abdominal stretch, taking control of the match. Watson shortly reverses a whip to a corner. Blood walks back out; Watson punches her in the stomach. She decks Blood with a European uppercut. Blood gets up and blocks a snap mare, then nails Watson with a double-axhandle chop, resuming her offensive. Watson shortly blocks an atomic drop, then executes an ipponzei; one, two, thr-shoulder up. Watson whips Blood to the ropes; she misses with a shoulderblock, then glances back and nails Blood with an elbow. She sends Blood to the side ropes; Blood returns with a side suplex, retaking control. Watson shortly counters a suplex with a vertical one. She scoops Blood up, then executes a fallaway slam, turning things around. She soon blocks an atomic drop; Blood counters a cobra clutch with an armdrag. She picks Watson up; Watson blocks being set on a turnbuckle. Blood settles for an abdominal stretch. Watson breaks the hold after 18 seconds, then executes a backflip cradle, only to get a two count. Watson sends Blood to the ropes, then nails her with a kick, going on the offensive. After ninety seconds Blood ducks a short clothesline, then executes a high cross body; Watson kicks out in two. Blood punishes her with a double-axhandle chop, retaking control. She shortly executes a Frankensteiner, then covers; one, two, shoulder up. Blood confronts the ref about a slow count; Watson throws her out of the ring. One ... two .. three ... four ... five ... six ... seven .. Blood reenters the ring. Watson takes Blood down with a fallaway slam, going back on the attack. After ninety seconds she executes a backflip cradle, then uses the ropes for leverage; the ref sees it and disqualifies Watson. Diamond reverses an Irish whip; Jane blocks an armdrag with a lariat, turning the match around. Diamond shortly blocks a double underhook something, then tags in Star. Cooper grabs a chair and attempts to enter the ring; Kim takes the chair away, then takes Cooper down with a karate kick. Hunter shortly slaps her; Cooper slaps her back. Hunter blocks a forearm smash; Cooper reverses a whip to a corner. She saunters in, then whips Hunter to the far ropes. Hunter blocks a snap mare; Cooper ducks a jumping side kick. She clotheslines Hunter, retaking control of the match. She soon executes a reverse cradle pinning Hunter. (7:08 - Cooper: 1, Hunter 0) Both women circle each other warily, teasing a test of strength. They lock together; Hunter breaks off and nails Cooper with a back heel kick, taking charge.
