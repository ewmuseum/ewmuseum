WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION MONDAY KNIGHTS Live from the Lake Arena in Springfield, Missouri 7/29/96 -----Main Event----- Double J v. Semiramis Delicious Dolls v. Bodacious Janet Muniz / "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln Lovely Liz Strohlheim v. Karen "Bugs" Huffman The Mistress of Death v. Tammi Cooper ------------------- The Mistress of Death (w/ Semiramis and Lu Chin) v. Tammi Cooper ------------------- Both women lock up, then the Mistress nails Cooper with a piledriver, taking control of the match. After two minutes the Mistress goes for a small package, but Cooper reverses it; one, kickout. Cooper follows up with an ipponzei and covers her; one, two, shoulder up. The Mistress whips Cooper into the ropes; Cooper rebounds and nails her with an elbow, evening the match up. Both women start trading blows. Cooper shortly breaks the stalemate with a back suplex. After two minutes the Mistress nails Cooper with a leaping forearm and covers her; one, two, shoulder up. The Mistress whips Cooper into a corner, then runs in shoulder-first. Cooper moves out of the way, then takes her down via a Soviet suplex, regaining her momentum. The Mistress shortly evens the match up by blocking a kick to the thigh. She whips Cooper into the ropes, then backdrops her, taking control. Cooper soon whips the Mistress into a corner and charges in, hammering her against the turnbuckles. Cooper pulls the Mistress out and follows up with a side suplex, turning the match back around. She soon goes for an enzuilariato, but the Mistress ducks it and puts Cooper on a turnbuckle. She takes Cooper down via a belly-to-back superplex, retaking control. Cooper shortly evens the match up with a backdrop. Both women stand toe-to-toe with each other. Cooper breaks the stalemate with a back suplex. The Mistress quickly evens the match up again with a lariat. Cooper shortly again ends the stand-off with another back suplex. She shortly goes for an over-the-shoulder stomachbreaker, but the Mistress counters with an elbowsmash, going back on the offensive. She shortly runs into the ropes; Cooper nails her with a kick and follows up with a double axhandle chop. The Mistress falls to the canvas; Cooper applies an STF. She lets go in 18 seconds and continues her assault. Cooper shortly reapplies the STF; she again lets go in 18 seconds and waits patiently for the Mistress to get up. When she does, Cooper drops her with the Bulldog, then pins her.
