WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION TUESDAY KNIGHTS 1/21/96 Live from the Lake Arena in Springfield, Missouri -----Main Event----- She-Devil v. Sellyna Shriek [Non-title match] Sweet Sue v. Li'l Cheetah [Princess of the WCA T & T Match] Nadia Williams v. Flower Power "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln v. The Vinnie Babe Valkyrie v. Angel Hunter Freedom v. Double J Nasty Tracy Turner v. Joanne Watson [Entourages banned to locker room] [Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.] [Fireworks explode as the camera shows a top-down view of the ring, the WCA logo in maroon letters on the white canvas. The camera descends and levels off, then circles the arena, showing the capacity audience. It stops at the other side of the aisle, where two WCA three-dimensional signs bracket the entryway; fireworks go off as the signs come into view. The image dissolves to commentator John Birmingham, seated at ringside with two monitors on his red-tableclothed table.] Birmingham: "Welcome, fans to another edition of Knights! Tonight you'll see Can-Am champion She-Devil take on Sellyna Shriek. Both of these women came out of grueling matches on Sunday night, but She-Devil was the more fortunate one, defeating Lovely Liz Strohlheim to retain the belt. Also tonight Sweet Sue faces Li'l Cheetah for the latter's Princess of the WCA Trophy and Tiara. Long-time fans will remember that Li'l Cheetah created these items out of Bodacious Janet Muniz's Munizican title and her gold locket. Now she's offering it up to Sue again; can Sue defeat the odds and defeat Cheetah, one of the greatest wrestlers in the WCA? We'll find out. Before that match Nadia Williams will face Flower Power. Like our main eventers tonight, Nadia and Flower both had matches at Stone Cold, but Flower was the only winner, defeating Valkyrie in a scaffold match, while the Pretty Lady lost the Television title to 'Psycho' Vicki V. In other action tonight, 'the Intimidator' Amber Axeln takes on the Vinnie Babe. The Intimidator, I am proud to say, is one of my favorite wrestlers, although some people in the back offices don't like her. At Stone Cold she defeated Bodacious Janet Muniz, Doombombing her from the apron to the concrete floor, doing serious damage to the Bodacious One. Right now Muniz's career may be over; on Thursday we'll show the second and what may be the last installment of her show Muniz's Musings. While I have no love for Muniz, I sincerely hopes she does recover. Plus, tonight Valkyrie takes on Angel Hunter. Both women are fan favorites, and both are gold-hungry, looking to climb the ladder to the top. One will suffer a setback in that goal tonight. Also, Freedom, victorious in her war with her sister at Stone Cold will be taking on the hard-working Double J. And in our opening match, Nasty Tracy Tur - oops, she lost the Turner name when Freedom pinned her Sunday - Nasty Tracy will take on Joanne Watson in an 'Entourages Banned from Ringside' match, which now means that only Watson's Hired Help is banned, as the Posse have thrown Tracy out. Here comes Tracy down the aisle now! She looks determined; let's hear what she has to say." Tracy: Excuse me for a second, but I need to definitely say something to all of the people, regardless of whether they are watching at home or are here in the arena. Joanne Watson, don't worry we will still be competing but first I must speak my mind. Mr. Kessler, I know that WCA air time is very special so I shall take as little of it as I can. Tracy: As all of you should know by now I am no longer involved with the Posse of Punishment. I have been voted out of the stable that I once helped put together by a group of people who I once called "friends." Jenny Black was supposedly my best friend but she had no problem with voting me out of the Posse. And Dream Master, the man who I thought would be standing by my side as I dealt with all of the battles that I have gotten myself in during my career in the WCA - he showed me how much he really cared. You know, when I left the arena in Buenos Aires I cried my heart out. [Tracy pauses] Tracy: So I go back to the hotel. Thankfully, I made arrangements to check into a seperate hotel from the rest of the Posse because I didn't want to be distracted as I prepared for my match against Freedom. And I will tell you, I had the worst night of sleep ever possible. You want to know why, because I started to actually do a lot of thinking about the things I've said and done to a whole lot of people during my involvment here in the WCA. There was a time last night when I said to myself, "Tracy......you've been such a b***h to a whole lot of people here in the WCA." You want to know something, last night that Tracy died!! That's right, there will be no more Nasty Tracy Turner giving the people of the WCA grief!! I have taken it upon myself to liberate the hate which poisoned my body and set it free. In honor of that accomplishment and as a tribute to the sister that I once treated with total and complete disdain I shall now ask all of you to start calling me Liberty. Freedom, if you are watching this in the locker room I hope that one day you will forgive me for all which I have said about you as well as all which I have done to you. You don't have to come out here right now and give me a hug becuase after everything I have done to you, I know that it won't be that simple. That is why I am determined to prove myself to you as well as the wrestling world how serious I am about changing my ways. Tracy: I had to get that out of the way but there are definitely some other people that I believe I need to apologize to specifically. The Mistress of Death, I hope that you can forgive me for the trouble I caused you. Thunder and Lightning I hope I can prove to you as well that I have reformed. I believe that I even owe an apology to Janet Muniz for an awful lot. I know that I had already apologized to her in the past but this time..........I really mean it! I know it will take a long time for me to undo the damage I caused towards some of the wrestlers of the WCA and maybe one day with some of you, perhaps I can wipe the slate clean. Tracy: Now I have some words for the Henchmen and the Posse!!! Personally I could care less if you eventually battled to flush each other down the toilet. However, if any of you get in my way, I will do my part to wipe you off the face of this planet, even if I have to do it alone. Liberty has arrived and she is most definitely here to stay. I thank you all for your time and now Joanne, you are first on my new hit parade and you can't accuse the Posse of saving me now!! ===== [Entourages Banned from Ringside] ---------- Liberty v. Joanne Watson ---------- Both women lock up, then Watson executes a slingshot facebuster. She follows up with a piledriver. Liberty stands up and shakes her head, clearing it. She takes Watson down with a rolling kick, going on the offensive. She soon runs into the ropes; Watson puts her in the Full Nelson. Liberty reaches the ropes after 12 seconds; Watson rewards her with a double underhook suplex, turning things around. After 2 1/2 minutes Watson runs into the ropes. Liberty catches her in spinning headscissors. Both women stand up; Liberty puts Watson in an abdominal stretch, resetting the pace of the match. Watson shortly blocks an armdrag takedown with a lariat. She kicks Liberty in the head as the latter gets back up. Watson executes a springbboard rana, retaking control of the match. After almost 3 minutes Watson takes Liberty down with a faceslam. She goes to one knee and puts Liberty in the Full Nelson. Liberty submits after 16 seconds. (7:59) --------- Freedom (w/ the Mistress of Death) v. Double J ---------- Double J ducks a spinning leg lariat. She whips Freedom to the ropes, then executes a small package. Freedom kicks out in two; Double J puts her in a leg hammerlock, taking control of the match. She shortly takes Freedom down with a clothesline, then does the Texas Two-Step as the latter picks herself up. Double J sends her to the ropes; both women take the other down in a dual clothesline. Freedom gets up ahead of Double J and runs to the ropes; Double J follows her in and drives her knee into the small of the back. Freedom responds with a spinning leg lariat, turning things around. Double J soon catches her in an inside cradle; Freedom gets her shoulder up in two. Double J puts her in a front facelock. Freedom manages to pull herself to the ropes in 30 seconds. She counters a hiptoss with a backslide, but only gets a two count. Freedom puts Double J in a sleeper, resuming her offensive. After a minute Double J executes a legdrag takedown, then applies a spinning toe hold, turning things around. After an intense 4 1/2 minutes Double J sends Freedom to the ropes. Freedom rebounds and plants Double J on the mat with a side suplex. Double J gets back on her feet, surprised. Freedom whips her to the ropes, then takes her down with an enzuigiri. Double J closes her eyes, then picks herself up and ducks a jumping elbow thrust. She sends Freedom to the ropes, then ducks a spinning leg lariat. She executes a legdrag takedown, retaking control. Freedom shortly blocks a bodyslam, then she runs to the ropes, rebounds, and backdrops Double J. She follows up with a bodyslam, then hammers her with an elbowdrop from the second turnbuckle. Freedom returns to the corner, then pummels Double J with the 4th of July and pins her. (10:44) ---------- Valkyrie (w/ "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln v. Angel Hunter (w/ Janet Strong) ---------- Both women lock up, then Hunter whips Valkyrie to the ropes. Valkyrie rebounds and nails her with a rolling kick. Hunter falls into the ropes, then stands upright again and keeps Valkyrie down with a forearm smash, going on the attack. Hunter shortly runs into the ropes; Valkyrie catches her totally off-guard and takes her down with the Valkyrian Storm. Hunter kicks out with force in two. She gets up, rubbing the back of her head, then puts Valkyrie in an abdominal suplex. Valkyrie quickly hiptosses her way out. Hunter gets up again and executes a belly-to-back, then puts Valkyrie in the Rack. Valkyrie kicks out in 11 seconds. (1:12) ---------- "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln (w/ Valkyrie) v. The Vinnie Babe (w/ Vinnie Gambino) ---------- Both women lock up. The Vinnie Babe ducks neck-scissors, then nails Axeln with a drop-kick to the knee. She executes a fisherman suplex and cover; one, two, kickout. The Vinnie Babe gets up and runs into the ropes. She misses with a clothesline, rebounds, and rocks Axeln with a roundhouse punch. Axeln blocks another and applies a front facelock, resetting the pace of the match. The Vinnie Babe soon picks her up. Axeln blocks being set on a turnbuckle; the Vinnie Babe drops her, then nails her with a drop-kick to the knee. She follows up with a fisherman suplex and covers; again Axeln kicks out in two. The Vinnie Babe executes a double underhook backbreaker, turning the match around. Axeln shortly blocks a snap mare. She runs into the ropes, then executes a headlock takedown, gaining the advantage. The Vinnie Babe shortly blocks an abdominal stretch with a hiptoss. She heads for the top rope; Axeln rolls away from a flying elbowdrop. The Vinnie Babe yanks her out of the ring, then rolls out as well. Axeln heads around the ringpost and reenters. The Vinnie Babe follows her in; Axeln executes an enzuilariato, taking control of the match. The Vinnie Babe soon nails her with an atomic drop. She follows up with a drag suplex; one, two, kickout. The Vinnie Babe argues with the ref over the speed of the count, then faces the crowd and raises her arms up. Axeln sits up, then gets up. The Vinnie Babe notices and runs into the ropes. She attempts to block another abdominal stretch, but Axeln in turn blocks her hiptoss with a backslide, only to get a two count. She blocks a double underhook backbreaker, then stands up and nails the Vinnie Babe with a back heel kick, going back on the offensive. The Vinnie Babe soon reverses a whip to the ropes, then backdrops Axeln. She drops and covers; Axeln gets her shoulder up in two. She sits up, then gets up and reverses a whip to a corner. Axeln runs in shoulder-first and rams into the Vinnie Babe, then the latter reverses a whip to the ropes. Axeln returns with neck-scissors, resuming her assault. She shortly runs into the ropes; the Vinnie Babe jumps a shoulderblock, then turns and nails Axeln with a kick to the midsection. She takes the Intimidator down with a kick to the midsection, turning the match around. Axeln shortly ducks a kick to the head. She executes a headlock takedown, then nails the Vinnie Babe with a swinging punch, retaking control. After ninety seconds she puts the Vinnie Babe in a cobra clutch; the Vinnie Babe submits after 5 seconds. (9:03) ---------- Nadia Williams (w/ Jonathan Schiz) v. Flower Power [w/ Dr. Insane] ---------- Both women lock up, then Power executes a backslide and puts her feet on the ropes for added leverage; Williams kicks out in two. Power executes a dragon suplex; again Williams kicks out in two. Power sends her to a corner. Williams slams into the turnbuckles, then staggers back out. Power executes a high cross body; Williams kicks out easily. Power heads to the top turnbuckle, then hammers Williams with a flying karate chop, continuing her offensive. Williams shortly rolls away from a kneedrop. She pulls Power up, then sends her to the ropes. Williams nails Power with a clothesline, taking charge. Power shortly reverses a whip to the ropes; Williams ducks an enzuilariato. She whips Power to a corner; Power charges out and doubles her over with a kick to the midsection. She returns to the corner and climbs the turnbuckles, then hammers Williams with a flying karate chop, taking control of the match. Williams very shortly reverses a whip to the ropes. She misses with a kick, then spins around and stops Power with an enzuilariato. Power gets back up; Williams reverses a whip to a corner. She runs in shoulder-first; Power moves out of the way. Williams nails the ringpost; Power pulls her out, then plants her on the canvas with a gutwrench suplex, gaining the advantage. After a minute she climbs the turnbuckles. Williams walks into the corner and throws her to the mat, then drops down and hooks a leg; Power kicks out in two. Williams hiptosses her, then runs into the ropes. Power misses with a shoulderblock, then spins around and knocks Williams down with a kick, taking control. Williams soon reverses a whip to the ropes. Power blocks a poke to the eye; Williams ducks a flying clothesline. She executes a ropeflip hiptoss, turning the match around. She soon executes a hiptoss. Power rolls to her knees and begs off; Williams runs to the ropes. Power stops her with an enzuilariato, then pounds her with a splash; Williams kicks out in two. She gets back on her feet, then blocks a pick-up. She whips Power to the ropes; Power blocks a facerake. She chokes Williams, then lets go in four and confronts the ref. Williams uses the opportunity to drive her knee into Power's back, regaining her momentum. Power shortly counters a pick-up with a lariat. She whips Williams to the ropes, then nails her with a shoulderblock, going back on the offensive. After 2 minutes Williams whips Power to the ropes, then jumps a kick. She decks Power with a clothesline, turning the match around again. Power shortly blocks a surfboard and stands up. She shows Williams the peace sign, then decks her with the Incense and Peppermints. Power drops down and pins Williams. (9:52) *Princess of the WCA T & T Match* ---------- Sweet Sue (w/ Lovely Liz Strohlheim and Karen "Bugs" Huffman) v. Li'l Cheetah (w/ Tall Troll) ---------- Both women circle each other, then lock up. Cheetah executes a stomachbreaker; Sue backdrops out of a bomb. She executes an armdrag takedown; Cheetah pops back up. Sue nails her with a forearm smash; Cheetah ignores it. Sue executes another armdrag, gaining the advantage. Cheetah shortly ducks a lariat. She whips Sue to the ropes, then knocks her down with a shoulderblock. She drops down for the pin; Sue kicks out in two. Cheetah gets up and demands a faster count; Sue catches her in an inside cradle. Cheetah kicks out in two. Both women stand up; Sue whips Cheetah to the ropes, then backdrops her, taking control of the match. After 2 minutes Cheetah blocks a forearm smash. Sue dodges a tackle; Cheetah blocks a headbutt. She takes Sue down in a front slam, turning the match around. A minute later Sue blocks a pick-up. She puts Cheetah in a cobra clutch; Cheetah manages to reach the ropes after 8 seconds. Sue runs into the ropes, then knocks Cheetah down with a shoulderblock, turning things around. She shortly puts Cheetah in a bearhug. Cheetah breaks the hold after 7 seconds, then whips Sue to the ropes. She executes a small package and pulls the tights; one, two, thr-Huffman points it out to the ref. Both women get up; Sue reverses a whip to a corner. Cheetah charges out, then counters a takeover suplex with a belly-to-belly; Sue kicks out in two. She rolls away from a diving headbutt and stands up. Cheetah picks herself up, then reverses a whip to the ropes. Sue returns with a punch to the midsection, retaking control. Cheetah shortly puts her into an abdominal stretch, taking charge. Sue shortly counters a backspin DDT with a belly-to-belly; Cheetah kicks out in two. Sue gets up first and nails her with a kneelift. She executes a single-leg takedown, gaining the advantage. After 3 minutes she runs into the ropes. Cheetah stops her with a kick, then removes the ref's belt and flogs Sue with it. The ref takes his belt away from her; Cheetah nails Sue with the Heart Punch, then pins her. (12:05) ===== Birmingham: "Li'l Cheetah again retains her unofficial Princess of the WCA Trophy and Tiara, defeating Sweet Sue for the second time in a defense of the T & T. Sweet Sue is starting to go into a losing streak, with losses in tag action to the Bounty Hunters, a loss to Lovely Liz, a loss to Karen at StoneCold, and a loss tonight. But I have no doubts that, like so many times before, she'll soon reverse the trend and end up stronger than ever before. Up next is our main event tonight. Since we are so close to the hour, if it runs over we'll keep the tapes rolling and present the end to you on the next Theatre of War. This is a non-title match between the Can-Am champion She-Devil and Sellyna Shriek. She-Devil has proven that her win over the Mistress was no fluke with a solid victory over Lovely Liz Strohlheim at Stone Cold. Sellyna Shriek, meanwhile, is looking for a victory to ignite her simmering career since her return to the WCA ring. Let's go to the ring announcer." Ring Announcer: "Ladies and gentlemen, tonight's main event is a non-title match set for one fall! Introducing first: accompanied to the ring by her manager Snakebite, from parts unknown, weighing in at 114 pounds, Sellyna Shriek!" [Many fans boo Shriek and Snakebite as they walk down the aisle. Shriek, dressed in a green-and-white striped bodysuit, ignores them and enters the ring.] "Her opponent: accompanied to the ring by her manager Vinnie Gambino, from New York, New York, weighing in at 222 pounds, the WCA Can-Am champion She-Devil!" [She-Devil and Gambino stomp down the aisle, with the majority of the arena hurling insults and taunts at them. They ignore the hostile crowd and enter the ring.] Birmingham's voice: "She-Devil hands her belt to Gambino, who holds it up proudly. Yeah, she earned it. Gambino leaves the ring. Referee Julian Pace calls for the bell; both women lock up! Shriek whips she-Devil to the ropes; She-Devil blocks a facerake. She whips Shriek to the side ropes, then hiptosses her. She flips off a particularly hostile fan, enraging the entire section. She-Devil rants at them, then turns and nails a standing Shriek with a kick to the midsection. She runs to the side ropes, ducks a clothesline, spins around and executes a short lariat; one, two, kickout. She-Devil immediately hammers Shriek with a diving headbutt. Shriek needs to start getting into the swing of things now. She begins by reversing a whip to the ropes, follows She-Devil in and drives her knee into the small of the back. She-Devil blocks a cobra clutch with an armdrag. "She lays Shriek out with a front-layout suplex. The crowd grows surly again; She-Devil gives them the finger! Now I see why we got a TV-M rating. Shriek sits up. She-Devil sees it an an invitation to apply a full - Shriek blocks it. She gets up; She-Devil executes a rolling reverse cradle, but only gets a one count. She-Devil scoops her up; Shriek blocks being set on a turnbuckle. She-Devil sets her down; Shriek reverses a whip to the ropes, then counters a pick-up with a lariat. She-Devil sits up, surprised. She gets up; Shriek takes her down with an ipponzei and covers; one, two, kickout. Shriek follows up with a tiger driver; She-Devil kicks out in two. "Shriek stands back up. She-Devil follows suit; Shriek takes her down with a backdrop driver! Sellyna certainly took some punishment from the Scandalous Ones at Stone Cold, but seems to have recovered nicely. She whips She-Devil to the ropes, then bodyslams her. The Can-Am champ picks herself up. Shriek slaps her; She-Devil returns it. Shriek dodges a forearm to the back, then slips behind the champ and nails her with a knee to the back. She executes an armdrag. Shriek prefers the mat holds, but can fight most any style that comes her way, making her a tough opponent. She-Devil blocks a backdrop and hits Shriek with a forearm to the back, taking her down. Shriek blocks a full nelson, then puts She-Devil in a cobra clutch. She-Devil quickly breaks the hold and stands up. Shriek sends her to a corner. "She-Devil blocks a belly-to-belly and shoves Shriek out of the corner. Shriek ducks neck-scissors. She-Devil picks herself up; Shriek slaps her. She-Devil returns it, then whips Shriek to the rope - Gambino pulls the top rope down! Pace walks over and warns him; Gambino jumps onto the apron as She-Devil heads to the floor. She nails Shriek with a forearm to the back - Gambino just spat into Pace's eye! Pace turns and calls for the bell. He lets a lot of things slide, but even he won't settle for such blatant abuse." RA: "Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the match as a result of a disqualification: in 4 minutes, 12 seconds, Sellyna Shriek!" [Pace heads to the floor, then manages to break Shriek and She-Devil apart. He raises Shriek's arm up in victory.] Birmingham: "Sellyna Shriek will win the match, not because she's the better wrestler, but because Vinnie can't control himself. Not the most dignified way to win, of course. Fans, we are out of time. Coming up this Thursday on Theatre of War, Freedom takes on Flower Power, 'Nasty Girl' Amber Jane hopes to improve her standing by facing Double D - that's Debbie Diamond to you, Monique makes her singles debut against Femme Fatale as her tag partner Mary Williams does the same against Television champion 'Psycho' Vicki V in a non-title match, and more! I'm John Birmingham; have a great evening!" [Closing credits appear.]
