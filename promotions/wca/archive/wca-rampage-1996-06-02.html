Crusha counters with a cross-body block and pins him. (20:00) -------------------- Double J v. Bunny (MGR: Hurly Burly w/ Brian Damage and Eddy Gonzalez) -------------------- Bunny goes for a flying clothesline, but Double J ducks it and goes for a legsweep faceslam. Double J whips her into the ropes, then mises with a clothesline. He shorlty goes for a side suplex, but Joe blocks itand takes him down with a knee to the back. Joe gets the big man in a German suplex and pins him. (6:27) <Women's Tag Team Titles Match> -------------------- Champions Delicious Dolls (Double D Debbie Diamond and Tammi Cooper) v. Street Assasins (Li'l Cheetah and the Assasin) (MGR: Tall Troll) -------------------- Diamond and Cheetah start. Diamond goes for an abdominal stretch, but Cheetah counters with a hiptoss and goes on the offensive with an elbowsmash. Diamond tags out to Cooper, who turns the match around with a lariat. She goes for the Bulldog, but Cheetah throws her off and attempts to whip her into a corner. Cooper reverses it, then charges in. She hits Cheetah, then continues her offensive with a hiptoss. Diamon shortly enters the ring and together they whip Cheetah into the ropes. Cheetah rebounds and blocks their double backdrop with a double headsmash. The Assasin enters the ring and the match degenerates into an all-out brawl. The ref forces the illegal women out, then Cheetah takes Cooper down with a kick. She goes for the pin, but Cooper kicks out in two. Cooper gets up and evens the match up with a backbreaker. She goes for a DDT, but Cheetah counters with a backdrop. Cooper in turn re-reverses it into a sunset flip; Cheetah kicks out in two. Both women stand toe-to-toe with each other. Cooper nails Cheetah with a spinning mule kick, then Cheetah takes her down with a Soviet suplex. 90 seconds later Cheetah breaks the stalemate with a figure-four leglock. Diamond makes the save, then Cooper tags out to Diamond. Both women hit Cheetah with a double elbowsmash before Cooper leaves the ring. Diamond goes for a bulldog, but Cheetah throws her off and goes on the offensive with a diving elbowsmash. The Assasin enters the ring and nails Diamond with a lariat. Cheetah follows up with a slingshot and continues. Cheetah shortly tags out to the Assasin, then nails Diamond with a dropkick. the Assasin follows up with an enzuigiri. Diamond evens the match up with an armdrag takedown. She goes for an abdominal stretch, but the Assasin counters with a hiptoss. Diamond in turn re-reverses it into a backslide; the Assasin kicks out in two. The Assasin whips Diamond into the ropes; Troll trips Diamond. The ref warns him while Diamond rebounds and takes the Assasin down with a shoulderblock. She goes for the cover, but the ref is still distracted with Troll. The Assasin takes control of the match with a fist to the midsection. Diamond shortly tags out to Cooper. Cooper nails the Assasin with a Samoan Drop, then goes for a lariat. The Assasin ducks it and tags out to Cheetah. They double backdrop Cooper; Cooper comes back with a double clothesline. She kicks the Assasin out of the ring and puts Cheetah in a sleeper. Cheetah elbowsmashes her way out after 16 seconds; Cooper rewards her with a swinging punch. Cheetah shortly turns the match around with a spinning DDT. Cooper tags out to Diamon a minute later; Diamond catches Cheetah in a Norther Lights suplex. Cheetah kicks out in tw; Diamond rewards her with a gutwrench suplex. Cooper shortly enters the ring, but gets cut off. The Assasin uses the opportunity to enter the ring and kick Diamond in the head. Diamond comes back with a clothesline; the Assasin leaves the ring. Diamond regains her momentum with flying headscissors. She takes Cheetah down with a short lariat; Cheetah kicks out of the ensuing cover in two. Diamond goes for a takeover suplex, but Cheetah counters with a belly-to-belly suplex and pins Diamond. The Street Assasins are the new Women's Tag Team Champions. (19:18)
