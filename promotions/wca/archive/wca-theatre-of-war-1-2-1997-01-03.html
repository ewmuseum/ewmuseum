Hanson rebounds and counters a takeover suplex with a belly-to-belly; Hunter kicks out in two. She gets up before Hanson and nails her with a chop, turning the match around. Hanson beats Hunter to her feet, then whips her to the ropes. She stops Hunter with a kick, going back on the offensive. She very shortly sends Hunter to the ropes again; Hunter returns with an elbow to the face. She follows up with a drop-kick to the knee, retaking control. Hanson shortly executes a sunset flip; Hunter easily kicks out. Hanson gets up and executes a fisherman suplex, pinning Hunter. (11:12) ---------- Double J v. Femme Fatale (w/ Snakebite) ---------- Fatale dodges a drop-kick to the knee; Double J backdrops out of a double underhook suplex. She whips Fatale to the ropes, then ducks a clothesline. Fatale rebounds again and reverses a small package; Double J kicks out in one. She blocks a facerake, then stands up and hiptosses Fatale. Fatale gets up and legsweeps Double J, beginning her assault. After 2 minutes Double J blocks a facerake. She nails Fatale with a clothesline, then applies an abdominal stretch, turning the match around. Fatale shortly dodges a drop-kick to the knee. Double J gets up; Fatale counters a bodyslam with a small package. Double J kicks out in two; both women stand up. Fatale executes a legsweep faceslam, retaking control of the match. Double J shortly nails her with a back heel kick, retaking control. After a minute Fatale blocks a punch. She nails Double J with a spinning mule kick, then whips her to the ropes. Double J returns with a bearhug, resuming her offensive. A minute later Fatale catches her in a fisherman suplex and covers; one, two, kickout. Fatale throws Double J out of the ring, then joins her on the floor and executes the Perfect Plex. Double J slowly gets up; Fatale nails her with a diving elbowsmash. She throws Double J back into the ring, then reenters herself. Double J blocks a side suplex and hiptosses her, retaking control. After an intense five minutes Fatale blocks another hiptoss and whips Double J to a corner. She charges in and executes a belly-to-belly suplex. Double J counters a double reverse chinlock with a facerake, then stands up and nails Fatale with a drop-kick to the knee. Fatale staggers, then stands upright and blocks a back heel kick. Double J reverses a whip to the ropes, then nails Fatale with a clothesline, retaking control. She very shortly whips Fatale to the ropes; Fatale rebounds and stops just behind Double J, then legsweeps her, turning the match around. Double J shortly ducks a leg lariat, then executes a backslide. Fatale kicks out just in time; both women stand up. Double J slaps her, then punches her. She executes a legdrag takedown, taking control of the match. After ninety seconds Fatale catches Double J in a double underhook suplex. She puts Double J on a turnbuckle, then climbs up and executes a belly-to-belly superplex. She covers Double J; the latter barely kicks out in time. Fatale pulls her up, then executes the Perfect Plex and pins Double J.
