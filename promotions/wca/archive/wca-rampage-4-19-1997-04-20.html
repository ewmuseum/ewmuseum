WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION ( http://www.inet-images.com/skessler/WCA/ ) RAMPAGE APRIL 19, 1997 Live from the Lake Arena in Springfield, Missouri -----Main Event----- =World Title Match= Shelly v. Janet Strong "Psycho" Vicki V v. Bodacious Janet Muniz (Best of 11 Match 7) The Scandal Sheet "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln v. Freedom Henchmen of Hades v. Hardcore / Rising Sun (WarGames Match) Shannon Stevens v. Liberty -------------------- Shannon Stevens (w/ Freedom) v. Liberty (w/ Bodacious Janet Muniz, "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln, and the Mistress of Death) -------------------- Both women lock up. Stevens blocks a pick-up with a forearm blow, then she executes a rolling reverse cradle; Liberty kicks out in two. Stevens gets up, then she sends Liberty back to the mat via a spinning leg lariat, taking control of the match. After 2 1/2 minutes Liberty blocks a waistlock suplex, then she executes a front-layout suplex. Stevens prevents a Boston crab and rolls out of the ring, then she gets up on the apron and reenters. Liberty sends her to the far ropes, then she nails Stevens with a chop, starting her own offense. After another 2 1/2 minutes Stevens turns a crucifix into a Samoan Drop. She puts Liberty into a camel clutch, turning the match back around. After 2 minutes she throws Liberty out of the ring, then she goes out after her. Stevens slams a standing Liberty's face onto the ringsteps, then Liberty whips her around to the ringpost. Liberty grabs Stevens' head and slams her face into the post, then she executes a back suplex. Liberty reenters the ring and rests while the ref starts counting; one .. two .. three .. four .. five .. six .. seven .. eight . Stevens reenters the ring. Liberty comes off a far turnbuckle with a flying axhandle, sending Stevens stumbling forward. Liberty goes to the corner nearest Stevens, then she executes a flying sunset flip, pinning Shannon. (7:16) (Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.) (Fireworks explode as the camera shows the crimson WCA logo on the white ring canvas. The camera descends rapidly, then levels off facing the aisle. The camera swerves left, panning out over the capacity crowd and circling the ring, ending its focus on the iridescent three-dimensional WCA letters bracketing the entrance to the backstage area. The referee for the first match heads to the ring.) *WarGames Match* (One-Minute Intervals) -------------------- Henchmen of Hades (Mary Williams, Monique, Blood, Nadia Williams, and Red) (w/ Crimson) v. Posse of Punishment ("Nasty Girl" Amber Jane, "Dominatress" Kathy Lynn, Aya Ichijou, Kazumi Hayakawa, and Asano Otani) -------------------- Monique and Jane start. Crimson hands Monique a chair, then she throws it into the cage and quickly enters, then she turns and closes the door. She turns around and gets hit by the chair as Jane swings it at her. Monique takes the chair away and uppercuts Jane with it. She grinds Jane's face into the cage, then sends her to the far ropes. Jane returns with an elbow to the face, taking control of the match. The Henchmen win the coin toss; Mary enters the ring. Jane meets her with an armdrag; Monique bangs her over the head with the chair, putting Jane on the defensive. Jane shortly takes Monique down via a belly-to-belly, then Mary misses with a legdrop, hammering her partner instead. Jane takes Mary down with a handspring elbow, managing to regain the upper hand. Ichijou shortly enters the cage. She drops down to avoid a springboard shoulderblock, then she gets back up and heads over to Jane and Monique. Williams runs after her, then Ichijou reverses a whip to the ropes. Mary hooks the ropes to avoid a drop-kick, then she puts Ichijou into a double reverse chinlock, taking control of their battle as Monique and Jane slug it out. The Henchman Red shortly hits the ring; she locks up with Ichijou, who drives her back to the cage door. Ichijou runs Red's face along the wire, then Red reverses an Irish whip and stops Ichijou with a facerake, taking control of their battle as Mary and Monique gang up on Jane in the background. The camera cuts to their battle; Mary decks Jane with a palm blow. She runs to the far ropes as Monique stomps on Jane's face. Mary hits her with a senton, then the camera reveals Otani entering the ring. Otani nails Red with a kick, then she rushes Mary and punches her. Mary legsweeps Otani, then she executes a standing moonsault, taking control of their battle. The camera shortly cuts to Monique battling with Ichijou; Monique puts her into a standing wristlock. Ichijou begins to work her way out as Jane takes Red down with a snap suplex in the background. Monique drops the hold just before Ichijou breaks free, then she powerslams Aya, taking control of their battle. Nadia very shortly hits the ring. She and Mary double-team Otani; Ichijou goes to her friend, then both women nail Nadia with a double cartwheel kick. Monique runs over and sends Otani to the ropes. Otani rebounds and rocks Monique with an elbow, separating them from the Nadia-Mary-Ichijou battle. Hayakawa enters the ring, making the match even again. Red shortly comes over and trades Otani for Jane. Otani nails her with a clothesline, sending Red to the mat. Otani stomps on Red, taking control of their battle. Otani shortly keeps Red down with a full nelson, then Nadia rushes over to them. Otani shoves Red toward her; Nadia knocks her partner aside, then she executes a forearm smash, putting Otani back on the defensive. Blood shortly enters the ring, then she chooses to help Red pummel Jane. Jane puts Red into a corner, then enters via a handspring elbow. Blood executes the same move, doing damage to both women in the corner. At the same time, Ichijou legsweeps Monique, then she puts her into an STF. Mary makes the save with the chair, then Hayakawa knocks Mary down and goes to a corner. She executes a flying shoulderblock, knocking Mary back into Blood. Jane uses the opportunity to rock Blood with a European uppercut, then Red puts her into a full nelson as Lynn enters the ring, making the scene complete. Lynn nails Blood with a drop-kick as Jane breaks Red's full nelson, taking control of their battle. Monique shortly takes the chair and brings it down on Ichijou's head, dazing her. Mary picks the chair up and hits Jane with it. Jane takes the chair away and buts it into Mary's back, then Lynn grabs the chair and hits Nadia with it. Lynn drops the chair; Hayakawa picks it up and swings at Nadia, who ducks down; Hayakawa nails Lynn. Blood hammers Hayakawa with a flying forearm, leaving Red to take on a stumbling Lynn. Lynn shortly nails Red with a swinging punch, then she sends Red flying into Nadia. Nadia falls to the mat; Lynn retrieves the chair, then she returns to a prone Nadia and stomps on her stomach. Lynn turns Nadia over and spikes the chair into the small of her back, then she flips Nadia back onto her back. Lynn puts Nadia into a scorpion deathlock. Nadia submits after 8 seconds; the Posse win the match. (11:08) -------------------- "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln (w/ Bodacious Janet Muniz, Liberty, and the Mistress of Death) v. Freedom (w/ Shannon Stevens) -------------------- Axeln runs to the ropes as soon as she enters the ring, then Freedom backdrops her as the bell rings. Axeln pops back up and reverses a whip to a corner, then she runs in and drives her knee into Freedom's ribs. Freedom reverses an Irish whip out; Axeln ducks a clothesline, rebounds, and backdrops Freedom, taking control of the match. Freedom shortly takes Axeln down with neck-scissors. She runs to a corner as Axeln gets back up, then she rebounds off the turnbuckles with a flying cross body press; Axeln easily kicks out. Freedom catches a rising Axeln in a backflip cradle; Axeln kicks out in two. Freedom plants Axeln on the mat via a front-layout suplex, taking control of the match. She shortly sends Axeln to the ropes; Axeln rebounds and rushes back to Freedom, putting her into an abdominal stretch in the center of the ring to slow the match down. She lets Freedom go after 24 seconds; Freedom runs to the ropes. Axeln sidesteps a drop-kick to the knee. Freedom briefly rests, then she gets back up, sidestepping a kneelift in the process. Axeln gets back up quicker; Freedom ducks neck-scissors. Axeln gets up slower than the last time, then Freedom sends her to the ropes. Axeln returns and takes Freedom down with a side suplex, retaking control of the match. She shortly sends Freedom to the ropes; Freedom rebounds and backdrops Axeln. She runs to the ropes, jumps over a rising Axeln, rebounds, and rocks the Intimidator with a kick. Freedom goes to a corner and climbs to the top, then she takes Axeln down with a flying somersault bodyblock, retaking control. She very shortly sends Axeln to a corner and runs in shoulder-first; Axeln raises her knees up to block her. Freedom staggers back into the center of the ring, then Axeln puts her into a full nelson, starting a new gameplan. Freedom shortly reverses an Irish whip, then she ducks a lariat and runs to a corner. Axeln follows her in; Freedom rebounds off the 'buckles with a flying cross body press, but only gets a one count. Axeln gets up as Freedom taunts some jeering fans. Axeln spins Freedom around and puts her into a front facelock, resuming her offense. After ninety seconds Freedom reverses an Irish whip. She misses a shoulderblock, Axeln misses with a clothesline, Freedom backdrops Axeln. Axeln gets back up; she blocks a legdrag, then Freedom executes a small package. Axeln quickly kicks out and gets back up. She runs to the ropes, then she blocks a back heel kick. Freedom ducks a kick tot he head; Axeln settles for a swinging punch, retaking control of the match. Freedom shortly blocks short-arm scissors. She gets up and executes a rolling reverse cradle; Axeln kicks out in two. Both women get back up, then Freedom rocks Axeln with a palm blow, taking control of the match. Axeln shortly counters a back suplex with a headlock takedown, turning things back around. Freedom shortly blocks an abdominal stretch with a hiptoss. She runs to the ropes, jumps over a rising Axeln, rebounds, jumps over Amber again, rebounds, and decks Axeln with a clothesline, retaking control once again. Axeln shortly backdrops Freedom in desperation, then she grabs Freedom by the hair and pulls her to a corner. Axeln flashes the V-sign, then she quickly executes the Doombomb. Axeln covers Freedom, pinning her. (10:12) ===== Sharpe: "'The Intimidator' Amber Axeln pulls a win out over Click-enemy Freedom, a win that almost wasn't.
