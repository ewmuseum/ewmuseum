WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION RAMPAGE 1/4/97 Stone Cold Tour Live from Bogota, Colombia -----Main Event----- <Television Title Match> Janet Strong v. "Dominatress" Kathy Lynn Angel Hunter v. Freedom Femme Fatale v. Vonya Bounty Hunters v. Springfield Succulents (Baseball Bat Best of 7 Series: Match 3) <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.> <Fireworks explode as the camera shows a top-down view of the ring, highlighting the maroon WCA logo on the canvas. The camera descends, panning to the right and leveling off, showing the capacity crowd. It focuses on the end of the aisle, where 'WCA' frames the entrance to the locker room. Several WCA officials walk down the aisle.> *Baseball Bat Best of 7 Series: Match 3* ---------- Bounty Hunters (Red and Black) (w/ Semiramis and Panthra) v. Springfield Succulents (Sweet Sue and Karen "Bugs" Huffman) (w/ Lovely Liz Strohlheim) ---------- Sue and Red lock up, then Red ducks a lariat and drops her bat. She applies a sleeper, taking control of the match. Sue shortly catches her in a back suplex and tags in Huffman. Black enters the ring and ensures Sue's departure, then returns to her corner as Red drives her knee into Huffman's back. She picks up her bat and nails Huffman in the head with it. Huffman staggers back, then blocks a standing wristlock and executes a German suplex; one, two, Panthra pulls the ref out of the ring. Huffman takes Red down with a drop-kick, taking control of the match. Red shortly tags in Black; Sue enters and shoves Red out of the ring, then she and Huffman send Black to the ropes. They nail her with a double blow from the butts of their bats, then Sue leaves as Huffman sends Black back to the ropes. Black returns with a bunt, going on the offensive. After a vicious minute she puts Huffman in a front facelock. Huffman hits Black's leg with her bat, breaking the hold. Huffman starts to cry, then tags in Sue. They nail Black with a double-swing, one to the back, the other to the front. They repeat the blows, then do the same yet again. The Succulents whip Black o the ropes, then deck her with a dual bat-clothesline. Sue drops her bat and follows up with a rolling reverse cradle; one, two, thr-kickout! Sue puts Black in a full nelson, resetting the pace of the match. After 3 minutes Black blocks another rolling reverse cradle and tags in Red. Huffman attempt to enter, but gets cut off. The Bounty Hunters double-kick Sue in the midsection, then whip her to the ropes. Sue returns, clotheslining Black and decking Red with her bat. Black rolls out of the ring as Red gets back on her feet. Sue staggers her with a headbutt; Red drops her bat, then counters a takeover suplex with a belly-to-belly. Sue easily kicks out; Black enters the ring and brings her bat down on Sue's head, laying her out on the mat. Red applies short arm scissors, going back on the offensive. After 2 minutes Sue escapes the punishment and tags in Huffman, who executes a rolling reverse cradle into a bridge. Red gets her shoulder up in two, then gets back up. Huffman executes a sunset flip; Red kicks out in two. Huffman throws Red out of the ring. The ref begins counting one, two, three, four, Red reenters. Huffman takes her down with a drop-kick, going on the attack. After 2 minutes Huffman sends Red to the ropes. Red returns and slaps Huffman, then throws her out of the ring and joins her on the floor. She whips Huffman to the guardrail, then slams her forehead on the steel twice. Panthra comes up on Huffman, but she nails her with a slap. Huffman throws Red back into the ring, then reenters herself. Sue joins her in the ring; Red ducks a double elbowsmash, then turns and nails them with a double clothesline. Sue leaves the ring as Red follows up with a chop, turning the match around. After 3 minutes she tags in Black. Sue enters the ring and throws Red out, then slugs Black with her bat. Huffman follows suit as Sue leaves the ring. Black blocks a German suplex, then sends Huffman into the ropes. She executes a rolling reverse cradle; Huffman kicks out in two. Both women stand up; Black takes Huffman down with a belly-to-belly; one, two, in the ropes. Huffman pulls herself up with the ropes, tears welling up in her eyes. Black nails her in the back with a swing of the bat, causing Huffman to cry openly. Black pulls her back into the ring; Huffman counters an abdominal stretch with a hiptoss. Black sits up, then picks herself up. She reverses a whip to the ropes; Huffman stops her with a kick, taking charge. Black very shortly blocks a full nelson, then executes an enzuigiri, turning things around. Huffman very shortly blocks a kick to the midsection, then throws Black out of the ring. She goes to the floor, then hammers Black in a side suplex. Huffman picks Black up, then sends her head-first into the guardrail. Panthra distracts Huffman, allowing Black to reenter the ring. Huffman rolls back in; Black applies an armlock leglock, taking charge. Huffman shortly blocks another armlock leglock, then tags out to Sue. Black dodges a kneelift; Sue lands on her back, then blocks a figure-four sleeper. She picks herself up, retrieves her bat, then sends Black to the ropes and nails her with a blow from her bat, going on the offensive. After 2 minutes she puts Black in a cobra clutch. Black breaks the hold after 5 seconds, then applies short arm scissors, turning the match around. After 2 minutes she sends Sue to a neutral corner, then runs in shoulder-first. Sue lurches out of the corner, tagging in Huffman. Sue moves the bat to her left hand, then brings it down in tandem with Huffman onto Black's collarbones. Sue follows up with a blow to the head, then leaves the ring. Huffman drops her bat and puts Black in a full nelson. Black quickly breaks the hold. She takes Huffman down with a DDT, changing the complexion of the match. Red shortly enter the ring, then sets up her and Black's bats on the mat. Semiramis keeps Sue occupied while the Bounty Hunters drive Huffman onto the bats with the Spiked Piledriver, then Black pins Huffman. The Bounty Hunters are 2-1 in the series. (22:58) ---------- Femme Fatale (w/ Snakebite) v. Vonya (w/ Night Crawler) ---------- Both women lock up, then Fatale takes her down with a rolling kick, taking control of the match. Vonya soon reverses a whip to the ropes. She blocks the Perfect Plex, then blocks a facerake. She nails Fatale with a kneelift, turning things around. Fatale shortly legsweeps her, then nails her with an elbowdrop, retaking control. Vonya shortly ducks a kick to the head. Fatale dodges a forearm to the back; Vonya rakes her face, then takes her down with a high cross body. She puts one foot on the ropes; Fatale gets her shoulder up in two. She stands up and runs into the ropes; Fatale pops up and takes her down with a rolling kick, resuming her assault. Vonya shortly counters a double reverse chinlock with a facerake, then hammers her with a side suplex, turning the match around again. She soon sends Fatale to a corner, then runs in shoulder-first. Fatale lifts her knee up, then whips Vonya to the ropes. Snakebite pulls the top rope down, dumping Vonya to the floor. Fatale leaves the ring on the side, then walks over to Vonya and throws her back into the ring. She stands up and nails her with a jumping elbow thrust, then sends her to the ropes. Vonya misses with a kick, then spins around and executes a belly-to-belly; one, two, kickout. Vonya gets up and runs into the ropes, rebounds and puts Fatale in a sleeper, regaining her momentum. Fatale shortly blocks a choke against the ropes and pushes Vonya back. She takes her down via a rolling kick, turning things around again. After 3 minutes she nails Vonya with a savate kick, then executes the Perfect Plex. Fatale covers Vonya, pinning her. (9:18) ---------- Angel Hunter v. Freedom ---------- Both women lock up, then Freedom sends Hunter to the ropes. Hunter ducks an elbow, then returns with an enzuigiri, starting her assault. Freedom soon blocks a belly-to-back with an elbowsmash. She ducks a lariat, then runs into the ropes. Hunter stops her with a kick, resuming her attack. She shortly runs into the ropes; Freedom nails her with a kick, turning things around. Hunter very shortly nails her with a kneelift, retaking control. She very shortly applies an armlock hammerlock. Freedom breaks the hold in 8 seconds, then executes an armdrag takedown. She follows up with a moonsault bodyblock; Hunter kicks out in one. Freedom follows up with a spinning elbowdrop, going back on the offensive. Hunter shortly executes an enzuigiri. She drops to the mat and puts Freedom in a full nelson, retaking control. She shortly sends freedom to the ropes; Freedom misses with a clothesline, rebounds and runs into a slap. She slaps Hunter back, then blocks another and takes Hunter down in a bulldog, turning things around again. After a fast-and-furious 4 minutes Hunter blocks a Northern-Lights suplex. She executes a belly-to-back. Both women pick themselves up, then Freedom blocks the Rack. Hunter settles for a legsweep, turning the match around. She shortly gets Freedom into the Rack; Freedom submits after 15 seconds.
