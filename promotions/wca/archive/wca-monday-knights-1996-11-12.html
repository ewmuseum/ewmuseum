She nails Kassandra with a clothesline, going back on the offensive. Kassandra shortly ends it with a double chickenwing. She shortly runs into the ropes. Knightsbridge rocks her with a clothesline, then follows up with a legsweep, renewing her attack. After 2 minutes Kassandra blocks a headbutt. She picks Knightsbridge up; Knightsbridge blocks being set on a turnbuckle. Kassandra settles for a tiger suplex, turning the match back around. She nails Kassandra with a back heel kick, turning things around. After 2 minutes she nails Kassandra with a back heel kick. Kassandra falls against the ropes, then staggers out into the ring. Knightsbridge backs up, then nails Kassandra with the Running Superkick. She pins Kassandra. (12:20) ---------- Shelly Rage (w/ Jenny Black and the Dream Master) v. Janet Strong ---------- Strong ducks an enzuilariato before the bell, then runs into the ropes. She kicks Rage in the midsection, taking control of the match. Rage very shortly reverses a whip to the ropes, then ducks a clothesline. She turns around and nails Strong with a clothesline, turning things around. Strong soon avoids a backflip cradle. Rage gets back on her feet; Strong sends her to the ropes. She counters a bodyslam with a small package; Rage reverses it in two. Strong kicks out in two and puts Rage in a double chickenwing, resuming her offensive. Strong quickly turns things around with a short lariat. Rage shortly throws her out of the ring. She joins Strong on the mat and nails her with an enzuigir. Strong blocks a double underhook piledriver and throws Rage back into the ring. She reenters, then runs into the ropes. Rage stops her with an elbow, gaining the advantage. Strong shortly ducks a flying clothesline, evening the mathc up. She quickly takes control with a DDT. She shortly sends Rage to the ropes. Rage comes back with an enzuigiri, going back on the offensive. After 2 minutes she runs into the ropes. Strong misses with a clothesline, spins around and ducks a clothesline, then rocks Rage with a jumping side kick. She catches Rage's fall, then sends her to the ropes. She hits Rage with a shoulderblock, then puts her in a front facelock, taking charge. Rage shortly counters a cobra clutch with an armdrag takedown, then nails Rage with a chop, turning things around again. She shortly catches Strong in a backflip cradle, pinning her. (7:49) ---------- Christy Cooper (w/ Jenny Black, Shelly Rage, Dr. Insane, and the Dream Master) v. Flair's Girls (Woman and Elizabeth) (w/ the Nature Boy) ---------- Woman runs itno the ropes, then puts Cooper in a full nelson, assuming control of the match. Cooper throws Elizabeth out of the ring, then plants Woman on the mat with a tiger suplex, going on the attack. After 2 minutes Woman turns the mathc around with a superkick. Cooper shortly takes her down with an earringer, retaking control. After 3 1/2 minutes Woman tags out to Elizabeth. They send Cooper to the ropes, then deck her with a double back heel kick, starting a series of double teams. The ref shortly convinces Woamn to leave as Elizabeth drives her knee into Cooper's back. She sends Cooper to the ropes; Cooper comes back with a kick, regaining her momentum. Elizabeth very shortly makes the tag to Woman, then hammers Cooper with a belly-to-back suplex. Woman keeps Cooper on the mat with a flying lariat. Elizabeth pulls Cooper up, then they send her to the ropes. They deck Cooper with a double back heel kick, again starting a series of double teams. Elizabeth very shortly leaves the irng; Woman continues the assault with a kick. She quickly tags out to Elizabeth; Cooper throws woman out of the ring. Elizabeth nails her with a kick, continuing their onslaught. After 2 1/2 minutes Cooper backdrops out of a front facelock. She rocks Elizabeth with a European uppercut, gaiining the advantage. After 2 minutes Woman enters the ring. She and Elizabeth send Cooper to the ropes, then nail her with a dobule fist to the midsection. Elziabeth follows up with a back suplex, retaking control. After 2 minutes Cooper ducks a thrust kick to the head. She executes a backbreaker, then plants Elizabeth on the mat with a tiger driver. She rolls Elizabeth onto her stomach, then puts her into Christy's Clutch. Elizabeth submits immediately. ---------- Lovely Liz Strohlheim v. Karen "Bugs" Huffman ---------- Sue accomapanies both wrestlers to ringside. Both women lock up, then Huffman reverses a whip to the ropes. Strohlheim comes back wiht an ipponzei. She covers; Huffman kicks out at the last second. Both women stand up. Huffman blocks a faceslam, then nails Strohlheim with a Doctor bomb. She puts Strohlheim in a kneelock; Strohlheim submits immediately. Up next is the first of two title defenses. 'The Intimidator' Amber Axeln has not been very active recently in the WCA, having to take some time off for personal reason. Now she's back, and after being abandoned by Lori Michaels and Janet Muniz wants to create a name for herself. She issued a challenge to the Mistress of Death for the Television title, and tonight she's coming to collect it! Let's go now to the ring."
