WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION STONE COLD January 19, 1997 Live from Buenos Aires, Argentina -----Main Event----- =World title Match= Big Norma Hudson v. Janet Strong =Tag Team Titltes Match= Tag Team Champions v. The Psycho Crushers =Can-Am Title Match= She-Devil v. Lovely Liz Strohlheim =Television Title Match= Nadia Williams v. "Psycho" Vicki V Sweet Sue v. Karen "Bugs" Huffman (I-Quit Baseball Bat Match) Valkyrie v. Flower Power (Scaffold match) "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln v. Bodacious Janet Muniz (Stretcher match) The Mistress of Death v. Semiramis (I Quit match) The Scandalous Ones v. Sellyna Shriek / Queen Cobra Freedom v. Nasty Tracy Turner (Falls-Count-Anywhere Winner gets Turner name) [Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.] [Fireworks explode as the camera shows a top-down view of the ring, the WCA logo in maroon letters on the white canvas. The camera descends and levels off, then circles the arena, showing the capacity audience. It stops at the other side of the aisle, where two WCA three-dimensional signs bracket the entryway; fireworks go off as the signs come into view. The image dissolves to commentator John Birmingham, seated at ringside with two monitors on his red-tableclothed table.] Birmingham: "Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to World CyberWrestling Association's pay-per-view spectacular event Stone Cold, coming at'cha live from Buenos Aires, Argentina! Tonight Big Norma Hudson will defend the World title against her friend and fellow superstar Janet Strong. Both of these women are fan favorites, and friends to boot, but when things get rough Janet's been known to resort to some questionable tactics. Can their friendship survive with such high stakes? We'll find out. Also, in tag team action the champions Hooked on Destruction face the Psycho Crushers, the number one contenders for the belts. This will be the second of two matches for 'Psycho' Vicki V, as she'll also face the Television champion Nadia Williams for the belt. Is Vicki strong enough to fight two matches so close together? But in between those two matches Can-Am champion She-Devil takes on Lovely Liz Strohlheim. She-Devil pulled of the upset victory of the century, defeating the Mistress of Death for the title. At the same time, Liz is desperate to shed her 'Loser Liz' image, and winning the Can-Am title is the perfect way to do so. Also, Sweet Sue faces Karen 'Bugs' Huffman in an I-Quit Baseball Bat Match. This match was originally going to be a Bounty Hunters / Springfield Succulent Final Match in their Baseball Bat Series, but the Bounty Hunters took the series last night. Angry and blaming each other, Sue and Karen agreed to this match to take its place. Plus, in a scaffold match Valkyrie faces Flower Power. These two women are 2-1 in Valkyrie's favor. This match is a little bit different than other scaffold matches, in that it has two parts. The scaffold will be next to the ring. Once one wrestler reaches the top level, the match enters the second phase, where both combatants fight on the scaffold until one is thrown off. Plus, 'the Intimidator' Amber Axeln faces her new arch-rival and former stablemate Bodacious Janet Muniz in a Stretcher match. This fight has been two months in the making; whomever wins tonight can definitely say they are the better woman. I want to say something here regarding the comments Janet Muniz has made. She claims that I am an Axeln fan, and she's right. I do happen to like Amber Axeln, but frankly I would root for anybody who opposes the Bodacious One, for her actions against someone whom always respected her. Yes, I know Muniz's stated reasons for her betrayal, but they seem more like a justification. And Muniz, regardless of my opinions, I still think you are a highly skilled wrestler . . . when you want to be. Plus, the Mistress of Death and Semiramis will be going after each other in an I Quit match. These two were former friends and together ruled the tag team ranks for three months, defeating almost every team in the WCA. However, after weeks of Posse attacks, the Mistress fell victim to 'Psycho' Vicki V's devastating Claw, putting her unconscious in a title match. Semiramis blamed the Mistress and turned her back on her, joining up with former enemy Panthra in her new Army. These two women are sure to give one heck of a performance as they vent their anger on each other. In other tag team action, the Scandalous Ones take on Sellyna Shriek and Queen Cobra. This will be Cobra's first match in the WCA; she'll be facing some stiff competition, taking on a team that is itching to climb the rankings ladder and become the Tag Team champions. And kicking off the action tonight, Freedom takes on her sister and Posse member Nasty Tracy Turner in a Falls-Count-Anywhere match where the winner earns the Turner name. And so, without further ado, let's kick off the action." ===== *Falls-Count-Anywhere Match* (Winner Gets the Turner Name) ---------- Freedom v. Nasty Tracy Turner ---------- Both women lock up. Freedom sends Turner to the ropes, then jumps a shoulderblock. She spins around and backdrops Turner, then keeps her down with a spinning leg lariat, taking control of the match. After ninety seconds she runs into the ropes. Turner stops her with a facerake; Freedom blocks standing short-arm scissors. Turner blocks a gutwrench suplex, then heads to the top rope. She executes flying headscissors, turning the match around. After 2 minutes Freedom ducks a flying clothesline. She throws Turner out of the ring, then goes through the ropes and hops down to the floor. She heads to the ring officials' table and grabs the ring bell, then wings it at Turner, nailing her. Freedom slams Turner's face on the guardrail; Turner blocks a tiger bomb. She backdrops Freedom over the barrier into the first row, then climbs over and puts Freedom in an abdominal stretch, resuming her assault. Freedom very shortly rams Turner's head into the guardrail at the head of the aisle, then throws her back over into ringside. Freedom throws Turner across the opposing barrier, then sets up a chair and side suplexes Turner onto it, retaking control of the match. The Dream Master shortly enters the stands. He hands Turner her kendo stick; she nails Freedom with it, then throws her over the guardrail back into ringside. Freedom gets back up; Turner nails her with a flying clothesline off the steel. Freedom gets back up and takes Turner's kendo stick away. She tosses it way out into the stands; Turner punishes her with a short clothesline, resuming her assault. Over the next four minutes the match moves between the stands lining the aisle to the end of the aisle, then back across to the opposing stands and finally back to the ring. Freedom then ducks a flying clothesline. Turner blocks a side suplex; Freedom backdrops out of a front-layout suplex. Turner gets back up and puts her in a full nelson. Freedom breaks the hold after 11 seconds. Turner backs up, begging off. Freedom nails her with a chop to the head, then plants Turner with a gutwrench suplex. Turner slowly gets back up. She counters a sleeper with a jawbreaker, then pulls Freedom up and sends her to a corner. Freedom rests a moment, then charges out. Turner meets her with a kick to the midsection. Freedom blocks a front-layout suplex. Turner begs off again; Freedom whips her to the ropes. She nails Turner with an enzuigiri, going back on the offensive. She soon moves the match to ringside, then dumps Turner over the guardrail. Turner blocks a bulldog, then puts Freedom in a bearhug. Freedom manages to break the hold; Turner slams her face onto the back of a chair. She follows up with a chop, turning things back around. Over the next minute Turner moves the match out of the arena to the entrance foyer. Turner nails Freedom with a rolling kick, sending her into the stairs' rail. Freedom heads down the stairs to the first floor, then heads to one of the rooms. She returns with a frying pan and heads back up the stairs. She nails Turner with the pan, turning the match back around. Freedom slowly moves the match down to the first floor. She soon grabs one of the television cameras, then brings it down on Turner's head. Freedom kicks Turner over, then steps on her; one, two, three.
