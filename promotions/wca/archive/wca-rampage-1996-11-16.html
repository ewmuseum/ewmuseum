WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION RAMPAGE Live from Paris, France 11/16/96 -----Main Event----- <World Title Match> "Psycho" Vicki V v. Panthra <Can-Am Title Match> Christy Cooper v. The Scandalous Ones ---------- Terror Twins (Terry Terror and Trudy Terror) (w/ "Masked" Mario Sanchez) v. The Scandalous Ones (Shelly Rage and Jenny Black) (w/ the Dream Master) ---------- Trudy and Rage lock up, then Rage runs into the ropes. Trudy stops her with a thrust kick to the head, taking control of the match. She shortly tags in Terry, then they send Rage to the ropes. They stop her with a double fist to the midsection, then Terry follows up with a double-axhandle chop, continuing the attack. She soon tags out to Trudy. Rage counters a cobra clutch with an armdrag takedown and tags out to Black. Trudy blocks a monkey flip, then executes an earringer. Black gets back up and ducks a kick to the head, then sends Trudy to the ropes. She nails Trudy with a shoulderblock, going on the offensive. After 2 1/2 minutes Trudy blocks a dragon suplex. She chops Black, then tags out to Terry. Terry executes a belly-to-back suplex. Black leaves the ring, then reenters at the two count. She sends Terry to the far ropes. Rage enters the ring and nails Terry with an enzuilariato. She leaves as Terry gets back on her feet. Terry puts Black in a Sammartino backbreaker, turning the match around. Black soon blocks a Sammartino backbreaker as Christy Cooper walks down the aisle. Black tags out to Rage; Terry blocks a bulldog. Black leaves the ring as Terry continues her assault with a Soviet suplex. Cooper takes a chair by the timekeeper's table. Rage shortly leaves the ring and scales the turnbukcles as both managers jump up onto the apron. They distract Terry; Rage nails her with a flying drop-kick, turning things around. Terry shortly ducks an enzuilariato and tags in Trudy. They send Rage to the ropes, then stop her with a double fist to the midsection. Black enters the ring and throws Terry out, then she and Rage nail Trudy with a double enzuilariato. Black leaves the ring as Trudy stands back up. Rage blocks a ropeburn and chops her, regaining her momentum. After ninety seconds Trudy ducks a flying clothesline. She executes a back suplex, going back on the attack. A minute later she tags out to Terry. Black enters the ring and throws Trudy out, then she and Rage nail Terry with a double enzuilariato. Black leaves as Terry gets on one knee. Rage keeps Terry on the mat with a moonsault into a faceslam. She covers Terry; one, two, and a forceful kickout. Both women stand up; Rage reverses a whip to the ropes. Terry ducks a clothesline, rebounds, and staggers Rage with a kick, taking charge. Rage shortly blocks a double underhook suplex and tags out to Black. Trudy enters the ring and throws Rage out, then she and Terry send Black to the ropes. Black ducks a double clothesline, rebounds, and nails the Twins with a dual clothesline. Trudy rolls out of the ring as Terry staggers back. Terry ducks a short lariat and throws Black out of the ring. She hops to the floor; Black takes her down with an earringer. Terry slowly gets back on her feet; Black knocks her into the ringpost. Terry reenters the ring, followed by Black. Black blocks a double chickenwing, then prevents being set on a turnbuckle. She knees Terry in the gut, then climbs the turnbuckles herself. Terry punches her in the stomach; Black falls onto the turnbuckle. Terry climbs up and takes her down with a superplex. She covers; Black kicks out in two. Terry follows up with an over-the-shoulder stomachbreaker, going on the offensive. Black shortly blocks a Soviet suplex and tags in Rage. They send Terry to the ropes; Terry comes back with a dual clothesline. She shortly puts Rage on the turnbuckle, then climbs up them from the outside. Cooper enters the ring and pulls Rage down, then drop-kicks Terry backwards out of the ring. Terry lands hard. The ref disqualifies the Scandalous Ones, who proceed to pounce on the down Terry. Trudy pulls off Black; they begin brawling. The Dream Master keeps Sanchez from interfering. WCA officials come down the aisle and break up the fight. They call for a stretcher to take the motionless Terry out of the arena. (Match: 16:57) ---------- Haley "Comet" Hanson (w/ Big Norma Hudson and Mr. Canuck) v. Janet Strong ---------- Both women lock up, then Strong applies an armbar. Hanson eventually breaks the hold in 22 seconds. Strong sends her to the ropes. Hanson misses with a kick, spins around, and nails her with a larriat. Strong blocks a sleeper and executes a short lariat, taking control of the match. After 3 minutes she puts Hanson in the Bearhug. Hanson breaks free in 10 seconds. She takes Strong down with neck-scissors, turning the match around. After 3 minutes she attempts a sunset backslide, but Strong manages to reverse it. Hanson kicks out and back in two, re-reversing it; Strong kicks out in two. Hanson and strong stand up; Strong ducks a spinning leg lariat. She executes a back suplex, turning the match back around. She soon puts Hanson back in the Bearhug; Hanson submits after 11 seconds.
