WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION ( http://www.inet-images.com/skessler/WCA/ ) THEATRE OF WAR APRIL 21, 1997 Live from the Lake Arena in Springfield, Missouri -----Main Event----- =World Title Match= Shelly Rage v. Mary Williams =Tag Team Titles Match= Bounty Hunters v. Delicious Dolls Interview with Helena "High Kick" Knightsbridge =Can-Am Title Match= "Nasty Girl" Amber Jane v. Angel Hunter "Psycho" Vicki V v. Bodacious Janet Muniz (Best of 11 Match 8) The Mistress of Death / Liberty v. Freedom / Shannon Stevens (Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.) (Fireworks explode as the camera shows the crimson WCA logo on the white ring canvas. The camera descends rapidly, then levels off facing the aisle. The camera swerves left, panning out over the capacity crowd and circling the ring, ending its focus on the iridescent three-dimensional WCA letters bracketing the entrance to the backstage area. The referee for the first match heads to the ring.) -------------------- The Mistress of Death / Liberty (w/ Bodacious Janet Muniz and "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln) v. Shannon Stevens / Freedom (w/ the Dream Master) -------------------- The bell sounds; Stevens and Freedom dump Liberty out of the ring, then they take the Mistress down with a double clothesline. Stevens rolls out of the ring and grabs the Mistress' feet, hiding while Freedom covers; one, two, three, Axeln and Muniz nail Stevens for her cheating, prompting a brawl on the floor. Freedom leaves the Mistress in the ring and goes out to help her partner; the Mistress follows her out. The Dream Master quickly goes in and pulls Stevens out of the brawl, then he ushers her and Freedom back to the back. This match sets the record for shortest match ever in the WCA. (Match: 0:08) ===== Mike Sharpe: "Freedom and Shannon Stevens makes an . . . unforgettable debut in the tag team ranks in the WCA, showing the world what they are all about, at least when it comes to the Mistress of Death. We still don't know how well their tag skills are, but we do know they certainly will have a good working relationship. One thing's for certain; the Mistress isn't through with Freedom yet. Later tonight fans is a challenge for the Can-Am championship, between the current champion 'Nasty Girl' Amber Jane and the challenger Angel Hunter. Muniz runs to the far ropes, then she knocks Vicki back down with a shoulderblock. Vicki gets back on her feet, then she reverses a whip to a corner. She meets Muniz's charge out with a fireman's carry. She keeps Muniz down with an earringer. Muniz easily blocks a fireman's carry, then Vicki blocks a facebite. She bodyslams Muniz.
