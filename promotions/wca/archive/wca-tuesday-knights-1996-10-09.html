WCA Tuesday Knights 10/8

WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION TUESDAY KNIGHTS Live from Calgary, Alberta 10/8/96 -----Main Event----- <Television Title Match> Li'l Cheetah v. Lovely Liz Strohlheim "Psycho" Vicki V v. Tygra The Assassin v. Panthra Christy Cooper v. Thunder Lolita Nasty Tracy Turner v. Janet Strong (Stretcher Match) <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA wrestling action> <Wide angle view of the inside of the arena shows a capacity crowd. The ring announcer, timekeepers, and other officials are walking down the aisle to get ready for the show.> *Stretcher Match* ---------- Nasty Tracy Turner (w/ Jenny Black, "Psycho" Vicki V, and the Dream Master) v. Janet Strong ---------- Turner goes for a Doctor Bomb, but Strong backdrops her. Turner gets back up and rocks her with a European uppercut, taking control of the match. Almost three minutes later, Turner sends Strong into the ropes. Strong staggers her with a shoulderblock, turning the match around. Turner soon throws Strong out of the ring and picks up the stretcher. She hits Strong with it, tthen puts Strong on it. The attendants lift the stretcher up and head to the back. Strong rolls off of it quickly and reenters the ring. She attempts a back suplex, but Turner blocks it and executes a front-layout suplex, resuming her onslaught. After 90 seconds Turner goes for a jack-knife. Strong counters with a backdrop. She nails Turner with a savate kick, turning the match around again. She soon goes for a kick to the midsection, but Turner blocks it and takes her down via a Doctor bomb, going back on the attack. After four minutes Turner attempts a whip tot he ropes, but Strong reverses it and follows her in. She drives her knee into Turner's back, then runs into the ropes. She executes a belly-to-belly suplex, turning the match around. She soon applies short arm scissors. Turner breaks the hold, then pulls Strong up and nails her with the Sweet Dreams, retaking control of the match. She soon whips Strong into the ropes. Strong comes back with an enzuigiri, going back on the offensive. She shortly tries to whip Turner into the ropes, but Turner reverses it. She stops Strong with a clothesline, then throws her out of the ring. Turner heads out after her, then takes Strong down with a leg lariat. She puts Strong on the stretcher; the attendants carry her back to the locker room. (16:18) ---------- Christy Cooper (w/ Nasty Tracy Turner, Mick Walker, and the Dream Master) v. Thunder Lolita (w/ Cactus Jack) ---------- Both women lock up, then Cooper executes an earringer, taking control of the match. Lolita quickly takes control with a knee to the midsection. She shortly sends Cooper into the ropes. Cooper comes back with a backdrop. She follows up with a backbreaker, turning the match around. Lolita soon nails her with a backspin DDT, ending her offensive. After two minutes Cooper nails her with a double-axhandle chop, retaking control. After two minutes she takes Lolita down with a tiger driver. Lolita kicks out in two and gets back up. She executes a backspin DDT, turning the match back around. She shortly goes for a European uppercut, but Cooper blocks it and again ends her offensive with a double-axhandle chop. Lolita shortly puts Cooper in a full nelson, retaking control. Cooper shortly rakes Lolita's face, taking control of the match. She soon climbs tot he top turnbuckle. Lolita punches her in the knee, causing Cooper to fall onto the turnbuckle. Lolita climbs up and executes a belly-to-belly superplex. She covers Cooper; Cooper kicks out at the last second. Lolita follows up with a stomp, going back on the offensive. She shortly sends Cooper into the ropes. Cooper comes back with a roundhouse punch, retaking control. She soon staggers Lolita with a lariat. Lolita responds with a clohtesline, turning the match back around. Cooper shortly spits in Lolita's eyes, then rakes the face. She takes Lolita down with a short lariat and applies the Christy's Clutch. Lolita submits immediately. ---------- The Assassin (w/ Li'l Cheetah) v. Panthra (w/ Tygra and Lu Chin) ---------- The Assassin nails Panthra with a swinging punch, going on the offensive. She soon applies an Indian deathlock. Panthra uses the roeps to break the hold, then legsweeps the Assassin. She goes for the pin; the Assassin barely kicks out in tome. Panthra follows up with leg scissors, taking control. She soon whips the Assassin into the ropes. Both women get hit with a double clothesline. The Assassin gets up first and punches Panthra, going back on the offensive. After five minutes she attempts a kick to the leg, but Panthra blocks it and sends the Assassin to the ropes. She naisl the Assassin with a clothesline, turning the match around. She soon sets the Assassin up for a piledriver, but the Assassin backdrops her. She chokes Panthra against the ropes, turning the match back around. Panthra shortly nails her with a kneelift, retaking control. After two minutes she attempts a Frankensteiner from the top rope, but the Assassin moves out of the way. She executes a belly-to-back suplex, going back on the offensive. She shortly places Panthra on a turnbuckle and climbs up. She takes Panthra down via a belly-to-belly superplex, then pins her. (12:10) ---------- "Psycho" Vicki V (w/ Shelley Rage and the Dream Master) v. Tygra (w/ Panthra and Lu Chin) ---------- Vicki goes for a tiger driver, but Tygra counters with a backdrop. Vicki in turn hangs on for a sunset flip; Tygra kicks out in two. Vicki keeps her down with a back heel kick, assuming control of the match. After five minutes Vicki whips Tygra into the ropes. Tygra comes back and drives her knee into Vicki's abdomen, starting her first serious offensive of the match. After three minutes she runs into the ropes. Vicki knocks her down with a shoulderblock, turning the match back around. She soon whips Tygra into a corner and runs in shoulder-first. Tygra moves out of the way and hits Vicki with a forearm to the back. She attempts a reverse cradle, but Vicki counters with a sitdown. Tygra kicks out in two; both women stand up. Tygra ducks a thrust kick to the head and rocks Vicki with a forearm smash, going back on the attack. Vicki tries several times over the next minute to retake control, but Tygra each time puts her back on the defensive. She takes firm control of the match with a fist to the midsection. After two more minutes Tygra whips Vicki into the ropes. Vicki comes back and hits Tygra with an elbow, turning the match around again. She shortly applies the Claw; Tygra submits after 10 seconds. (14:04) ===== Birmingham: "Vicki makes Tygra submit to the Claw, continuing her winning streak. Of the four Posse members seen tonight, only Jenny Black lost earlier to Double J. Fans, it's time for our main event, a match for the Television title. Lovely Liz Strohlheim has challenged the champ Li'l Cheetah for her belt. She's faced Cheetah before and lost; can she defeat her this time for the title? We'll find out in a few moments. Let's go now to the ring." Ring Announcer: "Ladies and gentlemen, tonight's main event is for the Television championship! Introducing first: representing the Top Catfighter's Alliance and accompanied to the ring by stablemates Sweet Sue and Karen 'Bugs' Huffman, from Cinnaminson, New Jersey, weighing in at 115 pounds, Lovely Liz Strohlheim!" <The TCA walks down the aisle to a mixed response from the crowd. Some ringside fans throw stuff at Sue, who glares at them. Strohlheim enters the ring.> "Her opponent and current Television champion: accompanied to the ring by her tag team partner the Assassin and her manager Tall Troll, from Detroit, Michigan, weighing in at 143 pounds, Li'l Cheetah!" <Troll leads the Street Assassins down the aisle. Cheetah takes some advice from Troll and reenters the ring in five. Strohlheim meets her with a snap suplex. Cheetah gets right back up and blocks a hiptoss.
