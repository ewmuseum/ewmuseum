WCA Rampage 8/4

5 August 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION RAMPAGE Live from Boston, MA 8/4/96 -----Main Event----- <World Title Match> Valkyrie v. Double J Sellyna Shriek v. Janet Strong "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln v. Semiramis "Destroyer" Dara Holt v. Big Norma Hudson Lovely Liz Strohlheim / Sweet Sue v. Delicious Dolls <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA wrestling action> <Wide angle view of the inside of the arena shows a capacity crowd. The ring announcer, timekeepers, and other officials are walking down the aisle to get ready for the show.> ------------------------- Lovely Liz Strohlheim / Sweet Sue v. Delicious Dolls ------------------------- The two TCA members, Strohlheim and Sue, walk down the aisle and enter the ring. The Dolls are introduced next, and Double D, Donna Star, and Tammi Cooper enter the ring. The Dolls start a little argument with themselves, then settle on Star and Cooper. Strohlheim and Cooper start. Both women lock up, then Strohlheim breaks off and runs into the ropes. She rebounds and applies an abdominal stretch, taking control of the match. Cooper quickly gains the advantage with a chop, going on the offensive. She soon goes for a flying karakte chop, but STrohlheim counters with a fist to the midsection and tags out to Sue. Sue turns the match back around with an ipponzei. She soon climbs the turnbuckles, but Cooper catches her and throws her to the mat. She follows up with an enzuigiri, retaking control. Sue shortly ends her offensive with a fist to th midsection. Cooper tags out to Star, then both women kick Sue in the midsection, regaining their momentum. Strohlheim soon enters the ring to help Sue out, but Cooper enters also and throws her out, then she and Star whip Sue into the ropes. Sue blocks a double backdrop and executes a double headsmash. Strohlheim rolls back in the ring and whip Star into the ropes. They deck her with a double clothesline, then Strohlheim leaves the ring as Sue goes back on the offensive with an over-the-shoulder stomachbreaker. Star gradually builds a comeback, then after 2 minutes hammers Sue with a takeover suplex and tags out to Cooper. Sue throws Cooper out of the ring. Cooper confers with Diamond, then rolls back in at the six count. She blocks a brain buster, then goes on the attack with a spin kick. Sue soon slows Cooper down with an elbowsmash and tags out to Strohlheim. Strohlheim applies an abdominal stretch, turning the match around. Cooper hiptosses her way out after 18 seconds; Strohlheim puts her on a turnbuckle. She climbs up as well, but Cooper shoves her off the turnbuckle and hops down. She applies a figure four leglock. Sue makes the save; Star enters the ring and nails Strohlheim with an enzuilariato, putting her back on the defensive. Strohlheim shortly manages to escape and tag out to Sue. She catches Cooper with a savate kick before leaving the ring. Sue follows up with an enzuilariato; Cooper comes back with a kick to the midsection, retaking control of the match. Sue soon ends her onslaught with a belly-to-belly suplex. She covers Cooper, but can only get a two count. Sue rewards her with a Boston crab, taking control. After two minutes she begins a series of high-impact blows, then caps it off with the Jersey Jaw Jacker. She covers Cooper and pins her. (15:49) ---------- John Birmingham: "Welcome fans to a special Sunday edition of Rampage! Tonight we'll see the end of Thursday's Tag Team championship match between the Click and the Street Assassins. Following that will be the television debut of one of the WCA's newest members, "Destroyer" Dara Holt as she takes on Big Norma Hudson. Then "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln goes against Semiramis in a qualifying match set by the WCA World champion Valkyrie; if Semiramis can get by Valkyrie's friend the Intimidator, she'll face the World champion this Saturday. Then, two newcomers to the WCA, Sellyna Shriek and Janet Strong, will go head-to-head. And in our main event, Double J challenges Valkyrie for the World title. Double J's had her ups and downs; time will only tell if she can pull an upset victory for the WCA's highest belt. Right now, though, as we promised Thursday we'll show you the ending of the Tag Team match-up." <Dissolve to:> Birmingham's voice: "Cheetah leaves the ring as the Intimidator nails the Assassin with an enzuigiri. She whips the Assassin into the ropes; the Assassin comes back with a dropkick to the knee. Amber stumbles; she comes back and chokes the Assassin! One, two, three, she applies an abdominal stretch. I'm impressed with all of these women; all have done their best in this match. The Assassin breaks the hold. She nails Amber with a dropkick to the knee. Amber blocks a ropeburn and applies a front facelock. The Assassin hooks the bottom rope with her foot. She nails the Intimidator with a rolling kick. The Assassin whips Axeln into the ropes - both women clothesline each other! "The Assassin gets up first and runs into the ropes. Axeln ducks a clothesline. The Assassin reboudns again and blocks a chokehold. Axeln blocks a fisherman suplex, then takes the Assassin down with neck-scissors. She throws the Assassin out of the ring to the floor. Pace begins the count; one, two, three, four, the Assassin reenters the ring. The Intimidator tags out to the Bodacious One. They whip the Assassin into the ropes, then nail her with a double back heel kick. Pace keeps Cheetah out of the ring. The Click whips the Assassin into the ropes, then nail her with a double elbowsmash. "Pace sends Amber out; Janet executes a tiger suplex into a cover; two, thr-shoulder up! Almost had new champions! Munzi bodyslams the Assassin, then runs into the ropes. The Assassin gets up and misses with an elbow. She stops Janet with a double axhandle chop and executes a belly-to-back suplex. Muniz reverses a whip into a corner and charges in - the Assassin moves out of the way! She tags out to Cheetah. They whip Muniz into the ropes, then double backdrop her. The Assassin leaves the ring as Cheetah throws Muniz to the floor. Janet reenters and tags out to Axeln. "Cheetah curses Janet, then runs into the ropes. She hits the Intimidator with a shoulderblock, then punches her in the temple. Axlen blocks a reverse neckbreaker, then chokes Cheetah against the ropes. One, two, three, four, she lets go. Janet reenters the ring, rested a little. They whip Cheetah into the ropes, then nail her with a double back heel kick. "Muniz leaves the ring as Cheetah reverses a whip to a corner. Cheetah tags out to the Assassin. Muniz enters the ring and boots Cheetah out, mocking her. She leaves the ring as Amber chops the Assassin. The Intimidator tags out to the Bodacious One. Cheetah enters the ring and throws Amber out, then she and the Assassin send Janet to the ropes. They nail Muniz with a double elbowsmash on the rebound, then the Assassin follows up with a kick to the head. She tags out to Cheetah. Cheetah puts Munzi in an abdominal stretch. Looks like the Click is starting to run out of gas. Cheetah lets go and punches Janet in the jaw. "She takes Munzi down with a Guerilla Press. Muniz gets to her feet and ducks a kick to the head. She tags out to the Intimidator. Pace cuts the Assassin off as the Click whip Cheetah into the ropes. They double punch Cheetah in the midsection, then Amber chokes her against the ropes. One, two, three, four, moment's release, one, two, three, four, moment's release, one, two, three, four, the Assassin makes the save. Amber ducks a short lariat, then throws the Assassin out of the ring. Muniz drops to the floor and grabs a chair. Axeln nails the Cheetah with a belly-to-belly suplex; Janet enters the ring and brings the chair down on Cheetah! Pace disqualifies her and Axeln - he's calling for the bell. Janet won't let up on Cheetah at all - the Assassin nails her with a shoulderblock. Amber pulls Janet away - Janet's furious!" RA: "Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the match as a result of a disqualification, and still Tag Team champions: in 18 minutes 40 seconds, the Street Assassins!" <The Assassin helps Cheetah up; Cheetah screams at the departing Muniz, who is still struggling with Axeln to get back to the ring and continue her brawl.> <Dissolve to live feed:> JB: "Muniz is still very upset, to make an understatement, about the match, and understandably so. Fans should know that a rematch between Li'l Cheetah and the Bodacious One is scheduled to take place very soon. Up next is a match between newcomer "Destroyer" Dara Holt and Big Norma Hudson. Fans who attended last week's Monday Knights will note that this is a rematch between these two women, as Holt made a very impressive debut by soundly defeating the huge Hudson. Hudson has since requested a rematch; tonight she'll get her chance to prove herself the better woman. Let's go to the ring." ---------- ------------------------- "Destroyer" Dara Holt v. Big Norma Hudson (w/ Mr. Canuck) ------------------------- Both women lock up, then Holt executes an inside cradle; one, two, kickout. Both women stand up; Holt sends Hudson into the ropes. Hudson rebounds and goes on the offensive with a legsweep faceslam. Holt quickly ends it with a chokehold; the ref reaches the five count and warns her. Holt ignores her and runs into the ropes. Hudson takes her down with an elbow, regaining her momentum. She soon blocks a facerake and whips Holt into the turnbuckles. Holt comes back and doubles her over with a hard kick to the midsection. She follows up with a double axhandle chop that drops Hudson to the mat, turning the match around. Hudson soon ends her assault with an atomic drop, retakign control of the match. Holt shortly reverses a whip into the corner, then rakes Hudson's face, turning the match back around. Hudson soon retakes control with a forearm to the back. She soon executes a belly-to-back suplex and covers Holt. One, two, in the ropes. Hudson pulls Holt into the middle of the ring and goes for a legdrop. Holt rolls away in time, then chokes Hudson. She lets go and covers her; Hudson kicks out in two and rolls away from a stomp. She gets up; both women begin trading blows. Holt gradually gains the edge over Hudson. She soon throws Hudson through the ropes to the floor and goes to the apron. She nails Hudson with a flying karate chop; Hudson reenters the ring. Holt follows her in; Hudson catches her in a Guerilla Press. The resilient Holt gets back up and rolls her up in an inside cradle, grabbing some tights; one, two, the ref sees it. Holt throws Hudson out of the ring. Mr. Canuck reassures Hudson, then she reenters at the five count. Holt rakes her face and executes a short clohtesline. Hudson returns the clothesline, then runs into the ropes. She hamemrs Holt with the Hudson Bay Splash and pins her. (11:03) Pre-Match Comments by Semiramis: ---------- Semiramis: Amber Axeln, you are known as the Intimidator. I am not intimidated. I am doing what I said that I would. Double J felt what it was like to wrestle me. Amanda, a member of the Click, has also felt my rage. It will just be another day at the office for me when I get you in the ring. Valkyrie, Double J, I will be watching the title match later tonight. I will be facing the winner of the match real soon. ------------------------- "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln (w/ Valkyrie and the Prince) v. Semiramis (w/ Lu Chin) ------------------------- Both women lock up, then Axeln whips Semiramis into the ropes. Semiramis rebounds and nails her with a legsweep faceslam, going on the offensive. She shortly attempts a flying clothesline, but Axeln ducks it, then applies a cobra clutch, taking control of the match. She shortly attempts to choke Semiramis, but the latter blocks it. Both women stand toe-to-toe with each other; Semiramis quickly ends the stalemate with a legsweep. She shortly goes for an earringer, but Axeln blocks it and whips her into the corner. She charges in, hammering Semiramis into the turnbuckles and going on the offensive. She shortly applies an armlock hammerlock. Semiramis breaks out of the hold 21 seconds later, then runs into the ropes. The Prince trips Semiramis up. The ref disqualifies Axeln, prompting protests from her, Valkyrie, and the Prince. Semiramis will face Valkyrie this Saturday at Rampage. (4:44) ------------------------- Sellyna Shriek v. Janet Strong ------------------------- Shriek applies a standing wristlock before the bell. Strong breaks the hold and takes control of the match with a knee to the back. Shriek quickly turns the match around with a Samoan Drop. She shortly runs into the ropes; Strong nails her with a chop, evening the match up. Both women begin trading blows. Shriek shortly ends the stand-off with a double underhook superplex. Strong shortly evens the match up again with a jumping elbow thrust. Both women stand toe-toe-toe with each other. Shriek breaks the stalemate with a powerslam into a cover; one, two, kickout. She pulls Strong up, then shoves her into the ropes. Strong reboudns and nails Shriek with a kick to the leg, going on the offensive. She shortly goes for a figure four, but Shriek blocks it and gets up. She executes a hiptoss, retaking control of the match. Strong quickly turns the match back around wiht an ipponzei. Shriek shortly ends her assault with a Doctor Bomb and covers her; one, two, kickout. Shriek follows up with a jack-knife powerbomb and covers her; one, two, shoulder up. Shriek punishes Strong with a takeover suplex, going on the attack. Strong builds a comeback, evening the match up with a swinging punch. Both women start trading blows again. Strong gradually gains the advantage. She shortly knocks Shriek down with an elbow and goes for the pin; one, two, shoulder up. Shriek gets up and ducks a short lariat, continuing the stand-off. Strong shortly takes firm control of the match by dropping Shriek with a kick to the midsection. Shriek soon turns the match around with a flying lariat. After 3 minutes Strong ends her offensive by countering a headlock takedown with a back suplex, retaking control. After two minutes, Shriek evens the match up by countering an abdominal stretch with a hiptoss. Both women stand toe-to-toe; Strong quickly takes control with a fist to the midsection. She shortly applies the Bearhug; Shriek submits after 7 seconds. (17:05) ---------- JB: "A very impressive debut by Janet Strong in the World CyberWrestling Association. But the competition is fierce here, and only the strong (no pun intended) survive. And now for our main event. Double J challenged the newly crowned champion Valkyrie to a title fight based on her relatively recent winning streak. Valkyrie was amused, since Double J's career has been turbulent, to say the least. She added a stipulation in the contract that Double J must defeat a major competitor to face her for the title. Since Double J already had a match scheduled with Semiramis, WCA officials in accord with Valkyrie considered that fulfillment of the clause. Well, as fans know, last Monday Double J fought heart and soul with Semiramis, but in the end the latter's strength, toughness, and conditioning won out. Recently though, Valkyrie took pity on Double J, and decided to award her a title match anyway. So tonight, a humiliated Double J will get her shot at true superstardom and take on the World champion herself. Here are some pre-recorded coments from Double J." ----- Double J says, "Soon ah will wrassle for the WCA World title! Ah'm really filled with mixed emotions right now. Ah'm thrilled to have the title chance, but yuh see, mah Dad died jest a few days ago and ah'm not really quite up to par yet. That's probably why ah didn't do so well in mah last match. But ah'm gonna dedicate mah title shot to mah Daddy and fight for him. Ah hope yer watchin' from up thar, daddy, because this match is fer yew!" ----- Ring Announcer: "Ladies and gentlemen, the following match is for the World Championship! Introducing the challenger: from Houston, Texas, weighing in at 229 pounds, Double J!" <Double J walks down the arena, slapping hands. She circles the ringside area before entering the ring, then does the Texas Two-Step.> "And the World champion: accompanied to the ring by her manager the Prince and 'the Intimidator' Amber Axeln, from Des Moines, Iowa, weighing in at 129 pounds, Valkyrie!" <Valkyrie and her entourage enter to a largely unreceptive crowd, although some disticnt cheers can be heard. She keeps Double J down with a leaping forearm and covers her - using the ropes; two, shoulder up. Valyrie throws Double J out of the ring. Double J avoids Axeln and reenters. Valkyrie greets her with a back heel kick. She climbs the turnbuckles; Double J ducks a diving elbowsmash. She nails Valkyrie with an elbowsmash! And follows up with a short lariat to a cover; one, two, kickout. "Valkyrie gets up and punishes Double J with a reverse neckbreaker. She puts her in a Boston crab. Methinks Valkyrie's looking ahead to Semiramis on Saturday. And speak of the devil . . . Semiramis decided to grab a front-row's seat of the match. We're out of time, fans; join us again on Monday, and have a good evening!" <Closing credits appear.>
