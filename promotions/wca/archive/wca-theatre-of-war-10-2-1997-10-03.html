WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION (http.// kessel.simplenet.com/WCA/ ) THEATRE OF WAR Live from the Lake Arena in Springfield, Missouri 10/2/97 -----Main Event----- Bodacious Janet Muniz v. "Psycho Queen" Vicki V Monique v. Christy Cooper Hardcore v. The Psycho Crushers Freedom v. Angel Hunter Blood v. Shelly Rage (Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.) (Fireworks explode as the camera shows the crimson WCA logo on the white ring canvas. The camera descends rapidly, then levels off facing the aisle. The camera swerves left, panning out over the capacity crowd and circling the ring, ending its focus on the iridescent three- dimensional WCA letters bracketing the entrance to the backstage area. The referee for the first match heads to the ring.) ---------- Blood (w/ Red) v. Shelly Rage (w/ Dr. Insane, “the Tax Collector” John Blair, Jenny Black, and Valkyrie) Referee: Mark White ---------- Both women lock up. Blood throws Rage out of the ring; one .. two, Rage reenters. Blood meets her with a dropkick, taking control of the match. Rage shortly ducks a flying clothesline. She waits for Blood to get up, then she executes a bridging back suplex, pinning her. (1:05) ---------- Freedom (w/ the Dream Master) v. Angel Hunter (w/ Janet Strong) Referee: Julian Pace ---------- Both women lock up. Hunter executes a clothesline, taking control of the match. She shortly executes a reverse cradle, pinning Freedom. As she and Janet celebrate their win, Panthra comes down the aisle with two policemen and a man in a suit. The man enters the ring and hands two pieces of paper to the women. They each read over their copy and look up at Panthra, stunned. The camera gets a shot of Janet’s paper, a restraining order that forbids Strong and Hunter to come within 100 feet of Panthra. Panthra looks at both women, looks down, and suddenly points out the distance to the police. They enter the ring and handcuff Strong and Hunter, then they escort them out of the building. (Match: 0:24) ---------- Hardcore (“Nasty Girl” Amber Jane and “Dominatress” Kathy Lynn) (w/ Dr. Insane, “the Tax Collector” John Blair, and Valkyrie) v. The Psycho Crushers (Mary Williams and Monique) Referee: Tina Nolke ---------- Williams throws Lynn out of the ring before the bell, then Jane blocks her legsweep faceslam. Williams and Monique trade places as the bell rings. Monique punches Jane in the stomach, dropping her to one knee and taking control of the match. She shortly tags out to Williams, who executes a snap mare. Jane quickly gets back up, then she blocks an armdrag takedown with a lariat. She rolls out of the ring to the apron, pops back up, and reenters the ring via a springboard dropkick, going on the attack. She shortly tags in Lynn; both women sends Williams to the ropes, then they take her down with a double clothesline. Jane leaves the ring as Lynn goes to a neutral corner. She steps through the ropes and begins her climb as Williams picks herself up. Lynn executes a flying lariat. She gets back up and backs away from a rising Williams. Williams stands, then she catches Lynn’s high cross body and turns it into a backbreaker. She tags in Monique, then both women whip Lynn to the ropes and nail her with a double fist to the midsection. Williams backs up, then she executes a leaping lariat. She rolls out of the ring as Lynn gets back up; Lynn ducks a jumping elbow thrust. She executes a dropkick, then she dances suggestively in the ring a second before tagging in Jane. Both women execute a double elbowdrop, then they pick her up. Lynn bodyslams her as Jane returns to her corner. Monique gets back on her feet; Jane takes her back down with a flying spinning leg lariat. Williams enters the ring; Nolke moves to cut her off. Hardcore picks Monique up and double-rake her face, then Lynn returns to her corner as Jane throws Monique out of the ring. One .. two .. three .. four .. five .. six .., Monique reenters the ring. She sidesteps a handspring elbow; Jane rebounds off the ropes, then Monique ducks neck-scissors. Jane gets back up; Monique grabs her head just as she stands and executes a reverse neckbreaker, taking control of the match. After a minute she tags in Williams; both women send Jane to the ropes, then they stop her with a double fist to the midsection. Lynn enters the ring and gets cut off; the Crushers use the moment to send Jane to the side ropes and double-kick her in the stomach, taking her down. Monique returns to her corner as Williams applies standing short-arm scissors, continuing the offensive. After 2 minutes Jane throws Williams out of the ring and heads to her corner as Nolke begins counting; one .. two .. three .. four .. five ., Williams reenters the ring. Jane tags in Lynn, then she goes for a headlock. Williams attempts to counter with a back suplex, but Jane in turn blocks that with a cross-body block, but she only gets a one count. Williams gets back up; Lynn comes off her turnbuckles with a flying dropkick, taking control of the match as Jane leaves the ring. After 2 ½ minutes she whips Williams to the ropes; Williams rebounds and ducks an elbow, rebounds again and knocks Lynn down with a shoulderblock. She goes for the pin; Lynn gets her shoulder up in two. Both women get up, then Williams headbutts Lynn, turning the match around. After a minute she tags in Monique; Jane enters the ring and throws her out, then they double-bodyslam Monique. Both women execute a double elbowdrop, then Jane rolls out of the ring. Lynn and Monique stand up; Lynn executes a rolling reverse cradle, but only gets a one count. Monique stands up, then she executes a back suplex, putting Lynn back on the defensive. After ninety seconds she runs to the ropes; Lynn jumps a shoulderblock and turns around, then she runs through Monique with a clothesline. Lynn tags in Jane as Monique gets up. Monique counters Lynn’s pick-up with a small package; Lynn kicks out in two. She rolls out of the ring as Jane keeps Monique from grabbing her head. She leaves the ring, turns around on the apron, and executes a springboard shoulderblock. Jane gets up and runs to the ropes; Monique stands and stops her with a kick, retaking control. Jane soon blocks a pick-up with her elbow, then she executes a headlock takedown, going back on the offensive. Monique soon sidesteps a handspring elbow. Jane picks herself up, then she turns a hiptoss into a backslide; Monique rolls it over in two, but only gets a one count. Both women get back up, then Jane blocks a reverse neckbreaker and whips Monique to a corner. She meets Monique’s charge out with a palm blow, retaking control of the match. Monique shortly blocks a hiptoss, then she punches Jane in the midsection, turning the match back around. Jane shortly takes her down with a leaping forearm, retaking control. After ninety seconds Jane blocks a facerake, then Monique turns a headlock into a back suplex. Both women get back up, then Jane reverses an Irish whip; Monique ducks a clothesline, rebounds again, and puts Jane into a bearhug, turning things around. Jane shortly turns a headlock into a back suplex, putting Monique back on the defensive. After another minute she tags in Lynn, then she takes Monique down with a Russian legsweep. Lynn follows up with a cover; Monique puts her foot on the bottom rope in two. Both women stand up, then Monique reverses an Irish whip. She picks Lynn up and pummels her in a Samoan Drop, going back on the offensive. She shortly executes a flying sunset flip; Lynn kicks out in one as the bell rings, signaling the time limit’s expiration. ---------- Monique (w/ Mary Williams and Red) v. Christy Cooper (w/ Dr. Insane, “the Tax Collector” John Blair, “Psycho Queen” Vicki V, and Valkyrie) Referee: Bob Jordan ---------- Monique punches Cooper in the stomach after entering the ring, then she executes a back suplex as the bell sounds, taking control of the match. Cooper shortly runs to the ropes, rebounds, and knocks Monique down with a shoulderblock, taking charge. She very shortly runs to the ropes again; Monique ducks a clothesline, spins around, and nails Cooper with her own, retaking control. Cooper shortly whips Monique to the ropes, then she nails her with a kick, retaking control again. After 2 ½ minutes Monique backdrops out of a set-up. Cooper gets back up; Monique whips her to the ropes and knocks her down with a shoulderblock. She heads for the apron; Cooper rolls away from a slingshot legdrop. She turns Monique over on the mat and puts her into an armlock leglock. Monique reaches the ropes after 10 seconds. She uses the ropes to get back up, then she grabs Cooper’s arm; Cooper reverses the Irish whip, then Monique rebounds and nails her with a lariat, turning the match back around. Cooper soon turns a headlock into a back suplex, going back on the offensive. Monique soon rolls away from an elbowdrop and gets back to her feet. Cooper picks herself up, then she catches a high cross body and turns it into a backbreaker. She puts Monique into a half Boston, going back on the offensive. After 2 minutes Monique backs away from a kneelift, then she whips Cooper to a corner and runs in shoulder-first, turning the match around. After ninety seconds Cooper blocks a pick-up, then she nails Monique with a kneelift, retaking control. She shortly takes Monique down with a backslide and pins her. (10:10) ===== SHARPE: “Christy Cooper takes advantage of Monique’s double booking tonight to get the pinfall with a simple backslide. Monique looked as impressive as ever, giving both matches her all. She’s certainly shown tremendous improvement in her abilities since she won the Television championship earlier this year. “Fans, up next is our main event. We were all stunned to learn that the WCA was undergoing a reorganization, and we were even more shocked to learn that Bodacious Janet Muniz had quit the league. However, she did inform us that she would fulfill her contractual obligations to this match, a match that she’s looked forward to for quite some time. Tonight she’ll take on the Psycho Queen Vicki V for one last time. Let’s go to the ring.” RING ANNOUNCER: “Ladies and gentlemen, tonight’s main event is set for one fall! Introducing first: representing the Posse of Punishment and accompanied to the ring by her managers Dr. Insane and “the Tax Collector” John Blair and by her stablemates Christy Cooper and Valkyrie, from Brooklyn, New York, weighing 150 pounds, ‘Psycho Queen’ Vicki V!” (The group walks down the aisle to a loud chorus of boos from the audience. Vicki wears her golden crown on her bald head, accenting her black leather outfit. She clenches and unclenches her hand with the spiked glove, ignoring the fans. The group enters the ring, then the wrestlers each go to a corner with the managers taking up the fourth. They shout out, “Posse forever!”) “Her opponent: from Rock Springs, Wyoming, weighing 128 pounds, Bodacious Janet Muniz!” (The entire arena boos even louder at Muniz’s name, then rapidly grows quiet as no sign of her appears.) (Little Laura comes out in shorts and a T-shirt and walks down the aisle! She gets into the ring and says, "My mommy doesn't want me to do this, but I told her I could beat you up and, even though I am just 7 years old, I know I can beat you! And if you get involved (points at Valkyrie), I'll beat you up, too! And you, and you, and you (points at each Posse member).) (The Posse are amused at the little girl continues, "I've never wrestled anybody before in my life. But Vicki, I hate you! And the rest of you Posse members, I hate you all, too! You hate my mommy, so I hate you! I've always wanted to fight you, and I'm as mad as I can be! I'll take you all, one by one! But before I fight you, let me say something to all you people out there who have booed my mommy!" (As Laura speaks, a female ring attendant dressed as a Hooter's Girl is slowly walking around the ring, picking up dirty towels and clothing. Her head is bowed and she is simply doing her job, seemingly oblivious to what is going on inside the ring. The ring attendant works from corner to corner until she gets to the final corner -- the Posse corner. ) (Valkyrie and Vicki notice her and throw their robes at her, not giving her a second look. They then look at Laura and are about to move toward her when suddenly the female ring attendant grabs a chair from ringside and attacks the entire Posse group from behind! The ring attendant, of course, is The Bodacious One, Janet Muniz!) (Janet cleans house with her chair, scattering the Posse and leaving Vicki and Valkyrie KOed in their corner. Janet whispers to Laura, who runs down to the bell and rings it. Janet Muniz says, "Let me save the ref the trouble and I will make the announcement! `Janet Muniz is disqualified at 00:00 of the match!") (Janet lowers the mike and begins to strut around the ring as the fans react some more. Janet then says into the mike, "This little episode was my last little foray into the WCA. I had some unfinished business with these two Wussies and I just finished it up! After all, this match meant nothing. It would have meant something, Sickie, if you would have let your fists do the talking instead of your mouth and climbed into the ring with me for my mandatory title defense! But no! You sent your second stringers after me, your aluminum can in the form of Valkyrie whom I had already crushed twice before! See what you get? Huh?") (Janet lowers the mike as she struts around the ring a little more as the crowd reacts, then raises the mike and says, "My business with these two Wussies is now complete! In fact, my business with the WCA is now complete! I am tired of the way the WCA has taken me for granted for the past 20 months and I am tired of how they misuse and abuse me on top of insulting me! WCA lamebrain fans, you boo me all you want now because this is the last time you'll get the chance! That's it, I quit -- no sh*t!" ) (The rest of the Posse, previously held off by Janet wielding her chair, suddenly charge the ring but Janet -- with Laura in tow -- quickly escapes by running down the aisle. A cameraman follows the two through the backstage area, out the door, and films them getting into Janet's Bronco. Janet quickly starts the vehicle and drives away -- headed for Rock Springs, Wyoming.) (In the ring.) SHARPE: “I knew Janet would have something up her sleeve, but I didn’t think she’d stoop so low as to put her daughter in danger. She’s lucky Valkyrie and Vicki aren’t the types to just beat up someone so little, unlike some other wrestlers here. If this is the kind of person Janet Muniz is really like –“ (He cuts himself short.) One thing’s for certain. Li’l Cheetah is going to be as angry as ever, now that she’ll never get the chance to take on Muniz in a WCA-sanctioned event. Fans, this little act has left us with quite a while to go in our program. We’ll be presenting you with one of our pre-show matches; this one is between ‘the Intimidator’ Amber Axeln and Red of the Bounty Hunters.” ===== ---------- “The Intimidator” Amber Axeln (w/ Liberty, “Mistress of Death” Krista Stevens, and Shannon Stevens) v. Red (w/ Panthra and Black) Referee: Julian Pace ---------- Both women lock up. Axeln blocks a standing wristlock and executes a headlock takedown, taking control of the match. She soon whips Red to a corner and runs in shoulder-first; Red raises her knees up to block her. Axeln staggers back; Red takes her down with a forearm smash. Axeln gets back up; Red executes a Russian legsweep. She gets up first, staying behind Axeln. Axeln picks herself up and ducks an enzuilariato, then she takes Red down with a lariat, resuming her offense. She very shortly sends Red to another corner and runs in shoulder-first; again Red blocks her with her knees. Red runs past Axeln to the far ropes, rebounds, and takes her down with a spinning leg lariat. She waits for Axeln to get back up, then she sends her to the opposite corner and runs in shoulder-first. Axeln raises her knees up to block her, then she decks Red with a back heel kick, retaking control. Red shortly reverses an Irish whip, then she catches Axeln in a belly-to-belly suplex. Axeln kicks out at the last second; Red puts her into a leg hammerlock. Axeln powers out of the hold in 12 seconds and gets back up. She decks Red with a swinging punch, resuming her gameplan. After a minute Red whips Axeln to the ropes; Panthra pulls the top rope down, dumping Axeln on the floor. One .. two .. three .. four .. five ., Axeln reenters the ring. Red meets her with a spinning leg lariat, turning the match around. After a minute Axeln blocks a standing wristlock. Both Krista Stevens and Panthra jump up onto the apron; Pace tries to send them back down as Red blocks a headbutt. Axeln turns a pick-up into a belly-to-belly, but Pace is still out of position. Pace turns back around as Axeln breaks the pin. She taunts Red as the latter gets up, then Red ducks neck-scissors. Axeln pops back up and blocks a pick-up, then Red blocks another. She nails Axeln with a rolling kick, taking control of the match. Axeln shortly blocks a pick-up, instead hitting Red with a forearm to the back. She follows up with a roundhouse punch. Red reverses an Irish whip and rocks Axeln with an elbow to the face, resuming her gameplan. After ninety seconds Axeln ducks an enzuilariato, then she takes Red down with a headbutt, turning the match around again. She soon executes a fisherman suplex; Red kicks out in one. Axeln complains about a slow count, enabling Red to execute an inside cradle; Axeln kicks out in two. Axeln gets back up; Red takes her down with a belly-to-belly. Axeln kicks out barely in time. Red puts her into a leg hammerlock, going back on the offensive. Axeln soon blocks an armlock hammerlock, then she runs to the ropes, rebounds, and nails Red with a back heel kick, turning things back around. Red shortly turns a headlock into a back suplex. She puts Axeln into a camel clutch, retaking control of the match. Axeln soon reverses an Irish whip, then she nails Red with an elbow. She puts Red into a front facelock, going back on the offensive. After 2 minutes Red sends Axeln into a corner. Axeln comes back out; Red takes her down with a lariat. Both women get back up, then Red sends Axeln to the ropes. Axeln rebounds, then she turns a pick-up into a small package, only to get a two count. Both women get back up, then Axeln takes Red back down with an enzuigiri, retaking control. After 2 ½ minutes Axeln runs to the ropes; Panthra trips her up, prompting a warning from Pace as Red blocks a headbutt. Red sends Axeln to a corner, then she meets Axeln’s charge out with a kick to the midsection. Axeln reverses a whip to the opposite corner, then she runs in shoulder-first and rams into Red. Axeln turns them around and steps onto the second turnbuckle, then she executes the Doombomb, pinning Red. (17:50) (Copyright notice appears over a shot of the ring.)
