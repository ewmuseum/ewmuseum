WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION (http.// kessel.simplenet.com/WCA/ ) RAMPAGE Live from Berlin, Germany 8/9/97 -----Main Event----- "Psycho Queen" Vicki V v. Freedom (Best of 13 Series: Match 2) Janet Strong v. Big Norma Hudson "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln v. Joanne Watson Hardcore v. Innocent Blood Luscious Lisa v. Megan van Drake (Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.) (Fireworks explode as the camera shows the crimson WCA logo on the white ring canvas. The camera descends rapidly, then levels off facing the aisle. The camera swerves left, panning out over the capacity crowd and circling the ring, ending its focus on the iridescent three-dimensional WCA letters bracketing the entrance to the backstage area. The referee for the first match heads to the ring.) ---------- Luscious Lisa (w/ Precious, Ashley Hamilton, Debbie Angel, Ginger Thompson, Samantha Strong, and Vicky Armstrong) v. Megan van Drake Referee: Mark White ---------- Lisa takes Drake down in a front slam. Drake sits up, then she gets up and reverses a whip to a corner. She runs in and nails Lisa with a series of European uppercuts and knee strikes. She backs out of the corner and nails Lisa with a chop, taking control of the match. Lisa shortly turns a Frankensteiner into a powerbomb; one, two, kickout. Lisa gets up and executes a senton; one, two, Drake kicks out with authority. She gets up and nails Lisa with an enzuigiri, staggering her. Drake goes to the apron and heads for a corner. Lisa regains her bearings, then she catches Drake’ s flying tackle. She sets Drake down and runs to the side ropes, then she takes Drake down with a lariat. Drake gets back up and blocks an atomic drop, then she kicks Lisa, taking control of the match. Lisa shortly nails her with a chop, taking charge. After 2 minutes Lisa picks Drake up; Drake prevents being set on a turnbuckle. Lisa sets her down and attempts a suplex, but Drake reverses it into a vertical suplex. Lisa slowly turns onto her stomach. She gets up somewhat stiffly; Drake kicks her in the leg hard. Lisa stands up and punches Drake, resuming her offense. She shortly whips Drake to the ropes; Drake rebounds and takes her down with a clothesline, turning the match around. Lisa soon nails her with a back heel kick, going back on the attack. She shortly whips Drake to the ropes; Drake rebounds and ducks an elbow, rebounds and backdrops Lisa. Lisa gets back up; Drake whips her to a corner. She meets Lisa’s charge out with a clothesline, retaking control. She soon whips Lisa to the ropes; Lisa rebounds and ducks an elbow, rebounds again. Both women get hit in a dual clothesline. Lisa gets up first and runs to the ropes; Drake gets up and takes her down with a lariat, resuming her offense. Lisa soon takes Drake down in a belly-to-belly suplex; one, two, kickout. Lisa gets up and kicks Drake hard, going back on the offensive. She shortly whips Drake to the ropes; Drake rebounds and ducks an elbow, rebounds again and takes Lisa down with a clothesline, retaking control. Lisa shortly blocks a pick-up, then she takes Drake down with a jawbreaker, retaking control of the match. Drake shortly blocks a double chickenwing, then she takes Lisa down in a dragon suplex; one, two, kickout. Drake picks Lisa up and puts her on a turnbuckle facing the crowd. She climbs up and executes a belly-to-back superplex, retaking control of the match. She soon takes Lisa down via a flying forearm and makes a cover; one .. two .. three. (9:38) ---------- Hardcore (“Nasty Girl” Amber Jane and “Dominatress” Kathy Lynn) (w/ Dr. Insane, “the Tax Collector” John Blair, and Valkyrie) v. Innocent Blood (Red and Blood) Referee: Esther Siler ---------- Jane and Red lock up. Blood reenters the ring, then both women send Jane to the ropes and nail her with a double elbowsmash. Lynn enters the ring and throws Blood out, then she takes Red down with a leaping lariat. Lynn rolls out of the ring as Jane goes to a neutral corner. Jane comes off the top with a flying spinning leg lariat, taking control of the match. She shortly runs to the ropes; Red executes a cartwheel kick, then she tags in Blood. They send Jane to the ropes and stop her with a double punch to the midsection. They take Jane down with a double cartwheel kick. Lynn enters the ring and decks Red, then she takes Blood down with a back suplex. Lynn assists Jane with a flying somersault headbutt on Blood, helping her partner keep control. Jane shortly tags her in; Lynn executes a slingshot splash, then she grabs the tights; two, shoulder up. Both women get up, then Lynn grabs Blood’s head and runs her face along the top cable, taking charge. Blood shortly reverses an Irish whip, then she executes a shoulderblock and tags in Red. They send Lynn to the ropes and double-backdrop her. Jane rushes into the ring and lays out Blood as Lynn gets back up. Blood rolls out of the ring as Hardcore sends Red to the ropes and deck her with a double clothesline. Jane returns to her corner as Lynn goes to a neutral one. Red gets back on her feet, then she punches a flying Lynn. Red tags out to Blood, then Lynn blocks a front facelock with a punch to the abdomen and tags in Jane. She reconsiders leaving the ring; Red rushes in and boots her through the ropes. She returns to her corner as Blood bodyslams Jane, taking control of the match. After a minute, Jane goes to the apron. She reenters via a springboard shoulderblock, turning things back around. She shortly whips Blood to the ropes; Blood rebounds and knocks her down with a shoulderblock, retaking control. Jane soon counters a pick-up with a small package; one, two, shoulder up. Blood gets up and runs to the ropes; Jane gets up and rocks Blood with an elbow. She goes out to the apron, then she comes back in the ring with a springboard shoulderblock, going back on the offensive. She shortly tags in Lynn, then she executes a Russian legsweep. Lynn follows up with a cover, using the ropes for leverage; Blood kicks out in two. Jane enters the ring; Red rushes in and decks Jane, then she whips Lynn to the ropes as Blood gets up. Lynn rebounds and grabs Red’s head, taking her down in a faceslam onto Blood’s head. Red rolls out of the ring as Lynn helps Blood to her feet. Lynn executes a drop-kick, retaking control of the match. She shortly takes Blood down in a back suplex, then she covers her with both feet on the ropes; two, Siler sees it and disqualifies Hardcore. (7:37) ---------- “The Intimidator” Amber Axeln (w/ Bodacious Janet Muniz, Liberty, “Mistress of Death” Krista Stevens, and Shannon Stevens) v. Joanne Watson (w/ Jane Gordon and Samantha Jones) Referees: Julian Pace ---------- Axeln kicks Watson in the head. Watson staggers back, then she blocks a roundhouse punch. Axeln ducks a short clothesline, turns around, and takes Watson down in a side suplex. Axeln gets up first, then Watson gets up and takes Axeln down in a fallaway slam, taking control of the match. Axeln soon ducks a spin kick, then she puts Watson into an abdominal stretch, restarting her own offense. Watson shortly backs away from a kneelift, letting Axeln fall to the mat. Watson quickly heads for a corner and slips through the ropes. She scales the turnbuckles, then she executes a flying somersault splash. Axeln easily gets her shoulder up to break the cover; Watson angrily hoists her up, then she grabs Axeln’s head and runs her face along the top rope. She yanks Axeln back, then Axeln ducks a leg lariat. She nails Watson with a back heel kick, retaking control. Watson shortly throws off a headlock, then she goes to a corner and gets on the second turnbuckle. She executes a flying forearm and covers; one, two, kickout. Watson applies a spinning toe hold, retaking control. After ninety seconds Axeln blocks a cobra clutch, then she turns around and rocks Watson with a roundhouse punch, turning things back around. After 2 1/2 minutes Watson blocks an armlock hammerlock, then she runs to the ropes, rebounds, and takes Axeln down with a flying clothesline, turning the match back around. After 2 minutes Watson puts the Full Nelson on Axeln; Axeln tries to go to the ropes, but Watson holds her back. Axeln writhes in pain. Watson keeps the hold on tight. After 12 seconds Fallen Angel rolls out from under the ring with a choke chain. She runs past Gordon and grabs Watson’s golf club. She enters the ring and nails Watson across the back of her head with the club, saving Axeln. Pace calls for the bell, disqualifying Axeln; Angel puts the collar around Axeln’s neck. She shoves Axeln out of the ring as the rest of the Click enters. Angel grabs Axeln’s chain and runs down the aisle, practically dragging the Intimidator. Muniz and both Stevens reach Angel first; Angel turns and uses her small size to dart between Muniz’s legs. She grabs the chain and pulls it hard, choking Axeln further before she jumps over the guardrail and disappears into the crowd. (Match: 9:35) ---------- Janet Strong (w/ Angel Hunter and the Dollhouse) v. Big Norma Hudson (w/ Mr. Canuck and Haley “Comet” Hanson) Referee: Mark White ---------- Janet stares at Norma for a moment, then she offers to shake her hand. Hudson shakes it, then both women lock up. Strong blocks a swinging punch, then she whips Hudson to the ropes and follows her in, clotheslining her to the floor. Hudson reenters the ring at the five count; Strong meets her with a jumping side kick, taking control of the match. Strong shortly takes Hudson down with a series of clotheslines, then she he makes a cover; one, two, kickout. Strong gets up and complains about a slow count; Hudson catches her in an inside cradle; one, two, kickout. Hudson runs to the ropes, rebounds, and hiptosses Strong, going on the offensive. After ninety seconds Hudson reverses a whip to a corner and runs in shoulder-first; Strong blocks her with her knees. She nails Hudson with a slap, then she chops Hudson, turning things back around. Hudson shortly blocks a swinging punch, then she executes a headlock takedown, retaking control. After a minute Strong kicks Hudson hard, putting the big woman back on the defensive. Hudson shortly blocks a tackle with a kick to the head. Strong straightens up as she staggers back; Hudson takes her down with a short clothesline, retaking control. After 2 1/2 minutes Strong kicks Hudson, then she runs past her to the ropes, rebounds, and executes an enzuilariato, turning the match around. Hudson shortly bodyslams Strong, retaking control. Strong soon blocks a legsweep faceslam, then Hudson ducks neck-scissors. Strong picks herself up and keeps Hudson from helping her. She runs to the ropes; Hudson nails her with a back kick to the leg. Strong blocks a kick to the midsection, then she applies a cobra clutch, retaking control. She shortly runs to the ropes; Hudson stops her with a shoulderblock, then she bodyslams Strong, retaking control. Hudson shortly backdrops Strong, then she makes a cover; one, two, three. Strong complains about a fast count, but to no avail. Strong turns to Hudson and pats her on the back as the Bounty Hunters come out of the stands. Hunter sees them coming and grabs her kendo stick and a baseball bat from under the ring. Hunter throws Strong the bat; the Hunters enter the ring and go through the Dolls with their bats. Strong nails Black with a swing; Black staggers forward, then she feints a high swing before hitting Strong in the kneecap. Hunter takes Red down with her kendo stick; Hamilton pulls Red out of the ring. Black throws Strong out of the ring, then she takes Thompson down in a DDT. Black quickly rolls out of the ring as the others close in. She comes around the corner and stops as she sees Hudson standing over Janet. Black surveys the scene, then she reenters the ring and quickly crosses to the other side and goes back through the ropes. She and Red take note of the situation, then they both dive under the ring. Samantha and Ashley lift the cover up, trying to find them. Black quickly grabs Janet’s ankles and pulls her under the ring. Red rolls out on the side and nails Vicky and Debbie with her bat as Black comes out, pulling Janet in tow. They pick Janet up and carry her over the guardrail, then they take her through the crowd. The Dollhouse move after them. A cameraman follows the group out of the arena. The Bounty Hunters take Janet into the public ladies’ room, then they put Janet’s head into a toilet and flush it. Red smashes a vent in and climbs up in it. Black takes a can of spraypaint and marks Strong with it, then she sprays it at Angel Hunter. Black turns and climbs into the vent, disappearing.
