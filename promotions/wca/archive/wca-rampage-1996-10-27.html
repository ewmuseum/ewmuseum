WCA Rampage 10/26

27 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION RAMPAGE 10/26/96 -----Main Event----- <Television Title Match> "Destroyer" Dara Holt v. Big Norma Hudson "Psycho" Vicki V v. Sweet Sue (non-title match) "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln v. Vogue Semiramis v. Janet Strong Double D v. Karen "Bugs" Huffman <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA wrestling action> <Wide angle view of the inside of the arena shows a capacity crowd. The ring announcer, timekeepers, and other officials are walking down the aisle to get ready for the show.> ---------- Double D (Debbie Diamond) (w/ Tammi Cooper) v. Karen "Bugs" Huffman (w/ Lovely Liz Strohlheim and Sweet Sue) ---------- Both women lock up, then Huffman sends Diamond into the ropes. She nails Diamond with a clothesline, taking control of the match. After 90 seconds Diamond blocks a double chickenwing. Huffman counters a sunset flip with a fist to the head. Diamond gets back up and ducks a kick to the head. She takes Huffman down with a short lariat, going on the offensive. Huffman shortly ducks a kick tot he head and nails Diamond with a bulldog, retaking control. After 2 minutes Diamond sidesteps a dropkick to the knee. She takes Huffman down with a backspin DDT, turning the match back around. After 4 minutes Huffman blocks a surfboard. She sends Diamond into a corner, then runs in shoulder-first, nailing her and turning the match around. She shortly throws Diamond out of the ring and joins her on the floor. Diamond nails her with the Sabot Kick, dazing Huffman. She sends Huffman into the ringsteps. Huffman slowly gets up. Cooper distracts her; Diamond slams her face onto the steps. Huffman nails Diamond with an elbowsmash and kicks Cooper in the midsection. She throws off Diamond's bulldog headlock. Diamond settles for a mule kick. Cooper charges Huffman, but Strohlheim nails her. Huffman nails Diamond with a double-axhandle chop and reenters the ring. Diamond follows her in, then reverses a whip tot he ropes. She jumps a kick, then nails Huffman with an elbow to the face, taking control. Huffman soon ends her assault by throwing her out of the ring. Diamond reenters at the six count. Huffman sends her into the ropes; Diamond comes back with a sunset flip and pins her. (11:10) ---------- Semiramis (w/ the Mistress of Death) v. Janet Strong ---------- Both women lock up, then Semiramis takes Strong down with a snap mare, taking control of the match. Strong shortly blocks a side suplex. Semiramis ducks a thrust kick to the head; Strong ducks an enzuilariato. Semiramis blocks a facerake; Strong blocks a faceslam. Semiramis punches her, ending the stalemate. Strong shortly blocks a clawhold and executes a takeover suplex, taking control of the match. She shortly catches Semiramis in a belly-to-belly suplex and pins her. (2:31) ---------- "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln (w/ Bodacious Janet Muniz v. Vogue (w/ Valkyrie, Vonya, "Destroyer" Dara Holt, and the Prince) ---------- Vogue counters a headlock takedown with a back suplex. Axeln gets back up and takes Vogue down with an enzuilariato, taking control of the match. A minute later Vogue blocks a slap and runs into the ropes. She knocks Axeln down with a shoulderblock, going back on the attack. After 2 minutes Axeln sends Vogue into the ropes. Muniz trips Vogue up; the ref warns her as Axeln chokes Vogue against the far ropes. The ref shortly ends his argument with Muniz and returns to the job. He begins counting; one, two, three, Axeln lets Vogue go, then hammers her with a side suplex, starting a new assault. Vogue shortly dodges a kneelift. She ducks an enzuilariato, then takes Axeln down with a swinging neckbreaker, retaking control. Axeln shortly takes her down with a slap and applies a kneelock, resuming her own attack. After 4 minutes Vogue backdrops out of a front facelock. She sends Axeln into a corner, then whips her into the ropes. She stops Axeln with a roundhouse punch, turning the match around. Axeln soon blocks a short lariat and sends Vogue into the ropes. She jumps a shoulderblock, then jumps a kick. Axeln turns around and chops Vogue, retaking control. Vogue soon counters a German suplex with an elbowsmash. Axeln prevents being set on a turnbuckle; Vogue settles for a spin kick, going back on the attack. She shortly executes the Flying Dropkick and goes for the pin. Axeln puts her foot on the ropes in two. Vogue pulls her further into the ring; Axeln counters a backspin DDT with a belly-to-belly suplex. She covers Vogue; one, two, the Prince puts Vogue's foot on the ropes. Axeln runs into the ropes, then jumps over a rising Vogue. She punches Vogue in the midsection. Valkyrie grabs the Prince's cane and enters the ring. She nails Axeln with the cane, causing Axeln to duck a short clothesline. Vogue nails Valkyrie instead, knocking her back out of the ring. Axeln stumbles into the ropes, then charges Vogue and nails her with a chop, retaking control. She shortly kicks Vogue in the head, then applies an armlock hammerlock. Vogue submits immediately. (14:20) *Non-title match* ---------- "Psycho" Vicki V (w/ Nasty Tracy Turner and the Dream Master) v. Sweet Sue (w/ Lovely Liz Strohlheim and Karen "Bugs" Huffman) ---------- Vicki nails Sue with a dropkick to the knee. Sue counters a front slam with a right jab, then nails Vicki with a jack-knife. She covers her; Vicki kicks out at the last second. Sue follows up with a kick to the head, taking control of the match. Vicki soon catches her in a Soviet suplex, retaking control. Sue shortly blocks a headbutt and runs into the ropes. Vicki backdrops her out of the ring, then goes through the ropes as well. She throws Sue back into the ring, then reenters and hammers Sue with a Samoan Drop, continuing her onslaught. After 4 minutes she blocks a sleeper. Sue blocks a full nelson and nails Vicki with a kick to the leg, turning the match around. Vicki soon blocks a neck snap and takes Sue down with a front-layout suplex, going back on the attack. Sue shortly counters a European uppercut with a backslide; Vicki easily kicks out. Sue applies a sleeper, retaking control. Vicki shortly blocks a side suplex; Sue ducks a thrust kick to the head. Vicki runs into the ropes, then takes Sue down with a leaping clothesline. She gets back up and runs into the ropes again. Sue takes her down with a hiptoss, then follows up with a jack-knife, retaking control. She shortly whips Vicki into the ropes. Vicki nails her with an elbow, going back on the offensive. After 2 1/2 seconds Sue counters a front slam with a right jab. She kicks Vicki in the leg, going back on the offensive. Vicki shortly counters a hiptoss with a backslide. Sue kicks out in two; Vicki runs into the ropes. She nails Sue with a dropkick to the knee, retaking control. She shortly applies the Claw. Sue submits in 10 seconds. (13:03) ===== Birmingham: "'Psycho' Vicki V avenges her loss to Sweet Sue earlier this year by forcing Sue to submit to her patented Claw, a move very worthy of the term 'finisher.' Up next is our main event; stay tuned!" --------------------COMMERCIAL BREAK-------------------- Birmingham: "Welcome back! Before we go on to tonight's main event, let's run down the card for tomorrow night's PPV event. The Tag Team titles will be on the line, as Darkness, Inc. defends against two members of the Click, 'Lethal' Lori Michaels and Bodacious Janet Muniz. The Click wants to retake the belts away from DI and dominate the tag team division, but they'll go up against two of the toughest women in the league, a team who've held the belt since the last PPV and has defeated a lot of the other top teams here in the WCA. The World title will be up for grabs, as Wildcat defends against fellow stablemate Adora Locklear. Wildcat has been under fire for offering her first defense against her friend; one thing's for sure - the winner is going to have to face a long line of challengers. 'Psycho' Vicki V will defend the Can-Am belt against Panthra. Panthra defeated the Psychotic One earlier in under a minute, and she's looking for that same victory tonight to earn the championship. The Television title will be up in a cage match as 'Destroyer' Dara Holt defends against Kelly Locklear. Kelly wasn't happy with Vicki's decision to fight Panthra tonight instead of her, so she has challenged Holt. Holt will defend tonight, offering the fight to Big Norma Hudson. This match could be scrapped if Norma wins tonight, as she'll be fighting at Hollehammer as well; more on that in a second. Also on the card for Hollehammer: Valkyrie takes on 'the Intimidator' Amber Axeln in a Texas Death match. Both of these women have recently taken some time off; both of these women want to reestablish themselves tomorrow night. Lovely Liz Strohlheim, Sweet Sue, and Janet Strong take on the Scandalous Ones in a six-woman tag match. The feud between the TCA and the Posse of Punishment continues, with Strong looking to avenge her loss against Christy Cooper. The Assassin takes on Big Norma Hudson. Both of these women want a win, especially the Assassin, who'll take on the reigning World champion this coming Thursday. Sierra takes on 'the Flawless One' Sydney Sheppard, Double J goes head-to-head with Femme Fatale. Nasty Tracy Turner and Karen 'Bugs' Huffman will duke it out in a Russian chain match with entourages banned from ringside, showing us the other battle in the TCA / Posse war. And kicking off the show tomorrow night, Haley 'Comet' Hanson goes head-to-head with Battle Axe Bobbet. Haley needs a win to help counter her recent slide; will tomorrow night be the night? "And now it's time for our main event. The first thing the new Television champion Dara Holt did after her victory was to offer Big Norma Hudson the chance to earn the belt. Holt stunned the wrestling world in her first match with the WCA by taking the 450-pound Hudson down with her Nuclear Blast choke slam. Hudson turned around and hammered Holt with her patented Hudson Bay Splash in their next encounter. Tonight, whose finisher will we see last? Let's go to the ring." Ring Announcer: "Ladies and gentlemen, the following match is for the Television championship! Introducing the challenger: accompanied to the ring by her tag team partner Haley 'Comet' Hanson and her manager Mr. Canuck, from Port Albany, Ontario, weighing 450 pounds, Big Norma Hudson!" <Thunder and Lightning and Mr. Canuck walk down the aisle to a huge, positive reception. They slap hands all the way, then enter the ring.> "Her opponent and current Television champion: representing the Evil Empire and accompanied to the ring by Valkyrie, Vogue, Vonya, and their manager the Prince, from Chicago, Illinois, weighing 168 pounds, 'Destroyer' Dara Holt!" <The Prince leads the Evil Empire down the aisle to an equal but opposite crowd response. The wrestlers ignore the crowd, then Holt enters the ring.> Birmingham's voice: "Holt wastes no time in starting the match as she sends Hudson to the ropes, then nails her with a short clothesline. Hanson and Mr. Canuck leave as the bell rings. Mark White is the referee. Hudson sends Holt to a corner. Holt comes back out; Hudson nails her with an elbowsmash - and again! She runs into the ropes. She takes Holt down with a shoulderblock and covers her; one, two, shoulder up. Both women stand up. Holt reverses a whip to the ropes. She stops Hudson with a kick. "Holt sends Hudson to a corner. Hudson comes back out - Holt drives her knee into Norma's back. She takes Hudson down with a legsweep faceslam! Hudson gets back on her feet. She blocks a chokehold. Holt drives her knee into the back again, then spins Hudson around. She hits Norma with a short clothesline, then rakes her face. She sends Hudson into the ropes, then follows her in and again executes a knee to the back. She applies a chokehold! White counts; one, two, three, four, Hudson breaks the hold! "She whips Holt to a corner and charges in - Holt moves out of the way! Hudson stops herself in time; she reverses a whip to the ropes. Holt comes back with a facerake. Hudson takes her down with a neck snap - and the crowd wants to see the Hudson Bay Splash! Norma acknowledges the chant, then keeps Dara down with a front-layout suplex. She's going for the pin; one, two, kickout! Hudson gets up. Holt executes an inside cradle; one, two, kickout. "Dara with another cradle; one, two, kickout. Both women stand up. Hudson blocks a chokehold; Holt ducks a lariat. She executes a knee to the back. Hudson quickly powerbombs her - and again! She covers Holt; one, two, kickout. Both women get back up. Hudson whips Holt into the ropes. Holt comes back with a kick to the midsection. Hudson ducks a short clothesline. "She takes Holt down with an earringer. Holt slowly gets back up. Hudson runs into the ropes. She ducks a clothesline, then rebounds and blocks a chokehold. She applies a clawhold. Holt breaks the hold! Hudson takes her down with a neck snap and drops the elb - nobody home! Holt goes to one knee and chokes Norma! One, two, three, four, Holt yells at the ref. She chokes Hudson again; one, two, three, four, Holt lets go. She confronts the ref as Hudson gets back up. Holt sees her and runs into the ropes. Hudson ducks a clothesline. "Holt rebounds again and reapplies the chokehold! One, two, three, four, Holt rakes Norma's face! She nails Hudson with a kick to the midsection; Hudson blocks another facerake and hits Holt with a forearm to the back. She takes Hudson down with a bodyslam, then follows up with a legdrop. Both women slowly get back up. Holt takes Hudson down in a swinging neckbreaker! They both stand up. Holt shoves Hudson into a corner, then walks in and chokes her again! One, two, three, four, Holt lets go. "Hudson blocks a legsweep faceslam, instead grabbing Holt's head and slamming it on the turnbuckle; one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, and ten! Holt falls back onto the mat! She gets back up. Hudson runs into the ropes. She knocks Holt down with a shoulderblock. Holt sits up and catches her in an inside cradle - got the tights! Hudson barely kicks out in time! Holt rakes her face and gets back to her feet. Hudson stands up; Holt nails her with a short clothesline. She reverses a whip to the ropes and nails Hudson with an elbow. She takes Norma down with a dropkick to the knee and covers her; one, two, kickout. "Holt chokes her again! One, two, three, Holt lets go - momentarily! One, two, three, four, Holt rakes Norma's face. Both women stand up. Holt sends Hudson into a corner and runs in shoulder-first. She rams Hudson, then whips her into the ropes. She grabs Hudson by the throat - puts her other hand on Hudson's back - and executes the Nuclear Blast! Holt covers her; one, two, three!" RA: "Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the match and still Television champion: in 11 minutes, 5 seconds, 'Destroyer' Dara Holt!" <The Empire enters the ring to celebrate Holt's victory; Hudson rolls out of the ring and heads back to the back with Hanson and Mr. Canuck.> Birmingham: "Dara Holt remains the Television champion, successfully defending against Big Norma Hudson. Fans, we are almost out of time. Tomorrow night is WCA's Hollehammer; there's still time to call your local cable company to order! I'm John Birmingham; have a good evening!" <Closing credits appear.>
