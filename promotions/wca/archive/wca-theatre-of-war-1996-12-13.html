WCA Theatre of War 12/12

13 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION THEATRE OF WAR 12/12/96 Live from the Lake Arena in Springfield, Missouri -----Main Event----- Valkyrie v. Flower Power Bodacious Janet Muniz v. Lovely Liz Strohlheim Angel Hunter v. Nasty Tracy Turner Thunder and Lightning v. Bounty Hunters The Scandalous Ones v. Hired Help <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.> <Fireworks explode as the camera shows a top-down view of the ring, highlighting the maroon WCA logo on the canvas. The camera descends, panning to the right and leveling off, showing the capacity crowd. It focuses on the end of the aisle, where 'WCA' frames the entrance to the locker room. Several WCA officials walk down the aisle.> ---------- The Scandalous Ones (Shelly Rage and Jenny Black) (w/ the Dream Master and Dr. Insane) v. Hired Help (Samantha Jones and Jane Gordon) (w/ Joanne Watson) ---------- Rage and Gordon lock up. Gordon nails Rage with a spinning leg lariat, then throws her out of the ring. Rage reenters at the four count; Gordon picks her up and drops her into an over-the-shoulderstomachbreaker, taking control of the match. Rage shortly ducks another spinning leg lariat and throws Gordon out of the ring. Gordon reenters at the seven count as Rage tags in Black. they send Gordon to the ropes, then stop her with a double kick to the midsection. The ref cuts Jones off as the Scandalous Ones nail Gordon with a double enzuilariato, starting a series of double teams. The ref shortly sends Rage out of the ring; Black hammers Gordon with a spinning elbowdrop, going on the attack. A minute later Gordon ducks neck-scissors and tags out to Jones. Jones reverses a whip to a corner. Black comes back witha kick to the midsection, taking control of the match. Jones shortly ducks neck-scissors, then applies a clawhold, turning the match around. After 2 minutes Rage enters the ring. The ref cuts Gordon off as the Scandalous Ones send Jones to the ropes, then nail her with a double kick to the midsection. Gordon feints past the ref and throws Rage out of the ring. She and Jones whip Black to the ropes, then nail her with a double kick to the midsection. Gordon leaves the ring as Jones takes Black down with a reverse neckbreaker. Black gets up rather slowly, then nails Jones with a dropkick to the knee. She tags out to Rage, who sends Jones to the ropes. Jones jumps a shoulderblock, then nails Rage with an elbow to the face, gaining the advantage. Rage very shortly blocks a clawhold and thorws Jones out of the ring. She joins her on the floor, then nails Jones with an enzuigiri. She sends Jones to the guardrail, then nails her with an enzuilariato. She pulls Jones up and throws her back into the ring. Rage heads to the top rope, then nails Jones with a flying dropkick. She gets up and runs into the ropes; Jones gets up and tags in Gordon, then nails Rage with a lariat. She leaves as Rage gets back up and blocks Gordon's Soviet suplex. Gordon counters a cobra clutch with an armdrag takedown; Rage blocks a side suplex. She puts Gordon in a dobule chickenwing submission, going back on the offensive. Rage shortly takes Gordon down with a flying cross body press, pinning her. (7:25) ---------- Thunder and Ligntning (Haley "Comet" Hanson and Big Norma Hudson) (w/ Mr. Canuck) v. Bounty Hunters (Black and Red) (w/ Panthra) ---------- Hudson and Black lock up, then Hudson sends Black to the ropes. She stops Black with a kick to the midsection, taking control of the match. She soon sends Black to a corner. Black comes back out with a kick to the midsection. She tags in Red, then they send Hudson to the ropes. They stop her with a double kick to the midsection, then Red follows up with a knee to the back. She sends Hudson to the ropes; Hudson comes back with a shoulderblock. Red gets up and ducks a lariat, then runs into the ropes. Hudson nails her with an elbow to the face, retaking control of the match. Red shortly ducks a lariat, then puts Hudson in a front facelock. Hanson makes the save; Hudson tags out to her. Black enters the ring; she and Red nail Hnson with a double enzuigiri. Black leaves as Hanson gets up. Hanson reverses a whip to the ropes, then hits Red with a shoulderblock, gaining the advantage. She shortly tags out to Hudson. Black enters the ring and throws Hanson out, then she and Red send Hudson to the ropes. Hudson ducks a double elbowsmash, rebounds again, and dual-clotheslines them. Black leaves as Red gets up and ducks a short clothesline. She runs into the ropes, then hits Hudson with a clothesline, turning the match around. Hudson shortly ducks a kick to the head, then takes Red down with a back suplex, retaking control. Red shortly catches her in a rolling reverse cradle; Hudson easily kicks out. Black enters the ring; she and Red begin hitting Hudson in the back with their forearms. The ref forces Black out as Red puts Hudson in a sleeper, going back on the offensive. Hudson shortly tags in Hanson. They nail Red with a double lariat. Hudson leaves as Hanson executes a Thesz press and covers; Red easily kicks out. She stands up and blocks a tiger suplex, then executes an ipponzei, taking charge. Hanson very shortly sends Red to the ropes. She follows her in and clotheslines Red to the floor. Red reenters at the five count; Hanson hammers her with an avalanche, taking charge. Red shortly puts Black in a sleeper, turning things back around. She shortly tags out to Black. black puts Hanson in a cobra clutch, continuing the assault. Hanson shortly ducks neck-scissors, then tags out to Hudson. Black blocks a spinebuster; Hudson reverses a whip to the ropes. Black stops her with a chop, then runs into the ropes. She sidesteps a tackle, then executes a short lariat, taking control of the match. Hudson shortly tags in Hanson, then they send Black to the ropes. they deck her with a double lariat, then Hudson leaves the ring. Black kicks back to her feet, then she kicks Hanson in the midsection, taking charge. Hanson shortly counters a figure four with a small package; Black kiks out in two. Hanson complains about a slow count, then tags out to Hudson. They nail Black with a double lariat, then Hudson runs into the ropes. Black stands up in time to duck a lariat, then runs into the ropes. She punches Hudson in the abdomen, retaking control of the match. After 2 minutes Hudson reverses a whip to the ropes. Black stops her with a palm blow, then takes her down via a Guerrilla Press, turning the match around. After 2 mre minutes Black reveres a whip to the ropes. She attempts to execute the Swinging Full Nelson, but Hudson's size prevents her. She lets go, then blocks an inverted powerbomb. She hits Hudson with a forearm to the back, turning the match bak around. After 2 more minutes Panthra gives her a baseball bat, then jumps up onto the apron to distract the ref. Mr. Canuck jumps up to warn the ref as Black uses Hudson's head as a ball. She drops down and covers Hudson as Panthra followed by Mr. Canuck return to the floor. The ref turns aroudn and counts; one, two, three. After the match, Panthra brings Red's bat into the ring. The Bounty Hunters proceed to pound on Thunder and Lightning; Mr. Canuck enters the ring to try to stop the beatings, but Panthra stops him instead. WCA security runs down the aisle; the Bounty Hunters stop and leave the ring, heading out through the stands. A stretcher is called out for Norma; Mr. Canuck supports Haley on their way out after Hudson. (Match: 18:40) ---------- Angel Hunter (w/ Janet Strong) v. Nasty Tracy Turner (w/ Christy Cooper, the Dream Master, and Dr. Insane) ---------- Both women lock up, then Hunter blocks a faceslam. She throws Turner out of the ring, then joins her on the floor. Tuerner ducks a kick to the head and knocks Hunter into the ringpost. She counters an ipponzei with a lariat, then reenters the ring. Hunter follows her in, then ducks a leaping forearm. She runs into the ropes, then takes Turner down with a kick, going on the offensive. After 2 minutes Turner dodges a dropkick to the knee. She takes Hunter down with a leaping forearm, going on the attack. Hunter shortly blocks an STF. Both women stand up; Turner sidestpes a dropkick to the knee.. Hunter backdrops out of a jack-knife powerbomb; Turner stands up and blocks a kick to the midsection. She executes a leg lariat, going back on the attack. Hunter very shortly blocks a standing wristlock. She nails Tuerner with a forearm smash. Turner ducks neck-scissors, then runs into the ropes. Hunter gets up and misses with a clothesline, then spins around and backdrops Turner. Turner stands up; Hunter sends her to the ropes, then rocks her with a back heel kick, taking control of the match. After 7 intense minutes Turner ducks a thrust kick to the head. She executes a reverse cradle; one, two, shoulder up. Turner tries again, but again only gets a two count. Both women stand up; Turner executes a front-layout suplex, turning the match around. Hunter shortly blocks a faceslam and executes a back suplex, retaking control. She shortly runs into the ropes. Turner backdrops her, then keeps her down with a single-leg takedown. Hunter picks herself up; Turner applies a standing wristlock, going back on the offensive. After 3 minutes she reverses a whip to the ropes. Hunter comes back with a chop, turning the match around again. After 3 minutes Hunter throws Turner out of the ring. She goes to the floor; Turner knocks her into the ringsteps, then slams her face on them. She throws Hunter back into the ring, then rolls back in herself. She covers Hunter and pins her. (16:39) ---------- Bodacious Janet Muniz (w/ Tall Troll) v. Lovely Liz Strohlheim ---------- Both women lock up, then Muniz executes a tiger suplex. Strohlheim kicks out in two and sits up. She gets back on her feet, then reverses a whip to a corner. She runs in shoulder-first, nailing Muniz and taking control of the match. She soon sends Muniz to a corner, then runs in shoulder-first. Muniz blocks her with her knee, then applies a front facelock. Strohlheim breaks the hold after 9 seconds; she blocks a ropeburn. Muniz reverses a whip to the ropes, then ducks a spin kick. Strohlheim counters a backspin DDT with a belly-to-belly suplex, then covers Muniz; one, two, kickout. Both women stand up; Strohlheim sends Muniz to the ropes, then runs in shoulder-first, nailing her and going back on the offensive. After 3 minutes Muniz counters a hiptoss with a backslide. Strohlheim kicks out in two. Both women stand up; Muniz sends Strohlheim to a corner. She runs in shoulder-first; Strohlheim blocks with her knee. She climbs the turnbuckles, then nails Muniz with a flying bulldog. Muniz gets back up and quickly executes a double underhook piledriver, turning the match around. Strohleim shortly blocks a tiger suplex, then tackles Muniz. She gets up, then decks Muniz with a back heel kick, retaking control. After ninety seconds Muniz catches her in a bodyslam. She follows up with a Hotshot, retaking control of the match. After 2 minutes she puts Strohlheim in the Latino Leg Split. Strohlheim submits after 5 seconds. (8:38) ===== Birmingham: "Bodacious Janet Muniz defeats Lovely Liz Strohlheim with her patented Latino Leg Split, one of the most painful holds in wrestling today. Muniz has defeated many an enemy with that particular hold. Before we go on to our main event tonight I want to address the comments made this morning by the Mistress of Death. She was concerned about the new championship committee's rules concerning the titles, since under those rules both of her titles would have to be defended during her time off later this month. Let's go backstage to the WCA commissioner and head of the championship committee, Mr. [name withheld]." Kessler <standing in front of a table>: "Mistress, the committee understands your concerns. Since you have notified the WCA about your leave before the committee was created you may have your choice of whether to defend before or after you leave. Since you have title shots pending between now and the time before you leave, you need to realize that you may not have not have one or both belts. If you decide to defend before you leave, that can be arranged, or if you want to wait until after you return, you may do so without punishment. Please let us know of your decision soon, and keep up the good work." <Camera focuses again on Birmingham> Birmingham: "There you have it fans, Commissioner Kessler telling the Mistress the decision is hers since she informed the WCA about her leave before the rule change. And now, it's time for our main event. Flower Power is a relatively new wrestler here in the WCA, but she's been making waves recently and has joined the notorious Posse of Punishment. Until recently, Valkyrie was just like her; vicious and rude to the fans. But since Valkyrie has recently changed ways, as it were, and turned herself around almost 180 degrees. Tonight both women will meet in the first of two matches these next two weeks. Let's go to the ring." Ring Announcer: "Ladies and gentlemen, tonight's main event is set for one fall! Introducing first: representing the Posse of Punishment, from Washington, D.C., weighing in at 145 pounds, Flower Power!" <The fans boo Power as she walks down the aisle carring a rattan cane. Power wears several flowers behind bothears. She enters the ring, then removes and raises one of the flowers toinflame the fans further.> "Her opponent: accompanied to the ring by hertag team partner 'the Intimdiator' Amber Axeln, from Des Moines, Iowa,weighing in at 129 pounds, Valkyrie!" <The fans cheer Valkyrie as she walksdown the aisle with Axeln at her side. Both wrestlers slap hands along theway, then they enter the ring.> Birmingham's voice: "Valkyrie and Power swing their arms, priming themselves for action. Axeln pats her friend on the shoulder, wishing her luck before leaving the ring. Power flashes the peace sign - hah! Referee Tina Nolke checks both wrestlers, then starts the match; collar-and-elbow lockup! Power slowly pushes Valkyrie to the ropes, then grabs her by the head and runs her face along the top rope! She lets go and heads to the top rope. Valkyrie spins around in time to duck a flying shoulderblock! Power gets up; Valkyrie executes a single-leg takedown! She follows up with a backslide; one, two, kickout. Valkyrie hits Power with an elbowsmash. She follows up with a springboard dropkick and goes for the pin; one, two, shoulder up. Valkyrie hammers Power with a fistdrop, then lets her get up. She nails Power with an enzuigiri to the face. Power gets up more slowly. Valkyrie sends her to the ropes. "Power rebounds and blocks a kick to the leg. She nails Valkyrie with an enzuilariato! Flower Power bends down and bites Valkyrie's nose! She pulls Valkyrie up and sends her to a corner. She backs up, then runs and leaps in with a Sti - Valkyrie moves out of the way! Power stubmles out backwards - Valkyrie takes her down with a reverse neckbreaker. She taunts Flower Power, then heads to the top rope. She comes down with a flying somersault splash! Valkyrie pulls Power up, then throws her out of the ring! She goes to the apron, then heads to the floor. Power takes her down with an enzuilariato, then hammers her with a splash. Valkyrie slowly stnads up, then Power throws her into the ring. She reenters herself, then faces the crowd and raises a flower. The crowd yells back their disapproval. Valkyrie slowly gets up. "Power boots her out of the ring! She goes to the floor, then nails Valkyrie with a dragon suplex. Valkyrie gets up and shoves Flower Power over the ringsteps. She gets on the apron, then executes flying headscissors! Power turns over, then blocks a chokehold. Valkyrie rolls her over again, then rams her head into the ringpost. She nails Power with an elbowsmash. Valkyrie reenters the ring. Power quickly follows her in, then avoids a backflip cradle. She heads to the top rope, then staggers Valkyrie with a flying karate chop. "She raises a flower as Valkyrie staggers around, then stuffs it into Valkyrie's mouth! She knocks Valkyrie down and goes for the pin; one, two, kickout. Power gets up and waits on Valkyrie, then executes a high cross body; one, two, shoulder up. Valkyrie rolls over, then raises up on her knees. She stands up, then blocks a bulldog. Valkyrie win" RA: "Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the match: in 4 minutes, 12 seconds, Valkyrie!" <Axeln enters the ring and raises Valkyrie's arm in victory. Coming up this Saturday we have Flower Power v. I'm John Birmingham; have a great evening!" <Closing credits appear.>
