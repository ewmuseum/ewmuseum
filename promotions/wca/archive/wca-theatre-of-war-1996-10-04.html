Christy Cooper (w/ Mick Walker and the Dream Master) ---------- Both women lock up, then Cooper whips Strong into the ropes. She gouges Strong's eyes, taking control of the match. She shortly attempts a jumping side kick, but Strong ducks it and legsweeps her. Cooper gets right back up and reverses a whip to the ropes. She attemtps another jumping side kick, but Strong ducks down again and takes her down with a savate kick, taking control. After four minutes she sends Cooper into the ropes. Cooper misses with an elbow, then nails Strong with a clothesline, turning the match around. She shortly attempts a roundhouse punch, but Strong blocks it and executes an enzuigiri, retaking control. She shortly goes for an earringer, but Cooper counters with a backdrop. Strong gets back to her feet; Cooper whips her into the ropes, then plants her with a swinging neckbreaker. Strong slowly gets back up; Cooper shoves her into a corner. Strong charges back out and rocks Cooper with a clothesline, going back on the offensive. She soon runs into the ropes. Cooper stops her with a shoulderblock, then nails her with a dropkick to the knee, turning the match back around. She soon gives the thumbs-up sign and applies the Christy's Clutch. Strong nails her with a back heel kick, turning the match around. She shortly goes for a back suplex, but Cooper counters with a cross bodyblock. Strong kicks out in two and gets back to her feet. Cooper gets up and puts her in a full nelson, retaking control. After 90 seconds Strong puts Cooper in a cobra clutch, starting a new assault. Cooper shortly retakes control with a double-axhandle chop. She shortly takes Strong down with a swinging neckbreaker, then gives the thumbs-up sign and puts Strong in the Christy's Clutch. Despite Strong's clearly-visible denials, Black calls for the bell, saying Strong submitted.
