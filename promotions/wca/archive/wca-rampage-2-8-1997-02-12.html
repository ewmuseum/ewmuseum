WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION RAMPAGE 2/8/96 Consecration Tour Live from Gabarone, Botswana -----Main Event----- She-Devil v. Angel Hunter (Can-Am title match) "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln v. Lovely Liz Strohlheim / Karen "Bugs" Huffman Joanne Watson v. Nadia Williams -------------------- Joanne Watson (w/ Jane Gordon and Samantha Jones) v. Nadia Williams (w/ Crimson and Kim) -------------------- Both women circle each other. They lock up, then Watson backs off and drop-kicks Williams. She heads to the top rope; Williams dodges a flying drop-kick. She runs into the ropes, ducks a clothesline, rebounds and nails Williams with a kick, taking control of the match. Watson soon whips her to the ropes. She misses with an elbow, then executes an ipponzei; one, two, kickout. Watson gets up first and drop-kicks Williams in the knee, gaining the advantage. She shortly catches Williams with a flying clothesline. Williams gets back up, then rakes Watson's face. She executes a headlock takedown, retaking control of the match. She shortly whips Watson to the ropes; Watson returns with a kick, retaking control. She shortly sends Williams to the ropes, then blocks a pick-up with a lariat. Williams gets back up and sidesteps a drop-kick to the knee. Watson stands and counters a European uppercut with a backslide, but only gets a two count. Watson executes a backflip cradle; Williams again kicks out in two. Watson heads for the top rope. Williams ducks a flying clothesline. She meets a standing Watson with a European uppercut, turning things around. Watson shortly rolls away from an elbowdrop from the second rope. She gets up, then meets Williams with a lariat, going back on the attack. Williams very shortly ducks a spin kick and climbs the turnbuckles. She executes a flying cross body press; one, two, kickout. Williams gets up and returns to the corner, then hammers Watson with a flying legdrop and covers; Watson again kicks out in two. Williams gets up, then rocks a slowly standing Watson with a European uppercut, taking charge again. She shortly whips Watson to the ropes; Watson knocks hr down with an elbow, then drops and hooks a leg. Williams kicks out in two; Watson pulls her up, then whips her to the ropes. She chokes Williams; one, two, three, switch of hands, one, two, three, Watson lets go and turns on the ref. Williams picks her up; Watson prevents being set on a turnbuckle. Williams settles for a double underhook backbreaker, retaking control. After 2 minutes she runs into the ropes. Watson meets her with a leaping lariat, turning the match around. After another 2 minutes Watson slaps Williams, then heads for the top rope. Williams runs in and punches her in the stomach; Watson slips and falls onto the turnbuckle. Williams climbs up, then executes a belly-to-belly superplex. She covers with a lateral press; Watson kicks out in two. Williams gets up and waits on Watson, who gets up much slower. Williams executes a hiptoss. She pulls Watson up; Watson prevents a pick-up with a lariat. She blocks a European uppercut. Williams executes a rana; one, two, thr-kickout! Williams hammers Watson with the Guillotine and pins her. (12:26) -------------------- Thunder and Lightning (Haley "Comet" Hanson and Big Norma Hudson) v. Lovely Liz Strohlheim and Karen "Bugs" Huffman (w/ Sweet Sue) -------------------- Strohlheim and Hanson circle each other. They lock up, then Strohlheim whips Hanson to the ropes, then executes an armdrag takedown. She hits Hanson with a forearm to the back, then pins her with a Victory Roll. She walks in, then whips Ichijou to the ropes. She ducks a clothesline, spins and jumps a shoulderblock, then dodges a drop-kick to the knee. She stomps on Ichijou, continuing the assault. After 2 minutes she puts Ichijou into a grapevine. Ichijou powers her way out after 8 seconds, then tags in Hayakawa. The Vinnie Babe enters the ring and throws Ichijou through the ropes, then she and Bryant double-bodyslam Hayakawa. The Vinnie Babe leaves as Hayakawa gets up and blocks a forearm smash. The Vinnie Babe shortly nails her with a kick to the head, then tags in Bryant. They whip Ichijou to the ropes, then nail her with a double kick to the midsection. The Babes double-backdrop her. Bryant pulls the ref around; the ref sees the pin and counts. One, two, three. (8:52) -------------------- Bounty Hunters (Red and Black) (w/ Panthra and Semiramis) v. The Mistress of Death and Freedom -------------------- Red and the Mistress lock up, then Red whips her to a corner. She charges in, smashing into the Mistress and going on the offensive. The Mistress shortly backdrops out of an earringer, then heads for the top rope. She executes a flying sunset flip; Red kicks out in two. The Mistress gets up and executes a belly-to-belly; one, two, kickout. The Mistress gets up; Red ducks a spinning leg lariat as she stands. She tags in Black; the Mistress ducks her kick to the head. Red leaves as the Mistress ducks Black's jumping side kick. Freedom enters the ring; Black ducks her savate kick, letting the Mistress take the blow. Black takes the Mistress down with a belly-to-belly; Freedom makes the save. She leaves as Black runs into the ropes. the Mistress pops up and takes her down with a clothesline, taking control of the match. Black shortly counters spinning headscissors with a Hotshot, then tags in Red. Freedom runs in and throws Black out. Red ducks her flying clothesline. Freedom gets up, then she and the Mistress send Red to the ropes and nail her with a double elbowsmash. The Mistress breaks the hold after 15 seconds, then tags out to Freedom. Freedom sends Red to the ropes, then tags her down with a leaping clothesline. Red gets back up; Freedom whips her to a corner. Red charges out and staggers Freedom with a clothesline as Black enters the ring. The Hunters whip Freedom to the ropes and nail her with a double elbowsmash. Black returns to her corner as Red follows up with a jumping side kick, resuming her assault. Freedom shortly reverses an Irish whip, then ducks a savate kick. She tags in the Mistress, then both women nail Red with a double spinning leg lariat. Black rushes in and throws Freedom out over the top rope, then she nails the Mistress with a forearm to the back. Red follows up with a knee to the back, retaking control. She shortly slaps the Mistress, who returns the same. Red dodges an Asai moonsault; the Mistress gets up and sidesteps a forearm to the back. Red blocks a double reverse chinlock with a facerake, then tags in Black. They whip the Mistress to the ropes, then nail her with a double clothesline. Black follows up with a fist to the midsection, taking control of the match. She soon tags in Red, then both women nail the Mistress with a double forearm to the back. Red follows up with a short lariat and covers with a lateral press; the Mistress gets her shoulder up in two. Red rewards her with a stomp, then puts her in headscissors, continuing the onslaught. The Mistress soon whips her to the ropes, then executes a single-leg takedown. She tags in Freedom; Black enters and throws the Mistress out, then she and Red send Freedom to the ropes. They nail her with a double elbowsmash, then Black returns to her corner as Red headbutts Freedom. Freedom blocks an earringer and executes an armdrag, taking control of the match. Red very shortly tags in Black; the Mistress rushes in and kicks Red through the ropes. She and Freedom whip Black to the far ropes, then double-clothesline her. The Mistress leaves the ring. Black gets up and reverses a whip to the ropes, then catches Freedom in a belly-to-belly; one, two, thr-kickout! Black puts Freedom in a grapevine, taking control of the match. Freedom soon reverses a whip to the ropes, then jumps a kick. She blocks a Full Nelson; Black settles for a short lariat and a lateral press-cover. Freedom kicks out in two, then dodges a forearm to the back. Black nails her with a kick to the midsection; Freedom ducks a short lariat. She executes a headlock takedown, then nails Black with an enzuigiri, turning the match around. Panthra soon hefts Red's bat, then hands Semiramis Black's. She picks up the bat and swings it wildly, keeping the Hunters at bay. Panthra moves in; the Mistress jabs her in the abdomen, then spikes the bat into the base of Panthra's neck. Red rushes in and takes the bat away from the Mistress; the latter leaves the ring and retrieves her Singapore cane. Semiramis stands on top of Freedom's back, challenging the Mistress to reenter the ring. Security does the job for her, rushing down the aisle as Panthra's Army flees through the stands.
