WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION ( http://www.inet-images.com/skessler/WCA/ ) THEATRE OF WAR 5/12/97 *Special 90-Minute Edition* Live from Kansas City, Missouri -----Main Event----- World Title Weapons-Filled Cage Match: Shelly Rage v. Mary Williams Janet Strong v. Freedom (Television Title Tournament Match) "Psycho" Vicki V v. Angel Hunter (non-title match) The Mistress of Death v. Christy Cooper Bodacious Janet Muniz v. The Assassin (Best of 11 Series: Match 5) (Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.) (Fireworks explode as the camera shows the crimson WCA logo on the white ring canvas. The camera descends rapidly, then levels off facing the aisle. The camera swerves left, panning out over the capacity crowd and circling the ring, ending its focus on the iridescent three-dimensional WCA letters bracketing the entrance to the backstage area. The referee for the first match heads to the ring. The camera focuses on commentator Mike Sharpe, seated at a ringside table holding two monitors.) *Best of 11: Match 5* -------------------- Bodacious Janet Muniz (w/ "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln, Liberty, and the Mistress of Death) v. The Assassin (w/ Li'l Cheetah) Referee: Tina Nolke -------------------- Both women circle each other. They lock up briefly, then Muniz shoves the Assassin back and takes her down with a giant swing. Cheetah taunts her; Muniz engages in a heated exchange with her as the Assassin gets up. Muniz turns around, then the Assassin blocks a kick to the midsection. Muniz reverses an Irish whip and catches the Assassin in a bearhug, taking control of the match. The Assassin soon blocks a full nelson, then she slips behind Muniz and executes a Russian legsweep. She gets up, then she puts a rising Muniz back on the mat with an Asai moonsault. The Assassin gets up and sets up for the Sharpshooter. Muniz reaches up and rakes her face, breaking free from the Assassin. Muniz gets up and runs to the ropes, then she nails the Assassin with a kick, resuming her attack. Muniz shortly chokes the Assassin; the Assassin reaches up and grabs Muniz by the throat also. One, two, three, both women let go. The Assassin turns and goes to a corner, then she heads for the top rope. The Assassin catches Muniz with a flying sunset flip; one, two, thr-kickout! Both women get up; the Assassin pokes Muniz in the eye, turning the match around. After 2 1/2 minutes she puts Muniz in a cross-face chickenwing; Muniz submits after 5 seconds. The series is now Muniz-3, Assassin-2. (4:54) -------------------- The Mistress of Death (w/ Bodacious Janet Muniz, "the Intimidator" Amber Axeln, and Liberty) v. Christy Cooper (w/ Dr. Insane, John Blair, "Psycho" Vicki V, and Valkyrie) Referee: Tina Nolke -------------------- Both women lock up, then Cooper takes the Mistress down with a rolling kick, taking control of the match. After 2 minutes she throws the Mistress out of the ring. Nolke tries to keep Cooper in the ring, but she feints past the ref and goes through the ropes. Liberty hands the Mistress a bottle; the Mistress breaks it over Cooper's head. She gets her Singapore cane and repeatedly beats Cooper with it. Valkyrie charges her, driving both of them into the center of the Click II members. They pull Valkyrie off of the Mistress and begin beating her; Nolke goes to the floor to try to restore order. Cooper slowly gets up, blood matting her hair. Nolke threatens to disqualify the Mistress as a way to end the fight; the Mistress and the others shove Valkyrie back to Dr. Insane. The Mistress gets back on the apron; Cooper pulls her down and shoves her to a guardrail. She walks over to the Mistress, then she whips her to the ringsteps. Cooper grabs the Mistress' head and slams it against the steps, then she yanks the Mistress back and hits her with a forearm smash. Cooper whips her to the far guardrail, then Blair gives her a bottle. The Mistress sidesteps a blow and rolls back into the ring. Cooper reenters the ring; Nolke takes the bottle away from her. Cooper ignores her and puts the Mistress into a bearhug, retaking control of the match. After a minute the Mistress scoops Cooper up and executes a sloppy backbreaker, beginning a new offense. After ninety seconds Cooper reverses an Irish whip, then Dr. Insane pulls the top rope down, dumping the Mistress on the floor. One .. two .. three .. four .. five .. six .. seven .. eight .. nine .. the Mistress reenters the ring and reverses a whip to a corner. She charges in; Cooper hefts her leg up to block her. She stops the Mistress' spin and puts her into a bearhug, turning things back around. She shortly throws the Mistress out of the ring. Nolke again unsuccessfully tries to keep Cooper in the ring as the Mistress gets up. The Mistress nails an approaching Dr. Insane with a kick, then she turns and takes Cooper down with an armdrag takedown. The Mistress goes to the floor as well, taking more damage than Cooper. Cooper gets back up and clears off the ring attendants' table. She pulls it into the middle of ringside space, then she puts the Mistress on the table. Cooper gets back up on the apron, then the Mistress rolls off the table away from a flying elbowdrop. Cooper breaks the table in half. Nolke continues her count, reaching 5. The Mistress breaks the count as Cooper gets back up. Cooper ducks a jumping elbow thrust; the Mistress shoves her to the guardrail. Cooper ducks a lariat and clears the ring officials' table, then she pulls it over the ringsteps. Cooper whips the Mistress into the ringpost. She breaks the count, then she sends the Mistress to the table. The Mistress hits the table and falls onto it, sprawled out as Cooper gets on the apron. The Mistress again rolls off the table onto the apron as Cooper attempts an elbowdrop. Cooper breaks the table in half and lands badly on the floor. She slowly gets up and wipes the blood from her eyes. Cooper heads back into the ring using the steps. She reenters the ring, then the Mistress ducks a clothesline. She goes back to Cooper and sets her up, then she executes the Kiss of Death. Cooper flops onto her back, out cold. The Mistress covers and pins her. (8:53) (Non-title Match) -------------------- "Psycho" Vicki V (w/ Dr. Insane, John Blair, and Valkyrie) v. Angel Hunter (w/ Janet Strong) Referee: Esther Siler -------------------- Vicki runs to the ropes, then Hunter stops her with a kick. She takes Vicki down with a vertical suplex, taking control of the match. She shortly whips Vicki to the ropes; Vicki rebounds and rocks her with a swinging punch, taking charge. Hunter shortly blocks a pick-up, then she whips Vicki to the ropes and takes her down with a spinning leg lariat, retaking control. Vicki shortly punches her again, putting Hunter back on the defensive. Hunter soon reverses a whip to a corner, then she runs in and pummels Vicki with a Stinger Splash. She rallies the cheers; Vicki uses the distraction to take her down with a tackle. She gets back up and hammers Hunter with a diving headbutt, resuming her assault. She shortly sends Hunter to a corner and charges in; Hunter moves out of the way. Vicki slams into the turnbuckles; Hunter takes her down with an inside cradle. Vicki barely kicks out in time. Hunter gets up, then she waits for Vicki to get up. Hunter nails Vicki with a chop, turning things back around. After 3 minutes she whips Vicki to the ropes; Vicki ducks a clothesline, rebounds, and doubles Hunter over with a kick. Vicki puts Hunter into a full nelson, slowing the match down. After 22 seconds she breaks the hold and whips Hunter to the ropes; Hunter rebounds and takes her down with an armdrag takedown. She picks Vicki up and bodyslams her, retaking control of the match. She shortly sends Vicki to a corner and charges in shoulder-first; Vicki moves out of the way. Hunter nails the ringpost, then Vicki pulls her back into the ring and takes her down with a kneelift. Vicki goes through the ropes and scales the turnbuckles. Strong gets up on the apron and shoves Vicki off the top rope; Vicki knocks Hunter back to the mat and lands on her; one, two, three. (7:19) *Television Title Tournament Match* -------------------- Janet Strong (w/ Angel Hunter) v. Freedom (w/ Shannon Stevens and the Dream Master) Referee: Mark White -------------------- Both women lock up. Freedom ducks an enzuilariato, then Strong sidesteps a drop-kick. She runs to the ropes; Freedom stops her with a kick, then she executes a sunset flip; one, two, kickout. Strong gets up and sends Freedom into a corner. She runs in, then she takes Freedom out with a legsweep faceslam. Freedom gets up and dodges a tackle. Strong slows down in the adjacent corner and gets on the second turnbuckle. Freedom drops to the mat to avoid a flying clothesline. She pops back up, then she meets a rising Strong with a jumping elbow thrust, taking control of the match. After a minute she runs to the ropes; Strong nails her with a kick, putting Freedom on the defensive. After 2 minutes Freedom nails Strong with a drop-kick to the knee, turning the match around again. Strong soon reverses a rolling reverse cradle; one, two, shoulder up. Strong gets up and runs into a corner, then she climbs onto the second turnbuckle and executes a flying cross body; Freedom kicks out in two. Strong gets up and goes back to the corner. She climbs to the top, then she executes a picture-perfect flying lariat, going back on the offensive. Freedom soon nails her with a lariat, turning things around again. She shortly executes the Showstopper; Strong blocks it with by pulling up her knees. Both women are down; White begins the count. One .. two .. three .. Strong gets back up and heads for a corner. Freedom struggles back to her feet; Strong takes her down with a flying lariat, retaking control. After a minute Freedom ducks a flying clothesline. She rests while Strong gets back up, then she nails Strong with a jumping elbow thrust, starting a new offense. After 2 minutes she throws Strong out of the ring and joins her on the floor. Strong grabs a fan's purse and nails Freedom with it. She hits Freedom again, then she throws Freedom back into the ring. She brings the purse with her into the ring and swings it at Freedom, who ducks it. She snatches the purse away and swings it at Strong; Strong grabs it again. Both women pull at the straps; Strong wins and hits Freedom with it again. She gives it to Hunter, who gives it to the owner as Strong puts Freedom into the Bear Hug. Freedom submits immediately. Janet Strong advances in the tournament. (9:45) Sharpe: "Janet Strong advances in the tournament with this match. Let's take a look at where things stand at the moment."
