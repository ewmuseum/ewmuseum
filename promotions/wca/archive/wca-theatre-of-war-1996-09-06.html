WCA Theatre of War 9/5

6 September 1996

WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION THEATRE OF WAR Live from Kansas City, Missouri 9/5/96 -----Main Event----- Valkyrie v. "Showgirl" Susan Cox Sweet Sue v. Sellyna Shriek Danielle Stewart v. Battle Axe Bobbet Lovely Liz Strohlheim v. Elizabeth Karen "Bugs" Huffman v. Woman <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA wrestling action> <Wide angle view of the inside of the arena shows a capacity crowd. The ring announcer, timekeepers, and other officials are walking down the aisle to get ready for the show.> ------------------------- Karen "Bugs" Huffman (w/ Lovely Liz Strohlheim) v. Woman ------------------------- Both women lock up, then Woman kicks Huffman in the head. She whips Huffman into the ropes; Huffman jumps a shoulderblock off the rebound. She rebounds again, then slaps Woman. She attempts a kick to the head, but Woman ducks it and goes for a front slam. Huffman punches her instead, then executes a Samoan Drop, taking control of the match. She shortly goes for a kick to the head, but Woman ducks it and sends her to a corner. She charges in, but Huffman moves out of the way. Woman stops herself, then dodges a spin kick and applies a cobra clutch, taking control. She shortly goes for a fireman's carry, but Huffman blocks it. Woman whips her into the ropes; Huffman takes her down with a kick and goes for the pin. Woman easily kicks out; Huffman rewards her witha snap suplex, retaking control. Woman shortly turns the match back around with a faceslam. She shortly goes for an atomic drop, but Huffman blocks it, evening up the match. Both women start trading blows. Woman takes the battle to the floor by throwing Huffman out of the ring. Woman hiptossses Huffman, then goes for a back heel kick. Huffman blocks it and throws Woman onto the ringsteps. Woman gets back up and chops Huffman, then nails her with a spinning mule kick. Huffman throws Woman back into the ring, then nails her with a bulldog. Woman comes back with a choke against the ropes, taking control of the match. She shortly sends Huffman into the ropes. Huffman comes back with a clothesline that sends Woman out of the ring. She quickly reenters; Huffman nails her with a kick to the head, taking control. She shortly goes for a kick to the head, but Woman ducks it and chops her, taking control. After 90 seconds she sends Huffman into the ropes. Huffman ducks an elbow, then rebounds. Both women get hit with a double clothesline. Woman gets up first and pulls Huffman up, then sends her into the ropes. Huffman stops on the rebound, then attempts a small package. Woman reverses it; one, kickout. Woman punches Huffman in the midsection, resuming her attack. She shortly attempts to send Huffman into a corner, but Huffman reverses it and runs in shoulder-first. She nails Woman, turning the match around. Woman slowly loses all of her momentum. After five minutes Huffman applies an armlock hammerlock. Woman quickly reaches the ropes; Huffman pulls her back into the center of the ring, then applies the Bearhug. Woman submits immediately. (14:46) ------------------------- Lovely Liz Strohlheim (w/ Karen "Bugs" Huffman) v. Elizabeth (w/ the Nature Boy) ------------------------- Both women lock up, then Strohlheim nails Elizabeth with a brain buster and covers her with the Shcoolgirl Pin; one, two, the Nature Boy puts Elizabeth's foot on the rope. Strohlheim pulls Elizabeth up, then sends her to the ropes. Elizabeth comes back with a kick, taking control of the match. She shortly goes for a bodyslam, but Strohlheim counters with a small package. Elizabeth kicks out in two and gets up. Strohlheim nails her with an enzuigiri to the face, retaking control. She shortly goes for an abdominal stretch, but Elizabeth counters with a hiptoss, retaking control. She shortly sends Strohlheim into the ropes; the Nature Boy trips Strohlheim. Elizabeth catches her and chokes her against the far ropes as the ref warns the Nature Boy. She then throws Elizabeth to the floor and joins her. She nails Strohlheim with a savate kick, then reenters the ring. Strohlheim talks with Huffman, then follows Elizabeth in. Elizabeth attempts a jumping DDT, but Strohlheim backdrops her instead, evening up the match. Both women start avoiding the other's moves. Elizabeth shortly goes for a back heel kick, but Strohlheim blocks it and hammers her in a snap suplex, taking control. A minute later she nails Elizabeth with a spin kick, then follows up with a brain buster. She covers with the Schoolgirl Pin and pins Elizabeth. (4:41) ------------------------- Danielle Stewart v. Battle Axe Bobbet (w/ John Bobbet) ------------------------- Both women lock up, then Stewart whips Bobbet into the ropes. She ducks an elbow; Bobbet rebounds again, then kicks Stewart, taking control. After a minute she throws Stewart out of the ring and joins her on the floor. Stewart takes her down with a rolling kick. Bobbet executes a legsweep faceslam. Stewart whips Bobbet into the guardrail and reenters the ring. Bobbet follows her in; Stewart executes a headlock takedown, going on the offensive. Bobbet quickly ends it with a bulldog. After almost four minutes Stewart avoids a legsweep faceslam and attempts a crucifix. Bobbet turns it into a Samoan Drop, then pulls Stewart up. Stewart retries the crucifix, getting Bobbet's shoulders down this time. Bobbet kicks out in two. Stewart blocks an armbar. Bobbet attempts a bodyslam, but Stewart executes a small package. Bobbet kicks out in two. Both women continue the stand-off. Stewart slowly gains the advantage. She executes a running powerslam into a cover, but Bobbet kicks out in one. Stewart complains about a slow count, then takes control of the match with a kick to the midsection. She soon hiptosses Bobbet. Bobbet gets back up and runs into the ropes. She ducks a clothesline, then rebounds again and knocks Stewart down with an elbow. She applies a spinning toe hold, going back on the offensive. Stewart quickly ends it by countering a spinebuster with a swinging neckbreaker. She shortly goes for a spin kick, but Bobbet ducks it and sends her into the ropes. She clotheslines Stewart, retaking control. Stewart shortly begins to build a comeback. After two minutes Bobbet hammers her with a Doctor Bomb, resetting the pace of the match. After two minutes Stewart evens the match up with a bulldog. Both women start trading blows. Stewart shortly ends the stalemate with a spin kick. Bobbet quickly chokes her against the ropes. The ref begins her count; one, two, three, four, Bobbet lets go. Stewart turns around and headbutts her, resuming her onslaught. She soon nails Bobbet with a swinging neckbreaker. She waits for Bobbet to get back up, then pins her with a sunset flip. (15:35) ------------------------- Sweet Sue (w/ Tall Troll) v. Sellyna Shriek ------------------------- Sue nails Shriek with an enzuilariato before the bell, assuming control of the match. Shriek quickly takes control with a swinging punch. Sue shortly blocks a takeover suplex and executes an over-the-shoulder stomachbreaker, retaking control. She shortly attempts to whip Shriek into the ropes, but Shriek reverses it. She executes a single-leg takedown, then stomps on Sue. She picks Sue up, then hammers her with a powerslam. Shriek turns it into a cover and pins Sue. (2:22) ===== Birmingham: "A very quick victory over the rapidly-falling Sweet Sue. Shriek is rapidly climbing up the rankings ladder; she's definitely overcoming her early losses here in the WCA. Sue seems to be taking the other route. "Before we go on to tonight's main event, let's talk about the Lords of the Ring. The first match that evening will be Danielle Stewart v. 'The Flawless One' Sydney Sheppard. Danielle is still a newcomer here, but already has two wins under her belt. The same, though, is true of Sydney. This match will put a bump in one of their careers. After that 'Showgirl' Susan Cox takes on Karen 'Bugs' Huffman. Cox took a brief hiatus from the WCA; she wants to make a noticable return to form, both tonight and at the PPV. Huffman, in the meantime, needs some more wins to advance up that rankings ladder. The last match before the main event at the PPV is the Tag Team titles submission match between the champions the Battle Kats and Darkness Inc. Until late these two teams were friends under the same manager. Darkness Inc. graciously let the Battle Kats have Lu Chin in their corner, while they prepared for their match alone. However, just recently the Battle Kats appeared to have betrayed that friendship by threatening to reveal the Mistress of Death's chart. She and Semiramis have ended their relationship with Lu Chin and have promised to defeat the Kats not only for the Tag belts, but also in the three-ring battle royale match later that evening. "As you probably know by now, only the top half of the WCA, the top twenty, will participate in the tournament. All wrestlers start in the top ring, or the World ring. As they get eliminated, they will proceed to the next ring, the Can-Am ring, then to the floor ring, or the Television ring. Ironically, the first person to be eliminated from the World ring and the first person to be eliminated from the Can-Am ring will get brief respites as they wait for an opponent to join them. The three survivors will get $1 million in prize money, plus the World ring's survivor will get a brand new car of thier choice, courtesy of the WCA. What about the singles belts? Here goes: if the Unified champion Bodacious Janet Muniz wins the World ring, she keeps both belts. If she's eliminated, the belts become separate again as the World belt will be an additional prize for that ring's survivor. If Muniz gets eliminated from the Can-Am ring, the Can-Am belt will become that ring's bonus prize. Also, as the name implies, the Television ring's bonus prize is the Television belt, as WCA officials have felt it's time to reinstate it. I took some heat from it, but I'll say it again; Muniz could walk in as Unified champion and walk out the Television champion. Or she could keep the Unified belts. Anything can happen in this event. Plus, as Semiramis mentioned in a flash earlier this week, old grudges and scores can be evened up in this tournament. "So just who will be in the tournament? The top twenty as of September 10, meaning the resulting rankings from the upcoming Monday Knights will determine the participants. This will give wrestlers time to prepare for what will most certainly be a most grueliing battle. As far as the Posse of Punishment is concerned, they have matches coming up this weekend, so they will get the opportunity to enter the tournament. Three rings, three titles, twenty wrestlers. It's time to see who is the best in the WCA!" --------------------COMMERCIAL BREAK--------------------- Ring Announcer: "Ladies and gentlemen, the following match is set for one fall. Introducing first: from Las Vegas, Nevada, weighing in at 125 pounds, 'Showgirl' Susan Cox!" <Cox walks down the aisle, ignoring the fans. She enters the ring.> "Her opponent: accompanied to the ring by the Prince, from Des Moines, Iowa, weighing in at 129 pounds, Valkyrie!" <Valkyrie and the Prince walk down the aisle. Valkyrie taunts the audience gleefully. They enter the ring to a wall of boos.> Birmingham's voice: "Our main event tonight is about to get underway. Cox wants to reestablish herself as one of the WCA's rising stars as Valkyrie, the head of the Evil Empire, wants to continue her path of destruction. The Prince leaves the ring. Valkyrie picks Cox up and sets her on the turnbuckle! She climbs up - there's the opening bell. Valkyrie with a belly-to-belly superplex and a cover; one, two, thr-shoulder up! The Prince comes up on her with his cane - she knocks it out of his hands! Valkyrie pulls her into the guardrail. Cox ducks an enzuigiri to the face. "She reenters the ring, followed by Valkyrie. Cox legsweeps her. Valkyrie gets back up - Cox picks her up and hammers her in a Samoan Drop. She covers Valkyrie; one, two, thr-shoulder up! Almost did it!
