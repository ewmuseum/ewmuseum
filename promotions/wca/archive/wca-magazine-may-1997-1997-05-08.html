WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION MAGAZINE May 1997 IN THIS ISSUE: From the Desk of the Commissioner News Feature Profiles Rankings Upcoming Matches <From the Desk of the Commissioner> Hello all WCA fans! Welcome to the May 1997 issue of the WCA Magazine! In this issue you'll read the latest news and announcements, plus we have two feature articles, one taking you the reader on a tour of the WCA's home, the Lake Arena, and the other is a special article written by me concerning the biggest news in the WCA right now, the possible formation of a union. In addition, you'll see how the wrestlers of the WCA measure up in our latest rankings report, and get a list of all upcoming matches. Remember, fans, that all cards are subject to change. April was a turbulent championship time for the WCA. The month began with the final match in the Tag Team Titles Mini-Tournament, between the two top-notch teams the Bounty Hunters and Hardcore. Although Hardcore gave it their best, they couldn't counter the awesome ability of the Bounty Hunters. Realizing this, they left the ring, thus insuring the Bounty Hunters wouldn't walk away with the belts. The Championship Committee decided to reschedule the Bounty Hunters against the team that the former champions, the Magic Mountain Warriors, left high and dry, the Delicious Dolls. Thus, on April 21 new Tag Team champions were crowned, the Bounty Hunters. In another development, Sweet Sue, who was required by contract to fight a wrestler on the April PPV Quake II, dropped her belt rather than find a new champion. As you know, the result is a Television title tournament that is moving forward at a brisk pace. Another result of Sue's actions is her demand that the WCA unionize. She and several other wrestlers of the WCA have petitioned me to call a vote on whether or not there will be a union. She's sent out a union card to all WCA wrestlers, including the two house wrestlers Dangerous Renee Lake and Linda Belle. Since she's given me all of the cards, I will allow a union vote. Her requests have formed some unusual alliances. For those who are interested, the wrestlers who signed their union cards are Haley "Comet" Hanson, the TCA (!) (aka Lovely Liz Strohlheim, Karen "Bugs" Huffman, and Sweet Sue), Li'l Cheetah, Big Norma Hudson, the Rising Sun (aka Aya Ichijou, Kazumi Hayakawa, and Asano Otani), Freedom, Helena "High Kick" Knightsbridge, Sellyna Shriek, and the Assassin. The TCA and Thunder and Lightning have been enemies for most of this year, with the TCA claiming Haley and Norma are traitors to the WCA, whatever that means. Freedom has no love for the Posse, but she and the Rising Sun are both in favour of the union. Li'l Cheetah hates almost everybody else who signed their cards except for the Assassin. April was also a turbulent time for two groups. During the first half of the month, the Click II and the Posse of Punishment escalated their vicious war against each other, with the Click II winning the majority of the matches. All that changed when the Posse of Punishment soundly triumphed over the Click II at Quake II in their tag team elimination match, and "Psycho" Vicki V beat Bodacious Janet Muniz at her own Best of 11 series. But the war isn't over yet, as the Mistress of Death has embarked on a quest to take on the members of the Posse once and for all. Also, the Click II's skeleton in its closet keeps rattling, with the return of Helena "High Kick" Knightsbridge to the WCA. Knightsbridge was forced out of the WCA due to her failing a steroids test, but she returned at Quake II, claiming that the test was tampered with. Investigators on both sides are continuing to look into the allegations. Another pleasant surprise was the reappearance of the Magic Mountain Warriors at Quake II, looking for their belts back. Sadly, the MMW was denied that opportunity as the Bounty Hunters successfully defended their belts through outside interference in that match. And finally, April will be known as a turbulent time for the roster of the WCA. The first change was at the loser-leaves-town match between Shannon Stevens and Semiramis. Stevens, taking most of the punishment in that match, managed to come out on top and defeated Semiramis, who as the contract required is no longer with the WCA. Also, toward the end of the month, five new wrestlers entered the WCA. They are Anaconda, "the Dragon" Selena Drakovich, Luscious Lisa, Dannielle "Danni" Moonstar, and Osamu Senshou. We at the WCA head offices wish these ladies the best of luck in their careers in the WCA, and look forward to seeing more of them in the ring in the near future. May also promises to be an exciting month for the WCA. The Television Title Tournament will be continuing, as the top wrestlers of the WCA duke it out for the gold. The Mistress of Death will be continuing her crusade against the Posse of Punishment, while the Henchmen of Hades will continue to strikes out at both the Posse and the Click II. The Bounty Hunters have a long list of challengers for the tag titles. The TCA will continue their work slowdown in protest of "unfair wages," fighting only each other until their demands are met. And Shelly Rage, whom many around the head offices are saying is the next great WCA champion a la Li'l Cheetah, the Mistress of Death, and Bodacious Janet Muniz, will continue to take on all comers for the belt. Time will tell if she truly is the next great WCA champion. <News> * As the editorial said, the cards have been presented for the formation of a union in the WCA. Thus, all wrestlers of the WCA should vote on the issue of unionization; should the wrestlers of the WCA be in a union or not? * Rumours have it the WCA is possibly looking into adding four additional cards to the schedule during May, a traditional sweeps month in American television. No one from the WCA offices or The Fantasy Sports Network had any comment. * Any wrestler who will not be active in the league during the summer months should let the WCA head offices know. <Features> "Home of the WCA" -by Byron Stewart The WCA began life in February 1996 in a small but cozy arena named after William H. Lake, one of Springfield, Missouri's leading businessman and father of WCA house wrestler Dangerous Renee Lake. The Lake Arena was constructed in 1978 to house one of the local universities' basketball teams while the school's own arena was being reconstructed after heavy tornado damage. After the Student Center was rebuilt, the Lake Arena became one of the area's convention centers, used for all sorts of purposes. In January of 1996, the then-president of the WCA, [name withheld], entered negotiations with Mr. Lake over using the arena as the home for all WCA events in the Springfield area. To begin our tour of the building, let's start with the entrance foyer, located on the south side of the structure. When you enter the double doors, you enter the building's main hallway. On either side halfway to the main arena spectators see two more glass double doors, leading to hallways that access various conference rooms on the left and the locker rooms, announcement booths, and the WCA training facilities on the right. For now, we'll proceed down the main hallway. At the head of the hall is the foyer entrance. On the right side is the concession stand and merchandise table, and on the left is the water fountain and public-use restrooms. The large viewscreen over the entrance to the arena proper is for the crowds, as they don't have to miss whatever event happens to be taking place there while they take care of what they need to. Just under the viewscreen is the archway to the main arena. As we go through it, the first thing spectators notice is that they are coming out on the mid-level of the arena. This 3000 seater is divided into eight sections, four lower sides and four upper sides. Each corner has special accommodations for wheelchairs and other handicapped patrons, and the entrance level aisle circling the arena was designed for wheelchair seating as well. As we said earlier, the Lake Arena used to be a basketball arena, but since the team returned to their own home the baskets and floor have been removed and renovated. Over each of the top sections is a monitor that shows a close-up view of whatever is happening on the floor. During WCA events each monitor shows the same view, which is usually (but not always) the same as the live feed on the WCA shows. Let's walk down to the floor. Depending on the event, chairs may or may not be set up on the floor of the building. Of course, there are floor sections at WCA events. On the right side of the main entrance at floor level is the entryway to the locker rooms. Let's head down that aisle. You'll notice that it forms a cross just under the stands, with the short end acting as the backstage interview area for all WCA events. At the cross-aisle, we can go to the left locker room, or the right. They both have the same layout, so let's go to the left one. As you enter, you can see that it was designed for both men and women, with separate toilet facilitites at the north end. Next to those are the showers. In the middle of the room is the lockers (!). You will note that the aisle from the arena door to the other door is extra wide. At the south end is an office for whomever is in charge of the group. When the WCA is in town, that office is used by the floor coordinator. At the south end of the locker room, you'll see two bulletin boards for messages and a beverage machine. On the east side is a door that leads to the hallway that eventually goes back to the main entrance hall. As we go out into that hallway, to the north is access to the WCA training facilities. To the south is the door to the other locker room and, if we follow the hall around the corner and to the west, the main hall. Let's go north, though, and check out what's up their. At the very end is a heavy door that leads to stairs. If we go up those, we'll come out on the announcement booth that sits over the locker rooms entrance to the floor. Since Mike Sharpe, the WCA's commentator, and other WCA officials sit at ringside the booth is used primarily for the WCA executives. Just before that door, though, on the east side is the double glass doors to the WCA Training Center. As we enter, we see that the first floor is primarily one large room. It contains three rings at the north end and various weightlifting equipment at the south end. The north wall holds the bulletin boards for announcements, while the south end has towel racks and a large mirror. The east side of the room holds five small offices, for the WCA instructors and trainers. You will note the double doors at the northeast corner, right across from the doors to the Center. Remember that the northern locker room's aisle was extra-wide? This is the path critically-injured wrestlers take when they need to be stretchered out. They go through the northern locker room, up the aisle, and across the training center to the waiting ambulance. Diagonally across the room from the emergency exit are the stairs leading to the aerobic room and track of the Center. On the north end of that is the main entrance and exit for WCA staff and wrestlers. Let's leave the WCA Training Center and head back to the main entrance. If we go across the Main Hall into the west hallway, we'll note that along the south side, then the west side, are the conference rooms for various events. Since the WCA rarely uses these rooms, during many shows other activities are taking place here. Well, that concludes our tour of the Lake Arena. I hope you enjoyed your tour of the facility. If you ever get a chance to see one of the WCA's shows here in person, I guarantee it's an experience you won't forget. <Profiles> This column is dedicated to the April's Wrestler of the Month, Shelly Rage, and not one but two Tag Teams of the Month, the Psycho Crushers and Innocent Blood. The WCA recently caught up with these women for these interviews. "Shelly Rage: Greatness in the Making?" WM: Congratulations, Shelly, in your successful defense of the belt against your tag team partner. Now, you've defended the belt against a host of competitors - Blood, Janet Strong, Mary Williams, Christy Cooper, not to mention taking the belt away from the Mistress of Death. Some would argue that many of those opponents are not the best of the league. Shelly: As far as I am concerned, the best wrestlers in this league are the ones who are brave enough to challenge for the most respected world title in our sport. We have heard all the stories of those who think that titles are meaningless! Well, those are the words of cowards who are afraid to test themselves and find out how great they really are. Women like Lil' Cheetah always claim that they are the best. Sorry ladies, the best wrestler in this sport is the one who wears this belt. And whether you like it or not the belt is around the waist of a member of the Posse!! WM: You've got quite a list of competitors lined up for a shot at the World title. What do you think of them . . . Janet Strong again, the Mistress' rematch, Mary Williams in a weapons-filled cage match, Femme Fatale? Shelly: I don't care if the Queen of England herself wanted a match. If anybody thinks they can take this title away from me, then issue the challenge and consider it accepted. WM: Shelly, many are saying you are the next great champion, ready to take your place in WCA history alongside such former champions as Li'l Cheetah and the four-time champion Bodacious Janet Muniz. You've certainly earned a reputation as a fighting champion. How long do you think your reign will last? Shelly: Quite simply, my reign will be as long as I want it to be. If I truly want to remain the champ, I must be willing to do anything it takes to keep this title. All of my future opponents will know what I am talking about after spending some time in the ring with me! WM: Now, you are the second member of the Posse of Punishment to become the World champion. I've noticed that at virtually the same time the Scandalous Ones have separated themselves from tag team action. Are you focusing on your current title before you pursue any others? Shelly: Jenny needs the opportunity to prove to herself that she has what it takes to get the job done! We still have some tag matches scheduled, such as the battle at Meltdown with Freedom and Shannon. However, she feels that in order to make ourselves better as a unit it would be better for us to pursue individual objectives. Don't worry the Scandalous Ones will not fade into oblivion. WM: Now, you've been in the WCA for just over a year, although and the rest of the Posse gang earned - quite spectacularly, I might add - a three-month suspension over the attack on Bodacious Janet Muniz less than a month since your debuts. You seemed to have such a promising future when you originally entered, then after the suspension you all seemed to have gotten a bit of ring rust. What do you attribute to your slow start last fall? Shelly: We didn't really have a sense of direction until Dr. Insane chose to take a more direct role in the wrestling activities of the Posse of Punishment. As we have all recently discovered, Dream Master wasn't exactly focused on wrestling either. If he was, Jenny and I would already be World Tag Champs!! WM: Speaking of Jenny, Shelly, you and she are certainly two very good wrestlers, and one heck of a tag team. How did the two of you meet? Shelly: Well, my dad left my mom when I was 3 years old and while she was pregnant with my sister. For a few years my mom tried to do a variety of things, such as working at the Candy Counter at the Mann's Chinese Theatre and serving as a cocktail waitress. Those jobs weren't satisfying her enough and they weren't paying very well so when Jenny's Dad posted an ad in the Los Angeles Times, she decided that would be a good opportunity for her to try something new. In the meantime I met Jenny and I started to go to school with her and Tracy. Yeah, Tracy and Jenny had been friends since nursery school but Jenny began to make time in order to be more with me. Even then Tracy was a little bit jealous but Jenny was the one who kept us in control. Jenny would go out of her way to introduce me to some of her friends and make me feel like I was a part of the clique. Jenny's parents would buy me clothes so I could fit in better with the group as well. As a sign of thanks I began to share with Jenny some of the tricks my mom taught me as far as fighting. Eventually Mr. Black would introduce my mom to the man who would eventually be my stepfather. It was at Jenny's sweet 16 party where they met. The fairy tale ending came true because they would get married six months later and my mom, my sister and I moved to his home in Encino. Actually, we lived next door to the Jackson family at the time, and we would always exchange pleasantries with them, even with Michael. WM: Shelly, you were present for Jenny's wedding that was supposed to take place at Quake II. How did the Dream Master's actions - I should say lack of - at the ceremony affect you? We know how it affected Jenny, but what's changed in your life? Shelly: At first it quite frankly shook me up because Dream Master was quite frankly a real close friend of mine. I look forward to seeing the wedding take place because of my relationship with both of the individuals involved. However, my love for Dream Master as a friend is no more. I look forward to taking away from him everything that means anything to him, starting with Freedom. WM: Shelly, just after that incident you and the rest of the Posse have rededicated yourselves to what the Posse does best; beating on people. There's rumours at the WCA headquarters that the Posse is headed for a round of fines or even another suspension, based on the attack on Mary Williams and earlier beatings on the Assassin and the Mistress of Death. What is your response? Shelly: I have noticed that the Annoying One has become a little less annoying since we decided to show up during her match against Janet Muniz, who competed as "R" at the time. The WCA should thank us for preventing this World Title from becoming a cheap two-bit belt. Since that match the Assassin has no longer been competing for the World Title and we believe that to be a good thing. Cowards should never be fortunate enough to wear this prestigious title. As far as the little number we did against the Mistress of Death - Paris was a wonderful venue and Jenny and I still talk about the events of that night. If you notice, it was just another mind game that the Posse chose to play on an opponent. And as far as that pathetic Mary Williams all she could talk about was how Jenny and I were distracted by what happened involving the Dream Master and Freedom. Mary Williams should have known better than not to interject her feeble mind into matters which quite frankly were of no concern to the members of the Henchmen! If the WCA wants to fine us for the matter, just make sure that those clowns tell Dr. Insane who to make out the fine to. If a suspension is in order, well I am sure that we'll fight it because we can. The members of the Posse have each been presented with a contract for a motion picture which will be shooting from the first of July to Aug. 15. If a suspension is enforced, well, let's just say that the ladies will have a little something to do!! WM: There's rumours that the WCA will be forming a union of wrestlers soon. You've made your feelings on the matter quite clear, but is there anything you wish to add? Shelly: If the TCA wants to go picket the WCA, that's their own misguided prerogative. The members of the Posse all believe the best way to earn better paying contracts here in this federation is to go out and wrestle hard each and every time we get the chance to do so. We don't expect anything to come to us.....we go out and get it!!! WM: Another situation that is a rather hot-button topic right now is Helena Knightsbridge's alleged steroid use. What do you think of her comments that the test was tampered with? Shelly: Oh remember Jenny and I have already conducted our own little investigation on that subject!! As far as we can tell, steroids were just some of the drugs that Helena took. I guess the WCA isn't concerned about the hardcore drugs!! Hey everybody assumes that the world's strongest woman, Christy Cooper, takes drugs but I can assure you that is absolutely not the case! WM: What attracted you and the other members of the Posse of Punishment to the WCA? Shelly: Tracy, Jackie (Freedom), Jenny and I had started the Posse while the three of us younger ones were still in high school. By that time Jackie had already changed over to being a good girl after her days of rebellion in high school on the east coast. What neither of the Turner girls would tell you is that their parents sent them to live with an aunt out on the West Coast because their parents couldn't stand them. Jackie desired to show her parents that she could be something by helping us get the Posse started. She was the one who introduced us to the Dream Master, who at the time was wrestling for a small independent federation at the time. His manager at the time was Dr. Insane, who had been managing under a different name at the time. Insane has always been a businessman who looks at what could be successful investments and he decided that he would start a wrestling school at the time. It was there where we met up with Vicki, who was a valet for the Dream Master at the time. We all had some skills but Insane felt that he could go out and make us better at what we do. So we spent a couple of years perfecting our craft. Jackie was the first one who had the opportunity to start wrestling, and that led to the animosity which still exists today between the Turner sisters. It was while we worked for a promotion as stage hands up in Canada that we were told that the WCA was looking for talent. Dr. Insane sent the Dream Master, who had already chosen to retire for reasons I won't go into, along with Tracy, Vicki, Jenny and I down to the WCA headquarters and auditioned for the Kessler, and he offered us contracts on the spot. Of course it always helps when the man discusses the numbers that were in that contract. WM: One last question; where are going now? You've become the World champion of what many say is the greatest women's wrestling league. Are there other mountains that you wish to conquer? Shelly: Well, Jenny and I sill would like to win the Tag Belts. I have given some thought to going out and bringing the belts from the lesser feds here to the WCA just to show who the best champ is! I can not be satisfied with what I have accomplished when I have so many years left in my career! "Henchmen of Hades: Hell Hath No Fury" WM: Ladies, I want to say congratulations on earning the coveted award of Tag Teams of the Month. Innocent Blood has made a particular impression, as they defeated the future Tag Team champions the Bounty Hunters at Quake II. The first obvious question is, are either of you going after the champions now that the belts are no longer vacant? Red: Of course we will. And what everybody in the WCA must understand is that it is written in that great book of life that the Tag Team titles are going to come to us. Whether it's the Psycho Crushers, whether it's Innocent Blood, I haven't looked, because that's irrelevant. Blood: We have a lot of work in the next few weeks - whether it's here in Angus or in the WCA, but that doesn't mean we're out of the race. We'll ask for a title shot when people will least expect it, and it doesn't matter who's champion at the time, the belts will come to us. WM: This award might not mean as much as it had in the past simply because April was a relatively quiet month for the tag teams in the WCA. What do you think is the cause of the lack of interest in tag wrestling? Blood: Take a look at the list of teams. You have 12 tag teams in total, 1 doesn't count because they won't fight anyone, and 7 are in what you call the big three. We're pretty much at war right now so we don't really have time for intra-stable matches. That cuts the number of possible matches a great deal. WM: What was your reaction to the announcement made by the Magic Mountain Warriors on March 20th regarding their decision to drop the titles? And what went through your minds when the MMW returned nearly a month later and asked for the belts back? Red: As far as we are concerned, they're the Magic Mountain Losers. Warriors don't run away from the bloodshed, twice, warriors don't abandon something and then try to whine their way back to it. Warriors fight. This is so ridiculous I don't think it even deserves our attention. We'll just clean their clocks when they swing by this way. Nadia: Basically, they've just given us one more reason to hate them. Not that we needed it. WM: Both teams in your stable are considered by many to be two of the best teams in the WCA. Who else in the tag ranks do you feel comes closest to you, both in attitude and in ability? Nadia: Hardcore. You guys haven't seen nothing yet. Back in OWL we were involved in one of the meanest feuds I've ever lived. And that wasn't their meanest feud. HOD's not bad either. The day Amber and Muniz will decide to cooperate as a unit, all teams will have to think for a second. But fortunately, that won't happen anytime soon. Red: Mark my words for all to read, the Bounty Hunters don't deserve those straps! They're just a bunch of bullies with baseball bats, and we're going to show them who's the toughest and the best in the WCA!!! WM: Now, the Henchmen seem to be back on track in defeating your sworn enemies, the Posse of Punishment. Why haven't you done what they have and added reinforcements to your stable? Blood: Too much hassle. My grandmother used to say "less dogs, less fleas". We're holding our own even though they are twice our size, we don't want to go down because of some internal problems. The day it will become an absolute necessity, maybe. But not before. WM: The Posse seem to have developed some chinks in their armor. They've lost the Can-Am belt, 'Psycho' Vicki V seems to have barely scraped past the Bodacious One Janet Muniz, and the members they've added this year - Hardcore, the Rising Sun, Valkyrie - seem to be more of a detriment to them than a benefit. Is the time right to really go after them with everything you've got? Mary: Half of what we've got should be enough. Vicki's shaky, Amber Jane has two close friends to nurse, Jenny is a mess... I have a date on May 12th against Shelly Rage, I can guarantee you she'll lose the world title before that. Blood: Internal problems. Internal problems will be their downfall. That's what happens when you get too greedy. WM: Mary, the Posse literally cost you the World title, prompting the Weapons-filled Cage Match rematch. Do you feel you can pin the World champion? Mary: Weapons-filled cage, we're not talking about pinning, we're talking annihilation. Walk around this town, ask anybody what happened to the poor souls who tried to punk me. They will all remember it for a long time. Shelly Rage, you tried to pull a fast one, but you played yourself. Punishment doesn't even begin to describe what I plan to do to you. WM: Turning to the third supergroup in the WCA, the Click II. Is it fair to say the Posse looks invincible next to this group, and how do you feel about them? Nadia: You're talking about the Posse falling down, they can't even get up. They're fine talents individually, but their egos are so big they will never be able to work as a team. We don't see them as a threat - not even close. WM: Now that the dust has settled in the 'Great Stable Rush' that occurred this time last month, how would you rate the results? For example, has it improved certain former singles wrestlers' careers, destroyed others, etc.? Red: They walk around, holding hands, singing in the rain and call themselves a stable. But when it comes down to it, they lack cohesion. The story of the Click II. Your world champion gets punked and you don't even have the decency to come to her aid. So being in a stable changed nothing to these four. Hardcore seem to do better, but I guess that's because Amber Jane is happy about spending time with the girl she has a crush on. Hey Amber, she's single again! I don't know a woman who lives and dies by her emotion more than Amber Jane. Anyway, that's all we can notice. Instead of having a few one-on-one feuds, we're call caught up in one big war, but that's all that changed. WM: How did the Henchmen of Hades get started? And why did you force Hardcore from the group? Blood: The Henchmen got started when Jonathan Schiz and Amber Jane wanted to flee Angus and take another fed by storm. So they summoned us to joined them. We quickly realised they had a good idea but would never have what it takes to make it work. So we decided to give them the boot and take care of business ourselves. Now Amber Jane and her slave joined the enemy out of sheer spite and Jonathan Schiz is running around this town claiming his support for the pedophiles of this world. It just goes to show that we were right in getting rid of them. They'll never amount to anything. WM: If you could do one thing over again in the WCA, what would it be? Blood: We should have never put up with Hardcore's bull for two months. Those girls are disgusting. We should have let them bring us here, then dispose of them like the trash that they are. Maybe we would be at the top by now. WM: I'd like to close this interview with a question regarding the topic of the day. You've already made your feelings known about the proposed union of WCA wrestlers. Is there anything you'd like to add? Red: Yes. TCA, if you want to go to Atlantic City, work for Donald Trump, wrestle a bunch of bimbos, fine. Goodbye, TCA, good riddance, this is the World Cyber-WRESTLING Association, so either get with the program or get the [expletive] out of Dodge.
