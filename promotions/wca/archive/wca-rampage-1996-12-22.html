WCA Rampage 12/21

22 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION RAMPAGE 12/21/96 Live from the Lake Arena in Springfield, Missouri -----Main Event----- "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln v. Flower Power (non-title match) Janet Strong v. Double D (Debbie Diamond) Nasty Tracy Turner v. Shelly Rage Nasty Tracy Turner v. Jenny Black Nasty Tracy Turner v. "Psycho" Vicki V Nasty Tracy Turner v. Christy Cooper Double J v. Sellyna Shriek ---------- Double J v. Sellyna Shriek (w/ Snakebite) ---------- Both women lock up, then Double J executes a front-layout suplex, taking control of the match. Shriek soon runs into the ropes. She counters a hiptoss with a backslide; Double J kicks out in two. She executes her own backslide; Shriek kicks out in two. She blocks a double underhook suplex; Double J attempts to counter an abdominal stretch with a hiptoss, but Shriek in turn counters with a backslide. Double J kicks out in two. Shriek rakes her face, then applies a double chickenwing, turning things around. Double J soon counters an abdominal stretch with a hiptoss. Shriek reverses a slingshot facebuster into a vertical suplex. Both women stand up; Double J punches Shriek, then again in the midsection. Shriek sidesteps a forearm to the back; Double J reverses a whip to the ropes. Shriek nails her with a clothesline; Double J blocks a double chickenwing. She gets up and takes Shriek down with a front-layout suplex, taking control of the match. Double J shortly counters a bodyslam with a small package; Shriek easily kicks out. She counters a crucifix with a Samoan Drop, going back on the attack. Double J very shortly blocks a ropeburn. She nails Shriek with a slingshot facebuster, then picks her up. Shriek blocks being set on a turnbuckle. She nails Double J with an elbowsmash. Double J hiptosses her. Shriek pops back up; Double J punches her in the stomach, then runs into the ropes. Shriek executes an armdrag takedown, then drops her knee into Double J's back, taking control of the match. She very shortly runs into the ropes; Double J follows her in and clotheslines her over the top rope. The ref keeps Double J in the ring as Shriek rolls back in. Double J covers her; one,, two, shoulder up. Double J climbs the turnbuckles, then comes down with a flying bulldog. She follows up with a fistdrop. Shriek absorbs the blow, then gets up and puts Double J in a double chickenwing, resetting the pace. Double J shortly blocks the Psychic Venom. She reverses a whip to a corner, then charges in and smashes into Shriek, regaining the advantage. After a grueling 5 minutes Shriek dodges a forearm to the back. She legsweeps Double J, then hammers her with a spinning elbowdrop, turning the match around. She shortly sends Double J to the ropes. Double J comes back with an elbow to the face. She executes a German suplex; Shriek easily kicks out. Double J keeps her down with neck-scissors, retaking control. Shriek shortly counters a crucifix with a Samoan Drop, then throws Double J out of the ring. She joins her on the floor as Snakebite distracts Double J. Shriek spins her around and slaps her. Double J blocks a bodyslam and reenters the ring. Shriek follows her in; Double J sends her to a corner. She charges in; Shriek lifts her legs up to block. She nails Double J with a double-axhandle chop, going back on the offensive. Double J shortly reverses a whip to the ropes. She nails Shriek with a kick, then sends Shriek back to the ropes. Both women get hit with a double clothesline. Double J recovers first and plants Shriek on the mat with a piledriver, resuming her assault. After 3 minutes Shriek blocks a powerslam with a lariat. She drops her knee into Double J's back, turning the match around again. Double J soon catches her in a belly-to-belly suplex; one, two, thr-shoulder up. Both women stand up; Double J nails Shriek with an elbowsmash, turning things back around. After 3 minutes Shriek counters a crucifix with a Samoan Drop. She puts Double J in a figure-four leglock, turning the match around. A minute later Double J blocks a Sammartino backbreaker. She nails Shriek with an elbowsmash, then picks her up and drops her into an over-the-shoulder stomachbreaker, going back on the attack. She soon puts Shriek in the Texas Cruncher; Shriek submits immediately. (26:43) <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.> <Fireworks explode as the camera shows a top-down view of the ring, highlighting the maroon WCA logo on the canvas. The camera descends, panning to the right and leveling off, showing the capacity crowd. It focuses on the end of the aisle, where 'WCA' frames the entrance to the locker room. Several WCA officials walk down the aisle.> *Posse of Punishment Gauntlet with Guest Referee the Dream Master* ---------- Nasty Tracy Turner v.. Jenny Black ---------- Both women lock up, then Black executes a dragon suplex. Turner easily kicks out; Black stands and runs into the ropes. Turner rocks her with an elbow, then takes her down with a jumping elbow thrust, going on the offensive. Black soon blocks a pickup with an elbowsmash. She throws Turner out of the ring. Turner reenters at the four count; Black sends her to the far ropes. She jumps a shoulderblock, then spins around and takes Turner down with a clothesline, turning things around. She shortly sends Turner into a corner, then charges in. Turner blocks her with a boot to the face, then hammers her with an avalanche, retaking control. After 4 minutes Turner attempts a sunset backslide, but Black fights and reverses it. Turner kicks out in two; both women stand up. Black sends Turner to the ropes, then takes her down with a slap, turning the match around. Turner shortly blocks a monkey flip. She executes a single-leg takedown, then nails Black with a leaping forearm, going back on the attack. She shortly sends Black to the ropes. Black comes back with neck-scissors. She throws Turner out of the ring. Turner reenters at the three count; Black takes her down with a monkey flip, turning things back around. She soon throws Black out of the ring and joins her on the floor. Turner shoves Black into the ringpost, then takes her down with spinning headscissors. She reenters the ring, followed by Black. Turner executes a leg lariat, going back on the attack. She soon attempts a high cross body, but Black catches her and drops her into a backbreaker. She hammers Turner with a spinning elbowdrop, turning things back around. Turner very shortly takes her down in a front-layout suplex, resuming her onslaught. She shortly takes Black down with a kick and goes for the pin. Black kicks out in two; Turner confronts the Dream Master about a slow count. Black seizes the opportunity to execute an inside cradle; Turner kicks out in two. Black puts Turner in a full nelson, resetting the pace of the match. Turner shortly throws Black out of the ring. She goes to the floor, then takes Black down with spinning headscissors. She throws Black back into the ring, then reenters via a springboard splash. Both women stand up. Turner executes a belly-to-back suplex, going on the offensive. After ninety seconds she decks Black with the Sweet Dreams and covers her; one, two, thr-kickout. Turner gets up and waits on Black, then takes her down with a swinging neckbreaker, resuming her assault. She shortly knocks Black down with a shoulderblock, then drops down and pins her. (14:19) ---------- Nasty Tracy Turner v. Shelly Rage ---------- Both women lock up, then Turner executes a brain buster, going on the attack. She shortly attempts a high cross body, but Rage catches her and drops her into a backbreaker, taking control. She very shortly runs into the ropes; Turner takes her down with a clothesline. She hammers Rage with a diving headbutt, resuming her attack. After ninety seconds she heads to the top rope. Rage goes to the corner and throws her to the mat, then climbs the turnbuckles and nails Turner with a flying clothesline. Turner blocks a dragon suplex; Rage counters a reverse cradle with a sit-down, pinning Turner. (3:22) ---------- Nasty Tracy Turner v. Christy Cooper ---------- Both women lock up, then Turner blocks a double chickenwing. Cooper blocks a swinging neckbreaker with a side suplex, then throws Turner out of the ring. Turner reenters at the five count; Cooper takes her down with a back suplex, taking control of the match. She soon sends Turner to the ropes. Turner misses with a shoulderblock, jumps a kick, misses with a clothesline, ducks an elbow, then nails Cooper with her own elbow to the face. Turner follows up with a reverse cradle; Cooper kicks out in two. Turner asks the Dream Master about the slow count, enabling Cooper to catch her in an inside cradle. Turner kicks out in two. Cooper heads to the top rope; Turner gets up and punches her in the stomach. Cooper falls onto the turnbuckle; Turner pulls her down by the hair, then covers her. Cooper kicks out in two and sits up. She gets back up, then reverses a whip to the ropes. She nails Turner with a lariat, going back on the attack. Turner very shortly ducks a diving shoulderblock, then applies a standing wristlock, going back on the attack. Cooper shortly reverses a whip to the ropes, then decks Turner with a rolling kick, retaking control. After 2 minutes Cooper sends Turner to a corner. She charges in shoulder-first; Turner blocks her with a knee. Cooper blocks a standing wristlock; Turner backdrops out of a DDT. Cooper sits up; Turner pulls her up, then sends her to a corner. She charges in and gets a boot to the face. Cooper sends Turner to the ropes, then nails her with a kick, regaining her momentum. Turner shortly ducks a lariat, then executes a belly-to-back suplex, turning the match around. Cooper shortly nails her with a double-axhandle chop, retaking control. She shortly puts Turner in a bow-and-arrow; Turner submits immediately. (8:38) ---------- Nasty Tracy Turner v. "Psycho" Vicki V ---------- Both women lock up, then Vicki powerslams Turner, going on the offensive. After 2 minutes she caps it with a tiger driver, pinning Turner. (2:02) ---------- Janet Strong (w/ Angel Hunter) v. Double D (Debbie Diamond) (w/ Donna Star and Tammi Cooper) ---------- Both women lock up, then Strong executes a short lariat and covers Diamond; one, two, kickout. Both women stand, then Strong executes an enzuigiri, taking control of the match. Diamond soon blocks another short lariat. Strong blocks a reverse neckbreaker; Diamond ducks a jumping side kick. She executes spinning headscissors into a cradle; Strong kicks out in two. Diamond complains about a slow count as Strong gets up. Strong reverses a whip to the ropes; Diamond ducks a savate kick and executes a legsweep faceslam, taking control of the match. After 2 minutes Strong catches her with a short lariat, turning the match around. A minute later Diamond throws Strong out of the ring, then joins her on the floor. She punches Strong twice, then reenters the ring. Strong rolls back in, then rolls away from a stomp and stands up. She nails Diamond with a knee to the back, then blocks a back suplex. She executes a belly-to-back suplex, retaking control of the match. Diamond very shortly takes her down with a reverse neckbreaker, going back on the attack. A minute later she throws Strong out of the ring and joins her on the floor. She knocks Strong into the ringsteps, then nails her with a backspin DDT. Strong slowly gets up. She ducks the Sabot kick, then legsweeps Diamond. Diamond gets back on her feet; Strong kicks her in the midsection, then follows up with an enzuigiri. She rolls back in the ring, followed by Diamond. Strong puts her in a grapevine, keeping the pressure on. After a minute she bites Diamond's face. Diamond falls to her knees and begs off; Strong runs into the ropes. Diamond nails her with a shoulderblock, then follows up with a European uppercut, taking control of the match. After a minute Strong blocks a Sammartino backbreaker. She executes a back suplex, turning things around. After 2 minutes Diamond blocks a belly-to-back suplex with an elbowsmash. She takes Strong down with a European uppercut, then applies short-arm scissors, turning the match around. After another 2 minutes Strong throws Diamond out of the ring, then joins her on the floor. She legsweeps Diamond, then knocks her into the ringsteps. Diamond responds by shoving Strong into the guardrail. She grabs Strong's head and slams her face onto the steel. Strong escapes to the ring. Diamond slides back in, then stands up. Strong nails her with a swinging punch, taking charge. After 2 minutes Diamond catches Strong in a back suplex, turning the match around. Strong shortly ducks a kick to the head; Diamond begs off. Strong takes her down in a belly-to-back suplex, retaking control. After 3 minutes she catches Diamond in a belly-to-belly suplex, pinning her. (21:03) ===== Birmingham: "Janet Strong avenges her cousin's loss to Double D, giving her all in a highly-contested match. Strong has a shot at the World title this Monday; judging on her performances tonight and last night, she'll give Amber Axeln a run for her money. Speaking of the Intimidator, she's in our main event tonight, taking on Flower Power in a non-title match. Both of these women have been through some hard times very recently, with Axeln losing the Television title to Christy Cooper and Flower Power in a bit of a slump before her win Friday. We'll bring you our main event after this time-out." --------------------COMMERCIAL BREAK-------------------- Ring Announcer: "Ladies and gentlemen, tonight's main event is a non-title match set for one fall! Introducing first: representing the Posse of Punishment and accompanied to the ring by the Dream Master and Dr. Insane, from Washington, D.C., weighing 145 pounds, Flower Power!" <Power leads the Dream Master and Dr. Insane down the aisle, ignoring the booing fans. They enter the ring. Power raises a flower; the audience boos louder.> "Her opponent and World champion: accompanied to the ring by her tag team partner Valkyrie, from Richmond, Virginia, weighing in at 125 pounds, 'the Intimidator' Amber Axeln!" <New Dawn walks down the aisle, slapping the cheering fans' hands. They enter the ring.> Birmingham's voice: "Amber shows Power and the crowd the World title, then hands it to Valkyrie, who wishes her partner luck and leaves the ring. Power glares at Axeln. The Dream Master and Dr. Insane leave the ring. Referee Bob Jordan calls for the bell, then both wrestlers lock up! Axeln slaps Power, then takes her down with neck-scissors. Both women stand, then Axeln nails Power with an enzuilariato. She follows up with an enzuigiri, then pulls Power into a corner. Axeln gets up on the second turnbuckle - and Power backdrops out of the Doombomb! She throws Amber out of the ring, then goes through the ropes to the floor. Axeln grabs Power by the head, then slams her face onto the ringsteps - Flower Power is busted open! She escapes to the ring; Axeln follows her in. Power rakes Amber's face, then - Axeln ducks a flying shoulderblock. Power gets back up and reverses a whip to the ropes. She knocks Axeln down with a shoulderblock, then goes for the pin. "Axeln kicks out in two. Power confronts Jordan about a slow co - Amber rolls her up! One, two, kickout. Power gets up and punishes the Intimidator with a chokehold! One, two, three, Power lets go. She rocks Amber with a European uppercut, then takes her down via a gutwrench suplex. Axeln rolls away from a kneedrop! Flower Power avoids a forearm to the back. She heads to the top rope, then comes down with a flying clothesline. Power returns to the turnbuckles, then keeps Amber down with a flying bulldog. She raises a flower up - and the crowd doesn't appreciate the sentiment! Axeln sits up. She gets back on her feet, shaking her head to clear it. "Axeln reverses a whip to a corner. Power rebounds off the turnbuckles! Axeln takes her down with a lariat. Power gets up and scoops Axeln up, then drops her into an over-the-shoulder stomachbreaker. She runs into the ropes, jumps a rising Axeln, rebounds, misses with a shoulderblock, rebounds - and runs into a kick! Axeln puts her in a front facelock. It's rather interesting that Nasty Tracy Turner only won one match in the Posse gauntlet; I guess the beating she took from Christy Cooper really softened her up for the kill. Axeln lets go - and Power dodges a kneelift! Amber sits up, then gets up - and nails Power with another kneelift! She follows up with a back heel kick, then kicks Power in the head. Power falls to the mat, ironically ducking another kick to the head! Axeln steps back, then runs into the ropes. She jumps Power, rebounds - and runs into a kick! "Power raises a flower to Axeln as the latter gets back up, then runs into the ropes. Axeln executes a headlock takedown, then runs into the ropes. Power stops her with a clothesline, then sends her to a corner. She charges in - and nails the turnbuckles with a Stinger Splash, as the Intimidator moves out of the way! Axeln sends Power to the ropes. Power recovers enough to duck a clothesline, then jumps a kick, then returns with a kick. Axeln blocks a bulldog and puts Power in an abdominal stretch. Power struggles for the ropes - nope, too far. Power gathers herself, then hiptosses Axeln. Axeln gets up and dumps Power out of the ring. She goes to the floor. "Axeln nails Power with a back heel kick. Power staggers back, then executes an enzuilariato. Axeln responds with a forearm to the back, then throws Power back into the ring. She reenters - an idea strikes her! She throws Power back out of the ring, then joins her on the floor. She decks Power with a back heel kick. Dr. Insane distracts her - Power gets up and shoves Amber into the guardrail. She nails Axeln with a spinning leg lariat. Power slams Axeln's head onto the concrete floor, then reenters the ring. Axeln picks herself up rather slowly. She reenters the ring at the four count. Power executes a high cr - the Intimidator catches her! She drops Power into a backbreaker. "Axeln puts her in a half Boston. The Intimidator has a busy schedule for the first part of our tour of Latin America. She'll be facing Janet Strong tomorrow night, then 'Destroyer' Dara Holt on Saturday, Big Norma Hudson next Monday, New Dawn faces the Vinnie Babes on the 4th, Axeln meets Semiramis on the 6th - looks like she'll be busy! Axeln lets go. Power slowly gets up - Axeln puts her in an abdominal stretch. The Intimidator has seemed to be haunted by Muniz' turn on her. In retrospect, I have to agree with many of the other wrestlers who pointed out that the 'R' character appeared too suddenly to be much of a coincidence. Axeln lets go. Power blocks a side suplex. Axeln shrugs off a bulldog; Power turns around and chokes her! One, two, three, Power sends Axeln to the ropes and follows her in - then chokes her against the top rope! One, two, three, she lets go. Oooh, Amber's mad now! Power raises her hands up, begging off. She sends Axeln to the ropes, then nails her with a shoulderblock and runs into the ropes. Axeln stops her with an elbow to the face, then runs into he ropes. "She takes Power down with a headlock. Power rolls to her knees and begs off again. Axeln kicks her squarely in the face, knocking Power back! She turns Power over, then pulls her into a corner. The Intimidator flashes the V-sign, then executes the Doombomb! Axeln drops down and covers; one, two, three!" RA: "Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the match: in 8 minutes, 13 seconds, 'the Intimidator' Amber Axeln!" <Valkyrie enters the ring and hands Axeln her belt, then both women raise their arms up in celebration. Power rolls out of the ring and heads back to the locker room as a figure dressed in a black bodysuit with a white 'R' on one thigh and black mask comes out of the stands.> Birmingham's voice: "It's 'R' again! Wait, she's too heavy to be Muniz! Whoever she is, she snatches the World title - then nails Valkyrie with it! Axeln punches her, then 'R' nails Axeln with a kick. Valkyrie pulls the mask off - it's Lindsay Clearwater! Muniz's secretary! Clearwater leaves the ring and grabs the mic!" Clearwater: "The Bodacious One gives her regards, Amber!" <She drops the mic and leaves down the aisle. Axeln and Valkyrie give chase, heading into the locker room.> Birmingham: "The Bodacious One continues with her mind games, keeping Axeln off-balance just as Axeln defeats Flower Power in a non-title match. Fans, we are out of time. Coming up this Monday on Monday Knights, live from Mexico City, we'll see Flower Power take on the Mistress of Death for the Can-Am title, Axeln versus Janet Strong for the World title, Thunder and Lightning against the Scandalous Ones, New Dawn meet Joanne Watson's Hired Help, Nasty Tracy Turner goes up against former fan favorite Semiramis, the Psycho Crushers make their debut against the Delicious Dolls, and 'Dominatress' Kathy Lynn makes her singles debut against 'Psycho' Vicki V in a submission match. I'm John Birmingham; have a great evening!" <Closing credits appear.>
