WCA Rampage 11/30

1 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION RAMPAGE From Budapest, Hungary 11/30/96 -----Main Event----- "Psycho" Vicki V / Christy Cooper v. The Scandalous Ones (Guest Referee Nasty Tracy Turner) Janet Strong v. Joanne Watson Thunder and Lightning v. The Vinnie Babes Donna Star v. Angel Hunter Helena "High Kick" Knightsbridge v. Flower Power <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.> <Fireworks explode as the camera shows a top-down view of the ring, highlighting the maroon WCA logo on the canvas. The camera descends, panning to the right and leveling off, showing the capacity crowd. It focuses on the end of the aisle, where 'WCA' frames the entrance to the locker room. Several WCA officials walk down the aisle.> ---------- Helena "High Kick" Knightsbridge v. Flower Power ---------- Both women lock up, then Knightsbridge bodyslams Power, taking control of the match. Power soon blocks a German suplex with an elbowsmash, then nails her with a flying karate chop, turning things around. She soon runs into the ropes. Knightsbridge takes her down with a clothesline, then puts her in a figure four leglock, going back on the attack. A minute later she runs into the ropes. Power catches her in a chokehold. She lets go in four and runs into the ropes. She decks Knightsbridge with a leaping clothesline, retaking control. A minute later Knightsbridge hammers her with a belly-to-back suplex. She applies a leg hammerlock, retaking control. After ninety seconds Power blocks an armbar. She sends Knightsbridge to the ropes, then stops her with a kick. She executes a high cross body; one, two, kickout. Both women stand up. Knightsbridge blocks a spinning leg lariat. Power blocks a back suplex and sends Knightsbridge to the ropes. She executes a Guerrilla Press, turning things around. Knightsbridge soon nails her with an elbowsmash and runs into the ropes. She nails Power with a kick, then sends her to the ropes. She knocks her down with a shoulderblock, then puts her in a leg hammerlock, turning things back around. After 4 minutes Power blocks an armbar and chokes Knightsbridge. She lets go in three; Knightsbridge reverses a whip to the ropes and misses with an elbow. Power rebounds again and knocks Knightsbridge down with an elbow, gaining the advantage. After a minute Knightsbridge rolls away from an elbowdrop. Both women stand up; Power executes a backslide. Knightsbridge kicks out in two; Power chokes her against the ropes. She lets go in four and rails at the ref, then chokes Knightsbridge again. The ref reaches five and disqualifies her. Power hops down to the floor, then grabs a chair. She reenters the ring with it as the ref raises Knightbridge's arm. Power nails her in the face with the chair, then decks her with the Incense and Peppermints. The ref sends her out of the ring. (13:58) ---------- Donna Star (w/ Double D - Debbie Diamond and Tammi Cooper) v. Angel Hunter ---------- Both women lock up, then Star sends Hunter to the ropes. Hunter comes back with a jumping side kick, taking control of the match. Star very shortly takes charge with an enzuilariato. After 2 1/2 minutes Hunter counters a takeover suplex with a belly-to-belly suplex. Star gets her shoulder up in two and gets up as Hunter gets the crowd to cheer. She turns around and nails Star with a drop-kick to the knee, turning the match around. Star soon decks her with a back heel kick, retaking control of the match. After 5 intense minutes Star applies an armlock leglock. Hunter breaks the hold in 16 seconds, then catches Star in a belly-to-back suplex. She puts Star in the Rack; Star hangs on. Hunter lets her go after 23 seconds and executes an enzuigiri, turning the match around. After a minute Star blocks a full nelson. Both women stand up; Hunter blocks a bodyslam. Star sidesteps a drop-kick, then puts Hunter in a leg hammerlock, going back on the offensive. After 3 1/2 minutes Hunter reverses a whip to the ropes. She misses with a kick, then executes a headlock takedown. Hunter keeps Star down with a jumping side kick, turning the match back around. She shortly sends Star to the ropes. Star misses with a shoulderblock, then follows Hunter in and nails her with a knee to the back. She sends Hunter to the ropes; Cooper trips Hunter up. The ref warns her; Cooper argues with him. The ref, agitated, disqualifies Star. (14:55) ---------- Thunder and Lightning (Haley "Comet" Hanson and Big Norma Hudson) (w/ Mr. Canuck) v. The Vinnie Babes (Sarah Bryant and the Vinnie Babe) (w/ Vinnie Gambino) ---------- Hanson and the Vinnie Babe lock up. Hanson executes a tiger suplex; one, two, shoulder up. Hanson heads to the top rope, then nails the Vinnie Babe with a flying somersault headbutt, going on the offensive. The Vinnie Babe soon tags in Bryant. Hanson avoids her spinning mule kick; the Vinnie Babe leaves the ring as Hanson executes a tiger driver. Bryant kicks out in two; Hanson tags in Hudson. They nail Bryant with a double lariat. Hanson leaves the ring as Bryant rolls away from a kneeling headbutt. Hudson blocks a scorpion deathlock and gets back on her feet. She applies a clawhold, taking control of the match. She shortly tags out to Hanson. Hanson runs into the ropes, then nails Bryant with a clothesline. Bryant ducks a spinning mule kick, then hits Hanson with a forearm smash. Hanson quickly tags out to Hudson. Hudson backdrops Bryant, then keeps her down with an earringer, retaking control. She soon tags in Hanson. The ref cuts off the Vinnie Babe as they send Bryant to the ropes. Bryant ducks a double elbowsmash, rebounds again, and nails both women with a double clothesline. She throws Hudson out; Hanson reverses a whip to the ropes. She jumps a kick, then ducks an enzuilariato. Hanson tags out to Hudson; Hudson nails Bryant with a kneebreaker, taking control of the match. She soon tags out to Hanson. Bryant executes a snap suplex, then applies an armlock leglock, turning the match around. Hanson shortly blocks a roundhouse punch and tags out to Hudson. Hudson executes a lariat, taking charge. She shortly sends Bryant into the ropes; Bryant sneaks a tag in, then she hits Hudson with a clothesline. Both Babes rock Hudson with a double roundhouse punch. Hudson ducks Bryant's thrust kick to the head, then blocks the Vinnie Babe's facerake. Hudson runs into the ropes, then nails the Vinnie Babe with a lariat, retaking control. She soon runs into the ropes. The Vinnie Babe stops Hudson with a kick, then tags out to Bryant. Both women take Hudson down with a double short clothesline, then Bryant applies a front facelock, turning the match back around. Hudson shortly ducks an enzuilariato and tags in Hanson. Both women send Bryant to the ropes, then double her over with a double kick to the midsection. Hanson follows up with a fisherman suplex, taking control of the match. After a minute Bryant blocks a standing wristlock and executes an enzuilariato, turning things around. Hanson shortly blocks a forearm smash and tags out to Hudson. The Vinnie Babe enters the ring and throws Hanson out, then she and Bryant send Hudson to the ropes and nail her with a double fist to the midsection. Bryant follows up with a standing wristlock, resuming her assault. Hudson shortly blocks a roundhouse punch and tags out to Hanson. Hanson executes a lariat, then follows up with a short lariat and pins Bryant. (11:55) ===== Birmingham: "Haley 'Comet' Hanson gets the pinfall for Thunder and Lightning in a virtual squash over newcomers the Vinnie Babes. This is a major setback for the Babes, as these first few matches in the WCA are the most important as far as rankings are concerned. In our next match, Janet Strong takes on Joanne Watson. Both women are vying on that same ladder to the top. Janet Strong's been on a winning streak as of late; Watson wants to end it, preferably in a vicious manner. Let's take you to these pre-recorded comments from Janet Strong." Janet: Well Joanne Watson, So you think you will beat me, well think again, and again!! I am on a mission, and if anyone gets in the way, it's going to be too bad for them!! I have my eyes set on a belt, and nothing, and nobody is going to get in my way, ...nobody! Posse, get ready for the beating of your poor, pathetic lives!! We are comming for you, and there won't be anything left when we are done!!!! Ring Announcer: "Ladies and gentlemen, the following match is set for one fall. Introducing first: accompanied to the ring by her Hired Help, from Los Angeles, California, weighing in at 135 pounds, Joanne Watson!" <Watson, Samantha Jones, and Jane Gordon walk down the aisles, ignoring the fans. Watson carries a golf club. They enter the ring.> "Her opponent: from Seattle, Washington, weighing in at 175 pounds, Janet Strong!" <Strong walks down the aisle, smiling at the cheering fans. She enters the ring.> Birmingham's voice: "Watson gives Gordon her golf club, then sends them both out of the ring. Senior referee Mark White checks both women, then signals the bell. Both women lock up! Strong blocks a ropeburn. She bites Watson's face; Watson counters a back suplex with a cross-body block. One, two, shoulder up. Both women stand up; Watson kicks Strong in the abdomen. She follows up with a lariat, then heads to the top rope. Watson comes down with a flying elbowdrop. Strong rolls over, then slowly stands up. Watson takes her down with a neck snap - Strong blocks an armlock leglock. She gets up and throws Watson out of the ring, then joins her on the floor. She punches Watson. Gordon distracts Strong, allowing Watson to throw her into the ring. "Watson follows her in as Strong runs into the ropes. Watson jumps a kick; Strong catches her with a savate kick. She follows up with an enzuigiri. Watson gets back on her feet; Strong executes a headlock takedown. Watson picks herself up more slowly. Strong runs into the ropes; Watson ducks a jumping side kick. She throws Strong out of the ring! White begins the count; one, two, three, four, Strong reenters the ring. She blocks a neck snap and applies a front facelock. Strong's recent record is amazing. She was winning a lot of matches back in October, then began a losing streak a few weeks ago. Now she's back on a winning streak. Strong lets Watson go and runs into the ropes. Watson stops her with a chokehold! White stops pleading and counts; one, two, three, Watson grabs Strong's head and pulls her over to the ropes, then runs the face along the top rope. "She puts Strong in an armbar; Strong quickly breaks the hold. She takes Watson down with an earringer, the applies her own armbar. Jones and Gordon encourage Watson; I wonder if they're paid to do that? Watson's arrogance may get the best of her yet. Strong lets go - Watson quickly executes a double underhook suplex! Strong gets up; Watson sends her to the ropes. She rocks Janet with an elbow, then rakes her face. She applies an armlock hammerlock; Strong quickly breaks the hold. Watson sends her to the ropes. Strong misses with an elbow, rebounds again, and knocks Watson down with a shoulderblock. She applies an armlock leglock. Watson's too close to the ropes! She uses them to break the hold as we go to commercial; stay tuned!" --------------------COMMERCIAL BREAK-------------------- Birmingham's voice: "We're back! During the break Strong tried to keep her advantage, but Watson slowly turned the tides. Going back to live action, Strong blocks a kick to the midsection. Watson ducks a kick to the head and heads to the top rope. Strong punches her in the stomach; Watson falls onto the turnbuckle! She leans forward and falls back into the ring! "Strong puts her in an armlock leglock. Watson struggles for the rope - and Gordon pushes it in so she can grab it! C'mon, keep it fair! Both women stand up. Watson ducks a jumping side kick. Strong blocks a bomb and executes a belly-to-belly; one, two, Gordon pulls White out of the ring! Boy, is he mad! He reenters the ring and calls for the bell; he's gonna disqualify Joanne!" RA: "Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the match as a result of a disqualification: in 7 minutes, 42 seconds, Janet Strong!" <Jones and Gordon enter the ring. Strong raises her arms in victory, then leaves the ring. Watson and Hired Help raise their arms as if they won, prompting a chorus of boos.> Birmingham: "Joanne Watson gets disqualified due to outside interference. No doubt there's gonna be some repercussions from this! Up next is our main event. On the last Theatre of War, Sweet Sue and Karen Huffman set up a special one-on-one match to help prepare for their tag bout at No Sanctuary. Not to be outdone, the Posse of Punishment have set up a match between the newly created team of 'Psycho' Vicki V / Christy Cooper and the Scandalous Ones, with Nasty Tracy Turner as the special guest referee. So, without further ado, let's go to the ring." RA: "Ladies and gentlemen, tonight's main event is a special intra-stable tag team match! Introducing the Guest Referee: followed by Dr. Insane and the Dream Master, Nasty Tracy Turner!" <Turner walks down the aisle in a WCA referee's uniform, followed by Dr. Insane and the Dream Master. Turner enters the ring.> "Introducing first: with a combined weight of 270 pounds, the Scandalous Ones!" <Shelly Rage and Jenny Black walk down the aisle. They enter the ring.> "Their opponents: with a combined weight of 428 pounds, 'Psycho' Vicki V and Christy Cooper!" <Vicki and Cooper walk down the aisle, then enter the ring.> "Rage bows out of the ring. Vicki and Cooper glance at each other, then Vicki leaves the ring. The bell rings, and both women lock up! Cooper jabs Black; Black executes a springboard drop-kick. She follows up with a bulldog! Cooper stands up; Black sends her to the ropes. Cooper comes back with a kick and runs into the ropes. Black decks her with a spin kick. Cooper gets up more slowly. Rage enters the ring; she and Black send Cooper to the ropes, then nail her with a double elbowsmash. Both women double-kick her in the midsection, then Rage leaves as Black executes another bulldog. She climbs the turnbuckles as Cooper gets up, then knocks her down with a flying shoulderblock. Black runs into the ropes, then keeps Cooper down with a diving shoulderblock. "Cooper picks herself up and applies an armbar. Black quickly breaks the hold; Christy tags in V. They double-drop-kick Black. Here comes Rage! She throws Christy out, then leaves as Black catches V in a Doctor bomb. She covers; one, two, kickout. Black puts V in a full nelson. The Scandalous Ones seem to be working together as a team more than in the past; maybe it's just me. Black lets go and plants V with another Doctor bomb. She covers; one, two, kickout. Black heads to the top rope, then hammers V with a flying somersault splash; one, two, kickout. "Both women stand up. Black runs into the ropes, then nails Vicki with a kick. She sends Vicki to a corner, then charges in - into a boot! Black reverses a whip to the ropes, then nails V with a bulldog. She gets back up, then runs into the ropes. Vicki backdrops her! She waits on Black, then keeps her down with a leaping clothesline. Black gets back up; V catches her in a powerslam, but only gets a two count. V pulls Black up - Black backdrops out of a double underhook something! V gets up and reverses a whip to the ropes. "Black counters a powerslam with a lariat, then tags out to Rage. Rage executes a double leg cradle; one, two, kickout. Rage complains about a slow count, distracting the ref so Black can enter - Cooper enters the ring and knocks Jenny back out! Rage blocks her DDT with a backdrop; Cooper leaves as Rage blocks V's back heel kick. She executes a dragon suplex and covers; one, two, kickout. V throws Rage out of the ring! Turner checks her former tag partner, then begins the count; one, two, three, four, five, Rage reenters the ring. "V executes a - Rage reverses the move into a vertical suplex! She hammers Vicki with an avalanche as Black enters the ring - Cooper enters and throws her out, then she and V take Rage down with a double drop-kick. Cooper leaves the ring as Shelly gets back on her feet. V punches her, then hammers her in a front slam. She keeps Rage down with a drop-kick, then covers her; one, shoulder up. Rage gets up and executes an enzuigiri. V pops back up and drop-kicks her. She follows up with her own enzuigiri. Rage gets up; she reverses a whip to the ropes. "V ducks a clothesline and rebounds; Rage blocks a facerake. V nails her with a bulldog, then runs into the ropes. She jumps a rising Rage, rebounds, ducks a clothesline, rebounds, and staggers Rage with a shoulderblock. She sends Shelly to the ropes; Rage comes back with a clothesline! V stumbles back - and falls out of the ring! Rage goes through the ropes and hops down onto the floor. V knocks her into the guardrail, then reenters the ring. Fans, we're out of time, but we'll keep the tape rolling and present the end of this match to you on Monday Knights. Have a great evening!" <Closing credits appear.>
