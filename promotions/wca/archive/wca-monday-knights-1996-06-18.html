WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION MONDAY KNIGHTS Live from the Lake Arena in Springfield, Missouri 6/17/96 Purple Hayes makes his ring debut tonight against Man Mountain Mo. In the WCA, *vacant* titles can be won by disqualification or countouts. -----Main Event----- <World Tag Team Titles Tournament First Round Match / North Ameircan Tag Team Titles Match> Hardcore Heaven v. Sue Upton Man Mountain Mo v. Purple Hayes ------------------------- Man Mountain Mo (MGR: Fred Holland) v. Purple Hayes (w/ Quicksilver) ------------------------- Purple Hayes runs into the ropes, then takes Mo down with a FrankenSteiner. He covers the big man, but Mo kicks out in two. Hayes goes on the offensive with a kick. Mo shortly counters a jack-knife powerbomb with a backdrop and turns the match around with a shoulderblock. He shortly runs into the ropes, but Hayes kicks him and turns the match back around with a knee to the back. He shortly applies a sleeper, but Mo after 13 seconds falls backward into the turnbuckles, breaking the hold. He takes Hayes out of the corner with a double underhook backbreaker, retaking control of the match. Hayes quickly blocks a roundhouse punch, eveing the match up. Both men stand toe-to-toe with each other until Holland shortly throws Mo a cheese grater. Hayes intercepts it and punches Mo with it, cutting the big man's face and sending him to the mat. Hayes drops the grater and goes for the pin; Holland puts Mo's foot on the bottom rope at the two count. Hayes goes for an elbowdrop, but Mo rolls out of the ring, then reenters at the nine count. He stands toe-to-toe with Hayes; Holland shortly scuttles into the ring and retireves the grater. He hands it to Mo on the way out; Mo tires to use it, but Hayes knocks it out of his hands and applies a sleeperhold. Mo goes under after 15 seconds. (5:04) ------------------------- Sweet Sue (MGR: Tall Troll) v. Sue Upton (MGR: Fred Scuttle) ------------------------- Upton whips Sue into the ropes, then nails her with a rolling kick, taking control of the match. She soon tries to whip Sue into a turnbuckle, but Sue reverses it and charges in shoulder-first. She hammers Upton, then throws her out of the ring. Upton reenters at the three count; Sue greets her with neck-scissors, taking control of the match. She shortly goes for an elbowdrop, but Upton rolls out of the way and applies a surfboard, retaking control of the match. Sue quickly turns the match back around with a choke against the ropes. The ref begins counting; he reaches five and begins to warn Sue, but she lets go before he can say anything and takes Upton down with a side suplex, continuing the match. After five minutes she runs into the ropes, but Upton catches her in a Doctor Bomb and covers her. Sue kicks out in two. Upton tries to send her into a corner, but Sue reverses it and charges in. Uptom moves out of the way, then applies a full nelson. Sue quickly retakes control of the match with an elbowsmash. After 90 seconds Upton evens the match up with a legsweep. Both women stand toe-to-toe with each other, then Sue shortly retakes control of the match with a European uppercut. She shortly nails Upton with the Jersey Jaw Jacker and pins her. (12:41) ------------------------- DA MOIDERER (MGR: Jayesh Chatlani) v. Johnny Vicious (MGR: Tall Troll) ------------------------- DA MOIDERER goes for a piledriver, but Vicious backdrops him, then waits for him to get to his feet. Vicious punches DA MOIDERER in the back of the head, sending him crashing to the mat and going on the offensive. He shortly throws DA MOIDERER to the floor and joins him there. Vicious shoves DA MOIDERER into the ringsteps, then slams his face into the ringpost. Troll comes up behind DA MOIDERER, distracting him long enough for Vicious to thrust kick him in the lower back. Vicious throws DA MOIDERER back into the ring, then takes him down with a tiger driver and goes for the pin. DA MOIDERER gets his shoulder up at the last second; Vicious continues his offensive with a bodyslam. DA MOIDERER shortly ends his assault with a backdrop. Troll tries to interfere, but Chatlani stops him. DA MOIDERER puts Vicious on the turnbuckle, then takes him down with a belly-to-belly superplex. He covers Vicious, but can only get a two count. Vicious gets back up and stands toe-to-toe with DA MOIDERER. He shortly takes control of the match with a snap suplex. DA MOIDERER shortly turns the match back around with a thrust kick to the head. Vicious begins to make a comeback after almost two minutes. DA MOIDERER soon goes for a bulldog, but Vicious throws him off and takes him down with a short clothesline. DA MOIDERER gets back up and punches him, then the two men begin brawling in the middle of the ring. Vicious shortly ends it with a full nelson, taking control of the match. DA MOIDERER almost immediately begins making a comeback, turning the match around almost a minute later ith a short clothesline. He shortly backdrops Vicious out of the ring, then goes after him. Vicious grabs DA MOIDERER by the ears and pulls him over to a corner of the ring. He powerslams DA MOIDERER on the ringsteps, then rolls him back into the ring. Vicious enters and hammers him with a gutwrench suplex, retaking control of the match. After a minute he kicks DA MOIDERER in the midsection, then hammers him with a flying cross body press. He turns it into a cover, but DA MOIDERER kicks out at the last second. Vicious whips him into the ropes, then hammers him with the Surfer Slam and pins him. (12:37) *Cage Match* ------------------------- "The Blade" Larry Brown v. Loxian (MGR: Harvey "the Mouth" Lippman) ------------------------- Brown assumes control of the match with a forearm to the back. Loxian quickly evens the match up with a flying clothesline. He calls for the door, but Brown pulls him back in before he can goe through the ropes. Loxian punishes him with forearm smash. Both men trade blows. Brown whips Loxian into the ropes; Loxian rebounds and rocks him with an elbow. Loxian runs into the opposing ropes, then Brown hits him with his own elbow. The stand-off continues until Brown nails Loxian with a thrust kick to the head. He calls for the door, but Loxian pulls him back in and takes control of the match with a clothesline. He shortly tries to climb out of the cage; he gets to the top rope before Brown grabs him and pulls him back in. Brown hammers him with an avalanche, then goes for an earringer as Loxian stands up. Loxian counters with a backdrop and calls for the door. He gets thourgh the ropes and into the doorway before Brown pulls him back in. He goes for a figure-four sleeper, but Loxian blocks it and retakes control of the match with a short clothesline. He shortly nails Brown with a butt-bump and pins him. (4:38) ------------------------- Delicious Dolls (Double D (Debbie Diamond) and Donna Star) v. Deeply Exotic (Deep Rest and Exotica) ------------------------- Diamond and Rest start. Afther they're introduction, Diamond and Star whip Rest into the ropes, then nail her with a double kick to the midsection. Star leaves the ring as Diamond goes on the offensive with a brain buster. She shortly goes for a kick to the thigh, but Rest blocks it. Star enters the ring and decks Rest with a savate kick, then leaves the ring as Diamond stops Rest from standing up with a lariat. Rest shortly after runs into the ropes, then nails Diamond with a choke slam, turning the match around. Diamond quickly evens te match up and stands toe-to-toe with her. Star soon enters the ring and bodyslams Rest, then Diamond follows up with a falling splash. She covers Rest, but can only get a two count. Exotica enters the ring and throws Star out, then tries to spin-kick Diamond. Diamond ducks it, then nails Rest with an ipponzei and takes control as Exotica leaves the ring. She shortly clotheslines Rest to the floor, then joins her. She begins chasing Rest around the ring; Rest goes around a corner, then rolls back in. Diamond follows suit; Rest greets her with a tiger suplex. She covers Diamond, but the latter kicks out in two. Rest tags out to Exotica, then DDT's Diamond. Exotica tries to follow up with a flying somersault splash, but Diamond rolls out of the way and nails her with a flying elbowdrop, regaining her momentum. After two minutes she runs into the ropes. Exotica backdrops her off the rebound, sending her falling to the floor. She reenters the ring, Exotica nails her with a spin kick, turning the match arond. She shortly whips Diamond into the ropes; Daimond clotheslines her on the rebound and tags out to Star. They whip Exotica into the ropes, then double kick her in the midsection, retaking control of the match. She shortly catches Exotica in a small package. Exotica kicks out in two and gets to her feet. She runs into the ropes, then nails Star with a kick, turning the match back around. Star quickly retakes control of the match with fist to the midsection. After two minutes she goes for a bodyslam, but Exotica counters with a small package. Star kicks out in two; Exotica nails her with a kneedrop. She attempts to put Star on a turnbuckle, but Star blocks it and tags out to Diamond. They nail Star with a double kneelift, then Star kicks her in the head and leaves the ring while Diamond takes control of the match with a double chickenwing. She shortly whips Exotica into the ropes, then Exotica comes back with a spin kick, turning the match around. She soon runs into the ropes, but Diamond hammers her with aa swinging neckbreaker and tags out to Star. Exotica hits Star with an elbow; Star comes back with a headlock takedown, retaking control of the match. She sortly goes for an avalanche, but Exotica kicks her in the head and tags out to Rest. She goes for a spin kick, but Star blcoks the blow by pulling Rest in the way. Exotica nails Rest, then leaves the ring as Star continues her offensive. She soon goes for a standing wristlock, but Res blocks it and hammers her with an over-the-shoulder stomachbreaker, turning the match back around. Star shortly tags out to Diamond. Diamond and Rest stand toe-to-toe with each other. Diamond shortly ends the stand-off with a swinging neckbreaker. She soon sends Rest into a corner; Rest charges out and rocks her with an elbow, taking control of the match. Exotica shortly enters the ring and takes Diamond down with a powerslam, then Rest follows up with a flying kneedrop. She puts Diamond in a chinlock deathlock; Diamond reaches the ropes in 5 seconds. Rest tries to choke her against the ropes, but Diamond blocks it and throws her out of the ring. She jons her, then picks her up and rams her back into the ringpost twice. She reenters the ring as the ref begins counting. Exotica and the other Alliance members try to get Rest back in the rig, but she is counted out as the ref reaches 10. (8:36) ------------------------- Goliath (MGR: Harvey "the Mouth" Lippman) v. The Hitman (MGR: The Godfather) ------------------------- The Hitman tires to end the match quickly with a German suplex, but Goliath kicks out in wo. The Hitman goes for a flying bulldog, but Golaith moves out of the way, then props the Hitman's neck on the ropes. He runs into the opposing ropes and jumps on the Hitman's back, then goes for a stomp. The Hitman rolls out of the way and stands toe-to-toe with him. The Hitman shortly breaks the stalemate with an elbowsmash. He shortly takes Golaith down with a high cross body and turns it into a cover. Goliath kicks out in two; The Hitman punishes him with a scorpion deathlock. Goliath uses his height to reach the ropes quickly; the Himtan tries to apply headscissors, but Goliath blocks it and throws him out of the ring. (7:24) <Television Title Match> (For the vacant Television title) ------------------------- Ruben Von Nazck (MGR: Jason Hate) v. Platinum Powers (w/ Purple Hayes and Quicksilver) ------------------------- Von Nazk rabbit-punches Powers before the bell, then Powers takes him down with a dropkick to the knee, going on the offensive. Von Nazck quuickly takes control of the match with a back heel kick. Powers just as quickly turns the match back around with a DDT. Von Nazck shorly climbs to the top turnbuckle. Powers walks back into the opposing corner, then charges in to Von Nazck's and knocks his feet apart. Von Nazck lands hard on the turnbuckle, then falls back into the ring. Powers continues his offensive with a cobra clutch. Von Nazck soon turns the match around with a kick to the thigh. Powers shortly evens the match up with a full nelson. Von Nazck breaks the hold after 11 seconds; Powers rewards him with a savate kick. Von Nazck comes back with a backspin DDT, regaining his momentum. After six-and-a-half minutes Powers catches him in a bell-to-belly supelx. He covers Von Nazck, but can only get a one count. Von Nazck goes for a dragon suplex, but Powers blocks it and stands toe-to-toe with him. Von Nazck shortly goes for a front facelock, but Powers counters with a backdrop. Von Nazck in turn re-reverses it into a sunset flip; Powers kicks otu in two. Von Nazck complains about a slow count, enabling Powers to catch him in an inside cradle. Von Nazck kicks out in two; Powers nails him with a forearm to the back, turning the match around. Von Nazck shortly tries to even the match up with a headlock takedown, but Powers counters with a back suplex, continuing his assault. He shorty DDTS Von Nazck, then waits for him to get to his feet. He spins Von Nazck around, then climbs to the second turnbuckle inside the ring and nails the Russian with the Powerdriver. He covers Von Nazck and pins him.
