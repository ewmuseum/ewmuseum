WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION MONDAY KNIGHTS 2/10/96 Consecration Tour Live from Harare, Zimbabwe -----Main Event----- She-Devil v. Nadia Williams (Can-Am title match) Bounty Hunters v. Janet Strong / Angel Hunter The Cat's Meow Hardcore v. The Vinnie Babes Blood v. Aya Ichijou Thunder and Lightning v. Lovely Liz Strohlheim / Sweet Sue [Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.] [Fireworks explode as the camera shows the crimson WCA logo on the white ring canvas. The camera descends rapidly, then levels off facing the aisle. The camera swerves left, panning out over the capacity crowd and circling the ring, ending its focus on the iridescent three-dimensional WCA letters bracketing the entrance to the backstage area. The referee for the first match heads to the ring.] -------------------- Thunder and Lightning (Haley "Comet" Hanson and Big Norma Hudson) (w/ Mr. Canuck) v. Lovely Liz Strohlheim / Sweet Sue (w/ Karen "Bugs" Huffman -------------------- Hanson and Strohlheim lock up. Strohlheim backs her up to the ropes, then sends her to the far ones. She applies an armbar, taking control of the match. Hanson soon blocks a back suplex with a headlock takedown, then tags in Hudson. She takes Strohlheim down with a lariat. Hudson follows up with a legsweep faceslam, going on the offensive. Hanson shortly reenters the ring; she and Hudson whip Strohlheim to the ropes. Strohlheim returns with a double clothesline. Hanson rolls out of the ring as Strohlheim nails Hudson with a rolling kick, retaking control. She shortly nails Hudson with a forearm to the back, then tags in Sue. They whip Hudson to the ropes; Hudson ducks a double elbowsmash, rebounds and double clotheslines them. Strohlheim rolls out of the ring as Sue gets back up. Hudson sends her to the ropes; Sue returns with a kick, taking control of the match. Hudson shortly reverses an Irish whip, then knocks Sue down with an elbow. She encourages the "Hudson-Bay-Splash" chants; Sue rolls out of the ring. Hudson follows her out; Sue sends her into the ringsteps. Hudson stands upright; Sue nails her with a headbutt. Hudson ducks a lariat, but Sue spins around and nails her with a kneelift. Hudson returns to the ring and tags in Hanson as Sue enters. They whip Sue back to the ropes; Sue ducks a double elbowsmash, rebounds and nails them with a double clothesline. Sue hiptosses Hanson, going on the attack. Hanson soon counters a back suplex with a cross-body block; one, two, kickout. Hanson gets up and goes to the apron, then keeps Sue down with a springboard lariat. She tags in Hudson. They whip Sue to the ropes; Sue ducks a double clothesline, then rebounds and shows them how it's done. Hanson leaves the ring as Hudson nails Sue with a headbutt, turning things back around. After ninety seconds Sue rolls away from a spinning elbowdrop. She tags in Strohlheim, then they whip Hudson to the ropes and stop her with a double kick to the midsection. Sue leaves the ring as Strohlheim sends Hudson into a corner. She runs in shoulder-first, but Hudson blocks her with her knees and tags in Hanson. Strohlheim blocks Hudson's legsweep faceslam; Hudson leaves as Strohlheim takes Hanson down with a single-leg takedown. She applies a leg hammerlock, taking control of the match. She soon applies an abdominal stretch. Hanson submits after 5 seconds. (6:59) -------------------- Blood (w/ Crimson and Kim) v. Aya Ichijou -------------------- Both women circle each other, then lock up. Blood executes a double-axhandle chop, then follows up with neck-scissors. Ichijou rolls away from an elbowdrop, then throws Blood out of the ring. One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, Blood reenters. Ichijou nails her with a palm blow; Blood reverses a whip to the ropes. Ichijou blocks a roundhouse punch, then rakes Blood's face and heads for the top rope. Blood ducks a flying shoulderblock, then tackles Ichijou as she gets up, taking control of the match. Ichijou very shortly catches her in a belly-to-belly suplex. Blood prevents a cover, then ducks a thrust kick to the head. Ichijou counters spinning headscissors with a Hotshot. Blood gets back up, then takes her down with an inverted powerbomb, going back on the offensive. Ichijou shortly blocks a figure-four sleeper. She applies a standing wristlock, taking charge. Blood shortly picks her up; Ichijou blocks being set on a turnbuckle. Blood punishes her with a backspin DDT, retaking control. After a minute Ichijou ducks an enzuigiri to the face, then executes spinning headscissors into a cradle. Blood easily kicks out; Ichijou rewards her with a back heel kick, turning the match around. After 3 minutes Blood blocks a sleeper with a jawbreaker. Ichijou sits up; Blood yanks her to her feet, then sends her into a corner. She charges in; Ichijou lunges out of the way. Blood rebounds off the turnbuckles, then turns around and ducks a thrust kick to the head. Ichijou sidesteps a tackle, then takes Blood down with an enzuigiri, regaining her momentum. After a minute Blood reverses a whip to the ropes, then takes Ichijou down with a kick, turning the match back around. After ninety seconds Blood attempts a high cross body, but Ichijou catches and drops her into a backbreaker. She nails a standing Blood with a thrust kick to the head. Blood falls back to the mat. She gets back up on her feet, then reverses a whip to a corner and charges in. Ichijou moves out of the way; Blood stops herself in time. She turns and blocks a facerake, then nails Ichijou with a double-axhandle chop, regaining her momentum. She shortly executes the Gutwrench Suplex and pins Ichijou. (11:07) -------------------- Hardcore ("Nasty Girl" Amber Jane and "Dominatress" Kathy Lynn) (w/ Crimson and Kim) v. The Vinnie Babes (Sarah Bryant and the Vinnie Babe) (w/ Vinnie Gambino) -------------------- Jane and Bryant lock up, then Jane whips her to the ropes. She nails Bryant with a chop, going on the offensive. She very shortly tags in Lynn, then takes Bryant down with a Russian legsweep. Lynn follows up with a Thesz press and covers; one, two, thr-shoulder up! Both women stand up. Lynn slaps Bryant, who returns the favor. The Vinnie Babe enters, then she and Bryant whip Lynn to the ropes and double-clothesline her. The Vinnie Babe leaves as Lynn reverses another Irish whip, then decks Bryant with a superkick, taking charge. Bryant shortly blocks a pick-up with an elbowjab, then tags out to the Vinnie Babe. The latter climbs her turnbuckles, then takes Lynn down with a flying forearm; one, two, kickout. The Vinnie Babe gets up; Lynn ducks a spinning mule kick as she stands, then tags out to Jane. Jane takes the Vinnie Babe down with a double underhook piledriver, taking control of the match. After a minute the Vinnie Babe reverses a whip to a corner, then meets a returning Jane with a kick to the midsection. She executes a snap mare. Jane gets back up and blocks an atomic drop, then executes a back suplex, regaining her momentum. After a minute she catches the Vinnie Babe in a reverse cradle; one, two, Gambino pulls the ref out of the ring. Angry, the ref disqualifies the Vinnie Babes.
