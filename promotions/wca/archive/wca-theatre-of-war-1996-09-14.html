WCA Theatre of War 9/12

14 September 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION THEATRE OF WAR Live from Denver, Colorado 9/12/96 -----Main Event----- Li'l Cheetah v. Sweet Sue (Princess of the WCA T&T) Valkyrie v. Danielle Stewart "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln v. Karen "Bugs" Huffman Lovely Liz Strohlheim v. "Too Sweet" Tracy Turner Warthog v. "Psycho" Vicki V <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA wrestling action> <Wide angle view of the inside of the arena shows a capacity crowd. The ring announcer, timekeepers, and other officials are walking down the aisle to get ready for the show. The camera focuses on John Birmingham, the television announcer.> ------------------------- Warthog (w/ Snakebite) v. "Psycho" Vicki V ------------------------- Both women lock up, then Vicki nails Warthog with a jumping DDT. Warthog sits up; Vicki throws her out of the ring and goes to the floor. Warthog rocks her with a thrust kick to the head. Vicki comes back with a bulldog. Warthog gets back up and executes a gutwrench suplex. She rolls back into the ring, followed by Vicki. Warthog plants her with a dragon suplex, taking control. Vicki shortly dodges a football tackle and runs into the ropes. She rebounds, then nails Warthog with a back heel kick, taking control. She soon whips Warthog into the ropes. Warthog comes back with spinning headscissors into a cradle. Vicki easily kicks out. Warthog climbs the turnbuckles, but Vicki throws her to the mat. Warthog gets back up and reverses a whip to the ropes, then nails Vicki with a rolling kick, turning the match back around. Vicki quickly retakes control with a bulldog. She shortly attempts to send Warthog into the ropes, but Warthog reverses it, then clotheslines herr on the rebound. She puts Vicki in a Sammartino backbreker, going back on the offensive. Vicki soon begins to regain some momentum. Warthog attempts a senton; Vicki rolls out of the way. She sends Warthog into a corner and charges in; Warthog moves out of the way. She attempts to put Vicki into an octopus hold, but Vicki hiptosses her instead. Vicki shortly ends the stand-off with a dropkick to the knee. She shortly sends Warthog into the ropes. Warthog catches her in a belly-to-belly suplex off the rebound and covers her; Vicki kicks out in two. Warthog follows up with a double chickenwing, turning the match around again. She shortly goes for a dragon suplex, but Vicki blocks it and rakes her face. She nails Warthog with a bulldog, retaking control. She soon runs into the ropes. Warthog ducks a clothesline, then jumps a kick. Vicki rebounds a third time, then puts the Claw on Warthog. Warthog submits after 5 seconds. (9:24) ------------------------- Lovely Liz Strohlheim (w/ Karen "Bugs" Huffman) v. "Too Sweet" Tracy Turner ------------------------- Both women lock up, then Turner nails Strohlheim with a leg lariat, taking control of the match. Strohlheim quickly ends her offensive with a double underhook suplex. She soon puts Turner on a turnbuckle and climbs up. Turner shoves Strohlheim off the turnbuckles, then nails her with an elbowdrop from the second turnbuckle, turning the match back around. She shortly runs into the ropes. Strohlheim ducks a clothesline, then bodyslams Turner, retaking control. She soon attempts an avalanche, but Turner counters with a kick to the head. She gets up and waits for Strohlheim to get to her feet. Strohlheim stands up, then ducks a spin kick. Turner settles for a front-layout suplex, retaking control. After 2 minutes Strohlheim ends her onslaught with a legsweep faceslam. After 2 minutes Turner turns the match back around with a European uppercut. Strohlheim begins to regain some momentum. After 2 minutes she reverses a whip to the ropes, then ducks a leaping forearm. Turner gets back up; Strohlheim rocks her with a back heel kick. Turner shortly retakes control with a jack-knife powerbomb. A minute later she attempts a spin kick, but Strohlheim ducks it and nails her with a bulldog, going back on the attack. Turner immediately begins to even the match up. 2 minutes later Strohlheim goes for a spin kick. Turner pulls the ref in the way, letting him take the blow. She hammers Strohlheim with a Doctor Bomb, retaking control. Strohlheim quickly turns the match around with a clothesline. She soon backdrops Turner out of the ring and goes to the floor. She smashes Turner's face on the guardrail, then Turner does the same to her. Strohlheim blocks a jack-knife and rolls back in the ring. Turner follows her in, then blocks a side suplex. She nails Strohlheim with an over-the-shoulder stomachbreaker, taking control. Strohlheim shortly retakes control with a spin kick. She shortly executes a headlock takedown, then pins Turner with the Schoolgirl Pin. (17:25) ------------------------- "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln (w/ "Lethal" Lori Michaels) v. Karen "Bugs" Huffman (w/ Lovely Liz Strohlheim) ------------------------- Both women lock up, then Axeln pounds on Huffman with a forearm to the back, taking control. She soon goes for an abdominal stretch, but Huffman counters with a hiptoss. She goes for a lariat, but Axeln ducks it. Huffman runs into the ropes, then executes a rolling reverse cradle into a bridge, taking control. She shortly whips Axeln into a corner and charges in shoulder-first. Axeln raises her knee, then executes a German suplex, retaking control. Huffman soon runs into the ropes, then nails Axeln with a back heel kick, going back on the offensive. She shortly sends Axeln into the ropes. Axeln comes back with neck-scissors, retaking control. Huffman shortly retakes control with a backbreaker. Axeln quickly clotheslines her, starting a series of high-impact blows. She soon chops Huffman. Huffman starts to cry. Axeln looks on her with disgust, then punches her hard, continuing. She shortly runs into the ropes. Huffman goes for a small package, but Axeln reverses it. Huffman re-reverses it in two; Axeln kicks out at the last second. Huffman applies an armbar, going back on the offensive. After 2 minutes Axeln throws Huffman out of the ring. The ref blocks her from going to the floor. Huffman reenters; Axeln puts her in a cobra clutch, turning the match around again. After two minutes Axeln goes for a kneelift, but Huffman sidesteps it. She attempts a Doctor Bomb, but Axeln backdrops her instead. She applies a kneelock, then lets go 20 seconds later and pulls Huffman into a corner. Axeln gives the V-sign, then plants Huffman with the Doombomb and pins her. (11:27) ------------------------- Valkyrie (w/ the Prince) v. Danielle Stewart ------------------------- Stewart sends Valkyrie into a corner and runs in shoulder-first. Valkyrie moves out of the way; Stewart stops herself just before ramming the ringpost. Valkyrie goes to another top turnbuckle, then goes for flying headscissors. Stewart ducks them, then sends Valkyire into the corner. She runs in shoulder-first, nailing Valkyrie and taking control of the match. A minute later she attempts an Irish whip to a corner, but Valkyrie reverses it. She nails Stewart with a spinning mule kick, going on the offensive. Stewart shortly hammers her with a side suplex, retaking control. Valkyrie shortly throws her out of the ring and goes to the floor. Stewart goes around the ringpost and reenters; Valkyrie chases after her. Stewart attemtps to send her into the ropes, but Valkyrie reverses it. She hits Stewart with a shoulderblock, taking control. Valkyrie shortly goes for a reverse neckbreaker, but Stewart blocks it and executes a Russian legsweep, retaking control. She soon goes for a bulldog, but Valkyrie shrugs it off and takes her down with a rolling kick, going back ont he offensive. Stewart quickly ends it with a faceslam. Valkyrie shortly puts her on a turnbuckle, then takes her down with a belly-to-belly superplex, starting another game plan. Stewart shortly retakes control with a fallaway slam. She shortly attempts a sunset flip, but Valkyrie counters with a sitdown. Stewart kicks out in two. Valkyrie accuses the ref of making a slow count, then goes for a backflip cradle. Stewart blocks it and attemtps a hiptoss. Valkyrie blocks it and goes for an enzuigiri to the face. Stewart ducks it. Valkyrie shortly throws Stewart into a corner and runs in shoulder-first, ending the stand-off. Stewart quickly retakes control with a short lariat. Valkyrie soon ends her attack with a chokehold. She soon goes for a diving elbowsmash, but Stewart ducks it. She sends Valkyrie into the ropes, then jumps a kick. Valkyrie stops just behind her, then takes her down with a short clothesline. She puts Stewart in a Boston crab. Stewart submits after 8 seconds. (11:09) ===== Birmingham: "Stewart suffers her first loss in the WCA as Valkyrie continues her winning streak, heading into the Lords of the Ring with a win on her side. It's interesting to note that she and Holt will represent the Evil Empire in the tournament, whereas the entire Click will be there. On the other hand, a lot of wrestlers hate Muniz, especially the Assassin. Muniz will no doubt be the primary target of a lot of wrestlers who want to humiliate and possibly injure her, and not just because she has both WCA singles belts right now. Up next is our main event, a match between the Assassin's tag team partner Li'l Cheetah and Sweet Sue. Cheetah, the holder of the unofficial Princess of the WCA T&T, has already defended it against Lovely Liz Strohlheim and Karen 'Bugs' Huffman, two of the three original TCA members. While Sue has seemingly distanced herself from the TCA, Cheetah still offered this chance against her. Sue has recently had a string of losses, and the last thing she needs to do is to go into the three-ring battle-royale on a losing streak. Let's go now to the ring." Ring Announcer: "Ladies and gentlemen, tonight's main event is for the Princess of the WCA T&T. Introducing first: from Medford, New Jersey, weighing in at 117 pounds, Sweet Sue!" <Sue walks down the aisle to a largely unappreciative audience. She ignores the crowd and enters the ring.> "Her opponent: from Detroit, Michigan, weighing in at 143 pounds, Li'l Cheetah!" <Cheetah walks down the aisle, carrying the T&T. The crowd seems evenly split in their greeting; some cheer, some boo. Cheetah enters the ring and holds them up for the crowd to see, then gives them to an official.> Birmingham's voice: "Mark White is the referee for this match. Both women look determined. The bell rings; collar-and-elbow lockup. Sue breaks off and nails Cheetah with an enzuilariato. She whips Cheetah into the ropes, then backdrops her. Sue's going for the pin; one, two, shoulder up. Both women stand up - Sue knocks Cheetah down with a short clothesline. Cheetah gets back up. She reverses a whip to the ropes. Sue jumps a kick; Cheetah rebounds again, then hits her with a shoulderblock. She runs into the ropes; Sue takes her down with a clothesline. Cheetah gets back up and reverses a whip to a corner. She runs in shoulder-first, nailing Sue. Cheetah is choking her! White begins counting one, two, three, four, Cheetah confronts him. "Sue walks out into the ring. Cheetah curses the ref, then executes a moonsault bodybl - Sue dodges it! She pulls Cheetah up and sends her into the ropes, then takes her down with a jumping lariat. Cheetah stands up and applies an ab - Sue hiptosses her. She follows up with a stomachbreaker, then drops a double-axhandle chop on her. Cheetah slowly gets back to her feet. Sue puts her in a sleeper! Cheetah lurches around the ring. She's slowing down. And grabs the top rope! Cheetah ducks neck-scissors. She puts Sue in an abdominal stretch. She's too close to the ropes; Sue grabs one to break the hold. "Sue reverses a whip to the far ropes. Cheetah punches her hard on the rebound, then kicks her in the head. She drives her knee into Sue's back, then scoo - Sue nails her with a la - Cheetah ducks it and applies a double chickenwing! Sue struggles to break free. Wonder where the Assassin is; she and Cheetah usually accomapany each other to the ring. Then again, the Assassin's probably getting ready to beat the heck out of Muniz. Cheetah lets go and hammers Sue in a back suplex. She executes a double leg cradle; Sue kicks out in two. Sue gets up and jack-knifes Cheetah, then covers; one, two, - whoah, Cheetah kicks out with autority. Both women stand up. Cheetah blocks a kick to the leg and hammers Sue in a Guerilla Press! "Cheetah goes to one knee and chokes Sue! One, two, three, four, momentary break, one, two, three, four, Cheetah turns Sue over and sets her up. Sue backdrops her instead! Cheetah gets back up and ducks a short clothesline. Sue applies a standing wristlock. We've really seen a change in attitude in Sue over the summer. When she entered the old WCA last spring, she was a lot like Karen Huffman; now she's really getting cocky. Cheetah breaks the hold. Sue nails her with a rolling kick. Cheetah applies an abdo - Sue hiptosses her. Cheetah comes back with a backspin DDT! Sue rolls over, holding her head. She stands up. Cheetah sends her into a corner. "Sue charges back out - right into an elbow. She blocks a dragon suplex and whips Sue itno the ropes. Cheetah backdrops her and goes for the pin; Sue kicks out in two. Cheetah gets up and runs into the ropes. Sue punches her in the midsection! Cheetah responds with a knee to the back. Sue nails her with a double axhandle chop. She applies another standing wristlock. Sue is grinning evilly - is she up to something? The TCA were never really a cohesive unit, anway. They spent more time fighting each other than other wrestlers, which is probably why they sort of split up. Cheetah breaks the hold. She hammers Sue with a dragon suplex and covers her; one, two, kickout. "Both women stand up. Sue blocks an armbar and executes a short clothesline. She follows up with a hiptoss, then puts Cheetah in a leg hammerlock. Uh-oh, Cheetah's got that 'You're gonna get it' look! Sue is determined; she tightens the hold. Cheetah begins pulling herself to the ropes. She makes it! Sue reluctantly lets go. Cheetah spins her around and sets her up - Sue blocks it and kicks her in the leg. Cheetah just stares at her. I've seen this before. Sue, I hope your life insurance is paid up. Sue reverses a whip to the ropes. She ducks a clothesline; Cheetah rebounds again and hammers her with a Guerilla Press. She follows up with a reverse neckbreaker, then executes a double leg cradle. Sue kicks out in two. "Cheetah throws her out of the ring. She goes to the floor, then ducks a short clothesline. Sue ducks a short lariat and reenters the ring. Cheetah follows her in - Sue nails her with a short clothesline, spinning her around. She applies a sleeper. Cheetah falls into a corner, smashing Sue against the turnbuckles! Cheetah takes Sue down with a Guerilla Press, then drives her knee into Sue's back. "She turns Sue over and covers her; one, two, in the ropes. Sue pulls Cheetah through the ropes! She rolls out of the ring. Sue throws Cheetah back into the ring and reenters herself. Cheetah ducks a short clothesline and takes Sue down with a back suplex. She throws Sue out of the ring and goes to the floor. Sue rocks her with a European uppercut. Cheetah knocks her into the ringpost. She blocks a hiptoss, then smashes Sue's face on the guardrail! Sue leans on the barrier - Cheetah dropkicks her! "She nails Sue with a backspin DDT on the concrete and reenters the ring. White begins his count. Cheetah thinks it's over. She may be right; six, seven, eight, nine, Sue reenters the ring. Cheetah grabs Sue's head; Sue blocks a choke against the ropes. She knocks Cheetah down with a forearm smash and covers her; Cheetah kicks out at the last second! Sue sends Cheetah into a corner. Cheetah rebounds off the turnbuckles! Sue executes a hip - Cheetah counters with a backslide! Sue kicks out at the last second; both women stand up. Cheetah nails Sue with the Heart Punch! Sue crumples to the mat; Cheetah turns her over with her foot, then goes to one knee. She places one hand on Sue; one, two, three!" RA: "Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the match, and still holder of the Princess of the WCA T&T: in 11 minutes, 46 seconds, Li'l Cheetah!" <Cheetah raises her arms in victory, then raises up the T&T.> Birmingham: "Cheetah defends - rather easily, if I'm any judge - the Princess of the WCA T&T, heading into the Lords of the Ring with a solid victory behind her. Conversely, Sweet Sue heads in with a solid loss behind her. Coming up this Saturday on Rampage: 'Too Sweet' Tracy Turner begins her assautl against the Click by taking on 'the Intimidator' Amber Axeln in a lumberjack strap match, The Scandalous Ones go head-to-head with Flair's Girls, 'Psycho' Vicki V and Warhead meet in a return match, and more! I'm John Birmingham, have a good evening!" <Closing credits appear.> -- ---------- [name withheld] [detail withheld] "Make it idiot-proof, and someone will make a better idiot."
