Jenny Black -------------------- Monique (w/ Crimson and Red) v. Jenny Black (w/ Dr. Insane, Shelly Rage, and Valkyrie) Referee: Julian Pace -------------------- Both women lock up. Monique blocks a hiptoss, then she executes an armdrag takedown. Black pops back up and blocks a hiptoss, then Monique blocks a knee to the midsection. She throws Black out of the ring. One .. two .. three .. four, Black reenters and rolls Monique up in a small package; Monique kicks out at the last second. Both women get up, Monique dodging a kneelift in the process. Black pops up again, then Monique dodges a cartwheel kick. She runs to the ropes; Black stops her with a shoulderblock, taking control of the match. Monique shortly dodges another cartwheel kick. She puts Black into a bearhug, starting her own offense. After 2 1/2 minutes she puts Black into another bearhug. Black breaks the hold in 14 seconds and applies a standing wristlock, putting Monique back on the defensive. Monique shortly blocks a spinning toe hold. She rolls out of the ring; Black follows her out. Monique whips her into the guardrail, then into the ringsteps. She sends Black back to the barrier, then she puts Black on the floor with a fallaway slam. Monique goes to the apron, then she hammers Black with a flying fistdrop. She reenters the ring and lets the ref start counting; one .. two .. three .. four .. five .. six .., Black rolls back into the ring. Monique applies a leg hammerlock, keeping the pressure on Black. After an intense 5 minutes a weakened Black reverses a whip to a corner. She meets Monique's charge out with a drop-kick, turning the match around. After 3 minutes of missed opportunities, Monique scoops Black up and pummels her with a Samoan Drop, turning the match back around. She soon catches Black in a reverse cradle, pinning her. (14:25) -------------------- Freedom (w/ Shannon Stevens and the Dream Master) v. Liberty (w/ Bodacious Janet Muniz, "the Intimidator" Amber Axeln, and the Mistress of Death) Referee: Tina Nolke -------------------- Freedom runs to the ropes, then she backdrops Liberty, taking control of the match. She soon dodges a backflip cradle; Liberty picks herself up and takes Freedom down via a front-layout suplex. She meets a standing Freedom with a backslide; Freedom easily kicks out. Freedom rolls away from a springboard splash and picks herself up. She waits on Liberty, then she sends her to the ropes. Liberty rebounds; Freedom blocks a back heel kick and takes her down with neck-scissors. Liberty gets up and catches a rising Freedom in a backslide; Freedom kicks out in two. Liberty executes sinning headscissors into a cradle; again Freedom kicks out in two. Liberty gets up and goes into a corner. Freedom gets back up, then she meets Liberty's flying karate chop with a fist to the midsection. Liberty shuffles forward, then she reverses an Irish whip and nails Freedom with a shoulderblock, taking control of the match. After 2 1/2 minutes Freedom ducks a flying shoulderblock. Liberty picks herself up, then she fights and reverses a backslide; one, two, kickout. Freedom blocks a suplex, then she nails Liberty with a drop-kick to the knee, turning the match around. She shortly goes to the top ropes; Liberty throws her off the turnbuckles. Liberty slips through the ropes and goes to the top, then she nails Freedom with a flying shoulderblock, retaking control. Freedom shortly ducks a savate kick, then she whips Liberty to the ropes. She ducks a clothesline, then she spins around and blocks a back heel kick. She sends Liberty to the side ropes and backdrops her, turning the match around. Liberty shortly ends her offense with a backslide, pinning her.
