WCA Rampage 12/14

14 December 1996

WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION RAMPAGE 12/14/96 Live from the Lake Arena in Springfield, Missouri -----Main Event----- <Tag Team Titles Submission Match> Darkness, Inc. v. "Psycho" Vicki V / Christy Cooper Janet Strong v. Samantha Jones "Destroyer" Dara Holt v. Angel Hunter The Scandalous Ones v. The Vinnie Babes <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.> <Fireworks explode as the camera shows a top-down view of the ring, highlighting the maroon WCA logo on the canvas. The camera descends, panning to the right and leveling off, showing the capacity crowd. It focuses on the end of the aisle, where 'WCA' frames the entrance to the locker room. Several WCA officials walk down the aisle.> ---------- The Scandalous Ones (Shelly Rage and Jenny Black) v. The Vinnie Babes ---------- Rage and Bryant lock up, then Rage ducks a thrust kick to the head. She runs into the ropes. Bryant blocks a hiptoss, then runs into the ropes. She jumps a kick, then knocks Rage down with a shoulderblock, going on the attack. Rage very shortly turns things around with an elbow to the face. Bryant soon blocks an armlock leglock, then gets back up and runs into the ropes. She catches Rage in a belly-to-belly suplex. Rage kicks out at the last second; both women stand up. Bryant executes a back suplex, going on the offensive. She shortly tags out to the Vinnie Babe, who nails Rage with a swinging neckbreaker. Both women stand up; Rage blocks a roundhouse and hits the Vinnie Babe with a double-axhandle chop. She follows up with a flying clothesline, going back on the offensive. She shortly nails the Vinnie Babe with a kick, then tags in Black. They send the Vinnie Babe to the ropes, then double-backdrop her. Rage leaves as Black applies a kneelock, continuing the assault. The Vinnie Babe shortly backdrops out of a Doctor bomb, then scoops Black up and bodyslams her, gaining the advantage. She soon sends Black to the ropes. Gambino trips Black up; the ref warns him as the Vinnie Babe tags in Bryant. Rage uses the ref's inattention to enter the ring and throw the Vinnie Babe out, then she and Black send Bryant to the ropes. They nail her with a double enzuilariato, then Rage leaves as Black keeps the Vinnie Babe down with a bulldog, retaking control. She tags out to Rage; Rage runs into the ropes, then misses with a kick. She turns around and executes a belly-to-belly suplex, starting a new assault. She soon catches Rage in another belly-to-belly and covers her; one, two, Black stomps on the back of Bryant's head. Rage gets back up slowly and heads to the top turnbuckle. She waits on Bryant, then takes her down again with a flying clothesline, going back on the offensive. She shortly tags in Black, then powerbombs Bryant. Black comes off their turnbuckles with a flying splash. One, two, kickout. They pull Bryant up, then double-punch her in the midsection. Black follows up with a spinning leg lariat, continuing the onslaught. She shortly tags out to Rage. Rage nails Bryant with a clothesline, continuing. She shortly tags in Black, then both women nail Bryant with a double enzuilariato. Black follows up with a flying spinning leg lariat, going on the offensive. Bryant shortly escapes the attack and tags in the Vinnie Babe. They faze Black with a double roundhouse, then the Vinnie Babe throws Black out of the ring and joins her on the floor. Black shoves her into a ringpost, then ducks her leaping forearm. She rolls back into the ring; the Vinnie Babe follows her in. Black nails her with a knee to the back, taking charge. She soon sends the Vinnie Babe to the ropes. The Vinnie Babe comes back with a shoulderblock, turning the match around. After 2 1/2 minutes Black ducks a leaping forearm. She takes the Vinnie Babe down with a monkey flip, then executes a dragon suplex. One, two, kickout. black tags in Rage. They nail the Vinnie Babe with a double enzuilariato, then Rage follows up with a spinning DDT, going on the offensive. A minute later the Vinnie Babe catches Rage in a dragon suplex. Rage quickly gets her shoulder up; the Vinnie Babe picks her up and bodyslams her, turning things back around. After 2 minutes Rage counters another bodyslam with a small package. The Vinnie Babe kicks out in two and stands up. Rage applies a cobra clutch, turning the match around again. The Vinnie Babe shortly blocks a powerbomb and tags in Bryant. Black enters the ring and throws the Vinnie Babe out, then she and Rage send Bryant to the ropes. They nail her with a double enzuilariato, then Black leaves the ring as Rage pulls Bryant up. She sends her to a corner, then charges in and smashes her against them, taking charge. A minute later she puts Bryant into a sleeper. Bryant jawbreaks out after 5 seconds, then stands up and sends Rage to the mat via a back heel kick, turning the match around. Black shortly enters the ring; she and Rage send Bryant to the ropes, then deck her with a double clothesline. Bryant picks herself up; Rage takes her down with an enzuigiri. Bryant shortly sends Rage out of the ring by the hair. Rage reenters as the Vinnie Babe slips through the ropes. She and Bryant execute the Double Gold Digger, then Bryant pins Rage. (19:20) ---------- "Destroyer" Dara Holt (w/ the Prince) v. Angel Hunter (w/ Janet Strong) ---------- Both women lock up, then Holt executes a Soviet suplex, gaining the advantage. After 2 minutes Hunter reverses a whip to a corner. She charges in with a Stinger splash, then sends Holt to the ropes. She knocks Holt down with a shoulderblock and covers her. Holt kicks out in two and gets to her feet. Hunter puts her in an abdominal stretch, turning the match around. She shortly runs into the ropes. Holt backdrops her, then executes a double leg cradle. Hunter gets her shoulder up in two. She stands up and reverses a whip to the ropes, then blocks an inverted atomic drop. Holt ducks a lariat and chokes her; one, two, three, four, she lets go and executes a legsweep faceslam, turning things back around. After 2 minutes Hunter catches her in a takeover suplex, turning the match around again. A minute later Holt counters an ipponzei with a lariat, then sends Hunter to the ropes. She nails Hunter with a clothesline, retaking control of the match. After 2 minutes Hunter reverses a whip to the ropes. She misses with a clothesline, then turns around and blocks a facerake. She takes Holt down with a belly-to-back suplex, then picks her up and puts her in the Rack. Holt submits after 13 seconds. (8:42) ---------- Janet Strong (w/ Angel Hunter) v. Samantha Jones (w/ Joanne Watson) ---------- Both women lock up, then Strong sends Jones to the ropes. Jones jumps a kick and hooks the ropes. She nails Strong with a short lariat; one, two, kickout. Jones headbutts Strong, taking control of the match. She shortly runs into the ropes. Strong nails her with a clothesline, taking charge. Jones soon ducks a jumping side kick, then puts Strong in a cobra clutch, retaking control. She shortly scoops Strong up, then plants her with the Running Powerslam. Strong kicks out with authority in two. Both women stand up; Jones nails Strong with a kneelift, continuing her assault. After 2 1/2 minutes Strong counters a swinging neckbreaker with a side suplex, turning the match around again. After an intense 5 1/2 minutes Strong runs into the ropes. Jones stops her with a clothesline, then executes a short lariat. Strong kicks out in two; Jones rolls her up in an inside cradle. Strong kicks out at the last second and stands up. Jones sends her to the ropes; Strong comes back with an enzuigiri. Almost 2 minutes later she caps her assault with the Bear Hug. Jones submits immediately. (11:59) ===== Birmingham: "Janet Strong virtually squashes Samantha Jones, using her weight and strength advantages to full effect. Joanne Watson has been a little disappointed in the performance of her Hired Help in the WCA; I'm sure she'll have a few words to say to Samantha tonight. In our commercials for tonight's show we've advertised a match between Hired Help and the team of 'the Intimidator' Amber Axeln and Valkyrie. However, with Samantha Jones' singles match tonight, the WCA decided to hold off on the tag team match until later this month. We apologize to both teams for the delay. Up next is a match for the Tag Team championships. Darkness, Inc. have held the belts for almost three months to the day. During the past month the Posse of Punishment in one form or another have tried to defeat them for the titles, but so far have been unable to do so. They have sworn that tonight will be their last attempt for the Tag titles, but have taken some drastic measures recently to soften up the extremely tough DI. Let's go now to the ring."
