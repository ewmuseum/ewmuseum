Elizabeth (w/ the Nature Boy) ------------------------- Both women lock up, the Huffman puts Elizabeth in an octopus hold. She lets go after 20 seconds, then sends Elizabeth into the ropes. Elizabeth kicks out in two; Huffman punishes her with a bulldog. A minute later Elizabeth attempts another back heel kick. Huffman blocks it and runs into the ropes. Huffman turns it over quickly, then backdrops out of a front facelock. She executes a double leg cradle, but only gets a two count. Huffman runs into the ropes; Elizabeth grabs her on the rebound and throws her out of the ring. Huffman quickly rolls back in; Elizabeth greets her with a kneelift, going on the offensive. She shortly caps it with the Figure Four. Huffman struggles to reach the ropes. She turns it over, but Elizabeth re-reverses it. Huffman pulls herself toward the ropes again, finally reaching them. Elizabeth lets her get up, then rocks her with a lariat. Huffman goes for an octopus hold; Elizabeth attempts to counters with a hiptoss, but Huffman re-reverses it into a backslide. Elizabeth kicks out in two. Huffman nails her with a dropkick to the knee, taking control. After two minutes she attempts a backbreaker, but Elizabeth blocks it and goes for a front facelock. Huffman backdrops out of it, evening the match. Both women counters the other; Huffman shortly takes control with a kick. After three minutes Huffman attempts a flying bulldog, but Elizabeth sidesteps it and runs into the ropes. She rocks Huffman with a right cross to the jaw, turning the match back around. Huffman soon backdrops her out of the ring and goes to the floor. Elizabeth hammers her in a belly-to-back suplex, then attempts a takeover suplex. Huffman counters with a belly-to-belly. The Nature Boy distracts her, long enough for Elizabeth to spin her around and execute a back suplex. Huffman gets back up and ducks a savate kick, then hammers Elizabeth in a dragon suplex. Elizabeth ducks a kick to the head and stands up. Huffman nails he with a lariat. Elizabeth shoves her over the ringsteps. Huffman gets back up and kicks her in the head. She attempts a bulldog, but Elizabeth shrugs her off and chokes her. She lets go at the nine count and reenters the ring. Huffman follows her in. Elizabeth bodyslams her, continuing her attack. She shortly goes for a lariat, but Huffman ducks it and puts her in the Bearhug. Elizabeth submits immdediately. (16:14) ------------------------- Double J v. "Too Sweet" Tracy Turner (w/ the Dream Master) ------------------------- Double J sends Turner into the ropes. She nails her with an enzuilariato, assuming control of the match. Turner quickly takes control with a rolling reverse cradle. Double J kicks out in two; Turner punishes her with a Doctor Bomb. Double J shortly blocks a European uppercut and runs into the ropes. Turner ducks an elbow, then a clothesline. Double J rebounds again aaand ducks an elbow, then backdrops Turner. She attempts to whip Turner into the ropes, but Turner reverses it. She hits Double J with a shoulderblock and follows up with an over-the-shoulder stomachbreaker, resuming her assault. After a minute she executes a rolling reverse cradle. Double J kicks out in two. Turner complains about a slow count, enabling Double J to roll her up in an inside cradle. Turner kicks out in two; Double J throws her out of the ring and goes to the floor. She knocks Turner into the ringsteps, then powerslams her on the concrete. Turner slowly gets up, then takes her down with a belly-to-back suplex and reenters the ring. Double J rolls back in. Turner attempts an avalanche, but Double J kicks her in the head instead, taking control of the match. She soon goes for a cruciffix, but Turner counters wiht a Samoan Drop, turning the match back around. She shortly executes the Sweet Dreams, then pins Double J.
