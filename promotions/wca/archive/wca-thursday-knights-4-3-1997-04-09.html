WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION THURSDAY KNIGHTS 4/3/97 From Rangoon, Burma -----Main Event----- =Tag Team Titles Tournament Final Match= Bounty Hunters v. Hardcore =Can-am Title Match= Li'l Cheetah v. Femme Fatale Muniz's Musings "Psycho" Vicki V v. Angel Hunter Lovely Liz Strohlheim v. Liberty New Dawn / Bodacious Janet Muniz v. The Scandalous Ones / Asano Otani (Street Fight / Entourages Banned From Ringside) <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.> <Fireworks explode as the camera shows the crimson WCA logo on the white ring canvas. The camera descends rapidly, then levels off facing the aisle. The camera swerves left, panning out over the capacity crowd and circling the ring, ending its focus on the iridescent three-dimensional WCA letters bracketing the entrance to the backstage area. The Scandalous Ones (Jenny Black and Shelly Rage) / Asano Otani -------------------- Valkyrie fails to show for the match, leaving Muniz to fend off both Black and Otani. The camera focuses on Axeln and Rage first; Rage counters a headlock with a back suplex. Axeln gets up and reverses a whip to the ropes; Rage takes her down with a leaping forerm and covers; one, two, kickout. Rage blows kisses to the crowd as Axeln gets up, then executes an inside cradle. Axeln gets her shoulder up in two and gets back up. She reverses a whip to a corner; Rage bumps into Muniz, throwing her off balance. Rage walks back out; Axeln takes her down with neck-scissors. Rage ducks an enzuilariato as she stands up; Otani walks over to the fight, moving the double-team to Axeln, putting her on the defensive. Otani shortly moves back to the Muniz / Black battle as Rage executes a handspring bodyblock. Black soon runs over to their battle and takes Axeln down with a fire-extinguisher-enhanced-clothesline. Axeln gets back up in time to roll over Rage's flying cross body press; Rage gets her shoulder up in two. Black returns to help Otani out against Muniz as Rage decks Axeln with a chair, retaking control of her battle. Black shortly moves over to their battle again; Muniz chases after her and nails her with a double-axhandle chop, then throws her into Otani. Muniz helps Axeln nail Rage with a double kneelift. Axeln keeps Rage down with neck-scissors, then Black runs over and nails her with the ring bell. Axeln turns and punches her, switching places with Muniz. Rage gets up and applies a standing wristlock, regaining the upper hand. Muniz soon throws Rage out of the ring, then joins her on the floor. Muniz chokes Rage, taking control of their battle. Axeln and Otani slug it out as Black sets up a table, then Otani puts Axeln on the table as Black goes to the apron. Black executes a flying kneedrop, causing the table to sag in the middle. Black gets up and whistles at Rage, then both women trade places and take down the Click members with dual clotheslines. Rage lets Otani take over beating Axeln and returns to help her tag partner execute a double kneelift. Axeln takes Otani down with a headlock; Rage returns to help as Black gets on the apron and takes Muniz down with a moonsault bodyblock, retaking control of their battle. Axeln takes Rage down with a drop-kick, then reenters the ring, followed by Otani, who brings a cane with her. Otani swings at Axeln, who ducks it. Rage takes off into the crowd as Axeln takes Otani down with a German suplex; Otani kicks out in two. Black shortly ducks a short clothesline on the floor, then executes a handspring bodyblock. Rage returns from her visit to the concession stands carrying a soda-pop canister. She hands it to a defensive Otani. Otani brings the canister down across Axeln's forehead, putting her back on the defensive. Muniz and Black shortly move their battle back to the ring, putting all five participants into the squared circle. Muniz nails Black with a knee to the stomach, then punches Rage and slaps Otani hard. Valkyrie walks down the aisle with a Click II banner to the delight of the fans. She enters the ring with the banner as a masked woman rolls out from under the ring with a Singapore cane Valkyrie rushes toward the Posse, then stops as the masked woman brings the cane down on Axeln, dazing her. The cane comes down again, knocking Axeln out as Valkyrie throws salt into Muniz's eyes. She proceeds to choke Muniz with the banner, causing the ref to throw the match out. The Posse leaves the ring, scouring for foreign objects. They return with as much as they can carry, then attack Axeln and Muniz. Liberty races down the aisle and enters the ring, keeping some Posse members at bay over Axeln with her kendo stick. 'Psycho' Vicki V runs down the aisle with a trash can, drops it, and slips in behind her, then spins Liberty around and applies the Claw with her spiked glove. Valkyrie demands a mic, then looks at the Click members with a pleased look. Valkyrie: "What a wonderful sight, now isn't it? First of all I would like all of you simple peasants in the audience to stand in awe for the greatest wrestling organization to ever walk the face of this earth, and I'm of course speaking of the Posse." <She is interrupted by a chorus of boos from the crowd.> "SHUT UP!! Oh, you hillbillies can cheer all you want for these ugly old dinosaurs lying her flat on their backs as they usually do, but deep down you know there's only one group that really matters around here, and it's my pleasure to announce that it just got a little bigger with the latest additions. Now, let's show these sissies what we really think of their precious Click II." The masked woman takes off the black heels, breaks them and throws them into the trash can. Valkyrie then sets the Click banner on fire and throws it into the trash can as well as the Posse sets up two tables. The Scandalous Ones place Muniz and Axeln on them. Valkyrie jumps on the Muniz as the masked woman jumps onto Axeln. They both shout "Let's give those wimpy WCA officials a reason for carrying you out on stretchers!!", then both proceed to execute a Valkyrian Storm on their victims, breaking the tables in half. The entire Posse then continues to beat and kick on their victims. A flood of WCA officials race down the aisle; the Posse continue their beating briefly, then voluntarily walk out with big smiles on their faces. Axeln and Muniz are indeed carried out on stretchers, escorted by Liberty. (14:03) -------------------- Lovely Liz Strohlheim (w/ Karen "Bugs" Huffman and Sweet Sue) v. Liberty -------------------- Both women lock up, then Liberty executes an armdrag takedown. She executes a crucifix; Strohlheim kicks out in two. She blocks an abdominal stretch with a hiptoss, then rolls Liberty over and puts her into a camel clutch. Liberty reaches for the ropes, managing to grab the bottom one. She uses them to pull herself up, then turns and surprises Strohlheim with a short clothesline, taking control of the match. Strohlheim shortly sends her to the ropes, then nails her with an elbow to the face, taking advantage of the attack on Liberty. After 3 minutes Liberty blocks a slap, then executes a fallaway slam. She screams out "Posse's going down!", then applies standing short-arm scissors, turning the match around. After 2 minutes she runs into the ropes; Strohlheim executes a high cross body, surprising Liberty and pinning her. (5:28) ------------------- "Psycho" Vicki V (w/ Dr. Insane and Christy Cooper) v. Angel Hunter (w/ Janet Strong) -------------------- Both women lock up, then Hunter breaks off and goes into a corner. She executes a flying clothesline; Vicki gets up and decks her with a swinging punch, going on the offensive. After 2 minutes Hunter reverses an Irish whip, then kicks Vicki in the leg, turning the match around. Vicki soon blocks a standing wristlock, then takes Hunter down with an earringer, going back on the attack. After a minute she takes Hunter down with a running powerslam; one, two, Hunter literally throws Vicki off her. Vicki sits up, surprised. She gets up; Hunter takes her down with a snap mare, starting a new offensive. Vicki shortly rolls away from an elbowdrop, then gets up and goes to a corner. She comes off the turnbuckles with a flying karate chop, then scoops Hunter up. Vicki executes a running powerslam; one, two, kickout. Vicki stands up and moves behind Hunter, then rolls her up with an inside cradle, pinning her.
