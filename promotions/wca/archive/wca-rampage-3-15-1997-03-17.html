WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION RAMPAGE 3/15/97 Live from the Lake Arena in Springfield, Missouri -----Main Event----- =Tag Team Titles Match= Magic Mountain Warriors v. The Scandalous Ones =Can-Am Title Match= Femme Fatale v. Lovely Liz Strohlheim Interview with Femme Fatale Janet Strong v. Freedom The Scandal Sheet Big Norma Hudson v. Liberty (Strap Match) *Strap Match* -------------------- Li'l Cheetah (w/ Tall Troll and the Assassin) v. Liberty -------------------- Both women lock up after being bound. Cheetah picks Liberty up and drops her into a stomachbreaker. She gathers up slack in the strap and chokes Liberty. She lets go; Liberty sits up, then blocks a double reverse chinlock with a facerake. She gets up and puts Cheetah into a front facelock, taking control of the match. After almost 4 minutes of solid offense, Liberty throws Cheetah out of the ring and goes after her. She rakes Cheetah's face, then shoves Cheetah over the ringsteps. Liberty clears off the nearest table and sets it up, then keeps Cheetah on the floor with an armdrag takedown. She rams Cheetah's head into the guardrail. Cheetah uses the barrier to pull herself up; Liberty rakes her face again, then throws Cheetah back into the ringsteps. Cheetah gets up a little faster; Liberty takes her to her knees with a double chop to the neck. The Assassin distracts Liberty as Cheetah gets up. Cheetah picks Liberty up and drops her into a stomachbreaker. She reenters the ring as much as the strap will allow. Liberty gets up on the apron; Cheetah charges her, knocking her off through the table. Cheetah gets pulled through the ropes as well, landing on Liberty. Cheetah gets up and yanks Liberty to her feet, then whips her into the ringpost. Cheetah throws Liberty back into the ring, then follows her in. She chokes Liberty, bringing her to her knees and turning the match around. After 3 1/2 minutes Liberty counters a backspin DDT with a belly-to-belly suplex; one, two, kickout. Cheetah rolls away from a stomp, then sets Liberty up. Liberty prevents being lifted up, then executes neck-scisssors, taking Cheetah down and turning the match back around. Cheetah shortly reverses a whip to a corner, then runs in and begins punching Liberty. She pulls a dazed Liberty out of the corner, then plants her on the mat with a jack-knife powerbomb. Liberty barely kicks out at the last second. Cheetah goes to the ropes to flirt with the fans as Liberty pulls herself up. She catches Cheetah in a full nelson, retaking control. Cheetah soon nails her with a swinging punch, going back on the offensive. Liberty shortly blocks a facebite with a kneelift, then executes a back suplex, retaking control. The Assassin soon enters the ring with a chair. She swings at Liberty, who ducks down. The Assassin nails Cheetah instead; Liberty throws her out of the ring, then turns and covers Cheetah; one, two, Cheetah throws Liberty off of her. She gets up, then grabs Liberty's arm and sends her into a corner. Liberty rebounds off the turnbuckles; Cheetah meets her with the Heart Punch, then pins her. (11:08) *Dog Collar Match* -------------------- "Psycho" Vicki V (w/ Dr. Insane and Christy Cooper) v. Mary Williams (w/ Crimson) -------------------- Williams dodges a leaping shoulderblock. She plants Vicki on the mat with a front-layout suplex, taking control of the match. Vicki shortly takes her down with a tackle. She gets up, then meets a standing Williams with a jumping elbow thrust, taking charge. Williams very shortly nails her with a kick to the midsection, then headbutts her, resuming her assault. Vicki shortly blocks a monkey flip. She executes a spinebuster; Williams kicks out in two. Vicki takes her down with a jumping elbow thrust and covers; one, two, shoulder up. Vicki picks Williams up, then plants her on the mat with a running powerslam, only to get a two count. Vicki punishes Williams with a diving headbutt, retaking control of the match. After ninety seconds Williams ducks a leaping clothesline. She counters a spinning powerbomb with spinning headscissors. Both women get back up; Williams executes a leg lariat, going back on the offensive. After 2 1/2 minutes she puts Vicki on a turnbuckle, then climbs up. Vicki blocks the Big Dipper with a headbutt, knocking Williams back to the mat and pulling Vicki down with her. Both women get back on their feet slowly. Williams shoves Vicki back into the corner. Vicki walks back out, then sidesteps a forearm to the back. She punches Williams in the stomach, turning the match back around. After a fast-and-furious 4 1/2 minutes, Williams blocks a pick-up. She goes over the top rope, then reenters the ring with a slingshot bodyblock; one, two, kickout. Williams keeps Vicki down with a belly-to-back suplex, turning the match around again. After an equally intense 5 minutes, Vicki blocks an armdrag with a lariat. She throws Williams out of the ring, then follows her out. She takes Williams down with an earringer, then throws her back into the ring. Williams blocks a fireman's carry, then executes her own. Vicki gets up and blocks a belly-to-back, then steps into a corner and gets on the second turnbuckle. She executes an awkward flying sunset flip, pinning Williams. (1:27) -------------------- Big Norma Hudson (w/ Mr. Canuck and Haley "Comet" Hanson) v. Angel Hunter (w/ Janet Strong) -------------------- Both women lock up. Hudson drives Hunter to the ropes, then breaks off and runs to the far ones. She nails Hunter with a shoulderblock, taking control of the match. Hunter shortly counters a legsweep faceslam with a faceslam of her own, taking charge. Hudson shortly blocks a standing wristlock, then takes Hunter down with an inverted backbreaker. After a minute Hunter nails Hudson with a chop to the neck, going back on the attack. Hudson soon blocks headscissors, then picks herself up off the mat. She puts Hunter into a bearhug. Hunter breaks the hold after 9 seconds; Hudson dodges a drop-kick to the knee. She keeps Hudson down with a Japanese armdrag takedown, then puts Hunter back into a bearhug, turning things back around. After 2 1/2 minutes Hunter sidesteps a forearm to the back. She throws Hudson out of the ring, then rests while the ref counts; one .. two .. three .. four .. five . Hudson reenters the ring. Hunter executes an inside cradle; one, two, kickout. Hunter slaps Hudson, then puts her in headscissors, turning the match back around. She shortly reverses a whip to a corner. Hudson walks back out, then blocks an inside cradle. Hunter gets back up and blocks a headbutt. She applies a standing wristlock; Hudson uses the ropes to break the hold. Hunter punishes her with a forearm blow, then she blocks a pick-up. Hudson dodges a drop-kick to the knee; Hunter slips away from a forearm to the back as she gets up. She kicks Hudson's legs out, then goes to the apron and hammers her with a slingshot legdrop, going back on the attack. After 2 1/2 minutes Hudson ducks a spinning leg lariat. Hunter picks herself up, moving away from another forearm to the back; Hudson barely avoids a kneelift. She sends Hunter to a corner, then runs in shoulder-first and rams into Hunter, turning the match around again. After 2 minutes she puts Hunter in leg scissors. Hunter breaks the hold after 32 seconds, then rolls out of the ring to the apron. She gets back on her feet, then reenters the ring via a slingshot legdrop, retaking control of the match. Hunter soon blocks an inside cradle, then runs to the ropes and pummels Hunter with the Hudson Bay Splash, pinning her.
