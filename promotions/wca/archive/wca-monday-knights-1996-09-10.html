WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION MONDAY KNIGHTS Live from Omaha, Nebraska 9/9/96 -----Main Event----- "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln v. "Destroyer" Dara Holt (Dumpster Match) Shadow Warriors v. Delicious Dolls Screen Sirens v. The Scandalous Ones Warhead v. "Psycho" Vicki V <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA wrestling action> <Wide angle view of the inside of the arena shows a capacity crowd. The ring announcer, timekeepers, and other officials are walking down the aisle to get ready for the show. The camera focuses on John Birmingham, the television announcer.> Birmingham: "Welcome fans to another installment of Monday Knights! Tonight we'll see another battle in the war between the Click and the Evil Empire, this time in the form of 'the Intimidator' Amber Axeln v. 'Destroyer' Dara Holt in a Dumpster match. These two ladies have no love and very little respect for each other; I imagine this will be a brutal battle to the end. Also tonight, the Delicious Dolls take on the Shadow Warriors. As fans know, the Dolls are on a comeback as of late; time will tell if they can keep their winning streak. In more tag team action, the Scandalous Ones go head-to-head with the Screen Sirens, and "Psycho" Vicki V returns from her break tonight to face one-half of the Top Guns, Warhead. But first, as we promised we're going to show you the end of last Saturday's main event tag team match between Darkness Inc. and the Scandalous Ones. Let's take you to that tape." <Tape picks up where Rampage left off. The words "Rampage - St. Paul, MN appears briefly at the bottom of the screen.> Birmingham's voice: "Rage reverses a whip to the ropes. Semiramis ducks a clothesline, then comes back with her own. She throws Rage out of the ring and goes to the floor. She punches Rage into the midsection - Rage whips her into the guardrail. She reenters the ring, followed by Semiramis. Rage nails her with an enzuigiri, then executes a spinnning DDT. She goes for the pin; one, two, Semiramis puts her foot on the bottom rope. Rage makes the tag to Black. They nail Semiamis with a double enzuilariato. The Mistress enters and throws Rage out. "Black ducks her lariat, then throws her out of the ring. She covers Semiramis; one, two, thr-shoulder up! Was Semiramis asleep? She's getting up now as Black goes to the apron. Black nails her with a springboard dropkick and makes the tag to Rage. The Mistress enters and throws Black out of the ring, then leaves herself. Semiramis is back up and runs into the ropes. She hits Rage with a clothesline and covers her; one, two, thr-kickout! Semiramis makes the tag to the Mistress, then bodyslams Rage. The Mistress follows up with a spinning DDT. "Rage slowly gets back up - she counters a backspin DDT with a bely-to-belly, but only gets a two count. Rage complains about a slow count - the Mistress nails her with a dropkick to the knee! Rage goes to it; the Mistress decks her with a lariat. She applies an armlock leglock. Black makes the save! Rage gets up and blocks a kick to the leg. She goes to the top turnbuckle, then executes a flying clothe - the Mistress ducks it! She spins around and puts Rage in a bow-and-arrow. Black makes the save again! Both women stand up. The Mistress runs into the ropes. Both women clothesline each other! "White begins his count; one, two, three, four, the Mistress stirs, six, seven, so does Rage, nine, the Mistress puts Rage in an armlock leglock. She quickly lets go, then pulls Rage up. She nails Rage with the Kiss of Death! The Mistress covers her; one, two, three!" RA: "Ladies and gentlemen, the winners of the match: in 24 minutes, Darkness, Inc.!" <White raises the Mistress' and Semiramis' arms in victory. Darkness, Inc. then heads back to the back, shortly followed by the Scandalous Ones as the house lights rise.> <Camera focuses again on Birmingham.> Birmingham: "The Scandalous Ones lose their first match back, effectively shutting them out of the Lords of the Ring tournament. At the same time, much has been made about the fact that it took the contenders for the Tag Team belts almost the entire time limit to get past this team, implying that they don't have a chance against the active Battle Kats. We'll see what happens this Sunday. Up next is another member of the Posse of Punishment, "Psycho" Vicki V. Let's go now to the ring." ===== ------------------------- Warhead (w/ Snakebite) v. "Psycho" Vicki V (w/ the Dream Master) ------------------------- Both women lock up, then Warhead takes control of the match with a faceslam. Vicki shortly evens the match up with a cross-body block. Warhead kicks out of the ensuing cover and stands toe-to-toe with her. She shortly retakes control with a shoulderblock. After two minutes she attempts a savate kick. Vicki ducks it; Warhead attempts a sunset flip. Vicki hooks her legs and sits down on her. Warhead raises up in two, reversing the cover. Vicki kicks out in two; Warhead applies an armbar, resuming her attack. Vicki shortly starts to build some momentum. She executes a brain buster, turning the match around. She shortly goes for a bridging back suplex, but Warhead counters with a headlock takedown, going back on the offensive. Vicki quickly ends it with a spinning mule kick. Warhead immediately begins to build a comeback. She gradually gains control of the match thrugh a series of backdrops and bodyblocks. Vicki slows her down with a front slam, firmly taking charge. A minute later Warhead sends Vicki into the ropes, then catches her in a spinning backbreaker on the rebound, turning the match back around. Vicki shortly in desperation attempts to apply the Claw, but Warhead ducks it. She picks Vicki up and puts her on a turnbuckle, then climbs up and nails her with a double underhook superplex. She covers Vicki, but can only get a two count. She punches Vicki as punishment, continuing her onslaught. Warhead shortly climbs the turnbuckles again, but Vicki throws her to the mat. Warhead gets back up and runs into the ropes. She DDT's Vicki, then hammers her with a somersault splash. Warhead pulls Vicki up by the neck. She executes a German suplex, pinning Vicki. (13:06) ------------------------- Screen Sirens ("Hollywood" Hyatt Tyler and "Bright Lights" Brandi Wilcox) (w/ Wildcat) v. The Scandalous Ones (Shelley Rage and Jenny Black) (w/ the Dream Master) ------------------------- Tyler and Black start. Both women lock up, then Tyler takes control of the match with a gutwrench suplex. Black shortly begins to even the match up with neck-scissors. She nails Tyler with a tiger driver, taking control. She shortly tags out to Rage, then nails Tyler with a flying shoulderblock. Tyler gets back up and avoids a backflip cradle, evening the match. She sends Rage into the ropes; Rage comes back with a backdrop. Tyler stumbles over the top rope. Rage goes to the floor and knocks Tyler into the ringsteps. Tyler gets back up and goes up them, then goes to the top turnbuckle. Rage climbs up on the apron and punches her in the midsection, sending her back into the ring the hard way. Tyler tags out to Wilcox as Rage reenters. Rage greets her with an earringer, continuing her attack. Wilcox shortly turns the match around with a flying spinning leg lariat. She soon goes for a springboard dropkick, but Rage sidesteps it, then tags out to Black. Black executes neck-scissors, then picks Wilcox up. Wilcox blocks being put on a top turnbuckle, then blocks a dragon suplex. She sends Black into the ropes, then hits her with a shoulderblock, regaining her momentum. Black soon ends her offensive with a monkey flip, turning the match back around. A minute later Wilcox turns the match back around with a double-axhandle chop. She shortly attempts a tackle, but Black dodges it and hits her with a forearm to the back, retaking control. Wlicox quickly turns the match back around with a kick. She tags out to Tyler. Tyler executes a sunset backslide; Rage makes the save. Tyler keeps the pressure on with an earringer. A minute later Black backdrops her and tags out to Rage. They send Tyler into the ropes, then double her over with a double kick to the midsection. Rage attemtps to follow up with a double reverse chinlock, but Tyler rakes her face instead. She shortly executes a sunset backslide, almost pinning Rage. Rage gets back up and chops her, evening the match. Both women start trading blows. Tyler shortly ends the stand-off with a double chickenwing. Rage soon turns the match around with a short clothesline. After three minutes she nails Tyler with a flying dropkick. Black goes to a neutral top turnbuckle as Rage climbs to the opposite one. They hammer Tyler with the Double Superfly Splash, then Rage goes for the pin. Tyler kicks out in two; Rage rewards her with an enzuigiri. She climbs the turnbuckles again; Tyler throws her to the mat. She nails Rage with an elbowsmash, turning the match around. Rage soon evens the match back up with a bodyslam. Both women stand toe-to-toe. Tyler soon ends the stalemate with a spinning backbreaker. Rage shortly escapes her onslaught and tags out to Black. Black enters and nails Tyler with an elbow, turning the match back around. She shortly applies short arm scissors. Tyler submits after 10 seconds. (16:42) ------------------------- Shadow Warriors (Valkyrie and Vonya) (w/ the Prince and Night Crawler) v. Delicious Dolls (Double D - Debbie Diamond and Donna Star) (w/ Tammi Cooper) ------------------------- Valkyrie and Star start. Both women lock up, then Valkyrie sends Star into the ropes. Star rebounds, then applies a cobra clutch, taking control of the match. After two minutes she goes for a short lariat. Valkyrie blocks it; Vonya enters the ring to make it two-on-one. They whip Star into the ropes. Star comes back with a double clothesline. She puts Valkyrie in a full nelson, regaining her momentum. She shortly applies an armlock leglock. Vonya makes the save; Valkyrie tags out to her. They send Star into the ropes, then nail her with a double kick to the midsection. Vonya shoves her to the ropes again; Star comes back with her own kick,. Vonya chokes her, turning the match around. She soon attempts to run Star's face along the top rope, but Star blocks it. She lunges at Diamond to make the tag; Diamond kicks Vonya in the midsection. Vonya blocks a legsweep facelsam, then nails Diamond with a swinging neckbreaker, taking control. Diamond soon kicks her in the head, then rolls her up in an inside cradle. Vonya kicks out in two. Diamond puts her in a surfboard, going on the offensive. After two minutes she attempts a flying kneedrop. Vonya rolls out of the way and throws Diamond out of the ring. Diamond hobbles along the side of the ring, then rolls back in. She gets up and attempts a superkick. Vonya ducks it and sends her into the turnbuckle. Diamond comes back and rocks her with an elbow, retaking control. She shortly attempts a Sammartino backbreaker, but Vonya blocks it and nails her with an over-the-shoulder stomachbreaker. Diamond rolls onto her knees and begs off. Vonya rewards her with a forearm smash. Diamond shortly nails her with a backspin DDT, taking control. Star shortly enters the ring. She and Diamond nail Vonya with a double double-axhandle chop. Vonya lunges at Valkyrie and makes the tag. They both punch Diamond, then Valkyrie takes her down with a backh heel kick. She shortly tags out to Vonya; Star enters the ring and boots Valkyrie out, then nails Vonya with a lariat. Diamond sends Vonya into the ropes, then nails her with a spinning leg lariat. She follows up with a somersault slam and pins Vonya. (11:18) ===== Birmingham: "The Delicious Dolls win another match, continuing their recent winning streak. Up next, fans, is our main event. As I said earlier, this is another battle between the Click and the Evil Empire, a sort of prelude to that nine-woman, three-team War Games match at the Lords of the Ring. Tonight, though, 'the Intimidator' Amber Axeln will take on 'Destroyer' Dara Holt in a Dumpster match. There are no countouts, no disqualifications, no time-limits in this match. Anything goes, and probably will. You can see them roll the dumpster down to the ring now - and they need some Arm and Hammer! Whew! Pity the poor soul who gets dumped in there. Let's go now to the ring." RA: "Ladies and gentlemen, tonight's main event is a Dumpster match. To win, a wrestler must put her opponent in the dumpster and close the lid.
