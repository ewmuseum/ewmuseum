None better than WCA

10 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
According to the FAQ, we are not supposed to say that this league is better than that league. So, I will skirt the FAQ by saying that there is no better wrestling e-fed around than the WCA, run by [name withheld]. I have joined more than 20 of them in three to four months and out of those 20, only Sam's is still running and is hot as a firecracker, even in these summer months. He sends out e-mails not three or four times a week. Try three or four times A DAY! That is the key to a successful league, as we all know. He puts a lot of time into it and it shows. And he has the best ladies wrestling around. If you want to try to get in, check out the Theater of War that is still in this newsgroup and e-mail him from there. Maybe you can get in -- last I heard he has a waiting list. But [name withheld] doesn't get nearly the ink he deserves -- so here's someone giving him some.
