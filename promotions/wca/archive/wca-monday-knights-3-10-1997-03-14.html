WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION MONDAY KNIGHTS 3/10/97 Live from the Lake Arena in Springfield, Missouri -----Main Event----- =Television Title Match= Freedom v. Karen "Bugs' Huffman Helena "High Kick" Knightsbridge v. "Nasty Girl" Amber Jane Donna Star v. Nadia Williams Hooked on Destruction v. New Dawn "Dominatress" Kathy Lynn v. Kazumi Hayakawa <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.> <Fireworks explode as the camera shows the crimson WCA logo on the white ring canvas. The camera descends rapidly, then levels off facing the aisle. The camera swerves left, panning out over the capacity crowd and circling the ring, ending its focus on the iridescent three-dimensional WCA letters bracketing the entrance to the backstage area. The referee for the first match heads to the ring.> -------------------- "Dominatress" Kathy Lynn (w/ "Nasty Girl"Amber Jane) v. Kazumi Hayakawa -------------------- Lynn enters the ring via a flying lariat, going on the offensive immediately. Hayakawa shortly blocks a snap mare, then sends Lynn to the ropes. She nails Lynn with a kick to the thigh, then Lynn reverses another Irish whip. She jumps a shoulderblock, then counters a pick-up with a small package; Hayakawa reverses it in two. Lynn kicks out in two; both women get back up. Hayakawa nails Lynn with a thrust kick to the head, then sends her to the ropes. Lynn knocks Hayakawa down with a shoulderblock, then applies standing short-arm scissors, retaking control. She shortly catches Hayakawa in a backslide, pinning her. (2:28) -------------------- Hooked on Destruction ("Psycho" Vicki V and Christy Cooper) (w/ Dr. Insane and the Dream Master) v. New Dawn ("The Intimidator" Amber Axeln and Valkyrie) -------------------- Axeln and Valkyrie dump Cooper over the top rope, then nail Vicki with a double back heel kick. Axeln keeps Vicki down with an enzuilariato. Valkyrie heads to a corner as Vicki gets up, then puts her back on the mat with a flying bulldog, taking control of the match. She soon tags in Axeln; Cooper gets cut off as she enters, allowing New Dawn to nail Vicki with a double kick to the midsection. They send Vicki to the ropes, then nail her with a double fist to the abdomen. Valkyrie goes to their corner as Axeln runs to the ropes; Vicki ducks neck-scissors. She nails Axeln with a lariat, turning things around. Axeln shortly blocks a flying karate chop with a fist to the midsection, then tags in Valkyrie. Cooper rushes into the ring and throws Axeln out, then returns to her corner as Valkyrie meets a flying Vicki with a fist to the midsection, retaking control of the match. Vicki very shortly ducks a flying clothesline. Valkyrie ducks a jumping elbow thrust; Vicki sidesteps a handspring elbow. She sidesteps a cartwheel kick; Valkyrie blocks an elbowsmash. She legsweeps Vicki, then goes to the top rope and hammers her with a flying headbutt, resuming her assault. After ninety seconds Vicki blocks a sunset flip with a punch. She keeps Valkyrie on the mat with an earringer. Valkyrie stands up; Vicki nails her with a jumping elbow thrust. Valkyrie stumbles back, then blocks another earringer. She tags in Axeln. Cooper gets cut off as New Dawn send Vicki to the ropes and double-backdrop her. Valkyrie leaves as Vicki pops back up and reverses another Irish whip, then nails Axeln with an elbow, taking charge. Axeln soon blocks a pick-up, then puts Vicki into a front facelock, going back on the offensive. After ninety seconds Vicki counters a headlock with a back suplex, turning things around once again. Axeln very shortly counters Vicki's headlock with her own back suplex, retaking control. She shortly tags in Valkyrie. Cooper gets cut off once again as New Dawn sends Vicki to the ropes and doubles her over with a double-punch to the stomach. They deck Vicki with a double back heel kick, then Axeln returns to her corner. Vicki gets back on her feet; Valkyrie meets her with a handspring elbow, continuing the assault. Vicki shortly blocks a chokehold and tags in Cooper. Axeln enters the ring and throws Vicki out, then Cooper ducks her enzuilariato. Axeln leaves as Valkyrie counters Cooper's reverse cradle with a sit-down, only to get a two count. Axeln enters the ring; Cooper blocks her side suplex. The ref forces Axeln out as Cooper takes Valkyrie down with a snap mare. She follows up with a belly-to-back, taking control of the match. After a minute she runs to the ropes; Valkyrie meets her with a kick, then tags out to Axeln. Axeln executes a fisherman suplex; Cooper kicks out in two and gets back up. She reverses a whip to a corner, then runs in shoulder-first, nailing Axeln. Cooper sends her to the opposite corner; Axeln rebounds off the turnbuckles and shuffles back into the ring. Cooper takes her down with a high cross body, only to get a two count. Both women get up; Axeln hits Cooper with a swinging punch, then follows up with a back heel kick, going back on the attack. Cooper soon turns a headlock into a back suplex. Vicki enters the ring as Axeln gets up; they send her to the ropes, then stop her with a double kick to the midsection. Valkyrie enters and throws Vicki out, then she and Axeln double-punch Cooper in the stomach. Valkyrie leaves as Cooper executes another back suplex. She puts Axeln in headscissors, retaking control of the match. Axeln soon blocks an inside cradle, then nails Cooper with a kick to the head, turning things back around again. She soon tags out to Valkyrie; both women send Cooper to the ropes, then double-backdrop her. Axeln returns to her corner as Valkyrie runs to the far ropes. Cooper gets up and follows her in; Valkyrie backdrops her out of the ring. Cooper lands hard, then picks herself up and reenters at the eight count. Valkyrie nails her with a back heel kick, continuing the offensive. She soon executes the Valkyrian Storm, then pins Cooper. Nadia Williams (w/ Red) -------------------- Both women circle each other, then lock up. Williams executes a single-leg takedown. She sends Star to a corner, then runs in and pummels her with the Stinger Splash, taking control of the match. After ninety seconds Star ducks a jumping elbow thrust. She executes a bridging back suplex; Williams kicks out in two. Star applies a leg hammerlock, turning things around. She shortly sends Williams to the ropes; Williams returns with a clothesline, resuming her assault. Williams shortly runs to the ropes; Star nails her with a kick. She executes an ipponzei; one, two, kickout. Star applies an armlock hammerlock; Williams goes to the ropes to break the hold. She takes Star down with a rana; Star easily kicks out. Williams throws her out of the ring. One .. two .. three .. four .. five .. Star reenters the ring. Williams takes her down with a clothesline, resuming her assault. She soon runs to the ropes; Star hits her with a clothesline, then puts her in a bearhug, going back on the attack. Williams shortly blocks a figure-four sleeper. She nails Star with a clothesline, starting a new offensive. She shortly whips Star to the ropes; Star rebounds and takes Williams down with a kick. Star drops and hooks a leg, only to get a two count. Star accuses the ref of bias as Williams picks herself up. Star puts Williams into an abdominal stretch, turning the match around. After 3 minutes Star applies a standing wristlock. Williams breaks the hold after 15 seconds, then whips Star to the ropes. Star rebounds and applies a headlock; Williams turns it into a back suplex, turning the match back around. Star shortly ducks a flying spinning leg lariat. Williams picks herself up; Star sends her to the ropes. She grabs Williams by the head and hurls her through the opposite ropes to the floor. Star steps through and joins her on the concrete. Williams picks Star up and throws her back into the ring, then enters and takes Star down with a rana; one, two, kickout. Williams heads back to the corner, then nails Star with a flying spinning leg lariat, resuming her offensive. After 2 minutes Star ducks a jumping elbow thrust. She executes an ipponzei; one, two, kickout. Both women get back up; Star applies a bearhug, turning the match back around again. After 4 minutes Williams prevents an armlock hammerlock. She takes Star down with a slap, then goes to a corner and leaps to the top turnbuckle. Williams nails Star with a flying spinning leg lariat. Both women get back on their feet; Williams sends Star to the ropes. Star returns with a takeover suplex, resuming her assault. She shortly runs to the ropes. Williams misses with an elbow, spins around and backdrops Williams out of the ring. One .. two .. three .. four .. five .. six, Star reenters the ring. Williams meets her with a drop-kick to the knee. She backs up as Star stands up again, then takes her down with a handspring elbow. Star gets back on her feet and executes a small package, but only gets a two count. Star executes a snap mare, then nails Williams with a clothesline, resuming her assault. She soon sends Williams to a corner, then runs in shoulder-first. Williams lunges out of the way; Star nails the ringpost. Williams grabs her singlet and yanks her back into the ring; Star falls backward to the mat. Williams 'slices' her throat, then hammers Star with the Guillotine. She hooks a leg, pinning Star.
