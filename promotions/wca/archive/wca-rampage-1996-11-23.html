WCA Rampage 11/23

23 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WORLD CYBERWRESLTING ASSOCIATION RAMPAGE Live from Bonn, Germany 11/23/96 -----Main Event----- <Tag Team Titles Submission Match> Darkness, Inc. v. Nasty Tracy Turner / Christy Cooper <World Title Match> "Psycho" Vicki V v. R "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln v. Janet Strong (non-title match) Lovely Liz Strohlheim v. Helena "High Kick" Knightsbridge Shadow Warriors v. Magic Mountain Warriors Tammi Cooper v. Freedom Flower Power v. She-Devil ---------- Flower Power v. She-Devil (w/ Vinnie Gambino) ---------- Both women lock up, then Power chokes She-Devil. She lets go at the four count and runs into the ropes. She-Devil stops her with a shoulderblock, then hammers her with a gutwrench suplex, taking control of the match. She shortly runs into the ropes, then ducks a clothesline. She misses with an elbow, then rebounds again and ducks the Incense and Peppermints. Power heads for the top rope and nails She-Devil with a flying karate chop, taking charge. She-Devil shortly reverses a whip to a corner. She runs in; Power blocks her with her boot. She-Devil reels back, then blocks a dragon suplex. Power counters a powerslam with a lariat, then applies an STF, resuming her offensive. She-Devil soon rolls away from a stomp. She gives the crowd the middle finger, then runs into the ropes. Power knocks her down with a shoulderblock and climbs the turnbuckles. She-Devil gets back on her feet and ducks a flying clothesline. She plants Power on the mat with a front-layout suplex. Power slowly gets back up, then counters a powerslam with a lariat. She climbs the turnbuckles, then keeps She-Devil down with a flying shoulderblock, regaining her momentum. She-Devil shortly catches her in a powerslam; one, two, thr-kickout. She-Devil salutes the crowd with her middle finger again, then nails Power with a spinning DDT, turning the match around. Power shortly counters a fisherman suplex with a small package; She-Devil kicks out in two. Power executes a backslide; She-Devil kicks out in two. Power drives her knee into She-Devil's back, retaking control. She-Devil shortly blocks a chokehold, then runs Power's face along the top rope. She bites Power's face, then nails her with a DDT, turning things around. Power shortly blocks a headbutt and nails She-Devil with a knee to the midsection, turning things back around. She-Devil soon sends her to the ropes, then takes her down with neck-scissors, going back on the offensive. Power shortly blocks a full nelson. She-Devil blocks a choke against the ropes; Power ducks neck-scissors. She quickly executes an over-the-shoulder stomachbreaker, retaking control of the match. She-Devil soon counters another stomachbreaker with an elbowsmash. She takes Power down with a front-layout suplex, going back on the attack. After a minute Power blocks a facerake. She nails She-Devil with a double underhook piledriver, retaking control. After a minute She-Devil ducks a flying clothesline. She hits Power with a forearm to the back, then nails her with a DDT, turning things around again. She shortly whips Power to the ropes. Power hooks the ropes, then surprises She-Devil with the Incense and Peppermints. She covers, but can only get a two count. Power puts She-Devil in a full nelson, slowing the match down. She-Devil shortly blocks a gutwrench suplex and runs into the ropes. Power decks her with the Incense and Peppermints, then pins her. (14:35) ---------- Tammi Cooper (w/ Double D - Debbie Diamond and Donna Star) v. Freedom ---------- Both women lock up, then Freedom ducks a short clothesline. She sends Cooper to the ropes, then nails her with a clothesline, taking control of the match. Cooper shortly blocks a bodyslam. She throws Freedom out of the ring. Freedom reenters at the seven count. She ducks a flying clothesline. Cooper gets back on her feet and reverses a whip to the ropes. She ducks a clothesline, rebounds and ducks another. Freedom straightens up, then throws off the Bulldog. Cooper picks herself up and sends Freedom to the ropes. Freedom comes back with a shoulderblock, retaking control of the match. Cooper very shortly counters a bodyslam with a small package. Freedom kicks out in two; Cooper plants her on the mat with a powerbomb, retaking control. She soon climbs to the top rope, but Freedom throws her to the mat. She pulls Cooper up; Cooper reverses a whip to the ropes. Both women get hit with a double clothesline; Freedom gets up first and nails Cooper with an enzuigiri, taking control of the match. Cooper shortly catches her in another powerbomb, retaking control. After 2 minutes Freedom avoids a diving shoulderlbock, then keeps Cooper down with a headlock takedown. Cooper gets back up; Freedom punches her in the midsection, turning the match around. Cooper very shortly reverses a whip to a corner and charges in. She smashes into Cooper, then nails her with a short clothesline, going back on the attack. Freedom shortly reverses a whip to a corner. Cooper staggers back out; Freedom executes a Northern Lights suplex, turning things back around. Cooper shortly reverses another whip to a corner, then nails Freedom with an enzuigiri, retaking control. She shortly whips Freedom into a corner and charges in. Freedom moves out of the way, then executes an arm drag takedown, taking charge. After a minute Cooper nails Freedom with a jumping side kick, going back on the offensive. During the next two minutes Freedom slowly evens up the match; both women are soon trading blows. Freedom rolls away from a flying splash, then executes a spinning elbow drop, starting a new assault. After 2 minutes she executes the 4th of July and pins Cooper. Magic Mountain Warriors (Mai and Mariko) ---------- Valkyrie and Mariko start. Both women lock up, then Valkyrie breaks off and climbs the turnbuckles. She executes flying headscissors, taking control of the match. Mariko very shortly blocks a kick to the leg and runs into the ropes. She puts Valkyrie in an abdominal stretch, taking charge. Valkyrie shortly counters a springboard rana with a double leg-hook; Mariko kicks out i two. Valkyrie scoops her up; Mariko blocks being set on a turnbuckle. Valkyrie settles for a chokehold. She lets go in five; the ref warns her. Mariko uses the distraction to nail Valkyrie with a jumping DDT, then tags in Mai as Valkyrie slowly get up. Both women nail Valkyrie with a double fist to the midsection, then then send her to the ropes. Valkyrie counters a double backdrop with a double headsmash, retaking control. She tags out to Vonya; Vonya chokes Mai against the ropes. She lets go in four; Mai blocs a sleeper and powerbombs Vonya. Vonya gets back up and puts Mai in a cobra clutch, regaining her momentum. After a minute Mai executes an elbowsmash, retaking control of the match. She tags out to Mariko. Mariko picks Vonya up; Vonya blocks being set on a turnbuckle. Mariko settles for a piledriver, turning things around. Vonya very shortly rolls away from a somersault legdrop, then rakes Mariko's face. She puts Mariko in a sleeper, resuming her assault. She shortly tags in Valkyrie. Both women hammer Mariko with a double fistdrop, then Mai enters the ring and throws Vonya out. She leaves the ring as Mariko gets up. Mariko executes a high cross body; Valkyrie kicks out in two. Mariko puts her in a full nelson, taking control of the match. She soon whips Valkyrie to the ropes, then ducks an enzuigiri to the face. She tags out to Mai; Vonya enters the ring and boots Mariko out. She and Valkyrie double-backdrop Mai, then Valkyrie covers her. Mai kicks out in two and stands up; Valkyrie rewards her with a rolling kick, resuming the attack. Mai shortly blocks a chokehold and tags out to Mariko. Mariko comes in with a flying clothesline, turning things around. She shortly tags out to Mai; Mai tags out to Mariko before Valkyrie can make a move. Mariko nails Valkyrie with a dropkick to the knee. Valkyrie goes down on it, then gets up and chokes Mariko, retaking control. After ninety seconds she tags out to Vonya. Mariko ducks a spinning mule kick and puts Vonya in a double chickenwing. Valkyrie makes the save; Vonya chokes Mariko against the ropes, retaking control. Mariko shortly tags out to Mai. Valkyrie enters the ring and nails Mai with a fist to the midsection, then Vonya rakes Mai's face, continuing the onslaught. She shortly tags out to Valkyrie. Valkyrie executes an enzuigiri to the face. Mai gets back up and reverses a whip to a corner. Valkyrie charges back out into a kick to the midsection. Mai tags out to Mariko; Mariko sends Valkyrie to the ropes. She nails Valkyrie with a jumping side kick, going on the attack. Valkyrie very shortly turns things around with a dragon suplex. After ninety seconds Mariko escapes to tag out to Mai. Mai puts Valkyrie in a double chickenwing, turning the match around. After ninety seconds Mai catches Valkyrie in a Doctor bomb and pins her. (15:16) ---------- Lovely Liz Strohlheim (w/ Karen "Bugs" Huffman and Sweet Sue) v. Helena "High Kick" Knightsbridge ---------- Both women lock up, then Strohlheim nails Knightsbridge with a legsweep faceslam, taking control of the match. Knightsbridge very shortly turns things around with an enzuilariato. After 2 minutes she runs into the ropes. Sue trips Knightsbridge up; Strohlheim blocks a headbutt. She executes a brain buster, then covers Knightsbridge with the Schoolgirl Pin. Knightsbridge forcefully kicks out in two, then sits up, rubbing the back of her head. She stands up; Strohlheim slaps her. Knightsbridge slaps her back, then punches her. Strohlheim ducks an enzuilariato and executes an earringer. She follows up with a side suplex. Knightsbridge slowly stands up, then blocks a legsweep faceslam. She executes a lariat, gaining the advantage. After 3 minutes Strohlheim sidesteps a kneelift. She nails Knightsbridge with a bulldog, turning the match around. After 3 more minutes she slaps Knightsbridge. Knightsbridge returns the same, then punches Strohlheim. She follows up with a kick to the head, turning the match back around. After ninety seconds Strohlheim ducks another kick to the head. She executes a savate kick, retaking control of the match. A minute later Knightsbridge reverses a whip to the ropes. She knocks Strohlheim down with an elbow to the face and covers her. Strohlheim kicks out in two; Knightsbridge puts her in a Boston crab, retaking control. Strohlheim shortly reverses a bodyslam, pinning Knightsbridge. (13:41) *Non-title match* ---------- "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln v. Janet Strong ---------- Both women lock up, then Axeln counters a headlock takedown with a back suplex. She hits Strong with a forearm to the back. Strong gets back up and applies an armbar, taking control of the match. Axeln soon blocks a takeover suplex and nails Strong with an enzuigiri. She pulls Strong to a corner, then puts her on the canvas with the Doombomb and covers her. Strong kicks out in the last second; Axeln pulls Strong around, then applies a kneelock, retaking control. After 2 minutes she sends Strong to the ropes. Strong comes back with a kick, taking Axeln down. Axeln blocks a Boston crab and gets back up. Strong blocks a roundhouse punch and executes a German suplex. Axeln kicks out in two; Strong applies a figure-four sleeper. Axeln breaks the hold in 5 seconds, then hammers Strong in a side suplex, retaking control. Strong shortly catches her in a belly-to-belly suplex; Axeln barely kicks out in time. Strong follows up with a short lariat, going back on the offensive She shortly sends Axeln to the ropes. Axeln counters a headlock takedown with a back suplex, retaking control. Strong quickly puts her back on the defensive with an enzuigiri. Axeln shortly blocks a scorpion deathlock, then gets up and runs into the ropes. She catches Strong in a belly-to-belly suplex, going back on the attack. After a minute she runs into the ropes. Strong backdrops her, then catches her in a German suplex. Axeln kicks out in two; Strong executes an enzuigiri, turning things around. She shortly puts Axeln in the Bearhug. Axeln breaks the hold after 11 seconds; Strong angrily throws her out of the ring. She joins Axeln on the Floor; Axeln counters a back suplex with a cross-body block. She reenters the ring, followed by Strong. Axeln blocks a back heel kick; Strong blocks a chokehold. She executes a belly-to-back suplex, going back on the attack. She shortly catches Axeln in a belly-to-belly suplex, pinning her.
