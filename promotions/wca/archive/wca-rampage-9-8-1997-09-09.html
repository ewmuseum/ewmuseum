WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION (http.// kessel.simplenet.com/WCA/ ) RAMPAGE Live from Toronto, Quebec 9/4/97 -----Main Event----- Shannon Stevens v. Shelly Rage "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln v. Joanne Watson (Best of 7 Match 1) The Scandal Sheet Megan van Drake v. Double J The Assassin v. Liberty "Nasty Girl" Amber Jane v. Janet Strong (Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.) (Fireworks explode as the camera shows the crimson WCA logo on the white ring canvas. The camera descends rapidly, then levels off facing the aisle. The camera swerves left, panning out over the capacity crowd and circling the ring, ending its focus on the iridescent three-dimensional WCA letters bracketing the entrance to the backstage area. The referee for the first match heads to the ring.) ---------- “Nasty Girl” Amber Jane (w/ Dr. Insane, “the Tax Collector” John Blair, “Dominatress” Kathy Lynn, and Valkyrie) v. Janet Strong (w/ Angel Hunter) Referee: Mark White ---------- Both women lock up. Strong throws Jane out of the ring, then she follows her to the floor. Strong slams Jane’s head on the guardrail twice as White begins the count. 2. 3. Strong whips Jane to the side ropes. Jane pitches headfirst over the guardrail into the stands. Strong walks over as Jane quickly gets up; Jane executes a springboard shoulderblock back into the ringside area. 4. Jane gets on the apron, then Strong rolls away from a flying splash. Strong grabs ahold of Jane and throws her down the side area into the ringsteps. She walks over and hauls Jane to her feet, then she slams Jane’s head onto the apron. She decks Jane with a swinging punch and reenters the ring. Jane picks herself up, then she goes back up the steps into the ring. Strong backs away from a drop-kick to the knee, then she goes for the pin; Jane kicks out in two. Strong plants Jane on the mat with a gutwrench suplex and makes a cover; Jane again kicks out in two. Jane rolls out of the ring to the apron, then she gets up and goes to a corner. She climbs the turnbuckles, then she takes Strong down with a flying clothesline, taking control of the match. Strong soon blocks a palm blow, then she runs to the ropes and knocks Jane down with a shoulderblock, going on the offensive. Jane very shortly rolls away from a standing moonsault and gets up. Strong picks herself up; Jane nails her with a drop-kick to the knee, resuming her gameplan. She soon runs to the ropes; Strong takes her down with a hard kick, then she goes for the pin. Jane gets her shoulder up in two. Both women get back up, then Strong executes an atomic drop, going back on the offensive. Jane shortly blocks a flying karate chop with a fist to the midsection, then she takes Strong down with a handspring elbow, retaking control of the match. After 2 1/2 minutes Strong picks Jane up and sets her on a turnbuckle facing the pro-Henchmen crowd. Strong climbs up, then she executes a belly-to-back superplex, turning the match around again. After another 2 1/2 minutes Strong sends Jane tot he ropes; Jane rebounds and backdrops Strong, then she makes a cover; Strong kicks out in two. Jane stands and goes to a corner. She slips through the ropes as Strong gets back up; Jane comes off the top with a flying clothesline, turning the match back around. Strong soon takes her down with a legsweep faceslam, going back on the offensive again. She shortly reverses an Irish whip; Jane backdrops her, putting her back on the defensive. Strong shortly ducks under a springboard shoulderblock. She steps through the ropes and goes to a corner. Jane picks herself up as Strong gets to the top ropes. Strong takes her back down with flying headscissors, retaking control. She shortly runs to the ropes and rebounds, ducks a clothesline and rebounds, misses with her own and rebounds again, and ducks neck-scissors. Strong goes to a corner and steps through the ropes. She climbs to the top turnbuckle, then she executes flying headscissors, taking Jane back down. Strong gets back up and rests as Jane gets up. Strong nails her with a jumping side kick. Lynn enters the ring and flogs Strong with her whip; the crowd cheers her on as White disqualifies Jane. Hunter enters the ring and takes the whip away from Lynn. She drops it at the ring apron as she and Strong leave. (Match: 10:56) ---------- The Assassin (w/ Li’l Cheetah) v. Liberty (w/ Bodacious Janet Muniz, “the Intimidator” Amber Axeln, “Mistress of Death” Krista Stevens, and Shannon Stevens) Referee: Bob Jordan ---------- Both women lock up. Liberty blocks a Russian legsweep with her elbow, then she executes a front-layout suplex, taking control of the match. The Assassin soon reverses an Irish whip, then she nails Liberty with a shoulderblock. She follows up with a belly-to-back suplex, starting her own offense. She shortly throws Liberty out of the ring and follows her out to the floor. Liberty blocks a sleeper, then she shoves the Assassin into the guardrail. She throws the Assassin back into the ring, then she reenters the ring herself. Both women get up; Liberty nails the Assassin with a savate kick, retaking control of the match. The Assassin soon rolls away from an elbowdrop and gets back up. Liberty picks herself up, then the Assassin sets her up for a suplex; Liberty blocks the lift and goes behind her. The Assassin blocks a tiger suplex, turns around, and picks Liberty up. Liberty blocks being set on a turnbuckle; the Assassin lifts her even higher and sets her down on the turnbuckle. She climbs up onto the second turnbuckle, then Liberty keeps her from hooking her arms and shoves her off the turnbuckle. Liberty hops down to the mat, then she puts the Assassin into a headlock, retaking control. After 2 1/2 minutes the Assassin reverses a whip to a corner, then she meets Liberty’s charge out with a kick to the midsection. She throws Liberty out of the ring, then she dives out after her; Liberty moves out of the way. Liberty grabs under the Assassin’s arms and hauls her up, then she slams the Assassin’s head on the apron and throws her back into the ring. She reenters and waits for the Assassin to get up. The Assassin uses the ropes to pull herself up, then Liberty executes a tiger suplex. The Assassin kicks out at the last second. She gets back on her feet slowly. Liberty nails her just as she stands upright with the Sweet Dreams. She covers the Assassin again; one .. two .. three. (7:16) ---------- Megan van Drake v. Double J Referee: Esther Siler ---------- Both women lock up. Double J turns a lariat into an armbar. Drake works her way out after 21 seconds; Double J puts her into a headlock. Drake breaks free in 5 seconds, then she throws off another headlock. She takes Double J down with a leaping shoulderblock, taking control of the match. Double J shortly blocks a dragon suplex, then she puts Drake into a bearhug, taking charge. She shortly whips Drake to the ropes; Drake rebounds and takes Double J down with a clothesline, retaking control. Double J shortly reverses an Irish whip, then she applies a chickenwing, putting Drake back on the defensive. Drake shortly blocks a kick to the midsection, then she executes a takeover suplex, retaking control again. After a minute Double J blocks a double reverse chinlock with a facerake, then she gets up and executes a legdrag takedown, going back on the offensive. She soon whips Drake to the ropes; Drake rebounds and executes a Japanese armdrag takedown, turning the match around. After 2 minutes Double J blocks standing short-arm scissors and gets back up. She applies a chickenwing; Drake quickly breaks the hold. Double J rewards her with a knee to the back, then Drake blocks a hiptoss and runs to the side ropes. She nails Double J with a shoulderblock, then she runs to the side ropes; Double J knocks her down with a shoulderblock, turning the match around. Drake soon hiptosses her, going back on the offensive. After ninety seconds Double J reverses an Irish whip, then she sidesteps a kick and backdrops Drake, turning things back around again. Drake shortly takes Double J down in a dragon suplex, but only gets a two count. Drake gets back up, then she whips Double J to the ropes and rocks her with an elbow to the face, changing her strategy. After 2 minutes Double J kicks Drake in the stomach, turning the match around again. After another 2 minutes Drake catches Double J in a Frankensteiner, giving a Xena yell in the process; Double J kicks out of the cover at the last second. Drake puts her into the Canadian Maple Leaf. Double J tries to power out of the hold, but can’t. She then tries to inch her way to the ropes. She then gives that up and tries to break the hold again, again failing. Double J tires one last time, but fails. Siler asks if she’s had enough; Double J shakes her head. Double J grows closer to passing out from the pain. Siler tells her to respond or she’ll stop the fight. Double J weakly nods in acknowledgment. Siler asks if she’s still there; Double J barely nods. Siler waits a few seconds, then she asks Double J again. Double J doesn’t respond; Siler stops the match 54 seconds after the hold was applied.
