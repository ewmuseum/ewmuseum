Bodacious Janet Muniz / "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln ------------------------- Metallica Chicks ("The Flawless One" Sydney Sheppard and "Heat Wave" Heather Hetfield) (MGR: Jason Hate) v. Bodacious Janet Muniz / "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln (MGRS: Tall Troll and the Prince) ------------------------- Sheppard and Axeln start. Sheppard goes on the offensive with a kick to the thigh. Axeln quickly starts to even up the match; she nails Sheppard with a vertical suplex. Hetfield soon enters the ring and together she and Sheppard double clothesline Axeln, slowing her momentum. Sheppard lets Axeln get back up then nails her with a spinebuster. She goes for the cover; Axeln kicks out at the last second. Sheppard continues her offensive as Man Mountain Mo comes to ringside. Hetfield shortly enters the ring; she places Axeln on the turnbuckle, then Sheppard takes her back down again with a belly-to-back superplex. They follow up with a double powerbomb, then they pull Axeln to her feet by her hair. They whip her into the ropes; Mo trips her up. Sheppard covers her with Mo holding her feet; he ducks down under the apron so the ref doesn't see him as Sheppard pins Axeln. (4:51) ------------------------- Double J v. "Hot Stuff' Erica Linley (MGR: Fred Scuttle) ------------------------- Double J goes on the offensive with a kick to the head. She soon whips Linley into the ropes; Linley comes back with a Steinerline and turns the match around. She shortly goes for a side suplex, but Double J blocks it and slaps her. Double J takes her down with a legsweep and goes for the pin. Linley kicks out in two; Doube J complains about a slow count, then goes back on the offensive with a knee to the back. Linley very shortly retakes control of the match by blocking a back heel kick and nailing Double J with a short lariat. Double J shortly begins to even the match up. Six minutes into her offensive, Linley tries to bodyslam Double J. Double J counters with a small package; one .. two .. Linley reverses it, one .. two .. Double J kicks out. Linly rewards Double J with a series of submission holds. She shortly climbs to the top turnbuckle, but Double J nails her with a headbutt to the midsection. Linley's feet slip and she falls onto the turnbuckle. Double J takes her down with neck-scissors and covers her; Linley kicks out in two. Double J turns hte match around with a spinning elbowdrop. Linley shortly blocks an armbar and tries to even the match up with a jack-knife powerbomb, but Double J backdrops her and continues her offensive with an armbar. She shortly goes for a short clothesline, but Linley ducks it and regains some momentum. Both women stand toe to toe with each other; Double J goes for a front facelock, but Linley counters with a backdrop. She tries to follow up with a fistdrop, but Double J rolls out of the way and gets back to her feet. She tries to apply another front facelock, but again Linley backdrops her. Double J in turn re-reverses it into a sunset flip and pins Linley. (15:23) -Non-title Match- ------------------------- Hardcore Heaven (Hardcore and X-Treme) v. Slammer Jammers (Slammer and Jammer) (MGR: Randy Walker) ------------------------- Hardcore and Slammer start. Slammer assumes control of the match with a lariat. He goes for a bodyslam, but Hardcore counters with a small package. Jammer kicks out in two; Hardcore follows up with the Powerbomb and goes for the pin. Jammer kicks out in two; Hardcore accuses the ref of making a slow count, then chokes Jammer. He lets go at the four count for only an instant, then lets go again at the three count and pulls Jammer up. He whips Jammer into the ropes, then takes him down with a kick. He covers Jammer with a Victory Roll and pins him. (4:35) ------------------------- Lovely Liz Strohlheim v. Sue Upton (MGR: Fred Scuttle) ------------------------- Strohlhiem goes for a brain buster, but Upton counters with a vertical suplex and takes control of the match. Strohlheim quickly whips her into the ropes; Upton comes back with a shoulderblock and punishes her with a bodyslam. She shortly goes for an enzuilariato, but Strohlheim ducks it and turns the match around with a forearm to the back. After three minutes she goes for an elbowdrop, but Upton rolls out of the way. She pulls Strohlheim to her feet, then whips her into the ropes. She goes for a dropkick, but Strohlheim hooks the ropes. Upton gets back up and turns the match back around with an octopus hold. Strohlheim gets back up and punches her in the midsection, then powerslams her and goes for the pin. Upton kicks out in two and regains her momentum with a short clothesline. She soon sends Strohlheim to a corner, then charges in shoulder first. Strohlheim moves out of the way; Upton rams the ringpost. Strohlheim pulls her out of the corner, then punches her in the back of the shoulder. She takes control of the match with a brain buster. Upton shortly evens the match up with an enzuilariato. Both women stand toe-to-toe with each other. Strohlheim shortly goes for a Norther Lights suplex, but Upton blocks it and takes her down with a legssweep. She goes for a flying bodypress, but Strohlheim rolls out of the way. The stalemate continues for another minute until Strohlheim goes for a headlock takedown. Upton counters with a back suplex, but Strohlheim in turn re-reverses it inot a cross-body block and a cover. Upton easily kicks out; Strohlheim rewards her with an elbowdrop. She shortly nails Upton with neckscissors and covers her with the Schoolgirl Pin; the ref counts one .. two .. three. (3:49) ------------------------- Lucky Joe v. Man Mountain Mo (MGR: Fred Holland) ------------------------- Both men start the match in a stand-off, then Joe shortly takes control with a knee to the back. He very shortly goes for a kick to the thigh, but Mo blocks it and evens the match up again with an elbow. Joe shortly retakes control of the match with a cobra clutch. Mo soon turns the match around with a kick to the back. He applies a Boston crab; Joe reaches the ropes after 18 seconds. Mo contineus his offensive for another five minutes until he tries to whip Joe into the ropes. Joe reverses it, then hits him with an elbow. Both men stand toe-to-toe with each other.
