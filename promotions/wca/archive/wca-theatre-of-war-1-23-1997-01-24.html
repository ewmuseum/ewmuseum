WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION THEATRE OF WAR 1/23/96 Live from the Lake Arena in Springfield, Missouri -----Main Event----- Sweet Sue v. The Assassin Valkyrie v. Vonya Muniz's Musings: Final Show Femme Fatale v. Monique Double D (Debbie Diamond) v. "Nasty Girl" Amber Jane Freedom v. Flower Power "Destroyer" Dara Holt v. Karen "Bugs" Huffman ---------- "Destroyer" Dara Holt (w/ the Prince) v. Karen "Bugs" Huffman (w/ Lovely Liz Strohlheim) ---------- Both women circle each other. They lock up; Holt blocks a forearm smash, then takes Huffman down in a fisherman suplex and covers; Huffman kicks out in two. Both women stand up; Holt whips Huffman to the ropes. Huffman returns with a backdrop, taking control of the match. She shortly takes Holt down with a rolling reverse cradle into a bridge, pinning her. (0:37) --------- Freedom (w/ the Mistress of Death) v. Flower Power (w/ Dr. Insane and the Dream Master) ---------- Both women lock up; Freedom executes a gutwrench suplex, going on the attack. Power very shortly dodges a slingshot. She grabs Freedom's arm as the other gets back up and whips her to the side ropes, then ducks a clothesline and spins around, taking Freedom down with an enzuilariato. Freedom gets up and reverses a whip to the ropes, then follows Power in and knocks her over the top with a clothesline. Freedom steps through the ropes; Power rolls away from an elbowdrop from the ringsteps. Freedom picks herself up, then pummels Power with a tiger bomb on the concrete. She reenters the ring. Power rolls at the five count; Freedom hammers her with an elbowdrop from the second turnbuckle. She climbs to the top rope, then pounds Power with the 4th of July; one, two, Dr. Insane pulls the ref out of the ring. Freedom heads to the top rope; the Dream Master gets on the apron and shoves her off. Freedom gets back up and blocks a ropeburn. She nails Power with a chop, regaining her momentum. After a minute she puts Power in an abdominal stretch. Power breaks the hold after 7 seconds, then bites Freedom's face. Power takes her down with a leaping clothesline, turning the match around. Freedom soon ducks a spinning leg lariat. Power attempts to counter a headlock takedown with a back suplex, but Freedom in turn counters with a cross-body block. Power kicks out in two; both women get up. Freedom sends Power to the ropes; Power misses with a clothesline, then stops and spins around, driving her knee into Freedom's back, regaining her momentum. Freedom soon blocks a ropeburn, then catches Power in a rolling reverse cradle, pinning her. (5:21) [Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.] [Fireworks explode as the camera shows a top-down view of the ring, the WCA logo in maroon letters on the white canvas. The camera descends and levels off, then circles the arena, showing the capacity audience. It stops at the other side of the aisle, where two WCA three-dimensional signs bracket the entryway; fireworks go off as the signs come into view. The image dissolves to commentator John Birmingham, seated at ringside with two monitors on his red-tableclothed table.] Birmingham: "Good evening, wrestling fans, and welcome to another edition of Theatre of War! The big story in the World CyberWrestling Association today is the attempt on Haley 'Comet' Hanson's life. Were it not for Franscois Simoneau getting her out of the way, we'd be reporting on the murder of Haley. Thankfully, though, no one was killed. The big question, though, is if Ms. Hanson will be able to compete this Saturday. But first, tonight in our main event you'll see Sweet Sue take on the Assassin. These two women don't like each other a whole lot; Sue's been rubbing the fact that she beat the Assassin with a short lariat back in September into everybody's face. The Assassin considers her lucky, and would love nothing better to prove the last loss a fluke. However, all of that is icing to the real reason this match is taking place, which is because Sweet Sue considers the Assassin to be a traitor to the WCA. Don't ask me to explain what that means; I know that all of the wrestlers in the WCA can wrestle in other leagues with the commissioner's blessings, but the TCA have taken exception to that rule. Thus this match will be the first of several 'loyalty' matches over the next few weeks between the TCA and the Assassin, the tag team Thunder and Lightning, and the Evil Empire. Speaking of the Empire, tonight Vaalkyrie will take on her former stablemate Vonya. Valkyrie is on her way to a World title shot, and is more than willing to use her former friends as steppingstones along the way. You will also see the last Muniz's Musings show. As you saw at Stone Cold, 'the Intimidator' Amber Axeln powerbombed her off of the apron onto a field gurney sitting on bare concrete. The reports show that the Bodacious One is paralyzed, perhaps permanently. Her career has ended, but tonight we'll show her last taped segment of Muniz's Musings as a farewell tribute to her. Plus, Monique goes head-to-head with rising star Femme Fatale. This will be Monique's first singles match in the WCA. She is a phenomenal tag team wrestler, but fighting solo is something else entirely. Tonight we'll see if she has what it takes. But first, we'll see another member of the Henchmen of Hades, 'Nasty Girl' Amber Jane, in action against Double D - that's Debbie Diamond. Jane continues to look for that elusive win to ignite her singles career; could this be the one that does it? Let's go to the ring." ===== ---------- Double D (Debbie Diamond) (w/ Tammi Cooper) v. "Nasty Girl" Amber Jane (w/ Jonathan Schiz) ---------- Both women lock up, then Diamond puts Jane in a Sammartino backbreaker, taking control of the match. Jane shortly blocks a spinning mule kick, then throws Diamond into the ropes and runs her face along the top cable. She bites Diamond's cheek, then takes her down in a reverse neckbreaker, turning things around. Diamond soon decks her with a superkick, going back on the offensive. After a minute Jane counters a sunset flip with a sit-down; Diamond gets her shoulder up in two. Jane rewards her with a double-axhandle chop. Diamond gets up and blocks a facebite, then nails Jane with a kick to the head, resuming her assault. After 2 minutes she executes an inside cradle, pinning Jane. (5:45) ---------- Femme Fatale (w/ Snakebite) v. Monique (w/ Jonathan Schiz) ---------- Both women lock up, then Monique blocks a ropeburn. She whips Fatale to the ropes; Fatale returns with a clothesline, taking charge. Monique shortly blocks a facerake, then whips Fatale to a corner. She charges in, slamming into Fatale. Fatale attempts to counter a spinebuster with a swinging neckbreaker, but Monique turns it into a side suplex. Fatale rolls over, then gets back up. Monique rolls her up in an inside cradle; Fatale kicks out with force in two. She rocks Monique with a European uppercut, resuming her assault. Monique soon whips her to the ropes. Fatale returns with a backdrop. Monique blocks a kneelock and gets up. She takes Fatale down in a spinebuster; Fatale kicks out in two. Both women stand up; Monique nails Fatale with a forearm smash, turning things around. After 3 minutes Fatale counters a Guerrilla Press with a rolling reverse cradle, but only gets a two count. Fatale takes her down with a fisherman suplex and covers; one, tow, shoulder up. Both women stand up; Fatale sends her to the ropes. Monique returns with an elbow to the face, then decks Fatale with a swinging punch, regaining her momentum. After a minute Fatale counters a splash by pulling up her knees. Monique rolls to the mat, then slowly stands up. She blocks an inside cradle; Fatale gets up and moves away from a forearm to the back. She dodges a tackle; Monique blocks another inside cradle. She punches Fatale, retaking control. After 2 minutes Fatale counters a spinebuster with a swinging neckbreaker. Monique rolls away from an elbowdrop; Fatale lands hard, then sits up. Monique pulls her up; Fatale reverses a whip to the ropes. She blocks a roundhouse punch, then rakes Monique's face. Fatale follows up with a kick to the head, turning the match around. Monique shortly ducks another, then takes Fatale down with an earringer. Fatale gets back on her feet; Monique drops her into a reverse neckbreaker. Fatale gazes up at the ceiling, then turns over and gets back on her feet. She dodges a tackle, then picks Monique up and puts her on a turnbuckle. She climbs up, then executes a belly-to-belly superplex, retaking control. She shortly runs into the ropes. Monique jumps a shoulderblock, ducks a clothesline, then knocks Fatale down with a shoulderblock, going back on the attack. Fatale shortly blocks a poke to the eye, then rakes Monique's face. She follows up with a rolling kick, turning things back around. Monique very shortly blocks another facerake. She nails Fatale with a forearm to the back, retaking control. Fatale soon blocks a forearm smash. She nail Monique with a chop, then runs into the ropes. She nails Monique with a clothesline, going back on the offensive. After ninety seconds she whips Monique to the ropes; Monique returns with a Guerrilla Press. She keeps Fatale on the canvas with an earringer, then scoops her up and bodyslams her. Monique glances at a corner, then picks Fatale up again. Fatale blocks being set on a turnbuckle. Monique sets her down; Fatale blocks an earringer, then runs Monique's face along the top rope, regaining the advantage. She shortly takes Monique down with an ipponzei and covers; one, two, Schiz puts Monique's foot on the bottom rope. The ref disqualifies Monique for outside interference.
