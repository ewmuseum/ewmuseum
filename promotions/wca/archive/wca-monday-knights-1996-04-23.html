WCA Monday Knights 4/22

23 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION MONDAY KNIGHTS 4/22/96 -----Main Event----- <Women's World Title Match> Li'l Cheetah v. Lovely Liz Strohlheim Wilber Q. McCoy v. Delta The Assasin v. Lady Starr Japanese Dragons v. Masters of Violence Karen "Bugs" Huffman v. Bobbi - Cage/Strap Match Mats Lindquist v. WarHawk - Loser-Leaves-Town Match The Serial KiLLa v. Ghetto Blaster The African Giant v. The Gravedigger Jupiter v. Richie Rich Donna Star / Johnny Vicious v. "The Destroyer" Shawn Mancubus / Psycho Sarah Fatal Justice v. Christian Kingsworth / "Vagabond" Vincent Vicious "The Flawless One" Sydney Sheppard v. Deep Rest Russian Ring Masters v. The Titans Construction Site v. Darkside Diego v. Quicksilver -------------------- Diego (MGR: The Mistress of Death) v. Quicksilver (MGR: Gunther Leeds) -------------------- Quicksilver starts the action off with a hiptoss. He follows up with a kneelock; Diego reaches the ropes after 5 seconds. Quicsilver executes a bulldog and follows up with another hiptoss. He applies a cobra clutch; Diego breaks the hold after 11 seconds and chokeslams Quicksilver. Diego then executes the Tiger Driver and gets the pinfall. (1:26) -------------------- Construction Site (Pile Driver and Steam Roller) (MGR: The Foreman) v. Darkside (The Grim Reaper and the Undertaker) (MGR: Pall Bearer) -------------------- Pile Driver gets a back heel kick in and executes a belly-to-belly suplex; the Grim Reaper kicks out in two. Steam Roller enters the ring; the Undertaker enters and throws him out, then he and the Reaper send Driver into the ropes and nail him with a double elbowsmash. The Reaper follows up with a moonsault bodyblock; Driver easily kicks out of the ensuing cover. Driver tries to turn the match around with a series of vicious blows, but the Reaper blocks each attempt. The Reapers sends him into the ropes, then Driver misses with a clothesline on the rebound. He rebounds off the far ropes and both men deck each other with a clothesline. Driver bites the Reaper on the cheek before getting back up. Driver tags out to Roller. The Reaper goes on the offensive with a swinging neckbreaker. The Foreman tries to interfere, but Bearer prevents him. The Reaper tags out to the Undertaker, who continues the assault against Roller. After three minutes of pounding on Roller, he tags back out to the Reaper. Together they kick Roller in the abdomen, then the Reaper goes for a backbreaker, but Roller blocks it and dropkicks the Reaper in the knee. He goes on the offensive with a bulldog. He sends the Reaper into the corner, then runs in shoulder first.. The Reaper raises his knee, then tags out to the Undertaker. The Undertaker stands toe-to-toe with Roller. He breaks the stalemate by tagging back out to the Reaper. Driver enters the ring to make it two-on-one. They punch the Reaper int the gut, then Driver goes for a lariat. The Reaper ducks, then blocks Roller's neck snap. He goes for a DDT, but Roller counters with a backdrop. Roller tags out to Driver; they nail the Reaper with a doulbe fist to the midsection, then Driver takes the offensive with a kick to the head. The Reaper escapes a minute later by tagging out to the Undertaker, who tries to even the match up with a headbutt. Driver absorbs the blow, then regains his momentum with a fist to the midsection. He runs into the ropes, misses with a clothesline, then punches the Undertaker square in the mouth. The Undertaker staggers into the ropes; Driver whips him into the opposing side and clotheslines him out of the ring. The ref counts one - two - three - four - five - six - seven - eight - nine - ten. Construction Site wins by countout. (18:33) -------------------- Russian Ring Masters (Knikita Van Vadar and Ruben Von Nazck) (MGR: Jason Hate) v. The Titans (Cronus and Atlas) (MGR: Gunther Leeds) -------------------- Cronus nails Van Vadar in the back with a forearm, then Von Nazck enters and nails him with a lariat. Van Vadar tries to follow up with a reverse neckbreaker, but Cronus blocks it and slaps him. Van Vadar goes for a DDT, but Cronus backdrops him and takes him down with a full nelson. Van Vadar reaches the ropes after 5 seconds; Cronus catches him in a cobra clutch; Van Vadar reaches the ropes after 17 seconds. Cronus sends Van Vadar into the ropes, but Van Vadar reverses it. Hate pulls the top rope down at the right time; Van Vadar follows him to the floor. Cronus knocks him into the ringsteps, then hits him with a forearm to the back. Cronus reenters the ring, followed by Van Vadar. Cronus regains his momentum with a bulldog, then shortly tags out to Atlas. Together they send Van Vadar into the ropes, then they catch him with a backdrop on the rebound. Van Vadar turns the match around with a lariat. He tags out to Von Nazck; Atlas meets him with a punch. He follows up with a powerbomb, but Von Nazck kicks out of the ensuing cover in two. Atlas tags out to Cronus; they both double-powerbomb Von Nazck, then Atlas tries to kick him in the head; Von Nazck ducks and lets the referee take the blow. Cronus runs into the ropes; Von Nazck hits him with an elbow, then powerslams him. The ref is still down and unable to count; Von Nazck pulls Cronus up and sends him into the roeps. Cronus catches him in a bulldog as the ref gets up. Cronus goes for a splash, but Von Nazck pulls his knees up. Von Nazck turns the match around with a kick to the thigh. Cronus escapes by blocking a back heel kick and tagging out to Atlas; Van Vadar enters the ring and throws Cronus out, then takes Atlas down with a headlock. Von Nazck follows with a jack-knife powerbomb; Atlas kicks out in two. Von Nazck gets him in a doulbe chickenwing; Cronus makes the save. Atlas turns the match around with a kneelift. He applies a sleeper, but Von Nazck jawbreaks his way out. Atlas stumbles into the ref, who falls over the top rope. Von Nazck turns the match back around with leg scissors. He goes for a piledriver, but Atlas backdrops him. Atlas executes a Hotshot, the tries to follow up with a kneelift, but Von Nazck steps aside. Atlas nails the ref instead. The ref punches him in the gut, then storms out of the ring as Von Nazck tags out to Van Vadar. Atlas executes an enzuilariato, then goes for a sleeper. Van Vadar blocks it and nails hjim with an inverted atomic drop. A new ref enters the ring as President Kessler stops the old one just before the curtain. Van Vadar continues the assault; Von Nazck enters the ring and punishes Atlas with a flying kneedrop. Vadar nails him with a Guerilla Press; Atlas kicks out in two. Van Vadar complains about a slow count, then whips Atlas into the ropes. Hate pulls the top rope down; the ref disqualifies the Russian Ring Masters. (14:11) -------------------- "The Flawless One" Sydney Sheppard (MGR: Jason Hate) v. Deep Rest (MGR: Mr. Sandman) -------------------- Deep Rest takes Sheppard down with a back heel kick, then follows up with a spinning mule kick. Sheppard ducks an enzuilariato and bodyslams her. She goes on the offensive with a diving headbutt. Deep Rest slowly regains her momentum. She tries to send Sheppard into the ropes, but Sheppard reverses it. Rest hits her with a shoulderblock on the rebound; Sheppard runs into the ropes, then goes for an enzuilariato. Rest ducks and takes Sheppard down with a flying clothesline. Hate tires to interfere, but Mr. Sandman prevents him from doing so. Rest climbs to the top turnbuckle, but Sheppard throws her to the mat. Rest gains a slight advantage with a neck snap and a chinlock deathlock. Sheppard breaks the hold after 12 seconds. Rest clotheslines Sheppard, then tries to take her back down with another neck snap; Sheppard blocks it and goes for a bodyslam. Rest takes her down with an Asai moonsault; Hate grabs his helmet and places it in Rest's hand. The ref turns around and sees the helmet; he disqualifies Deep Rest. (9:40) -------------------- Fatal Justice (Justice and the Executioner) v. Christian Kingsworth - MGR: Ambition / "Vagabond" Vincent Vicious - MGR: Alexia -------------------- Justice catches Kingsworth in a belly-to-belly suplex; Vicious kicks out in two. Vicious enters the ring and the two men nail Justice with a double forearm smash. Kingsworth goes for a faceslam, but Justice blocks it and takes him down with a lariat. He tries to follow up with a powerslam, but Kingsworth counters with a lariat of his own. He tags out to Vicious; together they hit Justice with a double forearm smash, then the Executioner enters the ring and sends Kingsworth flying through the ropes to crash into the guardrail. Fatal Justice takes Vicious down with a double legsweep, then Justice takes firm control of the match with a powerslam. Vicious shorly escapes by blocking a splash with his knees and tags out to Kingsworth. Kingsworth hits Justice with a clothesline, then Vicious reenters the ring and kicks Justice in the head. Vicious leaves the ring; Kingsworth ducks Justice's short laritat. Justice tags out to the Executioner, who turns the match around with a flying axhandle. Kingsworth evens the match with an abdominal stretch; Justice makes the save. The Executioner gets a superkick in before tagging out to Justice. Kingsworth retakes control of the match with a knee to the back. Justice regains some momentum with by blocking a forearm smash; he goes for a chokehold, but Kingsworth blocks it and sends Justice into the ropes. Justice backdrops him to the floor. He follows, then executes a short lariat on the floor. Ambition comes behind him, but he ignores her. Kingsworth reenters the ring, followed by Justice. Kingsworth picks Justice up and places him on the turnbuckle. He superplexes Justice to the floor. He goes for a flying axhandle, but the Executioner catches him and bodyslams him onto the guardrail. He pulls Kingsworth up and sends him into the ringsteps. Kingsworth rolls back in. Jusctice rolls back in at the eight count. Kignsworth continues his assault until he tags out to Vicious. Vicious keeps their control until Justice tackles him and runs him into the corner. Kignsworth enters the ring and together they even the match up with a double kick to the midsection. Vicious takes Justice down with a back suplex, then tags out to Kingsworth. They hit Justice with a doulbe forearm smash, then Kignsworth nails him with a knee in the back. He goes for the Scorpion Deathlock, but Jusctice kicks him off. Kingsworth goes for a reverse cradle, but Justice reverses it. Kingsworth kicks out in two; Vicious makes the save. He executes a diving shoulderblock; Justice gets his foot in the ropes in two. Kingsworth nails him with a swinging neckbreaker, then applies the Scorpion Deathlock. Justice submits after 5 seconds. (13:34) -------------------- Donna Star / Johnny Vicious - MGR: Bobbi v. "The Destroyer" Shawn Mancubus - MGR: Jason the Terribly Naughty One / Psycho Sarah -------------------- Mancubus powerbombs Vicious and goes for the Destroyersault, but Vicious catches him on the turnbuckle and pulls him back into the ring. Mancubus lands on his feet and continues his assault. He sends Vicious into the ropes; Vicious punches him on the rebound and applies a choke hold. He lets go after 4 seconds and tags out to Star; Mancubus tags out to Psycho Sarah. Star and Sarah stand toe-to-toe, with Sarah slowly gaining the upper hand. Star takes her down with a front facelock; Sarah reaches the ropes after 14 seconds. Star sends her into the ropes, but Sarah reverses it. Jason pulls the top rope down; Sarah follows Star to the floor. Bobbi comes behind her, but Sarah side suplexes her and rolls Star back into the ring. She tries to go for a scorpion deathlock, but Star kicks her away and goes on the offensive. She tags out to Vicious; Sarah tags out to Mancubus. Mancubus nails Vicious with a Frankensteiner, but Vicious easily kicks out. Mancubus turns the match around with a flying forearm. Vicious evens the match up by sending Mancubus into the ropes; Bobbi pulls the top rope down. Vicious follows to the floor; he sends Mancubus into the guardrail. Jason attempts to distract Vicious, but Bobbi chokes him with some string. Mancubus throws Vicious back into the ring, then punches Bobbi out. He takes Jason's leather strap then begins whipping Vicious with it; Jason gets excited and grabs the strap, taking over. The fans boo loudly; some of the ringside spectators begin throwing bottles into the ring. The ref disqualifies Mancubus and Sarah, then has security stop the carnage. (8:44) -------------------- Jupiter (MGR: Venus) v. Richie Rich -------------------- Jupiter starts the match off with a neck snap. He nails Rich with flying headscissors, then pulls him to his feet and sends him into the ropes. He tries to execute an armdrag takedown, but Rich counters with a lariat. Rich goes for a backspin DDT, but Jupiter blocks it and goes for a spinning leg lariat. Rich ducks and whips Jupiter into the ropes. He hits Jupiter with a shoulderblock, then takes control fo the match with a kick to the thigh. Jupiter evens the match up with a flying spinning leg lariat. He and Rich stand toe-to-toe, then Jupiter takes control with a thrust kick to the head. Rich turns the match back around with a chinlock deathlock. Jupiter ends his offensive with a handspring bodyblock. Rich quickly retakes control with a backbreaker. He goes for a dragon suplex, but Jupiter blocks it. Rich applies a scorpion deathlock; he lets go after 13 seconds and pins Jupiter with the Blank Check. (15:36) -------------------- The African Giant (MGR: Ted DiBiase) v. The Gravedigger (MGR: Pall Bearer) -------------------- The African Giant puts the Gravedigger in a clawhold before the bell; Gravedigger reaches the ropes after being trapped for 5 seconds. The African Giant goes for an enzuilariato, but the Gravedigger ducks; the African Giant settles for neck-scissors. Gravedigger attempts to place the African Giant on the turnbuckle, but the African Giant blocks him. Gravedigger chops him, then takes firm control of the match with a Samoan Drop. The African Giant evens the match up a little with a roundhouse right. He takes the Gravedigger down with neck scissors, then nails him with a knee to the back. he goes for a powerslam, but the Gravedigger counters with a lariat. He flattens the African Giant with a jack-knife powerbomb and goes for the cover. The ref counts one two three. The African Giant complains about a fast count. (7:34) -------------------- The Serial KiLLa (MGR: Jayesh Chatlani) v. Ghetto Blaster (MGR: Bodacious Janet Muniz) -------------------- The two brawlers trake power blows. The Serial KiLLa goes for a legsweep faceslam, but Blaster counters with a faceslam of his own. Blaster nails him with a swinging neckbreaker, then follows up with a diving elbow smash. The Serial KiLLa goes for a double underhook piledriver, but Blaster blocks it. The Serial KiLLa settles for a standing wristlock. Muniz enters the ring and breaks the hold after 11 seconds. The Serial KiLLa slowly gains an advantage over Blaster. Blaster takes control of the match with an elbow; he sends the Serial KiLLa to the mat and pins the Serial KiLLa with Muniz holding his feet on the ropes. The Serial KiLLa tells the ref what happens. The ref confirms this with the ringside announcer, then lets the match continue. Blaster punishes the Serial KiLLa with flying headscissors. The Serial KiLLa hits Blaster with a backdrop, then executes a legsweep faceslam, evening up the match. The Serial KiLLa takes blaster down with a chokehold; the ref counts one...two...three...four, the Serial KiLLa lets go and puts Blaster in a sleeper. Blaster jawbreaks his way out, but the Serial KiLLa doesn't let up on the onslaught. He executes an avalanche, then places Blaster on the top turnbuckle. He nails him with the Serial KiLLa's Revenge and wins the match. After the bell sounds, Blaster helps Muniz enter the ring. She gives him his barbwire baseball bat, and he butts her in the abdomen with it. He then swings it at the Serial KiLLa, busting his cheek open. Christian Kingsworth and Aqua come down the aisle as Muniz punches Blaster in the mouth. Aqua stops her from putting him in the Latino Leg Split; Kingsworth slaps her before all three men start punmeling the Serial KiLLa. Muniz backflips Aqua, then stomps on his balls three times. Aqua rolls out of the ring in pain. DA MOIDERER comes down to the ring; he takes Blaster's bat and nails all three men in the head with it in sucession. He then starts beating on Kingsworth; the rest of the CK horde comes from the back. DA MOIDERER and the Serial KiLLa both depart through the crowd; Muniz kicks Aqua in the groin one last time and plants a big punch in Blaster's mouth before taking off through the stands also. (14:23) *Loser-Leaves-Town Match* -------------------- Mats Lindquist v. WarHawk -------------------- Both men struggle to gain control during the opening part of the match. WarHawk takes firm control of the match with a slap; he follows by running into the ropes, then hits Lindquist with an elbow. Lindquist ends his offensive with a falling splash. WarHawk nails him with an inverted atomic drop, then goes for the Tomahawk. Lindquist blocks it, then executes a Russian legsweep. WarHawk stops the assault with a standing wristlock, but Lindquist breaks the hold after 8 seconds and goes back on the offensive. WarHawk slowly regains his momentum until Lindquist nails him with a tiger suplex. He pulls WarHawk to his feet, then sends him into the ropes. WarHawk reverses it, only to send Lindquist into the ref as well. WarHawk goes for a backspin DDT, but Lindquist counters with a belly-to-belly suplex. Since the ref is still down, Lindquist gets back up and places WarHawk on the turnbuckle. He goes for a double underhook superplex, but WarHawk throws him off the turnbuckle. He nails him with a flying legdrop, then goes for a backbreaker, but Lindquist blocks it. He thumbs WarHawk in the eyes, then places him on the turnbuckle. He takes him down with a superplex, then goes back on the offensive with a half Boston. WarHawk slowly evens up the match. He finally turns the match around with a jumping side kick. He knocks Lindquist down with a clothesline. Lindquist barely kicks out in three. Lindquist ends his offensive with a falling slam; WarHawk kicks out at the last second. He executes a bodyslam; WarHawk kicks out at the last second. Lindquist sends WarHawk into the ropes; he catches WarHawk in an inside cradle. The ref counts one .. two .. thr - WarHawk reverses it - one .. two .. three. Mats Lindquist departs the WCA with a 4-3 record. (26:27) *Cage/Strap Match* -------------------- Bobbi (MGR: Christian Kingsworth) v. Karen "Bugs" Huffman (MGR: Tall Troll) -------------------- Huffman takes her down with an enzuigiri, then executes a fistdrop. She takes Bobbi down with a back suplex, then goes for a second-rope flying kneedrop. Bobbi rolls in towards her and goes on the offensive with neck-scissors. Huffman turns the match around shortly with a clothesline. Bobbi uses the strap to whip Huffman into the ropes, then nails her with a leg lariat. Huffman quickly ends her offensive with a kneebreaker. She knees Bobbi in the back and chokes her with the strap. The ref counts one .. two .. three. Huffman lets goe, then reapplies the choke. The ref count one .. two .. three .. four; Huffman whips Bobbi into the ropes; Bobbi instead sends Huffman into the corner, then places her on the turnbuckle. She climbs to the top rope, then she wraps the strap around Huffman's neck, then leaps off the turnbuckle. The strap pulls Huffman down to the mat hard; Bobbi pins her with her foot. Troll has to help Huffman back to the locker room. (4:45) -------------------- Japanese Dragons (Super White Samurai and Super Blue Ninja) (MGR: "Duke" David Jones) v. Masters of Violence (Punisher and Demolisher) (MGR: Mistress of Death) -------------------- Samurai takes Punisher down with a jumping DDT as the bell rings; Punisher turns around and nails him with a clawhold. Samurai reaches the ropes after 10 seconds; Punisher hits him with a forearm in the back. Demolisher enters the ring and catches Samurai in a powerbomb. Punisher and he hit him with a double elbowdrop. Punisher delviers another as Demolisher leaves the ring. Punisher tags out to Demolisher, then kicks Samurai in the head. Demolisher puts him in a cobra clutch, but Ninja makes the save. Punihser enters the ring and together they double kick Samurai in the midsection. Demolisher sends Samurai into the ropes, but Samurai comes back with a clothesline, turning the match around. Samurai tags out to Ninja, who continues the assault. Demolisher slows him down with an elbow to the throat. He blocks Ninja's scorpion deathlock and tags out to Punisher. He powerbombs Ninja and Punisher follows with a flying headbutt. Punisher continues with the offensive until Ninja shortly ends it with a jumping DDT, firmly retaking control of the match. Demolisher enters the ring and tackles Ninja, then Punisher catches him in an inside cradle. The ref counts one .. two .. shoulder up. Punisher tags out to Demolisher, then both men take Ninja down with a double flying headbutt. Demolisher turns the match around with a double axhandle chop. Ninja escapes by tagging out to Samurai, who nails Demolisher with a flying somersault splash. . Demolisher ends his offensive with a fist to the midsection, then he slaps Samurai. The Mistress of Death throws him her Singapore cane, but Samurai intercepts it and nails Demolisher in the base of the skull with it. He goes for the cover; the Mistress of Death puts Demolisher's foot on the bottom rope at the two count. Demolisher tags out to Punisher. Samurai takes him down with a lariat, then executes a flying somersault splash. He goes for the cover; the ref counts one .. two .. the Mistress of Death distracts him. The ref disqualifies the Masters of Violence. (12:45) -------------------- The Assasin v. Lady Starr (MGR: "Duke" David Jones) -------------------- The Assasin opens up with a side suplex. She continues her offensive until Lady Starr shorly ends it with a double underhook backbreaker. The Assasin just as quickly blocks a Sammartino backbreaker and executes a standing wristlock. Starr reaches the ropes after 15 seconds. The Assasin regains her momentum with a kick to the thigh. Starr ends her second offensive by blocking a slap; she goes for a jumping DDT, but the Assasin goes for a backdrop. In turn, Starr re-reverses a sunset flip. Both women trade control of the match, then the Assasin goes back on the offensive with an elbowsmash. Starr turns the match around again with a slap; she takes the Assasin down with a tiger driver, but the Assasin kicks out in two. Starr complains about a slow count, then legsweeps the Assasin. She goes for a European uppercut, but the Assasin catches her in a belly-to-belly suplex. Starr takes her down with a double underhook suplex. She waits until she gets back up, then hits her with a backspin DDT. She waits again, then whips the Assasin into the ropes. She goes for a dropkick to the knee, but the Assasin holds onto the ropes. Lady Starr nails her with a double underhook backbreaker, then goes for a snap suplex. The Assasin counters with a vertical suplex. The Assasin kicks her in the back, then goes gor a standing wristlock. Starr blocks it and pins her with a DDT. (15:50) -------------------- Wilber Q. McCoy (w/ Cousin Zeke) v. Delta (MGRS: Sigma and Zeta) -------------------- Both men struggle for dominance duirng the opening part of the match. McCoy ends the stalemate with a lariat. He runs into the ropes; Sigma trips him. McCoy stumbles into Delta while the ref warns Sigma. Delta steadily evens the match up; he takes control with a headlock takedown. McCoy evens the match up by blocking an attempt to get him on the turnbuckle. Delta punches him; he punches Delta back. Delta goes for a jumping side kick; McCoy ducks and nails him with a piledriver. Delta gets up somewhat slowly and goes for a front facelock; McCoy backdrops him. Delta counters with a sunset flip; McCoy kicks out in two. Delta goes for a headlock takedown; McCoy goes for a back suplex. Delta counters in turn with a cross-body block and pins McCoy. (14:23) -------------------- Li'l Cheetah (MGR: Tall Troll) v. Lovely Liz Strohlheim (w/ Judge Dread) -------------------- Cheetah backspin DDT's Strohlheim before the bell, then tries to follow up with a dropkick. Strohlheim moves to the side, then puts Cheetah in an abdominal stretch. She lets go after 14 seconds; Cheetah throws her out of the ring. Strohlheim reenters at the three count. Strohlheim goes for a back suplex, but Cheetah blocks it and goes for a dropkick. Strohlheim steps back, then covers her with the Schoolgirl Pin. Cheetah barely kicks out, then finally nails Strohlheim with a dropkick. Strohlheim gets up and powerslams her. She goes for the cover; Troll distracts the ref. Li'l Cheetah goes on the offensive by rocking her with an elbow. She throws Strohlheim out of the ring and knocks her into the ringpost. She pops her with the seat of a folding chair and reeenters the ring. Strohlheim reenters at the eight count, but fnds herself on the defensive and Cheetah catches her in a belly-to-belly suplex. She misses with a shoulderblock, then she goes for the Heart Punch. Strohlheim steps to the side and takes Cheetah down with an armlock hammerlock, turning the match around. She whips Cheetah into the ropes, then nails her with a short lariat. Cheetah easily kicks out, then catches her in a belly-to-belly suplex. Judge Dread stops the count by placing Strohlheim's foot on the ropes. Li'l Cheetah rewards Strohlheim with a dropkick, going back on the offensive. Strohlheim shortly ends it with a faceslam. She pulls Cheetah back to her feet, then sends her back into the ropes. Cheetah nails her with the Heart Punch and pins her. Li'l Cheetah retains the Women's World title. (7:37)
