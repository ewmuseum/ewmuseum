WORLD CYBERWRESTLING ASSOCIATION RAMPAGE 3/22/97 From Tel Aviv, Israel -----Main Event----- =Tag Team Titles Tournament Round 1 Match 1= Hardcore v. Springfield Succulents =Can-Am Title Match= Lovely Liz Strohlheim v. Angel Hunter The Scandal Sheet Bounty Hunters v. Freedom / The Mistress of Death "Psycho" Vicki V v. "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.> <Fireworks explode as the camera shows the crimson WCA logo on the white ring canvas. The camera descends rapidly, then levels off facing the aisle. The camera swerves left, panning out over the capacity crowd and circling the ring, ending its focus on the iridescent three-dimensional WCA letters bracketing the entrance to the backstage area. The referee for the first match heads to the ring.> -------------------- "Psycho" Vicki V (w/ Dr. Insane and Christy Cooper) v. "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln (w/ Valkyrie and Bodacious Janet Muniz) -------------------- Both women circle each other. They lock up. Axeln blocks a hiptoss, then whips Vicki to the ropes. She stops Vicki with a kick; Vicki blocks a lariat with an armdrag takedown. Axeln gets back up and throws Vicki out of the ring. Vicki quickly reenters; Axeln meets her with a fisherman suplex, taking control of the match. Vicki soon sneaks a punch to the stomach in, then she backdrops Axeln, taking charge. After 2 minutes Liberty joins Muniz on the outside of the ring as Vicki runs to the far ropes. She backdrops Axeln, continuing her onslaught. Axeln soon blocks an inside cradle. She meets a standing Vicki with a swinging punch, then runs to the ropes. Vicki sends her reeling with an elbow to the face, then throws her out of the ring. The ref stops her from following; Vicki heads to a far corner. Axeln reenters the ring, then counters a flying sunset flip with a sit-down; one, two, kickout. Axeln nails Vicki with a back heel kick, turning the match back around. After a minute Vicki punches Axeln hard in the midsection. She follows up with a spinning DDT, turning things around. Axeln shortly sends Vicki to the ropes; Vicki bumps the ref out of the ring, rebounds and runs into Axeln's elbow. She falls to the mat; Axeln puts her in headscissors as the ref reenters the ring. Axeln tightens the hold, starting a series of holds. She soon reverses a whip to a corner; Vicki moves away from her charge in. Axeln stops herself in time, then reverses a whip to the far ropes. She executes a belly-to-belly suplex; Vicki kicks out in two. Both women get up; Vicki reverses another Irish whip, then jumps a shoulderblock. She executes a headlock takedown, gaining the advantage. Axeln soon puts her into a full nelson, retaking control of the match. She shortly attempts to slap Vicki, but the latter blocks and returns it. She sends Axeln to the ropes, then jumps a kick. Vicki blocks a pick-up, then goes to a corner. She executes flying headscissors, retaking control. Axeln soon blocks a kick to the midsection. She headbutts Vicki, going back on the offensive. After 2 minutes she sets Vicki up for the Doombomb; Vicki backdrops her instead. She scales the turnbuckles from the inside of the ring, then keeps Axeln on the mat with flying headscissors, turning the match back around. Axeln very shortly counters a pick-up with an inside cradle; Vicki kicks out in two. Axeln throws Vicki out of the ring, then steps through the ropes after her. She knocks Vicki into the ringsteps. Vicki picks herself up, then kicks Axeln in the stomach and throws her back into the ring. Axeln gets up as Vicki enters; Vicki counters a fisherman suplex with a small package, only to get a two count. Vicki plants Axeln firmly on the mat with a spinning powerbomb, going back on the offensive. Axeln shortly counters a flying axhandle with a fist to the midsection. Vicki gets back up; Axeln dodges her tackle. Axeln executes a fisherman suplex and covers; Vicki gets her shoulder up in two. Axeln nails Vicki with a chop as the latter gets on one knee, then runs to the ropes. She jumps a shoulderblock, then executes a lariat. Vicki blocks a cobra clutch with an armdrag takedown. She gets up and kicks Axeln's side, then runs to the far ropes. Liberty pulls the top rope down; the ref sees it and disqualifies Axeln. (15:50) -------------------- Bounty Hunters (Red and Black) (w/ Panthra and Semiramis) v. Freedom / The Mistress of Death (w/ Bodacious Janet Muniz and Liberty) -------------------- Shannon Stevens takes a ringside seat as Freedom and the Mistress are introduced. Freedom doesn't enter the ring, instead heading directly to her corner. Black steps out as Red takes the Mistress down with a takeover suplex. The Mistress gets up and sends Red to the ropes; Red returns with a rolling kick, taking control of the match. The Mistress soon blocks a tiger suplex, then executes an armdrag takedown, starting her own offensive. Red soon catches her in a gutwrench suplex, then tags out to Black, who throws the Mistress out of the ring. One .. two .. three, the Mistress reenters the ring. Red reenters with her; Freedom rushes in and throws her out. She returns to her corner as the Mistress takes Black down with a lariat, resuming her assault. Black shortly nails her with a clothesline, then she goes to a corner. Black keeps the Mistress down with a flying clothesline, taking charge. The Mistress shortly blocks leg scissors, then gets up and takes Black down with a lariat, retaking control. After ninety seconds Black counters a back suplex with a cross-body block; one, two, kickout. Black gets back up, then executes a leg lariat, turning things back around. The Mistress shortly takes her down with an armdrag takedown, retaking control. Black shortly catches her in a hiptoss. She follows up with a belly-to-belly; the Mistress kicks out in two. Black tags in Red; they whip the Mistress to the ropes, then stop her with a double kick to the midsection. They send her to the far ropes and deck her with a double spinning leg lariat. Black leaves the ring as the Mistress blocks a fireman's carry. She executes a back suplex, then tags in Freedom. The Mistress picks Red up; Red prevents being set on a turnbuckle. The Mistress leaves the ring as Freedom sends Red to the far ropes, then she ducks a spinning leg lariat. Red picks herself up and lunges away from a handspring elbow, tagging in Black. The Mistress enters and throws Red out of the ring, then she returns to her corner as Black blocks a front-layout suplex. Black executes a leg lariat, going back on the offensive. Freedom shortly executes an ipponzei; one, two, thr-kickout! Black sits up; Freedom yanks her to her feet, then sends her to a corner. Black rebounds off the turnbuckles; Freedom nails her with a drop-kick to the knee, taking control of the match. She shortly tags in the Mistress, then takes Black down with a lariat. Freedom rolls out of the ring as Black gets back up. The Mistress greets her with a jumping elbow thrust, continuing the assault. Black very shortly tags in Red, then both women nail the Mistress with a double forearm smash. Black follows up with a spinning leg lariat. She leaves as the Mistress gets back up. She blocks a tiger suplex; Red blocks a dragon suplex. Black enters the ring again and takes the Mistress down with a spinning leg lariat, giving control of the match to Red. the Mistress shortly catches her in an inside cradle; Red easily kicks out. The Mistress picks her up and puts her on a table, then climbs up and executes a superplex, turning things around again. After ninety seconds Red catches the Mistress in a belly-to-belly suplex; one, two, in the ropes. Red pulls the Mistress to her feet, then sends her to the ropes. The Mistress falls back to the mat weakly; Red goes to the apron, then hammers her with a slingshot legdrop, going back on the attack. Over a minute alter Red tags in Black. They send the Mistress to the ropes; the Mistress ducks a double clothesline, rebounds and decks them with her own. Red leaves the ring as the Mistress keeps Black down with a back suplex, retaking control again. She shortly tags in Freedom; Red enters and knocks the Mistress out of the ring, then she and Black send Freedom to the far ropes and execute a double spinning leg lariat. Black puts Freedom into a grapevine, going on the offensive. After a minute Freedom blocks a forearm smash. The Mistress enters; she and Freedom send Black to the ropes. They stop her with a double kick to the midsection as the ref stops Red from entering the ring. The Mistress decks Black with a thrust kick to the head, then leaves the ring. Black gets back up, shaking her head to clear it. Freedom executes a backflip cradle; Black gets her shoulder up in two. She gets up, then runs to the ropes. Freedom blocks a tilt-a-whirl suplex with an enzuilariato. Black gets back on her feet; Freedom whips her to the ropes. Black returns with a shoulderblock, sending Freedom falling back to land on her butt. Black puts Freedom into a double reverse chinlock, taking charge once again. After 2 slow minutes Freedom tags out to the Mistress. Black ducks the Mistress jumping elbow thrust, then takes her down with a tilt-a-whirl suplex. The Mistress blocks a rolling reverse cradle. She executes a spinning leg lariat, taking control of the match. She shortly sends Black to the ropes; Black comes back with a hard kick that knocks the Mistress down. She applies a grapevine. The Mistress makes her way to the ropes to break the hold. Black reluctantly lets go, then pulls the Mistress up and sends her to the far ropes. The Mistress rebounds and hits her with an elbow, resuming her offensive. Black soon blocks a spinning toe hold, then scrambles to tag in Red. Freedom enters the ring and throws Black out, then she returns to her corner as the Mistress executes an armdrag takedown, putting Red on the defensive. After 2 minutes Red blocks a back suplex. She executes a headlock takedown. The Mistress gets back up; Red takes her down with a Russian legsweep. She beats the Mistress to her feet, then executes a legdrag takedown. Red looks out at the crowd, then sends the Mistress flying out of the ring. The ref begins the count; one .. two .. three .. four .. five .. six .. seven .. eight .. nine .. ten. Freedom, with a look of disgust on her face, jumps off the apron and goes to Sharpe. She whispers something in his ear, then goes to Stevens and whispers in her ear. Freedom then leaves the arena.
