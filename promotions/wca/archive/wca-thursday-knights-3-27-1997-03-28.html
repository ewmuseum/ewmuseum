Karen "Bugs" Huffman Muniz's Musings Bounty Hunters v. Freedom / The Mistress of Death (Best of 3 Falls Cage Match) "Psycho" Vicki V v. Bodacious Janet Muniz (Best of 11 Match 2) Blood v. "Nasty Girl" Amber Jane Nadia Williams v. "Dominatress" Kathy Lynn <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.> <Fireworks explode as the camera shows the crimson WCA logo on the white ring canvas. Tonight in our main event Lovely Liz Strohlheim will face Karen 'Bugs' Huffman for the Can-Am title. Both of these squadmates are unafraid to test each other's strengths and weaknesses, and both women despise non-title matches. Before they can go at it, though, we'll see the presentation ceremony of the Click II's symbols, the black high-heels. Four of the leagues premier wrestlers, Bodacious Janet Muniz, the World champion the Mistress of Death, Liberty, and 'the Intimidator' Amber Axeln, will speak at the event, taped last week. Unfortunately, as those of you who watched Monday's Theatre of War know, Helena 'High Kick' Knightsbridge has been unceremoniously dumped from both the Click II and the World CyberWrestling Association after having failed a drug test for steroids. Plus, tonight you'll see a return match between the Bounty Hunters and the team of the Mistress of Death and Freedom . . . at least it may be the Mistress and Freedom. If you saw the latest Rampage, you know that Freedom left her partner after the Mistress lost the match to the Bounty Hunters the first time. Will she show up in her partner's corner tonight, or have the Mistress sister and rival step in again? We'll find out later tonight. As an interesting side note, John Birmingham's job as commentator is affected by the outcome, as he has allegedly slandered Panthra's Army in the past. Should the Army win, John is history. Also, 'Psycho' Vicki V and Bodacious Janet Muniz will go head-to-head in Match 2 of their Best of 11 series. Janet won the first match Monday, pinning Vicki through the pain of the Latino Leg Split in under a minute. I suspect the memory of that humiliation is eating at the Psychotic One, something to give Muniz pause tonight. Before that, though, Blood will take on 'Nasty Girl' Amber Jane. Longtime fans of the WCA will recognize this match as another battle between arch-enemies the Henchmen of Hades and the Posse of Punishment, although many will also see it as a chance for retribution, as Jane and her tag team partner were former members - heck, founders - of the Henchmen. Speaking of 'Dominatress' Kathy Lynn, she'll be kicking off tonight's show against Nadia Williams in the other Henchmen-Posse battle tonight. Let's go to the ring." ===== -------------------- Nadia Williams (w/ Crimson and Red) v. "Dominatress" Kathy Lynn (w/ "Nasty Girl" Amber Jane) -------------------- Williams throws Lynn out of the ring, then goes through the ropes to the floor. She nails Lynn with a jumping elbow thrust. The ref leans over the top rope, asking Williams just what is going on. Lynn uses the distraction to send Williams to the guardrail, then chokes her against it. The ref steps through the ropes; Lynn sees her coming and lets go, going back into the ring. Williams rubs her throat, then follows Lynn in; Lynn puts her in a front facelock. Williams breaks the hold after 11 second with a backdrop. She turns and executes a Victory Roll, pinning Lynn. (0:44) -------------------- Blood (w/ Red) v. "Nasty Girl" Amber Jane (w/ "Dominatress" Kathy Lynn) -------------------- Both women lock up. Jane breaks off and goes into a corner and leaps to the top turnbuckle. She executes a flying forearm and covers, but Blood easily kicks out. Jane dances suggestively for the male fans, then sends Blood to the ropes. Blood returns with a kick, taking control of the match. Jane sshortly counters spinning headscissors with a Hotshot. She goes to a corner, then executes a flying splash and covers; Blood kicks out in two. Jane follows up with a Sammartino slingshot, taking charge. After 3 minutes she runs to the ropes. Blood ducks a clothesline, then spins around. Both women get hit with a dual clothesline. They get up at the same time; Blood moves away from a drop-kick to the knee. She rushes to a corner and leaps to the top, then nails Jane with a flying drop-kick, turning the match back around. Jane shortly ducks a spinning leg lariat. Blood prevents standing short-arm scissors and gets back up. Jane reverses an Irish whip, misses with an elbow, then spins around and backdrops Blood over the top rope. Jane steps through the ropes, then pummels Blood with a flying splash off the apron. Blood gets up slowly; Jane drives her knee into the back. She shoves Blood into the ringsteps, then yanks Blood to her feet and whips her into the ringpost. Blood ducks neck-scissors and reenters the ring. Jane follows her in; Blood sends her to the far ropes, then executes spinning headscissors into a cradle, nearly pinning Jane. Blood punishes her with a bodyslam, going back on the offensive. Jane shortly dodges a drop-kick. She goes to the apron and heads for a corner, then pummels Blood with another flying splash; one, two, kickout. Both women get back up, then Blood backdrops out of a setup. Jane pops back up and executes a lariat, retaking control of the match. She shortly takes Blood down with a drop-kick to the knee, then scrambles for the cover, pinning Blood. (6:30) -------------------- "Psycho" Vicki V (w/ Dr. Insane and Christy Cooper) v. Bodacious Janet Muniz (w/ The Mistress of Death, Liberty, and "The Intimidator" Amber Axeln) -------------------- Both women circle each other warily. They lock up, then Vicki blocks a reverse neckbreaker and runs to the ropes. Muniz stops her with a clothesline, taking control of the match. Vicki soon takes her down with a tackle, taking charge. She soon runs to the ropes; Muniz misses with a clothesline, then backs up and uses Vicki's momentum for a bulldog, turning things back around. Vicki soon ducks a thrust kick to the head, then picks Muniz up and drops her into a backbreaker. Muniz gets up; Vicki sends her to a corner and runs in shoulder-first. Muniz lunges out of the way, letting Vicki hit the ringpost. She hits Vicki with a double-axhandle chop, then pulls Vicki out of the corner. Vicki blocks a ropeburn, then attempts to tackle Muniz. Muniz doesn't budge, but just stares at her. Vicki blocks a reverse neckbreaker, then takes Muniz down with a spinebuster; Muniz kicks out in two. Vicki gets up and goes to the apron, then to a corner. She comes off the top and hammers Muniz with a flying fistdrop, retaking control of the match. Muniz shortly bites her hand, then sends her to the ropes. Muniz catches a high cross body and turns it into a backbreaker, retaking control again. Vicki shortly shrugs off a bulldog, then goes to the apron and takes Muniz down with a springboard drop-kick, retaking control of the match. After 2 1/2 minutes Vicki heads for a corner and climbs to the top. Axeln gets on the apron and shoves Vicki off; the ref warns her as Muniz stomps on Vicki, going back on the offensive. After an intense 3 1/2 minutes Vicki counters a pick-up with a small package; Muniz kicks out in two. Vicki follows up with a spinning DDT, turning the match around again. After 3 more minutes of missed opportunities Muniz counters a flying sunset flip with a sit-down, grabbing Vicki's bootlaces to help pin her. (13:58) ===== Sharpe: "Bodacious Janet Muniz uses V's own tactics against her to secure the victory, making her 2-0 in the series.
