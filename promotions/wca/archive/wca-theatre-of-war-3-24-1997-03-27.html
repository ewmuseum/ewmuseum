Bodacious Janet Muniz (Best of 11 Match 1) Interview with Helena "High Kick" Knightsbridge Li'l Cheetah v. Femme Fatale Panthra's Army v. Freedom / The Mistress of Death / Liberty Helena "High Kick" Knightsbridge v. Shannon Stevens <Opening credits appear over a montage of WCA action.> <Fireworks explode as the camera shows the crimson WCA logo on the white ring canvas. Let’s go to the ring.” ===== -------------------- Helena “High Kick” Knightsbridge (w/ Bodacious Janet Muniz and the Mistress of Death) v. Shannon Stevens -------------------- Both women circle each other warily. They lock up; Stevens blocks a cradle. She nails Knightsbridge with a spinning leg lariat, taking control of the match. Knightsbridge soon blocks a Western lariat, then picks Stevens up and sets her on a turnbuckle. She climbs up and executes a superplex. Stevens counters a double leg cradle with a double leg-hook; Knightsbridge kicks out in two. Stevens executes a rolling reverse cradle, again only getting a two count. Knightsbridge blocks a full nelson; Stevens blocks a neck snap. Knightsbridge blocks a Western lariat and executes a fireman’s carry, turning things around. After an intense 4 1/2 minutes Stevens throws Knightsbridge out of the ring. She goes through the ropes to the floor, then whips Knightsbridge into the ringsteps. Knightsbrige gets back on her feet; Stevens takes her down with a spinning leg lariat, then breaks the count. She stands up on the apron, then goes to a corner and executes a springboard shoulderblock off the ringpost. Knightsbridge bangs her head against ring officials’ table, scraping her cheek open. Knightsbridge gets up slowly, then reenters the ring. Stevens gets back up and follows her in. Both women circle each other. Stevens prevents a fireman’s carry, then sends Knightsbridge to the ropes. Knightsbridge returns with an elbow to the face, taking Stevens down. Knightsbridge applies a Boston crab, retaking control. Stevens shortly ducks a flying forearm. She goes into a corner and waits on Knightsbridge from the top turnbuckle. Stevens executes a flying bulldog, turning the match around. After ninety seconds Knightsbridge prevents a belly-to-back with an elbowjab. She goes to a corner and climbs the turnbuckles, then executes a flying sunset flip. Stevens kicks out in two; Knihgtsbridge gets up and runs to the ropes. She executes a high cross body; Stevens gets her shoulder up in two. She nails Knightsbridge with a spinning leg lairat, resuming her assault. She shortly sends Knightsbridge to the ropes; Knightsbridge returns with a clothesline, turning things back around. After 2 minutes she puts Stevens in a full nelson. Stevens breaks the hold after 9 seconds, then puts KNightsbridge into an abdominal stretch, turning the match around once again. She soon executes spinning headscissors into a cradle, pinning Knightsbridge. (11:47) -------------------- Panthra’s Army (Red, Black, and Semiramis) (w/ Panthra) v. Shannon Stevens / The Mistress of Death / Liberty (w/ Bodacious Janet Muniz) -------------------- Red and the Mistress of Death lock up. the Mistress reverses a whip to a corner, then walks in. Red shortly tags in Black; they nail Stevens with a double forearm smash. Stevens blocks a rolling reverse cradle, then executes a Western lariat. Black picks herself up; Stevens sends her to a corner. She runs in; Black raises her boot up to block her. She hammers Stevnes with a double ax-handle chop, turning the match around. Stevens soon goes to a corner and climbs the turnbuckles. She executes a flying sunset flip; Black kicks out in two. Stevens follows up with a waistlock suplex; again, Black kicks out in two. Stevens returns to the corner, then keeps Black down wtiha flying somersault bodyblock, going back on the attack. The Mistress shortly enters the ring; Black ducks her thrust kick to the head. The Mistress instead nails Stevens, sending her reeling back. Black throws the Mistress out of the ring, then takes Stevens down with a belly-to-belly suplex, turning things back around. She shortly whips Stevens to the ropes, then takes her down with an elbow to the face. Black drops down and covers, pinning Stevens.
