Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

American Sports Entertainment opened in January of 2001 on a promise: no roleplay, no judges, no arguments over who wrote the better paragraph. Handlers would book their own outcomes and write their own angles, together, toward whatever story served the card best.

Its debut show, ASE's Wild, ran on the seventeenth of that month across ten broadcast segments, framed as a cable-television special breaking in on the WB's own evening schedule. Press conferences, backstage arrivals, and a full undercard-to-main-event slate carried the promotion's superstars through a single, complete night.

The federation asked its own audience, afterward, what had worked and what had not, expecting to refine the format in the weeks ahead.

Nothing survives past that request. Whatever came of the refinement, or of the promotion itself, is not part of the held record.
