ASE's Wild 1/17 (Debut Show) (part 9)

January 25, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The flashing red of an ambulance's strobe lights set the mood for our return from the break. Sure enough we're in the underground garage of the
Rose Garden staring at the back of an ambulance, it's rear doors thrown open and a gaggle of people surrounding a stretcher that appears headed on board.
If you guessed Vic Valari was the man on the stretcher then give yourself a star. At present Vic is laying down on the gurney, but it's obvious that he is both conscious and looking to get up. Paramedics keep pushing him back down and mumbling about his needing to sit still. It appears ol' Vic may have scored a concussion thanks to the attack he suffered just moments ago.]
[The shot begins to zoom out now, leaving the chaos of the ambulance scene to wallow in the background. As we draw back, two new people begin to come into view. ASE Reporter Jessica LaRue stands alongside the man who was to be Valari's tag team partner in tonight's main event, the "Starman", Lee
Clark. Jessica looks at Clark, who just seems to be staring off into space.
Getting her cue to begin, she leans in with a question.] JL: Lee, after what we witnessed just a few moments ago, and the fact that... [she makes reference to the scene just behind them] ...well, the fact that it's obvious Vic Valari won't be able to continue, where does that leave you and the state of the Tag Team Titles later tonight? Will Sire and
Pride be taking the match by forfeit?
[Suddenly Clark seems to snap out of his trance like state and looks straight down at LaRue.]
Clark: Forfeit? What in the hell are you talking about, Jess? Heck, you of all people should know me better than that. But let's step back for a second and take a look how this first night has gone. First of all, I get here to find that the Championship Committee in all its wisdom hasn't found me a suitable opponent. Instead, I go out there and make my debut for the company off the cameras in front of one of the local hopefuls.
[LaRue looks as if she is going to say something, but one quick look from
Clark stops her dead in her tracks.]
Clark: Then I go to get a coffee, and hear that Vic Valari, of all people, is being set up to get a shot at becoming one half of the very first ASE Tag
Team Champions, and not only that, but that this Hadley Mills character, using all that under-one-hour's-experience-on-the-job has the nerve to place me as Vic's partner. Heck, you only have to go back a few months to see what this man is really like, and the fact that Valari and myself do not get along is well documented.
[Again, LaRue appears to have some words of wisdom, but before one of them can leave her lips, Clark cuts her off again.]
Clark: But then comes the icing on the cake. Just as I get used to the idea that I have to step in the ring and work with Valari, some stupid fool looking to make a name for himself takes it upon himself to take out the man that was to be my tag team partner. And you know what, Jessica? It all seems a little too convenient. But that'll keep for another time. Right now, I have a job to do.
[Clark starts to turn and walk away, but Jessica says one more thing that stops Lee in his tracks.]
JL: So you intend to go out there and take on Sire and Pride all by yourself? Isn't that a little...
[Clark spins around and looks down at LaRue once more.] Clark: Nuts? Suicidal? Perhaps so. But at the end of the day, I have made a commitment to go out there and give the fans what they came for - that being the best performance that the ASE has to offer. You see, Jess, that's what the "Starman" is all about. Every show, every day, I go out there and give the fans the best performance in the ring that they will find anywhere in the world. Sure, there are guys that will keep them amused;
there are guys that make them laugh and cry, make them boo and feel intense hatred, but I am the kind of man that goes out there night after night and makes them leave this arena knowing that they have seen the best wrestling promotion in the business. Because that is what I do. And whether I take on one man or two, whether I fight for singles titles, tag titles or just for pride, I always give everything I have. I have given blood, sweat and tears for this business, and just because I have faced a number of setbacks tonight, I will always go out there and give my best.
So, Pride, Sire... don't make the mistake of coming to that ring thinking that your numerical advantage or your credentials will see you through this match. Because with Valari or without him, I am fixing to show the fans of
ASE, whether long time wrestling fans or newcomers, exactly why they called me the "Star of the NWC". And after tonight, if I do what you all believe is impossible and win the World Tag Team Titles, maybe I'll have earned the right to be called the "Star of the ASE". Because win, lose or draw, there is one thing you can be sure of folks - you will see exactly why I'm here.
And if you didn't know already, then watch that ring tonight, because it's about time I returned home.
JL: England? [Clark breaks into a wry smile.] Clark: No, Jess, my home isn't England, Detroit or Reno. No, my home is right out there... [pointing off screen] My home is that ring out there.
Now if you'll excuse me, I have business to see to. [Clark turns and walks away.] JL: There you have it, folks. Lee Clark against Sire and Pride tonight for the World Tag Team Titles.
[No time for nifty commentary on these latest developments. The show must go on, and as a result we switch to the back locker room where we are greeted by a shot of somebody's back. It's Devo Tremors back to be exact, and at the moment he is seen in state of partial undress as he packs up his belongings into his duffel bag. His shirt is off and he now has on a pair of faded blue jeans which are still unbuttoned and hanging loose around his hips. His hair is wet and the towel around his neck tells us he just recently stepped out of the shower.]
[Also present at the moment is Devo's longtime friend and fellow ASE Superstar "Explosive" Darren Fury. Having not wrestled this evening, Darren is himself already in street clothes as he leans back on a small folding chair, teetering back on it's hind legs with his feet up on the lockers next to him. He smiles casually to himself, no doubt anticipating the ribbing he's about to give his buddy over the earlier loss to Biff Markum. Sure enough, Darren soon digs in.]
Darren: [sarcastically] Hell of a match there, sporto! [Devo does not reply. In fact, he gives no indication of hearing Darren, although he obviously has. He merely continues about his business of packing away his gear.]
Darren: [grinning] Wow. Who would have thought that after all these years away from active competition, Biff would've been so sharp in there tonight.
I guess all that time broadcasting really... [making the quotation marks sign with his fingers] ..."refocused" him.
[Still no response from Tremors, who zips up the duffel bag he had been attending to and plops his butt down on a nearby stool. He begins to slip on a fresh pair of white gym socks. Fury, not to be ignored, presses the issue further.]
Darren: Think on the bright side, maybe he'll look at this win and decide to become an active wrestler again. Then you could say you helped a legend revive their career. [in mock awe] Hell, maybe he'd let you be his manager.
[By now we can tell that even though this is nothing more than some good natured ribbing on Darren's part, Devo is beginning to get pissed. With his back to Darren as he slips on his shoes, we can clearly see Devo's face is contorted into a grimace of anger. He's trying to bite his tongue but it is getting incredibly more difficult to do so. He breaks his silence for a moment.]
Devo: C'mon, dude, like... lay off. [Mistake. Sensing that the armor is beginning to crack, Fury pushes on, prepping for the kill.]
Darren: So tell me, slick, what did it feel like to get booed for the first time in your career?
[Devo speaks, but keeps his back to Fury.] Devo: It sucked, if you want to know the truth. And I'd kinda appreciate it if we could, you know... let it go.
Darren: I'm sorry, dude. I'm just trying to pick your brain. You know... in case I have to face the almighty force that is Biff Markum. Perhaps you could offer some advice on how to get out of that devastating Mercy Clutch.
I may end up facing him in this tournament thing. [Devo doesn't speak this time, instead emitting a heavy and audible sigh as he ties the laces on his sneakers.]
Darren: [condescendingly] Oh chin up, Buckaroo! All is not lost. Just because you tapped out to a lazy, overweight has-been is no reason to wear such a long face. You were just knocked off your game plan. Biff will do that to you. You were planning on coasting to an easy win, and instead found out that Biff was actually TRYING for a victory! I realize that must have taken you completely by surprise, but it happens.
You've been off for quite a while and are no doubt rusty! Yeah! Yeah, that's it! You were RUSTY! THAT'S the excuse you can give when you have to go out there and interview with Apter, Keller, Meltzer and all the rest of them. I'm SURE they'll buy it! "Devo was showing signs of ring rust when he was completely humiliated by tapping out to "The Master Of Donuts" Biff
Markum". Hehehehehehehehehe!!
[That's it. Devo spins around and bolts to his feet in one swift motion before firing out at his friend.]
Devo: ALRIGHT! I GET IT! "Ha ha, Devo lost to Biff. What a dork." You don't need to keep rubbing it in, dude!
[Sensing that the game is just about over, Darren drops his feet to the floor and comes forward in his chair, returning its legs to the ground. He begins to play apologetic, although it's still pretty clear he's not done having his fun.]
Darren: Hey hey! No need to get testy! Don't soil this beautiful moment of your career careening out of control by getting all bitchy!
[Darren smiles and snickers at his friend.] Darren: There's still hope. Just ask for a couple of matches against some of ASE's "enhancement talent" and you can bump that winning percentage back up a few points! That's the ticket!
Devo: Look, dude, it was like, an exhibition, okay? We're in the guy's hometown for crying out loud. I was doing a favor for a friend tonight, you know. You remember what that is, don't you? A friend? Oh wait, I'm like, talking to the guy who's concept of friendship is getting his butt kicked by some dude on rollerblades.
[This draws a quick smile to the face of Fury. If we could read his mind he'd be no doubt be saying to himself "Well alright, bout time he fired back. Good for him. Now let's see how far we can take this before the little guy completely snaps." After a brief pause in which we can clearly see Devo is mad at himself for losing his cool, even briefly, he returns to his gear and begins to pull on a white t-shirt.]
Darren: Ooh! Mister Tremors trying to aim below the belt there! Nmm mmm... vindictiveness does NOT become you, sir.
[Cranking up the ham, Darren turns away in faux pain, and then, loud enough so Devo can hear, says to himself...]
Darren: You try... you TRY to have some good, innocent fun at your fellow man's expense... only to have them get all snippy over you! DAMN ME! DAMN
MY TEASING WAYS! I HAVE BRUISED THE BOY'S FRAGILE EGO, AND NOW I MUST PAY
FOR MY SINS!!! AAAHHHHHHHHHH!!!! [And with a melodramatic scream of anguish, Darren throws himself over the table, pulling it along with him so that it lands on him when he hits the floor. He then lies there... the table covering him... and starts to giggle. Devo stares at him, the look on his face conveying exactly how he feels about Fury at the moment. They lock eyes for a second and Darren busts out laughing even more. It's at this point that Devo finally cracks a smile and begins to chuckle himself.]
Devo: Dude, you are like, such a jerk sometimes. [Devo reaches over to offer Darren a hand in getting to his feet and we cut away to yet another shot in another backstage area. A hallway to be exact.
And what we see is the typical pre-commercial bumper that showcases the combatants in the upcoming match as they walk intensely towards the arena.
In this case it's Lee "Starman" Clark, decked out in his wrestling gear and looking focused. He knows he's walking into an almost impossible challenge yet being the man who he is, he's not ready to back down just yet. His eyes burn into our cameras as he moves forward down the hall, eventually making a sharp left as the corridor turns and disappears from our sight.]
[:::::Commercial Break:::::]
