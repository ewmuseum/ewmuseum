Sports Entertainment"
As we like to call it, American Sports Entertainment is the "next logical step in the e-volution of sports entertainment". It is an e-wrestling fed with a different focus: a focus on storytelling, rather than winning and losing. Unlike a traditional e-fed, whose focus is on roleplay against an opponent, with then an impartial third party determines a match result based on which handler roleplayed "better", ASE is a promotion of the handlers, by the handlers, and for the handlers. The promotion is for the most part entirely booked by the handlers themselves, both in the creation of matches, and in the results of those matches as well. If you have become tired of the
"I roleplay - you roleplay - I roleplay again - we have a match" format of e-wrestling, or if you feel you have "e-volved" past the stage of caring whether or not you win or lose some meaningless roleplay battle then ASE is for you.
Ours is not a "competitive" e-wrestling fed, it is a "writer's" fed. It is a fed for those people who have become more interested in wrestling booking and storycraft - the creation, writing and execution of angles, plots, and wrestling-related stories. This is the place where you can nurture those feelings, and know that you'll will be working alongside like-minded individuals.
In ASE, gone is the traditional aspect of "roleplaying". No longer will random "filmed movies" appear on some ambiguous television programs and be used to determine match outcomes. Instead, handlers will work together to write anywhere from one to five (or more) segments for each wrestling card, all of which will progress the angles and stories they have created together. Handlers will also book themselves in matches against fellow handlers, and determine for themselves who gets put over, depending on what would make the most storyline sense. In ASE, taking a loss means absolutely nothing if it advances a plot thread towards a bigger story payoff down the road. And a win is only as impressive as how good your opponent is willing to make you.
The bottom line is trust. Trust in yourself to come up with wrestling ideas that have never been accomplished before, and trust in your fellow handlers to work with you towards a greater good of a wrestling card that kicks more ass than the one before it.
ASE opens on January 17 with the debut of "ASE's Wild". You could still be a part. - Ol' JBM Handler of Lee "Starman" Clark 2x SCWF World Champion MCW Tag, TV and Heavyweight Champion SCCW Heavyweight Champion HIW Tag Champion Now in ASE.... ...Let the games begin.
