ASE's Wild 1/17 (Debut Show) (part 5)

January 25, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We open up backstage where we find roving reporter Jessica LaRue standing in front of the ASE interview backdrop. From all outward appearances
Jessica is everything you'd want in a woman. Beautiful, blonde, busty - well put together in every way. And the skimpy, all-too-revealing outfit she has on only helps to fuel those lustful thoughts. She's a bad girl and we love her for it. Anyway, mic in hand, she smiles for a moment before realizing her camera is on.]
Jessica LaRue (JLR): Oh! Hi, everyone, Jessica LaRue here backstage at the
Rose Garden with a very special guest, one of the many ASE Superstars who we'll be seeing so much more of over the course of the next few weeks, months, even years, "Vicious" Vic Valari. Tell me...
[The camera pans out to put the Vicious One himself, Mr. Valari, into the shot. Dressed in a purple and black Adidas windsuit, Vic mugs for the camera a bit as LaRue waves a microphone in his general direction.]
JLR: What are your plans for the inaugural episode of ASE's Wild? Valari: Why, I am so glad you asked that in a completely non-scripted manner, Miss LaRue. [Jessica gives the camera an Excedrin headache look before turning back.] Valari: Ya see, I've been through a lot of downtime lately, and when a man like myself is sitting on his laurels, he begins to consider a great many things. The first thing I considered was "How am I ever going to get back to the peak I once achieved?". That is something which, while partly my responsibility, is up to fate to decide.
JLR: And the second thing? Valari: THE SECOND THING?! Jessica, you little impish she-devil, you. JLR: I try. Valari: The second thing is, "How can I redeem myself? How can I heal the wounds I've made in this business backstage and make a new, better man out of myself?"
JLR: Well, Vic, what does it matter whether or not you're a good person? Wrestling is a pretty cutthroat business. Valari: It doesn't have to be, Jessica. We put these burdens on ourselves. There are plenty of guys who take that approach and get buried forever. I was almost buried. I burned bridges and destroyed relationships, and I almost went past the point of no return. Jessica, this is a new beginning for me. This is a chance for me to wipe up all the bull crap and see if I can't do something better with myself. Tonight, given the chance, I intend to prove it.
[Vic walks off without much warning, leaving Jessica alone to shrug into the camera.] JLR: Uhh... there you have it. [Holding on the shot of Jessica looking thoroughly confused (a look which will become all-too-familiar as the weeks go by), we finally hear Norm and
The Sheik once again. Eventually we cut back to a shot of the duo seated at ringside.]
NR: Thanks, Jessica. What do you think, Sheik? The man known as Triple V,
"Vicious" Vic Valari, here in ASE.
TS: I'll tell you what I think - I think that this Triple V character is but a shell of the man he once was. What is this nonsense? [in a mocking voice] "This is a new beginning for me", "I want to do something better with myself". FOOEY! This man was once a great world champion in this business because he cared not about the stupid fans. Now? Now he has turned over a new leaf and it makes me sick. I hope he gets a venereal disease and dies for turning his back on what made him great.
NR: Excuse me?! TS: You heard me, you dog. NR: I ahh... I did indeed. Let's ahh... well, let's... let's see what's next on the agenda.
[Still wearing a look of shocked surprise as a result of the Sheik's harsh comments, a wide-eyed Norm Roberts begins to shuffle through his papers.
The Sheik scowls into the camera in a feeble attempt to intimidate the viewers.] NR: I guess... according to this it's time for-- [DING! DING! DING! DING! DING!] RA: ASE fans, our next match is a special Triangle Match! And it is to crown the first ever ASE World Tag Team Champions!
"RRRAAAAAAAAAAHHH!!" RA: Introducing team number one... [Out go the lights, on come the strobes, and in kicks the music, the jarring, opening chords of Living Colour's "Cult of Personality" ripping through the speakers. As the thunderous drums join in and the song begins to pound the audience into submission, all eyes turn towards the entrance.
From under the giant ASEVision step a pair of men who, unlike many of the legendary superstars already seen tonight, are not instantly recognizable.
One, an average-size but well built black man, steps out onto the ramp and immediately crosses his arms over his chest. He is a handsome fellow with a short afro and a pencil thin moustache who is currently decked out in a pair of red, leather pants and a plain black tanktop. His face is expressionless as he stands like a sentry upon the stage. This is Daryl "Street" Stark.]
[Next to him stands his tag team partner and polar opposite, Rob Lawless. At 6'6" 270 lbs., Lawless is considerably larger that Stark, with an expansive chest and broad shoulders. The best way to describe his build would be "thick". In contrast to Stark's dark brown almost black skin is
Lawless' pale white complexion. Where Stark's hair is short and tight,
Lawless sports a thick, unruly mane of black hair that hangs down to the middle of his back, his face clean shaven. Also of note is his attire which is a complete reverse of his partner's, with Lawless in black, leather pants and a red tanktop. With Stark standing still in an almost military stance,
Lawless sports a wide, devilish smile and raises both his hands high above his head in a pose of triumph. The crowd of course, knowing neither man by appearance, merely applauds politely.]
## Look in my eyes, what do you see? ## ## Cult of Personality ## ## I know your anger, I know your dreams ## ## I've been everything you want to be ## ## I'm the Cult of Personality ## ## Like Mussolini, and Kennedy ## ## I'm the Cult of Personality ## ## The Cult of Personality ## ## The Cult of Personality ## [With the music thumping along and vocalist Corey Glover screaming out his message, Stark & Lawless begin a slow walk towards the ring, allowing the fans to get a good, long look at two of the business' brightest young hopefuls. Stark, looking angered and annoyed, shoots harsh glances people's way while Lawless is much more playful, offering to slap hands with fans only to pull his hand away at the last second and laugh at the people he had duped. He's the like the class clown trapped in the body of the class bully.]
RA: First, from Boston, Massachusetts, he weighs in tonight at two hundred and fifteen pounds...
.....DARYL "SSSSSSSSSSTREET" STARRRRRK!!!!! [Not much in the way of recognition from the crowd, although given Stark's demeanor a few fans have chosen to boo.]
RA: And his partner, from St. Paul, Minnesota, he weighs in at two hundred and seventy pounds...
.....RRRRROB LAWWWWWLESSSSSSSSSSSS!!!!! [Again, little more than half-hearted applause and a smattering of boos is heard. The reaction from the crowd doesn't seem to matter much to either man though, as reaching the ring Stark leaps up onto the apron and vaults himself over the top rope and into the ring where he begins to bounce on the balls of his feet like a boxer. Lawless takes the slow route up the steps before ducking between the top and middle ropes and plopping back into a nearby corner where he begins to play with his hair as they await their opponents. The music finally cuts out.]
RA: And now, introducing team number two... ----- LOL introduction ----- NR: So where are they? TS: How am I supposed to know? NR: Umm... ahh... fans, I don't know what to say here. It appears... it appears as though LOL is a no-show.
TS: Typical American scum. Can never be counted on to show up to work on time. NR: Perhaps they got delayed somehow. TS: And perhaps they are selfish, lazy, sloths. NR: Nonetheless, we've got to keep this match moving... [As if hearing Norm himself, ring announcer Rod Apache presses on.] RA: And the third team this evening... [As has been done so many times in the wrestling past, Yngwie Malmsteen's "I
Am A Viking" begins with a loud gong, sending the crowd into a frenzy!]
[GONNNGGG!!!] "RRRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHH!!!" NR: Here they come! RA: And the final team, from Oslo, Norway... at a combined weight of seven hundred twenty nine pounds... SIGURD THE ELK! NAUSEOUS SNOWBLINDER!
.....NOOOOORRRRRSSSSSEEEEE!! .....COOOOOOOOOOOOOOODEEEEE!!!!! "RRRAAAAAAAAAAHHH!!" [However, just like what happened a few seconds earlier, nobody appears at the top of the ramp. As Lawless and Stark smile conspiratorially together in the ring, referee Wally Kenner waits patiently for their arrival.
However, none comes.] NR: Ahhh, not AGAIN! TS: I think that Gatorade may have done it's intended job! [That would be a very good guess, for the ASEVision suddenly springs to life and we get a wonderful shot of the back of Nauseous Snowblinder... who is leaning far into a large trash barrel back in the locker room. Scattered all around him on the floor are close to a dozen empty Gatorade bottles. A low growl begins to gurgle in the pit of Snowblinder's stomach and before we can say "regurgitation", the Viking drops his head into the barrel once more and lets out a huge--]
Snowblinder: WWWAAAAAAUUUUUUUGGGGGHHHH!!!! NR: MY GOD! TS: What a sickening display! NR: This isn't right! TS: I have to agree with you there, American flea. NR: Somebody get back there and help Nauseous out! TS: He's certainly living up to his name, isn't he? NR: Oh, knock it off! [The ASEVision continues to show shots of Nauseous as he hurls what remains of his breakfast, lunch and dinner into the barrel. His partner, Sigurd the
Elk, is noticeably absent.]
NR: Well that explains what happened to Norse Code. But what about the boys in LOL? Did they get delivered some Gatorade too?
TS: Maybe. Or perhaps they are frightened little girly men. NR: Either way, they're not here now. So what happens now? [Wally Kenner, the referee, looks around the ring helplessly, clearly unsure of what to do next. He looks over at Stark & Lawless who have remained for the most part in their corner enjoying the complete and total lack of action. Stark smiles a satisfying grin and reaches over to offer up some skin to his partner. Lawless drags his open palm across Stark's and then the two touch fists. Leaning over the rope towards the time keeper's table,
Lawless motions for a mic and one is eventually tossed up to him.]
NR: I certainly don't like the options at the moment, and neither do these fans, I'd bet. They're getting cheated out of a match.
TS: Too bad for them. NR: And now it appears as though Lawless is going to speak. I wonder if he's going to own up to the fact that this whole fiasco is his doing?
TS: You don't know that, Norman. NR: WE SAW HIM - PLAIN AS DAY! TS: That doesn't mean he caused that large, smelly European to vomit. I think the big ape was simply too scared to face an unknown team.
NR: Oh, please. [Mic in hand, Rob Lawless holds up a hand as if to hush the crowd. Problem is, they aren't really making all that much noise, a fact that he appears to be oblivious to.]
Lawless: So I'm thinking... this is supposed to be a tag TEAM match, right? [pause] But looking around, I only see one TEAM here. Now I've never claimed to be no rocket scientist, but I'm thinking it's gonna be pretty hard to have a tag team match with only ONE team. Ain't that right, Street?
[Stark does not answer verbally, instead acknowledging his partner with a slow and deliberate nod.]
NR: Yeah, and I wonder who's fault THAT is? TS: Hush, pig. Lawless: It's funny, we came here to ASE because we heard this was going to be where the big boys play. You know, the place where all the real stars would be. But looking around I don't see any stars. How about you, Street, do you see any stars?
[Again there is no audible reply, a simple shake of the head providing
Stark's answer.] Lawless: In fact, all I do see is you... and me... and those two tag team championship belts over there on that table. So I tell you what, Mr.
Referee guy, seeing as how our supposed opponents this evening didn't have the cajones to show up and face this... [he motions to himself and Stark]
...pair of nobodies... [a chuckle] ...then why don't you do your job, have them ring that bell, and get to counting. Cause in about ten seconds we're gonna crown us some tag team champions, whether these stars show up or not.
[Lawless chucks the mic to the unsuspecting referee who bobbles it around a few times before finally getting a grip on it. He hands the mic to the ring announcer on the outside and calls for the bell.]
[DING! DING! DING!] NR: They CAN'T be serious?! Who do these two think they are? TS: They are the ASE Tag Team Champions, Norman. Perhaps you should show them some respect and learn their names.
NR: This is ridiculous! [Not as ridiculous as the look of satisfaction plastered on Rob Lawless' face as Kenner begins his count. The crowd, by now fully aware that they are getting cheated out of the world tag title match, start booing loudly.]
ONE! NR: Somebody better put a stop to this! TWO! TS: Why? This is perfectly legitimate. These other coward teams did not see fit to show. I spit on them! PHTOOEY!
THREE! NR: Oh, for heaven's sake. FOUR! NR: Fans, I apologize for this, but it seams we really ARE going to crown our first champions here in ASE under these less than desirable circumstances.
FIVE! TS: Stop your whining. SIX! NR: I CAN'T believe this is how we're going to crown our World Tag Team
Champions. SEVEN! TS: Well it is! So get used to it! Stark and Lawless will make fine champions! EIGHT! NR: But it isn't right! It's these two joker's fault that there's no match! We've already SEEN what they've done to Norse Code! Can't the
Hadley Mills make some sort of decree?
TS: PAH! That simpleton has NO authority! Do not bring his name into this! NINE! NR: Yeah, but maybe he-- "RRRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHH!!!!!" NR: Huh?! [The huge roar from the crowd startles all three men in the ring, enough to make the referee stop his count. The reason for the roar is simple. FLYING down the ramp towards the ring is the larger member of Norse Code, Sigurd
The Elk!]
NR: THE ELK! HERE COMES THE ELK! NONE THE WORSE FOR WEAR! TS: I THOUGHT HE ALSO DRANK THE-- [The Sheik's words get stuck in his throat, as the wide-eyed Stark and
Lawless realize their plan has not worked to the full effect they were hoping. Sigurd slides his huge frame into the ring, and immediately he gets jumped by the other two. Both men begin stomping away at the giant Viking, but it's obvious that the mastodon is not feeling a thing.]
NR: KENNER LEAPS FROM THE RING! SIGURD POWERING TO HIS FEET! STARK AND LAWLESS TRYING THEIR BEST TO KEEP HIM DOWN! TS: That beast's stomach must be of cast-iron! [The Elk gets fully upright, taking numerous punches to the head and face. Lawless attempts a clothesline, but Sigurd ducks underneath, and this causes
Lawless to fall over the top rope to the floor below. Stark immediately turns to face the Viking, just as The Elk reaches under his tunic and pulls out a bottle of a bright yellow liquid.]
"RRRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHH!!!" NR: HE'S GOT GATORADE! HE'S GOT GATORADE! TS: WHAT IS THIS?!? [Stark rushes forward to go after the big man, but The Elk's right arm springs out and catches the shorter wrestler around the throat with a choke hold!]
NR: CHOKESLAM! GIVE HIM A CHOKESLAM, SIGURD! TS: BE QUIET, INFIDEL! [As Stark begins squirming, looking for an escape, Sigurd brings the bottle to his mouth and RIPS the lid off it with his teeth. He then turns menacingly to Stark, whose eyes go wide. The crowd erupts with understanding, just as The Elk shoves the bottle forward - RIGHT INTO THE
MAW OF STARK!!!]
NR: HE'S FORCE-FEEDING STARK! STARK'S DRINKING THE POISONED GATORADE!!! TS: WHERE IS LAWLESS?!? HE MUST STOP THE NORD!!! [Stark ends up drinking about half the bottle... the other half being spit up over himself and the Viking (as well as just dribbling out the sides of his mouth). Once the bottle is drained, The Elk lets the smaller black man drop... and Stark IMMEDIATELY covers his mouth with his hands. The entire arena can practically see him go green right there and then, and like a speeding bullet, he dives out of the ring and sprints towards the back.
Lawless, having witnessed this entire thing, looks between his partner and the Viking in the ring, trying to decide which way to turn. As Sigurd then reaches underneath and produces a SECOND bottle of the tainted Gatorade,
Lawless wisely decides to "help his partner", and makes a break for it back up the ramp. The Elk doesn't let the two off that easily, and he dives out of the ring to follow them both off - while the crowd continues it's cheers and applause.]
TS: THIS IS TOTALLY REPULSIVE! NR: Heck no! This is GREAT! TS: Those poor men-- NR: OH, STUFF IT! STARK AND LAWLESS ARE GETTING AS WELL AS THEY GAVE! I
ONLY HOPE SIGURD IS ABLE TO CATCH UP WITH LAWLESS AND OFFER HIM A DRINK AS
WELL! TS: But what does this do to our match?!? Who are our tag team champions?!? NR: Uh... I dunno! Sigurd ran down before the other two were awarded the match, so I have no idea!
TS: Oh, gods! Does this mean the little maggot MILLS will need to make a decision on who our champions are?
NR: Well, maybe not him, but I'm sure the Championship Committee will pass on a ruling to him. Hopefully we'll get that tonight, because I'd really like to know who our tag champions are going to be. We've got to take a break, but don't go anywhere as we'll have more exciting ASE's Wild coming up after these words from our sponsors.
TS: Silly capitalist customs. * * * * * [Of course as has been the trend all night we don't go right into the shameless hocking of products, instead taking a few moments to watch a portion of highlights from last week's press conference. The setting is the same as the previous bits, the podium in front of the giant ASE logo on a white backdrop. ASE Director of Talent Development, Stu Albright, makes the introduction.]
Albright: Ladies and gentlemen, here he is, the Millennium kid himself,
Devo Tremors! [Quick round of applause as the handsome young cruiserweight steps up to the podium. We cut quickly to midway through his opening statement.]
*** Tremors: So yeah, it was just an opportunity I don't think any of us could pass up. You know, it's like, to be there at the beginning, the have the chance to really be a part of something from the ground up, that's just cool, you know. Sometimes you need to take a chance and I really think coming to ASE was the right move, you know.
*** Tremors: I've been fortunate to like, have been granted a lot of opportunities to succeed early in my career, you know. Everywhere I've gone the promoters have treated me great and I really like, owe a lot to them for getting me where I am today. But this is a big chance for me to kinda, you know, like step out on my own and do something new. I'm psyched about the chance.
*** Reporter: What about your girlfriend, Exene Schwahling, will she be joining you in ASE? [Devo smiles almost an embarrassed smile at the question, and after ducking his head for a second chuckles a bit as he answers.]
Tremors: No, I don't think so. Exene's kinda got her own deal, you know, and she's pretty happy with where she's at. Yeah, we'd kinda like to be able to be closer together you know, and sure, working for the same company would help that, but she's still her own woman. I think maybe someday she may consider it, but for now she's really cool with where she is and what she's doing, and without a really deep woman's division here yet, I think she'd like feel she was being wasted here, you know.
*** Reporter: Any chance we'll see a reuniting with Tommy West? You guys seemed poised for something before he got hurt.
Tremors: Yeah, we kinda had some big stuff in the works before that, but hey, you know, accidents happen. Tommy's doin' real good and last time I checked he was hopin' to be back active in another month or so which is cool for him. As far as TWA goes, I don't know. He's got his own gig and he was always real comfortable in the NWC, so I like, I don't know if he'd be looking to move right now. His brother's doin' real well and I'm sure they'd like to work together in some way, so that'd be a big move. But hey, you know, never say never. I'd love to see him come here and I really think he and I could rip it up in ASE, but for now I just gotta like, do my own thing, you know. We're still good buds though.
*** Reporter: Where do you see yourself fitting in in ASE with such a crowded main event picture? Do you think you belong in the same group with the likes of Tony Pride and Eugene Robinson?
[Another embarrassed smile from the kid.] Tremors: Wow, you guys really like to stir stuff up, eh? [a laugh] Well, you know it's like, sure, I'd love to be able to throw my name into that hat and say I belong up there, but truth is I don't think I'm ready for that kind of thing yet. I've only been in this business for a little over two years and I got a lot to learn. Most of those guys have been at much longer and they've earned the right to headline...
[Another reporter blurts out before Devo can even finish.] Reporter: Trey Slater's only been around for two. Tremors: I'm sorry, what's that? Report: Trey Slater. He's only been in the business for about as long as you, yet he's generally considered main event material.
Tremors: Yeah, well he's Trey Slater, man, that's all he needs. [another laugh] Seriously, I appreciate the thought but for now I'm comfortable working my way into that category. We've got a lot of hungry guys here in
ASE, old and new, you know, so like, I don't think it'd be smart for me to get all high and mighty and start demanding a spot I'm not ready for. Some guys like Trey, they're just born with it, you know, while guys like me need to work a little more to make that jump. It's cool though. You know, with the way the things are gonna work around here I'll still be getting my chance to work with a lot of those big names. We all will. It's like, the approach here is much different than in other places. They really want to see everybody become a household name and they're gonna work towards that, you know. It's a wicked cool atmosphere. I'm jazzed about the possibilities.
*** [Cut to a shot of Devo waving as he steps down.] Tremors: Thanks, guys. This was cool. [And fade to black.] [:::::Commercial Break:::::]
