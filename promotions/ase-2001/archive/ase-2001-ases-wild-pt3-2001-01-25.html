ASE's Wild 1/17 (Debut Show) (part 3)

January 25, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Spotlight on center ring. There, with some 4,000 screaming fans acting as a chaotic backdrop, stand ASE announcers Norm Roberts and The Sheik.
Roberts is dressed in a red, button-down shirt with an ASE logo over the left breast pocket and a pair of tan slacks. In his mid-40's, Norm is an average looking man with an affable smile and a gentle face. His short brown hair is beginning to show traces of gray around the edges and the hairline is not where it once was, but these are facts that don't seem to bother Norm. He stands with both hands on the microphone that he currently holds in front of him.]
[The Sheik looks in many ways like you'd expect somebody with such a moniker to look. His skin is a dark tan, almost brown, his hair currently tucked up under a towel with a band holding it in place. (I'm sure those things have a name but I'll be damned if I can think of it -Ed.) His chin is currently covered by a patch of coarse, black hair, the lack of a moustache preventing it from being a pure goatee. Like his partner, The Sheik wears a red, button-down, ASE staff shirt, although his pants are black and extremely baggy. Not one for smiling, his lips are currently curled into a half-smirk/half-snarl, which is apparently as friendly as he gets. He too holds a mic, although he grips it with only his left hand while his right is balled into a fist and resting on his hip. It is a confident pose, particularly standing alongside Norm's somewhat mousy stance.]
[With the crowd raging behind him, Norm raises his mic slightly to his lips and begins to speak.]
NR: Hello, everyone, and welcome to ASE'S WIIIIIIIIIILDDD!!! "RRRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHH!!!" [Norm pauses to let the cheers rush over them. He smiles widely.] NR: My name is Norm Roberts and here with me is my partner, the legendary
Sheik! "BBBOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!" [The Sheik is somewhat taken back by the sudden surge in boos. Even though he's a heel (and damn proud of it), he was apparently still expecting to be cheered, at least on the first night. Surprised by the response he begins looking around frantically before screaming...]
TS: INFIDELS!! "RRRRRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHH!!!!!" [Which of course elicits a huge pop, confusing the Sheik even more. You can pretty much bet from here on out that he'll be suspicious of the fans, as if he wasn't already. Norm stifles a chuckle before moving on.]
NR: On behalf of everybody here at American Sports Entertainment we want to welcome you all to this very special night - the very first edition of ASE's
Wild! We've got quite a show lined up for you people tonight. In fact, how
'bout givin' it up for the Hunter and the Braveheart for that spectacular match!
"RRRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHH!!!" NR: It's gonna be tough to top that one tonight, but we're gonna give it our best! But before we do that we've got some very special announcements to make, don't we, Sheik?
TS: Yes, my ignorant American broadcast partner. Just to show how generous we are, even to such undeserving swine as these people here tonight...
INFIDELS! [Another massive cheer. Okay, he did that just for the cheap pop. You can tell by the slight smile on his face.]
TS: ...We are going to do the unthinkable. That is right, we are going to give you filthy swine here tonight - FREE CLOTHES!!
[As the crowd roars again, Norm produces a white t-shirt that he had been previously pinching under his right arm and unravels it, holding it up for the cameras to zoom in on. The image also appears on the giant ASEVision for all in the arena to see as well.]
NR: That's right. Each and every fan in attendance tonight will receive one of these very special t-shirts, compliments of ASE! These one-of-a-kind shirts will serve as the ultimate proof that YOU were here tonight to watch history be made!
[We get a good look at the shirt. On the front it reads "I was there the night ASE first got" just above a giant "Wild" logo. Norm flips it around and we see the back which bears the ASE logo and the company tagline "The next logical step in the evolution of sports entertainment."]
NR: You can pick up your shirt on your way out of the arena this evening or by presenting your ticket stub to any merchandise stand in the building during the show. All courtesy of ASE just for being here tonight!
"RRRAAAAAAAAAAHHH!!" TS: SILENCE, DOGS! "BOOOOOOOOOO!" TS: I demand silence, or I will not share with you the many secrets I have learned for tonight.
[Eventually the crowd begins to settle down and The Sheik continues.] TS: Better. Now it is time to reveal to you all, even though you are deserving of no more than my supreme and ultimate hatred, the details of the championships for this filth infested company. Tell the swine, Norman.
NR: Alrighty, Sheik, I'll do just that. Fans, we've got some exciting developments in regards to the ASE championship titles, and we're not gonna keep them a secret any longer. That's why next week, right here on ASE's
Wild we're going to determine our very first singles champion with a Battle
Royal for the North American Heavyweight Championship!
"RRRAAAAAAAAAAHHH!!" TS: Not only that, but we will also start a 16 man tournament to crown the first ever World Heavyweight Champion who will be coronated at our first pay-per-view, the Million Slam March. But that will also start next week because we do not want to waste such good matches on lowly people from
Portland.
"BBBOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!" NR: Now, now, Sheik, let's not be that way. These fans here tonight are gonna get something too.
TS: Yes, my unending scorn and hatred. NR: Besides that. You see, fans, tonight, right here at in Portland,
Oregon... [Insert cheap Mick Foley City Name Pop.] NR: ...We're going to crown the first champions in ASE's history! That's right, tonight, here at center ring you'll see three teams compete in a
Triangle Match for the ASE World Tag Team Championship!
"RRRAAAAAAAAAAHHH!!" NR: You'll see the exciting, young team of Stark & Lawless! You'll see the always entertaining duo of Lucky and Dan D. Lion, better known as LOL! And of course, what show we be complete without an appearance from everybody's favorite Vikings... NORSE COOOOODE!!!
"RRRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHH!!!" NR: And you'll see that RIGHT HERE TONIGHT! TS: But that's not all, pigs... [BEEEEEYYYOOOOO-SSSHHHHHHHHHH!!!] [Static. The Sheik covers his ears as the piercing sound of audio feedback drills into his mind from the P.A. System. As if on cue, the euphonic tune of "Money" by Pink Floyd begins to drown out the ear-splitting vibrant tones, which in turn heralds the arrival of the majestic being whose song alone brings out an enormous ovation of boos from the crowd. Norm Roberts begins back pedaling in the ring as the Sheik furiously throws his fist into the air.]
## Money, get away ## ## Get a good job with more pay and you're O.K. ## ## Money, it's a gas ## ## Grab that cash with both hands and make a stash ## ## New car, caviar, four star daydream, ## ## Think I'll buy me a football team ## [As Norm Roberts slips out of the ring, running straight for the announcer's table, the Sheik continues to stand his ground. Not knowing what he is about to get himself into he glances over his shoulder which is when he realizes he is standing there by his lonesome. Slowly the Sheik twists his neck around as his eyes attempt to gaze down the entrance way. That is until the clamorous explosion of pyro nearly sends him tumbling backwards.]
[FWOOM!!] [FWOOM!!] [FWOOM!!] [KAAA-BOOOOOOOOOOMMM!!!] [Three golden fireworks streak across the arena, erupting high over head as the flames from underneath the ramp ignite, sending a mushroom like explosion into the air. Moments later the smoke begins to part and it is then that the American Sports Entertainment fans get their first glimpse at the most electrifying man that they will ever get to see. Standing there, clad in riches from head to toe, top to bottom, is none other then the
"Grandeur of Greatness," the "Franchise of the Finest," the man who is going to live up to the name of this place and bring in the big bucks doing so -
Eugene "High Society" Robinson.]
## Money, get back ## ## I'm all right Jack, but keep your hands off my stack ## ## Money, it's a hit ## ## But don't give me that do goody good bullshit ## ## I'm in the hi-fidelity, first class traveling set ## ## And I think I need a Lear jet ## [The patented midnight black beret; his dirty blond hair cleanly cut all the way around his head; all the perks are there folks. All of them.
Robinson's steel, blue eyes scowl down towards ringside as he spins his golden cane off of the end of his suede shoes. Eugene's body slowly spins around, throwing the tails of his knee-length blazer around with him. The pearly white ensemble is spiced up with a navy green dress shirt, unbuttoned from the neck down, as his chiseled physique glistens underneath the arena lights.]
## Money, it's a crime ## ## Share it fairly but don't take a slice of my pie ## ## Money, so they say ## ## Is the root of all evil today ## ## But if you ask for a rise, it's no surprise that they're ## ## Giving none away. ## [Robinson throws the Sheik a stomach churning smirk as he approaches the steel steps. He points towards the legendary announcer with his priceless cane, a move which sends the Sheik into a frenzy in the ring. Robinson casually rolls his eyes, to the point that he catches a glimpse of Norm
Roberts ducking behind the announcer's table as he steps through the middle ropes. Sure enough, Robinson enters the ring, revealing a mic which he firmly clutches into his right hand as the music fades into oblivion.]
TS: Nobody interrupts the Sheik, I am a legend you inf-- [Robinson's hand stabs the Sheik's wrist, causing the announcer to drop his mic to the ground as Eugene lets his majestic eyes tantalize the helpless
ASE employee before he shoves him effortlessly into the corner of the ring.]
Robinson: I think you've gotten more time then you could ever deserve in the spotlight, Sheik, and I think you'll figure out real damn quick where your place in Sports Entertainment is. Ya see that?
[Robinson gestures towards Norm Roberts who cowardly kneels down behind the announcer's table.]
Robinson: That is where you belong - side by side with the imbeciles of this god-forsaken place. A freak like you, Sheik, you have no business in this ring and if I catch you one more time trying to steal my spotlight, then your going to figure out just why the hell so many familiar faces have fled to ASE in the first place. Now get out of my face.
"BBBOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!" [The Sheik obliges, rolling underneath the bottom rope as Robinson mockingly takes a step in his direction. The fans are unimpressed, booing Robinson who has unquestionably disturbed what was starting up as a fun filled
"family" show.] Robinson: Now for all you idiots out there wonderin' just why Eugene Robinson would be seen in some run of the mill, happy-go-lucky place like this, let me tell you right now that is sure as hell ain't to get some god damn free t-shirt! In fact, it has nothing to do with some fancy committee, some idealistic moron praising me with a few green bills, or even to see how quickly I can shut down some yuppie Ivy league school graduate just like I did to Anton MacTavish back in New England and Ken Nemesis in Seattle.
For you see, the one thing that has led me from my comfortable easy chair back in Minnesota to this god awful place is rather simple. It's called...
Unfinished business. [Robinson snarls at the crowd, throwing his weight around in the ring like a king in his castle.]
Robinson: When I found out from an unnamed source that this little YMCA boys and girls club was gettin' put together it made me laugh. And when I saw the sorry bunch of Jack Nobodies clusterin' the roster it nearly made me gag. But when I found out that the Old Folks' Home had let some of their animals out to piss on their own merit instead of in a hospital gown into some cheap plastic cup, it made me pack up my bags, bring the ass kickin'
boots, and bring the partying hats, because I can tell you right now I'm gonna be doin' the moonwalk in this ring in no time at all.
Top to bottom, there are more cowards in this place then blown opportunities in a Lee Clark mini-series but that isn't going to keep yours truly from zippin' them all up in body bags and droppin' them off in some back alley
Seattle dumpster.
[A mild pop is heard from the Portland, Oregon citizens in regard to that memorable night up in Seattle at SWF's last PPV, Warriors of Wrestling:
"Executive Decision." You just had to be there.] Robinson: But lets get one thing straight from this point on - as of right now, every single man or women that marches down that aisle is on my turf, in my world, and they are challenging anyone and everyone to get to me. I am the epitome of what you youngin's strive to be, and I am the livin'
legend that the heroes one day hope to become when their time in the spotlight is over. The rules in my playground are simple folks, any retard could figure them out, yes you too, Norse Code. Why you ask? Because there is only one damn rule in the house that Eugene Robinson will make famous, and that is to stay the hell out of my way and you won't be sacrificed like the royal bunch of enslaved b[bleep]s that you have all just become.
[Robinson pauses, casting a grin a mile long across his face as he struts towards the ring ropes, scraping his heels against the bloodless mat as if to mark his turf like a wild dog.]
Robinson: Save the bickering and whining for guys like Michael Bold. The man has a "soft" heart for worthless pieces of s[bleep]t. But if you think you can shower me with gifts or plead for mercy then you might as well march on over to the Hunter's locker and ask him just how well that works out.
For you see, I don't claim to be a nice guy, I don't claim to be the one that will give you some lucky break, I just claim to be an outcast from a council of incompetent morons who couldn't counter an offensive attack by the San Diego Chargers.
Which brings us to men like Devo Tremors, Darren Fury, and Vic Valari. Men that had one shining moment under the stars and then walked off into the sunset before putting their name on the line with their sorry asses. These men are known as the paper champions, the ones who were put over in those other places just to please the crowd, because at one time or another they were well liked before they realized that moment of glory was just that, a single moment that was about to be flushed down the toilet by someone like me.
Your accomplishments don't attract my eye, they simply make me realize just how fortunate you were to take advantage of a chance, a chance that won't see the light of day in American Sports Entertainment. Because unless I didn't make it clear all ready...
THERE ARE NO CHANCES! [The crowd is alarmed by Eugene's straight shooting speech, not knowing quite how to react as he lets his voice carry over the mic without any remorse.]
Robinson: So to the Daryl Stark's, Matthew MacDonald's, and Henry Johnson's of the world, unless you enjoy curtain jerkin' your entire career you might as well give up now. This is not a place for bible thumping Youth Group
Director's, Hank, and this sure as hell ain't a place for BloodAngel's twisted family life soap operas. This is Sports Entertainment, ladies and gentlemen. This is a place that was erected for men like me to do what they do best...
Entertain! So for the Tony Pride's and Steve Sire's of the world, the men who carried a council on their shoulders for quite some time before realizing they couldn't cut it anymore, there aren't going to be any welfare checks sent to your house after I'm done knockin' your heads around from pillar to post.
You thought you could hide from the man who not only filled your shoes but out grew them, Tony, but you have just been proved wrong. Let that ring into your head for a bit, Pride, and while you're at it you might as well send a message to that insipid partner of yours, Steve Sire, that he can't duck me anymore.
[There is it, the look that could kill. The razor sharp lips, the hate filled eyes casting inwards towards the jagged nose that is centered above the raw jawline of Eugene Robinson's face. Fittingly deemed the "smirk you wanna smack" you might as well get a good look at it now, because your going to be seeing it a lot more often now.]
Robinson: Tonight begins an era of excellence. Tonight marks the day that pure wrestling was tossed out the window and run over by a semi-truck that refuses to trail behind you anymore. I've caught up to you imbeciles and this time there's simply nowhere else for you boys to run off too.
Kiss your loved ones goodnight, because you've all just signed your lives over to one man that has no limits, and no mercy for the weak, the old, and unfortunate.
Welcome to world of the High Society. [Robinson spikes the mic down and sends a trail of spit right down after it, staining the crisp Wild ring mat for the first time as he steps between the ropes, blowing a kiss to the crowd who retaliates with an ovation of boos in his direction.]
NR: I hate that man. TS: I'm torn. He's a despicable scumbag, which I like. But he insulted me, which I don't.
NR: So the jury' still out on that one? TS: Jury? PAH! In my country-- NR: Let's not start that. [We quickly cut to ringside where James Naismith, earlier seen beaming following the terrific Bold-Hunter match, now appears to have had his enthusiasm dampened thanks to Robinson's arrogant ramblings. He frowns up at the ring until he realizes the cameras are on him, at which point he smiles and gives the old "thumbs up", apparently wanting to keep a positive outlook for the rest of the show.]
[Now, rather than sit through Norm wondering out loud what Robinson "meant" and The Sheik screaming "INFIDEL!" a couple hundred times (there'll be plenty of that later, trust me -Ed.), we switch to a shot of outside. A light drizzle drops down outside as fans who couldn't get tickets wait near the entrance of the Rose Garden for their favorite ASE Superstars to arrive.
And they don't have to wait long as a pink, stretch Cadillac pulls up to the entrance. And that can only mean one thing...]
NR: SIRE! "SEXY" STEVE SIRE IS HERE! TS: Joy to the world, as you people say. [Indeed, the limo comes to a quick stop and the rear door slowly opens to reveal Sire stepping out of the limo. He's decked out as always in a three piece suit, his blonde Fabio-like hair hanging down onto his shoulders.
Sire smiles to the fans, as the other door opens.] TS: Not him too! NR: Well will wonders never cease! [Out steps "The Foundation" Tony Pride, wearing black slacks, a cream colored shirt and a pair of silver, designer shades. Pride nods to the fans himself as the duo begin to make their way into the arena. You just know something good is going to happen tonight.]
NR: Sire and Pride are here... and they're together! OH MY! TS: That's about as exciting as a chess match in slow motion. NR: What could it mean?! TS: Why does everything have to always "mean" something to you people? It meant they shared a ride to the arena, you fool. Now pipe down.
[With a professional fluidity, the scene duly crossfades to reveal the backstage realms of the Rose Garden, where taking "center stage", with an undeniable sense of purpose fueling his every forward step, a squat but powerful figure is seen. For his part, this as yet unnamed male entity is attired in a simple black t-shirt/blue cargo pants/black boots ensemble, which is in turn almost completely enshrouded within the flowing folds of a raven-hued trenchcoat. His short cropped blonde hair serving to only further highlight the sense of purpose that resides behind his clear, green eyes, the clean shaven warrior comes to a halt and acknowledges the camera lens set front of him. His voice is low and calculated, but is by no means hostile in intent.]
Man: Well, I guess tonight's the night, huh? After all of the emotional suffering and the trying physical tribulations that I've endured over the last few months, I'm finally here... I'm now officially inducted into one of the bona fide "Shows" in professional wrestling.
[A half smile dances across the warrior's lips, but the effect is only fleeting in its duration. A "back to business" expression almost instantaneously takes over once more.]
Man: Now, I don't pretend to assume that I'm to be the next "big thing" or that I'm going to turn heads with my infectious, charismatic personality.
But one thing that everyone can be assured of is the fact that I am here in
ASE to compete! Of course, wins I cannot guarantee, and losses are going to be inevitable, so all that I can expect from myself is to give 100 percent, each and every time I step into that ring.
[Changing his posture, the "unnamed" raises his taped hands from his sides and places them upon his hips. Instinctively, he tilts his head slightly to one side, as he possibly, for the time being at least, concludes his verbal musings.]
Man: ...And 100 percent is what I shall always give. Irrespective of whom
I face, they're gonna know at the very least that they've been in a fight...
and who knows... tonight may even be the night for me to instigate a little something like that, too.
[Without any further ado, the fighter moves swiftly back into motion, deftly stepping past the cameraman and leaving the corridor in a state of cloying emptiness.]
* * * * * [Rather the whisk off to the commercial break as may have been expected, we are treated to some more "pre-recorded" footage from the ASE Press
Conference held the week previous to tonight's debut show.] [What we see is "The Foundation" Tony Pride who strolls up to the podium after exchanging an inside joke with his best friend, Steve Sire as they pass each other on the stage. Pride, immaculate as ever, wears a custom made, Armani, royal blue shirt and white pants with matching white shoes for the occasion. The sparkling of his platinum and gold Rolex shines from the flashing lights of the press. His appearance has somewhat changed in his nearly six months away from ring action. He's appeared to have bulked up to possibly the biggest he's ever been in his career. His normally bleach blonde hair is now showing it's natural auburn roots, and as he was kind enough to shave for the occasion, the trademark stubble gone from his face.
"The Foundation" smiles as he addresses the press.] Pride: The story of my life is it not? Following behind "Sexy" Steve Sire? [This gets a slight chuckle from the press and from the members of the panel as well.] Pride: When Stu and others came to me with the premise of the ASE, I have to admit I was more than a little nervous. I vowed to myself and my family that I'd be done with in-ring action when I retired last June. Yet as I've sat at home and watched the direction of sports entertainment and the way it's evolved, I couldn't help but to become a fan again, and have that urge to be apart of it.
Even though I've laid claim to being one of the best pure wrestlers this business has ever had to offer, I'd be lying if I said being a showman wasn't a huge part of what made me the man I am today. From the lavish outfits, to the lifestyle, all the way down to the catchphrases, entertaining people is what "The Foundation" is about. And Entertainment is what the ASE is going to be all about, and that's why I'm proud to be a part of it.
[We now begin to make brief cuts as we bounce to and from various press people as they ask Pride a battery of questions.]
*** Reporter: Uhm, Mr. Pride, you retired from the ring to spend more time with your family, but also to heal your ailing hip, which you had to have replace d earlier on in your career. How does your family feel about your return to in ring action, and how is your hip feeling? Can it once again take the rigors of the ring?
Pride: I did retire to spend more time with my family, and thanks to the less intense schedule of the ASE I can still fulfill those obligations. As far as my hip goes, I've spent the last six months working out like a mad man, wanting to be in the best shape of my life, for the rest of my life.
I've added fifteen pounds of muscle since I retired, and I feel I'm in the best shape of my career. The hip still does bother me, but it's no different than before. I worked through it before; I'll work through it now.
*** Reporter: Mr. Pride, as you made mention to earlier, you've always said you felt like you were in the shadow of Steve Sire, your professed best friend in this industry. With him also being in the ASE, does that cause a problem with you and more importantly, with your friendship?
Pride: [pausing for a moment as he looks at Steve Sire] Well, I think everyone knows that Steve and I are like brothers. Where he goes, I go, and vice versa. We've done battle before, and more often than that been side by side before. Here I'd hope for more of the latter than the former, but when it comes down to it, if we have to go toe-to-toe, then that's what we'll have to do.
Reporter: To see whom the better man is? Pride: There's no need to see that, he already knows it's me. [The room chuckles as Pride gives that trademark smirk and looks at Sire.] *** Reporter: It's no secret that in wrestling years, you're getting up their in age. And even towards the end of your career, we saw a lot more cautious arsenal on display from you; can we expect the same from you here in the
ASE? Pride: Jesus, a guy turns 33 years old a month ago and you're ready to have me drink Ensure. [he smirks] Nevertheless, I'd tell you to expect the unexpected from me. The ASE is a whole new ball game; it's the Evolutionary
Revolution of professional wrestling as we've come to know it. Which means the players in it must evolve and revolutionize their game along with it.
So you can expect classic FTP, and probably a little bit more. *** Reporter: Do you have any plans on your fiancée Destiny once again accompanying you to ringside? And if not Destiny, will any other woman be by your side?
Pride: Well, Destiny has her hands full with little Stephan as it is, and she wants to spend time just being a mother. If she ever had the urge to return to my side in the ring wars, we'd have to discuss it as a family.
But in all reality, it wouldn't happen in the foreseeable future, as she's currently two months pregnant with our second child.
[The room buzzes, as this is new news to most of the press, and the wrestlers.] Pride: As far as another woman, I seriously doubt it. I'm sure Steve will have enough of his women around to share the wealth.
*** Reporter: You've been World Champion in every organization you've worked for. You're a hall of famer in every place you've worked. What could possibly be your goals for the ASE, and is the World Championship one of them?
Pride: That's a good question. Others have said it before, if you're not in this business - regardless of the company - to become the World Champion, then you don't belong in this business. That being said, is it a goal?
Possibly. Do I need it to fulfill my career? Probably not. But rest assured I'm in the ASE to entertain and to prove I'm still one of best sports entertainers, slash, wrestlers on the face of god's green earth.
Because I hold one title that no one will ever be able to take away from me. And that's the title of being the man that is... WHHHHHAAAAATTTTTTTT'SSSSSSSSSS CAAAAAUUUUUUSIIIINNGGGGGG ALLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLL THHHIIIIIIIISSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSS!!!! GOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOD GAAAAAAWWWWWDDDDDDDDDD!! [Even the assembled press can't help but cheer this blatant plea for a pop. Pride smiles, as it's been a while for one of the most famous or infamous depending on who you are has been muttered.]
Pride: Thanks, as always, it was your pleasure. [Pride steps away from the podium as a few reporters throw out a couple more questions that he simply ignores. Finally the commercial comes.]
[:::::Commercial Break:::::]
