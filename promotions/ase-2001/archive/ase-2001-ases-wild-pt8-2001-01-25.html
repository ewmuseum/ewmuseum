ASE's Wild 1/17 (Debut Show) (part 8)

January 25, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Hmmm... commercial break is over, you say? Gee... I wonder where we'll go now. Perhaps... BACKSTAGE?! How'd you guess? Anyway, backstage is where we are, in the ever popular locker room to be exact. Johnny-on-the-spot
Kyle Wittman is there, standing side-by-side with... well... with a small, olive-skinned, cruiserweight body with a blur for a head. That's right - a blur for a head. Where this person's head should be the picture has been digitally altered in order for us not be able to make out the face. Why, you ask? Good question. Let's find out.]
Kyle Wittman (KW): We're backstage here at ASE's Wild and with me is luchadore sensation Octavio Mystique. And Octavio, I can't help but noticing you seem to be missing a major part of your attire. Namely your mask.
[The pint-sized cruiser begins yelling in Spanish in a very high pitched voice. Wittman looks at the blur and nods as if understanding completely everything that's being said, although even the most learned of Mexican linguist specialists would have a hard time making out Mystique's garbled
Spanish.]
KW: I see. So what you're saying is that somebody stole your mask out of your locker? Mystique: SI! SI! [And off he goes into more hysterical pseudo-Spanish rambling. I'd try and write some but hey, I don't speak Spanish and I don't care enough about this bit to look it up. Use your imaginations. Anyway, after another brief run of indecipherable mush, Wittman speaks again.]
KW: And you have no idea who took it or why they would take it? [You can guess what happens next. More rambling. And again we rely on
Wittman to translate, not bothering to question how he's managed to figure it out. Besides, odds are you're still transfixed by the weird, globby mess that is Mystique's head. Give in to the power.]
KW: Well, good luck in finding the culprit, Octavio. We look forward to seeing you in action in the coming weeks. Norm, Sheik... back to you.
[Kyle turns towards Octavio one more time and drops the mic slightly, although we can still pick up his comments before the picture cuts away.]
KW: You know, you should really get that looked it. [The blurry mess that is Mystique's head is seen shaking back and forth as we cut back to ringside and Norm and the Sheik.]
NR: Well there's an interesting development. TS: What in the name of Allah is wrong with that boy's head? NR: Actually, Sheik, I believe that was just a trick from the boys in the truck. They digitally altered Octavio's picture so we wouldn't see his true identity.
TS: Why? NR: Because he wasn't wearing his mask. TS: Pah, another silly American custom. NR: Actually, Sheik, it's a Mexican custom, not an American one. TS: Don't correct me, pig! NR: [*sigh*] Certainly. [The hyper, punk-tinged opening chords of Soundgarden's "My Wave" blast through the arena. The song, with it's bouncy beat and gritty sound is an immediate hit with the fans, although this being the first show they really don't have that Pavlovian response down yet since they don't know who it represents. That'll change soon enough though.]
## Take, if you want a slice ## ## If you want a piece ## ## If it feels alright ## ## Break, if you like the sound ## ## If it gets you up ## ## If it brings you down ## ## Share, if it makes you sleep ## ## If it sets you free ## ## If it helps you breathe ## [As the crowd bobs their heads in rhythm with the tune their attention turns towards the entrance. It's there that they get their first look at the next in tonight's line of ASE Superstars, as Devo Tremors steps out onto the ramp, fists raised high above his head in a triumphant stance.]
"RRRAAAAAAAAAAHHH!!" NR: There he is, Sheik! "The Future" is here! TS: Pah. He looks like a midget. [Midget might be a bit harsh, although there's no denying that at 5'11" and
205 lbs. he is not exactly a giant in this industry. Regardless, Devo's proven himself to be a more than capable performer over his short career.
His trim cruiserweight body glistens under the arena lights thanks to a healthy coat of body oil and his short, spiked, blond hair is also dripping with a fresh dose of water. His attire of choice tonight includes a brand new pair of silver wrestling tights with metallic blue shooting stars splashed all over them and a pair of matching blue boots. His t-shirt is the first of many Devo shirts he'll hock as the months go by, this one being a similar metallic blue with a black silhouette of Devo in mid-flight on the front, his body in a tight spiral like a torpedo as he delivers his famed
Tremor Driller finisher. In large silver letters above the figure are the words "The Future". Devo spins around to show us the back which reads
"Prepare to be chilled, spilled, and drilled" in jagged, silver lettering.]
## Don't come over here ## ## And piss on my gate ## ## Save it just keep it ## ## Off my wave ## [The fans go understandably nuts as Devo begins to jog towards the ring, the woman in attendance being particularly noisy with their appreciation for the cute but harmless superstar. Devo makes his way to the ring and leaps up onto the apron before vaulting himself over the top rope and into the ring.
With boundless energy he hops up onto one of the nearby corner buckles and plays to the crowd.]
"RRRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHH!!!" NR: One of the most popular athletes in the world today, and American
Sports Entertainment has him.
TS: Says you. He is overrated. NR: But listen to that crowd, Sheik... they love him. "RRRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHH!!!" TS: So? What do they know, the uncultured swine. [DING! DING! DING! DING! DING!] RA: ASE fans, our next match this evening is a special exhibition match! Introducing first, from Boston, Massachusetts, he weighs in tonight at two hundred and five pounds...
.....DEEEEEVOOOOO TRRRRRRRRRREMORRRRRRRRRRSSSSS!!!!! "RRRRRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHH!!!!!" [More cheers as Devo moves from corner to corner and pumps his fists to work the crowd. "My Wave" eventually cuts out and he drops back down to the mat.]
RA: And his opponent this evening... [There's no use in trying to talk when Angus Young is on the guitar with a rip-snorting power riff, and that's exactly what happens as the crunchy opening chord of AC/DC's "Inject The Venom" tears through the arena P.A.
Two quick blasts from the mighty Angus guitar leads to the pained vocals of
Brian Johnson, and with a view simple lines the mystery of who this song represents is all but revealed.]
## No mercy for the bad if they want it ## ## No mercy for the bad if they pleeeeeaaaaad ## ## No mercy for the bad if they want it ## ## No mercy from me! ## "RRRRRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHH!!!!!" [Anywhere else in the country and this song would probably mean nothing, but here in Portland, Oregon all it takes is a scratchy cry of "No Mercy!" to get fans to their feet. Native Son Biff Markum is in the house ya'll, and these fans couldn't be happier. The pop is almost deafening for the most famous wrestler to ever call Portland home, and the surprise factor of Biff making an appearance for ASE only adds to the excitement.]
"RRRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHH!!!" NR: OOHHHH, BOY! LISTEN TO THAT CROWD!! TS: WHAT IS WITH THESE PEOPLE?! NR: BIFF MARKUM IS ABOUT TO BE INTRODUCED AND THESE PEOPLE CAN'T WAIT!! RA: Hailing from right here in PORTLAND, OREGON... "RRRRRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHH!!!!!" RA: Please welcome the Dean of No Mercy University, and - FOR ONE NIGHT ONLY - ASE SUPERSTAR... ....."THE MASTER OF MERCY" BIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIFFFFFFFFFF MARRRRRKUMMMMM!!!!! "RRRRRRRRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!" [WHOOSH!] [WHOOSH!] [WHOOSH!] [WHOOSH!] [KRA-KAA-BOOOOOOOOMMM!!!] [With AC/DC still blaring through the speakers and pyro exploding all around, Biff Markum steps out from under the ASEVision and onto the stage at the top of the ramp. The crowd is going absolutely ballistic for the hometown hero and Biff eats up every second of it. The broad-chested ex-wrestler and current owner/instructor of one of ASE's two training schools is looking in tip top condition tonight. Well, at least as tip top as 38 year-old retired wrestlers can look. Over the years he has maintained his thick upperbody although his stomach has recently begun making up some of the lost ground, a rather pudgy potbelly now noticeable. His face is strong and rugged, his steely jaw tilted proudly upwards as he surveys the crowd with his soft brown eyes. And of course his hair - that hair - is perfectly styled and coifed, just like always.]
NR: They love Biff here in Portland! TS: They love woodland creatures here in Portland too. What's your point? NR: [ignoring his partner's rude comment] Truly a special surprise for the fans here in attendance tonight as they get to see one of their all-time heroes return to the ring for one night only as he takes on one of the hottest young stars in the business. Should be a tremendous match-up!
TS: I must say, I've faced the Master a few times during my career and I have no doubt that even at his age he will have what it takes to defeat this midget. Time it is for an old school beating. Excellent.
[Biff strolls confidently to the ring, slapping hands with his adoring fans as he goes, the entire procedure taking far too long, although no one is likely to tell him to speed it up. NR: I would expect we're going to get a nice clean match here, Sheik. TS: I would hope not. It's not like Biff to play fair. NR: True, he was known as a "do anything to win" type back in his day, but still, I think under tonight's circumstances he may play it a little different.
TS: A camel can never lose his hump, Norman. NR: What? [Devo and Biff shake hands to start the contest and Janice calls for the bell.] [DING! DING! DING!] NR: And here we go! "OOHHHHHHHHHH!!" NR: WOHHH! Tremors out of nowhere with a dropkick right to the chest! TS: How did he-- NR: Devo just leapt up with both feet and drilled Biff in the chest despite the fact that they still had their hands interlocked! What amazing athleticism shown there by the Millennium Kid.

TS: HA! Take that you high flying monkey! NR: Tremors nailed Biff with a cross body block but his momentum took him right up and over Biff and over the top rope. But he was able to prep for the fall and he's up before Biff even knew what hit him.
[WHAM!] TS: Oh, now THAT was a dirty trick! So much for your "clean match", Norman. NR: From the outside Devo just tripped Biff up with a yank of the ankle and now The Future is up on the apron... [WHAMM!!] ...SLINGSHOT LEGDROP! And a beauty by Tremors! Dropped a leg right across Biff's chest there.

[WHAM!] ...but now it's Devo's turn to roll out of the way! And he rolls right over and back and hooks his legs around Biff's shoulders...
CRUCIFIX! He pulls Biff back into a pin attempt! ..................................................ONE! ........................................................................... TWO-- NR: Barely a two count on that one as the Master of Mercy rolls free. TS: What is with these silly modern moves. NR: A CRUCIFIX?! That's not modern! TS: Yes, but he did it while Biff was sitting on his bloated behind. That is not the traditional method I can assure you.
NR: Oy. Anyway, both men up and-- OOF! Big clothesline from Markum, and
Tremors goes down hard! And not wasting any time here is Biff as he scoops
Devo up and locks him in for a suplex!

but Devo lands on his feet-- [WHAM!] And delivers a hip toss of his own! All two hundred and fifty plus pounds of Biff Markum just got flipped to the mat! Impressive.
TS: No it wasn't. His technique was all off. NR: But it did the trick for the time being, although Biff quickly scrambles to his feet...
[SMECK!] "OOOOOOOOOOHHHHH!!" NR: But Tremors with a BEAUTIFUL dropkick! TS: GAH! INFIDEL! Make him stop! NR: And I think that Biff has had enough, as he's heading for higher ground and rolling to the outside.

Biff off his game.

"SHOW NO MER-CY!" [clap clap clapclapclap] "SHOW NO MER-CY!" [clap clap clapclapclap] [On the outside Biff begins to beam brightly as the crowd cheers him on with the long forgotten "Show No Mercy" chant.]
"SHOW NO MER-CY!" [clap clap clapclapclap] NR: This is incredible! These people really do love Biff here in Portland. TS: That's because, as I have said A MILLION TIMES ALREADY... THEY LOVE THE OLD SCHOOL! NR: I think it's just because this is his hometown. [We cut momentarily to the broadcast position where the sheik has risen to his feet and is pounding on the table top like a demented madman. Norm is doing his best to remain calm.]
