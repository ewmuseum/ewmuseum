ASE's Wild 1/17 (Debut Show) (part 7)

January 25, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[No arena this time. No backstage location. This time we open on a simple setting of a maroon, cloth backdrop hanging down armed only with the dim illumination of an off-screen lamp. It's a fairly drab scene, although clearly the mood is intentional. A shadow then crosses the picture as the outline of a human form is cast upon the backdrop. The figure itself is still out of sight, but the presence is definitely felt. A voice begins to speak.]
Voice: You know, some of you may not know me, but most wrestling fans do. Why? Because over my career I've been probably the most controversial man in all of sports entertainment. My on-screen and off-screen personas are one in the same, and because of this, what you see is what you get.
[No, no, it's not Scott Hall, though the feed may be coming from the Betty
Ford Clinic. Instead this strange mystery man moves in front of the camera, the shot only catching him from about chest high down, a bottle of Jack
Daniels ol' No.7 clearly visible dangling from his right hand. Slowly the man begins to turn and face the camera and we see... well, its difficult to say.]
[What we can now make out is that this man, whoever he is, is currently wearing a half-white/half-black suit, split right down the middle. As the camera begins to slowly pan up we see that the suit was obviously tailored for a larger chested man, as the body, though in the suit, seems chiseled.
As we get to the head, we see a familiar face highlighted by short hair, a pair of ReVo sunglasses and a smile. He lifts the bottle of whiskey to his mouth, takes a pull, and wipes his lips.]
[Back in the arena where the crowd is watching this scene unfold on the
ASEVision, a massive pop is heard. They know who he is.]
[Such is the effect of the man known as Pots. Once a promising mid-carder with a willingness to push the envelope, a horrific accident has left him not only with a scarred face but a scarred psyche as well. What exactly does this "new" Pots bring to ASE? Let's listen in.]
Pots: It's been a while. As the no-compete clause in my last contract runs out, we fill the loop holes with acts of grandeur on the big screen. As you'll see, I'm a man of many things, and I've always taken them to the next level. Putting on the show was what I was there for, and I did my job the best.
[He takes another swig from the J.D.] Pots: Well, y'know what I've noticed? My no compete clause ends... REAL
SOON. And when the championship committee came to me asking me to join ASE,
I said I was out of the wrestling business. I was tired of closing down every joint I joined. I mean, hell, can't a guy get a world title shot, win the belt, and retire in peace? Oh, wait, all you folks in the crowd that know me KNOW that ain't the way it went down. But enough about the past.
The Committee came to me, and said, "Pots - you draw, and you're a team player. We need big names to bring in the ratings." Sooo... I looked at them and told them to put a number down on a piece of paper and I'll get back to them. So they did, and I did.
[Swilling again.] Pots: Well they must have the same negotiators as the last company I worked for because they basically told me they wouldn't censor my actions and they'd pay me real nice. So you know what that means, boys and girls?
[A low rumble is heard from the crowd in the arena. They seem to know where this is headed.]
Pots: That's right. The King of the Swerve is back. The man of the hour is me, and anybody else who don't like it can rot in hell. Because I'm going to do what I set out for last time, and that's TAKE OVER THIS TOWN! I don't care who I trample on, I don't care who tries to stop me, and I think it's been a long time coming. And anybody who does get in my way... heh, be it "Starman", or "The Foundation", or even you "Braveheart"... well, let's just say I think its high time I became King of the Hill as well as King of the Swerve.
Be prepared, folks... [That old, familiar smirk creeps across Pots' lips.] Pots: Because my reign of terror begins NEXT WEEK!! "RRRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHH!" [With one more drag from the bottle and a devilish grin, Pots wipes the back of his hand across his mouth and begins to chuckle in a truly sinister manner. The picture fades quickly to black and we are suddenly dumped into the laps of Norm and The Sheik at ringside. Sheik seems pleased with these recent developments while Norm has a slightly more surprised expression.]
NR: My goodness, some ominous words there from the man known as Pots. TS: He is a crazy man. And he backs down from no one. I admire those qualities. NR: I wonder what he meant by his reign of terror? And what of next week? TS: Silly fool, it is obvious that Pots intends to turn this company upside down and he has picked next week to start. Maybe I will get lucky and he will punk you out first, you stupid boar.
NR: Well I for one will be hoping he sticks to his fellow Superstars and leaves us out of this.
TS: American coward. NR: Actually, Sheik, I'm Canadian. TS: IT MATTERS NOT!! NR: Oooookayyyy... [Have you ever tried to crank a really quiet song on a stereo? No matter how high you turn that volume switch, the song never really seems to have the oomph or power you had hoped, right? Well that's what happens here as the Eagles' hit "Desperado", with it's stark piano lead-in begins to play over the arena sound system.]
[DING! DING! DING! DING! DING!] RA: ASE fans, our next match is a LAST MAN STANDING MATCH!! "RRRAAAAAAAAAAHHH!!" RA: Introducing first... NR: Whup! Time for a hard hitting debut for two more ASE Superstars,
Sheik! TS: BRILLIANT!!! Time for some ruthless carnage! [We cut away quickly to yet another shot of wrestling great James Naismith sitting at ringside. Once upon a time he was really enjoying himself. But now, having seen a promising new promotion turned into a circus thanks to a pair of puking tag teams and a no-doubt brutal upcoming Last Man Standing match, his face wears a look of contempt.]
RA: From Waxahachie, Texas... weighing in at two hundred and eighty-nine pounds... ....."THE OOOUUUTLAWWW" JOOOOOHN MCKOOOYYYYYYYYYY!!!!! [A smattering of boos meets the announcement of McCoy. Let's face, he's hardly a household name, and it all it takes is one look as his bloated, cowboy carcass to see why. McCoy looks like your typical tough Texan cowboy. With a gritty five o'clock shadow and a mouth full of tobacco, his body style is probably closest to former Freebird Terry "Bam Bam" Gordy. Not overly-muscular, but definitely a strong, powerful guy. He's got brown eyes, short-brush cut brown hair, a "Midnight Rider" tattoo on his left arm, and a "Desperado" tattoo on his right. He moseys his way to the ring.]
[Darkness begins flickering about the arena. Areas of the crowd go dark as the lights in their location flicker off and on with rapidity and randomness. Finally with one final flicker, all the lights shut off, and an odd blue fluorescent glow surrounds the entrance way. With a flicker the
ASEVision comes to life as "Chasm" by Klay Scott begins to come across the speakers.]
## Lost among the recesses of the undefined, ethereal psychotic haze ## ## Seemingly endless is the fall - no stable memories to recall ## [Slowly the fluorescent glow from the entrance begins to change. From the blue glow, it slowly shifts to an eerie red glow, and smoke begins to flow from the entrance way.]
## No sure warning ever heard ## ## You Are FRAGILE! ## [With the refrain a figure emerges from the darkened entranceway. He takes a single step and emerges from the smoke hazed entrance. His long black hair drapes across his face and he stands stock still as the music continues.]
## When you expect the pain - nothing more to ascertain ## ## When you respect the lie - deadly chasm opened wide ## [Each of the screens flicker to life once more with an image.] ## Victim of the killers seething in the deepest mind ## ## Ever existing out of phase ## [KRA-KABOOOOMMM!!] [KRA-KABOOOOMMM!!] [As the image plasters itself upon the screen, twin explosions bracket the rampway and highlight the face of BloodAngel. He wipes his hair back from his face and slowly begins making his way down the ramp as another figure makes its way from the smoke and begins to make their way down with him.
This is Kia Kupalo; her luscious figure greatly enhanced by her form hugging red backless mini skirt.]
## Steadily falling out of line - no trace of living left to find ## ## You came to make me one of your kind ## [BloodAngel stops occasionally to point at a few signs that stand out on his walk to the ring. As he stands at ringside he slowly removes his trenchcoat and hands it to Kia. With a quick smile she steps away from the ring and walks to take a seat beside the announcers table.]
## No sure warning ever heard ## ## You Are FRAGILE! ## [BloodAngel makes his way up the steps and stares out into the crowd before stepping into the ring. He raises his hands and the lights come back up as the music slowly dies down.]

DING!] NR: Forearms to the back and now a waistlock into a... [WHAMM!] OHHH!! INTO A HUGE BELLY-TO-BACK SUPLEX!! And B.A. quickly rolls out of the ring to the floor! TS: FINE! Take the fight to him on the outside, you big Texas slug! This is legal, no? NR: Anything goes in this Last Man Standing Match! TS: SHAZAM! NR: Both men on the outside now and McKoy goes for a right hand but it's blocked by BloodAngel! They tie-up and... [SPLUT!!] SEATED FACE PLANT BY

TS: What do you ask these stupid questions, Norman? There is no one here to answer them. NR: McKoy around this way... look out! TS: GET AWAY FROM ME, COW MAN!! NR: McKoy just knocked the Sheik right out of his seat! TS: Give me back my chair! NR: He's got a folding chair! But here comes BloodAngel who throws himself at McKoy!! [CLUNK!!] "OHHHHHHHHHH!!" NR: Alright, fans, back to the action as it appears as though McKoy suffered a bit of a scratch from that DDT as a small cut has opened just above his right eye.

[THUNK!] RIGHT INTO THE

The Outlaw has until ten to get to his feet or else BloodAngel is the winner!

Rider to follow him to the back. And McKoy, groggy as he is after that clothesline, is giving chase!
[BloodAngel disappears behind the curtain just under and to the left of the
ASEVision. Both McKoy and the cameraman get set to follow, but as McKoy goes through the curtain, he is immediately sent tumbling back out.
BloodAngel appears, holding a silver lid to a serving dish from the catering table which was just inside the backstage area. The lid now has a sizeable dent in it, and is partially smeared with McKoy's blood. BloodAngel holds the lid up to the crowd who cheers the sight.]
"RRRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHH!!!" NR: Dinner is served! HAHA!! TS: That was not funny, Norman. NR: Yes, well... BloodAngel pulls McKoy to his feet... [CRANK!!] AND DROPS HIM ONTO THE METAL WALKWAY WITH A PILEDRIVER!!! "OHHHHHHHHHH!!!" TS: This Outlaw person is a total joke! He is being beaten like a criminal. WHICH HE IS!! HA!! I LOVE AMERICAN JUSTICE!!
"HE'S HARD-CORE! HE'S HARD-CORE!" NR: And again this rowdy Portland crowd is getting behind BloodAngel. TS: They are thirsty for blood. This is why we should bring back live stonings! THINK OF THE RATINGS, NORMAN!!
[McKoy gets up to his hands and knees, but BloodAngel is right there to grab a fistful of hair and drag him into the back. The camera follows and turns the corner as BloodAngel flings McKoy onto the catering table, sending delicious condiments tumbling to the floor.]
"RRRAAAAAAAAAAHHH!!" NR: Into the back area they go and B.A. has got John McKoy in a precarious position here, laid out on that table. What's he got in store next?
TS: I do not know, but I would think it has something to do with BloodAngel climbing that nearby set of crates.
NR: I'd say you're right, Sheik! B.A. looking for some height as he scales those... well, those empty crates of Gatorade.
TS: Just so long as he doesn't drink them, he should be fine. NR: WELL NOT IF HE DOES THIS... B.A. LEAPS!!! [THUMP-KRRRUUUUUNCH!!!!!] NR: RIGHT THROUGH THE TABLE!! BLOODANGEL JUST LEAPT ONTO MCKOY AND BOTH

FIVE! SIX! NR: Who will get up first?! Or will either of them get up?! EIGHT! NR: And McKoy is to his feet! And B.A. pushes himself up to his knees as well, although I gotta say I think he took the worst of that table bump! He appears to have cut his forehead open on a piece of the table too.
TS: Look! That cowboy has a piece of animal in his hand! AAAHH!!! IT BETTER NOT BE A SACRED COW!! NR: Relax, Sheik, it looks to be a turkey leg... and he's BEATING B.A. about the head with it! "RRRAAAAAAAAAAHHH!!" [WHUMP! WHUMP! WHUMP!] TS: Talk about a food fight. [WHUMP! WHUMP! SNAP!] NR: WOOPS! The turkey leg just broke in half! So much for that weapon. TS: Yes, and now Thief McKoy will do it the old fashioned way... [THWUNK!!] TS: HE THROWS BLOODANGEL INTO WALL!!! [THWUNK!!] NR: OH MY!! McKoy just tossing BloodAngel around like a rag doll! [Apparently BloodAngel doesn't care too much for authority. What are the odds? Anyway, Kia comes strolling into our picture and she and her bloody man turn and head back into the bowels of the backstage area. We meanwhile cut to another portion of the backstage catacombs, more specifically to the area where the vending machines are. The shot is of looking down the hallway where we get a profile shot of Vic Valari, standing and waiting agonizingly as he stares at the coffee machine to dispense his selection.
As he looks into the machine forlornly, we see a new figure step into the picture at the end of the hall, his head down and buried into a mess of papers he carries. This would be Hadley Mills, still on the hunt for tag team opponents for the team of Pride & Sire for later in the show. It is perhaps due to his concern over this situation, or maybe just his natural clumsiness, that causes him to bump into Valari, just at that exact moment when Vic is pulling his long anticipated coffee from the machine.]
Valari: God damnit. Why don't you watch where you're going, you stupid idiot. It's just lucky for you that this stupid machine gave me a luke warm cup of coffee anyway.
Mills: Uhh, sorry, Mr. Valari. It's just with all the goings on around here tonight, I'm just not thinking straight.
[Suddenly the light dawns on marble head and Mills gets a "brilliant" idea. His mood brightens noticeably.] Mills: But you might be able to help me after all. Valari: This better be good. Mills: Well, I guess you ah, you know, may have seen the "dilemma" we've run into concerning the tag team championship.
Valari: Yes, and? Mills: Well, ahhh, I need a second team to take on Sire and Pride later tonight for the titles. And I was thiiiiinking... you know, perhaps you'd take it as an apology from me, for ahh, bumping into you, by being a part of that second team?
[Mills squints and scrunches his face and upperbody up as if he were expecting to be hit.] Valari: Don't I need a partner? I mean, I know I'm going out there to re-earn my reputation and all that, but taking on two men at once may just be a little difficult.
[Relieved that Triple V seems at least open to the idea, Mills relaxes his stance.] Mills: Ahh, yes, well... don't worry about that, Mr. Valari. I'll find you a partner. In the meantime, perhaps you should just go and get changed before the coffee stains that new shirt. That match will be shortly.
Valari: Sure, whatever. [under his breath] Weird kid. [Valari walks off towards the locker rooms while Hadley take a second to collect his thoughts. He mumbles to himself.]
Mills: Three down... one to go. [He is about to turn and go the other way when a voice makes him stop dead in his tracks.] Voice: Now that was a mistake, plain and simple. Mills: And what makes you say that? [The impressive frame of the former "Star of the NWC", Lee Clark appears on screen, looking down slightly at the much smaller Mills.]
Clark: Vic is a loner. And he's a treacherous, backstabbing lowlife. Trust me, if anyone should know about that, it's me. After all, he's turned his back on me on two separate occasions.
Mills: Oh, Mr. Clark. Well... ahh... you heard him earlier though. He said himself that he is a changed man. Why not give him the chance to prove it?

be... any..... body........ [Hadley begins to stare at Clark as yet another "idea" pops into his head.] Clark: Don't even go there, Mills. I know that glint in your eye. No way, no how, never.

Mills: Oh, but I can, Mr. Clark. You see, I was told by the committee to do "whatever was necessary" to find participants for tonight. You wouldn't want to go against their direct orders... would you?
[Clark scowls slightly, knowing he's trapped if he wants to succeed in ASE. Mills smiles smugly.] Mills: And anyway, look at it this way - you're getting a chance to hold the first piece of ASE gold to be given out. [to himself] You know, I'm really beginning to enjoy this.
Clark: Co-holding with Vic Valari? I could think of a lot better things, such as having all my skin removed. But let me just say this before you walk away all pleased with yourself. Should Vic turn on me out there, or anything go wrong in this match, it will be your hide.
[Clark turns and walks away, and suddenly, Hadley Mills is looking worried again. He swallows hard and we fade to the break.]
