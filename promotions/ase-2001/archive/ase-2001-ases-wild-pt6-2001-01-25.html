ASE's Wild 1/17 (Debut Show) (part 6)

January 25, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[A trio of heretofore unseen people now stand before us. On the left is a middle-aged man sporting a remote headset. He's pretty generic looking, with a well-trimmed goatee and short brown hair. He wears a red ASE t-shirt and carries a bundle of cable in his right hand while in his left is an apple which he currently bites into. On the right is a short, young woman with a black ASE ballcap pulled down almost over her eyes. She's hunched over in her stance, focused entirely on the information contained on the clipboard she cradles in her left arm. She appears to be biting her tongue as she frantically erases something from the attached sheet. In the middle stands Hadley Mills.]
[Making his on-air debut for ASE, Hadley Mills is a face the fans will come to know quite well as the company progresses. He looks young, considerably younger than his 33 years would dictate, with a soft, smooth face that looks like it's never seen the business end of a razor thanks to pure lack of need. He wears big round glasses on his squarish face, and his wide, baby blue eyes are magnified slightly by the thickness of the lenses. His soft blond hair is parted neatly on the right. At the moment he is wearing a red dress shirt with a black necktie that sports a "WB" logo (shilling for the network? how tacky). A remote headset of his own is currently resting around his neck while he presses a cell phone to his right ear. He appears to be listening intently to whatever's being told him, adding only assorted grunts of comprehension to the conversation.]
[For the record, Hadley Mills is the man who has been assigned the unenviable task of being the eyes, ears, and more importantly the mouth of the mysterious ASE Championship Committee. While he has attempted to preach to anyone who'll listen that he has no authority himself and is rather just a messenger, he is nonetheless the man through which all decisions of the
Committee will be delivered, and it is because of this that he is already a man who is mistrusted by the boys in the back. On his own he is an affable, albeit clumsy guy, and physically it's apparent he's not up to the task of telling pro wrestlers what to do and where to go. But regardless, the job is his for now and at the moment that means listening to the Committee themselves as they deliver his latest batch of instructions.]
Mills: Uh huh. [pause] Yes. [long pause] Sure, I get it. [longer pause] Ok. If I may-- [no you may not, and another pause] Are you sure?
[pause] But I-- [brief pause] Yes, but I-- [quickie] I don't know who--
[oh-so-brief pause] Yes, sir. [delay - that's like a pause] [*sigh*]
I'll give it my best. [With a look of resigned disappointment, Mills folds closed his cell phone and we watch as his shoulders droop in dejection. The man on his left, who it can now be revealed is in fact Wayne Harper, one of the assistant director for ASE's Wild, speaks up.]
Harper: So... [chew, chew, chew] ...what'd they say? [At this point the girl on the right, Mills' personal assistant Kelli Thomason, looks up from her erasing, eagerly awaiting the verdict.] Mills: Well... they said that since our tag team division seems more interested in behaving like members of a frat house than as actual paid entertainers, that they will not be competing for the tag team titles tonight.
Harper: [as if none of this was really "news"] Uh huh... [chew, chew] ...so? Mills: And, well, we're to hold a new tag team title match tonight featuring singles wrestlers as tag teams.
Harper: [chew] Cool. So who's it gonna be? [crunch - chew, chew, chew] [By now we can see the abject misery and fear in Mills' face. Obviously he doesn't like this next part of the assignment.]
Mills: That's the catch. They want me to decide. And I've got about twenty minutes to find suitable replacements. [he swallows hard]
Harper: Ahhh. [chew, chew] Sucks to be you, man. [amidst his chewing, he pats Hadley on the back] Let me know what you come up with. I'll be in the truck.
[And with that Harper turns and leaves. Mills stares blankly ahead, knowing he's going to have a miserable time trying to pair up the necessary wrestlers in order to make the match work.]
Mills: Hooooooh boy. [With Mills ready to wet his pants, the scene fades to black for a moment before rising back up on a new scene. We are obviously away from the Rose
Garden for this segment, and the promo that plays is clearly something shot in advance of the show itself. At rise we are looking into a jail cell; one that is currently empty save for the bed and other "accommodations". The steel, barred door is open at the moment, although that all changes as a young man is thrust through the door and into the cell. A mighty arm reaches into the shot and slides the door shut, locking it with a big set of keys before disappearing from view.]
[The man we see is currently decked out in the traditional orange jumpsuit of incarcerated people. With his back to us he stands up to a fully erect position, his loose and wild brown hair falling down to just below his shoulder line. A number is stenciled in black across his orange suit...]
A427596J56 [Without giving any indication of who he is, the man begins to speak. His voice is light and a bit scratchy.]
Man: Yeah, so this is where it all started going downhill. [Slowly he begins to turn around to face the camera.] Man: They can say whatever the hell they want, but I know the truth. I was a decent guy until they threw me in this godforsaken hole.
[Finally we get to see this stranger face-to-face. His face is sunken and thin, as if he had lost a lot of weight in a short amount of time. The makings of a beard clings to his face although any attempts to trim it have obviously not been bothered with. His eyes are a dark brown and his scraggly, brown hair hangs down into his eyes. As the saying goes, "he looks like death warmed over."]
Man: Sure, I had my troubles with the law on occasion. So what of it? Nobody's perfect. It was all just petty stuff anyway. I wasn't hurtin' anybody. That is until the county tossed my a[bleep]s in this cage and told me to "reform". Scumbags. They created me.
[Having taken a few moments to really absorb this figure before us, we can now see that despite the apparent weight loss, he's still a fairly decent sized man with a boney but muscular frame. Nothing a few weeks of home cooking wouldn't fix. A series of intricate tattoos run the length of his left arm, with all signs indicating that these are the only tattoos he sports.]
Man: So now they want to let me out. They want to put me back on the street cause they think they've done their job. After years of living like a rat they think I'm gonna be a better person than the man they put in here.
They're idiots. The man they put in here is long gone alright, but he's been replaced by something much scarier.
This society is backwards. We lock up poor folks who are just trying to scrape together a living while the real crooks, the scumwads in white shirts and ties cheat and rob and steal every day. It's sick. And they think they can wash their hands of the real people with their stupid prisons. But they're wrong. Dead wrong. Cuz all they do is create even bigger monsters than the ones they were hoping to get rid of. It's a cycle, see, and they're caught in it. Put away the petty thief only to unleash a criminal mastermind ten years later. The whole thing is crazy. And after all that?
They dump your a[bleep]s out on the street with a bag of old clothes and just enough money to get out of town and they expect you to make a better life for yourself.
Well screw that. I'm not gonna be just another victim of the system. Nope, not me. I got my sights set on something bigger. I'm gonna get 'em where it hurts, by sneaking right back into their lives and making them worship me. And they will. When they see what I can do they'll beg me to join them. To sell their crappy products and signs autographs for their kids.
[The man moves forward in the cell, wrapping his hands around some bars and pressing his face in between.]
Man: That's when I'll kick 'em in the teeth. And that's when the crime spree really begins. [A sick and devilish laugh begins to churn deep within this stranger, eventually spilling out in a low growl.]
Man: Heh heh heh... yeahhhhh... I'm comin' for ya boys. It's about time you people learned what doin' Hard Time is all about.
[More guttural laughing as the man pushes off from the bars and drops back into the shadow of his cell. Fade to black.]
* * * * * [The picture comes up on black and white footage of a large mass of people moving down a city street in one massive throng. Words appear on the screen, fading up from oblivion in tune with the deep, gravely voice that simultaneously speaks them.]
They're coming. Armed with ambition and fueled by self-righteousness that only few man can know, they won't be taking no for an answer.
[The pictures change from the relative peacefulness of the crowd to a dizzying montage of brutality featuring various political demonstrators being dragged and beaten by cops and opposing demonstrators.]
In January of 2001, American Sports Entertainment introduced you to a whole new type of "activism".
In March of 2001, they're bringing that fight to the people. [More shots of violent protest.] So leave your protest signs at home. And put away those petitions. Because this won't be a peaceful demonstration. And it ain't no sit-in. IT'S A MILLION SLAM MARCH. Live - Sunday, March 24, 2001 From the Joe Louis Arena in Detroit, Michigan Only on Pay-Per-View * * * * * [The interior of a bathroom is what greets us next, as we are in the men's room backstage at the Rose Garden. At present we see the form of a man in a black shirt and red pants as he's hunched over a toilet, apparently retching his guts out. Two guesses on who THIS is. We hear the ungodly sounds of vomiting echoing from inside the toilet.]
"HUUA... HUUA... HUUAAAAAAAGGGHHHH!!!" NR: OH MY! That is disGUSTing! [We now hear the sounds of heavy panting and occasional spitting coming from the man in the stall. Confirming our suspicions that this is in fact Daryl
Stark, his partner Rob Lawless pokes his head into our picture. The big man with the long, black hair leans in from the left of our screen and peers into the stall.]
Lawless: Hey there... you okay, G? [Stark's reply?] Stark: HUUAAAAAAAAGGGGHHHHH!!! [Lawless turns his face away from the stall and towards the camera, a wincing expression of disgust on his face.]
Lawless: Damn, man, that's raunchy. What did you eat today, anyway? NR: Oh, this is just too much. This jerk can't even have any sympathy for his own partner.
TS: On the contrary, you ker, I'd say he seems quite concerned. [By now Stark has pushed himself to his feet and turned around in the stall so that he's now facing the viewers at home. His face looks sickly; his eyes half open, his cheeks flush, and large chunks of drool and food particles clinging to his chin and cheeks. Nasty.]
Lawless: Oooh. Ugh. Not good. [Stark's eyes suddenly go to his partner. Lawless looks to be in the peak of health by the way, apparently having escaped the wrath of Sigurd the Elk and been spared of drinking his own demonic concoction. Stark speaks.]
Stark: "It's foolproof", he says. "I got it alllllll under control", he says. Bitch... give me one good reason why I shouldn't stab your punk-ass right now.
Lawless: Hey, no need to get testy. How was I supposed to now that Nord goof had a stomach like a nuclear waste dump? It worked on the other guy, didn't it?
Stark: Yeah. And it also worked on ME, dawg. Lawless: [with a sheepish grin] Heh. Yeah. Iya... I noticed that. [Stark begins to curl his lip into a menacing growl when he is suddenly overcome with another wave of nausea. His eyes bug out and he puffs out his cheeks before whipping around and dropping back to his knees over the porcelain throne.]
Lawless: I'll ahh... go get ya a towel, eh buddy? Stark: HUUAAAAAAAAGGGGHHHHH!!! Lawless: Yeah. I'll do that. [The shot zooms in on Stark as he continues to retch. Meanwhile those pesky
Sports Entertainment cameras can always find another reason to be in the back, and as we cut away and find ourselves looking over the shoulder of young Hadley Mills we realize we've found just such a reason. Hadley walks hurriedly like a flunkie, er, messenger down the hallway as he has obvious business to attend to. He makes his way past the Big Slob and John McKoy who chew on corndogs and guzzle down a carton of milk (they know their big push is around the corner... uhm, anyway). Mills stops in front of a door with a large star on it which simply reads, "Sexy". Mills burst into the door to find, well who else, "Sexy" Steve Sire sitting down in his plush love seat. The crowd pops loudly for him and the man who stands leaning against the wall watching the monitor, who's also known as "The Foundation"
Tony Pride. Pride and Sire look at Mills like he's lost, because he must be to be barging into their locker room like this. Mills just stands and stares at both men]
Sire: We didn't order any pizza. Must have been the Viking dudes down the hall. Now go away, meathead.
[Mills continues to stand and stare at both men. Pride unfolds his arms and slowly walks in front of Mills.]
Pride: Do you no comprende, mi amigo? No pizza. Adios. [Pride snaps his fingers in front of Mills' face which snaps him out of his star struck trance. As he gathers himself slowly, he begins to babble.]
Mills: [a nervous stutter] Uh, uhm... Mr. Pride and Mr. Sire... the championship committee has, uhm... [getting stern for some reason]
...they're ORDERING you to team up TONIGHT and wrestle for the World Tag
Team Championship.
[The crowd in the arena pops at the announcement. Meanwhile Sire stands up and gets right in Mills' face.]
Sire: Listen, meathead, nobody orders me around, and I'm sure the same can be said for my best buddy. So why don't you take your three dollar suit and clip-on tie and go tell this "championship committee" to find another pair of suckers, because--
[Pride cuts Sire off.] Pride: Hold on there, Steve. [putting his arm around Mills, facing him towards the door] So you're telling me, if Steve and I team up tonight and win, we'll become the ASE World Tag Team Champions?
Mills: [stuttering] Th-- tha-- that's correct. Pride: Well you run along and tell the "championship committee" that they have themselves a match tonight!
"RRRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHH!!!" Pride: Now go run along, and tell them. [Pride shoves Mills outside of the door, tossing him into McKoy and the Big
Slob who don't even pause in their pre-match "training". As the door slams we go back inside the locker room. Sire sits back down, looking curiously at Pride.]
Sire: What the hell'd you do that for? Now we have to go wrestle. I had plans with Erotica that were going to last all night long.
Pride: And they still can. They can just be a part of our victory celebration after the match. This is a golden opportunity. They just put the band back together and they don't even know it.
[Pride gives that trademark smirk.] Pride: Pride and Sire together again. The ASE and whatever lame team they throw at us won't know what hit them. It'll be just like old times, when we were every tag team's worst nightmare...
[Sire sits and thinks about it, before responding with his own trademarked devious grin. (what's with all the trademarks? -Ed.)]
Sire: ...And every woman's dream! [Pride and Sire both nod approvingly as we fade back to the announce table.] NR: WOW! The reuniting of Pride and Sire as a tag team! And they'll be battling for the tag team titles - TONIGHT! What a shocker!
TS: How'd those infidels get a title shot? That Mills fool could have teamed me with you and we'd be more suitable.
NR: Well I don't know about that. TS: Of course you don't know, you don't know anything, you're an American infidel! NR: Nevertheless, what a blockbuster that'll indeed be later tonight. Fans, we'll be right back! * * * * * [Time for one final clip segment from the previous week's Press Conference to introduce a number of ASE's first signees. This time it's... well, let's let Stu Albright do to honors.]
Albright: Finally Ladies and Gentlemen, it is my privilege to introduce to you the man the wrestling world knows as the "Starman"... Lee Clark.
[A number of bulbs flash as the former "Star of the NWC" makes his way to the podium. Clark is dressed in a very sharp two tone shirt, seeming blue or black depending on the angle you view it from. Black trousers and a pair of Gucci Shoes. We cut to a few lines into his opening remarks.]
*** Clark: To me, the ASE is a golden opportunity for the Sports Entertainment business. Too many of the larger companies are becoming too dependent on one or two stars, not giving others the chance to shine. This was part of the reason I left the National Wrestling Council. The ASE is a new dawn for the wrestling industry, and one that should provide high class entertainment for all the fans out there, without shunning any true talent, young or old.
Unlike many other "sports entertainers", I won't come out here and blind you with a flashy gimmick. What you see is what you get. I have given my life to this industry and there are few who understand it better than myself.
And I promise you this, all the fans of traditional skill and guts in the ring will not be disappointed with the ASE, at least not while I'm around.
*** Reporter: Starman, it's well known you lusted after the NWC world title while you were there, but you obviously came up short of ever winning it.
What have you done in your mind to put that quest to rest and will you be able to focus in ASE with it still in the back of your mind?
Clark: Obviously the NWC Title was a very important goal in my career, but my priorities began to change over my last few months with the council. It seemed to me that more and more, the new executive committee refused to look at me for championship material, seeing me as being the last remaining link to the NWC's past. Obviously at 28, I'm not exactly ready to be written off yet, so I have come to ASE with the goal in mind to prove to them that I am still the best technical wrestler ever to step into a ring. The NWC's loss is the ASE's gain, and I just hope to be able to show the fans of the ASE exactly what I gave the NWC for the past 3 years - a man who is fully committed to the cause, and a man who cares more about this business than money. A lot of men could do with learning that lesson.
*** Reporter: What about Eugene Robinson? You two have shared quite a bit of bad blood in the past and now you're both here. Any chance that that'll carry over?
Clark: Now don't get me wrong, I have a lot of respect for Eugene and his abilities in the ring. After facing him for the largest part of the year
2000, I know better than most what this man is capable of. But I hold nothing personal against Eugene for what he does in the ring. It's a business, and if you're not in to succeed, then you shouldn't be in it. I just think that at times Mr. Robinson does things the wrong way. And I'm usually the only man that will put him straight. If I have to do it again,
I will, but hopefully, he's learned his lesson.
*** Reporter: This is your first foray into a national company. Seeing as how your well known to being a big family man, how will being on the road effect you as opposed to the relative comfort of a regional base?
Clark: Obviously, this was my major concern about joining ASE. Being on the road for a lot of the year has been harmful to many family relationships in the industry before, and it weighed heavy on my mind before I signed on the dotted line. I sat and talked the deal through with my wife and my daughter for some time, and although I was reluctant, due to the age of my son Ethan, I was outvoted. My wife knows that I am at my happiest in the ring, competing against the best, and that in turn makes her happy. And my daughter loves to point me out to all her school friends when they watch the shows on TV. So, in brief, the answer to the question is that obviously it will be difficult, but I will continue to spend as much time with them as I can, and hopefully, things will work out. Failing that, I could always buy a mobile home and take them with me.
[This gets a small laugh from the crowd.] *** Reporter: You're more in the vein of the traditional "technical" wrestlers. Do you think you'll have a hard time converting to ASE since it seems to emphasize the "sports entertainment" over the wrestling?
Clark: I like to think that people haven't forgotten the sports side of the business. While admittedly I am not the greatest entertainer in the company on the microphone, I certainly more than hold my own in the ring, and that is where the fans look for their action. What I may lose to guys like
Steve, Eugene and Tony on the mic, I gain in ring skills. My ability to keep a crowd on edge during the match has never dwindled. But keep in mind that although people's tastes change, it has always been the in ring action that has kept the core support following week after week. And although we're all paid to entertain the crowd, we all do it in different ways. Just watch the ASE product, and you'll see what we mean by "Sports
Entertainment". [And with that we fade to black. Cue to ungodly product shilling machine.] [:::::Commercial Break:::::]
