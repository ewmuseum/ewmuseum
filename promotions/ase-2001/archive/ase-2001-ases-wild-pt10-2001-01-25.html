The building is jumping as the pair begin a confident walk to the ring.]

## Do you bury me when I'm gone? ## ## Do you teach me while I'm here? ## ## Just as soon as I belong... ## ## Then it's time I disappear! ## [Clark turns himself around and drops down to the canvas in one motion, staring across at his opponents.]

NR: VALARI!! VALARI!! TRIPLE V IS BACK!!! TS: IMPOSSIBLE!! "RRRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHH!!!" NR: "Vicious" Vic Valari is here! And he's not going down with a fight! With a white bandage wrapped around his damaged skull, he stares toward the ring with a ferocious intensity. Then, step by step, he begins to slow stumble forward down the ramp.
