Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

On 1 May 1996 a single post pitched an entire promotion built around one joke: every wrestler's name prefixed “Bad Boy,” under the tagline “Be a Bad Boy,... and act like it too.”

Its own invented roster carried the bit all the way through: Bad Boy Colt, Bad Boy Steve “The Slasher” Latini, Bad Boy “The Head Patriot” Desert Storm, and Bad Boy Edgar Figaro. The post admits itself in its own closing line: “I'm sorry,...but I just had to do this. Now,...can't we all just get along?”

A fan's reply three days later is the last trace of it anywhere in the record. No card, roster, or result of any kind survives, and its own operator later confirmed the whole thing had been a staged publicity stunt rather than an actual promotion.
