WXW Saturday Night Destruction

9 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
***************************************************************************** Saturday Night Destruction ***************************************************************************** Dave Scott: Welcome everyone to another edition of Saturday night Destruction! Tonight we've got a great lineup. Eternal Revenue faces White Phoenix and WXW Champion Hasa "Moon Tiger" Osaki in the main event.
Tony J: Don't forget the big tag title match-up between the two best teams in
WXW, the champs Damage, Inc. and the challengers, Billionaire Bill's
Diskk Krrash!
DS: We also have a couple of major announcements. Last time, on Wednesday Ring Wars, Rano, in a match with Dangerous Dan Diamond, suffered a severe knee injury. He will be out indefinitely.
TJ: The doctors have told him it looks career-threatening. After watching him land knee-first on the steel railing and then being put in a figure four, I think they could be right.
DS: Our second major announcement... professional wrestler "The Economist" Randy Blake has purchased a majority of the shares in WXW. With that controlling share, he has declared himself WXW President. We haven't had a president before, only a championship committee, so I don't know how this will affect anything.
TJ: Well, let me be the first to welcome Mr. Blake and let me say that I'm positive he will do a great job. DS: Sucking up already, Tony? TJ: He controls our jobs now, Scott. I'd suggest you do the same. DS: We'll have a full report on this development in the future. For now, let's get ready for some WXW action! Wasp Man w/Roscoe Law vs Gang Rhule This was a very close match with both wrestlers getting several near pinfalls. Rhule went for a splash in the corner, but Wasp Man moved out of the way just in time. Wasp Man followed with a dropkick and a moonsault to get the win.
Winner: Wasp Man by pin (9:55) DS: Law and Order are coming up next. Here is what they had to say prior to tonight's match-up... -------------- [Law] Well, well, guess we gotta play Dog Catcher tonight and put the Detroit Doggpound in it's place! [Order] Quite true. You see that undefeated streak of yours really doesn't matter to us because that will be coming to a end tonight.
[Order] So Detroit DP..come and get some of us..punks like you jump up to get beat down against us all the time and tonight will be no different.
-------------- Law and Order vs Detroit Doggpound w/"Lethal" Lyle Morrison Law and Order dominated early on by keeping the match inside the ring and mainly a technical contest. Tony Pitt reversed a Johnny Law Irish whip to turn the tables. Pitt hit his Springboard Legdrop, but Law managed to roll outside.
The Doggpound followed, and eventually all four wrestlers battled outside. Pitt was the only one aware of the count, and got back into the ring just before the ref reached ten.
Winner: Detroit Doggpound by CO (10:23) Vinnie, the Jersey Boy vs Metalhead Another very close match, neither Vinnie nor the Metalhead could get a long offensive series going. Vinnie attempted a powerbomb, but Metalhead turned it into a frankensteiner. Metalhead went to the top and tried to cross body block
Vinnie, but the Jersey Boy caught him and delivered a backbreaker. Finally, a routine sunset flip by Metalhead turned out to be the finisher to the match as Vinnie was surprisingly pinned.
Winner: Metalhead by pin (7:16) TJ: Up next is a great match-up. That lamebrain Brian Damage takes on the one and only Blade Larry Brown! DS: We asked Brown for his comments concerning the match... ---------------- THE BLADE LARRY BROWN SAYS: Brian Damage, you're about to get damaged when you step in the ring with
me! I have to say that you are quite an impressive wrestler but you're no
Larry Brown. The fact is, you're a man whose future is very dark. Come bell time, you're going to experience a dry throat and sweaty palms. Your stomach will get tight and and your knees will knock. Don't worry, it's totally natural. I mean you're in a position to have your career totally closed down. So Brain Damage, you're about to see why they call me the
Blade but, unfortunately for you, you're only going to see it once. ---------------- Brian Damage w/Hurly Burly vs "The Blade" Larry Brown Larry Brown attacked Damage before the bell and used every illegal tactic in his repertoire to wear down Damage - chokes, holding the ropes, low blows, and jabbing him with his hockey stick. Damage tried to fight back, but Brown always had an answer for whatever Damage tried, and it was usually a face rake or an eye gouge. Brown took the fight outside, and ended up with an impressive countout win. After having his hand raised in victory, Brown tried to further humiliate Damage. Damage fought back effectively this time and slammed Brown through a table before returning to the locker room. Brown eventually picked himself up and left to loud boos.
Winner: "The Blade" by CO (6:43) DS: Before our next match, let's hear from Order and Chaos... ------------- (Order and Chaos stand in front of their symbol, a modified yin-yang with Chaos's side made of constantly changing pastel colors and a jagged edge. Chaos faces away from the camera, watching the pretty colors.)
Order: I'm afraid we have not properly introduced ourselves. I am Order. Chaos: (he doesn't turn around) And I'm Kaos..no..that's not it...Xaos? No..Oh, wait. I'm Nancy Reagan! Order: You'll have to excuse my partner. He's having a bad reality right now. We are the anthropomorphic manifestations of the primal natures of order and chaos, the two most fundimantal forces in all of creation.
Chaos: Ooohhh...big words...rutabega... Order: And the fools at the WXW have put us against the Sailors? They know us well. Sailors have been falling prey to the forces of Nature for eons, and Nature is our slave. This will be but a distraction.
Chaos: (singing) Saaaaaaailing...takes me awayyyyy....do do do dooo doodo
Order: But next, it appears Law and Order still want a piece of us.
Chaos: (pointing at his partner) He's got your name...hahahaha! Order: That's right. And what are we going to do about it? Chaos: (turns toward the camera) We're going to TEAR IT OUT OF HIM! (He leaps at the camera as the screen turns black.)
------------- Order/Chaos vs the Sailors Order/Chaos completely manhandled the inexperienced Sailors. Chaos had the
Sailors at his mercy a couple of times, but rather than finish them off, he turned to the crowd and yelled incomprehensible insults at them. Order got his partner to tag him in. Order applied a half-nelson sleeper on Sailor I and put him out. Order/Chaos had apparently won, but Chaos wasn't finished.
He moonsaulted the unconcious Sailor and continued to beat on him after the referee had stopped the match. Chaos didn't take too kindly to the ref's warnings and shoved him to the mat. Order finally got his partner under control, but the ref had already reversed his decision, giving the Sailors an upset win.
Winner: Sailors by reverse decision (DQ) (4:45) Wolfgang Amadeus Skullcrusher vs Fisto Flash These two met a week ago, with Skullcrusher getting the win over Flash. This time, Fisto thought he was better prepared. Skullcrusher stayed away from
Flash for the first few minutes. He toyed with Fisto, apparently making fun of him by holding one of his hands behind his back throughout the early going.
Fisto became frustrated when it seemed Skullcrusher did not want to fight. Skullcrusher reached into his tights and pulled out a squirt gun, spraying water in Flash's face which drove him berzerk. Flash pounded on WAS and administered a brainbuster. As WAS laid on the mat in pain, Flash made fun of him, raising his metal fists in the air to the boos of the crowd. Flash took too much time, allowing WAS to regain his senses. Skullcrusher went on the attack. WAS threw Flash into the corner, running in after him. Flash caught him and executed a stungun which landed WAS's face on the turnbuckle. Flash rolled him up for the win, gaining a small measure of revenge.
Winner: Fisto Flash by pin (11:08) Irish Shamrock vs Nikita Korolov Irish Shamrock hiptossed and armdragged "the Bear" whenever he got close. Shamrock then went to work with a belly-to-back suplex and a gorilla press to put Korolov in trouble early on. Just two minutes into the match, a completely dumbfounded Korolov left the ring and headed to the locker room. Shamrock called to him from the ring to return, but he wanted no part of Shamrock. As the ref called for the bell, out of the crowd from behind Shamrock jumped "The
Blade" Larry Brown. Still not realizing Brown was there, Shamrock took a wicked shot from Brown's hockey stick to the back of the head. Larry Brown continued to beat on Shamrock until Brian Damage and Kid Danger made the save.
Shamrock had to be helped from the ring afterwards. Winner: Irish Shamrock by CO (2:14) WXW Tag Team Title Match: Damage, Inc vs Diskk Krrash w/Billionaire Bill Billionaire Bill's men had a second chance to win the tag titles. Damage, Inc beat them in the finals of the tag tournament to win the titles originally.
Diskk Krrash looked more impressive this time than last. Major Bug and General Failure slowed the match down to their pace early on. Bug used his weight against Shocker to wear him down. Maverick tried his luck against
General Failure, but Failure outwrestled him. Ten minutes into the match, we join it in progress...
DS: Shocker is trying to make the tag to Maverick. Failure drops an axe- handle on his back! TJ: That'll slow him up. DS: Failure drags Shocker back over to his corner. He tags in Major Bug. Bug hammers away at Shocker while his partner holds him. Finally,
Failure gets out of the ring. Shocker gets slammed to the mat by Bug.
He's in a lot of trouble. TJ: Billionaire Bill has never looked so happy. He knows he's close to winning those belts. DS: Actually, it's Diskk Krrash who are close to winning... TJ: They're nothing without Billionaire Bill. He deserves those belts if his men win. DS: Major Bug runs off the ropes...legdrop...missed! Shocker rolled out of the way...He's rolling all the way over to Maverick and he makes the tag! Maverick comes in and wails away on Major Bug. He just nailed
General Failure, too! Maverick picks up Bug and slams him.
TJ: Maverick's got a lot of power. Major Bug is a big man. DS: Maverick with a big clothesline! Now it's Major Bug who's in trouble! Shocker tags back in. Damage, Inc throw Bug into the ropes and... double shoulder block! Shocker rolls him up...1...2...kickout! TJ: Billionaire Bill is hollering to Bug to tag out! He's going ballistic! DS: Maverick tags in again and Damage Inc continues to double-team Major Bug. Maverick has Bug in a bear hug and Shocker clotheslines him! That's the Crippler! Maverick covers him...1...2...General Failure makes the save! Now all four men are in the ring! Failure has
Billionaire Bill's briefcase! He hits Shocker with it, and it opens, dumping Dos upgrades all over the ring. The referee calls for the bell as Failure frantically tries to put the upgrades back in the briefcase.
TJ: That was a weird ending. Failure was just showing Shocker some upgrades, and the ref disqualifies him. Go figure. Billionaire Bill is gonna be mad. Not only did they waste another opportunity to win the belts, but he didn't even move any software.
DS: Well, it's just one hard luck story after another when it comes to Billionaire Bill, isn't it? Something makes me think these fans don't have any sympathy for him.
Winner: Damage, Inc by DQ (11:57) DS: Another tag team contest is straight ahead in our main event! Hasa "Moon Tiger" Osaki and the White Phoenix team up for the first time to face the Chantor's Eternal Revenue. We've got comments from both teams...
-------------- The Chantor: AT THIS TIME I WILL SPEAK FOR MY MEN! OSAKI, MIDNIGHT AND OF COURSE PHEONIX, DON'T WORRY --- ETERNAL REVENUE IS HARDLY DONE WITH ANY OF YOU MISERABLE RATS! SAME GOES FOR ANYONE ELSE WHO WANTS TO FEEL THE PAIN! MY MAKE ME AN OFFER - AND REST ASSURED WE WILL FACE YOU PUNKS IN ANY TYPE OF MATCH! OSAKI, I THINK YOU AND MIDNIGHT DON'T REALIZE THAT
YOU ARE GOING AGAINST A COUPLE OF GENTLEMEN WHO WILL NOT STOP UNTIL THEY
HAVE DESTROYED YOU! NEXT WEEK- ONE WEEK BEFORE THE TAX DAY WE WILL HEAR FROM THE ONE MAN WHO IS HERE TO COLLECT FROM ALL OF YOU. --------------- --------------- (The White Phoenix and Hasa Osaki are sparring in Osaki's dojo as Sensei Yamamoto watches. Hasa catches the Phoenix with a spin kick- backfist combo that sends him flying. The Phoenix gets up and connects with a jumping double heel kick. He helps Hasa off the ground, they bow and clasp hands.)
Phoenix: Honorable Osaki, it is truly an honor to be your partner. But the time has come to put pleasantries aside. A meeting with our destiny awaits. We both have score to settle with our most dishonorable opponents.
Hasa: Indeed. Eternal Revenue seek to end us forever. They brim with false confidence, believing that they have all the advantages, size, strength, determination.
Phoenix: But they have never faced us as one unit. This Saturday, the reign of terror that the Deathmaster and the Tax Collector have held over the WXW will end forever.
Hasa: And the glory of our great spirit animals, the Tiger and the Phoenix, will carry us to victory. We are committed, and our intentions are pure. With the primal power of the fans at our backs, we will be blown to victory. Nothing can stand in our way.
Phoenix: I must bring up one point, though. After our victory in this match, I would very much like the honor of facing you once more in battle, great Moon Tiger. Even if the championship is not at stake, I wish to test my skills against you once more.
Hasa: Though I cannot guarantee that such a thing will be allowed by those in control of the federation, I will do my best to bring that about.
Mr. President, you are watching this, and, I hope, watching this with care. We are doing you a huge favour, ridding the federation of the vermin known as the Eternal Revenue. To repay the favour, grant White
Phoenix a title shot. Is this too much to ask?
Yamamoto: Enough talking. The Deathmaster and the Tax Collector talk too much as well. That's why they are going to lose.
(Osaki and Phoenix being sparring again. The scene ends on a shot of Hasa hitting the Phoenix with a palm strike to the face, the Phoenix's head snapping back and blood flying. Hard training is a necessity when honor and death are at stake...)
------------- Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, this is the main event for this evening. Introducing first, with their manager, the Chantor, from Death Valley and Washington, D.C., respectively, here are Deathmaster and the Tax
Collector... ETERNAL REVENUE!
(Crowd boos loudly) A: Their opponents... First, from Tokyo, Japan, with his manager, Sensei Yamamoto, here is the WXW World Champion... HASA "MOON TIGER" OSAKI!! (Crowd cheers loudly) A: His tag team partner for this evening... From San Francisco, California, here is the WHITE PHOENIX!! (Crowd cheers at first, then tapers off as Phoenix does not appear) TJ: Where is he? The guy probably chickened out instead of having to face Eternal Revenue. I can't really blame him. DS: Osaki and Yamamoto seem surprised as well. Deathmaster and Tax Collector are moving in on Osaki... Wait, the lights went out! TJ: The video wall is showing fire. What's going on? DS: Here he comes! He didn't chicken out! The White Phoenix is being dropped from the roof on a harness. TJ: And he's on fire! Oh, that's sick. DS: White Phoenix lands in the ring and unstraps himself... Tax Collector attacks him... Deathmaster attacks Osaki. We're underway! Hasa "Moon Tiger" Osaki & White Phoenix vs Eternal Revenue w/the Chantor
Eternal Revenue got off to a good start, but Phoenix and Osaki soon rallied.
They used their martial arts skills against the 7'2" Deathmaster in a more coordinated effort than many fans thought they would. They almost looked like tag team specialists rather than singles wrestlers. Tax Collector tagged in and hit Phoenix with a cheap shot to slow him down. Collector followed that up with a fallaway slam. Deathmaster came back in and chokeslammed Phoenix.
A lateral press only got a two count. Phoenix avoided a legdrop and tagged in the world champ. Osaki chopped down Deathmaster and dropkicked Tax Collector off the ring apron. Osaki and Phoenix were in control for the next few minutes, as we join the match in progress...
TJ: Tax Collector is in trouble. He's gotta make a tag to Deathmaster. DS: Osaki with a standing dropkick! He tags in Phoenix. Collector is whipped into the ropes... double clothesline! A cover by Phoenix... 1...2.. Tax Collector kicks out! Phoenix chops and kicks the tax man. Phoenix with a rollup! 1...2... Collector rolls it over...1...2.. and Phoenix just kicks out. I think Tax Collector had a hold of his tights that time. TJ: You would think that. You're so biased. DS: Osaki tags in, as does Deathmaster. Osaki with a running clothesline. The champion drops Deathmaster with a spin kick! Osaki is going nuts on Deathmaster! Osaki sets him up for the Tigerplex!
TJ: The Chantor just hit White Phoenix with something when he wasn't looking. He's hurt. Too bad. DS: Osaki hits the Tigerplex! 1...2...thr... no, it's broken up by Tax Collector! Deathmaster and Tax Collector are now beating on Osaki. Collector has a leather belt in his hand! He's choking Osaki! That's going to be a disqualification!
TJ: Phoenix is in no shape to help Osaki out here. Maybe the Moon Tiger shouldn't have picked such a wuss as a partner. DS: Deathmaster is pummeling Osaki as Tax Collector chokes him. Sensei Yamamoto is trying to help, but he gets shoved down. Here comes Jason
Midnight down the aisle!
TJ: And behind him are several other wrestlers. Maybe the Chantor should get his troops to retreat. You know, live to fight another day and all that. DS: And that's just what he does. Osaki is lying in the ring. Phoenix is on the floor. He still hasn't moved. Eternal Revenue will lose this match, but may have won the battle.
TJ: Face it. They've won the war. They showed everyone here tonight who was the better team. Deathmaster already showed last week who was the better wrestler in his match with the so-called champion.
Winner: Osaki & Phoenix by DQ (13:37) DS: Well, this was a wild card. TJ: If you escaped conscious, then you did good! DS: We'll see you next time on Ring Wars. Good night!
