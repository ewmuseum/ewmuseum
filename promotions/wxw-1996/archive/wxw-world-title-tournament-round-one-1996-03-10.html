WXW World Title Tournament Round One

10 March 1996

I'm Dave Scott, and with me is Tony J.
Tony J: Tonight we'll see the top sixteen contenders for the title go at it. I don't know about you, Scott, but I'm looking forward to some action. You've got some guys here who have held titles in other federations, and you also have some people I've never heard of. They're all coming out of the woodwork for this chance at the WXW title.
DS: Eight matches scheduled for this evening in front of four thousand fans at the WXW Arena in Boston, Massachusetts. This is going to be an exciting event. TJ: So, who's your pick? I've got to go with the huge Euphoria. He's got the size, the talent, and he don't take no for an answer. He's going to win it all. DS: Well, I think the odds-on favorites are Colt and Vinnie, the Jersey Boy. Both have done it before and both will have the support of the crowd behind them. TJ: Yeah, that and ten bucks will get you a ride home. Attitude is what matters and guys like Euphoria, the Ax, and Christian Kingsworth have it. Colt and Vinnie are headed to the prelims after tonight.
DS: Here's another look at the tournament brackets: WXW World Title Tournament Pairings Irish Shamrock \ Wolfgang Amadeus Skullcrusher / \ > Jason Midnight \ / \ Brian Damage / \ > Fisto Flash \ / \ Colt / \ / \ > \ Deathmaster \ / \ Gang Rhule / \ > WXW Champion The White Phoenix \ / Christian Kingsworth / \ / > / Dangerous Dan Diamond \ / \ / The Ax / \ / > Rano \ / Hasa "Moon Tiger" Osaki / \ / > Vinnie, the Jersey Boy \ / Euphoria / DS: Okay, let's get to our first match. Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, this is the first match of the evening. Coming down the aisle first, from Dublin, Ireland, here is the IRISH SHAMROCK! (Crowd cheers) A: His opponent, hailing from Salzburg, Austria, here is WOLFGANG AMADEUS SKULLCRUSHER! (Crowd cheers) Irish Shamrock vs Wolfgang Amadeus Skullcrusher Shamrock and Skullcrusher both entered to the cheers of the crowd. Skullcrusher "conducted" the crowd to get them to cheer even more. The two wrestlers' contrasting styles produced a great match. Shamrock used his power and technical wrestling ability to counteract Skullcrusher's aerial and martial arts tactics. After ten minutes of back and forth action,
Shamrock went for his 4 Leaf Clover figure four finisher, but Skullcrusher managed to reach the ropes to break the hold. From there, Wolfgang staged a comeback, launching a succession of chops and spin kicks which drove Irish
Shamrock to the arena floor. Skullcrusher followed him out with a somersault over the top rope, but Shamrock moved out of the way. The two continued to brawl outside the ring. After Shamrock whipped Skullcrusher into the ringpost, he rolled back into the ring, just beating the count. Irish
Shamrock advances to the quarter-finals with the countout victory. The two shook hands after the match to the applause of the crowd.
TJ: Oh, how sweet. Skullcrusher was just rammed into the ringpost, now he's shaking Shamrock's hand? Give me a break. DS: A very good match. Irish Shamrock advances with a tough win. Now before our next match-up, we have these pretaped comments from Jason Midnight and Brian Damage.... -------------------------------- [Cut to a image of a twilight sky...super-imposed in the background is a clock..and that clock has just struck Midnight!!!]
[Booming Voice] Midnight. A time when things begin anew, the day before has passed, and a new dawn is born. The time has come for a new dawn to be struck in the wrestling world, the WXW. And the man who will lead this new federation has arrived as well....PREPARE YOURSELF....for JASON MIDNIGHT!
[The clock in the sky has vanished, in its place is Jason Midnight dressed in blue wrestling tights and wearing a jacket that says
MIDNIGHT...he smiles] [Midnight] That's right my friends, you heard the Booming Voice, the time of Midnight has arrived! And I have come to take this federation and it's fans on a ride like no other. But we will be taking this trip in two seperate directions. Those who support me will ride to the top of this federation, the WXW championship! But those who try to stop and destroy me will get a one-way ticket to "Dreamland" courtesy of Jason Midnight, and after a trip there..you will never be the same. So WXW, get ready, the time is 12:00...MIDNIGHT IS HERE!!!
-------------------------------- Damage: 16-man tournament. I have to wrestle 15 times in a night to... Hurly: No, 4 times. It's elimination, not round robin. Damage: Robin? Where? Little birdie. Hurly: Oh shut up, Brian. Try to sound intelligent. Damage: I'll fight hard & win. OK? Hurly: It'll have to do. Brian & the B-man are coming after YOU, so watch out. Damage: (Jumping round) Why? Who's there? Hurly: Us. Damage: No, we're here. Unless I've been cloned but I haven't. Or if I have they haven't told me.
Or if they've told me I've forgotten. (Hurly shakes his head & starts playing 'When The Saints' on his trombone.) ---------------------------- A: This match-up is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, from Chicago, Illinois, here is JASON MIDNIGHT! (The arena lights dim. Little lights like stars appear and the "Star Wars" theme plays. The crowd cheers.) TJ: Ooooh, how spooky. Who does this guy think he is, Darth Vader? DS: It's not supposed to be spooky or evil, Tony. A: And his opponent, also from Chicago, with his manager, Hurly Burly, here is BRIAN DAMAGE! (Crowd cheers.) Jason Midnight vs Brian Damage w/Hurly Burly Brian Damage's manager, Hurly Burly, "entertained" the crowd during the match by playing his trombone. Midnight started off slowly, using technical maneuvres to defend against Damage's attacks. However, five minutes into the match, Brian finally caught Jason with a devastating clothesline. He then went to work with kicks and punches to soften up Midnight. Midnight managed to reverse a whip to the ropes but Damage ducked Midnight's spin kick. Damage accidentally took out Midnight's other leg when he ducked the kick. Jason was hardly able to stand the rest of the way, let alone mount much of an offense. Damage powerslammed Midnight, went to the top rope, and when
Midnight finally got to his feet, he hit the Death Sentence. Brian Damage took the match by pinfall. Midnight's injury was not as serious as it looked, and he should be ready to go for the Internet title battle royale.
TJ: You know, I'm almost impressed with this Brian Damage guy. He's kinda weird - and his manager is even weirder - but when he took out Midnight's leg... heh heh... you gotta respect that. DS: I think it was an accident. Although with Brian Damage, you never know whether he means to do anything or not. A: This is the third match of the night. Coming down the aisle, from Brooklyn, New York, here is FISTO FLASH! (Crowd boos.) A: And his opponent, from Austin, Texas, here is COLT! (Crowd cheers loudly.) Fisto Flash vs Colt The crowd was solidly behind the popular Colt in this match-up. Both men are heavyweights, each over 300 pounds. Flash and Colt both tried to overpower their opponent early on, but neither was very successful. Flash struck the first major blow in the match after Colt missed an elbowdrop. He executed a beautiful belly-to-belly suplex, but Colt kicked out of a pin attempt.
Fisto Flash continued to work over Colt, culminating in a power bomb and another cover. Colt once again kicked out before the three count. Flash got a little overconfident, however, and began to toy with Colt. As Flash stood on the middle rope with his metal fists in the air, the crowd showered him with boos. More importantly, it gave Colt time to recover. Flash attempted an elbowdrop from the second rope but Colt moved out of the way. Colt then was a house of fire, knocking Flash about with everything in his arsenal.
A powerslam by Colt looked to have ended the match, but Flash put his foot on the rope before the three. Colt went to the top turnbuckle and attempted a cross body block, but Flash caught him and turned him over into a powerslam and pinning combination. With a handful of tights, Flash pulled off the upset.
The crowd was not happy with the result and neither was Colt, who chased Fisto Flash back to the locker rooms. TJ: Not only is Colt a loser, but he's a sore loser. Fisto Flash beat him fair and square, then Colt chases him out of there. What a great role model. DS: You know as well as I do that that victory for Flash was tainted. TJ: I didn't see nothing. DS: Which doesn't surprise me. Here are some comments from the mysterious Deathmaster... -------------------- Deathmaster: There is a lot of pain and misery in this world.For example Children killing other children, parents killing their children, and children killing parents. WHY DO THEY DO IT? TO PAY HOMAGE TO ME,
DEATHMASTER. And soon, just maybe, a friend of mine will be entering this federation. Together, we will reign supreme. And now I must ask,
WHO WILL BE THE FIRST SACRIFICIAL LAMB OF THIS PROMOTION??? AHHH, GANG FOOL, YOU ARE THE NEXT CONTESTANT IN THE PRICE IS WRONG GAME??? RUN AWAY NOW AND YOU MAY WIN YOUR VERY LIFE! -------------------- A: This next match is scheduled for one fall. TJ: Duh! They all are! A: Coming down the aisle, accompanied by his manager, the Chantor, from Death Valley... DEATHMASTER! (Crowd boos.) A; And his opponent, from the Street, here is GANG RHULE! (Crowd cheers a little.) Deathmaster w/The Chantor vs Gang Rhule Gang Rhule, at just under four hundred pounds, faced off against the 7'2" Deathmaster. Gang Rhule was not intimidated by the eerie Deathmaster, and got off to a fast start. Rhule dropped Deathmaster with a savate kick and quickly followed with a couple of elbowdrops. Deathmaster tried to get outside to take a breather, but Rhule followed him out, continuing the onslaught. After Deathmaster was thrown back into the ring, Rhule leveled the Chantor whom had tried to interfere. Deathmaster got his second wind at this point, and caught Rhule with a knee lift as he entered the ring.
Deathmaster used chokes, eye-gouging, and any other illegal move he could get away with. A big leg drop was followed by a camel clutch, but Rhule would not submit. After escaping the hold, Rhule turned things around and applied a nerve hold to wear the seven footer down. Deathmaster caught Rhule with an elbow to the face, setting up a bulldog and a pinfall victory for Deathmaster at the thirteen minute mark. Deathmaster advances in the tournament.
DS: Deathmaster gets a fairly impressive win over Gang Rhule. Your thoughts, Tony? TJ: You never know. He could be the first WXW Champion. And so could this man... the White Phoenix! ----------------------- (The camera is focused on an eagle soaring high above. It pans down to show a man, facing away from the camera, over the cliff which he stands near. He is wearing a light grey gi, but shirtless. His back is covered with terrible scars, but a tattoo of a flaming bird is visible as well. He finishes tying on a headband.)
"Look upon the scars. Many years ago, the fire nearly killed me. It claimed my young sister. Fire is hungry. It lives. It destroys. Were it not for the blessing of the Spirit of the Phoenix, I would not live today."
(He turns toward the camera. His chest is scarred worse than his back. His eyes burn with intensity.)
"The fire claimed my sensei, my master and teacher. Tore him from my life. Left me alone in the world. Again, it was the Phoenix Spirit who guided me, this time to the WXW. Long have I waited for revenge. He is here.
The man who killed my sensei is here. I will find him, and destroy him. All the world shall see the power of the Phoenix, of flesh burned and tempered in flame.) (He performs a complex kata, a combination of lightning fast punches and kicks, and ends with a thundering eagle call, which is answered by the bird above.)
"I seek revenge, I seek honor, I seek glorious victory in battle. All these will be mine. You cannot defeat the power of The White Phoenix!"
(He turns away from the camera, walks to the edge of the cliff, and kneels down beside it.)
"Remember, in my homeland of China, white is the color of death." --------------------- A: Introducing first, from San Francisco, here is the WHITE PHOENIX! (Fairly good crowd pop.) A: His opponent, from Santa Monica, California, accompanied by Ambition, here is CHRISTIAN KINGSWORTH! (Crowd boos loudly. Lights dim.) TJ: It won't take long for Kingsworth to put this away... JEEZ LOUISE! Someone set the White Phoenix on fire! DS: He set himself on fire! I've never seen anything like that before! TJ: This guy has got some problems and shouldn't be allowed anywhere near a wrestling ring! If he sets himself on fire, what's he going to do to his opponents?
The White Phoenix vs Christian Kingsworth w/Ambition Kingsworth seemed spooked by Phoenix's pre-match display. He found it hard to even have to touch the Phoenix after that. The White Phoenix did not have the same problem, though, and manhandled the arrogant Kingsworth with his martial arts repertoire. Kingsworth escaped to the outside for advice and comfort from his manager, Ambition. Christian stalled as much as he could, but eventually he had to fight. Kingsworth went for a chicken wing and a clover leaf submission hold, but Phoenix would not be denied. After escaping
Kingsworth's submission holds, Phoenix let loose with a flurry of fists and feet that sent Kingsworth reeling. Kingsworth tried to get something going as he reversed an Irish whip and sent White Phoenix into the corner. He ran in behind, but Phoenix jumped to the second rope and surprised him with a cross body and a roll up. The White Phoenix got the win and the berth in the second round.
TJ: Ambition better get Kingsworth out of there before Phoenix starts playing with fire again! DS: I really don't think he would set anyone else on fire. He just does that to psyche his opponents out. And it worked in this match. TJ: They've got another flash queued up, so here it is... ----------------------- <scene opens with Dangerous Dan Diamond cruising down an ocean side road in a silver porsche. "Ultra" by KMFDM is playing on the stereo. DDD turns the music down.....>
"The WXW tournament is upon us. Some of the names in this league are familiar to me. Some aren't. It doesn't matter. A wrestler of my caliber can and will go through anybody. Well I got matched up against the Ax. I've never heard of you before, but as I said, it doesn't matter. I hope you enjoyed your first couple of days in the WXW, because after I lock you up in the Diamond-plex, you'll be running home to mommy!"
<DDD turns the music back up and takes off down the road.> ----------------------- A: Coming down the aisle now, from Clemson, South Carolina, here is DANGEROUS DAN DIAMOND! (Crowd cheers.) A: His opponent, from Boston, Massachusetts, here is the AX! (Crowd boos.) Dangerous Dan Diamond vs The Ax The Ax entered the ring looking determined yet unemotional. Diamond came out to the cheers of the fans. Diamond was able to frustrate the Ax by avoiding the Ax's attacks and playing to the crowd. The Ax just could not find an opening as Dangerous Dan had a counter for everything the Ax could throw at him. Diamond took control with a killer dropkick and a side slam. Diamond hit the Ax with a superkick and went for a cover, but the Ax kicked out easily.
Diamond hit a dropkick off the top rope, but the Ax was not beaten yet. The Ax at one point caught Diamond in mid air, but before he had a chance to do anything, Diamond was able to roll him up. Six minutes into the match, the
Ax went outside and brought a chair into the ring. When the referee tried to prevent him from using it, he decked the referee, resulting in a disqualification. The Ax managed to prove a point, however, by nailing his opponent with "the Ax", a vicious clothesline to the back of the head, and then finishing off Dan with a DDT. The Ax left to loud boos, which he seemed to ignore. Diamond gets the win by DQ, but also leaves with a headache.
TJ: Ha! Diamond gets the win, but he doesn't leave the ring looking like a winner to me. The Ax showed he's not going to take crap from anyone, referees included. DS: Striking an official will result in a fine, I'm sure. Wrestlers have to show respect toward officials. TJ: Only if they deserve it. That one obviously didn't. Speaking of people who deserve no respect, here is Hasa Osaki's manager, Sensei Yamamoto... ------------------------ SENSEI YAMAMOTO: And so a New Federation is born, and once again there will be a mixture of veterens and newcomers seeking to reach for the stars and grab the gold. There will be no doubt many individuals who will shout their names, beat their chests and proclaim themselves to be the real champion; but this is just showboating.
This is because there is only one real champion here; my student, Hasa Osaki. He not only has talent, he not only has the desire to become the first world champion here in WXW; be he also has the honour. He will bring fame and respect to the title because of his attitude and actions.
The spirits have spoken. The stars are aligned. Be prepared for the inevitable. ------------------------- A: This match is scheduled for one fall. Now coming down the aisle, from London, England, here is RANO! (Some cheers, some boos.) A: His opponent, hailing from Tokyo, Japan, with his manager, Sensei Yamamoto, here is HASA "MOON TIGER" OSAKI! (Crowd cheers again.) Rano vs Hasa "Moon Tiger" Osaki w/Sensei Yamamoto Rano got a mixed reaction from the crowd, while Hasa Osaki was pretty popular. "Moon Tiger" blew mist into the air before the match started to unnerve Rano. Rano did not seem to have his head in this match, missing moves left and right. Perhaps Osaki's speed and determination had something to do with that as well. It was pretty much back and forth early on, but about four minutes into the match, Osaki grabbed the ropes to avoid a dropkick and Rano landed on the back of his head hard. Osaki worked over that part for the next few minutes before setting Rano up for the end. Osaki executed a frankensteiner, hooked both legs, and leaned forward for the pin. Hasa "Moon Tiger" Osaki advances to the second round after a very impressive WXW debut.
DS: An impressive match for Hasa Osaki! Rano didn't know what hit him. TJ: Well, Rano has had better matches. I'm sure he'll turn things around before too long. A: Our final match of the night. Introducing first, from Newark, New Jersey, here is VINNIE, THE JERSEY BOY! (Vinnie rides in on his Hog to the cheers of the crowd.) A: His opponent, from parts unknown, with his manager, the Master of Conspiracy, here is EUPHORIA! (Crowd boos.) Vinnie, the Jersey Boy vs Euphoria w/The Master of Conspiracy The final match of the evening was one of the most exciting. Euphoria came in at 6'8" and 456 pounds, while Vinnie was 6'10" and 315. The two big men did not disappoint the fans as they showed what the big men can do. They clashed using all the power and brawling moves you would expect. Euphoria got the upper hand first, using his massive forearms to wear down the Jersey
Boy. Euphoria trapped Vinnie in the corner and used his weight to his advantage with a series of elbowsmashes and shoulder blocks. Vinnie fought back when Euphoria ducked his head for a back drop, and Vinnie nailed him with a knee lift. Vinnie's strategy was to get the big man to the ropes, and try to wear him down with chokeholds and elbows. Euphoria started to fight back, but Vinnie managed to pick him up and slam him. Vinnie was not able to go for the cover right away, giving Euphoria time to recuperate. The fight eventually went to the floor, where the Master of Conspiracy tried to get a pair of brass knuckles to his man, but Vinnie intercepted them. Once
Euphoria was back in the ring, Vinnie clobbered him with the brass knuckles while the Master stood on the ring apron complaining. Vinnie picked up the hard fought - though controversial - pinfall victory.
TJ: He cheated! That's the only way Euphoria was going to be denied. I'd hate to see what Euphoria is going to do now. He had plans made for after this Friday. He was going to be the guest of honor at the
White House as the first WXW Champion. DS: Well, he's just going to have to cancel those plans. Vinnie came through big time and he could go all the way. TJ: Who's going to take Euphoria's place at the banquet? You don't think Hillary can eat for six people, do you? This is horrible. I'm so disappointed. DS: We're just about out of time. As we leave you, here are the matches that will take place in twenty four hours. See you there!
