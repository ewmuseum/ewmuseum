Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The World Amateur Wrestling Federation survives in a single invitation, posted 2 December 1998 and describing itself, in its own words, as “only 1 week old and over 1000 hits and 43 wrestlers in it” — a Geocities-hosted promotion its own founder pitched as more active than the rivals it was competing against: “this isn't like all other E-feds it's special, why you say, cause it's mine, and hell just because it's updated on a regular basis instead of once a month like many feds.”

The invitation asks readers only to visit the site and, if they do, “leave your mark” in its guest book — signed simply, “Prez. JJ.”

No roster, card, or result survives past this invitation.
