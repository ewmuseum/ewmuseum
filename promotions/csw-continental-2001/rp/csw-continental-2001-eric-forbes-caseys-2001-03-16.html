"Extravagant" Eric Forbes, 16 March 2001

16 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade into a Gas Station, Caseys. "Extravagant" Eric Forbes is standing at the counter with his billfold in hand. He's wearing a pair of blue jeans and a plain white shortsleeved shirt. His hair is uncombed but it looks good nonetheless. He also has surgical tape around his nose, which was broken on
Monday Night Explosion by Steven Stellar.
Behind the counter is a male clerk (C) in his early 20's, with long black hair in a pony tale, and a pimply face. He looks a bit tired and unhappy.
Panning around, the surroundings reveal a well kept convenience store, that has a good supply of snack foods, and a healthy amount of CSW Posters, seeing as how it is located in Oklahoma City - home of CSW's most recent Explosion.]
EF: "Hehe, hey. How's it going? [pause, no response] It's the Explorer [pointing outside to his car by the gas pump] on the right." C: "That'll be $29.50." [Reaching into his billfold he rummages around, not pulling out anything. Eric shrugs, then bends over and picks up a large bucket full of coins, he dumps them all out on the counter.]
C: "Dude! What the hell are you doing?" EF: "Paying you." C: "Are you crazy?" EF: [pointing at the coins on the counter] "You can keep the change." C: "What the hell man!" [Eric starts walking to the door.] C: "[Bleep]er get back here!" [Eric turns, raises an eyebrow, and slowly walks back to the clerk, with a grin on his face.]
EF: "Whatcha want pal?" C: "Why the hell did you dump a bucket of quarters on my counter?" EF: "Just paying you. That a crime?" C: "You can't (bleep)ing do that! Pick 'em up!" EF: "Hey, kid. Do you know who I am?" C: "Should I care?" [Eric points to the CSW posters on the wall.] EF: "I work for Continental Superstars Wrestling." C: "Sure ya do you crazy motha(bleep)er. And I'm the pope." EF: "Are you calling _me_ a liar?" C: "Yeah, I am." EF: "Kid, do you know who your calling a liar? The names 'Extravagant' Eric
Forbes." C: "Yeah, and that's supposed to mean what?" EF: "See this broken nose [pointing to his nose]. Got that from Steven Stellar. See this [clenching a fist], I used that to defeat 'Your True Life Fantasy' Carl Blair." C: "See this [raising his middle finger]. Sure ya did." EF: "Kid, your starting to piss me off." C: "Your pissing me off you crazy mother(bleep)er, you dropped a buckets worth of coins on my counter!"
EF: "I was paying you! Geez... Reminds me of that 'JB' guy." C: "From CSW, John Burden? That guy's the best. Gotta love him." EF: "[mocking] Gotta love him! I ought drop another bucket of quarters on your head!" C: "First you've got to pick up the ones you dropped on my counter!" EF: "I aint picking up no quarters for a Burden loving freakazoid!" C: "What the hell did you just call me?" EF: "Burden loving freakazoid! Whatcha gonna do about it?" C: "I'll (bleep)ing break your other nose!" EF: "Kid, what the hell? I don't have another nose!" C: "YOU KNOW WHAT I MEAN!" [The kid begins swinging lefts and rights, all missing Eric by a few feet. Eric doesn't even need to dodge any of them.]
EF: "That's it. I'm outter here. Crazy kids... Our youth's going down the drain." [Eric turns and walks out of the Caseys' gas station, as the clerk yells from a distance "GET BACK HERE YOU MOTHER(BLEEP)ER! PICK UP THESE QUARTERS!" All the while the camera is focused on Eric, who is laughing hysterically.
Fade out.] - Malacian - Handles: "Extravagant" Eric Forbes [CSW]
