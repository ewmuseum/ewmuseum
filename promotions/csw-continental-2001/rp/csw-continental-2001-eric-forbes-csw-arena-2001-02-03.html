"Extravagant" Eric Forbes, 3 February 2001

3 February 2001 (critiqued the same evening)
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Part 1

Well folks, this is my second posting of "Extravagant" Eric Forbes. This is the oncard to be used at the CSW event, in reference to my previous posting. It includes special guest star "The Bandit" Jason Blake. Also, all comments appreciated. -------------------- [Fade into the private parking lot entrance of the CSW Arena. "Extravagant" Eric Forbes can be seen walking towards a security guard, probably keeping out unwanted guests. Eric is clad in his wresting gear, a white T-shirt, a pair of black spandex wrestling shorts, and an amateur wrestling kneepad hanging lazily over his left shoe. Of course he has his Teenage Ninja Turtles Backpack strapped around his shoulder.] EF: "Hey, how you doin' today? Fine day, huh?" [Eric tries to walk by the guard but the security guard steps in his way, pushing him backwards.] "Bub, this is the entry to the CSW Superstar locker rooms." EF: "I know! The name's "Extravagant" Eric Forbes, perhaps you haven't heard of me. Which is a bit unbelievable seeing as how I'm _the_ CSW Superstar, but that's allright. I won't pester you, I'll just be on my way." "No, you won't. Like I said, this area is for CSW Superstar's only." EF: "And like I said, I'm _the_ CSW Superstar. Geez, you security folk are all the same. First at the Envydia Hotel, and now here. What's next? Gosh!" "You know we get a lot of lunatics posing as wrestlers trying to get in here. But you're the first to ever actually dress up as one." EF: "Poser, lunatic! What are you talking about? I'm "Extravagant" Eric Forbes. There's no way that I'm a poser! Dammit, you should be ashamed of yourself. Talking back to me, not letting me in, pretending you don't know me. It's despicable. I tell you, I'll have your job if you don't let me through." [The security guard pushes Eric again, and he stumbles backwards.] "Bub, are you threatening me?" EF: "I'm not threatening anyone! I just want to get to my locker room. You're making me late!" "You're not getting in here without a pass." [Eric falls to his knees and begins shuffling through his Teenage Ninja Turtles Backpack. After a few moments the camera pans up to the Security Guard, looking somewhat upset.] EF: "I uhh... can't seem to find it." "Like I said, you're not getting in here without a pass." EF: "LISTEN HERE! I need to get in there and I'm tired of playing marry-go-round with you incompetent security guards! Now let me in!" [The security guard grabs Eric by his shirt just as "The Bandit" Jason Blake walks by.] JB: "Hey, hey! Whatcha doin Bob, that's "Extravagant" Eric Forbes. He's with me." [The security guard, obviously Bob, lets go of Eric. Eric straightens his shirt and walks by the guard with "The Bandit" Jason Blake. Walking we hear his words as he yells them.] EF: "SO THERE! NAH-NA-NAN-NAH! FREAKAZOID!" [Fade to black.] - Malacian - NAWL Nazi-Fedhead - NEO Staffer - Handles: Eric Forbes [CSW, AWP]
