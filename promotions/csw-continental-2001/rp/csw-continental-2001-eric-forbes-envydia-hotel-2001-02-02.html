"Extravagant" Eric Forbes, 2 February 2001

2 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Keep in mind that this is a character development type flash. It's an introductory thingy, so it doesn't discuss any upcoming matches or anything like that...
------------ [Fade into the Envydia Hotel, in New York City, New York. The lobby is full of exquisite furniture, paintings, and other assortments. "Extravagant" Eric
Forbes is standing next to the front desk, holding a neon green sleeping bag and a ninja-tutrles backpack, which is hanging lazily over his shoulder. He is wearing a pair of khaki pants, and a dark blue dress shirt. He has brown hair highlighted blonde, a slender body, and nice facial features. Suffice it to say that he is an attractive man, who is somewhat well built.]
"Welcome to the Envydia Hotel, Sir." EF: "Ahh... great place, five star. Definitely a place for Mr. Extravagant. At least I hope it's suitable.. after all, I am Mr. Extravagant."
"Sir, could I get your first name?" EF: "I just told you, Extravagant." "Are you sure?" EF: "Whatcha mean, it's my name. Of course I'm sure." "OK, your last name?" EF: "Forbes." "Well Mr. Extravagant Forbes, we've entered you into the computer and..." EF: "It's 'Extravagant' Eric Forbes. You forgot to ask me my middle name... Geez, what kind of establishment are you running here?" "Sorry Sir, but like I was saying, You're in our database now, and as soon as we get some openings we'll contact you."
EF: "Wait just a second. Database? How come I'm not getting a room now. You do know who I am, right?"
"'Extravagant' Eric Forbes." EF: "Yeah, the one the only. And by the way, that's Mr. Extravagant Eric Forbes to you." "Well Mr. Extravagant Eric Forbes, we don't have any openings. Like I said, you'll recieve a phone call when the Envydia Hotel is able to find a spot for you, until then..."
EF: "That's just unnaceptable. Gosh... this is just terrible. Almost unbelievable. I mean, I come here - expecting to recieve first star service, and what do I get? Someone messing up my name, and then telling me that they don't have room for me? Me?"
"Sir..." EF: "No... no, no. Not acceptable. I want to talk to the manager. Get him down here now." [The Clerk at the front desk picks up the telephone, obviously calling the manager, as Eric looks on, disgusted, and the clerk likewise.
After a few moments a heavier set man, wearing a red jacket, and a goatee walks over to the front desk, most likely the manager of the hotel. He has a snub face as he looks Eric over, and then begins speaking.]
"Err.. nice traveling gear." EF: "If only I could put it down.. you know, if I had a room." "Well I don't know if we can accompany you here this week. We are quite booked and..." EF: "No, no! Unnaceptable. I'm Eric Forbes, 'Extravagant' Eric Forbes. The newest addition to CSW, a professional wrestler. Adored by millions of children, looked on by people every day, expected to sign autographs. Dammit,
I'm famous. And you don't know who I am?"
"Sorry Sir, but we honestly do not know who you are, and if you don't have a reservation then we cannot..."
EF: "No! You must be in some sort of denial, I'm 'Extravagant' Eric Forbes. A household name. How could you not know who I am?"
"Sir, you are the one who is in denial. No one knows who you are, and like we've been saying, there are no open rooms."
[Eric looks around, and then runs towards a child sitting in one of the chairs next to the fireplace in the lobby. He grabs him by the arm and drags him over to the front desk.]
EF: "Kid, ya know who 'Extravagant' Eric Forbes is, right?" "Mister, I aints gots a clue who yous are." EF: "Rubbish. I'm your idol... Say kid, you want an autograph?" [Before the child has a chance to answer a woman, presumably his mother, comes up and grabs the child and takes him away, not forgetting to give Mr. Forbes a devlishly upset look.]
EF: "Ahh.. well, you saw it for your own eyes. The kid wanted an autograph, but his mother had to... Well, you saw it. He couldn't keep his composure."
"Very well, Sir." "Security!" [Eric looks around as two security gaurds, who were standing at the entrance, turn and head towards him. He starts whimpering as the camera fades to black, and we are left to wonder whether or not 'Extravagant' Eric Forbes we'll be put up by the po-po.]
