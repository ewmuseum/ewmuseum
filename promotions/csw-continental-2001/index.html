Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Continental Superstar Wrestling's own FAQ, posted 17 May 2001, opens with its founder's career resume — three earlier promotions before this one — and a table of contents running from “About the CSW” through its people, its titles, its weekly schedule and its pay-per-views. Four championships are named outright, a Heavyweight Title, a Heritage Title, a Young Lions Crown and a set of Tag Team Titles, defended on a Saturday roleplay-packet clock against a calendar of four annual shows: One Night Only in February, Shattered Dreams in May, Mind Games in August, and Cruise Control in November.

Its own roster shows up earliest and most vividly not in the FAQ but in one wrestler's own words. “Extravagant” Eric Forbes introduced himself across three character-development roleplays posted to the newsgroup for critique between February and March 2001 — turned away by a hotel clerk who has never heard of him, turned away again at the CSW Arena's own locker-room door until a fellow wrestler, “The Bandit” Jason Blake, waves him through, and finally paying for gas with a bucket of quarters in front of a convenience-store clerk who has heard of the promotion's own top interviewer but not of him. Between the three, Forbes names his own home arena, a house show broken nose dealt out by a rival, and a defeated opponent, filling in more of the promotion's own texture than its administrative FAQ ever does.

Nothing in the held record says whether Continental Superstar Wrestling ever staged a card of its own, crowned a first champion, or outlasted the year its FAQ was posted in.
