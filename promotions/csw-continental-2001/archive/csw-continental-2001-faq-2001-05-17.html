The people who make the CSW! -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- (CSW Co-Owner [name withheld]) Not much is know about this young man, other than the fact that he recently help to keep the CSW alive by purchasing a majority of it
Joey Johnson. He has made it known that he will not be a center piece of television as he feels that the federation is about the wrestlers not the owners.
(CSW Co-Owner Joey "IQ" Johnson) Joey has been involved with wrestling for over 10 years. He started as a Announcer for small indy's around. Then given his shot in AWMC he became a well known announcer. He invested his money wisely and hit it big. He decided to take his wrestling knowledge to the next level and open his own federation. Recent actions have forced Johnson to sell the majority of ownership to [name withheld]. Johnson is now the commissioner as [name withheld] does not want to be a center piece of television.
(Face Announcer John Stargel) Long time friend of Joey. They went round and round as the face/heel duo in AWMC. However Joey knew it took two to make the team work. He hired John on the spot.
(Heel Announcer "Hot stuff" Stevie Porter) An ex wrestler around the world. However Stevie suffered a serious injury keeping him out of the ring. Stevie was hired on to give his two cents in the matches. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- III.
