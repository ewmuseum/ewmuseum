ILWA — Card

"ILWA Fight for Survival"

19 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Put Five of the worlds most determined wrestlers in a reinforced glass cage with an array of blunt objects and what do you get - it's the first annual ILWA FIGHT FOR SURVIVAL!
[Jaques Beound]: Here we are at the first ILWA FIGHT FOR SURVIVAL and
Louis, I can hardly wait for the main event - a five man brawl, with no rules, no corners - NO ESCAPE!!!!
{CROWD GOES NUTS!} [LOUIS LEE]: That's right Jaques, it's roing to be a riot down there tonight - but lets not forget our other matchup. The new IWLA champion
The Southern man taking on the very popular and somewhat unpredictable
Chi Tung.
[Beound]: Ack, the Southern Man won't even break into a sweat. Tung hasn't beaten a ranked opponent yet - How'd he get a title shot.
[Lee]: Let's go and refresh our memories - let's take a look at what happened way back in the inaugral tounament of the IWLA.
{SCENE CHANGES TO THE INAUGRAL TOURNAMENT - the end of the battle royal for the belt. Three men remain in the ring, the tall Southern man is beating on Chi Tung while Lance 'the blade' Wright Lies in the corner in agony.}
[Lee]: We see here that the event is all but over when The Southern Man hurls Chi Tung from the ring, but Chi Tung climbs back onto the apron and WHAMMY.
[Beound]: That was the dirtiest cheapshot I've seen in all my days wrestling - Tung was illiminated he should have left the ring area.
Instead that loser Lance Wright won the belt. {Scene changes back to the commentators} [Lee]: That's right Jacques, but that wasn't the end of it. the southern man challenged both wrestlers to a match and just last week he defeated Blade to become the new ILWA champion.
[Beound]: And tonight he's going to finish the job by finishing that little chinese.... [Lee]: Let's go to Mike with the Mic. ########### [Mike]: Ladies and gentlemen, The following contest is for the ICWLA
Legend of Legends belt!
{Crowd explode as Chi Tung appears in the aisle} [Mike]:Introducing first the challenger, coming down the aisle to ringside, from the far east - Chi Tung.
[Mike]:And his opponent - undefeated in the ILWA and Legend of Legends -
The Southern Man.
The Match started at quite a pace with Chi Tung using his knowledge of martial arts and his superior speed to bombard the Southern Man. But for every attack Tung hit the Champ blocked one and as tung began to tire the Southern man went on the offensive. he hacked Tung to the ground with a series of clotheslines and then landed a splash off the top rope. Tung kicked out at two and then slipped out the bottom rope of the ring. Not wanting Tung to catch his breath the Southern Man chased him outside where he was knocked to the ground with a dropkick.
Back in the ring Tung made the most of his advantage and hacked away on the champion. We join the match at the 10 minute stage.
[Beound]: I can't believe this. That little wimp just won't stand still long enough for The southern man to cut him down.
[Lee]: I think that was the plan - Tung is going up the rope. Ohhhh -
The big elbow drop. We've got a new champion. One...Two...No way!
[Beound]: Ha, the Southern man kicks out as the challenger heads up to the top rope once again. I tell you Louis - The Southern Man is invincable - that's Tung's best attack and The Southern Man wasn't phased.
[Lee]: Not phased he's lying unconscious on the mat and here comes the big elbow drop again...NO!
[Beound]: OH...I love it! The Champ was playing possum and he's nailed
Chi Tung as he flew off the top rope. Here we go - Southern Slam - And that's got to be the match. One Two Three. Haha.
[Lee]: I can't believe the stamina of the Southern man. Chi Tung went for the high risk....
[Beound]: Shut up Louis, this is the bit I love. {The Southern man cracks open a can of Beer and pours it into Tung's mouth, slapping him when he doesn't swallow it...}
[Beound]: Haha...drink up! ######### {Scene changes to the locker room where PAIN is ready for the Fight for survival} [PAIN] - You people gonna feel PAIN, you guys are stupid enough to sign up in my match. This is a brawl, this is how I grew up - how I learned
to fight. So Come on down to ringside and feel my WRATH. NAUUHHHH! {The camera changes back to ringside where 10 foot reinforced glass walls have been placed around the ring. A glass door is the only way in and a garbage sute is the only way out.}
[Lee]: Well we already saw more than we bargained for in the Title match up, but now it's time for the main event. Slaughter, Pain, Knife,
Blade and the Right Reverend Johnny Maguiness all set to do battle.
[Beound]: And it's obvious who your choosing right from the start, but let me tell you about the number PAIN is going to do on that no good priest.
[Lee]:PAIN called this match, he wanted the best in the ILWA and he got them - it's going to be interesting. [Beound]: Look at what they got in there, they got a baseball bat, they got a dustbin lid, they got a set of fighting sticks, there's a broom in there and a.....what is that - is that a set of Brass Knuckles?
[Lee]: Definitely a very dangerous match up - Someone could end their career in there. Mind you, I think that was Pain's plan. Let's go to the Mic.
[MIKE]: This is a no holds barred match up. Competitors can be illiminated by being thrown down the refuse shute. The winner of the match up recieves a ten thousand dollar prize put forward by The doctor of Pain.
[MIKE]: Your contestants for this event. Ladies and Gentlemen, please welcome The Doctor of Pain himself!
[Pain[grabbing the Mike]]: This shouldn't take long. [MIKE]: And Hugh 'The Knife' Hogan. {The crowd cheers wildly as a well toned man slides into the ring and faces off with PAIN} [MIKE]: And now the former ILWA champion - Lance 'The Blade' Wright
{The master technician enters the ring, to the delight of the fans}
[MIKE]: Now, from the battlefields of the Middle-East - SLAUGHTER
[Slaughter growls at the fans as he enters the ring to loud Boo's} [MIKE]: And the final contestant... {From the PA the sound of church bells begin to ring and the crowd screams in delight} [MIKE]: ...hailing from the Vatican, Rome. {Mike raises his voice even louder to be heard over the roar of the crowd} [MIKE]: The Popes personal Envoy to the ILWA - The Right Revenerend
Johnny Maguiness! Pain nailed the reverend with a baseball bat as soon as he entered the ring leaving him lying clutching his back. Hugh Hogan came to his rescue and kicked the bat out the door of the ring. The door was shut and the match was under way. Slaughter went to work on Wright with a set of brass knuckles while Knife held of pain with the broom. Using the dustbin lid as a frisbee Pain nailed Knife in the head and used the broom to push him down the shute. The reverend got to his feet and was immeadiately nailed with the bin lid by Pain. Pain then tried shove the
Reverend out the shute but Maguiness resisted and nailed him with a short right hand. Meanwhile the former champion Blade had knocked the
Brass Knuckles out of Slaughters hands and had applied a sleeper hold.
Maguiness whipped Pain into the ropes but pain ran into Wright and slaughter breaking the hold. Pain picked up Wright and Headbutted him towards the Shute then nailed him over the back with the broom. The broom snapped in two and Pain easily dispensed the former champion down the shute.
The reverend was meanwhile applying a claw grip to Slaughters forehead - applying some holy retribution. The crowd screamed a warning as Pain approached from behind with a dustbin lid. At the last Possible moment the reverend turned around and hooked his other hand onto the forehead of Pain. Applying the Holy retribution with both hands. The crowd were screaming wildly at this stage as both Pain and Slaughter screamed in agony. Breaking the hold the reverend dragged Slaughter to the shute and eliminated him. We pick up the match with two contestants remaining.
[Lee]: The reverend eliminates Slaughter and now we have what we wanted. One on one the Revenend vs Pain! [Beound]: Haha - and the Reverend feeling pain as he gets nailed into the shute from behind. He's going to be eliminated - no - haha - Pain wants him to suffer.
[Lee]: Maguiness can hardly stand, someone should stop this - OH! A snap suplex from PAIN. Oh no! Someone should stop this!
[Beound]: No way - Maguiness signed for the match - he knew what was coming. Incidently how many times was that that Pain rammed his head into that bin lid?
[Lee]: This is rediculous, Pain is applying is setting the reverend up for a piledriver and he....knocks his brains out. The crowd hate this stop it!
[Beound]: Oh but they'd love it if the roles were reversed - and talking of reversed look at that inverted atomic drop. This is great.
[Lee]: Suplex, and finally Pain throws the reverend out the shute to the hard arena floor below...
[Beound]: Like the trash he is Louis, like the trash he is. This was great - we have to hold these fights more often. Pain keeps his money and the crowd goes home happy.
[Lee]: Happy, their hero has just been mutilated. {The lights dim as groans of agony begin to play over the PA, and the crowd Boos loudly} ILWA - A fed thats going places.
