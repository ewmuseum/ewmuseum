ILWA — Card

"ILWA Rumble"

30 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The first part of this was posted without a subject heading. Sorry for any problems. [Beound]: I have no doubt that this guy is the single most insane wrestler in the ILWA. People just don't get up from a Southern Man choke slam because - there! He'll do it to you again!
[Lee]: Southern Man certainly seems to have taken the wind out of
Blitzkreig's sails with those two choke slams. Travekker has knife in a gorilla press. He hurls Knife out of the ring. OUCH!! Knife landed on the guard rail and there's blood all over the place.
[Beound]: Heartbreaker has Travekker in the other corner. He's slapped on a sleeper and dragged Travekker towards the middle of the ring.
Hitman is helping Southern Man deal to Blitzkreig. The tag combination combining to do some serious damage to the rising star. You know - it's only a matter of time before the Hard Men become the world Tag champions.
[Lee]: Certainly a combination to be feared as Blitzkreig is finding out to his cost now. Meanwhile we've got Black Flagg showing enormous strength. He picked up Mumma Jumbo and slammed him. That is impressive!
[Beound]: And now he's moved him to the ropes and he's pushing him over. This would be a huge upset is Mumma Jumbo coming in at number 24 got eliminated now!
[Lee]: Blitzkreig in a similar situation. Coming in at 25 you would expect an athelete of him caliber to win this - but Hitman and Southern
Man, The Hard Men, have other ideas. Blitzkreig is caught in a double abdominal stretch. Meanwhile MUMMA JUMBO HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!!! BLACK
FLAGG SHOWING INCREDIBLE STRENGTH THREW HIM OUT BY HIMSELF!!!
[Beound]: That is a great show of strength by Flagg. No-one at the
Rumble for the Internet title could manage that!! Jumbo is gone and on the other side of the ring Blitzkreig is gone as well.
[Lee]: So then, the Heartbreaker the senior man in the ring. He's been there a long time now and it's showing against Travekker. Southern Man and Black Flagg are punching it out in center ring. Hitman just biding his time now. Black Flagg whipped to the ropes by Southern Man, shoulder block, but the 300 pound plus Flagg didn't go down. Hitman lifts Black Flagg up one rope and calls for the aid of Southern Man.
But Southern Man has gone after Travekker. Travekker caught in a back suplex. He looks hurt.
[Beound]: Black Flagg is fighting back well against the Hitman. It started with an eye rake, but now Black Flagg is landing a series of chops about the chest area of Hitman. Hitman fighting back. Let me tell you Louis, there are some tired bodies out there!!
[Lee]: Hitman whips Black Flagg to the rope. Heartbreaker is in the way. He ducks...BACK BODY DROP AND BLACK FLAGG IS GONE TOO!!!! Look at this though........
[Beound]: Uh-Oh! Heartbreaker just realised he helped the Hitman eliminate Black Flagg. Hitman charges in. Heartbreaker going for a back body drop. DDT by the Hitman. Heartbreaker been in there a long time now, he's starting to feel the heat, he made a bad decision there to drop his head on the Hitman.
[Lee]: Travekker and the Southern Man still punching it out on the far side of the ring. Hitman has Heartbreaker up and he turns him around and slaps on the sleeper hold. Travekker drops Southern Man. I think that's it for the Southern Man.
[Beound]: No he'll be back up again you watch. TRAVEKKER IS GOING TO
THE TOP!!! BIG ELBOW INTO THE BACK OF HITMAN'S HEAD!!!!! Hitman went down like a ton of bricks. He's groggy!!!
[Lee]: And the Southern Man is back up. He's circling Travekker. Heartbreaker and Hitman both out of it on the ground. Southern man and
Travekker lock up. Southern man with an irish whip to the rope. Big boot in the face of Travekker. Travekker comes back with a swinging forearm but Southern Man ducks low and scoops him over his shoulder. He manhandles him around and executes a standing Southern Slam!!!!!
[Beound]: That has to be it for Travekker. Southern Man picks him up and throws him over the top. Travekker is eliminated. Hey! What's going on here though?
[Lee]: Hitman has got back up and he's glaring at the Southern Man. Southern Man confused. Hitman shakes his head to clear it. He looks around him and sees the groggy Heartbreaker and the fully conscious
Southern Man.
[Beound]: I don't know what's going on here, maybe Hitman thinks it was
Southern Man who hit him. He's certainly mad.
[Lee]: The Southern man is trying to explain, but the hitman is yelling at him from point blank range. Hitman thinks that the Southern Man hit him, Southern Man not being given the chance to deny it. OH!! He shoved him! The Hitman shoved the Southern Man! SOUTHERN MAN SHOVED
HIM BACK!!!! [Beound]: This is bad!!! This is very bad!!! hitman in no condition to judge what happened, but Southern Man DON'T LIKE BEING PUSHED AROUND
BY ANYONE!!! [Lee]: They're standing chest to chest now, both men hurling abuse at the other, niether of them can hear a thing, the crowd is roaring out loud. Here comes the Godfather to ringside!!
[Beound]: Godfather will sort this out. Hitman shoving the Southern
Man once again and going to the Godfather. Southern Man follows him.
The Godfather trying to explain to Hitman but I doubt anyone can hear anything over the DIN of this crowd.
[Lee]: Oh and the Heartbreaker is back on his feet. The crowd goes crazy. Heartbreaker running at Hitman and Southern Man. They turn just
in time NO!!!!! Heartbreaker with a double clothesline!!! HE ELIMINATED BOTH OF THEM!!!! HEARTBREAKER IS THE CHAMPION!!!!!! SOUTHERN MAN AND HITMAN ELIMINATED WHILE THEY WERE ARGUING!!!!! [Beound]: Hitman is back in the ring - he's hammering on Heartbreaker. Southern Man looks around him in amazement, he can't believe what's going on. Godfather trying to carm him down, but Southern Man pushes him away and storms back down the isle.........
[Lee]: Meanwhile in the ring Hitman and Heartbreaker are going at it again. They're fighting like they just stepped out there. The
Heartbreaker is the winner, he gets the title shot against Rebellion, but Hitman seems to want to make sure that he doesn't get a chance. Get some officials in there!!!
{Some time later the ring is finally cleared, Heartbreaker stands beside Larry the Lungs his arms raised in triumph. The Crowd is screaming their hearts out}
[Larry]: LADIEEEESSSS AND GENTTLLLEEEMMEEENNNNN!!!! THE WINNER OF THIS RUMBLE - JOHN "THE HEEAAAAARRRTTTTBREEAKKEEERRR" RAAANDALLLLSSSSSSSSSS!!!!!! [Lee]: Well that's all for us. Next week the champion Randy Rebellion will do war against Heartbreaker for the Legend of Legends belt. Also on the line is the Tag belts with TLC defending against The Hard Men - and that should be interesting after tonight!!!!
[Beound]: I can hardy wait for comments from the Hitman and Southern
Man. But until next time - god bless from the ILWA crew!
