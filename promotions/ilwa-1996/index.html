An April-July 1996 Usenet e-fed run from the University of Otago whose dated show timeline -- a survival-style cage brawl, a title tournament, a battle royal, and a three-part pay-per-view -- survives complete with a three-belt title lineage. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Internet Legends of Wrestling Association ran out of the University of Otago in Dunedin, New Zealand, its operator self-disclosed only as “Rob.” Its earliest surviving show, Fight for Survival, billed itself on 19 April 1996 as “the first annual” entry in a format putting “five of the world's most determined wrestlers in a reinforced glass cage with an array of blunt objects” — the same card carrying the champion, The Southern Man, defending his title against Chi Tung by playing possum through a top-rope elbow drop before finishing him with a Southern Slam.

Trial of Champions, eleven days later, opened a new Internet Championship with a ten-man battle royal that debuted the newcomer Taz, ran a further Southern Man title defense against the South African Travekker, and staged “a match of redemption” between the Reverend and Slaughter.

The Rumble, on 30 May, ran that same Internet title through a battle-royal field deep enough to include the Hard Men — Hitman and Southern Man, already spoken of as future tag champions — whose own alliance cracked live in the ring when a disoriented Hitman wrongly blamed Southern Man for a blindside attack and shoved him, drawing an authority figure, the Godfather, out to intervene.

Its showpiece, Spring Thaw, ran 30 June and stacked returns onto title matches: a retired broadcaster, Jacques Beound, came back after four years to wrestle Wolverine; a four-way steel cage match ran alongside grudge bouts pairing Robski against Aussie Evans and Johnny “the Reverend” Maguiness against “Painbringer” Billy Sexton; and the card built to the Hard Men defending the tag titles against the Original Jersey Boys and John “the Heartbreaker” Randalls defending the ILWA Legend of Legends Championship against Bulldozer.

No closure notice survives. The last surviving document, 13 July 1996, is a recruitment post still actively seeking roleplayers.
