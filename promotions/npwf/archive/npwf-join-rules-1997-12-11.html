NPWF New Power Wrestling Federation 1. Introduction The NPWF is a new and hopefully exciting e-fed that will be ran using TNM 6.2 Plus. The matches I use are directly from the TNM Program. On each card I will add a short commentary about the match and other surprises that happened. The NPWF is a role-play/TNM E-federation, based on role-players using real wrestlers, from real federations. 2. Rules --You must role-play at least one time a week. Your role-play must be 5 lines or more. The role-play should cover what your wrestler wants to due, in terms of matches and belts, etc. The role-play must sound like it is coming from the wrestler in real life, that you are role-playing. --There will be one card each week, this card will take place on Saturday. Matches must be in to me and confirmed by Thursday of that week. For a match to occur both wrestlers must accept the match. --In the NPWF there will be stipulation matches, if you want this kind of match to happen. A normal match that takes place will have a time-limit of 30 minutes, (except for TV Title Matches which are 15 min. time limits), there will be DQ's, and countouts. --If you want to do a run-in during a match you must e-mail me by Thursday so I can include it on the card. Usually on Friday I run the matches through TNM. --You can have up to 4 wrestlers with a manager (if you want one). --These are the NPWF titles: -NPWF World Heavyweight Title -NPWF World Tag Team Titles -NPWF World Television Title (15 min. time limit for all TV Title matches) 3. Wrestlers That Are Already Taken ---Jerry Lawler, Jeff Jarrett, Jesse James, RockABilly (The Southern Posse) ---Chris Benoit, Dean Malenko, Hunter Hearst Helmsley, Shane Douglas ---Arn Anderson, The Steiner Brothers, Bret Hart (Manager: Paul Ellering) ---Stan Hansen, Steve Austin, Terry Gordy Barry Windham (Manager: Terry Funk) (The Wild Bunch) ---The Undertaker, Vader, Legion of Doom (Manager: Paul Bearer)
