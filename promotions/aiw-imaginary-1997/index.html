Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

American Imaginary Wrestling was pitched on 22 March 1997 by a handler who introduced himself as the former president of an already-folded fed, WAeW, and the handler of a character named J.P. Steele: “the American Imaginary Wrestling idea was created,” modeled, in his own words, on “e-wrestling's pioneer, play by mail wrestling.”

Its pitch set out a league built to run itself: matches decided by point-value stats rather than roleplay, a short “Trash Talk” line required only every ten days, a weekly AIW Bodyslam Newsletter carrying champions, rankings, and summarized reports for up to ten matches across seven cards, and four titles — the Americas, Pacific Coast, Midwest, and Americas Tag championships — contested weekly and settled every ten weeks at a cycle supercard.

No further sighting of the promotion survives anywhere in the record. Whether it ever ran a card, crowned a champion, or held its first supercard isn't in the surviving record.
