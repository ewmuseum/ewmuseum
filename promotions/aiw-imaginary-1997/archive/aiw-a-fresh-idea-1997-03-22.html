[READ] A Fresh Idea in e-wrestling

22 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Looking for a relaxing, easy league which won't take hours every day? Looking for a fun way to test a character and just relax? Then the newest idea in e-wrestling may be the answer! Hey, there, I'm [name withheld]. Former WAeW prez before it folded, and handler of J.P. Steele. I hope you've heard of me. :) I was thinking, gosh, I wish I could run a fed still, but I don't want to take up time. It's impossible to find writers, so what can I do? Then I remembered e-wrestling's pioneer, play by mail wrestling, such as the IWA. What if, I wondered, I did a spin off of THAT with play by e-mail! And thus, the American Imaginary Wrestling idea was created. This fed will be one that should be fun, easy, and not take up much time. Now, how will it work? Wins will be decided based on point value. Don't worry trying to figure it out too much, just try to catch on during the creation sheet. Basically, it's the better stats that wins. No, not the bigger wrestlers or the ones made to be superhumans, but the better overall stats. Each handler is allowed up to five wrestlers. The only roleplaying would be not very nessacary every ten days or so with a short little five-twenty five line thing for Trash Talk. This is just to make sure you're alive and still paying attention to the league. Same with the challenge sheet sent each week to see who you want to face. Alright, so how does this work more? Well, each week, the AIW Bodyslam Newsletter will be sent to the league. Champs, rankings, Trash Talk, and the card reports from each DAY will be posted. Now, the cards will be in summary form, mind you, and I mean _summary_. As in "Blah defeated Blah with the thingie at 7:05 . . . " type. However, the cards will have anywhere up to ten matches, and seven a week each updated in the report. The titles? The Americas title, the Pacific Coast title, the Midwest title, and the Americas tag title. These are up for grab once each week, and then they _can_ chantge at the cycle supercard. This supercard will feature about three matches live and the rest in the regular summary. Cycles last ten weeks. Alright, so you want in? Fill out the form and send it to [detail withheld] .
