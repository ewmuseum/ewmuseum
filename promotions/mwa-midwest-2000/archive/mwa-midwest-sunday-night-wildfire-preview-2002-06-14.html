MWA: Sunday Night Wildfire Preview, 16 June 2002

14 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{MWA} The Midwest Wrestling Alliance www-personal.umich.edu/~mmeola/mwa.html {MWA} Sunday Night Wildfire LIVE! From Assembly Hall in Champagne, IL On The Show..... *** When Mike Stryker reached the box hanging above the ring at Bad Blood during his deathmatch an explosion rocked Ron Wilkins Auditorium in St. Paul. Stryker was knocked back to the mat where he was pinned by Ruiner and lost the MWA Toughman Championship. Well, this Sunday Mike Stryker begins his quest to become the 2002 Struggle to Survive Champion. In addition to his match with Brian Holland, Stryker has promised to confront the person (or persons) that he feels is responsible for the henious exploding box. *** June 16th marks the one hundred fortieth day of Bobby Collins reign as Midwest Wrestling Champion. That achievement ties the record set by Mike Stryker in early 2001. Without a match on Sunday, Collins will no doubt break the record. How will "The Goods" and everyone's favorite personal assisstant celebrate their achievement? Will a new contender to Bobby Collins championship belt step up to spoil the fun? Find out Sunday. *** Bad Blood has come and gone. Midwest Wrestling has been rocked. One of the more developing situations comes from a press conference 10 days ago. Kenny Hill announced that he will compete in the Struggle to Survive Tournament. That leaves just Alexa Soriano and Mr. Varona as the Council of Authority. What happens when these two don't see eye to eye? Who will break the tie vote? ***Due to limited programming time, the eight Struggle to Survive Qualifying Matches that are scheduled to be held in Champagne can't all be aired on Sunday Night Wildfire. However, we will still get all four qualifiers from the Tri-State and International Divisions. While we don't know exactly what we will see on Wildfire, it will be at least four of the following eight matches...... Tri-State (1) Ruiner vs. (8) Officer Mendoza (2) Mike Stryker vs. (7) Brian Holland (3) Big Bad Bill vs. (6) Brian Bradley (4) Shane Andrews vs. (5) Chris Holland International (1) Lo Mein King vs. (8) Nightbreed JW (2) Pic Axe Jack vs. (7) Nightbreed IR (3) Christian Demonstone vs. (6) The Masked Menace (4) Rally Jackson vs. (5) Nightbreed EX *** What happens to the four matches we don't see? Tune into the MWA Newsline to find out. An announcement should be made later this week. *** All that, two goofs named Maddix and Howe, and MORE!!!!! *** Note: The four best roleplayed for matches will be the ones I write for Wildfire. If possible that number may extend to five or six, so long as it doesn't delay the date the card comes out. Stay tuned to the newsline for more on this subject matter.
