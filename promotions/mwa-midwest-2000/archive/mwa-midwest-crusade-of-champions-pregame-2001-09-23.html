Crusade of Champions 2001 (Pre-Game)

23 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Back in our regular Sunday Night Wildfire slot, we bring to you a shortened version of Wildfire. A "Pre-Game" Show if you will to send us into the
Pay-Per-View, and get those all important final buys from the fans.] [Our pre-game starts with the usual fade in from "MLB Today" to clips of last week's edition of Sunday Night Wildfire.]
Jack Maddix: Here we are. One on one. Mano a mano. Shaun McWhirter and Alan
Soriano. These two men will meet in one week's time as part of a six man tag match. A six man tag match that saw two other singles bouts here tonight.
Ben Howe: And those went one to each side. This is the momentum swinger for next Sunday. The momentum swinger for the Crusade of Champions!!!!
[The two men begin to circle and the fans rythmic clapping begins.] Jack Maddix: Here we go. They lock up and the struggle for power begins. Both men fighting back and forth looking for control.
Ben Howe: The classic collar and elbow tie-up. An amature wrestling move, that is often used to start professional matches.
Jack Maddix: Both men vying for control. Soriano backs McWhirter up a few steps. Alan Soriano the first to get control.
Ben Howe: That's because of how great he is. Jack Maddix: But wait. The equally great Shaun McWhirter now backing Soriano up. Sorinao backed up. To the center of the ring. Now further.
Ben Howe: Soriano has lost control. Jack Maddix: Knee to the gut by Soriano. He catches McWhirter off guard. Soriano with a big right hand blocked. McWhirter answers with a right. Ben Howe: And a solid right. He put Soriano on the mat. Jack Maddix: The roar of the fans is tremendous. [Soriano crawls up to his knees and then begins to beg.] Jack Maddix: Soriano is begging McWhirter off. Soriano is begging McWhirter not to hurt him. This is the most pathetic thing I've ever seen.
Ben Howe: I think I'm ashamed to be an Alan Soriano plan. The fans were right,
Jack. He really is a pussy.
Jack Maddix: McWhirter looking down at Soriano and smiling. McWhirter knows he has broken down the once proud Alan Soriano into a coward....OH WAIT!!!!
[CRACK!!!!!!] Jack Maddix: Michael Wall!!!! He just came out of nowhere and nailed Shaun
McWhirter with a chair.
[DING!!! DING!!! DING!!!] Jack Maddix: Wall, who I completely forgot was absent during the surrounding of the ring by all the players in this battle, rolled out from under the ring and sneak attacked Shaun McWhirter.
Ben Howe: Soriano, the mastermind, is now driving the chair down into the ribs of Shaun McWhirter. They are taking out McWhirter.
[The camera cuts to the back locker room where we see a brawl has broken out between all the men in Damage Inc. and all the men in The Resistance Force.]
Jack Maddix: Soriano repeatedly bashing McWhirter. These fans throwing stuff into the ring. They have turned violent, and we may have a riot here in
Milwaukee. This isn't good...this isn't good at all. Ben Howe: No it's very good. McWhirter is being taken apart. He's being destroyed. This is the perfect ending to tonight.
Jack Maddix: But he has to fight in the six man tag next week. Ben Howe: Oh darn, Jack....someone should have told Soriano. Then he would have shown compassion....YEAH RIGHT!!!! HA HA!!!!
Jack Maddix: Soriano now standing over McWhirter victorious. This is sick. McWhirter taken out seven days before the biggest night in MWA History. Killing Box, Toughman, Meola Bowl, and No Gimmicks Needed Matches aside. The fate of MWA hangs in the balance next week........
Ben Howe: And the power and momentum lie with MICHAEL WALL!!!!! [LOGO!!!!] THE PAY-PER-VIEW PRE-GAME SHOW!!!!! Live! From Comerica Park in Detroit, Michigan ["Right Now" by Van Halen begins to play as we fade from a shot of the logo. The words on your screen are now replaced with an image of the three men competing in the MWA Championship Match. Blottie on the right with the belt slung over his shoulder, Dream on the left, and Shane Andrews in the middle.
The words "Killing Box Match" are on the bottom of the screen. It fades away and then we get clips of how this match came to be.]
[The first clip of Brian J. Blottie throwing Shane Andrews through a flamming table at Conquer and Control. Then of Dream capturing the MWA Championship from Mike Stryker later that evening. Cut to Blottie winning the MWA
Championship from Dream a month later. A clip of Andrews turning his back on
Dream by attacking him combined with Andrews' attack on Dream during the steel cage match. Cut to Andrews putting Blottie through a flamming table, and finally to Dream putting both men through seperate tables a week ago in the
Best of Five Tables Match.]
[Van Halen fades out as we cut to a shot of Peter Hoffmann and Matt Madden sitting back stage in Comerica Park. A smile across the face of both men.]
Peter Hoffmann: Welcome everyone to this very special edition of the MWA "Pay-Per-View Pre-Game Show." I am your host Peter Hoffmann sitting along side, Matt Madden, here for the duration of the pre-game....
Matt Madden: That's where your wrong Petey. I'm the host of this show. And we're gonna talk about what I want to talk about. Starting with The Killing
Box Match.
Peter Hoffmann: Well, that's what is first on my format, so I won't put up an argument there. Matt, we just saw the clips of what brought these three men together here tonight. Since those events Druschel has signed this match, and the three men have given very candid interviews about it.
Matt Madden: Well, we all know that these three men, eat, breathe, and sleep,
Midwest Wrestling. And at the top of the Midwest Wrestling mountain is The MWA
Championship. Currently Blottie holds it, but anyone who lives for Midwest
Wrestling wants that title.
Peter Hoffmann: And with that desire for the belt comes a truely interesting fate. If you want that belt you have to be able to sacrifice so much. You have to put your family on the backburner. All your time has to go into training and preparing for your matches. And when it comes time to fight you have to be willing to leave it all in the ring. You can't have any reservations. For the few that compete for the MWA Championship, there is no thinking about tomorrow. They live for the here and now. They live for their moments with the title.
Matt Madden: And Pete, I really think that all three men in our Main Event tonight, fit that description. Shane Andrews has been training this week. He has been watching video tape this week. And in his free time he comes out and cuts interviews on Blottie and Dream. His only focus is the MWA Championship.
Peter Hoffmann: So are you picking him to win? Matt Madden: Actually, I just can't. I have to say that I think it will be
Brian J. Blottie retaining the MWA Championship tonight. Like him, and if you do you are in the few, or hate him he is still the best in MWA right now.
Peter Hoffmann: I have to disagree. I believe that Dream is at the top of his game right now. He proved it against Drake Istenia. He had time off, and he's back. No ring rust at all. He also comes to play when it's time. That's why my money is on him here tonight in the Killing Box Match.
Matt Madden: You don't know anything. Peter Hoffmann: We'll see...let's go to Rob Steele in the back. [Camera cuts to Rob Steele standing in between the Tag Team Champions, the Big
City Hitmen.]
Rob Steele: Fans I'm here with the tag champs, who both have their singles endeavors to deal with tonight. First off, Mike Stryker, you have a measue of revenge against Shawn Matthews to exact.
Mike Stryker: Your god Damn Right. Shawn Matthews walked out onto that field yesterday and gave a hell of a speech. And I really am dying to rip it apart.
First off, facing you has NOTHING to do with this change in attitude. It's based on the events that shook this world. My change in attitude has nothing to do with you. but it promises to be the one thing that you won't overcome.
This concept that Shawn Matthews is better than Mike Stryker is somthing I can't argue, yet. And I'm gonna walk out there, and on my own, take out the one man who says I can't beat him.
[Bailey pulls the mic toward himself.] Brandon Bailey: Whadaya mean alone? [Stryker looks confused as Bailey continues.] Brandon Bailey: Mike, you know he's not coming alone, you know Pandora and
Powers will be out there, you need me for this. You need me to keep them in check, so you can concentrate on Showtime.
Mike Stryker: Brandon, I gotta do this one by myself. Sorry Double B, but this is bigger than us.
Brandon Bailey: You're out of your mind if you think you can do this alone. [Stryker snaps a glare toward Bailey.] Mike Stryker: Hold on...I've been doing this ALONE for the better part of a year. I won a world title on my own, I held it for five months on my own, and to be honest, I should be getting another shot at it, ON MY OWN!!! Brandon, there's only one Hitman sitting here who can get things done on their own.....only one.
[Stryker storms off the set, Brandon Bailey looks in shock toward Rob Steele.] Brandon Bailey: Guess dis is how it's gonna be. Maybe Mikey should watch jus' what I can do out 'dere. That Sonofabitch thinks he's da captain? Well, times are gonna change, they's gonna change real soon. He wants to go to slaughter alone. I say let him, punk motherf*cker.
[Bailey proceeds to storm off. We cut back to Hoffmann and Madden.] Peter Hoffmann: Tensions obviously running a little high here on this tremendous day. What do you think Matt?
Matt Madden: The tag team titles broke up Extreme Reality. They broke up The
Raging Dragons. They broke up Badz and Wolf. Now their breaking up The Big
City Hitmen. It's like the belts got a curse on them.
Peter Hoffmann: Well, speaking of title belts, one that certainly doesn't have a curse is the MWA Toughman Championship. A lot of big names from Sentinel to
Shaun McWhirter to current Champion Chris Holland. The belt has a hard time staying around one man's waist for a long time, but it sure is a catalyst for good things here in MWA.
Matt Madden: And that's why Drake Istenia needs the belt so bad. He is a man who lacks definition, but that's kind of the way he likes it. He also likes people thinking he is one thing, then proving to be another. In the Struggle to Survive Tournament he was The Upset Kid. Then he shed that to become the main event player. All of a sudden he is a toughman. The kid is a real enigma.
Peter Hoffmann: An enigma that Chris Holland has the daunting task of figuring out. Holland has become very possesive of his MWA Toughman Championship belt recently. The man has more heart then a lot of people in MWA combined.
However, with a leather strap tying the men together and no rules, one has to wonder if heart will be enough.
Matt Madden: I don't think so. In my opinion heart isn't going to be enough. Not at all. Drake Istenia is walking out today with the belt. You see that which you do not understand you cannot defeat.
Peter Hoffmann: While that appears to be true, Chris Holland has pulled off bigger surprises. He was in the finals of the MWA Struggle to Survive
Tournament. He got there on heart. While, Istenia couldn't get there on being an unknown. That's why I think heart will overcome the mystery of Istenia today. I'm going with Holland.
[Fade to a clip of Chris Holland pinning Apollo Pandora to capture the MWA
Toughman Championship. From there we show Drake Istenia overcomming two opponents to become the number one contender to the championship. Next up is a clip of the two men working together against Powers and Pandora in a tag match.
Then the turn of Istenia on Holland in an eight man tag not much later. Finally, we get clips of the face to face confrontation they had a few weeks ago. Istenia getting in Holland's face only to get put through a table. We then see the graphic of Holland and Istenia, promoting the Strap Match for the
Toughman Title on the screen.]
[The graphic switches to that picturing eight men. Black Wolf, Brandon Bailey,
Chip Moore, Eric Stone, Mike Badz, Pic Axe Jack, Remy Wincott, and Sean Powers.
The words "Meola Bowl" are written across the bottom of the screen.] Peter Hoffmann: Eight men, one ring. Only one man can win The Meola Bowl
Trophy. Matt, this one starts out as a battle royal. Then when it is narrowed down to two men it becomes a one on one match. Truely a unique style match.
Matt Madden: Well, leave it up to Matt Meola and his staff to come up with some wild stuff and they will. Just look at what Meola and Wall came up with as the format for the MWA Championship Tournament a year ago. This one should be fun as well, but the men involved are looking more for pride than fun.
Peter Hoffmann: Well, my pick in this one is going to be Remy Wincott. Bailey seems distracted. Wolf, Badz, Moore and Pic Axe haven't been very vocal at all this week, which makes me question their desire. Eric Stone quite frankly is a joke, and Sean Powers is......
Matt Madden: ....going to be your winner. Sean Powers steps up to the plate tonight, and he's gonna hit more home runs then Barry Bonds can in a single season in one night. Remember I said that after it happens.
Peter Hoffmann: Well, there is still time for you at home to call your local cable or satellite provider to order Crusade of Champions. Let's take you now to Rob Steele who is standing by with new MWA signee.....Michael Mazin!!!
[The camera cuts to Mazin and Steele.] Rob Steele: Mr. Mazin....welcome to MWA!!! Now, let me get this straight. You are the brother of NACW/ICPW announcing legend Dan Mazin?
Michael Mazin: Why is it always about my brother? That's a big joke Steele. It should be about me. About Michael Mazin. Not about Dan. After I make my impact here on MWA, nobody is gonna know who Dan is, but.......
[Suddenly Eric Stone walks into the screen and grabs the mic out of Steele's hand. He pushes Steele away and gets in Mazin's face.]
Eric Stone: You have a brother named Dan? Jigga What??? Michael Mazin: I...... Eric Stone: You think your special.......Jigga What??? Micahel Mazin: Hey man, I'm conducting an interview...... Eric Stone: You call this an interview? Look at ya....you're pathetic!!!! [With that Stone clobbers Mazin with a right hand. The two men begin to brawl and MWA officials charge into the scene and break things up as we cut back to
Peter Hoffmann and Matt Madden.]
Peter Hoffmann: Well, that is an interesting situation. Matt Madden: That it is. Speaking of interesting, Matthews and Stryker should be an interesting battle. They had a classic a little over eleven months ago to determine the first MWA Champion.
Peter Hoffmann: And tonight, they will open the card with what should no doubt be another classic. All the peices to the puzzle are there.
Matt Madden: Peter, let me ask you something....why is Matthews vs. Stryker opening the card? On one hand you have the first MWA Champion ever. On the other hand you have the greatest MWA Champion ever. So why are these top level talents left jerking the curtain?
Peter Hoffmann: That is a legitamate question. Let's go to Rob Steele standing by with Drew Druschel in hopes to get an answer.
[The camera cuts to Steele and a very worried Drew Druschel. Drew is looking around not really focusing on Steele as he begins the interview.]
Rob Steele: Drew you have slated Stryker vs. Matthews to open the card. Some people feel this match has the potential to be the main event. So why will we instead see it as the opening bout?
[Drew's attention still seems distant as he looks responds.] Drew Druschel: Well, Rob....think about the first Wrestlemania. That night started a legacy. And everyone remembers three matches from it. They remember
The Main Event....because it was the Main Event.
Rob Steele: Very true. Drew Druschel: They also remember King Kong Bundy vs. Special Delivery Jones. Because that match was so short that it set a record. When records are set or titles change hands people remember it. It kind of steals attention from the
Main Event. Rob, do you know what the third match everyone remembers is?
Rob Steele: Um...... Drew Druschel: Tino Santana vs. The Executioner. And do you know why? It's because it was the opening bout. It was the beginning of the tradition. It is the match that has gone down in history as the first Wrestlemania Match ever.
Well, Rob, I'm not going to make the mistake of putting a forgettable match. I'm putting the match with two of the biggest names in MWA history on first. [Suddenly Brandon Bailey walks into the screen. Bailey comes up to Drew and begins speaking to the president. Drew is still acting very distant.]
Brandon Bailey: Listen, Drew. Mike won't let me come to ringside with him, and
I know if you don't do something about it then Pandora and Powers will be out there, and that could ruin the match. So I want you to ban everyone in MWA other than Stryker and Matthews from being near the ring during the opening bout.
[Drew continues to look around as if he is searching for something.] Drew Druschel: Uh....sure, Brando.....sounds good to me. [Bailey walks off happy.] Rob Steele: Drew, is there a reason you are so distracted? [Jack Maddix runs into the screen.] Jack Maddix: Drew is there any truth to the rumor that Cobra has yet to arrive here in Detroit? Is the Six Man Tag Match in jeopardy???
[Drew shoots a stern look at Jack.] Drew Druschel: Well, Jack.....ya know what....."No Comment!!!" [Drew walks off and we cut back to Madden and Hoffmann.] Peter Hoffmann: Is Cobra not in the building? Matt Madden: What happens if he is not? Peter Hoffmann: It's time for us to go over to the pay-per-view. For those of you that have ordered, enjoy the show. For those that have yet to do so, please do while we show you these final video clips.
[Fade to a shot of Alan Soriano pumbling Cobra back at Terminal Destiny in
March. Then of Michael Wall being pinned by Dream at the same show. We next see Alan Soriano fall from a cage through a table signalling the exit of both
Soriano and Wall from MWA. From there we see The Hooded Assassin attacks.
Followed by the unveiling of The Hooded Assassin as Cobra. Then of "Cobra" as actually Wall and Soriano. We then see clips of the night Skitzo and Bradley turn on Druschel to join Damage Inc. Then of McWhirter, Cobra, and Collins stepping up to form The Resistance Force. A shot of the six man tag graphic ends the show.]
[Fade to black.]
