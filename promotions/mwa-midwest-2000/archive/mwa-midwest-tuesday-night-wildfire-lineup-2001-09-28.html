Tuesday Night Wildfire Line-up

28 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{MWA} TUESDAY NIGHT WILDFIRE LIVE!!! From Louisville Gardens in Louisville, KY Main Event: Champion vs. Champion Non Title Match MWA Toughman Champion Chris Holland vs. MWA American Champion Skitzo Chris Holland's run continues. Skitzo's run with the newest Championship in
MWA begins. And they collide in the Main Event of a Wildfire based on the fallout of Crusade of Champions. At the PPV it was announced that MWA would be signing ACW Superstars. How will any ACW Superstars signed by Tuesday feel about Skitzo holding what was at one time the ACW Championship?
Grudge Match Brandon Bailey vs. Sean Powers Things have been heating up recently between Stryker, Bailey, Powers, and
Pandora. Fireworks should be expected in the weeks to come. What started at the PPV with the birth of Epidemic Manifesto will continue with this grudge match on Wildfire. If Powers wins his team will look for a Tag Title Shot!!!
Fallout Match Ian Bradley vs. Drake Istenia What will come of Ian Bradley now that Damage Inc. is dead? Or what is next for Drake Istenia after losing to Chris Holland. Two men that will try to keep the ball rolling before the influx of talent have to face each other on
Tuesday. Debut Match Michael Mazin vs. Eric Stone Ok, so this one doesn't deserve a lot of hype. But Mazin got his ass whooped during an interview by Eric Stone. And then Mazin cost Stone the battle royal match. I'm sure not either of these men are familiar with the term pride, but they are going to have to compete for it on Wildfire.
Also..... ***Dream, Andrews, and Blottie are all injured. Will any of them make it to Wildfire? Will any of them fade into the sunset because of injuries sustained? And how will the three men who main evented Crusade of Champions react to the
New President. We'll see Sunday.
***Michael Wall is the new MWA President. Needless to say that means the impact will be felt this Wildfire. "Nuff Said." ***Alan Soriano is fired!?!?! However, he made it clear that he isn't going anywhere. Will we see him on Wildfire? What about McWhirter and Collins who obviously can't be happy with their unfair termination? And what does Matt
Meola think of all of this? Or does he even have a say?
***All that, ACW Star Debuts, Tons More, and two goofs named Maddix and Howe....TUESDAY!!!! ***Deadline is the evening of 10/2 at Midnight Eastern!!!!
