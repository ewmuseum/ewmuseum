MWA Presents the 2002 Struggle to Survive Tournament

14 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Midwest Wrestling Alliance A Territorial Superpower Since September 2000 [name withheld] (CEO and Owner) in conjunction with Mr. Varona, Kenny Hill, and Alexa Soriano (The MWA Council of Authority) are proud to announce the 2002 Struggle to Survive Tournament. 2001 Struggle to Survive Highlights: *The Number One seed in both the East and West regions, MWA Title Holders in their own rights, fell to relative unknowns who made used the tournament to catapult their wrestling career. *Under the mask of Nightbreed, Jeremy Rhodes traveled to the Semifinals before being defeated by Shaun McWhirter and being forced to unmask. *Brian J. Blottie and Mike Stryker met in the Round of Sixteen. The duo battled in the second of their "Trilogy of Matches" with Blottie walking away as the victor and sealing his spot in the Quarterfinals. Struggle to Survive 2002: *Rally Jackson will be making his Midwest Wrestling return as a part of the 2nd Annual Tournament. Rally brings a legacy built in Hardkore World to the MWA Table with hopes of walking away with The Wolverine Trophy. *The MWA is expanding the tournament to include 4 divisions of 8. The Tri-State Region will contain the top eight wrestlers from New York, New Jersey, and Connecticut. This includes MWA Legend Mike Stryker, and LET Superstar Shane Andrews, and MWA American Champion Big Bad Bill. The International Division will showcase wrestlers from around the world not native to The United States. This includes Rally Jackson, Northern Wrestling Association Champion Lo Mein King, and the latest version of Nightbreed. Last years divisions of East and West have been transformed divisions representing the oceans that boarder our country. The Atlantic Division will be turned to by many as seven superstars will attempt to knock off the MWA Champion on the road to the finals. This division will showcase MWA Champion "The Goods" Bobby Collins, XCA Superstar Insane Wayne, and Michael Mazin. The Pacific Division will have much spotlight. This division features MWA Legends Cobra, Sean Powers, and Alan Soriano. And you can't forget the returning Struggle to Survive Champion Shaun McWhirter. Last year, "The Scottish Monster" Shaun McWhirter outlasted 31 other superstars to win the $50,000 prize and The Wolverine Trophy. More importantly he captured the title of "MWA Survivor" and bragging rights for winning the biggest tournament in MWA history. This June, McWhirter will try to repeat his feat. However, 31 other superstars from MWA and beyond will be attempting to stand in his way. Do you have what it takes to defeat McWhirter and Midwest Wrestling's best and become the 2002 MWA Struggle to Survive Winner? If you would like to compete in the 2002 Struggle to Survive, e-mail [name withheld] For an MWA Application at [detail withheld] .
