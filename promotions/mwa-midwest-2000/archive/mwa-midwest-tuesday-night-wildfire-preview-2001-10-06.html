Tuesday Night Wildfire Preview!

6 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{MWA} TUESDAY NIGHT WILDFIRE LIVE!!! From Rupp Arena at University of Kentucky in Lexington Main Event MWA Championship Brian J. Blottie vs. A Mystery Opponent!!! Who will Michael Wall name? Will it be the debuting Eddie Deegan? Will it be
Ian Bradley or Tristan Caine? Then again we ALL hope that it is Bobby Collins, who has returned to MWA. If Collins does get the shot and loses then he is OUT of MWA for good. But if he wins then the power will shift from Blottie and
Wall. It could be anybody, but it will certainly be a doozy!!!!
Mystery Match??? There have been heavy rumors in the past 48 hours of a Mystery Match being added to Wildfire. With Wall being pestered to hand out a title shot he just might throw two competitors into a bonus match if they are THAT anxious to get into the ring. Then again what says he'll stop at ONE bonus match??
Top Contenders Battle Ian Bradley vs. "Mr. Showtime" Shawn Matthews Some say this match will keep Bradley out of the Main Event. Regardless, I'm sure Ian is just hoping his opponent this week shows up. If Matthews does then it will be two guys trying to prove themselves to the MWA Crowd and the MWA
Officials. Everyone wants a shot at Blottie, and winning a match like this is a solid first step.
Grudge Match Mike Stryker vs. Jefferson Bickerstaff Not technically a debut match as Bickerstaff fought for MWA back in March/April of this year. However, Bickerstaff has improved during his time with NWA and
ACW. Now he's returned and will look to team with Shane Andrews to take the
MWA Tag Team Championship. While Mike Stryker objects to that he'll get his chance to stop Bickerstaffs quest this Sunday. No doubt that Andrews, Bailey and even Epidemic Manifesto will be watching this one closely.
Get The Chair From Me And Use It Match Pic Axe Jack vs. Bill2ill with Chris Holland as the Guest Official Unorthodox....Certainly!!! Entertaining.....No Doubt!!! MWA Toughman Champion
Chris Holland is headed into Sunday with one job. Hold a steel chair and don't count any pinfalls unless they are following a move directly using the said chair. Pic Axe Jack and Bill2ill are gonna have to be creative to win this one, but they aren't known as masters of the hardcore style because of X Rated adventures.
Also.... ***More Signings??? While there is nothing promised as of press time, you can be sure that anyone signed between now and Wildfire will find there way onto the show in some capacity. Speaking of which Rayge, David Erickson, and other recent signees should be in the house. What will their roles be?
***At the end of last Tuesday's Wildfire, Matt Meola made mention of a big announcement he had wanted to make. He said he'd be making it this weekend. What will it be and who will it effect? ***Will Soriano and McWhirter return this week? Or will they fade into the sunset? The arena is sold out, but Matt Meola could surely make an extra row in the front if Alan, Shaun, and company decide to show.
***All that, a ton of fun, two goofs named Maddix and Howe and MORE!!! Deadline is MONDAY Night at Midnight Eastern!!!
