Part 1

Now, I'm sure you're reading those names and going "A flake, an awesome handler, and... PATO~!". But let me get into the meat of this recruitment letter first before you go running off and joining the fed.
The Mid-West Wrestling Alliance has been open for a little over a year now, and just made it's first forray into the Pay-Per-View arena. It is based in, surprise, surprise, the Mid-West, with it's homebase in Ann Arbor, Michigan.
The MWA is run by Matt Meola, a longtime handler in e-wrestling, and a very easy-going and fun guy. He dedicates a _lot_ of his time to make sure the MWA is as good as it can be, and you can almost be guaranteed that you won't have obcenely late cards in the MWA, as Matt makes it his personal goal to get every card out a day or two within the posted release date.
Now, for what you need to know. The MWA is _not_ run via the flash packet system. Instead, all flashes go through YahooGroups, and Matt himself, and are then sent to everyone in the federation. If you want, you can flash everyday, three times a day, as there are no limits to the number of flashes you can do.
But, quality and not quantity are what counts in the MWA. A person that sends one really well-written, very concise, just overall great flash will get the victory over someone who sends a hundred crap flashes. Matt is a good judge when it comes to flashes, and is very fair. No favoritism here, buddy.
Another important aspect is that of "strats". The MWA does not require strats, though they are welcome. For the most part, you're expected to flash against the opponents you are put up against, and that's it. Now, if you _want_ to angle and strat, then you can, and Matt will follow strats as closely as possible. But if you don't create your own angles, Matt will gladly do it for you.
Convinced yet? Well, if you are, then I'm including the _real_ FAQ/application below. Fill it out, send it to
[detail withheld] , and then wait and see if you get into one of the most entertaining, most intriguing, and most interesting federations within the "underbelly" of e-wrestling... THE MWA!

Application ---------------- Introduction: ---------------- At the end of the Summer of 2000, Real American Wrestling was striving in it's fifth year. It was doing so well, that talent was overflowing. In order to keep talent from being put on the backburner, and to make RAW more of an elite fed that only the talented can get into the Midwest Wresling Alliance was founded by Matt Meola. The Midwest Wrestling Alliance (MWA for short) was supposed to be a RAW Farm League. Til RAW collapsed, that is. With RAW down,
MWA continues on. Our goal is now to be a territorial superpower that will dominate e-wrestling for years to come.
Since beginning in September of 2000, MWA has gone under a lot of changes. We have picked up the few rays of light from fallen federations such as RAW,
NECW, and ACW. We have experimented with running multiple shows a week. We momentarily stepped away from our small roots formula to run our first
"pay-per-view" for our one year anniversary show. We've had over 1000 roleplays and through our first year averaged 92 roleplays a month. However, recently that number has fallen. That's where bringing you into the federation comes in. But before we get to that, let me tell you a little about our style of e-wrestling.
The style of wrestling that MWA is going to present you will be a old school/new school mix of angles, storylines, and classic wrestling. I, like others I know, are tired of seeing pointless matches with off the wall stipulations every card. That is why MWA will be taking a less extreme approach to wrestling. You'll get a better feel of this as we go on. With that said, on to the Rules and Regulations.
---------------- 2. Rules and Regulations ---------------- (1) No Super Extreme Matches If you want to make challenges to other wrestlers that is fine. However, stipulation matches are special, and they are reserved for special occasions.
Therefore, you can't challenge anyone to a specific stipulation match. It is up to MWA management to determine what type of match each will be. Here in MWA we have a limited budget. So we are limited in the stipulation matches we can have. I'll add stipulation matches where I see appropriate.
(2.) Don't Whine. You are going to lose. That is a simple fact of life. These things happen. Get over them and move on. I don't need to hear about your problems. (3.) Be Appropriate As you'll read later on, we have a E-Groups RP system here in MWA. Keep the swearing down to a necessary minimum, and racial slurs out. PLEASE. I don't want to have to change it to a system in which I approve all outgoing rps.
-------------- 3. RP System -------------- The RP System is an Yahoo! Groups System. This means that you will email,
[detail withheld]
and they will send the RP out for you. This keeps the AOL people from using things like cool font, colors, and pictures to unfairly up the entertainment level of the RP. It also cuts out TOS problems.
Also you can't jack the list as you will never know exactly who is on it. As
I've stated above be appropriate. Don't overdue the swearing. If Stone Cold can't say it, you probably shouldn't say it either. Without censoring yourself that is. Also no racial slurs. I hate people that do that.
Now for the way I judge roleplays. I like to be entertained. I also like to hear a good wrestling promo. Mix the sports entertainment correctly and you will win. I'm not an English Major, but run it through spell check first.
Spelling errors are distracting and I don't like distractions. 40 Lines is a good average. If I start skimming your rp to get through it you haven't only bored me, but your good points are being missed. Shorter then 30 and you are probably not saying enough. Longer then 75 and you had better be good enough to entertain me. Other then that it has to be a mystery as to how I judge rps.
After all I will make a bad decision, as I am only human (gasp!), so I need some way to cover my ass.
---------------- 4. Titles ---------------- Midwest Championship Title: This is the one everyone is gunning for. It isn't the World Title, because we aren't a Worldwide Federation. We are a regional territorial superpower with a Championship Title that fits. Most commonly this belt will be reffered to as the MWA Championship. It is as prestigeous to MWA
Superstars as the WWF Championship is to the WWF Superstars.
Toughman Title: Throw the rules out when we contest this title. It's your basic Hardcore title except I'm cutting out all the nonsense bullshit that WWF and WCW give you in their "hardcore" matches.
American Title: This title came to MWA as part of a merger. Itwas brought into
MWA as the former ACW Championship, but with ACW being a fallen federation the belt needed a name change. Thus The American Title Drew Druschel defended it as part of a six-man tag match at MWA's first PPV and lost it. And for those who wonder, no this belt doesn't have to be held by an American.
Tag Team Titles: You can have one handler or two handler teams Other Important Info: Nobody will leave MWA wearing a title. If you quit while having the title, you will get SQUASHED on the next card. By joining MWA, you agree to the terms that state I can put you in matches whenever I want if you are a reigning MWA Champion, and you can't break ties to MWA until losing a belt.
---------------- 5. Weekly Schedule (Card Info) ---------------- Schedule: Sunday Night Wildfire: The week will kick off with our Sunday card. It will showcase 3-4 matches per week, and go under the name Wildfire. There are no Pay-per-view's here in MWA since we are a small time farm fed.
However, there are Supercards. A special name will be given to these cards and they will usually have 5 matches.
Monday: Monday is a day to roleplay for feedback to the card. Incase I'm behind this is my fallback day as there won't be anything on it.
Tuesday: On Tuesday is when I usually send out the newsletter Wednesday-Saturday: Roleplay your butts off. Card Info: As you can tell from the line-up we only have one card a week. I will be doing all the writing so I have to keep my workload down. Any sent after the established deadline (noon on the day of the card) will not count. Back to the
Supercards they will be filtered in when we have something big. They will be called by "cool inovative" names, and will showcase big time matches. Most fed's have these in forms of PPV's every month. I am going to save them so that they matter.

Part 2

E-Wrestlers of All Experiance, MWA is Looking for talent.... We've been around for a year and have a big roster, but recently interest has been slowing down. A lot of the guys in MWA I have been running with for some time now. I'd really like to get some fresh faces involved. I don't want to say that those who "suck" need not apply, because I like to think MWA is all about opportunity. I'm not going to claim we're the best fed on the net, or that nothing is better than MWA. Those types of things are outlandish and untruthful. Then again that's what advertising tends to be about. However here is the truth, straight from the horse's mouth. As MWA owner I strive to make MWA the best it can be. If that means having a no talent at the bottom or my roster and having IM conversations with him about how to become a better roleplayer each week then so be it. If it means bringing in the RSPWF rated
"top guys" only to see them quit after one day because they don't want to work their way up then so be it. I'm going to make MWA the best federation it can be. In order to do that I need those involved to work their asses off. So for all those that are willing to work, I'm willing to have under the MWA umbrella.
Anyway, I'm tired of rambling on when I should be studying for an exam or writing a card. So let me wrap up with this.
If you are at all interested then give MWA a shot. You may hate it. Then again you just may love it. I know from experiance that there is no e-federation for everyone. However, unless you give it a try you'll never know if MWA is for you.
Thanks for your time, MWA Owner Matt Meola
