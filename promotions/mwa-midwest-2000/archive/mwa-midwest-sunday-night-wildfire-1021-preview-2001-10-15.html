Sunday Night Wildfire 10/21 PREVIEW

15 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{MWA} SUNDAY NIGHT WILDFIRE LIVE!!! From The Air Canada Centre in Toronto, Ontario, Canada Main Event: Mixed Tag Team Contest Ian and Rebecca Bradley vs. Brian Blottie and Michael Wall Ok, now that you have read that over four or five times to make sure you were reading right....now that you've called someone else into the room to have them read it to be absolutely positive....now that your jaw is off the floor....on to the preview. Ian Bradley and Brian Blottie is becoming the hottest rivalry in the MWA Championship scene. While everyone wants to see
Collins/McWhirter/Soriano get the shot, Blottie is doing everything he can to make it not happen. While he does, Ian Bradley is stepping up and doing everything he can to make his shot happen. So Wall is gonna give Blottie a shot at Ian and at the same time put Ian's wife in danger. What will happen when Rebbeca Bradley makes her debut? What will happen when Ian and Blottie hook it up? Can "The Bomb" defeat The Champ?
For A Tag Title Shot Skitzo vs. Epidemic Manifesto So we've gone from weird to weirder. The MWA American Champion has a shot to earn the right to fight for the Tag Titles. Catch is that he doesn't have a partner. Where did this come from you ask? Well, Michael Wall is none to happy that Skitzo aided Ian Bradley in getting into the Main Event last week.
So he'll pay by facing two men this week. However to motivate the Powers/Pandora duo, he's added the catch that they can earn a shot at the Big
City Hitmen if they win.
MWA Toughman Championship Elimination Match Chris Holland vs. Bill2ill vs. Pic Axe Jack After last week these three men have unfinished business. They'll go at it for the Toughman Championship this week. And they'll do it elimination style.
Things should get crazy when these "hardcore icons" of Midwest Wrestling go at it. The burning question is as to which man will return to the United States as the reigning Midwest Wrestling Alliance Toughman Champion.
Six Men Sudden Death Debut Jack Johnson vs. David Erickson vs. Ruiner vs. Insane Wayne vs. Rayge vs. Eddie Deegan Johnson and Ruiner both played a part in ruining an opportunity for someone to get a shot at the MWA Championship last week. Deegan and Erickson are men that want a piece of Blottie but remain unproven. Insane Wayne and Rayge are crazy hardcore bastniches. So not only are we throwing all the men into the ring at once. But we are making it a one fall to a finish match. The stakes are high and only one man can come out looking good. Who will it be?
The Decision Alan Soriano vs. Shaun McWhirter Matt Meola has hired a legal team to work to override the firings that took place on September 23rd. However, due to expense they can only deal with the bringing back of one superstar at a time. Who will it be? Shaun McWhirter or
Alan Soriano? The Scottish Monster or The Amercian Patriot? The fans have the chance to voice their opinion in a poll at
http://groups.yahoo.com/group/MidwestWrestling/polls , however the ultimate decision is Matt Meola's. Who will he pick? Also..... ***Tempers are flaring and frustration is building. The animosity building under the new Michael Wall regime is coming to a head. Who will feel the brunt of the explosion? Who will benefit? Who will get burned?
***Jefferson Bickerstaff and Shane Andrews aren't happy that Epidemic Manifesto was picked over them for the match against Skitzo. What will they have to say
(or do) about it this weekend.
***Fans/Wrestlers video tapes voicing their opinions on why McWhirter/Soriano should be brought back will air. Will you send a tape? What will the tapes say? Can they persuade Matt Meola?
***Has the wave of debuts come to an end or will more Superstars be showing up on 10/21 as a surprise? ***Will Alan Soriano or Shaun McWhirter show up on the night where Matt Meola will make his big announcement? ***What else will happen? ***All that, two goofs named Maddix and Howe, and more as Wildfire airs this coming Sunday from Canada!!!! ROLEPLAY DEADLINE on the evening of 10/21 at MIDNIGHT EASTERN!!!
