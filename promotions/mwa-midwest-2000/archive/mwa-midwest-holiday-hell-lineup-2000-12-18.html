Holiday Hell Line-up

HOLIDAY HELL!!!! Live From The Odium in Chicago, Illinois on December 28th, 2000 ***TRIPLE MAIN EVENT*** ***TO DETERMINE THE COMPETITORS OF NEW YEAR'S BASH*** ***ON JANUARY 7th.*** Main Event #1 WORLD TITLE MATCH "The Goods" Bobby Collins (c) vs. "Supernatrual" Shane Starbuck Main Event #2 PINFALLS COUNT ANYWHERE Mike Stryker vs. The Dream Main Event #3 Cobra vs. "Mr. Showtime" Shawn Matthews ***TAG TEAM TOURNAMENT*** ***SEMIFINAL MATCHES*** Semifinal #1 Extreme Reality (Shaun McWhirter and Brian Rogers) vs. The House of Pain (Sentinel and Sammy Ferrara) Semifinal #2 D.C. Danger (Bob and A.J. Carroll) vs. The Burning Circle (Sinder and Anthony Dark) PLUS...... ***Alan Soriano will be in the house. What havoc will he bring to Holiday Hell? We'll find out. ***Matt Madden will bring The Buddy Christ(TM) with him. ***Trent Twilight is expected to speak to the Chicago crowd. What will he have to say this time? ***Brandon Bailey will return!!!! He is being given an open mic. Will he take advantage of it, or will he just reak havoc. ***It is a Supercard so expect all that and MUCH MORE!!!!
