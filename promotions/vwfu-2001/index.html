A June 2001 geocities promotion that grew from seven to seventeen wrestlers in its first nine days and renamed itself Supreme Championship Wrestling when the federation whose name it carried forced the change. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Viking Wrestling Federation Underground introduced itself on 13 June 2001 as a promotion still “structuring & developing,” opening its pitch to other feds first: an interfed rivalry, the post proposed, “something like what the WWF & WCW is currently & will be doing.” It grew quickly and said so in every plug that followed — seven wrestlers signed within five days, twelve within eight, seventeen within nine — each post inviting either real or invented wrestlers, though it said it preferred the invented kind and preferred newer handlers over veterans, and each one repeating that an interleague war with three other feds was already being arranged.

On 22 June 2001 the promotion announced its own new era under a new name: the Viking Wrestling Federation itself had forced VWFU to change its name or close, the post says, so it rebranded as the Supreme Championship Wrestling, called the switch no big deal — “we sound more professional” — and reported the roster holding at seventeen, still short-staffed on cruiserweights, mid-card heels, and the card-writers and columnists it needed to run the fed properly.

No card, roster, or result survives under either name. Whether the promised interleague war ever happened, and how or when the renamed Supreme Championship Wrestling closed, are both outside the surviving record.
