[SCW] A New Era...

22 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Supreme Championship Wrestling was formerly known as "Viking Wrestling Federation Underground", until the Viking Wrestling
Federation themselves forced us to change our name or close down. But it's no biggie; we sound more professional being known as the Supreme Championship
Wrestling. Anyway, the SCW has debuted a new design which looks more organized, and within 9 days, we have got 17 people on our roster; not bad for a fed that was written off by many "big boys of e-wrestling"! Granted, not all of our members are Nick Demolas, Sebastian Crows and such; but that's why we need you(yes, you) to show the others a thing or two.
  We accept only fake wrestlers, and we currently need: cruiserweights & mid-card heels. The
SCW is also in the midst of setting up an interleague with 3 other feds, which will no doubt result in some gripping storylines. So, if you are a new guy to the sport, or a veteran who wishes to add one more fed to his "conqured" list, come over to the SCW!!
  We are also in desperate need of staff-members, so that we can make the VWFU one of the best there is. No 2 men could run an entire e-fed, so we need all the help we can get!! So, if you a card-writer that can write at least write two 6 KB matches per show, or a columnist who wants to express his opinions, drop by the SCW.
  Hoping to see you there soon, ~CEO [name withheld]~   http://www.geocities.com/scwrestling2k1/
