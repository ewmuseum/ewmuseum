VWFU: Check Us Out!!

21 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Viking Wrestling Federation Underground has debuted a new design which looks more organized, and within 8 days, we have got 12 people on our roster; not bad for a fed that was written off by the VWF itself. Granted, not all of them are Nick Demolas and such, but that's why we need you(yes, you) to show the others a thing or two. We accept both real or fake wrestlers, but we rather fake wrestlers. Another advantage to joining up as a fake wrestler is that you will get more of a chance to take part in the interfed wars the VWFU is setting up with 3 other feds. So, if you are a new guy to the sport, or a veteran who wishes to add one more fed to his "conqured" list, come over to the VWFU!! Hoping to see you there soon, ~CEO [name withheld]~ http://www.geocities.com/vwf_underground/
