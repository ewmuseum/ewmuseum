NIWF Stable Review

29 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, you wanted a stable review...you got it!! Coporation of Success (COS) ******************************** This is one of the original stables that seems to be falling apart piece by piece. The COS already lost shining star Larry Gates to the Tower of Doom and made a mistake by firing AiR HiLL. However, look for Mr. Success to pull the trigger and make a deal somewhere along the line. Mr. Success has already made a bid to Jerry "The Bulldog" Jericho a few weeks ago but it was turned down.
Let's look at who Mr. Success has in his stable: Mr. Excitement Dropkick: Former champ, current #1 Contender for the NIWF Title! Dropkick is possibly the best wrestler the NIWF has ever had and part of the
COS!! If Mr. Success uses him correctly, he coulf bring back the gold in the
COS!!! Hightower: A very tall man....a real good technician. Hightower has never received any kind of big push in the NIWF but he has been in training to make
Mr. Success happy! Hightower, if used correctly, could do a lot for the COS!!!
Tax Collector: After a very slow start in the NIWF as a heel, Tax Collector turned to Mr. Success for help. Mr. Success seemed to fix the mistakes Tax
Collector made in the ring and made him a better business man.
Yuri Yachmenev: After not doing much for the Diamond Aces, Yuri finally decided to make a huge turn and reveal his true self!! Not more then a week later, Mr.
Success approached Yuri about joining the COS!! However, rumors have been speculating about just why Yuri joined the COS! People are saying he has joined just to get money to support his family back in Russia and now...he has enough!!
With Yuri involved on the Tower of Doom's side, one may wonder if there is another turn for Yuri in store!!
Arctic Blast: Arctic Blast are a newer tag team in the NIWF!! A fairly young tag team that has yet to make a name for themselves. The team consists of Jim
Arctic and Johnny Blast. With the guidance of Mr. Success and a little more wrestling training, Arctic Blast could go for the tag team gold and help bring
Mr. Success all the gold into the COS!!!!
Rumored to be going to the COS: Celestial Crusher, Chad "Lightning Kid" Shade,
Jerry "The Bulldog" Jericho, "Masters of Manuevers" Jeff Norwood, The Dream
Warrior.
Diamond Aces **************** Watch out for this heel tag team!! One of the more activally involved teams in the NIWF! These guys can be VERY dangerous and are looking to make a big move at the 8 on 8 match!! With two managers in Diamond Doug Hunter and Paul B.
Serious, the Diamond Aces have what it takes to become the top stable in the
NIWF! Let's look at who the Diamond Aces have so far:
Bobbo the Clown: Bobbo was originally a small name in the NIWF but seemed to become a bigger name when he joined the Diamond Aces! Bobbo first fueded with
Mr. Excitement Dropkick but eventually lost big time. Bobbo then went after AiR
HiLL but still could not win!! Bobbo finally got the services of the Diamond
Aces! I wonder who Bobbo's next victim could be?
Davey Rumble: This guy has made a huge statement in the NIWF!! From day 1,
Davey decided that the chokslam was the best thing ever!! He once took on the whole COS until Larry Gates ran in and stopped him! Gates and Rumble have feuded but Gates has always came out victorious! Davey has been quiet ever since....hmmmm!!
Jimmy Cougar: Jimmy Cougar is one half of the sort of seperated Glamour Boys! Jimmy has not wrestled much but seems to get involved whenever trouble stirs up. Jimmy has been in a few singles matches but has not won many. If Jimmy was used correctly, he could be US Champion!! However, it seems Diamond Doug has other plans for him!!
Jason Starr: This is OUR NIWF CHAMP!! Sometimes people would look at Starr as if he were a coward!! Starr has held onto the title for a long time and has proved his worthiness of being champ! There was some speculation regarding the
Starrlock but it was obvious that it was a normal sleeperhold with a lot of force behind it!! Starr has made successful title defenses as he reatains his title all the time!
Mystic Warriors: Another new team with some great talent. These guys are more of magicians and perform a lot of magic! If they wrestle enough matches, they could make a HUGE run for the titles! Thus far, these guys have came the closest to beating Lethal Force and taking away their title!!
Hooded Man Lucifer Kross: The Diamond Aces brought this man in for one purpose: The destroy the Other Hooded Man!! Lucifer Kross could be a very dangerous man if used correctly! Lucifer has plenty to offer to the NIWF. I look for Lucifer to soon feud with the Dream Warrior!
Rumored to Go to Diamond Aces: AiR HiLL, "Lucky Man" Dylan Dice, The Hole Brothers The Brotherhood ****************** This is one of the newer stables in the NIWF!!! A stable of Nazi's is a very dangerous thing to get involved in. These guys can be very dangerous when you cross their paths! Currently, The Brotherhood has something against The Hole
Family. Let's look at the members:
Fourth Reich: This tag team is one of the top tag teams in other feds and are looking to make a name for themeselves in the NIWF!!! Probably the best tag team that works as a team next to the champions. I expect these guys to win the title next before anybody else does.
Baron Von Brutality: This is another BIG man in the NIWF!!! Don't get in this guys path or you might get hurt!
The Eliminator: The Eliminator just recently had a huge win over Jerry "The
Bulldog" Jericho thanks to "Lucky Man" Dylan Dice! The Eliminator does have a rematch set for the next card! The Eliminator can possibly be one of the top wrestlers in this stable but Johnny Himmler needs to learn how to use him right!
Vladimier Zhamnov: This guy is one of the quietest guys in the stable. People have talked and said things about this guy possibly being gay!! Either that or he just likes to keep to himself. One wrestler by the name of Owen Hole used a comment like:
"If Zhamnov would talk, we would know more about his preference of the same sex by the way he conducts his speeches".
Rumors: None Tower of Doom ***************** One would have to wonder just how long these guys can hold up as a team! They seem to have a little heat amongst themselves on the inside and we have not seen them yet on the outside. AiR HiLL is the leader of this club and has these dangerous men in his stable:
Raekwon: This is the guy everybody loves to hate. Currently AiR HiLL's tag team partner, Raekwon is also an excellent singles wrestler. Raekwon seems to like to get actively involved in many matches that he is not even supposed to be at!
Watch out for this guy because he always makes a statement in the NIWF!!! Larry Gates: As somebody has put it before, what hasn't he done? Larry Gates has won the US Title not once but TWICE in the NIWF!!! Not only that, but Gates also won the NIWF Tag Team Championship with his partner Mr. Excitement Dropkick as the 2 Exciting Dudes. He has seemed to reunite with Dropkick but seems to want to stay in the stable as well! The question surrounding him: Can he do both?
AiR HiLL: There is plenty of speculation surrounding the leader of this stable! AiR HiLL was rumoured to have talks with the Diamond Aces but turned down any offers. AiR HiLL is also 2 time US Champ!! AiR HiLL has a match coming up where he teams with Jason Starr and one might have to wonder about HiLL's status in the Tower of Doom since he is teaming with Jason!!
"The Spartan" Troy Walters: After a name change, the Spartan seemed to go through an attitude change as well. Once a face, The Spartan seems to be nuetral at this time. The Spartan has made derogatory comments to his own teammate, Larry Gates but yet has a friendship with Raekwon. One may wonder, is this just a trap for Larry Gates or is the Spartan playing with everybody's mind?
Rumors: Jason Starr, Chad "Lightning Kid" Shade, Yuri Yachmenev, "Lucky Man" Dylan Dice "The Lucky 7 Club" ******************** This is another new stable that is not complete yet. Led by "Lucky Man" Dylan
Dice, this stable is looking for the most dangerous wrestlers in the NIWF!!
Let's take a look at who is currently in the stable: "Lucky Man" Dylan Dice: This guy has came in and immediately made an impact. Dice has already injured a man in the NIWF and plans on injuring more. Currently in a feud with Jerry "The Bulldog" Jericho, this guy might be a very lucky person if he came out of the feud alive!!!
Lethal Force: This is the biggest tag team in the NIWF!!! These guys are the current NIWF Tag Team Champions and have had 7 successful title defenses and held onto the title for a bit. Expect these guys to make a huge impact in this new club!!
Rumored to come in: Winterblast, Arctic Blast, Lethal Leaders, Yuri Yachmenev,
AiR HiLL, Raekwon, Larry Gates, Bobbo the Clown.
The Hole Family ***************** This is probably the team that will always stick together!! The Hole Family was started when Bob and Owen Hole brought in their two brothers, Ed and Daniel.
However, there could be an end to the Hole Family. Let's look at the members: Hole Brothers: This team consists of Bob and Owen Hole!!! These guys are the current #1 contenders for the tag team belts but every time they make a run for the titles, they seemed to be stopped by another team. The Hole Brothers could be finished in the NIWF as they have a retirement match against their rivals known as The Juice!!
Ed Hole: The biggest of the Hole Brothers..but yet the slowest! I believe that
Ed Hole is either mental or reetarded because he still acts like a child! Ed
Hole seems to make stupid mistakes in the ring but the Hole Family always seems to cover up his mistakes.
Daniel Hole: The queitest and the smallest and the youngest of the Hole Brothers! Daniel Hole was told by his oldest brothers to let them do the talking and that he can show his skills off in the ring. Daniel Hole is more of a 1-2-3 Kid type of wrestler, a daredevil flyer. With more years of training, or even a good manager, Daniel Hole could become a top wrestler!!!
The Allied Powers ******************* A new stable of faces in the NIWF!!! The leader is Hightower, which might mean that Hightower could be quitting the COS!!! I have heard plenty of rumors concerning people that want to come in. A few names to mention are Chad
"Lightning Kid" Shade, The Dynamite Kid, and maybe the return of the Bomb Squad!! Hightower has found a new member to bring into the NIWF called the
Dream Warrior and already they are after Larry Gates!! Raekwon could be another victim..or could he be turning face and joining the club?
Asian Invasion? ****************** Ok...the tag teams' name is Asian Invasion. Another face stable!! So far we have not seen any of these guys but sources say that a 4th member of the team will be a current NIWF wrestler. It could be Jerry "The Bulldog" Jericho. We will soon see Shunjarro and Ryushuki team up and face Fourth Reich in an oriental death match where glass is poured around the ring and on the fists of each member involved. A winner is determined whenever the other team can not continue! We have yet to hear anything about the other member known as Jon Pall
Isoclaw.
