Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Aerial Wrestling Entertainment survives in one document, a news post of 19 July 2004 announcing a change to its own Trios Division: CEO Jacob Matthews opened the division to any trios team, new or established, rather than requiring three wrestlers already under contract to form one, while keeping its existing 235-pound weight limit.

The same post names the promotions it hoped the change would draw from — South West Lucha Libre, Pro Wrestling INFINITY, Super Japan Pro Wrestling, and NEO International — quoting Matthews: “There's a lot of good teams out there... We're really hoping that some of those teams will take another look at what we have to offer here at Aerial Wrestling Entertainment and compete with our existing talent.”

Whether any of those teams signed, or what its Trios Division became, isn't in its surviving record, which holds nothing beyond this one announcement.
