Breaking News!

Jul 19, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In news that had been rumored since its inception, AWE CEO Jacob Matthews announced a change in the Trios Division today. This new change would allow _any_ Trios Teams, new or established, to sign contracts with AWE. Though still preferring to have existing wrestlers form teams within AWE, Matthews hopes that this change would bring even more interest into the Trios Division, and hopefully give existing teams from all over the world the chance to wrestle. "There's a lot of good teams out there," said Matthews. "We're really hoping that some of those teams will take another look at what we have to offer here at Aerial Wrestling Entertainment and compete with our existing talent." Since the announcement, Matthews is rumored to be looking all over for teams to sign. Most notably, the word has it that South West Lucha Libre (SWLL), Pro Wrestling INFINITY, Super Japan Pro Wrestling (SJPW) and NEO International have been tapped for talent-trading, but no definite answer has been given from any of these promotions. Only the future holds the answer for AWE and its Trios Division... and as of this announcement, the future looks really good. <----------> OOC: Well, I figured this announcement would come sooner or later. :) Though I haven't really pushed anyone to form teams as of yet, I think the sooner I get this out the better the Trios Division can be. What I'm looking for now is teams to join up... multiple handlers, one handlers, whatever. The same rules apply for the Trios Division as far as the weight limit (235lbs. and under), but no longer do three handlers have to comprise a team. So if you've got a character in for singles and you've got a tag team you'd like to have team with them, that's completely cool/ Spikes, if you could post this entire message to RSPWF and EW Roundtable, I'd appreciate it. :) Thanks guys... keep up the good work. - Jason
