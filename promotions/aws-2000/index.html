Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Avitar's Wrestling School opened with a single show, Tuesday Night Scrimmage, posted to the newsgroup on 18 January 2000 and hosted first on an outside message board before moving, that same week, to its own address.

Its own promotional copy billed itself as “the first school for e-wrestling,” delivered less as a plain announcement than as a stand-up bit from an in-character host, Dot Cohen, who described Avitar himself grading most of its roleplays, backed by a staff of instructors, and likened Tuesday Night Scrimmage to Nitro, “except… like different.”

Nothing else survives — no roster, no graded roleplay, no second show. Whether Avitar's Wrestling School ran again after that first Scrimmage, or how it came to an end, is not known.
