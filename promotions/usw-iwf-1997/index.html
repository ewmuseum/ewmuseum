Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

United States Wrestling ran as a league of a play-by-mail wrestling circuit, the IWF: its own title-lineage sheet states outright that “USW was a promotion that ran in the IWF from 1997 to ???.”

Its World Heavyweight Title opened on a tournament final at its own USW Coliseum, won by Mystic Michael Magic over Patrick “Paradigm” Shift, and passed through “The One” Orlando Rock, the Cuban Warlord (crowned at the circuit's own third annual wide pay-per-view, at Madison Square Garden), Kurt Bang and back to Patrick “Paradigm” Shift before its own record goes dark for several bulletins. Its World Tag Team Titles opened the same way, at that same circuit-wide pay-per-view, to the Rasslin' Rebels, and passed through the Balding Eagles to Southern Comfort after the Balding Eagles left the promotion, vacating the belts.

The record resumes at bulletin #63 across all five of its titles — by then it had added an American Television Title, a Women's Title and a Blood N Guts Tag Team Championship — and runs, with further unrecorded stretches of its own, through a final bulletin, #70, at which every title still had an active champion in place: Rocky Li holding the World Heavyweight Title, NC-17 the World Tag Team Titles, and Kabuki Dragon the Television Title while also sharing the Blood N Guts Tag Titles alongside the Women's Champion, Sara Ricci.

No document past bulletin #70 survives, and how — or whether — the promotion ended isn't in its surviving record.
