All right everybody welcome to the first column of "In The Eyes of the Bookie." Lets kick off our article right where the card for this Thursday does with Chad Storm vs. Curve. The SWA hasn't heard much from either competitors as of late, so I'm going to go with curve on this one just cuz he has the cooler name of the two. Don't get me wrong though, neither are names to write home about. Folks, I'll be betting a dog's small left nut on Curve this Thursday. Moving on in the card we see Mike Icon vs. Monica. All I can say is, "Who the hell is Monica?" I'll be betting one moderate, yet respectable sized dog testicle on Mike Icon for this Thursday's show. Next up we have Ron Heaps vs. Johnny Morris for the Number One contender slot for the TV title. This is a toughie. These two competitors seem very evenly matched. I really can't place either one as the winner of the match. My prediction will be a No Contest for this match up. I'll bet two microscopic chicken balls on this one. Now we finally come to a match that people actually care about, Beorn vs. Joseph for the Television Title. Ladies and Gent's place your college tuition on Beorn on this one. His recent affiliation with the AoA will stick his name to that title for a long while. Don't be surprised to see a little AoA interference should things swing Joseph's way. Put every nut you can find on the farm in Beorn's name this Thursday. The Number one contender for the Canadian Title between Justin Case and Gangsta will be another dandy, folks. When either of these men are on they can go head to head with just about any guy in this federation. If I had to place a bet on someone, I would go with Justin Case. Don't quote me on that one, it all comes down to who happens to be "on" on this particular night. I won't be placing any more than planter's nuts on this one. Make sure to keep your eye on this match. The next match on the card is Markus King vs. Josh Sandborn. Sandborn sucks. Markus King by a f*cking pair of testicles infected with elephantitus. Warren Peace's Canadian Title goes on the line against Fury in perhaps one of the most interesting matches of the night. Fury has proven himself in the Hardcore ranks, but is he really ready to become a Canadian Champion? I don't think so. I'd put my nuts on Warren Peace if I were you! The main event of the night is enough to make you wanna grab your testicles. Michael Sexton vs. Mark Mathews vs. the current champion, OutKast. Any number of things could happen in this match but I can guarantee you something outrageous will happen in this match. Be sure to expect a special guest appearance from one or more of the following: Beorn, The Hunter, Jimmy Smith, or Mirage. Beorn, Hunter and Sexton of course comply of the AoA, which is turning heads here in the SWA/AWA. And for those of you who have been in a cave the past 3 weeks, Jimmy Smith and Mark Mathews have joined to create the sex crazed BBI (Bad Boy Inc.) Mirage and OutKast haven't exactly been getting along, which is why I wouldn't be surprised to see Mirage make an appearance this Thursday. Mirage probably deserves a slot in this match and besides, does this guy really need a reason for interfering? Can anyone really make a prediction on this one? Hell no! If I had my balls in a guillotine, I'd probably be forced to put my balls on OutKast simply because, he is the SWA/AWA unified champion. But who knows what the hell could happen, this is the SWA/AWA, after all. That's all for this week, be sure to look out for my predictions on this Sunday's card. Well, this is the Psychic dude, signing off.
