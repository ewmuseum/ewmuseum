SWA/AWA Magazine: Fall Edition

SWA/AWA Magazine Fall Edition by: "The News Man" Mike Right Cover Story: New Faces and Old Well, what a crazy time of year in wrestling. Many new faces have busted on the sceen here in the SWA/AWA. Some new faces in new places, and some rookies are in the mix as well. One thing is fure shure, these debuting stars have really come to play here in the SWA/AWA. Such names as Mark Mathews, Justin Case, and Fury. What a lineup and I would like to focus on some of these names. First off we have Mark "The Mainevent" Mathews. He comes from the AWA off a ugly knee injury, and now has a SWA/AWA contract. Mathews, is good no doubt, but what seems to get Mathews in trouble in the past is his huge mouth, not to mention ego, and his love for himself. Mathews is ring technician to the extreme. No one can match him hold for hold, or that matter insult for insult. He's made some big noise here in a short amount of time. In the coming months we will see what "Mr.Sex and Violence" has, he has the tools to make it big. Now, let's focus on Justin Case, a man kinda cut from the same mold as Mathews. However, Case comes from a great career in the AODWF, capturing many titles, and making many enemies in the process. His desire to talk a good game and back it up is second to none. He calls himself "The Millenium Game" and believe you me he comes to play. Case, can hit a opponent with moves from anywhere. He just hasn't had the luck he would like so far here in the SWA/AWA. I see big things in the future for Justin Case, Mark and him have really turned heads with their charisma. Now, let me move onto another man with a plan, Fury. The new TV champion. This guy has shown to me nothing but raw power and skill in ring. He has the heart, and no care attitude that won him the TV Belt. Not headlining yet, Fury has beatin up on weaker opponents, I for one would like to see him in the ring with some bigger names. I feel he can make it to the top in a short time due to his, who gives a rat's ass style. Article 2: No Hardcore Division, Who Cares.... Well, many fans of the SWA/AWA, wonder where in the hell is ihe Hardcore Division and HC title in this fed. To tell ya the truth we don't need one, and don't really want one. Come on people think of some of the matches you see here in the SWA/AWA. Sure, we have plenty of hardcore action, is their a real need to have a title. No, hardcore in ring action speaks for itself, like in such other feds as ECW. Do they have a hardcore champion, no, and they don't need one just like us, people know the style of wrestling we have and bring to the ring. In many feds they toss a HC champ out their only to hold a title for maybe 2 or 3 weeks, then they get beat. Also in other federations hardcore divisions, the champ usually isn't the best, but the luckiest sucka in the business. Hardcore action, YES, hardcore title, maybe some other time. Artcile 3: SWA/AWA/CEWF Is it me, or are their way to many people representing way to many companies. Look at the facts folks. SWA/AWA/CEWF, all fightin and arguing over bragging rights. Well, atleast we have yet to see an all out feud between all the members of the three, but never the less tempers have been hot to say the least. For the CEWF, Mac, Rog, and Kast have had the majority to say in this war of words, and it has mainly been directed to the AWA and Rocky Stellar. Meanwhile, Stellar and X, have battled with Mac and Kast for the AWA, and Mathews has AWA pride as well, along with the great Post. Now for the SWA wrestlers, they haven't really got involved yet, in this battle for bragging righs. The only thing I can tell ya being a biased SWA/AWA reporter is that, the SWA/AWA are still active in one way or another, as for the CEWF....closed!!
