Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Seltor's Wrestling Association billed itself, in every surviving document, as "SWA/AWA" — a shared universe with a second promotion, its own in-fiction news column tracking a running feud between the two and a third, the CEWF, which the same column reports "closed." The fed's own Fall 2000 magazine names a fresh Television Champion, Fury, risen through its own Hardcore ranks; a wrestler- records page dated three months earlier shows the belt with Donny "The Dragon" Chacho, the two reigns bracketing an undated change no surviving document names. On its own 27 May 2001 card, the tag team Secret Service — Matt Cross and Will James — is billed defending the Tag Team Championship against Ktulu and Hardcore Craig.

The fed's own roster mixed real wrestlers and original characters on the same win/loss ledger — Bret Hart, Kurt Angle, Triple H, and Chris Jericho recorded beside Donny Chacho, Judge, and Omega — and its own in-fiction predictions column names a wider cast still in play as of its own writing: a unified SWA/AWA champion, OutKast; a Canadian Title held by Warren Peace; and a faction, the AoA, pulling three of the card's own biggest names into its own orbit. None of that is dated closely enough to structure here. Three of the fed's own message-board addresses survive linked from its own site — a ProBoards Newsboard and RP Board, an ezboard OOC Board — none of them fetched in this holding.

SWA/AWA sister fed, unresolved (AWA)

Sister promotion of SWA (Seltor, 1999) (1999-2001)
The fed’s own served corpus bills itself “SWA/AWA” throughout, and its own Fall 2000 magazine and undated predictions column both describe a genuine sister relationship, not a mere link exchange: wrestlers cross over between the two rosters (Mark Mathews billed as arriving “from the AWA” with “AWA pride”), the two feds share one branded World/unified championship by the time of the later column (OutKast billed “the SWA/AWA unified champion”), and a third promotion, the CEWF (“closed” per the fed’s own news column), is named alongside both in a three-way inter-fed rivalry. This sister AWA doesn’t match any other American Wrestling Alliance on record on hosting or dates, and is recorded here as its own, unresolved entity.
