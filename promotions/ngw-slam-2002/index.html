A Next Generation Wrestling of December 2002 whose debut broadcast from Penn State promised a number-one contender and its first tag champions. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Next Generation Wrestling made its debut at the end of 2002 with a special Saturday showing of Thursday Night Slam from the Bryce Jordan Center at Penn State, its president John 'The Beast' Steele promising a free T-shirt to every fan.

The night belonged to High Society. Blake Selters beat Ryu Shunagi in the first match the promotion held, with help from Frederick Stone on the apron, and when Blaine Edwards and Kaz Hahn were counted out together the tag titles Steele had offered went unawarded.

Wes Kendra beat Malachi Thompson in the main event to become number one contender for the NGW World Heavyweight Championship, and the show ended with Raine Steele, the co-owner and head of security, and Todd Clayborne clearing High Society from the ring.

That debut broadcast survives.
