BXWF/Preview

29 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
29 January 1997 The press area at the London Arena is cram packed full of journalists. A large table is draped in the banner of the BXWF. Many 'mikes' are laid out in front of and around the sides of the table. Joey 'the Jet' Koteas enters from a door in the corner of the room. As he is seated the flas of bulbs and whirrs of cameras fills the air. "Ladies and gentlemen of the press, it gives me great pleasure to welcome you all here on what I hope...no I KNOW will be the launch of the most sucesful E-wrestling league in the WWW. Many of you know me." Joey nods to several of the journalists seated at the front of the room. "I have participated in many e-leagues throughout the WWW and have come to the conclusion that many of the Presidents of these so called leagues have had favouritism to certain wrestlers. Why I cant tell you, which Leagues, well many of you will know which ones i am on about I'm sure. So what will be different in the BXWF?. Well firstly each and every wrestler here in the BXWF will be given the same oportunity as the next man to become the best there is. We will endorse any and every fight. We will not however stand for any fans, wrestlers or there handlers to bring this league into disrepute, by way of foul and abusive language to his/her team-mates adversaries, fans or controllers. At the first sign of this a wrestler or his handler or even both will find themselves heavilly punished and or even banned from wrestling for a certain amount of time. What difference will this make. Well We are currently in negotiations with the WWCTC, thats the World Wrestling Cable Television Consortium for those of you who dont allready know, to give them sole rights to broadcast all of our events. What differnence again your asking, well, the WWCTC will broadcast the weekly events to all its customers on there new channel 10 slot.......FREE!" The journalists in the room seem totally amazed at this statement. "And so why would the BXWF allow this to happen, well like I said we are still in Intense negotiations with them at this moment in time, but rather than take a percentage of there profits, which can sometimes be a gamble, we want a set amount of money each month from the WWCTC. thjis in turn will mean ALL our wrestlers will get Purse money. Win, lose or draw each and every man will recieve a set amount of money for every fight. the only time this may change is when special PPV or Title defences are on the line. As I say we are hoping to have this deal all tied up in the near future." "Now are there any questions?" "Mr Koteas can you tell us when the BXWF will actualy make its apearance?" "Yes, well as you know we were hoping for Febuary 1st but due to some unforseen dificulties we have had to put that back to the 15th of February." "Is it true that the BXWF is strugling to find wrestlers to take part in the new league?" "Too some extent this could be true, what I can tell you is that we have 10 pro-wrestlers here in BXWF and we are allways looking for more. So if you are a wrestler out there and think you have what it takes then contact me on [detail withheld] and we will be more than happy to see what we can offer you in return for your services here in the BXWF." "So Mr Koteas, does this mean the league will not and even may not be up and running even by the 15th?" "No it most certainatly does not, come what may February the 15th BXWF will be up and running." "When you say you are still looking for wrestlers what sort, I mean are you looking for the famous ones such as Hulk Hogan, Pshyco Sid and Sean Michaels?" "Absolutely not, we want only original and good wrestlers here in this league. We dont mind if you have wrestled in other leagues on the WWW but we do not want Famous Names here in the BXWF along the lines you just said." As he is about to take another question a man walks over to Joey and whispers something into his ear. A broad smile envelopes his face and the man shakes hands with him. "Ladies and gentlemen you will have to forgive me, but I will have to terminate this confrence here. It seems I am wanted in an urgent meeting with the WWCTC. So as I say Thank you all for coming and giving us your time and we look forward to our next confrence."
