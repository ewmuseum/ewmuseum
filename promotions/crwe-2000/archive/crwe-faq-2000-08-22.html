The big stars, yea, the MIGHT have a title. But guys, if you think.. a fued needs a title, your just wrong. With only THREE titles in the CRWE currently, there is NO way you can actually fued over a title.. for every damn fued.
1) CRWE East Coast Championship - The big, bad title here in the CRWE. The title everyone wants, but only the few will actually touch. Defended under normal rules, unless otherwise stated. Normally a 30 minutue time limit also.
2) CRWE Coastal Championship - The second highest title in the CRWE. This is normally held by the upcoming and coming, yes the future East Coast champion. Win this, your normally destined to carry the bigger title.. less you quit. 3) CRWE Coastally Postal Championship - The crazy, the insane title for the CRWE. These matches will ALWAYS be written by me since I have a very SICK mind. ; ) That is it. A cruiserweight title and a tag team title might be added later, if I can ever get the divisions above 5.
