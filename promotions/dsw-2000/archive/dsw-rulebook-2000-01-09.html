From the Desk of DSW President [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] ): 1. GENERAL WELCOME Greetings. My name is [name withheld] and this is DSW - Deep South
Wrestling. DSW is small, regional efed based in Huntsville, Alabama. We are an email based fed, which means that all Roleplaying takes place over the DSW Mailinglist. To find out more about DSW keep on reading.
2. ROLEPLAYING DSW is a roleplaying based fed, which means that, no, we don't use a simulator and, yes, the outcome of the matches is decided by roleplaying. To roleplay simply write an interview, promo, challenge or whatever for your wrestler and mail it to the DSW Mailinglist. You can roleplay at any time and as often as you like, just don't mail out a hundread messages every day.
3. WRESTLERS In DSW you may have up to two Wrestlers, which can be either two singles or one tag team. You may not have anything exceeding that limit. All wrestlers must be original which means that you can't have The
Undertaker, Stone Cold or The Rock or any rip-off characters (for example: Owen Austin or Bret Goldberg). Be creative. A 7'5 monster doesn't guarentee you success in here.
4. CARD AND SUPERCARDS There will be one show every week, entitled "DSW Southern Sunday". On that show there will be matches, interviews, title defenses and much more. Additionally there will be one supercard (instead of a PPV) every month. Whereas our weekly shows also features rookie and preliminary matches, the supercards will only feature the creame de la creme of DSW.
5. CHAMPIONSHIPS In DSW there are currently four championships, or titles: the DSW World Heavyweight Title, DSW World Tag Team Titles, DSW Regional Title and DSW Alabama Title. The world titles must be defended at least once every month, whereas the regional titles must be defended on *every* card, unless otherwise noted. Champ notes will be mailed out weekly.
6. APPLICATION FORM Allright, now that you know everything you need to know about DSW you may fill may fill out the DSW Application Form if you wanna join. I don't expect the most descriptive application from you, but you should at least spell-check your app and be a little bit more descriptive than just "wears black tights and boots".
