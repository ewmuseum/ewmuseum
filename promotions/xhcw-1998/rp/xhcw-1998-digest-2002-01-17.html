Although Howard Kent modified the characters in this first story, they are quite easy to discern from his real-life experience on the Memphis Mental Health Institute. The Bonedaddy was, of course, meant to be a comment on Skullberry while that young Dr. Joseph Kent was to be an autobiographical character of the author. Dr. Dumas, although some say is derivative from Howard Kent's experience, knowing the institute's prominient Dr. Franklyn Dunn, it is my belief that the character Dr. Dumas has more things in common with the person Howard Kent befriended the most in MMHI, Dr. Michael Ichido, who's been known as a very spiritual and philosophical person--something Dr.Dumas comes to highly represent in Dr. Kent's story. As far as Skullberry's real life story goes, there are many things which haven't yet been sorted out. It is common knowledge that he was a well-enough known personality in wrestling organizations such as the BWF, his own DWX, as well as in the popular BwA and it's predeccesor, the BwF. Through the span of his 3-year career as a sports entertainer he transformed into different personas, called gimmicks in wrestling jargon. Many wrestlers are famous for these occurences, although it is not known of anyone that could ever surpass Skullberry's record of having performed as approximately 50 different wrestling characters during his 3-year career. Some say that this is in essence the main root of Skullberry's madness, but it is my belief, that having suffered from this apparent schizophrenia, Skullberry is merely reacting to something else--something much more deep. The outburst of his 50 personalities is just the result of some other mental or emotional ailment--that is what I believe and that is what I want to prove. [January 18, 2002] It's January 17, 2002...yet I've written January 18, 2002 on the heading of this entry. I feel like I've already lived tomorrow. I've seen what tomorrow looks like. I haven't scribbled on napkins revolutionary thoughts on my whole investigation as I had oftenly done a few weeks ago. It semed like I lost the inspiration, or the intentions of going through with my proposed objectives. I've been reminded of my Skullberry project while having casual conversations with several of my colleagues...but I haven't internalized anything that could fuel my interest, perhaps something to keep me going. I stopped the research on Skullberry, I've stopped working, I stopped being an investigator...all at the same time. Seems like in the last two days I've lived a strangely distracted and meaningless life and I don't know how it came to be that I allowed myself that. Then this morning, while the cotton candy sensation was still bothering my head I opened the newspaper and there was no way I could avoid those big and bold letters...
