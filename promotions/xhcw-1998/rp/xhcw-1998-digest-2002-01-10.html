ON [A black screen is all that is seen. This sudden surge of "dead air" seems to puzzle the viewer. "Is there something wrong with the TV?" or "Maybe there's something wrong with the station?", immediate conclusions drawn by the viewer, an immediate self-thought of result made by an "intelligent viewer". How intelligent is someone if they don't even investigate hard enough? What good is it to search with the mouth when you should be searching with your eyes? At a closer glance, one would see that the screen is not all black, in the center lays a miniscule dot, a point, a period. But this dot slowly begins pulsing, it slowly becomes larger and larger. The ignorant mind of a person probably doesn't notice it, but it slowly inflate, increasing it's size, slowly but surely. The dot slowly turns into the large shine of the light and grows extremely large until the once black screen is flooded with nothing but a bright, white, shining light. The viewers eyes are pierced by this sudden change of scenery. They never saw it coming. They think this light just suddenly came out like a "thief in the night". But the real truth is that this light has always been there, right in front of their face, slowly growing before their eyes, but the retarded minds of the innocent don't notice it and don't even try to notice it until it has finally exploded all over their face like a bubble gum balloon. This bright white light finally stops growing and fades in to just an extremely close up gold glow. As the close-up begins moving away, hints of gold are seen. At a farther glance it is discovered that it's a title belt. The XHCW World Heavyweight Title belt. A powerful dark hand rests upon it, grasping it with great power. As the shot moves further back, a whole human figure is seen. His face hidden in the darkness as all there is, is a spotlight over the man's waist area down. The sound of a breath being inhaled and exhaled slowly grows in the background until finally we see the light glisten over the chin area of the face. Glistening with perspiration, he speaks.] The Juice: Stupid, stupid, stupid. All of you out there. You thought I was speaking of myself? Well you were surely proved damn wrong, weren't you? Did you think I would actually go on messed up in the head by a petty fall? I get it now, but you don't get it do you? You think I'm nothing but an act. You didn't know I was sick. You thought I was faking it. Well I WAS sick as a dog! "Was" being the magic word there. You think I'm that stupid to at least TRY to cure myself? I mean yes, Willow got me into the situation but she had no bad intentions in doing so. She was also the same person who finally healed me thanks to an old friend of hers. You think with all the money I have in the world that I can't acquire the finest assistance? Looks like I fooled you. [Juice finally leans forward, the light glistening off his face.] The Juice: You really think I would lose my title to that stupid piece of trash Duff? Duff may have put up a good fight, but it wasn't good enough! It was a stupid decision actually. I allowed him a shot since he was Mr. Big Talker and it got him nowhere! So he comes crawling back like the little bitch he is to Liermann. On his knees and begging "please, give me another shot, I'll do anything"! Little did he know that he just signed his death warrant. Duff thought he had it in the bag after my little accident. He thought he had the win so he took it easy. He hung out with the god damn "Scoobies" in some fantasy town and played house while I was being attended to by the best Wiccan personnel money, and love, can buy! Then I spent a whole day working out non-stop, trying to get myself back in shape not just mentally, but physically. Then finally, ahh, the big night. "Retribution", where we finally settled our score! He drew up his own conclusion, some ignorant thought made by his little mind, he expected to meet a mentally ill "Juice", but instead he stepped in there with the deadliest, scariest, most ruthless incarnation of The Juice that you could even imagine!!! John Shaft ain't got shit on me! I'm the TRUE bad mother… [We hear Willow's voice from the dark shadows of the room] Willow: Shut your mouth. The Juice: But I'm talking 'bout me! Willow: Then I can dig it. The Juice: But Saturday was not about my own personal "Retribution" against Duff Man, oh no. This was "Retribution" to those plebeians who call themselves "fans". Well, it looks like the shit hit the fans this time! Like the light that floods your screen, you didn't see what was coming, although it saw that it was coming towards YOU! How could The Juice join the great wrestling federation of his legendary tag partner and friend and NOT try to form some kind of alliance? Steve knows me and I know Steve! And I didn't get to where I am because of who I know! I got here because of what I've done! Steve knows I am the DAMN BEST around now! And you can't form a stable of your federations best wrestlers without THE best! Dehibilitator, he's great, "Crown Jewel" Brad Jones, well his nickname explains it all I think, then Mr. Liermann, not only the greatest, but the smartest! Oh and of course, myself, the best, the greatest, number one! With us four, we combine a power that is so absolute that when you hear our names said one after another, the word "doubt" doesn't even cross your mind!!!! [Juice stands up from the chair and walks up extremely close to the camera. His eyes are wide, veins bulging out of his forehead] The Juice: We are the best! And the only thing you can do is sit on your asses and watch us destroy everyone! [A final, but eerie shot of Juice's throbbing eyes are seen as the scene fades to black] OOC: Now I know I don't have a match this week and there really isn't a reason for me to write an rp, but I felt the need to roleplay especially after seeing Steve's inspirational post. I think it's true that XHCW is only starting to really blossom now and in a few months time we will be a melting pot of e-fed veterans and outstanding newcomers. I was flattered at coming in at 4th place for the RSPWF Rookie of the Year voting. Thanks to all the people that voted for me. It's an honor since I've only been doing this for about 9 months and lately I've seen myself in a dry spell, but reason occurrences have lifted my spirits and I feel like a new breath of life has entered me. It was great to see Steve's ideas about the expansion of the XHCW entity and I stand by it 100%. Because I know when Steve wants something, he'll pour his heart and soul to get it. My online priorities are mainly XHCW and ACW (an ezboard wrestling federation which I co-run) and I will always have time for both federations no matter what. So Steve, you know I'm here for whatever whenever and I've said the same many times before to my ACW colleagues. Personnel changes may occur, disagreements are inevitable, but it's all part of the consequences of the game. If we can avoid petty spats and try to keep everything in XHCW together and most of all, have the dedication needed, we have a chance of being one of the most popular e-federations on the internet. I hope I didn't sound too mushy, kiss ass, "win one for the gipper" there. I just totally agree with Steve that XHCW has a bright future.
