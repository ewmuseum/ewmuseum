Xtreme Hardcore Championship Wrestling: Assault

15 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XHCW Assault - 15 November 2001 ================================= ***Synopsis of Liermann Promo*** --At the top of the show, Liermann came out and said there was no competition in XHCW for his World Title, so he is setting up a Battle
Royal @ Awakening on the 24th. All XHCW wrestlers will be entered, and the winner will receive a World Title shot at the end of the Pay
Per View.
--He also cancelled the Fear/Professor match for the evening. He named Fear to an "Administrative consultant" position and said he will not be returning to active competition.
--Plugged the Pay Per View Battle Royal match, and solidified the following matches: "The Classic" John Sebastion v. Alec Metro -Ladder Inside A Barbed Wire Roofed Cage Match for XHCW TransAtlantic Title- "Ironman" James Loyal v. The Dehibilitator -Wheel of Death Match For XHCW World Hardcore Title- Rob Ericson v. Pico Flush -Chair Mountain Match- Over The Top Rope Battle Royal -Winner Faces Liermann At End of Night for XHCW World Heavyweight Title- Chris Cage v. Cos Sexxy -No Rope Barbed Wire Match- The two men start the match trying to throw each other into the ropes early on. Cage reverses one of Cos' attempts and gives him a spinebuster. Cage takes Cos down again and hits him with rapid offense hitting a dropkick, a tilt-a-whirl slam, and a slingshot into the barbed wire. Cage is fatigued early on due to the rapid offense and Cos, with some scratches on his chest, takes advantage. He uses several suplex variations then a DDT. Cos then switches to some submission moves and tries to keep Cage on the ground. Cage blocks a figure four attempt by Cos and turns it into a small package but only gets a 2 count. Cage bounces back up and gets Cos into the corner. He begins pummeling him with kicks and punches. Cage takes Cos and gives him a crossed arm belly to back suplex. Again Cage only gets a 2. He throws Cos into the barbed wire and rakes his back across the barbed wire. Cage picks up Cos and gives him the "Fallout" dropping Cos on his bloody cut up back. Cage climbs up and hits the "Lou Bomb", a swan dive senton bomb for the 1-2-3.
Winner: Chris Cage Pico Flush v. J-Dub -Glass Match- After the bell rings, Pico runs at J-Dub with his gold chair but J-Dub ducks. Pico hits J-Dub from behind with the gold chair. He then hits
J-Dub with the end part of the chair. Pico tries to suplex J-Dub but
J-Dub blocks it and tries sends Pico into the glass. They try several more times to send each other into the glass including a textbook drop toe hold by Pico. Pico lands on his feet behind J-Dub and hits him in the head with the gold chair this time. Pico throws J-Dub in the corner and continuously hits J-Dub in the chair. J-Dub sits in the corner bleeding at the head as Pico continuously hits him. Pico picks up the bloody J-Dub and places him on the top rope. Pico hits the
"Swirly", a tornado DDT from the top rope onto the glass for the 1-2-3 victory.
Winner: Pico Flush Dave Ery v. Bleeding Smile -Barbed Wire Baseball Bat Match- The two men started in opposite ends of the arena, as the crowd counted down from ten. As the crowd reached zero in the countdown,
Ery got to the ring first and grabbed the barbed wire bat. He swung it at Smile but speared Ery. Smile got the bat and started to drive the end into Ery's forehead. Smile turned Ery over and hit him several time with the bat as well. Smile went for a brainbuster but
Ery reversed the move. Ery went for an early pin but was unsuccessful. This early in the match Ery started bleeding from the head. After several power offense moves on Smile, Ery decided to go up top. He missed a moonsault though and Smile got Ery in a schoolboy pin but Ery kicked out at 2. As Smile and Ery both got up, Smile dropkicked Ery into the corner. Smile took the barbed wire bat and banged Ery up some more in the corner. Ery blocked one bat shot from
Smile and hit Smile back with the bat. Ery throws the bat into the middle of the ring and raises Smile up to the top rope. Ery goes for a superplex but Bleeding Smile blocks it. Smile kicks Ery and stands up. Smile gives Ery a powerbomb from the top turnbuckle. Ery's back hits the barbed wire bat and the bat breaks in half. Smile pins Ery
1-2-3. Winner: Bleeding Smile Insane Jack v. 2Short(c) -Thumbtack Match For XHCW World Light Heavyweight Title- This wasn't even a contest. Jack came out from the locker room, and was immediately met with a Vicious Groin Punch & Testicular Twist.
2Short whipped Jack into the ropes and caught him on the run with a springboard bulldog, landing him right in the thumbtacks. In a twisted display, 2Short stripped Jack of his shirt, and rolled him around in the thumbtacks. Adding insult to injury, she stood him up and hit him with a Backhand Bitchslap from Hell for the pin!
Winner and STILL XHCW World Lightheavyweight Champion: 2Short Diablo Smith v. The Dehibilitator(c) -Barbed Wire Brick Match For XHCW World Hardcore Title- Diablo Smith came out to virtually no crowd reaction. Smith pounced onto Dehibilitator as soon as the "old man" got into the ring. Diablo smashed Dehibilitator in the face a couple times with the use of one of the barbed wire bricks. Diablo went for his finisher, the "Devil's
Advocate", which is a variation of an Elevated Camel Clutch.
Dehibilitator had a razor blade tucked into his boot, which he reached, and sliced Diablo's leg open. From that point on,
Dehibilitator took advantage of the situation, leveling Diablo with various moves to impact Smith's neck: a brainbuster, followed by a
K-Driller style piledriver, and also a fisherman buster onto the barbed wire bricks. After Dehibilitator hit a Tiger Driver onto the bricks, he signaled for his finisher, "Total Dehibilitation", taking
Smith up to the top turnbuckle, only to drive him back down with a
Ganso bomb onto the pile of barbed wire coated bricks.
After the match, as Dehibilitator got his hand raised, a man, dressed in jeans and a black T-shirt with a jean vest on top of the shirt jumped over the guardrail at ringside. We identified him as former
XHCW World Hardcore Champion "Ironman" James Loyal. Loyal spun
Dehibilitator around, and struck him right in the face with his sledgehammer. Loyal attacked Dehibilitator's knee at this point, with shot after shot with the sledgehammer. Loyal spit on Dehibilitator and left Dehibilitator lying in the ring as his theme song - Danzig's
"How The Gods Kill" blasted through the PA system.
Winner and STILL XHCW World Hardcore Champion: The Dehibilitator
Rob Ericson v. The Juice(c)
-Dueling Lead Pipe Match For XHCW World Television Title- This match got off to a quick start, as both men went after it tooth and nail with the lead pipes. The two had a sort of a duel in the ring, trying to strike one another with the pipe, which was eventually won by Ericson, who planted a boot right in Juice's abdomen. He took
Juice to the canvas with an uppercut using the pipe. Blood flew from
Juice's mouth after the shot. Ericson went on the offensive from there, taking the Champ down with a spike DDT. Ericson clubbed the back of Juice some more. He whipped Juice into the corner, following him in with a clothesline. Ericson got up top, and went for a Rocker dropper off the top turnbuckle, but Juice caught him. From there,
Juice nailed him with a slight variation of his Mexican stretch bomb, known as the "D.O.L.". Juice was not able to make the cover for quite some time, but eventually rolled onto Rob, garnering a 2 count. Juice grabbed Ericson's pipe, and clubbed Rob almost to death with both of them. He stood Rob up, and swung at Rob's neck from opposite sides.
He connected, and Rob went down like a sack of potatoes. Juice posed for the fans, who weren't pleased. Juice stood Rob up again, and went for the same maneuver, but this time, Rob blocked Juice's arms, and nailed Juice with a headbutt. He backed Juice into the corner, and went for a spear, but Juice moved out of the way. Juice hit the Juicy
Smack onto Ericson out of the corner. Juice went up top, and went for his somersault legdrop, but Ericson moved out of the way. Ericson went for his "Fuckin' Frog Splash" but Juice moved out of the way.
Both men were hesitant to get back up, because of the high-impact misses, but both men stumbled around, and actually hit back to back, when Juice spun around and nailed his reverse suplex into an Ace
Crusher known as "The Juicer" for the 3 count in this epic match.
Winner and STILL XHCW World Television Champion: The Juice "The Classic" John Sebastion v. Alec Metro(c) -Roofed Cage Match For XHCW TransAtlantic Title- Both men sized each other up, and the tension in the building was so thick you could cut it with a chainsaw. The two men locked up, and
Metro put Classic in a headlock, to which Classic sent Metro into the ropes, catching him with a clothesline. Classic uses the cage to his benefit, ramming Metro into the side of the cage. Classic takes Metro down with a stalling vertical suplex. He digs into his tights and gets out a chain. Classic pummels Metro with rights and lefts using the chain. Metro is busted wide open. Classic chokes Metro with the chain. Metro nails Classic with a mule kick. Metro grabs the chain from Classic's hand, bounces off the ropes and takes Classic down with a Lou Thesz press, going at Classic with a major assault of punches with the chain, busting Classic open. Metro caught Classic with a spinning backfist, using the chain, and the blood flowed from
Classic's mouth after that shot. Metro lifted Classic back up to his feet, whipped him into the ropes and nailed Classic with his DV & DA, and went for the pin, grabbing a 2 count. A man leaps over the barricade, and it's Scotty "The Body" Fitzpatrick! He's got some bolt cutters in his hand. Fitzpatrick cut a hole in the side of the cage and entered the ring! Fitzpatrick swung at Metro with the bolt cutters, but nailed Classic right in the face. He then took out
Metro, as he went for the pin. Jack Daniels entered the ring at this point, as well, and blew a fireball into Fitzpatrick's face, which he partially blocked with his hands. Classic is back up on his feet.
Daniels swings his liquor bottle at Classic, but Classic moved and
Daniels smashed Metro with the bottle, shattering it into thousands of pieces. Daniels looks on in disbelief as Fitzpatrick spears him, and they both go out the hole in the cage! With Classic and Metro both down in the ring, and Fitzpatrick brawling with Daniels outside the cage it is pure MAYHEM! After much anticipation, Classic gets back to his feet, and leans on a turnbuckle. Metro staggers to his feet and runs at Classic, except Classic leaps over Metro, and rolls him into a pinning combination for a 2 count. Metro reverses for a 2 count as well. Classic rolls off, and runs to the ropes. He goes for a flying forearm on Metro, but Metro catches him, and drops him back with a one-armed fallaway slam he calls the "Cannon". He gets a 2 count.
Classic staggers up, and out of nowhere goes for his ClassicBeatSlam, but Metro elbows him in the gut, and reverses him into the Metro-nic
Blast...1....2....3!
Winner and STILL XHCW TransAtlantic Champion: Alec Metro
