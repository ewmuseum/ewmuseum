Xtreme Hardcore Championship Wrestling: Assault

8 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XHCW Assault - 11.08.01 ============================= (Mike Hawk is in the ring as XHCW Assault kicks off the air. Perry
Mason by Ozzy Osbourne begins to play. The crowd at first wants to cheer because of the song...then reality hits...and the Classic begins to walk to the ring in street clothes. He stops by some zit face kid who has a sign that reads..."The Classic Rocks the Chics and the Good
Guys". The Classic looks at the sign...bends his head side ways with a look of interest in his eyes, and then proceeds to knock the kid onto the floor. The crowds' boos become louder. The Classic wipes the puss that is on his knuckles on some nearby ole granny. She throws some vulgar language the Classic's way. He shouts... "RIGHT ON! He begins to walk to the ring, and enters his. His head is still heavily bandaged.)
Mike Hawk: "Classic...I see that you have made it back to an XHCW event..." Classic: "...yep..." Mike Hawk: "Which is surprising after..." Classic: "..yep.." Mike Hawk: "Do you know that the person who did that to you may very well..." Classic: "YEP." Mike Hawk: "Can you say anything besides yep?" Classic: "Yep..." Mike Hawk: "Are you going to say anything besides..." Classic: "Yep...." Mike Hawk: "......." Classic: "...." Mike Hawk: ".....um...." (The Classic Takes his TransAtlantic belt and just nails the Hawk...) Classic: "Last week, I was assa……" (John's mic is cut off and the screen lights up. It's a man with his face blurred out. He is standing outside of the Arena somewhere. He speaks.)
The man: (Deep, and muffled.) "Classic John Sebastion, too many times
I have watched you in the past wrestle and get away with snagging a belt. Back before the XHCW closed, you snagged the XHCW World
Heavyweight title from Luger's waist. And now you have the XHCW Trans
Atlantic Title…John, did you really think I was going to let you get away with it this time…No!"
(Classic tries to speak into the mic…nothing. The man begins to speak.) The man: (Deep, Muffled and angry.) "Just like you, isn't it? You can never share the spotlight; you only speak when I ask you questions.
Now, like I was saying, I take full responsibility for attacking you and Moose. I'm so responsible I will let you watch yourself getting attacked."
(The screen fades. A promo is seen, it reads: The Attack of "Classic" John Sebastian part 1. We watch as John is walking down the hall. He is wearing the TA title over his shoulder. We also see Moose walking behind him. Moose yells. He runs toward Classic. Classic then moves out of his way and Moose is knocked out. We see Classic try to get away. He is walking toward the curtains, and then all of a sudden, a man hits him in the head with a chair. His face is blurred. We fade back outside to the Mystery Man. He speaks.)
The man: (Deep and muffled.) "Look Classic, meet me tonight, outside the Arena, you want me, you got me, but make it a street fight for the
Trans Atlantic title. Besides…T & A is my specialty."
Classic: "It's about time....I knew ya was going to show...I'm gonna whip your butt, Classic style!"
Super Skit vs. Cos Sexxy -Dueling Kendo Stick Match- The match starts off fast paced with Super Skit showing his agility, hitting a cross body block and later a reverse hurricanrana. Super
Skit goes airborne but Cos moves. Cos slows down the pace when he hits Super Skit with the Kendo stick. He performs several power moves. Cos then works on Super Skit's right leg and performs an
Indian leg lock and uses the kendo for extra pressure on the leg but the hold was broken up twice. Sexxy proceeded to throw Super Skit out of the ring and rough him up on the outside but Super Skit gained the upper hand and used his kendo stick Cos hitting him several times in the head and back leaving welts in those areas. They went into the crowd and Super Skit performed an amazing springboard clothesline using the kendo stick, off the security barrier. As Super Skit brought Cos back to the ring, Super Skit got the first pin attempt in the match with a running powerbomb but Cos kicked out. Later on Cos was able to reverse a suplex attempt into a German suplex but Super
Skit kicked out of the bridge pin. After Super Skit hit a Cos in the stomach area in the gut with the kendo stick, he then performed another clothesline with the kendo stick. He then went up top and hit the "Home Planet" for a 1…2…3. As Super Skit was celebrating, Cos rolled out of the ring and grabbed his kendo stick. He then attacked
Super Skit hitting him numerous times in the leg and head area with the stick. Cos left the ring and left a bloody Super Skit lying.
Super Skit had to be taken out on a stretcher. Winner: Super Skit The Professor v. Insane Jack v. Fear -Electrified Barbed Wire Match- Professor and Insane Jack started brawling even before Fear got to the ring. Professor swung is 2x4 at Jack like baseball bat, hitting Jack in the shoulder area. Fear then finally made his entrance with a shopping cart full of chairs. Fear threw the chairs in hitting both men. He hopped on to the turnbuckle and hit a cross body block on both men. As Fear would try to his Jack with one of his chairs, Jack would duck and sparks would fly as Fear would hit the barbed wire with his chair. Professor went to dropkick Fear but hit Insane Jack instead. Jack got sent into the ropes and his body shook as the electric shocks hit him.
Fear hit his "Final Fear" on Insane Jack and pinned him to eliminate him. As Fear got up from the pin, Professor took advantage and threw
Fear into the electrified barbed wire. Professor got a roll-up but only good for a 2 count. Professor picks up Fear and tried to give him a side Russian leg sweep but Fear blocked it and got Professor in the "New Jersey Drive". Fear pinned him 1…2…3 for the win.
Winner: Fear Pico Flush is in his dressing room. He is looking over his newly signed XHCW Contract. He is jumped from behind by Rob Ericson.
Ericson nails Pico with a lead pipe. Ericson lifts Pico over his head, and lets him drop, but on the way down, catches him into a spike
DDT! Pico is left bleeding, and his dressing room is a complete mess, with things strewn everywhere, as the camera cuts out of the shot.
"Ironman" James Loyal v. Chris Jacobson -Dog Collar Match- Loyal jumped Jacobson before the referee could even put the collar on him. He started out by lashing Jacobson with the chain, across the back. Loyal then chose to drive Jacobson down with a stunning 360
DVD. When Jacobson finally got up to his feet, he ran towards Loyal, trying to take him down with a hurricanrana, but Loyal only reversed it into a snap powerbomb. Loyal locked on the dog collar to Jacobson.
He tossed Jacobson over the top rope, hanging him by the chain! Loyal kept pulling the chain, adding pressure. Finally, he pulled
Jacobson's limp body back into the ring, and hit the Steel Driver for the pin. Jacobson had to be helped back to the dressing room.
Winner: "Ironman" James Loyal 2Short w/Axis Barley v. Bel Biggz© -Barbed Wire Board Match For XHCW World Light Heavyweight Championship- Bel Biggz started out by whipping 2Short into the corner, and shoulder ramming her numerous times. As 2Short came out of the corner, Bel grabbed her by the hair and gave her a facebuster. He then went outside to grab one of the barbed wire boards. Axis met him and kicked him in the gut, then revealed a gun, which he used the butt end to uppercut Biggz. Biggz fell into the barbed wire board. 2Short regained her composure and went to the outside, grabbing Biggz, and rolled him into the ring. 2Short grabbed one of the barbed wire boards, sliding it in, underneath the bottom rope. Biggz stomped her, then he signaled for the Bel-Trasm. He got her into the position, but she nailed him right in the kidneys and reversed it with a swinging neckbreaker onto the barbed wire board. Axis threw something into the ring…2Short's nailgun! She used her famed "Backhand Bitch Slap From
Hell", but using the chassis of the nailgun. The stood over Bel and mercilessly fired a shot right into his left shoulder. From there, it was academic, as 2Short latched on to Biggz's leg, and he tapped instantly. 2Short and Axis celebrated in the ring, as EMT's came out and tended to Biggz's shoulder.
Winner and NEW XHCW World Light Heavyweight Champion: 2Short Chris Cage v. The Juice© -Barbed Wire Ladder Match For XHCW World Television Championship- This match started off very quickly. The champion got things in his favor at first, hitting Cage with some brawling technique. Cage eventually got the crowd going, and launched himself at Juice with a dropkick, which sent Juice to the outside. Cage bounced off the ropes and nailed the Champ with a corkscrew plancha without touching the ropes. The crowd really got into this one, chanting Cage's name.
Juice got up, and went for a chairshot, which was blocked by Cage. He nailed the "Unholy Driver", an Inverted Crucifix Bomb on the chair, on the arena floor. Cage climbed the top turnbuckle, and went for the
Spin Doctor to the arena floor, but Juice was playing ‘possum' and nailed Cage with a hard chairshot on the way down. Juice threw the chair at Cage, and then blasted him with the "Steel Eater". Juice rolled Cage into the ring, and grabbed the ladder. Juice hammered
Cage over and over with the barbed wire coated ladder, bludgeoning
Cage pretty badly. Juice set Cage up for the Juicy Blaster, but Cage countered with a low blow. He dropped Juice to the mat with the "Deep
Impact", a sit out double underhook facebuster. Cage mounted the turnbuckle again, this time, for a moonsault. As he was on the top, he gave his "L" taunt. Juice came from behind and hit Cage in the back with the barbed wire ladder. Juice positioned the ladder a bit away from the turnbuckle. Juice then, did the unthinkable. He got underneath Cage, and hit him with a version of the "D.O.L." (A
Mexican Stretch Bomb) from the second turnbuckle onto the barbed wire ladder. The Second Turnbuckle Mexican Stretch Bomb took enough out of
Cage for Juice to scamper up the ladder and grab his title, retaining it.
Winner and STILL XHCW World Television Champion: The Juice Moose v. The Dehibilitator© -Bug Zapper Match For XHCW World Hardcore Championship- Moose came out, and looked a few steps behind The Dehibilitator for the entire match. Dehibilitator snagged the advantage early, taking it to Moose with a heavy barrage of brawling. Dehibilitator whipped
Moose into one of the bug zappers. He charged into the corner, and nailed Moose with a splash. Dehibilitator caught Moose on the way out of the corner with a spike DDT. Dehibilitator set Moose up for a
Spicolli Driver, but Moose rolled off his shoulders, and took
Dehibilitator down with the "Avalanche", a cobra clutch slam. Moose lifted Dehibilitator back up to his feet and took him right back down with a lifting reverse DDT. Moose ran off the ropes and went for a leg drop, but Dehibilitator moved out of the way. Moose regained his composure, and lifted Dehibilitator up into a powerbomb position, showing an awesome amount of strength. He then took about 4 steps with him, and launched him head-first into the bug zapper! Moose grandstanded to the fans, and then covered Dehibilitator, for a 2 count. Moose lifted Dehibilitator into the same position, but as
Moose started to get mobile with Dehibilitator, Dehibilitator wriggled free. Moose regrouped and went for the Gore, but Dehibilitator side-stepped, and Moose gored the bug zapper! Dehibilitator pulled the bug zapper away from the corner, and laid it on its back.
Dehibilitator gave the signal for the Total Dehibilitation. He took
Moose up top and drove him down…HARD onto the bug zapper for the pin!
Winner and STILL XHCW World Hardcore Champion: The Dehibilitator
Mystery Man v. "The Classic" John Sebastion©
-Street Fight For XHCW TransAtlantic Title- Classic goes outside the arena. He looks around, and doesn't see anyone. Classic: "Big, bad, mystery man! Show yourself! Time to get a Classic
Buttkic…." Classic is cut off in mid-sentence by a man in a black mask. He nails the Classic in the back of the head with a lead pipe. A second man in a white outfit joins the confrontation. Classic fights back, kicking the man in black in the stomach and driving him into the ground with a facebuster. He grabs the pipe and nearly takes the man in white's head off with a shot to the face. Classic laughs to himself. Classic hits the ClassicBeatSlam on the man in white onto the concrete. A blonde woman enters the shot and nails Classic with a low blow.
Classic is reeling, and walks into the man in black. He nails Classic with a reverse Michinoku driver. He covers Classic. The man in white is now wearing a referee's shirt, and counts a 3 count.
Winner and NEW XHCW TransAtlantic Champion: Mystery Man The camera zooms in on the Mystery Man. He looks right into the camera, and takes off his mask…it's…
ALEC METRO! Metro laughs maniacally as the screen fades to black.
