XHCW: Retribution

7 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Screen fades up to a shot of the exterior of the United Center, in Chicago, Illinois. It is a brisk January evening. The camera angle switches to the rear of the arena. A limousine pulls into the camera shot. The license plate reads "XHCW1." The limousine driver exits the car and goes around, opening the rear door. The camera moves so we can see slightly into the limousine, and the first thing we see is a succulent thigh belonging to Stacy Keibler. She is wearing a black dress, with a slit up the side, most of the way up the dress. She smiles a million dollar smile into the camera. She moves aside, as we see "Sadistic" Steve Liermann exit the limo next. Liermann is wearing a red turtleneck sweater with a pair of slacks. The camera moves to a shot of the two. Just then, Liermann looks back into the limousine and we hear him say the words "It's Game Time..." as he closes the door to the limo. Liermann escorts Stacy into the building. Passing the camera, the Liermann and Stacy both grin evilly at the camera. The screen then cuts to the official XHCW Retribution promo, accompanied by Linkin Park's "A Place For My Head". As the promo concludes, the camera shot cuts to the interior of the United Center. People are going nuts, hoisting many signs. Among the signs seen are "Gore Me, Moose!", "Liermann Sucks", "Pico's Gonna Get FLUSHED!", and even a well-drawn caricature of "The Classic" John Sebastion and Scotty "The Body" Fitzpatrick, reading "The Classic Breakers" underneath. The camera angle cuts to a shot of Todd Anderson, and Zack Michaels at the broadcast table. Anderson is wearing a blue suit with red tie and thin-brimmed glasses. Michaels is wearing a black sweater and a leather jacket. He has his hair gelled and looks at the camera with a face that puts a Used Car salesman to shame.) Todd Anderson: Welcome everybody, to the United Center, in Chicago, Illinois. And welcome to XHCW Retribution. We have quite a few of big matches tonight -- five titles on the line tonight.
