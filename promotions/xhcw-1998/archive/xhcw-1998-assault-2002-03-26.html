Still holding on to Scorpio, Ramsus turns around and moves his arms up towards Scorpio's back into a bearhug position. Ramsus then drives Scorpio down with bearhug slam. Ramsus falls back into the corner and catches his breath for a moment. Ramsus then climbs up and sits on top the top turnbuckle. Ramsus launches off driving a knee to Scorpio's head. Ramsus takes Scorpio off the ground and puts him between his leg. Ramsus picks up Scorpio for "The Fall of Ramsus", a running powerbomb. Ramsus runs but as he reaches the center of the ring, Scorpio pushes off landing on his feet. Scorpio grabs Ramsus and bites him in the forehead! Ramsus holds his head in pain. Scorpio grabs Ramsus by the legs and pulls him off his feet. Scorpio lifts Ramsus off the ground and begins spinning him around! Scorpio gives Ramsus the big swing, he then stops and launches Ramsus into the corner! Ramsus' head hits the steel cage as he lands in the corner. Ramsus now lays up against the corner, blood now dripping from his forehead. Scorpio comes over and lifts Ramsus up sitting him atop the turnbuckle. Out of desperation, Ramsus gouges Scorpio in the eye. Ramsus grabs Scorpio's head then bangs it up against the side of the cage. Ramsus slams Scorpio's head up against the cage once more. Afterwards, he squashes Scorpio's head up against the cage and rakes it left and right. Scorpio screams in pain as the rough, rusty chain linked fence runs up against his skin. Ramsus then bends back Scorpio's head and connects with a reverse Tornado DDT off the top rope! Ramsus looks down at the fallen Scorpio who's bleeding as well from various cuts in the face caused by the steel cage. Ramsus then looks up at the cage. Ramsus climbs up the turnbuckle then begins climbing up the cage. It seems that Ramsus just about has the win under his belt. He arrives at the top of the cage and sits there looking down at Scorpio. He shakes his head then slowly pulls himself up to his feet. He stands atop the cage, only holding on to the steel wire that brought down the cage earlier for support. He raises his arms up as a small cheer erupts in the crowd. Ramsus jumps off the top soaring through the air like the legendary Jimmy Snuka! Although Ramsus' plan is foiled as Scorpio moves out just a split second before Ramsus reaches him and eats nothing but canvas! Both men are now down, Scorpio has slightly more energy. He has the cage door in his sight but he starts crawling towards the ropes instead. As Scorpio reaches the ropes, he pulls himself up to his feet. Then looks over at the fallen Ramsus. He points up to the skies, or more specifically, the stars as the fans boo and hiss at him. Scorpio smiles and slowly makes his way over to Ramsus. Ramsus is now on all fours, trying to get up but Scorpio kicks him in the gut knocking him back down to the floor. Although Ramsus doesn't stay down long as Scorpio picks him up in a powerbomb position, the first installment of the gruesome move known as "In The Stars", but Ramsus shakes his way out of the hold and lands behind Scorpio. Ramsus hooks Scorpio's arms, and turns around lifting Scorpio upside down back to back then drops him flat on his head for the "Divine Intervention"! Ramsus throws his body up against the side of the cage, obviously fatigued from hitting the last move and he begins climbing up the cage. As he reaches the top, he takes one look down at Scorpio. He opts to not take another risk this time and begins his descent. At the same time, Scorpio is now up and begins climbing the cage trying to catch up, but before he could even reach the top, Ramsus lands flat on his feet on the floor, clinching the victory. He points into the camera, and says the win is for 2Short... Phil McKrevis: Ladies and Gentlemen, the winner of this match and STILL XHCW World Lightheavyweight Champion, Ramsus Palantiri!
