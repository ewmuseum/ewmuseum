XHCW: Assault, 13 January 2002

13 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
===================================================== XHCW Assault - Metrodome - Minneapolis, MN - 01.13.02 ===================================================== (The lights in the arena begin to fade in and out, and then go dark as the guitar sirens of "Fixxxer" by Metallica begin to blare. Strobe lights begin to flicker as two shadowed figures make their way out onto the entrance ramp and stop, the larger of the two beginning to raise his arms up. About a minute and a half in, the guitars get heavy and pyro explodes, the lights coming on in a flash as we see The Dehibilitator standing there. There is a mixture of mostly boos, with a few cheers thrown in. His long nappy blond hair is hanging down and soaking wet. He's wearing a black leather jacket, black leather pants, his trademark black snakeskin Tony Lama cowboy boots, and the same James Loyal t-shirt he wore at retribution. Invection stands in front of him, facing. She's wearing an almost sheer cocktail dress that shows off her many tattoos, a black thong & bra, and six inch silver stiletto heels. Hetfield: Oh but can you tell what father's done..make this hole in a mothers son..just when all seems fine and I'm pain free...jab another pin...jab another pin in me!.. He then steps over the top rope and begins to walk around, still soaking up the boos and few cheers from the audience. (The crowd breaks out in boos, as the Dehibilitator starts to smile. Invection hands the mic. back to him.) Dehib: Such a warm welcome from such an appreciative group of people. Dehib: You people don't like that, do you? You want someone to play to you. You want someone to play to you, you want someone that makes you feel like you're accomplishing something just by plunking down half of your welfare check to come see us. Dehib: Pavlovian...look it up, it's standard ninth grade curriculum, I know that's above the level of most of you.. (More boos.) Dehib: You can't stand the fact that I don't come out here and slap hands. That I haven't written a book for you people. You can't stand the fact that I don't have some little "catch phrase" for all of you to bark along with, to validate your meaningless lives. Over two months. You hate the fact that I have dispatched every man that has tried to face me quickly and efficiently. I've done it with brutal precision. You hate the fact that this old man...this 48 year old broken down old man has beaten every one of your little young upstart "Extreme" pretty boys. You hate me for it, because you know that none of you will ever be able to achieve anywhere near this level of greatness.. (A "Loy-al" chant begins.) Dehib: You can keep chanting that all night, he's not coming back. He's done, he's "lost his smile" if you will. You know what I say to that? He flicks it open and proceeds to light the shirt, he olds it in his hand for a few moments as it goes up in flames before tossing it into the center of the ring.) Dehib: I'll give you people another lesson tonight..that is what is known as a metaphor. That is James Loyal's career. Up in smoke, if you will. (More boos.) Dehib: James, I know you're watching this at home right now. Your fractured tibia, your shattered knee, your torn ACL. They're probably driving you nuts with pain right now. My style of wrestling is perhaps even too extreme for a group that seems to espouse "extreme" wrestling. The bad blood over the years is still there, and perhaps for that reason they see fit to exclude the XHCW from most of their activities... (He pauses, and holds up his middle finger to the camera.) Dehib: This right here, I think this signifies exactly what I, and the rest of the XHCW think about you boys in the RSPWF, the directors, the "voters" if you will. We don't need you, I don't need you... (The crowd pops for that, he smiles and puts down his hand.) Dehib: But that leaves the XHCW locker room. You boys have talent. You're young, you've got that fire, but you're missing something. That killer instinct, that desire... (He pauses.) Dehib: Balls boys, you're missing balls. Are you no better than the people that pay money to see our shows? (Some boos.) Dehib: None of you..not one of you has spoken up to challenge me. Not one of you seeks to end my reign as Hardcore champion. Not one of you wants to take out the most dangerous man in sports entertainment...no...make that Wrestling today. Prove me wrong....I'm giving you thirty seconds..someone come out that entrance...someone..The first person gets a title shot, right here, right now. (He stands facing the entrance ramp, motioning...silence.) Dehib: Come on damnit! (He motions again, still no one.) Dehib: Will one of you boys grow a set? (Still no one.) Dehib: That's it..I give up on you. No title shot, nothing, none of you boys are willing to step up to the plate...No one in this promotion is in the same league as the Dehibilitator..that's fine...I... (The crowd begins to pop as the beginning of an unfamiliar tune play throughout the arena. The song is "Midnight Rider" by the Allman Brothers Band. Cage freed himself, and grabbed Borders off the ladder in a powerbomb position. Borders reverses the powerbomb attempt with a hurricanrana of his own. Borders rolls Cage onto one of the two tables set up in the ring. He takes him right back down with the "Border Line" through the table, garnering a HUGE pop, and a "Bor-Ders" chant from the crowd. He leaps off with a moonsault, locking Borders up on the way down, connecting with his Moonsault DDT he calls the "Devil's Dance". He draws the three count.
