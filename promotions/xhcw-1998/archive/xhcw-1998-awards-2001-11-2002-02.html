XHCW AWARDS for Nov. 2001 - Feb. 2002 The awards were voted upon by the members of XHCW. The voting structure was as follows: 1st place = 5 pts. 2nd place = 3 pts. 3rd place = 1 pt. Each person is shown with the total number of points they amassed. Thanks to all who voted, and if you don't like what you see and DIDN'T vote...That would be your problem.
