Got a Tag Team? Wanna take then to the hottest new fed hittin' the planet? Or.. maybe you're a singles wrestler who wants to take on some of our talent: Jack Vars James Pillow The Masked Superstar Jobber Kenneth Morlock The Illusive Dumbass That's a small sampling of the roster we have gathered after just _TWO_ days of recruiting. Just take a peek below, before ya click that X up top, okay? Read over the handbook.. give NDW a shot!

- [WTF is NDW?] - Well, it stands for National Domination Wrestling. NDW is a fantasy wrestling league. If you don't know what that is, I advise you go find out before reading any further. NDW will hopefuly be a fun, and sucessful experience. It will consist of two cards per week, pending wrestler participation.
Pay-Per-View events will be held Bi-Monthly, the first of which will happen one and a half months after the NDW's first card. NDW is a "Net Fed," which means there are _NO_ colors, fonts, bolds, underline etc type of stuff used in
Roleplays, Cards, or Updates. ORIGINAL Wrestlers only! No Steve Austin's, Hogans, or anytghing that looks like them! - [Staff - Fictional] - President - Nelson Montana Commissioner - "The Hammer" Alex Cote Head of Security - John Kroll - [Commentators] - Dustin Dage/Face James Royal/Heel Sarah Fire/Neutral Ring Announcer - Mr. Jaxon Head Referee - Charles Kauffman - [Staff - Real] - Owner - [name withheld] Vice President - [name withheld] - [Writers] - [name withheld] [name withheld] [name withheld] HIRING HIRING - [Senior Bookers] - [name withheld] [name withheld] - [Junior Booker] - HIRING - [Rules] - I don't want to sit here all day and give you rules. It's pretty simple.. know where the line is.. and don't cross it. There are a few things that I do _NOT
EVER_ want to see: [1] Putting down a handler - You may bash his wrestler all you want, but as soon as you go into reality, you've left fantasy.. and the NDW. You will be removed immediatly if it is serious enough.
[2] Racism, Degrading of Hertiage etc etc. There will be no use of slang words used toward a person of certain color, shape, size, gender and so on and so forth.
Another big thing here in NDW will be Roleplays. The deciding of a match is held mainly by RPs. Though, a match may be decided by an angle, or strat.. if the person who submits the strat/angle does not roleplay, then there is no way they're going to win.. no matter how crafty it may have sounded. I am not here to make myself look good, nor the NDW.. I am here to make NDW fun for _YOU_!
Not Roleplaying for a card will result in your not getting a match for the next card. If you do not roleplay by the following card, you will be suspended until we find out what the heck is going on. I understand you may have personal problems.. or that ungodly sickness: A life. So, if that is the case.. TRY to let us know what's going on.
- [Titles] - National Heavyweight Championship - If you wanna be the NDW Champ, you gotta work your ass off. This title is for ONLY the best. It will be defended at least once a month, and at all PPVs. Atlantic Heavyweight Championship - This is the equivalent to a United States, or Intercontinental Championship. It is given to wrestlers with big pushes, but not quite the biggest. Defended twice a month, and at most PPVs.
National Tag Team Championship - I think you can figgure it out. The best twosome of the NDW holds these straps. Defended twice a month, and at every PPV. Hardcore Championship - The craziest, most extreme wrestler will wear this one. No holds barred. No Count out. No DQ. Also, the champion may choose ANY type of gimmick match (ladder, cage, etc) for his title matches. Defended three times a month, and at most PPVs.
- [Cards] - Monday Maddness - Each and every Monday from an arena somewhere in the United States or Canada. LIVE on the JAM Network at 8:00pm. This is NDW's big show. Titles on the line.. angles unfolding... don't miss it! Thursday Tours - The name of the Thursday card will change after every PPV. The name will resemble the upcoming PPV, or some angle or something to that effect. The first one will be called the "Crossin' the Nation Tour,"
resembling the start of the league.. get it? - [Pay-Per-View] - Held Bi-Monthly, the BIG SHOWS in the league. This is where most titles will change, angles will start/end and fueds will burn high. The names will be decided upon when the date for the first one is chosen.
- [Getting over in NDW] - [1] ROLEPLAY! 'Nuff said. [2] Strats - Pick the right people to fued with, and let us know. Sneak attacks, or match distractions are always nice.
[3] In-Rings. Counts as a Roleplay.. done in the ring, at a card. Can ya believe that?! [4] Promos. Commercials etc are good too. Think of creative ways to get your wrestler over.
