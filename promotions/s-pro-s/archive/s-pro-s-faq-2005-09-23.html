Application 1. ** OWNER'S INTRODUCTION ** Greetings! This is Gordon Adair, inventor and founder of SUPER PRO-WRESTLING STARS, also known as S-PRO-S, or simply S-PRO for short. Some may recognize me or may have even worked with me at some point over the last 10 years. Although I've been in and out for the last few years, my record as a fed head is actually fairly extensive. You may remember me as the handler of the wrestler Flaming Bob (Bob Muretic), or fed head of the UWA (1996-1998), NWWA (1998-2001), and USCW (2003). I got my start in e-wrestling back in 1996 when it was at its climax in popularity. My first fed, the UWA, opened that same year. Despite the fact that I didn't know what I was doing, it was a huge success. The UWA handlers were very forgiving of the mistakes I made, allowing me to learn from them as I went along through the years. After that fed had run its course, I closed it and opened the NWWA in September of 1998. Like its predecessor, the NWWA was a huge success. For two years it was the most enjoyable e-fedding experience I'd ever had, but due to college I had to close it as well. After two unsuccessful attempts to revive it, I opened up USCW in January of 2003 with [name withheld]. USCW started out as the greatest fed I've ever run, but I soon found myself without a co-prez after Nick dropped out. [name withheld] took over for him temporarily, but soon dropped out as well. Even though USCW was gaining popularity, it was losing ground as most of my match writers informed me that they would be unable to write for much longer. Unable to bear the work load myself, I had to close USCW down for good. Now, due to the success of my home business, I find myself with more free time to jump back into this hobby. For the past few years I have been mentally conceiving an idea for a new fed. But not just a new e-fed... a new TYPE of e-fed. S-PRO-S is not going to be just another one of those feds where 16 guys get together and have a big tournament for a belt. I'm daring to be different here...to offer a new e-wrestling experience the likes of which you've probably never seen before. S-PRO-S will be a storyline-driven fed. Rather than existing solely on the shoulders of handlers to invent their own angles, in S-PRO-S a storyline will be in place before the first card ever airs. That storyline will grow, evolve, and change depending on the actions of the handlers. Every handler and every wrestler will be instantly submerged in the story. The possibilities are nearly endless. In addition, handlers will be encouraged to do what every other e-fed expects...which is to contact one another and work together on your own angles and feuds. The major ongoing S-PRO-S storyline will be in addition to your ideas, not instead of. 2. ** S-PRO-S STORYLINE ** In early 2005, retired wrestling promoter Gordon Adair was approached by former wrestling, comic book, and B-movie star, Captain Freedom. After having fallen out of the public eye in the mid 90's to be replaced by more "cutting edge" heroes, Captain Freedom had a grand plan to once again catapult himself into the living rooms of families all over the nation. His brainchild...Heroes of Wrestling, a 1-hour Saturday morning television program aimed toward pre-adolescents. Its operator and main star would of course be...CAPTAIN FREEDOM! Content with being retired from the wrestling world and enjoying the fruits of his labor, Gordon Adair had little desire to get back into the sport...but he saw this as his opportunity to cash in one more time. With the star power that Captain Freedom had to offer, Adair knew that signing his name next to the masked superhero's would be a sure money-maker. So with Adair's financial backing and network connections, Captain Freedom was given the green light to begin work on his show Heroes of Wrestling under Adair's banner of SUPER PRO-WRESTLING STARS. Adair's usual lack of diligence in this matter left him completely unaware of what Captain Freedom would soon be unleashing on cable airwaves across the nation. This would not just be another wrestling show for kids. Captain Freedom's goal for his 1-hour Heroes of Wrestling show was for it to be a program that would help instill a sense of patriotism and right-wing morality in children not fortunate enough to have parents to teach them these necessary values. Freedom's hope was to showcase the never-ending battle between good and evil. To illustrate this, good would be represented by the faction named "Captain Freedom & His Amazing Friends". To coincide with Freedom's own political and moral ideology, good would be represented by American wrestlers, or at very least citizens of a nation allied with America (except France). They would also represent a conservative political stance, affirming their unwavering support for President George W. Bush. Evil would be represented by another faction, named appropriately as "The Mindless Evil Horde". It would be up to Captain Freedom to make the distinction between good and evil. After all... Heroes of Wrestling is his show! As a result, the Mindless Evil Horde would contain everyone who Freedom deemed inelligible for membership in His Amazing Friends. Those finding themselves in the Mindless Evil Horde would be wrestlers who were drug users, liberals, or mixed races, as well as wrestlers who had two mommies or two daddies, wrestlers with "effeminate" long hair, and wrestlers not supportive of the war in Iraq. By booking himself against has-been, never-was, and never-will-be heels, Captain Freedom hopes to once again establish himself as a hero and a household name in American homes by accumulating many victories for himself and His Amazing Friends over the disorganized Mindless Evil Horde. What Captain Freedom does not realize however, is that he is just about to open Pandora's box. Heroes are only as great as the villains that they've fought, and the herosim of Captain Freedom & His Amazing Friends is about to be put to the test. There is a man who will make sure of this. 3. ** MISSION STATEMENT ** S-PRO-S is all about having fun. As far as I'm concerned, there are no big guys or little guys in the e-wrestling world. This game is all about character development, good writing, and most importantly, fun! Everyone who competes in S-PRO-S will be given an opportunity to succeed. I believe in giving pushes to handlers based on the quality of their work, the effort that they put forward, and their overall attitude. I believe that being a good handler constitutes more than writing good roleplays. Handlers will be held partially responsible for coming up with the material and ideas for their storylines and feuds. I'd much rather you come to me with your ideas than to come to me and say "what are your plans for me?" I will also be very active in the planning and implementing of storylines. My main duty will be to work these angles into entertaining cards and shows and create one great final product. S-PRO-S is a gimmick federation. It is not your typical "let's get 16 guys and have a big tournament to decide who will be the champion" type deal. This fed begins with a storyline in place which will have a definite impact on every wrestler that enters the fed. How the storyline evolves will be greatly dependent on the actions of the handlers and the in-character reactions to the storyline on cards and in roleplays. In S-PRO-S I will put great emphasis on creative storylines that are hyped and played out over time. Notice that I use the word "creative". Creative storylines consist of more than "me no like you, me fight you". This is a text based wrestling federation, and to keep it interesting I believe we need to use our brains and be creative. If you have the talent, the desire, and the personal character that SUPER PRO-WRESTLING STARS strives for, then you will have a blast here. If you want to dictate your terms and lay back on a reputation, you will be jobbed. 4. ** HANDLERS ** a. PREMISE S-PRO-S, by design, will be an e-wrestling federation unlike anything you've participated in before. It will be a definite alternative to the oversexed "Attitude" promotions that have become dominant since the 90's. The S-PRO-S product will be built on the precepts of great wrestling and long-running, well-planned storylines and feuds. If you were to ask me what real promotion that SUPER PRO-WRESTLING STARS would most resemble, I would say the WWF/NWA of the 80's and early 90's. Because I want to create a product similar to the forementioned, I will be putting certain elements into play that reflect wrestling during those times. Interviews. In modern "sports entertainment", in-ring interviews are commonplace and usually quite lengthy. But in the 1980's in-ring interviews were rare and reserved for very special occasions. This same element will be put into play in HoW. In-rings will not be permitted without permission, and then only under special circumstances. Rather than in-rings, I strongly suggest writing short backstage interviews for your wrestler prior to their match. Angles/Feuds. What are these? In modern "sports entertainment", these are used to build interest in and sell a monthly pay per view. They are usually short and rather unmemorable unless they are in some way shocking or controversial. In 1980's wrestling angles were longer, better planned, and much more memorable whether they were good or bad. Here in HoW, I want to build angles and feuds that are entertaining and memorable without having to rush them in order to set up a PPV main event. Supercards will be held once every few months to allow for months of exciting, well-planned buildup. b. ROLEPLAYING - FLASHES A flash/promo is the most necessary form of roleplaying that you can do in the S-PRO-S. If you've been in e-wrestling for very long, you know what this is. The best flashes come from unique settings that will enhance your character. Roleplays are graded based upon different criteria. I believe in quality over quantity, but this does not justify writing up a 2K flash in three minutes and expecting to win something. I also look at the time spent on a roleplay. If I'm comparing a mediocre 10K flash and a mediocre 4K flash, I'm most likely going to give the win to the person that wrote 10K. At the same time, if you write 20K of rambling just to impress the booker, I may very well give the win to your opponent who wrote a high quality 6K flash. Be original. Use humor or drama and a supporting cast to enhance things. How about content? It is aired on a cable television station, and anything that is not allowed on cable TV is not allowed here. In addition to this, there are certain things that I personally do not want to see as they will detract from the roleplaying environment that we are trying to create. 1. Swearing is allowed but must be used sparingly. In addition, you cannot use any words that you would not hear on TV. Also, I don't want to see any attempts to mask such words by bleeping or substituting a vowel for a *, i.e. sh*t, f*ck, or g*ddammit. I also don't want to see a lot of <bleep>. If you can't think of a way to write without needing to swear, then we don't need or want you in S-PRO-S. 2. Anything surpassing mild sexuality is prohibited. I don't want S-PRO-S to be dominated by sex, like the rest of the professional wrestling world. If you're horny go look up some porn. If you feel that an e-fed without kissing lesbians, nudity, and sex is out of touch...go join one that allows these things. S-PRO-S does not. In short, I am not impressed with flashes where wrestlers feel it necessary to refer to another wrestler's penis. It's not shocking. It's not funny. And it's overdone. If you feel that you cannot express yourself without commenting on another wrestler's penis or the use of it, please find another e-fed and try HoW again once you've graduated the 4th grade. I am very strict when it comes to having flashes sent in on time. A deadline will be posted every week. Sometimes I won't get to sending out the flash packet until it's a little late. So I not going to toss out a roleplay just because it's past the deadline. But basically, if I send out the flash packet before I get your flash, then you're out of luck. This will most likely result in you losing the match. Punctuality is important! c. ROLEPLAYING - STRATS In S-PRO-S, I urge you to write strats, but I don't require them. As the booker, I'm going to assume that everyone wants to win their match, and that they want to win it using the moves and the finisher that they put on their application. So there's no need to send a detailed strat telling me and the writers what moves you want to do in what order and whether or not you are going to look happy when you win. If someone sends a 3-page detailed strat with 10 or 12 spots to use, I may just tell them to write the entire match. Please keep the strats simple and to the point. But remember that if you want your wrestler to do something besides wrestle his common match and exit when it's finished, such as a post-match attack, interference, foreign object, or special strategy, you must send me your plan in the form of a strat. As far as ring entrances go, please do NOT send me the lyrics to an entire song and call it your ring entrance. I would actually prefer a more descriptive ring entrance with an instrumental for music. You can send lyrics, but if your entrance music lyrics take up 2 screens, I'm going to delete about 70% of them. Who actually reads all those lyrics anyway? In your strats just let me know if there's anything special that you want to do before, during, or after the match such as attack an opponent or eat a twinkie while pinning your opponent. Strats also cover anything you might want to do during the card that has no bearing on your match. If you have a backstage interview to do during the card, put it in your strat. If you want to go destroy a hot dog stand at some random point in the card, include it in your strat. d. ANGLE CREATION Your success in S-PRO-S is going to depend greatly on angle creation and storylines. It is through angles that memorable rivalries and storylines will be built which will lead to exciting matches. I would like handlers to contact one another and work together on angles so that they might put one another over. I will also work personally with handlers and feed them ideas or help them build on their own. e. KEY TO SUCCESS In order to be successful in S-PRO-S, you must put in the time to perform the weekly duties such as writing your flashes and strats. These things are required. It is also STRONGLY suggested that you get to know fellow handlers through e-mail or chatting. The best way to build feuds and angles is through direct collaboration with others involved in these feuds and angles. This type of collaboration also builds friendships and unity. f. Heroes of Wrestling Heroes of Wrestling is a weekly 1-hour Saturday morning program, featuring 4-5 matches. This is where most of the action in HoW will take place. Storylines will be forwarded toward their pinnacle at HoW supercards or pay-per-views. b. Verbal Warfare Verbal Warfare is a weekly flash packet that will be sent out every Wednesday. Check updates for due dates. c. Supercards S-PRO-S will likely hold up to 6 supercards per year. They will be aired in a 2 hour format on prime time cable television. Although there are no plans at this time for PPV's, the same emphasis will be placed on supercards, making them the biggest cards of the year featuring all the biggest title defenses and grudge matches. 6. ** PERSONALITIES ** a. Owner/President i. Gordon Adair. Gordon Adair is the owner and President of SUPER PRO-WRESTLING STARS. Although Adair prefers not to be active in the production of S-PW-S's television programs, Captain Freedom required Adair's assistance in launching his Heroes of Wrestling program. Why? Because frankly, Captain Freedom doesn't know what the hell he's doing. Adair originally thought this new project would mean easy cash for little effort, but has found himself being pulled more and more into the management of the project as time goes by. b. General Manager i. Captain Freedom. Captain Freedom is a former comic book, wrestling, and b-movie star who hit the big time back in the 1990's. He is a registered Republican and a strong supporter of President George W. Bush. Captain Freedom is described by fans as a true American patriot. By detractors, he's labelled as a delusional madman. In fact, he is a straight caucasian man with a strong sense of morality and right-wing ideology. As the founder and head official of Heroes of Wrestling, he is convinced that America needs a new hero in the battle between Good and Evil. Captain Freedom & His Amazing friends are out to give them what they need. c. Broadcasters i. Brian Calypso. In Captain Freedom's quest to find an announcer who knew the good from the bad and the ugly, his search came to an end at Brian Calypso. Calypso, one of the greatest good guy wrestlers of all time, suffered career ending injuries at the hands of the monstrous Dao in 1998. That did not hurt his attitude toward humanity or his work ethic one bit, as Brian Calypso is always ready to serve his country and Captain Freedom! ii. Jean Allen Coe. Jean Allen Coe is a bald Englishman with a little black moustache who is in his mid to late 50's. Coe was a top broadcast journalist in Europe, but left his continent to persue work in America where wrestling is much more popular and mainstream. Coe has a rather haughty and sarcastic attitude, which tends to cloud the idea that he is supposed to be one of the good guys. iii. Chris "Air" Fair". Although Fair has always endeavored to become a commentator, thus far his dream has eluded him. Fair got his first break in this position in 1998 when he took over for ailing commentator Dan Crawford in the UWA. Most fans found Fair to be extremely annoying as he oggled every woman and didn't seem to know the names of any wrestling moves at all. He was the first to call the moonsault a "flying gradunza" and the STF a "pig in a blanket". Fair has attempted to take on gimmicks and alter his personality in ways that would endear him more to promoters. Since the closure of USCW, Chris Fair has been working under the name "Mike Stand" in many independents. Now at the beckon call of Gordon Adair, Fair is back to work in HoE. No one knows for sure why his nickname is "Air". d. Ring Announcers i. Kate Younger. Kate is the neice of Captain Freedom, and the voice that introduces the stars of HoW in their approach to the ring. Kate once worked as a radio disc jockey in her home town, but came to fill a much needed vacancy in her uncle's promotion. Kate is 5'7" with black hair and looks good in tight clothes. 7. ** TITLE INFORMATION ** a. S-PRO-S Allied Nations Singles Title The wrestler who holds this title will be recognized by S-PRo-S as the greatest wrestler in the world. He will be preferably, according to Captain Freedom an American or at least a member of an allied country. Captain Freedom has appointed himself the first Allied Nations Heavyweight Champion, and hopes to hold on to the belt until he retires or hands it down to a worthy successor. b. S-PRO-S Allied Nations Tag Team Titles While tag team wrestling has been a joke over the past 10 years, S-PRO-S hopes to put the division back on the map. These two prestigious straps will mark their wearers as the greatest tag team in S-PRO-S. c. International Heavyweight Title The International Title is S-PRO-S's secondary title. Captain Freedom has taken special care to remind everyone that it is not as valuable as the Allied Nations Title by ordering it made half the size. Although the International title may not be as visually impressive, it can be argued that it is perhaps more valuable as Captain Freedom is not screening challengers for it, as he does with the Allied Nations title. d. High Impact Title The High Impact title is actually a Cruiserweight/Middleweight title that is fought over between those wrestlers who are a bit quicker on their feet and able to deal with the pressures of having to defend it on a weekly basis. To hold onto the High Impact title requires great stamina and endurance. To take it away requires an intense offense, as all matches for this title have a 10-minute time limit. 8. ** APPLICATION ** I require this application to be filled out.
