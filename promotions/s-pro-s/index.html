A storyline-driven federation built around a satirical patriotic superhero character's own television program, aired within a wider promotion staged by a returning e-wrestling operator. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

S-PRO-S's own handbook and F.A.Q., posted twice on 23 September 2005 from its own founder's two accounts, introduces itself as a storyline-driven promotion built around a plot already in place before its first card: a retired wrestling promoter, approached by a former wrestling-and-B-movie star calling himself Captain Freedom, bankrolls Freedom's own children's television program, Heroes of Wrestling. The document's own founder, a ten-year e-wrestling veteran who had previously run three earlier promotions, describes S-PRO-S as an attempt at a fed whose ongoing plot would grow from every handler's own actions rather than sit as background color.

The Heroes of Wrestling premise itself, as the F.A.Q. lays it out, staged a heavily one-sided culture-war satire: Captain Freedom sorted the roster into his own “Amazing Friends” and a rival “Mindless Evil Horde” along explicitly right-wing political and discriminatory lines, casting himself as the sole judge of which side any given wrestler belonged to.

Four championships are named in the same document — the Allied Nations Singles and Tag Team Titles, the International Title and the High Impact Title — though no result or reign for any of them survives.

The only other document under this name is a one-line reader reply three days later carrying no content of its own; whether Heroes of Wrestling, or S-PRO-S itself, ever aired a card isn't in the surviving record.
