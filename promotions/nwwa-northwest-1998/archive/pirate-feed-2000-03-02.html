He takes a deep breath and starts to speak, with a low,slow tone.) The Polish Crippler: Never thought it would come to this at last, but oh how the times always change.........
all I did was look upon this what is called the NWWA... and it all began to worry me greatly........why.... why..........you ask......... (Crippler keeps laughing) Voice: ......THIS IS THE END OF EVERYTHING.......... (the voice fades out as the word "EVERYTHING" echos thorugh the room. Crippler starts to talk)
The Polish Crippler: And the beast has spoken and the beast has said his final words and now I shall.........hahahahahahaa.....
I shall take on the form of the beast....... ......a beast that stops for nothing and cares for nothing...... .....I was involved before in many things........but like it has been spoken..........like it has been said before..........
...this is the end of everything............................the heaven has fallen and the the angels are about to be send forth........
.......remember Saint'em............this is the end of everything.......... (the scene fades to blood red quickly as the flames get higher) -=-=- 2. V8 -=-=- [{Camera shows the front of the NWWA North American Title. It then fades out and shows a battered V8. His nose is taped, and his shoulder
has a wrap with ice on it. Even though he has a broken nose and a hurt shoulder he is smiling.}] V8: What a way to come back. Winning the NWWA NA title. Man, it's a rush. The match for the title was all messed up though. Hell, I can't complain I won! Now that bragging is out of my system, I just want to say OutKast...Hell of a match man.
[{V8's smile fades from his face and he becomes serious.}] V8: I'm sure you all heard about what happened to me last week. I was attacked by gang members...More like my house was shot to hell. Well man, if any of you are watching this, I just want to let you know you all are nothin but a bunch of <bleep>. If you want me dead why don't ya come and do it yourself instead of hiding behind a gun? My god, even if your are going to come shoot up my house and try to kill me, don't miss...IT'S NOT THAT HARD! Enough about that though, you <bleep> aren't even worth the my breath.
V8: Next week I fight some dude whose name I don't know. Name isn't important really. Just show up. You have a better chance then OutKast did in defeatin' me, just because of some...Black Ninja. Can you take advantage of it though? OR are you going to let this chance slip through your fingers? If you don't come wanting to fight then get the hell out of the NWWA. This is a place where everything counts. No matter if your a rookie or a veteran.
[{V8's watch starts to beep and he looks down at it.}] V8: <bleep>, I gots to go, time for physical therapy. Stay tuned, same time, same channel, same place.
[{Camera zooms in to the belt again and then fades to black.}] -=-=- 3. Grudge -=-=- [The scene opens in a Seattle gym on west 154th st. Grudge is impressing the place with his uncanny strength and is gathering quite a crowd. The sweat is mingling with his face paint and threatening to expose the natural skin. Pushing her way through the crowd is NWWA star reporter, Jennifer Olson.]
Jen Olson: Excuse me. Grudge? May I have a word with you? Grudge: Why? You're going to say some lies about me, aren't you? Jen Olson: Not at all. In fact, I'm hear to get to the truth of some lies I think you've been telling.
[Grudge rises from his bench and throws on a shirt to cover his massively tattooed and incredibly chisled torso. The crowd dispurses and he walks with Olson out of the Gym.]
Grudge: Is that any way to talk to the hottest prospect the NWWA has ever seen? Jen Olson: There's no doubt that you made an impressive debut, but let's face it, you were going against two nobodies in the form of Mike
Branda and Diego Valesco. Lethal Slam is a two time tag champion and on a higher level. Grudge: Lethal Slam is a joke. He's shamed our community and forced me to pick up where he left off.
Jen Olson: Yes, let's talk about that. You claim to come from the same background as Lethal Slam. You claim you grew up together.
Grudge: That's right. Jen Olson: Yet you're from Seattle, Washington where Lethal Slam is from North Carolina. Grudge: I'm not originally from this area. I only recently made my home here when I began wrestling independently in the U.S. and in
Western Canada. Jen Olson: So you're saying you are from North Carolina like Lethal Slam. Grudge: Of course, I remember Ralleigh as if I were there yesterday. Jen Olson: Lethal lived closer to Charlotte. Grudge: Oh, that's what I meant. Slip of the tongue. Of course, I remember Charlotte as if I were there yesterday.
Jen Olson: Lethal Slam is from Fallston. Grudge: Yes, yes...Fallston, which is....which is just outside of Charlotte. Why I remember driving North into Charllote every time that.... Jen Olson: You'd have to drive SouthEast to get to Charllote. Grudge: Yes, I knew that....I'm just really bad with directions. Not my strong suit. Jen Olson: I don't think you've even been to North Carolina let alone been from the same place as Lethal Slam.
Grudge: OF COURSE I HAVE YOU LITTLE TART!! Jen Olson: Excuse me? Grudge: I mean...well it's just that I haven't been there in a while and I get my directions mixed up. But I am from there and everyone's dissappointed in Lethal Slam. Really.
Jen Olson: I've also heard that Mike Branda and Diego Valesco have never heard of you despite your claims that you have.
Grudge: Past federations, of course...we have a history. Of course they're going to deny it because I kicked their asses back then just like I did this Monday. I'm telling you. I'm telling the truth.
Jen Olson: I'm sorry, Grudge. Your stories just don't add up. Grudge: I KNEW IT!! I KNEW IT!! I KNEW YOU WERE OUT TO MAKE GRUDGE LOOK BAD WITH YOUR LIES!!! [He inches closer to Olson as she moves back.] Grudge: YOU'RE AGAINST ME. JUST LIKE LETHAL SLAM IS. JUST LIKE BRANDA AND VALESCO WERE. YOU'RE ALL AGAINST ME!!! Olson: Cut the tape. I'm outta here. [Olson suprisingly gets out of dodge real quick, but the cameraman is not so lucky. The scene cuts to static just after a slight shot of
Grudge grabbing the camera.] -=-=- 4. Bootleg DA Killa -=-=- [ Scene takes us outside the NWWA arena, Olympia Arena, Olympia Washington. Bootleg is beeing loaded in the ambulance, Bootleg startes to fade....] Man: Where LOSING HIM.... HIS PULSE IS DROPING... Man2 : GIVE US SOME HELP OVER HERE!!!!!! [ Bootleg is having a fit and his heart pulse is dropping.] Women: HOLD HIM.. I"M INJECTING IODINE IN HIM TO STOP FITTING! Man: HOLD HIM DOWN! [ Paramedics hold Bootleg as the iodine is beeing injected in his arm.] Man: OK.. PULSE IS BACK.. Lets load him on properly. [ Bootleg starts to revive and vomits some blood. The paramedics work on Bootleg and Bootleg becomes stable but all beaten to a bloody pulp. He can barely move or speak and can not hardly keep his eye's open.
Ambulance drives off and the scene fades to black.] -=-=- 5. Shotgun Lee -=-=- (Scene opens with Shotgun Lee on a Radio Talk Show somewhere in the Northeastern part of Nebraska. Some town called Wayne. He's wearing his normal attire, black jeans, cowboy boots, except he's wearing a
White T-Shirt. The DJ is sitting across from him, his on-air name is Red.) Red: Thank you for coming out today to meet with me Shotgun. I know it's a long flight from Texas.
Shotgun Lee: My pleasure Red. (Shotgun Lee is over twice the size of Red, probably even over 3 times his size.) Red: What inspired you to get into wrestling? Shotgun Lee: Well, I figured that I had the talent, and when I was doing Bull Riding, I felt I could do more. I'm still doing some Bull
Riding though. Red: Which would you say is harder on you to do? Shotgun Lee: What do you mean, which is more difficult? Red: Yeah. Shotgun Lee: Well, I'd say sometimes it's a toss up. Being Hardcore Champ and all, I have to use and beat up my body more in order to win. I still got beat up in Bull Riding, but it wasn't almost every week. Red: Do you think you will ever retire from Wrestling and just do Bull Riding? Shotgun Lee: Well, if I retire at all, it's going to be both. I consider myself someone who has to keep themselves busy.
Red: You have an upcoming match against a Black Ninja? Shotgun Lee: Yeah, never really heard much of the guy. I do have a little Martial Arts background though, so I think he won't be too touch to handle.
Red: You dealt pretty easily with that Sanchez fella, did you even break a sweat during that match?
Shotgun Lee: Well, I did more sweating leaving the ring than while I was in it. Red: You plan on dominating your upcoming opponent as well? Shotgun Lee: Well, I don't know what to expect from this kid. I'm gonna go in their and bust my ass to kick his ass, and that's about all
I can do. I do feel that my chances are pretty good though. Red: How does it feel not having your partner Colt there anymore? Shotgun Lee: Well, it's different. I really loved working with the guy in the ring. I'm still talking to him regularly. He backs me up
100%. Red: Here's my final question as we are running out of time Shotgun. You've said you're after even more gold. What do you mean by more gold? Shotgun Lee: Plain and simple, I'm after the big belt. You know and I know that the belt is going to be mine. I've worked to damn hard to not get a chance at it. Just wait and see, pretty soon I'll be standing in the middle of that ring, holding the World Title. And there's nothing anyone else can do about it.
Red: Thank you very much for your time Shotgun, it was a pleasure having you on the show. Shotgun Lee: The pleasure's all mine Red. Red: We're going to take a break, and then get back into more music, here on KWSC-FM Wayne, K92.
-=-=- 6. The Black Ninja -=-=- [Deep within a dusty Japanese field lies a set of hangers. Inside one of these hangers are two men negotiating. One is wearing a Hawaiian shirt and has slicked back black hair and a five o'clock shadow, the other is a masked figure now known only as The Black Ninja.]
The Black Ninja: I cannot afford such a price Slick. Slick: Then I can't take you! If you want to go to North America, or anywhere for that matter, you need cash.
The Black Ninja: But this trip affects the destiny of so many people. You must believe me. Slick: Look bub, my destiny is to collect enough money to retire in the Caribbean but you don't see me bothering everyone. The Black Ninja: But you don't understand..... Slick: No, you don't understand. If you want to fly to North America you'll need to pay me $5000 in advance.
[The Black Ninja stands still for a moment, he is obviously immersed in though.] The Black Ninja: Then I must return to the temple.... Master Mayamoto will be most displeased.
Slick: Wait a second.... did you say Master Mayamoto? The Black Ninja: Yes I did. Slick: THE Master Mayamoto??? The Black Ninja: Yes THE Master Mayamoto. He has sent me on a great journey to the West to complete my destiny. He must of been relying on the kindness and loyalty of his fellow countrymen but....
Slick: I'LL TAKE YOU!!! The Black Ninja: Are you sure? Slick: Yes, absolutely! The Black Ninja: But I haven't any money. Slick: This flight is on me. The Black Ninja: Hehehe. Thank you! You will not regret it! [Slick and The Black Ninja move towards a row of planes. They all seem quite worthy of an overseas flight. The Black Ninja points towards the biggest and best looking plane in the hanger.]
The Black Ninja: Will we be using that plane? Slick: Um...... no. [They walk down further to a medium sized, neat looking plane.] The Black Ninja: What about that one? Slick: Ummmmmmm...... No. [Finally they reach the end of the line where an old, rickety Cessna sits in the shadows.] Slick: Here! Ain't she a beauty? The Black Ninja: Ahhhhhh, ummmmm, is this plane safe? Slick: Of course! [Just as Slick makes this remark he leans against the passenger side door.... which falls of in his hands.]
The Black Ninja: What the??? Slick: EASILY FIXED. Nothing to worry about. The Black Ninja: I hope so..... [Slick reaches into the back compartment of the plane and removes a tool kit. He begins to converse once more as he screws the door back on.]
Slick: So..... you haven't got ANY money on you? The Black Ninja: No, none at all. Slick: Well if you plan to survive this trip you're gonna need a constant income. The Black Ninja: I'm sure I can work something out once I get there. Slick: It ain't that easy kid..... besides, I could get some work lined up for..... that's if you're interested of course.
The Black Ninja: What kind of work? Slick: Let's just say that you will get a chance to show off your.... talents. [Before The Black Ninja can respond Slick finishes screwing the door back on the passenger's side of the plane. He then places the tool kit back in the plane's back compartment.]
Slick: Ready to go? The Black Ninja: Ready as ever. Slick: Great! Now hop in. [Both men get into the plane and sit down. Slick starts the Cessna up and steers it down the runway.]
Slick: Hold onto your haaaaaaaaaats!!! [The plane sputters off into the open blue sky before the scene fades to black.] -=-=- Great job tonight guys, and I'll be sending out the next packet approxamately the same time tommorow!
