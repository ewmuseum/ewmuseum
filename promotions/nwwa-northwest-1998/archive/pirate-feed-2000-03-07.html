-=-=- NWWA Monday Night Madness 3-13-99 Key Arena, Seattle Washington -=-=- Rio D. Giniro vs. Mark Adams Zed-Funk(c) vs. Grudge -NWWA Canadian Title Match Ken Riggs vs. "Diamond" Joe Sanchez Shotgun Lee(c) vs. Saint'an vs. Black Ninja -NWWA US Hardcore Title match! -NO DQ, NO COUNTOUT V8(c) vs. "Trendsetter" Mike Branda -NWWA North American Title match Bootleg DA Killa vs. PAC -NWWA World Title Match -At WrestleRage 2, Winner will face the winner of a two day 16 man tournament held at the March 27th edition of Madness. -=0=- Tonights Show Includes: 1. The Black Ninja 2. Grudge -=-=- 1.Grudge -=-=- [Scene opens outside of the huge North-West Wrestling Association Head
Quarters in Portland, Oregon. The building towers over the surrounding establishments and casts a rather large shadow. On the street outside a large white limo pulls up followed by a black cadillac which does the same. The limo door opens and out steps Scott Swindell who is wearing a respectable looking suit while from the cadillac comes The Black Ninja accompanied by the shady character
Slick, who is wearing an ugly brown suit. The three men meet together at the entrance of NWWA HQ.]
Slick: This building is huge! Scott Swindell: Just one of the rewards of being the World leader in sports entertainment. Slick: I'll bet..... Scott Swindell: So gentlemen, shall we? [Swindell motions his arms towards the doorway inviting his guests into the building, which they happily oblige to. Once inside Swindell leads them down a huge hallway that has many rooms on either side and pictures of many past and present NWWA celebrities on the walls.]
Slick: See that guy in the picture Ninja? The Black Ninja: Yes. Slick: That's Gerald Sothington, one of the most powerful men to be involved with any sports organisation.
The Black Ninja: Will we be talking to him today? Scott Swindell: No, he passed the Presidency on to his son Kevin. That's who you'll be meeting today. [Slick looks at some more pictures in the hall and picks out another
NWWA celebrity.] Slick: Cool, Johnny Rotten! What's he up to at the moment? Scott Swindell: Johnny's getting some well deserved R & R until we decide what role he will play here in the NWWA.
[Just as Swindell turns around to continue walking down the hall James
Frost walks out of his office and bumps into him.]
Scott Swindell: Whoa! Sorry Jimmy. James Frost: No need to apologise. Slick: Y....Y.....Y.... You're James Frost! James Frost: Indeed I am. Slick: W.... W.....W.... Wow.... James Frost: Your friend seems a bit tongue-tied Scotty. Who might these gentlemen be? Scott Swindell: Well the rather speechless gentleman is Slick and this young man behind him is a major prospect for the NWWA, The Black Ninja.
James Frost: Sounds like someone to watch out for. Scott Swindell: Well if all goes to plan today it shouldn't be too long before you're calling one of his matches.
James Frost: Well good luck Ninja. [Frost shakes The Black Ninja's hand and walks down the hall.] Slick: Bye Mr. Frost! I love your work! James Frost: Thank you!..... *whispers* ... so do I, hehehe. [The three men finally reach the end of the hall where Kevin Sothington's office is.] Scott Swindell: Here we are guys.... ready to talk some biz with the boss? Slick: We sure are! [When Slick says this The Black Ninja grabs his arm.] The Black Ninja: No! Slick: What do you mean "No"? The Black Ninja: I need to go in the office on my own. Slick: What? Don't you trust me? The Black Ninja: It's not that Slick, it's just that....... Slick: What? Spit it out! The Black Ninja: You're too concerned about money. Slick: Money's very important. The Black Ninja: For you maybe, but that's not the purpose of my journey. I'm thankful just for the opportunity..... you've got to let me handle things my way.
Slick: Alright then man.... good luck. [Slick extends his hand and The Black Ninja shakes it before following
Scott Swindell into Kevin Sothington's office. Slick takes a seat outside and talks to a receptionist.]
Slick: Hey. Receptionist: Hello sir. Slick: What's it like working for the NWWA? Receptionist: It's different... to say the least. Slick: Really? Why's that? Receptionist: Because of all the zany characters and strange events that go on here. Slick: Oh.... [Slick can hear mumbling from Sothington's office. He tries to think of something to say to the receptionist.]
Slick: Why is Kevin Sothington's office on the bottom floor of HQ and not the top? Receptionist: Mr. Sothington isn't too keen on heights sir, so he prefers a view that's closer to the ground.
Slick: What's upstairs then? Receptionist: There are many facilities for visitors and celebrities. Restaurants, hotel rooms, an NWWA museum..... the list goes on. Slick: Ok, I'll have to check it out some time. [As Slick says this the voices from within the office become louder and
Swindell and The Black Ninja come walking through the door.]
The Black Ninja: Thank you Mr. Sothington, you won't regret your choice. Scott Swindell: Congratulations! Welcome to the NWWA! Slick: Alright! [Slick gives Ninja a high five and hugs Swindell.] Scott Swindell: Easy....easy....... Slick: Sorry.... got a bit carried away there. Scott Swindell: Not to worry...... Black Ninja has a big chance now, he has to use it wisely.
The Black Ninja: I will...... I will. [Scene fades to black.] ---------------------------------------------------------------------- [Next scene begins near Slick's black cadillac.] The Black Ninja: Where do we go now Slick? Slick: You go back in there to your hotel room. I'm heading back to
Japan. The Black Ninja: You're leaving? Slick: You've got your job.... I've got mine. I really have to go back. The Black Ninja: I understand...... [Ninja then reaches into his pocket and pulls out a large amount of cash. He hands it to Slick.]
Slick: What's this? The Black Ninja: It's yours. Slick: Where did you get it from? There must be more than seven thousand dollars here! The Black Ninja: It's a combination of the pit-fighting money and my signing bonus. Slick: You didn't have to...... The Black Ninja: I did. You put your faith and trust in me and now you shall share the rewards.
Slick: Thanks man.... [Slick gives Ninja a hug and goes over to open the door of the cadillac.] The Black Ninja: See Slick, good things come to those who wait. Slick: Hehehe, see you man.... take care of yourself. [Scene fades to black.] -=-=- 2. Grudge -=-=- [The streets of Seattle are dark and eerily vacant. The occasional car can be heard splashing a rain puddle in the distance. The rain itself is little more than a drizzle this evening but the surrounding wetness of the area suggests that a tempest has been through recently. The camera quivers back and forth as it gets out of the car and focuses on a man-hole cover nearby. The lid is off and there is a light inside the entrance to the sewer system. When the camera gets within six feet of the hole, it stops and stabalizes. Anarchy Love gets into the picture with a microphone in hand.]
Anarchy: Ok, on me in five...four...three... [pause] Anarchy: I'm here on the streets of Seattle in search of a newcomer in the NWWA who has been brilliant in the ring and, quite frankly, less than honest outside of the ring. In fact, I'm here attempting to get an interview with Grudge because Jennifer Olson refuses to do another interview with the man she describes as a pathalogical liar and a sociopath.
[Suddenly there is clanking and grumbling sounds and Anarchy Love looks down the hole.] Anarchy: I think we're in luck. [Grudge appears from the hole with his face in its usual red and black make-up with white tear below his left eye. He only shows his head above ground and Love kneels to get closer to the wrestler.]
Anarchy: Grudge, this is Anarchy Love for the NWWA. Why have you seen fit to spend any time at all in the sewers of Seattle?
Grudge: Have you seen any recent interviews of Zed-Funk? Anarchy: Ugh...yes. Please don't remind me. I have no idea why the
NWWA showed him eating his own feces.
Grudge: He's vermin, Anarchy. Pure vermin. Their kind do that sort of thing. That's why I'm in the sewer now. Not to emulate him. But to fight off his own kind. Vermin. With the millions of rats down here living off their own feces, I have a small (excuse the expression)
taste of what it's going to be like in the ring with Zed-Funk. I must fend off the vermin down here. I must fight off the filth and grime and fight my own instincts to spew in the face of such vile, pungent odors. Only then will I be able to combat Zed-Funk in the ring and become the new Canadian Champion.
Anarchy: And how are you doing? Grudge: Terrible!! It's gross down here. Let me out!!! [Anarchy steps back as Grudge pushes his way out of the hole and breathes deeply the fresh night air.]
Grudge: Christ, it stinks down there. I think I'm gonna puke. Anarchy: Easy there, Grudge. What happened to facing the vermin and the bile in order to beat Zed-Funk?
Grudge: Yuck, I couldn't take it. Anarchy: And you don't smell too good either. Grudge: Well, imagine what Zed-Funk's breath smells like! I mean the man eats his own shit!! What the hell is that?
Anarchy: I'm sure that this information you're telling America is new to them. Grudge: What do you mean? Didn't they see him do it on during his NWWA interview? Anarchy: Well, chances are, no. You see, we are finding out that most
NWWA viewers do not watch The Outlaw's interviews. That's the time when America usually goes to the refridgerator or the bathroom. We find that very little of our fans are interested in what the Outlaws have to say or do.
Grudge: Well, that's understandable. Anarchy: So, I suppose that you're not going to tell us of some past history you have with Zed-Funk.
Grudge: Nope, never met him. Anarchy: But with your past opponents, you claimed some form of history when in fact there was none. You claimed Mike Branda tried to have you banned from the NWWA. You claimed that Diego Valesco came into the NWWA with you as a team only to leave you by the curb. You claimed to be from the same town as Lethal Slam. Each one of those instances were contrived as a way to bring heat to the match up. Now, you're not claiming any such history with Zed-Funk?
Grudge: Are you kidding? The guy eats turds!!! What the hell am I supposed to say? I don't know him. Never did. And if there wasn't a belt involved, I wouldn't want to. Do you know where I can buy a gas mask?
Anarchy: No, I don't. Grudge: Well, enough about the human catfish. Let's talk about something else. Anarchy: Yes, let's talk about your finishers as of late. You won your debut match using the Atomic Splitter which belonged to former champion,
Sean Malcomb. Then you used Da Outkast's Phat Cadillac against Lethal
Slam. Why have you been doing this?
[Grudge begins walking down the street and Anarchy and the cameraman follow. He is removing parts of his clothing in an effort to relieve himself of the grime.]
Grudge: Well, there's two reasons. First, it's sort of a tribute to the legends of NWWA wrestling. The former greats. I used to watch this show all the time when I was with Calgary Stampede Wrestling. I used to love watching some of those great moves. The second reason is that they're affective. I can win matches with them, so I'm going to use them. Besides, everyone knows that I taught Da Outkast The Phat
Cadillac. Anarchy: What? You're now claiming credit for that move? Grudge: Of course. One day, Da Outkast came up to me and said, "Grudge? What say you give me a finishing move to get over down here in NWWA?" So I invented the Phat Cadillac and let him use it.
Only, I called it the Slim Pinto, but you get the idea. Anarchy: Now I know why Jen refused to do this interview. You really expect me to believe that he got that move from you?
Grudge: Oh yeah...we be tight home boys down in the hood. Anarchy: Oh, please. Grudge: We be kickin' it. Anarchy: Stop it, you sound silly. Grudge: BOIIIIIII. Anarchy: Stop, already!! Grudge: Ok, ok. Besides, I don't want to talk about that. I want to talk about something I heard from the Polish Crippler.
Anarchy: What about him? [They stop at a gas station where Grudge uses the free radiator water to begin hosing himself down.]
Grudge: Well, he was saying last week how he didn't like the new guys in NWWA and how he was upset with the lack of veterans. Well, with the exception of the Polish Crippler himself, I don't see any veterans getting in the damn ring. I mean, we've been on the air two weeks right now and the first ever champion and some guy who hits himself on the head with a chair don't want to get in a wrestling match.
Anarchy: You're talking about my brother, Rebel Love and... Grudge: (interrupting) Hey, no offense, but your brother is hiding from the new talent in the NWWA. He and the other guy have gotten involved in both main events so far. We have YET to have a main event in the NWWA. That can't be good for ratings. It's easy to double team someone with a chair or whatever, but it's hard to get in the ring one on one with an opponent and come out on top.
Anarchy: You happen to be talking about two wrestlers who have already proven their worth by...
Grudge: (again interrupting) So you're saying they have nothing left to prove? If that's true, then they should retire and leave the main events alone. But since they're here, they're fair game. They must be here for a reason, but it's damn for sure not to compete in the ring.
I look at the lineup for week #3 of the NWWA's comeback and they're not on it. After that is another highlight show and I doubt they'll be there. At least not wrestling. But they're probably going to show up for each main event in the next two weeks and turn the fans off with their bullshit. The fans won't have anymore of it and will start flipping the channel to the NAWC.
Anarchy: You're not supposed to mention them in an interview. [He puts down the hose and the two continue walking.] Grudge: Well, if they had any balls, they'd fight an opponent one-on- one in the ring. Anarchy: You know, there is the 16-man tournament coming in three weeks. The winner gets to fight for the championship at the pay per view.
Grudge: Yeah, and hopefully the old farts will haul their wrinkled asses in the ring and stop living off of past glory. And since I plan on winning that contest, I may have to go up against one or both of them.
[Grudge walks to a pay phone and picks up the receiver and dials 1-800-COLLECT.] Grudge: I'm assuming that you're not going to give me a ride home. Anarchy: Sniff...uh...not likely. Grudge: Then let's wrap up the interview so that I can call for a ride. Anarchy: I'm all done for today. Good luck on your future in the NWWA.
