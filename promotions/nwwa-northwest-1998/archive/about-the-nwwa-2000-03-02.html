NorthWest Wrestling Association http://northwwa.tripod.com The NorthWest Wrestling Association is an independent e-mail based federation based in the Northwest United States and Canada, particularly in Oregon, Washington and Idaho, as well as the provinces of British Columbia and Alberta. The NWWA runs one card a week titled by the weeknight's name followed by "Night Madness". Alternating weekly with Madness is the highlights card, NWWA Sunday HighLights.
The NWWA opened it's doors to wrestlers on July 27th, 1998 by current executive committee member "Stunning" Scott Swindell. In early December,
Swindell sold the NWWA to Gerald Sothington, a wealthy business tycoon.
The NorthWest Wrestling Association is an e-wrestling federation accepting only original wrestlers, and uses a closed flash system.
Think you have what it takes to be the best of the Northwest? Join the
NWWA today! In March 1999, The NWWA went through yet another front office change when due to personal stress, Gerald Sothington stepped down as president and appointed former NWWA World Champion and Legend Rebel
Love as president. Since this time, the NWWA has heavily recruited and become one of the fastest growing federations in wrestling history, if it was not already considered this before.
Now back under Sothington administration, The NWWA has been going strong for almost a year and a half and it's not showing any signs of letting up anytime soon.
