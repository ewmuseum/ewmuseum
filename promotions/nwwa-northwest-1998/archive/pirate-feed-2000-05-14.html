NWWA Monday Night Madness May 15, 2000 Rose Garden Arena, Portland Oregon -=-=- Debut Match "Dark Angel" Rick Stevens vs. Lethal Slam Debut Match Erik "The Future" Webber vs. Tommy Shamrock No Ropes Triple Threat US Hardcore Title Match Ace Brimshore vs. Jimmy Hinh vs. Saint'an Marvelous Mario vs. Maverick US Hardcore Title Contenders V8 vs. Shotgun Lee Canadian Title Match Main Event Black Ninja vs. WiggeyD -=0=- In Tonights Show : 1. Tommy Shamrock 2. Jimmy Hinh 3. Habib and Roy 4. The Black Ninja -=-=- 1. Tommy Shamrock -=-=- (Scene fades in with Tommy Shamrock sitting inside his hotel room walking towards a ringing phone. Shamrock sits down on the bed and turns the TV to ESPN. He then answers the phone.)
Tommy Shamrock - Hello? Voice - Meet me at the train station in three hours. You got that? Tommy Shamrock - Why and who are you? Your voice is all scrambled. Voice - You'll find out when we meet. Good bye. Tommy Shamrock - Bye. (Tommy Shamrock hangs the phone up and watches the TV. Shamrock notices the series between the Jazz and Blazers.)
Tommy Shamrock - What!?! The Blazers are up two games to none! But, the
Jazz have home court advantage! This is screwed up.
(Tommy Shamrock picks the phone up and dials two numbers.) Tommy Shamrock - Room service? Voice - Yes. How may we help you? Tommy Shamrock - Umm... Can I have more pillows delivered to me? Voice - How many? Tommy Shamrock - Two, please. Voice - Okay, we'll send someone up. (Tommy Shamrock hangs the phone up and sits back down. He turns the TV off and awaits the room service.)
Tommy Shamrock - Come on! (Tommy Shamrock hears a knock at his door and opens it up. A small maid wearing clothing too small walks in with two pillows. She fluffs them and lays them down.)
Tommy Shamrock - Thanks. I have a really bad back. Maid - Oh, a bad back. Tommy Shamrock - Yeah. Maid - Do you want me to fix it? I can massage it. Tommy Shamrock - Well, umm... (Before Tommy can finish, the maid hops on the bed and begins rubbing
Tommy's back. Tommy jumps up.)
Tommy Shamrock - I'll be fine and anyway, I am married. You see this ring? It says that I am in legal bond to someone.
Maid - Okay, sorry. (The maid leaves the room as Tommy Shamrock gets in the shower to get ready for his meeting.)
------------------------------------------------------------------------ (Scene opens with Tommy Shamrock getting in his car and driving off to the train station. Shamrock arrives at the station after about 15 minutes of driving through the city. Shamrock leaves the car looks for the person.)
Tommy Shamrock - Now I have to find them. (Suddenly, we hear gun shots as we see Shamrock and other people hit the ground. Shamrock crawls to his car and drives off towards his hotel...)
------------------------------------------------------------------------ (Tommy enters his room and sees the words, "You will die!" in black spray paint on the walls.)
Tommy Shamrock - Okay, I'm calling Kevin. This is freaking me out. (Tommy picks up the phone and calls NWWA HQ.) Tommy Shamrock - Can I speak with Kevin Sothington, please? It's an emergency. Other End - He's in a meeting... What do you need? Tommy Shamrock - Can you pull him out? I am kinda freaked out right now... Other End - I'll see. Who is this? Tommy Shamrock - Tommy Shamrock, one of his wrestlers. Other End - One moment. (Tommy listens to some "On Hold" music. Suddenly a gun shot is fired. It crashes through the window and hits the bed beside Tommy. Tommy hits the floor hiding from plain view.)
Tommy Shamrock - Damn. Hurry up... Kevin Sothington - Tommy? What's wrong? Tommy Shamrock - Yeah, Kevin? I don't like this town. Kevin Sothington - What's wrong with it? Tommy Shamrock - Well, first I get a mysterious phone call. Then I get shot at. Then I see the words 'You Will Die' written on my window. Then
I get shot at, again.
Kevin Sothington - Really? Tommy Shamrock - He just shot his gun at before I got a hold of you. Kevin Sothington - Tommy, get out of the hotel and catch a flight to the HQ. I'll meet you when you get here. Just make it safe!
Tommy Shamrock - Thanks, Kevin. (Tommy Shamrock hangs up the phone and sneaks out before the gunner can see him. Shamrock gets in his car. Scene fades to black with Shamrock driving away.)
-=-=- 2. Jimmy Hinh -=-= Scene is in what seems to be a waiting room. Lots of "business" type people are walking around and typing. Basically doing work. Judging by the various photographs of NWWA superstars from the past and present on the wall, the place is obviously NWWA headquarters.
Sitting in a seat minding his own business is Jimmy Hinh. He sits alone in the waiting room quietly, ignoring the people who walk past him.
Because this is NWWA headquarters, the people who work here have had many opportunities to see Hinh so they don't seem to be taking much notice of him.
He glances at his watch, then looks around for something to kill his boredom while he waits for Sothington to call him into the room. In the corner of his eye, he sees a copy of NWWA Madness magazine. Just as he picks it up and opens it, a voice calls him...
Secretary:- Mr. Hinh... Mr. Sothington is ready to see you now. (Hinh puts the magazine down and follows the secretary to a door which reads SOTHINGTON. The secretary knocks on the door)
Sothington:- (From inside) Come in. (The secretary opens the door for Hinh and goes back to her work as Hinh slowly enters the room. There are lots of NWWA memorabilia in the room, as well as photos of such NWWA greats as Rebel Love, Max Bradley, Mark
Marleau, Sean Malcomb and Frank Peregrine. Sitting back in a comfortable chair is Kevin Sothington. He gives Hinh a serious look and directs him to a chair)
Sothington:- Please, take a seat. (Hinh sits down in the chair in front of Sothington's desk. Then, silence.) Sothington:- (After a pause) Well? Hinh:- Well what? Sothington:- What have you got to say for yourself? You KNEW that interview you did was gonna anger Malcomb. Do you think you're tough challenging a man who is injured?
Hinh:- What do you mean? I thought he got over all that bullshit about being shot. Sothington:- Jimmy... I suggest you get all your facts straight before you say anything. Malcomb wants to fight you, but due to his condition he will not be able to.
(Hinh has a look on his face as if to say "I can't believe this!" A smirk crosses his face)
Hinh:- Is that what he told you? Sothington:- No. He thinks he's ready. But THESE state otherwise. (Sothington reaches into a drawer and pulls out an envolope. He throws them at Hinh, who slowly opens them and reads them. They are medical reports)
Hinh:- Uh... Are you sure I should be seeing these? Sothington:- Well, if I just told you he was injured, would you believe me? (Pause. By now, all Hinh's cockiness is gone) Hinh:- *musjfokgf* Sothington:- I beg your pardon? Hinh:- I said "Probably not." Sothington:- Come on... Does challenging someone who CANNOT answer your challenge make you feel big or something?
Hinh:- No. It's just that... Sothington:- What? Hinh:- I guess with this wrestling thing, we all grow egos. Come on, you've seen him backstage. He just can't keep his damn mouth shut about himself. I just figured that if he likes to talk so much then he wouldn't mind stepping in the ring to back it up.
Sothington:- Well... I hope you know your requested match won't happen. (pause) Hinh:- Fine. (Sothington is shocked with Hinh. He was expecting some protest, but
Hinh is surprisingly being co-operative)
Sothington:- Did you just say "fine?" Hinh:- Yeah. (Pause) Sothington:- I must say that I am pleasantly surprised. I was expecting you to argue about how he weasled his way out of a match.
Hinh:- Oh, don't get me wrong. For now, I will NOT be making fun of
Malcomb or his little skank of a friend. Because I know that if you DID sign the match, and when I kicked his ass all over the ring, he'll chuck a big orgasm about how he was injured. Nah, I'll wait. That way he's got no excuse.
Sothington:- You DO realize there is a chance you might lose and he'll make you eat those words, don't you?
Hinh:- ..... Sothington:- Do you? Hinh:- ...yes. But that's a price I'm willing to pay. Sothington:- (He shakes his head) I hope it's worth it son. (Pause. There is not much more these two men have to say to each other.) Sothington:- Well, that's all I wanted to talk to you about. Any other things you wanna discuss with me while I'm here?
Hinh:- No. I'll see you later. (With that, Hinh stands up and exits the young owner's office and walks down the corridor. As he walks, he spots a door with the words
COMMISSIONER written on it. He contemplates walking over and knocking on the door... But doesn't. Instead, he simply shakes his head and leaves the building. Fade to black)
-=-=- 3. Habib and Roy -=-=- [The scene begins in a crusty apartment in Calgary where dirty socks are strewn across pieces of furniture like the television and some sort of chest. Half empty beer cans litter the tables and rugs. There is a large yellow stain on the rug which neither Habib nor Roy are willing to talk about. The two cab drivers are sitting on what appears to be a couch. Habib is sitting cross-legged with his feet resting on his knees and is wearing a particularly high, red turbin today. Roy is sitting on the edge of the couch wearing white boxer shorts with red hearts and a formerly white t-shirt stained to a greasy brownish yellow. They are both watching the telivision anxiously.]
Roy: GOD DAMNIT!!! No mention of us!! Why aint't we mentioned!?? We're supposed ta be fightin' for da tag team gold dis week. Habib: Perhaps we are being getting a hosing from The Falcon and the
Jimmy Hinh. Roy: I tink you's right. Dey's tryin' ta hose us. Well, it ain't gonna woik. [Roy gets up from the couch and when he does, a cloud of potato chip pieces scatters across the room, vaulted from his lap. He goes to the kitchen phone and dials a rather long number.]
Peregrine: Hello? Roy: What da fuck, bird brain? Where's our tag team title shot? Peregrine: You're going to get it. Roy: Well den why ain't we on da Pirate Feed? Dey announced all da matches for next week and we ain't on it.
Peregrine: You're not? What about me and Jimmy? Roy: Jesus, don't you know your own friggin' schedule? You're not on it either. [Habib gets up from the couch and moves closer to Roy.] Habib: Do not let us be receiving the proverbial dick in the mouth from the playboy bastard. Do not let him push you around.
[Roy turns to Habib and covers the phone with his hand.] Roy: I won't Habib, and try not to talk like me wit' da swearin'. It don't sound right comin' from you. Just be yourself.
Habib: I can be saying the swear words if I be wishing it, God be damning it!! Roy: Ok, ok. [Roy turns his attention back to the phone.] Peregrine: Listen, I don't know what's going on. I thought Sothington was going to allow the match to take place. Why don't you talk to him about it?
Roy: No fuckin' way!! YOU talk ta him about it and you get us dat title match. We had a deal...you left the belts in Habib's cab and we signed dose contracts before given dem back to youse. You're gonna honnor dem contracts.
Peregrine: The contracts are with the NWWA. You made a deal with THEM. Roy: Fine...den we's just gonna tell all of Calgary dat the Frank
Peregrine and Jimmy Hinh are afraid to fight a couple of cabbies.
Peregrine: Hahaha....are you serious? Afraid to fight you? That's funny. Come on....this whole thing is like something from Candid
Camera. Habib: He is being laughing at us, is he not, Roy? I can be hearing him laughing at us. Roy: Yeah, he's laughin' at us, alright. Habib: Please to be giving me the phone, please. [Habib snatches the phone from Roy's hands and immediatley yells into the receiver with a high pitched voice.]
Habib: Listen here you falcon shit for brains. You will be giving us the tag team title match or you will be doing a dishonorable thing that will be follwing your career until you will be winding up in the gutter choking on your own excrament.
Peregrine:..... Habib: Are you still being there? Peregrine: BWAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!! Roy: He's still laughin' at us, ain't he? Habib: I am being afraid so. Peregrine: Listen. I'll find out what the deal is with Sothington....why he hasn't booked the match. You'll get your tag title match. [Peregrine tries to surpress his laughter but a few short burst of compressed air get past his lips.]
Habib: Really? You will be doing this for us? This week? Peregrine: I don't know about this week. Maybe they just pushed it back a week. I'll find out.
Habib: Very well then. Be having a good day. Peregrine: Yeah, bye. *click* Habib: I am believing that our dreams will be coming true very shortly. Roy: Yeah, da tag team champeens of da woild. I can almost smell it. Habib: I believe that you are currently smelling the pepperoni slice lodged in the hairs of your chest.
[Roy looks down and notices a pepperoni slice above the v-neck line of his t-shirt nestled inside his chest hair. He carefully plucks it out and pops it in his mouth.]
Roy: Hey!! Dis still tastes fresh even though it was from yesterday!!! Habib: I am sure to be writing home to India about it. [Scene ends.] -=-=- 4. The Black Ninja -=-=- (Scene opens back on the plane that The Black Ninja has caught to Japan. It's a few hours later and almost time for arrival yet he is still asleep. Then the pilot comes on the PA system with his smooth, reassuring voice.)
Pilot: Ahh.... ladies and gentlemen.... ahhh.... this is your Captain speaking. We are almost at our destination.....ahhhh.... please remain seated until instructed otherwise.... thank you.
(The pilot's announcement was quite short, but it was enough to wake The
Black Ninja from his sleep. He wakes up, yawns and turns his head to the huge woman next to him.)
Large Woman: ..... three sausages..... half a chicken..... ice cream..... chocolate mousse.... and that's it. Oh wait! I got hungry on the way to the airport so I had four hot dogs.
(Ninja looks at the woman half in amazement, half in shock. He can't believe that she had been answering his question the WHOLE flight, even while he was asleep.)
Large Woman: So, what did YOU have for lunch? (Just as the large woman asks this question, the plane starts to rumble and shake from side to side causing everyone to jerk around in their seats.)
Large Woman: WE'RE ALL GONNA DIE!!!!!!! The Black Ninja: Do not worry, I am sure it's just..... Large Woman: AHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!! AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!! (The large woman starts to go into hysterics as Ninja tries to calm her down. The plane rumbles once again, making matters worse.)
The Black Ninja: There is nothing to worry...... Large Woman: WE'RE GONNA LAND IN THE OCEAN!!!!!! The Black Ninja: .... about. Large Woman: THEY'LL NEVER FIND OUR ROTTING BODIES BEFORE THE WORMS DO!!!!!! (The Black Ninja can do nothing but slap his hand across his forehead. Luckily, just as he runs out of ideas, the pilot comes back onto the PA system with his ever so soothing voice.)
Pilot: Ladies and gentlemen we..... ahh..... are experiencing some turbulence.... ahhhh... and we can assure you it's nothing to worry about.
The Black Ninja: See miss. There is nothing to worry..... Pilot: HOLD ON!!!! I'VE LOST CONTROL!!!! WE'RE ALLLLL GONNA DIE!!!!!! (When this shocking news is heard, everyone on the plane starts yelling and screaming for their lives. The large woman is literally ripping her hair our, but Ninja doesn't seem to care anymore. The pilot then comes back on to the PA.)
Pilot: Hehe, I've always wanted to do that! We're almost there people! (The pilot dismisses his gag with ease by turning off the PA system. Everyone mumbles for a moment and then settles back down..... except for one woman. Yes, it's the large woman..... AGAIN. Now she's totally lost it. She has rolled herself up in a ball in her seat and she is sweating profusely.)
The Black Ninja: Miss? (Ninja shakes her arm, but gets no response. It was as if this woman was in an everlasting daydream. Ninja gives her arm another shake, but gets no response. Ninja has no choice but to call a flight attendant over to help.)
The Black Ninja: Excuse me..... I need some help. (When The Black Ninja calls, a brown haired flight attendant approaches him. She starts to speak to him in a friendly voice.)
Attendant: Yes sir? The Black Ninja: Do you have anything to help this woman out? Attendant: Well usually we'd give her a tranquilliser like valium..... The Black Ninja: Get her.... Attendant: .... but the crew just finished those off. The Black Ninja: Oh. Attendant: I could probably get a drink or something to eat. The Black Ninja: NO! I mean... I think she's had enough for one day.... would you mind if I went and checked out the back for something? Attendant: Well..... The Black Ninja: This woman really needs help. Attendant: .... ok...... but don't be long. (Ninja gets up out of his seat and walks behind the curtain where the flight crew stay. There and many cupboards and drawers around, and he checks them all..... nothing. He then checks the fridge.... nothing.
Then in the corner he spots a small cardboard box. There's a whole bunch of junk inside of it, which he quickly digs through. He reaches down to the bottom and pulls out a cassette tape. He then looks at the label which reads "Phil and I". He takes the tape over to the stereo that the flight attendants use to give out instructions on the plane.
He puts the tape in and presses play. At first there is a bit of static but then it clears up and two voices can be heard. One is the brown haired flight attendant's the other one is the pilot's.)
Pilot: Yeah...... ahhhhh...... yeah...... ahhhhhh.... yeah baby..... ohhhhh..... ahhhhhh Attendant: Ohhhhh yeeeaaaaahhhhhh! Pilot: You can control my gearstick any time baby.... ahhhh.... yeah..... ahhhhh (The Black Ninja doesn't know it, but the tape is playing through the whole plane. The flight attendant blushes and looks downwards before walking back behind the curtain. When she goes back there she quickly turns the tape off and throws Ninja out. As Ninja makes his way back to his seat, he notices that the large woman has snapped out of her trance.)
The Black Ninja: Glad to see you are alright miss. Large Woman: Would you like to join the mile high club too? (The large woman smiles at Ninja as he grimaces and the scene closes.) Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/ Before you buy.
