(This is a sample of the NWWA RP show, emailed out nightly. If you like what you see, come visit us at
http://northwwa.tripod.com ) Deadlines are as follows - - Roleplay Deadline is Friday at 5:00 PM CST (Normally it'd be midnight, but I have to leave town for the weeked) - Strat Deadline is TOMMOROW at Midnight CST -=-=- NWWA Monday Night Madness 2-26-99 Olympia Arena in Olympia, Washington. -=-=- - Saint'an vs. Lethal Slam Hardcore US Title Contendership Match Turnbuckles are on fire! - Thug Immortals vs. The Outlaws - Da Outkast vs. V8 NWWA North American Title Match - Shotgun Lee vs. "Diamond" Joe Sanchez Non-Title Hardcore Match - Steel Cage - Diego Velesco vs. "Trendsetter" Mike Branda - C Murder vs "The Supreme Superstar" Scott Roberts NWWA Canadian Contendership Match - Bootleg Da Killa vs Yawner NWWA World Title Match -=0=- Tonights Show Includes: 1. Grudge 2. Rebel Love 3. The Outlaws 4. Rio D. Giniro -=0=- -=-=- 1. Grudge -=-=- {As the darkness is eaten away by a single candle flame dancing of its own accord, a slender shadow creeps into the scene. The mere candlelight cannot show the face clearly, but the white tear stands out in the semi-darkness. Grudge kneels down and looks coldly into the camera and sighs.}
Grudge: There was a time when I trusted you, Mike Branda. You were a friend to me. We were as a close as brothers. I would have given you the shirt off my back. Until you decided to stab me in the back. You tried to take my NWWA debut match away from me. The card still doesn't have my name in it. But it won't work, Branda. I WILL be there at
Monday Night Madness and there will be a three way dance between us.
All the manipulating you've done...all the ass kissing to the higher ups...all your politics won't work.
You've spent a lot of time and energy trying to keep me out of the
NWWA. It was time ill-spent.
{The scene fades back and the image of Grudge and the flame freeze entirely. Jennifer Olson steps into the picture as if she were in a studio watching a tape of Grudge and filming over it.} Jen Olson: What you see here is a very mentally disturbed individual. I have confirmed with many sources that Mike Branda has no prior knowledge of the individual known as Grudge. Yet here he is making claims of a past history. Claiming that he knew Blanda and that they were close friends. I have found out that not only is this not true but that Grudge's accusations that Branda has tried to keep him out of the league are unfounded. We see more of this pattern as this interview continues.
{The scene fades back in and Olson is removed from the shot. We see only Grudge continuing his rants.}
Grudge: And Diego Velesco. How many times have I beaten you in other leagues? 5? 6? Too many to count. We came to the NWWA together, you and I, to make a difference. We were supposed to debut together. Yet who got the match at Soul Survivor? Who got the singles match with
Mike Branda? And who got screwed out of such opportunities.
Opportunities that I covet you take for granted. Matches that I cherish you squandor. And despite all this, you, just like Branda, have wanted me out of the NWWA picture. You asked the owner and the commissioner to get rid of me. You're both against me. Well, that's fine with me. You both do your worse at Monday Night Madness. I'll be there and I will take out my frustrations on both of you.
{He slowly rises and stands with a menacing posture.} Grudge: Branda...Velesco...I have a bone to pick with you. {The scene freezes again as Jen Olson steps into the picture.} Jen Olson: Once again he refers to a wrestler and once again that wrestler has no past recolection of him. Grudge insists that he's beaten Velesco on several occassions in other leagues yet the Diego assures us that they have never even met before. Grudge's complaints about Velesco and Branda approaching the NWWA offices are untrue. I personally have confirmed this with the owner and the commissioner.
Either Grudge is completely insane, a compulsive liar, or else behind all that make-up, pierced flesh and tattoos, Grudge has met these wrestlers in the past. For NWWA News, I'm Jennifer Olson.
{fade to black.} -=-=- 2. Rebel Love -=-=- [The scene opens with the camera entering through the gate at the end of
Rebel Love's driveway. As the camera enters the confines of the Love property Rebel Love's million dollar mansion can clearly be seen. The grass in the front yard is green; due to the Southern location, and well taken care of. The camera moves up the driveway and proceeds up the steps to the front deck of the mansion, a hand reaches out from behind the lens and knocks loudly on the door.]
*Rap, rap, rap* [The door opens and a good looking, young black woman stands in the entranceway. She greets the cameraman...]
Maid: Come in, Mr. Love is expecting you. [The camera moves inside and the lush interior of the mansion is seen in it's full splendor. The cameraman follows the maid to a room, just before entering she steps aside and allows the cameraman to enter.
Rebel Love and Southern Belle are sitting together on a love seat accross the room from the entrance. Rebel nods to the camera and speaks...]
Rebel Love: Go ahead and get set-up, this is where we'll do the interview. [Soon the camera is ready and a door bell tune is heard throughout the million dollar mansion.]
Rebel Love: Typical Olson. Here in the nick of time. [Jenn Olson, the long time NWWA head interviewer, is shown into the room by the Love mansion maid. She walks into the room hurriedly and approaches Rebel appologetically...]
Jenn Olson: Sorry I'm a little late... My flight was delayed. Rebel Love: It's no problem, don't worry about it. [Jenn Olson gets out a few papers from her briefcase and makes sure she is ready to conduct her interview. She looks to the cameraman and gives him a nod.]
Jenn Olson: Rebel Love, thank you for taking the time to do this interview today. Rebel Love: The fans deserve to hear what I have to say. Jenn Olson: You stepped down as the NWWA President, now everyone is wondering what Rebel Love intends to do next... Could you possibly give us some insight?
Rebel Love: I'm not jumping straight back in to the ring if that is what you mean... But I am interested in being a part of the NWWA.
Jenn Olson: That's good news. Did you see the interview I did with
Yawner recently? Rebel Love: Yes. Jenn Olson: Matt said that under your control, and I quote: "The NWWA has tried to get rid of me several times just to keep me from getting this far." Is that a true statement?
Rebel Love: I don't know why Matt would say such a thing. The truth is I always did like Yawner. He seems to think that the World is against him... That each and every person in the NWWA hates his existence. But it's just not true. Now, I'd like to speak to
Yawner... Matt, I'd like to assure you that I will do everything in my power to secure you a fair match against Bootleg DA Killa. Beyond that, I'd also like to assure Matt that he'll receive all the opportunity that he so richly deserves. If you're ever worried about someone interfering in your matches, just give me a call and
I'll be there. If you ever think the new management is giving you a raw deal, just give me a call and I'll be there.
Jenn Olson: That sounds like an offer any member of the NWWA would be excited to receive from the Rebel of Love.
Rebel Love: I hope Matt understands that I'm being sincere here. I'm sorry that he feels the way he does about my run as President. Now, in an attempt to put things right... Using my "stroke" as a member of the
NWWA's Executive Committee I've arranged for the world title match between Bootleg DA Killa and Yawner to have a special "mystery" referee added to the match. I'd like to assure Matt and Bootleg DA Killa that this is a referee whom I know will be fair and impartial, but can also handle himself in case anyone tries to interfere in this headlining
World Title Match. Someone who will stick to the rules regardless of the circumstances and who won't be intimidated by anyone in the ring.
Now, finally, I'd like to apologize to Matt for "holding him back" all that time. If I did indeed hold you back, it wasn't done on purpose and now I'm going to make it up to you.
Jenn Olson: Thank you very much for your time. [Scene fades.] ----- Once again, if you like what you see, take a look at the fed! It's the
