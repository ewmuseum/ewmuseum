Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

An e-mail federation with no fixed office and a changing cast of owners, this promotion staged one weekly card and rarely anything else — a single show, run under the same name in the same handful of cities, carried the whole promotion from one season into the next.

The card itself moved between three cities across a single region: an arena in the state capital, an arena in the region's largest city, and a garden in a neighboring state. A nightly sample distributed by e-mail carried each week's deadlines and a preview of the coming card to a wider audience than the card itself ever reached.

The promotion told its own front-office story as fiction — an executive committee, a family business changing hands, a headquarters tower staffed with characters who never wrestled a match. Behind that fiction, the real work of keeping the promotion running changed hands more than once, each new keeper opening its books to new talent and promising the tradition would continue.

By the promotion's second summer, a new keeper had folded a touring supercard into the weekly schedule and gone looking for new blood to fill out a roster thinned by a wave of departures. No closing notice survives; the last word on record is a recruiting call, still hunting for faces to fill out a card for the next week.
