Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

American Wrestling Federations, the AWF of its own banner and the AWF Army of its slogan, was a roleplay e-fed run from its own website. Its copy promised action “so intense and so realistic you may go to the emergancy room just logging in”. The site carried a role play board and a chat box, a hall of fame that stood under construction in October 2005, a magazine called AWF Glory, a page of archived shows, and videos arranged by its own American Media Entertainment.

Its front page of 23 October 2005 names David Perfect AWF World Heavyweight Champion for the first time, after he beat Crisis and Genocide, the champion, and bills the house show Glory for 31 October and the pay-per-view Hollywood Nights for 13 November. By May 2008 the page ranked its wrestlers with win-loss records, TheMan first at 31 and 12, Justin Hoover World Heavyweight Champion at 14 and 2, D Dawg Xtreme Champion and Mr Perfect United States Champion, and gave the quick results of the first Glory house show, reported on 2 February 2008, with the card for Barbedwire Bash and its deadline of 21 March 2008. Roleplays had to run 25 lines and be posted before the deadline to count.

On 12 December 2008 it wrote that it was on the verge of returning on 2 January 2009, with its shows streamed live on Justin.tv. Its page of April 2009 lists the World and United States titles vacant, five wrestlers listed for another run at the World title, two of them new, and an open bracket tournament planned to fill both belts. On 27 July 2009 it announced that it was no longer a fantasy wrestling league and was becoming a wrestling fan site with news, short video clips and a free fan forum; its front page of August 2010 carries that announcement beneath news of Sheamus, Hulk Hogan and the TNA debut.
