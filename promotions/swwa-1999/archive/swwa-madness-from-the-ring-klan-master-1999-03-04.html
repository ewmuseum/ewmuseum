Madness From The Ring: Blubber vs. Klan Master

4 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ Camera opens up to the SWWA control center. ] Bob: HELLOOOOOO EVERYONE!! This is Bob and with me as always, "Mr. Excitement" Joe . Joe: Thank you Bob. Welcome to the SWWA's tv show, "Madness From The Ring", Our first matchup is " African American Midget" Blubber going up against KKK's upcoming superstar, Klan Master!!! Lets get some word from Klan Master.
[ Camera rolls to an interview by the Klan Master ] [ Camera opens up to the Klan Master, standing in front of a burning cross. Klan Master has on the usual KKK outfit. ] Klan Master: Hey you ugly black midget. There is no place in wrestling for monkeys like you. Now I am telling you boy, why don't you stick in the sports that you apes are good at. You know, football, basketball, the usual nigger sport. Wrestling is a white man's sport. US white man own this sport.
[ Camera rolls back to Sam and Joe ] Bob: What strong comments. Joe: And vulgar too. Bob: Um.. lets get to the ring for some action. [ " African American Midget" Blubber walks out to no theme music. He is 3'4 in height and weighs 255 lbs. He slides into the ring. ]
[ Klan Master comes from under he ring with a chain. He goes behind Blubber and chokes him with it. Blubber is jumping up and down, gasping for air. ]
Joe: Blubber is dieing. Bob: I know. [ Klan Master punches Blubber. Blubber flips upside down. Klan Master then sets up Blubber for a KKK Powerbomb. ]
Bob: OH MY!!! A KKK POWERBOMB!! Can it be??? Joe: it's over!! oh god no!!! [ Klan Master picks up Blubber for the powerbomb. ] Joe: Blubber is up.. here comes the KKK powerbomb. <closes eyes> Bob: WAIT! WAIT! Blubber is rapidly punching Klan Master rapidly across the head. Joe: ! Klan Master took all the punches and withstand all of them. OH MY!! THE
KKK Powerbomb. It's over.
Bob: 1.....2..............3...!!!! KLAN MASTER WINS!!! [ KKK comes out running down the aisle and enters the ring. They all took turns assaulting Blubber the midget. ]
Bob: This people make me sick. Joe: AHAHHA!! THIS RULE!! WAIT WHO IS THAT?? Bob: Well I'll be damn, its Muhammand, "America's oldest black midget" and the father of Blubber. Wait, he has singapre cane. He enters the ring.
Joe: Muahammand raises the cane.. and <SPLAT. OH NO!!! Muhammand nails his own mini son, Blubber?
Bob: What in the world???? [ Muhammand takes out a KKK mask and puts it on his face ] Joe: THIS MAKES ME SICK!! Bob: Muhammand , America's oldest black midget, has joined the KKK and betrayed his mini son!! What else is gonna happen in the SWA?? Stay tune!!
< commerical break>
