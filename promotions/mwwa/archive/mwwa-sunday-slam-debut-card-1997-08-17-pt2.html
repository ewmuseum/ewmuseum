MWWA Sunday Slam: Debut Card, Part 2

17 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
VJ: Anyways.. Not a bad match, a nice way to open up the MWWA and an impressive showing by both men with Doug Baxter pulling it our at the end with a little help from Nithrex MC: I like both those guys, I think they will be going places around here, they don't take any bull and have no respect for the fans.. What more can you ask for... VJ: Well lets go to the back.. Brian Wolfe is standing by with some comments... <Camera switches to the Interview Area in the back> Brian Wolfe: Well it's almost time for me to make my debut in the MWWA. Prime Time soon you will be the first to feel The Wolfe Attack. I've did my homework on you and I know I'm in for a good fight but I will prevail and have the all important win. Prime Time don't try to take the low road and beat me by cheating. There will be h@ll to pay if you do. Prime Time be ready, be ready...Cause I know I am. hahaha (Brian leaves and heads toward the ring) <Camera switches back to the Announcers> MC: Oh God.. This guy is boring me already..... VJ: While The camera was in the back DDT came on over here to let me know that so far.. Tom E. Extream and Brad "Love Machine" Morris are both No Shows.. He said if they arnt here by the time there match is scheduled to take place, we will have a substitute match... MC: What the hell? Our first card and we got guys already wimping out cause they cant handle the pressure?!? VJ: Well I have a feeling if they do indeed No Show, then DDT will be handing down some Heavy Penalties against them... MC: He sure as hell better... Those numnutz are trying to give our Fed a bad name before we even get started VJ: And since when did you care about this fed? MC: Since you reminded me earlier who signs my Checks.. VJ: How did I know you would say that... Well, War Dog and Lt Leslie have cleared out and DDT is standing by in the ring so Its time for our next match...Brian Wolfe and Johnny "Prime Time" Johnston DDT: Well Folks, that's one down... are you ready for some more? <Crowd Pop> ******************************************** Brian Wolfe Vs Johnny "Prime Time" Johnston 20 Minute Time Limit Writer: Sean ******************************************** DDT: Well alright then... <"I Love it Loud" by Kiss begins blaring over the PA System> DDT: Introducing first... Making his Return to the Ring after a short lay-off.... Weighting in at 250 pounds.... This Is... BRIAN WOLLLFFFFEEEE!!!!!!! <Brian Wolfe walks from out behind the curtain with a very business like look on his face, he slaps a few hands on the way to the ring but shows hardly any emotion> MC: Jeez.. Has this guy been hanging around Curt Hennig or something? He looks a little pudgy around the middle... mabey a few to many brewski's.... Nice tattoo though.... VJ: Oh Brother... once again you cant even wait to see a guy wrestle before passing judgment on him.... DDT: And his opponent.... <Ode to Joy begins playing, (HHH Music)> DDT: The Current USWR Heavyweight Champion... Weighting in at 238 Pounds... This is JOHNNY "Prime Time" JOHNSTON!!!!!!!! <Out walks Johnston, strutting away, slightly arrogant looking... He taunts several fans but slaps hands with two men who appear to be wearing "Prime Time" T-Shirts> VJ: Well, Im sitting here looking at the Stat sheet and it seems to me this is gonna be a very competitive match... MC: Your paying to much attention to paper... I don't care what those Stats say.. Im holding judgment till I see them in action, but so far Im gonna go with "Prime Time" in this one... <Ding Ding Ding> MC: No.. Now Wolfe with the reversal.... Johnston with rolls out of it and gives Wolfe a knee to gut... Snapmare Takeover by Johnston and he locks it in... He is trying to wear down Wolfe early on... Oh.. Knee to the base of the neck by Johnston.... VJ: Impressive so far by Prime Time but its still early on... Wolfe is up... The lock up again in the center of the ring... Wolfe forces Johnston back into the ropes, the ref is calling for the break and Wolfe complies... Thumb to the Eye by Johnston.. That was Uncalled for.. Knee lift puts Wolfe flat on his butt... MC: I do like this guy.. He has class... and look how proud he is of himself.. You gotta love a guy who Prides himself on his work... VJ: You got that right, he is just gloating away.. And strutting around the ring... <boo's> MC: I really like this guy a lot, he tells the fans to stick it and he gets right back to work on Wolfe... VJ: Irish Whip by Johnston.. Spine Buster!! WHOA!!! Did you see that Counter.. That was amazing... Wolfe Locked his arm around the Head and ended up turning that into a Jumping DDT... that was Awesome.... MC: Wolfe with the cover!! 1.... KICKOUT!!!! VJ: Barely a One count... Johnston pulling himself up.. Wolfe locks a front Face long and drags Johnston down to the mat... He has it locked in pretty tight.. MC: He is Choking Him!!! VJ: Evidently not.. The ref just checked that and he firmly has it locked below the chin, not the throat.. MC: The ref checking with Johnston but he isn't going to give it up... Foot on the rope.. Johnston stretched out just far enough to get his foot on the bottom rope and the ref calls for the break... Wolfe takes a four count before doing so... VJ: Johnston pulling himself up by the ropes... Wolfe moving in... Shoulder Ram by Johnston to Wolfes Gut... Another..... MC: Johnston now up... Knifeedge Chop to Wolfes chest... and Another... Crowd: WHOOOOOOO!!!! VJ: A series of short right Jabs by Johnston..... Wolfe back into the ropes.. Johnston whips him across the ring... Johnston follows... Clothesline.. Both men topple over to the floor... MC: Well now its time to separate the men from the boys.... Both men hit hard but Johnston recovers first..... Ref: 1......... VJ: Johnston with a scoop and a slam on the floor.. Luckily the mats are there or Wolfe would be really aching from that Ref: 2......... MC: Johnston pulling Wolfe up... Ohhhh.... Wolfe grabbed Johnstons tights a flung him into the guardrail!!! VJ: Uh-Oh.. Looks like that may have opened a cut on Prime Time's forehead, he has a little bit of blood dripping down his face <Crowd Pop> Ref: 3........... MC: Johnston very dazed after that... Now Wolfe with a Whip into the Steel Post.. Again Johnston hits headfirst..... Ref: 4.......... VJ: Somehow Johnston stays on his feet... Wolfe scoops him up... Big Back Body Drop onto the floor..... Ref: 5........ MC: Wolfe slides back in breaking the Refs count.. Where is he going? VJ: Looks like Wolfe is entering the danger zone.... High Risk move coming.. Wolfe up to the Top.. I think he is going for a Splash to the outside... Yes... NO... Johnston moved out of the way and My god Did Wolfe hit that floor hard.. He could have injured some ribs on that one... MC: Johnston is up, he wipes his brow of that blood and flicks it onto the back of Wolfe as if to tell him to Stick It.... I LOVE this guy... VJ: Well why don't you marry him then.... Johnston picks up Wolfe and rolls him back into the ring.. He has Wolfe on his feet but Wolfe looks like he is out of it.. Irish Whip by Johnston... Wolfe off the opposite ropes... Oh... Johnston picks up a knee and nails Wolfe hard right in that rib area... MC: I think Johnston realizes just what we did, that Wolfe may have injured some ribs on that Splash and he is now taking advantage of that and working them over... VJ: Johnston drops an elbow and connects, again to the rib area.... He smells blood.... MC: Again Johnston is gloating, he knows this one is in the bag... VJ: Don't be so sure... MC: Johnston with a scoop and a slam... VJ: a Body Slam will play hell with your ribs, especially when they are hurt, and that's exactly what Prime Time is counting on.... MC: Johnston is looking to finish it, he goes up top... there he hoes.. REALITY CHECK.. Cover.. 1.... 2....... FOOT ON THE ROPE!!! MC: NO WAY!!! VJ: Whoa! Close one.. I cant believe Brian Wolfe had the presence of mind to get his foot on the rope after that top rope leg drop MC: Johnston up and Jawing with the Ref now about a slow account, and I agree with him.. That count was slow as hell... VJ: Wolfe is pulling himself up... Johnston better pay attention to the opponent instead of the ref... this is un-believable that Wolfe is still in this after the Reality Check... MC: Johnston see's him now.. Right Hook by Johnston.. NO!! Damn it.. Wolfe blocks it.. Wolfe With a Right of his own that connects.. Now Johnston.. Now Wolfe... VJ: Folks.. We got a slug-fest going on now.... Rights and Lefts by both men, neither getting the advantage.... Wolfe defiantly on a second wind now and Johnston bleeding more heavily from that cut on his forehead.. MC: Wolfe with a REAL Hard left to the jaw of Johnston... Prime Time is dazed... Wolfe grabs him.. Belly to Belly Suplex.. And he nails it... VJ: I don't know who that hurt more.. Wolfe is holding onto his ribs... MC: Johnston is still down though... Uh-oh... Wolfe grabs a leg.... He's going for the WOLFE ATTACK!!!! VJ: No.. Johnston pushed him off with his legs... Ohhh.. Wolfe sternum first into the turnbuckle, shaking up his ribs even more.... MC: Johnston up... Wolfe holding onto his ribs.. Kick to the gut by Johnston... SHATTERED DREAMS!!! Cover... 1..... 2......... 3.................... MC: YES! Johnston got him.... VJ: Johnston pulls it off with a pin after his Shattered Dreams Pile Driver.... lets get the official word.... <Ode to Joy starts playing> DDT: And your winner.... JOHNNY "PRIME TIME" JOHNSTONNNNNNN!!!!! <small pop, mostly boo's> VJ: Awesome Match! Johnston pulled off the win even after being busted open... The action just keeps getting better and better..... MC: Whoo! Johnston is defiantly going places around here.. He is simply awesome in the ring.... So whats up next? VJ: Looks like our first tag match of the evening.. Heavy Metal meeting Excalibur... but first we have a promo to show you for a Soon to be MWWA Team....
