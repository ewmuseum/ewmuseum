MWWA Sunday Slam: Debut Card, Part 1

17 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Voice: FINALLY.... It has Arrived!!!!!! <The screen goes black, a spinning object is shown in the back ground, seemingly getting closer and closer as it gets larger and larger> BAM!! <It seemingly Smashes into the screen of your TV> MWWA <Suddenly explosions remove the sign from the screen, ""Cowboys From Hell" by Pantera starts blaring through your TV as the Screen opens up on the MWWA Arena in Downtown Chicago, wall to wall the small building is packed with 800 screaming fans, the camera pans around and finally switches to the Broadcast Table> Vince Johnson: Hello Fans.. Im Vince Johnson and too my right is my Broadcast partner Marcus Calloway <Calloway makes a nodding gesture but looks like he doesn't care> VJ: And let us welcome you to the MWWA's Debut card..SUNDAY NIGHT SLAM... MC: Oh Happy happy joy joy... Big freakin deal.. We gotta bunch of losers to show off to you guys tonight but im told our Esteemed President has some words for everyone... VJ: Oh will you lighten up.. This is our first card and you already sound like you hate it.. Anyways.. Standing by in the ring.. Daniel Drew Thomas... DDT... President and CEO of Mid-West Wrestling Association... <Crowd Pop as the camera switches angles to the center of the ring, where a Tall Well Built Middle-Aged man stands Smiling> DDT: Chicago... are you ready? Crowd: YEA!!!!! DDT: You Sure Your Ready? Crowd: <louder> YEA!!!!! DDT: Well Alright then... Welcome to SUNDAY NIGHT SLAM!!!!.. Better Yet... Welcome to the M_W_W_A!!!!!!!!!!!!! My name is Daniel Drew Thomas, Im the President and CEO of the MWWA.. But please call me DDT... I gotta tell you ladies and gentlemen, I've spent many long years with the dream of this day arriving.. And now its here... Its here for you... We got the Top Stars... The Top Announcers.. Heck.. We even got a TV station to air our shows... So let me welcome all of you watching at home too... Well wait a minute.. What am I doing.. You didn't come here to listen to my big mouth.. Your here to see some WRESTLING!!!! <Crowd pop> DDT: Well alright then.. Lets not keep wasting time and lets get on with it... Oh, by the way.. Ill be serving as your Ring Announcer too.. What can I say, didn't feel like spending money on a Buffer... So here we go.... ARE YOU READY?!?! <Crowd Pop> ************************************************************** "Dimebag" Doug Baxter vs War Dog w/ Lt Leslie 20 Minute Time Limit Writer: Sean ***************************************************************** <Walk by Pantera begins blaring over the PA System> DDT: Introducing first... You may have caught him in action in the IEWF or BWFE.. He weights in at 252 Pounds.... this is DIMEBAG... DOUG BAAXXXTTTEEERRRRRRR!!!!!!!! <The fans generally cheer, not knowing what to expect till Baxter comes out with his hands raised high above his head and starting jawing with the fans, even acting like he will hit a few fans but then laughing and walking off> VJ: He... Take a look at this guy.. Nice goatee.. Not.... I hear he can wrestle with the best of em though MC: I like his attitude... He may look a little freaky but he seems like a decent guy.. You know first impressions are the most important... VJ: Yeah, whatever.. I don't think I need to tell you my impression... DDT: And his Opponent... You may have seen him in E.W.W.W. , W.W.W.C.W. or C.O.W.L.... He weights in at 268 pounds.. And he is escorted to the Ring by the ever Beautiful Lt. Leslie... this is WAR.. DOOOGGGGGGGG!!!!! <Again not much crowd reaction, not knowing what to expect.. War Dog emerges from the curtain along with Lt Leslie> MC: Looks more like Lt. Lessie to me... VJ: Marcus! We are on TV!! <War Dog gets halfway to ringside and suddenly makes a mad dash flying into the ring under the rope, barely giving DDT enough time to get out> VJ: Oh... It is on.... War Dog wasting no time.. <Ding, Ding, Ding> MC: Baxter looks like he was expecting this, War Snoop Doggie Dog misses with a big left, Baxter ducked out of the way VJ: Baxter now opening up with rights and left to the gut forcing War Dog back into the corner <Crowd going wild over the instant action> MC: I Defiantly like this Baxter.. He is a fighter.... Baxter with a whip out of the corner.. Damn .. reversal By Snoop... VJ: War Dog reverses.. Ohh.. Baxter into the opposite corner Sternum First... <Wham> VJ: Talk about Impact.... MC: Snoop wastes no time.. He follows in and scoops up Baxter for a Back Body Drop.. NO.. Baxter goes all the over and lands on his feet... VJ: Baxter catches him by surprise.. Front Waist Lock into a Belly to Belly Suplex and he hits it... MC: Snoop bounces right back up but clutches his back... VJ: Will you stop calling him that! His name is War Dog.. MC: Whatever... Baxter backs him into the ropes.. Irish Whip... War Dog off the opposite ropes.. Clothesline by Baxter... No.. Nobody home.. War Dog ducks down.. Off the other ropes .. Big Shoulder Block by War Dog takes Baxter down.. VJ: Baxter right back up though but War Dog once again bounces the ropes and lands another Shoulder Block.. Again sending Baxter down... MC: Well Snoop does have a slight weight advantage in this one and its paying off here... Baxter as not as quick to his feet this time... VJ: No problem there.. War Dog gives him a helping hand.. Scoop and a slam by War Dog... War Dog up and right back down with a Big Elbow drop and it hits home... MC: Well it looks like Snoop has got the advantage now... He Pulls Baxter to his feet.. Uh Oh... War Dog locks in an Abdominal Stretch and its in the center on the ring... VJ: War Dog with that big height advantage which makes the Abdominal Stretch all that more painful cause he can really wrap his body around you.... Baxter in some obvious pain, but I don't think there is any way War Dog will be able to make him submit to this move... MC: Baxter trying to break out of it but cant seem to do it.. He tries for a hip toss but cant get War Dog over... VJ: The ref is checking.. But No , Baxter wont give up... War Dog applies a little more pressure to it and Baxter winces in pain.. Again the ref checks but no luck... Baxter is not going to give it up... MC: Well Snoop finally realizes that and he breaks the hold, Baxter falls to the mat... VJ: War Dog laying the boot to Baxter now, not giving him any chance to recover... very smart move... again War Dog reaches down to lift Baxter up.. OHHHH.... Low Blow by Baxter... MC: Oh just say it.. He hit him in the balls.... VJ: Let me remind you again... WE ARE ON TV!!! MC: Baxter slow to his feet, but War Dog isn't going anywhere after that blow.. Ha.. He is just standing there holding his nuts... VJ: Baxter with a Uppercut.. He rocks War Dog back.. Front face Lock by Baxter.. He drags War Dog to the center of the ring and sets him up.... Vertical Suplex.... MC: I love it.. This guy is good... HA! Baxter flips off the fans! VJ: War Dog slow to his feet, he is met by Baxter.. Baxter with an Irish Whip.. FRANKENSTEINER!!!! NO!!!! MC: War Dog Holds on and turns it into a Sunset Flip... 1.... 2....... KICKOUT!!! VJ: Close one... Im actually surprised Baxter kicked out of that one, War Dog brought him over in that flip so fast Baxter's head really smacked the canvas hard.. MC: Well that is showing now, War Dog is up but Baxter is on his knees trying to shake out some cobwebs... VJ: War Dog with a hard kick to the gut, Baxter was lifted off the mat by that and he lands on his back... War Dog scoops him up... Uh Oh.. I think . Yes... He is going for the War Bomb!! Wait.. What the hell... Who is that? MC: I believe that would be "The Supreme Superstar" Nithrex coming down to ringside.... HA.. I had a chance to talk to him earlier.. He is awesome.. VJ: Well what is he doing here? Whats he doing... He just grabbed Lt Leslie! HE IS KISSING HER!!! MC: YEAH!! Show that Lessie what a real man is Nithrex!! VJ: War Dog has dropped Baxter and he is over on the ropes yelling at Nithrex, He looks enraged.... MC: Nithrex Drops the Lessie and starts taunting War Dog.. HA.. I think she fainted from excitement.. VJ: More like From Disgust... MC: Snoop better pay attention to Baxter... he is up... VJ: Baxter sneaking up behind War Dog and grabs him... MC: GERMAN SUPLEX BY BAXTER AND HE BRIDGES IT!!! 1..... 2......... 3............... VJ: Baxter gets him with a German Suplex!!! <Ding Ding Ding> <Walk by Pantera starts playing> DDT: Ladies and Gentlemen.. Your Winner... AND the First ever Win in the MWWA!!.. DIMEBAG... DOUG BAXXXXTTTTEEEERRRRRRRR!!!!!!!!!!!!!! <Many boos, but a few cheers can be heard> VJ: That was not fair.. If Nithrex hadn't been there the match would have belonged to War Dog, oh whats Nithrex doing to Lt Leslie now? Ugh.. He picks her up and Drops her flat on her back on the concrete floor.... He is disgusting... MC: He is Awesome.. And So is Baxter who incidently is headed for the Pay Window VJ: Oh Christ... Did you go to Broadcasting School at the Dusty Rhodes School of Idiots or something? MC: Nope.. Couldn't get into the classroom, his fat ass got stuck in the doorway <Vince laughs>
