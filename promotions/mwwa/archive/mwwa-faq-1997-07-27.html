Mid-West Wrestling Association: FAQ

27 July 1997

Hi Everyone, some of you may know know, others dont, ut my name is [name withheld], I handle Wrestlers in serveral of the Top Feds and have come up with this stupid idea along with [name withheld] to start a New Fed, our goal is not to be one of the "best" right from the start but to give everyone that competes, plenty of exicting action and slowly build. Ive included in this message our Feds FAQ and a link to our Homepage so please give it a look. * We are still looking for Bookers and Writers to help run the Fed. http://members.aol.com/[detail withheld]/rules.html Mid-West Wrestling Association: FAQ 1. Introduction The Mid-West Wrestling Association is Based out of Chicago,Ill. It is a Regional Fed, which holds shows all throughout the Mid-West. The MWWA is neither a Hardcore Fed or a Technical Fed, it is a Fed based upon good angles and good matches, which means it will be a mix of many different styles of wrestling. 2. The Founders [name withheld] - Former Interim President of the XWC and BAHW Handler of: Rick "The Reaper" Erickson (IEWF, WHW, EWA) [name withheld] - Former Interim Vice President of BAHW Handler of: "The Perfectionist" Nik Nyce (IEWF, NOW, SJPW) ????????????? - Sorry, he wishes to be nameless :) 3. Booking The MWWA is a BOOKED Fed, which means no simulator besides the human brain is used. All matches are decided by the Booking Committee which is as of right now, made up of the three founder's of the MWWA, at a later time, The Booking committee may be expanded to also include some wrestlers and writers. 4. How to Win All matches are decided by a vote from the Booking Committee, when voting, The committee considers many things, mainly Roleplaying, but also of good angle, good strats and all around Heat for the match. Heat is most often drawn from angles and Roleplays. Also taken into consideration is all around participation. 5. Roleplaying There is a Limit on the Number of Roleplays allowed per week, 3 Roleplays for the Roleplay packet, and one for a card, if it is for a card, it should be in Interview format or have something to do with the card or matches on the card. Also, a Small Pre-Match Interview or "Shoot" may be done for any match you have on a Live card. Cursing is to be held at a minimum , ASS, HELL, DAMN are acceptable but the others will not be accepted unless they are bleeped out. No Depictions of, Rape, Child Abuse, Racial Insults or attacks, etc... will be accepted.. RP's including anything we deem un-fit, will not be used and you will be warned for them, the second warning.. Well.. There is no second warning... Your out! Assault upon any of the Commentators will not be tolerated, as you will be able to see as you read on, all are Active Wrestlers oe Former Wrestlers and will most likely get revenge :-) 6. What is Roleplaying? If you are asking that, please don't apply..... 7. Handlers Each Handler is Allowed no More then TWO Singles wrestlers or ONE Tag Team and a Singles Wrestler (Tag Teams are considered One Wrestler unless the team is made up of Two Different Handlers) 8. Wrestlers ORIGINAL Wrestlers only, this includes blatant ripoffs, there will be NO Stone Cold anythings in this league... Blatant ripoffs of any REAL wrestler or of another Handlers wrestler will not be accepted. Wrestlers are Limited to No More then 450 lbs and 7'1, We gotta keep it realistic guys.. And if we get too many big men.. This limit will be lowered so that no more Big Men will be allowed in, We want a Good Mix of Small and large in the league. Wrestlers also can be no Smaller then 150 lbs and 5'5 , again, lets keep it realistic. 9. Titles (More may be added later) MWWA Heavyweight Title -(universal weight limit) Must be Defended AT LEAST once per month and on every Supercard MWWA Television Title - (290 lbs weight limit) Must Be Defended AT LEAST twice per month, on Televised Cards only, and on every Supercard. MWWA Tag Team Titles - (Universal weight limit) Must be Defended AT LEAST twice per month and on every Supercard 10. Cards Sunday Night - (Card to be named later) LIVE Televised Card - 5 - 8 matches Wednesday Night - (To be named later) HOUSE SHOW - 4-6 Summary Matches 2-3 Full Length Matches (May Become A LIVE card when we have more writers) Supercard - Every 6 to 8 weeks - 8 - 12 matches, ALL Titles will be defended, Weeks the Supercard is held , the Sunday show will be Canceled 11. Characters The MWWA is Presided over by Daniel Drew Thomas, he likes to be called by his initials though, DDT... DDT is Former wrestler from the 70's and 80's , although he is now getting up there in the years, he still is a power to be reckoned with, he long ago thought up the idea of the MWWA but it wasn't till recently that he received some financial backing in this project, in his search to build the perfect Wrestling league he came up with an idea... Who else better to call the action and help run the league, then Wrestlers themselves, so he went out and found Two of the Toughest and Best wrestlers around to be commentators....and Through them in with a Play by Play man.... Nik Nyce - A Tweener, you just never know what side of the fence he is going to lean on, He weights in as a Cruiserweight but can give any Heavyweight a run for there money... Rick Erickson - A Monster Heel, Standing nearly 7 feet tall and close to 350 lbs, This man is an intimidating force and a contender no matter where he competes Vince Johnson - A Former Wrestler who had to retire at an early age due a severe knee injury, He calls the Action nuetral but is a Solid Face when expressing his views None of the above men like each other as of press date, but Have agreed to put their differences on hold while calling the action in the MWWA in order to give you the best entertainment possible (it took a hell of a lot of money too)
