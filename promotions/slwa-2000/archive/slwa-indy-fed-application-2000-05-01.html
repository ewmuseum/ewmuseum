Are you searching for an e-fed? Well, I think that you should consider the SLWA (St. Lawrence Wreslting Association). It's a small, independant e-fed, based in the St. Lawrence river and gulf area, thus, it will tour the provinces of Quebec, New Brunswick, Nova Scotia, Prince Edwards' Island and Newfoundland. The SLWA will have two cards per week, the flashes will be sent through e-mail in flash packets, an update will be sent at least once a week, with detailed rankings. If you need more information, feel free to e-mail me at [detail withheld] , or to check out the web page at http://www.geocities.com/xwec_1999 . If you want to join right now, feel free to fill out the following application form. Thanks!!!
