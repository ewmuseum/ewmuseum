Introduction to the IWO Welcome to the International Wrestling Organization Handbook! Thank you for taking an interest in the IWO by reading on past the Outline. The IWO will consist of original wrestlers ONLY. At no time will the IWO ever accept non-original wrestlers. The IWO will be a booked federation. Before I wrap up this short introduction I want to thank you for taking the time to read my handbook, and I wish you good luck in all of your e-wrestling matches. 2. Staff A. Cards Sunday Night Fight (Strategy Not Required, but recommended) Sunday Night fight will be the only IWO show for now, until we got our roster up to a good size. There will be 4-6 matches and two scheduled in-ring interviews and usually a dark match or two which will not have commentary. All matches will be booked by the President unless a challenge is issued and accepted. Flashes for Sunday Night Fight must be received by 6:00 PM Pacific the day before the card. This show will be Hosted by Skyler Thomas and Jay Scallion B. Preview Shows Flash Session This will be the time when I will send out the flashes that I have received for that week. It will come out twice a week, on Wednesdays and Saturdays. This show will be hosted by Zack Nowell and Raymond Jenkins. C. Pay Per-Views There will be one PPV a month. Here's what they are January- Winter War February- Lovers Quarrel March- Wrestle City April- Spring Disaster May- May Mayhem June- Summer Blast July- Midsummer Bash August- Highway to Hell September- Fall Rumble October- Halloween Hell November- November Warzone December- Wrestlefest D. SuperCards There will be two SuperCards a year. All title will be defended at the SuperCard. Here is the line up for the Super Cards. June- Clash of the Titans December- Deadman Walking 4. Titles IWO Title This is the equivalent of the WWF or WCW World Title. It MUST be defended once a month and on both SuperCards. IWO United States Title This title must be defended twice a month and on both SuperCards IWO Light Heavy Weight Title The Light Heavy Weight Limit in the IWO is 240 pounds. If you wrestler is under that weight than they are eligible for the Light Heavy Weight Title, it MUST be defended three times a month and at both SuperCards. IWO Tag Team Titles These Titles will be defended once a month and at the SuperCards. 5. Rankings The rankings will go as followed. For every match you win you will receive a point and for every match you lose, you will lose a point. The person with the Highest amount of points will be deemed the IWO Title Top Contender, when the top contender has a title shot if he loses that match then he will automatically drop to 5 place. If there is a tie for the top contender spot the person with the least amount of loses will get the spot. 6. Application A.
