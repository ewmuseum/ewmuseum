Tag Teams 8. Closing [+++Introduction to the IWO+++] Welcome to the International Wrestling Organization Handbook! Thank you for taking an interest in the IWO by reading on past the Outline. The IWO will consist of original wrestlers ONLY. At no time will the IWO ever accept non-original wrestlers. The IWO will be a booked federation. Before I wrap up this short introduction I want to thank you for taking the time to read my handbook, and I wish you good luck in all of your e-wrestling matches. [+++Staff+++] A. Cards Sunday Night Fight (Strategy Not Required, but recommended) Sunday Night Fight will be the only IWO show for now, until we get our roster up to a good size. There will be 4-6 matches and two scheduled in-ring interviews and usually a dark match or two which will not have commentary. All matches will be booked by the Presidents unless a challenge is issued and accepted. Flashes for Sunday Night Fight must be received by 6:00 PM Pacific the day before the card. This show will be Hosted by Skyler Thomas and Jay Scallions B. Preview Shows Flash Session This will be the time when I will send out the flashes that I have received for that week. SuperCards There will be two SuperCards a year. All titles will be defended at the SuperCard. Here is the line up for the Super Cards. June- Clash of the Titans December- Deadman Walking [+++Rules and Regulations+++] The following are the Rules and Regulations for the IWO. These MUST be followed and you will be penalized if you don't follow them. 1) You are REQUIRED to Role-play once a week, even if you don't have a match, the first time you don't roleplay you will be warned, the second time you will be penalized and if you don't roleplay again then I will be forced to relieve you of your services in the IWO. 2) Role-plays must be sent to both [name withheld] and myself. 3) If you wish to interfere in a match send me a detailed start telling me what you want to happen and how so that our writers know exactly what to do. 4) The IWO is a booked fed, if you lose it is because he had better role-plays. TIP: It's not length that makes a roleplay good, it's quality. Just because you write long role-plays doesn't mean that you are necessarily going to win. 5) You may only uses certain cuss words in your role-plays. If it is a word that you know they don't use on the WWF you may still use it but it must be censored. 6) Remember to pick a gimmick that you will enjoy if you pick a gimmick that you are going to get bored chances are you will stop role-playing very quickly. [+++Titles+++] IWO Title This is the equivalent of the WWF or WCW World Title. It MUST be defended once a month and on both SuperCards. IWO United States Title This title must be defended twice a month and on both SuperCards IWO Juniorweight Title The Juniorweight Title is the equivalent of the IWO Title for the Juniorweights. Just because someone is a Juniorweight doesn't mean that they can't go for any other titles. Everyone is eligible for any title. The Juniorweight Limit in the IWO is 240 pounds. If you wrestler is at or under that weight than they are eligible for the Juniorweight Title, it MUST be defended once a month and at both SuperCards. IWO Tag Team Titles These Titles will be defended once a month and at the SuperCards. [+++Rankings+++] The rankings will go as followed. For every match you win you will receive a point and for every match you lose, you will lose a point. The person with the Highest amount of points will be deemed the IWO Title Top Contender, when the top contender has a title shot if he loses that match then he will automatically drop to 5th place. If there is a tie for the top contender spot then the two people who are tied will wrestle for the spot. [+++Application+++] A.
