Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Internet Championship Wrestling Federation began in September 1995 as an intergender promotion — men and women fighting for the same belts — simulated on the TNM wrestling program and overseen by someone known only as Mike. Its weekly television show, the ICWF Saturday Showdown, broadcast from the fictional Richter City, Florida, fronted by Commissioner Gordon Shaw and Matchmaker “Big” Jim Perry and called by a rotating booth of Jessica “Jessie” James, Bob Brodsky and Chad “Romero” Romero, later joined by Sam Wilson and Danny Lopez, picked up when the ICWF bought out the Southwestern Championship Wrestling promotion, and by Kris Erickson, formerly of the folded Louisiana Wrestling Alliance.

By its November 1995 recruitment posting, the ICWF was already crowning champions across six divisions: the Vision in Violet held the World Championship, Arlechino the Intercontinental Championship, “The Hunter” Kassandra Helman the Television Championship, the Snake Sisters the World Tag Championship, Fury the Intercontinental Tag Championship, and Rob Foster and the Masked Marauder the United States Tag Championship.

Its “Season's Beatings” card, 7 February 1996, ran on the back of a stable war that had already left United States Champion Angel with bandaged ribs after an attack by Dark Paladin; she defended the title anyway and lost it to Spanish Rose. The main event, for the promotion's own World Six-Man Championship, went to Feminine Force — Kelly Kandelski, Jessica Spangles and the Vision in Violet — over Rob Foster and the Body Girls, managed by the by-then World Champion the Masked Marauder, when Foster submitted at 23:12.

The promotion kept running into the summer: “Gorgeous” Gretchen retained the World Television Championship undefeated and stood as the World Champion's own top contender, and on 17 June 1996 the Masked Marauder retained the World Championship over Rob Foster amid an on-air dispute between the fed's own booking committee and a faction calling itself the S1W's.

No closure document survives. The last dated ICWF broadcast on record is that 17 June 1996 show, and how or when the Internet Championship Wrestling Federation's own run ended is not in the surviving record.
