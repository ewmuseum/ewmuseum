LAST part's a lie....
Mike ******************************************************************** * * * [name withheld] * * GM/O, The Miami Mercs * * Owner/President, The Internet Championship Wrestling Federation * * Founder and President, The Kirk-First Foundation * * (Our motto: "Fire!") * * * ******************************************************************** ===================================================================== ######## ######## ## ## ######## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ####### ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ######## ######## #### #### ## ===================================================================== THE INTERNET CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING FEDERATION ===================================================================== ADMIN: [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] ) ===================================================================== The ICWF is a mixed-style wrestling federation that features some of the most daring and exciting combinations of matches and angles in an e-mail wrestling fed. Interested players will be encouraged to participate via the submission of interviews and flashes. One well- received facet of the league has been the practice of allowing the match winners to elaborate on the match for the card, giving them a specific input on how their wrestler performs and behaves. Cards are generally put out on a once-per-week basis, with occasional delays attributable to the intrusion of the real world into the lives of the admin and the players.
------------ League Setup ------------ ------------------------- Title Structure - Singles ------------------------- ICWF World Championship Intercontinental Championship TV Chamionship North American Championship United States Championship -------------------------- Title Structure - Tag Team -------------------------- ICWF World Tag Championship Intercontinental Championship United States Championship The championship belts will be defended at every major card (no Hulk Hogans and Stings here!) Should an injury prevent a wrestler from meeting this obligation, then the league may grant a stay of up to two cards before stripping the belt and holding an appropriate contest to decide a new belt holder.
------------------- Player Requirements ------------------- Players are encouraged to generate angles and feuds amongst one another, and give this info to the admin for implementation.
Before each event, the admin will notify everyone that they need to provide some kind of agenda for their matches. This is where the
"personality" comes in, as players smash trophies, slap managers, steal urns, and so forth. For example, the manager for the Masked
Maniac knows that Joe Babyface is receiving a special plaque for community spirit during the evening's festivities. He sends this in:
"As the Mayor is handing Joe the plaque, my Maniac's gonna run in and clip him from behind, then bust that plaque over his noggin!"
Joe Babyface, being wary of this possibility, might send in: "I'm expecting somebody--probably the Maniac--to try something during my ceremony. So, I'm having my buddy, "Big Daddy Cold",
Edsel, watch from nearby. If the Maniac tries something, he's gonna come in and help!" The result would probably be a huge brawl during the ceremony, which would get resolved later in the ring.
Here are some of the more prominent personalities that will populate the ICWF: Commissioner: Gordon Shaw - Gordon was a noteworthy wrestler for a thirty-year span, who specialized in high-risk moves--and leading with his head. This left the acting commissioner of the ICWF a bit addled. His principle purpose is to enforce the rules and edicts of "The Board", although most people are perfectly aware that he's just a figurehead. He will inevitably be present in the packs of roving officials that attempt to separate enraged combatants.
Matchmaker: "Big" Jim Perry - Big Jim is an enigma of sorts in the ICWF. He is one of the most frequently referred to and infrequently seen officials in the organization. Rarely does a week go by without someone screaming, "You get
Jim Perry to set it up!", but he is only occasionally present to respond to their accusations of unfair booking. Once in a while Jim will be seen with
Gordon Shaw and/or with the packs of roving officials. "Big" Jim is actually around six feet tall, with an average build. His coke-bottle lens glasses are the subject of many a barb.
Announcers: Bob Brodsky - Bob's square jaw and articulate delivery make him the perfect choice for fan-friendly broadcasts. He frequently refers to the three years he spent with the Tampa Bay Buccaneers as a linebacker (he omits the fact that he spent more time mixing Gatorade than playing). When pressed by rulebreakers to back up his tough talk with possible action, he conjures a phantom knee injury as "the only thing keeping me from laying you out right now!" Brodsky obsesses on any amateur or professional sports experience the wrestlers might have, often inventing or heavily embellishing these details to where you wonder how you missed "Bulk"
Hogan's crucial sack of Steve Young in Super Bowl XXV.... Favorite quotes include, "It's gut check time for <x>!", "<x> is going for broke!", "That reminds me of the clotheslines I threw while playing professional football for the Tampa Bay Buccaneers from '83 to '85!", "That was a closed fist!
Where's the flag, ref?", "What a stud!", and the all-time classic, "That makes me sick!" Chad "Romeo" Romero - In contrast to Brodsky's coddling of the faces,
Romero is a commentator/occasional manager who relishes in needling Bob and the fans. Romero prefers silk shirts with a white or red kerchief in the breast pocket, along with designer sunglasses and an array of expensive jewelry. Some of Romero's favorite quips include "Better notify <x>'s next of kin!", "Maybe the great unwashed might fall for that, Brodsky--but not the kid! And speaking of the unwashed, didja forget it's Saturday?", and
"I heard <x> qualified for the Olympics. The Special Olympics."
Jessica "Jessie" James - Jessie is the perfect balance between Bob and
Chad. She offers objective analysis of what's happening in the ring, and will usually correct the excesses of her co-broadcasters when necessary.
Her beauty and poise have brought her consideration from mainstream news agencies for reporting assignments, but she rejects these offers from her love of wrestling. Her color commentary is a mix of match observations and corrections to her colleagues, such as, "Bob, I don't remember the Bucs going to the Super Bowl....", "Chad, that was a great dropkick--whether he kisses babies or not," and "I didn't see her pull the tights on that pin,
Bob. Sorry."
Sam Wilson and Danny Lopez - Sam and Danny are a pair of announcers carried over when the ICWF bought out Southwestern Championship Wresting. Sam is a belt-buckle-and-cowboy-hat kinda guy who says anything and everything that's on his mind. He usually opens any segment he hosts with a hearty
"YEEEEE-HAAAAA!", which doesn't always sit well with whomever he's interviewing. Danny is quieter--MUCH quieter--by comparison, although he's never at a loss for words. He is a pro-caliber announcer who usually notices details that elude his enthusiastic co-broadcaster. He is most likely to be given the least desirable interviewing assignments.
Kris Erickson - Kris was an announcer for the Louisiana Wrestling Alliance before it went belly-up. Kris is a straightforward play-by-play commentator with a remarkably even perspective on the match in progress.
He rarely declares favorites or offers negative judgements on the actions of the wrestlers. Short of Jessie James, he is also considered the most knowledgeable of the announcers.
Programs: The ICWF does one primary television show, the ICWF Saturday Showdown. It comes to the viewer from the fictitious locale of Richter City, Florida, and offers a wild variety of matches and interviews. The normal broadcast team is Jessie, Bob, and Chad, with Kris, Sam, or
Danny joining them on occasion. Those not announcing are usually left to conduct interviews with the wrestlers and get the "inside scoop" on developing angles.
The ICWF also does several regular pay-per-view events, the names and locations of which vary from season to season.
If you're interested, and would like to play, then you'll need to create one or more wrestlers. The league currently has a limit of five characters. All parties are encouraged to submit a mix of male and female characters, although this is by no means a requirement.

or "Face" neutral. --------------- Selecting Moves --------------- Next comes the process of selecting the moves your character will prefer during a match. They aren't the ONLY moves he/she will perform, but they most completely define the type of wrestler your character is. For example, an aerialist will have several flying maneuvers, and a power wrestler would opt for impact-related attacks.

Also, you will need to give the character a finisher when you're done selecting these moves, so if your character tends to put opponents away with the piledriver, then

--------------- Finishing Moves --------------- Every big-time pro wrestler uses a finishing maneuver. This is the most devastating move in the character's arsenal. Ric Flair's figure four, Paul Orndorff's piledriver, and Diesel's jack-knife power bomb are examples of these. To define the finisher, complete the following info:
* The name of the move (include a description if the name does not make it intuitively obvious what the move involves) * Whether the move is a submission or impact finisher * Three counters to your finisher (if any, or if known; choose from the moves list above) * Whether the move can be performed when coming off the ropes * The move from the above list that is most like your finisher * Whether your opponent will have to be on his/her back or stomach for the finisher to work * Whether the move requires your opponent to be placed on the turnbuckle * Whether your character can be thrown from the turnbuckle while performing the maneuver --------------- Foreign Objects --------------- Dastardly heel characters can elect to have a foreign object handy to use on their hapless opponents. If your character uses such a shortcut, provide its name (for example, a tennis racket, the sceptre, etc.)
--------------------- Dives Out Of The Ring --------------------- If your character performs a dive out of the ring, indicate this; you may optionally provide a special name for it as well (for example, you might refer to your dive as "The Pancake").
--------------------- The Rest Of The Story --------------------- Finally, any history or other pertinent info should be provided, to better round out the character.
If you'd like to play, then take the sample character sheet below and begin making your character or characters. When submitting your wrestlers, be sure to include an appropriate introductory interview or skit to bring your personnel to the attention of the other players.
For example, here's a skit from a current top bad guy in the ICWF, the Soultaker: ######################################################################### (The screen is totally black, with low, ominous music playing in the background.) [Whispered voice] Greetings, children. A blue light slowly begins to rise. From the shoulders up appears a man in a black cowl and robes, standing before a large, open book. Mist floats by as he focuses his gaze on the viewer.
[Man] My name is the Soultaker, and I bid thee welcome. My mission in here in the
Internet Championship Wrestling Federation is not about winning titles, or collecting trophies of a mortal sort. Rather, my purpose is to gain thine most precious aspect--thy soul itself. As I defeat thee, I shall transcribe thy names in my Tome of Darkness, and I shall have that most dear to you.
(The camera closes in, and his voice becomes a whisper once more.) [Soultaker] Guard thy soul, for I intend to have it, err long. ######################################################################### Of course, these segments can be longer and more involved, but this was just to give those unfamiliar with such things an idea. If your character is power-oriented, the setting might be a gymnasium. A former football player might present his case from an empty stadium wherein he used to play. An ex-volleyball player might appear on the beach where she made her name. The possibilities are endless.
