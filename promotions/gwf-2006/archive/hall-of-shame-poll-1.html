Those of you from the old board probably remember the GWF Hall of Shame topic, where I created one large topic naming tons of the strangest gimmicks, angles, etc. to happen in GWF. I've decided to bring the HOS back, but it's not going to be everything named at once. Instead, I'm doing a Wrestlecrap type of thing where there are two or three inductions every week or so. What will be inducted will be voted on, so you're able to choose 3 things you think belong in the GWF Hall of Shame. And in the off-chance you're offended that something involving you was put in(since I won't be seeking permission), just let me know and it'll be removed... And if you have nominations for the future, feel free to name them, but it has to be something IN-CHARACTER , so no OOC feuds for now.
