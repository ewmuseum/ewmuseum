SheltonSplash "GWF's Golden Standard" The Living Fairytale. "There's always a happy ending. No Fairytale Ends Sour. All Good Thing's Happen. The Good Guy's Win. And Bad Guy's Lose. Childhood Dream's Are Realised. Love Is Always A Given. No-one Is Ever Alone. There's Only Up's, And No Down's. All Smiles And No Frown's. Just When The Bad Part Comes. Here Comes Your Saving Chum's. Every One Has It's Alpha Male. ... ... But This Is Not A True Fairytale." There is no Bamby, Seven Dwarves or Little Bo Peep. This is the true story, everyone want's to know and keep. So pull up a chair or horse and saddle, ... This is the dark, dingy tale of one man's battle. Since that fateful day so many years ago, one man's battle for pride and sorrow has lead him through many different paths. Danger, Anguish, Success and Love. Though his true calling was a feeling much more superior... Confidence. Born March 28th 1989, He became just a mere mortal, with only minimal presence in this once known world. Bringing happiness to all who sought after it, his boyish charm was hard to resist. Growing up you'd think he had it all, but yet far from the truth, this once shy lad had suffering, pain, hurt and betrayal on his hands. Just brought into the world, and already been screwed, this young lad had no future and was all and doomed. He had that single sound running through his head, that piercing agony screeching was all that could be said. Was he hurting because he was beaten, picked on or mocked. No, simply he was born handicapped. Deafend and shocked. Born into the word without the ability to hear, this young lad had no choice but to only fear. Why were people moving there mouths with no sound? Were there any voices to be found? Waving the hands around in some sort of rhythm made him confused and and startled at sight, but this young boy wouldn't stop with no fight. Battling through his younger age through difficulties and desire, every step closer to that deep buring fire. At a young age his hearing was given back to him, what were he to do? At school he could hear and not understand, he seemed very dim. But no-one understood his pain and confusion, to them he was making an illusion. He fought through life giving it his all, but from the age of three had had only one goal. To battle inside that squared circle was was his big plan, to reach the pinnacle of the business was his only mission, could this little kid, nobody knows of, make it? You tell him. Because he sure think' so. Through school, college, university and work, he was a lonely man, on his own with no remorse. But soon he became famously... The Dark Horse. "We all know of championship's, ownership's, squabbles and battles. But this is not the end of his quest. You see his determination and morality brung him success in the ring, but he displayed all his passion and desire there which left him what outside? Nothing. The man has nothing, only faded memories and a shaded past, betrayal, anger, admonishment and astonishment are just some of what he feels. Sitting at home nursing a knee injury, barley walk nor stand, yet the fighting spirit of he who can. He sits for hours and hours with only one thing on his mind, is it the fact that he gave up his perfect woman and outside world, to run a company, just to be undermined by his peers? No. Was it the fact that he had a childhood dream and when he finally realised it, people looked at his achevement in disgust? No. Was it the fact that he put his body on the line, blood, sweat, tears, tables, ladders, electricity, barbed wire steel chairs... Was it the fact that even know he was in his own mind ...The single handed best? No. Yet the only thing he can sit there and think of is the dispicable, and unremandable act once portrayed against him. Let's take you back...
