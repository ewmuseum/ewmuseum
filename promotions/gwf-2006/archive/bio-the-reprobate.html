Entrance The ramp lights flicker and turn off... until "Purple Haze" by Jimi Hendrix hits and then the lights come back on, highlighted red and purple The stage center glowing purple with the sides being shined red. The Reprobate shoves the curtain out of the way as he steps from behind it, with his manager Christina Hernandez at his side. Rep wears his World Tag Team Title around his waist, as Christina wears the Interbrand Title around her waist, while holding a singapore cane. He walks down the ramp and ignores "Fuck Rep" signs on his way down. He reaches the ring at the end of the runway and holds the ropes down for Christina. She enters the ring and moves to the side as Rep takes his leather jacket off and throws it outside. Rep lowers down in to the corner and she follows, sitting in his lap as they await the next entrance.
