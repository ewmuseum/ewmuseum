GWF -> Moveset Area -> Sign-Ups and Character Updates The Soul of Philly TJ , has come to GWF The Soul Member {{•• Superstar Image ••}} ( Posted Image ) {{•• Real Name ••}} TJ {{•• In-Ring Name ••}} TJ {{•• NickName ••}} The Soul of Philly {{•• Hometown ••}} Philadelphia, PA {{•• Height ••}} 6’ 11’’ {{•• Weight ••}} 275 lbs {{•• Alignment ••}} Face {{•• Gimmick ••}} A Man that has finally accepted his past troubles and moved on. Yet when he anger rises, his eyes gain a gold look to them, showing his temper. {{•• Attire ••}} Outside of the ring, TJ wears his shirts from Extreme Championship Federation and either a zip up hoody or a fleece/jacket combination. He also wears either jeans or red track pants with a single white stripe running down the sides of his legs. The white stripe is next two two, smaller black stripes. He wears white Nike basketball shoes, size 23. But on serious times, like a contract signing or something along those lines, he wears a red and white suit. When he comes down to the ring, he often is wearing a Philadelphia related shirt or jersey that he takes off and gives to a fan. In the ring, TJ wears red denim shorts that have 215 going down his right leg and PHI going down his left leg. He wears a red padded glove on his right hand, the hand that he throws his punches with more often. He has red bball shoes that have his name on them. He has white knee pads and white elbow pads. For regular PPVs, he wears a reverse of this attire with white denim shorts with 215 and PHI in red lettering. He wears a pair of white bball shoes with red lettering, a white glove on his right hand. He also wears red elbow pads and red knee pads. For either the final match in a big fued, Title matches or any big, WM like PPV, he wears a golden attire. Gold shorts, red lettering, gold shoes with his name in red. He wears gold bball shoes with his name in red and a gold glove on his right hand. He also wears red elbow pads and red knee pads {{•• Entrance Music ••}} Unbroken - Killswitch Engage {{•• Entrance Moves ••}} NON CHAMP ENTRANCE – The beginning to Unbroken begins to play as the lights go out. Red, white, and gold lights flash as TJ rises up like Kurt Angle in TNA. TJ looks around. He then does a Goldberg like jump as red, white, and gold fireworks go off and the lights come on. At this same time, the lyrics come on. TJ walks down to the ring and slides in the ring. He goes to the corner and throws his arms in the air and then beats his chest with one arm. CHAMPION ENTRANCE – Same but he lays the title on the ground before jumping. He also holds the title like The Rock and when he goes to the corner, he holds the belt in his left hand as he beats his chest with his right arm. {{•• Wrestling Style ••}} Strength - Adapts to his opponents. Smaller – Use size. Bigger – Use agility and technical ability. He is very agile for his size. He has a limited background in am wrestling. Weakness - His anger can get the best of him and cost him the match. Not the best technical wrestler, though he tries, but if he faces someone to the likes of that style, he will try to out wrestle them instead of keeping to his true strengths. Ring Logic - Follows his strengths and tries to stay calm. He likes to hit the Soulbuster out of no where, almost RKO like. {{•• Finishing Move(s) ••}} • The Soulbuster = Grabs his opponent like he would for a Chokeslam but, throws them into the air and catches them in a Spinebuster position and then hits a Spinebuster Better for people smaller than him, but can be used on people his size or bigger, but it won't be as effective • P.O.D (Piledriver of Death) = Like The Cradleshock, but more like a piledriver. This is his secondary finisher and is used more often if someone kicks out of the Soulbuster or if they are his size or larger. {{•• Last Resort Finishing Move ••}} • Philly Cheese Lock = A Combination of a Sharpshooter and STF. Because of the size of TJ and the position that his opponent is in, this move is almost impossible to escape. {{•• Favourite Moves ••}} • Impact Bomb : Choke Bomb ( http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Mo7TK99Zr4 ) • 215 Hurtin’ : Bearhug into a Spinebuster • Philly Combo : 2 Powerbombs into a Powerslam • Cheesesteak Combo : 2 Suplexs into a DDT • TJT : DDT 15 in SvR: 07 • Spear ( http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0zYe3s8ib9k&feature=related ) • The 215 Drop : Hellavator ( [URL=http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hzBGch_YapM[/URL] ) • Diving Spear • Any other technical moves • Any other power moves {{•• Misc ••}} • Is winless in Prison Fight matches, yet is said to be the father of the Prison Fight match as he has been apart of all 3 ever taken place. • TJ created a match for an event in his fed, ICW, that was called the Carnage Cage. Similar to WCW's Triple Cage Match, but the winner had to climb a ladder in the 3rd cage and grab whatever was up for grabs. • TJ went 17-0 as TWRF tag team champions with his long time friend, John Miranda before TWRF shut down for the first time. • TJ has never won a World Title in his 4 year career though he has come close to many, he has never been able to break the figurative glass ceiling above him. {{•• Bio ••}} TJ was always the teddy bear pesona. Everyone knew he wasn't to be messed with, but he didn't try to fight people. He was a happy go lucky kid. But some times, he had times where he wasn't in control of his actions, which lead to some nasty fights in school. When he graduated, he knew what he was going to do: Be a pro wrestlers. He had been a fan since he was born. He began working out in a local school before moving onto ECW. But when it closed, TJ was left without a path to his dream. His dream was crushed. WWE wasn’t going to hire him; he was too raw for primetime. WCW was bought by WWE and he couldn’t deal with the guys from WWE in ROH or the other Indy promotions. He went on with his life. He got a job that was steady. He had an apartment that he could pay for and still have enough for things he wanted. After he got good at his job, the success made one co-worker angry at him. One day, when TJ's sister came by, the guy talked bad about her and TJ snaped, throwing him through a wall. TJ was sent to prison for assault for 3 years. It turned into 5 when he began to start fights and hurt prisoners and guards. It was a visit from his mentor that set him straight and he was released after his time was up. He went to many independent federations like TRWF, XRWS, and ECF. In the down time, he opened his own fed ICW. He was and now in WWE Resurrection. When he first entered, he went 2-1 including a battle royal where he pinned Edge and The Undertaker a shot at the World Title, but WWER shut down before he got that shot. He is currently the ECW TV Champion and now looks for more competition. {{•• SAMPLE RP ••}} ( Posted Image ) Scene 1 The.Motion.Picture.//.Scene.Title: All is not what is seems The.Actors.//.Those .Involved: TJ, Hada Long Rhodes, Kenny Hunter Cameos.//.Those.Metioned: A few Legends Dressed.2.Kill.//.TJ.Attire: Click Here The.Design.//.RP.For: Pre Main Event of Hardcore TV 2/18/09: Single Match against Edge for the ECW TV Title ..//Prolouge\\..
