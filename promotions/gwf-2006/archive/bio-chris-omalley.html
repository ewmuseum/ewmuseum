Superstar bio: Chris O'Malley

GWF -> GWF Superstars -> Current Superstars Chris O'Malley SheltonSplash "GWF's Golden Standard" ( Posted Image ) Name: Chris O'Malley Also Known As: The Roll, CJ. Nicknames: "The Revenger", "Mr Money in the Bank". Hometown: St. Paul, Minnesota Weight: 270 lbs. Height: 6' 4. Debut: May 2006. Achievements: x1 GWF Champion x1 Intercontinental Champion x1 Tag Team Champion x1 International Xtreme Champion Money in the Bank Legacy Cup Champion 2007 Finisher: "The Winter Solstice" Catchphrases: "Revenge is a dish best served cold!" "My revenge will leave you cold." Bio: In the ring Chris O'Malley is one of the GWF's strongest performers. Whether on a stick or beating the living "Crap" out of somebody, CJ impresses with everything that he does. Starting on very early with the Roll, CJ had a few memorable matches with Mark Madison, then known as "The Underbaker". This even included a match at the first ever GWF pay per view, and First ever Rise and Fall in the legendary "Basket Match" He then went on to become CJ and continued his fierce rivalry with foe Mark Madison and his newly aquatinted assassin "Omaha". CJ was International Xtreme Champion and once again went to Rise and Fall for the second year running to face Mark Madison, when the gold was on the line. These two men stole the show, in what could only be remembered as "Feud of the Year" CJ lost his championship that night to Madison, but after stealing the show soon went onto to bigger thing's. Soon after he was ousted and scouted, by two of the GWF's biggest names, the man who created the GWF, SheltonSplash and then GWF Champion, James Murphy. The two men looked at CJ with great promise and saw something of the future, with much deliberation the three quickly struck success creating the stable, "Cataclysm". The three then become the strongest of allies helping eachother win championship's and many matches, and amongst all of this Chris O'Malley managed to steal glory, and hog them limelight by becoming the first ever Legacy Cup Winner. Still young and fresh in with Cataclysm, Chris was on the rise but seemingly him and his two friends were trying to be bested by one of their main annoyances. Michael Toaster. Toast had beaten James for the GWF Championship, and was still proving himself a thorn in the side of Cataclysm. With James gone, Shelton had stepped up to run ship, but quickly turned his attentions onto the opportunity at hand. Shelton saw CJ's chance to take back the glory of Cataclysm, and coached and willed the young man on to his soon to be GWF Championship match at Locked N' Loaded 2007. CJ stepped into Locked N' Loaded full of glee and passion. He went toe to toe with the GWF Champion, Michael Toaster and with his stable partner SheltonSplash at his side and a special contact from Shelton. At the end of the night it was Chris O'Malley who was being announced as the winner with his arms held high, as both his members of Cataclysm stood by him, proud of the new champion. Chris O Malley was GWF champion. Chris had a good championship reign, it included defenses against the likes of Michael Toaster and a man he had met before Mark Madison. But it was an intense feud with then "Mr Money in the Bank" Alex Storm, that caught everyone's attention's. As the raging feud between Cataclysm and the newly formed "Resistance" was strong, nothing could dent the ego of the GWF Champion. And it was at Sunday Night Sacrifice in January of 2008 that Chris O'Malley would have to step to the plate and face Alex Storm who was cashing in his money in the bank. And after months of speculation when the time would strike for the doomed GWF Champion, CJ silenced his critics and proved everyone wrong when he defeated Alex Storm with no interference from neither Cataclysm nor Resistance. CJ had proved everyone wrong, and shown that being "Mr Money in the Bank" wasn’t it all was cracked up to be. It was finally at GWF's Valentines Vengeance 2008 where Chris O'Malley would lose his coveted GWF Championship in a hellacious Fatal 4 Way, but it was all okay because the Championship would be staying in the "Family" as stable mate SheltonSplash went on too win his historic first world's championship reign. This may have seemed like a huge dent in the career of CJ, but infact this led into another window of opportunity. Biased GWF owner Dave Shadow, insisted that one man alone could not hold two belts at the same time, even though this had happened before. Thus he stripped Cataclysm members SheltonSplash and James Murphy of their GWF Tag Team Championship's. Obviously Cataclysm, but Dave awarded James Murphy and Chris O'Malley the return title match. Though Shelton was furious about losing his half the tag championship's, it wasn’t long until new tag team champion's were made. Once again the "Family" remained Golden as CJ and James became the new tag champions , making CJ's goldenless state a short lived one. As GWF tag champions Chris and James were sat in the middle of a heated feud with two other teams fighting for their gold. These teams were Numbered Toast, consisting of the Number 4 and Michael Toaster. And the other team was one that the fans loved, and had already a proven history with all members of Cataclysm, they were Neo-Evolution. Kevin McAuliffe (Who Main Evented Rise and Fall II with James Murphy) and GWF Owner Dave Shadow (Who had previously stripped Cataclysm of the Tag Championships) Soon in amidst of all of this, Chris and James lost the tag titles too Numbered Toast, who would then drop them in the return match to Neo-Evolution. This was all happening in the run up too Rise and Fall III. In the run up to the mainstay of the GWF year, Chris had found himself on the upside of a winning opportunity, which would now lead him into a new light. One with possibly a Golden Future. CJ had won a slot in the upcoming Money in the Bank Ladder match at Rise and Fall, ironically a match where the winner would get a future GWF Championship shot, and yet it was Chris himself that proved even with this future advantageous championship shot, it was not destined you would win the championship, as it was only months ago he defeated Alex Storm when he was chasing in. Rise and Fall III night was a mixed bag for Cataclysm. James Murphy had gone to a draw with opponent and former rival Ken Davidson. And even though SheltonSplash had lost the GWF Championship, it seemed it still remained in Cataclysm's grasps, as Don Cicinni was quickly appointed the newest member of the faction. But yet it was Chris who managed to steal all the spotlight of the night for Cataclysm, as he rose high against many top caliber athletes of Damian DeNiro, Kevin McAuliffe, XO Malone, Michael Smart and Alan Christopher, and added another oh so impressive notch on his accolades becoming only the fourth man to be "Money in the Bank". Since then Chris has become the only lasting member of Cataclysm, as the group broke down with the sudden leaving of GWF Champion, Don Cicinni. The horrific Injury of former Champion, SheltonSplash and the betrayal of long time friend James Murphy. Yet still with all the chaos going on, Chris O'Malley has that all-important briefcase, in which he can strike.
