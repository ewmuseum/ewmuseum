The original Toaster vs. Number 4 match

GWF -> GWF Results and RPs -> GWF Archives The original Toaster vs. Number 4 match The Toaster Toaster... I started thinking of plans for a Toaster vs. Number 4 match ages ago in late 2007. This match was originally written in December and it was supposed to happen at the ECHW reunion show. When the brand split ended and I decided to team the two of them up, I decided that I wanted it to happen in GWF. That's also when I decided to add the Loser Leaves GWF stipulation. The original plan was for Number 4 to win and become my full-time character. It was only about a month ago that I decided to retire from RPing in general, so this match wasn't able to happen. I decided that I wanted Number 4 to retire unpinned and unsubmitted, so I changed the match from a normal match to a Toaster Match, where Toaster would win and retire anyway without Number 4 having to stay down. This match was also originally supposed to happen at Rise and Fall. Here's the original match. It has been unchanged since March. _____________________________________________________________________ Announcer: This match is a special match in which THE LOSER OF THE MATCH WILL LEAVE THE GWF! "Perfect Strangers" by Deep Purple hits the arena speakers and The Number 4 enters the arena to a loud mass of heat. This is the biggest match of his career, and the former GM of SND looks as confident as ever. The fans chant the name of his opponent, but they don't seem to be having any effect on The Number 4. When his music fades, the fans anxiously wait for his opponent, who will surely get a big ovation. "Jump" by Val Halen hits the speakers as well and one of the GWF's true legends, Metal Michael Toaster, enters the arena. Looking just as confident as his former protege in the ring, Toaster is all business as he walks down the long ramp. He enters the ring and looks as ready to fight as ever. Toaster charges right towards The Number 4 as the bell rings. Toaster hits The Number 4 with punch after punch, but The Number 4 endures them all, kicks Toaster in the gut, whips him to the ropes, and on the return, he nails Toaster with a Big Boot to the face! GWF Bill: Number 4 is trying to end it early here, it looks like. Number 4 picks Toaster up, gets him up in a powerbomb position, and takes him down with a powerbomb! Number 4 gets him up again before hitting powerbomb #2! He's not done. He repeats with the third powerbomb! The fans are afraid as Number 4 does the same thing, this time with a sit-down powerbomb! Toaster's back hits the mat with an unforgiving thud as the referee has no choice but to count. 1 ... 2 ... The fans are waiting for a shoulder to go up, a kickout to occur, anything to give Toaster a fighting chance. 3! The winner of this match, THE NUMBER 4! Bill: I can't believe it. We've just seen Toaster lose, dare I say, embarassingly. This is shocking! The Number 4 defeated his former mentor in record time! Alex: And not only that, but this was a Loser Leaves GWF match. We've just seen the end of Metal Michael Toaster's GWF career. Ben: It's about time too. Screw Toaster! Long live The Number 4! Bill: Ladies and Gentlemen, we're witnessing a very surreal moment in GWF history. "Perfect Strangers" by Deep Purple hits again and The Number 4 has his hand raises in victory. He poses on the turnbuckles to overwhelming boos as Toaster lies motionless on the canvas. Number 4 gives Toaster nothing but a glare as he leaves the arena. The fans throw trash in his direction until finally, he walks back through the curtain. Back in the ring, Toaster doesn't get up, address the fans, or anything. He just rolls to the outside of the ring and slowly walks up the ramp. He looks noticeably upset, but he manages to pick his head up as the fans cheer. Finally, he gets to the top of the ramp, raises his arms up one time to a standing ovation, and walks to the back. Rep Fad-Starter This looks a lot like a draft and it lacks any real emotion because the ending is summed up by the announcers in just a few short lines. The final product was much better and more drawn out.
