GWF -> Power 10 for March 27, 2009 Antite BBS created by Heretic of the IF Skin Zone . GWF -> GWF Extra stuff -> Power List Power 10 for March 27, 2009 , Brought to you by Chris Williams! Chris Williams I WILL NOT BACK DOWN!! Alright, without asking or getting permission from Toast (I just assumed he wouldn't care), I am reviving the Power List, although it will stay a Power 10 until more guys sign up. So, without further ado, here we go! 10. Mark Madison/JBW - While neither man competed in the first show back, both men were awarded a title shot. Mark, the former IX champion, will be competing for that International-Xtreme title that he holds so dear against the current champion, Baron Trotter. Meanwhile, JBW, a former GWF big-time veteran, is coming back with an instant Intercontinental title shot against "The Revenger" CJ O'Malley. 9. James Murphy - Falling in the opening round of the US Title tournament to longtime rival Kevin McAuliffe can't feel good. Can Murphy rebound this week with a surprise partner of his choosing and defeat the NeoEvolution team of McAuliffe and Chris Williams? 8. XO - "The Movement" founder XO lost in the opening round, but the man that defeated him, The Reprobate, turned out to become the runner-up in the US Title tournament. XO also has a few tricks up his sleeve, possibly involving Stan Vishis... be sure to keep an eye on them. 7. Robert Garland - Also losing in the opening round of the tournament was Robert Garland. However, he can take some solace in the fact that the man who bested him was none other than the man who bested them all - Star-Man. Also on the silver lining is some gold... a tag team title shot on SND tonight! Can Garland get the gold again? 6. Baron Trotter - No, Trotter did not have a match last week on FNB. However, the man remains the IX champion, and will have to defend his title next week against IX legend, Mark Madison. If he holds onto the gold, great things could be in store for Trotter. 5. Jeff Maximus - Just cracking into the top 5 is Jeff Maximus. He advanced into the semi-finals of the US Title tourney before falling to Star-Man, cutting his night short. However, Jeff is sure to be looking to make an impact on the rest of the tour, so we'll see what he has in store. 4. Kevin McAuliffe - His quest for US gold came up short, falling to Reprobate in the semi-finals. However, being in two matches in a row could not have hindered Kevin more. Tired and tuckered out, he was put away. This week, however, he has a chance to make a big statement teaming with Williams and put away the tag team duo of James Murphy and his surprise partner. 3. CJ - Also a man who did not compete was "The Revenger" CJ O'Malley. However, the guy everyone loves to hate is still GWF's Intercontinental champion, and that is something to be reckoned with. He has a chance to show this week whether the time off had an effect, as he is defending the IC title against John Beanshaw Wrathfield. 2. Reprobate - His journey to the finals of the United States tournament came up just short. He was narrowly defeated by Star-Man, due to some call to the gods of some sort. This sinister wrestler is sure to try and exact some sort of revenge this week, as he is set up for a rematch for the gold. Can Rep capitalize on his chance and become the United States Champion? #1 STAR-MAN - The man outlasted them all. 7 other men, 3 matches, 1 title. Star-Man fought tooth and nail, winning all three rounds and capturing the United States Championship Title. With gold around his waist and a target on his back, how will he fare this week, defending his newly won gold against none other than the man he bested to win it, The Reprobate. That's it for this week, watch SND tonight and see your favorites in action! ~Williams Rep Fad-Starter My debut on the power list and I'm just one spot shy of number one. BRRRRRRILIANT! SighOfRelief "The Revenger" No. 3 and I didn't even fight? :) I approve. Chris Williams I WILL NOT BACK DOWN!! I figure holding the title is definitely enough to warrant #3, but if any have other opinions of how to rank, lemme know. I wanna try and satisfy as many as possible with this system...
