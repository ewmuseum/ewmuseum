The arena lights dim, and the show returns from a tediously long commercial break. The Announcer climbs up the steel steps and makes his way in between the seconds and third ropes, before strolling into the middle of the ring. He begins to speak in a calm and assured voice. Announcer: The following contest is scheduled for one fall, and has a thirty minute time limit! It can be won through either pinfall, submission, or count out! The Announcer turns towards the entrance way, waiting. He then appears to get his cue, as he begins speaking again. Announcer: Introducing first, being accompanied to the ring by Robert Garland… From Washington D.C., and weighing in at two hundred and fourteen pounds; he is… AT FIRE! “Survival of the Sickest” by Saliva plays throughout the arena, as a huge explosion goes off. Flames burst upwards from the ramp, as the crowd go wild. Blue and gold spotlights flash all over the arena, before the arena lights themselves give off these colours. The crowd wait; anticipating… AT Fire makes his way onto the ramp from behind the curtain, and poses for the fans. He makes his way down the ramp, and at this point Robert Garland makes his way onto the stage. The crowd cheer for him, and the camera cuts back to AT Fire. He is shaking hands with and hugging fans as he gets closer to the ring. He makes his way into the ring, before quickly ascending up one of the turnbuckles and raising his arm. Again, the crowd go crazy for the Hall of Famer. The music fades, and Robert Garland wishes AT Fire good luck, before taking his place outside the ring. The Announcer makes his way into the middle of the ring once more, and readies himself to introduce the next competitor. Announcer: And his opponent, from London, England; weighing in at two hundred and forty two pounds… He is… JAMES MURPHY! “Stitches” by Allele hits the speakers, and James walks calmly onto the stage. The camera closes in on his face, and it is easy to tell that he is smirking. He raises his arms at either side of him, doing his patented “Murphy Pose”. To make it all the more dramatic, a wall of golden pyro falls behind him. James continues to do so for a number of seconds, as the jeers from the crowd grow louder and louder. He doesn’t seem to care, however, as he takes his time. He eventually drops his arms, before striding down to the rings. The fans look on in disgust, remembering some of the things that James Murphy has done in his career. He makes his way into the ring, and confidently leans on the turnbuckle, glaring across the ring at AT Fire, before switching his view to the ramp. The Announcer once again addresses the audience. Announcer: And finally, introducing tonight’s special guest referee… KEN DAVIDSON! “Siren” by Dale Oliver hits the speakers, and the crowd jump to their feet, cheering. Ken makes his way out from the back a few moments later, gathering more cheers. He is wearing his casual trousers and footwear, as well as his official referee shirt. He makes his way down to the ring, greeting his fans as he does so. He then climbs two of the four turnbuckles and poses, before making his way into the middle of the ring. The music dies down, and the Announcer leaves the ring, meaning that only AT Fire and James Murphy are left in there with him. He calls them in towards him, signalling for them to come over. AT strolls over straight away, not asking any questions. However, James takes longer to do so, as he is staring at Ken, not wanting to fall for any tricks that might be coming his way. However, soon enough, he too obliges, and Ken gives both men a brief talking to. He makes it clear that he wants a good, clean match. He then grins, before signalling for the bell to be rung. It rings, and the match has officially begun! GWF Bill: This match should be a cracker! Some of this company’s top talent will be on show here tonight! GWF Ben: I take it you’re talking about James Murphy there, Bill? GWF Alex: I think we’ll find that AT Fire is more a good match for Murphy, Ben. You don’t get into the Hall of Fame for just anything, you know! Ken Davidson steps slightly backwards, doing his best go get out of the way of the two competitors. James and AT Fire begin to circle around the ring, ready to pounce. Neither wants to make a mistake, so neither rushes to a decision on when to make their move. However, as they continue circling the gap between them inevitably grows smaller, and James stretches out an arm. AT accepts the invitation, and they lock up, before doing the same with the other arm. James has quite a large weight advantage, and is also a few inches taller than AT Fire, and after struggling briefly manages to push him back into a turnbuckle. He releases AT, and knees him in the stomach. Fire grabs the area where the knee went in, obviously in pain. James backs away, only to run forwards again, attempting a clothesline. AT Fire ducks it, and James’ arm collides with the turnbuckle. James turns around, trying to recover from his mistake, only to be met by a hugely athletic dropkick. James Murphy is once again sent reeling into the turnbuckle, before falling to his knees. AT thinks about attempting to connect with another offensive move, but James quickly clambers to his feet. AT Fire grins, and James smirks. Both men know they have a highly competitive match ahead of them. GWF Bill: Well, I think it can be said that James easily has the power advantage. However, AT Fire is one of the most agile people in this company’s history! Ken watches on, and James and AT Fire circle the ring once more, as Garland cheers on his partner from ringside.. Again they draw closer, and James again attempts to initiate a lock up. AT responds with by outstretching his arm and hitting a flying forearm smash! James clutches his face, but has no time to gather himself, as AT throws James against the ropes with an Irish whip! James comes back at great speed; AT attempts a spinning wheel kick! However, James ducks the attempt, and Fire crashes to the mat. AT holds his back in pain, and James quickly pounces, attempting the pinfall. Ken slowly makes his way over to the pinning attempt, and jumps to the ground. He raises his hand in the air to make the first count, before touching his face. He rolls around on the ground, screaming something along the lines of “MY EYE! AH, JESUS; THE PAIN!” The crowd chuckle, as does Garland, due to this obvious attempt to avoid making the count, and James quickly gets to his feet. GWF Ben: Ah, look at that! A man who goes on about playing by the rules won’t even make a fair count! This is what’s wrong with this company; people like him! How is James supposed to win this match if he can’t even get a one count? GWF Bill: The simple answer is… He’s not supposed to win; in Ken’s eyes, at least. GWF Alex: These are mind games at their finest, guys! Can you imagine what James Murphy must be feeling right now? As Alex Storm says this, James storms towards Ken, asking what’s going on. As James gets in Ken’s face, Ken points at the badge on his shirt, signalling that he’s in charge. Ken grins, knowing there is nothing James can do about it, as if he lays a hand on Ken, there’s a good chance he’ll be disqualified. James stares at Ken disapprovingly for a couple of moments longer… However, the staring contest is soon ended, as AT Fire rolls James up! Garland cheers his partner on, and Ken makes the count… … 1! … … 2! … NO! KICK OUT! James rolls out of the pin attempt and again focuses his anger on Ken, obviously feeling that it isn’t fair to count for only one competitor, and not the other. It also seems that the count was too fast for his liking. However, he soon turns back towards AT Fire. AT runs at him, going for a shoulder block, which successfully connects. James falls to the mat, again clutching his face, but AT wastes no time, and drags him to his feet. He lifts James high in the air, using all of his strength, and hits a devastating suplex! He ponders on whether to follow it up with a cover attempt briefly, but chooses not to, instead running towards the ropes and back, before jumping into the air and hitting a powerful leg drop! He hooks the leg, and again Ken is quickly down to make the count, as Garland cheers AT on. … 1! … … 2! … … 3! … … FOOT ON THE ROPE! … It appears that James managed to get his foot onto the bottom rope just in time to avoid being pinned. Ken Davidson has no choice but to let the match continue. GWF Ben: Even with all of the chips stacked against him, James still continues to show the resilience that is required to be great in this business. GWF Bill: AT Fire is handing James some real punishment out there! AT again pulls James to his feet, and James stands in the ring, groggily. Fire throws James against the ropes, and follows up, running after him. He attempts a clothesline! However, James stops at the ropes, and pulls the top rope down, sending AT crashing to the outside! AT Fire is on the outside, holding his head, obviously having hurt it on the way out. James points at Ken, telling him to start a count. Ken actually obliges this time, and James watches AT. … 1! … … 2! … …3! … … … … … What number comes next? :blink: … The crowd chuckle at Ken’s performance. As Garland watches on, so does James. He looks on blankly, and realises that this is a waste of time. He makes his way to the outside, and rolls AT back into the ring. Garland decides that enough is enough and begins hitting the canvas, attempting to get a chant going. The crowd oblige and begin willing AT to fight back. He manages to block a punch attempt from James, and hits a kick to the gut. He then hits a couple of punches of his own, which send James reeling. James is sent flying to the ropes with an Irish whip, and comes back to meet a Spinebuster! AT crushes James onto the mat, and the crowd are whipped into a frenzy! AT Fire takes a few steps back… Waiting for his moment… James begins to get to his knees… He runs forwards… Going for The Match Head ; trying to kick James square in the skull! No! James jumps to his feet and grabs AT’s head; going for a Gunnerdrop ! However, again AT counters! He pushes James into the turnbuckle, but James manages to stop himself before he collides with it… Only to be hit by a schoolboy pin! Ken is quickly down to the mat. A fast count!... … 1! … … 2! … … 3! … The match is over, and the Announcer climbs back into the ring. Garland too gets back into the ring. He congratulates AT Fire on his win. James is lying on the mat, looking upwards, stunned, as he’s lost some of his momentum on the last stop before Rise And Fall Three. Announcer: Here is your winner… AT FIRE! GWF Ben: James was duped by Davidson. That was a fast count. This is an outrage! GWF Alex: I think it’s safe to say James would have done exactly the same thing if the roles were reversed… GWF Bill: There you have it, folks. AT Fire has all the momentum in the world going into Rise And Fall Three! He can strike and win from any position, as we’ve just seen. AT Fire and Robert Garland make their way to the backstage area, leaving just James and Ken in the ring, as the Announcer has taken his seat at ringside. Ken grins and makes his way to the side of the ring, about to leave. However, he decides not to go, and turns around. James wanders towards him, and the two Rise and Fall Three opponents go nose to nose; eye to eye. Words are exchanged between the two, and finally Ken turns to leave. However, James pulls him back by his arm, and hits a devastating GUNNERDROP! Ken Davidson is laid out on the mat, as James Murphy stands “Murphy Posing” over his lifeless body. GWF Ben: If he does that this Sunday at Rise and Fall, Ken’s finished. If you remember Rise and Fall Two and what he did to Kevin McAuliffe… Well… Never turn your back on “Mr. Rise and Fall”… GWF Alex: If I know Ken Davidson, he won’t fall for the same trick twice. GWF Bill: I hope not, Alex. QUOTE Special thanks to Kevin McAuliffe Dave Shadow All your Sandwhich belong to me! Ladies and gentlemen, the following match is scheduled for one fall, and is a non title match up. Introducing first… "Success" begins to play as the lights in the arena start flashing red, white, and blue. The crowd begins to boo, knowing exactly who's coming out, as Damian DeNiro steps out from behind the curtain with his chain around his neck, and his United States Championship around his waist. He looks on at the crowd and throws his arms in the air as pyro goes off behind him, and he poses. Damian goes back into his normal posture and walks down the ramp slowly as the music plays… Bill: Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to yet another of our GWF.com exclusive matches, in which we will see a rematch of sorts from last week’s Friday Night Brawl. A champion vs champion match as well, as we continue our countdown to Rise and Fall 3. As Damian slides into the ring, "I Am" by Godsmack hits the speakers. GWF Champion Shelton Splash walks out through the curtains, his title firmly in hand. With a cocky smile on his face, he struts down the ramp, and hops up onto the apron. He stands, looking at Damian, as the crowd boo both men. Bill: Well, last week, Shelton managed to eliminate Damian DeNiro from the Champion of Champions match up, and following that, Damian went to Dave and requested another shot at the head champ. Alex : Damian does not like to lose, and he wants to prove he can hang with the big boys. Imagine what a psychological advantage he would gain over the other Money in the Bank participants if he managed to get a win over the champ tonight? Shelton and Damian hand their titles to officials outside the ring, as the ref signals for the bell to ring. Immediately, both men start circling the ring, starting to look for an opening to start things off. They move towards the center and both go for the collar and elbow tie up, testing their strength to begin with. The two are evenly matched, as they vie for some advantage in the middle of the ring, to no avail. The two break at the same time, and look at each other, both with little grins on their faces. The two know they are evenly matched, and start to try and think of a new strategy. Both men move back towards each other, but at the same time, the two go for kicks to the stomach, both connecting. Both men look at each other and turn, running at the ropes. On the rebound, they charge each other and both duck heads, expecting the other to go for a clothesline. Shelton stops and turns round, while Damian keeps running. Shelton tries to hit him with a jump kick as he comes back fast, but Damian sidesteps it. Damian swings a right hook, but Shelton ducks it. Damian swings a second, but Shelton dodges it again. Damian tries for a big boot, but Shelton steps to his side, and lets the US Champ go beside him. The two turn to face each other, before taking a step back, the crowd cheering. Bill: Wow, these two are even here tonight, neither man able to get an affective offence in yet on the other. They are going to have to come up with something new if they want to get the win here tonight. The two tie up again, but this time Damian manages to shift his weight. He grabs Shelton by the arm, and twists it round, applying an armlock on the World Champ. Damian releases the arm after a bit of tourqing it, and applies a headlock to his opponent. Shelton reaches his arm round Damian and leads him back into the ropes. Shelton whips Damian off, stopping the headlock, but as Damian hits the opposite ropes, he grabs hold and stops. Shelton charges him, but Damian drops his shoulder and throws Shelton up over the ropes. Shelton manages to grab the top rope, and hold on. He lands his feet on the apron, and lifts his foot over the rope, kicking Damian in the back of the head. Damian walks forward into the center of the ring, as Shelton jumps up and leaps forward, using the top rope to springboard himself. He lunges towards Damian, but as he does, DeNiro catches him with a big STO, slamming Shelton down on the mat. Shelton hits it hard and grabs the back of his head, as Damian goes for a cover… 1… 2… No! Shelton gets the shoulder up, though he still holds the back of his head. Damian throws a leg over Shelton, mounting him and starting to hit him with strong right punches to the head. Shelton pushes him off, lashing out with punches of his own, as Damian gets back to his feet. Shelton tries to get up, but as he does, Damian grabs him by the head, and starts dragging him over towards the corner. Damian pushes Shelton into the corner, and hits a European uppercut, snapping Shelton’s head back. Shelton retaliates by nailing a stiff knife edge chop to the chest of the US champ. Damian tries hitting another uppercut, but Shelton manages to hit another chop to the chest. Damian staggers back out into the middle of the ring, as Shelton follows after him. He jumps and grabs Damian’s head, hitting an inverted bulldog. Damian hits the canvas face first, as Shelton stays on the ground for the moment, the earlier STO still showing effects on him. The crowd start booing loudly, as XO and Sinister Minster walk out through the curtains at the top of the ramp, and start making their way down to the ring. XO walks up to the ring, and starts slamming his hands down, trying to tell Damian to get up. Bill: And here comes The Movement! How is Shelton going to get round this one? Shelton is up before Damian, and spots XO and Minister. He tells them to stay the hell out of his match, as he grabs Damian and picks him up to his feet. Shelton hits a knee to the gut, before lifting Damian up onto his shoulders. In the middle of the ring, Shelton spins him forward and brings him crashing down with a Termination Rock Bottom. Shelton grabs the leg and goes for the cover… 1… 2… No! Damian kicks out, much to Shelton’s disgust. Shelton sits up and fixes his knee pads, before getting back to his feet, and heading over towards the ropes. He climbs out onto the apron and starts to walk over towards the corner. As he does, the Sinister Minster gets up on the apron on the far side of the ring. The ref runs over to try and get him down, but as he does, XO gets up on the apron beside Shelton. Shelton hits him with a big punch, and sends XO back down to the mats outside. XO lands on his feet however, but Shelton isn’t finished with him. When XO turns back round, Shelton runs along the apron and tries to hit a Lou Thez press on him. XO side steps though, and Shelton lands on the ground knee first He immediately starts screaming, clutching his knee. He swears loudly, as the Minister gets down off the apron. The ref walks to the ropes and sees Shelton down on the ground. The ref starts giving out to XO, who starts saying that Shelton fell, and that he had nothing to do with it. Alex: Looks like Shelton could be seriously hurt there. He dropped down onto his knees, and those mats don’t give all that much protection from the concrete floor underneath! The ref starts screaming, and points at XO and Minister before pointing at the back. Bill: Looks like the ref is ejecting XO and Minister from ringside! XO doesn’t look upset though. He stares at Shelton who lies on the ground, holding his leg in pain, as Damian rolls under the bottom rope. Damian pats XO on the shoulder, and tells him he can go, before walking over to Shelton. XO heads backs up the ramp, as Damian starts hitting Shelton with some vicious kicks to the leg, taking every advantage he can find. He grabs Shelton by the head, and picks him up, throwing him into the ring under the bottom rope. He follows him in, and starts hitting him with even more kicks to the seemingly injured leg of Shelton. He grabs the leg, and drags him back to the ropes, placing the leg on the second rope. He takes a step back and jumps forward, hitting a knee drop across Shelton’s leg. Shelton grabs it and continues screaming; as Damian smells blood in the water and the end of the match near at hand. He grabs Shelton by the head, and tires to pick him up. As he does though, Shelton grabs the head and rolls him forward, applying a small package. 1… 2… No! Damian kicks out of the hold, and jumps back to his feet. Shelton tries to use the ropes to get up as well, but as he does, Damian ducks down and hits a shoulder lunge to the back of Shelton’s leg, taking him back down again. Shelton looks to be in a lot of pain, as Damian grabs his leg and drags him to the middle of the ring, pulling as fiercely as he can. He kicks Shelton in the face when he tries to sit up, before heading over to the corner. He lifts himself onto the second rope, and starts goading his opponent to get to his feet. Shelton rolls over onto his stomach and tries pushing himself up. He gets to one knee and lifts himself to his feet, although it looks to be a major ordeal to stay standing vertically. Damian lunges forward, looking for his “Jesus Piece” clothesline, but Shelton kicks him in the gut on his way down with his sore leg. Damian starts to keel over when Shelton grabs his head and connects with the DDT!!! Damian lies flat out, as Shelton rolls ontop of him, holding his knee with a spare hand. 1… 2… 3!!! HERE IS YOUR WINNER: SHELTON SPLASH! Shelton rolls off Damian, and sits up, as "I Am" begins to play through the arena. He grabs at the ropes to help himself up, his arm raised, as Damian slowly rolls out of the ring under the bottom rope. Bill : Well, Shelton wins, but at what cost? He seems to have banjaxed his leg somehow. Shelton grabs the title off the ref, and moves to the ropes to celebrate. As he does, Damian stands outside the ring, holding his head and seething at the mouth. He grabs a chair, and slides back into the ring behind Shelton. With his back still turned, Damian runs at Shelton and drives the chair into the back of his injuried leg. Bill: HEY! What the hell is he doing? Damian stands over Shelton as he rolls round on the ground, clutching his leg in pain. Damian drops and rolls out under the bottom rope, and starts heading back up the ramp, visibly pissed off by the result. The crowd boo, as medics dash down the ramp to check on the champion. Alex: Damian is a dangerous opponent. Despite the final result, I think Damian showed he can hang with the big boys, and is a strong contender for the Money in the Bank match. Bill : I just hope Shelton is ok to compete on Sunday. Something tells me that somewhere backstage, Don is smiling to himself! The medics try to check on Shelton who holds his leg, as Damian stands at the top of the ramp; he smiles looking back at Shelton before heading through the curtains.
