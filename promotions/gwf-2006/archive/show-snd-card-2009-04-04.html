GWF -> SND card for April 4th. Antite BBS created by Heretic of the IF Skin Zone . GWF -> GWF.com -> GWF.com SND card for April 4th. The Toaster Toaster... Like I said in the last card topic, for any former GWFers still wanting to participate in this tour, sign-ups are still open. On Friday Night Brawl, we crowned a new United States Champion in Star-Man. But that tournament was only the beginning of the reunion tour. This week, new GWF owner The Number 4 will return to the show he ran for a number of months - Saturday Night Dogfight. Numerous wrestlers are set to make their returns to the GWF this week, and a few titles will be defended as well. Promos must be posted by Friday, April 3rd at 3PM EST(8PM that night for UKers). Maximum of TWO per competitor. "You Fucking Own Me!" Match Chris "CJ" O'Malley© vs. John Beanshaw Wrathfield JBW made his surprise return to GWF on FNB, interrupting The Number 4 going off on a few GWF legends. They were both then interrupted by Chris "CJ" O'Malley, the Intercontinental Champion. JBW and CJ have some history with each-other. They had a lengthy feud over the International-Xtreme Championship from late 2006 until early 2007. They even shared the IX Championship for a time, but due to many of their matches being multi-man, there was never a proper ending. Next Saturday, the feud is revisited as CJ and JBW square off one more time. Of course, this match has a twist. GWF has taken a classic match type as has modified it a little bit. This match will will be a "You Fucking Own Me!" Match, which is just an "I Quit" match with a different thing being said to declare your loss. The loser of the match must declare that his opponent, does in fact, fucking own him...as opposed to just quitting. Will CJ prove that JBW belongs in the lowercard, or will JBW prove himself and win the biggest title of his career? United States Championship Christina Hernandez will be locked in a shark cage at ringside Star-Man© vs. The Reprobate On FNB, Star-Man won a rigorous tournament to capture the prestigious title and will make his first title defense one week from Saturday. Who better for an opponent than the man who came close to winning the final round of the tournament, The Reprobate? These two men will battle it out once again on SND, and Christina will be locked in a small cage at ringside as it happens. GWF Tag Team Championship Josh the Jersey Boy & Robert Garland vs. XO & Stan Vishis Last Friday, these four men were informed that there were "plans" in place for them. Those plans have been revealed, and it involves championship gold. The KKK, made up of Robert Garland and Josh the Jersey Boy, will take on two former Movement members, XO and Stan Vishis. Although XO and Stan were only together in the group for a number of days, they'll be forced to reunite against the well-established former Tag Team Champions, Robert Garland and JJB. NOTE: In order for this match to end up going as planned, both members of at least one team have to promo. An SND legend returns home Chris Williams & Kevin McAuliffe vs. James Murphy & A Partner of His Choice(?) This Saturday, two feud will be revisted, revenge will try to be sought, and a team will reunite...all in one match! Kevin McAuliffe, who was, in a way, taken out of the U.S. Championship tournament by James Murphy, will team with his former Neo-Evolution partner, Chris Williams...and the two will take on James Murphy himself and any partner of his choice. In addition to the long history of problems between James and Kevin....Chris Williams himself has a bone to pick as well. James Murphy [thought he] ended the career of the former United States Champion last Summer. The two were set to do battle at Rise & Fall before the incident. Neo-Evolution will reunite against James Murphy and anyone he wants to team up with. NOTE: If James doesn't select a partner by Tuesday, one will be selected for him and that person will be informed of it that same day, to give him time to promo. International-Xtreme Championship Baron Trotter© vs. Mark Madison Last week, Baron Trotter was happy to find out that Josh the Jersey Boy would NOT be the first challenger for the IX Championship. He seemed equally as happy when it was revealed that former champ Mark Madison WOULD be the #1 contender for the title. According to Baron, Mark looks old, worn, and out of shape. Is he? Mark has a chance to prove the champion wrong when he and Baron go one-on-one for the title.
