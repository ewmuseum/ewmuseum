GWF Insider: Reunion Edition

28 March 2009

GWF -> GWF Insider: Reunion Edition Antite BBS created by Heretic of the IF Skin Zone . GWF -> GWF Extra stuff -> GWF Insider GWF Insider: Reunion Edition The Toaster Toaster... GWF.com recently sent out some correspondents to snoop around and find any big news and rumors even remotely related to GWF. After a while of snooping, here's what they came up with. The World Championship After the GWF Arena closed down and the fans, wrestlers, and staff were gone...our correspondent, who just happens to be a new arena janitor, found a GWF Championship belt lying in a cardboard box under The Number 4's desk. It's unknown how long the belt had been there for, if it was real or just a replica, or if it'll ever even show up on television, but we know the belt is...or at least, was, in the area. Our man snatched the title and walked away with it. Job Openings A few hours before revealing himself as the new owner of GWF, The Number 4 got his own section in the Classified section of a local newspaper. He claimed to have a few office jobs available for anyone who wanted them. Rumor has it that three faces familiar to GWF fans are interested in returning to the company and this may be the way they do it. Proper Name? Before being allowed to take her spot behind the microphone, backstage announcer EVE Eve had to re-sign her GWF contract; a contract which was left in a pile of non-wrestler contracts in The Number 4's office and included her last name in her signature. Remember - Eve is the younger sister of GWF Bill, so if our correspondent ever responds to our phone calls, the three year-old mystery of what GWF Bill's last name is may finally be answered. Of course, this is the same guy who stole the GWF Championship so who knows if he could be trusted. The mystery remains...for now. A Death in the family Hardcore GWF fans will remember G-Ziss, the religious powerhouse who, with his televangelist manager, The Reverend, briefly worked for the promotion in 2006. We're sad to report that G-Ziss passed away this past Tuesday, March 24th. He was 37 years old. The cause of death has not been determined yet. The MNM curse strikes again? GWF's website hasn't updated its "Tour Dates" section since listing the return of Monday Night Massacre. Could it be that the infamous Monday show will once again put its curse to work and wind up putting the GWF to rest for good? EVE to be part of the reunion? Despite the fact that "Metal" Michael Toaster declined to be part of the GWF reunion, The Number 4 is apparently interested in paying tribute to his former tag team partner by making one reunion tour show dedicated to GWF's former developmental territory, Extremely Violent Entertainment. Fans have been wanting classics such as the Purgatory Pain Match and the Glass Attic Match to return since the originals a number of years ago. Is this a possibility? Especially since only two former EVE competitors are in the reunion? Two rings and loads of steel mesh. Our arena correspondent found two rings stored in a large room of the GWF Arena, with a bunch of pieces of steel mesh near them. This reporter is sure the GWF fans know what legendary match type features such structures. We'll keep you updated. That's all for this week's edition of GWF Insider. We'll be back next week when our new snooper checks out the backstage happenings during the taping of Saturday Night Dogfight!
