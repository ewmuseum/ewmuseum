The GWF Spirit Squad Basic Information: TIMEY! WOLFIE! TADGY! AZZY! LODY! In the very early days of GWF, these five names were the biggest stable in the GWF. In the days when GWF was meant to be a fun little coby of WWE, this male cheerleading squad, made up of Timey1, Wolfenstein, Tadg30, Azz_Vacuna, and Lodis3, primarily faced off aganst one superstar a week in a handicap match. Their short-lived GWF career included victories over legendary GWF jobber Shadowprince, and GWF legend John Beanshaw Wrathfield(by countout). On the 3rd episode of FNB taking place on May 5th of 2006, the group suffered a low blow and Wolfenstein left. In a match known as the Spirit Breaker match, Wolfie lost to Tadg. The rest of the group then went on to lose to DX. This was the end for good. Aftermath: After the breakup of the Spirit Squad, Azz and Timey faded away from the GWF. Lodis3 participated in the first 500-post Hardcore Battle Royal and became Hardcore Champion three times before he left GWF as well. Tadg went on to face Shelton Splash for the Intercontinental Championship at the first Rise and Fall. He lost and also faded away from GWF. Wolfenstein, however, achieved great success. He and former Spirit Squad opponent JBW formed the Billionaires Club and had a reign as GWF Tag Team Champions. He then became GWF Champion, and went onto become the first Undisputed Champion in GWF History. Wolfenstein and Tadg went on to be inducted into the GWF Hall of Fame. Not became of their days in the Squad, most likely. Legacy: The Spirit Squad will probably be most remembered for being Wolfenstein's first venture in GWF. In the long run, the group as a whole as unsuccessful and will remembered as another WWE clone in the early days of GWF, The Toaster Toaster... The Ministry of Darkbread History: Mark Madison saw himself in the old Hall of Shame numerous times. When he debuted in GWF, he was the Underbaker of the famous food division. Underbaker wasn't a gimmick full of shame, but his first stable wasn't a very good one. Underbaker recruited two newcomers to join him. One was named Todd Caroon, who once attacked a priest. The other was Biscotti 2 Hotty, who's infamous for his time in GWF. With manager Paul Cooker, this group did...well.....nothing really. They did have a win under their belts, as Todd Caroon won a battle royal full of other GWF superstars to keep his job with the company. Unfortunately, this was his first AND last match. The group did nothing of interest and will go down as just another short-lived group in GWF history. Aftermath: Underbaker went onto become Mark Madison, former International-Xtreme and Hardcore Champion. Biscotti went on to do some more infamous things in GWF. Todd Caroon.......did nothing and disappeared from GWF. The Toaster Toaster... Biscotti 2 Hotty: Chemotherapist History: Biscotti 2 Hotty, while part of the Ministry of Darkbread, decided to bring a young GWF fan with cancer into the ring. He killed him. That's just about it............ Legacy: It wasn't anything huge, but this incident is arguably the most famous moment in the history of GWF promo's. It's still talked about fairly often to this day, and will probably be talked about as long as the guys who where there continue to bring up the early days of GWF. The Toaster Toaster... The Food Division History: Toast Hogan was a great GWF Cruiserweight Champion, and yes, his gimmick was that of a Hulk Hogan ripoff. In June/July of 2006, a bunch of other people followed Toast Hogan's lead and created food-related parodies of WWE superstars. Among them were The Roll, The Underbaker, Bread "Wheatman" Tart, King Cookie, Kake, Biscotti 2 Hotty, and MaskedNacho. All these men were after Toast Hogan and the Cruiserweight title. People even petitioned for the CW title to be renamed the GWF Food Division title, since they, along with Toast, were the only one who cared about the belt. Despite what people say, Toast Hogan WAS NOT part of the food division. He inspired it, but he wasn't in it. The division died in early August when Shelton Splash banished them all. Aftermath The Underbaker went on to become Mark Madison and achieved great success in GWF. The Roll went on to become Chris "CJ" O'Malley, a former World, IX, and Tag Team Champion. Toast Hogan, the man who inspired, but WAS NOT PART OF the division, dropped his gimmick following the demise of the Food Division and became Metal Michael Toaster, winning the World, United States, and Tag Team Championships. Other than the infamous Biscotti, none of the other food division guys achived a damn thing in GWF.....although Bread Tart became an announcer for about two weeks. Legacy: The food division will be known as arguably the highest point of the Cruiserweight title, in addition to the starting point of Mark Madison and CJ.
