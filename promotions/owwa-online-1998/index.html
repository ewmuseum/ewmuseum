Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

OWWA's only surviving document is its own recruitment handbook, posted in early December 1998. It presents itself as “an electronic wrestling federation dedicated to bringing you loads of action and well thought out angles each and every week,” already “open for more than six months now,” and recovering from “a period when the federation was temporarily closed” before returning, in its own words, “back and better than ever.”

By its own account it ran two weekly shows, Tuesday Night Terror (the bigger of the two, up to eight matches) and Friday Night Fury, which it compared to “a 'WCW Thunder' show,” plus a monthly pay-per-view of up to ten matches. It carried four championships: a World Title open to any weight class, a Hardcore Title defended weekly in a gimmick match with no non-title matches permitted, a Cruiserweight Title reserved for wrestlers 235 pounds and under, and a Tag Team Title.

Matches were decided by a booking committee reading submitted roleplays for quality rather than simulated by stat. A handler could hold up to four wrestlers, two of whom had to form a tag team, and the handbook insisted on original characters, naming the knockoffs it did not want to see by name: “I don't want to see any 'Stone Cold' Alex Austins or Coldbergs.” Swearing was permitted, but kept, in its own words, to “words that you'd hear on Nitro or Raw.”

No card, roster, or later notice from the promotion survives.
