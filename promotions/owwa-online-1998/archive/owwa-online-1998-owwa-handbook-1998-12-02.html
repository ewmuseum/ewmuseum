The OWWA is an electronic wrestling federation dedicated to bringing you loads of action and well thought out angles each and every week. We've been open for more than six months now, and although we faced a period when the federation was temporarily closed, we are back and better than ever. We are a booked fed, which means that matches are written out instead of simulated. Booked feds offer more rewards for good roleplaying than do simulated feds, and in my opinion are more fun as well. 2.0 The Federation 2.1 Rules & Regulations Each handler may handle up to four wrestlers, but all four wrestlers must be kept active. Also, if a handler handles four wrestlers, two must be a tag team (but can still compete in singles). Original wrestlers only. I don't want to see any "Stone Cold" Alex Austins or Coldbergs. A wrestler constitutes as a cruiserweight if he is 235 lbs. or under. Don't accuse the booking committee of fixing matches, because it will get you kicked out. Also, don't ask me to fix a match for you. You agree to roleplay at least once per week. There are two cards per week, so one roleplay a week won't suffice if you want to win matches. Booking is based on quality and not quantity of roleplays, so be sure to write good roleplays when you write one. The OWWA Hardcore Title will be defended at least once a week in a gimmick match. There is a four wrestler OR two handler minimum when creating a stable. Swearing is allowed, but please keep it to words that you'd hear on Nitro or Raw. If you don't hear it on those shows, please censor it, or better yet, try and not use those words. The message board is for roleplaying. Please don't express yourself on the message board, or advertise on the message board. 2.2 Titles The OWWA has four titles. The OWWA World Title is the biggest prize in this federation. The World Title can be held by a wrestler in any weight class, and must be defended at least once a month. The OWWA Hardcore Title is another title that can be held by any weight class. The Hardcore Title will always be defended in gimmick matches, and must be defended at least once a week. There are no non-title matches in the Hardcore division. The OWWA Cruiserweight Title can only be held by those wrestlers that constitute as a Cruiserweight (weight under 235 pounds). This is the title for the high flyers. The last belt is the OWWA Tag Team Titles. The Tag Titles can be held by any team with any weight. 2.3 Title Contention Title Contention is a very important part of the OWWA standings. The number one contender for a belt will be granted a title shot. All others must beat the champion in a non-title match first. The Hardcore Title has no non-title matches, so the champion must defend against all challengers. 2.4 Weekly Shows There are two weekly shows in the OWWA, Tuesday Night Terror and Friday Night Fury. Terror is the bigger show, often featuring up to eight action packed matches, including an explosive main event. Fury is more of a "WCW Thunder" show. Fury will include matches and interviews as well, but will be a smaller card. 2.5 Pay-Per-Views Once a month, the OWWA will hold a pay-per-view, a huge show that features up to ten matches. Most, if not all, of the titles will be defended at the pay-per-view. Pay-Per-Views are where a wrestler can really prove his worth. 2.6 Roleplaying/Flashing Roleplaying is by far the most important part of the OWWA. A wrestler must roleplay at least once a week in order to remain active in the OWWA. Roleplays are what determine if you win or lose an upcoming match, and are based on quality rather than quantity. In roleplaying, your goal is to get your point across while remaining in character. Proper english and good description makes a good roleplay. Swearing, as is stated in the rules, is allowed, but it is frowned on, and will probably hurt your roleplay quality rather than help it. 2.7 Booking The booking committee is a group of handlers who have been chosen to help run the league. The booking committee determines the winners of matches by reading all of the roleplays, and determining which wrestler had a better roleplay. The bookers are impartial, and are the second to final word in who wins matches. 2.8 The Staff The OWWA Staff is short but sweet. I am the president of the league, and also write one card a week. The other card is written by Slade, a member of the booking committee. The other booker is Mike, a dedicated handler who was one of the originals in the OWWA. 2.9 Other The two things in this section are angles and alignment. First, angles. An angle is your wrestler's plan on action, and how he interacts with the other wrestlers in a certain period of time. For example, Joe Blow is feuding with The Crow because The Crow cost Joe Blow the Hardcore Title. That would be an angle. Angles help your push, and a good angle makes everything so much more fun. The other thing is alignment. Alignment is whether your wrestler is a good guy, a face, or a bad guy, a heel. There are also neutral people, who are in the middle between good an bad, called tweeners. An example of a face would be Lex Luger. He gets cheered by the crowd, and feuds with heels. And example of a tweener would be "Stone Cold" Stevie Austin. He gets cheered by the crowd, but is not above cheating to win a match. A heel would be someone like "Hollywood" Hogan, someone who will do anything it takes to win, and gets booed by the crowd. 3.0 Creating a Character 3.1 Character Creation Your character can be based on a real life wrestler, but please allow them to have differences as well. Like I said earlier, I don't want to see a "Stone Cold" Alex Austin doing the Stone Cold Stunner and running around screaming "can I get a hell yeah?!" and "stomping mudholes in people". Please try to make your wrestler as original as possible. As for physical attributes, take into consideration that a 500 lb. giant is going to move real slow, and he won't be doing a hurricanrana for his finisher. The opposite applies as well. A 200 lb. Cruiserweight isn't going to use a gorilla press as his finisher. Please just try to make wrestlers realistic. 3.2 Application Please fill out the following application and mail it to me at [detail withheld] with the subject "OWWA App".
