Whiplash (3 April 2008)

3 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWC Presents WHIPLASH Thursday, April 3rd, 2008 Taco Bell Arena Boise, ID [In the bottom right hand corner of the screen, it reads "Whiplash: Last Week". We fade into a black and white shot of Kenji Gosenkugi pounding away on Road Block.]
Beav: They’re eating this up! Kenji is on fire! Mouth: Not for long! [Kenji is clearly intent on demolishing Road Block as he sends Road Block into the corner and follows him in for a splash. Mouth: Oh no! He’s going for the finish! Beav: Road Block's DEAD! HE'S DEAD! Kirsta: Ty! No! It's not worth it...not tonight... [A few staff members run over and step between Boyd and Cross. Boyd's staring him down while three men hold him back, while Cross.]
Cross: You want a fight so bad? Meet me out in the ring...any place, any time. Boyd: Be careful what you ask for... [Cut back to ringside.] --MAIN EVENT - TAG TEAM MATCH - Ryu Nakajima & Kenji vs. Brad Jackson & James Lee-- Beav: I'm not sure what it is, but Tyler Boyd's been a bit on edge around here lately... Mouth: He's protective of his woman! What's wrong with that? Beav: What's WRONG with that? He's not even on the durn roster, and he's picking fights backstage every week it seems!
Mouth: He's "The Main Damn Attraction," Beav! He can do whatever the hell he wants! Beav: Well all I know is that we're down to our Main Event tonight, and this should be a heckuva tag team contest!
Mouth: This should be entertaining to say the least. Beav: Durn right, this is going to be a memorable night for CWC right here, three of the top dogs, in one match!
[The opening strings of “Getting High” by Ian Brown ring out around the arena and the crowd instinctively give a negative reaction as the lights dim down. The big screen provides the only light, and shows Ryu Nakajima standing in an empty room wearing a suit and shades. The camera zooms in slowly until just after the guitars kick in, when it switches to a montage of him in action. A burst of red pyros bit the house lights back up as we see Ryu Nakajima standing in the centre of the entryway, wearing a long white silk coat over his ring attire of red wrestling pants with silver flashes. The music wavers, and then blends seamlessly into "X" by X-Japan as Kenji Gosenkugi joins his partner on the ramp.]
Cassandra: Making their way to the ring now, at a combined weight of 441 pounds... RYU NAKAJIMA AND KENJI GOSENKUGI!!!
[Ryu strides quickly down the ramp ignoring the crowd, and leaps over the top rope. He climbs onto the far turnbuckle and surveys the crowd, before taking of his long coat and carefully rolling it up in the corner of the ring. He makes a signal for the referee to keep an eye out, and does a few stretches in preparation for the bell while Kenji hops up on the apron, pulling on the top rope.]
Beav: What’s up with Ryu? What a snob, look at him snubbing those fans. [The lights dim, heavy mist filling the ramp as strobe lights begin to pulse slowly. A steady bass throb begins, growing in volume, sounding much like a heartbeat. A single gunshot shatters the silence, followed by mocking laughter and Jackson's voice hurling insults before the music skips, and then the sounds of "Lies" by Evanescence filters through the speakers. Dark red strobes pulsate on the entranceway, and a dark figure moves among them, stepping forward as indigo fountains of pyrotechnic spark either side of him. He strides forwards, ignoring the crowd reaction. With a feedback squeal, the music bleeds into "Ghost Riders in the Sky" and James Lee comes out behind Jackson, looking almost ecstatic.]
Cassandra: Making their way to the ring now, the challengers... at a combined weight of 487 pounds... the team of JAMES LEE AND JACKSON!!
Mouth: Jackson does not look impressed at this tag situation. Beav: Can you blame him? James Lee's only wrestled a single match here, and rumor has it that was his first one in his professional career.
[Jackson circles the ring once, his eyes steady, a look of angry concentration on his face, before ascending the ring steps and climbing up on the apron.]
Mouth: Gotta wonder how much Jackson is fearing the outcome of this. Beav: Why? Jackson is a celebrated champion, on a hot streak! He's going to Acension as the tournament favorite and Lee is southern! Together they’re unstoppable.
Mouth: Rednecks, they know how to fight. Right, Beav? Beav: Lee and Kenji kicking off the match. This is going to blow the roof off the place! [Lee springs into action, going for a fast clothesline but Kenji ducks and follows up with a double-arm DDT. Kenji waits for Lee to get up, and they immediately collide in the middle of the ring, locked into a test of strength. Kenji lashes out with his foot, connecting a kick to the knee that sends Lee down. Kenji backs off, giving Lee the chance to regain his footing as he makes the tag to Ryu.]
Beav: Ryu is the legal man now, probably his first time for that. Mouth: Don’t quit your day job, Beav. You're a sucky comedian. What the hell is Ryu doing?
[Lee grabs Ryu, attempting to send him packing to the ropes with an Irish whip, but Ryu resists, pulling Lee off balance and right into his arms. He locks around Lee’s midsection and lifts him up, squeezing with a bear hug before flipping Lee backwards with a snap suplex. Ryu scrambles to his feet and collides with Lee, slamming into him with a shoulder block, and then takes him down with an armdrag takedown, and immediately locking on a crossface hold. Lee slams an elbow into Ryu’s sternum and strains for the corner, missing Brad Jackson’s outstretched hand by inches as Ryu pulls him back.]
Beav: Ryu is all over Lee, schooling that rookie in a technical supershow. Mouth: He’s all over the rookie like white on rice. Jackson's freaking out! Beav: I like Ryu Nakajima, his sheer physicality makes him a force to be reckoned with.
[Lee struggles towards the corner again, breaking out of the hold. Ryu grabs Lee from behind and executes a pump handle suplex on him. Lee is down and clearly feeling the impact with a look of surprise on his face. Lee shakes his head, trying like hell to clear the cobwebs. Ryu capitalizes, on the turnbuckle now and leaps off onto Lee with a perfectly timed leg drop. Ryu crawls over Lee and hits him with a back fist as he climbs back to his feet. He pulls Lee up by the hair and connects with a vertical suplex with amazing hang time, driving Lee almost through the mat. Ryu strolls over to his corner, and makes the tag to Kenji. Kenji goes to the top rope, and with a boost from Ryu, does a flip and splashes down on Lee.]
Mouth: Now that’s teamwork! These two are a well-oiled machine of tag team wrestling! Beav: I can’t believe it, Lee is battling back! [Lee breaks out of Kenji’s attempt for a facelock, and fires off a few punches, driving Kenji back towards Jackson, who’s standing on the second rope, leaning into the ring. Lee spins Kenji around and sends him packing towards the corner, following in with a corner splash and a quick tag to Jackson.]
Mouth: You have to give credit where credit is due, and Brad Jackson is one of the most technically sound wrestlers in this business.
Beav: For a psycho. Mouth: Period. Beav: Fine, for a psycho. Period. [Brad scoops Kenji up and levels him with an inverted atomic drop, following up with a few well-placed kicks to the ribs. Kenji grabs Jackson’s foot and hauls him off balance and right into a victory roll.]
Mouth: Count it! [The ref hits two and Jackson manages to roll through into his own pinfall, but after the one count he releases the hold, getting to his knees in the corner and right into a thumb to the eye courtesy of Ryu. Brad is back on his feet in an instant, one eye squinted shut as he circles Kenji warily. Brad feints towards the corner, lunging at Ryu who hops down off the apron, and then turns as he springs forward, slamming a vicious looking uppercut into Kenji’s jaw. Kenji shakes it off and fires back with one of his own that Brad barely dodges in time. Jackson explodes with fury, slamming a series of vicious punches into Kenji's chest and gut, sending him back against the ropes into his corner, where Lee waits. Lee slaps Jackson on the shoulder and dives between the ropes, taking Kenji down with a chopblock to the knees.]
Mouth: Oh man! Jackson looks pissed. He was manhandling Kenji all over the ring, and Lee just stole the tag.
Beav: Trouble in paradise. [Lee connects with a swinging bulldog on Kenji, driving his face into the mat. He floats over for the cover, and gets a quick two.]
Beav: Kenji manages to kick out. Lee looks confused; I think he thought this was in the bag! Look, he’s backing off to pick his next opening.
Mouth: Not backing off, he’s running scared. Look, Kenji's stalking him! [Kenji gets back to his feet, looking slightly worse for wear, and Lee charges in, only to have Kenji hook his arsm and level him with an underhook suplex. Lee gets up, staggering and stumbling before trying to crawl towards his partner, but Kenji pulls him back, applying a kneelock submission hold, before turning it over into a side leg lock. Jackson storms the ring, and breaks it up with a well-timed kick to Kenji’s face. Ryu leaps in and drives Jackson back, demolishing him with a Suplex 66 just as Kenji slips out of Lee’s attempt at a Samoan drop and nails a wicked neckbreaker DDT.]
Beav: Look at that shameless showboating. The crowd’s not liking it. Mouth: Can you blame them? [Jackson holds out his hand, and Lee takes it, shaking firmly. A flicker of something crosses Jackson’s face as Lee pulls his hand back, only to have Jackson grab him and send him packing into Kenji's grip.]
Beav: Jackson just fed his partner to the enemy! Mouth: WHAT THE HELL?! Beav: I’m disgusted. This is not how a champion acts. [Kenji launches himself and connects with Lee’s head, locking in the facelock and slamming him down on the unforgiving canvas with a dragon stunner. Lee rolls through the ropes to the floor and Ryu leaps off the apron and connects with a flying elbow to the back of Lee’s neck before rolling him into the ring.]
Mouth: Wait! Who’s the legal man? Beav: Kenji is, and he’s going for the pin, right here. Gakkarida! This is a travesty! I can’t believe Jackson is going to stand for this underhanded mess!
Mouth: Get over it. Swerves sell seats. [Kenji drops for the pin and Jackson gets between the ropes, and bodily pulls Kenji off Lee. He sends Kenji down hard with a gutwrech powerbomb, and turns around, right into a dropkick to the knee that he blocks at the last second, making the mistake of using his injured arm to do so. The kick connects solidly, and Jackson crumbles, his face bone white with pain as he slides out of the ring, sitting down hard on the floor.]
Beav: Jackson's down and out. [Kenji rolls Lee into a single leg crab, using the ropes for leverage, and hooking the back of Lee's tights for good measure. He gets the three count with ease while Ryu stands over Jackson, who's showing no signs of getting back up from his crouch on the mat.]
Mouth: I feel sick. Beav: Kenji and Ryu steal themselves a win, at Jackson's expense. Cassandra: Your winners, RYU NAKAJIMA AND KENJI GOSENKUGI!!! [WINNER VIA PINFALL: Nakajima & Gosenkugi]
