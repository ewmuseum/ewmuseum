The Big Bang Theory (31 January 2008)

31 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Live from the MGM Grand, Las Vegas, NV, 8:00 p.m. Eastern Standard Time Thursday, January 31st, 2008 --Let the Show Begin-- [The scene opens up in the casino of the MGM Grand in Las Vegas, Nevada, and says “Earlier Today” on the top of the screen, with the CWC logo in the bottom left hand corner. The camera pans around, and in one of the lounge areas, we see the Council Chairman Bryan Baxman sitting in a corner booth looking across the table at Michael Norcia, who’s chomping down on a rather large cigar. Baxman’s on his cell phone, however, and Norcia appears intent on listening to Baxman’s side of the conversation.]
Baxman: Yeah, everything’s good to go. Considering how short of a time we had to put this damn thing together, I think we’ve got all of our bases covered.
[Listening to the other person talk now, a man wearing an “MGM Staff” t-shirt with a radio headset in his ear approaches the booth as Norcia takes a sip of what we can only assume is a Jack and Coke. Baxman’s oblivious to the man, and Norcia shoots a dirty look up at him as if to say “What do you want?”]
Baxman: Listen, don’t worry about that. You hired me because I’m damn good at what I do, right? I’ve got this covered.
[Seeming a bit scared of Norcia, the staff member looks at his watch, and then stares at Baxman, hoping for his attention. Norcia finally looks up, and quietly - yet venomously - addresses him.]
Norcia: ‘the fuck do you want? We’re busy. [The staff member turns and looks to Norcia with a concerned look.] Staffer: We have a bit of a situation in the locker room... [Narrowing his eyes, Norcia sips his drink again before responding.] Norcia: What TYPE of situation? Staffer: There’s a man saying that he wants to sign a contract, and is demanding to speak with Mr. Baxman right away. He has no credentials to be back there...and he appears to be rather intoxicated.
[Norcia lets out a sigh, as Baxman continues his conversation on the phone.] Norcia: Did you at least get a name? [The staff member leans down and whispers something in Norcia’s ear, causing him to slam his drink down on the table, causing Baxman to stop mid sentence and give him a menacing look.]
Norcia: Please...tell me...that you’re shitting me. [His southern drawl has no humor in it right now, as he grips on his glass tighter, with his hand covered in his drink which splashed out, as Baxman covers the mouthpiece of his phone and pulls it away from his ear. He looks to the staff member first, and then to Norcia.]
Baxman: Is there a problem here? Norcia: A problem? You could call it that. Baxman: Big or small? Norcia: Depends on your point of view. But we need to go take care of this now. Baxman: As in RIGHT now? [Norcia nods.] Norcia: RIGHT now. [He changes his look up to the staff member.] Norcia: If this is a joke of any way, shape or form, don’t look forward to being able to drive yourself home from work tonight, son.
[The staffer nods his head and runs off, as Baxman ends his conversation and stands up, and Norcia gets up right after him.]
Baxman: So what’s the problem? Norcia: There’s a drunken idiot in the locker room demanding a contract. Baxman: And security hasn’t thrown him out.....why? ? Norcia: Just trust me...we need to take care of this ourselves, otherwise he’ll be a problem for a long, long time.
