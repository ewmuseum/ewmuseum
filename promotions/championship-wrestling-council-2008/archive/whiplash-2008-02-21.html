[Anderson quickly grabs Drakin from behind and shoves him shoulder first into the corner between the top and middle turnbuckles, driving the shoulder into the steel ring post. He then turns around to look for Savage Youth, but the fan favorite was waiting for him and catches him with a stinging right hand to the jaw. He unloads another one and then pushes Shane back off the ropes and whips him across the ring. Shane hits the far ropes and comes back to catch a dropkick, taking him down to the mat. Savage Youth quickly scrambles over to Anderson and hooks the leg for an early cover.]
Mouth: Savage Youth has a pin attempt! ONE!! Kickout! Beav: But Anderson kicks out right away! [Savage Youth gets back to his feet and reaches down to pull Anderson up, but Anderson, from his back, jabs his aggressor in the eye with a thumb and gets back to his feet. Savage Youth turns right back around into Anderson, still holding his eye, and catches a boot to the stomach before Shane hooks him into a front facelock and brings him up and over with a snap suplex. Getting right back to his feet, he reaches down and pulls Savage Youth back up again and hits him with a European Uppercut before grabbing his wrist and wrenching his arm into a wrist lock. Savage Youth looks for a way out of it, but doesn’t have to find one as Drakin slides back into the ring and clobbers Anderson from behind, forcing him to break the hold. Noah Drakin then unloads a hard chop across Savage Youth’s chest, grabs him for an Irish Whip, but Savage Youth reverses it and sends Drakin flying across the ring. Drakin comes back and leapfrogs over Savage Youth, hits the far ropes, and flies in at him with a cross body press, but Savage Youth ducks out of the way and Drakin finds nothing but the canvas.]
Beav: This is one barn burner of a pace they’ve started off on, Mouth! Mouth: We’re in Tucson....THE DESERT....there are NO barns here! Beav: I’m sure there are one or two around! Mouth: They don’t have Hoe-Downs in Arizona you damn redneck! They have Spring Break here! [Savage Youth quickly lays a few stomps into Drakin before turning his attention back to Shane Anderson, who’s just back to his feet. Savage Youth grabs him, clubs him across the back with a forearm, and then hooks him up for a swinging neckbreaker, hitting it nicely. Jumping back up to his feet again, he once again pulls Anderson up, hooks him up for a Fisherman’s Suplex, turns it over and holds for the pin attempt!]
Beav: Another pinning predicament! ONE!! TW... Kickout! Mouth: Still not enough! [Before either man can get back to their feet, Drakin rushes back into the scene and alternates stomps to each man who’s on the canvas before pulling up Savage Youth and scooping him up into his arms, dropping him down across his leg for a backbreaker. Now it’s Drakin with a cover on Savage Youth!]
ONE!!! TW... Kickout! Mouth: He couldn’t even get the two! [Drakin gets back to his feet again, but is blindsided by a hard punch to the temple, and stumbles away. Anderson stays on him though, and assaults him with a barrage of punches before hooking him up for a Northern Lights Suplex, slamming him down into the middle of the ring. Anderson gets up and looks down at Drakin, and quickly steps out through the ropes and up onto the apron, where he climbs to the top turnbuckle. He stands up straight quickly, arms raised in the air, and launches down with a flying headbutt right into the chest of Drakin. Anderson quickly finds his way back to Drakin and hooks the leg for a cover.]
ONE!!! TWO!!! Kickout! Beav: This time it was Anderson trying to get a pinfall, but Drakin managed to kick out! Mouth: None of these guys want to go down! Beav: Can you blame them! They’ll have to wait months to even SMELL CWC gold if they don’t get into this tournament!
Mouth: That’s because they’re LOSERS! [Anderson gets up again and mounts Drakin, unloading rights and lefts. Savage Youth is now back to his feet and pulls Anderson off of Drakin from behind, hooks him up in a rear waist lock, and hits a belly to back suplex, holding the bridge for a pin attempt!]
Beav: Savage Youth with a pin out of nowhere! He’s got him bridged! ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!! Mouth: NO WAY! Beav: Just like that, Shane Anderson’s been eliminated from the match! We’re down to Savage Youth and Noah Drakin now!
[Savage Youth gets back to his feet and sees Drakin still down, writhing in pain. He pulls the rookie wrestler up to his feet, nails him with a forearm to the jaw, and whips him across the ring off the ropes, and then springboards off the middle rope, catching Drakin with a springboard missile dropkick right in the center of the ring while he was on the run!]
Mouth: WOW! Beav: The timing on that was MIGHTY impressive! Mouth: No kidding! [Savage Youth rolls over him for a cover.] Beav: This could be it! ONE!!! TWO!!! THRE... Shoulder up! Mouth: NO! Beav: He got his shoulder up somehow! [Savage Youth looks at the referee in disbelief as he gets to one knee. He pulls Drakin up into a sitting position, and locks in a rear chin lock, driving his knee into his opponent’s lower back. He wrenches down trying to cut off the oxygen supply to the brain, and Drakin appears to be fading fast. The referee checks in with him, and when he doesn’t respond, raises his arm up in the air and lets it fall.]
ONE!!! Beav: Drakin may be out for the count, here! Mouth: And Savage Youth would move into the tournament? That can’t happen! The guy’s a complete putz!
[The referee raises his hand into the air again, and lets it fall a second time.] TWO!!! Beav: This could be it! Mouth: Damn it rookie, WAKE UP! [One last time, the referee raises his hand into the air, lets go, and Drakin catches it and holds it back up.]
Beav: NO! Drakin’s still in this! Mouth: Thank god... [Savage Youth tries to readjust his grip and wrench down harder, but Drakin is building some adrenaline up and is starting to press his way to his feet. Savage Youth tries to keep him down, but it’s to no avail, as he gets back to a vertical base, but the hold’s still locked in.]
Beav: He’s up! I can’t believe it! Mouth: Of COURSE he is! This rookie’s got what it takes! Beav: You were just calling him a no talent something or other a few minutes ago, Mouth...what’s wrong with you!
Mouth: Shut up, Beav. [Drakin manages to deliver an elbow right into the rib cage of Savage Youth, loosening up the hold. He does it again, loosening it up a little more. One more elbow finally breaks the hold, and Drakin connects with a stinging right hook to the jaw before running off the ropes and leveling Savage Youth with a big clothesline. Savage Youth gets back up and Drakin’s off the ropes again, and takes him down again with another clothesline. This time he just drops down with a quick elbow into the sternum of Savage Youth and holds a lateral press for the cover.]
ONE!!! TWO!!! TH... Shoulder up! Beav: It’s going to take more than that to keep Savage Youth down! Mouth: I don’t see how... [Drakin gets back to his feet again, reaches down to pull Savage Youth up, but Savage Youth grabs him and rolls him up for a cover!]
Beav: Out of nowhere! ONE!!! TWO!! THRE.... Kickout! Mouth: JUST IN TIME! [Both men jump back to their feet, but Drakin’s quicker on the attack, as he delivers a boot to the stomach, and then sets Savage Youth up for his finisher and hits it.]
Beav: HE CALLS THAT “LAST RITES!!!” Mouth: THAT’S GOTTA END IT! [Drakin has the leg hooked.] ONE!! TWO!! THREE!! Beav: NOAH DRAKIN WINS IN HIS FIRST MATCH AND ADVANCES INTO THE CHAMPIONSHIP TOURNAMENT TO FACE ADAM JAMES NEXT WEEK!
Mouth: I KNEW THIS GUY HAD TALENT! Beav: You did NOT! Mouth: Hey, you think I’m going to let YOU in on all of MY secrets? No way, fatso! [“Killing in the name of...” reprises, as Drakin gets to his feet and holds his hands high in the air. The referee walks over and puts his hand up with one of Drakin’s signifying the win.]
Cassandra: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of this match...NOAH DRAKIN!!! Beav: Well we finally know who all sixteen competitors in the tournament are, and after next week, both sides of the brackets will be set as we make our way towards the two championships! What a week it’s been! Will Ness be signing with CWC? Who’s Kirsta Lewis dating?
Mouth: When did you become a freaking gossip columnist? Beav: We’re all out of time! We’ll see you next week, folks! [The scene fades out with a zoomed in shot of Noah Drakin looking down at Savage Youth, who’s trying to get back up to his feet.]
[WINNER VIA PINFALL: NOAH DRAKIN]
