Whiplash (13 March 2008)

13 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWC Presents WHIPLASH Thursday, March 13th, 2008 Moby Arena - Colorado State University Denver, Colorado [The CWC logo flashes on and off the black screen in a matter of seconds, when in the bottom right hand corner of the screen, it reads “Last Week.” We then fade in to a black and white shot of Peter Lenex sitting staring into the camera in the locker room at the end of Whiplash last week. He is sitting still, on a steel folding chair, wearing all black, topped off by his black trademark trench coat. His face is void of any emotion, feeling, or display of affection. His grey eyes pierce forward like stones.]
"This goes out to anyone who is listening. Anyone who remembers who I am. Anyone who cares about who I am.
"I am a veteran of the business who has been thrown into obscurity. I am a veteran who has been ignored and insulted for the wrong reasons. No matter who you are, you have all ignored me. You have all displayed your ignorance to the world. My parents did so. My brother did so. My whole family did so. Just like the whole God forsaken lot of you in this damn building, the motherfucking staff included.
"Everywhere I go, I get thrown under the bus. I become yesterday's news because no one is willing to accept the fact that the game does not need to be dominated by the young thugs of this new generation. And all because I can't tell a fucking story. Because I can't paint a pretty picture for everyone to see. Because I have more passion for the purity of the sport, and not the fucking entertainment side of things. Because I have more intensity to seek out a win.
"Perhaps people are jealous? Perhaps people are afraid? Whatever the reason, it doesn't matter. It's too late for everyone to start spouting off their excuses. It's too late for all of your reasons.
"A few weeks ago, a successor was born. A child - my child - by the name Christopher. And when he grows up, after years have passed, he will be a success. But to do so, he must create a name for himself. He must crawl out from under my shadow, as if I never existed. He must learn to fight for the sport, and not for the surname of Lenex. Sure, he will hear the stories about me, but anyone remaining in the business will have no recollection of who I was.
"Just like now. "Just like me, he will rise to stardom without his parents help. He will fluorish in the face of inequality. He will become nothing more than a bastard making a name for himself."
[Lenex pauses and smiles.] "Let the bastards of the world rise up. Let them become what we before them could not." [He paises again and looks right into the camera.] "Christopher, this is for you." [In one fluid motion, Lenex raises his right hand up to his temple, and the scene goes black, and remains that way for a few seconds. We then fade into a quick shot of Bryan Baxman in the locker room talking to Commissioner Michael Norcia.]
Baxman: I honestly don't want this to be the theme of the show, but it's the right way to start it off.
[Norcia sighs as he takes a puff off of his cigar and nods slowly.] Norcia: I suppose. I just know I didn't see that shit coming... Baxman: Nobody did, Mike...nobody did. [With a somberness rarely seen over him, he pats Norcia on the shoulder and heads off camera, as Norcia walks off in the opposite direction.]
–Start the Show-- [Silence. In a place as large and filled with fans as the Moby Arena, silence was far louder than any amount of cheering could have been. It wasn't normal for such a large crowd to be quiet, but after the horrible events that occured exactly a week ago, the CWC fans couldn't help but remain silent, the shock from those brutal final moments of Peter Lenex's life leaving the typically vocal masses mute. As such, it wasn't with a bang (No pun intended) that Whiplash began, instead the camera's would find themselves focused upon the Jumbo Tron, where a black and white picture of Peter Lenex as best remembered, holding the last major title he was known to possess-The PWX World Heavyweight Title. Beneath that image, the words "Peter Lenex-1976-2008".]
Beav: Welcome to Whiplash, everyone.. Mouth: Yeah... Beav: It's not often that we start off with such a somber moment but, after last week, it's impossible to come into this show with a head of steam. However, the show must go on. So, without further ado...
[As Beav's voice trailed off, an ominous bell tolled out over the speaker system-a final show of respect to the fallen. For the most part, the crowd was silent-After all, the individual who they were saluting had given them much in the years they had watched him; for that much, they felt the need to be respectful. However, a surprisingly large number of fans were actually <i>booing</i>. All reactions were understandable, but still-The bell tolled on, counting higher and higher until the sixth roll, when the deafening ring was cut off, replaced by the high energy electric guitar that introduced 'X' by X-Japan.]
Beav: For God's sake... [Ignorant to Beav's dipleasure, the sound system continued to pump out the commanding riff, causing confusion amongst the majority of the fans while others, those who strongly condemned the actions Lenex had taken, actually <i>cheered</i> for the interuption. With Lenex's picture fading from the Jumbo Tron as the music grew in intensity, it was replaced by the Japanese flag, signaling the return of a man who had gone unnoticed for some time now. However, if the fans were ignorant to the meaning of said flag or who it represented, they would have their curiousities answered in the flesh as the curtains beneath the 'Tron parted, allowing a suit clad Japanese man to step through into the limelight-A place he had not been in a long time.]
Beav: ...Oh, this is disgusting. A man died here and instead of allowing him to receive the respect he deserves, this man is here to interupt it.
Mouth: ...So wait, you condone suicide? Beav: For crying out loud, Mouth. Not right now, okay? This is just wrong. No two ways about it, this is wrong.
[As Mouth goes about making a snarky comment about Beav's comments, the suit clad individual casually adjusted the collar of his dark grey blazer while his eyes, which were hidden beneath a pair of smoke tinted glasses, shifted around the arena. It was clear that he was most amused by the reaction he was recieving, but instead of milking it, the man would simply begin to make his way down to ringside. To those who recognized him, they would notice that the man looked somewhat different than he had the last time he had been seen; his hair had grown out, allowing those black locks to be pulled back into a ponytail, while the makings of a goat-tee had begun to form upon his chin. Otherwise, he was exactly the same as they remembered him. With a content smirk upon his face as he reached ringside, the man moved to the ringsteps and climbed up them with a certain haste, pausing just long enough to cast another brief look around the arena, which was still vibrating with the sound of confusion, as well as the sounds of approval and condemnation. It was only after he had taken a few moments to absorb it all that he would finally step between the ropes and into the ring, where he would take a moment to dust off his blazer before moving toward Cassandra, who had been waiting to call the first match post tribute.]
Beav: I can't believe this. I just can't believe this... Mouth: It's better than this bell crap. Beav: A man died last week! Mouth: And he did it to himself. So screw that-I'm not going to sit here and pretend Lenex was a great man; he killed himself on live television. I might be an asshole, but there are certain lows I won't sink to-This is one of them.
[As Beav struggles to find a retort, the man in the ring had taken the microphone from the woman, his left hand moving upward to give the mic a bit of a tap, testing it as 'X' began to fade out of earshot. Satisfied with the microphone, the man would nod slightly to himself before moving toward the center of the ring, a smirk upon his face as he brought the microphone up to his lips.]
???: Konbanwa, CWC... [A light chuckle escaped his lips after saying that infamous opening statement of his-The very same one he always used when addressing the public. The initials of the company might have been different, but the egotistical air of superiority and the light Japanese accent remained the same. It was something he carried with him with every step, every movement; the overpowering sense that he was just...better. And it only got worse.]
???: ...As some of you may know, my name is Kenji Gosenkugi-The Puroresu Warrior, as some like to call me. I'm a man of many talents and a good deal of achievements, all of which I'm sure you know about...Or, at least I'd expect you to know about.However, knowing how your culture prizes bleach blonde whores and reality television where you eat bugs for a dollar, I would not be suprised if you didnt.That's not the issue here, however...
Beav: Ugh, the ego... Mouth: I stand by my previous statement. Kenji: No, the issue here is the garbage you were just 'paying tribute' to-Peter Lenex.
[Immediately, a certain backlash to Kenji's words erupted. Of course, people wished to defend their fallen hero, but it wasn't as loud or as powerful as it could've been. The people who could've added that power were actually in favor of Kenji's words, going so far as to cheer him for them. Kenji, however, ignored this as though their cheers were the same as the occasional piece of trash that was thrown at him.]
Kenji: Don't misunderstand-I actually approve of the fact that he killed himself. [The supprort Kenji had immediately left as soon as he uttered that statement, turning their cheers into boo's. Loud, persistant boo's that almost overtook the sound coming from the PA. Kenji,however, remained undeterred by this. Infact, he simply ignored it all as he stood there, his face still bearing that smirk as he took in their hatred.]
Kenji: ...What? Do you ignorant bastards truly believe he had anything left that was worth living for? The man had gone from glory to embarrassment. Any value he had dried up long before last week. The only thing he could do to restore any measure of honor and meaning to his name and family was to end his existence. Some may call him a coward, however, I commend him for it...
[Oh, how the hatred flowed in. Boo's rained down along side of soda cups, all attempting to have their own impact upon Kenji but they all fell short. It was as if the arena was empty to him-The fans were just that insignificant. He had things he wished to say and nothing short of an army of individuals would stop him, so he continued on without a moment of hesitation.]
Kenji: ...I whole heartedly commend him, but just as quickly as I commend him for his actions-The lies he spewed in those scant few moments before he took his own life taint that approval I place upon the deed. Those lies, bold-faced and unapologetic, disgust me...
[A small twinge of annoyance played upon Kenji's lips, his reasoning unknown to the public just yet but it was clear that the man was more than a bit angry. However, rather than allow it to overpower him, he would force a certain stotic expression upon his face-The classic 'proper' appearance of the Japanese.]
Kenji: You see, as much as Lenex tried to make himself out to be some kind of Martyr to a cause no one cared about-Himself-the truth of the manner was far less...glamorous. While there is a certain dignity in death, Lenex's ultimate decision was not driven by the need for honor, nor the need for dignity when everything else had failed. No, Lenex killed himself for two reasons. Two simple, selfish reasons that have nothing to do with a passion for this sport, nor some kind of twisted encouragement for his bastard of a child. No, that might have been noble, if only slightly. Instead, Lenex killed himself out of greed and fear.
[Pulling the microphone away from his lips, Kenji would exhale softly, slowly before taking a look around the arena. While the crowd was still largely hot, that split was beginning to show itself once again. What Kenji was saying was by no means kind or popular, but just the same-It was impossible to disagree with for the most part. However the crowd reacted, Kenji continued on after taking a quick breath of air.]
Kenji: Greed and fear-Two of the most basic human emotions. Everyone wants to have the best of everything and everyone is afraid to lose it all-Anyone who says otherwise is a liar. Peter Lenex wanted it all, he just <i>had</i> to have it all...but ultimately, he failed. He didn't just fail, but he did so spectacularly. His star had faded and his name completely devoid of value, and instead of attempting to regain some of the lost glory that he so coveted through more conventional means, he chose the most pathetic way to get the attention he so craved. He was so greedy for the spotlight and so afraid of losing it that the only way he could obtain it once again, since it had become obvious that he was a worthless individual, was suicide...and to that, I have one thing to say-Fuck you.
[A large section of the Moby Arena would surprisingly go off with approval at Kenji's rare use of vulgarity. Kenji was typically one to use more prim and proper wordings, but it was obvious that he felt strongly about the issue. Even his face, which he had tried so hard to keep a certain nonchalant calmness about his expressions, had betrayed him. His contempt was clear-He truly hated Lenex for what he had done. Licking over his lips slightly, Kenji would nervously roll his jaw from left to right before turning slightly, giving the surrounding masses another one of those glances before resuming his rant.]
Kenji: Fuck you, Peter Lenex. Fuck you. Fuck you for lacking the manhood to pick your <i>precious</i> career up from the floor and make something of yourself again. Fuck you for lacking the courage to rise from adversity. Fuck you for trying to justify your actions. Fuck you for staining your son's future with your own cowardice and, finally, fuck you for taking the sport that I love and have chosen to dedicate myself to and shitting all over it, just so you can have your final bit of recognition. Fuck you.
[Kenji sneered, his voice filled with the deadliest of venoms. His body had contorted into a stance of thinly veiled rage. His general anger and disdain had grown so powerful, so call consuming, that it was attempting to leak out in every which way it could. His muscles spasmed, his lips twitched and twisted and hands balled randomly into tight, powerful fists. He was moments away from outright exploding, but before he could do such a thing-An intervention would occur. The sound of feedback rippled through the arena, punctuated by a palm slapping against a microphone. Kenji turned towards the ramp, spotting Jackson at the top, clapping mockingly.]
Jackson: Hey, Samurai Jack, they didn't tell me it was 'asshole with a microphone' night, otherwise I would have been out here sooner.
[Ever the asshole, Jackson couldn't help but take a certain pleasure in raining on someones parade as it were. Smirking down at Kenji, he would watch as the Japanese man turned to ace him, grinning all the more as the man mouthed murmured insults to him in his native tongue. However, Kenji wouldn't allow Jackson the satisfaction for too long. Despite his sneer, Kenji would make his best attempt to compose himself, his free hand adjusting the unbuttoned collar of his white dress shirt while his tongue idly dragged over his lips.]
Kenji: Hmph...Of all the people who could've come out here tonight, it had to be <i>you</i>.
[Kenji barely contained a growl as he looked down at Jackson, his eyes narrowing from behind his smoke-tinged glasses as he looked at the massive Mechanical Animal. His hatred from the man was obvious, but as the seconds passed he found himself more and more capable of retaining that cool, calm visage that he tried to project. In fact, by the time he had allowed the microphone to rise to his lips again, he had managed to shift completely back into that calm as though he wasn't just cursing the name of Peter Lenex moments before. He loathed Lenex, but Jackson was clearly more important in his mind.]
Kenji: ...I must admit though, I'm most pleased. You've saved me the trouble of having to call your ragged ass out here. Thank you for that much. Now, I can get down to the <i>real</i> reason I'm here...
[Jackson strode towards the ring, a grin on his lips as he ascended the ring steps, pausing there and leaning against the top turnbuckle. He rolled his eyes, making the disdain clear to Kenji before raising the microphone again.]
Jackson: Oh, please. By all means, don't let me interrupt you. I just couldn't help myself, really. You spouting all this trash, cursing Lenex up and down- you know you're giving that asshole just what he wanted, don't you? Fame. That name drop that he so desperately wanted, I mean, let's be honest here, he sure as shit wasn't getting that in the ring, losing to me three weeks in a row.
[Instinctively, Kenji's jaw tightened as Jackson climbed the ring steps. He was expecting him to enter the ring and give him that one thing he had desperately wanted-a fight- but it would seem that Jackson would have none of it on that night...thus far. Keeping his ground, Kenji would stare at Jackson for a few moments before simply shaking his head.]
Kenji: Ever the hypocrite, as per usual. Then again, I suppose I should thank you for it. After all, I can't think of a single person who has done more ranting and raving than you, bakayarou. I learned from the best, as they say...I digress though. I'm not in the mood to argue over the frivolous with you, Jackson-It's pointless. So please, why don't you indulge me and give me what I've come for?
[Kenji tilted his head downward, his glasses slipping down his nose just enough with the motion so that he could stare upon Jackson with those dark brown eyes. The invitation was open, begging to be taken. Jackson turned, appealing to the crowd as though he expected a response other than the chant that was rippling across the arena: "KICK HIS ASS! KICK HIS ASS!". Turning back to Kenji, Jackson stepped between the ropes with his eyes narrowed.]
Jackson: I'm sorry, I can't really give you that. I mean, I'm touched that you want to use my blade to finally commit seppuku and end this miserable existence, but didn't we have enough of that melodrama last week?
[Kenji held his ground even as Jackson, who was far taller and physically stronger than him, entered the ring. Most men would've cowed at the man's entrance and for good reason-Jackson was well known for being a monster when it came to a fight. Kenji didn't have that luxury even if he had wanted it-There was too much at stake for him, be it professionally or personally. Only the fans who knew of their past would understand that much. Smirking slightly in response to Jackson's quip, Kenji would nod slightly before reaching up and removing his glasses, folding them up within one hand before sliding them into his breast pocket.]
Kenji: Ever so witty. Seems that things never do change. But anyway, I'm aware of that much. This tournament you happen to find yourself in keeps me from that which I desire, for better or for worse. And that's fine-Afterall, who better to beat for a title than you, Jackson?
[Smiling in a manner that was far too pleasant for the amount of contempt each man had for one another, Kenji would look over Jackson for a few moments,contemplating things as it were before nodding slightly.]
Kenji: Yes, who better to beat, indeed. So how about we do this, Mr. Jackson? I'll play nice for now-I'll let you have your run for glory. After all, two titles are better than one. You have your run through the tournament, I'm sure that much will be easy for you and then, once you're finished with this relatively worthless roster-We can engage in this little dance of ours again. Hmm?
[Tilting his head, Kenji would smirk as the crowd reacted to his words. Kenji had put Jackson into something of a spot it would seem. He was one of the hands-on favorites in the tournament. Would he be so bold as to accept such a challenge before he even had possession of the title? It was an egotistical move, but these were two egotistical men.]
Kenji: ...Or is the Machine afraid that 'Samurai Jack' can finally burn out that engine of yours?
[Jackson shook his head, clearly amused at Kenji's words.] Jackson: I don't fear tedium, Kenji. I transcend it. You should know that by now. You wanna tangle, we'll tangle. Name the time, name the place, and I'll order a nice arrangement for your funeral. Hell, it's the least I can do for an old friend like you.
[Without a further word, Jackson dropped the microphone in the ring, and dropped, rolling under the ropes. He didn't look back as he strode up the ramp, accompanied by the loud pops of the crowd. He never looked back and Kenji never looked away as Jackson made his way toward the back. He hated the man-He always would. However, despite himself, he couldn't help but shadow the amusement that Jackson had displayed moments ago. So much so that he couldn't help but nod and smirk slightly, content with knowing that he would get exactly what he had asked for in due time, as Jackson disappeared into the back, leaving the viewers at home with little more than that pleased expression upon his face as the camera faded to black.]
--What's his problem?-- [The camera’s come on backstage where Blake Stone has arrived. His hair looks out of place and disheveled as he walks solemnly down the hall. The image of the happy, jovial kid is replaced by something more distant and alien in his eyes… He steps into the arena and walks towards his locker room and it is immediately evident that his clothes aren’t even clean… As he walks down the hall a CWC ringside tech nods to him.]
CWC Ringside Tech: Hey Blake, good luck in your match tonight. [Blake stops dead in his tracks. He adjusts his collar and then spins around in one motion grabbing the tech and pushing him against the wall with his forearm against his throat.]
Blake Stone: WHAT DID YOU SAY TO ME!? [The ringside tech turns red as the blood is cut off from his head.] CWC Ringside Tech: Hey uh…Good…luckkkk [His words stutter as Blake’s elbow continues to grind into his throat.] Blake Stone: I DON’T NEED LUCK! I DON’T NEED ANYTHING! ESPECIALLY FROM YOU! [Blake releases the guy and as the guy drops to the floor and regains his composure, Blake suddenly spins around with a roaring elbow and smashes it right into the guy’s throat. A sick sound can be heard and the guy starts spitting blood. Blake watches the man drop to the floor and he just stares at him.]
Blake Stone: How fortunate… Now I get to watch you die. [Blake stares at the man as blood comes out of his mouth and within moments the hallway fills with CWC officials. Blake turns from the crowd, frustrated that his fun was interrupted. He walks into his dressing room and slams the door leaving a sea of chaos behind him.]

Cassandra Randall: And his tag team partner... Hailing from Newark, New Jersey... Weighing in at three hundred and forty five pounds... Standing six foot eleven.. He is...Roadblock!!

Beav: He can't do that! Maggio can't be in a handicap match! Mouth: He just did Beav. [As Sayne and Garcia talk about who's going to be taking the first turn, Garcia finally nods his head and gets out of the ring, leaving Maggio and Sayne to lock up immediately. Mouth: He FINALLY shows up! Beav: Better late than never Mouth. [Roadblock gets up onto the apron, and he begins to clap for Maggio, who is being double teamed by Sayne and Garcia for a moment. Garcia was now in the ring, taking his time to go for the pin, taunting Roadblock, before he rolls up Maggio. The ref gets down and makes sure the roll up is legit before he begins to count.]
One... [Maggio manages to kick out, he crawls towards the corner where his partner has suddenly and miraculously appeared. Sayne has gotten back into the ring to help his own tag partner – but the ref stops him. Garcia grabs hold of Maggio, but Roadblock is there to make the save. Having been in the middle of taking off the guitar, he looks to it, then to Garcia. As if it almost pained him – he shattered the control over Garcia's head. Mouth: That's blatant and against the rules! The ref should do something about it!! Beav: He just can't Mouth, he didn't see it. [Roadblock was ontop of Garcia without a moments hesitation. Delivering boot stomp after boot stomp to his sides. The ref finally managing to get Sayne out of the ring, and turns back to the action at hand. Roadblock pulls Garcia up by his dreads, but then his eyes go wide. Making a slightly revolted face, he wipes his hands off on the back of his pants, before sending Garcia into the corner.
He turns to look at Maggio, holding up a finger as if saying 'one second' before he rushes towards his opponent, and delivering a slight splash. Garcia crumples down to the mat, Roadblock raising an eyebrow, before he moves back over to his corner and tags in Maggio. The official signifies there was a tag made as both men now ascend onto Garcia.
Roadblock pulls in Maggio to talk to him for a moment, before they clap as if they had been in a huddle. The crowd is on their feet cheering for these two newcomers, as Maggio pulls up Garcia and delivers a belly to belly suplex, sending him overhead.

The team of Maggio and Roadblock look to one another as they notice their opponents are both dazed and down for the count.
Roadblock scratches his head. As Sayne begins to twitch, he stomps into the downed opponent. Maggio delivers an elbow to Garcia for good measure. The tag partners then walk over to one another, and they begin to discuss. The ref glances around, and begins to count – but it seems to be half hearted and slow.]
Beav: And it seems as if the official doesn't mind both men being in at once. Mouth: This is ridiculous! [Maggio moves over, pulling up Sayne and suplexes him back down to the canvas. Roadblock has maneuvered over to Sayne and instead of suplexing him onto the canvas, he suplexes him right ontop of his partner, the fans go crazy, giving a loud pop as Maggio covers both men. He motions for Roadblock to get in on it, and Roadblock covers their lower half.]
