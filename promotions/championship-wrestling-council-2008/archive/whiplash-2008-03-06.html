Sure, he will hear the stories about me, but anyone remaining in the business will have no recollection of who I was.
"Just like now. "Just like me, he will rise to stardom without his parents help. He will fluorish in the face of inequality. He will become nothing more than a bastard making a name for himself."
[Lenex pauses and smiles.] "Let the bastards of the world rise up. Let them become what we before them could not." [He paises again and looks right into the camera.] "Christopher, this is for you." [In one fluid motion, Lenex raises his right hand up to his temple, and fires the gun that is in his hand. As the sound of the gunshot echoes around the empty room, Lenex falls sideways off of the chair and to the ground below, the gunshot having blown a good chunk of his head away. As he lays on the floor, his eyes lifeless and open, the blood pours from his wound, painting the floor the sweet red color of death. The gun lays beside him, and time slowly ticks on as the scene fades to black.]
