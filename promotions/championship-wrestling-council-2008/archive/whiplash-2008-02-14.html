Whiplash (14 February 2008)

14 February 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWC Presents WHIPLASH Thursday, February 14th, 2008 Fresno State University Fresno, California [The CWC logo flashes on and off the black screen in a matter of seconds, as we cut to a black and white shot from last week. Bryan Baxman and Kirsta Lewis are in the backstage area of the Bill Graham Civic Auditorium, as Baxman is looking for The Border Patrol.]
Baxman: Kirsta...have you seen The Border Patrol? Not only do I need The Flying Enchilada here...
Kirsta: I believe it’s “Burrito”... Baxman: Whatever...I need the other one here two, and nobody’s seen or heard from them since Tueday!
Kirsta: Sounds like you’ve got yourself a bit of a problem there, Bryan... [The scene cuts a head, and is now seen from a different angle, as Baxman looks none too pleased with the Hell Cat.]
Kirsta: And just what is THAT look for? Baxman: First off, you will address me as Mr. Baxman. Second of all, my problems are EVERYONE’S problems around here, do you understand? I need you to spread the word to the rest of the locker room that we need to find...
[Kirsta suddenly reaches out and puts her hand over his mouth, and her mischievous grin turns to a look of anger.]
Kirsta: Excuse me? I think you know that’s not quite true as far as I’m concerned...BRYAN! [Her emphasized stating of his first name echoes, as we cut ahead to later in the show where the two are in Baxman’s office backstage - the chairman sitting in his chair behind is desk staring across at Kirsta, who is sitting with her legs crossed, arms folded, and an angry look on her face.]
Baxman: Look Kirsta, I don’t know what you want me to do. You lost your qualifying match. I can’t just put you in the tournament anyway.
[Kirsta’s eyes narrow.] Kirsta: We’ll see. Your phone should be ringing any min... [She stops, as Baxman’s cell phone begins to ring and vibrate on his desk in front of him. He looks at the Caller ID screen and lets out a deep sigh, looking back up at Kirsta, who now has a smirk on her face, and the scene slows, as does the sound of the ring. We then cut just a couple of seconds as Baxman is now on the phone.]
Baxman: I understand that sir, but she lost her... [Obviously cut off, he leans back in his chair looking frustrated.] Baxman: But if I do that for her, I’m going to have to do it for everyone, or we’ll have mutiny on our hands.
[Baxman’s eyes open wide as he suddenly sits back up and grabs a pen, and starts scribbling notes on his paper. He finally stops, puts the pen down, and glares at Kirsta who’s smile is getting bigger as she’s obviously finding this funny.]
Baxman: Yes sir, I understand. [We cut ahead just another couple of seconds, as he’s hanging up the phone.] Baxman: Fine...you’re in the tournament. [Cut ahead to a shot of Kirsta from a different angle.] Kirsta: Hopefully now you understand how things are going to work around here... [Before he can respond, she leaves the office and slams the door behind her.] Baxman: This is getting to be a problem... [His last words echo as he leans back in his chair before we cut to a live shot of Bryan Baxman and Michael Norcia backstage.]
Baxman: We’ve got to get the reigns on Kirsta. Isn’t there anything you can do about this? [Norcia, wearing a light grey suit with a black shirt underneath takes his cigar out of his mouth and smirks.]
Norcia: Sorry pal...I’m not getting in the middle of this one. Baxman: But you... [Norcia quickly holds up his hand, causing Baxman to stop mid sentence.] Norcia: That has nothing to do what’s going on here in CWC. You knew what the fuck you were getting into when you took this job. Now you’ve got to deal with it.
[Letting out an obviously frustrated sigh, Baxman folds his arms across his chest and leans up against the wall.]
Baxman: So the inmates are running the asylum... [Norcia takes a puff from his cigar and laughs.] Norcia: Welcome to the professional wrestling business... --Start the Show-- [We cut to the opening Whiplash montage, as the opening electronic tones of Linkin Park’s “Points of Authority” begin to play, while we see flashes of the faces of various CWC wrestlers. Once the heavy guitar riff kicks in, we go through a quick action sequence before the “Whiplash” logo flashes all over the screen, and finally settles in the center as the music cuts out, and we move to inside of the Bill Graham Civic Auditorium. The place is almost filled to the 4,000 seat capacity it was set up at for the event, and the fans are hot, screaming and cheering, holding up signs such as “EVIL IS COMING!”, “JAX & GEORGIE - WANT A 3RD?”, and “I <3 DEMON JUICE!” The camera finally cuts to the ringside commentator’s table, where we find Hal “The Beav” Cleaver and Mikey “The Mouth” Munson wearing similar CWC polo shirts. Beav’s hair is combed in the usual schoolboy fashion, as Mouth has his New York Yankee’s cap turned backwards with his long hair tucked under the hat and behind his ears.]
Beav: Happy Valentine’s day everyone! This is Championship Wrestling Council’s Whiplash! Mouth: Valentine’s day’s for chumps. I’ll only honor it if the girl I’m with will honor “Steak and BJ Day!”
Beav: “Steak and BJ Day?” Mouth: Yes! It comes in March. Valentine’s Day is a total chick holiday...flowers, candy, jewelry...NOTHING a guy really WANTS to do! What men REALLY want, as we all know, is a good steak followed by a good BJ!

Beav: Oh my..............and that’s a real holiday? Mouth: It is on my calendar! Georgie gains her balance, as Jackson tells her to finish Lenex off...]
Beav: WAIT! WHAT IN THE WORLD IS CRAZED DOING!? Mouth: I don’t know...BUT HE’S GOT HIMSELF A CHAIR! [Crazed has the look of a man who’s snapped, as he jumps up onto the apron. He starts screaming at Jackson, as Georgie watches. Jackson, not intimidated heads straight for Crazed, who winds up and cracks Jackson over the head with the chair. Crazed climbs into the ring, chair in hand, and stands over Jackson, as Georgie jumps down and spins Crazed around just as the referee calls for the bell. Georgie tries to throw a punch, but Crazed catches it with his free hand and is visibly shaking before he begins screaming at her, hits a boot to the stomach, and cracks the chair over her head too.]
Beav: MY WORD!!! WHAT’S CRAZED DOING!?!? Mouth: Um...living up to his name? The guy’s a freaking LUNATIC!!! Cassandra: Ladies and gentlemen, the winners of your match due to disqualification...Anarchy Associated!!!
Beav: Well Jackson and Georgie have won this match, but I don’t think Crazed cares! Mouth: That’s obvious! [Lenex is back to his feet and sees the carnage that Crazed has caused, comes up behind him and pulls the chair out of his hand, tossing it aside. The two immediately get face to face and into a shoving match, and explode into fisticuffs.]
Beav: And now the team of Lenex and James has broken down! All heck has broken loose! And all of this on the eve of the beginning of the championship tournament which starts next week!
Mouth: Things are getting crazy around here, that’s for DAMN sure! Beav: Next week, we’ve got the first four matches of the first round, plus one final qualifying match to round out the brackets! Don’t miss it!!!
[The scene fades out with Adam James and Peter Lenex absolutely brawling, while Jackson, while groggy, is trying to pull Georgie out of the ring.]
[WINNERS VIA DQ: ANARCHY ASSOCIATED]
