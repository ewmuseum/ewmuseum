Whiplash (7 February 2008)

7 February 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWC Presents WHIPLASH Thursday, February 7th, 2009 Bill Graham Civic Auditorium San Francisco, California [The CWC logo flashes on and off the black screen in a matter of seconds, as we fade into a black and white clip of Bryan Baxman in the ring at “The Big Bang Theory.” In the bottom left hand corner of the screen it reads “The Big Bang Theory - Last Thursday.” As Baxman’s standing in the ring, we hear Beav’s voice echoing in the background.]
Beav: WELCOME WRESTLING FANS TO THE FIRST EVER CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING COUNCIL EVENT! I’M HAL CLEAVER SITTING BESIDE MY LONGTIME PARTNER MIKEY MUNSON AND THIS IS THE BIG BANG THEORY!
[The scene skips ahead to a point where Baxman is now in the middle of his address to the crowd.]
Baxman: There’s always been the wonder of just how our universe started...of how anything truly came to be. Some people still believe that God created the earth in 7 days, giving us everything we would ever need to grow as a society. If you ask me...that story has quite a few holes in it.
[The crowd gives a little bit of a laugh.] Beav: It’s a symbolic story from the bible! Mouth: Can it, fatty I’m trying to listen! Beav: I was only... Mouth: SHHHHHHHH!! [Baxman loosens up his tie a little bit, and turns to look at another section of the crowd.]
Baxman: A pretty large contingent of the scientific community seems to think that the universe was created by from a primordial condition of enormous density and temperature.
Beav: Did you understand what he just said? Mouth: Nope... Baxman: And then it blew up in a huge fiery explosion that would make the Nuclear Bomb the Americans dropped on Hiroshima look like a match in the wind That, my friends, is The Big Bang Theory in a nut shell.
[Cuts ahead another few seconds] Baxman: So what you’re about to see is a show that wrestling promotions that have been in business for years could only dream about putting together Tonight, you’re going to see some wrestling matches that you’d never see anywhere else!
[Cut ahead again] Baxman: Tonight, we put “The Big Bang Theory” to one of the biggest tests you can attempt....what happens when you start off a new wrestling company with one of the most stacked match line-ups this business has ever seen!
[With his last words echoing, the scene immediately cuts to action clips of “The Big Bang Theory” as “The Big Bang Theory” by Snoop Dogg’s Eastsidaz plays in the background. We see The Demon finishing off Cristiano da Cunha Oliveira, then “The Gambler” Ace being slammed through a table, then Obnoxio chokeslamming Savage Youth, then Adam “Crazed” James posing in a crucifix stance as Jeremiah Belmont is tied up in the ropes with his “crown of barbed wire thorns” wrapped tight around his head, then Ryu Nakajima in a quick exchange of technical wrestling moves, and finally ending with Fallen Angel cracking Hardcore Legend in the head with a steel chair, and Jackson hitting his finisher afterwards. Beav’s voice is the final thing that rings out as the scene fades to black.]
Beav: This certainly has been one heckuva “Big Bang” to start things off! --Where in the HELL are they?-- [We now cut to a live shot in the backstage area where Bryan Baxman is pacing in a hallway on his cell phone wearing a pair of black slacks and a white button up shirt with the collar undone.]
Baxman: I know they need to show up! I have my people trying to track them down right now. I KNOW they’re in town, because they were in the offices two days ago!
[He stops and listens on the other end.] Baxman: No sir, that won’t be necess... [He’s obviously cut off.] Baxman: Yeah, I understand. I’ll let you... [He stops in mid-sentence, and pulls the phone away from his ear looking at it, and then folds it closed.]
Baxman: So much for at least saying good bye and good luck... [Suddenly, Kirsta Lewis’ voice is heard from just off camera.] Kirsta: Problem? [The camera pans out just as Baxman turns and looks to his left, where Kirsta Lewis is standing there looking at him with a mischievous grin.]
Baxman: Kirsta...have you seen The Border Patrol? Not only do I need The Flying Enchilada here...
Kirsta: I believe it’s “Burrito”... Baxman: Whatever...I need the other one here two, and nobody’s seen or heard from them since Tuesday!
Kirsta: Sounds like you’ve got yourself a bit of a problem there, Bryan... [Baxman narrows her eyes.] Kirsta: And just what is THAT look for? Baxman: First off, you will address me as Mr. Baxman. Second of all, my problems are EVERYONE’S problems around here, do you understand? I need you to spread the word to the rest of the locker room that we need to find...
[Kirsta suddenly reaches out and puts her hand over his mouth, and her mischievous grin turns to a look of anger.]
Kirsta: Excuse me? I think you know that’s not quite true as far as I’m concerned...BRYAN! [She slowly pulls her hand away, and Baxman looks at her frustrated. He finally responds to her in a rather tongue-in-cheek manner.]
Baxman: I’m sorry, can you please help me out? Kirsta: That’s better. I’ll see what I can do...if I have the time. I’d hate for your boss to be in a bad mood over something like this.
[She winks at him and walks away, leaving him to watch her disappear around a corner.] Baxman: Dealing with her isn’t going to be easy... [He looks up as a staff member with a clipboard walks by.] Baxman: Hey! You! Have you seen two luchadors that don’t speak very much English walking around here at all?
[The man just shakes his head and walks on.] Baxman: Damn it...they could be anywhere in this city... --Start the Show-- [We cut to the opening Whiplash montage, as the opening electronic tones of Linkin Park’s “Points of Authority” begin to play, while we see flashes of the faces of various CWC wrestlers. Once the heavy guitar riff kicks in, we go through a quick action sequence before the “Whiplash” logo flashes all over the screen, and finally settles in the center as the music cuts out, and we move to inside of the Bill Graham Civic Auditorium. The place is almost filled to the 4,000 seat capacity it was set up at for the event, and the fans are hot, screaming and cheering, holding up signs such as “Purr for me Hell Cat!”, “MAKE THE VOICES STOP!”, and “TAKE ME BACK TO THE FUTURE SHANE!” The camera finally cuts to the ringside commentator’s table, where we find Hal “The Beav” Cleaver and Mikey “The Mouth” Munson wearing similar CWC polo shirts. Beav’s hair is combed in the usual schoolboy fashion, as Mouth has his New York Yankee’s cap turned backwards with his long hair tucked under the hat and behind his ears.]
Beav: WRESTLING FANS! WELCOME TO THE FIRST EDITION OF WHIPLASH! I’M HAL CLEAVER ALONGSIDE MY PARTNER MIKEY MUNSON, AND TONIGHT HAS BEEN DUBBED TRIPLE THREAT THURSDAY!
Mouth: That’s right my portly side kick! Tonight we’re going to qualify four more wrestlers into the 16 person tournament that will decide both the Showtime Championship AND the CWC World Championship!
Beav: And on top of that, we have a HUGE main event between three of the toughest sons of guns out there who have all qualified for the tournament already by winning their matches last week!
Mouth: Please tell me there’s no more “Sadistic Madness” or “Tai Chi” matches... Beav: First of all, it’s Tai PEI, Mouth! And no, tonight is nothing but a night of Triple Threat matches!
Mouth: Thank god... [Suddenly, the lights start to flicker in the arena, going almost black a couple of times, with a faint sound of a heart beat sounding. Right before the lights go back to normal, the heart beat stops and an evil laugh echoes out through the arena.]
Beav: What in heaven’s name was that? Mouth: Like I’M supposed to know? Beav: It was just one of those...remedial questions, Mouth... Mouth: ....rhetorical...the word...is...rhetorical. How many times have we been through this over the past TEN YEARS!!!
Beav: I don’t know, Mouth! I haven’t kept count! Anyway, we’re just getting word that Wes Carson has NOT shown up here in San Francisco, and that La Ultima Cebolla is going to be replacing him...
Mouth: But The Border Patrol’s not here either!!! And that’s the first match! --Celebrating Black History Month-- [We once again cut to the back, where we see on of San Francisco’s finest knocking on the door to Bryan Baxman’s office. Baxman opens the door, and sees the police officer, and looks at him slightly confused.]
Baxman: What can I do for you officer? Officer: I have a couple of men in custody that claim to work for you...care to claim them?
[Baxman raises an eyebrow, as the cop motions from off camera for someone to come by him. Entering the scene from the right is the team of The Border Patrol. However, The Flying Burrito is wearing a “Malcom X” hat over his mask with a traditional African robe on over his tights, and La Ultima Cebolla is wearing a Martin Luther King, Jr. T-shirt and dark sunglasses over his mask. Baxman’s eyes are wide open, trying to comprehend the scene.]
Baxman: Um...yeah...they’re mine. Where were they? Officer: Crashing a Black History Month celebration a few blocks away... [Baxman shoots a nasty glare at the tag team, who are excitable as always.] Baxman: What the HELL is wrong with you two? [The pair look at each other, and then back to Baxman. The Flying Burrito speaks first, with his words sounding extra comical considering his Hispanic accent.]
TFB: Peace...my brotha.... [La Ultima Cebolla holds up his right hand in a fist.] LUC: Black Power! [Baxman stands dumbfounded, rolling his eyes. Finally he looks to the officer, who speaks before the Chairman can.]
Officer: They’re all yours. Have a good night. [The officer walks off, and gets right into the faces of the luchador tag team.] Baxman: You know what, I don’t even want to know...but Cebolla, you’re wrestling tonight too!
LUC: Yo? En a wrestling match? Si! Si!Cuando? Baxman: NOW! Get out there! And Burrito! TFB: Si, my nigga? Baxman: What did you just....ugh...nevermind...GO GET THAT DAMN CRAP OFF OF YOU AND GET READY FOR YOUR MATCH!
[The two look at each other for a few seconds, and then back to Baxman, and at the same time....]
TFB & LUC: Que? [Baxman shakes his head in disgust, heads back into his office, and slams the door in their faces. Cut back to ringside.]
–Match: Tournament Qualifying Match - NC-17 vs. Taryn Terror vs. La Ultima Cebolla– Mouth: I can’t believe we have to watch those idiots again. What in the hell were they doing at a Black History Month celebration?
Beav: I don’t know, Mouth! They’re not even African American! Mouth: .....you don’t say...... CR: This match is a triple threat qualifying match, scheduled for one fall…introducing first…
['Hold On' – KoRn hits the arena sound system to a decent crowd pop.] The venue's lighting is dimmed slowly down to complete darkness, before flickering lights begin to illuminate both the crowd and the ring....
With aversion, this insertion... came so swiftly Cut the cord, from this day forward... We'll be drifting... A loud explosion of silver pyro from either side of the stage erupts in the pause of music, and from the smoke that was left in its wake, emerges Taryn TERROR. She stands at the top of the stage for a few moments wearing a long black coat over her usual ring attire, pointing out to the crowd. Taryn makes a T with her hands and holds them in the air before walking down the ramp towards the ring area, visibly walking slightly in rhythm with her own theme music.
This illness is like a monster that is... eating us alive... Hold on, be strong... So right, so wrong, With all of our senses... All of our defenses Hold on, be strong... She enters the ring between the middle and top ropes and stands up on one of the turnbuckles, taking off her coat, which has her usual blue and white TT Logo on the back of it. She swings the coat around her head and throws it out to the crowd, who all instinctively make a dive for it. Taryn smiles as they do so, turning around and taking a seat on the top turnbuckle.]
CR: …from Los Angeles, California…weighing 137 pounds…Taryn Terror! HC: A big night for this young lady, a win here and she’s on the fast track to superstardom.
MM: And a loss and it’s back to Wal-Mart where she belongs! CR…and her opponent… ["The Mexican Hat Dance" plays to pretty much no reaction from the crowd The lights in the arena start to fade, but have a hard time staying there as the power seems to fluctuate to the lighting. As La Ultima Cebolla comes out in his bright colored tights and mask, he jumps around as pyrotechnics start to go off, but as always, falter and just catch on fire on the stage, causing the staff to come out with fire extinguishers and turn put them out, La Ultima Cebolla to turn and look showing disappointed body language. He turns and head back towards the ring. He slides in under the bottom rope, and once again, sparkler cannons on the ring posts barely throw out a few sparks and some smoke before catching on fire again as the music dies out.]
CR: …from San Juan, Mexico…weighing 165 pounds… La Ultima Cebolla! HC: What’s the right word to describe this guy? Special? MM: Retard, simply put, he’s retarded! CR: And their opponent… [Fever For the Flava" by Hot Action Cop [The lights glare purple and pink, and suddenly "Fever For the Flava" by Hot Action Cop fires up. The video tron flashes us random images of babies, rainbows, small animals, and the kool-aid man before the words "Rated NC-17" appear across the screen. The crowd gets to their feet as an exuberant NC-17 appears at the top of the ramp way, loud cheers radiating throughout the arena. He starts to make his way down, slapping high-fives with the fans and generally just being a good guy when suddenly he stops dead in his tracks, as if he's forgotten something. That's when he spins around and points with both arms over his head at the beautiful Barbie Kellers, who is just now emerging from the back. The cheers get a little louder and even some catcalls can be heard as the couple make their way down to ringside, NC-17 holding his girlfriend's hand.]
CR: …from Gary, Indiana…weighing 234 pounds…NC-17 HC: A great young wrestler who could really… MM: Holy crap his girlfriend is smoking hot! HC: Do you mind? MM: Not at all, let’s go Barbie! Woo hoo! [A fantastic, hard hitting and high action contest between NC-17 and Taryn Terror, with LUC being the punching bag for them both. In the early going LUC is knocked out of the ring with a double drop kick, allowing NC-17 and Terror to trade quick reversals and some chain wrestling. NC-17 is able to use his power and size to take control after Terror goes for a hip toss and he blocks it before levelling her with a lariat. NC-17 knocks LUC back out the ring again and takes control with a standing drop kick on Terror for a two count and a fisherman’s suplex for another two count. LUC tries coming off the top rope, only to be met with a drop kick to the ribs by NC-17 for his trouble. The middle of the match sees Terror come back into it, she ducks a spinning kick from LUC and drills him with a snap suplex before leap frogging a spear from NC-17 and hitting him with a shining wizard kick for a two count. She takes control with a reverse DDT on LUC and then uses the ropes to springboard onto NC-17’s shoulders and flip him over with a hurricaranna for a close two count. The finish of the match comes with Terror avoiding a charge from NC-17 in the corner and then hitting him in the back with a drop kick that knocks him out the ring! LUC goes for a bulldog, but Terror blocks it and quickly nails the Terror-fied for the 3 count on LUC and the big win!]
[WINNER VIA PINFALL: TARYN TERROR] –An Arrival– [We are cutting backstage as backstage interviewer Tiffany Jackson is standing in the packing garage.]
Tiffany: Guys we are backstage and a limo pulled up a few moments ago and I am waiting to see who is getting out.
[The driver opens his door and then makes his way to the back, he opens the door and Shane Hahn appears.]
Tiffany: It’s Shane Hahn, a bright, young and tonight a very sharply dressed member of the roster. Shane where did you get all this money?
Voice: That would be me… [The camera turns and pans from the shoes up to a man holding a title and dressed incredibly sharp. He is followed by three women.]
Tiffany: Oh my god… Shane: Excuse us we have somewhere we have to be… --Aw, aren’t they cute?-- [The locker room was nearly pitch black dark, the Plexiglas over the fixtures shattered, leaving jagged holes that loomed overhead, almost ominous. One single, pitiful bulb still flickered with life, buzzing if one cared to listen hard enough to hear it. The broken bits of plastic and glass littered the floor like a minefield, leaving any approach to the man sitting on the simple wooden bench to be noticeable. That wasn’t his intention. The rampage had been borne of pure emotion, and not of any hidden agenda.
He heard footsteps crunching over the littered glass and plastic, and didn't bother looking up as he reached for the tape in his open duffel bag. He could see just fine in the red glow of the EXIT sign above the door.]
Jackson: Tell Baxman I'll pay for the damages. [Shaking her head she folded her arms in front of her.] Georgie: I would but... Uuuh... I'd rather not be the messenger that got shot. [Smirking at him lightly, she moved in front of him, taking the tape from him and looking at him for a moment with the cock of an eyebrow.]
Georgie: So uh... should I ask what happened, or should I chalk it up to the locker room picking on you?
[He shook his head, looking disgusted.] Jackson: I just... get so sick of this sometimes. This endless game, pretending to be something I'm not, so they'll cheer or boo. It doesn't matter how Wakefield spins what happened at the comic book store. They'll think it was all a work, just a stunt to put myself over again. Jerks like me, we don't do burnout. We keep on going, flipping off everyone into the sunset...
[Georgie shook her head as she crouched down in front of him, so they were eye to eye. Raising one slender eyebrow, she pulled his hand out so she could begin to tape it.]
Georgie: Yea, jerks like you. Because LORD knows you're the biggest jerk *rolling her eyes* the guy who held my hair back when I puked at Denny's aeons ago, the guy who carried me up to my bed. Oh and the guy who called me precisely at a minute past midnight every year on the twentieth.
[She smirked up at him with a faint, if not goofy grin.] Georgie: You're sick of the charade, because you're not yourself. I know.. I know it's not that easy in the wrestling business. But you're good at what you do, and you know who you are. They eat it up because you have oodles and oodles of charisma... And pecs of doom. You ooze this... stuff, yes... Stuff, that is original, one of a kind. That's why they love you.
Jackson: I ooze? Thanks, that makes me feel so much better. [He chuckled, and she looked pleased as a faint smile appeared on his lips.] Georgie: Well... You...are a guy and I mean... [She paused, clearing her throat.] Georgie: I uh... [She broke off again, laughing, unable to talk for another moment.] Georgie: Right! Forgot what I was saying.. You are this presence. Ephemeral, you walk into a room and it seems as if the natural current of electricity is amped to one hundred. If you ever went into politics, I swear to gods you'd be the first King of the US.
[Full bellied laughter surprised them both as it came from him.] Jackson: Damn, you had to go and ruin that. I was amping up one of the best 'funks' EVER. Now how am I supposed to go out there and break Cro-magnon man and the loony bin reject six ways to Sunday?
[He winked at her as she patted the tape around his wrist. Jackson flexed his fingers, and grinned before giving her the other hand.]
Georgie: Uuuh... Give me a second. [Scrunching up her face as her eyebrows drew together. Her lips pursing as she thought, this was her serious face which was indeed far too comical to BE serious. Snapping her fingers together, she looked up at him.]
Georgie: I know! Go out there, break Cro-magnon man and the loony bin reject... and if you do so.. I'll let you win at Guitar Hero.... aaand... maybe just -maybe- give you one of my back massages.
[Jackson cocked his head, pretending to consider the offer.] Jackson: I dunno... that was a pretty big maybe. Did you see how huge Lenex's thighs are? It's gonna be hard to put that boy down for the count. Upgrade that maybe to a definitely, and I think I've found my reason...
[Georgie smiled cheekily, and finished off the other hand, giving the knuckles a kiss before biting the tape free of the roll.]
Georgie: His thighs are as huge as my father's head. And I'm SO not joking there but... Okay... I'll upgrade that to a definitely, BUT... you have to buy me ice cream afterwards.
[What was this a hostage negotiation?] Jackson: Rocky Road or Triple Fudge Brownie? Georgie: Both. [Her eyebrows raised as that cheeky grin appeared once more, looking up at him, while she was still on her knees. Jackson sighed theatrically, and took her hands.]
Jackson: Fine. Both. Waffle cones. And that marshmallow fluff that you love so much. Georgie: Then sir, it looks like we have ourselves a deal. [Georgie laughed as she then leaned up, leaning in closer to him, a grin on her lips.] Georgie: Besides, after facing the Rich Bitch and the what... street fighter reject, I'm going to need a treat too.
[Jackson's gaze drifted to the monitor on the table, the only thing in the room not overturned or broken in some way.]

She let out the softest of giggles, as she turned her head and pressed her lips against his briefly before standing up.]
Georgie: So THAT'S why you wanted the Fluff.. [Winking at him as she pulled out of his embrace, she stopped in the door, blowing him a kiss. He snatched it from the air, and put it in his pocket. Their lucky ritual.]

He sees Kostoff, drops her, and Kostoff hits the Dominator on him, and goes for the cover, but with the referee out of position, Georgie gets back up and kicks him in the groin from behind, causing the big man to fall to his knees and roll away in pain. Georgie hooks Kaupp’s leg for the three count and picks up the victory.]
[WINNER VIA PINFALL: GEORGIE NICKLES] –A Man Reborn– [The scene opens up backstage, in the locker room of Peter Lenex. "You spend years carving a name for yourself, just to have it disrespected. "You spend months tossing aside vermin, just to have your accomplishments spat on. "You spend days proving your point, only to have it ignored. "Even if they tossed it all aside with a joke or two, it would have more of an impact than it did this week."
[He sits forward, now looking straight forward. Straight at us.] "But this week, I walked into the throng of two men who could care less what I did. They only care for my mistakes. Trying to pick apart my failures and pick apart where I went wrong. Where they think I'll go wrong this week. But they are also two men who do not believe in practice. Who do not believe in training. The errors I have made in the past, the ones that you are poking at, have been fixed. They have been dealt with.
"No longer do I let the worthless peons like Skylar Kelly get to me. No longer do I let some aging pseudo-philosopher tell me how to live my life. I have learned from every mistake I made, and I am better off for it.
"Unfortunately, the same can not be said for you. "I've taken what of your past I knew with good conscience. I've heard what you both had to say, and to take a few words from one of the two worth mentioning, I've heard it all before. And as I get better, and as everything that comes out of your mouth brings with it shades of re-runs, I can only smile. I can only smile, because as you talk in your ignorant talk, looking through your clouded vision - insane or not - I train to make your lives a living hell."
[He places his forearms on his knees and leans forward, still looking at us.] "I have warned the two of you. I have told you two that I will stop at nothing to achieve this job. And now, NOW, your time is coming to an end. NOW, I am going to show you what I have been preaching for the past few days. Since I walked through the doors of CWC. I am still the God of War, but I am also a man reborn.
"A man fueled by the hunger to destroy, the thirst to spill blood, the desire to watch life escape the bodies of the worthless. There is no more finishing third."
[He cracks a smile.] "I will be the ultimate victor." [The scene fades to black.] --Look who it is– [“Back in Time” by Huey Lewis and the News blares as Shane Hahn makes his way to the ring. He climbs into the ring and has a mic in hand.]
