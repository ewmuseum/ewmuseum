An e-fed whose first ten weeks survive: The Big Bang Theory of 31 January 2008, seven weekly Whiplash shows and the first Hell 2 Pay, with the tournament that was to crown its first World and Showtime champions on its first pay-per-view. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Championship Wrestling Council, which its own pages call the CWC, opened on 31 January 2008 with The Big Bang Theory from the MGM Grand in Las Vegas, a nine-match card on which its Chairman, Bryan Baxman, tells the crowd the fed will crown its two champions in a sixteen-man double-elimination tournament. Seven weekly Thursday shows called Whiplash followed, from San Francisco on 7 February to Boise on 3 April 2008, and a second, Sunday show, Hell 2 Pay, on 16 March; a guest, Tyler Boyd, gave it its name, the old flagship of Championship Caliber Wrestling, the promotion he says he broke into the business in.

The pages tell one running story. Qualifying matches fill the tournament field; Kirsta Lewis wins a place by a call to the chairman and joins the Demon in a group, C.O.D., that beats Jackson and Georgie Nickles with a guest's help; Theo Mavarelli unmasks from a cloaked entrance; and the Whiplash of 6 March 2008 closes on Peter Lenex shooting himself in his locker room, which the next week's show answers with a silent tribute and a speech against him. Its line-ups and home page run on to 24 April 2008, and it announced its first pay-per-view, The Ascension, for 4 May 2008 at the HP Pavilion in San Jose, to crown its first champions. How the tournament ended is not recorded.

The hobby wiki's article on the Championship Wrestling Council describes a community founded in 2008 that ran a tournament called the Ascension Invitational for a CWC World Heavyweight Championship.
