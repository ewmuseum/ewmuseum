A March 1998 self-declared federation that opened recruitment, published a blank wrestler application, and announced its own webpage within two weeks. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This HWF announced itself to rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy on 1 March 1998 under its own full name, the Hardcore Wrestling Federation, promising to become “one of the nest federations ever” and asking interested wrestlers to write in for an application form.

By 13 March it had a page to show for it: two more posts the same day gave its own AOL address, offered a fill-in-place application for anyone who did not want to visit the site first, and capped recruitment at 30 wrestlers.

No card, roster, or closure notice survives past that 13 March pair of posts; whether the promotion ever filled its 30 roster slots, or ran a card at all, is outside the surviving record.
