NWF!! NWF!! Sly Kent: They sure do love the NWF! Kevin McLoud: Who doesn't .. I for one? SK: They employ you Kevin .. I wouldn't talk about them like that. KM: Well .. I'm not you, Kent! SK: Back to Joe! Joe Blow: First making his way down the aisle .. he is a realative newcomer to the NWF .. here is .. GILBERT FERROS!!!!!!! Crowd: YEAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!! GIL-BERT!! GIL-BERT!! GIL-BERT!! GIL-BERT!! GIL-BERT!! GIL-BERT!! GIL-BERT!! Joe Blow: And his opponent ... here is .. AKZINE AZARRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRR!!!!!!!!!! Crowd: BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! [ding,ding,ding] SK: Here we go folks. Things are starting out with a lock up .. and a quick waistlock .. WAISTLOCK SUPLEX BY GILBERT! Akine shoots up to his feet and he goes at Gilbert, clothesline ducked by Akine. Now they both turn around towards each other. Both men throw a left, blocked by both men! Gilbert with a quick armdrag takedown that caught Akine off guard! Gilbert is going off the ropes, and Akine is starting to arise to his feet. Gilbert is heading towards him ... leap frog by Azar and Gilbert runs by him. Gilbert off the ropes once again .. and he jumps over Azar .. SUNSET FLIP! KM: But Akine kicks out right away! SK: He saw it coming .. apparently. Now they both are getting up to their feet .. and another elbow and collar tie up. They fight over towards the corner .. and the ref is calling for a clean break and we get one .. NO! Akine connects with a HARD shot to the head of Gilbert! KM: What wasn't a punch .. that was a SLAP! SK: And Gilbert sure doesn't look to happy about that. He breaks from the corner .. shocked that he got slapped and he is walking towards Azar and he is holding his hands off, begging him to lay off. Now Akine with a right, but Gilbert ducks it. Ferros whips him into the ropes quick as a cat .. and Akine comes shooting off .. DROPKICK AND AKINE GOES TO THE OUTSIDE! Crowd: YEAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!! KM: That sucks! Come on Azar .. take care of the "Mexican Sensation" as he likes to call himself! SK: He is a top notch wrestler, as he is showing us here. Akine is starting to get up on his feet on the outside .. and Gilbert comes charing off the ropes .. SLIDING DROPKICK TO THE OUTSIDE! But it misses Akine! Gilbert unloads with a left shot to the face of Akine. Apparently he intentionally missed with the sliding dropkick. He used it more as a slide technique than a dropkick manuever. Gilbert now grabs Akine by the arm .. and he .. WHIPS HIM INTO THE STEEL STEPS .. REVERSED! [CRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRASH!!] SWEET LORD! Gilbert went FLYING into the steel steps .. and crashed right into them hard as heck! KM: Goodness gracious .. he ain't gonna be getting up from THAT one! SK: No he isn't. Akine just rolled back into the ring and the ref has restarted the count. Ref: 1.. 2.. 3.. 4.. 5.. 6.. 7.. KM: Akine just rolled back to the outside of the ring and the ref started his count over again! SK: Akine is going over to the motionless body of Gilbert and he drags him to his feet. Akine now rolls him back into the ring .. and he follows in. Akine with the lateral press! This thing is OVER! 1.. 2.. 3... KM: ... SK: ... KM: THAT IS IT!? SK: NO! Gilbert got his foot on the bottom rope at the last moment .. by ACCIDENT! Akine is arguing with the ref about the call .. but he just ignores it and goes over to Ferros .. drops a leg across his throat! Akine now is stomping away on the chest of Gilbert, but Ferros hooks his arm on the ropes, and the ref forces Akine away from him. Azar isn't happy about it. But he waits for Gilbert to slowly arise to his feet. Gilbert is up .. with a great deal of help from the ropes .. and Akine comes over and pulls him away from the ropes. He whips him off the ropes .. and Gilbert comes off .. Akine puts his head down .. HARD KICK TO THE FACE BY GILBERT! Akine is reeling back .. HOOK CLOTHESLINE BY GILBERT AND BOTH MEN GO DOWN! KM: This is great! Both men are laid out on the mat .. and the ref is counting both men down! What would happen if they both got counted down? SK: Someone would end up with a bye in the next round I think! Ref: [with the count of the crowd.] 1.. 2.. 3.. 4.. 5.. 6.. 7.. 8.. ... 9...... SK: AKINE IS UP! The match will continue! Just in the nick of time as well! KM: That was sure a heart wrencher there folks. SK: Indeed. Akine is going over to Gilbert .. with a slightly limping leg .. and he brings him up to his feet. Akine tries to put him between his legs for something .. but Gilbert falls down to the mat. Gilbert is getting brought up again .. and the same thing happens. Akine now tries to get him up _one_ more time. Akine gets him up to his feet this time, and he puts him between his legs. He lifts him up .. [Crowd gasps] RAZOR'S EDGE! NO! Gilbert slips out of it .. and a ROLL UP! 1... 2.. 3!!! [ding,ding,ding] JB: THE WINNER VIA PINFALL in 13:12 .. GILBERT FERROS! SK: Great match .. let's get to our next match up! ------- TAG TEAM ACTION! Xolcious & Sean Darring vs. Bone Crushers by: Kent ------- This match was ALL Darring and Xolcious! They simply DESTROYED in this one. Darring started .. and he finished. He locked on a figure four after 1 1/2 minutes .. and got the submission!!
