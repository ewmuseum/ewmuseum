Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This NWF ran from a GeoCities/GNN-hosted homepage alongside its newsgroup presence, staging two weekly broadcasts, Wednesday Whiplash and Sunday Slam Frenzy, structured that January around a Regional Title Tournament.

The tournament's first round, on Sunday Slam Frenzy, put Gilbert Ferros past Akine Azar and Justin Justice past Klonis after an eighteen-minute battle decided by a top-rope front layout suplex. Its third first-round match turned into an upset with consequences beyond the bracket: the newcomer J.P. Steele beat the veteran Sabin Figaro with a small package, and a bitter Figaro grabbed a microphone afterward to announce his own retirement from e-wrestling on the spot.

Wednesday Whiplash carried its own drama the following week. The tag team Strictly Business stole a win over Total Kaos, of a stable called the Legendary Alliance, with a chair shot disguised mid-move; Justin Justice's brawl with Gilbert Ferros spilled into the crowd and drew in Ferros's own brother Ben and a mystery man called DELHAX before Justice won it; and the main event saw Lord Foul the Despiser out-last Chris Storm with a top-rope superplex, his own tag partner Lord Nikon the Impaler watching from ringside.

Its presidency changed hands more than once in the months that followed. Its own final outgoing president closed the promotion outright that June, announcing in his own words that the NWF he used to run was finished and that he was opening a new, unrelated promotion, PEW (Philadelphia E-Wrestling), in its place. No card, and no other document, survives past that announcement.
