LIVE GCW ACTION Wrestling Action Finally hits home in Ft. Worth, Texas House Show Results Location: GCW Arena, Ft. Worth, Texas Tickets Sold: 829 Remaining Seating: 2,171 Money Made on Tickets: $3,523.25 Money Made @ Concessions: $2,083.75 Workforce Pay: $2,770.00 Overall Profit for the night: $2,662.00 Company Profit so far: $5,007.25 Final Show Time: 2 hours, 31 minutes, 44 seconds Match 1: "The Insane One" Justin Sane vs. "The Man from the Alpes" Anton Strong A pretty good opener, many people came into the arena, looking at the programs and arguing who they thought would win all the matches, but the people were captivated by the match. Many familiar faces were seen in the crowd, over half of them were there the previous day... The match started with Justin Sane doing exactly what his name would figure, trying to attack the big man from behind. Anton didn't seem none too happy about his last match-up, and was going to be damned if something like that was going to happen again. He started pummeling Justin Sane soon after turning aroung. The seven foot, 450 pound Austrian Beast would not have it again. The fans were on their feet as they saw the fire light into his eyes. He scooped up Sane with the greatest of ease, and planted him into the mat. Justin Sane tried to get up, but was already in the grasps of big Anton Strong. He was able to catch his breath, for all the while it lasted him as Anton spun him around in the air with an
Airplane Spin, and droped him, right down on his head. Anton wasn't going to waste time. A last chance desperation move forced Sane to go for a low blow, but Anton seemed prepared from something to take place like that, and merely dropped his knee, and his entire body weight right across Sane's arm. Anton backed off a little bit, and waited for Justin to get up, and he did, holding his arm a bit. Anton charged, full head of steam, and nailed him with a running bell clap. Sane fell back into the ropes and bounced out. Anton locked his arms around Justin's upper body, and pitched him over his head with a belly-to-belly suplex. He calls it the Alpes Bomb, and it got him the pinfall, and the victory. The fans were riveted from the fast-paced action from the big man.
Winner: "The Man from the Alpes" Anton Strong via pinfall Time: 1:04 Match 2: "The Future of Wrestling" "Dynomite" Derek Sweger vs. Billy B. Hayes Many fans came into this one thinking that Billy B. Hayes was going to be one of those rolling stones, going over the competition, since his first match-up was a success, and that putting him in the ring with a wrestler with the opposite record was just going to further that a bit, but oh how times can change when you aren't looking. As for Billy B. Hayes, he wasn't looking when the bell rang, and when he turned around, he got leveled by a strong lariat from Derek Sweger. Hayes was knocked back into the corner, and Sweger tried to get him with a tornado DDT, but Billy just chucked the featherweight off of him. Sweger went right back on the attack, however. He dropkicked the legs out from under Hayes as he approached. He climbed on top of him, and started punching him, right in the face. Sweger tried to get the crowd into it, and he went up to the top rope. He got to the top, and started waving to get applause, and that he got. When Hayes got to his feet, Sweger took flight, and hit Billy B. with a cross body block. He pulled Billy back up to his feet, and whiped him into the far side. He charged in, but Hayes dropped out of the way, and out of the ring. As Sweger hit the buckle chest first, Billy yanked his legs out from underneath him, and pulled him into the ring post. Sweger hardly knew what hit him. Hayes was then in control of the match-up for a good long while, showing exactly why so far he had a perfect record in GCW. But the longevity of his onslaught was soon shortlived. He too attempted a follow-up to an Irish whip into the buckle, but by the time he got there, Derek was already perched on the top rope. Hayes hit the buckle and the impact spun him.
Sweger was perfectly lined up for a top rope bulldog. He tried a cover to no avail, but he got pumped from the feedback of the small crowd. He picked up the 278 pound Hayes and smaked him with a Derek Driver. He draged him further away from the ropes, and locked him into the Sweger Scream, Texas Cloverleaf, much to the admiration of the fans. The 6'5" Hayes couldn't reach the ropes, and had to tap.
Winner: "The Future of Wrestling" "Dynomite" Derek Sweger via submission Time: 14:56 Match 3: TeRrOr vs. Vincent Crow If ever there was a time when one man got his ass handed to him, it was this match-up. Vincent Crow came out to no reaction from the fans, but was followed down to the ring directly by the man off the payroll for now, TeRrOr, straight from out of the crowd. For some reason, he was drinking a beer that it's obvious he didn't buy himself... A steel folding chair was probably the only thing Crow would remember from the match-up, but that was only because of the close-up view he got as it struck him right across the face. He was finished right there, but TeRrOr was not. TeRrOr rolled him into the ring, still wearing the chair as a necklace, and he just went to town. It seemed that his neck may have been snapped for a brief moment, when TeRrOr came flying off the top rope with cross body splash right onto the chair. Crow, even in his state sat up from the pain, and TeRrOr some how managed to incorperate that chair into everthing he did in the ring. He took it from arond Vincent's neck, and placed it in the center of the ring. He managed to keep Crow standing long enough to run off the ropes and deliver a crucifix DDT, right across the steel.
From then on, TeRrOr was everything his name says in the ring. At one point, he even got a fan to toss in his own seat from the crowd. He set it down as a place to sit, and then went out to the fans. He asked a man in the front row if he could have the beer that was in his hand, which obviously was answered with a no, but he took it anyway, and went back into the ring, and took a seat to drink his beer. He was interupted however, by the timekeeper saying only five minutes were remaining in the time limit, and went back to work on Crow.
He made sure every once of his well being went into beating the snot out of that man, and waited until the thirty second remaining mark to even use his set-up move, the "Bitch Slap," a hanging suplex into a hangman's neckbreaker.
He was about to finish it off, but no matter how hard he tried, he couldn't get
Crow to stand, so back to the crowd he went for another beer... He again stole it, and made it back into the ring at the five second mark, to take a drink and step on his opponent for the three count. The fans were going mad...
Winner: TeRrOr via pinfall Time: 29:58 Match 4: Cadaver vs. "Icon" Ron Elliot This match started off close, but in the end it was a blowout. Cadaver came in, a little irritated, but Ron was ready to make the scene. Cadaver hit the ring, but immediately got worked on. He got through the ropes, and got a boot to the gut. He was whipped to the ropes, and nailed with a back body drop that
Ron may have had to work out a deal with the Devil for. He rans off the ropes, and came out with a leg drop, but unfortunately, no one was home. The battle raged on for an extended period of time... no one really gaining any more of an advantage than what Elliot had from the beginning. A double underhook suplex from Elliot here, a missle dropkick from Cadaver there, but no one had a definate advantage to capitalize with. In the later moments of the match-up,
Elliot whipped Cadaver to the ropes, and half-way ducked, when Cadaver tried a leap frog, he got a hold of him and nailed him with a stun gun. Elliot went to the top, and came crashing down with a leg drop. A few desperation moves from
Cadaver didn't help him much, Elliot was on a roll. Another attempted pass via frog leap failed for Cadaver and was the beginning of the end. The Icon nailed him with a double leg powerbomb, and immidiately hooked him into the
ICONoclast, a million dollar dream sleeper... he was out like a light bulb. Winner: "Icon" Ron Elliot via knockout Time: 14:39 Match 5: The Y-KiKi KiD vs. Al "The Knife" Hoolibay Some how the KiD managed to dominate this entire match-up. The huge Al Hoolibay was just too slow for the 150 pound wonder. It started off with The
KiD trying to fight him tooth and nail, which seemingly wouldn't work, but every time Al tried to mount an offensive, the kid would get away in the knick of time. A few hard punches to the face of Hoolibay didn't really do much, but after a while, it began to wear down the big man, not having the staying power in the ring the KiD had. But he still couldn't get knocked off of his verticle base. Y-KiKi tried to whip the Knife to the ropes, but was reverse. He slid under a big boot, and dropkicked the other leg out from underneath him, knocking him backward. The KiD went for a quick cover, but was easily thrown off. But before Al could get back to his feet, the Kid was already putting the boots to him. He gradually fought through it, only to be hit with a reverse side kick to the jaw, and shaken quite a bit. The KiD then tried to shoulder block the big man back to the mat, but nothing doing. After a few shot exchanges Al went off the ropes for his pass, but the KiD thought quickly and a drop toe hold left Hoolibay tasting canvas. He then started working over the knees of Hoolibay, trying to keep the beast on the mat. The tactics worked.
As soon as Hoolibay finally got up, he was on Spaghetti legs, and was easy pickens. A kick to the gut and a Wipeout rocker dropper later, only three seconds were left to go in the match.
Winner: The Y-KiKi KiD via pinfall Time: 7:23 Match 6: "Little Nasty" Allen Reynolds vs. "Playboy" George (w/ Nick Merchant) Allen Reynolds really shouldn't have even shown up for this match. It probably would've been less embarassing to take a no-show over his performance in the ring tonight. George sheerly outclassed him in the ring, basically schooled him in what wrestling was all about. Reynolds almost got a punch in at the get go, but taking heed from his manager at ringside, turned around in the nick of time to catch Reynolds off guard. From then on, it seemed that Reynolds was just off a step or two, nothing seemed to go his way. After a few knife edge chops in the corner, Reynolds walked right out into an inverted atomic drop, and right back into the corner, where his head got personal with the top turnbuckle, enough times to have the fans try to keep count. A hard whip to the opposite side led way to a suicide splash, Allen again was falling out of the corner, and he got whipped into a snapmare take down, right into a chinlock. Reynolds needed a quick breather, but wasn't going to get it with his air circulation being cut off. George was just haing fun with him, that's all it seemed to be. He synched in harder and harder with each passing moment.
A while later, Reynolds somehow managed to fight through it and get back to his feet. He ran off the ropes and tried a shoulder block, but got planted.
George waited for him to get up and then tried a kick to the gut, but it was caught, and Allen felt like he was finally going to make an offensive, that is until the enziguri caught him in the back of the head. He seemed to be out like a refrigerator light with the door closed. George really didn't feel like toying with him any longer, and just stood in the corner, waiting paitiently.
Little Nasty slowly made it to his feet, turned around, and was clocked in the jaw with an outstanding superkick. George leaped up to the top rope and worked the crowd a little bit. He leaped, and connected with an outstanding frog splash. He layed across his opponent, on his back, and just waited. Three seconds later, he was starting the celebrations.
Winner: "Playboy" George (w/ Nick Merchant) via pinfall Time: 10:11 Match 7: "The X-TREME Superstar" Zach Ryan vs. "Swing Man" Chris Boehanan It seemed like Boehanan's thoughts were in the same place as his partners in the previous match-up, as Allen Reynolds had his place in the books handed to him, so did Chris, neither of them the way they had planned. Chris did come down to the ring prepared. He brought with him a trash can, and sticking out of it was seen a kendo stick, an alluminum baseball bat, and a hockey stick for sure, but when he dumped it out in the ring, many more items seemed to appear, chains, brass knuckles, the works. He put on the knuckles and stood in the ring, waiting for Ryan to come out, but he was surprised if you will, when his head was implated into the very trash can he brought down to the ring with him.
Then, to add a bit of insult to the attack, he scooped Swing Man up, and slammed him down on that same trash can. Zach simply looked around the ring at all his beautiful new toys, and grabbed the hockey stick, he held it a little while, inspecting it, and then cracked it against Chris's stomach. He was laying the beating onto him, for being dumb enough to bring all these things to the ring. But Ryan knew what he wanted, and he saw plenty of them out there.
He ducked to the outside and grabbed steel chair after steel chair and chucked them into the ring, eight in all, then he made his way back to the ring, but
Swing Man was on fire at this point, he had one of the chairs ready, and charged the ropes at Zach, but great timing, and Zach pulled down the ropes, sending Chris right over the top and to the floor. The battle on the floor waged on, and all Boehanan really tried to do was save himself, by attempting to get back into the ring, but that kept the match from a possible countout.
He got sent into the guardrail, quite a few times actually, but the final time, he was clothesline right over it and into a mass of empty seats, but the spectators were moving into that general area, to get closer to the action than totally neccisary. Eventually the action moved back into the ring, but the toll was high for Chris Boehanan. He wasn't in the ring very long when Ryan finally noticed the brass knucks he was wearing... this sent Zach into a rage.
He scooped up Boehanan and tombstone piledrove him, right into a heap of steel chairs. He carried the limp body of Swing Man over to the corner, and climbed the turnbuckle... some how he was able to lift all that dead weight, and nailed the Acid Indegestion. The cover and count came up for him.
Winner: "The X-TREME Superstar" Zach Ryan via pinfall Time: 16:34 The Main Event: Match 8: "Ravenous" Ryan Jacob vs. "The Evil" angrysalad angrysalad showed up in the ridiculous attire he had previously been seen in, coming down to the ring, dancing to The Hustle, doing what possibly he considers to be the angrysalad dance. But the mood changed when the A-Town fans came out to be on their feet once again for Ryan Jacobs. The one sign in the crowd simply read, "Ah-lin-tun's proud." As "Sabatoge" was playing, the fans new what was ready to come. Jacob got the mic from the ring announcer before beginning, and made some comments regarding salad's sexuality no longer in question, and "stinking like shit" to hold true. salad then had a few words regarding that "Mister Jacobs is just a meany hissyfiss." And with a confused look on his face, Ryan began to assualt the caped crusader. salad didn't even know what to make of the situation. He blocked what he could, and fired back when able, and some how turned it all around on Jacobs. He whipped him to the ropes, and got him with a good drop toe hold. And like a bird through the night, he flew to the top rope, and swooped down on an unaware Jacobs as soon as he reached his feet, knocking him down with a top rope bulldog. salad then began to wave his arms around and run around the ring as if he were a chicken with his head cut off. He got a little too carried away, however, and fell on the hard end of a powerslam from Ryan Jacobs. Jacobs then placed himself on the top rope long enough to stare at the fans, and then drop down, legs across the ropes for a split-legged moonsault. angrysalad was in pain, that much was obvoius, but he fought through it and kicked out an attempted pinfall. He got a hold of Ryan when he reached his feet, and locked their arms together. He began to headbutt Ryan, three times, until he finally threw him backwards with a head and arm suplex. He ran to the ropes, but leaped to the top, and flipped backward with a moonsault, but Jacobs had long since moved out of the way, and he was ready to try something for impact. He ran up the turnbuckles and jumped as hard as he could, flipping backward, and somehow managed to connect a frog splash-sault. The crowd was rivited and on it's feet. He hooked the legs, but only managed a two count. The battle raged onward, switching back and forth with high risk moves, but no grand pay-off for either. Ryan got a little frustated with salad, and went outside to find his weapon from Tuesday, and ripped the ring announcer from his new wooden chair. He slid it into the ring, and opened it. He ran off the ropes, and tried to jump off the chair, but salad closed the chair, right before he would've landed on it, and Jacobs wiped out hard. salad pulled up Jacobs, right over the chair, and attempted a DDT, but Ravenous threw him down to the mat and quickly jumped with a leg drop, but angrysalad rolled out of the way, and he got nothing but chair. The time limit was approaching, as the time keeper stated they were at the five minutes remaining mark, but they kept exchanging, move for move, but a high cross body block attempt from angrysalad was somehow caught by the 155 pounder with a little help and support from the ring ropes, and he slam salad down hard on the mat, and went straight to the top. He leaped with his moonsault guillotine legdrop, and Oh My God was all that could be said. He hooked the legs, and got the well-earned three count.
