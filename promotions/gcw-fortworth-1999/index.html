Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Global Championship Wrestling opened in Fort Worth, Texas, in May 1999, and its only surviving document is the results report of a house show it staged a month later, on 4 June 1999, at its own GCW Arena — down to the account: 829 tickets sold, $3,523.25 taken at the gate and a further $2,083.75 at concessions, a profit of $2,662.00 on the night against $5,007.25 banked so far, and a final show time of two hours, thirty-one minutes and forty-four seconds. The report notes that over half the crowd had been there the night before, too, at a card no document of its own survives to record.

The card ran eight matches. “The Man from the Alpes” Anton Strong opened by pinning “The Insane One” with what the report calls the Alpes Bomb, a belly-to-belly suplex; “Dynomite” Derek Sweger made Billy B. Hayes tap to the Sweger Scream, a Texas Cloverleaf; TeRrOr beat Vincent Crow by pinfall after a chair shot and a beer taken from a ringside fan; and “Icon” Ron Elliot finished Cadaver with a sleeper the report names the ICONoclast.

The Y-KiKi KiD outlasted Al “The Knife” Hoolibay by working his legs; “ Playboy” George, at ringside with Nick Merchant, put away “Little Nasty” Allen Reynolds with a frog splash; “The X-TREME Superstar” Zach Ryan beat “Swing Man” Chris Boehanan in a weapons brawl with a move the report calls the Acid Indegestion; and in the main event, “Ravenous” Ryan Jacobs beat angrysalad — who wrestled the night in his own dancing, caped gimmick — with a moonsault guillotine legdrop.

No document survives after that night: no further card, no title match, and no notice of how or when GCW closed.
