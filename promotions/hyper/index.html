A GeoCities-hosted online federation, self-dated to almost three years old by 1999, whose annual hardcore pay-per-view crowns a first Hardcore Champion inside a burning barbed-wire cage. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

HYPER was already “almost 3 years” old by its own count when its April and May 1999 recruitment posts described it, placing its founding around 1996. Hosted on GeoCities, it billed itself plainly: “the best ONLINE federation for almost 3 years.”

Its own annual hardcore pay-per-view, Bloody Games (22 May 1999), staged a four-man tournament to crown its first-ever Hardcore Champion. The semifinals alone left both winners marked: Plymoth outlasted the Exterminator in a chair-and-blood brawl after his own mystery second turned the tide, and the self-described insane Dr. Diabolo beat Iceman by suplexing a steel barrier on top of him for the pin.

The undercard settled its own running scores. The Judge beat his own former mentor, Capt Hook, in a stretcher match, ending what the broadcast called “the rule of terror.” Steve Liermann won his own reinstatement to the roster, teaming with a newly arrived mystery partner, John Sebastian, to beat the Darksiders. And Mithras Hart took the Intercontinental Title from Mr. Genesis in a first-blood match, refusing to bleed through a baseball bat and two chair shots before finally opening the champion's own head with one.

The main event closed the tournament inside a barbed-wire steel cage set to catch fire after fifteen minutes. Dr. Diabolo's own clothes caught alight mid-match; Plymoth put the fire out on him with a DDT and pinned him to become the promotion's first Hardcore Champion — only for the newly dethroned Mithras Hart to emerge from the smoke and choke him out with a bullwhip, promising the arena worse to come.

The broadcast promised a following week's show, HYPERactive. No further document survives, and whether that show or any later card ran, or how the promotion ended, is not in the surviving record.
