AOLWF (Wild Card, 2000) — Card

American Online Wrestling Federation: Wild Card Announcement

23 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens up to the AOLWF Studios in Los Angeles, California. You can see the director, and the camera people preparing Jim Ross for LIVE Television. As usual Good Ol' JR has his Stenson Black Cowboy hat on, and a dark red AOLWF
Shirt. There's a TV screen behind him which shows clips of the wrestlers in the
AOLWF, and as soon as the director starts the countdown it goes to a live feed of JR. 5.....4...3....2....1...)
Jim: Hi everyone this is the very first AOLWF Report, and I'm Jim Ross comin' to ya live from the AOLWF Studios in Hollywood. There's a lotta stuff we need to get to here, but first I want to welcome ya'll. This is going to be a great ride, and I can't wait to get things started. Okay so now let me bring up the first ever AOLWF Card...which is going to be a Supercard. LIVE on FOX SPORTS, and it's going to be a real barn burner. I've got the Supercard Run down, and it looks like things are going to be added. Let me show you what we have right now.
WILD CARD October 29th, 2000 Live From City Walk at Universal Studios Hollywood, California Buff Bagwell vs. Hunter Hearst Helmsley The Sandman vs. Kurt Angle The Rock/Mystery Partner vs. 911/Big Dick Dudley (The Extreme Team) Now those are the matches that we've got right now. Of course there should be some more added as the weeks progress. Fans I'm tellin' you this as Good Ol'
JR...I would not miss this for the world. Mark it down on your calender, cancel your plans. Do whatever you have to do, because this is going to be one heck of
Wild Card!! For now I'm JR....and keep those BBQ signs up for me!!!
(Camera pulls away from Ross once again, and the director is talking to him as the cameras fade.)
