American Online Wrestling Federation: Sake of Conversation

21 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Okay just for the sake of conversation I'm going to bring up a few things. I've talked to a few people online, and from this RSPWF group and most of them have the same thing to say...
"Using Real Wrestler Feds, you have no chance for growth with the character." Of course I'm going to have to disagree. The reason is because how does a character grow? Honestly how has Triple H grown in the past year and a half?
Not because of his mic skills. I mean that has something to do with it, but that isn't the REAL Reason.
The REAL Reason behind it all is because of the writers. The people who makes the storylines, who make the angles, who put Triple H in the situations that he's in. They are the ones who get the ball rolling, and who get the character to be not so 1 Dimensional. These guys put a love triangle between Steph, her husband, and an outsider...and these guys react to it in the manner they can based on a million different things. I'd have to say that Triple H has grown in his character in the past year more than anyone else in the WWF, and now I think we're going to see other people become more 3D....like the Rock. He's going to have to choose between being a Face or sticking with blood eventually.
It's basic knowledge...but is he going to stick to his usual plan? NO...why...because he can't...HE HAS TO GROW. My point is that while some people may say that you can't grow in feds like the
AOLWF...I say you can. You can because the writing can make it happen. The writing will draw you so far in that you're going to have to give your guy a new side.
Someone told me the other day that in Real Wrestler feds you HAVE to stick to the original character. Although that is not true either...some of the funniest things have taken place in Real Wrestler feds.
Imagine Paul Wight being fed up with being called a "Giant" so he decides to team with Rey and be a Luchador.
What if Bob Backlund tries to get in touch with the "Young Kids" of today and tries to join DX?
What if Mark Madden said "Spinnaroni" one more time and a fan jumped out of the crowd and stabbed him in the eye with a fork then lit his fat ass on fire?
You characters do grow with the AOLWF, and it's just as much fun. It's not so much that Real Wrestler feds are crappy...or they aren't fun...it's the fact that they are different.
Don't you think it's time that Fake feds have some competition? I mean we've been doing this whole fake fed thing for years, but you haven't given the other a real try...WHY?
Maybe it's because you joined one a long time ago and it sucked. Maybe it's because you have been told by one of the TOP Rp'ers in the E-Fed world that "Real Wrestler feds suck...I am an E-Fed God follow me. "
Some of you people I know what to try it out....but you're afraid of what is going to happen to you. Maybe you'll like it? Maybe your friends will hate you because of it...HAHAH anyway the fact is that if you're thinking about it...you should at least try it.
We're all on this board to find a fed that gives us quality cards, lots of good storylines, and something fun to do while we're on the computer.
I am promising you that this is what the AOLWF offers. Take my word and you'll enjoy it for years to come....pass me up...and you'll be looking up at us eventually. I WILL get this fed off the ground...
