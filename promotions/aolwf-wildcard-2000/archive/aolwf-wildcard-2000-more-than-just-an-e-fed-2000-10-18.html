American Online Wrestling Federation: More Than Just an E-Fed

18 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Look I'm not going to come out here and talk about how our fed is the best, and how we have all this glitz and glamour. The fact is that the AOLWF is very new, but has the staff and the drive to become one of the most talked about feds on the planet.
I look on this RSWPF and I see a ton of talent. The card writers, the E-fed presidents, and I think you all could kick WCW's ass in a heart beat. Although
I see one thing that's missing, and an over abundance of another.
What is with there being no REAL Wrestler feds? Now don't get me wrong I know you people think that having Benoit fight Austin or what have you is a little uncreative, but my friends and I are the best creative team you're ever going to see. So with that I give you the...
*American Online Wrestling Federation. This is a fed that is going to have real wrestlers compete against each other for titles, respect, and a whole lot more.
How many times have you sat back and watched Rocky face Triple H and say this is stale? How many times have you said that you wish they would MOVE IT on this damn
Hunter/Steph/Kurt storyline?
How many times have you said that you wish that Booker T would stop wearing that disgusting white underwear he wears?
Or that you want to see Madden get a fork shoved in his eye, and then light his fat ass on fire?
IT CAN HAPPEN here in the AOLWF. We're not here just to book you matches, and we're not here to just go woth the flow. The AOLWF is going to be the best Real
Wrestler fed that you've ever seen, and that is a damn promise.
With your Role Plays and our storylines...and the heat that we'll build for the matches...it's going to be like your in the real thing.
The AOLWF is going to give you much more than matches. It's going to give you an experience like no other fed has.
If you're interested please let me know, and I'll get back to you ASAP. - [name withheld]
