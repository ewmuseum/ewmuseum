Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A newsgroup pitch introduces the promotion in October 2000 as brand new, promising real wrestlers competing for titles under booked storylines rather than a simple simulation. The founding post frames the idea as an answer to a gap on the board: a real-wrestler-portrayal circuit built with the same booked heat and angles as the fan-written federations already crowding the newsgroup.

A further round of recruiting posts followed within days, each repeating the same pitch and inviting anyone curious to get in touch, and a longer piece laid out the promotion's own creative case for why a real-roster federation could still let a character grow. One reply weighed in on where the pitch had landed, suggesting a better-suited audience for it; another reply, days later, reported slow but steady interest and a plan to open a tournament for the promotion's own world title once enough talent had signed on.

The one surviving broadcast is a debut supercard announcement: an in-character studio segment naming an October 29, 2000 show at Universal Studios Hollywood and three real-wrestler matchups meant to headline it. No result, roster or further broadcast survives past that announcement, and nothing in the held record says whether the card was ever staged.

The initials it chose were not new to the newsgroup: an earlier, unrelated federation had carried the same three letters through 1996 and 1997, three years before this one introduced itself under its own, slightly different full title. Nothing beyond the shared letters ties the two together.
