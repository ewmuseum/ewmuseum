Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Cyber Championship Wrestling opened on 7 January 2001 with a broadcast from a sold-out Staples Center. Its front-office figure, Brian Knight, stepped out of a limousine in a black business suit, and its announcers promised a federation where the playing field was level and the opening match counted as much as the main event.

The first night ran three matches. Thomas DiArgo, arriving with another federation's television title over his shoulder and a speech against every front office that had held him back, won the first of them, pinning "Silky Smooth" Ricky Perez with the D-Drop. "Texas" Jack Williams beat Mark Destructo, a surprise replacement for an opponent who never showed, with a top-rope neckbreaker called the Long Neck Bottle.

The main event settled a grudge six months old: the Renaissance Man, back from the broken ribs Doug Fury had given him, pinned Fury with the Shattered Dream after the unannounced arrival of "Outlaw" Joe Wayans froze Fury in the ring. The broadcast closed on Fury and Wayans staring each other down.

Knight used the same night to promise a Royal Rumble the following week, open to anyone entered by the next Friday, to crown the first CCW Heavyweight Champion, with the federation's Head Booker to be introduced at its end. No later card survives.
