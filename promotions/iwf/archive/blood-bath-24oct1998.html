Saturday Night Blood Bath — 24 October 1998

24 Oct 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
IWF Main Page IWF Control Centre Blood Bath Card Classic Wrestling IWF _ Newsboard IWF Saturday Night Blood Bath Recap Sunday 25th October 1998 - 17:00 (pg1) Welcome to the first edition of IWF re-cap to be broadcast from the IWF Additions site. This saturdays card saw the second bracket of the Brawl for tournament decided as well as a few shock results. Moving on to the first offering of the evening.... Straight into title action with the current North American Tag-Champions TX taking on Armageddon in a stipulated no re-match bout.  Theres no doubting that the biggest task facing Seek and Destroy is getting over with the fans, the match was bearly a minute old before chants of boring, boring echoed around the arena.  In fairness to the competitors the match wasn't boring at all.  The early exchanges saw TX taking control with Armageddon gaining the upper hand courtesy of a fire extinguisher in the middle stages. As the match rolled on Armageddons 'win at all costs' policy came back to haunt them.  With Triple X standing on the turnbuckle ready to hit the spinning DDT Damon Storm couldn't resist the temptation to become involved.  Foolishly he shoved Triple X off the turnbuckle in full view of referee James Beard.  You can guess the rest, DQ for the newcomers.  Triple X takes the mic and promises a re-match for Halloween Hell "Under their rules". A good starter for the evening with the only confusing point that Triple X and Phoenix hold the belts ...  I've checked the tape and it was definately MaK and Parzon that won the titles so what are these guys doing with them ???? The first of the evenings Brawl for Matches was up next.  The crowd were treat to a bout between North American Champ Coma and Youngbloods leader Bob "Monkey" Charlie.  The animosity between these two has been well documented and quite how they'd react in a shoot was anybodys guess.  With the Monkey making his return to the IWF after a lengthy lay off you can probably expect another verbal war between these guys. The first round was taken by Coma despite what the scorecards said.  Apparently Charlie managed two takedowns!! Not from where I was standing but its controvosy like that that makes these things interesting. The second and third rounds belonged to Coma who advanced into the next round. The next Brawl for all saw Jorgy of Team X-treme taking on 'Red Hot' Neil Scott of the Crash Team.  With two smaller guys in the ring the contest was faster paced than the Coma/Charlie bout and the fans at ringside watched a one sided first round as 'Red Hot' found himself on the wrong side of three takedowns. Facing a large deficit 'Red Hot' had to throw caution to the wind and came out looking for takedowns and found two of them.  With the scores tied and both men needing a big third round it was Jorgy who stepped up to the plate with a series of heavy punches and three takedowns for the win.  Jorgy will face Coma in an interesting conflict of styles in the next round. In lightweight action Youngblood member Superstar put on yet another impressive job racking up his third straight defeat to newcomer Wan Chung Hai, who himself was fighting on the back of two unsucessful championship bouts.  The crowd again showed thier lack of interest in the lightweight division by agian chanting 'boring,boring' which was again unfair.  A 360 Tornado kick brought about the 1-2-3 for Hai. Moving away from the matches for a second, IWF fans will know that Friday night marked the return of the Youngbloods to action.  Charlie and Cloak both spent some time ragging on Coma apparently critical of the fact that they believe he dosn't aim high enough and mocked him for sitting at home 'polishing his runners-up belt'.  They then both went on to spell out their championship ambitions leaving Psycho to re-ignite the fire between himself and Lightweight Champ Todd 'Ratboy' Prentiss.  No word from the Crash Team yet, but I'm sure they'll have plenty to say about what has been said, and I for one can't wait to see the match between Psycho and Ratboy.  Initially pencilled in for a Ratboys Domain match at In your computer the fans have been denied this match for a long time, personally I hope the front office don't throw these two at each other two quickly, they could do with time to build up the heat that they once had. In a grossly unfair bout the Japenese sensation Yoshihiro Tenzan fought out a handicap match against the Mexican Marauders.  A couple of weeks ago I speculated on a face turn for Tenzan and it seems couldn't have been further from the truth.  With hostility usually reserved for the Grim Reaper and Coma the crowed launched into a 'Tenzan sucks' chant.  On this performance Tenzan certainly dosn't suck ... no he didn't win but who would have expected him to ? Rico Robriguez won it for the marauders with a suave buster after Tenzan had lasted a respectable 11 minutes. No sooner had Tenzan got to his feet than Damon Storm made his second unscheduled appearance of the evening.  Weilding a baseball bat he knocked Tenzan back down leaving his nose blooded before grinning all the way back to the dressing rooms. The Brawl for All theme continued with two time IWF World Champion Rob 'The Legend' Williams doing battle with Archangel.  The bout started at a pace it was never going to continue at and we saw a slick first round with the former Navy Seal just edging the punches count and picking up a couple of takedowns along the way. Unfortunately for Archangel Williams stepped up a gear in the second round.  Taking two takedowns and easily winning the punch count Williams left Archangel without a point. With pride at steak... I mean stake, sorry getting a bit hungry there, Archangel was able to salvage the last round with a takedown and punch count victory over Williams' single takedown.  End result - Williams advances. [PART TWO] IWF Main Page IWF Control Centre Blood Bath Card Classic Wrestling IWF _ Newsboard IWF Saturday Night Blood Bath Recap Sunday 25th October 1998 - 17:00 (pg2) A forgone conclusion the next one.  John Constantine, the former North American Champ facing lightweight contender Tommy Gunn in a brawl for all match-up.  Lightweight vs. Heavyweight, the heavyweight's gonna take it right??  Wrong !!Constantine looked decidedly out of his element and found himself throwing plenty of leather but just too slow on the takedowns.  The first and third rounds went to Gunn with Constantine edging the second.  As Gunn celebrated Constantine headed off to the locker room looking a little frustrated and disappointed with himself. This result leaves Gunn facing Williams in the second round the winner to face the victor of the Coma/Jorgy boubt to decide bracket twos finalist. Back to some good old fashioned wrestling next as we see Intercontinental Champion Curt Raines squaring off against Cerberus. The match started off well enough with both guys giving it their all.  Raines was probably just getting the upper hand when disaster struck.  Everyone is well aware of the animosity that has built up between Parzon and Raines as of late.  Both guys are convinced that they are Minnesotas greatest gift to the world of e-wrestling and they seem hell bent on proving that to each other. Parzon made his way down to ringside and all parties concerned knew it was only a matter of time before he made an impression on the evening. With the match at the 16 minute mark Parzon threw the ringbell to Cerberus who, in opportunistic fashion used it to clean Raines' clock.  The winner and new Intercontinental Champion ... Cerberus. As Raines struggled to get to his feet he was forced to endure the gloating of a pleased Parzon. Word from the dressing room is that Raines was furious with Parzon after the match and promised him a serious injury at Halloween Hell. Looking forward to that one!! The main event of the evening saw a four way dance between Akira, MaK, Constantine and Spu (???) with the winner to receive a shot at Coma at Halloween Hell. In a good long match John Constantine shook off any ill effects of his brawl for all defeat and emerged the eventual winner. Despite interference from the rest of Team X-treme MaK didn't make a huge impact on the bout,  the Senton Bomb was used to eliminate Spu before TX again became involved by distracting the ref.  There was no helping MaK as the bout hit the 24th minute.  Constantine with a show of strength picked up MaK and dumped him onto the mats. Not wasting any more time Constantine went straight to work and eliminated his team mate and Warrior Clan leader Akira in less than ten seconds. So it looks like Coma-v-Constantine IV for Halloween Hell, quite how these guys end up paired up do often is a little odd but great for the fans to see such talents in the ring again. All in all the card was solid and entertaining, with only a week to go till Halloween Hell you can expect a lot of interviews over the next week to help build some heat. Till next week ....
