 [-Finally the figure quits swinging on the punching bag and turns toward the camera to pick up his water and you can tell right away that this is none other then Mitch Gatlin. Mitch has His long black hair up in a tight pony tail and is not wearing a shirt.
You can see that Mitch has several rather large scars on his chest and stomach. He also has on some black and red elastic Nike shorts with a pair of Nike Air
Max.-]  M "SF" G: Whew. . . That takes a lot out of a man.  [-Mitch pick's up his bottle of water and unscrews the cap and takes a huge gulp.-]  M "SF" G: I am just trying to get a jump start on my coemption. While they are all out partying, drinking, and sleeping. I am in the gym working out as hard as possible.
I have been here every day for the past week. I will always be one step ahead of any of my opponents. I want to make sure that I am faster, stronger, and smarter than anyone who steps in the ring with me. I also like to know that I am always on top of my game before I get into the ring.
 [-Mitch starts back punching the bag as he talks.-]  M "SF" G: "You see I have been in C.R.O.W for less than a week now and I already have a match. I have tonight to sorry excuse for wrestlers. They both are in need of some mental help. Now let me focus on my two opponents that good ol' Joel has lined up for me. I am facing Shawn Jamison and "The Human
Fireball" Smoke. Well,Shawn was the first ever sadistic champion so that has to mean something and Smoke was the first ever World Champion so that also has to mean something."
"You two wanna know what that means? Do you really want to know? Well, it means you to boys _was_ the best. It is a new age of wrestling and you can consider me the
representee. Now you are just a bit over the hill. You two were the first champions in C.R.O.W. That means you also was the first ones to ever lose the titles. So the way I see it is that any man can win a title, But it takes A real man to keep it." You both had your chances to be the "top dog" in the

C.R.O.W in its entire history. I am the _Future_. You are looking at greatness. I am going to prove my self by making an example out of you two.
Both of you are going to be made a fool of on your first day back. What kind of welcome home present is that for two former champions. You boys picked a bad time to come back to the C.R.O.W. There is a new _superstar_ in town and he is here to stay."
 [-Mitch starts punching the bag faster and throwing longer more drown out combos in to his punches.-]
 M "SF" G: "You see I am going into this match with something I have to prove and to top that off my back is against the wall and I have nothing to lose. That makes me a very dangerous man coming into this match."
 "Shawn you are going to be in a new world of hurt. You have never endured the pain I am going to put upon you. After this match you will know. That you are not the a Hard-core legend and that you are far from it. You see Shawn the difference between me and you is it is all about winning to you. For me on the other hand it is all about proving my point and I will win in the process of doing that."
 "Shawn just because you run around with a barb-wire bat all the time you think it makes you Hard-core. Jamison I have a few weapons up my sleeve that are not hard-core but Extreme. You see Hard-core and Extreme are different. I will show you this difference this Monday and I promise you one thing. Extreme hurts a lot more than hard-core does."
 [-Mitch stops punching the bag. He wipes the sweat from his forehead and reaches down and grab's his bottle of water. Mitch takes a big gulp of water and lay's down on his stomach. He extends his arms and starts doing push ups.-]
 M "SF" G: "Now onto a more mysterious man. "The Human Fireball" Smoke. Smoke you say you come straight from hell but that is funny I did not see you there. You was not in Woodland Hills. You did not have death in your face everyday. You did not have to make choices that deiced if you lived or you died. On an everyday basis. Smoke quit being fake and act as if you are not something out of a science fiction story because let me tell you Hell is real.
I should know I lived there for two years. It is not something you want to pretend you live in because if you want to go there so bad I will give you a taste of what hell is like come Monday. Smoke for you I am going to let my actions speak louder than words. Just remember you can't fight fire with fire unless you want to get _burnd_!"
 [-Mitch gets up from pumping off about a hundred push ups and gets right back on to the punching
bag.-]  M "SF" G: "I have been fighting to survive for so long now that I just do not know how not to. Jamison and Smoke are match is not going be a wrestling match or a fight but it shall be the survival of the fittest. It is not about who wins any more but it is about who will survive."
 "I thrive on the giving and receiving of pain. There is nothing I would like to do more than to look down and see Jamison and Smoke laying in a puddle of there on Blood. I am after
Adrian "The Hero" Sicily. Adrian you are the one who should be worried. These men are just a small example of what I am going to do to you when I get my hands on you.
