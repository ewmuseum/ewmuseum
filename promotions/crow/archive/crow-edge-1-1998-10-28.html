Upcoming News ++CROW REOPENS++ Recently, the CROW President, [name withheld] had a sudden change of heart and due to his un successful work with the Seattle Wrestling Promotions, he has now resigned as the head of that establishment and now looks like to once more sit back and pull the strings at his old establishment, the CROW. A press conference has been held and it was hopefully a hit, thus in this update, the proposed line-up, if the CROW is to open is below. Once again, it is you, the athletes who made the CROW what it once was, so if you want it back then tell me so. ++CROW IS ON THE LOOK.++ As you can see from the limited roster, which the CROW has, it is a shame and thus now, measures are being taken to hopefully recruit new and prospected athletes. I do not believe on taking athletes from other federations, so forget it. I will look on and post advertisements on RSPWF about the CROW reopening and then hopefully, I will get a response. More on this in future weeks. ++A Main Event could hit the CROW.++ The CROW is in talks with a great all round athlete and if he is recruited then consider him to show you the way the English do business and why he is the Main Event. ++The cards are dealt.++ A future hall of famer could be on his way to the newly formed CROW. This man is what some people might call a good all round kind of guy, but most of all, he is a superb athlete with a lot of potential. His gambling skills and wrestling skills go hand in hand. ++Flag who is he??++ The mysterious athlete who was once spotted at the last CROW PPV, Scorch Wave 98, who was he and what business does he have with the CROW, find out in recent weeks. Now that's all for the recent news. II.
