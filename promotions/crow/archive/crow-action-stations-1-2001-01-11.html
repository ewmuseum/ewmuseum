Kind regards ~[name withheld] CROW C.E.O for the Past Five Years http://crowrestling.com [ -- Under construction -- ] --------------------------------------------------------------------- THE CYBER REALM OF WRESTLING prepares its path back into the mainstream of sports entertainment. JANUARY 15th, live from CHICAGO comes The FIRST
EVER EDITION OF ACTION STATIONS... In the opening contest, the Former CROW Skyweight and Sadistic Champion
"THE PURE DRUG" LEVI ASH prepares the long road back to the top.
Standing in his way is the biggest stumbler a man could wish for, in
ADRIAN "THE HERO" SICILY. "The Hero" upon winning The Sadistic
Championship at Canadian Strife III has turned into a complete monster. Yet what affect wil this have on Levi? Has Adrian Sicily really split ways with Mario Dante? And just what is going on with the Sadistic title? Whatever happens, this sure is set to be a great opening contest.
The action then continues as two teams with a score to settle. THE
LITTLEST PET SHOP before the CROW had its problems was the #1 contenders and looked to have Rising Sun Revolution stumbling, that was until CATZ
N' DOGZ showed up. The Pitbull and The Stray Cat. Now these two teams shall meet for the first time and maybe just see which of them will be going for a Cat KNAP. And what with Jay Antiss watching this match closley, the tag team title scene will really be hotting up.
Since the CROW SKYWEIGHT Title has been VACTED due to a VERY unique
Challenge being ISSUED, the CROW has SANCTIONED A SIX MAN ROUND ROBIN
TOURNAMENT IN WHICH THE WINNER SHALL BE THE NEW SKYWEIGHT CHAMPION..
In The First of these Skyweight Battles, for the first time ever, MARIO
"THE ICEMAN" DANTE is set to go into the ring in singles competition.
Mario Dante, a man who nobody knows the style of,goes up against "THE
LIGHT BEARER" RUPERT FRANCIS, and personal assitant of "The Night
Haunter" Kyle Bain. Will "The Death Dealer" be watching on? And just what is the situation between "The Hero" and his tag team partner Mario
"The Iceman" Dante
The Second match in the CROW SKYWEIGHT ROUND ROBIN gets underway as Two
Former Skyweight Champions are matched together. In this match, There has never been a war like this. "EXOTIC" SEAN ISLE, the former Skyweight
Champion, a man who has an ego the size of a grapefruit, a man who took the WADANCER to new heights and at Canadian Strife III inside the realms of the steel cage, took the Wardancer's career from under him.
Wardancer, a man who due to the actions of Sean Isle has been called out of retirement, hoping that this time, he can wrong the rights that Isle has done.. But above all, once more prove why he is the Greatest
Skyweight the CROW has ever had.. The Network Title is then On the line as Current Champion and the cockiest wrestler around, "MR. TECHNICAL" JEFFERSON MONROE places the gold on the line against the former Network Champion, a man known only as "THE DRIFTER" ABBOT RIGHTEOUS. Will Monroe be taken to the school of
Science and get a lesson he'll never forget or can "The Drifter" drift his way over The Technical One.
Our Triultimate match of the night sees "THE BEEFCARVER" MATT FUNK, a former World Heavyweight Champion, he makes his CROW return after 1.5 years away from the ring. On the other side of the coin you have
JOHNATHAN HAMMER, a man who is clearly "The King Of Extreme". So what will happen when these two sadistic individuals get it on. And will this be a case of Hammering or shall Hammer be Carved up..
The PENULTIMATE match of the evening sees a spot of SADISTIC competition as TWO men VOWING for some BLOOD are set to do battle in a TWO OUT OF
THREE FALLS CONTEST. The Sadistic title ONCE held by Adrian Sicily before the CROW went on heitus, yet now since the CROW has it's fresh start, the maniacial JAGGED and the Devilish Man of God BISHOP himself shall do whatever it takes to prove their worth and not to mention to show just why these two men really defeine the word "hard-core".
Then it hits us.. The MAIN EVENT.. RICK STEVENS places the CROW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP on the line against not one but TWO other men. Not just ordinary men, but these are
"THE WARRIOR" LOGAN GUNZ and the only FIVE Time CROW World Champion "THE
TEXAS WHIRLWIND" STEVE HOUSTON. And what with this match being A SEVEN
TABLES OF FEAR, and what about "THE NIGHT HAUNTER" KYLE BAIN as the
SPECIAL REFEREE.. just who SHALL SURVIVE?
