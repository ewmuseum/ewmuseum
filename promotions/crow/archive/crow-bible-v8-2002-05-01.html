What is the CROW Aspiration? -------------------------------- The CROW stands for the Cyber Realm Of Wrestling, originally born as the Cyber Wrestling Federation in March of 1996. The CROW's first televised event was the Pay Per View entitled WrestleWars I: "The Beginning of Time", here all of the champions were determined. The founder of the CROW, and still the owner is a man simply known as [name withheld], or [name withheld] for short. The CROW is a worldwide operation, spanning across many places, yet like most federations, we don't claim to be the best, we make sure that our talent has what it takes to be the best. For that's what matters. Yet like most places, the CROW has a central location. The location since 1996 has changed somewhat. In 1996, the CROW was in the state of Chicago and it stayed there until February 1998. In February 1998, the CROW decided to have an equal partnership, and thus it joined forces with [name withheld]'s, EMWO (Electronic Mail Wrestling Organization), [name withheld]'s, EWO(Extreme Wrestling Organization) and the long standing IeWC, once run by [name withheld]'s best friend, [name withheld]. Yet soon after the partnership was formed, the EMWO decided to pull out, leaving the EWO, CROW and IeWC. [name withheld] and [name withheld] then took the reigns of the partnership and the Seattle Wrestling Promotion was formed. Soon after, in November of 1998, Joel decided to withdraw his footing with the SWP and thus wanted to go it alone. Joel then brought the CROW out of Seattle and by doing so was forced to move base to the Sunshine state of Florida. In November 1998, the CROW came back and dominated for another two more years, until February 2000. In February Joel underwent a major heart operation and thus was forced out of the business for quite sometime. When Joel was on the way to recovery, he had another stumbling block, for his mother recently passed away thus his hopes of drawing back into the CROW seemed to be fading further away. Joel placed all of his money into the funeral of his mother and thus was left near broken. Yet during the grieving period, Joel received a telephone call from one of his former associates, namely [name withheld]. Jay spoke with Joel and the two of them agreed that if they could find a third member of the team, then the CROW, the dream, the baby could grow once more. Thus Joel and Jay searched high and low and in the end, [name withheld], a 13 year School friend and former owner of WOLF (Wrestling's Only Legitimate Federation) decided that he would help out Joel and thus, the partnership was made. Joel, was to be the C.E.O, [name withheld] runs the Tag Team side of operations and [name withheld] is the one with all the money, he is the finance behind the CROW and plays a major part in what happens. All was going well for the CROW until [name withheld] decided that the love of power was great and thus he decided to take full ownership of the CROW, this of course led to an upheaval in the CROW, leading to Survival V: The Eliminator. As the PPV was about to go off the air, a gunshot was heard coming from the back, as the cameras panned out you were able to see that the C.E.O: [name withheld] had been shot in the back by a mystery assailant. From here the CROW was somewhat at and end. It took nine months for [name withheld] to recover and one day whilst in the Oxford Medical School of Science, Joel spoke to one of his close friends and it was decided from here that the two of them would bring the CROW back into operation. The CROW is open to any type of wrestler...heavyweight, skyweight, tag teams...we've got divisions for all of them. The most important factor in the CROW is FUN and IMAGINATION. If you are only out to ascertain victories, then please go to some backyard fed. "For Winning is not everything, it's the taking part that counts.." --------------------------------- 2. What are the titles here? ----------------------------- The CROW currently sanctions five championships, although there have been more in the past, and we're always keeping an eye open for a new division. ---------------------- 4.1. CROW World Title ---------------------- This is the most sought after title in the CROW. If you're the CROW World Heavyweight Champion, consider yourself the best wrestler in the promotion. Also though, the one people want to be, so consider this a "real challenge" and one which the champion has to meet every thirty days. Anybody in the top 5 can apply for a shot, yet it is given at the discretion of the booking team! ------------------------------- 4.2. CROW North American Title ------------------------------- The CROW's secondary title. Holding this makes you the #1 contender to the World Title (technically), and also a target for a lot of people. This title's lineage traces back to the original CROW International Title. Also must be defended once a month although more often is encouraged. To challenge you must be in the top 10. And again given at the discretion of The Force! ------------------------ 4.3 CROW Sadistic Title ------------------------ In 1997, the CROW adopted for the first time under the guidance of Chris Montoya, the CROW Extreme Championship. Yet in February 1998, "Sadistic" Shane Alexander captured the title. The Extreme title then due to the actions of "Kraze" Kris Keplin was melted down and thus [name withheld] decided to make a special Sadistic title, dedicated to Shane Alexander, as he was the Sadistic Champion for 18 months, the longest champion ever. This has to be defended any time, any place, any way. And anybody can challenge for it. (Not like 24/7 rule in WWF. Just you can not decline a match request! Anybody who is ranked 1-4 in the Skyweight Division and is under the legal amount can challenger for it. And this belt has to be defended every thirty days. -------------------------------- 4.5. CROW World Tag Team Titles -------------------------------- The CROW World Tag Team Titles. The most sought after title for the tag teams. Quite frankly because it's the only title for tag teams. The tag titles have to be defended at the discretion of management and nobody else can say different. Yet a title defense once a month is a must. ----------------------- 5. Once known as Bring Da House Down, now known as Action Stations. Hosted weekly by our very own Jessie Gerome and John Halloway. This is the place where the really big matches take place. What have I got to do to get by? ------------------------------------ Read below. :) --------------- 6.1. Roleplays --------------- To even get noticed in the CROW, you need to constantly Role-Play, develop your characters power of speech. The way to do this is as follows: - No player may use or portray another player's character in a Roleplay unless they have prior permission by that player. If this permission has been given, make sure to forward a copy of the Roleplay to the other player when sending to me. I need the all-clear by all parties involved before "airing" a Roleplay. - No player may include the following in a roleplay; racism, religious intolerance, explicit sexual acts, extreme violence, excessive profanity, breaking of kayfabe through "inside" terminology. All ROLE-PLAYS are sent out on Saturdays. They are placed inside the Flash Packet known as Say The Word.. ------------ 6.2. Strats ------------ A Strategy is a guideline for how a player would like their character to go about acting during a particular match. It can either be a game plan that will hopefully be employed while competing in a match, or it may be describing how a character will want to become involved in the action before, during or after a match (think interference or sneak attack). A match Strategy is a description of how a player would like their character to go about their match. It should describe things such as the pace a character wants to dictate, the path a character wishes to take in breaking-down and beating an opponent, and the defense and counter-attack plans that the character would like to have in reserve. Although a Strategy may seek a certain situation to be created during a match (a "spot"), it should not specifically state a whole series of maneuvers and holds. A decent, descriptive strat. should be around 5 to 10k in size, though there are no limits in either direction. Since match Strategies hold so much impact if it is a close match in determining an outcome, it is advised that one be written for each match a character is involved in. The other type of Strategy often involves a foreign party to a particular match becoming involved. Like a match strategy, a player should present what they would like their character to carry-out. In the case of writing sneak attacks or the like, do _not_ write it in "Flash" format (written like a Roleplay). If you have dialogue for your character to say, write it by all means.. but don't assume that everything will always go as planned. And most important of all... The Post Match Strategy In the CROW, what goes on during the match tells the story, 90% of the time. Often, however, the action continues long after the match is over, and entire stories are occasionally told immediately following a match. This may consist of immediate singles match challenge interviews, or a beatdown, or a refusal to release a finishing submission hold. ------------ 6.3. Angles ------------ The backbone to any fed is good angles. Without them, you've just got a bunch of imaginary guys rolling around on the mat. We want to know why your guy wants to win, why he hates his opponent, why he wears an eyepatch in the ring, why he freaks out when his opponent goes after his knee. It's stuff like that that makes people enjoy your character's work, and makes them want to see more of him/her/them. ------------------ 7. Booking Policy ------------------ As the CROW is a booked federation. Matches are mostly determined by the booking executive comittee, yet in some cases challenges are taken and then matches are granted. Yet if you are involved with an angle and you wish to challenge to further that angle then you will be given first priority over the other athletes. ---------------------------- 7.1 How are matches judged? ---------------------------- Many factors are taken into account when deciding the outcome of an e-wrestling match. The factors of an outcome can be divided up similar to slices of a pie, with the larger slices indicating a factor with great importance. CROW match outcomes break down into the following- 60% - Role-Plays To win the match which you have been assigned, you must be creative, not just start out by saying "I'm gonna beat ya." To say that would mean an automatic loss. Role-Playing though is the key to success as it builds up your character over a period of time. 30% - Strategies This includes the plan of attack and defense that each competitor will employ during the match. This factor also takes into account interference from other parties, and generally anything that can be planned to play a part in the match. 10% Heat The term "heat" is a result of what can be achieved through consistent long-term role-playing. The interview that a player writes today is not only a factor in their upcoming match. It also builds the character's reputation, develops a storyline and encourages the character to develop. ----------- 8. Webpage ----------- Yes, an official CROW webpage does exist.
