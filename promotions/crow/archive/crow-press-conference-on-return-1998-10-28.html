Press Conference On Return

28 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The CROW Banner appears on the jumbatron] ################################################################# # # # ___ ____________ ___ __ __ # # / __| ___ | _______ | \ \ / \ / / # #/ / ||\\ | | | | \ \ / /\ \ / / # #\ \ || \\ | | | | \ \ / / \ \ / / # # \ \__ || // | | | | \ \/ / \ \/ / # # \___/ || \_\ | |_______| | \ / \ / # # |___________| \_/ \/ # # http://www.geocities.com/colosseum/park/8943/index.htm # ################################################################# [The distinct sound of "Static by 'Bleach' blares from the PA system, a voice-over is then heard and then the music softens as the voice echoes.]
VO: "Some people say there is a place where heroes are found and legends are made. Others say that this place already exists, yet there is only one."
[The voice over then fades and then the music gets louder, the voice then softly comes back on as the music instantly stops.]
VO: "There is a place where the truth does lie and a place where it does indeed set you free, a place not far from here, and a place where the world sees what is in store."
[The voice then begins to crackle as the sound of sirens going off in the distance is then heard, the noise then gets louder and then it erupts into a fury. The voice then comes back on and this time it pauses as it says the words.]
VO: "The place does exist and the Elite are what we seek, the world shall know what we are and where we stand, for situated here is the worlds only legitimate federation and a place where
"Dreams Become Reality." [The voice then crackles as the music blares up and then the distance music fades as the graphics of the previous show go off the air. The following graphics in silver texture with marble intersects then hits the screens.]
______ _____ ______ ________ _________ /____ // /__\\ / ____/ /___ ___/ /________/ // / // _____// /___ /\ __ / / /\ /\ // _______ // / // / / ____// \ / / / / / \ / /// /___ __/ //___/ // / / /___ / /\ \ / / / / / /\ \ / /// // /______//_/ /_____//_/ \ \/ /__/ /__ / / \ \/ /// // \__//______//_/ \__///______// /________/ __________ / _______/ / / _________ _________ _ / / /_____ //_____ // \ __ \ \ // / /// / // \ / / \ \ // / /// / // /\ \ / / __\ ///____/ ///____/ // / \ \ / / /___//________//________//__/ \ \/ / \ / \/ [The graphics then fade into the front office of the CROW President, Joel Tennuchi. Joel Tennuchi then sitting at his exclusive desk coated with silver texture, black leather chair and a video recorder on the top of the desk. The camera then positions itself around and then it goes back to the desk and it then focuses in on what is appearing on the video monitor.]
{Derek Douglas now, with a hard Irish whip into the ropes, Douglas then setting up Jamison for the Flatliner. NO! From the crowds, it is Steve
Houston, Houston then begins to attack Derek Douglas. Jamison then sneaks up behind a now bloody Douglas and the cover:
. . . . . . . . . . 1 . . . . . . . . 2 . . . . . . 3 SM: WE HAVE A NEW CHAMPION! SHAWN JAMISON IS THE NEW TV CHAMPION!} [The camera then pauses as Joel Tennuchi begins to speak.] JT - "Now those were the days, I remember when these two men were great athletes, yet what happened, well I guess for some reason, I decided to lose it all and when I look back, can I really bring the CROW back to the status which it was at before it closed down.?"
[Joel Tennuchi then ponders, then continues.] JT - "What about it, could it be done. Can the CROW be once more formed into the place it once was, is it going to happen. If so, then when? Well after looking at the decisions which I made, I have to say not one of them reflected against what the superstars of CROW wanted. I deserted them, went against what they wanted and then what happened, well I guess it all went down hill. The CROW was my home, I built it and then I destroyed it, well why? I guess I just got tired of it all, but now I think to myself, well
"Enough is enough, and it's time to bring it back." [The cameraman then begins to chuckle, Joel Tennuchi then starts up again] JT - "It is no joking matter, I have decided that come November 25th 1998, what once stood proud amongst the rest is once more going to do it again. I lost it all when I closed the CROW and thus, as many have wanted it, it is now going to happen and thus November 10th 1998, I will return to the CROW front office and November 25th 1998, the CROW shall be reformed with the latest spectacular of a PPV, that being:
"Rejuvenation" [The crowds with a shock horror look, the cameraman can't believe what he has just heard. Joel Tennuchi then starts up again.]
JT _ "You ask me why, I have decided after nearly three months to reform the Cyber Realm Of Wrestling, well I guess I couldn't let something which the athletes enjoyed go to waste and thus if they are willing to come back to the place they used to call home and forgive me for my rough decisions, then as stated the CROW shall be brought back into operation. The CROW was my idea, I developed it and then I got tired of it, but in this game, I guess you just can't "Give it up."
It came to me to reopen it due to the vast queries I still keep on hearing, not only from former CROW Athletes, but also from those who saw what I did for the CROW and thus as it is done, the CROW shall be brought back. That is the real reason for calling this press conference, but also to say that during this time out, I have been out "Scouting" for new athletes and also a new co-president, dear Daniel Rushing, a man who I follow and respect so dearly, yet you have other matters to deal with, so I wish you all the best.
Thus due to his departure from CROW Front Office, please give a huge applause for the new CROW CO-President. I had numerous Applications, but out of them all, it was only one.
Thus without any further ado, let me introduce to you the candidates for this job offer. First, we have my dear and second best friend, that being
Christof Montoya. A man who I know so dearly and take his words at heart. He is my top choice, but we do have others. Next comes [name withheld], yet due to circumstances which I do not know, he is no longer associated with CROW
Enterprises Ltd and thus, I had to refuse his job offer, then finally, we had Levi Ash. This man, I do not know that well, apart from that he has always been there for me and I thank him so dearly. Now comes to the hard choice, the decision. Out of these 2 candidates, which one man is going to be my CROW
CO-President. [The sounds of drums rolling can then be heard, the curtain then unfolds and then Christof Montoya is seen awaiting the decision. Joel Tennuchi then starts up.]
JT - "Ladies and gentleman, the new CROW CO-President as of November 10th 1998, please welcome: CHRISTOF MONTOYA. May his stay be a good and proud one and most of all, may the CROW live long and prosper. So now that brings us to the end of this press conference, an update will be sanctioned soon on the upcoming PPV card, that is if the athletes wish to return. So for me, now I shall sign off, and please stay tuned for further updates as the time draws near."
[The camera then fades from Joel Tennuchi as the scene begins to fade out. The camera then fades to black.]
