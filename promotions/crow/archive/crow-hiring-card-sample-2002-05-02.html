CROW Hiring: Card Sample

2 May 2002

The CROW is open once again and I am looking for wrestlers to compete, enclosed is a sample match, so you know what kind of matches will be written up. So if you are interested please contact me at: [detail withheld] - Chris. -- [best viewd in "Courier New" size 10, full screen.] ______ _______ _____ __ __ ____________________________________ / _ \| _ \ / _ \ | | | | | | / \__\ |_/ // / \ \| | /\ | | WINNER RECIEVES CROW CONTRACT | | | __| _ <| | | | |/ \| | Jason Stormie vs. Derick Gerhold | | \_/ / | \ \\ \_/ /| /\ | Written by: [name withheld] | \______/|__| |__| \_____/ \__/ \__/|____________________________________| [The two men lock up and it's Jason Stormsie getting the upper hand sending Derick Gerhold into the ropes with an irish whip. Gerhold comes off the ropes and ducks the waiting clothesline, he bounces off the other side and connects with a big dropkick that sends Stormsie staggering back. Gerhold now delivers a knife edge chop, and another one, and another one!!] JEFF NASHTEIN: A tri-fector of knife edge chops... unbelievable. BEN SORROWITZ: I have hard great things about this kid, like when people need a practice dummy, they call on Gerhold. This won't last long, watch. [Gerhold now with a kick to the stomach of Stormsie, and Stormsie now with his head down. Gerhold comes off the ropes and... misses the attempted scissor's kick. Stormsie moved out of the way just in the nick of time. Stormsie now delivers a devastating short arm clothesline, sending Gerhold to the mat... hard.] BS: DID YOU SEE THAT!?!? He nearly took Derick Gerhold's head off his shoulders. I told you hee sucks! JN: Now, now... we still have a lot left to go. He could pull something off here. [Stormsie lifts Gerhold to his feet by the back of the head, he lifts Gerhold up and holds him high in the air in a stalling suplex... ... and FINALLY drops him hard to the mat with a beautiful suplex.] *[NICE CROWD POP!]* JN: Text-book suplex there Ben. Stormsie showing the world why he thinks he should be in the CROW. BS: Well, he's got my vote! [Stormsie slow to his feet as the move took his breath. He is now up and begins to lay some HARD boots to the back of Gerhold's head. The referee is asking for a break bu isn't getting it, he starts the count... ONE.. TWO.. THREE... ... and Stormsie finally stopping the vicious kicks. He grabs Gerhold up again and throws him to the outside. The referee telling Stormsie to bring it back in the ring but Stormsie goes out after Gerhold.] BS: I knew I liked this guy for a reason... HIGH INTENSITY!! I mean look at what he is doing here with Derick Gerhold, he lays some beatiful moves in the ring, but still wants to lay the punishment in by bringing it to the outside. JN: But matches can not be won on the outside, even you know that Ben. BS: Matches can't be won, but BONES CAN BE BROKEN! [Stormsie now with an irish whip, sending Gerhold crashing into the protective barrier around ringside. He looks to the crowd and follows in with a clothesline... but Gerhold ducks and sends Stormsie into the first row of fans.] JN: Here at CROW we like to bring the action TO the fans. And here is a prime example of how that EXACTLY looks. BS: Yeah but who cares, Gerhold is a loser. [Gerhold rolls back in the ring to stop the count and then rolls back out. Stormsie now jumps the guard-rail and falls to the mat outside the ring. Gerhold in after him, he lifts him up and... DROPS HIM WITH A HARD DDT on the OUTSIDE!! He now picks up Stormsie and rolls him in the ring, a cover and... ONE.... TWO..... THR... ... a kickout by Stormsie.] BS: [sighing in relief] I knew it all along, Stormsie isn't goin' out like that! JN: Sure you did... sure you did. [Gerhold now picking up Stormsie and laying three hard right hands sending Jason Stormsie to the far corner. He now sends Stormsie to the other side with an irish whip to the other side... but Stormsie reverses and sends Gerhold into the turnbuckles. Derick comes off the buckles staggered, and Stormsie NAILS a swinging neckbreaker that sends Gerhold down, holding his neck. Stormsie with the cover... ONE.... TWO..... THRE... ... and Gerhold JUST got the shoulder up in the nick of time.] JN: A close fall there for Stormsie, he was a milimeter away from getting the CROW contract. But Derick Gerhold showing just how much heart he really has. BS: When Stormie is done and WANTS to end this match, Gerhold is gonna need more than heart, he is gonna need and EMT and a good insurance plan. [Stormsie having a word with the ref, and Gerhold manages to sneak a small package in, the ref starts the count... ONE........ TW...... ... and Stormsie kicks out with authority. Jason Stormsie now looks pissed. He gets up and picks up Gerhold as well. He shoves Derick Gerhold's head in between his legs and picks him up for a powerbomb... CRASHING DOWN comes Derick Gerhold from a hard powerbomb, but Stormsie not going for the pin, instead he is going to the top rope.] JN: What could he be goin' for now? BS: I have heard of this move before, it is called "SHAKY STORM!" If he hits this one we will see the storm comin' to CROW. [Stormsie sizes up Gerhold who is still on the ground... he jumps and... ... NAILS THE SHAKY STORM, a top rope 450 splash. He goes for the cover and... ONE...... TWO......... THREEEEEEEEEEEEEE.... *[DING, DING, DING]* BS: YES!! He did it... Jason Stormsie is comin' to the CROW. I can't wait. JN: A fine match-up between Jason Stormsie and Derick Gerhold. Gerhold gave it all he could but came up short as Stormsie picks up the win. That's it folks and until next time, I am Jeff Nashtein and he's Ben Sorrowitz, and we're saying see ya next time! [The camera fades as Jason Stormsie is still in the ring with his hands raised in victory.]
