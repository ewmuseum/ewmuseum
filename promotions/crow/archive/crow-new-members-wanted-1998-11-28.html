Greetings to all RSPFW Readers, your friendly CROW President here, [name withheld], multiple federation handler and a great card writer. Anyway the topic of this post says it all. I am the CROW President, which stands for the Cyber Realm of Wrestling. To make this message easy to comprehend, I have split it into Four parts, these being: ----------------------------------- | | |I. CROW Background | |II. My Experience | |II. What places are available | |IV. What I require in an athlete. | |----------------------------------| I. CROW BackGround. The CROW started out in the month of Marh of this current year, 1998, it was created as a sister federation to the now OWF federation called the EMWO. Before the EMWO started up, the athletes which were previoulsy in the old NAWA which was run by [name withheld] were without a place to be, so thus, I formed the CROW. Then the EMWO was born, so we had both federations open at once. Many named athletes have come from the CROW and more will soon arrive. Anyway as the CROW became popular, it still stood firm and then due to a sudden merger with the EWO and the IeWC, it ceased trading, yet on November 15th 1998, it was rejuvinated with a stunning PPV, entitled Rejuvination, you can see this on http://www.geocities/com/colosseum/park/8943/rejuv.txt II. My Experience. Well this is exactly it. I have been in the business of e-wrestling since January 1998, I started out and am noted for my creation entitled: "Lord of Darkness" Doomsday Well once I had this, I then went into the business of writing matches for a federation called the IIWA, run by [name withheld] < [detail withheld] > and [name withheld]< [detail withheld] > After this I then started out firmly in the business of writing for the former IeWC, then moved on upwards to writing for the big time federations of the UEW, SCRA and HHWO. My writing skills are somewhat superb, but not that I am boasting as many think differently, still I am a very reliable person when it comes to federation management and match writing. Thus from here, I went into the business of being the Booker in the IIWA, left after it closed down to go to the EMWO and be their CO-President. I was here until it decided to cease and then change into the OWF. I have been the IeWC VP and now am the President of the CROW, Vice-President of the OWF and am noted for my match writing skills for numerous feds, too many to mention. Even though, sometimes I tend to speak in a Holy Manner, I am still loved all the way across the e-wrestling circuit. Finally in this section, I now handle as stated: "Lord of Darkness" Doomsday "Word" Joey Jackson Gospel Preachers. So I do have a vast amount of experience and I am happy to say enjoy every minute of it and the word life to me(What's that):D III. What Places are Available. Well I have a Roster at the moment of about 25, but a few will go pretty soon for non activity, this means that a few places will be available. I have a strong World Division of about 10 athletes, so far, I have 8, I am looking for 2 more. Light Heavy-weight Division is lacking somewhat, I have about 7, which I really want about 11. International Division is okay, none needed here. Tag Team Division, well I have just started this up, so I would dearly like a few teams to drop me a line. That's about it, a run down is below: 2 World 3-4 Light Heavyweights 4 Tag Teams 2-3 Extremists So if you feel you can fit into one of the above categories then please contact me with some details of yourselve. IV. What My Standards Are. This goes for everything I do, I like to see active role-players, ones who are good with the fans and can develop good angles with other athletes. Basically to apply to the CROW, you must have the following credentials: ~Be Active Every week(1 Role-Play a week) ~Have an understanding of how a booked federation is run ~Devise good solid angles(No copies from other feds or WCW/WWF) ~Be willing to abide by my rules of if it's on TV then it's okay. ~Have at least the incentive to do good role-plays in character. ~First Timers are allowed, yet must be good. So that's it, if you have what it takes to compete in the CROW then just send me a few details of yourselve and I will send you the CROW Official Hand-Book V 2.0 + an Contract(Not Binding if you don't want it to be( That's all really I have to say.
