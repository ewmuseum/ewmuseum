Scorch Wave 98 Post PPV: Update

30 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SCORCH WAVE 98 POST PPV UPDATE I. NEWS I. News 1. First of all I would like to thank all those who made SCORCH WAVE 98 into a hit and an event which people will always remember. I would also like to thank a good friend of mine [name withheld] for allowing the
CROW to conduct a behind closed doors interview. I would also like to thank those handlers who took part in the PPV and I hope they enjoyed it, for it was a pleasure to put out and an even greater pleasure to write. I have to say my condolences to the handler of Alex Nelson, may you pursue your career some place else.
2. After careful consideration with all parties involved, the closing of the CROW is about to happen, yet not all CROW is closing. As most of you know, the four federations: IeWC, EMWO, CROW and HHWO are all separate yet it has been decided and I know a lot of people are happy with the proposal that on September 14th 1998, all four federations will merger together to make one federation, the name will be Seattle Wrestling
Promotions. The SWP will be presided over by [name withheld], Creed,
[name withheld], SycickX and myself. The federation has at the moment a roster so far, yet all those who are on the roster will know who they are for invites to join the SWP will be sent to them. If you are not invited to join then if you show us you can work then you will be placed on the waiting/mailing list.
The invites will be sent out around about next week some time, the proposed schedule is the following:
Titles: World, Seattle Midweight, Juniorweight and Tag Team Cards: There are three cards a week- Monday, Thursday and Weekend. The cards will be all commentary format apart from the weekend one, that will be a house show with summarised results. The main card is on
Mondays and the middle card is Thursday. Role-Plays: These will all be sent to Creed, he will then send them out to everybody in flash packets. These will come out during the week, also on the weekend. Important flashes or really good ones will be kept for the Weekend House-show. The Role-Play rule is two maximum per match. Yet to get that extra push, 2 per week is a good number to do.
SWP Magazine: This will be placed on the Website, name has yet to be decided. Yet probably SWP Down-town. It will come out every month.
Website: This has to be decided upon, yet a new Website for the SWP will be created. 3. Smoke was stripped of his Light Heavyweight Championship and thus now it shall be decided for on the EMWO Grand Finale card on September 6th 98. Giant Leopard vs. "Lightning" Levi Ash, winner is the CROW LW Champion and thus automatically is entered into the SWP. My final note to you all is that I look foreword to seeing some of you, if not most of you in the SWP. So Far my CROW Roster who will be in the
SWP is(No Particular Order.) This does not include any EMWO stars like Douglas, Mad Goat, Destroyer, Houston or Shane Alexandra. They are already on the EMWO list, IeWC or the HHWO list, so they are staying.
My CROW list is 1. King Dare(CROW World Champion) 2. Rob the Rock(CROW International Champion) 3. Shawn Jamison(CROW TV Champion) 4. Beefcarver 5. "Death Dealer" Midnight Reign 6. "Lightning" Levi Ash(#2 cont. LW) 7. "King Of Hate" Tonic 8. Giant Leopard(#1 cont. LW) 9. "Son of the Devil" Chris Python 10. Suicido Tag Teams: 1. Da Gang 2. Element Of Pain **Gospel Preachers are on EMWO list** So all that is left for me to do is say good luck to all those who are on the list and commiseration's to those who had to be dropped.
See you all on September 14th for the first ever SWP card. Kind regards. [name withheld] Former CROW President/EMWO CO-President SWP CO-President ----------------------------------------------------------------------- The EDGE(c) is brought to you by (c)CROW Enterprises ltd.
