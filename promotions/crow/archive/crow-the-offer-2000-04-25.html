The Offer

25 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings all, It's been quite some time since I posted anything to this message forum. Yet today I felt like doing so. So here it is. My topic today is the subject of talent seeking.
Myself, being the President of the Cyber Realm Of Wrestling, a promotion which is in it's 4th year of operation is coming to this area seeking fresh species to work alongside.
One of my long term handlers [name withheld], all you know him as "The
Beefcarver" Matt Funk recently retired from the sport. Thus I am know down to lose a great superstar. Thus I'd like to offer the oppertunity to ANYBODY who wishes to sign up, to do so.
[name withheld] and Myself, will accept applications up until May 12th
2000. After this date, no aplications may be tendered. Yet as the CROW is established, and limited on roster allocation, I can ONLY take those serious enough to work alongside other handlers and who also have no attitude problems. For if it's ego you have then there's no way you'll ever enter the CROW, or any federation for that matter.
Anyway what I am looking for is the following 1. A gimmick, like that of Bret Hart of old. A pure face, a man who gets along with the fans and is pleasing to listen to, both on and off the stage. The applicant must be of a technical profession, ie be either a technically sound mat wrestler or a technically sound brawler.
The adequate weight would be not more then 260lbs and also would have to show originality. 2. A Japanese Sensation. The CROW wishes to braoden the horizens with its oriental style and thus would love to hear from those of a mixed martial arts background. Yet the applicant(s) must be aware of the
Japanese style, known as Poreasu. 3. A Group of wrestlers. The CROW has only one real Stable, that of The Territory. What the CROW needs is another group so that handlers can work along them and so that angles can produce. Yet this group would have to be either a one handler group(yet would have to dedicate time)
or preferebly a multi-handler group. 4. The CROW seeks to build up it's Skyweight scen, currently decimated by 'Exotic' Sean Isle. The CROW looks to broaden out, allowing 4-6 Skyweights in. Yet the correct applicants must be aware of the agility, and dare-devil risks which a Skyweight can take. Ideally their gimmick would be like that of Rey Mysterio Jr, Kidman, Lennie, Psycosis etc.
5. [name withheld] does a wonderful job looking after the Tag Team scene of things. Yet the CROW is always looking for fresh Tag Teams. Currently we have the scene dominated by The Perverse Romans(Nero & Caligula), yet oth teams which are active are God's Justice, The Littlest Pet
Shop, Rising Sun Revolution(YES RSR!), The Mafia Hit Squad, The Territory and Little/Large. Yet ideally the CROW would like to see at least 4-6 more tag teams join up. So that competition can be fast and straight down to business. And I guess Jay would like to see more action as well.
6. In May of this year, the CROW for the first time in 4 years is coming to the fore of Womens Wrestling. The week after Canadian Strife III: Spring Fling 2000[contact me to add to mail list] on May 3rd 2000, the CROW shall start an international All Ladies Tournament to crown the first ever CROW All Ladies Champion. Thus for this applications for female wrestlers, both heel and face are being accepted.
7. The CROW looks for one handler who is a good rookie, has shown expereince in the indy scene or even in the Circuits. This handler the CROW would like to start off at the bottom and then build that character up, working good angles in, and eventually building them up to being a SUPERSTAR. So if you've always wanted an oppertunity to be in a strong, dependent and long running federation then apply now.
Finally I have the FAQ made up, yet it's not on the site, due to me not putting it there yet. But the site for the CROW is located at
http://crownet.freeuk.net/crownet/ And I belive all information is on there. If not then please don't in hesitate in contact any one of us. And on that note, I hope all is well and thank you for listening.
