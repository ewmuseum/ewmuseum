CROW Mitch Gatlin [-Flash-] (part two)

January 28, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[-The scene opens up outside of the back of the arena in San Francisco, CA where Action Stations was just held. The viewer can see a medium size parking lot. There are all kinds of expensive cars leaving the lot. The camera catches a glimpse of various wrestlers getting into limos and expensive cars, as the parking lot quickly empties.-]
[-The camera changes views just as quick as a blink of an eye. The camera focus in on a huge double wide steel door surrounded by a brick wall.
Everything is quite and all of a sudden the door fly's open and slams against the brick wall and out walks Mitch Gatlin.-]
[-The camera pans over Mitch from top to bottom. Mitch is wearing a pair of tan work boots. Some dark Polo blue Jean pants and a black T-shirt that has
Too Damn Good written across the front of the shirt in red flaming letters and across the back it has "The New Breed" in bold yellow letters. Mitch has a rather large Nike bag in his left hand and in his right he has a piece of
paper.-] [-The camera pauses on Mitch's face. He has his hair that is normally in a ponytail draped across his face. It is hard to tell considering Mitch has a large sum of hair in his face but Mitch appears to be badly burned. Mitch also has a white bandage wrapped around the top of his forehead. Mitch also has a lit cigar clenched between his teeth.-]
MG: "Did I not deliver tonight or what. I came out here and did jus' what I said I was goin' ta do. You can bet your last dollar that it won't be the last time I follow up on my words."
[-Mitch walks outside of the door and into the parking lot. The parking lot is dead empty now. Mitch walks over to the curb and throws his bag to the ground.
Mitch then takes a seat on the edge of the curb and begins to look at his piece of paper he has in his right hand.-]
M "SF" G: "It is about time I get what I have been askin' for. So, it is
Adrian and Gatlin this Monday."
[-Mitch squints real hard while reading the paper." M "SF" G: "_What_. . . Not a sadistic match. Apparently no one has been listenin' tah me. The whole reason I came for Adrian is because he was not
Sadistic enough to hold that strap he _used_ to have."
"I will get to this issue soon enough. Right now I want to focus on Shawn
Jamison and Smoke. Well, boys tonight is what separated superiority from mediocrity."
"Jamison do ya see the difference in which one hurts more now? That is right
Shawn ya may have been tha "Hard-core Legend" but when you try to step up a level from being a legend and becoming a King than that is just a bit more than ya can chew. 'Cause Shawn I am the King. The "King Of Extreme and ya shall remember that. Shawn you talked all that [BLEEP] but when it was time to shut the [BLEEP] up and through down... Ya choked on your own [BLEEP]."
"Jamison I told ya not to come out here tonight and fight me that you would get jus' hurt and that is exactly what happen. I will give credit where credit is due though. Ya are a scrapper, but That is just not enough to compete wit'
someone like me. Ya have to be able to think on your feet son and that was something ya just did not do quick enough. Jamison don't get mad at your self.
Get mad at Joel for this one. Ya was way out of your league ever since Joel put your name on the same contract as mine."
"Ya'See Jamison you are just in a different class than I am. You are Hard-core and well... I am "The New Breed Of Wrestler." I am the superiority, I am not jus' some two-bit rookie who has never gotten his hands dirty. Jamison you are good, don't get me wrong. . . You were jus' not good _enough_."
[-Mitch lets off a slight smirk.-] M "SF" G: "Now onto Smoke. Smoke you was nothin' but a played out joke. I told ya to take your ass back to the loony bin before ya got hurt and look what happened to ya. Ya got carried out... I knew from the very begging' when I found out ya talked to your self thinking it was some imaginary powers called the "darkside," that you was going to be the least of my worries. You and
Jamison was just a small example of what I have in store for the rest of tha boys in C.R.O.W."
[-Mitch's face tenses up and he has a look of seriousness on his face.-] M "SF" G: "Now onto to "The Hero". Adrian I know ya got my message pal. Was it supposed to scare ya? Was it supposed to change anythin'? That is on you to deiced but to me it was just a small reminder. To let ya know there is a new top dog in town."
"Adrian ever since I stepped foot in CROW you did not pay any attention to what
I had to say and the entire time I was talkin' about you. Telling ya how _you_ was not worthy of that strap that ya _used_ to have. I am the one runnin'
things 'round here. If it was not for me Jagged would not have that strap draped across his shoulder and ya better believe I can take that strap from
Jagged jus' as quick as I took it from _you_!"
"Adrian I told ya time and time again. Grow some eye's in tha back of your skull 'cause I am comin' for ya. What'd ya think, I was jokin'? Ya better wake up Adrian 'cause I get me hands on ya soon... A lot sooner than later, that is for damn sure."
"I seen how ya have avoided this match being a Sadistic match and all. I cannot blame ya. I would not want to be you gettin' into the ring with someone like me and there being no rules. Ya seem ta think ya have the upper hand in this one. It is OK, ya can think what ya want to. 'Cause I know Adrian jus'
as well as ya know. I am tha _future_ of tha C.R.O.W and ya better not think for one-second I do not know how ta jus' plain out wrestle you."
"Adrian that is where ya are underestimating me and that is jus' one thing ya cannot afford ta do right now. Ya already did it once and look where it got ya... It got ya a nice bump on your noggin and it took that pretty strap that used ta drape across your shoulder away."
[-The tip of the cigar that is clenched between Mitch's teeth lights up a bright orangeish color as smoke begins to come out of his nose.-]
MG: "Jagged is a man with some serious issues. It seems everyone in this organization has somethin' wrong with there brain. I am jus' plain sick and tired of seein' all these nut cases running around here, talkin' to them self's. Jagged you owe me. I am the only reason you have that strap around your waist. If it was not for me you would most likely be in the hospital right now because Adrian and Donate would have had there way with ya."
"I am a man who likes to collect when someone owes me somethin'. Jagged prove to me you are a worthy champion. Give me a shot. I know you should not be worried I am just a [laughs] rookie. I cannot [laughs] hang with some like you."
"OK, who I am I kiddin'. You would not agree to get in tha same ring with you with that strap on the line. I know this and you know this. For three reasons. One is that I am a bit faster than you are. Two is I am a bit stronger than you are, and three is I am a hell of a lot smarter than you are."
[-Mitch's face turns from a slight smile to a crude stare.-] MG: "Now Donate, the man who train Adrian him self. The man that has had
Adrian stuck in the shadows. Donate you may be a "legend" in your on mind but when you stick your nose in my business I can promise you it is not something you want to do. I know you are just like everyone else in this business. Hard headed and have to be shown first hand. So, I know you are going to be stickin' your nose where it does not belong. So I will keep my eye on you."
"Adrian let me ask you one last thing. You are the "Hero" right? Yeah, that is what I thought I heard you say. My point is if you are "The Hero" then who is going to save you my friend? Who is going to save you from me?"
"Adrian Congratulations you are just another two-bit wrestler who is going to be on the list of people I beat on my way to the top."
[-Then from a distance the camera catches a glimpse of some head lights.-] M "SF" G: "How many people do I have to hurt until I prove my point?" [-The headlights pull up in front of Mitch. The headlights are the lights of white limo. Mitch picks up his bag and begins to open the door to the limo."
M "SF" G: "As many as it damn well takes." [-Mitch then tosses his bag into the limo and climbs inside as the scene fades.-] [name withheld] Handler Of. . .
