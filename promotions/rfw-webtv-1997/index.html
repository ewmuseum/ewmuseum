Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

RFW introduced itself to the newsgroup on 22 June 1997, its own founder promising three cards a week and a pay-per-view every six weeks, with a first card targeted for 1 July 1997.

By July it had a launch tournament running across four championships, named in its own record though no result or roster survives beyond the titles' own existence.

The promotion's trail ends on 20 July 1997, still seeking a writer for its matches. No closing statement, or any further document, survives it.
