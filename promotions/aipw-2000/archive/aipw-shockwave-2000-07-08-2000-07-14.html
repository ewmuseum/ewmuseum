[The crowd immediately lets out a loud mixed reaction as the form of
Rob Winters steps out from the curtains. The President of the AIPW is wearing a black t-shirt with the "AIPW" logo on it, along with long blue jeans and black boots. Winters walks down the aisle slowly, until he reaches the small red ramp that leads to the apron, and he walks up that as well. Edwards hands Winters the microphone as he makes his way into the ring. With an expression of malevolent determination etched onto his angular and heavily tanned countenance, the barrel-chested Anubis steps out from behind the curtains and strides purposefully down the aisle. The hardened soles of his bare feet caresses the cold steel of the walkway, as the monstrous Middle Eastern entity moves forever closer to the squared circle. A thin braid of raven hued hair sprouts from the base of Dehan's otherwise totally shaven head, and he wears not one shred of clothing upon his muscular upper torso ... an aspect which in turn shows off the full intricate glory of a very vivid tattoo of a majestic Phoenix that is inscribed across his broad chest. Tight, thigh length shorts of a black shade covers his lower extremities, while a heavy dose of white tape covers the Enigma's wrists and ankles/heels respectively. Seemingly ignoring the raucous reception afforded to him, Anubis steps over the top ring rope and glides with malicious intent towards the AIPW President. For the most part, Winters is oblivious to the overwhelming aura of loathing that oozes from Dehan's every pore. Presently, the house lights revert to their normal shade and intensity.]
RW: Dehan.. if I may call you that. Last week when I watched you absolutely dominate in your match.. a smile actually came to my face.
[Winters grins as he looks up at Anubis' towering and presently statuesque figure.] RW: The reason why a smile came to my face was simple. Anubis, you are quite simply one of the most dominant forces that I have ever seen in existence.
[A huge pop from the crowd!] RW: I mean.. look at you! You are 6'8", 312 pounds! My god! And you are indestructable as well! You just don't beat your opponents..
you absolutely _destroy_ them! And that is where an idea came to my mind.. maybe a premonition if you call it that.
[Winters peppy and almost hoppy stance changes into a more serious form as he looks up at Anubis. The Egima remains unmoving, but his dark eyes are alive with adrenaline-addled fury.]
RW: The premonition was to market you as the greatest force in professional wrestling! [A mixed pop comes from the fans.] RW: Do you see my vision, Dehan? Imagine this.. you go night in and night out.. absolutely destroying your opponents! And Im not talking about opponents that are even close to your stature or ability! We can pull out the jobbers from the back.. or even better.. homeless bums! You can murder them, Anubis!
[In reaction to this, Anubis' facial features twist into a seething mask of confusion, as the fans start to jeer at Winters statements.
For the first time in a while, Dehan betrays some movement, as he tilts his head questioningly to one side.]
RW: We can market the whole Kail'Lin thingy until were both stinkin rich! Shirts, hats, jerseys, video's.. even a Dehan Anubis teddy bear! We could call him Anubie!
[The crowds volume of jeers picks up louder as Winters continues.] RW: We will be stinkin' rich, Dehan! Stinkin rich! You and me living the high life! We can even get started tonight! Who can we put you against? Anyone will draw ratings when they are against you... who?
[Winters pauses for a second.] RW: I got it! How about... EL MUCHO GIGANTE! [The crowd lets out a huge heel pop as Winters grins with glee. Anubis' eyes narrow the merest fraction at the 'sacrifice' offered unto him, and he unconsciously flexes each massive fist at the thought of warring once more.]
RW: How about it, Dehan? What do you think? About the marketing.. the easy nights.. and most importantly.. the money. What do ya say, Dehan? [The reply from the Egyptian is not immediate ... Instead the crowd silently hang on the edge of their proverbial seats, as Anubis closes his eyes and lowers his head ... Then ...]
... RW: Dehan? ... RW: Ummmmmm, Dehan? ... [The Enigma's right hand flashes into motion, ripping the microphone from Winters' grasp in a virtual blur of speed. Startled from the abstract sense of serenity than had risen within him, the AIPW
President visibly flinches and attempts to backtrack a couple of steps. However, his massive employee has other ideas, and Anubis snakes out his left arm, cradles it behind Winters' neck and pulls him close. Dehan's answer to the President's ideas is succinct and to the point.]
DA: My foolish and ultimately deluded 'superior' ... words cannot adequately convey the disdain that Kail'Lin does feel for your plans to ridicule both her reputation and my standing as an unholy slayer of men who _choose_ to malign me. At this juncture, she has deemed that actions shall speak at a greater volume than mere verbal utterances ... and YOU shall be the recipient of her rage!
[With an ear-shattering chorus of encouragement washing over the entire Arena, Anubis casts aside the microphone, establishes a wholly inescapable grip upon his now wildly struggling prey, and presses him with ease above his head. Winters manages to emit a screeching wail of "NOOOOO", before becoming the unwilling victim of a high impact
Michinoku Driver! The cheers reach a crescendo, as the President's skull bounces savagely on the canvas. Oblivious to the swarm of
Security guards that are now flooding to the ring, Anubis yells a call of defiance to the skies, and locks on the soul-draining Kail'Lin's
Lifeblood clawhold to the heart of his semiconscious employer. Leading the pack, the head AIPW guard rushes over to assuage Dehan from his mission, but only succeeds in running face first in a snap Front Kick from a rising Enigma. His contact with Winters now broken, Anubis casts a baleful glance at the now heavily bloodied carnage that surrounds him, slides from the squared circle and storms through the frenzied crowd into the darkness. Making one hundred percent sure that the monstrous cult member was truly no longer in the vicinity, the remaining members of security sheepishly check on their fallen comrades.
