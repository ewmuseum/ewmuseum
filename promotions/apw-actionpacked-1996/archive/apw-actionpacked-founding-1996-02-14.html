APW - New league

14 February 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I would like to announce the opening of a new league, the APW *Action Packed Wrestling*. This league is runned by a simulator type system, which is all explained in the rules. I am looking for members to join. There is room for 20 singles wrestlers and 8 Tag Teams. You can have a maximum of 2 singles wrestlers, 1 singles/1 tag team or 1 tag team only. Sorry, a ladies division is not available. This league is open to one and all and we are especially looking for *newbies*, who are looking to gain some e-wrestling experience. One thing that we do ask, however, is that the wrestler you create for this league are brand new (exclusive to the APW). We don't want any wrestlers that are already in 10 different leagues. Also no real life wrestlers allowed. If interested, send e-mail to [detail withheld] I will send you the APW rules, which explains everything you'll need to know and I will send you a character creation sheet. We want to get this league going as soon as possible, so e-mail today!
