Barely Legal Wrestling - Rules & Application- 12/31/99 R o l e p l a y i n g -2 Roleplay Limit: We decided to put a limit on the number of roleplays allowed per card in order to make sure quality is at its absolute best. So when roleplaying, don't shoot for a "I'll just do this one now, and make a better one later" deal. Do you best, because you only have 2. The exception is PPVs. We will allow up to 4 RP's for PPV cards.
-OOC Policy: We really want to keep things under control in the BLW... well, sort of. While the cards will be complete mayhem, we want the RP board to stay OOC-Free. We won't threaten you about it, we just want it clean. Just as you figured, anyone who OOC's on the Roleplay board will be punished in one way or another.
Use of Other Wrestlers: We ask you not to use other BLW wrestlers in your roleplays unless they have given their consent or they are a stable/tag partnet of yours.
-Swearing & Racism Policy: We don't really give a flying [bleep] about cussing. Use it freely. But Racism, please, refrain from racism. We want to keep everyone happy as possible, and that won't happen with people making racial slurs towards one another.
E v e n t s -PPV Roleplaying: As stated above, the Roleplay Limit is raised to 4 for Pay-Per-Views. This way the PPVs are alot more active, which is good. -Event Roleplay Cut Off Times: The Cut-Off for all cards is Midnight on the day of every card. Exceptions will be made if we think Roleplay Activity isn't at its best or if there was a delayed card.
-Card Writing: All of the cards will be writen be the staff and myself. Each will be full play-by-play. If you have any ideas/segments, talk to myself or
President Aaron about it, capish? Good
