Barely Legal Wrestling - Press Conference - 12/31/99 [[[[The scene fades in on President Aaron and Jones along with other BLW
Staff Members sitting in the BLW Meeting Room, more commonly known as the lounge room of the Upper Cleveland Recreation Hall. President Jones is on the phone with BLW Reporter/Commentator Extraordinar, Ryan Van Tate]]]
President Jones: Listen to me Tate, I don't give a flying fuck how you get him here, but you guys better be here soon. The damn Press Conference starts in 10 minutes!
Ryan Van Tate: Don't worry Jonesy, we are on our way. But let me tell you, this was one hard son of a bitch to break out of jail. It all went down sorta like this...
[[[FLASHBACK: Ryan Van Tate and several convientently masked men are approaching an old run down prison, one sort of like you'd see in an old western movie. They approach the front door and Ryan Van Tate peeks in and see's the Sheriff tied up, and surrounded with chickens, badgers, wood chucks, and racoons.]]]
Ryan Van Tate: SWEET JESUS! LOOK AT THOSE COONS! But where is he? Masked Man #1: Where's who? Ryan Van Tate: You know, the guy we are suppose to break out. Masked Man #1: Oooooh, Doct... [[[The masked man is interupted by the breaking down of the door by a large mysterious man, who grabs Ryan Van Tate and runs off back to the black van with him. The man drives off with Tate, leaving the masked helpers behind.
The scene flashs forward to the BLW Meeting Room, and to the BLW Staff]]] President Jones: Wow Tate, thats really fucking special. But just get your ass to the arena! Oh, and if you fuckers stop for a chalupa or some shit,
I'm going to personally kick your pansy asses!
Ryan Van Tate: You hear that big guy, he said no chalupa. Yeah... I agree, fuck him. President Jones: What was that? Ryan Van Tate: Oh, nothing I was talking to Doc... President Jones: Shut the hell up you stupid wanker and get your ass to the arena. [[[President Jones hangs up the cell phone and looks over to President
Aaron, who looks at his watch, and shakes his head yes. The 4 men rise to their feet, and walk out of the Lounge Room and into the Upper Cleveland
Recreational Hall conference room, where reporters sit, awaiting the BLW
Staff. President Jones and his colleages take there seats, and President
Aaron takes his place at the podeum.]]]
President Aaron: Now, ladies and gentleman, we would like to welcome you to the BLW Arena for this Press Confer...
Male Reporter #1: Excuse me sir, but isn't this just a Bingo Hall? President Aaron: LISTEN HERE YOU LITTLE FUCK! IF YOU EVER CALL THE BLW ARENA
THAT AGAIN, JONES AND MYSELF WILL COME TO YOUR HOUSE AND RAPE YOUR WHOLE
FAMILY! OKAY!? OKAY! And the questions haven't even started yet, jesus. Now, this is a press conference to introduce the media to the BLW, and to basically break the ground. Now, are there any specific questions?
Male Reporter #1: Yeah, how do you pl... President Aaron: NO! You shut the fuck up!! I don't like your ass, get out! [[[President Aaron throws his pen at the man, who quickly scurries out the door. President Aaron gathers himself, and notices an attractive female reporter with a question.]]]
President Aaron: Yes you, but before you ask your question, I have one for you. Are you tired?
Female Reporter: No, why? President Aaron: Because you've been running threw my mind all day! [[[This gets a few chuckles out of the audience and President Aaron gives
President Jones a high five, and they all turn their attention back to the reporter.]]]
Female Reporter: Mmmkay, now, How does the BLW plan to determine its first
World Champion? President Aaron: That is a very good question, but this question can't be answered without a little assistance from 2 guys... Bring 'em in boys!
[[[Everyone in the room looks towards the side entrance where they see
Reckless and Dan Kiltz walk into the room, wearing the XWA title and GWA title. Reckless is still in superb shape from his FWF stint, but Kiltz has changed quite a bit. He is overweight, and dirty. He is wears his GWA World
Title over his shoulder, ontop of his camoflauge overalls. He and Reckless approach the table, and have a seat.]]]
President Aaron: Mr. Reckless and Mr. Klitz... Dan Kiltz: Um, that's Kiltz President Aaron: Whatever. Anyway, we would like to thank you for appearing today, even though you are not under BLW contract. Now, the reason for them being here is to present the BLW with the XWA and GWA World Titles.
Female Reporter: Why does the BLW want the World Titles of two defunct federations? President Jones: Its quite simple. President Aaron was the key player in the managment of the GWA, and I myself, along with Mr. Hovinga, ran the XWA. So we are taking these titles, and using them in order to make this industries most prestigous championship in all of wrestling... the BLW Triple Crown
Championship. GWA - BLW - XWA World Title's... now, President Aaron, how about you tell these people how this championship will be won.
President Aaron: Gladly. Now, the first BLW PPV, Guerrilla Warfare, is going down Sunday, January 30th, 2000. The main event of this match will be a never before seen GUERRILLA WARFARE MATCH!
Male Reporter #2: What the hell is that? President Jones: If you would shut the fuck up he would tell you. Fucking loser. Proceed, Aaron.
President Aaron: Alright... this is a Triple Stack Cage match. But this isn't going to be held inside a building, oh no. No building could contain this match, therefore, this match will be held here at the BLW Arena, or should I say, here ON TOP OF the BLW Arena.
Male Reporter #3: But wouldn't the weather play a factor in this match? President Aaron: Fuck Mother Nature. If that whore wants to screw with our match, then we'll kick her ass too! Now to further explain this match.
Hanging from the top of each of the cages will be one of the 3 World Titles. In each of the cages, there will be a match going on, simultaneously. These cage matches have a twist, they have ladder match rules. Except, there are no ladders. The competitors have to climb the cage, and get the belt by any means necessary. Then, once a winner is declared in each cage, there will be a...
President Jones: TRIPLE THREAT MATCH!!!!! President Aaron: Right, a Triple Threat Match for the Triple Crown Championship in the Center Cage. When there is one man left, then he is the first ever BLW Triple Crown Champion. Does this answer your question?
Female Reporter: Yes, it does answer my question. But I have another one, with the deep talent pool of the BLW, do you think there will be any desputes due to large ego's and whatnot? Sort of like what happened in the
GWA a while back. As a matter of fact, I believe one involved Reckless and
Klitz.
President Jones: Whoa whoa whoa Lady. The BLW is anything BUT a place for ego-ma... Dan Kiltz: Shut tha fuck up Jones. Listen har, you say thar was a disp... despud... however the fuck you say it Female Reporter: dispute... Dan Kiltz: Yeah, a dispute between me and this city boy Reckless? Well that is just plain out cow shit. Ain't no dispute happen, I just kicked his 'coon lovin' ass!
Reckless: Excuse me you fat peice of country fried shit? That match was a complete joke and you know it! There is no way in hell you could beat me now, I was defending the FWF World Title against Hayabusa and Nomad while you were back home in Georgia picking your god damn cotton! Yet you think you can beat my ass?
Dan Kiltz: Yeah, I sure enough do. As a matter-a-fact, I might just do it right now! [[[Kiltz throws his cup of water at Reckless and tackles him as they tumble onto the floor near the reporters. Everyone scrambles as Reckless tosses
Kiltz headfirst into a chair, and proceeds to ram his head into it several times. Kiltz stands up, headbutt's Reckless, causing his own John Deere hat to fall off. They go through one of the tables near the podeum and rolls on the floor for a while when Ryan Van Tate walks into the room]]]
Ryan Van Tate: SWEET JESUS! President Jones: TATE! WHERE THE FUCK HAVE YOU BEEN! Ryan Van Tate: Its not my fault! He made me stop and get the chalupa! I SWEAR! President Jones: Where the fuck is he then? Ryan Van Tate: I don't kn... [[[A strange noise is heard from outside the doors and Tate and Jones turn around to see what it is. Mean while, President Aaron is trying to stop
Kiltz and Reckless. Reckless tosses Kiltz onto a stack of chairs and then turns around and punches President Aaron, who oversells greatly and flips over one of the tables. The strange noise is getting closer.]]]
President Jones: What the fuck is that god damn noise??? Ryan Van Tate: Its... its... CRUNCHY? [[[Doctor Kool Aid Man runs into the room wearing a prison suit and a cowboy hat, and he is carrying a stuffed badger. DKAM rushes Reckless who is standing over a fallen Kiltz and clubs him in the back of the head with the stuffed badger. Reckless isn't even knocked down by it, but DKAM runs around the podeum as if it knocked him out. Reckless grabs the stuffed badger and rips its head off]]]
Ryan Van Tate: SWEET JESUS! HE RIPPED CRUNCHY'S HEAD OFF! Reckless: Shut up you sniveling little bitch, this here Stuffed Badger Head is my next ticket to fame! Who need's Lodi's Head? Damn sure ain't me!
[[[Reckless walks off as DKAM just stands there looking confused. He shruggs it off, and picks Kiltz up for President Jones to verbally abuse]]]
President Jones: Listen to me closely you bird huntin', pick-up truck drivin', stupid son of an illiterate bictch! Do you think you are BLW material? Huh? You aren't BLW SHIT!
[[[Kiltz spats at President Jones, who in turn slaps him. Then he wipes his face]]] President Jones: Now bitch, maybe I can salvage a little bit out of this
Press Conference gone bad. If you have any balls what-so-ever, you'll show up here at the BLW Arena on Sunday to take on this man right here, Doctor
Kool Aid Man. So what if you don't have a contract, IF YOU HAVE BALLS, YOU
WILL SHOW! Right Doc?
Doctor Kool Aid Man: Eh Dan, if you would have been in tha cell wit DKAM and all tha hard niggaz, we'da have fucked yo pancho crackah ass up yo. Kinda like a Tiajuana ho. At this Fuel Y.T., your gonna be Doctor Kool Aid Man's next Patient.
President Jones: This man knows what the fuck he speaks of, Kiltz, show up at Fuel, or there will be hell to pay... BARELY LEGAL STYLE!
[[[DKAM carries Kiltz off and throws him outside of the BLW Conference Room as Jones picks up Aaron, who holds his head and staggers out of the room.
The scene fades to black as the logo for Sunday Night Fuel flashes on the screen.]]]
