James Lefevbre.) AF: Introducing first, from Lafayette, Louisiana, he's accompanied by Cajun' James Lefevbre, and weighs 235 lbs.
Here is CHRIS STONEBREAKER!!!!! RD: Stonebreaker's got his hands full against competitors such as the Grim Reaper and Jackhammer J.P. Steele.
AF: Next, from Detroit, Michigan, weighing 293 lbs, he's led to the ring by Annie Mack. . .JACKHAMMER J.P.
STEELE!!!!! (The theme from Rocky plays as the fan favorite Jackhammer J.P. Steele emerges from behind the curtain and heads toward the ring with Annie Mack.)
AF: Finally, from Parts Unknown, weighing 390 lbs. . .THE GRIM REAPER!!!!!!!!!! ("Don't Fear the Reaper" by Blue Oyster Cult blares from the loudspeaker as the Reaper slowly makes his way to the ring.)
RD: The gangs all here so let's find out who's going to start things off. DG: It looks like Reaper's going to make that decision for the ref as he attacks Stonebreaker before the bell! Reaper bounces off the ropes and nails Stonebreaker with a flying forearm!! Quick cover: one, two, kickout by Stonebreaker.
RD: I don't know why he went for the cover right away. I don't think Stonebreaker would have stayed down for a ten count.
DG: It's called intimidation, Ross, something a girl like you wouldn't know anything about. If he scores a quick pinfall, Stonebreaker will know that Reaper is better than him.
RD: I guess I see your point there. DG: Of course I have a point there. It is DAVE GREGOR we're talking about here. Elbow to the face by Reaper, and he follows it up by sending Stonebreaker for the ride. He drops the Southern boy with a shoulderblock and makes the tag to Jackhammer J.P. Steele.
RD: Steele comes in and picks up Stonebreaker, placing him on the top turnbuckle! Steele going up with him! SUPERPLEX!!! STONEBREAKER CRASHES TO THE MAT! Irish whip by Steele, and he nearly takes Stonebreaker's head off with a viscious clothesline! There's the first use of the Taped Fist, as Steele punches Stonebreaker right between the eyes.
DG: Stonebreaker is a rookie in there with two veterens, not a good combination. Irish Whip, reversed by Stonebreaker! Steele bounces off, and Stonebreaker catches him with a belly-to-belly!!
Cover: one, kickout by Steele. He looks upset with himself, but he's going to have to take it out on the Grim Reaper because Stonebreaker made the tag.
RD: Reaper picks up Jackhammer and puts him on the turnbuckle! What goes around comes around as THE REAPER EXECUTES A BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPERPLEX!!!! COVER: ONE, TWO, KICKOUT BY STEELE!
DG: Reaper bounces off the ropes as Steele slowly makes his way to his feet, and he drills J.P. with a flying forearm!!
Cover: one, two, kickout again by Steele! RD: This match might go on all night. Reaper sends Steele in for the ride and hits him with an elbow! Steele drops down to the mat again. Reaper drags him up by the hair and hits him with a jumping elbow thrust!!!! He follows it up with a chop and now a forearm to the small of the back. Reaper is cold and calculating, not to mention undefeated thus far in TCW!
DG: He's won one match, and that was against Dark Death. Who HASN'T beaten Dark Death? Reaper is choking Steele out!!!!! This is no DQ!! JIM RUPP IS COUNTING A PIN!! ONE, TWO, THREE!!!!
STEELE IS PINNED BUT HE HAS UNTIL THE COUNT OF TEN TO GET TO HIS FEET!! ONE, TWO, THREE, FOUR, FI. .
.STEELE IS BACK UP! RD: Not for long as Reaper takes him down with a bulldog! The Grim Reaper drags Steele to the corner and tags out to Chris Stonebreaker. STEELE TAGS REAPER AS SOON AS HE LEAVES THE RING!!! SMART MOVE BY JACKHAMMER J.P. STEELE AS HE GETS TO CATCH A BREATHER!
DG: I don't think Stonebreaker saw Jackhammer make the tag! REAPER HITS HIM WITH A POWERBOMB!!! HE PUTS STONEBREAKER ON THE TOP ROPE! WE COULD BE SEEING THE BURIAL HERE! NO!!!!!
STONEBREAKER SQUIRMS AWAY AND NOW HE'S IN CONTROL ON THE TOP ROPE!!! FRONT-LAYOUT SUPERPLEX!!!! COVER: ONE, KICKOUT BY THE GRIM REAPER!
RD: What does it take to keep that man down? Reaper slowly rises to his feet, and Stonebreaker hits him with a chop right across the chest! Stonebreaker bounces off the ropes and hits the man from the dark side with a Steinerline!!!!
DG: I wish you would get the wrestlers' hometowns right. The Grim Reaper is not from the dark side; he is from Parts Unknown. I'm sure the citizens of Parts Unknown would not want to be associated with the citizens of the Dark Side.
Where is Parts Unknown anyway? Wyoming? RD: Huh? Lay off the drugs for awhile, OK Dave. Stonebreaker grabs the Reaper and drives his face into the mat with a faceslam!! Reverse neckbreaker by Stonebreaker, and he tags out to Steele.
DG: Steele charges in and. . .boy is he having a bad day. He ran right into a left hand from the Grim Reaper and the taped fist did its job. POWERBOMB BY THE GRIM REAPER!!!! He tags out to Stonebreaker.
Stonebreaker throws Jackhammer out of the ring AND JUMPS ON HIM WITH A FLYING SHOULDERBLOCK!!!! STEELE CAUGHT HIM!!! HE DROPS STONEBREAKER ON THE GUARDRAIL, AND STONEBREAKER IS BUSTED OPEN!!!!
RD: Steele rolls Chris back intothe ring and tags out to the Grim Reaper who puts Stonebreaker on the top turnbuckle!! He goes up after him and hits him with a belly-to-belly superplex!!!
Cover: one, two, shoulder up by Stonebreaker!! DG: Reaper picks Stonebreaker up to his feet and sends him into the ropes. Hiptoss by the Reaper! Stonebreaker bounces right up and swings with the taped fist, but Reaper ducks under it! GERMAN SUPLEX BY THE REAPER: ONE, TWO, KICKOUT BY STONEBREAKER!! Grim Reaper goes to the top rope!!!!
Stonebreaker somehow makes his way to his feet, but the Reaper hits him with a flying clothesline!!!!
Cover: one, two, thr. . .shoulder up by Stonebreaker. RD: This is a good wrestling match here. The last match was one helluva brawl, but this is a wrestling match.
DG: There's a great wrestling move by the Reaper as he punches Stonebreaker in the genitals!! He bounces off the ropes and goes for the flying forearm, but Stonebreaker ducks under it!!! Did he know that was coming or did he just double over in pain?
RD: Whatever the reason, he's lucky he did it. Reaper stands back up just in time to be dropped by a Steinerline!!!
Stonebreaker drops the knee to the Reaper's forehead and covers: one, two, kickout by the Reaper! He stands the Reaper up and executes a perfect back heel kick!!! The Reaper falls out of the ring!!
DG: This is not where Stonebreaker wants to be with the Grim Reaper. Stonebreaker jumps off the apron, driving the taped fist into the Reaper's head. He whips the Reaper into the guardrail!!!!
Stonebreaker introduces his head to the ringpost!!! Now, he decides that he likes the guardrail better and whips the Reaper into that again!
RD: Stonebreaker is handling himself quite well right now, but he might want to take it back into the ring. Chris slaps on a sleeper, but the Reaper counters it with a jawbreaker!!!!! That quickly the tide can turn in TCW! The Reaper is up and drives Stonebreaker's head into the ringsteps. He throws Stonebreaker into the ring and follows him in.
DG: The Reaper puts Stonebreaker up on the turnbuckle, and this looks like bad news for Chris as he joins him up there. HE HAS HIM UP AND HERE IT COMES! THE BURIAL!!!!! POSSIBLY THE MOST DEADLY MOVE IN TCW!!! COVER: ONE, TWO, THREE!!!! NOW STONEBREAKER HAS THE TEN COUNT! ONE, TWO, THREE, FOUR, FIVE, SIX, SEVEN, EIGHT, NINE, TEN!!!!!!!!!!!!! THE GRIM REAPER ADVANCES TO THE FINALS!
RD: He's basically got a handicap match in front of him, facing two members of the Palace of Power. Speaking of the P.O.P. , the Scorpion is set to face Carolina Crusher in our next match in a falls count anywhere match. These two have some history between them; let's take a look at what brought these two here.
DG: First, Carolina Crusher was offered a membership in the Palace of Power, but he turned it down. On November 16 he met the Scorpion, and, after the match, the P.O.P. made him regret his decision.
(Flashback to November 16 at Saturday Night Fever.) DG: Uh, oh looks like its message time as the P.O.P. as flooded the ring! Humiliator is the first one to him as he starts pounding away at the immobile Crusher. The tag champs now have him, and there's the Dallas Death!!!! Even more pressure on that back!! Flint has him now, and drills him with the Playboy's Dream!!!
This is great! RD: They have spraypaint now, and Jessica has just painted P.O.P. on Crusher in purple and black spraypaint! Here comes some help! Fabio Erotic, Scoult, the Scalpers, and the Birds of Prey have come down to the ring to help. We have ourselves a war!! Fabio goes right after Flint while Scoult takes care of Scorpion! Birds of Prey are battling the Dallas Connection, and the Scalpers are really doing a number on the Humiliator. Leon is trying to help everyone at once, but he's not enough!!! Finally, the P.O.P. leaves the ring,and the fan favorites help Carolina Crusher out of here.
(Back to realtime.) RD: Crusher was bruised and beaten, but he would get his revenge on the following Tuesday, at the North American Title Tournament Battle Royal as Scorp was headed to the ring.
(Flashback to November 19 at Tuesday Night Extreme.) THE SCORPION!!!!!!! RD: Another member of the Palace. He's coming off a big win over the Carolina Crusher on Satur. . .LOOK! OUT OF NOWHERE CAROLINA CRUSHER RUNS UP AND NAILS SCORPION WITH A STEEL CHAIR FROM BEHIND!!! CRUSHER GRABS SCORP AND HITS HIM WITH A DDT ON THE CONCRETE FLOOR!!!!!!!!
DG: It looks like Scorp isn't going to make it to the ring. Crusher runs to the ring, there's the bell, and Scorpion is officially eliminated!!!!!
(Back to the current time.) RD: This fued will continue tonight. Let's take it up to Arnie for the introductions. AF: This match is scheduled for one fall and is a FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE MATCH! Introducing first, a member of the Palace of Power, weighing 265 lbs. . .THE SCORPION!!!!!!!!
("Hellraiser" by Ozzy Osbourne plays as Scorpion and Jessica make their way to the ring.) RD: Remeber, the winner of this match gets a North American Title shot Saturday night back at TCW Pyramid.
DG: The Palace of Power has had a good night so far. Will they be able to keep it up? AF: And his opponent. . . (Carolina Crusher is thrown thru the curtain and falls on his face.) RD: What the hell??? DG: THE PALACE OF POWER IS EXACTING SOME REVENGE IN THIS MATCH! THEY ARE SLAPPING CRUSHER AROUND! TONY FLINT DRILLS THE CRUSHER WITH THE PLAYBOY'S DREAM ON THE CONCRETE, AND NOW JAMES HARPER HAS HIM UP IN THE HELLBINDER!!!!
RD: NOT AGAIN! HERE COMES SOUTHERN THUNDER!!!! RIGHT BEHIND THEM ARE THE SUPREME CASTLES!!!! THE PALACE OF POWER ARE OUTNUMBERED!!! THE HUMILIATOR THROWS CRUSHER INTO THE RING AND TAKES OFF TO THE BACK OF THE ARENA!!!
DG: SCORPION ROLLS UP CRUSHER! ONE, TWO, KICKOUT BY CRUSHER!!!!! THE REF WAS OUT OF POSITION!!!
RD: He was talking to Jessica, and he's over there again!! THE REFEREE JIM RUPP HAS EJECTED JESSICA FROM THE RINGSIDE AREA!!! WE FINALLY HAVE A ONE ON ONE MATCH!!
DG: She's his manager. Stay Jessica, it's a no disqualification match. RD: She's facing fines if she stays. Jessica is gone! DG: Scorp doesn't need her to beat this bum anyway. Enzuigiri by Scorpion!! I told you. Scorp bounces off the ropes, and CRUSHER CATCHES HIM IN A BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX!! ONE, TWO, KICKOUT BY SCORP.
RD: Crusher is rejuvanated, and he drills Scorpion with a piledriver!!! And he's taking it outside!! Crusher slams Scorp into the ringpost!!! He grabs the ringbell and drills Scorpion with it!
Crusher throws Scorp over the guardrail!!! DG: He's endangering the fans!! RD: Like you care about the safety of the fans! DG: Oh yeah. Good point! Hit the fat guy in the first row for me Scorp! RD: I wish he'd come over here and hit you. Crusher rakes the eyes of Scorpion and hits him again with the ring bell!
SCORPION IS BUSTED OPEN!! Crusher sees the blood and really starts laying into him now. Another shot with the ring bell!
DG: Crusher throws Scorp back over the guardrail and goes to get his chain!!! He wraps it around his fist and nails the Scorpion with a big right hand, opening the cut even further! Bodyslam by Crusher and a cover: one, two, kickout by Scorpion!
RD: Crusher stands up, BUT HE DROPPED THE CHAIN!! SCORPION PICKS IT UP AND HITS CRUSHER WITH IT!! HE TOSSES HIM BACK OVER THE GUARDRAIL!
DG: What a MORON! How could Carolina Crusher drop the chain? Scorp taking advantage of the situation with a kneelift! Scorpion has Crusher and leads him to the back of the arena by the Wheel!!!
He slams Crusher's head into the wheel! Scorp charges after Crusher with a clothesline, but he ducks under it!!!
CRUSHER FLINGS SCORP OVER SOME CHAIRS AND INTO THE CROWD!!! HE HITS SCORP WITH THE CHAIN AGAIN!!!!
RD: Crusher has his chain back! Vertical suplex by Crusher!!!! He's going for another, but Scorpion reverses it!
Scorpion drags him back toward the ring!!! He picks up the ring bell AND HITS CRUSHER IN THE FACE WITH IT! CRUSHER IS BUSTED OPEN!!!! SCORPION JUMPS UP ON THE GUARDRAIL!!! HE COMES OFF AND DRIVES THE RING BELL INTO CRUSHER'S FACE AGAIN!!! COVER: ONE, TWO, THREE!!!!!!!! HE GOT HIM!!!!
DG: The Palace of Power is now 3-0, with two matches left. Crusher just couldn't overcome the beating he took earlier from the entire stable.
RD: The Scorpion will meet the North American Champion on Saturday. That will be the winner of our next match: The Flashmaster vs. Titanacus. Let's hear some comments from the challenger.
(Flashmaster is back in the locker room with Ms. Jessica) Flashmaster : Titanticus you big fat fart headed goof, you got lucky back when you won that Battle Royal. The only reason nobody could toss you out because of you weight - you must weigh a ton. I've fought your kind in Battle Royal's before. I give you credit for being the toughest son of a bitch toeliminate, but in a match, I don't have to pick you up and throw you over the top, I just got to roll over and cover you.
Ms. Jessica : That's right Titanticus - prepare to lose. (Back to Dave and Ross.) DG: You big fat-headed goof? I bet Titanacus is frozen with fear after hearing those words.
RD: Titanacus won the battle royal to earn the title, and the Flashmaster got the title shot by finishing second. Let's take a look at the end of the battle royal.
(Flashback to November 19 at Tuesday Night Extreme.) RD: Flashmaster spins and hits the big guy with an enzuigiri!! The big guy is down!!! Flashmaster bounces off the ropes and hits him with a diving headbutt!! He brings the big guy to his feet. KEEP HIM ON THE GROUND!!
DG: Umm, how is he supposed to throw him over the top if he's on the ground. That would be a good strategy if this were a regular match, but it's not. Flashmaster whips Titanacus into the turnbuckle and follows him in to the corner shoulder-first!!! NO ONE HOME!! That move never works.
RD: TITANACUS PLACES FLASHMASTER UP ON THE TOP ROPE AND ROARS AT THE CROWD!!! THESE TWO HAVE BEEN WRESTLING FOR ALMOST AN HOUR! THE BIG GUY GOES UP AFTER HIM!!
MOSCOW SPIKE!!!!!!! THAT IS ONE OF THE DEADLIEST MOVES IN THE HISTORY OF WRESTLING!! DG: It's only matter of time now, Ross. That move took everything that the Flashmaster had left. Titanacus whips him into the ropes and nails him with a belly-to-belly suplex!!! He's going for a powerbomb. No!! Flashmaster shows he still has some fight by taking the big guy to the mat with a football tackle!!!
Flashmaster somehow gets back to his feet and drags Titanacus to his feet. Irish whip, but a reversal by Titanacus.
SPINEBUSTER BY TITANACUS!!! HE PICKS UP FLASHMASTER AND, YES!, HE THROWS HIM OVER THE TOP TO THE FLOOR!! TITANACUS IS THE FIRST TCW NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION!!!!!!!!!!!!!
(back to the current time.) RD: Obviously, in order to win tonight, the Flashmaster has to avoid the Moscow Spike. Let's take it up to Arnie for the introductions. AF: This match is for the TCW NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP!!! It is scheduled for one fall and has a thirty minute time limit. Introducing first, the challenger, from Miami, FL, led to the ring by Ms. Jessica, weighing 315 lbs, THE FLASHMASTER!!!!!
(The Flashmaster and Ms. Jessica come to the ring the music of Deep Purple. The fans give a mixed reaction. They don't like him, but they like Titanacus less.)
AF: And now, the champion. He stands seven feet, four inches tall and weighs 490 lbs. From Moscow, Russia, with his manager, Daniel Barrett, here is: TITANACUS!!!!!! ("Jesus Built My Hotrod" by Ministry plays as the huge Titanacus comes to the ring to a loud chorus of boos. He stops halfway and roars at the crowd.)
RD: That is one immense figure. Will Flashmaster be intimidated? DG: I don't think so. The two have met before, and that will give Flashmaster some confidence.
RD: You're right, Dave, as the Flashmaster attacks Titanacus before the bell. DG: I'm sorry, I didn't hear you. What did you say? RD: Flashmaster attacks Titanacus before the bell. DG: No, before that. RD: I said you're right, Dave. It was bound to happen sometime. Even a monkey can get the right answer sometimes.
DG: Be careful or I'll call Titanacus over here. Flashmaster whips Titanacus into the ropes and kicks him in the midsection. He bounces off and drills him with a forearm smash!! The big guy is reeling!
RD: Flashmaster needed a quick start, and he got it. Cross-corner whip by Flashmaster, and he's following him in, but Titanacus lifts his knee!!
DG: I think that move has worked about three times in the history of wrestling. POWERBOMB BY TITANACUS!!! One mistake and he makes you pay for it. Cover: one, two, shoulder up by Flashmaster. RD: Irish whip by Titanacus, but it's reversed by Flashmaster. BELLY-TO -BELLY BY FLASHMASTER! ONE, TWO, KICKOUT BY TITANACUS!!
DG: Wow, I'm impressed. I can't believe he got him up. Luckily he has momentum on his side. Flashmaster grabs the big guy and throws him over the top rope!! Too bad he couldn't do it at the battle royal.
RD: Titanacus was really caught of guard. PLANCHA BY FLASHMASTER!!! He's pulling out all the stops. He whips the big guy into the guardrail!! Flashmaster looks over to Ms. Jessica, who nods her approval. He smashes Titanacus into the ringsteps!!
DG: I like Ms. Jessica. She's got a bitchy streak to her that says "don't mess with me". RD: Whatever Dave. The two men have re-entered the ring. Flashmaster drives his knee into the Russian's back.
ENZUIGIRI AND THE BIG GUY GOES DOWN!!! FLASHMASTER QUICKLY TAKES ADVANTAGE BY HEADING TO THE TOP ROPE!!! FLYING LEGDROP!!!! COVER: ONE, TWO, KICKOUT BY THE CHAMPION!
