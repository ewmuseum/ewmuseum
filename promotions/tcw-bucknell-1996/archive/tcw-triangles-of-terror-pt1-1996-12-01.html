Total Championship Wrestling — Card

TCW: Triangles of Terror

1 December 1996 &mdash; Bryce Jordan Center, State College, PA
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scenes from TCW's one month existence flash on the screen as "Paranoid" by Black Sabbath plays. Shown are Masked Bulldog with the Atomic Bulldog on the concrete on Scoult; Dr. Doom holding the TCW World Title Belt aloft; Brandon Charm giving the Brandon Bomb to both members of P.O. Militia, Sgt. Woody coming off the top rope with an elbow driving Charm through a table, Southern Thunder winning the tag titles with a Thunder Strike, Carolina Crusher giving the DDT to Scorpion on the outside before the North American title battle royal, and Titanacus eliminating the Flashmaster to win the North American Title. The music fades to fans chanting TCW! TCW! TCW! TCW!
and the camera goes to Ross Douglas and Dave Gregor at the broadcast table.) RD: YOU ARE LOOKING LIVE AT THE BRYCE JORDAN CENTER IN STATE COLLEGE, PA. TONIGHT YOU WILL WITNESS HISTORY; TOTAL CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING'S FIRST PAY PER VIEW: TRIANGLES OF TERROR!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
DG: It's hard to believe that this league has come this far in such a short amount of time. Somehow, President Fisher has pulled it off, and we are here tonight.
RD: And what a card we have for you tonight. All three titles are on the line, World Champion Dr. Doom squares off against the hated Brandon Charm.
DG: He's not hated, the morons in the stands just misunderstand him. Also tonight, World Tag Team Champions Southern Thunder defend their belts against the former champs, the Dallas Connection. Plus, Titanacus puts his North American Title belt on the line against the Flashmaster.
RD: Don't forget about the falls-count-anywhere match between Carolina Crusher and the Scorpion. The winner of that match will face the North American champion Saturday on Saturday Night Fever.
DG: And the main attraction for the night is the Triangles of Terror battle royal itself. The top nine contenders in TCW square off in three one-fall triangle matches.
Each match will be a Spin the Wheel/Make the Deal gimmick match. The three winners go on to a final elimination triangle match, also Spin the Wheel/Make the Deal.
RD: The winner of Triangles of Terror will earn a World Title shot at the next PPV in January. The one thing I don't like about this tournament is the format of the first round. A wrestler can be eliminated without even entering the ring!!!
DG: I can't wait to get started. Let's take it back to Thomas Chernin at the wheel. (The scene moves to the back of the arena. Thomas Chernin and TCW President Dan Fisher stand beside the huge wheel listing eighteen possible gimmick matches.)
TC: Well folks, if you didn't understand the danger of this tournament, you will after looking at the matches on this wheel. We have ladder matches, barbed wire matches, stretcher matches, strap matches, first blood matches, table matches, etc. You name it, it's on the wheel. President Fisher, I understand you have an announcement about upcoming events here in TCW.
DF: Thank you, Thomas. As you know, the winner of Triangles of Terror will face the World Champion at the January PPV. Now, I can tell you an exact date: Tuesday, January 7, 1997. The format will be a Battle Bowl tournament with all wrestlers under contract with TCW on the card. I can also tell you where this monumental event will take place: the CoreStates Center in Philadelphia.
TC: WOW!! What a night that promises to be. However, tonight should be grand as well. The first match is just about ready, so President Fisher, will you do the honors of spinning the wheel.
DF: Of course. (Fisher gives the wheel a huge spin.) TC: That spin will indicate the gimmick match for the semifinal match pitting Fabio Erotic, the Humiliator, and Masked Bulldog. It's slowing down now. . .DOG COLLAR MATCH!!!! A triple dog collar match, another first in TCW history. Back to you at the broadcast table.
(The scene shifts back to Ross and Dave.) RD: This match should be interesting, Dave. Pinfalls and submissions don't count. The only way to win is to drag your opponent to all four corners. DG: There's the bell to get started so let's take it up to Arnie for the ring announcements.
AF: This match is scheduled for one fall with a thirty minute time limit. It is a SEMIFINAL match in the TRIANGLES OF TERROR TOURNAMENT and is a DOG COLLAR MATCH!!
Introducing first, from Orlando, FL led down the aisle by Fifi. (The crowd pops as Fifi is announced and explodes when Madonna's "Erotica" begins to play.)
AF: He weighs 234 lbs. Here is: FAAAAAABIO EROTIC!!!!!! (Fabio helps Fifi into the ring and receives his pre-match kiss.) AF: Next, from parts unknown, led to the ring by Maniac Bob, he weighs 290 lbs. ..MASKED BULLDOG!!!!!!! (The crowd boos as Masked Bulldog comes to the ring accompanied by Maniac Bob to the strains of "Bad Company" by Bad Company.)
AF: And their opponent, he's wearing the purple and black of the Palace of Power, from Your Deepest Nightmares, weighing 277 lbs. . .THE HUMILIATOR!!!!!!!!!
(Rey Misterio's music plays as the Humiliator comes to the ring led by Jessica. The crowd response is mostly boos with a smattering of cheers.)
DG: I think I'll have to go with Masked Bulldog to win this semifinal. After all, it is a dog collar match; it should be his speciality.
RD: You can throw records out of the window tonight. You never know, Bulldog might not even get into the ring.
DG: He might not as Fabio Erotic and the Humiliator have won the rights to start out. Fabio attacks the Humiliator as soon as the dog collars are attached!!! And who is this guy coming to ringside??
RD: IT'S BRIAN HAAS !!!! DG: Oh, that clears everything up. Who the hell is Brian Haas? RD: Haas just recently signed with TCW. Rumor has it that he has joined the Supreme Castles.
DG: Which would explain why Jessica isn't too happy that he has appeared. RD: She couldn't be too unhappy right now as the Humiliator has gained control by whipping Fabio with the chain connecting the dog collars. Humiliator executes a beautiful swinging neckbreaker!! He follows it up with a neck scissors. He hits him once again with the chain!!
DG: The Palace of Power has struggled recently. Tonight would be a perfect night to turn things around. Side headlock by Humiliator, but Fabio picks him up for a back suplex!!!! He's choking him with the chain now!!!! The little wuss has a mean streak after all!
RD: What did you expect from the Erotic One? Fabio whips him into the ropes and catches him coming off with a FRANKENSTEINER!!! That was impressive considering the dog collars.
DG: I'll admit that Erotic is a good wrestler; he just doesn't have that killer instinct. Russian legsweep by Fabio, now it's his turn to use the dog collar chain as a whip!! He's going for the turnbuckle!!!! There's the first one!! He's almost to the second one. . .Humiliator tackles him from behind!! Fabio reaches up and tags in Masked Bulldog.
RD: Fabio can't win by leaving the ring, but he can catch his wind. Bulldog rushes in and nails Humiliaor with a kneelift! He goes up to the top rope!!! Risky move for Humiliator only needs to tug on the chain. Bulldog flies off with a lariat!!!!
DG: I told you. This is Bulldog's kind of match. He's sending Humiliator for the ride, BUT HUMILIATOR REVERSES IT AND CATCHES BULLDOG WITH A SHORT CLOTHESLINE!!! BULLDOG IS DOWN CLUTCHING HIS THROAT. HE LOOKS REALLY HURT!
RD: HE MIGHT HAVE AGGRAVATED THAT LARYNX INJURY!! HUMILIATOR DRAGS HIM OVER TO THE FIRST TURNBUCKLE, TOUCHES IT, AND MOVES ON TO THE SECOND!!
DG: There's two. Bulldog is writhing in pain. It looks like he's having trouble breathing!! HUMILIATOR JUST HIT NUMBER THREE!!! ONE MORE AND HE'S IN THE FINALS!! FABIO RUNS IN THE RING TO STOP HIM, BUT HUMILIATOR DIVES!!! HE GOT IT!!! HUMILIATOR IS IN THE FINALS WITH A QUICK WIN OVER FABIO EROTIC AND MASKED BULLDOG!
RD: Fabio is not very happy with Masked Bulldog. I don't blame him. If Bulldog wasn't ready, he shouldn't have been in the ring.
AF: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of this match THE HUMILIATOR!!!!!!! DG: So, the Palace of Power gets off on the right foot here at Triangles of Terror. WAIT!!! BRIAN HAAS IS SNEAKING UP ON THE HUMILIATOR FROM BEHIND. HE DRILLS HIM WITH A CLOTHESLINE TO THE BACK OF THE HEAD!! POWERBOMB!!! LEON IS AFTER HIM, BUT HAAS ESCAPES INTO THE STANDS!
The Supreme Castles continue their attack on the Palace of Power. This will erupt soon. RD: We'll see if they keep it up throughout the night. Playboy Tony Flint is in the next match. Let's take it back to Thomas and President Fisher at the wheel.
(Scene shifts back to Chernin and Fisher at the wheel again.) TC: Before we get to the next match, let's take a look at what occurred earlier tonight. The P.O. Militia fought the Birds of Prey in a classic that ended with the crowd giving both teams a standing ovation. The match was scheduled for a twenty minute time limit, but when that time was up, President Fisher gave the crowd what they wanted, twenty more minutes. This match saw a total of ten finishing moves being executed, seven by the Birds of Prey. The teams showed heart, as they spilled sweat and blood. Only The Mountaineer Frank Pipik made it through the match without needing stitches. Finally, Soaring Eagle succombed to the Cold Stream Crunch after being hit with a baseball bat by Pipik. This match was a record that might never be broken barring Iron Man matches: thirty-five minutes.
(Chernin turns to President Fisher.) TC: President Fisher will you please spin the wheel for our next Triangles of Terror match.
(Fisher spins the wheel once again.) TC: This spins will determine the destiny of Playboy Tony Flint, his main enemy Scout, and Dark Ninja. Round and round it goes, and it stops at. . .CASKET MATCH!!!!!!! This promises to be a war guys.
(The scene shifts back to the broadcast table.) RD: A casket match! This should be interesting. DG: The wrestlers will really have to lay a beating on someone to win. That shouldn't be a problem for Scoult or Playboy Tony Flint. Those two HATE each other. Let's hear from Scoult, who's back with Thomas Chernin.
(cut back to the TCW Interview Podium) TC: Scoult, you are the ninth seed in the tournament. You are pretty lucky that Scorpion and Carolina Crusher, two men ranked higher than you, are in the falls count anywhere match.
Scoult: Well I am lucky. I get to face YOU FLINT! I am just sorry that Dark Ninja has to get involved. I just have to say I have an advantage. Only 3 men have ever been in a triangle match. I am one of them. In fact I won that match.
Now Flint have you ever heard of the saying that you should try, try, and try again. Well that's me. I will never stop trying. Just when you think that I'm gone I will get you. It will be a cloudy day on December 1st. Now a warning to everyone. I have a way to pay people back. That is a very big promise. I know one particular group of people who I want very badly. When the match I want to happen happens those people are going to pay. I know that this match will happen. Everyone in POP better watch out. Yes even you Scorpion and Humiliator.
TC: There you have it. Scoult is ready to go and so is Arnie. (cut to Arnie Foradori in the ring) AF: This match is scheduled for one fall with a thirty minute time limit. It is a SEMIFINAL match in the Triangles of Terror tournament and is a CASKET match. The only way to win is to stuff your opponent in the casket and slam the lid shut. Now, the participants, introducing first, he's a member of the Palace of Power.
(As Tony Flint's music starts, the fans erupt in a loud mixture of boos and cheers. The fans love to hate him.) AF: Led to the ring by Jessica, he's from Beverly Hills, California and weighs 285 lbs. He is. . .PLAYBOY TONY FLINT!!!!
AF: Next, from San Francisco, California, weighing 314 lbs, he is led to the ring by Bud Quast. Here is SCOULT!!!!!
("Lump" by Presidents of the U.S.A. starts as Scoult makes his way to the ring to the cheers of the fans. Some of his popularity has worn off over his losing streak).
RD: Scoult is the only man of the three to have been in a triangle match before, and he won it.
AF: Their opponent, a member of the Shingan Dojo, from Tokyo, Japan, weighing 245 lbs. . .DARK NINJA!!!!!! (Dean Malenko's music plays as Master Shingan leads the mysterious Dark Ninja to the ring.)
DG: Dark Ninja is still a relative unknown in TCW. He'll start out against Scoult in this semifinal match. Ninja whips Scoult into the ropes, and the two get hit with a double clothesline!!! That was a weird way to start.
RD: Ninja up first, and he sends Scoult into the corner. He runs in with a Stinger Splash, but Scoult moves out of the way! I think Ninja underestimated Scoult's quickness. Scoult drops down and takes Ninja's legs out from under him with a legsweep and follows it up by dropping the elbow to Ninja's throat! Russian legsweep by Scoult! Irish whip, reversed by Ninja!!! Ninja with a hiptoss, but Scoult reverses it into a backslide!!!
DG: HELLO! Scoult, anyone home?! You can't pin people in this match, and I don't think a backslide is going to knock anyone out. Irish whip by Scoult, but Ninja reverses it again and catches Scoult with a belly-to-belly suplex!
He makes the tag to Flint! RD: Here we go. Arch-rivals squaring off! Flint runs in and throws Scoult out of the ring! Why do I get the feeling that all hell is going to break loose? Flint out after him, but someone handed Scoult a road sign!!! SCOULT NAILS HIM WITH THE ROADSIGN, AGAIN!!! HE'S GOING TO THE TOP TURNBUCKLE!! YOU HAVE TO BE KIDDING ME! VAPORIZOR ON THE CONCRETE!!!! FLINT IS SERIOUSLY HURT!!
DG: Could Scoult finally be getting a win over Tony Flint? SCOULT SETS UP A TABLE!!! HE HAS FLINT ON TOP OF IT. BRAINBUSTER!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! SCOULT DROVE FLINTS HEAD RIGHT THROUGH THE TABLE, BREAKING IT IN HALF!!!!
RD: SCOULT WHIPS HIM INTO THE RINGSTEPS AND HITS HIM WITH THE ROADSIGN AGAIN! HE JUST PUT FLINT ON ANOTHER TABLE!!! HE'S ON THE TOP TURBUCKLE!!! VAPORIZOR!!!! FLINT DUCKED OUT OF THE WAY!!!!!!!!!
DG: What a turn of momentum that was. FLINT DRILLS SCOULT WITH A JUMPING DDT ON THE CONCRETE!! He drives the knee into the small of the back! Picking up Scoult by the hair, he executes a belly-to- back suplex!! Flint throws him into the ring tosses him into the corner to make the tag. I think he wants to send a message to Scoult that says he can beat him anytime he wants to.
RD: Ninja in, and he and Playboy Tony Flint are circling each other. Ninja nails him with a back heel kick! Sends him for a ride and catches him by surprise with a belly-to-belly!! Ninja whips Flint into the ropes, but Playboy comes back and take s him down with a neck-scissors!! He drags Ninja up and hits him with a snap suplex!!! Flint heading to the top.
DG: High-risk manuever. You need those to win a match like this. Frog splash by Flint!!!! And Flint is getting cocky, strutting around and yelling "Woo Hoo!" The match isn't over yet Tony.
RD: Flint puts Ninja in a front facelock. Could we see a DDT!? No!!!! Ninja grabs Flint by the tights and throws him to the outside! Ninja goes out after him. NINJA HAS THE RING BELL AND HITS FLINT WITH IT! PLAYBOY TONY FLINT IS BLEEDING! Dark Ninja throws Flint back in the ring!
DG: Big boot to the midsection, but Flint caught it! He spins him around and drills Ninja with an atomic drop!! Flint tags out to Scoult who reluctantaly sticks his hand out.
RD: Scoult might enjoy watching Flint get whupped on, but he knows that if Flint gets pinned, he's eliminated also.
Scoult slowly enters the ring and GETS DRILLED WITH AN ENZUIGIRI TO THE FACE!!! SCOULT DROPS LIKE A TON OF BRICKS! DG: NINJA HAS SIGNALLED FOR THE CASKET TO BE OPENED! HE ROLLS SCOULT INTO THE COFFIN AND SHUTS THE LI. . .SCOULT STICKS HIS ARM OUT!! THE MATCH ISN'T OVER!!
RD: That was a close call for Scoult. Ninja throws him out of the casket to the concrete and drops him with a superkick!! NINJA IS HANDED A SHOVEL!!!!!! HE HITS HIM RIGHT IN THE FACE, AND NOW SCOULT IS BUSTED OPEN! He tosses Scoult into the ringpost and sets up a table!!! Master Shingan is grinning from ear to ear!
NINJA HAS A KEYBOARD!!! WHERE DID THAT COME FROM?! DG: Who cares? ONE, TWO, THREE TIMES HE DRIVES THE KEYBOARD INTO SCOULT'S FACE!!! HE FOLLOWS IT UP WITH A WHIP INTO THE GUARDRAIL FOR GOOD MEASURE!!! RD: Ninja puts Scoult on the table and now HE'S going up top!! NINJA COMES FLYING OFF WITH A HEADBUTT AND THE TABLE BREAKS!!! SCOULT IS NOT MOVING!!!
DG: Masked Bulldog showed on our very first card that Scoult cannot handle himself outside the ring. Ninja whips Scoult into the ring steps again!!! Finally, the agony is over for Scoult as Ninja rolls him into the ring.
RD: But Ninja is going to the top rope again!! MOONSAULT!!!!!!! THAT'S NINJA'S FINISHING MOVE!!!
DG: ROLL HIM INTO THE CASKET YOU MORON!!! RD: Ninja's going to the top rope again! SCOULT JUST SAT UP AND THE CROWD IS GOING NUTS!!! DARK NINJA DOESN'T SEE HIM! SCOULT DRILLS HIM IN THE MIDSECTION, AND NINJA IS CROTCHED ON THE TOP TURNBUCKLE! DOUBLE UNDERHOOK SUPERPLEX BY SCOULT!!!! BOTH MEN ARE DOWN!!
DG: Scoult is stirring first though! HE MAKES THE TAG TO TONY FLINT!!! Flint drags Ninja to his feet and executes a belly-to-back suplex!!! Neck snap by Flint. He scoops Ninja up and throws him to the mat for a bodyslam!!! Flint is taking his turn at the top rope now!
RD: He's taking a lot of time up there. Flint lets out a "Woo Hoo!" and flies off with an elbowdrop! He caught him right across the throat!! FLINT SIGNALS FOR THE CASKET TO BE OPENED! HE ROLLS NINJA IN AND SLAMS THE LID AND STARTS TO CELEBRATE!! HE DIDN'T SEE NINJA GET HIS ARM IN THERE!
DG: NINJA SNEAKS BACK INTO THE RING AND IS RIGHT AGAINST THE ROPES BEHIND FLINT!!! JESSICA IS UP ON THE APRON! SHE JUST HIT DARK NINJA WITH HER PURSE!!!! NINJA FALLS RIGHT INTO FLINT!!! HE TURNS AROUND AND DRILLS HIM WITH, YES!!! PLAYBOY'S DREAM!!!!!!!!!
RD: What is in that purse? FLINT ROLLS NINJA OVER TO THE CASKET AND SLAMS THE LID SHUT!!!! IT'S OVER!!! PLAYBOY TONY FLINT JOINS FELLOW MEMBER OF THE PALACE OF POWER, THE HUMILIATOR, IN THE TRIANGLES OF TERROR FINAL!!!
DG: If every match tonight is as good as that one, this could be the greatest card of all time! Especially if people like Tony Flint keep winning!
RD: It's a sad day for wrestling if people like Tony Flint keep winning. Thomas is ready for the next spin solet's take it back to him.
(Scene once again changes to Thomas Chernin and President Fisher at the wheel.) TC: Before the final semifinal match, let's take a look back at what transpired earlier tonight. Two relative newcomers, Hardcore and Poncho Demento, squared off. Both men were undefeated, so something had to give. This match had a very strange ending. Hardcore once again got off to a quick start, nailing Demento with the Hostile Takeover only two minutes into the match, much like he did to Playboy Tony Flint.
However, David McKnight was able to distract referee Jim Rupp, allowing Poncho to recover enough to kick out.
Three more times during the match, Hardcore went for the Hostile Takeover, but each time he was thwarted by Demento. The third time he was back dropped from the top rope to the floor. Hardcore decided enough was enough and left, giving Poncho Demento the countout victory.
(Looking at President Fisher.) TC: So Dan, are we ready for match number three? DF: You bet we are. (Fisher spins the wheel once again.) TC: This spin is to determine the type of match for Jackhammer J.P. Steele, Chris Stonebreaker, and the Grim Reaper.
These three men will face off in a . . .TAPED FIST TEXAS DEATH MATCH!!! All three men will have their fists taped. After each pinfall, the referee will count to ten. If the wrestler fails to answer the count, the match is over.
Back to you Ross and Dave.
