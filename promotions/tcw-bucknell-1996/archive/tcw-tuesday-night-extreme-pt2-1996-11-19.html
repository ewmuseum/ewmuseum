TCW: Tuesday Night Extreme (11/19/96)

19 November 1996 &mdash; TCW Pyramid, Harrisburg, PA
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DG: Over on the other side of the ring, we have Jack the Ripper and the Humiliator going at it. Ripper runs at Humiliator WHO DUCKS DOWN AND BACKDROPS RIPPER OVER THE TOP ROPE!!! JACK THE RIPPER HAS BEEN ELIMINATED LESS THAN A MINUTE INTO THE MATCH!
RD: In the far corner, a couple of newcomers, Poncho Demento and the Flashmaster are exchanging blows. Flashmaster nails him with a German suplex! He's trying to throw Poncho over the top. . .HE DID IT! PONCHO DEMENTO HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!!!
DG: It looks like we have a double-team as Flashmaster and the Humiliator are doing a number on the Carolina Crusher. Double hiptoss by the two, and the Humiliator leaves the Flashmaster on his own. Crusher seizes the opportunity by catching Flashmaster in a dragon suplex!! He's biting the Flashmaster now. Carolina Crusher seems like a new man after his beating on Saturday! Dropkick by the Crusher, and now he's biting him again!
RD: Right in front of us, Playboy Tony Flint and Chris Stonebreaker are battling. Flint with a bionic elbow, and STONEBREAKER FALLS BACKWARDS RIGHT INTO CAROLINA CRUSHER! CRUSHER GRABS HIM BY THE HAIR AND THROWS HIM OVER THE TOP ROPE!!!!! CHRIS STONEBREAKER IS GONE!!!!!
DG: CRUSHER GOING AFTER FLINT. HE'S TRYING TO GET HIM OUT, but Flint is hooks his arm in the second rope to stay alive. We are now down to fourteen wrestlers with a shot at the North American Title.
RD: CRUSHER GOES OVER TO MASKED BULLDOG AND GRABS HIM FROM BEHIND!! HE RUNS TO THE ROPES AND THROWS BULLDOG OVER THE ROPES!!!! MANIAC BOB IS LIVID!!!
DG: Carolina Crusher has been on a rampage thus far. Four guys eliminated, with Scorpion making five, and we are down to 13 wrestlers alive. Dark Ninja finally calms him down with a shoulderblock.
RD: Titanacus and Brandon Charm are working together as expected, right now against Jackhammer J.P. Steele. Charm whips Titanacus into the ropes and holds Steele up for a shot from the big guy. STEELE DUCKS DOWN AND TITANACUS ACCIDENTLY HIT CHARM WITH A CLOTHESLINE!!!! CHARM WASN'T EXPECTING IT, AND HE DOESN'T HAVE HIS BALANCE!!
YES!!!! CHARM FALLS OVER THE TOP ROPE ONTO THE FLOOR!!! BRANDON CHARM HAS BEEN ELIMINATED BY TITANACUS OF ALL PEOPLE!!!!!
DG: NOOOO!!!! RD: It happened Dave. The guy is human. FABIO EROTIC HAS JUST ELIMINATED BALD BULL WITH A BACKBODY DROP!!! Their dropping out like flies right now Dave.
DG: Don't talk to me, I'm sulking. RD: Carolina Crusher and Tony Flint have finally found each other, AND FLINT HAS CRUSHER SET UP FOR THE PLAYBOY'S DREAM!! NO!!! BACKBODY DROP BY CRUSHER! FLINT STANDS UP, AND JESSICA JUST THREW HIM HER PURSE!!! FLINT NAILS CRUSHER OVER THE HEAD WITH IT AND THROWS CRUSHER OVER THE TOP ROPE!!!!
DG: Another one bites the dust. How many are left? RD: We're down to ten. Flint, Titanacus, Hardcore, Flashmaster, Humiliator, Dark Ninja, Dark Death, Fabio Erotic, Jackhammer, and Scoult.
DG: Flashmaster trying to throw Titanacus over the top. You can tell he's new here. He can't even lift him up. RD: Meanwhile, Flint and Hardcore are having quite a battle. Hardcore with a DDT!!! He goes up to the 2nd turnbuckle and jumps off with an elbowdrop, but FLINT ROLLS AWAY!! HE WAS PLAYING POSSUM! HE GRABS HARDCORE AND THROWS HIM OVER THE TOP ROPE!!
WAIT!!! HARDCORE LANDED ON THE APRON SO HE'S STILL IN. DG: NOT ANYMORE. FLINT SAW HIM AND A CLOTHESLINE SENDS HIM DOWN TO THE FLOOR. HARDCORE HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!! DG: The Humiliator and Dark Ninja continue to do battle. Humiliator send him for the ride and catches him coming off with a backdrop!! He whips him into the ropes again and executes a spinebuster slam!! HUMILIATION!!!! NO ONE HAS EVER GOTTEN UP FROM THAT MOVE!!! HUMILIATOR PICKS UP NINJA AND TOSSES HIM OVER. . .NO! NINJA HOOKS HIS LEG IN THE ROPE AND REMAINS IN THE BATTLE ROYAL!
RD: Flashmaster and Fabio Erotic aren't waiting until Saturday to make acquaintances! Flashmaster sends Fabio in for the ride, and FABIO COMES OFF WITH A FLYING LARIAT!!! FLASHMASTER DUCKS UNDER IT AND USES FABIO'S MOMENTUM TO TURN IT INTO A BACKDROP, AND FABIO EROTIC IS ELIMINATED!!!!!
DG: Eight wrestlers now with a chance at the belt. Remember, the runner-up spot is also important for it decides who will face the champ at Triangles of Terror. Dark Death and Titanacus are forming a strange alliance as they hit the Flashmaster with a double powerbomb!!!! Flashmaster is on the mat in pain, and Death drives his knee into the small of the back. Dark Death is going up top for the Guillotine!!!
LOOK!! MS. JESSICA, FLASHMASTER'S MANAGER, JUST SHOVED DEATH OFF THE TURNBUCKLE!!! Lucky for him he fell into the ring. RD: The Humiliator and Dark Ninja have found each other again, and this time Scoult is helping Ninja out. The two fan favorites bounce off the ropes and hit Humiliator with diving headbutts!!! Superkick from Ninja!!! Scoult drags Humiliator to his feet and leaves him for Ninja, who bounces off the ropes with a cross bodypress!!!!
HUMILIATOR CAUGHT HIM!! I DON'T KNOW WHERE HE FOUND THE ENERGY, BUT HE GOES OVER TO THE ROPES AND DUMPS DARK NINJA TOTHE FLOOR!!! DARK NINJA IS GONE!!
DG: Now Jackhammer J.P. Steele and Tony Flint are doing battle. These two have to be considered among the favorites to win. Flint with a snap suplex on Jackhammer, and he follows it up with a knee to the back!!! JESSICA IS UP ON THE APRON, AND SHE'S GETTING IN THE RING!!!! FLINT HOLDS ON TO JACKHAMMER, AND JESSICA SWINGS HER PURSE AT HIM!! STEELE DUCKS OUT OF THE WAY!!!! JESSICA HIT FLINT FLUSH IN THE FACE!!! SHE LOOKS TERRIFIED!!! STEELE ESCORTS HER OUT OF THE RING THE HARD WAY!!
OVER THE TOP ROPE!!! JESSICA HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!!! RD: That ought to teach her. Steele takes advantage of the situation with a German suplex!!! STEELE JUMPS ON THE TOP ROPE AND SPRINGS OFF WITH HIS SPRINGBOARD SPINWHEEL KICK!!! FLINT GOES DOWN HARD!!
DG: Risky move by Steele. If he lost his balance, he could have very easily fallen over the top rope and to the floor. Here comes the Humiliator to help Flint out.
This is where it pays to be in a stable like the Palace of Power. HE COMES RUNNING AT STEELE WITH A CLOTHESLINE, BUT HE DUCKS UNDER IT AND BACKDROPS HUMILIATOR TO THE FLOOR!!!! FLINT IS ON HIS OWN NOW!!!!
RD: We are down to six: Dark Death, Jackhammer J.P. Steele, The Flashmaster, Titanacus, Scoult, andPlayboy Tony Flint. Death is really taking it to the big man as he nails Titanacus with a spinning leg lariat!! He bounces off the ropes and hits a clothesline!! Death is climbing the ropes once again, AND HE COMES OFF WITH THE GUILLOTINE!!!!!! THROW HIM OUT DEATH!
DG: Before he even has the chance, Tony Flint catches him with a clothesline!! Flint nails him with a kick to the midsection, and he follows it up by dropping the elbow. Atomic drop by Flint AND DEATH GOES OUT TO THE FLOOR!!!
RD: But he went through the ropes Dave, not over the top, so Dark Death is still in it. Scoult meanwhile has picked up where Dark Death left off with Titanacus.
Scoult with a cross corner whip, and he follows him in shoulder first!!! Titanacus staggers out of the corner, only to be taken down by a legsweep!!! Now its Scoult's turn to head to the top! He comes off with a MOONSAULT!!!! BUT TITANACUS STEPS AWAY FROM IT!! SCOULT HITS THE MAT HARD!!
DG: And Titanacus steps out of trouble, but walks right into a SPRINGBOARD SPINWHEEL KICK FROM JACKHAMMER J.P. STEELE!!!!!! TITANACUS IS REELING CLOSE TO THE ROPES!!!
STEELE IS TRYING TO THROW HIM OUT!! HE CAN'T GET THE BIG GUY UP!! TITANACUS IS STILL ALIVE!
RD: Dark Death is now taking a crack at the big guy again!! Tony Flint from behind with a dropkick, and Death flies into Titanacus!! What a surprise, Tony Flint hitting someone with his back turned. The big guy catches him and start grinning! I don't like this one bit! POWERBOMB BY TITANACUS!! He follows it up with a side suplex and now a big boot to the face!!! TITANACUS TRYING TO THROW DARK DEATH OUT!!!! YES, HE GOT HIM!!! DEATH GOES OVER THE TOP ROPE AND DOWN TO THE FLOOR!!!
DG: The Flashmaster and Playboy Tony Flint are throwing punches right in front of us. MS. JESSICA IS UP ON THE APRON TO INTERFERE!!! NOT THIS TIME, AS JESSICA PULLS HER DOWN OFF. THE TWO JESSICAS ARE HAVING A STAREDOWN ON THE OUTSIDE!!!
RD: Inside the ring, Flashmaster has taken advantage of the mayhem by nailing Flint with a double underhook piledriver!!! JESSICA IS UP ON THE APRON, AND NOW MS.
JESSICA RETURNS THE FAVOR BY PULLING HER DOWN!!! JESSICA JUST SLAPPED MS. JESSICA, AND WE HAVE A CATFIGHT!!!!! SECURITY GUARDS FINALLY BREAK IT UP!!!
DG: What a night!! Flashmaster places Flint on the turnbuckle for a superplex!!!! Flint doesn't like the idea, so he throws Flashmaster off the top rope. FLINT COMES FLYING OFF WITH A FLYING NECK SCISSORS!!! PLAYBOY'S DREAM!!! WELCOME TO TCW, MR.
FLASHMASTER!! RD: Flint doesn't have a chance to throw Flashmaster out of the ring before Jackhammer grabs him and tries to throw him out!! FLINT IS ON THE VERGE!! HE HAS HIS LEG HOOKED TOO MUCH ON THE ROPES SO THE PLAYBOY IS STILL ALIVE!!!
DG: Flint able to get his feet on the ground, AND HE GRABS STEELE AND THROWS HIM OVER WITH A QUICK TOSS!! GREAT MOVE BY FLINT AS HE GRABBED STEELE'S TIGHTS, FELL BACKWARDS BETWEEN THE ROPES, AND BROUGHT STEELE WITH HIM, ONLY OVER THE TOP!!
RD: We are down to the final four!! Scoult, Tony Flint, Titanacus, and the Flashmaster!! Flashmaster is making an impressive debut in TCW!! Flint is gloating over his manuever to get Steele out, and SCOULT COMES UP AND SLAPS HIM!! FLINT IS PISSED, AND HE LAYS INTO SCOULT!!!!
DG: LOOK OUT, TONY!!! FLASHMASTER FROM BEHIND GRABS FLINT AND THROWS HIM OUT OF THE RING!!! LOOK AT MS. JESSICA LAUGHING!!!
RD: The Palace of Power has now officially been eliminated! Scoult and Flashmaster are talking while Titanacus is looking on. It seems like the two of them are going to make some sort of temporary alliance here to try to get the big man out. The two go towards Titanacus with Scoult in the lead. FLASHMASTER FROM BEHIND HITS SCOULT WITH A CLOTHESLINE!!! HE GRABS SCOULT BY THE HAIR, SCOOPS HIM UP, AND DUMPS HIM OVER THE TOP ROPE!!!! WE ARE DOWN TO TWO!!
DG: Smart thinking by the Flashmaster. I think I'm starting to like him. He's now insured himself of a match at Triangles of Terror. Flashmaster and Titanacus start circling each other. It's down to the two of them to decide the North American Champion!!
RD: Flashmaster spins and hits the big guy with an enzuigiri!! The big guy is down!!! Flashmaster bounces off the ropes and hits him with a diving headbutt!! He brings the big guy to his feet. KEEP HIM ON THE GROUND!!
DG: Umm, how is he supposed to throw him over the top if he's on the ground. That would be a good strategy if this were a regular match, but it's not. Flashmaster whips Titanacus into the turnbuckle and follows him in to the corner shoulder-first!!! NO ONE HOME!! That move never works.
RD: TITANACUS PLACES FLASHMASTER UP ON THE TOP ROPE AND ROARS AT THE CROWD!!! THESE TWO HAVE BEEN WRESTLING FOR ALMOST AN HOUR! THE BIG GUY GOES UP AFTER HIM!! MOSCOW SPIKE!!!!!!! THAT IS ONE OF THE DEADLIEST MOVES IN THE HISTORY OF WRESTLING!!
DG: It's only matter of time now, Ross. That move took everything that the Flashmaster had left. Titanacus whips him into the ropes and nails him with a belly-to-belly suplex!!! He's going for a powerbomb. No!! Flashmaster shows he still has some fight by taking the big guy to the mat with a football tackle!!!
Flashmaster somehow gets back to his feet and drags Titanacus to his feet. Irish whip, but a reversal by Titanacus. SPINEBUSTER BY TITANACUS!!! HE PICKS UP FLASHMASTER AND, YES!, HE THROWS HIM OVER THE TOP TO THE FLOOR!! TITANACUS IS THE FIRST TCW NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION!!!!!!!!!!!!!
AF: The winner of this match, and NEW!!! TCW NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION, TITANACUS!!!!!!! (Loud boos are heard from throughout the arena, as the big Russian holds his belt aloft.) RD: So Titanacus has the belt, and he'll get his first challenge at Triangles of Terror from the Flashmaster. Plus, we have new tag team champions, Southern Thunder, who dethroned the Dallas Connection. Made for a great night of wrestling didn't it Dave.
DG: It did indeed. TCW has arrived, and soon will take its place among the elite. I'm looking forward to Saturday with a bunch of debuts. Flashmaster vs. Fabio Erotic and Hardcore vs. Playboy Tony Flint.
RD: Don't forget about P.O. Militia vs. The Samurai Warriors and Dr. Doom and Brandon Charm vs. Jack the Ripper and his partner with a title shot on the line for Jack. Plus more!! So, for Dave Gregor, I'm Thomas Chernin saying see ya Saturday!
(Highlights of tonight's matches are shown as Bob Dylan's "The Times They Are A-changing" plays. We see Michael Traverson hit the Thunder Bolt on James Harper on the concrete floor, Southern Thunder celebrating with their newly won belts, Carolina Crusher giving Scorpion the DDT on the floor before the start of the battle royal, Titanacus hitting Brandon Charm with the clothesline eliminating Charm, Ms. Jessica and Jessica in a catfight, and, finally, Titanacus holding his belt on his shoulder as he roars at the crowd.)
