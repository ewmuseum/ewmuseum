TCW: Tuesday Night Extreme (11/19/96)

19 November 1996 &mdash; TCW Pyramid, Harrisburg, PA
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
("Today" by Smashing Pumpkins plays as pictures of the 18 men entered in the North American Title battle royal are shown. As each are shown, they fade to the background, eventually forming a circle around the TCW North American Title belt.
As the music fades, the fans chant of TCW! TCW! TCW! TCW! reaches deafening levels. The camera shifts to show Dave Gregor and Ross Douglas at the announcer's table.) RD: YOU ARE LOOKING LIVE AT THE INTERIOR OF THE TCW PYRAMID, AS WE ARE BACK HOME FOR TUESDAY NIGHT EXXXTRRRREEEEEMME!!!!
DG: Don't you think you're going a little overboard there, Ross. You really sound like a dork when you do that.
RD: I'm just excited about possibly the biggest card in TCW history! For the first time, two, count 'em, two titles will be on the line. First, tag team champions the Dallas Connection, defend the belt they won seven days ago in this very ring against Southern Thunder.
DG: Then, the big battle royal, featuring eighteen top TCW superstars doing in an all-out war to crown the first TCW North American Champion.
RD: Thomas Chernin was able to get back in the champs locker room for this interview. (cut to Dallas Connection's locker room) [Camera opens and the Dallas Connection are in the POP locker room getting ready for their match]
John: So James, whad'ya know about Southern Blunder? James: Hold on there big man. Remember they won last Tuesday, that's how they get to fight us. They may not be that bad.
John: C'mon James, we can't give them that much credit. They come in here and win one match and suddenly they have a shot at the title? You know, we worked real hard to get here and we won't let this beautiful gold go that easy.
James: Again, you're right. SOUTHERN BLUNDER, I HOPE YOU'RE READY, CAUSE HERE WE COME!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
John: Let's go take care of some business. [camera fades as the Dallas Connection heads toward the exit of the locker room area] (back to Dave and Ross) RD: They look ready to go and so are we, so let's take it up to Arnie Foradori. AF: This match is for the TCW WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP. It is set for one fall with a 30 minute time limit. Introducing first, the challengers. . .
(The sound of thunder is heard over the loudspeakers.) Led to the ring by their manager, Cajun' James Lefevbre, at total combined weight of 542 lbs, MICHAEL TRAVERSON AND JOHNATHAN VAN VLIET: SOUTHERN THUNDER!!!!!!!!!
(The crowd gives a huge pop to the talented duo.) And their opponents, the TCW World Tag Team Champions, from Dallas, TX. . . ("Hold the Dream" by Firehouse begins to play and the Dallas Connection walk out to a loud 50/50 mix of boos and cheers)
They are members of the Palace of Power. Led to the ring by Jessica, here are: JOHN AND JAMES HARPER, TOGETHER KNOWN AS THE DALLAS CONNECTION!!!!!!!!
DG: Jessica should have her boys pumped up for their first title defense. This should be a great match as I was impressed with Southern Thunder last week.
RD: As was I, Dave. A battle of the unbeatens here. Here we go!!!!! John Harper and Michael Traverson in to start, circling around each other feeling each other out.
DG: This is a sign of mutual respect. Neither man wants to do anything stupid right away. We finally get our first lockup, and Traverson wins this one with a gutwrench suplex!!! He sends Harper for the ride, leapfrog, and Harper comes off a second time to a dropkick to the knee! Backslide by Traverson: one, easy kickout by Harper.
RD: Traverson tried to catch Harper off guard with that one, and it didn't work. I think Jessica gets her boys too prepared for a match to let that happen. DG: HE CAUGHT HIM OFF GUARD THERE! POWERBOMB BY TRAVERSON!!! COVER: ONE, TWO, KICKOUT BY HARPER. We almost had new champions in an early pinfall. Traverson brings the champ and bounces off the ropes. Harper catches him with a big right hand right to the bridge of the nose. That turns the momentum around pretty quick.
RD: Harper follows it up with a big forearm smash, and now there's that power by John Harper, as he lift Traverson way up over his head for a Gorilla Press. John tags out to his brother, and the champs start to double-team. They send Traverson in for the ride and try to take his head off with a double clothesline. Another Irish Whip, and THEY TELEGRAPH A DOUBLE BACKDROP. TRAVERSON TAKES ADVANTAGE WITH A DOUBLE NOGGIN'
KNOCKER!! DG: Come on guys, don't do that crap. Traverson now sends James into the ropes and catches him coming off with a powerslam!!! He's ready to take it to the outside now as he throughs Harper out of the ring. Harper goes over to talk tot Jessica.
RD: TRAVERSON IS OUT AFTER HIM, BUT HARPER DOESN'T SEE HIM. HE TAPS HIM ON THE SHOULDER. HARPER TURNS AROUND RIGHT INTO A POWERBOMB!!!!!!! TRAVERSON WITH THE SPLASH TO COMPLETE THE THUNDER BOLT!!!! I THINK HARPER'S OUT COLD.
DG: Looks like the champs made the first mistake. Traverson brings Harper up by his hair, and sends him back down face first on the concrete. No mats in TCW!!
RD: TRAVERSON HAS HARPER ON A TABLE, AND THE FANS ARE ON THEIR FEET!! HE DROPS THE ELBOW, BUT HARPER MOVES!!!! Lucky for Traverson the table didn't break!
DG: James Harper is showing he has the heart of a champion with a kick to the midsection. He follows it up with a forearm to the small of the back and throws Traverson back into the ring. James somehow finds the energy to tag out to his brother.
RD: John immediately puts Traverson in a sleeperhold. Finally, he has enough of the stupid resthold and whips Traverson into the corner. Harper charges in after him, but Traverson get his leg up!! Traverson makes the tag to Johnathan Van Vliet!!!!
DG: You act like he's the savior or something. He's a 217 lb weakling. RD: Johnathan Van Vliet is one of the best high-fliers in wrestling. He proves it here with a jumping side kick. Van Vliet jumps up and wraps his legs around Harper's neck for a neck scissors takedown!!! Traverson comes back in the ring and its doubleteam time: Southern Thunder style.
DG: Southern Thunder whips Harper into the ropes and drill him with a double roundhouse right! Another Irish whip and another double roundhouse right!! Tink Smith FINALLY makes Traverson leave the ring. Side headlock by Van Vliet, but Harper counters it with a back suplex!!! He sends the shrimp for a ride and drills him with a spinebuster slam!!!! Cover: one, two, shoulder up by Van Vliet.
RD: That was a close one for John Harper, who has been in the ring since the second minute of this match! Harper drags Van Vliet over to the corner and scales the ropes.
The big guy fall backwards with a reverse flying elbowdrop!!! Bad move on the champ's part as he doesn't capitalize with a cover. DG: He might have gotten him if he had gone for the cover, but we'll never know. Harper whips the runt into the ropes, but Van Vliet comes off with a flying forearm!!!! ASAI MOONSAULT!!!!! ONE, TWO, THR. . .KICKOUT BY HARPER!!!! Van Vliet goes to the outside and REENTERS WITH A SLINGSHOT SPLASH!!! COVER: ONE, TWO, HARPER JUST GETS HIS SHOULDER UP!! Ok, Van Vliet has made me a believer.
RD: I told you he would. James Harper has seen enough. He comes in and drills Van Vliet with a clothesline to the back of the head! Irish whip by the champs and a double clothesline!! Another ride into the ropes for Van Vliet; this time he's greeted by a double fist to the midsection!
DG: The champs have regained control of this match, even though John's wrestled practically the whole thing. Another sleeper by Harper, but Van Vliet counters it with a jawbreaker!!! John looks angry as he charges after the cruiserweight, but Van Vliet dodges him and makes the tag!!!!
RD: Harper's caught in Southern Thunder's corner, and they hit him with a double roundhouse right! Traverson with a crucifix! One, two, thr. . . no! He got his shoulder up!!
DG: That wasn't even close, Ross. Traverson hits him with a reverse neckbreaker and now a cradle!! One, two, thr. . .kickout!! Traverson bringing Harper up to his feet, but Harper puts the thumb in his eye!!! That's the way to wrestle, guys. Cheat, cheat, and, if that doesn't work, cheat.
RD: You're a real jerk, you know that Dave. DG: Thank you. That almost hurts coming from you Ross. Anyway, while Ross was over there giving us our moral lesson for the day, John finally tagged out to his brother.
RD: Whatever. James whips Traverson into the corner, charges in shoulder first, but Traverson moves out of the way!! Harper hit the ring post hard!! Traverson leaves the ring and is handed a frying pan!! He's looking at it and wondering whether or not to use it. The fans are urging him on!!
DG: That's illegal!!! He shouldn't do that. RD: You're such a hypocrite, Dave. DG: Get it thru that thick skull of yours Ross that I don't give a damn what you think about me. Traverson looks like he's going to use the frying pan, and Yes!!!!!
right on top of the head of James Harper. RD: Traverson tags out to Van Vliet who immediately climbs to the top rope. Could we be seeing the Thunder Strike!? Van Vliet starts walking along the top rope!!
Traverson has James and drills him into the mat with a powerbomb!!! YES!!! VAN VLIET COMPLETES THE THUNDER STRIKE BY DROPPING THE LEG FROM THE TOP ROPE ON JAMES HARPER'S THROAT. ONE, TWO, NEW CHAM. . .HE KICKED OUT!!!! I CAN'T BELIEVE HE KICKED OUT.
DG: He has the heart of a champion, I tell you. Van Vliet can't believe it! He tags back out to Traverson who just shrugs and bounces off the ropes. JESSICA TRIPPED HIM. TINK SMITH IS OVER YELLING AT JESSICA!!! TRAVERSON PUTS HARPER IN AN ABDOMINAL STRETCH, AND TINK TURNS AROUND. TINK SMITH HAS CALLED FOR THE BELL!!!! I THINK HE DQ'D THE CHAMPS. HA HA HA!!! THEY'LL GET TO KEEP THE BELTS!!
AF: The winner of this match, as a result of a SUBMISSION, AND NNEEEEEEWWWWWW!!!! TCW WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS, MICHAEL TRAVERSON AND JOHNATHAN VAN VLIET: SOUTHERN THUNDER!!
RD: HE GAVEUP!!! JAMES HARPER GAVE UP, AND WE HAVE NEW CHAMPIONS!!!! WHAT A WAY TO START THE NIGHT!!
DG: This sucks! I can't believe those guys are the tag champs. Hopefully we'll get a better result from the battle royal.
RD: Speaking of the battle royal, let's go up to Arnie for the introductions. (As Arnie introduces the wrestler, each gets in the ring and waits.) AF: This battle royal is for the TCW NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP. The ONLY way to be eliminated is by being thrown over the top rope!! Now, the wrestlers. . .
FABIO EROTIC!!!!!! RD: He's had a rough start thus far in TCW. Tonight would be a great night to turn things around.
BALD BULL!!!!! DG: The newcomer from Istanbul will show his wares in the Pyramid for the first time tonight.
BRANDON CHARM!!!!!!! DG: Yes!!!! Brandon is the man! Need I say more. TITANACUS!!!!!!!! RD: At 7' 5" and 400+ lbs, Titanacus has to be considered a favorite. SCOULT!!!!! RD: A big man with a lot of agility. Two factors that help in a battle royal like this. CHRIS STONEBREAKER!!!! DG: Can Cajun' James Lefevbre leave tonight with two titles in his stable? It's not inconceivable.
PLAYBOY TONY FLINT!!!!!! RD: A member of the Palace of Power, Flint will have friends in there backing him up. DARK DEATH!!!!!! DG: He's made himself know quickly in TCW, but like Erotic and Stonebreaker, has yet to break into the win column.
PONCHO DEMENTO!!!!!!! RD: The newest member of the P.O. Militia, you can bet he'll be after Charm and Titanacus.
MASKED BULLDOG!!!!!!! DG: Maniac Bob already manages the world champ. Is Bulldog fully recovered from his injury yet?
DARK NINJA!!!!!! RD: A big unknown. Looked impressive in his debut on Saturday, but does he have what it takes to defeat 17 men?
JACKHAMMER J.P. STEELE!!!!!!! DG: Don't like him, but he's one of the favorites, coming off the win over Jack the Ripper in the steel cage.
THE FLASHMASTER!!!! RD: Yet another newcomer. He's already in a fued with Fabio Erotic. Look for those two to hook up.
THE HUMILIATOR!!!!!!! DG: Another member of the Palace of Power, he's undefeated thus far in TCW. HARDCORE!!!!!!!!! RD: Hardcore made the mistake of signing a contract with Tony Flint. That could bring the P.O.P. after him tonight.
JACK THE RIPPER!!!!!!! DG: He started off strong, but he's been in a slump recently. THE SCORPION!!!!!!! RD: Another member of the Palace. He's coming off a big win over the Carolina Crusher on Satur. . .LOOK! OUT OF NOWHERE CAROLINA CRUSHER RUNS UP AND NAILS SCORPION WITH A STEEL CHAIR FROM BEHIND!!! CRUSHER GRABS SCORP AND HITS HIM WITH A DDT ON THE CONCRETE FLOOR!!!!!!!!
DG: It looks like Scorp isn't going to make it to the ring. Crusher runs to the ring, there's the bell, and Scorpion is officially eliminated!!!!!
RD: That takes us down to 17 already. Crusher runs into the ring right into the arms of Dark Ninja!! Scoop and a slam by Ninja, and he's going to the top rope!! Not a smart move taking into account the rules of this match. Ninja comes off the top WITH A MOONSAULT BUT CRUSHER DUCKS UNDER IT!!!!!! Crusher scoops up Ninja and throws him to the mat with a bodyslam! Crusher rakes him across the face.
