TCW To Hold First PPV Dec. 1

14 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Total Championship Wrestling has announced it will be holding its first pay-per-view on Sunday, Dec. 1, from the Bryce Jordan Center in State College, PA.
The PPV is titled Triangles of Terror. As the name implies, the showcase event will be a triangle match tournament. The top nine singles wrestlers will do battle in three one-fall type triangle matches, with the winners advancing to the final triangle match, which will be elimination style. Every match in the tournament will be a Spin the Wheel, Make the Deal gimmick match. Also, all titles, the World, North American, and Tag, will be defended on the card. This fed just crowned its World and Tag champions, with the North American Title to be determined in a battle royal next Tuesday. There is still time to enter the battle royal, so join the next great efed, TCW, now by emailing me at [detail withheld] for a character creation sheet.
