TCW: Triangles of Terror

1 December 1996 &mdash; Bryce Jordan Center, State College, PA

DG: Flashmaster has not backed down at all. He bounces off the ropes and hits the North American champ with a splash! Flashmaster hooks the leg: one, two, kickout again.
RD: Flashmaster bounces off the ropes again and TITANACUS CATCHES HIM! CHOKESLAM BY THE BIG RUSSIAN!!! Titanacus roars at the crowd!
DG: It's like all that offense by the Flashmaster didn't even phase him. He sends the challenger for the ride and catches him with a spinebuster!!! One, two, Flashmaster gets his shoulder up!
RD: He drags Flashmaster to his feet and executes a powerful snap suplex! Irish whip, and Titanacus just follows him to the ropes, hits him with a clothesline, and knocks him out of the ring!
Titanacus turns and roars at the crowd and Dennis Arnold begins his count. DG: Flashmaster somehow gets back in at the count of two and runs at Titanacus. ANOTHER SPINEBUSTER! ONE, TWO, ANOTHER KICKOUT! RD: Titanacus goes to the top turnbuckle. Flashmaster struggles to his feet and is looking for the champion. FLYING CLOTHESLINE!! ONE, TWO, FLASHMASTER PUTS HIS FOOT ON THE BOTTOM ROPE!!!
DG: Titanacus stands up. MS. JESSICA SLIPPED FLASHMASTER HER CAN OF HAIRSPRAY. TITANACUS TURNS JUST IN TIME AND DUCKS UNDER IT!!! HE NAILS FLASHMASTER WITH A BIG RIGHT HAND AND TAKES THE HAIRSPRAY! HE SPRAYS THE FLASHMASTER IN THE EYES WITH THE HAIRSPRAY!!!
POWERBOMB BY TITANACUS!! RD: Flashmaster was able to recover from one mistake, but I don't know about a second. TITANACUS PUTS THE FLASHMASTER ON THE TURNBUCKLE AND GIVES THE SIGN FOR THE MOSCOW SPIKE!!!! HE GOES UP WITH THE CHALLENGER!!! THERE IT IS!!!! THAT WILL DO IT: ONE, TWO, THREE!!!!
TITANACUS HAS RETAINED HIS TITLE!!!! AF: The winner of this match, AND STILL TCW NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION, TITANACUS!!!!!!! DG: Titanacus remains undefeated in TCW. RD: That was his first singles match! DG: Every match he's been in, he's had his hand raised at the end. We're ready for what I think will be the match of the night, if not the match of the year. Southern Thunder vs. the Dallas Connection.
Dallas Connection won the titles at the World Tag Team Title tournament, a tournament which Southern Thunder did not enter. One week later, the two teams met with the belts on the line.
(Flashback to November 19 at Tuesday Night Extreme.) RD: Traverson tags out to Van Vliet who immediately climbs to the top rope. Could we be seeing the Thunder Strike!? Van Vliet starts walking along the top rope!! Traverson has James and drills him into the mat with a powerbomb!!! YES!!! VAN VLIET COMPLETES THE THUNDER STRIKE BY DROPPING THE LEG FROM THE TOP ROPE ON JAMES HARPER'S THROAT. ONE, TWO, NEW CHAM. . .HE KICKED OUT!!!! I CAN'T BELIEVE HE KICKED OUT.
DG: He has the heart of a champion, I tell you. Van Vliet can't believe it! He tags back out to Traverson who just shrugs and bounces off the ropes. JESSICA TRIPPED HIM. TINK SMITH IS OVER YELLING AT JESSICA!!!
TRAVERSON PUTS HARPER IN AN ABDOMINAL STRETCH, AND TINK TURNS AROUND. TINK SMITH HAS CALLED FOR THE BELL!!!! I THINK HE DQ'D THE CHAMPS. HA HA HA!!! THEY'LL GET TO KEEP THE BELTS!!
AF: The winner of this match, as a result of a SUBMISSION, AND NNEEEEEEWWWWWW!!!! TCW WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS, MICHAEL TRAVERSON AND JOHNATHAN VAN VLIET: SOUTHERN THUNDER!!
(Back to Triangles of Terror.) RD: If the two teams can top that match, it will be a sight to behold. President Dan Fisher immediately ordered a rematch, and that will take place in a few minutes.
DG: That's one of the few good decisions Fisher has made. We're ready to begin. Take it away, Arnie. AF: This match is scheduled for one fall with a thirty minute time limit. It is for the TCW WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP!!! Introducing first, the challengers. Led down the aisle by Jessica, from Dallas, Texas at a combined weight of 700 lbs, they are former tag team champions and are members of the Palace of Power. Ladies and gentlemen, JOHN AND JAMES HARPER, THE DALLAS CONNECTION!!!!!!!
("Hold the Dream" by Firehouse plays as the challengers make their way to ringside. For the Dallas Connection, the crowd mostly boos.) AF: And their opponents. . . (The sound of thunder is heard through the arena, followed closely by the roar of the crowd. The champions emerge from behind the curtain and the crowd erupts.)
AF: Accompanied by their manager, Cajun' James Lefevbre, at a total combined weight of 542 lbs, they are the current TCW WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS: MICHAEL TRAVERSON, JOHNATHAN VAN VLIET, together known as SOUTHERN THUNDER!!!!!!!!!!!
RD: All right!! Let's get this party started! DG: Oh, no. RD: What? DG: Look who the ref is. RD: Tink Smith, what about him? DG: Couldn't President Fisher find anyone with half a brain. I'm not even asking for a whole one. Tink's the kind of guy who shouldn't be allowed to have kids. He's bad enough. His kids are probably deformed.
RD: Quit it. Concentrate on calling the match. John Harper meets Traverson as soon as he steps into the ring, and we're under way!! Harper with a Irish whip, but Traverson reverses it. Leapfrog by Traverson, and Harper bounces off the ropes and runs into a kick! Inside cradle by Traverson: one, two, kickout by Harper.
DG: He didn't honestly think he'd get him already, did he? Wristlock by Traverson, and Harper powers out of it! He grabs Traverson for the ride and sends him over the top!
RD: TRAVERSON GRABBED ON TO THE ROPE AND IS PULLING HIMSELF IN! JESSICA SCREAMS AT JOHN, WHO TURNS AROUND AND DRILLS HIM WITH A LARIAT! He makes the tag to James.
DG: James runs into the ropes, and Traverson bounces off the opposite ropes. Traverson hits him with a flying clothesline!! He sends Harper in for the ride and catches him coming off with a high backdrop!
RD: Traverson is holding his side and grimacing in pain! I think he might have strained a muscle. John is yelling something to James! I think he's pointing out the injury! Big boot to the midsection by James, AND HE SLAPS ON AN ABDOMINAL STRETCH!!!! TRAVERSON IS IN A LOT OF PAIN, I DON'T KNOW IF HE CAN HOLD ON!
DG: They're right smack in the middle of the ring. Van Vliet is trying to get the crowd to help out Traverson.
(The crowd starts up a loud Thunder! Thunder! Thunder! chant.) RD: TRAVERSON TRYING TO HIPTOSS HIS WAY OUT OF IT! HARPER HOLDS ON TO THE TIGHTS, THOUGH! VAN VLIET HAS SEEN ENOUGH,AND HE'S CHARGING THE RING! JOHN IS THERE TO MEET HIM AND THROW HIM OUT!
DG: Traverson's been in the abdominal stretch for over a minute now! TINK SMITH HAS CALLED FOR THE BELL! WE HAVE NEW CHAMPIONS!!!!!
AF: The winners of this match, and NEW! TCW WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS: JOHN AND JAMES HARPER: THE DALLAS CONNECTION!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
RD: Too bad Traverson got injured. I was really looking forward to a five star match. DG: Well, we got a five star result! The entire Palace of Power is out to greet the new champs. High fives all around!
RD: They finally leave the champs to themselves, and Leon and Jessica lead them to the locker room. TWO GUYS JUMP OUT OF THE CROWD AND ATTACK THE CHAMPS FROM BEHIND! REVERSE DDT ON JAMES!
REVERSE DDT ON JOHN!!! THE ASSAILANTS JUMP BACK INTO THE CROWD ANDTAKE OFF! WHO WERE THOSE GUYS?! THEY WERE WEARING WRESTLING TIGHTS!
DG: One guy had Agony written on his tights. I think the guy that attacked John had Anguish on his tights. The champs are back up and looking for them. They're long gone by now.
RD: I don't think anything can put a damper on tonight for the new champs. It's time for the Triangles of Terror finals.
Once again back to Thomas at the wheel. (Cut to Thomas and Dan in the back by the wheel, now with a dent in it thanks to Carolina Crusher's head.)
TC: You had something you wanted to say concerning the tag situation, President Fisher. DF: Thank you, Thomas. Since an obvious fluke injury cost Southern Thunder the belts, I have decided that the Dallas Connection must defend them against Southern Thunder one last time. Next Tuesday, at Tuesday Night Extreme, Southern Thunder and Dallas Connection will fight once again. This time, no flukes. The match will be best of three falls. Now for the Triangles of Terror final.
(Fisher gives the wheel one final spin.) TC: The Humiliator, Playboy Tony Flint, and the Grim Reaper will wrestle in a . . .BASEBALL BAT MATCH!!!! A baseball bat will be in the ring, and it is fair game for anyone that can get to it.
(Cut back to Ross and Dave.) DG: Thank you Thomas. I don't know who will win this final, but I do know who won't. . .The Grim Reaper. He's wrestling a handicap match against two members of the Palace of Power.
RD: Good point Dave. I wouldn't count the Reaper out though. He's undefeated. Up to you Arnie.
AF: This match is the FINAL OF THE TRIANGLES OF TERROR TOURNAMENT! It is an elimination BASEBALL BAT MATCH and has NO TIME LIMIT!!! Introducing first, from Your Deepest Nightmarres, weighing 277 lbs. . .THE HUMILIATOR!!!
(Rey Misterio's music plays as the Humiliator walks with a purpose to the ring. Noticable by her abscence is Jessica. The crowd gives him a loud chorus of boos.) RD: No Jessica. She must be sitting this one out since two of her men are in it. DG: I bet Humiliator let her come down with Flint. They are the two that started the Palace of Power.
AF: Next, he's a member of the Palace of Power. (As Tony Flint's music starts, the fans erupt in a loud mixture of boos and cheers. The fans love to hate him.) AF: Led to the ring by Jessica, he's from Beverly Hills, California and weighs 285 lbs. He is. . .PLAYBOY TONY FLINT!!!!
DG: Looks like I'm right again. Flint and Humiliator shake hands, and Jessica puts her arm around the Humiliator to make him know that he's still a member.
AF: Finally, from Parts Unknown, weighing 390 lbs. . .THE GRIM REAPER!!!!!!!!!! ("Don't Fear the Reaper" by Blue Oyster Cult blares from the loudspeaker as the Reaper slowly makes his way to the ring.)
RD: The winner of this match gets a world title shot at the PPV in the CoreStates Center on January 7. It's the Reaper and the Humiliator to start. Reaper hits him with an elbowsmash!! Cross-corner whip, and Reaper follows him in, but Humiliator lifts his knee.
DG: They never learn, do they? THAT. . .MOVE. . .DOES. . .NOT. . .WORK!!!!! RD: Humiliator throws the Reaper outside the ring and goes out after him. We just have a huge brawl on the outside, which the Reaper is winning. Earringer by the Reaper!!! He throws the Humiliator into the guardrail!!! Reaper has the bat!!! HE TAKES A SWING AND CONNECTS WITH THE HUMILITORS HEAD!!! THE HUMILIATOR IS BUSTED OPEN!!!!
DG: Jessica is sneaking up on the Reaper from behind. THE GRIM REAPER SEES HER!!! HE THROWS THE BAT DOWN AND STALKS AFTER HER!!! THE HUMILIATOR PICKS UP THE BAT AND CHOKES THE REAPER!! JESSICA DID HER JOB!
RD: Humiliator rolls the Reaper into the ring and tags out to Tony Flint. Flint comes in and takes the Reaper down with a neck-scissors!!!! Humiliator tosses him the bat, and Flint starts choking the man from the Dark Side.
DG: PARTS UNKNOWN!!! RD: WHO CARES!!! NEITHER PLACE EXISTS!!! DG: Sure Ross. Next you're going to tell me there's no such thing as Santa Claus. RD: Why me? Flint punishes the Reaper even further with a jawbreaker!! Irish whip, and he catches the Reaper with a powerslam!! He backs up to measure the Reaper, who is slowly getting to his feet.
Clothesline by Flint, and the big man is hurt! An enzuigiri knocks him down to the mat! No cover though. Instead we get a strut and a "Woo Hoo!"
DG: Irish whip by Flint and a back body drop, WHICH THE REAPER TURNS INTO A SUNSET FLIP! ONE, TWO, SHOULDER UP BY FLINT! The Playboy is not happy!! Flint grabs him and puts him into a side headlock.
He runs and turns it into a bulldog!!! Dropkick by Flint! RD: He's going to the top turnbuckle. The Reaper is back on his feet!! FLYING CROSS BODY PRESS!!!! ONE, TWO, THR. . .KICKOUT BY THE REAPER!! Flint thought he had him. He's going to the top again! This time the Grim Reaper isn't getting up! FROG SPLASH BY FLINT!!!! DENNIS ARNOLD COUNTS: ONE, TWO, KICKOUT!
DG: I think the end is near for the Reaper. Belly-to-back suplex by Flint!!! He drops the elbow on the Reaper and grabs the bat! Smashes it into the Reaper's face!!! Flint goes for the cover, HE PUTS HIS FEET ON THE ROPES: ONE, TWO, THREE!!!!!!!! THE GRIM REAPER IS ELIMINATED!!! ARNOLD NEVER SAW FLINT PUT HIS FEET ON THE ROPES FOR LEVERAGE!
RD: We are down to the two member of the Palace of Power! Flint grabs the bat, and he's going after the Humiliator.
WAIT! WHAT'S THIS!! Jessica has entered the ring and is in between Flint and the Humiliator. Flint is telling her to move, but she doesn't want him to hit the Humiliator with the bat! JESSICA JUST KISSED THE HUMILIATOR!!
FLINT IS PISSED OFF NOW!!! JESSICA STARTS TO GO OUT OF THE RING!!! SHE STOPS AND DRILLS THE HUMILIATOR FROM BEHIND WITH HER PURSE! IT WAS A SETUP!!!
DG: And a good one at that! HUMILIATOR FALLS FORWARD RIGHT INTO THE SWING OF PLAYBOY TONY FLINT! DID YOU HEAR THAT CRACK!!! I THINK THE HUMILIATOR IS OUT COLD!!! FLINT DRAGS HIM TO HIS FEET!! PLAYBOY'S DREAM!!!!!!!!! COVER: ONE, TWO, THREE!!!!!!!!
PLAYBOY TONY FLINT IS THE WINNER OF THE TRIANGLES OF TERROR TOURNAMENT!!!! AF: The winner of this match, and the winner of Triangles of Terror. . .PLAYBOY TONY FLINT!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
RD: So Playboy Tony Flint will get a World Title Shot at the January Pay Per View. Tonight, Brandon Charm gets a World Title shot. Let's hear from the challenger. (Cut to prerecorded comments from Brandon Charm.) Daniel Barrett :- So Triangles of Terror is upon us. Flashmasterbates!!! Yeah, I'm talking to you. Are you ready to have your neck broken? It WILL happen... when Titanacus hits you with the Moscow Spike, your career will be over. And Fisher, I know what your plan is. You've set up Brandon & Doom to break up the A.P.C.W.
Connection but it won't happen. I ma... Brandon Charm :- Shut up Daniel, it's my time to talk. Fisher I bet you think you're really smart. Doom and I both knew it would come to this. The two most talented wrestlers in the world finally getting it on. You want the biggest buy rate in wrestling history and to destroy the A.P.C.W. Connection in the process.
Well, It ain't gonna happen! Our goal is still to finish this pathetic little ECW rip off promotion, and to make Dan Fisher the A.P.C.W. official chauffer.
Doom, before now I have stood with you, and no matter what happens tonight I will stand with you again. I will promise you one thing, tonight the best man WILL win. And that's the truth Baby!!
(Scene shifts to Arnie Foradori in the ring.) AF: Ladies and gentlemen, it is time for our main event!!! It is for the TCW WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP and is scheduled for one fall with a thirty minute time limit. Introducing first, the challenger. . .
("Strike It" by Dub War begins as Brandon Charm emerges from the curtain with Daniel Barrett. The crowd gives the loudest chorus of boos of the night. There are however, very audible "Brandon is God!
Brandon is God!" chants.) AF: He weighs in at 260 lbs, from Liverpool, England. . .BRANDON CHARM!!!!!!! RD: Brandon won this title match by teaming with Dr. Doom to defeat Jack the Ripper in a handicap match.
AF: His opponent is the TCW WORLD CHAMPION!!!! Led down the aisle by Maniac Bob, weighing 350 lbs, from Parts Unknown. . .DR. DOOM!!!!!!!!
("Bad Company" by Bad Company plays as the TCW Champion makes his way to the ring led by Maniac Bob. He receives a loud chorus of boos.)
RD: Do these people like anyone? DG: You can't get cheers around here unless you're Joe Paterno. . .or me. RD: You mean that nutcase named Keno Beezer pulled a gun on you because he LIKES you? Yeah, you are real popular. DG: I thought we weren't going to mention that. Doom and Charm circle each other in the ring. Doom charges in with a double-axehandle chop!! He takes Charm down with a legsweep! Charm gets right back up and goes right back down after a dropkick! DOOM SETS HIM UP FOR THE DOOMSDAY DDT!!!! BUT, CHARM GETS OUT OF IT WITH A LOW BLOW!!!
RD: A cheap shot by Charm. He takes the offensive now with a left hand to the midsection! Cross-corner whip, and CHARM FOLLOWS IT IN WITH A HANDSPRING ELBOW!!!
Impressive agility by Brandon Charm! DG: Charm is the man. Another cross-corner whip, but Doom reverses it! Charm bounces right out and catches Doom by surprise with a clothesline!! Enzuigiri by Charm, and he executes a Northern Lights suplex!!!! One, two, kickout by the champ!!!
RD: Charm drags the champ to his feet and its him with a spin kick!! Jumping elbowthrust by Charm!! Brandon is going to the top rope!!
DG: Watch this, Brandon showed me this one. FLYING SOMERSAULT SPLASH!!!!! Why aren't you covering Brandon?
RD: Charm leaves the ring and takes the microphone from Arnie!! He comes back in and hits Doom with it!! Charm goes to the outside. He's on the apron and re-enters with a slingshot splash!!! Doom is brought back to his feet by the hair and Charm hits him with a short-arm clothesline!! Dr. Doom falls out of the ring.
DG: Brandon has everything under control. HE JUMPS ON THE TOP ROPE, NOT THE TURNBUCKLE, THE ROPE, AND HITS DOOM ON THE OUTSIDE WITH A SPRINGBOARD SOMERSAULT PLANCHA!!!! HE SETS THE CHAMP UP FOR THE BRANDON BOMB!!! HE LIFTS HIM UP, BUT DOOM COUNTERS IT WITH A FRANKENSTEINER!!!!!!!! WHAT A MANUEVER BY THE CHAMPION!!
RD: He's showing agility no one knew he had! Doom has reclaimed command, and he whips Charm into the guardrail! Looks like he's going to whip him into the apron. . .No!
He spins him around and throws him into the guardrail again!!! Clothesline by Doom sends Charm over the guardrail and into the crowd!
DG: Hit the fat guy Brandon, hit the fat guy!!! RD: What has that guy ever done to you? DG: Nothing, there's a hot chick behind him. If he's there, I can't look at her. RD: You're supposed to be watching the match!! Plus, you're married! DG: I can look can't I. RD: I'm not going to bother. It isn't worth it. Doom is waiting for Charm in the ring, and Daniel Barrett helps Brandon back into the ring. Doom scoops up the challenger and drops him across his knee for a backbreaker!!!! POWERBOMB BY DOOM!!!
DG: He's not going for the cover. Oh, here's why. DOOMSDAY DDT!!!! HE CAUGHT BRANDON CHARM IN HIS FINISHER!!!! COVER: ONE, TWO, THR. . .DANIEL BARRETT PUT CHARM'S FOOT ON THE ROPE!!
RD: Jim Rupp is over talking to Barrett. Doom nails Charm with a right hand and now Maniac Bob is arguing with Rupp about Barrett's interference. BARRETT JUST THREW CHARM THE BULLWORKER!!!! HE HITS DOOM WITH IT AND GOES FOR THE PIN!!! ONE,TWO, NEW CHA. .
.KICKOUT BY DR. DOOM!!!!!! DG: Dr. Doom is not going to relinquish the belt easily. Charm sends Doom in for the ride, and BARRETT JUST TRIPPED DR. DOOM!!! JIM RUPP IS WARNING BARRETT WHILE CHARM JUSTS LAUGHS!!
RD: Save the laughing until you make sure you win Charm. Charm hits the champ with an enzuigiri!! A spin kick knocks Doom to the mat! MANIAC BOB IS UP ON THE APRON!!!!
HE STORMS THE RING AND HIT BRANDON CHARM WITH THE BRIEFCASE!!!! JIM RUPP HAS CALLED FOR THE BELL!!!!!
AF: The winner of this match, as a result of a disqualification. . .BRANDON CHARM!!!!! However, the title cannot change hands by disqualification. THEREFORE, STILL TCW WORLD CHAMPION. . . DR. DOOM!!!!!!!
DG: I'm upset that Brandon isn't the champ, but I have to respect Dr. Doom and Maniac Bob. They did what it took to keep the belt.
RD: You respect that? I'm glad I get away from you now. Triangles of Terror is history folks! For Thomas Chernin and Dave Gregor, I'm Ross Douglas saying so long until Saturday when TCW returns to the TCW Pyramid!!!! The main event will be the Scorpion against Titanacus for the North American Title Belt.
(Dave Matthew's "The Best of What's Around" plays as highlights of the Pay Per View play. The screen shows Tony Flint slamming the lid on the coffin with Dark Ninja inside, Brian Haas attacking the Humiliator, the Palace of Power jumping Carolina Crusher, Scorpion slamming Crusher's head against the wheel, Titanacus giving the Flashmaster the Moscow Spike, the Dallas Connection celebrating their second tag team title reign, two mystery men with a sneak attack on the newly crowned champions, and finally, side-by-side in a split screen, Brandon Charm getting his arm raised and Dr.
Doom holding up the TCW World Title.)
