TCW: Update (11/11/96)

11 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
There will be no preview show for the tag tournament. Sorry. Some of you may have noticed that Brandon Charm will be having a special interview tomorrow at Tuesday Night Extreme. His handler checked with me for this, and I agreed that what he wanted to say could only be said at the card.
This will not occur very often. Another update on future cards; all of your wrestlers should have at least one match under his belt after the 11/16 Saturday Night Fever. I assigned the new guys a match to get them started. Any problems contact me.
