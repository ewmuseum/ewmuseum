Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

ICW Online opened on 21 June 2002 describing itself as “a new e-wrestling federation that’s trying to do it right,” fielding, in its own words, “a few veteran e-wrestlers and a couple of new guys,” and hosted on a Cogeco home page rather than a dedicated site.

Its own reply on judging, posted the same day, sets out the standard it meant to hold matches to: scored on match quality and on how far a match furthered a developed storyline, with a good match that did not advance an angle counting against the writer rather than for them.

Readers replied approvingly within days of the launch post, but no roster, no card, and no closing notice survive; whether ICW Online staged a single card under that judging standard is not something its own surviving record answers.
