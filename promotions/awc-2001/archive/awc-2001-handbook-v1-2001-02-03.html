Strategies 3.3. Angles/Match Booking 4.1. Singles Application 4.2. Tag Team Application *** BACKGROUND (Kayfabe) *** Yes, that's right, while RMW and WAO may be "dead," the AWC (American
Wrestling Council) is not dead. RMW and WAO were purchased by the head of
Marquee Entertainment, for an undisclosed sum. Marquee Ent. has always wanted to try their hand at the production of a wrestling show, and with their seemingly endless supply of money, they will get their chance. Expect the same great things you saw from both of these federations to multiply exponentially, as the combined brains of Joshua Orloski and Tyrone Parker, bring their talent to the table, together.
*** BACKGROUND (OCC) *** This happens a lot, new federations pop up, lots of hype, a very large fan base, tremendous wrestlers, but no money. Everyone knows, no matter how great the talent, without money, nothing can happen. Two very bright "indy"
federations out there had this very same problem happen to them. WAO and RMW. Most people know the names of these federations, and know about at least some of the talent they possessed, but now, it's all gone.
Or is it? AWC is on the hunt for talent, and staff. Well, staff more than talent. Many WAO and RMW superstars are returning in the new form of the AWC. But, of course, there could always be some room open for new blood, get in touch with us, and we'll talk business. :)
Rumors have been flying around that AWC will be on over the top, hardcore promotion, but big wigs have started otherwise. In fact, AWC is trying to bring alittle wrestling back to wrestling. Sure, there will be some blood, and a pinch of gore, but wrestling will almost always be the main theme.
*** MEET THE STAFF *** TYRONE PARKER: The "Superstar" has signed a contract with Marquee Entertainment. This man was former President of the WAO and brought many high-caliber stars into the promotion. Expect no less.
JOSHUA ORLOSKI: Small on-screen role, but when he shows his face, expect things to happen. He knows the business, and he knows it isn't about giving the wrestlers what they want, it's all about the fans. If Mr. Orloski is in the house, and the fans want to see something, they will.
PADDY MARTIN: Head commentator of the AWC. He's experienced, bringing twenty years of wrestling knowledge to the table. He knows the sport in and out, and is the master of the mic.
WESLEY WADE: Martin's partner in crime. Wade's resume includes working for the WAO during its stay in the wrestling business. He's quite a handful. JEREMY WEST: AWC Ring Announcer. TAMMY TAYLOR: AWC's backstage correspondant. She's always has the scoop, and everyone wants a scoop of her. :) Has newscasting experience for local Tampa news broadcasts.
*** BROADCASTS *** STARS, STRIPES, AND SLAMS: The most fast-paced, action-packed two hours of television. 5-7 stellar matches where the AWC proves why they will reign atop the wrestling mountain. You want to be recognized? Well this is the show to be on.
***AFTER-HOURS: Sittin at home Monday night with nothing to do? Tune into After Hours! Live from Marquee Studios, Paddy Martin and Wesley Wade will bring to you the most fun filled hour in sports entertainment! Dancing, paryting, and most important... wrestling! 4 Matches (3 dark, 1 live) and also we will catch up with all the stars!
*** = In Development *** CHAMPIONSHIPS *** AWC World Heavyweight Championship: The belt everyone wants to hold. You think you are the best in the business? In order to prove it to the world, you will have to win this strap. Defended atleast once a month on television.
AWC Old Glory Championship: Second place isn't always bad. The AWC Old Glory
Championship is a stepping-stone to greatness within the company. Defended every week on Grapple Kings.
AWC Cruiserweight Championship: Think you are the best wrestler under 225 lbs? Prove it within the AWC and acquire the Cruiserweight title. Defended biweekly. AWC World Tag Team Championship: See World Heavyweight Championship. *** PAY PER VIEWS *** The AWC brings to you Pay Per Views six times a year. There are no set names or gimmicks for the cards, but expect eight stellar matches with drama and excitement added to the package. Our first Pay Per View will be in April, the name has yet to be announced.
*** ROLEPLAYING TIPS *** The AWC does not impose a limit on the number of flashes from each character per week. _HOWEVER_, chances are if you are scheduled for action and don't send in a flash, you won't be getting a check mark in the win column. Anyone who fails to produce flashes regularly, or remains silent for long periods of time, will be ejected from the league. In actual flashes and role-plays, swearing should be kept to a minimum. Unless absolutely necessary (and by that, agreement on the management's part is required), nothing stronger than ass should be said. If necessary, use [Bleep] in place of most swearing. Nothing that could get a show kicked off the air by the FCC should be used (racism, constant swearing, etc.).
Wrestlers are _NOT_ permitted to physically attack any and all officials, ring announcers, television announcers or interview personalities _UNLESS_ you receive permission. Stylized text is _NOT_ used in the AWC. One exclamation point is usually enough, and adding extra ones will only hold up the shows as they're edited out anyway.
*** STRATEGIES *** Strategies, or strats for short, are needed in order to win matches. It's up to you which kind of strategy you send in, a long drawn out one or a simple paragraph or two. "I wanna win, 'cause it'd be cool," is _NOT_ a strategy.
Focus on your opponent's weakness, and explain how your wrestler plans to wrestle against them. Failure to send in strats for long periods of time will result in a league ejection, much like non-flashing wrestlers.
*** ANGLES/MATCH BOOKING *** The mark of a good league is how well it's storylines and angles are thought out. Angles are what determines in most cases how and what matches are scheduled for the week. Flashes and strategies are dependent on good angles, otherwise proposed attacks may not be run unless they're for a specific purpose.
The easiest way to get matches: ask for them! Sending in challenges to other wrestlers are looked favorably on, and in most cases will be scheduled whenever possible. Again angles come into play, and those challenges that seem to "make sense" will be given preference in schedule dating. Title matches usually are given to those who prove themselves in past matches, and simply asking for a title match without reason (or storyline) won't get you one, guaranteed.
