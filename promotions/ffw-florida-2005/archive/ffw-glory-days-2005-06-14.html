GLORY DAYS! THE CHRONICLES OF FLORIDA'S FINEST WRESTLING Little but a memory in the minds of many, Florida's Finest Wrestling [FFW] was once the penultimate wrestling territory in the mid- to late 1980s. The home of many future superstars and wrestling greats, FFW, for many years, provided the State of Florida and the South in general with some of the greatest mat wars the business has ever known. Unfortunately, FFW would meet its untimely demise in 1998 when financial difficulties and the burgeoning success of Big League Wrestling [BLW] forced the league to fold and ultimately merge with the monopoly. However, FFW has left behind a legacy of wrestling not easily forgotten by true fans of professional wrestling. - - - - - - - - - - HISTORY - - - - - - - - - - In the Beginning (1978-1980). Florida's Finest Wrestling originally started in 1978 as a showcase for a group of Southern based wrestlers trying to get noticed by the triad of major wrestling organizations: Global Wrestling Federation [GWF], National Championship Wrestling of America [NCWA], and, of course, the aforementioned BLW. Spearheaded by former 70s great "Diamond" Jack Tatum, FFW, then called Florida All-Star Wrestling, primarily featured local talent wrestling in high school gyms and state fairs throughout the Jacksonville/North Florida area. Known for its brand of unusual characters, foremost among them the massive Samoan known as King Konga, and thrilling action, Florida All-Star Wrestling quickly gained in popularity. By 1980, Florida All-Star Wrestling was a mainstay in North Florida. Gaining Momentum (1981-1982). With significant profit gains and burgeoning competition from other Florida based wrestling organizations, primarily Sunshine State Wrestling [SSW], Tatum chose to reorganize Florida All-Star Wrestling and increase its exposure throughout the state. Renaming it Florida's Finest Wrestling, the league began spreading into the southern part of Florida, establishing strong footholds in Tallahassee and Orlando. Tatum also sanctioned a State Heavyweight Championship and sponsored a tournament for the title. In June 1982, FFW crowned its first FFW State champion, the charismatic "GQ" Johnny Handsome. FFW would later crown its first State Tag Team champions in the form of "Big" Brad and "Dangerous" Dan Baker, the Baker Boys. Breaking Down Doors (1983-1984). In 1983, FFW absorbed the struggling SSW, merging its own State titles with SSW's Florida titles. In an epic battle that still ranks as one of the best matches of all times, SSW's resident cheater and Florida champion "Dirty" Dick LeBaron defeated "GQ" Johnny Handsome to become the first ever FFW Heavyweight champion. In a grueling match of no less intensity, the Baker Boys would unite the FFW and SSW Tag Team titles by defeating the SSW titleholders, Mr. Puerto Rico II and Masked Menace. FFW would only continue to grow, extending its territory well into the Miami area, where its remaining competition in the state, South Florida Wrestling [SFW] still stood strong. However, SFW, like its predecessors, would eventually fold into FFW. By the end of 1984, FFW had become the only wrestling body of significance in the entire state. Trouble Arises (1984). However, FFW did not go into 1985 without its share of problems. Having flown under the radar for so long, FFW finally was noticed by BLW, which, by then, had grown into the top wrestling organization in the country. BLW began raiding talent from FFW, which refused to sign a talent development deal with BLW. However, this unfortunately did not stop BLW from raiding some of FFW's top draws, primarily FFW Heavyweight champion "Dirty" Dick LeBaron and FFW Tag Team champions Bryan Random and Billy Vance, the heart-throb duo known as the Tampa Bay Express [later repackaged in BLW as Heavy Metal]. - - - - - - - - - - RULES & INFORMATION - - - - - - - - - - GLORY DAYS! harkens back to the golden age of professional wrestling. Handlers who choose to participate will handle a character who will be participating in action that technically happened twenty years ago*. Great attention will be paid to realism and authenticity, as this is an attempt to capture the feeling of professional wrestling in the mid- to late 1980s. GLORY DAYS! is not an attempt to reinvent the wheel, per se. However, GLORY DAYS! is also not just a standard, angle based, biweekly e-wrestling league; it is the chronicling of a legend. Championships-along with wins and losses-are incidental; the main focus here is as follows: 1. To work together to tell a story of magnificent proportions. 2. To bring to life a unique brand of characters. 3. To produce a product unparalleled by any other in the genre. Characters. A handler's primary responsibility is to follow the path of a wrestler of their choice. Each character will be placed in certain situations to which the handler will have to react. The character's reaction-along with the subsequent results of his reaction-are the primary basis of the story. All handlers will handle a character that has already been created. A list of talent signed to FFW along with full-character descriptions has been included. Handlers may choose any one grappler (1) on the talent roster to handle. While all characters will remain property of FFW and cannot be used elsewhere, they can be personalized for the handler. Please consider the following: 1. Each character has his own status, which will represent where he stood as of December 31, 1984. As a result, some characters will, in fact, be preliminary wrestlers (jobbers), mid-card fodder, upper carders, and main event stars. Handlers will not be made abreast of their character's status until the league is set to begin. However, handlers should be aware that a character's status should be their least concern; the foremost concern is the progression of the story. 2. A handler may handle different characters from time to time. Retirements, call-ups to the larger leagues, injuries, and several other factors may cause for a handler's current character to leave FFW for varying periods of time. When this occurs, a handler may choose another character to handle from a roster of available talent. 3. A handler may be required to work in tandem with other handlers in tag-teams or six-man units. In 1985, singles wrestlers also frequently vied for tag team titles, and such will be the case in FFW. Any unwillingness to do so may result in a handler being dropped from FFW. Promos. All promos will be steeped in realism. In 1985, the emphasis in wrestling was on wrestling. Less talk, more action. As such, please consider the following: 1. Not everyone will merit equal microphone time. In fact, save for the titleholders and their primary contenders, no FFW talent will be allowed more than one (1) minute of microphone time to get their point across. 2. Promos are to be focused on either establishing the character's personality or addressing a grievance or ongoing feud with another competitor(s). No long length rants will be permitted. 3. All promos must take place in one of the following areas: in the hallway outside of the locker room, in front of the black backdrop with the FFW logo, inside the ring, inside the locker room, or at ringside. All promos must include an interviewer. 4. Promos will not contain any profanity whatsoever. Remember, this is wrestling circa 1985 being broadcast on network television. As such, no profanity will be tolerated. 5. A handler will be required to cut a promo only one (1) time during a two-week period. All promos will be embedded in the main card, the bi-weekly FFW Saturday Night. The deadline for promos will always be announced in advance of the card, usually within four to five days of the card being distributed. 6. All promos are to be sent to [detail withheld]. Cards. While the cards have technically already occurred, nothing has been pre-determined, per se. Consider this league as revisionist history: The history of FFW is being written for the first time ever. However, having said that, prior history will not be ignored. Previously established feuds will continue. Rankings from that time period will still stand. All roster members will maintain their status as of December 31, 1984 [the date before the MIAMI MAYHEM card and after the BLW roster raid]. However, on the MIAMI MAYHEM card, several grapplers will essentially make and break their careers. Each character's status will be revealed in the introductory portion of GLORY DAYS!, which will be made available prior to the first card, MIAMI MAYHEM. Time. For logistics purposes, every two-week period that passes in real time will represent one week of time in FFW. Website. A website for GLORY DAYS! is forthcoming. *GLORY DAYS! recaptures the heyday of FFW. As such, all of the events chronicled took place in the past, beginning with footage from the league's New Year's event MIAMI MAYHEM in Coral Gables, Florida, on January 1, 1985. - - - - - - - - - - TITLES - - - - - - - - - - FFW HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP* The top title in FFW, the Heavyweight title has an illustrious history. FFW Saturday Night was taped at several venues throughout the state, primarily in small scale auditoriums or high school gymnasiums. FFW does not sponsor supercards. In 1985, the supercard was essentially just beginning to take shape. However, whenever FFW travels to larger venues, in order to attract a crowd, the league will typically give the card a somewhat flamboyant name [e.g. MIAMI MAYHEM]. Since FFW cards have already taken place, per se, they are distributed on DVD as volumes of GLORY DAYS!: THE CHRONICLES OF FLORIDA'S FINEST WRESTLING. Each volume will represent six (6) weeks of FFW Saturday Night. - - - - - - - - - - STAFF - - - - - - - - - - "DIAMOND" JACK TATUM-FFW's presiding president, Tatum is the silent voice of authority of FFW. Tatum prefers to remain behind the scenes and only makes an appearance when strong armed tactics become necessary. DAVE KINCAID-FFW's play-by-play announcer since its days as the fledgling Florida All-Star Wrestling, Kincaid is, in effect, the voice of FFW. A virtual tome of wrestling knowledge, Kincaid never misses a call and calls it as he sees it. FREDDIE FLAMBOYANT-Putting the color in color commentating is FFW's own Freddie Flamboyant. The perennially pink pugilist was a mainstay in the Florida wrestling scene in the mid- to late 1970s. Known for his sharp wit and acid tongue, Flamboyant certainly will liven up the broadcast booth. SID CHAVEZ-Another holdover from the 1970s, Chavez is FFW's backstage interviewer. Having a good rapport with the talent, Chavez is able to find out all of the inside dirt and pose the intriguing questions. ROY HARDEE-The official announcer of FFW. Hardee energizes the crowd for the arrival of its favorite FFW superstars. - - - - - - - - - - ROSTER - - - - - - - - - - The following superstars are currently signed to FFW and available for use: "ROTTEN" JIMMY APPLE Height: 5'10" Weight: 225 pounds Hometown: Staten Island, New York Entrance Theme: "Rebel Yell" by Billy Idol Finisher: Brainbuster Description: A troublemaker if there ever was one, the brash New Yorker "Rotten" Jimmy Apple is the man everyone loves to hate. A cocky bastard, Apple is a nonetheless a sly and fierce competitor who abhors losing. A unique blend of technical wrestling and hardnosed brawling, Apple is a formidable competitor with an unrivaled mean streak and a penchant for the foreign object. BEAU BLAIR Height: 6'2" Weight: 240 pounds Hometown: Durham, North Carolina Entrance Theme: "Saturday Night Special" by Lynyrd Skynyrd Finisher: Figure four leglock Description: FFW's golden boy, Beau Blair is the league's top babyface. However, his charming, burly action hero looks betray a wealth of talent. Blair is FFW's most technically sound wrestler, a gifted counter wrestler with a vast repertoire of maneuvers at his disposal. Nobody in the league is more popular with the fans, as Blair's workman's ethic and rugged good looks have won the hearts of FFW followers. BEHEMOTH Height: 6'11" Weight: 400 pounds Hometown: Grand Rapids, Michigan Entrance Theme: "Iron Man" by Black Sabbath Finisher: Belly-to-belly suplex Description: The irresistible force meets the immovable object in the form of Behemoth. This monster towers over his opposition and undoubtedly the largest competitor in FFW. A slow and methodical plodder, Behemoth is nonetheless extremely dangerous because once he gets his hands on an opponent, there is little he can do but accept the loss. "COWBOY" CHASE BRADLEY Height: 6'4" Weight: 288 pounds Hometown: Lubbock, Texas Entrance Theme: "Country Boy Can Survive" by Hank Williams, Jr. Finisher: Running bulldog Description: A tough-talking Texan, "Cowboy" Chase Bradley definitely has the ability to back up those words. A no-nonsense roughneck, Bradley is perhaps the most versatile wrestler on the roster, with his ability to go hold for hold in a scientific match up or sling fists with the best brawlers. With his combination of size, speed, and toughness, Bradley will undoubtedly be a major contender in FFW for years to come. PIT BULL CURTIS Height: 6'3" Weight: 275 pounds Hometown: Patterson, New Jersey Entrance Theme: "The Message" by Grandmaster Flash and the Furious Five Finisher: Scoop power slam Description: Built like the vicious brute after which he is named (and with a temperament to match), Pit Bull Curtis is the pure powerhouse of FFW. A former linebacker for the Tampa Bay Buccaneers in the later 1970s, Curtis made an easy transition to wrestling. He even became a trendsetter, becoming the first African American to capture the FFW Heavyweight title. BIG CHIEF CYPRESS Height: 6'0" Weight: 270 pounds Hometown: Elk City, Oklahoma Entrance Theme: "I Walk the Line" by Johnny Cash Finisher: Flying ax-handle smash from top turnbuckle Description: A mainstay in Florida, the big Seminole warrior is always among the top draws in the promotion. Though the popular Native American often leaves Florida for the larger promotions, he always returns to much fanfare. Why do the fans love him? Because they know whenever The Chief is around, they will see wrestling at its finest as Cypress is one of the best all-around grapplers in the business. With his war chant and war dance, Cypress knows how to build a crowd into frenzy! "ROCK 'N ROLL" RONNIE DARREN Height: 5'11" Weight: 228 pounds Hometown: Union City, Tennessee Entrance Theme: "Jump" by Van Halen Finisher: Flying fist drop Description: The high-flying heartthrob of FFW, "Rock 'N Roll" Ronnie Darren is rocketing towards the top! A death defying high-flyer with the technical skill of even the wiliest veteran, Darren represents the next wave of FFW superstars. Darren is no doubt bound for success, and it is only a short amount of time before the fan favorite is wearing FFW gold. "GQ" JOHNNY HANDSOME Height: 6'0" Weight: 235 pounds Hometown: Savannah, Georgia Entrance Theme: "Give Me All Your Loving" by ZZ Top Finisher: Boston crab Description: Love him or hate him, "GQ" Johnny Handsome no doubt is one of FFW's top competitors. The flamboyant philanderer and primadonna, known equally for his stylish dress as he is for his technical ability in the ring, is a wily and unpredictable character. While he can be hero or villain depending on his mood, Handsome nonetheless is a fearsome competitor who is respected throughout the business. He especially is endeared to followers of FFW, as he is its very first champion and always a threat to hold championship gold at any time. KID KONGA Height: 5'11" Weight: 240 pounds Hometown: The Island of Samoa Entrance Theme: "Hawaiian War Chant" by Prince Leileohako Finisher: Flying body splash from the top turnbuckle Description: The son of wrestling great King Konga, Kid Konga seeks to make a name for himself in FFW. While not possessing his father's bulk or strength, he does have assets his father did not possess: speed and high-flying ability. The stocky Kid Konga betrays his build by being perhaps the fastest and most agile wrestler in FFW. Fans have marveled at the elevation the young Samoan native gets on his flying body splash attempts. MASKED MENACE Height: 6'3" Weight: 280 pounds Hometown: Parts unknown Entrance Theme: "Hells Bells" by AC/DC Finisher: Camel clutch Description: Undoubtedly the most dangerous wrestler in FFW, Masked Menace is always in the hunt for FFW gold, both as a singles competitor and as a member of a tag team. Masked Menace is the only person in FFW history to hold both the Heavyweight and Tag Team titles (which he did simultaneously in mid-1984). With a killer mean streak and vast repertoire of submission maneuvers, Masked Menace is a force to be reckoned with. MANIAC PSYCHO Height: 6'1" Weight: 258 pounds Hometown: Parts unknown Entrance Theme: "The Twilight Zone Theme" by Rod Serling Finisher: SST [inverted facelock brainbuster] Description: FFW's resident lunatic, Maniac Psycho was once half of one of FFW's most dominant tag teams, The Maniacs, alongside partner Maniac Lunatic. When Maniac Lunatic defected to BLW, Maniac Psycho was left alone to fend for himself, and he has certainly been able to do so. Unorthodox and certainly every bit of the psychopath his name implies. Maniac Psycho is always willing to put life and limb on the line for a victory. One might even suggest that Maniac Psycho is a pioneer of hardcore wrestling. KNUCKLES NOLAN Height: 6'8" Weight: 310 pounds Hometown: Alcatraz Island, California Entrance Theme: "Jailbreak" by AC/DC Finisher: Piledriver Description: Knuckles Nolan is hardnosed bruiser, a result of the harsh penal system. Self-described as a two-time loser, Nolan still, nonetheless, maintains a criminal mindset. In fact, Nolan even wears his orange prison jumpsuit to the ring! His rugged tactics and brawling ability have garnered him some success in the ring as he has held the FFW Tag Team titles alongside frequent tag team partner, Masked Menace. "PHENOMENAL" PHIL PRATT Height: 6'4" Weight: 260 pounds Hometown: Sun City, Florida Entrance Theme: "The Final Countdown" by Europe Finisher: Gorilla press slam Description: A virtual Adonis, "Phenomenal" Phil Pratt is FFW's resident hardbody. Blessed with the physique and the looks of a Grecian god, Pratt is a fan favorite with FFW's female fans. However, along with Pratt's good looks comes a solid wrestling knowledge, which, when blended by his pure power, makes him a dangerous competitor. Pratt is the new wave of FFW and has found success in being the first ever FFW Bay Area Heavyweight champion. MR. PUERTO RICO II Height: 5'10" Weight: 235 pounds Hometown: Ponce, Puerto Rico Entrance Theme: "La Borinqueña (Puerto Rican National Anthem)" Finisher: Lou Thesz press Description: The masked superstar known as Mr. Puerto Rico II has thrilled fans for nearly two decades throughout the South and in his native Caribbean. A tag team specialist, Mr. Puerto Rico II has held the FFW Tag Team title on two occasions with two different partners. However, with his mix of high-flying and technical ability, Mr. Puerto Rico II is always a threat for singles gold, as well. SCORCH Height: 6'2" Weight: 245 pounds Hometown: Tampa, Florida Entrance Theme: "Burn in Hell" by Twisted Sister Finisher: Inverted DDT Description: The enigma known solely as Scorch brings high energy to FFW. A bevy of energy and enthusiasm, Scorch represents part of the new breed of wrestler entering FFW. With his ornamental face paint and his spiked, bright orange hair, Scorch certainly draws attention. Combined with his full throttle wrestling style and his willingness to get the crowd involved, Scorch has become the most popular wrestler in FFW. SUTHERLAND SMITH Height: 6'3" Weight: 267 pounds Hometown: Pensacola, Florida Entrance Theme: "Freebird" by Lynyrd Skynyrd Finisher: DDT Description: After a stint in NCWA, FFW's prodigal son Sutherland Smith has finally returned home, and not a moment too soon! The two-time FFW Heavyweight champion is no doubt the most popular wrestler in FFW history. Known by the fans as the "Round Mound o' Pound," the burly North Florida native is a top notch brawler with a never-say-die attitude. His bloody wars with "GQ" Johnny Handsome, King Konga, and "Dirty" Dick LeBaron are the stuff of legend. "LUSCIOUS" LARRY TATUM Height: 6'1" Weight: 250 pounds Hometown: Jacksonville, Florida Entrance Theme: "We Are the Champions" by Queen Finisher: Belly-to-back suplex Description: The elder brother of the once infamous Tatum Brothers tandem, "Luscious" Larry Tatum has chosen not to hang up the tights. While many might argue that the Luscious One's best years are behind him, his performance in the ring suggests otherwise. A wily and skilled competitor, Tatum always seems to have a trick up his sleeve. VLADIMIR THE GREAT Height: 6'5" Weight: 275 pounds Hometown: Kiev, Russia Entrance Theme: "The Imperial March (Darth Vader's Theme)" by John Williams Finisher: Running ax-handle smash Description: Out of the frozen tundra of the Soviet Republic emerges the 275 pounds of Russian colossus in the form of Vladimir the Great. The Bolshevik Man of Steel is an unstoppable wrecking machine, driven to capture American gold and return it to Mother Russia. Led by the nefarious Tsar Aleksander, Vladimir's quest for gold certainly will not be a long one. - - - - - - - - - - MANAGERS/VALETS - - - - - - - - - - DEBBIE DARLING Behind every devious swindler is an equally devious woman, and few are capable of causing more harm than Debbie Darling, the valet to none other than "GQ" Johnny Handsome. A stunning piece of eye candy with the curves of a Playboy model, the statuesque blonde is not only beautiful but deadly. She can be as sweet as angel, or as venomous as an asp, depending, of course, on how Handsome feels himself. "FAST" EDDIE RASLO Having crossed nearly everyone who has come under his management, "Fast" Eddie Raslo is not exactly a hot commodity in FFW. The brash, annoying, and vulgar Raslo is hated by nearly everyone from fans to management to talent. Nonetheless, he manages to hang around for one reason: Sooner or later, his sharp wit and rapid tongue will be able to goad some unsuspecting wrestler to signing on with him and following his whims. TSAR ALEKSANDER The stout, gnarled figure standing beside Vladimir the Great is none other than the cunning mastermind known only as Tsar Aleksander. Grotesque and loathsome, The Tsar has only one goal: to bring an American wrestling title back to his homeland. A former GWF World champion, Aleksander, despite his advanced age, is still able to hold his own in a confrontation and is even willing to throw on the tights if it will guarantee a victory for the USSR. - - - - - - - - - - APPLICATION PROCESS - - - - - - - - - - To apply to handle a FFW character, simply fill out the application below and send it to [detail withheld]. - - - - - - - - - - APPLICATION - - - - - - - - - - HANDLER INFORMATION Handler's Name: Handler's E-mail: Handler's AOL IM Nickname: CHARACTER SELECTION* Pre-rank five FFW characters who you are interested in handling in order of preference (first choice first, second choice second, etc.).
