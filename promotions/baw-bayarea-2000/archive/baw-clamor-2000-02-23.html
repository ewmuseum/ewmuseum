Directly in front of the camera is a long line of running machines. The camera takes a slow pan around from left to right, taking a look at all the different weight machines as well as the rowing, stepping and cycling machines. Once we have stopped looking around we start to hear footsteps. They begin to come more frequent and louder and louder as if they are approaching. The camera turns around and standing there, wearing white sneekers, white shorts and work-out vest is none other than wrestling veteran
"Pure Athlete" Tony Virtue with towel in hand. After taking a few glances around at the surroundings,
Virtue looks straight in front of him at the camera and then speaks] TV: You know this is the home of _real_ athletes. The gymnasium or as many call it, the fitness centre is where all the world-class top athletes train including myself. You know, in recent years wrestling has gone one way and that's downhill. Nowadays, wrestlers dress up in colorful and glitzy attire. They are accompanied by several gimmicks such as 'hoes' and
'bodyguards'. Sports entertainment is ruining the great sport that is wrestling but soon, it will not.
Now Tony Virtue has returned to the squared circle, wrestling will return back to it's very competitive and technical old self.
[Tony takes his time by taking a gentle stroll towards the running machine. He steps onto the platform, taps in some digits and before you know it he's jogging.
The camera moves in front of him and he talks whilst jogging gently] TV : Now as you have probably already discovered, I am a very down to earth, calm and relaxed human being but there's a few things that I really cannot stand and that's all those internet jerks and nerds who write articles about how 'Tony Virtue is too old' and how
Tony Virtue is a has-been'. That right there is absolute ludicrous. Those nerds who sit in front of their computers all day every day are just jealous.
They are jealous of me because they know they I may be thirty seven years old but I'm still as quick, I'm still as talented and I'm still the greatest technical specimen in pro-wrestling and soon, the whole world will realise that again.
[Virtue begins to sweat slightly at the top of his fore-head. He takes a few breaths to cool him and calm him down before speaking once again]
TV : The reason why the whole world will realise that I'm still as good as I've always been even when I made my wrestling debut in the early eighties is because I have signed a contract to compete in Bay Area
Wrestling. The BAW visited me in my home in Texas and offered me a six figure deal to return to the ring and
I was happy to oblige. I make my re-debut back in pro-wrestling on February 24th against the self proclaimed, "Human Athrodisiac", Bradley Rose.
[The running machine starts to slow down making Tony stop jogging and instead turning into a fast walking pace]
TV : Bradley Rose. This guy is a prime example of what I meant when I spoke earlier about the way wrestling is been transmitted into "Sports Entertainment". He goes around calling himself the "Human Athrodisiac".
Let me tell you something Brad, this is professional _wrestling_. Not some little lovey-dovey party at your local disco for god's sake. Many people have said that you are one of the greatest youngsters in the game but I ask you, what game are they talking about? Because they damn sure aint talking about wrestling that's for sure. Rose, I'm gonna teach you a lesson in _technical_ wrestling and teach you a lesson in being a "pure athlete" like myself.
[The platform gets even slower until it comes to a complete halt. Tony steps off the platform and wipes his head with the towel before talking again]
TV : Rose you may go around calling yourself the "Human Athrodisiac" but what you're looking at right now is the "Human Supreme Machine" and you'll find out why come the 24th.
[Tony walks over towards the cycling machine. He makes himself comfortable on the seat and then begins rotating his legs on the pedals, taking too]
TV : As for the rest of the roster goes, they too will be taught a lesson. It seems none of these
"wrestlers" in BAW have a clue what pure talent and what being a pure athlete is all about. But I don't care. I'll be more than happy to show them. So Rose and all you others in BAW, come the 24th, watch my match very closely. Make sure you have a pen and some paper handy so you can make notes and maybe one day, you too will have the privalege of calling yourself a
"Pure Athlete". [Tony focuses on the cycling machine and starts realling pedalling as the scene fades out to darkness]
============================= ----------------------------- 2) "Devastating" Damon Knight ----------------------------- ============================= [Fade In.] [The picture on your screen opens up to a shot of a rathy loud, and elaborate building. This must be a high class joint. Their are velvet ropes, and gold poles with fans, and groupies standing around on the outside. In between the poles and velvet ropes a red carpet has been rolled out ....
.... Suddenly, into the scene, two bellhops walk into the picture. They are wearing the attire of those who are there to serve the people who are pulling up. Your analysis of their attire is anwsered when a brand pull two thousand
Mercades-Benz pulls up, and they take a cash tip, and pull away in the car for the man ...
... The picture changes angles all in a quick flash. With this change of angle you are looking into a small parking lot. About a fifteen to twenty space parking lot, infront of the "high class joint," as it was called, with about half of the spots filled up by limos of all sorts ...
... The camera zooms into these parking spaces. Walking by viewing the different designs of the vechiles, and trying to get a sneak peak at the celebrities that may be laid up in these beautiful automobiles ...
... While passing by one of the vechiles, you see a license plate which reads: "D.D.K." ... Being the huge wrestling fan that you are, you automatically know that those are the initials of the world renown, recent unretired wrestling superstar, "Devastating" Damon Knight! Your suspicion gets the best of the newly signed Bee Aye Dubbya superstar might be in this vechile, causing you to walk around to the tinted windows and try to get a sneak peak. In walking around, you see that the window is cracked, and the serial thrilla slash rich man, Damon Knight is in the vechile. Your dreams have came true as a man taps on the window, and it comes down a little more, then shoots back up. For a brief second, your dreams are shattered, however, the door swings open and out walks Damon Knight.
... Damon Knight is wearing that of a three piece Armani suit, along with matching alligator-skinned boots. Damon looks into the camera, with the always arrogant smirk plastuered to his face, begins to speak in his oh so sexy, yet ghetto tongue ...]
DK : Tha serial thrilla is always in tha lime light. (Shaking head) It never stops does it. Y'know, ever since I threw down tha john hancock onto that multi-million dollar Bay Area Wrestlin' contract, tha celly has been ringin' nonstop, and once again, I am throwing the wrestling world into limbo.
Everyone is on there toes trying to figure out what my first move in tha Bay
Area wrestlin' promotion is gon' be. Well yo, i'ma kill it here; my first move will be another superior move. Tha Devastatin' tha Nation tour is gon'
commence in Bee Aye Dubbya. [Damon Knight checks his black-pearl cufflinks and continues to talk.] DK : Now, off tha top, I must admit that I am upset. How dare those two nit-witted brothers who own this company throw me into a match with some loser on the bottom of the totem pole?
Off the top tha promoters should know that hough having me on the card they are going to be making that cash, but with me headlining the show, it would be even better.
[Damon shakes his head in disgust.] DK : Foolish, foolish men. However, I have people to do, and place to see, so I shall speak on my lacking opponent ... "Hollywood" Heartbreak Kris.
[Knight tries to resist the temptation of chuckling, but finds himself unable and lets out a cocky snicker before he takes to the verbal assault on his first
B.A.W. opponent.]
DK : Kirs, you are going to be used to set an example. I have not wrestled in months, however, I shall step into tha squared circle and devastate you just like I shall do to all comers.
Yes, Kris, you have been given tha priveledge to step foot in tha ring against
Damon Knight. You have the chance of the lifetime to hang onto my coat tails
... in my ring, and "Hollywood" i'ma let you in on a lil' secret ...
[Damon Knight looks dead into the camera, and in his arrogant ghetto tongue speaks.] DK : ... It'll prove ta be notin' less than devastatin! [Smirk.] DK : This night is going to be a big one for all of these people who are going to be following Damon Knight's devastatin' tha nation tour, that includes you too, Kris.
And if someone is so insolent as to not give me the respect I deserve...well,
Kris, I think you are going to have a clearer idea than anyone else of what happens to people who do not give me the respect I command.
See ya in tha ring. [Damon Knight once again, gets that arrogant look on his face. He snaps his fingers and two lovely young ladies come out of the car. One hands Damon Knight a maghony cane, while the other gives him a top hat. They then latch onto
Knight's arms, and he makes his way into the club.]
============================= ----------------------------- 3) "El Dub" Lawrence Williams ----------------------------- ============================= [It's Monday, February 21, 2000, and one man is currently occupying an otherwise vacuous BAW Arena. The continuous flow of blue chairs and gray concrete floor encircles the arena until the human figure interrupts the uniformity.
As the camera zooms in on the man, it becomes apparent that he is professional wrestling veteran Lawrence
Williams, now going by "El Dub." Lawrence is in the front row in a seated position. A plain white tee shirt, grey pants, and black shoes make up his attire. An almost humurous expression is manifest on his face as he places his right foot over his left foot--both of which are resting atop the steel ring barrier.
Before the camera can zoom in any further, Williams begins to speak.] LW: Eight months. [Lawrence momentarily pauses as he lets out a slight chuckle, putting both hands behind his head, expressing even more a casual mood.]
It's been eight months since I really felt like steppin into a wrestling ring. Yeah... Sure the
_thought_ has been there... But it's like when you a kid and you mad cause you got grounded...
Sure, you _think_ about killing yourself.. But you don't do it... Not cause you don't want to do it, but because you can't get past the thought of it.. You can't take the next step.
[Lawrence laughs again.] Well, maybe that wasn't the best of examples, considerin I'm actually _going_ to be steppin back into a wrestling ring. I don't want to go around suggestin that finally wrestling again is as good as finally jumpin off the Golden
Gate Bridge. [Williams gets up and begins looking around the Arena, sticking both hands into their respective pockets.] But maybe this _is_ some type of suicide. Lord knows the last time I tried comin back I almost fell into an eternal depression.. The wear and tear... The almost lack of gratitude in return for my hours of slave-like labor.
[Lawrence nods his head.] Yeah, you might call it suicide. [Now "El Dub" steps over the ring barrier onto the other side. He then slowly begins taking the walk up the ring steps... onto the apron... in between the middle and top ropes... and finally into the ring. He looks up as he speaks again.]
But I think this is a necessary suicide... A euthanasic necessity... Cause no matter _how_ bad the injuries... No matter _how_ deep the depression.. I _let_ the gravity of wrestling pull me back.
[Lawrence turns around and stares into the camera.] And now that I'm back... You can expect some changes from "El Dub." Yeah, the arrogance is still there. That's just me... who I am. But the attitude that this is a El Dub-centric universe is gone... I still believe I'm the best professional wrestler in this business...
But no more will I go about proving that with my mouth. It's all about wrestling from here on out. [Pause.] And someone is going to find out what exactly that last statement means. And it ain't gonna be pretty.
[Long, dramatic pause.] Trust me. [Fade.] ============================= ----------------------------- 4) "2 Smooth" Setzer Van Strife ----------------------------- ============================= [The scene slowly fades in.] [The camera opens to a totally black screen. No lights. No sounds. The scene stays transfixed for a moment, until it is interrupted by a light in the middle of the room, flickering on for a brief moment. It then flickers back off, and once again back on, this time for a bit longer. The camera tries to get a general feel of the room by the llumination of the lightbulb in the middle of the room, hanging from a string with another string hanging from it, to turn it on and off.
The room itself is small, no bigger than a typical bedroom, perhaps just a little smaller than one is used to. To the right, in between flickers of light, is a wooden desk, with a chipping, rotting wooden chair pushed neatly into it.
On top of the desk are random papers, which are scattered about. And then to the left, seated in the corner, is a figure, slumped down, his knees bent and close to his body. A Singapore Cane is in his hand, clenched tightly.
As the light flickers more infrequently, the man lifts his head to the camera, a devilish smirk upon his face. The man begins to lightly tap the cane on the wall, making a sharp noise. The man is then identified as he then changes his grin into one of sheer cockiness. And now, sitting there alone inside the room, is none other than '2 Smooth' Setzer Van Strife. Van Strife then starts to rap the cane against the wall a bit harder, then a bit harder still, before stopping.
Then, amongst the silence, Van Strife begins to speak.] SVS : You're probably expecting some lavish settings for someone of my caliber. Someone that has traveled the world over and again, showing the tremendous skills that I possess. Someone that lives for the spotlight, and isn't afraid to flaunt the fact that he's just _that much better_ than the rest of the roster. Someone that has been involved in promotions that many will only get to experience in their wildest dreams. So one must wonder, what am I doing here?
Well, even the most skilled and egotistical of men goes a bit crazy once in awhile. [Van Strife smirks.] SVS : My crusades of showing that I'm the best have now led me to the Bay
Area and the BAW. Although the wrestlers in this promotion leave something to be desired, I've been assured that only quality wrestlers have been accepted. However, my being in the BAW, and being the only viable champion leads me to believe that I was chosen for one reason, and one reason only. And that, my friends, is to become the BAW World
Heavyweight Champion. I've taken a look over this roster that the BAW boasts as one of the best. And, although this pool of somewhat skilled wrestlers would normally be a challenge... it's nothing but a mere cakewalk for yours truly. You see, I've encountered men over the past few years that many have called 'legends'.
I've been in matches that are so mindblowing, they're still being shown and hailed as some of the best wrestling ever.
So, you see, there's no other choice for the BAW World Champion. But, just in case anyone tries to get in my way...
[Van Strife lifts the Singapore Cane slightly, banging it against the wall once loudly.] SVS : ... I have the great equalizer on my side. [Van Strife sets the cane down on the ground and continues.] SVS : You see, it doesn't matter who I have to go through. You can put me up against a never-was like Damon Knight, someone that thinks he's the best thing running now that he's back in the sport. You can contract me to take on a hardcore psychopath like Zack Sharp, someone that thinks that he'll put people through tables and not actually _wrestle_. You can have me face any man on that roster, be it Tony Virtue, Ho Chi Mingh, or Tommy
Brooks. But in the end, there will be one man getting his hand raised, celebrating the BAW Heavyweight Title being strapped around his waist.
And you're taking a long, hard look at him. [Van Strife stands up slowly, picking up the Singapore Cane.] SVS : Now, this week I'm right where I belong, in the main event, though I am a bit curious as to why my opponent deserves a shot at me. You see,
Ryan Walters was once in a promotion with me, back in the Great Lakes area. There was a little tournament, in which the champion of the event won a shot at the champion somewhere down the road. And, amongst the list of people in this tournament was yours truly and, you guessed it, Ryan
Walters. Well, in the end, one of these men lost in the _first round_, while the other, well he went on to win the entire thing. One of these men went on to glory, while the other meagerly tries to eek out a career. Well, the man that won the entire shebang was none other than _me_. The man that lost before he even got a chance? Ryan Walters. You see, I don't think that Mister Walters _deserves_ a shot at me. After all, he couldn't even get out of the first round.
Why put a _champion_ against an utter _loser_? It just doesn't make sense. [Van Strife begins to walk towards the light fixture.] SVS : Walters, you disgust me. I've seen you in the past, running your mouth, trying to make promises that frankly, I never thought you'd be able to pull through. And, as I expected, you didn't pull them through.
And I'm assuming that you'll most likely be talking a big game, about how great you are, how many ladies you get, yadda yadda. But you see, the only thing you're proving to the masses is how you want to be just like me.
Don't get me wrong, kiddo. I've seen my share of wanna-be's in my career, but you take the cake. You have got to be the closest to a carbon copy that
I've seen. It's just sad that you'll never be me. Not in a million years. Sorry to break it to you kiddo, but the truth is, we already know who's going to win this match. You don't have a chance in hell.
However, I'm sure you'll think you have all the chances in the world. But just wait. You'll be equalized soon enough. [Van Strife lifts the Singapore Cane up in the air, then sends it crashing down onto the lightbulb, sending the scene back into total darkness, as the scene finally fades out to snow.]
============================= ----------------------------- 5) "Traditional" Tommy Brooks ----------------------------- ============================= ["Tradition will always overule the future....for the acts of Tradition, set the grounds for the acts of Future...." - A Wise Man]
[The scene enters the BAW Arena, the setting of pitch blackness nearly, and complete echo's of silence. The rows and rows of chairs stand still and empty, the arena all set up for the big opening show, "Battle in the Bay", coming
Feburary 24th, to open a revolution on the industry, unveiling a load of talent on the new BAW roster. With the tickets being sold out for weeks, the anticipation continues to grow as the now silent and dark arena, will soon be filled with crazy fans and spectacular athletes, who will hopefully live up to the anticipation, and shock the wrestling world...]
[The echo's of silence are brought to an abrupt halt, as a sniffle is heard from the center of the dark arena, where a professional sized wrestling ring sits, all set up and ready to go. As the darkness begins to clear a bit, the ring lights, which hang above the ring area, begin to fade in, lighting the arena just enough, to show a man in the ring, standing in one of the four corners, back against the turnbuckles, as he looks around the empty arena...]
[The man is in rather good shape, from the looks of things. A tight tee shirt, exclimates his body's muscle tone, as his shoulders are as broad as can be, his abs sticking out nearly a mile away from his belly button. His head is smooth, shaved to the skin on the sides, with slicked bleach blonde hair pushed forward, and a pair of black sunglasses to top it off, with a smile on the side. The tee shirt, which reads "Tradition Rulez" on the front, and seemingly
"24:7" on the back, is tucked into a pair of slightly loose blue jeans, which come down to a pair of street shoes, colored brown, with several scuff marks on them...A gold watch glimmers in the light as the man moves his wrist...]
TB : Only two more days left.... [The man breaks the silence once again, with four words coming out of his mouth, as he then continues....]
TB :Two more days, until the world will once again come to realize, that the Tradition of professional wrestling, will always out landish the styles of today and tomorrow. The Tradition, of wrestling, is what brought us here to today, and what will carry
the sport out into the future of tomorrow. The Tradition....my friends, is what I, Tommy Brooks, am all about.....
[The man now speaks with more integrity then before, introducing himself to the world...] TB : When I got the call from my agent about this place, I was skeptical. I've had better contracts, no doubt, but the talent and the oppurtunities that this place provided, was something that can be found no where else. The exposure, the unlimited accounts of exposure to true wrestling fans, called the attention of the Traditional one himself, and so I've brought myself here, to show to the world, what can be done inside, and outside of the wrestling ring.
[Tommy Brooks, now begins to pace the ropes, walking around the ring...] TB : It was nearly five years ago to this day, that I stepped into my first match of my career. I was only twenty years old, still in college, still working at a fast food resturant, still with the raunchy girlfriend who wouldn't slide into home anytime soon..... And while I was in the ring,
I thought to myself, will I be doing this five years from now? Will I be signed to a highly respective federation, where my talents will be showcased to the world? Well...where I'm at now, is about ten times more, then what I expected. I expected, to be stuck at the same job, with the same girl, with the same life. Now, the only thing that is the same, is the same girl, but she finally realized that TRADITION says, to slide into home after consuming marriage....."
[A small smirk from the man, as he comes to an stop in the center of the ring....] TB : So now, that brings me to the BAW, a place of talent and fortune. In only about sixty hours, give or take, "Traditional" Tommy Brooks, will make his long awaited, and nearly conctipated debut, into this very ring.....Now are you excited? I know you are, and I can guarntee, that every man in the front office is more excited then they ever have been, becuase they are just coming around the realize just what exactly they have in their hands to play with."
[Brooks now walks back to his original corner, and props to the middle turnbuckle..] TB : First off, to start the TRADITIONAL streak of Tommy Brooks, I've been lined up with a self proclaimed "International Superstar". Hmmm....when
I first tried to pronounce this name, all I got out of it was the same #12 Combo I order from my chinese stop down the street, but I'll give it just one more shot....Hoochie Ming? Hoe Chee Mingh? Well...whomever this man is, he better learn a taste of english, so someone can notify him just what he is going up against. I would really hate to destory this guy on his BAW debut, at the "Battle in the Bay", but if things begin to get ugly, it's my job to do, what I have to do....
TB : So Ho Ching......however you pronoucne that Combo meal.....I have two goals, for the Battle in the Bay, this Thursday, in this very arena, when
I step into the ring with you. First goal, is to get your ass to realize what Tradition is all about, and to show you the meaning of being....
_!!!TRADITIONALIZED!!!_ TB : And second....I'm going to teach you just enough english throughout the entire matchup, so by that time, you'll understand, these very, exact words my dear friends.....
[Tommy Brooks now takes off the sunglasses, and stares into the camera...] _TRADITION_ _WILL ALWAYS_ _!!!RULE!!!_ [After a pause, Brooks leaves the ring in silence, as the scene cuts to silence....]
