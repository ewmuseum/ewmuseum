Table of Contents: I.Introduction II.Titles III.Shows IV.People V.Roster VI.Rankings VII.Wednesday Warfare (Previous Results and Upcoming Event) VIII.Saturday Night (Previous Results and Upcoming Event) IX.Application X.Credits ============================================================================ I.Introduction . . . Hello, reader. My name is Ian Walsh and I am the one who is masterminding this whole thing. CWP is an e-wrestling federation. CWP stands for Continental Wrestling Promotion. CWP is one of countless internet-based wrestling federations. In CWP, you run a character or a tag team and follow the character's career from start to finish however long you desire to run that character in this federation. In CWP, roleplaying, strategy, and story-line are all important. Why? Well, in e-wrestling, these factors all combine to equal success. Roleplaying in short, is where you become the character that you run and speak for him. As time goes on, a web page will be made for CWP and this will be talked about in more detail. All roleplays are sent to me at: [detail withheld] Strategy . . . these are not as important as roleplays, but they are important nonetheless. A strategy is a summary of how you want your match to go. As you write one for your character, your opponent writes one for his. The strategies are then meshed together to give an idea of how the match is going to go. Strategy format for CWP is as follows: Strategy for (Name of wrestler/team) Entrance: Besides the entrance write-up, we want to know if there is anything special for this match, in particular, that you want done. Comments, notes, etc . . . During the match: Write a summary of what you want the character to do and how you want him to wrestle. After the match: If there is anything special you would like your character to say or do, then write it out in this section. If the time calls for it, it will be used. Key Points: List and number as many key points as you would like in this match. These will be used to my discretion. These can also be used to build angles. All strategies are to be sent to me at: [detail withheld] Story-lines/Angles: These can be sent to me at any time. I choose where a character goes depending on what is produced by the handler. However, you can change how your character's story-line goes by sending suggestions to me. Depending on time and what the angle would do, I will approve it and it will happen. But remember, it's to my discretion. All angle ideas are to be sent to me at: [detail withheld] , [detail withheld] , [detail withheld] , [detail withheld] ============================================================================ II.Titles . . . Intercontinental Heavyweight Title: Most important title in CWP. It's not a World Title, because CWP is only International. This title is the ultimate goal as titles are concerned. National Title: As CWP is international, a title is needed for the national division. The is the minor title in the universal singles division. Juniorweight Title: For wrestlers under 230, this is their title. Having this title means that you are the king of the CWP Juniorweight Wrestlers. Intercontinental Tag Team Titles: Most important rank in the tag team division. The team that holds these must be tough and have stamina for these titles are defended often. U-Cup: Each year, CWP will hold a Universal Tournament where the winner receives the U-Cup. Universal Cup . . . 32 men fight in a single elimination tournament. The final 8 will face off in a battle royal where the winner will become the U-Cup champion. **Titles may be changed, dropped, added in the future. ============================================================================ III.Shows . . . The schedule goes a little like this: Sunday: Nothing/PPV (Pay Per Views are every three months) Monday: Nothing Tuesday: Roleplays Sent Out Wednesday: Wednesday Warfare/Update Thursday: Nothing Friday: Roleplays Sent Out (Pre-Show) Saturday: Saturday Night/Update Wednesday Warfare: The minor show. Matches, except for the main event, or if important enough, a double main event, are written in summary form. This show is hosted by David Forthright and Michael Russenhoff. Strategies and roleplays for Wednesday Warfare are due 7:00 PM EDT Tuesdays. Pre-Show: A show hosted by David Forthright and Donald Blackstone. Roleplays are aired here. A run-down and preview will also be attractions of this show. Roleplays will only be accepted for the Pre-Show until Noon EDT on Fridays. Any others may be counted less and be put on Saturday Night. Saturday Night: This is the big show, so to speak. This show will have 5-6 written matches as well as a few dark matches. A report will be included in the beginning of each Saturday Night. Night's before a PPV, format may be changed to one like Wednesday Warfare's. This show is hosted by David Forthright and Seth Stevens. Strategies for this event are due the Wednesday before the event. There will be plenty of time to complete strategies. Any strategies sent after the 9:00 PM EDT Wednesday deadline will not be used. Roleplays for this event are due Friday at Noon EDT. ============================================================================ IV.People . . . This section lists the fictional people to be familiar with in CWP. President: Ian Walsh He will be a neutral with a slightly heelish characteristic. He will be involved in some angles. He will also be active. Commentators: David Forthright - David is the most knowledgeable commentator in the CWP. He is on every show. David is in his late 30's, is married, and has two kids. Seth Stevens - Seth is a wise-ass. He is in his ealry twenties and quite often refers to David as "Old man," and some obscene names, as well. Michael Russenhoff - Michael is less known to the public and works more behind the scenes with the booking committee. He is in his late 20's and down to earth. Donald Blackstone - He is a senior commentator and analyst. He is in his mid-50's and has studied wrestling for over 30 years. We are proud to have such a mind of the business. **He was also a manager in small federations after a brief stint as a wrestler in the late 60's. Ring Announcer - Deviant Soul Deviant is a massive man who is in his early 20's and a party animal. He has long blonde hair and stands a tall 6'9". He gets along with the fans and many other people. Basically, he's fun-loving. Reporter - Diana Freedman Diana is a gorgeous woman who likes to bear all, like a typical wrestling woman. Ah-ah, boys . . . she's taken by Devian Soul. Juggernaut - Head of security. He likes Diana Freedman, but she doesn't like him. Deviant Soul and Juggernaut are not friends because Juggernaut has a tendency to bother Diana. ============================================================================ V.Roster . . .
