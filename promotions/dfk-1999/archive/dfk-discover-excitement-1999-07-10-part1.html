=========================== BOUT REVIEW - Arena Report =========================== PROMOTION: Dream Factory K EVENT: Discover Excitement Tour VENUE: Tokyo Korkuen Hall CORRESPONDENT: Hideo Demura Christopher Reece's Dream Factory K made its debut tonight in the Mecca of purorseu, Tokyo's Korakuen Hall. The show took place before am enthusiastic sellout crowd of 2,150 which was treated to an exciting night of action.
The sellout had to be a relief to Reece since some experts had questioned the drawing power of his promotion's roster with only one true star performer and a generally young and inexperienced supporting cast, albeit most everyone on the roster shows great potential.
The sellout was no doubt helped by the promotional push Fighting TV Samurai! gave the event (which broadcast live on the network). While experts considered Shoji Suzuki as the only true draw on the event, following the show Reece had other thoughts. "I don't think [Suzuki] is the only draw. Gosho & Saotome also have alot of exposure here and I think it was the combination of those three along with the promising newcomers that drew the house. There's no doubt Suzuki is the main draw but I think the other guys helped the gate as well."
The ring had a nice techno look to it with black canvas trimmed in white, black cable ropes, red ring posts, and white corner pads with the Fighting TV
Samurai! logo on them. The ring apron was black with DFK in white letters in the center and the Samurai TV logo on either side.
The entranceway setup was a relatively simple one. Just your basic metal scaffolding entranceway with theatrical lights adorning the top and a black curtain sporting the simple DFK logo (a red circle trimmed in white with DFK wrote in the center in white letters).
The show opened with Christopher Reece coming to the ring, to a round of applause, and introducing referee Minoru Kurosawa, Head referee Kenichiro
Yamamoto, and official ring announcer... Sato of PJW fame. All three received polite rounds of applause from the crowd with the crowd going surprising wild for Sato who had somewhat of a cult following in the days of PJW.
Following those introductions it was time to officially introduce the entire
Dream Factory K roster with each wrestler making there way down the isle one by one.
The complete roster for this tour is as follows (in order of introduction): Brian Sczar, Kunihiro Kanemoto, Jun Yamamoto, "The Golden Lion" Mitsuhara
Misawa, Queero Bakemono, Jerry Brunk, Linus, Shinigami, Tim Anthony, Pierce
Bleed, Michael Darque, Heinosuke Gosho, Bandito Suicida, Yoshihiro Fujinami,
Oahu Sammy, Eiji Yatsu, Greg Gabbard, Riki Saotome, and Shoji Suzuki.
The crowd gave everyone introduced a polite round of applause with the exception of Yoshihiro Fujinami who was jeered heavily. The most notable responses went to Shoji Suzuki, Riki Saotome and the legend Jerry Brunk.
Following the introductions the wrestlers and officials lineup in two lines with Christopher Reece standing between the lines and faced towards the entranceway, above which a Japanese flag had been hung on the wall. The crowd joined the wrestlers in standing and facing the flag in preparation for the playing of the national anthem.
The preshow festivities continued following the anthem with Reece briefly addressing the crowd and the roster of DFK. Reece said that DFK was the realization of his dream, running a promotion, but that his dream isn't yet completely fulfilled. Reece said: "I didn't just dream of running a promotion of my own in Japan, my adopted homeland. I dreamed of producing a revolutionary promotion that leaves a lasting impact on the world of purorseu. Tonight the revolution of purorseu begins. Tonight the legacy of Dream Factory K begins."
Following Reece's speech (which showcased Reece's mastery of the Japanese language) the wrestlers and staff posed for pictures in the ring before clearing the ring and heading to the back. With the ring now empty the card was ready to officially begin.
The first match on the card pitted to Americans against one another in a first round Lion's Grand Prix tournament match. It was a historic as well as interesting match-up since both of the wrestlers are a little on the strange side.
Bleed was introduced first. A primal bellow boomed over the PA system followed by the punk beat of "Blood Orgy of the Atomic Fern" by the Dead Milkmen. Bleed then appeared in a flow of smoke backlit within the entranceway. As the song's lyrics began Bleed broke through the smoke running towards the ring, followed by Miss Misery who is dressed in Gothic style.
Bleed is a well muscled gaijin with multiply piercings in his face and a shaved head. His body is scared and features several tattoos. He wore a ripped black t-shirt with "Punk Your Ass" written across the front and the band logo of the
Inbreds across the back which he took off upon entering the ring. Bleed wrestled in a pair of loose fitting ragged, torn jeans and a pair of Doc
Martens. After making his way to the ring Bleed rolled under the bottom rope, sprung to his feet, performed what he calls the "Punk Stomp" around the ring, headbanging along the way.
Bleed then bounced off the ropes a few time before climbing the turnbuckle, cutting off his entrance music and yelling "Punk Yo' Ass!" to the crowd.
Sato announced Bleed as standing 6' 4", weighing in at 280lbs, and hailing from
Hell's Kitchen, New York, USA. Bleed drew boos from the crowd along with lightly scattered sounds of applause.
Darque then made his way to the ring "Sweet Dreams" by the Eurythmics as the lights dimmed in the arena. The sounds of the woman's moans then became slightly invasive over the entrance music as Darque emerged from the entranceway leading his "slave" towards the ring by a leash.
The slave, whomever she is, is a lovely Japanese woman, probably in her mid-twenties, with jet-black hair done in a tight ponytail, and was dressed in a very short skirt and a tight fitting leather corset. She kept her eyes downcast as he lead her to the ring, occasionally swatting her bare thigh with a riding crop when she moved too slowly for his tastes. As they reached the ring, Darque made the woman sit on the middle rope and hold up the top rope so he can enter.
Darque is a broad-chested, fairly tall man in his late twenties. He has dark black hair, trimmed very neatly that forms a widows-peak on his forehead. He also has a dark black goatee, well-manicured. His eyes are a very deep, ocean-blue. He is in very good shape physically, and although he doesn't have a bodybuilders "cut" physique, he still has very definite muscle tone.
Darque wore a long black leather duster that had lots of metal studs along the trim and metal spikes on the shoulders to the ring. This reporter has to admit that the duster certainly adds an aura of menace to Darque.
Once in the ring, the woman removed his leather duster and took the riding crop, then quickly dropped to her knees to kiss his feet before sliding out of the ring and taking her place in his corner, where she remained throughout the match.
Darque's wrestling attire consisted of black leather pants and wrestling boots with black leather bracers on his forearms. The boots and bracers had several buckles on them.
Sato introduced Darque as 6'4", 260 pounds, and wrestling out of Denver, Colorado, USA. The crowd gave Darque a mixed reaction, apparently unsure what to think of his "alternative" lifestyle. It was interesting to note that during Darque's entrance Bleed was shouting Dork at him which several in the crowd thought was funny while a few others actually picked up the chant.
Following Sato's announcement that the match, as well as all of the LGP 1st round matches, was one fall with a 30 minute time limit and a last minute, mid ring rules conference between the official for the match, Minoru Kurosawa, and the wrestlers, we were ready for action in the first ever DFK match.
*LION'S GRAND PRIX TOURNAMENT - FIRST ROUND MATCH* Match # 1 - Pierce Bleed v. Michael Darque Both men came to center ring and circled before tying up. Bleed controlled the tie-up and forced Darque into the corner. The ref stepped in and called for the break which Bleed appeared to be prepared to oblige but instead landed a headbutt (no doubt more effective due to the metal studs) which doubled Darque over the corner holding his face.
Bleed landed a few kicks and another headbutt, this time to the back of the head, before Irish whipping Darque across the ring, and hard into the corner.
Darque followed him in with a running knee lift to the chest. Bleed bounced off
Darque's chest, landing a few feet away, and caught Darque stumbling out of the corner with a DDT followed by a quick cover for a count of two. The crowd gave a very appreciative round of applause following the nicely executed sequence.
Following the near fall Bleed went right back on the offensive pulling Darque to a sitting position and applying a reverse chinlock, somewhat out of character given Bleed's bio doesn't portray him as much of a technician.
Darque slowly recovered from Bleed's early onslaught, turned into Bleed (forcing him to switch the chinlock to a side headlock), and made it to his feet. Darque pushed Bleed off and into the ropes. Darque went for a lariat coming out but Bleed ducked and attempted to catch him turning around with a forearm to the face but Darque ducked and caught Bleed spinning around with a dragon screw leg whip sending Bleed to the mat to the 'ahs' of the crowd.
Bleed made it back to his feet quickly and charged Darque only to be caught with a knee to the stomach followed by a uranage suplex that sent Bleed crashing hard to the canvas. Bleed showed his resiliency popping up quickly from the uranage only to be caught by a charging bleed with an ultra stiff lariat that turned Bleed inside out on his way down. Darque went for a quick cover but got only a two count and the 'ahs' of the crowd followed by a round of applause.
Darque pulled a groggy Bleed to his feet and pushed him into the corner. Darque landed a hard knife edge chop to the 'ohs' of the crowd. Darque then backed away and paused a moment before delivering another chop followed by a series of
3 more chops. Darque then pulled Bleed out of the corner and went for a swinging neck breaker, but Bleed spun through it and landed a quick kick to the midsection followed by a tremendous gutwrench powerbomb into a pinning combination for a close two and three-quarters count which brought the crowd to there feet.
Bleed quickly rolled to his feet, looking slightly frustrated, and landed a series of hard stomps to Darque's head and upper body. Appearing to have relieved his frustration with the stomps, Bleed pulled Darque to his feet and lifted the big man high above his head in a gorilla press, with one hand around
Darque's throat and the other on his crotch. Bleed held Darque in the air for several seconds before him down to the mat completing the move which he calls
"Morrissey Sucks." The move looked very impressive on a man the size of Darque.
Darque sat up in pain from the slam, but managed to roll to the floor outside of the ring before Bleed could continue his attack. Darque's rest for short lived however, as Bleed did a baseball slide under the bottom rope and nailed him from behind with a spinning back elbow to the back of the head. The impact of the blow sent Darque face first into the ring post.
Darque fell to his knees on the floor holding his face and the back of his head. Bleed approached from behind and landed a hard forearm to the back of
Darque's head and then pulled him to his feet with by his hair. Bleed then whipped Darque hard to the black guardrailing which surrounded the ring. The force of the move nearly sent Darque flying over the guardrail and into the crowd. Bleed followed Darque in with a kneelift and then grabbed the groggy
Darque and rolled him back in the ring.
Back in the ring Bleed appeared ready to end things as he pulled Darque to his feet and backed him into the corner. Bleed then lifted Darque up onto his shoulder for a powerslam. Bleed charged full speed across the ring and hit a wicked powerslam near the other corner, rolling to his feet as he hit the move.
The move, which he calls the Marathon Mugger, brought an 'ah' from the crowd. Bleed quickly turned around and cradled Daruqe for a near fall which was broken up at the last second when Darque got his hand on the bottom rope.
Following the near fall Bleed wasted no time in rolling Darque over onto his stomach out of the cradle. Bleed then pulled the stunned Darque to his feet and locked on his Dragon Rage finisher, which is a one armed dragon sleeper with the other arm throwing closed fist punches to the opponents chest and ribcage.
The move brought the fans to there feet in anticipation of the finish via submission, but after struggling in the move for nearly a minute Darque was able to fight his way to the ropes. Even with Darque draping his foot over the middle rope and the ref calling for the break Bleed refused to release the hold which brought boos from the crowd.
Darque took matters into his own hands, or feet as it would be, placed his foot on the second rope and propelled himself over the top off Bleed, flipping out of the hold to the 'ahs' of the crowd and reversing the hold into a dragon sleeper of his own in the process. Darque quickly picked Bleed up out of the dragon sleeper onto his shoulder, did a quick spin, and hit a nasty death valley driver to the 'ohs' of the crowd.
The move, called The Domination Driver, dropped Bleed directly on the back of his head/neck and drew an 'ah' from the crowd. The crowd came to there feet in anticipation of a pinfall as Darque covered Bleed, but Bleed was able to barely get his shoulder up before the 3 count. The crowd let out a collective 'oh'
which was followed by a round of applause. Darque came to his feet, undaunted, and pulled Bleed to his feet as well. Darque shot Bleed to the ropes and went for a lariat but Bleed ducked and rebounded off the ropes again with a flying forearm attempt which Darque side stepped. Bleed crashed to the mat but came back to his feet quickly only for
Darque to grab his hand and pull him toward him, with Darque catching him with a high angle Liger bomb which drew another 'ah' from the crowd. Darque held his position from the Liger bomb for the pinfall which brought the crowd to there feet to give both men several rounds of applause.
WINNER - Michael Darque at 10:03 Overall this was a great back and fourth opening match that showcased both men's skills. Darque's performance was impressive, as was Bleed's, even in defeat, as he showed a great deal of heart. Both men look to have bright futures in DFK.
The card kept rolling as only minutes after the opening match Sato entered the ring to introduce the second match of the evening, another LGP 1st rd match pitting journeyman Heinosuke Gosho against Tim Anthony.
Gosho was introduced first. He is a large bulky man with traditional oriental facial features. Gosho is balding, which reveals several scars on his forehead, but wears his hair long He has a full beard, which he keeps quite short.
Gosho entered to an instrumental version of "Bombing The L" by Fun Lovin' Criminals. He walked to the ring with a towel over his shoulders, which he threws into the crowd after reaching the ring. As he walked he appeared to be talking to himself while focused on the ring ahead of him.
Gosho wore a yellow unitard, with black trimming, yellow knee pads, and black boots. He was introduced as weighing in at 307lbs, standing 6'2", and making his residence in Tokyo. The crowd greeted Gosho warmly with several rounds of applause and scattered chants and calls of "Gosho".
Anthony's entrance immediately followed. The american has a clean shaven "Adonis" face with a strong jaw, silky auburn brown hair that he keeps short and tamed, which is matched with a pair of cinnamon colored eyes. He has an athletic, well defined, body that's compact. His skin is of a "honey" color or golden brown.
Anthony's manager Deirdre is a fiery red headed woman with a slender athletic body and creamy white skin. She has emerald green eyes and a sharp delicate nose.
Anthony emerged from the entranceway with Deirdre by his side to "Bummer" by
Monster Magnets. He greeted the fans with a sly smile as he made his way to the ring high fiving fans as he went. After reaching the ring he hoped onto the apron and helped Deirdre make her way up the ring steps and parts the ropes for her to enter the ring. Anthony followed her in with a vault over the top rope.
Anthony wore black tights that reached to his ankles and sported a design of green flames from the ankle to his hip. He also wore black wrestling boots and black elbow pads.
The gaijin was introduced as hailing from the United States by way of Lansing,
Illinois, standing 6'2" and weighing in at 230 pounds. The crowd gave Anthony a polite round of applause, which has to be considered a nice reception for a new comer to the purorseu scene.
With the introductions out of the way the crowd rose to there feet anticipating the start of the match.
*LION'S GRAND PRIX TOURNAMENT - FIRST ROUND MATCH* Match # 2 - Heinosuke Gosho v. Tim Anthony Both men came to center ring and locked up with the bigger Gosho controlling and quickly forcing Anthony into the ropes with the ref calling for a break.
The two locked up again following the break and with Gosho again controlling and forcing Anythony into the corner. Before the ref could call for the break
Anythony scaled the ropes to a sitting position on the top rope, still locked up with Gosho. Even with the added leverage of standing on the second rope
Anthony couldn't budge Gosho. The ref stepped in and forced the break with
Gosho backing away slowly towards center ring.
As Gosho was backing away to center ring Anthony leaped from the second rope with a flying shoulder block but was still unable to send Gosho down, instead finding himself f on the canvas. As Anthony came to his feet Gosho smiled and pounded his chest, indicating for Anthony to try again. The crowd came to its feet with a collective 'oh' as Anthony bounced off and charged Gosho with another shoulder block attempt. This time he was ale to move Gosho slightly.
Anthony went to the ropes again and charged again, this time with a lariat which made solid contact to Gosho chest and staggered him backwards again but still not down.
Gosho smiled again and called for Anthony to try again. Anthony charged the ropes and shot off, bypassing Gosho and shooting off the opposite side with a quick rabbit lariat to the back of Gosho's neck. The lariat seemed to serve for to anger Gosho more than anything.
Anthony wasted no time after hitting the rabbit lariat to continue this sprint, hit the ropes again, and charge with another shoulder block. This time he was met by Gosho with a thunderous lariat which leveled Anthony to the mat and drew a tremendous roar from the crowd.
Gosho wasted no time in pulling Anthony back to his feet and shooting him to the ropes. The big man attempted to catch Anthony coming off with a boot to the chest but Anthony slide under and met the big man turning around with a swinging neck breaker.
The crowd applauded Anthony's efforts as the American headed through the ropes and stood on the apron waiting for Gosho to come to his feet.
Anthony only waited long enough for the big man to make it to his knees before springing to the top rope and hitting a crisp missile dropkick squarely into
Gosho's chest. The dropkick sent the big man hard back to the mat and made the crowd take notice with a collective 'ah'.
Anthony followed up quickly by pulling a daised Gosho to his feet and leading him back to the corner. Anthony then went to the second rope and hit a beautiful tornado DDT that brought an 'ah' from the crowd. Anthony followed up the hot move with a cover which Gosho kicked out of at two.
Anthony backed to the ropes and waited for the bigger Gosho to make it to his feet. Anthony then charged and nailed him with a lariat which sent Gosho staggering backwards and into the ropes. Gosho rebound groggily off the ropes and was met by Anthony with a kick to the midsection which double the big man over. Anthony then struggled but was finally able to execute a powerbomb to the surprise of the crowd. The move clearly illustrated Anthony's strength and drew an 'oh' from the crowd followed by a round of applause.
Anthony choose not to go for a cover and instead positioned Gosho parallel to the nearest corner and then headed towards the ropes. Anthony went to the top rope and hit a text book frog splash as the crowd 'oh'ed. The move nearly earned Anthony the victory as Gosho was able to kickout at 2 and 3-quarters.
Anthony then struggled to bring a hurting Gosho to his feet and shot him into the ropes. Anthony went for a crucifix pinning combination, but Gosho was able to hold up Anthony's weight on his shoulders and prevent him from taking him down. Gosho stumbled awkwardly back towards the corner, attempting to slam
Anthony into the corner but Anthony was able to miss the corner pad and ended up laying on the top rope.
With Gosho propped up in the corner trying to get his breath, Anthony stepped down to the second rope behind Gosho and hooked on a nifty modified abdominal stretch while still partially standing on the second rope. The innovative move drew an 'oh' followed by an energetic round of applause from the crowd.
The ref called for Anthony to break the hold but Anthony managed to avoid DQ by stepping off the second rope and forcing Gosho to carry all his weight and then back onto the rope. Finally the ref gave up and instead concentrated on asking
Gosho if he submitted. After having the hold locked on around a minute and a half Gosho let out a loud yell and powered out of the hold, countering it by hip tossing Anthony from the second rope hard to the mat. The ultra high hip toss brought the crowd to its feet in applause.
Anthony landed hard on his back and setup in pain. He pauses briefly, clutching his back, before rolling to his feet only to be met head on by a charging Gosho with a huge lariat. The impact of the blow sent Anthony head over heels, cutting a complete flip before hitting the mat face down. The crowd gasped and
'ah'ed at the move with a chant of 'Gosho, Gosho' starting up following the lariat.
Gosho then brought Anthony to his feet and whipped him across the ring, hard into the corner. Gosho then followed him in with a big splash. Gosho backed out of the corner and waited as Anthony fell out of the corner. Gosho caught him coming out with a bulldog. The big man followed up with a cover but Anthony kicked out at two in a great near fall which surprised the crowd.
Gosho then brought Anthony to his feet and sent him right back down with a hard body slam. Gosho pulled his thumb across his throat, signaling to the crowd that Anthony was finished, before climbing to the top rope. The crowd came to there feet in anticipation. Gosho hit a nice moonsault, especially for a big man, but Anthony was able to move out of the way and Gosho crashed to the mat hard. The crowds roars quickly become a collective gasp and 'ah' went Gosho missed the big move.
With Gosho sitting on his knees, doubled over holding his chest and stomach,
Anthony wasted no time in going to the outside, grabbing the top rope, and hitting a sling shot Guillotine Legdrop that sent Gosho face fist into the mat.
The crowd 'ah'ed as Anthony rolled Gosho over and covered him. Anthony appeared to have the match won but Gosho got his shoulder up at the last second for a very dramatic near fall. The crowd went nuts following the near fall with some fans chanting 'Gosho', some 'Anthony', and others applauding.
Anthony paused to gather himself before continuing his assult. Anthony brought
Gosho to his feet, stepped beside him, and hit a nice side russian legsweep, rolling through for a cover. Gosho kicked out at 2 and a half.
Anthony came to his feet again, looking somewhat frustrated. He slowly pulled A very groggy and almost out of it Gosho to his feet and whipped him into the ropes. Anthony attempted to catch Gosho coming off with a belly to belly suplex, using Gosho's weight to generate extra momentum, but wasn't quite able to get him over and end up falling to the mat with Gosho landing on top of him.
Before the ref could get in position to make a count Gosho had rolled off of
Anthony and was trying to compose himself on the mat next to him. Both men moved groggily on the mat trying to gather themselves as the ref began to count. Gosho made it to his feet first at the count of 6, Anthony followed at the count of 8.
Gosho met Anthony as he made it back to his feet with a hard round house right hand that appeared to knock Anthony silly. Gosho draped the groggy Anthony over his back and hit a devastating Saoman drop. Gosho lay on top of Anthony for a cover and got a very close near fall. Following the kickout the crowd applauded as both Gosho and Anthony attempted to recover from the punishment both had suffered in the match.
Gosho made it to his feet and dragged a still groggy Anthony to his feet from a kneeling position. Gosho then kicked Anthony in the stomach, doubling him over, and pulled him into powerbomb position. Gosho doubled over in exhaustion before composing himself and hitting a bigtime powerbomb. Gosho cradled Anthony for the pinfall as the crowd 'ah'ed.
WINNER - Heinosuke Gosho at 11:56 The crowd gave a tremendous ovation to Gosho following his win and a few minutes later gave both men a strong round of applause. A very solid match.
Anthony showed alot of athleticism and resilience but in the end Gosho's size and experience was too much for him to handle. Anthony had to adjust his game somewhat to compensate for Gosho's size advantage so I'm greatly looking forward to see more of Anthony's arsenal tomorrow night against Brian Sczar and on future DFK cards.
Gosho showed good athleticism and fairly good movement for a big man in his DFK debut. He looks like a strong force in the LGP. No doubt the addition of the
Nippon Dream to his legacy would only strengthen his unlikely cult following.
