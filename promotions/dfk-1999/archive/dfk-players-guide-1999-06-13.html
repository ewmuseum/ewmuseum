What is Dream Factory K? ------------------------------------------- Dream Factory K, also known as DFK, is a small wrestling promotion based in
Japan, designed to develop young talent for Gunryo Pro. DFK is similar in style and purpose to the current Toryumon promotion in Japan as well as previous talent development promotions in Japan such as Wrestle Yum Factory and
Wrestle Y Gym. DFK runs events across Japan in various venues. Results are booked according to angles, roleplays, and strategies.
---------------------------------------------- 2. Success Through Dedication ---------------------------------------------- Success Through Dedication is the motto of DFK. In other words, the more you put into the fed and your character, the more you'll get out of it. If you're looking to obtain victory in DFK you must be willing to regularly roleplay, communicate with other handlers and the league president in order to work out storylines, etc., and send in strategies for your matches in order to win. The more thought that goes into the roleplays, strats, and angles you develop/send-in, the more time the staff will take to make sure that the aspects you want to get across are portrayed in matches.
----------------------------- 3. Getting Matches ----------------------------- The President of Dream Factory K is responsible for making the matches on all
DFK cards. Frequently, two handlers challenge each other to a match. If both handlers are in favor of the match, then it will generally be made, and take place as soon as possible.
Most times, however, wrestlers will be paired together in matches by the President due to ranking in the fed, past conflicts (i.e. a time limit draw the previous week, wrestler A pinning wrestler B in a Tag Match, wrestler A attacking wrestler B following a match last week, etc.), and various other factors.
------------------------------------------------ 4. How Matches Are Determined ------------------------------------------------ As touched upon earlier, DFK is a booked e-fed. Winners are chosen by the president and his consultants based on quality of roleplays, strategies, and participation in the development of angles. Often this process is EXTREMELY difficult. But you'll never hear the staff complaining about having to make difficult decisions, since that is an indication that the league is very competitive. There's an almost 100% chance that at sometime during your DFK experience, you'll disagree with a decision in the outcome of a match. This happens. Handle it in a mature manner, please. Yelling at the staff will get you nowhere besides fired, generally. Regardless, we hope that no matter what the outcome of your matches, you'll still enjoy your run in Dream Factory K.
------------ 5. Titles ------------ Currently only one championship is reconized in Dream Factory K: 1. The Nippon Dream: The top wrestler in Dream Factory K.. The title must be defended at least once every tour. This title defense will be scheduled by the
President. Additional title defenses can be scheduled by the champion through the President.
-------------------------------------- 6. Rankings & Title Shots -------------------------------------- Title shots in DFK are based upon rankings, although from time to time angles will play a part in the reception of title shots.
Rankings are based upon a variety of things, including: Winn/Loss record, push
(determined by Roleplays & strats), and current angles. After each week of tours, a DFK update, including singles rankings will be released. Title shots will be based upon these rankings, however, don't forget that title shots can also be granted by the champion to any wrestler at any time.
------------- 7. Cards ------------- While other e-feds present weekly cards, DFK instead presents monthly tours. These tours are generally between 1-3 weeks in length and feature between 3-5 cards per week. Generally one tour will take place each month. We feel this system is vastly more realistic to the Japanese schedule, and, in fact, more interesting in the long run.
Each tour will also feature at least one major card which will take place at a major venue and feature several highly anticipated matches.
These cards will be released in summary format. In this system cards are summarized in the form of newspaper style reports detailing the specific details of each match and the card as a whole.
--------------------- 8. Roleplaying --------------------- Roleplaying is the biggest determining factor in deciding the outcomes of matches in DFK. To roleplay simply e-mail flashes to:
[name withheld] . Roleplays should be quality flashes and not just simple one paragraph statements consisting of 5 lines of dialogue. All talent participating on upcoming cards are expected to roleplay.
Roleplays are judged on quality and not quanity. Therefore, take your time and produce at least one quality roleplay each week. There's no need to rush out two roleplays that ramble on without purpose just to put out a long roleplay.
If you have been scheduled for a match and are unable to roleplay due to time constraints, please inform the President so that any needed adjustments or changes can be made.
Roleplays will be released in a flash packet called "Warrior Spirits" each week. Since cards will not take place every week, handlers aren't required to roleplay during off-tour weeks, they are however expected to roleplay before the start of each week on-tour.
Handlers are expected to roleplay once per week Handlers can roleplay twice a week, once on Warrior Spirts and an in-ring or backstage interview can also be conducted for tour cards.
------------- 9. Strats ------------- Due to DFK's touring schedule where wrestlers participate in 3-5 matches per week, detailed strats are not expected for each match. A short strategy is encouraged for any match your wrestler is in, and are factored into the booking of the match. Strats also help the staff to portray your character more closely to the way you've intended.
At the beginning of each on-tour week handlers are expected to submit a small strat sheet to the president of the league containing small, 1-3 paragraph, strats for each match his wrestler will be involved in in the coming weeks.
For major shows on the tour wrestlers are *strongly* encouraged to submit a seperate detailed strat.
These strats can include a paragraph describing your characters entrance, general strategy, offense and defense, mindset, spots, & whatever else you'd like to include.
Post and pre-match angles may also be included in strats. ------------------------------ 10. The Staff of DFK ------------------------------ ACTUAL ------------- President - [name withheld] - [name withheld] FICTIONAL ----------------- Christopher Reece - The President and producer of Dream Factory K. Reece, former Pro Japan Wrestling and Super Hyper Visual Fighting broadcaster, is the founder of DFK. He has long be noted in the industry as having a keen eye for talent and now, with the help of G-Pro, he's able to combine that eye with his passion for wrestling into DFK.
At this point, the rest of the staff is really insignificant. Should the need arise, profiles of other staff members may be added to this guide.
------------------------------------- 11. Application Analyasis ------------------------------------- When filling out the application below to join Dream Factory K, please take your time and be as descriptive as possible. Your application is the main piece of information used when writing matches involving your character. Virtually every aspect of the match is taken from your application or strat. Therefore it is VERY important that you take your time when filling out the application.
Take special note of each section of the application, to ensure that the match writers are able to fully understand your wrestlers personality and ring style.
