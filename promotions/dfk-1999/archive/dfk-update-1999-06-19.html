Update 6-19-99

19 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The roster is shoring up nicely talent wise, although it is very heel heavy, and I fully expect to be able to hold the first tour as planned beginning in July. If you know of anyone who might be interested in the league please pass the Player's Guide onto them or have them email me. As of now I'll begin accepting roleplays with the first packet going out next Saturday. All handlers need to go ahead get an introductory roleplay in and possibly begin to setup some angles and/or tag team or trio team alliances. If you need to contact a handler contact me and I'll be glad to give you their email address. Below you'll be able to find the complete DFK roster, some preliminary information on the first tour (including tentative lineups for the first week of the tour), and the bracket for the Lion's Grand Prix tournament which will determine the first Nippon Dream champion. I've been very creative with the lineups for the tour and hopefully you'll be happy and intrigued with what your character has going on. The ongoing tournament makes it difficult to book the cards in some respects but I've done my best to give everyone fair and equal treatment. After the first week of the tour I'll sit down and hammer out the lineups for the remainder of the tour based on performances in the first week, etc.. I'm going to try and get some newspaper style articles on DFK's opening and first tour this week to help build some interest in the fed and give you an idea of what to expect. If you have any questions, comments, concerns, etc. please feel free to contact me.
