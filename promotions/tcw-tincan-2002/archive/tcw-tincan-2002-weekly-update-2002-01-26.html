+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ TCW Weekly Updates 25th January ======================================= Table Of Contents 1. News And Rumours 2. Signings 3. Upcoming Events 4. Miscellaneous ======================================= News & Rumours Damn, damn, damn... That little stunt [think] 'Sex Pistol' Jaime Roberts has cost TCW an arm and a leg. At least. A passer by heard Saunders suggesting each of his roster give a kidney to help TCW stay afloat.
But then of course he remembered that little clause in Roberts' contract, that is to say that he isn't working for a paid contract. In effect, this is charity work for Roberts and to put it bluntly...at the Satan's Children gig
Roberts was just another drug addled fan...
...so, He's going to be putting up all that money from the good ol' days to bail himself out.
Francis Vaclav Grande has been running around all week talking about what a superb mat technician he is, liar. This hasn't exactly gone down to well with the TCW roster. Grande is fast finding himself on the outside of the business he should be getting 'inside' knowledge for. Saunders is still hoping that Grande will be of some use, after all, he is writing a book.
Gotta be publicity in that. And talking of publicity, Roberts' stunt has pretty much killed any interest in Kansas City for quite a while, luckily Satan's Children aren't a 'gold disc' selling band so media coverage has been local only. That's not to say it won't be picked up...
Bungle's dig at Saunders hasn't gone un-noticed, expect him to be curtain jerking in Gettysburgh.
Fugitive continues to turn on the style in all his matches, the brutal hardcore match has, so far, been the most successful of TCW's tenure. Many
fans required about buying Fugitives 'Satans Children vs TCW' style T-Shirts. Speaking of Fugitive, The Thug On The Run had a tooth knocked out by Bungle as well as the usual cuts and bruises. It looks as if he will be sticking it out toothless due to money problems.
Bungles bump hurt, a lot. The Colts work ethic keeps improving, despite his rough treatment of Devlin its pretty certain that if he can hone his talents and bring his charisma across in the ring he will have a bright future. He certainly has the look.
Nobody can tell if Roberts is a good thing or a bad things. He draws but...well...he f*cks up as well.
Bubba Mckenzie has rejoined TCW after having his wages suspended, he is on his last warning before permanent expulsion and of course, the bad press that goes with it.
Former TCW headliner 'The Lost Boy' Jonathan Darling has been signed to a developmental contract with a well known federation.
Signings Coming "The Chief" Jack Savant - A 24 year veteran of the game, Savant is a wily old timer who just can't give it up.
Going [*]Bubba McKenzie - Last Chance Upcoming Event Febuary 3rd 2001 Gettysburgh, Pennsylvania Its back to Pennsylvania as James Saunders has booked out the Gettysburgh
Campus Main Hall of the Harrisburgh Area Community College for Gettysburgh
Address, TCW's first big event, you can all expect the budget to be pushed out that little bit further as Saunders tries to snare a TV deal for his company. We may even see that smoke machine. This show will be promoted from the beginning of the month so as to garner the maximum crowd, which could be as many as 650. With the previous show in Reading, TCW expects to have a small fan base of wrestling fans.
Scheduled show start is at 7pm and finish at 9pm Places Of Interest Various Battlefields Farnsworth House Civil War Mourning Theatre Gettysburgh Scenic Railway Susquehanna River boat The Campus This show is the culmination of the last month, it should cater for every fans interest. TCW has already held a show in PA so expect some familiar faces - remember the last show was a Town Fair.
Line-Up 'Playboy' Bubba McKenzie vs Bungle Bubba finds his way back to the TCW Tour bus this week where he will get to face the man that everyone fears, for all the wrong reasons.
Robert Devlin vs 'The Chief' Jack Savant Devlin looks unsure of himself against rookie opponents, Saunders sees the potential and wants to build Devlin up. Who better than the newcomer but
old-timer to help him along. 'Sex Pistol' Jaime Roberts and Francis Vaclav Grande vs Fugitive and The
Colt Grande hasn't shut up about beating Roberts since the dark day itself, he's pissed off everyone ESPECIALLY Fugitive. Roberts is paired with the little runt for his sins.
RP Deadline - Saturday Feb 2nd Midnight Strat Deadline - Thursday Jan 31st Midnight ++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ TCW is a federation based on the adaptation wrestlers must show for individual circumstances. Due to limited funds the interviews / flashes can only be filmed in certain places [ Listed under Places Of Interest ] and also the Tour Bus and in front of the TCW Logo Board.
In addition to these restrictions the roster must also cater for the audience to gain the biggest fan base, a show in an O.A.P. home will have a different feel to a show at a Death Metal Concert.
Participants must set out to :- a] Gain the fan interest via their promo's b] Be creative and original in their restrictions c] Produce a style of match most accessible to the audience.
Those who regularly achieve these goals will be rewarded with extra 'cash' with which to tailor themselves. The money may be spent on an alternate rp location, a more elaborate entrance, a new ring attire, more freedom in matches etc.
