What is The Tin Can? 1.1 Fantasy Explanation TCW is a promotion run on the basis that they will 'play' anywhere that pays. School fetes, supermarket openings, before a college football game.
The fed has no aspirations of greatness and is primarily used by new kids looking to gain experience, people looking to earn a few extra dollars and of course the old guys who've been their, done that but can't let go. TCW's biggest asset is its tour bus which is pretty much its only asset. A 15ft by
15ft ring and a small sound system also come into the equation. The TCW only looks to recoup its losses at its events, but be warned, the contracts are verbal and if you don't pull your weight you may very well find yourself looking for another journeyman's fed to earn your dollar.
1.2 Regular Explanation TCW is just a bit on the side for everybody involved, I don't see anybody solely roleplaying in TCW, rather guys who have a bit of free time at their disposal and want to fill it up. This fed is set up for you guys to have fun with, you have to adapt to the schedule, the time slots and the audience to pull something off. If you do you get a little bit of extra cash in your back pocket to work on a flashier roleplay or a smoke machine in your entrance!
2. TCW is run by long time sports fan James Saunders and a motley crew of minimum wage workers. For Saunders it is a full time job but if it ever stops being rewarding he has a business degree to fall back on.
3. The TCW is run out of James Saunders' office at his home in Florida, bookings will come in every couple of days via telephone, email and perhaps even the odd one via...da...da..daaa...the post.
All TCW shows are videotaped as part of a reference tape library. Should TCW somehow acquire a TV deal these will be valuable tools in giving the fed a background. These tapes are later edited with promo's from the wrestlers recorded at the event itself.
TCW has limited time on its shows, sometimes as little as a single hour to give the fans their moneys worth. This plays a crucial role in the matches and subsequent promo's, a show at a village fete would have to be more toned down than that of a late night show and so forth. The participants must find a way to get themselves over in the shortest amount of time and adapt themselves to their surroundings and audience.
As far as what you guys will see, well, Im not going to be writing vast cards because I don't have the time, sorry folks. Unless you guys volunteer they are going to be recaps and highlight shows. But before you all run away, this fed is about how you can adapt to scenarios. Incidentally, the highlights will be good;-)
4. Where is the TCW based? The TCW goes wherever it is wanted, the tour bus has an onboard office for Saunders to work his magic. 5. How are matches decided? The TCW is a booked league, we do not utilise simulators, dice, cards, snakes and ladders or over complicated computer programs. We decide it all by ourselves with that little known about human organ, the brain. There are three factors that will affect the outcome of the match, win or lose it has something to do with: - 1) Flashes 2)
Strategies 3) Participation. Don't panic, if you read on a little bit more this will all be explained.
6. How do I succeed in the TCW? We told you to keep reading. 6.1 Flashes or Roleplays, both the same thing but we have to cater for all the difficult types out there. This is what will put you on the map, roleplays are the only thing that other people will get to see from you.
This is of MASSIVE importance in deciding the match outcome and the future. In a one off match the person with the better roleplay wins. The thing that makes TCW different is that you cannot bust out a rp set on a Mongolian mountain because, well, dammit, that costs money. TCW fits its promo's to its situation and therefore you guys have got limited locations: -
1] The TCW Tour Bus and all that comes with it. 2] In Front of the TCW curtain which can be draped over just about anything. 3] The show location, now this could be a village fete or a school disco. This is where you can get more creative folks, there are almost endless possibilities to these and each week they will be different. I like creativity, get creative.
4] On the show itself, in the ring, do your thing but don't you dare take up to much time. So there you have it, TCW is about getting yourself loved or hated with the fans as quickly as possible. As time goes by you might get a reputation but working all over the country it's unlikely you can count on this straight away. TCW has a 1 rp a week limit, heck, Im in no fantasy world. This fed is just a bit of fun and you good people dont want to have to worry about keeping up with a sporadic poster do you? If you really want you can do an on card flash as well but not all of these will get aired.
6.2 Strats. Some people love them, some people hate them. They can be a pain in the arse. But, when in TCW you do them. They aren't your usual strat however, they wont decide your matches but they will help you get the match you want. One novelty is that you get to write your own ending to the match in accordance with the surroundings, find something appropriate and you may well see it end your match. Similarly if you produce a strat for a match that the fans will like you may well be rewarded with a bit of extra cash for either an improved entrance or a rp in a different location. The better you do on your flashes and angles the more generous Mr Saunders will be with his wallet.
6.3 Angle Ideas. Well, TCW works all around the country so its unlikely that one village fete in Orlando will know what happened at a Supermarket opening in Texas. Saying that, if you can come up with a spark that can work in one show GO FOR IT. Its all about what you can pull off folks. If it takes off and gets a good reception that Saunders wallet may be opening up for you to do it again, bigger and better. TCW may hold shows in the same city for a few consecutive days, you can run something longer over those. You have to look at the schedule, look at the audience and adapt, adapt, adapt.
6.4 Misc. Stuff . Lets get one thing out now, you are not going to have a
178-0-0 record. You will lose. Its pretty much that simple. The true champions are people who accept this as part of the game. The people who participate with anticipation and good will. After all, this is only a game isn't it? I like nice people, I don't like egocentric, psychotic maniacs who are likely to hunt me down and beat me with a dead fish because they lost a match. Attitude is a very important factor, participation is a very important factor. We don't want people who are going to quit to go elsewhere after a week, we want people who are dedicated to the cause and take it all in good spirits. I know this fed is stupid, but its stupid in a good way!
7. What are the shows that TCW runs? 7.1 TCW runs shows where it can get them, no TV deal means no stupid name. Its just a TCW show with a date next to it. TCW gets contracts all over the country and will aim to make a bit of money to perhaps hire somewhere out for its bi-monthly Big Event. The shows may last 1 hour, 2 hours, 3 hours or be going all damn day if thats what is required. They may even be booked 3 consecutive nights in the same city, park, caravan site. Who knows, its a lottery, adapt, adapt, adapt!
7.2 Pay Per View. Um, well, its not on TV so its not really a pay per view. Well, the fans have to pay. Hmmm. Anyway, this is the show TCW might crack out a bit more money for and really pull out the stops for. Remember that smoke machine folks, well, here it is! Bear in mind, all PPV's are shown to
TV executives so if you guys pull it out TCW may get a spot of media coverage.
8. Who are the personalities of the TCW? 8.1 James Saunders likes wrestling plain and simple. He also likes money. Moving in to wrestling promotion seems like the obvious step to take so he does it. The man behind everything, you have a problem contact him, you want a bit of extra money, contact him. Your wifes sleeping around, contact him.
8.2 Wes Griffin is the head road agent. That is to say he drives the bus. 8.3 Mark Davis is a highly skilled amateur photographer, he's here to video the matches basically.
8.4 The referees don't damn well matter, we all know that, usually picked off of the street, TCW has one ageing ref who has worked with all the greats.
? Jeff Maiden. What more do you want to know? 9. What titles does the TCW maintain? 9.1 TCW Heavyweight Title is the best a man in TCW can get. Thats not saying much but it is a sign of status. If you hold this you can get a bit more cash in your pocket to do with as you will.
9.2 TCW Cruiserweight Title. Everybody loves a good cruiserweight match, these usually kick off the shows with a bang. To win this? Just twizzle.
9.3 TCW Tag Team Titles. For those of you who like to work hand in hand with another monster of the wrestling business. Um, yeah.
10.1 What are the main policies of the TCW? 10.1 Flash Policy. OK, you have to roleplay, its that simple. You apply, you roleplay unless you give me a reason why not. There is a 1 rp limit because that's what I told you. A weekly flash packet will be sent out the day of the event. The TCW plays to a wide range and has no set identity. You want to get XtRemE ( or however it is now spelt) you don't do it in front of a nursery school. You want to film inside a church, well, unless TCW is playing near a church tough. You have to adapt to the audience and the location, that's what I want to see.
9.3 Strategy Policy. Same as with the rp's basically. You will not be sending in a strat laced with chair-shots if TCW is working an O.A.P home.
Its just not right dammit! You need to cater for your audience, give me something that can get you over to the audience. A drunken stag party will want to see something to make them cringe. A Village Fete won't. You also get to write the last moments of the match, if it works I'll use it, so think about that as well.
9.4 Angle Policy. Angles can run over a single show, a series of shows or over a year ( if you can figure out how the hell to do that with TCW restrictions). If you are drawing heat you will get more leniency as to what you can do, you may be able to swear in front of the school kids, you may be able to use a chair in front of them but its got to be worth it. James
Saunders doesn't want to get sued damn it.
9.5 Booking Policy. This is very, very simple. The better you are doing the higher up the card you are likely to be, got some cash, got some heat you may well find yourselves at the pinnacle of e-wrestling. Headlining the MSG
Gift Shop Carpark.
10 . TCW is a mail fed because running a website takes ages, I know, I run them in my alternate life. I aim to get weekly updates out to you. You can expect to see the forthcoming schedule, the crowd expectancy, the locations etc etc as well as news and rumours.
12. If this propaganda has grabbed your attention the application process is relatively simple. Go and fill out an application form found on the site and send it to us. Bear in mind that not everyone will get accepted, some people may have to wait a few days/weeks/months for a spot on the roster to open up. Despite all out good intentions we cannot maintain the wants and desires of 679 members of the roster.
