++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ TCW January 23rd Recorded As part Of TCW Tape Archives Kansas City, Missouri ================================================== What do you get when you cross Missouri, A death metal band and several thousand crazed Goths?
The site for TCW's next show of course. Tin Can Wrestling takes its roadshow to Missouri to warm up the fans at the latest Satan's Children gig. The band famous for proclaiming 'We Sacrifice
Small Cildren' are said to be big fans of all things violence related. That is why, tonight, TCW goes XtReMe! [Thats how all the hip kids spell it nowadays.]

City. The result? Things started off badly as Roberts tripped over some of the rigging and lost his microphone into the crowd. He snatched a mic from Mickey Cockrake and stumbled his way through half the song in a drunken stupor before being distracted by a rather buxom, but also rather illegal crowd surfer.
Just when you thought things couldn't get any worse he grabs her from the security team and hoists her up onto the stage, a moment of wild ecstasy for the girl [onstage with Satan's Children, who wouldn't be joyous] is turned into a life changing incident as Roberts belches in her face and sticks his vodka encased tongue down her throat.
Some of the crew run on and separate Roberts from his prey, a fist fight ensues but somehow Roberts comes out on top, he lurches towards the girl and rips her clothes away to reveal, well, not much. The topless girl is chased around the ring by Roberts before he grabs her and throws her down into the moshpit.
As if nothing has happened, Roberts picks up the mic and continues...a completely different song.
He looks around the band before slurring out some insults, squares up to
Cesspool and slaps him across the face...
___THHHAAAWAAAANNNNGGGG___ A swift electric guitar to the head of Roberts and all hell breaks loose, fans storm the stage, Roberts gets a DAMN good kicking and...well...
TCW is left to foot the bill. Bollocks. ===================================================
