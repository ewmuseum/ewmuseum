++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ TCW January 13th Recorded As part Of TCW Tape Archives St. Hilliard, Ohio ================================================== Tin Can Wrestling turned up in Ohio for its second show of the season for a charity show organised by The Lions Club, no-one anywhere knows who they are or why they have a name like an Ice Hockey Club but they offered cash. And that's all that matters.
The big news coming into this show is the demotion of 'Sex Pistol' Jaime
Roberts to curtain-jerker, no matter as his name still carries as much weight as it ever has.
A crowd of around 200 have been lured to the show which has been advertised on a SMALL scale. The Lions Club have provided seating and a small sandwich stall. All of which is included in the $4 entrance fee.
================================================== A TCW logo flashes briefly. Very briefly. Its 6pm, the final ticket stub has been collected, the last wrist has been stamped as The Lions Club building main hall has its fire doors closed.
This, the only event happening in St. Hilliard this side of the next millennium has attracted a fair crowd, whole families have turned up expecting some good old fashioned 'wrasslin' action.
With a roster like TCW's they wont be disappointed. Ok, stop laughing. No, seriously. ================================================== The show begins with TCW Promoter announcing the Lions Club raffle winners, so, if any of you have a 56, 23, 08 or 79 you could have won.
Rip It Up by 28 days signals the beginning of the show, Saunders warms the crowd up by talking about the good work the charity does in the area of St.
Hilliard [That's the first cheap pop of the night] and even gets in a mention for the St. Hilliard Horndogs. Saunders moves on to build up TCW to a National Fed with a HUGE budget, MASSIVE stars and unquestionable motives!
"THIS IS TCW" He cries out to a small ripple of applause. Tough crowd. Time to bring out the big guns. "Ladies and gentlemen, about to grace you with his presence. weighing in at
215 pounds. and hailing from
Sheffield, England, the 'City of Steel and Sex'. Accompanied by his manager, Claire Maynard. He has been called the one, the only, the TRULY great Junior Heavyweight."
'Livin' La Vida Loca' cranks out over the cheapo TCW sound system, starting a chorus of boos from most of the
males in the crowd. A blonde 20 year old girl walks out, raising her arms to acknowledge the cheers of teenage boys everywhere, then leaning forward she blows a kiss at a TCW's newly bought camera, before turning to point back to the entranceway, from which the 'Sex Pistol' emerges.
JS: 'Sex Pistol' JAIME ROBERTS!!! For all his attitude Roberts is still a big name player in the sport, his finely crafted character reeks of arrogance as the crowd instantly loathe him.
Son of a Gun by Nirvana begins to play, the crowd remain relatively quiet. The Colt runs out enthusiastically slapping hands with the fans on the way to ring side, the youngster certainly has the look of a pro and his enthusiasm brings him a nice cheer as Saunders introduces him. He slides into the ring and bounces off the ropes testing their elasticity.
================================================== 'Sex Pistol' Jaime Roberts vs. The Colt ================================================== The Colt has a beaming smile on his face at simply standing in the ring with a true star, he nods his head towards Roberts and offers out his hand.
Roberts eyes up Colt with a smirk on his face, the youngster just doesn't see it coming as 'The Sex Pistol' floors him with a big forearm to a chorus of jeers.
Colt backs himself to the corner, Roberts waves him up only to come in with a driving knee, Colt gasps for breath as Roberts pulls him out of the corner. He pauses for a moment chuckling to himself before planting The Colt with a snapping single arm DDT.
Roberts drops down for the cover... 1... 2... Roberts pulls The Colt up by the hair to break his own cover. The crowd are really getting on 'Sex Pistol's' back by now, even more so because they know who the hell he is.
Roberts slaps Colt across the face before pulling him up to his feet, a quick knee to the gut sends the rookie down to one knee. Roberts whips Colt to the ropes but the 265lber reverses, Colt telegraphs the back body drop and gets a STIFF kick to the face. Colt reels back and is sent down by a sweet drop-kick from the ring wise veteran.
Roberts again makes the cover but again breaks it up after two. He plays the crowd for a few seconds, parading The Colt from one side to the next before finally whipping him to the corner.
Roberts turns full circle before charging but Colt gets a boot up. Roberts sells it well, puffing out his cheeks before again charging. Colt explodes out of the corner with a big time lariat. Somewhere in Japan someone marks out. Colt drops down for the cover but 'The Sex Pistol' gets out after 1.
The Colt tries to take control with a chinlock but Roberts turns it into a rollup, Colt powers out and takes Roberts over with an armdrag. Roberts is up quickly ducking a clothesline. Colt turns straight into a drop toe hold.
They exchange holds on the canvas before breaking away, Roberts draws some heat with some faux applause for The Colt [who is looking less and less happy with Roberts]. They tie up and Colt easily overpowers Roberts into a waist lock, he scoots behind and avoids a couple of Roberts elbows before flipping him over...
...but Roberts lands on his feet following the German Suplex, the fans call out but The Colt can't react in time to avoid a Roberts flying forearm.
The Colt somehow remains on his feet, Roberts chops the chest a couple of times before swiping a kick into The Colt's chest. Colt stumbles backwards and Roberts goes for a second, Colt catches his foot but gets caught by an enzinguri [Roberts makes DAMN sure everyone feels that one].
Colt is down and is not playing it up, Roberts buys himself some extra time to play up the crowd and the beautiful Claire Maynard on the outside. The
Colt finally finds his feet but walks straight into a Roberts right hand.
The Colt fires back, and again, and again. Roberts is on the back foot and the crowd come alive. The Colt is tagging away on Roberts like his life depended on it, he grabs Roberts and whips him to the ropes.
Roberts holds on to the top rope purposefully cutting short The Colt's comeback, he taps the side of his head to much crowd heat.
The Colt looks visibly put out by Roberts as the pair meet in the middle of the ring. Roberts no sells a couple of Colt right hands before literally being knocked off of his feet by a BIG OL' clothesline. Colt doesn't hold back at all and is quick to work over Roberts.
Colt pulls Roberts up but gets a thumb to the eye, Roberts again taps his head and swings out a big kick, Colt catches it again this time ducking the incoming enzinguri. Roberts is pulled into a reverse waistlock and DUMPED on the back of his head following a rough looking German Suplex.
The Colt makes his way up to the top... ...Top rope elbow from The Colt, all 265lbs of him come crashing down. 1... 2... 2 1/2... 'Sex Pistol' leaves it until the last possible moment before rolling a shoulder. The close fall has Maynard and some of the fans up off of their seats.
The Colt begins to stomp away on Roberts but is cut short by a low blow as
Maynard causes the distraction, Roberts follows it up with a sweet
Superkick... ...and then an asai moonsault... ...he goes up top... ...and hits his GLORY DAZE senton bomb, the move gets a cheer from the fans simply because they've seen it on TV. 'Sex Pistol' plays the cheer for heat as he shows his cocky, arrogant side before getting a peck on the cheek from
Maynard.
The Colt takes a couple of chops across the chest to back him onto the ropes but begins to brawl his way back into the match. Roberts reels back as Colt lands some fierce rights and lefts before a powerful whip to the ropes...
...a BIG College Football tackle knocks Roberts to the mat with a THUD, the crowd give a mighty cheer as Colt crawls over for the cover...
1 2 Shoulder up! Colt pulls Roberts up before planting him with a powerslam, the ring ropes shake with the impact to another old school pop.
Colt drops an elbow into the chest before again whipping Roberts to the ropes, he again goes for the football tackle but Roberts catches him in mid air...
DD[muthafuggin]T...Colt sells it like a Quarterback bouncing up onto his back. Roberts points to the top and begins to climb. He sets himself on the top but Colt dives across and crotches the old timer who falls down to the mat.
A few moments rest for the duo before The Colt makes it to his feet, Maynard gets up onto the ring apron and shouts at the ref belatedly protesting Colts use of the ropes to dingle dangle [???] her man.
Yes, you guessed it... LOW BLOW from Roberts to some nice old school heat... SPINNING NECKBREAKER... The way that only an old timer can deliver it, Colt swings himself around to an unbelievable degree before Roberts makes the cover...
1 2 [...] 3! That's all she wrote! The Colt goes down to the man with the name value,
Roberts is still drawing the heel heat from the fans as The Colt sells the neckbreaker in the ring.
But of course he can't leave them like that, both he and Maynard turn to
Colt and help him to his feet before applauding the crowd. Heh, so much for a heel. Roberts plays the fans at the wish of James Saunders who lurks in the background.
The fans are clearly hyped at seeing this famous face, lets face it, Ronald
McDonald would get a rise in St. Hilliard so TCW takes a couple of minutes break for Roberts to reel in the publicity.
When we come back its Robert Devlin vs Bill Gates' Evil Twin. ================================================== Robert Devlin vs. Bill Gates' Evil Twin ================================================== First out down the makeshift aisle is Bill Gates' Evil Twin. He has his ever-present briefcase under his arm, and the CD-Rom drive as well. He gets a small reaction from the crowd, half "you look a bit nasty", half "hey, I remember a dark match you were in once".
Second out of the curtain, the fresh faced rookie Robert Devlin, his honey blonde hair bouncing around slightly. He plays up to the crowd slightly, and manages to get a reaction from them, a positive one.
After the referee gives the obligatory check of the boots. we start. Gates seems to be feeling Devlin out to start off with, reluctant to go for anything. The fresh-faced youngster offers him a test of strength, which he rejects. Attempting to pancake Devlin with an unforeseen punch, Gates instead finds his arm grabbed and whipped into the ropes. On the rebound
Devlin leaps over him, but third time round the oldtimer manages to take
Devlin off his feet with a stiff clothesline.
Gates puts the boot in a few times to sounds of disapproval from the crowd, but misses the elbow drop. Getting up, he finds Devlin's foot flying towards it. Opting for the grab, he instead becomes the receiving end of an enzinguri, knocking him back down to the mat. Getting up one more time, he receives a Japanese armdrag for his troubles.
The wily, and unemployable, veteran calls for some time out, which Devlin uses to gee the fans up some more. Attempting to sneak up from behind, Gates grabs Devlin by his flowing locks, and tries to drag him into the suplex position. On the first count, Gates can't get him up. Tries again. Second time. he fails to get him up. Third time, Devlin shifts his weight and suplexes the faux-software giant.
The next time Gates rises from the ground. he is Japanese armdragged one more time, and then wanders into a high dropkick. Devlin runs the ropes. and comes out with a blisteringly fast spear, directly into the balding one's stomach.
Gates rolls under the bottom rope, and runs around the ring. Comedy chase around the ring follows, which ends when both men get back into the ring, and Gates finds the time to uppercut Devlin, staggering in him slightly.
Taking advantage of his confusion, Gates lifts up Devlin, and finally gets to hit his vertical suplex.
Gates goes for the first cover of the match. 1 2 Devlin kicks out at two. Gates shows some annoyance at this, but gets back on the job quickly, hitting Devlin with a German suplex, non-bridged, and then putting the boot in again, laughing. He turns to the crowd to offer some words of "smack".
He turns round. into a snap suplex. Fans are firmly behind Devlin now, as he comes off the ropes with an asai moonsault. Going for a secondary Asai, moonsault, Gates lifts the knees, winding Devlin. Both men are down, as the referee starts the double count.
He gets to six. before Devlin stumbles to his feet, and manages a Guilotine legdrop.. He pauses to decide whether or not cover. and in his confusion gets a kick to the jimmy for his troubles. Gates gets up, butterfly suplex,
German suplex, goes for another German. gets reversed, and Devlin follows-up with a springboard elbow into the face. Gates is wandering about confused,
Devlin goes to springboard one more time.
. . . THE NIGHTCAP! Devlin's Rocker Dropper variation sends Gates down. Hooks the leg, cover.. 1 2 3! Devlin follows up last weeks victory over the TCW protege Rick Johnson with an equally assured display against BGET, the fans couldn't care less about
Gates [who could?] but Devlin has them hyped after his display. A bit naive to the trained eye but St. Hilliard doesn't have any trained eyes.
Devlin wanders out of the ring, dazed slightly, but elated. He stops off with some fans on the way out. Gates. doesn't move for a short while, but soon sulks off in a, well, sulk.
================================================== Bungle vs. Bubba McKenzie ================================================== Word in the back has a Bubba McKenzie no-show, TCW is not a place for no-shows and the ritual humiliation will begin shortly.
Somewhere in the back Bungle is lubricating himself up for his match [or the after show bash], somewhere near the unemployment line Bubba McKenzie is considering his future.
TCW's God, as you may now refer to him, makes his way out in front of the fans who are on the verge of enjoying the show. A good next match would really get them pumped for the main event, sadly thanks to a no-show that's not going to happen. In fact, things do NOT look good at all.
JS - St. Hilliard, are you enjoying the show? Gotta get those darn cheap pops in at a time like this! JS - We have ANOTHER familiar face for you up next, you might want to give a warm welcome to former X80D Hardcore Champion...BUNGLE!
Ok, so I don't know X80D either, but take this at face value. He's been on
TV, TV's are in Ohio...Bungle = Ratings?
JS - But we have a slight problem, Bungle needs an opponent for tonight, do we have any takers?
Oh well of course we do, this is a charity event isn't it...quick...pick that one who looks remarkably like TCW youngster Jet Stream Jackson [minus cape and blue pants]...
Jackson waves his hand frantically, Saunders surveys the crowd before giving
Jackson the nod. The apparent 'hometown' Jackson runs to the ring in street clothes.
'Song2' starts to play and Bungle walks out in his normal bear outfit. He looks around at the people watching and walks to the ring, he tries to high five some old ladies but they end up hitting him with their handbags, he makes his escape rolling onto the apron but trips over the bottom rope entering the ring.
Bungle gets a nice reception due to his vague semblance of recognition but the fans are firmly behind young St. Hilliard Jackson.
Bungle starts things off by pushing Jackson in the chest, Jackson gets another shove. The fans urge him to take it to Bungle but Jackson plays up to his role, Bungle whips him against the ropes and swings out a big clothesline.
Jackson ducks it but backs away. Bungle slaps him across the face once...twice...three...four...fi-
No, Jackson fights back with a shove of his own on Bungle to a riotous cheer. Bungle violently snaps Jackson with a DDT but refuses to make the cover. Instead the bear begins to chat with some of the fans, Jackson is busy making himself look like a regular Joe.
Bungle turns around to find Jackson still on his back, the Bear slowly pulls
Jackson up before stepping back. Jackson staggers around the ring clutching his head before...
__SNAP__ Another DDT rocks him, Bungle looks down and again begins to talk to the fans. He even takes the time to sign an autograph or two before Jackson taps him on the shoulder...ELBOW! Bungle hurls himself over the top rope like a
Romanian Gymnast giving Jackson, and the city, their moment of glory.
Oh, but it can't last. Bungle rolls back into the ring and laughs off a couple more shots from
Jackson before whipping him to the ropes...
CRISS... CROSS... BULLDAAAAWWWWGGGG... Bungle flattens Jackson with the move to a dishearteningly silent reaction, nobody likes to see someone they might know get beaten up by a big gay bear.
Especially not in St. Hilliard. [Heh, fuck em if they can't take a joke.] ONE TWO THREE And that folks, is what happens if you no show, improvisation, second rate matches and general feelings of mutiny backstage.
Cue people leaving. ================================================== Fugitive vs. Francis Vaclav Grande vs. 'The Lost Boy' Jonathan Darling ================================================== Its up to you three, please...save my show. Those are the words of 'encouragement' from the James Saunders, Fugitive, the experienced and also well recognised Philadelphia native will be banking on some fan support. Grande just can't figure out how the hell he got into this mess and Darling is _hyped_ about simply performing.
Its up to you three, please...save my show. Vaclav Grande comes to ringside first, no music, no wrestling gear, no physique, no wrestling ability...but anyway, he makes his way to the ring bespectacled, white shirt and farah slacks. Looks like one of the boys have lent him their boots as well.
This odd little man doesn't get much in the way of a reaction other than a bemused 'um?' The hall becomes bathed in some rather eerie music as Jonathan Darling is sent out into never never [screwup] land.
Running out to the ring, Darling slaps hands with the younger fans, messes up their hair, that sort of thing. Making his way around before jumping up onto the ring apron, he uses the ropes to flip into the ring.
Darling's energy lifts the crowd somewhat for the intro of Fugitive. A wailing police siren soon turns into 'Perfect Crime' by Guns and Roses,
Fugitive waists no time in getting to the ring and instantly charges at
Grande.
The crowd pick up on the visible dislike directed at Grande who turns and slides to the outside, Darling jumps over the top rope and cuts him off with a HIGH dropkick knocking him to the floor. Darling rolls Grande up into the ring where Fugitive is waiting.
The COMPLETELY untrained Grande is pulled to his feet only to be THUMPED down with a BIG clothesline, Fugitive quickly pulls him up and SLAMS him against the turnbuckle.
Understandably Darling is keeping well away! Fugitive walks Grande over to each of the four turnbuckles slamming him face first one after the other.
Grande stumbles backwards straight into a Darling arm-drag. The rookie quickly pulls Grande up and takes him over with a snap mare before placing a boot to the back of the head.
Darling pulls Grande up and pushes him towards Fugitive, the pair share a somewhat misleading look of conflict before Grande is taken down with a
Russian Leg Sweep. Fugitive and Darling share another caustic look before their gaze drops to Grande. Darling hops to the outside and waves his hand in the air to a mild cheer...
...slingshot leg drop garners more response... ...Fugitive drops an elbow down onto Grande... ...then an elbow from Darling... ...another from Fugitive... And so on, Grande is shaking about all over the ring as the Chinese Firedrill [I have it on good authority that that is the term] continues. Finally Fugitive calls it quits and pulls Grande up before hurling him over the top rope. Darling and Fugitive both offer out their hands [to a cheer] and tie up. Fugitive has the weight and leverage and uses it to back Darling to one knee. The duo work a nice routine of armdrags and counters before breaking away, neither man holding an advantage. Grande, clearly the educated man [sadly not in wrestling] chooses this break in the action to make his grand return, a weak forearm to the back of Fugitive does more to irritate than damage.
Grande quickly realises his mistake and turns to run only to be grabbed by the scruff of the neck.
Fugitive staggers Grande with a well placed forearm before taking him over with a snap suplex. He calls Darling over and together the pair whip Grande to the ropes...
...Their double drop-kick attempt doesn't connect as Grande simply stumbles to the floor on his way back...
Grande slowly tries to crawl away but of course is cut off by Darling. The youngster works Grande over by stomping away in the corner as Fugitive dusts himself down, Fugitive calls for Darling to 'move it' before charging at
Grande...
...Did Grande just move? ...Good Lord yes! Grande sidesteps and Fugitive eats turnbuckle, Darling is caught unawares and takes a diving headbutt...to the 'peter pans' to a rousing crowd _OOOH_. Grande turns around and gives Fugitive the same!
__OOOH__ Darling is hobbling around the ring... __OOOH__ Grande seems pleased with himself, apparently this is an easy way to claim a victory! __OOOH__ Another to Fugitive... Get the man a darn rulebook, this isn't how we play this game... __OOOOOOOH__ Down to his knees Grande raises a double low blow on Fugitive and Darling, the referee comes in...
___OOOOOOOHHHH___ Aw shucks, he'll never work for us again! Grande gets to his feet and begins to jump around like a big girl, the sissy. Saying that, he has left three grown men writhing in agony at his feet.
This is NOT a popular move by Grande and he is heckled by the majority of the fans, only those in the school book-club seem impressed.
Slowly but surely the referee finds his [shaky] feet, he waves for the bell...DING DING DING, thats it...GRANDE HAS WO....
...BEEN DQ'd! __OOOOOOOHHH__ STOP THIS LUNATIC...BLOODY POETS! Grande doesn't seem content as he drags Darling to his feet, the youngster takes a bit of poetic justice [geddit?] with a slap across the face, Grande drops down to one knee...
[MASSIVE cheer] But is stopped by Fugitive, the Thug On The Run kicks Grande FIRMLY in the gut before setting him up for a cross powerbomb, Grande struggles...
...struggles... ...fails... and THUD, that puts a stop to Grande's 'sperm crunching' crusade. This unusual turn of events has left everybody mildly confused to say the least and as Fugitive and Darling step out to ringside all seems lost.
Sadly, things don't get any better. TCW's Second show, a disaster. Bugger. ================================================== © Tin Can Productions 2002, Not For Distribution ================================================== The show seems to come off well and many of the fans hang around the hall treating themselves to free tea and biscuits, several of the TCW roster, minus Grande come out and join the festivities.
Bungle and Devlin seem in deep conversation around the doorway. Bungle, still in his bear costume leans in a little closer...
...watch that hand... ...sadly Devlin's not into bear back [geddit?] and Bungle gets a cup of coffee in his face.
Bill Gates' Evil Twin seems concerned with the receptionists computer system, having booted it up without authority he soon discovers it to be running on a local operating system. A small scene follows which sees Gates hurling a small pot plant across the room.
It provides a welcome distraction for Fugitive who makes off with three wheelchairs, a Tea-maid and a small quantity of custard cream biscuits.
=================================================== TCW is a federation based on the adaptation wrestlers must show for individual circumstances. Due to limited funds the interviews / flashes can only be filmed in certain places [ Listed under Places Of Interest ] and also the Tour Bus and in front of the TCW Logo Board.
In addition to these restrictions the roster must also cater for the audience to gain the biggest fan base, a show in an O.A.P. home will have a different feel to a show at a Death Metal Concert.
Participants must set out to :- a] Gain the fan interest via their promo's b] Be creative and original in their restrictions c] Produce a style of match most accessible to the audience.
Those who regularly achieve these goals will be rewarded with extra 'cash' with which to tailor themselves. The money may be spent on an alternate rp location, a more elaborate entrance, a new ring attire, more freedom in matches etc.
