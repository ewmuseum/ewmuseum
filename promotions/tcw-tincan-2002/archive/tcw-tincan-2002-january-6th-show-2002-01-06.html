++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ TCW January 6th Recorded As part Of TCW Tape Archives Reading, Pennsylvania ================================================== Tin Can Wrestling took its first show of 2002 and the first that James
Saunders has paid to be recorded to the Reading New Nears Fair in the South
East of Pennsylvania.
The show is introduced by Paul Hoh the City Council Chairman and plugged throughout the day over the tanoys, a crowd of around 270 turn up at 3pm to see TCW Rip It Up.
================================================== A TCW logo flashes briefly. Very briefly. The weather is fine as the New Years Fair is lavished in cool winter sunshine, families walk hand in hand with their children taking in the sights and sounds.
An announcement comes out over the tannoy :- " Ladies and gentlemen your attention please, in just a few moments Reading will be welcoming Tin Can Wrestling to its fair, if you would care, please follow the signs to the enclosed area. Thank you."
TCW's first audience. The people of Pennsylvania. I guess you can never get all you wish for ;) ================================================== Rip It Up by 28 Days signals the start of the show and out comes James
Saunders, TCW promoter, booker and wheeler dealer extra-ordinaire.
Saunders warms the crowd up for a few minutes slipping in a few cheap pops before thanking everyone for turning up to see TCW's first recorded show.
From behind the crude TCW curtain steps Bill Gates' Evil Twin, he bustles to the ring in a white pin stripe shirt carrying a Microsoft Suitcase to some mild heat. The Evil twin rips the mic from Saunders and uses it to wipe the sweat from his brow.
BGET - " I'm sorry to tell you all this folks but you haven't paid for this show and as such you are breaching numerous piracy laws, this product is not to be distributed illegally and as such, it is my duty as Bill Gates' Evil
Twin to declare this show IN BREACH OF ARTICLE 42. This program has performed an illegal operation and HAS been shut..."
A big drunken Pennsylvanian cheer comes up as 'Great Balls Of Fire' begins to play, Jet Stream Jackson in full superhero gear rushes the ring and stands eye to eye with the Evil Twin.
Jackson pulls on the suspenders of The Evil twin before pinging them back with a tremendous _SLAAAAP_, Gates stumbles backwards before falling on his rear to another big Pennsylvanian cheer.

Gates whips Jackson to the ropes and sets himself for a clothesline, Jackson just manages to avoid it and kicks the thigh.
Jackson is sweating adrenaline in this his first match, he struggles to find the hand of gates as he takes him over with an armdrag and quickly slips it into an armbar. sweep around. Rick Johnson steps out before the fans and holds out his hand to a pretty young girl motioning for her to kiss it. Before she can, Rick pulls his hand back with a look of disgust all over his face as he makes his way up into the ring.
