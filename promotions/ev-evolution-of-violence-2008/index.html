Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Evolution of Violence, eV, opened on 17 August 2008 with ReVolution #001 at the Crown Center in Fayetteville, North Carolina, and the fed's own results page keeps the whole night. Its owner, Reaper, appeared on screen in a black and red suit to introduce the fed's new faces and lay out his plan for a tournament to crown a champion, promising grave consequences for those who had failed him. Seven matches followed, mostly tournament matches: a handicap match won by forfeit when the Clayton Brothers' car went up in flames, Peter Gilmour wrecking one match and winning another, two no-shows, and a Hangman's Horror main event between Michael Dredge and MJ that Reaper called a draw. Two of its wrestlers, Peter Gilmour and Joseph Equinox, had been champions of a federation called sVo, which the page says had closed.

The record around it is two pages. A home page of that week turns the night into storylines: Talon must run a gauntlet of seven opponents, Gilmour wants the championship, and Dredge and MJ go again. The announced card for ReVolution #002, 24 August 2008 at the Greensboro Coliseum Complex, shows its matches only as pictures, with a roleplay deadline the night before, and a Triple Threat whose winner entered a No Limits title run. The roster and champions pages are pictures too: four belts, each with three empty slots, and eleven roster figures with no names. Nothing after the 24 August card survives.
