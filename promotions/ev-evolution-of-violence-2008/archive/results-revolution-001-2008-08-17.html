ReVolution #001 8/17/08 Fayetteville, NC - Crown Center Opening The show opened to the image of Reaper standing in the middle of the ring wearing a black and red suit. Reaper went through introducing all of the new faces of the organization and to thank everyone for coming out. Reaper then proceeded to inform everyone of his vision for eV and the tournament and how many had failed him and there would be grave consequences for such.
--------------------- Handicap Match Nathan Paradine vs. The Clayton Brothers This match was over before it began. The Clayton Brothers never showed up to the arena. Paradine stood in the middle of the ring waiting on his match. Reaper stepped out onto the stage after a few minutes and announced that the Clayton Brothers was a waste of time and would not be heard from again. Everyone looked around in wonder as the eVtv cut on to show the Brothers in their rental car. The picture then shows a similar looking car in flames upside down by the side of the road. We dont see the actual accident or anything, we dont even see the Brothers themselves just the burning vehicle. Paradine was awarded the win due to forfiet and was promised by Reaper a match he wouldn't forget next week.
------------------------ Old Friends Michael Dredge is seen entering the locker room just arriving at the event wearing his cowboy attire. As he is walking down the hall way a EV official approaches him.
EV Official: Mr. Dredge your late… Michael Dredge: So what. EV Official: The boss isn’t happy with you showing up late Mr. Dredge. Michael Dredge: Again I say so what? EV Official: The boss is already stressed out because he wants everything to run smoothly on the first event and you showing up late already makes the company look bad.
Michael Dredge: Look I really don’t care just point me in the direction of my dressing room.
EV Official leads Dredge to the dressing room door further down the hallway and Dredge instantly spots a sign saying EV Dressing room. Dredge looks irritated and gets a tone to his voice.
Michael Dredge: I said my dressing room not the dressing room where all the other nobodies have to go to change.
EV Official: Reaper said if you want your own dressing room that you will have to earn it by proving your worth here in EV Mr. Dredge.
Michael Dredge has a furious expression wash over his face as he begins to shout at the EV Official.
Michael Dredge: There is no way I am going to share a dressing room with the entire damn roster! Who does Reaper think he is trying to belittle me !? First he puts me against his crazy brother and now he expects me to be cramped in a room full of guys that ain’t no where close to me level?! Who does he think he is?!
Ev official: Ummm Mr. Dredge he is the boss and what he says goes. Dredge grabs the man up by the collar of his shirt and pulls him towards him and glares at him.
Michael Dredge: Well you tell the boss that come the next event this wont happen again and if it does then me and him are going out back to settle whatever problem he has got with me. Back when we was running together we was cool with one another we was like brothers and ever since he started his own company it seems like he has a chip on his shoulder one that I am about to fix….
Ev Official: Hey don’t take it out on me man I’m just doing my job. Dredge shoves the Ev Official down to the floor then kicks the door open causing everyone in the room to fall silent as he walks in and slams the door behind him.
------------------------- Tournament Match Kale Forne vs. Nanu Nanu came down for his match verses Kale. Both men gave it their all going back and forth back and forth neither one backing down. Eventually though the pace got to much and Nanu started to get sloppy. Kale went for a few big moves but in the end both men tumbled over the ropes after Kale went for a clothesline both men went over the top and landed hard on the outside. As they fought suddenly from the crowd appeared Peter Gilmour. Gilmour ambushes both men and begins to decimate the two tearing them from limb to limb. Gilmour throws Nanu into the steel steps over and over until his arm looks to be hanging from his body. Gilmour then proceeded to beat Kale with a chair until he was nothing but a bloody lump of flesh. Gilmour grabbed a mic and proclaimed himself the Xtreme Icon and the future eV champion.
------------------------ Bitter Enemies [We cut to the back where we see EV head interviewer Lucas Newman standing by in his black suit and pants with red tie holding a microphone. He spins the microphone in his hands a few times and then looks up as the floor manager is giving him the cue to begin.]
Lucas: Hello wrestling fans, this is Lucas Newman reporting for EV wrestling and tonight we debut with a spectacular show. We got some matches to crown a brand new EV champion. One of those matches is Joseph Equinox taking on my guest at this time. He is a former 3 time hardcore champion and aformer world champion, he is the Xtreme Icon, PETER GILMOUR!
[Peter comes into the scene wearing a black jacket and tights. He is not wearing his mask anymore as he burned it on the last sVo show. Next to Peter is his girlfriend Rose Smith in a stunning red blouse and black skirt. Rose puts her arm around and man and eyes Lucas up down.]
Rose: Who the hell you supposed to be? Col. Sanders? Peter: No isn't he that dude from Days of Our Lives? He's been with that Sammi bitch right? What a loser.
[Peter and Rose chuckle as the joke was not amusing to Lucas.] Lucas: Anyways, welcome to EV. Tonight, you are in a tournament match with someone you know pretty well in Joseph Equinox. Your thoughts.
Peter: Lucas, you are right. I am going into battle against a man I've known pretty well. I face a man who I have been chasing for the past few months. You know I was in sVo with him and he stole MY Hardcore title. I got 1 match with him, Lucas. ONE! I did lose but I was still near the top of the hardcore division and he knew I was a threat to his title. But since sVo closed its doors, I never got that another chance to fight him. But now we are here in EV. It's a new day, a new fed and the circumstances are different. Tonight, we don't fight over a belt. We fight to get closer to a belt.
[Peter turns to the camera.] Peter: You see Joseph, me and you are similar in some ways. We were both champions in sVo. We are both very strong. but the one thing we don't have in common, is our goals. My goal is to destroy you and work my way up the ladder in this tournament and become the NEW EV Champion. Tonight, we put the past behind us and move forward. Unfortunately, you will not move on in the tournament. I WILL! Tonight, I will break every bone in your body and I will show the rest of the boys in this tournament, that I am not man to be taken lightly. I will WIN this tournament and I WILL become the NEW EV World Champion. Joseph....
[Peter gets even closer to the camera.] Peter: GOOD LUCK! [Peter pulls back from the camera as Rose kisses the camera with her sexy lips and leaves her lipstick on it as they leave the scene. We go back to ringside.]
---------------------- Tournament Match TJ Raven vs. Howie Banks TJ Raven comes out as does Banks, the two fight for a few minutes Banks seems almost out of it. Raven stays ontop of Banks and never lets him breath. Raven has the match in the bag and he knows it. Raven finally puts Banks away and gets the three. A few minutes later Reaper comes out onto the stage and makes down to the ring sliding under the bottom rope. Raven drops down and out of the ring knowing this is nothing to do with him. Reaper proceeds to kick the shit out of Banks over and over calling him a worthless piece of shit and not worthy to be in his federation. Reaper then told Banks he would be in a special match the next week and if he lost the match he would have to suffer the ultimate consequence.
---------------------- Wanna Hear A Story MJ and Travis Williams Passing back and forth, after applying my face paint. I await the main event, where Steven Steele will suffer an awful defeat at my hands. Suddenly, I hear a knocking at my door. "COME IN" I scream to the door.
In walks some young kid, "Hey man, the name is MJ!" The youngster seems excited to be in my locker room. I glare at him, looking him up and down, before speaking. "Something I can help you with?"
The kid smiles, "Yes sir, I wanted to tell you a story about a kid who became, how do you say, CRAZY!" I put my hand up, as I do not have time for the nonsense.
"I tell you what MJ, how about you get me a cup of coffee, and I will tell you about a man who went from the streets to condos. How a young boy with mental scars became a superstar worldly known!" I laugh as I said this to him.
The youngster nods, and quickly runs out the room, "I thought they drug tested wrestlers these days. That kid has to be hyped up on some blow or some PCP. For christ sakes, does he think I care about his sappy story?"
Travis walks from his locker room, towards the ready position. Awaiting his chance to make eV history in Fayetteville, North Carolina!
---------------------- Tournament Match Joseph Equinox vs. Peter Gilmour Equinox starts to come down to the ring only to get jumped from behind by Gilmour. Gilmour doesn't let Equinox off the stage and just beats him down with massive forarms and high impactful fists. Gilmour grabs Nox up for a powerbomb and runs and throws Nox off the stage into equipment before the match even begins. Gilmour then drug Nox into the ring for the offical start of the match. Gilmour made quick work of Nox and got the easy three.
-------------------- Tournament Match K.V.T vs. Talon Talon comes out and waits for KVT to show. Reaper comes out and announces that KVT like Banks will be in a special match the next week sense she didn't have enough courtesy to show awarding the win to Talon. Talon smarted off to Reaper about this being bullshit. Reaper raised an eyebrow and announced sense Talon wanted a "real" match that he'd give him more hell than he could stand next week.
------------------- Tournament Match Steven Steel vs. Travis Williams Travis and Steel put on one hell of a show going back and forth for several minutes before Williams took the advantage. Williams would keep Steel realing for a while but Steel would get back into the match with a few high impact moves. Steel would then keep Williams down with a barrage of technical moves. After controling the match for about ten minutes Steel went for a shoulder block in the corner but Williams moved out of the way and Steel hit the pole so hard you could see his shoulder hanging out of place. Williams took quick advantage and dropped steel in a fuji armbar which Steel tapped from immediately.
