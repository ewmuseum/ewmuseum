I have been in the e-wrestling game on and off since about 1995. I was first a fan. At ten years old, I was introduced to "fantasy wrestling bulletin boards". Not knowing what I know now, I was the biggest mark you would ever see. I watched WWF, WCW, USWA, and later on in early 1996... ECW. I came into this game to have fun. And thats what I want to do with my next brainchild.
Just being bored one Friday night, I opened up my little Outlook Express, looked through RSPWF, and saw nothing new in the e-wrestling world. Simply stagnant. I thought to myself, "damn this sucks", So an idea popped in my head. I need to do something that could bring atleast _some_ interest back into e-dub.
Therefore, I have constructed Pro Wrestling ALPHA.ALPHA is a _strictly cruiserweight wrestling organization_. We will be 65% workrate, 35% sports entertainment. Also, it will be a bi-weekly wrestling promotion, thus shows will mean more, and will be more consistent.
In order for ALPHA to be a success, I need the following: ---> A dedicated staff. I can't do this by myself. I mean I have a desire to pull this off, but I need help. Some people that are willing to take a load off my shoulders. For instance, a Vice President, and about 4-5 dedicated match writers.
---> A dedicated roster. Inactive rosters do not help an e-fed at all. I'm looking for guys to put out quality stuff, and are having fun in the process. A good 15-20 guys would be nice to get ALPHA off the ground.
---> Federations willing to do inter-promotional work. More specifically, "puroresu" e-federations out there (i.e. E-Pro, NEO Wrestling, etc). ALPHA wants to lead cruiserweight wrestling to the promised land, and also would like to help out others in the process.
If these three key points could come to pass, I garauntee you that the ALPHA would be a success.
If you are interested in becoming a part of this promotion, e-mail me at [detail withheld] . The past is the past, i'm looking forward. === DEVELOPMENT === * KAYFABE * Sitting at home one evening, Darren Donaldsworth was watching wrestling. After two hours of sitting on the couch, watching a sport he loved, he realized something...
wrestling has gotten stale. Same face after face on the screen, doing the same thing they've done since the TV show "Blossom" was cool. So, from that point on he thought to himself...
"i'm going to make wrestling cool again". Darren Donaldsworth, son of Philip & Mary Donaldsworth, who are two very successful business lawyers. Darren has ownership in a local chain of taverns in
Boston, Massachusetts. He also has a degree in business law and accounting. He knows the only way for a wrestling promotion to succeed is by doing well financially. Donaldsworth, instantly took a flight to Hollywood, California and met with many Production companies. He wanted to start a new, fresh exciting wrestling promotion on prime time cable television. EMPIRE Entertainment took him up on his offer, as Donaldsworth was given the title of "President of Operations".
With Donaldsworth's financial backing, he got a deal for a Saturday Night television show to take place every week live from EMPIRE Entertainment's studio. He also got a deal on GEN X-TV (which had WAO on their networks last summer/fall)
to broadcast that show. Now with timeslots, and staff in place... Donaldsworth is looking for only one thing... talent. And he has an unique way of getting it. Only tune in to find out.

=== CHAMPIONSHIPS === Pro Wrestling ALPHA has three titles under the organization. Those prestigious belts are...
ALPHA WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: The king of the cruiserweights. Holding this title means you are at the top of the mountain, for the time being. Can you hold onto the title and prove your worthyness to the promotion? Or will you be just another face on the wall?
ALPHA TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP: The stepping stone to the ALPHA World Title. This belt is defended on every show, _period_. Holding your own as ALPHA Television Champion will gain respect for the fans and the wrestlers in the back.
