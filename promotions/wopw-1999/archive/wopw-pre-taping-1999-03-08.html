WoPW — Card

Pre-Taping

8 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The fans were cheering loudly as B.J. Perris entered the ring for the matches prior to the World of Professional Wrestling Television Program.)
<Perris> Good evening ladies and gentlemen . . . we have some very exciting matches for you this evening before our television taping and the WoPW Tag Team
Title Match between the champions, Young Justice and the challengers, Pep
Preston and the “Hardbody”, Robert Smith.
Our first match of the evening . . . from Greenville, Arkansas . . . weighing in at 213 pounds . . . David Dickens!!!!!!
(There was a pretty healthy cheer as the youthful Dickens made his way to the ring for the opening match of the evening. Entering the ring, Dickens was talking to referee Joey Dayton.)
<Perris> And his opponent . . . calling Daytona Beach home . . . at 6’ . . . 200 pounds . . . accompanied to the ring by his manager, Mean Darlene . . . here is Gary Gordon!!!! (The lovely Mean Darlene led the way to the ring followed by Gordon. The fans were cheering very loudly but it was debatable as to who the cheer was for.
With Darlene holding the ring ropes open, Gary Gordon entered the ring. After a few words from Joey Dayton, the match was under way.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** With only 10 minutes, the match started off fast and furious as Gordon took it right to Dickens taking him to the mat several times with Clotheslines and Hip
Tosses. Keeping him there however was another story as each time Gordon put him down, Dickens kept popping right back up.
3 minutes in, Dickens turned the tide and started giving back to Gordon starting with a Snap Suplex followed by a Twisting Leg Drop off of the middle ropes into a pinning predicament but was pushed off.
Gordon was up to the task and took Dickens into the corner, working him over with several Forearms and a Knee Lift before putting him down with a Hip Toss
Body Slam. All Gordon could get was a two count.
The two men went back and forth past the 5 minute mark until Dickens went to the air and landed a Moonsault after putting Gordon down with a Clothesline off of the ropes. Rather than go for the pin, Dickens made a mistake and headed back for the ropes. A second Moonsault attempt followed but Gordon rolled out of the way just in time.
Gordon sent Dickens into the far ropes but never got a chance for the return as
Mean Darlene reached into the ring and tripped Dickens who fell flat on his face. Gordon pulled up Dickens and planted him with a Piledriver before getting the pin.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** <Perris> Your winner . . . at 7:23 . . . Gary Gordon!!!! (The fans cheered as Mean Darlene led the victorious Gordon to the back of the arena. Darlene threatened several fans on the trip as they attempted to “touch” her but were pushed back by the accompanying Security.
As Darlene and Gordon disappeared into the back, a chant of “WoPW” . . . “WoPW”
. . . “WoPW” . . . started up as Perris re-entered the ring.)
<Perris> Before our next match . . . allow me to introduce . . . Marcelo Cruz . . . (The fans were cheering as Cruz made his way quickly to the ring, slapping hands as he went before doing a Somersault into the ring, landing gracefully on his feet.
Cruz waved at the fans as he moved closer to Perris, holding the mic.) <Perris> Marcelo . . . anything you would like to pass on the fans about your stay here in the WoPW?
<Marcelo Cruz> Sure! This has been a great experience so far for me . . . this promotion has been great . . . the fans have been wonderful . . . and the wrestling talent is fantastic, from the bottom of the roster to the top . . .
<Perris> The only negatives would have to be the attack by your former partner. Suicido? <Marcelo Cruz> Yeah . . . I never saw that one coming. I guess there are differences in what drives each of us to step into the ring . . . I’m just not so sure what it was that Suicido got out of it.
I will say though . . . what goes around, comes around . . . and one of these days, Suicido is going to be on the recieving end of a major ass kicking!
(The fans went crazy over Cruz’ prediction while Perris laughed.) <Perris> How does it feel to get such a positive endorsement from the Offspring? <Marcelo Cruz> It’s great! I mean, the guy’s only 19 and look at what he has accomplished . . . titles here and there . . . he’s a class act all the way and has the talent to back up what he does. Who wouldn’t want an endorsement from a man like that?
<Perris> Okay . . . that takes care of the Offspring . . . what are your feelings on his “opposite number”, Juan Garza?
<Marcelo Cruz> Juan’s . . . well, it’s hard to say. The guy has talent that’s for sure or he wouldn’t be wearing that belt around his waist . . .
<Perris> True . . . <Marcelo Cruz> But he’s hard to figure out sometimes. He and the Offspring have their differences and that’s something I hope to stay out of. If Juan is proud of his Texas hertiage . . . that’s cool by me. I’m from Mexico . . . I’m proud of who and what I am . . . what I do in the ring goes beyond of where I’m from.
<Perris> It does help to shape your style though . . . <Marcelo Cruz> Yeah that’s true . . . I grew up around some great Luchadores, so that is what I learned.
<Perris> Marcelo . . . what do you see in your future here in the WoPW? <Marcelo Cruz> I see gold . . . I want a crack at the King of Hardcore Belt. It doesn’t matter to me who has it, I just want a chance to challenge for it. The
Tag Titles wouldn’t be too bad either . . . though I think Young Justice has a solid lock on them.
I have a series of matches against Suicido with our first bout going to a time limit draw . . . next time will be a little different. I also wouldn’t mind tagging with the Offspring and going after Suicido and Garza or even a 6-man with Miller and the Offspring.
<Perris> That is a match that I would love to see! You and the Offspring against Suicido and Juan Garza. With the animousity between Spring and Garza and the past between you and Suicido . . . that would be an exciting match to see . . .
I want to wish you all the luck in your stay here in the WoPW. Ladies and gentlemen . . . Marcelo Cruz!!!!!
(The fans were cheering loudly as Perris and Cruz shook hands before Marcelo left the ring and headed to the back.)
<Perris> Our next match . . . (Perris stopped as “New Attitude” Nate Hopkins stepped out from the back and made his way to the ring to a pretty healthy cheer. Entering the ring, Hopkins swiped the mic away from Perris before “asking” him to step back.)
<Nate Hopkins> You know . . . I am getting tired of hearing all this crap about the toughest guy in this promotion . . . Mike Smith is the “true” King of
Hardcore . . . the Offspring is the “Extreme Luchadore” . . . Juan Garza is the
“King of Hardcore” because he has the belt around his waist . . .
Oh please . . . You want to see the baddest guy in this promotion? You want to take on the toughest guy here? Then knock off all the talk . . . sign the contracts and put your asses on the line . . . because I . . .
(Ms. Vanessa Benitez walked out from the back in a very tight bright red dress that showed alot of skin. She was followed by the black clad woman known only as Cat, as she walked slowly to the ring in her impossibly high heels. The catcalls and whistles really came out as she entered the ring. With Cat standing behind her, Benitez asked for the mic.)
<Vanessa Benitez> You think you’re such a badass, Hopkins? You think you can take on the champion or the former champion? You think you can beat the man who ran Evil K out of Florida?
(Hopkins was standing there confidently with his arms folded over his chest, nodding his head up and down.)
If you’re so bad . . . why don’t you fight my man first? <Nate Hopkins> Who? Jason Hex? Mike Smith? Bring on either of those pussies . . . <Vanessa Benitez> Neither of them. <Nate Hopkins> Who then? Bring him on! <Vanessa Benitez> Alright . . . here is the newest member of the West Coast
Connection . . .
(Ozzy Osbourne’s “Crazy Train” started to play as Carl Jacobs stepped out from the back and made his way to the ring. Entering the ring, Jacobs and Hopkins went nose-to-nose before Hopkins took a step back.)
<Nate Hopkins> Get Suicido out here and make this a three-way! (The fans were cheering as Hopkins went up the ropes.) <Perris> Ladies and gentlemen . . . from Mexico City, Mexico . . . the masked
Suicido!!!!! (Suicido came out from the back to pretty heavy booing as he made his way into the ring while Perris, Vanessa Benitez and Cat made their exit. Joey Dayton went to the center of the ring and called for the bell.)
***Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Suicido came flying out of his corner and dropped Jacobs with a Drop Kick. Hopkins followed with an Elbow Drop that took the wind out of Suicido’s sails. Going to work, Nate Hopkins went after both wrestlers with a series of power moves . . . Clotheslines and Suplexes . . . outbrawling both men for the first
4 minutes if the match.
Suicido caught a break as Hopkins turned his attention to Jacobs. A high risk
Top Rope Moonsault wiped out Hopkins and a Hurricanrana took Jacobs out of the ring, landing hard on the concrete floor. Suicido followed with a Suicide Dive further punishing Jacobs on the outside of the ring.
By this time, Hopkins was outside of the ring as well and clobbered Suicido with a chair over the head, flooring him. A Kick to the back by Cat stopped
Hopkins from delivering damage to Jacobs.
With a chair of his own, Jacobs knocked out Hopkins with a blow over the head as referee Joey Dayton came out of the ring to get control of things. The match was over 5 minutes old as Suicido got back into the thick of things with a
Flying Clothesline that knocked Jacobs down.
Dayton was talking to a downed Hopkins who was laying there holding the back of his head. Carl Jacobs was back on his feet and applied a Fujiwara Armbar on
Suicido while Vanessa Benitez was screaming in his face.
Jacobs took the match back into the ring and continued to work over Suicido’s arm with Armbars and Hammerlocks with an occassional Forearm Smash as well as a
Stomp once knocked to the mat.
10 minutes into the match, it looked as if Carl Jacobs had things well in hand with a Hammerlocked Dragon Sleeper but was broken up by the referee as Cat had ahold of Suicido’s leg and was pulling on it for extra leverage.
Jacobs dropped Suicido and turned only to be looking face-to-face with Hopkins who dropped him with an Overhead Belly-to-Belly Suplex. Bridging, Hopkins took the pin.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Hopkins rolled out of the ring and made his way to the back holding his head while the fans cheered loudly for the winner. The ladies helped Carl Jacobs to his feet while Joey Dayton called for help to assist Suicido.)
<Perris> Your winner . . . at 13:12 . . . “New Attitude” Nate Hopkins!!!!! We’ll be right back with this week’s World of Professional Wrestling!!!!!! (The chants of “WoPW! . . . WoPW! . . . WoPW! . . .” started as everyone rose to their feet as preparations for the tv taping began.)
