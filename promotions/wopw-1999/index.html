Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

World of Professional Wrestling's surviving record is built around one televised pre-taping segment, aired 8 March 1999 ahead of that week's broadcast; announcer B.J. Perris introduced the night's undercard bouts ahead of the week's main card, a Tag Team Title defense by the reigning champions, Young Justice.

The undercard staged a manager-assisted win — Gary Gordon, led by manager Mean Darlene, piledriving David Dickens for the pin — and an interview with Marcelo Cruz, who used the segment to size up the promotion's own title picture: endorsing tag champion the Offspring, weighing in on King of Hardcore Champion Juan Garza (“the guy's only 19 . . . titles here and there”), and outlining a running feud with a former partner, the masked Suicido.

The segment closed with a triple threat: “New Attitude” Nate Hopkins calling out all comers, met first by Carl Jacobs of the West Coast Connection (accompanied by Vanessa Benitez and a woman known only as Cat) and then by Suicido himself. Hopkins won with a belly-to-belly suplex after the two rivals had already worked each other over with chairs.

No further broadcast, and no full card result, survives; how the Tag Team or King of Hardcore pictures resolved beyond this night is not recorded.
