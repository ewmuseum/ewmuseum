EMWO — Special Report

EMWO Special Report, 2 September 1998

2 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[the scene opens in the FWH news room, where anchor man(or goat), Mad Goat is seated at a news desk.] Mad Goat: Ladies and Gentlemen.....a disturbing turn of events has come up in the EMWO's closing. Earlier today, EMWO President, Lawrence Williams announced his true reason for closing the EMWO. Here is the footage.. ==cuts to clip== [the scene fades into the office of the EMWO President. The camera gets a close up of the teary-eyed Lawrence Williams, who is seated at his desk.] LW: EMWO Members and fans.....I haven't been entirely honest with all of you. You all deserve the true reason for the EMWO's closing....and I intend to tell you everything. [the fire in LW's eyes starts to burn] LW: One man caused our beloved EMWO to crumble.....JOE BOXCAR!!!! Thats right! He seud the EMWO for millions of dollars for "wrongful termination of contract!" HE RUINED THE EMWO!!!! [in a fit of rage, Lawrence pushes over his desk and runs up to the camera] LW:(yelling)Listen up Joe! I WANT YOU OUTTA THIS SPORT...GONE....HISTORY!!! I'LL DO ANYTHING IN MY POWER TO MAKE SURE YOU ===end of clip=== Mad Goat: I've never seen Lawrence Williams in that condition before, but just a few minutes ago, wrestling's self proclaimed "favorite homeless man" Joe Boxcar responded to Lawrence's threats. ===cuts to clip=== [several members of the Press are busy trying to get pictures and answers from "Wrestling's favorite Homeless guy" Joe Boxcar. Joe is standing at the podeum with his manager, Mo "Diamond" Jackson standing next to him] -=you hear feed back from the microphone and everyone quiets down=- [Joe gets a big grin on his face] Joe: The EMWO is dead! [he laughs with his manager. The press members don't like it and they start yelling at him] Joe: I KILLED THE EMWO!! That bum Lawrence Williams shoulda thought his plans through before he messed with "Homeless" Joe Boxcar! [Mo steps up] Mo: How do ya like dat Mista Williams.....you sawrry bum! I told you to take yaw sawrry EMWO and cram it, but instead....you closed it! I guess you is too stup-ed to und-a-stand da difference ba-tween cram....and close. Either way...you is still a bum! At da final EMWO PPV, Grand Finale......my client, Joe Boxcar is going to treat you to a beatin'! Joe: and to make things a little more interesting, I would like to make a challenge to Lawrence Williams! [the crowd begins to mumble] Joe: Lawrence...lets up the stakes in this match.....winner eliminates the other, permantly. If you win, I'll retire from wrestling, but if I win.....kiss your wrestling career good-bye 'cause its all over! [the crowd gets loud, begging to get there questions answered. Mo's eyes are wide and he is screaming at Joe] Mo: WHAT DA HELL DO YA THINK YOU IS DOIN'!!!!! YOU'S ONLY BEEN IN DA BUSINESS 4 WEEKS, AND ALREADY YOU'RE THROWIN' IT AWAY.....LAWRENCE COULD END YAWOUR CAREER!!! Joe: Don't worry, I'll win. Lawrence, I'm expecting your answer soon.... [the press members get wild] ==end of clip== Mad Goat: Well thats it! Its either Lawrence or Joe, but one thing is for sure. After EMWO's Grande Finale, one of them will be forced to retire from Wrestling! We will now return to the original program. [camera fades out]
