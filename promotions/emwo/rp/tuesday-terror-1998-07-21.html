As usual, the camera starts in the nose bleeds, making its way down past row upon row of excited EMWO fans. All the fans are on their feet as they try to get on the camera. They hold up their signs, flick off the camera, shake their heads, anything to be on camera. The camera now begins to make its way down the entrance way, catching the fans leaning over the ring barrier trying to touch the camera. We now bend around the first turn of the ringside area, and we see Tommy Sessons and Bruce Bingham having a pre-card conversation, and then they realise that they're on the air.] TS: Welcome, ladies and gentlemen, to another edition of EMWO's *other* card, Tuesday Terror. I'm your host Tommy Sessons, and here's my partner who's in a lot of scrutiny as of late, "Bad Boy" Bruce Bingham. Bruce? BB: First and foremost, I want t'say that I'm innocent, and that these rumours of me being the prank caller are false. I really don't care 'bout King Snake, so I wouldn't prank call the friggin' Saturday Night Live set sayin' I was King Snake. So let's end all of that right here. TS: What about your upcoming match with 'Furious' Freddy Jenkins? BB: What about it? I can't wait to get back inside that ring once more as this fourty year old body totally destroys that jerk, Furious! TS: You seem a bit steamed today, Bruce. BB: Wouldn't you be? I mean I've been accused, blamed, pointed at, laughed at, yelled at, an' the whole nine yards jus' 'cause of this weak rumor. TS: Well, I guess I would be, too. Let's talk about the line-up for Tuesday Terror! The first match will have a great match between "Speed" Jack Harvey and "Lethal Weapon" Kevin Norman. BB: Yeah. Both of these fellas are lookin' for their first EMWO win, an' a victory tonight would send Kevin Norman a happy man to the EMWO Coliseum on the second. TS: It sure would. The next match will be "SADISTIC" Shane Alexander taking on Richard Mott. Not much is known about Richard Mott, but we *do* know that Shane Alexander is a man who loves cruelty, and that can't mean anything food for what's his face. BB: Sure can't, Tommy. Mott should be careful this matchup, 'cause anything could happen in the ring with this feller. TS: Sure could. The third match of the evening will be Rising Sun, Inc's own Giant Kabukii taking on the incomparable Roscoe Babba Jenkins! Jenkins once again continues his drive for win number one in the EMWO, and it's not going to get any better as he has a pulled groin, and he's going up against a man who knows no fear. BB: He sure don't. Jenkins is in some trouble, so I guess it must be the last name, eh? TS: [laughing] I guess so. The main event of the night will be Paul Dare Vs. Tyrone Johnson in an interesting match. Johnson said that this will be his last match here in the EMWO, and this is Paul Dare's first. Who will win this match? BB: No one knows, nor cares. I don't believe that I got stuck talkin' on this here *other* show. This is a disgrace to the Bingham name. TS: Let's send it to the ring with the first match of the evening. _______ |__ ____|_____ ________________________________________________ |____ ____| Tuesday Terror - Miami - Miami, FL | | | |"Lethal Weapon" Kevin Norman Vs. "Speed" Jack Harvey |_| | | | |Written by [name withheld] |_|_____________________________________________________ In probaly the best match of the night, Jack Harvey make his EMWO wrestling debuts as he locked up in a great match. In the beginning Norman got the advantage with a drop kick, sending Harvey to the mat, but Norman attempted a quick elbow, but Harvey's speed enabled him to quickly roll out of the way. Harvey then picked Norman up, and took him down with a big snap suplex. Harvey then went on a small series as he hit Norman with a drop kick, spinning heel kick, frankensteiner, and a head scissors takedown. Harvey then whipped Norman into the ropes, and went for a head scissors takedown again, but this time Norman was able to turn it into a tilt-a-whirl back breaker. Norman then took the match outside as he slammed Norman's back up against the two steel ring steps, seperating both pieces. Norman then went on to hit a bodyslam, piledriver, moonsault, and then a super kick, the prelude to the LethalSlam. Norman set Harvey up, and then took him down with a big LeathalSlam, gaining the 1, 2, and then the 3. WINNER: "Lethal Weapon" Kevin Norman - 10:24 TS: Great debut for Kevin Norman as Jack Harvey falls to one and one in the rankings. BB: Kevin Norman is a great 'rassler, an' he's goin' to go far in this league. I can jus' fill it. TS: I can feel it, too, Bruce. Let's move right along to the next match on the card. It's "SADISTIC" Shane Alexander vs. Richard Mott. _______ |__ ____|_____ ________________________________________________ |____ ____| Tuesday Terror - Reunion Arena - Dallas, TX | | | |"SADISTIC" Shane Alexander Vs. Richard Mott |_| | | | |Written by [name withheld] |_|_____________________________________________________ This was one helluva first match for Shane Alexander. Not only did he totally destroyer Richard Mott, but he lived up to his name in the process. Alexander used a lot of cheating moves like choking Richard Mott between both ropes, and uncovering the turnbuckles late in the match. Shane took the match outside of the ring a few times, belting Mott with a chair each of the three times, and powerbombing him onto the steel ring steps as well. Once Alexander threw Mott into the ring for the last time, he hit Richard Mott with a big DDT off the top for the win. WINNER: "SADISTIC" Shane Alexander - 7:39 TS: Great debut for Shane Alexander as well. He totally ripped Richard Mott in that ring. And here come the EMTs. Richard Mott has just retired from the world of professional wrestling, folks. BB: Y'know, I've neva' seen a 'rassler debut an' retire in the same night. But y'know what they say, anything's possible in the EMWO! TS: That's exactly what they say. Well, while the EMTs are loading Richard Mott onto the stretcher, let's talk about Summer Sizzler for a second, Bruce. BB: Okay. TS: Let's talk about this free-for-all. You Vs. Freddy Jenkins. If I am correct, this is the first ever announcer vs. annoucer match that ever been wrestled under a [name withheld] Entertainment federation, right? BB: I don't give uh damn. All I know is that Bruce Bingham is lacin' up the boots one last time, an' I'm uh totally rip that little faggot, Jenkins, up! TS: We'll talk more about Summer Sizzler, but they're done loading the now retired Richard Mott onto the stretcher, and we're ready for the third match. _______ |__ ____|_____ ________________________________________________ |____ ____| Tuesday Terror - Reunion Arena - Dallas, TX | | | |Giant Kabukii Vs. Roscoe Babba Jenkins |_| | | | |Written by [name withheld] |_|_____________________________________________________ In a fairly easy match for Kabukii, he worked on the lower back of Jenkins for the whole match. The match started off with a Kabukii gut wrench suplex, and Kabukii never looked back from there. He went on to hit Jenkins with a backbreaker, sideslam, and a big bear hug, wearing down the back of Roscoe Babba Jenkins. Jenkins then got a small advantage when he hit Kabukii with a big chop, but Kabukii just sent him to the mat with a clothesline over the top rope. Kabukii then hit Jenkins with a big splash into the corner, and that set Jenkins up for the big Bonzaii Guillotine Leg Drop, getting 1, 2, and the 3. WINNER: Giant Kabukii - 7:33 BB: An' Giant Kabukii has also debuted successfully here in the EMWO. Whole 'lotta debuts, Tommy. An' it seems as if the EMWO's talent roster jus' keeps gettin' bigger an' bigger. TS: It sure does. Though the EMWO's closed to new wrestlers as of now, after the PPV a few spots will open, and we'll be open to wrestlers for about a week. BB: An' I'm sure that'll be one hectic week for Lawrence. TS: One week he got he got over twelve applications in his mailbox. I don't think he's looking forward to that part. BB: I wouldn't, either. But you know what it's time for. TS: I sure do. It's time for the Tuesday Terror main event. This week it's Paul Dare taking on Tyrone Johnson. The Gangsta, Tyrone Johnson. Let's get to the match. _______ |__ ____|_____ ________________________________________________ |____ E ____| Tuesday Terror - Reunion Arena - Dallas, TX | |M|V|"Flamboyant" Paul Dare Vs. Tyrone Johnson |_|A|E| I|N|Written by [name withheld] N|T|_____________________________________________________ In a rather boring match, Paul Dare literally swept the floor with Tyrone Johnson. Johnson put up no offensive effort whatsoever, and Paul Dare took advantage of that big time. Paul Dare then went on to hit Johnson with a swinging neck breaker, spiked piledriver, and a side suplex. Johnson then got a little bit of an advantage when he hit Dare with a big splash in the corner. Johnson then went for another body splash, but Dare hit him with a back elbow, and then hit Johnson with a russian leg sweep and a DDT. Dare then hit Johnson with the Market Crash, and got the pinfall. WINNER: "Flamboyant" Paul Dare - 10:39 TS: And "Flamboyant" Paul Dare also debuts successfully here on Tuesday Terror! BB: An' that's a wrap, folks. TS: That's right. Ladies and gentlemen, we're out of tiiime on TERROR!
