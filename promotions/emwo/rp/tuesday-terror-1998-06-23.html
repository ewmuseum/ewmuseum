EMWO — Tuesday Terror

EMWO Tuesday Terror, 23 June 1998

23 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

He hops off the apron, and lands right ontop of Muerto X. He stands X up, and takes his head, slamming it into the corner of the ring apron, quite a few times. BIG FACE POP! Vars then picks Muerto up, and drives his back right into the steel pole! BIG FACE POP!! Vars lets Muerto drop to the ground, and he celebrates with a few of the fans in the front row...] FJ: Man. He's whippin' him so bad that he has time to mingle with the fans. Muerto is takin' a beat down tonight! I don't think he can survive another minute in that ring with Vars. [As the last parts of the clips roll, a voice-over is then heard.] VO: Jack Vars. Underrated? Overrated? You be the judge. [More clips of Vars roll.] [Vars slides Muerto into the ring, and then slides his feet so that the ring post is between his two legs. Vars then takes Muerto's bad knee, and slams it against the ring post. Muerto reaches for his knee, but Vars hits him with a big right hand, sending him back to the mat. Vars then kicks the ringsteps away, and takes Muerto's legs, applying a deathlock on Muerto X, between the steel pole! BIG FACE POP!!!...] TS: Look at the ref! The man is irate. I mean he's totally outraged. He's beside himself. He doesn't want to disqualify Jack Vars, but he doesn't want to see Muerto X get pounded into the mat illegaly! C'mon, ref! Do yer job! [The ref finally is able to pry apart Muerto and Jack, and pulls Muerto into the center of the ring, dropping Vars right on his head. Vars gets up and shakes it off, and slides into the ring. He flips Muerto onto his stomach, and hits him with a knee breaker, making Muerto wince in pain once again. Jack hits Muerto with this move a few times before finally standing Muerto up. Jack then picks Muerto up, and hits him with a knee breaker drop. Jack rolls Muerto over, and hooks the leg up: 1-----2--- Big kickout by Muerto...] TS: Well, Jack Vars is once again putting a hurtin' on Muerto X. There's really nothing Muerto can do with a bad knee. Jack Vars just keeps pickin' away at that knee of Muerto's. It's really payin' off. FJ: Uh huh! [Clips of "Smasher" John Harrison wrestling are seen.] [Harrison struggles to his feet, tired from the beat down that he's gotten, and pulls the shocked Ramacees up. He takes Ramacees, and puts him between the second and top turnbuckle. Harrison then goes to the opposite ropes, and runs. He then jumps and sits ontop of Ramacees, crushing him on impact. The crowd boos Harrison for this, but he laughs at the crowd. He then takes Ramacees feet, and dumps him to the outside. The ref tries to warn Harrison about his behavior, but Harrison shrugs him off, and hops to the outside. When there, Harrison stands Ramacees up, and slams his head on the security railing quite a few times, and then whips him into the ring post. Ramacees crashes into the pole, and then falls down on his back...] TS: OH MAN! Did you hear the collision? That sounded like a helment hitting steel! Not a head! FJ: Maybe he has a thick head. TS: Stop it. [Harrison ignores the referee's warning, and stands Ramacees up. He throws him into a standing headscissors, and then drops him on his head with a big spiked piledriver. BIG HEEL POP! Roxanne cheers her man on as he picks Ramacees apart. Harrison stands and then picks Ramacees up. He holds as if he was going to hit him with a fall away slam, but then drives his back right into the steel pole, and then darts him into the ring...] TS: Harrison has made himself an advantage by working on Ramacees outside of the ring. That's not a bad idea, seeing that he didn't get himself disqualified. FJ: Yep, that's my kind of wrestling. [Harrison follows Ramacees into the ring, and then stands him up. He delivers a few knife edged chops, and then whips him into the ropes. Ramacees comes off the rebound and Harrison nails him with a big tilt-a- whirl back breaker, still working on the back. Harrison stands up, and signals that the match is coming to a close. He throws Ramacees into the ropes again, and when Ramacees comes off the rebound Harrison nails him with a big spinebuster slam, sending shockwaves throughout the whole arena...] [Voice-Over starts up again.] VO: "Smasher" John Harrison. Does he win because he uses illegal tactics? Or does he win because he's a great wrestler? You be the judge. [Harrison stands Ramacees up, and throws him into the corner. Harrison walks to the corner, and puts Ramacees on the top rope. Harrison climbs the ropes himself, and locks Ramacees up, and nails him with a top rope brainbuster, The Devastator. Harrison puts a lazy cover on Ramacees as the ref's in the position to make the count: 1------2------3! BIG HEEL POP!...] [A split-screen is now shown with "Smasher" John Harrison on the left and Ramacees on the right. The EMWO Heavyweight Title now is seen faintly in the distance.] VO: Tonight these men do battle to decide just who the overrated wrestler is! Tonight on TUESDAY TERROR! [The screen then shatters into thousands of pieces as we're taken to the old U.S. Arena in Washington, D.C. The capacity crowd jumps to its feet as they find out they're on the air. The crowd, arrayed in the all new EMWO merchandise, pump their fists in the air, screaming in syllables, "MAF-I-A, MAF-I-A!". All of a sudden, fireworks shoot out of their canisters atop the huge video wall above the entrance curtains. The fireworks, screaming as they spiral out of their canisters, arch up into the air, and then all of a sudden explode, showering down into thousands of white sparks, dissapearing as they touch the steel-plated entrance way.] [The Mafia chant is still being chanted in unison, the fireworks still shooting out of their canisters. The lights then turn off, and blue, red, and purple spotlights are put into the center of the ring, circumnutating around a figure, a figure that we've all come to know as 'Confident' Lawrence Williams, President of the EMWO. Lawrence, with an arrogant look on his face, smirks as the cameras flash, igniting the sky in a bright yellow light. The lights then come back on, and the fireworks display is over. The Mafia chant dies down to a minimum, and Lawrence Williams is left in the ring, with a microphone in his right hand, and a blue and white Los Angeles Dodgers hat on his head, twisted to the right.] LW: Ladies and gentlemen, WELCOME to EMWO's Tuesday Terror! [The lights then turn off, and twenty foot flames shoot out of each of the four ringposts. The flames wave back and forth, making the crowd pop in excitement. They start the chant of "MAF-I-A" again, once again pumping their fists in unison. The flames, lighting up even the nukes and crannies of the U.S. Arena, die down, and then the lights come back on, and Lawrence is once again left.] LW: Once again the EMWO has showed the reason why we were given 1st place in the EMWO Magazine Fireworks Display Competition! Once again, hello boys and girls; ladies and gentlemen to yet another Tuesday Terror. Steve Benoit is at home, having a family problems, so I'm subsituting at the announcing position. [A small heel pop goes around the crowd, leaving Lawrence silent, waiting for it to die down.] LW: Ladies and gentlemen, the main event of tonight's card will dissapoint you not! Jack Vars and "Smasher" John Harrison will fight in a match to prove who's the real underrated wrestler, and who's just hype! But to much demand, I have made the match, a NO DISQUALIFICATIONS MATCH! [A hella big face pop erupts from the crowd. The fans jump to their feet, throwing both hands in the air, screaming for the hardcore main event match.] LW: That's right. "Smasher" John Harrison Vs. Jack Vars in a no dqs match! Enjoy the show! [A small pop goes through the crowd as Lawrence throws the mic to an unknown source, and rolls out of the ring, and walks ever-so-arrogantly to the Tuesday Terror announcing table where Tommy Sessons awaits.] TS: Welcome, Mr. Williams. LW: Thank you, Tommy. I'm glad to be back where I should have been in the first place. You know, I might give Duke Williams a break from the in-ring interviews, and give the job to Steve Benoit. TS: You'd be announcing for Tuesday Terror then? LW: No. That would belong to "Bad Boy" Bruce Bingham. TS: Lord, no! He's not coming back, is he? LW: Oh, he's going to bigger and better than ever. I may decide to do announcing at Saturday Night Live. It'd be Jenkins, you, and me, announcing for the main event of EMWO shows! TS: Oh boy! That would be fun. Why would you want to give the veteran Duke a break? I mean that man has been doing interviews for you and [name withheld] since Internet Pro Wrestling, Generation 1! LW: True. But he's come to me complaining about the treatment that he gets from a few EMWO superstars. I won't mention any names, Destroyer. I won't tolerate anymore harrassment, Destroyer! TS: I thought you weren't mentioning any names. LW: I'm not. I'm just using Destroyer as an example. Don't worry, it's not Destroyer that I'm talking about. Well, what's up, Tommy? TS: Nothing much. LW: No, I mean on the card! TS: Oh. Well, in the first match of the night, EMWO tag team competition starts as the Element of Pain gets ready to take on the JoA! I still do not know what the JoA means! Do you know, Lawrence? LW: Nope. So guy thought it might stand for Jobbers of America, but I don't know what a jobber is. Do you? TS: Nope. JoA Writer: [name withheld] :::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: In the beginning of the match, Element started the match off for EoP. Element locked up with Tom, and immediately got the match started off in his favor by nailing him with a big gut wrench suplex. The next series of moves consisted of drop kicks, DDT, snap suplex, and a bodyslam. Element went for the cover, but Scott, Tom's partner came in and broke up the count. Wound then came in and short arm clotheslined Scott out of the ring. Wound went out of the ring to work on Scott, nailing him with a big whip into the security railing, and a smash on the ring post. Inside the ring, however, Element maintained control of the match. Hitting Tom with a guillotine leg drop off the top rope, and a big double underhook powerbomb. Element failed to cover, however, as he climbed to the top rope, and nailed Scott with a big corkscrew plancha. The crowd went unequivocally beserk when this happened. Element and Wound double teamed Scott on the outside, forearm and double axehandle smashing him on his back, and then nailing him with a big double suplex. The ref almost counted Element out, but Mr. Menace hopped on the apron, and distracted him. Element then told Wound to get in the match, and Wound did just that. Wound rolled into the ring, and Tom jumped him. Stomping on his back and such, Tom tried to get the advantage. He stood Wound up, but Wound absolutely devasted him with a big football tackle, picking Tom up in the air atleast three feet. As Wound worked on Tom inside the ring, Element was having his fun with Scott outside the ring. Wound then gave Element "the sign", and Element quickly disposed of Scott by nailing him with a front face suplex, but only into the front row of the audience! Element then hopped on the apron as Wound, in the ring, held Tom up on his shoulders, and Element jumped off the top rope, and nailed him with a big drop kick. Wound then covered Scott as Element simply posed in the ring with Mr. Menace who had silently migrated into the ring during EoP's finisher. WINNERS: Element of Pain - 8:23 TS: Oh my God! What an extreme match we just saw! Here comes the EMTs down to ringside to help the JoA up off the ground. I think they're going to need a spatula to get Scott out of the audience. This looks like a car wreck! LW: Okay, Jim Ross. TS: Why does everyone say that when I say something looks like a car wreck. LW: Because that's what Jim Ross always says. TS: Who the heck is Jim Ross? LW: Some pleasently plump loser who works up in Stamford, Connecticut. That was a hardcore match that we've just seen, and I've very interested in hardcore matches all of a sudden. Usually I'm not a very big fan on hardcore matches, but these fans really liked what they saw, so I'm going to watch the main event closely! Tommy? TS: Well, I'm sure that the D-Brigade is very content with the debut of Element of Pain! These guys looked really good, and they are great teammates! A lot of teamwork is on EoP's side! LW: It sure is. Well, the next match will have another D-Brigade member, Destroyer, taking on "Tha True Gangsta" Tyrone Johnson! What about this match, Tommy? TS: Well, I have a few words. The D-Brigade will be five and zero after this match! That's all I've got to say! LW: Whoa! Tommy's trying to go-hard! "Tha True Gangsta" Tyrone Johnson Writer: [name withheld] :::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: In so far the most evenly matched match, Destroyer started off the match taking it to Tyrone Johnson. Starting the match off with a big knee lift, and then following that with a big swinging neck breaker told Tyrone Johnson that he'd better shape up, or ship out. Destroyer hit a series of moves that consisted of a running powerslam, double underhook suplex, short arm clothesline, fallaway slam, and a big splash in the corner. Destroyer then whipped Johnson into the corner, and went for a running back elbow, but Tyrone Johnson moved out of the way, crashing Destroyer into the turnbuckle. Johnson then nailed Destroyer with a flurry of kicks to his chest, knocking Destroyer down into the corner, only squatting because his arms were hooked around the ropes. Johnson then placed Destroyer on the top rope, and then in a display of true athleticism drop kicked Destoyer to the outside mat. This received a bigger pop than Element's corkscrew plancha off the top rope. Johnson threw his hands up in the air, and the crowd responded in a big face pop. Mr. Menance, however, helped Destroyer up from the devasting move. Then from out of the crowd came in Element, and he had a chair. He came from behind Tyrone Johnson, and gave him a good thwack in the back, sending him down to his face, receiving another big face pop. Element hopped out of the ring with the chair, and then the referee turned around to see Tyrone Johnson knocked out. The referee didn't see what happened, but Mr. Menace rolled Destroyer into the ring. Destoyer stood Tyrone Johnson up, and then nailed him with a big Destroyer Bomb, and pinned him. WINNER: Destroyer - 6:21 LW: Man, what a match we just saw. Destroyer had the match the whole time, but Tyrone Johnson had one last wind. He drop kicked Destoyer to the outside. That single move received a bigger pop than I did! TS: Lawrence, they booed you. LW: No they didn't. They were saying Mooooooooose. Like that guy who plays football for the Dallas Cowgirls. TS: Oh Lord! LW: Well, Element is really a fan favorite here so far. He just thwacked Tyrone Johnson good in the back, and Tyrone Johnson is out. They're helping him out of the ring. That's the second time the EMTs had to come down and help a wrestler get up. Tuesday Terror should renamed Extreme Tuesday, or something. TS: No! That wouldn't be good for public relations, Lawrence. You've got to think about your and EMWO's status here now. It's not just you wrestling anymore, man. LW: What the hell are you talkin' about, Tommy? You're a wacko. Now, it's time for the last match before the highly anticipated hardcore main event match! TS: It has "Mr. Charisma" Christian York going up against Mystic. Now what do you think about Mr. York, Lawrence? LW: I think this guy is crazy and dillusional. I don't know who he thinks he is going around telling_me_what to do. He'll end up finding his butt fined if he doesn't watch out. TS: Did you see his training session? LW: Yeah. That was pretty damn funny. TS: What kind of President are you, man? C'mon! That wasn't funny, that was very disrespectful! LW: Yeah right. You just have to learn how to broaden your horizons, Tommy! TS: Broaden my horizons? LW: Yeah! You heard me. This guy is funny, yet you make yourself succumb into this man's "cracks." They're meant for funny, Tommy. Take it easy. Just because he called your Tony "The Tiger" doesn't mean that you have to hate this man's guts for the rest of your ever-boring life! TS: I'm very dissapointed in you, Lawrence. LW: Oh yeah. Guess what? TS: What? LW: They're grrrrrrrrrrrreat! TS: Oh whatever, man! ____ _ _ _ _ ___ | ___|| \/ |\\ /\ /// _ \::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: | __| | | \\//\\// ||_|| TUESDAY TERROR |____||_||_| \/ \/ \___/ "Mr. Charisma" Christian York Vs. Mystic Writer: [name withheld] :::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: In the fastest match of the night, Christian York absolutely destroyed Mystic. Christian came down to ringside with a bottle of Mistic and his manager, Denise. York climbed into the ring, and the offended Mystic charged him. He nailed him with a flurry of rights and lefts to the head, and then whipped Christian York into the ropes. York came off the rebound and nailed Mystic with a clothesline; the match was over. York then went on to nail Mystic with a big spiked piledriver, DDT, powerbomb, gut wrench suplex, front face suplex, and big powerslam. Christian York was out here to prove a point, and a point he proved. Denise then hopped on the apron and York slid out of the ring, and grabbed his glass Mistic bottle. York then rolled into the ring, and then gutted Mystic with the bottle, and then dinged Mystic aross his face with the glass as if he were going for a home run. Denise then hopped off the apron, and the referee turned around, only to see Christian York nail Mystic with his finisher "The Class Act", and then covered. WINNER: "Mr. Charisma" Christian York - 4:54 TS: See, Lawrence? That is totally sickening. There's no way that York should be able to compete in the EMWO with that kind of crap! LW: Tommy, chill, bro! You're killin' me here. Cheating and using foreign objects are all apart of the game! It's going to happen, and the ref never sees this! I think it was a beautiful strategy by Christian and it was well executed. That's just the way it goes. TS: Oh man. I'm not going to be looking forward to Saturday Night Lives. LW: Don't worry, Tommy. The changes don't take place this week, but Steve said that he won't be back for awhile, so you're lookin' at your co- host for the next three plus weeks! TS: Sweet Jesus! Let's get to the main event! LW: What are you talking about? Before we get to the main event we always talk about the main event! You're not focusing, Tommy. TS: Oh yeah. Well, since you made this a no disqualifications match, expect anything and everything. Me knowing Jack Vars, this man is going to be using everything he can! LW: Well, fans, here it is. This is the determining factor that tells me whether or not to use more hardcore matches in the EMWO! If all goes well in this match, expect to be seeing some_major_changes in the card lineup of the EMWO! Maybe even Independence Day Decimation! TS: Whoah! ____ _ _ _ _ ___ | ___|| \/ |\\ /\ /// _ \::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: | __| | | \\//\\// ||_|| TUESDAY TERROR |____||_||_| \/ \/ \___/ Jack Vars Vs. "Smasher" John Harrison Writer: [name withheld] :::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: After Harrison was called to the ring, Jack Vars didn't even wait for Duke to announce him. He came flying down to ringside with a garbage can and nailed Harrison who had his back turned in the ring. Vars started nailing Harrison with rights and lefts, and then nailed him with a big belly to belly suplex, flattening Harrison on his back. Vars then hopped out of the ring, and threw a chair inside the ring. The fans immediately gave him a big pop, and started chanting "Vars, Vars, Vars, Vars." Vars sat the chair down, and then picked Harrison up, and threw him into a standing head scissors. He then picked Harrison up, and delivered a punishing spiked piledriver right on the seat of the chair. This received without a doubt the biggest pop of the night. Vars picked up the lacerated Harrison, and slammed his head on the chair again. Vars then moved the chair out of the way and covered Harrison. Harrison kicked out after a long two count, and then Vars stood him up again. Vars whipped Harrison into the ropes, and off the rebound, Vars nailed Harrison with a big powerslam right onto the chair. Another big face pop was received from the crowd as the crowd was loving the hardcore match. Vars wouldn't stop there. He used the chair to cut Harrison on the forehead, and then to crack him on the back of his head. The crowd was absolutely electric. Vars then took the match outside when he threw Harrison into the front row of the audience. Vars stepped up onto the top rope, but right when he stepped off someone must have tampered with the controls, because the flames used in the beginning of the match suddenly shot out of the ringposts, charcoling Vars in his back. Vars landed into the front row, but Harrison managed to move, making Vars break two chairs on the way down. Harrison and Vars, both down, were barely moving. Harrison was the first to move, though. He stood Vars up, and slammed his head into the security railing. Burnt pieces of Vars' back fell to the ground, making a little girl scream. Harrison then took one guy's beer, and poured it on the scabs on Vars' back, intesifying Vars' pain. Harrison then took another beer, fliped Vars over the railing and onto his back. Harrison took the cup of beer, and splashed it in Vars' face. Harrison then took Vars, and threw him into the ring. Harrison got under the ring, and came back with a table. The crowd immediately gave him a pop as he set the table up. Vars was still in the ring, rolling around in crucial pain. Harrison put the table adjacent to the ring, and then slid into the ring. Harrison picked Vars up, and put him into a standing headscissors. A powerbomb through a table was set for Vars. Harrison tried to pick Vars up, but something kicked in in Vars' senses, and he blocked it, and picked Harrison up, nailing him with a big backbody drop onto the table. Vars took awhile to regain focus, and he saw that Harrison was lying on the table, but the table wasn't broken. Vars immediately went to the top rope, and threw his hands in the air. The crowd responded in one helluva big pop, and Vars nailed Harrison with a 480 splash on Harrison, breaking the table. Vars, surged with adrenaline stood the broken Harrison up, and rolled him into the ring, and got in, but not before getting a hug from Cindy McGlothlan. Vars rolled into the ring, and signaled for his finisher. He asked Cindy for something, and Cindy reached under the apron, and pulled out yet another table! The crowd then started chanting "Cindy, Cindy." All she did was wave and roll the table into the ring. Vars picked it up, and stood it right next to the turnbuckles. Vars picked Harrison up, and stood him on the top rope, and then threw him into the standing headscissors. He signalled for the varSDrop, and the crowd went balistic. Vars picked him up, and hopped off the turnbuckle, smashing through the table, nailing Harrison with a big piledriver off the top rope through a table. Vars didn't even cover Harrison, he just landed on him. Winner: Jack Vars - 16:40 TS: Jesus Christ! Look at the carnage in the ring. I don't believe that these two men just went through this! I just don't believe it. They both just have to have respect for one another, because what these two men just went through is best desribed as Hell! LW: Well, I'm thinking about this match, and the way that the crowd interacted, I just might start making some more hardcore matches in the EMWO. I'll have to talk to Killjoy and Doomsday. TS: Look at this! Neither man can even sit up. They're both out. Here comes the EMTs for the third time in four matches. This has to be a world record or something! Today's Tuesday will go down in EMWO history as the most hardcore card ever! LW: It just might, Tommy. It just might. Well, we're out of time. For Tommy Sessons, and the whole EMWO family, I'm the President, Lawrence Williams signing off for Tuesday Terror!
