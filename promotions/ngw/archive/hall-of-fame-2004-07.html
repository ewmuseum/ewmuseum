Hall of Fame

July 2004

NGW Hall of Fame Inducted: 2-9-00 Title History: NGW World Title(1), NGW American Title(1), NGW Tag Team Title(1), NGW Triple Tag Titles(1) Reason Inducted: He Started the XWF/NGW along with Scottyood and became the 1st ever President. Ice Inducted: 2-9-00 Title History: NGW World(3), NGW American(3), NGW Transatlantic(1), NGW Untamed(1), NGW Xtreme(6), NGW Tag Team(3), NGW Triple Tag Team(2), NGW Hardcore(1) Reason Inducted: Scottywood helped create the federation along with Ice. He takes care of the web site, apps, promoting, deals with wrestlers. He brough the NGW out of the dead after a failed DSW deal and created the NGW. Even with running the fed he has also managed to wrestle and be one of the most decorated wrestlers in the fed. Scottywood Inducted: 3-24-00 Title History: NGW American Title(1), NGW Transatlantic Title(2), NGW Triple Tag Title(1) Reason Inducted: He was the XWF's first commish. Talented wrestler but had his career cut short due too lack of time. The Magician Inducted: 6-2-00 Title History: NGW World Title(2), NGW American Title(2), NGW Xtreme Title(1), NGW Tag Team Titles(2) Reason Inducted: The Denouncer has helped the NGW out tremendously. His input on subjects and level mind have saved the NGW from trouble. Another great wrestler who has had many famous brawls with Scottywood in some insane matches. The Denouncer Inducted: 6-2-00 Title History: NGW Transatlantic Title(1), NGW Tag Team Title(1), NGW Triple Tag Teams Titles(1) Reason Inducted: Prime Time has written some of the best matches in NGW history like the main event at Xtrememania I, the Battle Royal where HBK won his 1st World Title. He also has had one of the longest NGW Title reigns when he held the Transatlantic Title for 90 days. Prime Time Inducted: 6-2-00 Title History: NGW World Title(1), NGW Transatlantic Title(1), NGW Untamed Title(1), NGW Triple Tag Team Title(1) Reason Inducted: Calvin Riley who was known as Revolution X for much of his NGW career has been on of the biggest contributors to the NGW. Helping out any way he can with results, publicity and ideas. He helped make the newest NGW web site which is to debut at Xtrememania III. After retireing from wrestling in the NGW he became the chairman and wrestlered in the PWA, NTICW and for GW. He was the person we introduced the NGW to PTC and without his help the NGW would never have the publicity we do today. Calvin Riley (Revolution X) Inducted: 7-8-00 Title History: NGW Tag Team Title(1), NGW Hardcore Title(1) Reason Inducted: Great helper with results in the early XWF days. He retired early and became the XWF Commish. X-Rated Inducted: 7-25-00 Title History (Combined): NGW World(1), NGW American(2), NGW European(3), NGW Xtreme(2), NGW Tag Team(2), NGW Hardcore(2), NGW Women's(2) Reason Inducted: The Hardy Boyz, Dex and Lita who were controled by a man known as Slacker has been one of the most helpfull staff memebers ever. If it isn't writting results, help book TNS, come up with ideas, keep Scotty in check then it is something else. Slacker dominated the ring and the backstage and was one of the most productive wrestlers in the fed's history. Without him the NGW may not be where it is. The Hardy Boyz/Dex/Lita (Slacker) Inducted: 10-27-00 Title History: NGW World(1), NGW American(1), NGW Untamed(1), NGW Tag Team(2), NGW Hardcore(1), KOX(1) Reason Inducted: Being one of the most carefree wrestleres helped establish him as one of the most hardcore wrestlers in the fed. He was the creator of the NGW Untamed Title after throwing the Hardcore Title over the Hoover Dam and the burning it after it was recovered. Brian Hardy Inducted: 9-19-01 Title History: NGW European(1), NGW Xtreme(1), NGW Untamed(2), NGW Women's(2) Reason Inducted: Trish Stratus was the 1st ever NGW women to win a man's title, no not just once but 4 different times. She also dominated the women's divison with 2 different title reigns. She is a inspiration for women everywhere who want to be as equal as men. Trish Stratus Inducted: 6-16-02 Title History: NGW World Title(1), NGW American Title(3), NGW Tag Team Titles(2), Reason Inducted: In a era where real wrestlers graced the XWF, Triple H was one of the most dominating forces in the old XWF. Winning the NGW World title a record 4 times and holding the American Title for a record 92 days HHH has set records that others will have a hard time trying to break. Triple H Inducted: 6-16-02 Title History: NGW American Title(2), NGW Transatlantic Title(2), NGW Xtreme Title(2), NGW Untamed Title(1), 2002 Royal Rumble Winner Reason Inducted: Even though Bloody Roar has never won the World Title dosen't mean he isn't a legend. Winning everthing but the World and Tag Titles, if he ever returns he could become the 3rd ever All-Around champion. Known alot for his work out of the ring, in the staff Bloody Roar was the backbone of the NGW for over a year. His help and mind helped raise the NGW to the federation it is today. Bloody Roar Inducted: 6-16-02 Title History: NGW World Title(1), NGW American Title(2), NGW Transatlantic Title(1), NGW Tag Team Title(2), NGW 3 Man Tag Title(1), 2001 Royal Rumble Winner Reason Inducted: With a great historu of titles and acomplishment Tuff Tony has shown he is one of the best wrestlers to grace the NGW. Also his help as Commish in the NGW helped make the NGW grow during the years. Once part of the great Unlimited stable Tuff has left wrestling due to plaging injuries, though with the hope of one day returning to the ring. Tuff Tony Inducted: 6-16-02 Title History: NGW Transatlantic Champion(1), NGW Untamed Champion(1) Reason Inducted: Even though this great wrestler spent little time in the NGW the mark left bvy him was huge. His artistic skill with Poser helped boost the NGW into a new level in wrestling. As DWR of the NGW he helped draw talent to the NGW and write some great matches for the NGW transcripts. If not for problems whihc cut his career short he would hqve most certainly become the NGW World Champion some day. The Charm Inducted: 6-16-02 Title History: NGW American Title(1), NGW Transatlantic Title(1), NGW Xtreme Title(1), NGW Women's Title(1) Reason Inducted: Some say he has had the greatest rookie run of any NGW wrestler ever. This man has gone from regular wrestler to NGW American Champion, co-leader of the biggest stable in NGW and Vice President of the NGW. It is without a doubt he is the current backbone of NGW, being head writter and ruling with an iron fist when it comes to slacking wrestlers. Time Bomb will most certainly be a name associated with greatness in the NGW as he has helped the NGW sore to the levels it has in the PTC interfed world. Time Bomb Inducted: 6-16-02 Title History: NGW World Title(2), NGW American Title(1), NGW Transatlantic Title(1), NGW Untamed Title(2), NGW Xtreme Title(1), NGW Tag Team Title(1), NGW Triple Tag Titles(1) Reason Inducted: Being crowned the 2nd ever NGW All-Around champion is an accomplishment only him and owner Scottywood have achived to this date. He has proven he can beat anyone at any level and that he is truly one of the best wrestlers in the NGW. Jeremy Davis will be known as the ultimate fan favorite who the fan were crazy about and a wrestling style few opponets could beat. Jeremy Davis Inducted: 3-22-03 Title History: NGW Tag Team Title(1) Reason Inducted: John Hitchin known as Commish Hitchin through most of his NGW carrer was one of the most rock solide bad ass staffers in NGW and certainly someone you don't wanna cross. In the ring he was even meaner. Shattering the old record for longest Tag Team Title reign which stands at 243 and is still going. He has alos been apart of two of the most lethal allainces in NGW, HitchBomb Industries and Methods of Mayhem. He is also the man that revolutionized the money system that NGW now uses. John Hitchin Inducted: 3-22-03 Title History: NTW Tag Team Title(1), AW Tag Team Title(1) Staff Jobs: NGW Director of Talent, NGW CEO, AW Vice President, Co-Owner of AW Reason Inducted: KrazE not known for his wrestling talent as much as his staffing but he is equally as good in both. He has helped NGW rebuild from the split with AW and has brought numerous wrestlers to the fed. In the ring he has won both the NTW and AW Tag Team Titles, never actually loosing either belt. Also he is one of the only undefeated wrestlers in AW. He is still currently with NGW and will hopefully help the fed succed in its new enviroment RS. KrazE Inducted: 3-22-03 Staff Jobs: NGW Chairman Reason Inducted: Munny McMahon is one of the main people that help NGW make a name for itself in PTC. After trouble with PTC it was NGW that went out on a limb to sign the idea filled staffer, and the risk paid off 10 fold. McMahon went on one of the biggest signing sprees in PTC history signing talent for NGW. His ideas and motivation helped the fed succed in PTC. McMahon later left NGW and PTC and decided to start his own interfed, the current home of NGW....Revolution Studios. Munny McMahon Inducted: 2-10-04 Title History: NGW Tag Team Titles(1) Reason Inducted: One of the greatest Tag Team Wrestlers in NGW history may have only won the title once but dominated while he held it. A truly dedicated wrestler in NGW BigBadBrad did not fear anyone and would never back down from a fight, or a beer. It seemed at times he fought more in a bar then he did in the ring. He comes out of retirement next month to try and improve his HOF career with a win in the biggest match of the year...The Battle Dome. BigBadBrad
