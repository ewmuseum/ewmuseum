Hokori Meiyo 04 — Warriors

December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Welcome...Warriors of the World Soon you all will be embarking on a journey where your paths, have yet to be determined. Over the next several weeks, you will encounter many obstacles on your path, other warriors with the same aspirations as yourself will stand before you with the same goal in mind...to conquer. Each week you will be tasked to prepare for your upcoming opponent, a maximum of 3 role plays per round will be admitted. There are no seedings, in war, a number next to your name is meaningless, what is done on the field of battle is remembered. There are no restrictions as to who can join. Meaning, whether you are in an independent fed, or you are in PTC or any other interfed, all warriors will be allowed to participate. Each round will be judged by a predetermined amount of judges. Judges will review the role plays, vote on who they deem victorious. The accumulated total of votes will declare a decisive winner. Decisions are final, accept defeat with honor, and as a lesson, to improve on your weaknesses, so that one day, you may turn the tide and stand above your vanquished enemies...victorious.
