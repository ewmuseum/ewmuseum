Who is the President?

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Who is the President? - Riley Reporter Chris Smith here at NGW headquarters in New York, New York. Lately a lot of questions have been posed concerning the identity of our staff here in NGW. We of course know that Scottywood, will again, be at the helm of NGW, that Benny Juventud despite recent contraversy will take the role of CEO, and the Cheif of Operations job has been taken by Corey Gibson, but two positions remain blank on the list of staff members on NGW.com. The buzz as of late has been surrounding the unknown Presidential identity here in NGW. He or she has promised to send some kind of message during the upcoming Tuesday Night Storm, but we still do not know if it will be done in person, or via some other entity. One thing is for sure- nobody knows the true identity of the President of Neo-NGW. Despite that, we are sure that whomever it may be, will continue in the tradition of great Presidents in NGW. The questions still remains: Who is the President? Friday, February 14, 2003 TNS Card Up!!
