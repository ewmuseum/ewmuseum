The Summery Show: NGW Tuesday Night Storm Date: 2-22-05 Location: Hartford Civic Center; Hartford, CT Match #1 Xtreme Title Match Jorge Samuelsson vs. Robb Hardy (Hardcore Match) Winner: Jorge Samuelsson Match #2 American Title Match Scottywood vs. HBC vs. Reggie Rivid vs. Adrian Williams (Fatal Fourway Match) Winner: Scottywood Match #3 NGW World Heavyweight Title Math John Hitchin vs. Frankie the Cameraman (Singles Match) Winner: John Hitchin Decision was reversed by NGW president Andrew O'Brien the following day. Scene opens with Scottywood sitting at his desk and shaking his head. He is looking at a list of entrants into the 2005 Battle Dome.:: Scottywood: I see 17 entrants….were at least 13 short….well, were gonna have to change that….Time to hit the phones and start making some calls. ::Scottywood smiles as the camera fades out to the Storm opening with clips from the past 5 years of Storm. “Survival of the Sickest” by Saliva plays over the P.A. system of the sold out Hartford Civic Center as the crowd goes wild. I’m the one, the only, Shane Logan and with me is the ever so perverted Terrence Reed. Terrence: Damn Straight! Shane: We got 3 big matches for ya tonight, including the American and World titles on the line. Terrence: Tonight is the night that Scottywood looses that title. Shane: It could be as he fights 3 other wrestlers in a fatal four way match. But up first we have Jorge Samuelsson versus Robb Hardy. Terrence: Not more Hardy, ug. I’m gonna take a crap. Shane: Hey! Nothin but a fuckin coward! Just like Scottywood, just like Hitchin, just like all those fuckin assholes in the back. ::Suddenly “Voices” by Disturbed blasts over the P.A. system and the Storm doors open and out walks NGW President Andrew O’Brien. He stays on the stage with mic in hand as the crowd is cheering that he has shut up Hardy, for the moment.:: Andrew: Well well well Mr. Hardy you decided to try and trash talk me in the back. Well guess what im a boss who can kick your ass across the ground and back again. Now since you have decided to be an ass I think I am going to put some drastic points across here! Hardy: Ya? So the fuck what? Andrew: Here is a few tidbits for you from the guys in the back. Nobody likes you least of all myself. You’ve done nothing but whined and bitched and moaned. Now there is something I can do about it! :: The crowd hushes into a jumbled mass of sounds and whispers.:: Andrew: Your ass is FIRED! Get the hell out of my show! ::Hardy’s jaw drops as the shock quickly turns to anger.:: Hardy: Your firing me!?! Well fuck you! If your firing me then I’m gonna tear this fuckin place apart! :: Andrew O’Brien goes to step towards the ring tearing off his shirt as his large form stares down Hardy.:: Andrew: Touch anything on this show and I’ll kick your ass from here to Zimbabwe! ::Hardy pulls out a 3 inch knife from his pocket and opens it up and is about to start tearing up the ring floor with it, when suddenly:: ::OOOOOOOOOHHHHHHHHH, You gotta keep em separated.:: ::“Boulevard of Broken Dreams” by Green Day plays and the Civic Center explodes. The Storm door opens and out walks Scottywood in a Wolfpack jersey and a hockey stick wrapped in barbed wire.:: Shane: Its Scottywood and a barbed wire hockey stick! Terrence: Shit man….. ::Scottywood runs downs the ramp towards the ring and slides in. Hardy raises the knife for defense but Scotty swings with the stick which is much longer and connects to the head of Hardy knocking him to the mat.:: Shane: Scottywood shoots and scores! ::Scottywood drops the stick on the mat and lifts Hardy to his feet. Reggie Rivid! :: “Hit the Lights” by Metallica blasts over the P.A. system as the Storm door opens and Rivid makes his way down the ramp.:: Shane: The resident veteran here in NGW, looking to add another title to his great NGW career Timothy Ferra: And one of his opponents, from Vancouver, British Columbia and weighing in at 265 pounds….Mr. High Society, Adrian Williams! :: “All My Life” by the Foo Fighters plays over the P.A. system as the Storm door opens and out walks Mr. Adrian Williams and Miss Rhiannon.:: Shane: And when you see Mr. Williams you always have to keep the thought of watching out for Mr. Hitchin in the back of your mind, especially Scottywood. Terrence: Here is the man that is going to dethrone Scottywood of his title tonight. I just have that feeling. Timothy Ferra: And now making her way to the ring from Toronto, Canada and weighing in at 140 pounds…NGW’s Resident Diva….The HeartBreak Chick! :: “LaLa” by Ashlee Simpson plays through the loudspeaker as little red hearts fall from the ceiling. Shane: Because your not a wrestler. ::HBC motions to the crowd that the title belt will be hers as she struts to the ring and flips over the ring ropes, climbing to the top with her arms outstretched before back flipping off into the middle of the ring.:: ::OOOOOOOHHHHHH, You gotta keep em Separated:: ::“Boulevard of Broken Dream” by Green Day plays as The Civic Center explodes with cheers. This isn’t gonna be as easy as his match at Out Cold. HBC, Adrian and Rivid are all blood thirsty for that title and they will not let Scottywood go with one hell of a fight. Terrence: I hope some blood is spilled tonight, especially Scottywood. I can’t wait to see him laid out in that ring was any of those 3 wrestlers holds that title above their head. ::The referee calls for the bell as Scottywood slides out of the ring and as the other 3 wrestler start attacking each other.:: Terrence: What a cowardly move by Scottywood. Shane: It’s not cowardly, its smart. ::Williams and Rivid are the first to tee off on each other and Scottywood watches contently. But HBC sees Scottywood and climbs the turnbuckles and before Scottywood can notice she flies off the turnbuckle and hits Scottywood with a cross-body block.:: Shane: What a move by HBC! ::HBC gets to her feet and lifts Scottywood to his and slides him into the ring. Scottywood stumbles up to his feet and HBC comes off the ropes going for another cross-body but Scottywood counters with a big boot sending HBC to the mat.:: Shane: And HBC just got laid out by Scottywood. ::Scottywood turns to Williams who is stomping away at Rivid and Scottywood pulls him off Rivid and clothesline him to the mat. Shane: What is he doing out here? ::Hitchin slowly walks down the ramp as the wrestlers start to climb to their feet from the carnage at ring side. HBC is up on her feet and she slides into the ring as Rivid stumbles up and she just connects with the Kiss of Death standing side kick knocking Rivid out.:: Shane: Could be over right here as she goes for the pinfall. Ref: 1…2..no! ::Scottywood is able to slide into the ring and break the cover up at the last second.
