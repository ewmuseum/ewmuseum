More >> HBC Bio Wednesday, March 24, 2004 The Storm before the Havok! - Scott Wilson @ 12:29 am EST. With 1 week untill Border Line everyone was trying to get the leg up going into the PPV. But for some, nothing could go right. Like Birrdy who was defeated by Jeff James or Joe Dimmer who fell victum to the World champion Scott King. And like always Copperhead Red came up short as he was allowed to track John Hitchin down in the arena and try and pin him for the title, but he attacked the wrong guy which led to Hitchin destroying Red for a second time. In the only upset of the night Rob Williams pulled pff a victory over Ty Sanders in a hard fought match. Dallas Reeves also disposed of Jason Starr with some help from enemy Reggie Rivid who Starr pissed off and got a chair shot to the skull for that. But Rivid wasn't done and gave Dallas Reeves a chair shot of his own from Rivid. In the main event it was The HellRaisers getting their 7th Tag Team win with Antheus against Lorenzo Guevera, Robb Hardy and Aesino in a elimination tag match. Sunday, March 21, 2004 Border Line card filling in - Scott Wilson @ 12:47 pm EST. As of today the current Border Line card is shaping up to be one hell of a card. Right now their is 6 big time matches. John Hitchin defends his Xtreme Title against Asesino. Dallas Reeves defends his American Title against Reggie Rivid. Also NGW Owner Scottywoos returns to one on one action against Robb Hardy in a NGW first....Hockey Match. Also 23 of the 30 wrestlers in the Battle Dome match have been announced leaving 7 surprise superstars. To check out the entire Border Line card head to http://ngw.theewe.net/battledome/pc.html Wednesday, March 17, 2004 Storm: Royal Rumble, The Hitch, Asesino arrested, Rivid, Johnson.... - Scott Wilson @ 4:55 pm EST. What a night in Connecticut it was. The card was packed with matches and the event didn't disapoint. Lorenzo Guevera started the event out with a huge upset in his first NGW match against the 4-0 Jeff James. After that we heard from Scott King who spoke on why he spit in the face of freind John Hitchin. Then it was John Hitchin's Battle Dome qualifing and Xtreme title match against Bullet. John easily disposed of the man but that wasn't it. Scottywood came out and stated that was only half the match, and he also had to face Guile. Again John Hitchin easily disposed of Guile but Scottywood was not done. He then announced that John would be entering The Dome in the #1 position. Hitchin was irate but calmed down when Robb Hardy attacked Scottywood from behind busting him open and giving him a mild concussion. Scott King came out and taunted John Hitchin. Also Reggie Rivid defeated The Charm to get a chance to face Dallas Reeves at Border Line 2004 for the American title. Scottywood was then taken to a local Connecticut hospital where he was treated for his cuts and minor concussion. Then we saw Dante Locks and Jack Carrington dispose of Robb Hardy in the 2 out of 3 tables match. In the main event we saw a 7 man royal rumble where the winner would enter The Battle Dome in the number 30 position. The last 4 saw BigBadBrad, Ty Sanders, Asesino and Chris Johnson. Brad was soon eliminated by Sanders and then Sanders eliminated by Asesino. The rookie Asesino faced off against Chris Johnson and almost had the match won a few times but in the end it was Chris Johnson who scored the victory when Asesino hit the outside mat. After the match Sheriff" Dallas Reeves came out with Connecticut State Troopers and had Asesino arrested for Aggravated Assault for cutting off a ladies fingers, Cruelty to Animals for the murder of a dog, Criminal Mischief for the car smashing, Break and Enter and Public Disturbances.
