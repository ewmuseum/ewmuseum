Friday Night eXtreme Location: TCC Arena in Tucson, Arizona Attendance: 12,640 Soul Grinder vs. Herman The match started with a bang from Soul Grinder. He began to run circles around the less then spectacular Herman. Soul Grinder finished him off quick with a few punches to the head and then hitting him with a quick snap suplex for the pin. Soul Grinder got the impressive win over the unorthidox and un-impressive Herman. Winner: Soul Grinder Billy Bush vs. Pax We open to Billy Bush and Pax fighting it out in the ring, Pax is destroying Billy Bush, any defense Bush tries fails and ends in a solid shot from Pax. Pax slides out of the ring and grabs a chair, he slides back into the ring only to have it drop kicked back into his face by Billy Bush who lands horribly after hitting the drop kick hurting him as much as it hurt Pax, both men lye motionless. We cut to Pax who is perched up on the top rope staring down at the rising Billy Bush who does not seem to realize what is coming, Pax jumps off only to have Billy Bush trip over his own feet in fall making Pax over shoot him and land back first on the chair that he took into the ring earlier this match. We cut again to see Billy Bush taking it to Pax for once, he hits a tornado DDT on Pax which shocks the crowd considering all the members of FUK aren't known for their wrestling abilities at the least. Billy Bush follows up the tornado DDT with a climb to the top rope he holds up his arm with one middle finger in the air, FUK's way of saying all or nothing, he fly's off the top rope going for a rolling hangover leg drop, Pax sees him coming as he quickly rolls out of harms way. Billy Bush hits the mat and just lays there motionless. 1… 2… Billy Bush kicks out! Pax seems upset over that as he lifts Billy Bush back to his feet and stares him in his eyes as he picks him up for what appears to be a brain buster only to set Bush on the top rope, Pax smiles as he and bush flip off and he nails a Flipping Powerbomb on Bush, Pax rises after a few seconds then climbs to the top rope and he hits a shooting star press for the win! Winner: Pax 17 Days ::We pick up with the NGW National Champion entering to a bunch of heat from the fans, they turned on him slowly but surely and now it seems he cannot win them back. KrazE gets into the ring.:: ::We cut to select phrases KrazE spoke…:: KrazE- I am done with busting my ass week in and week out trying to please you pieces of crap. This is an all new and improved KrazE and the last thing I am worried about is you fans cheering for me. ::KrazE takes heat for those comments.:: KrazE- I an the National champion and that means I am better than every last one of you and no matter what you will never, ever be at the level I am on. ::KrazE takes even more heat as a "you suck" chant echoes threw the arena.:: KrazE- How did I know you were going to come out with that? What are you pissed that every penny you used on KrazE merchandise has been wasted? Are you… ::The lights shut off and a loud screech can be heard as if all the electricity to the mic have been suddenly shut off. The fans in the arena start screaming, no one knows what's going on. KrazE can be heard from the ring screaming for the power as well. The camera then points at the Generationtron. It shows a bunch of maintenance workers sprinting down a hallway with flashlights. The hallway is just about big enough to fit one man threw. Then come to a room labeled Power Room. Inside it is pitch dark and only a severed wire can be seen flopping around on the floor of the room like a fish out of water. One of the maintance men reach over for the backup switch...:: Voice- I wouldn't if I were you ::The workers slowly back out of the room, but the camera man stays.:: Voice- Listen here KrazE, you think your a great champion don't you. You think that you are the man, correct..? Don't answer that, because I and every other living being in this arena knows the answer. Well your time will come, you are the National Champion. ::KrazE receives a new generator power mic:: KrazE- You damn straight I am, who are you..show yourself you coward!! Voice- My image has nothing to do with this...who am I..? KrazE- Did I stutter? Voice- I am Synxx KrazE- Who the hell are you Synxx? Synxx- The man who has come to test such a champion. KrazE- That's right I AM THE NATIONAL CHAMPION. Synxx- Champion? Champion...oh yes...that belt means you are the national champion...yes you are, but for how long sir? How long until someone of greater magnitude takes you down for the one two three? Just how long? KrazE- As long as I want to be. Synxx- Really? Cause right here right now I have a paper stating my contract with Next Generation Wrestling as well as a match in ink stating that you must defend your title on the 28th of November. KrazE- Oh Yeah...? to who? Synxx- To me. KrazE- Good I'm glad, I'll be ready for you, along with any other half-brained person who wants to try and take it from me. I'll be ready, don't you worry. ::The Generationtron shows not even an outline of a person in the room anymore. The crew re-enters the room and one flicks the back up switch. All the lights are restored, including the one in the Power Room. Just a bunch of wires and a paper can be seen. The camera focuses on the paper and it reads...:: "17 Days" ::The scene cuts out.:: Krazzy Kid + Kold Killa vs. Adrian Williams + Blake Penndragon When the Krazzy Alliance walked down the ramp, they seems to have a cocky swagger. The usual cocky swagger that you would see from they're opponents, Adrian Williams and Blake Penndragon. Kold Killa walked down to the ramp and jumped into the ring right away. The two seemed pumped for this match. Penndragon and Williams strutted down the ring drinking champagne. The threw it into the audience getting a fairly big pop. This match had High Stakes implications as these two tag-teams will be squaring off for the tag team belts in Vegas. Kold Killa and Penndragon started the bout off. Kold Dragon seemed to dominate early with a few kicks to the head. Krazzy Kidd and Kold Killa seemed to know how to work as a tag team keeping Penndragon close to they're corner at all times. They wore him down and went for an eventually pin. Adrian helped his friend kick out by charging from the corner. But while the ref's back was turned, Kold Killa and Krazzy stomped Penndragon. The match continued with Krazzy Kidd whipping Penndragon around the ring like a rag doll. Suddenly, Penndragon used his last ounce of strength to hit a superb hurricanranna on Krazzy and got the quick tag on the Hyped Adrian Williams. Adrian then dominated the match from there on in, he picked up the pin on Kold Killa for the win. Many Me is in a Hercules costume, Louis Lei in his normal nice looking attire, NC 17 in a cheap evening gown, and we all know what Billy Bush is wearing his homemade Greenday tee, cut off black jeans, and his beat down pair of classic van's no matter the weather. They beat on Pax leaving him battered as they run off out of the arena and out of sight. :: Pax: Oh I will get that little piece of crap. :: Pax rises to his feet and dusts himself off as he stares in the direction that FUK ran. :: © 2003 Next Generation Entertainment, All Rights Reserved.
