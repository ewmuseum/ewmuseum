NGW Money System This is the new NGW Money system put together by Commish Hitchin, Scottywood and Time Bomb. The Money system has been implemented to try and boost participation in the NGW. The money system will be run by Commish Hitchin for the most part. The NGW money system all brings with it NGW contracts which will include a salary. If you have any suggestions please contact Scottywood at [detail withheld]. RP MONEY DISTRIBUTION Ok, new way of doing this now…LINES AREN'T BEING CASHED. Instead, the minds of the NGW staff have come together and decided that instead of lines…we're going to GRADE your RP's like you were in school. Creativity, content, resourcefulness, and reference to match are the main things that will be looked at when grading. Every rp submitted by a NGW wrestler shall be graded and then all grades from official AW rp graders will be averaged for a final grade for that wrestler. F = 0 - 50% = $0 EARNED D = 51 - 69% = $10,000 EARNED C- = 70 - 73% = $30,000 EARNED C = 74 - 76% = $40,000 EARNED C+ = 77 - 79% = $50,000 EARNED B- = 80 - 83% = $65,000 EARNED B = 84 - 86% = $80, 000 EARNED B+ = 87 - 89% = $90,000 EARNED A- = 90 - 93% = $100,000 EARNED A = 94 - 96% = $120,000 EARNED A+ = 97 - 100% = $150,000 EARNED HOW RPS WILL BE GRADED Size: The size of the rp will not be graded by the length. If rp grader's are cought mentioning size in there mark, the grade will be terminated and the rp will be graded again. Quality: This is where the most points are lost. It's quality over quanity. An rp with alot of fillers will lose alot of this grade. So make your rp's nice long paragraphs. Ambience: A perfect rp will be very descriptive. Anything that isn't describe will get a low mark: Example, if you say you are going to the mall, also include the name and the size. Storyline: Will be judged on how the rp flows. If you are talking about an oppenent, then all of a sudden, switch to talking about a place, you will loose marks. Make sure that the rp has a purpose too. Technical: Will be judged on who many errors you make and how easy it is to read. So if you have alot of errors and the grader can't read or understand, you will lose massive marks.
