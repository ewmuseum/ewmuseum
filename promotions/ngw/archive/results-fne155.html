Date: 10-16-03 Location: Hartford Civic Center Capacity: 13,467 Date: 10-16-03 Location: Hartford Civic Center Capacity: 13,467 Conquest vs. Phoenix Rose Seems like Conquest was not ready for this match at all. Rose dominated from the beginning tossing Conquest around the ring like a toy. Conquest had a brief moment of offence late in the match where he hit a super kick on Rose. Conquest climbs up the turnbuckles and went for the Eye of the Dragon which is a Shooting Star Press. But when Conquest turned his back to prepare for his finisher Rose jumped up from the mat and when Conquest was upside down in mid air Rose hit a nasty spear. He lifts Conquest up off the mat and tucks his neck into his stomach and hits his finisher Ashes to Ashes/Dust to Dust and picks up the pinfall. Winner: Phoenix Rose Big Bad Brad vs. Van Heist At first it was made clear that during this match the Xtreme Title was not 24/7 nor as it on the line. The match itself was back and forth. Both men trading move after move. But then Brad clotheslined Van Heist to the outside. Brad then slammed Van heist head first into the ring post. Brad then lifted Heist up and slammed him down on the ring barricade. At this time the referee got to a ten count and throw the match out. Brad cures at the referee and then grabs Van Heist and throws him onto the announcer table. He climbed up their with him and raises him up onto his shoulders for the BigBadBomb. But Van Heist someone was able to fight back with some rights to the head of Brad dazing him. Heist then raised himself up and stood on Brad's Shoulders and dropped down and converted it into his Unforgiven sit down piledriver finisher through the announce table. Brad was taken out on a stretcher and Van Heist had to be helped up the run way by the NGW trainer. Winner: Double DQ Ricky Blaze vs. Outblaze We open to Ricky Blaze tossing Outblaze into the corner and pounding away at him... The ref pulls Ricky Blaze off of the now dazed Outblaze that seems to be unable to defend himself against the relentless assault that Ricky Blaze is bringing to the table in this match.... We cut to the half way point in the match where we still see Ricky Blaze on top but he is now just having fun with Outblaze by tossing him around and dodging his many attempts to defend himself.... We now see what appears to be near the end of the match where Ricky Blaze makes a pin and lifts up the motionless Outblaze buying himself more time to destroy him.... We cut to a powerful move by Ricky Blaze that makes the fans all at the same time in sync scream "holy shit!", Rickey Blaze follows it up with a quick pin and the win. Winner: Ricky Blaze Scottywood + KrazE vs. Adrian Williams + Blake Penndragon Scottywood and KrazE started to take advantage of the No DQ stipulation of this match quickly as the came down to the ring with a shopping cart full of goodies. Trash can, hockey sticks, taser, playstation 2 and more. Scottywood is wearing a Red Sox's Jersey and gets a mixed reaction from the crowd who seems to be split of Red Sox’s and Yankee fans. Adrian and Blake try to make their way out but Scottywood and KrazE cut them off and begin a brutal attack sending them all over and off the ramp. Late in the match which has finally gotten into the ring. Two tables are set up next to each other and a ladder is standing tall. Scottywood and Blake are battle it out on top of the ladder while KrazE is trying to get Adrian on the table. KrazE then hits a nasty DDT and is able to roll Adrian onto the table. KrazE climbs the turnbuckle. Scottywood meanwhile has gotten the upper hand on Blake and has his head under his arm pit. KrazE jumps off the turnbuckle with a elbow drop and Scottywood jumps off the ladder with a DDT and all 4 men go crashing through the two tables leaving a mess of twisted wood and bodies. The ref checks up on the wrestlers and sees the arm of Adrian over the chest of Scottywood and he ref starts to count but Scottywood somehow is able to kick out. All four men slowly get to their feet and Adrian grabs a nearby steel chair and drills KrazE in the head busting him open. Blake has Scottywood in a full nelson head lock and Adrian squares up Scottywood. But Scottywood hits Blake with a low-blow and the chair shot knocks out Blake. Scottywood slides out of the ring and grabs himself his hockey stick. Adrian slides over to KrazE and goes for a pinfall on his bloody body but KrazE somehow kicks out at 2! Scottywood enters the ring and delivers a nasty shot with that hockey stick to the back of Adrian and KrazE connects with a neck breaker before he falls. KrazE hooks the leg and gets the pinfall. Winner: KrazE and Scottywood Scott King vs. Strajk We open to Scott King and Strajk fighting in the ring the fans oo and aw at the solid shots King is on the receiving end of from Strajk. King gets knocked to the mat as Strajk starts to pick him up King snaps up with a back body drop that has thunderous effects as they echo threw the arena. We cut to Strajk perched up on the top rope in a daze as the referee starts the 10 count while King sets up Strajk for a move that is sure to have a devastating end, King and Strajk's bodies fly threw the air ending in a super Powerbomb by King. We cut to 3/4 to the end of the match where Strajk is still getting his life beaten out of him... King Irish whips Strajk into the ropes, King bends down to body drop his opponent, as Strajk telegraphs the move which makes King meet face to foot with Strajk’s boot. Both men lye motionless as the ref is at his 7 count. King slowly tries to fight to his feet as his opponent readily awaits his arrival... The fans seem confused and don't know how to react as Adrian Williams comes walking out from the backroom, Strajk's attention quickly gets diverted to the pending threat of Adrian Williams opening up the chance that Scott King needs to finish the match, King Spins around Strajk and hits the Thunder From DownUnder Jackhammer on Strajk and makes the cover for the 3 count. Adrian Williams is on the outside and as King reaches through the ropes to get the World Title belt from the time keeper, Williams slides in and starts kicking into Strajk. King turns and sees the assault and runs at Williams with the belt. Williams seeing King dives out through the ropes and looks back at King standing over Strajk holding the belt. As Williams backs back up the ramp, King helps up Strajk and offers him a handshake. Strajk Shakes hands with King and King raises Strajk’s Arm up and pats him on the back before leaving the ring.
