Rules

July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NGW rules 1. No racial or ethnical comments in your roleplays but swearing is permited. If you do use racial or ethnical remarks then you can be fired, it is viewed and decided upon the NGW staff on the punishment. 2. If a champion is suspended or goes on vacation for more than a period of 4 weeks, then the title will be stripped. This is to keep things going with active wrestlers rather than unactive wrestlers. 3. USE THE RP BOARDS FOR RPs ONLY! If it is not an rp use the general conversation board. 4. Matches will be written as an equal fight no matter what "powers" your guy may have unless oked by the staff or who your facing. 5. The NGW staff reserves the right to edit, censor or use any part or entire RPs posted on the NGW forums without user's permission. 6. No spamming NGW members, No Advertising, No Chain letter. No e-mailing the fed to ask a question but you can post one on the forum board, you may e-mail staff members if is about the NGW in general. 7. No harassing members of the fed or staff. We have 0 tolerance for this. 8. 2 wrestlers max per handler unless other wise noted 9. All required parts of the application must be filled out to be considered. 10. You MUST supply a sample rp in your NGW application, unless pardoned by a member of the Board. 11. You must rp once in your first match or you can be fired immediately. 12. If you are rejected from the NGW you must wait 1 day from piror application to fill out a new application 13. To fire a OOC staffer the other staffers need 75% of the staff to vote for it. 14. Scottywood has last word on all staff vs staff fights unless he is involved 15. No using anyones writing without credit given that also means qoutes. 16. If you disappear without a trace and without notice, you will not be welcomed back. There is no way an individual comes into a situation where they can't get on a computer for a minute to tell NGW staff whats going on. (Extreme situations may be excused if proper proof is brought forth.) 17. No reposting a closed topic on the forums, if it was closed it was for a reason. 18. No sandbaging if you do that you can be jobbed. 19. Make your own storylines and angles, the staff runs the fed not who you handle. 20. No passing titles or giving them away unless oked by the staff. 21. Respect your fellow fed members. 22. No linking other feds in any part of NGW unless otherwise oked by the staff of NGW. 23. The staff have no titles, look at their "jobs" on the staff page to chose which one may help you best then contact them and wait for a response. 24. No porn in Rp's, Sig's, or anything on the NGW site. 25. No show once you get warned in pm's, twice in a row you get suspended for a week, three times in a row your fired unless otherwise said.
