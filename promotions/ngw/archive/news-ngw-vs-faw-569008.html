NGW vs. FAW - Riley As heard on FAW/NGW radio today at 3pm EST, NGW will face off aaginst FAW in a 8 man tag match, with 4 repersentative from each fed fighting. Scottywood and Jamie (FAW Owner) traded comments on todays show and both announced their 4 wrestlers for the match. NGW's 4 men will be Dave Eclipse, The British Bomber, Spawn and Cyrus Skar. Eclipse and Bomber both have been comfirmed for the match as Spawn and Skar are both tentative. As for FAW, Jamie announced that his four will be Omega Fro, 9th Wonder of Foam, Rzarector, Jim Phoniex. The Date of the event will be released in the next few days. But till then the questions remain. Is NGW better then FAW? and will "trash of FAW" as Jamie refered to them, be able to topple their former owner.
