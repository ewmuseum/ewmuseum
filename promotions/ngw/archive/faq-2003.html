F.A.Q.

December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NGW F.A.Q -What does NGW stand for? Next Generation Wrestling. -What is an Efed? An E-fed is a elecrtronic federation in which you make a charater an role play as it. -What is a role play? A role play is basically a part of a story of who you handle. -What does handle mean? Handle is the term used for the charater you control. -What is an Ifed(interfed)? An ifed is a group of feds that form a community. -Whats a gimmick? How your wrestler acts. -How does e-fedding work? You roleplay for your match and then wait for results. -Why can't I use a real (WWE,NWA, etc..) wrestler? Because they suck and are not original. -How do I get fueds started? You start them, find a partner to work them and set it up. -Where do I submit storylines? To the "submit a storyline" link on the main page. -What's a trash talk? Sorta like making fun of people. -Why is Extreme a trash talk/recap show? Because it works better that way. -Why doesn't the staff have job titles? Because we are all equal, when something needs to be done one of the staff does it if it is in their "job" list. -Why did I lose? Because you were out role played. -Why did I lose when I role played more? maybe the staff felt it wasn't your time to win. -Can I join as a tag team only? Yes but please say you only want to do tag matches in your application. -Can I use the HTML, design, or banners on the NGW site? You must first ask then get an answer. -Whats "backstage"? "Backstage" also known as the backstage script, is where you log in and edit your roster page as you see fit. -Why can't I get a poser right away? Because we don't make them for brand new people you need to be around for awhile. -If I want to quit what do I do? E-mail one of the staff. -What don't I do if I don't want to be banned from the forums when I leave NGW? DO NOT post a good-bye or anything like that on the forums unless you first got the OK to do so. -Why can't my guy act just like The Rock from WWE but have a different name and look? Because we are all orignal, nothing can be reused. -Who made NGW? Scottywood and Ice. -What's an ARC? An ARC is a time peroid where you and someone you are paired up with or you choose run a angle, you can extend the arc time if you please just tell the staff. More to be added as we get asked stuff.
