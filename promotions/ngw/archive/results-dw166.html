Death Wish 2003 The night started off with a bang. Prometheus arrived at the arena with N.A.S. Ready to do business, and capture the World Heavyweight title. Scottywood had different plans however. He jumped Promo in the parking lot with a pipe, taking Prometheus down to the ground. Bullet and Guile were to have none of it. Even with a pipe, the Sovereigns and Prometheus were just too much for Scottywood, as they left him for dead, out cold in the parking lot. World Tag Team Title Match Dante Locks/Chris Johnson vs The Krazzy Alliance In the opening match of the night, the Krazzy Alliance looked to have things under control. Locks and Johnson were on their heels until they found a huge momentum shift, in the form of Krazzy Kidd refusing to accept Bone Collecta's tag. Johnson rolled up Bone for the pin, and new NGW tag team champions were crowned. Winners: Dante Locks and Chris Johnson After the match, Bone Collecta got into Krazzy's face, demanding an explanation. All Krazzy had to do, was point to the ramp, where NGW's newest aquisition, Iceman, appeared. He made his way down to the ring, and Bone Collecta was beat to the ground. The NEW Krazzy Alliance was formed. Who is Iceman? And what sort of relationship and history does he have with Krazzy Kidd? #1 Contendership for Xtreme Title Battle Royal Soul Grinder vs Sid Jackson vs Copperhead Red vs Pax vs The Pinnicle In the second match of the night, the #1 contendership for the Xtreme title was up for grabs in an five man over the top battle royal. The match was literally over before it began, as Pax decided that his best friend was made of steel, and had four legs. Slipping a steel chair into the ring, Pax made short work of his opponents. Throwing all four of his opponents over the top rope, making him the downright #1 contender. Winner: Pax Backstage, KrazE is in his Commissioner's office. With FUK beside him, he tells them that their services are no longer needed, thus cancelling out the Ladder Match for the National Title. Much to his dismay, KrazE's power, came to an end. Corey Gibson busted into KrazE's office, and claimed that HE had newly been appointed Commissioner, and that in fact the Ladder Match WOULD take place. KrazE left the office, angry...as Corey Gibson sinisterly smiled. Xtreme Title Match Reggie Rivid(c) vs Damien Ryan In a match that never saw the interior lights of the arena, this Xtreme title match was a parking lot brawl. Rivid and Ryan battled back and forth in a match of the two toughest, most rugged men in NGW. The match came to a bloody end, as the men scaled ontop of one of the NGW engineering semi-trucks. Reggie Rivid was balancing on the edge of the trailor, Ryan charged at him, Rivid sidestepped him, and threw him from the high level...crashing him down ontop of Calvin Riley's limo. Rivid leaps down with a body splash. Pinning Damien Ryan, and retaining his title. Winner: Reggie Rivid Meanwhile, KrazE appears again, with Bone Collecta, discussing Corey Gibson's insane power trip and the Krazzy Alliance. Both KrazE and Bone Collecta entered Hitchin's office and the door closed behind them. National Title Ladder Match KrazE(c) vs FUK Even though he had just went to Hitchin, Hitchin's hands were tied regarding the ladder match. FUK, who had taken out KrazE earlier in the month, were ready to take on KrazE. But KrazE had some plans of his own. When things were looking as if they weren't going to work out for KrazE, Pax made his way down to the ring, and made his presence felt. As both members of FUK were distracted by the presence of Pax, Kraze seized opportunity, and laid out his opponents with the ladder, raising up, to retain his National Title. Winner: KrazE Hitchin emerges from his office, and literally takes the Commissioner office door off of it's hinges. Demanding to know who gave him power, Gibson just laughed, and told Hitchin that Donny Diamond is the one number one contender for the World Title which will be defended at the first Storm of 2004. Hitchin would have none of it, not only did he say that Donny Diamond was hereby fired for not showing up at the PPV, but that Corey Gibson, as fast as he had gained power, had just lost it...and that his "services were no longer needed" and to "clear out his desk." Gibson was livid...Hitchin had struck. American Title Tournament Finals, Triple Threat Match KrazE vs Denouncer vs Bone Collecta In a match that was originally scheduled for a double jeopardy stipulation, Hitchin turned this one into a triple threat match, due to Donny Diamond's no-show/firing. Denouncer went on the offensive right away, taking advantage of Collecta's inexperience, and KrazE's fatigue from the match before. KrazE seemed to have no chance at all. Bone, with his mind on other things, was never able to mount an offense. So in a match that was pretty much solely between Denouncer and KrazE, a legendary match ensued...or so we thought. Hitchin's hatred for Denouncer continued, as Hitchin laid out the referee, right as Denouncer had the match won. Denouncer went at Hitchin, but ending up finding nothing but KrazE. KrazE took advantaged of an enraged Denouncer, finally hitting him with the Final Drop. Hitchin recovers the referee, and the count was made. KrazE did it again. Winner: KrazE KrazE had just become a double champion...speaking of champion, Reggie Rivid was on his way out of the arena. He was done for the night. In the parking lot, his truck was destroyed, stripped. Rivid was angry...ANGRY! Storming around the parking lot, Rivid was surprised as a man came behind him with a sledgehammer...Van Heist! RETURNED! He stood over Rivid, and told him that he was comin' for that title.
