First NGW Roleplay

24 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I’m off to see the wizard, the wonderful wizard of Oz “My journey since I last seen you has been a long and arduous one. I have been travelling for the past two months non-stop, only to lie down and rest my head for a while. I have met countless people since I left also, all different in their own unique ways. Saying that though, they are pretty much the same if you know what I mean. One of the most unique people I have ever met though is the man who called himself the wizard of Oz. He thought he could do everything, like make things disappear and maybe cut me in half with a saw…I never let him try that one. Anyways, one day I woke to hear a phone ringing near by…” [FLASH] [The Real Deal could be seen lying on a small patch of finely cut, vibrant green grass. He looked half asleep and different from the last time we saw him. His hair was shorter than it used to be, not very short but about half the length it was. Its colour was also different as the bright blue that it used to be looke to come at the tips of it. The rest was jet black and it sat back on his head behind his ears, parted in the middle] [He sat up and looked around for a second. He checked the pockets of his slightly baggie jeans and then the pockets on his jacket. He showed a sigh of relief before using his arms to push him up off the ground] [Looks wise, Deal hadn’t changed a bit. His face was still well chiselled and he still had that evil look about him. The only real noticeable different about him was the goatee that he had grown. Three lines of hair came from his chin from just below his mouth to underneath his jaw. One from the centre, one just to the left and one just to the right] [Deal then stretched his arms above his head and tilted backwards to stretch his back also. After doing so he went back into his pocket and produced a packet of cigarettes. After pulling a Marlboro from its packet and lit up. He picked up his backpack and began to walk along the dusty path, which was right beside him. Nothing could be seen in the distance, no towns or even any buildings. It looked like a path to no-where] Random Encounter [An hour into his walk, Deal rubbed his brow and looked along the road which now had been totally covered with the blazing sunlight that would be expected in deserts. For all Deal knew he could have been in one. He had been travelling without a map for some while and was just relying on others’ directions to help him on his quest. His prayers were answered when he saw someone walking just a little bit up the road from him, heading the opposite way. It was hard to miss the other person who was wearing a large blue cloak. Deal then shouted out] The Real Deal: HEY!!! [The person looked up towards Deal but didn’t really acknowledge him. Maybe because he was too far away and that they’d be passing him at some point soon anyway. Deal assumed this also but when the person finally did come to pass it was a very different situation] [It was hard to see but the person in the cloak had long fair hair, which curled down to past their shoulders and out through the hood on their cloak. Deal stood in front of them hoping they would stop and give him some ideas as to where he was going. This was not the case though as the person attempted to plough right through him. They were totally unsuccessful and fell backwards to the ground] [After falling to the ground the person’s hood got pulled backwards and they were revealed as a young woman. Deal walked towards her and put his hand out for her to pull herself up. She decided to take the offer and grabbed Deal’s hand but halfway up she didn’t expect what would happen] [Real Deal let go of her hand and she went plummeting back to the ground like a pile of bricks. Deal laughed and then gave an evil grin which looked much more cocky than usual. The woman who had been dropped to the ground looked furious as she went completely red. She looked to be in her mid twenties and seemed to look after herself quite well. Deal walked towards her again and put his hand out before laughing and retracting it as quick as he had the last time. The woman was not impressed and her tongue lashed out at him like a whip from hell] Woman: WHO IN THE HELL DO YOU THINK YOU ARE?! [Deal rubbed his chin in a sarcastic manner and pretended to think to himself] The Real Deal: Hmmm…let me think. I don’t really think I’m anybody. I just know who I am. [At this the woman stood up as she was getting more and more pissed of at Deal every second that he was being arrogant and sarcastic to her. She walked straight up to him and slapped him in the face] Woman: That’s what you get you pig! [This time Deal looked in utter shock at being called a pig. She didn’t know it but this was another sarcastic expression on his face, as at this point in time he couldn’t really give a damn. The woman tried to get past him once again but he kept on blocking the passage. The woman looked up at him again before swinging round and trying to slap him again. This time though, Deal was ready and caught her arm. He held it there for a while and gave her a extremely evil look before getting right in her face. He took the last draw of his cigarette that he had lit up and blew and the smoke at her] The Real Deal: Now tell me…who are you and why did you try and push past me? You must be ultimately brace or ultimately stupid. [The woman backed down, pushed her hair back with her hand and replied] Woman: I’m Erica. I work for the Trails of Oz. [Deal looked at her in a way that she knew that he didn’t have a clue what that was] Erica: You’re not from around here are you? [Deal grinned then replied] The Real Deal: Further away than you would even think. What’s the Trails of Oz anyway? [He then pointed towards her cloak] The Real Deal: Is that your uniform? [She looked down at her cloak and smiled] Erica: Oh no…this is just to help me avoid talking to strangers. [She then went straight onto talking about the Trails] Erica: The Trails of Oz are a number of different challenges that put the body through many types of sensations including pleasure, passion, suffering, sadness and pain. This is only a small number of the things encountered while in there. Only people with a strong heart and mind can survive the Trails and face off against Oz at the end. [Deal looked rather interested in these Trails she spoke of] [FLASH] “I don’t know what it was but somehow, even though I had only talked to this girl for about five minutes, I trusted her and found out that maybe this Trails thing could do wonders for me. Having to face of against someone at the end didn’t bother me at all. It probably made me want to rise to the challenge anymore. I hadn’t had a fight in a very long time and I needed to keep myself in top condition. The woman, Erica wanted to know things about me also” [FLASH] [Erica was now the one who was curious] Erica: How come you stopped me from going past you? [Deal held his right hand up then raised his index finger] The Real Deal: First of all to find out where the hell I am. [Deal then raised his middle finger] The Real Deal: Secondly because you didn’t answer me. [Deal then lifted his fourth finger] The Real Deal: Thirdly because you tried to push past me. [He gave a sly grin before talking again] The Real Deal: Any of those reasons help you understand? [Erica looked down s if she was embarrassed but then looked up to answer his questions. She held up her right hand and raised her index finger] Erica: Firstly we are in the centre of the dirt road between the two towns of Balon and Nakard. Both are too small to be shown on any map because they only have a small number of buildings and not many people live in them anymore. [She then raised her middle finger] Erica: I didn’t talk to you because you’re a stranger as I said before and talking to strangers is bad. [She then held up her fourth finger] Erica: And I tried to push past you because…umm…well…I don’t know really. It just seemed right at the time. [Deal grinned then turned so he faced out into the wilderness. His left side pointing towards Balon and his right side towards Nakard] The Real Deal: I came from Balon. When I was there it was empty, the buildings had been totally abandoned and some were even half destroyed. [Erica looked at him in shock. He did not see her as she was standing behind him but he heard her with ease] Erica: You are joking right? [Deal turned around slowly and tilted his head to the left a little. He looked at her confused before asking her the obvious question] The Real Deal: Why would I be joking? [Erica’s hands fell into her hands and she started to cry. Deal looked towards her but didn’t feel any of her pain. Here he was, standing with someone he didn’t even know, and he had just said something, which had made her break down into tears. He just stood and waited for her to stop crying but that took a very long time] [FLASH] “I really didn’t know what to say to her. It must have been what I said that made her break down but I don’t know how. How could she not have known that the town had been deserted? I wasn’t going to tell her the other things I saw cause then she really wouldn’t have believed me” [FLASH] What I saw “I arrived at Balon in the early hours of the day, must have been around eight in the morning. I had just gotten up from Rallies Peak in the mountains and saw that it was the closest place nearby, so I decided to check it out and get directions to continue on my journey. When I finally reached it after about an hours walk I was freaked out at what I saw” [FLASH] [The Real Deal stood in front of a large sign which read ‘Welcome to Balon, Please enjoy your stay’] [Deal walked in under the sign and looked to either side of the very small settlement. On his right were two wooden shacks, quite large in size with two floors. They looked well built and were in perfect condition. At the front of them were large wooden decks with numerous chairs laid out on them. There were wooden steps also at the each side of the buildings, obviously to access the upstairs areas] [On his left was another building. This was very small compared to the others and had a sign at the front door. This building wasn’t as well kept together as the others. The side panels of it had started to fall off. It even looked as if someone had tried to rip it apart. Each of the four windows were completely smashed through and the curtains swung free into the open air. Deal moved closer to take a look at the sign. It read ‘Those who choose to stand in this town are worthy of nothing but destruction on their souls though those who chose to stand in this stand and survive are worthy of being King of all the land’] [Deal really didn’t know what to make of this. He put his left hand down to the handle on the door and turned it open. The sun lit up the inside room as if there was no walls. Deal soon discovered that the roof had been totally ripped off the top of the building. He moved forwards in the small hallway, which had two doors, one on either side of it. He decided to take the door on the left because it was slightly open. The room he entered through this door was not a pretty sight. Everything inside was either smashed, turned over, broken or completely burnt to a crisp. There was also a very strong scent of something rotting coming from the corner. Wanting to know more, Deal moved towards were the smell was coming from. There was a large piece of cloth laying over whatever it was…Deal removed it] [FLASH] “I swear I nearly threw up. I had never seen anything like this in all my life. It was a dead cow just lying there being devoured by maggots and all types of insects and other larvae. I covered it back up and got out of there. I can still smell that scent now thinking back on it. It was completely vile. I had to move on into the other room then but it was worse” [FLASH] [Deal walked slowly backwards and out of the room. He turned directly around and moved towards the other door, which was closed. He reached down and turned the handle. The door wouldn’t move so he decided to kick it] [The door went through first time when The Real Deal kicked it. It flew right off its hinges and landed on the floor. Once in, Deal was horrified at what he saw. He didn’t really know what to think of what was going on. In front of him was a person, roughly the same height as him but much slimmer and much more charred to the bone. The fired carcass sent shivers down his spine like he had never experienced. To make it worse he looked up to find that they had been hung before hand] [Deal turned and darted for the door and made his way out front. There, he sat down and took a breather before he saw something dart past him. He turned his head to the left and noticed that a person had stopped just to the left of him. Deal stood up and walked towards them] [FLASH] “I don’t know if I was just seeing things but this guy was moving faster than anything I had ever seen. When he turned round and looked at me, all I could do was stand up and look straight at him. I tried to speak but I just couldn’t and when I did he just vanished into thin air…I really can’t explain it” [FLASH] Back to Erica [Thinking back on the town really got Deal thinking, especially what it had said on the door. He had stood in that town and he had survived it…but he had been thinking of travelling back there with Erica though it did not seem a good idea at this point in time] [Erica had stopped crying now and was looking through her things before looking up at Deal. She spoke softly and sad] Erica: My family…my son… [Deal responded in the only way he could] The Real Deal: I will come with you…as long as you take me to the Trails of Oz after we visit. [Erica looked up at Deal and nodded] Erica: Let’s go! [The two turned towards Balon and started to walk] [FLASH] “This is what seemed to be a little detour on my journey through uncharted lands but it soon turned out to be something completely out of the ordinary” [The sun began to beat down on the old track on which they travelled. Erica had now taken her cloak off and was seen to be wearing a long pink dress with flat pink shoes. Immediately after Deal saw this he wanted to comment so badly but he was afraid that he might of annoyed her more than she was. He decided to go for the better option] The Real Deal: Nice dress. [He flashes a smile over to Erica but her face still stays at a frown. Deal had no idea what she could be thinking about but he knew it had to be. It was pretty obvious by this point. He spoke out once again, this time inquiring about her sadness] The Real Deal: How come you’re so sad? [Erica looked over to him with bulging eyes and fell to the ground crying. She started to explain to him what she was thinking] Erica: My family…they…they live in Balon…and you say that it’s deserted. They haven’t left that town in the time they’ve lived. Why would they just leave now? [Deal looked at her confused] The Real Deal: Apologies but I cannot answer that question. I could only tell you what I saw. [She looked up towards him once more and pleaded with him to tell her what had happened] Erica: You’ve got to tell me what you saw. The Real Deal: I said I could but I’m not going to. You need to see this for yourself. [They both fell silent as Erica nodded and stood back up to her feet. Deal put his hand on her shoulder as they turned to head towards Balon once again] Raiders of Balon [Erica and Deal made it to Balon after another thirty minute walk. Not a mutter had been heard across the baron plains as they made their way to the small ghost town. As they made it to the gates Deal stopped as Erica ran on. She turned into the first house on the left. This was the building that was the least destroyed. Deal waited a moment before rushing towards Erica as she pounced out the door scream to Deal] Erica: You told me this place was deserted! [Deal looked in shock as she said this] The Real Deal: It was when I got here. Erica: Well it’s not anymore. Run! [The two began to run quickly out of the town along the path, which lead back to Nakard. Behind them chased four white horses each with a knight jockeying on top of each of them. Each of the four knights carried a large lance. Deal looked round and his pace severely quickened at the sight of these monstrosities] The Real Deal: Who the hell are they? Erica: The raiders. They hunt for nothing but wealth and greed. The Real Deal: Well I don’t plan on sharing any of my wealth with them. [Deal grabbed Erica by the arm and threw her up on his back and continued running. The horses gained quickly on them though and were nearly in range for the knight’s lances. Two of the raiders moved to the outside and made a pass on Deal then cut him off in front. He stopped and dropped to the ground. Now both Deal and Erica were surrounded. Real Deal thought to himself] “This is not good” [As soon as he thought this he watched as the four lances came towards him and Erica…] [AWAKENING] [Deal shot up from the large double bed in his hotel room covered in a cold sweat. He slowly made his way to his feet after rolling off the bed and hitting the floor. After making his way to his feet he walked towards the light coming from the blind covered window and unleashed the light onto the room] The Real Deal: Ah Milan… [Deal soaked in the fresh air and tilted his head back to feel the full effect of the sun that bore down upon him. He stretched his neck for a couple of seconds which helped him get a glimpse of a women sitting by the fountain outside making a painting] “I don’t think I had ever gotten ready that fast. The dream that night was crazy and I just had to get out of there. I raced down the stairs and flung myself out the front door. It was pure instinct but I just walked up to her and…” The Real Deal: I’ve never seen you here before, you from Milan? [The young woman looked up from her painting and smiled] Woman: Hey, and to answer you question…HEY!!! [Before she got the chance to answer a man ran fully past her painting and lifted it on the way past. Deal looked at her] The Real Deal: Don’t worry…I’ll sort it out. [With that Deal turned and sprinted after the man who looked rather tubby and was starting to become red faced already. Deal was in peak physical condition and started gaining on the man. At the bridge, Deal finally caught up with the man and grabbed him by the back of the neck] The Real Deal: Where do you think you’re going? [The man turned and pulled out a revolver. He pointed it straight at Deal but said nothing. Deal backed off a little but not for long as he dart forward and hit the man with a hard swinging backhand. Deal looked on in horror though as the man toppled over the bridge and fell into the deep waters. Within minutes the Italian police were on the scene. They talked to Deal for a moment before cuffing him and placing him in the back of a patrol vehicle. The woman who The Real Deal had originally planned to talk to watched on as he was taken away. He knew somehow that this would not be the last time they would meet] [Fade to black]
