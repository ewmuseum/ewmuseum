The Promice Land

March 23rd
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Promice Land Lorenzo Guevera Next Generation Wrestling Promos #50 Date: March 23rd Location: Hilton Hotel The Promise Land “An' here I go again on my own Goin' down the only road I've ever known, Like a drifter I was born to walk alone An' I've made up my mind I ain't wasting no more time” Are the words we hear singing from the mouth of Lorenzo Guevera as he exits the bathroom in the luxurious suite in the Hilton hotel, Boston Mass. From what we can see it looks like Lorenzo is in a happy mood, he has a big wide smile on his face, he has a skip in his walk and he is singing. “Tho' I keep searching for an answer, I never seem to find what I'm looking for Oh Lord, I pray you give me strength to carry on, 'Cos I know what it means to walk along the lonely street of dreams” Follows the last verse, as he walks over and past the Jacuzzi where we last spotted him with Mr Vlad-Scott Woodson, his views last time where mainly on the six man tag match that happens in four days time at a special addition of storm. This week’s storm only has a selected number of matches because the pay per view is only a week away and the office and promotional team at Next Generation Wrestling don’t want the talent to be over used. As we follow Lorenzo we find out he is heading to the roof. “I still cannot believe your signing that song” Vlad says looking over the rooftop onto the Boston streets below. “Well it’s your fault, you’re the one who signed us up to Karaoke” I replied to Vlad. I mean I do like to have fun when I am in training, to keep into the normal life of a everyday person, because when you’re in training that’s all it is. Eat, sleep and drink training, watching everything you do. So I like to do some stuff to keep me in the real world, its not that I am a bad singer, its just Karaoke! Who in the right mind signs up for that kind of thing when you are not drunk? “I never knew you had to sing” Vlad replied. “What? How the hell do you not sing at Karaoke?” I replied to him, it’s not like it’s a hard thing to forget when you’re drunk. The people around you never let you down for it. Sometimes I really think that Vlad’s not all there, anyhow I don’t ditch him for that as I know he really cannot sing, so it will be funny as hell tonight. “What song are you going to sing Vlad?” I asked him next. “I haven’t quite decided, but whatever I choose it will blow you all away” he replied with a firm look on his face. “you may underestimate me Lorenzo, but you have never heard me sing have you, I mean really sing, back in Belarus I used to be in a band, I sang and we was great, I will show you, I will prove you wrong” “Calm down, its not like you’re going to have a fight with me.” He was so hyped up, maybe I have underestimated him, looked over him as no competition. “Fine ill go practice some more” I responded. “In fact I am going to the gym; I’ve got a few moves I want to work over, will you be joining me?” I said to him. “No, I’ve got to plan a few things, I may be down in twenty minutes though” he responded. I left the room to where Vlad just walked over to the bed and stretched out, he picked up the phone as I closed the door, I walked a few paces towards the lift then pressed the bell to call it up to the floor I was on. “Ding” it rang as it arrived. As it opened the silent riffs of a song played from inside the chariot of a lift, I entered it and pressed the button to the ground floor. As it dropped floors I could see that there were some paparazzi in the lobby. It looks like they are trying to get a interview or some comments with some superstars staying at the hotel before heading to the PPV up in Los Angeles. As I the door opened the flashes began, it was like a wave of strobe effects, I thought it was like me moments before my music hits and I step out of the curtain to entertain. It was a great feeling to know that after my performance on storm and my debut the week before that people where starting to recognize me. That’s great I thought to myself as I walked out and had to force myself through the crowd. “Can I get a few words Mr Guevera” I heard from a female voice, I turned around to see a young American girl with a girl next door look to her. “Yes you can miss” I said to her. She got her camera crew together and I took her into the gym area for a interview on some subjects I know nothing of, but I feel they will be about my wrestling career. As we went into the gym there was already a Next Generation Wrestling ring set up for the workers to practice on. “Can we do the interview in the ring?” she asked me “Sure, we can” I replied as I grabbed a few chairs and slid them into the ring, the camera crew set up and we was ready to go. “This is Karel Pomperelli reporting for central news, with Next Generation Wrestling Star, and current roster Member Lorenzo Guevera.” The camera turns around to see Karel sat opposite Lorenzo “So First off, what made you decide to take up wrestling Lorenzo?” Says Karel “Well I thought that this question was going to come up first, so ill tell you the story. A long time ago in Pamplona where I was raised for the record, I was sitting at home deciding what to do next with my life, then I started to read newspaper on the table where it showed a article of Sky skiing” is said to her. “Sky Skiing?” She responded “Yes, you jump out of a plane with a snowboard and fall to the grown; obviously you have a parachute with you. Anyhow, after I saw that article I was soon on my way to America to try it out for myself. It was on the third day over there that Vlad and I went to a pro wrestling show, and that show changed my life, it got me started in a sport I wish never ends” I replied. “I see, and how did you come to Next Generation Wrestling?” She asked me “Well it started when I asked the company who I was watching, I asked them if I could tryout and work for them, apparently I was a natural to the sport and they took me on. A few months later I received a letter from Scott Woodson inviting me south for a trail, I passed that then last week I made my debut” “So this is your debut year?” she said “Indeed, I have been wrestling for less than six months, and I love it” I replied. “Travelling on the road every month is hard, how do you cope with it?” “Well its my job, at the end of the day I have to travel to fight, so it doesn’t bother me that much, the only thing that can get on my nerves about travelling is Vlad, being with him so much gets annoying.” I replied. “Where is Vlad now?” Karel says looking into the eyes of Lorenzo “He is upstairs planning something for tonight” I said looking into hers “What happens tonight?” she was intrigued “Well he entered us into a Karaoke competition” I waited for a laugh “Hahaha” she burst out laughing, I mean who wouldn’t, and my chances of scoring with a girl away from home go down the drain once again because of Vlad. “Hmm I know” I said to her “Well I think that’s all we need from you Lorenzo” she said. “Thank you” I said to her She then sat up and left the ring and exited through a door, I grabbed the chairs and rolled out the ring. I heard the camera’s click with flashes and the door open once again, this time in stepped Vlad, he was wearing sunglasses and a cowboy hat on. “What the hell? You look like a Russian Johnny cash” I said to him as he closed the door behind them. “Its ok, I am in costume, those people outside never knew who I was, it’s a great disguise I am telling you. Plus I found this hat at the bar; no-one had it on so I borrowed it” he replied to me. “You took a hat from the bar, wait! what the hell are you doing in the bar, you know that’s off limits for us” I replied in a angry voice to him, see when we train or prepare for matches we stay of the evil alcohol, as that makes us slow in the ring and we may hit each other when we are drunk. Or the worse could happen and we could injure ourselves in the process. “So what new moves have you been practicing for the past twenty minutes?” he said to me. “well none, first I just has a interview with this woman, and now you have arrived, I haven’t been in the ring yet, so it looks like you was just in time, ill put some music on” I replied to him as I walked over to the PA system to switch on some music to train to. “We are so young our lives have just begun but already we're considering escape from this world” “Gash, I hate this song” I said as I turned off the song and put in a new compact Disk, I am not a fan of slow music and His Infernal Majesty’s version of Join me just isn’t my type of music. I walked over to the stack of disks in the corner, finding one I liked was a hard choice but finally found one. I placed it in the disk player; put it on track four ran into ring for the intro just starting up. “Where do I take this pain of mine I run but it stays right by my side So tear me open and pour me out There's things inside that scream and shout And the pain still hates me So hold me until it sleeps” This is more my style of music, nice hard riffs by Kirk Hammett on the guitar and the pounding drums of Lars Ulrich, yes I prefer the sounds of Metallica. As I slide into the center of the ring I bring my knees up and run against the ropes a few times before slowing down and climbing to the turn buckles. I waited up there as Vlad stepped nervously into the ring. “So we just have a quick match for a warm up then go do some karaoke ok?” he said to me. “Sure” I replied, not taking in what he said and looking for a fight with him. We locked up in a collar and elbow tie up in the center of the ring, as I felt him trying to get me in a wrist lock I stopped him and shoved him back. He fell back on his backside as I shook my head at him. I could see in his eye that he was getting frustrated; he got back up and tried once more this time as we locked up he put his knee into my gut sending me down to the floor. I lay there on my front as Vlad dropped down and picked my arm and placed it into a crossed reverse Armbar, I rolled through and tried to reverse it, it worked, he then grabbed the ropes as I released the hold. “Get him Vlad” was shouted as I turned around to see that about twenty reporters had heard the news and come looking in the room, there camera’s where all set up and they was getting a pay per view special of a exhibition match between Vlad and Lorenzo Guevera. Vlad turned around with a smile on his face as he walked up to me and shoved me. I looked down before looking back at him and slapping him right across the face. Next came a fury of left and rights by myself, I then grabbed his left arm and whipped him into the ropes with all my weight behind him, as he ran he hit the ropes and bounced back, unfortunately for him all he received as a dropkick in the face. The crowd clapped as I hit my dropkick, as when I do a dropkick I flip backwards and land on my stomach like a Moonsault. Vlad got up only to receive another dropkick, I ran against the ropes and did a cart-wheel whilst coming back before hitting a standing Moonsault onto the body of Vlad. However he knew it was coming and held his knees up. I cracked my ribs against the knees and rolled in agony. As I rolled over he quickly grabbed my legs and locked in a single leg crab. As I was crawling to the ropes he then dropped the leg and turned it into a STF. I was in pure hell until I managed to make it to the ropes. He broke the hold and I made it to the outside to catch a breather. I looked up to see Vlad run to the ropes getting ready for a rope flip, I quickly reached on the grown to grab a chair, as Vlad dived over a swung the chair like a baseball bat and connected on his head like a person hitting a home run. A thud was heard all around as the camera’s flashed to see the great shot. Vlad just simply dropped and grabbed his head. I then dropped the chair and picked him up by the head, I rolled him into the ring. “You!” I shouted at one of the reporters. “Get in here and do the count” in rolled a man around fourty who made the count. “One” “Two” “Kick out – two only” Vlad got his left shoulder up as I was shocked, I am sure that chair shot would have finished him off. I got up and started to kick him; I ran to the ropes and jumped on him hitting a standing shooting star press. The crowd applauded in my agility as I landed on Vlad. I covered him once again, this time I was going to get the three counts. “One” “Two” “Three” “Lorenzo Wins the match” says the reporter I got up and started to celebrate, I cannot be beaten you see, as they just saw a demonstration I thought it was time to keep in character and get on my mic skills. So I walked over to a reporter leaned over the ropes and grabbed the mic. “You see Antheus! I am in my prime; you may have the advantage of the experience over me but like you found out on storm this week that I can wrestle with your ability as well. You may also be seven foot four and a tall person but like I have said before Antheus your height doesn’t bother me, I will take you down to the grown like I have done in the past. I am twenty one years old and in my prime. I have a un-defeated record and I am going to this pay per vie with two views winning my match with you and James then taking myself on to become the forth person to win the battle dome. “ “James, you are not a challenge for me either, I beat you last week, I stopped your four and zero record and you never liked it one bit, after the match you was pissed I know you was. People called the match a upset, I called the match as the start of a revolution. The Lorenzo Guevera Revolution. You see I cannot be beat and I won’t be beat. On March thirty you and Antheus will see how much I want to win and succeed in this company, that’s why I won’t be able to be stopped. But for now, I must go and train some more, as you are just not a option for me anymore, I have twenty seven more people to think about beating. “Oh and Scottywood, you may be the boss around here, but if your in that ring when I make my return to the ring for the second time of the night then I wont hold back, yes you can fire me and yes you are in a bad state but you stand in the way of my dream and you will be beaten. That goes for everyone who thinks that they can win this match, exactly one year ago me and Vlad sat in the crowd to see the battle dome match, when timebomb won the match. “One year on and I am here, I am competing for the title, I cannot justify how much that this completion means to me, which is why I wont give less than one hundred and eleven percent. Risks will be taken and sacrifices will be made but at the end of the night I will be the one standing there with my arm raised, streamers falling down from the rafters, my name in the record books and on my way for the shot at the world champion. And I won’t stop because I can’t be stopped for I am Lorenzo Guevera!” With that I dropped the mic in a rage of fury and slid under the bottom rope. I walked out of the door and across the Lobby and straight into the elevator where Vlad was already in. he had a pissed look on his face. “What?” I said looking at him out of breath. “Why the hell did you hit me with a chair?” he replied in a angry tone “I was just wrestling Vlad; you should know weather it is an exhibition match or even a charity match or the real thing I don’t give less than my best, least it was a quick match, did that standing press hurt?” I asked him as it kept him down for the three counts. “Yes it did, it’s a nice looking move” he replied. “Ding” went the elevator as we arrived back up at our floor. We both walked forward and into the suite where we was staying, I immediately took off my shoes as my feet was killing, I looked over at the clock as It read eight fourty five. “Karaoke starts in fifteen minutes Vlad, im going to get showered and dressed and ill see you down there ok?” I said to Vlad who wondered into the other room “yea, that’s good with me” he said from the other room. It took me about five minutes to shower and get ready, I was looking in my wardrobe to find what to wear, I decided that to look like a eighties rock icon it should be the tight leathers and white shirt. I looked into the mirror as I did my hair; I heard the door bang as Vlad left for downstairs. This was my time I was ready its time for karaoke. I left the room and looked at my watch, it was eight fifty five, and there was no time to wait for the elevator so I ran down the stairs, I mean I couldn’t be late, I mean I was first up, I got downstairs and ran into the bar where there was a stage set up with a banner reading “Hilton King of Karaoke 2004”, I briefly sat down and started to pant as the woman started the proceedings. “First up we have Mr Lorenzo Guevera” As I stood up to a round of applause I walked onto the stage and grabbed the mic, the lights turned off and a spotlight came on. Then the drummer tapped his stick together. “I WANNA ROCK!” I screamed as the band got into the flow of a cover of twisted sisters I wanna rock. “wanna rock! (rock) i wanna rock! (rock) i want to rock (rock) i wanna rock! (rock)” I started to jump around on the stage really getting into the song ”turn it down you say, well all i got to say to you is time and time again i say, 'no!' no! no, no, no, no, no! tell me not to play well, all i got to say to you when you tell me not to play, i say, 'no!' no! no, no, no, no, no! so, if you ask me why i like the way i play it there's only one thing i can say to you” I started to do pelvic thrusts at everything in sight ”i wanna rock! (rock) i wanna rock! (rock) i want to rock (rock) i wanna rock! (rock)” I did some more jumping around ”there's a feelin' that i get from nothin' else and there ain't nothin' in the world that makes me go! go! go, go, go, go, go! turn the power up i've waited for so long so i could hear my favorite song so, let's go! go! go, go, go, go, go! when it's like this i feel the music shootin' through me there's nothin' else that i would rather do i wanna rock! (rock) i wanna rock! (rock) i want to rock (rock) i wanna rock! (rock) rock (rock) i wanna rock” As the riff finished the crowd cheered aloud. It was great not only did I put on one of the best performances ever; also I never made a fool out of myself. I walked off stage as the next competitor got up, he was about six foot three, looked roughish had a strong Canadian accent and started to sing pour some sugar on me, you know the song, the crap def leopard one. I sat there at my table for the next fourty minutes waiting to watch Vlad, then the time had come, he was last up. He strutted up the stage as the lights dimmed he stopped in the middle of the stage. He pointed up to the god and then walked over to the band and whispered something in the guitarist’s ear. The band’s guitarist walked off stage and then back on with another guitar and handed it to Vlad. Vlad took the guitar and started to play a hard rock riff with it. After a few moments he slid his hand down to the frets and played a low note so the band behind him joined in. the lights started to flash in a strobe effect as Vlad began to sing. “Got a ticket for a ride Never stop until I'm satisfied It's a party on the sun Feel the heat comin' from my gun Flyin' high on Rock n Roll Like a fever burnin' in my soul” Vlad then centred himself in the middle of the stage as some pyro’s at each side of flames burst up as he started to sing in the chorus with the band singing with him. ”Livin' the life I was born to live Givin it all I've got to give Lovin' every minute of what I do Come on, come on, come on, you can live it too I like it hard I like it fast Nobody told me it was meant to last I get my lovin'' when I can Spread the word all over this land” I was shocked at the performance we were seeing from Vlad. But he continued to go on. ”I wanna rock it all night and day So don't 'cha tell me there's another way Livin' the life I was born to live Givin' it all I've got to give You're invited if you wanna come Better get ready to have some fun” Then to the crowds and my own disbelief he broke out into a guitar solo, this was just excellent, he was sliding his hand all over the guitar, he was just like the great Jimmy Page, with lights flashing from the media in the corner he went back to the song to sing. “Livin' the life I was born to live Givin it all I've got to give Livin' the life I was born to live Givin' it all I've got to give Lovin' every minute of what I do Come on, come on, come on, you can live it TOO! I wanna live the life” The song finished and we all knew who had won this time, it was Vlad, this was his moment to enjoy, so I left to allow him to soak it up. I walked out of the room and over to Karel who was in the lobby area. “Still here?” I said to her in a questioning tone “Yea I am, I thought you had karaoke” she responded. “I did, Vlad just won it, its his time to celebrate now” I told her. “And it will be your time on Sunday?” She was trying to get me to talk about my views on the dome match. “Yes, Yes It will, do you want my views on the dome?” “I sure do, ok…we are rolling” she and her crew said. “Can you see me, up here? That’s right it’s me, Lorenzo Guevera, the man who is going to give the best show on march 30th, it will be me who starts the card off in a three way match against James and Antheus, then I will return later on already winning one match to become the newest number one contender for the world title by winning the famous battle dome.” “Yes there are others that the media have been asking about, who do I think will win it and who other predict, I am just worried why no-body has picked Lorenzo, I am well able to pull out a victory from nowhere, just ask James how I pinned him last week with a screwdriver. The pin fall can come from anywhere. Some are backing Chris Johnson, some Dallas reeves and some are even backing Antheus to walk out the winner. I would disagree and say they won’t win.” “Yes this will be my first Next Generation Wrestling and only Pay per View I have ever competed at and only the second match in my full professional career so of course there are some nerves, in fact I am always nervous before a match, but if the fear wasn’t there then the payoff wouldn’t be that much for me. I love the fear and the adrenaline that this match will bring me to is so huge that words cannot describe it.” “Yes this match will take me to the limits and I hope I will be able to pull off a victory, if I win the match then I stand to be the youngest competitor to win the battle dome and I will become more of the Next Generation History. History is taught around the world and if my name appears there then it will be awesome, I strive to win this and you will see by the look on my face come the thirtieth. Because when that music hits, the lights flash and the crowd cheers, Lorenzo Guevera will be on his way to claming his dream.” “And that’s to become the winner of the two thousand and four battle dome champion”
