"How long Doc?"

26 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[the screen opens up in a parking lot surrounded by a set of huge buildings, with the noise of heavy traffic all around. The smell of the air is fresh, unlike the stench of New York, but a clean Canadian smell, of trees and fresh oxygen. Two consecutive car horns go off and the camera catches a white Silverados dirrectionals flash. A door slams shut and NGW's Damien Ryan walks from in between his car and another to head towards the building entrance. He seems to be walking kind of funny, in a bent sideways sort of walk, obviously from his recent back injury. He heads towards the building entrance as the camera takes a view of the massive structure. Above the entrance doors a sign reads: Toronto General Hospital Rehabilitation and Therapy. Ryan walks on in, having trouble opening the door. The screen cuts into the lobby where Ryan walks to the front desk to find a dark woman typing away on a laptop. Her long red fingernails striking the keys causing echo throughout the lobby. Ryan reaches the desk and the woman, obtrusivly chewing gum, looks up at Ryan still typing away.] Woman: Can I help you? [Ryan looks at her in a weird way, knowing that she does not know who he is. His ego causing him to get agitated because he feels EVERYONE should know who Damien Ryan is, he keeps his cool.] Damien Ryan: Yeah, Damien Ryan...[making his name noticable by saying it a little louder]....to see Dr. Richard Colby. Woman [still typing]: Take a seat and I'll call you when he's ready. [Ryan turns around and heads over to the chairs. The place is empty so he has a choice of which chair to sit in, yet they are all the same style. A modern, steel type of chair. Ryan squints his eyes and gives a moan as he walks over to one of the chairs to sit down. He turns his body and bends over slightly to sits down, moaning from the pain as his bottom reaches the seat. He sits back, trying to find a comfortable position for his back in the very uncomfortable chair. He starts getting agrivated from the stupid chair. He starts thinking to himself, I'm here to fix my back and this meaningless chair, this chair!, is making my back, MY back, Damien Ryan's back, even worse.] Woman: Dr. Colby is ready to see you now. [Ryan throws his head up, startled from the woman's very annoying, piercing voice in the silent room. Ryan gets up, pushing the seat down with his hands for extra leverage to take pressure away from his ailing back. He walks past the woman with an agrivated look in his eye, yet the woman's eyes stay locked on her computer screen.] Woman: Take the elevator to the sixth floor, down the hall and to the left. Ryan: Yeah, I know, thanks. [Ryan continues to walk off, shaking his head, he just wanted to knock that god damn chick's head through that computer screen, normally something that would not cross his mind ever, but in the situation of his anger over the past couple weeks, he's in the mood to make anybody suffer. The thoughts of his two straight losses to Reggie Rivid haunt his brain, and the thought of him falling through the roof of the 18-wheeler than Ryan himself brough to the arena, not only angers him but sends shocks to his lower back. Ryan, not used to losing walks with a look on his face as saying, get the hell out of my way, I don't want to deal with you. Knowing that he's not walking down this hospital hallway not being titled the NGW Xtreme Champion gets to him so much, that he throws his arm and hits the 'UP' button the elevator so hard that the woman at the desk down the hallway is startled and stops typing. Ryan doesn't look back. After reaching the sixth floor Ryan, still walking funny opens the door to Dr. Colby's office. He walks in on the doctor on the phone. The doctor smiles as Ryan appears in his doorway, and gives him a hand gesture to come on in and take a seat.] Dr. Colby [on the phone]: Yes, he just walked in right now, we're going to take care of some business right now. We'll see how everything works out then I'll give you a call so you can present a medical document to the judge tomorrow morning. Ok. Bye. [He hangs up the phone, stands up, looks at Ryan and throws out his hand for a handshake.] Dr. Colby: Mr. Ryan, good to see you! [They shake hands, then the doctor take a seat.] Dr. Colby: Please, sit down. [Ryan, notices the seat to be similar to the seats in the lobby. Ryan rolls his eyes and sits down in the chair, sending sharp pains into his back.] Dr. Colby: So, how you feeling? Ryan: Not too good. Doesn't seem to be getting any better. You need to figure something out here, Doc. I can't be taking off two months. Dr. Colby: Atleast two months, Damien. It could take longer. [Ryan dropps his head into the palms of his hands.] Dr. Colby: Why don't you say we head down to the lab and find you something to wear on your back, while your resting. [About a few minutes of traveling on the elevator and reaching a laboratory, we see Rayn sitting on a phsician bed with his arms raised above his head, Ryan squinting his eyes from the pain. The doctor wraps a back brace around the Ryan's back and stomach, and uses the valcro to hold it together.] Dr. Colby: How does that feel? Ryan: It fits, I guess. Dr. Colby: I know this is hard Damien, but it's the only for this to heal, this injury is not a surgical injury so we can't cut you open for this, so you're going to have to deal with this for now. [Ryan nods his head] Dr. Colby: Now, wear this every single night when you go to bed, try to lie on your back, with your eyes looking at the ceiling. Now, if you want this to heal quicker, you can wear this under a shirt. It can be unnoticable if you where a sweatshirt or a really baggy shirt. Ok? Ryan: Yeah. Dr. Colby: No physical activities, comprendo? Ryan: Yes sir. Dr. Colby: Best thing I can tell you to stay in shape is just to eat right for now, stay healthy. When the back heals, then you can do some conditioning and exercising so you can get back to normal. But for now....it's all time. [screen fades out]
