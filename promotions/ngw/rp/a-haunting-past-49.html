A Haunting Past

A Haunting Past Jeff James Next Generation Wrestling Promos #49 Date: 1/25/04 Location: East Lansing, Michigan It was Friday night, January 23rd, 2004. It was a snowy Michigan night, as flakes fluttered to the ground. The snow was supposed to accumulate to 9 inches. All of the snow on the ground and trees, made everything look much prettier than normal out the window view from Outback Steakhouse. Although the snow was quite the pretty sight, it did something else to my mind. There’s something about those cold snowy January days, when you look outside, and it gives you this feeling. It looks pretty, but it depresses me. I have a great life, great family, great career, great girlfriend, and yet this snowy January day still finds a way to depress me. I looked across the table into Ashley’s bright blue eyes, it was almost like time was frozen for a few minutes, I was drifting back into my early teenage years. It was January 23rd, 1998 I was a young 17 year old that woke up in the morning without a worry. I didn’t know shit about the real life, but when I would go to sleep that night I would be a completely changed person. January 23rd, 1998 would be a day that would be tattooed in my head and mind for eternity. When I woke up that morning, I thought this day could possibly be one of the greatest nights of my life. Not only would it be a day that I would remember for being my 17th birthday, but for much more than that. That night was the all famous Turn-About dance for our high school. It was like Home-coming only the girls asks the guys to the dance, vice-versa of Homecoming. My girlfriend at the time Liz asked me of course. She had a lot different friends than I had. She talking me into coming with her in her group, and not going with the group of kids I had grown up with. The group of kids I had grown up with, I had suddenly been separated for this night, and some of them forever. Mark, Nick, Brandon, Ryan, Jen, Meg, Laura, and Kim, kids who I had been to every party ever at, enjoy my best laughs with, we were suddenly 2. It was a decision I would really grow to regret over time, not that I knew anything would happen. But life is so fragile, treat every breath, every second like it’s your last. Liz was a real cute girl, and a great person, but the great things we did that night wouldn’t even be remembered. After the dance, our group all broke off, Liz and I went back to her house, and hung out. That whole night though had a different feel than normal. Really I should’ve been having a great time, a great night, but I really wasn’t.I loved Liz she's a great girl but she's a girl I've known for a year, opposed to people I've known nearly my entire life. Earlier in the day though us guys played a football game out at the school grounds. It was 4th down, Brandon was back in the pocket throwing, I was playing free safety, I could tell by the way he was looking who he was throwing too, he was going to Mark, so I read it early, as Brandon released the ball I was waiting for the kill on Mark. The ball hit his hand, as I crushed him to the cold ground, with slight patches of snow all over. “What the fuck man, that’s pass interference” , Mark yelled “ Whatever, you’re a fucking joke, learn how to play you piece of shit”, I yelled back. You have to understand though we’re best friends, we’ve been best friends since kindergarten, and this talk was more heated than normal though. We took over on downs, it was 9-9, touchdowns counted as 1 point, we always played to 10, and you had 4 downs to go all the way down field, in a way it was like College Football Overtime. My friend Steve was playing Quarterback for us, I lined up as a Running back, he hiked the ball, everyone down field was covered, so he threw a short screen to me, I grabbed the ball and started running down field, Mark came in on defense, when he got within reach of me I put my arm out and stiff arm him, and drove him off for a few yards, I could tell he was pissed, he eventually took all of his strength and dropped me backwards. “You’re playing like a fucking dick wad, what are you trying to do, is stiff arming allowed in fucking back yard football now” he yelled I got up and we got in each others face’s and started pushing “You’ve been a fucking little girl your entire life, you’re nothing, a nobody period, why don’t you go ask the 1st grade girls if they want to play, I’m out bitch”, those were the last words I ever said to him, my best fucking friend in the entire world. Despite our fight earlier, I still believed I belonged with them this night, instead of here in Liz’s room. It was 3 am we were just chilling watching a movie. Then I heard my phone ring, I almost expected it though, I mean Turn-About night, everyone’s up still, but it would be a phone call that would change me forever. It would turn me into a completely different person. “ Yo , whatup” I said No one responded, it was like a few moments of silence, kind of strange “Hello?” I replied again “Jeff, it’s Steve, listen man, I hate to break it to you but Mark, Nick, Laura, and Kim are dead” I didn’t believe him when he first said it, I thought he was playing some joke on me. There’s no way he was serious. Anyways I should’ve been with them this entire night anyways. “ What’s this some kind of late night prank steveo?” I said “ Listen Jeff, they are fucking….. dead…..dead” Steve said. “ I don’t believe you” I said “ You can either take my word, or you can take the Obituary’s word, I hate to be the one to tell you this”, Steve said “What the fuck happened?”, I was in shock, but I still believe him “ They weren’t drinking first off, they left to go teepee some girl’s house in Woodridge. They got hit by a drunk driver, they’re dead, he’s alive, bottom line” There were just seconds of akward silence, I really was going to be a mess in about a minute. 4 of the people who were closest to me in this entire world were taken away from me, from some piece of shit drunken driver. “ Steve, I’m gonna break down, I gotta go” I said “I’m real sorry man I know how close they were to you” he said I hung up the phone, Liz could tell by the look of me how wrong something was. “What’s wrong babe?”, she said To be honest I couldn’t even tell her right then, all I know is the last words I said to my best friend was , “You’ve been a fucking little girl your entire life, you’re nothing, a nobody period, why don’t you go ask the 1st grade girls if they want to play, I’m out bitch”. I couldn’t think straight. I was waiting for Steve to call back, and say something like just kidding, or I got you good this time. No, this was for real, this isn’t some movie, some video game. People don’t come back from the dead, you don’t have 15 lives, you can’t run dead on with another car and still be alive. It made me a completely changed person, my life turned a different direction. My life can basically be divided into 2 lives, 2 chapters, before January 23rd 1998, and after. I remember leaving Liz’s house immediately and just going home and crying for hours and hours. I punched my basement wall as hard as I could, it bled all over, and I still have the scar from it right above my left pink knuckle. Why me? Why now? It was a learning experience for me and a tough one in this game of life. After that day, any party I’d be at if anyone I knew was drunk and would come close to picking up keys, the only way they would do it is by walking by my dead body. I would fight anyone that would even try to drive home drunk, I was this close to swallowing my cousin’s keys on one occasion. You never know where this crazy maze of life will lead you every morning that you wake up…… ' I know it's a tough day for you' Ashley said, as she grabbed my hand 'It's one thing to know about it, and it's another thing to live through it' The same day I would be celebrating my birthday I would be suffering over four of my closest friends death's, ironic huh? Even more so, sometimes I feel like I should've been there and died with them in that very car. I should be 6 feet below decaying, it obviously wasn't my destiny, there was much more instore for me in this life.
