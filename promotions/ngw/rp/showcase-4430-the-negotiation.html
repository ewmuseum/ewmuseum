"The Negotiation"

2 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::Scene:: The scene opens back up in the dark little room, where Robb Hardy and Rob Zone are sitting across from the two FBI agents, Agent Brown and Agent Bryant. Agent Brown as a smirk on his face, he is thinking how much he wants to beat Robb Hardy's ass. Agent Bryant has a serious look on his face, the smirk from Brown and the serious look from Bryant are freaking the hell out of Rob Zone. Rob Zone's legs are shaking frantically, because he has never been in this type of position before, he has no clue in what the hell is going on. Now, were continuing off, from when Robb Hardy said, "What kind of a deal?" Agent Brown: Alright you piece of trash, this is the....... Agent Bryant: Shut up Brown! I am going to do the talking, just because you don't like where Robb Hardy came from, don't get all angry. I came from the same place as him anyway Brown, you wanna get all angry at me? Do you have anger deep inside against me? Agent Brown: Y........ Agent Bryant: And do you want to actually get fired? Agent Brown: Uh, no of course not, Sir. Agent Bryant: I am glad you see it my way Brown. Now, Robb, heres the deal. We actually thank you for taking out Leroy James' men. We were trying to take them out, but you did it officiantly, accept for sending that tape to NGW. Now Robb, your not in any trouble at all. Rob Zone: Really? Agent Bryant: Shut up, I wasn't talking to you. If I say "Robb" I am talking to Hardy, if I say Robert, I am talking to you, understand? Rob Zone: Alright, sir. Robb Hardy: Just keep your mouth shut Zone. Agent Bryant: Now Robb, here's the deal. You have to follow my orders in every single way, I am letting you and Robert(Rob Zone) free. But, their's something you must do for me. Robb Hardy: ::Sighs:: What? Agent Bryant: You have to capture Leroy James, dead or alive. No one can no about this deal, alright Robb? Robb Hardy: My lips are sealed. Agent Bryant: Not even your wife. Robb Hardy: ::Robb's eyes water, but he doesn't cry:: My wife will have to know, because those bastards have her too. Agent Bryant: So they have your wife and 1 year old baby!? Robb Hardy: Yeah, that's why I need to get out of here quickly, I need to pay the bail, so I can leave and save my wife, or atleast die to save her. Agent Bryant: That is not necessary. We will get you out of here without bail Robb. ::Scene:: Rob Zone is beginning to feel better, and not so nervous. Robb Hardy looks sad and depressed, but with an angered look to. Robb Hardy: I need you to hurry Bryant, because I need my cell phone! Get it for me! Leroy James should be calling anytime soon. Agent Bryant: Really?! Brown! Go to the locker where they keep his stuff, grab his clothes, wallet, jewelry, cell phone, everything! Same with Rob Zone's stuff. But Robert(Rob Zone) you go with him, I need to talk with Robb Hardy alone. Rob Zone: Alright. ::Scene:: Rob Zone gets up a little more nervous now, and leaves the room with Brown. Agent Brown: If I wanted to, I would kick your ass right now. Rob Zone: ::Heart pounding:: Uh........ Agent Brown: Exactly, your scared as hell, I thought you would be. Rob Zone: ::Gains a little composure:: Heh, yeah right Brown, scared of a fat bellied old fool like you? Go get some donuts buddy, cause you need some, to you they will help you lose weight. Agent Brown: Why you little!! ::Scene:: Agent Brown goes to punch Rob Zone, but Rob Zone ducks and Agent Brown punches the wall, breaking his hand. Rob Zone: You idiot. Agent Brown: AH DAMN IT! Rob Zone: Now lets go get those clothes. ::Scene:: The scene goes back to Robb Hardy and Agent Bryant, with Robb Hardy thinking in his head, blaming himself for all this. Bryant then stands up calmly, and begins to speak. Agent Bryant: Robb, I know what kind of situation your in, and I feel bad for you. Robb Hardy: Oh really? You've been in a position like this? I truly believe you Bryant. Agent Bryant: Oh I have, and I didn't get to save my wife you see. The force wouldn't let me on this case because it involved my wife, and I would get to mad. I tried getting into the case by myself, and once I finally had them, and I was going to catch em, but the force threw me in jail, because they knew about my plans. They took me out of the CIA and put me here in a high position in the FBI. Now you know something very confidential. Robb Hardy: How come those bastards would never help me Bryant? They never helped me find the man who killed my first wife, who I miss very much, she was my high school sweet heart. Agent Bryant: You went to high school? Robb Hardy: Yeah I did, I also had a job too, raising my two brothers. I was also on the wrestling team. I didn't get the best grades, but I tried my best. Then, two fateful years ago, while I was in EPW, Red Wolf, a wrestler of that fed that is even sicker then me, killed my wife. I was not the only victim. He killed Chaos X's son and wife. He was one sick sunava..... Agent Bryant: Okay Robb, I get you point, I see why you want to get out of here, you want to save your wife and child. Robb Hardy: It's my fault that my wife died carrying my child, and it's also my fault that my parents died. I was away at middle school, so were my brothers. My mom and dad got in an argument, my dad and mom were fighting on the balcony at our apartment. My littliest brother Nick was watching. My dad pushed my mom over the balcony, accidentally. So my dad got the gun, got down on his knees, and blew his brains out. If I would of been there, I would have stopped the fighting as usual. But I had to be at school that day. DAMN IT! Agent Bryant: I understand Robb, we will get you out of here right away, but you need to come to our headquarters. Robb Hardy: ::A very sad expression is on Robb's face as he begins to speak:: Okay... ::Scene:: all of sudden Rob Zone and Agent Brown walking into the room with the clothes, one of Browns hands are just hanging down motionless, with a caste on it. Agent Bryant: What happened to your hand Brown? Agent Brown: Rob Zone broke it. Rob Zone: I did not Sir! He tried punching me, and I ducked, so his hand hit the wall. Agent Bryant: I believe you Zone. Now, Brown, you need to be taught a thing or two in this business. Just because your the law doesn't mean you can do anything you want, you have to be a professional, I can't believe your an FBI agent, hell Brown, I will get your ass put back into the LAPD. Agent Brown: No, not again! PLEASE NO! NOT THE LAPD! Agent Bryant: That's what I thought Brown. Oh yeah, Robb, we forgot one more thing, NGW sent you a tape of Dante Lock's last promo, would you like to see? Robb Hardy: Sure, why not. ::Scene:: Agent Bryant puts in the tape, and Robb begins to watch it. A even sadder expression comes on Robb's face. At the end of the promo, Robb stands up, and begins to speak. Robb Hardy: Dante, I feel really sorry for you, you've gone through some of the things I have, but not all of them, but that's not gonna stop me from coming to Storm and winning against you and that Canadian piece of trash Carrington. You can say CJ beat me around like a little puppet in EPW, I admit, he did beat me in matches, but I was still a top competitor, and CJ beating me doesn't stop making me a TOP COMPETITOR! Deal with it Dante, I still am, and always will be, A TOP COMPETITOR! N....... Agent Bryant: Okay Robb, we'll make sure to send him that message, now you and Zone get dressed, and we will be waiting out here. ::Scene:: Agent Bryant and Agent Brown walk outside, and begin to speak while the two change. Agent Bryant: Why did you do that Brown? Your going to ruin the whole operation! Agent Brown: I am tired of dealing with pieces of trash like these two. Agent Bryant: Listen Brown, I can pretty much tell that these two are good guys, Robb was just trying to save his kid, don't blame him for anything, I can tell that Robb Hardy is trying to change his life around, and Zone? Hell, he just got brought into this because he is friends with Hardy. Now Brown, leave these two guys alone, there pretty good guys, I know Robb Hardy has killed around 3 people that we know of, but it's not like those people didn't deserve it. Robb actually has been cleaning up the streets, that why he is peoples worst nightmare on those Los Angeles streets. Agent Brown: I guess your right Bryant..... ::Scene:: All of a sudden Rob Zone and Robb Hardy walk back out in there formal attire, finally out of those jail clothing. Agent Bryant slaps his hands together and begins to speak. Agent Bryant: Your free men! Now, you must come to our headquarters to get you ready for going to Leroy James' house. We will also get you badges, making you FBI agents for a week. Sound good? Robb Hardy: Sounds aight to me.... How bout you Zone? Rob Zone: Good to me, now lets get going. Agent Bryant: Alright, follow us. ::Scene:: With that, Rob Zone and Robb Hardy both follow Brown and Bryant out of the jail's secret questioning room. The camera then goes to Robb Hardy's face, with a sad expression as the scene fades. ::Scen Fades::
