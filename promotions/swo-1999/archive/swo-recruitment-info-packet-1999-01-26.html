Final Word ============================================================= I. Introduction We are the SWO, Resistance is Futile, you will be assimilated! The Surpreme Wrestling Organazation is a federation thought up by [name withheld]. He wanted to see some of wrestlings best and brightest stars in the same ring. So he went into his bank account and got his savings. Even though it wasn't enough he still keep trying. Richard went to Faygo Cola
Company and got some money to get started. Now he can't wait to get the SWO started.
Richard brings his former wrestling days into the SWO. He wanted the SWO to be and extreme and tridition together. He didn't want to go over the edge of extreme but he wanted just enough to keep the fans in intersest. So he came up with some ideas and is thinking of more.
============================================================= II. Roleplaying Roleplaying is going to be done to the whole federation. I will set a limit on how many RPs you can send out bcause I don't want one guy sending out a million short RPs through out the day no one can come back agenst. It makes the federation funner and better for all the members.
Item One: A rule that all of you know. QUALITY OVER QUANTITY! If someone sends me 500 RPs that only say "I'm gonna whoop you're ass because I can", and someone else sends me a VERY LONG RPs that actually goes somewhere... well, you tell ME who would win!
Item Two: Cursing will be allowed ONLY to a certain extreme. Roleplays with a curse word in every sentence won't be accepted.
Item Three: Any Racial Slurs or Shoots will be immediatedly discarded. The handler who sent in the roleplay will have consocenuses. They will eather be suspened or lose the stateis. Or even fired. There is enough in the world so lets not bring it here.
Item Four: For a roleplay to count towards a match it must be snt before the roleplay deadline. This way no one will get cheated because not everyone's life is online. So this helps all the members. It must be sent before or it won't count.
============================================================= III. Shows The SWO will have one weekly card. It will be on Saturday and will be called SWO Slamfest. It will have a least eight matchs a card and will be play-by-play. Some titles will be denfeded here and all things leading up to the PPV will happen here.
============================================================= IV. Pay-Per-Views The SWO will hold one PPV a month. They will usely be on the first or second Friday of the month. All titles will be defened here. The most hardcore matchs of the SWO will be at the PPVs. They will be name based on the month or fued or such.
============================================================= V. Rules All feds have to have rules. Here are the SWOs I.No complaing!! If you lose it is because I think the other person did a better job. So if you ask me why you lost or how. I will igrone you.
II.No sucking-up. I hate when people try to suck-up. I will amke you do worse then with out it. It wastes my time and yours.
III.Don't blame me because you lose. If you lose it won't be my fault. You lost because your opponant out RPed you or did a better job roleplaying. I don't pick you to win because I like you.
IV.No trying to chance desions. All decisions are final. If you want to try to change my mind go a head but it is not going to work and you are wasteing you time.
V.No making weired wrestlers. No one over 7'4" and 550 lbs. This gets out of hand and you don't see hardly anybody of this height or weight. So don't go over that or you will be ignored.
VI.No killing people in your RPs. This maybe fake but that is going over the line. Also on actacking th president or board members.
VII.Most of all have fun. This is for fun and not to get worked up about. If you do it ruines it for the other people.
VIII:NO TOSing. Me or anyone else. It ruins the fun for everyone else. ============================================================= VI. Titles & Challenges There are five belts in the SWO. "SWO World Wrestling Championship" - The Granddaddy of them all. The Elite of the Elite hold this belt, and defend it at every Pay-Per-View andsometimes at
Slamfest. "SWO Hardcore Championship" - This is the belt that is defened at every card. Mostly in regular matchs and at the PPVs in hardcore matchs. It is a belt for the fighting champion/
"SWO North American Championship" - This is the belt that will be defened at every other card and PPV. It is useally held for a long time because the champions are hardworkers.
"SWO International Title" - This is the belt that is a warm-up for the SWO World title. It is held by very few because it is hard to win. The champions are almost at World title statis but close.
"SWO World Wrestling Tag Championship" - This is the World title for tag teams. It will usally be defened at the PPV and sometimes at Slamfest. Most tag teams want it but few hold it.
This is the way challenging for the belt goes. I. Champions may challenge whomever they wish, but must accept at least one challenge per their title. II. The number one contender my challenge when ever he wants unless the title is on the line or a match as been signed for a card the next week or PPV.
============================================================= VII. SWO Staff(Characters) Vince Magnum-He is the main play-by player for the SWO. He knows most of the wrestling moves and calles matchs right down the middle.
Joey Gail:He is a hard hitting question asking kind of guy. He knows how and what to do to get a reaction from wrestlers.
Carly Needham:She likes wrestling because her husban Eron the Relentless is a former IWO great. She picks people to win and give mainly color commintry.
