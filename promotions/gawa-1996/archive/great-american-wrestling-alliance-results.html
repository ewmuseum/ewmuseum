Great American Wrestling Alliance results

GAWA Results Great American Annual--- 1) Johnny Champion b Todd Winner with the Johnny JumpUp 2)The Milkman beat the Child after hitting him with a milk bottle 3)Mike Reynolds b George North with the Figure Five 4) The Monarch, Mike Royal won a twenty man battle royale for the GAWA World Title, eliminating the Teamster, Travis Bickle,Go Mifune,The Assassin, and Red Justice Summerblast--- Johnny Champion b the Teamster with the Johnny JumpUp Travis Bickle beat the Child in a stretcher match Mike Reynolds & George North b The Homo & Butch Leather Jocko b Giant Johnson Go Mifune b The Assassin with the Nerve Pinch Mike Royal defended his title against Red Justice in a loser must retire match Halloween Hell--- The White Knights beat Reynolds & North Giant Johnson beat the Child with a choke slam through a table Go Mifune beat KKK Jocko eliminated Butch Leather to win the ten man battle Royal Mike Royal defended his title against Johnny Champion in a loser must retire match. Decemberbrawl--- White Knights b Homo & Leather KKK beat the Child when he blew fire in his face Go Mifune beat the Crusher with the Nerve Pinch The Teamster won the triangle match, beating Todd Winner and the Assassin to become the top contender Mike Royal defended his title agaist Jocko in a loser must retire match Harley Davidson won the Top Of The Heap Battle Royal, eliminating George North,Mike Reynolds,Travis Bickle, the Milkman,Merry Andrew and Slaughterhouse. Super Slam--- White Knights beat KKK & The Assassin with the Lance Slaughterhose beat the Child with aDDT on e bed of nails in a no DQ match Harley Davidson beat Go Mifune with the Chopper Todd Winner b the Milkman Travis Bickle won the "Bragging Rights" tournament fo the top contender spot, last deafeating Giant Johnson Mike Royal b The Teamster with a chair to the back in a loser must retire title match Slamfest---- White knights beat Todd Winner and Giant Johnson Butch Leather beat the Child when he pulled the Child's tights off and the Child ran away KKK beat the Homo Harley Davidson eliminated Giant Johnson,Go Mifune,The Assassin,The Milkman,Todd Winner,and George North,While Slaughterhouse eliminated Mike Reynolds, Merry Andrew, and the Crusher from the Top Contender Battle Royal,yet both men also eliminated eachother in the finish,creating controversey as to who the top contender really is. Mike Royal beat Travis Bickle in a loser must retire cage match for the GAWA World title. ---------Silvergaze "Get That Piece Of Crap Outta My Ring!" ---Steve Austin
