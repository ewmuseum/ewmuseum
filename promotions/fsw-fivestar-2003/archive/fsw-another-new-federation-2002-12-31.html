Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, I'm Pete, you may know me as the guy who runs Superslam or the handler of Derek Irvin or Debonair. Anyways, I don't know if any of you read night one of Superslam, but basically it was announced that I'll be opening up a federation after I put out the final two nights of Superslam - it'll be called Five Star Wrestling. I'll be putting out an official FAQ and do some official recruiting down the road, when we get a little bit closer to go time. However, if you know me or know you're going to want to be a part of this federation, you can send me an app now. I'm gonna want to keep the roster small and applying early could only help you. So, send me any old application you have lying around for the character you want to handle, or use the one included below. NOTE!
