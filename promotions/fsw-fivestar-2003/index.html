Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Five Star Wrestling recruited toward a first supercard, Day of Reckoning, named as roughly a week away in a post of 25 July 2003; the card itself followed within two weeks. Its own results were hosted off-site, but a third-party review of the show survives, recapping the card's own middle stretch in detail.

That review's own centerpiece is the promotion's first FSW Tag Team Championship, decided in a gauntlet that narrowed to a ladder match: America's Least Wanted fell early to Scarlett and Bratzke, who were themselves beaten by Youth Gone Wild, who then faced the returning Mason Walker in the ladder finish. Walker and Youth Gone Wild's Extasy each grabbed a title belt as the ladder fell, and Walker was first back to his feet — only for Extasy's own previously injured partner to recover, chairshot him, and grab the second belt, giving Youth Gone Wild both belts and the win.

The same card ran a triple threat won by John Shock, who pinned Johnny Malibu with a Yakuza Kick over the also-competing Vandal Gomez, and interstitial angles including Ricard confronting Rosco Riggins and Fire and Ice interrupting Youth Gone Wild's own championship celebration.

A weekly show, Saturday Night Slam, followed and survives in pointer form through its twelfth edition on 12 October 2003. No document past that date survives here, and how the promotion's own run continued or ended isn't in the surviving record.
