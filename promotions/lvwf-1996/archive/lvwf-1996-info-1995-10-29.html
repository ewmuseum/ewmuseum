WELCOME TO THE LVWF, the Las Vegas Wrestling Federation. This league is open to anybody! You can have as many wrestlers as you want, as long they are active, and original. Are you tired of joining a fed, then the fed quickly closes? This will not happen here! News letters will be sent out daily. And cards will be at least once a week
(Fridays) and maybe twice a week. Supercards will be every 4-6 weeks. Your wrestler can be signals, tag team, six-man or all of them. Only one thing if the face/heel ratio gets out of line I may have to ask to change, but that is about my only rule. You get bonuses for role-playing daily (this is how you win better) This league is not very complicated,
(besides the creation sheet) it's mainly going to be built on the managers role-play. You get a star for each wrestler by role-playing. Stars help you in matches, so you can tell me when you issue your challenges how many stars you want (1-5). If you are losing you can change your stats for 10 stars. That's about it...I'll send updates later, so below is the creation sheet.
