OLWA — Witch Hunt, 15 November 1997

15 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, I run an e-fed called the OLWA. Recently, I have had some people quit because they were unable to come online anymore. And I need more people to take their places. You can have real or fake wrestlers, up to four. I've got the Cruiserweight belt, US Belt, Tag Team Belts, and the WORLD Belt. Soon to introduce the TV Belt. Two cards a week, Monday and Thurday. A PPV once a month on Mondays, with a the normal Monday card moved to the previous Saturday. Why so few cards? To give people time to RP even if they can't get online as much as they'd like. If you're interested, please e-mail me about it, and we'll put something together. Signed and sincerely . . . DanteBK P. S. I've decided to post a couple of my best matches, to show you an example of what you might see twice a week in the OLWA. Our last match tonight, is Bane against Shawn Michaels in a pinfalls count anywhere match! This match starts off fast as Michaels goes in for a drop kick! Bane doesn't even move. Michaels with another drop kick! A spinning heel kick! Bane still hasn't moved. Michaels goes into the far ropes, runs forward and jumps off with a cross bodyblock! Bane catches him, and still hasn't taken one step back. Bane picks Michaels up and tosses him right over the top rope to the outside!! Michaels lands HARD a few feet up the steel ramp!! Bane simply steps out of the ring and pins Shawn Michaels. The referee runs over, and 1, 2, but Michaels lifts his shoulder up! Bane stands up. Michaels is getting up, Bane takes a swing at him, Michaels ducks and goes behind Bane. Michaels jumps up Bane's shoulders, spins around going for a flying headscissors shades of every Luchadore Mike Tenay knows! Bane is thrown off the ramp and he falls inbetween the ramp and guard rail! Michaels landed with a bit of a somersault a foot or so down the ramp. Michaels is up, he runs over and jumps off the ramp onto a now standing Bane. And Bane catches his him! Bane slams him down in a spinebuster on the cement floor! Bane with a cover, the referee goes over and starts counting 1, 2, but Michaels puts a foot in the railing. The referee decides to call of the pin. Huh? We'll have to ask President Beck later. Bane picks Michaels up and throws him on the ramp. Bane climbs up and is standing on the ramp looking down at Michaels. Bane picks Michaels up, and punches him. Bane is holding Michaels up by his hair. Bane throws Michaels a few feet up the ramp. He's almost all the way up the ramp to the flat area there. Michaels is getting up. Bane is slowly walking up to Michaels. Michaels steps onto the flat area. Bane is standing at the edge of the flat area, almost on the ramp. Michaels is starting to look awake. He sees Bane. Michaels rears back for the Sweet Chin Music, Bane doesn't move out of the way. Superkick!! Bane falls back, does a backwards somersault down the ramp and stands up again! Michaels is turned around with his hands raised in victory. Michaels turns around, ducks and runs over to pin Bane where he thought he'd be. Bane grabs Michaels, and lifts him up with a power bomb!! He threw him a couple feet back to the flat area with that!! Michaels has GOT to be out! Bane could go for the cover, but he decides to pick Michaels up for the Bane Breaker. Michaels is staring at the ceiling and Bane is walking over to the edge of the flat area where he stands about 15 feet from the cement floor. What's he doing?? I think he's going to throw Michaels off! Michaels wiggles and gets out of it, he falls behind Bane and a drop kick knocks Bane off the flat area!! Bane hit the ground HARD and may have hit his head!! Bane is . . getting up?? Bane is standing! Michaels reaches through the curtain and takes out a steel chair. Michaels throws the chair down at Bane and Bane catches it before it lands on his head! Michaels jumps off with a missle drop kick!! Michaels drove the steel chair Bane was holding right into Bane's head!! Michaels is laid out on the floor and so is Bane! The referee is coming down. He doesn't really know what to do, I don't think there's supposed to be a 10-count in a pinfalls count anywhere match. About 45 seconds go by, both men are starting to stir. Michaels is up, Bane is up. They're standing with their backs to each other. They both turn around and SUPERKICK!!! Bane is down again! Michaels with the cover! 1! 2!! 3!!! What an INCREDIBLE victory from Shawn Michaels!! He will go on to face Tim Tower at Witch Hunt tomorrow, I can't imagine him being completely recovered by then! We're out of the time ladies and gentlemen, this has been quite a night! The OLWA WORLD Title Belt is being lowered from the top of the arena on a rope, a ladder is brought into the ring. Shawn Michaels music comes on and he dances his way to the ring, accompianed by Jenna Jameson. Tim Tower's music is on, but he doesn't dance. He is accompianed by Christine. The bell rings, and Michaels immediately starts climbing the ladder as fast as he can. Tower runs out from his corner and tackles the ladder! The ladder falls and drops Michaels, lower back first, onto the top rope. Michaels slips over and falls out of the ring, he lands on his feet. Tower rights the ladder and is climbing it. Michaels slides in the ring and runs over to drop kick Tower in the hip, knocking him off the ladder. Tower is getting up, Michaels climbs a few rungs on the ladder and jumps off with a clothesline! Michaels kips-up! Michaels looks pretty confident, he's climbing the ladder. Tower is up, he grabs Michaels from behind and pulls him off throwing him against the ropes and whipping him into the ladder! This match is still young, and Michaels has been taking some bumps! The ladder is down, Michaels is down. Tower is picking Michaels up and he slams his head into a nearby turnbuckle. Tower pulls him a little closer to the middle and whips him across, Michaels running forward, he ducks a forearm, bounces off the other side, slides under Tower's legs and a standing drop kick pushes Tower into the ropes. Michaels runs forward and clotheslines Tower over the top rope! Michaels outside the ring, he picks up Tower with a scoop slam onto the announce table and Tower slides off and lands on the other side. And I think I'm going to take my headset and the nifty extension cord and stand over away from this table. Michaels back in the ring, he gets the ladder, pushes it together and lays it on the top rope. Tower is up, he's standing on the table. Michaels over into the far ropes, he runs up the ladder hitting every step along the way and jumps off with a cross body right into Tim Tower!! Tower and Michaels fly off the table and smash into the steel railing! Michaels bounced off Tower and landed on the same fans who got the Copycat earlier. They're sure getting their money's worth. Both men are slow to get up. Tower's standing, Michaels is climbing over the railing Tower grabs him and lifts him up in a Gorilla press into a slam on the table! That's no taped up Public Enemy table folks, if that thing breaks it will break for real! Tower is standing on the table, he picks up Shawn Michaels and looks like he is setting him up for the Tower Bomb. Michaels grabs Tower in a double leg takedown, Tower is trying not to fall off the table. He gets up and Michaels goes for a spinning heel kick but Tower ducks and Michaels falls onto the floor! Michaels getting up and Tower with a moonsault! Tower rolls in the ring and sets up the ladder. And here comes Bane! He comes into the ring and grabs Tim Tower, he lifts him up high in the air, power bomb position. And Bane smashes Tower down into the ladder!! Tower could be hurt. Michaels is coming into the ring and yelling at Bane. I think he's asking what Bane is doing out here. Bane grabs Michaels by the neck and lifts him up for a one-handed chokeslam!! Bane leaves the ring and doesn't look back. Both men are laid out flat. Tim Tower is lying on top of the ladder, Michaels is nearby counting the lights. It's been about 45 seconds now. Michaels is trying to get up, he falls down near the ropes on the same side as the aisle. Tower is getting up, he picks up the ladder and is stumbling around a bit. He's not fully here, I don't think. He's now standing inside of the upright ladder. Michaels is standing. He looks over at Tower and goes for the Superkick through the rungs of the ladder and smacking Tower right in the jaw!! Tower falls down, which moves the ladder back a few inches. Michaels is climbing up the ladder. He's standing on top of the ladder, he's a few inches away from the belt because of the position of the ladder. He jumps for it and snatches the belt right off the rope and falls down to smack into the canvas! Shawn Michaels is your NEW OLWA WORLD Heavyweight Champion! He is trying to get up, he has his hands in the air! Tower is starting to get up, he comes over to Michaels and . . . shakes his hand! Tim Tower is leaving the ring, as Buff Bagwell and Syxx come out to help Shawn Michaels celebrate! We're out of time, ladies and gentlemen. Thank you for watching Witch Hunt!
