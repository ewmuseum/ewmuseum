Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Extreme Power, a Portuguese fan's own blog-hosted e-fed, booked the real WWE roster under its own invented company banner across at least two weekly shows in December 2006. Its sixth broadcast, on 8 December, saw its general manager sign AJ Styles for a triple-threat Intercontinental Championship challenge the following week, Batista take out the Dudley Boyz in a handicap match after losing a number-one-contender bout to Rob Van Dam, and D-Generation X capture the Tag Team Championship from Edge and Randy Orton in a sequence of interference finishing with a Pedigree.

A further show the next night, staged as an extra card on the promotion's own “Spain tour,” saw D-Generation X retain those titles against Chris Jericho and Bubba Ray Dudley, Batista beat Kurt Angle to avenge his own loss to Rob Van Dam, and Scotty 2 Hotty and Jimmy Wang Yang steal a win from Sylvan and MVP with a blind tag.

After a hiatus, the promotion's own operator announced a comeback on 12 February 2007 with two new signings, Brock Lesnar and Joey Mercury, and posted a full set of predictions for a comeback card — including a three-way for the Intercontinental Championship and Batista defending the World Championship against Undertaker.

No further capture survives to confirm whether that comeback card, or anything after it, was ever staged.
