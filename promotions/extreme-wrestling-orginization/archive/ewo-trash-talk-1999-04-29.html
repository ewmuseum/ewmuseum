Trash Talk

EWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWO [the scene fades in moments after Ragnarok wins the Heavyweight Belt. We are in the New Apocalypse locker room and the champagne is flowing...people are cheering....and there are quite a few beautiful women running around wearing only digitized mosaics. The cameras approach Rags]
CM : Rags!...Rags!!....can we get a word? Rags : WOOOOOHOOOOO!!!! [holding the belt over his head] It has ALL come full circle..Titan started this dance a year ago......but he dropped the ball......and I sure as HELL picked it up! The New Apocalypse is JUST warming up boys...don't you forget it. Juan?...congrats on winning the IC belt...but it just goes to show that you are merely a shadow of your former self. You couldn't scratch with the big cats...so you got your ass kicked out of the sandbox. And Johnny.....<smirk>....LHW title?....damn brother.....if I knew it was THAT easy....I'd start having flashbacks of my own...<laughing>...now.....I know that we were all associated with each other at one point......but I needed to move on to bigger and better things....[holding up the belt again]..and it seems I have. Chain Gang is soft....it has no edge. Hassan..Billy Ray....you know what I mean....you can feel it. Well....the only "hardness" the Chain Gang has will be the swelling when I get done with them. Juan?...Johnny?....*please*....for your own saftey.....stay happy with the belts you have...you couldn't handle the ride if you tried for this one. Now....on to other news....[reaching off camera and grabbing a "New Apocalypse" T-Shirt...holding it up to the camera]....I told you this was coming.....now...you *know* who you are....the ball is on your court. My offer is open...but it won't be for long....think it over.....and now....the BEST DAMN CHAMP this fed has SEEN is gonna have me some fun!
CM : best champ?...no offense....but look at the company you are now in. Not to belittle your accomplisment...
Rags : Sammy....[Rags motions to the cameraman. We see Sammy come walking over to the cameraman...then we hear a "THUD"....the picture shakes and slips...then resumes...from behind the camera we hear Sammy]
Sammy : Go ahead bro. Rags : That's right EWO....the *BEST DAMN CHAMP* you have ever seen....2 days into the eWo's new reign....and I'm 7-0. Top that. This interview is over. [camera fades] [scene opens in Bobby Harbinger's office. There are 3 people sitting inside..Ragnarok..with the Heavyweight belt...."Streetwise" Sammy Paulison, and Rocky "Clutch" Cammarelli. Rocky is sitting on Bobby's desk....Sammy is in a chair...looking like he is in a funk....and Rags is in Bobby's chair smoking a cigar. The sounds of papparazzi are outside....the trio is ignoring them.]
Rags : C'mon dawg...what's bugging you? Sammy : [looking to Rags] We're supposed to be a team right? Rags : and we're not? Sammy : Don't feel that way Rags...not one damn bit. Rags : [sitting up...] what are you saying Sammy? Sammy : C'mon damnit.....ever since Street Machine joined the RoT...now
NA...we've been "second fiddle" to [mockingly dramatic] the BEST DAMN
CHAMPION..yadda yadda yadda......right Rocky?...back me up man.. [Rocky stares at the floor...and says nothing] [Sammy stands quickly...kicking over the chair he was sitting in . The sudden movement causes Rags to stand quickly as well.]
Sammy : DAMNIT Rocky!....don't YOU go AWOL on me now! Rags : SAMMY!....Check yourself man......I don't know WHAT is up with you lately..but FIRST you snap on Bobby last week...and now Rocky?...Maybe YOU are the problem.
[Sammy grabs Rags by the front of his T-Shirt...rage in his eyes. Rocky continues to stare at the floor. Rags glares at Sammy with fire in his eyes...but no expression on his face. When Rags speaks...it is in a calm
...measured tone]
Rags : Sammy?....think very carefully about your next move brother. I don't know what's in your head right now man......but think. Think if you have enough to put me down right now......think if you have the balls to try.....cause if the answer to either of those questions is "no".....we are gonna have the shortest argument in the history of this office....and Rocky over there will start wrestling singles.
[Sammy.....trembling with rage...lets go.] Rags : Now.....[grabbing his belt] I am gonna go outside and deal with the
"ravaging masses" to give you two some time to get your head straight.
[stands and leaves. As the door opens we are almost blinded by flash-bulbs as cameras click away. Rags closes the door.]
[Rocky looks at Sammy] Rocky : what the F*CK is WRONG with you man? Do you have ANY IDEA how BAD that makes us look?
Sammy : Tell me I'm wrong. Rocky : Damn RIGHT you're wrong!. If it wasn't for Rags...WE'D STILL BE IN
JAIL!!!...Remeber THAT?...Jail??...the thing with the walls?....and the bars?
Sammy : And the guards with the bullets that shot Vinnie? Rocky : Knock that sh*t off Sammy. You know as well as I do that wasn't
Rags' fault. So why are you using all this energy to get mad at HIM?....you
SHOULD have used it when we fought Death Row.....maybe we would have won then....
Sammy : You saying *I* dropped the ball? Rocky : I'm *saying* you need to focus that energy correctly. We have looked like total *humps* lately..and I think we need to "regroup". You're jealousy of Rags having the Heavyweight belt needs to stop. He's getting press cause he won. *WE* win....*WE* get press...*that's* the way it goes..get it?
Besides....Street Machine is more than you and I...... [Sammy looks confused] Rocky : You forget Vinnie already? [Sammy looks to the ground] Rocky : Now.....Vinnie is *still* part of this group..even if he's in a chair...and even if he's not part of our tag-team. Know what I'm sayin?
Let's do this for Vin. We need to support him too. You keep walking around with your head up your ass....is not helping him.
Sammy : You're right man.....[shaking his head].....I....forgot we were a team..... Rocky : Check it Sammy......we got a match against Death Row in the upcomming card........ Sammy : [evil grin]...Revenge *IS* sweeter than Christmas. Rocky : NOW you're talking bro....... Sammy : [holding out his hand].....for Vinnie... Rocky : [grabs Sammy's hand]....for Vinnie.... [the duo stand and head for the door. As they open the door the papparazzi turn to look at them....]
Rocky : Folks?....we have an announcement...... [camera fades] EWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWO [scene fades in on a panoramic view of a horizon. Everything seems to have this slight golden sheen to it...very surreal. We see small sihlouets in the backround...flying across the sky; not small because they are tiny...but because they are far away. They appear to be humans......humans with wings...drifting across a sky that is in permanent sunrise. The scene slowly pans down to a man..roughly eight feet tall, and very well built. He has waist-length, pin straight, honey-blond hair, painfully bright blue eyes, and a clean shaven face. He is wearing golden armor on his left arm from his elbow to his wrist, his right shoulder, both shins, and a gold colored battle skirt. His name is Ahura Mazda, and he is very displeased. He glares down at a figure crouched at his feet. The figure has long black hair, and large white angel wings. His face is obscured by his hair. Ahura Mazda unfolds his arms from his chest.....without saying a word, he raises his right arm and points. The camera shifts behind the crouching figure as the figure snaps his head up to look at Ahura Mazda in disbelief. Ahura Mazda continues to glare at the figure...his eyes getting harder. The figure stands.......and walks of camera. We slowly pan in on Ahura Mazda's face and see one lone tear drifting down from his left eye. The scene shifts to the figure standing infront of a large, gold gate. We still do not see the figure's face. The gold gate opens....and the figure walks through.
Immediately we are given a sense of vertigo as the figure plummets.....the gate dissappearing from view above us. The figure tumbles uncontrolably downward...his wings finally catching some sort of wind..but this only causes the figure to plummet in a sickening spiral. The camera cuts to the ground....which is tinted ominously red. Without warning a figure crashes to the ground on his back...small tufts of flame bursting out from under him.
He rolls over....still obscuring his face. He rests on his hands and knees as the camera starts to spin around him.....slowly at first...then...gradually picking up speed. The tips of his wings start to singe...then burn. As the flames travel up his wings....they almost appear to be turning into bat-like wings...but then...even they crumble off into ash. As the wings burn off completely...we notice for the first time..a distinguishing mark on the bicep of the figure's right arm. A tattoo that reads "RoT". The figure stands, and glares to the heavens. We spin around in fron of him to realize that this is Ragnarok. He reaches for the sky...holding the Heavyweight Belt in one hand...and the Hardcore belt in the other and screams at the top of his lungs...] Rags : AT LEAST I HAVE THESE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [jump cut] [Rags sits up in bed in a cold sweat, shaking slightly. The woman with the dirty blond hair next to him shifts slightly...but does not waken. Rags looks to his night-stand where he sees a half eaten Snickers bar, and a rented copy of "The Prophecy"]
Rags : [rolling over and trying to get back to sleep...]..Man.....I have to stop eating chocolate before I go to bed]
[scene fades] EWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWO [scene opens in the New Apocalypse press room....only the NA banners and colors are not flying today....no...the room is decked out in the colors of the Jersey City Street Machine. Bobby Harbinger is behind the podium...and
"Streetwise" Sammy Paulison, and Rocky "Clutch" Cammarelli sit on either side of him. Rags, Gina, and Vinnie are no where to be found] Bobby : Before we begin, I would like to adress the obvious questions that everyone will have. First...Vinnie "Stone Pony" Cepacelli and Gina
Pettigrano are not here due to Vinnie's continuing physical therapy. Ragnarok is not here because this is not his day. This day is for the Street
Machine. No questions regarding Ragnarok will be answered.....OK?
Now...Ladies and Gentlemen I give you....The Jersey City Street Machine!!!!!!!!!! [Bobby steps aside as Sammy and Rocky take the podium] Sammy : We mainly wanted to pull you all here to dispell alot of the rumors that have been circulating around about us..and...to preview our *new* commercial.
Rocky : So....who's first? [Hands go up and Sammy points to one of the first] Scott K. : Is the JCSM gonna split so the two of you can pursue singles titles? Rocky : Ya know...that's the popular rumor we've heard too...and NO...that is completely false. The JCSM is, was and will be the best Tag Team in the EWO. Sammy : Rocky and I have had our differences lately, but everyone goes through that. We have solidified our position and we will once again rule the EWO's Tag division. Next?
[hands go up again....Rocky picks one] Chris Z. : How has joining New Apocalypse affected your mindset? Rocky : Sammy?....you want to feild this one? Sammy : Our mindset has not changed....we are just getting there through different means now. We don't just try to elevate ourselves....but...the group as a whole.
Rocky : I agree. We can't just think of ourselves anymore. We think about the greater needs of the NA. It has taken a while...but we are there. If ya don't think so....check out what we do to Death Row on Saturday.
Now....Bobby?...lights please. [the lights fade as the commercial plays on a large screen] [the commercial opens with Rocky and Sammmy on their Harley's cruising towards the camera. Interspersed with this footage we see clips from some of their greatest matches.....Death Row.....Cyber Raiders.....La
Familia.......crushing scenes of chair shots.....powerslams.....piledrivers...and of course...Exit 14a. ( a combination Super-Plex from one corner while the partner frog splashes from the other ). The bikes whiz by the camera as a T-Shirt spins into view. The front has the JCSM logo on it with the words "If ya can't ride with the Big
Boys......" and on the back in bold letters "Keep your toys in the yard!"
The scene then fades it to view all tyhe JCSM apparel....T-shirts, leather trenches...denim jackets....baseball hats.....]
V/O : Think you got what it takes to ride with the Machine? Show YOUR true colors with the OFFICAL JCSM line. And remember....if it doesn't say JCSM on it [Rocky and Sammy's voices] IT AIN'T <BEEP>!!
[lights come back up as Sammy and Rocky head back to the podium.] Rocky : Death Row? Be prepared. This isn't the *old* JCSM....We have our sights set on one thing....those belts...um....2........2 things...<chuckling>...and we ain't gonna stop till they are back around our waists.
Sammy : We *were* top dogs.....and we WILL be top dogs again. We are gonna take EWO by storm, just like we did the first time...only *this* time...the wounds won't heal so quickly. Death Row?....you will be the stepping stone.
When people look back through the history of the EWO...they will look at
April 10th and say "THAT was where the second reign began."
Rocky : Now...if you'll excuse us.....this meeting is over. [everyone starts to file out] [camera fades]
