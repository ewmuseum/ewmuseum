Open to New Talent!

29 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RSPWFers, The EWO recently closed it's doors to new talent, but as of now, it is back open due to the fact that I will be removing ALL the slackers and replacing them with consistant roleplayers who are willing to participate and have a good time. We're kinda overloaded in the Tag Team department, but if you
REALLY want your tag team in, I'm sure we can work something out. :-)
Brief History: The EWO opened April 1st, 1997 and was very successful until it closed in
June of '98. March 20, 1999; the EWO re-opened with a bang! Since then, it has been going strong, and hopes to stay that way. May 16th, the EWO will present King of Extreme '99: No Limits
Minor notes: The EWO is NOT, I repeat, NOT a hardcore rules fed. However, we do have a hardcore title AND we occasionally have extreme rule matches.
About the EWO: We have one major card a week, Slammin' Saturday Night, and every now and then, we'll have a house show which is darked. Flashing is a MUST, but it's not the only way you can win. Winners are determined by heat/push/storyline and ALL of that comes from roleplaying and being an active member.
