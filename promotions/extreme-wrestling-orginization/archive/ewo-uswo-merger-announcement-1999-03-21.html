[fade in: we're inside and auditorium packed with people. On the stage, sits EWO President Aaron Kozak and USWO President [name withheld]. Aaron steps up to the podium and the crowd comes to a hush] Kozak- "EHEM...(FEEDBACK)....last night after a long discussion with the United States Wrestling Organization president, [name withheld]....I came to a dramatic conclusion. Neither the USWO or the EWO will survive on there own" [the crowd mumbles] Kozak- "The odds are entirely against it....neither Nick, nor I have the financial support to keep them going, so in order to save BOTH of our feds, we made a decision...for the good of both feds...." [the crowd mumbles louder] Kozak- "On April 4th, 1999....the Extreme Wrestling Organization and the United States Wrestling Organization....will become ONE!" [the crowd begins to talk among themselves and screaming questions at Mr. Kozak. He quiets them down] Kozak- "April 4th, our two worlds will come together at huge Pay-Per-View event and all title holders will be decided that night! Any questions?" [as hands shoot up, the scene fades out] =====out of character====== EWOers/USWOers, Yes Folks! We are merging! WOOHOO....but...before we can do anything, I'm going to need all of your help. We can make this a big success or we can screw it up, your choice. I personally want this to be big. I've always had fun running the EWO, I've done it for over a year, so all I ask is that you give me an Nick a little time to get settled before deciding you're not interested, cool?
