Where's JIMMY "ME SMASH"? 5.  RAGNAROK speaks EWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWO It's been a rough couple of weeks, eWo-ites!  KoZaK Sports' CEO AARON KOZAK has stepped down as president of the eWo, leaving operation of the world's greatest e-wrestling federation in the hands of his former Vice-President, now eWo President DAVID NIKITHSER.  We caught up with President Nikithser in his office at KoZaK Plaza and got these comments, the first since his taking office at the helm of the eWo. EWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWO (Camera pans from the doorway of a large corner office to a seated figure at a large mahogany desk.  It's eWo President David Nikithser -- as he notices the camera, he removes his glasses and folds his hands on the desk, addressing the viewing public) Good evening.  For those of you who are new to the eWo, welcome.  I'm David Nikithser, President of the Extreme Wrestling Organization. The departure of Aaron Kozak as president of the organization came as a shock to everyone.  Once the initial gravity of the announcement had passed, it became apparent that the organization could not die as a result of Kozak's resignation.  We at the eWo have developed a reputation for putting on exciting showcases featuring the Internet's greatest talent.  As Aaron's Vice-President, I helped insure that this level of excellence was never compromised. Therefore, it seemed logical that when Aaron stepped down, I would take his place at the top of the organization, to make sure that everyone in the organization experienced a smooth transition of power. Further to that, it has been decided by myself and the eWo executive committee that there will be no changes to our current title holders and contender positions.  There will be a brief "transitional period" while we re-organize the front office and make some necessary changes to our roster, which will require us to limit the number of upcoming cards over the next few weeks.  I promise that the Extreme Wrestling Organization will be back to its full capacity very soon, and I thank you all for your continued support. (camera fades) EWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWO One of those title holders is [name withheld], former EWO World Champion and current Light-Heavyweight champion.  This Saturday, at the eWo's first "Slammin' Saturday Night" since the shake-up upstairs, Garza will face MR. MACPHISTO of the Hellfire Club in a non-title contest. Garza had this to say about his opponent this weekend: EWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWO (Scene opens in Juan's backyard, Juan is doing laps in his Olympic size pool, when he gets to the shallow end of the pool he touches the side of the pool and shouts out a number 23.....24.....25.....26.....this goes on for a good ten minutes then Juan comes gets out of the pool and starts to dry off) Juan: Mr. Macphisto I still have guard with you, you tried to hurt my friend, my partner, Johnny Law, do you remember that?  You should, it was the HC tournament, it was your match, but then you had Strahd help you out and cheat, but Johnny still won.  Well, from what I heard my brother say, Johnny was gonna ditch me well, I don't really care, ya see even though that was gonna happen at the time he was my friend, so I hold a grudge, it will be settled at SSN though, don't worry I will settle it, I will get in the ring and I will show why I am still the eWo LHW Champion! Daniel, I hear what you say, and ya know what, I believe you....I know that most people are saying, WHAT!?!?!  Why?  Well, I'll tell you why, because Daniel you are my brother and you will always be my brother and I know that you would never try to hurt me, I know you said you were thinking of hurting me, well, you didn't and I still have the eWo LHW belt around my waist, so Daniel I am going to leave Chain Gang, yeah that's right Juan will no longer be Chain Gang!  Never again will I join them, never, oh am I outta time?  My trainer says I gotta go because I need to get the exercise for my match against Macphisto (Camera fades to black as Juan dive into the pool and start doing laps again) EWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWO Bombshell statement from Juan, as the imminent collapse of the CHAIN GANG grows even closer!  And what it brother DANIEL GARZA's opinion of this, as well as his feelings toward wrestling AMERICAN PSYCHO, who we are still unsure of as far as his status in the Chain Gang?  Daniel had this to say: EWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWO (Scene opens at a bar, Daneil has six or seven empty shot glasses in front of him, he looks as if he hasn't shaved in a couple of days, He looks really bad, his hair is messed up and he is babbling something about Chain Gang) Daniel: I can't believe it.....I was so stupid....what was I thinking.....American Psycho, The new Prez of the eWo has sanctioned a match between us, I don't believe me and you have ever met in the ring, well, Psycho I was going to join you, before all hell broke lose in the eWo I was planning on turing on my own flesh and blood, I planned on making him lose the title, then pandemonium broke out and I realized that what I was doing was wrong, you were tempting me, you are like the devil himself, I will strike down with a furious blow!  I will make sure that you remember the name a Daniel Garza! Chain Gang, there was a group that was all about Pride and Glory!  There was a group that fought for the good in life, well, Juan as much as I hate to say this, that was all a bunch of Bull.  I learned that from a source that must remain unidentified.  This person informed that they were going to kick you out of Chain Gang, yes that's right, they had been working out a plan all along with American Psycho, he was going to win your title with my help then they were all going to turn, I did not know about this and I am not sure if they were going to turn on me or not, but I did know they were going to turn on you, so Juan I say ditch Chain Gang, I am going to I hope you do the same. (Camera fades out as Daniel has two more shots) EWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWO Sounds like if it were up to Daniel, there'd be no Psycho in his Gang! We'll have more, including the status of current EWO World Champion JIMMY "ME SMASH" after this. Welcome Back to Trash Talk.  We're about to roll some never-before-seen footage of what happened backstage after Hardcore Heaven, where JIMMY "ME SMASH" defeated EWO mainstay TITAN for the World Championship.  We know you've heard the rumors, now see for yourself: EWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWO Scene opens in a locker room.  A row of lockers line the back wall, and a pair of small benches standing in front of them. Jimmy "Me Smash" enters the room, carrying his newly won Heavyweight Championship Belt.  He is closely followed by his coach, JT. JT - "Well done Jimmy!  I knew you could beat that loud mouth braggart Titan." Jimmy stares at his reflection in the belt. JT - "Jimmy!" Jimmy looks up ponderously. JT - "YOU SMASHED!" Jimmy jumps up shouting "ME SMASH!  ME SMASH!" Vicki walks in, wearing a black, form-fitting body stocking. She runs up and hugs Jimmy, cheering. Vicki - "You did it!" Jimmy - "ME SMASH!" Vicki, crying happily - "Tonight's purse, plus the money I won betting on you, and the money we already had saved over the last few months, means we have enough to buy a house big enough for you to romp around in without breaking anything." Jimmy stares at Vicki. Vicki - "We don't need any more money, honey.  You can stop wrestling now.  Well, we have a contract to wrestle in Kozak's Invitational, but after that, you're through." Jimmy, quietly - "Smash?" Vicki - "You don't have to anymore.  No more smash." Jimmy, very faintly - "Nooo smash?" Vicki - "No more smash Jimmy.  You don't have to.  We can go back to just living quietly.  C'mon.  Lets get some oreos to celebrate." Vicki starts to leave, leading Jimmy out by the hand. Jimmy sluggishly follows. JT starts to follow, but stops in the door glancing back.  He shakes his head almost mirthfully and then gets the belt from where Jimmy dropped it. JT walks out, hitting the lights switch as he goes. EWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWO I don't know what this means for the World Title, eWo fans. . .hopefully we'll get an answer this Saturday Night. In this Saturday's Main Event, GODSEND must defend his recently-won Hardcore Championship against a man who many claim is the most dangerous man in the eWo -- RAGNAROK.  We caught up with Rags, who had this to say about his upcoming match: EWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWO <<camera jump-cuts through shakey black-and-white clips of Titan losing the belt, all the matches leading up to the Hardcore Title, Ragnarok punking Titan with a steel chair.....camera then cuts to the eWo conference room. Ragnarok, Bobby Harbinger, and Pandora are sitting at the conference table. Rags is wearing his RoT hockey jersey, Bobby is wearing a black double-breasted suit with black shirt and red tie, and Pandora is wearing a pair of faded cut-off denim shorts, and an RoT T-Shirt with the sleeves cut off and cropped very short, exposing her midriff>> Rags : OK people...lets make this short and sweet, K? <<reporters hands leap into the air, as President Nikithser looks on from the back of the room...hoping this doesn't get out of hand.  Bobby points to one of the reporters.>> Reporter : Ragnarok....don't you think that the RoT is now doomed to fail?..seeing as you are now the only member? Rags : Bobby...hand me that list... <<Bobby leans over and hands Rags a sheet of paper>> Rags : Ok.....Lemme see...WE lost Titan...hmmmm...the eWo lost 90% of Chain Gang, the tag team champs Death Row, AND the new Champ, Jimmy "Me Smash". Seems you punks got the worst of the deal there huh? <<once again reporters hand fly into the air....another one is picked>> reporter 2 : What was the explaination for you punking Titan? Rags : The RoT will only survive on determination...and will thrive on perfection.  Titan dropped the ball...for that he was punished. <<a female reporter stands...a sly smirk on her face>> female reporter : Umm...Mr. Ragnarok.  Are the rumors true that you are looking to....expand your stable..perhaps another valet?.... <<Pandora quickly stands>> Pandora : It is SO important for your continued survival that you do not complete that sentence missy.  Listen here.  Ragnarok and the RoT are goin' places..you hear?  They are...and will be..the new standard that all will try to reach.  Bobby and I are behind Rags 100%. Now...if you're done flirting with my man why don't you sit your ass back down in that chair before I put you so far into it they'll need the jaws of life and a can of Crisco to get you out. <<mortified...and scared for her life..the reporter sits.>> Bobby : Well....I think Pandora has pretty much said it all.  The RoT will continue; stronger than ever. <<Nikithser starts to stand...hand raised>> Bobby : Thank you everyone....no further questions. <<The reporters start to stand....readying to leave>> Nikithser : Wait JUST a second there..Mr. "RoT" <<the room falls into a hush>> Rags : <smirking>...yes? Nikithser : This is still MY fed.  KoZaK sports STILL owns it.  You want to have your little "wrestling revolution"....fine.....but stop talking like this is a corporate takeover. Rags :  <smirking even bigger>  we'll see.......we shall see.... <<camera fades>> EWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWOEWO Nothing to say at all about the Hardcore Title match, but several opinions on the state of the eWo roster, and the Reign Of Terror. We'll just have to wait and see the outcome this Saturday in East Rutherford -- at SLAMMIN' SATURDAY NIGHT!  See you next week!
