About the President 10. Enjoy! ------------------------------------------------- 1. What is the EWO? The EWO, Extreme Wrestling Organization, is an e-mail wrestling federation that began April 1st, 1997. It was one of the top feds in the game until its closing in June of 1998. December 13, 1998, the EWO officially re-opened, hoping to continue its tradition of exellance. 2. Playing the game We at the EWO, take pride in the fact that we are a booked fed. A booked fed is a fed that decides the outcome of matches by how well one roleplays for there wrestler. We do NOT use a simulator of any kind. There are three ways you can roleplay: Flashes- This is the "bread and butter of every fed!" Flashing is the format in which wrestlers state their opinions on other wrestlers, problems in the feds, or just to talk about an upcoming match. A flash should have the following: 1)Set the scene 2)Wrestler comments 3)Ending(ie:[fade out]) Flashes don't ALWAYS have to be like that, but the average flash usually follows that format. Here's an sample flash: [the scene opens in the computer room of Extreme Wrestling Organization president, Aaron Kozak. He is dressed in a pair of dirty faded blue jeans and a stained sweatshirt he bought at Wal-Mart. He leans his chair back on its back legs and strechtes. He yawns and scratches his stomach just before his chair falls over] (SMASH) Aaron- "Aww crap!" [He sets his chair back up and plops down onto it. He turns his body to the so that he is facing the camera] Aaron- "Mary had a little Lamb....whose fleece was white as snow...." [he takes a sip of some water] Aaron- "and everywhere that Mary went..." [Aaron glares into the camera] Aaron- "The lamb was sure to go" [Aaron turns back to his computer and begins typing as the camera fades to black] In-Rings- I love these things. Not only do that add length to the cards, but in my opinion, they're more entertaining than simple flashes. An in-ring is nothing more than a wrestler speaking in the ring, hence the name "in-ring." You just come out during a show, and talk. The only difference between a regular flash and an in-ring, is that is should contain crowd responses(ie: cheering). Strats- These are what you write in order to interfere in matches, do something cool during a match, or plans for your match. I do not require these, but I HIGHLY RECCOMEND THEM for EVERY match you participate in. These can be the difference between a win and a loss, plus it really helps out the writers. A strat should look like this: Wrestler: Joey Jo Bob Event: Slammin' Saturday Night Match: Joey Jo Bob VS Mike Rotch Plans: This is where you tell me what you want to happen. You could attack someone back stage before the match, have the lights go out after the match, do pretty much what ever you want, within reason. You should also include your plans for the match. Like, do you plan to concentrate on one part of the body(ie: left leg), or use a wide variety of power moves. Just different things like that, be creative. 3. Winning/Losing Like I originally stated, the EWO is a booked fed, but the difference between the EWO and most other feds is, that we don't determine the winner by just how well your roleplay. We pick the winners by heat/push/storyline. Why do we need to roleplay then? Simple. If you don't roleplay, you won't get a push, which means you won't build any heat, and that will gradually drop you to the bottom of the ranks. 4. Cards Until the EWO grows, there will only be one card a week, plus a house show every now and then. Our main card takes place on Saturday, Slammin' Saturday Night. Slammin' Saturday Night has 8 live matches plus 5 Dark matches. That's a total of 13 matches of pure entertainment. We write matches using the ABS format: [action in here] Sam- "commentary in here" 6. Divisions There are 3 divisions in the EWO. Heavyweight, Light- Heavyweight, and Tag Team: Heavyweight- These are the big boys of the EWO. Those that are 230 pounds or higher fight in this division. Light-Heavyweights- This division is filled with high flyers and speed demons. If you way under 230 pounds, this is the division for you. Tag Teams- A division for teams of two to face another team of two. The EWO is known for the Tag Team division. We have some of the best Tag Teams in the game. 7. Titles "What does everybody want?" TITLES! This is the gold. What you've fought all those wrestlers for. These are the things that seperate the champs from the chumps. Titles. How do you earn this prize? Work, and once you get it, you STILL have to work to keep it. Once you have one, you might as well paint a target on your back. World Championship Title- The Biggin'! If you hold this title, you rule the EWO, but only the top 10 contendors get a shot at this prize. That is, unless the holder challenges you, but don't hold your breath. They worked hard to get that title, they want you to also! It must be defended once a month. Intercontinental Title- This is the Number 2 title in the EWO. Anyone can get a shot at it, and it must be defended 3 times every 2 months. Light-Heavyweight Title- Here is the title for those in the Light-Heavyweight divison. It must be defended once every 3 weeks. Tag Team Titles- If your in the Tag Team division, these are what you're shooting for. The Tag Titles must be defended every 3 weeks. 8.
