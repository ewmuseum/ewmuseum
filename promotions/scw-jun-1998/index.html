Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A community member plugged another SCW in a single post on 19 June 1998, describing it as a new federation “run by a long time e wrestler” with two cards a week planned and a tournament for its singles and tag titles “as soon as we get enough wrestlers.”

No title, card, or wrestler is named in what survives; the promise of the tournament is the closest the record comes to attesting the federation actually ran.

That single recruiting plug is the whole of its surviving record, with no closure notice or later post to say what became of it.
