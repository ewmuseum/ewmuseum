Loaded Preview

3 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
****Coming at you this Friday Night, 7th July**** CWF Loaded- Johnny Nowhere versus "The Future" Jay Flash Rumours are swirling around the CWF that Nowhere and Flash, two exciting young talents signed within the last couple of weeks, have a history dating back to their very earliest days at school. We've all seen that Nowhere is a recluse, a social failure, a manic depressive....does Jay Flash have something to do with the reason why he has become such a loser? We may find out on Loaded but, even if we don't, we're bound to see a sensational match, as both Johnny Nowhere and Jay Flash have shown the world that they are able to combine high impact maneuvres with the most outrageous and daring feats possible in the ring.
White Knight versus "Jagged" Jack Levinsky Making his CWF debut, White Knight is famed for his ability to fly through the air during matches performing moves that a 200 pound luchadore would frown at, an amazing talent considering he's well over 300 pounds. Will we see his unique brand of dare devil offence on Loaded against Jack Levinsky?
Tune in to find out! Extreme Tag Team Match- Luther Reagan and X-Treme versus Jim Mason and Grant Ralton Three of these competitors- Reagan, X-Treme and Mason- have held the CWF
Extreme title at some point in their careers, in fact Luther Reagan holds it right at this moment...so they are going to be totally at home in this extreme environment, which pits Terminal Chaos members Reagan and X-Treme against Mason and Ralton. Ah yes, Grant Ralton. The reigning CWF
Champion....but can he cut it in such a violent matchup? Cage Match for number one contendership to the CWF Championship- Axl Larson versus Nick Cage CWF Intercontinental Champion Nick Cage and former CWF Champion Axl Larson both want their shot at Grant Ralton and the belt, so CWF owner Ryan O'Neill gave them their chance this week on Loaded....a fifteen foot high steel cage....the first man out wins the right to take on Ralton in a sixty minute
Iron Man Match for the belt at our next pay per view event. Both of these two men have proven themselves to be amongst the best technical wrestlers on the planet....but who will come out on top?
Tune in this Friday to find out!!
