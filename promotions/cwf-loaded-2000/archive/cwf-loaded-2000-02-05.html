Loaded

To join this fed, just mail [detail withheld] The usual CWF Pyrotechnic display kicks off CWF Loaded, to the delight of the thousands of fans in attendance.
Johnny Anderson: Hello everybody, and welcome to the Championship
Wrestling Federation- Loaded!
Luke Richards: E-Wrestling Zone viewers, welcome to your CWF debut, and let me promise you that tonight you are going to see it all and then some!
JA: I couldn't agree more with you on that front Johnny. The most important question in the CWF at the moment is, of course, just who is "The Phantom?"
LR: For those not in the know on this scene, there has been a man stalking the federation's superstars for some time under the guise of a masked Phantom. It's got to the point where we think he might just be on the verge of revealing himself, and God help him when he does.
JA: I think it's important to point out that this man hasn't been stalking ALL the superstars in the CWF, just the Unholy Alliance.
LR: The band formed by our former world champion, Suicidal Johnny
Flame, containing himself, Dave Bean, Melanie Rolenza and Hotrod. All four of them have been having problems with this guy for sure, and it so very nearly culminated last week on another CWF show, Cybershock!
It was Johnny Flame and Dave Bean against X-Treme and The Phantom, and just check out what happened!
Cut to footage: JA: Flame and Bean are going for the Phantom, they want his mask off! LR: Unbelievable! He throws them both to the floor.....look at the worry in the face of X-Treme!
JA: X-Treme is too shocked by this man's strength to climb the cage! LR: Remember, there are tables in this ring, two of them.....and I'm sure we're going to see them broken!
JA: Flame is the first to his feet, they are both still double teaming
The Phantom, as we have come to know him.
LR: And X-Treme has finally taken it upon himself to try and climb out of the cage.....he hasn't even thrown a punch yet....and yes, he's going to get out!
JA: He's out! X-Treme wins, and I guess that means he and The Phantom are tag team champions!
LR: No, Johnny, he said whoever went with him tonight would be a one night only- so X-Treme and Jack Leer are the champions!
JA: And look at the Phantom- he has both Johnny Flame and Dave Bean by the neck! Double chokeslam! The power on the man is unbelievable!
LR: The lights have gone off again.............. JA: Well, we at least have them back....Phantom is out of the cage....and he's got a microphone!
Phantom (Speaking with a voice synthesiser): At Fire and Fury, I'm gonna win the CWF title......whoever wins the Sky Dive Battle Royal will defend their belt against me the same night........and Unholy
Alliance, I've only just started with you!
JA: Damn! We can't tell if we know his voice, he's got one of those weird synthesisers! End footage JA: One of the shortest main events in CWF history Luke, and a chilling warning for the Unholy Alliance from The Phantom.
"Song 2" by Blur plays as fireworks light up Intercontinental champion
Kurt Jackson's path down to the ring.
LR: Kurt Jackson! What does he want? JA: I'm sure we're about to find out. Kurt Jackson: I was watching from the back last Saturday night, on
Cybershock, as a cage was lowered down into the ring and four men stepped into it to fight it out for the CWF tag team championship.....
Fans cheer wildly. Kurt Jackson: And I was forced to watch one of the most sickeningly poor matches in the history of the CWF! I mean, what did those guys think they were doing? It's obvious that whoever this Phantom guy is, he's a very powerful man, and I respect him for that. No, no, I'm out here tonight because no longer am I satisfied with being the
Championship Wrestling Federation's prestigious intercontinental champion. In my opinion, the tag team of X-Treme and Jack Leer, although talented, are not worthy of being tag team champions, and I'm calling them out here tonight to take me and my darling partner on for those belts!
LR: Darling partner? What's he talking about? "Afraid" by Motley Crue plays as The Enforcer steps out from the back with a huge grin on his face.
JA: The Enforcer? Son of a gun! Kurt Jackson: That's right! Myself and The Enforcer, the next CWF tag team champions, crowned tonight in this very ring!
LR: Hey, check out the video wall! X-Treme and Jack Leer! Jack Leer: You know what, I'm really pissed off about events from last
Cybershock. Dave Bean, you came down to the ring, you hit me with a timekeepers bell, and as a result I was unable to fight for these tag team title belts against you....but my number one, X-Treme, did fight, and you and Johnny Flame got your asses whipped! (Laughs)
LR: Crowd not getting over on this one! JA: Leer just doesn't get it, does he? These fans love Johnny Flame! Jack Leer: So, with title belts in hand, I will say this......You will fight myself and X-Treme for the tag team title tonight, on one condition.........you must take on the guy who describes himself as "The most extreme man in wrestling today," Dave Bean, in a two on one handicap match, and you must knock him out! If you can leave that man a bloody, unconcious wreck on the ring apron, then we will put the titles on the line against you....but if you can't, you can kiss your chance goodbye.
JA: Handicap match! Can Dave Bean survive both The Enforcer and Kurt
Jackson? LR: Don't be an idiot Johnny, of course he can't! What a mastermind that Jack Leer really is!
Jack Leer: And while we're talking handicap matches- as women's commisioner, I am offering Melanie Rolenza and Jesse the chance to get back the women's championship. All you two girls have to do is beat my champion Houston in another handicap match tonight, and I'll declare the title vacant, you two can go at it whenever you want.
LR: Great! Three women at once! JA: Has Leer finished? Jack Leer: So, boys, hopefully I'll be seeing you later, ha ha ha. LR: He's confident, give him that. JA: Oh he's confident all right. Complete jackass too. Kurt Jackson: Well, if we could bring attention back to the ring.....myself and The Enforcer WILL tonight lay the hurt on Dave
Bean......then you'll see who the real tag team champions in this federation are, once and for all!
LR: Well, Jackson and his newfound partner The Enforcer making their way from the ring, but first of all, we have that women's match!
JA: I can see your blood pressure rising already! "Ready To Go" by Republica plays as Melanie Rolenza come out from the back to a huge ovation, somersaulting her way into the ring.
JA: Great ovation as always for one of the most entertaining in-ring athletes in wrestling history, the great Melanie Rolenza.
LR: You know what Johnny, I don't think I've met a single fan in a long time that doesn't think Melanie Rolenza should be women's champion.
JA: Would have happened a long time ago had it not been for Jack Leer. "Live and Let Die" by Guns N Roses plays as Jesse comes out to a good reception. LR: Here's my tip for the title, power, beauty, everything you could want. JA: We shall see. "Swimsuit Issue" by Sonic Youth plays as Houston makes her way down to the ring with the womens championship in hand.
LR: Rolenza and Jesse make no bones about starting this one off Johnny....whipping their opponent into the ropes....double drop toehold! JA: Houston's face right into the canvas like a sack of potatoes! LR: Now Jesse holds Houston in place as Mel goes up top for one of her famous aerial maneuvres......and look at that! Shooting star press from the top takes Houston down!
JA: She's already struggling Luke, that's for sure. LR: Oh, I have faith. JA: Houston making a minor recovery with a quick sidewalk slam on
Jesse. LR: See, I knew it would happen this way. JA: Now she's trying to choke Jesse out....but Rolenza comes in with a kick from behind to send Houston make to the mat.
LR: Jesse lays into the champion with big kicks......Rolenza is going up top again......wow! That move was like a top rope famasser!
JA: Rolenza is one hell of an athlete, that's for sure! LR: Houston is beaten! Oh no! Hey, wait.....Jack Leer is coming down to the ring! He's come to save his champion!
JA: Damn! He's coming up into the ring! LR: Laying down a punch on Melanie Rolenza! And Jesse! Good God, that's going too far! JA: The referee calls for the bell, and Houston retains her championship! What a con artist!
LR: But still, all credit to Houston.... JA: Oh gimme a break! As it is, Houston hangs onto the strap for another show, but eventually she's going to lose it, take it from me.
LR: I doubt that very much. JA: Next up we have the handicap match between the team of Kurt Jackson and The Enforcer against Dave Bean! LR: Well, Jackson and The Enforcer are coming out to Kurt's own "Song
2" and that great entrance, but I bet Dave Bean is really terrified backstage!
JA: Here comes Dave Bean to face the music. "Still D.R.E" by Doctor Dre plays as Dave Bean comes out from the back to a huge ovation. JA: Bean runs straight into the ring, and this match is on. Dave gets the upperhand immediatly with a drop toe hold on The Enforcer, but Kurt
Jackson starts laying those kicks in Dave Bean.
LR: He's our extreme champion, but that counts for nothing here. Look at Jackson holding him up while Enforcer buries those punches deep into
Bean's chest!
JA: That's tough, but Bean can take anything and get up for more. Kurt Jackson wants to lock Bean up...does.....Fisherman's suplex! LR: Screams of pain from Bean! JA: Enfocer putting on a figure four leglock.......Bean finds the ropes! LR: Now Dave Bean slides out of the ring onto the apron....Enforcer gets out there...DDT onto the ring apron! These two are tearing Bean apart!
JA: And look at the video wall Luke, Jack Leer is enjoying it! But X-
Treme, X-Treme doesn't look so overjoyed!
LR: What, you think that Dave Bean and X-Treme respect each other? X-
Treme must be loving this.
JA: I'm not so sure. As it is, Bean is making a slight comeback with an assault on Kurt Jackson....Enforcer grabs him from behind......fishermans suplex through our table! God lord!
LR: Bean has taken two massive suplexes in this match already! And now he's sitting there almost dead in a pile that used to be our announce table!
JA: Jackson and The Enforcer are both going up to the turnbuckle....surely they are not going to do this....Yes!
LR: They both threw themselves from the turnbuckle onto Dave Bean, onto our announce table, and Bean is bleeding!
JA: The referee might well want this match stopped now before Bean takes severe damage. LR: If he hasn't already, that is. JA: Jackson now drags his prone opponent back into the ring......and drops him with a double underhook suplex!
LR: Enforcer hauls him up by his head.......drop kicks him back down! JA: The pair must now be thinking about covering and winning their tag team titles.....but what? What's happening on the apron?
LR: Somebodies coming down to the ring? JA: I don't have a clue Luke, but he's hitting the ring! Attacking
The Enforcer! Punches him to the floor!
LR: Evenflow DDT on Kurt Jackson! Dave Bean's guardian angel!! JA: But who the hell is he? LR: Wait just a second.....Jack Leer is hitting the ring again! Laying down more into Dave Bean! But this guy puts an Evenflow DDT on him! Leer, Jackson and The Enforcer all laid out, Dave Bean in trouble, but who is this man?
JA: Well, I don't know, we don't know, but for now we won't know, because he's leaving the ring just as quickly as he entered it!
LR: What a match! But the thick of it is, Jackson and The Enforcer do not get to fight it out for the tag team championship!
JA: Oh, poor them. I'm hearing that Kevin Richardson has CWF champion X-Treme in the back! Kevin Richardson: Thanks Johnny. I'm here with X-Treme, who I hear has some news for us tonight.
X-Treme: Damn right Kevin. I've got a message for Jack Leer, that sleazy women's commisioner who just keeps on using and abusing people as if they are the dizzy bimbo's who he surrounds himself with all the time. I've been with this guy for some time, and we made a great tag team, but let me tell you something, the time has come for us to go our seperate ways.
KR: Seperate ways? X-Treme: That's right. How can I hang around with a man who enjoys.....takes great pleasure.....in sending Dave Bean to his professional death against Kurt Jackson and The Enforcer? He loved it, sitting there in the back, laughing and joking. But I have a conscience. This federation means the world to me, I am CWF champion and the only way I am going to prove myself to the fans, and to the people in the back who think I'm an unworthy champion, is to always do the right thing. Wrestling has pretty much abandoned it's morals as far as I can make out, and it's people like Jack Leer who are dragging it down further and further. I've been around this guy for so long that I now have my own favourite girls, and I'm grateful for the time
I've had, but it's time to step out from the back and do the right thing.....and that thing is to end all ties with Jack Leer. The tag team titles are now vacant, anyone who wants them, come and get them.
KR: It's over? X-Treme: It's over, and now I have to concentrate on defending this prestigious CWF championship. Okay, that's really all I want to say.
KR: Thanks. Back to you Luke. LR: So, it seems that Jack Leer has finally gone too far! JA: Indeed it would seem that way! Good for X-Treme! LR: Something else is coming up on the video wall.....it's Joe Jordan? JA: And he's perhaps the most unpopular figure in the industry right about now....listen to that chant.
LR: Don't you idiots call him an asshole! Joe Jordan: Come on you ponce, get my bag. Chauffeur: Yes sir. Joe Jordan: Now wait out here till I get back. You got that? Chauffeur: Yes....yes, sir. Joe snatches the bag and walks into the building, to be met by the officials. Official: Hey there. I gonna have to see some I.D. Joe Jordan: Don't you know who I am! Are you blind? Official 2: Look, we just need to have some I.D. shown. Please, sir. Joe Jordan: Okay, I'll just get it outta my bag. LR: Jordan! He's pulling out a crowbar! Smack over their heads! That's the way to do it! JA: That guy has lost it, mad I tell you! LR: He's walking right towards Johnny Flame's locker room! JA: Oh no! Joe knocks on the door and gets a parcel out of his bag. Johnny Flame: Who is it? Joe Jordan: Jordan....Joe Jordan. Johnny Flame: Get outta here you little freak! Joe Jordan: No, don't be like that, I'm not going to hurt you. I've brought you a present, you know, a peace offering.
Flame opens the door and Joe passes him the parcel. Joe Jordan: See, nothin' to be scared of, just a friendly present. Johnny Flame: Well what is it? Joe Jordan: You'll find out......seeya later buddy. Johnny Flame: Yeah....thanks. JA: What the hell was that he gave him? LR: Has Joe turned over a new leaf? The camera shows Flame opening the parcel. It's a fluffy teddy bear. LR: Oh, isn't it cute! Johnny Flame: What? What the hell? LR: He's chucking it in the bin! There's just no pleasing some people! JA: Why give him a teddy? Is he taking the piss? LR: Hey, I think Joe is coming down to the ring! "Afrika Shox" by Leftfield plays as Joe walks down into the ring. It seems he's gone to the trash and picked the teddy out, carrying it by his side. The fans chant "Asshole".
Joe Jordan: I don't get this. I try to make a peace offering with
Johnny Flame but all he does is discard my nice little fuzzy teddy bear, throw it in the trash! Why? It's all of you losers forcing him into this mentality, isn't it!
LR: Yeah! Losers! Joe Jordan: Well all you bastards in the crowd can kiss my ass! JA: Oh come on Joe! Joe Jordan: Now, I don't want any shit going down today. Flame, just come down here, collect your ever so gracious gift and go. I would be very grateful.
Glass smashes...."No Joke" by Limp Bizkit plays and the crowd go wild. JA: My God you should listen to this ovation! Flame steps onto the entrance ramp with a microphone. Johnny Flame: Now Jo, I know that you mean well......but I don't want your old baby toys man. It's just not me!
Joe Jordan: Why? This really hurts me Johnny. I want to be your friend! Johnny Flame: How about I ask the thousands in attendance tonight if, after all the crap you've put me through, I should be your friend?
Boo's resound throughout the arena. Flame smiles. Joe Jordan: Why do you live for these idiots Johnny? Just take the teddy and I'll leave the building!
Johnny Flame: And how can I trust you? Knowing Joe Jordan, this is all some lousy trick! Joe Jordan: Ok......Hey, you, take this and give it Johnny. Jordan gives the teddy to a stagehand. He takes it to Flame. Joe Jordan: There we go, nothing to be worried about. Now you can just go to your locker room and I'll leave.
JA: I think Flame is a little puzzled at this display to be honest. LR: Jordan is leaving the arena as promised.......but they won't shake hands yet! JA; Flame is coming into the ring! The fans are going wild....aniticpating his words.... "Smells Like Teen Spirit" plays as CWF president Ryan O'Neill steps out from the back with a microphone.
Ryan O'Neill: Johnny, Johnny....before you even start..I have a propostion for you tonight. You want to get your hands on the mystical "Phantom" of the CWF, right?
Fans cheer. Ryan O'Neill: Well, I'm going to give you a one off opportunity to get one over on this guy once and for all.....Phantom, get your ass out here right now, I'm gonna ref it, you versus Johnny Flame, if you got the balls to do it!
The lights go out on the arena, as The Phantom comes down to the ring. The lights go on, without any music or lighting, with the Phantom already in the ring, attacking Flame. The bear falls out of the ring.
JA: And we have a match up! These two trading punches....Flame gets the upperhand and sends this masked man to the ropes.....and straight into a side backbreaker! What strength from Johnny Flame, who wants to be the first ever two time CWF champion!
LR: Phantom gets straight up and rolls Flame into the corner.....impressive armdrag floors Johnny Flame.......and now the
Phantom, whoever he may be, increasing the pain with an elevated crab!
JA: Flame gets to the ropes....Ryan O'Neill now in the ring to officiate the match, forces the Phantom to release the hold...Flame gets a drop toe hold!
LR: Boots to the face of The Phantom! JA: Phantom with a powerbomb out of the ring! Flame hit the mat with a bang! LR: Now The Phantom looks for a plancha...but Flame gets out of the way! The Phantom hit the steel railings!
JA: Flame is trying to prise the mask off! But The Phantom is able to get him away.....and then throws Flame into the crowd!
LR: The pair of them now brawling into the crowd! Trading punches as they move through the crowd.......somebody passes Johnny Flame a bottle!
JA: Smash! Right over the Phantom's head! He makes his way back down to the ring, more than enough of that for him thank you very much!
LR: Flame comes down to the ring again as well...Phantom lets him through the ropes, ties him up......Samoan drop! The strength on this mystery wrestler is unreal!
JA: But Flame has got back up! Rolls Phantom into the rope......flying clothesline! Now he looks to go up top.........but the Phantom is up....has Flame by the neck.........top rope chokeslam!
Flame is bleeding! LR: Phantom himself goes up top.....jumps......Flame got a boot to his crotch! Johnny applies a reverse chinlock, trying to yank that mask off at the same time! But he can't get it off!
JA: Phantom pushes him away with authority.......overhead belly to belly from the Phantom......he places Flame on the top rope......overhead belly to belly again!
LR: Flame is beginning to fall behind in this match, you can see the look of worry in president Ryan O'Neill's eyes.
JA: Flame gets to his feet, but how concious is he of his actions....he's just walked straight into an inverted piledriver!
That's got to be lights out! LR: Phantom pulls Johnny Flame, perhaps the biggest franchise in the federation, to ring centre, and now puts on at anklelock submission hold......what! Somehow Flame got to the ropes!
JA: He hits the Phantom with a four punch combination......gutwrench powerbomb! Johnny Flame back in the hunt! Phantom goes for a powerbomb..........Flame reverses! Gets out of it and counters with a vertical suplex! Phantom is losing it!
LR: Well that's unbelievable! JA: Fans get behind Johnny Flame as he goes on the offensive.......axhandle smash! Flame goes up top...........kamikazi headbutt!
LR: Stepover facelock from Flame.........is the Phantom going to have to submit and let Flame rip his mask off....no! He threw him off!
JA: Flame is exhausted....whipped into the ropes by The Phantom.......he has Flame by the neck.......chokeslam over the top rope!! Wait a second! Fury, and I'm sure we'll here a lot more about all tonight's developments. Until then, goodnight!
