Black Inferno — Press Release

17 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
For the Commisionship of the CWF Women's Division: Jack Leer versus "Jagged" Jack Levinsky- Ever since "Jagged" Jack Levinsky won the hearts of the Championship Wrestling Federations two resident sex addicts, Houston and Melanie Rolenza, the big man has been at loggerheads with CWF Women's Commisioner Jack Leer.
Jack thought that he had assembled an era of womens competition that would ensure entertaining T and A filled battles every week on CWF Loaded, but
Jack was only to happy to squash his plans. At first the two had a tolerable relationship, but then Melanie and Houston began pulling out of events and generally disrupting the division to spend time with Jack, much to the chagrin of Mr Leer. To add to the two's hostilities, Jack is of course the brother of CWF Women's Champion Jenna Levinsky-Ralton, the only woman here in the CWF who outrightly refuses to obey Leer's commands.
Therefore, things got physical as Leer's attempts to end Jenna's reign as womens champion were distrupted by Levinsky, and before long the two had set up this match, with Jack Leer's position as CWF Women's Commisioner on the line. Both of these men know how to entertain the fans in the ring, but expect this one to be more of an extreme brawl than anything else...after all, neither of them is going to be holding anything back after recent events....
CWF Intercontinental Championship Tournament Semi-Finals: "Suicidal" Johnny Flame versus White Knight- Since Nick Cage surrended the CWF Intercontinental Championship to pursue his dream of landing the big one, the CWF World belt, there have been many wrestlers looking to get their hands on the Intercontinental strap in any way they can. X-Treme, Joe Jordan and current CWF Champion Grant Ralton all won the Intercontinental title before hitting the jackpot at world level, and the four competitors who have made it this far in the CWF
Intercontinental Championship tournament know that winning the belt will be the first step towards making a name for themselves in the history books.
"Suicidal" Johnny Flame was, of course, the first ever CWF Champion, winning a titanic and legendary battle with Dave Bean at our very first Pay Per View event, Halloween Extreme, last October. Flame has the pedigree, but in the shape of White Knight, he also has a man who will be more than capable of defeating him in the ring. Expect this one to be fast, furious and painful for both competitors, and the winner? It's impossible to even make a prediction...
Universal Warrior versus Johnny Nowhere- Johnny Nowhere, a name already very much in the hearts of the CWF fans. In just a few short weeks here, he has put it all on the line in his matches, pulling off victories over some big name opponents, and winning his shot at the Intercontinental belt..although he'll have to go through the powerhouse that is Universal Warrior, who will make his CWF debut at the event. Is the in-ring technical genius and vast array of moves possesed by Johnny Nowhere enough to beat this huge competitor?
CWF Women's Championship Match: Jenna Levinsky-Ralton (c) versus Houston
(Naked Match)- Women's wrestling, CWF style!! Houston has never had any qualms about taking of all of her clothes, be it for a wrestling match or otherwise, but this will be our first chance to see Jenna Levinsky-Ralton in her birthday suit...in fact, Jenna has always seen herself as an advocate for feminism, outrightly refusing to adhere to CWF Women's Commisioner Jack Leer's somewhat sexist rules...until now...at husband Grant Raltons request, she will disrobe and put it ALL on the line in this match, her CWF Championship at stake, against a determined Houston. If you are a red blooded male, reach for the telephone now and order this pay per view!
For the rights to the Championship Wrestling Federation: Ryan O'Neill versus Joe Levinsky-
Who own the CWF? Is it the founding father, the man who built the empire from the ground up, Ryan O'Neill...or is it Joe Levinsky, the multi-millionare owner of the UBC network, who believes that he has it in writing that O'Neill sold the CWF over to him? O'Neill has never denied for a second the fact that he accepted money from Joe to save the CWF from extinction, in exchange for Joe's 49% stake in the company...but when
Levinsky wanted to move the CWF onto his UBC network, O'Neill made his stand, outrightly refusing to allow the move to take place. Joe, father of
Jenna and Jack Levinsky, two highly rated CWF competitors, got violent, even going so far as to have O'Neill kidnapped and drugged into signing away the
CWF deal. Ryan wasn't too happy, to say the least, and he and Joe have agreed to settle the rights for the number one wrestling show on television- in a wrestling ring. In his younger days, Joe was quite a talent, and we all know Ryan O'Neill's pedigree...one half of the one time IWO
Intercontinental tag team champions, the O'Neill brothers, just one of his many achievements...but we also know that it was a crushing back injury, from which Ryan has only just fully recovered, that led to his retirment from the squared circle, and to his founding of the CWF....so can he cut it?
With the rights to the company he loves at stake, expect him to put it all on the line!! CWF Extreme Championship Match: Luther Reagan (c) versus X-Treme- The two toughest bastards in wrestling history, plain and simple. They went at it at our last pay per view event, Nightmare's Revenge, with Reagan shocking the world by beating X-Treme for the belt, a feat nobody thought he or anybody else in the business could achieve. The two went on to become good friends and fellow Terminal Chaos members, establishing a fearsome reputation, but Reagan always knew that his new buddy would one day knock on his door and ask for a title shot...and he's only too happy to oblige.
Expect to see hardcore taken to a new level in this match, as we all know that X-Treme has no respect for the body of himself or anybody else, and
Luther Reagan....well, Reagan was extreme enough to take the belt off him in the first place, and that speaks volumes in itself......
CWF Intercontinental Championship Tournament Final- Four men have made it as far as this pay per view, and by this stage only two will remain...."Suicidal" Johnny Flame takes on White Knight and the
Universal Warrior faces Johnny Nowhere, the possibilities concerning who will make it to this final match are many and it's impossible to predict just what will happen....we have seen Johnny Nowhere and White Knight electrify the arena with their displays in recent weeks, and the pedigree of
Flame and the Universal Warrior cannot be discredited.....but, whoever makes it this far, they will know that becoming CWF Intercontinental Champion is not so much reaching the top of one mountain...but taking the first step towards the big one, the world title. Over half of the men who have been
CWF Intercontinental Champion have gone on to win the world belt....which of these four men will be victorious here and join that prestigious list?
Axl Larson versus Jim Mason- Axl Larson, perhaps the most popular performer in CWF history, was beaten by
Grant Ralton for his world title in the historic Four Way Hell In A Cell match at our last pay per view extravaganza, CWF: Nightmare's Revenge.
Since then, he has been working hard as hell for a rematch, but Grant Ralton has always dodged his challenge...however, it always seemed clear that it would be Larson who would get his shot at the Ralton at this pay per view....until Nick Cage threw down the Intercontinental belt and demanded
HIS opportunity to become world champion....CWF Owner Ryan O'Neill recognised Cage's talent, and gave him his chance to wrestle the number one contenders slot away from Larson, which he did in spectacular fashion in a best of three falls match just a week ago....where does that leave Larson?
Quite simply put, Axl has been put in this match by CWF Champion Grant
Ralton simply for Ralton's own amusement...if Larson wins, he becomes number one contender, no questions asked, for next months Pay Per View....but should he lose the match, to former CWF Extreme Champion Jim
Mason.....should he lose this match, Ralton will be able to demote Larson to the bottom of the ladder, forcing the defomation of Terminal Chaos, the
Larson-led group of wrestlers resisting Ralton and his Dynasty. Axl knows that one mistake could cost him his position as one of the top dogs in the
CWF....so expect to see him on top form against Jim Mason!
60 Minute Iron Man Match for the CWF Championship- Grant Ralton (c) versus
Nick Cage Cage and Ralton participated in one of the greatest feuds in CWF history, both in the ring and on the microphone, for the Intercontinental
Championship belt just a few short months ago. Despite the fact that Nick
Cage eventually beat Ralton for that strap, it was Grant who was able to snake his way up the ladder to world title status, winning the big one from
Axl Larson at our last Pay Per View event. Nick Cage watched, waiting for his opportunity, asserting himself as by far the number one man at his level. But his level, Intercontinental level, was not enough for him. He threw down his belt, proved his worth by beating Axl Larson to take the number one contendership to him, and gave Ralton the challenge of a 60 minute Iron Man match, to finally settle the issue between them as to just who is the greatest technical wrestler in the CWF. This will be an amazing match, for both purists and casual fans alike....don't miss it!
