30:00 Johnny Anderson: Ladies and gentlemen, may I, Johnny Anderson, welcome you to the Championship Wrestling Federation, and invite you to join us on the wild ride that tonight will be, CWF: Black Inferno!! You remember our last extravanganza, right? If you ordered that pay per view event, it will still be as fresh in your memory as it was that night you saw it, if you didn't order it.......ha, well, I would advise you NOT to make that mistake twice!!
The main event, the first ever four way Hell In A Cell match in professional wrestling history, brought together Jack Levinsky, Luther Reagan, Grant
Ralton and CWF Champion Axl Larson for the big one, the CWF World
Title....and the results were nothing short of astounding!! We saw Larson thrown THROUGH the cell by Grant Ralton, Luther Reagan climb up the underside only to have his hand STAMPED ON by Axl Larson on top of the cell, sending him fifteen feet to the canvas...those four men put it all on the line in that match, which many have called the greatest of all time, and of course, nobody in their right mind would have called it any less that a big juicy five stars....however, tonight...tonight, we have a different proposition for you altogether.....Grant Ralton was the man who rose from nowhere to win the CWF title at our last event, Nightmare's
Revenge....tonight, he puts that title on the line in what will surely be far more a test of his skill and character than the legendary Hell In A Cell encounter....he takes on the man many feel is THE NUMBER ONE technical wrestler in the CWF..if not the world......in the shape of Nick Cage, who appeared on talk show host Jim Rome's "The Last Word" on the Fox Sports
Network last week....

Jim Rome - Tonight, sitting next to me is a man who is one of the most outspoken professional wrestlers in the business today. Jim Rome - Now some of your fellow workers have criticized that sort of opinion for years. I thought it was particularly funny how recently, you basically cursed out your boss on national television. The bottom line is that I'm just not afraid to stand up for myself. Jim Rome - Here's another question. For years, and this was often brought up by Minnesota Governor, Jesse Ventura, what about a union? Do you think that unionization of wrestlers would serve really any purpose in the world of professional wrestling?
Nick Cage - Well, I'm not so sure about that. With a union, everyone would have a base pay that spread across the board. And that would really take out the reward that those of us with a lot of talent. And I'm not just talking ring skills, but those of us who bring the crowd out to the arena, and those of us who make the crowd pop while we are out there...
[Nick Cage pauses to think for a brief second.] Nick Cage - The reward of bringing home a big paycheck would be gone because we would be making just as much as the guys on the bottom. I love this business just as much as the next guy. I live, eat, and breathe professional wrestling. And I think that I deserve to be rewarded for that. Don't you?
Jim Rome - Well yes, I can see your point with that. You know, for years,
Jesse Ventura always rallied in the business for a union. And there were always rumors of guys like Hulk Hogan, who went against the union idea because it is believed that he believed that he would be making a lot less money than what he made. Is your opinion really based on the fact that you don't like the idea of a union or are you just afraid that a union will cut down your paycheck a lot.
Nick Cage - Don't get me wrong. I see the benefits of a union. Health care, retirement plans, and all that. It is great. But if wrestling becomes unionized then the reward for the talented is gone. The incentive for being
GOOD, is gone. The money? The money isn't really the issue. I'd do what I do for half the money that I make now, and I'd still be happy. But there just has got to be a reward. This may sound bad, but it is true. Money serves as a motivation. And it is a reward for doing good things. Anyone else who tells you otherwise is joking themselves...
You know, if you want to see the talent pool in professional wrestling dry up REAL quickly, then start a union. But if you want to keep mostly everyone happy, especially if you are a promoter who wants to keep everyone happy...keep paying the crowd drawers the big money because if they go away, so does the business as a whole.
Jim Rome - Alright, my guest is Nick Cage, and you are watching "The Final
Word" on the Fox Sports Network. We will be back after this break!

Nick Cage - Hey, I had my spot earned before that... Jim Rome - And well, this weekend, you head into Black Inferno against Grant
Ralton for the CWF World Heavyweight Title. How does it feel to be headed to the main event at the pay per view?
Nick Cage - Pretty good. I've spent about ten hours a day this past week, since Loaded, training. I'm really working my butt off for this one. I really expect to have people talking about this match for a long, long time.
I've been in this position before. So I'm no stranger to working a big main event. But this one is especially important because I'm really looking forward to this opportunity.
Jim Rome - Against, Grant Ralton for the World Heavyweight Title, right? Nick Cage - Exactly. We've butted heads before. We've verbally tore eachother apart for months. But this one...this is the big one between me and him. Not much else matters when you are going for the World Heavyweight
Championship and we both know that.
Jim Rome - What are your feelings on Grant Ralton? What do you think about him? Nick Cage - Oh geez, I try not to. Well, Ralton is a great athlete. There is no doubt about that one. But, on a personal level, I don't much care for him, but I can respect his talents for what he does.
Jim Rome - Yes, Ralton is quite an athlete, no doubt about it. Well, Nick
Cage, I want to thank you for being my guest...
Nick Cage - Thank you! Jim Rome - Ladies and gentlemen, the pay-per-view is called Black Inferno, it airs this Saturday night, and please call your cable company for more information! Nick, thanks again! We will be right back!
