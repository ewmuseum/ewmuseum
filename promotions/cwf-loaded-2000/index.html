Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Championship Wrestling Federation opened in the autumn of 1999 as one more small e-mail-and-newsgroup promotion, its roster built from original ring personas rather than any real-world company's own names. By the start of 2000 it had settled into a weekly broadcast called Loaded, carried by a two-man announce team, with a hardcore division running alongside the main roster and a masked stalker haunting a faction of the champion's own circle.

February's run built that mystery toward a first pay-per-view, Fire and Fury, staged as the masked man's own promised confrontation with the champion. A company-ownership storyline followed through the spring and into June's Nightmares Revenge, a card built around a four-way steel-cage match for the top title and a first-blood grudge match settling one of the roster's own long-running feuds.

By midsummer the promotion had split its roster into two rival factions, one loyal to the sitting champion and one resisting him, staging that rivalry across a third pay-per-view, Black Inferno, built around a tournament for the vacated intercontinental title and a match to decide who legally controlled the company itself. Weekly Loaded broadcasts continued through the summer months.

The founder left the presidency before the year was out, and a successor took over the company's day-to-day running, working from a members list an earlier accident had scattered. Under his name the promotion returned to the newsgroup in the final week of December with a five-part Loaded broadcast, its own future beyond the turn of the year not recorded here.
