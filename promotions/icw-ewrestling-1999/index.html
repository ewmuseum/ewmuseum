Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A federation under the bare initials ICW dates its own founding to 12 September 1999, per a first-anniversary recruiting post published a year and a day later. Through the spring of 2000 its own recruiting notices describe a running two-cards-a-week, one-pay-per-view-a-month schedule, seeking midcarders, tag teams, a commissioner, and a card writer to round out an existing roster.

The promotion's own tag team championship belonged, by June 2000, to a team billed as the Amish Rake Fighters, defended against newsgroup-recruited competition; its own signature match stipulation, the Belt in a Bag on a Slippery Pole match, and later an escalated Double Belt in a Bag Slippery Pole Weapons Match, were advertised by name across its recruiting posts through the autumn. A results notice the same summer billed the promotion, without further elaboration, as "the fed that killed Hank Brimstone."

In mid-August 2000 the promotion's own operator announced a shutdown, citing an inability to keep card writers, only to reopen within a few weeks under the same banner and the same web address, seeking four or five new wrestlers to round back out the roster.

Its own one-year anniversary post, 13 September 2000, frames the promotion as heading into "year 2," inviting midcarders and main eventers alike and describing plans to rebuild a hardcore division. No later document survives.

The bare ICW initials were shared across the newsgroup by more than a dozen contemporaries and predecessors separately attested elsewhere in this registry; no document here links this federation to any of them.
