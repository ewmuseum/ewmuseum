Check out the IEW!!!

Apr 06, 2000, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello there, this is Vinnie Romano, you may know me as The Devastator, 'Superstar' Steve Davidson, or "The Falcone" Anthony Falcone. You may have fought one of my characters in the HWA, WWA, EPWA, NAWA, NAWF, EnWa, ACE, the
UWA, what have you. But this time, instead of wrestling, I am opening up yet another E-Fed.
This new promotion will be called the IEW, International Extreme Wrestling. I have experience in running a fed, so this will go as smoothly as possible for all of you.
So, at least give us a look, you never know..here's the address http://members.tripod.com/IEWrestling/index.htm Please consider joining...you will not be disappointed. If you don't roleplay anymore...the front office has many vacant positions!
