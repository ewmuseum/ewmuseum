Info 8) Interested? -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------- 1) Introduction -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------- I would like to first off welcome you all to a great opportunity for anyone that is truly interested in E-Wrestling. The IEW has a great, hard-working staff that dedicates a lot of its time for the promotion. Yes..some of you that received this handbook may remember the IEW from a half a year ago. I had very little dedication in both staff and wrestlers. But this time will be different. I have found a great staff and we are all looking forward to making the IEW as enjoyable as possible. The IEW is not limited to just the best role-players or just to weak role-players...what we're looking for is people who want to be doing this and will put some time and care into their role-plays. We're not asking for somebody that will spend 6 hours on a role-play, 5 time a week. The IEW will have House Shows, two weekly cards,
Pay-Per-Views, and Interfed Pay-Per-Views. We look to have a varied level of skill in the promotion. The talent will be spread out in the IEW, with titles for wrestlers of different ability. We are an equal opportunity promotion.
You will never be cheated by our staff....We will listen to any questions, comments, or concerns you have. The IEW will be an excellent experience for those who join with us. You will improve your role-playing skills, meet new friends, and have fun. By the way....we only allow fake wrestlers!
Role-playing will be done through e-mail. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------- 2) IEW Staff -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---------- Real IEW Owner/President: Vinnie Romano - Vinnie has been a part of E-Wrestling for over four years now...going on five. He has been in previous feds such as ACE
Wrestling, EPWA, EnWa, WWA, ICW 2000, NAWF, X-WW, EHW, HWA, UWA, NAWA, and UWA.
Currently, he role-plays with his wrestler, "The Falcon" Anthony Falcone, but he used to handle characters such as The Devastator and 'Superstar' Steve
Davidson. His experience in running a fed is also good. After the Owner of the EPWA stepped down, Vinnie was appointed the new President of the promotion and ran it until its downfall 5 months later. He is also a very active member of the UWA staff, writing results, booking cards, etc. In April of
2000...Vinnie had a plan to open the IEW to the e-fed world; however, that mission fell short due to lack of participation on both ends of the stick.
Half a year later, Vinnie is ready to try once more, positive that the IEW will be one awesome fed.

(Heel) Referees: Paul Mitchell (Head Referee) - Jack Holt - Nick Donahue - Adam Sorrel - Kurt Fitz - Eddie Bailey - Stan Dicks - Lloyd Baxter - Tito King -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------- 3) Rules -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------- 1.  You can have 1 fake wrestler or 1 fake tag team with 1 manager.  A tag team will be strictly tag team in the beginning.  After we get started, you can go out for singles, but if you join as a tag team, you are primarily a tag team.
 If you are a singles wrestler, you can team-up with another member of the fed and go for team and singles titles.  No rip offs like "Stone Cold" Bob or
"Heartbreak" Bill.  The name of the game is originality.
2. No TOSing any member or me of the fed!  If you do, I don't care if your the World Champ, your fired!  People join this fed to have fun and do some role-playing, not to get there AOL account terminated because they used foul language in their Role-play.
3. All foul language is permitted, but if your on a TV show or something, keep it real and censor it out like f[beep]k or sh*t.  Please don't get out of control though. 4. No racist remarks whatsoever.  No one knows if your opponent in real life is black, white, Hispanic, so don't say anything your not sure of.   If you let one slip I won't be mad, but, don't do it all the time.
5. No spamming throughout the fed anything.  Whether your selling your Playstation or advertising your fed, don't do it.   I'll probably make this fed
BCC anyway.  I think it's easier.  E-mail me your advertisement and I will put it in the commercials I will have on the cards.
6. If you have a problem with something, either E-mail me or put a little OOC (Out Of Character) Note before or after your Role-play. 7. Do not attack me or any other wrestler that isn't yours in your Role-plays. Don't use me or anyone else without their permission.  If you do use someone in an RP without there permission, I will delete the Role-play and it will not count in your match.  You must E-Mail that person, and myself.  If that person agrees, I must know about it.
8. No stealing anyone's pics, macros, disclaimers, or anything else.  If you have there permission then it is fine with me, but, you should give some credit underneath whatever you take to the person who let you use it.
9. We allow any gimmicks as you want here in the IEW, But don't use real wrestlers sayings or alter them and use it.   Example, DON'T use "That's the bottom line because Blaze said so!"
10. If you lose, you lose. Don't whine about it.  Simply E-mail me, in a respectable manner, and tell me about it.  I will not screw anybody. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------- 4) Salary System (Rankings) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------- The rankings will be decided by the salary system...as follows: All wrestlers start at 5,000 $7,000 - Winning World Title $5,000 - Winning North Eastern, Hardcore, Cruiserweight, or Tag Team Titles $1,000 - Successful World Title Defense $800 - Successful North Eastern, Hardcore, Cruiserweight or Tag Title Defense $500 - Wrestle In PPV Main Event $500 - Winning Match (Pin, Submission, Knockout) $400 - Winning Match (DQ, Count-out) $300 - Win by No- Show (Opponent Doesn't Show Up) $200 - Draw $150 - Wrestle In Extreme Main Event $100 - Every match wrestled in -$100 - Lose Match (DQ, Count-out) -$200 - Lose Match (Pin, Submission, Knockout) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------- 5) Card Information -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------- IEW Presents: Tuesday Night Extreme--   The commentary is done by Jamie Walsh,
Mike Cooper, and Dan Campbell with guests from the outside world every now and then.  There are commercials, so if you join you can make one and send it to me.  It can be for your wrestlers merchandise, it can be like a stable commercial or it can advertise your fed.  If you want your wrestler to do an
Interview with our interviewer. It will count as a Role-play.  The results will be nice and big.  There will be about 8 matches on this card.
IEW Presents: Saturday Night Massacre -- The commentary is done by Craig Smith and Matt Rodgers. This is a smaller card, kind of like a mini-Thunder or
Smackdown.  Rare title defenses.  Only about 4 matches. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------- 6) Title Information -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------- The IEW World Heavyweight Title: This is the biggest and best title of the federation.  Whoever wins this will have had to put in a lot of hours and a lot of hard work.  It can be defended in an Extreme match, although, it does not have to be defended in an extreme match.  This title is truly for the only
ELITE wrestlers of the IEW.  It's holder will have to defend it at least on two regular cards a month and most PPV.  Look for this wrestler to have a future in the UWA if he defends the title successfully.
The IEW Hard-core Title: This title is a highly respected title, for it belongs to the craziest, most HARD-CORE, S.o.B. in the federation.  It is only defended is Very Hard-core matches.  Either I or the Champion chooses the stipulations in the matches.  It is defended at least three times a month at regular cards, along with every PPV.  May be the next HaRdCoRe Icon of the UWA.
The IEW Cruiserweight Title: This is the title where the men under 235 pounds really show their stuff.  This title holder is the to Cruiserweight in the IEW and is not to be messed with.  Considering that this is an Extreme Fed, this title may or may not be defended in an Extreme Match.  It is defended at least three times a month at regular cards along with every PPV.  If he can prove himself worthy, this Cruiserweight may one day make it to the UWA.
The IEW Tag Team Titles: These pair of titles is for the two men who can work together the best. They click when it comes time to wrestle.  These are really the second best titles you can have, because in order to achieve these, you gotta work your butt off.  It can be defended in an Extreme match, although, it does not have to be defended in an extreme match.  They are defended at least three times a month on regular cards and every PPV.  Look for these two wrestlers to have a future in the UWA if they defend their titles successfully.
