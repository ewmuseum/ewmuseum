EGWF: SATURDAY NIGHT

8 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Saturday Night March 8 1997 We are somewhere in some woods. We seem to be seeing from the perspective of a man exhausted, running, perhaps fearing for his life. All of a sudden the forest in front on him explodes and he screams as we see his point of view fall to the ground. We look to a blood red sky and hear,"ARMEGEDON IS COMING....YOU WILL NOT SURVIVE!!!") (We are then in the arena of tonights card. We see our usual hosts, Jake and Brad) Jake: Thats right, Armegedon is coming! The first ppv in the EGWF,and it will be a ppv only like the EGWF can put on! So how about tonights matches everyone? We have 2 titles up for grabs tonight, as well as a friend vs. friend match! We regret to inform you all that The Eliminators vs. The Deranged Ones match will not go on tonight, Both of their flights were post ponned due to a severe winter storm in Maine where they were supposed to fly from. That match will be rescheduled soon. But now, on with tonights card, Brad, what match are you looking forward to? Brad: Me??? Hmmm.... Specifically the Ares vs. Senator Johnson match for the Rocky Mountain Title. That looks to be one hell of a match. Also the Blaro vs. G-Funk match. Friend vs. Friend, I tell you, something is bound to come out of that match!!! Jake: Brad, No kidding buddy. But lets get to our first match, Rainman vs. Justice Served. If Rainman wins, J.S. will be suspended for 1 week, if Justice wins, he gets Miss Liberty back. Lets get to the match.... Rainman vs. Justice Served They lock up. Rainman executes a side headlock, but Justice throws him into the ropes. Rainman nails a clothesline. Rainman runs into the ropes, and is caught in a powerslam. 1, 2, kickout. Justice climbs the top rope. Justice jumps off with a flying elbowdrop, but Rainman rolls away. Justice has hurt his elbow. It looks like it has a slight spasm. Rainman runs at Justice... Rainman jumps and nails a savate kick. Justice falls out of the ring. Rainman jumps on him with a springboard dropkick. Justice falls into the guardrail, where a fan's soda gets hit by Justice's elbow that is spasming.The fan punches Justice in the face. Justice falls, and Rainman kicks him in the elbow. Rainman pulls him up and throws him in the ring. Rainman covers... 1, 2, kickout... Rainman climbs the top rope. Justice is getting up, and Rainman is letting him... Justice's elbow is spasming really bad. He elbows the ref in the head! The ref is out cold... Justice throws Rainman off, but Rainman catches him with a victroy roll! Rainman calls for another ref... 1, 2, he kicks out because it took so long for the other ref. Rainman is mad at the official.... Justice gets up and elbows (by accident) Rainman in the back. Rainman kicks him in the nuts with a backward kick. Rainman wakes up the other ref and the second ref leaves. Rainman executes a piledriver and covers. 1...2...kickout. Rainman pulls him up... Justice's elbow hits the ref AGAIN, this time in the chest area.... The ref calls for a DQ!!! Ladies and Gentleman,The winner, as a result of a disqualification, RAINMAN!!! JusticeServed is hereby SUSPENDED from the federation for a week and Rainman gets to keep Miss Liberty! Jake: OH NO, He gets suspended on a dq!!! Oh man!!! Brad:Damn good match though.. This one should be great too! Skyline vs. Ben Ferros!!! Skyline vs. Hollywood Ben Ferros Skyline and Ferros lock up, Skyline picks up Ferros, and slams him down. Skyline pulls Ferros to his feet, and Ferros punches Skyline in the nose. Ferros hits Skyline with a DDT. FERROS COVERS ONE..TWO..Kickout Ferros hits Skyline with a Piledriver. Ferros goes to the top rope. As Skyline stands up, Ferros Hits him with a Missile Dropkick. FERROS COVERS ONE..TWO..THR.KICKOUT! What's this? SHOCKER RUNS DOWN TO RINGSIDE?!? Shocker gets on the Ring Apron, meanwhile, Ferros hits Skyline with the Ferros Falls FERROS COVERS, BUT THE REFEREE IS DISTRACTED The Ref gets Shocker off the apron THE REFEREE COUNTS ONE..TWO..THRE.KICKOUT?!?SKYLINE KICKED OUT!?!?!Black Cobra and Shocker are now BOTH outside the ring?!? Black Angel climbs on to the Ring Apron. Black Cobra runs into the ring. Black Cobra nails Ferros with the Decapitator, and as Black Cobra leaves the ring, Skyline falls on Ferros, Black Angel leaves the apron. THE REFEREE COUNTS ONE..TWO..THREE!!! LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, HERE IS YOUR WINNER, SKYLINE Brad: GOD D@#N IT!!!! Why do people always interfere in matches of importance? Jesus Christ!! Jake:Calm down Brad, it's okay. Now, to our first of 2 title matches tonight. The Studlys vs. The Irish Inmates for The Rocky Mountain Championships!! The Studly's (Bubba Ray Studly/Dances w/ Studly) vs. Irish Inmates (Chris/Jimmy Kelly) Before the match... Bubba Ray and Dances say, "OK, Goodfellas... we know you REALLY want a beaten... so, why dont we make this a NO DQ Tag Match?", and Jimmy Kelly responded by saying, "Fine, you HICKS!!! Lets go!" Bubba and Chris lock up. Bubba nails a kick to the groin. Chris falls...Bubba jumps on him repeatedly. Bubba kicks him in the teeth many times, making him bleed... Bubba tags Dances. Dances piledrives him and throws him out of the ring. Bubba and Dances pull up the mat, revealing hard concrete. Bubba powerbombs him onto it! Hes out cold. Jimmy comes over with a chair and NAILS Bubba in the back of the head, knocking him out. Dances sees and runs.... Goodfella Kevin Kelly comes to ringside. Kevin clotheslines Dances into the post. Dances is hurt. Uh Oh, NKB and Blaro are at ringside... Rainman comes down.... Rainman and Blaro brawl, and go to the back of the arena. NKB comes behind Kevin and cracks a steel stairwell over his head! NKB attacks Chris Kelly with the stairs. He drops them on his face! Chris is bleeding heavily. NKB dropkicks Jimmy in the head, knocking him out. NKB grabs a frying pan and nails Dances in the head, he then nails Bubba in the back of the head. He knocked everyone out! NKB leaves, after throwing Dances and Chris in the ring. Dances rolls on top of Chris... 1, 2, 3! The Winners, THE STUDLY'S! They are the new Rocky Mtn. Champs!!!!!!!!! Brad: YES YES YES. Now that was a nice, honest, clean fair match huh??? Jake: Uh, Yeah, Uh, Okay Brad. I wish the Studly's the best of luck as the Rocky Mountain Champions. It's obvious that the Irish Inmates will have titles soon! And now to our Friend vs. Friend match!!! Blaro vs. G-Funk They lock up. Blaro nails him with an arm-drag takeover. Blaro nails him with a knee drop square on the face. G-Funk is bleeding all over. Blaro puts G-Funk on the top, Blaro nails him with a superplex onto the mat. G-Funk is hurt bad. Blaro stands him up and clips him in the knee. G-Funk falls in tremendous pain. G-Funk can't keep up with Blaro. Blaro decides to put G-Funk out of his misery. He pins G-Funk squarely in the middle of the ring 1-2-3. Blaro leaves the ring. G-Funk stands up and grabs the mic... HEY, YOU ALL THINK IT'S FUNNY THAT I LOST HUH???(starts to cry)YOU THINK IT'S FUNNY??!?!??!? Well I quit!!! I quit everythinkg, H.O.E., T.I.M. and especially EGWF!!! I am done, leave me alone. (He walks away for a final time to the back area.) Brad:No....No...NO WAY!!! I can't believe he quit! he just went out and bailed on the EGWF! Jake: He will be very sorely missed here...AND NOW...THE MATCH EVERYONE HAS WAITED FOR!!! THE MATCH FOR THE ROCKY MOUNTAIN TITLE!!! Senator Johnson vs. Ares!!!! Lets get to it!! Senator Johnson vs. Ares for the Rocky Mountain Title Both of the men are in the ring face to face. The bell rings to sound the match. They are talking trash to each other. They lock up. Ares gets the early upper hand with an armbar. Senator Johnson is down on one knee. Ares kicks him in the back. Johnson falls to two knees. Ares stands him up and nails him with a standing drop kick to the chin. Johnson falls to the ground again. Ares whips him into the ropes and misses with a clothsline. Johnson bounces off the ropes and nails him with a clip to the knee. Johnson pulls him to the outside. Johnson picks him up in a gorilla press position and drops him nuts first on the steal barracade. Ares screams. Johnson pulls him off and whips him into the side of the ring back first. Johnson executes a ddt on the outside. Johnson throws him back into the ring. Johnson climbs directly to the top, he hops off with a huge leg drop off the top rope. Ares moves, and Johnson lands hard on the mat. Ares slowly gets to his feet, he kicks the slowly rising Johnson in the head. Johnson falls to the mat, and rolls to the outside. Johnson grabs a chair and throws it in the ring. Ares picks it up and threatens Johnson to get in the ring. The ref turns him around and starts yelling at Ares on the inside of the ring. Rainman comes out of no where and nails Johnson from behind with a reverse ddt on the solid concrete floor. He then stands Johnson up and clips him in his hurt knee. Johnson lays on the outside exhausted. Rainman takes off but first he drops a polaroid picture on Johnson of his girlfriend Miss Liberty beat up with bruises and cuts on her face. Johnson lays knocked out on the floor. Ares gets out of the ring, he picks up the hurt Johnson and suplexes him onto the steal steps. Johnson looks hurt. Ares picks up the pictures and laughs at them. Ares throws him in the ring. He bodyslams Johnson onto the mat and then climbs to the top. Ares jumps off the top with a reverse moonsault!!! Ares covers 1-2-Kickout!!! Ares whips him into the corner and Johnson falls to a stting position in the corner. Ares goes outside and grabs the picture, he comes back in and taughts Johnson with the photo. When Johnson realizes whats on the photo he nails Ares with a punch to the throat. Ares falls to the mat in a sudden thrust of pain. Johnson stands up with a new found fury. He nails Ares with a barage of kicks and punches to the face and body. He picks Ares up and and nails him with a belly-to-belly suplex. Ares is laying on the ground completely out of it. Senator Johnson signals for "THE FILIBUSTER". He nails Ares with it and covers with one foot on Ares chest. Ladies and Gentleman, The Winner, and NEW ROCKY MOUNTAIN CHAMPION.....SENATOR JOHNSON. Johnson pics up a mic..."MISS LIBERTY, This is all for you! I swear to god on my life that I will bring you back to my loving arms! And Rainman, YOUR DAYS ARE NUMBERED...YOU WILL BE FILIBUSTERED!!!! Jake:YES YES YES!!! THE SENATOR WINS, THE SENATOR WINS!!! THAT WAS ONCE AMAZING MATCH! Brad:I am dumbfounded. I can't say anything....I don't....I don't.... I don't know what to say!!! Jake: Well Ladies and Gentleman, I want to wish you all a good week and we will keep you all updated on Armegedon as information comes through the wire....See you all at Wednesday Night....For all of us here at the EGWF to all of you.....GOOD NIGHT!!! ----------------------------------------------------------- © EGWF Entertainment 1997 http://www.geocities.com/Hollywood/9022/EGWF.html Extreme Global Wrestling Federation The Dynasty of all E-Feds!
