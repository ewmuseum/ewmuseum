Weekend to Remember 2 -- Final Flash Packet (part 4)

May 21, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TO BE ADDED TO THE MAILING LIST E-MAIL [detail withheld] ********************************** * "Problem Child" Chris Stringer * ********************************** [The camera opens on a shot of a.... high school chemistry lab? Yes, that's exactly what it is, complete with two tables loaded up with all sorts of beakers, tubes, bunsen burners and other things that you broke when your teacher wasn't looking, then blamed on the dumb bully standing next to you. In addition to the tables, there is one of those big fume hoods, where the teacher mixes up the potentially dangerous chemical compounds. Panning over to the other end of the room... we see those stupid desks attached to chairs that pervade high schools throughout the nation, formed into a neat 6x6 grid. At the front of the room, there is a teacher's desk and chalkboard. At the board, frantically figuring out some equation or other, is "The Problem Child" Chris
Stringer, wearing a long white lab coat and green safety goggles. Sitting at the teacher's desk, her attention focused on Chris figuring out the equation, is the fiery redhead, Cherry Bomb, who is also dressed in a white lab coat and safety goggles, though her lab coat bulges suggestively in places Chris's doesn't. Luke Morningstar is standing at the classroom door, standing guard in a -- you guessed it -- a white lab coat. Glen Peeps is watching Stringer excitedly, nodding his head up and down like a dorky bobblehead, wearing big-ass Poindexter glasses, complete with the tape down the middle, and a lab coat, of course. Cherry Bomb is the first to notice the camera, and she nudges
Stringer, who wheels around, spots the camera, takes his goggles off and begins his diatribe.]
Chris Stringer: Ah... how good of you to come. Welcome, welcome to "Mr. Stringer's World," a place where we today will not only learn about chemistry and science, but also logic. Yes, I know you may find logic a somewhat (making quote marks with his fingers) _boring_ subject, but it will have a large part to play in the outcome of the Weekend to Remember 2 Gauntlet Match, which we will be analyzing today. First, though, you will notice all of my associates are wearing safety goggles...
[Here, Cherry Bomb pipes up to add her two cents. No, she wasn't asked to add her two cents, but that's never stopped her before.]
Cherry Bomb: ...in case Chris Del Rio shows up. Seeing as he spent almost all of his last promo wanking his crank about himself, we figured we should protect ourselves against... um... "unexpected" spills that could cause irreparable damage to one's eyes. According to our research, he's known for not _going the distance_, if you catch my drift.
Chris Stringer: Quite right, Cherry. Anyhoo, pundits and prognosticators the world over... okay, maybe not the world over, but all over the U.S.... or maybe all over the South... wait, maybe not the South, but definitely Florida.... if not Florida, at least in Orlando... well, okay, maybe just Jay Ralston's house.
But anyway, there's a few people wondering just who will win the Gauntlet match, with such seemingly evenly matched competitors fighting one another for the honors!
[At this point, the whiny, squeaky voice of Glen Peeps perks up.] Glen Peeps: Seemingly being the properative (yeah, we know that's not a word, but hey, Glen's an idiot) word, Chris! HO HO!
[Chris shoots his classic "Jeez, you're a freakin' idiot" glance at Glen, then turns back to the camera.]
Chris Stringer: Exactly, Glen. What we're going to do on "Mr. Stringer's
World" today is using _LOGIC_ -- oof, there's that ugly word again -- eliminate the candidates who _can't_ win the match, then, you'll know who _will_ win the match, thus allowing you to use that time to go to the bathroom or get some more beer or shoot some heroin or do whatever you need to do to make watching this card tolerable. So, without further ado.....
[The screen quickly wipes to a purple background with a "Mr. Wizard's" lightning bolt that says "Candidate #1 -- Super Scott." Then, it wipes back to the smiling faces of Cherry Bomb and Chris Stringer.]
Cherry Bomb: Normally, we would just disqualify Scott for giving himself the name "Super Scott." Naming yourself with a superlative is usually a sure sign that you have some deep-seated insecurities about your abilities and/or manhood.
Chris Stringer: But... that's not a valid reason for dismissing him out of hand. Super Scott apparently is a well-known name in the wrestling world, and
I'm sure he's mentally competent enough to pump gas at a 7-11. No, what makes him certain to go down in defeat is this... can we roll the footage?
[The screen switches to a bit of Super Scott's promo from last week.] SUPER SCOTT: Del Rio and Stringer and that Xavier dude -- you guys just stay outta my way and I'll take it easy on you.
[The screen quickly cuts back to Stringer.] Chris Stringer: And there's the error. Assuming that just because someone
"stays out of your way," that they're not dangerous. Don't worry, Scott, I don't plan on getting in your way. If you see my old buddy Apex backstage, though, you might want to have a chat with him. I'm sure he'll let you know that you'd rather have me _in_ your way, because then at least you know where I am.... So, one down, four to go.
[The screen makes the same wipe to the "Mr. Wizard" screen as before. Now it says "Candidate #2 -- Xaiver Feyr." Quick wipe back to Stringer and Cherry.]
Chris Stringer: Now, take Xaiver Feyr... Cherry Bomb: Please. Chris Stringer: Hardcore is _SO_ 1999! Was your little skit by the campfire supposed to scare us, Xaiver? Huh? Well, impresses me it not. I've only been wrestling for six months, and I haven't been watching it for much longer than that. But so many people try to one-up each other with this hardcore crap that none of it is shocking anymore. What are you going to do that will truly shock us, Xaiver?
Cherry Bomb: Do a tiger driver off a triple flaming cage through a table? Chris Stringer: Seen it. Yawn. Cherry Bomb: Wrap your arm in barbed wire, then smash a glass pane, then clothesline your opponent with your arm that's wrapped in barbed wire and has glass shards coming out of it?
Chris Stringer: Seen that too. Double Yawn. Cherry Bomb: Bring a live donkey to the ring and smoke its pole in front of the audience? Chris Stringer: Seen... well, actually, no, I haven't seen that. In fact,
Xaiver, instead of wrestling, why don't you do that? It'll be a hell of a lot more entertaining and original than anything you'll do in the ring, and you might not get embarrassed. See, this is so easy when you do it _logically_....
two down, three to go... [The same "Mr. Wizard's" wipe as before. This time, the screen reads "Candidate #3 -- Chris Del Rio."] Chris Stringer: Now, this guy, well, women scream at him... Cherry Bomb: Mostly because his face looks like a vaginal bloodfart, but nonetheless, they _do_ scream...
Chris Stringer: ... and, at first glance, he might have the ability, the panache, the raw testicular fortitude to come out on top in this match.
Cherry Bomb: Alas, he lacks maturity. Chris Stringer: Exactly. He does not realize women can be more than highly advanced natural masturbation devices.... a weakness that will cost him should he come into the ring against me... you see, Chris, Cherry is much more than a... what was the word he used?
[Here, the voice of Glen Peeps cuts in from off camera.] Glen Peeps: I think it was cockshark. HO HO! Funny word, huh? [The camera pans over just quickly enough to see Cherry stun Glen with a huge bitch slap that almost makes his head do a 180.]
Chris Stringer: *chuckles*... Hey, speak when you're spoken to, Glen. Anyhow,
Cherry is more of another "c" word... a catalyst. Now, to dumb it down for people of Mr. Del Rio's intelligence level, that means she makes things happen, and Glen here has a scientific demonstration to help you all understand what a catalyst is, right, Glen?
[Glen, sufficiently recovered from the shot to the face, nods excitedly and grins like an idiot, then runs over to the fume hood, where a container of water is sitting, with a grey block of some unidentified substance sitting next to it.]
Chris Stringer: Now, in that fume hood is a bucket of water, but not _just_ a bucket of water, boys and girls. No, there is also something you can't see...
air, and specifically, oxygen. Now, the water and the oxygen are coming into contact, but they aren't doing anything, because there is not the proper
_catalyst_ for a reaction to occur. Hold up the block, Glen. [Glen, wearing white lab gloves, holds up the block.] Chris Stringer: Glen is holding in his hands a block of pure sodium. Now, don't go thinking that it's salt -- if you eat that, you'll go blind and be belching electric sparks for the rest of your life. No, that's pure _mineral_ sodium. Glen, show the kids at home what happens when we add our _catalyst_ of sodium to the water.
[Glen takes out a knife and begins to cut off a little piece of the block.] Chris Stringer: No, Glen. The whole thing. [Glen looks back at Stringer and Cherry with a shocked, panicked look on his face, eyes bulging. Chris and Cherry merely calmly nod their heads... Glen, very gingerly, adds the _whole block_ of sodium to the water, and you know what's coming, don't you?]
***BOOM!!!!*** [The sodium, reacting with the water, explodes, pretty much obliterating the fume hood. Little tongues of flame can be seen around the fume hood, and smoke is everywhere. Quickly, Luke Morningstar, in his first important action of the day, is on the scene with a fire extinguisher, putting out any flames that arose. The camera pans over to Glen Peeps, who is hacking up a lung, and shows his face covered with a black soot like substance that gives him the appearance of being in "black face." A couple of strands of his hair are burning, too, until Luke puts them out. The scene cuts back to Stringer and Cherry.]
Chris Stringer: See, children? Never underestimate the effect a catalyst can have. Chris Del Rio already has, and that's why he has no chance of winning.
Wow, the list is getting short here... we're down to two, and I'm almost hesitant to ... ah, *bleep* it. Let's piss off Mike Waldrop while we're at it.
[Yup, the old Mr. Wizard wipe again. This time is says "Candidate #4 -- Mike
Waldrop."] Chris Stringer: Now this is just a hopeless case... Cherry Bomb: You said it, Chris. Anyone we talk to, they say this guy has already reached as high as he's going to go, and there's no where to go but down. He's 19. NINETEEN! He's younger than me! Hell, he's younger than
_you_!
Chris Stringer: How must it feel, to know that you're on the downslope of your career, and you're not even old enough to legally drink yet? The depression that must overcome you, the doubts you must harbor about your own abilities, about whether you made the right choices in life....
Cherry Bomb: But that's not why he's going to lose. Chris Stringer: Quite right, Cherry. And to be fair, it's not his fault. You see, his specialty is not "Wrestling." It's "Extreme Mail." Which, as near as
I can tell, is an X-Games sport where you take a package from Ted Kaczynski and deliver it to O.J. Simpson in a truck built by Timothy McVeigh. I mean, not to get off on a tangent here...
Cherry Bomb (very hurriedly to Glen and Luke): *Bleep*, he's getting off on one of his tangents! Vamp!
[Glen and Luke look at each other real quick, Glen with a _very_ concerned look on his face, then the screen wipes across again, this time saying "Glen Peeps will now entertain the audience."]
[When we get back to the action, still hearing Chris Stringer talking in the background, we see Luke Morningstar lifting Glen Peeps by the belt loops on his plaid pants. Glen looks like he's about to have an uncontrolled bowel movement.]
Glen Peeps: Ah... ah... okay... we're now g-g... going to de... de... mostrate (yeah, I know he pronounced it wrong, but the guys scared *bleep*less, okay?) the theory of gra... gravi... ity...
Cherry Bomb: By showing you children at home what happens when you throw someone out a second story window! Hit it, Luke!
[With that, Luke quickly runs and throws Glen out the window. Except, well, they forgot to _open_ the window. Glen crashes through the window, jeez, they didn't kill him, did they?...
... Nope. As the camera follows Glen's path out the window, it fixes its gaze on the ground, which is covered with one of those massive Hollywood stunt man airbags. It then zooms in on Glen, who is waving up at the camera and grinning like an idiot, none the worse for wear. The camera then goes back to Stringer, who is finishing up his tangent.]
Chris Stringer: ... and really, anyone, even I, even Luke, even Cherry, hell, even _Glen_ can do "Extreme," therefore eliminating Mike Waldrop... and leaving yours truly to walk away from the Gauntlet as the winner! (looking at Cherry and Luke, who have disappointed looks on their faces) Okay, what's wrong?
[Luke finally speaks, in a deep, doleful voice.] Luke Morningstar: We missed. Chris Stringer: What? Cherry Bomb: Well, more accurately, we hit the bag with Glen. Chris Stringer: WHAT??!! Dammit, how am I going to show these people what
"extreme" is and how inane it is unless we see some gratuitous blood??!!!
(shouting out the window) HEY GLEN!!! [Glen yells back from the ground outside, barely audible.] Glen Peeps: Yeah, Chris? Chris Stringer: Get up here! We need to do another take! Glen Peeps: WHAT??? [Fade to black.] =========================================================== *************** * Luke Kinsey * *************** [We open to the lockerroom, somewhere, most specifically a bench. On this bench sits Luke Kinsey with his head in his hands. He wears black jean shorts and a black short sleeved button down shirt. As he stares at the floor, he can be heard mumbling to himself.]
LK: F[bleep]in' flake...leavin' me out ta dry...asshole...sonofabitch...sheep f[bleep]er... [Yup, Luke is talking about his former partner, Kevin Slater. But let's not talk about him...he brings the whole show down.]
LK: Who the hell am I gonna get to be my partner now? Sheeyat...this is Styles and Martinez...people should be knockin' down my door to fight this guys. [...] LK: Is it me...? [Luke stands up, and looks in the mirror, confusion on his face. After a minute, a familiar smirk returns to his mug.]
LK: Naaaaah. Can't be...everyone loves me. But dammit...I'm in a friggin bind here. I need a partner, pronto.
[Luke turns around, thoughtfully scratching his chin when he sees a locker. Not just any locker...it has words on it. Those words... "JOHNNY AXIS" with a big star on both sides. Kinsey wanders over to it, before inspiration hits.]
LK: That prick has to know someone who hates Martinez and Styles...maybe he's good for something after all. Besides wearing ugly shirts of course.
[Luke plays with the handle to no avail, and backs off. He studies the door meticulously and unexpectedly unleashes a kick that would make them puro guys jealous to the bottom of the door, and the door swings open, thus proving that taking the hall pass in high school for hours and roaming the hallways is educational. Luke looks inside the locker with interest.]
LK: Hmmm...deodarant...socks...those ugly orange sunglasses... [Luke picks up a thin hardcover book, about 300 pages.] LK:..."How To Be a Convincing Heel,"...wow, he has that book too. [Luke discards the book and resumes digging.] LK: Ah yes...here we are. An electronic organizer. Let's turn it on here... [BEEP!] LK: <reading> Jay Ay's Book of Freaks. Press three for phone numbers... [BEEP!] LK: Let's see here...Catanzaro....Shade...Gotham...Nick Demola...Rick Styles...Salvat--RICK STYLES?!?! Holy [bleep], prank calls here I come. Gotta remember that one... [Luke turns to the back of Johnny's book and rips out a piece of paper, and quickly writes down the wretched phone number.]
LK: Anyway...Cassanini...Alan's Adult Video's... [Luke raises an eyebrow and keeps going.] LK: ...Ron Strickland...WHOA! [Luke's eyes bulge out of his head as he comes across a name, but he is too shocked to even say it.]
LK: He _KNOWS_ him?!?! Oh man, he'd work. He'd be awesome...totally great. We'd be unstoppable, friggin invincible...he's a definite keeper. [Again, Luke rips a page from the back of Johnny's book and scribbles down the unknown number, shoving the paper deep into his pocket.]
LK: Heh...Johnny Axis _is_ useful after all. Welp...looks like I'm done with this. [Luke takes the organizer in his left hand and nonchalantly throws it in the trash can as he walks on by, out the door. From the hallway, he can be heard saying to someone...]
LK: Hey buddy, where's the nearest pay phone? [...as the camera fades.] =========================================================== ************************* * "Top Dog" Rick Styles * ************************* [Scene opens to a dark room where only a desk light allows us to see. At this desk sits the "Top Dog" Rick Styles with a pen in hand. Styles takes a clipboard with sheets of paper attached and begins writing...]
Dear Luke, So, it would seem last week you went out of your way to get my attention. Well fine, I notice you, does that make you happy? No-talent hack you refer to me as? Well shoot, that's not nice. But I'll digress and let me record speak for itself.
It would seem that, based on your comments, you don't have much respect for me. Well, that's perfectly fine because respect isn't a word I'd use to explain my thoughts towards you either. The only difference is, I'm a proven commodity and you...well you're Luke.
Come Weekend to Remember, I'm not there to earn your respect. I'm not there to prove to you that I am truly the legend I claim to be...although your believing this is just a side-effect of what is going to happen. This time around, my intentions are small. I am simply looking to prove that I'm better then you. I'm simply looking to use you and your partner as an opportunity to let out some pent-up frustration by ramming your heads into the Thunderdome cage as many times as I can and as hard as I can. Basically, I'm going to beat respect into you,
Luke, and I'll be enjoying myself the entire time. The Weekend to Remember will certainly be that for you and your tag team partner, Luke. MY partner and I will make sure of it. In conclusion, I promise you the following: while your statements about the "Top Dog"
Rick Styles were hardly kind ones, and will probably continue to be unkind up until we meet in Orlando..they will NOT mirror the statements you will make about me AFTER our meeting in the Citrus Bowl.
Your humiliation and defeat is my purpose inside that cage...I will not be denied, I will not be turned away and I will not be thought of as any less than as your superior.
Sincerely, The "Top Dog" Rick Styles [Styles removes the page from the clipboard, folds it in three and places it in an envelope. After licking and sealing the envelope, the
"Top Dog" takes another sheet of paper and begins to write...] Dear Alex, I was so very glad to hear that you have chosen to team up with me for the Thunderdome cage match at the Weekend to Remember, not because you see me as a friend or an ally, but simply because you have a sense of loyalty to the stable that we once were and that's enough of a reason for you to team with someone that you say you'd much rather be beating the snot out of.
My, how your words touch me. To think that after I found you as rookie in a punk league, brought you into the New Alliance, took you under my wing as a friend and helped lead you in the right direction...after all of this, you would have some sort of disdain for me. Very interesting, very selfish.
Frankly, I truly don't understand where this disdain comes from, Alex. If it's about the fact that I chose to challenge you for the UEW Ultimate title years back as opposed to allying myself with you, then understand, that was never personal, it was all about business. You had the belt, thus I had to oppose you rather then join you. If you don't understand the difference between business and personal, then perhaps my lessons in the New Alliance were lost on you.
I also point out that this took place years ago and I'd have to believe that by establishing NA pride in you, it certainly should be worth a lot more in your "emotions" then one lousy match, one lousy title change.
However, let me help you see things a bit clearer, and thus, force you to make a decision as far as where you stand.
If the only reason you choose to see me as an ally is because of those two letters, "NA" and for no other reason, then understand the following...The New Alliance is long dead. It meant the world to me as well, for that matter it was what defined me for years prior to your even entering this sport, but it no longer exists. Kyle Kamikaze, the
Suicidal Stallions, The J'ster...all of them are long retired from this sport and the heart of the New Alliance has stopped beating years ago.
We are all that's left of that fine stable, but it's no longer relevent. Your loyalty to something that no longer exists shouldn't be what motivates you anymore. Now, if the idea of us getting in there and pounding heads like the old days still interests you, I'm all for it.
The chance to team up with an old friend against two losers is always a great time and one I look forward to. Of course, that is the question...
Are we still allies, Alex? Is there still a bond between the "Top Dog" and the "Last American Badass"? Or did that friendship go out along with the New Alliance and thus, Christmas and Birthday cards no longer need to be exchanged?
I guess that's a decision you need to search down deep and find before we enter the Thunderdome Cage together. Can we still trust and rely on each other without superficial NA pride??
By the way, Alex, Happy Birthday. Sincerely, The "Top Dog" Rick Styles [Styles removes the page from the clipboard, folds it in three and places it in an envelope. After licking and sealing the envelope, the
"Top Dog" takes another sheet of paper and begins to write...] Dear Kevin, SAY SOMETHING FOR GOD'S SAKES!!! Sincerely, The "Top Dog" Rick Styles [Styles removes the page from the clipboard, folds it in three and places it in an envelope. After licking and sealing the envelope, Styles takes it, along with the other two, and leaves the room after shutting off the light.]
=========================================================== ********************************* * "Mr. Excitement" Alex Extreme * ********************************* [The camera opens to a darkened room...suddenly a shred of light produced as a film projector is turned on revealing a movie screen as well as dozen of empty chairs...the movie counts down....
5 4 3 2 1, Picture start here...the screen turns vibrant with a polished black in white while cheesy wartime cinema music plays...the title? The history of the UEW...]
V/O: Started in 1996, the UEW is sysnomous with great and big name professional wrestling. [Cut to a shot of Eric Smart and Mercenary holding the UEW title...] Since it's infancy the UEW has had it heroes... [The camera cuts to shots of Professor Payne, Ivan Kolinski, and Jeff Matthews...] And it's villians... [The camera cuts to a shot of Rick Styles, Rob Magnum, and Robski...] And it's youngersterrrrrrzzzzzzzz..... [The plugs is pulled as the image of a young "Mr. Excitement" Alex Extreme is briefly apparent...the room is now dark until the single match is lit and suddenly the screen is a flame. The flames illuminate the man walking in front of the screen and...
darkness. All this black and quiet until a man's voice can be heard...] Man: If all the world is indeed a stage, than my role as it's player as signifigant increased in the last five years.
[Suddenly the flood lights turn revealing a massive Broadway styled empty theater. On stage sitting out looking out toward the sea of empty seats, "Mr.
Excitement" Alex Extreme.] AE: When I began my foray into professional wrestling, I was a navie innocent with a penchant for mischief. Slowly my warm and loving smile was replaced by one of convience to appeal to the masses that I derive pleasure from something.
The truth? I gain pleasure through other's misery. My own personal happiness was dragged from my soul long ago by those who wished me to be more than I was.
Today, that person is replaced by the one you see now. The one that won championships and lauded by the masses for the work he's done. Gone are the days of laughter and campy humor. Today I'm the wrestling superstar everyone wanted to me to be.
I hope their happy. [The theatre goes black as Cypruss Hill's Rock Superstar plays as slowly the house lights come to dim to reveal the sillouette of Extreme still sitting there...]
AE: They were right. I had big dreams of making big green. I wanted to be a big shot, heavy hitter on tha mainstream, look trendy in tha bently just like those before me.
But I learned a few things to be a star man never act freindly You wanna have big fame? Let me explain. First they played like all danm day
'cause as long as you sell everything's okay but then you get dissed by media and fans and then things never felt tha same way then they been.
Yeah to be a wrestling superstar ain't easy. It's a lose of everything else you used to hold dear. People wonder why I try to live life so fast? The naughty women, the booze, the playa lifestyle are all byproducts of the emptiness when
I come home to the quiet place I call home. My silence, my prision.
[He pauses for a moment...] AE: So what does this have to do with Weekend to Remember 2? Everything. I has to do with the days when Alex Extreme could be Alex Extreme and not worry who was watching or what would be said. It was a time when I tried to set everything right and do things by the book.
I think that's when I realized I'd didn't go by the book. I realized the world was mine when I decided to take it for myself. That day when I sat in the hospital, wondering how I could allow Matthews to cost me the UEW title and watch Styles float away into the city of New York.
In Orlando, one of the men who cost me my innocence that day will face me at the event that since that time hasn't been held "officially". An Extreme
IronMan match? Really, now Creed did you think you could win a match named after me? You want to play in my world? You can fathom. You'd have nightmares that would make your lil' Angel darlin squeeze your arm and never let go.
In ain't some of the UEW guys you've faced before. Get cocky with me and I'll make you pay. But then you already found that out once before didn't you? As the blood trickled from your head in Las Vegas, you wondered how much worse it would have been had I not fought a battle royale and my first round opponent.
My guess is you question if you would have beat me at all that day if things had been reversed.
So Creed when you decide to face me in Orlando, don't think of me as the man you got lucky beating. That's because your prescene reminds me of days when I wasn't such a bastard. Days I truly wish for again somehow. But then when once the curtain goes up, nothing can be stopped...
It's showtime. [He whips his head around and looks into the camera with the usual bravdo and fake wicked smile.]
You won't win because I'm Alex Extreme and... and... you helped destroy what little soul I had. It's that simple Creed...that simple. [Slowly a curtain comes down as Cypruss Hill's "Rock Superstar" fades away and then the picture to black.]
