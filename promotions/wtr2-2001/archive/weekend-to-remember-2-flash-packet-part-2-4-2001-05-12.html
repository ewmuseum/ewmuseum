(The house itself is large yet not ostentatious. Made from the same stone and wood that inhabits this land, it is strong and built to withstand the harsh elements that continually assault the region. Within the house it is warm…without it is cold. The stone walls sheltering and warming he who dwells within. For the owner of this house is a solitary man…made so by choice yet maybe more so by fate. For he walks a path not easily maintained by the common lay-person. He is known as "The Lone Wolf", "The Black One". These are not the only names that he goes by…yet these are the names that he has chosen for himself. Names that aptly describe his demeanour, his life and the ideals that he tries to uphold. For after so many months of hardship he continues his quest, his lone crusade against villainy wherever he may find it.
For this man is Justice itself…this man is Throm The Black.) (The picture cuts to the back of the house. There is a swimming pool cut into the stone patio and the water is calm and clear, reflecting the sky. A rhythmic sound can be heard and it pounds out again and again. As the camera moves from the pool to the side of the hill upon which the house is built, we see Throm The Black…The Black One, landing blow after blow upon cut sections of wood. Cleaving them into smaller more manageable parts and with each swing of the axe, his muscle laden body sprays off sweat…paying silent homage to his uncommonly high endurance.)
(He is wearing black work boots, Black Leather pants and his top is bare, sweat covered from his exertion in the autumn sun. His hair hangs loose, sweat soaked and clinging to his shoulders as he lands blow after blow, splitting piece after piece of wood. We then see his face. It is set with a look of grim determination, as if chiselled from stone, yet from amongst the rock is a pool of life, his azure blue eyes. Shining with an unknown wisdom and a fury second to none.)
Throm - So the role call hath been made. Thus the warriors will come. From far and wide they will travel. Gathering together to stake their claim to fame and glory…fame and glory that will elude many of them…yet still…they will come. For they count themselves not amongst those who will be vanquished, but those who will prove triumphant. They believe that they bring with them the tool necessary to achieve victory…yet in truth…whatever they bring will not be enough to match what I have.
Throm - For I bring to battle two things which no other Man, Warrior, Heathen nor Swine may carry. I have a pure heart, immune to the normal temptations of the layman, stronger than any ally and more fierce than the most savage beast.
Yet this is not the only weapon in my armoury that I wield…for I also carry
Mjollnir. Craft from stone and baptised in the blood of mine enemies it is second to none in the devastation that it may wreck. Tis with these two weapons that very soon, thy will see me go to war.
Throm - Weekend to Remember II. Such a grand name for a battle, yet grand it will be. The best fighters from the realm will gather, the most dangerous of adversaries in the most gruelling of bouts…and all for the glory that victory brings. Tis at The Weekend to Remember II that a war will take place. A war waged by 4 factions, each battling for the right to call themselves "Better",
"Stronger" and more deadly than the others. Tis this battle that I find myself looking to.
Throm - Allied with other warriors that I know well, Solo, a mercenary of the fiercest variety, HeLLfiRe, a creation of Satan and Demon Spawn itself and
Kambion, a young man, innocent in the ways of the world yet more than capable of defending himself in it. Tis side by side that I find myself fighting with these three mighty warriors, for that is what they are, fighting against three factions in what could be our most challenging battle yet.

"Messiah" Ray Hagan, Mike Masters and Art Blake. Now I am sure that in their own land and in their own battleground that these Cravens are worthy of a
"reputation". I gather that they may also have achieved victory once or twice…yet never before have they faced the primordial might of The Black One.
