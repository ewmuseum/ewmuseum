Weekend to Remember 2 -- Final Flash Packet (part 7)

May 21, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TO BE ADDED TO THE MAILING LIST E-MAIL [detail withheld] ************************* * "Dirty" Doyle Woodall * ************************* [The camera opens on "Dirty" Doyle Woodall, clad in baggy blue jeans, a white t-shirt that reads "Sweetan's Bar & Grill - San Antonio, Texas", and a red bandana tied around his unruly black hair. A smoldering cigar juts from his clenched teeth, and he is holding a piece of paper. Woodall removes the cigar from his teeth and addresses the camera...]
"They say that ever' fight is won and lost b'fore it's ever fought. And the way I got it figgered, if yer gonna win a fight, ya gotta know who it is yer gonna be standin' up against."
[Woodall holds the paper up...] "So I took a little time scroungin' up the names o' the poor bastards that're puttin' life and limb on the line is this little tournament, and all I got to say is...
... what a piss-poor bunch o' folks we got here." [Woodall takes a drag from his smoke...] "First off, we a fella that calls hisself the Demon-Boy. I ain't heard nor seen too much about this little devil, but I'm guessin' they don't call him the Demon-Boy 'cause he spends his time walkin' old ladies across the street and sellin' Girl Scout cookies. I just hope yer feelin' a little homesick, Demon-Boy, 'cause if I meet up with ya in this tournament, I'm definitely gonna do my best to send yer ass straight back to Hell."
[Woodall takes another drag from his cigar...] "Next on the list, we got Maxwell Creed. A big-time lawman, this guy seems to be. I've heard his name a time or two, and some folks think this guy's got what it takes to win the whole damned thang. But from where I stand, this guy's got about as much chance o' takin' that cup as some o' those donut-eatin' flat-foots I know from back home. New York City might be a big scary place to some folks, Creed, but that little act yer pullin'
don't scare the Dirty One a damned bit. I got a lot o' bad blood with the men in blue, son, and if we happen to step into that ring t'gether, I'm gonna spill some o' yers, ya can b'lieve that."
[Another pull from the cigar...] "Warlock? What the hell? Ever'time I think of a warlock, I think about that fruitcake on that old television show. Oh, I'm sorry... can't say fruitcake anymore. Anywhich, unless yer gonna conjure up some evil spirits to take the Dirty One down, my best advice to you, Warlock, is to git on yer little broom and fly yer ass on outta this tournament, or I'll see to it that yer broom b'comes a permanent part o' yer backside."
[Yet another drag...] "Some o' these other folks don't even rate mentionin', if ya ask the Dirty
One. Shade? We done covered that no-talent bastard. Apex? Don't know from him and really don't give a damn. So that leaves you... Mr. Robinson."
[Another pull from the cigar...] "I heard ya talkin' yer smack about the Dirty One, Eugene, and it seems ya can't figure ol' Dirty Doyle out... but I got you pegged, amigo. Ya see,
Eugene, I've seen yer kind from day one in this business. A little bit o'
talent and a whole lot o' mouth, runnin' around with a piece o' painted-up store-bought tail that takes yer crap and makes ya feel like a real man, livin' the high life, and collectin' a fat paycheck for doin' nothin' more than runnin' yer head and beatin' on folks that would last a hot minute in a New Mexico bar fight. Ya had a lot o' bad thangs to say about the Dirty
One, and why shouldn't ya? Ya ain't expectin' to meet up with me no way.
Just like ever'body else, yer expectin' the Dirty One to bow outta this thang early, pavin' the way for you to waltz on into the finals and claim yer just reward. Well, I got news for ya Eugene... that just flat-out ain't gonna happen."
[Woodall takes the last drag from the cigar, drops the butt, and grinds it out with his boot...]
"So if I was you, Eugene, I'd hope like hell that somebody catches the luckiest break o' their life and takes the Dirty One out o' this thang early. 'Cause if ol' Dirty Doyle does make it to Mr. Robinson's neighborhood, there's gonna be hell to pay. And unlike yer little hussy,
Dirty Doyle ain't gonna take his payment in cash..."
[Woodall grins wickedly...] "... I gonna take it outta yer ass." [Fade to black.] =========================================================== ********************************** * Eugene "High Society" Robinson * ********************************** "I've found myself thinking about my past as of late. They say it is ones past who shapes who he is in the present.
[Twenty-five years ago.] [Fade in on a small country like homesite in the outskirts of Minneapolis. A small almost lifeless town, wedged behind the borders of White Bear Lake, hidden in the valley of nothingness, as if it didn't even exist. There are no children running on the streets, no fathers parading their brand new automobiles down the roads, nobody. Nobody that we can see at least.]
[We cut to pale gray single story house and roll up the grassy front yard where we move inside the cracked doorway. In the distance we hear a faint cry, a wailing voice of a disturbed little boy. Blonde hair, blue eyes, a slender child is he. His knees grind into the carpet floor, his body buckles at the waist, but his eyes peer up, glaring between the fingers that cover his face.]
[Before him lies his father, literally lifeless on the sofa. Blood soaks through his cotton shirt, dripping down his arm as remains motionless.
Forever. The child screams as a man tramples out the front door, his mother in his arms, and suddenly the child's broken heart comes together and he storms after the man, tears trickling down his cheeks. His mother cries out to him, demanding that he stay put but a young Eugene Robinson doesn't listen, he never did. Eugene charges a now attentive man whose face remains hidden underneath the black cloak and he lunges towards him with such a force that Hercules himself would be proud. But the child falls, he falls at the boot of the man who stole his father's life and now steals his mothers to take as his own.]
[Present day.] [Black screen.] "Growing up my father demanded absolute perfection from me, he demanded I did everything the right way and then some. No matter what it was, who it was against, I always had to come out on top and I always had to leave the other children behind in their misery, defeated, and conquered.
He was everything I ever wanted to be. Brash, bold, intelligent, respected and unstoppable. In my eyes, he was perfect, in his eyes, I wanted to see myself. The night he passed away I looked into those same eyes that he looked at me with, and what I saw was a man who had been destroyed. I saw a man who let others be close to him, I saw a man who for one brief moment let his guard down. I realized that the only man capable of defeating my father was himself. I realized that he was as weak as those that he had conquered but I was too blind to see it before.
The man who murdered my father... [Pause.] ...set me free." [Fade in.] [Enter the temple known to us as the Dynasty Tower. Lavish furnishings, state of the art electronic software, Piccaso's hanging from the walls.
There's no need to go through this endless tirade once more, you should know the scene by now.]
"People keep tellin' me I've dug myself in a hole that Brody Thunder himself couldn't climb out of. They say I keep diggin' deeper and deeper with everythin' I say, to a point that Zombie Corners himself would seize to be able to exist in, even if he is trapped in that f[bleep]ed up little world that Apex calls his imagination.
To those people I simply laugh, they don't know me. Nobody knows me but I'm sure they'll try and tell you who I am. I'm sure everyone will try and tell you who Eugene Robinson is and what he is and isn't capable of doing.
Again, I laugh." [The voice is familiar by now, at least it should be. Though at this time it's dry, as bland as you'll ever hear it though somehow, some way, it still bares that ruthless accent that makes you quiver, makes you fear him though your mind tells you to stand strong. It is the voice of the wealthy snot nosed bastard of a man who has taken the world by storm and lives under the burning spotlight that is provided by Sarah Reed in a Universal place.]
[We stare at him, once again looking through the ceiling-to-floor window behind him that reveals the sun gently setting on another day and falling over the neon sky. His fingers intertwine almost perfectly, meticulously, behind his neck, his face half hidden in shadows as he stares downward. His sandy blond hair is slicked back, every strand, every thread perfectly gelled into place. But despite the clean shaven visage, the sharp suit, the spit shining clean office there is something remarkably predatory about him.
Something unreal; possibly from his raw features, maybe it's that dagger's slash of a smile, maybe it's the gleam in his eyes, his steel blue eyes.]
"This week I get to face a man, a hero, who will tell me 1000 different ways as to how he's going to save the world. He'll probably list his entire resume as if he's applying for a new job at the local mom and pop diner.
He'll tell us again and again how he's on his way up the ranks of place that has long since been flushed down the toilet after a promising start.
However, what he won't do is impress me." [Robinson spares a sharp smile, showing any real emotion for the first time.] "I'm sure he'll sit in his little squad car and think of ways to take me down, I'm sure he'll continue to play the roll of the savior who was above the likes of me only to turn around and have his feelings crushed by nothing more then a few words. Suddenly I have given the Kodiak Cop a reason to fight, a reason to live, a reason to want to kick my ass.
I'm so glad I could inspire you Maxwell, really, I'm honored that your fuses are about as long and thick as the blue balls you had while lusting over midgets at the local Gentlemen's club."
[Robinson sneers as he reaches down to his oak desk, scooping up a glass of wine with his fingertips.]
"I'm sure Maxwell will insult me, where I'm from, and call Sass a whore as often as he can. If he doesn't, someone else will. But really Maxwell, your creativity amazes me that much. You'll snarl, you'll snap, and you'll preach to me about the lifestyle I live. My response? I'll wonder what I could have possibly said to upset you and then wonder if this is a wrestling intervention or a roast. Whether I should offer you the number of a psychiatrist, or simply laugh at your piss poor verbal delivery. I'm not going to sit here and tell the world that I will beat Maxwell, Johnny,
Warlock or Jay Ralston himself to a bloody pulp, I could, but I won't. I'm not a pretender Maxwell, I'm not a disturbed individual who carries around a badge as reassurance to his manhood. Unlike the rest of these little inbreeds, I make but one guarantee."
[Robinson sips from his wine.] "I fully realize that some of you don't see the pointlessness of arguing with one another about who'd beat whose ass, who kisses whose ass, and in
Dirty Done-Little's case whose so hard-core that they would eat their own ass if meant their survival. To each one of you, I look forward to you all mumbling these mouthed threats about being the biggest, baddest, most under rated, under appreciated, I've got four arms dislocated son of a b[bleep] to ever grace a wrestling ring.
And I can see it now. Now that Eugene Robinson has released another promotional video the Kodiak Cop will raise his badge, twirl his little baton, and he'll swing his handcuffs around in the air just long enough to pop major wood. We've seen it all ready, whose to say it won't happen again?
One begins to wonder if Maxwell Creed is indeed an officer, or merely a stalker dressed up as one. I wish I could say I understand it, however I've never been low enough on the ladder to feel the need to chase anyone around."
[He sets the glass down, running his devious tongue along his lower lip, licking the wine from it.]
"But that is neither here nor there. Maxwell Creed can fight the good fight it's truly no concern of mine, hardly any of my business. It's not something I feel the need to watch over with binoculars and a tape recorder because quite frankly I don't care and only a spineless coward would ever resort to measures that extreme. So if you are asking yourself why am I bringing this up? It's simple. 'Cuz before Creed goes off on his ill advised tirade about conquering NEW and his meteoric rise to the top of it by defeating men who are in fact not champions of any kind, I should remind him that this isn't Tampa Bay and I sure as hell am not that little runt
Madison J. Valentine who ironically enough I made my personal b[bleep] in
Global Pro Wrestling on my way to the World Title.
But I'm not Madison Valentine, and unless I miss my guess, it is Eugene
Robinson, not MJV, who Creed is facing at Weekend to Remember. So while you bore the world from your little Bat Cave don't forget for a damn second just who exactly it is you are facing. We don't have a past or present with each other Maxwell. What we did against others in fact has no baring at all on what I'll do against you. All we have is the future, that's ALL we have.
But from the looks of things, your future is headed straight down the toilet with NEW to a whirl pool of shit. And while your down there kiddo, pulling the logs of crap off of your skin, tell MJV, Tommy Gunn, and Adam Rogers that Eugene Robinson says hello."
[It's quite awkward seeing a cold hearted man such as Robinson batting his fingers into the camera but that is in fact what he is doing, mockingly saying hello to his former colleagues in GPW.]
"And where as Apex believes he may make it to the finals of the R-Cup, where as cousin' f[bleep]in' red necks believe Warlock will win, and where as
Johnny Axis paints pictures on a wall to give him fans that tell encourage him to succeed Eugene Robinson makes his guarantee.
'Cuz I'm not a believer in fate, I don't need to make up little fairy tells about race car drivers and god knows what to tell myself I'm going to come out on top. So if you didn't learn anything from me over these past few minutes then stick the wooden corndog sticks in yer ears and clean out that wax 'cuz if you only remember one thing I say then let it be this."
[Zoom in on the face of Eugene Robinson, baring the most infamous facial expression to ever exist, the famous "smirk you wanna smack." His jaded lips pressed together, his steel blue eyes barreling down upon you. The man is in his element and when Eugene Robinson is on, nothing can touch him.]
"I KNOW I will walk away from Weekend to Remember as the R-Cup champion and for you morons out there who have been sittin' too close to the television set during the latest Cops in Tampa Bay show gone horribly wrong."
[Robinson wrenches back, arrogant smile across his face.] "Winning the R-Cup is the ONLY guarantee I will give you this week! It's not for the people, it's not for the little starving children in Ethiopia.
Eh-eh. This one folks.... [Pause.] ...This one's for me." [Darkness.] [End.] =========================================================== ****************************** * "Kodiak Cop" Maxwell Creed * ****************************** [Cut to a black screen. Slowly, large white letters begin to fade into the screen as a deep voiceover reads them out to everyone in T.V. Land.]
V/O: The following does not necessarily represent the thoughts and views of
Weekend to Remember participants and organizers. They are the views of the organization of Wrestlers Urging Safe Sports Year-round...
[Fade to black. Slowly a golden eagle, wrapped in an American flag fades into the screen. After a few seconds, other words begin to fade in around the symbol, spelling out... "W.U.S.S.Y.'s Most Wanted."]
[Cut to a giant room filled with countless computer geeks plugging away at their computers. It is an analyst's computer center... the place where numerous deadly criminals are profiled before they are tracked down and brought to justice. The camera moves along the rows of desk and cubicles until it arrives to one over large oak desk, covered in files. The name tag on the desk reads "Maxwell Creed"...but alas he is no where to be seen.]
[After a few seconds a the brown docker-clad legs of a man enters on to the scene. The camera slowly works itself up the man's body revealing a thick, navy blue with a NYPD insignia on the breast. The man just happens to be the current Anti-Hardcore Activist of the Weekend to Remember tournament..."The
Kodiak Cop" Maxwell Creed.]
MC: Hello there ladies and gentlemen. [Creed runs his hand over his short, jet black hair... why? Good question. It can't go anywhere with the amount of gel he has in it...] MC: But more importantly... hello to all you TRUE blue wrestling fans out there! Now I realize that many of you out there were quite worried about my little run in with Eugene ROBERTSON last week. I would first like so send out a
GIANT thank you to all of you out there that sent cards, emails and fruit baskets wishing for my speedy recovery...
[Creed's face suddenly becomes angry for a moment.] MC: As for that ONE person who sent me that book on the "how to's" of seducing women, I say to you that Maxwell Creed is a gentlemen and needs
ABSOLUTELY no help in that area of his life!
[Creed leans himself against the desk behind him as his smile slowly returns.] MC: Now that is out of the way, we get get down to the business at hand. On today's show we well be covering a topic which is very dear to not only my heart... but to the heart of each and everyone of you TRUE wrestling fans out there in T.V. Land.
Today, we will be speaking about the few, non-talented folk who love splatter violence, and cherish the downfall of professional wrestling.
These folks that we will be talking about have brought down the honor of professional wrestling. They have repeated raped the virginal qualities that this grand sport was born and found upon AND finally no longer will their molesting actions be overlooked.
[Creed pushes himself off of the desk and takes a step closer to the camera.] MC: But no longer. Today we will be taking an indepth look into those criminals that have victimized this great sport.
[Creed pauses for a moment to let it all sink in.] MC: So let us begin by covering the men in this tournament that have a higher then 30% chance in heck of winning the whole deal...
[Creed turns around and begins to shuffle through all of the files on the desk behind him. Finally he pulls out a file, turns around and looks at the file.]
MC: ...... [Several seconds pass as Creed says nothing.] MC: ...... [Finally... he speaks.] MC: Okay... let's be honest about all of this. There is no possible way that any ONE of the competitors in this little tournament has a shot at winning the R-Cup. Well all except me. And I am a man of high respect in this field so it would be pointless to try and say anything.
But the rest of the men... a "Dirty Dog"... a Warlock... men who spend more time on two bit commercials trying to gain "hype" for themselves. I mean come on... since when are wrestlers media whores???
And then of course they allow a man with psychological problems into the tournament. Excuse me, am I the only one that sees that mental patients do
NOT make for a safe working environment???
[With that Creed tosses the file to the ground! Then he realizes that he still needs what's in it and quickly drops to the floor to gather all the papers from it. The camera follows him.]
MC: Ladies and gentlemen... I will speak to you today onl about one man. A man that CLAIMS to love technical wrestling, but is merely using that claim to advance himself in the world today. Wrestling fans... I am speaking of
Eugene Robinson.
[Creed finally finds the paper he needs and returns to sitting on top of the desk.] MC: He is a man, blessed with the gift of money. He has always had the silver spoon shoved in his mouth. But yet, has he become a respectable man of this great sport? No, he has not. ALL he has done is gone and used the sport to try and make a name for himself. Obviously because his wrestling skill cannot.
[Creed slowly approaches the camera.] MC: And that is not all. He regularly "pimps" his very own girlfriend out in order to take advantage of poor, innocent law abiding citizens.
Now I ask you... Is THIS the type of man you want representing your professional sport of champions???? [As we are now treated with extreme close up of Creed, his face is one of a sickened look.]
MC: Anyone who celebrates a man like this must be seriously deficient in all aspects of the mind.
[Creed shakes his head solemnly for a moment, trying to regain his calmness over this disgust outrage to professional wrestling.]
MC: Wrestling fans.... Eugene Robinson's reign of terror over this grand sport has officially come to an end. After I finish him off within the opening moments of our match, I will then place him under arrest so that he will never ever bother the wrestling world again.
But I must ask you fans out there for one thing.... When the time comes... gather behind the one man that you all truly want to win the tournament so that he may take the R-Cup and win it for each and every one of you out there...
Simply because... he is a.... [Creed pauses as the seconds begin to multiply...] MC: A Wrestler.... Urging.... Safe.... Sports.... Year-round.... You got it... A W.U.S.S.Y.....
