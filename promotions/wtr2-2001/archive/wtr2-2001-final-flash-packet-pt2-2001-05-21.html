Clifton: "I hope they know how lucky they are." [Of course, it wasn't all that long ago that he WAS the guy at that table, and he did have a nice looking young woman across the table from him, and they were sharing stupid stories of no consequence. But it seems so long ago now, and out of reach.]

Clifton (softly): "She probably was." [The waitress returns with the check.] Waitress: "Here you go." [Clifton examines the bill to see what he owes, while...] Waitress: "Hey, where's your date?" [Clifton cringes.] Waitress: "Are you here by yourself? How come?"
