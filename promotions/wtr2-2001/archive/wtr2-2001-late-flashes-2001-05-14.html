Weekend to Remember 2 -- Late Flashes

May 14, 2001, 12:00:00 AM

Johnny Axis ******************************* * "Sizzling" Scott Sexton * ******************************* [the camera slowly fades in, showing a shot of the Mohawk Valley Wrestling studio in the MVW headquarters based inside of Oneida, New York. There are spotlights all throughout the room, pointed towards the center where in front of a MVW backdrop is a leather chair with the back turned towards the camera.
As lights slowly go on, there is revealed to be a man sitting in that chair, before he reveals himself though, you hear a distinct voice.]
VOICE - Weekend to Remember? __I'll__ give you something to remember. [the chair spins around] ["sizzling" SCOTT SEXTON] Guess who is joining the party. [grins] [Scott Sexton sits there, before the camera with a smile across his face. He has on a white bandanna, with a pair of dark black Oakley sunglasses below covering his eyes. Going down further is a blue and tan baggy Phat Farm polo shirt, with baggy black jeans below that as well. He sits there, leaned back in the chair before finally leaning forward and continuing to speak towards the camera.]
["sizzling" SCOTT SEXTON] I mean, of course I'm here. Do you __REALLY__ think they'd have a collaboration of wrestling's greatest talent, and NOT involve Scottie Sexton?
No, you don't. [smiles again] Dante Madison, Jason Patrick, Donovan Keltrane, and Scott Sexton, put that together and what do you get? Team MVW. The foursome thats gonna go into the Civil War match and put rivalries, grudges aside and form the most dominant unit of them all and, well, [shrugs] win.
I could sit here all day and night and talk up about my opponents and lay the verbal tongue lashing like only I can, but I won't. I don't need to, because my focus, my goal is to let my talent do the talking and it will, soon enough.
AVW, SeWF, and nWa can go ahead and step in that ring and give it everything they've got, their blood, their sweat, and their tears and all of it will amount to absolutely NOTHING. Because what it all comes down to, is across that ring is the best damn talent in professional wrestling today, and with that being said, their days are numbered. I will hold nothing back, and neither should any of you. You think that Scott Sexton is playing around?
Try me ... Please ... Because if you do, your sappy asses will take in the beating of a lifetime courtesy of the "Sizzling" one himself. I don't give a damn what your name is, what titles you hold, how big you are, how tall you are, and how good you __THINK__ you are, because no matter how you slice it ...
Scott Sexton __IS__ better! Jason Patrick, do your part. Dante Madison, do your part. And Donovan Keltrane, [pauses] you son of a bitch, you do your part. And Scottie Sexton __WILL_ do his and then some, guaranteed ... Because he is ... HOTTER than HELL. [fade out] ========================================= ************************************ * "Darkhart" Donovan Keltrane * ************************************ [The darkness fades, revealing what appears to be a dimly light throne room. The dim flame of the fading sun reflects off of the gold paneling on the walls and throne. Seated in the darkness is the figure of Donovan Keltrane. He seems relaxed, leg's crossed regally, his chin resting on a balled fist. The silence is deafening as the camera pans in, focusing somewhat on the eerie blue glow that seems to eminate from his eyes.]
Keltrane: A Civil War match, eh? The best of a variety of federations to meet in a match to determine a dominant.. species in this business. How utterly intriguing... and yet again, how .... misplaced. Misplaced.. by the makeup of the teams. I know little of the others involved, save those from my realm of influence ... but I believe the MVW team holds the mark of normality for each team. Joining myself are two very talented individuals. Two men that I have faced off against, in some form or fashion. And I dare say that they would easily eradicate the competition facing us in this match on their own, without my help. Jason Patrick... and Dante Madison. Add myself amongst those ranks and you have, litaerally, a juggernaut. A machine capable of utter destruction. Destruction that is met out without mercy, without feeling ...
but with complete beauty in its every detail. Needless to say, the trio we create would be awe-inspiring and devastating .... and then there is the wildcard. A rip-off artist with no originality, no self-produced talent and no merit other than being one of sheer annoyance. His name need not even be mention...
[A pause. Again the deafening silence. Keltrane shifts, leaning back in his chair, his burning eyes focused on the camera.]
Keltrane: But it matters not. To those others who will compete in this clash of federations, I say good luck. You're going to need it. Be prepared for the worst, because the damage you received will be far worse than your imaginations can conjure up. [He shrugs slightly, a bit of a laugh coming from him.] When all is said and done, you will remember this even whenever you seen the moon.
The night is pitch, and the black wind howls. Fear the darkness ... it is your natural enemy. Dette oide....[Keltrane utters as he stands and spreads his arms.] Boku no hono!
[Purple flames erupt from the sides of the throne, encasing Keltrane in the wavering, flickering purple light.]
Keltrane: Sugu raku ni shite yaru.... and remember. Those who fear the darkness .... have never seen what the light can do....
[A crescent moon slashes onto the screen in front of Keltrane... and the background fades, leaving only the sharp blade of Luna in the center of the screen.]
========================================== ************** * HeLLfiRe * ************** [We are on an open field. The camera pans around, looking for something. We finally find HeLLfiRe, rifle in his hands, shoot down a deer. He walks over to it and lifts it by the antlers.]
HeLLfiRe: When a deer is taken down, is it dead? [The wind blows through his hair.] HeLLfiRe: Does anything ever really die? I hope not, because I wouldn't be here right now. Because if nothing dies, then this deer would lay here for eternity in pain...pain an agony.
Maybe I would like that no one dies. [HeLLfiRe drops the deer.] HeLLfiRe: So we'll leave it here. My point is, this R-Cup could be similar. I am placed in a match with 3 men I don't care for at all. Throm the Black,
Solo, and KamBion. But, we shall co-exist, I know this, and when we leave the opponent there, lying, suffering, but not dying, but feeling the worst pain possible. That is what I'm here for. So if you like it or not, I will take the competition and slay, leaving them in unremarkable pain, because to live on
Earth is a short time, but to live in HeLL...
YOU STAY FOR ETERNITY!!!!! [The camera pans out and the scene ends.] =========================================== *************** * Super Scott * *************** Scene opens poolside where Super Scott is laying out in the sun --- and two or three bimbos are swimming in his pool]
SUPER SCOTT - You've never heard of me, Mikey Waldrop? You've NEVER heard of me, Mikey Waldrop? Let me get this perfectly straight ... you've never heard of me Mikey? I thought that was a humourous way to get under my skin -- and then you go on to rag on my name --- talking about how I'm some sort of wannabe
Super Hero? Now I'm wondering -- maybe you actually HAVEN'T heard of me -- which is pathetic.
[Super lights up a Cuban Cigar and takes a hit] SUPER SCOTT - Enough about Waldrop -- he's a 19 year old punk kid. These young punks remind me of the NBA. Those young bastards are making a mockery of the
NBA -- ratings are down, nobody gives a shit about the league. And you got these little assholes running around showing absolutely no respect to the guys that paved the way for them. Back in the "good-ole days" ... we did it right.
But at least Iverson and Carter don't try to pretend that Michael Jordan and
Magic Johnson never existed. What the hell are you trying to do by pissing me off? If Iverson did that to Michael, MJ would challenge him to a game and then proceed to kick his ass. Well, now you've given me no choice. We've already got a match, but until about 10 minutes ago - I could give two shits about it.
But now I'm a bit pissed off. [Super takes another hit of his stogie] SUPER SCOTT - A 19 year old punk kid is getting to me, one of the greatest wrestlers of all-time. What the hell is happening to me? I should be main eventing -- not wasting my time with this crap. But oh well, it'll be fun torturing this dumbass. Ugh... I really don't know what the hell else to talk about. Del Rio and Stringer and that Xavier dude -- you guys just stay outta my way and I'll take it easy on you. I respect the fact that you guys haven't done anything to piss me off. Hell, if any of you guys wanna help me take this jackass out -- I'll help you in whatever it is you wanna do. We'll coast into the finals of this gauntlet - and then I'll wipe my ass with your face.
[Super pauses and looks at a loss for words] SUPER SCOTT - I really don't have too much to say right now ... I won't spend hours boring everyone to tears with my ramblings like the young pukes have made real popular these days -- so I'll end this interview early. For once the best talker in the history of the sport is at a loss for words. But that's okay --
I'll do my talking in the ring. I'm out. ============================================ **************************************** * "Last American Badass" Alex Martinez * **************************************** [Its simple, and deceptively so. A man in blue jeans, a t-shirt, and a leather jacket standing in front of a plain white background. But stop and look at the details, and you’ll see that there’s more than what appears at first glance. You’ll see that the t-shirt has two gold letters on it,
“NA,” and those with a memory long enough may understand the significance of those two letters. You’ll see that the leather jacket is a bit old, and is spattered with dried up blood stains. And you’ll see that the man himself is just as weathered. His face is scarred, his nose crooked. But you’ll also see intensity in his brown eyes, a hatred that fills his heart, and a desire to crush anything that stands in front of him. You’ll see the Alex
Martinez, the man many know as the Last American Badass.] AM: Some people are askin’ me why I’m teamin’ with Styles. Some people can’t understand why it is Rick and I keep crossin’ paths, despite the bad blood ‘tween us. Sometimes, people don’t understand that honor runs deeper than crap that’s happened in the past. And sometimes, people don’t understand that the blood ya shed workin’ with a man is thicker than the blood ya spilled fightin’ that man.
But don’t worry, I’m gonna explain it all to ya. So, look closely, ‘cuz what I gotta say and show ya is important. And trust me, I’d rather break a man’s legs than repeat myself to him. So just pay attention, and it might keep ya out of traction.
See these two letters here represent somethin’. [Martinez indicates the “NA” on his t-shirt.] AM: Somethin’ that people might think is dead. Somethin’ that people might think was overhyped even in its prime. But its somethin’ that means the whole world to me, and somethin’ that means even more than that to Rick
Styles. Two letters... N and A. Two letters that mean the New Alliance. And I ain’t just blowin’ smoke up your ass when I tell ya that the NA stands for more than just two words. The NA is now, has been, and always will be nothin’ but _the_ standard of excellence in the world of sports.
The NA means bein’ the elite. Bein’ better than the crème of the crop. It means that night in and night out, ya prove to yourself, to the world, and more importantly, to your partners, that not even god himself can stand up to ya. Individually, we are the best... together, we’re beyond invincible.
All of this, it used to be called NA Pride. It used to mean that ya always did your best, and that everythin’ ya did was for the good of the time. The
NA is gone now, in all but memory. There ain’t five guys rulin’ the world, the way it used to be. But though the team is dead, that NA Pride lasts
_forever_. It don’t never die. NA Pride is what this is all about. ‘Cuz now, that Pride is something else. Somethin’ I like to think of as honor. [A pause, as Martinez draws a breath. His eyes seethe with hate and intensity.] AM: Styles and I have had our differences. That’s the fact of the matter. And someday, I still intend on gettin’ him in the ring, to settle the debt
‘tween us. But when that day comes, it ain’t gonna change what I’m talkin’
‘bout. Even if we end up cripplin’ each other to settle that debt, it won’t ever kill the part of he and I that are NA. And maybe ya can’t understand that ‘cuz ya ain’t never been part of somethin’ like the NA.
Maybe its something ya have to live through to understand what it means. I can’t say. So why am I fightin’ alongside a man whose ass I wanna kick? Because I ain’t got a choice in the matter. NA Pride is in my heart... the honor of
bein’ NA defines me, in a very real way. Why am I fightin’ alongside Styles? ‘Cuz there ain’t no other place I should be. As for our opponents? You two boys have signed the wrong contract. Ya signed somethin’ that’s gonna bring ya a whole world of pain. Before its all said and done, you two will be wishin’ you’d signed up for a stint in the Israeli army, instead of tryin’ to fight Styles and me.
Kinsey... I dunno much about you. Hell, I ain’t ever heard of ya before. And maybe that makes ya a wild card. Or maybe, and my guess is, more likely, that just means I’m gonna have the pleasure of beatin’ the hell outta someone I just met. Don’t go thinkin’ that bein’ unknown is in your favor. The only thing it means is I’ll have to ask you your name before I
Firebomb ya to hell.
And Slater? Don’t make me laugh. I’ve been in the same locker room as
Slater. And I know, without a doubt, there ain’t nothin’ left of the man.
You’re a guy who’s livin’ off glory no one on the whole damn planet remembers. You’re makin’ your annual attempt at a comeback. I’m gonna do ya a favor and put ya outta your misery once and for all.
You two boys don’t stand a chance. No one does, when they’re fightin’ two people who used to be NA. The only thing you boys can look forward to is a serious lesson in pain. That, and gettin’ your asses...
BURNED!!! [Fade to Black] ============================================= *************** * Johnny Axis * *************** [Stepping out from stage right is a rerun of the previous promotional video seen last week. Yes, it's Johnny Axis. Once more the Axis-Man is before the WTR backdrop, which hangs on a cinderblock wall. Oh yeah, he's got on the "Media Whore" black T-Shirt with white print. He's got his yellow-lensed sunglasses. He's stylin' and profil-er, professional wrestlin'...
(Whoops, almost ripped off the Nature B-- uh, nature guy. Y'know, that park ranger? Heh. Heh. Anyway...)
Regardless, Johnny's lookin' good. But pissed. Good and pissed. Yeah, that pretty much covers it.]
JA: Okay, that's it. This is stupid. This ain't my bag, dig? [Turning towards the tarp behind him (not in front of him), Johnny shows off the back of his shirt, which reads "Evil man, Evil empire".]
JA: First of all, I'd like to officially voice now my objection to the setup of this show! This is _not_ my normal working environment!
[Johnny turns back from the generic Weekend to Remember tarp. A LOOK OF
... mild annoyance in his partially obscured eyes (you can still see through the shades. Duh).]
JA: I mean, gawd! Generic _and_ contrived? The Axis-Man needs a crowd! [RIPPING down the backdrop tarp, Johnny reveals an INCREDIBLE ... white cinderblock wall. Well, yeah, we all knew that was there.]
JA: Okay, lemme see here... [Withdrawing a can of black spraypaint from a pocket, Johnny starts to, essentially, draw all over the wall.]
JA: Okay, dig this. Here's a racetrack. Here's the crowd. And over here ... we got those guys that say "he went around the damn road again, geez, this is boring".
We call them "announcers", boys and girls. [The artwork isn't exactly DaVinci, or Van Gogh, Picasso ... hell, it isn't even Bob Ross (hey, shaddup, Ross was cool!), but it gets the job done. Now Johnny has a proper backdrop!]
JA: OKAY! Cue the cheers! [MASSIVE ORGASMIC RIDICULOUSLY LOUD FACE POP THAT JUST ROCKS THE WHOLE
FREAKIN' PLACE! The screen actually shakes. Johnny obviously put some thought into this gag...
But wait, Johnny's a bad guy now. Why's he getting cheered? Eh, does it matter?] JA: Now THERE is the in crowd! The crowd that cheers the Center of it
All. Those ever-lovin' true fanboys and worshippers of the Age of Axis!
Now... The Almighty Hype Machine has come to drop a little knowledge on all y'all's skulls~! It's not a commonly known fact, but the Axis-Man's all about teachin' the little Axis-Freaks when he's not puttin' some unworthy asses' fool head through the canvas. Damn skippy.
Lesson one: Apex. [Johnny clears his throat, coughs into a clenched fist, and gestures with his can of spray paint.]
JA: Apex ... Apex. What is an Apex? Most people don't even know. Way to be obscure, bitchboy.
Lemme just take a second to define "Apex". An apex, not Ape X, which is
"Planet of the Apes"' version of Malcolm X, is the tightest turn you can turn 'round a corner when you're driving a car. Or something like that.
So this guy, he calls himself "Apex". This Apex, he worships some guy named "Corners". Apex corner... Apexing a corner is turning 'round a corner as sharply as you can.
So is Apex ... a racecar driver? [Putting a hand to his goatee, Johnny looks as if he thinks he's on to something. Yes, indeed, Apex is a racecar driver! Okay, well, he's not that stupid. But hey, it was funny, okay!? Just to illustrate, Johnny spraypaints a tiny, rough drawing of a car into the scene, with a huge word "APEX" to it's upper right, an arrow pointing at the vehicle itself.]
JA: Thinking about it, I became of Apex' existence at about the same time that Dale Earnhardt _died_, so maybe, like Elvis, Dale didn't die, he just moved into a different profession. While the illustrious Mr
Presley moved on to push brooms as a Janitor in Buffalo, New York along with Marilyn Monroe and James Dean, Dale became Apex, and now gets beaten half to death every week by big ugly men in the World Wrestling
Organization! BOOYA! My investigative skills surpass Sherlock Holmes! Or John Holmes... Hell. Pick one. *cough* [Chuckling, and it's even more painfully obvious that even Johnny isn't taking this one seriously. Total mock. You betcha.]
JA: But Sherlock never existed, and John is dead. So screw it, right? That's how the man made his living. Also how he died ... no blood getting to his brain and all that.
REGARDLESS! [Another cough. Johnny smoothes down his mustache and goatee, checks his pockets for additional cans of paint, and yes, he does adjust his jock.
Damn, sometimes it just won't sit still.] JA: After watching some tapes of Apex material, one thing has become painfully clear. He's a moron! Seriously, he talks to an imaginary friend, or maybe just a figment, who the hell cares? You can't see, hear, smell, taste (not that you'd wanna taste a zombie, but still), or touch the sumbitch, but we're supposed to believe he's there?
What's more, he takes some of the worst shots in wrestling today, only to lose repeatedly! He'd make a great lackey for the Axis-Man, if he weren't being such a damn roadblock on my path to glory. But you can not stop the man with the whole world in his hands! No you can't!
Ape X, seriously, buddy, you and me, we could be pals, were things a little different. As it stands, you're a big 90, a right angle, while the Almighty Hype Machine is the three-sixty. One full rotation.
You're two seventy short of one round trip, bitchboy, and BOOYA, it's a long way from Brazil to Paris.
The crux of the matter is this. Without a good set of tires, an Apex is crap. But the whole world rotates on an Axis ... and you are _no_ different.
Listen close, eat it, breathe it, CHOKE ON IT ... live it, because the Axis-Man is bigger than God. Damn skippy. [Turning back to face the cinderblock wall, Axis shakes his can of paint. Fade to black as Johnny sprays himself into the scene on the back wall, overlapping the whole race course in a big, black face. End.]
