"Kodiak Cop" Maxwell Creed ********** * Ketiak * ********** Title: Peregrinatio (Latin for 'journey') [Scene opens to the streets of a random American metropolitan city in the daytime. We see what is expected from any random American metropolitan city in the daytime - sprawling square-miles, towering skyscrapers, plenty of traffic both vehicle and human, pollution of all forms and levels, drowned in an ocean of honks and curse-words. In the midst of it all, we see a camera following - albeit with difficulty due to the the crowd - the footsteps of an ambling
Ketiak. We see Ketiak walking at a quasi-lumbering pace, not too fast, but not slow enough to incur the wrath of his fellow walkmates. We then hear a voiceover tinged with weariness. The gruff tones, the staccatoed sighs, the voice of a disillusioned Ketiak...]
V/O: This is a walk of allegory. Every step I take is but a manifestation of the loneliness gripping my heart, a representation of the stirring demons inside my soul. I walk to escape. But, somehow, I am inexplicably resigned...
Convinced that I am walking straight into the Forest of Fear, but I cannot bring myself to care. I hear the heartcries of the people surrounding me. I take deviant joy in the workings of their tormentor Tribal Madness . Alas, my meagre pleasures are brief, as I am cruelly reminded that my suffering is greater than those of them. Oh wreched demons of my soulpit! So much acute is my pain. Like an ad-infinitum inferno.
[Ketiak walks faster than before, as if evading something or someone. If it was the pesky cameramen he could be successful, as they find it progressively difficult to keep up with him. After a few tiring moments, Ketiak pauses and his gaze turns to the opposite right side of the road. Crossing it, we then see the object of his focus - a restaraunt named 'Little Penang Cafe'. Ketiak then hesitates for a brief second before gracing the entrance.
The short balding Chinese proprietor greets Ketiak with a hint of familiarity in his voice, and Ketiak enters. We see the minimalistic and cramped interior of the place, square tables circles by four square chairs. Its sole spice lies in the wall decorations of a China junk miniature, a carved statue of an laughing Buddha sitting cross-legged and a mini-Malaysian flag propped in sponge placed on the counter. Ketiak sits on one of the squared chairs, and a conversation is started.]
Proprietor: You from Malaysia ar? Ketiak: Yep. I'm from Sabah. Proprietor: Walauweh. Sabah a bit the far from where I come from lar... Ketiak: (Interrupts with a smile) Penang right? Proprietor: Yup, korek. A bit obvious from the kopitiam's name and all huh? Anyway, my name's Ah Niu. Ketiak: My name's Ketiak. Ah Niu: Oh, you the UEW superstar right? I like wrestling a lot lar, can put people through tables. You pretty good leh. Make our nation proud. Malaysia boleh! (OOC: Malaysia can!)
Ketiak: (With a hint of amusement) Hehe, can take my order now? Ah Niu: Oh solli solli, just happy to see a fellow Malaysian. Haiya, these
Westerners all bloody ignorant one, think Malaysia is in Africa or something.
So bodoh... (OOC: That's Malay for friggin' idiotic -_- ) Ketiak: Ok man. (Smiles a smile of finality) I would like a Char Kuey Teow and a Teh Tarik (OOC again: Feel free to contact me on AIM to find out what are they) please. Quick arr, I don't have all day.
Ah Niu: Okay boss... Will cook best food for you. [As the proprietor is both waiter AND cook, he rushes into the kitchen. As business is not exactly booming these days, Ketiak is in a state of solitude.
Even in its briefness, Ketiak takes the intervallic time and starts pondering again...] V/O: It's good to experience the comforts of home somewhat. A whiff of paradise in the purgatory that is punishing me. Punishing me for my sole iniquity - for being tormented endlessly and yet not going insane from the pain. But now the constant floggings had got to me, for I can see vaguely into the realm of madness. I can see spectres of my past, the souls whom I had destroyed on my way on the road to glory. They are haunting me, they are demanding compensation for the terrible things I'd inflicted on them.
But why should I? Isn't a lifetime of anguish a small price to pay for all the hurt I had caused to those that oppose me? The pleasures are temporary at best, but who is to blame me for being a helpless hedonist? Arrgghh... Tribal
Madness is a sneaky seductress, docile would I face the consenquences in the morning as I enjoy the night...
[Thankfully, Ketiak's thoughts are interrupted by the aroma of fried flat noodles and the borderline irritating chirp of the perpetually cheerful Ah Niu.
He gobbles down his food as gasistromic pleasure displaces mental anguish. 10 minutes later, he is done. Saying his Malaysian goodbyes (selamat tinggal, God blezz ya!), he then pays his bill and exits, continuing in his pilgrimage to nowhere.]
V/O: From solace to wretched reality once again. The concrete jungle. An environment that the Malaysian Madman had adapted to rather easily. Yet, since that terrible kidnapping a month ago, the lack of peace in this place had taken even greater amplication. Every car-honk a threat, every curse-word a warcry, every person walking the streets a denizen of the Enemy. Thus, I block my mind from such matters. Repression may not be an answer, but I wish to stifle what is inevitable.
[In his directionless walking, Ketiak than switch his focus to the north and continues on. We see a change of looks on his face, and his grimace fades into a frown.]
V/O: And on to more important matters - Weekend to Remember... A match against the one known as the Spectre... One who recieves pain with pleasure...
One who does just know evil, but simply is the icarnation of it... Am I able to overcome such odds? What had happened to the Ketiak of old? The Ketiak where questions were not asked and answers of annhilation were given with nary a concern? Is this called wisdom? Is this called the demise to foolish youth?
Perhaps it is. Perhaps caution is a symbol of maturity. Then again, perhaps not. For perhaps Tribal Madness is all that is needed to bring upon bloodshed.
Perhaps the depraved pleasures of beating your fellow man to a pulp is all that is needed to bring upon excellence. Perhaps the sounds of bones breaking and voices of hopelessness are all that is needed to spur me on - to kill, steal and destroy...
[A small snarl develops on Ketiak's face, passerbys are shocked and move out of his way. Far away. Ketiak walks faster and faster, even to a pace of a jog.
Repressed feelings seemingly emerge, and his steps become erratic.] V/O: I can feel the demons calling inside me. I can feel the key to that prison turning by an unseen hand. I don't want the hand to move, but as it goes against my will, I feel a sense of serenity. I see the movement around me slowing down like the freeze frame of a movie. I sense the prison being open, and I feel those repressed feelings coming forth. I am but helpless, as the caged beast within me starts to take form, manifested in this frail body...
Yet I feel invinsible. I feel an urge to kill, to cause somebody pain, to revel in their tears.
[Ketiak takes an alarming detour to one of the city's seedy districts, he then take another turn into one of its manifold dark alleys. From the corner of our eyes we see a dishrevelled old man, and he is a picture of a generic homeless man, with tattered clothes and impoverished frame. He runs to the direction of
Ketiak, not knowing of the danger befalling him.]
Bum: Can you spare me some cutter? [Ketiak stops and stares into his direction. He clutches his fist and clenches his teeth and just stands unspeaking.]
Bum: Can you spare me some CUTTER? [Without provocation Ketiak nails a haymaker onto the poor man's jaw. The man lets out a painful yelp before falling to the ground. Ketiak then takes him up by the collar and smashes him down again with a flurry of punches. Ketiak than kick him and stomp on him as if he was a mudhole.]
V/O: I can feel this man's innocence, his only sin being poverty. But who am
I to judge? Who am I to determine who would live and who would die? Who am I to decide if my fellow WTR wrestlers deserve this heinous a fate?
[The bum's bones break, leaving sickening sounds in tandem with Ketiak's uncertain thoughts... His screams for help a facade for Ketiak's growing sentiments.]
V/O: In fairness, I shall proclaim that everyone I face would get equal suffering. Who said the Malaysian Assassin cannot be just? Come WTR I will unleash the Beast - consciousless, bloodlustful, and malovelent. The Beast which is possesing my body now.
[Ketiak throws a few final jabs for good measure. The bum lies still but still breathing, helplessness for a kind soul to come by. Ketiak sees his handiwork, and prostrates before the ground from all his sweaty effort. We hear a small laugh bubbling forth from his crossed hands covering his bowed head. Ketiak's head rises, and he is laughing manically, finding mirth in the proceedings.]
V/O: Oh what joy to do the work of the Beast Within. Come WTR, everyone in that ring... Their hearts are mine... Their souls are mine... Their heads are mine...
[With a goodbye kick to the homeless man Ketiak then walks briskly back to his hotel. The journey comes full circle as the scene at last fades to black...]
=========================================================== *********** * Spectre * *********** [The scene opens on a closeup of an hourglass. The the dark oak frame stands a foot long from end to end, and the sands run steadily into the lower chamber of the timepiece, inexorably filling it. The camera shifts focus to reveal the macabre visage of The Spectre staring intently at sands...a faraway look in his eyes. The camera pans, revealing that the dreadlocked man is currently in a bare room. A single lightbulb hangs from a wire in the ceiling, illuminating the enclosure. The hourglass itself rests upon a single stool, which is the only furnishing apparent in the bunker-like room. After a moment, the ghoulish wrestler picks up the hourglass and begins to nonchalantly hold it in his left hand as he paces around the bare room.]
"Time time time...tick tock, tick tock. Our date with our destiny approaches. Friend Ketiak has consulted dreams...he has called upon his Tribal Madness for strength. He has told us that we shall be dust upon the completion of our battle with him.
That may well be. [Spectre gestures with the hourglass] As the sands remind us, the life is draining out of each and every person in the world while we speak to the unblinking eye of the camera...ourselves included. Ketiak has promised we would be made unto dust, and we shall be eventually.
This is true. Does this rule us? Does this frighten us? Hardly. We've accepted what our
Beast demands. It demands blood. It demands pain. It demands life. Our own as well as that of others. We have no problem meeting that demand. We relish every moment of it. We live for nothing else.
Many make such claims. Many claim that they fear nothing. They desire us to prove our statements...asking us if we think it's easy to do so..."
[The Spectre pauses for a moment, a speculative look crossing his face...then, without warning, he grips the hourglass with both hands and rams the glass sand resiviour hard into his forehead, shattering it, and sending sand flying everywhere. As he discards the shattered frame, a line of blood begins to steadily flow down his forehead, ranging in between his eyesocket and the right side of the bridge of his nose. Slowly the crimson stream curls around the right side of The Spectre's mouth, and begins to form droplets off of his jaw.]
"Yes. It is JUST...THAT...EASY. Ketiak, our pain and discomfort means nothing to us. Our Beast will be served. At Weekend to Remember, we WILL break you.
We will show you what it is to face a true predator. Your dreams, your visions, your tricks. None of them will help you."
[Spectre pauses for a moment, collecting a droplet of blood with his right index finger. He holds the crimson teardrop between the camera and himself for a moment, looking at it with an almost lazy fascination.]
"Prepare yourself. Prepare yourself to be punished for the lies that you have perpetrated. For your claims of understanding the nature of a Hunter.
Prepare, friend Ketiak... [The Spectre swats the lightbulb with the back of his left hand, sending it swinging on its wire, and crashing into the ceiling...plunging the room into an inky blackness...]
"Fear the Dark." =========================================================== ******************* * Throm the Black * ******************* (We open to a dense forest. It is daytime and the sun streaks down through openings in the canopy overhead, as if the very gods themselves are striking out from the heavens. In the forest Pine, Oak and Ash trees are common, all of them old, if not ancient. It is quiet. Many of these trees have witnessed times of war and peace, men, women and children living, loving and dying.
Etching out a life for themselves within the forest, living off the land…each person meaningful, yet fleeting within this passage of time.)
(We catch a glimpse of movement, black, snorting. Hiding in the shadowed undergrowth. It moves again…a Boar. It moves slowly through the forest, grazing upon the grass in the quiet of the day. Alert, its head lifts, sniffing the air. Suddenly the Boar bounds out from the undergrowth, sensing something nearby…danger…fear rises within the beast. Must get away…hunter…)
(As the Boar races forward we see a blur. Grey, reflecting the suns rays as it moves through the air. The Boar forms a balls, rolls and then lies still.)
(The camera cuts and a figure clad in black steps out from behind a tree. You can tell that it is a man from his build. Tall and muscular he is dressed in black from head to toe. Black pants, Boots and a long black, hooded robe. He moves forward and kneels by the Boar. As he does so he folds down the hood revealing golden blonde hair that shines in the errant suns ray. The camera moves overhead and we see him retrieve his weapon. It is a Hammer. Grey stone with a brown leather handle.)
(This weapon gives away the identity of the man. The shoulder length golden hair and the azure blue eyes. A face wise, yet still young and features strong, as if carved from stone. This man is a Warrior and a Hunter. This man is Throm The Black.)
Throm - It be a good day for the hunt, for I shall eat well this night. Swift is the Boar, and vicious yet swift, nor vicious was not good enough. For as always happens, sooner or later the Hunter catches his prey, the strong overcomes the weak and the Warrior transcends the men whom surround him. Thus shall it be when I go to battle. A battle where the victories achieved will echo throughout the land. Where mighty warriors will perform great deeds and the warriors amongst men shall stand tall. I shall be one of those Warriors.
The battle will be most aptly named "Weekend to Remember 2". (Throm then picks up and tucks away Mjollnir, his War-Hammer. He walks a few paces to a break in the trees and then the camera cuts to look over his shoulder. He is looking out over a cliff drop that falls down to a river far below. Stretched out in front of him is a valley, thickly forested and home to all varieties of hunting game.)
Throm - For now some of my adversaries have broken their silence and I know a little of what I face. Each battle clan has sent out its finest warriors…all of whom must now learn the meaning of defeat. They must march forward despite the fear in their bellies and the uneasiness in their bowels and they must fight in a civil war. For they now know that they cannot overcome the combined might of my allies, Mjollnir and I.
Throm - First there is the Clan of Whoresons, hailing from a battleground that goes by the name of the MVW. Only two of their number have spoken…yet from these two I have drawn what I need to know. Dante Madison…a whelp. His courage is refreshing, yet Weekend to Remember 2 will prove to be a harsh lesson on his path to glory. For just as the strong overcome the weak so does the Alpha Male force down his younger rivals. Dante, heed mine words for they fortell thy fate. Turn away from this battle. Turn away and save thyself the beating…for young as thy is, tis not right that thy marches to battle unaware of the savagery that will be unleashed upon thee. Consider thyself warned…for whenst we meet, t'will not be as a master and student…as thy could learn a lot from a Warrior such as I, but as adversaries. And as adversaries I can show thee no mercy…tis sad, yet I will do what I must to achieve victory. And when I look to thy ally I see not a warrior but a coward.
Jason Patrick. Here is a man built upon words, not substance. He does not have it within him to achieve great deeds and thus will mine words prove truth in battle. For whereas I stand tall as a warrior destined for honour and glory, to finally rest in Odin's hallowed hall, thee walks a different path.
Thee walks a path that takes thee not to victory but to a most dishonourable defeat. Thy words will not prove to be thy armour, nor thy weapons…for thee has no chance of surviving the power of The Black One and his warriors.
Especially so as thy other allies have been silent…scared into silence. I smell thy fear Donovan Keltrane and Scott Sexton. I smell thy fear and I hunger for thy defeat.
Throm - But these two Whoresons are not the only one's to have spoken out against The Black One.
(Throm then holds Mjollnir and hefts it in his hand) Throm - Art Blake. I heard thy words Art Blake and I look upon them and thee with distain. Thy scorned The Black One and made jests that Mjollnir may be socially impractical…yet smirk not for thee was right. Battle is not for the weak at heart, nor for the socially correct. For I have seen thee, in thy clean suits and attending thy social clubs. Tis sheep like thee that fall victim to Wolves like me. For thee understands not the way of the warrior, nor the path to glory. Thee understands only "afternoon Tea and biscuits"
(The words sounding somehow strange and foreign coming from Throm) Throm - When thee and I meet in battle thy will wish that the days of golf courses and martini's could last forever. Thee is unaccustomed to the viciousness of war and the savagery of the battle that I will wage against thee. Thee is destined for a nightmare ride…mark my words.
(Throm continues to heft Mjollnir and look out over the panoramic view) Throm - Then we go from Weakling to Whelp. Dante Madison is not the only whelp to partake in this battle. Burt Clifton…still living with thy mother. How in the name of the Gods does thy expect to contend with me. Thee is hardly a man let alone a warrior. When thee steps into battle t'will be a hard yet necessary lesson in the way of the warrior. I shall teach thee pain then I shall teach thee defeat. For thee has not a chance in Hades of competing upon the same level as I. Thy only chance of salvation rests in the valor of thy compatriots…Jarred Matthews and the "Messiah" Ray Hagen and they fear me so that they do not speak. They fear their demise at the hands of The Black One.
Matthews a craven coward and Ray Hagen…a silent messiah…Craven dogs…I shall savour their defeat and yet it shall come to pass…so I fortell it to be.
(Throm then gets a more intense look upon his face.) Throm - However…there be one faction that may prove trouble…those Heathens that hail from the Battle Clan of the AVW. But as The Black One fortold so too shall they be vanquished.
Throm - Jake Davids and Leevon Parks…silent as the fallen hero's they will become. Their silence tells a tale. A tale of fear and cowardice the like of which has not before been seen…except for the other cravens I face. Yet for the two whom did speak out I look down upon thee. As The Black One said, so
Laramee has to be dragged into battle kicking and screaming, like a scared child. Thee thinks to face me in battle, child?…thee thinks that thy stands a chance…a child against the mightiest of warriors? Thee is mistaken if thee thinks that thy will escape unscathed. For thy road is long and hard…thy reward a useless and bleeding body…a spirit broken. Tis unfortunate that thee must face thy executioner…unfortunate.
(Throm is then silent for a few moments before continuing) Throm - ...Max Pain. Other than mine ally Solo and I, thee stands tall as a warrior. Yet from thy words I sense dissention in thy ranks. The Black One has seen thy weakness and it is a weakness that I shall exploit, as the master exploits the slave. The infighting within thy camp will prove to be thy undoing. Tis a bad omen for thy chances in this battle, for t'will take more than a lone effort to win this day and thy independence is thy weakness. Thee says that thy opponents will…
(The camera then focuses on Throms eyes) Throm - FEEL THE PAIN…well in battle, pain is a given. Pain is expected…and oft times pain is welcome. It tells a man that he is alive.
Pain is fleeting…justice is final and I bring thee justice, Max Pain. I am the hand of justice itself and I will not rest till thee has experienced justice and defeat in the same night. For against a Lone Wolf such as myself thy stands a small chance…but against mine allies and Mjollnir…thee can consider thy battle lost.
(Throm then stares out once more…his gaze seeming to lose focus as he thinks) Throm - Aye…tis good to sing songs of battle and tell tales of glorious victories. For such will mine allies and I do. Solo…the modern day warrior.
Unmatched in battle and no equal exists that can match him for viciousness…except maybe mineself.
Throm - Zan "The Miracle" Morningstar. This young fighter fights for a true cause…an honest cause…with a heart as pure as his we cannot falter.
Throm - and HeLLfiRe. Although he remains silent he waits…he broods over the destruction that he shall unleash. For no other human is as HaRdCoRe as this man…when in battle with him, anything goes.
Throm - And finally there is The Black One. The Lone Wolf…enforcer of Justice and defender of truth. I shall not waiver in the face of adversity for battle makes one strong…and after a battle such as this, I shall be very, very strong indeed.
Throm - I wish thee luck adversaries of mine for as sure as I stand here…thy gods have forsaken thee. Pitted against The Black One in battle thy see me…The victorious warrior standing tall amongst thee. Thee will see that true warriors live by a code, the code of the warrior…where in battle…
Throm - THERE IS NO QUARTER ASKED… Throm - AND Throm - NONE Throm - GIVEN!!! (Fade to Black) =========================================================== TO BE ADDED TO THE MAILING LIST E-MAIL [detail withheld]
