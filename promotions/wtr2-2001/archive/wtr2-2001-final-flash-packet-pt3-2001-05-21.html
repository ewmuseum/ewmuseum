IT'S A SMALL WORLD AFTER ALL!!! IT'S A SMALL WORLD AFTER ALL!!! IT'S A SMALL...SMALL....WORLD!!! [Oh yeah. C'mon...the Weekend 2 Remember is taking place in Orlando. You didn't think Laramee would pass up on a chance to go to Disneyworld, did you? And yes, there he is...sitting on the boat that travels through the
"small world" ride. He's swinging his head back and forth...singing along, while wearing those Mickey Mouse ears on his head. Sitting next to him on the ride is everyone's favorite AVW interviewer, Mike Lewis. He looks like he's far from enjoying himself, and keeps checking his watch. Finally, the extremely annoying ride comes to an end, and Laramee and Lewis exit the ride, with the camera close behind. As they make their way past screaming kids,
Lewis asks a question...]
ML: LAR...can we get on with your Weekend 2 Remember interview NOW? You keep putting it off...and so far we've rode the teacups, the dumbo planes, Splash
Mountain, and Small World.
[Lar looks at him with puppy dog eyes.] LR: Please Mr. Lewis...just _one_ more ride? We can cut the interview while waiting in that insanely long line...
ML: Alright, alright. [Lar and Lewis walk past the old married couples, the screaming kids, and the teenagers who are up to no good. Laramee stops to point out everything they pass to Lewis, who would rather be anywhere then here with Laramee. Finally, the two settle into a _HUGE_ line, with no end in sight. The people in front of them, a middle aged couple, don't seem to like the fact there's a camera behind them, getting them in the shot. Laramee is grinning like an idiot of course.]
ML: Man...this really isn't fair. Sampson makes me fly down here on the same plane as you, makes me stay in the same room as you...and now, this. Can we please just get on with this interview?
LR: [While looking towards the end of the endless line.] Man...what ride is this, anyway? [Turns toward Lewis] Sure Mr. Lewis, ask away.
ML: First off...teamwork is going to be important in this. Have you spoken to your three teammates at all?
LR: Well... [Lar looks somewhat embarrassed.] ...I tried. I was going to walk into Mr.Parks' hotel room, but there were...some, uh....funny sounds coming from it. I didn't want to disturb...whatever was going on in there. Then I tried Mr. Davids...and
I did get a "hi" in before he told me I wasn't worthy of his prescience.
So, no, not really. ML: What about Max Pain? [Lar laughs out loud.] LR: Sorry Mr.Lewis...but I like my neck. Now, I'm sure Mr.Pain is really a good guy...but, he scares me. [Lar looks back toward the line.]Is a line that long...legal?
ML: Well...what your your opponents? The teams from the SEWF, MVW, and the nWa? They've all had some statements concerning _your_ team...what are thoughts on the opposing teams?
[Lar rubs his chin.] LR: Listen Mr.Lewis, I'm going to be honest with you. They all scare the hell out of me. Now, we've got team Sewf...
ML: That's S-E-W-F. Not "sewf." LR: Right, right. First off, I've got to say to all the SEWF wrestlers...I love your fed! I'm a _HUGE_ fan! I watch your weekly shows.
I order those Pay Per Views of yours _all_ the time... [Lewis clears his throat. He smells a rat. When he speaks, he does so sarcastically...] ML: Right Lar, you're a big SEWF fan. So...what's the name of their weekly show? You know...the one you watch _ALL_ the time? Come to think of it, do you even know who their top champion is?
LR: [Yelling.]MY GOD THIS LINE IS HUGE!! ARE WE EVER GOING TO REACH THE RIDE?? [Lewis smirks. In front of Laramee, the couple throws him a dirty look for yelling.] ML: Ummmhmmm...kinda what I thought. Fine...we've got team MVW. A contender to defeat you guys if their ever was one.
LR: MVW? "Mohawk Valley Wrestling?" Great! Another "Valley" promotion! They'll have to go easy on me...er, us...won't they? C'mon, us valley boys have to stick together! You know, if Mr.Sampson wouldn't get mad at me for it, I would wear my MVW T-Shirt to the Civil War match.
ML: Oh right...that shirt. What's on the front again? [Lar curls his lip and folds his arms. He gives Lewis a look to kill for about ten seconds. Then, he moves on.]
LR: Last one...nWa. nWa...man, that sure sounds familiar. ML: I would hope it does. You were once in that fed, remember? [Lar's brow wrinkles, the gears turn, sweat rolls off his forehead...then,
BINGO!] LR: OH YEAH!! [The people in front of Laramee and Lewis are getting extremely annoyed at what's going on behind them. One of them, a middle aged male, stomps out of line, angrily.]
LR: That's the hang out of my old buddy, Ethan! Yeah, me and him are tight. He's not going to see this, is he? [Lewis shrugs] Well, my nWa problems are over, that's for sure. I'll just give him a ring, he'll make sure those guys don't lay a finger on me. Man...I've got it made.
[Lar grins with his goofy grin...as someone taps him on the shoulder. He turns around...to face a security guard. The security guard makes a "come with me"
motion, and Laramee refuses.] SG: Listen sir, we've received a compliant about your disturbing this line. Your going to have to come with me. [The security guard becomes more forceful...and Laramee makes a run for it! As he runs, he yells "fight the corporate giant!! Disney's crooked!" The security guard chases him, wielding a can of mace. Lewis shakes his head, and turns to the camera.]
ML: Well...as they hunt down and mace Lar, this is Mike Lewis, signing off for the Weekend 2 Remember.
[As the camera begins it fade, we see Laramee running in the background. He's smiling because he's avoiding the big security guards. What he doesn't expect, however, is a Mickey Mouse mascot to jump out of nowhere, and tackle him down.
The mouse pins him to the ground, as the security guards move in...and we...] [Bade to Flack.] =========================================================== ****************************** * "Mr. Flawless" Jake Davids * ****************************** [Before the scene is set sound can be heard.....the gentle crashing of waves as they make contact with a non-moving object. . .]

[Jake smirks and slides off his shades revealing piercing baby blue eyes. . .] "MF"JD: With that comes it all.....Comes the ability to waltz in here and degrade the others that think they have a shot.....the ability to walk in and make this thing a true Week End To REMEMBER. =========================================================== ************ * Max Pain * ************ [ The scene opens to the outside of a building. The camera pans over to the windows on the front of the building. They are covered in a gray hazy colour, but by looking inside, you see people sitting at tables, and a bar in the far background. The camera then focuses on the door, which has a sign on the window that says…" River Rat Bar. ENTER AT YOUR OWN RISK!! " The cameraman pushes open the door, and it begins to creak as it opens. All the people that were busy watching the TV or playing pool, or talking, stop and look at the intruder. The camera moves over to the barkeeper, who has his back to the crowd. As the cameraman does this, all eyes stay focused on the man. He comes up to the bar, but before he can say a word, the barkeep speaks. ]
Barkeep: I got two things to tell ya. First off, to stay here you must buy a beer, no questions asked.
[ The cameraman grabs some money and puts it down on the bar. The barkeep turns around, and puts the beer bottle down in front of him very hard. The barkeep then begins to polish one of the glasses, as the cameraman grabs the bottle, swearing under his breath. ]
Barkeep: My second thing is, if you are lookin’ for the big guy, he is over in that room. But I’d be careful if I were you. He is mighty ticked, and no one wants to bother him. Good luck.
[ The cameraman nods, and moves to the other room. He begins to think to himself, "What a moron. Does he not know what I do for a……"]
### WHOOSH ### [ The cameraman jerks the camera back, as he sees what flew by him to the left. It focuses on a dart board, which has a picture of 4 logos on it, and a dart in the middle of one. As the cameraman tries to gain his composure, he zooms in on the picture. It shows a pic of the logos of MVW, nWa, SEWF, and AVW. The dart that flew by, hit the pic of the MVW logo squarely in the middle of the picture of Keltrane, who is superimposed over the MVW logo. ]
MP: You are lucky you didn’t come a few minutes earlier. I wasn’t really aiming back then. [ The camera turns to the left, and see the seven foot phenom, Max Pain. Max is dressed in a black leather jacket, blue jeans, and his hair is tied back into a pony tail. He grabs a bottle of beer on the table near him, and takes a swig. He slams the bottle down on the table, and grabs another dart of the table. The camera man steps back and zooms in on Max. ]
MP: Smart man, not like the guys over there. What geniouses. First off, these guys open their mouth to talk, and that in turn is scary. I mean, if they said anything intelligent is an act of God. Keltrane, the dark character, I am supposed to be scared of your dark crap. Boy, I have faced scaring things eating at Taco Bell. And this team has two rookies, who are excited to be there. It is great you are happy to be there, ra, ra. The fun time is over, and I am telling you now, you will never forget this night, that is true. However, it will be the night that the AVW show you exactly what "excellence" really is capable.
[ Max starts playing with the dart. He flips it in the air, and catches it in his hand with the base in the hand, not drawing any blood. ]
MP: They say it is better to work in numbers, but could we not find someone more intelligent to face than SEWF. I mean, I understand that some of your guys would want to talk. I mean, hell, taking one look at me, would scare you [censor]. But, to face Davids, Parks, and [shakes his head], Milhouse, okay skip that last one. I mean, SWEF, one of your guys may not be able to get to the arena, if mommy doesn’t drive him. Clifton, are you sure we won’t
be going past your bedtime. I can’t believe this is the competition. I mean, the Messiah is supposed to be their best, their champ, and yet, he is about the same height as Milhouse. Sampsy, I am beginning to think that this was some sort of punishment. Oh well, at least, I get to shut these twits up. So
SEWF, I am not going to warn you again. You will find out what I am capable of, so I am telling you this, all your fates has been sealed, and the punishment you all will receive will be the worst any of you have ever seen.
I PROMISE YOU THAT!!!! [ Max grabs the dart in mid flip, and in one motion throws it to the dart board. ] ### SWOOSH### [ The camera moves to the board, and the dart lands right between the eyes of the picture of Clifton, who is on the SEWF logo. Max grabs his bottle of beer, and lifts it up from the table. ]
MP: Up next in the batch of three stooges is our local product, the nWa. Ohhhh, I am so scared. I am surprised that they are still around, as I heard that they were just exploding right off the map. I mean, I thought those guys would not be allowed on the plane, at least they will bomb out at this
PPV. I mean, hell, they are probably all wanted on America’s Most Wanted.
HeLLfiRe, who is still in search of a brain, and personality, never mind a disagreement with some street kids. This is Satan’s spawn, hell boy,
Milhouse has more anger in him when he doesn’t get his ice cream. KamBion, or the next Karate Kid. Boy, you can use your "wax on, wax off" to buff up my bike when you get to the arena. Actually, I think Parks might be able to give you some better lessons on "wax on, wax off". Then Mr. Speak Loudly, and Get a Little Stick. Mighty Mouse, I thought you were still stuck on cartoon land. You are talking about heart, and trust. Boy, you won’t find someone else who will live up to that name. You know what I am capable of, and what I can do. You have even faced my wrath, so I maybe you think you got bigger marbles now that you are the big [censor] in the nWa. Like a famous person once said…"I eat pieces of [censor] like you for breakfast.
But Mr. Da Mang himself, Solo. Are you sure what side of you we will meet this time. You change personality more times these Texans have meals in one day. Solo, I think you forgot how I almost crippled your ass back in the good old days. Last I left you, you were trying to get my left boot out of your ass. But that’s okay I just shove the other one so that you have a matching pair.
[ Max takes a drink from his beer. ] MP: I am not playing anymore old man. You can play all these so-called mind games, or warfare you want. I don’t. I won’t sneak behind you like you do.
I will come straight at you and the rest of the AVW, with all my guns ablazin’. This cannon [ flexes his muscle ] and this bomb [ clinches his fist ] will take you out, and there will be no survivors. That I can
guaran-DAMN-tee you. [ Suddenly grabs a dart, in his other hand, off the table, and throws it quickly at the board. ]
### SWOOSH### [ The dart was thrown so hard that more than the point when through the pic of Solo, and that it obliterated the head. Max smiles as he puts his beer down on the table, and grabs the last dart. ]
MP: But my concern is greatest for my team. I mean, boys this is not a good sign. Our champ, Mr. Flawless has not been seen since I beat his ass on
Extreme. Davids, for this night, and this night only, I will work with you. Am I doing this for the AVW? You know that I only do things that benefit me. I am your insurance policy, as after this card, I am making sure that title will be mine for the taking. I don’t want you injured so I can’t take it.
So, think of me as your personal body guard. And Parks, are we still looking for those whores of yours. Hell, there has got to be some just around the corner of this bar. So, what the hell is the difference. You the right head of yours, and get into the game. Your ability is amazing, and hell, you can beat any of these twits, but forget about them broads. After this, you can do whatever you want. If not, and you become trouble, then I get rid of trouble realllllll quick. Your choice. And Milhouse? Ice cream?
You have got to be kidding me. You know what? I will take you to the Dairy
Queen of your choice, and I’ll buy you the largest sundae in the world. Does that sound good? And, you can celebrate during our victory party. Okay, so pick out the largest one, and it is yours. But, if you screw around at W2R, then I will make sure that all you will eat is ice cream…..THROUGH A

He throws it with anger and power. ] ### SWOOSH ### ### BANG ### [ The dart was thrown with such force, that it caused the board to fall to the ground. =========================================================== ******************************** * "Poison Youth" Kyojin Yakuma * ******************************** [Fade into the open evening atmosphere of Chiba Public Park Gymnasium in Tokyo, Japan. A few street lights illuminate the park as well as the full moon that looms high above in the night sky. Sitting inside the well sized gray wrestling ring that is placed in the middle of the park is a small 22 year old Japanese man. He corners himself against one of the turnbuckles and sits there quietly. His black dreadlocks drape in front of his face and disguise the rest of his facial appearance. He's attired in a pair of white on black camouflage pants, black combat boots, and a black t-shirt with the words "Eyes On Me" inscribed across the front in white lettering.
The leaves of the trees calmly sway back and forth and contribute to the soothing evening atmosphere. The young man begins to slowly speak in a thick and confused Japanese accent.]
Man: I'm guessing intelligence is not the game you play, Yamada. [He slowly combs away the black dreadlocks to reveal the facial appearance of one, Kyojin Yakuma. The white contact lens, the nasty red scar that bisects the left eye, the black eyeliner -- it's all in place.]
KY: Your weak ramblings of moron are embarrassing to say least. Your regardings to yourself are enough to make me vomiting. And to be frankly, you stand but little chance at winning a thumb war contest against me...
Let alone a wrestling match. [He slowly rubs his eyes and continues.] KY: Badness Genome? Boy, you are nothing but an annoying bastard. You're hiding your no talent with your stupidities. You, Yamada...
You're trying to run from the destiny. [Pause.] KY: You were lucky enough to being born the nephew of TORA Wanizame. If it weren't for him, you'd still be playing yourself in whatever high school it was you came from.
[After allowing himself a laugh, he continues.] KY: I am the reality check, Yamada. The genome of bad faces off against the youth of poison. It's almost as if poetry.
[The camera allows itself a slow zoom on Yakuma's face. His lips remain still and his lopsided looking eyes focused with intensity.]
KY: This ring will be soon surrounded by hundreds of fans. All of them,
Yamada, chanting the poison youth naming. They are no fools. You cannot trick them into be believing that you are the greater of men. They realize that I'm the only celebrated supernatural type wrestler entering in this match. You are merely of the coward bitch specimen that awaits defeat.
[He nods.] KY: It's of the destiny to step in the ring together. It's as if it was written in the book keeping of time that we would meet. The two of us stepping through these ropes...
[He extends a right arm and shakes the bottom rope that ties to the turnbuckle just to the right of him.]
KY: And one leaving a glorifying champion. That man, the best man, is the one who calls himself "Poison Youth".
[Yakuma stretches out his arms and slowly uses the ropes leverage to pull himself to his feet. As he does so, his head hangs downward and he quietly continues to laugh to himself. Fade out.]
=========================================================== ***************** * Kyohei Yamada * ***************** [Open to a rather large gym, furnished with a wrestling ring, punching bags, weight benches... the whole nine yards. Numerous young Japanese wrestlers are busily working away with their teachers. Some work with the bags, while others go through their grueling squat routines. Still, two more spar in the ring. The camera pans across the gym, towards the entrance, where a new face appears. His youthful, pockmarked face and his short black hair, with the front being grown out, spiked up, and dyed blonde, the new arrival is easily identifiable as "Bad Genome" Kyohei Yamada.
Yamada wears an old yellow Sex Pistols tank top and a pair of grey workout shorts. He also totes a black gym bag. Yamada purposefully walks past several young trainees, verging on exhaustion from performing countless squats, affording them little more than a condescending glance. Arriving at a corner of the gym, where a black dummy is propped up against the wall, Yamada stops and drops his gym bag. He bends down and begins unzipping the bag, not bothering to look up as he speaks.]
KY: Yakuma, who calls yourself "Poison Youth". We have battle coming soonly. Now is a time to make a special preparation.
[Yamada withdraws from the bag a long white fighting spirit style headband, with a red Japanese sun in the center. He ties the band tightly around his head, leaving two long ribbons to flow down his back. He now looks up at the camera, a hard, determined look in his eyes.]
KY: We can neither one give us up. The only choice is to fight our all. But who can manage to make great sacrifice? The sacrifice of all your energies into training and into the very fighting. One very approaching time to make our endless struggles!
[Yamada now turns from the camera and goes into a fighting stance, facing the dummy. He bows his head and begins to breathe very deeply and pronouncedly. After a long period of concentration, he explodes.]
KY: HEEEEEEEEEEEEE-YAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA!!! [With his rear foot, Yamada lashes out with a vicious kick upwards between the dummy's legs, right into the groin area with a force so hard that the dummy actually jumps off the ground several inches.
Several of gym's trainees look up in confusion as Yamada jumps up and down with glee.] KY: Yes! I am master! I know I can win! [Flashing a victory sign with one hand, and extending a middle finger with the other, Yamada gives the camera a crooked grin.
Fade out.] =========================================================== TO BE ADDED TO THE MAILING LIST E-MAIL [detail withheld]
