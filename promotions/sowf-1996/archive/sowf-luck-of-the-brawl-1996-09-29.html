Saturn and Cooter Lee Roy vs. Steve "The Slasher" Latini and Mailman Melvin 4. Zadfar and Rocky Reynolds vs. B.D. and Fact Man 5. Mike Doonesbury and Menace II Society vs. Kronus and Juggernaut 6. SOWF Television Title match: "The Sensation" Steve Simpson vs. American Warrior 7. SOWF United States Title Match: "The Machine" Kevin Chamber vs. Red Scorpion 8. SOWF Tag Team Title Match: Public Rudeness vs. Lucky Kid and QuickSilver 9. SOWF World Title match: Bishop vs. Dark Master MB: Welcome to Luck Of The Brawl! Welcome to San Antonio, Texas, and the
Alamodome! I'm Mike Buchanan and with me is Drew Vanover.
DV: Geeze! It's so loud in here I can't even hear myself brag! What's with everyone? MB: This is the Supreme Online Wrestling Federation's first appearnce in
San Antonio and everyone is hyped up.
DV: Well, I sure am. Tonight is going to be a night of surprises. Juggernaut makes his return, Menace II Society has to team up with Mike
Doonesbury, and there's going to be plenty more, I'm sure.
MB: Yeah. Luck of the Brawl may be the SOWF's pay-per-view that will provide the biggest surprises. Drew, what is your prediction for our opening match?
DV: I'd have to say Wildstar and Orbit. Wildstar has beaten Mark Destructo before, and Orbit almost got Bishop, SOWF World Champion, pinned for three. Ring Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, our first match for Luck of the
Brawl is a tag team match, scheduled for one fall. Coming down the aisle, with a combined weight of 597 pounds, is Mark Destructo and the Inferno
Kid! (mild pop)
DV: Geeze! Destructo is huge! MB: He is a monster, isn't he? DV: I would't go THAT far . . . B.D. is a helluva lot stronger. RA: And their opponents: with a combined weight of 520 pounds, Wildstar and Orville Ray Bob Tucker! (big pop)
DV: Look at Orbit . . . he's wearing a Budweiser cap! On Tuesday it was
Coors, wasn't it?
MB: Sorry, Drew. I don't pay attention to that. *****Mark Destructo and Inferno Kid vs Orville Ray Bob Tucker (Orbit) and Wildstar***** Destructo and Orbit start. As they go for a lock up, Orbit moves and
Destructo is in the corner. Orbit chops him. Whip to other buckle.
Armdrag takedown. Pulls Destructo up. Short-arm clothesline. Orbit runs ropes. Destructo gets up and knocks Orbit down. Pulls him up, powerbomb.
Tags in Inferno. Lifts him up over his head and throws him on Orbit, 2 count. Inferno leg drops him. Climbs to 2nd rope, tries to jump, but slips. Orbit climbs up, superplex. Tags in Wildstar. He hits Inferno with a superkick. Cover, 2 count. Tries the Sharpshooter, but Destructo comes in and shoves him off. Inferno rolls out of the ring. Destructo pulls Wildstar into the Destructo Backbreaker. Orbit comes in and flips
Destructo. Wildstar crawls over and tags Orbit. Orbit wraps Destructo into an abdominal stretch. Inferno comes in to break it up and Wildstar does a spinebuster on Inferno. Orbit does the STF on Destructo.
RA: Ladies and gentlemen, here are your winners: Orbit and Wildstar! (big pop) DV: The Trailer Park Assassins said they like butt-whomping matches . . . MB: Wait a second . . . our partner, Jack Masters has some news for us. JM: Mike, Drew, my news is about the next match. First, Steve Sampson has ended his SOWF career, and the other three people in the match, Axel
"The Ax" Frenchman, Starlette, and the Hitman have all been misrouted to the wrong airport . . . apparently that match just won't happen tonight.
DV: Aw, man! Sampson's gone! MB: And there is now one less match than planned. . . RA: Our next tag team is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, with a combined weight of 590 pounds, Saturn and Cooter Lee Roy (mixed reactions)
DV: I still can't believe Saturn has to team up with that hick . . . RA: Their opponents, with a combined weight of 535 pounds, w/ "The Referee" Stanley Cupp, is Steve "The Slasher" Latini and Mailman Melvin! (big pop) Steve "The Slasher" Latini emerges from a limo pulled up outside the Trump
Tower in New York City. He is met by a pack of journalists, all shouting questions in his direction.
Reporter #1: Mr. Latini, what are your plans for the Supreme Online
Wresling Federation? Latini: Plans? Plans? Lemme tell you something, sparky! The Slasher doesn't make plans, he makes promises! And I'm promising you one thing,
Steve Simpson. That SOWF Television Championship will be mine in the very near future! You can count on that! The Slasher is unbeaten in the SOWF, and I'm gonna stay that way, too! Simpson, the list starts with you, pal!
I'm gonna take that title from you, then it's Kevin Chamber's turn! I'm going after his belt, and then Bishop's World Title!
Reporter #2: But surely, Mr. Latini, you don't expect everyone else in the SOWF to roll over while you make a run for the titles?
Latini: My man, that would be the quickest, easiest and least painful way for the SOWF to give The Slasher what he wants, but no, it probably won't happen that way. The suits in the front office know the power I possess.
They know that if they cut The Slasher loose in this federation, I'll walk all over each and every person around! So they're gonna use lawyers and contracts and legal mumbo-jumbo to make The Slasher wait in line for the title shots!
Reporter #1: So do you have enough patience to "wait in line", as you say? Latini: Listen here, Elmo, if they make The Slasher wait,it's gonna do nothing but hurt themselves in the long run. Ya see, Simpson, for every week you make me wait for the title shot I deserve, it's another week I have to think about just how much I'm gonna make you suffer! Remember,
Simpson, I've got my eyes on you, and it won't be long before you get to look deep into my eyes and see your destiny - and that destiny is a big
NOTHING! Latini marches past the reporters and heads into the Trump Tower. *****Saturn and Cooter Lee Roy vs Steve "The Slasher" Latini w/"The Referee" Stanley Cupp and Mailman Melvin***** Saturn and Latini lock up. Saturn tries an Atomic Drop. Latini tries to reverse it, but Saturn gets if off first. Clothesline. He is still on his feet. He tries an ezuiguiri, but Latini ducks it. Tags in Melvin.
He pulls Saturn up and into the Mailbag Slam. RA: Ladies and gentlemen, here are your winners: Steve "The Slasher" Latini and Mailman Melvin! (big pop) DV: No way! That sucks! Saturn couldn't do anything in there! MB: He should've tagged in Cooter. DV: That's what I'm talking about. . . he couldn't tag him, they're on different teams. Saturn works well with Kronus and Kronus only!
MB: We'll see Kronus later on, teaming up with Juggernaut, who returns from suspension. DV: Finally . . . is Mr. Extreme STILL hurt? What a baby! MB: He'll be back in two weeks, and now, let's go to ringside . . . RA: This next tag team match is scheduled for one fall. Coming down the aisle, with a combined weight of 502 pounds, is Zadfar and Rocky Reynolds
(mixed crowd reactions) Their opponents, with a combined weight of 611 pounds, are Fact Man and B.D., along with Boopsie! (mixed reactions)
*****Zadfar and Rocky Reynolds vs B.D. w/ Boopsie and Fact Man***** B.D. and Zadfar start out. Lock up. He shoves Zadfar to the mat. Pulls him up, whips him. Zadfar tries a cross body, but B.D. catches him.
Suplex. Pulls him back up, powerbomb. Cover, 2 count. Whip to the buckle. Charges and hits a clothesline. Tags in Fact Man. He gorilla presses Zadfar. Whip, and he puts on his sleeper hold. Reynolds coms off the top rope with a double ax. Ref counts to 7, then Fact Man moves.
Zadfar suddenly pops up. He tags Reynolds, runs ropes, tries a crucifix, but Fact Man does a Samoan Drop. Tags in B.D. Chokeslam. Neckbreaker.
Cover. RA: Your winners: B.D. and Fact Man! (mixed reactions) MB: B.D., the first SOWF World Champion is climbing back up the ladder, and is looking for another title shot.
DV: He deserves it, too. He's worked the hardest to get a shot at it. Harder than ANYone in the SOWF. MB: He has worked hard, and I'm sure he'll get a shot soon. The next match is going to be the wildest one tonight, I think.
RA: Our next tag team match is scheduled for one fall. Coming down the aisle, with a combined weight of 428 pounds is Menace II Society and Mike
Doonesbury! (heel pop)
DV: Two great cruiserweights, but they hate each other . . . RA: Their opponents, weighing at 756 pounnds combined, are Kronus, and, making his return to the SOWF after his suspension, is Juggernaut! (Huge heel pop)
MB: the fans definitely don't want to see him back! *****Menace II Society and Mike Doonesbury vs Kronus and Juggernaut***** Juggernaut and Menace start out. Menace doesn't want to fight, so he tags
Doonesbury. As soon as Doonesbury comes toward Juggernaut he pounds on
Doonesbury. He falls into a corner and Juggernaut continues to pound on him. Whip to the other corner, and Juggernaut splashes on him. Tags in
Kronus. He gorilla presses Kronus onto Doonesbury, 2 count. Whip to the other buckle. Kronus chrages with a wheel kick, but Doonesbury ducks.
Ref counts to 9 before Kronus gets back in the ring. Tags in Juggernaut. Doonesbury tries a cross body, but he catches him and flattens him on the canvas, 2 count. B.D. walks down to ringside. Juggernaut sees him and gets distracted. Doonesbury drop kicks Juggernaut. Then he climbs the ropes and quickly does the Doonesbury Drop. Instead of covering, he tags
Menace in. Menace shoves Doonesbury. Doonesbury returns and punches
Menace. He charges and Doonesbury back-bodydrops Menace onto Juggernaut and the ref thinks it's a cover..
RA: Your winners: Menace II Society and Mike Doonesbury! (heel pop) MB: That was an interesting match and Menace and wanted no part of it. DV: That's smart, too. He didn't want to fight Kronus or Juggernaut, but it ended with him making the pin.
RA: The next match is scheduled for one fall, and is for the SOWF Television Championship! (pop) Introducing first, weighing 380 pounds, the challenger, American Warrior! (big pop)
MB: Any predictions for this one? DV: As much as I like Simpson and hate AW, I think there'll be a new T.V. Champion tonight! RA: His opponent, weighing 264 pounds, SOWF Television Champion, "The
Sensation" Steve Simpson! (heel pop)
*****SOWF Television Title Match: "The Sensation" Steve Simpson vs American Warrior***** As Simpson walks around the ring with the bellt, AW runs the ropes, tackle. Whip, big boot. Whip to the buckle, AW charges. Knee lift.
Tries another whip, reversed. Tries a back-bodydrop, but AW powerbombs him. Cover. RA: Here is your winner, a NEW SOWF Television Champion, American Warrior! (big pop) MB: American Warrior has won the T.V. Title! And on Tuesday Triumphs, he will get a World Title shot!
DV: Everything is rolling in American Warrior's way, and if he keeps this momentum going, he'll get the World Title.
MB: That is possible, but next is the United States Championship Match. RA: This next match is scheduled for one fall and is for the SOWF United
States Championship! (pop) first the challenger, weighing 227 pounds,
Red Scorpion! (mixed reactions) His opponent, weighing 288 pounds,
U. S. Champion, "The Machine" Kevin Chamber! (heel pop) Kevin Chamber is walking in front outside the arena . . . Chamber: Look at the shining star tonight! Gleaming, polished metal and smoothly oiled hydraulic presses, and they're going to take the latest person to step in the ring and turn him into a pile of mush! You want to know what that clicking sound is? It's the sound of my timer, waiting to go off and tear and shred like the machine of destruction I am! And when
I'm done with this one, I'm going after EVERYONE ELSE!!
*****SOWF United States Title Match: "The Machine" Kevin Chamber vs Red Scorpion***** Sshortly after the bell rings, Dark Master runs into the ring and attacks
Chamber. Smacks him over the head with a chair, disqualifying Red
Scorpion. RA: Here's your winner . . . on behalf of a disqualification, "The Machine" Kevin Chamber! (heel pop) MB: Look at that! Dark Master is destroying Chamber! Ohhhhh . . . a DDT on the concrete!
DV: A great match ruined because Chamber wanted to feud with Dark Master a month ago, so Dark Master got back at him. Isn't this what Chamber wanted? A big brawl with Dark Master?
MB: It was, but more likely during Dark Master's match, not his own! RA: This next tag team matchup is scheduled for one fall, and is for the
SOWF Tag Team Titles! First, the challengers, with a 450 pound combined weight . . . QuickSilver and Lucky Kid! (huge pop) Their opponents, with a combined weight of 733 pounds, SOWF Tag Team Champion, Public Rudeness!
(huge heel pop) Camera shows Public Rudeness watching Tuesday Triumphs in a filthy, scummy apartment in some un-named slum.
Aroma Man: Check these jerks out, Dan. They got a lot of nerve talking s**t about us. Calling us Public NICEness and stuff!
Disgusting Dan: Yeah, remember back when we got kicked out of boy scouts. I helped that little old lady cross the street and then you decked her on the sidewalk, put her in the Gas Chamber, and took her SSI check.
Aroma Man: Yeah, those were the good old days. Now we have all these two-bit punks wanting to make a name for them at our expense. And the expense of our title reign. Well, maybe if these clowns want our title so bad they'll put theirs up against ours in an Iron Lung Match.
Disgusting Dan: Sounds good to me, and that goes to anyone else here in the SOWF. . . tags or singles. We'll treat 'em rude in public!
*****SOWF Tag Team Title Match: Public Rudeness vs QuickSilver and Lucky Kid***** Aroma Man and Disgusting Dan attack Lucky Kid and QuickSilver immediately. Aroma Man clotheslines QuickSilver over the top and he follows. Dan suplexes Lucky Kid. Gorilla press. Cover, 2 count. Headlock. Three sharp punches to his face. Tags in Aroma Man. Leg drop. Pulls him up, powerbomb. Runs ropes, tries the Gas Chamber, but Lucky moves and tags
QuickSilver. Whips Aroma to the buckle, charges, and hits a dropkick.
Roll up, 1 count. Pulls him up, suplex. Pushes him to a buckle. Two chops. Climbs ropes, Frankensteiner. Cover, but Dan puts Aroma's foot on the rope. QuickSilver does his finisher, The QuickPlex. Suddenly, Dan comes in, lariat on QuickSilver. Lucky Kid quickly flies off the top rope, dropkick on Dan. Whips Aroma, spinebuster. Ref shoves Lucky outside, and QuickSilver goes for the cover. Dan puts the Oriental
Earlock on QuickSilver. Throws Aroma on top of QuickSilver. RA: The winners of this bout, still SOWF Tag Team Champions, Public
Rudeness! (huge heel pop)
DV: Yeah! Just like they said. They'd send Lucky Kid and QuickSilver to kindergarten! MB: They are both . . . wait! Here comes Hardcore Heaven! They're pounding on PR. Oh,no! Total Elimination on Disgusting Dan! Get the . .
. oh, there are the refs! DV: Even though no official decision is made yet, I can guarantee you they'll be fighting at Halloween in Hell.
MB: I'd bet on it . . . now it's World Title time! DV: Yeah, when Bishop's reign ends! Dark Master, I predict, is your next
World Champion!
RA: Our main event for tonight is scheduled for one fall and is for the
SOWF World Championship! (huge pop) First the challenger, weighing 270 pounds, with "The Head" Steve Lampommery, is Dark Master! (heel pop) His opponent, weighing 280 pounds, accompanied by Redeemer . . .
DV: Oh, no . . . RA: . . . is SOWF World Champion, Bishop! (huge pop) Bishop stands alone in the church, the large television screen on the wall shows the scene as the camera pulls away from Tuesday Triumphs.
Bishop: American Warrior, B.D., Dark Master. Three of you . . . four of us. You're still overmatched, little men. American Warrior, soon, soon, my son. Title or no title, I will deal with you. B.D., if you want a shot at Redeemer, ask him, my misguided son. I promise you though,
Redeemer is one crazy entity. You might not make it out of there alive. Dark Master, soon you shall dance to the death, little man. Two men shall enter that ring, one shall leave intact and with the title. This weekend, you and I shall take this league to new levels. Oh, and B.D., I never said I respected you, how could I respect a man who whines every minute of every day? Go in peace, my children.
*****SOWF World Title Match: Bishop w/ Redeemer vs Dark Master w/ "The Head" Steve Lampommery***** Lock up. Bishop winds up Dark Master's arm. Whip, back-bodydrop. Pulls him up, whip to the buckle. Chop. Hiptoss. Cover, 1 count. Headlock.
After awhile, Bishop releases the hold. Whip. Ducks clothesline, but
Redeemer trips Dark Master. He rolls out of the ring and they start yelling. Bishop pulls Dark Master to the ring apron. Tries a suplex, but he reverses it, and Bishop lands on Redeemer. Throws him into the ring.
Whip, clothesline. Cover, 2 count. Pulls him into a figure 4. After awhile, Bishop reverses it. Hold is broken. Dark Master whips Bishop into the buckle, and signals for the Darkest Hour. Redeemer shakes the ropes and Dark Master slips. Bishop grabs Dark Master and pops off the
Cathedral. RA: The winner of this bout, and STILL SOWF World Champion . . . Bishop!! (huge pop) DV: No! I don't believe it! MB: Bishop has overcome all odds and retained the World Title! And now, let's look again at the highlights of Luck of The Brawl. This is Mike
Buchanan, for Drew Vanover, saying so long everyone.
