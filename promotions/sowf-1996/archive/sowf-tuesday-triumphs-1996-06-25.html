SOWF Tuesday Triumphs Tag Team Title match: RattleSnake and BengalTiger vs. Pike and Redeemer US tourney 1st round match: Inferno Kid vs. Starlette " " " : "The Rock" Drake Erich vs. Dark Master " " " : Axel "The Ax" Frenchman vs. Wildstar US tourney QF match: The Hitman vs. QuickSilver Mike Buchanan: Welcome everybody to the CoreStates Spectrum in Philedelphia, PA! We have a great card tonight. We willsee someone become the first ever SOWF Tag Team Champions!
Drew Vanover: Yeah, Yeah, but the US tourney matches are better. The
Hitman is gonna tear up QuickSilver in our first quarter-finals match as revenge for him beating Juggernaut.
MB: Also our final three first round matches are also tonight: Dark
Master vs. "The Rock" Drake Erich, Inferno Kid vs. Starlette and Axel "The
Ax" Frenchman.
DV: I hate to say it, but Wildstar will win that one because Zadfar will disqualify Axel, cuz there is no way Zadfar is gonna let Wildstar get away with what he did last week.
MB: Yeah, but our first match RattleSnake and BengalTiger versus Pike and
Redeemer, with the winner becoming the Tag Team Champions.
Pike annd Tiger begin the match. Pike becomes distracted by fans at ringside, and Tiger rolls up Pike.
Winners-RattleSnake and BengalTiger, first SOWF Tag Team Champions Ring annoncer Eric Simpson: Here are your winners, and SOWF Tag Team
Champions, RattleSnake and BengalTiger! [cheers]
DV: Whoa! I don't belive it! Aw man! MB: You mean you were rooting for Pike and Redeemer? DV: I'd rather have them tag champs then those other guys. MB: Well live with it Drew. They are our Tag Team Champions! ES: Ladies and gentelmen, this next match isscheduled for one-fall, and is a US tourney first round match! Introduceing first, weighing 265 pounds, coming down the aisle with Ms. Amber, is Starlette! [boos] His opponenet, weighing 243 pounds, coming down the aisle withh Gideon, we have the Inferno Kid! [cheers]
As Starlette, is still taking of his silver robe, Inferno attacks him with a drop kick from the top rope. Runs the ropes, and splahes on Starlette.
Irish whips him, and does a back-body-drop. Sets Starlette in front of buckle, and does his finisher, The Rotator. Splashes on Starlette from the top rope and hooks the leg.
Winner-Inferno Kid DV: I don't beieve it. Inferno advances in the tournement? Know he'll have to fight Faulkenburg next week. I hope they bothh get disqualified or counted out or something.
MB: Anyway, Starlette will battle Fact Man in his debut next week. DV: Fact Man is such a stupid name. Whatta loser. MB: It might be but he is 7'1 358 pounds! DV: Aw shut up. ES: This first round US tourney match is one-fall and will feature the
275 pound Wildstar! [cheers] His opponent, weighing 305 lbs., Axel "The
Ax" Frenchman! [cheers]
Lock up. They break. Wildstar runs the ropes leaps over Axel's attempted back-body-drop, comes of other rope and does a spinning kick. Cover, one-count. Irish whip, reversed. Axel hits him with an elbow. Quick cover, one-count. Gets up and lock up. Axel chops Wildstar. Grabs his head and does a side suplex on Wildstar. Climbs ropes, elbow drop.
Cover, 2-count. Clotheslines Wildstar over the top rope. Ref counts to 7 before Wildstar enters the ring. As Wildstar walks towards Axel, he picks him up and does The Ax, and running powerslam.
Winner-Axel "The Ax" Frenchman DV: I told you Axel would win! MB: You said Wildstar would advance because of a DQ from Zadfar! DV: Shut up. MB: Whatever..... now we have "The Rock" Drake Erich against Dark Master. DV: This isn't fair. I like both of these guys, and I really hate this. So do the fans! Listen, they are booing the bracketings of the tourney! MB: Whatever you say, Drew. Dark Master takes fast control and attacks Erich. When he goes for a head-butt, Erich blocks it. He clotheslines Dark Master over the top.
Ref counts to 5 before he re-enters the ring. Erich grabs him and does a belly to belly suplex. Lifts up Dark Master and drops his head on the buckle. Goes to top rope but Dark Master flips him to the middle of the ring. He does a figure 4 on Erich, but he pulls himself over to the ropes. Hold broken. Master powerbombs Erich. Cover, 2-count. As Dark
Master sets up a choke slam, Erich hits him in the eyes. Erich German
Suplex's Master. Cover, 2-count. He climbs the ropes, but as he reaches the top, he slips and hits his nads on the steel. Master grabs him and sets up the Darkest Hour, but "Assassin" Brent Murdock hits Dark Master.
Erich and Murdock pound on Dark Master for a while until officals break it up. Winner-Dark Master by DQ DV: Man, this really makes me mad. Those are my three favorite wreslers! I don't like this one bit. MB: I bet you don't. I understand we have annoncement from SOWF prez. Jacob Gerry concerning our first PPV, coming live from the Baltimore Arena in Baltimore, MD, When Titans Tussle.
JG: I have signed two matches for WTT so far. First, there will be a no
DQ, no time-limit falls-count-anywhere match between "The Machine" Kevin
Chamber and the American Warrior. The main event has also has been signed. B.D. will defend the SOWF World Title against Bishop in a lumberjack-ladder match. Each man will hhave 7 lumberjacks, but the only way to win is to get the ladder and grab the belt from the rafters!
MB: WOW! A lumberjack-ladder match for the title! That must be a first. DV: Hey Mike, don't worry. B.D.'ll blow Bishop away! MB: That will be an excellent match. But now, our main event! Our first
US tourney quarter finals match!
ES: This next match is scheduled for one-fall and is our first US tourney
QF match! Introducing first, weighing 245 lbs., accompanied by The
Godfather, we have The Hitman! [boos] His opponent, weighing 245 pounds,
QuickSilver! [cheers]
Hitman runs ropes. QuickSilver tries a clothesline, ducked. Quick tries a back-body-drop, but Hitman catches him in a pile-driver. Cover, one-count. Hitman tries a brainbuster, blocked. Quick bounces off the ropes and does a legdrop. Runs ropes, hits him with a knee. Sets Hitman on the ropes, and does a Frankensteiner. Cover, 2-count. QuickSilver tries DDT but Hitman rolls out of the ring. The Godfather distracts the ref on the other side of the ring. Quick chases Hitman, but he hits Quick with a chair. They roll into the ring. Cover, 2-count. Hitman complains to the ref, Quick does a small package, 2-count. They brawl a while, ref knocked unconscious. They roll out of the ring Juggernaut attacks
QuickSilver. They beat on QuickSilver for a while. Ref wakes uo annd finnally DQ's Hitman.
Winner-QuickSilver by DQ DV: Stupid ref. MB: What? DV: Nothing. I didn't say anything. MB: I have been told that if Juggernaut gets involved in any more US tourney matches, he will be suspended indefinitly! We have a great card next week. Here it is.
