A 1996 Usenet promotion whose weekly Tuesday Triumphs show built toward three pay-per-views, crowning champions through a twenty-man battle royal, a lumberjack-ladder match, and a steel cage. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Supreme Online Wrestling Federation announced its own launch on 9 June 1996, naming its opening card Tuesday Triumphs and laying out an unusual path to its first three titles in the same post: a battle royal to crown the Supreme World Champion, the sixteen wrestlers it eliminated feeding a tournament for the US Championship, and that tournament's last three survivors meeting in a triangle match, Triple Threat, for the Television Championship. A first pay-per-view, When Titans Tussle, was already named for that July.

The battle royal ran on schedule two days later and crowned B.D. as the first Supreme World Champion — “I am the FIRST Supreme World Champion of the SOWF!!! I HAVE CONQUERED ALL!!!” — with RattleSnake and BengalTiger becoming its first Tag Team Champions on a later Tuesday Triumphs card. B.D. still held the World Title as of a 7 July house show in Uniondale, New York, but had dropped it to Bishop by the time of When Titans Tussle that same month.

Its third pay-per-view, Luck of the Brawl, ran 29 September from the Alamodome in San Antonio: Bishop retained the World Title over Dark Master, Public Rudeness kept the Tag Team Championship against QuickSilver and Lucky Kid, and American Warrior took the Television Championship from Steve Simpson. The card's own undercard carried a recurring in-fiction thread built around Steve “The Slasher” Latini, who cut a promo outside Trump Tower vowing to take the Television, United States and World Championships from their holders one after another, in that order.

How it ended isn't in the surviving record. Its last surviving document, a recruitment sheet dated 29 December 1996, still describes the promotion as running “since early June” and still open for new wrestlers.
