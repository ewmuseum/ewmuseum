VERBAL LACERATIONS: CRIMSON TIGER
<><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><>
[ Up close and personal - right in the face of the man known as
Crimson Tiger. ]
Crimson Tiger - My brother was the most honorable man I ever met in my entire lifetime. He lived his life by his own rules, but he never intentionally run over people to get what he wanted. I cannot honestly say I’ve ever met someone quite like him. Then again, I am certain I never will.
Condor gained his respect in Japan. He fought for nearly every promotion in that country. He was praised for his martial arts and high flying abilities. I was once told he was the best gaijin junior heavyweight to wrestle there in over five years.
I looked up to my brother. He was my hero. He was only six years older than me, but at times he felt like a father.
He is the person responsible for introducing me to this sport. He brought me to Japan with him, and ‘showed me the ropes’ so to speak. The experience is one of my most valued memories. He quickly thrust me into his daily regime. At the first sign of daylight, he woke up and began his workout. I was forced to do the same.
[ Leaning back in the chair he sits, CT looks up in the air as if he is pulling memories from his mind. ]
Crimson Tiger - I remember going to that gym every day for a solid five months. How could I forget? We didn’t commute, we ran there. Keep in mind, the gym was two miles from his apartment. It was the first part of the workout. Then there was the specific training for pro wrestling. I remember hitting the mat at least two hundred times a day. The smell of old, dried sweat was enough to make anybody nauseous. I like to believe it served a purpose though. I never stayed on the mat any longer than I had to. It indirectly taught me a lesson.
After five months of busted foreheads, noses, and mouths, not to mention bruises every where imaginable on my body, I was told I was ready to turn pro. According to the school my brother trained at, it was ritual to be giving your first in-ring name by your mentor. The amount of bleeding I did led my brother to label me Crimson Tiger.
And I hated it. I requested a change, but Condor told me I couldn’t until I gained respect. Considering the amount I respected him, you could never realize how much I also despised my brother. I knew the name was a rib on me. I wanted self- justification. I did what I thought was the most logical thing to do.
Well, it seemed that way at the time. I challenged Condor. Heh,
I was crazy. He owned me. Thats the most accurate way to describe it. To add insult to injury, I got my forehead busted open by a diving forearm shot. He wound up making me submit to a scorpion deathlock. After the match, I was angry with him. It wasn’t about losing. It wasn’t about the laceration. It was still about that darn name.
[ CT shakes his head, almost as if he was in the midst of a day dream. He sits upright and leans forward, resting his elbows on his knees. ]
CT - I’m straying off what I am suppose to be talking about. [ A voice, out of view, speaks up. Its a man with a low, squeaky tone. ] Man - Damien Churchill is your topic. [ CT shakes his head. Familiar position, but nonetheless he is there again. ] CT - You North American promotions sure like to hear some babble. I’ve said it before, but unless you lived in Detroit at the time its not been heard before. Fans won’t be paying admission price to see me talk trash with a guy who looks twice my size. They want to know if how good I am inside the ring. They want to know if 'he CAN go or he SHOULD go!’ This isn’t a damn oratory contest, its a blood sport. Proper utilization of verbs and prepositions don’t compare to flawless execution of slams and suplexes.
This Churchill guy, I don’t know much about him. I’ve heard he’s raised a lot of hell in damn near every promotion he’s ever wrestled in. Other than his fetish for intense brutality, I can’t say I know anything else about the ‘War Machine’.
I guess it could be said I’m up shit creek without a paddle. He’s a foot taller, nearly a hundred pounds heavier, and he’s a brute. If I don’t watch out, he’s gonna treat me like a eight year old boy and literally kick the hell out of me. Whats that analogy you all like to use for matches like this?
[ The man out of view speaks up again. ] Man - David vs. Goliath, perhaps? [ CT nods his head. ] CT - Yeah, yeah. Thats it. Let me ask you one question. Where the hell can I get myself a slingshot?
[ Fade out. ] <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><>
VERBAL LACERATIONS: XANDER XAVIERO
<><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><>
[The camera opened up outside the OW building where a large solidly built man stands leaning back up against the front door.
His head is shaved bald and a pair of dark glasses are over his eyes, hanging off his shoulders is a long black trench coat that comes down around the back of his knees. A cigarette hangs from the man's lips as his hand raises up to pull it away and blow a lone of smoke through the air. He tilts his head slowly to the side and looks directly into the camera.]
Man: Yo...welcome to MY house... [He takes another drag from the cig and flicks it aside, he continues to lean back against the wall folding his arms across his chest].
Man: Outlaw Wrestling is only a few days away from kicking off, and look who's stuck in the opening...Me...Xander Xaviero...most people would probably be pissed being stuck in the 1st match and not the main event...but I'm good with that. This is MY Arena...
that is MY ring...and it's only right for me to crisin it with the blood of the poor son of a bitch I've been scheduled.
[XX's eyes turn into a slight smirk as he stares into the camera. He raises his hand to his glasses and removes them, stuffing them in his pocket as his cold gaze penetrates the camera]
XX: So..Extreme Machine 2.0...Scary...Xander Xaviero...Scarier. Extreme Machine 2.0...the latest version, which means there was atleast one other version that couldn't cut it...so they decided to upgrades, and we all know upgrades are the same thing as the originial they just look different but are still crap! And like previous versions that always seemed to crash, I'm gonna help version 2.0 along and crash him straight into the mat.
[XX makes a fist and casually cracks his knuckles as he grinds his fist into the palm of his hand. He glances down to his knuckles and grins.]
XX: And EM 2.0...I'm gonna make you bleed...and I'm gonna make you bleed BAD! But don't think I've singled you out...right now
I got 4 other jackasses who need to be put in their place, and I plan on doing it soon enough. Crimson Tiger, I'm just gonna end up stuffing him in a suit case and sending him back to Japan.
Sean Gunn, doesn't even exist in my world...if I saw him as being anything more then a minor annoyance...I may do something about him! Ernie is just gonna be knocked into his homoerotic sesame fansties if he's stupid enough to cross me. And the only man I'd need to actually pay attention to would be Damien
Churchill, another big guy who hits like a house and doesn't care who he hurts. Respectable in some places, but we're big fish in a little pond...so when I eat you before you have a chance to grow any bigger, don't be surprised. Eliminate the competition before it becomes a threat, and EM 2.0 is going to be eliminated 1st...and if anyone's got a problem with that...do somethin'!
[XX reaches out and grabs the camera lense as it starts to fade go snowy before fading to black.]

VERBAL LACERATIONS: Q <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><>
[The OW studio. Tim Mackoney and Q, the Outlaw Wrestlings newest signee are behind the desks. The desks are lightweight, a little taller than the ones you might be accustomed to watching WWE.]
[Q is a decently tall man, beige skinnned, with short black nappy hair to go with his receding hairline. He wears a beige trenchcoat with a white t-shirt on under it reading "YODA IS
GOD." A Star Wars fan I'd gather.]
Mackoney: "Well Q, I would like to thank you for giving me the chance to talk with you for a few moments regarding your recent signing to Outlaw Wrestling. Now, you have what I would say a radically different background from every wrestler I have ever met. I'm reading that you are... a card carrying member of
MENSA?" Q: "Correct. I actually received the highest score possible in the history of IQ tests the first time I took the test and have subsequently scored higher on every test thereafter."
Mackoney: "Interesting. Yet you professionally wrestle?" Q: "Yes. My intelligence has nothing to do with my desire to wrestle. I have held jobs in the past that were geared to be
'challenging' for myself, but I found them tedious and boring.
Instead, I have opted to take part in a venue which not only tests the brain, but rather the physical as well."
Mackoney: "Who trained you? And how much experience do you have?" Q: "I trained under C.W. Anderson of ECW fame for a period of one year. Outlaw Wrestling will be my first venture into professional wrestling."
Mackoney: "So you have never wrestled in a ring, professionally?" Q: "No." Mackoney: "There are some tough guys signed to the fledgling
Outlaw Wrestling, what makes you think that you can take the grueling punishment these guys will dish out."
Q: "The process is simple. I possess one trait my competitors do not, intelligence. I will use this prowess and exploit any challengers in the ring. My intelligence will not be limited to the ring however, I will manipulate, bend, and sometimes even break the rules in order to get ahead in Outlaw Wrestling.
Winning is the first and foremost reason why I, or anyone for that matter is signed to professionally wrestle."
Mackoney: "You seem pretty confident. In closing is there some- thing that you would like to tell the superstars of Outlaw
Wrestling?" Q: "I would like to invite any challenger in the Outlaw Wrestling promotion to face me. If you do face me, you will participate in a match like no other. The match will be physical, but to a lesser extent. You will have to be psycho- logically aware at all times, because I will use my intelligence to gain an advantage over you. I will stop at nothing to win the match. Thank you, Mr. Mackoney, I will be leaving now. Thank you for the opportunity."
[Fade to black] <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><>
VERBAL LACERATIONS: "WAR MACHINE" DAMIEN CHURCHILL
<><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><>
[The pungent odor of a half-smoked cigar fills the dimly lit room. The room appears to be quite bare, with just a stool, and a dirty window all what's visible. And of course, a man sitting in the stool.]
[The smoke from the cigar fills this less-than-spectacular room. As the light filters in from the window, it gives a hauntingly angelic aura to the settings.]
Man: Rule number one... [The man drops his cigar to the ground and stomps it out. He reaches over and turns on a nearby lamp, so we can finally see who he is.]
"War Machine" Damien Churchill: Niggaz gonna die...daily. [Damien runs his right hand over his bald head.] "War Machine" Damien Churchill: That's the name of the game. I've been doin' this for only three years, but I've learned more in the last three years then what some of the players of the game learn in an entire career. I've bled more, I've worked harder, I've hurt more, on both ends, and I've seen the peaks and the valleys.
I've been out of it for almost a year now. Before I left, I was on a warpath a mile long and a mile wide. I've given six concussions, three broken legs, and have placed eight different people into the hospital while doing some underground shoot- fights and boxing in Cleveland and Florida. I'm not a wrestler,
I'm a killing machine. DSW heavyweight Champion. CEW Anarchy
Champion. There's not a damn thing I can't do or haven't done.
And I'm only twenty-six years old. [Damien grins and looks off to the side for a second.] "War Machine" Damien Churchill: About two years ago someone coined me the "Walking Apocalypse". That's not a moniker, boys and girls. I destroy everything that's in my way. I've practically killed more careers than cancer. And now, Outlaw
Wrestling gets to find out first hand what the War Machine, what the KILLING MACHINE is all about.
Crimson Tiger, you're first. Whatever it is you've got, you better bring it all to the Bone Orchard, behbeh. Because it's not gonna be pretty. You'll find out what Damien Churchill is first-hand on Saturday. I'm gonna make and example and set the precedence for pure unadulterated violence in OW. You'll be just another victim.
[Fade out.] <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> "CARTE BLANCHE" ERNIE VRANCH <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><>
[Fade in.] [The darkness fades in to a sunny ranch in Northeast Texas, specific location unknown. We are inside a mansion located towards the back of the ranch, owned by one "CARTE BLANCHE"
ERNIE VRANCH, a new OW signee, scheduled to face off against a fellow Texan, Sean Gunn, in the first House Show for Outlaw
Wrestling at Bone Orchard, the official OW studio. The setting of the house is inside what looks to be a game room of sorts,
80's style Baige and Brown carpeting are across the floor, with a Professional pool table in the center of the room, a couple of pinball machines towards the back wall, a Roulette spinner and table next to the pinball machines, as well as a poker table, and a small bar. The walls are also decorated with pictures of
Ernie Vranch, young and old, throughout his career. Some pictures depict his short-lived, yet dominant rally through the
WMWC and TVW rosters.]
[In the back of the room, sitting down in front of the poker table with a bottle of Evian water, is the one and only "Carte
Blanche" Ernie Vranch. He's wearing a pair of Black sweatpants, a white t-shirt tucked in to that, and a pair of regular Classic
Reeboks.]
[Ernie sits quietly, almost in a deep thought of sorts. Suddenly, we here a door opening towards the back of the room, and two footsteps before a stout man, donning a White suit with a Black and Brown tie and Black undershirt, a Texas Custom made hat, and a pair of snake skin boots, walks discretely in the room, twirling a wooden cane. It's Ernie's manager, Joey Magnum.
He starts tapping Ernie on the shoulder from behind. Ernie turns around abruptly.] ERNIE VRANCH: (turning around towards Joey) "So, did you get in touch?" JOEY MAGNUM: (tips cane up) "Dalton and Conrad Lowe?" ERNIE VRANCH: "Who else would I be talking about putz?!" JOEY MAGNUM: "No, the secretary lady said they were busy getting things ready for the first OW show .. "
[Ernie stands up and kicks over the poker table. He's obviously frustrated about something.]
ERNIE VRANCH: "Those two swindling idiots. They better make sure they have the proper accomodations I incquired about, and if not, there's going to be some Verbal Assault directed towards them next I get chance!"
JOEY MAGNUM: "Calm down Ernie, in just a few short days, you finally get that extra bit of exposure. I see another Mountain for you to climb in the Outlaw Wrestling, and this first show, predominantly can determine where these guys put you. You don't want something to happen during this match, and all the sudden you're in the bottom brackets."
ERNIE VRANCH: "Joey .. HAVE YOU LOST YOUR MIND?!" (Joey steps back and looks up at Ernie) JOEY MAGNUM: "No, I'm ju--" ERNIE VRANCH: (interrupting Joey mid-sentence) "Joey, you've been with me since my early years. You know me better than this.
They looked at my resume, those two up tops knew exactly where I belonged, and that was right at the top, as you can see by my position right in the Main Event against that punk kid Sean
Gunn. A virtual nobody, Joey. Have you heard of this guy's name in the Tri-State area? Where's he comin' from?"
JOEY MAGNUM: "Stop gettin' so hyper Ernie. If it's the top you want, and you got your sites set right on that Outlaw
Championship, then you sure as hell better go for it. This guy's a fellow Texan. Just think of it, first match of the promotion, and already we got a man worthy of fighting."
[Ernie paces the room, his left hand scratching the end of his chin, in another brainstorm. Joey waits to hear from him. Ernie grows a sinister grin.]
ERNIE VRANCH: "I know exactly what this place needs Joey. This place needs a 'ROLE-MODEL'. A person who's got an intelligent
IQ, a moundhill of experience under his wing, and loads of potential to come. They need someone clean, young, and with some financing and savings, not a no toothed, welfare ridden, trailor park scumbag. That's exactly what I showed up here for. Not to mow down the competition. To MAKE THE SOUND OF MY NAME a chilling one, where people start listening to you if you bring it up. That's Ernie VRANCH. The Dictator of the Ring, the man who's turned careers into dust in the past."
[All this time, Joey's listened patiently, as Ernie pauses in mid-thought.Joey questions this.] JOEY MAGNUM: "Well how we gonna' do this Ernie? We need some type of plan." ERNIE VRANCH: "We don't need a plan, Joey. We don't need a blue- print of what to do, because it's all so simple. The plan here is to simply keep the competition once step behind, and us one step ahead. And if it means CHEATING; if it means BACKSTABBING;
if it means BENDING THE RULES, then we'll do it. It starts this
Saturday Night Joey, and we got five days to plan this out."
JOEY MAGNUM: "I say you get on the weight benches and jump ropes, and start training. It's been TWO YEARS since you've stepped in the ring Ernie. There's more to this than just doing what we've done in the past. There's more to stealin', sneak attackin' and interfering."
ERNIE VRANCH: "This I know. I mean, Joey, I've been at 100 percent for more than 4 months, I'm stronger than I was in '99.
Sean Gunn .. sounds like your typical TEXAS name. Sean, I'm not lieing down threats or promises, I'm laying down facts. This first show, understand you are wrestling a man who's been held up in a shell for two years of tortuous pain, shankled in rehabilitation, and plagued by slow recovery. This ambition, anger, and utter PARASITISM I have on this underworld industry..
is all about to explode in your face. Don't worry, Sean, denial is just a temporary roadblock. I'm the better athlete here, and
I got more at stake. I got a federation to takeover, and two putz of Owners to hustle. To me, your just another fly on the windshield."
[Joey's up towards Ernie now, somewhat smiling, at Ernie's self- fed intensity. They both seem motivated now to give the Outlaw
Wrestling all they've got.]
ERNIE VRANCH: "Joey, I say we make our trip out to the Bone
Orchard and see how they organize this place. That way we can pan out our agenda."
JOEY MAGNUM: "Of course we shall." ERNIE VRANCH: (looking into the camera now, the arrogance and anger have merged together inside Vranch.) "Dalton and Conrad
Lowe, you've got your week to handle business and make sure you have everything setup like I asked. Regardless of that, you can consider your first show mine. The King among Peasant's. The
Dictator sitting over top the land.. "
[Ernie's face has grown red with energy. He seems deadset on what he wants.] ERNIE VRANCH: "Outlaw Wrestling .. Carte Blanche Vranch is now in your world .. beware for that one inevitable thing .. "
[Ernie's sadistic grin grows in length as the camera closes in for the final shot.] ERNIE VRANCH: "CHANGE!" [Ernie holds his calculating facial expression for a few moments, as Joey rubs Ernie's shoulders. Ernie is obviously pumped up about the first match, and showing an overly confident side. He turns around quickly, and back out of the camera's view, being trailed by "Lieutenant" Magnum.]
[Fade Out.]
