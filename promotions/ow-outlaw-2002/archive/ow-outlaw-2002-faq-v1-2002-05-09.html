INTRODUCTION <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> Welcome to Outlaw Wrestling! 1.1 What is Outlaw Wrestling? Outlaw Wrestling is an e-fed. Or electronic federation. Based out of Texas, we cover alittle of Oklahoma and Arkansas. We are an up and coming fed. Featuring small venues, little to no pryotecnics, and a cheap PA system.
1.2 Who runs Outlaw Wrestling? Outlaw Wrestling is run by [name withheld] and [name withheld]. We have both run federations before and know what workload is ahead of us. Together we hold a combination of about
8-9 years in e-wrestling. Together we plan to make Outlaw Wrestling one of the most realistic e-feds around. 1.3 Outlaw Wrestling's Storyline On February 12, 2002 Daniel Lowe, Dalton and Conrad's father passed away in his sleep at the age of 57.
Leaving his two sons his estate, money, and life insurance, they split it and decided to invest it in a business together.
The two brothers always enjoyed professional wrestling and both had skills in business. So on March 1st, 2002 they announced the arrival of a new promotion. Their promotion, Outlaw Wrestling.
It's headquarters have been setup inside their hometown of Dallas, Texas. The two setup a schedule where every other two weeks they'd do a tour in Oklahoma and
Arkansas, occasionally traveling else where. With a small gathering following, they signed a deal with a local TV network. WKPX agreed to play an hour long show starting at 11 PM.
Even through the two's bickering they managed to put together an announce team consisting of one man, Shane
McCord. Along with Tim Mackoney and Delilah Rhodes as reporters/interviewers. Off to a good start and making waves amongst wrestling anaylist, Outlaw Wrestling has begun.
<><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> 2. TELEVISION <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> 2.1 Weekly Shows 2.1.1 OW: Low Down This is your basic weekly update. It comes out once a week right after TLS. It features post show results, rankings, news, schedules, etc;
2.1.2 Verbal Lacerations This is a roleplay packet, for those flashes that aren't to be placed on the card. These come out every Monday night.
2.1.3 The Line in the Sand This is our weekly card, where everything goes down. It features two hours, one hour with three dark matches airing and a second hour with three live matches airing. It is in PBP format.
2.3 Supercards Once every five to six weeks, Outlaw Wrestling will hold a supercard featuring 5-6 live matches.
<><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> 3. CHAMPIONSHIPS <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> 3.1 Outlaw Championship This is the big belt. If you hold this belt your number one in OW. It holds high prestige and getting a shot at it is not an easy task. You have to earn it.
3.2 Tri-State Championship This title covers Texas, Oklahoma, and Arkansas. It's defended every other week and changes hands often.
3.3 Badlands Championship This is for the up and comers, your capable of stepping up to the next level and the Badlands championship shows it.
3.3 Double Deuce Championship Your standard tag team titles. <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> 4. CHARACTERS <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> 4.1 Owner/CEO - Dalton Lowe Dalton looks like a dapper mobster. Always sporting a brown leather jacket. His hair is slicked back. He's a thick, squatty fellow.
4.2 Owner/CEO - Conrad Lowe This is the man doing the dirty work for Dalton. He's an honest guy, trying to do the right thing at the right time. He's a stickler for the rules and hates when others break them.
4.3 Interviewers 4.3.1 Tim Mackoney Tim Mackoney knows his way around the block. He's worked various promotions and always asks the most daring questions. Which is often why he gets pushed around.
4.3.2 Delilah Rhodes Blonde, big breasts, high skirts, need I say more? Well, her IQ fits her description she's a blonde ditz. 4.4 Commentator - Shane McCord Shane McCord is a straight shooter. He calls it how it is and takes no sides in doing so. He has experience in other promotions, commentating.
4.5 Referee - Timothy Jackson He's a retired pro wrestler who's career never went anywhere, who knows the rules in and out. Don't try and break them in front of him, he'll be quick to get in the way as he backs down from no one.
<><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> 5. ROLEPLAYING / ANGLES <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> This is what determines the winners in Outlaw Wrestling. If you don't roleplay you don't win. It's as simple as that. I strive for quality rather than quantity. I'm looking for at the most one flash per week, I'd rather you put all your effort into one roleplay rather than three.
Another thing, don't make your roleplay a novel. Length doesn't matter to me, as long as you get your point accross.
Like I said match outcomes are determined by roleplaying, except if the outcome is angled, where the winner has been determined before the match by the handlers.
Don't make any mention of real wrestlers in OW. Or real feds. I'd rather you keep your mentioning of other federations to a minimum. Don't use other people's characters in your roleplay without their permission. <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> 6. STRATEGIES <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> These things are a life saver when it comes to writing matches. Too bad most people tend to not do them. If you come to me bitching about how your character was portrayed wrong or didn't do the right moves in his match and you didn't send in a strat you can blame yourself.
If you want your character portrayed perfect, send one of these in. In it should include...
Offense, Defense, Several Spots, Win/Lose Situation, Ring Entrance, and anything else you feel necessary. <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> 7.
