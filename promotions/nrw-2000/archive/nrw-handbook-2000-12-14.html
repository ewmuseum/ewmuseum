I have been in the e-wrestling industry since
1998. I've been in several e-wrestling organizations as a wrestler and a writer. I've also was a helper. I've helped run several federations, and now
I've finally decided to open my own. I've wrestled in the National Wrestling Council, in the late Lone Star Wrestling region. I also have wrestled as another wrestler in other regions in the National Wrestling Council.
In my region, I can't guarantee instant success, however with time, I hope to make NRW one of the most fun regional organizations to date. I also cannot promise cards that are on time 100 percent of the time, but they will not be more than a day or two late. That is a promise to you, the handler. I also promise you the best in story lines. Our talent will be great as every wrestler has potential. I also will try to make this a very enjoyable experience.
Our style is somewhat like all other feds. We will try to bring up new ideas and new matches. In our effort, we hope to provide you, the handler with a fun time with no cost whatsoever. This is a hobby, and it's fun, so it will be at no cost to join. The only thing we require of you is you have fun.
I own NRW, while my partner, [name withheld] will take care of any problems the
NRW might face. The handlers may instant message, e-mail, ICQ page or mIRC message us whenever they want and ask us questions. We're free to all questions and will answer anything for you. If you have an idea for an angle, please tell us. If you wish to wrestle one person in particular, let us know.
We're open to new ideas. Please continue reading. We hope that the Niagara Regional Wrestling is the next federation to home your wrestler(s). We hope to not only kick up e-wrestling to another notch, but to entertain every wrestling fan imaginable.
Lastly, enjoy our handbook. [name withheld] NRW Owner Niagara Regional Promotions Inc. <~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~> 2) NRW Information This is everything you wanted to know about the NRW plus more! 2.1) What exactly is the NRW? The letters "NRW" stand for "Niagara Regional Wrestling." The NRW is based out of Buffalo, New York. The NRW covers the regions of Western New York, and
Southern Ontario, Canada. This region is gearing up for their new federation. We've already sold out tickets for the first 3 weeks of shows in the NRW Arena in Tonawanda, New York. They are anticipating the best of wrestling action we have to offer.
NRW is promoted by Niagara Regional Promotions Inc. out of Niagara Falls, New York and is owned by [name withheld]. Since Shane owns both, he's decided to base it solely out of the Niagara Region. However, those in the Northeast, and most of Ontario, Canada will be able to watch the shows because of the shows being on local stations.
The talent in NRW will have a fair pay and will require to wrestle in unique, fun matches. NRW is based upon normal wrestling matches, we occasionally venture off stride and sign unique, "extreme" matches. This is done to ensure all sorts of wrestling fans can have an enjoyable time.
2.2) Rules and Regulations A) I don't care what anyone says, but the first and most important rule of this league is to have fun. That's why there is e-wrestling. B) The only way to have fun is you must role-play and participate. We provide a message board to post you roleplays. All we ask is a _MAXIMUM_ of three roleplays per week. Anymore will not be counted unless the roleplay is exceptionally well. Also, full strats are not needed for the most part.
There's more information that will be posted on the website. Once we feel you're participating enough in weekly cards, we'll put you into angles we feel fit. If you wish to feed us angle ideas, then we're just a click away.
However, the NRW reserves rights to put you in matches we see fit. We will also hold a chat on AOL or mIRC before every Barely Legal card to collaborate about angles and whatnot. For any reason you become inactive or 2 weeks to a month without notice, you will be taken off the roster and mailing list with no questions asked.
C) Also a huge rule. Do *NOT EVER* break KAYFABE. This means you may not mention any real life characters such as; The Rock, Steve Austin, Goldberg, etc. This means you can't mention anyone from any *REAL LIFE* organizations.
The only characters you may mention are those in this fed and this fed only. With the exception of family members or friends of your e-wrestler. If you break this rule, you will automatically job in the match you are in. If you continue doing this, you will be fired and banned from NRW's message boards.
D) Length of roleplays aren't a big deal in NRW. As long as the roleplay is good and conveys a point, it could be any length. However, keep in mind that real long roleplays (over 5 or 6k) tend to be very boring and time consuming to read. Not to mention that rp's under 3k usually contain no "body." I guess
I'm saying a good roleplay can are usually between 3 and 6k. Anything longer and shorter can be acceptable. It's all on your preference and angle.
Basically, write what you think is enjoyable and entertaining for the whole federation to read.
E) Absolutely NO OOC (out of character) comments allowed in roleplays. Everything and anything is to be written about the NRW and the match you're in. If you want to post something out of character, we have a separate board for that. Go there.
F) As earlier stated, any roleplay after your third will just be discarded 99.9% of the time and usually won't be read. It's considered spamming and warnings will be thrown.
G) At any time can you attack other wrestlers in your roleplay. You may only attack your opponent or whoever IF the handler of that person let's you. If he/she does not, then you will be given a warning. A second time will get you fired.
H) No bitching. No whining. If you lose, then whine in a form of a roleplay as long as it's in character. If you whine about a belt you won and you think you should be a higher title contender, then you earn it. However if you bitch, we'll just ignore it and throw you off of the roster. We won't tolerate bitching.
2.3) Card Information We have a weekly card called "NRW Over The Falls." This show will be used to develop angles, have interviews and matches. This card will often take place in small arenas and gyms around the Niagara Region. The cards will be fully written unless otherwise noted by the fed heads. It's the only card of the week and it occurs on Thursday nights.
On the last Sunday of each month (Unless noted otherwise) will be our "supercard" persay. The card is known as "Barely Legal" with a subtitle. This card will be housed in larger arenas (HSBC Arena, etc.) and normally, every title will be defended on this card. When there is a week with a Barely Legal card, there will be no Over The Falls. This gives the handler the maximum amount of time to roleplay and the card writers to write.
2.3.1) Contracts and Matches There is no official way of making a contract in NRW. However, asking one of the fed heads to wrestle someone is, in a sense, a contract. If we see fit that you should wrestle someone else, then it'll happen. The NRW committee reserves rights to put you in any match that see fit, though. If there is a time where you cannot wrestle, then you must give the executive committee at least 2 weeks notice. If there is no notice, and we put you in a match, you will job. No questions asked.
Any type of match is sanctionable in NRW at the discretion of the NRW executive committee. Once again, we have the right to sign any match we see fit to entertain the public.
2.4) Rankings There won't be rankings in NRW. There will be a win loss record that can be accessible, but the will not effect pushes. We will push you on how well you roleplay and how much you participate. I mean, how many times have you seen the 'real world' wrestling pay any attention to rankings?
2.4.1) Title Information A) NRW Heavyweight Title This title means a lot to NRW. It's the highest you can go in the NRW. The wrestle who holds this belt is the workhorse of the fed. He'll have the biggest say in what happens in his angles and whatnot. He will carry the most weight of the fed on his shoulders. You have to be the best of this fed in order to hold this 30 pounds of platinum around your waste. This title will only be defended on Barely Legal, unless otherwise noted. Although, having this title will require you to at least show up on every card. Whether you wrestle or not.
B) NRW Niagara Falls Title This is the title most say is the final stepping stone to the world title. This title is for those who are almost ready to hold the title that means everything. The United States championship will be one of the best men in the fed, and will also have much of the weight on their shoulders. They have a big part in running this place also. The US title will be defended 2 times a month.
C) NRW Television Title This title is the hardest of the 3 singles titles to hold in NRW. This title will be defended 3 times a month. The matches for this title has a time limit of 10 minutes on weekly cards and 15 on Barely Legal. To hold this title for a long period of time proves your loyalty and participation in NRW. If you prove yourself with this title, you will be ready to move up the ladder and become a contender to the US Title, or every the Heavyweight Title.
D) NRW Tag Team Title This is like the Heavyweight Title. The team who holds this title is the best pair of wrestlers in NRW. They have the most say on what happens in the tag team division and will carry most of the weight on their shoulders.
