World Pro Wrestling Federation News ________________________________________________________________________ Truly Number 1! ________________________________________________________________________ WPWF Members, The first WPWF Card, Sunday Extreme will be April 20. Wrestlers need to start challenging each other and sending in role-plays. This card is going to be great! Remmeber 2 role-plays are required per week! Thank You, [name withheld] ************************************************************************ WPWF Interview Session: ************************************************************************ The Extreme Team ready to rock! ===================================================== Paul E.- ECW is dead! I have brought the very best of ECW to the WPWF. Raven, Sabu, Taz, and Terry Funk are going to destroy all of you wannabes! My man Sabu has a challenge for that piece of trash Hunter Hearst Helmsley! Helmsley, Sabu will smash you through a table and he will show his EXTREME Side! (Taz walks in and looks ready for action) Taz- Paul, thanks for bringing me to WPWF! I will make you proud by slapping a Tazmission on any who dares to cross me! I give myself an open contract for Sunday Exztreme on April 20! Anyone dare to cross the line of Taz? (Raven gets the mic and talks) Raven: The blood is going to rush and people are going to bleed when I, Raven, former ECW World Champion beats the hell out of you! (Terry Funk jumps in and grabs a mic.) Terry Funk: Hardcore U! Terry Funk style! WPWF Boys I dare you to challenge me! I will beat the shit out of those WCW and WWF Assholes! EXTREME! (Camera goes back to Paul E.) Paul E.: My men are ready to annihilate all of the WPWF and grab all the WPWF titles! ************************************************************************ WPWF Sunday Extreme April 20: ************************************************************************ Taz(w/Paul E.)has an open contract ************************************************************************ All comments, suggestions, wrestlers you are giving up, or anything WPWF related should go to: [detail withheld] ************************************************************************ WPWF INTERVIEW COUNT 4/11/97 to 4/18/97 ************************************************************************ WPWFINTERVIEW COUNT...I will use this chart to keep track of the amount of interviews that WPWF members are doing each week. The rule is TWO INTERVIEWS (OR MORE) A WEEK!!!!! Remember, QUALITY of RP's AND GREAT ANGLES also go a long way in determining who gets the TITLE SHOTS!
