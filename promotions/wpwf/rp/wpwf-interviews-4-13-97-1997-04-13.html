World Pro Wrestling Federation News 4/13/97 ________________________________________________________________________ Truly Number 1! ________________________________________________________________________ WPWF Members, I am trying as hard as I can to get new members here in the WPWF. If you know anyone interested in joining and E-Fed have them contact me at [detail withheld] ************************************************************************ WPWF Interview Session: The Ruthless Posse The New Elite Horsemen Nash/Michaels Al Snow Owen Hart Killer Inc. ************************************************************************ The Ruthless Posse is out to get the Extreme Team ===================================================== Francine: Terry Funk, Taz, Team Extreme, you have made your life a living hell by accepting the challenge of the Shooter, and the Man they Call Vader. Vader: Everywhere I go, I hear about the legend. The Legend. Well, I am going to retire the legend after I beat the crap out of him during our Texas Death Match. I would like to be the first one to say, Goodbye, and get the hell out of my league. Francine: Monkey Boy, can you hear us? The Shooter is going to make you submit to the Texas Cloverleaf, and after he wins, he's not gonna get off of you, he's gonna keep it on till you are paralyzed. Ain't that right Dean? Dean: (Nods his head, and smiles.) Doc: Midnight Express, you haven't answered our challenge yet. Are you afraid??? Gordy: Well, Doc, everybody's afraid of Death Valley, and why shouldn't they be? We are the best tag team in the world, and there ain't no doubt about. Francine: Jimmy Hart, Jim Cornette, you are looking at the most extreme manager in the league. I am the best, and I will lead my men to glory, why you lead your men to doom. Later. ************************************************************************ The New Elite Four Horsemen making their presence known! ===================================================== Arn Anderson standing in the middle of the ring> Arn:Welcome all you WPWF fans out there. I am here tonight on this occasion to speak my mind and show you what is next in the career of Arn Anderson.Throughout many years I have busted my ass in the world of proffesional wrestling as a Horseman. Now as age catches up to me, I realize that I don't have what it takes to be what I want me to be because of my career ending injury. Well the fact of the matter is, I see a bunch of talentless fools going around calling themselves real wrestlers. The fact is, I am not gonna sit back and watch the Sport of Wrestling take a downfall, I will make the most elite group of wrestlers that ever laid face on the earth. So you thought the old horsemen were something? You haven't seen a thing yet, because the men I have chosen will dominate each and every competitor here in the WPWF! We are the start of something new, we are THE NEW ELITE HORSEMEN!! Now onto business, time to introduce the men that I have chosen. First off, let me welcome one of the hardest working, greatest wrestlers in the sport today with an attitude that can win ya a lot of matches, STEVE AUSTIN! <Steve Austins Theme Plays as he enters the ring with a loud pop and takes the mic> Austin:Arn, me and you go back a long way son. You took me up and brought me to WCW at the beginning of my career. You made me what I am today. Thanks to you, I am what I am today! The Bottom line is, I will be part of the most elite team in the WPWF, and we're gonna whip some ass! A reason I came here is because the WWF was screwing me all over the place because they thought I was a Jackass! You dont treat me like that son! Then there is this filthy piece of trash that calls himself Shawn Michaels! He runs away here to the WPWF thinking he can get away from me! I have whipped everyone's ass but his, he has been hiding from my ass ever since I came to pro-wrestling so how about you get your sorry ass in the ring with me! When I get my hands on you Shawn, I'll give ya a can of Whoopass and you won't know what hit you! Austin 3:16 says I'm gonna whipp your sorry little playgirl ass wether you like or you don't, because STONECOLD SAID SO! Arn:Now, onto our next member, please welcome one of the greatest athletes I have seen around, Lionheart CHRIS BENOIT! <Benoit Enters the ring with a stern face and grabs the mic> Benoit:Thank You Arn for letting me join your Stable. You led me to great success to WCW, and now I am here in the WPWF to continue my legacy! My continous journey to the top of the ladder will continue here in the WPWF and I will anihilate all of my enemies! I am silent, but indeed am extremely violent and everyone I face will see that. <Benoit holds up the 4 Horsemen Sign> Arn:Next comes one Wrestler that I have had great battles with in the past. He is one wrestler I really respect, Hunter Hearst Helmsley! <HHH enters the ring in his usual tights and a 4 horsemen shirt> Hunter:You know Arn, I've been watching you all my life and I really gotta say I look up to you. You are a man of great experience and I will enjoy being a horseman! You pityful and moronic fans will see me dominate the competition and get all the women like I always DO! Arn: Now onto a man that has the attitude to be a horsemen, but can get out of control sometimes....please welcome the loose cannon Brian Pillman! <Pillman walks out with a cold look on his face> Pillman:Well here I am! I am back! You know it's pretty funny how I have wandered through all feds...the WCW...well they couldn't contain me....the WWF...well they didn't even let me get in the ring...and now I am here in the WPWF! Will you contain me? I DON"T FUCKING THINK SO! I AM A LOOSE CANNON AND I'M GONNA KILL WHOEVER FUCKING MESSES WITH ME! Hey Malenko, you think you got what it takes? Bring it on! WHOEVER WANTS A PIECE OF ME, BRING IT ON! What are you gonna do when you get a dose of PILLMAN 9MM! <The Team leaves holding up the Horsemen sign> ************************************************************************ Nash and Michaels proving the WPWF has tag-teams! ===================================================== Shawn Michaels & Kevin Nash w/ Jimmy Hart Shawn Michaels "Gordy... Williams? Who the hell do these guys think they are?!?! First of all they say there aren't any tag teams.... Are you blind? Shawn and Kevin Nash.... The Heart Break Express baby is going to be on a roll!" Kevin Nash "Thats right, HBK and Nash back together again! But, this time we don't have Vince up our ass about breaking up and we don't care what the fans think, because we're on top! Bischoff and Vince aren't factors anymore and we dumped Razor and Syxx who were making us look like fools. You know we only respect one other member of the Kliq that being Hunter Hearst Helmsley and we our sending out an invitation to him to form an alliance with us. However, the Elite Horseman are real men and so are we.... Not like Gordy and Doc... The two oldest farts in the book trying to make a run for the gold one last time..." Shawn Michaels "Dr. Death Steve Williams and Terry "Bam Bam" Gordy if you have a problem with us deal with it. The Midnight Express can also deal with us when we go after the tag titles. We respect all tag teams that respect us... However, we have no problem kicking these tag teams cans around the stadium!" Jimmy Hart "President... Get us signed up for the matches!!!!" ************************************************************************ Al Snow wants a piece of Benoit ===================================================== Al Snow w/ Jimmy Hart Al Snow "Chris Benoit the Crippler?
