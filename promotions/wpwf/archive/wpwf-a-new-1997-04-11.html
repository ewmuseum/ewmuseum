WPWF A New!

11 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The WPWF is starting over again. I don't like the direction we are taking, this time there won't be a homepage for the WPWF, so I put more time into making the E-LEAGUE Better! We are still the WPWF. We are going to have 2 cards a week! WPWF Sunday Extreme WPWF Thursday Night Fights There will be 4 titles now: WPWF World Title- This title will be determined by a Battle Royal WPWF Extreme Title- This Title will be determined in a series of EXTREME Match-Ups! This will be a tournament with matches like, Death-Matches, Barbed Wire Matches, and other Extreme Matches. The Extreme Title will be defended only in Extreme type matches WPWF World Tag Team Titles- These titles will be determined in a tournament. WPWF Unified American Title- This is the American WPWF Title, this will be determined by a tournament too. We will not have pay-per-views because I don't see a need for them. You can have up to 4 wrestlers and 1 manager. 2 ROLE-PLAYS ARE REQUIRED PER WEEK! Please E-mail me if you are interested in the WPWF at: [detail withheld] Thank You!
