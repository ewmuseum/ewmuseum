WPWF We Ain't Role Models Card

7 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Head Hunters (w/Paul E. Dangerously) vs Dean Malenko and Owen Hart The Headhunters went right after Owen Hart and Dean Malenko not wasting anytime at all. This match involved foreign objects as Head Hunter #1 used a baseball against the head of Owen Hart, busting him wide open. Malenko and Head Hunter #2 were brawling on the outside and both of them then came back in the ring. Head Hunter #2 threw Malenko out of the ring and Malenko couldn’t get up. The Head Hunters won after a count-out against Malenko. The winners are The Head Hunters. Time of match: 0:13:48 The Powerhouses (Ahmed and Bulldog) vs The Legion of Doom (w/Mr. Fuji) This match went up and down, with both teams in control at different times. The Legion of Doom took control of this match in the end. The turning point in this match was when Davey Boy Smith was preparing to jump on Hawk with a flying cross body, but Hawk moved out of the way. The Bulldog was out cold and Hawk took control of this. Hawk got the Doomsday Device and nailed the Bulldog in the head with it to get the pin. The winners are The Road Warriors. Time of match: 0:11:54 Yokozuna (w/Mr. Fuji) vs Arn Anderson (w/Woman) - No Disqualifications Yokozuna took control of this match in the beginning. He hit some great moves, like a back suplex, and a bearhug. This match eventually went to the outside of the ring. where Arn Anderson DDT’ed Yokozuna on the concrete floor! But Yokozuna took control again using a wide variety of suplexes. When Yokozuna and Arn Anderson came back into the ring Anderson hit a DDT and went for pin. Yokozuna kicked out at 2 1/2, then Mr. Fuji threw in his cane to Yokozuna! Yokozuna hit Arn with the can and go the pin. The winner is Yokozuna. Time of match: 0:13:19 Lex Luger and Chris Benoit (w/Woman) vs. Bret Hart and Steve Austin After brawling on over the floor the team of Chris Benoit and Lex Luger came out on top. Chris Benoit hit a Tombstone Piledriver on Austin, which busted a cut in Austin’s head. Benoit threw Austin back into the ring and nailed him with a Dragon Suplex to get the pin. The winners are Chris Benoit and Lex Luger. Time of match: 0:26:31 Bam Bam Bigelow (w/Madusa) vs Sabu (w/Paul E. Dangerously) Paul E. was again got involved in Sabu’s match. Paul E. threw a telephone into the ring, Sabu took it and hit Bigelow with it to get the pin. Sabu was doing just fine without Paul E.’s interference The winner is Sabu. Time of match: 0:17:01 The Giant (w/Mr. Fuji) vs Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/Kimberly)- Falls Count Anywhere This match went from the ring, to outside the ring, to the mid-arena area. Hunter Hearst Helmsley was handling The Giant very well. Helmsley had The Giant in the position for the Pedigree, but The Giant countered with a backdrop and got the pin. The winner is The Giant. Time of match: 0:08:29.
