WPWF Battle of the Titans

2 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Bret Hart vs. Dan Severn Bret Hart attacked Dan Severn before the bell. Dan Severn really put on a clinic, doing all kinds of holds on Bret Hart, arm locks, cobra-clutch, and suplexes. Bret Hart bumped into to the referee James Beard and knocked him out. Bret Hart had Dan Severn in a backslide and had the three count on him, but there was no referee to count. It looked like Bret would win the match kicking Dan Severn and getting him in an inside cradle, but Dan Severn wouldn’t give up. He kicked out of the pin and nailed the “Hitman” with a magnificent belly-to-belly suplex to get the win. Through most of the match Ric Flair was begging off to Ken Shamrock, Ken wouldn’t give him any mercy. The winner is Dan Severn. Time of match: 0:20:06 Ken Shamrock vs Ric Flair (w/Woman) Ken “The Rock” Shamrock went right after The “Nature Boy” Ric Flair before the bell. Shamrock got Ric Flair into an adminal stretch, which he held locked in for 11 seconds. Ric Flair did get control for awhile, but Shamrock was just too much for him. Flair was never able to lock in the Figure-Four-Leg-Lock. Ken Shamrock won after a kick to the mid-section and made Ric Flair submit to a short arm scissors hold. The winner is Ken Shamrock. Time of match: 0:25:25 The Eliminators (w/Francine) vs. Demoltion (w/Sunny) This match was all The Eliminators! The totally dominated Demoltion. Saturn nailed Ax with neck-breakers, punches, and headbutts. Ax tagged out to Smash, but he couldn’t get the job done either. Right when Smash got in the ring he was double-teamed by The Eliminators, they nailed him with a kick to the stomach. Kronos placed Smash on the top-turnbuckle and did a great looking Superplex for the pin. The winners are The Eliminators. Time of match: 0:03:10 Lex Luger (w/Woman) vs Vader (w/Francine) Luger and Vader went right after each other! At the beginning of the match Vader threw Luger out of the ring. Vader went outside the ring and powerbombed Lex Luger on the concrete floor! Francine then came over and distracted Luger, giving Vader the chance to punish him some more. This match was never in control by Vader on Luger, but Vader finally hit Lex Luger with a sunset flip and got the pin. The winner is Vader. Time of match: 0:14:35 Taz (w/Francine) vs Dean Malenko Taz and Malenko really put on a show for the fans tonight. This was a very technical match with both wrestlers executing suplexes, reversals, submission locks, and take-downs. Taz then left the ring and got a baseball hit! Taz swinged at Malenko, but Malenko moved out of the way. Dean took the bat from Taz and swung it at him. Taz moved out of the way and took the bat from Malenko again. Malenko took the bat once again from Taz and hit point-blank in the center of Taz’s face with it. Malrnko took down Taz with a Frankensteiner from the top rope! Taz thought got up and got the baseball bat again. He hit Malenko in the side of the head with the bat and took control of the match. Taz won it all after a fireman’s carry. The winner is Taz. Time of match: 0:24:30 Shawn Michaels (w/Sunny) vs Sabu (w/Paul E. Dangerously) Sabu really showed his “Extreme” side tonight. During the match he got a fire extinguisher and hit Shawn Michaels with it not once, but twice! Paul E. also got into the match, tripping Shawn Michaels from the outside. Shawn Michaels regained his focus and took it right to Sabu! Paul E. was constantly trying to get the attention of Michaels. Shawn got sick of Paul E.’s crap and punched him in the teeth! Shawn was really on a roll for awhile, but that all ended. Sabu got a microphone from the announcer’s table and knocks out Shawn with it. Sabu did a beautiful moonsault on Shawn for the pin. The winner is Sabu. Time of match: 0:12:58 ************************************************************************ Our cameras have got up with Rick Rude and DDP! ************************************************************************ (The camera focuses in on a white stretch limo pulling up to the hotel. The limo stops and out steps Diamond Club Members Rick Rude and Hunetr Hearst Helmsley) Rick Rude: Get that camera out of our simply ravishing faces. In fact that is exactly what we are "SIMPLY RAVISHING", The official tag team of The Diamond Club and very soon we will be the WPWF World Tag Team Champions. Hunter Hearst Helmsley: Giant. I am glad to see that there is a man in this federation who will accept my challenge. I will wrestle you one on one... unless you want to get you partner Yokozuna and make this a tag team match so "SIMPLY RAVISHING" can show why we are the next World Tag Team Champions. So the choice is up to you singles or tag team. Either way, a pedigree is still coming your way. (Rude and Helmsley turn away from the camera and enter the Hotel) ************************************************************************
