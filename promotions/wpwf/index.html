Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The World Pro Wrestling Federation opened on 26 March 1997 as a real-wrestler federation simulated on TNM 6.2, members allowed up to four real wrestlers apiece, with two weekly cards planned from the start — Battle of the Titans and We Ain't Role Models — three championships (World, World Tag Team, Television), and a pay-per-view every other month. By the next day its own founder had already fielded six wrestlers and two managers: Taz, Vader, The Eliminators, Mr. Perfect, Shawn Michaels, and Demolition, managed by Francine and Sunny.

Battle of the Titans staged six matches, its results posted 2 April though the card itself had been billed for 6 April at Madison Square Garden: Dan Severn beat Bret Hart (20:06); Ken Shamrock submitted Ric Flair (25:25); The Eliminators beat Demolition (3:10); Vader beat Lex Luger (14:35); Taz beat Dean Malenko (24:30); and Sabu beat Shawn Michaels (12:58), closing on a promo from Rick Rude and Hunter Hearst Helmsley challenging for the World Tag Team Titles.

A second card, We Ain't Role Models, followed five days later with six more: The Head Hunters beat Dean Malenko and Owen Hart by count-out (13:48); The Road Warriors beat The Powerhouses (11:54); Yokozuna beat Arn Anderson in a no-disqualification match (13:19); Chris Benoit and Lex Luger beat Bret Hart and Steve Austin (26:31); Sabu beat Bam Bam Bigelow (17:01); and The Giant beat Hunter Hearst Helmsley, falls count anywhere (8:29).

On 11 April the promotion announced it was “starting over again,” dropping its own homepage to put more effort into “making the E-LEAGUE Better,” renaming its two weekly shows Sunday Extreme and Thursday Night Fights, scrapping pay-per-views outright, doubling its roleplay requirement, and replacing its old title picture with four new championships — the World Title now decided by battle royal, a new Extreme Title and Unified American Title each by tournament, and the Tag Team Titles by tournament as well. The relaunch's own first interview session built toward the debut Sunday Extreme, set for 20 April, pitting a newly formed faction, the Extreme Team (Raven, Sabu, Taz, and Terry Funk, led by manager Paul E. Dangerously), against the Ruthless Posse, led by Francine, with Vader challenging Dean Malenko to a Texas Death Match along the way.

No document survives after 13 April 1997. Sunday Extreme, Thursday Night Fights, and all four of the relaunch's own new championships never produced a result in the record.
