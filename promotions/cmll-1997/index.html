Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Championship Mexican Lucha Libre introduced itself twice in September 1997, in a founding advertisement issued 2 September and revised eight days later. Both open the same way: “E-Wrestling....MEXICAN STYLE!!” — a promotion staged under Mexican rules, two out of three falls, running two cards a week, Tuesday Night Titans and One Crazy Saturday Night.

The two editions disagree on their own title lineup: the first names a Heavyweight Title, a Six Man Tag Team Title and a Mini Title; the second drops the six-man and mini belts for a Mexican Television Title and a Heavyweight Tag Team Title instead. Both list an overlapping but not identical roster of invented luchadores — CALO, Chico Jericho, El Cockroach and “Suave” Paco Gonzalez appear in each; Skywalker Vampyro appears only in the second.

Both editions claim the promotion was already tied into the wider e-fed scene, associated with several other federations and slated for “the Worldwide Pay Per View that is in the works with other great feds” — more than a dozen promotions named in the ad as fellow invitees.

No further document survives. Which title lineup, if either, actually held, whether the promised pay-per-view ever happened, or how long Championship Mexican Lucha Libre ran past its second week is not something the surviving record answers.
