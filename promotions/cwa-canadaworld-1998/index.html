Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Canada World Alliance introduced itself in a single homepage-promotion post dated 18 August 1998, its own operator inviting “All You Wrestling Fans” to visit “My CWA Hompage” and promising every member “a title shot at any belt you want” and “life long protecction.”

The same post directed newcomers to a second, newly forming federation hosted on the same site, the WWHWA, framing sign-up there as the way into the Canada World Alliance itself.

No roster, card, title, or closure notice from either promotion survives.
