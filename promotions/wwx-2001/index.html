Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

WWX’s whole surviving record is two recruitment notices, posted a day apart in June 2001 to rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy. The first calls it “a big new e-fed” with “plenty of titles up for grabs,” claims ownership of “about two other smaller e-feds,” and points readers to a site hosted on Homestead. Nothing in the record dates when the promotion itself actually opened.

The second notice, posted the following day by its own operator, laid out how the promotion meant to run: matches would go to “the person doing the best roleplays and not... losers who can only do 1 liners,” the roster would blend invented wrestlers with real ones, and “inter-fed rosters” would link WWX to the other federations it said it owned.

No card, title, or roster survives to show whether any of that took shape. How the promotion ended, and whether the promised cross-federation rosters ever existed, isn’t in the surviving record.
