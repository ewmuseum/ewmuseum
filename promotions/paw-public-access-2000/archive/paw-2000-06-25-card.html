PAW — Card Archive

6.25 Card

25 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera opened inside a small room with three college aged men and a woman sitting before several television monitors and a sound board, working diligently.
One of the men was on his feet talking to someone on his headset, giving them directions of where to be for the start of the show when there was a loud pounding on the door that startled all four within. Turning, they were surprised to see Jason Flint smiling as he entered the room.
Stepping inside, he closed the door with his foot and turned back to the persons in the room. The one with the headset looked at Flint then back at his collegues.)
Headset guy: Hey ... you can’t be in here! We’ve got a show to run ... (Flint brought the man to silence as he raised a hand clutching his Singapore
Cane.) Jason Flint: Who’s in charge here? (The man with the headset took a step forward as the other three pointed at him. Nodding his head, Flint lashed out with the Cane, knocking the man to the floor.
Taking a deep sigh, he turned to the other three who stared at him with big eyes.) I’ll ask again ... who’s in charge here? (The three looked at each other then to the one on the floor groaning in pain. Without a word, they all pointed directly at Flint who smiled. Raising his other hand, holding onto a videotape, he had only one thing to say.)
Excellent ... (Large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession.) .... 5 ... .... 4 ... .... 3 ... .... 2 ... .... 1 ... (White Zombie's "More Human than Human" begins as flashes of the PAW stars are shown. Skip Westervelt ... Johnny Green ... Justin Hart ... Drake Russell ...
Jason Flint ... and finally the PAW Texas Heavyweight Champion, Jake Morgan, standing in the middle of the PAW ring, the belt raised over his head.)
After an explosion, the scene fades to black and the PAW logo disappears before fading in on host Aaron Starr standing in front of the PAW banner off to the side of the television studio set.)
Aaron Starr: Hello ladies and gentlemen and welcome to Public Access Wrestling. We’ve got four big matches this evening with Jake Morgan in for perhaps the fight of his career as he defends his Texas Heavyweight Title against recent rival Johnny Green ...
(Starr stops as he puts a hand to his ear, looking down at the floor. With a quizzical look, he peers up at the camera.)
I’m getting word that Shane Cornell has snapped in the back and has attacked
... of all people, Pep Preston?!?!?!
(Leaving Starr, the camera made it’s way to the back where Lisa Doyle was while
Shane Cornell was being restrained by the PAW Security. Flashing to the floor, the camera found Pep Preston laying there having been smacked over the head with a now dented garbage can that was on floor near him.
As several wrestlers came out into the hallway, Cornell was ranting and raving as the Security moved him out of the way.)
Shane Cornell: Now it’s just you and me, Maeda ... this time your ass is mine! (Zeroing in on the unmoving Preston, the camera faded out ... only to fade in on Jason Flint, who is walking down a hallway, twirling some athletic tape on his fingers, chewing away on some gum.)
Voice: Jason! (Flint turns to face the person who called out to him, in a near defensive stance, just to relax as Kimberly Davis walks up.)
Jason Flint: What ... do you want, Kim? Kimberly Davis: I want to know what you are doing. Jason Flint: ... getting ready for my match? Kimberly Davis: Not that, this whole “I don’t give a damn” attitude. You’re destroying yourself.
Jason Flint: Well Kim, what can I say. I am a bastard and will burn for my sins in the afterlife. But you were right, though, I do have only myself to blame.
Kimberly Davis: But ... Jason Flint: Don’t speak, Kim, you can’t have it both ways. You want me to burn, I’m on the right path. What do you care if I destroy my body for this promotion? At least I’m doing it to myself and not dragging people down like the State Club Boyz or you or Johnny. You don’t like ... don’t fucking watch my matches. Excuse me.
(Flint walks off, leaving Davis bewildered and shaking her head in disbelief. The camera made it’s way back to the ring and the cheering fans as two of the three wrestlers for the first match waited in the ring.
With Shane Cornell being led out to the ring by Security, he looked surprised to see Skip Westervelt and Maeda both in the ring. Climbing up onto the ring apron, he was pulled inside by long time enemy Christian Maeda. With a disgusted wave, Peter Daye called for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Maeda pulled Cornell up and locked in a Full Nelson. Westervelt took them both to the mat with a Leg Lariat. Getting to his feet quickly, he pulled Cornell across the ring and sent him for a ride with an Irish Whip right into Maeda who was waiting to level the helpless man with a Clothesline. Cornell spun through the air and landed hard on the mat.
Pulled up and back into a Full Nelson, Maeda threw Cornell over with a Suplex out of the hold. Hooking a leg, Maeda got a 2 count from Daye before Skip nailed him with a boot to the back.
Maeda and Skip locked up in the center of the ring fighting for control, spinning into an Arm Bar but was knocked off as Cornell came in swinging. After knocking Maeda into a corner, Cornell let fly with Reverse Knife Hands which thudded loudly off his opponent’s chest.
Spinning out of the corner, Maeda reversed positions and fired back with several of his own before jumping up onto the ropes and wrapping his legs around Cornell’s neck, taking him over with a Head Scissors.
Bouncing off the ropes, Westervelt ran in and landed a Leg Drop onto the shaken
Cornell. Skip took a Knee to the back from Maeda who then flipped Cornell over, pulling a leg back into a Half Boston Crab. With Maeda sitting back, Daye watched Cornell for a submission but none came as Skip came in with the save as he booted Maeda in the head with a Spinning Back Kick.
Maeda was down when Cornell and Westervelt locked up in the center of the ring. Shane gained control and spun Skip over onto his back out of an Arm Bar. An agile Skip kipped to his feet but found himself on his back from boot to the head.
The fans were cheering as Skip’s partner dove into the ring and hammered Cornell to his knees with a Double Axe Handle. The partners were up hammering away at Maeda and Cornell in opposite corners before spinning out and sending their opponent across the ring. Maeda and Cornell passed each other only to run into the outstretched arm of both Skip and Chip.
With both men floored, Westervelt and the obese Chip climbed the corner and from the top dove off ... Skip landed a Senton on Maeda while Chip’s Diving
Headbutt caught Cornell in the crotch. Scrambling, both Skip and Chip hooked a leg and leaned back for the pin. Shrugging his shoulders, Daye dropped to the mat and double counted, slapping the mat for the 1 ... 2 ... 3 ...
The fans cheered as Skip Westervelt and Chip rolled out of the ring in their impromptu match and made their way to the back. Inside the ring, Maeda was up on his feet screaming at Daye while Shane Cornell pulled himself to his feet.
Maeda shoved Daye back before drawing a warning. Daye waved a finger in Maeda’s face. With a smile, Maeda turned away from Daye and Stomped Cornell in the thigh.
Grabbing his opponent, Maeda lifted Cornell into the air and brought him down with a Fisherman Suplex. After strutting around the ring, the handsome Maeda
Whipped Cornell into the ropes and caught him. Spinning him around, Maeda dropped to the mat clutching the hapless Cornell with a Reverse Russian
Legsweep. Rolling over the top, Maeda waited while Daye slapped out a second 3 count. *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (After stomping the downed Cornell, Maeda emptied the contents of his nose before walking off and leaving the ring. With the fans cheering as the camera passed through their ranks, it made it’s way into the back where the weeklong absent B.J. Perris was talking to Johnny Green. The angered look on his face told the whole story as Green nodded his head in agreement.)
Perris: I don’t care what you have to do but I want you to find Flint and that damned tape of his ... if it exists! Do whatever you have to ...
(Green smiled as he popped his knuckles.) Johnny Green: What about Russell? (An exasperated Perris shook his head.) Perris: I want it done right! (Snapping his head around, Perris saw the camera focussing in on him.) Get that damned camera off me! (Green moved forward and put his hand over the lens as it faded to snow. With a smooth transition, another camera cut back to a sitting Jason Flint, who is taping his wrists and hands in what looked like a broom closet. Dressed in a black T-shirt with “Fear is Nothing” and a pair khaki shorts, sneakers and kneepads. Aaron Starr sneaks in and lays down a mic and nervously squirms off.)
Flint grabs the mic but turns away from the camera, looking at something else.) Jason Flint: I’ve got to believe Justin that your heart just isn’t into this match. In the last couple of weeks, I’ve watched you throw Kool-Aid at my poster and then you had better things to do like go out clubbing or whatever and ended up tearing my poster again.
(Flint pulls a long flat box up to him and opens it. Inside is a stack of Jason
Flint posters, back in the day when he was the PAW Champion.)
Here, Justin. You like to tear posters, well there you go. All the posters you want to tear, drench, burn, or whatever.
(Flint turns to the camera, a sly sneer curling his lips.) All I know is that soon, in that squared circle over there, something truly
SERIOUS will happen. I will seriously beat your ass. I will seriously cripple you. I will SERIOUSLY make you bleed.
(A dry laugh cracks out) You don’t want to take this match seriously, Hart? Fine with me, I don’t care. All I want is to prove to you fucking people how serious I can get and what carnage I can unleash. Here take this fucking thing.
(Flint tosses the mic to the side, where Aaron Starr fumbles it but still catches it. Standing, the door flies open and an angered Justin Hart rages in.
After forcing Flint back with several Right Hands, Hart caught a Forearm to the head that rocked him.
Grabbing Hart by the head, Flint pushed him out of the room and into the hallway. Using the walls, Flint bounced Hart’s head several times as he led him down the hallway finally making it out to the ring.
After rolling Hart inside, Flint called for the bell as referee Peter Daye rushed out from the back.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Flint pummeled Hart early on and kept him backpeddling, unable to get anything started on offense. Slamming Hart back into a corner, Flint drove a Knee into his midsection doubling him over. A quick Knee Lift snapped him back up and
Left caught him on the chin causing Hart to slump down in the corner.
From across the ring, Flint charged him and landed on top of Hart’s chest, bucking up and down before rolling off and back to his feet. Pulling Hart up by the arm, Flint worked out the kinks with an Arm Wringer then twisted into an
Arm Bar.
Hart was in pain as Flint pushed down on the elbow. A Back Elbow caught Flint flush in the face which brought only a smile ... not the desired effect.
Kicking out his foot, Flint took Hart off of his and dropped down with a Leg
Drop onto the arm.
Grabbing the arm again, Flint rolled Hart to his stomach and pulled the arm behind him. Using his leg, Flint bent the arm backwards causing Hart to cry out in pain but not enough to make him submit. Using all his strength, Hart crawled forward and grabbed the ropes for a break.
Stepping back, Flint momentarily stopped only to charge right back in and send
Hart over the ropes and to the floor with a Clothesline. Hitting a Baseball
Slide, Flint joined Hart outside.
The fans were on their feet as Flint kept up his attack, sending Hart crashing into the ring steps then the ring barrier. A Drop Kick sent Hart over the railing and into the first row. Security moved in as Flint pulled the railing in closer to the ring.
Climbing back into the ring, Flint made a running dash across the ring and hit the ropes ... diving to the outside and spinning his body into a Somersault
Splash landing squarely on Hart. The audience was silent as Flint slowly pulled himself to his feet.
After Stomping Hart several times, he pulled him up and laid his arm across the ring barrier. Grabbing up a chair, Flint hopped up onto the ring barrier and raised his arm. Jumping up, he landed across Hart’s arm with his weapon.
Bending the chair in half, Flint fell off to the floor as the fans started chanting his name.
Daye was in to look at Hart’s arm and was ready to call the match when he was pushed away as Hart regained his feet. Out on the floor, the two started throwing punches at each other ... a one-armed Hart backed Flint up as several of his blows struck home.
A Hip Toss led into more Stomping as Hart craddled his limp arm. Rolling Flint into the ring, Hart came off the second rope with a Leg Drop and hooked Flint’s leg. Daye hit the mat once before Flint got a shoulder up.
Locking up in the center, Flint took Hart over with an Arm Drag into an Arm Bar and continued working the arm. Pushing it to the mat, Flint dropped a Knee on it twice.
Climbing the ropes, Flint jumped off with an Elbow Drop that missed as Hart rolled out of the way. Punching away at Flint with his one good arm, Hart worked his man into the corner then used his boot for a Choke.
A Low Blow caught Hart by surprise knocking him to the mat. Dragging Hart to the center of the ring, Flint’s Somersault Leg Drop landed square on the arm but rather than go for the pin, he pulled Hart to his feet and led him across the ring. Pushing him into the corner, Flint climbed the ropes still holding firmly onto Hart’s wrist. Flipping to the ring apron, he dropped to the floor, bringing the elbow down onto the turnbuckle, sending jolts of pain through
Hart’s body.
Daye was checking on the shaken Hart. Coming back into the ring, Flint grabbed the arm and spun it around before climbing the ropes again. Balancing himself with uncanny ability, Flint went for a stroll along the top rope, pulling Hart along as he went. At the center of the rope, Flint jumped into the air and
Scissored Hart’s arm with his legs. Flipping Hart over, Flint landed heavily on the mat with the arm still firmly in his grasp. Using both his arms and legs,
Flint had Hart tapping out from the pain.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Flint rolled from the ring and marched off to the back as the fans cheered while Peter Daye called for help from the back for Hart who was obviously in pain as he held his battered arm.
Leaving the ring, the camera made it’s way into the back once again, this time finding Aaron Starr with Lisa Doyle walking in the hallway.)
Aaron Starr: You and Russell seem to be awfully chummy lately ... helping you with your homework?
(The smug Starr continued walking as Doyle stopped, resting her hands on her hips as she watched her collegue walk off.)
Lisa Doyle: WHAT!?!?!?!?! (Leaving them, the camera found the Texas Heavyweight Champion with his manager, Mean Darlene in the hallway. A slightly perturbed Morgan spoke, flailing his arms about while Darlene rolled her eyes.)
Jake Morgan: All right, God Damnit! What's it going to take? What do you want, you fucking bloodsucker?
(Darlene clamped her hand over her eyes, shaking her head in disgust. Grabbing
Morgan by the arm, she spun him around to look at her.)
Mean Darlene: I keep telling you ... there is no tape! Flint’s playing mind games again. He’s trying to goad you into a match!
(Snapping around, Morgan addressed the camera.) Jake Morgan: If it's all that you claim that it is, I'll give you whatever you want ... (Darlene grabbed the Champion once again and spun him around.) Mean Darlene: Jake ... listen to me. It’s not real ... there is no videotape. He is trying to get you into a match. He’s after the belt and trying anything he can think of to get it back. Jake ... there is no videotape.
(Morgan pulled away from her grasp and turned back to the camera.) Jake Morgan: If it's not I'll make you eat that thing. (The camera faded out before focussing in on the ring where the Trashman and
Drake Russell waited for Peter Daye to call for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** The two locked up in the center of the ring and the bigger Russell pushed Trash back into the ropes with Daye calling for a break. The Champ stepped back with his hands up for a clean break before stepping forward and letting go with a big Chop.
There were a few boos as the Trashman was sent across the ring. On the return,
Russell Leap Frogged over him and caught him on the return, dumping him on his ass with a Hip Toss. Dropping down, Trash took an Elbow to the chest and a 1 count as Russell looked to end it early.
Circling, they locked up again, this time, Trash backing Russell up. Pushing him into a corner, Trash took a warning from Daye and stepped back ... taking a
Chop to the chest.
Angered, Trash fired in several Right Hands and staggered the Champ. Pushing his head down, the Trashman lifted Russell up for a Powerbomb but rather than drop him onto the mat, he rushed forward and smashed him backfirst into the top turnbuckle.
Taking exception to the shot to the chest, Trash started Stomping the downed
Russell before leaving the ring and tossing a chair in. Russell ducked a chairshot then grabbed Trash by the arm and sent him across the ring. On the return, Trash went down, taking a boot to the face. A Leg Drop followed into another pin attempt which netted the Champ a 2 count this time.
On his feet, Russell tossed Trash around with two Snap Suplexes and a Tilt-A-Whirl Backbreaker before taking a moment to talk to his fans. After strutting around the ring, Russell returned to the action by putting the boots to his opponent.
With a Snap Mare, Russell went into a Reverse Chinlock and called for Daye to check on the unmoving Trash. The ref checked for a Choke which had Russell shaking his head. Struggling while the fans cheered, Trashman worked back to his feet and knocked Russell off with a well placed Elbow to the ribs. Bouncing off the ropes, he floored the Champion with a Clothesline. Hitting the ropes, he came down with a Leg Drop that missed it’s mark as Russell rolled out at the last second.
A Boot to the face knocked Trash to his knees where he took a second shot to the face. With Trash on his back, Russell bounced off the ropes and came down with a Leg Drop of his own which did the same as Trash’s ... nothing.
With a Body Slam, Trashman booted Russell out of the ring as the Champ tried to roll out of the way. Stepping onto the apron, Trash went into a run and flew off with an Apron Dive, Somersaulting onto the prone Russell.
Grabbing the Champ by his hair, Trash banged his head down onto the ring barrier. To his credit, Russell fought back as the two battled around the ring before heading in once again.
Taking a Back Body Drop off the ropes, Trashman came back and hit a Spear. Leaving the ring, he set up a table on the outside as the fans jumped to their feet. Hammering away with several Right Hands, Trash pushed the Champion up the ropes. Standing on the top rope, Trashman pointed down at the table before leaping into the air and trying to land on top of Russell’s neck. Pushing forward, Russell sent Trashman flying off the top rope alone to fall backfirst through the table, splintering it as he landed on the hard floor.
Clearing his head, Russell climbed through the ropes to the outside of the ring where he pulled a groggy Trashman to his feet. Pushing Trash’s head down,
Russell grabbed his opponent with a Double Underhook, looking up just in time as several fans cried out ... to see a rampaging Shane Cornell charging at him with a chair held overhead.
Letting go of Trashman’s arms, Russell backed away quickly. Spinning around,
Trashman took the chair shot that rang out loudly and fell to the floor in a heap. Holding the badly dented chair in his hands, Cornell looked at Trashman then to Russell and shrugged his shoulders as a cheer went up. With the chair raised overhead, Cornell never saw the bandaged Pep Preston rush out to the ring. Wrapping his arms around Cornell’s midsection, Pep lifted the man off his feet and snapped him backwards onto the floor with a Belly-to-Back Suplex.
After booting Cornell several times, the angered Preston stormed off, slapping hands with the fans before disappearing into the back.
Looking at Daye then over to the unmoving Cornell and a bleeding Trashman,
Russell grabbed up Trash and rolled him back into the ring. Placing him in the center of the ring, the Champ climbed the ropes before leaping off and hitting the Total Decimation and taking the count.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Rolling out of the ring, Drake Russell snatched up his belt and headed to the back before the camera broke away and found Aaron Starr and Lisa Doyle in front of the PAW banner. A disgusted Starr shook his head as he looked over at the smiling Doyle.)
Aaron Starr: And that ladies and gentlemen ... is our Television Champion ... stealing yet another victory from the jaws of defeat ... (Catching Starr’s dripping sarcasm, Doyle snapped her head around and shot her co-host a deadly stare.) Lisa Doyle: That’s right, Aaron ... Drake Russell ... with his superior skills proved once again that there is no one in this promotion to derail him from his
Destiny ... and a shot at the Texas Heavyweight Title ...
(As the two were about to start arguing, the Texas Heavyweight Champion, Jake
Morgan followed Mean Darlene out from the back. The beautiful Darlene held
Morgan’s belt over her head for the fans to see as Jake neared the ring, calling for a mic.)
Jake Morgan: Johnny, you think that not being focused has kept you from winning all this time?
No, it wasn't that. I've already admitted that you are smart, talented and tough but those things haven't seemed to help you win either. Despite all of the reasons you should beat me, you haven't. You really want to know why?
(Morgan looked out at the fans as Darlene flipped the belt over her shoulder and held her arms out asking why.)
It's because I'm smarter, better and tougher than you can ever hope to be
Johnny. I'm not relying on my past wins, I'm relying on your inability to get the job done. You claim that you are through with Jason but he's still around.
You claimed that you would make Hart pay for his treatment of you but he's still talking smack everyday. You've claimed many times in the past that you were going to take me out, end my career but I'm still here, and I'm still on top of this promotion. So Johnny, quit talking about doing something and for once in your miserable, pathetic career, just do it!
(Dropping the mic, he and Darlene entered the ring when Johnny Green walked out confidently from the back. Stooping to pick up the mic Morgan dropped, he took a little stroll around the ring.)
Johnny Green: Jake ... Jake ... Jake ... when will you ever learn? Don’t get me wrong ... though your little speech would do Nike proud ... you’re not talking to one of those other chumps in the back ...
I’m not Sherlock Cornell ... I’m not the Trashman ... Pep Preston or any one of the other guys at the bottom of the ladder ... no Jake, what we have here is a bonafide contender to your belt ... and though you might have beaten me in the past ... like you said ... that was in the past ...
What’s done is done ... it’s time to change history ... let’s do this ... (Dropping the mic, Green slid into the ring and after being checked over by
Peter Daye, the bell was sounded.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Morgan charged in and hit the mat from a Drop Toe Hold. Allowed to stand,
Morgan charged in and was put down with a Japanese Arm Drag and a second one.
On his feet, the Champion looked at Green who shrugged before motioning for
Morgan to come and get some ...
Charging in, he sent Green to the mat with a savage Shoulder Butt and Fist Drop before scooping him off the canvas and Power Slamming Green. Momentarily stunned, Green took a 2 count before shaking Jake off.
Shaking his head and clearing out the cobwebs, Green caught Morgan between the eyes with a Punch that slowed the Champ long enough for Green to reach in and pull Morgan off his feet. Grabbing Morgan’s legs, Green flipped forward, putting his shoulders down to the mat for a 2 count before kicking out.
Reversing a Whip into the ropes, Morgan sent Green for the ride and caught him with a Boot to the face on the return. Green was on his back holding his jaw before being pulled up and slammed into the far corner. Staggering out of the corner, the challenger caught a Clothesline and dropped.
Pulled up roughly, Green was put down with a Short Clothesline and was on the recieving end of a beating both inside and outside of the ring. The fans, led by Mean Darlene were cheering for the Champ as he pummeled Green.
Morgan pulled out all the stops as he attempted to prove to Johnny Green once and for all just who the better man was. There was a reason why Morgan was the
Texas Heavyweight Champion ... the two-time Champion.
Morgan’s Superplex rocked Green who rolled from the ring and staggered about, throwing several Punches in the air, trying to hit an imaginary foe that just wasn’t there. Following to the outside, Morgan dove from the apron and landed a
Flying Clothesline, taking both men to the floor.
With a Facefirst Suplex onto the hard floor, Morgan pulled a table out from under the ring and set it up. Pushing Johnny into the ring, Morgan climbed up onto the apron and pulled Green up. With Green on the insdie of the ring,
Morgan attempted to lift him for a Suplex but Green fought him off, driving a
Shoulder into his stomach.
Diving over the top, Green pulled Morgan down onto .. and through the table with a Sunset Flip. Morgan crashed onto the floor. The fans gasped as Green pulled himself to his feet and staggered before dropping down on top of Morgan.
Daye hit the floor twice before a resiliant Morgan lifted a shoulder. Pulling himself up, Green staggered about as Darlene moved in to help Morgan who was still out. Seeing Darlene, Green grabbed her by the hair and spun her around. Pushing her down into a Standing Head Scissors, Green moved around so that she was facing the ring, her butt towards the fans ... and the camera before lifting her up for a Powerbomb.
Holding her up as she squirmed to get out of his grasp gave Morgan time to get to his feet and nail Green with a Punch. Darlene was able to slide off Green and get out of the way as Morgan and Green started hitting each other with
Lefts and Rights. Morgan backed Green up with heavy blows but caught a Knee to the crotch and dropped to a knee. A Double Axe Handle to the back was followed a Knee to the head.
Pushing Morgan into the ring, Green followed in carrying a chair. After Whipping Morgan into the ropes, Green leveled him with the chair to the midsection. Dropped to his knees, Morgan was tagged on the head with the chair and dropped to all fours as blood dripped down from a cut on his brow.
Hitting Morgan several times across the back, Green tossed the chair aside and pulled the bleeding Morgan to his feet. Trying to lift Morgan was blocked as
Green found an arm around his neck and with a slap on the back, the Champ dropped Green onto the chair with a DDT. Darlene slammed her fists on the apron pleading with Morgan to get to his feet. With both men down, Daye started a count.
Pulling himself up with the ropes, Morgan laid into Green and bloodied his knuckles on Green’s forehead. Green’s Low Blow caught Morgan and backed him up.
A Drop Kick put Morgan down in the corner on the seat of his pants where he took a Boot to the face.
After driving a Knee into Morgan’s face, Green picked him up and put him in the
Tree of Woe, leaving him hanging upside down. With Mean Darlene up on the apron trying to free Morgan, Green pushed her away with a hand to the face. Returning to business at hand, Green grabbed up the chair and laid it across Morgan’s face. Stepping back, Green took a dash across the ring and hit a Baseball
Slide, driving the chair into Morgan’s face with a crack.
Morgan’s legs came undone and dropped to the mat, trying to pull himself up with the ropes and was draped across them when a loud thumping sound came across the PA system.
*THUMP* *THUMP* Voice: Is this on? It was Jason Flint’s voice and his face soon appeared on the television monitors made available to the fans.
Jason Flint: Hey Jakey ... how’s it going? Seeing as you’re in a match, I’ll keep this quick for ya ... It’s a little preview from that classic called
“Jakey Gets Screwed” ... maybe you’ve heard of it?
Flint’s face was replaced by a picture of B.J. Perris and Johnny Green having a conversation, though there was no sound to accompany any of this, it was obvious they were talking. Turning from Green, Perris seemed to be talking to someone who was out of camerashot. It went on for several seconds before restarting. Again and again it played while in the ring, Morgan laid across the middle rope and struggled to get to his feet. Johnny Green in the meantime was arguing with Peter Daye about the proceedings when a cheer went up. Looking around for the commotion, Green turned right into a swinging Jason Flint who decked him with the Texas Heavyweight Title Belt. Taking the blow flush in the face, Green crumpled to the mat.
Flint was up on the ropes looking down on Green when the video abruptly stopped. Seconds went by before the face of B.J. Perris came on. Standing there, Perris was busy pulling videotape from it’s plastic housing, a smile was on his face.
Perris: Lovely filmwork there, Jason ... perhaps you should take up another profession in your spare time ... anyway ... as I promised you last week, I have a little surprise ... it’s just a little something I put together for you
... something you might call your worst fucking nightmare!!!
Perris started laughing when someone walked out from the back ... a very large someone. Ducking his head as he stepped out, the man was huge ... from his completely shaven head with the exception of a top knot pulled tightly back and hanging down ... down his large bare muscular chest ... to his billowy black
Sultan’s pants which ended in large red boots with an upturned pointed toe ...
he was big! Perris: Allow me to introduce you to 7 feet, 400 plus pounds of fury ... meet
Khan!!!!! Flint’s eyes went wide as the big man climbed into the ring over the top rope. With his deed done for the day, Flint was ready to run off when he turned to see both Kat Collins and the mysterious woman wearing the Offspring mask waiting for him on the ring apron.
Flint stopped in his tracks trapped like a rat. Backpeddling, he found himself in the grasp of the giant. Spun around in an impossibly big hand, Flint was lifted high into the air and slammed down with a One Handed Chokeslam that shook the ring.
With Flint’s body twitching on the canvas, Peter Daye called for the bell, ending the match because of the pandemonium.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (The camera focussed in on the giant who stood over Flint’s still twitching body while in the background, B.J. Perris laughed hysterically as the credits rolled.)
