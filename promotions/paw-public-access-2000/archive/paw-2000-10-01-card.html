10.1 Card

1 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

(The camera opens on Seth at a payphone outside of the PAw television studio.
Zooming in, we listen to his conversation.) Seth: Just do it. No, I don't care how much money it takes. I'll draw on my inheritance money. I still have that. What do you mean, I don't have that? How come no one ever told me about that? {sigh} Just get it done, OK? Yeah, I know. You're the best. That's why I came to you. OK. Later. (He hangs up the phone just as another person comes to use it. Shaking his head, he walks away as the camera leaves him. During the chamge of scenery, a voice is heard in the background before fading in on a locker room.)
Voice: Pretty weak ... that's all that is. (The scene opens up on Justin Hart, sitting reverse style in a chair. He's talking to a man whose appearance is obscured by a newspaper. Not knowing who it is or caring, Hart replies to his comment.)
Justin Hart: What is? Voice: What seems to be going on here in PAW ... with all the recent cuts and such, it just doesn't seem to have the same feel to it.
Justin Hart: Hmmm ... you have a point there, friend. (The man, who appears to be unaware of who he's talking to, reaches over to grab and take a swig of a longneck. Hart looks over at the people walking by while snacking on a slice of pizza.)
Voice: Just ain't the same as the good ol' days. Now I was around when we had seasoned pros performing week in and week out. But look at this ... what it's come to now. No one has the same work ethics as the vets anymore. There was a time when regardless of circumstances, we knew where we stood, as well as the fed. Now with all the cutbacks and lazy bastards, where's the REAL talent?
(Hart gets up, choking down the last bite of his pizza. Standing at the door, he looks back.)
Justin Hart: Well, there's the PAW Texas Heavyweight Champ ... gotta give him credit. He's got talent ... right?
Voice: Who ... that black militant jackass?!?! He's the worst of all! Justin Hart: Say what?!?! (Hart suddenly jerks to attention. He walks over and snatches the newspaper from the man to look him straight in the eye. He's as startled as the man behind the paper when they both realize who the other is.)
Justin Hart/Jake Morgan: YOU!!!! (Both men go nose to nose as the scene cuts away to the California Boy, Seth leaning back against a wall. With the camera zooming in, he sighs before speaking to himself.)
Seth: I’m stuck! I can’t get released and I can’t just walk out ... what the Hell am I going to do? (Unaware that he’s being listened to, Seth’s head whips around in surprise when he gets an answer.)
Woman’s voice: It could be worse ... Seth: What? Who? (The camera follws his gaze, zooming in on a saddened Lisa Doyle, leaning back against the same wall.)
Lisa Doyle: At least your boyfriend wasn’t fired ... and you don’t have some goofball chasing you around ...
Seth: I don’t have a ... Lisa Doyle: No need to explain ... (Doyle’s face bunches up in an unpleasant frown.) Here we go again ... (As she points off, the camera follows, focussing in on the Television Champion, Christian Maeda as he approaches ... then walks on by without so much as looking at his partner or Lisa Doyle.)
Christian Maeda: Hey!!!!! Perris!!!!! (Both Doyle and Seth shrug their shoulders as Maeda walks on. Following the Champion, the camera comes to a halt as Maeda grabs President B.J. Perris by the shoulder.)

What can I do for you? Christian Maeda:I want you to call off the match between H-Bomb and myself. Perris: Why would I call off a match? Christian Maeda: I figure you owe me one. Perris: Owe you! For what? Christian Maeda: For being the only star in this pathetic little promotion! If it wasn't for me you would have had to shut the doors.
(Perris chuckles.) Perris: Okay, if that's the case, then you should have no problem winning your match. Christian Maeda: That is true but that's not the issue. You see, Hiro doesn't deserve to be in the ring with me! He doesn't deserve my belt! And, he certainly doesn't deserve to put his dirty Chinese hands on me!
(Perris fidgets as he looks down at his watch once again.) Perris: I'll see what I can do. (Maeda smiles as they shake hands.) Christian Maeda: I knew you'd see it my way! Perris: By the way, didn’t I already grant you your release last week? (A confused Perris looks at a flabbergasted Maeda before shaking his head and walking off when large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession.)
.... 5 ... .... 4 ... .... 3 ... .... 2 ... .... 1 ... (White Zombie's "More Human than Human" begins as flashes of the PAW stars in action are shown. Seth ... Skip Westervelt ... Hiroshima Nagasaki ... Jake

Aaron Starr: Another week and another action packed card! We’ve got two big matches this week with the Television Title up for grabs as Hiroshima Nagasaki attempts to pry it from the clutching fingers of Christian Maeda ...

Woman in the mask: In fact, we’re not going to have any matches this evening ... nor will there be any next week or the following week ... (A surprised Starr looked around as the boos started coming in.) I’m here to pull the plug on tonight’s show ... which effectively does the same for the promotion.

Woman in the mask: I wish there was some other way but you know as well as I do with UNT pulling out, you’ll no longer have a weekly television program or a venue. Woman in the mask: That’s right. If you win, I sell my half of the promotion to the university ... and they keep you on your feet. I walk away and you never hear from me again.
Perris: And if you win? Woman in the mask: If I win ... you sell your half of the promotion and you walk away. Either way, Public Access Wrestling lives ...

Perris: Alright ... I choose Khan to fight for me ... (The masked woman shook her head.) Woman in the mask: I’m sorry ... he was let go two weeks ago. Perris: Johnny Green? (The woman shook her head again causing Perris to gulp.) Woman in the mask: Sorry. Perris: He’s gone, too? (The woman in the mask looked around at the silent crowd before stepping forward.) All you have to do B.J. is beat ... Justin Hart ... (The fans went nuts while Perris went white as a sheet, running his fingers through his dyed blond locks. Turning, the woman in the mask led her entourage to the back, leaving the camera focussed on a shocked Aaron Starr.)
Aaron Starr: Justin Hart ... B.J. Perris ... tonight! Now let’s go to the ring for our first match of the evening!

Jake who entwines his legs and arms with Seth's and rolls him up for the pin but he only gets a 2 count.

Pushing her back, Seth complains to the ref who moves in to seperate the two allowing Seth to continue on his opponent.

Lunging, Morgan staggers Seth with a Forearm to the head. A Boot to the stomach doubles him over before being lifted into the air out a Standing Head Scissors.
Running towards the remaining table, Jake puts him through it with a running Powerbomb. The fans go silent as Seth lays amid the rubble of the table, unmoving. Roaring his approval, Jake rolls Seth back into the ring and pins him. *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding ***

Staggering back into the corner, Perris took a beating as Hart climbed up onto the ropes and fired away with a dozen or so Punches. Stepping back out of the corner, the Champ watched as Perris fell facefirst onto the mat.
After taking a bow, Hart used a Gutwrench to pull Perris up then fall backwards with a Belly-to-Back Suplex. Bridging, Hart had Perris in a very bad predicament. Flint dropped to the mat and slapped out a 2 count until seeing
Perris with a hand on the bottom rope. A disgusted Hart backed away while Perris argued with Flint who wagged a warning finger. Turning back to the match, Perris caught a Clothesline that nearly took his head off. Climbing up the ropes in the corner, Hart slapped his elbow before leaping off. The Elbow Drop hit the mat as Perris rolled out of the way.
Scrambling, Perris saw an opening and latched onto Hart’s ankle. Looking for a submission or at least a way to stop Hart, Perris held on for dear life. With a grimace on his face, Hart struggled to pull himself across the ring to the ropes and his salvation. He was not about to give up.
Inches away, Hart was pulled to the ropes by the woman in the mask who backed away from the ring before Flint could see her. Hart used the ropes to return to his feet and was limping slightly as Perris came in with a couple of Front
Kicks. Flint allowed a Kick to Hart’s head before pushing Perris back for a rope break. Shaking his head, Hart was complaining which drew a shrug from Flint.
Moving forward to lock up, Hart found himself tripped up by the ref who got a foot in the way.
With Hart on the mat, Perris dropped down and again attacked the ankle. Flint turned away from the action to argue with the woman in the mask over his action. Flint kept touching his ear as if he couldn’t hear her.
Finally turning back to see Perris with a pinning predicament, Flint dropped to the mat and slowly slapped a 1 count before Hart tossed off Perris. Perris complained about the slow count while Hart pulled himself to his feet.
Grabbing Perris from behind, Hart fell back with a Side Russian Leg Sweep. Rolling back to his feet, Hart threatened Flint who stepped back and waved a finger in his face. Hart raised a fist but stepped back when Flint threatened to throw the match out.
Pulling Perris to his feet, Hart drove a Boot into Perris’ midsection before throwing himself back against the ropes. Bouncing forward, Hart jumped into the air before smashing Perris’ face into the canvas. On the outside of the ring, the woman in the mask applauded her hand picked “champion’s” actions.
Pulling Perris up, Hart worked him up the ropes, climbing up as well. Balancing on the top rope, Hart dropped down onto the top rope as the ref slapped Hart’s foot out from under him. Dropping across the rope brought a groan from the fans as Hart fell to the mat in pain.
With both men down, Flint leaned back against the ropes after warning the woman in the mask back to the floor from the ring apron. Hart was back on his feet, giving Flint an evil look which didn’t seem to faze the ref one bit. Flint’s reply was to point at the ropes and shake his head.
Moving back to the center of the ring, Flint urged the two wrestlers back to action ... Hart leaning against the ropes and Perris still seated on the top rope in the corner. Moving back to his opponent, Hart pulled Perris off the top rope and Gorilla Pressed him into the air.
Stepping out from underneath him, Hart dropped Perris like a sack of potatoes. Grabbing him up by an arm, Hart Whipped him hard into the oposite corner. Waiting across the ring, Hart waited until Flint looked in on Perris before taking off. Dashing across the ring, Hart jumped into the air and nailed both men with the Hang Time.
Both men fell to the mat as Hart limped back to the center of the ring and pumped his arms into the air. Dragging Perris back to the center of the ring by a leg, Hart locked on the Urban Lockdown. Perris struggled as Hart leaned back, putting more pressure on Perris’ back.
With nowhere to go in the center of the ring, the end was inevitable as Perris hopelessly tapped out.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Flint moved in to hold Hart’s arm up but the Texas Champion was not about to let that happen as he walked away and grabbed up his coveted belt.
The woman in the mask and the rest of her entourage clapped their approval as Hart marched off. In the center of the ring, Flint leaned over the still downed Perris and pulled something from his pocket. After dropping it onto Perris’ chest, he walked out of the ring leaving the beaten and now former owner of Public Access Wrestling all alone.
