7.2 Card

2 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The cameras open up as Shane Cornell is making his way to the Campus Theater. As he steps towards the backstage doors, he stops. The camera pans around behind him as he is looking at a table that has been set up in front of the backstage doors.)
Shane Cornell: What the hell? What kind of sick joke is this? (Cornell shakes his head as he goes around the table and pulls on the double doors. Suddenly, Jason Flint comes charging out, smashing a ceramic jar of some sort, shattering it across Cornell's face, staggering the "True Franchise."
Flint storms in and grabs Cornell by his neck by both hands, raising him into the air and driving him and Cornell through the table, shattering it. Kipping to his feet, Flint stumbles a bit before grabbing a garbage can. Dumping out the contents over the downed Cornell, Flint grabs Cornell by the shirt and pulls him up.)
Jason Flint: Adios Cornell... (Cornell's eyes just roll back into his head, as Flint shoves him back down and heads back into the building.)
Two more to go.... (Leaving them, the camera finds PAW President B.J. Perris in the hallways of the PAW Television studio. Moving through the hallway, led by Shane, the head of Security, Perris makes his way to the back entrance where he encounters wrestler Justin Hart.
Hart, with his arm in a sling, makes stern glances at Security and studio personel as he goes, stopping as Perris and Shane blocks his path.)
Perris: We have to talk ... (Hart looks bored as he awaits to hear what Perris has to say.) I’ve been on the phone with the doctor who checked your arm ... he says you can’t wrestle. Justin Hart: WHAT!?!?!?!? Perris: You heard me ... under doctor’s orders ... I’m not allowing you to wrestle ... not until you get a doctor’s clearance. Consider yourself out of commission ...
(Pushing past Perris, Hart grunts as he walks off. Shane steps forward to grab him but is stopped by a hand on the shoulder from the boss.)
Let him go ... (As Perris stands there shaking his head, he is approached by a casually dressed middle aged gentlemen followed by several members of the television crew. Of note, the head of the production crew with his head still bandaged from last week’s attack by Jason Flint.)
Perris: Ah ... Mr. Gibson, what can I do for you this evening? Mr. Gibson: I think it’s time we came to an understanding ... my students were going to produce your program ... for credit and a nominal fee ... there was no discussion of them being involved in the ... the physicality of this ... this barbaric display ...
(Perris tries to calm the man but seems to have no luck.) Dean Hurley may have agreed to your company being on campus and your shows produced here but I’m putting my foot down when one of my students gets injured ...
I will simply not tolerate this ... (Angered, he points to the bandaged head of his student.) Do I make myself clear? (Perris nods his head in agreement.) Perris: I understand and rest assured that this will never happen again. I promise you ... certain persons will be ... reprimanded ...
(In a huff, the man storms off followed by the student crew. Leaning over to
Shane, Perris gives orders.)
Get Khan ... and put him on Flint ... (With a nod of the head, Shane walks off, leaving Perris there alone ... alone momentarily as he is approached by referee Peter Daye.)
Peter Daye: A minute of your time? (Perris looks at his watch before looking at Daye.) Perris: Shoot ... Peter Daye: Lately ... I haven’t been allowed to do my job ... in your little vendetta against Flint, you’ve been sticking your nose into matches ... and as your Senior Referee ... it’s got to stop ...
(Perris chuckles as he looks down at his watch again.) Perris: Time’s up ... (Looking away from Daye, Perris sees someone he’s been waiting for and rushes off after them.)
Jake! (Following Perris, he approaches the Texas Heavyweight Champion entering the building with manager Mean Darlene. The Champion does not look happy based solely on the scowl on his face.)
Jake ... just the man I want to see ... (Morgan shoots Perris a disinterested eye.) Jake Morgan: Yeah ... Perris: About this tape of Flint’s ... it’s not real ... he’s just trying to get a shot at your belt and get you to force me to sanction a bout ... that’s all ... head games ...
(Morgan’s manager, Mean Darlene steps forward and slaps the Champ on the shoulder.) Mean Darlene: See ... that’s what I’ve been telling you all week ... forget him. You’ve got this Maeda guy to destroy ... don’t let Flint get under your skin.
(A reluctant Morgan nods his head before heading off. As he leaves, Darlene shrugs here shoulders and gives Perris a questioning look as the President shakes his head. With his Champion gone, Perris breathes a sigh of relief as large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession.)

Dropping to a knee, he brought Maeda down across his knee with a Side Breaker. With the two men down, Morgan pulled a table out from under the ring and set it up on the floor. Pulling Maeda up, he caught a Double Axe Handle to the back before Thorne bludgeoned his head into the table. Pulled back by his hair,
Morgan caught Thorne with an Elbow.
Taking a Kick to the back of his knee, Morgan grabbed Maeda and slammed his head into the turnbuckle then hoisted him up with a Fireman’s Carry. Spinning
Maeda off his shoulders, Morgan brought him down through the table with a Slam.
Raising his arms into the air, Morgan waited until Thorne regained his feet before putting him down with a Spear. Taking a minute to catch his breath, he grabbed up Maeda and slammed him into the barrier. Sitting down, Maeda was in a bad predicament as Mean Darlene held the Title Belt over his face. Taking a running start, Morgan came in feet first and kicked the belt into Maeda’s face which brought chants of “EXTREME!” from the fans.
After hugging his manager, Morgan rolled Maeda into the ring and covered him, getting a 2 count. Pushed into the ropes, Maeda was spun through the air with a big Clothesline before taking a Suplex. Morgan had a cover but was tapped on the shoulder by Daye who pointed at Maeda’s foot on the bottom rope.
Pulling Maeda to the center of the ring, Morgan had a few choice words for the rookie. Darlene tossed the belt to Morgan who put it down on Maeda’s face before coming down with a Leg Drop. Maeda popped up seated with a slight trickle of blood from his head.
Morgan strolled around the ring with his held high overhead when Maeda jumped up and grabbed the Champ and put him down with the Total Heartbreak, his
Reverse Russian Legsweep finisher. Grabbing a leg, Maeda locked in a Half
Boston Crab. Daye moved in looking for the submission when Mean Darlene jumped up onto the ring apron.
The fans were booing as Maeda sat back trying to bend Morgan in half. Mean
Darlene was vehemently arguing with Daye and grabbed him by the shirt to pull him closer.
Focussed on Morgan, Maeda didn’t see the person entering the ring from behind. Rushing forward, Drake Russell jumped into the air and grabbed Maeda by the hair and Bulldogged him into the mat. Rolling out of the ring, Russell never looked back as he disappeared through the doorway to the bowels of the studio.
Pulling himself to his feet, Jake Morgan dropped the belt in the center of the ring then pulled Maeda to his feet. Wrapping an arm around his head, Morgan looked out at the fans. Slapping Christian Maeda on the back, Morgan fell backwards with a DDT, driving Maeda’s face into the belt.
Letting go of Daye, Darlene dropped to the outside of the ring. Turning, Daye saw Morgan hook a leg for the pin as he dropped for the 3 count.
