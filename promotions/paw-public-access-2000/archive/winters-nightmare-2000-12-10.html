Winter's Nightmare: PAW versus Japan

10 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

(The camera pans the outside of the Denton Convention Center, moving through the small packed parking lot, up to the front doors as fans file in for the evening’s event.
Passing by the throng of fans, the camera continues around the circular building, making it’s way to the back where it focusses in on a solitary figure in an OU jacket to fight the chill of the evening air. With a serious look on his face, the clean cut man with his short, gelled hair, square jaw and hawkish nose cut a menacing figure as he approached the back entrance of the building.

Voice: Whoa momma! (Spinning on her heels, Sparks was confronted by the blue sequined white outfit of a hip swinging Paco of the Heartbreakers as he approached.)

(Hart looked off to the side, tickled at what he was hearing.) Security #1 Just follow us ... please? (Hart picked up his duffelbag, then flipped them the bird.) Justin Hart: Nope. (Having given his answer, he walked inside, leaving the two security guards out there wondering what happened.)

Masked Woman: I don’t know what to say, other than thank you. (Handing it back to Davis, the masked woman and Nakamura shook hands while the rest of Team Japan bowed deeply.)

*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding ***
With the bell both men came out of their respective corners with a differing style. Tsaruta was slow and methodical as he circled, working to cut off the ring and the frenetic Gannon bounced back and forth, going one way then the other as if hyped up on too much caffeine.
A cheer went up as the two locked up in the center, the bigger Tsaruta pushing Kidd back into the ropes, the ref moving in looking for a break. Tsaruta stepped back cleanly. On the move again, the locked up with Gannon gaining control, spinning into a Go Behind and taking the arm into a Hammerlock.
Tsaruta spun one way then the other trying to break free before dipping down and throwing Kidd over out of a Fireman’s Carry and into an Arm Bar.
Kidd tried to move to the ropes for a break but the bigger Tsaruta held him firmly in place. On the outside of the ring Tsaruta’s manager, Angel Mishima clapped her approval as she shouted instructions. Letting go of the hold,
Tsaruta smashed several Elbows to Kidd’s shoulder, taking him to one knee. Rolling forward then back, Gannon reversed positions, locking in an Arm Bar of his own. Twisting the arm, Kidd worked the elbow over then moved down to the wrist, pulling down sharply and stomping heavily on the mat as he jumped up and down. Not rushed, Tsaruta fired in a Back Elbow and staggered Kidd.
Moving in with a battering Forearm, Tsaruta then hit with a Double Throat Chop. A scoop up and spin into a Tilt-A-Whirl Backbreaker put Kidd on the mat and into a pinning predicament. Akatani dropped to the mat and slapped out a 2 count.
There was a sigh of relief as the ref came up showing two fingers in the near pinfall. Whipped into the ropes, Gannon shot across the ring, ducking under a
Clothesline and continuing on. Hitting the ropes, he came back with Tsaruta ducking down to take him over. Rolling over his opponent’s back, Gannon landed on his feet, hooking his arms and falling to his knees with a Backslide. Kidd returned the favor with a 2 count of his own.
Moving in, Tsaruta was taken down and thrown to the mat with an Arm Drag but rolled to his feet. Moving in again, he lashed out with a Front Kick. With
Kidd’s head dropping down, Tsaruta grabbed his arms for a Double Underwing. Lifting Kidd into the air, Tsaruta dropped him onto his back with a Powerbomb and held him down for a count. The ref was in position to get a 2 count before
Kidd rolled a shoulder. Holding onto Kidd, Tsaruta lifted him into the air again showing great strength. Being held up, Kidd wrapped his legs around Tsaruta’s neck and spun his body until they were both facing the same direction. Hooking the shoulders with his legs, Kidd rolled forward and put Tsaruta to the mat with a Victory
Roll. Tsaruta kicked out on a 2 count. Both men bounced back to their feet as the fans cheered. Gannon started circling, clapping his hands to get the fans into the match to which they responded. Moving in, Kidd was thrown over with an Arm Drag but landed on his feet, jumping up onto the corner ropes in one fluid motion and throwing his body back and twist into a twisting Body Press. Akatani hit the mat for a 1 count as a ready Tsaruta threw his attacker off.
Kidd came in again and was taken to the mat with a Drop Toe Hold. All over Kidd in a hurry, Tsaruta wrapped him up and dropped back with a Suspended Surfboard.
Akatani was in looking for a submission but Kidd held on, shaking his head. Releasing the hold, Tsaruta Whipped Kidd hard into the corner and charged in. With a Tip Up, Kidd came out of the corner over Tsaruta and behind him. Grabbing Tsaruta’s head, Kidd bashed it into the top turnbuckle repeatedly until the ref moved in with a count.
Spinning Tsaruta around, Gannon fired away with Reverse Knife Hands while Akatani counted, waving a finger in the young wrestler’s face. Ignoring him, Kidd jumped up and took Tsaruta over with a Monkey Flip to the center of the ring. Sitting up, Tsaruta took a Drop Kick to the back of his head. On the outside, Angel bowed her head, shaking it as the fans cheered Kidd’s building momentum. With a big grin, Kidd grabbed Tsaruta’s arm and lifted it into the air before coming down across it with a Leg Drop. Pulling Tsaruta to his knees, Kidd wrapped the arm around his leg and rolled his body, grabbing the free arm and putting his opponent to the mat with a La Majistral.
With his back on the mat and Angel Mishima slapping the canvas, Tsaruta barely worked free of the hold before being counted out. The fans reacted to the near fall as Akatani again came up with two fingers in the air.
Pulled to his feet, Tsaruta was put right back down with a Snap Suplex and was covered, pushing Kidd off on a 2 count. Tsaruta was slow to get up and took a
Stomping before being pulled close to a corner. Climbing the ropes, Kidd went to the top and jumped off, sitting down across the ropes and throwing his body back snapping over and landing on top with a Moonsault. The only problem was that Tsaruta was cooperating and laying still as his knees shot up catching
Kidd in the stomach. With Kidd on the mat in pain, Tsaruta was once again on his feet taking orders from Angel. After Stomping Gannon and pulling him to the center of the ring,
Tsaruta went into high gear flooring Gannon with a Reverse Russian Leg Sweep. With a Whip into the ropes, Gannon was put down on the return with a Knee to the midsection as he charged in. Spinning through the air, Gannon hit the mat hard and rolled to the ring apron from the force of the blow.
Pulling him up by his hair, Tsaruta brought him back into the ring with a Suplex. Crossing Kidd’s arms across his chest and standing over him with his arms outstretched drew the ire of the crowd as they started booing, sensing the beginning of the end for the debuting Kidd Gannon.
With only Angel Mishima’s applause, Tsaruta dropped forward with a Headbutt. Rising to his feet rather than going for the pin brought more jeering from the fans and a finger waving from the referee.
Walking around the ring, Tsaruta looked to Mishima as if for some direction getting a shaking head from the beautiful woman. Returning to Gannon with a scowl on his face, Tsaruta pulled him to his knees, taking a Shoulder to the stomach for it but stopping any further action with an Elbow to the back of his opponent.
Kidd was pulled to his feet and Whipped around for a ride into the ropes. Heading across the ring, he fired back on the return, catching a surprised Tsaruta with a Flying Cross Body and hooked a leg for the pin as they landed. The ref was right there for the count, nearly slapping out the 3 before Gannon was thrown off.
Lashing out with a Front Kick, Kidd caught Tsaruta coming in before dashing up the ropes. From the top, the leapt off flying over Tsaruta, catching him on the way over and pulling him to the mat with his Super Sunset Flip into a pin attempt for a 2 count.
An Open Palm Strike stopped Kidd in his tracks and was followed up with a Knee Lift. Whipped into the ropes, Kidd was caught up and thrown back over Tsaruta’s head. Landing on his feet, Kidd shot forward into the ropes, bouncing back and returning to the turning Tsaruta with a Flying Forearm.
Bouncing to his feet, Kidd pulled Tsaruta up and reared back with a closed Fist. Akatani caught the arm and pulled it back. Spinning around, Kidd caught a wagging finger from the ref with a warning about closed fisted Punches. A
Tsaruta Knee to the back brought Kidd back into the match. Locking his arms around Gannon’s body, Tsaruta threw him over with a Sambo Suplex. Slowly walking around the downed Gannon, Tsaruta listened to the booing filtering in.
Stopping to threaten some fans, Tsaruta neared the ropes where Mishima was watching. Again the scowling wrestler looked to his manager for some direction, getting the same reaction as before ... a shaking head with downcast eyes.
With Jinbei Akatani looking in on Gannon, Tsaruta reached into his trunks and pulled free a length of chain given to him by Lonny Nakamura. Wrapping the cold metal links around his fist, the hardcore wrestler looked to the head shaking
Mishima. The scowl slowly melted into a slight grin as he unwrapped the chain and tossed it to his manager.
Moving back in, Tsaruta pulled Gannon to his feet and Whipped him into the far ropes, catching him on the return. With one fluid motion, Kidd was lifted into a Vertical position and held as Tsaruta turned to the fans. With a bellow,
Tsaruta was about to drop but lost his hold as Kidd squirmed and dropped behind him. Spinning Tsaruta around, Kidd jumped into the air and twisted his body as he Scissor Locked his body and rolled to the mat with a Forward Rolling Cradle.
Akatani hit at twice until Tsaruta rolled his body and pinned Gannon to the mat. Akatani rolled to his back and thrust two fingers in the air for the second pin attempt just before Gannon rolled through and put Tsaruta’s shoulders down again.
Chant’s of “KIDD!!!!!” echoed through the convention center as the two men continued to roll around the mat, getting near pinfalls. Nearing the ropes,
Kidd Gannon grabbed and held onto the middle rope as Akatani’s hand hit the mat. A frantic Angel Mishima was screaming at the referee as his hand dropped for a second time. Hearing her cries, he looked up just after Gannon let go of the ropes ... his hand hitting the mat for the third time and the PAW team had it’s first victory of the evening.
