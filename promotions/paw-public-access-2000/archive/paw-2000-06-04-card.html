6.4 Card

4 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The cameras catch up with the former Champion, Jason Flint who is making his way into the PAW television studio. Flint is quiet and lost in his CD that's blaring through his headphones. There, nearby, Aaron Starr is sketching something in his notepad as he notices the former champion, making his way in.)
<Aaron Starr> Jason! (Flint continues to walk as Starr has to literally step in front of Flint to stop him. Flint reluctantly stops and pulls off his headphones.)
<Jason Flint> What Starr? What the hell do you want? <Aaron Starr> As the "Voice of the Fans" could you tell me what your feelings are now one week later of Back and Bad are? You've been unusually quiet this week.
<Jason Flint> Sure...Aaron, I'll tell you what my feelings are about right now. (Flint rubs his eyes before putting his headphones into his backpack. Suddenly
Flint charges at Starr, slamming him into the nearby brick wall, clenching
Starr's shirt.)
My feelings right now, Aaron, is that of STILL PISSED OFF. Back and Bad has come and gone. Back were assholes like Adam Manson and Neo Goof, take your choice of which one I'm talking about. Bad was for Johnny Green and Dan Glass to turn on me and cost me my belt. Back was the Urban Street Soldier, Justin
Hart. Bad was him becoming the new PAW champion. And right now....
(Flint sneers at a sweating Starr.) Back is me....back on track to do a little house cleaning. Bad is for what's going to happen today.....got it?
<Aaron Starr> Got....it. <Jason Flint> Good. See ya around there, "Voice." (Flint lets go of Starr and points a single finger at him as Flint enters the building when large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession.)
... 5 ... ... 4 ... ... 3 ... ... 2 ... ... 1 ... (Rob Zombie's "More Human than Human" starts to play loudly as clips of Public
Access Wrestling 2000's Superstars in action passes ... Skip Westervelt ...
Drake Russell ... Jake Morgan ... Jason Flint ... Johnny Green and finally, the
Texas Heavyweight Champion, Justin Hart, standing in the center of the ring, his arms crossed, the belt around his waist...
With an explosion, the scenes disappear from the screen and are replaced by the logo of Public Access Wrestling 2000 as it moves forward, filling the screen.
The song suddenly stops and with a smooth transition, fades out ... Fading in on Lisa Doyle, who has replaced a roughed up Aaron Starr at the start of the program.)
<Lisa Doyle> Welcome one and all to Public Access Wrestling! I'm Lisa Doyle, filling in for your usual host Aaron Starr ...
(Doyle holds a hand to her ear listening intently before looking up at the fans and the home viewers.)
I'm getting word that a fight has broken out in the back and PAW Television
Champion Drake Russell has attacked the Trashman, his partner for this evening's match against Jason Flint ...
Let's go to the back! (The camera breaks away into the backstage area to find Drake Russell slamming a garbage can over a bloody Trashman's head. A second blast knocks Trash to his knees before Russell pushes the cameraman out of the way before resuming his pasting of Trash, denting the can flat on one side and covering it in blood.
Tossing the can aside, he he pulls Trash to his feet and Whips him into the wall, catching him and dropping to the floor with a Reverse DDT. Picking himself up, Russell threatens the cameraman before picking up Trash and slamming him down with a Double Underhook Back Breaker.
PAW Security moves in to seperate the wrestlers but back away as Russell agains threatens them. With Shane, the head of Security moving in to reason with the
Champion, Russell turns and walks off.
The camera zooms in on the bleeding Trashman, blood pouring out of his head before breaking away to another part of the backstage area ...
... to Jason Flint and Kat Collins having a moment together. Kat sitting on
Flint's lap with her arms around Flint's neck, smiles, as a reluctant Flint is lost somewhere in thought.)
<Kat Collins> Penny for your thoughts. <Jason Flint> You sure you want to hear it? <Kat Collins> Is it good news? (Flint cracks a sly smile.) <Jason Flint> Depends. Kat....my dear Kat....my thoughts of the past week have been circling through the events that lead to my fall from grace. And in conclusion, if everyone wants no part of Jason Flint, the I don't want any part of them. Kat....you're done.
(Flint stands up, sliding Kat off of his lap onto the floor.) <Kat Collins> W-what do you mean "done?" <Jason Flint> Done babe, I'm done with you. Go on your merry way and leave me the fuck alone. I too remember back when you lured me to that hotel room and had your brother jump me. I don't need that in my life. So you can go FUCK
YOURSELF for all I care.
(Flint begins to make his way towards a door, when a chair comes flying through, striking the wall.)
<Kat Collins> BASTARD! <Jason Flint> Yep, ain't life a bitch. (Flint opens the door and then leaves. With Flint leaving the room, the camera returned to a smiling Kat Collins. She started laughing before turning around and looking back at another door which was opening. Stepping into the room, a shapely figure wearing the Offspring's mask stood before Flint's former lady.)
<Kat Collins> Did you hear that? (The masked woman nodded her head up and down.) Fuck me? Well ... fuck him! (The camera faded out as the two women laughed. Moving back to ringside, the fans were cheering as the youthful J was being introduced and across from him a bored Shane Cornell disgustedly waved at him then the fans.
After being checked over by the ref, the match was started.) *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Things started off slowly which prompted some boos from the fans and even a chant of "Boring" before J kicked it up a notch and took to the air, hitting a top rope Hurricanrana for a pin attempt.
Keeping on the offensive, J's aerial assault had Cornell in all kinds of trouble. Hitting with a Flying Shoulder Block, a Springboard Dropkick and a
Sunset Flip, J nearly took the match several times only to be thwarted as
Cornell barely kicked out each time.
After being Whipped into a corner, Cornell caught a charging J with a Boot to the face, then landing a Leg Drop and making the cover. Daye slapped the mat for a 1 count as J kicked out. Pulling J to his feet, Cornell tossed him to the outside of the ring before strutting around. The fans booed loudly while several pelted the cocky wrestler with crumpled cups and other objects. Cornell pointed a threatening finger before waving them off.
Leaving the ring, he rolled J back in and continued the beating, Stomping J. Pulling him back up, Cornall lifted him into the air for a Suplex but a wiry J slid off his back and landed on his feet behind Cornell. Grabbing an arm, J sent him into the far ropes and took him down with a Drop Kick to the face.
Hitting the ropes, J came down with a Knee to the face and making a cover. On the 2 count, Cornell, still not quite done, lifted his shoulder.
Charging for the far ropes, J jumped up onto the top rope and started to throw his body backwards when his feet slipped off and he landed flat on his back in the middle of the ring. Looking at the two downed wrestlers, Daye started his count. Cornell was up first and pulled J up by his hair and smashed him into a corner, working him over with Reverse Knife Hands to the chest.
Rearing back, Cornell threw a big Punch that tagged J and buckled his knees. Pulling J from the corner, Cornell spun him around into a Dragon Sleeper. Sticking his tongue out at the camera as it zoomed into his face, Cornell lifted J into the air. Kicking his legs, J flipped over behind his oppoent and hooked his arms. Dropping to the mat on his knees, J leaned forward and pulled
Cornell down, pinning his shoulders to the canvas with a Backslide. With
Cornell kicking his legs trying to get free, Daye dropped to the mat and slapped out a 3 count.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (J jumped up onto the ropes and raised his arms in victory as the lackluster
Cornell rolled out of the ring and made his way to the back followed by boos from the small but vocal crowd. With Cornell disappearing into the back so too did the camera ... to the backstage area where Jason Flint is walking around in the back, halfheartedly. Turning the corner, Flint almost crashes into the likes of Kimberly Davis, sitting on some boxes, thumbing through a magazine.)
<Jason Flint> Sorry. <Kimberly Davis> Not a Problem. I mean the effects of last week are probably lingering over your deflated head nowadays.
<Jason Flint> Yeah, sure. <Kimberly Davis> At least you still have Kat right? Oh wait, you don't....awww too bad. (Flint cracks a bored smile.) <Jason Flint> Anything else, Kim? Anything else you want to throw at me? Tell you what, if you got another insult to give me, why don't you move that ass of yours and find me, I'll be here all fucking day. Now if you'll excuse me.
(Flint passes by a stunned Davis as the camera leaves them and finds Mean
Darlene walking determindly through the hallway when she is grabbed by the arm.
Looking starled, she whips her head around to face her grasper, Jake Morgan.) <Jake Morgan> Where the Hell have you been? Last week, you're gone and now you show up late ... what's going on?
(Pulling free from his grip, she turns and starts to walk off.) <Mean Darlene> I've been ... busy ... now let's go make you the Texas Heavyweight Champion ... (As she walks off, the camera zooms in on Morgan's confused face. Leaving them, the camera moves on to find the "dynamic duo" of Chip and Skip Westervelt walking through the halls. Westervelt, leading the way talks to the listening
Chip.)
<Skip Westervelt> What is this place coming to? The current Television Champ in a handicap match against the former Texas Heavyweight Champ ... and you want worse?
Justin Hart throws down the Texas Heavyweight Title after holding it for less than a week? Can you believe it?
I mean, what the Hell is going on here? Does no one defend their titles anymore? (Spinning on his heels, Skip grabs Chip by his shirt and shakes him.) Does no one in this promotion know what these titles represent? Do they? What is wrong with them?
(Chip shrugged his shoulders at the shaking Westervelt before the camera moved back to the ring when "Bittersweet" by Fuel hits at as Jason Flint makes his way to the ring, still dressed in his street clothes and backpack. Making his way into the ring, Flint motions for a mic.
<Jason Flint> You know....Jake Morgan said that my time was up. Perris said that I would get mine in the end. And I know Justin Hart is eating up the moment again and again. Fine. Does that bother me, no.
(Flint looks down and shakes his head no.) It took the likes of some flash of cash to bring back the delinquents of PAW to beat me down. It took the likes of Johnny Green and his promise to cost me the belt. And it took the likes of Dan Glass....poor Dan Glass to wise up and look out for number one. Many factors in this fairy tale, but there are more chapters to be written.
(Flint leans heavily onto the ring ropes, eyeing the camera.) Its amazing that even though I'm not the PAW Champion, I'm still a man with a bullseye on his chest. I'm not the champion but Drake Russell still wants a piece of me. Gee, Drake, I figured that since you've got these blinders on about getting a belt, I'd be a shadow in the back of your mind, guess not. And
I thought you had brains, Mr. Destiny. You prattle on and on about bags and destiny, but what destiny is there in beating me ... now? I have nothing ... no
Entourage, no Johnny Green ... no one. You want me ... well here I am. Bring out my two darling opponents and let's get it this on.
("Spiders" by System of a Down was next as the NEW PAW Television Champion,
Drake Russell made his way from the back. With the TV belt strapped snugly around Russell's waist, Russell was very focused on the fact that he would get his hands on the former champion again. Climbing into the ring, Russell called for a mic.)
<Drake Russell> I really hate to disappoint you, Flint but there's been a change in plans for our match. It seems that the Trashman has come down with something ...
(A smiling Russell looked out at the fans as he removed his belt and handed it to someone on the outside of the ring.)
Seems that he's come down with is a case of Russellitis ... (There was a chorus of boos that filtered out of the crowd while across the ring, Flint yawned.)
So ... it looks like this is your lucky day ... or mine ... as we get to go one on one! (Flint tosses his backpack to the outside as Daye calls for the bell.) *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Flint and Russell circled each other when Flint suddenly dropped to the mat on his back, motioning for his opponent to pin him. Confused, Russell stood there when rushing out from the back a bandaged Trashman hit the ring and tagged
Russell across the back of his head with a Singapore Cane.
Knocking Russell to the mat, Trash put the Boots to the prone man and kicked him from the ring, following as Russell staggered into the back.
Propped up on his elbow, Flint sighed before he smiled and pulled Daye down on top of him and slapped the mat himself before pushing the surprised ref off of him.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** ( Flint rolled to his feet when "Crazy Train" began to play and the promoter/president B.J. Perris made his way into the ring.)
<Perris> Oh no, you're not getting off this easy Flint! (Flint sighed as he leaned back heavily into the corner as the long haired
Perris approached him.)
You think you're going to get the last laugh in by trying to make me look like a fool? Not in this lifetime, mister!
(Flint looked down as Perris jabbed a finger into his chest. With Perris stepping back, Flint rubbed at the spot in his chest where he was being poked before stepping out of the corner.
Nodding his head in agreement, Flint lashed out and grabbed Perris by the shoulders before falling back on the canvas. With a struggling Perris on top of him, Flint laid there calling for Daye to count. Hesitant at first, Daye finally dropped to the mat and slapped out a 3 count. Pushing Perris off of him, Flint rolled back to his feet and started to leave the ring.)
<Perris> Now wait a goddamn minute! (Perris pushed his long reddish hair from his face before shouting into the mic.) I'm not done with you yet! (Flint looks at Perris with an almost bored look, grabbing the mic away .) <Jason Flint> What now, Perris? What are you going to do? Maybe a First Blood match? (Flint hopped out of the ring and grabbed some fan's soda can and proceeded to empty it onto the floor. Satisfied that the can was empty, Flint began to bash the can over his forehead, cutting himself.)
<Jason Flint> How's that? You win again. Forget it, man. Both you and that yutz, Daye have wins over me today which makes you both winners. So why don't you just let me leave and I'll job to Pep Preston next week or something. But until then ... later.
(Flint drops the mic down and picks up his backpack and begins to make his way to the back. Perris blocks his way and begins to shout at him.)
<BJ Perris> Why you son of a bitch! You get into that ring ... or it'll cost you your job.
(Raising a single eyebrow at the promoter, Flint snatched the mic away from an irate Perris.)
<Jason Flint> Do you promise that, Perris? Will you fire me? And end your "fun" with me? Promises....promises, Perris. You won't fire me, you want me to twist in the wind some more, so if you're not going to do it now.....get the fuck out of my way.
(Flint slams the mic into the chest of a glowing Perris, who mumbles something to Flint as he steps to the side and leaves the ring. Flint turned back to
Perris when a bloody Drake Russell popped out from the back and smacked Flint in the back with a chair. Falling into the ring apron, Flint took two more shots from the chair before he was rolled into the ring where he took a severe beating from both Russell and Perris. Perris bounced off the ropes and came down with a Leg Drop before dragging Flint to the middle of the ring and holding down his arms.
Taking that as a cue, Russell climbed the ropes to a mixed chorus of cheers and boos. From there, he smiled as he listened to the fans before leaping off ...
it was "Total Decimation" for Flint, Drake Russell's 450 Splash finisher. Perris and Russell left the ring and headed to the back while Flint laid there looking up at the ceiling ... his eyes open. Just a few short months ago, he was at the top of the world and now, just like that, Flint was near the botom of it, looking up.
Leaving the former Champion in the ring, the camera made it's way to the back focussing in on the current Texas Heavyweight Champion, "the Street Soldier"
Justin Hart. Hart was pacing back and forth when Perris and Drake Russell walked by. Ignoring both men, Hart continued his pacing, only cocking his head at the comment from the passing Russell.)
<Drake Russell> Mr. Chip on his shoulder ... (Hart whirled around and shot Russell a look. Stopping, the Television Champion turned to face him as Peris stepped inbetween the two to stop a confrontation.)
<Justin Hart> What did you say? <Drake Russell> You heard me ... <Justin Hart> How about I slap that comment out of your head? <Drake Russell> Yeah don't you ... (Both men moved towards each other ready for a go at it right in the hallway as
Perris pulled Russell back.)
<Justin Hart> Going to hide behind Perris, now? Let him go! (Perris shook his head as he pulled Russell away.) <Drake Russell> No hiding ... you know where I am. (Russell walked off patting the belt on his shoulder, a smile on his face before the camera made it's way to the ring where "If You Want Blood, You've
Got It" by AC/DC plays over the speakers. Jake Morgan, followed by Mean
Darlene, makes his way out of the back, moving toward the ring. Climbing into the ring, he raises a microphone to his lips.)
<Jake Morgan> I'm a Bastard? Well, that's the first thing that you got right since you came back Hart. Yes, I am a Bastard, and I have NEVER said that I
ALONE carried this Promotion. What I said, you stupid little Piss-ant, is that
I have been here day in and day out, before you arrived, and since you left. I have been here fighting to EARN whatever I have received here, both the good and the bad. You waltz in here after your severely lackluster, previous tenure, stroll into the Title match and you think that you EARNED it? Then you play dumb, acting like you knew nothing about what was going on? You pretend to be the good guy and ask me why I think you would stoop to Flint's level, now? It's quite simple actually, I don't think that you stooped to those levels, I know it. Everyone saw what happened on Sunday, Justin. It was no secret. You may not have actually locked me in the closet but you knew something had been done to me. You say you know me so well, then you KNOW that there is NO WAY that I would step aside and let ANYONE take my spot in a title match. NO WAY!
(He starts to shake a little as his voice takes on an angrier tone. Mean Darlene nodded her head in agreement as she comforted Morgan with a hand on the shoulder.) You accuse me of whining? All I've done is state the facts, if that upsets you, then too Fucking Bad! But if what I said has no merit, then why did you vacate the belt? If I was just whining like you claim, why would you give up something that you EARNED? Yeah, that's what I thought. Quite frankly, I don't care if you hate me, loathe me, or if you just want to be me. I'm still going to kick your ass. You think that I'm only after the belt? Well, hey you're right, I am.
I have been since the day I walked into EW. (He smiles briefly.) Face the facts, I AM better than the last Champion, Hart. You were the last
Champion and I know I'm better than you. Another reason is that, I was the last person to actually WIN that belt. Not take it, not steal it, not have it given to me but actually win it.
(Pause.) Oh by the way, I'm not the only one who can raise that belt back up to the levels that it used to be, I'm just the BEST one to do it. So get your BITCH ass out here and let's get this thing going.
(Dropping the mic, Morgan waits for Hart to show. He didn't wait long as Hart confidently strolled from the back and made his way to the ring. Stepping inside, he romved his wraparound sunglasses and glared at Morgan, who returned the gaze. There was a heavy tension inside the ring when "Bittersweet" by Fuel hits and Jason Flint breaks through the curtain, baseball bat in hand.
Seeing Flint, both Hart and Morgan focus on the former champion. Morgan begins to climb through the ropes, but Daye is right there restraining him as Flint points with his bat at the King of Hardkore.)
<Jason Flint> Jake Morgan ... everyone knows that there's no love lost between us. But one thing is sure, at Back and Bad, we were both SCREWED. Me, I got my ass handed to me by Johnny Green, Dan Glass and all these EX-PAWers. You got it easy, you got locked in a broom closet, missing your SECOND chance at me and the PAW belt.
(Morgan nods his head in agreement as Flint continues to speak.) But you know ... I look at the situation at hand, Justin Ass-hole on one side and you on the other, I don't see the true prize. Hart vacated that title so things can be fair, so you BOTH can be challengers for it. How ...
(Flint stopped for a second, pondering just the right word for the occassion.) ... gayass ... can one be? You beat me, Hart, and then drop the belt like a piece of trash.
(Flint digs into his pants pocket and produces a waded up piece of paper and drops it to the ground.)
Only a pussy like you would do such a thing. Not even a title defense to your name. Pathetic and tragic. But make no mistake about it, Hart ... Morgan, my time will return and stab your eyes when that time comes, because it'll be sooner than you think.
(Flint drops the mic, extending his arms outward and then slipping through the curtain. With Flint gone, the wrestlers began a staredown in the center of the ring forcing Peter Daye to break them apart. Warning them back to their corners, Daye called for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Rushing in, Hart caught an Uppercut which set off Morgan as he came on swinging. Relentless in his attack, Morgan was a whirlwind as hecaught Hart with everything he threw ... a flurry of Punches, Kicks and Elbows. Whatever wrestling skill Jake Morgan has, he tossed it completely out the window.
With a Belly-to-Back Suplex, Morgan went for the first pin attempt of the match, getting a 2 count before Hart kicked out. Staggered by Morgan's onslaught., Hart bought some room and time with a Back Elbow to the head.
Coming back with Punches of his own, Hart was not about to be undone by his adversary, showing he had power in his fists as he forced Morgan back across the ring.
Pushing Morgan into the corner, Hart put the boots to him before backing up and charging forward and nailing his Corner Splash. As Morgan stumbled out of the corner, Hart put him down with a Spinning Back Kick. Rolling the former Champ over, Hart hooked a leg for the pin. Daye slapped the mat twice before Hart was pushed off.
Whipping Jake into the far corner, Hart put him down again, this time with a
Spinning Leg Lariat. Using the ropes, Hart propelled himself into the air and came down with a Leg Drop across the throat.
Throwing himself back against the ropes, Hart rushed forward and landed a second Leg Drop onto Morgan, rolling him up for the count. A 1 count was all he got this time.
Using Boots to the head, Hart had Morgan in enough trouble to roll him over and slap on the Urban Deathlock. Leaning his weight back, he tried desperately to break Morgan in half. In pain, Morgan tried to crawl forward but was having no luck as Hart sunk the hold in.
Distressed, Mean Darlene reached into the ring and tried to grab Morgan but he was just out of reach. Near frantic, Darlene could only pace back and forth then try to reach into the ring to help.
Though he was screaming out in pain, Morgan continued to try to claw his way towards the ropes and salvation. With Daye asking for the submission, Morgan shook his head no.
Slowly but surely, Morgan crawled forward enough to get a small assist from
Darlene in order to get to the ropes and force a break.
Angered, Hart began arguing with Daye and going so far as to grab the ref by his lapels which drew a stern warning and a threat to end the match and declare
Morgan the winner. Seeing that his behavior was getting him nowhere with Daye,
Hart returned to the action.
Turning, Hart was grabbed by a waiting MOrgan who wrapped him up for a Reverse
Russian Leg Sweep. Reacting quickly, Hart blocked Morgan before throwing his body back and taking Morgan down with a Side Russian Leg Sweep.
Groggy, Morgan was sent slamming into the far corner where he took a second
Corner Splash. After taking a Boot to the midsection, Morgan was grabbed around his head and put down with a Leaping DDT. Crawling over the top of Morgan, Hart pushed down on his opponent but Morgan was not cooperating as he kicked out at
2.
Pushed into a corner, Morgan took several shots to the face before lunging forward and taking Hart down with a big Clothesline. Pulling Hart up by the hair, Morgan spun him around before pulling him in close and hammering him with a Short Arm Clothesline, spinning Hart through the air.
Hart was out on the mat as Morgan staggered towards the ropes and climbed up to the middle rope, coming off with an Elbow Drop. Up the ropes a second time,
Morgan leapt off again, this time coming down with a Kneedrop. As he came down,
Hart rolled out of the way forcing Morgan to jam his knee into the canvas.
Climbing the ropes, Hart waited for Morgan to pull himself to his feet before jumping off with a Flying Clothesline. Both men were down on the mat drawing a count from Daye. On and on he counted with Hart finally getting to his feet first.
Moving in with a Double Axe Handle, Hart took an Elbow to the stomach then was put on his backside with a Knee Lift. Holding onto the ropes, Morgan started
Stomping the downed Hart until Daye stepped in and forced him back. Turning to argue with Daye, Morgan wasn't ready as Hart pulled him down with an Inside
Cradle.
Daye slapped out a 2 count before Morgan kicked out. Pulled to his feet, Morgan locked up with Hart in the center of the ring before Hart worked in a Side
Headlock. Trying to push Morgan towards the ropes, Hart was blocked and sent instead. Hitting the far ropes, he foound himself on the mat in a hurry as Mean
Darlene reached in and tripped him up. Capitalizing on his situation, Morgan rushed in and flattened Hart with a Drop Kick to the back of his head.
Pulling Hart to his feet, Morgan raised an arm and whirled it around before lifting Hart and bringing him down with a Spinebuster. The fans were howling as
Hart skipped in pain across the ring. Following Hart, Morgan grabbed an arm and sent him across the ring. Following with his arm extended he hit Hart just as
Hart hit him ... it was a Double Clothesline, putting both men onto the mat.
The fans were trying to get Morgan back to his feet with the cheers as he pulled himself up using the ropes. Picking Hart up, Morgan Body Slammed him and followed with a Leg Drop. Pushing his opponent into the corner, Morgan mounted the ropes, raising his fist before bringing it down over and over again onto
Hart's head.
Pushing Morgan off, Hart sent him flying. Running in, Hart dropepd an Elbow and hit canvas. Up quickly, Hart was wrapped up in Morgan's Oriental Spike. Knowing he was in for a world of hurt, Hart slammed his head backwards breaking the hold. Grabbing an arm, he twisted Morgan's arm into an Arm Bar then flipped
Morgan over onto the mat. Kicking his feet up, Morgan caught Hart in the chin.
Back up, they locked up in the center of the ring when the fans started booing loudly as Johnny Green it the ring. Moving in, he booted Hart in the back before hitting Morgan with a Clothesline. Mean Darlene was immediately on the ring apron complaining. A laughing Green left the ring with Peter Daye hot on his heels.
Rolling to his feet, Hart pulled Morgan up and planted him with the first of two successive Rolling German Suplexes. Ready to finish him off with a third, he caught an Elbow to the head. The second blow made him release his grip allowing Morgan to dip in behind, locking on the Cobra Clutch. Hart's arms flailed wildly trying to break Morgan's grasp but soon went limp.
With a sharp snap upwards, Morgan lifted Hart off his feet and slammed him down on his back with a Suplex. Bridging, Morgan waited until Daye slid back into the ring and slapped out a three count.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (The fans were cheering loudly as Mean Darlene rushed into the ring and raised the arms of the NEW PAW Texas Heavyweight Champion as the belt changed hands for the second time in 2 weeks.
Arms raised in victory, Morgan celebrated with the fans as the show came to an end. As the credits rolled, the camera moved to the outside of the building where a lone Jason Flint was struggling with his backpack. Taking it off his back, he looked at the unzipped pack before reaching in to see if everything was there.
Pulling his hand free, he looked confused, holding a video tape as the camera faded to black.)
