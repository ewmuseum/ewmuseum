PAW — Card Archive

10.22 Card

22 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera opens on Kimberly Davis greeting two men wearing identical dark suits with their eyes hidden sunglasses, Shaking hands, Kimberly leads the men down the hall, presumably to meet with the boss over some important matter.
As the camera pulls back and watches them walk away, someone steps out from a dark recess hidden from view and watches the procession as well. As they disapper, Emilio Ramirez turns towards the camera with his eyes as large as saucers.)
Emilio Ramirez: The INS!?!?!?!? (The camera fades on his look of fear before moving on. With a quick exchange, the camera moves elsewhere as Lisa Doyle knocks on the door of a dressing room.
Opening the door is Drake Russell. Seeing her, he smiles and opens the door so that she can enter. He closes the door and we switch camera angles to inside of the room. Russell continues putting on his boots.)
Drake Russell: Hey, did you need to get an interview? (She gives him a hug.) Lisa Doyle: Among other things. Just wanted to see you and see how you were doing. (Looking up at her, he takes her hand.) Drake Russell: I'm great, thanks. So, what else did you need? (She turns a little red in the face and smiles.) Lisa Doyle: Well, a lot of people are wondering why exactly you are having a hard time dealing with the current Television champion, Hiroshima Nagasaki. I know we have heard you view on this man before, but since Jason Flint has already promised his wrath on Nagasaki, how does this further affect your mind state towards this entire situation?
(He runs his hand through his hair and gives off a sigh.) Drake Russell: I have thought about that since I last heard what Flint said to
Nagasaki. Regardless of what happened in the past between us, I don't have any problems with it. I'll actually be looking forward to the first time he steps back into the ring in however many twenty plus days that are left.
(Doyle nods in agreement and continues.) Lisa Doyle: We have seen the wars fought in the ring between you two, do you think that we have seen the end of it between Jason Flint and Drake Russell?
(Looking away for a few seconds, he shakes his head no very slowly and looks back.) Drake Russell: I don't know if it's over between me and him, ask him what he thinks. Regardless though, I am looking forward to him coming back and maybe even working on the same page against Nagasaki. I guess we'll find out when his days are up.
(She pats him on the shoulder.) Lisa Doyle: That's all I needed. Thanks, hon. I've got to get going now, I'll see you in a little bit.
( She takes his hand for a second and then walks away, extending each others arms until they release.)
Drake Russell: Okay, I'll see you after the show. (She exits as the scene fades into Seth entering the building. Following the
Cali Boy, the camera moves in behind him as he stops in front of his designated dressing room. He shakes his head when he notices the new nameplate bearing both his name and that of his new partner, Paco. Turning the knob, he opens the door and goes inside.
Stopping dead in his tracks, Seth can only slap his forehead as he looks around at the redecorated room done up in “Post Modern Elvis”. Everywhere there’s
Elvis memorabilia ... from movie posters to album covers to the 6-foot Elvis stand-up and in the middle of it all is Paco wearing his Heartbreaker “action wear” gyrating his hips to a tune playing on his Walkman.
Seeing his partner, Paco takes off the headsets.) Paco: Isn’t this great?!?!?! (Seth stands there dumbfounded as the camera leaves him for another part of the backstage area. The camera follows behind a well dressed man as he walks the back of the PAW television studio. Making his way through the somewhat busy corridor, the man looks around, revealing himself to be Japanese manager Lonny
Nakamura before descending stairs into the bowels of the studio.
Coming to the bottom of the stairs, Nakamura continues on his journey, stopping in front of a door before rapping lightly. Several seconds pass as Nakamura waits, adjusting his tie when the door opens and a head pops out ... that of
Jake Morgan.)
Jake Morgan: What do you want? (Nakamura smils as he makes a slight bow to the man peering out from the door.) Lonny Nakamura: Would you be so kind as to tell Darlene I would have a word with her? (Morgan frowns as he looks back into the room.) Jake Morgan: She’s not here ... Lonny Nakamura: Ah ... would you tell her ... (Morgan looks back once again as a female voice somewhere inside the room asks who it was. Pulling his head back inside, Morgan closes the door in Nakamura’s face when large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession.)
.... 5 ... .... 4 ... .... 3 ... .... 2 ... .... 1 ... (White Zombie's "More Human than Human" begins as flashes of the PAW stars in action are shown. Seth ... Skip Westervelt ... Justin Hart ... Jason Flint ...
Jake Morgan and finally the PAW Television Champion, Hiroshima Nagasaki, standing in the middle of the PAW ring, the belt raised over his head.)
After an explosion, the scene fades to black and the PAW logo disappears before fading back in and panning the fans packed into the small television studio.With the camera making it’s way through, many hold their signs aloft bearing such slogans as "Blue Suede Dudes", "Denton Correctional Facility, home of Justin Hart" and "Nagasucky".
After making it’s way through the boisterious crowd of some 50 or so, the camera settles in on a laughing Aaron Starr and Jason Flint,standing in front of the PAW banner.
Aaron Starr: Welcome once again, Denton, Texas to Public Access Wrestling ... and just like last week, welcome back to my collegue ... the former PAW Texas
Heavyweight and Tag Team Champion ... Jason Flint ...
(Flint gives a little salute with a small glass bottle in his hand.) Jason Flint: Thank you, Aaron ... and thank you Denton, Texas ... (A small chant of Flint’s name is heard in the background bringing a bigger grin to his face.)
Ah ... these fans ... there’s none like them anywhere else ... Aaron Starr: Now ... I see you’ve brought a little something along this week
... (Flint grins as he holds up the small bottle up for both the fans and camera alike. Zooming in for a closer look, the camera picks up the label of the vodka bottle.
Nodding his head, Flint steps forward with his grin melting away for a more serious demeanor.)
Jason Flint: You know … I’ve been called a lot of things ... Coward, Paper
Champion and the Bane of Hart’s existence ... all pretty names given to me by people that I just WHALLOPPED for the shits and giggles of it all but NEVER ...
never have I been tagged for being a part of a "conspiracy" like I was this past week by Hiroshima Nagasaki.
(Flint pauses to look at his bottle of vodka.) Hiro … let me make something absolutely clear for you. You are like this bottle here. (Flint holds the bottle aloft to show the fans.) When you first came into PAW, you were full to the brim with catchy and WAAAAAAY overused phrases. But since then, you’ve slowly drained away, emptying yourself and boring the hell out of the whole lot of us.
How about zipping your yap long enough in order to start the gerbil on his treadmill in that big head of yours so you can come to the obvious conclusion of things.
(Flint smiles as he holds one finger up.) Two things come to mind ... ONE … there was no conspiracy ... (A second finger shoots up into the air.) ... and TWO … Justin Hart was going to beat you ... As if that’s not bad enough, you want to add me to the mix ... and that my friend is a very bad mistake. Singling me out was a mistake that you will regret.
You say that I’m full of bravado but here’s a reality check ... EVERYBODY LOSES
EVENTUALLY. And just like this bottle…
(Flint opens the bottle and pours out the leftover liquid onto the floor.) You’re running out of time, too. Don’t single me out again, Hiro or your time will simply end ...
(The cheering turned to boos as the Elvis look-alike, Paco stepped out from the back. Stopping to make a hip gyration then smooth his fair back, Paco stepped up to Flint, grabbing the mic away from him.)
Paco: For a former wrestler, you have alot of nerve ... given an announcing job, I’d think you’d be just a little more appreciative of me and the rest of the boys in the back who come out here week in and week out ... I think you’d have some nicer things to say about those of us who help to pay your checks ...
You see, Flint ... these people don’t want to see you stand here with Aaron
Starr and flap your gums ... oh no, what they want to see is the high flying, hip gyratin’ Heartbreakers ...
(Turning from Flint, Paco starts gyrating his hips.) Whoa momma ... don’t step on my blue suede shoes ... (Finishing to the laughing of the fans, Paco turned back to Flint who was looking down at his bottle. Unfolding his arms from his chest, Paco jabbed his finger into Flint’s chest.)
Now whattaya have ta say to that? (Looking back up, Flint swung his arm and smashed the bottle over Paco’s head. As Paco staggered backwards, Flint removed his blazer, handing it to Starr then grabbing the wrestler by the ear.
Leading the sequin covered man to the ring by his ear, Flint rolled him in. Following right behind to chants of his name, Flint pulled Paco to his feet and sent him into the ropes. On the return, Paco was caught up in a Fireman’s Carry and held for several seconds before being slammed down into a Gutbuster Drop.
Kicking him over onto his back, Flint mounted the top turnbuckle, leaping off and spinning through the air, he lands his Corkscrew Splash, the Shockwave.
Laying on top of the flattened Paco, Flint waits until the fans count out loud to three before getting off. Dusting off his clothing, Flint leaves the ring before the camera goes back to a surprised Aaron Starr.)
Aaron Starr: We’ll be right back with our first match of the evening! (The camera left the ringside area to find Jake Morgan standing alone in the back of the PAW studios. He is dressed for his match, which is due to start in just a few minutes. He looks up at the camera, scowls and begins to speak.)
Jake Mirgan : Drake, I had hoped that when you returned from the unemployment line, that you would have changed. Unfortunately I was wrong, you are the same self centered, egotistical, lazy, boring bastard that you always were.
You don't get it do you? The reason why you were fired and that Jackass Hart wasn't, is simple. While I may not have liked him, he at least put out an effort, you didn't and judging from your recent actions, you still don't.
(Pause.) In a few minutes, you're going to have to do something that you're not used to ... work. If you rely on Skip to carry the match, then you’re going to lose. If you try to half-ass it like you usually do, then Trash and I are going to hurt you. If you concern yourself more with Lisa than with us, we will destroy you. Play time's over Drake. It's time for you to find out what " Wrestling the Way It Was Meant to Be!" is all about.
(Jake storms off just before the camera focusses in on Kimberly Davis leading the two dark suited men through another part of the backstage area.)
Kimberly Davis: As you can see, we are a small operation but we’ve been able to make the best of all available resources offered to us with a full production team right here on the North Texas campus ...
Man #1: And wrestlers? Kimberly Davis: We have a roster composed of some local talent as well as a few from around the country. Our current champion is from Japan and was brought in on a working agreement with Lonny Nakamura ...
We’re not the biggest but we can put on an entertaining show ... (Stopping, Kimberly grabs a green haired passer-by and stops him.) Why don’t you make yourself useful and bring some coffee into the office ... (The man shakes his head and walks off as Kimberly turns back to the two men.) Here we are ... (The camera leaves them and follows the green haired man when she holds the door open for the others. He looks angry, muttering under his breath as he makes his way through the hall.)
Green haired man: Make myself useful ... (He uses a mocking tone, imitating Kimberly Davis as he continues on his way.) Why don’t you make yourself useful and bring some coffee into the office ... (Shaking his head, he passes production people as he heads off to do as he’s told.) I’ll get you some coffee alright ... (Rounding the corner, he sees an approaching Drake Russell heading for his match. With a look of fright, the green haired man ducks around the corner out of sight.)
They were his flowers ... (With Drake Russell passing, the camera follows him as he walks out to the ring, climbing inside. Getting handed a mic, he steps back through the ring ropes. Tapping the mic produces a little bit of feedback.)
Drake Russell: Is this thing on? (The crowd responds with a resounding "YES"!! Drake smiles and looks at the crowd he shakes his head laughingly.)
Now, Jake Morgan, it seems like you've got a little bit of problem with me lately for some reason. First, you blame me for losing your title to Hart, then you say that you LET me win our match last Sunday.
(Shrugging his shoulders and raising his hands a bit, he looks as if he's questioning how Jake Morgan got those ideas.)
First of all, I never claimed that my returning was destiny, jackass, it was luck. After being let go from the PAW, I was gonna retire because this is the only place that I belong. This is my home, despite what it seems that you all want. If I didn't get a call from the new owners here, I wouldn't be wrestling anywhere.
(He shakes his head and goes on.) As for your claims that you LET me win last Sunday was a result of your generosity, well I have to differ with that a little bit. I only gave myself credit for fighting a good match and getting the pin, which I did. What did you want? Did you want credit for doing the same, well I can give it to you, all but the pinning part. You weren't there for that because you were saving
Darlene and you know what? I'm glad because no woman gets hit by a man, that's just the way things go.
(He looks to the crowd and smiles as some women clap for what he said.) When I grow up, Jake? Consider me a man, because you weren't telling me anything that I don't know. Don't put words in my mouth or talk down to me because, well, I just don't take kindly to it. You take yourself so seriously and yet you overlook things that matter in our match. You made this out to be a singles match, Morgan. Look at the card, there are two other men involved here, don't overlook them just because you are "The Great" Jake Morgan. Please ...
(He appears disgusted.) As for your lesson in humility, I've heard that one before. To be honest with you, I took it more seriously the last time I heard it from Flint. He was always just a step ahead of the rest, including you. Listen man, this isn't the same Russell that you think you used to know. I'm tired of listening to everyone claim that I am going to do this or do that. Frankly, Morgan, you have no idea what you're talking about so, I suggest you come out here right now and learn firsthand that this time around, I don't play.
(He drops the microphone with the emergence of Skip Westervelt. Walking confidently to the ring, Skip slides in and scoops up the mic.)
Skip Westervelt: There’s been alot of talking going on this week, so I’m going to keep this short and sweet ... Jake, I’m not going to come out here and deny that you’ve been at or near the top of this promotion since day one ...
I’m not going to sit here and tell everyone that you haven’t deserved everything you’ve earned up to this point ...
What you have done however, is ruffled a few feathers this week with your comments ... you’ve taken a match between four men who have each carried gold in this promotion in the past ... you with the Texas Heavyweight Title ... the
Trashman was a former Tag Team Champion while Drake and myself have carried the
Television Title on seperate occassions ... a match which most likely will turn out to be one of the better fought matches in this promotion’s history ... and you’ve turned it into something personal ...
You’ve shot off your mouth about how this is going to be a lesson in humility for my partner ... well Jake, here’s something else you’ve earned this evening
...
You’ve earned yourself one big fat ass whipping! (Pausing a moment to listen to the mixed reaction, Skip turns back to Drake
Russell.) Like the man says ... let that be a lesson to you ... (The fans started cheering as Jake and Trash rush the ring and begin attacking their opponents. Jake pairs off with Drake with Trash going after Skip. Daye breaks them up and forces Drake and Jake into their respective corners before calling for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Things start of fast and furious with Trash taking it to Skip, hammering him across the ring. A helping hand from his partner allows Trash to open up with
Knife Edge Chops in his corner before Daye comes in to break things up.
An Eye Gouge gives Skip room to maneuver as he moves away from the enemy corner. Sending Trash across the ring before a series of Leap Frogs and Drop
Downs, Westervelt tags Trashman with a big Spinebuster Slam for a cover and a 1 count.
After verbal jabs at Morgan, Skip tags in his partner who hammers Trashman around the ring with a series of Right Hands. A Belly-to-Belly Suplex has
Trashman in trouble and in another pinning predicament. A timely save by Morgan keeps the match going.
With the ref pushing Morgan from the ring gives Russell and Westervelt enough time for a Double Leg Drop off the ropes following a Slam. Skip dives out of the ring just in time not to be seen as Drake drops for the cover. Again, Trash gets a shoulder up in time.
Up and arguing with the ref lets Trash escape to the safety of his corner and more importantly a tag. Jake Launches himself into the ring and is taken down with a Hip-toss. After dropping a Leg across Morgan’s throat, Russell follows it up with a Sleeper, pulling the man back to his feet.
Morgan quickly slips to the side and picks up Drake on his head with a Side
Suplex. Woozy, he falls prey to a series of Suplexes by Jake. Losing his bearings in the ring, Jake drops Drake near his corner allowing him to tag in
Skip.
Not seeing the tag, he takes several hits from behind and is then double teamed. They whip him across the ring and nail him with a double Dropkick as he comes bouncing back.
Rolling to the outside, Morgan is taken down as Russell comes flying out of the ring with a Pescado. A Westervelt Flying Elbow brings the Trashman running and touches off a brawl with all four men going at it on the outside.
Skip gets bloodied as Trash launches him into Morgan standing his ground with a chair in hand. Having it bounced off his head opens him up. Drake is able to take Trashman down but finds himself on the recieving end of a brutal chairshot into a Double Whip into the ring barrier. With their opponents down, Morgan and
Trash celebrate then pull tables from under the ring.
Setting them up on the outside, Trash helps get Westervelt up onto Morgan’s shoulders then Spikes him as Morgan Powerbombs him through the table. The fans are cheering as they turn their attention to Drake Russell who has a surprise for them. Swinging wildly, Russell lays into them with the concealed bell, bouncing it off their heads.
Pulling Trashman up onto the apron, Russell uses the ropes to propel himself into the air for a Hurricanrana, snapping Trash over and bringing him crashing down into the table.
Getting back to his feet with his arms in the air, Russell takes a Forearm to the back of the head before being spun around by the shoulder. Taking a Front
Kick, Russell has his head slammed into the floor from a DDT.
Peter Daye moves in to seperate both parties as a bat wielding Trashman helps
Morgan to his feet while Skip Westervelt pulls Russell away. Threatening both teams, Daye warns them both back to their respective corners.
With two men in the ring, Morgan and Skip lock up in the center. Out of a Side
Headlock, Morgan Whips his opponent across the ring, taking him down with a stiff Shoulder on the return. Missing with a Knee Drop, Morgan eyes Westervelt as he gets back to his feet. Another Lock up brings on the same result with
Skip sent across the ring and returning to duck under a Clothesline. Speeding across the ring, Skip bounces off the ropes once again and finds a ducking
Morgan who powers him over with a Back Body Drop.
Moving to the far corner, Morgan taunts Russell as Daye comes in to break it up. The lull in the action and a distracted ref is a perfect opportunity for the Trashman to land a Splash off the top ropes.
Turning back to the action, Morgan drops for a cover but has it broken up as
Russell charges in. Daye pushes Drake from ring then the Trashman but not before they Double team the helpless Westervelt with a Tilt-A-Whirl Body
Slam/Senton combination. From his corner, Russell wants a tag but Skip is far too out of it. Standing defiantly in the center of the ring, Morgan throws Skip to his corner and motions for Russell to come in.
With a tag, Russell charges in and goes right on the attack. Firing in Chops to the chest, he backs Morgan up until the former Texas Champion fires away with
Punches ... there is no science to it, it’s just a brawl.
Back and forth the two go exchanging blows all over the ring. Duck under a thrown Punch, Russell with a Go Behind wraps Morgan up around the waist but takes a backwards Kick to the groin that drops him to his knees.
Lifting Russell by the throat, Jake sets him up for a Chokeslam but takes a
Thumb to the eyes. Stepping away from each other, the two begin to circle the ring when Morgan motions for a Test of Strength. Locking fingers slowly, the two go chest to chest until Morgan forces Russell back. With both men straining, Russell falters and is powered to a knee. With the fans cheering,
Russell fights back to his feet and staggers Morgan with a Head Butt, breaking the hold.
With a Knee to the stomach, Russell lands a Gutwrench Powerbomb and holds on for a 2 count. With the near pinfall, Russell moves in for the kill but finds
Morgan not out of the fight yet.
Again they battle it out, exchanging powermoves around the ring ... Russell’s
Brainbuster and Sidewalk Slam and Morgan’s Cobra Clutch Bulldog and Guillotine
Face Driver lead to near pinfalls with the fans on their feet during the entire exchange.
Following brief tag-ins to both Skip Westervelt and the Trashman, the momentum of the match swings to Drake and Skip as they pound the Trashman, nearly taking the match on a Skippicurana if not for the timely intervention of Morgan who runs both opponents from the ring.
Clearing the ring, Daye acknowledges a tag from Morgan. Hammering Westervelt to the mat with a Short Arm Clothesline, Jake pulls his man up, lifting him for a
Powerbomb only to have Skip slide off. Hitting Jake with an Uppercut, Skip flattens him with a Jumping Bulldog.
With Morgan flat on his back, Skip walks around the ring taunting Jake’s partner. Motioning for the Trashman to come into the ring, he gets a little too close to the corner where he’s hit in the stomach by Trash’s Louisville
Slugger. Having the air driven from his lungs, Skip’s an easy target for a
Schoolboy Roll-up.
It’s academic when the Trashman intercepts an intervening Drake Russell. *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (With the match over, there comes some words between both parties followed by some shoving. With Security hitting the ring at the ref’s order, the camera breaks away to the back.
The camera sweeps the office of the PAW’s new boss before coming to rest upon the masked woman sitting behind her desk. Working on papers before her, the woman lifts her head at the sound of the knocking coming from outside.)
Woman in the Offspring mask: Come in! (The door opens inwards and the face of Lisa Doyle comes into view.) Lisa Doyle: You wanted to see me? (The masked woman puts down her pen as she motions for Doyle to step inside.) Woman in the Offspring mask:Yes Lisa, I did. Please ... have a seat. (Doyle sits down before the boss, looking quickly around the room and at the pictures of wrestlers on the wall then turning her full attention to the woman sitting before her.)
I think we need to have a little talk ... Lisa Doyle: Yes? Woman in the Offspring mask: Yes ... as you know, we’ve been making changes throughout the promotion .. and will continue to do so until we can get back on our feet or at least to where I think we need to be ...
Lisa Doyle: Right ... Woman in the Offspring mask: Changes are being made everywhere ... sacrifices are having to be made for the benefit of us all ... things are no longer as they were under Perris ...
Lisa Doyle: Right ... Woman in the Offspring mask: So ... let me get to the point ... I’ve been watching you work and I’ve talked to others within the company who are impressed with your work ethic as well as your professionalism ...
You can go far in this company, young lady ... (Doyle shifts in her seat looking a little more relaxed than just seconds ago.) but ... in order for you to move up, you have to make a few slight changes ... Lisa Doyle: Yes? Woman in the Offspring mask: Yes ... you’re relationship with Drake Russell ... Lisa Doyle: Oh?!?! Woman in the Offspring mask: It might have been alright under the previous owner but now it has an aire of unprofessionalism ... it shows and unmasked bias towards one of the PAW’s competitors and might be misconscrued by others as just a tad unfair ...
Now, far be it for me to stand in the way of what you do outside of your career but we’re not talking about what goes on behind closed doors ... we’re talking about how the two of you carry on during business ...
As a professional, I’m sure you can appreciate where I’m coming from and as the consumate employee ... I’m sure you’ll do the right thing ...
(A slight frown overcomes Doyle as she looks down for a few seconds then looks up at the woman in front of her.)
Lise Doyle: Yes ma’am, I will ... (Getting up from her seat, Doyle walks out of the office as the cameras return to the ring.
The familiar Kodo drums and gong that signal the arrival of the PAW Television Champion begins On the third Gong, Nagasaki steps from the back, pauses for a moment and heads toward the ring. The PAW fans erupt with a chorus of boos and taunts that are ignored by the champion. Making his way to ringside, Nagasaki makes a short detour toward ringside announcers Aaron
Starr and Jason Flint. Looking first at Starr, who wisely cowers behind Flint, Nagasaki and Flint stand nose to nose for several seconds. Neither man makes any move to back away from the other.
Starr finally breaks the silence.) Aaron Starr: The television champ has joined Jason and myself here at ringside. Your match is about to start champ what can we do for you? (Nagasaki continuing to stare directly at Flint.) Hiroshima Nagasaki: Give me the microphone. (Starr quickly hands Nagasaki his microphone. Never diverting his gaze from
Flint, Nagasaki steps back and enters the ring. When the crowd noise begins to die down, the H-Bomb speaks.)
Blah blah blah. I am so sick and tired of hearing every wrestler here in PAW complain about the H-Bomb. From Jake "I've been dropped on my head too many times to know better" Morgan to Drake "More boring than when he was fired"
Russell, everyone complains about me. But when you think about it there are only two truths that matter. Truth #1 ... Since I have been here, I have always challenged the so-called great wrestlers of this organization, and have never backed away from my words. Truth #2 ... I, Hiroshima Nagasaki, am the ONLY champion here in PAW. Until any one of you prove able to defeat the H-Bomb then I suggest you shut the Hell up before you land at Ground Zero. And that goes double for "announcers." Come on out Seth, I am ready to make an example out of you.
(Looking toward Starr and Flint, Nagasaki intentionally throws the microphone at Flint before stepping into the middle of the ring and facing the walkway as
Seth makes his way toward the ring.
With a brightly colored Paco leading the way in their new blue sequined outfits, Seth follows behind with his head dropped, his eyes on the floor.
Making his way into the ring, Peter Daye calls for the bell.) *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Nagasaki remains poised in the center of the ring as Seth circles the champion looking for an opening. After feinting several times and darting back out of reach, Seth finally moves toward the champion for a Collar and Elbow tieup.
Before Seth gets close enough, Nagasaki nails him with a kneelift to the stomach followed by several right hands to the head.
Backing Seth toward the ropes, Nagasaki is hit with a Forearm Smash to the head when Seth rebounds off the ropes. A stunned Nagasaki takes Drop Kick before sent into the far ropes. On the return, he’s taken down with a Drop Toe Hold.
Wasting no time, Seth bounces off the ropes and comes in with an elbow drop. Hitting the ropes and arcing his body through the air with a Moonsault off the middle ropes, he hits only canvas.
Both men are quickly back to their feet. Locking up again, Seth is pushed back toward the corner by the larger champion. Peter Daye looks for a clean break as
Seth hits the turnbuckle but Nagasaki Punches Seth as Daye tries to step between them. A warning about not stepping away draws a blatant low blow on the stunned Seth as a response from the smiling Nagasaki
Incensed, Daye lays into the champion with a verbal barrage which is ignored as he lifts the prone Seth to his feet by the hair. Locking his arms around
Seth's waist, Nagasaki lifts him up and slams him down with a Belly-to-Back
Suplex and follows this up with several Boot Stomps to the midsection.
After throwing his opponent into the ropes, Nagasaki tries for a back elbow but misses the ducking Seth completely. On the rebound, Seth lands a picture perfect Drop Kick that sends the champion through the ropes to the outside floor.
Slingshotting himself off the top rope to the outside, Seth crushes the stunned
Nagasaki with a beautiful Flying Elbow. Seth, with a second wind, begins to hit Nagasaki with lefts and rights before throwing him into the ringside railing. Seth, leading Nagasaki by the hair, tosses the battered champ back into the ring.
Playing to the crowd, Seth takes a little too long to climb the ropes and launch himself for a Splash. A recovering Nagasaki raises his knees as Seth lands. Rising to his feet and laughing maniacally, Nagasaki scoops up his opponent and lands the Nagasaki driver.
Not finished with his opponent, the champion climbs the ropes to deliver the
H-Bomb but before he can leap from the turnbuckle and certainly end the match,
Paco jumps to the apron and shakes the ropes causing Nagasaki to fall to the mat.
Rolling to his side, Seth attempts the pin and gains a 2 count. A Handspring
Back Elbow later, Seth has the champ backed into the corner where he Irish
Whips Nagasaki towards the far turnbuckle.
Hitting solidly, Nagasaki seems to come alive, bouncing off and landing a
Flying Knee straight to an incoming Seth's face. Nagasaki locks on the
Unconditional Surrender on the staggering Seth. Checking on Seth’s condition,
Daye wastes no time in calling for the bell.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Paco jumps into the ring to save his partner proves ineffectual as Nagasaki's foot connects with Paco's head while the Oriental Spike is still applied to
Seth. Releasing Seth, Nagasaki grabs his belt from Daye and refuses to allow the ref to raise his arm as the victor.
Unexpectedly, "Another One Bites the Dust" by Queen blares through the speakers as Nagasaki raises the belt. Calling for a microphone causes the crowd to jeer loudly. Grabbing it away from the ref, the champion says one line before laughing hysterically, tossing the mike to the ground and heading to the back ... "Let THAT be a lesson to YOU!"
With the Champion walking off triumphantly to the crowd’s booing, the credits roll, bringing the show to an end.)
