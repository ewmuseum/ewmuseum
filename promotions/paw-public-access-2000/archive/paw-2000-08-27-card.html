PAW — Card Archive

8.27 Card

27 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Man: Adjust the lens ... everything's fuzzy, ya' mook! Man#2: Christ ... you can do this yourself, you know ... (Maintenance check. Time to check on equipment to see if it's functioning properly. The two stagehands busily adjust the camera's zoom, checking to see if the new lens fitted on the thing can ever get into focus. One turns it one way ...)
No! the other way! Like this! (One turns it another. Finally the camera comes into focus, just in time to see
Justin Hart walking towards them at a brisk pace, muttering something to himself.)
Hart: Two-toned muther ... yeah, I remember you ... (Hart grabs the man with the camera and slams him against the wall to get his attention. Snarling, he makes a request ... or rather, gives an order.)
Is this thing on? Man#2: W ... we're performing maintenance checks on the equipment... Hart: Nevermind that ... I got a message for someone ... (Hart releases the man and signals for him to start recording. A few seconds later, the other shows up with a mic. Hart points a finger at the camera and makes his point clear.)
Offspring ... so you've decided to show your painted face in PAW once again, huh? Up to your old tricks too I see. Where's Trashman? He decide he was too good to hang with someone like you? Well, in either case, looks like you got yourself a new stoolie to fill the void. Must be lonely in that mind of yours.
In either case, you haven't changed a bit. You waltz in here and immediately these people forget all your shortcomings and cheap stunts. They still buy your bull aparently. Well for those of us with fond memories of you... what, 2 ... 3 at the most? We remember you for the bastard you really are. You and that damn bat.
(Hart spits to the side and picks up a broom. Breaking it over his knee, he sticks the sharp end in the cameraman's view.)
You'll get the "point" of this message soon enough, 'Spring. Times have changed... and so have I. Hopefully we'll lock up in the future, and then I can thank you ever so properly for supposedly introducing me to "hardcore wrestling"...
... by stomping a mudhole in your painted ass! (Hart taps the camera lens with the stick and tosses it aside. Soon he's gone.) Man: Whoa. Talk about issues... Man#2: Awww christ! he scratched the lens! Man: He ... well, shit. (The two men started arguing when large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession.)
.... 5 ... .... 4 ... .... 3 ... .... 2 ... .... 1 ... (White Zombie's "More Human than Human" begins as flashes of the PAW stars are shown. Skip Westervelt ... Johnny Green ... Justin Hart ... Drake Russell ...
Christian Maeda and finally the PAW Texas Heavyweight Champion, Jake Morgan, standing in the middle of the PAW ring, the belt raised over his head.)
After an explosion, the scene fades to black and the PAW logo disappears before fading in on a smiling Aaron Starr standing in front of the PAW red, white and black banner. In the background, the sounds of a cheering crowd can be heard prompting Starr to wave at the fans, his smile getting bigger by the second.)
Aaron Starr: Welcome wrestling fans ... as if you didn’t know, I’m Aaron Starr and this is ... Public Access Wrestling! One week removed from the Summer
Burnout supercard and though Jason Flint is by now long gone from the promotion
... it’s plain to see that some things never change ...
Case in point, the re-emergence of the Offspring ... a former Heavyweight
Champion, having taken the title from Jake Morgan in Mexico ...
The Offspring would go on to have the belt “stolen” by Jason Flint and his erstwhile ally, Johnny Green. From there, the Offspring left the promotion ...
that is until last week when he made a return to Denton, Texas and the PAW. Not one to mince words, a rather unhappy Offspring had a few choice zingers for several of the PAW’s more prominant wrestlers ... including to the newly formed
“Heartbreakers” ... Christian Maeda and Seth ...
Here is just one reply from one half of the Heartbreakers, Seth ... (Starr fades away as black-and-white footage from old World War II movies plays. From German bombers air raiding Britain to Uncle Sam's smiling face, many different aspects of the war are represented. The announcer's commentary is scattered through this.)
Announcer: Some wars are fought on the ground. Some wars are fought at sea. Some wars are fought in the air. (Suddenly, in stark contrast to the scenes before, an empty wrestling ring circles on the screen.)
And some wars are fought ... in the ring! (An animated bomb falls onto the rin, and an explosion causes the camera to fade to black for a moment. Then, a picture of Seth appears on the screen.)
Seth ... (The picture of Seth splits into half. On the other half of the screen is a picture of Christian Maeda.)
Christian Maeda ... together, they've been dubbed the Heartbreakers. (Both pictures fade out, only to be replaced by a single picture of both Ryan
Miller and The Offspring.)
The Offspring ... Ryan Miller ... former fan favorites, only to abandon them. In any war, there can be only one winner. This one will be no exception. Who will it be? Seth: It's gonna be me! Announcer: Don't touch that dial, or you might miss the war of the millennium. (Once again the wrestling ring is shown. This time, there are flags hanging from each of the turnbuckles. Two of them have Christian Maeda's face on it.
The other two have Seth's. With that we fade back to Aaron Starr.) Aaron Starr: A war ... is that’s what’s on the horizon? Will the Offspring and
Ryan Miller answer the challenge from the newly formed Heartbreakers?
(The fans started to cheer loudly, causing Starr to look off-camera. As he did, so did the camera, following his train of vision until training in on Jake
Morgan stepping out from the back and making his way towards the host of the show.
Stepping up next to Starr, Morgan tossed his title belt over his shoulder.) I’ve been joined by the Texas Heavyweight Champ ... Jake Morgan: They know who I am, Aaron ... what they want to hear is what I have to say ...
(Starr nods his head in agreement, thrusting his mic forward.) It seems that everyone’s in an uproar this week just because the Offspring’s come back to Public Access Wrestling ... sides are being drawn and alliances have been formed ...
Aaron Starr: There is strength in numbers ... Jake Morgan: Did everyone go rushing off like a bunch of chickens without their heads when Jason Flint brought in his boyz?
Aaron Starr: Well ... no, but ... Jake Morgan: Did they do it when Adam Manson marched his large carcass in here? What about Linus and the rest of his lame wrestlers from South Texas? Was there this big panic? In a nutshell, Aaron ... the answer is no. Two men come in here and attack Pep
Preston, make a few remarks and all of a sudden everyone’s up in arms over it
...
Now don’t get me wrong, the Offspring has talent ... he’s been a Champion wherever he’s been and as I understand it, he was a Tag Champ with this Ryan
Miller ...
Aaron Starr: Right, in Florida they were the WoPW Tag Team Champions ... Jake Morgan: I don't know what the hell he wants or what he’s talking about but if he wants a fight ... any place, any time ...
And ... if you want a shot at MY title, you have to work your way up from the bottom, just like everyone else did. Once every single person in front of you has had their shot, then you can have yours.
(A loud booing follows the Offspring as he makes his way to the ring.) Offspring: Jake ... Jake ... Jake ... I see that some things never change. You’re the still the same ... Mr. Do-gooder with a big mouth ... why don’t you back up what you’re blowing?
Oh ... but wait, you’ve got a big match tonight against Drake Russell ... Jake Morgan: I do have a match later tonight but I'm more than willing to kick your ass right now!
(The fans were all on their feet anticipating a confrontation as Morgan took a step forward.)
Offspring: You talk a good fight, Jake ... you might be ready to kick my ass, as you say but what about him?
(Morgan turned to see who the Offspring was pointing to. As he did so, Ryan
Miller whalloped him over the head with a chair. Dropping the belt, Morgan staggered away, led towards the ring as Miller hit him twice more with the chair before tossing it aside. With a helping hand, Morgan was rolled into the ring.
Pulled to his feet, Morgan was Whipped into the far ropes by both men, ducking under their Punches on the return. Following through and bouncing back, he nailed them both with a Double Clothesline. The fans roared right along with
Morgan as he thrust his fists into the air to celebrate before pulling the
Offspring to his feet.
Rearing back for a Punch, Jake took a Thumb to the eye. Again sent into the ropes, Morgan was taken down with an Offspring Drop Kick. Miller came off the middle rope with an Elbow Drop. With the Offspring leaving the ring for the back, Miller hammered Jake into the mat with two German Suplexes.
The fans were booing as Offy returned with a ladder. After propping it up in the corner, they Whipped Jake into the ladder. Hitting hard, he staggered out of the corner and hit the mat facefirst. Climbing the ropes to the turnbuckle,
Spring jumped forward and “rode” the ladder down as it landed on top of Morgan.
Opening it up, they laid Morgan between the legs. Miller held Morgan’s head clamped between the two legs of the ladder while the Offspring returned to the ropes. Jumping off, he spun his body, landing with his legs and snapping the ladder shut around Morgan’s head.
The Champ was lying in a pool of his own blood as Pep Preston and the Trashman ran out from the back to make the save. Hitting the ring, they chased Spring and Miller from the ring. An irate Trashman called for a mic while Preston checked on Morgan.)
Trashman: I don’t know what’s gotten into you, Offspring, and even though we’ve been partners in the past, I’m not going to stand back and let you beat on one lone man ... what are you going to do now that the odds are a little more even?
(Miller and Spring looked at each other before diving into the ring. Miller took a Singapore Cane shot between the eyes as he bounced back to his feet. The
Offspring was more fortunate as he ducked under Trash’s blow, rolling to his back. Wrapping him up with the cane, the Offspring fell back to the mat with a
Russian Leg Sweep.
Kipping back to his feet, Spring grabbed at referee Peter Daye who was helping
Preston to get Jake Morgan out of the ring. After being shoved back several times, Daye called for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Morgan was handed off to Security and helped to the back when Miller and Spring left the ring for Preston. After hammering him with Fists and Forearms, they led him back into the ring and worked him over like a well-oiled machine.
A couple of quick Double Team moves had Preston in trouble when Miller hoisted
Pep into the air with a Bear Hug. His partner climbed the ropes and jumped off, landing on top of Pep’s shoulders before spinning to the mat with a
Hurricanrana. Bouncing back to his feet, Spring took a Forearm from Trashman but the surprise attack was over as Miller came in with a Bulldog, driving Trash into the canvas. Leaping to the top turnbuckle in one smooth motion, Spring came off with a Split-legged Moonsault.
Miller dragged Preston to the center of the ring and dropped him on top of the ladder while the Offspring started running back and forth across the ring, building up a head of steam as he bounced off the ropes. Passing several times, the Offspring hopped across the prone Preston. As he passed this last time,
Miller lifted one end of the ladder off the mat. Bouncing and returning, Spring leapt into the air and Somersaulted, landing on Preston with a Senton. The ladder buckled and bent inwards, spilling Preston to the mat as the fans chanted “Holy Shit!”.
Dropping to the mat, the Offspring rolled Preston up and waited for Daye to slap out a 3 count. As Daye called for the bell, the Offspring snapped up
Preston’s half of the Tag Belts.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (The fans were booing as Miler and the Offspring left the ring after giving
Preston a few extra kicks for good measure. Miller slapped his partner on the back while they walked off, the belt carried above the Offspring’s head.
They almost made it to the back when they were stopped in their tracks by the
Heartbreakers and Black Thorne. Maeda was pointing at both men then the ring as they walked forward.
Trying to sidestep the newest threat, Miller and Spring were stopped by Black
Thorne who stepped into their path. With their opponents getting into the ring,
Seth grabbed up a mic.)
Seth: Not so fast ... you still have to deal with us ... (Miller and Spring looked at each other then hit Baseball Slides to the outside, setting off a brawl around the ring as Daye called for the bell again.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Spring and MIller were getting the upperhand as they battled it out around the ring but Thorne turned out to be the equalizer as he smacked Miller across the back with a chair. After rolling Miller inside, both parties returned to their respective corners.
Miller and Maeda were the legal men in first. Still stunned by the chairshot,
Miller took a Drop Kick to the chin but was able to power out of a cover.
Pulled to his feet, Miller blocked a second Drop Kick and sent Maeda to the mat with a thud.
Spring was in with a tag and put Maeda down with a couple of Japanese Arm
Drags. Showing resiliancy, Maeda came right back with a couple of deep Arm
Drags of his own. Eyeing each other, the two locked up in the center of the ring before Spring pushed his opponent into his corner and the waiting arms of
Ryan Miller. With Maeda being held, Spring fired in several Chops to the chest and stepped back with a warning. While he was arguing with the ref, Miller spun
Maeda around and hit with a Right Hand between the eyes. Seth rushed into the ring but was stopped short of his target as Spring pushed Daye into his path.
An argument ensued in the center of the ring with Seth, Spring and Daye right in the middle. Finally, Seth left the ring and Spring returned to Maeda. A tag brought in Miller but not before they dropped Maeda with a Spinebuster Bomb/Leg
Drop combination.
An arrogant Miller sent Maeda into the far corner and motioned for Seth to come and get his. Seth was all too happy to charge in. Fired up, Seth ran right into a Drop Toe Hold. A tag brought in the Offpsring, who helped Miller with a
Double Leg Splitter.
The fans groaned in pain as the pair poured it on, roughing up Seth with Double
Team moves. Both men tossed Seth into the air and stepped out from underneath him, letting him fall to the canvas. A Double Elbow Drop from opposite corners led to a near pinfall.
Ushering Miller from the ring, Spring kept up the attack before being reversed out of the corner. Sent into the far ropes, Offspring caught a Knee to the back from Maeda. With a tag, Maeda flipped over the ropes into a Leg Drop and pin for a 1 count.
Pulling up Spring by the hair, Maeda pushed him back into the corner then fired away with a barrage of Punches that had Offy on the ropes. Not letting up,
Maeda drove his Knee into the Offspring’s midsection. He reluctantly backed away with a count from Daye. Across the ring, Miller was complaining to the ref.
Seth was leaning across the ropes looking for a tag but Maeda either ignored him or wasn’t watching. Maeda was too busy slamming Spring’s head into the turnbuckle. Stepping back, Maeda allowed Spring to fall to the mat. Seth and
Maeda had some heated words over a tag just before the Offspring pulled him to the mat with an Inside Cradle but Seth was in quickly with a Boot to Spring’s back to break up the near pinfall.
Seth was still complaining about a tag as he was led from the ring by Daye. This allowed Miller to come in and Stomp Maeda in the back. Black Thorne was up on the apron ready to come in when Daye rushed over to warn him from the ring.
Thorne made several threatening gestures but Daye was steadfast. Inside the ring, Maeda was pulled to his feet and sent into the ropes. On the return, he ducked under a Double Clothesline and followed through to make a tag to Seth who was Springboarding into the ring with a Double Clothesline of his own, taking both Spring and Miller to the canvas.
Climbing the corner quickly, he launched himself into the air and landed an
Elbow Drop from the top on Miller that shook the ring. Seth made a cover but
Daye was reluctant to drop to the mat when Spring spun him around claiming to be the legal man in.
Grabbed from behind, Seth was spun around but was able to counter Spring and send him off into the far ropes where he caught a Super Kick to the back of his head. Staggering out of his corner, Spring caught a second Super Kick, this one from Seth.
Moving in for the kill, Seth caught a clubbing Forearm to the back and was knocked to his knees, giving the Offspring a breather ... not much of one as he was spun around and grabbed by Black Thorne. Lifted off his feet, Spring was bounced off the canvas with a Belly-to-Belly Suplex.
All five men were in the ring going at it as Peter Daye tried to break things up and regain control of the match. Offspring and Miller were able to slip to the outside bringing on the boobirds. Seth and Maeda wanted the match to continue but were pushed back by Daye as their opponents made good their escape, the Offpsring lofting the Tag Belt he took from Preston into the air as he went.
It was deja vu as Spring and Miller stopped in their tracks. This time it was a smiling Justin Hart. Reaching under his coat, Hart produced a baseball bat and started tapping it on the floor as he slowly advanced.
Miller inched to the side to try and take Hart down from a different angle. Suddenly both men rushed him. Hart took the offensive, slamming the hilt of the bat into Miller's breadbasket, doubling him over. Switching stances on the fly,
Hart took a swing at Offspring only to be beaten to the punch as the Tag Belt slammed into his face.
WIth Maeda and Seth in pursuit, Spring grabbed MIller and left for the back. WIth all parties disappearing into the back, Daye called for the bell ending their match as the fans howled.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (With the ringside area cleared, the camera made it’s way into the back, cutting to Drake Russell sitting in the back lacing his boots when a flustered
Lisa Doyle flings open his dressing room door. She plops down onto the couch next to Russell.)
Drake Russell: Okay, what's wrong? You can't tell me nothing because I know somethings up. (She pulls her hair back and holds it in place.) Lisa Doyle: Maeda is getting ridiculous. He's always touching me and making me feel uncomfortable. I can't stand him or his moronic attitude. Isn't there anything that you can do?
(She drops her hair down. Russell moves closer and runs his fingers through her hair until he reaches her back where he rests his hand.)
Drake Russell: I was just thinking about that and I have a few things in mind that we can do. You'll just have to trust me.
(She looks at him with an inquisitive look.) Lisa Doyle: Like what? (Russell smiles and gets closer to her. Covering his mouth, he whispers in her ear before getting up and walking out of the room for his match.)
Well ... if that doesn’t work, Drake ... then I have a little something that just might ...
(The camera leaves a grinning Lisa Doyle and finds the woman wearing the Offspring mask followed by Kat Collins approaching B.J. Perris.) Woman in mask: B.J., we really need to talk ... I’ve been going over all these figures and the expenditures are starting to pile up. We need to take ...
Perris: Sure ... you do whatever you need to do to fix it ... partner ... (With a smile Perris walks off, leaving the woman in the mask turning towards
Collins and shrugging her shoulders.)
Woman in mask: Fix it? I don’t think he realizes how bad it is ... (Turning, she spots just the person she was looking for.) Fredrick! Just the man I was looking for! (As she walks off, the camera makes it’s way back to the ring where Drake
Russell has just been announced and is making his way towards the ring.
Walking around the outside, he slaps a few hands before stepping inside. Standing in the center of the ring with a mic in hand, he taps it a few times only to hear the feedback.)
Drake Russell: Is this on? (The hyped crowd shouts back with a resounding "YES!". Russell smiles and looks around to the crowd.)
You know ... I have always said that I am a man of destiny. Agree or disagree with me, either way is fine. Regardless, I have always come out here with a purpose. Things change from week to week but the one thing that is always on my mind is proving it to you and to everyone in the back that I am all that I say that I am and more.
What better way to prove to everyone that I am what I say I am? This match isn't just for the Texas Heavyweight title tonight. To me it's worth much more.
If I can do what I know I can it will be worth more than just the prestige of holding the Texas title. Hopefully gaining your respect and the respect of my peers. I know that there is no better place to do this than right here in
Denton, Texas, in front of the best fucking fans in the world. So, Jake
Morgan, come on out and let's do this. (Tossing the mic out of the ring, Russell patiently waits for his opponent as the fans get a bit restless. Keeping himself warm with stretches and squats,
Russell chats with the ref as he continues to wait.
As time goes on, the crowd starts buzzing, becoming even more restless when "If you want blood, You've got it!" blares out over the speakers. A cheer starts up as Mean Darlene is out frost from the back and appears to be trying to dissuade a bandaged Jake Morgan from continuing to the ring.
With his eyes locked on the ring and Drake Russell, the Texas Heavyweight
Champion looks anything but focussed on the matter at hand. Moving past her, he climbs into the ring and prepares himself for a title defense, handing off the belt to the ref.
Darlene, up on the ring apron, is desperately trying to get Peter Daye to not call for the bell. The official can only question Morgan who nods that he’s ready to fight. Shrugging his shoulders, Daye calls for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Morgan and Russell met in the center of the ring. Russell had a sly grin on his face which Morgan didn’t take kindly to, shoving him back. Charging in Morgan tagged Russell with a Forearm to the side of the head before pulling back on an arm which was countered as Russell avoided the Shortarm Clothesline.
Twisting Morgan’s arm into a Hammerlock, Russell pushed him forward into the ropes before dropping back and rolling him up. With shoulders on the mat, Daye dropped for a 2 count before a kickout.
Back on their feet and locked up, Morgan pushed Russell back into a corner and opened up with Lefts and Rights. The fans counted the blows when Russell reversed position and started in with Reverse Knife Hands. With a Knee to
Morgan’s chest, Russell threw him out of the corner with a Monkey Flip.
Following up with a Drop Kick to the back of Morgan’s head, Russell was able to get another 2 count pin attempt.
On the outside of the ring, Mean Darlene was hammering her fists into the ring apron trying to get the Champ fired up.
A Knee Lift snapped the Champ’s head back before he was taken over with a Hip
Toss. A Leg Drop missed it’s target as Morgan rolled out of the way. Pulling
Russell to his feet, Morgan went behind and latched his arms around Russell’s waist. An attempt to lift him off his feet didn’t work and only got worse as
Russell Kicked backwards and caught the Champ.
Jumping onto Morgan’s back, Russell Scissored his legs and rolled to the mat, taking Morgan over with him and rolling his shoulders down. Daye dropped down
... 1 ... 2 ... 3 ... NO! Morgan got the shoulder up just in time. Back on his feet, Daye held his hand up showing the 2 count. Locking up in the center of the ring, Russell came out on top with an Arm Bar but took an Elbow to the chin and was staggered.
Grabbed by the arm, he was sent into the far ropes and took a boot to the face on the return. Morgan hooked a leg after connecting with an Elbow Drop. Russell pushed Morgan off on a 2 count.
Locking up, Morgan went for a Vertical Suplex but was countered and taken down with Russell’s own Snap Suplex. Morgan looked a little groggy as he sat up which left him wide open for a Double Booting to the face. Darlene was up on the apron complaining as Russell pulled Morgan.
Ignoring her, Russell sent Morgan into the ropes and blasted him with a Spinning Heel Kick. Jumping up, Drake hit the middle ropes and threw his body back into a Moonsault, Landing on top, Russell was able to hook a leg but had carried a little too close to the ropes which allowed Darlene to drape Morgan’s foot on the bottom rope getting a call for a break from Daye.
On his feet, Russell had a few words for the smiling Mean Darlene who was all too happy to answer in her “Universal Sign Language”. Russell should have been paying closer attention, taking a Forearm to the head causing him to tumble from the ring. With Morgan following to the outside, the two men started brawling around the ring. Morgan got the better of the challenger with hammering Punches and a DDT onto the floor which gave him time to pull a table out from under the ring and lean it back against the ring barrier. A warning from the ref was for naught as Morgan ignored him. Grabbing an arm, Morgan spun around to send Russell into the table but found himself being Whipped around and sent instead into the ring barrier. Morgan’s weight drove it back into the fans in the front row as Security moved in to keep the fans back.
After several boots to the head, Russell pulled Morgan to his feet and slung him over his back. Spinning around, Russell adjusted the weight across his shoulders but didn’t count on Mean Darlene to get involved as she Super Kicked him. Falling backwards, Russell dropped Morgan through the table and sending splinters everywhere.
After a warning from Daye, she pleaded with Morgan to get back to his feet, only it was Russell who was up first. Rubbing his jaw, Russell eyed Darlene and pointed at her. Backing away and pleading with Russell, she was about to dart off but was grabbed by the air and pulled close. Angered, Russell let her have a piece of his mind but Morgan came in with a Forearm to the kidneys before he was able to do anything to her.
Spinning around to meet Morgan’s attack, Russell was on the recieving end of a
Low Blow from Darlene from behind. In pain, he staggered right into Morgan’s waiting arms where he was planted into the floor with a Single Arm DDT.
Morgan was back on his feet, taking a breather when the fans got loud. Looking around, Morgan caught Justin Hart pushing Darlene out of the way then driving the end of his bat into the Champion’s stomach doubling him over.
After a warning from Daye, Hart rolled Morgan into the ring. Sent into the ropes, Morgan bounced back right into Hart’s raised foot. Pumped up, Hart sauntered around the ring as Morgan tried to get back to his feet.
Pulling Morgan up, Hart stopped and spun around catching Darlene moving in for a Low Blow. Waving a finger in her face, Hart grabbed her and Whipped her into the opposite corner. With her slamming in hard, Hart charged into the corner and flew in with his Hang Time Corner Splash. The fans were howling as she dropped and rolled from the ring to the floor.
Heading back to Morgan, Hart pulled the now bleeding Champion up and across his shoulders before dropping back with a Samoan Drop. Thoroughly in control, Hart left Morgan on the canvas and turned his attention to Drake Russell on the outside. With a running start, Hart chugged across the ring, diving over the ropes with a Suicide Dive, wiping out Russell. The fans were on their feet as
Hart slapped a few hands around the ring.
Liberating a chair, he tossed it back inside where Jake Morgan was trying to pull himself to his feet using the ropes. Kicking the chair to the center of the ring, Hart pulled Morgan over and tried to lift him into the air. Slowly,
Hart stuggled to Gorilla Press the Champion which was no easy feet. Gritting his teeth, Hart pushed Morgan over his head but the Champion dropped off behind
Hart, wrapping an arm around his head and pulling him back and down into the chair with a Reverse DDT. Hart banged loudly on the chair as Morgan staggered back to his feet.
Grabbed from behind, Morgan took an Atomic Drop from Russell who was back into the match. With Morgan not a threat, Russell pulled Hart to his feet and
“repaid” the Suicide Dive with a Double Underhook Back Breaker. Pushing Hart up onto the top turnbuckle, Russell whirled his arm in the air signalling for a Supercanrana from the top. Jumping into the air, he wrapped his legs around Hart’s neck and started to spin but Hart was waiting ...
standing up from his seated position, Hart jumped off and sat down into a
Powerbomb. Hitting the ring with such force, they both bounced several inches off the canvas which brought the fans to their feet chanting “Holy Shit!”.
Across the ring, Mean Darlene was trying to help Morgan. His bandages red with his blood, Jake Morgan had seen better days. Across the ring, he staggered, pulling Hart to his feet and hammering him into the corner. Hart fought back, winging Morgan with Punches of his own. Peter Daye stepped inbetween the two and pushed them back. With a couple of feet distance between them, they both lunged forward the instant Daye was clear, knocking each other to the mat with a Double Clothesline.
With all three wrestlers on the mat, Peter Daye started a count. The fans were on their feet counting along ...
... 4 .... ... 5 ... ... 6 ... ... 7 ... Mean Darlene was slamming her fists into the mat as Daye counted. All three men were struggling to rise to their feet before a ten count could be issued.
... 8 ... ... 9 ... Morgan was up first, pulling Russell to his feet. Sending him into the ropes,
Morgan nailed him with a Drop Kick. Using the ropes to pull himself to his feet, Morgan lifted Russell off his feet and slammed him into the mat with a
Double Leg Slam then dropping down on top of him for a 3 count from Daye.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Daye called for the bell and rolled out of the ring to retrieve Jake’s Texas
Title Belt. Back in the ring, he handed it to the Champion as he raised his arm in victory.
The fans were cheering when Justin Hart ran in, driving a Knee into Morgan’s back and knocking him to the mat. After pushing Daye out of the way, Hart grabbed Morgan’s legs and wrapped him up in the Urban Lockdown as the credits started to roll ...)
