PAW — Card Archive

8.13 Card

13 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 3 —

(The camera opened on the outside of the PAW television studio on a shady character propped up against the wall, eyeing the scene. He checked his watch repeatedly. A security guard comes by and sees him there.)
Security Guard: Hey pal, no loitering. Get a move on ... (The guy simply ignored him.) Say, pal, didn't you hear me? Man: Not now. I'm waiting for someone. (The guard looked at him in utter disbelief. Motioning for his walkie-talkie, the man suddenly sprang up when a familiar black NSX pulled up into the parking lot. With a duffelbag over his shoulder, Justin Hart stepped out and headed for the building when he was suddenly cut off.)
Man: Hart ... baby ... amigo! (Hart looked at the man with a blank, suprised stare.) Hart, boobola ... let's schmooze ... have I got a proposition for you ... (Hart exchanged bewildered glances with the Security Guard before addressing the stranger.)
Justin Hart: Are you sick or just mental? (Chuckling, the man placed a hand on Hart's shoulder. Hart quiclkly brushed it aside.) Man: Hah ... gotta love this guy's sense of humor! Hah!! he kills me! Does he kill you? He slays me! HAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!
(The security guard inched over to Hart and whispered in his ear.) Security Guard: Wanna borrow my gun? Justin Hart: Hold that though t... say, strange man ... who are you and what do you want? (The man produced a card from his odd colored jacket and flashed it in Hart's face. Before Hart could take the card however, he stuck it back in his jacket.)
Man: Marty ... Marty Charlatan ... manager of Champions. I've been all over the world, and in the corners of some of the greatest champions of all time. Like uh ... what's his face and that other guy ... and there was one more that I can't recall at this point ... anyway, I'm here to offer my services to the new breed of champions. I have an eye for potential greatness, kid.
(Hart leaned over to the security guard.) Justin Hart: Is he serious? Security Guard: I'm afraid so... (Hart looked at Charlatan and cut him off in mid sentence.) Justin Hart: Not interested, Mr. ... uh ... Swindler ... but I may have someone who you could help. Goes by the name of Seth. Sorry, but I can't really tell you where he is at the moment. He's in there somewhere. Maybe he can use your services.
(Tipping his hat, Charlatan took his leave. After stepping behind the doors, Hart and the security guard burst into fits of uncontrolable laughter when the camera moved back inside where Aaron Starr catches up with Jake Morgan. The
Champ seems less than pleased.) Aaron Starr: Hey Champ, you wanted a few minutes to speak on what's going on in PAW? (Jake turns to Starr and gently takes the mic from his hands.) Jake Morgan: Yes Aaron, I wanted a few minutes to speak but not about the stuff going on here. I want to talk about something I heard on the PAW hotline.
(Starr takes a few steps back, worry creeping onto his face. Jake reaches over and grabs him by the front of his suit.)
Relax Starr, you’re safe. I'm not talking about you. (Starr relaxes a little bit and smoothes out the front of his crumpled suit.) I'm here to talk about that piece of shit, Billy Joe Gannon. Gannon, you have the balls to challenge me? What gives you the right to do that? If I remember right you couldn't handle it here, so you left with your tail between your legs, travelled to American X Wrestling and won that excuse for a title that they call a Heavyweight Championship.
(He pulls the Texas Heavyweight Title off his waist and raises it to the camera.) This is a Heavyweight Championship and if I'm not mistaken it's one that you couldn't win. I've done it TWICE! So what makes you think you can win now? I guess you forgot what TEXAS WRESTLING is all about. Maybe you had better stick to wrestling pigs or sheep, whatever it is you do in Arkansas, because if you step foot into a PAW ring, I'll kick your ass and send you crying back to that PIG STY you call a wrestling federation.
(Jake tosses the mic to Aaron and walks away. Leaving Starr who breathes a sigh of relief, a voice was heard mumbling.)
Voice: Take out Hart. Take out Hart. Take out Hart. (Looking for the speaker, the camera finally trained itself on a pacing “California Golden Boy”, Seth.) Seth: Take out Hart. Take out Hart. Take out Hart. (Pausing to take a couple of deep breaths, he calmed himself down enough to look into a mirror.)
All right, Seth. You know what's on the line. You can beat this street punk. You can do it! (He resumes his walk.) Now, I'm going to go out there and kick some a ... er, I mean butt! (Leaving Seth, the camera moves to another part of the studio where Jake Morgan is walking in the back. He comes to a door, looks around then enters the room.
When the door shuts, the name Jason Flint is on the door. A few seconds later the sound of crashing and breaking objects can be heard when large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession.)
.... 5 ... .... 4 ... .... 3 ... .... 2 ... .... 1 ... (White Zombie's "More Human than Human" begins as flashes of the PAW stars are shown. Skip Westervelt ... Johnny Green ... Justin Hart ... Drake Russell ...
Jason Flint ... and finally the PAW Texas Heavyweight Champion, Jake Morgan, standing in the middle of the PAW ring, the belt raised over his head.)
After an explosion, the scene fades to black and the PAW logo disappears before fading in on Lisa Doyle standing in the back. Behind her seems to be a flurry of commotion.)
Lisa Doyle: Lisa Doyle here and as you can see, I’m in the back of the television studio ... We're still not sure what's happened here but as you can plainly see, Jason Flint's Tag Team partner, Pep Preston has been brutally attacked. His assailant has not been determined as of yet.
(The cameras show three EMT's working on Pep. Pep is covered in blood and unconscious. The EMT's are trying to get him to regain consciousness but he appears to be in bad shape.)
As soon as we get more information, we’ll pass it along (Lisa turns and smiles as Drake Russell steps into view to get a better look. Drake gives her a concerned look and gently touches the bandages on her head.) Hello Drake ... Drake Russell: I hope you are feeling better today. (A noise can be heard off camera. Both Lisa and Drake turn to see what it is when Jake Morgan rushes into the camera shot.)
Jake Morgan: It's nice that your concerned about her well being but WHERE THE HELL WERE YOU ON SUNDAY? We were a team. I'm supposed to be able to depend on you. It seems though, that you don't care about this team. I guess there isn't enough personal glory in it. You said that you wouldn't let Flint and Khan distract you from our match. I guess you lied. Instead of watching my back like you were supposed to you were in the back chasing
Flint. Drake Russell: What are you bitching about? I got the pin. Jake Morgan: If you had been where you were supposed to be, by my side, instead of seeking out your own personal vendettas, Darlene NEVER would have been put through a table.
Drake Russell: You don't know that. (Jake gets right up in Drake's face.) Jake Morgan: Since you weren't at ringside when it happened then I guess we will never know will we? Together we stood, divided we fell. You did exactly what Jason wanted you to do, so did I. I trusted you. I won't make that mistake again. You let your petty distractions get in the way of a good tag tea, and others got hurt in the process. Your girlfriend for example.
(Jake points at Lisa who looks terrified that a fight is about to break out.) Don't worry Lisa, unlike Drake, I'm not letting anything distract me from my goal this week ... FLINT!
(Jake turns and leaves Lisa in Drake's arms. Moving on, Kim Davis is seen walking towards a dressing room with Jason Flint’s name plate on it and opens the door only to stops in her tracks.)
Kim Davis: What the!?!?!?!?! (The interior of room is a mess. The locker is turned over, all of the ring attire is now a pile of shredded cloth. Flint’s boots are shoved in the toilet, and his tag team title is in the only upright object in the room, the trash can. He turns to Kim. With a hand covering her mouth and obviously disturbed by the scene before her, she kicks the door closed.
Leaving the back for the ringside area, the camera passes through the small but boisterious crowd before finding the host of the show, Aaron Starr standing with a grinning promotion President B.J. Perris.)
Aaron Starr: Welcome ladies and gentlemen to Public Access Wrestling! With me tonight as we open our show is none other than B.J. Perris ... thank you for joining me tonight ...
Perris: It’s good to be here! Aaron Starr: We’re a week out from what has to be Public Access Wrestling’s biggest event ... Summer Burnout! and by that look on your face, I can tell you’ve got some news to share with us.
Perris: That’s right, Aaron ... one week and by what we’ve already seen tonight, things are really starting to heat up ... and it’s only going to get hotter!
We’ve had one match already announced ... tonight, I’ve got a few more for you, starting with the debut of Texas Talent, Shane Silver ...
Aaron Starr: Silver, not known in many circles outside of the Texas Indy scene, is a fantastic talent who will surely impress here in the PAW ... and as I understand it, we have this piece from Mr. Silver himself ...
(The camera fades out on Starr and Perris into the taped message as a man of average build, for a wrestler, steps into view in front of the PAW banner. His hair, done up into dreadlocks and in turn, are pulled back into a loose ponytail which hangs just past his shoulders. He's decked out in a simple t-shirt with an Adidas logo on the right breast. He's wearing a pair of blue jeans and his hands are shoved into his pockets.)
MAN: Seems I'm going to be the new face around PAW. I heard PAW was the place for opportunity, and Shane Silver opened the door when he heard it knocking.
(He nods.) It may be a corny analogy but it's the truth. Spent a good deal of my life wrestling around Texas, so I figured why not try a place that has some television exposure for once. Where I come from, it's impossible to even hope for a network affiliate to carry a local promotion on their programming. Going on down to public access shows isn't even better. So why not? Why not come to a place with television exposure and get the name out there?
(He pauses for a moment.) It's all about getting yourself out there; getting the exposure tomove onto bigger and better things. For some of you? This may be the starting ground. To date, this is the biggest thing that's happened in my career.
(He chuckles.) I can just hear the comments now, but I'll worry about that bridge when I come to it. As for promises? I won't make any, except for one. You can bet I'm going to try to the best of my ability to move above and beyond the rest of the competition. Until I'm scheduled, just consider me the new face around for just a bit. Observation is the key. I plan on doing just that this week.
(Shane walks off as the camera cuts back to Starr and Perris. Aaron Starr: Two matches signed but I have a feeling that’s not all you’ve got for us ... Perris: And you’d be right, Aaron. A couple of weeks back, the Television Champion, Christian Maeda offered to put his title on the line against fellow Californian Seth ... it has now been signed! And what Supercard would be complete without a Texas Heavyweight Title Defense? Aaron Starr: Since Jake Morgan took the strap from Justin Hart, he’s had a very light schedule ... who will he be defending against?
Perris: It was a tough decision but I had to go with the #1 Contender! We’ll be seeing a rematch of Morgan and the “Street Soldier” Justin Hart in a double
Main Event. Aaron Starr: By that grin on your face, I have a feeling there is more. Perris: And again, you’d be right, Aaron. In the other Main Event, I’ve added a little stipulation ... after taking in everything that has gone on over the last couple of months, it’s time to put an ending on things ...
This little game of chess with it’s moves and countermoves by Jason Flint and myself has gone on long enough. It’s time to finally put an end to it all. It’s time to prove my point ... you don’t bite the hand that feeds you!
Aaron Starr: And the stipulation on the match? Perris: It’s quite simple, Aaron ... this promotion just isn’t big enough for the two of us ... one of us has to go!
(Starr looks shocked.) This match is for all the marbles ... Loser Leaves Town! Me or him ... for 90 days! Aaron Starr: 90 days?!?!?! Perris: After Khan gets his hands on Flint ... you’ll have seen the last of Jason Flint! Aaron Starr: There you have it fans! Four matches signed with more to come! Let’s go to the ring for the first match of the evening ... me, I’ve got an interview to conduct!
(Starr rushes off followed by the camera instead of going to the ring. As he leaves Perris, Starr walks into the back where he passes the Television
Champion Christian Maeda and his bodyguard, Black Thorne. After passing Maeda, Starr stops with a smile on his face.) Aaron Starr: Ah ... Christian, just the man I was looking for. Christian Maeda: Me? Aaron Starr: I saw Lisa earlier and she was looking for you. Said something about meeting her in her dressing room or something ...
Christian Maeda: Really? Aaron Starr: You betcha ... but if you’ll excuse me, I’ve got a Jason Flint to talk to! (Starr rushes off, leaving Maeda and Thorne.) Christian Maeda: She wants me ... but first, we’ve got a little business to take care of! (Black Thorne grinned evilly as he pounded his hand with a baseball bat. As the pair followed off after Starr, the camera returned to the ring where Seth was entering.
In the center of the ring, he looked genuinely hurt as the fans booed him loudly.)

— Part 2 of 3 —

Seth: Awwwww ... and here I was going to show you some more slides of
Californina ... but with that reception, I’m not going to share them with you!
(The boos suddenly became cheers which seemed to frustrate him more.) Okay, be that way ... see if I care. Instead I’ll talk about a piece of trash by the name of Justin Hart. Since you’re the #1 Contender and we have a little unfinished business left ... why don’t you come to the ring and show us all just how tough you are?
(There were some cheers as Hart stepped out from the back and made his way towards the ring. Ignoring the fans, Hart was focussed on Seth as he climbed into the ring. Referee Peter Daye stepped between the two as Hart closed the distance. Hart was jawing at Seth as Daye pushed him back. After a deadly glance, Daye stepped back and pointed a warning finger at the “Street Soldier”.
Stepping back to the center of the ring, he called for the bell.) *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Eyeing each other for a couple of seconds, they locked up in the center of the ring. Pushed back, Seth was staggered by a sudden Head Butt out of nowhere.
Shaking his head, Seth took savgae Chops to the chest that sent him back into his corner. Mounting the ropes, Hart let fly with Right Hands that were counted off by the fans. Repeatedly, his rights hit their mark as Seth fell back against the ropes. Holding his fist up, Hart let Seth have it with one last
Haymaker. Moving back across the ring, Hart took off into a run and came flying into the corner with his Hangtime Splash. Stepping back, he allowed Seth to fall facefirst to the mat.
Rather than go for an early pin, he pulled Seth to his feet before hammering him back to the mat with a Clothesline. Bouncing his head off the mat, Seth shot back into an upright position, taking a Boot to the head. Laying flat on the canvas, Seth was all setup for a Leg Drop but was able to move out of the way as Hart landed.
Back on his feet and holding his tailbone, Hart moved around the ring until taking a Drop Kick to the knee and going down. Following up, Seth showed him how to execute a proper Leg Drop.
Hooking the leg, Seth was thrown off on a 1 count from Daye. Back on his feet, Hart wrapped his arms around Seth from behind but took a Boot to the groin. Releasing his grip, Hart was grabbed and dropped with a Snap Suplex before taking a couple of Knee Drops to the head. Bouncing off the ropes, Seth came in with a Drop Kick to the back of Hart’s head and was quickly picking up momentum in the match.
Pulling Hart to his feet, Seth yanked him into a Super Kick that staggered the man. Whipped across the ring, Hart returned into a Spinning Wheel Kick that landed high to the chest. After propping Hart into the corner, Seth left the ring for the apron. Using the ropes, he came back in with a Somersault
Slingshot, landing on Hart’s groin. Hart and every male in the audience was in pain as Seth returned to his feet. Darting across the ring, Seth returned with a Drop Kick to Hart’s face. With a Monkey Flip, he sent Hart into the center of the ring where he was able to drop for a cover, getting a 2 count this time.
With Seth now in control, the tide turned off of a blocked Suplex and was countered with a Belly-to-Belly Suplex. Shaken up, Hart had to use the ropes to pull himself up as he left the ring to retrieve a chair. Throwing it in, he returned to the still downed Seth. After setting it up, he roughly grabbed up
Seth before planting him facefirst into the chair and crushing it. Despite warnings from Daye, Hart left the ring and pulled a table out from underneath it. With the table set up on the floor, he rolled back into the ring and pulled Seth up. Pushing him to the top turnbuckle, both men precariously balanced themselves before Hart pushed Seth off from the top. There was a collective gasp as Seth hit the table and fell through it as it splintered under his weight.
With Hart looking down on his handiwork, a chant of “Holy shit!” started up. His opponent not moving, Justin Hart climbed down from his perch and left the ring, making his way to the back. Daye immediately called for the bell and summoned help.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Hart disappeared into the back followed by the fading chants from the fans. As the camera followed him on his journey, Hart moved past the production personnel and Security until he crosses pathes with the Texas Heavyweight
Champion, Jake Morgan who bumps into him as he passes.) Justin Hart: Watch it , asshole. (Jake stops and turns on the Street Soldier.) Jake Morgan: What? (The two went face to face when Hart poked Morgan him in the chest.) Justin Hart: I said, watch it ASSHOLE! (Jake smiles and half turns away from Hart. Spinning back around, he levels Hart with a punch to the face. Hart, surprised by the cheap shot, is knocked for a loop as Jake leans over the downed man.)
Jake Morgan: I told you to stay out of my way. (He reaches over and grabs a nearby trash can, crushing Hart with it.) And I fucking meant it! (Throwing the smashed can down, Jake storms off as the cameras fade deep into the bowels of the building where a sweaty Aaron Starr probes the dark for Jason
Flint. Stumbling over a patch of water, Starr curses to himself before picking himself back up and going eye to eye with Flint.)
Aaron Starr: Jason ... Jason Flint: Got to hand it to you, Aaron, you are a true bloodhound of the press … (Starr smiles proudly, puffing out his chest, as Flint hops onto a crate and leans against the wall.)
Aaron Starr: Sooo … there’s a lot of upset people up there that wants a piece of you for what you did last week with Mean Darlene and Lisa Doyle.
Jason Flint: Well … Aaron, lets just say that some people are just getting too uppity around here and someone had to show them who’s who. I know I’m not liked
… I’m despised by my peers and I don’t care. Perris bats me around like a ball between the paws of a cat, patiently waiting to strike, sending his big gun, Khan to send me a message. Well, message received. Khan and myself… main event, Summer Burnout! … all the stops will be pulled out as me and him finally square off.
(Flint pauses for a second, looking around then down at Starr.) But that is ten days away, plenty of time for anything and everything. So today, people, there will be something happening today … another “incident” of sorts. Just watch out … cause it just might be you …
(Flint smiles, pointing at Starr who begins to backpedal out of there as Flint begins to laugh when out of nowhere, Christian Maeda tries to hit Flint with the Television Title as he swoops in out of the darkness, having followed Aaron
Starr into Flint’s lair. Flint side steps the attack and shoves Maeda face first into the wall, causing him to fall to the ground and drop the Television Title. Snatching up his nearby Singapore Cane, Flint hits Maeda over the head with it. Raising the cane to hit Maeda again, Flint is interrupted by a bat shot to the back from Black
Thorne. Thorne continues to hit a downed Flint with the bat, focusing in on his ribs and stomach. A bloodied Maeda gets up and stumbles over to retrieve a steel chair. He unfolds the chair, setting it up.)
Christian Maeda: Thorney! Bring the bitch to me! (Black Thorne brings Flint over to Maeda. They stand Flint up in front of the chair. Christian gets Jason in position for the Total Heartbreak.
Maeda executes the move, slamming Flint face first on the chair. Standing over Flint, Maeda shouts his message.) That was for Lisa! Don't you ever touch her again! (Security finally arrives, causing Maeda and Thorne to retreat. Moving to entrance to the studio, the camera comes upon several PAW fans retreating to their seats from bathroom breaks or for drinks and popcorn.
A young man wearing a UNT t-shirt talking to his companion and not paying attention suddenly stops, spilling his popcorn as he walks into the wall ...
looking upwards, he sees that it’s actually the man-monster known as Khan. Gulping deeply, the young man is grabbed around the throat and lifted off his feet. Swinging his massive frame around, Khan Chokeslams the young man through a table full of PAW merchandise. Turning back to the young man’s friend, Khan snatches the box of popcorn in his hand and walks off as the gathered fans part.
Moving backstage once again, a beaten Jason Flint staggers down the hallway into the arms of a worried Kimberly Davis.)
Kim Davis: My god ... what happened? Jason Flint: Just another day at the office ... (Flint lets out a weak laugh as Kimberly leads him down the hallway.) Kim Davis: You’re not going to want to go in there ... (Propping Flint up against the doorjam, she opens the door to the demolished room.) Let me go and get you some help ... (Waving a hand at her, he staggers inside. She closes the door and turns to walk away when a hand reaches out and covers her mouth. She gets out a startled cry before the hand silences her and drags her away. The door opens a few seconds later. Jason Flint sticks his head out and looks around for the source of the sound. Seeing nothing he shrugs and closes the door.
Leaving him, the camera fades to the ring where “No Fucking War!” by Seven Year Bitch, blares out over the speakers. Mean Darlene bursts through the curtains, dragging Kim Davis by the hair. Jake Morgan follows closely behind carrying a barbed wire wrapped table. Inside the ring, Morgan sets up the table before producing a mic.)
Jake Morgan: Flint, I know you're in the back, so listen up. I know that you are "Mr. I Don't Give Fuck, about anyone or anything," but I think I may have found the one thing you do care about ... KIM.
You attacked my manager, now I have your valet in my hands. What should I do with her? Be the bigger man and let her go? No, I don't think so. You set up the rules for this little game, and you've had your turn, now it's mine. Unlike you though, I will give you a chance to save her. Come on Flint, come out and prove to everyone that you care about Kim.
(He waits for a minute or so but no Flint.) Okay ... you had your chance. (Jake walks over and grabs Kim. Darlene pulls some lighter fluid and a charcoal lighter out of her jeans. She soaks the table and gets ready to light it, when Jason Flint limps out from the back. After getting a mic from
Starr, he stands outside of the ring.) Jason Flint: What are you going to do Jake? Put her through that table? Go ahead ... I can't wait to see you do it.
(Kim gets a look of utter horror on her face as Jason says that. Jake grabs Kim's head and puts it between his legs setting up a Powerbomb. Darlene gets ready to light it but Flint speaks again.)
That's right Mr. High and Mighty go ahead and put her through the table and become just like me. You claim that you are better than me, but here you go stooping to my tactics. I knew you were full of shit, now everyone else does too.
(Flint has an evil grin on his face, which turns to a triumphant smile when Jake releases Kim. Kim tries to get away but Darlene grabs her by the hair. Jake turns to Flint.) Jake Morgan: You're right, I can't do it. (The crowd boos and Jason laughs.) But SHE can! (The smile on Jason's face melts to a look of surprise. Jake lights the table as Darlene Powerbombs Kim.
It's too late when Flint dives into the ring as a chant of "HOLY SHIT!" starts up. Inside the ring, Flint is smashed with the championship belt to the face.
With Flint down, Jake taunts him while Darlene continues to assault the unconscious woman. After referee Peter Daye is pushed aside by Morgan, he calls for the bell signalling for help.

— Part 3 of 3 —

*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding ***
Rather than the PAW Security, he gets Drake Russell bursting out of the back to attack Flint. Morgan steps back as Russell puts the boots to the downed Flint as once again, Daye calls for the bell.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** The scene is utter chaos as Security hits the ring and is forced back by Morgan who is threatening them with his belt. Sprinting out from the back, Justin Hart pushes his way through and sliding into the ring. With both Hart and Russell
Booting the downed Flint, Darlene slides out of the ring for safety. Pulled to his feet, Flint is double Whipped across the ring right into the belt of a waiting Jake Morgan. Smacked in the head, Flint spins through the air before landing on the canvas.
Continuing with his Stomping, Hart is pulled back by Drake Russell who wants in just as badly. With the two men going nose to nose and arguing, the Texas
Champion ended the conversation as he wrapped his arms around Hart’s waist and planted him into the canvas with a Belly to Back Suplex.
Spun around by the shoulder, Morgan took a Right Hand to the face and fell backwards by an irate Drake Russell. No longer being held back, Security hit the ring and wrapped up Russell who looked up to see Jason Flint up on the top rope. Diving off, Flint Splashed him as well as the Security, taking them all to the mat which brought the fans to their feet.
Peter Daye left the ring and helped Kim Davis to the outside, carrying her to the back as the fight continued.
Flint staggered to his feet but was put right back down by a chunk of the demolished table courtesy of Morgan who in turn took a blow to the back of his head by Hart. Grabbing up the table part, Hart turned Morgan over and started to strangle him with it. A Low Blow by Mean Darlene ended that with Jake falling to the canvas choking and Justin Hart holding the family jewels.
Trying to leave the ring, Darlene was grabbed by Flint who had an evil grin on his face as he pulled her head down into a Standing Head Scissors. After slapping her on the behind several times, he snapped her up into the air and brought her right back down into a Powerbomb ... turnabout is fairplay.
Pulled outside the ring, Russell fought his way free of Security after having laid several of them out with Right Hands, backing the rest of them up with a chair he had liberated from a fan. Diving right back in, he nailed Flint in the back, knocking him to his knees. Spinning around, a surprised Russell had the chair Kicked back into his face by an angered Jake Morgan.
Morgan looked around for the next person to attack when he took a baseball bat to the stomach and was doubled over by Black Thorne. Bent at the waist, he was
Bulldogged into the mat by Christian Maeda who had joined the fray. Justin Hart came in firing Punches, knocking Thorne aside before going after Morgan while Maeda ignored them and went right after Flint. Pulling him to his feet, Maeda sent him across the ring and on the return took him to the outside of the ring with a Drop Kick.
Back on his feet, Maeda threw his arms into the air which brought out the boobirds which brought a smile to his face. Turning around, he was taken to the mat with a vicious Spear by Drake Russell. Russell hit Maeda so hard that the blow carried him into Hart, dislodging him from Morgan.
Taking a chair from Mean Darlene, the Champion laid out Hart and Russell with it as he bent it over their heads and was ready to use it on Maeda, who hightailed it from the ring. Turning as he heard a warning from the outside,
Morgan went down as Flint came off the ropes with a Spinning Heel Kick. On the mat, Flint was pulled to the outside by Maeda and Black Thorne before being worked over. Slumped back against the apron, he took a hammering as the two beat him with Fists and the Bat. His savior came in the form of Kimberly Davis, who despite being bloodied and burned, ran back out to Flint’s rescue,
Singapore Cane in hand. After taking Maeda down, she was grabbed by Thorne who shoved her roughly back. Given some breathing room, Flint caught the cane as it was tossed to him. Slapping Black Thorne on the shoulder, he brought it down across his head with a furious impact.
Not a small man, Thorne staggered backwards but kept his feet ... with a second blow however, he wasn’t so lucky and hit the floor.
Inside the ring, Morgan had shoved Russell back into a corner and was hitting him with Rights and Lefts then stepping back just as Justin Hart came flying in with his Corner Splash. Hitting big “Air”, Hart slammed into an unsuspecting
Drake Russell. With Russell dropping to the mat, Morgan grabbed Hart and slapped an arm around his head, pulling him down with a DDT.
With Flint leading Kimberly to the back and Morgan the only one on his feet, the Security hit the ring and seperated everyone as the referee called for the bell a final time, ending things.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Leaving ringside and the cheering fans, the cameras focussed in on B.J. Perris in the back with newcomer Shane Silver.)
Perris: Shane ... I know you’re going to see that you made the right decision by coming north and giving us a go ...
(Silver nodded his head in agreement when they were approached by the woman in the Offspring mask with a clipboard in hand and Kat Collins, who had a wooden paddle on her shoulder.
Snapping her head back, the woman cleared the light colored dreads from her mask, revealing the mesh covered eyelets.)
Woman in mask: B.J. ... I’ve been looking over some of these expenditures for the last month and well ...
(Perris gave her a dirty look as he spun around to face her.) Perris: Not now ... can’t you see that I’m showing one of our newest wrestlers around? Woman in mask: That’s what I’m talking about ... with what you’re paying Khan and in bringing in this so-called talent, not to mention the cost of this supercard next week ... we may not ...
Perris: I said not now ... Before protesting, the head of Shane the Security Guard pops out from a doorway as he looks into the hallway.)
Shane: Can I get some help here?! (Perris and Silver make their way over, looking into his office.) Perris: Damn. Not my desk! FLINT!!!! (The camera pans the room where Johnny Green has been crushed through Perris’ desk as the evidence points to a scuffle with the final conclusion on the floor.
