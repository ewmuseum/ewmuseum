PAW — Card Archive

1.7 Card

7 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 4 —

(Another week, another show as Aaron Starr and Lisa Doyle make their way from the back and circle the ring celebrating with fans for the first show of the new year.
With the small but boisterous cheering their approval, Starr holds the ring ropes open for the lovely Doyle.)
Aaron Starr: Hello ... and welcome to Public Access Wrestling! We’re kicking off the new year in proper PAW style with two big matches this evening that are just the beginning of what we have on tap for you this year!
Lisa Doyle: That’s right, Aaron. Tonight, despite having a #1 Contender in the Trashman ... seven men are going to fight it out in a Battle Royal to determine who the next #1 guy is ... behind our Television Champion, Drake
Russell. Following on the heels of that match, Drake Russell squares off against Jake Morgan and Oklahoma’s favorite son, Brutal Brian Floyd! Aaron Starr: Speaking of the Trashman, we have a little interview time before going to our television taping ... and here he is ... ladies and gentlemen ...
the Trashman! (A cheer rises as the thin man dressed in a black t-shirt ands jeans makes his way out from the back. Stopping on his way to the ring, a smile crosses his lip as her pumps his fist into the air.
A chant of “Trash!” follows his entering the ring. Leveling his Singapora Cane at the fans, he gives a small salute before turning back to Starr and Doyle.) Thank you for joining us, Trash and Happy New Year. Trashman: Thanks for having me, Aaron ... Lisa. It’s great to be here in front of these great fans.
(The chant grows louder bringing a pause in the interview. With prompting from Starr, the sound dies down in the small television studio.) Aaron Starr: How’s that for a welcome, Trash? Trashman: It doesn’t get any better than that, Aaron ... Aaron Starr: I gotta ask ... what’s going through your mind just a few minutes away from a Battle Royal to crown someone the #1 Contender to Drake
Russell’s title? A match thet you’re not even involved in? Trashman: Well, Aaron ... to be honest, I’m not sure. The way I look at it, no matter who wins tonight will still have to go through me to get that shot at
Drake Russell ... Lisa Doyle: There have been rumors of you going to Japan on tour ... perhaps that has something to do with it?
Trashman: Well ... I was invited to go to Japan to participate in the Japan Puroresu Association’s January tour but that had to be pushed back to February. No matter how much of an honor it would be to wrestle against some of the greats in Japan ... nothing is going to take me away from wrestling right here
... not now with everything heating up as it is ... I wouldn’t have it any other way ... these fans wouldn’t have it any other way! (The fans start chanting “P-A-DUB!” bringing a smile to those inside the ring.) The way I see, guys ... there’s a little bit of “House Cleaning” to be done here before anyone goes elsewhere to wrestle. Until that happens, I’ll be right here!
(The chanting dies away and is replaced by booing as Hiroshima Nagasaki and Jake Morgan are led from the back by manager Lonny Nakamura to the ring. With a confident pair of wrestlers standing behind him, Nakamura steps forward with a mic in hand.)
Lonny Nakamura: The ungrateful behavior of many of the wrestlers and officials in PAW is heartbreaking. That men as honorable and popular as Jake Morgan and
Hiroshima Nagasaki can be repeatedly cheated ... is just not right! (His stare hardens as he looks towards the smiling Trashman.) You can come out here and cry all you want about being the rightful #1 Contender but all you have to do is look at the two men standing behind me to see the “Power” behind this promotion ... the true Television Champion and the #1 Contender.
And after tonight, they will once again be on the right path, leading this promotion in the right direction ... something you, Jason Flint, Drake Russell
... and not even your returning hero, the Offspring could do! (Nagasaki steps forward closer to Trashman.) You want a shot at the title? (Trashman calmly nods his head up and down.) Don’t you worry ... you’ll get it just as everyone else in the back stupid enough to step into the squared circle with one of these two men will get theirs ...
No favorites ... everyone will be beaten equally ... (Nakamura is joined in laughing by his two wrestlers which is not well liked by the fans who are more than happy to voice their opinion. Turning away, Nakamura hands his mic over to Jake Morgan.)
Jake Morgan: Sit down and ... SHUT THE HELL UP! The only two men who matter in PAW have arrived and you morons need to sit down and pay us the respect we deserve. (Morgan waits, with a smile on his face, for the angry crowd to die down before continuing.)
I've been thinkin. Thinkin about how week after week after week, Hiro and I bust our asses to make this federation worth a damn and how week in and week out ...
ALL ... WE ... DO ... IS WIN! And still despite all that, we're the ones who ALWAYS get SCREWED. I'm sick of it, I know Hiro's pissed, so this shit stops NOW!
Offspring? You never should have come back. Especially not now. Flint, Hart, Russell ... Burt? Each and every one of you bastards are about to get crushed by the Unstoppable Machine and the H-Bomb. You were all warned but not a single one of you could remove your heads from your asses long enough to realize just what kind of trouble you're really in. Well ...
Tonight all of you will get your first taste of our Reign of Terror! (Turning to Nagasaki, Morgan slaps him on the chest before handing the mic over. Nagasaki takes his own sweet time before launching into it.)
Hiroshima Nagasaki: No one in this organization will ever remain television champion as long as I am around. Your days are numbered pretender. Robbing the
H-Bomb of his title is the worst thing you could ever have done. NOW, I understand what Morgan-san has been speaking of this entire time. And now, everyone in Pathetic American Wrestling will suffer the consequences. I will not hold back.
Weapons, rulebreaking, attacks in the back ... all that I despise are now tools of the trade that I will use against anyone stupid enough to stand against me or my partner. Remember, when you are suffering from the beatings Jake and I have inflicted ... WE did not want this. You ... all of you, had a hand in creating this monster.
Lest anyone forgets, the road to glory in PAW still and always will, run through us. Stand in our way ... bring anything you might need ... come as a united front ... it won’t matter.
(Morgan steps forward to where both men are standing side by side.) WE ... are the strongest, WE ... are the most talented, WE ... are the most determined, WE ... are the reasons that all of this still exists, AND WE ARE ANGRY! And all of you are at GROUND ZERO! (Letting the microphone drop, he and Morgan launch themselves into action at Trashman. Swinging his weapon, Trash catches Morgan in the head before being knocked to the mat by a clubbing Forearm from Morgan’s partner. Once on the ground, Trashman is an easy target for Nagasaki’s boot.
Shaking out the cobwebs after being driven to one knee, Morgan jumps in with the pummeling of the young wrestler. Starr’s attempt to distract the two wrestlers ends quickly with a threat to his own safety before he wisely leaves the ring, ushering Lisa Doyle away from the brutal action.
After taking a stiff Short Arm Clothesline from Morgan, the downed Trashman then takes Nagasaki’s “H-Bomb” off the top rope that shakes the ring.
With the booing growing louder by the second, Nagasaki and Morgan join in with the insults as they turn them back at the fans while Nakamura joins in, repeatedly Slapping Trash. Morgan’s holding the ropes open for the fans in the front row shouting obsenities and making rude gestures goes unanswered as
PAW Security moves in the keep both parties seperate. Shaking their head in disgust, both men get a look of surprise as their challenge is met by one lone man walking out from the back ... the masked
Offspring. Making his way towards the ring as well as possible for a man with a heavy brace on his knee, Spring slides into the ring ready for action.
Despite the odds, Nakamura steps in to push back both of his wrestlers to the displeasure of the fans. Leading them from the ring, the trio leaves the
Offspring alone in the ring to the cheering of the fans.)

— Part 2 of 4 —

(The camera opened on Justin Hart entering the PAW television studio. With a bag for his gear over his shoulder, Hart warded off a few production persons with his icy stare before a commotion caught his attention.
Dropping his bag, Hart rushed off as the camera left him to focus in on Hiroshima Nagasaki and the Black Ravens beating on a hapless Chip. Under Nagasaki’s direction, the Ravens set up a table for the Televsion Champion to drive the obese man through with a “Nagasaki Driver”.
With Chip splintering the table, Justin Hart rushed into view, slinging Punches and scattering the Ravens before turning his attention towards the TV Champion.
The two men squared off, looking at each for a few brief seconds before Security intervened and seperated both parties. Leaving the scene, the camera found the green haired Toadie making his way through the back only to be stopped by Kidd Gannon, looking out from his dressing room ... the one with the big gold star on the door with the word
“SUPERSTAR” in it’s center.) Kidd Gannon: My water? Where’s my water? (A confused Toadie looked back at the wrestler unable to utter a word.) My bottled water? (The green haired man shrugged his shoulders.) Do you know who I am? If I were you ... I’d hustle my ass and get that bottled water in a hurry ... or else! (Shaking his head, Toadie walked off as the disgusted Gannon stood there watching.) Why do they hire these people? (Turning his head, Gannon was approached by the sequined Paco who pulled out his two imaginary “pistols” and “fired” them. With an indignant look,
Gannon rolled his eye.) Paco: So ... I was wondering ... with your return to PAW as a wrestler, you’re going to need someone to get your back ... and who better than ...
WHOA MOMMA!!!!! (With a sudden hip gyration, Paco went into near convulsions before stopping and looking back at Gannon.)
... me to watch your back as a member of the Heartbreakers? Whataya say. compadre? (With a heavy sigh, Gannon ducked back into his dressing room and slammed the door. In doing so, the large star decoration came loose and swung down, revealing the Men’s Room sign underneath.
Moving to another part of the backstage area, Jake Morgan came into view. Leaning back against the wall for support, Morgan, with a pained look on his face, rubbed at his forehead and the welt upon it.
Preoccupied, Morgan never noticed Mean Darlene and brother Jeremy moving closer.) Mean Darlene: Jake? Jake are you okay? (Morgan stopped his rubbing and looked confused for a second.) Jake Morgan: Darlene? Jeremy? (Mean Darlene smiled back at Morgan’s younger brother just before there was a load smacking sound and Jake Morgan hit the floor. Standing where Morgan had been was the Oklahoma Sooner jacketed Brutal Brian Floyd holding a chair. A big smile etched across his face as large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession.)
.... 5 .... .... 4 .... .... 3 .... .... 2 .... .... 1 .... (White Zombie's "More Human than Human" begins as flashes of Hiroshima Nagasaki in action are shown. Scene after scene quickly flash by of past battles ...
Running his thumb across his throat before a limp Drake Russell is planted into the canvas with a Nagasaki Driver…
The “Street Soldier” Justin Hart takes a Double Thrust to the Throat and with a Snap Suplex, put into a pinning predicament ...
Pep Preston is flat on his back as Nagasaki leaps from the top turnbuckle and lands the “H-Bomb” ...
In slow motion, Aaron Starr turns right into a ball of fire thrown into his face ... And finally, a battered Jason Flint with Nagasaki and turncoat Jake Morgan standing over him.)
After an explosion, the scene fades to black and the PAW logo disappears before fading back in and panning the small television studio and the fifty or so cheering fans surrounding the ring on three sides.
Moving through the small crowd, the camera zooms in on the broadcast duo of Aaron Starr and Ishimoto Sakai.) Aaron Starr: Thank you for joining us for our very first television broadcast of 2001. We’ve got two very big matches this evening that are sure to have ramifications in the months to come.
Ish Sakai: You can say that again, Starr ... tonight Public Access Wrestling’s two best wrestlers AGAIN establish themselves as the dominate force in the promotion.
Aaron Starr: Let me guess ... you’re referrring to Hiro Nagasaki and Jake Morgan? Ish Sakai: Of course, who else would I be speaking of? Perhaps the injured Jason Flint? Maybe Justin Hart? Wait a second ... was I speaking of Kidd Gannon? Of course I wasn’t. There are two men who stand heads and shoulders above the rest here ...
(Starr rolls his eyes as the fans start booing.) Aaron Starr: I have a feeling that quite a few of those names you mentioned as well as a few others like the current Television Champion, Drake Russell would have something to say about that little claim of yours ... besides, your former
Champion only seems capable of doing his work with some help as he and the Black Ravens did earlier in the back on a defenseless Chip for no reason whatsoever ... Ish Sakai: No reason?!?!?!? He insulted the great Hiroshima Nagasaki! Aaron Starr: Insulted? You have got to be kidding me? Ish Sakai: Not at all. Chip is a perfect example of everything wrong with American Wrestling and each time he sets foot in this building, he is an insult to this great sport of ours.
An insult to this sport, insults Hiro’s great fans and as such, Hiroshima Nagasaki himself is insulted ... Aaron Starr: His “fans”? I think I’m being insulted now ... (A startled Starr is interrupted as the gong and kodo drums sound loudly announcing the entrance of Hiroshima Nagasaki and manager Lonny Nakamura. The pair enters the ring to the loud booing, largely ignoring the fans on their way.)
Ish Sakai: Just in time to save us from more of your slanted opinions ... (Following out from the ring are Kidd Gannon, Justin Hart, Skip Westervelt and Jason Flint. WIth Peter Daye checking over each of the wrestlers as they are being announced to the fans, a masked man makes his way out from the back ...
newcomer Suicide Youth circles the outside of the ring, slapping a few hands then looking over each of the wrestlers inside. Though studying them intently, he draws little if any attention from the other participants, many of whom are already having a few words as Hart and Westervelt “share their thoughts” with the smiling Nagasaki.
Ready to begin, the wrestlers all turned as a voice boomed out over the PA system.) Voice: Hold on ... (Stepping out from the back was the masked Offspring with a mic in hand. There was a small cheer for the long time indy wrestler who had at one point made a mark upon the face of Denton and her wrestling organization.)
Offspring: Looking around the ring, I’m not counting seven heads as advertised for this Battle Royal. It would appear that one of you is not here and with a quick once over, it appears Chip isn’t here ... isn’t that right, Nagasaki?
He’s not here because you and yours saw fit to eliminate him just as you and Morgan attacked the Trashman earlier ... Now, you might have made the odds a little better in your favor and given time, you might have eliminated everyone else inside that ring with you but seeing as that didn’t happen and seeing as these fans want to see you get your ass kicked by six other men tonight ... I’m ready, willing and able to throw my mask into the ring and do just that ...
(Chants of “P-A-DUB!” started up as the Offspring threw down his mic and made his way towards the ring, stopping only when another voice peeled out from the PA system.)
Voice: Whoa! Hold on, Offspring! (Now it was the Offspring’s turn to stop and look back as a dapperly dressed man came quickly out from the back waving a piece of paper in his hand.
Followers of the indy scene would recognize the man as Niles Neptune. Approaching Offy, the pair seemed to be arguing over something as they both pointed intermittendly towards the ring and it’s occupants. With the fans eager for action and calling for Nagasaki’s blood while several staredowns were going on inside the ring, the former Champion left the ring and made a beeline for an unaware Offspring. Smacking him in the back of the head with a
Forearm, Nagasaki dragged the masked wrestler towards the ring and rolled him inside and joining him as referee Peter Daye called for the bell.)

— Part 3 of 4 —

*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding ***
With no time for caution, the wrestlers inside the ring rushed to the attack, pairing off as best they could. Nagasaki hammered the unsuspecting Offspring while Flint and Hart paired off and traded shots. Kidd and Skip became involved in a shoving match that quickly escalated into an exchange of Knife Hand Chops.
There was a cheer as a bandaged Chip ran out from the back and rolled into the ring ready to help Skip, pushing Gannon into the corner where they took turns hammering him under a barrage of Punches before dropping him with a Double hip
Toss. Kipping back to his feet, Gannon struck with a Double Back Elbow, catching both of his attackers flush on the jaw and staggering them.
Seeing the opening he was looking for to make his entrance, Suicide Youth used the ropes to vault himself into the ring for a Head Scissors Take Down on Skip while Gannon used the distraction for a Hip Toss of his own on the obese Chip.
In the center of the ring, Flint and Hart continued to trade Punches until Nagasaki, who left the downed Offspring, hit a solid Roundhouse Kick to the back of Flint's head. With Flint crumbling, Nagasaki was rewarded with a series of Punches from Hart that staggered him. Backing up, Nagasaki tripped over
Flint as he regained his feet thanks to a shove from Hart. The crowd roared with laughter. Flint wasted no time in nailing Hart with a groin shot followed by a perfectly placed Kneelift that sent Hart sprawling.
On the other side of the ring, Gannon and Suicide Youth teamed up in an attempt to lift Chip over the top rope. The portly man squirmed and wiggled until he was free. Rushing over to help his partner, Skip missed the ducking Gannon with a Flying Cross Body Block instead diving over the top rope and being eliminated.
With Chip trying to apologize to Skip, Youth popped off the middle rope and nailed him in the back with a Spinning Leg Lariat that would have sent him to the floor if not for Skip’s save. On the offensive, Gannon and Youth continued to pound away at the helpless Chip.
With Hart down, Flint's undivided attention is solely on Nagasaki. Not backing down, Nagasaki exchanged Punches with Flint,succeeding in gaining the upper hand. A Double Thrust to the throat followed by a Knee to the forehead levels
Flint. Lifting his prone opponent onto his shoulders, Nagasaki prepared to dump him over the top. A quick twist of the body and Flint was able to slip off. The
Offspring moving in to spin Nagasaki around gave Flint a few precious seconds. Or rather, a second as the returning Hart came flying in, knocking Flint off his feet with a Spear. Slamming Flint’s head into the turnbuckle, Hart hammered away.
Pushing Nagasaki off into the ropes, then taking him down with an Arm Drag, the Offspring followed up with a Drop Kick knocking the former Champion off his feet. Like an animal, Offspring stalked his prey before pulling Nagasaki to his feet. A ready Nagasaki with an Eye Rake stopped the masked man. A Nagasaki
Driver left the Offspring sprawled out on the mat. After throwing Chip into the ropes, Gannon and Youth combine on Double Spin Kicks. Bouncing off the ropes, Suicide Youth landed a Running Senton while Gannon mounted the ropes. Jumping off, Gannon landed a Senton of his own and rolled back to his feet ... right into a Super Kick from Nagasaki that sent him crashing back against the ropes. Charging in, Gannon was eliminated with a stiff Clothesline.
Leaving Chip, the masked Youth rushed in to attack Nagasaki. Two punches is all he lands before Nagasaki tosses him into the nearby corner and begins to unmercifully pound him. Chip, regaining his feet, landed a Corner Splash on both men. Nagasaki stumbled out of the corner while Suicide Youth fell flat on his face. The crowd cheered Chip's belly shaking celebration in the center of the ring.
With a decisive advantage on Flint, Hart went to work on his hated enemy with a series of Chops culminating in a Spinebuster that shook the ring. Leaving Flint down on the mat, Hart grabbed Nagasaki and brought him back to Flint, pulling him up to his feet. With a handful of hair, Hart smacked both of their heads together, stunning them.
Grabbed from behind, Hart was spun around by the Offspring and sent across the ring into the celebrating Chip. The collision sent the two men into a pushing match that resulted in Chip being laid out with the “1-8-7”.
Having cleared himself some room, the Offspring hobbled back for a running start at the corner where Nagasaki and Flint were locked in a struggle to gain control along the ropes. Jumping into the air, the Offspring swung his body around to land backfirst into both men. Falling back to the mat, Nagasaki was barely missed as the Offspring projectile crashed into Flint, the result ...
both men falling over the ropes and crashing heavily on the floor below. And then there were four ... A recovering Justin Hart returns to the offensive by Kicking Nagasaki from behind before overwhelming the H-bomb with wild Punches. Unrelenting, the
Street Soldier drove Nagasaki into the corner where a combination of Bodyshots and Right Hands to the head put Nagasaki on the ropes. Whipping Nagasaki across the ring into the opposite turnbuckle, Hart nailed him with the Hangtime
Splash. Hart's celebration is brought to an early end when Chip crushed him with a Shoulder Block. Following up with a Body Slam allowed Chip to celebrate while Suicide Youth had regained his senses enough to work over
Nagasaki with Boots to the chest and head. Dragging Nagasaki out of the corner, Youth went up the ropes for a Corkscrew Leg Drop off the middle rope. Chip, grabbing Hart by the hair and returning him to his feet, pulled the former Texas Heavyweight champion up the ropes to the top.
Regaining his senses, Hart began to land blow after blow to Chip's head with one hand while holding on to the rope for dear life with the other. Hart appears to almost be free as Chip's grip begins to falter but an Elbow from the large man ends that. Climbing to the top rope and balancing precariously, Chip was sent hurtling towards the floor by a Drop Kick off the ropes from Suicide
Youth. A chant of “Holy Shit!” from the fans followed Chip’s fall to the hard floor that left three men inside the ring.
Locking up in the center of the ring, Hart and Suicide Youth struggled briefly for control before Hart rammed the smaller masked wrestler into the corner.
Youth ducked out of the way of a Back Elbow, escaping the corner. A Front Kick was followed up by a Whip into the far corner before he chased Hart with a
Handspring Back Elbow. Knocked silly, Hart stumbled out of the corner into a Snap Suplex. Youth went to the well once too often, missing with a Springboard Moonsault as his target rolled out of the way.
Hart was pulled to his feet and flattened with a Belly-to-Belly Suplex. Ripped back to his feet, Hart was spun around and put in the "Unconditional
Surrender". Hart’s arms flailed away as he tried to escape the Oriental Spike and might have gone under if not for the third man in the ring who came off the ropes and landed a Missile Drop Kick, sending all three men to the canvas.
Bouncing back to his feet, the masked man landed a Flying Elbow Drop before pulling Nagasaki to his feet which drew the attention of Lonny Nakamura. From the apron, Nakamura shouted at the ref about tights being pulled as his wrestler was snapped over with a Snap Suplex.
Throwing himself back against the ropes, Suicide Youth knocked Nakamura to the floor which brought a cheer from the fans. Spinning back to the action, he stared into the raging eyes of Justin Hart and threw his hands up defend himself.
Turning, Hart went back to Nagasaki and pulled him to his feet. With a nod of the head, Hart called Youth over. Both men wrapped an arm around the head of the limp Nagasaki before falling back and planting him into the canvas with a
Double DDT. The crowd went nuts as both men bounced back to their feet with a nod of acknowledgement. Each grabbing a leg, they fell back to the mat, making
Nagasaki cry out in pain from a Leg Splitter. With one man down writhing pain, Suicide Youth and Hart turned their attention towards each other. A Collar and Elbow Tie-up led to an Irish Whip with Youth being sent across the ring. On the return, Youth ducked under a Punch and continued across the ring, nailing Hart with a Flying Cross Body Block.
Pulling Hart back to his feet, Youth spun around to Whip him across the ring but was countered. Sent across the ring, Suicide Youth hit the ropes and returned, attempting to go over Hart with a Leap Frog, only to be caught in
mid-air and slammed down with a Powerbomb. Pulling Suicide Youth up, Hart Gorilla Pressed him over his head and made his way towards the ropes. Hart’s bellow was silenced by a Kick from Hiro
Nagasaki. falling back against the ropes, Hart dropped Youth over the top rope, eliminating him from the contest.
And then there were two ... Pulling Hart to his feet, Nagasaki Whipped him across the ring and deposited his opponent on his backside with a Back Body Drop. Following a Leg Drop, Hart was pulled back to his feet and pushed up into the air and held vertically.
Twisting his body, Hart slid off and landed on his feet behind Nagasaki. Spinning Nagasaki around, Hart lashed out with a Front Kick that was blocked before the man from Japan lunged forward with a Diving Clothesline, taking his opponent to the mat.
Hitting the ropes, Nagasaki softened Hart up with a Knee Drop before pulling him up for a Fireman’s Carry, falling to his knees with a Back Breaker Drop.
The fans were booing as Nagasaki returned to his feet and Stomped Hart repeatedly. Leaving the beating, Nagasaki argued with some fans in the front row, inviting them into the ring by holding the ropes open.
With a disgusted wave, he returned to Hart who was struggling to pull himself to his feet using the ropes. Nagasaki was caught by surprise, taking a Forearm
Uppercut to the chin. Knocked back, Nagasaki was on the defensive as Hart came on with Punches.
Wrapping his arms around Nagasaki’s waist, Hart lifted him for an Atomic Drop. Hart followed Nagasaki as he skipped away holding his tailbone, to deliver more damage, clubbing him from behind with a Double Axe Handle.
Pushing Nagasaki into the corner and up the ropes, Hart dumped the former Champion on the mat with a Belly-to-Belly Suplex off the bottom rope. The fans were cheering as Hart followed the crawling Nagasaki who was looking for safety. Hammering Nagasaki in the back with several well placed Stomps brought manager Nakamura onto the ring apron again with complaints for the ref. Daye argued with the manager while Hart tied Nagasaki up for a Side Russian Leg
Sweep. The move never happened as Nagasaki jabbed a Thumb into Hart’s eye. Pulling Hart’s head down, Nagasaki drove his Knees upwards, knocking the Street Soldier back against the ropes. Holding him place with a Forearm, Nagasaki drove his Open Palm into Hart’s chest. Hart was holding onto the ropes as
Nagasaki continued to try to drive him to the outside. Rearing back with his head and driving it forward, Hart reversed positions and opened up with Lefts and Rights of his own that had Nagasaki on the ropes ...
quite literally. That is until Lonny Nakamura was up on the ring apron drawing Hart’s attention. Grabbing Nakamura, Hart smiled as he reared back to punch the pesty manager. Grabbing Hart’s head, Nakamura dropped from the apron to the floor, bringing Hart down throatfirst across the top rope. Leaving for the other side of the ring, Nakamura drew a heavy warning from the referee.
With Hart holding his throat, it was simple for Nagasaki to dump the man over the top rope and take the match.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding ***

— Part 4 of 4 —

(Moving to the center of the ring with his head down , a battered but
victorious Hiroshima Nagasaki dropped to one knee and punched both his hands into the air while the fans booed loudly before chanting “BULLSHIT!”
On the outside of the ring, a dejected Justin Hart, hands on hips, kicked at the ring railing befor glaring back into the ring at his opponent.
Leaving the ringside area, the camera found Drake Russell in the back. Making his way through the halls, he seemed to be looking for someone as his head darted from side to side. With Kimberky Davis passing, he reached out and grabbed her by the arm.)
Drake Russell: Kimberly ... you haven’t seen Lisa anywhere have you? I’ve been looking all over for her!
(Davis stopped to think for a second, scratching her head as she did so.) Kimberly Davis: Come to think of it, I did see her awhile ago ... she was heading downstairs ... then I passed Pep Preston and the Black Ravens as I came upstairs ...
Say ... you better get ready for your match ... you’re up ... (Leaving them, the camera found a pacing Brutal Brian Floyd in his dressing room. Back and forth the man went, working himself up into a frenzy before bellowing out loudly.)
Brutal Brian Floyd: LET'S ... GET ... BRUUUTAAAL!!! (The camera faded out on Oklahoma’s native son only to find a dejected Suicide Youth sitting in the back shaking his head. The masked man sat with his head lowered pondering what went so horribly wrong in his big chance.
With a dejected sigh, he looked up to see the green haired Toadie standing there with a big grin on his face.)
Toadie: Nice match ... Suicide Youth: Yeah ... right, whatever ... (Youth shook his head.) Toadie: No really, that’s what Kidd Gannon said ... (Snapping his head up, the masked man looked at the grinning production assitant.) Suicide Youth: Yeah ... Toadie: Yeah ... he said in a few more years, you might be good enough to scrape the shit off the bottom of a Gannon’s boots ...
(Suicide Youth popped up out of his seat and grabbed Toadie by the shirt and shook him.) Hey man, don’t hit me ... I’m just the messenger ... (Pushing Toadie away, Suicide Youth stormed off leaving the green haired man laughing to himself. Moving back to the ring, “One Step Closer” by Linkin
Park plays over the speakers as Jake Morgan walks out of the back. Drawing the fans ire, Jake threatens few of the fans who have gotten a little too close. Rearing back to strike, Jake laughs at the cowering fans before he continued his walk to the ring. Slowly climbing into the ring, he raised a microphone to his lips.)
Jak Morgane: So here I am. Center ring, ALONE. I've had my say and I stand here ready and willing to back up every word.
(Several waded cups fly out of the crowd, one hitting him in the right shoulder.) Drake, school is in session, now come on out and teach me my LESSON. (Russell's music hits and he walks out of the back. The crowd roars it approval for the Television Champion. Walking to the ring, he stopped with
Jake's words.) Hold on there, CHAMP. Where's your friend, more importantly, where's your help? Come on Brian ... (Morgan sarcastically rolled his eyes.) LET'S ... GET ... BRUUUTAAAL!!! (Drake just shakes his head and points his cane to the back. "Serious Juju" by Sammy Hagar played with Floyd stepping out from the back, chair in hand. Shaking his head, Floyd pointed his red and white painted chair at Morgan, bringing the fans to their feet cheering until both men were standing shoulder to shoulder.
Dropping the mic and motioning for both of them to "come get some", Morgan’s smile disappeared. Looking at each other, the two men rushed the ring with weapons in hand.
Referee “Junior” Akatani called for the bell as Morgan put the boots to Drake Russell sliding in under the ropes. *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Not allowing Russell to get back to his feet, a determined Morgan put the boots to his back ... that is until Brian Floyd did what he does best and that’s
“Getting Brutal”. There was a loud cracking sound that quieted the crowd as Floyd let Morgan have it with his Oklahoma painted chair. Morgan wobbled from the blow, backing up several steps before taking a second one.
The ref was in with a warning, struggling to pull the chair away from the wrestler but didn’t have any luck. Shoving Junior aside, Floyd brought the fans to his feet as he shoved the chair into the air then pointed at a now bleeding Jake Morgan trying to pull himself to his feet.
Halfway up, Morgan motioned for Floyd to bring it ... and bring it he did. Swinging wildly, Floyd missed Morgan as he ducked under the blow towards the center of the ring ... right into Drake Russell who was ready and waiting with his Singapore Cane.
Lashing out repeatedly, Russell splintered his weapon over Morgan’s head bring on a crimson flow that quickly covered his face. The ref was in pulling
Russell back and arguing with him while Morgan struggled to stay on his feet, held up only by the ring ropes he fell back against.
Pushing the ref out out of the way, Russell and Floyd each grabbed an arm and sent Morgan across the ring. On the return, he took a pair of Boots to the face that put him down flat on his back. Before either could make a cover, the ref was in their faces with a warning that brought boos from the fans that were all on their feet.
After arguing, both Floyd and Russell pushed the much smaller man out of the way before turning back towards Morgan who was ready and waiting as he launched himself into the air, taking them both down with a Flying Clothesline.
Back on his feet, Morgan snapped his long hair, now caked with his own blood, back out of his face then shook both fists at his opponents. Pulling Russell up, Morgan put him down with a Belly-to-Back Suplex, bridging into a pin attempt. Akatani dropped to the mat, slapping out a 2 count until Floyd broke up the count with a Leg Drop.
Pushing Morgan into the corner, Floyd bloodied his fist on Morgan’s head as he climbed the ropes and rained down blows to the fans’ delight as they counted along. Dropping down from his position, Floyd stepped out of the corner to pick up Morgan and Suplex him into the mat.
With Russell pulling Morgan to his feet, Floyd motioned for his “ally” to send Morgan. With a Whip, Morgan was sent across the ring and caught. Spun around by Floyd, Morgan was planted into the canvas with an Exploder Suplex and covered. Akatani was slow to get to the count which brought boos from the disapproving fans. Morgan kicked out on a slow 1 count.
A disgusted Floyd complained while Russell lifted Morgan into the air for a Gutwrench Powerbomb and held it for the pin. Of course, there was no count as Akatani argued with Floyd. Russell’s Tilt-A-Whirl Slam put Morgan flat on his back for a follow-up Leg Drop. Pushing Morgan into the corner, Russell came in and caught an Elbow. Spinning out of the corner, the Champ was planted with a DDT. Stepping over Russell’s body, his smile showing through the blood covering his face, Morgan wanted more from Floyd and the Oklahoma boy was one to disappoint as he ran in firing away. The two slugged away with Punches with
Morgan getting the better of the exchange as he backed Floyd across the ring. Making it into a corner, Morgan doubled Floyd up with a Knee to the stomach and an Uppercut to put him on the seat of his pants. Holding onto the ropes, Morgan drove a Boot into Floyd’s throat. The referee closely watched Floyd for a submission despite the fact that Morgan was holding onto the ropes, a fact the small by rowdy crowd as all too happy to point out.
Moving in from behind, Russell wrapped Morgan up in a Sleeper and fell back to the canvas with it firmly locked in place. Despite being a good two feet from the ropes, Junior broke up the hold, bringing on more of the booing from the fans. An argument ensued with the ref claiming Morgan reached the ropes.
Disgusted, Russell pulled Morgan to his feet and hammered him with Punches before doubling him over with a Front Kick. With a Double Underhook, Russell attempted to lift Morgan who struggled. Refusing to be lifted, Morgan threw
Russell over with a Back Body Drop before falling to his knees. With Floyd back in the mix and retrieving his chair, Morgan fled the ring. Throwing the chair down, an angered Floyd shot a glance at his erstwhile ally. Throwing themselves back against the far ropes, both men shot across the ring, sliding to the outside at the last second and driving into Morgan with a pair of Baseball Slides.
Tagged by the two, Morgan went flying back into the ring barrier, pushing it back several feet. With Russell pulling a table out from underneath the ring and setting it up, Floyd lifted Morgan before dropping him down across the barrier.
The fans were slapping Floyd on the back as he pulled Morgan along. After ramming his head into the ringpost, Floyd’s Elbow drove Morgan to his knees.
Calling for a chair from the fans, Floyd turned back to Morgan to deliver a shot but was surprised to find the chair driven into his chest.
Russell stopped Morgan with Punches to the back. Pulled to his feet, Morgan surprised Russell as he caught him with a Short Arm Clothesline. The fans were booing loudly as Morgan lifted Drake Russell into the air and drove him down through the table with a Spinebuster.
Pulling Floyd to his feet, Morgan pushed him back into the ring and taunted the fans before grabbing Floyd’s legs and rolling him to his back. Interlocking their legs, Morgan sat down into a Figure Four Leg Lock. Leaning back, Morgan was able to get ahold of the ropes and used them for leverage as Floyd fiercely fought to break the hold. Ref Junior Akatani watched closely, asking Floyd for the submission but the Oklahoma veteran gritted his teeth and shook his head.
With the fans howling, Floyd sat up and took a couple of pokes at Morgan but was having no luck in breaking the hold. Throwing his body from side to side, slowly but surely, Floyd was able to turn over, putting the pressure on
Morgan’s legs. Able to get the ropes, Morgan got an immediate break. Pulling himself to his feet, Morgan took a Forearm to the head by the returning Drake
Russell before diving into the ring with a Sunset Flip, pulling Morgan down to the canvas. Holding on for the pin, Russell’s and everyone else’s attention was caught by the feminine shriek that blared loud over the PA system followed by the maniacal laughter of Hiroshima Nagasaki.
Listening to the pleading voice, Russell let Morgan loose. Female Voice: NO! STOP! LEAVE ME ALONE! Again there was a shrill scream followed by the laughter. There was a loud splintering sound followed by silence until Hiroshima Nagasaki’s voice poured out.
Hiroshima Nagasaki: You took something from me Drake and since you haven't done the HONORABLE thing and returned it, I've decided to take something from you!
Nagasaki was laughing hysterically as Drake Russell shot from the ring and rushed off for the back.
With Floyd and Morgan left alone, chants of “FLOYD! FLOYD! FLOYD!” ripped through the small television studio as the two men circled. Locked up in the center of the ring, Morgan came out with an Arm Bar which was reversed and countered with a Drop Toe Hold. With a Float Over, Floyd pulled Morgan to his feet with a Front Facelock, turning into a Side Headlock.
Pushed off into the far ropes, Morgan took Floyd down on the return with a Shoulder Butt. Hopping over Floyd, Morgan threw himself into the ropes, shooting back for an Elbow Drop.
Hammering Floyd with Forearms, Morgan Whipped him across the ring. Following in, Morgan hit the turnbuckles as Floyd Tipped Up out of the corner. Wrapping his arms around Jake’s waist before falling back with a Belly-to-Back Suplex.
Making the cover, Floyd got a slow 2 count. Arguing with the ref did no good as several objects littered the ring, bringing in Security to ringside. Grabbed from behind and locked in the Cobra Clutch, Floyd was Suplexed out of the hold but rather than take the pin, Morgan left the ring and grabbed up Floyd’s OU jacket. Dropping it down onto Floyd’s face, Morgan grabbed up his chair and dropped it on top of the jacket. From the middle rope Jake came flying off and landed on top of the chair with a Leg Drop.
Flipping off fans, Morgan pulled the jacket out from under the chair and held it up for all to see. With a shout, he pulled it into two equal halves, tearing it apart. Throwing it down, he spat upon it with a smile on his bloody face.
Dropping across Floyd for the pin, Morgan waited as Akatani slapped the mat once ... twice ... then nothing. Getting back to his feet, Morgan found the ref pulled from the ring by referee Peter Daye. The two refs argued as the fans cheered until grabbing Akatani, Daye dropped him into the floor with a DDT.
A surprised Morgan was spun around, taking a Front Kick before being lifted for a Powerbomb that shook the ring. Grabbing Morgan’s legs, Floyd flipped backwards, bridging for the other half of the “Brutal Bomb”. Daye slid into the ring and slapped the mat with a count. Once his hand hit ... twice ... and again, nothing.
On the outside of the ring, Pep Preston and the Black Ravens were led to the ring by Lonny Nakamura where they pulled Daye from the ring.and worked him over.
Leaving the ring to help the ref, Floyd was pulled back in by Jake Morgan. Pulled to the center of the ring, Morgan wrapped an arm around Floyd’s head, raising his other arm into the air before bring it down across his opponent’s back, driving him down into Floyd’s own chair with a DDT.
Making the cover, Morgan smiled as the fans booed Junior Akatani being pushed into the ring to make the count.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (The fans booed loudly as Morgan and his associates worked over both Floyd and Daye until the Offspring and the Trashman ran out from the back. Grabbing Morgan, Nakamura and the rest bolted from the ring. Morgan tried to get back into the ring at Offy’s and Trash’s urging but Nakamura and Preston would not release their grip on the bleeding wrestler.
Leaving the ring and the loud booing that followed Nakamura’s Team Japan, the camera found it’s way into the back and the sounds of battle. After zooming in on an unconscious Lisa Doyle laying in the remains of a splintered table, the camera moved on to find a bloody Drake Russell locked in combat with an equally bloody Hiroshima Nagasaki before the credits rolled bringing the show to an end.)
