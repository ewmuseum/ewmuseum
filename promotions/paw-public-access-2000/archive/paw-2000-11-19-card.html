11.19 Card

19 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera opens in the backstage area upon manager Lonny Nakamura and two identical looking Japanese men wearing black suits, who stand silently behind.
As the camera moves in closer, the bespeckled Nakamura flashes a wide grin.) Lonny Nakamura: Fans of Pathetic American Wrestling ... tonight, the INVASION begins!!!!! (Nakamura starts laughing as the two men behind him nod their heads in agreement. Leaving the maniacal laughter behind, the camera opens in the community locker rooms where the under card guys dress.
Pep Preston, the Trashman and Emilio Ramirez are present and discussing the recent events as the camera enters their world, when the door suddenly bangs open loudly, and Jake Morgan staggers in. He drops down on the bench and looks up at the empty locker.)
Jake Morgan: What the Fuck happened to my stuff? (Jake looks over to Pep.) Okay, Pep, who the hell is playing the joke around here? I'm really not in the mood. (Pep just looks blankly at Jake.) Pep Preston: Ummmm ... Jake? You haven't used this locker room in about eight months. You've had a private one since then, don't you remember?
(Jake looks at Pep, then ducks his head and rubs his eyes. He sheepishly looks around at the rest of the guys.)
Jake Morgan: Yeah, Pep, I was just trying to pull a joke on you guys. Later. (Jake quickly grabs his bags and rushes out of the room. As the door closes, he leans against it and bows his head. Fading out on Morgan, the cameras catch up to Jason Flint coming into the UNT studio, dressed warmly, patting his hands.)
Jason Flint: Damn! It’s cold! (A hand pokes out at Flint with a steaming cup of coffee. The camera pans over to see Kimberly Davis, smiling.)
You read my mind! Kimberly Davis: Indeed. Here is today’s format and Jason … Jason Flint: Yes? Kimberly Davis: Be nice. Jason Flint: All right, all right. (Flint smiles and walks off to the vending machines, looking over the format and scoffing at most of it. Passing by the Coke machine, Flint pauses to look over to see two Asian men dressed in black suits, standing there.)
Jason Flint: You guys … NWC? (The two look at each other, then back at Flint.) O-kay … Are ya mimes? (Again they look at each other and then back to Flint.) Wait one second… (Flint takes a hard look at the two, sticking his thumb out like an artist would at a canvas.)
Got it … MIB. You do know that Halloween was a while back? Just kidding. Wait a minute, are you two…
(Flint looks over his notes.) …the Black Ravens? (The two men take a hard look at Flint before walking off. Flint shrugs his shoulders before continuing on.)
Jason Flint: Sheesh … doesn’t anyone from Japan have a fucking sense of humor? (The camera fades and then fades back in as Drake Russell makes his way into the building. Shaking off the cold, Russell opens up his long jacket to reveal his latest t-shirt, “My Time, Your Blood.” Russell pops his neck loudly, before walking on.
As he walks, the hip-gyrating antics of one Paco, wobbles on by.) Paco: Drake! Esse! It’s been a week, what about it? (Paco curls his lips and shakes his hips, dropping his voice to that of Johnny
Bravo, of course with a slight Hispanic twang to it.)
You … me … the New Heartbreakers? Drake Russell: Didn’t I tell you last time the answer was no? Paco: Well, since you did pull my hunka-hunka burning butt from the fire with
Jake Morgan last week, I just thought you were reconsidering.
Drake Russell: Uh … no. You and me will NEVER happen. Period. And today, you’ll see why … (Russell continues on his way as a stunned Paco just stands there, his arms open.) Paco: Was it something I said? (The camera fades again and cuts to Jake Morgan leaning over a sink in the bathroom. With hands on either side of the sink and looking at himself in a mirror in front of him, the sounds of his breathing are heard.
His long black hair hangs low over his face as he takes a deep breath before sneering at visage in front of him. Throwing back his head, he says one thing
...)
Jake Morgan: Pain … (Suddenly Morgan slams his head into the mirror, shattering it in a rain of glass fragments. Pulling back, he looks at himself in the mirror, a stream of blood flowing from a gash in his forehead. A surprised look comes to his face when he realizes that he’s not alone.)
Voice: I wouldn’t do that if I were you ... (The camera fades as large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession.) .... 5 .... .... 4 .... .... 3 .... .... 2 .... .... 1 .... (White Zombie's "More Human than Human" begins as flashes of Hiroshima Nagasaki in action are shown. Scene after scene quickly flash by of past battles ...
Running his thumb across his throat before a limp Drake Russell is planted into the canvas with a Nagasaki Driver…
Following a Flying Knee straight to an incoming Seth's face, Nagasaki locks on the “Unconditional Surrender” for a win ...
The “Street Soldier” Justin Hart takes a Double Thrust to the Throat and with a
Snap Suplex, put into a pinning predicament ...
Pep Preston is flat on his back as Nagasaki leaps from the top turnbuckle and lands the “H-Bomb” ...
In slow motion, Aaron Starr turns right into a ball of fire thrown into his face and finally the PAW Television Champion standing in the middle of the ring, the belt raised over his head.)
After an explosion, the scene fades to black and the PAW logo disappears before fading back in and panning the fans and their signs held aloft such as "Time’s almost Up!!", "Darlene=Ratings!!" and "Jake Knows the Internet!".
After making it's way through the boisterous crowd of some 50 or so, the camera settles in on the co-hosts of PAW, a returning Aaron Starr and Jason Flint.
Starr still has a couple of bandages on his face as he looks at the changing attire of the PAW studio and the massive amount of Hiroshima Nagasaki there is around the two of them.)
Aaron Starr: Hello everyone! I’m Aaron Starr… he’s Jason Flint … and this is
PAW Hell. Just kidding, folks, this is Public Access Wrestling!
Jason Flint: No, no, you had it right the first time, Aaron, Hell is exactly where we are, on the threshold of a dark dank road to the abyss but never you fear my friends, there is a light coming down.
Me. Aaron Starr: That’s right, folks, Jason Flint’s NINETY days of suspension comes to an end this week. NEXT WEEK, Jason Flint climbs through those ropes a free man!
Jason Flint: A free man, indeed. Three long months and now it’s time to take matters into my own hands. Exact a little unfinished business that I have with some people. Nothing will be the same when I get to lace up the boots again and show some people what the real deal is.
Aaron Starr: Nonetheless, this is best news to come through here in PAW. The game changes next week and now …
Jason Flint: Now, for all you people, you get to enjoy one last BORING day of
PAW with today’s three matches.
Aaron Starr: Today, we’ve got Paco Presley going up against Drake Russell. Then
Jake Morgan and the Trashman take on the Black Ravens from Japan!
Jason Flint: And as much “fun” as that sounds it’s nothing compared to the main event of the current “Asian SuperEGO”, Television Champion Hiroshima Nagasaki going up against the awesome power of he who would be “Japanese.” His name …
Pep Preston.
I hope I don’t fall asleep. Aaron Starr: It would seem that good ole Pep has fallen out of favor with the
Asian Assassin and is going to settle things inside the squared circle but before we go any further, we have this taped interview between Lisa Doyle and our own esteemed owner, the Masked Woman!
(The duo fades as Lisa Doyle and the woman in the Offspring Mask are standing in front of the “new” PAW banner, with a picture of Nagasaki standing with the belt and a rising sun logo behind him.)
Lisa Doyle: I’m here with the owner of PAW, the Masked Woman, with her comments on Sunday’s activities.
Masked Woman: Thank you, Lisa. Sunday’s show is very special, not only with
Jason Flint’s suspension soon to be lifted and some personal issues to deal with but it also begins the countdown for the December10th Super Card, Winter’s
Nightmare: PAW versus Japan. Winter can be a cruel mistress with her icy cold grasp but this Card promises to make that loving embrace look like a summer breeze ...
Lisa Doyle: Is it true that the main event could possibly be Jason Flint versus
Hiroshima Nagasaki for the PAW Television Title?
Masked Woman: While that would be a great Main Event match, we will more than likely see the Champion taking on the Number One Contender but let’s take nothing away from any of the other matches which could be in the Main Event spotlight themselves as the PAW goes head to head with wrestlers of the Japan
Puroresu Association and quite possibly other promotions ...
Lisa Doyle: This weekend, you have other business to attend concerning the PAW which will take you all the way to Chicago ...
Masked Woman: That’s right, Lisa ... I’ll be in Chicago as a guest of Brittney
Christopher, the owner of American Wrestling Promotions in order to hammer out some final details which may find the two promotions working closely together in the very near future ...
Lisa Doyle: There you have it, guys ... now back to you! (The smiling Doyle fades from sight and is replaced by Starr and Flint, who seem to be a little disgruntled.)
Jason Flint: Number One Contender?! Aaron Starr: It’s better than bow tie and leather shorts. Jason Flint: This … is true. Aaron Starr: Let’s go to the ring! (The camera pans over to the ring, where the hip-gyrating antics of Paco are going through the motions to “Blue Suede Shoes” at the expense of the fans.
The lights dim as “Bitter” from Union Underground plays and the “Man of Destiny” walks out. Singapore cane in hand, Drake points it at back to the backstage area . Pulling out a mic of his own from his long coat, Russell thumps it a couple of times before raising it to his lips.)
Drake Russell: You know there are a couple of people who have a problem with me. Trashman, you think I haven’t paid my dues, done my tour of duty or proven myself to deserve that shot at the PAW title ... well, you’re just WRONG!
I was a fighting champion when I held the PAW Television title. I didn’t weasel my way out of matches like Flint over there did. School was in session every
Sunday and my lessons were laid out in the middle of that ring.
My talent speaks for itself, the drive to take what’s mine is here and soon a new lesson is about to be learned: You don’t want a piece of Drake Russell when
I’m angry ... and let me tell you, I’m VERY angry right now!
Let THIS … be a lesson to you! (Russell climbs into the ring, dropping off his mic, his cane and his long jacket. After referee Peter Daye finishes checking both men, he calls for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Paco scooted all around the ring with a little flair and a lot of hip shaking while his opponent looked on. It wasn’t until Russell popped the young man with a heavy Clothesline that knocked the Graceland fanatic for a loop.
Pulling Paco to his feet, Russell followed up with a series of hard Elbow
Smashes to the face, pushing him back into a corner. A determined Drake Russell laid into the smaller Paco with Knofe Hand Chops that popped loudly off the bare chest ... each strike leaving an inflamed red mark.
A quick Snapmare and Paco was flipped onto his back, gazing up at the lights, just before a hard Leg Drop across his throat, leaving the young man gasping for breath.
Pulling him back to his feet, Russell slung Paco into the ropes, but Paco was ready on the return and ducked out of the way, just to hit the ropes and come flying back with a Slingshot Side Kick, knocking Russell face first into the mat.
Paco went onto the attack with a Boot to the throat to cut off Russell’s air and to keep Russell down. Breaking the obvious Choke, Paco pushed Russell into the corner and held onto the ropes as he lowered a Knee into Russell’s throat, again trying to choke him. Daye pulled Paco away from the corner and in the ensuing argument gave Drake Russell precious moments to recover.
With Daye and Paco going back and forth, the wrestler stepped back and started gyrating his hips which brought a roll of the eyes from the ref. Back on his feet, Russell grabbed Paco by the shoulder and spun him around. Lifted off his feet, Paco was put right back down with a Body Slam and had that followed up by a Leg Drop as Russell bounced off the ropes before dropping a Leg Across Paco’s throat. Sitting there, Russell almost took the match as Daye hit the canvas twice intil a shoulder was lifted at the last second.
Hitting the ropes, Russell threw his body backwards and landed a perfect Springboard Moonsault and made another cover as he hooked a leg. Daye dropped to the mat and slapped out a 2 count, holding two fingers in the air as Paco barely lifted a shoulder in time.
After being Whipped into the far ropes and taken down by a Spinning Back Kick,
Paco was sitting up trying to shake the cobwebs out. Pulled back to his feet,
Paco was lifted and propped on the corner ropes. Climbing, Russell hammered in several Right Hands on his groggy opponent. Balancing himself carefully on the top, Russell dropped Paco with a Reverse Frankensteiner from top rope.
Rolling back to his feet, Russell walked around the ring looking down at his fallen opponent. There was no hip gyrating, no dancing and certainly no snappy banter ... Paco laid there looking up at the lights.
Leaving the ring, Russell stalked around the outside, slapping a few hands as he went before pulling a table out from under the ring. Setting it up on the floor, Russell pointed back into the ring at the unmoving Paco who was being attended to by the ref. Rolling back into the ring, Russell pushed Daye out of the way as he helped Paco to his feet before tossing the Heartbreaker from the ring like a rag doll.
On the outside, he was led around the ring by his ear before being put into a
Standing Head Scissors. Holding the helpless Paco in place, Russell pointed towards the table just before lifting Paco off his feet. With Paco in place,
Russell dashed towards the table, hitting him with a Running Crucifix powerbomb through table, shattering it and taking out the poor hunka hunka stud.
Chants of “PAW!” went up from the fans as Drake Russell returned to his feet only to get an earful of Peter Daye who wanted the match back inside the ring.
Kicking aside parts of the demolished table, Russell dragged Paco back to the ring, rolling him inside.
Dragging him to the center of the ring, Russell made his way back to a corner and started to climb. From the top, he held his arms out from his sides and listened to the increasingly loud audience as they rose to their feet in anticipation of what was to come.
Leaping from the top, he spun through the air and landed his "Total Decimation". Both men bounced off the canvas as Drake Russell landed the big move. It was academic for referee Peter Daye to count to three, after Russell hooked a leg and took home the victory.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Rather than accepting his victory and leaving, Russell grabbed up his Singapore Cane and mic. Standing over the downed Heartbreaker, Russell prodded the young man with his weapon.)
Drake Russell: This lesson was meant for those in the back who doubt my skill … my ability … and most importantly, my destiny on regaining the PAW Television
Title. If anyone has a problem with that, I’ll see you next week.
Let that be a lesson to you all! (And with that Russell drops his mic for the final time and leaves. The cameras cut away back to the announcing team of Aaron Starr and Jason Flint.)
Aaron Starr: An impressive win by Drake Russell, wouldn’t you say? Jason Flint: No … Aaron, I wouldn’t say … (The two announcers chuckle between themselves.) But what I will say is, that this little “contest” was a little too tipped in
Drake’s favor. Size, speed and talent were on El Destiny’s side. Paco, when you wake up, I have only one thing to say … the jumpsuits NEED to go, bud.
Aaron Starr: We’ve gotten word that there’s an incident occurring in the backstage area! (The cameras fade from Starr and Flint to the backstage, where Jake Morgan and the Trashman are both being attended to by Denton EMT’s. Mean Darlene is answering questions to an officer.)
Mean Darlene: This is where I found them! Our match is next and when they didn’t show, I went looking for them and found them like this!
(The cameras pull in on Morgan who is being checked out by the EMT’s. Morgan’s eyes are glazed over but he was still muttering something.)
Jake Morgan: 13 … 13 … 13 … (The cameras cut back to Aaron Starr and Jason Flint, who stand there, speechless, looking at each other.)
Jason Flint: O … kaaay. Aaron Starr: Well … uh ... (The sounds of kodo drums and gongs break the silence as manager Lonny Nakamura leads the way with the PAW Television Champion, Hiroshima Nagasaki and two other gentlemen, clad in black ring attire accompanying them to the ring.
Walking by the announcing table, Flint is seen reading a magazine and not even paying any attention to who’s around.
Nakamura slams his hand down on the table in front of Flint, only to get a returning gesture of his own, which brought the crowd to its feet, mocking the manager and his wrestlers.
Nagasaki snatches away Flint’s magazine and glares down on him. Rolling his eyes, Flint pulls out another magazine and tosses out a mic for Nakamura.)
Lonny Nakamura: INSOLENT American DOG! Show the proper respect to your champion. (Flint pauses a bit, before sitting his magazine down and pulling out a mic of his own.) Jason Flint: I’m going to play a little Jake Morgan here and give you a small history lesson, Lonny-boy. WHO bombed your ass in WW 2? Who broke the back of the great Rising Sun Navy? WHO made you look like a tiny little speck of dirt, swept to the side of history?
AMERICA. (The chants of “USA! USA!” start, annoying Hiroshima and Company.) Look at you Hiro, you possess the two names of the two greatest acts of American ingenuity and destructive power for the 20th Century, and two of the most horrific chapters in your country’s history. So you like to be called the
H-bomb, how appropriate. Do you honestly believe yourself a Phoenix that has risen from the ashes of
YOUR COUNTRY’S FAILURE, to come here to TEXAS, USA, of all places, to exert your “revenge” against the poor lazy Americans?
Well … how patriotic. But that’s not what these people want to see. Not a history lesson or a 101
Learn to Speak Japanese seminar … what they want is for you to go one on one with me and see who is Number One?
(“Jas-on Flint!” Chants begin from the excited fans.) But that’s not the case today. It won’t be the case for a while, will it, bosslady? So in the immortal words of Jake Morgan: Why don’t you shut … the … fuck up, get into that ring and do your job.
And stop wasting my time. (With a loud thump, Flint slams the mic down and walks away. Nagasaki has to be restrained by Nakamura and the Black Ravens from charging Flint who disappears into the back.
With the chants of “Jas-on Flint!” filling the studio, the Japanese contingent never see the bandaged Jake and Trashman rushing out from the back with weapons in hand, Jake with a chair and Trashman has his barbwire wrapped bat.
Attacking from behind, the “Good Guys” lay out the Black Ravens before taking it Nagasaki after pushing Lonny Nakamura to the floor. Peter Daye intercedes, stepping between Nagasaki and the threatening Morgan with raised chair. A confrontation between the two is avoided when sounds of the Black Ravens doubling up on Trashman are heard.
Turning to help his partner, Jake gives Lonny Nakamura time to lead Hiro Nagasaki away from the battle which brings a change in the chanting as “Coward” is bellowed out by one then all in the crowd.

Referee Peter Daye stepped in to stop the attack and get both men into the ring. While arguing with both Morgan and Trash, Daye unwittingly helped the
Black Ravens as they were able to shake out the cobwebs and climb onto the ring apron.
Both men bloody, leap from the apron and take out all three with Apron Splashes. All five men are down as the fans cheer for Morgan and Trashman, trying to help them. Down for several minutes, both parties continue to brawl around the ring with the Americans getting the better of the identical Black
Ravens.
Regaining his feet, referee Peter Daye called for the bell and help from Security before the match even started.) *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (With Security swarming over the four wrestlers and pulling them apart, both parties start to make their way towards the back ... Morgan being lead away by the Trashman after seperating themselves from Security.
Walking away from the scene with a few remaining words for their opponents,
Trash is knocked down followed by Jake as he turns to see who leveled his partner with a shot to the head. The camera focuses on a smiling Drake Russell holding a bent out of shape chair in his hands before breaking away to the back and Lisa Doyle standing, microphone in hand, in front of a door marked "PAW meeting room." As always a broad smile adorns her face. Apparently signaled by the cameraman, Doyle begins to speak in an excited tone.)
Lisa Doyle: Earlier today, I was informed of an important meeting between the major players of Public Access Wrestling to decide the future direction of the promotion. This meeting, which I'm sure all PAW fans are eager to know about, is taking place behind this door as we speak. Luckily, we have been allowed inside to listen in on the major decisions.
One has to wonder exactly which issues are being discussed and who all is involved in the decision making. Which organization will PAW join ... National Wrestling Council or the UAWP? What new talent will be coming to Denton,Texas? Let's go inside and find out!
(Turning and opening the door, Doyle appears shocked to see only Lonny Nakamura and PAW Television champion Hiroshima Nagasaki around a large conference table. No other PAW officials appear to be involved. Doyle stands motionless at the door until Nakamura looks up from the table smiles and waves her in.)
Lonny Nakamura: Aaahh! Come in ... Come in! I am sure the fans of this organization would not want to miss out on the happenings of this important meeting.
(Nakamura wears a wicked little grin on his face while a grimacing Nagasaki looks none too happy as he paces back and forth. Turning away from the surprised Lisa Doyle, the two men resume their conversation. There is one small problem though ... the discussion is in Japanese.)
Lisa Doyle: But .. .Where is ... ? Hiroshima Nagasaki: (Spinning around with a scowl on his face) Just because you were invited here does not mean we expect to be interupted.
Lonny Nakamura: We'll be with you in a moment. (The heated conversation continues for another minute as Nakamura seems to be trying to calm Nagasaki. Several times, the name “Black Ravens” is mentioned by the Champion. Walking over to field questions at Nakamura's urging, Nagasaki stops before Doyle then brushes her aside as he storms from the room.
A flustered Doyle regains her composure as she looks to an apolgetic Nakamura.) Lisa Doyle: So... Lonny Nakamura: I am sorry ... but the Champion has much on his mind right now. I would take this time to address those who are upset about the changes that have been instituted and will continue to make in PAW, and you know who you are, remember this ... when we first arrived on the scene here in PAW, this organization was floundering and barely staying afloat.
Since that time though, the Champion has taken this federation upon his shoulders and carried it into respectability. Months ago, PAW was seen as a small, untalented operation but now PAW consistently earns high marks from those who follow this great sport. Hiroshima Nagasaki and myself have single handedly molded PAW into what it is today. We are not done yet though. Several important and sweeping changes are still in the works. That is all!
(Doyle turns back to the camera as Nakamura rushes from the room.) Lisa Doyle: Who do these guys think they are? Geez! (Scene fades as Doyle stands shaking her head. Leaving the room, the camera makes it’s way through the hallways until settling in on Mean Darlene using a payphone. Zooming in closer, her conversation is picked up.)
Mean Darlene: ... right, I think you should be here. He really needs you ... (She goes silent as she listens to whomever is on the other end.) No ... Jeremy, listen to me. He won’t listen to a word I’ve been saying for the past several weeks. He won’t listen to me ... and I think it’s alot worse than a little concussion.
Still he won’t take any time off ... right. No ... now! Okay ... right. Good bye. (Hanging the phone up, she sighs deeply when a whistle is heard. Turning, she sees the green haired production assistant looking at her backside as he walks by. Shaking her head in disgust, she storms off.
The green haired man watches as she leaves, laughing at her until he suddenly stops. Turning to see what he’s walked into, he gulps deeply as looks into the angered face of Drake Russell.)
Green haired man: Hey ... Drac ... (Standing with his hands on his hips, Russell’s facial features are stone as he stares back, unanswering until he hears someone behind him.)
Voice: I don’t think you want to do that ... (With Russell turning towards the voice, the green haired man wastes no time in making his escape as the camera returns to the ring where Peter Daye is talking to Pep Preston when a loud gong is sounded followed by the familar kodo drums marking the entrance of the PAW Television Champion, Hiroshima Nagasaki followed by his manager Lonny Nakamura.
The Champion stepped into the ring and started stretching after handing his title belt to Nakamura who marched it around the ring. After clearing the ring,
Daye called for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** The Champion catiously circled the as if stalking his prey before locking up with Preston in the center of the ring. With a Go Behind, Nagasaki worked a string of moves, taking Preston down with a Waist Lock and a Float Over into a
Reverse Chinlock. Pulling back on Preston’s head, the Champ asked the ref for a submission.
Moving to the veteran’s legs, Nagasaki locked on an Inverted Indian Deathlock from a standing position. Laughing hysterically, he applied pressure to both of
Pep’s legs before falling backwards and bridging. Not giving up, Preston clawed his way towards the ropes, getting a slow break as Nagasaki finally released the hold.
Using the ropes, Preston pulled himself to his feet but looked unsteady as he came away. Rushing in, Nagasaki took Preston off his feet, hitting with a Drop
Kick to the knees that had already been worked on.
Out of a Handstand, he dropped a Knee into Preston’s already hurting knee. The challenger was holding his knee on the mat as Nagasaki kneeled close by and looked out at the booing fans. Dropping lazily for the pin, he was pushed off on a 1 count.
Pulling Preston up by the hair, Nagasaki sent him across the ring and knocked him down with a Flying Back Elbow that hit squarely on the chin. A Somersault
Leg Drop followed into another lazy pin attempt, this time however, Nagasaki pulled Preston up at the 2 count, drawing both boos from the fans as well as a warning from the ref.
Back to circling, Nagasaki struck with a swift Spinning Kick, hitting Pep on the shoulder and knocking back into the ropes. Pushing Pep into the corner, the
Champ stepped back and fired away with Kicks to the head before listening to the warning referee.
Catching the stumbling Preston as he fell from the corner, Nagasaki locked in the "Unconditional Surrender" and moved to the center of the ring. Pep Preston was in trouble and soon his flailing arms ceased to move about as he succumbed to the Oriental Spike.
Lifting Preston’s limp arm a third time, Daye called for the bell and the end of the match.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Nakamura entered the ring as the fans booed the Champion who dropped Preston. Trying to leave the ring, Nagasaki was stopped by the referee who pointed back at the prone Preston. Ignoring Daye and stepping between the ropes drew more verbal abuse from the referee who then turned to the manager and let him know he was serious.
As Nagasaki started to walk away, Daye started to count which brought a pleading Nakamura over to the Champion. The two talked briefly as Daye continued his count. Disgusted, Nagasaki reentered the ring and pulled Preston into a seated position. Standing behind him, Nagasaki rubbed his hands together quickly before lashing out with a strike between Preston’s shoulder blades.
Preston was suddenly awake as Nagasaki left the ring and made his way to the back, pelted by whatever the fans had at hand.
Moving towards the back, he stopped at the announcer’s table and the lone seated Aaron Starr. Making some comments and laughing at Starr brought more boos until a loud cheer went up as Skip Westervelt walked out from the back with mic in hand.
Lonny Nakamura stepped in front of the Champion and tried to calm him as the new challenger approached.)
Skip Westervelt: For the last few weeks now, you’ve been making this promotion over in your image ...
(Westervelt looked around while the crowd booed loudly at the nodding Nagasaki.) We’ve had to listen to you tell us how you’ve been carrying this promotion on your back ... we’ve had to look at your ugly, slanty eyed face plastered all over the place ...
(Walking up to the nearest banner with Nagasaki’s face upon, Skip pulls it down from the wall, throwing it to the ground and stepping on it.)
All these things you’ve done for us here in Public Access Wrestling ... for the fans ... for all of us in the back ... and for the promotion in general ...
(Skip smiled as he looked around the studio.) I guess it’s time we thanked you for everything you’ve done for us ... we owe you alot, Hiro ...
(A shocked Nagasaki and Nakamura looked at each other then back to the approaching Westervelt. Stepping up, Skip held out his hand in a gesture of good faith which brought louder booing. Nagasaki was hesitant to take
Westervelt’s hand but finally gripped it. Shaking drew more booing and objects from the now standing fans.)
I’d say more than we might be able to give back to you ... but let me be the first to try to repay the debt ... with a little make-over of my own ...
(Out of nowhere, Skip swung his arm and smacked the mic into Nagasaki’s head, staggering him. Pushing the surprised Nakamura out of the way, Skip pushed
Nagasaki into the ring and called for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Pushing Nagasaki off for the far ropes, Skip started the impromptu match with a
Leg Lariat that floored the Champion. Kipping to his feet, Westervelt kept up the pressure with a couple of Elbow Drops and a Snap Suplex.
Rolling over the downed Nakamura, Skip hooked a leg for a 2 count. Showing the ref how the count should have been, Skip returned to the match with a Snapmare, tossing Nagasaki over by the hair then slamming a Knee into his back. From behind, Skip slapped on an Arm Bar on the seated Champion. From a standing position, Westervelt flipped over Nagasaki, stretching out the arm as he rolled back to his feet.
Turning, he hit the Champion with a Kick to the face. With Nagasaki flat on his back, Skip started bouncing off the ropes, criss-crossing the ring and building up a head of steam. Hitting the ropes one more time turned out to be one time too many as Nakamura from the ring apron drove his knee into Skip’s back.
After stumbling forward, Skip caught himself and turned on Nakamura, grabbing him by the shirt and pulling him forward. Raising a fist in threatening manner, he reared back to let it fly but caught a Kick in the back.
Catching Westervelt by the head from behind, Nagasaki fell back with an Inverted DDT, dropping Skip on his head. Having recovered, Nagasaki lifted Skip off the mat and onto his shoulders. Swinging him around, Nagasaki launched him off and planted him into the canvas with the "Nagasaki Driver".
The fans were booing the sudden turn in the match as the Champ scrambled up the ropes. From the top, he punched his fist into the air before leaping off and coming down solidily on top of Westervelt with "The H-Bomb".
As both men slowly tried to regain their feet, the crowd erupted when someone shot from the back towards the ring ...
It was Justin Hart! Hart slid inside and grabbed Westervelt by the hair. Holding him up, Hart gave him a viscious Haymaker that sent him over the ropes. Turning his attention back in the ring, Hart taunted a dizzy Nagasaki, who didn't even know he was there until it was too late. Turning around, Nagasaki was dropped with Hart's new finisher, “the 1-8-7” ... a Kick to the stomach into a Leaping DDT.
Dropping down on all fours, Hart taunted the Champ more as Daye called for the bell and Security hit the ring to restore order.
