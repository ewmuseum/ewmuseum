10.29 Card

29 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera opens on one member of the “new” Heartbreakers, Paco sliding back and forth across the floor in his socks, decked out in his red sequined outfit.
Sitting in a chair watching is his partner, Seth, who looks sick to his stomach as he watches. Hanging from a hook nearby is his own matching outfit.)
Paco: So, I was thinking ... you remember how things were when you and Christian were together ... and I was just coming in with y’all? Do you remember? (Seth buries his face in his arm as he shakes his head.) What if we make it like the old days? You ... me ... and ... someone else? What if there were three Heartbreakers? Whattya say?
(An audible groan is heard from Seth from beneath his arm.) Three of us ... (Paco slides across the floor again when he breaks out into his best Johnny
Bravo.) ... and a whole lotta lovin’ to go around, momma ... (With another groan, Seth gets up from his chair and walks out of the room as the camera fades into Jake Morgan pacing back and forth in front of his dressing room. The shirtless man looks just a little ticked off as he glances repeatedly at the watch on his wrist, shaking his head each time he does so.
Back and forth he goes, stopping only when approached by a young man with green hair.) Green haired man: Yo ... Blake! (Morgan gives the man a stern look.) I was sent to give you a hand ... Jake Morgan: What? Green haired man: A hand ... I was sent to give you a hand. That’s my job around here ... I help with whatever you guys need. You need something ... I’m your hook-up!
You need me to wash your truck ... you need a little info off the internet to run down your opponents with .... I’m the guy to see ...
(Morgan’s hands go to his hips as he steps up in front of the man.) You need me to carry one of those “Shut the fuck up!” signs for you to the ring
... I’m ...
(An angered Morgan puts a hand up cutting the man off.) Jake Morgan: If I were you, I’d turn right around and run up those stairs because you’ve got all of three seconds to live ...
... 1 ... Green haired man: But ... Jake Morgan: ... 2 ... (The green haired man turns and bolts up the stairs leaving Morgan to shake his head. Moving on, the camera finds Lisa Doyle standing with Jake Morgan’s sometime partner, the Trashman. With the camera zooming in on the pair in the stairwell, Doyle turns her mic on Trash.)
Trashman: You know as well as I do that tonight’s Main Event shouldn’t be happening ... no way Drake Russell gets a shot at the Television Title before some of us do ... no way.
The guy was fired a few weeks back and all of a sudden, he’s in the #1 spot taking on Nagasaki ...
Lisa Doyle: Yes, but to be fair ... the “H-Bomb” has faced off against the likes of the Heartbreakers and Emilio Ramirez ...
Trashman: All guys who have been around longer and have been busting their asses off ... unlike your boyfriend ...
(Doyle’s eyes shift to the floor.) Lisa Doyle: He’s not my boyfriend ... Trashman: Whatever ... in any case, my man Jake Morgan deserves a legitimate shot at the belt ...
(The dark clad Drake Russell walks through the interview on his way to the dressing rooms as the Trashman taps him on the shoulder.)
That’s right ... you ... (Stopping, Russell turns to confront the hardcore wrestler, lowering his sunglasses on his nose.)
You don’t deserve to have a shot at the damn Television Title ... Hell, you didn’t deserve to have been rehired ...
Drake Russell: Look ... you have a problem with me ... we settle it inside the ring ... anytime you want to.
(Lisa is pushed back by both men as they go nose to nose.) Trashman: I’d love to ... Morgan would love to ... Drake Russell: Anytime ... (Trashman smiles as he nods his head before firing off a Right Hook as Doyle screams and steps back. Staggering Russell, the Trashman fires off Punch after
Punch, not letting up. With a slumping Russell, Trashman grabs up his Singapore
Cane and lets him have it across the head several times, busting him open in the process.
Watching the beating, Lisa Doyle grabs Trashman, stopping the Caning. Stopping long enough to move Doyle out of the way, Trash turns to restart the onslaught when he takes a Low Blow.
Standing up, the bleeding Russell grabs his own Cane and pulls Trashman to his feet. Wrapping the weapon around his opponent’s neck, Russell falls back with a
Side Russian Leg Sweep. From a Top Mounted position, Russell rains down blows when he takes a Boot to the back of his head. Knocked off of the Trashman,
Russell turns around to see a grinning Jake Morgan swinging a wrecking
ball-like that connects. Pulling Russell to his feet, Morgan wraps an arm around his neck and slaps him on the back for a DDT in the confined space. Getting back to his feet, Jake looks in on Trashman before grabbing Russell by the arm when Lisa Doyle tries to pull him off as she calls for Security, her voice booming in the stairwell.)
Jake Morgan: He’s going to need alot more than Security when I get finished with him ... why don’t you make yourself scarce ... you don’t want to see this
...
(After smashing Russell facefirst into the wall, Jake pulls back on his arm, taking him down with a Short Arm Clothesline. With Drake Russell flat on his back, Jake wipes his hands while looking down at his handiwork. A worried Lisa
Doyle again tries to pull Morgan away, getting his attention as she yanks on his arm.)
Didn’t I already tell you ... you don’t want to be here ... (The smiling Morgan lurches forward into Doyle, knocking her over. Losing his balance, he pitches forward, tripping up as he tumbles down the stairs from the blow delivered from behind ... a Super Kick from a very bloody Drake Russell.
Before things get further out of hand, Russell is swarmed over by the Security. Spitting down the stairs, Russell mutters as he goes limp in the arms of the
Security force.)
Drake Russell: Let that be a lesson to you ... (The camera switches angles, zooming in on an unmoving Jake Morgan lying at the foot of the staircase as large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession.)
.... 5 ... .... 4 ... .... 3 ... .... 2 ... .... 1 ... (White Zombie's "More Human than Human" begins as flashes of the PAW stars in action are shown. Seth ... Skip Westervelt ... Drake Russell ... Jason Flint
... Jake Morgan and finally the PAW Television Champion, Hiroshima Nagasaki, standing in the middle of the PAW ring, the belt raised over his head.)
After an explosion, the scene fades to black and the PAW logo disappears before fading back in and panning the fans and their signs held aloft such as "Let that be a lesson to you!", "Mean Darlene is extreme!" and "Don’t step on the
Heartbreakers".
After making it’s way through the boisterious crowd of some 50 or so, the camera settles in on a laughing Aaron Starr and Jason Flint,standing in the black and orange Halloween decorated studio in front of the PAW banner.)
Aaron Starr: Welcome to our Halloween edition of Public Access Wrestling! (Starr turns to his cohost, Jason Flint.) Is it fun yet? Jason Flint: I’m not sure “fun” is the operative word here Aaron because there are still some days left on my wrestling suspension ... but I will say tonight might be downright spooky ... what with those fun-loving Heartbreakers taking on “Mr. Conversation” Jake Morgan and the “Hardcore Kid”, the Trashman ... not to mention our Main Event match as “Zero” Nagasaki defends his Television Title against ...
Aaron Starr: “Mr. Density”, Drake Russell ... Jason Flint: Right ... Aaron Starr: Speaking of our TV Champion ... (The camera moves to Hiro Nagasaki coming out from the back led by manager
Lonny Nakamura. The two walk right up with the Champion narrowing his eyes and eyeballing Flint.
Nagasaki says something in Japanese to Nakamura who nods his head and motions for one of the mics. Getting the mic, Nagasaki says something in Japanese then nods his head.)
Lonny Nakamura: Your Champion says that tonight he will show you all what happens to those who badmouth him ... tonight’s lesson will be taken out on the challenger, Drake Russell ...
(Starr looks angered while the fans boo loudly.) Aaron Starr: What’s going on here? You know perfectly well that he speaks
English ... (Nakamura holds up a hand cutting Starr off before Nagasaki rattles off another barrage of Japanese.)
Lonny Nakamura: Your Champion says that each week one of your so-called challengers steps into the ring, he will used as an example until such a time that he, Hiroshima Nagasaki, is treated fairly and recognized as the True
Champion that he is ...
No longer will he allow this promotion ... it’s owners ... it’s wrestlers ... or it’s announcers to continue to treat him as has been done in the past ... It is far past time for him to take matters into his own hands ... (Flint smirks as the emotionless Nagasaki stands there before saying something to Nakamura, pointing at Flint.)
... and if he must ... punishment will be handed out ... to wrestler and announcer alike ...
(Flint rolled his eyes as the boos continued from the crowd, growing louder in volume.) Is that understood? Aaron Starr: Listen here ... no one is afraid of your threats ... you’re standing in the presence of the former Texas Heavyweight Champion, who held it longer than anyone else here ...
If I were you ... I’d be the one worrying ... (Starr turned back to Flint shaking his head in disbelief. Nakamura stepped out of the way as Starr turned to continue.)
... and another thing ... (Nagasaki opened his hands and unleashed a Fireball in the direction of the hosts of the show ... Flint got his hands up in front of his face but Starr took the brillant flash flush in the face, dropping to the floor and screaming in pain.
The room went silent as Starr screamed out in pain, writhing on the floor. Flint went to Starr’s aid while Nagasaki laughed hysterically as Security arrived on the scene to keep further violence from happening. Nakamura led
Nagasaki away as the camera made it’s way into the back where Jake Morgan was being rolled to the back of the building flat on his back with his neck in a harness to hold it straight.
Following behind and held by paramedics was a heavily bandaged Trashman and a worried Mean Darlene. Carefully but quickly, the paramedics rushed to the outside of the studio where an ambulance awaited. Putting him into the back,
Trashman and Mean Darlene climbed in. With the doors closed, the ambulance rushed off into the night.
The camera moved back inside where Drake Russell was being bandaged and checked over paramedics while PAW Security watched. Standing in the background was Lisa
Doyle, tears streaming down her face.
A female paramedic shined a light in Russell’s eye while talking to him.) Paramedic: I think it’s safe to say that you’re finished for the night. We need to get you to the hospital and stitch you up ... you’re bleeding pretty badly ...
(Russell shook his head as he pushed himself to his feet, wobbling badly before steadying himself and walking off.)
Drake Russell: I’ve got some business to take care of first ... (Russell stormed off as the camera left him to find the PAW’s new owner in her
Offspring mask sitting behind her desk and looking out at her “Girl Friday”
Kimberly Davis.)
Woman in the Offspring mask: Any calls? (Davis shook her head at the question.) ... and you alerted Security? (Davis nodded her head.) Kimberly Davis: Stop worrying ... it was probably only a prank. Besides, we are so off the beaten path ... what could he possibly want coming here to wrestle?
None of our people have ever faced him before ... so it’s highly unlikely he’s after someone ...
Woman in the Offspring mask: I know but that’s what worries me ... they don’t call him LOCO for nothing ...
(Davis shrugged her shoulders as the camera left her for the ring where a lone
Jason Flint stood in the ring with the Heartbreakers on the apron. Flint tapped his mic.)
Jason Flint: Ladies and gentlemen ... due to an injury backstage, Jake Morgan and the Trashman are unable to compete tonight against the Heartbreakers ...
(A loud chorus of boos rolled into the ring followed by several objects as Paco wiggled his hips for his “adoring fans”. A loud voice boomed over the PA system getting everyone’s attention.)
Voice: Waaaaaaaaaaaaait ... onnnnnnnnnnnnnnnne ... second! (All eyes turned to see the Lone Wolves, Skip Westervelt and Chip emerge from the back and make their way towards the ring.)
These people came here tonight to see one royal asskicking ... (The booing turned to cheers as the pair approached the ring.) ... and ... far be it for us, the Lone Wolves to give them just that! Morgan and the “Hardcore Kid” are not the only two men who willing ... and able ...
(Skip chuckled as he looked to Chip.) Well ... in this case, maybe they’re not able ... but rest assured the Lone
Wolves are both willing and able to kick ourselves some ass ...
(Finishing his sentence, both Skip and Chip were knocked to the floor as the
Heartbreakers came flying over the ropes with Springboard Planchas. With a brawl started on the outside of the ring, referee Peter Daye called for the bell and an impromtu tag match was underway.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** The Heartbreakers were suddenly on the defensive as the bigger Lone Wolves came back firing Punches and Double Drop Kicks on the floor. Both members of the
Heartbreakers were introduced to the ring barrier before the match went back inside the ring.
Like a well oiled machine not seen in the past, the Lone Wolves looked like championship material as they took the offensive, keeping the Heartbreakers off their game with fast tags and crisp double teaming.
Paco took a big Powerbomb from Chip which was followed by a Leg Drop off the top from his partner. Chip Splashed and hooked a leg for a near pinfall which was broken up as Seth charged in with a Drop Kick.
With Seth pushed from the ring, the Lone Wolves manhandled Paco with a Double
Suplex followed by a Double Elbow Drop off the ropes. Skip rolled him up for the pin but Daye called for a break as Chip was the legal man in.
Paco was able to make a tag while Skip argued with the ref. Bouncing off the ropes, Seth rushed in with a Shoulder Butt that didn’t budge the big man.
Wanting more, Chip motioned to his opponent to try again. Bouncing off the ropes again, Seth built up a head of steam and came in again and again, it had the same effect ... none.
Telling Seth to bounce off the ropes several times, Chip stood in the center of the ring with his hands behind his back. Back and forth, Seth criss-crossed the ring and with a full head of steam, dropped his shoulder a third time ... this time however, it was Chip who sent Seth flying, flexing his big belly.
Stumbling backwards, Seth hit the mat but rolled gracefully to his feet, bouncing off the mat. The only problem was that he got just a little too close to his opponent’s corner, taking a boot to the back as Skip swung his leg up.
Stumbling forward, Seth landed in the arms of Chip who wrapped him up and swung around, planting him into the canvas with a big Belly-to-Belly Suplex. With
Seth laid out, Chip made a tag to his partner who mounted the ropes.
Pausing for a few seconds, Skip leapt from the top and landed a Somersault
Senton. Paco hit the ring but a Diving Clothesline from Chip took care of him.
With a handful of tights, Skip rolled Seth up for the win. *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (The Lone Wolves left the ring with their arms raised to the fans’ cheering while Seth picked himself off the mat and complained about his sequined tights being held. Daye waved him off as the cameras made their way into the back to find Drake Russell, his head bandaged, making his way through the halls of the backstage area.
Not looking too well, Russell stumbled several times, catching himself and leaning against the wall until steadying himself. before continuing. Hearing his name, he stopped to see Lisa Doyle following him ... a distraught Lisa
Doyle.)
Lisa Doyle: You can’t fight Nagasaki ... you might have a concussion ... (Putting a finger to her lips, he quieted her.) Drake Russell: I have to ... (Turning, he started off on his way, leaving Doyle alone. The camera found
Kimberly Davis walking with the two men in black suits from last week in the bowels of the studio.)
Kimberly Davis: I’d like to apologize for the boss for not taking you to talk to the wrestlers ... she’s not herself this week. It’s this LOCO guy who has her on edge.
(The two men look at each other for a second, their eyes meeting from behind their mirrored shades.)
Man in suit #1: LOCO? (Davis stopped and nodded her head.) Kimberly Davis: Yes? Man in suit #2: Are we talking about the wrestler who was kicked out of the NWC some time ago? That LOCO?
Kimberly Davis: LOCO ... yes, that LOCO ... (The camera made it’s way back to the ring when the loud Kodo drums, followed by an equally loud gong announced the arrival of the PAW Television Champion and manager Lonny Nakamura.
Making their way into the ring, Nagasaki raised a threatening hand to the referee who backed away as he issued a warning. Waiting for the boos to die down, Nakamura called for a mic which was brought to him.)
Lonny Nakamura: It seems that tonight’s unfortunate opponent ... or should I say victim ... will not be with us this evening ...
It seems that he was already unfortunately disposed of by the equally disgusting and inept Jake Morgan who took it upon himself to keep all of you people from seeing a lesson in technical wrestling as the Champion dismantled yet another American challenger ... a rather poor excuse for a challenger, I might add ...
(Nagasaki laughed as the booing grew ever louder.) I suppose as is customary ... management will send someone out to fill Drake
Russell’s shoes in order to entertain you sitting here as well as those at home who have tuned in ... someone who will be just as easily be disposed of.
So send out the next victim ... whether it’s the toughest person in the back you can find ... or your so-called poor excuse of an announcer and former
Champion ... we don’t care who it is ...
(There was a loud cheer that went through the studio as one man stepped out from the back and made his way slowly to the ring ... or rather, shuffled towards the ring, his head bandaged from the earlier brutal attack ... Drake
Russell.
Climbing up onto the ring apron, Russell asked for a mic of his own.) Drake Russell: Don’t be so quick to bring someone else into our match before we’ve gone round and round, Nagasaki ...
You’ve got something that belongs to me and after tonight will be where it rightfully belongs ... right after I teach you a lesson that’s been long in coming!
(Throwing down the mic, Russell fell victim to a Drop Kick that knocked him off the ring apron to the floor. Daye called for the start of the match as Lonny
Nakamura fled for the safety outside the ring.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Waiting for Russell to get back to his feet, Nagasaki ran across the ring and exited with a Baseball Slide. He slammed in Russell and knocked him hard back into barrier.
Grabbing an arm, Nagasaki sent him headlong into the barrier on the other side of the ring. Charging in behind, Nagasaki jumped up onto the ring steps and launched himself off with a Somersault Body Press that sent both men sprawling onto the floor.
After pulling the bloody bandages from Drake’s head, Nagasaki bit into his forehead, busting him open again as the blood began to flow. Wiping the blood from his lips, the Champion sneered at the booing crowd before rolling Russell back into the ring.
Making a quick cover, Nagasaki was pushed off just before the ref’s hand hit the mat for the final time. On the outside of the ring, Nakamura went back and forth with the hostile fans while also offering words of encouragement.
Pulling Russell to his feet, Nagasaki lifted him into the air but found himself empty handed as his opponent slid off. Wrapping his arms around Nagasaki’s waist, Russell stunned him with a Release German Suplex. Nearly passing out from the loss of blood, Russell staggered across the ring and dropped down for a pin attempt. A feeble attempt at best as Nagasaki kicked out on a 1 count.
After locking up in the center of the ring, Nagasaki gained control with a Side
Head Lock before opening up with Right Hands. With Russell bent at the waist,
Nagasaki grabbed him around the middle and dumped him with a Salto Suplex. A
Leg Drop followed into a lazy cover which was kicked out of on a 2 count.
Bouncing off the ropes, Nagasaki came back in with another Leg Drop but this one missed entirely as Russell rolled to the outside of the ring. Nagasaki’s
Hurricanrana took both men to the floor. Daye stood over both men and started a count until Nagasaki returned to his feet.
Lifting Russell into the air, Nagasaki dropped him down throatfirst across the barrier. Hitting the floor, Russell gripped his throat in pain as he rolled around. Nagasaki taunted the fan as he pointed down at Drake Russell before trying to Stomp him into submission.
Laying Russell across his knee, Nagasaki tried to break his back but let go of the hold as Daye moved in. Obeying the ref, Nagasaki took the match back into the ring where he inflicted further damage. A Dragon Screw Leg Drag, Japanese
Arm Drags, a Flying Back Elbow and a Fireman’s Carry into an Arm Bar all whittled away at the challenger but at no time did Drake Russell submit or give up.
Beaten and bloody, no one would have faulted Drake Russell for quitting but that wasn’t the case as he started to come back, catching Nagasaki with a Head
Butt. A Drop Kick was followed by a high arcing Moonsault which prompted Lonny
Nakamura up onto the ring apron to complain.
Grabbing Nakamura by his jacket, Russell dumped into the ring the hard way. With a raised and threatening fist, he let the pleading manager have it between the eyes. Grabbing Nakamura, Russell pulled him to the center of the ring when the fans erupted. Setting Nagasaki’s manager up for a Double Underhook Back
Breaker, Drake Russell never saw him enter the ring ... he didn’t see Jake
Morgan standing there wielding the chair ... until it was too late.
The chair bounced sharply off of Russell’s bloody head making a sickening sound that had the fans cringing. Throwing down the chair, an angered Morgan left just as quickly as he arrived.
Looking around the ring before kicking the chair to the outside, Nagasaki slowly ran his thumb across his throat before lifting the limp Russell off his feet, planting him with the Nagasaki Driver but rather than going for the pin left the ring, grabbing up both his manager and title belt before disgustedly leaving for the back.
There was a barrage of objects from the disapproving fans that pelted the
Champion as he guided Nakamura to the back. With no alternative, Peter Daye called for the bell, ending the match.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (With the loud booing fading off, the camera found Kimberly Davis with the two men.) Kimberly Davis: Okay gentlemen, I don’t know who you want to start with but there’s Emilio Ramirez, one of our luchadores ...
(She pointed towards the wrestler as he obliviously walked down the hall. stopping only when he heard his name being shouted. Turning, he froze when he saw the party approach.)
Gentlemen, this is Emilio Ramirez ... (She turned to Ramirez who hadn’t moved a muscle with the exception of his eyes that were wide with terror.)
... they just want to ask you a few questions ... Man in suit #1: Hello Emilio ... we’re with the Ndub ... (Ramirez’ eyes darted from one to the other.) Emilio Ramirez: The INS!!!!! (Kimberly Davis and the two men questioningly looked at one another as Ramirez suddenly ran off just as the credits began to roll bringing the program to a close.)
