7.16 Card

16 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera opens on a stack of games ... Jingo, Yahtzee, Backgammon, Chess and several others sitting in their boxes, set upon a table. An arm comes in and sweeps them up as the camera pulls back to find the owner, Chip. Turning, he starts to leave the room when the door opens and tag partner and self-proclaimed “Lone Wolf” Skip Westervelt steps inside.)
Skip Westervelt: Chip ... you ready? We got a match ... (Seeing Chip standing there with the games, he throws his arms into the air.) What’s all this? Chip: Hart said it’s game time ... so, I brought my favorite games! (Slapping his forehead, the camera fades out on his last words.) Skip Westervelt: Oh brother! (Leaving for the outside, the camera focusses in on the arrival of Jason Flint. The young man is dressed in black khaki cargo pants and dark gray T-shirt with his trusty backpack in tow and his Singapore Cane in his hand. Flint's bandages are gone, replaced by a couple of residual stitches.
A security guard eyeballs Flint, as Flint goes in, giving the guard the finger. Inside the UNT personnel scamper around, getting the last of the remaining preparations for the show done. And in the middle of the pandemonium, stands the man-monster, Khan.
Khan stood over the television crew like a vulture, looking for its next meal. Pausing, Flint waits for his moment to strike but stops short as the big man turns and sees his opponent for the evening. Khan isn’t the only one who sees
Flint enter as Shane leads several of the PAW Security into position between the two.
Flint backs off, pointing at Khan, who growls at Flint. Flint sneers back at him before disappearing off into the bowels of the building before large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession.)
.... 5 ... .... 4 ... .... 3 ... .... 2 ... .... 1 ... (White Zombie's "More Human than Human" begins as flashes of the PAW stars are shown. Skip Westervelt ... Johnny Green ... Justin Hart ... Drake Russell ...
Jason Flint ... and finally the PAW Texas Heavyweight Champion, Jake Morgan, standing in the middle of the PAW ring, the belt raised over his head.)
After an explosion, the scene fades to black and the PAW logo disappears before fading in on host Aaron Starr standing in front of the PAW banner off to the side of the television studio set.)
Aaron Starr: Greetings wrestling fans for another action packed evening of
Public Access Wrestling! Last week’s big card looks to be topped this week as the re-instated Tag Team Titles are up for grabs as the former two time Tag
Champion Jason Flint battles alongside Pep Preston against the Texas
Heavyweight Champion, Jake Morgan and the man-monster Khan! With several other matches before the Main Event ... it looks to be another hot
Texas evening full of wrestling ...
(Starr stops as he looks off.) It looks like someone is getting into the ring even as we speak ... (Shifting to the ring, an older man is being followed by a younger one. PAW regulars would recognize the pair as a Mr. Gibson and the young man Jason Flint beat with his Singapore Cane two weeks ago. Inside, the older one pulls out a mic.)
Mr. Gibson: Hello everyone ... my name is Mr. Fred Gibson ... and though you might not know me, I’m the head on the UNT RTVF Department ... the very people who responsible for producing this program for you ...
Standing with me is student and the producer/director of this very program ... the very same person who was beaten savagely just two weeks ago ... (Gibson stopped as Perris strolled out from the back and entered the ring.) And ... as of this moment, there has been no suspensions ... no punishment ... no accountablity for the actions of one wrestler ... Now ... when this local promotion was in trouble of going under, the University of North Texas has stepped in to help a local production ... we’ve been gracious enough to step in and give you a place to wrestle ... right here in this buildling on campus that serves as your television studio ... we’ve helped with the production of the telecasts as well as alleviated some of the costs by donating our time and manpower ... and how are we repayed?
(Gibson looked over to Perris before continuing.) I’ll tell you how ... by brutally attacking my students! Mr. Perris, I have yet to hear one iota of regret or an apology to Mr. Dover here ...
(Perris stood there nodding his head in agreement.) Perris: You’re right ... Fred ... can I call you Fred? (Gibson nodded his head up and down.) Without your help, Public Access Wrestling might have been forced to close it’s doors, so on behalf of my staff and employees, we here at Public Access
Wrestling with to extend our humblest apologies to you ... to the University
... and to Be ...
(The fans rumbled as Jason Flint ran out from the back and slid into the ring. Seeing Flint bouncing to his feet, Gibson shrieked like a little girl as he ran behind Perris. Turning, Dover’s face went white as he looked into the manical grin of Jason Flint who raised his weapon before bringing it down and cracking it across his target’s head.
PAW Security rushed out from the back but as they neared the ring, Flint threatened both Perris and Gibson, stopping them in their tracks. Only after getting a mic did Flint step back and relax.)
Jason Flint: You know, Perris ... the only one to blame here is you ... you’re the one who started this vendetta against me ... what would you call it ...
your little “Lesson in Humility”? You started it but I promise you, I’m going to finish it ... no matter how far down you kick me and no matter how many times you have your big thug, Khan attack me ...
(Behind Perris, Gibson was clutching at the President as he tried to remain out of harm’s way.)
You’ve been the one sending your boys out to get me or have done your best to stack the odds against me ... over the last few weeks, it’s been you who’s had the upper hand and let me be the first to say that not many people would be able to be here standing up to you now ...
Like I’ve said before ... I’ve been able to overcome whatever you’ve thrown at me ... I’ve even enjoyed it ... but I’ve been doing some thinking this past week ... I think it’s time to turn this around ... it’s time that you were taught a lesson ...
(Lifting his Cane quickly, Flint got another shriek from Gibson who pulled
Perris closer to him in the corner where he cowered in fear.)
So ... today, I’ve decided to that’s it time to show you what a lesson is ... in fact, three lessons ... (Perris was confused as he watched Flint walk around the ring, thinking.) You like ... movies? Ever see “A Christmas Carol”? You know the one where
Scrooge is visited by three ghosts?
Well ... I don’t have ghosts for you but you will be visited three times today by the three lessons ... consider this your “Lesson of the PAW’s Future”!
(After threatening to hit Perris, Flint stepped through the ropes and made his way to the back, keeping the Security at bay with his weapon. Leaving the ring, the camera found a pacing Television Champion, Drake Russell in his private dressing room.
As he went back and forth, he failed to notice PAW Personality Lisa Doyle standing in the doorway in a seductive pose until she cleared her throat.)
Lisa Doyle: Helllllllllloooooooo ... Champ ... (With a look of concern and disinterest, he turned towards her as she pursed her lips and blew a kiss in his direction.)
Drake Russell: Not now Lisa ... I’m busy ... (With his foot, he closed the door on her just before the camera moved back to the ring where Aaron Starr waited with mic in hand.)
Aaron Starr: Ladies and gentlemen ... new to Public Access Wrestling ... Sky
King!!!!! (The fans were largely quiet until Skip Westervelt with Chip in tow emerged from the back and made their way into the ring. The two wrestlers flanked Starr and smiled at him when Skip grabbed away the mic.)

(Shaking his head in disgust, Starr grabbed the mic back.) Aaron Starr: Ladies and gentlemen ... new to Public Access Wrestling ... Sky
