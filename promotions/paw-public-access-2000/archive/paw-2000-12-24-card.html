PAW — Card Archive

12.24 Card

24 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 3 —

(The festively decorated studio in it’s Christmasy red and white lights up with noise as the red and white clad Aaron Starr and cohost Lisa Doyle walk out from the back and circle the ring.
Both in red and white matching sweaters, slap hands as they go, Lisa hanging back, handing out small presents to those in the first couple of rows before entering the ring.)
Aaron Starr: T’was the night before Christmas ... and all through the house ... not a creature was stirring ... not even that Nagasaki louse! (Starr smiles at the responsive laughter from the packed house of some 50 or so PAW fans without a better way to spend their Christmas Eve.) Glad you could all join us this night before Christmas, folks ... and joining me before we begin our television taping is the lovely Lisa Doyle!
(Starr turns to his cohost.) Have you been a good girl this year, Lisa? Lisa Doyle: Why don’t you ask me the day after tomorrow, Aaron? I can answer that AFTER Santa comes to visit ...
Aaron Starr: Alright, Lisa ... what do we have on tap today for those persons who joined us in person?
Lisa Doyle: A little wrestling action as Drake Russell takes on Pep Preston. Aaron Starr: I bet you can’t wait to see that one, Lisa. Lisa Doyle: I’d be willing to bet that alot of people are looking forward to this match, Aaron as the PAW goes to war against the team from Japan yet again.
Pep might be from here but he’s taking money from Lonny Nakamura ... and that makes him one of “them”.
Aaron Starr: One of “them”, Lisa? (Starr chuckles as the fans cheer loudly with the Trashman stepping out from the back and making his way to the ring.)
Looks like we’re about to be joined by the Trashman ... welcome Trashman and congratulations on your Number One Ranking ... marking you as the guy next in line to face off against TV Champ Hiroshima Nagasaki.
Trashman: Thanks, Aaron ... Lisa ... Lias Doyle: You must be excited about getting a shot at Nagasaki and the Television Title? Trashman: You know, Lisa ... I’ve had a taste of the gold before when I was one half of the Tag Champions with the Offspring, so the belt’s not the thing for me.
What I’m more interested in doing is a little “exterminating”. You see, we’ve been infested with these bugs ... it started with one and it’s grown. What I aim to do is start by stomping out these pesky insects one by one until we’ve made it all the way to the “queen” ...
Aaron Starr: Bugs? Trashman: That’s right, Aaron ... bugs. Nasty little bugs that need to be ground up under our shoes.
(Laughter quickly changes to a chorus of boos as Lonny Nakamura walks out from the back and gets into the ring.)
Lonny Nakamura: You can insult all you want. Say these funny things ... get these ignorant people to laugh at your jokes but the truth of it all is that without YOUR Champion, Hiro Nagasaki ... there is no Public Access Wrestling.
(The boos grow louder by the second as Trashman eyeballs the manager.) No one in this promotion has captured YOUR imagination as Hiroshima has. No one has carried this promotion on his back like Hiroshima Nagasaki ... and you,
Trashman, would be wise to speak better of those who are far superior to anything you have ever done inside the squared circle ...
Aaron Starr: Now hold on there, Lonny ... the Trashman is a former Tag Team Titleholder with his partner the Offspring ... Lonny Nakamura: Ah yes, another of your second-rate saviors ... yet another man who would have fallen victim to Hiroshima Nagasaki’s superior wrestling skills.
(Lisa Doyle steps inbetween Trashman and Nakamura.) Trashman: Say what you want, Nakamura but remember this ... one of these days ... today ... tomorrow ... next week ... maybe even next month, sometime down that road your boy Nagasaki and I are going to mix it up inside this ring ...
and when that happens, your Champion is going to be one very unhappy boy ... (Shoving Nakamura back, Trash leaves the ring and slaps hands with several fans before disappearing into the back followed by Nakamura seconds later with booing on his heels before he, too is gone from sight.)
Aaron Starr: The Trashman with a warning for Nagasaki and the rest of Nakamura’s “Team Japan” ... wait here he is, Lisa ... Drake Russell ... (The fans cheer as Russell steps out from the back and slides into the ring after slapping a few high fives. Stepping inbetween Starr and Doyle, the smiling Russell waves to the cheering crowd.)
Drake ... big match tonight. Pep Preston is probably considered the weak link in Team Japan’s armor so to speak ... but you and I both know that Pep is a seasoned veteran ...
(The handsome Russell turns to Starr and smiles as he removes his mirrored shades. Folding them up, he slides them into the inside pocket of his long black coat, pulling it back just enough to show the few ladies in the small audience his bare chest.)
Drake Russell: Aaron ... I don’t want to talk about Pep or any one of his “associates” and I put the emphasis on “ass” ... (The fans howl their approval.) What I do want to talk about is a little tradition in the Russell household about this time of year.
(Digging into his pocket, Russell pulls out a small sprig of Mistletoe and holds it over his head.)
You know this tradition, Aaron? Aaron Starr: That I do, Drake ... and it looks like some of the women in our studio audience would just love to help you out ...
(Starr and Russell smile at the cheers from the women moving to the ring barrier to get close.)
Drake Russell: Sorry ladies ... (Grabbing Lisa Doyle, Russell pulls her close and dips her as he plants one on her. A few whistles from the fans while in the embrace changes to boos as Pep
Preston rushes out from the back and hits the ring, coming up fighting. With a Double Axe Handle to Russell’s back, dropping him to the mat, Starr and Doyle flee the ring while a thin Japanese man in a ref’s shirt calls for the bell.) *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Kicking Russell to the stomach, Pep moved him around the ring before coming down with an Elbow. With Drake writing in pain, Pep’s up on his feet taunting the fans then his opponent.
Pulling him to his feet, Pep’s Body Slam and Knee Drop are followed by a pin attempt and a 2 count from the ref. An Eye Gauge leads to being pushed into the corner where Pep opens up with Reverse Knife Hands and a Knee to the Stomach.
Pulled to his feet in an Arm Bar, Drake takes a Knee to the Stomach before sent across the ring where he ducks under a thrown Punch. Bouncing off the back ropes, Drake shoots forward into a Flying Cross Body Block, taking Pep to the mat. Hooking a leg, he gets a 2 count from the ref before being pushed off.
Working quickly, Russell’s Punches rock Preston before Whipping him across the ring. On the return, Pep is put down with a Sidewalk Slam. Using the ropes,
Russell criss-crosses the ring before jumping off the middle rope with a Springboard Moonsault. The cheers of the fans are not unheard despite their small numbers. Jumping up onto the ropes, Russell acknowledges their cheers before hopping up into the air and coming down with a rope assisted Knee Drop. The Japanese referee is in with a warning.
Climbing the ropes to the top and turning back to his opponent, Russell launches himself off with a Missile Drop Kick, wiping out Preston as he turns back towards Drake. The ref nearly slaps out a near pinfall.
Disgusted, Russell argues with the ref over a slow count, slapping his own hands trying to teach the new referee how to count to three. Shaking his head,
Russell turns back to Preston taking a Punch to the stomach. Whipped into the ropes, Russell catches himself while Preston dips his head for a Back Body
Drop. Not going any further, Drake kicks forward catching Pep in the nose and putting him down on the the seat of his pants.
With Pep on the mat, Drake’s Drop Kick to the face and Belly-to-Belly Suplex set him up for a Moonsault off the top and cover for the near pin.
Whipping Pep into the ropes, Drake took him down on the return with a Jump Spinning Back Kick. Rolling from the ring, Drake took a breather as he circles the ring and slaps some hands before re-entering.
Pulling Pep up by his hair, Drake caught a Back Elbow and a Thumb to the eye as Preston worked himself free. Grabbing Russell’s hair, Pep slammed his face into the turnbuckle before raking his eyes along the top rope.
Boos were followed by objects thrown into the ring by the small by very boisterious crowd that was now on it’s feet. Pep taunted them as Security moved into place while Drake was on one knee trying to clear his eyes.
Preston was still jabbering away at the crowd while pulling Russell to his feet before taking a Knee to the gut. A well thrown Punch knocked Pep back into the ropes and right into the turning Drake’s arms as he shot forward. Dropping to the mat, Drake Russell took out Pep Preston with a Stunner.
His eyes still bothering him, Drake pulled Pep to his feet and into a Hammerlock, lifting the man off his feet then falling back with a Suplex. Pep was like putty as he was pulled to his feet for a Double Underhook Back
Breaker. Hooking a leg, Drake Russell took the win with the fans counting right alongside the referee slapping the mat.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Drake started to climb the corner ropes as the fans howled for Pep’s blood. Balancing himself at top, Drake was joined in the ring by the Black Ravens as they dashed out from the back.
Shaking the ropes, the threw Russell from the top before he could nail Preston with the Total Decimation. Russell was down and covered in an instant as the
Ravens started a “Boot Fest” on the downed wrestler. Helpless, Russell was kicked around the ring as the booing continued, growing in volume with Hiroshima Nagasaki stepping out from the back and walking slowly to the ring. After taunting fans, the hysterical Nagasaki was up on the ring apron when the Trashman and Justin Hart rush out from the back, sending the
Japanese wrestlers fleeing. With the ring clear, Aaron Starr addresses the cheering fans.) Aaron Starr: ... don’t you go away!!!!

(The camera opens on Oklahoma’s Brutal Brian Floyd walking into the PAW television studio out of the equally brutal cold. Stepping inside, Floyd looks around with a determined look on his face before heading off to his destination.
Following Floyd on his way, the camera swings around to catch manager Lonny Nakamura stepping out with a smile on his face. Stopping in his tracks, Floyd stands to his full six foot four.)
Lonny Nakamura: Ah ... just the man I was looking for ... (Floyd stood unblinking, waiting for Nakamura to say his piece.) I’d like to discuss ... business ... (Reaching into his jacket pocket, Nakamura produced a roll of bills. Floyd smiled as he looked from Nakamura’s hand back to his face.)
... and your match with Justin Hart ... Brian Floyd: Yeah? Lonny Nakamura: I want him taken out ... crippled ... put out of action for good ... (Floyd scratched his chin as he again looked at Nakamura’s hand clutching the rolled cash.)
Brian Floyd: One question ... why you wearing those sunglasses? (Nakamura’s hand shot to the black frames of his glasses, adjusting them.) Take them off. (Nakamura took half a step back, holding onto his glasses before slowly lowering them revealing the shiner given to him at Winter’s Nightmare two weeks ago by Devil Tsaruta. Floyd’s grin widened after whistling at Nakamura’s
“battle wound”.) Lonny Nakamura: Now about our deal ... (Before finishing his sentence, Floyd reared back and hit Nakamura, spinning him to the floor. Smiling down at his handiwork, Floyd removed his red OU jacket and covered Nakamura with before walking off laughing to himself.
Leaving Floyd, the camera found Justin Hart in his dressing room with only a dim light illuminating the room. Standing there in the middle of the floor, breathing heavily with perspiration running down his face. Rubbing it in, he slid his hand across the scratch on his forehead when there was a knock at the door.

Toadie: Dude ... Justin Hart: Good thing I was dressed ... what is it? Toadie: Uhh, your match is coming up in a few minutes ... (Hart gave out a sigh then waved for the man to go away.) Justin Hart: Fine, fine. Hit that switch on your way out. (The hand flicked on the light then closed the door behind him. Hart looked around to the mirror looking at himself. His eyes burning with intensity.)
Battle time, Floyd. Let's Rumble.... (With the camera moving back to the studio entrance, Jason Flint and Kimberly Davis are met by Mean Darlene and Jeremy Morgan.) Jason Flint: Look Darlene ... before you start with Jake’s concussion crap ... save it because lady, I don’t want to hear it ... Mean Darlene: But Jason ... (Putting a hand to his mouth, Flint’s “zipping” motion cuts her off as he walks off with Davis. The camera followed the head shaking Flint as he looked to
Davis.) Jason Flint: Can you believe her? I’m in a handicap match and she wants me to take it easy on Morgan ... sheeeeesh ...
(About to answer, Kimberly Davis stops with a surprised look from Flint who is looking down at the floor. Moving to see the object of his surprise, the camera focusses in on the prone form of Lonny Nakamura underneath the red and white OU jacket of Brian Floyd.
Laughing, Flint steps over Nakamura’s body as large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession...)
.... 5 .... .... 4 .... .... 3 .... .... 2 .... .... 1 .... (Brian Setzer's cover of "Jingle Bell Rock" begins as the PAW logo spins into place followed by flashes of Hiroshima Nagasaki in action are shown. Scene after scene quickly flash by of past battles ...

The “Street Soldier” Justin Hart takes a Double Thrust to the Throat and with a Snap Suplex, put into a pinning predicament ... Pep Preston is flat on his back as Nagasaki leaps from the top turnbuckle and lands the “H-Bomb” ...
In slow motion, Aaron Starr turns right into a ball of fire thrown into his face ... A battered Jason Flint with Nagasaki and turncoat Jake Morgan standing over him and finally the PAW Television Champion standing in the middle of the ring, the belt raised over his head.)

I can't think of ONE time in your life that you have beat me. Maybe in your dreams but never in real life you fucking Jackass. Not only do you have

(He stopped to wipe his face off as another beer landed close.) I seem to have made the wrong impression last week. All of you, from these simpleton fans ...
(More boos,and trash rain down.) ... to those morons in the back, seem to think that I was searching for approval of my actions. Let me make this clear ... I wasn't. I don't care what any of you think. I did what I did for me and not any of you. IF anyone in the back has a problem with this ...
(Chants of " You Sold Out! " rose from the crowd.) ... then they can step out here and do something about it. (He waited for a few seconds, then begins to speak again.) That's what I thought. Bunch of chicken shi ... (Dollybraid’s “Hero” started up over the PA as Jason Flint rushed out from the back and hit the ring. Sliding in under the ropes, Flint popped up to his feet ready for action as he and Morgan came to blows.
With everyone on their feet cheering for Flint as the two slugged it out, Morgan’s partner Hiroshima Nagasaki and a Japanese man in a ref’s shirt made their way to the ring.
As Nagasaki leveled Flint with a shot from his TV belt, the referee called for the bell.) *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Pulling Flint to his feet, they both grabbed an arm into a Double Arm Bar. Twisting, they put Flint down on a knee as the crowd roared it’s support for the former Champion.
Shouting out in pain, Flint forced himself back to his feet before flipping backwards. Jumping, he broke the hold with a Double Drop Kick that caught both of his opponents, knocking them to the canvas. Both men rolled to the outside of the ring to regroup when Flint launched himself out of the ring with a Tope
Con Hilo, sending all three to the floor in a heap as the fans cheered their approval. Flint slowly rose to his feet first and was confronted by “Junior” Akatani with a stern warning. Shoving him back, Flint started in on Nagasaki with the boots until Morgan dropped him with a solid shot to the back.
Holding Flint, Morgan gave Nagasaki a perfect opening for his stiff Reverse Knife Hands to the chest. Flint’s chest reddened with each blow which brought on a barrage of objects from the fans as Security moved in to make sure that they remained of their side of the barricade. After a Double Thrust to the throat, Nagasaki turned on the fans and threatened several in front row, inviting them to step across the barrier.
With his partner busy, Morgan threw a chair down on the floor before DDT’ing Flint down onto it. Flint’s body twitched several times before he was pulled back to his feet, his head a bloody mess after being busted open on the chair.
Holding Flint’s head, Morgan corkscrewed his knuckle into the wound then holding up a bloody fist to the booing fans.
With more chants of “Sellout!”, Morgan left Flint for his partner while he tried to get them to shut up which only seemed to egg them on.
After a Snap Suplex onto the chair, Nagasaki sat down into a Leg Drop and laughed at the fans who continued their jeering.
Things look bad as Nagasaki and Morgan take turns pounding him into the ring barrier several times while the referee stands back and watches the action.
With Morgan setting up a table on the floor, Nagasaki rammed a knee repeatedly into Flint’s face as he slumped back against the barrier. Taking several Chops to the head, Flint looked out for the count as Nagasaki laughed in his face.
Summoning strength from somewhere, Flint took Nagasaki down with a Low Blow. Pulling himself to his feet, Flint ran towards Morgan who had his back to the action. Jumping into the air, Flint grabbed Morgan’s head from behind and slammed his face into the table with a Bulldog, shattering it.
Grabbing Nagasaki and tossing him into the ring, Flint Whipped him across the ring and nailed him with a Roundhouse Kick.
Pulling Nagasaki back to his feet, Flint again Whipped him into the far ropes, this time Leap Frogging but went to the well once too often and was caught by his opponent. Throwing his body backwards, Flint took Nagasaki down with a
Victory Roll, getting a near pinfall out of it. Climbing the ropes, Flint came off with a Split Legged Moonsault, hooking a leg for the pin but Morgan’s Elbow Drop broke up the 2 count.
Pulled to his feet, Morgan sent Flint across the ring, ducking down as he returned only to have Flint roll over his back. Landing on his feet, Flint was ready for action but ran into a stiff Super Kick that took him off his feet.
A limp Jason Flint was put down with a Russian Leg Sweep before being pinned to the mat. The ref slapped quickly but came up as Flint barely got his shoulder up.
A Nagasaki Driver and Morgan’s Cobra Clutch figured to be the end of the match but the profusely bleeding Flint struggled forward towards the ropes, forcing a break.
Morgan drove Flint to his knees with a shot to the back before they sent him crashing to the outside with a Double Clothesline.
The pair joined him on the outside to pummel him to the heavy booing of the fans. After Nagasaki’s Belly-to-Belly Suplex onto the floor, Morgan rolled both him and a table into the ring.
While Nagasaki set it up, Morgan continued to pepper Flint’s bloody face with blows in the corner as the ref stood back. With a cue from his partner, Morgan lifted Flint up and spun around to blast him through the table with a
Spinebuster before Nagasaki leapt off the and hit the H-Bomb Fist Drop from the top. Morgan made the cover but pulled Flint’s head up just before the ref’s hand hit the mat a final time. The boos continued as Morgan Snapmared Flint and Nagasaki
Stomped the downed man. The booing continued until Jeremy Morgan stormed out from the back despite a protesting Mean Darlene following on his heels. Sliding into the ring, the younger Morgan went right after Nagasaki, driving him from the ring with several Right Hands that caught him by surprise.
On the outside of the ring, Jeremy kept the pressure up, hammering Nagasaki into the ring barrier and not letting up. Turning his attention to the actions of his younger brother, Jake Morgan left Flint on the mat.
Staggering forward, Flint let Morgan have it with a Double Axe Handle before spinning him around for a Tilt-A-Whirl Slam.
With Team Japan reeling, the cheering grew to deafening proportions inside the small studio. With Jeremy Morgan taking care of himself, Flint left the ring and staggered to the outside where he liberated a chair from a fan. Climbing the ropes, Flint pumped his arm into the air ready for an Arabian Facebuster.
Before he could launch himself into the ring, Mean Darlene reached in and pulled Jake to the outside. Helping him to his feet, Darlene pleaded with
Morgan to come with her. Shoving her to the floor gave Flint all the room he needed as he launched himself off the ropes and came down, slamming the chair into Morgan’s head. Both men were out on the floor while on the other side of the ring, Nagasaki gained the upper hand with an Eye Rake and a Back Elbow.
Puching Jeremy into the ring, the TV Champ locked on the Unconditional Surrender. The younger Morgan’s arms flailed away trying to get free but it was too late as “Junior” Akatani called for the bell bringing this match to an end.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Nagasaki laughed as Jeremy Morgan slipped into unconsciousness and went limp in the Champ’s arms. With Nagasaki refusing to release his hold, several wrestlers from the back hit the ring ... the Trashman, Drake Russell, Skip
Westervelt and Chip followed by the Black Ravens and Pep Preston. There was a stand-off as the fans cheered the “good guys”. With the Trashman holding off the Black Ravens and Pep Preston with his Singapore Cane, Skip pulled Flint with Mean Darlene’s help out of harm’s way while a glaring Drake
Russell stood outside the ring listening to the taunting Nagasaki who was inviting him into the ring.
There was a cheer as Russell climbed up onto the ring apron and slowly climmbed inside. The two started circling each other while the ref removed Jeremy Morgan from the ring. At Nagasaki’s urging, “Junior” called for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** The two continued circling until Nagasaki motioned for a Test of Strength. Cautiously accepting, they went chest to chest fighting for control. Pushing Russell back into a corner, Nagasaki stepped over the locked hands and broke the hold before driving a Reverse Knife Hand into Drake’s chest.
Russell was staggered with a Back Elbow then took a Jumping Knee high to the chest. With Russell dropping to the seat of his pants, Nagasaki drove his Boots into his opponent repeatedly.
Pulling Russell to his feet, the smiling Nagasaki had his Belly-to-Back Suplex blocked and was countered as Russell attempted a Hip Toss but was reversed and pulled roughly to the mat by his hair. Dropping down, the Champ hooked a leg for a 2 count before being thrown off.
Angered, Nagasaki Whipped Russell into a corner and followed in, finding the corner empty following Drake’s Tip-up. Wrapping his arms around Nagasaki’s waist from behind and trying to lift him off his feet, Drake was stopped as
Nagasaki Kicked backwards, catching him low. A Clothesline put the challenger down. Russell reversed Nagasaki’s hold, putting him down with a Body Slam and an Elbow Drop into a cover. Akatani slapped a 1 count before Nagasaki raised a shoulder. Lifted for a Suplex, Nagasaki slid off behind Russell. Spinning around, Drake caught a Double Ear Clap before being grabbed by the arm. Spinning around
Nagasaki was set to send Russell off across the ring but found himself sent instead. Caught be surprise, Nagasaki slammed into an unsuspecting referee.
On the outside of the ring, Team Japan set to storm the ring but were attacked by Flint, Trashman and the Lone Wolves and held off. Shaken up, Nagasaki staggered out of his corner into a Front Kick and Russell’s Double Underhook
Back Breaker. Sliding Nagasaki off his knee, Russell made a cover but got no count as Akatani was down after being hit by the Champ ... that is until referee Peter Daye hit the ring. Sliding in, Daye slapped out a fast 3 count before calling for the bell and leaving the ring.

Christmas came a day early to Denton and Public Access Wrestling before the credits began to roll.)
