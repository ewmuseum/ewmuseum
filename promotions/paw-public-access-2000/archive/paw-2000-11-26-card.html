11.26 Card

26 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 4 —

(The camera opens up in the office of the masked owner of the promotion. She sits at her desk, tapping a pen and staring at Justin Hart, leaning back in his chair, a smile on his face. Behind her stands her “Girl Friday”, Kimberly Davis and the Head of Security, Shane.)
Justin Hart: Miss me? Masked Woman: Not in the least. (Hart stood and struck a mock pose, feigning that his feelings had been hurt.) Justin Hart: Why Miss Offspring ... you wound me to the quick ... whatever the hell that means. Here I am, fresh out of incarceration, full of pep, ready to rock and you treat me this way? Why, one in my position would get the impression that you were holding something against me.
(Hart chuckled. The lady, however, was not amused.) Masked Woman: As it stands, you're still under contract with us, which means you still have a job ... but don't think for one minute that it means you have job security. Any more incidents and next time you get out of jail, you won't have a job to come back to.
(Hart gave her a disgusted look. He obviously didn't care.) Justin Hart: Do you really think I give a damn if you wave my job in my face? Ever since I came here, my career has been one screw-job after the next. As it stands, I only have one thing to say about your threat ...
(Hart walks over to the door and begins to leave but has one last thing to say.) You know where you can go? Straight to hell ... have a pleasant evening, (With that, Hart slams the door on his way out. Once gone, however, she could still hear him laughing at her. Shaking her head, she turns back to the Head of
Security.) Masked Woman: Watch him ... he’s not going to disrupt any business ... especially not now! (Shane nods his head as the camera fades out. With a smooth switch, the camera moves to a different part of the studio where Mean Darlene stands. looking impatient. Tapping her foot and looking at her watch, she turns suddenly when
Lonny Nakamura walks up and grabs her by the arm.) Mean Darlene: WHA!?!?! (The smiling Nakamura lets go of the startled woman.) Lonny Nakamura: Do you have the papers? Mean Darlene: I think we’ve already settled this issue ... I can’t, I’m sorry ... (Nakamura’s eyes narrow behind his glasses and his tone hardens.) Lonny Nakamura: If you want to manage champions again, you’ll do the right thing. Do not play games with me, Darlene.
Mean Darlene: I told you ... (Reaching out, Nakamura snatches her arm and pulls her suddenly closer.) Lonny Nakamura: Do not play games with me ... (Before another word is exchanged, they are joined by a third person. A young man, a dead ringer for a shorter version of Jake Morgan with sandy blonde hair, steps in and pushes Nakamura back.)
Young man: Hey! Leave her alone! (Surprised, Nakamura retreats several steps, smoothing out the wrinkles from his immaculate suit. Wagging a finger, Nakamura answers.)
Lonny Nakamura: Do not play games with me ... (Turning, Nakamura storms off. With him gone, the camera returns to Darlene and the young man.)
Young man: What was that all about? Mean Darlene: I ... have no idea, Jeremy. (Leaving the pair, the camera joins the green haired production assistant strutting through the back of the PAW television studio. He takes out a note, glances at it and rolls his eyes.)
Green haired man: I see how it is, come here, go there. Sheesh ... (Coming to a door, he stops and knocks.) You called for me? (The door opens quickly and a black-gloved hand reaches around pulling the green haired man in through the door. Shifting automatically to a camera inside of the room we see Drake Russell is the owner of those gloved hands. Holding the frightened green haired man up against the door, Drake points to the television sitting on a table.)
Drake Russell: Watch ... (The television displays images of a recent PAW show. As if the shots were looped, the same thing shows over and over again; the green haired man pinching
Lisa Doyle's bottom, her slapping him and then screaming at him. They continue to roll as Drake looks at him intensly. The green haired man at a loss for words, looks back to Drake.)
Green haired man: Listen ... uh ... you know how it is. It's just a guy thing. I know she's your girlfriend or something ... (Drake interrupts.) Drake Russell: What you think you know doesn't mean a damn thing to me. Under the circumstances, I don't really want to talk to you about anything that has to do with me. Just call me a friend ...
(The green haired man nods his head up and down lightning fast.) Green haired man: O ... okay, friend. It won't happen ag ... ( Another interruption from Drake.) Drake Russell: You're damned right it WILL NOT EVER HAPPEN AGAIN. If it does, I'll be seeing you again. Now get the hell outta my sight. (He opens the door and shoves the green haired man through before slamming the door just as large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession.)
.... 5 .... .... 4 .... .... 3 .... .... 2 .... .... 1 .... (White Zombie's "More Human than Human" begins as flashes of Hiroshima Nagasaki in action are shown. Scene after scene quickly flash by of past battles ...
Running his thumb across his throat before a limp Drake Russell is planted into the canvas with a Nagasaki Driver…
Following a Flying Knee straight to an incoming Seth's face, Nagasaki locks on the “Unconditional Surrender” for a win ...
The “Street Soldier” Justin Hart takes a Double Thrust to the Throat and with a Snap Suplex, put into a pinning predicament ... Pep Preston is flat on his back as Nagasaki leaps from the top turnbuckle and lands the “H-Bomb” ...
In slow motion, Aaron Starr turns right into a ball of fire thrown into his face and finally the PAW Television Champion standing in the middle of the ring, the belt raised over his head.)
After an explosion, the scene fades to black and the PAW logo disappears before fading back in and panning the fans and their signs held aloft such as "Guess
Who’s Back!!", "Japan Sucks!!" and "Where’s LOCO, Yes that LOCO?". After panning through the small crowd, the camera zooms in on a smiling Aaron Starr, waving to the fans. Turning to the camera, Aaron scoops up his microphone. Aaron Starr: Welcome to Public Access Wrestling! This week's show promises to be another exciting step toward Winter's Nightmare, PAW's next
Supercard. The current turbulent times and the return of several wrestlers to the PAW fold has created a rush of adrenaline throughout the promotion that we know you fans must feel as well.
(A loud roar goes out from the small crowd causing Starr's already wide smile to grow even wider.)
We have three great matches tonight including the return of my favorite and yours ... JASON FLINT!
(As the crowd begins to work itself into a frenzy of cheers, a gong begins to sound and kodo drums begin to play. Instantly knowing to whom the entrance music belongs, the crowd begins to boo with an increasing fervor even before PAW Television Champion Hiroshima Nagasaki steps from the back. PAW television commentator Ishimoto Sakai accompanies the champion towards the announcers table.
Starr lets out an exasperated sigh as they approach.) Damnit! Now what? (Upon reaching Starr, Nagasaki snatches the microphone from the young announcer's hand and hands it to Sakai. Though quick to anger, Aaron gladly backs off and sits down after the champion steps forward and taps him in the chest threateningly.)
Ishimoto Sakai: Hello! I am your host, Ishimoto Sakai and this (motioning around the studio) is Public Access Wrestling! Tonight's lesser bouts are wrestling commentator Jason Flint versus Pep Preston and Jake Morgan taking on
Drake Russell in a moronic match obviously inspired by an American ... the ladder match. Then, the champion will grace us with his prescence and embarrass "The Prison Punk" Justin Hart. Word is that Hart had a big following in jail with the majority of inmates saying "He was good."
(Nagasaki and Sakai both begin laughing as the crowd shows its disaproval. Leaning over to Sakai, Nagasaki whispers in his ear. Sakai nods his head.) Now if everyone could quiet down, we are lucky enough to have the champion here with us and he has decided to honor us with a few words.
(The crowd immediately bursts into a chant of "FUCK YOU!" as Sakai hands Nagasaki the microphone. Even Aaron Starr tries to get in on the act until Nagasaki stares him down. Finally, with Sakai running around hushing the crowd, the crowd lets up enough to allow Nagasaki to be heard.)
Hiroshima Nagasaki: I have given tonight's match with Hart much thought. Justin Hart represents everything I find despicable about PAW. He and those like him are the reason I have taken it upon myself to change this organization. Thankfully, the changes continue to progress nicely.
Unfortunately, there are still small obstacles, like Hart, to overcome. Do not worry loyal PAW fans your champion will not allow these hindrances to impede me.
Now, about tonight. Hart? Not once have you been man enough to face me alone in the ring. The only time we have met, you found it necessary to attack me in the back while my guard was down so that you could gain the advantage. And last week you waited until after I had beaten 2 men to attack me from behind. Why is that little man? The H-Bomb will tell you why.
Because, just like everyone else in PAW you know that your pitiful skills are no match for someone as talented as myself.
To prove this, I will rob you of any chance you might have to attack me from behind. How you say? By scheduling our match for right NOW!
(Nagasaki walks over, climbs to the apron, and jumps into the ring. Nagasaki raises his hand to quiet the crowd and resumes speaking. Meanwhile, the Black
Ravens step from the back and aproach the ring.) And ... to insure that none of your American "friends" try to save you from your fate, the Black Ravens will be ringside. Come Hart ... Come to your DOOM!
(After leaning over the ropes to hand Sakai the microphone, Nagasaki exchanges words with the Black Ravens who shake their heads in total agreement. The camera pans over to Sakai.)
Ishimoto Sakai: WOW! What an announcement! Now, we just have to await the arrival of Justin Hart so that his beating can commence!
(Dollybraid’s “Hero” begins to blare through the sound system and the strobe lights begin to flickers as someone who hasn’t walked through the curtain as a wrestler in some time, steps out from the back.
Walking slowly and deliberately, Jason Flint comes strolling in, absorbing the chants of “Welcome back!” as he makes his way to the ring. Dressed in a pair of dark khaki cargo shorts, black knee pads and a pair of black and white wrestling boots, Flint topped his ensemble with that of a black and red Coop bowling shirt, with the devil silkscreen on the back.
Making the rounds ringside, at the expense of a glaring Hiroshima Nagasaki, Flint slaps the hands of the fans all the way to the announcing table where, Aaron Starr and Ishimoto Sakai are. Pushing the little Asian man back into his seat, Flint graciously shakes the hand of Starr and grabs a mic from him.)
Jason Flint: Thanks there, Aaron. (Flint turns from Starr and looks towards the glaring Nagasaki.) Now ... you ... I really do hate to be the bearer of bad news on my first day back at the “office” but Justin Hart isn’t here to kick your ass yet ...
(Flint smiles as chants of “Kick his ass!” start up around the studio.) Maybe ... you’d settle on me stepping in for him? (With the chants growing louder, Nagasaki motions for Flint to enter the ring as Lonny Nakamura rushes out from the back waving his arm and shaking his head.
Giving Flint a wide berth, Nakamura climbs into the ring and has some words with the Champion that seems to anger Nagasaki.
The two argue until finally Nakamura motions for a mic.) Lonny Nakamura: You will get your chance soon enough, Jason Flint ... but not today ... the Champion has ... contractual obligations to fulfill and that is against Justin Hart ...
(Flint shakes his head in disgust as the fans’ booing grows louder with each passing second. Nagasaki moves forward but is stopped by both Black Ravens and
Nakamura. Led from the ring, Nagasaki makes several attempts to get to an unmoving Flint, staring angrily and having a few words with the returning wrestler but is held back until he disappears from view into the back. Shaking his head, Flint climbs into the ring.)
Jason Flint: Figures ... Now where was I? Oh yes ... IT IS TIME, my friends to start the long road back to where I should be … where you … the people want me … back … ON TOP.
(Flint stops as the chanting of his last name starts up. In the middle of the pause, Pep Preston walks to the ring and the chant changes to cascading boos.)
Now … Pep … don’t get me wrong, we live in the greatest country in the world and if you want to all of the sudden proclaim yourself to the world … Japanese,
Russian or French, who am I to say no? (Flint shrugs, smiling.) And you can proclaim to the world that you’re … green, yellow, blue or purple for all I care, you’d still be ole Pep to me. But what ya did do, a couple of weeks back is stick that miserable nose of yours into business that had nothing to do with you, and now … now you are going to pay for that mistake.
(Flint pauses a second, his smile melting away, Flint points straight to the veteran.) But as much of a surprise THAT was to me … to these fans … bitch, I have a couple of surprises of my own!
(On their feet, the fans clap their approval while Preston stands there shaking his head.) Pep Preston: Whoa ... whoa ... whoa ... hold on a second, Jason ... (With the fans booing and chanting in the background, Preston tried to shush them which of course, didn’t work. They quieted some when Flint held a hand up.)
You’re right ... I was wrong in what I did. I never should have listened to Nakamura and Nagasaki ... Once upon a time, you and I were tag partners ... worse yet, we used to be Champions. You had my back and I had yours. But ... in this promotion, where guys can get fired, rehired and thrust right into title contention ... where guys can get arrested and then thrown in against the Champion ... and in a promotion where a guy can be suspended and when he comes back ... the fans all love him ...
(Flint smiled when his name was was being chanted.) I had to do something. Guys like me who have been here from the very beginning don’t get a break. Oh sure, I was thrown in as your partner but is was supposed to backfire on you ... it was done as a joke in hopes that you would lose and lose badly ...
Well ... Lonny Nakamura made an offer. He made promises that suddenly had me in the limelight ... so I took it ...
(Boos filtered in from the audience.) I took the easy way ... and it was a mistake. I made a big mistake. For my attack on you, they promised me the world but instead, you saw what happened to me and here, tonight you want to show me the error of my ways inside this very ring ...
You’re right ... and I was wrong ... can you forgive me? (A confused Flint took a step back as Preston thrust out his hand in a gesture of friendship. The fans were again booing as Flint looked to the shrugging referee who wanted no part of what was going on.
Putting one hand behind his back, Preston again thrust his hand forward and chided Flint into accepting his apology.)
I can’t make you take my hand but I can offer an apology ... (Chants of “Kick his ass!” started up again that had Flint looking out into the audience.) I AM SORRY ... (Flint looked at the hand in front of him then to Preston’s face as he stood there then back to the hand before taking it and shaking with Pep.)
I’m sorry Jason ... sorry, I didn’t do this sooner! (Swinging his arm up, Preston slammed the mic into Flint’s head, staggering the man as the fans booed. As Flint stumbled backwards, Preston stood back and laughed while the identical Black Ravens rushed the ring. Diving in, they went right on the attack, Stomping the downed Flint and then tearing the shirt off his back.
Shredding the shirt, Preston and the Ravens were peppered with objects from the crowd, waded up papers and cups were tossed in as Preston wrapped the shirt around Flint’s neck from behind and strangled him with it. Peter Daye got involved trying to break up the action but was pushed back by one of the
Ravens. Stepping back, Preston let the Ravens working their magic as they pulled Flint to his feet. With each man grabbing an arm, they threw him onto his back with a
Double Hip Toss, lifting him high into the air and slamming him down. After taking shots at him, they pulled him up to his feet and Whipped him into the far ropes. On the return, both men held their arms out for a Double
Clothesline but Flint ducked under it at the last second. Continuing on for the ropes again, Flint bounced off and shot right back at his adversaries. Catching them by an arm, Flint pulled back sharply then grunted loudly as he threw both men over with a Double Hip Toss of his own. Taken by surprise, the Black Ravens rolled to the outside of the ring.
Pep was busy taunting the crowd as he continued to shred Flint’s shirt, oblivious to what was going on behind him. Reaching out, Flint grabbed Preston by the hair and yanked sharply back, pulling Preston to the mat with one quick motion. With the action and only two wrestlers in the ring, Daye called for the bell.)

— Part 2 of 4 —

*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding ***
Looking up, Preston saw Flint’s frame quickly approaching with a Leg Drop across his throat. With Preston down on the mat clutching his throat, Flint threw himself back against the ropes and took off across the ring. Grabbing the ropes at the last second, Flint used them to propel himself to the outside of the ring, slamming into the Black Ravens with a Tope Con Hilo. All three men hit the floor hard as the fans jumped to their feet for a better look.
Having landed hard, Flint was first to his feet but was limping slightly as he re-entered the ring. Using a series of Right Hands to back Pep into the corner,
Flint spun him around before slamming his head into the top turnbuckle. Once, twice, three times, Pep’s bounced off the pad as the fans counted out loudly.
Lifting Pep off his feet, Flint fell backwards with a Suplex out of the corner. Scrambling up the ropes, Flint turned towards the audience. Jumping off, he sat down on the ropes and bounced off as he threw his body backwards, landing a
Moonsault on the prone Pep ... an unmoving Pep. Rather than take the easy pin, Flint rolled back to his feet and dashed across the ring, hitting the mat and sliding to the outside, again wiping out the
Black Ravens with a Baseball Slide. Celebrating with the fans, Flint relieved as fan of his chair and tossed it into the ring. Returning to Pep, Flint pulled him to his feet and scooped him up and walked him into a corner. The busy Flint draped Pep’s legs over the middle rope, pushing his body to the mat in a Tree of Woe from the middle rope instead of the top rope. In this position, Pep’s head and shoulders were more on the mat. After a Boot to the midsection, Flint grabbed up the chair and climbed the corner ropes. Just before getting to the top, Flint stopped and stepped on Preston’s groin as he raised the chair into the air.
Down below, the Black Ravens were getting to their feet but stepped back when Flint pointed the chair at them as a warning. Turning back to the ring, Flint leapt from the top and swung the chair underneath him, landing with a loud bang on top of Preston’s face with an Arabian Facebuster.
The crowd went nuts as Flint rolled back to his feet and punched both arms into the air. Limping back to the corner, Flint worked Pep free of the ropes and dragged him to the center of the ring.
Pulling Preston to his knees, Flint entwined Preston’s arm with his leg. Stepping over and reaching for Pep’s free arm, Flint rolled Pep to the mat with a La Majistral, pinning his shoulders to the mat.
Daye hit the mat and slapped out a 3 count, giving Flint a victory in his return to the PAW after a ninety day exile.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Flint rolled to the outside of the ring and limped as he celebrated his victory with the fans as he took a stroll through them, climbing over the ring barrier.
Leaving Flint to his celebration, the camera returned to the ring where the Black Ravens were busy helping Pep Preston to his feet before leaving for the back and Television Champ Hiro Nagasaki along with manager Lonny Nakamura huddled around a television monitor and watching the end of the previous match.
After Flint rolls Preston up for the victory, Nagasaki slams his hand down on the table in front of him.)
Hiroshima Nagasaki: Damn Americans! (Turning to Nakamura, the angered Nagasaki rattles off a stream of Japanese with the only recognizable word being Flint said several times. A nodding
Nakamura tries to calm the Champion before he storms off.) Lonny Nakamura: Soon ... very soon ... you’ll get your hands on Jason Flint and he’ll wish he never returned to wrestling ...
(The camera left the laughing manager, returning to ringside where the fans were loudly cheering the finish of Flint’s successful return to the ring before moving back to the broadcast table and the hosts of the show, Aaron Starr and
Ishimoto Sakai. Starr is celebrating right along with the fans as he leads a cheer for the victorious Flint.)
Aaron Starr: A very successful return to the ring by our former Champion, Jason Flint ... wouldn’t you say so, Ish? (Ishimoto rolls his eyes.) Ishimoto Sakai: Yes ... a victory here in the open of our show against an inferior American wrestler ...
Aaron Starr: Uhm Ish ... let’s not forget an ass kicking on YOUR Black Ravens by Jason as well ... or did you forget that? Three on one and Jason looked right on top of his game!
Ishimoto Sakai: I think they are Koreans ... Aaron Starr: Oh Ish ... you’re still sore over being locked away in the closet last week! Ishimoto Sakai: Don’t you worry about that, Starr. An investigation is underway into the incident from last week that kept me from doing my duties for the company. Fines and suspensions will be levied against all participants involved
... but enough about me, let’s talk about the true star of this promotion, the PAW Televison Champion ... Aaron Starr: Speaking of coming out of the closet, Hiroshima Nagasaki takes on the returning Justin Hart after Hart did a number last week on YOUR champ ...
but right now, we have these words from Jake Morgan before he takes on Drake Russell in a Ladder Match! (The camera fades out on Starr and Sakai, opening on Jake Morgan, standing in the interview area, with his head bowed down. He slowly raises it, and looks at the camera. His eyes have the same haunted look that they had earlier in the week and it looks like he hasn't slept in days. He reaches his left hand up and brushes a stray patch of hair out of his eyes, then begins to speak.)
Jake Morgan: I find it interesting that you claim to want to settle all of your differences in the ring, Drake. Especially since you have been known to attack people in the back with that famous Singapore Cane of yours. I also find it interesting that you complain about outside interference in a match.
You've been known to place yourself in matches that weren't yours a few times in your career. Basically, I just find it interesting that a hypocrite like you , thinks he has a chance against a man like me in a Ladder Match.
(He looks down again but he continues to talk.) You don't stand a chance Drake, you never have. You can bring your best game to the ring and it won't be enough. You can bring ten of your best friends, at the top of their game and it won't be enough.
(He raises his head back up and screams into the camera.) Hell, you can bring all of the PAW roster to help you and it still won't be enough to beat me at my own game. I ask for no quarter and I give none. You've been warned. ENTER AT YOUR OWN RISK!
(He walks away as the camera fades back into Starr and Sakai.) Aaron Starr: Harsh words from Morgan ... and it sounds like Drake Russell is coming to the ring by the sound of the music ...
("Bitter" by Union Underground blares through the speakers and the fans give a rousing cheer for Drake Russell as he steps from the back, microphone in hand.
Wearing his usual wrestling pants with "Destiny" written on the side of it, it is also accompanied by his new PAW t-shirt, "My Time ... Your Blood." He steps to the guardrail and hops over it. Walking into the crowd of some 50 to 60 people, he raises the mic, allowing the pleased fans to scream their cheers for all to be heard. Finished, he hops back over.
Climbing onto the apron, he raises his Singapore cane into the air before pointing to the crowd, who is still cheering. Placing one leg followed by the other through the ropes, he enters the ring. He holds his position in the middle of the ring before the crowd settles down a bit.)
Drake Russell: Seeing that this past week was Thanksgiving, I sat down at home and started thinking about all of the things that I was thankful for. I made a list and I brought it here with me so that I could share a few with you.
( He reaches down into the front of his pants, reaching around. This draws a few female fans to whistle. He smiles and shakes his head before bringing a piece of paper back up. Unwrapping the paper and looking at it, he clears his throat.)
One ... I am thankful for having a job in what is in my mind, minus the Nagasaki bullshit signs, the best damned wrestling organization. Two ... I am thankful for each and every single fan that has graced the PAW arena with their presence here tonight.
Three ... I am thankful that God has given Jake Morgan the ability to talk. ( He smiles and shrugs.) Okay, okay ... the last one was a joke. Listen, Jakey, I heard what you said and just like every other time, I would really like to know where you are pulling this information from. Your ass seems to be the general location, though.
( He crumbles the paper and tosses it to the crowd.) You won the King of HardKore tournament last year, this is true. Did anyone care or does anyone care now? Simple answer, not at all. I didn't even enter the damn thing because it wasn't what I wanted to do. Is that why you have such a problem with me? Could be...
( He looks like he is weighing things in his hands.) ... doubt it, though. And what's this about you wanting to become the man you used to be? When people used to fear you? I honestly DO NOT have any recollection of when this was.
Oh yeah, that's right, because it never happened. When do you propose this was, Jake? Was it before or after the many ass kickings that Flint gave you? Without a doubt, Jake, everyone in the back was quaking in our boots at the thought of you but it wasn't because we were afraid.  
( He holds up his finger and looks up.) On second thought, we were afraid of you. We were of afraid that you were going to bleed on our clothes. So, to stay with this festive time, I'm in the mood to give you what you want. I'll give you an ass kicking and make you the man that was feared, the bleeding buffoon named Jake Morgan. Come on out, Jake and recieve your former self in one swift beating.
(He tosses the mic down. Stepping back, Russell waited on Morgan and was a bit surprised to see the masked Owner of the promotion stepping out from the back and followed to the ring by Kimberly Davis. Climbing into the ring, the masked woman took a mic from Davis.)
Masked Woman: Before we start this match, I’d like to add a little something ... over the past few weeks now, Drake ... you have been fighting with Jake Morgan and his partner, the Trashman. The three of you have been fighting it out not only inside the ring but you’ve been duking it out in the back as well and even though the odds have not always been in your favor ... you have stepped up to each challenge and win or lose, you have been putting on a show for the PAW fans ...
So ... I am here to make this Ladder Match just a little more special. As an extra incentive for both you and Jake besides the bragging rights of a win over the other, I am prepared to make this match for to become the possible #1
Contender. (Russell nodded his head in agreement as the fans cheered.) The winner of this match will go on next week to face Jason Flint to become the #1 Contender and for the right to take on Hiroshima Nagasaki in two weeks at Winter’s Nightmare ... All you have to do is climb that ladder and pluck this contract down ... (The masked woman took a piece of paper from Kimberly Davis before handing it back to her. With some help from the referee, Davis set up the ladder then climbed, where she put the contract. There were several whistles and catcalls as she climbed down.
Daye folded the ladder up and put it back down on the apron when Mean Darlene marched out from the back and climbed into the ring. With the fans cheering, she asked for the mic from the masked woman.)
Mean Darlene: You can’t go on with this match, Jake isn’t up to it ... (The fans started booing when Darlene waved them off.) Look ... he’s been taking a beating over the past couple of weeks and with a match each week, he doesn’t have the time to heal. Get a doctor to check him over and he’ll tell you that Jake’s suffering from a concussion ...
(Drake Russell was busy talking to the ref and pointing at the contract hanging from the ceiling.)
Masked Woman: A concussion? Jake saw a doctor earlier today and was cleared to wrestle ... but if you want to forfeit the match to Drake Russell, as his manager ... you’re well within your rights to do so ...
Mean Darlene: Yes, I ... (AC/DC’s “If You Want Blood, You Got It” started up over the PA system as Jake Morgan stalked his way out to the ring. Climbing inside, he looked around the ring before focussing his eyes on Mean Darlene.)
Jake ... tell them. You’re not ready ... tell them about the headaches ... (Jake tore the mic from Darlene’s hands.) Jake Morgan: Ring the bell! (Darlene picked up the mic after Morgan threw it down.) Mean Darlene: Jake, if you won’t listen to me ... listen to your brother, Jeremy ... (A young man looking like a slightly younger and smaller version of Jake made his way into the ring, taking the mic from Darlene.)
Jeremy Morgan: Jake, listen to Darlene ... you’re not up to this. Look at you, man ... you’re a mess. Take some time off to heal ... you need it ...
(Looking from Darlene to his brother Jeremy, Jake squinted his eyes before shoving them both out of the way and charging across the ring. With the masked woman and Kimberly Davis scrambling out of the ring, Petter Daye called for the bell as Jake nailed Drake Russell with a Knee Lift.)

— Part 3 of 4 —

*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding ***
After Stomping the downed Russell repeatedly with his boots, Jake pulled him back to his feet to level him with a Short Arm Clothesline. With Russell down,
Jake pulled off his t-shirt and tossed it out into the crowd that sent them into a frenzy. Pulling Drake to his feet, Jake sent him into the far ropes, taking him down on the return with a Body Scissor Press. From the mounted position, Morgan flailed away with Punches until Peter Daye came in and broke the two up.
Morgan came up with fists ready to swing as Daye backed away with a stern warning. Thnking better of it, Morgan returned to Russell, putting the Boots to him then turning away and moving for the ladder.
Picking it up, Morgan dropped it on top of the challenger, pinning him underneath it. Heading to the nearest turnbuckle, Jake climbed and sat on the top before leaping off and landing on top of the ladder with a Leg Drop. The loud cracking sound brought a cheer from the fans as Jake rolled to the mat then back to his feet.
Pushing it aside, he pulled Russell out from under it and set him up for a Suplex. Blocking with his leg, Drake Russell reversed the move and snapped Jake over with a Suplex of his own, spinning in order to slam Jake across the ladder.
Jake let out an anguished cry of pain as he landed hard on his back. Rolling off and onto his knees, Jake took a Drop Kick to the face that put him flat on his back.
Setting the ladder leaning up in a corner, Russell pulled Jake to his feet and swung him around and Whipped him into the leaning ladder. Staggering out of the corner, Jake was flipped over Drake’s back with a Back Body Drop as he came forward.
