1.21 Card

21 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 4 —

[A somber Aaron Starr walks out from the back alone, making his way to the ring. This week, however, there is no smiling, no waving, no high-fives and none of the usual fanfare the fans have come to know prior to going to the television taping.
The cheering dies away as Starr stands in the center of the ring with his mic in hand. Taking a few seconds, Starr looks out at the audience, stirring up a few lone cheers and whistles until finally the entire studio audience, though small as it is, goes silent in anticipation of what is about to be said.
The deadly silence is eerie and an unheard of thing here in Public Access Wrestling from the small but devote small-town crowd. Alone inside the ring, Starr looks around as one by one, he is joined in the studio by his fellow Public Access Wrestling co-workers ... one by one they file out from the back ... good and bad ... heel and face until they line the outside of the ring standing shoulder to shoulder, just as silent as the fans in the audience.
Drake Russell, Jason Flint, Justin Hart, Hiroshima Nagasaki, Jake Morgan, the Offspring, Pep Preston, Emilio Ramirez, the Black Ravens, Paco, the Trashman, Skip Westervelt, Chip, Lonny Nakamura, Toadie, Shane, Mean Darelene, Kimberly Davis, Fredrick A. Gibson, Lisa Doyle, Ishimoto Sakai, Junior Akatani, Peter Daye, Niles Neptune and finally, the masked owner of the promotion. With the procession from the back ended, Starr lifts the mic to his lips.] Aaron Starr: Today, we are brought together because of a loss ... a loss that extends into our family ... our extended family that makes the loss and pain of one of ours into the loss for us all ...
... and though we can never replace the loss of a grandfather, know in your heart that we all share in your loss ... and are here for you in your time of need ...
Join us now in our ten bell salute ... [In unison, the heads of those gathered around the ring drop down while the fans in the packed audience rise to their feet before the ring bell tolls.]
*** Ding *** *** Ding *** *** Ding *** *** Ding *** *** Ding *** *** Ding *** *** Ding *** *** Ding *** *** Ding *** *** Ding *** [With the ending of the bell, the PAW employees walk just as silently as they came into the back until the ring is clear.]
*Fedhead Note* [detail withheld], the handler of Kidd Gannon, lost his grandfather earlier this week. Our condolences, Derek.

— Part 3 of 4 —

[Handing the mic back to Starr, she stormed off followed by Russell to the chanting of the fans. “P-A-DUB!”.
With them gone, Starr shook his head while the Mexican man helped a woozy Sakai back to his feet. While helping him to the back, both he and Sakai were pushed aside by the suited Jason Flint as he dragged Paco out from the back by the ear.
The fans roared their approval as Flint rolled Paco into the ring, joining him as he quickly stripped off his jacket and tie. Referee Peter Daye called for the bell.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Pushing Paco off across the ring, Flint flattened him with a Drop Kick on the return. Springing back to his feet, Flint removed his shirt, kicked off his shoes and socks before going back to work.
Throwing himself back against the ropes, Flint sprang back, jumping into the air and hitting a dizzy Paco with a Leg Lariat. With the fans cheering, Flint bolted upright, pointed down at the Elvis lookalike and ran his thumb across his throat before coming down with an Elbow Drop.
Darting off across the ring, Flint hit the middle ropes and threw his body back into an Asai Moonsault. Daye dropped to the mat ready for a count, raising his arm into the air to slap the canvas but found a head shaking Flint looking him in the eye.
Pulling Paco to his feet, Flint him smashing into the far corner. Dashing across the ring, Flint’s Handspring Back Elbow knocked Paco off his feet.
Shaking his head, Flint pulled Paco to his feet, scooped him up and put him right back down with a Body Slam. He followed with a Somersault Leg Drop across
Paco’s throat. Hefting Paco into a Fireman’s Carry, Flint shifted his weight in the center of the ring before spinning his opponent off into a Gutbuster Drop. With Paco down in the center of the ring, Flint pointed at the corner before mounting the ropes.
Punching his arms into the air, he leapt off as the fans jumped to their feet. Spinning through the air, he landed a picture perfect Corkscrew Splash. Hooking a leg, Flint waited until Daye slapped out a 3 count for the end of the match.
There was no argument, this one was a done deal as Flint returned to his feet and the cheering fans. Nodding his head, Flint soaked in the cheers which changed to boos as he was joined inside the ring by Pep Preston from behind.
Charging in, Preston knocked Flint to the mat with a Running Knee to the back. Taunting Flint, Preston hammered him with a Forearm. Out of an Arm Bar, Preston shot Flint off into the far ropes only to catch him on the return in a Bear
Hug. Lifting Flint off his feet, Pep spun him around, dropping to the canvas with a Spinebuster Slam.
Flint rolled on the mat in pain while Daye and Preston had words over his involvement. Daye didn’t step back when Preston threatened him with a shaking fist. Ignoring the ref, Pep pulled Flint to his feet from behind, wrapping him up in a Waistlock. A Back Elbow broke the hold allowing Flint to hit the ropes and spring back for a Reverse Bulldog, slamming Preston’s head into the canvas.
Following a Tilt-A-Whirl Slam, Flint mounted the corner ropes but found an empty ring as Preston rolled to the outside for a breather. After having words with some fans, Preston turned back to the action to find Flint leaping off the turnbuckle. Landing on Pep’s shoulders, Flint spun his body into a
Hurricanrana onto the hard floor. Slow to get up, Flint limped slightly around the ring slapping hands before pulling a table out from underneath it. Setting it up on the floor, Flint hoisted Preston up onto it before walking away. Climbing over the railing,
Flint borrowed a chair from a member of the audience. Banging it loudly against the ring barrier, Flint shook his head then returned it. Asking for another one, he did the same only this time nodded his head before crossing the barrier.
From the floor, Flint looked out to the audience pointing at the ring apron. With a cheer of approval, he climbed onto it. With a maniacal gleam in his eye, he glanced down at the unmoving Preston while Daye administered a count before admonishing Flint who was holding the chair.
Looking around, Flint shook his head before pointing at the turnbuckle. The fans cheered their answer loudly, sending Flint off climbing again. From the top, Flint balanced himself from the top turnbuckle, motioning for Daye to get out of the way. Not heeding Flint’s words, Daye was sent scrambling as Flint leapt off. Flying through the air, Flint swung chair underneath him before landing on top if Pep and driving him through the table. A chant of “Holy
Shit!’ rose as the fans jumped to their feet for a better look at the two men amongst the splintered table.
Dropping to the floor, Daye slapped out a 3 count with Flint on top of Preston. Calling for the bell, the ref was in helping Flint to his feet, raising his hand. *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** [Flint staggered off with the cheers following in his wake while Daye called for help to the ring for the still downed Preston. Morgan against the ropes. Getting back to his feet, Morgan once again became a target as Hart rushed forward with fists flying. With a Drop Toe Hold as he sidestepped the lunging
Hart, Morgan dropped his opponent down across the ropes by the throat. Pulling Hart to his feet, Morgan wrenched in a Side Head Lock. Grinding it in, Morgan led Hart around the ring, close enough to his own corner to taunt Russell who was not above taking a swipe at Morgan. The ref stepped in with a quick warning.
Powering out, Hart sent Jake across the ring into the far ropes. On the rebound, Jake ducked a Clothesline continuing on to the ropes. Bouncing off a second time, Morgan shot right back, driving a shoulder into Morgan and putting him down on the mat.
Pulling Hart nack up into a Side Head Lock, Morgan pulled his adversary back into his corner to make a tag. Wrapping up Hart, Morgan fell back with a Side
Russian Leg Sweep while Nagasaki mounted the ropes, coming off with the “H-Bomb” Fist Drop. Grabbing Hart’s legs, Nagasaki fell back pushing Hart into the air with a Slingshot right into a Clothesline from the waiting Morgan who was ushered from the ring. With the fans booing, Nagasaki taunted Russell on the outside who was quick to duck into the ring only to be stopped by the ref allowing Nagasaki to throw
Hart into his corner where Morgan held his arms while the Japanese wrestler laid into him with Reverse Knife Hands that popped loudly off his chest.
As soon as the ref returned to the match, Morgan let go. Lifting Hart onto his shoulders into a Fireman’s Carry, Nagasaki rolled him off, dumping him onto the mat while holding on with an Arm Bar. Hart was in pain being watched by the ref as Nagasaki worked the arm trying to damage it. He laughed maniacally as
Akatani asked the head shaking Hart. Twisting the arm left and right, Nagasaki would not allow Hart to get back to his feet no matter what he did. Stepping over Hart’s body, Nagasaki rolled to the mat, putting shoulders to the mat for a 2 count until Hart slammed his feet against Nagasaki’s ears.
Shaking his arm, Hart fired away with Left Hands that backed the former Champ across the ring. Throwing his body back against the ropes, Hart shot forward knocking Nagasaki through the ropes to the ring apron with a Diving Shoulder
Butt. With Morgan going to help his partner, Hart had two targets as he dove over the ropes to the outside, taking both men down with a Slingshot Splash. The fans were on their feet cheering with all three men down on the ground.
First to his feet, Hart grabbed both men and pulled them up before slamming their heads together. With them staggered, Hart smiled as he turned on the lurking Nakamura who backed away quickly. Morgan and Nagasaki came to their manager’s aid as they both nailed Hart with blows before pushing him into the ring.
Russell shot into the ring but was intercepted by the ref while Nagasaki sent Hart across the ring. As he returned, he ran right into a waiting Morgan who caught him up around the waist and drove him into the mat with a Spinebuster.
Nagasaki followed with a Leg Drop. With the fans booing, Morgan and Nagasaki shared a high five while Nakamura slapped the ring apron. Turning back to Hart, they were surprised as they walked into a Double Low Blow before being hammered to the mat with a Double
Clothesline. With the Rising Sons down, Hart staggered back to his corner ready to make a tag only to find Russell walking away, then disappearing into the back. An indignant Hart was grabbed from behind and lifted into the air before being slammed down with a Double Suplex.
A Double Bulldog was followed by a Powerbomb/Top Rope Leg Drop combination that finally drew a warning from the ref. With urging from Nakamura, Morgan drapped himself across the fallen Hart when a huge cheer went up. Getting off of Hart on a 2 count, Morgan watched as Drake Russell returned to the ring pushing a shopping cart full of “goodies’ and a ladder on top of it.
Throwing items into the ring, Russell threw the shopping cart in over the ropes before pushing the ladder into the ring with the Rising Sons bailing from the ring. Nagasaki led his team away while the fans booed them. Morgan waved off a taunting Russell and was pulled away by Nagasaki.
Shaking his head, Russell produced a mic. Drake Russell: What’s the matter, boys? Don’t have the balls ... or ... is this what you’re looking for?
Both Morgan and Nagasaki turned back the ring to see Russell holding his title belt aloft and motioning for them to come and get it. Despite pleading from their manager, they took off for the ring ... and the waiting Russell. Bouncing to their feet, the got just what they wanted ... the PAW Television Title belt as Russell slammed into their heads, knocking them to the mat.
Pointing down at Morgan, Russell picked up a street sign and held it up for all to see. Denton Drive came down on top of Morgan’s head then was blasted into the charging Nagasaki.
Joined by Hart, Russell slammed the shopping cart’s corner into Morgan’s crotch while Hart broke a crutch across his back. With Russell pummeling
Nagasaki in the corner, Hart pulled a cheese grater from the mat and rubbed it in his face.
The flailing Nagasaki dropped to the canvas in pain and was pulled from the ring by his manager, his face a bloody mess.
While Hart beat Morgan across the back with a golf club, Russell busied himself with the ladder, setting it up in the center of the ring then helping Hart drag
Morgan to the center of the ring. With a nod of the head from Russell, Hart climbed to the top while down below, Russell dropped the shopping cart across
Morgan’s leg. From the top, Hart leapt off, landing on top of the cart with a Leg Drop that brought everyone to their feet chanting “Holy Shit” while Morgan writhed in pain. Hart rolled around on the mat holding his tailbone as Morgan was pulled from the ring. The chanting continued as Akatani warned the pained Hart. Taking the ladder down and wedged it in the ropes, Russell pulled the ref away from Hart and
Whipped him across the ring where he smacked solidly into the ladder in the corner. With the ref slumping against the ropes, Russell kicked a clear path to the corner before pulling Hart to his feet and spinning around and sending him into the same corner. Nearing the corner, Hart went airborne, smashing into the small ref with the “Hangtime” Corner Splash.
Setting the ladder back up, Russell climbed and celebrated with his partner and the fans who were still cheering. From the other side of the ring, Nagasaki tipped the ladder over and sent Russell hurtling into the ropes where he snapped back to the mat clutching his chest and neck.
Hart drove a Front Kick into Nagasaki’s midsection before letting him have it across the back of the head with a cookie sheet. Spun around, Hart took a Boot as well before being driven into the mat with a Morgan DDT. Before being able to do more, Morgan took a Knee to the back before being driven into the ropes where he bounced back into another Knee. Dropping his head, Morgan was put into a Double Underhook and was lifted into the air where he was brought down on top of the by now crushed shopping cart.
A Super Kick from Nagasaki dropped Russell, bringing on boos from the fans for the bloody wrestler. Propping the ladder up in the corner, Nagasaki Suplexed
Russell into the ladder, bending it badly. Turning Russell upside down into the Tree of Woe, Nagasaki was up on the ropes, stepping on Russell’s crotch bringing a moan of pain from the fans as well while he laughed out at them.
On the other side of the ring, Morgan was pummeling Hart in the opposite corner. Getting the better of the exchange, Morgan rocked Hart before Nagasaki came over with the ladder. Dropping it on the floor, he helped Morgan get Hart into position on the top rope. Lifting Hart up onto Morgan’s shoulders,
Nagasaki stepped out of the way before his partner drove Hart down across the ladder with a Top Rope Powerbomb that shook the ring. Again the fans were chanting loudly. Sitting there with a pinning predicament, Morgan waited for the ref’s count but none came as Junior Akatani was laying out on the floor having rolled to safety.
A disgusted Morgan let go of Hart with some prompting from his partner as the pair used their boots on the helpless Drake Russell. He was saved only by
Justin Hart who charged in, leaping into the air to crush both Morgan and Nagasaki ... that was the plan anyway as he was snatched out of the air. Pushed into the air, Hart took a Double Slam.
With Nakamura scrambling to set up a table on the outside, Hart was sent into the ropes and on the return, was sent flying out of the ring with a Double Back
Body Drop. Hitting the table, Hart’s weight shattered it as the fans were once again on their feet chanting.
The Rising Sons worked over the unmoving Hart with a severe Boot Stomping until Russell came to Hart’s aid swinging his Singapore Cane. Both Morgan and Nagasaki were knocked to the floor from the blows giving Hart a breather as Russell splintered his weapon. Pushing Nagasaki into the ring, Russell fired Nagasaki off into the far ropes, taking him down with a Jump Spinning Back Kick. Off the ropes, Russell landed on top of Nagasaki with a Moonsault and hooked a leg for the cover, again there was no count until referee Peter Daye slid into the ring and slapped a 1 count when Nagasaki got a shoulder up.
Pulled to his feet, Nagasaki landed a Knee Lift then a Double Thrust to Throat. Pushing Russell into the corner, Nagasaki put a cookie sheet into his opponent’s face before hitting with a Running Drop Kick that smashed the thin metal.
On the outside, a badly bleeding Morgan was being helped to his feet by Nakamura when an angered Hart grabbed him from behind, wrapping the OU jacket around Jake’s throat. Dropping to the floor, Hart strangled the already faced
Morgan with his own jacket. Nakamura tried to break Hart’s grip but was having no luck when Jeremy Morgan ran out from the back, Spearing the manager as he turned to the cheering.
Finished with Nakamura, Jeremy turned his attention to Hart and tried to break the deathgrip around his brother’s throat. That is until Mean Darlene ran out and pulled Jeremy away allowing Hart to continue.
Inside the ring, Nagasaki pulled a limp Russell to his feet and motioned for the “Unconditional Surrender” just as the bell sounded ending the match.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** [Security hit the ringside area as Daye called for the bell a second time, to pull everyone apart before the camera zoomed in on the twisted Hart’s face as he struggled to deprive Morgan of oxygen just before the credits began to roll bringing the show to an end.]
