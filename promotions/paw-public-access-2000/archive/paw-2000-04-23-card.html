Groggy after the first couple of blows, Jake Morgan stopped fighting back and was fighting just to hold onto the cage. Two more blows hit home before Morgan fell from the top of the cage and crashed onto the canvas below.
With no one to hamper his climb, a confident Drake Russell made it to the top and started to pull himself over ... the only problem was that he wasn't alone as Mean Darlene scrambled up the side of the cage and started hitting Russell.
Struggling to fend off her blows, he was able to pull her over the top and onto his side. Falling backwards, the two plummeted from the top and hit the mat joining an unmoving Jake Morgan.
Standing over both wrestlers, referee Peter Daye started a slow count on the two men. As his count went on, the bell sounded signalling a time limit draw.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (With the match over, Daye called for help from the back while Perris unlocked the cage door and bolted inside to help Russell. The fans were on their feet cheering loudly when the camera broke away to somewhere in the back focussing in on a wrestling mask ... not just any mask but the one used by the man that had impersonated the "Extreme Luchadore", the Offspring many months ago.
Staying focussed on the mask, a hand ... a woman's hand reached into view and picked up the mask and moved it away.
Moving to the outside of the building, the camera made it's way along a brick wall before coming to a stop on a shape ... etched in what appeared to be white chalk was a handprint ... a solitary handprint which was the last thing seen before the camera faded to black.
