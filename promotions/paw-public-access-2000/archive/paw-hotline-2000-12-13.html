PAW Hotline!

13 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*** Ring *** *** Ring *** *** Ring *** (There is an audible clicking sound as someone "answers" the phone and the voice of Public Access Wrestling’s Aaron Starr is heard, loud and clear.)
Hello wrestling fan ... Aaron Starr, here, making his big return with a big thank you for calling the PAW’s Ring Action Hotline ... all the news and notes from Public Access Wrestling!
Since time is money ... let's get to the action ... x-x-x-x-x Bragging Rights ... For those of you who missed last weekend's Winter's Nightmare Supercard, you missed a sensational event which saw the team from Japan eke out a victory over the PAW superstars.
For a recap of the show, check out the Public Access website: http://www.geocities.com/perrishex/PPV.html
That's all I'm going to say about it! x-x-x-x-x Off Again, On Again ... With several week's of negotiations, it seems that Public Access Wrestling has taken steps to complete a deal with the American Wrestling Promotions.
What direction this may lead to with Ms. Brittney Christopher's promotion is unknown at this time. Stay tuned for more details.
x-x-x-x-x Fly In The Ointment? Another snag seems to have popped up in the negotiations between Public Access Wrestling and the heads of the prestigious National Wrestling Council. It seems that the reduction from three belts to only the remaining Television Title was too much. Sources say that PAW is scrambling to rectify this small oversight. Could we see the reinstatement of the Texas Heavyweight Title? And if so, would
Justin Hart, the last reigning champion be recogized once again?
My guess is with the resent influx of lighter wrestlers in Kidd Gannon and XYZ joining Paco, Emilio Ramirez, the Black Ravens and the Trashman, we'll be seeing something for them.
x-x-x-x-x Going Home ... With the passing of Winter's Nightmare, several of the wrestlers representing the Japanese team have already headed home. The first wave of wrestlers to leave the north Texas area were Devil Tsaruta and his manager Angel Mishima,
Aroku Murakami and the "Iron Chef" Keiji Kobe. Referee Jinbei Akatani also joined the wrestlers back to the Orient due to health reasons. According to Lonny Nakamura, Akatani's son will be filling in for his father in the very near future.
Slated to stay with the promotion are the tag team tandem, the Black Ravens and hardcore wrestler, XYZ as well as Nakamura and TV Champion, Hiroshima Nagasaki.
Good riddance I say .. and hopefully, that list of names will grow in number very soon! x-x-x-x-x The Boyz Are Back In Town ... A return of Kidd Gannon to the ranks of Public Access Wrestling was met with positive approval following his wrestling debut with a victory over Japan's
Devil Tsaruta. Word is that Gannon might be recieving an uppercard push early next year if his workrate continues as expected.
Also impressive was the performance of Drake Russell. Despite his Supercard loss to NMW Television Champion, Aroku Murakami, the PAW front office remains impressed with his work of late as his well rounded wrestling style seems adaptable to all opponents. Will the "real" Drake Russell stand up and push forward into the upper echelon and resume a feud with the top stars of PAW?
Only time will tell. x-x-x-x-x It's Brutal! Oklahoma boy and ring veteran, Brutal Brian Floyd has signed a contract with Public Access Wrestling and has been immediately pushed into main event status. Terms of the contract were not released but a main event push right from the start might be used to entice Mr. Floyd to wrestle exclusively for Public
Access Wrestling and forego his Rocky Mountain Wrestling comittments. Continuing to add to it's roster, Public Access Wrestling officials maintain that it wishes to remain with it's roots as it fills it's roster with local wrestlers and those from "closer to home" such as Floyd, despite the fact that several "larger names" have passed through the territory in search of employment.
x-x-x-x-x Match Made in Heaven? Last weekend's surprising ending left a bad taste in alot of mouthes but none bigger than two of the persons that are the focal point of the story ...
manager of Jake Morgan, Mean Darlene and Jason Flint. It seems that revenge is in the offing for both parties and how this all plays out remains to be seen but it should be noted that Mean Darlene has managed champions wherever she's been!
Another run with a title for Jason Flint? Perhaps ... x-x-x-x-x Heads Will Roll! Livid would be a good word to define PAW's president following the Winter's Nightmare Supercard. The target of those feelings? Try on one Justin Hart for size. It seems that the self defined "Resident S.O.B." has gotten under the skin of the promotion's bigwig with his antics of late and though having claimed not to be anything like her predecessor, has claimed "Mr. Hart needs to be taught a lesson."
x-x-x-x-x This week's presentation of the PAW's Top Ten Wrestlers finds "Brutal" Brian Floyd debuting at the #10 spot . . . from there we find . . . #9 . . . Kidd Gannon . . . #8 . . . Paco . . . #7 . . . Skip Westervelt . . . #6 . . . Drake Russell . . . #5 . . . Justin Hart . . . #4 . . . XYZ . . . #3 . . . Jason Flint . . . #2 . . . Jake Morgan . . . #1 . . . the Trashman . . . . . . and the PAW's Television Champion . . . Hiroshima Nagasaki x-x-x-x-x That last bit of information brings us to a close of this recording ... come back next week for more hardhitting PAW information, news, rankings and wrestling action ... for Black Cat Entertainment and Public Access Wrestling
... this has been Aaron Starr! (There is a clicking sound as the recording comes to an end followed shortly thereafter by the dial tone.)
