PAW — Card Archive

4.30 Card

30 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera opens on Skip Westervelt and cohort Chip standing in the hallways of the PAW television studio before the show begins.)
<Skip Westervelt> Failed? Our war is over? Buddy ... that's what he thinks. You know, it really steams my hide that he wins his little belt and all of a sudden
Neo Draven is the Golden Boy ... how quickly he forgets who kicked his ass just two weeks ago ...
(Chip stands there shaking his head trying to get Westervelt to stop talking. Ignoring his compatriot, Skip continues on.) And ... we beat the crap out of his precious Sasha. How stupid can the two of them be? Like they had a chance to beat the most dynamic tag team in the PAW?
(Chip's eyes are wide as his hand starts waving back and forth across his throat to get Westervelt's attention.)
Well ... tonight, Mr. Neo Draven is going to have his ass handed to him ... and then we'll see if this little feud is over ...
What? (Chip's finger points past Skip who turns to see Drake Russell walk by. With the camera zooming in on Russell, a smile creases his lips as he walks off.
Once he is gone, the camera returns to Skip.) Ah ... he didn't hear me ... (The camera smoothly fades out on Skip and Chip and settles in on B.J. Perris and the "Electric Referee" Dan Glass somewhere else in the back.)
<Perris> I'm sorry, Dan ... there's very little I can do for you. Really ... my hands are tied.
(Perris grins as he holds his hands up in front of him.) I wish I could help you out but you are technically Flint's employee ... I only pay for your in-ring referee skills. Anything that happens to you in the back are out of my hands.
I'm sure that Jason will be able to take care of your problems ... now if you'll excuse me, I have a program to run ...
(Perris turns and darts off leaving the "Electric Referee" standing there with his mouth open. Moving to the parking lot, the camera comes in on one half of the PAW Tag Champs, Johnny Green standing with the three members of the State
Club Boyz, who until last week had been Jason Flint's biggest supporters.)
<Johnny Green> I guess this is it then? You guys won't reconsider? (All three shake their heads as they pick up their bags after exchanging handshakes with Green.)
<Tony> If you were smart ... you'd ditch his ass and come along with us before he drags you down with him ...
<Curtis> Yeah ... his little empire is sinking faster than the Titanic. Man, it's only a matter of time before he brings you down with him.
(Green slowly shakes his head.) <Johnny Green> Sorry guys ... I made a promise ... I can't ... <Joe> Good luck, man ... <Johnny Green> Yeah ... you, too ... (As they walk off and leave Green alone, Tony shouts a final warning.) <Tony> Get out before he takes you down, too ... (With the words echoing off into the distance, the camera heads back inside onto Jake Morgan standing alone in a dreary room, pipes line the ceiling overhead and the dripping water echoes somewhere in the background. A single light bulb dangling from an overhead fixture is the only source of illumination.
Standing silent, Morgan slowly lifts his head and peers into the camera.) <Jake Morgan> Later on tonight I have a match with Brian Cage but before that,
I want to get something off my chest. Russell, you said that my assumption about Perris was wrong. I proclaimed that he turned his back on me and supported you. Well, you're right, he never turned his back on me.
(Looking up, he swats at the light bulb, hitting it and causing it to swing back in forth in a pendular motion. With each swing, it creates shadows that move from one side of his face to the other.)
He couldn't betray me, if he never truly supported me. After the 13 incident, we came to an uneasy truce but it was never a friendship. It was a business relationship. I was Darlene's bodyguard, plain and simple. It was never anything more. For a while, I benefited from it, that time is obviously over.
Perris, you're a smart man. You're a businessman and as far as I'm concerned, our BUSINESS relationship has not changed, it's business as usual.
(The corners of his lips turn upwards looking quite eerie with the shadows created by the still swinging bulb.)
But that only goes for you and me, BJ. You're off limits but everyone else is fair game. That includes your boy, Drake and anyone else that gets in my way.
PAW, you have been warned! (Lifting his meaty hand, Morgan reaches towards the bulb and flicks a single finger at it. Hitting with deadly accuracy, there is a popping sound as he is swallowed up by darkness followed by large red numbers bursting onto the screen in rapid succession.)
... 5 ... ... 4 ... ... 3 ... ... 2 ... ... 1 ... (Rob Zombie's "More Human than Human" starts to play loudly as clips of Public
Access Wrestling 2000's Superstars in action passes ... Skip Westervelt ...
Drake Russell ... Jake Morgan ... Neo Draven and Sasha ... Brian Cage ... Johnny Green and finally, the Texas Heavyweight Champion, Jason Flint, standing in the center of the ring lifting his title belt skyward ...
With an explosion, the scenes disappear from the screen and are replaced by the logo of Public Access Wrestling 2000 as it moves forward, filling the screen.
The song suddenly stops and with a smooth transition, fades out ... With a just as smooth fade in, the camera pans inside ring where host Aaron
Starr stands with B.J. Perris.)
<Aaron Starr> Another week ... another edition of Public Access Wrestling and joining me today ... as always a pleasure ... B.J. Perris ...
<Perris> Thank you, Aaron ... <Aaron Starr> B.J. ... one question on everyone's mind ... the #1 Contender ... Jake Morgan or Drake Russell? Who is going to be the one getting a legitimate shot at Jason Flint's title ...
Not wanting to dig into your business ... but you seem to be backing Drake
Russell as your handpicked man to unseat Flint who has stood up to all challenges for the past six months ... even with his recent handicap matches
...
Is Russell the man despite what Jake Morgan has done for you in the past? And what has this done between you and Mean Darlene who is obviously giving her endorsement to Jake?
What gives? (Perris could only smile as he turned to Starr.) <Perris> Aaron ... I wish we had more time to go into this in depth but with only an hour to get this great card underway ... we have to move onto more pressing matters ...
<Aaron Starr> More pressing? <Perris> That's right, Aaron ... next week's Hardkore Karnage tournament. With everything that's been going on as of late, we've gotten away from what we are all about ...
<Aaron Starr> Wrestling? <Perris> Wrestling with an edge ... a hardcore edge ... we're getting back to the kind of wrestling that brought in the likes of Jake Morgan and Brian Cage as well as former participants such as the Offspring and Evil K ...
We're getting back to basics ... and this tournament will crown one man who stands heads and shoulders above rest ...
<Aaron Starr> Back to basics? <Perris> Hardcore wrestling ... the way it was meant to be ... not the stuff you'll see elsewhere passed off as true hardcore but some of the most brutal matches ever seen in the United States ...
<Aaron Starr> Next week ... it all begins ... Hardkore Karnage ... but now we break away to the back where my collegue, Lisa Doyle is waiting ...
(The camera breaks away from Perris and Starr only to go to a black screen for long seconds before an image appears ... a single handprint on the black field.
For long seconds the hand sits there in view before it quickly disappears and the camera returns to Starr and Perris.)
<Aaron Starr> Well ladies and gentlemen ... it appears that we are having technical difficulties ...
(A smattering of boos came from the fans as the PAW Texas Heavyweight Champion,
Jason Flint stalked out to the ring with a mic in hand.)
<Jason Flint> Aaron Starr ... you need to shut your mouth because no one ... and I mean no one cares one iota about your technical difficulties ... <Aaron Starr> Jason Flint ... (Flint gave Starr a harsh stare before going on.) <Jason Flint> One more word escapes your big mouth and I'm going to show what a
"technical dificulty" is ... as I said, no one cares about a little handprint
... unless Perris is ready to play some patty cake with his boy, Drake Russell
...
(Putting his hand over Starr's face, Flint pushed him out of the way before stepping up to the unflinching Perris.)
And you ... two weeks in a row now, you've thrown staggering numbers of opponents at me ... two-on-one ... three-on-one ... it doesn't matter how many you throw at me ... the Texas Heavyweight Champion is going to knock them down
... and in the end ... when this is all said and done ... the "Electric One" is going to knock you down ...
Now ... bring on my opponents and let's get this over with ... (Heading to the ring, Flint was joined shortly by his three opponents. Daye called for the bell when Pep Preston joined Flint inside to the fans chanting
"Pep, Pep, Pep!".)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** One-on-one, Jason Flint showed Preston, Ramirez and Paco why he was Texas
Champion as he put on a wrestling clinic, countering their moves with little effort and fighting from a purely defensive posture. Last week, he was facing untrained men this week's opponents were a little better.
After putting Paco down with Tilt-A-Whirl Back Breaker, Flint found himself facing Preston who was being cheered by the fans as they chanted his name.
Going back and forth, Pep had Flint backed into the corner and was firing away with Reverse Knifehands before being spun into the corner and taking Flint's
Chops right back. Flint went down as Emilio Ramirez ran and hammered Flint to the canvas with a Leg Lariat. It was now two-on-one and in short order all three men were in the ring beating the downed Champion while the referee watched on. Peter Daye wasn't about to stop the beating.
Grabbing Flint and hoisting him into the air, Preston and Ramirez held him aloft while Paco climbed the corner ropes. Coming off, Paco dropped a Leg across Flint's chest and spun him through the air out of their grasp. That might have been the match right there but that wasn't the case as help came in the form of his tag partner, Johnny Green.
Hitting the ring, Green cleared the ring with a flurry of Forearms and Punches that sent Flint's opponents in all directions. After helping Flint to his feet,
Green taunted the others leaning over the ropes and inviting them back in.
Turning on Flint quickly, Green grabbed the Champ by the arm and sent him across the ring into the far ropes, away from his opponents. Hitting the ropes and bouncing off, Flint returned with a full head of steam and at the last second was caught by Green and tossed up and over the ropes to the outside.
With momentum from the ropes and Green's "push", Flint went flying through the air and took Paco and Ramirez to the ground with a Plancha. Green followed suit and came flying to the outside and wiped out Preston with a Double Axe Handle.
With a high-five, Green and Flint sent all three men into the ring barrier. With them down, Flint and Green pulled Daye from the ring and pushed him back and forth between them before laying him out with a couple of right hands. They again high-fived as they rolled Daye back int othe ring and from the outside pulled him along the canvas and into the ringpost which brought a collective groan from all the male fans.
Daye was rolling around in pain when Flint and Green worked over Preston in the center of the ring with an assortment of Double Team moves showing why they were the Tag Champs. A Double Suplex into a combination Hip Toss/Senton Bomb had Preston flat on his back but with no referee to count, the match continued.
Tossing Preston aside both men left the ring to collect one of his partners and after working over Paco were blindsided themselves.
Jake Morgan rushed out from the back and hit Green then Flint from behind. Stomping them both to make sure they were down, Morgan pulled a table from under the ring and set it up on the floor before rolling Flint back into the ring and joining him. Lifting him up onto his back, Morgan ran across the ring and threw Flint off his shoulders and out of the ring and sent him crashing through the table on the outside.
Pushing Flint back into the ring, Morgan rolled Paco into the ring and onto
Flint. There was a big smile on his face as he walked away from the ring towards the back as Daye slapped out a count.
... 1 ... ... 2 ... ... 3 ... NO! Flint somehow got his shoulder up at the last second. Morgan was shocked. Paco was on his feet and took off for the far ropes. Bouncing off, he criss-crossed the ring, hopping over the downed Flint only to have his feet taken out from under him by a wobbly Johnny Green.
Turning, Paco pointed at Green and looked as if preparing to Slingshot himself out of the ring but was grabbed from behind and pulled to the mat with an
Inside Cradle. Daye was in bad shape, slapping out a 3 count despite Flint's handful of Paco's tights.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Seeing his plan backfire, Morgan was about to head to the ring but stopped short and dropped to the floor when Kat Collins snuck up behind him and nailed him with a perfectly placed Low Blow.
Running out, she jumped into Flint's arms, nearly bowling him over in the process. Green caught them both before they headed towards the back, stopping when Perris held up a hand.)
<Perris> I hate to kick a man while he's down but ... (Green pulled the mic away from Perris and looked angered.) <Johnny Green> Look Perris ... I don't know what your beef is but you better open your eyes and look around this ring ... Jason and I just beat your three wrestlers .. and if I'm not mistaken, they had a little help from Morgan, who doesn't look too great himself ...
(Green pointed towards Morgan who was being helped to his feet by Mean Darlene.) Way I see things ... we just kicked your ass ... <Perris> It's good to see that you have such loyal friends ... few as they may be ... <Johnny Green> The State Club Boyz may have flown the coop ... but I made a promise and that's something you're just going to have to deal with!
<Perris> Interesting choice of words ... and as I was saying before I was so rudely interrupted ... I hate to kick a man while he's down ... but in this case ... what the Hell?
(Perris smiled as the PAW Security stepped from the back.) Effective immediately ... you're vacating the Tag Titles ... have a nice day
... (Green was restrained by Flint as Perris strolled to the back, seperated from
Mean Team 2000 by Security.)
<Jason Flint> You might have stripped me of the Tag Titles but I'm still the
Texas Heavyweight Champion ...
(Perris stopped and turned back towards the fuming Green and Flint.) <Perris> All in due time, Flint ... all in due time ... (Perris disappeared into the back leaving the now former Tag Champs ringside. Just as Perris disappeared, so too did the camera as it came upon a lone Neo
Draven walking through the back of the studio. Nearing a door, he stopped and ducked his head to peer at a note stuck to it.)
<Neo Draven> What the?!?!?!? (Scratching his head, a female voice was heard in the background wishing him luck. Turning to see the wellwisher, Neo was looking at Kimberly Davis. A smiling Kimberly Davis. Looking down the hallway in both directions, Neo scanned the area for Flint, Green or anyone else in his entourage.)
What the?!?!?!? <Kimberly Davis> I just wanted to wish you luck against Russell. Work his legs and you should do well ...
(Having said her piece, she turned and walked off. Neo shook his head in disbelief as the camera moved past him over his shoulder, focussing in on the note on the door addressed to the TV Champion.
"Watch your back, Neo - Skip plans on teaching you a lesson tonight." (Leaving a stumped Neo Draven, the camera found Flint leaning heavily on Kat
Collins as she lead him and Green down the hallway.)
<Johnny Green> He did it ... he took our belts! <Jason Flint> Don't sweat it ... I still have the Texas Title ... he's not getting that away from me. We're fine ...
<Johnny Green> That's your belt, Jason ... not mine ... <Jason Flint> We're in this together, Johnny-O ... it doesn't matter who has the belt ... MT2K is Back and Bad!
(Green rolled his eyes as he exhaled then muttered under his breath.) <Johnny Green> It's just bad ... (Flint didn't get to answer when they were approached by the "Electric Referee" Dan Glass.) <Dan Glass> Jason ... Johnny ... (All three turned to see Glass quickly approaching.) Guys ... we need to talk ... (Flint rolled his eyes and turned towards Johnny.) <Jason Flint> You take care of this Johnny ... (Turning, he and Kat Collins walked off leaving Green and Glass in the hallway.) Moving from the halls, the camera made it's way back to the ring after passing through the fans. A pacing Brian Cage looked like a caged tiger going back and forth waiting for his match with Jake Morgan ... and not one to disappoint
Morgan came running out from the back and hit the ring, coming up throwing
Punches as Daye called for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Morgan was fired up as he came in and staggered Cage, knocking him back across the ring ... nothing fancy, just an old fashioned asskicking. Mixing in a few
Bodyslams and a big Powerbomb, Morgan had Cage on the defensive.
For whatever reason, Cage didn't look at all in the fight and was taking a beating both inside the ring and out as Morgan moved the fight to the floor.
Whipping Cage into the ring barrier and ring steps seemed to do all Morgan wanted as he rolled Cage back into the ring and dropped for a count. Daye hit a
2 and a half before Cage got a shoulder up. Obviously, Cage wasn't out of it yet.
Pulling Cage to his feet, Morgan found that his opponent had some life in him afterall. Staging a come back, Cage rocked Morgan with a Face Rake and followed that up with a Boot to the midsection into a Dragon Suplex. Taking a breather,
Cage rolled to the outside and pulled a table out from under the ring and set it up before pulling Morgan to the outside to join him.
He was blocked as he tried to lift Morgan in the air who in turn tried to lift
Cage. Pushing each other away, they resumed their fisticuffs around the ring then going over the barrier into the fans. Cage tossed several chairs at Morgan who grabbed one up and slammed it down over Cage's head. The self proclaimed
Hardcore Icon staggered briefly then let out a roar ... he took Morgan's best chairshot and was still standing.
The second one did the trick. Looking like he was about to shake it off, Cage dropped to the ground. Climbing back over the barrier, Morgan set up the table on the ring apron and ring barrier then went to collect the still downed Cage but was confronted by Cage's unknown companion Sara E. Xavier. Morgan looked bored to tears as Sara pointed an accusing finger at him.
Something she said snapped Morgan to attention as he looked out over the fans who were cheering loudly. With a smile on his face, he grabbed her by the hair and pulled her close before spinning her up onto his shoulder. Dropping down, he planted her into the floor with a Piledriver.
Heading over the barrier and leaving the twitching Sara, Morgan grabbed Cage back across the barrier. Flowing down Cage's head was a small trail of blood.
With a Standing Head Scissors, Morgan attempted to lift a struggling Cage up but was unable to. Lifting up, Cage sent Morgan over with a Back Body Drop onto the hard floor. Seeing Sara still on the floor sent Cage into a rage.
After throwing the table on top of Morgan, he climbed onto the ring apron and threw his body off and spun into a Senton on top of the table and Morgan, cracking it. Holding his arm, Cage kicked the table off of Morgan before climbing back onto the apron.
Morgan's enemy of the past few weeks, Drake Russell strolled casually out to the ring. As he neared the ring, he grabbed up a chair which caught Cage's attention as Morgan struggled to get back to his feet. Cage came nearer to
Russell wanting to know his intentions. Hefting the chair, Russell pointed at
Morgan with it then motioned to Cage to finish off the #1 Contender. Warily,
Cage went back to the apron and prepared to leap off and hit Morgan. Just as
Cage took flight, Russell struck, hitting the flying man with the chair and was caught and Powerslammed by Morgan onto the floor.
Out of a staredown, Russell turned and left the stunned fans and the ring area for the back.
Shaking his head, Morgan pulled Cage into the ring and laid him across the middle ropes, leaving his head and arms hanging out of the ring. Climbing the corner ropes, Morgan jumped off and landed a Guillotine Leg Drop. Rolling Cage from the apron back into the ring, he dropped down for the pin.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Morgan silently left the ring and made his way to the back when the camera broke away and focussed on Aaron Starr and Lisa Doyle walking in the back.)
<Aaron Starr> You know Lisa, I'm really starting to get over with the fans ... (Lisa's eyes rolled back.) The fans love me ... even all the guys in the back think I'm bigtime ... even the ladies think I'm something special ... in fact, I think I could have any one of them ... if I wanted them ...
(Lisa Doyle grimaced as the camera left them and focussed in on Kat Collins in one of the dressing rooms. She looked relaxed as she leaned back against the wall.)
<Kat Collins> Everything's going according to plan but what's next? (Collins seemed to be talking to herself as no one else was visible. A woman's voice replied.)
<Voice> Now we wait ... (Kat nodded her head in agreement as the camera moved around trying to find the person she's talking to. Moving, the camera focussed in on a mirror and a reflection ... the reflection of a masked face ... the mask of the Offspring
...
On the move once again, the camera focussed in on referee Peter Daye looking around in the back hallways of the studio. In place, he looked one they then turned back towards the other. Satisfied that no one in view, he reached back into his pocket and pulled forth a small metallic flask. Uncapping it, he looked down and lifted the falsk in a salute before taking a swig of it's contents. Wiping his mouth on his sleeve, he closed the flask and returned it to it's hiding place before walking off.
Staying there, the camera pointed downwards after a groan came from the floor. On the ground was a nearly unconcious "Electric Referee" Dan Glass. Fading out on Glass, the camera moved to another part of the back where Perris was talking to Drake Russell. Perris was doing all the talking while Russell listened intently.)
<Perris> You need to start acting like a Champion ... (Russell looked confused as Perris continued.) Don't you worry about the extras like Darlene ... concentrate on Flint and beating Morgan ... let me handle Darlene. Your only concerns should be inside that ring ... tonight go beat Neo's ass ... and next up Flint's hide ...
(Perris marched off leaving a confused Drake Russell standing there as the camera shifted back to the ring where a lone Neo Draven was standing pulling on the ropes and loosening up before the start of the match.
Perris, then Drake Russell burst from the back and made their way to the ring. Referee Peter Daye took the belt and showed it to Russell who seemed not to be too interested in it as he waved off the ref. Handing off the belt tot he outside, Daye called for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** With a nod, the two men locked up in the center of the ring. Russell took control with a Headlock and took Neo to the mat. Daye watched closely for a choke while Neo did his best to try to break free, bouncing from one side to the other trying to bring both men to their feet. Several times, he got back to his feet but was pushed right back down each time.
Wrenching in the Side Headlock, Russell brought Neo back to his feet and sent him into the far ropes. On the return, Neo was taken to the mat with a Shoulder
Butt from the bigger Russell. Following with a Somersault Leg Drop, Russell hit nothing but mat as Neo rolled out of the way.
Kipping to his feet, Neo's Drop Kick to the face hit squarely. Dropping across
Russell, Neo got a 1 count from Daye.
Throwing himself back against the ropes, Neo came in and took Russell down with a running Head Scissors. Jumping up onto the ropes, Neo flipped backwards with a Moonsault and missed his target.
Back on his feet, Russell pushed Neo into the corner and began hammering away with a series of Right Hands while standing on the middle ropes. Dropping down,
Russell Whipped Neo into the far corner and followed in with his Shoulder lowered.
A Snap Suplex put Neo down which was followed by a Knee Drop off the middle ropes out of the corner. Russell dropped for a cover but found no one counting.
Getting back to his feet, he found Peter Daye arguing with Kimberly Davis who had made her way to the ring and was standing on the apron.
Grabbing Daye by the shoulder, he spun the ref around to give him a piece of his mind but was rolled up. Daye hit the mat twice before Russell kicked out.
Both men were on their feet circling when Skip Westervelt and Chip rushed the ring looking for a fight.
The two men attacked Neo, totally ignoring Drake Russell, hammering him into a corner. Waiting for them to finish, Russell walked around the ring shaking his head as Westervelt and his partner put the boots to Neo. Peter Daye tried to step in between them but was pushed to the mat.
Joining them in the ring was Brian Cage who slid in under the ropes and pushed them out of the way before taking up their place and Stomping the downed Neo
Draven.
Still shaking his head, Russell came in and tagged Brian Cage then Skip and
Chip with a Right Hand. Offering a hand to Neo Draven, Russell helped him to his feet which touched off a brawl in the ring.
Russell and Neo Double Hip Tossed Cage as well as Skip and Chip which was followed up by a Moonsault by the Television Champion on Skip. After sending
Chip careening into the corner, they Whipped Skip into him. Cage came on and hit Neo before being grabbed from behind and put down by a Drake Russell
Suplex. Having taken care of the intruders, the two wrestlers eyed each other when Jake
Morgan slipped into the ring and bashed Neo in the face with his own Title Belt knocking him to the canvas.
With Neo down, Morgan and Russell went eye-to-eye and were having some words. Morgan had a smirk on his face as he stepped back while Russell continued to talk, thrusting a finger into Morgan's chest. Looking down at Russell's hand,
Morgan smacked him in the face with the belt, staggering Russell. Grabbing him,
Morgan lifted him into the air and put him down with a Face First Powerbomb.
Grabbing Russell and dragging him on top of Neo Draven, Morgan pushed Daye out of the way and called for a mic as he left the ring.
<Jake Morgan> Destiny had nothing to do with you wining that title Drake. It wasn't your superior skills. It was me. I GAVE you that belt!
Before making his way to the back, he motioned for Daye to make a count. Dropping to the canvas, Daye slapped out a 3 count which brought a smile to
Morgan's face. On the apron, a frantic Kimberly Davis caught Daye's attention.
She was pointing down at Neo Draven's foot on the ropes. Seeing this, Daye waved his arms calling off his 3 count and continuing the match.
Morgan's jaw dropped but was clobbered from behind by a chair from Brian Cage. Skip Westervelt and Chip got involved once again on the outside of the ring, setting up a table before attacking Cage.
Lifting him up, they put Cage through the table which had the fans going crazy from the action. Inside the ring, Drake Russell was holding his head as he pulled himself up. Seeing the Title Belt on the mat, Russell slid out of the ring and staggered to the back ignoring the battle going on outside the ring.
With Russell disappearing into the back, referee Peter Daye called for the bell. *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (After Kimberly Davis left the ring and ran to the back, the camera followed the battle that was going on around the ring as the fans were chanting "Holy shit!" with Cage's Suicide Dive with a chair onto Chip, badly busting him open.
Around the ring, Skip Westervelt and Jake Morgan were exchanging Punches while
Cage splintered another table, throwing Chip through it. The camera zoomed in on the bloody face of Chip before going off the air.
