PAW — Card Archive

2.4 Card

4 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 4 —

(A lone Aaron Starr walks out from the back with a big grin as the cheering begins from the small but loud studio audience. Making his way around the ring,
Starr slaps hands as many fans as possible before stepping between the ropes. Once inside the ring, the smiling host waves them to quiet with a cutting motion across his throat.)
Aaron Starr: We’ve got a great show for you this week but before we begin, let me pass along some news that many of you have already heard earlier in the week on the Hotline.
Three of our finest will be making the jump to the big time as Continental Superstars Wrestling has come calling for Jason Flint, Kid Gannon and the ... (The public address system kicks on, a loud hiss issuing from it, before the unmistakable voice of Dexter Holland croons out…
“Midnight, no sleep …Inside… you SCREAM, too!” With this outburst, the quick, pulsing sounds of Nirvana’s “Even in his Youth” fill the arena as former PAW wrestling competitor, former Extreme Wrestling, Black Cat, World Of Professional Wrestling champion the Offspring begins the long walk to the ring from the entrance area. Upon seeing the braided “freak” moving at a slow gate decked in a pair of black carpenter jeans, black sneakers and an unbuttoned black denim shirt with an oddly familiar twin N logo embroidered in purple on the left breast, his hair flopped down over his face and his familiar mask apparently left in the back yet his face still mysteriously hidden by his hair ... some of the fans, one might even presume from the noise the majority of the fans, cheered loudly before an isolated chant of “please don’t go, please don’t go!” broke
out near the ring. Hobbling slightly, the Offspring leaned out to one fan, holding a purple sign with a crude but earnest drawing of the young grappler on it, and gave a quick high five and thumbs up before rolling under the ring ropes and stepping into the middle of the ring.
As the young luchadore stood in the middle of the ring, looking over the crowd, the music looped over, the relatively short track starting over. The
‘Spring produced a microphone from his back pocket, and running a thumb across his throat, he indicated for his music to stop. Immediately, the track stopped, just as the tortured voice of Kurt Cobain warbled out “I’ve got nothing left to prove …” The Offspring’s head darts toward the PA system, cocking to one side.)
Offspring: Shit! Did someone who works for PAW just actually pay attention to me? (Most of the crowd seems confused by this comment, for it is far from what anyone expected. At least one fan understands, however, as a loud voice screams out, “This place is so FUCKED without you guys!” The young man nods and one can only imagine the smile forming on his lips, somewhere in the darkness.)
As many of you know, the “main event” tonight was supposed to be myself and GD West against the men who claim to run this promotion, the Rising Sons. (The Offspring stops, allowing the crowd to boo. And boo they do.) I meant to tell you two, by the way … great gimmick. The whole son meaning descendant but also being the same word as that red orb on your snot ra… I mean flag. I’m sure that everyone in the audience was really impressed during the one point three seconds it took all of them to realize how punny you were trying to be.
But … that’s not to be. Instead you’ll all be watching as the Street Soldier, Justin Hart, takes my place. And I wish Justin and Mr. West luck, but whatever they do, I’m sure nothing will change. Lonny Nakamura’s charges will continue their political bullshit backstage and through whatever means is necessary, you’ll get to see the Rising Sons proclaiming victory after victory. Of course Nagasaki lost his title to the “paper” champion he so often debunks but that’s not positive Lonny spin. So don’t think about what the man who LOST to a paper champion must be. Don’t take your logic that far. It won’t serve you here.
But again, that’s not why I’m here. As I am sure some of you know, my bags have been packed, AGAIN, by this promotion.
(The crowd boos once again.) It’s tru… (He stops, shaking his head. “too far,” he mumbles under his breath. “Where did that even come from?”) I will be moving on to what the promotion bills as bigger and better things. I will be ... hopefully soon ... debuting in Continental Superstars Wrestling.
And while I appreciate the opportunity that Mr. Brian Banks is offering me and will do my very best to show the fans in CSW what the Extreme Luchadore is all about, it means I will have to leave without actually doing what I set out to do.
I came back for all of you. I came back to show you that what I told you all two years ago, what I came out here and bled, came out here and broke bones and ripped muscles, came out here and took shot after shot from anyone and everyone that came out of the little house that started as this promotion’s headquarters, everyone who came wandering into town and everyone who came walking down that aisle was true.
(With his free hand the Offspring waves about to the crowd, seeming to be inviting them to come forward into the ring. No one does, of course.)
We had something. And we still do. I’m leaving … (Another loud chorus of boos.) … but if you all look inside yourselves, the spirit of Extreme Wrestling, the spirit that formed PAW, the spirit of the best fans in the state of Texas if not on the face of the Earth, is still there. You don’t need me to come out here and scream, “La Raza!” You don’t need me to proclaim another round of the “Sudden, Violent Revolution.”
I told the competitors and administration here, as well as all of you, something back when I held the championship that defined wrestling in
Denton, Texas. I sat at the top of a ladder and I told all of you that even if I fell ... even if the Offspring was to be destroyed ... the spirit would live on in each and every one of you. And it does.
(The ‘Spring pauses, obviously overpowered by the moment.) I came back here hoping I could save you … that I could remind you … of what you had. And I thought, until this week, that I’d failed miserably. I didn’t manage to finish my business with Nagasaki and Morgan, I didn’t manage to win any glory for those of you who stood behind me … all I did was come to the ring and inadvertently eliminate your newfound hero ... who is going with me, it seems, Jason Flint from having a title shot, then get my ass handed to me by two men, a manager, a baby, thirteen tiny elves and
Captain Crunch. (A few laughs from the fans, who seem to realize that though he is leaving them, some of the old Offspring,a man they had forgotten, had returned.)
It wasn’t really about me helping you find anything, though. The Sudden, Violent Revolution lives on, though it may have a different name and may live on in people who don’t claim it as their own. But it lives in Justin Hart, in
Drake Russell, in the Trashman. It’s here, all around you. It lives in the fans of Denton, Texas. (A loud pop. The Offspring claps himself, tapping the microphone as he does so.) So even though I fail, I succeed. I leave you all with this message. This is NOT over. Fuck the people backstage. Fuck Lonny Nakamura. Fuck that bitch who thinks she can parade around Denton wearing MY face. Fuck Jake Morgan and Hiroshima Nagasaki. Fuck the politics of PAW … I’m out of here. (The Offspring climbs onto the second turnbuckle, raising his arms in mock triumph.) Thank you for your forgiveness, Denton. It won’t be forgotten. (With that the Offspring tosses the microphone into the center of the ring and leaves, as only seems appropriate, through the crowd, vanishing among the college students in the middle of the audience.)

— Part 2 of 4 —

(The camera opened on Justin Hart at a table in the back, deep in thought. Here he sat, almost motionless oblivious to the movement around him ... until GD
West appeared out of nowhere, slamming his fist down on the table, attempting to get a response out of Hart, who simply looked up.)
GD West: All right, boy, lissen here. Normally I wouldn't do something like this but as it appears, I've been appointed your babysitter in this match ...
(Hart shot him an icy stare.) I plan on teaching those glory boys of the Orient the value of a good, sound whuppin and I don't need to have to bail you out of every situation that you get yourself into. I KNOW I can handle myself, hell I could probably do a better job in this match on my own! But I'm not. So what I'm saying is this, pull your weight ... or I drop you like dead weight.
Got it? (Pushing away from the table, Hart circled until coming face to face with West.) Justin Hart: Now listen to me, "puppy-dog." You may talk tough to Morgan and Nagasaki but as far as I'm concerned, you're about as tough as Scooby-Doo on Ny-Quil. If I have to rely on you to fight my battles, the world'll come to an end. I was fine before you got here and I'll be better when you leave. So my suggestion to you is to get through this match and shut that biscuit-eating mouth of yours ...
(West shot an arm forward, grabbing Hart by the shirt, pushing him back into the wall, until he was suddenly shoved back. Hart growled, ready for action until West let out a chuckle.
GD West: Those boys are in a lot of trouble. (Hart flashed him a smile as the camera faded away to a woman standing in front of the mirror “fixing her face”. Moving in closer, Lisa Doyle can be seen in the reflection of the mirror from behind as she applies lipstick when suddenly the lights go out bathing her in total darkness startling the young woman.)
Lisa Doyle: Damn! (The sounds of a door opening is heard, again startling the woman.) Hello? Anyone there? (Following familiar maniacal laughter, Doyle let out a blood curdling scream realizing she wasn’t alone. The door burst open and and the lights came back on finding Drake Russell with trusty Singapore Cane in hand.)
Drake Russell: Lisa?!?!?! (Doyle rushed into Russell’s arms before cutting away to the backstage area where Jason Flint and Kimberly Davis were quietly enjoying a lunch together.)
Kimberly Davis: So, when do you leave? Jason Flint: A couple of days. Kimberly Davis: It'll be strange without you here. Jason Flint: I know, it’s no walk in the park for me either. (An irate Jake Morgan came stomping through, eyeing Flint and stopping to point a finger at the former champion.)
Jake Morgan: You ... Jason Flint: Yes? (Flint stands up to Morgan, who pokes him in the chest.) Jake Morgan: Big man. Going to the bigs. What are you now too fucking good for PAW? (Flint curls a smile.) Jason Flint: No, just you ... (Morgan sneered at Flint and reared back when Flint shot his hands up in front of him.) Let me just remind you, Jake ... I no longer work here, I'm a guest of Kimberly. You strike me, I’ll see to it that you and Hart will be swapping spit in the Pen for assault.
Understand? I’d hate to see you chalk up another loss to me, since you've NEVER beaten me in the ring. (Morgan's face turns beet red as he storms off down the hall.) Jake Morgan: FUCK YOU! (Flint turns and waves, smiling.) Jason Flint: Remember jackass, YOU NEVER COULD BEAT ME! (Turning back to Davis, he sits down.) Now ... where were we? (The couple faded away as large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession.) .... 5 .... .... 4 .... .... 3 .... .... 2 .... .... 1 .... (White Zombie's "More Human than Human" played in the background as a bloody faced Jake Morgan taking a chair over the head from Justin Hart flashed quickly by.
With a quick burst, a close up of Morgan’s face at the time of impact was frozen on the screen as a woman’s seductive voice chimed in.)
Voice: MMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMM ... I just love the Pain ... (Morgan’s face disappeared and was replaced by Drake Russell slamming a shoulder into one of the Black Ravens, knocking him to the mat before throwing himself back against the ropes and using the momentum to land a somersault senton.
The landing was frozen in time as Russell lands on his opponent.) I just love the Action ... (Russell was replaced by the team of the Rising Sons as Morgan pushed the Offspring up the ropes and onto his partner’s shoulders. From the top turnbuckle, Hiroshima Nagasaki sat down into a super powerbomb, holding for a pin attempt.
Nagasaki’s maniacal look was flash framed as he held the hapless Offspring in place.) I just love Wrestling ... the way it was meant to be ... (Nagasaki was replaced by Television Champion Drake Russell proudly holding his belt aloft in the center of the ring.)
PUBLIC ACCESS WRESTLING! (With an explosion, the PAW logo spun into view before fading away, to be replaced by the small television studio and the fifty or so cheering fans surrounding the ring on three sides. Gone are the yellow and red PAW banners bearing the visage of Hiroshima Nagasaki having been replaced by the more familiar red, black and white ones.
Moving through the small crowd, the camera zooms in on Aaron Starr and broadcast partner, Ishimoto Sakai.]
Aaron Starr: Welcome! Aaron Starr here, as we head into our second month of 2001! With me as usual ... my broadcast partner, Ishimoto Sakai. (The smiling Sakai took a curt bow.) Big happenings this week, Ish as Public Access Wrestling enters a new era with three of our old favorites leaving us for the Continental Superstars Wrestling promotion ...
Ish Sakai: A new era which will be the showcasing of the true champions of this promotion in the Rising Sons ... a pair of men who will be finally recognized for their excellence inside the ring ...
(The fans booed loudly bringing a smile to Starr’s face.) Aaron Starr: I don’t think they agree with your “excellence” there, Ish ... (A heavier dose of booing turned both announcers’ heads to find manager of Team Japan, Lonny Nakamura making his way from the back.) What now? (The agitated Nakamura wasn’t afraid to exchange words with the fans as he made a beeline for Starr and Sakai.)
Lonny Nakamura: This is an outrage! First, the remodeling of the studio was done without my knowledge. Second, you want to talk about three men being run out of this promotion?
Aaron Starr: Run out? Now see here ... Lonny Nakamura: Jason Flint and the Offspring can barely walk ... forget about proving themselves inside the ring against superior wrestlers as the Rising
Sons ... And as for Kid Gannon, I think we can seperate the men from the boys ... If you want to talk about wrestlers Aaron Starr ... you need to address the Rising Sons and the rest of Team Japan. The very people that will carry this promotion upon their backs if need be!
(Nakamura waved off the boos which quickly turned to cheers as Justin Hart walked out from the back. Hart had a grin on his face as he slowly made his way towards Nakamura who backpeddled out of the way.
Nearing Nakamura, Hart made a gesture with his hand imitating a mouth. A chant of “Kick his ass!” went up bringing a bigger grin from Hart. Nonchalantly holding a hand out, Hart took the mic from Starr.)
Justin Hart: Let’s talk about seperating the men from the boys ... let’s talk about carrying anything on their backs, Nakamura. Alone or together, your boys are going to have one angry Sonnuvabitch breathing down their necks ...
Now, without the Flints and the Offsprings around, that means there’s a bigger piece of ass-kicking coming from yours truly ... and today is just a little taste of a whole lotta ass stomping that’s gonna be happening in that ring!
(Hart handed the mic back to Starr before grabbing Nakamura by the lapels. The cheers quickly turned to boos as Hiroshima Nagasaki and Jake Morgan walked out from the back, heading for Hart.
Pushing their manager away, Hart folded his arms across his chest and waited. Nagasaki pulled Nakamura out of the way while Morgan jawed with the fans. The studio became even more crowded as the masked woman followed by Kimberly Davis and Fredrick A. Gibson made their way out from the back.) Masked Woman: Well ... isn’t this a pretty sight. It seems that today we have several unhappy people who can’t play together. First, we have the Offspring and his potty mouth and now we have all of you who won’t play nice together
... What am I to do? I’ll tell you what I’m going to do. (Turning on her heel, she thrust an accusing finger at Lonny Nakamura.) There will be fines ... (Nakamura smiled as he slipped his hand inside his sports coat.) ... AND ... suspensions! (An irate Nakamura had to be held back as the fans cheered.) There will be no more attacks on my people. Is that clear? Next one ... and your “boys” will be sitting on the sidelines watching all the “fun”.
Now, get out of here ... (Hart laughed as Morgan and Nagasaki pulled Nakamura into the back before disappearing from view. Turning, the masked woman pointed at Ishimoto Sakai.)
And you ... with the National Wrestling Council falling through, I no longer need to carry two announcers ... therefore you’re expendable.
(Sakai’s eyes went wide and the audience burst into laughter.) I hear one more tired prison joke ... you’re gone ... (Starr grinned wide as Sakai swallowed deeply before sitting down at the announcers’ table. Not missing a thing, the masked woman looked at Starr.)
Mr. Starr? Something funny? (Starr’s head snapped around, looking at the boss. Shaking his head vigorously, he quickly took his seat. Turning around, she looked straight at the last remaining person ... Justin Hart.)
And finally, Mr. Hart ... (Hart crossed his arms and looked uninterested as the woman leveled a finger at him.) You seem to have a problem. It seems like every other week, you are being carted off in handcuffs for something you’ve done ... this last time for doing something to the property of not one of your opponents ... but that of one of my people ... Mr. Fredrick A. Gibson ...
And that my friend, is a no-no ... But because you were “duped”, Mr. Gibson helped alleviate the financial burden upon your friends and family by helping with your bail.
(Hart looked over at the smiling Gibson who waved at the wrestler.) Now, in order to pay back Mr. Gi bson who so generously bailed your ass out of jail, I think a little lesson in humility is in order here ...
(Hart’s eyes narrowed as he awaited his punishment.) You, for the time being will become Fredrick’s Assistant Guy ... (Hart’s jaw dropped while Gibson wrung his hands together and smiled. The fans howled as Hart shook his head. Nodding here head, the masked woman led her assitants away, stopping to tell Hart one final thing.)
Please, do behave ...

— Part 3 of 4 —

(With her disappearing into the back, the fans chanted “You are screwed!” at the livid Hart. Leaving the ringside area, the camera found Jason Flint in the back with the green haired Toadie who was shaking his head as Flint tried to hand him something.)
Jason Flint: It’s my last day here. Look at it as a going away present ... Toadie: Sorry, no can do. (Flint fished around in his pocket, pulling free some bills.) Jason Flint: How about for five? (Toadie’s eyes lit up as he eyed the green in Flint’s hand.) Toadie: Okay, but I won’t do it for less than three ... (Flint smiled as he peeled off three dollars and handed it to the man along with a cassette tape.)
Jason Flint: You strike a hard bargain. (The camera made it’s way back to ringside and the cheering fans as the Television Champion, Drake Russell made his way to the ring. Inside, an impatient Skip Westervelt paced back and forth.
After removing his long coat and dropping his singapore cane, Russell entered the ring as referee Junior Akatani called for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Locking up in the center of the ring, Westervelt made the first move with a go behind into a waist lock. A back elbow caught Skip in the head as Russell reversed positions, moving from the waist lock of his own into a hammerlock.
Spinning left, then right, Skip looked for an escape. Countering Skip’s movement, Russell took the hammerlock into an arm bar, then a side headlock.
Pushed off, Russell was sent into the far ropes. There was no return as Russell grabbed the ropes.
Locking up again, the two repeated the same holds and escapes. This time, Skip dropped his head for a back body drop on the rebounding Drake Russell only to take a kick to the head. Snapping up, Westervelt was an easy target as the champion grabbed him around the neck and swung around for a spinning neckbreaker.
Making the cover, the ref slapped a 2 count before the challenger popped a shoulder off the mat.
Pulling Skip off the mat, Drake sent him on his way, taking him down off the far ropes with a jump spinning back kick. With Westervelt holding his face in pain, Russell came down across his neck with a leg drop, sitting down for a cover and a 1 count.
Climbing up the ropes, Russell waited for Westervelt to regain his feet before launching himself into the air. Catching Russell’s flying cross body block,
Westervelt spun into a powerslam that shook the ring. With a cover, a disappointed Skip argued with the ref over a 2 count. Not backing away, Akatani held two fingers firmly in Westervelt’s face.
Shaking his head, Westervelt came down with a knee drop before climbing up the ropes. A woobly Russell was back up but not for long as Westervelt landed on his shoulders and spun into a Head Scissor Takedown.
An irish whip sent Russell hard into the corner and was followed by the charging Westervelt, only the champ wasn’t “at home” as he side stepped the charging man.
Slamming into the turnbuckles, Westervelt was spun around before taking some vicious chops to the chest that had the fans howling their approval. With a snap mare out of the corner, Russell followed with a rope assisted splash, landing squarely on Skip.
Pulling Skip to his feet, Russell pulled him forward, bent at the waist, hooking the arms. He released the double underhook as Pep Preston hit the ring to a chorus of boos.
Ducking under Pep’s clothesline, Russell threw himself back against the ropes and shot forward, leaving his feet to land a flying clothesline of his own.
With a whip into the ropes, Pep was thrown high into the air with a back body drop. The outside of the ring didn’t afford Preston an escape as Russell followed to the outside with a plancha that took both men to the floor and brought a cheer from the fans.
A back suplex on the hard floor drew a louder cheer as well as a warning from the ref. Pushing Akatani out of the way, Russell lifted Preston into the air then dropped him down across the ring barrier before rolling him back into the ring.
With Skip Westervelt whipping Preston across the ring, Russell waited on the ring apron before flying into the ring with a slingshot shoulder butt. Pulling
Preston to the center of the ring, Russell and Westervelt criss-crossed the ring before droping elbows onto the prone man.
Another whip into the ropes resulted in a sidewalk slam which was followed by a Westervelt diving headbutt. Pulling the dizzy Preston to his feet, Russell and Westervelt roughly swung him into the air before falling back and planting him facefirst into the canvas.
Akatani was complaining to the two men as they shared a high five. Both Russell and Westervelt argued with Akatani while a loud dose of booing rose from the audience as Hiroshima Nagasaki marched out from the back alone.
As the Rising Son member circled the ring, having words with the fans, he caught the attention of Drake Russell inside.
Nagasaki ignored Russell’s invitation into the ring before drawing another warning from the ref. Turning away from Nagasaki, Russell joined his erstwhile partner across the ring up the ropes. Turning his back on Preston, Westervelt launched himself into the air and landed with a moonsault, rolling to his feet.
Not to be outdone, Russell jumped from his position, spinning his body and landing the Total Decimation and hooking a leg.
Slow to drop to the mat, Akatani slapped the mat ... once ... twice ... then nothing as Hiro Nagasaki pulled the Japanese referee from the ring. With the two men on the outsided arguing, Westervelt dropped to the mat and finished the job, slapping out a 3 count of his own and calling for the bell.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (With the count over, Westervelt pulled Russell back to his feet and spun around, sending the champion across the ring. Nearing the ropes, Russell grabbed hold and flipped over and out of the ring, landing on top of both
Nagasaki and Akatani, taking them to the floor. Both wrestlers were quick to their feet and eyeing the other, ready for more as PAW Security stepped in before anything escalated. Chants of “P-A-DUB!” started as Nagasaki was led away to the back with Pep Preston while Drake
Russell grabbed up his title belt and shook hands with Skip Westervelt before raising his hand into the air and fading from view.
Moving to the back, the camera found one angry Justin Hart pacing in his dressing room. Back and forth he went, stopping to throw a chair out of the way then resuming as it clattered onto the floor.)
Justin Hart: This is all bullshit! Fredrick’s Assistant Guy? What the hell does that mean? (Hart spun around at the knock on his door. Pulling it open, he found Kimberly Davis standing there.) Kimberly Davis: You’re up ... (Shoving her out of the way, Hart stormed out of the room.) Justin Hart: You bet I am ... (With Davis rolling her eyes, the camera moved elsewhere ... to Jeremy Morgan, younger brother of Jake Morgan. Making his way through the back hallways,
Morgan stops when approached by her brother’s former manager, Mean Darlene.) Jeremy Morgan: Come on Darlene ... (Closing her eyes, Darlene shook her head.) Jake’s match is next ... Mean Darlene: No, Jeremy ... your brother made his choice. It’s time for a little “tough love”.
(With Darlene walking off a confused Jeremy watched her go.) Jeremy Morgan: What? (The camera moves back to ringside where the noise rose to a roar when the kodo drums and gong heralded the arrival of the Rising Sons. On the third gong,
Hiroshima Nagasaki and Jake Morgan stepped from the back with Lonny Nakamura in tow. Morgan's smirk is in stark contrast to the determined angry scowl worn by the H-Bomb.
Stopping half way to the ring, the Rising Sons watched their opponents when all of a sudden “Kung Fu Fighting” blasted out over the loudspeakers. Nakamura and the Rising Sons were livid as the fans and their opponents laughed loudly until finally the music stopped.
The angered Nagasaki took a mic from manager Nakamura and glared at the two pacing men inside the ring.)
Hiroshima Nagasaki: There are times when one must bypass the normal channels and take matters into his own hands. Despite having been cheated out of my title and ripped off numerous times after that by the PAW infrastructure, I have yet to do so. UNTIL NOW! I have grown weary waiting on Russell to come out from behind the skirt of our president. I have become sick due to the actions of "referee" Peter Daye. No longer will the Rising
Sons allow anyone in this organization to keep from us what is rightfully ours. As you hide in the back cowering in your locker room, trying to hide behind the belt you KNOW you stole from me, remember that there is no escape from either of us.
Since you will not come to Ground Zero, I will bring Ground Zero to you. (Morgan walked up and took the mic from his partner and pointed an accusing finger at GD West.)
Jake Morgan: Fuck us? No, FUCK YOU! You come in here insulting this promotion, these fans, this city and state, hoping to anger us. Well, shithead, you should have done you're research a little better. I don't give a fuck about these people, this city or this state.
(The booing started up bringing Jake to point at the crowd.) I live in this city. I shop at the same stores that these people do. Drive the same roads, walk the same streets. Do they say "Hi" to me? Do they treat me like they would any other human being?
(He lowers his hand.) No, they call me names like traitor, uncle Tom and scum of the Earth. They FUCKING SPIT on me! I could care less what you say about them or this city. They aren't worthy of my notice or protection. (He begins to shake with fury.) All I care about is destruction. Yours, Hart's, Russell's and most importantly Flint's. He was the one man that I never beat in a singles match. The one man that I felt I had something to prove to but he left. Now I can't beat him, I can't leave his bloody corpse at my feet. I can't see that little Bitch Kim
Davis crying over his broken body. (Lonny places a hand on Jake's shoulder but is quickly and violently shrugged off. Jake turns his rage filled face on his manager, who took a step back before turning back to the ring.)
So here's what I'm going to do. I'm going to hurt everyone who gets in my way until that masked Bitch, brings his scrawny ass back to stop me. That means that you two jackasses are up first.
(He moves his attention to Hart.) You think you're insane? Boy, you're not even close. Those little temper tantrums that you are throwing don't even come close to crossing the line. The shit that I've got planned for you ... that's insane. I've beaten you physically. I've beaten you mentally. Now, I'm going to beat you emotionally.
You see Hart, while there are certain things you won't do, I don't have that problem. Everyone and Everything is fair game. Your car, your everpresent sunglasses, your favorite jacket, and even ... your cousin. Have you talked to him since you left him at the jail?
(Hart nearly leapt over the ropes to get to Jake, but was held back by both the ref and West.)
Go ahead GD, let him go. I'll break his fucking back, then move on to your junkyard dog ass. You think taking the name of a vicious dog is going to scare me? Wrong once again, bitch! I don't scare and I'm afraid of no man or beast.
You talk about what you are going to do, how you're going to hurt us but you do nothing. So quit your barking and try to take a bite out of me. You'll find that I bite back. You may be " the Meanest Motherfucker in the National
Wrestlin' Council," but in case you haven't noticed this isn't the NWC. It's PAW and belt or no belt, I'm the TOP DOG here. (The crowd began to chant "DRAKE.") You said it was time to die? Well, you're right, it's your time! (Jake hit the ring followed by Nagasaki bringing Peter Daye to call for the bell.)

— Part 4 of 4 —

*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding ***
All four men were in the ring with the team of West and Hart taking it to the Rising Sons right off the bat but not allowing their opponents to get to their feet. Hart sent Morgan into the ropes who shot back into the arms of the short but powerful West. With a bellow, West scooped Morgan off his feet for a powerslam.
A boot to the back of his head from Nagasaki ended the celebration but a watchful Hart slammed the Asian Assassin’s face into the mat with a bulldog.
Jumping to his feet Hart rammed an elbow into Morgan’s head, knocking him through the ropes to the apron. With Hart bpooting Morgan to the outside and following, Nagasaki and West were left in the ring to mix it up.
Circling, the two locked up with West getting control and sending Nagasaki into the far ropes. Dropping his shoulder, Nagasaki rammed it into West on the return. Irresistible force hit immovable object with neiter man budging. With a smirk on his face, West motioned for Nagasaki to come again. Throwing himself back into the ropes, Hiro shot forward into the sidestepping West who took him down with a drop toe hold. Rolling Nagasaki over, West worked the leg with a spinning toe hold only to be kicked off.
Charging forward, West knocked Nagasaki back into the corner with a hard clothesline before opening up with popping reverse knife hands. A thumb to the eye stopped his progress, drawing a warning for Nagasaki from Daye.
On the outside of the ring, Morgan and Hart were going at it like men who didn’t like each other which was very much the case. The fans were howling their approval as Hart lifted Morgan off his feet for an atomic drop and a clubbing forearm that knocked the Rising Son off his feet. With Morgan slumping back against the ring barrier, Hart drove a boot into Morgan’s throat.
Inside the ring, a belly-to-belly suplex put West on his back for a 1 count before getting shoulder off of the mat. Pushing the NWC wrestler into the corner, Nagasaki unloaded with several knees to the head as he pulled West’s head down.
The ref’s warning and count ended Nagasaki’s Thai style fighting. Arguing with the ref broke Nagasaki’s attention allowing West to take him down with a clothesline. Crawling to get away, Nagasaki found himself in a cobra clutch as
West looked to put an ending to the match early on. Lonny Nakamura seeing his man Morgan in trouble neared the action on the outside, slamming a kick into the side of Hart’s head. Caught by surprise,
Hart spun around and smiled when he saw Lonny Nakamura standing there. Backpeddling, Nakamura was grabbed and lifted off his feet before being swung around and slammed into the ring railing.
Morgan’s ax handle blow staggered Hart for a knee lift that put him down on the floor. Following a boot stomping, Morgan’s vertical suplex had Hart down for the count.
Unwilling to give up, Nagasaki slowly crawled forward to reach the ropes for a break. Making the break, West waited until Nagasaki stood, wrapping arms around his waist for a german suplex. Rolling back to his feet, West lifted him again and threw Nagasaki around like a ragdoll with a second german suplex.
Ready for a third, West was knocked off his feet by Morgan who rushed into the ring. Daye was in pulling Morgan away as he stomped West into the canvas.
Arguing with the ref, Morgan was spun around and grabbed. Showing his power, West lifted Morgan off his feet with a belly-to-belly overhead suplex. Storming back to his corner, West tagged in Hart who rushed in, taking both Morgan and
Nagasaki off their feet with stiff lariats. Whipping Nagasaki into the far ropes, Hart lifted a boot and drove it into the returning Nagasaki’s face.

With a whip into the ropes, Hart lifted the returning Nagasaki into the air, pressing him over his head before stepping back and dropping the Japanese star across the top rope. Falling back to the mat, Nagasaki was clutching his throat.
Taunting Morgan in his corner, Hart pulled Nagasaki to his feet before taking a back elbow to the head. Grabbing an arm, Nagasaki sunk in an arm bar before a hip toss. With Hart sitting on the mat, Nagasaki drove a knee into the nack of his head then stepped over his arm and rolled his body to the mat and putting his opponent’s shoulders down. Daye slapped out a 2 count before asking for a break as Hart grabbed the ropes.
Pushing Hart into his corner, Nagasaki’s stiff chops were followed by boots to the midsection while partner Morgan held Hart in place. Daye warned Morgan and called for a break out of the corner. Making a tag, he lifted Hart into the air and held him while Morgan climbed the ropes.
Diving over the top of Hart, Morgan’s neck snap neckbreaker had the man down and seemingly out. Motioning for West to come in, Morgan pushed the ref in front of him to block the incoming rottweiler.
Grabbing Hart’s legs, Morgan sat down into a leg drop that brought a groan from the male fans in the studio. On the outside of the ring, Nakamura was busy shouting instructions to the Rising Sons.
With Morgan sitting in place, Nagasaki was into the match with a slingshot leg drop of his own, rolling to the outside before being seen. West was complaining as Morgan hammered away with forearms and uppercuts before making a quick tag.
With a whip into the ropes, Morgan caught Hart on the return and lifted him into the air, spinning around and dropping him with a spinebuster slam. Out of the corner, Nagasaki was back into the match with the H-Bomb, making the cover for a 2 count. Hart still had some fight left in him.
Pulled to his feet, Hart took a double thrust to the throat before being put down with a leg drag and taking an elbow drop. Rolling back to his feet,
Nagasaki pumped his arms into the air bringing the boobirds out from the small but enthusiastic crowd. A tag brought Morgan in for a double snap suplex.
Pulled to his feet, Hart was spun through the air with a short arm clothesline. Making his way to the apron, Morgan flipped off some fans before shooting back into the ring using the ropes for a slingshot leg drop. The only problem ...
Hart rolled out of the way at the last second. Staggering back to his feet, Hart took a boot to the back, pushing him forward into the tag with his partner. West was in like a shot, charging forward like a rampaging beast. Flying fists backed Morgan up before being lifted off his feet and planted with a Front Suplex. With a stepover toehold, West’s cobra clutch combo brought a smile to his face as he locked on his patented WeSTF. Daye was watching closely for a submission as Jake fought it.
Twice Daye was diverted as Nagasaki ran in to break things, both times stopped short of his goal. Luckily for the Rising Sons, they weren’t alone as Lonny
Nakamura got into the act and grabbed West’s foot from the outside and pulled him from his grasp. Hart was in the ring, pulling Nakamura into the ring as
Daye tried desperately to keep Nagasaki out. Pushing Nakamura into the air, Hart’s gorilla press slam shook the ring as Nagasaki broke free from the ref and joined the fight, hammering Hart and knocking him out of the ring.
Arguing with the ref, West pulled Morgan to his knees before taking a low blow that caught him off guard. Rolling to the outside, Morgan pulled West out to touch off another brawl with all four men involved.
Hart and Nagasaki exchanged blows while Morgan slammed West into the ring barrier and steps, busting him open. Leaving the bleeding wrestler, Morgan joined his team mate to double team Hart.
Driving a knee into his back, Morgan hoisted Hart into the air for a brainbuster onto the concrete while Nagasaki pulled a table from underneath the ring. Morgan grabbed a chair away from a fan and smashed it over the now bloody
Hart’s head before dumping him onto the table while Nagasaki climbed up onto the apron before leaping off and splashing Hart through it.
With the fans booing loudly, Morgan turned right into West’s kick to the face that put him down. After helping Hart to his feet, they rolled Nagasaki back into the ring. Despite Daye’s protests, the two worked Nagasaki over with a series of double team moves, hitting with double clotheslines off a double whip into the ropes, following up with a spiked piledriver. West dropped for the cover but was pulled to the outside of the ring by Morgan on the 2 count.
Without hesitation, Hart ran off and dove to the outside, taking Morgan to the floor. He and West whipped Morgan into the ring barrier before Lonny Nakamura was back in on the act with a kick to Hart. Grabbed by the hair, Nakamura was wobbled by a right cross. Leading Nakamura around the ring, Hart grabbed a chair and set it up on the floor, slamming Nakamura’s face into the seat before holding his down and prompting his partner to “give it to him”.
Nagasaki intervened, smacking Hart and allowing Nagasaki to slither away. Hammering away at West, Nagasaki hefted him before sitting down into the Nagasaki Driver on top of the chair and crushing it. Rolling West into the ring, Nagasaki body slammed him then dragged him across the ring and laid him throatfirst across the middle rope. With a running start,
Nagasaki landing sitting on West’s back, using the ropes to bounce up and down, driving West down into the rope. With a threatening gesture, Nagasaki broke the hold on the ropes.
Spinning West around for the Oriental spike, Nagasaki was knocked off his feet as Hart drove a shoulder into his back. After a boot stomping Hart helped West to his feet before pushing Nagasaki into the corner in a seated position. from across the ring, West charged forward, slamming his knee into Nagasaki’s face.
Hart wiped blood from his face as he pulled Nagasaki up, leaning him in the corner for the Hang Time ... and hit it he did. Stepping back, he gave Nagasaki all the room he needed to fall flat on his face. Pulling Nagasaki up, West pushed him up the ropes for a Tornado DDT.
Rolling Nagasaki to his back, West barked out at him before slamming himself back against the ropes ... a waiting Jake Morgan pulled them down, low bridging the muscular wrestler and sending him tumbling out of the ring.
Turning to see West take the spill to the outside, Hart started to leave the ring when he was pulled to the mat with a school boy roll-up by Nagasaki. With
Daye dropping to slap out a count, Nagasaki grabbed a handful of tights and held on for dear life. 1 ... 2 ... 3 ... it was over.

Morgan down. Nagasaki’s kick from behind seperated West from Morgan, allowing his partner to pull the downed man away.
