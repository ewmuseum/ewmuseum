King of Hardkore 2001

1 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello ... I run Public Access Wrestling. Some of you might know me, most of you don't ... and I am sure a few of you wish I just dropped dead. Anyway ... I am here to announce our second Annual King of Hardkore Tournament. What I am looking for are individuals that might be interested in getting involved in our first big event of this year.
Last year's big event, Winter's Nightmare: PAW vs Japan was a big success as well as a lot of fun for everyone involved ... for the handlers in PAW as well as those wrestlers who came in from other feds (Japan Puroresu Association, the
NNEW, etc.) to participate.
I am looking to do something similar, bringing in wrestlers from other promotions as well as independent guys who might be interested in our little hardcore tournament. I'm a looking for wrestlers from smaller promotions as a first choice, moving up to the larger promotions, until all the spots are filled.
I don't have any more details other than the event will take place in the first half of March as I am putting out feelers to see who might be interested in participating.
The only people I won't be taking are those who were involved in our last event as I would like to involve different wrestlers (and handlers).
Thanks Brian Public Access Wrestling http://www.geocities.com/perrishex/paw.html
