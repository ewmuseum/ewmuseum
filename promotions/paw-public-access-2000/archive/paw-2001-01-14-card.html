PAW — Card Archive

1.14 Card

14 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 4 —

(A lone Aaron Starr walks out from the back and makes his way into the ring before the small but wildly cheering crowd of fans. Slapping hands across the ring barrier, Starr makes his way around the ring before climbing inside.
Stepping between the ropes, Starr moves to the center of the ring with a smile on his face.)
Aaron Starr: Hello, wrestling fans! I’m glad you could all make it to this week’s television taping!
(Starr waves off the cheering fans to continue.) As you can see, I’m out here alone this week after last week’s heinous actions of Team Japan’s Hiroshima Nagasaki and his brutal attack on my cohost, Lisa Doyle ...
(The cheering turns quickly to booing as again, Starr waves them to quiet.) I’m happy to report that Lisa, though still sore, is doing just fine and will be back with us next week.
Now, I’m not sure what is going to come out of this attack but I am pretty sure some heavy fines will be levied and some action will be brought to bear from the promotion’s front office ...
(A chant of “P-A-DUB!” starts up as the fans jump to their feet. Looking around, Starr spies the PAW Television Champion, Drake Russell emerge from the back and make his way to the ring.)
And ... if not by the front office ... then by this man! (Russell makes his way into the ring, throwing his title belt over his shoulder as he shakes hands with Starr.)
Drake Russell! Thanks for joining us champ ... (Russell nods his head as he looks out to the cheering audience and the few whistles blown his way.)
Now, you and I have not seen eye to eye in the past ... and maybe I was a little hasty in my comments ...
(Adjusting his belt over his shoulder, Russell whips his dark glasses off his face and glares at Starr who takes a half step backwards.)
Drake Russell: Aaron ... let’s let the past be just that. What I want to say is that Lisa says hello and wishes she could be here for tonight’s taping but took the doctor’s advice and stayed home.
Aaron Starr: That’s good to hear. What took place last week was just awful ... a heinous attack by a supposedly honorable man in Hiroshima Nagasaki ... all because he feels slighted in you taking the belt from him ... (Russell shakes his head as he brings a finger to his lips, quieting Starr.) Drake Russell: Shhhhhhhhhhh ... Aaron, let’s let the past be the past. I don’t want to talk about last week. What’s done is done.
(A surprised Starr stammers on his words.) Aaron Starr: But ... but ... but ... Drake Russell: It’s a done deal, Aaron ... what happened to Lisa is over. It’s done with. I can never undo what happened to her. (Russell turns from Starr and looks out to the fans.) Nagasaki delivered a message. He wrote it ... he delivered it with his mark stamped upon it’s face ...
(Russell taps the belt on his shoulder.) All for what? For this ... for this leather strap and it’s metal plates. All for this belt.
(Russell turns back to Starr and slaps him heavily upon the shoulder.) You delivered your message and opened my eyes, Nagasaki. It worked. You got my attention just like you wanted ... only you’re not going to like my reply!
(Pulling his glasses out from inside his floor length coat, Russell puts them back on before leaving the ring and heading to the back following his cryptic message.
With Russell’s name being chanted in the background, Starr can only nod his head up and down until the champion disappears into the back.)
Aaron Starr: The PAW Television Champion with a word of warning for the #1 Contender, Hiroshima Nagasaki. (With the chanting dying down, Starr smiles as he continues in the center of the ring.) Ladies and gentlemen, we were supposed to have a match between two up and coming lightweights between Kidd Gannon and Suicide Youth but a no-show tonight is going to prevent that from happening ...
(Starr nods his head in agreement with the loudly booing fans when suddenly ZZ Top’s “La Grange” starts to blare over the PA system and a smiling Kidd Gannon steps out from the back. Walking out, Kidd stops and points with his right hand towards the fans. Shooting his left out in the same direction, Gannon suddenly punches both arms over his head, crossing them in an “X”. Slicing downward with his arms brings the music to a half before he takes off and slides in under the ring ropes.
Bouncing off the ropes, Kidd went up the near corner bringing the fans into a cheering frenzy before throwing his body back, flipping out of the corner and landing on his feet. Turning to join Starr in the middle of the ring, Kidd waves at the fans.)
Thanks for joining me, Kidd ... unfortunately, we won’t be joined by Suicide Youth for your match ... Kidd Gannon: Aaron ... it’s good to be here. You know, my family name ... as much as it is revered in the sport of professional wrestling ... it’s also my bane?
Aaron Starr: Kidd? Kidd Gannon: That’s right, Arron ... it’s not the wonderful thing you ... these fans or anyone else out there might think it is ... In fact, in time, it might prove to more of an anchor around my throat ... a hinderance more than a help.
Aaron Starr: How so? Kidd Gannon: Just like tonight, my family name is going to chase away opponent after opponent regardless of what I can do inside the squared circle ...
(Reaching into the pocket of his jeans, he pulls out a waded ball. Unfurling it, he smiles as he holds the mask of the Suicide Youth in his hands. Holding it up to his face, he loudly blows his nose into it. Grimacing as he takes a peek at the contents of his nostrils, he quickly balls it up and slaps it into the front pocket of his unbuttoned sleeveless shirt.)
This Aaron, is just the first in a long line of opponents who would rather take the high road and run for the hills rather than face one of the Gannon Clan ...
(Kidd wrings his hands together when he is greeted by a loud chorus of boos. A surprised Gannon looks around to find Lonny Nakamura, the manager of Team Japan making his way into the ring.
Snatching the mic away from Starr, a furious Nakamura paces the ring trying to quiet the booing but having very little luck.)
Lonny Nakamura: How dare you waste the time of true wrestling fans with this ... this ... pathetic excuse of a wrestler. No one wants to see you wrestle ... and certainly no one wants to hear you talk. Oh no, what these fans want to hear about ... what they want to see is true wrestlers like Hiroshima Nagasaki and Jake Morgan ...
What they want is wrestling excellence and precision ... (Nakamura stops as the fans let loose with the booing.) SHUT YOUR MOUTHES!!!!! (This only makes the booing louder. Shaking his head, Kidd grabs Nakamura and spins him around, grabbing away the mic and handing it back to Starr. The surprised Nakamura backpeddles from the young fist wieldling Gannon.
A chant of “Kick His Ass!” starts up as Gannon grabs Nakamura by the hair as he tries to flee the ring. Spinning Nakamura around, Kidd sends him off into the far ropes, taking him down with a Spinning Heel Kick. Bouncing to his feet,
Gannon climbs the ropes and celebrates with the cheering fans as referee Peter Daye hits the ring and calls for the bell.) *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Pulling Nakamura up by the hair, Kidd shakes his head and wags a finger at the manager after a failed Low Blow that is caught between his legs. Pulling the begging Nakamura to his feet, Kidd rears back and drops him with a shot between the eyes.
Spinning his body around, Kidd sits down into a Corkscrew Leg Drop. Kipping to his feet, Kidd circles the ring, clapping his hands getting the fans into the match before hitting a Drop Kick to Nakamura and knocking him back into the corner.
Slumping down, Nakamura was an easy targer for Gannon who came flying across from the other side of the ring, hitting the corner with a Leg Lariat. Rolling back to his feet out of the corner, Kidd with a leg and arm, drags Nakamura out of the corner before mounting the ropes.
Pumping his fists into the air, Gannon jumps up, coming down onto the ropes and using them to snap him over to land a Split Legged Moonsault. Making the cover,
Gannon waited until a 2 count before pulling Nakamura up by his hair. With a Body Slam in the center of the ring, Kidd’s repeated Handstand Knee Drops softened the manager up even further. Back up the ropes, a Frog Splash shook the ring, bringing the fans to their feet and a bow from Kidd as he bounced right up to his feet.
Rolling out of the ring, Gannon took a little stroll before liberating a chair from a fan in the front row. Pushing it into the ring, Gannon climbed up onto the ring apron, using the ropes to somersault back in.
Pulling Lonny to his feet, Kidd spun him upside down before propping him up in the corner in a Tree of Woe. Several Boots to the midsection stopped
Nakamura’s squirming. After putting the chair over Nakamura’s face, Gannon backs away to the opposite of the ring before taking off into a dead run and hitting the chair with a picture perfect Baseball Slide, driving the chair into
Nakamura’s face with a loud clang. Up in a hurry, Gannon grabs up the chair and sets it up in the center of the ring when the boobirds returned as Pep Preston runs out from the back to rescue his manager. Pulling Nakamura from the ring, Pep was trying to steady his manager. Seeing his opponent pulled from the ring, Kidd takes off into a run again, hitting the corner and going up in one swift move. Leaping off, Kidd was high in the air before landing on Preston’s shoulders. Throwing his body backwards, Gannon tosses Pep over with a Hurricanrana that had the fans chanting “Holy Shit!” as the two hit the hard floor.
On his feet, Gannon fell back against the ring barrier for a breather allowing the fans to slap him on the back before he went back on the attack. Giving
Preston a severe Stomping, Gannon pulls Nakamura along by the air back into the ring. Plopping him down onto the chair in a seated fashion, Kidd bolts for the ropes, bouncing off and shooting forward. With a loud pop, Kidd landed a Drop
Kick to Nakamura’s face, spilling him backwards out of the chair onto the mat. With Daye kicking the chair out of the way, Gannon was up the ropes in a hurry, springing off and landing the twisting “Gannonsault on the unmoving Nakamura.
Hooking a leg, it was over just like that. *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Gannon was up and out of the ring in a hurry celebrating with the fans as a groggy Pep Preston went to Nakamura’s rescue.)

— Part 2 of 4 —

(The camera opened on Justin Hart walking in the back, glancing from side to side. He gave the people he passed the once-over. Looking back at him, they cleared a path, so as to not get in his way. This annoyed him even more.)
Justin Hart: Oh relax, chicken-shits. I'm not looking for any of you. (Hart suddenly staggered a bit as someone nudged him off balance with a stiff shoulder. Looking around, he saw none other than Jake Morgan. Morgan didn't even stop to acknowledge him as he simply kept walking. Hart scowled at him.)
I swear ... one day ... (Morgan stopped and turned around. Smiling at Hart, he pointed at him, followed by a slow cut throat. Hart replied with a smile ... and a pair of middle fingers. Both men dropped their smiles.)
I'm right here ... I ain't going anywhere ... (Hart's challenge went unanswered as Morgan waved him off and left. Hart looked on, rubbing the top of his head where he was struck earlier in the week.)
I swear ... when I find the son of a bitch that hit me from behind, pain ... glorious pain ... (Hart ducked back around the corner, peeking out at a passing Ishimoto Sakai, walking at a brisk pace, straightening his trademark bowtie. Hart peered out from around the corner, a wicked grin crept across his face.)
But first ... (Hart laughed before he followed behind him, mocking his silly walk when the camera left him. Zooming in on a solitary door with a big cardboard star bearing the name “Kidd Gannon” on it, the camera pulled back as it whipped open and Kidd Gannon stuck his head out, looking from side to side, then down.)
Kidd Gannon: Oh shi ... (Moving down, the camera picked up Gannon stomping out the flaming brown bag outside the door. Several times, Gannon slammed his foot down upon the bag until the fire was out.
His smile disappeared as he sniffed the air with his nose wrinkling. Lifting his foot, he stared down at his brown splattered boots before ducking back inside and slamming the door.)
Oh shi ... (With the door closing, a snickering person came into view as the camera swung around to catch the green haired Toadie leaving the scene of the the crime with a box tucked under his arm. Watching where he had come from, the laughing
Toadie abruptly stopped as he bumped into someting hard ... something big. Looking up, the production assistant gulped hard as he looked into the grinning face of Jake Morgan.)
Jake Morgan: What have you got there, Toadie? Toadie: Uhm ... package for Brian Floyd? Jake Morgan: Is that so? (Toadie nervously bobbed his head up and down.) Who’s it from? Toadie: The Offspring? (Morgan thumbed his stubbly chin.) Jake Morgan: Is that so? Well ... well ... well ... why don’t I see to it that Mr. Floyd gets his package ...
(Grabbing the package away from Toadie, Morgan shuffled him off before starting down the hall in the other direction. Opening the package, Morgan smiled as he peered inside. Leaving Morgan, the camera came upon two PAW stagehands.)
Stagehand #1: Well.. where is he? (The other stagehand looked dumbfounded as he shrugged his shoulders.) Stagehand#2: Uhh, who were we looking for again? (Walking by, the head of PAW security, Shane, stopped when called to.) #1: Shane, help me out here? Shane: Sure. (Shane tapped his badge anxiously as the man told him his problem.) No telling where ... "Mr. Sakai" could have gotten to. (The first stagehand scratched his head.) #1: We were supposed to escort him to the studio ... (Shane nodded in agreement.) Shane: Well, I’ll keep my eyes peeled for him. If he turns up I'll ... (He trailed off in mid sentence at the sight of a whistling Justin Hart headed his way. With a smile, Hart handed something to Shane.)
You didn't ... (Hart's grin widened, as his face took on an evil tone.) Justin Hart: I did. Little bastard. Shane: Okay ... where is he? (Hart pointed to the broom closet at the end of the hall. Patting Shane on the shoulder, he continued on his merry way, leaving the three men.
Fumbling with a set of keys, Shane opened the door causing a gagged Ishimoto Sakai to spill out. Kneeling down, Shane shook his head before pulling duct tape off of Sakai’s mouth. He chuckled as he dropped Sakai's bowtie.)
Mr. 1-8-7 strikes again ... (The camera leaves the back for the studio as Dollybraid's "Hero" begins to play as Jason Flint, with mic in hand, makes his way to the ring. Dressed down, a baggy pair of flannel pants, sandals and a grey T-shirt, Flint looks worn with his Oakleys covering his eyes, a little hungover. Climbing into the ring,
Flint circles a bit, trying to find the words.) Jason Flint: You know ... there seems to be some sort of miscommunication that is going on around here in PAW. Maybe its all the Japanese fuckers who can't pronounce "L's" or something or maybe they learned their English watching
Barney ... I don't know. (Flint shrugs, shaking his head.) But there seems to be the notion that Jason Flint can't defend himself. That Jason Flint can't get the job done anymore. And that Jason Flint should ... just go away. And the person who seems to make these notions public, happens to be that little FUCKER over there.
(Flint points off to the announcing table where Ishimoto Sakai has just arrived. The little man nervously shakes his head.)
That's right, tiny. YOU. You seem to be the root of my woes right now. You run your mouth and INFECT this promotion with your bullshit. You infect these people with your lies and you make these wrestlers believe that I can be beaten.
(Flint climbs out of the ring and moves closer to the Sakai, who is sweating some serious bullets and looking towards the back for a sign of help.)
People like Hiroshima Nagasaki and Jake Morgan, who suddenly have grown balls to doubt my skills ... they mock my talent ... they belittle me. Well ... let me tell you a thing or two, "Team Japan," I've faced bigger, badder people that the six of you and if all you can do is call me names ... then booga booga to you too!
Come out here and prove that you can pin my shoulders to the mat cleanly, because we all know that you can't. All I'm saying is PROVE ME WRONG. And you, you shit...
(Flint grabs Sakai by his lapels and pulls him up to his feet.) I told you that if you start talking shit about me again, I was going to get some payback. ("Kick his Ass!" chants begin, oddly enough, with the group of fans behind Sakai, who tries to quiet them. Flint shoves Sakai back down into his chair, faking a punch at him and causing him to flinch before hiding behind his notes.
Flint shakes his head again, returning to the mic.) Pathetic. But there's someone else who likes to call people names too. His name, Justin Hart. If its not Nakasucky this ... it’s FLINT YOU’RE THE BANE
OF MY EXISTENCE!!! Last time I checked, Hart, this is PAW and not some playground name game. Today, I'm going to show you why I am Jason Flint, a man that shouldn't be trifled with or crossed.
You like to stick your grubby fingers into a lot of pies here in PAW thinking you can be the fucking badass you see yourself in your dreams.
It’s time for you and that chip on your shoulder to ... WAKE UP!!!!! (The camera zoomed in on Flint’s evil grin as large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession.)
.... 5 .... .... 4 .... .... 3 .... .... 2 .... .... 1 .... (White Zombie's "More Human than Human" begins as flashes of Hiroshima Nagasaki in action are shown. Scene after scene quickly flash by of past battles ...
Running his thumb across his throat before a limp Drake Russell is planted into the canvas with a Nagasaki Driver…
The "Street Soldier" Justin Hart takes a Double Thrust to the Throat and with a Snap Suplex, put into a pinning predicament ... Pep Preston is flat on his back as Nagasaki leaps from the top turnbuckle and lands the "H-Bomb" ...
In slow motion, Aaron Starr turns right into a ball of fire thrown into his face ... And finally, a battered Jason Flint with Nagasaki and turncoat Jake Morgan standing over him.)
(After an explosion, the scene fades to black and the PAW logo disappears before fading back in and panning the small television studio and the fifty or so cheering fans surrounding the ring on three sides.
Moving through the small crowd, the camera zooms in on the broadcast duo of Aaron Starr and Ishimoto Sakai.) Aaron Starr: Hello everyone and welcome to your weekly dose of Public Access Wrestling. I'm Aaron Starr and with me ... as usual ... Ish Sakai: is me, Ishimoto Sakai, the mouth of Team Japan! (Starr raised an eyebrow to the announcer.) Aaron Starr: The what? Ish Sakai: You heard me. I am the mouth of Team Japan. Only my elegant words can properly describe the carnage that Team Japan can do through these pitiful ranks you call American wrestlers.
(Starr grimaced at the comment, rolling his eyes and shaking his head.) Aaron Starr: O-kay. As the "mouth" here dribbles on about Team Japan, I've just been informed there is trouble in the backstage area.
(Fading to the back as the camera in the backstage area is lying on the floor looking up at an awkward angle capturing Justin Hart, holding a chair and hovering over Jason Flint, who has collapsed against a nearby wall.
Flint is busted open pretty badly, his bloodied face imprinted on the white brick wall and blood prints of him trying to get to his feet. Security is there, protecting Flint and holding Hart back.)
Justin Hart: Are you out of your mind?! I didn't do this! Security Guard: Sure Hart, tell me another one. Justin Hart: BITCH, I TOLD you I didn't do this! If anything, I WANT his ass in the ring, not like this ... all pathetic and bleeding. Shit.
(Hart drops the bloodied, mangled chair and shrugs off the Guard who leads him away. The camera pans over to the paramedics attending to Flint and then cuts back to Starr and Sakai.)
Ish Sakai: Oh ... it looks like Flint has had a fight with a chair and LOST. I wonder if Peter Daye was around to witness the chair pin Flint CLEANLY?
(Starr shook his head at the comment when a cheer went up as the Television Champion made his way from the back. Passing the announcers’ table, he swiped up a mic before climbing into the ring.)
Drake Russell: I’ve been waiting a week for this ... let’s get this bitch started!!!!! (Russell dropped the mic and removed his long coat and shades in preparation of his match.)
Aaron Starr: This is going to be a war, Ish and I, for one, would not want to be a Black Raven right about now!
Ish Sakai: Why? Because you would be a talented wrestler? (Before Starr could answer, the Black Ravens marched out from the back to the booing of the fans. Entering the ring, ref Peter Daye chased the other Black
Raven from the ring before calling for the bell.)

— Part 3 of 4 —

*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding ***
Shooting from his corner, Russell locked up and gained control with a Side Headlock. The Raven tried several times to push Russell off for the far ropes but had no luck as the champ held on for dear life.
Wrapping his arms around Russell’s midsection, the Raven lifted the champ off his feet, ready to fall back but was thwarted as Drake flipped back, landing on his feet behind his opponent. The Raven looked around before taking a Forearm from behind. Knocked forward, the Japanese wrestler found himself back in the
Side Headlock. Pushing Russell off for far the ropes, the Black Raven hit the mat hard as Russell slammed a Shoulder into him on the return. Racing off for the ropes, Russell bounced off, coming back into a Somersault Senton. Rolling back to his feet, Russell was up the corner waiting for the groggy Black Raven to get back to his feet. Leaping off, Russell put his man down with a Head Scissors
Takedown. Kicking at the ropes, Russell chased off the other Black Raven who had started to slide into the ring. A hasty retreat rather than a quick Kick to the head.
The ref held Russell back from leaving the ring and attacking the other Raven who was being booed heavily by the small crowd as he taunted the champion.
Whipping the Raven into the far ropes, Russell threw himself back against the ropes in order to propel himself forward where he smashed into #1 with Flying
Shoulder. Rolling over, Russell hooked a leg for a 2 count, releasing his hold as #2 attempted to enter the ring for the save.
Pushing Daye out of the way, Russell grabbed #2 by the hair and pulled him into the ring. Backing the man across the ring with a series of Lefts and Rights,
Russell opened up with Reverse Knife Hands punishing #2 until #1 with a Double Axe Handle to the back stopped him from his onslaught. Each man grabbing an arm pulled Russell from the corner. Whipping him across the ring, they readied for his return only they didn’t count on him to come flying through the air, taking them both down with a Flying Double Clothesline.
With the battered Black Ravens exiting the ring, Russell celebrated with the cheering fans. Leaning on each other for support, the Ravens looked up in surprise as Russell came flying out at them, launching himself off the ropes with an Asai Moonsault.
After Whipping one of the Ravens into the ring barrier, Russell smashed the other one into the ring steps with a loud clang. Putting his Boots to one,
Russell did the same to the other while the fans chanted his name in unison. Pushing one up against the barrier, Russell retrieved his Singapore Cane the delivered a beating, the fans counting along as his weapon swished through the air and landed on target until it literally shattered.
With one down, Russell busied himself, pulling a table out from under the ring. Pushing the barrier back and giving himself plenty of room, Russell set the table up before dragging the other Black Raven up onto the apron. Standing back to back, Russell lifted the Raven into a Crucifix. Throwing his weight forward,
Russell shot the Raven off his back and into the air. The fans jumped to their feet as the Raven hit the table and shattered it. Chants of “Holy Shit!” filled the studio as a serious Russell grabbed the other Raven and dragged him into the ring.
Spinning around and sending his opponent off, Russell took him down on the return with a Spinning Back Kick. Rolling to his feet, Russell mounted the ropes waiting. When the Raven returned to his feet, Russell jumped from the top, coming down with an Elbow to the head. The Raven fall back flat on the mat.
Standing in the center of the ring over his fallen opponent, Russell dragged his thumb across his throat while pointing down at the man on the canvas..
pulling him roughly to his feet, Russell hooked the arms and spun him through the air, bringing him down across his knee with his patented Double Underhook
Back Breaker. Pushing the man off, Russell returned to his feet before dragging the man to the middle of the ring. Taunting the Black Raven, Russell grabbed his legs and rolled him to his chest, slapping on a Boston Crab.
The Black Raven member cried out in pain from the two moves, the Back Breaker that softened him up and the Crab that was the icing on the cake. He tapped out quickly.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Daye called for the bell and asked for the break but Drake Russell was not about to let go. Struggling to pull Russell off of the Black Raven, Daye called for the bell again and some help from the back.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (PAW Security hit the ring to break Russell from his hold as the Black Raven cried out in pain. Finally, Drake relented and released his grip, finishing off with a quick Kick as he was pulled away from the downed man.
With the fans cheering, Drake Russell was led to the back before the camera faded away to a small office, where Jason Flint and Kimberly Davis are.
Flint was laying down on a small couch, his head bandaged but from the looks of the shirt that was once gray, Flint had lost a lot of blood. Kimberly Davis was sitting near him, stroking his blood encrusted hair as a knock comes from the door and Justin Hart made his way in.)
Kimberly Davis: Can't you just go away! You've done enough! Justin Hart: Don't get all defensive on me! I told you I didn't do this. I would rather beat his ass in the ring than ambush him like some fucking coward.
Kimberly Davis: Like you did last week, attacking Nagasaki and Russell? Justin Hart: Whatever, sister. I took advantage of a situation that fell in my lap. I've already told you all, that I HATE everyone here and if I can get my potshots in on that teriakyi bowl eating Nagasaki and kick Russell when he's down, shit yeah I will. This thing between me and Flint, I WANTED in the ring.
He talked his shit and now I'm was going to collect it. But someone got to him first. Believe me or not ... Kimberly Davis: Well, I don't care about what you have to say. Just get the hell out of here!
(Hart shakes his head in disgust before slamming the door shut. Moving to another part of the back, the camera found an irate Lonny Nakamura holding an ice pack to his head animatedly talking to the masked owner of the promotion.
Standing behind the manager was Pep Preston.) Lonny Nakamura: I want to know what you’re going to do about all these attacks upon my wrestlers and myself? I ... we ... are not going to tolerate this anymore!
(The masked owner, flanked by Shane and Fredrick A. Gibson, stood silent as she listened, nodding her head as Nakamura waved his arms about.)
Well!?!?!?! What are you going to do about it? Masked Woman: Do? I’m not going to do anything about it ... (A furious Nakamura stepped forward but was pulled back by Preston as Shane stepped inbetween both parties.)
Lonny Nakamura: WHAT!?!?!?!? Masked Woman: You heard me. I’d like to think of it as being even ... you attacked several of the wrestlers as well as staff members ... if anything,
I’m going to fine you ... (Turning, the owner walked off followed by Gibson then Shane. Fading on Nakamura held back by his wrestler, the camera returned to the ring as Public Enemy's "Bring the Noise" blared with Justin Hart making his way to the ring. Eyeing both Aaron Starr and Ishimoto Sakai, Hart snatched up a mic and climbing into the ring.)
**Thump**Thump**Thump** Justin Hart: I was supposed to have a match against Jason Flint today ... a match that I was looking forward to, so I could shut that trap of his up once and for all. But seeing that he’s a bloody mess now and I don't have someone to beat up. Maybe there's someone in the back who wants a piece of me ... if so
BRING IT! (Hart slammed the mic down hard as he waited for anyone to come out. "Hero" began to play as the fans cheered a bandaged Jason Flint making his way out from the back. Tearing his bloody shirt off, Flint smiled at Hart, while pulling out his own mic.)
Jason Flint: Come on, Hart ... I thought I was going to be your punching bag for the evening? I thought you wanted to break my bones? Come on, brother,
STAIN THIS MAT WITH MY FUCKING BLOOD! (The cheering turned to boos as Hiroshima Nagasaki and an OU jacketed Jake Morgan walked out from the back. Inside the ring, Hart smiled as he motioned for any or all of them to join him for an ass kicking.
Spinning around Flint looked from the smiling Morgan to the approaching Nagasaki. Throwing his hands up, a brillant light flared from his hands right into the face of Flint. Dropping to the ground, Flint’s face was on fire as
Aaron Starr rushed in to help put out the flames while Nagasaki laughed maniacally. Pulling Starr away, Nagasaki hit the announcer with a stiff Front Kick before lifting him into the air and slamming him down through the announcer’s table with a Powerbomb.
Hart started to leave the ring but stopped as Morgan wagged a finger in his direction.) Jake Morgan: Looks like you’re finished for the evening Hart, you’re on our time now. Do us all a favor and let us take care of our business.
(Chants of “Kick His Ass!” rained in from the audience as Morgan and Nagasaki neared the ring.) If you inbred, backwoods, cousin loving hicks would shut up for just a few minutes ... (He lowers the mic as the noise level rises higher than before. He shook his head in disgust, talking to Hiro until the noise died down. As soon as he raises the mic to his lips again, the fans begian to shout. Ignoring them, he went on.)
Go ahead, you bunch of Rednecks, I've got all day. (Smiling as the noise died down, he continued.) I’d start with Brutal Brian Floyd ... (Cheers rose from the rowdy crowd again.) But ... seeing as “Pretty Boy” Floyd ran off crying over like a little child, I don’t have to talk about him ...
(He paused as a chant of “Shut The Fuck Up!” started.) Speaking of children, let's talk about the Offspring. The very name brings the image of children to your mind, it's fitting too, since that it what Offspring acts like, a spoiled little child. Nothing is ever your fault is it Spring?
When I first joined Extreme Wrestling, you were all over me, insulting me, criticizing me and telling me that I didn't have what it took to make it here.
When I asked why, you told me that it was my own fault for not doing things your way. I was a fucking rookie. I made some mistakes but I did things my own way, and you couldn't stand that could you?
