PAW — Card Archive

10.15 Card

15 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 4 —

(The camera opens on Aaron Starr outside of the PAW studios. He looks bored but that quickly changes when the sounds of a vehicle pulling up is heard.
Straightening his tie, he prepares to greet the new arrival. The camera angle switches to a broader look. Jake Morgan is shown getting out of his pickup then reaches into the back and pulls out a gym bag. Throwing it over his shoulder, he heads into the building. As Morgan approaches, Aaron steps out and puts the microphone in his face.)
Aaron Starr: Jake, can I get a few words with you? (Jake looks at Starr for a second then drops his bag to the ground. ) Jake Morgan: Sure Aaron, now's as good a time as any. Aaron Starr: Ok, great. First off, do you have anything that you want to say about your match later today?
Jake Morgan: No, not really. Unlike my opponents, I'll let my talking be done in the ring. (Starr looks a little disappointed but continues on.) Aaron Starr: Okay, then can you tell me where Darlene is and why she didn't arrive with you as usual?
(Jake's look turns from one of indifference, to one of controlled anger, instantly. He doesn't say anything for a few seconds, but after taking a deep breath, he answers Starr.)
Jake Morgan: No comment. Aaron Starr: You don't know where she is? Jake Morgan: What part of "no comment " didn't you understand Aaron? (Starr throws his hands up in the air, then gives Jake an exasperated look.) Aaron Starr: Well, is there ANYTHING that you do want to talk about? Jake Morgan: Yes, there is. (Aaron gets a big smile on his face and thrusts the microphone back into Jake's face.) Jake Morgan: I want to talk about that Promo stealing, Woman beating piece of ... (Jake stops talking then smiles. He holds a finger up to the camera.) Skip, you may want to cover your ears. Mr. Potty-mouth is about to go off on a tirade. (He stops smiling, then continues on with his previous thought.) ... SHIT, Justin Hart. (Pause.) Hart, I always thought you were stupid and your recent actions have proven it to me. Two weeks ago I cut a promo on this show and a few hours later, you do my promo almost word for fucking word. Have you ever had an original thought?
Have you ever done anything on your own or does someone else have to come up with an idea first so you can rip it off? I mean let's face it, when you first came into this fed you were a joke. Walking around making up lists and other stupid things. The only way you started to see some success was to copy someone else.
You started calling yourself the" Toughest SOB" and saying that you were the best wrestler in the fed. All of this happened overnight. I have a few problems with that.
How can you be the best or the toughest, when you have never beaten ME? I know you won the belt in a match with me but it wasn't my shoulders that were pinned and you know that if Nagasaki hadn't interfered, that I never would have allowed you to score the pin on Drake.
You know it and so does everyone else. This bullshit about how you're the baddest man around is getting old. You are nothing, that's why you were included in that list of losers that I mentioned a few weeks ago. Hell, you're the top of that heap. You talk a good game Hart but in all of the times we've met in the ring, how many times have you beaten me? How many times have I left you laying in a heap?
Make no mistake about it, we ARE on a collision course and I won't step aside. It's way past time you were put in your place. If I'm the one that has to do it, then so be it!
(Aaron gives Jake a surprised look.) One more thing, the Boss took that belt off you for the good of the promotion, if you can't see that then that's your problem but don't you ever lay your hands on her or any other woman in this promotion ever, EVER again. This isn't your HOOD, it's PAW. We don't treat women that way around here. A fact you will soon understand.
(Jake reaches down and grabs his bag. He gives Aaron a quick nod, then enters the building.)
Aaron Starr: There you have it folks, it seems we have an old War, that's about to be restarted!
(The camera fades out on a smiling Aaron Starr before making it’s way inside where several of the PAW’s faithful are huddled around a small televison monitor. Those men ... Pep Preston, Emilio Ramirez, the Trashman, Skip
Westervelt and Chip, stand silent and listen to the words of the “Man of Destiny”, Drake Russell during his Sunday bit.) Voice of Drake Russell: You don't know me but you will soon enough ... (Skip Westervelt slaps his forehead as the words echo out.) Skip Westervelt: What a moron! Voice of Drake Russell: If that doesn't scare you then I have three letters that will ... INS.
(One man’s eyes grow large over the uttered sentence. Stepping back from the rest, Emilio Ramirez’ eyes dart from one side to the other looking to see if he’s being watched. Muttering under his breath, the man takes off, leaving his compatriots there, watching as he dashes off.)
Emilio Ramirez: INS? Por dios! (As the group watches him leave, Drake’s last words are heard from the monitor.) Voice of Drake Russell: Enjoy your time here and let that be a lesson to you! (With Russell’s words echoing out into the distance, the camera finds a disgruntled green-haired man stalking the back hallways of the television studio carrying a vase of lovely flowers before him. As he makes his way past production people, he is mumbling to himself.)
Green-haired man: Give the flowers to her ... do you think you can do that for me? Do you know what she looks like?
(The man lets out a deep sigh as he continues on his way before shouting out.) Do I look like an idiot to you? (Realizing that he shouted out loudly, he sheepishly looks around at those in the halls who might have heard. Seeing shaking heads, he rushes off to complete his task. As he continues through the halls, he continues muttering to himself until stopping in front of a door.
With the flowers in front of his face, he looks back to see who might be watching him when he reaches out and without looking knocks on the door.
Without looking, he brushes the arrangement aside to clear his hand’s path to the door ... at the same time, clearing the blocked name plate ... JUSTIN HART.
Hearing the door open, the smiling man goes white when he sees who is standing there before him ... with his hands on his hips, an angered Justin Hart looks disapprovingly down at the delivery then to the bearer.)
Justin Hart: What do you want? (The man smiles at Hart while pushing the vase forward.) Green-haired man: These are for you ... (Pushing them into Hart’s hands, the man runs off as fast as possible leaving a confused Hart shaking his head. Looking at the flowers, Hart reaches in and pulls free a card.)
Justin Hart: Thinking of you ... (Hart looks shocked as he continues reading the card.) Drake?!?!?!?!? (Nostrils flaring, Hart drops the vase with a clatter before slamming his door shut and storming off.)
Oh you’ll be thinking of me, Drake Russell ... from Intensive Care!!!!! (Hart storms off when the camera fades out on him only to find another of the PAW’s somewhat disgruntled wrestlers ... Seth. Sitting in a chair turned backwards, Seth holds his face in his hands as he laments.)
Seth: What she mean by “company men”? What is that supposed to mean? (Seth shakes his head before looking up.) I’ve had success with a tag title reign and as part of the Heartbreakers? What is that supposed to mean? We held the belts for what? Three weeks?
(Seth looks over as a head pops through a nearby curtain ... that of Paco.) Paco: Hey man ... don’t sweat it. It’s all going to be cool, ese. We’re company men, now ... we’re the men! We got the stroke!
Seth: Stroke? Paco: Yeah, ese ... it’s all going to be cool ... and this new look for the Heartbreakers is going to be the best thing that ever happened to your career ... and you can take that to the bank! Gimme one second and I’ll be finished. (Paco’s head disappears behind the curtain leaving Seth shaking his head in disbelief. Seconds later, a loud zipping sound is heard from behind the curtain before it is pulled back and a new look Paco steps out.
Wearing white overalls covered in sequins, Paco jumps out.) Ta da ... (A flabbergasted Seth’s jaw drops open as he stares in utter silence. Giving a little crotch chop and sounding more like Johnny Bravo than Elvis, Paco snaps his head sharply from side to side.)
Look at my package! (Seth slaps his forehead before fading into a memory and is replaced by Jason Flint making his way through the double doors of the studio and turns a quick corner. Continuing down the hallway, Flint passes by the resident SOB, Justin
Hart, who is glaring at a vending machine.) Jason Flint: Imposing the force there, Jedi Master? Justin Hart: Shove it, Flint. Jason Flint: Okay, whatever...loser. (Hart turns and walks up to Flint, going eye to eye with the former champion.) Justin Hart: You sure like to belittle people don't ya? What a big man you are, Flint, a washed up wannabe. (Flint curls a smirk at Hart.) Jason Flint: Look here, Gameboy, when are you going to wake up and smell the coffee and see that the games being played are being played on you for a reason.
Maybe cause its' your gutless. Or stupid. Or truly the loser that everyone knows that you are. (Hart suddenly shoves Flint into the wall, driving his forearm into Flint's throat and eyeballs him, sneering.)
Justin Hart: You ... sonavabitch ... (Still smiling, Flint looks back at Hart.) Jason Flint: Yeah ... that's me. But you've got more important things to deal with today, don't ya champ ... that is, EX-champ. Let me go or Security over there will take you out of this building and you're little shot will end before it starts.
(Hart turns his head at Shane and his other PAW security, who shrugs at Hart, who releases Flint and storms off.)
Shane: What was that all about? Jason Flint: Panty problems, I guess. (Both men laugh just as large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession.) .... 5 ... .... 4 ... .... 3 ... .... 2 ... .... 1 ... (White Zombie's "More Human than Human" begins as flashes of the PAW stars in action are shown. Seth ... Skip Westervelt ... Justin Hart ... Jason Flint ...
and Jake Morgan finally the PAW Television Champion, Hiroshima Nagasaki, standing in the middle of the PAW ring, the belt raised over his head.)
After an explosion, the scene fades to black and the PAW logo disappears before fading back in and panning the fans and their signs held aloft such as "NWC
4EVER", "Where’s my list?" and "Shut the F@ck Up!". After making it’s way through the boisterious crowd of some 50 or so, the camera settles in on a laughing Aaron Starr and Jason Flint,standing in front of the PAW banner.
Aaron Starr: We’re back for another exciting week of Public Access Wrestling ... (Starr turns towards his co-host.) Is it fun yet? (Flint nods his head as he looks back to Starr then towards the viewers.) Jason Flint: You bet it is, Aaron ... big happenings in the promotion last week some of which happened to two of your favorite guys ...
Aaron Starr: You can say that again, Jason ... first, we had the Texas Heavyweight Champion, Justin Hart stripped of his title by the new ownership in order to apease the heads of the National Wrestling Council ... one of the longest standing wrestling organizations with promotions wrestling under it’s banner across this country ...
Jason Flint: It couldn’t have happened to a better person ... Aaron Starr: Also returning to the ranks, someone who has gotten under your skin as well as mine ... Drake Russell ...
Jason Flint: “Mr. Density” has returned ... it should make for some great fun, Aaron ... Aaron Starr: Speaking of Drake Russell, let’s see what he has to say ...

Those guys are gone now and I'm still here. Why do you think that is? I don't cry about anything, I recieved a call telling me that my place on the roster had been opened again after your friend Maeda left after a run as a less-than-credible tag performer. If you think that saying you're going to end my career is actually going to do it then you've got another thing coming tonight. If you think that then you better get out of town, stick out your thumb and bum a ride back to Cali because no one here will care about you when you stick around to find out just how wrong you are.

Here I am at ground zero, waiting for the H-Bomb. Why would I stay fired when I knew that I had a chance to come back and make H-bomb jokes?
Watching you wrestle is just the exact same as listening to you talk, funny with a bit of downright comedy. Believe me, you aren't the first foreigner to talk a big talk. Luckily the others took on other nifty skills like farming or

H-bomb, I think that Starr left it in the toilet around last weeks card. If you're lucky, it could still be there. You know those teenage kids, always doing something in the bathroom, right Aaron?
(He runs his hand through his hair and smiles, lifting his patented glasses up to reveal his eyes as he does so. He drops his Ray-Bans back down.)
The bottom line is that I am here to stay. If you all think that I am such a loser then what makes you think that losing again will break me? Come on, try to kill my career, if you can do it I guess I'll be gone. But just like every other time before, I'm back,and better than ever, regardless of what you think.
I'll let that be a lesson to you. See you a little later on. (He turns his back to the camera before he is replaced by a grinning Starr and Flint.) Aaron Starr: Oh ... the fun is back, Jason ... Jason Flint: It’s all about the couches, Aaron ... it’s all about the couches! Aaron Starr: Before heading to the ring for our first match of the evening, our colleague, Lisa Doyle caught up with our new boss who was not too happy about last week’s attack by Justin Hart ...
Jason Flint: Not happy in the least ... (The camera fades into the back where Lisa Doyle is standing with the Woman wearing the Offspring mask and her “right-hand person” Kimberly Davis.)
Lisa Doyle: Aaron, Jason ... I’m here with the new owner of the PAW ... (Doyle turns to the masked woman with her mic in hand.) Anything you’d like to say ... Woman in the Offspring mask: Yes Lisa, I have quite a few things to say but I’m only going to address one person ...
Lisa Doyle: That person being the former Texas Heavyweight Champion, Justin Hart? Woman in the Offspring mask: It’s a situation that needs to be taken care of and it will be done tonight ...
(Before the woman can finish, Jake Morgan walks into view followed by Mean Darlene.) Lisa Doyle: We’ve just been joined by Jake Morgan ... (Morgan looks angered as he stands there with his hands on his hips before turning towards the new boss.)
Jake Morgan: If there’s anything I can do ... I’d be more than happy to offer any assistance. What Hart did last week was just plain wrong ... and I’m not going to stand back and allow ...

Sitting there for the pin, Seth didn’t get a count as Jake lifted him off the canvas by the throat and held him aloft before Choke Slamming him.

Morgan and led into a Back Slide. Peter Daye slapped out a near pinfall. With both men sitting up, Seth used the ropes to Slingshot himself back into the ring and landed a Boot to the heads of both men. Cause there's nothing to cover up. And if that's what you consider putting the moves on someone, you're dumber than you look! So stop using me as an excuse for your unfaithfulness. If you got problems with me commenting on the lady's looks, try complaining about it to me. She's lucky I felt like being nice that week.
(Hart turns and walks away as the camera switches back to Starr and Flint.) Jason Flint: What’s that saying about looking a gift horse in the mouth? Aaron Starr: Coming off of having his title stripped, Justin Hart was thrust right back into the title hunt with tonight’s match-up with Television
Champion, Hiro Nagasaki ... Jason, he does not sound happy. Jason Flint: I’d say that was an understatement, Aaron but as a former Champion, I have to tell you ... there’s title on the line and you have to let go of all the anger and BS ... it may not be the Texas Heavyweight Title but it’s the only title!
I don’t expect Hiroshima Nagasaki to roll over for the self-proclaimed resident S.O.B. ... today or any day ... Aaron Starr: And under the guidance of Lonny Nakamura, the TV Champion looks doubly tough ...
Speaking of the Television Champion, let’s go to the back where he has something to say to tonight’s opponent ...
(The camera on a lone Hiroshima Nagasaki preparing for tonight's title defense. The H-Bomb appears dressed and ready. His hands are taped and he is already wearing his wrestling gear. Nagasaki appears to be focused and ready. Turning toward the camera, Nagasaki addresses all those who are watching.)
All day I have sat here, focusing on tonight's match. The opportunity to humiliate this piece of garbage thrills me. Some say that this will be my toughest match.
Some say that Hart may be the one man in PAW who can stand toe to toe with me. Some think that Hart may even be able to do the one thing no one else has been able to do ...
(Long Pause) Survive an encounter with the most destructive force in Public Access Wrestling. That is the only option available to you Hart. Remove the thought from your mind that victory is attainable and instead make sure you focus on not losing more than your honor. Do not despair Hart ... you will not be the last to fall before the might of the H-Bomb, many others will follow.
(Standing from the bench in front of the lockers, Nagasaki unleashes a savagely wicked yell that seems to echo forever within the small confines of the room. With the echo still reverberating, Nagasaki leans toward the camera.)
The H-Bomb is ready!!! (Slapping himself in the face repeatedly, Nagasaki walks out the door and toward the ring. The faint sound of kodo drums and gongs can be heard in the background as the camera fades back into the hosts of the show.)
Aaron Starr: He sounds ready, Justin ... Jason Flint: Focussed and ready to step in against anyone ... tonight it’s Justin Hart ... and it should be a good one ... Aaron Starr: With the sound of those drums, here comes the Champion ... (The fans awaited the arrival of the champion but no one came out of the back. The entranceway remained empty as Aaron Starr grasped his headset.) Uhh, fans, I don't know what could be keeping Nagasaki ... (Jason Flint looked on but was concentrating on the news he was recieving through his headset.)
Jason Flint: I’m getting word that there's some kind of mixup in the back, and the participants in our next match are right in the middle of it! We're switching to our backstage feed ...

(Following that last comment, the scene switches to the backstage area where
Hart and Nagasaki are already mixing it up even before hitting the ring! Both men are hell bent on causing more damage to the other than they'll allow! First
Nagasaki strikes with a Front Kick to the stomach. With Hart doubled over, Nagasaki blasts him with a Double Axe Handle the back. Down to a knee, Hart's nailed with a Scissor Kick to the back of the head. Moving in for the kill,
Nagasaki gets careless as Hart grabs him by the tights and yanks him into the wall. Staggering away clutching his face, Hart capitalizes by grabbing a nearby fire extinguisher and nailing him in the back with it.)
Justin Hart: You wanna battle champ... HUH?!?! (Throwing the extinguisher down on Nagasaki's knee, Hart grabs the leg and drops an Elbow across it. Nagasaki grunts aloud as his leg is wrenched back. A few seconds later, Hart attempts to lock him in the Urban Lockdown but a rake to the eyes quickly puts an end to that. Nagasaki gets to his feet with a little difficulty then nails Hart with a Standing Side Kick. Hart hits the floor with a thud, stunned from the impact. Nagasaki then begins to walk away trying to regain the feeling in his leg, only to be clipped from behind by
Hart. Grabbing him by the head, Hart drags the champion out to the ring. Pushing Peter Daye out of the way, Hart leads Nagasaki into the ring for the start of the match.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Right off the bat, Hart unloads the big guns with repeated German Suplexes and bridging on the third one for a pin attempt. Daye nearly slaps the mat a third time before Nagasaki was able to kick out.
Showing his rage, Hart flexes at a weary Nagasaki as he pulls himself to his feet using the ropes. Moving in again, Hart catches a Front Kick to the chest and a Double Chop to the neck.
Spinning around, Nagasaki with a Back Kick to the knee nearly took Hart off his feet, dropping him to a knee. Moving in, Nagasaki took a Low Blow that sent him to the mat in pain. Stretching the leg, Hart moved in and held onto the ropes for some well placed Boots to the head before clamping a boot down across
Nagasaki’s neck. Peter Daye was in with a count looking for a break. Only after several warnings was Hart willing to break the hold.
Daye followed Hart as he walked away with a stern warning, taking steps back as the “Street Soldier” turned on him with a warning of his own.
Pulling Nagasaki to his feet, Hart wasn’t ready for the Thumb to the eye. Stepping back, holding his hands over his eyes, Hart was grabbed around the waist for a Belly-to-Belly Suplex.
Momentarily stunned and flat on his back allowed Nagasaki to use his boots to Rake Hart’s eye. Now it was Nagasaki’s turn to draw a warning. Looking Daye in the eye, the only response the Champion had for the ref was manical laughter.
Grabbing Hart by the hair, Nagasaki dragged Hart’s face across the ring ropes to further damage the eyes. Hart, though blinded, swung back and connected with an Elbow that shook free his attacker.
Grabbing an arm, Hart swung Nagasaki around and sent him for a ride into the far ropes. Diving forward on the return, Hart leveled his opponent with a
Flying Shoulder Butt. Making a cover, Hart was tossed off on a 2 count. Angered, Hart went with the heavy Punches, staggering Nagasaki. As he moved in for the kill, Hart caught a Chop to the chest followed by several more stiff
Reverse Knife Hands. Moving Hart back into the corner, Nagasaki popped in a Double Thrust to the Throat and a Snap Suplex. Rolling over Hart, Daye hit the mat twice. Spinning Hart into the Oriental Spike, Nagasaki took a Back Fist to the face and a Side Russian Leg Sweep, countering his move. From a mounted position,
Hart rained down Punches, many of which got through the Champ’s guard. Pulling Nagasaki to his feet, Hart Whipped him across the ring and took him down with a Clothesline. Hart climbed the ropes and celebrated his victory.
Returning to Nagasaki, Hart pulled him to his feet and again spun him around for a Whip into the ropes, this time however, it was countered and Hart was sent across the ring. On the return, Nagasaki caught an arm and fell back with a picture perfect Japanese Arm Drag.
A Body Slam put Hart down a second time which was followed by a Leg Drop. Hart barely rolled out of the way. Both men returned to their feet and Locked up in the center of the ring. Nagasaki gained control with a Hammerlock. Hart spun one way then the other to break free before hitting an Elbow to the Face. WIth a Go Behind, Hart was able to push Nagasaki forward into the ropes. As they bounced off, Hart fell back, rolling Nagasaki up for a pin attempt.
Nagasaki rolled a shoulder off the mat just in time. Hart was up complaining about a slow count. Nagasaki, too was up but didn’t waste any time going back on the offensive. Firing away with a Side Kick, Nagasaki missed the dodging
Hart and nailed the ref dead on. Peter Daye hit the canvas like a ton of bricks. Grabbing an arm, Hart slammed Nagasaki into the far corner and came rushing across the ring. Leaping into the air to deliver the Hang Time Corner Splash,
Hart smashed into the turnbuckles. Without wasting a second, Nagasaki moved in and flattened Hart with a Nagasaki Driver and made a cover. Without a ref to make a count, the Champion looked around to find Jason Flint diving into the ring and slap the mat ...
Justin Hart barely lifted a shoulder in time. The fans were howling as both men returned to their feet. Circling the ring, they locked up and Hart was in a
Side Headlock. Pushing Nagasaki off, Hart sent him across the ring. On the return, Hart timed his Drop Kick, flooring Nagasaki.
With his eyes on Flint, Hart landed a Knee Drop off the ropes before rolling him over and slapping on the Urban Lockdown. That’s when the bell sounded.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Hart was dumbfounded as he looked to Flint for foul play but all he got was a shrug of the shoulders. Looking around, they saw their masked employer step out from the back and make her way to the ring followed by Aaron Starr.
Hart dropped Nagasaki’s legs and made his way towards the ropes closest to his new boss. Nearing the ring, she took the mic from Starr.) Woman in the Offspring mask: I think this match has gone on long enough ... (She was met with a loud chorus of boos before she continued.) It’s time to pay the piper, so to speak ... as all of you know, Public Access Wrestling is under new leadership ... gone are the days of the former employer’s mismanagement of funding and his superstars ... gone are the days of his petty feuds and bounties ... gone are the days of a promotion on a one way swirly down the toilet ...
In case you haven’t noticed, Mr. Hart ... I am NOT B.J. Perris ... I am neither a wrestler nor did I ever invite you to put your hands upon my person. Perris might have taken that invitation as a chance to get into the ring but those days are over ...
Neither you nor anyone else under my employ will ever touch me in a threatening manner again ...
That is a warning ... Now, I am a firm believer in one bearing the consquences of one’s actions. For every action there is a reaction ... and your actions last week call for a reaction ... in this case, a very harsh reaction ...
(Jake Morgan walked out from the back with his hands on hips and a smile on his lips.) Something that will serve as a reminder not only to you but to anyone else who might have an idea to duplicate your behavior ... but as I said, I am not a wrestler ...
(Morgan took several steps forward towards the ring when the masked woman reached out and placed a hand on his chest holding him back much to his surprise.)
No ... this punishment is not going to be handed down by one of your peers ... it’s going to come from them ... (Turning back, she motioned towards the uniformed officers that poured out from the back and made their way to the ring. Climbing inside, Nagasaki was laughing hysterically as they moved in on Hart.
Hart could only smile and shake his head as they slapped on the cuffs and led him from the ring. As he passed the Morgan, words were said and the two were seperated by both the uniformed officers as well as the PAW Security that had come ringside.
With Hart being dragged off into the back, the camera left the ring and found a distraught Seth sitting on the floor of the hallway in the back with his face buried in his hands. It was obviously Seth, still dressed in his rather exotic attire.)
Seth: I can’t believe this. Can it get any worse? (Seth’s head snapped around when there was an answer to his question.) Voice: Don’t ask ... you might not like the answer ... (A small smile crossed his lips when he saw Lisa Doyle walking in closer.) Seth: Oh ... All I wanted was to get out of this damn promotion and get the Hell out of Texas ... is that too much to ask? Just release me! Do I get that? Oh no ... I’m in this get-up that I can barely move in and I’m paired with someone who I haven’t even wrestled with before ...
Lisa Doyle: It could have been Chip ... Seth: Shhhhhhhhh ... don’t say that too loudly. Someone might hear you. (Doyle starts laughing as she looks at Seth’s getup.) Lisa Doyle: I’m sorry.
