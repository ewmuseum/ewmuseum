PAW — Card Archive

4.14 Card

14 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera opens in the back of the PAW television studio with promoter B.J. Perris and Drake Russell deep in conversation.) <Perris> Look ... you came to me in good faith ... and I'm going to do the same by you ... you just have to trust me on this. Darlene wouldn't ... allow me ...
to put you in the #1 Contender's spot ... <Drake Russell> Allow you? <Perris> What can I say? Anyway ... after you whip Morgan's ass tonight ... you're the man ... it's as simple as that ... you'll be the next Texas Heavyweight Champion ... (Perris starts to walk off but stops and turns back towards Russell. Giving a wink, he continues.)
All you have to do is trust me ... (With Perris walking off, Russell can only smile to himself. Moving from the hallways to the outside of the building, the camera comes upon the former Texas
Heavyweight Champion, Jake Morgan and Mean Darlene making their way towards the back entrance. In a relatively good mood, Darlene waves at the few fans that are standing out back to catch a glimpse of their favorite. With Morgan stopping to sign a few autographs, the camera moves back inside to find one half of the PAW Tag Team Champs, Johnny Green talking with the three members of the State Club Boyz.)
<Johnny Green> Guys ... guys ... it's me you're talking to ... remember ... me, your buddy Johnny Green ... what's the problem?
(None of the three say anything until finally Tony steps forward after eyeing his frat brothers.)
<Tony> Look Johnny, nothing against you but we're a little tired of it all. We come out like we're supposed to and we pull our weight around here but ...
<Johnny Green> Yeah? <Tony> Well ... we keep getting our asses beat on ... and what do we get out of it? Nothing but a big fat beating for it. No chicks, no title shots and no appreciation.
<Johnny Green> Guys, guys ... I appreciate you ... I was there with you last week ... <Curtis> It's not you, man ... Jason bailed on us! <Joseph> We had his back and he just left us there ... you, too ... <Tony> Hell, he left Kimberly there ... <Johnny Green> I know ... Jason and I need to have a little ... talk ... (Unable to utter another word, Green is hammered from behind by a wild swinging
Brian Cage. Moving on, Cage's chain wrapped fist laid into the SCB's when large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession.)
... 5 ... ... 4 ... ... 3 ... ... 2 ... ... 1 ... (Rob Zombie's "More Human than Human" starts to play loudly as clips of Public
Access Wrestling 2000's Superstars in action passes ... Skip Westervelt ...
Drake Morgan ... Jake Morgan ... Neo Draven and Sasha ... Brian Cage ... Johnny
Green and finally, the Texas Heavyweight Champion, Jason Flint, standing in the center of the ring lifting his title belt skyward ...
With an explosion, the scenes disappear from the screen and are replaced by the logo of Public Access Wrestling 2000 as it moves forward, filling the screen.
The song suddenly stops and with a smooth transition, fades out ... With a just as smooth fade in, the camera pans inside ring where host Aaron
Starr stands.) <Aaron Starr> Good evening ladies and gentlemen and welcome to another edition of Public Access Wrestling ... as you know, it's wrestling the way it was meant to be!
Before heading to the ring for exciting wrestling action ... Lisa Doyle is in the back with Mean Darlene and the former Texas Heavyweight Champion, Jake
Morgan ...
(The camera moved to the back on the trio of Lisa Doyle, Mean Darlene and Jake
Morgan.) <Lisa Doyle> Thank you, Aaron ... I'm here with Mean Darlene and Jake Morgan ... (Doyle turns towards and irate Darlene.) Darlene ... anything you'd like to address before what might possibly be Jake's biggest match to date .. a loss here and it might be some time before Jake gets another shot at the Texas Heavyweight Title ... your thoughts?
<Mean Darlene> One word comes to mind right now ... Destiny ... Drake Russell said it but I have a feeling that he said the wrong thing ... the word he was looking for was audacity ...
That's what he has ... he has the audacity to come into this promotion just a few short weeks ago and we're already hearing him cry about what he thinks is the match-up everyone wants to see?
Are you kidding me? Listen up, Drake ... you're not the #1 Contender ... he is
... (Pointing back at Morgan.) You're not the #1 Contender and after today, Jake Morgan will have proven that point! (With a disgusted wave, Darlene storms off followed by Morgan. The camera moved back to the ring where the first match of the evening was just starting.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Neo Draven, protecting Sasha found himself in against the quick tags and double teams of Skip and Chip. Skip's Hurricanrana almost took the match but was broken up by Sasha. Grabbing Neo's valet by the hair and dragging her into the ring, Skip tagged in his partner who climbed the ropes.
Pushing Sasha out of the way, Neo hit the flying Chip with a Drop Kick as the overweight wrestler jumped from the top. Hooking a leg, he waited for the referee to hit the 3 count only to have his pin attempt broken up by Skip.
With Chip down, Skip and Neo battled around the ring both inside and out which saw some hard bumps that brought the fans to their feet. An Asai Moonsault put
Skip down but Chip followed with an Elbow Drop off the apron that hurt Neo who was already nursing wounds from the previous weeks.
Taking Neo Draven back into the ring and beating on him some more, Skip tossed him back into his own corner and called for Sasha. Seeing her man in no shape to continue, she stepped in.
It was short and ugly as she was put down with a Dragon Suplex and pinned following Chip's top rope Splash.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Adding insult to injury, Skip held her down while Chip climbed the ropes a second time and came flying off with a top rope Leg Drop before the two men headed to the back amid boos from the fans. Leaving the victorious couple, the camera headed to the back where Brian Cage was patiently awaiting his match for the Texas Heavyweight Title.
Brian Cage is sitting in the back wrapping a chain around his fist before he licks it. Looking down at his watch, a smile slowly starts to fade away when he notices the camera. With a cold stare, he starts to talk)
<Brian Cage> Flint, time's ticking down just like your title reign is!!! One question ... where's your posse?
(Brian gives an evil smile) Flint, every time I look at my watch and see the time ticking away the more and more my palms start to sweat and my legs start to shake!!! Because tonight in front of all those PAW fans, they are going to see the dawning of a new era ...
the era of Brian Cage!!! (Tipping his head back, he lifts his chain and starts to laugh. Leaving Cage's room, the camera finds itself back outside training on the PAW Texas
Heavyweight Champion. With a bag slung over his shoulder, the lone Flint is approached by Lisa Doyle.)
<Lisa Doyle> Jason! Any comments on arriving ... MINUS the State Club Boyz,
Kimberly Davis, or your tag partner Johnny Green?
<Jason Flint> Don't you have anyone else to interview, Lisa? No, I don't have any comments about why I'm arriving here alone. Maybe its because I've sent the
Boyz out first to scope out the area. Maybe I have a "master" plan. MAYBE they had easier traffic! Which....which was the case, traffic sucked.
<Lisa Doyle> Okay ... any last parting shots at your opponent, Brian Cage? <Jason Flint> Sure. Brian Cage, you can cart your ass here with that Gentlemen's Club reject on your arm and pretend to live in that fantasy world where dreams can really come true, but the sad fact is ... Jason Flint is going to beat your ass and give you a taste of good old fashion REALITY.
<Lisa Doyle> Cage did beat you the last time the two of you met ... <Jason Flint> Oh Whooptee-freaking-doo, Lisa. He pounded me with that chain of his....how original. But what you forgot to tell these nice people is that
Jason Flint wiped the fucking floor with Brian Cage up and down this building, cutting him and smearing his bloody face across the damn walls. What Cage was was lucky. Not this time....not this time.
(Turning on his heel, Flint left the interviewer alone outside shrugging her shoulders. Back inside, a gorgeous woman is leaning against the wall with her back to the viewers. Starting from her heels, up the back of her legs then around and up her back, the camera watches as she tries to fix her shoe.
Finishing, she turns around and we are aware that it's Brian Cage's ladyfriend,
Sara E. Xavier. Looking startled, the camera pulls back to reveal two men leering at her from behind.
Laughing, the two men walk past her.) <Skip Westervelt> Nice ... (The multicolor haired Chip sticks his tongue out, wiggling it up and down.) <Chip> Hey baby ... (A door opens and Brian Cage sticks his head out.) <Brian Cage> What the fuck ... (Seeing the rude gesture sets Cage off, sending him into a fury as he lays into
Chip, knocking him to the floor with hammering Forearms before being swarmed over by Security. Leaving the back for the ring, it focussed in on Mean Darlene leading Jake Morgan to the ring. With Morgan holding the ropes open for her,
Darlene climbed inside with a mic in hand.)
<Mean Darlene> Perris ... I've got a really simple question for you that I hope you consider before going on ... Do you know what the fuck you're doing?
I mean ... I want you to listen to what I have to say then consider it before you make anymore promises to that halfwit Russell ...
(Raising an arm, she quieted the cheering fans.) Think back five or six months ago when you approached me ... and begged me to leave the current Texas Champion at that time, the Offspring. Remember? Do you remember when you asked me to join the "winning team"?
Do you remember that? You wanted me to leave the side of a nearly unstoppable force ... and perhaps the most popular wrestler here in your promotion to stand by your side ...
And after hours and hours of rolling your offer through my head ... I did just that. I betrayed a man who believed in me because in you ... I saw someone with the same goals and aspirations as myself.
Now, let's move that clock a few months forward ... when one of your wrestlers,
13 decided that he was going to teach you a lesson ... he was going to break you down through me ... he took great pleasure in tormenting you through me ...
Do you remember that? Remember seeing me beaten ... battered ... bloodied ... and there wasn't a damn thing you could do about it ... Until ... you set aside your differences with this man ... (The fans cheer when Darlene motions towards Morgan standing silent in the ring.) And after he took everything that 13 threw at him ... getting knocked down and beaten upon ... each time, he got right back up and took more ... and when 13 was history ... Jake stood by your side ... ready for whenever you needed him
... never questioning your decisions and never bitching about getting screwed out of title shots that were rightfully his ... until now ...
You see Perris, we've both stood by your side ... but we're ... I'm not going to stand aside and let you make deals with Drake Russell because your vision is clouded by this little thing you've got going on with Jason Flint ...
You want to bring respectibility to the Texas Heavyweight Title ... then let a man who has been nothing but class bring it home for you ... and if this isn't sinking in ... then bring out Russell and let's get this over with because one way or another, Jake Morgan is walking out of this ring as the #1 Contender!
(Darlene tossed the mic aside when Drake Russell walked out from the back and made his way to the ring. Moving to the center of the ring, he and Morgan went eye-to-eye. After the two had a few words, referee Peter Daye stepped in and pushed them apart before calling for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Locking up in the center, neither man was able to gain control before pushing away from each other. Several more times, they locked up but to no avail.
Calling for a Test of Strength, Morgan took a boot to the stomach into a Spinning Neck Breaker. Pushing him into the corner, Russell pounded on the former Champion before being pushed back by the ref.
Out of the corner with an Arm Drag, Morgan was pulled up in a seated position with an Arm Bar. Letting go of the arm, Russell dropped quickly with a Drop
Kick to the face and made a quick cover for a 2 count.
Dropping in behind Morgan, Russell wrapped him up in a Sleeper. Watching closely, Daye lifted Morgan's arm. Jake was fading away as the arm dropped.
Lifting it a second time, it went down. Asking for the submission, he lifted it a third time. Letting it go, the arm didn't drop. Fighting his way to his feet,
Morgan broke the hold with well placed Elbows to the stomach. A Punch to the head backed Russell into the ropes where he was lifted into the air and taken down with a Brain Buster. Still feeling the effects of the Sleeper, Morgan was slow to make a cover, doing so and getting a 1 count.
With a Whip into the ropes, Russell was put down with a Knee to the stomach on the return. Grabbing Russell's leg, Morgan's Spinning Toe Holds were followed by an Elbow Drop as he worked the knee.
Signalling for the Figure Four, Morgan never got the chance as the State Club
Boyz hit the ring and leveled him with a chair. Pulling him to his feet, Morgan was held in place from both sides as Curtis bent the chair over his head.
With Joe and Tony working over Morgan, Curtis never saw Drake Russell until it was too late when he was dropped with a Spinning Back Kick. Pulling him up,
Russell put him right back down with a Double Underhook Back Breaker.
Tony rushed in with a Punch which Russell ducked under, wrapping his arms around his attacker's waist as he spun around. Tony was lifted off his feet and hit the canvas with a Belly-to-Belly Suplex.
A Drop Kick sent Tony out of the ring in a hurry. Leaving Morgan, Joe hit
Russell from behind before he was grabbed and pulled into a devastating Short
Arm Clothesline. Looking at each other from across the ring, each man grabbed one of the State Club Boyz inside the ring.
Morgan hit the mat with a DDT while Russell's Gut Wrench Powerbomb knocked out
Curtis. Rolling over their respective victims, they waited while Peter Daye slapped out a double 3 count.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Back on their feet, the two watched each other as Mean Darlene entered the ring. With a smile, Russell offered his hand to Morgan only to have Darlene to slap it away as she led her man from the ring to a healthy chorus of cheers.
Moving to the back, the camera came upon Johnny Green walking with Kimberly
Davis.) <Johnny Green> I think we need to find Jason, the Boyz just ran in on Morgan and Russell and got their asses handed to them ...
<Kimberly Davis> Last I saw, he snuck into the place ... I saw him going in here ... (Pointing at the nearby door, Kimberly pushed the door open and stopped dead in her tracks by the sight that greeted her ... standing before her was Jason
Flint wrapped in the embrace of Kat Collins.)
What the ... (Hearing the voices, Flint pushed Kat of off him and sheepishly looked at
Kimberly and Johnny standing there before she stormed off.)
<Jason Flint> Wait ... it's not ... oh damn ... (Looking back at the smiling Kat, Flint grabbed up his belt and ran from the room following Kim. Passing Johnny he barely heard what his partner said.)
<Johnny Green> I think your ship is quickly sinking ... (With Flint out of the room, the camera moved to another part of the back where
Neo Draven was angrily stalking through the hallways. Opening doors and kicking those that weren't open, Neo seemed to be looking for someone. As he grabbed a passing Security guy, Jason Flint ran by.)
<Neo Draven> You see Skip or that fat bozo? (With the man shaking his head, Neo let him go and continued his search through the back. Returning to Flint who had been stopped by his "Electric Referee" Dan
Glass, the camera zoomed in.)
<Dan Glass> Jason ... I think we need to talk ... (Flint was scanning the hallway rather than fully listening.) <Jason Flint> Sure ... sure ... anything you want, Dan ... <Dan Glass> I've been thinking ... <Jason Flint> That's great, Dan ... look let's get together later ... we'll do lunch ... (Racing off, Flint brushed off Glass as he continued his search. Not finding
Kimberly, a disappointed Flint tossed his belt over his shoulder and shrugged as he made his way to the ring, ready to work out his aggression on Brian Cage.
"Remote Control" by the Beastie Boyz signal the entrance of the PAW Texas
Heavyweight Champion. As Flint makes his way in, his normal ring attire is replaced by cargo shorts, a MT2K T-shirt, knee and elbow pads, and a pair of white sneakers. And slung over his shoulder....the PAW belt, Flint smiles at his opponent before producing a mic.
<Jason Flint> Brian Cage....this whole week, you've run your mouth, screaming at the top of your lungs, doing nothing but bitch and complain. Do you really think any of these idiots want to hear what you have to say. We're not talking about stock tips here folks, we're talking about this moron here....a person with the IQ of the pet rock!
Flint points to Cage who it livid with anger. <Jason Flint> I didn't think so. So Cage...you wanted me to bring the PAW belt all shined up....well here it is. But I didn't do it for you, because
Cage.....in ten seconds I'm going to FUCK UP your world as you know it! Flint tosses the mic and belt to the side and makes his way to the ring. Pulled in, the match was underway with Daye calling for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Cage wasted no time in taking it to the Champion. Despite beating Flint just a few weeks ago, this one counted and Cage knew that ... this one wasn't going to slip away from his grasp.
Body Slammed, Flint found himself flat on his back but was close enough to grab the ropes for the break. Daye reached over and smacked Flint's hand off before counting again, slapping out a 2 count. Flint took a boot to the face before being dumped out of the ring.
On the outside, Cage continued the attack, sending the Champ into the ring steps. After lifting Flint into the air and dropping him facefirst into the apron, Cage rolled him back into the ring.
Flint was on the canvas when Cage pulled out his chain and held it up for the fans before he started wrapping it around his fist. One his knees and pleading to be spared. Seeing that wasn't going to work, Flint turned to the ref and called for a time out. Rolling his eyes, Daye tipped his hand several times to his mouth as if he was taking a drink. With a wave and a laugh, he walked off.
Cage was smiling at the look of fear of Flint's face as he walked in. Rearing back, Cage took a Low Blow and staggered back. Flint got back into the match with a Hip Toss and a follow-up Senton as he bounced off the ropes. A little strut around the ring and the Champ was back in business as he stomped Cage after kicking the chain out of the way.
Jumping up onto the ropes, Flint flipped through the air landing a Split Legged
Moonsault and getting a near fall with a cover. Kipping to his feet, Flint climbed the ropes again and raised his arms into the air before leaping off and spinning through the air with the Shockwave.
Cage rolled out of the way of the Corkscrew Splash. Hitting hard, Flint rolled on the canvas in pain. Cage landed an Elbow Drop before pulling him to his feet. A Spinebuster led into a Choke Slam and a pin attempt with a very fast 2 count.
Pulled back up, Flint ducked Cage's grasp and spun in behind him with a Spinning Back Kick. Sending him forward into the ropes, Cage bounced back into
Flint's grasp and lifted off his feet in a Fireman's Carry. Spinning several times to disorient Cage, Flint dropped him down onto his knee with a Gutbuster
Drop.
After being sent across the ring with a Slingshot, Cage was up and countered
Flint with a Headbutt. Staggered, Flint went down from a Drop Kick. Scooped up for a Body Slam, Flint rolled off Cage. Grabbing an arm, Flint spun around to send cage into the ropes but was blocked and sent instead. On the return, he ran into Cage's boot.
Following Flint to the outside, the two traded blows that carried them into the audience. With Security moving in to give them more room, Cage blocked Flint's counter then hit with a DDT onto a chair.
Tossing Flint over the ring barrier and back into the ring, Cage took his time as he climbed the ropes and waited for Flint to get to his feet. Climbing, he found the three members of the State Club Boyz walking out from the back.
Nearing the ring, they watched as Flint floundered as he tried to get to his feet, totally helpless.
Each man smiled as they watched the helpless Flint before walking off. Tony gave a disgusted wave as he left only to turn into the waiting stare of Jake
Morgan who had walked out from the back. Morgan had a big grin on his face as he slowly advanced which had the SCB's moving in the opposite direction.
Climbing down from his perch, Cage had come to the near ropes and was holding the ropes open for the SCB's who seemed to want no part of that. Charging in,
Morgan knocked one of them to the floor as the other two scattered. Turning,
Morgan was knocked to the floor by a Spearing Johnny Green who rushed out from the back.
Wanting to get some himself, Cage started to climb out of the ring when Jason
Flint tagged him with a Low Blow by a chain wrapped fist. Pulling the challenger back into the ring, Flint Whipped him into the far ropes and caught him on the return with a Head Scissors Takedown. Caught by surprise, Cage was put up onto the ropes and taken down by a Super Hurricanrana.
The fans were cheering with Sara E. Xavier stepping out from the back and making her way to the ring. She was interrupted by Kat Collins who slapped her on the shoulder and spun her around. Grabbing her around the head, Kat planted her with a DDT.
With a Top Rope Leg Drop, Flint retained his title as a reluctant Peter Daye hit the 3 count.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Grabbing up his belt, Flint ran into the back, disappearing from sight. The fans were cheering wildly when the camera broke away to the back and focussed in on a smiling Skip Westervelt and Chip.)
<Skip Westervelt> First it was your precious little Sasha and next it's your TV
Title ... (Westervelt stopped himself with a waving hand.) Let me rephrase that ... my Televison Title ... next, I'm going to be taking back my TV Title ... and just for kicks, if Sasha is stupid enough to show up
... the Chipster just might have to Splash her one more time ...
(With the pair laughing, a figure jumped into view attacking them ... it was
Neo Draven with some paybacks as the camera faded to black.)
