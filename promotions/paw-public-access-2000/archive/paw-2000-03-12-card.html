PAW — Card Archive

3.12 Card

12 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera opens in the parking where we find the Dead Domain members ... Neo
Draven, Sasha and Chip walking. Passing fans milling about, the three near the entrance to the PAW television studio where Skip Westervelt rushes up from behind and knocks Neo Draven to the ground before he is swarmed over by
Security that rushes out. With the few fans cheering, both parties are seperated ... Skip Westervelt shouting at at Neo Draven to come and get some. For his part, Neo Draven is being held back by partner Chip as the lovely black clad Sasha steps forward and blows a kiss at Westervelt, enraging him further.
With Skip still struggling to break free, the Dead Domain members turns and disappear inside before the camera fades out.
With a smooth transition, the camera fades in on the tag team duo of Pep Preston and Emilio Ramirez inside the PAW 2000 locker room. The veteran Preston steps forward wringing his hands.)
<Pep Preston> With Mean Team 2000 getting back together and stealing the Tag
Belts, there hasn't been one "real" tag team out there to take them one ... oh sure, you have some great wrestlers coming together like Jake Morgan and Justin
Hart joining forces ... but no one thrown together like that is going to beat a well oiled machine like Flint and Green ... not until we get a shot at the belts!
(The smaller Ramirez holds his index finger up, showing who is #1 before they fade from view when large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession.)
... 5 ... ... 4 ... ... 3 ... ... 2 ... ... 1 ... (Rob Zombie's "More Human than Human" starts to play loudly as clips of Public
Access Wrestling 2000's Superstars in action passes ... Skip Westervelt ...
Justin Hart ... Jake Morgan ... Neo Draven and Sasha ... Brian Cage ... Johnny
Green and finally, the Texas Heavyweight Champion, Jason Flint, standing in the center of the ring lifting his title belt skyward ...
With an explosion, the scenes disappear from the screen and are replaced by the logo of Public Access Wrestling 2000 as it moves forward, filling the screen.
The song suddenly stops and with a smooth transition, fades out ... With a just as smooth fade in, the camera pans the wrestling ring surrounded by a vocal but small group of fans before passing over the small studio set.
Standing off to the side, in front of the PAW Banner, host Aaron Starr smiles into the camera.)
<Aaron Starr> Hello everyone and welcome once again to Public Access Wrestling
2000! We have a great line-up for this evening ... not only does Jason Flint defend his PAW Texas Heavyweight Title but we have a Cage Match between hardcore standout Brian Cage and relative newcomer Drake Russell ...
(There was a smattering of cheers and boos as Jason Flint walked out from the back followed by Kimberly Davis and the State Club Boyz. After handing his
Title Belt to Kimberly, Flint looked over the top of his sunglasses out at the loud and rowdy fans before turning his attention back towards Starr then tearing the microphone from his grasp.
Startled, Aaron Starr took a couple of steps back as Flint addressed the fans.) <Jason Flint> I'm getting tired of all this talking ... and you Aaron Starr, boring these people ... can you hear that? I can hear the distinct sound of snoring.
(The booing grew a little louder as Flint gestured towards the seats.) But enough of this ... I think these people want to see some wrestling ... right now! So ... Neo get your ass out here ... it's time for your beating! (Flint dropped the mic and headed into the ring as the lights dimmed briefly as
"Dead Souls" by Nine Inch Nails started to play when the members of the Dead
Domain walked out from the back.
Neo Draven rolled into the ring and had a few words for the champion as referee
Peter Daye took the Texas Title Belt from Kimberly and paraded around the ring with it, stopping in front of Neo Draven to show him what he was fighting for.
After handing it back to Kimberly, he called for the bell.) *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Draven and Flint started off slow as they circled the ring before locking up in the center. Flint went on the offensive with a Hip Toss, following up with a chain of moves into a Leg Drop and Scissoring the arm into a Wristlock. Draven made it to his feet but was taken right back down.
Daye called for a break, saying that Draven touched the ropes which brought the
Champion up and arguing. It almost cost him as Draven rolled him up in a School
Boy, getting a 2 count in the process.
Leaving the ring, Flint went down as the suicidal Draven dove to the outside with a Plancha. After several boots to the back from the State Club Boyz,
Draven was rolled back into the ring where Flint came back to the action landing a Slingshot Leg Drop. Draven pushed him off on a near 3 count ... a somewhat slow count.
With a Knee to the stomach, Flint was put down by a Gut Wrench Suplex allowing
Draven to go to the air with a Split Legged Suplex. On the pin attempt, Draven was pulled off by Curtis of the SCB's. Chip came running around the ring, putting down Joseph with a Clothesline then running into Tony.
The State Club Boyz drew a stern warning from the ref before retreating to their corner. Inside the ring, Flint and Draven wrestled for control. Draven
Whipped Flint into the ropes and dove over the top on the return, taking Flint down with a Sunset Flip. A Boot to the head broke the hold.
Pulling Draven up by his hair, Flint Snapmared him over, Drop Kicking him in the back of the head. Pushing Draven into ta corner, Flint's Roundhouse Kick had the man out on his feet before climbing the ropes and jumping onto Draven and taking him down with a Hurricarana. With a German Suplex, Flint pinned
Draven's shoulders to the mat but Daye stopped the count as he tried to usher
Chip from the ring who had entered, complaining about one of the State Club
Boyz being on the ring apron.
Curtis tossed a chair into the ring, leaving the apron as Daye returned to the match. Flint climbed the ropes and jumped off with his finisher, the Shockwave.
Twisting through the air, Flint landed on the mat with his Corkscrew Splash. Having rolled out of the way, Draven was back on his feet angrily Stomping away before grabbing up the chair and swatting Flint over the head with it.
Bouncing off the ropes, Draven dropped an Elbow on Flint. Getting back to his feet, Draven threw himself back against the ropes again, this time taking a
Boot to the back by Tony from the apron. Stumbling forward, he found himself
Speared.
Following a Short Arm Clothesline, Flint went to town putting Draven down with several Arm Drags then working the arm over with an Armbar into a Wristlock and back into an Armbar. Pushing Draven to his knees, Flint rolled his body to the mat with a Step Over Armbar Cradle. Flint got a 2 count before a kick out.
Draven slid out of the ring and was desperately trying to work some feeling back into the arm. Flint didn't waste any time as he mounted the top turnbuckle and came off landing a Double Axehandle to Draven's shoulder. While shouting a command for a table to the SCB's, Flint never saw Chip coming in, taking the
Drop Kick to the back. That was not going to go unpaid as Tony and Curtis came in and took care of Draven's large partner.
Getting a table set up, Flint allowed Draven to get in a shot from behind, knocking him to his knees with a hammering Forearm. With Flint down, Joseph went after Draven. Going toe to toe, Draven got the upped hand thanks to Sasha who came in and lit up Joseph's face with a huge fireball.
Standing over the writhing SCB member, Sasha gloated until Kimberly Davis grabbed her by the hair and spun her around. Two Forearms were followed by a
Hip Toss Powerslam onto the hard floor and Sasha was finished for the evening.
Pushing Kimberly off of Sasha, Draven was tapped on the shoulder. Snapping his head around, Draven saw a smiling Skip Westervelt with a chair held overhead.
Bringing it down over Draven's cranium with a sickening sound brought a collective gasp from the fans. Skip had a big smile on his face as he made his way to the back stopping long enough to threaten the SCB's from further attacking Chip. As they turned to help Joseph, Skip disappeared into the back.
Referee Peter Daye started counting with both legal wrestlers down. Halfway through the 20 count, Flint regained his feet. Stumbling forward towards
Kimberly, he helped her up returning to the action when sure she was okay. Grabbing Draven up roughly, Flint tossed him across the table then climbed into the ring. Going up the ropes, he leapt off spinning his body through the air and crashing through the table with his Corkscrew Splash finisher, the
Shockwave. Draped over Neo Draven, Daye had no recourse but to slap out a 3 count. *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (The fans were cheering at the finish of the match as Flint was pulled to his feet. Peter Daye quickly called for help from the back as Flint went to grab his Texas Title Belt.
The camera broke away from the ring and panned the small but very vocal fans who were all on their feet clapping for the action. Fading out from the ring area, the camera found it's way into the back of the studio were Lisa Doyle was standing with PAW President B.J. Perris.)
<Lisa Doyle> I'm here with B.J. Perris, President of Public Access Wrestling
... <Perris> Lisa ... sometimes you get what you wish for ... Jason Flint's tired of all the talk, so I'm going to give him exactly what he wants! April 2nd ...
Primal Rage ... assuming you still have it ... you're going to put the PAW 2000
Texas Heavyweight Title on the line against the former Champion, Jake Morgan
... and to make this a little more interesting, we're going to make it a Ladder
Match!
The time for talking is over! (An angered Perris storms off leaving a bewildered Lisa Doyle standing there. The camera moved away to another part of the back where EMT's were carrying Neo
Draven out on a stretcher, being followed by a worried Sasha and a just as concerned Chip. As they made their way to the back of the building, the voice of Jake Morgan was heard.)
<Jake Morgan> Hey ... fireball ... (Both Sasha and Chip turned around to see the smiling ... and advancing Morgan. Stepping inbetween Morgan and his target, Chip was ready for a fight when PAW
Security rushed in to keep both parties separated. As Neo Draven was taken out of the building, Jake Morgan was lead away in the opposite direction.
Moving back to the ring area, Skip Westervelt was being introduced by Aaron
Starr as he entered the ring. With Skip and Johnny Green inthe ring, all that was missing was Jake Morgan.
AC/DC's "If you want blood you got it" blared over the PA system as Morgan walked out to the ring. He didn't look too happy as Green called for a mic.)
<Johnny Green> Okay students ... let's go over our lessons ... one more time! (Green walked around the ring, stopping in the center as he held up a hand with his fingers outstretched.)
Jake Morgan has been taught quite a few things by quit a few people since coming here to wrestle ... let's see ...
There was Perris ... (Green dropped a finger.) ... 13 ... (One by one, his fingers dropped. Jake Morgan stepped up to the center of the ring, getting into Green's face.)
... the Offspring ... ... Jason Flint ... (Green's thumb was sticking straight up from his hand, the last remaining digit on his hand still up.)
Which brings us to ... today, Morgan ... what lesson can you be taught today? What can I teach you? Let's try this one? (Green dropped his thumb, balling his hand into a fist and smashing Morgan in the head. With the start of the action, referee Peter Daye called for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Green dropped down and started hammering away with a pistonlike RIght Hand. Stopped only by the ref, Green Bodyslammed Morgan before dropping a leg across his throat. The match didn't start off too well for the former Champion as
Green continued with his assault which was being booed by the ringside fans.
The tide turned after a Suplex when Skip Westervelt got involved with a Somersault Leg Drop onto Green and a pin attempt. Daye slapped out a 2 count before Morgan pulled him up by the hair. Tossing Westervelt aside, Morgan
Powerbombed Green before taking a boot to the back of his head. Green ducked under a Clothesline after a Whip into the ropes but ran right into Green's boot.
Green took a Kick to the stomach then was laid out with a Side Russian Leg
Sweep. Mountiing the ropes, Westervelt celebrated for a second before being pulled down from Morgan, taking a clubbing Forearm Smash. Pulled off his knees,
Skip was tossed across the ring with a Double Arm Suplex. Green came in with a
Running Knee to Morgan's chest, knocking him back into the corner. After several Knife Hand Chops, Green went across the ring and took off with a running start. In the center if the ring, he was dropped to the mat by a Drop
Toe Hold.
Pulled up, Green was Bulldogged and in quick succession, Morgan put a boot into the face of Green's attacker. Whipped into the ropes, Green was scooped into the air but was able to slide off of Morgan's back, dropping him with a
Inverted DDT. Morgan kicked out on a 2 count. Reversing a Whip from Skip, Morgan sent him into the ropes then took him down on the return with a Big Back Body Drop. Roaring out his approval, he never saw
Johnny Green coming into the ring with a Slingshot Drop Kick. With thew others down, Green went to his corner, getting a chair from Tony of the State Club
Boyz.
Banging the chair on the canvas, Green waited until Morgan got back to his feet before smashing it over his head. Morgan dropped like a sack of potatoes. Green held the dented chair up for the fans to see which brought a howl of cheers and boos alike. Getting back to his feet, the now bleeding Morgan motioned for
Green to deliver another blow. Looking around Green did just that, bringing the chair down on top of Morgan's head. Morgan took a small step backwards no-selling the blow and motioning for more. By now, the fans were really howling as Green wound up for another blow getting the same result.
Morgan raised a shaking fist and slowly approached Green who dropped to his knees and was begging off. Morgan reared back with a fist but was grabbed by two of the State Club Boyz who had come to the ring for the match. Blocking blows from both Tony and Curtis, Morgan took them down with two tremendous
Right Hands.
Green crumbled as Morgan drove his Elbow into the top of his head. With Gren down, Morgan scooped up the dented chair and held it up, pointing down at the prone Green. The fans were cheering loudly then started booing as Morgan tossed the chair down.
Pulling Green up, Morgan took a Drop Kick to the back from Skip Westervelt. Grabbing him off the ropes, Skip drop down with a Hangman's Neckbreaker. Bouncing up onto the middle rope, Westervelt came off with a Moonsault that missed it's mark as Morgan rolled out of the way at the last second. Morgan dropped and Elbow but missed as well as Skip returned the favor. Frustrated,
Morgan pummeled him before taking him up the rops in the corner.
Dropping back with a Superplex, Morgan rolled over Skip, getting a 2 count before being pulled off by Green. With Westervelt down, Green and Morgan battled around the ring, throwing everything they had at each other ...
Punching, Kicking, Choking one another. A Double Drop Kick by Westervelt sent them both over the ropes and to the outside. With a running start, Westervelt dove to the outside, taking both men down. The State Club Boyz seperated the three and started to work over Jake
Morgan, drawing a count from referee.
After backing away from the action, Green took over on Morgan Whipping him into the ring barrier and setting off another brawl, this time around the outside of the ring. Chants of "EXTREME!" went up after Morgan's Double Underhook Suplex on Westervelt and a Bodyslam of Green onto the ring steps. With a momentary reprieve, Morgan pulled a table out from under the ring. Leaning it back against the ring barrier, Morgan grabbed Westervelt around the waist and lifted him into the air, dropping him down through the table. Daye was instantly in
Skip's face checking to see if he was okay.
Grabbing Green up, Morgan took two Rights to his bloody head but was still able to drop Green down across the ring barrier, crotching him. Green looked hurt before he slid off.
Pulling Westervelt from the table, Morgan looked around before planting him with a Sidewalk Slam. Dropping down, he pinned Skip for a 2 count when the
SCB's were back in the picture, pulling him off. A warning from Daye as well as big Right Hand from Morgan sent them scurrying. Rolling Westervelt back into the ring, Morgan returned to Green.
With the fans cleared away by Security, Morgan was given room to operate, picking up Green and dropping him facefirst onto a chair, collapsing it.
Throwing Green over the barrier, Morgan rolled him back into the ring. With a
Whip into the ropes, Morgan took Green down with a Shoulder Block.
Pushing Green into the air and holding him there, Morgan was caught by Westervelt's Spinning Back Kick, falling backwards and dropping Green on top of himself. Morgan tossed Green off on a 2 count.
Westervelt's Face Driver and a Double Suplex off a Whip into the ropes had
Morgan in bad trouble ... his only saving grace coming from Green and
Westervelt battling it out around the ring, trading moves. Westervelt got the upperhand on an Eye Rake and a Rolling Senton. Before going for the pin, Green was pulled from the ring by the SCB's.
After some gestures, Skip turned his attention back to Morgan, working him up the ropes. Getting on the top, Westervelt balanced himself as he set up for the
Super SkippiPlex. Almost getting him in position, Westervelt lost his balance as Johnny Green shook the ring ropes ... sending both men tumbling. Spinning his body, Morgan grabbed Skip around the head and put him down with a DDT.
Nearly out himself, Morgan barely had an arm draped over Westervelt. Green tried to rush into the ring but was bumped off the apron as referee Peter
Daye threw his body back against the ropes, hitting Green and sending himi falling into the SCB's, knocking them over. Dropping to the mat, Daye slapped out a fast 3 count on the canvas.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (The fans erupted as Daye lifted Morgan's hand into the air while on the outside of the ring, an angry Johnny Green had to be restrained by the State
Club Boyz. Green was shouting at Morgan the whole way before he was pulled into the back.
In no shape himself, Morgan didn't follow, dropping to his knees and catching his breath. Skip Westervelt rolled out of the ring holding his head. Staggering forward, Westervelt approached Morgan and eyeballed him. The crowd went silent as the two looked at each other for long seconds. Finally, Westervelt thrust his hand forward which was taken by Morgan. After shaking, Westervelt raised
Morgan's hand which brought a round of cheers.
Leaving the ring, the camera made it's way into the back where Lisa Doyle was standing with Brian Cage.)
<Lisa Doyle> Brian ... thoughts on your upcoming Cage Match against newcomer
Drake Russell?
(Cage grinned.) <Brian Cage> LOOK! I'm not here to take about Drake Russell! <Doyle took a step back as Cage barked at her.) I am the youth of the PAF'NW !!! Not this dead wanna be jackass Neo Draven!!! I am calling for a youth movement to be lead by one man the main man in the PAW !!!
(Cage laughed as he paused.) No, not Jason Flint ... the true PAW Champion, Jake Morgan!!! Morgan, if you show me the way I can be a good friend for you and your quest to get your title back ... so, Morgan what do you say?
(Cage stood there wringing his hands before he was knocked to his knees with a chairshot from behind. Stepping into view was an angered Drake Russell.)
<Drake Russell> What did I tell you? (A second shot put Cage down.) What did I tell you? I told you not to overlook me! (Throwing the chair aside, Russell pulled Cage up by his hair, taking a Low
Blow. Dropping on top of him, Cage started wailing away with his fists touching off a brawl in the back as both men started their match just a little early.
Security rushed in to pry the two apart as Lisa Doyle scrambled out of the way. With Security pulling them away, things got out of hand as soon others became involved in the battle as it was joined by Neo Draven and Chip, Skip
Westervelt, Jake Morgan, Desperado, the State Club Boyz, Johnny Green and Paco. With tempers flaring, people paired off as the Security did their best to restore order. As soon as they pulled two wrestlers off each other, two more were going at it. This continued for several minutes as the combatants battled it up and down the hallway.
All fighting stopped with a blood curdling screech. Turning to see the source, everyone saw Neo Draven's Sasha laying out on the floor and Jake Morgan walking away from the scene.
The camera moved away from the back and made it's way to the ring where Aaron
Starr was announcing the participants of the next match. The fans were already drooling for the final match of the evening when Drake Russell walked out to the ring surrounded by PAW Security. After pulling sharply on the cage to test it, he entered.
With Russell inside the ring, Korn's "Blind" played over the PA announcing the arrival of Brian Cage. There was a mixed reception to the hardcore wrestler.
With a disgusted wave, he flipped off a few of his loudest detractors and neared the cage. Inside, Russell stepped back allowing his opponent to come inside.
As referee Peter Daye locked the two men inside, a voice was heard over the PA system. Hearing the voice, Cage and Russell turned around to see Johnny Green walking out towards the ring, followed by two of the State Club Boyz.)
<Johnny Green> You know, Cage ... you're living in a dreamworld if you think you're the future of this promotion ...
(Green laughed as he looked at the fans.) And ... you're sadly mistaken if you think these idiots sitting out here getting ready to watch the two of bash each other's brains in think for one second that you are part of this so-called "Youth Movement" you're calling for ...
(Green smiled as the boos cascaded in from the crowd.) I'll agree ... you're a movement alright ... (Green and both SCB's were laughing as Green approached the cage.) ... a bowel movement ... before you start promising to return belts to their rightful owner ... you better deal with your current problems ...
(Saying that, Green stepped back as Russell slammed into Cage. Green stepped back towards the fans, reaching across the ring barrier, taking a chair from a fan. Closing it, he tossed it over the top of the cage into the ring while
Russell picked up Cage and put him down with a Body Slam., prompting Peter Daye to call for the bell from the outside of the cage.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Pulling Cage to his feet, Russell smashed his head over and over into the turnbuckle before spinning him around and hitting with Reverse Knife Hands to the chest. The fans counted out as each blow landed solidly. As Cage slumped into the corner, Russell grabbed up the chair and held it up, then brought it down, denting it over Cage's head.
Slumping down to the seat of his pants, Cage looked finished before he even started. With Cage down, Drake Russell started to climb up the cage, getting nearly to the top when Cage grabbed the cage and started pulling on it, dislodging Russell and causing him to nearly fall off. It was enough to slow his climb, giving Cage time to get up and grab ahold of Russell's leg. Using all his strength, Cage tugged Russell from the cage, sending both men to the canvas.
From a top mounted position, Cage reigned down blows before being tossed off. With his leg, Cage blocked a Belly-to-Belly Suplex, taking Russell over with one of his own. Following that up with two Elbow Drops, Cage was in control.
Sending Russell into the ropes with a Whip into the ropes, Cage took him down with a Clothesline then dropping down with a Headbutt. Climbing up the ropes,
Cage came down with a Leg Drop across Russell's throat.
Back on his feet, Russell jabbed a thumb into Cage's eye, driving him back. Doubled over from a Boot to the stomach, Cage was dropped with a Double Underhook Back Breaker. With Cage back on his feet, Russell took a Back Elbow to the head then sent down with a Short Arm Clothesline.
Cage walked around the ring and collected the chair, glaring at Johnny Green who was still watching. Clunking Russell over the head brought the first blood to the match. Throwing down the chair in the center of the ring, Cage DDT'ed
Drake Russell onto it, laying him out in the center of the ring. Dropping down with a naked Choke, Cage did his best to try to send Drake Russell into unconsciousness.
Satisfied, Cage left the man and started to climb the cage. From the top rope,
Cage reached up towards the top and continued his climb when Russell followed, hammering repeated Forearms into Cage's back. Doing his best to hold on, Cage fired back with several rights of his own. Kicking a foot out, Cage caught
Russell knocking him from the cage but losing his hold as well, sprawling to the mat.
Back on his feet first, Drake Russell hit a Spinning Back Kick knocking him into the ropes following with a quick DDT. Hitting the ropes, Russell came off with a Corkscrew Splash.
Cage was busted open as his head was ground into the metal wire. Reversing a
Whip, Cage sent Russell across the ring, sending him up over the ropes.
Catching him staggering backwards, Cage dropped with a Neckbreaker. The match continued with both men power moves in the center of the ring. Drake appeared to be out on his feet after suffering a Chokeslam and a Tilt-A-Whirl
Backbreaker. He was able to come back with a Sidewalk Slam and followed that up with a Moonsault before locking on a Boston Crab.
In pain, Cage crawled forward, reaching the ropes. Pulling himself up, Cage was able to make Russell lift up, easing some of the pressure. Turning around,
Russell held onto Cage's legs as he continued to move up on the ropes. Pulling
Cage free, Russell dropped him on his face. With his opponent down, he started to climb once again getting as high as the top rope before he was caught and put down with a Powerbomb. Sitting on the top turnbuckle, Cage dropped a Knee, rolling free.
Back in control, Cage's Reverse DDT seemed to take all the fight out of Russell. The fans sensing the end of the fight at hand began booing. Putting the chair over Russell's face, Cage came down with a Leg Drop with a loud clang.
With blood running down his face, Russell was led around the ring, having his raked along the ring ropes. Snapping his head back, he caught Cage in the face staggering him. Wheeling around, Russell's Back Fist also caught him flush in the nose. A Kick put him down on the mat where Russell followed with a Drop
Kick to the face.
Lifted off his feet with a Full Nelson, Cage shifted his weight and threw
Russell over. A Boot to the head sent Russell into the corner. Grabbing the ropes and jumping into the air, Cage came down with a Knee into Russell's leg.
Sending Russell across the ring with a Slingshot, Cage came back with an Elbow
Drop that missed. Both men were slowly wearing the other down. Circling, each man looked for an opening, taking several potshots with jabs. Catching an arm,
Russell took Cage down with a Fujiwara Armbar.
Cage was pulled to his feet and slipped in behind Russell but took two Back
Elbows to the face. Reversing positions, Russell dropped Cage with a
Belly-to-Back Crossed Arm Suplex onto his head. Leaving Cage in pain, Drake Russell began to climb the cage. Battered, Brian
Cage followed behind. Swinging a leg over the top, Russell was almost out when
Cage caught hold of him by the leg. Using the leg to pull himself up, Cage also got to the top and an arm around Russell's waist. Trying to fight him off,
Russell hit with an elbow to Cage's head knocking him free and sending him falling to the mat.
Without anyone to hold him back, Russell was able to swing his other leg over the cage and climb down for the win.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (The fans were on their feet cheering as the bloodied Drake Russell lifted his arms into the air as the show came to an end.)
