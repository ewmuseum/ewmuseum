5.7 Card

7 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera opens in the barbwire surrounded ring on the four competitors for tonight's action. Jake Morgan reaches behind him and pulls out a mic. He slowly brings it to his lips and begins to speak.)
<Jake Morgan> I stand in the ring with three men who have more guts, more BALLS than any of the so-called FUCKING "Elite." Hell, the supposed "BEST'" that PAW can offer were too Chicken-shit to step into this ring. They were afraid that when it comes down to it, that they just couldn't make the grade. They are afraid that they will fail in front of the fans, and then what will they be?
Will they still be the "Elite?" Maybe, or maybe not, but at least they won't be the "Fucking Cowards" that they are now.
(He pauses as the crowd reacts.) There's still time boys and we have plenty of time. Come on out and show us just how fucking good you are.
(He waits and when nothing happens, he turns back to the men in the ring.) Just like I thought. Fuck them. It's our time, let's do this. (The fans cheer loudly as Morgan throws down the mic and exits the ring, heading for the back. The camera fades into the back of the PAW television studio upon show host Aaron Starr walking with Lisa Doyle. An animated Starr waves his arms around excitedly as he talks to the following Doyle.)
<Aaron Starr> ... all week, we've been talking. Because I'm over with both the fans and the guys in the back ... B.J.'s going to be expanding my role in the company ...
(Doyle can only roll her eyes at the thought as Starr continues.) Maybe ... I can talk B.J. into doing something for you ... you know, B.J. and I are just like this ...
(A smug Starr holds his crossed fingers up to show how "tight" he and the president are. Again, Doyle says nothing as she lets out an exasperated sigh along with her eye rolling. Walking past the pair is the former PAW Texas
Heavyweight Champion, Jake Morgan.
Starr stops and turns back towards Morgan and with a cocky little grin winks at
Doyle as he speaks.)
Hey Champ ... how's it going? (Stopping dead in his tracks, Morgan turns and grabs Starr by the throat and shoves him back against the wall.)
<Jake Morgan> What's that supposed to mean? (Without an answer, Morgan is struck from behind by an enraged Johnny Green who came in out of nowhere. Pulling Starr out of the way, Doyle quickly vacates the area as Green pummels the surprised Morgan with a chain-wrapped fist, beating him to the ground and leaving him a bloody mess.
Leaving the back, the camera finds it's way outside the building as a black stretch limo pulls up and stops. A man in an immaculate suit steps out of the car and makes his way towards one of the back doors. Holding it open, he steps aside as two large men in identical suits step out. A third person steps out of the vehicle but is obscured by the trio of men who block the camera's view.
Trying to move around them for a better look, a hand goes up and blocks the lens as large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession.)
... 5 ... ... 4 ... ... 3 ... ... 2 ... ... 1 ... (Rob Zombie's "More Human than Human" starts to play loudly as clips of Public
Access Wrestling 2000's Superstars in action passes ... Skip Westervelt ...
Drake Russell ... Jake Morgan ... Neo Draven and Sasha ... Brian Cage ... Johnny Green and finally, the Texas Heavyweight Champion, Jason Flint, standing in the center of the ring lifting his title belt skyward ...
With an explosion, the scenes disappear from the screen and are replaced by the logo of Public Access Wrestling 2000 as it moves forward, filling the screen.
The song suddenly stops and with a smooth transition, fades out ... With a just as smooth fade in, the camera pans the ring area where the fans are cheering loudly before focussing in on a lone B.J. Perris standing in front of his desk with a mic in hand.)
<Perris> Welcome to Public Access Wrestling ... and tonight's opening round of
HardKore Karnage!
(Perris smiles as the fans cheer, waiting long enough for the noise to die down before continuing.)
We have two big matches for you that promise to encompass hardcore wrestling
... the best ... and worst of extreme wrestling ... around the ring are alternating "pits" of glass and thumbtacks as well as stacked barbed wire wrapped tables.
I must warn you that these matches are not for the squeamish and are not for everyone in the viewing audience. Our first draw in the tournament has hardcore battler Brian Cage taking on the high flying daredevil Paco in an explosive match that can only be called "Last Man Standing" ... who will be the last man standing in a match with explosive barbed wire boards ... and in the second match ... Jake Morgan takes on Pep Preston in a barbed wire "Dog Collar" match
... with the winners of today's fights advancing to next week's finals for this
...
(Perris motions off-camera which is followed as the camera focusses in on a tall trophy standing nearly 5 feet tall.After looking it over, the camera returns to Perris who is holding a hand to his ear before looking.)
I'm getting word from the back that Jake Morgan is ... let's go to Lisa Doyle
... (Fading into view, Lisa Doyle can be seen standing in front of EMT's as they are working on someone in the background.)
<Lisa Doyle> B.J. can you hear me? I'm standing in the back where Jake Morgan was just viciously beaten by MT2K member Johnny Green ... he came in out of nowhere and hit Jake with a chain ... busting him open badly as you can tell by the amount of blood ...
(Lisa points off towards a huge puddle of blood on the floor and a red smear along the wall.)
After beating Morgan into near unconsciousness, Green was forced off by PAW
Security. From the looks of things ... I don't think Jake Morgan can come back from this today ... back to you B.J. ...
(The camera returned to the ring where Paco was awaiting the arrival of his opponent, Brian Cage. As he waited, he checked on the barbed wire boards that were propped up in each of the corners.
"Blind" by Korn started to play as Cage strolled out from the back and made his way into the ring. After last instructions from the ref, the match was underway.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** The smaller Paco came out ready to fight as he went on the offensive concentrating on Cage's knee. A Drop Kick to the knee put Cage down which was followed by several Stomps and a Leg Drop across it off the ropes. Cage was limping badly after Paco dropped his weight down on the leg that was propped up on the middle rope.
Trying to Whip Cage into the corner was no good as the self proclaimed hardcore icon stopped short of the board just waiting for him. Turning around, Cage had a smile on his face but took a Drop Kick to the chest that sent him back into barbed wire board. Shattering it as his weight collapsed it set off the explosive charge that sent smoke and loud boom through the studio. Paco drew first blood.
Sending Cage repeatedly into the ropes and taking him down with an assortment of Leg Lariats and Diving Shoulder Blocks, Paco dropped across Cage for the pin but was tossed off each time.
Tossing Cage to the outside, Paco climbed the ropes and jumped off and wrapped his legs around his opponet's neck. Spinning his body, Paco sent Cage into the large pit filled with thumbtacks.
Back on his feet, the profusely bleeding Cage grinned at Paco before hammering him with Forearms, driving him back into the ring apron. After a Boot to the stomach, Cage pulled free one of the barbed wire wrapped tables and laid it unopened across the pit full of glass shards. Dragging Paco up onto the apron,
Cage lifted him into the air before dropping down, Power Slamming him through the table and into the glass.
Pulling Paco up by his hair, Cage dug a shard of glass into his head, opening him up. With a Body Slam onto the broken table, Cage looked to have things well in hand. Beating the man around the ring, Brian Cage continued to grin before
Powerbombing Paco into the thumbtacks.
Rolling Paco into the ring, Cage pushed in a table as well. Not satisfied, he pulled loose a strand of barbed wire from the broken table and wrapped it around his arm then joined Paco who was trying to pull himself to his feet, his face covered in blood. What he got was a pummeling as Cage worked him over with the Forearm which was now covered in Paco's blood.
With his opponent slumped against the ropes, Cage set up the table in the center of the ring then put one of the boards on top of it. Throwing Paco up over his shoulder, Cage sat down into a Powerbomb, dropping Paco onto and through the table that collapsed with an explosion and a burst of light and smoke.
Paco spasmed on the wreckage of the table while Cage laid there flat on his back. Peter Daye was in talking to Paco who was not moving. On the outside of the ring, Sara E. Xavier, who had played no part in the match watched with a grimace on her face as Cage stirred.
Back on his feet, he leaned heavily on the ropes and watched as a hearty Paco dragged himiself towards the ropes and tried to pull himself to his feet. The fans cheered when Paco motioned for Cage to come and get some ... and Cage did just that, throwing heavy Punches backing the smaller man into a corner.
Lifting Paco up the ropes, Cage never expected to have a pair of legs wrapped his neck but clamped on tightly and was able to spin his body, throwing Cage over with a Leg Scissor Takedown. Hoping up quickly, Paco hit the ropes and came off with a Moonsault. Criss-crossing the ring and hitting the ropes, he built up steam for a Somersault Senton but hit canvas as Cage rolled out of the way at the last second.
Not in the best shape himself, Cage Whipped Paco into the ropes and caught him on the rebound, putting him down with a Sidewalk Slam. Lifting him up over his shoulders, Cage ran towards one of the two remaining boards. Stopping short, he tossed Paco off his back and through the board which exploded with a loud bang.
Twisted up in barbed wire and the broken and burning wood, Paco needed help from the ref who called for the bell, ending the match as Cage dropped to his knees.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Help rushed out from the back and hit the ring. Cage was slow to get to his feet but finally did so and left the ring with some help from Sara who had come over. The fans were going crazy when "Wait and Bleed" by Slipknot stared to play over the PA system as three men dressed in black suits walked out from the back and stood in a line facing Brian Cage. They were joined by a fourth man in a white suit who had a big smile on his face.
The man pulled of his dark glasses and scanned the small but very vocal crowd.) <Man> Well ... what do we have here? Some backward Texas town watching something the cat dragged in doing something he calls wrestling ...
(The fans started booing at the laughing man. In front of him, his three men stood unmoving.)
Long time no see, Brian ... (Cage pushed Sara behind him as he limped forward a couple of steps which brought a reaction from the three suited goons as they tightened up their ranks.)
<Brian Cage> Last time I saw you and your jackoffs, Watt, I left you all in a pool of your own blood ...
<Watt> Well, Brian as the saying goes ... times change ... people change ... well, in your case ... you seem to be doing the same as always ... getting your ass kicked by lesser *ahem* "talent" ...
(The three men laughed as the man in white's joke. Cage inched forward but stopped at Sara's urging.)
<Brian Cage> What do you want, Shawn? <Shawn Watt> Me? Want? (Watt stopped and smiled as he looked out at the fans who were silent, hanging on every word.)
You know damn well why we're here, Brian ... (Sara dropped down behind Cage and hit him with an uppercut to the crotch. The fans groaned as Cage sunk to his knees with the men in black moving in. Two
Stomped Cage while the third set up one of the tables on the outside.
The man in white was busy removing rings and jewelry before rolling up his sleeve as his men laid into Cage who was all but helpless. Two pulled up Cage and held in tight as their leader stepped up, finishing with his sleeve.
Cracking a big grin, he hit Cage with several big Right Hands before turning and walking off, stopping in front of Sara and offering her his arm. Taking it, the two walked off.
With the table in place, one man poured something over it's top before setting it on fire while the other two men lifted Cage and dropped him on top of it.
Cage went through the burning table and was rolling on the ground as the three men walked off. Peter Daye rushed in to help Cage as the camera broke away to the back to find Johnny Green beating Pep Preston in one of the dressing rooms.
Green had him by the throat as he hammered away.) <Johnny Green> Doesn't look like you're going to Perris' little party today ... (Green continued to pound away as Preston dropped to the floor.) Isn't that just too bad ... (Leaving the image of Green and Pep Preston behind, the camera made it's way to the outside of the television studio where the limo of Shawn Watt and his henchmen was left. As the camera zooms in, something is apparently not right.
Moving ever closer, the car is covered in paper stuck all over it ... on those pages, a single image ... a black handprint on the white paper ...
Moving back inside, the camera finds Drake Russell standing with B.J. Perris watching a monitor with the image of Johnny Green standing inside the ring.)
<Johnny Green> With Morgan taken care of ... who is it going to be? (In the background, the chants of the fans were heard as they chanted the name of Pep Preston. Johnny Green could only smile at the sound.)
Oh yeah ... Pep Preston ... I just kicked his ass in the back. I don't think he's going to be able to make it tonight.
(The fans were starting to boo.) Wait a second ... I know who you can bring out. Why not bring "Mr. Disgrace" himself, Drake Russell? Unless of course, he's got his head so far up Perris' ass and he can't hear me ... C'mon Russell, why not show us all what your "open season" means ... big bad
Drake Russell ...
(The monitor went dead as Perris stepped forward and turned it off as he shook his head. Drake Russell was fuming and starting to leave the room when he was grabbed by Perris and whirled around.)
<Perris> Where the Hell do you think you're going? I keep telling you ... start thinking and acting like a champion ...
(Turning Perris walked off shaking his head as he left the room while Russell could only stare in disbelief.)
I'll take care of this ... (The camera followed Perris as he walked from the back out into the studio area and headed for the ring with a mic in hand.)
<Johnny Green> Ask for an opponent and you get a weasel ... what's the matter
Perris? Drake got a hangnail ... or maybe you want to step into the ring with me and get your ass handed to you?
(Perris smiled as he approached the ring when the fans let out a big cheer as a bandaged Jake Morgan ran out from the back and slid into the ring. Peter Daye stepped between the two wrestlers and pointed at the collar on the mat between the two. Morgan fastened the collar around his neck while Daye handed the other end to his opponent. Morgan pulled back sharply on the twisted strands of barbed wire pulling Green towards him then leveling him with a Clothesline.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Depsite the fact that they were was some six feet of wire attatched to the collars each men wore, they didn't go more than a feet away from each other as they slugged away at each other. In the middle of the ring, they wailed away making this look more like a boxing match rather than wrestling.Lefts and
Rights landed in this anything goes hardcore match. Referee Peter Daye stayed clear of the winging arms.
Morgan's stinging Punches were beginning to take their toll as Green slumped back against the ropes. Ducking under a Punch meant to take off his head, Green
Jabbed a thumb into Morgan's eye and came out of the corner with Reverse Knife
Hands that popped loudly off Morgan's chest. Spinning him around, Green pushed
Morgan into the ropes before Raking the barbed wire across his back, tearing his t-shirt and leaving red marks.
Dropping to his knees. Morgan had the wire looped around his neck and was being strangled from behind. There was a trickle of blood starting to flow from under the chin as Green pulled back. Lashing back with an Elbow, staggered Green away
.. at least 6 feet away for the time being.
With a Boot to the head, Morgan was back in business then pulling Green to his feet and hitting with a Clothesline that carried both men to the outside of the ring and into the thumbtacks. Both men were covered as they scrambled to get back to their feet, Morgan standing first.
Pushing Green's head down, the former Champ snapped him back with a Knee Lift before scooping him up and Slamming Green down into the thumbtacks again.
Green's cry of pain showed this was all business between the two. Rolling out,
Green was picking tacks out of the back of his head. Morgan smashed an Elbow into the back Green's head knocking him to the ground then got in behind him and applied the Camel Clutch. Morgan had a crazed look on his face as he leaned back.
Daye was watching closely for a submission but none came as Green shook his head. Getting off of Green, Morgan pulled a table over and hit Green then pushed his head down into the barbed wire wrapped around it. Pulling Green up by his hair, Morgan led him around the ring showing the fans the blood that was dripping down from his forehead.
A Back Elbow caught Morgan unawares who staggered back. A Boot to the groin and a Suplex put him down where Green ripped the torn t-shirt off his back before
Whipping him with the wire seperating them. Bloody welts appeared on Morgan's back as he twisted and turned to get out of the way but was having no luck.
Yanking up on the wire, Green pulled Morgan to his feet and hit him repeatedly in the back, driving him around the ring back to the table that was laying on the ground. Pointing to it before trying to lift Morgan, Green found himself blocked before he was moving through the air and Back Body Dropped onto the table. With the wire biting into his back, Green sat up quickly, taking a Boot to the back of his head.
Green rolled back to his feet and walked away from the ring followed of course by Morgan. Grabbing an arm, Morgan Whipped Green headfirst into the studio set, smashing him into the desk. Half dragging Green back to the ring, Morgan rolled him in and continued with the beating. After a DDT, Morgan pulled Green up the ropes and took him down with a Superplex before leaving the ring.
Punching at the glass ringside, Morgan wrapped his hand with a strip from his shirt then picked up an evilly sharp looking shard. Inside the ring, Morgan pulled Green close by his collar and dug the glass into his forehead, bringing even more blood that started to drip down. Chants of "Holy Shit" started as
Green was draped over the middle rope and Morgan once again left the ring.
Heading for a table, Morgan was just out of reach because of the tether between he and Green. Pulling on the wire, he pulled Green out of the ring and into the thumbtacks allowing himself to get to the table.
Picking it up, he tossed it into the ring then pulled Green back in with him. With the downed member of MT2K, Morgan propped the table in a corner and pointed down at Green who was bleeding profusely and trying to get back to his feet.
Hitting Green with Forearms, Morgan positioned him front of the table before running in with an arm extended. Green ducked in underneath and whipped his leg around catching Morgan in the back with a Spinning Back Kick, pushing the charging man facefirst into the table and breaking it in half.
The kick sent Green off-balance and to the canvas while Peter Daye pulled part of the table off an unmoving Morgan. Pulling himself to his feet, Green worked at the collar, pulling it from his neck and attatching it to the top rope.
Pushing Daye out of the way, Green yanked a bleeding Morgan to his feet and pushed him up the corner ropes then jumped up on the ropes himself and Kicking
Morgan in the face, sending him out of the ring and crashing down into the glass with a crash. Gasps came from the fans as Morgan fell facefirst into the sharp broken glass, his face now a bloody mess.
Free to move around without being constrained, Green picked up a potion of the broken table and climbed back up the ropes and waited for Morgan to regain his feet. Finally getting back up, Morgan pulled at the collar that fastened him to the ropes when Green came flying out of the ring and drove the table and barbed wire wrapped around it into Morgan's face. Both men crumpled to the ground drawing a count from Daye.
Both men got to their feet and once again were throwing Punches at each other. Ducking under, Green grabbed Morgan's arm and spun around to Whip him away from the ring. Going only as far as the collar around his throat would allow, Morgan was pulled back and dropped to the ground.
After having words from fans across the ring barrier, Green grabbed away a chair and returned to Morgan who was stuggling to get back to his feet.
Swinging the chair, Green missed his target as Morgan ducked his head then dove in taking Green off his feet and back into the razor sharp glass. On top of
Green, Morgan Punched away at the bloody face of his opponent before getting off and taking the collar off.
Outside of the ring, Morgan grabbed another table and pushed it into the ring before heading back and and dragging Green in with him. After a couple of Elbow
Drops, Morgan set up the table near the corner before returning to Johnny Green and pulling him along. From the top, Morgan whipped his arm around in the air before grabing Green around the neck and jumping off the ropes. Spinning around, Morgan drove Green down into ... and through the table with a Tornado
DDT. Amid the broken table and barbed wire, neither man moved which silenced the crowd before a chant of Morgan's name spread through the seats. Lifting first an arm, then up onto his knees, bleeding profusely, Morgan crawled on top of
Johnny Green. Dropping to the mat, Daye slapped out a 3 count and called for the bell.
*** Ding *** DIng *** Ding *** (Rolling out of the ring, Morgan limped to the back with his arms raised as the fans cheered loudly. The camera left the ringside area and the two wrestlers, focussing in on a face ... the long haired familiar face of the hardcore combatant known as Evil K!)
<Evil K> Hardcore? You don't know what Hardcore is ... (The evil looking man smiled a toothy grin.) But ... you will when Evil K brings his brand of high flying, death defying extreme wrestling to Public Access Wrestling ... next week for the finals of
Hardkore Karnage!
(Pointing into the camera, the screen goes black.) x-x-x-x-x Black Cat Entertainment©2000
