PAW — Card Archive

11.12 Card

12 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera opens on the green haired man strolling through the back of the PAw televsion studio. The look of disgust on his face and the mutterings under his breath tell his mood.)
Green haired man: What part of I’ll be there in a minute didn’t you understand? (His mocking tone disappears as he shakes his head and his voice returns to normal as he continues to talk to no one in particular as he moves down the busy hall.)
I’ll show what part I didn’t get, Morgan ... (A smile bursts onto his face as he passes a bending Lisa Doyle, her well formed bottom “looking up” at him. Stroking his stubbly chin, he reaches out and pinches it. Doing so, he grins until an angered Lisa Doyle stands and faces him. With ligthning speed, her hand lashes out and connects with the man’s cheek, making a loud slapping sound as it connects.)
Lisa Doyle: DON’T EVER DO THAT AGAIN! (Turning, she storms off leaving the green haired man stroking his reddened face. Moving to another part of the backstage area, the camera finds Mean
Darlene walking through the halls with her bags slung over her shoulder.
Smiling, she waves to employees as she makes her way through the back. Stopping in front a door, she twists and turns the doorknob but can't seem to get it open. With a surprised look on her face, Darlene tries the handle a second time but once again finds it won't open. Frustrated, she drops her bags and pounds on the door. When it doesn't open, she kicks it in frustration. Looking for an answer, she grabs the green haired production assistant as he walks by.)
Mean Darlene: Hey why is my locker room locked? Green haired man: Hey, don't ask me, your boyfriend locked it once he got here. Mean Darlene: Jake? Well, don't you have a key? Green haired man: I'm sure there's one around here somewhere but nobody's going to get it. Mean Darlene: Why? Green haired man: No one wants to get in his way ... the dude is losing it. (Darlene shakes her head and starts to walk off.) Where are you going? Mean Darlene: To get a key, moron. (He looks at her walking away, then at her bags. He smiles as the camera fades out on him before coming into focus on Lisa Doyle standing with Jake
Morgan’s sometime partner, the Trashman.
Doyle readies herself as Trash shifts from one foot to the other.) Lisa Doyle: Lisa Doyle here with the Trashman ... (She turns to the man carrying a Singapore Cane who snaps his head in her direction as she speaks.)
Trashman ... in the past few weeks, there seems to be an awful lot of animosity coming from you towards Drake Russell ... perhaps you can fill us in as to why?
Why all the dislike for this man who has just returned to PAW action? Trashman: Why, Lisa? Why? (Doyle sheepishly nods her head.) For months, we had to listen to the rantings about this man being the “Destiny” of Public Access Wrestling. During that same time, we had to endure him getting all kinds of chances and shot after shot at all the titles here ... the Texas
Title ... the Television Title ... even the Tag Titles ...
We’ve listened to all the trash talking about being the best but you and know that’s just a bunch of bullcrap ... we know that he was too scared to get involved in our little Hardcore Tournament last year ...
We know that he can’t hold onto a title if he gets his hands on it ... he lost his fleeting grasp on the Television Title to Christian Maeda and later had the
Tag Titles stripped from him and his little buddy, Maeda some months later.
Hell, Jake and I would have taken it from them given the chance ... And after being released when our former owner couldn’t afford to pay him, he was brought back ... and thrown right into the thick of things for the only remaining belt ... the PAW Television Title ...
Thrown right into a situation he doesn’t deserve ... (Trashman turns to Doyle and pokes her in the shoulder repeatedly.) ... and if you need more than that ... take this ... (Turning towards the camera, Trashman points directly into it.) Russell ... I just don’t like you! (As he storms off, the camera focusses in on the shrugging Lisa Doyle when large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession.)
.... 5 ... .... 4 ... .... 3 ... .... 2 ... .... 1 ... (White Zombie's "More Human than Human" begins as flashes of Hiroshima Nagasaki in action are shown. Scene after scene quickly flash by of past battles ...
Running his thumb across his throat before a limp Drake Russell is planted into the canvas with a Nagasaki Driver ...
Following a Flying Knee straight to an incoming Seth's face, Nagasaki locks on the “Unconditional Surrender” for a win ...
The “Street Soldier” Justin Hart takes a Double Thrust to the Throat and with a
Snap Suplex, put into a pinning predicament ...
Pep Preston is flat on his back as Nagasaki leaps from the top turnbuckle and lands the “H-Bomb” ...
In slow motion, Aaron Starr turns right into a ball of fire thrown into his face and finally the PAW Television Champion standing in the middle of the ring, the belt raised over his head.)
After an explosion, the scene fades to black and the PAW logo disappears before fading back in and panning the fans and their signs held aloft such as "Don’t like it? Go home!", "Destiny!" and "Almost back!".
After making it's way through the boisterious crowd of some 50 or so, the camera settles in on a short Asian man wearing a PAW blazer, standing in front of the new Hiroshima Nagasaki PAW banner. The smiling man adjusts his glasses while a few boos filter in from the audience.)
Asian man: Welcome to Public Access Wrestling ... I am your host, Ishimoto
Sakai ... (The man bows towards the camera with a short dip of his head which brings in a few more boos which quickly turn to cheers as Sakai is joined by a confused looking Jason Flint as he steps out from the back and looks at his co-host for the show.)
Jason Flint: Who is this?!?!?! (The man gives a similar bow as Flint takes his spot beside his new co-host.) Ishimoto Sakai: I am your host, Ishimoto Sakai ... (Flint disbelievingly shakes his head.) Jason Flint: Who? Ishimoto Sakai: Ish ... (Waving a hand, Flint cuts him off.) Jason Flint: Look, Ish ... I don’t give a rat’s ass who you are ... I can take over from here ...
(Turning away, Flint nods his head at the cheering fans.) Ishimoto Sakai: I am sorry Flint-san but in order to satisfy the National
Wrestling Council ... we are required to carry a two man announce team. With
Aaron Starr ... unable to “face up” to his duties after his unfortunate
“accident”, I have been signed on to be your co-host ... Jason Flint: Right ... (Flint rolls his eyes.) Anywho ... we have to big matches as we creep ever closer to my re-instatement ... (The fans cheer loudly.) Ishimoto Sakai: Yes ... we open with a contest of former Champions between Jake
Morgan and Drake Russell ...
Jason Flint: Rightio Ish ... after last week’s confrontation, this one has all the makings of a blood bath as Jake comes looking for some payback after taking a spill down a flight of stairs ...
... and did I mention, I was going to be back inside the squared circle soon? (It’s Sakai’s turn to roll his eyes as the fans let out a loud cheer.) Ishimoto Sakai: Yes, Jason ... you already did ... but enough about your antics as we move into the main event as the Lone Wolves bravely face the PAW
Television Champion, Hiroshima Nagasaki and the newly “hired gun”, Pep Preston
... who I believe was a partner of yours, Jason ... how that must have felt last week to be taken out by a friend ...
(Flint’s eyes narrow as he looks to his partner, Sakai.) Jason Flint: Right ... but back to the sleeper of a main event ... Ishimoto Sakai: Sleeper? The only ones that are going to be sleeping are the
Lone Wolves as they find themselves in the throes of defeat by the man who has single-handedly shaped this promotion in his image ...
(Flint chuckles as he adjusts his collar.) Jason Flint: Thanks Ish ... but I’m not in this match ... Ishimoto Sakai: Oh no, Jason ... you misunderstand me, I’m referring to the
“H-Bomb” and to see what kind of impact our Champion has had on this promotion, all one has to do is but look around ...
Ah ... but enough for now about our one true Champion ... Drake Russell has emerged from the back and his making his way to the ring ...
(Flint mutters under his breath but the words are drowned out by the rousing cheer for Russell. Climbing up onto the ring apron, he points at them with his
Singapore Cane before entering the ring.
Inside, he draws a warning from referee Peter Daye for the weapon before dropping it to the outside of the ring. A loud cheer goes up as AC/DC’s “If you want blood you got it” starts up over the PA system.
The music brings the fans to their feet as Drake Russell patiently waits in the ring for for his opponent. The cheers die down as no Morgan comes out from the back.
Restarting the music two more times and still no Jake, Daye begins to count him out. Into the count, the fans realize what is happening and start booing the ref’s actions.
At the count of Eight, Jake walks out from the back. The ref stops his count as Jake makes his way to the ring ignoring the the fans as he passes, concentrating solely on rubbing his temple. Entering the ring without incident,
Jake’s thoughts appear to be anywhere but the ring as Daye calls for the bell.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Without taking his eyes off of Jake, Drake begins to point to the ringside spot that Darlene usually occupies. He starts to talk to Jake but gets nothing but a blank stare from him. Drake gets frustrated and shoves Jake.
Jake is pushed back a few feet but he doesn't even react, he just steps back up to Drake. They do this a few times before Drake turns to the ref in frustration. As soon as he diverts his attention, Jake moves into action.
After grabbing Drake and taking him down with a Reverse Neck-Breaker, Jake hops on top and starts to Choke him. Daye pulls him off of the choking Russell who can only gasp for breath. Shoving the ref out of the way, he delivers a
Dropkick to the sitting Drake. Drake falls back clutching his nose from the high blow to the face.
Without hesitation, Morgan grabs Drake by the hair and lifts him to his feet. Jake hooks Drake up for a Suplex but has it reversed and Jake lands hard. Up and bouncing off the ropes, Russell drops a Fist on Jake's head. Dazed from the attack, Jake can only lay there as Drake lays the boots on.
Forcing his opponent from the ring, Drake steps through the ropes and waits until Jake stands up before delivering a Clothesline off the apron. Drake then picks Jake up and rams his head into the ring post.
On the outside of the ring, Russell grabbed up a chair and smashed it over
Morgan’s head. Bouncing twice more off his cranium with a dull thud, Russell busted Morgan open. Dazed and walking away from the attack, Morgan was like a zombie as he tried to get away, tripping over the ring steps as he fled.
Daye and Russell were arguing about taking the fight back into the ring when he saw Morgan fall flat on his face. Shoving Daye out of the way, Russell took off into a sprint as he followed, springing off the steps and landing an Elbow
Drop.
Holding his arm, Russell pulled a table out from unnder the ring and set it up leaning back against the ring barrier. Pulling Jake to his feet, Russell swung him around and Whipped him into the table. As Morgan bounced back off of it,
Russell grabbed him up and Suplexed him onto the hard floor.
Pulling Morgan back to his feet, Russell took a Forearm to the head before being led around the ring and smashed into anything and everything ... the ringpost, ring steps and barrier. Punching Russell in the head from a Side
Headlock, Morgan led him away from the ring and towards the studio set-up.
Pushing Russell down onto the announcers’ table as Flint and Sakai abandoned their post, Morgan opened up with Right Hands then jabbed a mic in Russell’s eyes, grinding it in until Peter Daye pulled him back and warned him. Pushing the ref out of the way, Morgan wrapped a cord around Russell’s throat and started to strangle him with it as he dragged him across the floor.
Pulling back on the cord, Russell stopped being dragged and returned to his feet. As Morgan moved in closer, Russell hit him with a Clothesline. With
Morgan down, Russell retrieved a full garbage can and brought it down across
Morgan’s head, emptying it’s contents.
Pulling Morgan to his feet, Russell rammed his head into the wall, leaving a bloody spot that had the fans howling. Setting up Morgan for a Suplex, Russell abandoned the move when he saw the Trashman dashing out of the back with his
Singapore Cane poised to strike. Stepping out of the way at the last second as the Cane swooshed through the air, Russell watched as it came down across
Morgan’s head, dropping him to the floor.
Stopping as he took his partner out with the blow meant for Russell, Trash took a Front Kick then was lifted into the air and brought down with a Double
Underhook Backbreaker. Moving the fight back into the ring, Drake tosses Jake back in then climbs the ropes and jumps off with a Senton Bomb. With Jake rolling out of the way, he capitalizes on the stunned Russell and goes for the pin, getting only a 2 count.
Jake grabs Drake and Whips him across the ring. As Drake comes off, Jake goes for the boot to the face, but Drake ducks under and continues on to bounce off the opposite ropes. On the return, Jake catches him up and brings him down with a big Power Slam. An exhausted Jake falls across Drake. The ref drops and slaps out a quick 3 count.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Drake gets up quickly and argues with the Ref about a fast count, saying he got a shoulder up but the ref tells him his decision is final.
With an ensuing argument, Jake steps up behind Drake and grabs him by the back of the neck and tosses him out of the ring. Without hesitation, Jake launches himself over the ropes and hits Drake. Taking Morgan’s full weight,
Drake falls back into the ringside barrier, driving Jake's head into the barrier into the barrier as well. With both men stunned, the camera moves in on the unmoving Morgan as blood trickles from his head before the camera leaves the ringside area, focussing in on Mean Darlene walking with a man holding a ring of keys as they approach her dressing room.)
Mean Darlene: This one right here ... (The man nods his head as he tries several keys until he finds the right one. Stepping aside, he opens the door for Darlene.) Thank you ... (With a nod of his head, the man walks off. Darlene’s smile melts away as she turns to pick up her bags that are no longer where she dropped them.)
My bags are gone ... (Leaving her, the camera finds the outrageously dressed Paco in his tight white
Heartbreakers jumpsuit making his way down the hall with a bit of bounce in his step, almost dancing his way through the corridor. Swiveling his hips as he goes, he abruptly stops in his tracks and his face lights up as he sees something off-camera.)
Paco: Just the man I was looking for ... (Swinging around, the camera zooms in on object of Paco’s eye ... Chip ... wearing a big white diaper. Getting accustomed to the sight, it’s not a diaper but rather what a sumo wrestler might wear. Standing there in the hallway is the obese untanned Chip wearing very little. His dark hair pulled back into a topknot and his eyes pulled back and held in a somewhat almond shape by clear tape, giving his face an even stranger appearance.)
CHIP! Sumo Chip: Hai Paco: Hi to you, too ... say, I was wondering ... you would make a perfect addition to the Heartbreakers ... you’re lovable and cuddly ... like a big teddybear ...
(Cocking his head, Chip strokes his chin as he thinks it over.) Sumo Chip: Hmmmmmmmmm ... (Leaving them, the camera finds a skulking Emilio Ramirez hiding in the shadows deep within the bowels of the studio. Watching intently, he spies a whistling green haired man carrying some baggage.)
Emilio Ramirez: Pssssssttttttt ... (Startled, the green haired man drops the bags and jumps back several feet. Looking into the shadows, he sees Ramirez and shakes his head.) Green haired man: You almost gave me a heartattack! Emilio Ramirez: Shhhhhh ... they’ll hear you. Green haired man: What? Who’s going to hear me? Emilio Ramirez: Those guys from the INS ... Green haired man: Oh ... those guys ... (The green haired man smiles as he looks down at his bags.) I think I can help you get past them ... (The camera fades on the two men ducking into a nearby room when it moves to capture the PAW Television champion Hiroshima Nagasaki sitting on a bench in the PAW lockerroom. Just exiting the room, wearing the rising sun leather jacket, is Pep Preston. Nagasaki shakes his head and laughs at the departing wrestler. Nagasaki, all smiles turns to thecamera and speaks.)
Hiroshima Nagasaki: Another great week for PAW as the H-Bomb and Nakamura-san continue the much needed makeover. I think it is safe to predict that soon PAW will become a magnificent spectacle of Japanese wrestling right here in the
United States. PAW and Denton, Texas should feel lucky that Nakamura-san and I have chosen this as our new home. Together we will transform a mediocre organization into a mecca of exciting japanese wrestling.
(Nagasaki's smile is quickly replaced with the familiar scowl.) Unfortunately several people have decided to stand in the way of progress. One by one each of you will be made to pay for your words and actions. Our current television announcer has taken it upon himself to make sure I single him out for a beating. The day is almost upon us when Flint must lay down the microphone and try to back up your words in the ring. Hiroshima Nagasaki runs from no man, so step between the ring ropes if ... you ... dare!
While I am on the subject of Flint, we should determine who is really the
"Bitch." Is it the H-Bomb just because Flint said I was a bitch? I think not.
Saying someone is a bitch and making someone your bitch are two totally separate things, you imbecile.
On Sunday, you were brave enough to call me a bitch, but I was good enough to make you my bitch in front of all your fans. Listening to your arrogant, rambling attempts to turn the focus back on yourself is becoming very tiresome has-been. You are so pathetic! Any idiot can attack someone from behind, look at Russell but you can not even get that right. Admit the fact you are no match for one as talented as I and perhaps the H-Bomb can find it in his heart to not destroy you. Keep running your mouth though and I will use you as my new heavy bag.
(Leaning close to the camera, Nagasaki begins snickering.) Oh ... and about Preston, Preston will learn an important lesson tonight ... that the H-Bomb does not work with Americans, he destroys them. (Walking toward the lockerroom door, Nagasaki begins to laugh maniacally. Leaving the room, Nagasaki joins Pep Preston and manager Lonny Nakamura as they make their way from the back towards the ring. As they approach the way out from the back, the familiar gonging is heard followed by the Kodo drums ... and is in turn followed by the all too familiar booing from the fans as they emerge from the back.
Panning the announcers, Flint rolls his eyes as Ishimoto Sakai claps for his countryman. Stopping at their table, the two exchange bows.
With the Champion locking his eyes on Flint brings the announcer to his feet. Pep Preston steps between the two which brings a smile to Nagasaki’s face. Words are exchanged which brings the two closer as Flint moves in to grab
Nagasaki but again Preston intervenes to keep them apart. Manager Nakamura steers Nagasaki away from the table and back towards the ring. With some distance between them, Preston releases Flint and joins the pair ringside.
Nakamura slaps Preston in the back of the head and orders him to hold the ropes open for him and the Champ. Entering the ring, Nagasaki hands his belt to
Nakamura who holds it high overhead.
Once they were all inside the ring, Peter Daye tried to check the Champ’s boots but threats kept the ref from doing his job. With Daye and Nakamura having words over the Champion, the boos turned to cheers as Skip Westervelt of the
Lone Wolves stepped out from the back. By himself, he walked around the outside of the ring, slapping fan’s hands as he went until hopping up onto the ring apron then somersaulting himself into the ring.
Moving back and forth like a caged animal, Westervelt’s eyes never left the
Champion ignored everyone around him ... his opponent, the ref, the fans’ booing.
With Westervelt talking to the ref, Nagasaki and Nakamura were deep in conversation, pointing every now and then to the opposite corner. Shaking his head, Nagasaki started to leave the ring when he called for a mike which was brought to him by Ishimoto Sakai. With the mic in hand, the Champ made his way to the floor.)
Hiroshima Nagasaki: It would appear that you have left someone at home ... and being an honorable man, I will not fight you two on one ...
(Westervelt was on the move again, motioning for Nagasaki to come and get it.) Fighting me alone might very likely end your career but taking on both myself and this gaijin dog is suicide ...
(Nagasaki motioned back towards Preston which brought on more booing.) ... but today is your lucky day. Since you and your partner, who is most assuredly hiding somewhere in the back too scared to face someone of my caliber, are so entertaining as funny men ... I am going to give you a break.
Instead of facing me, you’ll have to only face Pep Preston ... (Nagasaki dropped the mic as he started to walk away from the ring. Chants of
“Coward!” started up from the fans as he walked away. Not wanting to miss an opportunity, Skip came flying out of the ring and laid out Nagasaki with a
Pescado.
Pulling the surprised man up by his hair, Skip fired off Punches like there was no tomorrow. Again and again the Punches fell as he staggered the Champion. An intervening Lonny Nakamura with a Super Kick caught Skip in the chin and knocked him away from the Champ.
With some harsh words from his new boss, Pep Preston pulled Westervelt into the ring as Peter Daye called for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Preston worked from an early advantage on the stunned Westervelt. A Sidewalk
Slam off an Irish Whip and a Knee Drop to the face looked like it was going to be a very short match but Skip was able to get a shoulder up just before Daye’s hand slapped the mat a third time.
Nakamura was livid as he shouted orders at Preston, who seemed just a little hesitant. Backing him into a corner, Pep started in with Knife Hand Chops until
Skip slid down into a seated position and found a boot in his throat. Using the ropes for added leverage, Preston cut off Westervelt’s air. He backed away as
Daye started a count for the choke in the ropes.
Up on the ring apron, Nakamura yelled at Preston to finish him off while Nagasaki was busy threatening fans in the front rows. Security made it’s way from the bacl and took up several positions around the ring just in case.
Whipping Westervelt into the far ropes, Preston wasn’t ready for the return as his opponent jumped into the air and caught Pep around the neck with his legs.
Throwing his body back towards the mat, Skip threw Pep over with a Head Scissors Takedown. Rolling back to his feet, an angered Skip was ready for more. Lonny Nakamura jumped up onto the ring apron and was ready to enter the ring when he was caught by the ref. A stern warning sent him back to the apron and out of the ring.
Seeing Nakamura on the ring apron, Skip promptly knocked him off with a Drop
Kick that sent the manager sprawling to the floor. Pep was still on the mat as
Skip kipped to his feet ready for more action. With a running start, Skip nailed Pep with a Baseball Slide and sent him out of the ring.
With a staggered Pep on the outside, Skip darted across the ring and used the ropes to throw his body over them to the outside, nailing Preston with a
Somersault Splash. The fans were howling their approval with both men down on the floor. Nagasaki was in with a quick Stomp but was backed off by a warning referee. Nakamura got involved and moved the Champ back as the fans pelted him with cups and other objects. Forgetting about the other wrestlers, Nagasaki pushed back a fan who had gotten a little too close.
After slamming Preston into the ring barrier, Westervelt hit Nagasaki with a
Forearm to the temple, taking him to the floor. Nakamura ran in and distracted
Westervelt from any further attacks on his Champion. Before things could get worse, Peter Daye was in to break things up.
Backing away, Westervelt returned to the attack on Preston. After pointing at
Nagasaki, he rammed Preston into the ringpost then caught him and lifted him itn othe air before falling back with a Suplex. From the ring steps, he dropped to the floor with an Elbow Drop and made a cover when Nagasaki pushed forward.
Seeing Nagasaki stepping closer, Skip forgot about the pin attempt and returned to his feet. The two were talking to each other and pointing fingers until
Nakamura pulled him away.
Rolling Preston back into the ring, Westervelt went back on the attack with a
Back Elbow that landed solidly. Taking his time as he circled the ring,
Westervelt was watching the smiling Nagasaki on the outside. Pulling Preston to his feet, Skip hoisted him into the air and held him there before dropping to the mat with a “SkippiBuster”. Dropped on his head, Pep
Preston was going nowhere.
Climbing the ropes, Westervelt pointed down at Nagasaki before leaping from his perch and landing a Senton. Leaning back, Westervelt waited while Daye slapped out a three count.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (The fans were cheering for the win when Nagasaki slid into the ring only to level Westervelt with a Spinning Back Kick from behind. Skip hit the mat while
Peter Daye rushed in to admonish the TV Champ. Spun around and grabbed by the seat of his pants, Daye was hurled over the top rope and sent crashing to the floor.
The fans were booing loudly as Nagasaki and Nakamura turned their attention to the downed Westervelt and worked him over as they Stomped him repeatedly.
Emerging from the back and hitting the ring to make the rescue was the oddly dressed “Sumo” Chip. A Double Axe Handle caught Nakamura but Chip stopped in his tracks when he caught a Double Thrust to the throat from the Champ.
Whipped into the ropes, the obese man returned to the waiting arms of Nagasaki. Showing immense power, Nagasaki lifted Chip off his feet and planted him into the canvas with a Belly-to-Belly Suplex that shook the ring.
The sequined Paco hit the ring next and was brought in the hard way as Nagasaki pulled him in over the ropes. With a Body Slam, Paco was put down and a Leg
Drop from Nakamura made sure he wasn’t moving. Climbing the ropes, Nagasaki came flying off the top and landed "The H-Bomb". Paco was out.
The fans were littering the ring with objects as Nagasaki pulled Pep Preston to his feet. While being held, Preston was slapped repeatedly in the face by manager Nakamura. The boos grew louder as the struggling Preston was held in place as the slapping continued. Spinning him around, Nagasaki pushed him into the corner where he opened up with Knees to the head and chest, pulling Pep’s head down into each blow.
Finished, he pulled Preston to the center of the ring where he lifted him into the air before dropping down into a "Nagasaki Driver". Preston twitched several times as Nagasaki returned to his feet with a smile on his face.
That was enough. A roar came out of the crowd as Jason Flint stood back as long as he could before rushing the ring. After hammering both Nagasaki and Nakamura to the canvas, the announcer pulled off his blazer before returning to the attack. Putting the boots to both, Flint drove them from the ring.
The fans were chanting his name as Nakamura and Nagasaki regrouped on the outside of the ring. Seeing his opening, Flint dashed across the ring and jumped up onto the top rope. Spinning his body around in one fluid motion,
Flint threw his body backwards into an Asai Moonsault, taking the other two down with him.
Having his fall broken, Flint was quick to jump back up and celebrated with the fans, slapping a few hands as the volume continued to rise from the small crowd.
Rolling Nagasaki back into the ring, Flint continued with the punishment as he sent the Champ across the ring. On the return, Flint took him off his feet with a Spinning Heel Kick. Jumping to his feet, Flint ran across the ring and hit the middle rope. Jumping off it, he swung his body and landed a Corkscrew Leg
Drop. Sitting on top of Nagasaki, Flint held his arms out as the fans cheered loudly.
Lonny Nakamura, with a shoe to Flint’s head ended the celebration. The Champion was angry as he pulled Flint to his feet. Making a gesture with his thumb across his throat, Nagasaki picked Flint up onto his shoulders. Adjusting the weight, he climbed the corner ropes and from the top jumped off. Driving Flint into the canvas with a "Nagasaki Driver" brought maniacal laughter from the
Champ.
After spitting upon the downed announcer, Nagasaki was led from the ring by his manager followed by the booing of the crowd. With them disappearing into the back, the camera returned to the ring and the carnage before settling in on an unmoving Jason Flint.
Leaving the ringside area, the camera found it’s way into the back where a slumping Jake Morgan, his head bandaged from his earlier fight, leaned heavily against the wall. Using it to try to pull himself along, Morgan fell back.
Rushing in to help him was Lisa Doyle.) Lisa Doyle: Here, let me help you ... (Morgan looked at her with half closed eyes.) Jake Morgan: Darlene? Lisa Doyle: No, Jake ... (Pushing her away from him, Jake pushed off from the wall and staggered away. Making his way down the hall, Morgan spied who he was looking for ... Darlene walking with the annoying green haired production assistant. Moving as quickly as possible, Jake lumbered forward, grabbing Darlene by the shoulder.)
Jake Morgan: Darlene? (Spinning her around, he was just as surprised as Emilio Ramirez hiding under a dark wig and wearing Darlene’s clothes.)
Emilio Ramirez: Uhmmmmmm ... no? (Morgan leveled Ramirez with a Clothesline which nearly knocked him over before eyeing the laughing green haired man. Seeing that he was being looked at, the green haired man ran off as the credits began to roll.
