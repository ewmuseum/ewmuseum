12.17 Card

17 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

(The small studio audience cheers as Lisa Doyle and Aaron Starr walk out from the back and make their way around the ring, slapping out high-fives with the fans before climbing between the ropes. Dressed in similar blazers bearing the
PAW logo, both wait with mics in hand for the cheering to subside.) Aaron Starr: Welcome once again to Public Access Wrestling! I’m Aaron Starr and this beautiful young woman is Lisa Doyle!
(Whistles and catcalls follow a pirouette by Doyle.) We’re glad you could all make it to our weekly television taping following this cold snap ...
Lisa Doyle: Aaron ... it might have been a frozen winter wonderland during the week but last weekend’s Winter’s Nightmare was anything but that ...
(Starr shakes his head as he puts it in his hand before looking up as the fans boo.) Aaron Starr: Lisa, I can’t speak for any of these people sitting here today but I for one ... DO NOT want to hear about last week’s Supercard. All I have to say is this war is just getting started ...
(A cheer rises from the fans causing Starr and Doyle to turn as Jason Flint marches out from the back. Making his way around the ring, Flint celebrates with the audience before jumping up onto the ring apron and somersaulting into the ring. With a smile on his face, Flint shakes hands with both cohosts before waving to the fans.)
And ... this is one man who is going to be leading the charge! Jason, as always, it’s a pleasure ...
(Flint smiles as the crowd grows louder, clapping and cheering.) Jason Flint: The pleasure is all mine. Aaron Starr: Jason ... I think you were just as surprised as the rest of us last week by Jake Morgan’s siding with Hiroshima Nagasaki and the rest of
Nakamura’s Team Japan. What I want to know ... what we all want to know is what are you going to do about it? Jason Flint: Do? I’m not going to do anything, Aaron ... Aaron Starr: WHAT!?!?!?! Surely, you’re not going to tell me that after what transpired last week, you’re not going to do anything about it?
Jason Flint: Aaron, it’s not up to me to do anything about what happened. What I will do however is hit them where it counts ... and that’s right here inside this ring with every victory over Nagasaki ... over Morgan ... over any of
Lonny Nakamura’s other stooges hitting them where it counts until I’ve finally pried the Television Belt from Hiro’s clutching fingers ...
That’s what I’m going to do about it ... (Slapping Starr on the shoulder, Flint waves before walking off to the back. With Flint leaving, the fans clap and cheer his exit as Starr turns back to Doyle.) Aaron Starr: Lisa, I don’t know if I buy any of what he just said but I know one thing’s for sure, I wouldn’t want to be Lonny Nakamura or any of his Team
Japan ... (Both Starr and Doyle look around as the fading cheers erupt in a heavy dose of booing. Turning around they see broadcasting cohort, Ishoimoto Sakai being dragged out from the back by Justin Hart ... an angered Justin Hart.)
Seems that we’re being joined early by our broadcast partner Ish ... very much against his will ...
(Stepping back, they make plenty of room for Hart to throw Sakai into the ring and join him.)
Ish ... Justin ... (Sakai’s on his knees pleading with Hart to let him go but gets only a big smile and a raised fist for his begging. The boos grow louder causing everyone inside the ring to turn back to see the cause ... the masked President leading the Head of Security, Shane and Kimberly Davis, followed by Lonny Nakamura, the
Black Ravens and referee Peter Daye. Nearing the ring, the President and her entourage stop outside.) Masked Woman: Mr. Hart ... there is something to be said for the man who learns from his mistakes ...
(The booing stops as the President continues.) ... you are not that man. I can see from your actions that you haven’t learned one little thing from your time behind bars because no matter what I’ve said to you ... here you are again doing the very thing that landed you in trouble in the first place ...
Now ... I can understand your anger. Under my predecessor, you feel you were treated unfairly but as I’ve said before, I am not B.J. Perris ...
(Taking a walk on the outside of the ring, she continues.) And I know what you’re thinking ... I treated you unfairly. You’re angry because I not only got rid of the Tag Team Titles but I also threw out the
Texas Heavyweight Title ... a title that you held at the time ... Oh sure ... you’re angry, aren’t you? You’re mad as Hell at me and this is all because of that ...
Isn’t it? Well, Mr. Hart ... what I did, I did for the good of the promotion. I didn’t do it for you ... I didn’t do it for me. No, I did it for the good of every person that walks through those doors in the back every week to put on this show ...
from the lowly janitor to the highest paid employee. I did it for all of us ... and I’d do it again if given the chance. You see, Mr. Hart ... I didn’t do it to you. I did it for all of us. Now, I’ll admit ... I do feel responsible for some of this anger you harbor and because of that, I have been treating you with kid gloves. Where another employee might have been fired for the same actions you took, I had you arrested to go off and cool your heels ...
When you needed a direction to focus that anger, I’ve been the one to put you in matches to allow you to do just that and as I can see, last week’s hardcore match against XYZ wasn’t enough for you ...
Alright, Mr. Hart ... we’re going to play it your way ... You’re going to se that I can play hardball, too. You want to push me, I push back. I’m going to give you a match ... and Mr. Hart you’re going to love this
... Since you seem to like to bully people around, I’m going to switch the tables. Right here and now, you are going to take on the Black Ravens in a Handicap Match ... (The fans cheer as a big grin comes over Hart’s face as Lonny Nakamura starts arguing with the masked woman.)
What are you waiting for? RIng that damn bell! (Turning, the woman walks off followed by Shane and Kim Davis. Peter Daye slides into the ring and calls for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Nakamura was pulling the Ravens to the back when Hart led the fans in a cheer, calling them “Pussies!”. Inside the ring, Peter Daye starts a count on the
Black Ravens. Hitting seven, both men hurl themselves towards the ring. WIth both men on the apron, Hart shoots forward with a Punch knocking the first one to the floor. With a handful of hair, Hart pulls the second into the ring, dumping him on the canvas.
Pulling Raven #2 to his feet, Hart Presses him over his head before Slamming him to the mat. With a cheer, Hart drops down with a sudden Leg Drop that traps the smaller wrestler under his leg. From his position, Hart gets a 1 count from the ref before letting up.
After a Release German Suplex, Hart gets down in a Three Point Stance across the ring from the wobbling wrestler. Charging across the ring, Hart launches himself through the air, hitting the Black Raven with a Flying Shoulder, driving him from the ring.
Black Raven #1 rushes into the ring as his partner hits the floor. Attacking the downed Hart with a Boot Stomping, Black Raven #1 pulls his opponent to his feet, lashing out with a Chop to the chest.
Spinning around to send Hart across the ring, Raven #1 is countered and pulled back into a Knee to the midsection. Lifted into the air, Raven #1 is sent hopping across the ring following Hart’s Atomic Drop. Charging forward, Hart spins Raven #1 with a stiff Clothesline and a follow-up Elbow Drop.
A Kick to the back momentarily stops Hart in his tracks as #1’s partner rejoins the “party”. Blocking a Chop, Hart fires back with several Punches, backing #2 across the ring. Swinging #2 around, Hart fires him off across the ring and into the opposite corner. Hitting the corner backfirst, #2 slumps down as Hart races across the ring, jumping into the air and slamming into his foe with the
Hang Time Corner Press. Stepping back, Hart grins as #2 falls flat on his face. Pulling #1 back to his feet, Hart wraps him up for a Side Russian Leg Sweep before tossing him from the ring and following to the outside.
After hammering in Fists to the head, Hart pulls a table out from underneath the ring. Setting it up despite warnings from the ref, Hart returns to the ring with Black Raven #1 flat on the table.
Listening to the cheering while Lonny Nakamura argues with the ref, Hart dashes off across the ring. Diving over the top rope, Hart becomes a two hundred and fifty-four pound projectile, crashing with #1 through the table.
Getting back to his feet, Hart pumps his fists into the air as the fans cheer loudly until manager Lonny Nakamura lands a Side Kick to Hart’s ribs. Turning around slowly, Hart’s eyes narrow as he slowly steps forward towards the backpeddling manager. Grabbing a handful of hair, Hart drags Nakamura into the ring. A chant of “Kick his ass!” erupts from the crowd as a wide-eyed Nakamura pleads for his life.
Letting go of Nakamura, Hart smiles before driving a boot into his midsection, grabbing hair and slamming Nakamura’s face into the canvas with the 1-8-7.
Rolling Nakamura to his back, Hart presses both hands onto Nakamura’s chest for a 3 count from the shrugging referee.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Rolling from the ring, Hart slaps a few hands before heading into the back to a chorus of cheers. With the battered Black Ravens helping Lonny Nakamura to the back, Aaron Starr and Lisa Doyle approach the ring once again.)
Aaron Starr: Do not go away ... we’re just getting started!

Jason Flint: Forget him, Darlene, he’s lost on his little fantasyland ride, never to return. What’s important for me today is to find his ass and remind him of whom he just fucked with.

Man: You’re a little pecker, aren’t you? (Flint looked from the man back to Darlene, whose eyes flashed no recognition. Shaking his head, Flint walked off after his partner with the man’s laughter echoing in the background as large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession...)

Am I the only one who finds this shirt appropriate for Flint? Because if not, I would like to welcome Flint to his own personal Hell. At this very moment,

A Hell where Flint must stand in front of a mirror and admit to himself that I am better than he will EVER be...
A Hell where I, Hiroshima Nagasaki, have stripped from him everything he holds so dear ... Give up Flint! Your words no longer hold any meaning. Climbing into the ring, Russell stripped off his long black heavy coat, dropping it and his weapon to the floor below. Following last minute instructions from the ref, the match was started.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Charging out with the bell, Drake Russell locked up with Kidd in the center of the ring but found himself quickly in a Side Headlock as his opponent rolled behind him into a Hammerlock then continued on to the other side. With control of the situation, Kidd shot Drake Russell off to the opposite side of the ring.
On the return, Russell ducked under the Kidd’s Leap Frog and continued on to the other side of the ring. Hitting the ropes once again, Drake flew through the air as Kidd caught him and dumped him with a fluid Hip Toss.
Dropping down, Kidd sat down with a Leg Drop before rolling quickly to his feet and bouncing off the ropes and into a Standing Senton for a 2 count before being pushed off.
With his opponent sitting up, Kidd slammed himself back against the ropes and shot forward into a Drop Kick to the back of Russell’s head. Jumping up, Kidd hit the ropes and threw himself back into a Moonsault from the middle ropes, crashing onto the canvas as the prone Russell rolled out of the way.
With Kidd down, Russell slowly rose to his feet, shaking out the cobwebs. Pulling Kidd to his feet, Russell snapped him over with a pair of Snap Suplexes. A cover netted a 2 count from Daye. Sending Kidd slamming into the far corner off an Irish Whip, Russell charged in, hitting the turnbuckle as Kidd went up and out of the way with a Tip Up Wrapping his legs around Drake’s neck, Kidd spun him to the mat with a Head Scissor Takedown.
Rolling to the outside of the ring, Russell grabbed a few seconds to get his head back into the match. Bouncing off the ropes, Kidd charged across the ring, feigning a Tope, instead twirling his body through the ropes, spinning himself to his feet inside the ring.
Kidd stepped back allowing Russell back into the ring without encouragement from the ref. Circling, the two tapped hands before locking up in the center of the ring.
Spinning behind Kidd, Russell put on a Hammerlock, lifting Kidd off his feet and fell back with a Suplex. Holding on, Kidd was pinned to the mat, kicking out just shy of a 3 count.
Kidd was up complaining about his tights being pulled when he took a Front Kick. Dropping forward, Kidd found himself spinning through the air with Russell’s trademark Double-Underhook Back Breaker. Sitting on the top turnbuckle, Russell jumped off and landed a Fist Drop before making a cover. Daye slapped the mat twice and was about to hit it for a final time Kidd barely kicked out.

Drop to the inner thigh then Handstand Knee Drops. Pulling Kidd to his feet, Russell wrapped him up in a Waistlock, lifting him into the air and dropping him down with a Kneebreaker. Stepping back, he gave
Gannon time to pull himself up using the ropes. After asking the ref for a submission, Russell returned with boots to the leg.

Russell over before using the ropes to launch himself into the ring and pull Drake to the mat with a Sunset Flip. Making the cover, Gannon was thrown off on a 1 count. Limping, Gannon caught Russell with a Front Kick of his own and an Elbow Drop off the middle rope before being taken down with a Knee Clip.
Back on the leg, Russell continued to wrench it with a continous Spinning Toe Hold until forced to break as the begging Kidd grabbed the ropes. Using the ropes, Kidd was pulled to the mat with an Inside Cradle and pinned to the mat for a 2 count.
On his feet, Kidd was again complaining about his tights being held. Waved off, Kidd was grabbed and taken down with a Belly-to-Belly Suplex. Pulled to his feet in an Arm Bar, Kidd flipped backwards, reversing the hold with an Arm Bar of his own.
Countering with a Back Elbow, Russell was back in control with an Arm Bar which was again countered into Gannon’s favor. Leaping into the air, Gannon pushed
Russell forward as he came down with his leg across the held arm, dropping his full weight down and slamming Russell facefirst into the mat. Rolling Russell to his back, Gannon climbed the ropes, coming off with a Splash and making a cover for a near pinfall.
Back up the corner ropes, Kidd had trouble balancing himself before being grabbed and thrown from the top. With Gannon flat on his back, Russell climbed and came off with a Top Rope Leg Drop that shook the ring.
Continuing on the leg, Russell wrenched in a Half Boston Crab, sitting back in the center of the ring. Kidd put his hands over his face to concel the pain from the ref who asked for a submission. Slamming a fist into the mat, Kidd shook his head before trying to crawl towards the ropes. Slowly, he made his way forward as Russell continously asked the ref for a submission.
Finally making it to the ropes, Gannon got a break from the hold but the damage was done. Leaning heavily against the ropes, Kidd was pulled back into the fight by a Dragon Screw Leg Whip. Holding on to the leg, Russell put him right back into another Half Boston Crab. Rolling too far, Russell allowed Kidd to get hold of the ropes and bringing an immediate break.
Shaking his head, Russell was workman-like as he wrapped Kidd’s legs with his own and pulled back on his arms as he sat down, pulling the young wrestler into the air with an Elevated Surfboard. Gannon was in pain as Russell pushed his legs forward into the air, applying more pressure.
Tipping his body, Gannon escaped as he clutched the ropes. Keeping up the pressure and not giving the rookie a breather, Russell pulled Gannon away from the ropes into an Abdominal Stretch. Falling forward, Gannon pulled Russell over as both men hit the mat.
Using the ropes to pull himself to his feet, Gannon could barely stand. Pulled off the ropes, Gannon was half dragged to the center of the ring in a Sleeper.
Gannon’s arms flailed as Russell fell back to the mat and held on. Going out quickly, Gannon weakly tapped out.
