PAW Roleplay Packet #1

4 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
X-X-X-X-X PUBLIC ACCESS WRESTLING Presents: Professional Wrestling The Way It Was Meant To Be! July 4, 2000 X-X-X-X-X Roleplay Packet #1 X-X-X-X-X Justin Hart (The scene opens in a gym. People are sweating away trying to trim the fat, so to speak. A couple are sparring in the ring set in the middle of the room. The two combatants are sizing each other up before the sparring starts. Scene change, a lone man stands punching a heavy duty punching bag. Attacking it relentlessly with one hand, the man hits harder and harder. Upon closer inspection, you recognize the face, Justin Hart. Looking even closer, you see that he's punching with his injured hand.)
Hart: Russel... you got lucky. You talk smack, run your mouth, and when the time comes, you hide behind the guard dogs. That's fine... but remember, you can't hide forever.
(Hart stops and shakes the feeling back into his hand, then resumes the ritual.) Hart: Back to the matter at hand. The 9th of July. We gonna set off fireworks a few days late. Cause I'm in a Terrible Three-way match with two of my worst enemies... Jason Flint...
(Hart bends over, sticking a finger in his mouth to induce vomiting.) Hart: ...and the ever lovin' Jake Morgan, our beloved PAW champion... or something like that. Listen, boys, I'm not gonna debate who's better... I'm not gonna choose sides... hell, I'm not even gonna waste any more time with this promo...
(Hart turns to look the camera down...) Hart: I'm gonna just beat the hell outta both of you. End of story... end of you. (Hart turns back and resumes his "one-armed boxing" as the scene fades to black.) X-X-X-X-X Kimberly Davis (The camera opens inside a locker room in a grainy black and white. Moving along a row of lockers, the camera final settle in on someone sitting on the floor with her back against the lockers.
Dressed in jeans and a t-shirt, the woman with long light colored curly hair sits with her knees pulled up and her head down against them, covering her face.
Sitting up sharply, she slams her head back against the locker directly behind her causing it to rattle from the blow. Looking up at the ceiling, Kimberly
Davis can be now made out.)
Kimberly Davis: What’s it going to take? (Taking a deep breath, she stops for long seconds as she composes herself then continuing.)
How long is this going to go on ... before ... (Sniffling, she stiffles the tears as her voice breaks.) ... before ... it’s too late ... How many times can you put your body on the line? How many times can Khan slam you down before he breaks you?
(Slamming her head backwards, she smashes it back against the locker a second time ... this time much harder than before.)
How many times will continue to defy Perris before you realize that you can’t win ... no matter what you do?
What’s it going to take ... (Throwing her head back, she slams the locker again.) *SLAM* ... before you realize ... *SLAM* ... you’re a ... *SLAM* ... beaten man ... *SLAM* (Closing her eyes, she puts her head down slightly then reaches back and touches the back of her head. Running her fingers through her hair, she pulls it forward, holding it out towards the camera ... her fingers covered in dark fluid.)
Stop it now ... before it stops you ... (Putting her head down into her knees as she pulls then up to her chest, she rocks back and forth as the camera fades to black.)
X-X-X-X-X Sky King It's dusk in the city. The night is drawing in but it's still hot. The kind of hot that stops you sleeping well, tossing and turning all night until the alarm shakes you out of your funk. Hot.
Under a bridge by the old railway, a man shuffles around, trying to make his collection of things tidy. Sure, he's surrounded by filth and degredation, but this is his little corner of the planet, his home. He remembers back to before.
Before he came to be living under the railway tracks in a small town north of
Dallas. He sighs.
He thinks of his wife and child, no doubt trying as hard to get to sleep as everyone else in this godforsaken land. He thinks of them and cries, and wishes he could be back with them....
But there is work to be done. A lot of work. He reaches for the brown envelope left for him by his mysterious benefactor. He pulls out the picture, an 8x10 of a PAW wrestler, and a small piece of paper falls to the ground. On it are directions to the next Public Access show, and some scribbled instructions. And the photo? That would be telling...
X-X-X-X-X Aaron Starr (The camera opens on a circular table with a stack of loose leaf paper sitting at it’s very center. Zooming in and wheeling around to be positioned over the top, the uppermost page shows written in black and colored in red marker, the title of the piece.)
Voice: The culmination of a lifetime of work done in the matter of weeks ... (A hand comes into view and thumbs through the document showing page after page of handwritten blue ink interspersed with several small photos taped in.
FInished, the hand puts the document back down and straightens the pages back up.) Everything you wanted to know about Public Access Wrestling ... (Pulling back, the camera finds the owner of the voice ... sitting at the table, leaning back casually in his chair and wearing an ugly brown blazer is
PAW television host and author/wrestling historian Aaron Starr. With a curt nod of his head, he lifts a pipe to his mouth and clamps down upon it. Looking over the document, he removes the pipe from his mouth as he turns back towards the camera.)
Everything you wanted to know about Public Access Wrestling ... from it’s earliest days as Extreme Wrestling through today ...
Showcasing her Superstars ... past and present ... from Jason Flint to the
Offspring ... and the not so super stars ... Kerry Powell ... Shane Cornell ...
Neowolf ... and finally, Drake Russell ... A look at the rookie talent ... Billy Joe Gannon ... Kidd Gannon ... Christian
Maeda ... the Hardcore Kid, Trashman and the PAW’s latest addition, Seth ...
(Starr’s eyebrow rises as he pats the stack of papers with his hand.) Get it all right here in ... the STARR REPORT! (The camera fades to black with Starr nodding his head up and down.) X-X-X-X-X Christian Maeda (The scene opens somewhere in the PAW backstage area. Christian Maeda, with a small bandage on his forehead, and Black Thorne are walking towards the camera.)
Maeda: Well, I'm coming off my first main event, where I was cheated out of a win, against the great Jake Morgan.
(Maeda sneers.) Maeda: Now, in my second main event, I face the cheater, Drake "The Snake" Russell, for the Television Title. (Maeda cracks a slight smile.) Maeda: That's right Drake, you and "The Heartbreaker" get it on, in the first of what promises to be many encounters! I sure hope that you enjoyed being the
TV champ because, come this Sunday, you will lose the belt to me, the better man!
(Maeda puts his hair in a ponytail.) Maeda: Let's review what we already know, shall we. I'm more talented than you. I'm better looking than you. I'm more charismatic than you. And, Lisa
Doyle is definitely more attracted to me, than you. To prove that very fact,
I'm going to call Lisa, right now. Thorney?
(Black Thorne hands Christian a cell phone. Maeda dials making sure the camera can't see.)
Maeda: Hi, Lisa! It's me, Christian! So, you finally woke up after last night's marathon!
(Lisa Doyle can be seen walking towards Maeda and Thorne.) Maeda: Yeah, I'm talking to that jackass, Russell! Doyle: Excuse me, Christian. (Maeda's eyes bug out of his head.) Maeda: Uh...hi...um...Lisa, I wasn't talking to you on the phone. Doyle: Whatever, leave me alone! (Doyle pushes past Maeda. Maeda puts his hand in front of the camera. Fade to black.) X-X-X-X-X Jake Morgan (The camera opens on Jake Morgan and Mean Darlene. Jake has bandages covering the various parts of his body that were hurt in his match this past Sunday. He is wearing a black PAW T-shirt, khaki cargo shorts, and black and white
Converse hi-tops. His title belt rests around his waist. Darlene puts her hand around his waist, and snuggles up close to him as he begins to speak.)
Jake: Flint, Flint ,Flint. Do you think that I'm going to let your little mind games work against me again? No, I don't think so. BJ and Darlene are right, you've got nothing.That video tape is a sham, just like your title reign. If you did have something, you wouldn't wait this long to reveal your hand. For once your tricks are backfiring on you. I've let you mislead me before, I won't let you do it again. I have no reason to trust you, and even less reason to distrust Darlene. So you can forget it! Your plan didn't work. I'm tired of your games Flint, and I'm tired of you.
(He pauses and moves away from Darlene a little bit.) Sunday you get a shot at me Flint. Well, me and the man who stole your belt,
Justin Hart. Quite frankly, I'd be content just to let the two of you beat the hell out of each other, but what fun would that be. No, I don't like either of you. I think that you were both disgraces to this belt and to PAW as a whole.
For months I chased you Flint, but you always weaseled out of a direct confrontation. I finally had you in a position where you couldn't get away, and then Hart stuck his big nose in our business. He didn't belong, he knew it, but he didn't care. As a result
this belt was disgraced even further. While I won it back, and brought an amount of respectability to the belt, I don't think that Justice was served.
This weekend I get a chance to make two wrongs into one right. Sunday, I put an end to old business, and an end to you two JERKS.
(He grabs Darlene around the waist, and pushes the camera out of his way as they move on past. Fade to black.)
X-X-X-X-X 2K (The camera opens upon the dynamic duo affectionately known these days as 2K
... prior to which they were simply known as Skip Westervelt and Chip. Standing in front of the Public Access red, white and black banner, the pair are dressed alike in their black tights with “2K” all up and down the legs as well as a black vest over their otherwise bare chests.
Standing to the right, Skip smiles into the camera while his compatriot Chip looks down at the large bulbous belly that hangs comically over his tights, sticking out from his vest.)
Skip Westervelt: Sunday ... (Skip slaps his hands loudly together and starts rubbing them together as he hesitates before speaking again.)
This Sunday marks just another lesson in the making ... a lesson that has fallen into the laps of just two of the best tag team wrestlers in this promotion ...
(Nodding his head, Skip slaps Chip on the chest that has the overweight wrestler snapping his head up and looking very surprised.)
Chip: Who? Mean Team 2000 is getting back together? (Skip’s smile melts away into a frown as he slowly turns his head in the direction of his partner. With the look, Chip knows he’s said something wrong looking down like a puppy who’s been yelled at.)
Skip Westervelt: NO! Jason Flint and Johnny Green have not gotten back together
... what I’m talking about is the lesson that has fallen onto our shoulders as two of the best tag team wrestlers in this promotion ... us ... the team known only as 2K ... you and me ... Skip Westervelt and Chip ... have been asked to teach a lesson ...
(Looking down at his stomach, Chip’s hand snakes it’s way down as he sticks a finger into his belly button and starts moving it around, digging for something.)
We’ve been asked to teach a lesson to two men who have come to Public Access
Wrestling in hopes of getting their careers off the ground ... two men who have been thrown together into a match as partners in order to face us ... a well oiled wrestling machine that is undoubtedly at the top of the promotion’s “food chain” ...
(With a look of concentration, Chip’s “digging” has gotten more pronounced until he pulls his finger free. Looking down at his “prize”, a piece of blue fuzz, he tries to shake it from his finger with no luck. Flicking it vigorously, it dislodges itself and hits Skip on the arm.
Turning his head slowly, Skip looks down at the object on his arm then to his partner who can only smile as he reaches out and plucks it away from
Westervelt’s arm. Rolling his eyes and shaking his head in disgust, Westervelt turns back towards the camera to continue.)
Prince Prodigy ... All American Steve Stone ... this is no longer 1999 and you can sit in the back and talk about the Cubbies all you want while listening to
Purple Reign but come this Sunday ... these two Lone Wolves are going to give you a welcome to Texas and Public Access Wrestling that neither you nor anyone else will soon forget!
(Spinning around, Ship puts his back to the camera showing the embroidered lettering upon the back of his black vest ... in big letters, the words “ONE
WOLF” are prominantly displayed while in front of “ONE” the outline of the letter L can be seen by the stitching that once held it in place as it now dangles down held only by a single thread.
Nodding his head, Skip points directly into the camera.) This Sunday ... the Welcome Wagon comes to town! (Chip smiles as he waves before the camera fades to black.) X-X-X-X-X Drake Russell ( The scene opens with a shot of Drake Russell and Lisa Doyle sitting on the infamous leather couch in Drake's very own personal dressing room. They are sitting directly next to eachother and appear to be locked in conversation.
Drake has his Television title around his waist and Lisa has a microphone in her hand. We listen in their conversation.)
Lisa Doyle: He thinks that I like him. It is almost sad how deluded and confused he is. He says hi and I say hi back...nothing more. I would rather talk to the inanimate Khan before even engaging in a conversation with him. I swear..
( Drake jumps in and pats her on the leg gently. He attempts to console her.) Drake Russell: It is all going to be okay. It is not your fault that you are beautiful and wanted my every man that sees you. Look at it this way...he already has Black Thorne, he does not need another girlfriend.
( Lisa laughs and smiles at Drake and she begins to speak again.) Lisa Doyle: Well, Drake...you said that you needed an interview and here I am. I enjoy being your own personal interviewer. We make a great team. ( Drake stands up and adjusts his Television title. He extends his hand to her and she reaches out and accepts. He pulls her to her feet and he speaks.)
Drake Russell: Let's go to the interview back-drop. It would look better coming from there.
( She nods and they exit Drake's room. They walk by everyone that is in the back working. As they are walking they see Aaron Starr looking in a mirror picking his nose. They both shake their heads and continue walking until they come upon the backdrop. Doyle makes sure that everything is good and that she has questions. She begins speaking.)
Lisa Doyle: Ladies and gentlemen I am joined now by the Television title holder, Drake Russell. Drake, you interfered in a match involving Jake Morgan and Christian Maeda, causing Maeda to lose. Why did you do this?
( Drake nods and listens to what she has to say. He smiles and inhales before speaking.) Drake Russell: Well, to be honest with you Lisa, it was not because Jake
Morgan could not defeat that panty-waste by himself. You need to realize,
Maeda, that Morgan is the Texas Heavyweight Champion, he could have and would have defeated you even without my interference. In fact, he can defeat anyone at any time which is why he is the champ...well, except for maybe the man of destiny.
( Lisa shakes her head and shoots her next query.) Lisa Doyle: Maeda has even gone as far to say that you are jealous of him and his "Heartbreaker" ways. What do you have to say to that?
(Drake adjust his dark blue sunglasses on his face and sets his Television title on his shoulder. He answers Lisa.)
Drake Russell: Jealous of him? No. What is it that I could possibly be jealous of him about, Lisa? It it because he has the Television title? Is it because of all of the women that he has wracked up since being in the PAW? No,
Lisa. It is none of the above. He has not done or had either of those things.
Yet, still, he expects me to be jealous of him? To me, it comes off pretty simple. It is nothing but the opposite of what he thinks. He wants to be me and he wants what is mine. The simple part of it is that...he can never have it.
( Lisa takes in what Drake has just said. She continues he questioning.) Lisa Doyle: And what of his talk about me? He says that I am attracted to him. You Drake, more than anyone, knows.
( Drake smiles and nods his head in agreement. He scratches his head and answers the question with a smile on his face, as he plays along.)
Drake Russell: Well Ms. Doyle, I have spoken with Lisa quite a bit and she seems pretty upset about the whole thing. Not only does she seem rather disgusted by the entire show, but she also finds it laughable. She has informed me many times that not only would she not enjoy engaging in conversation with him, but she would not touch him with with a ten foot...a one-hundred foot pole. I believe that she would like to leave all of the touching of the "Heartbreaker" up to Black Thorne.
( Lisa and Drake smile and she asks another question.) Lisa Doyle: And Drake, what of this Heartbreak Hotel he makes mention to? Do you plan on making a reservation?
( Drake shakes his head no and speaks.) Drake Russell: Actually, I think that I will be making a reservation for two. These two people seem quite close lately and I believe that they need some time alone, if you know what I mean, Lisa. I will make a reservation for Christian
Maeda and Black Thorne. After Sunday, Christian will need some consoling by
Thorney as you like to call him. And, judging by the looks of things, Thorney will be up for the task.
( Drake pauses for a moment and then begins again.) Drake Russell: Speaking of reservations...Maeda, you should have has some serious reservations before involving yourself in anything that I do. Make sure that the next time that you challenge someone, you do your homework on that person. You do not know what you are going up against Sunday. Just to let you in on things, it is called destiny, my friend. Your mouth has sealed your fate and decided your destiny for you...this Sunday come prepared to lose.
This belt if far too precious a prize to be held by someone as undeserving as you... ( Drake looks away from the camera and looks to Lisa. He speaks.) Drake Russell: Would you like to do this with me? ( Doyle smiles and shakes her head in agreement.) Lisa Doyle and Drake Russell(in unison): Let that be a lesson to you. ( The two smile and walk away from the backdrop together as the scene fades to black.) X-X-X-X-X Seth (Fans are packed in the PAW arena to watch some good old-fashioned wrestling. To start off, some unfamiliar music plays over the speakers, which a few members of the crowd recognize as "Surf Song" by Fenix TX.
When Seth comes out, there is a mixed reaction. The fans don't quite know whether they should cheer or boo the Californian. The camera focuses on several signs in the audience: "Seth, go back to California!", "Marry me,
Seth!", and "R.I.P. J, 1978-2000." Seth finally steps into the ring, microphone in hand, and begin to address the fans.)
Seth: You know, out of all the places that I've ever been, California may be the best, but Texas is a close second.
(The crowd boos at this.) Seth: What? What's wrong? That's a compliment! I mean, all of you are from
Texas, so you think that Texas is the best state, right?
(The audience pops loudly answering Seth's question.) Seth: Well, I come from California, so it's only natural that I think California is the best. Right? Ah, never mind. Let's just get down to business. This Sunday, Seth officially has his debut match, against someone known as Pep Preston. It promises to be an awesome display of technical wrestling and athleticism...ha, ha, ha, ha! Oh, I crack my self up. They're won't be time for any technical wrestling, because as soon as that opening bell rings, I'll already have had Pep staring at the lights, while the referee counts 1...2...3. You see, I don't have anything against Peppy, but I've got to prove myself to the world, the management, the other wrestlers, and, especially, the fans. Come this Sunday night, each and every one of you will see that Seth isn't just another pretty face.
Seth: Now, it's time for my favorite part of the show, where some of the lucky fans in the front row get to ask me questions. Let's see...
(Seth climbs out of the ring and starts looking for a lucky audience member. He finds a teenage boy and goes over to him.) Seth: You! Yeah, you! You have a question to ask me? Teenage Boy: Um..yeah, I was, uh, like, wondering if you've ever, you know, like, met Johnny Libido, like, you know?
Seth: ... ... Why does everyone ask me that? No I've never met him, and no, I will not say his name, because I don't feel like getting sued. Next question.
(Seth starts walking around, and he finds a young black man, about in his 30s, wearing a Dallas Cowboys jersey. Seth walks over to him.)
Seth: And you, sir, do you have any questions to ask me? Man: Yeah. Who do you think is gonna win the Super Bowl? Seth: ... ... What does that have to do with anything? I don't know, do I look like a linebacker or something? Well...if I had to guess, I'd say the New
Orleans Saints.
Man: The Saints? Are you crazy or somethin'? I think the Saints ain't won a game since 1976!
Seth: OK, anyway, I think we have time for one more question. (Seth walks over to a beautiful young lady.) Seth: I was going to ask you if you had a question for me, but after looking at you, I think that I have a question for you...will you go out with me?
(The woman, shocked, slaps Seth in the face, and storms away. Seth just stands there, dumb-founded.)
Seth: I..{clears throat}..This interview is over. (He quickly storms backstage. The audience, somewhat amused by his antics, begin to cheer a little bit, before settling back in their seats, and waiting for the action to start.)
X-X-X-X-X Jason Flint (“Southtown” by POD is playing as the cameras open on UNT’s Fouts Field, as
Jason Flint is standing on a set of bleachers, his arms outstretched as the breeze blows through his waist-tied shirt, waving it like a flag.
Outside the stadium, the Fourth of July celebration festivities are beginning. The local radio station, The Edge, is here, hence the music, and people are setting up tents and other booths.)
<Jason Flint> As much as I would like to stay here and watch the fireworks show hosted by the Kwanais Club, but duty calls.
(Flint hops down and begins to walk down the steps.) Why is it that I’m sensing that this three-way dance will turn into a four-way party, where I’m the getting screwed….again.
(Hopping over the short wall onto the astroturf, Flint slumps down that wall and sits.) No doubt, Mr. Khan will be Lurching around, with the nagging urge to chokeslam me through something. We’ve got busted Justin Hart, with the bad wing, courtesy of yours truly. And then, the PAW Champion, Jake Morgan, a man that is clueless at how close the Sword of Damocles is hanging over him.
(Flint begins to bite his upper lip, pondering his next words.) How much can I say about these two people. Not much really. Justin Hart is an ass, while Morgan I feel more pity that you have “other” things circulating around, to face me this weekend and are just too dumb to see it.
(Just sitting there, Flint gazes over the bright green astroturf, shielding his eye at the brightness of the day.)
Justin Hart got a taste of what I’m all about nowadays. Morgan, the worm as turned, I can give as good as I take now.
No Boyz… No Green…. Not even a Dan Glass in sight. (Flint looks straight into the camera.) What I’ve learned in these past weeks, all the blood that was spilled, all the bones that were broken, all the mayhem that spilled from the ring…
…is that I liked it. I enjoyed it. Every…single….minute of it. What you two better do is kill me, because if not,
I just….may have too much fun….
I’m far more dangerous now, than I have ever been before, and you two get front seat tickets as I take care of a little business.
FADE X-X-X-X-X SUMMER BURNOUT AD #1 (The camera opens in a darkened room upon an old 8 mm movie projector where an older man is loading up a reel of film. Threading the film, he stops to look at the pair of tossle haired kids before going back to his project ... his old hands threading the machine like a master who has done this for a lifetime as his “admirers” watch with interest.
With the film rolled onto the second reel, the man hits the switch and the projector comes to life as the reels begin to spin. Sitting down, the old man points towards the screen where black and white images of men flying through the air inside a wrestling ring can be seen ...
Recognizable men ... Public Access Wrestling’s Jason Flint ... Television
Champion Drake Russell ... Skip Westervelt and Chip ... Christian Maeda ...
Johnny Green ... Desperado ... Justin Hart ... Moving back to the old man watching, he smiles as the scenes play and the children look surprised as one of the wrestlers jumps from the top turnbuckle to the outside of the ring where he drives an opponent through a table to the floor.
Back on the screen, the wrestling action is replaced by a single man standing in the center of the ring with his hands spread out from his body as a lovely woman with dark hair wearing black leather puts the PAW Texas Heavyweight Title belt around his waist ... Champion Jake Morgan and manager Mean Darlene ...
The reel suddenly finishes and the screen goes white as the sounds of the film slapping against the projector can be heard in the background when a booming voice comes on.)
Voice: August 20th ... the University of North Texas and NTTV are proud to present Public Access Wrestling’s summer supercard extravaganza ...
SUMMER BURNOUT! Catch all the action ... catch all the drama ... catch all of the hard hitting wrestling ... as Denton, Texas heats up for a scorcher of a Summer ...
Live from the Denton Civic Center ... tickets now on sale at all area Piggly
Wiggly stores and the UNT Ticket window!
(With the projector turned off, the camera fades to black as a phone number for more information slides across the screen.)
