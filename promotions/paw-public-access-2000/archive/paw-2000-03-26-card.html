PAW — Card Archive

3.26 Card

26 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera opened on Pep Preston putting a beating on the self-proclaimed enigma, Kyle Arcin with hammering Forearms in the corner followed by a Cradle
Suplex. Arcin was able to break the pin attempt on a 2 count from the ref.
Whipped into the ropes, Arcin was scooped up but reversed the hold as he put
Preston down with a Spinning Head Scissors. Bouncing off the ropes, he came off with an awkward Senton that missed it's target as Pep rolled out of the way.
Pulling Arcin to his feet, Pep wasn't ready for the Uppercut that staggered him back into the ropes. Backing up, Arcin took off on the run and dove over the top of his opponent, hooking his legs around Preston's neck. With his momentum carrying him to the outside, he pulled Pep out of the ring as well ... both men hit the floor very hard.
Referee Peter Daye was slowly counting both men out as they struggled to get to their feet. Holding his ribs, Arcin got to his feet liberating a chair from a fan and setting it up on the ring apron. Several Stomps later, Arcin dragged
Pep up onto the apron and sat him down onto the chair. Heading back into the ring with the referee on his heels, Arcin again dashed across the ring, jumping up onto the ropes and leaping off ... hitting Pep in the face with a Missile
Drop Kick, again both men hit the floor very hard.
Chants of "Holy Shit" started up around the small television studio as the fans rose to their feet. Pep, with blood running from his nose, was pulled to his feet and Kicked back into the ring barrier. From the apron, Arcin landed a
Flying Clothesline that took Pep over to the other side.
Running along the edge of the barrier, Arcin landed on top of the prone Preston with a Suicide Dive, hooking a leg for the pin and a win in his PAW debut.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Daye raised a battered Kyle Arcin's arm into the air as the PAW Security stepped in to move people back. Climbing back over the barrier, large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession.)
... 5 ... ... 4 ... ... 3 ... ... 2 ... ... 1 ... (Rob Zombie's "More Human than Human" starts to play loudly as clips of Public
Access Wrestling 2000's Superstars in action passes ... Skip Westervelt ...
Justin Hart ... Jake Morgan ... Neo Draven and Sasha ... Brian Cage ... Johnny
Green and finally, the Texas Heavyweight Champion, Jason Flint, standing in the center of the ring lifting his title belt skyward ...
With an explosion, the scenes disappear from the screen and are replaced by the logo of Public Access Wrestling 2000 as it moves forward, filling the screen.
The song suddenly stops and with a smooth transition, fades out ... With a just as smooth fade in, the camera pans the wrestling ring surrounded by a vocal but small group of fans before passing over the small studio set.
Standing off to the side, in front of the PAW Banner, host Aaron Starr, side-by-side with President B.J. Perris, smiles into the camera.)
<Aaron Starr> Welcome wrestling fans to Public Access Wrestling 2000! Just one week shy of what promises to be the biggest show in our short but illustrious history ... Primal Rage! And joining me this evening is B.J. Perris, PAW
President ... (Starr turns towards Perris.) B.J. ... <Perris> Aaron ... as always it's a pleasure ... <Aaron Starr> One week away, B.J. ... your thoughts on Primal Rage? We have two matches already ... what else can we look forward to?
<Perris> Aaron ... I aim to please ... and in doing so, I take into account what the fans want to see ... with that in mind, it's been some time since we had a good old fashioned hardcore brawl ... making our third match to be announced an as of yet to be determined gimmick match between Brian Cage and
Drake Russell ... and with a Television Champion being crowned, we need to determine who the #1 Contender to that title is going to be ... opening the show with an Over-the-Top Battle Royale ...
Who knows what else can come out in a week's time? (Before continuing, Perris was interrupted by Skip Westervelt who marched out from the back with Chip in tow. Carrying a mic of his own, Skip waved off the
President.)
<Skip Westervelt> You know ... I'm getting sick and tired of listening to people around this place putting this man down ...
(Westervelt motioned towards the obese Chip.) You might think it's funny but what I see here is a lean, mean fighting machine
... no longer is he under the yoke of "Dracula" and Sunbeam ... no more of this
Fred Fomain crap ... from now on ... it's a serious beating as we take things all the way to the top!
(AC/DC's "If you want blood, You've got it!" blared out over the PA as Jake
Morgan ran out from the back and leveled Skip with a Clothesline. Grabbing the backpeddling Chip, Morgan rolled him into the ring. The ref called for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Chip was hammered around the ring before being pushed into a corner. Mounting the ropes, Morgan Punched away as the fans counted each blow that fell on it's target. Stepping back, Morgan watched as Chip fell flat on his face, taking a
Leg Drop across the back of his head.
Pulled to his feet, Chip took a Short Arm Clothesline and a Leg Lariat after being Whipped into the ropes. Following a Body Slam, Morgan knocked Skip from the ring apron with a Back Elbow before returning to the fight.
Whipping Chip into the ropes, Morgan pressed the big man into the air, dropping him onto his shoulders and going into a spin. Going around and around, Morgan dropped Chip to the mat. Dizzy from the Airplane Spin, Chip wobbled around the ring, taking a Boot to the face and falling over the top rope.
Before going to the outside, Morgan was tripped up by Skip Westervelt who took out his legs. Dragged out of the ring by, Morgan landed on the back of his head. With Morgan down, Skip directed Chip to the ring apron where he came off with a big Splash. Morgan was able to roll a shoulder off the floor on a near pinfall.
A Double Whip sent the former Champion into the ring barrier. Holding a chair up to Morgan's face, Skip shouted to his partner who came running in with a
Shoulder Butt, driving the chair into Morgan's face.
The fans were booing as Chip Body Slammed the former Champion while Skip did his best to get the fans involved. Pulled up by his hair, Morgan was led around the ring towards the ring steps, blocking Chip as he tried to slam his head down. With an Elbow to the gut, Morgan slammed Chip's head down. Taking a shot to the back, Morgan spun around and sent Skip to the floor with a Short Arm
Clothesline.
The steps rang out loudly with Chip being Powerbombed on top of them. With Chip holding his back and out of the action, Morgan chased Skip around the ring.
Diving in then out, Skip was able to avoid the angered Morgan. With a warning from Daye, Morgan took the match back to the ring after tossing a chair to the inside. Despite complaints from Westervelt, Daye let the match go on as Morgan beat Chip around the ring for the next few minutes. Morgan's
Piledriver onto the chair signaled the end for Chip who quickly found himself locked in the Cobra Clutch.
A fast tap out followed. *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (The fans were cheering as Morgan stood victorious inside the ring while Skip led Chip to the back. Ready to leave for the back, Morgan found Skip Westervelt leading the State Club Boyz to the ring. The fans were booing as the SCB's entered the ring.)
<Johnny Green> You know, Jake ... Jason's a real patient customer ... rather than kick your ass in the back this week ... he's going to wait until next week at Primal Rage to make it official ...
(Green was smiling as the State Club Boyz circled Morgan ... looking like sharks drawn to blood.)
Now ... me on the otherhand, I'm not so content to sit back and wait ... I'd rather kick your ass right here and now ... but I'm going to take a page from
Jason's book and wait ...
But ... seeing as the Boyz don't have anything else to do ... they're going to kick your ass ... Get him, Boyz ...
(All three members rushed in. Morgan took down the first one who got to him but the other two piled on, swarming over Morgan. While they dished out a beating, the camera followed Skip Westervelt and Chip into the back where they were both confronted by Neo Draven.
Leaning the beaten Chip against the wall, Skip found himself eye to eye with the angered Neo Draven. For the past couple of weeks, Skip had been embroiled in a one-sided war of words with the leader of the Dead Domain.
As the two eyed each other, Skip spun around when tapped on the shoulder. He ducked out of the way just in time as Sasha stepped in and blew a large fireball in his direction. Barely dodging, the conflagration caught Neo Daven instead. Dropping to the ground screaming, Neo was in pain while Skip grabbed
Chip and high-tailed it out of there.
Moving away, the camera came upon Lisa Doyle standing in a small secluded area in the back of the studio. She is skimming over cards and papers preparing for her interview with Drake Russell. She notices threats to MT2K, Brian Cage, and
Neo Draven. She looks up from her cards and speaks to the camera man.)
<Lisa Doyle> Hey, what kind of mind-set is this man in? I mean honestly, what kind of moron do you have to be to challenge the Mean Team 2000, or Brian Cage and Neo Draven to a gimmick match?
(As she finishes say thing a figure appears in the lower left hand side of the cameras shot. It is Drake Russell, he addresses Lisa.)
<Drake Russell> Is that my first question? (Lisa looks startled.) <Lisa Doyle> I'm so sorry, Mr. Russell. <Drake Russell> It's fine Lisa, my aren't you a piece of eye candy? Well, back to your question...you have to be in a special mind-set. You could be like
Brian Cage and walk around drooling and spitting out sentence fragments to every camera that you see, or you could be like me. I have a winning mentality and a mind-set that is targeted on winning matches and defeating weaklings which is, by the way, what Cage is.
<Lisa Doyle> Drake, you are by now aware of Brian Cage and his hardcore background, why would you issue a gimmick match challenge for your fight tonight?
(Drake breathes in and exhales with an answer.) <Drake Russell> He thinks that he is special because he can swing chairs and smash tables...this is my way of showing him that anyone can do it, and noone is special because of it. I will prove to him and Neo Draven tonight that the only way to win is with a good, sound strategy.
<Lisa Doyle> That stategy would be what? <Drake Russell> That strategy being having a clear mind and not thinking that you are better than your opponents, but by proving it.
(Lisa looks at a card.) <Lisa Doyle> Cage also claimed that you had an ego and that you are trying to control President Perris, what do you have to say about that?
Drake Russell: Again, mere sentence fragments that have no meaning. If he must know, my ego is only flamed by the gasoline that you pour on it. You are you rediculous claims of me attempting to run things is what makes my head grow because I know that you are jealous of me, Drake Russell.
<Lisa Doyle> A whole lot of talk of Brian Cage and not much of Draven, why is this? <Drake Russell> Lisa, this man has nothing left in his poor body. He is the underdog in every match that he is in, and most of the time you would root for the underdog. Not this time, Lisa. He is not the problem in this fight, it lies in the mediocrity of Cage. Neo, I am not overlooking you, because I know that on one night, anyone can beat anyone, but tonight is not your night.
<Lisa Doyle> On a different note Drake, you have issued challenges to the Mean
Team 2000 many times and so far, no matches, why do you believe this is?
(Drake laughs quietly to himself.) <Drake Russell> It's simply a matter of fear. They fear what they cannot understand, and they cannot understand why I am so good. They are afraid that
I would take them out one at a time and you know what Ms. Doyle? They are absolutely correct. I would love to have a match with either of them...if they are men enough to accept. But for now Lisa, I am going to lace up my boots once again and show these kids in the arena why I am the best performer in this federation, let that be a lesson to you hun.
(Drake walks out of the set and Lisa is left alone.) <Lisa Doyle> What this man is capable of is something we are all wondering about. (Once again on the move, the camera came in on a former member of the Texas
Terrors, Kat Collins. Dressed in a short tight mini skirt and blouse, Kat walked through the hall to the "click-clack" of her stiletto heels. With her smiling and blowing a kiss, the camera returned to the ring where Brian Cage and Drake Russell were fighting it out.
Russell had already been busted open, presumably from the chair Cage was swinging. The fans were cheering as Russell wrestled the chair away from Cage and beat him around the ring, throwing him into anything that was standing still ... the ring apron, the ring barrier, even Whipping him into the PAW
Security bowling them over.
After fighting into the crowd and parting them, kicking over chairs as they went ... Russell led Cage back into the ring with referee Dan Glass calling for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** As the two men continued to duke it out, there was one curious absence from the match ... Neo Draven was nowhere to be seen. Though busted open early, Russell had Cage reeling with a Double Underhook Back Breaker and nearly taking the pinfall.
On his feet, the confident and cocky Russell waved his arms around trying to incite the crowd that was finally settling back down into their seats. Working
Cage up the ropes, Russell seemed to have the match in control when Cage Gouged his eyes and pushed him from the top, coming off the top himself with an Elbow
Drop.
Blocking a Hip Toss, Russell took Cage off his feet with one of his own and dropping to the mat with a Leg Drop. Pushing Cage over, Russell began working his opponent's knee but was forced to break as Cage reached the ropes.
With a Whip into the ropes, Russell caught him on the return with a quick DDT. Slingshotting Cage into the corner, Russell went for a Hurricanrana which was turned into a Face First Powerbomb.
Cage's Neck Breaker had Russell in trouble before going to retrieve a new chair. Back in the ring, a Superkick propped Russell up in the corner before
"Mr. Hardcore" came in swinging with the chair. Cage hit the turnbuckle as
Russell ducked out of the corner and caught the man around the waist and dumped him on his head with a Belly-to-Back Suplex. Climbing through the ropes,
Russell came back into the ring with a Spingboard Splash and a cover for a 2 count. A Whip into the ropes and a Jump Spinning Back Kick put Cage onto his backside which was followed by a Dropkick to the face. Pulling Cage to his feet, Russell sunk in an Arm Bar. Cage's Back Elbows missed their mark as Russell deftly stepped to the side and drove his Knee into Cage's flank. Jumping up onto the ropes, Russell spun his body bringing his leg down over the back of Cage's neck pushing him to the mat with a Guillotine Face Driver.
Rather than go for the pin, Russell strutted around the ring bringing the fans to their feet ... or did he? Spinning around, he took a shot to the face from a redfaced Neo Draven who was weilding a chain.
His face red from the fireball he took earlier, began hammering away at the already bleeding Russell with the chain. Cage's chair to the back stopped that.
Throwing the chair down, Cage grabbed up Neo and DDT'd him onto the chair. Booting Russell from the ring, Cage took the fight to the outside which was joined quickly by Neo with an Asai Moonsault that took all three men down.
First on his feet, Russell lifted Neo into the air and dropped him down on top the ring barrier throatfirst.
Spinning Russell around, Cage backed him up with two Right Hands and a Head
Butt before Whipping him into the ring steps. Charging in, Cage meet the ring steps up close and personal, taken down with a Drop Toe Hold.
Coming up bloodyfaced, Cage went down from a Russell Belly-to-Belly Suplex onto the floor. A Dropkick from Neo put Russell down. Pulling a table out from under the ring, Neo was dropped to his knees from a hammering Forearm to the back from Cage. Russell grabbed both by the hair and slammed their heads together.
Staggering away, they gave Russell time to set the table up and pull a second one from underneath and set it up.
A chairshot to the head from Neo Draven stopped him in his tracks. Getting
Russell in a Standing Head Scissors was stopped as Cage charged in with a powerful Clothesline. Cage and Neo Draven Punched each other with Cage getting the better of the exchange and Whipping the smaller Neo into the ring barrier.
Charging in behind, Cage was taken over the top of the barrier with a modified
Back Body Drop.
Hitting the top of the barrier, Neo landed a Corkscrew Leg Drop. Tossing him over the barrier to the other side, Neo then Whipped him into the tables toppling them over before moving on to Russell. A Knee Lift staggered the bleeding Russell who in turn decked Neo with a lunging Clothesline. After a
Facebuster onto the floor, Russell kicked Cage out of the way before stacking the tables on top of each other.
Pulling Neo Draven onto the ring apron, Russell Hip Tossed him over onto the tables sending him crashing down through them. Wiping away the blood before going back on the attack, the bell sounded.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (The fans were booing the time limit draw as Glass looked in on Neo Draven who was still down in the wreckage of the two tables. Russell with his arms raised headed to the back leaving both Brian Cage and Neo Draven down when the camera broke away as pictures of the PAW's superstars flashes followed by letters spelling out P-R-I-M-A-L R-A-G-E in red letters. As the last letter finds it's place, they appear to drip as a male voice is heard.)
<Voiceover> April 2nd ... the Campus Theater in downtown Denton, Texas plays host to Public Access Wrestling's biggest event of the new millenium ... Primal
Rage ... Where differences are settled in the ring!
An Over-the-Top Battle Royale to determine the #1 Contendership for the PAW
Television Title!
Skip Westervelt and Neo Draven settle their differences of late in a match that will crown a new Television Champion!
Brian Cage ... looking to cement his hardcore reputation and revenge to an earlier setback ... takes on Drake Russell in an as yet to be determined gimmick match!
Jake Morgan, the former Texas Heavyweight Champion, comes to reclaim what he says is rightfully his when he faces off against Jason Flint, the current champion in a Ladder Match. Will Flint be able to stave off the challenge and retain his title?
All this and more ... April 2nd at PRIMAL RAGE! (With the scene fading out, there is a smooth fade in on the ring where Aaron
Starr is waiting, mic in hand.)
<Aaron Starr> Ladies and Gentlemen, give it up to MT2K! “Remote Control” by the Beastie Boys blares out as MT2K make their way out. Johnny Green and Jason Flint head the way, while the State Club Boyz and Kimberly Davis follow suite. Climbing into the ring, Flint makes a cutting gesture over his neck to cut the music.
<Aaron Starr> Guys… (But before Starr could ask his first question, Flint grabs the mic away and pushes him away and into the SCBs who begin to yell at him to get out of the ring.)
<Jason Flint> First of all, thanks for holding the mic for the best goddamn tag team here in PAW, but Starr, your pathetic line of questions can wait for later, the people want to hear what is on MT2K's minds. So Shooo.
(Flint waves off Starr, bringing some booing from the crowd, as Flint hands off the mic to Johnny Green.)
<Johnny Green> As much as we would like to hear from all our fans, right now….SHUT UP! (More boos for the Tag Champions as a small barrage of garbage hits the ring, causing the SCBs to start griping at the fans.)
Anywho, next week at Primal Rage, the major question is the main event. A ladder match for Jason’s Texas Heavyweight Championship belt. A match against
Jake Morgan This one’s for you Jakie, if history serves, at every major event in the promotion, the belt exchanged hands. So the question is: Will Jake
Morgan be able to become the 2-time PAW Texas Heavyweight Champion…..or not?
(Green hands over the mic to a smiling Jason Flint) <Jason Flint> I think….for my final answer it’ll be NOT! Jake Morgan, we’ve had this song and dance before and the outcome at Primal Rage is going to be the same. Let me tell you how Primal Rage is going to happen….first you’ll drive up in the shiny blue truck only to have my Boyz dump a whole lotta mud on that fine truck of yours, causing you to run off and wash it down again, because we all know that you like to wash that truck of yours. Then, when you come back here, theere will be an array of large words for you to look up on the internet, so you can tell us what they mean, because we all know that you like the internet. From there, we’ll give a shout out to Frenchy’s and have them come out and mow your lawn, because you like to watch people mow your lawn, go figure.
(Flint shrugs his shoulders and rolls his eyes.) But what it comes down to is this Jake, you didn’t have the means before and you certainly won’t have the means at Primal Rage. Ladder match or not,
Extremist, at Primal Rage, when the dust settles….hopefully not on that fine truck of yours, but when the dust settles and its all said and done, Jason
Flint will walk out of Primal Rage as the PAW Texas Heavyweight Champion and you’ll still be the FORMER Texas Heavyweight Champion. With that all said and done with…..I believe we have a match, right Johnny?
(Green nodded his head in agreement which was followed by the two challengers to the titles, Desperado and Paco. Sliding in under the ropes, Paco bounced to his feet and was pointing at the Texas Heavyweight Champion. Flint was rolling his eyes as he waved off the man he had beaten just weeks ago in a rather convincing fashion. With a wave of his hand, referee Peter Daye called for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Green and Desperado started off the match, keeping the action in Green's corner which allowed for swift tags for the Champions as they worked the masked man over. Green's Missile Drop Kick followed by Flint's Slingshot Leg Drop into a near fall which was broken up by Paco's Stomp to the back.
Flint was taunting Paco who was bing ushered out of the ring while Johnny Green pulled Desperado to the outside for the State Club Boyz to get their shots in.
With Paco out of the ring, Daye was warning Flint with taking his title away if they SCB's continued to interfere ... and with their past history, Flint shouted for his boyz to clear out. Using the ropes, Flint left the ring with a
Double Axe Handle to the outside.
Pulling Desperado up, Flint ordered the SCB's to ready a table as he hammered away with Fists to his masked face. Paco charged across the ring only to be held back by the ref while Flint dropped Desperado onto the table. Climbing back into the ring, Flint went up the ropes and tagged in Green before leaping from the top and driving Desperado through the table with a Corkscrew Splash.
Rolling out of the way, Flint was barely missed by Green who came flying out of the ring with an Asai Moonsault landing on top of the masked man.
After a Double Brainbuster onto the floor, Green and Flint were put down by
Paco as he came flying out of the ring with a Plancha. The small wrestler was pulled off the champs by Tony of the State Club Boyz and tossed aside. With a second warning, he went back to his corner away from the action.
Battling around the ring, MT2K kept the pressure up before bringing it back into the ring. Off a Double Whip into the ropes, Green's Drop Toe Hold put
Desperado down followed by Flint's Standing Senton.
Rushing into the ring, Paco was sent into the ropes by Green before being scooped up by Flint on the return and Powerslammed. Both opponents were tossed to the outside then taken down with a Double Baseball Slide that knocked both
Desperado and Paco into the ring barrier.
Rolling Desperado into the ring, Green's Whip into the ropes was followed by
Flint's Leg Lariat. Dragging Desperado into their corner, Flint and Green did a
"Chinese Fire Drill" of tags and Slingshot Leg Drops before Flint pushed
Desperado to the center of the ring and mounted the corner. Pumping his arms into the air, Flint leapt off and spun through the air, landing his Corkscrew
Splash ... the Shockwave.
Hooking Desperado's leg, Flint waited for Daye to drop to the canvas to slap out the 3 count for the match.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (The champions were tossed their belts and celebrating their victory when
AC/DC's "If you want blood, You've got it!" blared over the speakers system.
Strolling out from the back, Jake Morgan had a smile on his face, shaking his head as he made his way towards the ring.)
<Jake Morgan> You have exactly one week until that precious belt of yours makes it's way back onto MY waist where it belongs!
(Flint was nodding his head and rolling his eyes while making while Green motioned for Morgan to get into the ring..)
Thanks for the invitation Green, but I think I'll wait ... one week, Flint ... one week! (Turning, Jake Morgan walked off as the fans chanted "EXTREME!" as the show came to an end.)
