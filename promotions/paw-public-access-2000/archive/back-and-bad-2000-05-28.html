I see what you're up to ... and I don't like it one little bit ... (Green stepped forward and rested a hand on Flint's shoulder.) You see, some months back ... I made a promise ... despite what's come to pass between Jason and myself, I made a promise ...
(Flint looked back at Green and nodded his head in agreement with his tag team partner.) Over the last month, alot has happened to the Jason Flint camp ... first, you've done everything in your power to embarrass us as well take away everything we've earned. Add that to the internal bickering because of Jason's self-centered ego driven personality ... MT2K and those around us have been at war ... enough that the State Club Boyz left followed by the "Electric Referee"
Dan Glass ... Kim Davis can also be added to that list of Missing In Action as well because quite frankly, I have no idea what she's up to ... and to be honest, I don't care ...
You see, through it all ... there has been two constants in the equation ... Jason Flint has been the Texas Heavyweight Champion and I've been there by his side ... Johnny Green ... the other half of MT2K ... the often forgotten half of MT2K ...
And why? All because I made a promise ... and tonight that promise is going to be fullfilled ... I said by my side, I'd make you a Champion ... you'd be the best
... together, we'd reign supreme over this promotion ... (Flint's head bobbed up and down and gestured around his middle as Green talked. Seeing Flint agree with himi, a smile spread across his lips.)
It's time to make good on my promise ... (The sound of a heartbeat started over the PA ... low in volume at first but steadily increased in loudness. On the back wall, an image slowly came into focus ... a handprint ... as single bloody handprint ...
Seeing it on the backwall, Flint's eyes got big as he looked around the ring then back at Green who was pointing at the image on the wall. With the heartbeat continuing, Green's arm wheeled around and pointed towards where
Perris was standing and out from the back he came ... One man ... A familiar man ... A man who was quite familiar with the Texas Heavyweight Champion ... Justin Hart ... Dressed in black tights with yellow and orange patches running down the legs with black boots, the African-American man removed the black leather Jin Kazama flame embroidered jacket and tossed it aside as he strolled casually towards the ring.
Flint's eyes were wide has he looked back at his partenr who could do nothing but crack a big smile before lashing out with a Right Hand that put the Champ down. With Hart sliding into the ring, Peter Daye called for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** There was no feeling out period as Hart rushed in to attack. This was what he wanted months ago when he was a regular of the promotion, crawling slowly but steadily up the ladder ... a match that would bring him both respectability as well as a title ... the Texas Heavyweight Championship.
With a barrage of Forearms, he backed Flint into a corner before opening up with Reverse Knife Hands that popped loudly off the Champ's chest. With Flint slumping back in the corner, Hart snapped his head back with two big Forearm
Shivers. Turning out of the corner, Hart had the notion of putting Flint to the mat with a Hip Toss but was blocked. From the same position, Flint reversed things and put Hart down with a Hip Toss Power Slam. Dropping for the cover, Flint found
Peter Daye with a slow count which powered out of 1 count. Back on their feet, the two circled each other before locking up in the center of the ring. Flint hit Hart with an Elbow then tied him up with a Side
Headlock. Pushing things forward, Hart tried to toss Flint off of him but had no luck as Flint went along with him.
Hart tried to push him off towards the far ropes but Flint wasn't letting go of his opponent's head. Wrestling him to the mat, Flint was determined to keep him grounded. Pushing Hart down, Flint tired to cut off his air but was being watched closely by the referee for a choke. The two started arguing which brought a count from Daye forcing Flint to break the hold.
Hart sent Flint into the far ropes who ducked under a Punch on the return. Continuing on, Flint hit the far ropes and was ready to biounce back towards his opponet but found himself staring at the mat as one of the lumberjacks reached in and pulled his feet out from under him. Brian Cage and Adam Manson pulled him from the ring and worked him over with Lefts and Rights before rolling him back into the ring.
Thrown towards the ropes again, Flint was put down hard by a big Clothesline that spun him through the air. Pulling Flint to his feet and ducking behind him, Hart threw him around like a ragdoll with a series of German Suplexes ...
once ... twice ... three times a very determined Hart dropepd the Champ on the canvas. Leading Flint to the ropes and raking his eyes along it's length, Hart went down as Flint Kicked backwards. With Hart down, Flint Stomped him several times before grabbing a leg and twisting a foot. Holding on, Flint tried to further damge the leg, dropping a Knee into the back of Hart's thigh. Hart was howling out in pain as Flint continued to work the leg, this time going for a Spinning
Toe Hold. Flint kept yelling at Daye to ask Hart if he wanted to submit. Shaking his head no, Flint added to the pain as he Dropped an Elbow down then went for a cover. Another slow 1 count came from Daye.
Flint let up on Hart and began arguing with the ref over the counts. Pushing Daye back didn't help the situation as Daye pushed back. Angered, Flint moved in to take out Daye but found himself flat on his back as Ron and Tara Collins grabbed him by his angles and dragged him out of the ring. A flying Justin Hart dove over the top rope to the outside, crushing Flint underneath him.
That side of the ring became very busy as the lumberjacks run in hitting both wrestlers. Flint was bounced like a ping pong ball back and forth between the
Offspring and Raskal. Hart took some stiff shots from the very big Adam Manson before being thrown back into the ring. Flint took a few extra shots from the Neos ... Wolf and
Draven before he was tossed back in. Back in the ring, Hart nailed a Suplex and covered Flint, getting a near pinfall. Whipped into the ropes, Flint took a Boot to the midsection which was followed by a Jumping DDT. Another cover got the same results as Flint got his shoulder up at the last second.
Flint again went on the offensive showing exactly why he has been the Champion for the last 5 months. With a Foreman's Carry, Flint spun Hart off of his shoulder and down across his knee. Hart was writhing on the mat when Flint went up the ropes and jumped off and landed a Senton.
The fans were booing when the Champion strolled around the ring and and pointed at his former partner Johnny Green who just smiled. Returning to Hart, Flint worked him over for the next couple of minutes hitting a Brainbuster as well as a Tilt-A-Whirl Slam that gained near pinfalls. Daye's reluctance to hit the mat a third time lead to shoving matches between the two both times.
A Drop Kick to Hart's knee put him down and allowed Flint to chase the ref around the ring before he dove for the safety of the outside. Flint was caught by a limping Hart who dropped down with a Side Russian Leg Sweep but was pushed off before Daye slid back into the ring.
The fight went back and forth with the two trading big shots ... Hart's Superplex shook the ring but his knee buckled when he Gorilla Pressed Flint over his head. He was able to throw Flint over before he collapsed to the mat.
Flint's Leg Lariat was followed by a Somersault Leg Drop. Flint covered and again got a slow count from the referee.
Up, Flint pushed Daye into a corner and was threatening him when Hart slammed a Double Axe Handle across his back. Flint fell forward into Daye, knocking him to the mat leaving them without a ref.
Fighting on, neither man gained control of the match for an extended period of time, trading shots back and forth.
The fans were going crazy as the match marched on, going past 10 ... 20 ... 30 ... 40 ... into 50 minutes of elapsed time. After begging off, Flint dupped Hart before hitting him below the belt. Flint was laughing as he glared out of the ring at Johnny Green who just stood there watching the match. Turning just in time, Flint caught motion ... a person diving at him off of the ropes. It was the Offspring who came flying in smashing the Texas Title Belt into Flint's head and knocking him to the mat.
With the Offspring leaving the ring, Hart moved ... wrapping up Flint's legs and leaning back into the Urban Lockdown. Hart howled out as he strained to break Flint in half. To his credit, Flint clawed his way inch by inch towards the ring ropes but found no relief as he grabbed them. With no referee, Hart was not about to let go.
The lumberjacks on the outside parted as a second man in ref's strips dove into the ring. Getting down in Flint's face, the ref ... the former "Electric
Referee" Dan Glass asked if Flint was ready to give up. Protesting that he had the ropes, Flint was Kicked free by Johnny Green who jumped up onto the apron.
Looking at Flint hitting the mat facefirst, Glass called for the bell. *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (The fans went nuts as Justin Hart dropped Flint and raised his arms into the air. Green rushed into the ring and handed the Texas Heavyweight Belt to the promotion's newest Champion ...
The camera left ringside as Green and Hart made their way to the back before a brawl broke out around the ring as the lumberjacks went at it. Making it's way into the back, the camera came upon several PAW Security and Production people standing around a door while one of them was holding a large keyring full of keys.
Trying them and getting nowhere, the man continued while beyond, the person trapped inside pounded on door constantly. Finally, sticking another key in, he was able to turn the knob, unlocking the door. It flew open and an angered Jake
Morgan burst forth ... swinging ... Wading through Security and Production people alike, Morgan was like a madman sending people scattering as he cleared them out of the way before rushing off as the camera faded to black.)
