Aftre locking up in the center of the ring, Flint and Russell traded off Arm Bars, Side Headlocks and Reversals of the said moves until Westervelt got involved. All three were trading back and forth with the odd man out hitting the one in control and taking his place.
Russell had Westervelt locked up when Flint knocked them both through the ropes with a Drop Kick. Skip hit the floor hard as Flint slid out of the ring. On the outside, he and Russell squared off throwing Punches that had blood streaming down Russell’s bandaged head. He was a mess as Perris moved in with a chair and smashed it over Flint’s head.
Under directions from Perris, Russell pulled a table out from under the ring and set it up on the floor before tossing Flint over the top of it. There was a trickle of blood from the chairshot as Russell threw him on top of the table before heading back into the ring and hitting the ropes. Throwing himself backwards, Russell stuck a perfect Asai Moonsault ... only Flint rolled of the table, leaving Russell to hit the floor alone.
After hitting Perris and slamming him facedown into the floor, Flint went to work, pulling a portion of the ring barrier apart and bridging it across both the ring apron and another barrier section. Throwing Russell on top, he looked back at the crowd with a crazy look before running his thumb across his throat and climbing back into the ring.
Climbing the turnbuckle, he ignored the warning referee before launching hiimself into the air. Spinning, he landed backfirst with a Senton bending the steel framework in half ... the only problem was that Russell had returned the favor and rolled off at the last second.
Bent in an awkward “V” shape, Flint did not move as Russell pulled himself to his feet. Looking around at both Flint and the still downed, Russell grabbed up the chair Perris used and threw it down on the floor as the fans chanted “Holy
Shit!” Pulling Flint up, Russell planted him into the chair with a DDT. The move had done it’s job, busting Flint wide open. After rolling Flint back into the ring, the Champ had a bloody nosed Skip Westervelt to contend with.
Whipping Russell into the ropes, he took the Champion down with a wellplaced knee to the stomach. Stomping at Russell’s bloody head brought Perris up onto the apron to argue with Daye. A Whip and a subsequent Sidewalk Slam had
Westervelt hooking Russell’s leg for a pin but Daye was distracted by Perris until Flint grabbed the prez and tossed him off the apron, right onto the twisted metal frame of the barrier he had crashed through.
The fans went silent as Perris laid there until he twitched. By the time Daye saw the pin, Russell was able to kick out. Flint came in and Dropped an elbow onto Skip.
Slow to get back to their feet, both Flint and Russell circled each other as the fans cheered loudly until they locked up. Gaining control, Russell took him over with an Arm Drag. Rolling back to his feet, Flint was taken over with a second one before Russell locked in an Arm Bar. Pulling Flint to his feet,
Russell worked the arm, able to deny Flint all chances of breaking the hold, keeping the action in the center of the ring.
Slipping in behind Flint, Russell fended off several Back Elbows before taking Flint down with a Belly-to-Back Suplex. A cover only yielded a 2 count as Flint got the shoulder up at the last second.
Lifting Flint into the air, Russell brought him down with a Double Underhook Backbreaker before dropping down. Daye slapped the mat before tapping Russell on the shoulder ... Flint had a foot on the ropes.
Perris, who had pulled himself off the broken barrier, was back up on the apron and shouting at Daye to end the match. After hitting Russell with a Low Blow,
Flint retrieved his Singapore Cane. Perris grabbed Daye by the collar and leveled Daye in a fit of anger. Stepping over Daye, Flint nailed Perris in the head with the cane, knocked him off of the apron and onto the floor.
Taking a shot to the back from Skip Westervelt, Flint put him down with a cane shot to the head, opening him up. After lighting up on Westervelt with the cane, Flint tossed the splintered weapon aside before leaving the ring.
After returning with a garbage can, he dragged Russell to the middle of the ring then put the can over Russell’s head. Climbing to the top, he looked out over the ring where Russell, Westervelt and Daye were all down. Jumping from the top, he spun through the air with a Corkscrew Splash ... the Shockwave, crushing the can over Russell’s head. With the fans chanting, Flint slowly got to his feet, his face now a bloody mess.
Slapping Daye on the face to bring him around, Flint never saw the persons enter the ring until it was too late. Struck from behind, Flint dropped to his knees in pain from her Low Blow. With Flint writhing on the mat, a woman wearing the Offspring’s mask, complete with the braids hanging down in her face, ordered Kat Collins to pull Flint up. Held from behind, Flint could no more than sturggle as the woman swung her leg up and booted him in the face
Leaving the ring, she was followed by Kat who had to get in a few Stomps of her own. Holding his back, a bleeding Perris pulled Drake Russell from the ring and led him to the back while Peter Daye pulled himself up and called for the bell ...
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Daye called for help in the ring as he slumped heavily against the ring ropes. The small but boisterious crowd was going crazy as the credits for the end of the show started to run.)
