12.3 Card

3 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 4 —

(The camera opens in the back of the television studio where a black Honda pulls up. With the car parked, the camera is turned back towards a waiting
Lonny Nakamura.) Lonny Nakamura: Right on time ... (The camera turns back to the car where the mysterious XYZ steps out, cloaked in black. His fist is clenched over something as Nakamura approaches.)
Are you ready? XYZ: Why wouldn't I be? One of the biggest nights of my career Nakamura-san and a night where I must prove myself to you and the rest of the wrestling world.
Hart and Russell stand no chance tonight, no chance. We Japanese are too FAR superior to lose than them Nakamura-san, I can promise you that!
Lonny Nakamura: Don't worry XYZ, everything will work out. Now are you sure you know tonight's gameplan?
XYZ: Hai, I know it quite well ... I think it is simply perfect! The way you beautifully described and created the plan, just excellent! Nagasaki-san and I make an awesome tag team tonight, we take out American scum!
Lonny Nakamura: Yes, you will. We must hurry now. XYZ: Of course Nakamura-san. (The two then walk off to together before the camera zooms in on the tool in XYZ's hand is that foriegn object he split Hart open with last week. Moving back inside the studio, the camera comes to bear on the door of Jake Morgan's dressing room. Swinging around, it catches Darlene and Jeremy approaching as they speak to each other in hushed tones.)
Darlene: Jeremy, we have to do something. I'll go get the doctor. After what happened this past week, there is no way they are going to let him compete tonight.
(Jeremy gets a dubious look on his face.) Jeremy: I don't know Darlene, if they really cared they would have stopped him long before this.
(Darlene shakes her head.) Darlene: You’d think they’d pick up on a concussion at least. Something ... anything to keep him out of the ring. (Jeremy shrugs.) Jeremy Morgan: Don’t worry, I'll try to keep him locked up. (Before walking off, the door opens and a short oriental man with thick glasses wearing a white lab coat steps out. Closing the door behind him, he is startled to see Darlene and Jeremy standing there.)
Darlene: You’re the doctor? Doctor: Hai! Darlene: You looked Jake over? Doctor: Hai! (Darlene looks over at Jeremy who can only shrug.) Darlene: So ... he's unfit right? Doctor: No! (Jeremy's jaw drops.) Darlene: What are you talking about? You've talked to him. He isn't okay. There is no way he’s fit to wrestle tonight. You’ve got to be kidding me?
Doctor: Hai! (Darlene stands there shocked, as Jeremy gives her an" I told you so" look.) Jeremy Morgan: What a second ... doctor, can you say anything other than “hai” or “no”? (With a serious look on his face, the doctor tilts his head for a second and ponders the question.)
Doctor: Hai! Darlene: This is some kind of joke. You don’t even speak English do you? Nakamura put you up to this? DO ... YOU ... SPEAK ... ENGLISH? Doctor: Hai! (Jeremy slaps his forehead as Darlene sighs deeply.) Darlene: Look ... I’m going to find the boss and put a stop to this ... (Darlene starts to walk off as the doctor starts to shake his head.) Doctor: My decision stands ... Jake Morgan is fit to wrestle ... (The doctor pushes past the pair and walks off. Laughter is heard down the hallway catching Darlene and Jeremy’s attention. Swinging that way, the camera finds the PAW Television Champion, Hiroshima Nagasaki before smoothly fading out.
The camera fades in on Lonny Nakamura standing in front of a modified PAW banner done up in red and white with the face of it’s Television Champion,
Hiroshima Nagasaki gracing it. The well dressed Japanese manager in his expensive suit wrings his hands before turning back and looking over the men standing with him. Two of them shirtless with scars visible from previous battles inside the squared circle while a third man stands silently staring straight ahead, the lower half of his face covered by a mask and finally, a fourth man completely covered, his face, hidden, underneath a hood.
Turning back towards the camera, an evil grin spreads across Nakamura’s lips.) Lonny Nakamura: With only a week before Public Access Wrestling’s big event, Winter’s Nightmare, the opposition to your puny promotion has started to roll in ... your Television Champion’s fellow countrymen have begun to invade your tiny, backwards promotion ... all for one simple reason ...
(Nakamura claps his hands loudly and laughs. Regaining his composure, he adjusts his glasses before continuing.)
... to prove to you how poor your brand of American wrestling is. Your Champion, Hiroshima Nagasaki and these men ... (Nakamura motions to the men behind him.) ... will show you why the PAW is truly ... Pathetic American Wrestling ... (Stepping back, Nakamura slaps the masked man on the shoulder. The man gives a quick nod of the head, starring deeply into the camera, a scar running from his left temple to his cheek bone is visible.)
"The Dragon" Aroku Murakami ... (Moving towards the smaller of the shirtless men, Nakamura gives and recieves a kurt bow from the handsome man in black wrestling trunks.)
Devil Tsaruta ... (Smiling again, Nakamura steps past Tsaruta towards the taller of the scarred men.) ... and I believe you are already familiar .. or at least Justin Hart is familiar with a true up and coming icon in what you Americans have dubbed
“Garbage Wrestling”, the mysterious XYZ ... (The four men laugh until Nakamura aburptly stops. With a quizzical look on his face, he looks at the fifth man standing there, the one covered from head to toe. Looking from that lone man then to the others, Nakamura turns back to the camera for a second then back to the hooded person.
Reaching for the hood, Nakamura gives it a quick yank revealing Emilio Ramirez underneath. The smiling Ramirez looks at Nakamura then to the others.)
Emilio Ramirez: INS? Lonny Nakamura: What the?!?!?!?! (The nervous Ramirez looks at each of the men before they pounce on him, knocking him to the floor and give him a stomping when the camera fades out.
Leaving the beating, the camera finds referee Peter Daye in the backstage area drinking a Dr. Pepper when Jason Flint walks up and slaps him hard on the back.
The surprise of the blow causes Daye to expel his last gulp all over the place. Turning, he frowns when he sees Flint.) Jason Flint: Good to see that you’re not hitting the bottle ... (Tapping Daye on the shoulder, the smiling Flint walks off leaving the shocked referee alone.)
Peter Daye: God, I hate that guy! (The angered Daye shakes his head in disgust as the camera fades out on him before shifting to the outside of the television studio and the backstage area, away from lights and the few fans still filtering in. On a chilly evening, a security guard shivers.)
Guard #1: Damn it's cold out here ... (He looks over at the other, who's huddled in a jacket. To say the least, he's warmer.) When's the end of our shift? (The second guard looks at him and shrugs.) Guard #2: Beats the hell out of me. Didn't you wear a coat to work today? Guard #1: No ... It WAS warmer when I left the house today. (Out of nowhere, he's hit in the face with a jacket. A Jin Kazama leather jacket. He uncovers his face in time to hear a pissed off Justin Hart walk by.)
Justin Hart: Here ... now stop your fuckin' whining!! (The two security guards look on as Hart steps inside, muttering all the way.) I'm gonna put a stop to all these fucking "prison jokes." (Hart kicks the door open as large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession...)
.... 5 .... .... 4 .... .... 3 .... .... 2 .... .... 1 .... (White Zombie's "More Human than Human" begins as flashes of Hiroshima Nagasaki in action are shown. Scene after scene quickly flash by of past battles ...
Running his thumb across his throat before a limp Drake Russell is planted into the canvas with a Nagasaki Driver…
Following a Flying Knee straight to an incoming Seth's face, Nagasaki locks on the “Unconditional Surrender” for a win ...
The “Street Soldier” Justin Hart takes a Double Thrust to the Throat and with a Snap Suplex, put into a pinning predicament ... Pep Preston is flat on his back as Nagasaki leaps from the top turnbuckle and lands the “H-Bomb” ...
In slow motion, Aaron Starr turns right into a ball of fire thrown into his face and finally the PAW Television Champion standing in the middle of the ring, the belt raised over his head.)
After an explosion, the scene fades to black and the PAW logo disappears before fading back in and panning the small television studio and the fifty or so fans surrounding the ring on three sides, many with signs held aloft such as "One
More Week!!", "Nagasucky!!" and "I’m Turning Japanese!". Moving through the small crowd, the camera zooms in on a smiling Aaron Starr, waving to the fans and his cohost, Ishimoto Sakai. Turning to the camera, Aaron scoops up his microphone.
Aaron Starr: Public Access Wrestling is one week away from the big event, Ish ... one more week until we see the superstars of the PAW run your countrymen ... (Sakai slowly turns his head towards Starr, giving him an evil look.) Ishimoto Sakai: Run my countrymen? I think it is you who are somewhat mistaken, Starr ... next week at the Winter’s Nightmare Supercard ... you and these fans are going to have the privilege of seeing some of the most exciting and talented young wrestlers from Japan take on your group of slackers and underachievers ...
We’ve already seen what the psychotic XYZ is capable of as he carved up the head of your Justin Hart ... and everyone knows that neither Jason Flint nor
Jake Morgan has what it takes to defeat YOUR PAW Television Champion, Hiroshima Nagasaki ... Aaron Starr: Wait one second ... Ishimoto Sakai: You throw in the Black Ravens, Devil Tsuruta and the “Dragon” Aroku Murakami ... what chance do your pathetic wrestlers have? (A chant of “PAW!” starts up from the boisterious crowd, making Sakai laugh.) Chant all you want ... in one week, we will all know the truth ... Aaron Starr: You know, Ish ... I have half a mind to lock you in a closet myself ... (With an angered look, Sakai whips his head around and looks at his broadcast partner.) Ishimoto Sakai: And if I find you had anything to do with that incident ... you will be very sorry ...
Aaron Starr: For those of you who weren’t with us, my partner is referring to his unfortunate disappearance from the show two weeks ago ... let’s go to the clip ...
Ishimoto Sakai: WHAT!?!?!?! (The camera fades on the two men as a video clip plays ... the camera found it’s way into the back and the green haired production assistant. Stopping in his tracks, the man listened for something. Craning his neck, he listened intently for a few seconds until stepping up slowly to the door.
Putting an ear to the door, he listened closely, jumping back at the sound of a thumping coming from within. Taking several steps backwards, he stood there for several more seconds until the sound was once again heard from within.
Moving a hand to the doorknob, he pulled the door open to find a bound Ishimoto Sakai inside a broom closet, gray duct tape across his mouth. Stepping forward, the green haired man reached for the tape and gave it a sharp pull, freeing the announcer’s mouth from which poured a yelp of pain followed by a stream of unintelligible words most likely in Japanese. With the clip over, Starr and
Sakai faded back into view.) Aaron Starr: I have no idea what you said but I’d bet it was an earful though I’m sorry to say we don’t have time for a translation now. We’ve got two big matches, kicking things off with a tag team tilt featuring the team of Justin
Hart and Drake Russell facing the TV Champion Hiroshima Nagasaki and XYZ. (Starr turns towards the now smiling Sakai.) Time for a little payback, eh Ish? I’m sure Justin Hart would love to get his hands around this XYZ’s throat for carving him last week ...
Ishimoto Sakai: Two of Japan’s finest young wrestlers taking on what is little more than a pair of thugs ...
Aaron Starr: One of those “thugs” took the Champion to the limit last week until he was jumped by your fine outstanding young wrestlers ... and let’s not forget, it was Hart who planted XYZ into the canvas with the “1-8-7” ...
Ishimoto Sakai: After attacking him from behind ... Aaron Starr: Ish ... what match were you watching? The man was jumped by three men ... (Sakai shakes his head while waving a hand in Starr’s face.) Ishimoto Sakai: Merely protecting the Television Champion from a heinous attack ... (Sakai’s words are drowned out by a cheer from the crowd as someone comes walking out from the back. A shirtless young man in jeans and boots, with his head wrapped in a black and silver bandana strolls out with a smile on his face. Nodding his head, the man points to the fans before heading over towards the seated Aaron Starr and Ishimoto Sakai.
With the man approaching, Starr jumps up from his seat with mic in hand.) Aaron Starr: Kidd Gannon ... (Approaching, Gannon slaps Starr on the shoulder before looking around at the redecorated studio.)
What are you doing here? Kidd Gannon: You know, Aaron ... I’ve been away for just a little too long. Six or so months away from here and this place certainly looks a whole lot different than I remember ...
Aaron Starr: Welcome back, Kidd. What brings you back to Public Access Wrestling? Are you here to manage again? Kidd Gannon: It’s good to be back No Aaron, I’m here to lace up the boots and see a little ring action but first, I’ve gotta ask ... what’s with all of this? (Starr turns to see the Hiroshima Nagasaki decorated PAW banners hanging on the wall. Starr smiles as he turns back to Gannon.)
Aaron Starr: Well, Kidd ... that’s a long, long story ... (Sakai steps up to the handsome young man.) Ishimoto Sakai: That just happens to be Hiroshima Nagasaki ... the Champion of this promotion ... YOUR Champion ... and if you’re not careful, the man who will beat you inside that squared circle ...
(Gannon smiles as he runs his hand through the stubbly hair on his chin.) Kidd Gannon: Is that so? (Gannon turns towards Starr, pointing at Sakai.) He doesn’t know who I am, does he? (Starr shakes his head as Gannon steps closer to Sakai.) I don’t know who you are ... and I don’t know who YOUR Champion is but that sounds like a challenge to me ... and us Gannon’s don’t back down from any challenges ...
(Pushing Sakai out of the way, Gannon walks to the nearest banner and yanks it down. Wading it up, he throws it down on the ground. Wiping his hands, he returns to the dumbfounded Sakai and the smirking Starr.)
That’s what I think of your Champion ... (Chants of “Welcome Back!” from the crowd sweeps through the studio, bringing a smile to Gannon. The chanting dies away quickly and is replaced by boos as
Lonny Nakamura storms out from the back. Stepping back, Gannon watches as the Japanese manager steps up to Sakai with his trusty microphone. A furious Nakamura pushes his finger into Gannon’s chest to make a point.)
Lonny Nakamura: I don’t know who you are but I suggest to leave this studio before I have you thrown out!
(Gannon looks down at the finger repeatedly jabbing him in the chest, then with a smile on his face looks back up at Nakamura. Moving with blinding speed,
Gannon grabs Nakamura by the lapels and shoves him back. Surprised, the now angered Nakamura steps forward only to stop as Gannon raises a fist. Gulping hard, Nakamura steps back as Gannon inches forward, his fist shaking, ready to strike. Putting his hands up, Nakamura pleads with Gannon not to hit him, stepping behind Ishimota Sakai.
Gannon raises his fist as a chant of “Kick his ass” rises from the crowd. Stepping closer to the begging Nakamura, Gannon is suddenly spun around by a handsome Japanese man in black boots that come up to his black knee pads, with greyish silver leg guards and small black wrestling trunks.)
Ishimoto Sakai: DEVIL TSARUTA!!!!! DEVIL TSARUTA!!!!! (Surprised, Gannon has no time to defend himself as he takes a Double Throat Chop. Staggering backwards, Gannon is grabbed and pulled closer before put down with a Reverse Russian Leg Sweep.
With Gannon laying on the floor and twitching, Tsaruta returns to his feet and nonchalantly walks around the studio, getting between everyone and Lonny
Nakamura. Crossing his arms over his scarred chest, Tsaruta stands silently. As the boos continue to filter in from the seats, Nakamura whispers something into Tsaruta’s ear before pulling the mic away from Sakai.)
Lonny Nakamura: There’s your challenge!!!!! (Leaving Nakamura, Tsaruta yanks Gannon up by his bandana, leading him towards the ring. Starting to push Gannon into the ring, Tsaruta takes a Punch to the face. Caught by surprise, Tsaruta staggers back as Gannon rolls into the ring.
Throwing himself back against the far ropes, the young wrestler darts across the ring, building up a head of steam for a Tope Con Hilo that takes out both
Devil Tsaruta and Lonny Nakamura. With Starr and Sakai stepping in to seperate both parties as they stagger back to their feet, they are joined by PAW Security that has hit ringside to pull them apart.
As they are separated, Gannon has a few last words for Nakamura and Tsaruta while being led to the back. With order restored, the camera focusses back in on Starr and Sakai.)
Aaron Starr: We’ll be right back, folks with our first match of the evening ... Ishimota Sakai: As the PAW Television Champion, Hiroshima Nagasaki and XYZ take on Justin Hart and Drake Russell!

— Part 2 of 4 —

(The camera fades on the two just as they start to argue before making it’s way into the back and finding Jake Morgan followed by Mean Darlene and brother
Jeremy. Clutching his forehead, he begins to complain about a headache.) Jake Morgan: Damn head's pounding ... (Darlene fishes around in her pouch looking for an aspirin, but to no avail.) Mean Darlene: Look Jake, why don’t we just get you in the truck and head on home? You can lie down ...
Jeremy, why don’t you go bring the truck around? (Agreeing, the younger Morgan heads off. She looks up just in time to see Jake ... with Hart in his face.) (The two stand there waiting for the other to make his move when a sharp whistle is heard, getting both men’s attention. Turning, they see Shane, head of PAW Security leaning against the wall.)
Shane: Okay guys ... playtime’s over ... you’re up, Hart ... (Turning back to Hart, Morgan shoves him back.) Jake Morgan: Get the Hell out of my face. (As Darlene leads Morgan away, Hart waves them off.) Justin Hart: Fuck me ... fuck Russell ... fuck Flint... and FUCK YOU!!!!! Shane: Okay, tough guy ... let’s go ... (Hart shoots Shane a look as the muscular Security guard leads him away. Leaving them, the camera leaves for another part of the backstage area where the “One-man Heartbreaker”, Paco is strolling. Dressed in his white sequined outfit, Paco walks defiantly towards his destination ... wherever that might be until the identical looking Black Ravens step out from the shadows. The two
Asian men look at each other before turning back to Paco and leering at him.) Paco: Whoa, little brothers ... you shouldn’t jump out at someone like that. You could scare him to death. (The two men look at each other and shake their heads before turning back.) I said ... (Speaking loudly, he repeats the same thing only this time much more slowly.) Whoa ... little brothers ... you shouldn’t jump out ... at someone like that ... You could ... scare him ... to death!!!!! (Finished, Paco does a little hip swivel before cocking his head back at the two men.) Didja get it? — Part 3 of 4 —

(Raising the still stunned Hart to a seated position after the pin, Nagasaki
locked on the “Unconditional Surrender” with Xyz standing guard. Hart went limp in the Champ’s hold while Xyz booted Russell when trying to save Hart.
Only the approach of show Security caused the champion to release the Spike and leave the ring. Leaving the ring and heading to the back, the two men bowed to each other before taunting the crowd to a torrent of boos.
