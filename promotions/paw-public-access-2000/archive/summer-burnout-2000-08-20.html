Summer Burnout!

20 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 6 —

(The camera opens on the elegantly dressed woman in the Offspring’s mask walking down the hallway. In her light blue dress and heels, the woman snaps back her head, clearing her face of the golden dreadlocks hanging down in her face, giving a clearer view of her ornate mask.
Lifting a clipboard from her side, she shakes her head as she glances at it before pushing a doorway open and stepping inside. Into the room she glides only to find what appears to be the preparations of a party as promotion
President B.J. Perris looks on as caterers set everything up.) Woman in the mask: WHAT THE?!?!?!?! (Hearing the voice, the smiling Perris turns his head.) What is all this? (Moving towards the double tables stacked with food, she lifts a bottle from an opened crate.)
Champagne? Are you crazy? Perris: Not at all ... just a little ... celebration after tonight’s festivities. Tonight’s the big night and I aim to celebrate in fashion!
Woman in the mask: Yeah, well ... we need to talk. Perris: Speak away, my dear ... Woman in the mask: I’ve been looking over the booking sheets and there are a few names on here that I don’t remember seeing from our earlier meetings ...
Ryan Miller? Perris: Right ... Woman in the mask: The Offspring? Perris: Just a little something to ... spice things up ... Woman in the mask: Spice things up? Did you see how much they’re asking per appearance? Perris: Not to worry, my dear ... not to worry ... (Taking her by the arm, Perris leads her out of the room before closing the door.) Woman in the mask: Not to worry, huh ... I think you better start worrying ... (The camera leaves the woman as she walks away down the hall. Moving to another part of the backstage area, Mean Darlene quietly unpacks her things, looking at herself in a mirror before walking off to another room with the camera in tow.
As she moves back and forth in the other room, Jake Morgan can be heard.) Mean Darlene: You're going to destroy Hart today and show everyone that no one should screw with you.
Jake Morgan: Yeah, but what I really want right now is Flint. Let me just get through this match with Hart and I'll take care of Flint soon enough.
Mean Darlene: In time, Jake ... in time. (Darlene walks back into her room with a small makeup case. As she passes the camera, a gasp is heard. The camera pans over and shows Darlene looking into that same mirror but it has something written on it. It says: "Tonight!" and underneath that, a single polaroid picture taped to the mirror.
Darlene pulls the picture off and takes a look at it.) Mean Darlene: Damn ... (Leaving Darlene, the camera focusses in on on Lisa Doyle, who sits quietly on a small sofa, thumbing through a magazine. As she continues to thumb, a small knock on the door is heard.)
Lisa Doyle: Come in ... Drake. (Blushing slightly and smiling, Doyle still thumbs through here, looking down. Two hands begin to massage Doyle's neck.) Nice... Voice: Thanks. (Doyle freezes in her tracks, her magazine begins to tremble at that voice.) Lisa Doyle: D ... Drake? Voice: Nope. (Doyle spins around, ending up eye to eye with Jason Flint, who smiles at her.) Jason Flint: Hey there, hot stuff ... heh ... (Terrified, Doyle backs herself into a corner, fearing for her safety. Flint hovers over her, before kneeling down, placing a finger over his mouth.)
Shhhh ... now we don't want anyone to hear this little conversation we're having ... do we, Lisa?
(Doyle nervously shakes her head, no.) Exactly, because if someone ... oh like Drakee-poo ... would come in ... (Flint picks up a nearby lamp and smashes it onto the ground, right at the feet of Lisa Doyle, who cringes in fear.)
... that would have to happen to him. But its not, not yet. I want you to take a good long look at the "big" picture right now. This fear that you are feeling
... is real. The terro r... is real. I AM REAL. (Flint curls an evil sneer at the cowering Doyle.) I don't want to hurt you, Lisa. Its the farthest thing from my mind. But Drake wants this personal ... Maeda wants this personal. Well, I'm ready to make it personal.
Their affections will turn into infliction, as I inflict the pain ... You tell them to ease back off of me or the next time the body count will be high and the pain will be immence. Go tell them, my white rabbit ... run ...
RUN! (Flint gestures to go past him and Lisa Doyle takes the advantage to hightail it out of there, leaving Flint laughing as large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession.)
.... 5 ... .... 4 ... .... 3 ... .... 2 ... .... 1 ... (Republica's "Ready to Go" starts to play loudly as a single road sign in the shape of Texas is seen. As the camera begins to pull in, a series of flashes of
PAW Superstars are shown. Stars like Skip Westervelt ... Seth ... Trashman ... Drake Russell ... Christian Maeda ... Khan ... Jason Flint and finally the PAW Texas Heavyweight Champion, Jake Morgan, with Mean Darlene. Then as the camera pulls in tight on the sign, two words are seen: SUMMER BURNOUT!, before the sign ignites. With an explosion, the scenes disappear from the screen and are replaced by the logo of Public Access Wrestling 2000 as it moves forward. The song suddenly stops and with a smooth transition, fades out...
With the logo disappearing, the camera zooms in on Aaron Starr standing in front of the PAW logo. Starr, dressed in a sharp tuxedo, smiles smugly at the camera.)
Aaron Starr: The temperture is high, the climate is dry, and we are on the verge of a ... SUMMER BURNOUT! Hello everybody, I'm Aaron Starr, the HOST of
Public Access Wrestling and we're here LIVE at the Denton Civic Center, where shortly this quaint center will explode into a warzone!
With an action packed card set, with a double main event, to boot, it'll be a page in PAW history, that none of us will ever forget!
We've got the newcomer, Shane Silver going head to head with Skip Westervelt ... He calls himself the "Child of Destiny" ... Drake Russell takes on Johnny Green ... The PAW Television strap is on the line as "The Heartbreaker" Christian Maeda puts up the gold against California's favorite son, Seth...
The PAW Texas Heavyweight belt is up for grabs as Jake Morgan defends it against PAW's resident toughest S.O.B. ... Justin Hart ...
And finally a "Loser Leaves Town" match as Jason Flint goes one on one with Perris' hired gun, the man-monster, KHAN! If you lose, you're gone for 90 days! Can Khan put Flint away for good or will the "Electric One" spark a victory and send our promoter packing?
Summer Burnout! ... it’s here ... IT’S NOW! Lets head to the ring! (The camera left the smiling Starr and made it’s way through the small packed arena of several hundred screaming fans. Moving to the ring, the PAW newcomer,
Shane Silver waited for his opponent Skip Westervelt to make his presence known. The lights flickered as “Bitter” by The Union Underground started up as two men walked out from the back. Both men in identical black tights and white boots made their way to the ring ... definitely not Skip Westervelt and tag partner
Chip. As the pair made their way into the ring a loud cheer boomed from the audience and a chant of “WELCOME BACK!” started up. The shorter of the two called for a mic and tapped it several times before speaking into it.
Man: Is this on? (A resounding “YES!” shot back from the fans which brought a smile to the man’s face.) Hello, Denton, Texas ... are you ready to see Public Access Wrestling’s most dynamic tag team tandem?
(Again it was “YES!”.) Then let me introduce myself ... I’m Ryan Miller and this masked man standing to my right is someone you should all be familiar with ... the OFFSPRING!
(The muscular masked man snapped his head back, freeing his face of the tightly wound curls for a brief second before they dropped back into place.)
Audience: WELCOME BACK! ... WELCOME BACK! ... WELCOME BACK! ... (The two men shared a glance as the fans stood and clapped their approval of the returning former Champion.)
Offspring: Time ... It’s been a long time since I left Denton, Texas ... in fact, too long ... but as you can plainly see, that’s all about to change because here I am ...
returned to this little shithole of a promotion ... and you fans who were so quick to forget all about me once I was gone ...
It didn’t matter to you that I carried this promotion on my shoulders for so many months ... that I made this promotion what it was ... and without me, in those early days ... this place would have gone bankrupt ...
How quickly you forgot it was me that took us from the backyard and put us into Supercards here in the Denton Civic Center ... then finally because of me, onto television ...
(Shane Silver was arguing with the referee Peter Daye as Ryan Miller tried to calm him.) But with me gone, you were all ready to jump onto the bandwagon of the few guys who jumped into the void trying to fill my shoes ... your Jason Flints and your
Jake Morgans ... How quickly you forget ... (The fans quieted and a few boos filtered into the ring.) Have no fear ... we’re here to show you ... and everyone in the back the error of your ways ...
(Miller booted Silver in the gut, doubling him up then dropping him with a Double Underhook Powerbomb. Getting up, he pushed aside a protesting Daye while the Offspring climbed to the top of the ropes before diving off and landing a
450 Splash.) You bring us your Television Champion ... send out your Tag Team Champions ... let’s see if your Texas Heavyweight Champion has the balls to step into the ring against me and get some more of what I gave him the first time!
(The fans cheered as Pep Preston stepped out from the back and made his way towards the ring with his half of the Tag belts over his shoulder.)
Pep Preston: I don’t know what this is all about ... but anytime you want a match for these belts ... you just let me know!
(The Offspring laughed as he stepped closer to Preston.) Offspring: Is that so? Anytime? (Both Miller and Spring were looking around.) Only problem seems to be that you’re here alone ... I don’t see your partner anywhere ... (Miller slugged Preston with a Forearm, staggering him before sending across the ring into the far ropes. On the return, Miller caught him up and planted him with a Spinebuster Slam. Hositing Preston into the air, Miller waited while the Offspring dashed across the ring and hit the ropes. Jumping up, he propelled his body backwards and took Pep down with a Spinning Heel Kick.
Kipping back to his feet, Spring grabbed up both his mic and the Tag belt.) Anytime, old man ... anytime ... (After throwing the belt down, Spring spat before he and Miller walked off to the boos of some very unhappy fans. Before disappearing into the back, Miller disgustedly waved them off.
Leaving the ring, the camera found a surprised Aaron Starr in front of the PAW banner.) Aaron Starr: The Offspring returns?!?!?! And has brought a friend along for the ride?!?!?!?! Attacking Pep Preston and putting down the Texas Heavyweight
Champion, Jake Morgan? Has he lost his mind?!?!?!? (Shaking his head, Starr composes himself.) Let’s hear what the “Golden Boy” Seth has to say before he steps into the ring with Christian Maeda and the PAW Television Title ...

— Part 2 of 6 —

(The camera leaves Starr and focusses in on Seth sitting in his locker room, just finishing lacing up his boots. When he does finish, he looks up at the camera.)
Seth: Tonight is a very important night for me. It's the night where I prove, once and for all, that I belong in the upper echelon of wrestlers in this company. I know that a lot of people don't take me seriously. They think that
I'm nothing but a pretty boy from California who's had a couple of freak victories. Well, how does that explain that in my entire PAW career, I have never been pinned for a three-count or made to submit? For all intents and purposes, I am undefeated. Are you undefeated, Justin Hart? No. Are you undefeated, Jake Morgan? No. Who else in the company is undefeated right now? I can't think of anyone off the top of my head.
Tonight, not only do I gain another victory under my belt, but I'll get a new belt under my belt. The TV title will force people to give me the respect that
I deserve. (He stands up and puts on a pair of sunglasses that was sitting next to him.) Now, if you'll excuse me, I've got a title to win. (He exits the room, slamming the door behind him. The camera pans over to Seth's T-Shirt, laying on the bench by where he sat. It then zooms in on the now-famous words printed on the back: "It's Gonna Be Me."
Fading out on the shirt, the camera faded back in on the head of the UNT RVTF Department, Fredrick A. Gibson, leaving his office. After closing the door and locking it, he turned to walk away when he was greeted by a sight that brought out a loud girlish shriek ... standing there was noneother than a grinning
Jason Flint.) Jason Flint: Cool it pops, I’m not going to hurt you ... yet ... (Flint chuckled as he leveled his Singapore cane at the older man.) As long as you do as we discussed last week ... we’re cool ... right? Fredrick A. Gibson: C ... cool ... right ... (Flint tapped Gibson on the shoulder with his cane before turning and walking off. With Flint gone, Gibson’s eyes narrowed as he whispered under his breath.)
We’re cool alright, Flint ... cool ... (Making it’s way back to the ring, the camera found the Champion, Christian Maeda, along with his bodyguard Black Thorne waiting when the "Surf Song" by Fenix TX started up over the loud speaker. A fired up Seth charged out from the back and made his way to the ring to a cascade of boos.
Diving into the ring, he bounced to his feet as his opponent called for a mic. Met with boos, Maeda tapped the Television Title belt around his waist.) Christian Maeda: Hello, Denton! (Crowd cheers.) You dirty, inbred, illiterate pieces of crap! (Crowd boos, Maeda smiles as he turns towards his opponent.) Maeda: Seth, I like you ... (A chant of “He’s a queer!” started up which brought a frown to Maeda’s face. With a disparaging wave, he turned back to Seth.) I think you and I would make a great team, "The Heartbreakers," if you will. But, tonight we go to war for this ... (Maeda holds up the Television Title.) In order to show you, that I have no ill feelings towards you and true to my word ... I’m going to grant your request. No interference ... no Thorney.
Thorney, would you please return to the back? (Thorne stares coldly at Maeda and returns to the back.) Thank you ... Seth, let's do this. And may the best man win! (Maeda points at himself and throws the microphone aside. After checking Maeda’s boots, Daye called for the bell.) *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Seth started to circle the ring when Maeda marched out to the middle and extended his heand. Of course, the fans warned Seth who warily moved in closer.
Extending his own hand, Seth pulled it back as the fans became louder. Waving his arm, Maeda told them to be quiet before turning back to Seth with a smile on his face.
Finally, after more prompting from his opponent, Seth shook Maeda’s hand. Finished, the two started to circle before locking up in the center of the ring. Maeda came out with a Wristlock which he pushed into a Top Wrister. Aware of his surroundings, Seth made for the ropes, bringing on a break. Without a second’s hesitation, Maeda stepped back.
Locking up again, Seth pushed Maeda back into the ropes and sent him across the ring. Bouncing off the ropes on the return, Maeda hopped over Seth who dropped to the mat. Continuing on, Maeda returned as Seth went high into the air with a
Leap Frog. Timing it just right, Seth wrapped his legs around the Champ’s neck and flipped him over with a Hurricanrana. Reaching back, he hooked Maeda’s legs and put his shoulders to the mat for a 2 count.
Back on their feet, Maeda acknowledged Seth’s move with a nod of his head before locking up again. Back and forth they struggled with a series of
Waistlocks, reversing positions several times. After taking an Elbow to the jaw, Seth rolled Maeda up for a 1 count.
With a Whip into the ropes, Seth looked ready to pounce but heistated just a second ... which was what Maeda was looking for as he came in with a
Clothesline. With a Body Slam, Maeda went for a Reverse Chinlock, pulling Seth to his feet before working in a Sleeper. Daye was watching closely as Seth flailed his arms trying to break free. Again, aware of his position, Seth made for the ropes and a clean break. Pointing at his opponent, Maeda nodded his head.
As Seth moved in, Maeda took him off his feet with a Leg Drag. Grabbing Seth’s legs, Maeda was put down with a Boot. Both men kipped to their feet and squared off in the center of the ring with their fists raised.
Locking up, Maeda came out with an Arm Bar into a Hammerlock. Ducking down, Seth reversed it, going behind his opponent. Taking another Elbow to the jaw, Seth dropped down and pulled Maeda down with him thanks to an Inside Cradle. Daye hit the mat quickly and slapped out a near 3 count on the surprised Champion. Playing it safe, Seth kept up the pressure with a Body Slam and Chops to the chest in the corner. With the fans counting out loudly each blow, Maeda blocked on and reversed positions, throwing Chops of his own. A Hip Toss took Seth out of the corner and to the mat with Maeda following up on a Leg Drop. Hooking a leg and using the ropes for added leverage, Maeda had the near fall. Luckily for the challenger, Daye saw both feet on the ropes.
Claiming innocence, Maeda backed away from the action. Whipping Seth into the ropes, Maeda took him down with a Drop Toe Hold and rolled along with him right into a Single Leg Boston Crab. Leaning back, Maeda strained as he tried to end the match but a game Seth was not about to give up. In pain, Seth didn’t give in as Daye asked him again and again.
Letting up on the leg, Maeda backed away and allowed Seth the time to get back to his feet. Circling again, Seth had a noticable limp. Moving in to lock up,
Maeda was taken over with a deep Arm Drag which was held onto as Seth wrenched in an Arm Bar. Pulled back to his feet, Maeda turned to work free but was having no luck.
Hitting the ropes, Seth jumped up and scissored the arm, spinning his body as he took Maeda off his feet to the mat. Back up quickly, he came off the ropes with a Leg Drop, making a fast cover. Maeda kicked out on a 2 count.
Shaking his head, Seth continued to limp around the ring. Coming in, he took a Drop Kick to the knees and went down. On the mat in pain, he took an Elbow Drop and was covered. Daye slapped out a 2 count.
What started slowly was gaining in speed as both Maeda and Seth stepped it up a notch. His hestitation to go to the air was thrown out the window as Seth landed several high risk maneuvers. A perfect Moonsault was followed by the
“Hang Ten”, a Flying Senton Splash. Trying to finish things with his “Surf’s Up” DDT didn’t work out as Maeda countered it, sending him over with a Back Body Drop.
Maeda’s Superkick/Fisherman Suplex combination looked like the end of the match but rather than go for the pin, he took Seth up the ropes for the Maedaplex when Jason Flint, Singapore Cane in hand, slid into the ring. As Maeda worked himself into position, Flint unleashes three quick smacks with the Cane. Losing his balance, Maeda and Seth both hit the mat.
Black Thorne rushes out from the back as Flint spins around looking at the large bodyguard. Smiling, Flint makes for a hasty retreat, with Thorne giving chase.
Pulling himself back to his feet, a confused Seth pauses a bit to look at the fallen Seth then back to the retreating Flint and Thorne.
Black Thorne turning back to see Seth standing over the fallen Christian Maeda, didn’t see Flint come back and spray Thorne in the face with a fire extinguisher until it was too late. Blinded, Thorne got laid out, taking the fire extinguisher to the head. Finished Flint grabbed up his cane making back to the back.
Scratching his head, Seth didn’t want it to end this way. Stepping back, he gave Maeda several seconds to move ...
He should have taken the pin when he had the chance because he was joined in the ring by the Offspring and Ryan Miller. Wrapping his arms around Seth’s waist, Miller put him down with a German Suplex. Bouncing off the ropes, the
Offspring nailed him with a Somersault Leg Drop. With the fans booing, they pulled Christian Maeda up the ropes before putting him back down with a Double Superplex. After pushing Peter Daye around some, they left the ring to a chorus of boos.
With both Seth and Maeda down, Peter Daye called for the bell, bringing this one to an end.

Circling it, he slapped the hands of several fans before climbing up onto the ring apron and entering the ring.

*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Green and Russell met in the center of the ring and went nose to nose. Green’s lips were moving a mile a minute which seemed to make Russell grin.
With a sudden shove, Russell went backwards which brought a smile from Green. Coming back, Russell put Green on the seat of his pants with a hard shove of his own. Back on his feet, Green started to circle as Russell shrugged his shoulders. Raising a hand, Green called for a Test of Strength which his opponent was only too happy to oblige. Locking fingers slowly, they went chest to chest looking for an advantage. WIth all their might, each pushed back against the other neither gaining or losing ground.
Green lifted a leg and dropped it down on Russell’s arm, breaking his grip. Pulling back with his other arm, he leveled Russell with a Short Arm Clothesline. An Elbow Drop missed it’s target as both men rolled back to their feet. Charging in, Russell was pulled over with an Arm Drag but was able to roll right back to his feet. With urging from Russell, Green did the same and rushed in, taken over with a Japanese Arm Drag. With a Forearm, he rocked Green back into the corner before climbing the ropes and raining down blows, The fans were all too happy to show their grasp of the number line as they counted along. A
Monkey Flip brought Green out of the corner which was followed by a Splash off the second rope.
After a DDT, Green rolled from the ring to catch his breath and was followed to the outside. Grabbed by his hair, Green was led around the ring only to have his head slammed into the ringpost. Solidly in control, Russell grabbed Green and Whipped him shoulderfirst into the post. Hitting hard, Green dropped to a knee then took a Boot to the face.
Pulled up, Green was lifted vertically into the air for a Suplex but was able to wiggle free. Grabbing Russell from behind, Green slammed hiim into the hard floor with a Dragon DDT. Back on his feet, Green put the Boots to Russell before pulling him to his feet and Whipping him into the ring railing. As he closed the distance, Green stopped long enough to tell some fans to shut up.
Reaching in, he took a blow to the stomach and staggered back. A Forearm snapped his head back which led into an old fashioned brawl as the two slugged it out around the outside of the ring with referee Peter Daye following the action. Green had Russell in trouble but had the tide turned on him as he slammed his fist into the bell when his punch was blocked. A series of Punches staggered Green who retreated as best he could.

The fans were all on their feet, silent while Dan Glass stood there unsure of what to do. With prompting from Perris, Glass dropped to the mat and slapped out a fast 3 count and called for the bell.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Just like that it was over. Khan rose to his feet and was pelted with a variety of objects as he left the ring. Glass immediately called for help from the back that was answered quickly by the EMT’s and Kimberly Davis who hit the ring.
Perris was beside himself as he and Khan disappeared into the back but the fans were silent as they watched Flint being lifted onto a gurney and rolled from the ring to the back.
On his way out, the silence gave way to a chant ... “JA-SON FLINT!” ... “JA-SON FLINT!” ... “JA-SON FLINT!” ... A rousing cheer rose as Flint weakly lifted his arm as he was wheeled out followed by a teary eyed Davis. Leaving the ring, the camera found a smiling
Drake Russell in his dressing room. Walking, Russell stretched his arms out before looking down at the table before him.)
Drake Russell: What’s this? (Reaching down, he pulled up a small piles of pages and smiled.) The Starr Report ... hmmm ... (Thumbing through it, his smile widened.) Drake Russell ... oh this ought to be good ... Drake Russell ... former Television Champion ... strengths ... the formation of an alliance with promotion President B.J. Perris is perhaps this wrestler’s greatest asset which has garnered him several attempts at title matches which in this reporter’s opinion belongs to more deserving persons ...
(Shaking his head, Russell mutters under his breath as he continues to read.) Not the best technical wrestler despite his own high opinion of his ring skills ... belongs opening cards rather than main events ... Why that ... (The camera breaks away as he dumps the pages into the wastebasket. Back in the pandemonium of the PAW backstage area, the camera finds Jason Flint being rolled past a jubulant Perris with a smiling Fredrick A. Gibson.
As Flint is wheeled past, Perris gives a little good-bye wave and laughs. Suddenly bolting upright, Flint rolls off the gurney and grabs one of hte EMT’s trying to calm him. Spinning the man around, Flint pulls something from the man’s medical “holster” at his side before pushing him out of the way.
Pushing aside the shrieking Gibson, Flint grabs Perris by the collar and shoves him against the wall as everyone freezes.)
Jason Flint: Ninety days ... three very short months ... I just want to give you a little something to remember me by ... something that’s going to be staring you back in the face each morning you wake up ...
Perris: You bast ... (Perris’ voice broke off as Flint jabbed the object into his head, busting him open. As Perris slumped against the wall, Flint went to work ... using the scissors and snipping away strand after strand of Perris' long dark hair. There was a grin on his face as Perris cried out.)
NO!!!!! (As the EMT’s recovered and pulled Flint away, the bloody faced wrestler smiled as Perris slid down the wall looking down at his hair and blood in his hands as the camera faded into credits.)
