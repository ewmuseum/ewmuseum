Rolling back to his feet a second time, Morgan put Flint right back down with a second Suplex ... again getting a near pinfall before a kick out.
Tossing Flint into the ropes, Morgan leveled him with a Boot to the face and followed that up with a Leg Drop before sending Flint into his own corner for
Pep Preston. Pep was in when there was a bit of a commotion coming out from the back as Lisa Doyle was trying hinder Drake Russell on his way to the ring.
Despite having a bloody bandage on his head, Russell was determined to have a go at the match, going so far as to push Lisa Doyle to the ground. From the ring apron, Russell pounded the turnbuckle for a tag.
Seeing his partner, Morgan made the tag and Russell came in to clean house. Big right hands took out both Preston and Flint forcing them from the ring. Both men looked beaten when Russell followed them to the outside by taking a dash across the ring and jumping up onto the top rope. Spinning his body around,
Russell leapt off and took them down with a picture perfect Asai Moonsault.
Not letting up for a second on the tag champs, Russell then Morgan continued to hammer away on the outside of the ring. Grabbing Preston and throwing him into the air, they both stepped away as Pep came down on top of the ring barrier.
Morgan pulled Flint to his feet and Whipped him towards Drake Russell who took him off his feet with a powerful Clothesline. Dragging Flint to his feet,
Morgan did likewise with a Short Arm Clothesline of his own.
After Whipping Flint into the barrier that was pushed well into the first row,
Russell drove Flint into his knee with a Double Underhook Backbreaker while
Morgan pulled a table out from under the ring.
Pushing Flint up onto the apron, Russell climbed back into the ring. Using the ropes, Russell flew over Flint and pulled him over with a Sunset Flip into a
Powerbomb through the table while Jake Morgan cracked a chair over Preston’s head.
Pushing Pep back into the ring, Morgan and Russell used a series of quick tags to work over their opponents. A Spinebuster-Top Rope Splash combo led to another near pinfall which was broken up by Flint diving into the ring and hitting Russell in the back.
Pulled to his feet, Flint was sent for a ride into the ropes but ducked on the return. Following through to the other side of the ring, Flint came back and took Russell down with a Spinning Heel Kick but took a chair to the back of the head thrown in by Morgan as he made the cover. Daye slapped out a 1 count but was up quickly with a warning. This touched things off as all four men spilled into the ring and the fists flew until the fight carried to the outside of the ring.
Fighting it out over the ring barrier and into the crowd, Russell and Morgan regained control of the match. Preston took a Double Leg Slam onto a chair as the fans seperated.
Taking the fight back into the ring. it was Flint and Russell going at it when
Flint struck with an Eye Rake and Boot to the stomach which he followed with a
Face Driver. Morgan hit the ring and drove a Forearm into Flint’s back before a pin attempt.
The fans were booing as Perris and Khan made their way to the ring. Daye had another battle on his hands as all four men were going at inside the ring while
Perris climbed up onto the ring apron.
Khan was busy pulling a table out from under the ring while Perris was trying to get close enough to hit someone as the ref was caught in the middle of
Morgan and Preston. Moving in close enough to the wrestlers, Perris swung a leg up and caught Preston in the head and shoulders which caught Daye’s attention.
Across the ring, Russell was about to send Flint into the far ropes when the move was reversed and Flint sent Russell instead. Making a beeline for Daye,
Russell slammed into him which set off a chain reaction as he in turn slammed intop Perris and knocked him from the apron to the floor to the delight of the fans.
Perris was down as were Preston, Russell and Peter Daye. Morgan turned to a limping Flint when Khan pulled Flint from the ring. After hammering him with a meaty Forearm, he lifted Flint off his feet and put him down with a Chokeslam through the table he set up.
After pulling Drake Russell to his feet, Morgan and his partner dumped Preston with a Double DDT. Rolling Preston over, Morgan dropped for the cover but had no count with Daye still down. Diving into the ring, Perris slapped out the 3 count and the PAW had new Tag Team Champions.
Perris grabbed up the belts and handed them to the new Champions as Khan walked off to the back after staring at Drake Russell. Russell seemed upset as he pulled the belt away from Perris and started to leave the ring while Perris congratulated Morgan who had been joined in the ring by manager Mean Darlene.
On the outside of the ring, a bloody Jason Flint pulled Pep Preston from the ring and started to leave for the back.
The ref pulled himself to his feet and called for the bell. *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Holding his head, he made his way across the ring and grabbed the belt away from Drake Russell then pulled it out of the hands of a surprised Jake Morgan.
Everyone in the studio went silent as Daye left the ring and handed them back to Flint and Preston.
Perris was livid and looked ready to kill Daye but was held back by Morgan and
Mean Darlene. No one was more surprised than Flint and Preston as they stood there waiting for an explanation as well.
After calling for the bell again, Daye called for a mic.) *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Peter Daye: Due to outside interference ... this match has ended in a Double
Disqualification ... Jason Flint and Pep Preston are STILL the PAW Tag Team
Champions!!!!!
(There was a mixed reaction to the decision as Flint and Preston limped off but not before Flint flipped off everyone inside the ring. With a disgusted wave,
Drake Russell left the ring and headed to the back as the cameras left the ringside area.
Emerging in the back, it found Justin Hart leaving the studio and making his way out of the building. Several fans were in the back hoping to catch one of their favorite wrestlers on the way out when there was a whistle that caught
Hart’s attention. Looking around, he was surprised as a garbage can slammed into his head and put him down.
The camera zoomed in on his assailant ... Seth before the cameras faded to black and the credits began to roll.)
