PAW — Card Archive

11.5 Card

5 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The cameras catch up to the current PAW Television Champion, Hiroshima Nagasaki entering the building with manager Lonny Nakamura. As the two continue down the corridor, Nagasaki's pace grows slower and slower as he looks down each and every connecting corrider as iflooking for someone. )
Voice: Looking for someone, Ass? (Nagasaki spins to face a smirking Jason Flint looking right at him. Nagasaki drops his bag and starts to take off his jacket as Flint waves him off.)
Jason Flint: Cool down there, sport ... I said I'd git ya sometime today, it doesn't necessarily mean now, even though I had my chance to bust your head wide open ... so why don't you ... fucking scoot ... Go burn some incense or play keep away with a stone, grasshopper. Shoo Shoo ...
(Flint shoos the irate champion as Nakamura pulls his protege away. A smile comes over Flint's face before the camera fades out and smoothly fades in on a determined looking Drake Russell making his way through the bowels of the PAW television studio. With a big match this evening, Russell looks focussed as he passes production people until he hears his name.)
Voice: Drake! Drake! Hold up! (Turning, he sees Paco of the Heartbreakers in a blue sequined outfit moving towards him ... blue suspenders on his bare chest into blue sequined tights with an emphasis on tight, down to his little blue booties. Stifling a chuckle,
Russell shakes his head as the wrestler approaches.)
Paco: You got a minute? (All the answer Paco gets is a stern look.) I was wondering if you might want to hook up with the most dynamic tag team here in the PAW ... you see, the Heartbreakers are looking for a few good men ...
Whoa momma, I’m on fire! (Russell laughs as Paco breaks into his “Johnny Bravo” and gives a couple of hip gyrations before returning to normal ... normal for a man in a tight fitting blue sequined outfit.)
Seth and I thought you’d be perfect ... Drake Russell: Didn’t Seth quit? Paco: Uhm ... well ... uh ... not really ... (Struggling for words, Paco stops suddenly as his gaze gets riveted somewhere else. Looking in the same direction, both the camera and Drake Russell see what has caught his attention ... puffs of smoke coming out from under a door just down the hall.
With the camera returning, Paco slaps Russell on the shoulder and returns to his Johnny Bravo schtick.)
Nice talking to ya ... hoo ha ... gotta run! (With Paco dashing off the camera leaves the head shaking Russell and follows the odd wrestler as he bolts down the hall in his all too tight tights.
Stopping in front of the door marked as “Graceland, Home of the Heartbreakers”,
Paco pushes the door open where the camera focusses in on a pile of burning sequined outfits in the middle of the floor.
Scanning the room, he spies the six foot tall standing Elvis holding a sign that reads in big black block letters: I QUIT!
The camera comes to focus in on a lone Jake Morgan walking quickly through the halls of the studio. Passing several people, he reaches out an arm and grabs a production person, pulling him forward and into his face.)
Jake Morgan: Have you seen Darlene? (The young man with a scared look on his face quickly shakes his head. Not the answer he was looking for, Morgan shoves the man away, grabbing another as he passes.)
Have you seen Darlene? (Getting the same result, Morgan pushes the man away before storming off. Moving to the inside of an office, the camera zooms in on the mysterious mask wearing owner of the promotion. Sitting behind her desk, the woman looks down at the yellow, red and white posters adorning the studio courtesy of Hiroshima
Nagasaki and his manager.
Shaking her head in disgust, she looks up at Aaron Starr, his face heavily bandaged on the left side, sitting on the couch in front of the desk. With a knock at the door, she averts her attention towards the entrance. The doors opens slightly as the head of a familiar Kimberly Davis pops in.)
Kimberly Davis: He’s here ... (With a nod of her head, the door opens and in walks the manager of Television
Champion, Nagasaki Hiroshima. Dressed in a sharp suit, Lonny Nakamura steps inside and after a slight bow puts his briefcase down. Looking back at Starr,
Nakamura gives a slight bow of his head before having a seat.)
Woman in the Offspring mask: First it’s Hart, now it’s your man, Nagasaki ... (Nakamura smiles as he nods his head.) As the new owner of this promotion, I can’t allow this kind of behavior to continue ... not with the NWC decision so close and with new talent coming in
... I can’t allow this type of behavior towards my employees ... what goes on inside the ring is one thing but this is totally unacceptable ...
(With a serious look upon his face, Nakamura nods his understanding.) Just as I did with Hart, I was prepared to do the same with Hiro but under the circumstances ...
Lonny Nakamura: Circumstances? Woman in the Offspring mask: Yes, Aaron has decided not to press charges ... and has asked that no diciplinary action more than a fine be levied ... (Nakamura nods his head.) But I feel that a very stiff fine is in order so this will not happen again ... (Nakamura calmly places his briefcase onto the desk and opens it. Pulling out a checkbook, Nakamura starts writing.)
I think five thousand dollars will make Hiro think twice. (Without batting an eye, Nakamura starts writing when he suddenly stops and tears the check out, crumpling it into a little ball before writing again.
Finished, he pulls the check out and hands it to the woman.) Lonny Nakamura: That’s five thousand for the fine ... and five thousand for the next time when one of your announcers wants to unjustly badmouth your one true
Champion who carries this promotion on his back ...
Woman in the Offspring mask: And the other five thousand? Lonny Nakamura: That’s for the new banners and posters ... (Calmly closing his case, he stands up and takes a bow before leaving the room. As the door closes behind him, the camera returns to an angered Aaron Starr sitting still with his balled fist waving.)
Aaron Starr: Your time’s coming ... (The camera fades on him shaking his head as large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession.)
.... 5 ... .... 4 ... .... 3 ... .... 2 ... .... 1 ... (White Zombie's "More Human than Human" begins as flashes of Hiroshima Nagasaki in action are shown. Scene after scene quickly flash by of past battles ...
Running his thumb across his throat before a limp Drake Russell is planted into the canvas with a Nagasaki Driver ...
Following a Flying Knee straight to an incoming Seth's face, Nagasaki locks on the “Unconditional Surrender” for a win ...
The “Street Soldier” Justin Hart takes a Double Thrust to the Throat and with a
Snap Suplex, put into a pinning predicament ...
Pep Preston is flat on his back as Nagasaki leaps from the top turnbuckle and lands the “H-Bomb” ...
In slow motion, Aaron Starr turns right into a ball of fire thrown into his face and finally the PAW Television Champion standing in the middle of the ring, the belt raised over his head.)
After an explosion, the scene fades to black and the PAW logo disappears before fading back in and panning the fans and their signs held aloft such as "Paco is my hip gyrating Hero!!", "East BEATS West!" and "15 Days!".
After making it's way through the boisterious crowd of some 50 or so, the camera settles in on a stern Jason Flint,standing in front of the newly placed
Hiroshima Nagasaki PAW banner. Flint motions to the camera to wait one second as he turns and tears down the banner.)
Jason Flint: Hello everyone, Jason Flint here hosting without my esteemed collegue Aaron Starr to present to you ... Public Access Wrestling! Last week a war spilled from the ring to the back as Drake Russell fended off both the
Trashman and Jake Morgan as these men sought to destroy each other. Later in the day Russell had a match for the Television Title but since his battles with
Trashy and Morgan he ... was ... unable ... waitaminute who writes this crap?
(Flint walks off camera and returns with the cue cards, tossing them to the side) Like I would give props to Russell?!! Russell came up short because ... One ... He's a puss! Two ... Jake Morgan wrapped a chair around his head during the match! And three ... He's a puss. But anyway ... We've got two great matches up for ya, the first, Drake Russell takes on the Trashman in what would be considered practically a no holds barred match and the Television Champ goes up against Paco and all that hip gyrating
...
(A loud gong and the Kodo drums sounded bringing a sigh and a disgusted look from Flint as the PAW Television Champion Hiroshima Nagasaki, accompanied by his manager Lonny Nakamura, make their way towards Flint.
Welcomed with a chorus of boos from the fans, Nagasaki takes a microphone from
Nakamura and waits for the crowd noise to die down. The disgusted look on
Nagasaki's face as his manager attempts to quiet the crowd prompts the fans to begin a chant of "WE WANT FLINT!". Several seconds pass as Flint helps rile up the crowd. Turning toward Flint, Nagasaki begins to talk over the crowd who finally quiet down so they can hear what is being said.)
Nagasaki: ... changes in the look of PAW. Nakamura-san and I have taken it upon ourselves to better Public Access Wrestling. Already several aesthetically pleasing decorations have replaced the awful logos of the past.
(While Nakamura applauds the champions words, the crowd lets loose with a torrent of jeers. Looking around at the loud crowd, many of which are on their feet shouting their displeasure, Nagasaki holds up his hand for quiet.)
Nagasaki: I come before you stupid Americans to promise that the H-Bomb has decided to carry Public Access Wrestling to the next level. As the most honorable, talented and dedicated man in PAW, I realize that for PAW to survive
I, Hiroshima Nagasaki, must carry the organization into the future until that time that it can attract other talented JAPANESE wrestlers.
(Again the crowd unleashes its disapproval while Nakamura shakes his head in agreement.) Let me assure you, Nakamura-san has many more changes planned that are just around the corner.
(Looking directly at Flint) And I think you will really enjoy them. No, no there is no reason to thank me. As the top attraction in Pathetic American Wrestling, it is my duty to turn this organization into something better. And rest assured what the H-Bomb wants, the H-Bomb gets.
Changes are coming, even if I have to BURN down all of PAW and everyone in it to do it. (Setting down the microphone, Nagasaki throws a fireball into the air before walking to the back with Nakamura. Flint takes a step back shaking his head as the camera settles in on the puff of smoke rising before returning to the host of the show.)
Jason Flint: We’ll be right back for our first match! (The camera fades away into the back on Jake Morgan still walking in the back looking around. Not finding who he’s looking for, he grabs a certain green haired passer-by. Getting him by the arm, Morgan tugs him to attention.)
Jake Morgan: Have you seen Darlene? (The green haired man looks startled by the action for a second as he looks around before looking Jake right in the eye as he pulls away.)
Green haired man: What do I look like? Her press secretary? (Overcome with rage, Jake looks like he’s about to hit the young man when the green haired man suddenly runs off. With no one around to take his anger out on, Morgan slams his fist into the wall.
With a smooth cut, the camera focusses in on Pep Preston standing in the hallway with Skip Westervelt and Emilio Ramirez. The men are talking when an approaching Lonny Nakamura walks up.
Turning towards Preston, Nakamura nods his head in a quick bow which sends the other two walking off.)
Skip Westervelt: See you later, Pep ... (As the two walk off, Preston turns to the smiling Nakamura.) Lonny Nakamura: You are a former Tag Team Champion, no? Pep Preston: Right, held the title for a short time with Jason ... (Nakamura rubs his hands together before putting an arm around the wrestler and walking him off.)
Lonny Nakamura: I have a little proposition for you ... (As they discuss business, the camera fades back into the ring where referee
Peter Daye is checking over the boots of Drake Russell. There is a loud cheer as his opponent for the evening, the Trashman, is announced and emerges from the back and makes his way into the ring. Sliding in, he is immediately attacked by Drake Russell who pushes the ref out of the way.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Boots to the head of Trash puts Russell on the offensive. With the ref moving in to break it up, Russell obliges as he steps back. Circling around Trash as he tries to pick himself up, Russell charges in with a Drop Kick to the face that hits solidly.
A quick cover results in a 1 count from the ref. On the return from a Whip into the ropes, Trash is sent over with a Back Body Drop and takes a follow-up Elbow to the chest. A Knee Drop completes the string of moves that leads into another pinning predicament and this time a 2 count before Trash’s shoulder is off the mat.
A few words between Daye and Russell gives Trash a few precious seconds to catch his breath which he makes the most of. With Russell moving in again for more, Trash lashes out with a Low Blow. Staggered, Russell is now on the recieving end as Trash flies over him into a Sunset Flip. He gets a near pinfall on the move as Russell barely kicks out.
On their feet, Trash sends him into the ropes and takes him down with a Depp
Arm Drag. Not letting go of the arm, Trash twists into an Arm Bar then works the wrist, pulling down hard on it.
Missing with a Back Elbow, Russell is taken to the mat with a Drop Toe Hold. With a Float Over, Trash pulls Drake up with a Front Chancre then spins his body into a Swinging Neck Breaker. Bouncing off the ropes, Trash comes in with a Twisting Leg Drop and makes the cover but having carried his man too close to the ropes gets the pin attempt broken up as Daye notices a leg draped across the bottom rope.
On their feet and circling, the pair lock up in the center of the ring. Russell gets the advantage following a Forearm Uppercut. Grabbing an arm, Russell spins around to send him into the ropes but has the move reversed and is sent instead. On the return, he drops a shoulder and knocks Trash to the mat.
With a Gutwrench, Russell lifts Trash off the mat before sitting down into a
Facefirst Powerbomb. Bouncing off the mat, Trash rolls to the safety of the outside but is given no time as Russell follows.
Hammering Forearms to the back stagger Trash around the ring as he’s followed by a relentless Drake Russell. Trashman goes down after being slammed into the corner post. After delivering several Stomps to the back, Russell pull a table out from under the ring. Setting it up on the outside, Russell pulls Trash to his feet, slamming his head down into it.
The fans are on their feet cheering as Trash takes a Body Slam onto the hard floor and an Elbow Drop. Peter Daye is in trying to get the match back into the ring which brings an argument from Russell. Out on his feet, Trash uses the table and the ring apron to pull himself back to his feet.
Russell is Drop Kicked into the ref from behind. With both men down, Trash has the time to retrieve his Singapore Cane from around the ring. Returning to
Russell, Trash wraps it around the man’s neck and drops backwards into the ring barrier with a Side Russian Leg Sweep. Both men hit the barrier hard and are down as Daye starts a count on them.
Thc cheering fans count along with the ref until Trash gets back to his feet first. Using the end of the cane, Trash drives it into the sternum of Drake
Russell doubling him over then taking several whacks across the back with the weapon. Daye gets involved trying to pull the weapon away but to no avail.
Waiting on the apron until Russell stands up, Trash leaps off and lands across his opponent’s shoulders. Spinning his body while dropping backwards, Trash throws Russell over with a Hurricanrana.
After tossing Russell back into the ring, Trash Whipped him across the ring only to catch a Spinning Heel Kick as he returned. Dropping down for the pin,
Daye slapped out a 2 count for the near fall.
Slamming Trash hard into a corner, Russell opened up with Reverse Knife Hand
Chops. Grabbing Russell and reversing positions, Trashman fired back with Chops of his own. He kept popping Right Hands into Russell’s chest until a Knee Lift caught him.
Staggering backwards, Trash caught a Front Kick and nearly went down. Reaching in for the Double Underhook, Russell almost had him set up but a counter into an Arm Bar ended that. Making his way into the ropes, Russell waited for Daye to call for a break.
As the Trashman stepped back with his hands raised, Russell landed a Super Kick to the jaw. With Trash hitting the mat, Russell darts across the ring, leaping up onto the top rope. Throwing his body backwards, he landed a high arcing
Moonsault. Hooking a leg, he rolled Trash up for the win with Daye slapping out a 3 count.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (The fans were on their feet cheering as Russell bounces back to his feet. Spinning around, he sees the reason for their cheering ... Jake Morgan standing in the center of the ring.
Lashing out with a boot to the gut, Drake Russell was doubled over for a brief second. Wrapping an arm around his neck, Jake slaps him on the back as he fell backwards, driving Russell facefirst into the canvas with a heavy thud.
Rolling back to his feet, Jake wasn’t quite finished yet as he pulls Russell up across his shoulders. Holding him there for several seconds, Jake spins Russell out from his body as he sits down into a Powerbomb. With Russell flat on his back and looking up at the ceiling, a smiling Jake looks out at the fans.
Rising to his feet, Jake lifts Russell up off the canvas. Wrapping his arms around Russell’s waist, Jake lifts him off his feet and spins into a
Spinebuster Bomb that shakes the ring. A twitching Drake Russell was in no shape to defend himself as Jake lifts him off of the canvas one more time. On his back in a Crucifix, Jake runs across the ring. As he nears the ropes, he lets go of Russell’s body which shot forward out of the ring and goes crashing down through the table that was set up earlier.
Looking down at his handiwork, Morgan can only smile at the limp Russell laying amongst the fragments of the demolished table as Security hits the ring. Morgan backs away and helps the Trashman from the ring.
The camera follows the pair into the back as they make their way towards the dressing rooms. As the continue on their way, the cheering of the fans dies away as they go deeper into the studio bowels.)
Jake Morgan: If I could only get this ringing in my ears to stop ... (The cameras fade out on the pair and into Jason Flint, who is in a semi-private conversation with the new boss of PAW, the masked woman. Flint seems to be pleading his case.)
Jason Flint: Come on .. there's only 15 days left, I've been a good boy, let me back in. I'm beggin ya ... PLEASE!
Woman in the Offspring Mask: I would really like to, Jason, but a deal is a deal. You lost to Khan and have to sit your full suspension.
Jason Flint: How fair is that?! (Flint smiles jokingly.) I mean, Perris ... Khan .. those two are GONE, why punish me further when the two people that I offended aren't even here? Please ... com'n!
Please ... please. (Flint puts his two hands together and gives his best puppy dog eyes pout.) Woman in the Offspring Mask: As cute as your begging is, Jason, no. You'll just have to deal with it for a couple of more weeks.
Jason Flint: Dammit! Fair Enough ... had to try. (The camera heads back to the ring where Peter Daye and Chip await the arrival of the Television Champion. The gong and Kodo drums mark his arrival as he steps out out the back by himself. Making his way slowly to the ring, the fans give him a heavy dose of booing which brings a smile to his face.
Walking by the fans, he grabs away a sign with the number of days in Flint’s exile and tears it in half which inflames the crowd promoting Security to move in. After laughing maniacally, Nagasaki climbs into the ring and dips between the ropes.
The referee takes the Television belt away from Nagasaki and shows it to the challenger before calling for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** The overweight Chip, with instructions from Skip Westervelt patrolling the outside of the ring, charges in. Dropping to a knee, Nagasaki drives his hand into the soft and fleshy folds of Chip’s belly, stopping him in his tracks.
Rising quickly, a Double Thrust to the throat staggers Chip and a Drop Kick takes him down to the mat. Circling slowly, Nagasaki waits for the fat man to get back to his feet while also keeping an eye on Chip’s Lone Wolves partner,
Skip.
Back to his feet, Chip takes a heavy Kick to the side. A second one is caught as Chip traps the leg against his side. His smile quickly disappears as
Nagasaki jumps up and hits him in the back of the head with an Enzuigiri. Both men are down on the mat before Nagasaki kips to his feet. Rolling Chip over, the Champion makes a cover and gets a 2 count before Daye breaks up the pin when he sees Chip’s foot on the bottom rope. Walking away quickly is Skip.
Complaining to the ref of Skip’s interference does no good other than to draw boos from the crowd. Pulling Chip to the center of the ring, Nagasaki grabs both of his opponent’s feet and falls backwards, sending him Slingshoting towards the corner. Chip eats a face full of top turnbuckle.
Staggering back out of corner, Chip is scooped up onto Nagasaki’s shoulders and spun. Around and around the Champ spins until Chip flies off with Nagasaki having a firm grip on an arm. The big man hits the mat and is immediately in an
Arm Bar.
Trying to make his way to the ropes, Chip is pulled back each time with a sharp yank on the arm. Nagasaki looks to be enjoying the pain inflicted onto his opponent as Daye asks for a submission. Each time, Chip shakes his head. On the outside of the ring, Skip Westervelt starts pounding the ring apron and gets the fans to clap and stomp their feet.
With newfound energy, Chip smashes back with an Elbow, catching Nagasaki flush on the chin. A second one hits it’s target as well causing Nagasaki to loosen his grip before the third one lands and forces him to break his hold.
Shaking his deadened arm, Chip looks angry as he moves in. A “Belly Buck” knocks Nagasaki back into a corner. Taking a few steps backwards, Chip hits on the gas as he comes comes flying across the ring. Jumping into the air, he looks to crush Nagasaki in the corner but the Champion steps out of the way at the last second.
Slamming into the corner, Chip takes a couple of steps out of the corner and falls flat on his back. Grabbing the ropes, Nagasaki uses them to propel himself into the air and comes Splashing down on top of the obese Chip. With a week cover, Daye slaps the mat twice before Nagasaki is hurled off.
Jumping onto the ring apron, Skip Westervelt starts complaining about pulling hair but is waved off by the ref.
Grabbing an arm, Nagasaki pulls Chip to his feet and sends him across the ring. Bouncing off the far ropes, Chip returns and and is taken down by a Jumping
Knee that catches him high on the chest.
Mounting the second rope, Nagasaki comes off with a Fist Drop that impacts solidly. A shout goes out from the fans as Nagasaki drapes himself over the prone Chip.
From out of the back, Jason Flint, with chair in hand, climbs the corner ropes and comes crashing down with an Arabian Facebuster ... only Nagasaki rolled out of the way at the last second unknown to his attacker. Rolling to his back,
Nagasaki laid there as Flint gathered up his chair. Chants of "FLINT!" rose up as he stood over the downed television champion, pointing and yelling at him.
Grabbing a mic, Flint pointed to the unmoving Nagasaki. Jason Flint: You know why it was THIS FUCKING EASY?! Flint turns as someone from the crowd said something. That's right! Just like that guy said ... CAUSE HE'S A BITCH! The cameras pan over to the bunch of fans who start to chant "He's a Bitch!" as
Flint throws down the mic and stands there looking at Nagasaki. Peter Daye moves in to argue with Flint who raises the chair in his hand and ends any discussion.
As the chanting continues, they are joined by another person who slids into the ring. Wearing a black leather jacket with a symbol of the “Rising Sun” on the back, the man positioned himself behind Flint. Running forward, he leaps into the air and grabs Flint around the head and uses his weight and momentum to drive the former Champion’s head into the mat with a Bulldog.
Flint’s assailant roes back to his feet and turned towards the fans as he threatened the ref ... it was Pep Preston!
The fans started booing as Nagasaki rose to his feet and started laughing at the downed Flint. Both men put the Boots to the announcer which drew the ire of the fans as they pelted the ring with cups and other items.
Pulling Flint to his feet, they wrapped him up in the ropes. Daye moved in to help Flint get free but he was pushed aside. Nagasaki moved right up to Flint’s face and slapped him hard. The blow resulting in loudest booing of the evening.
Skip Westervelt’s intervention was short lived and he gets hammered by Preston and goes falling to the outside of the ring.
Nagasaki slaps Flint on the face several more times to bring him around before pulling Chip to his feet. Standing behind his opponent, Nagasaki locks on the
"Unconditional Surrender". Chip’s arms flail away before going limp, the entire time Nagasaki is laughing. As Daye calls for the bell, Nagasaki drops the obese man and grabs up his Television Title.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Making his way back to Flint, Nagasaki holds the belt in front of his face, taunting him. With the booing growing louder and more objects being thrown into the ring, Pep
Preston leads Hiroshima Nagasaki away from the ring and to the back. As they disappear, the camera moves back to the struggling Flint, his arms entwined by the ropes, his eyes burning with hatred. With Daye helping to free him, the camera makes it’s way to the back where Jake Morgan is still on the prowl.
Stopping people as they go by, he has only one question for each of them ... “Have you seen Darlene?”. Each time, he gets the same answer or a shake of the head, leaving him muttering under his breath. Continuing his search, he heads down a flight of stairs and passes several doors before he stops in front of one ... Hiroshima's dressing room.
Without knocking he shoves the door open and finds Darlene with Lonny Nakamura sitting at a table looking at a stack of papers. Startled, their eyes go wide with surprise at the sudden intrusion which an angry look Morgan’s face.
Shaking his head in disgust, Jake turns to storm off as Darlene jumps out of her chair, ready to follow.)
Mean Darlene: Jake wait, it's not what you think ... (Jake doesn't wait as he slams the door hard. A loud cracking noise can be heard. Darlene rushes to the door but finds it jammed. She pounds her fist on the door. On the outside, Jake storms away when Paco walks up and tries to speak to him.)
Paco: Hola amigo ... how would you like to ... (In no mood for small talk, Jake throws him up against the wall and continues on.) Voice: HEY! Got a minute for me? (Hearing the voice, Jake stops and turns to see Drake Russell with Singapore
Cane in hand. Charging forward, Russell unloads as he strikes Morgan several times across the head. Taken by surprise, Jake stumbles backwards, sliding against the wall and smearing it with his blood, having been busted open. With blood running down into his face, Jake slumps to the floor, looking up at his assailant as the credits begin to roll.
