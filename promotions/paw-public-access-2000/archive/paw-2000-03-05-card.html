3.5 Card

5 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera opens on a small darkened hole with light pouring from the other side. Moving in closer and peeking through the hole like some cheap voyeur, Neo
Draven and his lovely Sasha can be seen on the other side locked in a passionate embrace within a small room most likely serving as their dressing room.
After breaking off the long kiss, Neo Draven says something to Sasha which causes her to look disappointed before he leaves the room. Shaking her head in disappointment, she hears her lover shout out.)
<Neo Draven> CHIP!!!!! (On the other side of the wall watching this all unfold, a scurrying is heard as the watcher moves away from his vantage point. His voice is heard after hearing Neo Draven's shouts.)
<Voice> Uh oh ... (The camera moves away quickly when large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession.)
... 5 ... ... 4 ... ... 3 ... ... 2 ... ... 1 ... (Rob Zombie's "More Human than Human" starts to play loudly as clips of Public
Access Wrestling 2000's Superstars in action passes ... Skip Westervelt ...
Justin Hart ... Jake Morgan ... Neo Draven and Sasha ... Brian Cage ... Johnny
Green and finally, the Texas Heavyweight Champion, Jason Flint, standing in the center of the ring lifting his title belt skyward ...
With an explosion, the scenes disappear from the screen and are replaced by the logo of Public Access Wrestling 2000 as it moves forward, filling the screen.
The song suddenly stops and with a smooth transition, fades out ... With a just as smooth fade in, the camera pans the wrestling ring surrounded by a vocal but small group of fans before passing over the small studio set.
Standing off to the side, in front of the PAW Banner, host Aaron Starr smiles into the camera.)
<Aaron Starr> Good evening and welcome once again to Public Access Wrestling
2000! Joining me this evening is PAW President B.J. Perris ...
(Turning towards Perris.) I trust you are well after Thursday's explosive match between Jake Morgan and
Justin Hart when they took on Jason Flint's State Club Boyz?
<Perris> I'm doing fine Aaron, just fine ... I just wonder if after tonight's big 6-man tag team main event if Flint and his cohorts can say the same ...
<Aaron Starr> Indeed ... With reputations and egos at stake in such a big match as well as past histories ... just who is going to referee this match?
<Perris> With Jason Flint's past history with head referee Peter Daye ... we have a problem, even if he wasn't injured in a brutal attack just last week.
<Aaron Starr> By the same token, I guess it's safe to say that Dan Glass, the
"Electric Referee" won't be involved either ...
<Perris> You'd be correct ... which leaves only one very obvious choice to call this one ... and call it fairly ...
(Perris smiles as he pats himself on the chest.) That would be me ... <Aaron Starr> Even after the events of Thursday, you can get into that ring and call a fair match? Not that anyone would doubt your word but surely no one would fault you for swinging the match in favor of at least Jake Morgan ... who was able to bury all differences with you and help to take care of your little problem with 13 ...
<Perris> Aaron ... fair ... right down the middle ... that's how I'm going to call it! Before leaving the promotion, some things said by our former Texas
Heavyweight Champion about our tainted belt really hit home with me ...
<Aaron Starr> You're referring to the "Extreme Luchadore", the Offspring ... <Perris> Right ... his words really struck home about what our top belt means
... and after hearing his comments ... I'm going to do all in my power to see that we get great matches that are decided by the two combatants inside the squared circle and not by the guys in the striped shirts ... tonight, with six men inside the ring all trying to kill each other, I'm going to see that no one else gets involved!
<Aaron Starr> And there you have it folks ... from the head man himself! Now, before we get onto our first match, we're going to the back where my esteemed collegue is standing by with Skip Westervelt!
(Aaron cocks his head as puts a hand over his ear.) Lisa? Can you hear me? (The camera leaves Aaron Starr and B.J. Perris in the studio area for the back where Lisa Doyle is standing with a pissed off looking Skip Westervelt.)
<Lisa Doyle> Yes, Aaron ... I can hear you ... (Doyle smiles as she gets her cue that they're on.) I am standing with the former Television Champion, Skip Westervelt who joins
Jason Flint and Johnny Green, two members of Mean Team 2000.
(Turning towards Westervelt, she pushes a mic into his face. Rolling his eyes, with his hands on his hips, he shoots her a look.)
Skip ... your thoughts on tonight's match as a partner on MT2K? <Skip Westervelt> Look ... I don't understand exactly what's going on here ... last week, I'm brought in to stand side by side with Jason Flint as his partner and the next thing I know is that I'm being rolled up by his ex-partner with their own referee slapping out the three count ... and across the ring, Flint is pinning Hart ...
You want to explain to me how it is that Flint and Green are declared the new tag champs? I don't get it.
<Lisa Doyle> And tonight's match? <Skip Westervelt> What about it? What can I tell you about it? I don't understand this either? One week, I'm being screwed over by my so-called partner and the next week, I'm expected to stand with him and the guy who took me out last week ... all like nothing's happened?
(Skip shakes his head in disgust.) I don't get it ... (Westervelt turns and walks off leaving Lisa Doyle standing alone.) <Lisa Doyle> And there you have it Aaron ... a very confused Skip Westervelt
... (Moving back to the ring area, the camera found Aaron Starr inside the ring along with Pep Preston and referee Dan Glass.)
<Aaron Starr> And his opponent ... standing 6'4" and weighing in at 240 pounds
... here all the way from Perfection, California ... here is Drake Russell!!!!
(Well muscled, the black haired man made his way to the ring, getting a few cheers and whistles as he climbed into the ring and made his way to the top rope, raising his arms in the process. Jumping down, he pulled off his black leather pants and t-shirt. Tossing them out of the ring, he was ready to go in his boots and little tights.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Pep initiated Russell into the PAW with two big Arm Drags that sent him across the ring. Rolling back to his feet, Russell smiled as he pointed over to
Preston. Locking up in the center of the ring, Russell gained control with a
Top Wristlock.
Pushing forward, Pep put Russell down, tripping him with a leg. Dropping down,
Pep got a 1 count before he was tossed off. Some of the fans were shouting out
Pep's name as his small fanbase began to grow larger over the past few weeks.
After being Whipped into the ropes, Russell came off and leveled Preston with a
Clothesline that spun the man around. Pulling Pep to his feet, Russell dropped him with a quick DDT, getting a near pinfall out if it.
Having a few words with the ref over what he thought was a slow count, Russell went back to work. Pushing Pep back into the ropes, Russell pulled him forward, driving a Knee into his midsection. Doubled over, Pep was caught up and spun around in a Double Underhook Back Breaker.
Leaving the ring area, Drake Russell took a little stroll around the ring. When followed to the outside by the ref, Russell blew him off when asked to get back into the match. Only when Russell started walking off the set after giving a disgusted wave at his still downed opponent, did Dan Glass get involved as he started a count, threatening to count him out.
Making his way towards the ref, Russell gave him a threatening glare before entering the ring to finish the job he had started. Pulling Pep to his feet,
Russell Whipped him intothe far ropes and caught him on the return with a
Spinning Back Kick that sent him sprawling to the outside of the ring. Inside the ring, Russell had a smirk on his face.
Waiting for Pep to get back to his feet, Russell joined him on the outside with a Diving Shoulder Butt that flattened Preston and brought a collective gasp from the fans. Pep was pulled roughly to his feet then sent backfirst into the ring barrier. Slumped against it, Pep took several Reverse Knifehands that popped loudly off his chest. A Belly-to-Belly Suplex put Pep down hard onto the floor where Russell followed with a Leg Drop Across the throat.
Throwing Pep back into the ring, Russell came back in with a Slingshot Clothesline before climbing the ropes and putting the match to bed with a high arcing Top Rope Moonsault.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Rolling from the ring, Drake Russell flexed his muscles before walking off to the back. In his first match, Drake Russell had an impresive PAW debut.
The camera left the ring where the ref was helping Pep Preston to his feet and heading to somewhere in the back coming upon Skip Westervelt talking to the
"Neo-Goth" Chip.) <Skip Westervelt> What the Hell is all this crap? (Westervelt gestured to Chip's jet black shiny hair and the whitened face he was now sporting. Chip got a confused look on his face as he pulled two little tufts of hair up into what appeared to be little horns, bringing a groan from
Skip.)
<Chip> What? <Skip Westervelt> Don't you think this has gone on long enough? First it was the Texas Terror fixation and now it's this ...
(Chip's partner, Neo Draven walked into the conversation getting into Westervelt's face and pointing.) <Neo Draven> Do not tread where the dead things dwell ... (With his cryptic warning, Neo Draven walked off followed by Chip who swung his cap in front of his face, covering the lower half. With them gone, Skip could only shug his shoulders and shout out after the them.)
<Skip Westervelt> Who's Fred Fomain? (Leaving Skip, the camera followed Neo Draven and Chip to the ring where Emilio
Ramirez was waiting inside. Handing his long black coat to Chip, Draven turned his attention towards his opponent when Dan Glass called for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Bouncing off the ropes, Draven caught Ramirez with a Leg Lariat followed up by
Corkscrew Elbow off the ropes. A fast 1 count was all he got before a kick out.
Fast paced as expected, Ramirez caught Neo with a Back Elbow and a Russian Leg
Sweep. Hitting the ropes, he came off with a Somersault Senton, landing him flat on his back as Neo rolled out of the way.
Hitting the ropes himself, Neo dropped dpwn with a Leg Drop that hit nothing but canvas before he took a Dropkick to the face by the slippery Ramirez.
Whipping Ramirez into the corner, Neo followed in with a Splash. Allowing
Ramirez to fall on his face, Draven climbed the ropes and came off with a
Rolling Knee Drop. Bouncing back to his feet, Draven nailed a Gut Wrench Suplex in the center of the ring before pulling Ramirez to the ring apron. With his opponent on the apron, Neo came over the top with a Sunset Flip into Powerbomb on the floor.
Slow to get back to his feet, Neo pushed the ring barrier back as security moved everyone out of the way. Leaping up onto the barrier, he came off with a
Corkscrew Leg Drop that landed squarely.
Neo was getting a lecture from the ref when Chip put Ramirez across his back and dropped him onto the hard floor. Back on his feet, he hissed at the fans with his cape pulled in front of his face.
Throwing Ramirez over the barrier, Neo went back up onto the ring apron. Leaping off, he landed a leg over his opponent's head and neck, driving him down into the barrier. Chants of "EXTREME!" went up as Neo and Chip Whipped
Ramirez back into the ring apron before rolling him back into the ring.
Whipped into the ropes, Ramirez was sent flying with a Flying Head Scissor
Takedown and covered. Glass slapped out a 2 count. Neo caught a Punch to the head before dropped onto mat with a Knee Lift. Ramirez went for the pin following a Slingshot Leg Drop, getting a 1 count.
Charging at each other, they went down from a Double Clothesline. Chip pulled
Neo to the outside. The fans were cheering as a groggy Neo and Chip were hit with a Plancha and knocked to the floor. All three men landed hard on the floor and were down as Dan Glass checked on them.
Chip and Glass were arguing as Neo was pulled to his feet. Ramirez spun around to Whip Neo into the ring barrier but was reversed and sent flying instead.
Pulled to his feet and Body Slammed down on top of the ring steps, Ramirez took a chair to the face as Neo Draven sprang from the apron.
Taking the match back into the ring, Neo Draven hit with several high risk moves off the ropes before having Chip pull a table out from under the ring.
Setting it up, Chip draped Ramirez across the top of it before Neo Draven put the man through it with a Senton Bomb. Pulled to his feet by Chip, Draven hit the ropes for one final time, landing a Springboard Splash and taking the pin.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (The fans were cheering as Neo Draven and Chip left the ring and made their way to the back after saying a prayer over the body of the still downed Emilio
Ramirez. After they left, Dan Glass called for help. The ring was cleared of the broken table as the fans watched on until Ramirez was helped to his feet and made his way into the back under his own power. He waved as they clapped before he disappeared into the back.
The fans didn't have to wait long for the action to continue as Jason Flint led his "Electric Entourage" of Kimberly Davis and the State Club Boyz to the ring.
After they entered the ring, Johnny Green in neon green fatigues made his way out from the back. After high fiving in the ring, they were joined by their
"third", Skip Westervelt.)
<Aaron Starr> Introducing first ... in this match, set for one fall with a 30 minute time limit ... weighing in at a combined weight of 655 pounds ... here is your PAW Texas Heavyweight Champion and one half of the PAW Tag Team
Champions, Jason Flint ... his MT2K partner and the other half of the PAW Tag
Team Champions, Johnny Green ... amd the former Television Champion, Skip
Westervelt!!!!!
(The fans were cheering loudly as their opponents emerged from the back.) And their opponents this evening ... weighing in at a combined 709 pounds here are ... Jake Morgan, Justin Hart and Brian Cage!!!!!
(Hart climbed into the ring and went right after Flint only to hold up as the
SCB's stepped in front. As they awaited the referee, Jason Flint called for a mic.)
<Jason Flint> This doesn't seem fair somehow ... (All eyes were on Flint as he paused momentarily.) Even with a very noticeable weight advantage going to you ... (Flint motioned to the opposite corner and his opponents.) You can't deny the lack of wrestling skill and ring saavy the three of you bring to this match ... and not wanting to take full advantage of your serious lack of skill as well as personal hygiene ... I think that today would be better served as a test for the three of you ... a test where each of you
PROVES that you are worthy of stepping into the ring with this much talent ...
(There were several boos from the fans as Flint waited for them to quiet before continuing.)
We just want to make sure for your health as well as the fans enjoyment ... that each of you will be able to hold up your end of the match and not have to rely on us to carry you ... so what I propose instead of the inevitable where
Johnny and myself beat you around this ring and show you why we are the champions ... we let you face talent more on your own skill level ...
(Flint and Green smiled as the fans booed.) Boyz ... if you will ... (The State Club Boyz were ready for a fight as the motioned for one of their opponents to come and get it. Flint, Green and Kimberly cleared out of the ring leaving a confused Skip Westervelt standing there doumbfounded.
A livid Hart beat on the ring apron while Morgan did his best to incite the incensed fans more than already had been done.
In his referee stripes, B.J. Perris walked out to the ring with a mic in hand and a smile on his face.)
<Perris> Booking on the fly again ... are we, Flint? (Perris closed his eyes as he shook his head.) You Jason, there's one little problem with your scenerio ... you're not in charge ... I am ... so unless you want to forfet the Texas title as well as the tag belts, I suggest you have your buffoons clear out of that ring in a hurry!
(The fans were cheering loudly. Green had his hands over his ears from the loudness while Flint ran for his belts being held by Kimberly.)
And ... I do mean in a hurry! (Flint was frantically waving the SCB's out of the ring as the cheering continued.) Now ... in order to make sure that this match goes off without a hitch ... I suggest ... no, this is an order ... Kim and the State Club Boyz remove themselves from the ring ... NOW!
(Flint was frantically waving his arms at Perris telling him no but Perris was steadfast and nodding his head up and down.)
I'm waiting Jason ... the call is yours ... all yours ... (Looking from his belts then over to Kimberly and back to his belts, Flint hung his head for a brief second before issuing his orders for them to clear out to the back. With it down to just 6 wrestlers, Perris called for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Skip was sent in first despite protesting while in the other corner, Morgan and
Hart were embroiled in a bit of a discussion as both men wanted in first ...
Cage made the decision for them as he climbed in and went after Skip. Looking up, Cage pushed Skip into the corner and started hammering away with Punches.
The ring quickly filled as the other four men rushed into the ring and paired off with Hart and Flint going at while Green and Morgan squared off.
Perris lost control of the match as Morgan tossed Green over the top rope and took their fight to the outside while Hart put Flint was Gorilla Pressed into the air then slammed. Cage nailed Skip in the face with a Head Butt before bringing him own with a Suplex and covering for the pin. Perris was trying to get Hart out of the ring before finaling seeing the pin attempt and dropping for the count, breaking the hold when seeing Skip's foot on the bottom rope.
On the outside, Morgan Punched Green to the floor before snapping him up and
Powerbombing him. The fans were cheering as Morgan signalled for a second one but was taken over with a Back Body Drop. After several boots to the head,
Green went back to his corner.
Hart finally left the ring with warnings from Perris as Skip was thrown over out of an Arm Wringer and Boot into the back. Dragging Skip to his corner, Cage tagged in Hart. Climbing the ropes, Hart came off with a Double Axe Handle.
Skip was sent halfway across the ring with a T-Bone Suplex. Skip was pulled up by his hair and sent across the ring with a Whip into the corner. Hitting hard,
Skip staggered out of the corner and was taken down with a Clothesline.
Standing back, Hart eyed his opponent's corner and motioned for Flint. Skip crawled back to his corner and tagged in a reluctant Flint. Running in,
Hart missed a ducking Flint who threw himself back against the ropes and caught
Hart with a Roundhouse Kick. Making a quick tag, Flint brought in Green who put
Hart down with a Missile Drop Kick. Green dropped for the pin but had it broken up as Morgan rushed in with a boot into the back. He and Hart dropped Green with a Double Suplex that once again brought the others into the ring.
Perris had a brawl on his hands ... Morgan and Hart pushed Flint into a corner and started Punching away. Morgan was wailing away on the hapless Flint when he was grabbed from behind by Hart and spun out of the way. Taking his place, Hart started in on Flint.
Cage tossed a chair into the ring and went after Westervelt who did his best to get out of the way, leaving the ring. Threatening Westervelt, Cage never saw
Johnny Green come in from behind with a Knee into the back, sending him over the ropes and to the outside.
Across the ring, Morgan and Hart were nose to nose arguing over who was going to beat on Flint. Seeing an opening, Flint took them both down, lunging forward and hitting with a Double Clothesline.
Outside the ring, Westervelt and Green were busy doing a number on Brian Cage, whippinghim into the ring barrier. Westervelt came off with a Leg Drop from the apron that had Cage in trouble.
Flint covered Morgan but no count as Perris explained that it was Hart who was the legal man in. Pushing Hart up against the ropes, Flint Kneed him in the stomach before hopping up onto the ropes themn jumping off and driving his face into the canvas with a Face Driver. A cocky Flint dropped down and lazily covered Hart but was tossed off easily.
Complaining about the slow count, Flint was grabbed from behind and taken down with a Reverse DDT. Westervelt and Green were in the ring to break up a pin attempt and were dropped by Morgan's Double Clothesline.
It was 2 on 3 as they started brawling in the ring again. Morgan picked up the chair that Cage tossed into the ring earlier and hit Green with it but also caught Hart on the backswing. Perris and Morgan cleared the ring who then was on the recieving end of a warning from the ref.
With Hart flat on his back, Flint dropped for a cover getting a 2 count. Pulling Hart up, Flint pulled him over to his corner and smacked his head several times into the turnbuckle before tagging Green. With Flint Whipping
Hart into the far ropes, Green took him down with a Slingshot Leg Lariat.
Blocking a blow, Hart Eye Raked Green and Whipped him into the corner. With
Morgan holding his arms, Green took severl Knife Hand Chops to the chest before coming in on a tag.
With a Short Arm Clothesline, Morgan leveled Green then slapped him in the
Cobra Clutch. Perris watched closely for a submission. Green appeared to be fading fast when Perris broke up the hold. Morgan was in the ref's face fast arguing when he saw Hart in the ring with him. Hart had slapped him on the back for a tag. Both men were staring each other down before leaving the ring.
Green looked like a ragdoll as Hart tossed him around with 3 consecutive German
Suplexes when he was tagged out by Brian Cage as he pushed Green back into the corner.
Cage hammered several Back Elbows to Green's head before Power Slamming him. A following Leg Drop missed it's mark with Green rolling out of the way. Trying to crawl to his corner didn't work as Cage pulled him back to the center of the ring, dropping an Elbow in the middle of his back.
Flint and Westervelt rushed the ring, touching off another brawl that again spilled out of the ring with Morgan and Hart fighting it out with Green while inside the ring, Flint took Cage off of his feet and Skip came off the top ropes with a Senton Splash. Whipping Cage into the ropes, Flint took him down with a Drop Kick to the knees and Skip hit a Somersault Leg Drop.
Morgan left Hart for the ring and attacked Westervelt. From a Side Headlock,
Morgan sent Skip into the far ropes, taking him down in the return with a Boot to the head. He was then blindsided by a Forearm from Flint.
Hart took Flint down with a Spin Kick off the ropes. Threatened with more punishment, Flint was on his kness begging off. Hart smiled as he grabbed
Flint's hair and reared back for a big Punch. Before getting it off however,
Flint struck with a Low Blow. A Johnny Green Drop Kick sent him through the ropes and to the outside.
Flint was hammered back into the corner by Morgan. Taking several Chops to the chest, Flint slumped down in the corner. Morgan put a boot into Flint's throat, using the ropes for leverage when Skip Westervelt Kicked him in the back before he was taken out by Brian Cage swinging a chair.
Grabbing the chair away from Cage, Morgan reared back to hit Flint with it when the Texas Heavyweight Champion grabbed Perris and pulled him in front of him
... too late to get out of the way as Morgan swung wildly ... hitting Perris and wrapping the chair around his head.
The fans were on their feet as the referee slumped to the ground as the brawl continued ... Skip Westervelt took Cage to the floor from the ring with a
Skippicarana while Hart began pounding in the Champion. Johnny Green pulled
Morgan away and the two started beating on each other. Back and forth the two men went, trading shots until they fought around the ring then disappearing into the back.
The State Club Boyz came running out of the back and hit the ring as Perris, still laying on the canvas lifted his head and called for the bell.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (The PAW security rushed the ring to restore order pulling the members of both teams apart. The fans were on their feet cheering loudly as Hart fought to escape the grasp of several security guards who were holding him back. He was pointing at Flint who was nodding his head up and down as he was being led away to the back.
As the camera faded out on the end of the show, it faded back in outside where
Jake Morgan and Johnny Green were still fighting it out in the parking lot trading Punches as the credits rolled.)
