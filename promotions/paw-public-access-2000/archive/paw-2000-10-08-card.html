10.8 Card

8 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 2 —

(The camera opens on manager Mean Darlene walking the backstage hallways of the television studio passing UNT production people. She smiles at the turning heads of all the young men who stop what they’re doing in order to watch her walk by.
As she rounds a corner, she comes face to face with the “H-bomb” Hiroshima Nagasaki, the new PAW Television Champion. Alone, a worried look comes over her face as Nagasaki grins devilishly back at her.
Taking several steps back quickly, Darlene watches as Nagasaki wrings his hands while slowly stepping forward. Raising his hand in a threatening manner,
Nagasaki stops at the shrill voice that echoes through the hall in his native tongue. Both look around to see as sharply dressed, well groomed Japanese man quickly approaching, continuing with a stream of Japanese words which cause the wrestler to step backwards.
Stepping between Mean Darlene and Nagasaki, the man continues what only can be a harsh berating as Nagasaki drops his head before bowing very deeply twice, then walking off.
With the threat gone, the Japanese man turns abruptly towards Mean Darlene, taking a small but kurt bow. Those who follow professional wrestling and the
PAW might recognize manager Lonny Nakamura, grinning from behind his wire rimmed glasses.) Lonny Nakamura: A thousand pardons, Miss Darlene ... you have to forgive a young headstrong wrestler who thinks more with his brawn than his brains ...
one day he’ll learn when to pick his battles and the difference between enemies ... and important allies ... Again ... a thousand pardons ... (Stepping forward, Nakamura takes Darlene’s hand and kisses it before walking off. Standing there with a puzzled look on her face, Darlene is joined by Jake
Morgan who looks equally puzzled.) Jake Morgan: What was that all about? Mean Darlene: I have no idea ... (The former Texas Champion shakes his head in disgust just before the camera heads to the back entrance of the studio. One of the promotion’s t-shirted
Security men holds the door open for a man wearing a long coat, much better suited for the sudden cold front than the hot Texas summer.
With a duffel bag over one shoulder and a Singapore Cane in his hand, the man steps in and stops. Dropping his bag, he swipes off his shades and takes a look around ... revealing himself to be a former Television Champion, the once fired
Drake Russell.) Drake Russell: Destiny ... that’s what it is ... destiny ... (Replacing the shades, Russell grabs up the bag and heads into the bowels of the building. The camera fades out as Russell walks away before smoothly fading in on a man with his back to the camera, standing before the Public Access
Wrestling banner. Filmed in a grainy and dull sepia hue, the usually brillant banner lacks it’s normal colorful tones ... an appearance of something filmed in the early days of cameras. With the camera pulling back, the man begins to speak ... his voice interrupted by an occassional crackle heard in the background from the old decrepit film.)
Man: This is the story of a small wrestling promotion in Texas, with it’s humble beginnings and the man who made it what it is today ... the man who had a vision and knew all that was good with professional wrestling ... how he took it and everyone associated with the promotion, threw them upon his back and carried it all into prominence ... guess what?
(The man spreads his arms out from his sides as the camera pulls back. With the man turning slowly around, the camera does speedy zoom in on the man’s face ...
a big smile crosses the lips of the speaker ... Jason Flint.) Jason Flint: I’M BACK!!!!! (The camera slowly pulls back on the now laughing Flint before large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession.)
.... 5 ... .... 4 ... .... 3 ... .... 2 ... .... 1 ... (White Zombie's "More Human than Human" begins as flashes of the PAW stars in action are shown. Seth ... Skip Westervelt ... Hiroshima Nagasaki ... Jason
Flint ... and Jake Morgan finally the PAW Texas Heavyweight Champion, Justin Hart, standing in the middle of the PAW ring, the belt raised over his head.) After an explosion, the scene fades to black and the PAW logo disappears before fading back in and panning the fans and their signs held aloft such as "Bye bye
Perris", "The Heartbreakers suck!" and "F.A.G. is G.A.Y.". After making it’s way through the boisterious crowd of some 50 or so, the camera settles in on a smiling Aaron Starr standing in front of the PAW banner off to the side of the ring with a similarly dressed Jason Flint, both men wearing black blazers.)
Aaron Starr: Welcome ... (Chants of “Welcome Back!” start up from the standing fans inside the studio which brings a wave and a smile from Jason Flint.)
... to Public Access Wrestling! As you can all see ... I’ve been joined by your favorite ... and mine ... Jason Flint!
(The two shake hands.) A welcome back is in order, Jason ... a welcome back to a promotion that is somewhat different from when you were last here!
Jason Flint: Thank you, Aaron ... everyone ... It certainly has a new look and feel ... one that I’m sure is going to be just the start of great things ...
Aaron Starr: A new look ... new ownership ... all with the ouster of one man ... who I’m sure you’ve shed no tears over since last week, Jason. Jason Flint: You hit the nail on the head, Aaron ... with Perris finished here in Public Access Wrestling, we now have new owners who have made a pledge to bring this promotion to the heights that it ... and the fans so richly deserve
... I have a feeling that our mysterious masked boss and the University of North Texas investors are going to do everything in their power to make this all ... “fun” again ... Aaron Starr: Now, earlier this week we were addressed as to some of the changes that we could expect ...
Jason Flint: And some things, no one expected, Aaron ... there were some real curveballs thrown in there ...
Aaron Starr: Why don’t we just show the taped meeting ... (The camera fades out on the smiling hosts and in on what looks like a classroom with the gathered employees of the PAW sitting in chairs before a podium where the woman in the Offspring mask stands. Behind her are Fredrick A.
Gibson and Kimberly Davis.) Woman in the mask: Okay ... I guess everyone who is coming is already here, so let’s get started ...
(Paco, wearing a Heartbreaker t-shirt jumps up from his seat to be seen.) Paco: Christian and Seth will be here any minute now ... you can start and I’ll fill them in later.
Woman in the mask: Right ... let me start off by welcoming you all here to our first meeting under the new regime ... everything will run just as it did under
B.J., only alot smoother and without the worry of not being paid ... The monetary situation was the very first thing we’ve addressed and you can all rest assured that you will all be paid each and every week ...
(Several claps come from the listeners.) Now ... onto the changes ... let me make this clear right from the outset ... we want to put the fun back into Public Access Wrestling ... we want the fans as well as everyone involved to have fun ... not only will there be the usual great wrestling but we want the fans more involved.
Each of you will brought in for discussions about your characters as well as gimmicks and angles over the next few weeks for some tweaking in order for us to get to where we want to be ...
Look at this as more of a change in attitude rather than a cosmetic change. We want to get rid of the “heavyhandedness” of the Perris regime and get you more involved with your characters and careers ...
As for the change in personnel ... looking around the room, you might have noticed a few returning faces ... Drake Russell and Jason Flint ...
(A groan is heard from two different directions ... one from Aaron Starr and one from Justin Hart.)
... are back and ready for business. It should be noted even though Jason has been brought back into the fold, he is expected to honor his “exile” from the ring for the full 90 days as originally set ... but no one said Mr. Flint couldn’t work for the promotion in some capacity. For now, Jason will be a co-host on the program with Aaron and handle color work and interviews for both television and radio.
Aaron Starr: YES! (Starr’s outburst brings laughter which causes him to sink down into his seat.) Woman in the mask: I take that as a good thing, Aaron? Okay ... with the money issues handled, we are also bringing back some of those who were released from their contracts in the past few weeks. Johnny Green and any of the other local talent will be brought back before we look elsewhere for our talent ...
We’re a local promotion ... we’ll go with local wrestlers first ... then go outside that sphere if needed. We have started auditioning some wrestlers this past week but no one has been signed as of yet.
Our last order of business pertains to expansion. As some of you may or may not know, we’ve petitioned the National Wrestling Council ... the most prestigious wrestling body in our sport today for affiliateship. What we’d like to do is put this promotion on the map. We’d like to bring in top talent as well as get some of your names and faces introduced on a national level rather than the regional level as we have all these months.
As an affiliated promotion should this come about, we would be able to bring in some top rated talent from around the world as well as have access to a recognized World Title in both single and tag matches.
Up to this point, no one knows who we are or who you all are ... with induction into the NWC, that would, of course, all change.
With every good thing, there is a down side. In order to improve our chances for NWC recognition, we have to change our regional affiliation. The NWC already has it’s regional promotion for the Southwest which means our Texas
Title would not be recognized under their rules since their promotions in this region would have first dibs on a Texas Title.
As a show of good faith, we will forego the Texas Title as of this minute ... it is officially desolved. Public Access Wrestling, as of this minute is officially a local promotion ... leaving the promotion with only a Television
Title. (The gathered wrestlers take amongst themselves at the dropped bombshell, several turning towards an angered Justin Hart.)
I’m sorry, Justin but it’s for the benefit of the promotion. We will take into consideration other titles in the very near future but until we hear something from the NWC, we will stay with the one title.
(Hart gets up from his chair and walks out of the room.) That’s all I have for you today. Thank you ... (As the meeting comes to a close, the camera fades into Starr and a grinning Jason Flint.) Aaron Starr: A bombshell to say the least ... Justin Hart stripped of his Texas Title for the “good of the company”, leaving Hiroshima Nagasaki with the last remaining PAW title ...
As much as I hate to say it, Jason ... it just doesn’t seem right ... Jason Flint: I hear you, Aaron ... but let me interject that after my reign as the Texas Champion, the gold looked just a little dull ...
(Blur's "Song 2" blasted, bringing a cheer from the fans. Stepping out into the open, Justin Hart stood momentarily before making the long walk to the ring. He jumped onto the apron, leaned back and sprang over the ropes in a somersault.
Climbing the turnbuckles, he pumped the crowd into a frenzy. He rolled out of the corner and gestured for a microphone.)
Justin Hart: ...aaaaaaaaaaaaah. Now this is the heart and soul here! (Hart pointed at the crowd as he spun around.) Woooo hooooo! What a week. First we drop some talent, then we lost ... the boss! And yours truly got to live the American dream ... to open a can of whoop ass on the man in charge!
(The crowd roared at the last comment. Hart looked around, nodding.) And the best thing is ... PAW lives on. Unfortunately, so do a couple of the cockroaches that have infested the building over time.
(Stepping forward, Hart leaned on the ropes and spoke very clearly.) I'm talking about YOU, Jason Flint. (A chant of Flint’s name brought a wave from the former Champion.) Things were so peaceful while you were gone. And what happens ... HE' S appointed as my guest referee. And the lady wonders why the match pissed me off so much. The bane of my existence ... the man who seems determined to keep me under his thumb ... primes for a return. And I intend to throw him one hell of a welcome back party!
(Hart walks around in deep thought.) Next up, Jake Morgan. He's been rather quiet as of late. Which is good. I like him that way. Saves me a lot of grief. But one thing he said I can't let slide
... accusing us ... the remaining faction ... of being a bunch of losers. While I can't speak for those sorry bastards, I personally take offense on my own behalf. Well, it's to be expected. And now he has a goal ... a goal he means to obtain no matter what happens to fall into that path. Furthermore, he conveniently targets everyone in the fed ... even those who ain't even wasting time with him. Reality check, Morgan ... the threats aren't working. If I'm in the way, then that's too bad. You either go around or we collide ... plain and simple. So your best bet is to handle the REAL problem and quit making new ones.
And speaking of the real problem ... the H-Bomb is getting on my last ... damn ... nerve!!!! (Hart looks up at the crowd who get worked up all over again.) Now I was planning on kicking the shit out of Maeda first for the chair shot ... but since he's not here anymore, things have changed. I'm the focal point of your rage and anger ...
(He pauses.) ... and now ... you're my next TARGET! I don't crawl ... I don't beg ... and I don't fear you. We've both wanted this for some time, I say we stop beating around the bush and get it on! You intend to strip me of my dignity ... and I intend to break every bone in that eyesore of a body of yours! Guess which one of us gets what he wants? GROUND ZERO will never be the same again. And I guarantee that when all is said and done, Justin Hart will emerge from the ashes ... the ashes of your pitiful existence!
(Hart drops the mic and disappears into the back. With him gone, the camera focusses back on Starr and Flint.)
Aaron Starr: Not a word about the belt ... Jason Flint: You have to remember Aaron, no one’s ever accused Justin Flint of being the brightest kid on the block ... but then again, targeting Nagasaki puts him in line for a shot at the Television Title ...
Aaron Starr: Good point there, Jason ... now to be fair, I was able to get in a few questions with the former Texas Heavyweight Champion after the meeting, so let’s roll the tape!
(The camera fades out on the pair and back in on Aaron Starr and an angered Justin Hart standing in the back.) Aaron Starr: Aaron Starr here with Justin Hart ... the now deposed Texas Heavyweight Champion ... (The “title” draws a growl deep in the throat of Hart which makes Starr take a step back.) Justin ... your thoughts on the news just handed down from our new boss? Tell us how you feel with ...
(Hart suddenly turns on Starr and grabs him by the throat and pushes him back against the wall.)
Justin Hart: How do I feel? (Starr meekly nods his head in response.) How do you think I feel, Starr? Probably alot like you do right now ... with the air being squeezed out of me ... unable to breathe ... a little lightheaded
... (Releasing his grip, Hart turns and looks directly into the camera.) I’m not a happy man, Starr but let me tell you this ... and everyone else out there listening ... you and everyone else out there knows who the real Champion is ... you know who the big dog is in this promotion and you better believe I’m going to go out there each and every night to prove it ...
Skip Westervelt ... the newly returned Drake Russell ... the Heartbreakers ... Jake Morgan ... Hiroshima Nagasaki ... you want some “fun” ... oh, I’m going to have some fun beating in your brains!
No use crying over spilt milk, Starr ... it’s time to make someone pay! (Pushing Starr back, Hart stormed off. The camera returned to Starr and Flint.)

— Part 2 of 2 —

Jason Flint: Not a happy person, is he?
Aaron Starr: No, he’s not ... (Starr adjusts his collar when some booing is heard in the backgroun.) wait a second, it looks like we have Paco heading to the ring ... let’s go to the ring ... (The camera left the laughing cohosts and made it’s way to the ring where a lone Paco, wearing a Heartbreaker t-shirt, climbed into the ring with a mic in hand.)
Paco: Okay ... okay ... tonight’s first match with Black Thorne taking on Emilio Ramirez is being delayed as we wait on the arrival of your favorite tag team, the Heartbreakers!
(Paco jumps back at the overwhelming chorus of booing of the fans followed by several items being tossed into the ring, missing the dodging wrestler. Paco looks for help from the referee who can only shrug when the boos turn to cheers as Jake Morgan and Mean Darlene step out from the back. Making his way into the ring, Jake holds the ropes open for Darlene.
Inside, he calmly approaches the center of the ring and motions for the mic from Paco.) Jake Morgan: You know ... I'm sick of Christian Maeda ... I'm sick of Black Thorne and I'm sick of Maeda’s girlfriend, Seth but since none of them are here, there’s very little I can do ...
So, here's my solution to a big problem. What do you say if I just kick your ass instead? (Paco goes wide eyed as he steps back, shaking his head no while Morgan shakes his head yes.)
But let's make this fun. If you win, I'm gone from PAW. I’ll just walk away ... I will never have to watch another one of your pathetic promos or one of your boring matches ever again.
Paco: And if you win? (Morgan stroked his chin before rearing back and blasting Paco with the mic. Grabbing the reeling Paco’s arm, Jake Whipped him around and pulled back on the appendage, leveling the man with a Short Arm Clothesline. As cheesr rose from the fans, Peter Daye called for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Helping Paco to his feet, Jake lifted him up across his shoulders and started to spin. Around and around Morgan went with the Airplane Spin before stopping and sending Paco off his shoulder and flat onto the mat with a thud.
Pulling Paco up by the hair, Jake led him into the corner and introduced him to the turnbuckle several times, letting him fall to the canvas with the last blow. Climbing the ropes, Morgan came down with an Elbow Drop that landed squarely.
Not finished, Jake returned to his feet and put the boots to the Heartbreaker wannabe, kicking him to the center of the ring. With Paco flat on his face,
Jake reached down and shred the shirt off his opponent’s back. Punching the shirt into the air, Jake tore it further into smaller pieces then held it to his nose where he cleared the contents of his nostrils then rammed it into a struggling Paco’s face.
Paco crawled away while Daye admonished Morgan for his behavior. Nodding his head in agreement, Morgan watched as Paco sat in the corner and wiped his face clean of whatever Morgan blew into the shirt. Taking a running start, Morgan came flying in with both Boots that landed solidly to Paco’s face.
Morgan rolled to his feet while Paco fell out of the ring more than rolled to safety. Mean Darlene was up on the ring apron leading a cheer while Morgan waited for Paco to stand. As his opponent did so, Morgan dashed across the ring and dove through the ropes taking both men to the hard floor.
Slow to get back to his feet, Morgan picked Paco up off his feet then dropped him down into the ring barrier. Paco hit with his face and snapped back to the floor which brought a bloodthirsty cheer.
After slamming him into the steps, Jake sent the overmatched Paco careening into the barrier. Morgan moved in for the kill, slapping hands as he closed in.
Paco lashed out with a Punch to Jake’s midsection but was no match as Morgan came back with a Punch of his own. With an evil grin on his face, Jake lifted
Paco back to his feet with a firm grip on his ear. Paco struggled but stopped with increased pressure, crying out in pain before being rolled back into the ring.
From the apron, Jake waited until Paco got back to his feet before Slingshotting himself back and leveling him with a Clothesline. In the center of the ring, Jake celebrated with a roar which brought the fans to their feet.
Paco scuttled across the ring begging for mercy as Morgan followed him. Pulled to his feet, Paco was spun around and lifted off his feet from behind where he was slammed down onto Jake’s knee. Paco bounced across the ring in pain from the Atomic Drop.
Following across the ring, Morgan grabbed Paco and sent him across the ring, catching him up on the return and planting him into the canvas with a
Spinebuster. Kicking Paco over, Jake locked on the STF and forced a tap out. *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Peter Daye had to come in and force Morgan to unlock the hold but he refused. Mean Darlene, at Daye’s insistance came into the ring and pulled Morgan off. With Mean Darlene leading Morgan off, the camera made it’s way into the back where several of the PAW/UNT production people were gathered together listening to one of their own ... a man with green hair.)
Man with green hair: So ... after he bumped me and made me spill some of my drinks, I snapped and threw the tray on him. He was drenched in Dr. Pepper and coffee. Man .. was a I pissed. Here I was doing my job and this bozo makes me spill my drinks ...
(Those listening laughed as the green haired man continued with his story.) Let me tell you ... he’s not that tough. I pushed him back. I’m ready to go to town on his ass and he’s got the look in his eye.
(The rest were all smiles and nodding their heads.) He’s scared ... I can smell it ... (The smiles disappear quickly.) Yeah, he’s scared alright ... I can smell it ... (Several of the young men in front of him gulp deeply, a few step back when a voice comes from behind the storyteller.)
Voice: Scared, huh? You can smell it? (The green haired man nods his head up and down.) Man with green hair: Sure did ... I was ready to bust him one ... (The man turned to see a smiling Justin Hart standing there. With his eyes bugging out, the man with the green hair turned and ran off, pushing through his cohorts. A cold stare from Hart scattered the rest of the employees.
Leaving the back, the camera returned to the PAW banner where Flint and Starr were.) Aaron Starr: Ladies and gentlemen ... the next match was scheduled to be a tag team tilt but we’ve got a no-show on the Heartbreakers ...
Jason Flint: No Heartbreakers and the waiting tandem of Pep Preston and the Trashman inside the ring. Looks like we’re going to see how the new boss fixes this little problem before the fans get restless.
Aaron Starr: Just the first problem for the ... (Suddenly, Starr is interupted by the gong and kodo drums that signal the arrival of Hiroshima Nagasaki. Starr looks toward the ringside entrance as Nagasaki steps from the back to a cascade of boos. Nagasaki is dressed in his wrestling gear and is carrying the TV belt in his left hand. Ignoring the crowd, Nagasaki makes his way toward Aaron Starr. One eager young fan streches to touch the champion but is quickly rebuked when Nagasaki slaps his hand away.
Nagasaki entered the ring casually and walked to the middle.) Aaron Starr: Don’t tell me Hiroshima Nagasaki is going to take them both on? (Nagasaki motioned for both men across the ring from him to come closer. Standing there unmoving, Nagasaki was motionless as Preston and Trashman approached. They looked confused as Nagasaki just stood there.
Both men shrugged their shoulders before Nagasaki struck like lightning with a Double Thrust to both of their throats. Both men fell back when Nagasaki motioned for Peter Daye to call for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Trashman was snapped up and dumped on the mat with a Belly-to-Belly Suplex. Turning to Trash’s partner, Nagasaki reached in and took Preston down with a Dragon Screw Leg Drag. Rolling back to his feet, Nagasaki Kicked Preston in the face with a stiff Front Kick. Trashman was back on his feet and was caught by the whirling Nagasaki with a Spinning Back Kick. Trashman feel back against the ropes and was taken to the outside with a Side Kick to the head. Spilling over the ropes, Trashman landed hard on the floor. Peter Daye came in with a warning but backed away quickly as Nagasaki spun around with his leg raised for a kick. With the ref backpeddling, Nagasaki lowered the leg and pointed at the downed Preston.
Climbing the ropes, Nagasaki sat on the top turnbuckle until Pep returned to his feet. Wobbling badly, Pep took a Double Thrust to the throat and was taken off his feet with Nagasaki dropping down and sweeping his legs out from under him.
Kipping back to his feet, Nagasaki threw himself back against the ropes before dropping down with a Leg Drop across Preston’s throat. Daye hit the mat with a
2 count as Nagasaki sat there with his leg across Preston. The match should have been over but Nagasaki wasn’t done. Removing his leg from Preston,
Nagasaki broke the count. Pulling Preston to his feet, Nagasaki hammered him into the corner with several Knife Hand Chops. Pulling his head down, Nagasaki unloaded with Knees to the face which drew a warning from Daye again.
After threatening Daye, Nagasaki took Preston down with a Snap Suplex and followed that up with the H-Bomb, a top rope Fist Drop.
Moving to the center of the ring, Nagasaki motioned for the end of the match. Lifting Preston into the air, Nagasaki finished him off with the Nagasaki Driver. Holding Pep to the canvas, Nagasaki patiently took a 3 count for the win while the fans loudly booed.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (After throwing Preston out of the ring, Nagasaki motioned for a mic from Aaron Starr. Starr climbed into the ring and handed the mic to Nagasaki before turning to leave. A hand on his shoulder kept in place. Turning, Starr found
Nagasaki wanting him to stay.) Hiroshima Nagasaki: It has been five weeks since the arrival of the H-Bomb onto the PAW scene. In that time I have annihilated everyone who has stood in my way.
(A loud chorus of boos filtered into the ring.) Last week I claimed possesion of one of the PAW titles and this week, I find that it is the only remaining title but this does not satisfy my hunger.
I came to your miserable country to prove Japan superior to in every way. Just as I said, I have won your title ... now, all that remains is the annhilation of each of your wrestlers ... your weak American wrestlers!
Your former Champion, Justin Hart continues to shy away from facing me in the ring. Your former "champion" knows he stands no chance against someone of my caliber. Your Heartbreakers fear me and Jake Morgan is a weakling who hides behind his woman ...
You show me no respect by not scheduling the TELEVISION champion to wrestle tonight bu I have already taken care of that ...
(The crowd begins to jeer loudly, taunting him as an angered Nagasaki points to both Trashman and Pep Preston.)
SHUT UP! From this point forward, I will be an integral part of every PAW show that is aired on television whether a match is scheduled or not. Someone will be destroyed by the H-Bomb and no one is exempt. It could be anyone ... a wrestler, a fan, the president ... even an announcer.
(Nagasaki slowly turned towards Aaron Starr who was just realizing he was in trouble. With that said, Nagasaki grabs an obviously worried Starr by the throat and glares at him.)
I have not appreciated some of the comments you have made about me during shows and on your hotline. Did you think I would not notice you little weasel?
Perhaps you should be tonight's victim? (Spinning Starr around, Nagasaki put him into the Unconditional Surrender. Starr’s arms flailed away but soon went limp from the lack of blood to the brain. The fans cheered loudly as Nagasaki dropped Starr to the canvas and
Jason Flint hopped onto the ring apron. Stepping back, Nagasaki motioned for Flint to come and get it. Removing his blazer, Flint climbed between the ropes as Peter Daye stepped between the two, holding Flint back.
Nagasaki’s eyes were locked on Flint who was arguing with the referee. Something caught Nagasaki’s eye ... someone stepping out from the back. Whoever it was caught Flint and Daye’s eye as well.
That someone was Drake Russell. With Singapore Cane in hand, Russell pointed first at Flint then to Nagasaki in the ring when the PAW Security hit the ring to break things up.
The fans were chanting “PAW!” loudly as order was restored and Flint went to the aid of the unconscious Aaron Starr just before the camera broke away to the back and Jake Morgan, who was alone.
As he stepped around the corner, he ran into Kimberly Davis who was just as startled as he was.)
In unison: I’m sorry ... (Morgan stepped back from the dress wearing Davis and smiled.) Kimberly Davis: Can you do me a favor? My zipper is stuck on this stupid dress ... would you try it? Jake Morgan: Uhm ... sure ... (Turning around, Kimberly lifted her hair, exposing the zipper. Jake tugged at it for a few seconds before it pulled down. Turning around, Kimberly smiled.)
Kimberly Davis: You’re sweet ... thanks ... (Moving in quickly, she kissed Jake on the cheek before walking off. Standing there, Jake heard a voice from behind.)
Voice What was that all about? (Jake spun around to see Mean Darlene with her hands on her hips glaring at him.) Jake Morgan: Um ... ah ... I don’t know ... (The camera left Morgan to find an explanation as it moved to the back exit of the television studio. The door opened and the California Boy, Seth walked in
... just a little late. Stepping in with a bag over his shoulder, he made his way through the hallway when he passed Justin Hart who was going in the opposite direction with a bag over his shoulder, leaving the show.
Passing Seth, Hart stopped and wheeled around.) Justin Hart: HEY! (Hearing Hart shout out, Seth turned around.) Seth: What? Justin Hart: You know ... I was really looking forward to kicking the shit out of your buddy, Maeda but he didn’t show ...
Seth: Right ... (Seth shook his head as he turned to walk away stopping only when Hart yelled out again.) Justin Hart: HEY! Seth: Yeah? Justin Hart: Maeda didn’t show ... so I guess you’ll do! (Charging forward, Hart nailed Seth with a Forearm to the head. Momentarily stunned, Seth was grabbed and Whipped around into the wall. Smacking into it with a dull thud, Seth staggered backwards when he was grabbed again and led down the hallway.
Finding what he was looking for, Hart slammed Seth’s head into the glass encased fire extinguisher. With the sound of break glass, Hart dropped Seth and wiped his hands before walking off with a smile on his face.)
There ... that’s better ... (Justin Hart whistled a little tune as he walked off and the credits started to roll.) x-x-x-x-x Black Cat Entertainment©2000
