Primal Rage

3 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 3 —

Campus Theater standing with his arms crossed in front of promoter B.J. Perris. By the smug look on Flint's face and the redness of Perris' one would think they were in an argument. Shielded by Perris is referee Peter Daye.)

Green did as he was told, again having little effect as the blood streamed down.

Morgan up, Green Body Slammed him onto the hood. Grabbing Morgan's feet, Green pulled him off of the hood and flat onto his back ... hitting the asphalt hard.
Fighting back and forth, Morgan slammed into Green hard, Spearing him and driving him into the side of a car, denting the door panel. Both men were slow to get up ... Green needing help from Security to do so.
Green was pulled away when an angered Morgan staggered back to his feet and came in running with a big Punch to the head. The blow knocked down Green as well as the men holding him.
Tapped on the shoulder, Morgan spun around and took a face full of Mace from Kimberly Davis. Blinded, Morgan put her down with a Clothesline, spinning her through the air as he slumped against a nearby car. Pulling back, the camera panned the "battlezone" and all the downed, bloody participants before moving back inside where Peter Daye was calling for the bell in the first match.

Daye slowly counted with both men down. Arcin was first to his feet before struggling to get back up onto the stage. Pulling a table out from under the ring, he tossed it down to floor level.

Back Elbows ... one to the face and a second one to the stomach. With Arcin charging in, Powell ducked out of the way of a poorly thrown Punch. Sliding in behind, Powell's Belly-to-Back Suplex put Arcin down. Arching his back, Powell bnridged into a 2 count before a kick out.

Arcin's Moonsault landed squarely, sending both men through the table bringing everyone to their feet.
Daye dropped to the ground checking on both wrestlers who were in a heap, mixed in with the broken table. Rolling off of Powell, Arcin seemed to be in pain as he tried to get back to his feet as the chants of "EXTREME!" started around the theater.
