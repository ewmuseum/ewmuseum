PAW — Card Archive

9.17 Card

17 September 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 2 —

(The camera opens inside the PAW television studio for yet another week of wrestling action. Somewhere in the back stands the woman in the Offspring mask looking down at a clipboard in her hands.
Dressed in a tight blue sequined dress and matching heels, the woman is a picture of loveliness with the exception of her mask and the tangled dyed dreads hanging down into her face. Shaking her head, she continues to look down, muttering under her breath.)
Woman in the mask: First we lose the Campus theater, then Desperado, Kat Collins, now Khan ... Johnny Green ... What’s next? (Shaking her head in silence, she looks up as the Head of Security, Shane steps up and whispers into her ear.)
WHAT!?!?!? (Startled by whatever news she’s been given, she snaps her head around, looking down the hallways for someone.)
Where are they? (With Shane jerking a thumb back from where he came, she takes off in that direction as large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession.)
.... 5 ... .... 4 ... .... 3 ... .... 2 ... .... 1 ... (White Zombie's "More Human than Human" begins as flashes of the PAW stars in action are shown. Seth ... Skip Westervelt ... Johnny Green ... Justin Hart ...
Drake Russell ... Christian Maeda and finally the PAW Texas Heavyweight Champion, Justin Hart, standing in the middle of the PAW ring, the belt raised over his head.)
After an explosion, the scene fades to black and the PAW logo disappears before fading back in and panning the fans and their signs held aloft such as "Get
Well Soon Jake", "Somebody DROP the H-Bomb" and "How many more days till Flint gets back?".
After making it’s way through the boisterious crowd of some 50 or so, the camera settles in on a smiling Aaron Starr standing in front of the PAW banner off to the side of the ring.)
Aaron Starr: Welcome once again ... (The camera pans the cheering fans and their surroundings which is being remodeled as the backdrop shows scaffolding and partially painted walls before returning to Starr.)
I am your host Aaron Starr and this ... this is Public Access Wrestling! We have a big show tis evening ... so let’s go to the ring!
(The camera makes it’s way to the ring where a hyperactive Paco is bouncing all over the place, waiting for his opponent when the lights dim and “Bitter” by
San Antonio’s Union Underground starts to play followed by the new look Drake Russell makes his way out from the back in his long overcoat and his Singapore Cane. A mixed greeting follows in his wake as Russell climbs into the ring, leveling his weapon at Paco. Referee Peter Daye is over quickly with a warning about the weapon which draws the ire of Russell as well.
The lights flicker back to life and the music abruptly ends before Daye calls for the bell and the start of the match.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** A darting Paco stops as Russell holds the Cane up in front of him. Backing away quickly, Paco complains to the referee who once again warns Russell. Taking a step back to his corner, Drake Russell watches as Paco and Daye discuss the situation. While getting assurances from Daye that the weapon will play no part in the match, Russell strikes ... like a snake in the grass, Russell lashes out with the Cane, hitting Paco across the head.
Sent reeling, Paco catches two more strokes across the head before falling back against the ropes. Grabbing an arm, Russell Whips him across the ring into the far ropes. On the return, Paco takes a blow to the midsection, doubling him over.
Dropping the weapon, Russell lifts him up for a Double Underhook Back Breaker, slamming him down across his knee. Snatching up the Cane, Russell pushes Daye out of the way before climbing the ropes. After punching his weapon into the air, Russell leaps from the drop, coming down with a Fist Drop that shakes the ring.
After strutting around the ring, Russell returns with a Leg Drop across Paco’s throat, sitting there as Daye drops down for a count. With a 2 count, Russell lifts his leg off of his opponent, stopping Daye.
With a Boot Stomping, Russell pulls Paco up and sends him across the ring where he battered his helpless opponent into the mat with a Spinning Heel Kick as he returned. Using the Cane, Russell dropped to the mat and used the weapon to
Choke Paco which drew an immediate warning and subsequent count from the ref. Leading Paco around the ring by a deathgrip on his ear, Russell takes him back to the center of the ring. With a Headbutt, Russell wrapped his arms around his much smaller opponent. Lifting him high into the air before swinging his body around and taking Paco down with a Belly-to-Belly Suplex.
Rising slowly to his feet, Russell headed to the corner and started to climb up. Balancing himself on the ropes, Russell pushed his arms into the air before leaping off. Spinning gracefully through the air, Drake Russell did a complete spin ... and then some as crushed Paco underneath him with the Total Decimation
... a 450 Splash. Both men bounced off the mat with the move but it was academic as Daye dropped to join them and slapped out a 3 count.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Back on his feet, Russell called for a mic. Getting it, he moved closer to the fans.) Drake Russell: This is how a former Champion is treated around here? Me? A Main Eventer who’s destiny is to lead this promotion on his back into the future and what do we get?
(Russell motions back to the still unmoving Paco.) We get this ... I get moved to opening match status against lesser opponents when I should be challenging Justin Hart who carries MY Texas Heavyweight Belt around his middle
... I should be banging heads against the likes of Jake Morgan and Christian Maeda instead I’m beating Paco into unconsciousness ...
Well, take this match and my words here today ... and let that be a lesson to you! (Throwing down the mic, Drake Russell stormed off out of the ring and disappeared into the back. Leaving the ring, the camera also made it’s way into the back, finding Television Champion Christian Maeda in the locker room staring at both of his titles.)
Christian Maeda: You know Thorney, I can’t decide which of these belts I like better ... is it the Television Title that shows my wrestling excellence as an individual inside the squared circle ... or is it the Tag strap which I plucked away as leader of the Heartbreakers?
I just can’t decide ... (Turning, Maeda looks back at his bodyguard, Black Thorne, standing there silently.) Which do you like better? (The big man just shrugs his shoulders which draws a wave of the hand from Maeda.) It doesn’t really matter because after tonight, I’ll also have the Texas Heavyweight Title for the clean sweep ... but the one prize that eludes me is the lovely Lisa Doyle ...
(Maeda sighs deeply when he is interrupted by a knock at the door.) See who that is, will you Thorney? (Black Thorne opens the door to find the Head of Security leading a man inside.) Man: Are you Christian Maeda? Christian Maeda: Yes ... Man: Good ... is that one of the Tag Team belts? (A questioning Maeda looks at the smiling man before answering.) Christian Maeda: Yes ... Man: Good ... (The man snatches the belt away, tucking it under his arm.) Christian Maeda: HEY!?!?!? Man: If your boss doesn’t pay for the belts, then you no longer have belts ... (The man turns towards Shane.) Now, if you’ll be so kind as to lead me to the other belt ... (With the two men leaving the room, Maeda can just sit there looking totally surprised as the camera fades out into Mean Darlene standing outside her dressing room, pacing. She was concerned with the condition of Morgan. Rubbing the back of her neck, she had seen better days herself. She hadn't even noticed
Justin Hart walk up behind her.) Justin Hart: Darlene? (Mean Darlene jumped forward about 5 feet as she was startled from hearing his voice. Turning around, she stepped back scowling.)
Mean Darlene: What the hell do you want? (Hart shrugged his shoulders.) Justin Hart: Yeah, I get a kick out of poking fun at guys laid out on stretchers... (Mean Darlene glared on.) You know ... that look on your face really doesn't suit you. Mean Darlene: What do you want? (Hart kicked at the floor, hesitant about asking.) Justin Hart: How is Morgan? He really took a beating in that last match ... and you know what I mean, too.
(She was suprised to hear Hart voicing his concern about Jake’s condition.) Mean Darlene: Nothing too extensive ... in fact, he should be just fine for his next match. So don't get too comfortable with that title ... cause you know he'll want it back.
Justin Hart: Sure, sure... (Hart turned to walk away. He stopped suddenly and looked back at her...) Don't take this the wrong way ... you may be a pain in the nuts... but you're one of the cutest pain in the nuts I've ever had the displeasure of dealing with. Morgan's a lucky guy.
(With that, Hart took his leave, leaving Darlene standing there shaking her head at his last remark. Leaving the back for ringside, the camera found the
Offspring and Seth in opposite corners. Strangely, Seth was alone while the Offspring was accompanied by Ryan Miller. Offspring and Miller went over last second details while Seth patiently waited for the start of the match.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** The match started fast and furious with the Offspring taking it to the California Boy. With a Whip into the ropes, Seth was tugged down by Ryan Miller who reached into the ring. Popping back to his feet, Seth was up and pointing a finger at Miller. Turning his back was a mistake as the Offspring booted him with a Drop Kick to the back. With Miller tugging down the rope, Seth was up and over to the outside.
The ref was over with a warning as Miller stepped away. With Seth getting back to his feet, the Offspring launched himself over the ropes and hit a Plancha.
On the outside, Seth had both men to deal with. A Double Whip into the ring barrier looked bad as Seth hit then slid to the ground holding his back.
A Double Drop Kick sent Seth flying over the barrier into the crowd. With a chair in hand, the Offspring jumped off the barrier and slammed the chair down over his opponent’s head, dropping him. Daye’s warnings brought the fight back to the ring as Seth was dumped unceremoniously over the barrier.
Sent back in, Seth was further manhandled. Sent into the ropes, Seth hit the mat, sliding in under the Offspring and carrying to the outside where he booted
Ryan Miller in the face with a perfect Baseball Slide. A whirlwind of Punches, Seth had Miller reeling but that didn’t take care of the Offspring who reminded Seth that his match was with the masked man and not his partner. Flying from the top ropes, Offspring hammered Seth to the ground with a Double Axe Handle to the back.
After Whipping Seth into the ring post, Miller scooped him up for a Body Slam which the Offspring followed from the ring steps with a Senton. Using his boot to choke Seth, the Offspring was shielded from Daye as Miller argued with the ref.
Pushing Seth up against the apron, Offspring hit hard with Reverse Knife Hands that were counted off by the fans. Blocking one, Seth hit the Offspring between the eyes and opened up with Knife Hands of his own. With a Boot to the midsection, the Offpsring was doubled over then found himself facedown on the floor as Seth hit a Guillotine Face Driver.
Rolling Offspring over, Seth had a pin attempt but an obstructing Miller blocked Daye’s vision. With several fans starting their own count, Daye pushed
Miller out of the way and slapped a 1 count on the floor before the Offspring got a shoulder up.
Arguing with Daye and Miller, Seth found himself in a Full Nelson while Offy stepped to the side and hooked a leg before falling backwards with a Russian
Leg Sweep variation. Rolling on top, Offspring rained down Punches until Daye pulled him off.
Both Offspring and Miller backed away under the accusing finger of the referee, giving Seth precious seconds to clear his head. After threatening the ref,
Spring pulled Seth up and rolled him back into the ring. Whipping Seth into the ropes, Spring took him down with a Knee as he shot back. Bouncing off the ropes, the Offspring landed a Somersault Leg Drop across Seth’s throat for a cover. Daye hit the mat once before Seth lifted a shoulder. Landing a Knee Drop, the Offspring was up and jawing at Daye while heading for the ropes. Climbing to the top, he jumped off, launching himself across the ring for a big Elbow Drop. Seth wasn’t about to lay there, rolling out of the way at the last second.
Groggy, he pulled himself to his feet, using the ropes. The Offspring was back on his feet, holding his elbow. There was a grimace on his face as he took
Seth’s legs out from under him with a Drop Kick to the knees. Miller pushed a chair into the ring for his partner to the boos of the fans who were fast to point the move out to the referee. Miller threatened several front row fans after attempts to quiet them didn’t work.
Kicking the chair to the center of the ring, Offpsring grabbed Seth by the hair and pulled him along. Wrapping an arm around Seth’s head brought a grimace of pain to his face which gave Seth enough time to counter with a Suplex of his own.
Taking the chair and pushing it into the ropes in the corner, Seth half dragged Offspring to the opposite corner where he bounced his head off of the top turnbuckle several times. Stepping away from the corner, Seth was ready to send
Offspring into the chair only to be blocked and have his move reversed. Seth was sent across the ring where he slammed into the chair. Staggering backwards out of the corner, Seth was grabbed up and lifted into the air from behind.
While the Offspring was putting the finishing touches on his opponent, Ryan Miller found that he had his hands full as Christian Maeda’s bodyguard Black Thorne charged out from the back. The pummeling of Miller caught Daye’s eye and brought him to their side of the ring.
Falling back with a Suplex, Offspring rolled Seth to his back for the pin but without a ref, there was no count. Add to the fact that Seth’s partner slid into the ring and grabbed up the chair, there wasn’t going to be a pin.
Swinging, Maeda crushed Offspring’s head with the chair. Releasing his grip, the Offspring fell back to the mat as Seth rolled the Offspring to his back.
Maeda dove to the outside to get Daye’s attention. Spinning around, Daye saw the pin attempt and dropped to the mat.
Slapping out a 3 count, it was over. *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding ***

— Part 2 of 2 —

(With the match over, Maeda pulled Seth from the ring and led him to the back
while Black Thorne broke off his fighting and followed. Miller argued with Peter Daye while the Offspring tried to pull himself together as the camera disappeared into the back ...
... into the back where the masked woman was standing outside of a dressing room door with a young, attractive woman by her side. With her knocking, the door opened to reveal Drake Russell inside.)
Drake Russell: Yes? Woman in the mask: I hate to be the bearer of bad news but with the promotion’s financial status of late, we’ve had to make several sacrifices. In order to free up money to pay past debts, we’re having to let some of the wrestlers go
... Unfortunately, you’re one of the victims of our financial crunch ... Drake Russell: WHAT!?!?!? Woman in the mask: I’m going to have to let you go ... as much as it saddens me to have to release, I have to do what is best for the company.
On the bright side of things, Ms. Jessica Falcon here would like to offer you work in her promotion out of Arizona ...
(The young woman steps forward to speak to the baffled Russell as the camera breaks away to the backstage entrance where Hiroshima Nagasaki is slowly making his way towards the building. Though dressed in street clothes, the H-Bomb's menacing scowl and game face are already in place.
Stepping up to the camera, the H-Bomb bends down close to the camera where only his face can be seen and speaks.)
Hiroshima Nagasaki: Tonight Extremist I, Hiroshima Nagasaki will make an example out of you in front of the entire PAW organization and all its fans.
Prepare to be annihilated. (With that said, he makes his way into the building and opening the door, Nagasaki is met square in the face by a chair. As the H-Bomb crumples to the floor, the Extremist hits him fullforce on the back with a second chair shot.
Picking Nagasaki up by the hair, the Extremist begins dragging him through the backstage area toward the ring making sure to take every opportunity to smash
Hiroshima into numerous objects. Upon reaching ringside, Extremist continues to punish Nagasaki with punches and kicks.
Following a brutal clothesline, Nagasaki is rammed head first into the ringpost. Nagasaki is busted wide open and appears nearly unconscious as he is thrown into the ring. The Extremist follows his opponent into the ring with a flying elbow off the top rope.
With both combatants inside the ring, Daye calls for the bell.) *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Diving into the ring, Daye slaps out a count and somehow Nagasaki is able to kick out of the pin. With a look of frustration on his face, the Extremist picks Nagasaki up and drives him into the mat with a Vertical Suplex.
Looking confident, Extremist begins to ascend the ropes. The Extremist barely misses with a Legdrop as Nagasaki rolls out of the way. Rising unsteadily to his feet, H-Bomb catches the Extremist straight in the throat with double chop as he gets up.
Nagasaki grabs his opponent's left leg and lays in with a Dragonscrew Legdrag. Holding on to the leg, Nagasaki raises his opponent up and delivers a second Dragonscrew. The Extremist clutches his knee in obvious pain. Not letting up for a second, Hiro begins to work over the already injured knee twisting it, and dropping knees on it repeatedly. Nagasaki allows his opponent to get to his feet long enough to be knocked back to the canvas as Nagasaki
Dropkicks the Extremist's injured knee. Placing the Extremist's foot over the second rope, Nagasaki climbs to the second rope and jumps onto the appendage. Extremist screams in pain as
Nagasaki stalks around the ring. The ref begins checking on the injured knee to see if the match needs to be stopped.
As Nagasaki waits in the corner, Mean Darlene walks out from the back and hits him from behind with a Singapore Cane. Much to her surprise, the blow has no effect on the H-Bomb, who turns toward Darlene with a smile on his face.
Taking off for the back with Nagasaki hot on her trail, Darlene disappears. The camera follows Nagasaki into the back as he unsuccessfully tries to find her. In the ring, with the Extremist still down holding his knee, Daye starts acount. A furious Nagasaki, giving up on finding Darlene heads back towards the ring only to arrive at ringside in time to be counted out.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Angered, Nagasaki jumps into the ring scoops up the Extremist and lands the Nagasaki Driver before being pushed back by the ref. Leaving the ring after threatening Daye, he pulled a table out from underneath the ring apron. Setting it up on the outside, he pulled it into position in front of the scaffolding.
Not finished with his work, Nagasaki pulled a second table out and set it up on top of the first one.
Going back into the ring, he pulled the Extremist to the outside. Pointing at the tables then to the scaffolding, Nagasaki brought the fans to their feet as he started to climb to the top with.
Finally making it to the top, Nagasaki looks down at the tables and gestures with his thumb across his throat before pulling the Extremist up. Out of a
Standing Head Scissors, the Extremist was hefted into the air as Nagasaki turned towards the tables below the scaffolding.
Wrapping his legs around Nagasaki’s neck, the Extremist threw his body backwards with a Hurrincanrana but rather than pull Nagasaki over the edge, he was released and sent hurtling towards the tables. Twisting his body, the
Extremist hit the first table, going through it then doing the same to the second one. The fans gasped at the fall which had Nagasaki laughing and pointing down at the unmoving Extremist. Smacking his elbow several times, Nagasaki did the unthinkable and leapt from the top, hitting an El bow Drop.
The fans stood silent before Nagasaki returned to his feet and walked off to the back as help rushed out. After several minutes, the Extremist was strapped down to a board and wheeled away to the back.
The crowd started buzzing once again when Blur's "Song 2" blasted from the loudspeakers. Out steps "Street Soldier" Justin Hart ... PAW Texas Heavyweight belt in hand. He stands in the entranceway, looking around at the crowd.
Suddenly, he hops the guardrail, walking through the crowd, slapping hands while he swims through the sea of fans. Finally making his way back to the railing, he hops back over and slides into the ring. Walking over to the corner, he holds the belt high above his head in one hand, and speaks into the mic.)
Justin Hart: What's up ... TEXAAAAAAAAAAAAS! (Hart stands on the turnbuckle listening to the crowd.) Notice anything different about me? Oh yeah ... picked up some fresh new jewlery. .. (Hart hops down and places the belt against his waist. Looking around at the fans, he turns and gives everyone a good look.)
Now it's said by some that my very posession of this belt tarnishes it ... well, in the next few weeks, we'll find out if that's true or not ... that's right, FUCK what Russell said. I'm not ready to give this thing up! I did that once in the interest of fair play and got nothing but an earache. Now I'm gonna do the talking. For starters, in response to Russell ... wait your turn ...
you're bound to get a shot and I ain't gonna run. So when it happens, it happens ... and there won't be any doubt about the outcome ...
(Hart takes the belt back off and holds it in his hand.) Next we have Chop Saki ... Hiroshima Nagasaki to his friends ... that's why I call him Chop Saki. See, when you come in here and insult PAW and American wrestlers, you insult me. I'm American ... I wrestle and I'm a part of this federation. That was mistake number 1. Insulting me in the hall, number 2. Now you threaten me ... me!!! The resident toughest SOB! Strike three, asswipe!
Shine the belt up for you, huh? I'll polish it with your tongue first! Here in America, boy, we do things differently than what you claim over there in the Land of the Rising Sun. Slap down a Good Ol' Boy and he just might get up and introduce you to a rightly good ass-whuupin. So, I'm gonna say this as plainly as I can ...
... DON'T fuck with me, gaijin. (Hart slips off his wraparounds. Getting a good swing, he pitches them into the crowd.) Seth ... again, thanks for the sympathy ... but ain't you got enough gold? You greedy little bastard! Ah hell, it's to be expected ... just another
Californian with "gold fever" I guess. Say is it just me or can these PAW guys actually be a bunch of caring, soft-hearted individuals? Who knows, right? My soft side appreciates the concern ...
(Hart hangs his head down out of view, then holds it up again, with a stern expression on his face...)
... but my BUSINESS side wants me to remind you idiots that I'm not lying down for NOBODY. So do your thing, earn your shots, line 'em up, Perris...
... and I'll knock 'em down! Time to open a can of whoop ass, folks! Maeda ... GET OUT HERE ... NOW!!! (Christian Maeda was only too happy to stroll out from the back followed by his bodyguard, Black Thorne. Maeda didn’t look too happy with only the PAW
Television Title over his shoulder as he climbed into the ring. Ass soon as he was in the ring, Peter Daye had his hands full as both Champions met in the center of the ring and went nose to nose. There was some heavy talking between the two. The fans were on their feet egging the two on when the fireworks started. Maeda shoved Hart out of his face and the Texas Champ retaliated by shoving Maeda to the mat and then nailing
Black Thorne with a Right Cross. With the battle starting, Daye called for the bell.) *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Both men vacated the ring, leaving Hart alone with Peter Daye. In the center, Hart was ready to go, his fists balled and a look on his face that could have killed. Maeda tried to dive back into the ring but each time, Hart was there to start in with the Boots to the back. On the other side of the ring, Black Thorne bought Maeda time to get into the ring as he himself entered which drew Hart’s attention.
Punches were exchanged between the two until Maeda was in the ring spinning Hart around. Thorne took a powder as soon as Maeda launched a fusillade of Punches that had Hart staggering backwards. Whipping him into the ropes, Maeda caught Hart on the return and wrapped him up, turning his body into an Abdominal Stretch. Maeda almost had it locked in but Hart was able to counter with a Hip Toss. Hart followed that with a Leg
Drop. Grabbing Maeda up, Hart put him up across his shoulders but wasn’t able to hold his squirming opponent. Snapping around, Hart drove a Boot into Maeda’s midsection then grabbed his hair before driving his face into the mat. Rolling
Maeda to his back, Hart hooked a leg for a 2 count. With a Whip into the ropes, Hart followed with his Corner Splash, the Hangtime. Stepping back, Hart caught the staggering Maeda out of the corner, planting his head into the mat with a DDT.
Before getting a cover, Hart had Black Thorne to contend with as the bodyguard entered the ring. With Hart being pulled off of the prone Maeda, he and Thorne went at it throwing a flurry of Punches. Things were even as both men landed a number of shots until Hart blocked a couple of blows and reared back with one big shot.
Hart backed Thorne into a corner when Seth walloped him in the back with a chair. After knocking Hart to the mat, he helped Maeda pull the man back to his feet for a Maeda Fisherman Suplex.
The fans were booing the Heartbreakers who were busy celebrating in the ring when their little party was broken up by Hiroshima Nagasaki who slid into the ring.
A stiff Kick sent Seth out of the ring and a series of hard Chops and a Double Thrust to the throat did in Christian Maeda which in turn led to Thorne and Nagasaki mixing it up in the center of the ring. Nagasaki’s Spinning Back Kick caught Thorne on the chin and sent the big man down. Spinning on his heels, Nagasaki pointed at the Texas Heavyweight Champion before sliding his thumb across his throat. The Champ didn’t flinch, catching
Nagasaki’s leg on the Spinning Back Kick on his shoulder. Leaning his weight forward, Hart slammed Nagasaki down to the mat.
From a top mounted position, Hart started firing away with Punches until Seth dove off the top turnbuckle, catching Hart with an Elbow on the way down.
Maeda flipped back into the ring with a Slingshot Somersault Leg Drop. Things were getting out of hand as Thorne slid a table into the ring with Hart down holding his head.
Daye finally called for the bell when Thorne shoved him out of the way before setting the table up. The bell sounded the end of the match with the ref throwing it out.
** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Several wrestlers rushed out from the back to the ring ... Pep Preston, Emilio Ramirez, Paco, Skip Westervelt and Chip all hit the ring to break things up followed by several members of the PAW Security.
The fans were chanting “PAW!” when the camera broke away to the back where a pacing Offspring was being watched by partner Ryan Miller. Back and forth the agitated wrestler went until there was a knock at the door.
Jerking his braided head around, the masked man stopped his pacing. A second knock snapped him to attention before he approached the door.
Opening it, he was greeted by the sight of the woman in his mask and a lovely woman he hadn’t seen before.)
Offspring: What do you want, chica? Woman in the mask: I hate to be the bearer of bad news but with the promotion’s financial status of late, we’ve had to make several sacrifices ... which means we’re having to let some of the wrestlers go ...
Unfortunately, you and Mr. Miller are both victims of our financial crunch ... Offspring & Ryan Miller: WHAT!?!?!? Woman in the mask: I’m going to have to let you go ... I have to do what is best for the company.
On the bright side of things, Ms. Jessica Falcon here would like to offer you work in her promotion out of Arizona ...
(The young woman steps forward to speak to the baffled pair only to have the door slammed in her face as the credits begin to roll.)
