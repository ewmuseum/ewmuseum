9.10 Card

10 September 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera opens in the back of the PAW television studio on the new look, blonde and short haired President B.J. Perris walking down the hallway. Passing production people, he stops when he hears a familiar voice call out to him.
Turning, he sees the mysterious woman in the Offspring mask accompanied by Kat
Collins. With a sigh, he rolls his eyes in anticipation of what is about to come.)
Woman in mask: There you are ... I’ve been looking all over for you. Perris: Well, you’ve found me ... what can I do for you? (Lifting her clipboard and taking a quick glance, the woman levels her eyes back on Perris.)
Woman in mask: I’ve been going over this week’s figures and we’re over budget. We’ve been over with the last few shows and the new people you’ve brought in haven’t increased our revenue one bit ...
(Perris absentmindedly looks down at his wrist to wear a watch would sit ... if he actually wore one.)
Perris: Okay ... let’s see ... what can we do to fix things ... (Perris ponders his question for a few seconds before his eyes light up and he snaps his fingers.)
I’ve got it ... fire Emilio Ramirez! (After saying that, his smiles turns suddenly into a frown.) No wait ... I pay him and Paco alot less than Desperado. There you go ... fire
Desperado ...
(With a smile on his face, Perris turns to walk away when the woman protests.) Woman in mask: You can’t just fire him like that ... besides that’s still not enough. Perris: Okay ... Kat, you’re fired! There you go. That’s two paychecks saved. Now if you’ll excuse me, the show is about to start ... (Perris wheels around and leaves just as quickly as he came, leaving both women dumbfounded before fading away into Skip Westervelt rounding the corne. Coming up on the door, he saw it open. Someone was making an awful racket inside.
Running forward, he soon stopped dead in his tracks.) Skip Westervelt: Accccckk! (Ducking behind the corner, he barely avoided detection. Hart stormed out of his dressing room. Waiting for him to disappear around the corner, Skip ducked inside. He found chairs thrown all over, and Chip lying in a heap under a table!)
Chip! Buddy! (Skip removed the table from his partner and slapped him in an attempt to revive him.) Chip: Owww! Skip Westervelt: Sorry. What did that maniac want?!?! (Chip just shook his head. Skip looked him over. He wasn't busted open but he was in rough shape. Chip reached over and handed Skip a mangled video tape.)
Skip: *Gulp* Is that ... is that what I think it is? Justin Hart: It sure is ... (Skip wheeled around to see Justin Hart standing in the doorway.) Skip Westervelt: He didn't do anything! I'm the one who scratched the car! (Suddenly Skip cupped his hand over his mouth, realizing what he just admitted to.) Justin Hart: That's why I came back... (Hart lunged forward, kicking Skip in the face as the scene cut away. Leaving the pummeling, the camera moved away to another part of the television studio area and new arrival, Hiroshima Nagasaki.
Making his way with a determined gait, he stopped at the smiling face of program cohost Lisa Doyle.)
Lisa Doyle: Do you have a second? (Nagasaki frowned as he looked her up then down without saying a word.) I need to ask a favor. For weeks now ... long before you arrived, Christian
Maeda has been pawing at me and leering every chance he gets. He was even in my dressing room, waiting for me in nothing more than his boxers.
Imagine that. In his boxers no less. Nothing on but his boxers and his great big smile. No matter where I go, no matter what I do ... there he is. I turn around and there he is.
Can you imagine what that’s like? (Nagasaki’s eyes narrowed.) Of course you can’t ... you’re a man. Well, let me tell you, it’s nerve wracking. I can’t take it anymore. That’s where you come in ...
(With not even the slightest reaction from Nagasaki, Doyle stops talking and looks at him.)
Have you even understood a word I’ve said? DO ... YOU ... UNDERSTAND ... ME? (Nagasaki smiles as he reaches out and covers Doyle’s face with his big hand and pushes her to the floor as large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession.)
.... 5 ... .... 4 ... .... 3 ... .... 2 ... .... 1 ... (White Zombie's "More Human than Human" begins as flashes of the PAW stars in action are shown. Seth ... Skip Westervelt ... Johnny Green ... Justin Hart ...
Drake Russell ... Christian Maeda and finally the PAW Texas Heavyweight Champion, Jake Morgan, standing in the middle of the PAW ring, the belt raised over his head.)
After an explosion, the scene fades to black and the PAW logo disappears before fading back in on a smiling Aaron Starr standing in front of the PAW banner.)
Aaron Starr: Welcome back to another week of Public Access Wrestling ... I’m your host Aaron Starr and ...
(Starr stops as President B.J. Perris steps into view with a big smile on his face.) I’ve just been joined by promotion President B.J. Perris ... Perris: Aaron ... I’m just here to say a few things then I’m off to the back where I belong and we can get to the wrestling ...
(The fans started booing as the Offspring and Ryan Miller stepped out. The
Offspring swiped away the mic from Starr.)
Offspring: Why don’t you do us all a favir old man and head for the back right now and let the two greatest wrestlers to grace your little promotion have their time to address these so-called wrestling fans ...
(The boos grew louder as the masked Offspring gestured to Perris to “shoo”.) Go ahead now ... go back to your office and do what you do best ... run this promotion into the ground ...
(Perris stepped forward and grabbed the mic back from the masked Offspring. Held back by his partner, the Offspring relaxed from his ready to pounce posture.) Perris: Listen here you ... you might have come back and you might be showing us all this new side of you that we’ve never seen before but what I’m hearing the same old thing ...
You cried when Flint ran in and stole your title when you were the Texas Heavyweight Champion ... I had to listen to it then and now I’m hearing it again with the Tag Belt ...
You went on and on about the belt no longer being respected ... no longer being this promotion’s top prize if anyone could run in and steal the belt. I listened to what you had to say back then ... I listened and I did something about it. I made a promise to you that I was going to return the status of the belt ... and I did just that.
Now ... after you come back, you’re crying once again about not getting the belt that you took from Pep Preston ... or at least one of the belts ...
So now ... after I made a promise to you months ago and I uphold the decision in your so-called “win”, you’re crying about it ...
(Miller pulled Offspring back.) So ... let me tell you what I’m going to do ... right here, right now ... I’m sanctioning a match for the Tag straps ... tonight, the belts are on the line with you and Miller taking on Pep Preston and a partner of his choice ...
Now, win those belts ... (The Offspring took a step back after being granted what he wanted.) And ... before I forget, this is a threeway ... you can add the Heartbreakers to the mix ...
(The fans started booing loudly.) May the best team win ... (Perris handed the mic back to Aaron Starr and started to walk off when the team of Pep Preston and the Trashman stepped out from the back and made their way to the ring being led by Kimberly Davis who was carrying one of the belts.
With both teams entering the ring from opposite sides, the third team to the party exited the back and confidently strolled to the ring to a hearty chorus of boos. Both Maeda and Seth flipped into the ring while Black Thorne paced the outside. After all three teams were checked out by referee Peter Daye, the match was started.)

Offspring offered up a juicy Super Kick.
He dropped down and hooked a leg for a fast 2 count. Seth was into the ring with a Running Boot to break it up. Both Miller and the Trashman joined the fracas and touched off a brawl which spilled to the outside of the ring.
Miller and the Offspring worked over Seth while Black Thorne and Maeda took care of Preston. Trashman used his Singapore Cane to handle both Miller and
Offy only to be hammered down with a Clothesline from Seth. With Kim Davis using the tag belt, she dropped the California Boy.
Getting control back, Daye got things back into the ring as Pep was tossed back in and was joined by Seth and Miller. With the third down, Miller and Seth locked up in the center of the ring. Twisting in an Arm Bar, Seth changed positions with Miller several times before the Offspring’s partner was able to get behind his opponent and slam him into the mat with a Belly-to-Back Suplex.
Making a tag, Miller stepped out of the way just as the Offspring was back into the ring with a Split Legged Moonsault. He was able to cover for the near pinfall.
Preston charged in and nailed the Offspring with a Shoulder Butt but was also taken down by a Drop Kick. Seth and Maeda worked the double team magic with a
Double Suplex but were taken down by Miller’s Diving Double Clothesline before they could do the same to the Offspring.
Picked up and brought down across Miller’s knee with a Back Breaker, Maeda was on the recieving end of a Top Rope Leg Drop as the two continued to work their double teaming magic.
Miller pointed down at Seth before he and Offy jumped into the air and took
Black Thorne down with a Double Drop Kick, knocking him from the apron. Sharing a high five, they turned into a Double Drop Kick from the Heartbreakers that carried them out of the ring.
With a running start, both men dove out of the ring with Planchas ... getting different results. Maeda nailed Miller but the quicker Offspring side stepped the blow and helped jamming Seth into the ring railing. Black Thorne tried to take Spring’s head off with a Clothesline, making the timely save.
A Missile Drop Kick by the Trashman took him out and again, all seven men were on the outside brawling around the ring. Preston and Maeda worked over Miller while Trashman and Thorne traded blows. Seperated from the pack, the Offspring laid Seth back against the barrier so that he was looking up at the ceiling.
Climbing up onto the apron, he flew off and landed a Guillotine Leg Drop that snapped Seth over and into the fans that parted to give both men more room.
With Daye trying to regain control, Kim Davis was able to slip into the action, grabbing Offy by the hair poking out from the top of his mask. Catching him in the ear with a Right Cross stopped him momentarily and certainly caught his attention. Turning, he returned the favor and grabbed her by the hair pulling her closer.
Snapping her up and across the barrier, he fell backwards, putting her down with a Suplex. The fans were chanting “Holy Shit” as she landed hard on the floor.
Across the ring, Trashman had busted Black Thorne open with his cane but found himself in the big man’s grasp before being slammed down with a Choke Slam. He was more than happy to do the same ... opening Trash up with a stiff chair shot. Daye was in his face but Thorne pushed him out of the way as he moved in on Pep Preston.
Maeda booted Miller in the gut and attempted to lift him. Stuggling, Miller was able to drop free then wrap his arms around Maeda’s waist. Thinking quickly,
Maeda grabbed the ring barrier in front of him before he was lifted off his feet. Holding on for dear life, Maeda Kicked backwards, catching Miller low.
Reaching back, Maeda sat down into a Reverse Neck Breaker. Seeing his partner in trouble, the Offspring hit the ring railing and balanced himself as he ran along it’s thin surface. Whistling as he closed the distance caught Maeda’s notice. With the TV Champ turning, Offspring launched himself into the air and landed with his legs around his opponent’s neck. Falling backwards, he dumped Christian with a Hurricanrana.
Rising to his feet, Spring turned into the pummeling hands of Seth who had returned. With help from Black Thorne, they sent the masked man hard into the ring barrier. Leaning back against it and in trouble, the Offspring caught Seth in the kisser with a boot as Thorne Whipped him forward. As Seth fell to the ground, Thorne charged in and was sent over the railing into the first row with a Back Body Drop.
Pep Preston caught Offspring around the waist and dropped him with a German
Suplex into a Bridge and a 1 count before Miller made the save. Setting up a table, Preston was taken out of the picture with a Spiked Pile Driver through the table.
The pair brought boos from the fans as they celebrated before heading across the barrier for Seth and Maeda. With Security pushing it’s way into to keep the wrestlers and the fans seperated, Miller and Spring opened up on the
Heartbreakers fighting their way through the small but boisterious crowd. Black
Thorne turned out to be the equalizer as he helped the Heartbreakers get the upperhand and battle their way back to the ring.
With four men in the ring, Miller and Offspring reversed the Heartbreakers’ hold and sent them into the far ropes before taking them over with Back Body
Drops. From the corner, they landed a Double Leg Drop. Daye hit a 2 count as both men covered for the near pinfall.
With the Heartbreakers kicking out, Miller and Offspring pulled them up and were ready to send them across the ring had the favor done to them instead.
Across the ring they went and pulled down as Black Thorne tripped them up and pulled them to the outside.
Nailing Black Thorne, they were taken down with a Double Baseball Slide. Seth was ready to continue the war but was pulled away by Maeda and led around the ring towards the groggy Preston. Tossing him into the ring, they Whipped the helpless Pep into the far ropes. As returned, Preston went down from a Seth
Spinning Leg Kick and Maeda followed with a Moonsault off the middle rope.
Making the cover, he hooked a leg as Daye slapped out the 3 count. *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (The fans booed as Seth jumped up onto the ropes to celebrate while his partner grabbed up both belts that were ringside and made a dash for the back. Seeing
Maeda take off, Seth followed suit.
The Offspring helped Miller to his feet before complaining to the referee before the camera broke away to the back one of the dressing rooms, where a recent Christian Maeda vs. Seth match played on the television monitor.
Panning over to Hiroshima Nagasaki sitting on the couch absorbed in the match. After several long seconds, Nagasaki begins to laugh.) Hiroshima Nagasaki: Pitiful ... truly pitiful. Soon that belt will be mine. Just the first step to owning all of P.athetic

(rewinding the tape to a previous match, Nagasaki finally looks over at the camera and starts shouting.)
The H-Bomb is not worthy to challenge for the title, Maeda? The H-Bomb does not deserve to be in the same ring with you? You must be joking. My skills far surpass yours. I know it, PAW knows it .... and soon you will live it. Stupid gaijin, all you have done is upset me. Not a wise move. Tonight you will understand why and you will wish you had surrendered the belt when you were warned.
(Nagasaki turns away from the camera and pushes play on the remote. The television is showing a different Maeda match as it melts away into the “Man of
Destiny” Drake Russell admiring himself and his long overcoat in front of a mirror.
Looking at himself from behind his mirrored shades, a smile crosses his lips before he turns and walks out of the room. With the door closing behind him, someone comes into view wearing a PAW shirt ... one of the production crew.
As the man recoils his cable, he shakes his head, looking into the camera.) Production Crewman: Doesn’t he know it’s been over a 100 degrees here for the past few weeks?
(Laughter is heard from behind the camera as it fades into B.J. Perris talking with Justin Hart in the hallway.)
Perris: Ok ... look, plots and scripts aside, you know why you're here, Hart. You walked out on a show. And so far, I haven't even gotten an explanation. So
I'm giving you a chance to tell me why you did it.
(Hart leaned forward.) Justin Hart: That night, earlier in the show, I got a long-distance call from my mother. She told me that my father was rushed to the emergency room ... he had suffered a stroke. I wanted to go see him, but I wasn't exactly falling over myself trying to get there right away. I figured he'd be okay with time.
(Perris scratched his chin.) Perris: I see ... (Hart hung his head.) Justin Hart: He ... died 2 days later. I didn't even have a chance to say good bye... (Perris walked over and places a hand on Hart's shoulder.) Perris: I'm sorry. (Hart wiped the tears that had welled up in his eyes and nodded.) Justin Hart: Yeah. Perris: Under the circumstances, I don't think participating this week is a good idea. You're not focused mentally. I suggest taking some time off.
(Hart waved a hand, refusing looking upward, then back at Perris.) Justin Hart: No ... I need to do something to help take my mind off this. Concentrating on the job can help me in that case. (Perris nodded in agreement. The two men were about to part when they were shoved aside by Hiroshima Nagasaki who walked past.)
HEY! (Turning and seeing Hart, Nagasaki burned holes with his stare.) Hey, the "cold stare" is my gimmick ... get your own. (Nagasaki walks closer, laughing at him.) Hiroshima Nagasaki: You are startled so easily. Like all Americans, you act as though you are a fool. Perhaps that is the case, gaijin?
(Hart is shocked at the sudden insult. Shock soon transfers into anger... and you know how Hart is...)
Justin Hart: You wanna run that by me again, rice-patty boy? (Nagasaki pokes a finger into Hart's chest. Hart responds by slapping it away. Soon the two of them are nose to nose.) Hiroshima Nagasaki: My business is not with you, gaijin. When your time comes, you will be forced to surrender... UNCONDITIONALLY!

Westervelt and Chip ... a rather roughed up pair, waited inside the ring.

Drag.

Nagasaki wasn’t ready to cover.

Nagasaki strolled casually to the back to a chorus of boos.
Daye stood there with the Extremist flat on his back and both Skip and Chip out of action. Starting a count, Daye was up to 8 when Skip’s head popped up over the apron, looking into the ring. With no Nagasaki about, Skip dove into the ring and rolled up the Extremist for the 3 count.

Daye as well.

Back in the ring, Hart and Russell battled for control. From Arm Bar to Hammerlock to Go Behind, they blocked and reversed each other’s holds. Russell’s Eye Rake caught Hart by surprise but he was able to break free of a
Hip Toss attempt. Russell broke up a try at an Abdominal Stretch but wasn’t so lucky as Hart fell back with a Side Russian Leg Sweep. Throwing his weight back, Hart’s head bounced off the canvas.
Both men were down while on the outside of the ring, Hiroshima Nagasaki grabbed the ref by the seat of his pants and flung him inside. Seeing both men down,
Daye crawled over, still feeling the effects of being hit by Morgan, and slapped out a count on the canvas.
Rolling to his back, Daye called for the bell. *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (The crowd was silent as they watched Daye pull himself to his feet and get the
Texas Heavyweight Title belt. Standing over both men, he reached down and grabbed at an arm ... Drake Russell’s.
Lifting the arm, there was a small cheer just before he pushed it aside and reached down once again, this time lifting Justin Hart’s arm. Dropping the belt across Hart’s chest, Daye fell backwards against the ropes and called for help from the back as the credits started to roll.)
