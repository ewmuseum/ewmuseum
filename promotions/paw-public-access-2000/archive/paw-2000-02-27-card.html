2.27 Card

27 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera opens on the Public Access Wrestling red, white and black banner with the Tag Team Champions, Justin Hart and Johnny Green. Green, with his tag belt over his shoulder, steps forward.)
<Johnny Green> Do you remember this, Jason? (Green smiles as he taps the belt lightly.) Do you remember what it was like to stand side by side with a partner ... with both men at the top of the promotion? Do you remember that?
(Green's smile disappears.) Well ... I remember how it was ... I remember ... (He looks down for a second before snapping his head back up, a grin stretching across his face.)
That was in the past, Jason ... things are different now and though you stand at the top of the heap as the Texas Heavyweight Champion ... today, this man ...
(Green slaps Hart on the shoulder.) ... and I are going to show you and your "mystery partner" what true champions look like ... tell him Justin ...
(Hart moved forward with an angered look on his face.) <Justin Hart> That's right, Flint ... today we're going to kick your <BLEEP>ing ass ... tomorrow, it's your Texas Heavyweight Title!
(The pair walked off as large red numbers burst onto the screen in rapid succession.) ... 5 ... ... 4 ... ... 3 ... ... 2 ... ... 1 ... (Rob Zombie's "More Human than Human starts to play loudly as clips of Public
Access Wrestling 2000's Superstars in action passes ... Justin Hart ... Jake
Morgan ... Neo Draven and Sasha ... Brian Cage and finally, the Texas
Heavyweight Champion, Jason Flint, standing in the center of the ring lifting his title belt skyward ...
With an explosion, the scenes disappear from the screen and are replaced by the logo of Public Access Wrestling 2000 as it moves forward, filling the screen.
The song suddenly stops and with a smooth transition, fades out ... With a just as smooth fade in, the camera pans the wrestling ring surrounded by a vocal but small group of fans before passing over the small studio set.
Standing off to the side, in front of the PAW Banner, host Aaron Starr smiled into the camera.)
<Aaron Starr> Welcome ladies and gentlemen to Public Access Wrestling 2000! We have three big matches this evening with the PAW Tag Team Titles up for grabs in the main event ... but before getting to the wrestling action, Lisa Doyle is in the back with the Texas Heavyweight Champion, Jason Flint ...
(Looking off, Starr continues.) Lisa? Can you hear me? (Fading into the backstage area, Lisa Doyle is seen standing with Flint and the rest of his entourage.)
<Lisa Doyle> Aaron ... I'm with the Champion, Jason Flint ... (Doyle turns towards a smiling Flint.) On everyone's mind is a single simple question ... who is going to be your partner for tonight's Tag Team Match against Justin Hart and your former partner, Johnny Green?
<Jason Flint> Lisa ... Lisa ... Lisa ... The time is here and the place is now. Its time for the moment of truth, who is my tag team partner? If Mr. Hart had it his way, he would defend those worthless pieces of gold against me and my partner of my choosing. That's if he had his way, but no no no, he went out and got himself a partner. A GOOD
Partner, the other half of the Greatest Tag Team of Extreme Wrestling, Johnny
Green. A partner whose going to carry Hart to whatever outcome is going to happen today.
But I digress, back to the issue of my partner today. Hmmmm.....who could it be, could it be Kimberly Davis?
(Kimberly Davis smiles as Flint taps her on the shoulder.) Or perhaps one of the State Club Boyz? (The boys crack their knuckles while grinning for the camera.) No....no, unfortunately, its not any of them. My partner is the one......the only.....the BOSS, BJ PERRIS!!
(Fading out on a smiling Flint and a stunned Lisa Doyle, the camera opened in the ring as referee Peter Daye called for the bell in the first match.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Desperado shot across the ring, going straight for Brian Cage. The two men exchanged Punches and Chops with neither gaining a visible advantage. Hammering each other around the ring, they never saw the smaller Emilio Ramirez flying at them with a Slingshot Clothesline until it was too late.
The first one back to his feet, Cage pulled up Ramirez and slammed a meaty
Forearm into his chest before sending flying out of the ring. On the attack,
Cage focussed all his energy on the masked Desperado, punishing him with a
Superkick into a Neckbreaker. Daye slapped out a 2 count.
Dismissing Ramirez was a mistake as he changed the tempo of the match with a
Missile Dropkick that sent Cage over the top rope and out onto the floor. After a Leg Scissor Takedown on Desperado, Ramirez went after a groggy Cage. Leaving the ring with a Baseball Slide, Ramirez was surprised as Cage swatted his legs away blocking the move. Ramirez hit the floor very hard and was holding the back of his head as Cage came in with a series of Stomps.
Desperado, came off the apron with a Double Axe Handle stopping Cage in his tracks. A Snapmare into a Reverse Chinlock had the referee watching closely for either a Chokehold from Desperado or a submission from Cage ... neither came as
Cage powered his way to his feet and broke the hold with a couple of Elbows to the stomach.
Leading Desperado around the ring, Cage slammed his head into the ring steps before going off to grab a chair. Returning, he found Emilio Ramirez Stomping
Desperado. Ramirez went down from the first Chairshot followed by Desperado from the second one. After throwing the chair into the ring, Cage rolled
Ramirez back into the ring. Dragging him up the ropes, Cage punched an arm into the air before dropping Ramirez headfirst onto the chair with a DDT. Rolling over, Ramirez, Cage got the 3 count.
Peter Daye was about to call for the bell when he was spun around by Desperado who wanted things to continue ... Last Man Standing. The fans chanting "LET
THEM GO!" seemed to influence Daye who continued the match.
Desperado turned in time to take a blast over the head from the chair, dropping like a ton of bricks. With Desperado down, Cage pulled up Ramirez and sent him into the far ropes. On the return, Cage snatched him up around the waist and dropped him down on top of Desperado with a Gutwrench Powerbomb.
The fans were cheering as Cage rose to his feet and raised his arms into the air. A Shortarm Clothesline dropped the masked man before Cage set up the chair a few feet out of the corner. Draping Desperado across the chair, Cage left him to climb the ropes. Jumping from the top, Cage landed a Leg Drop that crushed both Desperado and the chair.
Daye, checking on an unmoving Desperado, was pushed out of the way as Cage dropped down, hooked a leg and waited for the 3 count. Desperado never budged.
It was over. *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (After pulling away from Daye, Cage left the ring as the fans cheered only to be confronted on the outside of the ring by Neo Draven's valet, Sasha. The raven-haired beauty showed no fear as she stepped right up to Cage and pointed an accusing finger in his face.
Cage didn't look amused as Sasha gave him a piece of her mind until he had enough and pushed her out of the way. Not to be detered, Sasha continued the verbal abuse until he turned around again and started in with a few words of his own, then shoving her roughly to the ground.
Cage continued to berate her, never seeing who came out of the back ... a most unexpected ally in the form of an overweight Chip. Gone were the black tights with the words "Texas Terrors" along the legs, having been replaced by purple tights with "Dead Domain" in white lettering down the legs.
A Kick to the back caught Cage by surprise followed by a Double Axe Handle
Smash. Cage turned and leveled Chip with a Low Blow before dropping from the ring apron with a liberated garbage can. Cage tossed aside the badly dented can before laying into Chip with his boots.
A disgusted but victorious Brian Cage stormed off as the camera left the ring area for the back where Lisa Doyle was standing by with PAW President B.J.
Perris.) <LIsa Doyle> B.J., your thoughts on being picked as Jason Flint's mystery partner for today's tag team match?
(The long haired man sighed heavily as he rolled his eyes and shook his head.) <Perris> What can I say, Lisa? This is just another of Flint's stunts ... <LIsa Doyle> Yes, but are you going to honor his wishes and fight alongside him today? Even though you haven't stepped into the ring in months, following your brutal barbed wire match with the Offspring ... you are still listed as an active wrestler ... even getting a ranking ...
<Perris> Yeah ... I may not like it but I will honor it and fight as his partner. (The camera moved back to the ring where Aaron Starr was slipping through the ropes rto the outside of the ring after announcing the participants of the next match. After checking the boots of both Jake Morgan and Neo Draven, referee Dan
Glass called for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Morgan was having a few words with Sasha when Draven came in from behind with a
Clothesline. Following a Kick to the head of Morgan, Draven rolled him up for a
1 count.
After being pulled to his feet, Morgan took command, laying into Draven's midsection with a barrage of Punches before sending him across the ring, hitting with a Spinebuster on the return. With Draven writhing in pain, Morgan dropped a Knee, hitting canvas as Draven rolled out of the way.
Bouncing quickly off the ropes, Draven hit a Somersault Legdrop, getting a 1 count out of it. Pulling Morgan to his feet, Draven Raked his eyes then Punched him across the ring into a corner. Grabbing hair, Draven repeatedly rammed
Morgan's head into the turnbuckle as the fans counted out each blow. On the last blow, Morgan blocked it, spun out of the corner and drove Draven's head into the turnbuckle before dropping him with a Belly-to-Back Suplex.. Morgan went for the pin getting a 2 count until Glass tapped him on the shoulder pointing out Draven's foot on the ropes. Sasha was standing nearby with a smile on her face.
After pointing out Sasha to Glass, Morgan went back to work, nailing Draven with a Samoan Drop. Glass slapped out a 2 count when Draven rolled his shoulder up. Lifted off his feet, Draven was able to roll out of Morgan's Suplex attempt and hit him in the back with a Knee. Grabbing him from behind, Draven hit the mat with an Inverted DDT, getting a 2 count of his own.
Morgan rolled to the outside of the ring shaking his head. He didn't have long to catch his breath as Draven came flying over the top rope, taking him down with a Tope. The fans were cheering with both men down on the floor as Security moved to keep the fans cleared out of the way.
Morgan sent Draven careening into the ring steps before lifting him into the air and dropping him throatfirst onto the ring barrier. Throwing Draven over, the fans parted as Morgan climbed the barrier and landed a Leg Drop. Pushing
Draven into the barrier, Morgan started Punching him before Sasha came over.
Looking up, Morgan was greeted with a Fireball to the face. A writhing Morgan, fell back onto the floor.
Grabbing a chair, Draven cleared the fans as he set it up. Walking away, he took a running start, hitting the chair and landing a Senton. Pulling the chain from his neck, Draven wrapped it around his fist before pulling Morgan to his feet and throwimng him back against the barrier. With the chain, Draven opened
Morgan up as he pummeled his face. Sasha was laughing madly as Morgan took a beating, still clutching his burnt face.
After being dumped over the ring barrier, Morgan was rolled back into the ring. Draven remained on the outside, pulling a table from underneath the ring and setting it up. Climbing back into the ring, Draven pulled Morgan up the ropes but was thrown to the mat. Morgan dropepd and Elbow from the second rope before picking Draven up and lifting him for a Vertical Suplex. Displaying his strength, Morgan held his opponent in the air for a long time before dropping him across the ropes. With Draven across the ropes, Morgan slowly climbed the ropes, dropping down with a Guillotine Leg Drop that brought a gasp from the fans. Not finished, Morgan left the ring to move the table into place and set up a second table.
While Jake was busy with the tables, Sasha was in the ring trying to revive
Draven. Morgan was pulling the second table into place when Draven came flying over the top rope, landing on Morgan's shoulders and taking him over with a
Hurricanrana.
A groggy and bleeding Morgan was first to his feet, picking up Draven and lifting him into the air out a Standing Head Scissors. Running towards the tables, Morgan put Draven through the first table with a Running Powerbomb.
Morgan had Sasha jump onto his back as he pulled himself out of the broken table. Throwing her off his back, Morgan rolled Draven back into the ring.
Pulling Draven to his feet, Morgan caught a Chop to the throat before being
Whipped into the far ropes and taken down with a Spinning Leg Lariat on the return. A Knee Drop off the middle ropes had Morgan in trouble as he was then
Whipped hard into the corner where he took another Leg Lariat, crashing to the mat.
Across where Draven was pulling Morgan to his feet, Chip was up on the apron with a raised fist motioning to Draven to send his opponent. Spinning the bleeding Morgan, Draven was caught by surprise as he was sent across the ring
... right into Chip who was sent sprawling to the floor.
After putting Draven down with a Powerslam, Morgan tossed him to the outside of the ring. Dropping him across the table still on the outside, Morgan climbed back onto the apron and dove off, Splashing Draven through the table and taking the 3 count.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (The fans were cheering as Morgan got to his feet and limped away as Sasha argued with the ref. The camera panned the fans as the Security moved in and cleaned up the area around the ring before once again moving into the back somewhere ...
A shirtless PAW President B.J. Perris, in his fighting gear of boots and black sweatpants and taped fists was talking to someone who was just out of camerashot. Perris seemed to be listening as his head nodded up and down.)
<Perris> I agree ... I think this is for the best ... right, there is at least some ... history involved ...
(Leaving the back for the ring, the camera came upon Jason Flint being led to the ring by his entourage of Kimberly Davis and the State Club Boyz. There was an even mix of cheering and booing for them as the Texas Heavyweight Champion entered the ring.
As the champion strolled around the ring, as Aaron Starr announced the opponents.) <Aaron Starr> And making their way to the ring ... at a combined weight of 489 pounds ... the Public Access Wrestling Tag Team Champions ... here is Justin
"Street Soldier" Hart and Johnny "The Machine" Green!
(A smiled crossed Hart's lips as the fans cheered his and Green's entrance. Entering the ring, Hart and Green high-fived before going over last minute plans as B.J. Perris and Mean Darlene walked out to the ring.)
<Perris> You know, Flint ... I've been giving this alot of thought since you announced me as your tag team partner this evening ...
(The crowd quieted to a near whisper as Perris continued.) ... and though I said yes to being your partner ... I've been thinking about the "What If's" ... what if you and I should beat Hart and Green in this match and take the tag belts ...
What if? What if we win? What if we manage to beat them and walk out of here as the tag champs? Would I be able to wrestle week in and week out to defend these titles? Would I be able to stand by your side against any challengers ... or would it be the case of one man again having to defend these belts by himself because I have other duties within the promotion to attend?
With that all in mind, I'm going to have to turn you down, Flint ... (Flint's eyes went big as the fans booed.) But ... let me say this ... as I wasn't about to allow Justin Hart to defnd the tag belts by himself against you ... I won't leave you alone either ... so I went out and handpicked a partner for you ... someone that you are familiar with and you might even say, have a little bit of a history with ...
(Perris stopped as the fans started chanting ... "PEP! PEP! PEP!". Perris waved them off.) No ... it's this man ... (Perris motioned to the back when Skip Westervelt stepped out. Standing with his hands on his hips, Skip looked out at the fans. Inside the ring, Flint dropped his head onto Kimberly's shoulder as she comforted him. In the opposite corner, Hart and Green were openly laughing.
Walking out and grabbing the mic away from Perris, Skip waited for everyone to quiet down.)
<Skip Westervelt> I have one little thing to say ... It's Go-Time! (Westervelt climbed into the ring as Flint's entourage was ushered out by the referee.) <Perris> I almost forgot ... in order to insure that there is no "help" from outside sources during the match ... I've called upon a "One Man Crew" to serve as the enforcer ... just in case anyone not involved in the match wants to get involved ...
("Blind" by Korn started to play as Brian Cage, carrying a barbed wire wrapped
2x4 over his shoulder walked out to the ring. Flint was arguing loudly with ref
Peter Daye only to get a little smirk from the official. Stepping back, Daye called for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Hart was in first and motioning for Flint who immediately sent in Skip. Loosening up, Skip danced around Hart looking like a boxer as he flicked out jabs that missed their mark. Eyeing Skip, Hart lashed out with Jabs of his own, connecting and snapping his head back.
Grabbing an arm, Hart sent Skip across the ring with a well sunk in Arm Drag. Rolling to his feet, Skip popped up only to be sent back down from a Forearm by
Green for getting too close to the enemy corner. Staggering across the ring,
Skip walked right into Hart's Boot to the midsection. Throwing himself back against the ropes, Hart grabbed Westervelt's hair on the return and dropped down driving his face into the mat.
Hart rolled him over for the pin, having it broken on a 2 count as Flint rushed into the ring. With Flint being ushered out of the ring, Hart tagged in Green and they Double Suplexed Skip Westervelt. Skip tried to crawl away to his corner only to be dragged by his feet back to the center of the ring. Flipping him over, Green threatened to Stomp a pleading Skip in the "jewels". Daye warned Green who instead went down with a Leg Drop before tagging Hart back in.
With a Double Whip into the ropes, Skip was pushed into the air for a Gorilla
Press and a following Slam. Hitting the ropes, Hart came in with a driving
Elbow Drop. Flint was pleading with Skip to make a tag. Pushing Skip into his corner, Hart smacked him in the chest with Reverse Knife Hand chops that sounded painful. Peter Daye came in and warned Green who was holding
Westervelt's arms as his partner slapped away. Flint charged into the ring but was stopped by the ref.
Curtis and Tony of the State Club Boyz went up onto the ring apron but were chased away by the watchful Brian Cage.
Hart tagged in Green before Skip was sent into the far ropes and taken down with a Double Clothesline. Inside the ring, Green was pointing at Flint before
Whipping Skip back into his own corner where he was able to make a tag for the first time.
Flint warily entered the ring as the two began to circle, locking up in the center of the ring. They struggled to gain control before pushing each other off. Again they locked up and again they broke. Green, nodding his head, raised his hand calling for a Test of Strength. One hand then two as the men went chest to chest. Flint seemed to gain control as he forced Green back but then it changed as Flint was pushed back. Again, they broke the evenly matched hold.
Circling, they locked up and Green gained control with a Side Headlock. From this position, Flint reversed it only to have it countered by Green. Without ever breaking contact, Flint and Green blocked, reversed and countered each other's moves ... Arm Bars, Wristlocks, Front Facelocks, Side Headlocks,
Hammerlocks, Half Nelsons, Sit Outs, Arm Drags, Hip Tosses, Floatovers ... technical move after technical move, they went back and forth, evenly matched with neither man to get control of the other for too long. Finally, they broke contact.
Stepping back from each other, the fans cheered loudly as Green gave a slight nod of the head to his opponent which was in turn returned. With a shrug of his shoulders, Green stepped back and tagged in Hart. Hart was wringing his hands as he came into the ring, ready to tear Flint's head off.
They locked up in the center with Flint getting the upperhand trying to lock on an Abdominal Stretch. Without panic, Hart countered with a Side Russian Leg
Sweep. Both bouncing to their feet, Flint was taken over with an Arm Drag but rolled to his feet. Jumping up onto the ropes, Flint threw himself backwards with a Bodypress, hitting Hart squarely but rather than falling backwards, Hart caught him, bringing him down across his knee with a Sidebreaker. Dropping
Flint to the mat, he dropped for a 1 count from Daye.
First calling for a Timeout then ducking under a Punch, Flint went behind and caught Hart around the waist dumping him with a German Suplex. Rolling to his feet with Hart still in his grasp, Flint dropped him with a second German
Suplex, this time Bridging into a pin. A reluctant Daye dropped to the canvas and slapped out a 2 count.
Tagging in Skip, Hart was sent into the far ropes and finished off with a
Double Roundhouse Kick. Lifting Hart onto his shoulders in a seated position,
Flint walked him over towards the corner where Skip Westervelt was climbing.
Jumping off, Skip landed on Hart's shoulders and took him over with a Hurricanrana that had everyone on their feet as the ring shook. Skip almost had the 3 count but Hart was somehow able to get a shoulder up in time. Whipping Hart into the corner where Flint was, Westervelt turned and taunted Green who rushed into the ring but was stopped by Daye. Flint hammered
Forearms into Hart's chest before Skip returned ... landing a Leg Lariat.
Jumping up onto the ropes, Skip dropped down with a Knee Drop, using the ropes to propel himself back up then down with several more Knees to the head and chest of the slumped Hart.
Pulling Hart to his feet, Skip jumped up on the ropes in the corner. Holding
Hart's arm up in the air, Skip took a little stroll along the top rope, showing great balance. Jumping off the ropes, Westervelt Scissored Hart's arm, spinning him down to the mat. Working the wrist, Skip was forced to break the hold as
Hart grabbed the ropes.
Skip had a few words for the ref as Hart tagged in Green who came in with a
Clothesline. Pushing Skip into his corner, Green began Stomping him until he slid down to the mat. Pulling Skip up by his hair, Green hit with several big
Right Hands before tagging Hart back in.
Walking back across the ring and pointing a finger at Flint, Hart turned back towards Skip and ran at him, leaping into the air and landing a Corner Splash.
Staggering out of the corner, Skip fell flat on his face. Stepping over the body of Westervelt, Hart motioned for Flint to come in as he grabbed both arms and pulled back. Leaning his body back, Hart pulled Skip as far back as possible. Dropping down in front of both men, the ref watched closely. He didn't have to watch long as Flint charged into the ring and slammed his boot into Hart's head, knocking him from Westervelt. Flint left the ring as Hart charged after him but was stopped by the ref.
Westervelt hit a Low Blow on Hart, knocking him to his knees then tagging in
Flint. Hart was lifted across Flint's shoulders in a Fireman's Carry before being dropped forward into a Gutbuster Drop. Flint got a 2 count before Hart kicked out.
Flint was up on the ropes listening to the fans when he was pulled down from behind and rolled up in a Small Package barely kicking out before a 3 count.
Back on their feet, the two battled back and forth, landing big moves against each other ... Hart's Leaping DDT, Atomic Drop and Full Nelson Suplex nearly put Flint away who was game enough to come back with moves of his own ...
Tilt-A-Whirl Slam, Underhook Back Body Drop and Arm Trap Leglock. The fans cheered as the two continued their assault inside and outside of the ring trading Punches as well as going to the air with Suicide Dives, Planchas and Splashes.
Brian Cage moved in after Flint put Hart through a table as the State Club Boyz closed in but thought better of it as the 2x4 was waved in their direction, backing away as fast as possible.
Hart was pulled to his feet but was able to hit Flint with an Uppercut and followed with a Knee Lift before rolling Flint back into the ring. Referee
Peter Daye climbed back into the ring but was pulled off the ring apron by
Johnny Green, hitting his head on the floor.
Hart Whipped Flint into the far ropes and on the return, both men went down from a Double Clothesline. The fans were cheering loudly trying their best to get both or either wrestler to their feet. On the outside of the ring, Kimberly
Davis did her best to get Flint to his feet as she pounded the ring apron with her fists.
B.J. Perris was on the outside of the ring attending to referee Peter Daye as
Mean Darlene headed to the back for help.
Inside the ring, Justin Hart rose slowly to his feet and wobbled, using the ropes to steady himself, jarring Flint with a Boot to the head before rolling him over and wrapping him with the Urban Lockdown. Flint clawed his way forward trying to reach the ropes but he was too far ... there was no way that he was going to reach them before he passed out from the pain.
Perris and the EMT's lifted Daye and carried him to the back followed by Mean
Darlene. Hart roared out as he leaned back trying his best to snap Flint in two. Skip was reaching out towards his partner as the fans continued to cheer. Hart's partner Johnny Green came into the ring and out of nowhere leveled Hart with a
Clothesline from behind and breaking the hold. The crowd was silenced and shocked as Skip Westervelt rushed in and was taken down by Green's Roundhouse
Kick.
Pulling Flint to his feet, Green led him over to the corner. Slowly making hisway to the top, FLint propelled himself through the air, landing a Corkscrew
Splash. After a Tilt-A-Whirl Piledriver from Green on Skip Westervelt, both men were on top of their opponents with a pinning situation ... the only problem
... no referee. That wasn't a problem for long as the "Electric Referee" Dan
Glass dove into the ring ans slapped out a double pin, calling for the bell.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Glass grabbed up the belts and rolled back into the ring and handed one to both Green and Flint before raising their hands in victory. Kimberly Davis and the SCB's hit the ring to celebrate the victory and the reunion of the promotion's most successful tag team ... Mean Team 2000!
The State Club Boyz started Kicking the downed Hart when Brian Cage hit the ring and cleared it with his barbed wire wrapped 2x4. On the outside as Green and Davis led the battered Flint out of the ring, the SCB's taunted Cage as the program came to an end.)
