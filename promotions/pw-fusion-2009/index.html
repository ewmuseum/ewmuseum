A one-author Portuguese booking, founded as PWF and renamed Professional Wrestling Fusion after its first show, whose main event was won when a smaller wrestler's chair-shot interference let "The Legend" Bruno finish Johny "The Killer". Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The promotion's own founding post, 5 November 2009, frames its own history as starting the day after its founder watched a WWE Royal Rumble and decided, with a friend, to build “a mini amateur wrestling company” of their own. Short, at first, for Professional Wrestling Federation, it named Bruno Alves its president, João Paulo its general manager, a weekly show called Nitro, and an eight-wrestler roster under one still-vacant PWF Title.

Its roster filled out over the following two weeks: Johny “The Killer” and Tiago “The” Master signed first, out of Aveiro and Lisbon; “The Legend” Bruno followed, billed as a former champion of an amateur promotion out of Maia; and “The Mini Dude” Bé, Cláudio and Hélder “The Player” rounded it out days before the first show. A sponsorship deal with an auto-repair company, “Mecarranjo,” and a second championship, the XTag Titles, arrived in the same stretch.

That first show ran 14 November 2009 before a reported crowd of about 100: Pedro Ribeiro beat Vítor Martins by TKO, Tiago “The” Master beat a two-man field with a Cobra Clutch on Cláudio, and the main event turned on interference — with the referee down, Bé knocked Johny “The Killer” out with a steel chair, letting “The Legend” Bruno finish him with a top-rope Double Arm DDT. The promotion's own post-show note cites a 0.2 rating and a next show meant to crown its first titleholder.

That next show never surfaces in what survives. After “health problems” and a gap of more than a month, the promotion returned on 22 December 2009 renamed Professional Wrestling Fusion, keeping its own initials, and announced a battle royal to finally crown a champion at a show still to come. No later post, result, or title change is on record.
