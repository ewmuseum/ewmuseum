Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

TCW: A Quiet Retirement is one author's diary of Total Championship Wrestling, the CornellVerse company that began as the Hollyweird Grappling Company, kept in a Total Extreme Wrestling game from the 2008 data. Its narrator, Horatio Dangerous, is a retired wrestler who takes over the booking from Joel Bryant after the Just Another Day? pay-per-view in March 2008 at the owner Tommy Cornell's call. The diary is dressed as a book from TCW's own books line, with a foreword dated January 2011 and a page of notes, and its author's preface says it follows the standard CornellVerse data.

Each show is a script of angles, promos and matches with a letter rating for every match and a Show Rating, preceded by a preview post with a prediction key and followed by prize notes for the readers who called the card, with the narrator's dated entries between shows: a roster review in February, backstage scenes, discipline, his Spotlight Interviews and his own commentary seat.

The first ten shows, April and May 2008, set Tommy Cornell's Syndicate, which holds almost all the gold, against Painful Procedure and its frontman Troy Tornado. Cornell beats Tornado at Where Angels Fear To Tread; a Board representative, Mayhem Midden, arrives over Cornell's protest to make the matches the fans want; at Total Mayhem 13 Edd Stone's All Action Championship gives the Syndicate all four belts for one match, before Koshiro Ino takes the International Championship from Wolf Hawkins, and Tornado beats Cornell only by disqualification. Eric Tyler campaigns for respect for tradition against Aaron Andrews, and American Buffalo pays off a twelve-year-old betrayal on Tyson Baine.

The diary's first ten shows are served here whole, as seven pages (the front matter, four months and two pay-per-views), with the roster, titles and reigns they establish. The saved copy runs to 2,540 pages; the rest is collected and not yet served.
