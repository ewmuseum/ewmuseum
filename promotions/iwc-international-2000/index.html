Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The International Wrestling Council ran a New Year's Eve pay-per-view, Countdown 99, in the last days of 1999, and its own record shows a full year already behind it before that broadcast — a July 2000 recruitment post recalls going "one year not ranked on the RSPWF" before hoping for a ranking in the new one. No dated founding document survives.

Through 2000 the federation ran a rotating slate of numbered supercards — Armageddon, Wrestlefest, Blaze & Glory — built around its own on-screen president, and carried an International, a Continental and a Television championship of its own. A co-presidency briefly widened the office in early 2000 before reverting to a single president by autumn.

The federation ran on a bi-weekly-to-weekly schedule, hosted its own archive of cards and roleplays on a free web page, and returned more than once through the year to open recruitment specifically for women wrestlers, citing a thin division as its own persistent gap.

The last held document is a 13 December 2000 announcement of the year-end Fall From Grace pay-per-view, on which the operator signs as "FORMER President" — a change of leadership the surviving record does not otherwise explain. No closing statement for the federation itself survives.
