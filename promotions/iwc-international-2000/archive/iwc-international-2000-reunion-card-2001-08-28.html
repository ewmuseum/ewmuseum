IWC Reunion Card announced!!!

August 28, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NIGHT ONE 9/29/01 Below is a list of participants in a mini-tournament within the cards, similar to Hart of Champions if you can remember that. The winner of the tournament gets a $100,000 purse. Also, thanks to the folks at the
MGM Grand the winner will receive a weeklong stay in one of their 1,500-square-feet Marquee sweets. The first round and second round matches will be normal matches. The final one…well that’ll be a surprise.
First Round Mike Gillis vs. Throm the Black “The New Legend” Kenneth Morlock vs. Thomas “Roman” Lawerence Laramee vs. Brian Taylor Tommy Grayson vs. Tyler “American Made” Johnson HaRdCoRe Battle Royal Ian Blade vs. Corwin Haves vs. Hysteria vs. Taylor Knox vs. Seth “The Gambit” Alexander A chance once and for all for IWC and CAWF’ers to prove once and for all who is the toughest of the tough. Add in to the pot that this is taking place in the City of Sin and you have the makings for the likes of a match no one has seen before. Throw in the talent that is involved, the results could be catastrophic…for Las Vegas that is.
Grudge Match Reed “The Real Man” Harrington vs. Christian “The Monster” Bridges Bridges took Harrington under his wing and this is how “The Real Man” repays him? “The Monster” taught Reed everything he knew…or did he? Does the teacher have one more ace up his sleeve? Or will the student become the teacher?
PoE Self-Destructs Ladder Match Hitman & Talon vs. Trio You read that right. Arguably the most dominant stable in CAWF history has decided to give the fans something they’ve always wanted but never witnessed
before, PoE vs. PoE. Who really is the Perfection of Execution? No dissention within the PoE ranks or bad blood here, just the makings for a jolly good show.
MAIN EVENT Iron Man Match Spikyjim vs. Shawn “The Scorpion” Viper The second most requested match for the card. No Establishment or Rudeboys to call for calvalry. This is just to see who the better man is. No gimmicks, no angles, just unadulterated carnage. Time to sack up or pack up.
NIGHT TWO 9/30/01 CAWF Tribute Match Manabeing vs. Dr. D. Capitator These two are CAWF legends. Manabeing was the cornerstone of the CAWF and Dr. D was the resident enforcer of the CAWF. What will happen when these two get in the ring? Has all these years taken something out of them, or just given them more time for their anger to stew?
Semi-Final Match Winner of Grayson/Johnson vs. Winner of Laramee/Taylor Texas Death Match Jim “The Crusher” Johnson vs. Ground Zero This match just proves that old feuds never die, and if they do…they die hard. Can the Southern roughneck defeat The Crusher? Or is it time to take out the trash?
Semi-Final Match Winner of Lawerence/Morlock vs. Winner of Throm/Gillis Grudge Match Solo vs. Adam Krist This one is for bragging rites and to finally pit Da Mang against “The Cutting Edge” in the squared circle. The two have had a checkered past covering two feds and never stepped into the ring mono y mono.
Finals for the Tournament Grudge Match Loaded vs. Brian “Thunder” Storm Is it time for the number one protégé to take what is rightfully his? What is going to happen when the unstoppable force from IWC sees that there is a Storm on the horizon? This match is just a powder keg waiting to go off and when it does they will probably be talking about it years from now. This looks to be one for the ages.
MAIN EVENT Hell on Earth "Cold Blooded" Chris Courtade vs Wes "The Eliminator" Coleman The most requested match for the card! Any idea why? One of the most heated feuds in wrestling. The previous Hell on Earth proved to be the breaking point for both. A point where they simply did not care about their own bodies, the only thing that kept them going was the desire to hurt the other one even more.
Blood will be spilled, bones may be broken, and careers may be ended.
