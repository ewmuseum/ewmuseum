SUPERSTAR SUMMIT (Pt. 2)

9 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
J.R.N. - "If Super Scott wants to win this match then this should be his game plan. He needs to take some time to regroup when he gets nailed like that. But he better not make it a habit or he might get counted out." B.N. - "You're absolutely right J.R., and now Super rolls back into the ring. Psychrobat is waiting for him. Super walks up to him. Quick FOREARM SMASH by Psychrobat! Man he's fast! Psychrobat takes advantage. He swings Super to he ropes, he comes off, Psychrobat with a FLYING HEADSCISSORS TAKEDOWN!! He keeps Super's head clamped between those thighs. Super hops up and gets out! Psychrobat pounces to his feet and Super meets him with a vicious CLOTHESLINE! Psychrobat goes right back down! Super STOMPS on his head! OH! He jumps on Psychrobat's stomach with both feet! He picks up Psychrobat and delivers a SNAP SUPLEX! Psychrobat is being dragged over by a corner. Super goes up top. LEG DROP from up top!" J.R.N. - "I didn't expect Super Scott to dictate this match any, but he's really proving me wrong! I'm glad I didn't bet on this match. Super picks up Psychrobat...BODYSLAM!! He drops and ELBOW across his throat! He picks him up again, BELLY-TO-BELLY!! Psychrobat is pretty limp at this moment. Super picks him up again, wait, whats he doing? He puts him over his shoulder! OH NO!! He's running towards the ropes!! OH MAN!! He just launched all 243 lbs. of Psychrobat over the top rope!!!! That's solid concrete out there! Psychrobat is in a world of hurt right now. Ringmaster is over there to try and help Psychrobat to his feet. He succeeds, but Psychrobat is still grabbing his back." B.N. - "LOOK OUT!!! OH!! Super Scott just JUMPED through the ropes on BOTH of them!! Ringmaster is on the floor and so is Psychrobat again!! Scott picks up Ringmaster and throws him out of the way. He's got Psychrobat now and tosses him into the ring. He goes in after him. STOMP to the head! He picks him up...puts him over his shoulder AGAIN! Oh no! Not again! He's running......POWERSLAM!! WHEW!! Even though it's gotta be killing him...that was actually good luck for Psychrobat. Super pulls him up. SHORT ARM CLOTHESLINE and Psychrobat goes back into the corner. Super backs up. He's running in. SPLASH in the corner! Psychrobat hits his knees and falls flat on his face! Super is going for the cover now!" 1 ..2 ....3 NO!! J.R.N. - "Lucky for Psychrobat he was near the ropes! He got his leg up there in the nick of time. Super grabs Psychrobat and sits him on the top rope. He's going for a move from up top! He's going for a SUPERPLEX! NO!! Psychrobat shoved him off! Super is on the mat! He's getting up now! Psychrobat rockets off with a DROPKICK that meets Super head on!! Super falls! Psychrobat with a fast LEG DROP. He grabs Super by the hair. He pulls him up. PILEDRIVER coming up! YES!! He nailed it! Super's head just bounced a few times before he fell! Psychrobat is going up top now. Super is just laying there. Psychrobat turns to face the crowd!! MOONSAULT!! WHOA!! Psychrobat kinda turned it into a MOONSAULT/KNEE DROP to thead!!! THAT man RULES the sky people!!" B.N. - "Excellent move! Now Psychrobat picks up Scott. GUTBUSTER!!! Scott is the one hurting now. But it looks as though Psychrobat is still feeling the effects of landing outside the ring. He's got his hand on his lower back. Psychrobat swings Super to the ropes, FLYING FOREARM!! He's going back up top. Super gets up. Psychrobat just did a WHEEL KICK from the top turnbuckle!! Super went flying half way across the ring! Super is up. Psychrobat DROPKICKS him to the outside!! Psychrobat scales the turnbuckles. Super is outside of the ring. He turns around!! Psychrobat jumps!!! SUMMERSAULT PRESS!! WOW!! BOTH men are down now!!" J.R.N. - "I"ve said it before, but those moves always tend to work to neither mans advantage." B.N. - "It would depend on how hurt the other guy is J.R., wouldn't it?" J.R.N. - "I guess you're right. Anyway, uh-oh! Ringmaster picks up Super!! OH!! He SLAMMED his face into the guard rail!!! Super is out man! Psychrobat is to his feet again. He rolls Super back into the ring. Super is weary but getting up. Psychrobat pulls on the ropes...he launches himself!! CLOTHESLINE!! Super falls back into the ropes. Psychrobat gets up quickly! Super walks over to him. ROUNDHOUSE KICK!! Super goes right back down! Psychrobat pulls him up. FRONT FACE SUPLEX!! He picks him up to his feet again. OUCH!! I'm not sure what that move is called so I'll just call it what everyone else does. A ROCKER DROPPER!! But it sure did some damage just now! Psychrobat goes for the cover!" 1 ..2 ....NO!! B.N. - "I'm loving this match so far J.R.! Psychrobat throws Super into the ropes. WHOA!! He goes UP and OVER the top turnbuckle!!! Psychrobat is quick to climb to the top! OH MY!!! He's no Yokozuna but Psychrobat just BONZAI DROPPED Super Scott on the OUTSIDE!!! He rolls him back inside. He grabs Super by the wrist. He's running toward the opposite ropes! Psychrobat leaps over the ropes and almost takes Super's arm with him!!! He crawls back inside and pulls Super over to the turnbuckle. Oh no. He's pulling his arm outside the ring and wrapping it around he steel post! Here comes Ringmaster with a chair!!! OUCH!!! He just squashed Super's arm with that chair!! Super is in the ring rolling around trying to stop the pain!! Psychrobat runs in and puts him in an ARM BAR." J.R.N. - "FINALLY one of these men has picked a body part and worked on it. So far it's been some killer moves but none particularly working on a certain body part. Psychrobat is doing that now. Brian, it's just like taking a sword and cutting Super's arm off with it." B.N. - "You're right J.R., as always." J.R.N. - "Yeeeeeaaaaah, but what are gonna do? It's a gift." B.N. - "Uh...uh-huh. Anyway, back to the match. Psychrobat really has that hold clamped in good. Super Scott is reaching for the ropes but can't quite get 'em. There he goes. He's scooting a little. Aaaaaaaaaannd.....he got 'em! Psychrobat has to break the hold now. Psychrobat picks up Super and sends him into the ropes. CLOTHESLINE! NO! A miss! Super comes back and OH! He just applied a sleeper hold onto Psychrobat!" J.R.N. - "Where did THAT come from?!" B.N. - "Somewhere deep down my man!! Psychrobat is slowly going out! Say, isn't that sleeper similar to Psychrobat's partner Monkeyshine's?" J.R.N. - "I believe so but Monkeyshine uses his version of the Million Dollar Dream and calls it the Lullaby." B.N. - "Ah, I see. Psychrobat sure is feeling the effects of it though. Maybe Monkeyshine should have shown him how to escape it quickly." J.R.N. - "You're right there." B.N. - "Wait a minute, Psychrobat is starting to mount a comeback here. He's to one knee now. He's on his feet! He ELBOWS Super in the stomach!! Again!! Again!! The hold is broken! Psychrobat bounces off the ropes...CROSS BODY BLOCK!!! The cover!" 1 ..2 ...NO!! B.N. - "Super got out! Psychrobat is going for a POWERBOMB!! NO! Super rolls over Psychrobat and SUNSET FLIPS him!! The pin!!" 1 ..2 ....3 NO!!! B.N. - "Psychrobat rolls over! He CLOTHESLINES Super who's still sitting on the mat! Thats the speed of Psychrobat! Psychrobat goes up top. FLYING ELBOW SMASH!!! He got it!! He puts Super in a HEADLOCK now. He runs! BULLDOG!! NO!! Super pushed him off and Psychrobat went flying into the corner!! He's all tangled up!! Super grabs him from behind!! DRAGON SUPLEX!!! Super goes up top now! MOONSAULT!!! How did he do that?!?!?!" 1 ..2 ....NO!! J.R.N. - "This match so far has been nothing short of amazing!! Psychrobat and Super get up at the same time! Psychrobat runs at him! ARMDRAG TAKEDOWN by Super! Psychrobat is up again. He's running at Super. HIP TOSS on the Bat!! Super runs at him with a CLOTHESLINE and connects!! BELLY-TO-BELLY from Super!!! Super is taking Psychrobat up top now. OH NO!! This isn't the SCREWDRIVER is it?! YES!!! IT IS!!! He's setting up Psychrobat for it!! {Crowd gasps} OH GOD!!!!!!!!!" B.N. - "DID YOU SEE THAT?!?!?!" J.R.N. - {Stunned} "Uh-huh." B.N. - "Ladies and gentlemen, Psychrobat just raised up and FLIPPED Super Scott OVER the turnbuckle and out to the floor!!! And folks...he's NOT moving. Psychrobat doesn't seem to care though! He's signaling that he's gonna wear the strap after tonight!! HE JUMPS!!! SPLASH!!! OH MAN!! He connected BIG TIME on Super Scott!! Psychrobat gets right back up! he throws Super Scott into the ring." J.R.N. - "Wait a minute. Whats Ringmaster doing? He's leaving! Why?" B.N. - "You got me. Wait, lets stay with Ringmaster if we can because Psychrobat just seems to be putting the boots to Super. Ringmaster is walking to the side of the entrance way to the aisle. Oh no! I see what he's doing! He's dragging a TABLE back to ringside!! He's setting it up in the aisle way! Now Psychrobat has a limp bodied Super Scott and he's dragging him outside the ring! He's lying him on the table! NO!! Super EYE GOUGES him!! He throws Ringmaster aside! He's got a chair!! He's got a chair!! WHACK!! Right across the back of Psychrobat! He goes down!! Super PILEDRIVES him on the concrete!!! Now he's laying Psychrobat on the table!! He goes to the top turnbuckle!! He jumps with an ELBOW!!! CRACK!!!!" {The breaking of a table is heard} J.R.N. - "HE NAILED IT!!! Now their BOTH in trouble!! The referee is laying on the count finally and he's up to five!" 6...7...8...9 B.N. - "Ringmaster picked up Psychrobat and pushed him inside the ring. Super is kinda getting up but surely not as he predicted he would. Super is up and Psychrobat is pulling himself up with the ropes. The referee is kinda pushing Psychrobat away to make sure that Scott can get in the ring safely. He's not looking either and Ringmaster notices!! HE WHIPPED SUPER!!! He just cracked that whip right across Super's shoulder!! He goes back down!!" {About a minute of Psychrobat interrupting the count goes on.} B.N. - "Super is finally back in the ring. Psychrobat knocks him right down and puts him in a CAMEL CLUTCH! He's really pulling back on Super's chin. You can see the spit flying everywhere from Super's mouth!" J.R.N. - "Thank you!! Like the fans at home don't see it! It's not like they want to hear it either!" B.N. - "We've got blind fans!!" J.R.N. - "Oh brother." B.N. - "Uh-oh, Super is mustering up the strength to try and get up! Psychrobat is still on his shoulders! YES!! Super is up!! Psychrobat doesn't know what to do!" J.R.N. - "YES HE DOES!!! He turns it into a VICTORY ROLL!!!" 1 ..2 ....3! B.N. - "Did he get him?!?!" J.R.N. - "I'm not sure!" B.N. - "NO!!! He didn't!! Referee Frank Johanson holds up two fingers!!" J.R.N. - "Psychrobat picks up Super for a POWERBOMB again, but NO!! Super falls backwards into a BRIDGE PIN!!" 1 ..2 ....NO!! B.N. - "Psychrobat is somehow rising off the mat!! The man has speed AND power!! He turns it around into a BACKSLIDE!!!" 1 ..2 ....NO!! B.N. - "Super rolls over and gets out! He's up now! Psychrobat is getting up! DDT!!! Super nailed him with it!! The pin!" 1 ..2 ....thr..NO!! J.R.N. - "These two men are ready to end it now!! Super pulls Psychrobat to his feet. He CHOPS him and Psychrobat goes right back down!! Super puts him in the TEXAS CLOVER LEAF!!! Psychrobat is in pain!! That's gotta be killing his already injured back! Oh no! Ringmaster is climbing up on the apron!! Super lets go and walks over to him! Psychrobat is slowly getting up! Super grabs Ringmaster by the collar! Psychrobat from behind! SUPERKICK to back of Super's cranium!! He goes limp and falls back into Psychrobat's arms! BELLY-TO-BACK!!! The pin!!" 1 ..2 ....3!! B.N. - "NO WAY!! I don't believe this!! Super got out!!! Psychrobat sits him up on the top turnbuckle. He leaps into the air! What?! FRANKENSTEINER!!! WHOA!!! Pin attempt!!" 1 ..2 ....NO!! B.N. - "Super gets his foot on the rope!!" J.R.N. - "Look who's coming down the aisle in an overcoat!!" B.N. - "It's CHERRY CHEESECAKE!!!" J.R.N. - "She walks over and stands by Ringmaster as the converse about something." B.N. - "Anyway, Psychrobat sends Super into the turnbuckles! He runs in with a HANDSPRING ELBOW but MISSES!!! Super just fell out of the way!! Psychrobat is lying on his face in the middle of the ring!! Super STOMPS him! Super climbs the ropes now and faces the crowd! He's lambasting them with rude comments!! Uh-oh! Psychrobat is sneaking up from behind!" J.R.N. - "Forget Psychrobat! Whats Cherry Cheesecake doing?!?! She's walking over to face Super Scott! She hollers at him and he looks down from the turnbuckle!!!" B.N. - "Psychrobat is still regrouping!!" J.R.N. - "WHOA MOMMA!!!!!!!!!!!!! Cherry just opened up the overcoat and Super's eyes are bulging out into the third row and his tounge is touching the outside of the ring!!!!! GET A BETTER CAMERA SHOT!! Super is the only one that's getting a peek!!!!" B.N. - "Forget that J.R.!!! Psychrobat is coming up from behind Super Scott!! He's on the second rope behind Super and Super doesn't know it!! THERE IT IS!!! THE BIG TOP!!!! THE COVER!!!" 1 ..2 ....3!!!!! DING!! DING!! DING!! DING!! DING!! B.N. - "PSYCHROBAT WINS!!!! J.R.N. - "But Cherry's covered up again and I didn't see a damn thing!!!" B.N. - "Lets get the official word from Joe!!" Joe - "Ladies and gentlemen...the winner of the match in 34:59......and the FIRST EVER UNIFIED TV CHAMPION......PSYCHROOOOOOOBBAAAAAATTT!!!!!!!" B.N. - "WOW!! What a match this was!! Unfortunately, this puts the FASW up TWO to nothing! Can the EWA come back from this one? Does it get any better? Lets find out....I believe we are going over to Rick with an EWA match!! Rick??" Indeed! Wow, a new TV Champ in the form of Psychrobat! WHAT A MATCH....and we have only had TWO OF THEM! Next up....the rumble in the prison...the Unification Tag Match, and the BIG 8-Man respect matchup! Is the EWA and the FASW on fire? I would say so! -PrezRick FASW - 2 EWA - 0 --
