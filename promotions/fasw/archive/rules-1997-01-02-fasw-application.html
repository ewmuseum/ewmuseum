Hello all. I'm [name withheld]. I'm posting this here because I come from the so called 'loop feds'. Well, I didn't want this league to just become a clone of the other feds. I hope some of you that have never visited the EWA, PCW, or DPW, or even have visited but only for a short time, decide to give us a try. I'd like to see your stuff. The fed should start around late January or early February, so don't expect a whole lot to happen right away. I sincerely hope to see some of you there, and thanks for taking the time (if you do) to check this out. - Brian +--+--+-- Hello, everyone. As you may know, the FASW officially opened it's doors again last night at New Year's Bash! Cool, huh? I think so too. Now I'm hoping that this will interest a lot of you into joining. I'll supply you with a brief history of the FASW to familiarize some of you that weren't fortunate enough to enjoy it the first time. The FASW started out owned by [name withheld] (the infamous Super Scott :)). He did a wonderful job at maintaining a league that everyone could enjoy. It seemed like there was never a dull moment. In April of '96, the FASW merged with the EWA at Superstar Summit after a best of seven matches. Well it is now breaking away to bring you that FASW all of us who were apart of it loved. Before you roll your eyes or disregard this message, let me assure that this is NOT "just another fed". I've had experience running the EWA and everyone seemed to enjoy themselves. We even did the "impossible" once. Sent a PPV out without receiving any complaints back. Can you believe? :) I've also had some good success in the roleplaying field (sometimes making an ass of myself to do it :)). I'm now gonna supply you with a list of rules (I only have a few) and such for the fed. Also, I've got a couple of writers already, if anyone is interested in joining the FASW writing staff (I need good, reliable people) then please speak up on IRC (in #e-wrestling on EFnet) or drop me a line at [detail withheld]. If you have any questions after reading this, send your question to the same address. I hope you see you there! - Brian ROLEPLAYING RULES +--+--+--+--+--+-- 1) NO KILLING: I think it's cheap heat, and it's unrealistic. I'm not the only one. 2) RP MINIMUM: There is a minimum of one roleplay/flash a week. I don't want anyone to enter and expect to get a match or win just by waiting the longest. You've gotta earn it. If there is a good reason you can't or didn't, then all you gotta do is tell me. But if you just didn't RP, the first time I'll give you a reminder, and the second time be prepared to be asked if you really want to be here or not. 3) RP MAX: There is a roleplay maximum of ONE per day. That doesn't mean you HAVE to RP once a day. All it means is that if you do roleplay/flash on that day, you can only do one. This is easier on both you and I. CHALLENGING +--+--+--+--+--+-- 1) ROLEPLAYS: Challenge people in your roleplays. I'm listening, and so are they. 2) E-MAIL ME: Send a challenge to [detail withheld] and I'll do my damnedest to get it signed for ya. WINS ARE BASED ON ... +--+--+--+--+--+-- 1) QUALITY: You could send it 10, you're opponent could send in 6. If his/hers are better, then they've earned the win. 2) QUANTITY: Yep. It counts. 3) CONSISTENCY: Being a regular roleplayer will definitely advance your chances of winning when it comes down to the wire. 4) STATUS: It may seem unfair, but your status is a small portion (VERY minimal) of what can push you to come out on top in a fight too close to call. But ask around. While running the EWA there were a few surprises when someone who wasn't expected to lose did because he was legitimately out-roleplayed.
