SUPERSTAR SUMMIT-TOMORROW!

6 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yes, the most anticipated event in E-Wrestling HISTORY is TOMORROW. Perhaps the two BEST leagues on the ENTIRE INTERNET (FASW and EWA) are going head to head in a best of 9 series. The league which gets the most victories "takes over" the other. EVERYTHING is on the line. Oh, and its not JUST EWA and FASW, althought that would surely be enough! EMWC, GWF, CSWA, WEW, DPW, UGW, and many other great feds WILL be THERE! I'm trying to get somebody to post this history making event on RSPWF so that you guys' can see the BIGGEST and the BEST event EVER! If your wrestler would like to be a part of EWA or FASW, first off, you must be a very good roleplayer. We've got the BEST collection of roleplayers ANYWHERE! If you'd like to go at it with the BEST, contact ME or [detail withheld] While your at it, check out the FASW Web Page at: http://members.aol.com/[detail withheld]/fasw.htm Once again, Superstar Summit is TOMORROW! Wrestlers appearing are: Super Scott Heartbreak Kid Trey Porter Dark Destroyer Hellshock Sabin Figaro Maniacal Mailman Psychrobat Gunnar "The Grizzly" Gaines Silencer Arcin Apocolypse Mick Morton Monkeyshine Devil's Desciples The Cripplers Good Knights Kempo Witch King T.R. Ela Vigilante Offensive Alex Adams Drac "The Raven" Draven and.......MANY MANY MANY MORE! Do NOT miss this extravaganza!!! FASW World Title Match- Hellshock (champ) vs. Gunnar "The Grizzly" Gaines EWA World Title Match- Dark Destroyer (champ) vs. Offensive Alex Adams Winners of both bouts face off in a Unification Match EWA North American Champ vs. FASW North American Champ (Unification Bout/ Spin the Wheel, Make the Deal) HB Kid (EWA) vs. Maniacal Mailman EWA TV Champ vs. FASW TV Champ (Unification Match/Ironman Match) Super Scott (EWA) vs. Psychrobat (FASW) Those are SOME of the more anticipated bouts, but there is MUCH MUCH MUCH more! Do NOT miss it!
