A 1995-96 loop federation that lost a best-of-seven series and its own independence to the EWA, then relaunched on New Year's Day 1997 with a rulebook and a title slate but never posted a card of its own. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The FASW ran through 1995 and early 1996 under an owner the community remembered by his own wrestling persona, “the infamous Super Scott.” A 4 February 1996 recruitment ad named ten of its stars — Hellshock, Dark Destroyer, Heartbreak Kid, Arcin, Silencer, Sabin Figaro, Mick Morton, Witch King, the Joker, and Gunnar “The Grizzly” Gaines — “and MUCH MUCH more,” and claimed pay-per-view bragging rights over every other league on the Net.

That run ended at Superstar Summit, 6 April 1996, billed as the biggest event in e-wrestling history: a best-of-nine interfed series against the EWA with the winner “taking over” the loser, staged in front of a card that also drew the EMWC, GWF, CSWA, WEW, DPW and UGW. Hellshock defended the federation's own World Championship against Gunnar “The Grizzly” Gaines in a match whose outcome the surviving record does not preserve, and Psychrobat beat the rival league's own Super Scott in a 34-minute, 59-second Television Title Unification Match to become the first-ever unified TV Champion, putting the promotion up two matches to none in the series — the last dated document of its own independent run.

A new operator, Brian, reopened the promotion at “New Year's Bash” on 1 January 1997, recounting its history for newcomers and laying out its rules: no killing angles, a minimum of one roleplay a week and a cap of one a day, wins judged on quality, quantity, consistency and status, four championships — World Heavyweight, North American, Television, and World Tag Team — and two weekly shows, Saturday Night Riot and Wednesday Wipeout.

A masked representative from the relaunched promotion was sighted at a rival league's special as late as May 1997, but no further card of its own survives past that relaunch application. Whether it ever ran an independent show again under Brian is not in the surviving record.
