TITLE LINEAGES

CPW World Heavyweight Title

Nick "Nitro" Glyceron beat Dr. Jerry Pain M.D. in Cycle #6, February 1998.

Dr. Jerry Pain M.D beat Nick "Nitro" Glyceron in Cycle #10, June 1998.

Deadbolt beat Dr. Jerry Pain M.D in Cycle #16, December 1998.

El Doctor Quiero beat The Inquisitor in Cycle #21, May 1999 (Due to Deadbolt leaving CPW, there was a tournament to crown the new champion, and this was the finals.)

Lucien Champion beat El Doctor Quiero in Cycle #31, March 2000.

Harvester of Nightmares beat Lucien Champion in Cycle #35, July 2000.

"Phoenix" Steve Christiansen beat Harvester of Nightmares in Cycle #37, September 2000.

Khan beat "Phoenix" Steve Christiansen in Cycle #41, January 2001 (This match also included Freddie Fuller and Tim Szokan.)

Lucien Champion beat Khan in Cycle #44, May 2001.

Macho King" Rick Wolverine (AKA "God Complex" Legion Cage) beat Lucien Champion in Cycle #48, September 2001.

Lucien Champion beat "Macho King" Rick Wolverine in Cycle #51, December 2001.

David Mann beat Lucien Champion in Cycle #53, March 2002.

Lucien Champion beat David Mann in Cycle #54, April 2002.

Mac Daddy Freddie Fuller beat Lucien Champion in Cycle #55, May 2002.

"God Complex" Legion Cage beat Mac Daddy Freddie Fuller in Cycle #57, July 2002.

"The Immortal" Amadeus beat "God Complex" Legion Cage in Cycle #60, September 2002.

King Butcher & "God Complex" Legion Cage beat Will Scarlet, Andre Pride, Mac Daddy Freddie Fuller, and The Phoenix Steve Christiansen to become Co-Champs in Cycle #62, December 2002.

"God Complex" Legion Cage beat King Butcher to become the Undisputed Champion in Cycle #66, May 2003.

Brock the Pulverizer beat "God Complex" Legion Cage on Tuesday Night Dynamite in Cycle #79, September 2004 (This match also included King Butcher, Kaos Knight, and Ethan Quinn.)

CPW World Tag Team Title

The Midnight Crusaders beat Los Doctores in a tournament final in #22, June 1999.

The Sex Machines beat The Midnight Crusaders in bull #26, October 1999.

The Midnight Crusaders beat the Sex Machines in bull #28, December 1999.

The Sex Machines beat The Midnight Crusaders in bull #32, April 2000.

The Nice Guys beat The Sex Machines in bull #34, June 2000. This was a triple threat in which The Midnight Crusaders also participated.

The Underdogs won the tag titles via forfeit in bull #35, July 2000, when their opponents and champions, The Nice Guys, appeared on USW television and tossed down the titles.

The Devil's Own ("Cold Blooded" Chris Stone & "Hot Stuff" Aaron Fleury) beat The Underdogs in Cycle #48, September 2001.

The Underdogs (2) beat The Devil's Own in Cycle #54, April 2002.

The Devil's Own (2) beat The Underdogs in Cycle #57, July 2002.

The Underdogs (3) beat The Devil's Own in Cycle #60, September 2002.

The Blackhearts (Jesse & Sterling) beat The Underdogs in Cycle #63, January 2003.

The Underdogs (4) beat The Blackhearts in Cycle #66, May 2003.

The Bad Seeds (Billy Childress & Ronnie Frown) beat The Underdogs in Cycle #69, August 2003.

The Beautiful People ("Narcissist" Nathan Stone & "Hot Stuff" Aaron Fleury, 3) beat The Bad Seeds in Cycle #72, November 2003.

Heart & Soul (Christian Michaels & Jazzman) beat The Beautiful People in Cycle #78, July 2004.

The York Brothers (Dustin & Matt) beat Heart & Soul in Cycle #79, September 2004.

CPW International Television Title

El Doctor Pepper beat The Inquisitor in a tournament final in #23, July 1999, to become what was then known as the Provincial champion.

*Note, this title began as the CPW Provincial title, but became known as the CPW International title at the beginning of Nathan Stone's title win in bull #44, as CPW wanted to remove the regional feel from the belt.*

"Way Hot" Scott Colton beat Will Scarlet & El Doctor Pepper in a triple threat to claim the vacant title in bull #32 in April, 2000. Once CPW management changed in bull #31, the title was declared vacant.

"Hotstuff" Chris Schimtt beat "Way Hot" Scott Colton in bull #40, December 2000.

"The Narcissist" Nathan Stone beat "Hotstuff" Chris Schmitt in bull #44, May 2001. Note, it was at the start of this reign that the title became known by its current name.

Andre Pride beat "the Narcissist" Nathan Stone in bulletin #56, June 2002.

"Narcissist" Nathan Stone beat Andre Pride in bulletin #57, July 2002.

"Mad Dog" Tim Zaegel beat "The Narcissist" Nathan Stone in Cycle #60, September 2002.

"Ironheart" Lance Rave beat "Mad Dog" Tim Zaegel in Cycle #61, September 2002.

Andre Pride beat "Ironheart" Lance Rave in Cycle #64, February 2003.

"Time Bomb" Blaze Colton beat Andre Pride in Cycle #69, August 2003.

"The Immortal" Amadeus beat "Time Bomb" Blaze Colton in Cycle #71, October 2003.

Kaos Knight beat "The Immortal" Amadeus in Cycle #75, February 2004.

King Butcher beat Kaos Knight in Cycle #77, May 2004.

*Ethan Quinn beat King Butcher in Cycle #80, October 2004. *IWF closed after cycle #79, but CPW's "#80" show was posted on the message board.*

CPW Young Blood Challenge Title

Joey Steele defeated King Butcher, Cool Flexa and Johnny Corvin in a 4-way dance in bull #44 to become the first champion at the Pacific Coliseum in Vancouver, British Columbia.

Killer Mann beat Joey Steele in bull #45 at the CPW Arena in Toronto, Ontario.

CPW Lethal Ladies Title

Sex Goddess Sunny Beach beat Leathal Lisa, Bloody Mary & Angie "Clutch" Johnston in a fourway dance in bull #46 at the Saddledome in Calgary, Alberta.
