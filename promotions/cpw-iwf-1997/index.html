A Canadian-set league of a play-by-mail wrestling game, running its own World Heavyweight Championship through nineteen reigns from 1998 into the game's own 2004 closure. Forty-six wrestlers are recorded across its own seven titles. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Canadian Pride Wrestling ran, in its own words, “in the IWF from 1997 to ???” — a Canadian-set league of the play-by- mail wrestling game, staging cards across the country from Vancouver's Pacific Coliseum to a CPW Arena of its own in Toronto to the Saddledome in Calgary.

Its World Heavyweight Championship ran nineteen reigns between February 1998 and September 2004. Lucien Champion held it four separate times, more than anyone else in its lineage; a five-man scramble in December 2002 briefly split it into a Co-Champions arrangement between King Butcher and “God Complex” Legion Cage before Cage unified it as sole, Undisputed Champion the following May.

Six further titles ran alongside it — World Tag Team, International Television (renamed partway through its own history from “Provincial,” in the league's own words, “to remove the regional feel from the belt”), Young Blood Challenge, Lethal Ladies, North Continental, and Bunkhouse — though its own lineage sheet notes real gaps in three of them after 1997's own bulletin #57.

Its closing beat is stated in its own words: the IWF closed after Cycle #79 in September 2004, but Canadian Pride Wrestling's own Cycle #80 card — Ethan Quinn beating King Butcher for the International Television Championship — was still posted to the message board afterward, its own last title change run past the end of the game that had hosted it.
