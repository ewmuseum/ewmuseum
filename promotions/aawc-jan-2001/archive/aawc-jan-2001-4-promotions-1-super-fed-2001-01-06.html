[AAWC] 4 Promotions, 1 Super Fed, 4 fedheads...what's not to like?

January 6, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
AAWC - Quick Rundown, for a complete FAQ, write [detail withheld] , and one of us will send you one out promptly. -AAWC is a fed, broken up into 4 promotions/territories. Each Promotion has a fedhead/roster (small) of it's own, and they put on a show together. Inter promotion and Outter promotion matches are all signed. Promotions can trade/swap talent...talent can leave a promotion etc.... Basically, each promotion comes from a different part of the country. We have a promotion in the Northeast, Northwest, Southeast, and Southwest. It's like the old NWA breakup, where there are several promotions under one banner, but instead, all the promotions are combining their efforts to take the fed national. It's spawned from the merging of 4 feds, IWC, XWW, RPWF, UWA. Lots of talent, should really rock. Fed starts in late January, app's are being accepted until Jan 10th, 2001. We already got quite a few, but, we gave the members of the fed first crack at getting there in, then we decided to send it out on RSPWF. TAGS and WOMEN wrestlers are acceptable. We're going to try and establish a division for both, BUT, depends on the demand for them. =========================================================== *Send to: [detail withheld] ******************* Volunteer ******************* Can you write matches?
