Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The AFW is known only through its own recruitment posts, which put its founding around November 1996 and, by July 1997, claimed eight months running three cards a week and a monthly pay-per-view by e-mail.

Its members pitched it against the genre's own clichés rather than on any card of its own: one recruiting post promised an alternative to feds full of “cheap knock off wrestlers like Brett Kart and Stone Cold Shawn Austin” and flashes that said nothing more than “I am the best and you suck punk! I will beat you because i am the greatest ever!”, and promised that “no one gets ignored” on its own roster. A week later, a different member floated the idea of a community e-zine for e-wrestling generally, offering to help get it started.

No card, roster, or result of its own survives. Nothing after 18 July 1997 is in the record.
