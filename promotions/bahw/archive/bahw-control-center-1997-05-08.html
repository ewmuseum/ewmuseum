BAHW Control Center, 8 May 1997

8 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
KC: Hello fans, once again it is me, Kendall Cunningham bringing you a BAHW Control Center! Well fans, we have a lot to cover and only 30 minutes to bring it to you, so let's get started! As most of you know, [name withheld] has put up the official Bad Ass Hardcore Wrestling website. It really is a great site and it's very informative. Check it out at: http://members.aol.com/Marleyrulz/bahw.html and send feedback to either the Prez or Sean. Also, the BAHW is proud to announce that it has signed several newcomers. Some we can mention here and they are; "The Raging Russian" Mikhail Zhukov, The Dream Master, Assault, Predator, and Smoke. Now, the other two newcomers will debut shortly...they are both products of the NEFW, and one will join one of the major stables here in BAHW, the other will be wrestling at BAHW Mayhem on May 18th against "Hardcore" John Stilwater for a new BAHW title called the Tough Man Championship which will only be defended in No DQ matches! That match could quite possibly steal the show! Right now, let's take a look at the Mayhem card! **************************************************************************** ********************** BAD ASS HARDCORE WRESTLING proudly presents.... BAHW Mayhem on Sunday May 18th LIVE from the Pond in Anahiem, California MAIN EVENT World Title Match Rick "The Reaper" Erickson vs. Retribution United States Title Match "The Perfectionist" Nik Nyce vs. "The Nordic Nightmare" Janick Ericksen Tough Man Championship "Hardcore" John Stilwater vs. ?????????? Television Title Match Falls Count Anywhere Smasher vs. Viper Tag Team Title Match Three Way Dance Goodfellas vs. Destroyers vs. Cutting Edge #1 Contenders Match Team Canada vs. Bordeau & Adams Steel Cage Grudge Match "The Terminator" Assaf Levavy vs. "The Showstopper" Simon Lebec Cruiserweight Title Match Damion vs. "The Shroud" Anthony Alred "The Raging Russian" Mikhail Zhukov vs. Metal Militia **************************************************************************** ********************** KC: Wow! What a card! I'm sooooo excited about this one! And speaking of great cards, we've got a great one Saturday Night! **************************************************************************** ********************** BAHW Extreme Saturday Nite (5/10/97) Main Event Team Canada vs. Sudden Impact "The Raging Russian" Mikhail Zhukov vs. Assault "The Shroud" Anthony Alred vs. "Serious" Stan Hunter The Dream Master vs. The Junkie "The Terminator" Assaf Levavy vs. "The Stryker King" Bigman Mike Adams vs. Smoke The Apocalypse Militia vs. The Hearts Of Darkness **************************************************************************** **********************
