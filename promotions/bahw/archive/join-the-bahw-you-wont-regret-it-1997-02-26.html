Okay now, your probably thinking that im just another fed head trying to get wrestlers in my league, we your wrong, I am only acting as the inteim president whil our Prez [name withheld] is out of town on a Bussiness deal. Im not going to tell you that this is the best league outthere or anything like that, but Ill tell you this, I am normally just a handler of a Wrestler, ive been in many Booked leagues (i personally dis-like simulators) and this has been the best one Ive come accross that was open to wrestlers, Im sure there are many better ones but they are usually closes to new wresters and Ive never been able to get in any of them. This league however was open when I applied and Ive loved it ever since, Ive even pulled my Wrestler out of other leagues so I could devote all my roleplaying and such to this league. Thats how much I like it. We have two weekly cards, and one monthly PPV. Roleplaying Flashes come out everyday and they arnt little weak two line flashes either, our currnet wresters put alot into their flashes and have come up with some pretty orininal ideas. Here are some basic rules: 1. Original Wrestlers only, and no ripoffs, we already have a Steve Austin imitator (who we are trying to talk into changing). BE original, make your wrestler unique. 2. Currently we are only accepting one singles wrester or one tag team per new applicant, we arnt a new Fed, we have been around for over 2 months under this name and have actually been around for over a year when you figure most of our wrestlers and writers used to be with another fed untill differences in opinions caused a split sorta like WCW/NWA. 3. Tag Teams are currently a high prority, our tag ranks have dropped lately due to all kinds of reasons so we are looking to rebuild that division. 4. No Andre "The Giants" we already have one or two good big men, we dont need anymore. 5. You must know how to roleplay and be able to roleplay alot, this is a booked league, matches are decided alot on roleplaying. 6. Dont brownose the fed heads, itll only get you a championship, JUST KIDDING well Ive talked enough, why dont you check out our homepage and if your interested sign up on the form you'll find there... BE warned though, the page sucks, we are a wrestling fed, not page designers <G> http://www.geocities.com/TimeSquare/Alley/2335/
