Interview with the BAHW WORLD CHAMPION

24 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
***************** EDITED FOR PROFANITY********************* <backstage following Hardcore Haven, in the personal lockeroom of the World Champ, Rick "The Reaper" Erickson.. Erickson is is being given medical attention by the BAHW head Trainer, the trainer is insiting that Erickson go to a hosptal to tend to his facial lacerations but Rick refuses and insists that the Trainer stich them up himself... The Champ looks to be confused and angry at the same time, he keeps muttering something under his breath but you cant make it out> Reporter: Mr Erickson.... any comments on the interference of James Havok in your matchup tonight? Reaper: yeah... I do have a few things to say about that ...James....Welcome.... Welcome to the beggining... The beggining of the end of your career... I told you that you would get your shot, you just had to be patient, but you couldnt do that, you had to get involved, dont feed me or anyone else this s**t you arnt interested in this title right here, you proved tonight that you are...Damn it, I should have taken you out Saturday night when I had the chance, but no... I was stupid, I figured thats not what The World Champion does, I figured leave you be and when your time came to meet me in the ring, then I would handle you... IT wont happen again, thats a promise, your gonna pay and you are going to pay dearly you miserable sack of shit, Im gonna whip you like a bitch, hell I might just make you my bitch... This Title here got to my head, it got me soft, well thank you James, you woke me up... NOW YOUR GONNA F**KIN DIE... Reporter: What about Aaron Aikmen.. It certainly appears that this match tonight did not in any way setle your differences, will their be a rematch? Reaper: Let me guess, that little pansie is already cryin that he was robbed isnt he? Reporter: Well no, we have not been able to get an interview with him yet Reaper: Well Aaron, ill save you the trouble, you wernt robbed, you were saved, I had you... I could have put you down at any moment if I wanted too but your little buddy saved your butt... its not gonna happen again, next time i get you in that ring Im gonna put you down and you wont get up, this is far from over, because of Havok I didnt get to prove what a fraud you are, Im gonna beat you down so bad your your mother is gonna feel it.. Im gonna send you right back to texas where you belong.. mabey you'll get lucky and old Vince will paint you uop and put you in a tag team with good old Dustin, ... Texas, where the men are men, and their women are men too.. Oh and whats up with the bird man? huh Aaron, I saw your match Saturday with Lebec, you got something going, you forming the stable of ripoffs or something like that, Im surprised he didnt show up to save your ass too... oh thats right, he got put down by The Woodsman, to bad, i woulda liked kicking both of your asses at once. Reporter: Mr Erickson... Im sure this question is burning in the minds of most of our fans, who were the two men that came to your aid? Reaper: Dont worry about it, thats personal bussiness, I dont know what the f**k they doing here, I dont want too know either Reporter: You seemed shocked when you saw them aiding you, actually I think a better word would be bewildered, I dont think anyone here in the BAHW has ever seen you like that Reaper: I SAID DONT YOU F**KING WORRY ABOUT IT, WHY DONT YOU GET THE F**K OUT OF HERE YOU SACK OF S**T BEFORE I DO TO YOU WHAT I DIDNT GET TO DO TO AIKMEN Trainer: Hold still Rick, i only have one more stitch.. Reaper: F**K YOU TOO, ALL OF YOU GET THE F**K OUT <The Champ pushes the Trainer away, leaving one of his laceration still partially open> Reaper: LEAVE NOW DAMN IT!!!!! <Erickson picks up a chair and throws it in the direction of the camera and the screen goes to static>
