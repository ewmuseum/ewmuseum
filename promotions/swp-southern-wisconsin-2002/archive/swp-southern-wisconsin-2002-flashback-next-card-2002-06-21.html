SWP Flashback/Next Card

21 June 2002

[detail withheld] . ************************************************* [The swirling SWP logo gives way to the Flashback studio, but instead of
Jason Havner's smiling mug, the camera opens on 'Gorgeous' Mike Garvey chomping on a frayed cigar.]
GARVEY: "What's up kids? Since Havner is doing press for the upcoming pay-per-view, I had to get out of bed on a Saturday morning and come down here to tape this for you. I ain't gonna be as nice as Havner, and I'll just say my piece and get the hell out. Okay?
"We had a lot of action in the SWP this week. A four men moved onto the second bracket in the Midwest Title Tourney and Jean-Luc Page earned his place in the World Championship match because of a nice assist from
'Hardcore' Harlacker.But, let's get on with it. "Johnny Haaglund defeated Oz Walker and 'Hardcore' Harlacker defeated
Spivey Michaels. They'll meet in the second round of the tourney. As will
Triple J and Frank Maddox. Should be a good pair of matches. The winners of those two matches will move on to meet for the Midwest Title. If you're a betting man, I'm looking for a Maddox/Harlacker match for the title.
"The third match of the night will be Spivey Michaels meeting The Harlem
Hitman, thanks to Spivey's assist against the Hitman in Triple J's match.
The Hitman demanded some ring time against the Buckingham Brawler. Should be nice piece of ringwork."
"After that, 'Hardcore' Harlacker will meet Tuulenux in a STRETCHER MATCH: The first man to be beaten unconscious and strapped to a stretch by the other man loses. Tuulenux demanded this match after the close of Action, this week. Tuulenux challenge Harlacker and Harlacker accepted. Tuulenux was so angry about the ending of the cage match with Jean-Luc Page that he tore up most of the backstage. It wasn't pretty. Harlacker never backs down from a challenge, and he accepted. So, this means Harlacker has the possibility of wrestling THREE separate matches in one night, including one where he could get the unholy crap beaten out of him. If he gets through them all with wins, he may be the baddest motherfu---- on the planet.
"What? Oh, did I? I can't say motherfu---- on TV? Sh--. Whoops.there I go again." "Next on the card will be the Midwest Title Match, bracket A winner vs. bracket B winner, which will lead us into the Main Event: "Jean-Luc Page will challenge Hades for the World Title. However, remember that R.J. Sandstrom has sworn that Page will not win the World Title and he has a contingency plan in a man whom we have not seen! Is he a current SWP member? Is he a new guy? I don't know.Also, just because he felt like it.Sandstrom has named HIMSELF the SPECIAL GUEST REFEREE! Sounds like Page might as well not even show up.will he?
"Tune into 'Wicked Ways' to find out. I'm out of here." Haaglund v. Harlacker Triple J v. Maddox Michaels v. Harlem Hitman Harlacker v. Tuulenux --Stretcher Match Midwest Championship --Haaglund or Harlacker v. Triple J or Maddox World Title Match --Page v. Hades --R.J. Sandstrom as special guest referee Flashes due by WEDNESDAY JUNE 26. PPV out on Friday. **Advance notice: There will be no card July 5 because I'm moving to Madison that weekend. Cards should resume the week of July 12, I'll send you email confirmation.
Thanks! Sean
