SWP Flashback

1 June 2002

pay-per-view for the title. ======================================================== ["Fanfare for the Common Man" blasts from the speakers and the Commissioner,
R.J. Sandstrom sprints onto the entrance ramp. He is fuming. He stares at
Page and Page stares back, a wild look in his eyes and a strange glint in his eyes.
The image freezes and dissolves into a spinning SWP logo while the opening guitar strains for Metallica's "Ride the Lightning" explode into loud, rocking noise. The camera pans back and shows Jason Havner, bespectacled and wearing an SWP sportcoat, seated at an announce table.]
HAVNER: "Welcome, ladies and gentlemen, to SWP Flashback, and what you just saw was the closing image at last night's show. The SWP Board of Directors snuck behind the commissioner's back and hired a man whom the commissioner swore would never wrestle on SWP mats: Jean-Luc Page.
"Page's entrance at the end of the main event, a match between "Hardcore" Harlacker and newcomer the Harlem Hitman, showcased the so-called "Crazy
Canuck's" penchant for violence and confusion. Page attacked four of the
SWP's superstars with a hockey stick, then almost ripped Johnny Haaglund in half when the young warrior came down to try and stop him.
"After the cameras stopped rolling, the mayhem continued. Page escaped
Commissioner Sandstrom's security guards by fleeing through the crowd, chased by a very angry Hades. Page fled the arena in his own car, leaving
Hades cursing him in the parking garage. This is the second time in two weeks that Hades has had someone kick him while he was down. No doubt, the brawler will have words for Page.and possibly, the man who punked him out last week, "Hardcore" Harlacker.
"Also involved in that skirmish at the end of the show, Tuulenux, the
Harlem Hitman, and "Hardcore" Harlacker-the man who received the brunt of
Jean-Luc Page's rage-were escorted to the back, continuing their fight with words instead of fists. Once backstage, Tuulenux was able to get his hands on "Hardcore" Harlacker one last time, whipping the big Texan into a souvenir kiosk. When the kiosk collapsed on Harlacker, there was a moment of confusion and Tuulenux used that moment to disappear without a trace.
Harlacker sustained no severe injuries, other than a few minor scrapes from the kiosk, but he did have a nasty lump on the side of his head where
Jean-Luc Page hit him. "The Harlem Hitman, apparently in a rage over being attacked first by
Tuulenux, and then by Page, destroyed a locker room, overturning everything in sight, and apparently even exchanged some words and gestures with Larry
Czamura-who, himself, was livid over losing to Johnny Haaglund earlier.
"Speaking of the young warrior, after his second victory over members of the Badger Boys, Haaglund gave a valiant attempt to stop Jean-Luc Page's attack and was rewarded with a reverse suplex onto a guardrail and a kick to the skull. Haaglund broke one rib that had been cracked earlier in the evening after an unprovoked attacked by MacEvoy and Czamura. Haaglund was taken to the hospital for X-rays but he has said that this injury will not keep him out of the title match next week! --More on that in a minute.
"Also in action last night, Spivey Michaels lost to "Mighty" Evan MacEvoy after MacEvoy squeezed the consciousness out of Michaels and left him in a heap outside the ring! Michaels, however, may have had his mind in another place, after he made an offer to Triple J that Triple J had no problems refusing. Michaels has put himself in as Triple J's self-proclaimed
"back-up" and interfered in Triple J's match against Oz Walker by giving the high-flyer a Spivey Dive, successfully disqualifying Oz Walker, to allow
Triple J the victory! That trio of cruiserweight talent has some issues amongst them. It will be interesting to see how Walker and Triple J react to Michaels's involvement.
"All in all-it was a HUGE night of action for SWP. And to top it all off-the commissioner has ordered the first Pay-Per-View of this upstart federation. Next week, all the superstars currently on the roster will be meeting in a Rumble-two men start out in the ring, and every two minutes, another man joins. Elimination occurs when you get thrown over the top rope to the ground. Last man in the ring will be given the title belt and crowned the very first SWP World Title Holder! No doubt all the superstars will be wanting to get their hands on that strap!
"However, the three men with the toughest chances are the first three men in the ring! Commissioner Sandstrom has sentences "Hardcore" Harlacker and
Hades to start off this contest! These two men have no love for each other-actually, I don't think Hades has any love for anyone. After one-hundred and twenty seconds alone in the ring together, the next superstar out will be none other than the Romanian Nightmare, Tuulenux!
"Hardcore" Harlacker has angered both of these men, and though it's not much, there is a modicum of respect between Hades and Tuulenux-"Hardcore"
Harlacker may have placed himself in a situation where his fate may have already been decided.
"The rest of the order of entrance will be determined by random draw just before the match starts! The only person who will know will be Commissioner
Sandstrom and he has said he will be ringside during this pay-per-view, in order to make sure nothing funny goes on. He will be announcing the competitors-no one backstage will know when they come out!
"It's sure to be an exciting night of action in the SWP next week as we film the very first SWP pay-per-view! Make sure to join us next Friday night for the 'Brawl-4-All'! The superstars will certainly have things to say about this week's action, and probably have some comments for the PPV!
"Thanks for joining me here on Flashback. For everyone at SWP, I'm Jason
Havner. Good night, and we'll see you next week!"
