SWP PPV Wrap-Up

9 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The SWP is still seeking applications from talented handlers. We have a pretty good product if I do say so myself, and a few more dedicated, imaginative people would really help it out.
EMail: [detail withheld] for more info. * * * * * * * [A stirring, slow-motion image of Hades, draped over the ropes, barely able to stand, trying to lift the new World Title Belt above his head opens SWP
Flashback. After a few moments, the image dissolves to the face of Jason
Havner, bespectacled and wearing a finely tailored SWP sportcoat.]
HAVNER: "That was the scene on Friday as Hades won the Brawl-4-All in order to be crowned the very first SWP World Title Holder! Hades, the second man to the ring, had to outlast EVERYTHING from brutal finishers to an almost-certain elimination from Jean-Luc Page to summon up the ultimate effort and take Page out when he became distracted.
"This week on SWP Flashback, we'll recap all the action of the Rumble, as well as hear from Commissioner R.J. Sandstrom himself on the next card!
Sandstrom has promised to name a number one contender for the World Title and he promised information on the Midwest Title contest! He may reveal the latter today when he shows up live in the studio!"
"Back to the action at hand, though-The Brawl-4-All Rumble was a chance to have all the superstars in the SWP get into the ring and see if they could outlast all the others! Commissioner Sandstrom had ordered that Hades and
"Hardcore" Harlacker start the Rumble, based on their past history. Hades finally got Harlacker in a ring by himself. The two went at it until
Tuulenux entered at 00:02:00. Here's the run down of the rest of the action:
00:04:00 "Mighty" Evan MacEvoy enters 00:04:25 Tuulenux eliminated "Hardcore" Harlacker 00:06:00 Spivey Michaels enters 00:08:00 The Harlem Hitman enters 00:10:00 Larry Czamura hits the ring 00:12:00 "Triple J" Jeremy Jasper Jenkins enters 00:14:00 "The Wizard" Oz Walker enters 00:16:00 "The Viking" Johnny Haaglund enters 00:18:00 Jean-Luc Page enters 00:19:30 Oz Walker eliminated The Harlem Hitman 00:20:00 "Cowboy" Kirk Collins enters 00:22:50 Oz Walker eliminated "Cowboy" Kirk Collins 00:25:24 Hades eliminated Tuulenux --"Hardcore" Harlacker attacks Tuulenux outside the ring 00:42:04 Hades eliminated Oz Walker 00:50:02 "Triple J" eliminated "Mighty" Evan MacEvoy 01:01:15 Jean-Luc Page eliminated Johnny Haaglund --Cameras show devastation in the back-Harlacker and Darkmoon are unconscious in the back and a messy trail shows that Tuulenux has left.looking for someone?
01:04:26 Hades and Triple J eliminated Larry Czamura 01:08:40 Hades eliminated Spivey Michaels 01:12:12 Jean-Luc Page eliminated "Triple J" --Hades and Page are left in the ring alone 01:20:00 Pages attempts to eliminate Hades-but Sandstrom summons Damian
Ozzbourne to the ring.
01:21:30 Damian Ozzbourne enters 01:23:30 Damian Ozzbourne leaves ring 01:24:00 Jean-Luc Page is nearly eliminated by Sandstrom 01:26:07 With Page distracted, Hades eliminated Page. "And there you have it ladies and gentlemen, the run-down of the World
Title contest. Hades was crowned the very first SWP World Champion.
"At this point in the show, I'd like to invite our very special guest,
Commissioner R.J. Sandstrom, to the table to make the announcement for the next SWP card! Commissioner?"
["Fanfare for the Common Man" sounds in the background and Sandstrom quickly takes a seat at the announce table. He looks good for a man who just made a permanent enemy in Jean-Luc Page.]
SANDSTROM: "Thanks, Havner. It's good to be here. I have a lot to talk about, specifically, the naming of the number one contender. I will be doing that at the next SWP live event! There are a lot of good choices.
"I'd like to offer my congratulations to Hades at this point, too. He had an amazing night and I think he truly deserved the title. I hope he has a long, and glorious run.
"At this point, I would also like to address Damian Ozzbourne.Ozzbourne, we had a deal! A verbal contract! What kind of man are you? I brought you in to do a job and as far as I'm concerned, you failed miserably! But, I'll deal with you in my own right later on.No sense in airing that laundry here.
"The next card.by special request, the Harlem Hitman has asked for Oz Walker one-on-one. Apparently the Hitman has some issues over being eliminated and he would like to take those up with Mr. Walker personally.so, that match is
GRANTED. "Spivey Michaels will be taking on our newcomer, 'Cowboy' Kirk Collins in a singles match. I want to get a better of idea about what Collins is capable of doing in the ring. Be warned Mr. Collins-despite your differences in size, Spivey Michaels is no pushover.remember, he outlasted you in the
Brawl-4-All. "In the next match-up, I'm proud to introduce the first of TWO tag-team match-ups for the next card! Firstly, "Triple J" and Johnny Haaglund will take on The Badger Boys! The Badger Boys have been pestering me greatly about the tag-team titles. Boys, consider this a warm-up.
"And, in the main event, I think we're going to do a little something different.I see how so many of our boys can't work and play together well and I'd like to change it. So this will be a tag-team match-Jean-Luc
Page.will team with.the World Champion, HADES, in a tag match against Tuulenux and Damien Ozzbourne!" HAVNER: "Wait-is that a wise decision, Commissioner?" SANDSTROM: "They'll either learn to work together or self-destruct. Either way, it will be fun to watch. Take care of yourself, Havner."
[Sandstrom leaves the table and walks off the set.] HAVNER: "You heard it straight from the Commissioner's mouth this week! Big matches, big week of action! Tune in!"
[The screen fades to black] The Harlem Hitman v. Oz Walker Spivey Michaels v. "Cowboy" Kirk Collins "Triple J" and "The Viking" Johnny Haaglund v. The Badger Boys .and the main event: Jean-Luc Page and Hades v. Tuulenux and Damian Ozzbourne Flashes are due by WEDNESDAY, JUNE 12 Please get them in.no flashes, you job.
