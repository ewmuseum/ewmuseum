SWP Flashback 6.14.02

15 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As always, the SWP is looking for talented and original handlers...please see the other cards posted on this newsgroup or email
[detail withheld] if you're interested in joining. It's a laid-back, well run organization... * * * * * * * * [Jason Havner is behind the desk for another quick recap of this week's SWP action and a glimpse into what we should see in the coming week!]
HAVNER: "We're only two weeks away from the SWP's next pay-per-view, 'Wicked
Ways' and the action in the SWP is heating up to an intense level! Prior to a huge night of action, Commissioner R. J. Sandstrom announced the brackets for the Midwest Title Tournament, and in a controversial decision, selected
SWP favorite Tuulenux over the 'Crazy Canuck' Jean-Luc Page, despite Page's
near-win of the Brawl-4-All Rumble! "Firstly, let's get to the action. In the opening match, 'The Wizard' Oz
Walker defeated The Harlem Hitman with his patented 'No Place Like Home'
superkick. "The SWP was then ground to a halt through an amazing, unannounced video vignette-a Chicago cop who has been released from the force: Frank Maddox.
My colleague, 'Gorgeous' Mike Garvey, and I were as confused as everyone else in the arena-but that interruption became important later on.
"Tuulenux came out next-in his macabre, bizarre style, and essentially had words for a whole cadre of SWP talent-including the Commissioner! Tuulenux is the man to watch right now in the SWP. He's been skulking in the shadows, but with his new rank of Number One Contender-he may very well be the most dangerous man in the SWP.
"Tuulenux reinforced this point in the next match: Newcomer 'Cowboy' Kirk
Collins was assaulted at near the end of his match against Spivey Michaels, by the Romanian Nightmare! Tuulenux slid into the ring and KO'd Collins with Ethel, leaving the crowbar in the ring with a 'message' for Collins's partner, "Hardcore" Harlacker!"
"In the next match, Johnny Haaglund was teaming with Triple J against the
Badger Boys in what would be the first tag-team match on SWP! The Badger
Boys, talented in the twos, were able to hammer out a victory when Haaglund was flung from the ring and Larry Czamura was able to get the 'Lights Out'
on Triple J. Rumor has it that the Badger Boys have a big announcement coming up this week.stay tuned!
"Haaglund, however, was gunning for a win in that match.he took out some frustration by tossing a humbled Triple J across the ring and stalking out like a spoiled brat. Spivey Michaels tore out of the backstage area, though, and dropkicked Haaglund off the ten-foot stage at the entrance!
Haaglund hit the floor hard, but was okay. What is with Michaels and his
"protection" of Triple J?
"The big Main Event was up next-Now, to put this into perspective.take four men who hate each other.then, split them in half and tell them to play nice.
It wasn't a pretty scene. The biggest surprise came when Damian Ozzbourne came out and pronounced his respect for Tuulenux. Ozzbourne is a man to whom respect is a rare commodity. However, he pledged fairness and cooperation for this match.
"Hades, the current World Title holder, came out third to the ring. He is clearly enjoying his new rank. Jean-Luc Page was last to the ring, disgusted at being passed up by the Commissioner and disgusted with
Tuulenux, the Number One Contender, whom he will be facing next week in a steel cage match!
"The match itself was brutal. Tables, chairs, and a frying pan all found their way into the ring or ring area. Hades took a brutal assault at the hands of Damian Ozzbourne, a born wrecking machine! Ozz put Hades through a table, but Hades returned the favor with an absolutely wicked tornado DDT that planted Ozz's face directly onto a steel chair, cutting his face and breaking his nose! Ozzbourne began the bloodletting that night, but he wasn
't going to be the last!
"Jean-Luc Page demonstrated his impressive technical superiority in the ring, but got caught with an Ozzbourne frying pan shot, busting his head open like a ripe melon. Page and Ozzbourne were bleeding like animals-then, double-damage-Hades was on the ring apron, in his corner, and Ozzbourne slammed him with the pan for good measure! Hades was also busted open.
Hades flew off the ring apron from the pan shot and slammed into the metal guardrails surrounding the ring. He hit headfirst and was out cold, leaving his tag-team partner to fend off those two animals by himself!
"Ozzbourne caught Page and administered his devastating finisher, The End, but Page somehow kicked out. That was the beginning of the end for
Ozzbourne. Ozz missed a springboard dropkick, his feet landing on the guardrail, so he was unable to get anything to keep him from slamming his head on the mats outside the ring. Ozzbourne took some good shots at the hands of Jean-Luc Page before being able to tag out to Tuulenux.
"Tuulenux missed his first shot at Page and fell victim to the Canadian
Collapse. Page could have wrapped up the match right there and then-but his mind is already on next week. Page saw the opportunity to deal some damage to Tuulenux, and while the green monster was out, Page grabbed a steel chair.
"Just before he could smack Tuulenux with the chair, Ozzbourne dove in front of the chair and took one of the biggest hits possible, knocking him unconscious, leaving him in a pool of his own blood! Ozzbourne gave the ultimate sacrifice for his partner-his own skull!
"Tuulenux capitalized on Jean-Luc Page's lack of focus after that event, and grabbed the pin with his finisher, The Descent! Hades was attempting to come back, but couldn't make it. Tuulenux and Damian Ozzbourne gained the win.
"However, the night wasn't over. 'Cowboy' Kirk Collins rushed to the ring and distracted Tuulenux, while, from out of the crowd, 'Hardcore' Harlacker stole into the ring and clobbered Tuulenux with his own crowbar! Tuulenux then fell victim once again to the 'Hardcore Death Drop'! The bad blood between Tuulenux and Harlacker continues to grow, and you just know that
Tuulenux will not let this die!
"'The Viking' Johnny Haaglund rushed to the ring to stop the cowboys' assault, but two-on-one was too much for Haaglund. However, before anything could happen to him, a hooded figure emerged from the crowd and slapped
'Hardcore' Harlacker in an ankle-lock submission! Frank Maddox is in the
SWP! Maddox and Haaglund were able to clear the ring with some help from
Hades-who used his World Title belt to thump Collins out of the ring. The three men were left in a stare-down that ended when Hades left the ring.
"Another huge night of action.What's next for the SWP? I don't know if I'm the one to answer that. All I can do is give you the brief rundown of what to expect this week!
"It will be a big night of matches. The first round of the Midwest Title
Tournament is beginning. The first match of the night will be 'The Viking'
Johnny Haaglund against 'The Wizard' Oz Walker. Haaglund is the odds-on favorite to walk away with this one, but don't count out Oz Walker-he has moves up his sleeve and a devastating superkick.
"The second match of the night will be 'Hardcore' Harlacker against Spivey
Michaels. Harlacker is the favorite in this match as well, but Michaels is a giant-killer. Don't count the scrappy Brit out.
"The third match will be The Harlem Hitman meeting 'Triple J' Jeremy Jasper
Jenkins. I'll give this match even money, with Triple J being the possible winner. Triple J is a much better technical wrestler than the Hitman, and combine that with his aerial skills, I'd almost bet on this match.however, if my mother taught me anything, it's never bet on a street hustler from
Harlem. The Hitman could pull this one out.
"The fourth and final match of the first round for the Midwest Title has been changed. 'Cowboy' Kirk Collins was set to meet Damian Ozzbourne, however, since Ozzbourne's injuries in the tag-match this week, he will not be cleared to wrestle. Ozzbourne suffered a broken nose and a concussion, as well as a few stitches. Therefore, taking Ozzbourne's place will be the man who had Collin's friend, 'Hardcore' Harlacker, in that vicious
ankle-lock, Frank Maddox! "This match could go either way.it just depends on who wants it more. Maddox is a skilled technician, but he'll have to get through 6-feet-5-inches-three-hundred pounds of pure Texas brutality. "The final match of the evening this week will be the Number One Contender's
Match between Tuulenux, the reigning top contender, and Jean-Luc Page, the man who wants that post! This match, as decreed by Commissioner Sandstrom, will take place in a steel cage! The winner is the first man who gets out of the cage alive!
"There will be a whole night of thrills, spills, and probably, surprises on our way to 'Wicked Ways' on June 28. This next night of SWP action is not to be missed! The last show before a PPV! Too much to see, too much to think! I'm drained just thinking of the action!
"For all of us at SWP, I'm Jason Havner. Thanks for joining me." [The camera fades to a spinning SWP logo.] * * * * * * * * Haaglund v. Walker Harlacker v. Michaels Hitman v. Triple J Collins v. Maddox Page v. Tuulenux in a cage for the #1 contender's spot. Flashes due by WEDNESDAY JUNE 19, 9:00PM CST --If you can't get them in by that time or need extra time or whatever, please let me know.better to cut deals than to job out when you have a shot.. Thanks! Sean
