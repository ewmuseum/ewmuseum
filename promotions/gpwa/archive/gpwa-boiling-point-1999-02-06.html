There is a large GPWA banner in the background, with a row of minature flags (Texas, Oklahoma, and Kansas). There are two rows of monitors displaying matches from End of the Beginning -- specifically the top rope bulldog by Tad Detmer on The Angry Chef for the pin. GPWA merchandise is placed all around the studio in a messy fashion that still looks classy to sports fans. There are two chairs on the stage.]
[The music begins to fade as the lights come up. Dexter St. Claire and Timmy Uyuklu take their seats. Dexter is wearing standard issue GPWA dark blue slacks and sport coat with a white shirt and dark tie. Timmy is wearing a Hawaiian flowerdy shirt and shorts with Converse canvas tennis shoes.]
St.Claire: Hello wrestling fans! Dexter St.Claire and Timmy "The Turk" Uyuklu filling in for Dante Rogers and Melvin Moeller who are both at home with the flu this week. I can't begin to tell you how happy I am to be your host here today!
Uyuklu: Speak for yourself St.Claire. I was nice and warm on vacation in Hawaii when they called me up and said I had to do this gig. Only reason I'm here now is because they said I'd be doing this show with Victoria Matthews.
So just get your young incompetent ass out of that chair and go find Victoria. St.Claire: Sorry Turk. Victoria is away on a special assignment. Uyuklu: WHAT?!?! St.Claire: She's on a spec... Uyuklu: Ok! I heard you the first time! You can bet your candy ass that Musfurter is going to hear about this! St.Claire: Ok....Well....I guess we will go ahead and roll the first clip. Uyuklu: It better be someone worth seeing! St.Claire: "Bullywug" Glenn Hud... Uyuklu: Oh god! Just roll it! [Off mic] Does anyone have any aspirin? This is going to be a long show.
***** ["Bullywug" Glenn Hudson sits out on a roadside curb, watching the world do it's worldly thing. The wind blowing in his brown hair, Hudson takes a deep breath of fresh air, a day-dreaming smirk appears on his face when he exhales. He rests his hands on his denim-clad knees and peers out into his surroundings a little longer.. before turning his head towards us and giving a knowing nod.]
Wug: Cleansing. So cleansing being out here. Just a roadside. I only really appreciate times like this now and then. Usually I'm too busy, too preoccupied, or already feel I have my stuff in perspective.
[A car quickly passes by, and speeds away in the distance.] Wug: But out here.. it's gives me a chance to ponder the really important things in life. Like how best I could one-up American Patriot's new best friend. Or what exact species of insect has made a new home in Hawk Hunter's rectum. Or whether I should take some time out and refine my Jordan Stone impression a little more. Then sometimes I get face with the really hard ones. Whether to make fun of Larry Pickens' immediate family, or maybe travel back a few generations and maybe find an even better raw nerve on
Chickens Pickens to hit with a sledgehammer.. [Glenn chuckles to himself. He's probably already come to a decision on the latter, but just brought it up to piss that special someone off.]
Wug: But you can't avoid the more serious things in life. Like work. Poe found out, at work, that when I'm not half asleep with my head up my butt,
I'm still Glenn Hudson. And that usually means one thing, which I won't spoil for those of you who haven't stepped into the squared circle with me yet.
[Another car passes, going the opposite way.] Wug: But I will say this. I am a man of earthly vices. There are some things
I just _have_ to do. One of those things is having fun at the expense of
Pickens' Army, whether it be the Librarian, the Chef, the Kelp-Farmer, the
Wildlife-Photographer, or even the Little General himself. Until the band runs out of songs, the dance continues, boys. So keep practising those steps because you may need to perform them sooner than you think.
[Fade to asphalt.] ********* St.Claire: Glenn Hudson serving notice to the Pickens Army.... Uyuklu: Let me tell you something son. No one serves anything to the Pickens
Army. They simply take whatever they want and what they want is that punk
Detmer's head on a platter. My money's on the Pickens Army accomplishing that task.
St.Claire: Well Turk, I agree that the Army has runoff more than their fair share of enemies but I don't think they will be getting rid of the new Great
Plains Heavyweight Champion, Tad Detmer, very soon.
Uyuklu: First of all, Detmer stole that belt. Larry Pickens will not rest until that poor excuse of a V.P. we have around here strips that belt from
Detmer and gives it to it's one and only rightful owner..... Larry Pickens!
St.Claire: Well that remains to me seen. Up next we are going to hear from the former Great Plains Tag-Team champs.
[Timmy buries his face in his hands and shakes his head back and forth.] Uyuklu [muttering]: Why me? Oh God why me? [Dexter looks at Turk strangely and then looks back into the camera.] St.Claire: Roll that clip. ********* [We are at the Detroit Auto Show. There are people milling around everywhere, looking at next year's cars (or possible cars). One particularly eye-catching prototype is the Dodge Charger R/T, an extremely sporty looking four-door. And this is where we find Jimmy
Lightning and Billy Thunder, as Thunder is pointing out various details to a pretty blonde woman. Lightning is standing next to a
mocca-skinned woman wearing dreadlocks. The entire group is dressed casually, with Thunder & Lightning both wearing sweatshirts, jeans and work boots. Thunder's shirt has
"MOPAR" on the front, while Lightning's shirt reads: "PARENTAL ADVISORY: EXPLICIT CONTENT" As soon as Jimmy realizes he's on camera, he ignores everything else.] Jimmy Lightning: YO!....Wuzzup GPWA! Thunder an' Lightning here in Dee-Troit, checkin' out tha latest in superfly rides. But I suppose we gotta get serious for a minute. We gotta talk about that last match of are's, at Enda tha Beginning. Talk to 'em, Big T!
Billy Thunder: Well, in case anybody didn't notice, we lost our belts. And we only have ourselves to blame. Ya see, everytime we've got in the ring, we *guaranteed* that we'd walk away with a victory.
But if you replay End of the Beginning, you'll notice that in all the excitement, all the pre-match posturing, we somehow forgot to guarantee winning. Now, there might have been some contributing factors, like the fact that the match was in reality three men against two, but we're not gonna cry about that. We take full responsibility for losing the titles.
Jimmy Lightning: An' Black an' Orange, ya'll can be damn sure that ya didn't win belts 'cuz you were the better team! <BLEEP!>, even
Mr. Brown got fed up with your two non-wrestlin' butts! But we ain't mad atcha. We held them titles for a good ten months an' we're damn sure that you won't be wearin'em anywhere near that long!
Billy Thunder: Black and Orange....We know....All the fans know....And most of all, *you* *know*....In a fair fight, you couldn't beat us on your best night. So enjoy your title reign while it lasts, 'cause it won't be long. Right now, we're gonna sit back and enjoy the cars....and the ladies....[Their dates put their arms around them.]....but Thunder and Lightning aren't goin' anywhere.
Jimmy Lightning: That's right, Thunder an' Lightning will most definitely be back in tha mix real quick....And we *guarantee* that!
********* Uyuklu: Just once I'd like to see those guys say something that wasn't an excuse for them being so pathetic.
St.Claire: I would hardly call them pathetic. They held those belts for a very long time with a lot of tough competition chasing after them.
Uyuklu: I only have one thing to say on the subject. The right guys have the belts now. St.Claire: You might be in the minority with that opinion, Turk. Uyuklu: That just goes to show you that the majority isn't always right. St.Claire: Ok... Well let's hear from another tag team that's been climbing the ranks fast here in the GPWA. The Man & The Myth better pay close attention to this team because they have gold in the future in my opinion.
Uyuklu: Just goes to show how dumb you are. Roll the stinking clip. [Off mic] Where in the hell are those aspirin!?!?
******** [In a dark room, Time Stands Still sits a couple of feet apart in seperate chairs. Their hair is over there eyes and and the lighting only allows you to see that they have on matching outfits and are smoking what looks to be cigars.]
Damon: The biggest card in ICBINR's history and I only got one word... Damien: ...BORING! Thompson jumps ship... Damon: ...Stone wins again... Damien: ...BIG DEAL! Damon: Some idiot decides to make an entrance from a big cardboard volcanoe... Damien (sarcasticly): ...oooooohhhh,aaaaaaaaaahhhhh..... Damon: OH BUT WAIT! THE ARSENAL IS HERE! Damien: WHO!?!? Damon: I forgot already. Damien: Don't forget Man and Myth winning the belts. Damon: We'll get to that later, but I guess you have to lose in order to get title shots. Damien: And let's not forget that some jerk decides to take a leak in the ring. Damon: So why was it boring? Damien: No Time Stands Still! We had just finished pinning what was then the tag team champs. And what do we get?
Damon: Backstage passes, and finger sandwiches. Damien: Look, if you want to turn this fed around stop putting losers in matches. Damon: But I guess they think TST ain't for real. Damien: Well let's show them a side of TST that they ain't seen yet. Damon: And how about this, a non-title triangle match. Damien: Will they go for that? Damon: Only time will tell! (Camera fades to black) ********** St.Claire: The Drake brothers asking for a non-title triangle match there. Definitely a match that could put some people in the seats. Uyuklu: More like a match that could put some people to sleep. You want a match that will put some people in the seats? A match that would be worth the price of admission?
St.Claire: Well, I'm afraid to say yes, but what match do you think would do that? Uyuklu: Tad Detmer vs the Pickens Army in a cage, Tad has to wear a straight jacket and the Army gets ball bats. Now that would be worth the price of admission!
St.Claire: Why do I get the feeling that only you would enjoy that Turk? Uyuklu: What do you mean only me? Larry Pickens would enjoy it too! St.Claire: Yes, I'm sure he would but I don't see it happening. Ok, we have to take a quick commercial break but when we come back. We are going to hear from the man that was crowned ICBINR U.S. Heavyweight Champion at End Of The
Beginning...
Uyuklu: MISS TIFFANY?!?! St.Claire: Ummmm....No...Reginald Parkhurst III. Uyuklu: But Miss Tiffany will be there? St.Claire: I'm sure she will be Turk. Uyuklu: That's all that matters. [Fade to commercial] ********** [The scene opens to two women setting in a coffee shop. Both women are decent looking and wearing secretarial type clothing.]
Woman 1: I don't know what to do. I'm stuck in a dead end job and I can barely make ends meet anymore. I called the local junior college and they said it would take two years to earn a degree. I don't know what I'm going to do!

[The woman gets a look of surprise and joy on her face.] Voiceover: Bill collectors and financiers have you down? Boss been nagging you to finish that project for little or no pay? Join the high paying field of prostitution! The Masters & Johnson College can have you off your feet and on your back in weeks. With our professional staff and learn at your own pace classrooms. You can be earning the big bucks that professional prostitutes make each day.
[The scene changes to Woman 1 in a motel room. You can only see her from the shoulders up as her head bounces closer and closer to the headboard.]
Woman 1: Wow! I never knew it could be so easy! Voiceover: Vaginal, oral, anal, S&M, bondage and even domination techniques can be yours to use on your road to financial freedom.
[The scene changes to a skinny caucasian man wearing a genuine fur coat with lots of gold chains, a black fedora with a big feather band and rings studded with diamonds on his hands.]
Man: Last week I was about ready to file for bankruptcy. This week I'm here to tell you that pimpin' aint never been easier!
Voiceover: That's right men! Call now and you can enroll in our 6 week Pimpin' 101 course! Man: Come on over here and let me show you some of these fine ho's my friend. Voiceover: Masters & Johnsons College is SMBDA Certified. ********** ["Psycho Man," by Black Sabbath plays as the camera opens on the Boiling Point studio. Timmy pops a couple of aspirin in his mouth as the lights fade back up.] St.Claire: We're back! Uyuklu: You say that like it's a good thing. St.Claire: It is a good thing Turk. Uyuklu: Not if you just flew back in from a shortened vacation in Hawaii it isn't. St.Claire: Ok, well we have a clip from Reginald Parkhurst...Along with Miss
Tiffany, Turk.
Uyuklu: Well at least my eyes will be happy to be here. ********** [Black Screen.] Announcer: And now, a word from *your*.... [A video package rolls as the announcer continues.] .....United States Heavyweight Champion.... [Shot of Reginald holding the U.S. belt from End of the Beginning.] .....The Master of the Piledriver.... [Jump cuts of Reginald executing a few of his versions of the piledriver, with the last one being the Scarsdale Spike.]
.....*Reginald*....*Parkhurst*....the *THIRD!!!* [A still of Reginald in his robe, the belt around his waist, and Miss Tiffany at his side looking beautiful. The screen then dissolves into a shot of a wooden door that reads: REGINALD PARKHURST, III The door is opened carefully from the inside (making it impossible to see inside) and the lovely Miss Tiffany exits, holding a folder.
Today she is wearing a fuschia jacket, which seems likely to unbutton at any moment, and matching skirt and high heels. As she walks away from the door to a nearby desk, the camera focuses on her shapely legs.]
Miss Tiffany: Um....Over here! [The camera shifts position as Tiffany sits down behind a relatively large wooden desk, complete with computer, intercom phones, desk blotter, fancy looking pen holder and paper tray, etc. Prominently displayed is a carved wooden nameplate that reads "MISS TIFFANY".
Windows line one wall, which offers a good view of the metropolis below. As the camera takes this in, we realize that we are in the office outside of Parkhurst's corner office.]
Miss Tiffany: Mr. Parkhurst has given me a statement to read. He states.... "I, Reginald Parkhurst III, United States Heavyweight Champion and Master of the Pildriver, want to apologize to all of my fans for not being available at this time. I realize that, although Miss Tiffany is truly a capable personal assistant, my fans would prefer to see me directly. However, as a direct consequence of my status as United
States Heavyweight Champion, I am currently in the final stages of negotiations for several lucrative advertising endorsements."
Miss Tiffany: And I think it's safe to say the Mr. Parkhurst encourages everyone watching to use Crotch-Blok brand anti-itch cream, the Melt-A-Belly Diet Plan, Anal Ice hemoroid medicine--
Reginald Parkhurst: [On the office intercom] MISS TIFFANY!!! Just read what I wrote on the paper!
Miss Tiffany: Yes, Mr. Parkhurst! Reggie--Er, Mr. *Parkhurst* further states.... "I want to thank all of my many fans for their thousands upon thousands of letters, faxes, and e-mails congratulating him on his victory. Though I won't be able to answer them all because of the sheer volume, I want my fans to know that I care about them sincerely and that I, Reginald Parkhurst III, the Master of the Piledriver, will demonstrate my deep appreciation by defending my United States
Heavyweight Championship against only the most qualified challengers, assuming that President Stillwell can find any qualified challengers.
And when I do do come to your backwater, pile-of-cow-dung town to defend my Championship, I request that you treat me with respect and admiration that Reginald Parkhurst III, Master of the Piledriver and
United States Champion deserve." Miss Tiffany: Mr. Parkhurst has no further statement at this time. [She presses the intercom button] Right, Mr Parkhurst? Reginald Parkhurst: Of course I have no further comment! Didn't I tell you I was in an important meeting?!?!? [The sound of a video game can be heard in the background.] Now, don't interrupt me until my next appointment!
Miss Tiffany: Well, gentleman, I'm afraid that's all there is for today. [The screen dissolves back into the still of Reginald in his robe, the belt around his waist, and Miss Tiffany at his side looking beautiful while the announcer voices over.]
Announcer: The preceding announcement was brought to by your United States Champion, the Master of the Piledriver, Reginald Parkhurst III. ********* Uyuklu: Now that's how I like to see Parkhurst. St.Claire: He wasn't even in the shot. Uyuklu: He wasn't? I barely noticed. St.Claire: Ok, well I recently had an interview with Larry Pickens from his hospital room after End Of The Beginning. Here's what he had to say.
Uyuklu: Finally some quality programming. ********* (We see Dexter St. Claire in a hospital setting, he's talking to a lady at a desk and she points down the hall. The camera follows him down until they get to room #405. They slowly peek inside and the first sight we see is Larry Pickens laying bedside to what appears to be the Violent
Librarian in a partial body cast. Larry is seen picking through VL's food until he notices the camera)
Pickens: GET THE HELL OUT OF HERE! St. Claire: But Mr. Pickens ...... Pickens: How did you get here? St. Claire: We were instructed to get some words from you and we called your house, but Mrs. Pickens told us to look for you here.
Pickens: It figures you were sent here. That damn fool Musfurter sent you to rub it in my face. Well, I'm not gonna disappoint. I was gonna prepare some words, but I think this particular circumstance deserves some free form. Ahem.
(Larry adjusts his shirt and tie, then licks his hair back and takes a serious stance) Pickens: I know what you want Musfurter, but you aren't going to get it. I'm a calm, cool, and collected Larry Pickens. Now the results of the End of the Beginning can only be described as the Good, the Bad, and The Ugly
....... NOT TO MENTION THE BULL<beep>! DAMN YOU MUSFURTER .... I KNOW YOU
SET ME UP ... YOU AND THAT PINT SIZED TWERP! St. Claire: Mr. Pickens please. Some constraint. (Larry re-adjusts himself, licks his hair back again and begins calmly) Pickens: In the first event of the night, we came in and did exactly as we promised. We rid the GPWA of some long overdue to be hauled away trash. We took him to the curb, then booted him into the can ourselves.
And my Librarian walked away from the fray unscathed. St. Claire: He doesn't look to unscathed ... Pickens: Shut up junior! That man could still punk you out in a full body cast so watch your mouth. Anyways ... thats where things turned against me. Me, honest Larry Pickens. I go out everyday and play by the rules to give every fan his due enjoyment. Me, your old buddy Larry

St. Claire: Its getting pretty deep in here. Pickens: St. Claire, one more peep out of you and you're getting a one way trip to Palookaville! Not only did you just sour the actions of my
Librarian. We worked hard ... well it was actually pretty easy ..... that guy is a chump after all, but anyways .... we worked to rid you of a useless redneck taking up salary room, but our life is in grave danger .....

UP! Anyways, is that the final straw? No, of course not. They have the gall to let that piece of whale ass sneak into the match after the Chef had it won against his opponent, after wrestling one match already, then he sneaks out and does a total Pearl Harbor job on him! Not once did we even face the wrestler we were supposed to, nonetheless we persevered and paid the price trying to give the most to the fans. Tad Detmer, you are a vericose vein on the leg of society. Frankly, that belt should be shipped straight here since there was never a contract against Tad Detmer, but thats fine. We'll sidestep all that crap .... we just want our re-match.
Straight up. Detmer is cowering at the thought because he KNOWS he can't beat a member of the Pickens Army. Every time, it ends the same. Detmer down for the three, even stretchered out on a couple of occasions. This time will be no different. I've had enough of your plotting and conniving

Detmer's ass in no time. St. Claire: I think we got what we came for. Thank you Mr. Pickens. Pickens: And a hearty bugger off to you. Get out of here, we have serious Army business to take care of.

Uyuklu: You better make sure your insurance is paid up after Larry Pickens hears that. St.Claire: I'll be sure and do that Turk.... Larry demanding a rematch for the title and word coming from the GPWA front offices says he's going to be getting it at GPWA On The Road.

Why don'tcha join me Dex. I bet you'd be a hit on the floor baby. Get that funk flow throuhg ya and we'll have you doing tyhe
Charleston in a matter of minutes. St. Claire: Thanks Jive. Maybe I will join ya after I finish my duties here. Jive: Great Dex. I'll see ya then and bring them boogie shoes. (Scene fades to black as Dex tries some very awkward looking dance step)
