Rules and Regulations 1. What is the goal of the WWWA? The goal of the WWWA is to continue to provide a solid Fantasy wrestling experience to all of those involved and NOT involved in the league, and to continue on it's current path of growth and prosperity through continued construction of solid entertainment in the form of well written and well crafted gimmicks and angles, by means of a talented writing staff, in fact, the best writer's in the ewrestling world (in fact, the writing staff currently only contains one person - me :).

What are the titles here? 3.1 WWWA World Wide Heavyweight Championship The biggest title in the WWWA, and a major player in the overall world of fantasy wrestling. This title is the Hardiest belt to get in the WWWA because only the toughtest and the greatest get the chance to harnest it.
Only the best of the best can hold this title, and it changes hands very unoften. 3.2 WWWA World Tag Team Heavyweight Championship This is the title for the tag team division, which has active competition in the WWWA. If you think you have what it takes, submit a team, and, well, take that team to the top; if you can! Bwahahahahaha!! (sorry I had to add that in for effect)
3.3 WWWA Extreme Hardcore Heavyweight Championhsip This has gotta be one of the most violent titles in the sport today. Past matches have ranged everywhere from C4 matches, electrocution matches, spidernet death matches (which is a net in the ring which contains broken glass, thumbtacks, nails, etc) and other very violent and ignorant ways of pulverizing each other.
3.4 WWWA World Light-Heavyweight Championship This is for wrestlers how compete in the weight of 240 lbs and under, to no minimum weight. The best lighweights and juniors in the WWWA go for the gold here, so let's see if your guy can make the cut!
3.4 WWWA Continental Heavyweight Championship This is a secondary belt for the wrestlers that aren't good enough for the Heavyweight Championship in the WWWA but it is a hard belt to get still.
3.5 WWWA National Heavyweight Championship This is the the "Extra" belt you might say or you can say it is like the
T.V. Title in the WCW, the belt doesn't mean much to most people.
4. What are the cards in the WWWA? 4.1 Monday Night Voltage Monday Night Voltage is the flagship show of our two weekly cards. It happens every Monday, and the biggest names in the WWWA compete on this card.
4.2 Thursday Overdrive Thursday Overdrive is our other weekly card, which not only showcases the big names in the WWWA, but gives the new and underdeveloped talent a chance in the spotlight. It happens every Thursday.
4.3 PPV's Pay Per Views are held every 3 weeks on Saturday's only. Some of the
PPV's that are comming up are the following:
- Hardcore Heaven - Straight To Hell - Meltdown - Virese - Wrestledom - Winter War (Ha, this one won't be for awhile!) 4.4 Non PPV Supercards We occasionally throw in a non PPV supercard every now and then. Here they are: - King of Extreme Tournament - Holy Hell - Continental Bash These don't happen too often, so don't get too concerned with them in any particular. 5. Who are the staff members? 5.1 Front Office 5.1.1 Commissioner Rhett Johnson Commissioner Johnson is a good Commish, when he wants to be. Johnson plays by only one rule: my way or the highway. He is all for himself and his gang of troops, who carry out orders for him on a whim.
5.2 Announcers and Interviewers 5.2.1 Jimmi Ball Jimmi Ball is one of the top announcers in the game today. He announces Monday Night Voltage, and all of the major WWWA PPV's.
5.2.2 Lacey Road Lacey Road is one tough blonde! Although very sexy, she has a wrestling history herself, and can take out almost any man. She announces for WWWA Thursday Overdrive, and some of the non PPV supercards.
5.2.3 Shane Street Somewhat of a nerd boy, who can always manage to say the wrong thing at the wrong time, Shane Street is the main interviewer in the WWWA. He also acts as a replacement commentator on the WWWA shows, if something happens to one of the other regular commentators.
5.3 Referees 5.3.1 Michael Wizard Michael Wizard is the head official in the WWWA. He is very stringent with the rules, and gives a good, moderate count. He always stands up for what is right in the ring, sometimes too often.
5.3.2 George Ford Ford is very lax with the rules. He counts to quick, and most of the time he'll just turn a blind eye to some serious cheating, as he used to be a rule bending wrestler himself. He has retired, though, and now is a ref for the WWWA.
5.3.3 Kelly Hope Hope is the model ref. He doesn't count too slow nor too fast, he keeps everything fair, and encorporates the eye-for-an-eye feel in his matches. An overall perfect referee, if there ever was one.
5.4 Commentators 5.4.1 Paul Rip Rip is the typical unbiased commentator. He works hard at his play-by-play commentary, and he also has to work hard in just working with his partner, Mace White, who enjoys driving Rip up the wall. He will admit that he loves new talent, which explains the help he gaves to the new comers a leg in the WWWA. But he is the overall neutral character commentator in wrestling today.
5.4.2 Mace White The Black Jack is not only a slack jawed son-of-a-gun, he can also be a pervert. He's the type of fellow who roots for the women, and cheers on the rulebreakers. A classic villain, in every definition of the word.

Contacting Me 1. Simmed or Hand Written Results? All results are hand written and Simed in the WWWA, mainly by myslef. This lends to the best quality of cards that the WWWA could possibley have, and it makes things very flexible and fun for everyone.
2. How matches are deteremined Matches are determined mainly by the length and QUALITY of RPs that people submit, and if you do or do not submit a strat (AKA strategy) for your matches. Simple as that, and winners and outcomes are determined from there by me, and by me only. I want to just state one thing about winning and losing, is that your wrestler will lose, and at some point everyone loses a big match, so don't be upset if it happens. It will happen. This league is fair to all participents, as long as everyone participates. The bottom line is that the more you participate, the more you will win. Simple as that.
3. Roleplaying Roleplaying, better known as RPing, is the foundation to the WWWA. I will go over RPing through a few different sections in a moment. Before I do that, though, I just want to say is that the more you RP, the more you will be succesful in the WWWA, simple as that.
3.1 Using the RP board The RP board on our site is only to be used for RPs, and RPs only. Any other message will be either deleted or ignored from the board. The other board on our site, the Message Board, is used for all purpose and generic messages. RPs can be of any length at all, small or large, and can use any topic at all, as long as it DOES NOT violate the RP rules listed a little farther down below. Otherwise, have fun RPing!
3.2 Writing RPs Pretty much see above for writing RPs, and below for the Rules and Regs of the RP board. 3.3 RP posting Rules and Regulations Any breach of the rules and/or regs of the RP board can result in a stiff punishment handed out and decided upon at the time of the offending
RP. And, on another note, people do NOT need to tell me what RPs are offensive and what RPs are not. I make the decision there, whether to punish or do whatnot to the offending poster. It is between me, that poster, and the victim of the RP (if there is one). It is NOT anyone else's business, otherwise.
Here are the rules for posting RPs: - you cannot use another person's character without direct permission from that person. I will be giving out a list of people to contact, who they handle, and various contact methods at the beginning of next week. Breach of this rule can result in severe punishment
- you cannot book matches on the RP. That means you can't say that there will be a match on a certain date. Only I can do that, as TWO people are head bookers of WWWA.
- you cannot make any racial, religious, or basically any offensive and harassing remarks to other on the boards. This will result in dismissal from the WWWA, as we don't stand for prejudism in any shape or form
- you cannot post rules and/or regulations on the board. Only I can do that, as I run the WWWA. This will result in severe punishment - acts of violence cannot become too violent. Any acts involving such things that would permanently or severly injure another wrestler are prohibited. Examples of the such are shootings, stabbings, killings, paralyzing, and others along this line. You need permission from me before you can post such thing. It wouldn't make much sense to shot someone, then wrestle them the next day, would it?
- foul language is fine, as long it is kept low. If you need more clarification on this, I can provide you with rules on what to and what not to say if you email me at
[detail withheld] - you cannot write an RP for another person - non RP posts are relegated to the Message Board ONLY - final rule is to have fun and be creative with your RP. The more unique it is, the more attention it will get 3.4 RP due dates RPs due dates are to be announced every week in the newsletters. If you do not have a match for that week, you are still very welcome to RP, but the basis is that you need to RP at least once per match. So if you have two matches that week, you'll have to RP once for each match (at least). There is no limit on the amount of RPs you can post.
4. Strats Strats are good tools to control how your wrestler acts in a match. Below is guidelines to writing a quick strat. Remember, a strat only takes about 30 seconds to write.
4.1 Writing Strats Here is a list of things to include in a strat. The strat should only take about 30 seconds to write
- wrestlers entrance (if this isn't included, a defualt one will be used) - wrestlers strategy for the match - suggested spots or ideas for things to happen in the match - what your wrestler would do if he or she won or lost - other intereferences or run ins you want to make during that card 4.2 Strat due dates Due dates are announced periodically, same as RPs. One final note on strats, they will NOT be followed to a T by writers of your match, but will be used as a guideline to how you want your character to act in said match.
5. Message Board The same rules apply to the message board as the RP board. The Message board is used to post such things as generic messages, challenges that you don't want to make in an RP, questions, comments, and occasionally opinions, and any thing that you can think of.
6. Making Challenges I prefer it if you make challenges on the RP board or the Message board, as people can view it and respond to it, rather than sending challenges to me.
7. Interviews If you are scheduled for an interview (interview schedules are released the preceeding week), you need to send one in to me. An interview can cover any topic in the world your wrestler wants to talk about, but I liketo keep them in line to what is currently developing in the fed. So basically it's best to stay on topic here. Think of the interviews as being similar to those that you see on RAW on the WWF every week. Write according to that, as they only need to be a paragraph or two long. There is no punishment for failing to submit an interview, other than the fact that you won't get an interview for quite awhile.
8. Intereference/Run-Ins If you want to run into a match and interefere, either email it to me or include it in your strat, and I'll let you know whether you'll be able to do it or not.
9. Newsletter Updates and topics can be found at the site, but are mainly in the newsletter, which is distrubuted after every card, and sometimes more. The newsletters include: news, hot topics, rankings, stables, results, upcoming cards and more.
