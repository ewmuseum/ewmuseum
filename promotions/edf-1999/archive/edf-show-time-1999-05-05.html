EDF Show Time

5 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Tuesday Show Time begins in Chicago, Illinois with fireworks exploding. Some signs read "Rocky Rules!" "We want the PEOPLE’S TAG TEAM!" and “Future EDF
World Champ: ShadowHawk!”)
Patrick: Welcome to Tuesday Show Time! We have three great matches tonight! Romo: That’s right! And the first one is the best! Mervin vs Dhamon ShadowHawk! Patrick: Dhamon trying to get revenge for an earlier loss to Mervin. But the what a main event! The undefeated Blade vs. The Self Proclaimed People’s Champ,
The Rock.
Romo: Self proclaimed is right! He has no shot at being the champ. Patrick: And what about that huge announcement the prez has for us today! Romo: It’s huge! He told me this morning what it was! Patrick: What? He did not. You’re lying. Romo: Am not. It’s going to spread waves through EDF. Patrick: Then what is it if you know? Romo: I’m not telling, you’ll just have to wait. Patrick: That’s what I thought. (No Chance in Hell Plays as ICP comes out and walks to the ring) ICP: YO YO YO.. (Crowd Cheers) ICP: Of course, you all know the.... Crowd yells: ICP IN DA HOUSE ICP: There will be a tag match tonight and I will be in it. But who will my partner be? Well my partner will be the second member of the TEAM PEOPLE
(Crowd Cheers) My new stable. And when you make a group you look for members you can trust. Members you can rely on. You look for blood. So with no more of this bull
(bleep) talk, lets get this team going
(Crowd Cheers) The second member of TEAM PEOPLE (No Chance in hell music plays as a big man walks to the ring with a shirt that says PFC) ICP: That’s right, this is my tag team partner and my brother PFC (Crowd Cheers) PFC: No more fun and games, New Age Outlaws. It is time to listen what I have to say. This is what I have to say. NO CHANCE IN HELL this tag team will lose in a people’s street fight
(Crowd Cheers) ICP: Well New Age Outlaws you might have thought this would be an easy 2 on 1. If I were you I would take some time to think of a new game plan. (Crowd Cheer as ICP and PFC leaves the ring) Patrick: Whoa! ICP has a brother and now they are together! Romo: What a development! Brothers to turn on each other! Patrick: WHAT?! Romo: All brothers do that! It will happen! Patrick: I don’t know about you. Michael: Introducing first, 6’2” and 245 lbs. from Morecambe, England, MERVIN
(Mervin walks to the ring with the only sound being crowd reaction. The crowd is booing loudly for him. Mervin gets a mic)
Mervin: Hey Shadowhawk! You'd better believe that I want you another match, even though I nearly snapped you in two the first time. I'm just sick and tired of you and your interfering, so lets make this a hardcore rules match that means no DQ AND falls count anywhere! The choice is yours BOY. You can go out in a blaze of glory by appearing along side me, or you can go on fighting undercard matches against nobodies
Michael: His opponent, accompanied to the ring by Jennifer L. Hewitt, a 6’2” and 245 lbs. from Blair, WI, DHAMON SHADOWHAWK
(DX theme theme plays while Dhamon walks to the ring. The crowd is booing loudly for him.)
Dhamon: You want a non-DQ, falls counts anywhere match? Are you stupid? I’m going to kick your ass!
Ding, ding Patrick: Here we go! Mervin looks pissed off! Can’t blame him, ShadowHawk left him to get slaughtered last week!
………. Patrick: Hawk with the advantage after that nice DDT! (ShadowHawk plays to the crowd. Mervin slowly gets up. Mervin grabs Hawk from behind and rolls him over for a cover. Todd Williamson counts …1…2 Kick out by
Hawk)
Patrick: Dhamon making yet another mistake by celebrating. He does it every match and it always kills him!
Romo: Go Hawk! Beat him! (ShadowHawk takes Mervin off his feet with a short-arm clothesline. ShadowHawk delivers a kick to the head of Mervin. ShadowHawk lifts Mervin up and drops him on the mat.)
Patrick: Hawk in complete control. Romo: Go Hawk! (ShadowHawk dumps Mervin over the top rope. Hawk picks Mervin up. Hawk attempts to throw Mervin in the steel stairs but Mervin reverses)
Romo: WHAM!!! Patrick: Dhamon his hard! That might have broken his shoulder! (Mervin slams Dhamon’s shoulder into the ring post once, twice, and a third time. Mervin suplexs ShadowHawk onto the announce table.)
Patrick: Holy crap! What a move! Hawk is still on the table and Mervin is coming this way! Romo: Mervin is on the table! They’re both right in front of us! Patrick: ARM BAR! Will Hawk submit after all the hits he has taken to his shoulder? Hawk is yelling but he hasn’t given up yet!
Romo: Hey Mervin! Let Hottie help! (Jennifer pokes Mervin in the eyes.) Patrick: What the heck is she doing! Romo: She slipped! Ahhhhh! (Mervin grabs Jennifer and points a finger at her. ShadowHawk grabs him. Hawk hits a reverse DDT onto the table. The table broke.)
Romo: I can’t believe he was going to hit her! Patrick: What! He was going to do no such thing! Heck of a move by Dhamon though. (Hawk grabs Mervin and Mervin hits Hawk with a low blow. Mervin takes a steel chair and slams it into Hawk’s shoulder.)
Patrick: Once again Mervin is working on that shoulder. He’s setting Hawk up. ARM BAR! He’s got it again! Romo: Don’t give up Hawk! You’re the best! Patrick: No! Where is she going? (Jennifer grabs the steel chair and slams it onto Mervin’s head once and then a second time.)
Patrick: No! That’s going too far even for a manager. (ShadowHawk rolls Mervin over and covers. Todd counts …1…2…3) Michael: Ladies and gentlemen, the winners of the match, DHAMON SHADOWHAWK!!! Patrick: I can’t believe what just happened! Romo: What a Hottie! What an ending! Hawk wins again! Patrick: Again?! That’s his first one! Romo: Details, details! Patrick: Holy cow, ShadowHawk went backstage fast. Like he had to go somewhere. Antway, the streetfight is about to begin. The New Age Outlaws, Helrazor, and
Kidman are already outside.
Romo: Maybe ICP and PFC or whatever won’t show up, those wimps. Patrick: I doubt it. (The People’s Team is seen walking out onto the street and the crowd inside erupts) Patrick: What an ovation! And now we’re ready to begin! (Kristine Timm signals for the start and the bell inside rings to official start the match.)
Patrick: This should be awesome! (Kidman goes after Road Dogg, Helrazor and ICP lock up, and Billy Gunn and PFC) ………. Patrick: This match is shortening every career here! Romo: Good thing they blocked off the street! (Dogg slams Kidman, Helrazor suplexs PFC to the pavement, and Gunn presses ICP and throws him down.)
Patrick: The Outlaws seem to be in control if one team can be in control of this match. Romo: This street fight is too organized! They cleaned up the darn street before the match and there are no weapons to use.
Patrick: Sorry, what do you want? Romo: Well, I want, huh! Headlights! Patrick: I see them too! They are heading towards the fight. (Kidman DDTs Road Dogg, Gunn attacks Kidman from behind, ICP is trying to reach his feet, PFC hits a powerbomb on Helrazor.)
Patrick: What is going on! A pick up just busted through the wooden barricades used to block off the street.
(ICP reaches his feet. The pick up heads right for ICP and slams on its breaks. It screeches to a halt and the side slams right into ICP.) Patrick: Oh my god! ICP got hit! Who is driving! Romo: Blade was driving! Dhamon is the passenger! That is why he left the last match so quickly!
Patrick: PFC is heading towards them. He throws a right but Blade blocks it. He slams PFC into the truck. Dhamon put ICP into the back. Blade is putting PFC into the back of the truck bed. Helrazor is stubblimg to his feet. He grabs
Dhamon and hits him once, twice, and a third time. Dhamon is leaning against the truck to stay on his feet.
Romo: Helrazor’s in trouble! Blade’s got him from behind! Patrick: Helrazor is up and ETERNAL NIGHT!!! Helrazor is down. Blade and Dhamon are getting in the truck, Gunn and Dogg are pummeling Kidman and their backs are to what is happening to ICP and PFC!
Romo: Gunn hit a rocker dropper on Kidman! (Gunn covers. Timm counts …1…2…3. Kristine Timm raises The Outlaws hands while the bell in the arena rings.)
Michael: Ladies and gentlemen, the winners of the match, THE NEW AGE OUTLAWS!!! Patrick: The Outlaws won the match but they looked confused on what just happened! They seen the very end when the truck speeded off.
Romo: Where is Blade going. He has a match! Patrick: It may be canceled! He may not care! He still has a little time as the prez will be making his announcement.
(It’s All Good [by Will Smith] plays and President Dale Halverson comes out with a mic. He walks down the ramp and gets into the ring.)
Dale: Ladies and gentlemen, you all heard that I have a huge announcement today. A week from tomorrow, a very important piece of paper will leave my desk.
Romo: that doesn’t tell much! Dale:That paper will have a very important diagram on it. It will have a diagram of the Championship Tournament!
(Crowd cheers) Dale: EDF has gone long enough without a champion. That diagram will have the bracket for the tournament. I wish all the wrestler luck!
(Crowd cheers) Patrick: What an announcement! A champion will soon be crowned! I can’t wait! Romo: My pre-bracket prediction is Dhamon ShadowHawk because he is the best. Patrick: Or because he may have you to help. Anyway, the last match is set to begin if it does happen. Who knows if Blade will be here.
Michael: Introducing first, a 6’5”, 275 lbs. wrestler from the South Pacific, THE ROCK!!! (Do you smell what The Rock is cooking?…. Rock theme plays. The Rock goes down to the ring and huge “Rocky” chants are heard through out the building.)
Michael Lancor: His opponent, accompanied to the ring by Jennifer L. Hewitt, a
6’5”, 265 lbs. wrestler from Taylor, WI, BLADE!!!
(Come With Me [Puff Daddy] plays. The crowd is booing loudly. About 5 seconds pass.) Patrick: Well, I don’t see him. If he doesn’t show, will it be a forfiet? Romo: Better not be! Of course, The Rock would take the easy win while Blade is busy. Michael Lancor: His opponent , accompanied to the ring by Jennifer L. Hewitt, a
6’5”, 265 lbs. wrestler from Taylor, WI, BLADE!!!
(Come With Me [Puff Daddy] plays. The crowd is booing even louder. About 5 seconds pass.) Rock: Blade! I don’t want a forfeit! I want you to get your roody (Crowd joins in and cheers) Rock: poo candy ass down here so I can check you into the Smackdown Hotel! (crowd erupts) Michael Lancor: His opponent, accompanied to the ring by Jennifer L. Hewitt, a
6’5”, 265 lbs. wrestler from Taylor, WI, BLADE!!!
(Come With Me [Puff Daddy] plays. The crowd is booing loudly. Blade walks out to the top off the ramp.)
Patrick: About time! He’s coming down the ramp and Rock is waiting in the ring! Romo: Blade stopped about half way down and is taunting Rock. (Dhamon ShadowHawk comes out from behind the crowd with a steel chair. Hawk slides into the ring)
Romo: SLAM!!! Rock got smashed! Patrick: A second chair shot and a third! Blade and Hewitt are in the ring now. Blade and Hawk are pummeling The Rock. Romo: Yes! Patrick: No! No! There setting The Rock up. Blade is going to end Rock’s career. Romo: ETERNAL NIGHT onto the chair! Yes!!! (Blade gets a mic and the crowd is booing loudly) Blade: Well, the title is as good as mine! (Crowd boos) Blade: Why? Easy. ICP, Mervin, and The Rock have all been defeated today! ICP’s career is finished and he and his brother at some gas station resting by the rest of the rhinos. Rock won’t ever be able to get up. Mervin lost his match and even though we didn’t take special measures to take him out, he doesn’t scare us. He’s not a contender.
(Crowd is getting louder) Blade: ICP, you said you were going to watch ShadowHawk so he couldn’t interfere. Well, we stopped you before you could stop him. And with you loss today, I am now the undesputted best wrestler in EDF. I am undefeated and between the undefeateds, I have wrestled the most. Wait, this match should be
The Rock by DQ. Nope! The bell never rang. The best wrestler in EDF is BLADE!!!
(Crowd boos even louder) (Blade, Dhamon ShadowHawk, and Jennifer L. Hewitt all leave to the back) Patrick: That piece of crap! He’s prowd that he may have ended some careers tonight! Romo: It was smart though! A great plan! Captured ICP and his brother so they can’t stop them from destroying The Rock!
Patrick: Blade is right, he won’t lose via DQ because the bell never rang but
The Rock will also not pick up a loss from being knocked out.
Romo: What a great night! Patrick: Well, I for one hope that they meet in the tournament and I hope The
Rock gets his revenge! That’s all for tonight folks! See you next week!
