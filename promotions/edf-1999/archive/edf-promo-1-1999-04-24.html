EDF — News

EDF Promo #1

24 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene starts with flashes of EDF wrestlers doing signature moves including
ICP, Dhamon ShadowHawk, Blade, and others.)
A man's voice: Their challenge, to become EDF WORLD CHAMPION. (Shows many clips from EDF weekly shows.) A man's voice: Which one will come out on top? (Shows clips of the wrestlers from shoulders up) A man's voice: Only one can be the First champion of this league that is unlike no other. (Camera switches and you see a man dressed very nicely) Hello. I'm Dale, the President of EDF. EDF has been running over three weeks now and when I started EDF, I didn't want to start this league like ALL the other leagues. There was no 16-man tournament to start the league. No, instead, the wrestlers must prove themselves first. I started with about 17 wrestlers and only 6 of those men are performing in the ring. We've had about
6 newcomers who have also performed in the ring. These men who are performing, will get spots in the Championship Tournament. All other leagues give these
Tourney spots to the first 16 who join, but EDF gives to the best 16 who perform in the ring. If there are 20 who qualify for consideration for the tourney, the best 16 will get in. Our first Champion will be a wrestler who has already proved himself in the ring and not some fluke who wrestled for one tournament. No, to qualify for this tournament, you must prove yourself before you get in. We will not make it a 16-man tournament if 16 men don't qualify.
There is still a little time to qualify for the tournament. Send in your application now and start wrestling and you could still qualify for the
Championship Tournament. EDF is challenging you to be the first man to be EDF
World Champion.
(Switches back to some more EDF highlights and then the words 'The Tournament' appear on the screen) A man's voice: It is coming. Limited time is what you have to qualify. Or have these images scared you?
