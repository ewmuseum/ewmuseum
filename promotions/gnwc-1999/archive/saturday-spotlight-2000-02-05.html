GNWC Saturday Spotlight [02/06/2000]

5 February 2000

The GNWC Spotlight logo raises up and the spotlights become brighter and brighter until the screen turns totally white and we cut into the special press room in GNWC Headquarters where Gold
Rogers and Commissioner Ben Tuckwell are sitting behind a desk with a big black screen directly behind them.]
GR: Welcome to our regular GNWC Saturday Program, SPOTLIGHT! I’m Gold Rogers and sitting beside me is the commissioner himself, Ben Tuckwell!
BT: Hey everyone, it’s nice to come back into Regina to tape this telecast. I hear we have a lot of interviews to get through today so Gold, start it now! GR: We begin the show with some footage from Drago that we missed for Tuesday Spotlight! (Scene opens Drago is eating in a Chinese restaurant. He turns and sees the camera) Drago: You know Strand I'm kinda getting a little pissed you see I could have sworn I won a wrestling match that made me the number one contender for the Hardcore championship. Now about a month has gone by and old Drago here has waited and waited and waited for his shot and it still hasn't come my way. Not to mention you bring in a mystery opponent who turns out to be some no name loser and he wins my hardcore title. So I'm gonna get down to the point here I want my shot this Riot I'm sick and tired of you loser's dodging me so this Riot I want what is rightfully mine a shot at the GNWC
Hardcore title. If I don't get this I'm gonna get really pissed and someone is gonna pay.
(Drago turns his back on the camera and the camera fades away.) BT: Even though I have an extreme disliking for Strand, you see Drago I book the majority of matches that go on the shows. I was the one who brought in the hardcore mystery opponent and he is quite a wrestler in my opinion! If you want a shot at the Hardcore Title, I’ll give you a shot at the Hardcore
Title because quite frankly, you don’t have the balls to compete in the hardcore arena! Next week at RIOT, you will have your shot against either
Dark Fox or Dennis Windham, get it?
GR: That sounds fair enough Mr. Tuckwell, that’s one match out of the way for RIOT in Cleveland! Our next piece of footage is from “The Asian
Bombshell” Oni Shugenja talking about his upcoming match with Ric Beauty! [Scene] The "Asian Bombshell" is standing in front of a black cloth back ground, looks like he is concentrating on words for this interview and not some gimmick.
[Oni] "First off I would like to welcome the new talent here in the GNWC,
Bedlam, Puah, Holloway, and Shawn Greene, I doubt any of you will last long, this fed is for the best of the best and unless your very good or very lucky then you will end up like Bob and his buddies!"
[Scene] Shugenja looks like he is trying not to laugh, then changes the subject. [Oni] "Speaking of new people, Ric Beauty comes in here, has a couple matches, then wants a shot at my gold? I don’t think so pal! After this riot you won’t be beautiful anymore! When you get in the ring with me you better be ready for the explosion! Because I’m a lot harder to beat than the ref at a pay per view, come to think of it, "The Bombshell" has never been beat!"
[Scene] "The Asian Bombshell" shifts around a bit, as if trying to think of how to word his next statement.
[Oni] "I'll finish off by talking about the pay per view, number one, I think Rage should have won that match, Ao didn’t stand a chance! Strand is just trying to help his favorite little friend because lets face it, Lord Ao needs all the help he can get! And Maul, if you want a shot at me, come and get it! I'll defend my title anywhere, anytime, against anybody, in any kind of a match! And finally, I like dark fox, he has been screwed over so many times and still has that fighting spirit! I'm sorry I beat the crap out of his little brother, but they are both great athletes! Dark fox WILL get his title back!"
[Scene] the screen fades to black. GR: Oni Shugenja sounded a man on a mission and almost sounded like he was guaranteeing that Dark Fox will regain his Hardcore Title at RIOT.
BT: Some of the stuff he said was right, like Rage should’ve won the title at Fatal Bonds but a lot of it was complete and utter bullcrap. I mean come on, Dark Fox doesn’t stand a chance against Windham because he isn’t good enough to beat him! Who’s next Rogers?
GR: I think that phrase is copyrighted by a certain company! BT: Shut up, I meant who is talking next! GR: Um… Lord Bradbury from Sussex in England! BT: Ah, the man who I hired back in Texas and now he wants a shot at my commissioner’s spot! I think this could be quite amusing so play the footage!
Lord Bradbury is sitting on the top deck of a yacht, He is wearing cabana wear, comfortable, all white clothes. From the looks of the background, he seems to be docked in some Caribbean port, but it is hard to tell. Bradbury takes a sip from what looks like ice tea, but knowing Bradbury, it’s probably got some kind of strong liquor in it. Bradbury confirms that by reaching his hand down under his beach chair, and pulling up a bottle of
Captain Morgan’s Puerto Rican Spiced Rum, and pouring a swig of it into his iced tea. He takes a sip from the straw, and begins to talk.
I have few great passions in this world, fine pipe tobacco, wrestling, and a good stiff drink. Anyway let’s get down to business. Here we are in beautiful Antigua, once a British island by the way. We are here for several reasons. One is cause I'm rich as hell and can do what ever I damn well please, two because I always love coming to Antigua, getting away from the horrible stress from working in North America, what an ungodly place that is. And three because; well there really is no three, so what the hell, lets get started
[At this point Bradbury stands up, grabs his favorite hickory cane with the brass head, and walks onto shore, the camera man follows, and the interview is conducted while walking along the sunny beaches of Antigua.]
This Hawaiian guy, Puah, is very amusing to me. He comes here acting like he's this nice Hawaiian. But let me tell you something, some of you may or may not know, but my best friend Mika Salo, (no, not the formula 1 driver)
is a former worlds strongest man, back in 1995. Now that was Puah's first year competing, and he was an asshole. When all the competitors would go out to have a good time, he would sit in his room and say we weren’t taking this seriously enough, and that he would smoke us all. Well when it came time for the competition, it didn't quite work out that way, see, he finished 8th out of 10 guys. Now that was the one and only year Mika competed in the competition, but I have no idea how he won that contest 3 times, he must be doing something to get an advantage. Hint Hint, Wink Wink. In fact I suggest you test this guy for any illegal substance, cause he is not the nice guy he pretends to be, he's just putting on a facade so he can screw all you fans over later.
[At this point Bradbury is absorbed into the hustle and bustle of St.Johns,
Antigua's Capital City]
BT: Hmm… I brought Puah into this company and I can guarantee Lord Bradbury that we do drug testing before accepting them into the GNWC. He is a legitimate three times World Strongest Man, I think he’s won it 3 times from the past 4 years and if he listens to the advice I give him, he could go far in this company!
GR: He has got the build to do well and I’m sure we will see at SLAM! When he takes on Michael Holloway, another newcomer!
BT: Unfortunately Gold, you won’t be at SLAM! Because I have hired a new commentator from back in Texas named Lance Richardson and alongside me, we will make the best commentating duo the World has ever seen!
GR: But… but that can’t be! BT: Heh, it is! Hit the next interview guys! (The camera fades in on a gym. Scotty Summers is practising on a punchbag. The punchbag has the outline of a man on it. Scotty turns round and grabs a bottle of water. He tips some of it over his head and drinks the rest, throwing the bottle at the cameraman.
SS: I think it's time that the world saw just how seriously I'm taking this match on Sunday. You see, I don't appreciate being taken advantage of an'
that's what’s been happenin'. I come in to the GNWC lookin' to prove how perfect I am to the world an' the "Asian Bombshell" decides that his mirror doesn't like my face! Now, we all know he's a rip-off of me, he wants to be what I am!! An' I don't appreciate that one bit. Then, I get screwed by Ben
Tuckwell, David Rage, and Thomas Rainka - the three stooges wannabes!
Believe me, they're all gonna pay when I'm through with them. I'm gonna show them a close up of what I can do when I'm mad!
And now I have to fight in a triple-threat keep your job match - and no surprise I win! So then, I'm given another tournament to win!! Well, you can treat me like that - but just watch what happens!!
(The camera fades out as Scotty starts hitting the bag again.) BT: Aren’t you going to start talking again Rogers? You still get to commentate on RIOT you know?
GR: Yes… BT: About that interview, Scotty Summers can practise on a punching bag all he likes but it isn’t going to help him one bit! I am the good looking one around here, the latest craze in fashion and he can call me a member of the three stooges all he likes but remember Mr. Summers, ACTIONS speak louder than words! Start talking Rogers before I make you!
GR: Yes, next we have Puah the Hawaiian Love Machine! BT: It seems like we have a lot of loving guys around this federation don’t you think Rogers?
(The scene opens at Hartsfield International Airport in Atlanta, Georgia. Today is an especially busy travel day for some reason or another. As the camera pans around this crowd seems as normal as any other day, except for one thing ... a 6'3”, 310lb. Hawaiian named Puah!! As the crowd rushes back and forth , everyone is quite careful not to bump into the big Hawaiian as he strolls casually toward boarding gate B12. With the cameras rolling ,
Puah begins to speak ...)
Puah: I don't understand it! They have direct flights from China to Los
Angeles , Norway to Texas but do you think they would have one from Hawaii to Carolina?! Heck no , they have to stop less then seven hours away and switch airliners! So now instead of doing my interview from the comforts of my luxury suite , I have to do it right smack dab in a crowd of frenzied businessmen and women. Ah well , I always did like Atlanta.
(Puah takes a sip off a can of diet coke he holds in his right hand and continues.) Puah: In less then two days , my debut here in the GNWC will finally be official. It'll be me and you one on one , Michael Holloway and I must admit
I am looking forward to the challenge. I have done my homework and studied the tapes of your past matches and one thing is for sure , you Mr. Holloway are one heck of a competitor.
(Puah gives a wink towards a female flight attendant as she walks by him pulling her rollaway luggage. She throws the big man a smile before continuing onward towards her destination.)
Puah: As I was saying Mr.Holloway , it will be a pleasure competing with an athlete of such calibre. I just hope that there will be no hard feelings after I defeat you. Afterall in this sport , it's all about winning ...
friendships come second. I have no doubts that you will take me to my limits Mr. Holloway , there is even a chance that you may sneak a victory away from me , that's just how it goes. You win some , you lose some. However , the "Hawaiian Love Machine"
doesn't lose very often. (Puah takes down the rest of his cola and tosses the can into the garbage. He approaches his boarding gate and the sun shining in from the large windows overlooking the airfield is blinding. He reaches into the pocket of his silk , Island print shirt and pulls out a pair of sunglasses, which he promptly places over his eyes and continues.)
Puah: Now three days after my encounter with Mr. Holloway , I will get my first big test here in the GNWC as I will square off against a man known as
Seth Bishopp. His stature is comparable to mine , at 6'8" and 326lbs he will be a force to be reckoned with! Mr.Bishopp , you boast a record of 10-4-4 and that is quite impressive! You have been a Worlds champion and a tag team champion , which only goes to show just how tough of an opponent you will be for me , but believe me Mr. Bishopp ... if anyone is up to the challenge , this native Hawaiian is!!
I have proven myself the Worlds Strongest Man on three different occasions. My physical strength is great , but not at great as my inner strength and with that combination Mr. Bishopp , you will be defeated!! A victory over an accomplished athlete such as you Mr.Bishopp , may be all I need to take that next step towards the gold , that next step towards being the champion
You have been there before Bishopp , you know what it means to be immortalized a the World's champion and you know what it takes to get there.
Well so do I and Mr. Bishopp on February 9th at the Charlotte Coliseum in
Charlotte , North Carolina ....
(Puah slides his sunglasses down the brim of his nose , exposing his eyes, which stare intensely into the camera lens ...)
you will know what it's like to be defeated at the hands of Puah the "Hawaiian Love Machine"!! (With that said , Puah turns his attention to the boarding gate and the attractive , female attendant greeting the guests as they board. Puah whispers something into her ear, which causes her to giggle and blush as the scene fades.)
BT: Holloway and Puah at SLAM! Should be a good close match and I can see the winner of that match making a name for himself later down the track in the GNWC. I think this is our final interview now and it comes from Bedlam, another man who will debut at SLAM! Against Shawn Greene I think it is!
Scene begins with Bedlam and Eve Starburst in front of a GNWC backdrop. Bedlam is decked out in a black leather biker jacket, thick brown dog collar, and a black t-shirt. The t-shirt, in white choppy letters, says
"LOC." Under the "LOC" is a red anarchy symbol. He also wears a pair of brown leather pants tucked into a pair of combat boots. His long black hair covers much of his face, save his mouth. Beside him is the beguiling Eve
Starburst, wearing black lipstick, eyeliner, and nail polish. Her black
(slightly longer than) shoulder-length hair is tied back in two pigtails.
Her maroon babydoll dress essentuates her pleasurable curves. A pair of combat boots hide the bottom of the (slightly torn) fishnet stockings she is wearing. And to top it off a clove cigarette is resting snugly between her right index and middle fingers.
EVE STARBURST: The fury of The Lord of Chaos falls down upon the GNWC like a hard rain. A hard, BLACK rain. Disorder Personified himself makes his RIOT debut this Wednesday. Against Drago, a tag team co-champion no less. You're a big boy, Drago, and an impressive one at that. But size and achievements mean nothing to a man such as Bedlam.
Eve takes a drag from her clove and take a long exhale. EVE: Bedlam has taken down bigger men than you. And opponents whose career successes matches yours. The Lord of Chaos has an arsenal the likes of which you have never seen.
BEDLAM: Drago, like my beautiful associate says, you have achieved greatness here in the GNWC. You are hardly the ideal first match for Riot. But I do have two advantages over you. One, I guarantee I can move a lot faster than you and pose quite a small target. And two, you know absolutely nothing about me. I have been out of any sort of spotlight for quite some time now.
That gives me a distinct advantage over you. You are clueless as to what I can do. All I have to do is watch all tapes of Riot, and I know your entire bag of tricks. And despite your reputation here, I predict that bag of tricks is quite limited.
EVE: You forgot to mention your third advantage over the big guy. BEDLAM: Please enlighten me and the television audience as to what that may be. EVE: Your complete and utter disregard for your own personal safety. You will attempt manuevers only the craziest, or most stupid, people would ever try. You take risks. You want success more than the other guy. Of course you leave the ring barely able to walk and then you never allow yourself to recuperate because you're too stubborn to take a break when you need one,
and -- BEDLAM: Thank you for your kind words, Eve. EVE: No problem. BEDLAM: Drago, this Wednesday you will walk into the ring with Disorder
Personified. I guarantee you will walk out the loser. You will throw everything you can at The Lord of Chaos, but it will be all for naught. Our meeting will give you nightmares for ages to come.
EVE: Sweet dreams, Drago. BEDLAM: Carpe Noctem. End Scene. BT: That’s what I call a good interview, Bedlam is a damn fine speaker and I
’m going to take it upon myself that he makes it up to the top in the Great
Northern Wrestling Coalition. Disorder Personified, Carpe Noctem, all excellent stuff! GO BEDLAM!
GR: For Commissioner Tuckwell, I’m Gold Rogers and I’ll see you at RIOT while Tuckwell will be at SLAM! No doubt!
BT: Hold on a second, I’m getting word that there is one final piece of footage from the man who holds the World Title, MANIAC DAVID RAGE!
The Torrance Institute Of Mental Health of Pittsburgh. Snow covers the building, and the acres of land that seem to stretch for endless miles, surrounded by the fence, as it rattles when the cold wind rushes through.
Smoke rises from the chimneys, that stand tall at each corner of the main building. The Guard sits in his shack, near the main entrance. He is drinking hot chocolate, and trying to keep warm. His eyes are glued to one of the many monitors that line the walls of his shack. On this monitor, we can see a close-up of of someone's icy eyes. The eyes don't move. The eyes don't blink... He just stares, through the monitor, and at the guard. The image on the screen has a fuzzy bluish tint to it.
The Guard can't take his eyes off the monitor. He pushes a button, and we can see a wider angle of the man... Those icy eyes belong to Maniac David
Rage.
Maniac has a wicked smile on his face, and hatred in his icy eyes. He's wrapped in a straight jacket, and he sits in a corner of the quiet room...
It's walls are white, and barren. The floors are made of shiny tile, which compliments the quiet calming walls.
Wicked laughter echoes off the walls, but it's not coming from Maniac. Could this be the voice???
Maniac: I hear you... I HEAR YOU... Maniac seems to be in a demented bliss. He tilts his head back, closes his eyes and waits for the voice.
The Guard shakes his head, finishes his hot chocolate, and pours some more. Maniac's head falls down, and his icy eyes are bloodshot, and glassy, the affect of Divine Guidance.
Maniac: I HEAR YOU... I HEAR YOU... I HEAR YOU... Maniac begins to bashes head against the wall, as the laughter in the room becomes louder, echoing. Maniac's eyes flare, and they scan madly around the barren room. There's no one there. He lets out an lions roar, and the veins on his neck flare... And he rips the straight jacket away from him.
Maniac charges towards the steel door, flying into it with all his might, smashing into it, and falling back to the floor. Maniac smiles, rises to his feet, and slams into the door again. He's putting a few dents into it, but it's not going to move.
Maniac: Voice says "wait..." On The Security Monitor. The Guard watches Maniac return to the corner. He gets on his phone, dials a number, and waits for someone to answer.
Guard: Yeah. Send someone to the quiet room. Tell them to be very careful. You're welcome. A white delivery van pulls up to the gate. The Guard steps out of the shack, and looks the van over.
Back inside. Maniac stares at the steel door. We can hear it creak open, as light from the hall outside fills the room, as A Guard steps inside. Maniac smiles to himself. He has his hands wrapped around his body, as if the straight jacket were still functional.
Maniac: I knew you'd come. The Guard ignores him. Maniac stands, and leaves the room with the guard. BT: Excellent, see you at SLAM! While Rogers will be at RIOT, hahaha! [The screen fades as Tuckwell finishes on his usual note, taunting Rogers with his hands.]
