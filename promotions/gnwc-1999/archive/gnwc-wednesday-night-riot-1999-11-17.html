GNWC Wednesday night riot November, 17, 1999 [Crawl Away by Tool plays, the screen shows Lord Ao crushing an opponent with The Enforcing Action, followed by "Top Gun" Ben Tuckwell hitting the
Top Gun on Shugenja. Grappler Snakeman hitting an opponent with a frozen trout, screen flashes, Shugenja is screaming while holding an opponent in the Shugenja Buster, the screen flashes, The Celtic Connection is nailing their hapless opponents with liquor bottles, the screen flashes, The
Devestators are lowering themselves to the ring, the screen flashes, and The
GNWC Logo hits the screen. The camera pans the crowd of screaming fans, almost every fan has a sign, one reads, "Snakeman is DA BOMB!", another reads, "Tuckwell is the real deal!", and another reads, "The Kliq is the sh!t.". The camera pans the arena and stops on the announcers area where
Gold Rogers and "The Grifter" Chris Starr sit.]
GR: "We have a show for you tonight, welcome to Wednesday Night Riot on TSN! We have a segment that was recorded earlier by a hidden camera, let's cut to the footage." [The MonsterTron lights up and has a shot of the car park, where a black stretch limo pulls up. The back door opens and GNWC World Heavyweight
Champion Ben Tuckwell steps out dressed in street clothes, baggy cargo shorts and a white Nike t-shirt. He has a cast around his broken right arm and this arm hangs down as he walks into the arena. The camera follows him in, where he stops at a message board, reading the lineup for RIOT.]
Tuckwell: "WHAT THE [BLEEP] IS THIS [BLEEP]. LORD AO AND XCALIBUR IN AN
INTERCONTINENTAL TITLE MATCH... TONIGHT??? [BLEEP]ING AO IS SUPPOSED TO BE
SUSPENDED, THIS IS BULL[BLEEP]. STRAND BETTER HAVE A GOOD EXPLANATION FOR
THIS!" [Tuckwell storms off down the hallway and slams his fist through a wall as the MonsterTron fades to black.]
GR: "What is this we have word that President Strand and Vice President
Temrick are heading towards the ring."
[Come as You Are by Nirvana plays over the sound system, the Monster Tron flashes on and a picture of the President and Vice President plays, the curtain opens and Strand and Temrick make their way to the ring. President
Strand grabs the mic from Blake Carver and puts it close to his mouth. ]
President Strand: "As was announced last 'Riot,' former World Champion, and current Intercontinental Champion, "The Enforcer" Lord Ao, has been suspended from active competition, for his actions last week. Furthermore he has been fined $20,000"
(The crowd boos, and starts to give President Strand a lot of heat.) President Strand: "I know how you all feel about 'The Enforcer' but Ben
Tuckwell was seriously injured (Crowd screams and cheers), and we cannot afford to let wrestlers...."

Lord Ao: "Strand, the idea of being suspended and fined just doesn't work for me. See I talked to my lawyer, and he had a look at my contract. Lets just see what he had to say shall we? Bring it up on the Video-Tron."

Not only does it state that you cannot fine him, but that he has a GUARANTEED Title for Title match with 'Top Gun' Ben
Tuckwell at the Pay Per View 'Revenge.' I therefore have obtained a court injunction, forcing you to hold up your part of Lord Ao's contract."
Judge Wallis: "Based on this information, I have issued an injunction, from the Court of Queens Bench, in the Province of Saskatchewan, stating that
Lord Ao WILL FACE BEN TUCKWELL FOR THE TITLE AT 'REVENGE.' Furthermore, he will not be fined, and he will retain his Intercontinental Title, as it is needed to be part of his match with Tuckwell. Since your rules state that a
Champion must defend his title once every thirty days, I am ordering that
Lord Ao be allowed full privileges in your roster, and the right to defend his Intercontinental Title until the Pay Per View!"
(Video-Tron cuts away) Lord Ao: "So tonight on 'Riot' I think there is another member of the Kliq who needs a beating. Xcalibur, since you obviously want a beating real bad, why don't you bring your punk a$$ to the ring so I can shove this here belt down your throat!!!"
[Crowd cheers, as "The Enforcer" leaves the ring. President Strand is in shock, as he confers with VP Temrick. "God Of Thunder" once again plays over the sound system as "The Enforcer" Lord Ao leaves the ring area. Strand and
Temrick follow]

6'8", and weighing in at 310 lbs., this is Chris Gaines!

Blake Carver: "Coming to the ring at this time, standing 6 feet, 5 inches tall, and weighing in at 275 lbs, he is the master of 'The Second Coming,'
he is the 'Hope' of the GNWC, he is the GNWC World Television Champion, he is JESUS MARTINEZ!!!"
Martinez makes his way down the aisle, as the fans cheer. He has a cross strapped to his back. As he gets closer to the ring, his cross suddenly explodes with fireworks shooting from every angle. Martinez is startled, as he squirms to undo the exploding cross.
Gold Rogers: "Good God, fireworks just went off ON Martinez's cross!!!!" Grappler Snakeman charges down the ramp, jumping towards Martinez, hitting a thundering drop-kick to the head of the bigger man. Snakeman then grabs
Jesus, and throws him headfirst into the ring. Martinez is disoriented, as
Grappler Snakeman climbs into the ring. Snakeman whips Jesus into the ropes.
Martinez rebounds, and comes back at the Grappler, hitting a mammoth Cross-Body block. Martinez climbs up to the top rope, and quickly goes for
"The Second Coming." Jesus flies off the top rope, smashing his elbow into
Snakeman's sternum.
The Grifter: "Oh look, here come those Irish punks now!" The fans suddenly roar, as the Celtic Connection rush down the ramp, and jump into the ring.
Gold Rogers: "You had better watch your mouth Chris, there are two of them, and only one of you"
Maul pulls Martinez off of Snakeman, and begins savagely pounding him. Wruck grabs the Television Title, and tosses it to Maul, who proceeeds to smash it against Jesus' face. Wruck jumps out of the ring, and grabs a folding table. He slides it into the ring, then sets the dazed Snakeman up on top rope, in the corner. Wruck scoops up Snakeman, then falls backwards onto the table with a Celtic Driver, smashing through it as the Grapplers neck and shoulders impact with immense force. Maul is still savagely attacking Martinez with the belt, when all of a sudden, the fans jump to their feet, and begin to scream. "The Enforcer" Lord Ao barrels down the aisle, and slides under the bottom rope and into the ring. He quickly comes to his feet, and runs at Wruck, slamming into him with a thundering Power Spear. Maul slides out of the ring, leaving Jesus bloodied.
He pulls his nearly-unconscious brother from the ring, and stares at "The
Enforcer" with intense hatred. Lord Ao stands in the ring, his eyes blazing with intensity. As Wruck and Maul make their way up the ramp, Ao helps up
Snakeman, who seems to be all right. "The Enforcer" then drags Martinez to his feet. He helps the injured man out of the ring, and up the walkway. A man jumps the rails and runs up behind Grappler Snakeman and nails him with a tire iron, he then grabs Martinez's TV Title and disappears into the crowd.
GR: "Who in the heck is that and what is he doing stealing Martinez' s title?" TG: "Who knows anything can happen in the GNWC!" ["Give it Away" by the Red Hot Chili Peppers hits the arena and the crowd give a mixed reaction, mostly boos. The lights flash green and purple and

[Strand shows from behind the curtain in a suit holding a microphone, and he receives an instant face pop.]

Tuckwell: "My second wish is to add a stipulation to the Xcalibur and Lord
AO contest later on tonight. If Lord AO loses, THE SUSPENSION STANDS, GOT
IT?" Strand: "If Lord Ao loses to Xcalibur, I will guaran(damn)tee that the
Suspension will stand you got that!"
Tuckwell: "I don't care what you say! My third wish is to... BE THE REFEREE FOR THAT MATCH!" Strand: "TUCKWELL, THIS IS GOING TO FAR! YOUR ARM IS BROKEN, YOU COULD GET HURT!" Tuckwell: "I'll tell you right now that having a nutcase breaking my arm is going to far, anyway, I promise to be fair!"
Strand: "YOU BETTER BE FAIR, OR I'LL STRIP THAT WORLD TITLE FROM AROUND YOUR WAIST AND RELEGATE YOU TO OPENING LEVEL STATUS!!!" [Strand leaves angry and Tuckwell stands in the ring.] Tuckwell: "Referee fairly... YEAH RIGHT... HAHAHA!" [Tuckwell laughs out loud as "Give it Away" by the Red Hot Chili Peppers hits and he leaves the ring and up the ramp. ]

Intercontinental Championship!

6'7", and weighing in at 318 lbs; he is the 'NEW FRANCHISE' and leader of the KLIQ; he is XCALIBUR!

His eyes burn with an intense hatred, and his gaze never falters from Xcalibur. Lord Ao grabs a sign from the audience which says "1 down in the Kliq, 3 more to go!" The
Enforcer holds the sign up for Xcalibur to see, and points to it.]

[AO grabs his head, while Xcalibur gets up and holds his arms in the air, claiming a victory. AO though, gets up, grinning.
