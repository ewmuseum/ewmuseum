GREAT NORTHERN WRESTLING COALITION Handbook Version 1.1 Last Updated: January 27th 2000 Web: http://lightning.prohosting.com/~gnwc [Introduction] About the GNWC GNWC Staff Titles [Rules] Roleplaying Rules Matches Sneak Attacks [Television Events] Wednesday Night RIOT Pay Per Views [Applications] Singles Application Tag Team Application +-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ INTRODUCTION +-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ ABOUT THE GNWC The Great Northern Wrestling Coalition is an e-mail based fantasy wrestling promotion. The GNWC is a booked federation, meaning results are determined by the administrative board. The Great Northern Wrestling Coalition was founded in August 1999 by Chris Strand and had their first show on September
1st 1999. At the end of December 1999, President Strand bought out a small regional federation in Texas called the Lone Star Wrestling Union and merged operations. Ben Tuckwell, the owner of the LSWU and well known GNWC wrestler became commissioner of the organization and leads a rebel group who are angry at the GNWC for buying out their company.
GNWC STAFF *Real People* Chris: He is the owner of the GNWC and takes on the big jobs such as putting together the weekly cards and pay per views. He is known for his characters
'Hateful' CJ Bender and 'The Grifter' Chris Starr.
Kieron: Kieron handles Ben Tuckwell and does a lot of behind the scenes work. He is involved in match writing and takes care of the weekly update and the GNWC website.
Lee: Lee handles Seth Bishopp and takes care of the GNWC Magazine. *On-Camera Staff* Announcers Blake Carver: Blake didn't know what to do his career at the local radio station had ended due to the change in format of the station. He was on his last dollar when President Strand called him and asked him to become a ring announcer. Carver jumped at the chance, and he has been with the federation ever since.
Gold Rogers: Has been a fan of the sport for as long as he can remember. When he heard that a Professional Wrestling Federation was starting in his neck of the woods, he applied quickly. Gold had found his glory in sports reporting at the local major junior hockey games for a local radio station, but he wanted more. He accepted the job with the GNWC on the spot.
'The Grifter' Chris Starr: This wiley veteran has been around for a while. He has held many titles, in various federations. When President Strand approached him about a job in the GNWC the Grifter told him to show him the figures. When President Strand put the money on the table, the Grifter laughed and told him that he was insulted. The Grifter then joked that he would do colour commentary for that amount, President Strand agreed and both men shook hands to seal the deal.
Referees 'Top Gun' Ben Tuckwell: The head referee of the GNWC after he made a contract for himself as commissioner. Tuckwell is one referee you do not want to have because he never calls the match fairly and always favors towards his friends.
Scott Hunter: Hunter has wrestled in many small name federations but never got anywhere until he decided to put on the black and white stripes and become a GNWC referee. He is not afraid to call for a disqualification if he is touched or something illegal happens.
Damien Gibbs: A former boxing referee, he is one tough customer. Does not call for DQ's and if you try to lay a hand on this guy, he will punch you right in the mouth.
TITLES World Heavyweight Title This is the top title in the GNWC. The wrestler who holds this belt is usually the best wrestler in the federation. The number 1 contender will be decided by the bookers on heat drawn, angles and if there is a good feud between the top contender and the champion.
International Title This title was formerly called the television title and is considered by many a stepping stone to launching a successful career in the Great Northern
Wrestling Coalition.
Hardcore Title All hardcore title matches will be no DQ, no countout and have falls count anywhere unless prior stipulations have been arranged. To get a title shot in this division, you must either show interest for the title or think of a creative kind of hardcore match that you would like your wrestler to compete in.
World Tag Team Titles The World Tag Team Titles are the GNWC's top honor for tag teams. These titles are as good as the World Heavyweight Title.
+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ RULES +-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ ROLEPLAYING RULES The limit on roleplaying is two out of ring roleplays (flashes) and one in-ring roleplay per week. Your flashes will be seen at Spotlight which goes to air on Tuesday and Saturday.
RULE ONE: Censor up all major swear words with [BLEEP] or [CENSORED]. You are allowed to swear on Spotlight but for in-ring interviews at RIOT, don't say anything that wouldn't be said on WWF Raw is War or else it won't be used.
RULE TWO: Remain realistic at all times. Don't have your wrestler fly or turn into a reptile, stuff like that.
RULE THREE: Don't brag about titles you've won in federations other than the GNWC. RULE FOUR: Under no circumstances may you physically attack another wrestler or any GNWC officials in a roleplay unless you have prior permission from the handler of the character. If you want to attack another wrestler, create an angle or a joint interview, you can find all contacts on the contacts page
RULE FIVE: GNWC administration reserves the right to refuse flashes that contain inappropriate material (i.e. racism, sexual intercourse) as well as refuse material that is sent for a match. If you argue, you can be fired real quick. If you have questions on why you lost, contact either Chris or
Kieron by ICQ or E-Mail.
MATCHES The result of matches is determined by roleplaying, strategies and effort. If you put a lot of effort into the federation, you will eventually get a push. The way to get matches is by being signed randomly, challenging people in your roleplays or sending in a formal request to the head office.
SNEAK ATTACKS Attacks and interference are permitted but the GNWC board has the right to refuse them. Attacks will never be used to affect the outcome of a match and will only be used if it:
A. Furthers an angle or B: The person you attack was already going to lose. +-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ TELEVISION EVENTS +-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ WEDNESDAY NIGHT RIOT Wednesday Night RIOT is the Great Northern Wrestling Coalition's main show. It happens every week unless a pay per view was just concluded and it is written in play-by-play style. Matches are live and the announcer's team is led by Gold Rogers and Chris Starr. RIOT is held in arenas all over North
America.
PAY PER VIEWS The GNWC has one monthly pay per view where all titles will be defended. The pay per view event is usually where all feuds and angles end and usually provides for an entertaining night.
