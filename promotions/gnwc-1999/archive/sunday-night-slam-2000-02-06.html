++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ FOUR WAY ELIMINATION MATCH Scotty Summers vs. Shinn Yoshimasga vs. Tat vs. The Man Called Bob ++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ Every man made their own individual entrance and the match began fast-paced with all four men battling it out in the ring. Eventually the referee got it all under control with The Man Called Bob starting against Tat. Bob began well with a double underhook suplex and then went for the irish whip but it was reversed and as Bob went bouncing off the ropes, Tat tried for a spin kick but Bob ducked the maneuver and took Tat down with a legsweep. Bob then executed a knee drop and told the crowd to shut up, which left them not knowing how to react to The Man Called Bob. Bob tags out to Shinn Yoshimasga and Shinn immediately went to work on Tat, putting him in a sleeperhold before it was broken after 14 seconds. After the hold was broken, Shinn made the tag to Scotty Summers who last week sent Xcalibur and Sharif Ramahan packing. Tat charged at Summers and the Playboy did well to use a hiptoss to throw Tat down to the mat. Scotty then went for a short-arm clothesline but
Tat gave The Playboy some of his own medicine, countering it with a hiptoss.
Tat made the cover but only got a two count and Tat decided to take a break by tagging The Man Called Bob back into action. Bob kicked Summers back down to the mat before sending him into the ropes and both men went down after a double clothesline. Summers was the first to get up and rolled Bob to the outside. The Playboy sent Bob flying into the steel steps and then hit a standing dropkick. The two men re-entered the ring and after another dropkick and a standing moonsault, Scotty made the 3 count and cost Bob his job in the GNWC.
Tat took Bob's place in the ring and Summers used a slingshot bodyblock on
Tat and went for the pin but Tat got the shoulder up after the two count.
The Playboy used a snap mare and then went up top to nail the "Southern
Exposure" Shooting Star Press for the second three count in less than a minute. Both Tat and The Man Called Bob had been released from their jobs in the GNWC and now it was left down to Scotty Summers and Shinn Yoshimasga.
Shinn took Summers down with a leg lariat and hooked the leg only to get a two count. Shinn lifted Scotty to his feet and went for a dropkick but
Summers smartly avoided it with a side-step and Yoshimasga only hit air. The
Playboy then used a diving elbow smash and a clothesline to get the momentum but Shinn managed to get it back after countering a second diving elbow smash with a faceslam. Yoshimasga then placed Scotty Summers upon the top turnbuckle and took him back down to earth with a superplex. Yoshimasga went for the cover once more, desperate to keep his job but only got the two count. Shinn tried to finish it off with a big superkick but Summers ducked the move and used a tombstone piledriver followed by the "Southern Exposure"
once again for the 3 count and was the only man out of the four to keep his job in the Great Northern Wrestling Coalition.

++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ SINGLES MATCH Puah the "Hawaiian Love Machine" vs. Michael Holloway ++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ After the respective entrances, the match got started right away. Puah went for a DDT but Holloway countered it with a backdrop. Holloway hit Puah in the jaw with a Roundhouse Right but Puah didn't seem to care. Puah then bodyslammed Holloway into the mat followed by a powerbomb for a two count.
After a kneedrop and splash, Puah went for the pinfall again but got another two count. Puah set up Michael Holloway for a Northern Lights suplex but it was reversed into a DDT by Holloway and then followed up by a fistdrop.
Holloway sent Puah into the ropes and took him down with a Spinebuster Slam. Holloway went for a clothesline but the "Hawaiian Love Machine" ducked it and then executed a monsterous belly-to-belly suplex on the man from
Rochester, New York. After a headbutt, Puah went up to the second rope and executed a big splash but once again, Michael Holloway got the shoulder up after only two. Puah went for a DDT but Holloway rolled him up into a small package and got a two count.
Puah then got up looking angry and delivered the "Puah Plunge" Death Valley
Driver and things looked over as Puah hooked the leg but Holloway showed a great fighting spirit, kicking out at two and nine tenths. Puah looked confused and delivered a legdrop but only got a near fall once again.
Holloway was almost unconscious but was still kicking out, but after a second "Puah Plunge", Puah the "Hawaiian Love Machine" came away with the 3 count after a very hard fought match.
After the match, The Enforcer came down and offered his hand and both he and
Puah shook hands which left Commissioner Tuckwell shocked. The fans were chanting Puah's name as both he and Lord Ao made their way up the ramp as
SLAM! went off air.
Winner: Puah the "Hawaiian Love Machine" via Pinfall [12:48] ++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ GREAT NORTHERN WRESTLING COALITION SUNDAY SLAM [02-06-2000]
