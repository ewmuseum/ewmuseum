NWWWWF Showdown (the Pre-PPV Show), part two

17 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mike: We're back folks, and if you will pay attention to the giant video screens, we can now count down the seconds until ALL ON THE LINE begins!!! (camera cuts to a shot of the giant video screen, which now has a big timer on it.. it's at 21 seconds...) Mike: This is your LAST CHANCE to order ALL ON THE LINE!!! Call you cable company now!! Crowd: 10!! 9!! 8!! 7!! (the clock is cut off.. what is on the screen now is fotage of NWWWWF Superstars doing dropkicks, piledrivers, chair attacks, all while the Feds Logo circles the screen untill the last shot, of Thunder Gums in the middle of Reigning Thunder is shown and the logo is all that is left on screen. The screen cuts to Thunder Gums film being attacked by Masters of the Powerbomb, Stonebreaker, and of Rick Rage laughing in the ring. TGs voice over top of film, which is replayed again and again: Gentlemen. Allow me to introduce...our targets! Ha! Ha! Ha!) Chant of Horsemen start to fill the arena as three cloaked figures and Thunder Gums come step down the aisle. TG: People...THIS IS THE FUTURE! Like it or not folks....Thunder Gums is here to stay. You see...people DON'T cross Thunder Gums...EVER. Something that just is not done. IF it does happen..like last week when Stonebreaker and the Masterbombs...or whatever the hell they want to be called..attacked me...it meant more than just revenge. I am declaring WAR on the NWWWWF! Horsemen 4 are here! This ain't no corporate take over...it is simply that Thunder Gums, and my associates...will insure the saftey of ANY and ALL wreslters here. That is right. <people are puzzled> Let's just say that Jo Wreslter and Johnny Superstar are gonna fight...if either wants to MAKE SURE that they win a match...or MAKE SURE that the other man doesn't get up...THE HORSEMEN 4 are the ones to call. Now...I am sure that you are all wondering who the hell these guys think they are. Allow me to introduce the HORSEMEN! Lights dim, spotlight on THunder Gums and the First Horsemen, a lot smaller than TG: Allow me to introduce...out of Up State NY, out of Riker's Island, re-defining the meaning of air wresling...HIGH RISK! <High Risk drops his cloak, gets a table out from under the ring, is noticably scarred on his right cheek, climbs to the top rope, and leapes, with the table in his hands, onto the floor, table splits, people pop, he leaves ringside> Now...I know that people in wreslting today want to be extreme...want to be dangerous...well High Risk will show the true meaning of these words...and tkaes no shit from anyone. One of the Horsemen drops to his butt in middle of ring...sounds are heard TG:My second Horsemen is a real mean SOB...you see...in a street fight you do what you gotta do...and my big man...very, very big man <shadow of towering, 6'11" frame over TG> KNOWS WHAT TO DO! HE IS every extreme wrestler, every big wreslter, every strong and smart wreslter all in one. The only man ever to defeat Thunder Gums, <crowd cheers>, SHUT UP! I've had enough of you people...screw this! Screw you President! Put me on here as some kind of freak show to get people to watch...this ought to do it! <TG nods to 2nd Horsemen> LOCO, get on his ass! <Loco leaves ring over to booth with Mike, Shane, and Buddy, grabbing Mike and picking him right off his feet> Mike: Thunder Gums! I AM THE ONE WHO HAS HAD ENOUGH! If you do not finish the free for all, then you are disqualified for tonights event! <crowd puzzled and boos> TG: You really think that tonight's PPV can go on without Thunder Gums? Give it up. Loco keep him up there..I got a BIG, BIG surprise for you Mr. Pres.You remember an old partner of mine...<Mike looking shocked, obviously knowing what is happening> Let me unlock him. <Moves over to sitting Horsemen, lifts cloak, and unlocks a lock, crazy, crazy man jumps up, missing hair, hasn't shaved, scarred, and obviously pissed off> TG: World...allow me to introduce...KHUJO! <at sound of name, Khujo tears into a turnbuckle, Loco drops stunned Mike, and gets into ring> TG: Horsemen are ready! Is the NWWWWF? <Khujo is tearing away at the turnbuckle, officials come down to ring to bail out the pres, obviously fearing for his life, Khujo spots pres, and just sits in ring, laughing> Shane: LOOK! <officials turn around to find High Risk atop the NWWWWF big screen, with a ladder across himself, jumping onto offical crowd> TG: All hell is gonna break loose tonight folks..so fork out some green and glue your eyes on the machine! <Loco powerbombs two officials, Khujo is still laughing in centre of ring, TG is telling fans off and throwing the garbage back at them!> Shane: hehehe!!
