Don't ever expect to see him in the ring again." Darcy: "I don't understand. If he's such a maniac, then who let him enter the NWWWWF?" Doctor: "He was under the control of his uncle who has raised him since he witnessed his parents death. He did a horrible job of raising him. He didn't send him to school, and he didn't care for him. He was beaten up pretty badly by a gang, and hasn't fully recovered. Hell, he hasn't even showed signs of recovery. I don't know why, but his uncle signed him to the NWWWWF by lying about all sorts of facts about him. After his brutal attack during his first match, we were called to take him away.
