The Last Few Seconds Before "All On The Line"

17 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Voice: The NWWWWF's first Pay Per View.. All On The Line.. It plans to settle a few scores... With Flawless getting a chance for revenge against Ghreec.. (camera shows clips of Ghreec using everything in sight to beat the crap out of Flawless, and we hear the voices of the announcers) Mike: What's wrong with Ghreec?! He's SNAPPED!! Shane: I'll tell you this though.. Flawless will definately want revenge!! Voice: The first ever Ultra Tag Team Champions will be decided tonight as well in a special, four-way tag match! (camera fades into various shots of the tag teams in the NWWWWF, followed by a clip of Mike standing at a press conference) Mike: I have no choice but to make a four way tag-team match at All On The Line, the winning team recieving the Ultra Tag Team Belts!! Voice: And in a last minute decision.. Jake Montana will be taking on Hell's Angel for the Ultra Title.. (camera cuts to a shot of Jake Montana's scene on Showdown) J.M.: I ain't fightin' tonght!!!!!! I have a match under appeal.. (camera cuts to the footage of Madman and Kris, where we see Madman slip Kris a couple of bills and say "Thanks again, man.. You did a great job!") Mike: WELL! That's all I need to see boys.. Madman, you and Kris are both FIRED! Which means I have no choice but to allow Jake Montana's appeal to pass!! JAKE GOOD LUCK IN THE FINALS!! Voice: Kayoss is sick and tired of Sandman's constant interference.. And he has already ended the career of ONE superstar.. Could he make it two? (camera shows clip of the hospital scene..) Doctor: I am afraid to tell you this but Scott Strong will never be able to wrestle again (camera cuts to clips of Sandman grabbing Kayoss in the back, and the two having an all out brawl.) Buddy: They hate each other!!! Voice: Another belt will be decided tonight as the Elite belt will be awarded to the winner of a very unique, very grueling Absolute Chaos battle royal.. 27 men.. 27 weapons.. Only one will walk away champ. (camera shows Darcy announcing the order of participants along with the weapons they will use) Voice: Thunder Gums has mady many enemies here in the NWWWWF.. But his new group, The Horsemen 4, plan to get a little bit of revenge against the men that punished him. (camera shows Thunder Gums getting pummeled by The Masters Of The Powerbomb and Stonebreaker, while Rick Rage laughs his ass off.) Mike: He's finally getting what he deserves!! (camera then cuts to various clips of Showdown, where Thunder Gums intros his partners) Thunder Gums: Allow me to introduce the HORSEMEN! (camera fades to another shot) Thunder Gums:...HIGH RISK! <High Risk drops his cloak, gets a table out from under the ring, is noticably scarred on his right cheek, climbs to the top rope, and leapes, with the table in his hands, onto the floor, table splits, people pop, he leaves ringside> (camera fades again) Thunder Gums: LOCO, get on his ass! <Loco leaves ring over to booth with Mike, Shane, and Buddy, grabbing Mike and picking him right off his feet> (camera cuts to the intro of the final horseman) TG: World...allow me to introduce...KHUJO! <at sound of name, Khujo tears into a turnbuckle, Loco drops stunned Mike, and gets into ring> <officials turn around to find High Risk atop the NWWWWF big screen, with a ladder across himself, jumping onto offical crowd> TG: Horsemen are ready! Is the NWWWWF? <Loco powerbombs two officials, Khujo is still laughing in centre of ring, TG is telling fans off and throwing the garbage back at them!> Voice: NWWWWF. All On The Line. Order Now.
