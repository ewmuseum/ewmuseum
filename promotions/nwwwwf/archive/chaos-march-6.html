Chaos! (March 6)

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with a bright yellow background, and the whirling NWWWWF logo slowly fades into the shot. We then hear Rico, the voice of the NWWWWF's voice "THE NWWWWF. ANYTHING CAN HAPPEN." (camera cuts to a shot of the ring) Mike: NOW, LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, AN INTERVIEW WITH THE ONE... THE ONLY.... MASTER OF DISASTER... Shane: -<yawn>- Mike: ...THE SULTAN OF SWAT... MONNNNNTANNNNNNNAAAAAAA!!!!!!!!!!! ("I am a Real American" blares through the arena. The crowd is going bananas! You have been truely magnificent since the day you walked into this federation, you've gotten a few wins under your belt, how do you feel about your progress thus far? Jake: I'm tired of all these NWWWWF superstars who are doin' alot of talkin'!!! The bottom line, Mike is that none of them remain in the NWWWF championship tournement!!! When I have that belt around my waste, you're all welcome to step into my neighbourhood... THE RING!! Not some hotel room where all they do is alot of talkin'... Mike: What about the Madman? He could be a real test for you... Jake: Madman, have you actually pinned a guy yet? It seems to me as though you haven't fought anybody worth the spit that comes outta' my mouth!!!! Well, I tell ya' this much: you got yourself a match now big man! You got yourself in a whole lot of trouble. There ain't nothin' that's gonna' stop me from gettin' that title.. certainly not the likes of you!!! (The crowd goes crazy as "I am a Real American" comes back on! I've done better interviews though. In any case, he's right about Madman. He's fought two tag-team wrestlers in singles matches. Of course they are going to be a little easier to beat.. Come on!! And if they don't, aren't they supposed to be told that the match is going to start with them out of the ring?? Shane: Don't ask me!! HE'S the pres! Mike: I'm not sure.. But for now, Madman is the winner.. If Jake has a problem with it, he can file an appeal to the board of directors. Shane: We have a board of directors? Mike: Yeah, us 3. Shane: Cool! (The Extreme Theme Song begins to play over the PA) Shane: This guy is an idiot.. Why put your shot at the Ultra Title on the line?! Mike: Pride? Shane: Stupidity. Kayoss has given Scott a piledriver RIGHT ON THE CONCRETE!!! Scott is down, and Kayoss drags him over to the steps! Scott is still down, but Kayoss has his leg propped up on the steps? Shane: Yup!
