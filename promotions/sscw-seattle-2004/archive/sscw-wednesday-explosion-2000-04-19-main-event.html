SSCW Wednesday Night Explosion! (Main Event)

19 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene fades back from commercials to the announcing desk, where Chad Brown and Stevie T. Dee are looking at the camera. ] Chad Brown: Ladies and gentlemen, it is finally time for our MAIN EVENT! [Suddenly, the arena fills with the sounds of a tropical island drumbeats and the lights begin to dim. The capacity crowd rise to their feet in hopes to catch a glimpse of the man whom will soon be making his way out from the makeshift "backstage". The entrany way begins to glow of a green and fills with a light fog made by SSCW's lighting staff. The shiloette of large individual can be seen emerging from the haze. ] Chad Brown: "THE LOVE MACHINE"! [The drumbeats grow louder as Puah the "Hawaiian Love Machine" emerges and fans erupt showering the big islander with cheers. Puah dressed in his navy blue & gold wrestling gear , complete with the traditional flower lei around his neck , makes his way to the ring acknowledging the fans along the way. ] Arnie Myers: From Honolulu, Hawaii, weighing in at 310 pounds, and standing 6 feet 3 inches, ladies and gentlemen -- PUAH "THE HAWAIIAN LOVE MACHINE"! [CROWDS: POP!] [Once ringside Puah scopes out the frenzied fans as they reach out to slap hands with him. After a few seconds , Puah sets his sights on a hottie seated in the front. He approaches, removing the lei from around his neck and gently placing it around hers. Pauh recieves a kiss on the cheek for his efforts and then makes his way into the ring to prepare for his opponent. ] Stevie T. Dee: JEESUS, what sewer did that thing crawl out from? Chad Brown: I thought that to be a very attractive young woman, STD. STD: Well, we all know about your "habits". Chad Brown: [Takes a deep breath.] ... How long do they have you under contract for? STD: Six years, baby. [Suddenly, "Born to be Wild" by Steppenwolf begins to blare over the sound system.] Stevie T. Dee: YES! Stephen C. Bourdo, the new official role-model for today's impressionable young youth, is right here, tonight! [The camera focuses in on the entrance way. A few moments pass, and nothing. Another few moments, the music still playing ... and nothing. ] Chad Brown: Hey, what's this?! [ From behind the large mass of fans, a large slick black limosuine screeches up and comes to a quick hault. Jim Johnston (head of security) and the rest of the crack SSCW security staff begin to part the sea of fans and create a make shift path to the ring from the limo. ] Stevie T. Dee: Fashionably late, baby. [ The limo door slowly opens. Out comes Stephen C. Bourdo, slowly with a smirk on his face. He slowly stands up, walk a few feet and stands with his hands on his hips allowing the crowd to admire him. He hides his purple tights with a long, flashy, glittering black robe, covered in white feathers around the end. On the back of this robe is a Superman symbol, with a "B" in place of the S, written in old English handwriting. His long, shiny, auburn hair is pulled back in a pony tail, resting on the top his ring robe.] Stevie T. Dee: Now, you see Chad. This is _exactly_ what I'm talking about. Wrestlers like Stephen C. Bourdo are the ones who garner _my_ respect. Chad Brown: Well, I'm sure Stephen can die a happy man now, Steve. [Stephen struts down to the ring, directly through the crowd, smiling to all the booing fans, attempting to shake hands but having fans pull away from him. He stops at the ring steps, steps half way up, and poses for the fans with his hands around the back of his head. Finally, he steps into the ring.] Arnie Myers: "Ladies and gentlemen, in the ring at this time, hailing from Toronto, Canada, and weighing in tonight at 230 pounds, this is STEPHEN C. BOURDO!" [At that, Stephen stands in the middle of the ring, and slides off his robe. He hands it to the ring attendent, and immediately walks to the referee and offers his hand in friendship. The referee shakes his hand, as Bourdo smiles and does some final stretching.] Stevie T. Dee: Look at this guy! A class act all the way. If I had kids --who lived with me-- I would make sure they someday lead good, productive lives by having them look up this good ole American, SCB. Chad Brown: He's probably the most arrogant man in SSCW right now, and he will have his hands full tonight with Puah "THE HAWAIIAN LOVE MACHINE"! [DING!!! DING!!! DING!!!] Chad Brown: Here we go with our main event for this evening! Remember, folks, this is a big opportunity. This is a round one match of the WORLD TITLE TOURNAMENT! [ The two men slowly walk up to each other. They are soon face-to-face, toe-to-toe. Puah, looking as intense as ever says a few words to Stephen Bourdo. A frown grows on Bourdo's face as he looks out to the audience. ] [SLAPPPP!] Chad Brown: Oh man! Bourdo lays a literal smack down ( :-) ) right across Puah's face! Puah quickly strikes back with a right fist that staggers Bourdo. Another! And another that sends Bourdo flying! [Bourdo stumbles and finds himself in the turnbuckle.] STD: The phat man with a _HUGE_ splash! Chad Brown: Puah wraps his mighty arms around the waist of Stephen Bourdo and slams him down to the mat with a belly-to-belly suplex. [Puah arises to his feet, flexes and winks to one of the girls ringside.] Chad Brown: Puah starts to put the boots to Bourdo! Stevie T. Dee: Just stomping away with some well placed, boots, err feet to the chest of Stephen C. That there's bunyan country! ["The Hawaiian Love Machine" now gaining momentum, drops an elbow across the neck of Stephen C. Bourdo. The massive Puah lifts his opponent to his feet and _levels_ him with a headbutt. ] CB: Puah is now implementing a methodical pace, keeping this match at his speed. A wise decision when you're facing someone as quick and agile as Stephen C. Bourdo. STD: Good point, _but_ once Puah makes a mistake, once he slips up, SCB will take advantage! CB: Puah has picked up Stephen, and throws him across his side with a perfectly executed gutwrench suplex. His power perfectly compliments his percision! ["The Love Machine" grabs a hand full of hair and pulls Bourdo to his feet. ] CB: Puah sends the much smaller Bourdo to the ropes. Bourdo bounces back, ducks a clothesline, and CONNECTS WITH A DROPKICK DIRECTLY ON THE KNEE! STD: HA! HA! What'd I tell ya? [A close up shot of Stephen C. as he stands and smiles brightly at the crowd.] Stephen C. Bourdo: YES!!! [CROWD: BOOOOOO!] CB: Stephen Bourdo gets the attention of the ref and points over into the crowd. The ref looks and Stephen C. with a fist to the nether region of Puah! CHEAP! STD: SMART! [Once again, the crowd let's SCB know how they feel about him. Stephen C. Bourdo gets a questioning look on his face as if he wonders why they're booing him.] CB: Stephen C. Bourdo grabs a hold of the massive ham hock of a leg, and connects with a knee slam to Puah! And another! He's getting as much height on these as he possibly can! STD: Ah yeah, and now he's going for the FIGURE FOUR! [Stephen C. grabs hold of Puah's right leg, twirls around, and locks in the tortuous figure four. ] Puah "The Hawaiian Love Machine": AGGGHHHH! STD: Ha! That has _got_ to be painful! CB: Stephen C. Bourdo is really cinching this thing in. [A closeup shot of SCB's face shows a mixture of a faint smile and pure agression as he adds on the pressure.] STD: SCB knows what he's doing! He's focusing on this right leg of the larger Puah! Takin' him down systematically! CB: Puah's shoulders are on the mat! 1... 2... Puah gets his shoulder up! [Puah seemingly gets a surge of energy and raises up off the mat. Suddenly, he cocks his fist back and connects with a right directly to the face SCB. The crowd bursts into cheers with Puah's effort. ] CB: Bourdo quickly lets go of this hold and man, is he in pain! [SCB, holding his face, gets up and walks to the corner of the ring. ] CB: Puah is getting up, but with a slight limp in his right leg! What's this?! Puah get's himself in a 3 point stance! STD: Oh, crap! [With a deafening yell, Puah charges at SCB and GORES him with a devastating football tackle!] CB: OH MY GOD! [CROWD: HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! ] STD: Somebody call a medic, ambulance, surgeon general! SOMEBODY! [The instant replay shows just as SCB turns around, Puah levels him, whipping his neck forward and slamming him to the mat. ] CB: Bourdo is _not_ moving! STD: Get up, SCB! [Puah slowly arises to his feet, as the crowd is still buzzing after his spear. Puah places a bare-footed stomp to the stomach of Stephen and then bends over, picking him up. ] CB: Puah pulls Stephen C. Bourdo forward, and lifts him _HIGH_ above his head! He has him in a gorilla press slam position! A tremendous showing of strength here! Puah was a former competitor and _winner_ of the World's Strongest Man competition! [After holding SCB for about ten seconds, Puah's drops SCB behind his back and gets the applause from the fans for his showing of strength. Stephen, slowly gets to his feet and leans on the ropes as Puah soaks up the claps. ] STD: With as much talent as SCB has, I honestly don't know if wrestling Puah before Sunday's big royale is a smart thing! Puah : AGGHH! [ Puah runs forward toward SCB but Stephen pulls the top rope and Puah is launched over to the outside! ] CB: OH! "The Hawaiian Love Machine" lands _very_ awkwardly on his right leg! STD: Ow! [ Puah winces in pain as the referee climbs out of the ring and checks on the leg of Puah. SCB looks as if he's about to follow when the ref tells him to stay in the ring. ] CB: Yeah, right! As if Bourdo is going to listen to the ref! STD: Look, he's staying back. A good man, that Stephen C. Bourdo. CB: Puah is trying his best to get back to his feet. He's holding on to the guardrail as he tries to walk. His right leg has been through a lot of punishment throughout this match and this just adds to it. It doesn't look like he's able to walk on it. STD: STEPHEN C. BOURDO WITH A TOP ROPE SUICIDE PLANCHA! CB: PUAH PULLS THE REFEREE INFRONT OF HIM AND SCB CRASHES INTO KEVIN JUSTICE! MAN! STD: HA HA! Look at that! Puah walks on that leg as if it has never been hurt! He was playing possum! [ Puah rolls back into the ring and reaches down into his tights. ] CB: SCB is slowly getting back up ... he climbs up on the apron, gets into the ring, and A FISTFULL OF POWDER TO THE EYES STEPHEN C. BOURDO! STD: Puah kicks him in the stomach AND PERFORMS THE PUAH PLUNGE!!! (Death Valley Driver) CB: Puah with the cover! ... No ref! Kevin Justice is out! Puah has him beat! [Puah, visibly upset, hits the mat in frustration. He rolls out of the ring, and walks over to the fallen Kevin "Swift" Justice. ] CB: Puah is kicking around the ref! Come on, don't take it out on him! STD: I kinda like it! [Puah is looking out into the crowd, as SCB rolls out of the ring. SCB runs and dropkicks Puah directly in the back, knocking him into a ringside fan. ] CB: Oh! Puah just knocked into that fan and knocked his pop all over him! STD: That's no regular fan! That's "The Two Step Viper" T N T! [ T N T stares into the eyes of Puah and then practically jumps on him with a barrage of fists. ] CB: These two are going at it! And there's "Devastating" Damon Knight and "Hard Rock" Roland Stone trying to pull T N T and Puah off of each other! T N T throws a fist at "Devastating" Damon Knight! AND KNIGHT FIRES BACK! Roland trying to pull these guys off each other! [A man is seeing running down the aisle, and to the action. He's recognized as Joe "The Prophet" Forbes. ] STD: It's The Prophet! CB: What's he doing here?! HE ATTACKS DAMON KNIGHT! STD: And look! In the crowd, there's Mark Bryant pounding away on "El Guapo" Carlos Gonzalez! CB: And Richard Cyrus Marsh joins him! Putting the boots to the Spaniard! There's Victor Cortez! He jumps on the back of Mark Bryant! [The camera angle changes, showing the entrance way where Janus and "Fabuloso" Ronald Brookshire are trading fists.] CB: Janus dropkicks Brookshire into the crowd! And there's "24 Karat" Kerry Powell slamming "The Boondocks Bruiser" Taylor Ryley! This is total mayhem! STD: I LOVE IT! CB: We have a near riot going on here! [The scene switches back to the melay between Damon Knight and T N T. Roland Stone, still trying to break things up, is tapped on the shoulder. He turns around, and sees a _big_ man, dressed in black. ] BIG MAN: This is from Simon Sphinx ... "with love". CB: THIS LARGE MAN BLASTS ROLAND STONE IN THE FACE! [The scene switches to the limosuine. Ji Hang Lee appears once again, shouts something in Korean, and moonsaults off the limosuine on to "Courageous" Chris Jameson. ] CB: MY GOD! THIS CROWD IS GOING ABSOLUTELY NUTS! STD: SO ARE THESE WRESTLERS! I LOVE IT! [The scene switches to inside the ring where "Regular" Joe Davies is seen standing on the apron trading punches with Jefferey Keenan. Davies knees Keenan in the stomach, and suplexes him up and over and Keenan lands hard on the concrete below! ] CB: MY GOODNESS. JIM JOHNSTON AND SECURITY ARE TRYING DESPERATELY TO RESTORE ORDER! [The camera switches to a view of the entrance way where Bradley Rose is seeing shaking his head at the mayhem before him. ] [CRAAAACK!!!] [Josh Poma levels Bradley Rose with a chair shot to the back!] CB: OH MAN! STD: Look! Bourdo and Puah are back in the ring! They are absolutely sorrounded by chaos here! CB: Puah sends Bourdo to the ropes, HITS HIM WITH THE CLOTHESLINE -- NO! BOURDO CRUCIFIXES PUAH! KEVIN JUSTICE IN THE RING! 1!!! 2!!!! 3!!!!! STEPHEN C. BOURDO WINS IT! AS THE ENTIRE SSCW ROSTER IS BRAWLING IN THE STREETS OF SEATTLE! [Stephen C. Bourdo, in a fit of joy, jumps up onto the second turnbuckle as Puah slaps the mat in anger, yelling out a sailors list of cuss words. Stephen Bourdo, with a smile on his face, raises his hands into the air.] STD: STEPHEN DID IT! HE DID IT! HE ADVANCES IN THE WORLD TITLE TOURNAMENT! CB: Ladies and gentlemen, thanks for joining us on the first ever broadcast of Stars and Stripes Championship Wrestling! It's been quite a night! Be sure to join us Sunday Night for SURVIVAL!!! [The scene fades as "Born To Be Wild" plays while Stephen C. celebrates his victory with his hands in the air, as chaos sorrounds the ring. ] SSCW Productions Copyright 2000 All Rights Reserved
