SSCW Sunday Night SURVIVAL! (Part 1 of 4)

30 April 2000

[There is quiet buzz over the crowd in the SSCW arena as the big screens all through suddenly shoot on, a camera moving quickly through the backstage area giving everyone their outlook of the sceen. A voice that is recognizable as Lindsey Florentino is heard off screen.] LF: This is Lindsey Florentine, and I'd like to welcome everybody to our _second_ Sunday Night Survival in the SSCW. Later tonight we have the biggest match yet in the SSCW, and the one that could have the most repercussions. Not only is this match a first round match up in the world title tournament, but its also a no holds barred contest. [A pop rises from the crowd at the mention at this type of a match!] LF: I've received word that Jack Cody has been walking throughout the SSCW arena for nearly an hour... [A monstrous face pop shoots across the arena as the image of a Jack Cody comes into view! Jack has on his usual blue jeans and brown boots and has on a gray SSCW t-shirt. Jack is walking around in the back, while seemingly inspecting the area.] LF: Jack... Jack... [Cody turns and sees the crew.] LF: Jack, I was wondering if you'd give us some comments on what went down between you and Kerry Powell last week, leading up to your match? JC: Lindsey, I was itchin' to get my chance in the squared circle, and when Kerry came out and made the comments he did, it lit a fire in me. This squirt came out and basically called every man in the SSCW lockerrooms worthless. Kerry was gettin' a little too big for his britches, and when he layed down that challenge, I intended to set him straight [The crowd cheers when they hear this.]. LF: And what about the comments that Powell made, do you believe they had any validity at all? JC: Those comments had 'bout as much validity as a Saturday mornin' cartoon. [Pop] Maybe Kerry does have a finely polished body. Maybe, just maybe, he does have all the talent he thinks he does. But I've said it before and I'll say it again, talent ain't the only thing that makes a wrestler great. Ya gotta have a little more than just what God gave ya, ya need the total package. And by the those comments he made last week, he displayed to the whole world that he doesn't have that total package. LF: Well now the real question is what exactly have you been doing pacing through the arena? JC: Lindsey, it won't be too much longer 'til its showtime, and I plan on bein' prepared for all circumstances. LF: What exactly do you mean? JC: Well ya'll know that when I get it on with Kerry Powell tonight, anything goes. So the way I see it, this baby could end up all over this arena. And well... I have no intentions of walkin' out the loser tonight, so I figured I'd better be familiar with all the surroundings. LF: So are you planning on bringing the action to the back tonight? JC: To be honest, there's only one thing that I'm plannin' on for sure tonight, and thats givin' it to Kerry Powell no matter where we are. [Pop for Cody!] It's not really a matter of _plannin'_ on bringin' the action back here, its more about bein' ready in case this is where I gotta fight. LF: Well Jack, everybody knows that "24 Karat" Kerry Powell isn't exactly a trustworthy, law abiding citizen. What do you plan to counter with if Kerry resorts to usings some type of foreign objects? JC: It looks like tonight I'm goin' to be fightin' fire with fire. And tonight that'll be perfectly legal. But I wanna tell Kerry something right now... Kerry, bring all the bats, chairs and tables that ya want. But if you decide to use 'em, you better make sure that you either knock me out or kill me 'cause I'm gonna keep comin' right back at ya until one of us is done for the night. LF: And what about your comments about somebody getting injured tonight? JC: I'm not wishin' anybody injuries, I'm just statin' that when I'm in the ring, I don't show much mercy. I'm goin' out to give the fans what they payed for, and that means not lettin' up for a second of the match. So until that final bell sounds, I'll be givin' _all_ of my heart, body and soul, whether that puts me or anybody else at risk or not. LF: Any final comments for the fans? JC: [Looking straight into the camera.] No let ups, and no let downs. I'm not goin' to let up in any part of this match for various reasons... One, I plan on makin' a run for that world title. Two, I have to get it my all every time I step in the ring, simply for pride. And the third and final reason is letting up would mean letting the fans _down_, which will _not_ happen. [The fans cheer as Jack turns and goes back to inspecting the arena.] LF: Well thats all for now folks, its time to start Sunday Night Survival! [The scene quickly fades from Florentino...] ["Come With Me" by Puff Daddy and Jimmy Page begins to blast through your speakers. We open to a shot soaring through the orange and purple clouds of a beautiful sunset. The shot begins to move from the clouds, and we're able to see down below. We see a desolate, war-ravaged field. The once bright green grass is now just a dead brown as of empty foxholes and abandoned war vehicles dot the landscape.] [ The figure of a man walking off into the orange horizon is seen. The camera begins to soar, getting closer and closer to this man. As the view gets nearer, we see this man as a US soldier. He is wearing a green and black pair of fatigues, a helmet, with a pack slung over his shoulder and a gun slung over the other. ] [The camera gets up of his face. The solider looks intensely off into the distance. A new scar adorns his cheek. He knows his purpose, he knows his duty.... He knows his destiny. ] #UH HUH, YEAH# [The scene explodes as the music picks up. A shot of a torn up, bloody, red white and silver American flag slowly flies across the screen. The words "STARS AND STRIPES CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING" fade on to the screen.] #Hear my cries,Hear my call# #Lend me your ears, See my fall# [ A clip of Ji Hang Lee moonsaulting off the limo on Wednesday Night Explosion is shown.] #See my error, Know my faults #Time halts, See my loss # [ A clip of Levi Ash looking straight into the camera is seen . ] #Know I'm lacking , Backtracking# #Where I met you# [ A shot of "24 Karat" Kerry Powell performing the Family Jewels on "Boondocks Bruiser" Taylor Ryley.] #Pistol packing, Itchy finger# #Trigger happy, Try to trap me# [A clip of "The Human Aphrodisiac" Bradley Rose performing the Wham Bam Thank You Ma'am on Stephen C. Bourdo. ] #Close your eyes# #COME WITH ME# [The flag reappears, slowly flying onto the screen. A few words form on to the screen.] _Sunday_ _Night_ __SURVIVAL!__ ["KABOOOOOOOM". Yet another explosion. The scene is now of inside the SSCW Arena where approximately 1,500 fans have packed themselves into this former pillow factory. Besides the ringside seats, there are large bleachers on three sides of the ring. Around the ring is guardrail and the announcing table. The ring, with it's dark blue mat and SSCW logo in the middle, has red, white and blue ropes. The apron is black and has the red, white and silver SSCW flag/logo on it. From the ring is an entry way that leads to the small ramp and the curtains. Around the curtains are a modest set of lights, and above an average size video wall sits. ] [The camera pans across the rowdy crowd. A few signs are seen which read "I CAME TO SEE THE CAROLINA CRUSHER", "Keenan: Where the _Gold_ Belongs!", "I enjoy wrestling very much", and "Bradley Rose is my aphrodisiac". The scene now switches to the announcing booth where Chad Brown and Steve T. Dee are sitting. Chad Brown with a huge smile on his face, a red SSCW T-shirt and a pair of khakis sits along side of Stevie T. Dee. Stevie T. Dee is standing up looking into the crowd and getting chants of "STD! STD! STD!". STD is wearing a pair of loose baggy jeans, a yellow and black striped vest which resembles a bee , a pink bandanna, and a slick gold chain around his neck. He sits down and smirks into the camera.] Chad Brown: Welcome to the second broadcast of SSCW Sunday Night SURVIVAL! We're back here for another action packed show! Stevie T. Dee: You are _damn_ right! And if you thought the last SURVIVAL was somethin', you haven't seen _no_thing yet! Chad Brown: You'd think that last week's show, with Jefferey Keenan winning the US Title, the big announcement about the titles, and the appearance of Levi Ash would be a tough act to follow ... It is, but we're doing it here tonight! We are jam-packed with World Title Tournament Matches ordered by Lance Avery. Stevie T. Dee: You ain't kiddin'. Lance wanted to get this show on the road as soon as he could, he wanted to find out who the man in SSCW really is, and dammut, he's doing it! Chad Brown: I can honestly say I haven't seen the talent level and competition for one title so high as it is in SSCW. This tournament is going to be something! Stevie T. Keenan heads to the top rope, and gets up there with some speed. Jeffrey stays down, while T N T makes a cover: 1 -- kickout!] CB: I may not like Jeffrey Keenan but he won't go out that easily. [Both men are up, and they go into a collar and elbow tie up. T N T gets the upper hand and lands a head lock on Keenan. He cinches it on tighter and tighter while Keenan writhes in pain. Keenan then starts to try to overpower T N T and begins to make his way to the ropes. Jeff makes it to the ropes and the hold is immediately broken. Pulling T N T up with a handful of hair, Keenan lands another DDT! Keenan covers again: 1 -- 2-- no!] SD: Damnit, why does T N T just keep prolonging the inevitable? CB: He's showing some heart, that's for sure. [Keenan picks up T N T off the mat again and tries to land a vertical suplex. When he has T N T half way up, the "Two Step Viper" suddenly brings himself back down to the ring. He then reverses the suplex and slams Keenan into the mat! Pop from the crowd! T N T then picks up Keenan off the mat and puts him on his shoulders, and slaps on a torture rack. T N T then comes flying in with a splash, but Keenan gets out of the way and T N T slams into the steel ringpost.] SD: That looked like it hurt a little bit. T N T is nearly cut in half and is lifeless in the ring. Back to the top ropes is where Jeffrey heads, and he stands there for a moment as the fans yell out at him. He then jumps, and this times _lands_ a moonsault! He covers T N T: 1 -- 2 -- not quite!!] CB: Now that one was very close! SD: I thought he had it myself, thats a questionable count speed by the referee. [Keenan is nearly off the wall because he cannot understand why that didn't end the match. But Jeffrey decided to continue his attack. Picking up T N T, he goes for a reverse suplex. But T N T slides out the back and lands on his feet behind Jeffrey Keenan. Keenan turns around and T N T meets him with a running lariat. The lariat knocks Keenan down, and T N T runs to the opposite ropes, bounces back and lands a big elbow drop.] CB: He's back... again!
