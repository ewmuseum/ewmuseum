SSCW Sunday Night SURVIVAL! (Part 3 of 4)

30 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[MYERS]: "Introducing first ..." #She said don't hand me no lines and keep your hands to yourself ... # [The famous line from "Keep Your Hands to Yourself" by Georgia Satellites resonates throughout the arena. The crowd immediately rise to their feet, a chorus of cheers begins to build ...] [MYERS]: "From the Music City, NASHVILLE, TENNESSEE! ... weighing in at two hundred, thirty one pounds ... here is 'MR. MUSIC CITY' ... DENNY LANGHAM!" [The aisle curtain parts ... Enter "Mr. Music City" Denny Langham. Langham slowly struts into the arena to a resounding cheer from the fans. A cocky grin begins to spread across his face as he pauses to absorb their reaction. The young Tennessean - garbed in a black bandana, a white t-shirt that reads in bold black letters "MR. MUSIC CITY, Nashville, TN" on the front and has a picture of a flaming black Taylor Model 615 Jumbo guitar on the rear, a set of black trunks and boots, a pair of black kneepads and a black elbow pad on his right elbow - begins to work his way towards ringside as the fans with aisle seats clamor and jostle to get a glimpse or touch him ...] [Langham enters the ring and immediately holds up his black Taylor Model 615 Jumbo guitar into the air to another roar from the crowd. He climbs to the second rope, guitar held high and begins talking smack with the crowd. He hands the guitar to a ringside attendant before flinging his bandana and t-shirt out to the crowd. He dismounts the second rope and loosens up for action as his music fades out ...] [As the crowd continues to cheer, Langham approaches Myers, who hesitantly hands him the microphone ...] [LANGHAM]: "Wait a minute ... wait a minute ... Before we bring out lil' Mark Bryant to take his beatin' like a man, I feel like I need to address a few thangs ..." [Langham lowers the microphone and basks in the fan's cheers.] [LANGHAM]: "I been sittin' here all week long, listenin' to Bryant run his mouth 'bout what he's gonna do to Denny Langham, how he's gonna kick my 'lil' country boy ass' or whatever he was mumblin' about. Can't understand a thang the fool was talkin' 'bout 'cause while Mark Bryant might be tall and strong, he's 'bout as bright as an unlit candle." [The crowd pops for the insult.] [LANGHAM]: "Well, Mr. Bryant, if yer back there, and if ya could just pull yer fingers outta yer ears and yer thumb outta yer ass for five seconds, I just got a few things ya oughta know. In this world, Mark Bryant, yer usually allowed to make three mistakes. Now, yer first one was even signin' a contract with SSCW when ya knew damn well 'Mr. Music City' was on his way ..." [Langham pauses again for a crowd response.] [LANGHAM]: "Mistake numero dos was when yer sorry ass went ahead and insulted Denny Langham and the great state of Tennessee. So, I tell ya what. Why don't ya go ahead and bring yerself on down to ringside, make mistake number three by facin' the greatest thang to come out of Tennessee since Peyton Manning and move the hell on outta my way so I can get my World title sooner than later." [Langham hands the microphone back to Myers as the crowd pops ...] Stevie T. Dee: This is going to be an amazing match, don't you think so Brownie? Chad Brown: Langham and Bryant will definatly be worth the fan's ticket prices! [Suddenly, Mark Bryant is seen running fromt he top of the entrance way, heading straight for the ring. With his massive size, seeing him run at this speed is quite a sight. He hits the ring and begins to pace, eyeing Langham like mad. Surprisingly, Bryant waits for the bell to ring.] STD: What are you doing! Go get him! CB: Whoever that man was before the break, wants Langham out of the tourny and Bryant wants to advance. Bryant is taking no chances at getting DQed. DING!!! DING!!! DING!!! CB: There's the bell! And Bryant walks slowly to Langham. Denny doesn't back off and walks right up to him. STD: Ha! Those country bumpkins aren't the sharpest tool in the shed ... Or the fastest tractor in the backyard... CB: Langham backs off for a sec, and lays in with a big right hand! ["Mr. Music City" whips, the much bigger Mark Bryant into the ropes, as Bryant goes off the ropes, Langham rushes at him but, Bryant just runs him over.] Dee: Bryant just showed everyone why size _is_ important is wrestling. Brown: Yea, Bryant may be bigger, but Langham is much better technically. [Bryant quickly pulls Langham, who is still rather dazed, to his feet. He then throws him backwards with a devistating release german suplex.] Dee: I think you may want to retract that last statement about Bryant's technical skills.. Brown: Hmm, for once, you may be right... Dee: What do 'ya mean _once_? [Bryant, pretty much thinking he has the match won, already, slowly stalks over to the fallen "Mr. Music City." As Bryant turns to yell at the audience, Langham slowly climbs to his feet. He stands waiting for Bryant to turn around.] Brown: Langham is sure gonna suprise Bryant here! [Bryant slowly turns around, and as he does, he catches a huge forearm blow from Langham. Bryant takes a few steps back, charges at Langham, who ducks, and trips Bryant.] Dee: Langham isn't as strong, but he is alot smarter than the big fella! Brown: Langham just grabbed Bryant's legs.. Could he be going for the Langham Lock!?! [Langham almost locks Bryant's legs in his version of the sharpshooter, but Bryant uses his size advantage to kick Langham right off of him.] Brown: Wow, bigger is better, and bigger is stronger... Dee: Yea, I bet _you_ here that alot... Haha!! [Bryant slowly climbs to his feet, as does Langham. They once again, lock up in the middle of the ring. This time around, Bryant just _throws_ Langham into the corner.] Brown: Bryant is backing up, he might try to spear him here! Dee: No! No!! Langham sidesteped! Bryant hit the tunrbuckles, full force! [Bryant is laying near the turnbuckles, obviously in pain. Langham quickly reacts and rolls Bryant up in a schoolboy.] REF: ONE!!! TWO!!!! THR-NO! Kickout by Bryant! [Langham is quick back to his feet, he grabs the tre trunk of a right leg of Bryants and connects with a knee smash. Another, and then locks on a spinning toe hold.] CB: Denny goes to work on the knee. This is one thing you do _not_ want from Langham when against him. He's methodical, and wants he starts on a certain area, he won't relent and you will feel the pain! STD: Yeah, like a LEECH. [After a few moments, Bryant sends a big boot to the jaw of Langham. Langham wisely catches the foot, turns around and locks in the figure four!] CB: Nice move by Denny Langham! STD: Pssh. What's that gonna do? CB: Why don't you take a look at Bryant and ask him what it does. [Bryant yells in pain as Langham applies the pressure. Bryant, the big man that he is, doesn't have to reach far for the ropes and does so, making the ref let the hold go.] STD: An advantage of being big! CB: Mark Bryant pulls himself back up on the ropes, "Mr. Music City" runs at him, AND IS LEVELED BY A CLOTHESLINE FROM THE DEPTHS HELL! [The force, so strong, twirls Langham around and both Bryant and Denny are on the mat. ] STD: Good lord! That was beautiful! CB: Bryant now back up his feet, but look at that, a noticeable limp. He signals to the crowd for something. [Bryant picks up the fallen Langham Bryant picks up the fallen Langham. Bryant grabs Denny from behind and locks Denny's wrist with one arm and the chin with the other. Bryant suddenly, cocks back sending Denny over and landing him _straight_ on his head. The crowd pops at this move.] CB: PIGMY-PLEX! STD: In case you are wondering about the name, if you are ever hit with this move it is guaranteed to make you at least an inch shorter. CB: And this crowd, although no exactly on the side of Bryant is appluasing in appreiciation for that move! [Bryant gets up and unrelentlessly puts the boots to Langham. Bryant pulls Langham up by his hair, getting some smack from the ref.] CB: Bryant puts Denny's head between his legs! Oh, no. I think we're gonna see a powerbomb! [Bryant lifts up Langham for the powerbomb, with a surge of energy, Langham puts the fists to Mark's head. Bryant drops Denny to the mat. Langham then kicks Bryant in the hurt knee.] STD: A kick to the shin! Little schoolgirls do that move, not hicks! CB: It was a well-placed kick to the knee, and Langham SUPERKICKS BRYANT! [Bryant falls like a freshly cut tree, straight to the mat. Langham then yells to the crowd and grabs a foot of Bryant.] CB: OH YEAH! "THE LANGHAM LOCK"! [Denny then locks on his version of a scorpion deathlock on Bryant. Mark starts yelling pain once again.] STD: COME ON BRYANT! YOU CAN GET OUT! [Bryant has no choice but to tap out as pain gets to be too much.] CB: The work to the knee was what did it. A nice performance from both men! STD: ... Damn cheater! CB: How did he cheat? STD: You didn't see it?! CB: No, I didn't. STD: Forget it! I'm not gonna try to explain it to you. ARNIE MYERS: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN... Your winner, and advancing on to the second round, "MR. MUSIC CITY" DENNY LANGHAM! [Crowd pops!] CB: We'll be right back with our main event!
