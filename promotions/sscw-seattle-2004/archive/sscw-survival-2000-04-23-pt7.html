SSCW Sunday Night SURVIVAL! (Part 7 of 7)

23 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CB: Alright, we're back! And not much has changed over the break. STD: But if anyone is in control right, now, it's Roland Stone! He's leveled nearly everyone thus far with a clothesline! [The view shows Roland Stone with a forearm to the back of T N T. Roland grabs a hold of "Courageous" Chris Jameson and throws him over his head with a belly-to-belly suplex!] CB: What strength! [Richard Cyrus Marsh is seen yelling out towards the entrance way. A few moments later, a large man walks from the SSCW curtain. ] CB: That's Mark Bryant! STD: He stalking towards the ring! [Mark Bryant, with his massive 6'10" frame steps up on to the apron and steps over the top rope. He walks directly toward Roland Stone. The two stand toe-to-toe.] CB: Oh man! Look at these two! I wouldn't be surprised if these two were the strongest the SSCW has to offer! STD: Bryant grabs Stone by the neck and lifts him up! Oh! CB: He walks him over to the ropes! AND DUMPS HIM OVER! My goodness! [Bryant, with a smirk on his face, walks back towards Richard Cyrus Marsh. He is bumped as Stephen C. Bourdo walks backwards into him only to be shoved as hard as possible to the mat by Bryant. Bryant and Marsh shake hands and smile at each other. ] CB: What is going on between these two?! STD: RCM was scouting Bryant on Explosion. He obviously saw some potential in this guy! [Bryant leaves the ring to a chorus of boos. He slowly walks back eyeing the fans as he leaves.] CB: Chris Jameson gets a jump on RCM! This young guy is tenacious! He hits him with elbows, fists, knees! He's tearing Marsh to pieces! STD: Where's Mark Bryant when you need him? [Bradley Rose bounces off the opposite ropes and runs toward the two.] CB: BRADLEY ROSE DOUBLE CLOTHESLINES BOTH RICHARD CYRUS MARSH AND CHIRS JAMESON UP AND OVER THE TOP ROPE TO THE FLOOR! [ Chris Jameson is seen slapping the outside mat in frustration. Bradley Rose looks on with a smile forming on his face.] STD: Another _two_ bite the dust, Chad. CB: Just five left. Who is going to be the first ever champion in SSCW?! [Simon Spinx swings at at Jefferey Keenan. Keenan ducks, grabs hold of Spinx's leg and trips him. Keenan turns him over and applies a painful Cobra Clutch!] STD: Aggh! Keenan, a relentless fighter. You gotta love it, Chad. CB: T N T and SCB taking it each other in the middle of the ring. Stephen C. Bourdo with a highdropkick to the face of T N T! Both T N T and Bourdo get right back up. Bourdo with a clothesline, Viper ducks, comes back around and hits a Samoan Drop on SCB! STD: Stephen is hurt, but you can't keep a leader of todays youth down! Bourdo struggles to get back to his feet. T N T runs at him, SCB DUCKS AND BACKDROPS T N T OUT OF THE RING! HA HA HA! Stephen C. Bourdo: AM I GREAT OR WHAT?! Woo! CB: SCB will just lure you in, make you think you have control and take advantage! STD: Spinx and Keenan are still going at each other! CB: Spinx sends Keenan to the ropes, ducks and sends him over with a backdrop! Keenan lands on his feet! Spinx turns around and meets a SUPERKICK TO THE CHIN! Simon stumbles backwards into the ropes. Keenan bounces off the opposite side of the ring, runs foward and dropkicks Spinx out of the ring! [The crowd cheers as Spinx is eliminated. His big brother, Omega comes to console him. Omega picks up Simon and carries him from ringside to the back as deragotory chants and even trash is thrown their way.] STD: And then there we three! We are down to the final three in this thing. Bradley Rose, Stephen C. Bourdo and "Simply Golden " Jeff Keenan! CB: Bradley Rose, who had a match earlier in the evening, is sitting in the corner. He's been taking a breather for the last minute or so. STD: I'm surprised he's made it this long with that match he had with Josh Poma. [The three look at each, all on oppsite sides of the ring. None of them make a move. Bourdo then says something to Bradley.] Stephen C. Bourdo: Me and you! Come on, buddy, me and you! We can take this guy! [Bradley Rose nods and the two immiediatly attack Jefferey Keenan. The two pulverize him with forearms to the back and kicks all over. ] Stephen C. Bourdo: YES! [SCB goes for a high five with Bradley Rose. Rose reluctantly does it. Rose then gestures for Bourdo to take Keenan. ] STD: Like I said earlier. Team work is the only way to go in this thing. CB: You may be right. All three men have taken a beating, but as of now, Bourdo looks to be in the best condition. Bourdo is choking Keenan in the corner. He lets go and turns around, BRADLEY ROSE GRABS HIM, THROWS HIS ARM OVER HIS SHOULDER AND WHAM BAM THANK YOU MA'AM [A quicker more devasting version of the Rock Bottom!!! [The crowd erupts as they see Bourdo get the Wham Bam Thank You Ma'am. Rose pops right back up and walks to the ropes. He yells something at both competitors, and jumps over the top rope, and to the floor.] STD: He's eliminated himself! CB: Why'd he do that! [Rose heads for the other side of the ring towards Arnie Myers as Keenan slowly gets up, heading for the top rope. Keenan waits for the wobbly Bourdo to get to his feet. Keenan turns around moonsaults off and catches Bourdo with a reverse DDT!] CB: GOLD RUSH! GOLD RUSH!!! KEENAN'S HOLDING HIS SIDE, HE IS HURT! HE PICKS UP BOURDO, AND THROWS HIM OVER THE TOP ROPE! KEENAN WINS IT! THE FIRST EVER SSCW UNITED STATES CHAMPION! "SIMPLY GOLDEN" JEFFEREY KEENAN! [Bradley Rose now has grabbed hold of a mic. As he heads for the back curtain, he points at Keenan in the ring. ] Bradley Rose: Hey, Keenan, don't think for a second I did you a favor. I, Bradley Rose, just have much _bigger_ aspirations in this organization as will soon become evident. [Keenan looks on questioningly as Bradley turns around and heads for the curtain. Keenan is handed the US Championship by Kevin Justice to a surprisingly large amount of cheers. Most are happy that neither Rose or Bourdo walked away with the belt.] CB: Congratulations to Jefferey Keenan, the first ever champion in SSCW! That's something to be proud of! STD: No doubt about it! A tremendous amount of guts shown by Keenan. [Suddenly, someone from the crowd wearing a black hooded sweatshirt and black jeans slides uder the ropes and into the ring. The unsuspecting Keenan holds his title belt high up into the air. ] STD: Who the hell is that?! [ The unknown man suddenly blindsides "Simply Golden" sending him to the mat.] CB: What's going on here! Who is that?! [The man pulls the hood from over his head. The camera gets a close-up shot of his face. The fans go absolutely nuts as they recognize this man.] CB: THATS- THATS- THATS LEVI ASH!!! STD: LEVI ASH IS IN SSCW! [The ruckus crowd continues their cheering as the camera gets a closeup shot of Levi Ash's face. An evil grin slowly forms on his face as he looks down upon the fallen US Champion. ] CB: My god! We'll see you on Explosion! [The scene slowly fades to a shot of Ash's face.] SSCW Productions Copyright 2000 All Rights Reserved
