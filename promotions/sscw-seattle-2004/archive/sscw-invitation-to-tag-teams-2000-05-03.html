An Invitation to Tag-Teams!

3 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
 Hey there, This is Lance Avery, the president of SSCW. I'm dropping a line here on RSPWF to all the tag-teams on the e-wrestling scene. We are currently a little short in our tag-team division. We have three high-quality teams, but unfortunately, that just doesn't cut it. If you are looking for a good fed, with good cards, good competition, and a fed where _fun_ is Emphasized with a capital E... Then SSCW is the place for you :-). There is an abundance of opportunity for a tag-team here. On the night of May 21, SSCW's first Mega-Event/Supercard, the tag titles will be decided in a ladder match involving all current tag-teams on the roster. If you're at all interested, e-mail me at [detail withheld] or check out the FAQ that will be posted on RSPWF in just a few short minutes. Thanks, Lance
