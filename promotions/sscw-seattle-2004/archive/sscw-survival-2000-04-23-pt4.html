SSCW Sunday Night SURVIVAL! (Part 4 of 7)

23 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Suddenly, "Nobody Does It Better" by Warren G and Nate Dogg begins to play over the modest SSCW PA system. Nate Dogg's melodious voice is heard.] #_NOBODY_ # #DOES IT BETTER# #_NOBODY_ # #DOES IT BETTER# #THEY CAN COME CLOSER THAN CLOSE# #ORIGINAL THEY NEVER WILL BE# #WE BUMPIN' FROM COAST TO COAST# #WE'RE JUST TRYING TO MAKE YOU SEE# #NOBODY DOES IT BETTER# [The music quickly picks up and from the entrance way comes SSCW President and CEO, Lance Avery. Avery, with his wavy dark blonde hair, a new dark blue armony suit and italian loafers walks down the aisle. In his hands, he's holding two SSCW Title belts. He shakes a few hands as he makes his way towards the ring. He stops abruptly stops and notices the brand new SSCW apron, complete with the new SSCW logo. Avery looks at the camera and says "Ah, yeah. This is it!". He climbs up the steps, into the ring, and is handed a mic by Arnie Myers. Avery puts the two glistening titles by his feet as he begins to speak.] Lance Avery: [Takes a deep breath.] Seattle ... _THIS_ IS_ SUNDAY NIGHT _SUR_VIV_AL!!! [POP!!!] [He pauses to take a look at the crowd before him with a smile forming on his face.] Lance Avery: Thank you... Thank you all. Seattle, for many a years, SSCW has been just a thought. Just a vague idea in the back of my mind. But to see this arena, and to see this ring, this action ... and these fans. [Pop] It makes me think, that _all_ the work. All the sweat and tears poured into this operation, the nights I stayed up late to work on this, all the weekends I could've spent with my wonderful girlfriend-- WAS WORTH IT. [Pop!!!] Lance Avery: And now, Seattle, and the rest of the wrestling world, get strapped in because SSCW is gonna take you for a ride. Now, tonight we've promised the full and complete brackets for the World Title Tournament. Worry not, I have them all right here. [Pats his front pocket.] But before I go into that, I'd like to talk about something. SSCW is a _lot_ like America. A land of opportunity. _Anyone_ can succeed --if they take that opportunity. _Anyone_ can fail, if they don't. You see, I give opportunity. In the same respect, if you don't take that opportunity, there's 23 others ready to jump at the chance. When a wrestler enters the SSCW federation, before they see the camera's, before they lace up their boots, each and everyone is given that same opportunity. Which brings me to my point. "Devastating" Damon Knight ... [Nice face pop as Lance nods his head.] A great wrestler, undoubtedtly. One that many consider a "big name" in this sport. [Shrugs] He had an opportunity. A _great_ opportunity, in fact. A spot in the World Title Tournament. Did he take it? _NO._ His opponent "The Prophet" Joe Forbes. Did he take it? Unfortunately, no. So that match, and those two spots in this tournament will be given to people who want that chance. I'll be glad to give it to them. [Smiles.] Now, as for these brackets. We've already seen two of the eight first round matches take place, with Bradley Rose and Stephen C. Bourdo advancing. All of these first and second round matches will be taking place throught the next few weeks on SSCW television. The semi-finals and finals will occur on our first ever "Mega Event", entitled "A Night of Champions: The Final Four". Without any further adieu, here are the brackets! [Avery pulls out a piece of paper from his front breast pocket, unfolds it, and begin to read. ] Lance Avery: Now, we've already seen two stars advance in this tournament thus far. These being Stephen C. Bourdo with a win over Puah "The Hawaiian Love Machine", and Bradley Rose just earlier defeated Josh Poma to advance. Without any further adieu, here are the rest of the matches. Stephen C. Bourdo Vs. ----------------Stephen C. Bourdo. Puah "The Hawaiian Love Machine" Ji Hang Lee Vs. Richard Cyrus Marsh "Supernatural" Simon Sphinx Vs. A Mystery Man to be announced by Wednesday Night Explosion 'Simply Golden' Jeffrey Keenan Vs. "The Two-Step Viper" T N T A Mystery Man to be announced by Wednesday Night Explosion Vs. "Courageous" Chris Jameson Mark Bryant Vs. "Mr. Music City" Denny Langham And since these two want to get on as soon as possible, why not do it in the tournament? Jack Cody Vs. "24 Karat" Kerry Powell Josh Poma Vs. ----------------Bradley Rose Bradley Rose [The crowd gives at least a small pop for each of the matches announced, Lance looks on with a smile.] Lance Avery: Those, ladies and gentlemen, are your official SSCW World Title Tournament Brackets! [Pop!] But I'm not done! Here we have the World Title, and tonight we have the US Title being announced. That leaves us with just _one_ other title to take care of... The Tag-Team title belts! [Avery bends over and grabs the two titles.] First, I'd like bring out "El Guapo" Carlos Gonzalez and Victor Cortez, El Ultimo Dos! [ "System of a Down" by Spiders plays. As the lights grow dim the video tron explodes on with the El Guapos face then Pyros explode off the ramp way ad Carlos makes his appearance and the word "El Guapo" appears above him in Pyros. The two make their way into the ring and stand near Lance Avery.] Lance Avery: Now, as unfortunate as this is... You two are the only tag-team in competing in SSCW rings. That means, quite frankly, that you guys are the best tag-team we have to offer. I have no choice, with no other competition for you two, to present El Ultimo Dos with the tag-team title straps. [El Ultimo Dos both have very surprised and delighted expressions on their face. Lance Avery begins to hand them the titles when ...] [The lights go out as the opening seconds of "Carpe Diem Baby" by Metallica blares through the arena. The men in the crowd quickly go nuts as sluttly dressed woman walks out, stopping every few feet for a glamour pose. Behind her, a man known as Jarred Matthews steps out and raises his arms in victory. Followed close behind by partner Chadd Mathews. JM and Chadd hit the ring, jumping over the top rope and holds the bottom and middle ropes open for Sarah to get in. The men flip out once again as she teases just a little more by shaking her ass. Jarred looks around pissed as if he's going to beat one of the happy fans. The music dies down as Jarred and Chadd walk over to Lance Avery, opposite of El Ultimo Dos. Sarah struts around the ring, flirting as various chants begin.] Chadd Mathews: Whoa- Whoa-Whoa! Let's not get too anxious with all the title giving here, now Lance. Ya see, we, _Top_Notch_, have come here to be your savior, Lance. Jarred Mathews: That's right. El Ultimo Dos, if you two f'n Spaniards think you were gonna get off that easy, and just have these belts handed to you.. HA! Think again! Ya see these two belts? They are as good as ours if you two jackasses are all we have to contend with. Victor Cortez: No, no, no.. You have et ahl wrong. Vury wrong. Chu see, these belts, they are ours. El Presidente, he hand them to us. Eesn't that right, Meester Avery? Lance Avery: [Looks questioningly at the two teams as if he ponders what he's about to say.] Well... Victor, you're right, in a way. I said, that since El Ultimo Dos had no competition, they would be handed the tag belts. It looks as if you have some competition in the form of Top Notch. Therefore, these belts will be on the line ... [thinks for a moment] Two weeks from now, May 7, in a ladder match between all tag-teams currently on the SSCW roster by April 30th. [CROWD POPS!] Good luck, and may the best team win! [With that, the camera cuts to the backstage area. Lindsey Florentino is walking around, obviously looking for someone. The camera follows her, and after a bit of searching, she runs into Ji Hang Lee. Ji is currently in street clothes, which consists of a blue t-shirt with the South Korean national symbol on the front(the blue and red yin-yang thing, you know what I'm talking about), black jeans, and tennis shoes.] LF: Excuse me, Mr. Lee? JHL: An-nyong Ha-shim-ni-ka. LF: I don't speak Korean. JHL: Good evening, Miss Florentino. LF: Well, good evening to you as well. May I ask you a few questions? JHL: Ask away. LF: First of all, what are your thoughts on your first match? JHL: I have to give credit where it's due. Terrance Goldsworthy may take a lot of time getting to the ring, and he may not think twice about tradition, but when it comes to wrestling, he gave me a run for my money on Wednesday night. He did a good job, but I guess it wasn't good enough. LF: Second, do you have any thoughts on the ongoing World Title tournament? JHL: Yes, in fact, I do. I've held numerous titles in the Far East, so I know what it takes to be a champion. Obviously, Puah the Hawaiian Love Machine doesn't have it, and that's why he's out of the tournament. But it takes a number of things. You've got to be able to wrestle, of course. LF: Of course. JHL: But if you ask me about it, a man's got to have respect for the rules, and for tradition, to be a true champion. That's something I don't see in a lot of you American wrestlers. You're all just out for the money and the fame, and you know it. LF: Finally... your nationalism seems quite strong. Is there anything you'd like to say about that? JHL: Miss Florentino, I may be patriotic, but that's one thing. To call yourself a role-model for an entire country is a whole different thing. The men who do this know who I'm talking about. In fact, there is one man who calls himself the American role-model, and says that he is synonymous with Stars and Stripes. He says these things despite the fact that he's Canadian. LF: You wouldn't be referring to Stephen C. Bourdo, would you? JHL: You got it. The only thing worse than an American pig is a Canadian who wishes he were an American pig. Mr. Bourdo, my patriotism is one thing, but your false nationalism is another. There are two reasons you're not the American role-model: you're not American, and you're nobody's role-model. No man in his right mind wants to follow in the footsteps of a man who can't accept the fact that he's Canadian. I can smell your insecurity from a mile away. LF: Well, I guess that's all there is to say. JHL: An-nyong-hi Chu-mu-ship-sio, Miss Florentino. LF: Lets get back to the ring! [The scene fades to commercials.] PREZ'S NOTE: If you'd like to compete in the tag-team title ladder match, find a partner by the 30th and let me know.
