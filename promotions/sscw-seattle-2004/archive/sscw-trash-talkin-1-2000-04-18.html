BTW, check the new two dark matches on the slightly updated schedule below. ) ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ * In This Issue: * ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ * Ji Hang Lee * Stephen C. Bourdo ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ v^v^v^v^v JI HANG LEE ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ v^v^v^v^v [The camera fades in on the front of an eight-story apartment building in Seattle. Bus #17 pulls up to the front, and an Oriental man steps off the bus. He is about 5'10", appears to have a little beef, and has black hair, no facial hair, and brown, slanted eyes. He is currently wearing a long, black and blue rain slicker, and he is carrying a red and blue umbrella over his head, as it is raining cats and dogs. He runs into the cover of the building.] [Inside, the man puts his umbrella away and walks straight for the elevator, ignoring the people around him in the lobby. The camera follows him in, and he pushes the button to go to the seventh floor.] Man: Ch'oum poepgetsumnida. Chonun Ji Hang Lee imnida. [Subtitles: Hello. I am Ji Hang Lee.] JHL: Ji Hang Lee, for those who do not know me. I have wrestled in the Orient for eleven years, and I'm a black belt in Tae Kwon Do. [A bell sounds, the door opens, and Ji Hang Lee steps out of the elevator. He walks down the hall to Room #704, fiddles with his keys before finding the right one, and unlocks the door. He enters, and removes his rain slicker, which he hangs up.] JHL: I've been in Seattle for less than a week, and you wouldn't believe how many of those slant-eye jokes I've had to deal with. Apparently, you Americans find something funny about the fact that my eyes are slanted. [Ji Hang settles down on a couch. Now, we see a full decor of the room. A South Korean flag is behind him, and nearby is a poster of him, with writing in Japanese on the bottom.] JHL: Besides, Americans have a sort of "lumping" that cannot be compared to. When they see an Asian person, they immediately assume that he's Chinese or Japanese. Why did I come to America in the first place? Now, everybody must be beginning to think that I complain a lot. I don't. I'm making valid generalizations about Americans. Let me make some more... nah, I'd better not. All I'm showing you now is that I can talk. I haven't shown a soul in the USA that I can wrestle, that I, the Korean star, can keep up with the American heroes. [Ji Hang coughs, and as he's doing so, he utters the word "pigs."] JHL: Whew... and I've picked up a cold of some sort from those people I'm working with. I knew that adjusting to America would take a while. Anyway... back to what I was saying. Now, I do not know if Terrance Goldsworthy is your idea of a hero, but to me, he's not. I've seen what American wrestlers can do. Any American who's had a week-long reign as the champion of the Trailer Park Wrestling League can come to Japan or Korea and convert his fifteen minutes of fame into loud cheers... no matter whether or not they can WRESTLE. Do Oriental stars get the same treatment when they come to America? Heck, no! They get booed, they get scowled at. But worst of all, they are met with the good old "U.S.A.!" chant. There is no worse treatment that a foreigner can get. Most of us don't hate the U.S.A., whether or not we think its people are pigs. So I'm out to change everything about American views of foreign wrestlers. It won't happen overnight, but I'm going to show them that I can wrestle much, MUCH better than my American counterparts. If I don't get cheers, so be it. That's not what I'm here for. I'm here for RESPECT. So, this Wednesday night, no matter how he acts... twenty dollars says that Terrance Goldsworthy will be subject to the cheers of the fans. But, too bad for you, Terrance. You will also be subject to a beating from Ji Hang Lee. Which matters the most? [Fade to black.] ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ v^v^v^v^v STEPHEN C. BOURDO ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ v^v^v^v^v [All the can be seen is darkness, accompanied by slight hum, resembling the hum of a cassette tape. After a couple seconds of that, "Yankee Doodle" starts up.] [The lights slowly dim on, revealing a man standing in front a backdrop of the American flag. The man is dressed in a long, purple, satin robe, feathered around the ends in black. His long, auburn hair is spilling over his beautiful robe, shining in the lights. He stands tilted to the side, one arm across his side and the other holding his chin in a "thinking" pose, with his chin pointing to the air.] SCB : "My fans! My followers! My future subjects! I, Stephen C. Bourdo, have arrived to be your modern day superhero!" [At that, the tape begins speeding up really fast, in the chipmunk's voice fashion. All of a sudden, the insides of the cassette come flying into the view of the camera, flying all over Bourdo. He quickly dumps it to the floor, before continuing.] SCB : "Excuse my little mishaps. I want to get back on topic. I am not only your modern day superhero, no. I am also what you would call your true American hero as well. Stars and Stripes Championship Wrestling will soon be renamed Stephen C. Bourdo championship wrestling, as the stars and stripes and I are one in the same. I am here to guide, to enlighten, and to teach you all the ways of a true hero. I will save all of the fans of SSCW the true way to live a decent life, and not be gutter trash like many of you are. There is no reason to live like the SSCW fans do with me around." [SCB gives another "heroic" pose.] SCB : "So, Mr. Puah. We engage in battle in the first round in the tournament of champions! I am ever so excited, to be honest. My only gripe with this match is that I am doing battle with a Hawaiian? I thought this was about the Stars and Stripes! And I'm battling a foreigner? Well, that gives me only more reasons to want to win for _MY_ fans! After all, my job is to be a rolemodel to the children, and a leader to all the misguided fans." [SCB removes his robe, revealing his shirtless upper body.] SCB : "Then there is this 'battle royal' I'm to be a participant of. Talk about an insult. Stuffing _ME_ in the ring with 11 other sweaty, smelly, low class wrestlers? Appalled. Appalled. I'm am completely appalled. In fact, if I even decide to enter that ring, do _NOT_ expect me to stay long. Just the thoughts... ugh. I... ugh..... I need a bath." [Fade to black and Bourdo is seen trying to rub the germs off his body with his robe.] ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ v^v^v^v^v A good first edition. Here is the schedule once again. *^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v* -*^v^v^v^v[Upcoming Shows]v^v^v^v^*- *^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v* [Excerpt from The Seattle Times] (SEATTLE, WA) It all begins the night of April 19. The community has been buzzing with the rumors and plans of the often times ecentric, local entrepenuer, and now owner of the Stars and Stripes Championship Wrestling, Lance Avery. Lance has made it very clear to the public, that SSCW will not only make money for Seattle, but " SSCW is here to give this place what it needs! SOME ENTERTAINMENT!" With the SSCW Arena being completed a month ago, Lance now brings his exciting mixture of local talent and (inter)national stars into the wrestling ring. According to many wrestling "insiders", this is quite the list of superstars and promises to be one of the more promising promotions to come along in a while. The SSCW Arena, a former glass factory, was not really "completed" but remodeled and redone in preperation for the hard hitting action on its way to the rainy city. The building seats approximately 1,500 wrestling fans and is actually a good facility according to the owner: "You know, most leaugues that just start out, they may be able to grab a building that can fit 600 or so on a good day and they're just _renting_ the place. Not here, SSCW Arena is the mecca of Stars and Stripes and this building is owned by yours truly." Lance Avery invites the Seattle community to attend the SSCW "Open House" Wednesday evening where SSCW stars will be on hand and even a few wrestling matches will take place. This will be held outside of the SSCW Arena as final preparations are made for Sunday Night's festivities. As the fans grow eager with every passing day, and the hype draws to close, Wednesday night, Stars and Stripes Championship Wrestling is here.
