Terrance Goldsworthy is an African-American male who has a bald head, wearing platinum-colored Oakley Sunglasses over his eyes. He is also wearing a metallic-platinum colored vinyl jacket. Underwards, he wears his wrestling gear in which he wears long metallic silver tights with matching knee pads. On the back of his tights is a green "$" airbrushed with his name under it in cursive text. Down both pants legs reads "GOLDSWORTHY" in gold cursive text. He looks at them showing no emotion whatsoever and finally enters the ring. As he enters the ring, he removes his jacket and hands it to the timekeeper as "Whoa" begins to fade.] Arnold Myers: And his opponent....Hailing from Seoul, South Korea. He stands at 5'10 and weights 208lbs, JI!!! HANG!!!! LEEEEE!!!!!!!!!! [Traditional Korean music begins playing over the loudspeakers, as Ji Hang Lee walks out to a few cheers from the crowd. He dons a pair of black karate pants and a turquoise tank top. He stands at the top of the aisle and waves the South Korean flag. He then hangs it over his shoulder and walks to the ring, his attention totally focused on the ring and his opponent. He steps into the ring and paces around, waiting for the match to begin.] Chad Brown: A sort of half hearted reaction for Lee. Any reasoning behind this that comes to your mind Stevie? Stevie T. Dee: Well Chad, Lee comes from a place where tradition and respect are very important. Many western fans, not just Americans, scoff at tradition and everyone assumes that the Asian wrestlers are all just guys who don't say anything and talk a lot of insane risks for the fans reaction. Chad Brown: You can be suprisingly insightful when you want to be. Stevie T. Dee: Go to hell! [Both wrestlers stand in the ring at different corners. The ref signals for the bell to ring and the match is off to a start. Both men come out of their corner and stop in the center with a collar elbow tie up. Both men jocking for position. Goldsworthy backs Lee in the corner. Both men break the hold, Lee calling for a clean break with his hands high above his head. Goldsworthy with a backhand chop and the crowd responds with a loud "Wooohhh" a second, and third, and fourth, and Lee ducks under the fifth. Goldsworthy turns around and Lee is there with a series of lefts and rights and backhand chops. The crowd is starting to come alive. Lee grabs Goldsworthy's hand and sends him for an Irish whip. Goldsworthy holds onto to Lee's hand and tries to reverse it, but Lee continues to go around in a circle while both men and holding onto each other's and jocking for position, Lee tries to snag a German suplex. Goldsworthy takes a leg out from under Lee however. Goldsworthy tries for a spinning leg lock, but Lee kicks him off into the turnbuckle, and Lee springs up. Both men are down to a face off.] Brown: Amazing. The opening minutes of a match can usually be a good indication of where we're going to go in this match, and all I have to say is damn. Stevie T. Dee: Something that I always liked to do is start off the match with a bang Chad. And I think most of these guys know that they'll lose the fans attention really quickly. That however was one of the best exchanges of the night though. [Both men circle each other, and tie up once more. This time Lee slips behind Goldsworthy and hits that German suplex he was looking for earlier. Goldsworthy is down, Lee up to his feet, goes for a leg drop, but Goldsworthy rolls out of the way. Behind Lee now, Goldsworthy gets up, Lee is up to his feet, Goldsworthy hits a side Russian leg sweep. Both men get up to their feet, Lee with a round house kick, ducked by Goldsworthy. Goldsworthy grabs Lee by the waist, lifts him, and drops him neck first into the ropes. Lee stumbles around and Goldsworthy hits a neck breaker. Followed up by a cover, 1..2 Kickout.] Brown: Terrance Goldsworthy is putting on a good showing here against Lee. And the crowd for the most part just seems to hate everything that he does. Stevie T. Dee: Well that's a real shame Chad because Goldsworthy, pound for pound is one of the best technicians here in SSCW. Brown: Well, pound for pound he's in there with one of the best high flyers in the SSCW. [Lee is brought up and taken down with a snap mare take down followed up by a dropkick to the back of the neck and head. Goldsworthy looks at his prone opponent and picks him up taking him over to the corner. Goldsworthy goes to the second turnbuckle and hooks Lee up for a tornado ddt. He leaps as Lee gets a good lock around his opponent. Lee uses Goldsworthy's momentum and is able to block the tornado ddt. Lee lifts Goldsworthy up and hits a northern lights suplex. 1..Bridge by Goldsworthy. He turns this around now and Lee is in a dangerous position, Goldsworthy lifts him up for a powerbomb. Lee with a few desperation hits, Attempts a hurincanrana, but ends up just slipping off of Goldsworthy's shoulders. Lee down to his feet and tries to kick the legs out from under Goldsworthy with a leg sweep to the back of Goldsworthy's legs. Goldsworthy jumps however avoiding the kick. Lee is up, and ducks an oncoming Goldsworthy clothesline. Dropkick by Lee misses. Goldsworthy rushes in, but is tripped by that same leg sweep and now both men are down. Both men are up to their feet. Goldsworthy with a boot to the midsection, and he throws Lee to the outside and follows suit.] Brown: Oh boy, Larry, you should know better than anyone, the outside is a very dangerous place no matter what kind of match it is. Stevie T. Dee: Of course, there are chairs, tables, the railing, and after that the fans. And one or two mistakes out here and it'll cost you your career. Not that it would matter to Hang Lee. [Goldsworthy throws Lee into the ring post and the crowd groans. Goldsworthy goes to pick Lee up, but Lee grabs Goldsworthy's trunks and pulls him into the ring post as well. Goldsworthy stumbles and falls over the guard railing and into a chair. Lee pulls the guard rail close to the ring, the fans scatter as Goldsworthy tries to gather himself while sprawled out on two chairs. Lee ascends the turnbuckles from the outside. With his back turned, he leaps, and hits an almost textbook corkscrew plancha. He takes out himself and Goldsworthy, the crowd is going nuts and somehow, Ji Hang Lee is getting to his feet!! Lee drags himself and Goldsworthy back into the ring. Goldsworthy tries a desperation clothesline, but Lee goes under hooking the full nelson. Lee lifts up once but Goldsworthy fights it. He lifts up a second time, arching his back, both men land, Lee's hit the dragon suplex with a perfect bridge. Goldsworthy is out of it, 1...2 Kick out] Brown: Good God, Good God. Stevie, I don't believe it! He kicked out. Terrance Goldsworthy KICKED OUT!!! Stevie T. Dee: Don't be so surprised Chad! [Lee gets to his feet and quickly goes outside of the ring. He grabs a chair and goes back into the ring. Before he can use it, Goldsworthy hits a low blow. Small package, 1...2..NO!!! Lee out at the last second, the ref's hands was almost inches away from ending the match. Goldsworthy up, he has that chair. He's about ready to knock Lee's head into section S row E seat X. Lee, is up to his feet, Goldsworthy with two steps, and a big swing. Lee ducks, Goldsworthy comes full circle, a kick to the gut. The chair is dropped, Lee grabs Goldsworthy's head and drives it into the mat ala X-Pac's X-Factor. Lee with a sloppy cover. 1...2..KICKOUT!!!!] Brown: It's almost as if Lee had nothing left in him, and it was just desperation. [Goldsworthy up, Lee sets up a chair.
