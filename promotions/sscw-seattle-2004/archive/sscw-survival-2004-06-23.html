[Excerpt from The Seattle Times] (SEATTLE, WA) It all begins the night of July 26th. The community has been buzzing with the rumors and plans of the often times ecentric, local entrepenuer, and now owner of the Stars and Stripes Championship Wrestling, Lance Avery. Lance has made it very clear to the public, that SSCW will not only make money for Seattle, but " SSCW is here to give this place what it needs! SOME ENTERTAINMENT!" Lance now brings his exciting mixture of local talent and (inter)national stars into the wrestling ring. According to many wrestling "insiders", this is quite the list of superstars and promises to be one of the more promising promotions to come along in a while. The SSCW Arena, a former glass factory, a week late in it's completion schedule was not really "completed" is being remodeled and redone in preperation for the hard hitting action on its way to the Emerald City. The building seats approximately 1,500 wrestling fans and is actually a good facility according to the owner: "You know, most leaugues that just start out, they may be able to grab a building that can fit 600 or so on a good day and they're just _renting_ the place. Not here, SSCW Arena is the mecca of Stars and Stripes and this building is owned by yours truly." Lance Avery invites the Seattle community to attend the innagural SSCW Saturday Night Survival! This will be held outside of the SSCW Arena as final preparations are made in the building. The former glass factory is expected to be complete in it's construction late next week. As the fans grow eager with every passing day, and the hype draws to close, Stars and Stripes Championship Wrestling is finally here. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- SSCW SATURDAY NIGHT SURVIVAL *LIVE* From _outside_ the SSCW Arena, Located in downtown Seattle, Washington!
