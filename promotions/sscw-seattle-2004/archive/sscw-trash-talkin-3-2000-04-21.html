I don't think you get it, I've been in the biz for over 3 years! _I don't do dark matches!_ [Pause.] Oh, so the _schedule_ is already made up, what if I break your little schedule, huh, what then? [While Powell is pacing around he notices the camera, which as first, freaks the hell out of him before he knows what it is. Then, he continues talking.] KP: Oh, so you'll fire me? I don't think you know... [Pause.] What do you mean "I'm expendable".. [Pause.] Alright, listen, we can talk this over later, for some strange reason there seems to be a _camera_ in my living room... [Powell violently hangs up the telephone and paces towards the camera. He looks directly at the camera with a scowl, but that soon fades away into a smile.] KP: Hmm, I don't know how to start, my first time on an SSCW camera.. [As if an idea just popped into his head.] _I know!_.. WHY THE _HELL_ DO I HAVE A DARK MATCH!! Against someone with a name like.. "Boondocks Bruiser" Taylor Ryley!! [Very, VERY sarcasticly.] Hmm, he sounds like he is at my my level.. [Powell, visibly annoyed at his debut match, paces around the room a few more times, running his fingers through his golden blonde hair. He stops, points at the camera, opening his mouth, but doesn't say anything, he just walks away muttering something barely audible.] KP: [Under his breathe as he walks away.] Pff, Dark match... [The scene fades to black as Powell walks out of the room.]
