AN INTRODUCTION =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- SSCW...   A name that has lingered in the name of one wrestling fan for nearly all his life...      SSCW is a federation that originated from it's creative and at times, hard headed creator, Lance Avery. Avery has been a wrestler for nine hard years and a wrestling fan all of his life. At age 26, about two years ago, he was going after the only thing that eluded him his entire career. A world championship. In the federation known as CIW (Championship International Wrestling), Lance was a star. Having conquered the tag ranks with good friend Orlando Robinson, dominating the US Title scene and running rampant with the North American title... He started the journey. The long hard quest to finally achieve what every wrestler dreams of becoming, a World Champion. Only three weeks away from his scheduled title shot, Lance sustained a horrendous right hip injury after being dropkicked off the top turnbuckle and landing on the cold steel steps below.  Lance was sidelined for over seven months, missing his beloved title shot and soon being released from CIW. Doctors told him that he should and could no longer compete in the squared circle. Devastated by the unjust news, he went into a serious depression, lasting nearly two months. His long time girlfriend, Ashley Parks, was there for him. Trying to coax him to getting his life back together. Until one day, she struck a chord. The idea of running his own wrestling promotion was one that had always been in the back of his mind. He pondered for quite some time before taking true action. He saved up as much money as he possibly could before setting out. It took him a few months but he finally found a company that could support his idea.        The American Sports Network took Avery's word and gave him most of the money he'd need to get things started. They told him that if SSCW got off to a good enough beginning, he'd see more money roll in.       With all the dough he was given, Lance began on a one man quest. For ten months he searched, wandered and looked everywhere for the whole shebang. He found rings, belts, staff and he even purchased an official SSCW Arena in. The man worked twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week, _everday_. With a lot of blood, sweat and tears poured into the operation, Avery is ready to present SSCW to the public... =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-   2. BEHIND THE SCENES (OOC INTRO)  =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- I love writing. And these days, I think I enjoy writing a good piece of wrestling as much as actually watching it on television.  The spirit of SSCW falls in line with this ideal.  In the vein of such classic leagues as IIWF and many others, the goal of SSCW is the banding together of great, creative writers.        Sure, competition and titles will be here, but that's besides the point.  Angles, feuds and fun are what it's all about. I want us to create some epic, memorable storylines and characters here, and I want you to be a part of it.  =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-   3. WEEKLY STARS AND STRIPES SCHEDULE  =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- There are no "RP packets" per se, in SSCW. Nor newsletters of any kind. Everything is presented to you in a broadcast television style format. This is what the weekly SSCW schedule looks like. 3.1 ***MONDAY FLASH FLASH FORWARD*** Flash Forward is hosted by "Lethal" Larry Cobalt and brings to you the rumblings from around the SSCW. It features a recap of Saturday Night Survival, "Lethal" Larry's personal Rankings Rundown, and interviews from the stars of SSCW. 3.2 ***WEDNESDAY NIGHT LIGHTS OUT*** This is SSCW's dark match show, hosted by Lindsey Florentino, Michael Bay and special guest Stevie T. Dee. Along with the dark match report, you'll get an overview of current fed feuds and news, there is also many words from around the league. 3.3 ***FRIDAY NIGHT COUNTDOWN*** The final weekly SSCW installment before Survival! Hosted by Chad Brown and Stevie T. Dee. Countdown features all your favorite SSCW stars latest promos, and the official SSCW rankings straight from the desk of President Avery. 3.4 ***SATURDAY NIGHT SURVIVAL*** SURVIVAL is SSCW's main weekly card. As the week builds up to a fever pitch, Survival is here to give you your LIVE SSCW fix from all across the nation. The biggest and baddest happenings will be showcased on this event. It will feature all the important matches and is used as a scene for many kinds of title disputes. This show is hosted by Chad Brown and the one and only Stevie T. Dee. 3.5 ***SUPERCARDS*** In SSCW Supercards's are a special event. Super you might say! Being an event like no other, on Supercards all bets are off and all the major players are involved. These are held on approximately a 6-8 week cycle. All major feuds and big time matches are decided on these shows. Our first supercard will be Stars and Stripes Forever, SSCW's greatest event of the year. STARS AND STRIPES FOREVER: RED, WHITE, AND BRUISE *Live* from the First Union Center in Philadelphia, PA July 18, 2004  =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-   4. VICTORY ROAD =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-      Titles in SSCW are not as frequent as you may see them in other federations because we believe that titles, (similar to Supercards/PPVs) are something of prestige, honor, and utmost pride. 4.1 *** SSCW WORLD TITLE *** This title is for the absolute best. The King of The Mountain. If you hold this title you can proudly say that you are one, if not _THE_, best the wrestling world has to offer. This title is defended at least once a month. 4.2 *** SSCW UNITED STATES TITLE *** Second, and without any doubt, very prestigious, is the US Title. The United States title and it's champion represents the land of the free, and the home of the brave. This is thought as a close second to the World Championship. This title is defended within a month, but usually more than the World Title. 4.3 *** SSCW WORLD TAG-TEAM TITLES *** This title is for the best two man tandem in all the land. Only the best team can hold this. The champs must defend it once a month.  =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- 5. SSCW PERSONALITIES  =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- 5.1 Head Honchos   ***SSCW CEO and President*** - LANCE AVERY See Introduction to SSCW   ***Owner of SSCW and American Sports Network*** - MR. DAVE SIMMONS Dave saw a great deal of devotion and love to the sport of wrestling in the eyes of Lance. As the only man who would give Lance the money to get SSCW off the ground, the two have developed a great working relationship. Simmons himself is in his late fifties. Usually refered to as "Bud" by friends, family, and coworkers. Dave is a wrestling fan himself, and although may not watch every show, he stays involved with SSCW goings-on. If there is ever a problem too big, too insane, and/or too controversial for even Avery to make a decision on, Mr. Simmons will intervene. ***SSCW Commissioner, Head of Talent Recruiting*** - ORLANDO ROBINSON Orlando Robinson is long time friends with Lance. Ever since their days together in CIW, the two have been close. Orlando oversees most of the smaller daily happenings of the federation, is head of talent recruiting, and holds a seat in the booking committee. Orlando Robinson is a big, black man, a former weightlifter and wrestling champion. A very dapper individual, he always likes to look his best and dress for every occasion.  His short fuse harkens back to his wrestling days and he's not always the calm, smooth, guy you'd see walking around in the office. 5.2 - SSCW ANNOUNCERS   *** CHAD BROWN *** Chad Brown is the main announcer for SSCW programming. A former Pac-10 collegiate football and basketball announcer has brought his skill to the SSCW. Chad Brown is no rookie to the world of wrestling, however, as he has been announcing for the past five years in various promotions. Chad prefers the good guys, but being the professional he is, tries his best to call things straight down the middle. You can see him on both Saturday Night Survival, Flash Forward, and many other SSCW broadcasts. *** STEVIE T. DEE *** A loudmouth if there ever was one, Stevie is the dysfunctional glue that holds the two man Survival team together. Stevie T. Dee wears the craziest outfits, and loudest clothes. He prefers the bad guys and will certainly let you know it. He can be seen on SSCW Saturday Night Survival and Countdown. *** "LETHAL" LARRY COBALT *** "Lethal" Larry is a long time wrestler, in his late forties. He brings to the announcing table 20 years of wrestling experience and knowledge. Larry, a throwback to the good ole days, always prefers a great technical contest. "The Lethal One" called it quits last year at the tender age of 47. A very proud man, he's a great addition to the staff. You can see him and his wisdom on Flash Forward. 5.3 - SSCW INTERVIEWERS *** LINDSEY FLORENTINO ***- If there are any backstage happenings or a wrestler feels the need to speak, the adorable Lindsey Florentino is there. If you ever need an interview to be done, there's only one woman for the job. :) She also spends some time hosting Wednesday Night Lights Out. *** MICHAEL BAY *** Only 22, and straight out of college, Michael Bay is a wrestling fan at heart, but somewhat new to the scene. However, Lance saw potential in this young kid, and Michael is one to take the ball and run with it. He does many interviews and also helps on the set of Lights Out. 5.4 - SSCW RING ANNOUNCER *** ARNOLD MYERS *** Those closest to him call him Arnie. Arnie is in his late thirties and is showing signs of male pattern baldness as you can tell with his constant changing of comb-overs, toupees, and Rogaine treatment. Despite being only 5'6", Arnie, with his deep voice, does a great job announcing the superstars of SSCW. 5.5 - SSCW TIMEKEEPER *** JULIUS ROSE ***   Some say he has been weird ever since losing the Ring Announcer job to Arnie Myers. Whatever the case may be, Julius Rose is as annoying as can be. Always trying to elevate his status and garner attention, Julius Rose gets into the spotlight wherever he can. In fact, he likes to call himself "The Worlds Most Famous Timekeeper". 5.6 SSCW Head of Security *** JIM JOHNSTON *** A former independent wrestler, sidelined with a career-ending back injury, Jim Johnston and his ever-ready staff are always there when a fight gets too out of control in or out of the ring. 5.7 SSCW Referees *** KEVIN "SWIFT" JUSTICE Always in tip top shape, and giving a 100%, this is the usual Main Event referee and SSCW's best ref. *** MORGAN BILLIS *** A good ref, who always calls it straight down the middle. *** "ROAD" GILL JONES *** Gill is SSCW's oldest ref, and although, not the fastest, or the most in shape, or even the most able to see, Gill does his best.  =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- 7.
