SSCW Sunday Night SURVIVAL! (Part 5 of 7)

23 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene fades back into the SSCW Arena. A cage now sorrounds the ring as staff members are making final adjustments to it. ] CB: Well now we have got a match that should have everybody on the edge of their seats... a steel cage match pitting "Hard Rock" Roland Stone against "Supernatural" Simon Spinx. SD: Well I can't say that I care much for either, Chad, so I just hope they beat the hell outta each other. Let's get this one over with... MYERS: Ladies and gentlemen, this next match is a steel cage match! [Pop!] The only way to be victorious is to be the first man to walk out the door of the steel cage, and plant both feet on the outside floor. Introducing first... ##Papa was a rollin' stone ...## ##Wherever he laid his hat was his home-e-e-e## ##And when he died ...## ##All he left us was alone ...## [The crowd on-hand immediately rises to their feet in anticipation. A resounding chorus of cheers resounds throughout the arena.] [MYERS]: "From Decatur, Georgia, weighing in at two hundred, seventy-five pounds ... here is 'THE HARD ROCK' ... ROLAND STONE!" [The aisle curtain parts and "THE HARD ROCK" ROLAND STONE enters the arena to a resurging response from the fans. The massive African American grappler stands just beyond the aisle curtain, his head lowered in concentration. His prominent pectorals flex for a moment before he raises his head, an intense look burning in his dark brown eyes, and works his way down the aisle. Fans with aisle seats grab at him, a gesture he neither rewards nor objects.] [Stone enters the ring and raises a solid black fist. The crowd responds with raised fists and cheers as Stone paces the ring, awaiting his opponent. Music fades out ...] SD: Well this guy doesn't look like much... ["Blue Monday" by Orgy roars throughout the arena speakers. First Omega walks through the ring entrance. He stands in at six feet, ten inches and is all muscle. He is dressed in a tight black shirt and wears nice black pants. Than, "Supernatural" Simon Spinx walks through the curtain. He is wearing reddish-glittery robe which says "Man of a Thousand Partners" on it. As he makes his way to the ring, he blows kisses to people in the front row. Some fans are even throwing objects at him, but he doesn't seem to mind at all.] MYERS: And his opponent... from Las Vegas, Nevada... weighing in at 265 pounds... here is "Supernatural" SIMON SPINX! CB: Now this man is strange. SD: That's for sure. But at least we don't have to watch another one of those suckups like Roland Stone. CB: [sarcastically] I know they're your favorite. And my god, look at the size of Omega! Well folks, this one is ready to go. [Spinx slowly makes his way up the steps, through the ropes and into the ring. The ref closes the door behind him, and Spinx does a little warm up.] SD: Both men are in the steel cage, now let's see some blood or something! [Spinx walks toward Stone, and immediately places his hand on him, but not a in a wrestling type way. Stone jumps quickly, and slaps Sphinx's hands away.] CB: What is Spinx trying to do? This is a wrestling match. [Stone then charges as Spinx and begins to rain down rights and lefts right into his face. Spinx falls clumsily into the ropes, and Stone backs up. Roland then takes off and flies at Spinx, who immediately drops to the canvas. Stone flies for Spinx, and nails his head into the steel cage. Pop from the crowd! Stone is grabbing at his head, as Simon comes up behind him, and hits him with a reverse suplex. Stone stays on the mat, as Spinx bounces off the rope and comes back down on him with a big elbow to the chest.] SD: Well this strange character, Spinx seems to be in control so far. [Picking Stone up, Spinx pulls him straight into an inverted atomic drop that has Roland Stone grabbing at his groin area. Spinx grabs Stone, and whips him into the ropes. Bouncing back, Simon attempts a backbody drop, but telegraphs the move, and Stone counters by bringing up a knee to the face. Spinx shoots straight and is then caught by a running lariat that puts him on his back. Stone puts a few boots the face before running himself into the ropes. While Roland is about to bounce back, Omega sticks his hand inside the cage and grabs his ankle, tripping him. Stone stumbles, but catches himself, and turns toward Omega. The two starting jawing back and forth, while Spinx sneaks up behind Roland Stone.] CB: Watch it Roland! Behind you! SD: Bust somebody open! [Simon jumps and delivers a knee to the back of Roland that once again sends him flying into the cage, head first. Stone ricochetes back, and is immediately hit with a snap suplex.] SD: Is he bleeding? Is he busted open? CB: Sorry to disappoint you, Stevie, but there isn't a drop in that ring. SD: Damn... [Both men are back on their feet, and Spinx lunges at Stone. Stone dodges the attack and cathes Simon into a simple snap mare takedown. The move does little damage, and both men quickly make their back to their feet. They tie up, and Stone throws Spinx into the ropes. Spinx comes running back, Stone grabs him and throws him head first into the cage. Spinx staggers back toward Roland, and Roland does the same to him on the opposite side of the cage. The crowd lets out a pop from this action! Simon is dazed, and once again makes his way back toward Stone. Stone grabs him and tosses him over head into a powerful belly to belly suplex.] CB: What a great combination of moves from Roland Stone. He has taken control of this match up. [Simon Spinx stays down, and Roland Stone goes over and begins to climb the ropes. Finally reaching the top turnbuckle, the crowd cheers for Stone to land a high impact move. Roland steadies himself by holding on to the cage, getting reading to jump. But then Omega comes over and begins to shake the cage. Stone's legs begin to shake as he struggles to try and keep himself balanced on the turnbuckle. This gives Spinx time to regain himself, and he makes his way toward the corner where Stone is. Omega then steps his interference and Stone turns his attention back to Simon Spinx. But its too late as Spinx grabs him and sends him flying across the ring, landing squarely on his back in the middle of the ring!] CB: This is a disgrace! Omega has no business getting involved in this match. He's a manager, and nothing else! SD: Well as much as Spinx freaks me out, I gotta admit that I've taken a liking to that manager of his. CB: You certainly would. [Stone somehow gets to his feet quickly, and both men head straight for each other. Spinx attempts a hip toss, but is blocked, and Stone reverses it into a hip toss of his own. Spinx grabs his backside while picks him up and turn his around into a DDT. Spinx is down and Roland goes over and says something to Omega, that makes the fans shout their approval! Roland picks up and Spinx and they tie up once again. Spinx give a rake to the eyes that breaks the hold, and sends Stone pacing backward. Simon picks him up and hits a devestating sidewalk slam. Once Roland is down, Spinx begins to head for the door. When he is close enough, the referee opens it, and Spinx begins to put his first leg through the ropes. But, Stone runs over and grabs Spinx's arm, and literally throws him back into the ring, as the ref shuts the door.] SD: Finally, somebody was smart and went for the door. CB: Well Stevie, I still think its a little early for either man to be able to make it out of the cage. SD: Nobody has been able to take real control of this match, so I think both are ripe for the pickens right now. [Spinx is not hurt though, and catches a running Roland Stone and whirls him into a powerbomb. Stone finally seems to be out of energy and just lays on the mat. Spinx gets him onto his feet, and measure him up with three vicious chops that are heard throughout the stadium. He then grabs Stone and whips him into the ropes. When he comes back, Simon pick him up in the air, before tossing Stone over his head and caushing him to crahs into the steel cage!] SD: Finally! There is a small cut over the left eye of Roland that is trickling down a little bit of blood! CB: That cut could prove to be worse than just a simple small cut, because it will leak blood directly into the eye of Roland Stone. As much as I hate to say it, I think Roland Stone is done in this one. [Spinx steps back and begins to play to the crowd, who in turn begin to boo him. Roland Stone is on the opposite side of the ring trying to recuperate. Spinx has his back toward Stone, when Stone suddenly gets a second wind and takes off toward Simon. Right when Simon turns around he is caught a big spear from the "Hard Rock."] CB: Yes! Roland Stone is still in this match up! He just hit the best move he has, the "Stone's Breaker!" SD: And Stone is going for the door. [Indeed he is, as Roland Stone begins to slowly make his way to the door, as Simon Spinx is out cold in the middle of the ring. The refereee opens the door and crowd screams as Stone puts his head between the ropes and tries to make his way out. But suddenly Omega breaks into a dead sprint and swings the cage, and the cage catches Stone squarely in the face and sends him flying back into the ring. A huge heel pop from the crowd goes up!] CB: Somebody get Omega out of this arena! He may have just cost Roland Stone this match! SD: I love it! Omega is the man. [Spinx is starting to come to, and he makes his way back onto two legs. Omega gets into the ring and tosses a pair of handcuffs to Simon, while also pulling out a pair for himself. They drag Stone toward a side of the cage, and both of them latch a pair of cuffs to each hand of Roland Stone. Omega and Simon then latch the other ends of the cuffs to steel cage! Spinx is then seen whispering something into the ear of Omega, and Omega backs away. Simon has come to and is irate. Spinx walks over to Roland, and begins to rub his chest! Stone goes wild, and begins kicking his feet and screaming.] SD: Damn, this guy is fruity... [Spinx then walks away with a big smile on his face. Omega walks toward Simon and hoits him up onto his shoulders. Omega then walks toward the door and carries his brother to the floor.] [Ding, Ding, Ding!] MYERS: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of this match.... "Supernatural" SIMON SPINX! [The scene fades as Simon Spinx blows a kiss into the camera.]
