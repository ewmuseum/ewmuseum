Keenan's wearing his trademark custom made gold Oakley sunglasses. He's got an "I'm Pefect" grin on his face, and is posing for all to see. The ring announcer begins...] [Jeff does his trademark pose, a standing backflip followed up by him holding his arms out to the sides, and laughing at the crowd. Keenan then begins to slowly, casually strut down the aisle, acknowledging a female fan with a 'Jeff Keenan's Sexy' poster. Jeff then reaches the ring, and climbs up the steps, then onto the turnbuckle. Keenan then backflips off, but now as the music fades, he gets the mic and has on a huge cocky grin.] JK: Tonight, is the dance. It's me and eleven other fools in the battle royal for the United states title. Now, I'm a type of guy that doesn't normally single out opponents on the microphone, but there is something I need to say. In his interview earlier this week, Simon Spinx showed _all_ just how stupid his is. He can't even speak right. Show the clip please. [Jeff smiles as they show the clip...] SS: Next we have 'Simply Golden' Jeffrey Keenan. I wonder if that refers to his hair color? And is his natural color? We all know the way to find that out. Step out of my Keenan, I only Naturals. [Camera focuses back on Jeff who's laughing.] JK: See my point. And is his natural hair color? What is he trying to say? Also...'Step out of my Keenan'...Step out of your what? Finally, 'I only naturals'. Wow, I'm just in awe of that fact that he thinks he's intimidating, but he can't even talk right. What the hell. Honestly, they must have extremely lowered reauirements, for somebody as Simon Spinx to use a micropohne. [Jeff listens as the crowd is still laughing.] JK: Well, the way I feel on the matter, is how the hell can a fool like Simon Spinx expect to defeat people in the ring if he talks like a god damn second grader? Anyway, a man such as myself doesn't harp on everybody. Only fools who can't come up with anything to say look at the list, then verbally bash every single person on it. [Jeff grins cockily again.] JK: Me...I do most of my bashing in the ring. Later tonight, all the men in the battle royal will understand that, and they will all have a new found respect, for one...Jeffrey Keenan. The United States title is a hefty goal. One an athlete only of my stature can achieve. I honestly don't give a sh-t whether you like me or not, but I will win in this damn ring tonight and...I will be _your_ SSCW United States heavyweight champion. Eleven other men in the back think they have the balls to defeat the franchise. Well that's just not gonna happen. Yeah...I'll cheat. I'll do what-EVER it takes. You see, I'm only twenty-two years old, but I've been through some sh-t that nobody'll ever want to experience. True I have it all...but I earned it. I've busted my everyday to see if I had what it takes. I do, and that's why I dare your asses to come down to this damn ring later tonight, prove that your better than me, and walk out with the title. I KNOW...that's not going to happen. Why...because I'm not normal. I'm...Simply...[Grining more than ever.]Golden. [Jeff drops the mic, and heads to the back.] Chad Brown: Very confident is "Simply Golden" Jefferey Keenan, but now, ladies and gents, the dark match report from Michael Bay and my main man "Lethal" Larry Cobalt! Stevie T. Dee: That guy is still here? [The scene fades into a desk with a set of TVs behind it. All the TVs are playing various clips of SSCW superstars in action. Sitting at the desk are new recruit to the SSCW interviewing staff, Michael Bay. Beside him, is northwest wrestling legend "Lethal" Larry Cobalt. ] Michael Bay: Welcome to the first edition of SURVIVAL's DARK REPORT! I will be your host along with the one and only "Lethal" Larry Cobalt. "Lethal" Larry Cobalt: It's an honor to be here tonight. [Smiles] I hear we have quite the matchup to let all the good fans know about. Michael Bay: Without a doubt! Before tonight's official fesitivities began the crowd was warmed up with an exciting bouts. "24 Karat" Kerry Powell squaring off against a local favorite "The Boondocks Bruiser" Taylor Ryley. Larry, this was a great warmup match. "Lethal" Larry Cobalt: Certainly. "24 Karat", the much smaller of the two, had to use his speed and agility to it's fullest and he did so very well. Michael Bay: Let's take a look! [The scene now switches to a shot of "24 Karat" Kerry Powell cockily strutting towards the ring.] Michael Bay: Cocky isn't the word for this guy. It's more like money-grubbingly-annoying. "Lethal" Larry Cobalt: This guy even through a few dollar bills at some fans who yelling at him! Cheap bastard only gave out ones. [We now switch to a view of the massive "Boondocks Bruiser" Taylor Ryley, a big man at 7 feet, with unbuttoned overalls, combat boots, a huge stomach and a John Deere hat. Quickly, the big Ryley runs as fast as he can to Kerry Powell. Powell ducks out of the way, and dropkicks Ryley in the back and sends him into the turnbuckle. ] "Lethal" Larry Cobalt: Like I said, using his speed! [The view switches to a shot of "24 Karat" Kerry Powell kicking Taylor in the stomach and then connecting with a swinging neckbreaker. Another clip shows Kerry diving off the top rope with a crossbody, only to be caught by "The Bruiser", hoisted over his shoulder and then _slammed_ into the mat with a tremendous powerslam. ] Michael Bay: At times it looked like Taylor Ryley's power was just too much for The Golden Boy. [Another clip shows Taylor Ryley lifting up the much smaller Powell and then slamming him into the mat with a diving powerbomb.] Michael Bay: But Powell pulled out all the stops. [A clip of Kerry delivering a DDT out of nowhere. Kerry gets back up, points and yells something into the face of "The Boondocks Bruiser". Kerry then walks to his legs, seperates them and then heads for the top turnbuckle. Kerry climbs the ropes, jumps off and delivers a legdrop to the crotch of Ryley, he then covers him cockliy for the 1, 2, and 3.] Michael Bay: Kerry finished the match with his unqiue finishing maneuver the Family Jewels! "Lethal" Larry Cobalt: I honestly didn't think he'd be able to pull it out, but Kerry used his head, and came out on top! Michael Bay: Well, thats all for this edition of the DARK REPORT! [Just as the dark match report is finished, the SSCW Arena's lights fade out, and the song "Money" by Pink Floyd begins to play over the speaker system. The arena is almost pitch black, and the crowd is silent. As Roger Waters says the word "Money" for the first time in the song, _HUGE_ golden pyros shoot up and none other than Kerry Powell begins walking down to the ring.] Chad Brown: What is Powell doing out here, he was already in a dark match!?!! Stevie T. Dee: Well Brownie boy, maybe you didn't hear Powell's interview about his little _"dark match."_ Chad Brown: Still, he has no reason to come out here and ruin what these people paid for.. The show might have to end early because of this! [Meanwhile, Powell, dressed in kaki pants and a tight golf shirt, is climbing into the ring. He calls to ringside for a mic and within seconds is tossed one. He looks into the crowd, almost squinting.] Kerry Powell: Hmm, decent crowd.. Maybe, if I had a _real_ match it would be a sellout crowd.. Wait, what am I thinking, it would definitely be a sellout crowd!! Chad Brown: It seems someone is a little into themselves.. Kerry Powell: I don't think _Avery_ knows what he has on his hands. [Powell takes a few seconds to admire himself.] I mean, come on, look at me folks, I am the perfect physical specimen.. [Heel pop, with Powell taking a few second to flex.] Kerry Powell: Now, as I was saying, Lance Avery doesn't know how much he ripped off the fans watching at home by not letting me wrestle on the televised matches.. I know I have more talent than _Steven_C._Bourdo _ has, the same goes for Bradley Rose and Jeffery Keenan.. Stevie T. Dee: That sounds like a challenge to me! Kerry Powell: So what I am doing right now, is putting down an open challange to anyone with big enough marbles to step into the ring with "The Golden Boy," "24 Karat" Kerry Powell.. I have what it takes to go toe-to-toe with anyone in the SSCW, if not the whole wrestling business.. Hell, you don't gotta wait 'til next week, someone can come down right now... [A voice-over comes over the PA system.] V/O: [In a scrambled voice.] Jack Cody. [The first beat of "Never Say Die" by Guns N Rosescomes on.] V/O: [In a deep gothic voice.] Carolina. [A strong drumb beat blasts through the stadium.] V/O: [In a deep gothic voice.] Crusher. [The fans erupts as "Never Say Die" begins to play loudly over the speakers. It feels like the roof is going to blow off of the arena before, finally a massive man steps out from behind the curtain. The people are out of their seats yelling for the 6'10" "Carolina Crusher," as he stands at the top of the ramp and stares out at his admirers with a smile on his face.] Chad Brown: THAT'S JACK CODY! Stevie T. Dee: What the hell is he doing here?! [Jack Cody stands at the top of the entry way, with a mic. ] Jack Cody: A challenge, aye... [Kerry Powell quickly interrupts. ] Kerry Powell: Hey fat ass, I already beat one country bumpkin earlier tonight, and I won't mind sticking my fist down your throat- Jack Cody: Watch it, squirt. I don't think you know quite what yer talkin' bout. But if you have a challenge wide open to all, consider it taken by "The Carolina Crusher" Jack Cody! Kerry Powell: Is that so? Well, Tex, you have yourself a match, if you can pull yourself away from your tractor. Howabout next week, same time, same channel. Jack Cody: Next week? What's wrong with now? [Jack Cody drops the mic as the fans pop. Kerry Powell looks a little shocked, but stays in the ring. Cody runs down the aisle, slides into the ring but Powell gets a jump on him a begins to lay the boots on him. Cody grabs the leg of Powell, slams him down and jumps on him. He begins to lay the fists on him, the two starts rolling around on the mat, trading punches.] Chad Brown: We have a fight on our hands! STD: AH YES! THESE TWO MEN ARE TEARING EACH OTHER APART! [The SSCW referee's, headed by Kevin "Swift" Justice with Jim Johnston and his security crew jump into the ring and attempt to break things apart. ] STD: No! No! What are you doing?! Chad Brown: Folks, we'll be right back!
