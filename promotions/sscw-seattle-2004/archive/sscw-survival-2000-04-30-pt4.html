SSCW Sunday Night SURVIVAL! (Part 4 of 4)

30 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene fades into the raucus SSCW arena.] V/O: [In a scrambled voice.] Jack Cody. [The first beat of "Never Say Die" comes on.] V/O: [In a deep gothic voice.] Carolina. [A strong drumb beat blasts through the stadium.] V/O: [In a deep gothic voice.] Crusher. [The fans erupts as "Never Say Die" begins to play loudly over the speakers. It feels like the roof is going to blow off of the arena before, finally a massive man steps out from behind the curtain. The people are out of their seats yelling for the "Carolina Crusher," as he stands at the top of the ramp and stares out at his admirers with a smile on his face, while hold a baseball bat in his left hand.] AM : Making his way to the ring, hailing from Buiess Creek, North Carolina... he weighs in at 322 pounds... he's known as the "Carolina Crusher"... here is, JACK CODY! [The 6'10" Cody finally starts his long descent toward the ring, with the fans still on their feet. Jack is dressed in his usual ring attire, full length black pants with "Carolina" and Crusher down opposite legs in blue, along with his jet black boots and black elbow pads with blue stars. As Cody takes his time down the aisle, fans are slapping him on the back and receiving some of his _big_ high fives. Cody reaches the ring and steps over the top rope with ease. Striding straight to the center of the ring, Jack stands there until the final note of his theme song is played. He then raises his arms, and the bat, high above his head and gets a _huge_ face pop from the crowd. A chant of "Carolina" *clap* *clap* *clap* *clap* *clap*-- "Crusher" *clap* *clap* *clap* *clap* *clap*, echoes throughout the arena.] SD : Booooooooo. CB : What? SD : You heard me. BOOOOOOOOOO!! ["Money" by Pink Floyd begins to play over the speakers, the chang of coins and money machines are heard as the lights fade out. As Roger Waters says "Money" the first time the light shoot back on to a golden color.] [Announcer]: Coming to the ring now, from the beautiful beaches of Malibu, California.. Weighting in at... Uhh, 2 billion dollars... ? _"24 KARAT"_ _KERRRRRRY POWWWWWWELLLLLLL!_ [While the announcer is saying this, Powell, clad in a golden sleveless robe, walks down the ramp. The gold lights shimmer off his blond hair, as he climbs into the ring. He takes off the robe and throws it aside, climbing the ropes, lifting his fists in the air. He jumps down and begins to walk towards his oppenent to get an early jump on the bell.] SD : YEEEEAAAAAHHHHHHH!!!!! CB : What? SD : Damnit, buy a miracle ear. YEAAAAAAAH!!! [In the ring, Powell extends his hand to Cody, smirking and Cody meets the offer with a clean handshake as the bell rings. The two athletes quickly move to a collar and elbow tie-up, with Cody using his weight advantage to move Powell to the ropes.  Following a clean break, both wrestlers tie up once again, this time with Powell managing to maneuver Cody into a standing side headlock which he grinds on forcefully.  Cody breaks the hold by sending Powell into the ropes... Powell ducks the clothesline attempt by Cody off of the rebound and cathes Cody with a nice Japanese armdrag on the return.  Cody is quickly up, only to fall prey to another armdrag take down and then a third, which Powell follows up by holding on to Cody's arm and applying an armbar with his legs.] CB : Great speed shown here by Powell.  He's got the bigger Cody off balance. SD : That's actually good strategy on his part.  I mean, he's giving up four inches and over fifty pounds to the "Caterpillar Crusher", so speed might be his only advantage.  Well, that and a good steel chair. [Cody has managed to get to his feet and applies a hard left boot the stomach of Powell and follows by snapmaring the smaller man over by his bandaged hands.  Powell immediately screams in pain, but Cody shows little remorse, grabbing Powell's hand and stomping them into the mat with his boot.  Powell rolls over in pain, and Cody acknowledges the referee's request for a break as he checks on the injury.] SD : What's Cody doing?  Kick the man some more! CB : Cody is showing good sportsmanship here by allowing Powell's hands to be checked out. SD : Yeah, but only after he stomped on them.  That proves the boy can' be that clueless -- follow up! [Powell slightly shoves the referee away as he gets to his feet in a show of his willingness to go on.  Cody imediately is on the former Chesire with a hard right hand and a chop across the chest, and then sends him to the ropes only to catch him with a beautiful flying dropkick. Cody then sets Powell up for a piledriver, hoisting him up only to have Powell to kick and force Cody to set him down and try it again. The second attempt is reversed by Powell with a back body drop, which leaves Powell shaking his hands from the exertion in lifting the bigger Cody up and over.  Powell is quick to forget his pain and nails Cody with a series of kicks to the chest and a hard backward kick which sends Cody crashing to the mat.  Powell then springs over the top rope to the outside ring apron, and, as Cody rises to his feet, he uses the top rope to hit Cody with a beautiful springboard somersault dropkick.  The crowd pops loud for the acrobatic move, and Powell seems to feed off this energy.  He quickly heads to the top rope, balances high on the top turnbuckle, and catches the rising Cody with a sunset flip from the top which results in a two count.  Undaunted, Powell hits the ropes once more and catches Cody with a beautiful spinwheel kick.  The subsequent cover and grapevining of the leg results in a near fall with Cody kicking out at the last possible instant.] CB : That one was close, folks!  I thought for sure that there was no way that Cody would kick out of that one. SD : There you go thinking again. [Powell manages to get to his feet and comtemplates his next move after stomping a hard right boot to the head of Cody.  Instead of a high impact move, Powell uses his brain and tries to catch Cody off guard with a nice rolling small package that nets another two count.  Powell quickly gets up, picks Cody up from the mat, and irish whips him to the corner.  Cody, having hit the turnbuckle hard with his back, slumps down in the corner, ripe for the picking.  Powell sprints to the opposite corner, prepares himself, and then dashes across the ring and attempts a cartwheel kick to the corner -- only to find nothing but air as Cody slips out at the last instant!  Cody is quick to attack, offering up several stiff European uppercuts and a slingshot suplex that leaves both men breathless in the middle of the ring.] CB : What a match brought to you here, live from the SSCW Arena in Seattle, Washington.  This match has been everything that I thought it would be and more! SD : I'll give both men credit, Brown.  This match hasn't quite been the snooze-fest I predicted it to be.  Of course, a little blood, a few chair shots, and maybe "Cop a Feel" Jack Cody going headfirst through a table would make this match that much better!  And biscuits!  Couldn't be a great match without biscuits! [Both wrestlers lie exhausted in the middle of the ring, chests heaving at the exertion put into this match.  As the referee puts the count on the pair, the crowd begins to cheer for both men to get up.  THREE... FOUR... Powell shows the first signs of stirring... SIX... SEVEN... and is the first one to his feet.  A tired Jack Cody slowly stirs near Powell's feet.  Shaking the cobwebs from his head, Powell realizes that the advantage is his and makes his way to the nearby corner and slowly climbs to the top rope.] CB : This could be it, Stevie T!  If Powell hits this maneuver, it'll be all over. SD : Cody is slow to recover in the ring.  I hear from Lindsey Florentino that he's the same way in the bedroom! [Powell perches up on the turnbuckle as he waits for Cody to rise to his feet.  The noise level of the crowd grows in anticipation as Cody gets to his feet...] CB : Here comes Powell with a hurricanrana from the top! NO! Jack Cody catches him in mid air and turns it into a makeshift POWERBOMB! [The referee quickly moves in for the count as Cody follows the move with a cover and a grapevine.  The crows counts with the count... [O N E!!!] . . . . . [T W O!!!] . . . . . KICKOUT!! Both men are hurt, but Cody winces but gets to his feet soon enough. Powell rolls to the outside and clobbers Cody with an overhand right. Cody responds with a snaking left jab, but he cannot match Powell in brawling, and the veteran batters him back with a series of blows. Powell grabs Cody by the head and cracks it across the steel ring steps! Heel pop from the crowd! Powell rolls Cody back into the ring.] SD : Way to go Kerry! Take no prisoners! CB : Perhaps Jack Cody was letting his youthful enthusiasm get the better of him, in attempting to brawl toe-to-toe with Powell. There are few men who can match blows with 24 Karat! [Powell adjusts his tights and steps back into the ring. Cody is getting to his feet once again but Powell cuts him down with a lariat. The veteran methodically works over Cody's body with a series of fistdrops, elbowdrops and stomps. Powell grabs Cody's legs and goes for a figure four. Cody struggles a little but Powell manages to place his legs in position and locks on the hold! Immediately the strain is visible on Jack Cody's face and he raises his fists to the air, attempting to draw on all his reserves of endurance.] SD : Rulebreakers with solid scientific skills are among the most dangerous men in the sport. Kerry Powell knows when to bust heads and when to crack legs. CB : I think you're underestimating the reserves of Jack Cody's stamina, T. He doesn't look like he's gonna submit. [The ref asks Cody if he is ready to give in and the rookie shakes his head furiously, though the pain is clearly evident on his face. Kerry locks the hold even tighter, willing his foe to submit. Some of the younger fans at ringside start up a "CRUSH-ER! CRUSH-ER!" chant. Powell, seeing that his young opponent has more endurance than he suspected, grabs hold of the bottom rope to add more leverage to the hold. Cody lets out a gasp of pain, but the referee turns around and notices the illegality. He slaps Powell' hands away from the ropes and yells at him to release the hold! Powell is not too happy, but complies. He leaps up and gets in the referee's face. Meanwhile Jack Cody staggers to his feet, limping noticably.] SD : What an injustice! The referee is clearly babysitting Jack Cody. I demand the hold be locked on again! CB : Don't be ridiculous, Scoop! Powell tried to cheat and for once justice was served. [The ref tells Powell to "let me take care of the officiating while you take care of the wrestling!" Powell curses loudly and turns back to Jack Cody, who is now standing right in front of him! Powell makes a clumsy grapple, but Cody slips behind, locks his arms around Powell' head and...] CB : Oh! He bulldogged Powell right into the canvas! SD : This is disgraceful, the ref is clearly against Kerry! Get My Cunt down here to stop the match! CB : That is Mike Hunt, Stevie. SD : That's what I said, you moron! [The crowd gives Jack a solid face pop. Cody takes his time to get up again, still wincing from his strained leg. But he needn't hurry, since Powell is rolling around on the canvas clutching his head. Cody hauls Powell up, sets him in position, and executes a crunching piledriver! Another face pop from the crowd. Cody rolls over Powell and hooks the leg. The ref puts on the count: [O N E!!!] . . . . . [T W O!!!] . . . . . kickout by Powell! Cody gets up, runs to the ropes, bounds off and goes for a big splash. Powell rolls out of the way at the last moment and Cody winds himself on the mat! Both men are slow getting to their feet, feeling the pace. Cody has the faster reflexes and stuns Powell with a kick to the midsection. Cody grabs Powell and whips him to the ropes. Powell comes bounding back and Cody hits him with a spinning knee-lift! Powell staggers back against the ropes and Cody doesn't give him a chance to recover, hitting him with a mighty clothesline and sending him toppling outside the ring! Loud face pop from the crowd! Cody goes running to the opposite ropes, bounds off, runs back across and sails over the top rope, hitting Kerry Powell with a plancha dive just as the veteran is getting to his feet! Both men smash into the steel crowd railings, with Powell taking the brunt of the bump. Awed pop from the crowd!] CB : What a turn-around for the books! Jack Cody is something of a pioneering stylist in the SSCW; he's a big guy but he prefers a fast-paced, high-flying match. SD : But does he have the killer instinct, Brown? When it comes down to it, that is the most important thing for a fighter to possess. Kerry Powell has that dangerous ingredient, and that is why he'll triumph. CB : Well, right now Kerry is not displaying much of his famed killer instinct at all. He's getting shown up by the skills of this young lion, Jack Cody! [Jack Cody gets up and climbs up onto the apron, intent on taking the fight back into the ring where it belongs. Powell staggers up behind him, giddy from the succession of blows he's taken. Jack Cody attempts to climb over the ropes, but Powell grabs hold of his legs! The crowd gives a mixed pop as the veteran attempts to yank Cody back down to the outside! Cody scrambles and loses his grip, and as he comes down his throat catches on the top rope, snapping his head back brutally! Shocked gasp from the crowd! Cody slumps onto the arena floor, clutching at his throat and gagging. Powell immediately grabs hold of Cody's head, and uses his other hand to pummel the "Real Deal" repeatedly on the bridge of his nose and forehead! The ref's count is getting close, so once again Powell shoves his opponent back into the ring and quickly follows.] SD : See, little boy? There's no way a young whelp like Jack Cody can hold an advantage on Kerry Powell for long. Powell is too experienced, too ringwise and too wiley for that. CB : Well, I don't question Powell's resilience, but I do quesiton some of the tactics he used to turn the match around. SD : What are you talking about, Brown? That's Powell' famed killer instinct you were babbling on about before! CB : You were the one who started on about that, Scoop! SD : I was not! CB : [sighs] Let's just concentrate on the match shall we? [Powell drags up Jack Cody, who's nose is swelling up slightly from being bludgeoned repeatedly. Powell hooks one arm, then the other and executes a powerful double-underhook suplex! Powell maintains the hooked arm position, gets to his feet, hauling up Cody with him, and executes a second double-underhook suplex! Awed pop from the crowd! Once again, Powell gets to his feet, dragging up Cody with him, but this time he shifts his arm position and executes a crunching belly-to-belly suplex! The "Carolina Crusher" is motionless after the three suplex variations.] CB : Very impressive work from Kerry! Powell may very well be the master of the suplex in the SSCW. SD : That's it for Jack Danie... I mean Jack Cody! His brief fling with stardom is over! [Powell goes for the cover: [O N E!!!] . . . . . [T W O!!!] . . . . . [T H R E E???]' CB : KICKOUT! I CANNOT BELIEVE THIS! SD : Damnit. Damnit. Damnit. [Powell shakes his head and turns towards the turnbuckles. With Cody still on the mat, Powell scampers up to the top turnbuckle, raising his arms to the heavens, flash bulbs popping left and right. Powell leaps off with a flying headbutt and connects...!] CB : LLOOOOOOOOOK OOOUUUUT!! [...With the mat.] CB : This match has been grueling to both men. Powell is still down, and what the hell is Cody doing? SD : That big lug is gonna try going from the top rope! This could cost him the match. [Jack begins to the climb the ropes as the fans cheer him at every step. But before Cody is even to the top, the batter Kerry Powell is on his feet and over to the corner. When Jack is at the top rope, Kerry immediately shakes the ropes, causing Cody to lose his balance and drop straight down, crashing his groin area down to the turnbuckle! The crowd definitely feels Jack's pain as they scream.] SD : That'll ruin your weekend... [Powell is now on the offensive as he also climbs to the top turnbuckle, and both men are perched atop there. Powell hooks one of Jack's arms behind his head, and is ready to execute a superplex! Kerry attempts to jump back, but can't get Jack off the turnbuckle. The crowd is in anticipation of what is going to happen. Kerry gathers his strength and attempts the suplex again, but once again is blocked. This time Jack fires a right as the crowd jump to their feet. Another right has Kerry losing his balance.] CB : [excited] What's going to happen? What's going to happen? SD : Settle down, old man. [Jack stands up and grabs Kerry. He hoists Powell up and spins him around in mid-air. Now having Powell in an inverted position, Jack springs into the air, and comes down with the sound of a cannon as Kerry Powell's head is slammed into the canvas! _Unbelieveable_ pop from the fans in the arena!!] CB : SOUTHERN JUSDICE! SOUTHERN JUSDICE! IT'S OVER, COVER HIM JACK!! SD : No, come on Kerry! [Both men are exhausted on the mat as Jack slides over and throws his body on top of Powell's. The ref runs over and makes the count: 1 -- 2 -- 3!! Ding, ding, ding!] SD : Dammit! CB : He did! Jack Cody has made a successful debut in the SSCW! What a match. AM : Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of this match... JACK CODY! CB: Jack Cody, another man survives the first round! What a match! That is all for this edition of SNS! Be sure to tune in next week!
