Looking for Writers/Bookers

8 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, Stars and Stripes Championship Wrestling is currently on the lookout for creative people willing to write on a weekly basis and have fun :-) I'm not asking for much, maybe a match or two a week (or more if you wish). We're also looking for possible bookers. If you like to see your ideas and visions become a semi-reality (this is fantasy wrestling, afterall) then SSCW is the place for you. Once again, creative people looking for fun. If you'd like to know what SSCW is all about, please read the following (originally posted on Tuesday). ________ Welcome one, welcome all! This fateful day, April 4th, 2000, in my hopes, will mark an important event in E-Wrestling. The reason... I'm sending an invitation out to the entire e-wrestling world. What are you invited to, you ask? :) You have been invited to join what has the potential to become a great fantasy wrestling federation. It's as simple as that. All the ingredients are here. Fantastic cards, a fantastic webpage, a hard-working, reliable staff, and great competition. What I am presenting to you is simple. FUN. Imagine this if you wheel. :) I want you to remember back to the first year or so of your career. Remember how fun it was? Remember sending in those first role-plays? Remember waiting eagerly for that card to come out and then feeling that much better when you won your first match? Remember your first great feud? It was fun, and those were the days. This feeling is what I want to bring back to e-wrestling because I miss it just as much you do. Let's face it for the umpteenth time. E-wrestling isn't quite what it use to be. Bringing back the good times is what I'm aiming for. And I have the tools to do it. The staff is here to ensure on time cards, efficiency in every department, great matches, and ensuring that it will take many a disastrous events to force this federation to even ponder the thought of dying out. The cards are here. With over five years of RSPWF, AOL and e-wrestling experience I have seen great cards. I will incorporate what I have seen, including _great_ angles, impromptu matches and scenarios, and that feeling of having great time all into one exciting federation that is the SSCW. Now, I'd like to introduce to you, to the world :) ... An Introduction ¨˜°²~·-.¸.¸,¸.·'`·..·Â ´'·.¸,¸.¸.-·~²°˜¨ SSCW... A name that has lingered in the name of one wrestling fan for nearly all his life... SSCW is a federation that originated from it's creative and at times, hard headed creator, Lance Avery. Avery has been a wrestler for nine hard years and a wrestling fan all of his life. At age 26, about two years ago, he was going after the only thing that eluded him his entire career. A world championship. In the federation known as CIW (Championship International Wrestling), Lance was a star. Having conquered the tag ranks with good friend Orlando Robinson, dominating the US Title scene and running rampant with the North American title... He started the journey. The long hard quest to finally achieve what every wrestler dreams of becoming, a World Champion. Only three weeks away from his scheduled title shot, Lance sustained a horrendous right hip injury after being dropkicked off the top turnbuckle and landing on the cold steel steps below. Lance was sidelined for over seven months, missing his beloved title shot and soon being released from CIW. Doctor's told him that he should and could no longer compete in the squared circle. Devastated by the unjust news, he went into a serious depression, lasting nearly two months. His long time girlfriend, Ashley Parks, was there for him. Trying to coax him to getting his life back together. Until one day, she struck a chord. The idea of running his own wrestling promotion was one that had always been in the back of his mind. He pondered for quite some time before taking true action. He saved up as much money as he possibly could before setting out. It took him a few months but he finally found a company that could support his idea. The American Sports Network took Avery's word and gave him most of the money he'd need to get things started. They told him that if SSCW got off to a good enough beginning, he'd see more money roll in. With all the dough he was given, Lance began on a one man quest. For ten months he searched, wandered and looked everywhere for the whole shebang. He found rings, belts, staff and he even purchased an official SSCW Arena in. The man worked twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week, _everday_. With a lot of blood, sweat and tears poured into the operation, Avery is ready to present SSCW to the public... 2. What You Can Expect If and hopefully when you join SSCW, you can expect a lot of things. First of all, you can expect fun. SSCW provides you with (for the most part) on time, fully written and detailed cards. You can expect to be entertained. I want to get everyone involved in something and will try my hardest to make sure that happens. Besides all o' that, I like to have some good ole competition. In SSCW, competition and role-playing will play a major in what goes on in the shows and in your characters career. And with that fantastic segway, I bring you to... 3. The Weekly Stars and Stripes Schedule 3.1 Role-play packs Role-plays are sent out in packs by yours truly [ [detail withheld] ] . There will be three role-play packs per week. This does not mean you have to role-play three times a week, of course, but it gives everyone time to role-play in a week's time. Role-plays are sent on: * Tuesday afternoon * Friday afternoon * Saturday afternoon 3.2. Wednesday Night Explosion! Explosion is more of the SSCW undercard comparable to Heat or Saturday Night Main Event. The card will not feature more than five to six matches and it will contain lower rate matches. It will be a card used mostly to build feuds, develop storylines and as a showcase for newer talent. This show is hosted by Chad Brown and "Lethal" Larry Cobalt. 3.3. Sunday Night SURVIVAL! SURVIVAL is SSCW’s main weekly card. The biggest and baddest happenings will be showcased on this event. It will feature important matches and is used as a scene for any kinds of title matches. Simply put, this is where it's at :) This show is hosted by Chad Brown and the one and only Stevie T. Dee. 3.4 PPVs and Mega-Events In SSCW PPV's are a special event. An event like no other, on PPV's, all bets are off and all the major players are involved. PPV's are held every other month in Stars and Stripes Championship Wrestling because here, they are something unlike any other. All major feuds and big time matches are decided here on these shows. However, in the months without PPV's Mega-Events are held and broadcast (for free) on the American Sports Network. These shows can be compared to Clash of The Champions. These shows are very big, but not quite a PPV. 3.5 Weekly Updates Weekly updates are sent shortly after events. Updates include the latest happenings and goings on in the federation, the rankings, the latest card schedule and usually a touching personal message from me :) SSCW's rankings are decided by the traditional Win/Loss record, your current push, role-playing and effort. 4. Victory Road Titles in SSCW are not as frequent as you may see them in other federations because we believe that titles, (similar to PPVs) are something of prestige, honor, and utmost pride. SSCW World Championship Belt- This title is for the absolute best. The King of The Mountain. If you hold this title you can proudly say that you are one, if not _THE_, best the wrestling world has to offer. This title is usually defended at least once a month. SSCW United States Championship Belt- Second, and without any doubt, very prestigious, is the US Title. The United States title and it's champion represents the land of the free, and the home of the brave. This is thought as a close second to the World Championship. This title is defended within a month, but usually more than the World Title. SSCW World Tag-Team Championship Belt- This title is for the best two man tandem in all the land. Only the best team can hold this. The champs must defend it once a month. 5. SSCW Personalities 5.1 Head Honchos SSCW CEO and President - Lance Avery See Introduction to SSCW Owner of SSCW and American Sports Network - Chester Taylor Chester saw a great deal of devotion and love to the sport of wrestling in the eyes of Lance. As the only man who would give Lance the money to get SSCW off the ground, the two have developed a great working relationship. Chester himself is in his late fifties. Usually refered to as Bud by friends, family, and coworkers. Chester is a wrestling fan himself, and although may not watch every show, he stays involved with SSCW goings-on. If there is ever a problem too big, too insane, and/or too controversial for even Avery to make a decision on, Chester will intervene. SSCW Commissioner, Head of Talent Recruiting - Orlando Robinson Orlando Robinson is long time friends with Lance. Ever since their days together in CIW, the two have been close. Orlando oversees most of the smaller daily happenings of the federation, is head of talent recruiting, and holds a seat in the booking committee. Orlando Robinson is a big, black man, a former weightlifter and wrestling champion. A very dapper individual, he always likes to look his best and dress for every occasion. His short fuse harkens back to his wrestling days and he's not always the calm, smooth, guy you'd see walking around in the office. 5.2 SSCW Announcers - - Chad Brown - Chad Brown is the main announcer for SSCW programming. A former Pac-10 collegiate football and basketball announcer has brought his skill to the SSCW. Chad Brown is no rookie to the world of wrestling, however, as he has been announcing for the past five years in various promotions. Chad prefers the good guys, or the babyfaces, but being the professional he is, tries his best to call things straight down the middle. You can see him on both Sunday Night Survival and Explosion and many other SSCW broadcasts. - Stevie T. Dee - A loudmouth if there ever was one, Stevie is the dysfunctional glue that holds the two man Survival team together. Stevie T. Dee wears the craziest outfits, and brightest clothes. He prefers the bad guys, or heels and will certainly let you know it. He can be seen on SSCW Sunday Night Survival. - "Lethal" Larry Cobalt - "Lethal" Larry is a long time wrestler, in his late forties. He brings to the announcing table 20 years of wrestling experience and knowledge. Larry, a throwback to the good ole days, always prefers a great technical contest. "The Lethal One" called it quits last year at the tender age of 47. A very proud man, he's a great addition to the announce team. He can be seen on Wednesday Night Explosion. 5.3 SSCW Interviewers - - Lindsey Florentino - If there are any backstage happenings or a wrestler feels the need to speak, the adorable Lindsey Florentino is there. If you ever need an interview to be done, there's only one woman for the job. :) - Michael Bay - Only 22, and straight out of college, Michael Bay is a wrestling fan at heart, but somewhat new to the scene. However, Lance saw potential in this young kid, and Michael is one to take the ball and run with it. SSCW Ring Announcer - Arnold Myers Those closest to him call him Arnie. Arnie is in his late thirties and is showing signs of male pattern baldness as you can tell with his constant changing of comb-overs, toupees, and Rogaine treatment. Despite being only 5'6", Arnie, with his deep voice, does a great job announcing the superstars of SSCW. SSCW Timekeeper - Julius Rose Some say he has been weird ever since losing the Ring Announcer job to Arnie Myers. Whatever the case may be, Julius Rose is as annoying as can be. Always trying to elevate his status and garner attention, Julius Rose gets into the spotlight wherever he can. In fact, he likes to call himself "The Worlds Most Famous Timekeeper". SSCW Head of Security - Jim Johnston A former independent wrestler, sidelined with a career-ending back injury, Jim Johnston and his ever-ready staff are always there when a fight gets too out of control in or out of the ring. SSCW Referees Kevin "Swift" Justice Always in tip top shape, and giving a 100%, this is the usual Main Event referee and SSCW's best ref. Kevin Billis A good ref, who always calls it straight down the middle. "Road" Gill Jones Gill is SSCW's oldest ref, and although, not the fastest, or the most in shape, or even the most able to see, Gill does his best. 7. The Application And finally the application.
