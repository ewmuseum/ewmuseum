[The scene fades back to the announcing table.] CB: Welcome back! Next we have oursleves an Orting Strap Match! STD: Well, Chad, I along with nearly everyone else in the viewing audience, have no idea what the hell an Orting Strap Match is. Care to clue us in? CB: If you were to open an atlas and actually read something, Steve, you'd find that Orting is a dark, desolate, "hickish" town, located in the nether regions of Washington State. It's a place where average 4th grade boy is 6 feet tall and weighs 300 pounds. Where the dress code is overalls and a straw hat, where 9 times out of ten your cousin is probably your brother and Technology is the red truck down the road. In Orting, there are only four last names ... Redneck isn't just a bodily condition, it's a way of life. Spawned from this culture is a sick, sadistic past time in which two men are connected at the wrist with a large leather cowhide strap. In Orting, this is game is thought of as a jump-rope, a schoolyard mainstay but also as a way to end the fussin' and a feudin'... Lance, having grown up and traveled extensively in the state of Washington, has seen this horrid practice and has brought it here... In SSCW... [A long pause.] STD: A stirring rendition, no doubt. CB: You like it? I wrote it myself. STD: [Pats Chad's shoulder] You should be proud of yourself... [Makes an expression conveying "What in the hell...?"] CB: Oh, I am. And with a little background on the subject we are now ready for SSCW's FIRST EVER... Ash is wearing black boots, black parachute pants, white sash. He is wearing a white Superman shirt, and is sporting a brass walking cane. He points to "his adoring public" as they boo him heavly. Ash makes a few "thank you, thanks you" gestures to the crowd as he continues struting to the ring. Once in the ring he blows a kiss to the crowd and bows for everyone] Ring Announcer:In the ring, hailing from Malibu, California, and standing at 5 ft 11 inches, weighing in at 225 pounds, he is, "Mr. Fame", the um, uh, well, HERE IS, LEVI. [Both men come to the center of the ring where the referee is holding the leath strap in his hands. He reaches with one end, and latches it to the right wrist of Chris Jameson, and then attaches the other end to the right wrist of Levi Ash. Ding, Ding, Ding!] SD: [sarcastically] And we're off. [As soon as the the strap is attached to the wrist of Ash, he attacks, nearly knocking over the referee in the process. He nail flurries of punches on Jameson, sending him back into the ropes. Once against the ropes, Jameson tries to get away by attemtping a clothesline, but Ash ducks it. When Chris turns around and comes running back, he is caught by Levi with a dropkick to the face. James goes down and Levi yells out toward the crowd. Big heel pop!] CB: Well these fans are making their dislike for Levi Ash obvious here tonight. SD: Yeah, but Ash is just loving it. [Indeed, Ash is soaking up all the boos that the crowd can give him. Jameson begins to stir on the mat, and he suddenly jerks the leather strap and pulls Ash down to the mat with him. Jameson hops on top of Ash and begins to throw combinations of punches. He stands up and brings Ash to his feet with him. Throwing Levi into the ropes, he hits him with a snap suplex that plants both men on the mat.] CB: I'm surprised that these men can execute some of these moves even while being strapped together. SD: Well the strap is long enough to allow for some mobility. [Jameson jumps back up, and runs to the top rope as Levi slowly gets to his feet. When Ash is stable and on his feet, Jameson leaps from the top turnbuckle and lands a double axe handle, sending Ash back down. Chris immediately runs back to the top rope, and the crowd jumps up. But this time Ash is on his feet quicker, and with a toss of the rope, sends Jameson crashing to the canvas from the top rope, landing on his head!] CB: My god! I think Jameson is knocked out. SD: Well he went to the well one too many times. He got lucky he hit that move on Levi the first time, I don't know why he thought he could land it again. [Now its Levi that is sprinting toward the corner. He scurries up to the top rope and immediately leaps, landing with a flying elbow onto Jameson. Staying aggressive, Ash picks Jameson up and instantaneouly plants him with a DDT. Ash covers: 1 -- 2 -- kick out!] CB: It is way too early for pin. SD: I don't know, those were some big moves that Ash hit. [Ash immediately begins to complain to the ref about a slow count, and fans once again show their disdain to an oblivious Levi Ash. Jameson comes to, and Ash runs off and ropes, and comes back toward Chris. Ash attemtps a hurricanrana, but Jameson catches him and slams him down with a powerbomb!] CB: Great counter! Jameson isn't giving in just yet. SD: Puh-leaze, that was not a great counter. Levi just made a mistake and telegraphed the move. CB: I don't agree on that one. [The fans let out a face pop as Jameson picks up a weary Levi Ash and nail a back suplex. Now Jameson attempts a cover: 1 -- 2 -- no! Jameson continues to the offense as he goes to the second turnbuckle and hits Ash with a diving lariat! He tries another cover: 1 -- not even a two count!] SD: I think Jameson better learn that it takes more than two moves to put away Levi Ash. [Both men get to their feet, and Ash attempts to throw Jameson into the corner, but Chris reverses it and instead throws Levi into the corner. Ash comes stumbling out, and Jameson attemtps a superkick, but his leg is caught by Ash. Levi sweeps Chris' other leg out from under him and then jumps on him. Ash grabs the excessive strap and begins to choke Jameson! The fans erupt into a chorus of boos! The ref begins to lay the count on Ash, but he pays no attention to it at all, and continues the choke.] CB: Somebody stop this! Ref, disqualify him! He's going to kill Jameson! SD: Now this is strategy. CB: What strategy? SD: [laughing] He's wearing Chris Jameson out by not allowing him to breathe. [Ash doesn't look as if he's going to stop the choke anytime soon, until finally the referee physically removes Ash from Jameson. Ash is irate, and raises his hand toward the ref, but stops himself before he hits him.] CB: Good thing Levi held that back, or he definitely would have been disqualified. [Jameson is lifeless on the mat. And Ash begins to point at him and yell out toward the fans once again. Levi then goes back to where he is comfortable, on the top rope.] CB: Now this is dangerous, we've seen some troubles from the top rope in this match. SD: I think he should go back to choking with the strap. [Ash doesn't even hesitate as he jumps from the corner, and splashes down with a 450 splash right onto Jameson!] CB: That could very well have killed Chris Jameson! SD: That's the idea. Cover him Levi! [But Levi doesn't go for the cover, instead, going back to a choke with the leather strap! Levi wraps the leather around Jameson's neck a couple of times before picking him up. Ash then grabs hold of Chris, runs, and throws him over the top rope!] CB: NO! MY GOD, CHRIS JAMESON IS LITERALLY HUNG OVER THE TOP ROPE BY THAT LEATHER STRAP!
