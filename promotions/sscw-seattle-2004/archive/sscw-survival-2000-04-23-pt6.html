SSCW Sunday Night SURVIVAL! (Part 6 of 7)

23 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene fades into the broadcast booth where Chad Brown and Stevie T. Dee are sitting. Chad Brown looks happily into the camera while Stevie T. Dee is seen talking to a young woman behind him. ] Chad Brown: We are just moments away from the big 12 man battle royale, but first a look into the day of the life of "The Two-Step Viper" T N T! [The scene opens up at the docking bay of a private airport in Chicago, Illinois. The focus of the picture is a private jet painted in Neon Green, Yellow and Black with "Near Death X-perience" written along the length of the plane. You see a fair amount of runway crewmembers doing the final checks on the plane for liftoff, but there's one problem: the passenger isn't there yet. He was supposed to be there about 15 minutes ago, and there's no sign of The Two-Step Viper anywhere. The cameraman, who was told to be there to document the trip with no further knowledge, is now getting worried and starts to walk to and fro. While he's walking, you see him focus in on the two sexy flight attendants, one brunette and one blonde, who look at their watches, waiting for T N T to show up. The two flight attendants then start to talk about general gossip-type stuff, unknowing of the cameraman's whereabouts. The cameraman, being the sly little devil that he is, decides T N T might get a kick out of a nice shot of some nice ass. So, he zooms in on the brunette's backside. The cameraman thinks "Wow, I love what the boss makes his employees wear to work!". The flight attendants' wardrobe on this flight consists of a navy blue mini-skirt, which is VERY high cut, and a tight white, silk blouse. The grounds crew is now starting to get mad, but the flight attendants continue chatting the day away. He continues to tape the footage of the finely toned ass, until he gets a tap on his back. The cameraman spins around as if he was caught looking at a porn magazine to see that a crewman with the neon orange pointer thingies and headphones on is standing behind him.)::: CREWMAN: :::(Yelling over the hum of the engine.)::: Excuse me, sir? We're gonna need to get you on board the plane at this time. CAMERAMAN: :::(Also yells to communicate with the grounds crewman.)::: Hey whoa, what about T N T? Any sign of him? CREWMAN: None whatsoever. Sir, if he doesn't show up soon, he may not be on board this flight. We need to get you on board! CAMERAMAN: Hey now, T N T instructed me to get on this flight so that I could document HIM, not the flight conditions. I can't get on board unless he's on with me, or I swear he'll fire me on the spot! :::(He thinks for a second, then realizes something.)::: Hey, wait a second! You work for him too! You all can't take off without him! How can you say something so stupid as to leaving him behind? CREWMAN: Listen, I don't work for him. I just keep the runways clear for the airport, and according to air traffic control, there's another flight coming in about 10 minutes on the runway you'll be taking off of. If you don't go soon, you won't be able to lift off! CAMERAMAN: Listen, you can't do this! He might as well of just booked a flight on Delta! You all better wait for him, or he'll fire you, and if he can't do that, he'll see to it that you are fired! You don't think he'll do it? Well, you'll be in for a suprise.......... CREWMAN: OK, OK, Listen! This plane won't leave without T N T being on it. I tell you what, why don't you make yourself comfortable in the plane. The sooner everyone's on board, the sooner the pilot can get ready for take off. CAMERAMAN: No way, I'm waiting right........ CREWMAN: Look, those flight attendants you were eyeing just a second ago are getting on board. Why don't you follow their example! CAMERAMAN: You raise a good point, my man. Thank you. :::(The cameraman then walks over to the stairway to the plane. As he makes his way up the staircase, he is greeted by the blonde flight attendant. He acknowledges it and proceeds to the main cabin of the jet. The interior of the jet is custom made. There are six to seven leather seats on one side with a leather couch on the other. In the very back of the plane is a fully equipped bar, which happens to be run by the brunette stewardess. All around the cabin are about 5 or 6 speakers for the surround sound system for the stereo. In other words, this plane is LOADED! The cameraman sits in one of the leather seats and makes himself comfortable. Then, the brunette flight attendant walks from behind the bar and to the cameraman's seat.)::: STEWARDESS: Hello there, my name's Staci and I'll be your flight attendant. Can I get you a drink while we wait for lift-off? CAMERAMAN: Well hello there, Staci. Yes, I could use a Martini.........shaken, not stirred! STACI: :::(giggles)::: Coming right up, Mr. Bond. :::(Laughs as she walks back to the bar.)::: :::(The cameraman then gives off a sigh as he scored some points with the flight attendant, he thinks. "Well, I think I'm gonna enjoy this flight! If only T N T would get here, we could go to wherever we're going." The cameraman thinks. Just then, you hear the screeching of tires from outside the plane. The sound's getting louder, and louder, and louder, until the driver slams on the breaks and the even louder sound of burning rubber comes to a halt. The cameraman then gets up from his seat and goes to the stairwell to see who it was. When he gets there, he sees a "The Two-Stepper" Dodge Viper, which is red with fire designs painted on the side just in front of the stairway with T N T behind the wheel. T N T jumps over the door of the driver's seat and heads to the stairway. The cameraman goes back into the cabin and takes his seat while waiting for T N T. T N T is wearing his trademark out-of-ring attire: Black NDX T-shirt, pair of Tommy Jeans, pair of DMX cross-trainers, and of course, the red Frogskins Oakley's. He then dashes up the stairs where he's greeted by the flight attendant. As he walks by the bar, he sees that the flight attendant is preparing the cameraman's drink.)::: T N T: Hey babe, I'll have a scotch on rocks. Been a hard day. STACI: Sure thing, T. T N T: I take it my cameraboy is here? STACI: He sure is, that cute little button-nose. T N T: Hey now, don't go trying to hit on my personal employees. He's got work to do on this flight. :::(His tone of voice changes to a more pleasant voice.)::: Then again, I don't know how on Earth he's gonna keep his mind on his work with you around at all. STACI: Awwwww, that's so sweet! Ya know, I think that when i went to get his drink, he was a little "naughty" with that camera of his! T N T: Are you suprised? It's not like he bats from the other side of the plate, or so i think........ Awh well, I'm gonna go take a seat. When is this bitch getting off the ground? STACI: We're basically ready for take-off right now, as far as I know. The grounds' crew's been ready for 15 minutes now. We just needed you to show up, that's all. T N T: Aw, how thoughtful of them, considering they're ON MY FUCKING PAYROLL! Well, let the pilot know we're all set. STACI: I'm sure he's doing that right now. T N T: Great. :::(He walks down the cabin of the plane and sits across from the cameraman, who's starting to look relaxed.)::: Well if it isn't the cameraboy! You been waitin' long, son? CAMERAMAN: Personally, I haven't been. I just got here ten minutes ago, but apparently the people down there have been worried about their whole runway schedule and some shit. T N T: Runway schedule? They're worried about other planes? Fuck 'em! For all I care, they can take this plane and park it sideways on their damn runway! Anyway, I'm in no mood to hear their whinin' about the Two-Step Viper holding them up. CAMERAMAN: Well, no need to hold it in any longer. What's on your mind? T N T: No no no, cameraboy, there's a place and a time for me to say what I gotta say, and this isn't that time or place. For now, I'm gonna enjoy this flight back to the promise land. When we get there, I'll meet up with Gaffney and we'll have ourselves a little chat, whatcha say? CAMERAMAN: That's fine, I mean, what am I gonna say to you? But there's one thing I want to ask you that's been running through my mind for a little bit. Where exactly are we going? T N T: C'mon, I thought you'd figure this one out by now! We're going back to the A.T.L. There I'm gonna meet up with Franchise. Man, I can't even remember the last time I saw that guy! It's been, what, over 9 months now? It's gonna be great to hook up with him again...... :::(Just then, Staci the Stewardess walks down the aisle with both of the men's drinks at hand.)::: Well look who finally graces us with her presence! Does it really take that long to make two drinks, Staci? STACI: Not usually, but this rebel over here likes his Martini's shaken, not stirred. It takes a little longer to make. :::(Hands the drinks to proper person.)::: Anyway, I wanted to make sure you all liked them. T N T: :::(staring at the cameraman.)::: Shaken, not stirred? Staci, if you'll excuse us for a moment. STACI: Sure thing, T. :::(Walks off to the front of the cabin.)::: T N T: Son, if you're trying to work your game on Staci over there, you're wasting your time! CAMERAMAN: Whatcha mean by wasting my time? T N T: Well, two reasons, Reason #1: No one, not even a pathetic lowlife needs to work to get Staci. She's easier than a bitch in heat! And Reason #2: She's already told me she caught you zooming in on her ass! You sly little devil, you! CAMERAMAN: Whoa wait, she knew that? But how? She was never facing me! T N T: Oh, the other stewardess, Amanda, saw you do the whole thing. You didn't see her laughing at you? :::(Cameraman doesn't respond.)::: Aw, don't be embarrassed. You'd just be doing something that I would do in that instance. Anyway, she doesn't hold it against you at all. If anything, she was flattered when she found out. CAMERAMAN: I can't believe she found out. My life is over. :::(Just then the plane starts to move out of the docking bay. It's approaching the runway and almost immediately goes to the air.)::: T N T: :::(Laughing)::: Well, I hope for her sake your life isn't over. Thank god this plane's moving. We're gonna be late as it is, and the boyz are gonna be picking us up. :::(Takes the first sip of his scotch.)::: Well, one thing's for sure, she sure can make a good drink. CAMERAMAN: That's for sure. Hey, anything you want me to do while we're in the air? T N T: Well, I'm probably just gonna take my drink to the back room and catch some Z's. I am really tired. CAMERAMAN: Is that part of the reason why you were so late? You slept in? Man, I thought you'd have a better........... T N T: Son, what have I told you time and time again, huh? Don't ever take me for a fool! I swear, after all the times through the years that you've questioned my intelligence, it's a wonder I haven't fired you! Or at least beaten the crap out of you! :::(Looks away from the camera and tries to regain his composure and cool down.)::: I'm sorry, I'm just really cranky right now. I'm sure you can understand that. Now, where was I? Oh yes, do I look like a person who would oversleep a flight? C'mon, you know me better than that! If you must know, the reason I was late today was because traffic was a bitch! And, unfortunately for me, running over vehicles with my hummer isn't considered to be very PR, so I was kinda in a deadlock, OK? CAMERAMAN: Hey man, it's cool with me. So, it's what, an hour flight? If you're gonna sleep, you'd better go now. T N T: What, you're trying to get rid of me now? What's the deal? Oh wait, never mind, I smell it. Ya know what, maybe YOU could use that back room more than I could, if ya know what I mean. CAMERAMAN: :::(Whispering)::: Hold on, now. Do you think she would? T N T: Hey kid, you forget who she works for. If I say I wanna steak, she asks "Medium or Rare?", if I wanna some coffee, she says "One cube or two?", and if I say I wanna fuck, she says "You want on top or bottom?" CAMERAMAN: Yeah, but she works for you, not me. T N T: It doesn't matter. If I say "Make this guy comfortable.", she says "How does he like it?" Man, it's no problem. Anyway, she's been eyeing you this whole flight! There, take a look for yourself. :::(The cameraman turns around to see Staci staring seductively at him.)::: I'm gonna go get some sleep. Feel free to turn that camera off if you want. :::(T N T gets up from his seat and walks toward the back of the cabin. There, Staci walks up to him.)::: STACI: Is there anything I can give........I mean get you? T N T: That's quite alright, Staci. I'm gonna go take a nap. STACI: OK, sir. Pleasant dreams. T N T: Oh, and Staci? Feel free to do whatever you want with him, if you know what I mean. STACI: My pleasure, sir. :::(T N T opens the door to the back room, walks in, and shuts it behind him. Staci then turns away from the door and looks right at the cameraman.)::: You wanna put that camera down? CAMERAMAN: Anything you say, ma'am. [The camera gets placed in the seat where T N T was sitting and cuts off. The scene fades back into the arena where all 12 competitors are standing in the ring awaiting the bell. ] Chad Brown: An intresting segment to say the least... Stevie T. Dee: I don't know about you, but T N T Airlines are the friendliest skies around. I know where I'm getting my Frequent Flyer Miles from now on! Not only that, but I just got this chick's phone number, check it out! Chad Brown: [Sarcasticly.] You're quite the studmuffin, STD. Stevie T. Dee: Hey, what did I tell you about that. I don't bat from both sides of the base, Chad, and for the last time ... NO! Chad Brown: [Sighs.] Anyhow, it's time for our _MAIN_EVENT_! Take it away, Arnie! Arnie Myers: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN ... It is now time for our much anticipated main event, the 12 man battle royale for the SSCW United States Championship! The twelve competitors in the ring are as follows: [pulls out a card.] "The Two-Step Viper" T N T, 'Simply Golden' Jeffrey Keenan, "Supernatural" Simon Spinx, "Courageous" Chris Jameson, "The Human Aphrodisiac" Bradley Rose, Richard Cyrus Marsh, Steven C. Bourdo, "Regular" Joe Davies,"The Hard Rock" Roland Stone, Victor Cortez, Joe "The Prophet" Forbes, and "Devastating" Damon Knight! Now... LET'S GET IT ON! [DING!!! DING!!! DING!!!] Stevie T. Dee: A nice Mills Lane impression by Arnie Myers. Chad Brown: We are underway in this all important bout! "Couragous" Chris Jameson attacks Joe Forbes, Jefferey Keenan and Roland Stone attack Spinx, The Two Step Viper lays the fists on Joe Davies! Richard Cyrus Marsh tackles Vicotr Cortez! STD: And look! Rose and Stephen C. Bourdo are waiting it out! CB: A wise move on the part of Rose, having already had a match earlier in the night. STD: Oh, not for long though! Rose gets SCB with an eye gouge! [The camera angle switches to show Roland Stone and Chris Jameson pounding Joe Forbes in the corner. Jameson backs off for a second, and grabs hold of Stone and delivers a lighting quick back suplex.] STD: Gotta love it from the young guy! [Jameson then goes back to work on Joe Forbes. He picks him up, gets him in a front chancery and lifts him up.] CB: BRAINBUSTER ON JOE FORBES BY CHRIS JAMESON! [A shot of T N T performing a Death Valley Driver on Jefferey Keenan. ] CB: T N T is looking great out there tonight. STD: He should be, with all the "traveling" he does. [The scene switches back to Joe Forbes who is just leaning on the ropes, trying to regain composure. ] CB: Joe Forbes has taken a beating here in the early goings. STD: Watch out! CB: Chris Jameson runs from behind, grabs Forbes and dumps him over the ropes! Forbes has been eliminated! 11 men left! Jameson turns around, right into a knee lift from Stephen C. Bourdo! Stephen Bourdo gets the attention of Bradley Rose, who was looking for someone to hurt. Bradley Rose and SCB start to boot the hell out of Jameson in the corner! Bradley Rose goes for the choke! And SCB continues to stomp the chest of Jameson. [Suddenly, "Devastating" Damon Knight is seen running from the opposite side of the ring. He dropkicks SCB sending him out of the ring. ] STD: There goes Bourdo falls through the second rope! [Damon Knight taps on the shoulder of Bradley Rose. Rose turns around!] CB: SUPERKICK BY DAMON KNIGHT! STD: I tell ya, that kick is one of the best in the land! CB: "The Human Aphrodisiac" falls to the mat! [The scene switches to Richard Cyrus Marsh and Victor Cortez. Marsh has Victor Cortez in a sleeperhold.] STD: A sleeperhold in a battle royale? CB: It may be pretty wise. Battle royales are one of the most grueling matches as it is. If you drain the energy early on thats one less guy you have to worry about! STD: He's really cinching it in. It's more of a choke! CB: LOW BLOW by one half of El Ultimo Dos! [Victor Cortez turns around and hits a martial arts kick to the leg of Marsh, and another. Cortez hits an uppercut and then jumps on the shoulders of RCM!] STD: A Hurrancanrana by the Spaniard! [Most of the crowd gets up an looks toward the entry way as Victor Cortez pulls RCM back to his feet. Three figures are seen jogging towards the ring. They are recognized from earlier as the team of Top Notch, Jarred and Chadd Mathews along with their manager Sarah. ] CB: What are these two doing out here! STD: First blood, baby! You gotta love it. CB: Well, Top Notch has garnered the attention of Victor Cortez. Cortez with some words directed toward Chadd. Jarred grabs the head of Cortez! Richard Cyrus Marsh is back up to his feet! He comes from behind and with the help of Top Notch they eliminate Victor Cortez! [Top Notch starts laughing, pointing, and yelling into the face of Victor Cortez. As they walk away, Sarah walks over the chest of Victor Cortez.] STD: A fiesty one! Ha! Look at that, Simon Spinx with Roland Stone in the corner! He's punching him in the corner with his crotch in his face! HA HA! Roland Stone: AGHHHH!!! [Roland Stone _slams_ Spinx down with a devastating spinebuster!] CB: Don't think for a second that Roland Stone forgot about earlier tonight! [Roland Stone gets back up and yells to the fans who reply in cheers.] STD: Watch it, Stone. In case you forgot about him, Omega is out there! CB: Stephen C. Bourdo with a high knee to the back of Stone! American hero my ass! STD: Please! Show some respect! Bourdo _bleeds_ red white and blue! CB: He's Canadian for pete's sake! STD: I'm gonna pretend you didn't say that ... CB: [Sighs] Anyhow, Chris Jameson and "Devastating" Damon Knight with a double suplex on Bradley Rose! [Damon Knight slowly sits back up and smiles toward the crowd. T N T runs off the opposite ropes and dropkicks Knight in the face!] STD: OH! Did you see that!? Wait here comes "Regular" Joe Davies and "Supernatural" Simon Spinx with a double clothesline on Jeffery Keenan! HE'S THROWN OVER THE ROPES! STD: WHAT?! CB: But he somehow manages to stay on the apron! STD: He hung on to the top rope! CB: Joe Davies and Simon Spinx are celebrating their "victory". Spinx hugs Joe Davies! Haha! It looks like Keenan is going to try the same thing with Spinx! CB: Not so fast. Here comes Omega running towards Keenan who is still on the apron! CB: Don't let his appearance and "flamboyancy" fool you, he is a _very_ capable wrestler! [The view switches to Bradley Rose and "The Two Step Viper" T N T trying to throw to Damon Knight over the top rope. They are pulling and struggling as hard as they can. These two are working it to perfection! [ Rose backs off for a second and T N T hits Knight with a diving reverse DDT! A very smart man, that Bradley Rose! CB: "The Human Aphrodisiac" picks up DDK, and throws him over the top rope! STD: Another one bites the dust! How many do we have left? CB: Eight men are left in the ring! Folks, we'll be right back! [The scene fades as Chris Jameson is seen dropkicking Stephen C. Bourdo.]
