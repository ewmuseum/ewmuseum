SSCW Wednesday Night Explosion! (Part 1 of 3)

19 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Original E-Mail : Hey everyone, sorry for the tardiness of this card. The person responsible for the first two matches of this card, unexpectedly never signed on Wednesday, and I never got his matches. I guess it's not so unexpected, because as a fedhead, you have to be prepared for this :-) It was a mad dash, but I managed to finish my main event, and find two people who could write these matches in the time that we had. We worked hard, and got it done. I hope this serves as a testament to the fact that I believe SSCW is a federation that I refuse to let go into endless garbage can of week-long and trash feds. I stayed up til 12:10, with a track meet tomorrow to make sure this got out before I went to sleep. I probably shouldn't have done that, but I told myself that this would be out _tonight_ :-) I thought I did alright with my match, having not written in about a year, it got easier as I went on. I wish to thank Matt1427, DWaugfh4dx for the help with the match writing on such short notice, and TJordan55 for the nice little commercials. I wish to thank everyone else for their in-ringers, RPs, and the positive feedback I've recieved. Thanks a lot you guys, and enjoy the show! (Be sure to read the main event!) Ratings. Nobody attracts crowds like I, and you will see soon enough that my wrestling prowess cannot be matched. Do you think that I give a damn about the United States Title? Hell no, I don't. I'm here for the big gold. I will represent this organization as your leader. Your hero. Your God. [Heel pop!] BR : And I'll show you why Saturday. As for tonight, you can go home now. There is no talent besides I, Bradley Rose, and _I_ am not going to stay in this stinking arena and congregate with those lower than me any longer. So, until Saturday... [Mark Bryant arrives outside the SSCW arena in his red jeep wrangler, as he comes to a stop in the parking lot a camera crew approaches him about an interview. He opens the doors and tell them to get out of his way. He walks around to the back of the jeep and gets his bags and slams the door. He walks past the reporter and into the back of the arena. As he walks through the door Richard Cyrus Marsh can be seen in the background checking him out, and as he walks past the two come face to face. Marsh and Bryant both nod their heads and Bryant continues on his way to the locker room. Once in the locker room Bryant has changed into his wrestling attire and is seated on a bench when reporter walks into the locker room and request a few words from Bryant.] Reporter: Mark I was wondering if we could get a few words from you before we go live on your match tonight and being a part of the birth of the SSCW? Bryant: You want a few words, I got a few words for you. How about getting the hell out of my face. You want a few more words, tell you what I'll do. [Bryant stands up and grabs the reporter around his neck and shoves him into the lockers in the room, he looks into the camera and tell the camera man to leave the camera on his way out. The camera man tells Bryant that he cannot leave the camera as the screen fades to black. A few seconds and some kicking and screams later the camera is set up on a chair facing a bench in front of the lockers. They are paying me a good chuck of money to come here and kick ass. You see that's what I do, I beat the hell out of people for money. Well that's a mighty fancy name you got there, to damn bad I'm gonna have to whip your ass. El Guapo I have been in the sport a long time, long enough to know that guys with fancy names don't last to long in a tough mans sport. From the sounds of your name, it doesn't sound like you are from around here so let me give you a little piece of advice, guys like you....pretty boys with fancy names have always fared better in the "light weight" divisions of wrestling. Boy you are not man enough to face me. I am gonna make short work of you, and when I am done I want you to know one thing. It's nothing personal, it's business. Remember I get paid to beat people up, you just happen to be the first person on that list. Another thing that I want to get off my chest, I want to know why in the hell I was flown all the way out here to Seattle to wrestle some pretty boy in an opening match. I am a former two time world champion, I have wrestled in more countries than EL Guapo has been alive in years, which my friend after tonight will be severely shortened. I'm gonna go out tonight and I am gonna beat this boys ass, not for the money but just because I am just in the fighting mood. I'm gonna go out there in a few minutes and show my skills, and I want everyone in the back to pay real close attention because my services are for hire. I get paid, to beat people up. SSCW you have put on notice that I am here, and I am not alone. [The scene fades back to the outside of the SSCW Arena. ] [All of a sudden, "Spiders" by System of the Down plays through the arena.
