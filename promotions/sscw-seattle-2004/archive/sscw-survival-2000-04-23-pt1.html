SSCW Sunday Night SURVIVAL! (Part 1 of 7)

23 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
["Come With Me" by Puff Daddy and Jimmy Page begins to blast through your speakers. We open to a shot soaring through the orange and purple clouds of a beautiful sunset. The shot begins to move from the clouds, and we're able to see down below. We see a desolate, war-ravaged field. The once bright green grass is now just a dead brown as of empty foxholes and abandoned war vehicles dot the landscape.] [ The figure of a man walking off into the orange horizon is seen. The camera begins to soar, getting closer and closer to this man. As the view gets nearer, we see this man as a US soldier. He is wearing a green and black pair of fatigues, a helmet, with a pack slung over his shoulder and a gun slung over the other. ] [The camera gets up of his face. The solider looks intensely off into the distance. A new scar adorns his cheek. He knows his purpose, he knows his duty.... He knows his destiny. ] #UH HUH, YEAH# [The scene explodes as the music picks up. A shot of a torn up, bloody, red white and silver American flag slowly flies across the screen. The words "STARS AND STRIPES CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING" fade on to the screen.] #Hear my cries,Hear my call# #Lend me your ears, See my fall# [ A clip of Ji Hang Lee moonsaulting off the limo on Wednesday Night Explosion is shown.] #See my error, Know my faults #Time halts, See my loss # [ A clip of Puah "The Hawaiian Love Machine" _goring_ Stephen C. Bourdo with a spear. ] #Know I'm lacking , Backtracking# #Where I met you# [ A shot of Omega, Simon Sphinx manager,_blasting_ "Hard Rock" Roland Stone in the face is seen.] #Pistol packing, Itchy finger# #Trigger happy, Try to trap me# [A clip of "The Human Aphrodisiac" Bradley Rose flexing his bronze pectorals with a smirk on his face runs across the screen. ] #Close your eyes# #COME WITH ME# [The flag reappears, slowly flying onto the screen. A few words form on to the screen.] _Sunday_ _Night_ __SURVIVAL!__ ["KABOOOOOOOM". Yet another explosion. The scene is now of inside the SSCW Arena where approximately 1,500 fans have packed themselves into this former pillow factory. Besides the ringside seats, there are large bleachers on three sides of the ring. Around the ring is guardrail and the announcing table. The ring, with it's dark blue mat and SSCW logo in the middle, has red, white and blue ropes. The apron is black and has the red, white and silver, SSCW flag/logo on it. From the ring is an entry way that leads to the small ramp and the curtains. Around the curtains are a modest set of lights, and above an average size video wall sits. ] [The camera pans across the rowdy crowd. A few signs are seen which read "Stephen C. Bourdo: A Real American", "I Bought "Why I'm Simply Golden", "SIGNS ARE FOR LOSERS", and "Vince Fears Avery". The scene now switches to the announcing booth where Chad Brown and Steve T. Dee are sitting. Chad Brown with a huge smile on his face, a black SSCW T-shirt and a pair of khakis sits along side of Stevie T. Dee. Stevie T. Dee is standing up looking into the crowd and getting chants of "STD! STD! STD!". STD is wearing a pair of faded jeans, a tie-died shirt which reads "STD: Sexually Transmitted Dee", a pirates hat, and a pair of golden oakleys. He sits down and smirks into the camera.] Chad Brown: [Trying to yell over the crowd.] WELCOME TO SEATTLE, WELCOME TO THE SSCW ARENA, WELCOME TO _SUN_DAY_NIGHT SURVIVAL!!! Stevie T. Dee: This is the show, right here! It doesn't get much better! Chad Brown: Ladies and gentlemen, we have quite the show lined-up. The first ever crowning of a champion in SSCW with the US Title Battle Royale, a steel cage match up between Roland Stone and the questionable Simon Sphinx- Stevie T. Dee: He's eccentric! And at least he admits what he wants in life, Chad. Chad Brown: ... Anyhow, not only that but we'll have the announcement of the World Title Brackets! Stevie T. Dee: Speakin' o' which, two, count-em _TWO_ World Title Tournament matches tonight. Chad Brown: And now, let's take it Lindsey Florentino! [Camera cuts to ringside where SSCW interviewer, Lindsey Florentino, stands in the center of the ring with microphone in hand. A low murmur of cheers escapes from the crowd gathered inside the SSCW Arena, mostly catcalls and whistles aimed at Florentino.] [FLORENTINO]: "Welcome, everybody, to the first ever Sunday Night SURVIVAL!" [The fans let loose a chorus of cheers as Florentino smiles.] [FLORENTINO]: "There is so much on tap this evening that one would think that things couldn't possibly get any more exciting in SSCW than they already are. But, I've just received word from President Avery and SSCW Commissioner Robinson that SSCW has just inked a deal with another top star in this sport and he's here tonight!" [The crowd lets loose with another cheer.] [FLORENTINO]: "So, without further adieu ... boys, HIT THE MUSIC!" #She said don't hand me no lines and keep your hands to yourself ...# [The familiar guitar licks of the Georgia Satellites' "Keep Your Hands to Yourself" resonate throughout the arena. The camera cuts to the aisle curtain which immediately parts ... to reveal no one ...] [FLORENTINO]: "Ladies and gentleman, I give you 'MR. MUSIC CITY' ... DENNY LANGHAM!" [A beat ... Then "Mr. Music City" Denny Langham, clad in a black t-shirt, faded dungarees, black cowboy boots and a bandana tied over his golden blond curly locks, steps into the arena, guitar strapped to his back. The Tennessee native starts a slow, deliberate strut before backing down the aisle, Taylor Series 615 Jumbo black guitar raised high into the air. A modest pop escapes from the fans as Langham enters the ring and takes his place next to Florentino. Music fades out.] [FLORENTINO]: "Welcome to Seattle, Denny Langham, and welcome to SSCW!" [The hometown crowd lets loose a resilient cheer.] [FLORENTINO]: "What brings you all the way from the South to the West Coast?" [LANGHAM]: "Let me tell ya, darlin'. It's simple mathematics. See, it ain't hard at all. Lance Avery got on the horn and threw a couple of equations my way and I liked the sound of it. He basically said to me, Denny Langham plus SSCW equals the best damn wrestling organization on the planet. He says to me, Denny Langham is equal to world champion. And more importantly, Avery was kind enough to quote me a price I definitely could not refuse." [FLORENTINO]: "Now everybody knows about your reputation in the South. But tell us, what makes you think you can compete here in SSCW with the likes of Bradley Rose ..." [The crowd reacts to the name of "The Human Aphrodisiac" ...] [FLORENTINO]: " ... 'Devastating' Damon Knight ..." [Another reaction from the crowd ...] [FLORENTINO]: " ... and the many other talented competitors on our roster?" [LANGHAM]: "Lindsey, Lindsey, Lindsey. [small laugh] I was told yer one smart cookie, so I'm gonna let the fact that yer askin' all the wrong questions slide by." [A curious look arises on Florentino's face.] [LANGHAM]: "Ya see, Lindsey, it ain't 'bout what makes me think I can compete with Rose, Knight or whoever the hell else ya got here. It ain't 'bout what I think I'm gonna do here in SSCW. What it IS 'bout is what makes Rose, Knight and the rest of the cannon fodder ya got assembled here THINK that they hang with 'Mr. Music City'." [The crowd cheers as Langham continues to boast ...] [LANGHAM]: "What it IS 'bout is what I WILL do here in SSCW. And in case yer wonderin' what that is, well, that's also easy to figure out. I will do the same thing that I've done from coast to coast, state to state, city to city, arena to arena and that is take every single man they throw into the squared circle with, twist 'em up so badly that when the doctor performs the autopsy, their own momma won't know who the hell it is they got there on the cold slab and walk away lookin' TOO DAMN GOOD." [The crowd lets loose another cheer as Langham raises his guitar into the air. Florentino moves to speak ... but Langham cuts her off.] [LANGHAM]: "And one more thing there, Lindsey. I been told that there's two belts 'round here in SSCW. Well, the way that I see it is this: Everybody knows that Denny Langham OWNS the South. Everybody WILL know that Denny Langham is the best in the West. And ya better believe that I'm the one ya don't wanna test in ALL the US, so I guess that just leaves the world for me to conquer. And the way that I see it, judgin' by what y'all got here in SSCW ..." [A cocky grin returns on Langham's face.] [LANGHAM]: "That ain't gon' be no trouble at all." [Langham's music cranks back in as he climbs to the second turnbuckle, raising his guitar high to a pop from the crowd ...] Chad Brown: Howabout that! Stevie T. Dee: I'm tellin' ya, these "SupaStars" are coming in by the truckload! Chad Brown: We'll be right back after this short break!
