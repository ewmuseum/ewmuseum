The community has been buzzing with the rumors and plans of the often times ecentric, local entrepenuer, and now owner of the Stars and Stripes Championship Wrestling, Lance Avery. Lance has made it very clear to the public, that SSCW will not only make money for Seattle, but " SSCW is here to give this place what it needs! SOME ENTERTAINMENT!" With the SSCW Arena being completed a month ago, Lance now brings his exciting mixture of local talent and (inter)national stars into the wrestling ring. According to many wrestling "insiders", this is quite the list of superstars and promises to be one of the more promising promotions to come along in a while. The SSCW Arena, a former glass factory, was not really "completed" but remodeled and redone in preperation for the hard hitting action on its way to the rainy city. The building seats approximately 1,500 wrestling fans and is actually a good facility according to the owner: "You know, most leaugues that just start out, they may be able to grab a building that can fit 600 or so on a good day and they're just _renting_ the place. Not here, SSCW Arena is the mecca of Stars and Stripes and this building is owned by yours truly." Lance Avery invites the Seattle community to attend the SSCW "Open House" Wednesday evening where SSCW stars will be on hand and even a few wrestling matches will take place. This will be held outside of the SSCW Arena as final preparations are made for Sunday Night's festivities. As the fans grow eager with every passing day, and the hype draws to close, Wednesday night, Stars and Stripes Championship Wrestling is here.
