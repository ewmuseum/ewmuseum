ACW American Championship~ Current Holder- Vacant This is the second biggest title in ACW, it is still just as important though. The wrestlers hometown has to be in America in order for them to be eligible for this title. It will be defended Once or twice a month also.
ACW Continental Championship~ Current Holder- Vacant Well unlike the American Championship, your hometown has to be out of the United States of America in order to be eligible for this title. Im sure there wont be a lot of Non-US roleplayers to start though. Or atleast their characters. This will start once I get like 4 or 6 non US roleplayers. ACW TV Championship~ Current Holder- Vacant This title will be defended three times a month usually. It ma be Four or it may be Two though it really depends. It is definally the weakest title and It is for Heavyweights AND Cruiserweights.
ACW Hardcore Championship~ Current Holder- Vacant In this division, your win/loss means squat and everyone could care less about your accomplishments in the fed.
