UWWC — Weekly Card

"UWWC Sunday Slaughter - 21 January 1996"

21 January 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
UWWC SUNDAY SLAUGHTER - 1/21/96 - Northlands Coloseum (19,166), Edmonton
STYLES: "Hello wrestling fans and welcome to another edition of UWWC
SUNDAY SLAUGHTER, the highest rated weekly two hour wrestling program on television. I'm your host Joey Styles. This week,
UWWC SUNDAY SLAUGHTER is coming to you LIVE from the Northlands
Coloseum here in beautiful Edmonton, Alberta, Canada. We have
five great matches for you this week. You'll see Skip vs. Bam Bam Bigelow, the Eliminators vs. the Smoking Gunns, Jushin Liger vs. Kurasawa, the Bad Breed vs. Yokozuna and Owen Hart, and, in the main event, Psicosis will defend his UWWC JR. Heavyweight Title vs. Dean Malenko. In addition to these great matches, we will also hear comments from "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair concering the Four Horsemen, Paul E. Dangersouly concering the
Dangerous Alliance, and, two new managers here in the UWWC,
Teddy Long and Sonny Onoo!"
LONG: "As much as America hates to admit it, we live in a world of hate, racism, and prejudice. Its all around us, from or jobs, to school, television, and even in professional sports. Racism is a problem is professional sports, especially in the world of professional wrestling. Black pro-wrestlers have always been looked over for title shots, always been forced to work low to mid card matches, always been forced to be jobbers. But this is no more. I came to the UWWC to make sure that this does not happen anymore. Just last night I signed four of the best black wrestlers every to take part in this sport, Booker T and Stevie
Ray, Harlem Heat, Too Cold Scorpio, and Mr. Hughes. My men and I will be know as THE MOD SQUAD. Next week I will announce the names of four other wrestlers who will join THE MOD SQUAD. Who will they be? You have to tune in next week to find out. SEE
YA, AND I WOULDN'T WANNA BE YA!" STYLES: "Very important comments from Teddy Long concering his reason for being here in the UWWC and his creation of an all black stable,
THE MOD SQUAD. We'll have comments from Sonny Onoo, Ric Flair and Paul E. later in the show. Now lets go down to the ring for our first match."
SKIP with SUNNY VS. BAM BAM BIGELOW The mactch started out with Bam Bam taking the early advantage. He used his superior power advantage to manhandle Skip. The tides changed after
Sunny triped Bam Bam, but Bam Bam recovered to defeat Skip.
TIME: 24 min. 19 se, THE ELIMINATORS VS. THE SMOKING GUNNS This was a non-title bout for the UWWC U.S. Tag Team Champions, the
Eliminators. They should be very thankful for that because the Smoking
Gunns kicked their *sses. Bart pinned Saturn to win the match.
STYLES: "Our next match will be Jushin Liger vs. Kurasawa, but before we go down to the ring, lets listen to comments from Sonny Onoo."
ONOO: "My name is Sonny Onoo and I am the greatest manager that the world has ever seen. I have come to the UWWC for three reason, to lead the top Japanese wrestlers in the UWWC, to win some gold, and to make a lot of money. There are many great Japanese wrestlers in the UWWC, but I can not manage them all. So I decided to pick the eight best and form THE YAKUZA. I first four members of THE
YAKUZA will be Shinya Hashimoto, Keiji Muto, Junji Hirata, and
Jushi Liger. Next week I will announce the other four members of
THE YAKUZA. SAYOUNARA!" JUSHIN LIGER VS. KURASAWA Liger has just been named as one of the charter members of THE YAKUZA, what an honor. Liger is taking on one of his fellow countrymen,
Kurasawa. Could Kurasawa be one of the other for members that Sonny Onoo was talking about? Who knows. But I do know is that being a part of THE
YAKUZA did not help Liger because he submitted to Kurasawa's arm bar.
TIME: 27 min. 24 sec. BAD BREED VS. YOKOZUNA AND OWEN HART A big non-title bout for the UWWC World Tag Team Champions, Yoko and
Hart, as they are taking on the Bad Breed. Yoko and Hart must have been thinking about their big title match aganist the Steiner Brothers because they lost to the Bad Breed by DQ when Owen Hart hit Ian with a steel chair.
TIME: 6 min. 12 sec. STYLES: "Before we go to our main event, we have some comments from the one and only Paul E. Dangersouly."
PAUL E: "Last week, I told you that I was bringing the DANGEROUS ALLIANCE to the UWWC and I did. I told you that the first twomembers would be Taz and 911. This week I have decided taht the other three members will be Sabu, Dean Malenko and Chris Benoit. What do you think about that? The DANGERSOUS ALLIANCE has only one goal in mind and that is to take over the UWWC!
PSICOSIS VS. DEAN MALENKO On paper this would be a great match and the wrestlers did not dissapoint. Both men put on a tremendous display of scientific skill.
Psicosis did his usual luchia moves and Malenko was Malenko. But in the end, Psicosis came out on top and retained his UWWC Jr. Heavyweight
Title.
TIME: 8 min. 48 sec. STYLES: "I know that I promised and interview with Ric Flair, but we're out of time. If you want to see the interview, please tune in to UWWC RING REPORT later in the week. I'm Joey Styles, see you next week."
