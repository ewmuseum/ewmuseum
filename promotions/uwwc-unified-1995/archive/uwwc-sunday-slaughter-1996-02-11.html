"UWWC Sunday Slaughter - 11 February 1996"

11 February 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
UWWC SUNDAY SLAUGHTER - 2/11/96 - Maple Leaf Gardens - Toronto, Canada STYLES: "Hello wrestling fans and welcome to UWWC SUNDAY SLAUGHTER, wrestling hottest weekly two hour program. I'm you host, Joey Styles. We are coming to you
LIVE from Maple Leaf Gardens in beautiful Toronto, Canada. We have a tremendous program for you tonight. Togight we have: Public Enemy vs. Malenko/Benoit, Masa
Saito vs. Bret Hart, the Steiner Brothers vs. Gary Albright and Stan Hansen,
Toshiaki Kawada vs. Johnny Ace, the Eliminators vs. the Bodydonnas for the Eliminators' U.S. Tag Team titles, Osamu Nishimura will defend his U.S. Title vs. Hiromichi Fuyuki and in the main event, the Smoking Gunns will take on Eagle/Patriot. The winner of the last match will be declaied the NEW UWWC World Tag
Team Champions. So without further ado, lets go down to ringside for our first match."
PUBLIC ENEMY VS. DEAN MALENKO and CHRIS BENOIT w/PAUL E. DANGEROUSLY These two teams have been envolved in a feud with each other since Febuary of last year and two this day it still rages on. But this time, Malenko and Benoit win the match when Malenko pinned Rocco Rock.
TIME: 9 min. 34 sec. MASA SAITO VS. BRET HART Saito may be a bit past his prime, but he still managed to give Hart a good match. But in the end, Hart came out on top. TIME: 17 min. 58 sec. THE STEINER BROTHERS VS. GARY ALBRIGHT and STAN HANSEN This was a rematch from their 30 min. plus match from the week before in which
Albright and Hansen came out on top. But this week was a different story. The
Steiner Bro. gained some measure of revenge when Scott pinned Albright.
TIME: 11 min. TOSHIAKI KAWADA VS. JOHNNY ACE Pound for pound, Kawada is one of the best wrestlers in the sport. He proved this point when he defeated Ace.
TIME: 10 min. 16 sec. THE ELIMINATORS (U.S. Tag Team Champions) vs. THE BODYDONNAS Skip pinned Saturn to win the match and the titles. TIME: 19 min. 22 sec. (Bodydonnas are the new UWWC U.S. Tag Team Champions) OSAMU NISHIMURA (U.S. Champion) vs. HIROMICHI FUYUKI Nishimura pins Fuyuki to retain the UWWC U.S. Title. TIME: 8 min. 36 sec. SMOKING GUNNS VS. EAGLE/PATRIOT Billy Gunn pinned Eagle to win the match and become the NEW UWWC World Tag Team
Champions.
TIME: 5 min. 38 sec.
