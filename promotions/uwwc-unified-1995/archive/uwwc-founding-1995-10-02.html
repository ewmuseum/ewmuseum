"Unified World Wrestling Council (UWWC)"

2 October 1995
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A couple of months ago, I created the Unified World Wrestling Council (UWWC). This e-league is not open to the public, it is just for my "what if..." matches. The UWWC runs daily house shows around the world. Once a week, on Sundays, the UWWC does a televised card UWWC SUNDAY SLAUGHTER. On these cards there are 11 matches, 5 single, 5 tag team and one title match. Each week, one of the 4 UWWC champions defends his title aganist a random challenger. Every two months the UWWC has a PPV card in which all of the titles must be defened aganist the #1 contender for each title. The PPV also includes a match featuring the #2 vs. #3 contenders for the tag team title and the two highest ranked singles wrestlers who didn't get a title shot. In addition there are six other matches, 3 tag team and 3 singles. That's a total of 12 big matches. There are for titles in the UWWC, UWWC World Heavyweight Title, UWWC World Tag Team Titles, UWWC Jr. Heavyweight Title and the UWWC TV Title. On July 16, 1995, the UWWC had four tournanments to determine the first champions. The first UWWC World Champion was Taz, he defeated the Sandman in the tournanment final. Since then, Shawn Micheals, El Samurai,Barry Horowitz, and Antonio Inoki have all be champion. The current champion is Kurasawa. The UWWC Tag Team Champions are as follows, Nasty Boys, Scott Norton and Mike Enos, the Steiner Brothers, Public Enemy and the current champions the Blue Bloods. The UWWC Jr. Champions are as follows, Masanobu Fuchi, El Samurai, Sabu, 2 Cold Scorpio and the current champion Steve Richards. And finally the UWWC TV Champions, Sampson Fuyuki, Tommy Dreamer, Tatsumi Fujinami, Hiroshi Hase and the current champion Hulk Hogan. On September 23, 1995 the UWWC had thier second PPV AUTUM ARMAGADDON. AA took place at the RCA Dome in Indianpolis, IN in front of 69,567. Here are the results of the PPV.
