"UWWC Ring Report - 16 February 1996"

16 February 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
STYLES: "Hello wrestling fans and welcome to another edition of UWWC RING REPORT,
I'm your host Joey Styles. I have some very BIG news for you this week concering the UWWC World Tag Team Titles, UWWC SUNDAY SLAUGHTER, and UWWC SUPER SPRING
SPECIALITY MATCH SPECTICULAR. Let's start by talikg about the UWWC World Tag Team
Titles. As you know Yokozuna and Owen Hart split as a tag team and as a result, the tag team titles were vacated and the new champions would be determined by a tag team tournament. Last Sunday, Febuary 11, the Smoking Gunns defeated Egale/Patriot to become the NEW UWWC World Tag Team Champions. You may also know that the week before that the WWF President, Roddy Piper, stripped the Smoking Gunns of the WWF tag team titles because Bart Gunn needed neck surgery. After this announcement was made, UWWC President, Rodney F. Graves, tried to presuade the Gunns to drop out of the UWWC World Tag Team Title tournament but they refused. The Smoking Gunns went to the defeat Eagle/Patriot in the tournament finals last Sunday and as a result became the NEW UWWC World Tag Team Champions. After the match, a very important discovery was made. It was discovered that Bart Gunn had reinjured his neck during the macth. The next night, the Smoking Gunns took on the Gangstas and Bart's neck took a pounding. After the match, one of UWWC's doctors declaired Bart unfit to wrestle and as a result, the Smoking Gunns were stripped of the UWWC World Tag Team
Titles. So for the second time in three weeks, President Graves has declaied that the UWWC World Tag Team Titles will be put up for grabs in a week long tournament.
The tournament will take place during our house show cards next week. Here is next week's break down."
2/19/96 Steiner Brothers vs. Lost Boys Nasty Boys vs. Akiyama/Omori Faces of Fear vs. Men on a Mission Kobashi/Misawa vs. Pitbulls 2/20/96 Buck/Slater vs. Gangstas Sting/Luger vs. Kawada/Taue Bodydonnas vs. Eagle/Patriot Eliminators vs. Hashimoto/Hirata 2/21/96 Quarterfinals 2/22/96 Semifinals 2/25/96 Finals "Secondly, I need to talk about this Sunday's UWWC SUNDAY SLAUGHTER. SS will be coming to you LIVE from the Paladium in Ottawa, Canada. This Sunday you will see:
Eagle/Patriot vs. 2 Cold Scorpio/Sandman w/Teddy Long, Juventud Guerrero vs. Pitbull
2, the Eliminators vs. Kawada/Taue, Steve Austin w/Bobby "The Brain" vs. Antonio
Inoki, and, in the main event, Psicosis will defend the UWWC JR. Title vs. Ultimo
Dragon. You will see all that and much more tomorrow night. Finally, some matches have already been signed for SSSMS. They are: Sting will defend his UWWC TV Title vs. #1 Contender, Osamu Nishimura will defend the UWWC U.S. Title vs. the #1
Contender, and the Gangstas vs. Nasty Boys in a New York Street Fight, NO DQ, NO
TIME LIMIT, PINFALLS COUNT ANYWHERE. In the coming weeks, for matches will be announced. Well folks, that all the time that we have for this week but please tune in tomorrow to UWWC SUNDAY SLAUGHTER, TV's most exciting weekly two hour wrestling program."
