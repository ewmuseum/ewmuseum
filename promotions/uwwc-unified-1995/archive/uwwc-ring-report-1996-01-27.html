"UWWC Ring Report - 27 January 1996"

27 January 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
STYLES: "Hello wrestling fans and welcome to UWWC RING REPORT, I'm your host Joey Styles. This week's RING REPORT is comming to you
LIVE from Calgary, Alberta, Canada, the site of tomorrow's UWWC
SUNDAY SLAUGHTER. We have a gigantic card instore for you
tomorrow night. You will see Toshiaki Kawada and Akira Taue vs. Men on a Mission, Eddy Guerrero vs. Koji Kanemoto, the Road Warriors vs. the Eliminators, Sting vs. Brian Christopher, Yokozuna and Owen Hart will defend the UWWC World Tag Team Titles vs. the Steiner Brothers, and Steve Richards will defend the UWWC World Heavyweight Title vs. Abdullah the Butcher. Also on tomorrow's card, we will be hearing comments from Sonny Onoo and Teddy Long concering membership in both of their stables,
THE YAKUZA and THE MOD SQUAD. At the conclusion of last week's
UWWC SUNDAY SLAUGHTER, we were supposed to hear comments from
"The Nature Boy" Ric Flair concering the FOUR HORSEMEN, but we ran out of time. So, today we are going to hear those pre-recorded comments from "The Nature Boy"...
FLAIR: "When you think of greatness, you think of the FOUR HORSEMEN. When you think of titles, you think of the FOUR HORSEMEN. When you think of total dominance, you think of the FOUR HORSEMEN.
When you think of the best thing going today, you think of the
FOUR HORSEMEN. The FOUR HORSEMEN ruled the world of pro-wrestling in the 80's and now we are back in the 90's to do the same thing. Arn Anderson and myself are the only two original FOUR HORSEMEN that are left, but that's OK because we have two replacements that will make the FOUR HORSEMEN even bigger and better than ever. So without further ado, the two newest members of the FOUR HORSEMEN are "Stunning" Steve Austin and "The Franchise" Shane Douglas. That's right, Steve Austin and Shane Douglas are the newest HORSEMEN. And since we are the
GREATEST group of HORSEMEN ever put together, we had the greatest manager that pro-wrestling has ever seen, Bobby "The Brain"
Heenen. What do you have to say about that pally! The FOUR
HORSEMEN ride once again, and we're coming to get that belts.
Whoooooooooo....." STYLES: "That was an amazing announcement from Ric Flair concering the
FOUR HORSEMEN. On last week's SUNDAY SLAUGHTER, we were also told that Paul E. Dangersouly has added Dean Malenko, Chris
Benoit, and Sabu, along with Taz and 911, to the Dangerous
Alliance. Don't forget, tomorrow Sonny Onoo and Teddy Long will also have some major announcements conering their stables. Two more things before I go, one is concering the UWWC Tag Team
Champions, Yokozuna and Owen Hart and the other is concering the next UWWC PPV, SUPER SPRING SPECIALITY MATCH SPECTICULAR. In recent weeks there has been a lot of friction between these two men. Some of it is expected, but it seems to be just a bit too much sometimes. I'm not saying that they are on the verge of breaking up or anything, but I'm saying thier is some friction between the two. Anyway, SSSMS will take place on Sunday, March
3, 1996 and it will be coming to you live from Olympic Stadium in Montreal, Canada. All six titles will be up for grabs, ten matches in all. Each match will be a speciality match of some kind. This will make for a very interesting card. I'll have more about this event next week. I'm sorry but we're out of time, but please tune in tomorrow to see UWWC SUNDAY SLAUGHTER!
