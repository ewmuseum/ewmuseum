UWWC — Pay-Per-View

"UWWC Autumn Armageddon"

3-5 October 1995
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello wrestling fans and welcome to the UWWC's second PPV AUTUM ARMAGADDON. I am you host "The Hot Rod" Rodney Graves. Tonight we are coming to you live from the RCA Dome in Indianpolis, IN. 69,567 wrestling fans are jammed packed inside the RCA Dome to witness the biggest PPV of the year. We have 12 big matches lined up for you tonight,four of those matches are title matches. So without further ado, lets go down to the ring for our first match.

RENAGADE VS. KOJI KANEMOTO This without a doubt had to be the worst match of the night. Renagade was the usual Renagade. The match only lasted 5 min. because Kanemoto was DQ because he threw Renagade over the top rope. Renagade was so bad that after the match the fed fired him.

RAZOR RAMON VS. GENICHIRO TENYRU This was one of those matches that actually looked better in the ring than it did on paper. Both wrestlers gave it their all and pulled out all of the stops. Tenryu was busted open at the 30 min. mark when Ramon hit him with a steel chair. Even though he was a bloody mess, Tenryu continued. The match ended when Tenyru rolled up Ramon with a small package after Ramon ran into the turnbuckle.

Match time: 40 min. 37 sec. NORIO HONAGA VS. TAKAYUKI IIZUKA This was the best "wrestling" match of the entire night. Both men showed the American wrestling fans why the best wrestling action is in Japan. Both wrestlers took turns countering each other holds. One min. Honaga would have the advantage and the next it would belong to Iizuka. Both men even took the action to the air, and the fans loved it. Iizuka and Honaga took turn preforming extremely high risk leaps from the ring to the floor. Iizuka hit Honaga with a moosault through a table and Honaga hit Iizuka with a Flying Space Tiger Drop. The fans loved it. The end of the came when Iizuka hit Honaga with a German Suplex to get the pin. After the match, both men bowed to geach other and the crowed gave them the biggest pop of the night.

Match Time: 47 min. 36 sec. TOSHIAKI KAWADA and AKIRA TAEUE VS. HARLEM HEAT This match featured a contrast of styles, the scientific skills of Kawada and Taeue vs. the power of HH. The match was fought both inside and outside of the ring. Kawada hit Booker T with the Tiger Bomb to get the 1..2..3.

Match Time: 10 min. 38 sec. POWER WARRIORS(HAWK and KENSUKE SASAKI) VS. DEAN MALENKO and CHRIS BENOIT This was a very one sided match. Malenko and Benoit kept Sasaki in the ring the entire time. Each time he would try and tag Hawk they would cut him off. Sasaki took one hell of a beating before Malenko pinned him with a Tiger Bomb.

Match Time: 7 min. 35 sec. TOMMY DREAMER VS. RIKI CHOSHU This was one of those matches that looked better on papaer than it did in the ring. Dreamer looked out of it during the entire match. No one is sure if he was under the weather, if he was injured, or if he was thinking about Catus Jack. The only thing that is known is that he didn't wrestle worth shit. Choshu easily pinned Dreamer with a Power Slam.

Match Time: 4 min. 12 sec. THE STEINER BROTHERS (RICK and SCOTT) VS. JUN AKIYAMA and TAKAO OMORI The Steiner Brothers are often considered to be the best tag team in pro-wrestling today, but on this night, Akiyama and Omori didn't aggree. This was a great match between two great tag teams even though it didn't last very long. Akiyama reversed Scott's Belly-to-Belly Suplex to get the pin.

Match Time: 7 min. 20 sec. HIRO HASE (UWWC TV CHAMPION) VS. HULK HOGAN Hase started off good, but this ended up being just another typical Hogan match. When it looked like Hase had Hogan beat, Hogan did that damn "Hulk-up" thing, gave Hase the boot to the face and then that damn Legdrop.

Match Time: 12 min. 6 sec. (Hogan becomes the new UWWC TV CHAMPION) TO BE CONTINUED... STEVE RICHARDS (UWWC JR. HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION) VS. 2 COLD SCORPIO Besides the Honaga/Iizuka match, this was the best match of the entire night. Scorpio was looking to become the first repeat champion in the UWWC, but Richards wasn't ready to give up the belt. Scorpio was Scorpio, dazzling the fans with a variety of high risk aerial moves. Richards, on the other hand, looked very good for a change. He matched Scorpio hold for hold. Both men took it to the EXTREME! Richards was busted open at the 30 min. mark when he went for the Flying Space Tiger Drop. He missed Scorpio and ended up hitting his head on the steel guard rail. Scorpio had plenty of chances to win the match, but Raven would always interfer. The match ended when Buhella distracted the ref long enough for Raven to hit Scorpio with a steel chair and then DDT him on the same chair. Raven then placed Richards' body on tho of Scorpio's to get the pin.

Match Time: 37 min. 3 sec. (Richards retains the UWWC JR. TITLE) PUBLIC ENEMY (UWWC WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS) VS. THE BLUE BLOODS PE had just won the tag team titles only days before the PPV when they defeated the Steiner Brothers at a house show at the Spectrum. PE's four day title reign would be the shortest in the UWWC history. Bobby Eaton ened the match when he hit Johnny Grunge with the Tower of London Leg Drop of the top rope. BTW, Grunge was busted open during this match.

Match Time: 19 min. 8 sec. (The BB's are the new UWWC TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS) KURASAWA (UWWC WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION) VS. BRIAN PILLMAN This was a very one-sided match. Kurasawa went on the attack before the bell sounded. Kurasawa worked on Pillman's arm the entire match before he applied the Japanese Armbar to get the submission.

Match Time: 6 min. 32 sec. (KURASAWA retains the UWWC WORLD TITLE) For the next two and a half months, the UWWC will be on a tour of Japan. The next UWWC PPV will be TURMOIL IN TOKYO and it will be coming to you live from the Tokyo Dome in Tokyo, Japan on Thanksgiving Day. That's all for tonight, but please tune in every Sunday night to ESPN to see all of the top UWWC stars on SUNDAY NIGHT SLAUGHTER. Good night everyone, I'll see you in Tokyo. I hope you liked this card. Please send me some feed back.
Rodney F. Graves Handler of the WIWF World Heavyweight Champion, AKIRA HASHIMOTO. THE JUICE IS LOOSE!!!
