[SCRA] The Scraps, 15 December 1997, Part 2

15 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 2 ) | | \ | | Doug "Grizzly" Adams vs. Phi Theta Kappa / |___ | | | | Writer: JG (_________________________________________________________________________ [Ed Jaspard comes into the ring carrying a bouquet of flowers and bright makeup on. He gets into the ring and wipes a tear from his eye.] EJ - "[sniff]This is the happiest day of my life. This matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 40 minute time limit. Coming down the aisle first from Big Bear, California and weighing in at 277lbs., he is Doug "Grizzly" Adams!!" [The roar of a grizzly blares from the PA, followed by the sound of a heartbeat coinciding with the beat of native American drums. Grizzly gallops to the ring, much like a bear runs, stopping to wait for Nemesis along the way he lets the young fans hug and pet him, if their parents will allow it. Once he reaches the ring, he jumps in and roars at the crowd. He then places his necklace on the back of the ring post. He gallops along the ring apron as Nemisis calls him.] EJ - "I love you all so much. Well, his opponents weigh in at a combined weight of 482. They are Phi Theta Kappa." [We will rock you by Queen is played over the PA. Allen and Brad come out in thier Toga's to the chant of "TOGA!, TOGA!" from the fans. They stroll down to the ring and each find a cute young lady to kiss and have take their togas off to keep. They then enter the ring and prepare to wrestle.] DING! DING! DING! [Jenkins starts out in the ring with Adams, but was obviously overpowered by the "grizzlybear". Adams pushes Jenkins into the turnbuckle and gallops around the ring a bit.] JH - "Look at this goof." JG - "He's showing his happiness." JH - "By galloping? I don't know about you, but when I'm feeling down, I log on to the internet and when I log off, enjoy a Marlboro." JG - "I don't know if I can continue working with you knowing what you did." JH - "Did? I'm planning on doing it again tonight." [Grey moves his chair away.] JG - "Maybe I should say to out Mexican broadcasters....Ola!" JH - "What a goof, he doesn't even know how to speak Spanish." [Jenkins hops up top and hits a double axe handle ontop of Adams' back. Jenkins gives a knee lift to the bent over Adams. Adams falls to the ground as Jenkins tags out to Thompson. Jenkins holds onto Grizzly's arm as Thompson hits a legdrop ontop of it. Thompson goes back up top and hits a butt-drop ontop of Grizzly's chest. Thompson begins punching Grizzly on his nose. Thompson tags back out to Jenkins and goes to pick up Grizzly, but Adams sits up and gets his hand around his neck. Jenkins goes over to help, but Adams grabs his neck too. Adams picks them both up and hits a double chokeslam.] JG - "What a great display of power by the 250 plus pound Doug Adams." JH - "What's up with Juan Sandwich and Julio Kabasa or whatever?" JG - "Juan Sanchez and Julio Kobissino?" JH - "Are those you're little pet names for one another?" JG - "No, those are their real names. Did you know both of them are really from Australia?" JH - "How come they speak fluent spanish?" JG - "It's amazing what eating too much spinich can do." JH - "What does that have to do with anything?" JG - "Nothing, get my joke?" JH - "Very sad. Very, very bad joke." [Adams picks up Thompson and throws him over the ropes. Jenkins gets up slowly only to be met by a DDT. Thompson gets up and gets back into the ring. He sneak attacks Adams, hitting him with a chair, while the ref is attending Jenkins. Adams feels nothing and stays standing up. He turns around and gives him an atomic drop, followed by a vicious clothesline. He goes over and gets Jenkins, picking him up and hitting a powerslam. Adams goes over to Nemisis and begins bragging to him. Jenkins takes out Adams' knee and gives him a spinning toe hold.] JG - "Very affective. Taking out the central balance is smart, when you're fighting a big man." JH - "Yea, but Adams is a smart guy himself. I mean, he dressed himself this morning." JG - "Doesn't everyone dress themselves each morning?" JH - "I mean I want to, but I just don't have the energy each morning. I I mean I get Electra to put on the shirt, Black Kat does the underwear, quite well, might I add. And Miss Frank does the pants. Oh yea, and Nemisis does the shoes. I mean with a life like that, why would you want to put the effort in each morning." JG - "Jack, why do you even get up each morning?" JH - "I have to make money don't I?" JG - "If you sleep all day, all the money you need is for rent. You can go on unemployment and make the money that way." JH - "That was beautiful. Gimme a kiss honey." JG - "I'd prefer not to." [Nemisis jumps onto the mat and distracts the ref. But as he's doing so, Thompson jumps to the top turnbuckle and gives him an elbowdrop. Adams eventually grabs the ropes and forces Jenkins to break the hold. Jenkins picks the big man up and throws him into the ropes. He hits a big back body drop and goes for the cover. 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Phi Theta Kappa almost got the victory there. They almost avenged their loss against Adams last week." JH - "Don't count Adams out though, I mean the last time you counted him out of it, he hit a double chokeslam. Now that's relativety." JG - "What's relativity have to do with this?" JH - "Oh sorry, flash back to grade eight. I'm tellin' ya Grey, I'm losing it. And you're driving me over the edge." [Jenkins goes to pick up Adams, but he gets him in a Tongan Death Grip. Jenkins swipes Adams' hand aside and stomps on him. Jenkins goes over and tags out to Thompson. The two pick him up and throw him into the ropes. Adams runs through a double clothesline and spins them around and hits a double DDT. Adams picks up Thompson above his head and waits for Jenkins to get up. Adams throws Thompson ontop of Jenkins causing them both to fall to the ground. Jenkins slides to the outside and Adams picks up Thompson. Adams throws Thompson into the turnbuckle and places him up top. He hits a superplex and goes for the cover. 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Amazing strength by Doug Adams." JH - "Yea, but can he pat his head and rub his tummy at the same time?" JG - "I don't kn..." JH - "I guess so, or how else would he be able to use shampoo and soap at the same....wait, have you seen him take a shower?" [Admas pops back up and hits a bodyslam. Adams picks Jenkins up and puts him in the bear buster. Jenkins holds on until Thompson runs in and dropkicks Adams in the back, forcing him to release the hold.] JG - "Adams again almost had the victory." JH - "Does he shower after matches?" JG - "I don't..." JH - "Before dinner? When he wakes up? On a special occasion? Does he shower bi-weekly? Bi-monthly? Bi-annually?" JG - "Let's get back to the action." [Jenkins is out cold. Adams gets up first and throws Jenkins into the ropes. Adams telegraphs a back body drop and Jenkins hits a sunset flip. 1! KICKOUT! Jenkins springs up and hits a spinning heel kick as Adams gets to his feet. Jenkins tags out to Thompson as the hit double elbows off the top. They both jumps on Adams for the pin. 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Did you see the strength of Adams, throwing both men over top of him is quite a feat." JH - "Feet, does he wash his feet?" [Jenkins exits the ring as Thompson hits a big splash off the ropes. He picks Adams up and hits a standing dropkick. Adams stays on his feet as Thompson goes off the ropes and hits a cross body block sending Adams down. Thompson springs up and picks up Adams. He attempts another standing dropkick but Adams grabs his feet. Adams then puts him in a weak Boston Crab which Thompson easily gets out of. Adams grabs Thompson and throws him into the ropes. Adams goes for a big boot, but Thompson ducks it. Thompson hits a crucifix which sends Adams to the mat.] JG - "Wow, you know what they say, the bigger they are, the harder they fall." JH - "Yea, and the smaller they are the less it hurts. Wow, Lifetaker has an easy life." [Jenkins gets tagged in by Thompson as they attempt Hangover. Adams blocks it, but doesn't see Jenkins on the top. Adams lets out a mighty roar and as he turns around, Jenkins hits a flying crossbody, sending him to the mat. Jenkins goes for the cover 1! 2! 3!!!!!] JG - "Phi Theta Kappa finally get to raise their arms in victory against Adams." JH - "Arms, does he wash his arms?" JG - "I don't know." [Nemesis grabs Grizzly by the ear, smacking him around on the way back to the ring. Phi Theta Kappa looks on in disgust and soon follows.] JG - "Nemesis sure has a stranglehold over Grizzly." JH - "Because, without Nemesis, Grizz would be a nobody." JG - "I don't know about that. Pure power goes a long way in this sport." JH - "But, nowhere when you can't back it up with mic skills." JG - "That's definitely debatable. Well, we have the debut of J.W. Hardin coming up next. I wonder if Ken Curtis is watching." JH - "He's always watching...." JG - "I thought we left that crap back in the old league." JH - "Just had to get it out of my system." JG - "Let's go to Ed Jaspard for the intros." ___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 3 ) | | \ | | "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin vs. Sgt. Pepper / |___ | | | | Writer: TD (_________________________________________________________________________ [The ring is empty.] JH - "Where's Jaspie?" [Suddenly, a huge rev of a motorcycle comes over the speakers as Jaspard pulls into the arena. He clumsily bikes down the aisle, knocking into the guard rail on the way down. Jaspard abandons the bike and runs to the ring, escaping the clutch of many angered fans. This is not the end of Ed's troubles, though. He attempts to climb into the ring, but his leather biker pants are too tight. Jaspard eventually has to be rolled underneath the ropes by a group of security at ringside.] EJ - "These things are tight! I guess we'll be getting to the intros now. Coming down the aisle first, Sgt. Pepper!" [The lonely hearts band comes out and starts to play a song from the Beatles as Sgt. Pepper and Mal Epstein and Penelope Lane walk out into the stadium. Sgt. Pepper starts to make his way down to the ring with Penelope Lane and they each start to slap the fans hands. Sgt. Pepper is halfway to the ring when a fan grabs him and pulls him into the crowd. It's "Outlaw" J. W. Hardin.] JG - "What's Hardin doing in the crowd!" [Jaspard falls in the middle of the ring, unable to get up.] JG - "Ed's stuck!" JH - "What a moron. I've said it once, I'll say it again: where the hell do we get these guys?" [Hardin, seeing Jaspard stranded in the ring, keeps Pepper on the outside for a bit longer. Hardin body slams Pepper onto the concrete, then picks him up and throws him back into the walk way. Hardin bulldogs Pepper, then picks Pepper back up and sends him into the gaurdrail. The paramedics run into the ring and rescue Ed.] JH - "This could be on Rescue 911! Get the Jaws of Life out here!" [Hardin throws Pepper into the ring and follows after him.] DING! DING! DING! [Hardin starts to rapidly punch Pepper everywhere from the gut to the face, with the ref constantly stopping Hardin for closed fist punches. Hardin tosses Pepper into the ropes and gives him a boot to the face, knocking Pepper down to the ground. Hardin kicks pepper in the gut, then starts to choke Pepper with his foot.] JG - "Brutal tactics by J.W. Hardin." JH - "Hey, that's why they call him the meanest hombre on earth, Grey." [The ref breaks it after a four count. Hardin hits an elbow drop, then goes for a quick pin... 1! 2! KICKOUT! Hardin picks Pepper up by his hair, and hits a piledriver.] JH - "Yank it all out! You don't need hair, right Grey?" JG - "Very funny, Jack. If I'm not mistaken, you seem to be greying a little bit, too." JH - "That's not grey hair, that's highlights." [Hardin bounces off of the ropes and goes for a leg drop, but Pepper moved out of the way. Hardin is the first man up, but Pepper brings him back down with a leg sweep. Pepper gets up and meets Hardin with a kick to the midsection. Pepper then hits a reverse neckbreaker. Pepper places Hardin in a Boston crab, but Hardin just stands right up using his power, then falls backwards, landing on Pepper. Hardin gets up and gives Pepper an elbow drop, followed by a Texas cloverleaf.] JG - "That's the sloppiest Texas cloverleaf I have ever seen." JH - "Just because he's from Texas, it doesn't mean he can do the move, Grey. Hardin is a brawler, not a technician." JG - "Although, I hear he has been working on a hurricarana." JH - "Don't even joke about that." [Hardin places Pepper into a headlock. Pepper starts to give Hardin elbows in the side, but then Hardin drops Pepper with a double axe-handle. Hardin picks up Pepper and irish whips him into the turnbuckle. Hardin chases after Pepper but Pepper stops Hardin in his tracks with a boot to the face. Pepper then hits a fisherman suplex. Both men get right back up and Pepper bounces off of the ropes, hitting Hardin with a shoulder block. Hardin doesn't budge. Pepper bounces off the ropes again and again hits Hardin with a shoulder block. Hardin just laughs at Pepper, and taunts him to do it again.] JH - "Can you believe that, Grey? The Englishman thinks he can battle with Hardin! What a fool!" JG - "I think there's a line up of English people gunning for Hardin." JH - "What?" JG - "Nothing, Jack. Just watch the match." [Pepper again runs at Hardin, but this time Pepper hits a frankensteiner. Pepper immediately starts to pummel Hardin with punches to the face. Pepper picks Hardin up into a powerslam. Pepper then places Hardin into a bow and arrow submission, but Hardin uses his power to break the hold. Pepper gets up and places Hardin into a suplex, but Pepper can't get Hardin up. Hardin tosses Pepper over his shoulder. Hardin then chases after Pepper and clotheslines him. Hardin gets up to the second rope and hits an elbow drop. Hardin then picks up Pepper and throws him into the ropes. Pepper bounces off and Hardin meets him with a choke hold, followed by a chokeslam. Hardin goes for the pin... 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Hardin looks to be angry." JH - "Can you blame 'im? Pepper isn't staying down for the count. We need to get better jobbers." JG - "Pepper isn't a jobber. He is a former TV champ." JH - "Key word: former. And, look who holds that title now. It's sure carrying a lot of prestige." [Hardin picks up Pepper, checks to see where the ref is, and throws Pepper right into the ref, knocking the ref out. Hardin chuckles, then picks up Pepper and gorilla presses him to the outside. Hardin throws Pepper into the steel railing, then Hardin powerbombs Pepper onto the ring announcers table, shattering it.] JG - "Oh my! J.W. Hardin is dismantling Sgt. Pepper!" JH - "Haven't seen this before. Hardin comes in, destroys some over the hill jobber and sets his sight on greater things." JG - "Jack, you've been delving into his past." JH - "I know my stuff, baby." [Hardin grabs Pepper and goes to Irish whip him into the steel pole, but Pepper stops himself and hits Hardin with a knee to the groin. Pepper then hits a reverse neckbreaker. Pepper picks up a chair and hits Hardin over the head with it. Pepper then re-enters into the ring, jumps up to the third rope, and hits a flying body block onto Hardin. Pepper picks up Hardin and tosses him into the ring and goes for the pin. Unfortunately the ref is still out cold.] JG - "The ref is still out! It won't matter if he can get the cover or not!" JH - "Pepper is a moron. Can't he tell that the ref is out?" JG - "Obviously not." [Ted Jaspard runs down to the ring and assumes the roll of referee.] JH - "What's this moron doing here?" JG - "Good question. Should the referee be knocked out, Tommy Gregory should assume the duties." JH - "Plus, the Jaspard rule: No school, no need. I bet Ted can't count past two." [Jaspard begins the count... 1! Hardin tosses Pepper off of him. Hardin gets up and Pepper punches Hardin in the face. Hardin returns the favor, and the two get into a fist fight. Hardin gets the advantage when he blocks one of Pepper's punches and hits a suplex. Hardin then starts to choke Pepper with his boot, and Ted Jaspard goes to get Hardin off of Pepper, but Hardin just throws Ted back, sending him right out of the ring.] JH - "Guess that'll keep the Jaspard's out of here." JG - "We still have Ahmed, Ned..." JH - "That's great, Grey. Here comes Tommy Gregory to referee the match!" [Hardin keeps applying pressure when Pepper, out of desperation, hits Hardin right where the sun don't shine, sending Hardin down. Pepper is slow to get up, and Hardin is up at the same time. Hardin quickly kicks Pepper in the stomach, then places Pepper into a headlock, followed by a bulldog. Hardin hits a elbow drop, then quickly gets up and hits a powerbomb. Hardin picks Pepper up and sends him into the ropes. Pepper grabs onto the ropes, and Hardin chases after Pepper clotheslining Pepper over the top. Hardin goes to follow Pepper outside, but Gregory forces him to stay in the ring.] JG - "Tommy Gregory is standing up to Hardin! I don't think I've seen that before!" JH - "Gregory better watch out. Those martial artists don't have too many skills against the brawlers like Hardin." [Gregory is able to stop Hardin who just grins. Pepper walks into the ring under his own power. Hardin grabs Pepper and Irish whips him into the corner. Hardin chases after Pepper, and hits a body block squishing Pepper, sending him back down to the ground. Hardin picks Pepper up and hits a piledriver. At this point, Hardin says something to Gregory, getting a shout back from Gregory.] JG - "J.W. Hardin and Tommy Gregory are already butting heads!" JH - "I didn't think it would take too long." [Hardin picks up Pepper, hitting closed fists punches, which Gregory again warns Hardin about. Hardin obliges and begins humiliating Pepper with slaps to the face. As the crowd boos loudly, Hardin stops the onslaught and hits a swinging neckbreaker on Pepper. Hardin gets on the mat and applies a figure four.] JG - "Now, that's humiliating. Hardin is trying to beat Sgt. Pepper with a variation of Pepper's own move." JH - "Ain't it great?" JG - "There's just one flaw..." [Pepper begins to reverse the figure four into his version, the Crabalocker.] JG - "He is reversing the move!" JH - "Not for long, Grey!" [Hardin rakes the eyes of Pepper, forcing Pepper to release the hold. Hardin quickly gets to his feet, then grabs Pepper's legs and slingshots him into the turnbuckle. Pepper hits the turnbuckle and sticks, allowing Hardin to follow in and apply a full nelson. Hardin quickly releases the full nelson, grabbing Pepper around the waist and hitting a backbreaker. Hardin lifts up Pepper and hits...] JG - "Outlaw's Curse! You know what that means?" JH - "It's coming, Grey. The Cattlebuster is gonna do some bustin'!" [Hardin throws Pepper back in the ring, hitting the Cattlebuster DDT. He quickly covers.. 1! 2! 3!] JG - "J.W. Hardin gets the win in his Scraps de..." [Suddenly, a familiar voice booms over the public address speakers...] CKC - "Hardin!" [The crowd turns to see "Cowboy" Ken Curtis, in street clothes and with his black hat pulled down over his eyes, standing in the center of the entrance ramp, mic in hand.] CKC - "Pretty impressive showin' there against the mop-top. But when you an' I step between those ropes, you ain't gonna be hearin' no "Abbey Road". The only sound you'll be hearin' is the sound of breakin' bones an' the squeak of a stretcher wheel! You made a big mistake comin' here lookin' for the Cowboy, ol' man, an' don't you think for a minute that I ain't gonna make you pay for steppin' into my spotlight! You got a thumpin' comin', gramps, an' the Cowboy's gonna be the one dishin' it out!" [The crowd pops heavy for the Cowboy as Hardin stares hard at Curtis, a wicked grin on his face...] CKC - "As far as I'm concerned, we ain't gotta wait until Rasslefest. We can have this tussle right here, right now!" [Hardin motions to ringside for a microphone. He grabs the mic and grins at Curtis.] JWH - "Well, well. Momma Curtis musta had on a reaaaal short skirt tonight fer her lil' boy to crawl out from under it. But then, I hear a lot o' hombres spend time under yer momma's skirt." [Curtis pulls the cigarillo from his teeth, drops it to the ground, grounds it out with his boot, and breaks into a full sprint to the ring! He slides under the bottom rope and pops up, where he is met by Hardin, fists afire! The crowd roars its approval as Curtis tackles Hardin and they fall, rolling around on the mat, fists and elbows flying! Suddenly, the ring fills with security guards and referees, pulling the two cowboys apart!] JH - "Well, it looks like Gregory's guy his ducks in a row, here!" JG - "What did it take, about 1-point-2 seconds for those guards to hit that ring?" JH - "Let 'im go at it, I say!" [The referees and the guards get Hardin and Curtis pulled apart, and Curtis is pulled from the ring! Hardin breaks free and rolls under the rope, attacking Curtis again! Both men throw and land punches as the guards and referees finally get them seperated again! Curtis is pulled up the ramp, pointing his finger and screaming at Hardin! Hardin continues to eye Curtis until Curtis is pulled behind the cutains, then struggles to get loose. Hardin appears calm, then bolts for the curtain, the referees and guards in hot pursuit! He hits the curtains and disappears, followed by the guards and referees!] JH - "Somebody get a camera back there!" JG - "No way, we've got another match ready to go!" JH - "Screw that! This is hot stuff!" JG - "Stick to the gameplan, Hazard! Let's go to the ring! This next matchup should be an exciting one, Jack!" JH - "Yeah... about as exciting as an insurance seminar. Wake me when it's over, will ya?" JG - "Lighten up, Hazard. This match is Morales' first title defense of his newly captured Television title. Any title match is a big one in my book." JH - "I guess the fact that the gold is on the line could make this a somewhat interesting matchup... maybe."
