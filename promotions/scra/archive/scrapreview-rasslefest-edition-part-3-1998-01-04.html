[SCRA] ScraPreview [3/3] (Rasslefest edition)

4 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
++============================================++ "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin vs. "Cowboy" Ken Curtis ++============================================++ JG - "There could be a big debate for what actually is the main event on this card, but this match has to be considered as a very likely candidate." JH - "Damn straight. Jacks' gonna crush Lucifer and hand 'im a defeat to leave the federation with. This one has so much heat, so much animosity, it's tough not to call it the main event, even though it's still basically the midcard." JG - "But, they are relatively newcomers. The SCRA fans haven't gotten to know these two fine athletes enough to launch them into main event status." JH - "Who cares about the damn SCRA fans. They are all a bunch of stupid morons who get their money from their mommies to watch the show and hope a valet's dress pops open. We should be appealing to the worldwide wrestling fans. And, these two appeal most." JG - "I guess. We'll see how it all unfolds in this matchup. Here's what the Outlaw had to say." [SCENE: From ten stories above New York's Times Square, the camera pans over the hundreds of thousands of revelers ready to ring in the New Year. Spotlights zig-zag across the throngs of partiers as they mingle in the streets. As the camera zooms out, the colorful masses below take on the impression of small bees swarming in a hive. The drone of their joyous yells and laughs slowly fades as the shot finally pulls back to the darkness atop the building, where a lone figure stands looking down at the street. His black duster billows in the wind and a black cowboy hat is pulled low. As he pulls a small cigar from his mouth, he sends a trail of smoke winding into the cold air It is just another New Year's Eve for "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin.] JWH - "Should old 'quaintance be fergot." [Hardin scowls and pitches his burning cigar over the edge toward the crowd.] JWH - "Well don't worry. Ya ain't gonna ferget this old 'quaintance, Curtis. In fact, I reckon you got more in common with this night... this year... than even _you_ know. In just a coupla minutes, 1997 ain't gonna be nothin' but a memory fer them people down there. Good, bad, or otherwise, ain't none of 'em gonna care no more. At Rasslefest, yer gonna feel yer own time slippin' away, Curtis. Yer December has come and this match is gonna merely be a countdown to the end fer ya. Lots o' people are gonna be waitin' fer the end o' yer rise in the SCRA, and ya know somethin'? Just like the passing o' 1997... ain't none of 'em gonna care about ya no more." [For the first time, Hardin turns and faces the camera. The wind howls across the rooftop and whips his duster across his body. Hardin's dark eyes bore into the camera.] JWH - "Ever'body else is lookin' ahead, but I got plenty o' reasons to look back at 1997. Call 'em _personal_ reasons. An' yer a big part o' that, Curtis. See, ya gotta pick yer friends careful like in this world, but you ain't done such a good job o' that. Mebbe that's yer only fault... but I doubt it. Naw, I ain't sorry to see '97 draw to a close and I ain't gonna shed no tears when you go down at Rasslefest. In fact, I'm gonna enjoy the hell outta it." [Hardin begins to grin, but retracts it as a thought crosses his mind.] JWH - "But now we got us a lil' unexpected gristle in the grub. Tommy Gregory done got hisself involved in this match as the referee. Well Gregory, I like to give lessons instead o' advice, but I'm gonna make a rare exception in yer case. Stay outta my business. I don't care if yer the referee. I don't care if yer Cowboy Ken's lil' Cowboy Barbie. I don't care if yer the flamin' queen o' England. That ring ain't gonna be a place fer flyboys when the bell rings. Me and Curtis... we're gonna brawl. We're gonna bleed. But there ain't but one o' us gonna have his hand raised. An' if ya stick yer nose where it don't belong, Gregory, ya ain't gonna be around long 'nuff to see my hand in the air." [Hardin turns back to the ledge and looks back down at the throng. The excitement of the crowd can be heard as midnight approaches.] JWH - "Can ya hear 'em, Curtis? Can ya hear 'em calling fer yer demise? Like yer buddy once said, "Bell to bell an' straight through hell." It's gonna be a long journey, but the endin'... well, it's only gonna take three seconds...the longest three seconds o' yer life. If ya wipe the blood outta yer eyes, yer gonna see this "old man" standin' over ya and you'll know that yer time has run out. Naw, you ain't gonna ferget this old 'quaintance, Curtis." [Hardin looks at the large red plastic apple descending down a wire as the crowd chants the countdown to midnight... and the new year. They chant from below: "Three... two... one..." Hardin turns to the camera one last time and his voice is heard as almost a whisper over the roar of the crowd.] JWH - "This new year belongs to the Outlaw." [Fade.] JG - "Hardin promising big things coming up in 1998. I'm sure he would like to start it off with a big win over his enemy, Ken Curtis." JH - "It's gonna be a tellin' battle. One of these guys, if not both, is gonna be the world champ in 1998. Whoever wins this is gonna get there first." JG - "Here's what Curtis had to say." [The scene opens on the main thoroughfare through what appears to be a faded Old West ghost town. The doors and windows of most of the wooden buildings that line the street on both sides are boarded up and the wind is whipping the dust from the street into tiny twisters. A lone figure, dressed in a black boots, black jeans, a black leather jacket, and topped by a black, flat-crowned cowboy hat, walks with his back to us down the street. The camera follows him as he begins to speak...] CKC - "Many a man met his maker on this stretch o' road." [The wind whips the swinging doors of a saloon as Curtis continues down the street, walking with his back to the camera...] CKC - "Some of 'em deserved killin' and some of 'em didn't. But ya see, these men didn't give a damn who was right an' who was wrong. There was principals involved. Sometimes, it was money. Sometimes, it was a woman. Sometimes, it weren't nothin' but a cross word or the raisin' of an eyebrow. But these men, they all had one thang in common... they weren't afraid to kill or be killed fightin' to keep what was theirs, whether it was a bag full o' coins, a sweet smellin' split tail, or just plain ol' foolish pride. These kinda men're hard to find these day, but they're out there... ya just gotta know where to look. For some fool reason, J.W. Hardin went lookin' for one o' those men... an' damned if he didn't find one." [Curtis stops and turns to face the camera, his hat pulled low over his eyes...] CKC - "Hardin, I ain't gonna stand up here an' throw no threats at ya. As far as I'm concerned, there ain't nothin' I can say to you that you ain't done already heard, an' there ain't nothin' I can threaten ya with that's gonna scare ya, no way. You an' me, we're too much alike an' we know each other too well." [Curtis pauses for a moment, pulls a filter-tipped cigarillo from his breast pocket, and lights it with a quick flip of his brass Zippo lighter. He draws hard and exhales, the blue-grey smoke disappearing quickly in the wind...] CKC - "But one thang I will say is this... you dealt this play, big man, when you rode into the SCRA on that broken-down horse you call a reputation an' tried to stick a shovel in the Cowboy's claim. And I ain't made my name what it is in this business by rollin' over an' playin' dog for nobody, not even the big, bad, son-of-a-biscuit-eater they call the "Outlaw". [Curtis takes a hard pull from his cigarillo, then stares hard into the camera as it zooms in...] CKC - "You want what the Cowboy's got, hall-o'-famer?" Come an' take it. [Curtis turns and walks away as the scene fades to black.] JG - "Both men are extremely focussed. Coming from these two experience cowboys, it's no surprise." JH - "Can't we get all our wrestlers to do that?" JG - "Some do it sparingly. Others perform well. Others just sit on the sidelines and watch, then are thrust in the ring and are crushed." JH - "Those ones are my favourite! The squashers!" ++===========================================================++ "Golden King" Aladdin Style vs. "Silverblade" Anthony Logan ++===========================================================++ JG - "Finally: brother against brother comes to. Style and Logan have been going at it for longer than anybody in this league. They've been going at it since they were children." JH - "It's these kinds of rivalries wrestling thrives on. We need to turn the German Eagles on each other." JG - "I don't see that happening." JH - "We've got Destiny and Flash, who are brothers through adoption, figthing. Two is good, but we need more." JG - "Here's what Aladdin Style had to say." [The scene opens in the parking lot of a roadhouse. Motorcyles fill the lot. Bikers are fighting on the outside, but the camera ignores them, instead moving though the door and into the bar. The inside is just as rowdy, with bikers talking loud and aggressively and Back of Bitch by Guns and Roses playing over the speakers. The camera moves down the aaisle, towards a booth in the back. There is a sense of deja vu...like the audience has been here before. There is a man sitting alone, drinking Guiness in the booth. As we move closer, it becomes obvious that it is Aladdin Style, dressed in jeans and a Gaimen's Death T-shirt. The camera closes in on Style's face as he looks up to speak: AS - "Four months ago, Aladdin Style and Jason Gore met at this very bar to announce to the SCRA the coming of the House of Lords. We lifted our glasses to the air and vowed to dominate the tag team division of the SCRA like no tag team has ever dominated before. We delivered. Less than 30 days later we were defending the tag titles over and over against every team in the federation. We were the kings, unbeatable. And we stayed unbeatable! The noblest fan favorites and the vilest villians fell to our feet. We began as hated, but the fans gradually came over to our side. Why? Although we were brutal and merciless, we were also fair and honest. We were smarter, tougher and better skilled than anyone in our division. We were men. Skill rises to the top. Jason Gore and I spoke earlier today. The tests on Jason's knee has come back, and the prognosis is not good. "Lord" Jason Gore, the best tag team wrestler the SCRA will ever see, will never wrestle again. This bar isn't a church, but it's holy ground. As it marked the coming of the greatest tag team in history, it also marks the beginning of my solo career. From this day forward, the Golden King Aladdin Style walks alone. I have a few things to address, so bear with me. First, Ken Curtis. You are all man and a yard wide, my friend. You asked if it was guts or stupidity that got me in the ring with you. Neither, Cowboy. It was respect. This was a match I knew was going to be tough, We both got what we wanted out of it, big man. You got a victory over a world class opponent, and experience against a brilliant technical wrestler. I got my first solo match against a future World Champion. This wasn't a match I went into looking to win. For me, this was training - singles wrestling 101. I thank you for the lessons. Next time, though, one of us will have the gold around our waist - and the results will be drastically different. Second, Eddie Jacks. You put down my brother for facing "nobodies?" You call the House of Lords nobodies? You're twice my size, but half the man I am. Get in the ring with me, you fat, tired piece of crap, and that beating you got from my brother will seem like a pillow fight. You've been hanging on to your career for a long time, but the days of your reign are almost over. That last bit of inspiration and luck that won you the title was the last of your spirit old man. You wonder why Logan has an extended campaign against me and laughs at your World Title? Because I'm twice the man you are. If the gold is around your waist, then the gold means nothing. And speaking of Anthony Logan.... Rasslefest, January 4th. The Silver Knight vs the Golden King in a Dog Collar match. A freaking Dog Collar match. This is you're doing, Logan. Every time we face, you've needed an edge. A bum knee isn't good enough for you, big man? Alright, then, Dog Collar it is. I'm not the aerialist you've been watching, Logan. This isn't the advantage you thought it was. You remain the strike master, but I'm the king of leverage and inertia. You're just giving me more tools to work with, brother. You've said you wanted to pay me back for the pain I caused you, in your deluded mind. You did. You said that you wanted to destroy my adopted brother, because he replaced you in my life. You did. Your whole life has been dedicated to trying to be the man I am. In reaching for the sky, in trying to achieve what I've achieved, you've reached the stars. But remember, big man, I'm the original. I'm the man who's footsteps you've walked in. You destroyed my partner, because he replaced you in my life. Congratulations, you've got your role back. You're the latest wrestler who's almost, but not quite, as good as me. You're the latest wrestler I've carried to stardom. You're the latest wrestler who owes their career to the Golden King. It's all come down to this. All your planning, all your scheming, all your hopes and dreams ... it all comes down to four corners and a sixty-minute time limit. All your talking, all your sneak attacks, and all your mania - combine it all and it distills to the one truth you've spoken to date. Anthony Logan ... mate in one." [The camera starts to move slowly back down the aisle. A new song comes over the speakers: BURN by the Cure. The haunting rage of it's guitar echos through the bar as the bikers fall silent. As the camera leaves the bar, the opening words of the song can be heard. "Don't look, don't look the shadow's free......"] JG - "And the news finally hits, Jason Gore is no more." JH - "Good ryhming, uh, uh, stryming." JG - "What's a stryming?" JH - "You know, when somebody strymes." JG - "I don't want to get into this with you." ++========================================++ Activists from Hell vs. Praetorian Guard ++========================================++ JG - "We'll get a real tag team champion as this one is sure to settle the score." JH - "It's gonna be 3-0 Guard." JG - "You can only pick up 2 of the 3 belts. If you get 2 in a row, the match is over." JH - "Can I have the remaining belt? It can be our belt, Grey, for being the best tag team in the league!" JG - "What about my belt?" JH - "Everybody knows I carry the team." JG - "Here's the Praetorian Guard." [SCENE: In the SCRA locker rooms following the conclusion of last week's Powerhouse show. Naugrim and Baer, both dressed in street clothes, stand next to a nervous-looking Keith Spencer, who quickly begins the interview.] KS - "So, you....uh....claim to have been robbed of the tag titles. Uh...how do you feel about that upcoming....uh....ladder match for "vacant" belts?" TN - "'Tis an honest question, one that I shall endeavor to answer. We've nary a concern about a match that will only serve to showcase our superiority in this foul league." KS - "(gulping nervously) And your opinions on the Activists?" BB - "'Dem lyin', cheatin', sonumabitches is gonna git their asses beat......dey just ain't gonna be able to pull off none o' dat airborne and purty shit....." TN - "Exactly, William. By neutralizing the superior aerial skills of Zacharius Rage, we shalt stifle their potent attack." KS - "So you're not hesitating in predicting a Guard victory at Rasslefest?" [Baer rears his head back and laughs.] TN - "Aye, goodman Spencer. 'Twould be unconscionable to entertain any other thoughts. Belief leads to security, two concepts that we doth consider essential to our success as professionals." [Pause as Spencer contemplates the meaning of Naugrim's words.] KS - "Eloquently put, Tulkas. And...uh....what about rumors that House of Pancakes relations just aren't that rosey....uh...as of right now?" TN - "Ah, the lying words of knaves and braggarts hath penetrated even the sacred holds of men of the pen and microphone...Nay, Spencer, these rumors art naught but the venomous bile of vindictive varlets." KS - "Uh, that's about all the time we have for this interviewer... (softly, almost an aside) can we get an interpreter in here?" [Naugrim shakes Spencer's hand vigorously, then glowers at the camera and opens his mouth to speak...] KS - "We're out of time, folks!" TN - "Eh?" KS - "Uh, I'm not paid enough for quality interviews, Tulk. Five minutes is the limit on my contract. Heck, I'm not really supposed to speak on the air." [Naugrim and Baer look at each other, shaking their heads.] TN - "Allow us one last word...." [Fade.] JH - "Damn this underbudget league! Can't we spare a few seconds for a team who can actually speak?" JG - "Tulkas Naugrim speaks rather eloquently..." JH - "He string well." JG - "Uh, yes. But, we have strict production guidelines. I'm surprised the interview lasted that long." JH - "What was the time on that thing? I think I was in the shower." JG - "I guess we're lucky it cut out." JH - "Very funny, Grey. You wish you could have had a peep." ++===============================++ Johnny Destiny vs. Nathan Flash ++===============================++ JG - "These two former partners meet for the first time. Yet, it will be the last time as one man will leave." JH - "Ain't it great? We're gonna see Flash take home the title and Destiny get booted! Whoo!" JG - "I wonder if they still live together..." JH - "That's the dumbest thing you've ever said. Roll Flash's flash." JG - "Hehehe, clever." [The scene opens to a deserted piece of land in the West. A man is walking with only plants and weeds in sight. He gets to a train track and begins walking beside it. Looking at the ground, he begins to speak.] NF - "I've returned to my home...where I've spent so many years, taking the abuse from my soon to be fallen foe, and 'brother' Johnny Destiny. These incandescent losses put forth by the icon, the man all kids want to become, put me over the edge. The Great Canadian returns, and right away gets a title shot. That's bulls**t!" [Flash looks up revealing a serious look on his face.] NF - "These events happening to the luckiest son of a bi**h on the face of God's green earth, have put me in his shadow. The massive shadow of the Great Johnny Destiny. The shadow, just a symbol of his massive ego. Well Johnny, it's time to step out of your shadow and onto my road to victory." [We find a brief smile on his face which is soon wiped away. He has a mean look on his face.] NF - "Johnny, you've been crowned North American Champion, but when you feel the wrath of the new revalution of today's superstars, you're gonna feel like the NCW days were heaven. [Begins screaming.] NF - "And once I'm finished with you, Johnny Destiny, I'm gonna rape, pillage, and plunder this entire federation. But what I'd like to know is how do you feel? Being one of the members of the rich and refuge. Sitting paralyzed with fear of Nathan F'n Flash, who does, says whatever he wants. Ha ha ha." [He crosses over the tracks and looks back to the camera.] NF - "Just one easy step on my long way to victory." [A man is seen walking into a sunrise as the camera slowly fades.] JH - "I think I've heard that somewhere." JG - "Huh?" JH - "Who knows. Must've been drunk." JG - "Perfect lead-in to Destiny's flash. Roll it!" [Destiny stands atop a building in New York City. He stares over the rumbling activities which make New York the city it is. The cabs attempting to get through, the people walking the busy streets. The camera pans back up and sees Johnny Destiny.] JD - "It was on this very building that last night was hopping, due to the new year." [He looks up and goes cross-eyed.] JD - "He he, a bird." [He shakes his head and looks back at the camera.] JD - "Winston Churchill once gave a speech where he used 7 words." [Pauses.] JD - "Never, never, never, never, never, never quit." [Pauses.] JD - "Remember that, Flash, as I stand over ya tomorrow night." [Destiny walks off the screen.] JH - "Destiny must still be a little buzzed from the party." JG - "Seven powerful words. I remember that speech." JH - "Yeah, you were alive back then, weren't ya?" ++=============================++ Eddy "Flap" Jacks vs. Lucifer ++=============================++ JG - "Lucifer gets his final shot at the World title. If he wins, he'll be walking away with it." JH - "Yet, in Lucifer fashion, he'll be on the mat easily. This guys already had too many shots, why do we give him another one?" JG - "He earned it at Fatal Fall. Unfortunately, others have proven they deserve it more between now and Fatal Fall." JH - "This booking stuff is strange." JG - "Let's get Jacks' comments." [SCENE: The crudely and inefficiently set-up SCRA interviewing area. Eddy Jacks, countenance grim yet somewhat contemplative, looks dead-on at the camera and begins to speak. His bloodshot-red eyes bely his inner fire, reflecting instead the wear and tear of his hectic schedule.] EJ - "I learned yer ass once, kid. Broke yer leg and killed yer promisin' career. Ya helped make me, though, an' that's somethin'....that's why poundin' yer injury-addled ass is just gonna be too much." [Jacks laughs.] EJ - "And what's a few laughs between friends, eh? Ya mightta beat me, back then...but it ain't back then an' I'm sure as hell not the same script-readin', ass-kissin' Eddy Jacks that cared so much about winnin' that he didin't pay attention to what he was doin'. Yer fightin' the next best thing, son....an' ya ain't no more giant, just some used-up talent that's gettin' one last shot at the big-time. Hell, yer even headlinin' the biggest card in this piss-poor league's history, an' that's somethin', I guess." [Pause.] EJ - "Heard that this might be yer last fight, ace. Too bad...ya was a bad gimmick then, eh, an' yer a worse gimmick now. An' me? I'm the best thing goin' these days, a livin' legend in a little fairy-land. Reinvented myself all nasty, pounded some punks, then got regarded as the hardest -line mf'er this side o' the IIWF. Not bad for a guy that once prided himself on workin' with a jobber like ya was....a guy that thought speakin' all flowery, eh, an' pretendin' ta be some great "patriot" was the surest road ta glory." [Pause.] EJ - "An' ya was a jobber, wasn't ya? Yer ass was losin' to Lifetaker back when I was in diapers, claimin' ya was "hard-core" an' pretendin' to be somethin' ya wasn't....somethin' ya could never aspire ta be. Me? The signs were always there, kid....ya knew I was somethin' ya never could be. Ya built up your little "stable", thought ya could handle me....vanity killed ya, ace, as ya watched yer friends leave and me just standin' over yer ashes and ya couldn't do a thing 'cuz you were powerless ta do anythin'..... Now we're goin' at it, one last dance between pals...one last chance for a dyin' career...one shot for me ta prove once an' for all who the best this league ever had is...." [Pause.] EJ - "Pack yer bags, slick, 'cuz I'm takin' ya the full 60...." [Fade.] JG - "Bringing back the good ol' days." JH - "Good? They were just old." JG - "I'd have a tough time disagreeing with ya. Things were grim back then. But, they're looking bright, now! We're just a short time away from the biggest night in SCRA history. Rasslefest is coming in under 24 hours! Good night!" -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
