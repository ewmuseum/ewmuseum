[The scene fades in to a beach with a beautiful sun glowing in the sky. Several gorgeous women lie on the sand with their beach towels, sunbathing. Others are surfing on the large waves. A largely muscular man stalks his way across the beach with a sly smile on his face. He spots a puny, tapped rim glasses geek talking with a fine looking brunette over by a hotdog sand. He grabs the small young geek and tosses him down a ways. The bully then starts sweet talking the brunette in the bikini. The geek hops back up, stomps over to the bully and taps him on the shoulder. The bully whirls around to only have the geeks legs wrapps around his head. The geek flips the bully down to the sand with a hurricarana. The bully slowly gets back up, shakes his head and gives up, stomping away....] JG - "(VO) Who said the bullies always win? It's Beach Blastoff...the sun, the sand and the surf is there...and there's the sweat, the scars and the stitches..." [The geek is shown being hoisted up in the air by a large group of women.] JG - "(VO) Coming to pay-per-view, July fifth. Call you cable company for details!" [The girls accidently lose their grip and the geek falls to his face. The camera focuses on his sand covered face. He spits out a mouth full of sand.] JG - "(VO) Did we mention the sand?"
