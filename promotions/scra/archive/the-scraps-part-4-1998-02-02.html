[SCRA] The Scraps [4/5] (2 February 1998)

2 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 5 ) | | \ | | Siberian Express vs. Praetorian Guard / |___ | | | | Writer: AG (_________________________________________________________________________ [Jaspard enters the ring with a cardboard cutout of himself. Of course, the cutout has different proportions than Jaspard himself.] EJ - "Me and my tag partner are ready to introduce this next matchup! It's got a 20 minute time limit and is scheduled for one fall!" [The Dance of the Sugar Plum Fairies begins playing.] EJ - "Coming down the aisle first, hailing from 596 lbs., here are... say it partner!" [Jaspard puts the mic up to the cutout which to the surprise of Jaspard, says nothing.] EJ - "Fine. I'll say it. The Praetorian Guard!" [The Guard begin their walk into the arena, mocking the fans along the way. Suddenly...the Guard's music is interrupted by a vaguely familiar riff.] JG - "What the...? JH - "Who the...? JG - "I know this music...but.....it _can't_ be! JH - "No way...._no_ way! They're supposed to be gone for good! Why can't they be gone for good!" [The Praetorian Guard stop in their tracks en route to the ring. The crowd's response to the music is one of disbelief. Everyone's heads twist from side-to-side looking...searching...hoping... Finally, the blasting of "Down Rodeo" by Rage in the Cage kicks into gear as the Guard remain frozen in the aisle] JG - "[Still searching; but finding nothing] Well, the Guard must have changed their music..." JH - "Stick it to those bastards! We ran them down south!" JG - "That would be east, Jack. What an interesting choice...damn...it would have been a nice surprise...but it's not to hap---" JH - "[Interrupting as the music continues] ---OH NO! NO! IT CAN'T BE!! LOOK UP THERE!" [As Hazard points to the cheap seats up top, the camera quickly pans over into the direction of what is now a huge commotion in the crowd. A small pop of yells and cheers is suddenly spreading like wildfire throughout... The camera finally settles into focus to the owners of the music...] JG - "I KNEW IT! I KNEW IT!" JH - "...but _now_?" JG - "THE ACTIVISTS FROM HELL ARE IN THE HOUSE!! THEY'RE BACK! THEY'RE BACK! AND LISTEN TO THE CROWD!" [The noise becomes deafening as the long dreadlocks of "The Liberal" Zak Rage and the meaty body of "The Advocate" Joshua Baker, do indeed, make their way down the steps up in the rafters. Not a single backside is in their seats. The music is drowned out from applause, cheering and whistles as the two men have difficulty with their trek to the ring due to the demand for handshakes and handslaps. With smiles, both Baker and Rage oblige the attention-seekers as they finally make their way ringside.] JG - "[Barely audible] This is amazing...I have _never_ heard a crowd so loud in my life!" JH - "I gotta admit... these nimrods love The Activists! Now...gimme some aspirin; I've got a headache from all this noise..." JG - "It's a touching moment, Jack! Live with the pain!" JH - "I am! Do you know how painful it is to see these two bums back in a ring? I thought we were free from that sight for life." JG - "And as The Activists slide into the ring...just look at the faces on the Praetorian Guard! They look absolutely petrified!" JH - "Like a deer in headlights. But, these deer can fight." [The decibel level reaches it's peek as both Rage and Baker, tanned from the Cambodian sun, jump to the second turnbuckle of a chosen corner. As they raise their hands high...the noise becomes unbearable. They hop down and Rage, with a sly smile and a quick nod, asks for a microphone. He is quickly taken up on that request. The Praetorian Guard, a bit upset that their thunder has been stolen, take two angry steps towards the ring...Zak Rage, microphone in hand and with a sly smile, holds up one fore-finger and waves it back-and-forth...] ZR - "[Still smiling towards the oncoming Guard] Gentlemen...I don't think coming to the ring would be the wisest move of your careers..." JH - "Huh? If they can't go to the ring, how can they wrestle?" JG - "You always know how to ruin a delightful moment." [As the Guard once again freeze, the crowd errupts at the sound of the much-missed voice and sharp wit of Zak Rage...] ZR - "[Still smiling] As a matter of fact...I think _everyone_ in this place should wish our dear friends, the Praetorian Guard, adieu..." [As one, the crowd starts waving and screaming "goodbye" to the Guard. After five seconds of demanding their exit, the embarrassed Guard reluctantly leave the area. After they leave...the crowd stands and cheers itself for their job well done...] JG - "It's pandemonium in here! This is unbelievable!" [Rage and Baker smile at each other] ZR - "[Sparkle in his eyes] WE'RE BAAAAAAACK!!!" [The sound of Hazard sobbing is quickly drowned by another huge eruption as Rage approvingly nods his head. Baker manages a smile as well as he stands, arms crossed in the ring...] ZR - "[Over the crowd] You're all probably wondering just why the heck... or better yet, just _how_ the heck did we manage to leave sunny Cambodia...[smile]...well, it's simple. Remember those two dozen kids you saw in that video?" [Many in the crowd offer a "yes"] ZR - "[Nodding] Well, we adopted them..." JH - "No! First they come back. Then they bring a group of little kids to fill up the day care offices even more! What's next?" [..Rage nods as the crowd buzzes...] ZR - "Yep....all of them. And by doing so...[shrugs]...we were given permission to come back. Aaaaaaaaaaand...[sly smile]...I think Joshua and I have a _weeeeeeee_ bit of unfinished business to take care of right here in the SCRA!" [Another eruption by the crowd] JG - "I think wee is an understatement." ZR - "[Holding a hand up to hush the crowd as he smiles] Seeing as time is a bit limited here; I'm gonna go kinda fast,if that's alright.." [He uses his fingers as counting tools as he continues] ZR - "[Headscratch] Number One: The politics of the SCRA administration..." JH - "Ha! Let 'em have it!" [...the crowd murmurs...Grey squirms...] ZR - "[A mini-chuckle] ...politics..politics...politics. It seems as though whenever Joshua and I send a little blip of ourselves to the SCRA Towers for you fans to view.....[shrug and a smile]....the boys upstairs lose it!" [Crowd pop] ZR - "[Nodding] Call it a conspiracy? ....I think so......Sooo, in the future....umm.._if_ there's a future, I'll be delivering these tapes myself to Mr. Grey. But, let's not put the cart before the horse...." [Zak switches the microphone from one hand to another as the crowd listens intently] ZR - "We have a little offer to the boys in the Towers. Joshua and I completely understand that....[shrugs]....sometimes people lose. _However_..it seems as though there was a bit of funky circumstance surrounding our loss to the Guard back on Rasslefest. I'll gladly get into the gory details, but I think that there's enough mud-slinging going on these days; just ask Bill Clinton." JH - "I got a joke about that." [Grey lifts his head from the table, recovering from the severe public relations beating the league is taking and mumbles something.] JG - "No......w." [The crowd chuckles as does Joshua Baker] ZR - "[smiling and eyes sparkling] Here is the offer we will make to the boys in the towers. We get one match with the Guard. _One_ match. If we win, we get another chance at the title.....Oh...I see that the SCRA sprung for more leather-and-gold....[shake of the head]... waste of money...._anyways_..." [...crowd chuckles again at the memory of the orange Unicef boxes...] ZR - "....If we beat the Guard; we get to tango with the German Eagles and Joshua and I continue to do our thing in the SCRA...." [The crowd pops at the thought of the Activists returning] ZR - "[With a smile as he scratches his beard] If we should _lose_ the match..Joshua and myself...along with our 24 new mouths to feed; will venture on and do our own thing." [Shrugs...] ZR - "You see....we don't demand much....but...we do _want_ the Guard..." [the crowd claps as Rage hands the microphone to Joshua] JB - "[Calm and matter-of-factly] Zacharius was overly kind a few minutes ago..unfortunately...I won't be as forgiving..." [The crowd pops in appreciation of Baker's frankness. Rage smiles and shakes his head at his best friend's candidness.] JB - "[Slightly pointing at the camera] The administration..[headshake].. you boys are about as bad as the current one in the White House. I think it's safe to say that all of you went to the same instructive seminar. And, I think it's also safe to say that all of you flunked the mini-exam afterwards." JH - "It's true! I can't believe I'm agreeing with that moron!" [Grey again drops his head as the crowd chuckles at the deadpan humor] JB - "The Guard...Zacharius and I saw the fear in your eyes a few minutes ago. And...if our request for this match is granted, that fear that we saw will be multiplied by ten-fold 'cause I will personally create more pain for you than you could ever imagine. You're a disgrace. Period." [The crowd mumbles in anticipation of this potential rematch. JB - "If we win, then it's on to the Eagles and to what _everyone_ knows is rightfully ours." [Crowd pop] JB - "If we lose....then we'll hang 'em up for good. So...what'll it be, boys? Do you gentlemen have the guts to admit your mistakes? Will you do the right thing and grant our request? Or...will you do what you do best and cower behind your desks; cover your ears, nose and eyes and pretend that nothing ever happened........We look forward to your answer." [Joshua sets the microphone on the apron as the crowd rises to their feet. Again, the decibel level reaches new levels as Rage slaps Baker on the back and they exit the ring. They head back up to their cheap seats and the hundreds of awaiting fans.] JG - "When will we hear an answer from the Guard?" JH - "Right now!" [The Praetorian Guard, complete with the managerial staff and Eddy Jacks, walks into the arena, again. Baer leads the way, his head brimming with anger. He grabs the mic in the middle of the ring before the rest of the House of Pancakes can even catch up.] BB - "You sonumabitches is gonna git their asses beat!" [The rest of the HoP enter the ring, push Baer around for his quick response, but soon agree. They stare into the rafters, motioning for the Activists to come back. Rage somehow gets a microphone and offers his response...] ZR - "See you Sunday." [As Rage finishes, the Activists leave the crowd, followed by a string of Activists groupees.] JG - "It's done! This Sunday, The Activists from Hell against the Praetorian Guard! Wow!" JH - "Yee-haw. We get to see the Guard destroy the Activists, again." [As the Guard motions towards the Activists, the Siberian Express, angered at their wait backstage, burst into the arena and attack the Guard from behind.] DING! DING! DING! JG - "I guess the Siberian Express don't really care much for the Activists return." JH - "Exactly. Who does care about this? I know Baer's itching to beat that little snot Rage, again, but other than that, who really cares?" [Bolotov dumps Baer over the top rope, leaving Dragovich to lay a beating on Naugrim. Bolotov walks over to help his partner as the two set up for a double team move. They send Naugrim into the ropes, but Naugrim comes back with a flying cross body. The Express, however, catch Naugrim and quickly drop down with a double backbreaker. Bolotov exits the ring, allowing Dragovich to continue with the attack.] JH - "That thing was amazing. They got in a backbreaker, a kneebreaker, a shoulderbreaker..." JG - "We get the picture." JH - "If only my knees were the size of the Express' put together. I bet they almost are because of all those operations I had." JG - "Operations?" JH - "I had to retire from wrestling because of my knees. Didn't you know that?" [Dragovich hops onto Naugrims back, applying a camel clutch. Baer walks in to attempt and break the hold, but Dragovich moves away and Baer hits Naugrim with an elbowdrop. Bolotov rushes the ring and clotheslines Baer over the top. Bolotov climbs out of the ring, following Baer.] JG - "The referee needs to get control of this match. Although it's only a few minutes old, he has already lost all control." JH - "Do they ever have control of a match? What exactly can a referee do to a wrestler? They're all wimps!" [Dragovich resumes the camel clutch, working the back of Naugrim. On the outside, though, Bolotov and Baer start throwing punches. This continues until Gudonov rushes in with a chair and nails Baer in the back. Baer slumps into Bolotov's arms, allowing Bolotov to nail a belly to belly suplex onto the concrete. Tain Surefoot moves around the ring to help out Baer, but is too late as Bolotov is protecting Gudonov from an attack.] JG - "It's like Alexander Gudonov as the Siberian Express under mind control. They do what he says." JH - "The ruthless managers are the best managers. Actually, no. The hootered managers are the best managers. Ruthless comes second." [Dragovich releases the camel clutch and climbs to the second turnbuckle. As Naugrim gets to his knees, Dragovich takes off with a flying axehandle, connecting in the back and dropping Naugrim to the mat again. Dragovich gets to his feet and throws Naugrim into the Express turnbuckle. He follows in with a clothesline. Dragovich tags in Bolotov and the two set up for a double team move. Bolotov executs a double underhook backbreaker while Dragovich takes off the ropes and drops the knee across the chest of Naugrim.] JG - "What a double team move! I guess the training is paying off for these two." JH - "Back in Mother Russia, we train hard." [Bolotov uses his power to lift Naugrim up. He hoists Naugrim onto his shoulder, then powerslams him to the mat. Bolotov again lifts Naugrim up, this time into a gorilla press. He tosses Naugrim into the Guard corner, urging Baer to tag in. Baer does.] JG - "It's a showdown of the two most powerful men in the tag ranks today!" JH - "This is wrestling. Toss Dragovich and Naugrim outside and let them use the foreign objects to add excitement, but just leave these two alone in the ring." [Baer moves into the ring as Bolotov signals for a test of strength. Baer agrees and the two lock up, trying to get position. Bolotov seems to have an advantage going, but kicks Baer in the midsection, quickly followed by a fireman's takedown. Baer gets up quickly, but Bolotov drops him with a running clothesline. Again, Baer gets up rather quickly, but Bolotov dives in with a Thesz press. He starts unleashing an attack of punches to the head of Baer. Bolotov stops punching and runs off the ropes, hitting a big legdrop across the chest of Baer. He covers... 1! KICKOUT!] JG - "Almost had him!" JH - "Did I ever say my Clinton joke?" JG - "No. Please don't. It's probably bad." JH - "Judge for yourself. It refers to Canada, though, my proud country. Our dollar may be at the lowest price ever, we may be having some of the coldest winters ever, but at least Chretien's not trying to bone you!" JG - "That's disgusting...but true." [Bolotov lifts up Baer and tosses him into the Express corner. He begins rubbing Baer's head into the turnbuckle, then tags in Dragovich. The two set up and connect with a double snap suplex. Dragovich rolls on the mat and applies an armbar. Baer rolls his way out of it with vicious elbows to the chest, then sends Dragovich running into the ropes. Baer bends over to quickly for a back body drop which allows Dragovich to move in and connect with a swinging neckbreaker.] JG - "The Siberian Express have dominated this entire matchup. I don't think the Praetorian Guard has had one shot!" JH - "It's the damn Activists. They distracted the Guard in the early part of the match!" JG - "Stop making excuses. You know it and I know it that the Siberian Express are just the better team tonight." [Dragovich moves over to Baer and lifts him to his feet. He starts tossing a series of rights and lefts, pushing Baer back into the turnbuckle. Dragovich follows in, Irish whipping Baer into the opposite turnbuckle. Baer bounces off the turnbuckle and is caught by a Dragovich scoop slam. Baer wearily gets up and moves back towards Dragovich who promptly takes him down with a hiptoss. Dragovich dives onto the mat as well, applying an armbar.] JG - "Smart move by Ivan Dragovich to wear down the powerful Bill Baer. Without his upper body strength, Baer is pretty much just a punching bag." JH - "And an ugly one at that. He must've got the stuffing beat out of him already." [Baer begins to work his way out of the armbar and gets to his feet, but Dragovich fights back with a quick facerake. As Baer staggers back, Dragovich catches him in an inverted atomic drop quickly followed by a kick to the midsection. Baer catches the foot, but is soon met by an enzuigiri. As Baer drops to the mat, Dragovich runs off the ropes and leaps with a flying headbutt into the chest. Baer doubles over, but is quickly put into an abdominal stretch by Dragovich.] JG - "Dragovich is systematically breaking down every aspect of Bill Baer. He's got the arms done and now the chest. The only thing left is the legs." JH - "And the face." JG - "What?" JH - "We weren't talking about women?" [Dragovich pulls Baer over to his corner and tags in Bolotov. Bolotov starts stomping away at Baer's chest, while still in the abdominal stretch. Dragovich releases the hold, allowing his partner to attack Baer. He grabs Baer by the head in a full nelson, then lifts Baer to his feet. As Baer squirms around trying to get out, Bolotov slams him down to the mat. Bolotov picks up Baer, again, then drops him down with a DDT. He quickly moves off the ropes and drops an elbow across the chest. Bolotov gets right back up and sprints off the ropes, again nailing an elbow drop across the chest. By this time, Baer is in the corner, attempting to pull himself up. He does, but is met by a kick to the midsection by Bolotov, followed by an inverted atomic drop. As Baer bends, Bolotov sets up and nails a powerbomb.] JG - "What power by Andrei Bolotov! He just powerbombed the 300 plus pound Bill Baer!" JH - "The Russians have strength. Baer's from Charlotte. What kind of wrestler would come from there?" [Bolotov drags Baer into the Express corner, then tags in Dragovich. Dragovich lifts up Baer and places him in the corner, unleashing a flurry of punches to the head. As Baer slumps to the mat, Dragovich begins stomping away. Dragovich lifts Baer to his feet, then nails a piledriver in the middle of the ring. He moves down to the legs and applies an ankle lock submission.] JG - "He's attacking the legs!" JH - "Don't forget the face." JG - "Huh?" JH - "What?" [Dragovich releases the hold soon after, but holds Baer's leg against the mat. He springs himself up, driving his knee onto Baer's leg. Dragovich repeats this a number of times.] JG - "I don't think Bill Baer can move. After the destruction of his entire body, he's done for." JH - "This match brings new respect for the mad Russians. The Praetorian Guard are probably still at the top of the list, but only marginally." [Dragovich tags in Bolotov, again, who quickly applies a bear hug on Baer.] JH - "A bear hug on Bill Baer! Ha!" JG - "Iron Curtain! They just nailed the Iron Curtain!" JH - "The cover...he ain't gettin' up, though." 1! 2! 3! JG - "What a match. The Siberian Express thoroughly dominated the Praetorian Guard." JH - "It was the Activists! They screwed everything up!" JG - "If that's what you want to believe, fine. I just think that the Guard are in disarray right now." JH - "Disarray to the outsider. Inside, they are one tightly knit group." JG - "This next matchup should be an exciting one! Derek Mota has proven that he can mix it up with the bigger guys, and Logan is still enjoying his recent success! This one should be a good one!" JH - "I'm more impressed with Mota every week. The guy knows no fear and seems impervious to pain." JG - "Impervious? I'm impressed, Hazard! You been reading the dictionary?" JH - "Shutup, Grey. I can't help it that I went to public school." JG - "Let's go to the ring!" -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
