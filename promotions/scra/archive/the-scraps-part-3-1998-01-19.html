Your first competitor..." ["Burn" by The Cure blares from the arena speakers as "Golden King" Aladdin Style makes his way through the entrance curtain and down the ramp...] JG - "Wait a minute, a fan's jumped out of the crowd! He's attacking Style on the ramp!" JH - "That's no fan, that's Prodigy!" JG - "Prodigy's taking early control, here!" JH - "I like this new attitude the kid's got!" [Prodigy picks Style up and bodyslams him on the ramp! He backs up... elbow-drop across Style's chest! Style's hurting as Produgy picks him up and drags him towards the ring! Black Kat follows them, urging Prodigy on! Prodigy drags Style to the ring and posts him! Style hits the floor as Prodigy starts putting the boots to him!] JG - "Wellington needs to gain control here!" JH - "The bell hasn't even rung, yet! This match hasn't even started!" JG - "My point, exactly!" [Prodigy pulls Style to his feet and irish-whips him into the ring steps! Style hits hard and grimaces in pain as Prodigy closes in!] JG - "Where is this brutality coming from?" JH - "Hey, the kid's trying to make a move! Can you blame him?" JG - "Who's he going to impress with this crap?" JH - "He's impressing the hell outta me!" [Prodigy finally rolls Style into the ring and climbs in! He rips Style's jacket off and begins choking him with it! Wellington calls for the bell!] DING! DING! DING! [Wellington starts his count but Prodigy releases the choke and gets to his feet, stomping Style into the mat! The crowd pops heavy heel as Prodigy goes to work with the boots! Prodigy pulls Style to his feet and irish-whips him into the ropes! Style rebounds... running lariat by Prodigy! Style is down, here's a cover!] 1! 2! JG - "No! Style kicked out!" JH - "The kid's going for the win! You gotta respect that!" [Prodigy pulls Style to his feet, but Style gets a boot into his gut! Another! Prodigy releases Style, who propels himself into the ropes! Style rebounds with a cross-body-block... no! Prodigy caches Style in mid-air and spins him into a 360 powerslam! Prodigy pops to his feet and gives the crowd a yell, illiciting a huge heel pop!] JG - "Prodigy had better stay on Style!" JH - "I agree! Forget the fans kid, get the win!" [Prodigy pulls Style to his feet and sets him up... vertical suplex! Prodigy pops to his feet and drops a forearm across Style's forehead! Prodigy pops up... another forearm-drop! Prodigy bounds off the ropes... flying legdrop! Here's a cover!] 1! 2! JG - "No! Style got the shoulder up!" JH - "This Style's pretty tough, I'll give him that!" [Prodigy, obviously frustrated, slaps the mat and points a finger at Wellington, asking for a quicker count. Wellington blows him off as he gets to his feet, pulling Style up! Prodigy irish-whips Style into the corner and follows him in with a running lariat! Style slumps but Prodigy pulls him up and picks him up, setting him on the top rope!] JG - "Style's in a world of hurt, here! He's posting no offense!" JH - "I think this one's gonna be over real soon!" [Prodigy climbs to the second rope and sets Style up for the superplex! Style throws a fist into Prodigy's gut! Another! Another! Style fires a couple of blows to Prodigy's head then pushes Prodigy off the ropes with his feet! Prodigy hits the mat, but immediately starst to get to his feet! Style stands ont eh second rope... missle dropkick! Style scrambles to cover Prodigy!] 1! 2! JG - "No! Prodigy kicked out rather solidly, there!" JH - "Style's finally getting some offense going! [Style gets to his feet and grabs Prodigy as he rises, hitting him with a short-arm clothesline. Prodigy staggers back as Style propels himself into the ropes and hits him with a flying forearm! As Prodigy hits the mat, Style hurdles him and jumps onto the second rope...] JG - "Moonsault!" JH - "But he's not going for the cover!" JG - "I think he realizes he needs to wear Prodigy down a bit!" [Style drops an elbow across Prodigy's throat, then pulls him to his feet... belly-to-belly suplex!] JG - "Wow! That took some strength!" JH - "This Style is just a bit impressive!" [Style lays a couple of boots into Prodigy, then heads for the corner!] JG - "He's going up top!" [Style poses for the crowd and leaps...] JH - "Nobody home! Style missed with the flying elbow!" JG - "Cover him, Prodigy!" [Prodigy gets to all fours and crawls to Style, covering him!] 1! 2! 3! JG - "He got him!" JH - "No! Wellington is waving off the three-count! He says Style got the shoulder up! What a crock!" [Prodigy gets up and in Wellington's face, slapping his hands together in a slow mocking three-count!] JG - "And here comes Style!" [Style comes in from behind, and rolls up Prodigy!] 1! 2! JG - "No! Prodigy kicks out! Prodigy's up and he's stomping the heck out of Style!" JH - "He's gonna stomp his guts out!" [Prodigy pulls Style to his feet and irish-whips him intothe ropes! Style rebounds, ducks a clothesline, off the opposite ropes...] JG - "Hurracanrana by..." JH - "No! Prodigy reversed it into a massive powerbomb!" [Prodigy picks Style up and sets him up...] JG - "He's going for a vertical suplex..." JH - "No! Style reversed it into a small package!" 1! 2! RE-REVERSAL! 1! 2! 3! DING! DING! DING! EJ - "The winner of this match, as a result of a pinfall, Prodigy!" JG - "Prodigy got him! What a match!" JH - "It's over? We had so many close falls in that one, I felt like I was falling." JG - "What?" JH - "Don't you know when you wake up, your brain isn't functioning properly? That's what I'm like now. With Style going all fan-man and Prodigy begin, well, Prodigy, I lost interest in that match." JG - "This next one should get your attention. We've got J.W. Hardin and Derek Mota!" JH - "Stick this sucker in the main event! We need more matches like these to pull the ratings up." JG - "Er, yes. Let's go to Ed Jaspard for the intros!" ___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 6 ) | | \ | | "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin vs. Derek Mota / |___ | | | | Writer: JG (_________________________________________________________________________ [Ed Jaspard comes into the ring in overall shorts, a white and red plaid shirt covered by the overalls, red and white striped stockings and a red wig.] EJ - "I'm a bad wittle boy." [Jaspard gives an odd smile and nibbles on the tip of his first finger.] EJ - "Well, this contest is scheduled for one fall, and if you will, in memory of Ted, who will be having surgery done on Wednesday. Coming down the aisle first, from Toronto, Ontario, Canada, weighing in at 224 pounds, Derek Mota!" [The Great Southern Trendkill begins playing over the PA very heavily. The music starts instantly with heavy guitars and drums and the vocals start with a long scream. The fans see Mota emerge from the back and let out a huge pop. Mota runs to the ring and jumps to the turnbuckles. He looks around at the audience and smiles psychadelically. His music fades out as Ed Jaspard begins speaking again.] EJ - "His opponent, from Dry Gulch, Texas, weighing in at 353 pounds, "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin!" [A red spotlight bathes the entrance. Hardin enters to the Outlaw Blues. He walks slowly to the ring, wearing his usual black duster and his cowboy hat pulled low. He ignores the fans on his way to the ring. He slowly enters the ring, removes his hat and duster, and leans against the top turnbuckle in one corner while staring at his Mota.] DING! DING! DING! [The two men circle each other with the much bigger J.W. Hardin pushing Mota down and letting out a little chuckle.] JG - "This isn't fair. Derek Mota gives up 129 pound weight difference!" JH - "Why was this match even signed? I like both of these guys. Doesn't out booking staff know not to book matches with two guys I like? I don't know who I want to win!" [Hardin goes over to the little man and picks him up by his throat and immediately starts choking him above his head. Hardin places him on the top turnbuckle and grabs his throat and leg lifting Mota over his head, dropping him flat on his face. As Hardin goes to pick Mota up, he rolls out of the way and springs to his feet. Mota dropkicks Hardin as Hardin merely staggers back. Mota dropkicks Hardin again as he hits the turnbuckle in a slouched position. Mota runs to the corner and begins delivering vigorous rights to the temple of J.W. Hardin. Hardin falls to his rear as Mota continues to punch.] JG - "See how Mota used his speed to get in control? That's what a seasoned veteran does." JH - "What about J.W. Hardin? He's a veteran." JG - "But how many cruiserweights have you seen Hardin take on? Mota's used to taking on bigger guys." JH - "Like me." [Mota gets off Hardin as Hardin slowly gets to his feet. Mota runs from the other corner at Hardin, but Hardin ducks as Mota merely jumps over Hardin onto the second rope, jumping off and hitting a DDT. Mota gets up and jumps to the top turnbuckle hitting an elbow. Mota picks Hardin up and throws him into the ropes. Mota hits a headscissors takedown and jumps to his feet.] JG - "He's really fired up tonight." [Mota picks Hardin up and elbows him in the back of the neck. When Hardin gets bent over, Mota runs off the ropes and hits a scissors kick to the back of the neck. Mota turns Hardin over and goes for the pin... 1! 2! KICKOUT! Mota picks Hardin up and hits a legsweep, sending Hardin over to the corner. Mota steps over Hardin to get to the turnbuckles and hits a split-legged moonsault. Mota gets off of Hardin and picks him up, throwing him into the turnbuckle. Mota follows him up and hits a clothesline.] JG - "Mota almost got in front of Hardin there." JH - "Cause Hardin is too busy trying not to wrip a seam." JG - "I don't think he'll like that." JH - "I know, but this is the only opportunity I'll have to make a witty comment!" [Mota jumps to the second turnbuckle and hits a monkey flip. Mota ran into the ropes and jumped up sitting on Hardin's sternum. Mota started delivering rights to the nose of J.W. Hardin. Mota gets off Hardin and jumps to the second turnbuckle. He hits a moonsault and springs to his feet. Mota picks Hardin up and throws him into the ropes, hitting a clothesline. Mota drags Hardin over to the turnbuckle and as he attempts a moonsault, Hardin moves out of the way forcing Mota to hit nobody. Hardin gets up and picks Mota up by the armpits. He throws Mota into the ropes and hits a powerslam.] JG - "Oh, Hardin is really using his size to his advantage." JH - "Listen, Grey, if you were 300 plus pounds, and you were facing a 220 pound man full of pizzaz, what would you do? It's an obvious decision. Like trying to distanguish butter from cheese." JG - "But that doesn't make sense. What kind of idiot can't distinguish butter from..." JH - "This idiot." [Grey breaks out in laughter.] JH - "Shut up, it's only at those fancy restaurants where they..." JG - "Why would a waiter put a block of cheese on a table?" [Hardin goes for the pin... 1! 2! KICKOUT! Hardin picks Mota up and throws him into the turnbuckle. Mota falls to the ground because of the impact. Hardin walks over to Mota and jumps to the second rope. Hardin takes off with a big splash onto the back of Derek Mota. Hardin just lays there as Mota's legs are kicking frantically. Hardin gets up because of the ref threatening to disqualify him. Hardin picks Mota up and hits a tilt-a-whirl sidewalk slam. Hardin eventually gets up and hits a vicious elbow to the throat. He leans into it and begins choking Mota. Hardin gets up as the ref counts to 4 and picks up Mota with him. Hardin picks Mota up over his head and holds him there for a few seconds before dropping him over his knee and pressing Mota's chin down.] JG - "It's not often that J.W. Hardin uses a submission move, but he seems to be slowing down the cruiserweight." JH - "Grey, I'm gonna talk to your brother to raising the cruiserweight limit to 360 pounds." JG - "Well, that defeats the principle of even having a cruiserweight division." [Mota slips off Hardin's leg as Hardin gloats over top of him. Hardin drives a forearm to the back of Derek Mota. Mota begins to turn over and as Hardin goes for another forearm shot, Mota grabs the arm of J.W. Hardin and twists it. Mota springs up and twists it again. Mota hits a sidekick to the chin of J.W. Hardin.] JG - "He just kicked the chin of the 6'10 giant." [Hardin falls to the ground as Mota goes off the ropes hitting a legdrop across the throat of J.W. Hardin. Mota uses his leg to choke Hardin, thus returning the favour. Mota gets up and picks Hardin up with him. Mota hits a standing dropkick which sends J.W. Hardin to the turnbuckle. Mota again delivers a series of rights. Mota jumps to the second turnbuckle and delivers a hurricarrana on the big man.] JG - "Good strategy by Mota. If you can't get the big man up, use what you're dealt." JH - "[mockingly]Yes, excellent strategy." [Mota gets up and jumps on Hardin. He begins laying furious rights and lefts to the nose of J.W. Hardin. Mota gets up and puts J.W. Hardin in a figure four. Hardin looks like he's in pain and reaches sparingly for the ropes. Mota grabs Hardin's arm and pulls him further away from the ropes. Hardin, though, chops Mota's arm and turns over, reversing the hold.] JG - "Mota wasn't thinking, the figure four is one of J.W. Hardin's trademark holds." JH - "So, he knew how to reverse it." JG - "[screaming] Would you let me finish a sentance?" [Grey and Hazard argue to the extent that you can't even hear what they're saying. Hazard dies down and all you can hear is Grey screaming saying...] JG - "I can run the mile in six minutes! I can run the mile in six minutes." [After some time, Mota is able to turn Hardin back over, who grabs the ropes. Mota gets up first and hits a springboard moonsault on the exhausted J.W. Hardin. Mota goes for the pin... 1! 2! KICKOUT! Hardin shoves Mota off of him and gets up slowly. Mota grabs Hardin by the hair and goes over to the ropes. Mota springs over the ropes, hitting a hotshot on Hardin, whos hair was still attached to Mota's hand. Mota gets back in the ring and throws Hardin through the first and second ropes. Mota jumps to the top and attempts a asai moonsault, but J.W. Hardin catches him upside down. Hardin pulls him up onto his shoulder and powerslams Mota through a table.] JG - "Looks like a sliver of the table caught Derek Mota on the forehead. He's staring to bleed." JH - "Chalk another one up for the Outlaw." [Hardin gets up first and picks Mota up across his shoulders. He walks over to the corner of the ring and does a helicopter spin, hitting Mota's knee on the ringpost. Hardin puts Mota down as he falls down on his back. Mota grabs his knee as Hardin picks Mota up. Hardin places Mota in a full nelson and walks over by the ring steps. Hardin steps back with one leg and kicks the back of Mota's knee forcing it to hit the ring steps. Hardin repeats a couple of times before letting go. Mota tries to get up but collapses on his knee. Hardin picks Mota up and throws him into the ring.] JG - "Stop the match, ref. Derek Mota really seems hurt." JH - "Let the tough, Canadian bastard go on!" [Hardin steps through the ropes and picks Mota up. He throws him into the ropes but Mota collapses. Hardin hits a hard elbow to the back of Derek Mota. Hardin goes off the ropes again and tries it, but Mota moves out of the way. Hardin is on the ground for a long time as Mota makes his way to his feet. Mota tries to go up top, but as he gets to the top, his knee gives out and he falls to the outside. Hardin gets up and is puzzled to the whereabouts of Derek Mota. Hardin looks around and argues with the ref about this. As he's doing so, Derek Mota sneaks in and small packages J.W. Hardin.] JG - "He's got 'em. 1...2...oh, Hardin just gets a shoulder up!" [Mota stays on the ground as J.W. Hardin continues to work on the knee. Hardin grabs hold of the knee and drives his own knee into it. Hardin repeats as the ref attempts to pull him off. Hardin doesn't budge and gets off at his own time. Hardin gets up and grabs Derek Mota's leg. Hardin hits it with a series of elbows and finishes off with a leg drop to the knee. Hardin picks Mota up and hits a knee-breaker.] JH - "Hmm, haven't seen that move before." JG - "I guess it's a Hardin original." [Hardin goes for the cover, but as he does so, Mota uses his good leg to put it on the ropes and the ref forces Hardin to get off. Hardin turns Mota around so his bad knee is closer to the ropes and goes for the cover. Mota uses all his strength and gets his leg on the ropes at 2.] JG - "Wow, talk about courage. Any mortal man would've stopped fighting a long time ago, but not Derek Mota." JH - "I gotta give him a lot of credit. Staying this long with J.W. Hardin is a feat only Ken Curtis has done so far in this league. I respect the man and I think J.W. Hardin does too." [Hardin picks Mota up and has him ready for a belly-to-back suplex. Mota uses the good leg to give J.W. Hardin a mule kick. Hardin falls to the ground as Mota uses the ropes to keep him up. Mota climbs to the top with a determined look on his face.] JG - "Look at the determination on this man's face." [Hardin is beginning to get to his feet and as Mota takes off with The Main Attraction, J.W. Hardin reverses it into the Cattlebuster DDT.] JG - "The Cattlebuster DDT! Hardin hit the Cattlebuster DDT!" JH - "When he saw Mota turn around, he just knew he had the ability to reverse it." [Hardin sits up and Mota rolls out of the ring. The ref is counting as Mota struggles to get to his feet. Hardin climbs out of the ring and grabs Mota. He hoists Mota high over his head and tosses him onto Jon Grey's lap.] JG - "What the?!" JH - "Hey, Derek. How's it hanging?" [As Grey and Hazard attempt to get Mota off them, Hardin yells out "Too many brawlers?" Hardin then picks Mota up over his head and tosses him over the top rope. Hardin walks into the ring and drags Mota into the middle of the ring. Hardin slaps on the figure four but Mota refuses to give up. Mota is screaming in pain and doing whatever it takes to break the hold. Hardin simply applies more pressure as Mota grasps wildly for the ropes. The ref signals for the bell and awards the match to J.W. Hardin.] JG - "Mota didn't give up! Derek Mota should still be in this match." [As the paramedics rush to the ring J.W. Hardin starts to walk back to the dressing room. As Hardin gets to the top of the ramp, Mota signals for him to come. As Mota is being placed on the stretcher, Hardin approaches it. Mota rolls off to one side and throws the stretcher at Hardin. Hardin falls hard to the ground and Mota hits him with a fury of fists. Mota rolls Hardin back in the ring and pins him. The ref counts... 1! 2! 3!] JH - "What the hell just happened?" JG - "Who won?" JH - "I....don't know." JG - "Okay, Ed Jaspard is talking to the ref and will soon be ready to set things straight." JH - "Can Ed Jaspard ever set anything straight? I mean with his stupidity and all, you can't really trust him." [Ed Jaspard grabs the mike and begins talking] EJ - "Ladies and gentlemen, I have been informed by referee, Hans Foresmits that the winner of this match, via submission is "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin!" [Outlaw Blues comes on the speakers as Hardin starts to gloat.] JH - "I guess J.W. Hardin is the victor in this one." JG - "Not necessarily, here comes Tommy Gregory." [Gregory begins talking to Ed Jaspard and Hans Foresmits and after a long talk, Gregory comes back into the ring. At this point, J.W. Hardin is already halfway up the ramp. Outlaw Blues cuts off and leaves Hardin looking at the ring.] EJ - "Ladies and gentlemen, I have been informed by Tommy Gregory that the winner of this match, via pinfall is Derek Mota!" [Hardin starts going belistic and starts back to the ring. Gregory grabs the mike and begins talking.] TG - "Could I have your attention, please." JH - "Yes, teach!" [The crowd goes silent.] TG - "Mr. Mota wished to continue the match and referee Hans Foresmits rung the bell under false pretense. Mr. Mota did not submit to the figure four and thus the match went on. Mr. Mota then pinned J.W. Hardin and therefore got the victory." [The Great Southern Trendkill comes over the PA as Derek Mota hobbles to the top turnbuckle. At this point, President Grey came down to the ring and grabs a mike.] AG - "Mr. Hardin....Mr. Mota....I have reviewed this match backstage and thought me coming out would be the only way to settle this. In a match filled with controversy, this match is being counted as a no contest. Thank You." [President Grey leaves as the camera cuts back to Jon Grey and Jack Hazard.] JH - "Look at your brother, drunk with power." JG - "It was the only fair way to settle things." JH - "Couldn't we just play paper, scissors, stone to settle it? I hate ties!"
