[SCRA] The Scraps [2/4]

19 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 2 ) | | \ | | Siberian Express vs. The Pros / |___ | | | | Writer: AG (_________________________________________________________________________ [Jaspard climbs in the ring, saluting the crowd.] EJ - "Ready to go, Sarg. This matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. Coming down the aisle first, weighing in at a total combined weight of 280 lbs., accompanied by Tavis, here comes Grant and Walt Jabasco, the Pros!" [The Mission Impossible theme plays over the PA system as Tavis enters first, receiving a "Welcome Home" pop from the crowd. The other two Pros enter as well, walking to the ring slowly, slapping with the crowd. They reach the ring as Jaspard continues the intros.] EJ - "And their opponents, weighing in at 598 lbs. and hailing from the motherland, Russia, they are the Siberian Express!" [Gudonov walks to the ring, eyes on his opponents, receiving a loud boo from the crowd. The other two members, however, cannot be found.] JG - "Where are Dragovich and Bolotov?" JH - "Right there!" [From the back, Bolotov and Dragovich jump in the ring, nailing all three Pros from behind. They toss Tavis out of the ring, allowing Alexander Gudonov to get a few cheap shots in. Bolotov and Dragovich then work on Grant, tossing him out, before going after Jabasco.] DING! DING! DING! JG - "We're underway with a bang!" JH - "Love those sneak attacks." [Bolotov exits as Dragovich turns to Jabasco amd hits a standing dropkick. Jabasco is quickly up and staggers back to the corner. Dragovich charges, hitting a big splash in the corner. He goes to work on Jabasco with a series of knife edged chops. Dragovich hops to the second rope and starts firing shots to the head.] JH - "Count, you morons!" JG - "I don't even think Dragovich can count to ten. I'm not sure how good english these Siberian Expressers speak." JH - "They english fine." [Jabasco gets out of the hold, much to the delight of the crowd, and carries Dragovich to the middle of the ring for an inverted atomic drop. Dragovich staggers to his corner and tags in Bolotov who charges Jabasco and is dropped by a lariat. Jabasco drops two quick knees to Bolotov's right leg and then pulls him up to toss him into the Pros' corner. Jabasco charges, but Bolotov is move out of the way and grab Jabasco around his shoulders. He nails a fallaway slam.] JG - "Such power displayed by the big Russian." JH - "How many Russians aren't big?" JG - "Is that a joke?" JH - "No a question. Did it sound like a joke?" JG - "Yes." JH - "Than it was a joke. Chalk another one up in the incentives portion of the contract." [Bolotov picks up Jabasco and tosses him into the Siberian Express corner. Bolotov runs in with a lariat, then moves off. He goes to Grant, slaps him in the head which causes the illegal Pro to storm the ring. The referee stops him, back turned to the Express who are performing some double team tactics in the corner.] JG - "Turn around, ref!" JH - "Hehehe, classic double teaming. I love this stuff." [Dragovich and Bolotov both enter the ring, without a tag, and lift Jabasco into a double vertical suplex. They drop him hard in the center of the ring. Bolotov remains in the ring, referee not aware that Dragovich should be in. He Irish whips Jabasco into the corner, but goes down for a back body drop too early. Jabasco drops to the mat, chops the throat and then nails a DDT. Dragovich comes in off the top rope and hits Jabasco in the back of the head with an axehandle.] JG - "The referee has lost all control here!" JH - "No he hasn't. Jabasco is just stupid. Dragovich is the legal man, but Jabasco attacked Bolotov." JG - "It was the other way around." JH - "Whatever. The referee has lost all control. Just let them fight until one guy bleeds." [Dragovich whips Jabasco and then nails a spinebuster. He covers... 1! 2! Grant comes in! Bolotov storms the ring, evening the score at two on two.] JG - "All control is lost! It's just a free for all here!" [The referee gets control, forcing Bolotov out, then Grant. Dragovich applies an armbar, but is short lived. Jabasco begins with elbows to the head, then gets free of the hold for good. He finally releases himself from the hold, then tosses Dragovich into the ropes. Dragovich ducks a clothesline, latches on for a crucifix, but Jabasco holds firm. He drops back with a fall away slam.] JG - "Jabasco needs to make the tag. He's been in there for too long." JH - "He's been in SCRA for too long! Get him out of the ring for good. That's what I always say." [Grant starts stomping his feet on the outside, helping Jabasco get up. Jabasco does, walks over to Grant, but is yanked away by Dragovich. Dragovich tries to pick Jabasco up, but Jabasco rolls him up! 1! 2! KICKOUT! Jabasco dives into his corner and tags in Grant!] JG - "Hot tag!" JH - "As opposed to a cold one?" [Grant jumps in and sends Dragovich running, nailing a hiptoss on the way back. Dragovich gets back up and is taken down again by a hiptoss. Dragovich gets up, runs at Grant and jumps into a flying cross body block. Grant catches him and converts the move into a backbreaker. He covers... 1! Save by Bolotov! Grant gets back up, unphased from the Bolotov attack. He scoops up Dragovich and slams him to the mat. Grant picks up Dragovich and sends him running into the ropes, catching him in a sleeper.] JG - "He's got him in a sleeper!" JH - "He's had me in a sleeper for a while, too. It shouldn't take Dragovich too long to conk out from this match." JG - "Very funny, Jack. This match has been classic tag action. We've seen all the makings of a typical tag match." JH - "Boring." [Dragovich begins to make his way out of the sleeper with repeated elbows to the midsection of Jabasco. Jabasco releases the hold, allowing Dragovich to drop to the mat and catch his breath. Jabasco drops right with him, putting the sleeper back on.] JH - "Not again!" JG - "It seems to be working. We'll see how Jabasco holds this second attempt on." [The referee raises the hand...once....twice...Bolotov comes in and kicks Jabasco in the back of the head. The referee ushers him out, as Grant tags in for Jabasco. The referee turns to see the Pros double teaming Dragovich, but disallows the tag. He pushes Grant to the corner, forcing Jabasco to stay in. Bolotov re-enters, dragging Dragovich to the Express corner. As the referee turns, Bolotov tags himself in and attacks Jabasco with vicious kicks to the back of the head.] JH - "Now that's smarts. Bolotov may look like a big, dumb Russian, but he's got a peanut up in that noggin of his." JG - "And maybe only a peanut." JH - "It's not how big it is, it's how well you can use it. Then again, when talking in other terms..." JG - "Can we try and go one show without making sexual references?" JH - "No. That's another thing in my contract. Remember, sex sells." [Bolotov picks up Jabasco and sets him up for a powerbomb. He nails the move. Bolotov takes his time, mocking the fans near ringside. He moves into Jabasco, but is monkey flipped! Bolotov gets back up, runs at Jabasco, but is kicked in the midsection. Jabasco hits a swinging neckbreaker and tags in Grant. The Pros both kick Bolotov in the midsection, then hit Pro-Pain! Grant covers... 1! 2! 3!] JG - "The Pros won!" JH - "Two upsets in one night!" JG - "The Pros have been tag champs here." JH - "That's saying a lot." JG - "It could be an upset, but many won't call it that. Either way, it's a win for the Pros." JH - "Few and far between, but they got one tonight. A lucky one, I might add." JG - "Who is the crowd straining... uh-oh, it's "Cowboy" Ken Curtis and he doesn't look too happy." JH - "Why is that surprising? The man pulled a pistol on the Top Turnbuckle guys, for Pete's sake!" [Curtis, dressed in street clothes, makes his way down the ramp, but stops halfway down to jaw with a fan wearing a black, "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin foam cowboy hat! He yanks the hat off the fan's head and feigns wiping his butt with it before tossing it back to the fan! A wicked smile crosses his face as he turns to head down toward the ring, but the smile quickly disappears into his normal, clenched-teeth scowl as he rolls into the ring and calls for a house mic. Someone hands him a mic and he pulls a sheet of paper from his back pocket, unfolding it. The crowd boos him mercilessly, though we can hear a faint "Cowboy! Cowboy! Cowboy!" chant.] JH - "Turn the mic on! Let's hear what the man has to say!" JG - "I'm not sure if we want to do that!" [Curtis turns to address the crowd...] CKC - "You people shut yer damned mouths!" [Of course, the crowd boos louder, causing Curtis to rush the ropes and point a finger at a fan at ringside, who responds with the middle-finger salute. Curtis returns the gesture, then makes his way back to center ring...] CKC - "This here piece o' paper was just posted on the bulletin board in the locker room. An' either somebody's got their head up their ass, or they jus' plain ain' been payin' attention t' what's been goin' on aroun' here lately! This here piece o' paper, straight from the desk of Alex Grey, says that the Cowboy _ain't_ the number one contender to the strap that ol' lard-ass Eddy Jacks is totin' aroun'!" [This illicits a huge pop from the crowd, which pisses the Cowboy off even more, if that's possible...] CKC - "It says here that a tender-foot by the name o' Gangsta Impasse is the number-one contender!" [The crowd pops for the mention of Impasse's name, pissing the Cowboy off even further. Curtis crumbles the paper up and tosses it into the crowd...] CKC - "I got news for you, Grey, an' anybody else who thinks the Cowboy is gonna take a backseat to some low-life piece o' street trash like Gangsta Impasse! I ain' standin' in line behind no Too-pack Shay-ker wanna-be, so after I get through beatin' the hell outta that other piece o' gutter garbage tonight, I say me an' ol' baggy drawers have us a little..." [The crowd redirects its attention to the wrestler entrance and pops, as Gangsta Impasse angrily makes his way through the entrance curtain and down the ramp.] CKC - "Well, well. I guess you turned that jungle noise ya call music off long enough t' hear what the Cowboy had t' say, huh? Come on up here an' git some, mama's boy!" [Impasse heads toward the ring, but is caught and held back by Tommy Gregory and a couple of security officials!] CKC - "Let 'im go, Gregory! I don' want nothin' holdin' 'im back 'cept for fear an' common sense!" JH - "Curtis sure knows how to stir the hornets up, doesn't he?" JG - "He'd better be careful, Impasse is one tough customer!" JG - "Are you kidding me? Curtis just laid out the toughest of 'em all!" JH - "That may be true, but Impasse is as tough as they come! Just ask Stanlee Maxx!" [Impasse points and yells at Curtis! Curtis drops the mic and beckons Impasse to the ring with both hands and a wicked smile, but Gregory and his officials hold tight, pulling Impasse back through the curtain and out of sight.] CKC - "I _am_ the number one contender t' that strap, Grey, an' I _will_ git what's comin' t' me, you can believe that!" [With that, Curtis drops the mic and leaves the ring, headed back up the ramp and through the curtain...] JG - "Yeah, but you may not like what's coming to you, Mr. Curtis!" JH - "Shutup, Grey! The Cowboy's right and you can't stand it!" JG - "We'll see what Stanlee Maxx has to say about that tonight!" JH - "I already know. It involves the words "hurt" and "don't"." JG - "It could. Next up, we've got the TV title on the line! Rock Bottom Robbie takes on Doug "Grizzly" Adams! Let's go to the ring for the intros!" ___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 3 ) | | \ | | Rock Bottom Robbie vs. Doug "Grizzly" Adams / |___ | | | | Writer: AG (_________________________________________________________________________ [Jaspard hops in the ring, bear suit hurled over his head.] EJ - "I summon the powers of the bear to help me with this intro. Here comes one of us...Grizzly!" [The primal roars of the bear come over the speaker as Adams makes his way to the ring, disoriented without Nemesis. The camera cuts away from Adams to Jaspard who jumps out of the ring, grabbing a new prop.] EJ - "I summon the powers of the brew to help me with this intro. Straight from Rock Bottom bar and billiards, Rock Bottom Robbie!" JH - "That beer's no new prop for Jaspard." JG - "I don't think it's new for anyone in this league." [Little Things comes over the speakers as Robbie runs down the aisle with his belt, yielding it high over his head. He nails Adams in the back of the head, then tosses Adams into the ring.] DING! DING! DING! JG - "He's going right after Adams! What an animal." JH - "I hate Robbie, but at least he's willing to get dirty." [Robbie has Adams in the corner and unloads with forearm shots and uppercuts... and Adams stuns him with a facerake, before pushing himself up to the second turnbuckle and taking Robbie down to his knees with a flying axehandle.] JG - "Both these men want to get right at the brawling." JH - "Maybe we can get this match over with." [Adams takes a bunch of forearm shots to the back of Robbie's head, and then applies a headlock while Robbie is on his knees. Robbie pushes himself back to his feet, and pushes Adams off, nailing a big clothesline as Adams comes back.] JG - "Robbie's pumped tonight. I wouldn't be surprised if we saw a quick match here." JH - "That's what we're hoping for. If you don't deliver, there'll be hell to pay." [Adams gets back to his feet, but Robbies is on the attack with a headbutt Robbie picks Adams back up, sends him into the ropes and nails a shoulderblock. Robbie picks him straight back up, but slams him back to the mat with a body slam. Robbie comes of the ropes with an elbowdrop, but Adams moves out of the way. Adams moves off the ropes himself and attempts an elbowdrop, but Robbie moves out of the way. Robbie applies a chinlock on the mat.] JG - "Robbie is showing off some of his newfound technical ability." JH - "That's what we want to see. Technical stuff. Real appealing match you booked here." [Robbie pulls Adam's shoulders to the mat into a pin... 1! 2! KICKOUT! Robbie releases the chinlock before getting up and running into the ropes, hitting a big legdrop.] JH - "Why couldn't he have stayed down for that one?" JG - "Maybe he'll stay down with the big legdrop. That's been effective in the past." JH - "Yet pathetically crappy." [Robbie rolls over and drops another leg. He picks up Adams and hits the Rock Bottom Slam!] JG - "The Slam already!" JH - "Hey, it was short." [Robbie covers... 1! 2! 3!] JG - "He got him! Robbie continues to roll on with his TV title." JH - "That's a belt to be proud of." JG - "It hasn't had the greatest champions, but they hold it with pride. The next matchup should be a great one, too. We've got the German Eagles and Phi Theta Kappa." JH - "Yipee." JG - "I know you don't mean that. Let's go to Ed Jaspard for the ring intros." ___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 4 ) | | \ | | German Eagles vs. Phi Theta Kappa / |___ | | | | Writer: AG (_________________________________________________________________________ EJ - "This matchup is scheduled for one fall with a twenty minute time limit. hailing from Trier, Germany and weighing in at 480 lbs., the German Eagles!" [The Eagles then enter the ring to the German National anthem, and slowly march their way down, avoiding the fans, and just sitting in the ring.] EJ - "And their opponents, weighing in at a total combined weight of 482 lbs., Allen Jenkins and Brad Thompson, Phi Theta Kappa!" [Their national anthem is cut short by We Will Rock You as the Phi Theta Kappa team quickly comes down to the ring in togas, with the fans all screaming "Toga!". They find one lucky lady sitting near the front of the ring to take their robes off, they then jump into the ring, ready for battle.] DING! DING! DING! [Joseph Eagle and Allen Jenkins lock up. Jenkins knocks Eagle down with a fireman's carry to the mat, then applies an armbar, holding Eagle to the mat. Eagle soon works his way to his feet by spinning around on the mat. Eagle elbows Jenkins and goes into the ropes. Eagle ducks under a lariat attempt but on the way back is met by a powerslam by Jenkins.] JG - "Good comeback by Jenkins and I guess Joseph Eagle, too." JH - "Stop making small talk. Let's get this thing over with." JG - "What? You don't like this match. That's odd." JH - "Very funny, Grey. This match has about as much appeal as a mule and Cindy Crawford in bed." JG - "What?" JH - "Good question." [Jenkins gets up quickly and runs into the ropes. He leaps high in the air and connects with a legdrop. Jenkins picks up Eagle and sends him into the ropes, connecting with a dropkick. He tries to put on an STF, but Eagle dives to the ropes.] JG - "Smart move by Joseph Eagle." JH - "Is it Joseph Eagle? I thought Chris was in the ring. Can we make these guys wear name tags?" JG - "How would they stay on?" JH - "Pins in the chest. I don't mind seeing a little blood." [Jenkins grabs Eagle by the hair and lifts him up. Jenkins nails a snap suplex, then tags in Thompson. The two hit a perfectly executed double clothesline.] JH - "PTK is about as bonded as a team can get. But, that doesn't surprise me." JG - "Again with the remarks. Just call a match straight." JH - "Yes, the German Eagles are straight." [Thompson puts Eagle in a camel clutch. He holds this move on while Eagle is struggling to move towards the ropes.] JG - "He just might make it!" [Eagle, with a final pull of strength, lifts Thompson off the mat and grabs a hold of the ropes. Thompson gets off Eagle and stands him up. He whips Eagle into the ropes and connects with a dropkick. Thompson pounces right back on and throws Eagle into the turnbuckle. He follows in and connects with a lariat. Eagle staggers out and Thompson kicks him in the gut. Eagle bends over and, with lightning like quickness, Thompson goes off the ropes and slams Eagle down with a bulldog.] JG - "A great combination by Brad Thompson! I don't think the German Eagles have executed a wrestling move tonight!" JH - "They have before?" [Thompson runs off the ropes and connects with an elbowdrop. He puts on a figure four. Eagle screams in pain and the ref drops to the mat. Eagle says he doesn't want to give in. Eagle reverses the figure four! Thompson releases the hold, then sends Eagle into the ropes. Eagle ducks underneath a clothesline, but comes back and hits a dropkick.] JG - "Joseph Eagle needs to make a tag. He has been in the ring for pretty much the entire match." JH - "Just let him suffer. I would die laughing if his brother turned his back for the tag." [Thompson springs up, but is taken down by a hiptoss. Eagle quickly applies an armbar. Thompson spins around the mat, gets to his feet and attempts an elbow to the head. Eagle ducks, then hits a back suplex. Thompson gets back up, but Eagle scoops him up into a shoulderbreaker. Joseph tags in his brother who applies an armbar on the shoulder.] JG - "He made the tag!" JH - "What difference does it make? Same move, same trunks, same appearance. It's like the same guy is in the ring!" [Thompson performs similar jumps and spins to work his way out of the armbar. This time, instead of a hammerlock, he twists Eagle's arm. Eagle quickly twists Thompson's arm back. He sends Thompson into the ropes. Thompson ducks a clothesline. Eagle tries to hiptoss Thompson, but Thompson blocks it. He attempts a hiptoss of his own, but Eagle blocks this. Eagle takes Thompson down with a drop toe hold into an ankle lock submission.] JH - "This technical stuff is crap. Even I would rather see the Eagles fly high than stay on the mat." JG - "I kind of agree with you there. But, whatever works." [Eagle releases the hold, tags in his brother and they nail the Double German Suplex.] JG - "They hit their finisher!" JH - "How many times are we going to see people hit their finisher out of the blue tonight?" JG - "I guess a few more!" [Joseph covers... 1! 2! 3!] JG - "He got him! The Eagles notch another victory!" JH - "Yeah." JG - "Jack, this next matchup features two men at different stages in their careers, Aladdin Style and Prodigy. Style is a former tag-team wrestler looking to make it in the singles ranks, while Prodigy is just looking to make it!" JH - "Style may have the edge here, but Prodigy needs to make a statement! Both men need to make a strong push to get the push, you know what I mean?" JG - "I know exactly what you mean. Let's go to the ring!" -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
