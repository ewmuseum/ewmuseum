[SCRA] The Scraps [3/5] (2 February 1998)

2 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 4 ) | | \ | | Rock Bottom Robbie vs. Prodigy / |___ | | | | Writer: TG (_________________________________________________________________________ [The audience applauds as Jaspard returns to the ring. Jaspard delights in the applause and attempts to step in, but his belt gets caught on the ropes. His pants unfasten and, well, you know. Jaspard pulls them back up and as the true warrior he is, continues the announcements.] EJ - "This is a one-fall match with twenty minute time limit and is for the SCRA Televison Title!" Jaspard stops, as Smashing Pumpkins and 'Bullet with Butterfuly Wings' roars throughout the arena.] EJ - "First, approaching the ring with his manager Black Kat, hailing from Toronto, Ontario and weighing in at 276 lbs: Prodigy!" [Prodigy, led by Black Kat, walk down the aisle to a mixture of cheers and boos. Both slap hands with aisleside fans. Once ringside, Prodigy walks up the steps and hold the ring ropes open for Kat. Kat steps through and Prodigy joins him. As the music dies down, Prodigy pulls his hair out of pony-tail and hands his sunglasses to Kat. Jaspers addresses the audience again.] EJ - "His opponent, hailing Intercourse, Pennsylvania and weighing in at 245 lbs, he is the current SCRA Television champion: Rock Bottom Robbie!" ['Little Things' by Bush blasts through the speakers to a huge face pop by the fans. Rock Bottom Robbie walks through the curtains, holding his TV title belt in the air and the roar of the crowd increases. He walks quickly to the ring, slapping hands with the fans as he goes. He climbs in the ring and hands the title belt over to the ref. Prodigy stares at the belt as it's being handed over, then turns away, holding the ring ropes open for Black Kat to exit.] DING! DING! DING! [Rock Bottom Robbie and Prodigy stand toe to toe, nose in the middle of the ring in a staredown. For several seconds neither moves, then the bell to start the match sounds. Instantly, both competitors unload on each other with fast, powerful punches. Neither backs down nor flinches for several seconds of repeated blows, until Prodigy loses the rhythm and takes three sucessive shots to the head.] JH - "They're going nuts! These two wimps can actually throw a punch! Who'da thunk it?" JG - "I already knew they could both brawl. Remember the preview last night?" JH - "Oh, right. I sort of tune out for those." [He stumbles backwards, off balance. Robbie stays on him until finally the bigger man drops to the mat. Prodigy rolls out of the ring to avoid Robbie's assault. Robbie slides out after him.] JH - "And, getting extreme! We might actually see a match between these two midcarders!" [On the outside, Prodigy nails Robbie with a kick to the gut, doubling Robbie over. Taking a couple steps backwards, Prodigy rushes forward with a clothesline, which Robbie ducks under and counters with a back body drop. Rock Bottom picks up his challenger and irish whips him into the ring barricade. Prodigy collapses in pain. Robbie slides back in the ring to break the ten-count.] JH - "Hehehe. So far so good, eh Grey?" JG - "Quite. Just remember that a match isn't decided by how much blood or how many punches..." JH - "It may not decide the winner, but it decides how good it is." [Prodigy uses the ring apron to haul himself upright and then rolls back into the ring into Robbie's waiting arms. Robbie takes Prodigy over with a butterfly suplex, following it up with a camel clutch. Prodidy howls in pain. The referee asks Prodigy if he wants to quit, but Prodigy refuses.] JG - "Robbie's got him locked in. This could spell the end of Prodigy." JH - "Unless he twists him into a really, really weird shape, there's no way that Prodigy can somehow spell "the end of Prodigy." [After a few moments, Robbie releases the Camel Clutch, and drops an elbow on Prodigy's kidney area. Robbie picks up Prodigy, pushes him into the corner and lets fly a knife-edge chop that echos through the arena. Prodigy winces in pain.] JH - "Whoo!" [Robbie irish whips Prodigy into the other corner and charges with a clothesline. Prodigy ducks under it and Robbie hits the corner, hard, bouncing backwards a couple steps. Prodigy nails Robbie with a desperate double-axehandle to the back of the neck. Both wrestlers collapse.] JG - "Prodigy's dead tired while Robbie just got nailed. Both men are down for the count!" JH - "Double TKO! That would be a great way to end this slugfest. That was sarcasm, by the way Grey." JG - "I know." [Prodigy gets up first, then picks up Robbie by the hair. Prodigy nails a pair of headbutts, but Robbie can't fall because Prodigy's holding him up. Prodigy puts Robbie in a face-lock and signals for the ddt, but Robbie reaches up and pulls the challenger over into a small package. 1! KICKOUT! Prodigy is furious and starts stomping on Robbie. Robbie goes fetal, trying to protect himself from the assault. Prodigy calms a bit, and picks Robbie, nailing a beautiful belly-to-back suplex. Robbie lands hard in the center of the ring. Prodigy goes to the top and flies with a elbow, catching Robbie just below the throat. Prodigy goes for the pin. 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Two close pinfalls! I thought Robbie had him earlier with a surprise move, then I thought Prodigy had the win after that devastating elbowdrop." JH - "Matches never end after those moves. Actually, small packages are quite good match enders. They are the thorn in my side." JG - "You've got enough thorns to make a rose bush." [Robbie rolls over onto his stomach to avoid another pin attempt, but Prodigy hauls him to his feet. Prodigy connects with a trio of European uppercuts, each moving Robbie backwards until the champ is up against the ropes. Prodigy irish whips Robbie across the ring, but Robbie reverses it, and nails Prodigy with a brutal gourdbuster. Prodigy stands up quickly as if he was trying to no-sell, but wobbles a bit. Robbie rebounds off the ropes and runs at Prodigy, but Prodigy backbodydrops him to the floor.] JH - "Robbie looks like he's drunk in there!" JG - "If he is, which I doubt, Prodigy just knocked some sense into him with that back body drop." JH - "He is drunk! Don't you know what Rock Bottom is? A bar!" [Robbie crawls back up on the ring apron and is met by Prodigy. Prodigy grabs Robbie's throat and lifts him into the air and over the top ropes. Robbie comes crashing down hard in the ring from Prodigy choke-slam. Prodigy covers. 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JH - "What a powerful chokeslam! Did you see how Prodigy knelt down towards the mat before he hit it? That signals extra power." JG - "Aren't you in a sarcastic move, Jack. I thought it was a well executed chokeslam, especially for somebody of Prodigy's size." JH - "Forgot. We've got Mr. Tiny over here." JG - "There's nothing wrong with smaller wrestlers. Prodigy ranks in between big and small." [Robbie is bleeding a little from the backdrop to the floor. Prodigy stays on him with European uppercuts. Robbie tries to fight back, but Prodigy stays on him. Prodigy attempts the ddt again, but this time Robbie can't counter. He goes down hard. Prodigy covers again. 1! 2! Prodigy pulls Robbie off the mat to break the count and shakes his head, refusing the three-count.] JG - "What the hell?" JH - "I knew he was stupid! He left a perfectly good opportunity for a title!" JG - "That could cost him later on in the match." [Instead, he picks up Robbie and sets him up for the Last Ride In. Just before Prodigy can hit it, Robbie slides out, grabs Prodigy in a Tiger Suplex. 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JH - "I would have laughed until I pissed if Robbie got the pin there. Do you know how bad that would've been for Prodigy?" JG - "Pretty bad." JH - "Pretty bad? That would've been freakin' horrible! I would kill myself if that happened to me!" [Prodigy and Robbie stumble to their feet. Robbie bodyslams Prodigy into the middle of the ring, then runs to opposite corner. As Prodigy staggers to his feet, Robbie is winding up for a Lariat clothesline. Prodigy turns around just in time to see Robbie run at him, ducks under and nails the Last Ride In. Prodigy covers. 1! 2! 3!] JG - "He got him! New TV champ!" JH - "The prestigious title finds a new home. I can't believe how much this affects my life." JG - "Next match is a good one. We've got former tag champs against possible future tag champs." JH - "Possible? For sure, baby!" JG - "Let's go to Ed for the intros." -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
