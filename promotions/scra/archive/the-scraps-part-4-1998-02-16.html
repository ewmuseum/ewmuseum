[SCRA] The Scraps [4/6] (16 February 1998)

___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 5 ) | | \ | | Siberian Express vs. German Eagles / |___ | | | | Writer: Greg A. (_________________________________________________________________________ [Comrades Inc. walks out to the announce desk. Comrade Jonah Ulanov get Jack Hazard's seat in exchange for some money. Zangief, and Johnstone stand around ringside.] JG - "Uh, hello. Jack?" [Hazard just waves his hand as he exits the arena, hands full of money." EJ - "Ladies & Gentlemen first of all from Russia at a total combined weight of 598 lbs "Russian Bear" Andrei Bolotov, and Ivan Dragovich, the Siberian Express." [Gudonov comes out, and walks straight to the ring ignoring the fans only giving them a few taunts about 10 feet behind comes Ivan Dragovich. Dragovich has no expression on his face. Then another 5 feet behind The Bear comes out. They all enter the ring. On the outside Comrade Zangief flexes for the Siberian Express.] EJ - "And their opponents from Germany at a total combined weight of 480 lbs former SCRA World Tag Team Champions Chris, and Joseph Eagle, The German Eagles!" [The German National Anthemn plays over the PA. They are wearing black, red, and gold shorts. With Shoulder flags. The fans give them some boos, and some cheers. The Comrades begin to laugh as they see The German Eagles come out. The Eagles enter the ring, and our prepared to wrestle.] DING! DING! DING! JG - "The Comrades find The German Eagles rather amusing don't they?" CJU - "Can you blame them. The German Eagles buttnikhov kissers, and they fight for Germany. Don't you find them funny?" JG - "Actually I don't." [Chris Eagle starts the match for The German Eagles while Ivan Dragovich starts it for the Express. Dragovich, and Eagle lock up, and Chris is sent into the ropes. He leapfrogs Dragovich, and then stops himself. Dragovich turns around, and gets hit with a hurricanrana. Chris Eagle rolls him up, and the ref counts... 1! 2! KICKOUT!. Eagle whips him into the corner and runs at him with a clothesline. He tags out to Joseph who comes in, and nails Ivan with a European Uppercut. He then sends him into the ropes, and hits Dragovich with a dropkick.] JG - "Devastating dropkick from the Chris Eagle, but he's not going for the cover." CJU - "First that dropkick not devastating it veak. Second just rookie mistake. Comrades Inc. never make that mistake." JG - "(sigh) I'm sure they wouldn't Comrade Jonah." CJU - "That Mr. Comrade Jonah to you!" (Joseph Eagle is on the top rope, and jumps down with a Flying Senton. Joseph Eagle then picks him up, and whips him into the corner then follows him with a lariat. Joseph then tags out to Chris who comes in, and executes a double underhook suplex. Then gets up, and goes to the top rope coming down with a somersault legdrop. Dragovich manages to get to his feet, and nails Chris with a right hand, and another, and another. Dragovich whips him into the Eagles corner where Chris tags out to Joseph. Dragovich doesn't realize it, and grabs Chris, but Chris sneaks behind, and Joseph is already behind. They execute the Double German Suplex. Joseph covers him.. 1! 2! But Bolotov makes the save.] JG - "Dragovich is taking a beating in that ring, and there's no telling what will happen next." CJU - "Yes it always same headscissor next." JG - "I seriously doubt..." [Dragovich gets up, and whips Joseph into the ropes, and is caught with a flying headscissors. Then Joseph whips Dragovich into the ropes, and goes for a dropkick, but Dragovich hangs onto the ropes. Joseph falls head first to the match, and is hurt. Dragovich tags out to Bolotov. "The Russian Bear" comes in, and powerslams Joseph. Joseph rolls back to his corner, and tags out to Chris. Chris comes in, and goes for a cross body, but Bolotov catches him in mid air, and powerslams him. Then goes into the ropes, and comes off with a kneedrop. Bolotov kneels over Chris Eagle, and begins to pound away as the ref warns Bolotov. Bolotov yells at the ref who quickly backs away.] JG - "He's abusing the ref isn't that illegal." CJU - "It not against law, but it not nice thing to do." JG - "I know that, but just forget it." CJU - "Vell Siberian Express fellow Soviets ve cheer them on." JG - "Ever think of taking English." CJU - "Make fun of my accent vell Comrade Johnstone vorking on English, and then he vill help us learn." [Bolotov picks up Chris Eagle, and nails him with an elbow to the back which sends him down. "The Russian Bear" then lifts Chris Eagle back up, and executes a backbreaker. Bolotov then tags out to Dragovich who goes for a bodyslam, but Chris counters with an inside cradle, and the ref counts 1! KICKOUT! Dragovich gets back to his feet, whips Eagle into the corner, and follows him in with a lariat. Then Chris Eagle staggers out of the corner, and into a belly-to-belly suplex. Dragovich then executes a kneedrop, but Chris Eagle moves out of the way. Chris on his knees, and nails him with a clothesline. Both men are down, and their shoulders are on the mat..1! 2! 3! 4! 5! Then Dragovich rolls his arm over onto Chris, and gets a pin... 1! 2! IN THE ROPES!] JG - "It was almost over for the German Eagles right there." CJU - "Even the German Eagles can get out of puny cover like that. They try show off for Comrades! Comrades laughing their heads off at way Eagles wrestler. HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!" JG - "Well they aren't former tag team champions for nothing." CJU - "Your right they tag team champions because Comrades Inc. not here to beat them up over weekend." JG - "If you say so. WOW!!! Did you see that armdrag he almost sent Chris all the way across the ring." [After the armdrag Dragovich lifts Chris up, and goes for a piledriver. However Chris manages to backbody drop him, but Dragovich lands on his feet and takes him over with a backslide. Still Chris manages to slip over, and counter with a swinging neckbreaker.] JG - "The men both showing off there ability to counter their opponents moves." CJU - "No ability just luck." [Chris Eagle goes to the top rope, and executes a huge frog splash, and goes for the pin. The ref counts... 1! 2!...] CJU - " It over he got him." JG - " It's not over yet he managed to get his shoulder up." CJU - " Go Drago!!" [Drago rolls over to his corner where he tags in Bolotov who comes in with an axehandle, but Chris Eagle immediately tags out to Joseph who meets Bolotov with a drop kick, and then a leg drop. Bolotov gets up, but is met with a spinning reverse kick. However Bolotov doesn't feel it, and nails Eagle with a right hand. Then goes for a gorilla press, but Joseph slips behind him. Then Bolotov executes a jawbreaker, and then lifts up Joseph for a powerbomb. Joseph tries to reverse it into a hurricanrana, but to no avail. Bolotov then executes a huge elbow smash right across the chest of Joseph. Joseph gets up, and whips him into the ropes then leap frogs him, and tags in Chris. Joseph leapfrogs Bolotov again, and then Chris runs at him with a crossbody, but Bolotov ducks, and Chris hits the mat. Bolotov goes to the corner, and runs at them with a double clothesline.] JG - "This has been a grueling match so far, and there is still a whole lot of fight left in these 4 men. You can see it in their eyes." CJU - "Plenty fight in Express, but in Eagles there none." JG - "If there isn't how come they almost mounted a huge offense?" CJU - "Keyvord _ALMOST_." (Chris jumps onto the second rope, and then missile drop kicks Bolotov. Then jumps springboards off of the bottom rope, and executes an elbow drop across the stomach of "The Russian Bear". Bolotov crawls to his corner, and makes the tag. Chris Eagle does the same. Dragovich, and Joseph hit each other with a clothesline, but Joseph lands on top of Drago! 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "And the brawl continues. Neither team is giving up they are simply refusing to." CJU - "Eagles just had their tongues slapped around too much you can see them trying to say "I CAN'T TAKE ANYMORE" JG - "I'm really starting to see the good qualities in Jack Hazard." CJU - "You no like me? I no like you either." [Dragovich crawls to his feet, and begins to stomp Joseph. He lifts Joseph up, and executes a gutwrench suplex. Then goes into the ropes, and executes a legdrop, but Joseph rolls out of the way. Dragovich whips Joseph into the corner, and he tags out to Chris, but Drago doesn't realize it, and tags out to Bolotov who grabs Joseph in a bear hug, and Chris comes from behind, and kicks Bolotov in the heel sending him crashing to the mat. Then Dragovich nails Chris with an elbowdrop.] JG - "All 4 men are in the ring." CJU - "That illegal." JG - "Yeah, but the fans are loving every moment of it, and that's what counts." [The ref tries to take control of the match. The Eagles are being pummeled now that this has turned into a brawl, but Joseph pulls off a headscissors takeover on Dragovich. While Bolotov in the opposite side of the ring is pummeling Chris.] JG - "Joseph has just taken Dragovich down, and now he's walking over to Bolotov." CJU - "The ref should DQ everyone." JG - "I don't think that would be a wise idea." CJU - "Why not?" JG - "That would only make them angry." CJU - "It illegal to hit referee. That mean they get fined, and suspended." JG - "You are right, but I don't think they'd care right now." [Joseph Eagle flies off the top rope with a dropkick to the back of Bolotov's head. Chris then takes Bolotov over with a hurricanrana, and the ref counts... 1! 2! But Gudonov gets up on the apron to distract the ref. Then Dragovich and Joseph leave the ring.] JG - "They almost had them there. You know managers should really be banned from ringside." CJU - "You know nothing about managing." [Then Comrade Zangief gets up on the arpon and enters the ring then drives the Russian flag into the back of Chris Eagle's head. Then he powerslams him down to the mat. Zangief quickly rolls out of the ring. Gudonov jumps down As Dragovich tells Bolotov to come in who puts Joseph in a bearhug, and then Dragovich comes off the ropes, and executes a running clothesline.] JG - "IRON CURTAIN, and it is over." 1! 2! 3! CJU - "Now Comrades go to work. HEHEHE" [Then Comrade Johnstone gets into the ring followed by Comrade Zangief. Zangief executes a side backbreaker on Jospeh Eagle. Chris tries to save him, but us thrown out of the ring by Johnstone. Zangief then sets Joseph up for a piledriver, and goes to the top rope. He leaps off in the air, and executes the Russian Spinning Pildriver. The referee calls for the bell, but Comrade Johnstone doesn't care as he lifts up Joseph Eagle, and locks him in the Ukranian Bear Clutch squeezing the life out of him.] JG - "This is disgusting the Eagles never tried to hurt anybody." CJU - "German Eagles not nice men. Comrade Inc. nice men." JG - "If they are why are they doing this?" CJU - "To teach Eagles lesson." [Comrade Johnstone still has the Bear Clutch on Jospeh Eagle as Chris Eagle tries to come in again he whacks Johnstone in the back of the head with a double axehandle, but Johnstone only makes the hold tighter. Johnstone now shakes Joseph around while still keeping him locked in the hold a crack is heard, and Comrade Jonah Ulanov leaves the announce desk, and gets Johnstone to break the hold. Then Chris comes in, and bounces off the ropes going for a double lariat, but is met with the Soviet Slam. Suddenly, Sengir sprints out of the back!] JG - "It's Sengir! He's trying to save his German friends!" [Sengir dives into the ring, fighting the larger Russians. He clears the ring of Comrades Inc., then looks down towards Joseph Eagle.] JG - "Joseph Eagle is hurt. That Bear Clutch may have broken his ribs." [Hazard walks back to ringside, seeing his seat is open again.] JH - "What's going on...hey! The German's hurt!" JG - "Yes, he is." JH - "Finally! I thought those morons would just keep coming back. Is that...Sengir?!" JG - "Yup." JH - "No! I thought we reached our maximum German level with the German Eagles. I don't know how much I Germanity I can take!" JG - "Well, you may not have to take Joseph Eagle. He looks hurt." JH - "Good riddance to him. Is Sengir Chris' brother? You know, Sengir Eagle?" JG - "No." JH - "Oh. That's good." [As paramedics wheel Joseph out of the ring, Keith Spencer walks in.] KS - "Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of last week's battle royal to gain the title shot at Spring Spectacle against the World Champ at that time, Gangsta Impasse!" [John Drake enters the arena first, pumping up the crowd by raising the roof and offering various tribal yells. Gangsta soon follows, wearing his boxing robe, hood pulled over his face. He begins shadow boxing his way down the aisle, following closely behind John Drake, who is still raising the roof. Drake hops onto the ring apron and holds open the ropes as Gangsta walks in.] KS - "Gangsta Impasse, how does it feel to become the first winner of the Ice Cage match." GI - "Well, ye know Spence, i' I said I didn't care bout de shot at de title, I'd be lyin'. But, de thing I'm most proud uv i' de fact dat I'm gon' face Ken Curtis..." KS - "What if he's not the champ at that time?" GI - "I already proo'd dat I can beat dem otha buoys who gettin' dey-re shot, so I eitha gon' be champ, or git Ken Curtis. Ken Curtis would be betta, but I gon take what I can git. I already started trainin' fer Curtis an' if he ain't champ at Spectacle, he gon be de first te git de shot." KS - "Well, Ken Curtis prooved to be tough in your earlier battle, are you sure you can beat him?" GI - "Do I think I can beat im? Spence, I know I can beat his sorray cowboy ass! But jest fer ya, I gon' take a survey. All de playaz in de house who think I can beat Curtis, MAKE SOME NOISE!" [Huge pop] KS - "Well, onto another topic, uh..." GI - "Hold up, I gots te address sumptin. Te make shuah I gots Curtis at Spectacle, I address'd someone te make shuah ye keep on te de title." KS - "Who's that?" GI - "I think you buoys in de rumble, especially Hardin know who I'm talkin' bout. Ma buoy, de leada uv de jWo, Stree Smawtz. He gon' make shuah dat Ken Curtis holds onte dat belt." KS - "Now, about your match with J.W. Hardin tonight, what's your strategy?" [With the sound of Hardin's name, the crowd erupts in a chorus of boos.] GI - "I ain't gon' tell you. I mean Hardin probably backstage, keepin' focused on dis lil' TV in dat locka room, flippin' back an forth between dis an' de All American Porn, Rambone, so, I ain't gon tell ya. But, look fer some kitchen pots, and spaghetti, cause when I finish wit' Hardin, I still be mighty hungry." KS - "Well, what do you want to prove to Hardin tonight?" GI - "Ye know, all dat stuff bout beatin' a legend one on one, it kinda gets te ya head. But, I wanna make Gangsta Impasse a household name an' de first step is beatin' de legend himself, Jay Dubba-ya Hardin!" KS - "I think there's one last thing everyone wants to know, and that's why did you burn down Ken Curtis' barn?" GI - "One reason, an' one reason only. Te take frum him, what he took frum me...ma pride. He put so much time inte dat bawn an' now it's all gone. All it takes i' one strike uv a match fer it all te go up in flames. Ye knew I waz comin' fer ya an' ye took me too lightly. Well, maybe now you'll take me more serious." [Suddenly, an all-too familiar voice booms over the arena speakers...] CKC - "IMPASSE!" [Impasse looks up, as "Cowboy" Ken Curtis' face flashes onto the giant television screen above the wrestler's entrance. Curtis is in the parking lot outside the arena...] CKC - "Ya know, sweetheart, nobody can accuse me o' bein' a devoutly religious man, but there's one little passage in the Old Testement that I hold dear t' m' heart... ... an that's 'an eye for eye, an' a tooth for a tooth'." [Curtis turns as the camera pulls back, revealing a tow-truck backing up to Impasse's Mercedes Benz. Impasse's eyes widen in fright as Curtis motions the tow-truck back...] CKC - "Bring it on back, fella!" [Impasse yells something incoherent at the screen, as the driver exits the truck and hooks to tow-line up to the back of Impasse's Benz. Curtis turns the to camera with a wicked grin, then back to the truck. The driver jumps back into the truck and begins to lift the Benz' back-end up with the tow-line. The Benz' alarm goes off, lights flashing and horn blowing. Curtis turns to face the camera once again...] CKC - "Later, _homey_." [The scene fades to black as Impasse bolts from the ring, up the ramp, and through the entrance curtains!] JG - "Good Lord! Curtis has hijacked Impasse's car!" JH - "And he just got it back from the repairs that had to be done from he and Curtis' 'Barn vs Benz' match! I wonder what the Cowboy has in mind!?" JG - "Whatever it is, you can bet it doesn't involve Turtle Wax!" JH - "Can't we get a camera back there!?" JG - "I think... can we get...?" [Suddenly, the scene changes to the back, where Impasse is racing through the hall! We can hear him mumbling something as he races toward the parking lot, and he suddenly burst through a door out into the lot, just as the tow-truck is pulling away, Curtis following behind in his black Ford F-150! Curtis leans out the window of his truck and gives Impasse the finger as he drives off! Impasse chases the trucks for a moment, then stops as they pull away into the distance. The camera cuts back to the announcer's table...] JG - "Well, something tells me that's the last we're going to see of Impasse's car. This thing has spun way out of control!" JH - "Oh, I think we'll see Impasse's car at least one more time. I don't think Curtis would deprive us of seeing what he has planned for that shiny new Benz!" JG - "You may be right, Jack! You may just be right!" JH - "May? I'm always right!" JG - "We've reached one of the night's more exciting matches. High-flyer Derek Mota is matched up against Sean Watts, arguably one of the league's better technical wrestlers." JH - "Yea, turn on the VCR and walk out of the room 'cuz this one's guaranteed to be as dull as they come. Buncha aerialists...ruining our sport." JG - "Derek Mota is one of the most exciting men in our sport. He's an ex-IIWF cruiserweight champion, something not too many others can claim on their resume." JH - "That little Canadian bastard? Where's he get off calling himself a cruiserweight?"
