[SCRA] Winter Carnival [2/4] (8 February 1998)

8 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens in the back of a mansion there is a regulation ringing floating in the middle of a gigantic pool. The ring has in the place where the emblem of the organization usually goes a picture of the Russian and Ukraine flags. Then Comrade Johnstone, Comrade Zangief, and Comrade Jonah Ulanov walk out, and swim to the ring.] CJU - "Peoples of SCRA lend me your ears. I here not to harm you. Not to hurt you, but to help save your lives. That right Comrade Johnstone?" CJ - "That right Comrade Jonah!" CJU - "You see even Comrade Johnstone know I trying to help SCRA loserikhovs. Now I trying to warn you of Comrades Inc. They very good tag team. They standing right next to me. They Comrade Zangief, and Comrade Johnstone. They very big men, but they not mean men. They come here to try and make lot of money. Not to hurt people, but in order to make lot of money they have to hurt people. That right Comrade Johnstone?" CJ - "That right Comrade Jonah! [They decide not to swim after all and come out of the water, and put in a flaot. They all get into the float.] CJU - "Now Comrade Zangief like to send message to people in TV land...." CJ - "That right Comrade Jonah!" CJU - "I didn't say "That right Comrade Johnstone?" CJ - "Oh sorry go ahead." CJU - "That right Comrade Jonhnstone?" CJ - "That right Comrade Jonah!" CZ - "That right fellow Comrades. I vant to send message to current Vorld tag team shampions German Eagles. I part German although I mostly Russian, and me like you. Still I going to have to beat you up because you not with Comrades Inc., and if you not with Comrades Inc. then you no good to any of use here at all. And if you no good you deserve to get beat up." [He sticks a cigar in his mouth, and then stands up at the bow of the float, and the float tips over.] ALL - "AYE! AYE! AYE! AYE!" [The float tips over, and then after a few seconds Comrade Johnstone, and Comrade Jonah resurface.] CJU - "That was close, but now we all here. That right Comrade Johnstone?" CJ - "That wrong Comrade Jonah. No 3rd Comrade." CJU - "Then where he went. Oh no remember his hair so heavy he can't swim. Aye. We must go and get him. That right Comrade Johnstone?" CJ - "Just go get him Comrade Jonah!" [They go under, and then return with Comrade Zangief above the water.] CJ - "I guesd finish what Comrade Zangief was saying. We going to beat up everyone in SCRA. There will be no one safe from us. We will work our way up through rankings, and get win over top talent like Proctorian Gaul, and Cold Vinter. Except we agree vith Cold Vinter. US do suck. Unfrotunately if you go there you can make lot of money. We thought that only place you can make lot of money til SCRA hire us. That Right Comrade Jonah?" CJU - "That right Comra...hey that my line! CJ - "Sorry!" CJU - "That Right Comrade Johnstone?" CJ - "That right Comrade Jonah!" [Comrade Jonah pulls out a lighter. He clicks it once.] CZ - "Hey I remember I didn't have chlorine so I filled pool with gasoline." CJU - "You vhat!?" [Comrade Jonah sees the flame go up, and a fire starts in the pool. The ring goes up in flames as they try to swim for the dock. They begin to cry.] CZ - "My beautiful ring! I can't bare to watch. Less go get training thingers, and train for big match next week." [The camera fades to black.] JG - "Comrades Inc. certainly seems ready to take their spot among the top tag teams in SCRA today." JH - "That's gonna be tough to do. We've got such a deep and talented tag roster, the Comrades will have a tough time building their way up the ladder." JG - "I know you were being sarcastic. You can just say it out and out, we've got a weak tag roster. It looked promising in the end of 1997, with the signings of promising tag teams Phi Theta Kappa, Cold Winter and the Siberian Express. They added to the already competitive Activists from Hell, Praetorian Guard, German Eagles and the Pros. But, the Activists left, Cold Winter and PTK just didn't do anything and the Siberian Express are just now getting into the groove." JH - "All that makes for a battle royal with two competing tag teams, the Siberian Express and the ever dangerous Pros." JG - "Speaking of the Express, we have these comments before we hit the ring." [Camera opens inside the semi-plush office of Siberian Express frontman, Alexander Gudonov. The room is empty and quiet. All the lights are off, all the furniture is neatly in it's place, and Gudonov's desk is clean and clear. The camera pans around the office once and then starts to shake as the cameraman starts to take the camera off it's tri-pod. The voice of the cameraman can be heard as he anfrily takes the equipment apart.] Cameraman - "Six-thirty! They said six thirty! SO here I am at six forty five....all by myself! I wish they would have said something, I had to miss Wheel of Fortune for this! Man is Grey gonna hear about this! This is the last time I......" [His sentence is cut off as a person jiggles the lock on the door to the office. A surprised and muffled voice can be heard on the other side as the lock moves around without effort. The doorknob turns and the heavy oak door opens to reveal the frame of Alexander Gudonov. Behind him stand the two principle members of the Siberian Express, Ivan Dragovich and The Russian Bear each, carrying two or more suitcases. Once in the three drop the cases and stare at the camera man.] AG - "What the hell are you doing here!? Who in the world did you get into this office...the door was locked?!" Cameraman - "Your secretary let me in." AG - "Why! We weren't here!" Cameraman - "Well she said you were expected back soon from Russia, and I had to get in to get your comments on the upcoming Tag Team battle Royal at Winter Carnival." AG - "Comments eh? Well I saw that display the rest of the "teams" put forth on the Preview. I just about laughed my way off of my airplane seat! But first things first sonny boy. (He looks out his window to the sunny, and mild winter that most of Ontario currently enjoys.) This ain't winter! We just came back from Siberia, and this place is summer compared to it. Got it?!" Cameraman - "Sorry sir. Can I get your comments now?" AG - "Sure, but what's there to say really? None of the teams here are even taking the Express seriously. The only team that did were those idiots, Pros and they only mentioned in their whining that we were higher on the ranks than they were. Oh WAH WAH WAH!!! Cry for me Pros! You're even more pathetic then we first thought. Our loss to you is an even greater insult to us. An insult that won't go on much longer. None of the other teams really warrant a few word from me. The Praetorian Guard were wrestled like pre-schoolers in our match. I'm surprised they had the belts for so long with those skills. We finished them off with ease, and will do so again. The German Eagles are wrestlings version of Wally and the Beaver. Pathetic, useless nice guys that don't belong in the ring. They should be prancing around a sound stage with that stupid purple dinosaur! Go ahead Eagles.....keep kissing everyones butts in this league! Oh you're so good, you're the best, you'll do so well in the Battle Royal. BAH! (Spits on the floor). Keep kissing butts Eagles, and the Siberian Express will be happy to show you where it gets you in wrestling." [He regains his composure, straightens his tie, and continues.] AG - "And last but not least, The Activists from Suburbia. Another pathetic and overrated team. The only team that seems to be making any waves at all in the royal. And why? For what! They don't deserve the buzz they're getting and come the royal, We'll show you why! Now get out of here! We have to unpack!" [FADE] __ __ _______________________________________________________________ | |__| | / ] Tag Team Battle Royal | | | |/ / Competitors: Siberian Express, Cold Winter | | | / / Phi Theta Kappa, The Pros | ` ' \_ Weenie Machines \ / | Writer: Alex G. \_/\_/ \____|____________________________________________________________ [The entire Jaspard Clan stands in the middle of the ring with Ed, including the recovering Ted.] EJ - "I'm here with the entire Jaspard family to showcase that we are truly the best tag team in the world." [Ted nods his head, not able to speak yet.] EJ - "The best announcing tag team in the world." [The entire Jaspard family nods their head with Ed.] EJ - "So, Grey, Hazard, we're coming to getcha." JH - "I'm frightened, Grey. Your big shot brother might fire me and give Ed the job!" JG - "Actually, Ed does make a significant amount less than you. And, he has a following. Don't put the change out of the question." JH - "Stop stirring rumours." EJ - "This matchup is the tag team battle royal! The final three competitors will compete tomorrow night in a triangle match to decide when they receive their title shots! Coming down first..." JH - "Oh geez, this is gonna be a long intro." EJ - "The Siberian Express!" [The Express begin to walk out, soon followed by the Pros. It's apparent that the intros won't be as long as before due to time restrictions.] EJ - "The Pros! Cold Winter! Phi Theta Kappa! The Weenie Machines!" [The two jobbers begin walking down the aisle, revelling in their newly found spotlight.] JH - "How'd they get in?" JG - "The German Eagles used their championship pulling power to convince the bookers that their countrymen, the Weenie Machines, deserved to be in there as much as anybody." [The teams hit the ring, ready to fight. The Praetorian Guard runs down to the ring and slides in, starting to clean house.] JH - "Ha! Look at them go!" JG - "What are they doing here? They already fought tonight!" JH - "It's all non-tag champs. I believe the Praetorian Guard aren't tag champs." JG - "Then the Activists can compete!" JH - "They've already got their title shot." DING! DING! DING! [Bill Baer attacks both members of the Weenie Machines, instantly eliminating Oscar. Mayer, however, is able to slide away into the corner and hide from the monster Baer. Jabasco moves in and attacks Ivan Dragovich, the two exchanging punches. Bolotov moves at Cold Winter, attacking both men with one clubbing blow. Grant attacks Allen Jenkins, along with Tulkas Naugrim. Baer moves towards Brad Thompson as Mayer still sits in the corner, hiding out of fear.] JH - "They're just going nuts! The entire tag division is throwing punches all at once! I love it!" JG - "Look at poor Mayer. Without his brother in there, he looks a little lost." JH - "That's what you get when playing with the big boys. Or, in his case, the big dogs." JG - "I think Baer is looking to eat Mayer!" [Along with Tulkas Naugrim, Grant lifts Jenkins over the top.] JG - "Allen Jenkins is gone!" JH - "Was he ever really in?" [But, shortly after, Naugrim turns on Grant and clotheslines him in the back of the head, sending Grant over the top.] JH - "Grant's gone! Way to go Tulky!" [Naugrim moves towards his partner, working on Thompson some more. Bolotov continues with clubbing blows to the head, sending Blizzard moving back to the turnbuckle and Icelander staggering back. Mayer takes this opportunity to get up and hit a belly to back suplex on Icelander. Icelander gets back up and runs at Mayer, but is back body dropped over the top by the hot dog boy!] JG - "Mayer eliminated Icelander! Holy cow!" JH - "Was he ever in it, either?" JG - "I don't really think so. In fact, if the Praetorian Guard didn't come in, this probably would have been a normal tag match between the Pros and Siberian Express after a while." JH - "That's pathetic." JG - "I know." [Icelander's partner, Blizzard, also has a bad fate as Andrei Bolotov continues to pound him in the turnbuckle with massive shoulderblocks. Dragovich and Jabasco continue to fight until they bump into the Praetorian Guard working over Thompson. Baer runs at the two and drops them with a double clothesline. This allows Thompson to get a quick chop to the throat on Naugrim and get up, running at Baer. Baer, however, is able to move around and execute a powerslam on the frat boy. Jabasco moves over and executes a sweep kick, dropping Baer. He picks up Thompson and runs towards the ropes, trying to toss Thompson out. But, Dragovich dives in and grabs Jabasco's leg, stopping him from eliminating Thompson. Naugrim picks up where Jabasco left off, attempting to throw out Thompson, but Dragovich again dives in and stops him.] JG - "They're fighting over who can eliminated Brad Thompson!" JH - "That's going to be a better fight than the elimination itself. Hell, that's going to be a better fight than this whole damn battle royal." [Baer gets up, but is hit from behind by Jabasco. Jabasco leaps onto Baer's stomach and starts unleashing blows right beside Dragovich who has Naugrim in an ankle lock submission. Mayer gets up from his crouch and runs at Thompson, dropkicking him from behind and over the top!] JH - "That's two! Mayer has a chance to win this thing!" JG - "I can't believe what I'm seeing here. Mayer comes in, brother already eliminated, virtually no wrestling skills or guts, yet he's eliminated two men and is doing better than Phi Theta Kappa..." [As Grey ends his sentence, Bolotov has Blizzard on the top turnbuckle and gets the final push over the top.] JG - "And Cold Winter!" JH - "We're already down to six men! How pathetic is that?" JG - "I never said the tag division was strong. It could certainly use a good influx of talent." [Bolotov turns his attention to the rest of the ring, seeing his partner with an ankle lock on Naugrim and Jabasco throwing punches at Baer. He turns to the corner beside him and sees Mayer crouched in the fetal position.] JG - "The Russian Baer's going after Mayer! What a horrible sight!" JH - "He's going to eat Mayer! Mayer's going to win the battle royal by being inside Bolotov's stomach!" JG - "True. I guess if Bolotov wins, Mayer will also win due to not being eliminated." [Bolotov moves into the corner and bends to get Mayer, but Mayer lifts his hand in a Three Stooges-like eye poke. He crawls in between Bolotov's legs around the ring until he bumps into Naugrim. Bolotov follows Mayer and goes to grab Mayer, but Mayer tumbles out of the way, bringing Bolotov's hand down on Naugrim's forehead in the claw position.] JH - "The Siberian Express have Tulkas Naugrim double teamed. This can't be good for the former tag champ." JG - "Keen observation, Jack." JH - "Hey, you're not the only one. You've got the easy job. You try doing colour commentary for this league. Sometimes it's a pretty pathetic sight." JG - "That would make it easier to joke about, wouldn't it?" [Dragovich releases the hold, allowing his partner to execute the claw with even more force. Bolotov actually lifts Naugrim off the ground and slams him to the mat. Dragovich uses the opportunity to attack Jabasco, as Jabasco did to him earlier. He tackles Jabasco to the mat, then starts to unleash a series of punches to the head. Baer gets up soon after, looking towards his partner who is getting pummeled on the mat. Baer pushes Bolotov and the two lock eyes.] JH - "It's Bear vs. Baer!" JG - "Note that those are spelled differently for those of you who do not have the closed caption function on." [Baer and Bolotov start throwing monstrous punches at each other as Naugrim recoves elsewhere. Mayer, again the opportunistic one, moves in and grabs Naugrim, executing...] JG - "Weenification!" JH - "Huh?" JG - "Shooter McNutty's got the Nutter Cutter, the Weenie Machines have Weenification. That was one half of it." JH - "I guess one half isn't enough to put out Naugrim. I don't even think the whole thig could do it." [Naugrim gets up a little sluggish, but is soon tossed over the top by a running clothesline by Mayer!] JG - "He eliminated the former tag champ! He eliminated Tulkas Naugrim!" JH - "Holy cow. Mayer eliminating somebody like Phi Theta Kappa or Cold Winter is nothing big, but Naugrim, that's pretty damn huge. This guy's gonna be a star...except he's German." [Baer drops Bolotov with a thunderous punch, then moves towards Mayer who starts backpedelling again. Baer follows Mayer around the ring only to bump into Bolotov, again, who delivers a vicious low blow from the mat. Bolotov gets up and grabs Mayer by the head, ready to toss him out for the embarassment from before. He lifts Mayer up in a gorilla press, holding him there for the pictures, then tosses Mayer over the top a few feet into the snow bank beside the ring.] JH - "Faceplant, dude." JG - "The streak has ended. Andrei Bolotov eliminated Mayer. It's a sad, sad day." JH - "It was inevitable, though." [After tossing Mayer over, Bolotov turns to Baer, who is still clutching his midsection, and runs at him with a lariat. Baer drops to the mat, allowing Bolotov to switch his attention to his partner who is currently attacking Jabasco with a figure four. Bolotov starts dropping elbows to the chest of Jabasco, furthering the attack. Baer gets up and thinks of dropping an elbow on Jabasco's chest, too, but changes his mind and drops the fist to the head of Dragovich, breaking the figure four. Bolotov moves towards Baer, but is met with a rake to the eyes. Jabasco rolls under the ropes for a breather.] JH - "He's gone! He eliminated himself! What a moron!" JG - "It's an over the top battle royal. Jabasco is still in it." -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
