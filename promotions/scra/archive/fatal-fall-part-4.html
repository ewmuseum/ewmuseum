[SCRA] Fatal Fall, Part 4

13 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
An SCRA Production http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html ____________________________________________________________________________ |_____ Match 4 | |___ Lifetaker vs. X-Factor | Writer: OB |_________________________________________________________________________ [The camera pans out of the announcer's booth and focuses on the ring, zooming in to make visible a shot of Tommy Fetters holding a mic rather limply in one of his puffy, fat hands. He puts it to his lips and begins to speak.] TF - "This match is scheduled for one fall and will decide the World Championship! Our first competitor, the first champion in a Mr. Grey-run league, the mysterious X-Factor!" [Man in the Box plays as X-Factor walks to the ring, rubbing his chest hair and shaking his head at fans who attempt to acknowledge. He enters the ring, runs his hands through his unkempt hair, and nods at Mr. Fetters. Fetters, coated in sweat, begins to speak again. However, he collapses in center ring. The fans scream. X-Factor rushes over to aid the fallen announcer. Lifetaker's music, which has begun to play, is suddenly cut off.] JG - "Oh my god! Jack, hold the booth! I've got to go check on Tommy!" JH (Groggilly) - "What? Huh? Something happened? Uh, okay." [Grey is seen arriving at the ring, followed by Killer Compton and a stream of jobbers (including the Wondertown Wonders and Rodney Smith). The medical staff rushes out with stretchers. The whole crowd now falls silent. Fetters' chest rises and falls in heavy, dramatic bursts. The emotion reaches a crescendo as the staff attempts to regulate Fetters' heartbeat before moving him. Jon Grey is joined by Alex Grey, Nathan Jones, and Oliver Bateman at ringside. The executive staff, attired formally, has a look of great concern on their faces.] JH (nervously) - "My god. I don't know what to say. For once, I don't know what to say. This isn't how Fatal Fall was to go. My god, my god. I'm cutting down to Alex, who's talking to the executive council down at ringside." [The camera is switched to a shot of Jon Grey with the president and his two VPs.] JG - "What's the situation, Alex? This was totally unforeseen. How are we going to cope?" AG - "The card will go on, Jon. The fans demand it. Mr. Fetters would wish it so. The final intro will be done by a new addition to our staff, Tommy "Charon Wraith" Gregory." JG - "Have they re-started his heart?" AG - "I don't think he actually quit breathing. From what I heard, Mr. Fetters has an arythmic heartbeat. The heartbeat grew irregular and caused this." OB - "Reports indicate that he will be okay." NJ (points) - "Look, they're moving him! He's giving a weak thumbs-up! Mr. Fetters is okay!" [As Mr. Fetters is carted off, Tommy Gregory, a squarely-built man dressed in an official SCRA blazer, rushes to the ring. He waits until the massive circus in the ring fades away, then begins the final portion of the intros. Jon Grey, who has rejoined Jack Hazard in the announcer's booth, begins to speak.] JG - "This was the most scary thing I've ever seen. It's horrible that this had to happen during Fatal Fall, an event that is most certainly being watched by Fetters' family." JH - "I concur, Grey. For once, we agree." [Gregory begins the introductions.] TG - "X-Factor is opposed by the SCRA's current World Champion! He bills himself as the "Little Big Man" and is one of the top 20 wrestlers in the world right now! Here he is, Lifetaker!" [I Am the Walrus by the Beatles plays as Lifetaker, clad in a LG-monogrammed jacket, jogs out to the ring. He shakes hands with a few of the fans but keeps his gaze forward. He enters the ring and begins to stretch.] JG - "Here we are, Jack. Any predictions?" JH - "Not right now, Grey. I'm not really thinking. This whole thing's just too weird." [The bell rings and Lifetaker locks up with X-Factor. The league's first champ leaps out to an early advantage, hammering Lifetaker with a series of elbowdrops and chops. He whips the world champ into the ropes and levels him with a clothesline.] JG - "X-Factor jumping out to an early lead! All that time in the ring must have given some time to ruminate over his strategy?" JH - "Ruminate? Oh, I'm too worn out to say anything." JG - "Jack, you see almost kind tonight. Were you moved by what just happened?" JH - "Uh...no, of course. I'm as hard as stone, a true rock." [X-Factor also appears rocklike in the ring as he continues to dominate Lifetaker with strength moves like powerbombs and bodyslams. However, the moves appear to do little damage to 'Taker, as he uses an opening to nail X-Factor with a fisherman's suplex. He nails a senton and follows it up by working X-Factor on the mat with an armbar.] JG - "The fortunes change quickly. Lifetaker's using his athleticism to take over the match." [Lifetaker displays incredible strength, gorilla-pressing X-Factor onto the mat. X-Factor leaps up and grabs his back, waddling around the ring in visible pain. The referee asks him if he is able to continue. X-Factor nods his head. The match continues.] JG - "What power! Lifetaker's a great wrestler. He truly has strength and speed, in a small package." [Lifetaker continues to pound X-Factor, using a beautiful chickenwing suplex to knock the wind out of his opponent's sails. X-Factor regains his feet, albeit slowly, and is levelled quickly by a Japanese legsweep. Lifetaker yanks X-Factor back up and hammers him with an European uppercut.] JG - "X-Factor's looking rusty." JH - "This guy's on the wane in the league. He jobs tag team matches and somehow gets a world title match. What gives?" [Lifetaker jacks up X-Factor with another brutal uppercut, opening a slight cut under Factor's eyes. The referee again asks Factor if he wishes to continue. X-Factor nods his head. As the ref backs away, Lifetaker slams into X-Factor with a brutal football tackle. X-Factor falls to the ground lifelessly. Lifetaker sticks X-Factor's neck in the ropes and hits neckbreaker on ther ring apron. X-Factor collapses to the ground.] JG - "This one's all over, all over except for the shouting." JH - "What domination! I have newfound respect for Lifetaker. I always knew X-Factor was a puke, and Lifetaker took it to him. This wasn't a main event. It took that Tommy Fetters thing to make it that, bless them man's soul. I wish him well." JG - "Jack, you're sounding kind." [Lifetaker nails an elbowdrop from the top rope, then puts X-Factor in the Leg Lock. The match ends when X-Factor submits. Lifetaker gets up, kicks his prone foe, then shouts "Your career is over!". He walks away, jumping out of the ring and grabbing his title from the scorer's table. He then jogs to the dressing room. Half way back, though, a masked man jumps out from the crowd.] JG - "Phantom is attacking Lifetaker!" JH - "Come on! We have had enough monstrosities for one night." [Phantom hits Lifetaker in the back. He grabs the World Title and walks to the curtain. Before he leaves, Phantom takes off his mask and places it on the belt.] JH - "What was that? Some sort of cryptic message?" JG - "Who knows. Lifetaker is going to get up sore after that attack, though. Wait until he sees the mask on the belt." [X-Factor is escorted out by several security guards as the fans boo his lackluster performance. He is pelted by foam cups and bags of popcorn as he makes his way towards the dressing room.] JG - "What a match! What an event! What a spectacle! What a champion that Lifetaker is!" JH - "What an over "thing" the X-Factor gimmick is! I'm glad your he's gone. It's too bad it took so long. He'll never be anything again." JG - "Never say never, Jack. This is the SCRA. Anything can happen here." JH - "Never heard that before, some animal foundation I think." JG - "Yes, I see. We still have more action for you, folks! The three ring royal rumble is still coming up. This promises to be one of the most exciting events in wrestling history!" JH - "Save all that crap. Is Gregory still out here?" JG - "He is waiting in the ring." JH - "He won't be able to fill Tommy's shoes, but go on Tommy. Wait a minute, both their names are Tommy?" JG - "An odd coincidence. Here are the inroductions." ____________________________________________________________________________ |_____ Match 5 | |___ Three Ring Royal Rumbles | Writer: AG |_________________________________________________________________________ TF - "Here it is, your main event! The rules are as follows. Twenty-six SCRA wrestlers will begin the event. One will start out in ring 1 and a new one will come out every minute and a half. Once the wrestler is eliminated from ring 1, they move onto ring 2 where they continue fighting. Should they be eliminated one more time, they move onto ring 3 for a final chance. After being eliminated from ring 3, their night is over. These monstrous tasks are all for shots at SCRA gold! The winner of ring 1 gets a title shot at the biggest pay per view of the year, Rasslefest! The winner of ring 2 gets a North American title shot also at Rasslefest! The winner of the third ring will get a Scraps title shot on October 20!" "Ladies and gentleman, let's start this rumble!" [Crowd goes wild. They all look towards the curtain to see who will be coming down.] JG - "It's always exciting, not knowing who is coming and all." JH - "Don't you have the order?" JG - "Not with me. It is more exciting this way!" [Dance of the Sugar Plum Fairies as the large frame of Eddy "Flap" Jacks lumbers to the ring.] JG - "Eddy "Flap" Jacks gets the unlucky number one. He will have to wrestle all other 25 men to get the win." JH - "His fat will keep him in. Nobody could lift that mass over the top." [Blind comes on the speakers, the crowd sees Sublime rush down to the ring.] JG - "Sublime and Eddy "Flap" Jacks are the first two men! Looks like Sublime has one goal in mind, brawl." JH - "That's not his goal. His goal is not to get squashed by Eddy. That would be embarassing, being flat in the middle of the ring as all the other wrestlers come in and out." [Eddy catches Sublime as he was getting up with a hard right. Sublime comes back with another one. Both men exchange punches.] JG - "If they keep fighting at this pace, this match could get ugly, fast." JH - "It already was ugly. I never noticed before, but Sublime's little acrobatic body is a disgusting site." [Eddy stumbles back to the ropes. Sublime walks in and dropkicks Jacks, almost sending him to the outside. Sublime picks up Jacks and throws him into the ropes. Sublime hits a clothesline. Sublime goes off the ropes and hits an elbowdrop. He picks up Jacks and whips him into the turnbuckle. Sublime runs in, but Jacks moves out of the way, sending Sublime diving into the ringpost.] JG - "Sublime almost eliminated himself there." JH - "Isn't this an over the top? That would have been through the middle." [Jacks drags Sublime back in. He hits an elbowdrop coming off the ropes. Jacks then picks up Sublime and bodyslams him in the middle of the ring. Jacks runs off the ropes and comes back with a big splash.] JH - "Look at the vertical on that! JG - "He truely got some good height there. I am sure Sublime wasn't too pleased when he saw the giant mass of Eddy "Flap" Jacks towering over him and about to fall." JH - "He wasn't too pleased? Hmmmm. I never thought of it that way, Grey. I always thought people liked getting squished and having a spatula lift them up!" [Jacks showboats to the crowd who respond in a negative manner. He picks up Sublime and throws him into the ropes. Jacks nails Sublime with a lariat. Jacks bends over to pick up Sublime and sets Sublime up for a suplex as music comes on the speakers.] JG - "What music is that?" [Down Rodeo becomes the recognizable song as "The Advocate" Joshua Baker. walks down the aisle with his partner beside him. They have something hidden behind their back, but nobody can tell what it is, yet.] JH - "When can we tell Mr. Activist that only one person is allowed in the ring." JG - "I don't think he wants in the ring." [The two Activists hit ringside and make no motion of getting in the ring. Jacks stares at the two as they stop at a side of the ring and pull out signs.] JG - "They are protesting!" JH - "What the hell are they protesting? What the hell can they protest about in this fuc..." JG - "I think I just proved their point. Censorship." [As the Activists walk around the ring, firing up the fans, Jacks forgets about Sublime. Sublime crawls up behind Jacks and hits a back suplex.] JG - "A great display of power by Sublime. He seems to be occupied by the two Activists, though." JH - "Can't we get security out here?" JG - "It is freedom of expression. They can do...oh my!" JH - "What was that for?" JG - "I don't know. Sublime just stuck both his middle fingers up at the Activists. Yet, they seem happy! What are they saying?" JH - "Express yourself." What kind of morons are these guys?" [Sublime turns around and sees Jacks getting up. He bends to pick up Jacks, but Jacks nails Sublime with a right. Jacks gets to his feet and clotheslines Sublime. He goes off the ropes and nails a leg drop, sending the mass of his fat on top of Sublime. Jacks gets back up slowly, moving back towards the Activists.] JG - "They are having some heated words, but none that they haven't exchanged before. Monday night was pretty much the worst thing that could have happened, both teams exploding." JH - "If these morons are out here, though, Lucifer will be alone in the ring!" [Jacks trys grabbing at Baker's signs. Baker moves it out of the way. Jacks looks distraught and moves back to the center of the ring. He picks up Sublime and hits a vertical suplex. He pushes Sublime over to the corner and goes to the ropes facing the entrance ramp.] JG - "Looks like Eddy is waiting for the next competitor." JH - "Looks like it is Eddy's friend. Eddy is holding the ropes open!" [Dance of the Sugar Plum Fairies once again plays throughout the stadium as Tulkas "The Troll" Naugrim walks out, looking at Eddy. The two begin laughing at the two Activists who start chanting even louder.] JH - "Come on, Tulkas. Shut those morons up!" [Tulkas enters the ring. He and Eddy pick up Sublime and hit a double suplex. They don't pay attention to Sublime, but move straight over to the Activist's side.] JG - "Once again, these two Pit of Damnation members causing trouble for the two House of Pancakes members." [Tulkas swipes at a sign, but misses. He grins and tries again. This time, as Baker's sign is brought back, Rage nails Tulkas with his own sign, sending Tulkas falling back to the mat. Jacks yells for a referee and screams frantically at the Activists. The Activists simply grin and look at their broken sign. Rage looks sad, but Baker goes under the ring and pulls out a sign.] JG - "Always bring a spare!" JH - "How can you joke at a time like this? Where are the referee's when you need them?" JG - "I heard there was a referee convention this weekend. Most of our referees are there. The ones who have already had matches, left soon after their matches." JH - "So, it is a free for all in the ring?" JG - "Basically." JH - "Do the wrestlers know?" JG - "Nope." JH - "Can I tell them?" JG - "Does it matter what I say?" [Hazard tries yelling into the ring, but his voice is muffled by the ever rising chants of the Activists. The crowd seem to get behind them now, which disturbs Jacks even more. He picks up Sublime and throws him into the ropes, nailing a powerslam on the way back. Jacks laughes at the fallen wrestler and goes to his comrade.] JG - "Jacks is trying to shake some life into Tulkas. Look at Tulkas, he looks out cold." JH - "There are movements! Look, he just twitched his arm." JG - "Eddy just moved his arm." JH - "Great! Working together as a team, they are bound to get Tulkas up." [Jacks once again picks up Sublime, this time hitting a vertical suplex. He walks back to his partner and again tries to shake some life into him. Jacks walks away from his partner as Hero blares through the stadium.] JG - "Green Knight! Green Knight is joining the mayhem." JH - "Just toss him out so we can get onto more important people." [Green Knight hits the ring and goes straight for Tulkas. Jacks blocks his way. The two exchange some blows. Knight dropkicks Jacks and picks up Tulkas. He whips him into the ropes and nails a back body drop. Jacks gets up and goes at Knight, again. Knight, again, hits a dropkick.] JG - "Green Knight is fired up tonight! He floored Eddy "Flap" Jacks twice and almost sent Tulkas "The Troll" Naugrim out with that back body drop." [Knight picks up Tulkas and whips him into the turnbuckle. He follows in with a clothesline. Knight starts hitting Tulkas in the midsection with punches. He then starts chopping at Tulkas, almost sending him over the top with each blow. Jacks gets up and sees his partner in trouble. He also sees Sublime squirming. Jacks picks up Sublime and flings him over the top rope.] JG - "First man eliminated! Sublime is the first man eliminated!" [Sublime wearily gets up, walking into the second ring. He sits on the mat, waiting for somebody else to be eliminated.]
