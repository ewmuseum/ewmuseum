[SCRA] The Scraps [5/5] (9 February 1998)

9 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Steel Cage World Title Shot Match ) | | \ | | "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin, Gangsta Impasse, / | | | | | "The Perfectionist" Nik Nyce, Chase Hunter, Prodigy / |___ | | | | Writer: Tyler D. (__________________________________________________________________________ [A Cage is towering over the ring, as Ed Jaspard stands in the ring, wearing a prisoners outfit.] EJ - " - "This match is scheduled for many pins, bumps, bruises, bloodied faces, and squares. There is a 60 minute time limit to boot with an anything goes rule. Plus, oh yeah, we gots ourselves a cage covered in snow. Why in the hell is there snow. The head honchos are pretty weird. Ah well, coming down the aisle, weighing in at a monsterous 276 pounds, accompanied by Black Kat, the new TV Title holder, Prodigy." ["Bullet With Butterfly Wings" by the Smashing Pumpkins blares over the speakers a Kat and Prodigy make their way to the ring. The fans start to cheer, and both of them slap all of the outstretched arms. Prodigy leaps into the ring, and Jaspard begins the next introduction.] EJ - ""The next man coming down to the ring is Hunter, Chase Hunter, weighing in at a skimpy 220 pounds." ["Push" by Moist blares through the speakers, as Hunter comes down the aisle. Half way down the aisle, Hunter stops, looks around, and starts to do back hand springs until he reaches the ring, then he leap frogs into the arena, and a laser show goes off behind of him. The fans start to cheer loudly and, suddenly, Hunter looks at them and pulls some sort of cord, his chest inflating rapidly.] JG - "What the hell?" JH - "It's his little muscle suit!" [As Hunter flexes his "muscles" Jimi Hendrix's guitar is heard throughout the stadium, as Nik Nyce enters the arena] JH - "Ummm, if Ed doesn't want to get his but kicked, he might want to shut up." EJ - ""And his opponent, weighing in at 234 pounds, "The Perfectionist", yeah right, like he's that great, Nik Nyce." [Nik Nyce quickly runs down to the ring, and hops in.] JG - "You know, for once Hazard, I'm agreeing with you on this one, making fun of three wrestlers is not a good idea, especially when they are all in the ring ready to kill everybody." EJ - ""Our next competitor, the man with a speech impediment, weighing in at a grotesque 280 lbs, Gangsta Impasse." [California Love cuts off Jimi Hendrix, and GI slowly makes his way to the ring. The Notorious Killaz lead the way, and stop at the end of the aisle as GI enters the ring.] EJ - ""Oh man, this is gonna be cool. Not only do we have Gangsta, Nyce, Hunter, and Prodigy beating each others brains out, but we also get the privilege of watching this man, the big man, the Outlaw, J. W. Hardin. Hey, what do you suppose the J. W. stands for?" JH - "Good question. What does the J. W. stand for?" JG - "John Wesley." [Jaspard exits the ring as "Outlaw Blues" comes on the speakers. Hardin moves slowly to the ring, ignoring all of the fans, and as he enters the ring, the cage begins to lower as all five men stare at one another.] JG - "As the cage is being lowered, let me explain the rules to you. There is a steel cage that will enclose the entire ring, standing at a tall 15 feet, and having a square radius of 15 feet on each side. There is one door, which will be padlocked. No way in, no way out, except by pinfall or by submission. Anything goes." JH - "You forgot the most important thing, Grey." JG - "Oh, and what might that be?" JH - "Snowball fights. See, there is tonnes of snow resting atop that cage, and if anyone hits the cage, then the snow could fall off. I can't wait to see a good snowball fight." JG - "Yes, well, the cage is lowered, Tommy Gregory is checking around to make sure no one is under the ring, and now he's padlocking the door, holding onto the key." [After Gregory shuts and locks the door, the bell rings, and all five men stand still. DING! DING! DING! Nyce makes the first move, and dives across at Hunter, hitting him with a football tackle and begins to pound away, although the suit absorbs most of the attack. Prodigy, feeling gutsy, elbows Hardin across the jaw, and kicks GI in the stomach, following with a DDT. Nyce picks up Hunter and hits a quick suplex, as Prodigy picks up Hardin, and goes for a piledriver, but Hardin blocks the attempt and sends Prodigy flying with a back body drop. Gangsta gets up and taps Hardin on the shoulder. Hardin turns around to be met in mid air with a standing still drop kick.] JH - "I told you this would be good, now all that we need is some popcorn." JG - "Tons of action to start things off here. All of the competitors are taking no chances, making no allies, and relying on no one but themselves." [Nyce lifts up Hunter and puts him in the corner, and begins to hit hard chops across the chest, again the effect is very little due to the giant suit. Nyce attempts to get some damage by Irish whipping Hunter into the other corner, Hunter reverses it, and sends Nyce flying into Prodigy who was getting up from the back body drop. Gangsta grabs Hardin and suplex's him. Hunter then grabs Nyce and hits a springboard drop kick. Prodigy gets up and places Hardin in a camel clutch. Gangsta, however, grabs Prodigy and throws him off. Gangsta grabs Hardin and begins to throw punches when Prodigy comes running back at Gangsta and hits a body block, which didn't even move Impasse.] JG - "Oh oh, that doesn't look good for Prodigy." JH - "Tell me Grey, when does it ever look good for a competitor when he hits a powerful move that doesn't do any affect?" [Impasse turns around, grabs Prodigy by the throat and choke slams him to the ground. Hunter picks up Nyce and hits a savate kick, followed with a jumping DDT. Impasse then picks up Prodigy and hits an atomic drop, sending Prodigy into Hardin, who lifts Prodigy up, and hits a backbreaker. Hardin goes for the cover on Prodigy... 1! 2! But Hunter breaks the cover by Hardin.] JG - "We almost had our first elimination here, lucky for Prodigy that Hunter stepped in." JH - "Yeah, but if I know Hardin, that's going to piss him off, and Hunter is gonna get his face planted in the floor." [Hunter kicks Hardin on the mat, then leaps in the air and comes crashing down hard with his big "muscle" suit. The suit deflates a little from the blow.] JH - "The ship is sinking!" [Hunter grabs Hardin and throws him into the ropes, setting Hardin up for a clothesline. Gangsta then grabs Hunter from the back and hits a fisherman's carry. Prodigy grabs Gangsta and hits a European Uppercut. Prodigy goes to follow it with a punch, but Gangsta blocks it and headbutts Prodigy. Impasse then grabs Prodigy and hits a piledriver. Nyce finally gets up, and starts to brawl with Hunter. Nyce throws Hunter into the ropes, but Hunter grabs onto the ropes. Nyce takes off and hits a clothesline that sends both men out of the ring. The suit deflates completely.] JH - "Woohoo! Maybe now we'll see the blood that everybody wants to see." JG - "Jack, I'm sure nobody wants to see blood." JH - "Are you kidding? Society thrives on action, blood, guts, and big hooters. It's in our genes." [Hardin has gotten up, and as he does, Gangsta irish whips Prodigy in Hardin's direction. Hardin notices this, and quickly bends over, hitting a back body drop that sends Prodigy flying. Hunter starts to pick up Nyce, when Nyce hits a low blow that sends Hunter to the ground screaming in pain.] JH - "Oh billy, that has to hurt!" JG - "Who's Billy?" JH - "Haven't you ever seen Cable Guy with Jim Carey?" JG - "No." JH - "You haven't? Rent it, it's amazing." [Hardin lifts up Prodigy and throws him into the ropes hitting a powerslam. Hardin then moves out of the way as Impasse climbs to the top rope and hits a suicide planche. Impasse goes for the cover... 1! Hardin jumps on Gangsta! 2! 3!] JH - "Two people pinned Prodigy! What a loser!" JG - "That's not embarassing. If somebody pinned two others, then that's embarassing. You got it mixed up." [Hardin lifts up Impasse and begins to brawl with him immediately as Prodigy starts to walk out of the ring. As he's waiting for the door to be unlocked, Nyce throws Hunter into Prodigy, squishing the two men into the cage, and causing a bit of snow to fall down.] JH - "Talk about adding insault to injury." JG - "Prodigy is the first man to have to leave the ring, we're now down to four men. This should be very interesting, no more double teamings until the next man is pinned." [Hunter picks up Nyce over his shoulder and uses him as a battering ram, ramming him head first into the cage, cutting open Nyce's face. Impasse and Hardin are still throwing lefts and rights, when Impasse ducks under one of Hardin's punches and hits an awesome uppercut, followed by a few closed-fisted punches, that drive Hardin to the ropes. Impasse grabs Hardin and hits a snapsuplex. Hunter grabs Nyce and piledrives him into the floor. Hunter starts climbing the side of the cage, gets about half way up, then takes off with an elbowdrop right across the forehead of Nik Nyce! The cut widens.] JH - "More snow, more blood, what more could a guy ask for?" JG - "I dunno Jack, but you never quite know what can happen here. Oh my god, did you just see that?" JH - "That's what I wanted to see." JG - "Impasse just gorilla pressed Hardin to the outside, throwing him onto both Hunter and Nyce. What a power move by Gangsta, pressing the 300 plus pounds of Hardin. Unbelieveable." [With Hardin landing on the two men, and slightly hitting the cage, more snow has fallen down. Hardin slowly gets up, see's Impasse, who, at the right moment, dives over the top rope, landing on Hardin, sending him into the cage, with more snow falling down. Impasse starts to throw lefts and rights at Hardin's face, when Hunter throws a snowball, hitting Gangsta.] JG - "Now children, we're suppose to be wrestlers, not little kids at a playground. Stop throwing snow." JH - "Are you nuts Grey? I love a good snowball fight." [Impasse looks at Hunter, grabs some snow and starts to wail it at Hunter. Hardin slowly gets up, looks at Nyce, who looks as dumbfounded as Hardin is, and both men clothesline their foes. Hardin throws Impasse into the ring, followed by Nyce throwing Hunter in. Hardin climbs back into the ring and hits a clothesline on Gangsta. Nyce grabs Hunter, and hits a Belly to Belly suplex. Nyce then grabs Hunter and pushes him into the corner. Hardin does the same to Impasse, as both wrestles irish whip their enemies into each other, knocking them out.] JG - "Mid air collosion. That had to hurt." JH - "Nah, they're just playing. Let's get another snowball fight going." [Hardin grabs Impasse and hits a powerbomb. Nyce hits a Nyceplex, 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Almost had another pin here. Somebody is bound to give soon." JH - "What would happen if nobody got anymore pins after Prodigy?" JG - "Well, they would keep wrestling until there was a cover." JH - "You mean we could be here all night? C'mon Hardin, kick some butt in there, I don't want to be here with Grey all night." [Nyce picks up Hunter and throws him into the ropes, going for a back body drop, but Hunter sees it and hit's a swinging neckbreaker. Hardin starts to choke Impasse with his boot. Hardin then grabs Impasse and kicks him in the balls, followed by a reverse neckbreaker.] JG - "Oy, that had to hurt." JH - "That was just plain cruel. I can understand squaring somebody out of desperation, but when it's just for the fun of it, c'mon, that's mean." JG - "That's Hardin." [Hunter slowly gets up and grabs Nyce, placing him in an armbar. Hunter quickly then hits a gutbuster. He climbs to the top of the turnbuckle, to the side of the cage, then to the roof. Hunter grabs the roof of the cage and starts swinging himself across. Hardin grabs the fallen Impasse and throws him into the ropes, hitting a forearm smash. Hunter grabs the CHIA PET out of his shorts.] JG - "What's he doing with that thing?" JH - "I dunno, but nothing good can come from this." [Hardin grabs Impasse and hits a DDT. Hardin goes for the cover... 1! 2! KICKOUT! Hardin picks up Impasse again and hits a neckbreaker. Hardin then places Impasse into a camel clutch. Hunter grabs Nyce, and hits a suplex. Hunter then grabs the Chia Pet™ and climbs to the top rope. As Nyce makes his way to his feet, Hunter drops off the top of the cage and nails Nyce with the Chia Pet right in the head! Hunter goes for the cover... 1! 2! 3!] JG - "My god!!! There's moss, dirt and hard clay everywhere!!" JH - "Like I said, when you mix in a Chia Pet™ with four wrestlers, nothing good can happen." JG - "Nyce needs medical attention, we better get him out of there." [Gregory quickly unlocks the door, and lets Nyce out, with doctors rushing him out of the ring. Meanwhile, Hardin has broken the camel clutch off of Impasse. Hardin goes for the cover... 1! 2! Hunter breaks the cover by landing an elbow on Hardin. Hunter grabs Hardin and throws him out of the ring, landing against the cage, causing more snow to fall, this time in the middle of the ring. Hunter then climbs up to the top rope and hits a moonsault onto Gangsta. Hunter again climbs to the top rope, gives a little flex, and then Hardin grabs Hunter and hauls him down from the top rope onto the floor.] JG - "Ouch. That had to hurt." JH - "Hunter has to learn, only go for the same move once. Twice never ever works." [Hardin grabs Hunter and powerbombs him. Hardin then takes Hunter and throws him into the cage. Hardin grabs Hunter and throws him into the next side of the cage, followed by the other side and the other side, then throws Hunter back into the ring.] JH - "Hunter, meet cage, cage Hunter. Now that you two are aquainted, let's have some fun." [Impasse has slowly gotten up and sees Hunter. Impasse grabs Hunter and throws him into the corner, and follows him in with an elbow, but Hunter moves out of the way in the nick of time, only to be met by a clothesline from Hardin. Hardin lifts up Hunter and goes for a powerbomb, but Hunter uses the momentum and flies over Hardin, dragging Hardin into a small package... 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Almost had Hardin out of this match." JH - "Won't happen. You'll never see Hardin lose to a small package. Why? Well, number one, he's over 300 pounds. Number two, he's not small, and Number three, it just doesn't happen." [Hunter slowly gets up and meets Hardin with a few chops to the chest. Hunter then throws Hardin into the ropes and hits a fierce clothesline, which drops Hardin. Hunter then grabs Impasse and throws him into the ropes, going for a back body drop. Gangsta sees it all the way and quickly reverses it into a swinging neckbreaker. Gangsta then grabs Hardin, throws him off the ropes, bounces off the ropes, and both men hit a double clothesline, with Hardin landing on Hunter's head, then rolling off.] JG - "All three men are down and out." JH - "Are there countouts? And if so, what happens if all three men are counted out?" JG - "Don't worry, no countouts. Remember, no rules in this ice cage match." JH - "Yeah, yeah, just trying to get the viewers thinking a little, that's all." [All three men are still out, until Hunter slowly starts to move around, followed by Gangsta and Hardin. Hunter quickly gets to his feet first, but stumbles back to a corner. He sees Hardin, and goes to hit a body block, but Hardin hits a snap mare. Impasse follows it with an elbow on Hunter. Impasse then grabs Hunter and throws him into the ropes. Both Hardin and Impasse go for a double clothesline, but Hunter ducks under it. On the return, Hunter hits each man simultaneously with a clothesline, which does absolutely nothing to them. Impasse grabs Hunter and Irish whips him into the corner, followed by Hardin who hits a body block, then Impasse who hits a clothesline sending Hunter over the top rope and to the outside.] JG - "Looks like Impasse and Hardin are taking it to Hunter." JH - "You don't say Grey. What was your first clue? The attempted double clothesline?" JG - "Shut up Jack, we don't need your sarcasm here." [Impasse slides out of the ring, followed by Hardin. Impasse goes to pick up Hunter, and Hunter throws snow into Impasses' eyes, causing Gangsta to stagger back. Hunter then hits a standing dropkick onto Hardin, and then grabs Gangsta and hits a running bulldog. Hunter turns around, but only to be met with a clothesline from Hardin. Hardin then picks up Hunter and drops him square on his head onto the steel steps. Impasse gets up, Hardin throws Hunter at Gangsta, and Impasse ducks down, sending Hunter up in mid air, landing down on his back.] JH - "Attention all Air Hunter passengers, we will be crash landing any moment now." JG - "Hunter took major hight on that back body drop, flying higher then the top rope, then landing down hard on his back." [Gangsta throws Hunter into the ring, as both Hardin and Impasse get in the ring. Impasse climbs up to the top rope and hits an awesome frankensteiner. Impasse then moves off of Hunter, as Hardin hits a suicide plancha from the top rope onto Hunter. Gangsta then throws Hardin off of Hunter, picks up Hunter, and hits the Gangsta Impact. Impasse goes for the cover... 1! 2! 3!] JG - "Our third competitor is now gone, however, he isn't moving from the ring. He's just lying there." JH - "Well, at least Hardin kicked him out of the ring so he won't get in the way." [Gregory drags Hunter out of the cage, as both Hardin and Impasse stare down at each other. Both men get toe to toe and stare down at one another, waiting for someone to flinch. The hulking 6'10", 353lb Hardin looks down slightly at the smaller, but muscular 6'8" 280 lb Impasse. The stare down gets so close that only a piece of paper could fit between the two men. Hardin ends it first with a closed fisted punch to Impasses stomach. Impasse returns it with a closed punch uppercut. Hardin then starts to throw punches wildly, with Impasse returning everyone. It has turned into a basic hockey fight with the two powerhouses brawling like mad men.] JH - "Alright. This is damn action. Give credit where credit is due. I never thought that Gangsta Impasse could stand toe to toe with Hardin, but he did." JG - "Great brawling skills being demonstrated by both men." [Hardin eventually backs Impasse into the corner, but Impasse punches his way out of the corner. Impasse stops the onsault of punches when he drops back off the ropes and hits a shoulder block that just stands up both men. Hardin looks at Impasse, grins, and the stare down starts over again, with a catch, Hardin raises his hand for a test of strength.] JH - "You know it was a good fist fight when their knuckles are bleeding. Look at Hardin and Gangsta, their knuckles are either swollen or bleeding. Now a test of strength. God I love this game." JG - "A test of strength that will be tough for both men. Hardin definetly has the mass, but Impasse has the strength. Let's see what happens." [Impasse takes a step back and enters into the test of strength. Hardin lowers his other hand, and as Gangsta goes to meet the other hand, Hardin kicks Impasse in the stomach, followed by a reverse neckbreaker.] JG - "I knew that one of the two men would cheat." JH - "What, you expect a fair fight." [Hardin lifts up Impasse and body slams him. Hardin then throws Impasse into the ropes and hits a belly to belly suplex. Hardin then drops down for the cover... 1! 2! KICKOUT! Hardin then places Impasse into a figure four leg lock, and Gangsta screams in agony. However, Street Smarts comes running down to the ring. He stands at the cage, looking on, watching Hardin place more and more pressure on Impasse.] JH - "What in the hell is he doing down here?" JG - "I have no idea. He can't get in the ring, so why Street Smarts is down here is beyond me." [Hardin breaks the lock and picks up Impasse and hits a pile driver. Hardin then stomps on Impasses knee repeatedly. Hardin picks up Impasse and suplex's him. Hardin then throws Gangsta off the ropes and hits a back body drop. Hardin bounces off the ropes and hits an elbow drop. Hardin then grabs Gangsta and goes to Irish whip him into the corner, but Gangsta reverses it and sends Hardin into the opposite corner, hitting the ref.] JG - "It's bad enough that it's an all out brawl, but now, with the ref out cold, who's gonna count the pin?" JH - "Maybe that's why Street Smarts is here." [Impasse grabs Hardin and throws him to the outside. Impasse then runs off the ropes and hits a flying swan dive, crashing down hard on Hardin. Impasse gets up and picks up Hardin, hitting a suplex. Impasse then lifts up the protective covering over the ground and grabs Hardin, hitting a tombstone on the concrete.] JG - "This is maddness. Impasse could seriously injure, if not kill Hardin." JH - "Yeah. I know I like to watch the blood fly, but this is getting out of hand." [Impasse then looks under the ring, see's some loose wire, grabs it, and begins to strangle Hardin with it. Hardin reaches out in exasperation, and in desperation, punches Impasse right in the nuts, dropping Gangsta. Both men are out.] JG - "Oy, that had to hurt." JH - "Desperate times call for desperate measures." [Hardin slowly gets up only to be met with a chop to the chest from Impasse. Impasse grabs Hardin and Irish whips him into the steel cage. Impasse runs at Hardin, Hardin sees him, and hits a back body drop that sends Gangsta flying into the steel cage, hitting it, then sliding down. Hardin quickly jumps onto Gangsta, and begins pummeling him with punches. Hardin picks up Gangsta onto his shoulder and does his torpedo run into the steel cage. Hardin goes to do it again, but Impasse slides off of Hardin, and rams Hardin into the cage. Gangsta then climbs up to the top rope and hits an elbow drop onto Hardin.] JG - "Great action here in the ice cage. This is unbelieveable." JH - "In the great words of the comic book superhero Gambit, Life don't get much better than this." [Gangsta gets up and throws Hardin into the ring. By this time, the ref has slowly awaken. Impasse picks up Hardin and hits a Powerbomb. Impasse then bounces off the ropes and hits a leg drop, and goes for the cover... 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JH - "C'mon ref, get in the game, that was the slowest count ever." JG - "Well, it's Impasse's fault for knocking the ref out." JH - "So what. The ref's in this league shouldn't get knocked out that quick. That could have been the match right there." [Impasse starts to yell at the ref for a faster count, giving Hardin enough time to get up and regain his composure. Hardin walks over to Impasse, taps him on the shoulder, and hits a European Uppercut as Gangsta turns around. Hardin then whips Gangsta into the corner and follows in with a flying body block. Hardin climbs up to the second rope and begins to pummel Gangsta with punches. After ten, Hardin stops, gets off, as Gangsta falls off onto the ground.] JG - "Hardin is really taking it to Gangsta Impasse now. Look at those fierce punches." JH - "Well, what do you expect from the big man, to just let Impasse try to win. I don't think so vern." JG - "Who's vern?" JH - "Haven't you ever seen any of the Ernest movies, ya know, Ernest goes to Camp?" JG - "No." JH - "You're hopeless. Go rent them, they're classics." [Hardin picks up Impasse's limp body and hits a powerslam. Hardin goes for the cover... 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Near fall for Hardin." JH - "Ummm, Grey, what's Street Smarts doing?" [Street Smarts has begun to climb to the top of the cage, before anyone can catch him. Gregory starts to climb up after him, but Smarts throws a snowball at Gregory, sending the chief of security back to the ground.] JG - "I have no idea Jack, but nothing good can come from this." [Hardin grabs Impasse and throws him into the ropes, hitting a swinging neckbreaker. Hardin then grabs Gangsta and hits a powerbomb. Hardin grabs Impasse and drags him over to the corner, with Impasse's legs outstretched to the outside. Hardin drops to the outside and places Impasse into a ringpost figure four leg lock!] JH - "Those hurt bad. Especially when you have a 350 pound-plus man trying to rip your legs off." JG - "This does not look good for Impasse. With no count outs or disqualifications, Hardin could sit there all day." [Impasse screams in pain, and uses all his might to break the hold. Hardin gets up and jumps back into the ring. Hardin grabs Impasse's limp body and throws him into the ropes, and hits the Cattle Buster DDT, knocking Impasse out.] JG - "It could be all over with that folks." JH - "Hold on, what's Smarts doing?" [Street Smarts built up a pile of snow during the battles, and is jumping ontop of it. Suddenly it gives way, and all the snow falls down, dropping on top of Hardin, knocking Hardin out.] JG - "Oh my god. Hardin is half covered in the snow. I guess the alliance with Street Smarts has paid off for Gangsta but yet again." JH - "Who ever thought that a jobber could do so much for the league. First he started up the jWo, now he has single handedly knocked out Hardin." [Gangsta slowly begins to get up, notices Hardin buried in the snow, smiles at Street Smarts, grabs Hardin and hits a Helleplex. Gangsta goes for the pin... 1! 2! 3!] JG - "Gangsta Impasse at Spring Spectacle! Right now, it's Ken Curtis against Gangsta Impasse!" JH - "Curtis wants to go now!" [Gangsta runs out of the cage, up the ramp where Curtis stands. He looks around at the crowd, then motions for the belt around his waist. Curtis points to the belt, then Gangsta runs at him.] JG - "They want to go right now! Gangsta wants to get at Curtis right now, just in case Curtis loses the belt! Folks, we've got to go, good night!" -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
