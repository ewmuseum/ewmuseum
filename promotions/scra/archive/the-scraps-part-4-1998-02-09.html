[SCRA] The Scraps [4/5] (9 February 1998)

9 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| World Tag Team Titles Match ) | | \ | | Activists from Hell vs. German Eagles / |___ | | | | Writer: Jon Grey (_________________________________________________________________________ [Jingle Bells comes over the PA as Ed Jaspard comes out from behind the curtain, sporting a toque, snow pants, mittens, boots and a winter coat. He's holding a GT Snowracer in his hand. He slides down the ramp on his snowracer and when he gets to the floorpads, the snowracer gets caught, sending Ed over the front, halfway under the ring. He gets up and rolls in the ring.] EJ - "That was fun. Again! Again!" [Ed removes his toque, leaving his hair in a mess.] EJ - "Well, this matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit and is for the SCRA Tag Team Championships. Coming down the aisle first, from Minneapolis, Minnesota and weighing in at a total combined weight of 520lbs., they are The Activists from Hell." [Down Rodeo comes over the PA as Baker and Rage make their way down to the ring. They hand out fliers to the fans in the first and second rows and as they get to the ring. They get into the ring and throw the remaining fliers to the crowd. Down Rodeo is interrupted by the German National Anthem.] EJ - "And their opponents, from Trier, Germany, they weigh in at 480lbs. and are the current SCRA Tag Team Champions. They are the German Eagles!" [The Eagles come out from behind the curtains, Miss Frank on arm, German flags in hand, waving proudly. Ocki has the belts slung over his shoulder as the Eagles get into the ring. The Eagles escort Miss Frank into the ring and jump over the ropes themselves. Ocki hands the belts to the ref who holds them up.] DING! DING! DING! [Rage and Joseph start off in the ring with a lock up in the center of the ring with Joseph pushes Rage to the floor. Rage staggers to the turnbuckle and gets to his feet. The two lock up again with Joseph getting Rage in a hammerlock. Joseph grabs Rage in a waistlock and attempts a release German suplex, but Rage lands on his feet. Rage clips Joseph's knee and kips up to his feet.] JG - "Rage is fired up tonight." JH - "This match is gonna be boring. I mean the German Eagles against the Activists? I mean gee whiz, these four are pretty boys." JG - "Gee whiz?" [Rage goes off the ropes and hits a springboard legdrop. Rage then jumps off Joseph and picks him up. Rage throws him into the ropes and hits him with a hurricarrana into a victory roll. Joseph kicks out right away and pops up to his feet, getting met by a clothesline. Rage goes over and tags Baker, and the two pick Joseph up. They hit a double vertical suplex as Rage leaves the ring.] JG - "The Activists are showing great teamwork here tonight, they could just come away with the tag titles tonight." JH - "Only to be beaten by the Guard at a later date." [Baker picks Joseph up and hits a cradle suplex. Baker picks Joseph up and throws him into the ropes. He hits a Russian leg sweep, followed by a measured elbow. Baker then climbs to the second turnbuckle and hits a elbow. Baker rolls Joseph over and places him in a chinlock. Joseph makes his way to his feet and elbows his way out. Joseph goes off the ropes, hitting a crossbody block, sending Baker to the mat.] JG - "The Eagles are coming back!" JH - "Shut up." JG - "[mockingly]Shut up. Jeez, you're such a baby." JH - "Look, there's no need for that sarcastic tone." [Baker starts to get up and as he does, Joseph goes into the ropes. Baker falls to the ground, forcing Joseph to jump over him. Baker gets up, only to be met by a hurricarrana. But, Baker was ready and powerbombed Joseph to the mat. Baker then picks Joseph up and as he attempts to throw him into the ropes, Joseph reverses it. Joseph ducks down for a back body drop, but Baker catches him with a quick DDT.] JG - "Got him there." JH - "I don't know who to cheer for. I mean I hate the Eagles, but I love Miss Frank, and I hate the Activists, but I want them to beat on the Eagles. Then Miss Frank will be mine. All mine!" JG - "Enough of your pornographic fantasies, Jack." [Baker then picks Joseph up and places his head between his legs. Baker hooks Joseph's arms and hits a Double Underhook Side slam. Baker tags out to Rage who jumps to the top rope. Rage takes off with a Shooting Star Press and goes for the pin... 1! 2! .....] JH - "Get Ocki off the mat! Hey, I wonder if Ocki likes Hockey." JG - "I don't know, but the ref stopped the count. The Activists could've just won the tag titles and Ocki ruined it. I like the Eagles and all, but Ocki just went too far." JH - "It's okay, Grey, Dr. Hazard is here to help you with your psychological problems." [Rage then tags back out to Baker and twists Joseph's arm. Baker goes off the top with a double axe-handle to the arm. Baker then picks Joseph up and as he attempts a bodyslam, Joseph holds onto Baker's neck and lands on his feet behind Baker. He hits a reverse DDT and falls to the mat.] JG - "It's a race to the partners." JH - "I don't care. This match sucks. Want a donut? We have a variety of flavours. Chocolate Glazed? Apple Fritter? You name it." JG - "What do you work for Tim Horton's now?" [Joseph tags Chris. Chris runs at Baker, hitting him with a clothesline, followed by one to Rage who was sneaking up behind him. As Baker gets up, Chris hits him with a dropkick and as Rage gets to his feet, is met with a hurricarrana. Rage exits the ring as Chris picks up Baker. Chris hits Baker with a 360° Sidewalk Slam.] JG - "Chris is fired up tonight." JH - "You said that about Baker earlier." JG - "Two people can be fired up." [Baker starts to hold his back, but makes his way to his feet. Chris hits Baker with a series of European Uppercuts, which sends him into the turnbuckle. Chris places Baker on the top and climbs there himself. He hits Baker with a hurricarrana and tags out to Joseph.] JG - "This could prove trouble. Remember what happened to the Guard last night." [Chris climbs one turnbuckle while Joseph climbs another. They wait for Baker to get up and when he does, the two hit a double flying drop kick. Chris exits the ring, only to be tagged back in by Joseph. Chris climbs to the top and hits a guillatine legdrop. Chris holds Baker's head and hits him with a standing dropkick. Baker staggers back to the turnbuckle as Chris goes to the opposite turnbuckle, running at Baker, hitting him with a spinning heel kick. Chris ends up outside and grabs Baker's leg, forcing him to fall on his face. Chris pulls Baker's legs back, forcing Baker's legs to go on either side of the ringpost. Chris then wraps Baker's leg around the ringpost in a four.] JG - "He's really applying the pressure here." JH - "You know what? The Eagles could be a team of breakfast. I mean they could be Crust and Juice Bagel. Their finisher could be the HotCakePlex." JG - "It's amazing what your mind can think of when you put it to something." [Baker sneaks around the ring and hits Chris with a clothesline. Joseph however, sees Baker and starts running on the outside of the ring apron, hitting Baker with a hurricarrana, sending him into the guardrail. As Chris starts to get up, Rage moves to the outside. Chris scoops Rage up and bodyslams him on the mats. Chris then goes over to the timekeeper's table and takes the chair, hitting Rage over the head with it. As Joseph gets up, he grabs a chair. The Activists start getting up and as the two brothers attempt to hit the Activists, the two dropkick the chairs, hitting the Eagles in the face with their own chairs.] JG - "Wow, we're getting pretty extreme here, Jack." JH - "This isn't extreme. The line betweem wuss and extreme is when a limb is disabled, or blood starts coming out of their eyes." JG - "That's very insightful." [Rage gets up first, with Baker bodyslamming Chris onto a table. Baker holds Chris' arms as Rage climbs to the top. Joseph clothesline's Baker, which allows Chris to move out of the way just in time....] JG - "Moonsault through the table. Zak Rage went right through the table." JH - "Nobody home." [Chris climbs back in the ring as Joseph pushes Rage back in the ring. Chris climbs to the top and takes off with a big splash. The cover... 1! 2! KICKOUT! Chris tags out to Joseph and picks Rage up. They throw Rage off the ropes and hit a double dropkick. Joseph picks Rage up and again throws him into the ropes. He hits a belly-to-belly suplex and jumps to the second turnbuckle. Joseph takes off and hits a guillatine legdrop and walks over to Chris and makes another tag.] JG - "The Eagles are trying to keep the fresh man in with those quick tags." [The Eagles pick Rage up and hit a double atomic drop. Joseph doesn't leave the ring yet, which forces Baker into the ring. The ref pushes Baker back as the Eagles throw Rage into the ropes again. They hit a double clothesline as Joseph exits the ring. Chris picks Rage up in a fireman's carry, but as he's doing so, Rage hooks Chris' head and uses his momentum to slide over and hit a quick DDT.] JG - "Rage is looking to make a tag here." JH - "Here's Ocki the 'Ocki player again." JG - "Get him off the mat." [As the ref looks to get Ocki the Moselman off the mat, Rage makes the tag. As Baker gets in, Ocki drops and the ref forces Baker to leave, having Rage continue.] JH - "The ref didn't see the tag." [As Rage slowly gets to his feet, the Soviet National Anthemn plays Comrades Inc. walks down to the ring in the middle of the German Eagles match.] JG - "What is this new team doing here?" JH - "I guess to liven up the action!" [Chris sees them, but pays no attention to them. Chris picks Rage up, but Rage punches him in the gut. Comrade Jonah Ulanov gets up onto the top rope, and distracts the referee. Chris is now in the corner as Comrade Johnstone gets on top of the ring apron and smashes a Russian flag over the head of Chris Eagle then Comrade Zangief puts him in the position for a piledriver.] JH - "What's he doing?" JG - "I don't know, but he picked up Chris Eagle with ease there." [Comrade Zangief leaps and spins in the air until he crashes to the mat.] JG - "That must be The Russian Spinning Piledriver. He picked him up with ease that's a 245 lb man in there." JH - "I guess that's why they call him the "Russian Wrecker" eh?" JG - "No witty come backs I'm surprised." [Comrades Inc. then exit the ring as Comrade Jonah Ulanov jumps down from the apron, and waves the Ukraine flag in the air proudly. Comrade Jonah sits down at the announce desk.] CJU - "We kick the German Eeagles buttnikhovs. That Right Comrade Johnstone?" CJ - "That Right Comrade Jonah!" [They leave the announce desk, and go back to the lockers.] JG - "What was that all about?" JH - "I guess they wanted to make a big splash, or should I say, spike in the league." [As Chris Eagle is layed, sprawled out on the mat, Rage drapes his arm over Chris as the ref counts.... 1! 2! 3!] JH - "New Champions!" JG - "I don't think this is the way the Activists would like to win those titles, though." JH - "Oh, really? That's too bad." [The Activists raise their hand in victory, then grab the titles, walking into the crowd and holding them high.] JG - "The people's champions." JH - "Who cares about them. Look at Joseph Eagle run after Comrades Inc.!" JG - "His brother is still laid out, but is recovering from that vicious blow. I guess the German Eagles will have something to say to the comrades." JH - "Who cares. Let's hope they don't." JG - "Well, next up is the big one. This could be the match of the year." JH - "It better be. We've got a cage, that pretty much explains why." JG - "It should be a grueling match for all parties involved." JH - "But, for some a little more grueling." JG - "Folks, we picked up some comments from Chase Hunter earlier tonight. He had some things to talk about before going into this big cage match tonight. Let's go to those pre-recorded comments from Mr. Hunter right now, then go to the ring for the intros." [Scene cuts over to a close up of Chase. We can't see anything but his face. His expression is actually quite calm. There is some kind of air gushing sound that keeps going in small intervals.] CH - "You know, after doing so great last night at Winter Carnival, I feel so pumped. [The camera pans back a bit. We see Chase is in an inflatable muscle suit. He almost seems like he's wearing an inflatable male doll, and looks rather...interesting on him. The camera stops at his midsection. We can still hear that strange air gushing sound somewhere.] CH - "I told you I was going in there prepared. I was ready to hit you guys with everything I had...and did!! Guess those times in the mosh pit really helped. What you don't seem to understand is this. I threw out three guys, and kept going despite that the odds were against me. I survived and I'm going for a little something called a chance for a world title shot. Imagine that, huh? That lucky punk, fresh to the fed, and all ready in yet another main event." [The camera begins to pan away a little bit further. We still can't quite see what the air gushing sound is that keeps going.] CH - "I worked my keester off over in Europe. I've been in scaffold matches, ladder matches, tuxedo matches, Province of Queensbury matches, and even cannibal matches. Man, now that was one nasty type of fight. Good thing I wasn't stuck in that pot, myself. Never mind, you really don't want to hear about it." [The camera finally pans to show Chase completely. A workman is squatting down beside him and using a bicycle pump on Chase's inflatible suit. The workman finally stops and starts walking away. Chase's tone of voice is a bit sarcastic.] CH - "Great job, buddy!! Geez, didn't even cap the suit. How do you expect to keep air in these inflatible muscle suits without capping it? Ah, to be a minimum wager. I knew that all too well." [Chase struts around the room a bit like a body builder. His arm outstretched a bit. He stops strutting to begin posing and flexing with the inflatible muscles.] CH - "Don't worry, I'm not going to stoop so low as to say something like "I'm going to pump you up!!!" So anyway, where was I? Oh yes...that cage match. Well, let's see now, is the McGuyver of professional wrestling going in prepared?" [Chase flexes a bit more.] CH - "You betcha!! I take advantage of every situation, and trust me... this will be no different." [Chase flexes again by slamming his fists together a few times in front of him. He then swings one hand around a few times before cupping his ear.] CH - "So what about the guys that want a piece of the most versatile wrestler of all time? Let's start with our ever unintellectuable, has to talk through a manager, carnavore loving Mr. Gumshoe thing-a-ma-bob. Buddy, you've got a problem with me and my past. I've got a problem with the present and future. You want a piece of me, all ya gotta do is have a wee little talk with our fine prez and set a match up. Now...if you want some kind of specialty match...that can be arranged as well. I've got the perfect one all ready. You let me know...I might be willing to let you know. Oh yeah...by the way, I might want to remind you that I kind of eliminated you last night." [Chase crossing his eyes and flexes again. If it's not obvious now, he's making fun of just about every single possible muscle bound moron out there.] CH - "So who else was there? Ah yes...Nik Nyce. A bad actor? A fake studio? An action movie rip-off? Maybe it was, maybe it wasn't. 'm glad you got a good laugh out of it, though. Keep in mind of something...I plan on following through with all my actions." [Chase starts making his way towards the door. He stops and turns around for a moment.] CH - "As for the most versatile wrestler of all time? Well, I'm off to win me a world title shot if you don't mind." [Chase begins to walk out the door...and gets stuck. The rubber, inflated suit squeaks against the door frame. Chase steps back and steps to the side. He side steps out the door as the scene fades out.] -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
