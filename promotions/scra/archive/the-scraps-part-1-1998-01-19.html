[SCRA] The Scraps [1/4]

19 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
___________________________________________________________________________ ___ _ ___ |_ _|| |_ ___ / __> ___ _ _ ___ ___ ___ | | | . |/ ._> \__ \/ | '| '_><_> || . \<_-< |_| |_|_|\___. <___/\_|_.|_| <___|| _//__/ |_| ** LIVE ** JANUARY 19, 1997 ___________________________________________________________________________ [Song 2 comes over the PA as Nathan Flash emerges from the back, belt slung over his shoulder. He makes it to the ring and stands cross-armed beside Keith Spencer. Fireworks go off behind him as he remains motionless. His beard has grown a bit and looks rather straggely. The music dies down as Spencer turns to Flash.] KS - "Nathan Flash, impressive victory on January 4. At the end of that match, Mr. Destiny showed a great amount of sportsmanship showing the world what wrestling should be like. You don't seem too enthusiastic about your victory as I thought you'd be. Care to explain?" NF - "Care to explain? Why the hell do you think I'd be mad? Is it the kicking out of the league of my friend...my brother? Is it the fact that the SCRA doesn't give a damn about my needs? Is it that the SCRA cut off Johnny Destiny's contract without pay? Why the hell shouldn't I be mad?" KS - "Well, you seemed kind of distraught...." NF - "Yea, I've been distraught! I haven't slept a wink for two weeks! You know what that feels like? Do you know how hard it is to try to touch your friend after you've almost crippled him? Johnny touched me at Rasslefest in a way no other superstar in this or any other God foresaken league has. Not Derek Mota, the man with the most enthusiasm in the league. Not Eddy "Flap" Jacks, the most powerful man in the league. Not even fellow Canadians like Prodigy and again Derek Mota...." KS - "It seems like a very hard time for....." NF - "Don't cut me off, you low-life!" [Huge heel pop] NF - "Johnny..." [Faces the camera] NF - "If you're watching....listen to me. This league has done only bad things for us. This league has torn this family apart. I never wanted to fight you, but President Grey pushed me into it. Johnny...if you're watching, somewhere...anywhere...come home! All I want is my brother back." [Flash looks at the roof, a glow in his eyes. He looks at Spencer with a torn look in his eyes.] NF - "You know Spence...you can take this belt and shove it up your corn shoot. I don't want it!" [Flash throws the belt to the ground as Ed Jaspard runs in and grabs it.] NF - "This league has only caused the Destiny family grief and this belt was the straw that broke the camel's back. So, Keith Spencer...I QUIT!" [Flash starts to walk out of the ring, but walks back for one final thing.] NF - "I don't want to be remembered as the jackass who almost crippled his brother....but as the man you are seeing right now. So in the words of the great Raine Maida...I can't live here anymore...but it's hard when you reach for that door." [Flash exits the ring and runs through to the back.] JH - "What did that quote have to do with anything?" JG - "He's saying he can't stay here anymore, but it's hard to go." JH - "All I have to say is that guy is psycho. He babbled more in that interview than I can remember. And, he changed his attitude so many times." JG - "Sounds like you." JH - "Very funny." JG - "Bottom line is that the belt is vacant! What a way to start out the evening. We've also got the World title on the line, Curtis and Maxx, Hardin and Mota, Prodigy and Style! What a way to end the night, too." JH - "And those guys in between can just act as a time period for us all to booze up before the big boys. Then again, we'll be so drunk by the time it gets to the main event, it wouldn't matter if we had you and Jacks in there." JG - "Very funny. As Jack skipped, we've got the Pros and Siberian Express and German Eagles and Phi Theta Kappa in tag action tonight. Rock Bottom Robbie defends his belt against Doug Adams." JH - "Yeehaw. How come Adams gets all these title shots and stuff." JG - "This is his first one." JH - "Oh. Well, he doesn't deserve it." JG - "But, to open it up, let's go to Ed Jaspard!" ___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 1 ) | | \ | | "Perfectionist" Nik Nyce vs. Bobby McAllister / |___ | | | | Writer: AG (_________________________________________________________________________ [Jaspard stands in the ring, holding the now vacated North American title high over his head.] EJ - "I would just like to say that asides from my kid being born, this is the greatest day of my life!" JH - "Haven't heard that one before." JG - "Who ever said Jaspard was original?" JH - "Not me, especially with all the inbreeding going on in the clan." EJ - "This matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 20 limit time limit." JH - "Did he just say..." JG - "Ed gets confused easily." EJ - "Coming down the aisle first, weighing in at 234 lbs. and hailing from Chicago, Illinois, he is "The Perfectionist" Nik Nyce!" [Jimi Hendrix's Star Spangled Banners plays over the speakers as Nyce enters the arena. He walks down the aisle, black tights with Perfectionist blazed in the back. Nyce makes a fairly speedy approach to the ring, hopping in and jarring with Jaspard for the gold.] JH - "Speedy entrances. My kind of man." EJ - "And his opponent, accompanied by Victoria Glenn, and weighing in at 224 lbs., here comes Bobby McAllister!" [The Marine Hymm plays in the background as McAllister and Glenn emerge. Glenn leads the way to the ring, slapping hands with the fans. McAllister follows behind, hitting the other side. As Glenn nears the ring, McAllister hops onto the apron and holds open the ropes. He hops in himself, disrobes, then holds the ropes open for Glenn to exit.] JG - "Why did she enter?" JH - "Who cares. I think I saw up her skirt!" [The two lock up in the middle of the ring, but Nyce quickly hits a kick to midsection which ends the tie up. Nyce grabs McAllister by the hair and tosses him back to the mat. He spins McAllister onto his stomach and grinds McAllister's head into the mat.] JG - "Nik Nyce is really trying to make a mockery of Bobby McAllister. Both these men are debuting and both want to make an impression." JH - "Both men have made an impression the size of Victoria Glenn's chest. Nyce, however, made a good impression. The guy can really go." [Nyce stops the embarassment and walks towards the ropes, receiving a loud heel pop from the crowd. McAllister uses this to get up and walk behind Nyce. He spins Nyce around and nails a standing dropkick, sending Nyce over the top rope. McAllister turns to the crowd who give a loud face pop.] JG - "These two men are already getting loud responses from the crowd. That can only be good things." JH - "If they're boos. The crowd knows squat." [Nyce gets back up onto the apron, ego hurt more than body. McAllister walks over to grab Nyce, but Nyce drives his shoulder into the midsection. McAllister bends over, allowing Nyce to hit a sunset flip... 1! 2! KICKOUT! Both men leap to their feet and eye each other. They lock up in the middle of the ring again, this time with Nyce moving around into a hammerlock. McAllister ducks behind Nyce and reverses the hold. Nyce re-reverses the hold. McAllister attempts a different tactic and lifts Nyce onto his shoulders, then drops back into a fallaway slam. McAllister runs into the ropes, but Nyce rolls over, forcing McAllister to jump over. As McAllister bounces off the ropes, Nyce hits a monkey flip. Nyce moves in and applies a scissorlock across McAllister's chest. Nyce releases the hold and applies a matted waistlock. McAllister squirms around the mat and gets to his feet, but Nyce takes him down with a waistlock suplex. McAllister springs to his feet and runs at Nyce, but Nyce takes him down with a armdrag, quickly followed by an armbar.] JG - "It's a great battle tonight between two technicians. Nyce and McAllister know their games." JH - "But, what a crappy game it is. How many variations of these moves do you need?" JG - "I guess the more the merrier, right?" [McAllister wraps his feet around Nyce's head, breaking the hold. Nyce rolls on the mat and flips over McAllister into a pin... 1! 2! KICKOUT! Again, both men leap to their feet. They lock up in the middle of the ring.] JH - "Too many damn lockups!" [McAllister flips Nyce's arm over his shoulder and hits a bodyslam. McAllister runs into the ropes, but Nyce rolls underneath again. McAllister leaps over but on the way back, stops himself before the monkey flip. He grabs Nyce's legs and catapults him into the ropes. Nyce bounces off, allowing McAllister to grab Nyce and nail a spinning sidewalk slam. McAllister picks up Nyce and whips him into the ropes, hitting a running lariat. He moves towards the top turnbuckle, waits for Nyce to get up and takes off, but Nyce ducks away from a bulldog. Nyce hops on top of McAllister and applies a grapevine submission. McAllister rolls towards the ropes, but Nyce doesn't release the hold. McAllister screams at the referee who attempts to pull Nyce off, but can't.] JH - "Look at McAllister cry! What a wimp!" JG - "He's taking a lot of abuse in that ring. Nik Nyce is not letting go of the grapevine." [Nyce releases the hold and lifts up McAllister who begins to throw a series of hard right hands, all of which Nyce ducks away from. Victoria Glenn calls her man over. McAllister ducks between the ropes, but is pushed to the outside by Nyce who hits a flying knee from behind.] JH - "He nailed him right in the butt! Ha!" JG - "That's real funny, Jack." [Glenn attempts to lift her man up, but Nyce walks outside and stops the two from conversing. He starts to sweet talk Glenn who attempts to move away. McAllister gets up, infuriated at Nyce. He spins Nyce around, kicks him in the midsection and then nails a double underhook suplex. McAllister moves to get a chair, but Glenn stops him.] JH - "She's really whipping him." JG - "Very funny, Jack. It's not in McAllister's best interest to continue his temper tantrums." [McAllister tosses Nyce back in the ring and soon follows. Nyce backpedals towards the opposite side of the ring, but McAllister catches up. Nyce attempts a facerake, but McAllister grabs Nyce's hand, then lifts Nyce to his feet. He slings Nyce onto his shoulder and drops down with a powerslam. McAllister climbs to the top rope and takes flight with a legdrop, but Nyce moves out of the way.] JH - "Tried those high impact moves too many times." JG - "McAllister has to go for it in this match. He cannot go toe to toe with Nyce in the technical department, so he has to use his power moves the best he can." JH - "Who would want to use technical moves? I'm still stumped by that." [Nyce goes over for the kill and hits an dropkick on the sitting up McAllister. Nyce picks McAllister up and throws him into the ropes. Nyce hits a hiptoss into an armbar submission. McAllister works his way over to the ropes and gets up. Nyce stands back and lets McAllister get up. Nyce runs at McAllister, but McAllister sees him. McAllister ducks and hits a back body drop. Nyce lands on the other side of the ropes and turns McAllister around hitting a hotshot, dropping to the floor himself.] JH - "Nyce is showing the rookie what playing in the big leagues is all about." JG - "McAllister doesn't seem to be a stranger in the ring, though." [Nyce rolls back into the ring and stomps on McAllister who's holding his throat. Nyce is warned by the ref and allows McAllister to get up. Nyce goes over to McAllister and recieves a thumb in the eyes. McAllister runs off the ropes and hits a kneelift on the bent over Nik Nyce. McAllister picks Nyce up and goes for an irish whip. Nyce reverses it and goes for a clothesline. McAllister ducks it and hooks the arm. He hits a crucifix and keeps Nyce down for a pin... 1! KICKOUT! McAllister picks Nyce up and chops him into the corner. McAllister places Nyce on the top turnbuckle and jumps to the second turnbuckle. McAllister delivers one punch followed by an attempted frankensteiner off the top. Nyce holds on and McAllister falls back without Nik Nyce. Nyce takes off with a big splash on the fallen Bobby McAllister.] JH - "Ha ha, Nyce got em that time." JG - "McAllister should've stayed with his power attack." [Nyce picks McAllister up and punches him in the chin. Nyce then goes off the ropes and hits a spinning heel kick. Nyce then follows up with a spinning toe hold. McAllister managed to turn over thus making Nyce lose his grip. McAllister is slow to get up and when he does get up he puts Nyce in a headlock. Nyce throws him off the ropes and as McAllister nears, Nyce attempts a clotheline, but misses. When McAllister came back, Nyce got the knee up and sent McAllister across the ring. Nyce then attempted to pick McAllister up, but McAllister put Nyce in a small package. Nyce kicked out right away and recieved a clothesline from the already standing Bobby McAllister.] JG - "Hmmm, what a turn of events, huh Jack?" JH - "Shut up, I'm trying to make contact with my wife through mental telepathy. I will not behome tonight...I will not be home tonight." [McAllister picked up Nyce and hit a vicious bodyslam. McAllister proceeded to jump on Nyce and punch him in the skull. McAllister picked up Nyce and threw him into the ropes. McAllister catches him and hits a spinning sidewalk slam. Nyce starts holding his back as McAllister picks him up. McAllister places Nyce's head between his legs and hits a piledriver.] JG - "Great show of strength by the newcomer, Bobby McAllister." JH - "That isn't power. If he was really strong, he would've gotten him up for a powerbomb." JG - "Okay, Jack, let's see you hit a powerbomb on Nik Nyce." JH - "Gladly." JG - "After the match, I'll watch you confront him." JH - "I didn't mean now!" [McAllister runs off the ropes and as he goes up for a big splash but as he's in the air, he sees Nyce get his knees up, and hit them hard. Nyce rolls onto McAllister and applies a matted abdominal stretch. McAllister hits an elbow to the head, startling Nyce. Nyce drops back and gets up, allowing McAllister time as well. McAllister attempts to take quick advantage with a dropkick, but Nyce swipes him away. Nyce hops on the mat and applies a camel clutch, but McAllister is able to grab onto the ropes.] JG - "Nik Nyce is attempting to really wear down McAllister with his technical skills." JH - "This stuff is wearing me down. I don't know how much of this sleepfest I can take. I appreciate Nyce's attitude and all, but the technical stuff has to go." [Nyce picks up McAllister and sends him into the turnbuckle, following in with a big splash. As McAllister drops to the mat, Nyce climbs to the second turnbuckle and leaps off with an elbowdrop. He connects across the leg of McAllister.] JG - "Nyce must be setting up for Standing Nycely." JH - "Catchy name, eh?" JG - "Very." [Nyce grabs hold of McAllister's left leg and drops the elbow across the knee. He repeats this...again...again. Satisfied at the setup, Nyce attempts to lock on Standing Nycely, but McAllister counters with a DDT!] JG - "He nailed the DDT!" [McAllister covers... 1! 2! 3!] JG - "He got him! Bobby McAllister pinned Nik Nyce!" JH - "What? I thought Nyce was this big shot star?" JG - "He is! He has held titles all around the world. I guess this would have to be considered an upset." JH - "Finally! We never have upsets in SCRA! I want to see one of our jobbers win on Friday!" JG - "It could happen, I guess. But, you're right. Things to seem a little normal for SCRA." [The eerie sounds of Burn by the Cure seep out of the speakers and over the crowd. The crowd pops into a thunderous roar "Sty - al! Sty - al! Sty - al!" Just as the bass kicks in, Aladdin Style pushes through the curtain. Style walks slowly to the ring, turning and pointing to the corners of the arena. When he gets to the ring, he walks around it once before climbing to the apron, climbing to the top turnbuckle, and jumping to the mat. He takes the microphone from the announcer and addresses the crowd.] AS - "Oh, how sweet it is! Anthony Logan, listen to this crowd. Look at the ratings. Two matches and I'm the number seven contender. You took NOTHING away from me! Jon Grey, don't worry about my size on my account. What you said on Uncut was very true - smaller guys do have a disadvantage against their larger foes. That's an excuse many subpar wrestlers use. However, after the rookie year, smaller wrestlers have had long enough to learn to compensate for their weaknesses and capitalize on their strengths. You will never hear The Golden King crying about losing because some big guy beat him up. No chance. I know how to beat the big men. I know how to make them frustrated, how to play to their egos and how to run them out of gas. I'd rather go one-on-one with an Eddie Jacks than a Derek Mota. I KNOW I can beat a fat tub of lard like our so-called world champ. Derek Mota's got so much talent that it'll take all I've got to beat him. Moot point. Tonight on Scraps, I've got one of the bigger competitors in the SCRA: Prodigy. 6'6" and over 275 lbs. He's a tough man. More than that, he's the kind of wrestler I like. He's smart and technically gifted. These are attributes I feel the wrestling world should appreciate more than they do. Too bad he's up against me tonight. Prodigy, I want you to know you've got my respect. Too bad you are ranked higher than me. You are the next rung in the ladder on my road to Number One Contender. As smart as you are, I'm smarter. As technically sound as you are, I'm the best technician the SCRA. I've got the award to prove it. You may be bigger than I am, you may be stronger than I am - but as a technical wrestler you have to know that your strength and my speed isn't going to matter in this match. It's mind against mind, move against move, will against will - a clinic of the sweet science. And when it's all said and done, and the referee raises my hand in victory, you'll know that, you were beaten by the very best." JG - "There goes a changed man. Aladdin Style has really adopted a new attitude since his brother sidelined him a few months ago." JH - "And it sucks. I don't know if he can even beat Prodigy!" JG - "We'll see. Next up, we've got the Siberian Express facing off with the Pros. This should be an outstanding tag team affair." JH - "Squish!" JG - "I wonder if Tavis will be with the Pros." JH - "Unless he got a breat implant, I don't want to see it. Wait, even if he got a breat implant, I don't want to see him. Wait, if he did get a breat implant, I _definitely_ don't want to see him." JG - "Thanks for the mindless babbling, Jack. It really eats up the time." JH - "I remember back in the glory days..." JG - "Let's go to Ed Jaspard for the intros." -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
