[SCRA] Winter Carnival [3/4] (8 February 1998)

8 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Dragovich recovers quickly and moves to help his partner with a dropkick to the back of Baer. Bolotov quickly recovers...but is nailed in the back of the head by a snowball.] JH - "Who threw that?" JG - "I don't know, but Andrei Bolotov doesn't look too happy." JH - "He never looks happy. The man is a freaking animal. He's a bear!" [Bolotov turns around, looking angered more than usual, when he is hit with another snowball. As Dragovich attacks Baer, Jabasco moves in from the other side of the ring and attacks Bolotov from behind!] JG - "It was Walt Jabasco! His covert experiences helped him nail Bolotov without anybody noticing." JH - "Get that little bastard! Eat him!" [Jabasco starts throwing hard judo chops to the chest and throat of Bolotov. Dragovich sees his partner getting attacked and moves away from Baer. Dragovich grabs Jabasco from behind and hits a back suplex, following up with a series of punches to Jabasco.] JG - "The Siberian Express are really at an advantage right now. With two wrestlers left, they can watch each other's back." JH - "And, if they eliminate one more person, they will have _two_ title shots over the next few weeks." JG - "That would be a scary time for the champs at the time, whoever they may be." [Baer gets up from the attack and moves into Jabasco, hitting a dropkick which sends Jabasco running into Bolotov who scoops Jabasco into a bodyslam. Bolotov returns his attention to Jabasco which allows Baer to move into Dragovich and start an attack in the corner. He starts throwing punches and kicks to wear down the Russian. Bolotov is in the other corner doing the same to Jabasco, but Jabasco ducks under a swinging punch and moves behind Bolotov. He quickly hits a flying knee to the lower back of Bolotov.] JG - "Although the Express has two men, it doesn't do much when both are getting attacked." JH - "It won't stay that way for long. The Siberian Express absorb the blows and come back for more. When the other guy is too tired to continue, the Express refunds the punishment." JG - "At least they don't refund that food they were eating today. What is that stuff?" JH - "Some Russian food. Didn't look too appetizing." [Baer lifts Dragovich onto the top turnbuckle, attempting to push him over the top, but can't get the final push. Jabasco, in the other corner, continues with a series of knee drives into the chest of Bolotov. Dragovich tries to slide his way out of the top turnbuckle and onto the apron, putting himself in a comprimising position of elimination. Baer moves towards Dragovich and attempts to push him onto the floor, but Dragovich hits a shoulderblock. Dragovich hops over with a sunset flip, but Baer catches him on his back. Baer falls back, crushing Dragovich into the mat. He picks up Dragovich and tosses him into the turnbuckle, following with a running lariat. Again, he puts him on the top turnbuckle, but can't push him over.] JG - "Baer can't push Dragovich over the top. He's not going to get him over this way." JH - "It's just a way to get a breather. These guys have been in there for a while, they need to rest for a bit." [Jabasco stops his attack on Bolotov and moves towards Dragovich to help Baer eliminate him. Together, the two get Dragovich over the top!" JG - "Ivan Dragovich is gone! He's been eliminated!" JH - "How did that happen?" JG - "We've got the three wrestlers in tomorrow night's triple threat match. Baer, Bolotov and Jabasco!" JH - "Jabasco stands no chance. He's a little forest animal in with two hungry bears." JG - "You don't know what a bear would eat, do you?" JH - "No." [Bolotov turns to see his partner flying over the top and runs at the two who put him there. He double clotheslines them, getting Baer over the top, but Jabasco ducks.] JG - "He eliminated Bill Baer, but Jabasco's still in it!" [Bolotov turns around to see Jabasco flying at him with a dropkick, sending Bolotov over the top!] JG - "Bolotov's gone! Jabasco wins!" JH - "No! How did he win?" JG - "The covert skills! He used his sneaking to dodge the bullet and get the win! Wow!" JH - "Wow is right. I can't believe this thing we call a "Pro" can win a battle royal. He can barely win a match!" JG - "The singles battle royal is going to have a tough time topping that one." JH - "No, it won't." JG - "Before we get to the battle royal, we've got some taped comments from "Cowboy" Ken Curtis, the current World champ." [Jack Hazard stands in front of an SCRA banner, mic in hand.] JH - "We are here in the SCRA interview area, awaiting the arrival of the new SCRA World Heavyweight Champion, "Cowboy" Ken Curtis. He is scheduled..." [Suddenly, we hear a door slam and Curtis, dressed in black with his hat pulled low, comes into view! He is obviously very angry, and this causes Jacks to slink back a bit as Curtis crowds him. Curtis glares at Hazard and then at the camera...] JH - "Well, first of all, Cowboy, let me congratulate you on your win over Eddy Jacks to become the SCRA World Heavyweight Champion. I know the night was tempered..." CKC - "Tempered ain' the word for it, Hazard. Beatin' Jacks wasn't nothin' special, the man was ripe for the pickin'. If he'd've spent as much time in the gym as he did in the damned buffett line, he might still have the gold aroun' 'is waist. But like I said when I first came here, it was only a matter o' time b'fore the Cowboy took what he wanted. But I ain' here t' talk about that... ... I'm here t' talk about that low-life piece o' street trash that calls hisself "Gangsta Impasse". Impasse, you and yer "boys in the hood", or whatever the hell ya call yerselves, ya raised the stakes last Monday when you put the match t' my barn and damned near killed my horses..." JH - "And Tommy Gregory!" CKC - "Do I look like I give a dog damn about Tommy Gregory? If he was any kinda man, he woulda stopped those boys from torchin' my barn! As far as I'm concerned, he coulda burnt up in there! No, I don' give a damn about Gregory, an' if I see his chicken-s<BEEP> ass, I'm gonna kick another hole in it! My horses are my most prized possesions, Hazard, an' Impasse and his ghetto pals nearly took 'em from me! Let me say this t' Impasse right now..." [Curtis stares hard into the camera,his eyes on fire with rage, and points a finger at it...] CKC - "Impasse, when you put that match t' my barn last week, you might as well been puttin' the match to yer career, son! I ain' about cryin' an' complainin', I'm about bringin' hell by the bucketful, an' as far as I'm concerned, Impasse, yer already two-thirds a dead man as sure as I'm standin' here! Nobody...NOBODY disrespects my home, _boy_, an' the next time you an' me lock up in that ring, I'm gonna teach you the lesson that yer daddy woulda taught ya, if he hadn'a left yer mama when you was brewin' in her belly! You better hope God has more mercy on ya than the I will, son, 'cause I'm takin' ya to the whippin' post, an' with God and this nimrod Hazard as my witness, yer ass BELONGS TO THE COWBOY!" [Curtis abruptly turns and walks away, leaving Hazard speechless as the scene fades to black] JG - "Curtis is irate at what happened to his barn. It won't be long before he and Gangsta Impasse lock up, again." JH - "March 1, the International Invitationals of eWrestling. Call your local cable company and order night one!" JG - "And, night two. SCRA wrestler, "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin is competing in the Deschenes Cup. He may well bring it home for SCRA." JH - "He's bringing it home for one person and one person only, himself. Don't you stay up with things, Grey?" JG - "Sorry, but at least I know who NLWP is." JH - "Who are they! Tell me!" JG - "Let's start out the singles battle royal with one more taped comment from newcomer, "The Iconoclast" Sean Watts." [As the scene fades in, it quickly becomes apparent that the setting is a rather run-down hotel room, the camera finally focuses in on TV set atop a ratty table, and is held in place with steel restraining bars so that it can't be easily carted off. Seconds later, a figure clad in natty blue boxers and a tee-shirt with the Wesley Willis Fiasco drops atop the table to sit next to the television. A toothbrush is removed from his mouth and the figure begins to speak.] SW - "Good morning, SCRA. [He pauses to wipe a bit of toothpaste dribble from his chin.] Consider this a wake-up call if you feel so inclined. I'm Sean Watts, and you can call me 'Sensational', the 'Iconoclast', the 'Slacker', whatever you like. I'm not here to sell tee-shirts. I'm not here to sell tickets. I'm not here to start a fan-club, to seek adulation, or to "kick ass". I'm not even here for the simple purpose of getting respect, or picking up titles. I'm here on my own agenda. On my terms. I'm here to wrestle. I know, that's something that might seem like a shock to people, it almost makes me a throwback, in fact. I saw that this place is filled with some talent, and what's left of the 80s influence on wrestling; guys who are ghosts, prodigal idiots, and even vampires." [Sean steps off of the table, one hand going back to brushing his teeth as the other brushes back his lengthy and quite unruly blond hair. The camera turns to follow him into the bathroom, catching him in the rather unattractive act of stooping over the sink and drinking water straight from the faucet, rinsing, then spitting back in the basin. He stands up before speaking once more.] SW - "And here I'd been told all these good things about SCRA. About how it was the 'future' of wrestling, and was filled with talent. Sure, there are some legitimate wrestlers here, but there're quite a few folks who watched a little too much wrestling in the 80s, and got confused between MTV and this sport. They might sell shirts and do all those other things, but in the end, they'd better learn to wrestle or they'd better get ready to be exposed as frauds. I know what the typical wrestler who is new to a federation does. They go on and on about their record. Their accomplishments, all the titles they've won, and how they immediately deserve a shot at some title based on all these other things that have happened in the past. Generally, they make total asses out of themselves. Ask Sengir all about it. [A toothpaste encrusted smile comes across his face as the scene abruptly cuts to static.] __ __ _______________________________________________________________ | |__| | / ] Singles Battle Royal | | | |/ / | | | / / Competitors: Every non-jobber singles wrestler | ` ' \_ \ / | Writer: Alex G. \_/\_/ \____|____________________________________________________________ [Jaspard is nowhere to be found.] JG - "Where the hell is Ed?" JH - "I don't know. Why are you asking me?" [Suddenly, a loud swoosh is heard from outside the ring. Jaspard comes crashing down the snowy hills with two tiny home-made skis on each foot. One of the skis can't take the pressure and the buckle unlatches, sending Jaspard flailing out of control on one ski. He hits the snowbank, gets catapulted through the air and into the side of the ring. Security ushers Ed to the side, unable to complete his duties as ring announcer.] JG - "I guess we don't need intros. The competitors for tonight's battle royal: every singles competitor." JH - "And here they come!" [Prodigy comes out first, soon followed by the entire singles roster, then Chase Hunter at the back of the pack. Surprisingly peacefully, the 18 men climb into the ring and just look at each other, each man trying to grab a good corner.] DING! DING! DING! [Suddenly, Hunter springs himself onto the top turnbuckle, then moonsaults onto the pile of wrestlers in the middle of the ring!] JG - "Holy cow! Hunter just cleared about seven wrestlers with that moonsault!" JH - "He also almost got caught by Jacks. If Jacks would've got him, the night's over for ol' Chase. What a shame, eh?" [A wild brawl breaks out as every wrestler attempts to punch or kick the man beside him. As the wrestlers fight, a familiar voice comes over the PA system. A man is shown on the big screen as some wrestlers come to a hault to see who it is. The screen is pretty much black with the shadow of a boy sitting in sight.] VOICE - "I've come here fer the last couple-a weeks te see what this league waz all about. What I've seen i' a bunch o' cruiserweights who can't beat a fly an' have no more than one hair on dey-re chest. Well, boys, I'm here an' ready te kick some ass! Ma name is Ponyboy Kertins. Most people know me as being Golden, but thats a whole other story. I'm ready te fight an' ain't nobody gon' stop me." [The screen goes completely black as Doug "Grizzly" Adams, who was watching is clotheslined from the back by Nik Nyce, sending him over the ropes. A ruckus is heard in the crowd as Ponyboy jumps over the guardrail and jumps to the top rope. He jumps off, hitting The Phantom with a hurricarrana from the top rope.] JG - "Grizzly's gone! Kertins' here! What else is going to happen?" JH - "Who the hell is this Ponyboy? I thought we were going for some big name star and we get this runt?" [The brawl continues, fine lines starting to divide different corners. Eddy Jacks remains in the corner with a bunch of the top contenders, Gangsta Impasse, Anthony Logan and Derek Mota, throwing hard punches. J.W. Hardin directs traffic from the back, inflicting pain on his new found enemy. Style sees his brother and Mota in the attack and dives into the fray, dropping Hardin into the bunch. Style drags out Mota and sends him running into the ropes, hitting a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker. Logan, Gangsta and Hardin get back up, attacking Jacks again.] JG - "Looks like we've got a few enemies for Eddy Jacks here. All the guys who couldn't beat him, or did by disqualification, are trying to exact a measure of revenge." JH - "Except Hardin. He just likes to beat people up." [Rock Bottom Robbie and Sgt. Pepper have the current TV champ, Prodigy, in the corner and are attacking with alternating kicks and boots from either man. In the same corner, Chase Hunter stands crouched, trying to stay away from the main points of attack. Out of nowhere, Gwaii Edenshaw lunges into Hunter, surprising Hunter with a series of stomps. In the middle of the ring, beside Style and Mota's brawl, Nik Nyce and Mike Justice stand facing Victoria Glenn, flexing their muscles in an attempt to get some attention. From the back, Bobby McAllister dives in with a double forearm shot to either man. Sean Watts sits in the corner, watching the action.] JH - "Look at Watts! He's so lazy, he doesn't want to fight!" JG - "That might be smarts, not lazy. He's escaping all the attack." JH - "A little punch never hurt anybody." [The newest addition to the roster, Ponyboy Kertins, stands over Phantom, throwing a series of left and rights to the head. Kertins lifts up Phantom and sends him running into the ropes, executing a hurricarana. Kertins gets off Phantom who gets up quickly, but is nailed by a dropkick by Kertins and flies over the top.] JH - "Phantom's gone! Who is this Kertins?!" JG - "I don't know, but he just eliminated a former champ. He's got to have at least some talent." JH - "Not necessarily. Look at the list of former champs we've got. Phantom? Sgt. Pepper? Rock Bottom Robbie? And a current champ, Prodigy?" [The entourage has Jacks on the turnbuckle, trying to push him over the top. But, the big man won't budge. Jacks rakes the eyes of Hardin, kicks Gangsta in the midsection then climbs down at Logan. He grabs the martial artist and nails a DDT. Hardin gets back up and runs at Jacks with a lariat, almost sending the big man over top. But, Jacks holds on and nails a kick to the groin to send Hardin back. Jacks runs at Hardin and Gangsta with a double clothesline, nailing them both. This drops Hardin onto Style who releases the figure four he had on Mota. Style hits a standing somersault legdrop onto Hardin's neck, then puts on a scissorlock around the large neck. Mota gets up and dropkicks Style in the back of the head, quickly applying a chinlock to save Hardin.] JG - "We're seeing people work together here that I never thought would. Hardin and Mota, Hardin, Gangsta and Logan..." JH - "Watts and himself. I thought this guy was all down on himself, but he seems to be working well right now." [Jacks turns to see Logan getting up from the clothesline before and runs at him with another one. Logan sees this coming and hops on with a crucifix, but Jacks catches him. Jacks moves towards the ropes and drops Logan over the top. Logan lands on the apron, saving himself, but Jacks quickly kicks Logan in the stomach which ends Logan's night.] JG - "Anthony Logan's gone!" JH - "We're starting to filter out faster than a New York sewer." JG - "And filtering better, too." [As Jacks looks rather happy from his elimination of Logan, Gangsta runs in from behind and attempts to lift Jacks' leg over the top. Hardin comes in and attempts to help out. Meanwhile, Kertins, being alone and all, runs into the corner at Robbie and Pepper, hitting a back suplex on Robbie, followed by a monkey flip on Pepper. The monkey flip sends Pepper into the center of the ring where McAllister is getting double teamed by Nyce and Justice. Pepper grabs Justice and decks him with a hard right. He does the same to Nyce, then helps up McAllister. The two move in and start to attack the now grounded Nyce and Justice. Kertins helps out Prodigy in brawling with Robbie. Robbie tries to fight back, but can't take out the two men. Edenshaw whips Hunter into the three brawling men, following in with a big splash. But, Hunter moves. Kertins turns around and hits a dropkick to Edenshaw, sending him stumbling over a bent over Hunter. Pepper and McAllister both have Nyce and Justice, respectively, in figure fours.] JG - "Everybody's got somebody they want to kill! It's unbelievable here!" JH - "Unbelievable only partially describes this. We've got about five guys trying to lift Jacks over the top and three others trying to throw a punch." [The two men, Hardin and Gangsta, are joined by McAllister who releases the figure four. The three lift Jacks...and get him!] JG - "Jacks is gone! The big man is gone!" JH - "That's almost 400 lbs. of pure beef that just made a giant hole in the snow. I think that hole went 20 metres deep!" -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
