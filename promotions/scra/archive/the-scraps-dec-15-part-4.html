[SCRA] The Scraps, 15 December 1997, Part 4

15 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 6 ) | | \ | | "Cowboy" Ken Curtis vs. Reverend Aristotle Jones / |___ | | | | Writer: AG (_________________________________________________________________________ [Jaspard stands in the center of the ring, running around in circles trying to grab something.] JH - "It worked! Look at Jaspard's butt!" [The camera attempts to focus in, but can only get an average shot. A small piece of paper reading "KICK ME" is taped on.] JG - "Very amusing, Jack. Would you care to remove it?" JH - "I'm not going near there, Grey! That thing could explode at any minute. Get Taskan out here. I'm sure he would like it." JG - "He is no longer with us." JH - "Shucks." [Jaspard gives up grabbing the sign and starts the intros.] EJ - "This matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. Coming down the aisle first, hailing from Austin, Texas and weighing in at 260 lbs., here is "Cowboy" Ken Curtis!" [Curtis enters the arena, this time donning his wrestling attire instead of the street clothes we saw previously. As he jogs to the ring, Gimme Back my Bullets by Lynard Skynard begins playing. Curtis ignores the jeering fans, hitting the ring with a pure business attitude.] EJ - "And now, welcome weighing in at 240 pounds, the Reverend Aristotle Jones!" [The Beatles kick into gear with The Word. Aristotle makes his weigh into the arena and down the aisle holding up the Good Book for all to see. He makes his way into the ring and displays the Bible again. At this point, Curtis rushes over to Jones and attacks from behind with an axehandle.] DING! DING! DING! JG - "Ken Curtis is off quickly here! He is wasting no time in taking it to Reverend Jones." JH - "That's how it should be. None of that lock up stuff to waste time. Excuse me for a second." [Hazard walks over to Jaspard and gives him a swift foot to the beind.] JH - "Ha! I can't get in trouble for that!" [Jones drops to the mat, but Curtis continues to stomp away. As Jones crawls towards the ropes, Curtis takes off his vest and begins to choke Jones with it.] JG - "Get in there, ref!" JH - "What's he going to do? Call the fashion police for bad use of a good vest? This stuff is perfectly legal!" JG - "Choking an opponent is not legal." JH - "What about Latrell Sprewell? He sure got away easy." JG - "I guess choking a respected mentor like a coach is different." [Curtis picks Jones back up and flings him to the ropes, where he hits a dropkick as Jones comes back to him. Curtis begins to kick Jones on the mat, he then applies a bow and arrow submission hold.] JG - "Curtis is dominating the kid right now." JH - "He must have sinned." JG - "I doubt it, Reverend Jones is a very God fearing man." JH - "Well, he better start worrying a little more about Ken Curtis and a little less about God. It doesn't matter though, the kid obviously isn't any good, he looks like jWo material to me." JG - "I'd be careful what you say Jack, God will do more than sue you." [Curtis quickly releases the bow and arrow, lifting Jones to his feet and quickly slinging him over Curtis' shoulder. Curtis walks towards the center of the ring and drops to the mat with a death valley driver. As Jones wiggles in the center of the ring, Curtis moves off the ropes and comes down hard with a knee drop to the head area. Curtis gets right back up, lifting Jones with him. He lifts Jones high in the air for a vertical suplex...] JG - "A great display of power by Ken Curtis. Jones is straight up there, and it doesn't look like he is coming down." JH - "Of course he'll be comin' down, Grey. It's just a matter of when and where." [As Jones falters forward, Curtis falls back, bringing Jones down to the mat hard. Curtis climbs to the second turnbuckle and takes off, hitting a legdrop. Curtis grabs his behind, obviously taking a little bit of a bump from the move. He quickly rids himself of the pain, picking up Jones and sending him into the ropes. Jones ducks a clothesline and comes back with a flying cross body block. He goes for the cover... 1! 2KICKOUT! As Curtis tosses Jones off, he leaps to his feet, angered and ready to take out Jones. However, Jones stays on the mat and drops Curtis with a drop toe hold into an ankle lock submission. Curtis is able to grab hold of the ropes, releasing the hold. Jones gets to his feet, taking Curtis with him. Jones sends Curtis into the ropes, bending over for a back body drop, but Curtis leapfrogs Jones and appears on the other side, hitting a low blow.] JH - "That's gotta hurt'em. I don't think ol' Rev will be having kids." JG - "I don't think that was in his future anyways, but nonetheless, it hurt." JH - "No kids? How can you go through life like that? I can't go a week without burning the midnight oil, if you know what I mean." [As Jones bends over, Curtis reaches his hands through the legs and around the neck, tossing Jones over top with a pumphandle slam. Curtis gets right back up and takes Jones with him, whipping him into the ropes. Curtis grabs Jones and hits a spinebuster, but doesn't release the legs. Instead, he flips himself back, springing Jones into the ropes with a hotshot. As Jones bounces back, Curtis rolls him up... 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Close call! It looked like Ken Curtis set that one up perfectly for the pin." JH - "This whole match has been set up perfectly. Curtis is the exc..." JG - "I don't think you should be saying that. Especially about Ken Curtis." JH - "What, you don't think he can go?" JG - "He can brawl all right. But to be the (mumbling) excellence of execution (talks loudly again) you have to be able to wear your opponent down on the mat, like Reverend Jones." JH - "And look where that's getting him." [Curtis gets up, taking Jones with him. He sets up for a powerslam, but instead of tossing Jones down on the mat, Curtis throws Jones over the top, crashing through a table on the outside.] JH - "Now, that's extreme!" JG - "Which isn't always a good thing." JH - "Com'on, Grey, the best matches are always the bloody ones." [Curtis climbs onto the apron, taking off with an elbowdrop. He connects on Jones, but takes a lot of the impact. Curtis gets up slowly, lifting Jones to his feet. He takes Jones and whips him into the ring steps, sending the steps flying into the guard rail. Curtis walks over and grabs the steps, attempting to drive them down on Jones, but Jones kicks upward from the ground, sending the steps into Curtis' head.] JG - "Curtis is busted wide open!" JH - "Ah, two of the three keys to a good match have been fulfilled." JG - "Care to share?" JH - "The Killer B's: brawling, blood and broken bones. If only Curtis will snap Jones' head with High Noon, this match will be top notch." [Jones lifts Curtis and tosses him into the ring, going to work on the legs with elbows to the knee. As Curtis' knees soften, Jones gets on the mat and wraps on a figure four.] JG - "This could be it! Jones has the figure four wrapped on tight!" [Curtis begins to move about, trying to reverse the hold. He cannot reverse it, but is able to pull himself towards the ropes forcing Jones to break the hold. Jones gets up and takes Curtis with him. He wraps his hands around Curtis into a waistlock. Curtis elbows Jones in the head, causing Jones to release the hold and duck his head down. Curtis runs off the ropes, coming up from behind with a bulldog.] JH - "He's setting up for High Noon! That was just a sample for the pain and agony Jones will be tasting soon!" [Curtis gets back up, lifting Jones with him. He starts delivering hard right hands, moving Jones back into the turnbuckle. Curtis whips Jones across the ring, but Jones is able to grab a hold of the turnbuckle and lift himself up as Curtis runs in. He times it right and lands behind Curtis, grabbing Curtis around the waist and sending Curtis into the middle of the ring with a release German suplex. Curtis gets up quickly and runs at Jones, but Jones takes him down with a Russian legsweep. He quickly locks on a hammerlock.] JG - "Reverend Jones is dissecting Curtis on the mat." JH - "Better not say that. You know how Jones and those Activists freaks are. They'll be coming in saying it's cruelty to animals or something." JG - "Curtis isn't an animal." JH - "If you don't think Curtis is an animal, you've got another thing comin' to ya." [Curtis grabs Jones' leg with his free arm, causing Jones to release the hold. Both men free each other and stand up. Curtis runs at Jones with a short arm lariat, taking the Reverend down. Reverend moves towards the ropes, attempting to lift himself up, but Curtis grabs him and flings him across the ring. Curtis grabs Jones by the waist and hits a backbreaker. Curtis gets up quickly and rolls Jones onto his stomach, leaving the back exposed. Curtis runs off the ropes and drops down hard with his knee, directly to the center of Jones' back. Curtis stays on the mat, holding Jones' arms down and thrusting himself up for more kneedrops. Curtis lifts Jones to his feet, but quickly drops him down with a swinging neckbreaker.] JG - "Ken Curtis is really attacking the back of Reverend Jones. He is really setting up for High Noon." JH - "It's going to be fun to see Jones' head get smashed into the mat, then bounce off like a basketball." [Curtis grabs Jones, lifting him back up. Curtis toys with Jones, mocking him in front of the crowd by slapping him and getting down on his knees, as if he was praying. Jones leaps onto Curtis' shoulders and attempts a headscissors, but is unable to get very much momentum because of Curtis' relatively low position. Jones falls off the shoulders and gets to his feet, only to be grabbed by Curtis and driven into the mat with a side suplex. Curtis climbs onto the second turnbuckle..] JG - "Ken Curtis showing some aerial skills? I must be seeing things." JH - "Curtis can do it all, Grey. From tyin' cattle to flyin' battle, he is the all around athlete." JG - "That wasn't half bad, Jack. Did you think of that just now?" JH - "What? You think I stayed up last night until 1:00 am thinking of that line?" JG - "All of us aren't fortunate enough to party with Phi Theta Kappa. I wasn't sure..." JH - "Enough, Grey. Curtis is taking off!" [As soon as Jones gets to his feet, Curtis takes off with a flying forearm. Jones gets clocked on the top of the head and rolls back to the ropes. Curtis quickly moves over, lifting Jones to his feet and delivering a belly to back suplex. Jones gets to his knees, but is pulled all the way up by Curtis who then delivers a belly to belly suplex.] JH - "All we need is belly to side and we have all the corners covered!" [Curtis takes his time and walks around the ring, parading to the fans. He lazily walks over to Jones and bends to pick him up. Jones, however, is able to get a forearm shot in the midsection, sending Curtis staggering back. Jones continues with the forearms to the midsection as Curtis continues to move back. When he gets to his feet, Jones runs into the ropes and meets Curtis in the center of the ring with a dropkick. Curtis falls to the ground, but is quickly scooped up by Jones and send into the ropes. Jones bends over for a back body drop, but Curtis sees the move coming and stops himself. He places Jones' head between his knees and delivers a swift piledriver.] JH - "That's gotta hurt. I love that move, Grey. Remember the song a while back?" JG - "Vaguely. I don't think I really want to remember it though." JH - "Are you saying wresters can't sing?" JG - "That's what I'm saying, Jack." [Curtis picks up Jones and irish whips him into the corner, following in with a lariat. Curtis again climbs to the ropes and as Jones begins to lean forward, takes off and connects with High Noon.] JG - "High Noon! It's over!" [Curtis covers Jones... 1! 2! 3!] JH - "Curtis wins! What a fighter, eh Grey?" [Curtis quickly gets up and leaves the ring area with his clothing. As he walks down the aisle, he passes...] JG - "Derek Mota! What's he doing here?" JH - "He enjoys the good heroes like Curtis just as much as we do." [Mota climbs into the ring, grabbing a microphone and looking at the fallen Reverend.] DM - "Let me tell you a story ... I keep on hearing about this place they call SCRA, how it's the hottest fed going these days. I got "Mr." Grey on the phone, promising me the glory, kissin' between the cheeks ta get me signin' the dotted line ... And I did." [Mota bounces against the ropes and hits Jones with a Jumping Elbow Drop, keeping him on the mat. Derek Mota once again grabs the microphone and begins to speak.] DM - "Then I found out I've been bracketed. "Mota's a top cruiser", I hear. Mota's a cinch ta win that new cruiser title, people keep sayin' around the dressing rooms. And then who the hell do I have goin' up against me for this title? The "Reverend" Aristotle Jones. The man who's weighin' in at a porky 240 pounds. A man who has no right, NO RIGHT to be competin' for a cruiserweight title! Dammit, Jones, if I'm gonna put my damn name on this title, I ain't gonna be facin' you for the thing! And that's why I'm takin' you out ... right ... now!!! I'll show you what being a cruiserweight is all about!" [Derek Mota climbs to the top rope, Jones slowly beginning to stir on the mat. Mota stands upright, arms outstretched to the crowd, who pop loudly as Mota launches himself gracefully from the top rope with a ...] JH - "Shooting Star Press!!!" JG - "That move is so graceful, especially when performed by a top cruiserweight like Derek Mota. I still don't understand why he is out here, though." JH - "Do you really need an excuse to beat on Jones?" [Mota lifts Jones to his feet and starts throwing hard rights, moving Jones into the center of the ring. Mota runs against the rope, taking off for a hurricarana...] JG - "Blocked! Reverend Jones stopped the hurricarana and..." JH - "Powerbomb! Oh no! Say it ain't so, Grey!" [Jones gets up and places a figure four on Mota, attempting to take out Mota's precious vertical base. Mota is able to break out of the hold before it is locked on fully. Both men spring to their feet and start throwing furious lefts and rights. Tommy Gregory and the security staff rush down the aisle to break up the two.] JH - "Let 'em fight! Damn security staff, why'd we get 'em?" [The staff steps in between the two wrestlers, but a few workers are sent flying by a furious Derek Mota who continues to unleash punches. He walks over to Jones, diving at him and taking the Reverend down. Mota begins hurling lefts and rights on the mat as security is able to yank him off.] JG - "Derek Mota is mad!" JH - "I guess he really doesn't want Jones in the cruiserweight division, eh Grey?" [Security drags Mota to the back, literally. Jones walks peacefully down the aisle, not wanting any extra help.] JG - "What a turn of events." JH - "You're telling me, Grey. Hardin one week, Mota the next. Who are we getting next week?" JG - "I don't know. I don't think anybody, but..." JH - "Yeah, yeah, anything can happen, blah, blah, blah." JG - "Well, if you'll excuse me for a moment, I have an interview to conduct with one Aladdin Style." JH - "Before Anthony Logan's match? That guy's got balls." [Jon Grey enters the ring with microphone in hand. He looks out at the crowd, slowly turning around to see all parts of the arena, as if trying to look each and every person in attendance in the eye. He raises his left hand, silencing the crowd, and slowly raises the microphone to his face.] JG - "Ladies and gentlemen....from Fredericton, New Brunswick, Canada..." [Suddenly he throws his left down, gripped in a fist. "Aladdin Style!" The loud, eerie sounds of the Cure's Burn single fill the air. The crowds erupts in loud cheering, quickly turning in to chants of "LORDS - LORDS - LORDS - LORDS!" The curtains part as Aladdin Style - goatee and moustache shaved off, visibly heavier and dressed in street clothes - walks deliberately through. He walks halfway to the ring with a cold, determined expression. The crowd's overwhelmingly postive response finally gets to him, and he breaks out in a huge grin. He raises both arms in the air and turns slowly around. Still grinning, he walks the rest of the way to the ring. He hops on the ring apron, climbs to the top turnbuckle and somersaults to the mat, landing gracefully on one leg - the leg Anthony Logan injured. Taking the microphone from Grey's hand, he lifts it to the rythm of the crowds chanting. "LORDS - LORDS - LORDS - LORDS!"] AS - "(Laughing). "Mr. Grey, it's so good to be back! Listen up, Logan! You hear this?" [Aladdin walks around the ring, climbing each turnbuckle and holding the microphone out to the fans. The fans on each side respond even louder. "LORDS - LORDS - LORDS - LORDS!" Style, grinning ear to ear, returns to Grey.] AS - "You want to excorsize the past Logan? You want your Golden King? I've got your GOLDEN KING right here!" [The crowd cheers thunderously. Style can't talk over the noise, and patiently waits fo them to stop.] AS - "I've got a few things to say to you before my partner, Jason Gore, kicks your kickboxer ass. First and foremost - congratulations on the three-count. Well done, big man. You've always wanted to do that but were never man enough. Law of averages, kickboxer. Law of averages. Given enough tries, you'd take me once. But there's an old man, a genius at this game, who said "you did it once. Let's see you do it again!" Thirteen times he said that, but I'm just saying it once. Let's see you try to be the man you've always wished you were. Last week I turned on the television to catch the SCRA Preview. I watched on as the worst kind of filth poured out of your lying, manipulative lips. Ten years of hatred-driven martial arts training haven't taught you a damned thing, Logan. You still have the same mistakes, the same tragic flaws that you've always had. You're the "Perfect Weapon," are you? Well, "Perfect Weapon," fancy lights aren't going to beat me. Kicking at shadows isn't going to beat me. There's no hiding under a mask this time. I know who you are. Despite your words, you are still that cowering, whimpering little brat that I took that knife from ten years ago. For all your discipline, you still snap under pressure. I'm the master of making people lose it and I know buttons on your psyche you've forgotten you've had. You lose control against me, wild child, and you'll never get it back. You've got a hole in your style, wild child. It's 5'8" tall and 230 lbs of solid talent. And if by chance the hole's not quite big enough for me to fit through right now.... you're facing Jason Gore tonight....." [The crowd cheers as Style smiles an evil smile....] AS - "... and that hole will be a whole lot bigger by the time he's done with you. Faster and stronger I'll give you, kickboxer. But smarter? Never. The House of Lords has said it a thousand times to a hundred opponents.....you may be bigger...you may be badder...but I always was and always will be so goddamn much better than you." [With that, Jason Gore walks into the aisle. Style jumps to the top turnbuckle and somersaults to the floor, landing on both feet. The crowds chant of "LORDS - LORDS - LORDS - LORDS!" resumes as Aladdin Style walks back down the aisle, meeting half way with Jason Gore.
