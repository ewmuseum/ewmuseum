[SCRA] ScraPreview [3/4] (7 February 1998)

7 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:: Phantom JG - "The mysterious man of SCRA stands no chance in this one, to put it quite bluntly. He's got the speed and technical skills, but those don't do anything here. Unless he can come up with some major mind game, I expect early elimination for this small man." JH - "And maybe he'll go for good, eh?" JG - "Maybe." :: Chase Hunter [Heavy metal music blares through a dark screen. The sounds of what seems to be like wild, uncaged animals. The scene finally fades in. The wild animal sounds die down a bit. Jon Grey stands outside a dance club, the heavy music playing loudly within. He seems rather nervous to be standing around such an establishment.] JG - "Ladies and gentlemen, I received a phone call from Chase Hunter. He told me to meet him at this address...but this is insane!! There's prostitution, there's open drug use...I-I'm going in anyway, as crazy as that sounds." [Jon pushes his way through the smoke filled, spray painted area. He makes his way into the club and into the main area. On the entire dance floor, nearly one hundred screaming moshers dance and flop around. An unknown heavy metal band is seen on the stage. They seem to be freely allow moshers to stage dive at their own discretion.) JG - "I-I-I've never experienced such madness. Mindless, senseless... MADNESS!! Wait a minute..." [Chase Hunter is seen buried amongst the crowd. He seems to be fitting in quite well, despite that he's in his regular wrestling attire, still. One of the times that he's thrown around, he spots the cameraman and Jon. He seems to say a happy "Hey!!" He then seems to be yelling to his fellow moshers to carry him towards Jon and the cameraman. Chase starts to actually bodysurf his way towards Jon. He finally reaches Jon and hops down. He motions both the cameraman and Jon towards the exit. Before he heads out the door, he grabs his jean jacket and starts walking down the street. He stuffs his hands in his pockets.] JG - "Chase, you're in SCRA, now. How does that make you feel?" CH - "Jon, my life hasn't been easy. I don't like talking about it. I'll say that I'm finally starting to feel at ease back in North America." JG - "What is it you hope to accomplish in this federation?" CH - "To make a name for myself in anyway possible. I'm totally fresh... in more way than one...but fresh to this fed. I was way over confident with Aladdin. I thought for sure that simply being the most versatile would be enough...obviously not. Now I've gotta _prove_ I'm the most versatile wrestler of all time. Trust me, I am...and I'll prove it!!" JG - "You seem to take a loss well..." CH - "Hey, being the most versatile means I'm versatile emotionally as well..." JG - "So what about the upcoming battle royal. The winner gets a world title shot. The last five will get a title shot eventually. The remaining 8 are put into a tournament for the North American championship." CH - "There's a lot of guys that are going to be in there. Hell, I was practicing in that mosh pit to see what it'll be like to be so crowded. Toughen myself up. Nothing's nastier than a mosh pit, I can assure you. You think the ones here are bad, trying being over in Europe. Whew...still gives me nightmares. Oh wait, that was my ballroom dancing. I get those two confused sometimes." JG - "Now, what about another new comer that has said some quite nasty things about you...." CH - "Oh, you mean that Aztek warrior, Mr. Gumshoe thing-a-ma-bob. Yeah, I'm thinking about some surprises for him..." JG - "No, what about the things he has said...about your past..." CH - "Don't mention the past, Jon, please..." JG - "Fine, but he obviously thinks you haven't paid for your crimes. And his name is "Haida Warrior" Gwaii Edenshaw. Not...well...whatever you called him." CH - "Hey, his name don't matter. If he wants a fight, he's got a fight. When you step in the ring, the past becomes the here and now. Sure, I've done some bad things in my life. Lord knows I've paid for them dearly. I...don't want to discuss it, okay? Really..." JG - "I'm sorry. It seems someone else has picked up an interest in you as well. Someone by the name of Bobby McAllister." CH - "Oh yeah, him...well, he doesn't seem to have a problem with me. I've got nothing against a guy who wants to be friends with Chase Hunter." [They continue walking down the street. A small, red stray cat comes up and rubs against Chase's leg. Chase picks the animal up and cuddles with it.] CH - "Hello. What's your name, little guy? Hmm, no name tag, huh? How do you like the name Chaser, hmmm?" JG - "Uh, Chase?" [Chase suddenly brings his attention back to Jon.] CH - "Oh...sorry." [Chase keeps holding the cat as they continue walking down the street.] JG - "So, is there anything else you would like to say before we wrap this up?" CH - "Just that I look forward to having more fun in my first North American federation. More fun equals more guys who will probably ant to kick my booty. I've got the skeletons in the closet...but I think when you really think about it...everyone does." JG - "This is a rather new side of you, Chase." CH - "Well, I'm trying to think serious...at least to keep the right winning frame of mind for the battle royal." JG - "Thanks Chase for your time, and good luck in the battle royal." CH - "Thanks, I think I'll need it." [Chase walks up a set of stairs of an appartment building and through the door. Fade out.] JH - "Have fun in those mosh pits, Grey?" JG - "It was quite the experience. I didn't know how to react when I saw all those, well, animals coming at me." JH - "Like you do when you see Grizzly coming at you: sneeze in his face and laugh when he falls back." JG - "Hunter probably can fare well, but not win. His versatility is an amazing asset in a match like this, but the specialization of a brawler probably outweighs Hunter's brawling skills. And, his size isn't all that great. But, I hear Hunter's got some plans in store for this one..." JH - "Another cartoon?" JG - "I don't think so." JH - "Drats." :: "Haida Warrior" Gwaii Edenshaw [The camera is set back in the final row of the stadium and slowly begins to pan down to the stadium floor, in the process capturing the sight and sounds of the work crew setting the event for the Winter Carnival. In front of the ring which is almost completely set up, stands Sir John Caboto and the Haida Warrior Gwaii Edenshaw. Edenshaw is staring in various directions and the constant sounds coming from all over the stadium seem to make him nervous.] JC - "In the beginning, there was land and water, Raven, was the only person on the face of the earth. In the beginning Raven was lonely." [Edenshaw nods his head smiling as the creation story is recounted by Caboto.] JC - "So Raven went in search of something to amuse him and he found on the beach a clamshell, inside this clamshell he could here the sounds of tiny voices which were screaming to be let out. Raven thought for a moment and then decided to fly the clamshell high into the air and drop so as to let the people out. He did this, and the shell cracked just enough for the tiny people to come out. These people were not the Haida people that live today, but rather their ancestors who are now long gone." [Caboto stops and takes a deep breath] JC - "I find a remarkable parralell to this story and the one which is about to take place this Sunday. The ring will be full of people, just like that clamshell, and they'll all be screaming to be let out once they set their eyes on the Haida Warrior Gwaii Edenshaw." [Caboto begins to grin and chuckle, he can hardly contain himself as he gets more and more worked up. Edenshaw's face has lost the smile that it held while the story was being told and now wears that familiar mix of anger and intensity.] JC - "And this surge of people out of the ring will change the face of the SCRA forever, because it will mark the changing of the guard in the SCRA and the emergence of a new force. And the sorry wrestlers who have hung around the SCRA for too long now will be a mere memory, like those first people to come out of the clamshell. And don't think we've forgotten about you Mr. Chase Hunter, because we haven't. Your pretty little mug will be the first thing that the Warrior looks for when he gets in that ring and he'll lay the beating on you that your father should have. And when it's all said and done your face will plastered so far into the floor that it'll be more of a permanent fixture than your mother's back on her neighbours bed." [Edenshaw gets his face worked up and his breathing quickens and he begins to yell as the camera turns away into the stands.] JH - "Everybody's telling stories tonight, eh?" JG - "I guess people want to share their knowledge." JH - "Can I share my knowledge?" JG - "No. Gwaii Edenshaw stands with a very outside shot at winning. On the plus side are his size and animal instincts. But, on the minus size are his inexperience, especially in matches like this. I don't know how much that will play in, but I expect it to be at least a limiting factor." JH - "That's sharing some knowledge. What was that in again, science or something?" JG - "Sure." :: "Hot Stuff" Mike Justice [Camera fades into a standard SCRA training facility. It's entirely empty, except for the likes of SCRA rookie sensation "Hot Stuff" Mike Justice. Justice is running on a treadmill, and blasting Donna Summer's "Hot Stuff" in the background] MJ - "You SCRA people ALWAYS have to come and bug Mike Justice when he doesn't want you around. I suppose you just want a look at my muscles, then?" [Justice attempts to flex, but almost loses his balance on the treadmill.] MJ - "Haha, Mike Justice won't fall for that trick twice!" [Justice smiles] MJ - "This weekened i'm supposed to be in some big, fancy battle royal. There's going to be a lot of little girly men coming to attack the sexiest man in the SCRA. I understand how jealousy works, and I won't be suprised to see little boys like Nik Nyce and Chase Hunter trying to hurt me." [Justice pauses to shut off the treadmill] MJ - "Do you boys see this great body of mine?" [Justice makes a sweeping gesture across his chest] MJ - "This body is like a double-edged sword. It can make a lady fall over me, because i'm so sexy. But you know what else? This body can hurt people, too." [Justice grabs a water bottle and moves over in front of a nearby mirror. He takes a gulp of water from the bottle, and then tosses it over his shoulder. He smiles and begins to flex in front of the mirror] MJ - "That's right, all you girly boys aren't going to have a very fun weekend..." [Camera fades out] JH - "Have you and Mikey talked yet?" JG - "No. Why?" JH - "I figured with his looks and all, you'd be jumping all over him." JG - "I'm not into that and I'm sure Mike's not, either. Anyways, Justice probably has an outside chance at this thing. His main skills are technical, but those, as stated about ten times earlier, are of no use here. Instead, he'll have to rely on his size to find a new style more suited to this match." JH - "Or, he can just amaze everybody with his good looks. Do you think these pipes match up with Justice?" JG - "Not even close." :: Sengir [Fade in: the reception of a rather-expensive looking hotel. A blonde woman sits behind the reception desk, she is wearing heavy make up and a red suit. Boys in similar suits are running around, busily trying to fulfill their guests' wishes. The quality of the furniture and the candelabras hanging from the ceiling indicate a very expensive hotel. Suddenly, the door swings open, and through the door we can see that it is snowing heavily. Sengir and Jezra Wagner walk in, both carrying a suitcase. Sengir is a man in maybe his early-twenties, his long black hair is hanging down to his shoulders and is covered in snow. He is wearing a washed-out blue jeans and a black jeans jacket over a black "Sisters of Mercy" t-shirt. Jezra is a stunning beauty in maybe her midtwenties, her whitish-blond hair falls to her shoulderblades. Her pale skin is almost alabaster. She is wearing a white blouse, unbuttoned to reveal the delicate curves of her small, firm breasts. She is wearing a short blue jeans, cut off above her knees, and Roman sandals, accenting her sculpted calves. Her finger- and toenails are painted in a shimmering silver. Sengir shivers due to the cold and walks up to the receptionist] S - "Hello... brrrrrrrrrr.... is this the... what's it called..." [He flickers through some papers from his wallet, then pulls out a small pamphlet] S - "...Silver Swan hotel?" Receptionist - "I am sorry, Sir, but we have to ask you to leave. The Silver Swan has a very strict dress code, and we cannot permit..." J - ""Look, snotnose. We've reserved, and SCRA bigwigs have it all paid for. I'm Jez Wagner, and he's Sengir, you'd better open your baby blues real wide and check your book." Receptionist - "I am sorry, Ms.Wagner, Mr.Sengir, I could not know. Of course, the SCRA officials have instructed us with full coverage of our needs, and we will make sure that your luggage will be brought to your suite." [Sengir wipes some snow off of his hair, looking a little displeased] S - "Say, Missy, how many stars does this hotal have?" Receptionist - "The Silver Swan has four stars and we have provided our customers with luxury accomodation since 1837. We have a heated indoor swimming pool and a very exquisite bar with a dancing floor and SCRA has instructed us to reserve our well-equipped gym for your convenience, we also have a ski resort and..." J - "Four stars? Will do. Now, where's that boy who was meant to carry our stuff?" S - "Huh, come on, let's go to our room. I want a hot bath and go to bad. Damn, Canada is cold!" J - "Hm, I kinda like it..." S - "That's cause you're from Siberia. And if you ever throw a snowball at me again I'll make sure you'll sleep in the kitchen." J - "Heh, couldn't resist. But I'm all for a hot bath... now where did that kid go who was meant to carry my bags?" [A boy in a red suit walks up, bows to Jezra, and grabs her and Sengir's bags. Sengir chuckles, pets him on the head, and stuffs a dollar into his suit. All three are walking towards into the elevator, and the boy presses the uppermost button. The doors close, and the elevator starts moving upwards...] J - "Hey, boy... yeah, you... what's your name?" Boy - "Uhm, me? My name is Guillaume, Mylady, and I have been instructed to do whatever you ask me..." J - "Guillaume. Cute. I always liked those French-Canadian names. Hey, Guillaume, I was going to take a bath, and I need someone to rub my back... care to join me?" [Guillaume immediately turns red and swallows hard, gasping for air. Jezra has a big, cheesy grin on her face, and Sengir smirks] Guillaume - "Uh, I don't think I can... uhm... do that... I... uhm..." [Jezra steps next to poor Guillaume and teasingly rubs her perfect body against his, stroking gently over his hair. Guillaume's face is bright red] J(acting offended) - "But Guillaume... are you trying to say that you don't like me...?" Guillaume - "Nnnngh... no... I mean, uhm, I... who wouldn't... but..." [The elevator has reached it's destination, and the doors open] S - "Come on, Jez, let's try again when that dude has reached puberty." [Sengir and Jezra grab their bags and leave the elevator, Jezra teasingly blows a kiss to poor Guillaume as the scene fades to black] JH - "She's pretty good lookin', eh?" JG - "I guess..." JH - "Oh, you wouldn't care anyways." JG - "(sighs) Sengir's got little chance of this, but that never stopped him before. He's been known to step up at challenges and meet them head on..." JH - "And butt heads, then fall back on his German butt." JG - "...This will probably be his next challenge. But, his size does not go well in this event." JH - "He just doesn't go well. That's what you get when hiring Germans, though." :: "The Iconoclast" Sean Watts JG - "The enigma of the league. I don't really know how Watt is going to do. It seems like he doesn't care about matches, but that isn't the case. Does he care about this one? We'll find out tomorrow night. Even if he does, though, it's going to be tough to win due to his size and wrestling skills." JH - "This Watts is a weird one. He puts himself down and in the process builds his opponent's confidence." JG - "He does have an odd outlook. Then again, so do most of the people in this league." JH - "Yeah.
