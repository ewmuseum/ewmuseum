[SCRA] Fatal Fall, Part 2

13 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
An SCRA Production http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html [Green Knight walks down the aisle, accompanied by his best man, Lifetaker, and all of the "Face" wrestlers in SCRA are already in the ring. Rush, Sgt. Pepper (with a bandage on his head), "Hot" Brad Baldwin, Rock Bottom Robbie, Killer Compton. Charon Wraith also makes an appearance for his former ally.] ["Here Comes the Bride" begins to play, and Squire Kathleen Pijor walks down the aisle in a beautiful wedding dress and walks into the ring. She takes Green Knight by the hand and the minister begins how to speak.] Minister - "Dearly beloved, we are gathered here today to join these two people in holy matrimony. The ring, please." [Sgt. Pepper is the ring bearer, and he gives them the rings.] GK - "With this ring, I thee wed." KP - "With this ring, I thee wed." M - "Do you, Green Knight, take this lady to be your husband, to have and to hold, for better or for worse, in sickness and in health, til death do you part?" GK - "I do." M - "Do you, Kathleen Pijor, take this man to be your husband, to have and to hold, for better or for worse, in sickness and health, til death do yuo part?" KP - "I do." M - "Well, if anyone has any reason why these two should not be wed, please come forward now, or forever hold your peace..." [No one answers the call] M - "Well, by the power invested in me by God and the province of Ontario, I now pronounce you man and wife. You may kiss the bride." [They kiss, and there is that big music at the end of a wedding. Lifetaker sprays champagne at GK, who simply laughs. Even Tyrany Dios congratulates Green Knight.] JG - "So touching! I love weddings!" JH - "Why? This one sucked, there weren't any good chicks." JG - "Look in the crowd, I am sure you will find one." JH - "No, I checked before the show. The only people here are fat, lazy bums." JG - "Our typical wrestling fan! We love you guys. Just stay on those couches, eating chips every Monday night." JH - "That's enough for the shameless promotion. Might I add, though, go far Lays chips!" JG - "Jack, Doritos are our sponsors!" JH - "So, this isn't the Scraps, is it?" JG - "The tag team titles are on the line in the next match. The Pros escaped with a win over Justice Inc. However, the way they won it was not convincing." JH - "Nor were any of their wins." JG - "With Justice Inc. splitting up, The Pros had an easy victory. House of Lords are coming in thinking they can beat the Pros, who aren't the "real" champs." JH - "Hey Grey, what do you say you and I step in the ring one time for the tag team championship? Get some of that hanging weight of ya." JG - "I don't think so." JH - "Come on! Jackie boy will teach you how to do it!" JG - "Let's leave the wrestling to our superstars. Tommy Fetters has the announcements for this matchup." ____________________________________________________________________________ |_____ Match 2 | |___ House of Lords vs. The Pros | Writer: CW&AG |_________________________________________________________________________ TF - "This matchup is scheduled for one fall for the SCRA Tag Team titles! Coming down the aisle first, your SCRA champions, at a total combined weight of 490 lbs. and accompanied by Miss Frank, here are Tavis B and Grant, The Pros!" [The Ramones come over the speakers as the Pros walk slowly to the ring, lead by Miss Frank. There SCRA tag titles are draped over there shoulders as they smack hands with a few fans. Grant holds the ropes open for Miss Frank as they step into the ring.] JG - "The Pros spending considerably more time with the fans. Since winning the belts, they have been more and more receptive to the fans." TF - "And their opponents, coming from Fredericton, New Brunswick, weighing in at a total combined weight of 490 lbs., the House of Lords!" [For Whom the Bell Tolls blares over the speakers as Aladdin Styles and Jason Gore walk through the curtain. They ignore fans, only stopping to tell some to "shut up", as they get to the ring they whisper something to eachother and then get into the ring.] JG - "Both teams weighing the exact same amount. It is distributed differently between them, though." [Grant and Styles start off, Grant takes the early advantage taking Styles down with a pair of armdrags. Styles gets up and tags out to Gore. Grant continues with armdrags getting Gore off balance. Gore rolls to the outside to confer with Styles. After about 7 seconds Gore gets back into the ring.] JH - "See the smarts by the House of Lords? When things aren't going your way, take a breather and get right back into it. I know I have used that piece of advice late at night, if you know what I mean." JG - "Unfortunately, I can usually pick up on what you mean." [Gore kicks Grant in the gut and follows that with two headbutts sending Grant to the mat. Gore picks him up and delivers a body slam followed by a knee drop. Gore tags out to Styles who whips Grant into the ropes, he puts his head down early and Grant delivers a ddt.] JG - "The Pros fighting back. Gore made a big mistake there, going down way too early." JH - "That will be the only mistake he makes, I guarantee it." [Grant slowly gets to his feet and tags out to Tavis who flys in delivering several blows to Styles head, sending him to the outside.] JG - "Hot tag! Look out!" [Tavis follows him out, Gore and Grant come over and the four men brawl on the floor. Gore goes flying into the railing, and Styles is sent back into the ring by Tavis.] JG - "The referee has to regain control of this matchup or it will get out of hand." JH - "Let them fight on the floor for a bit. A little out of the ring brawling never hurt." [Tavis picks up Styles and delivers a belly to belly. He grabs Styles legs and applies a boston crab. Gore jumps into the ring and breaks the hold, but Grant also comes in. Grant clotheslines Styles over the ropes as Gore and Tavis are exchanging blows inside the ring.] JG - "Again, this match getting out of control. The referee has to step in and take control." JH - "The referee's can't do a thing. That's why they are always the getting beat up. We need to teach them how to fight. Get them involved in the matches. Wouldn't that be something, Grey." [The ref forces Grant and Gore out of the ring, as Styles finally gets back into the ring.] JG - "That was some forceful action by the referee. Can't argue with somebody who will do that." JH - "Yeah you can, you just might not win." JG - "Then what is the poink of arguing?" JH - "To annoy people." JG - "No it's not." JH - "Yes it is!" JG - "No it's not." JH - "Yes it is!" JG - "Jack, you are starting to annoy me." JH - "See? It works!" [Tavis goes for a punch, but Styles ducks and catchs Tavis in a belly to back suplex. Styles goes for the cover.] JG - "New champs? No! Kickout by Tavis." JH - "Almost there, Styles. Keep it up, buddy." [Styles manages to get to his corner and tags Gore, he catches Tavis before he can tag Grant, and Gore goes to work. He pummels Tavis with kicks, and stomps. He picks up Tavis and delivers an atomic drop followed by a running clothesline sending Tavis high in the air.] JH - "Look at that height! Gore has some power in his little body!" JG - "He doesn't use it often, but when he does, he shines." [Gore drops an elbow and then drags Tavis over to the corner. He climbs the ropes and delivers a leg drop and then goes for a cover.] JH - "Come on Jason! You got this one!" JG - "Another kickout by Tavis." [Gore pushes Tavis into his corner as he distracts the ref and Styles begins choking Tavis.] JG - "Get in there ref! Why do referees always fall for these tricks?" JH - "Because, as I have said a million times before, referees are morons! Why do you think they sign up to be in a job with all the risks that refereeing has?" JG - "The glory and honour." JH - "What glory? What honour?" [Tavis falls over as Gore tags in Styles. Styles stomps on Taviss face and then chokes Tavis with his boot. The ref warns Styles. Styles picks up Tavis and whips him into the ropes, Tavis ducks a clothesline and then hits Styles with a flying forearm. Tavis crawls to his corner and manages to tag Grant who comes in with a fury. Delivering body slam after body slam to Styles.] JG - "Hot tag for Grant! He is cleaning house with Styles." [Grant then whips Styles into the ropes and delivers a shoulder block sending Styles to the mat. Gore, on the outside walks over to the Pros corner and starts to taunt Tavis, who attacks Gore, they're brawling on the floor. The ref jumps to the outside to break the two apart.] JG - "Gore with an unprovoked attack on Tavis. The referee, once again, seems to be distracted by this." JH - "Styles should take this time to get Grant. Tavis isn't there to screw it up." [Gore goes back to his corner grabbing a chair. Tavis is still trying to get to Gore, but the ref is holding him back. On the inside Grant is still dominating. He whips Styles into the ropes and runs into the other side ropes, where Gore is waiting with the chair. He nails Grant with it, Styles rolls up the unconsious man. The ref gets back into the ring.] JH - "Get in there ref! Count for the new champs!" JG - "1......2.....3! House of Lords are the new tag team champs!" JH - "Finally! At long last, a good tag team champion in this league!" [The Activists from Hell run down to ringside. They whisper something to the referee who looks astonished.] JH - "What are those two idiots doing?" JG - "I don't know, but the referee wants to say something. He is getting Fetters to do it." TF - "The referee has just been informed that this is not the real Aladdin Styles!" JH - "What! You are kidding!" [Aladdin Styles comes running out, freeing himself of ropes which apparently kept him backstage. He runs at the imposter Styles who jets out of the ring. The imposter rolls under the ring as Styles gives up chase.] JG - "Who was the imposter? Why did he tie up Styles? What did the Activists from Hell have to do with it?" JH - "All these questions answered, tonight on 20/20." TF - "This match will continue with the real Aladdin Styles!" [The House of Lords seem to be exchanging heated words with the Activists, who are apparently responsible for the mix up. The Activists grin and walk back to the locker room.] JG - "I guess the Activists had something to do with it! They did want revenge against the House of Lords, but who is the imposter?" JH - "Let's get Leslie Nielson out here!" [The House of Lords still look stunned. The Pros see this and attack the team. Tavis grabs Gore and bodyslams him while Grant clotheslines Styles over the top ropes. Styles lands hard on the outside. He looks under the ring.] JG - "Styles making sure the imposter isn't there." JH - "Where did he go?" JG - "Under the ring, but it appears that he isn't there anymore." [Tavis continues to work on Gore. He picks Gore up and throws him into the ropes, nailing a lariat. Tavis goes off the ropes himself and hits an elbowdrop. Tavis puts on an armbar, which stays on for a few seconds, but Styles runs in and breaks it up. Grant tries to get in the ring, but the referee holds him back. Styles continues to kick Tavis as the ref wasn't looking.] JH - "Great tag team strategy by the House of Lords." JG - "Don't you mean great cheating?" JH - "It isn't cheating unless you get caught. Once the referee turns around, they will stop as if nothing happened. Pretty slick." [Styles exits the ring just before the referee turns around. Gore picks up Tavis and whips him into the ropes. Gore nails a dropkick. He runs over and grabs Tavis before he rolls outside.] JH - "Sneaky bastard! Trying to get away from the punishment." [Gore drags Tavis in the middle of the ring and applies a figure four. Tavis holds on, but screams in pain.] JG - "Tavis doesn't want to give up the gold! He isn't giving up!" [Grant runs into the ring, but is met by Styles who flies across with a cross body block. This sends both men over the top ropes. The referee follows them outside, trying to break them up.] JH - "Get in there, ref. Tavis is going to give up and you have to be ready for it." [The German Eagles sprint to the ring. With the ref's back turned, both Eagles get in the ring, Joseph carrying a chair. Gore lets go of the hold. Joseph advances on Gore. He winds up for the chair shot, but Gore flings Tavis in front of him. The chair whacks Tavis, knocking him out.] JG - "Joseph Eagle just nailed Tavis with the chair!" JH - "Pin him!" JG - "The Eagles better get out of there before the ref turns around." [The Eagles flee the scene. Gore jumps on Tavis. Styles tells the ref to get back in the ring. The ref slides in and counts the one, two, three. Grant rolls under just after the three, trying to free his partner.] JG - "New Tag Team Champs!" JH - "For real?" JG - "For real! The referee didn't see all the chair action and is giving the House of Lords the gold!" JH - "And I didn't even need to get involved. Excuse me while I congratulate my team." [Jack wriggles free of his chair and climbs into the ring. The Pros walk away with Tavis a little woozy. Jack whispers something in the House of Lords ear as they celebrate. The Lords slide under the ring, continuing to celebrate on their way to the locker room. Jack returns to the table.] JG - "What did you say to them?" JH - "I said I was sorry I couldn't be in their corner, but I was with them in spirit. Those morons in the offices told me that if I was to get involved, they would fire me. Normally, I would lunge at the chance to get out of this job, but it is porn month on channel 48, pay per view. I need all the money I can get." JG - "I see. Your whimsical ways never cease to amaze." JH - "When did you learn poetry?" JG - "That just came to my head. I don't know where I learned it." JH - "Well, cut it out. You are beginning to become as bad as the loser, Phantom." JG - "He is a much more skilled poet than myself. That just came up. Much like Rock Bottom Robbie who skyrocketed himself to the top by winning the North American belt. He still has to fighgt Silver Stone, though." JH - "This could be the final blow in an illustrous feud. They have fought backstage, in the ring, outside and even in the memorable billiard room brawl. Silver Stone will finally put the punk, Robbie where he should be, at the bottom of the rankings." JG - "I don't know about that. Robbie is a changed man since winning the gold. He has exhibiting a lot of confidence. That should carry over today and prove to help him." JH - "Confidence will not help you against somebody the caliber of the silver man." JG - "That has yet to be seen. Let's see what happens. Tommy, the introductions, please."
