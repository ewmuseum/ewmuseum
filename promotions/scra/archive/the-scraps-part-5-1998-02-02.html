[SCRA] The Scraps [5/5] (2 February 1998)

2 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 6 ) | | \ | | Derek Mota vs. "Silverblade" Anthony Logan / |___ | | | | Writer: GR (_________________________________________________________________________ [The camera pans down to the ring, where Ed Jaspard stands, mic in hand] EJ - "Ladies and gentlemen, your next event is set for one fall. Your first competitor..." [Pantera's "The Great Southern Trendkill" blares from the arena speakers as Derek Mota splits the entrance curtain and makes his way to the ring. Mota ignores the fans, despite their _huge_ pop and attempts to reach out and touch him as he walks the ramp.] EJ - "... he hails from Toronto, Ontario and weighs in at 224 lbs, he is DEREK MOTA!" [Big crowd pop as Mota hits the ring and begins to stretch...] EJ - "And his opponent..." [Aerosmith's "Walk This Way" replaces Pantera as Anthony Logan splits the curtain and heads down the ramp. A fan reaches out to touch him, but Logan slaps the fan's hand away. The fan spills his beer and gives Logan the finger, but Logan turns back towards the ring...] JG - "Mota's outside and he's attacking Logan!" JH - "Go get him, Mota!" [Mota lays in the punches to Logan's head, then grabs him and whips him into the guardrail! Mota charges... nobody home! Logan moves and Mota crashes into the guardrail! Logan pulls Mota from behind and lays a forearm into the small of his back! Mota falls forward and Logan puts the boots to him!] JG - "The referee needs to gain control here!" JH - "No way! Let 'em go at it!" [The ref is outside, he admonishes Logan to take it to the ring! Logan pulls Mota up by the hair and pulls him to the ring, rolling him inside! DING! DING! DING! Logan rolls in, but Mota is up and he stomps Logan! Mota pulls Logan to his feet, irish-whip! Logan bounces off the ropes... flying forearm by Mota puts Logan on his back! Mota leaps... flying elbow-drop! Here's a cover... 1! 2! JG - "No! Logan kicked out!" JH - "Wishful thinking by Mota there!" [Mota pulls Logan to his feet and sets him up... snap suplex! Mota pops up and propels himself into the ropes... flying splash!] JG - "No! Logan got the knees up into Mota's midsection!" JH - "Mota's not hurt! He can't be hurt!" [Logan gets up slowly and grabs Mota, pulling him to his feet. He lays in a couple of punches to Mota's head, then irish-whips him into the ropes! Mota rebounds... running lariat by Logan puts Mota on his back! Logan stomps Mota, then pulls him up draping him across the middle rope! Logan sits on Mota's back, forcing his throat down across the rope! the ref counts to 4, and Logan releases! Logan charges across the ring, off the ropes...] JG - "Flying sitdown by Logan!" JH - "What the hell is a 'flying sitdown'?" JG - "Whatever that move is called! You know, that one..." JH - "Whatever." [Logan pulls Mota from the ropes and sets him up... vertical suplex! Here's a cover... 1! 2! JG - "No, Mota got the shoulder up!" JH - "We almost had a winner there!" [Logan pulls Mota up... European uppercut! Another! Mota is backed against the ropes! Logan backs up and charges, clotheslining Mota over the top rope and to the floor!] JG - "Mota hit hard! This could be it!" JH - "Are you kidding me? You could run over Mota with a truck and he'd keep coming back!" [Logan steps through the ropes and leaps from the apron... flying legdrop! Logan pops up quickly and pulls Mota to his feet as the referee counts!] 3.....4.....5..... [Logan grabs Mota by the hair and throws him into the ring steps! Mota cringeds in pain, holding his head! Logan grabs him and pulls him up... but Mota lands a punch to the gut! Another! Another! Mota is up...] JG - "DDT! Mota hit Logan with a DDT on the outside!" [Mota rolls back into the ring to stop the count, but scales the turnbuckle!] JG - "What is this madman going to do now?" JH - "My guess would be something suicidal, homicidal, geno..." JG - "That'll be enough of that!" JH - "Sorry." [Logan makes his way to his feet and Mota leaps...] JG - "Oh my God! Mota with a plancha from the top rope! Both men hit the guardrail there!" JH - "Unbelieveable!" [Mota slowly gets to his feet and pulls Logan back up, rolling him into the ring! Mota rolls jumps onto the apron and scales the turnbuckle from outside! Logan slowly gets to this feet and turns... missle dropkick by Mota! Mota scrambles over for the cover... 1! 2! 3! JG - "He got him!" JH - "No! Logan got the shoulder up!" JG - "Look at Mota! Boy, is he pissed!" [Mota is up and in the referee's face, but the ref admonishes him that the count was good! Mota turns around and pulls Logan to his feet, setting him up... Northern Lights Suplex! Logan hits hard, but Mota pops up and drops a knee across Logan's forehead! Logan grabs his head, but Mota is up and he hurdles the prone Logan and jumps, bouncing off the scond rope...] JG - "Moonsault! Here's a cover!" 1! 2! JG - "No! Logan kicked out!" [Mota slaps the mat in frustration, and gets up, pulling Logan to his feet! Mota irish-whips Logan... no! Reveresed by Logan, Mota into the corner! Mota grabs the rope and leaps as Logan charges in, but Logan grabs him and turns... powerslam! Logan covers...] 1! 2! [Mota kicks out! Logan pulls Mota to his feet and sets him up... belly-to-belly suplex! Logan pops up and propels himself into the ropes... flying legdrop! Here's another cover!] 1! 2! JG - "No! Mota kicked out! Man, this kid can take a lot of punishment!" JH - "The human pinball!" [Logan pulls Mota to his feet and irish-whips him into the ropes! Logan bends down for the back body-drop, but Mota catches him by the head... spinning neckbreaker! Both men are down!] JG - "The referee's counting both men down!" 1.....2.....3.....4..... [Mota rolls to the ropes and pulls himself up, but Logan is up! Mota charges Logan... running lariat! Logan is down, Mota heads to the corner! Mota scales the turnbuckle, facing the crowd! He thrusts his arms in the air and leaps...] JG - "Moonsault!" JH - "And there's nobody home! Logan moved at the last second!" JG - "Logan needs to go for the cover right now!" [Logan crawls over to Mota and covers him!] 1! 2! JG - "Unbelieveable! Mota got the shoulder up!" JH - "How many times can we see this kid do this?" [Logan gets up, pulling Mota up. He pulls Mota to the corner and nails a knife-edge chop! Another! He picks Mota up and sits him on the top turnbuckle!] JG - "Logan's going in for the kill!" [Logan scales the turnbuckle and sets Mota up... no! Mota delivers a punch to the midsection! Another! Another! Logan leans back and Mota shoves him from the turnbuckle!] JG - "Mota's got the upperhand! He's... wait a minute, who is this?" JH - "It's Aladdin Style!" [Style runs to ringside as Mota stands up on the turnbuckle! Style hops up on the apron and shoves Mota off the turnbuckle to the mat! The referee turns and calls for the bell!] JG - "Style has interfered! He's cost his brother the match!" JH - "That's not gonna sit well with Logan!" [Style jumps in the ring and begins pummelling Mota, who can barely defend himself! Style straddles Mota and lays the punches in, but Logan comes up from behind and pulls Style off of Mota! Style pops up, Logan is in his face!] JH - "Get him, Logan!" JG - "Mota's rolling out of the ring to escape the beatdown and Logan and Style are arguing!" [The referee steps in between the two brothers, and Style gives Logan the evil-eye as he leaves the ring.] JG - "Looks like some unfinished business there between Logan and Style!" JH - "I'm thinking cage match, whadda ya think, Grey?" JG - "Maybe... maybe." JH - "Your brother won't go for it. His idea of hardcore is a foam nerfball as a foreign object." JG - "That's very true. But, we've got to move onto the main event! "Cowboy" Ken Curtis gets his shot at the title against Eddy "Flap" Jacks!" JH - "A real slugfest." JG - "It sure will be. Let's go to Ed for the intros." ___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 7 ) | | \ | | "Cowboy" Ken Curtis vs. Eddy "Flap" Jacks / |___ | | | | Writer: AG (_________________________________________________________________________ [The lights go dark save a spotlight over Ed Jaspard.] EJ - "Ladies and gentlemen, at this special event, I present to you... The Jaspard Girls!" [The lights go on as a group of girls, aged no more than 4 stand in the corners of the ring. They start dancing, Jaspard following with them. Ed doesn't have the same coordination, though, and tumbles into one of the girls, setting off a chain reaction and knocking down each girl.] JH - "Grey, new announcer. I'm begging you." [Jaspard gets back up, ego slightly hurt, again.] EJ - "This matchup is scheduled for one fall with a remaining TV time limit! It is for the SCRA World championship title! Coming down the aisle first, hailing from Austin, Texas and weighing in at 260 lbs., "Cowboy" Ken Curtis!" [Gimme back my Bullets by Lynard Skynard fills the stadium PA system as Curtis jogs into the arena. The fans boo heavily, forcing Curtis to stop and attack a group sitting at ringside. He continues to the ring, takes off his hat and vest and awaits the champ.] EJ - "And, his opponent, weighing in at 398 lbs. and hailing from Ottawa, Ontario, he is accompanied to the ring by "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin, here comes Eddy "Flap" Jacks!" [The Dance of the Sugar Plum Fairies comes over the speakers as Jacks comes into the arena, Hardin walking right beside him. The two seem to be talking over some sort of strategy.] JH - "Where's the rest of the House of Pancakes?" JG - "I guess Hardin provides the protection. They're probably backstage with the Praetorian Guard, too." DING! DING! DING! JG - "And, we're underway!" [As Jacks climbs onto the apron, Curtis runs at him with a flying knee. Jacks gets knocked off the apron into Hardin who stops the big man's fall. Hardin calms Jacks, talks strategy and releases the champ again.] JG - "Surprisingly, J.W. Hardin is acting as a calming influence for Eddy Jacks." JH - "I'm still surprised at the amount of beef we have around the ring. We've got more pounds than Rhode Island!" [Jacks climbs onto the apron, Curtis tries the flying knee again, but Jacks moves out of the way, sending Curtis bouncing off the ropes and onto the mat. Jacks places his arms around the ropes and flips himself over the top, legdropping Curtis. Jacks gets back up and gives a thumbs up to Hardin. Hardin just stares back.] JG - "I guess a little too calm. Hardin's got the ice cold glare he is known for. He wants to put it to Curtis." JH - "Defeats always eat away at you until you start eating back. Of course, it's hard to eat a human, unless you're somebody like Jacks. I guess Hardin wants Jacks to eat Curtis!" JG - "That would certainly add some more bulk to Jacks' already immense proportions." [Jacks applies a headlock which Curtis quickly gets out of with swift elbows to the midsection. Curtis pushes Jacks into the ropes, drops to the mat, forcing Jacks to jump over him, gets back up and hits a spinebuster. Curtis keeps a hold of Jacks' legs and catapults him into the turnbuckle. Jacks bounces off only to be caught with a belly to back suplex by Curtis.] JG - "Curtis is firing on all cylinders! He's really going after Jacks right now." JH - "It only takes three moves to wear Jacks out. Few can get three moves in, but once you've got three, Jacks is too tired to continue." [Curtis climbs to the second rope and leaps off with a fistdrop. He quickly moves towards Jacks' head and applies a front facelock. Jacks screams in pain, but is able to move himself towards the ropes and get his foot out, breaking the hold. Curtis releases it and sends Jacks running into the ropes. He attempts a lariat, but Jacks ducks underneath. Jacks comes back and hits a football tackle on Curtis, rolls onto Curtis' chest and starts unleashing a series of rights and lefts.] JG - "Jacks is in his game now. He's going nuts on Ken Curtis!" JH - "Jacks goes nuts on anything made of meat. For that matter, Jacks would go nuts on nuts!" [Curtis kicks Jacks off, then starts throwing his own series of punches. He moves behind Jacks and applies a chinlock. Jacks easily works his way out, using his massive arms to lift Curtis' arms off Jacks' chin. Jacks delivers an elbow to the midsection. He works his way up with repeated elbows. Once completely on his feet, the champ runs into the ropes, coming at Curtis with a clothesline. Curtis ducks the clothesline and on Jacks' way back, catches him with a back body drop.] JG - "How did he get Jacks up there? Jacks weighs well over 300 pounds, almost 400, yet Curtis boosted him into the air like nothing!" JH - "Once that incredible mass gets flying, it goes." [Curtis is quick to lift up Jacks, hitting a scoop slam. He runs off the ropes, but misses an elbowdrop. Curtis stays on the mat for a moment, but gets up first, walking over to Jacks. Jacks is able to hit an eyerake, giving him time to get up. Jacks gets onto his feet and starts an attack of European uppercuts. He pushes Curtis back into the ropes, then sends Curtis running. As Curtis bounces off the ropes, Jacks runs at him with a clothesline, sending Curtis over the top. Hardin walks over to Curtis.] JG - "This could get ugly. Hardin is itching to get at Curtis and he might have the chance." JH - "The nails in the coffin." [Hardin walks over to Curtis and tosses him back in the ring.] JG - "Huh?" JH - "Uh...he wanted to wait until later for revenge." [Jacks nails a gutwrench suplex and quickly covers... 1! KICKOUT! Jacks goes to pick up Curtis, but is met by a chop to the throat. Curtis gets up and grabs Jacks in a belly to belly suplex. Curtis holds down Jacks' arm and lifts himself into the air into a handstand. He thrusts his knee down hard at the hand. Curtis gets up and begins stomping on the arm.] JG - "He's going to break Jacks' arm!" JH - "Where will the belt go if the arm's gone? Nowhere to sling it." JG - "That would be the shoulder, Jack. You sling the belt over your shoulder." JH - "Same thing." [Curtis lifts Jacks to his feet, but Jacks rakes the eyes. Jacks follows this up with a kick to the midsection and a punch to the face. He continues to deliver hard rights and lefts, moving Curtis back into the turnbuckle. Jacks whips Curtis into the opposite corner, following in with a flying splash. Curtis moves away, sending Jacks crashing face first into the turnbuckle. Curtis dropkicks Jacks to the mat as he bounced off the turnbuckle.] JH - "How many face rakes have we seen in this match?" JG - "I don't know. About three or four." JH - "That's good. Good matches have upwards of nine. In fact, the best matches involve all face rakes. You can tell when people wrestle in those matches because beside their eyes are a series of scratch marks resembling a cat's whiskers." [Curtis grabs Jacks' arm and applies an armbar. Jacks is able to reverse the move and gets to his feet, attacking Curtis with a series of European uppercuts. Jacks sends Curtis running into the ropes, but Curtis is able to control himself and take down Jacks with a drop toe hold. He quickly applies a Texas Cloverleaf.] JG - "Curtis' got the move hooked on! He's been working on this one and he may have won a title with it!" JH - "Jacks is not going to quit with this one. That World title means too much to him." JG - "I don't know if he has a choice! With the pain he's experiencing right now, it's better to save his career than keep the title." JH - "Careers are over-rated. If Jacks' career ends, he can always get a broadcasting job." [Curtis continues with the hold, applying even more pressure to the lower back of Jacks. However, Jacks doesn't give up. He continues to fight his way around the mat, trying to free himself. Jacks gets himself into push up position and kicks off Curtis!] JG - "What strength by the champ!" JH - "There's more to that blubber than just fat. There's muscle down there, too." [Jacks gets up slowly, then drops Curtis with a running clothesline. Curtis gets back up, but is met by an atomic drop. Curtis stays on his feet for a second, but is again dropped by a running clothesline by Jacks.] JH - "His moves are limited, but he executes them with power." [Jacks hops onto the mat and applies a dragon sleeper.] JH - "Maybe there's no power in this move. I guess everybody has to know one boring move, right?" JG - "This is a very effective move. I don't know what the hell it does, but it's got to be effective if everybody uses it." JH - "Right. Peer pressure!" [Jacks gets to his feet, still holding Curtis in the dragon sleeper, then lifts Curtis up in an inverted suplex. He drops Curtis onto the mat. Jacks runs off the ropes and nails an elbowdrop, then lifts Curtis to his feet and connects with a doctor bomb. Jacks goes for the cover... 1! 2! KICKOUT! Curtis rolls underneath the ropes as Jacks follows. Jacks attempts to lift Curtis up, but Curtis gets a quick shoulderblock in. He leaps over the ropes into a sunset flip... 1! KICKOUT!] JG - "Near pinfall by both men!" JH - "Yes, those one counts keep me on the edge of my seat." [Both men get up at the same time, staring at each other. Jacks runs at Curtis with a lariat, but Curtis ducks underneath and catches Jacks in a sidewalk slam. Jacks rolls back up, but is met by a kick to the midsection by Curtis. Curtis runs into the ropes and comes in with a field goal-like kick. Jacks flies into the center of the ring, allowing Curtis to climb to the top rope and take off with a legdrop.] JH - "See him skid on the mat? I love watching that move when they have to jump far! They bounce!" JG - "That's nice, Jack. If you can do better, I'd like to see it." JH - "I'm not saying I can do better. It's just that it's fun to watch a bunch of old men skid on their butts!" [Curtis picks up Jacks and drops him to the mat with a double underhook suplex. He quickly runs off the ropes and drops the knee across the chest of the champ. As Curtis yanks his knee out of the blubber, Jacks gets up on his own. Curtis drops him again with a Northern Lights suplex. As Jacks attempts to get up, Curtis moves in with an elbow to the back of the head, dropping Jacks to the mat. Curtis lifts Jacks to his feet, then drops him quickly with a Russian legsweep. Curtis climbs to the second ropes and drops with a forearm drop. Curtis gets back up and runs into the ropes, but Jacks is waiting with his head bent over for a back body drop. Curtis sees him, though, and stops and nails a swining neckbreaker. He goes for the cover... 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Curtis is going nuts on Jacks! He's dropped Jacks more times than a Jose Canseco drops flyballs!" JH - "He's coming up here, eh?" JG - "I know. I shudder at the tought." [Jacks rolls out of the ring to talk things over with Hardin. Curtis waits in the ring, urging Jacks to come back. Hardin looks up at Curtis, waving his finger as in "one second". Hardin turns back to Jacks, kicks him in the midsection and nails a Cattle Buster!] JG - "Hardin nailed Jacks with the Cattle Buster! He turned on Eddy Jacks!" JH - "This guy never ceases to amaze me." [Hardin rolls Jacks back in the ring, allowing Curtis to pin him. Hardin walks away as the ref starts the count... 1! 2! As the referee is about to drop his hand for the three count, the video screen lights up.] GI - "Damn Curtis, what the hell de ya feed deese ho-ses? Dey smell as bad as yo mamma afta eatin' a truckload uh chilidogs." [The ref resumes the count... 3!] JG - "New champ!" [Curtis gets off Jacks and watches the video screen.] JH - "That Curtis is a tough cookie." [The scene opens up with Gangsta Impasse and John Drake are raking horse maneur into a small corner. Shooter McNutty, Street Smarts and Tommy Gregory supervise while Gangsta and Drake look at each other, nod and apply noseplugs. Gangsta starts to speak with his speech a little high due to the noseplugs.] GI - "When I went inte dat match, ma whole reputation waz on de line. I knew I could beatchoo an' had no problems proovin' it. But when you moved frum dat divin' headbutt, I felt what happens when a rookie meets a veteran an' makes too many risks." [Gangsta shovels a shovelfull of horse maneur and throws it, almost hitting Gregory.] SS - "AW MAN! I GOTTA SHIT!" SM - "So go, almighty. [Shooter cocks his finger shooting Street Smarts.]" [As the two men shovel, Street Smarts leaves for a potty break.] GI - "Curtis, you think that since you've gotten a title shot, you can beat the fat man? Hell buoy, he got a whole House 'hind him, dey're ain't no way he gon lose te a guy like ya wit no protection." [The three continue shovelling as a car horn is heard in the back. Street Smarts drives Gangsta Impasse's red benz through the barnyard door and hits Gregory.] GI - "Streetz, you ma man. I knew I could count an ya'll te make sure Greggie couldn't stop us." [Gangsta Impasse opens up the trunk of his car and pulls out a stereo. He turns it up to full blast as rap music blares from the speakers. Gangsta throws the rakes on Tommy Gregory.] GI - "Hey Curtis, say goo'bye te yer goats an' yer chickens. Say goo'bye te yer horses an' yer cows, cause dis i' de last time yer eva seein' em." [Gangsta, Drake, Street Smarts and Shooter McNutty begin trashing Curtis' barn, ripping down the wood and throwing the horse shit and hay all over the place. The basically finish trashing it as they all get in Gangsta's car.] GI - "An now, de nail in de coffin." [Gangsta strikes a match as it sparks on a piece of wood. The fire eventually spreads as the four take off in Gangsta's benz. The scene finishes with a video of the barn and Gregory laying on the floor.] -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
