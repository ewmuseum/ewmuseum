SCRA — Rules & Structure

[SCRA] Info Guide

13 October 1997

My name is [name withheld], president of SCRA. I just posted our second ever pay per view, Fatal Fall. I think the show went very well and hope anybody who read it enjoyed it. But, now to the task at hand: recruiting. We are looking for both singles and tag team wrestlers. If you are interested, please continue reading below as it states basic information about the league, followed by an application. Second, anybody who would be interested in writing one or two 300 line matches per week, please email me at [detail withheld] Thank you for your time, [name withheld] SCRA - http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html SCRA PLAYER'S GUIDE How to get in When you sign up, we are looking for a lot in the little space you have. One, you need a valid email address. Two, we are looking for realism. We don't want no 8 feet guys or 799 lbs guys, just like the real thing, we want realistic people. We also want originality. Earlier on, we accepted almost everyone because we needed people. Now we want to see original characters who can fit into many plots. We also want a lot of effort into your wrestlers appearance and background. We want to know where he has come from and what he is like so that again, we can place him into plots. Don't be too controversial as that won't get you in. How does it work Matches are scheduled every Tuesday for the Scraps. If you didn't flash on Sunday, you don't get a match for the next week. We book the matches to fit in with our angles. Obviously, if you stay active, you will get a better push. Title shots are given out by top rankings. You will get to the top of the rankings by winning the matches, discussed below. What you have to do We expect ONE flash per week, for Sunday. The flash on Sunday is what decides whether you wrestle during the week or not. If you submit a flash on Sunday, you get a match during the week. This way, we get lots of flashes and everybody stays involved or they don't wrestle (except for maybe a job). Failure to submit a flash for the first time results in no match the next week. Failure to do so the second time results in a two week suspension. Failure to do so a third time results in ban from the league. Every so often, we will give you a list of questions to answer as an interview for your wrestler. You MUST complete these questions by the time we specify. Failure to do so will result in us having to complete the interview which may go against what you want. The interviews must be good, solid answers, not one word. Feel free to submit interviews whenever you want and we will include them. Remember who our interviewer's are: Bull Dog Brad Carson and Jack Robinson. More on them in the staff section. If you are unhappy about the direction your wrestler is going, email the president. We can discuss angles for your wrestler to follow. You are the SCRAPER (pronounced skra-pr, stands for Simply Canadian Rasslin Alliance Personel for an E-fed Rassler). You are responsible for roleplaying your wrestler. Make sure you keep on the ball and include worthwhile flashes which help develop your character. I think we all know what is acceptable and unacceptable for flashes. If you don't, just think about this: If you are thinking about putting something in, would you be offended if it was about your character? More about the publications Something from SCRA comes out almost every day, here they are: The Monday Night Scraps - Airs Mondays, written by our talented staff of dedicated writers. It is basically just 8 matches between the top wrestlers. Includes interviews and the Bull Dog's Bark interview segment and incorporates flashes. There will usually be a title match on it, usually the NA title, or tag titles. Hosted by Jon Grey (face) and Jack Hazard (heel). Tuesday - Probing SCRA, a show with no matches. It includes a highlight and lowlight segment from the previous night and a look ahead at next Friday's and Monday's show. Insights from the hosts, Tyranny Dios (former wrestler, heel) and Warren Whirry (face) are also frequent. Thursday - Harvey's Presents Thursday Fanite, a fan appreciation show. Includes one match as selected by the fans and a question and answer segment. Hosted by two Harvey's employees, John and Eric. Friday - Double W Report, a full blow by blow house show report given by none other than Double W, Warren Whirry. Saturday - Spinebuster Scoops, official SCRA magazine. Complete with rumours, news, articles, lists and more. Sunday - ScraPreview, our flash show. It is hosted by Jon Grey and Jack Hazard who offer brief insights into some matches. Comments from every wrestler are shown here. (This is where the flashes are!) The gold! We currently have 4 belts: SCRA World Title: For the number one wrestler in the league, all apply SCRA North American Title: Defended more often, usually twice a month plus the pay per view. Scraps TV Title: For wrestlers under 280 lbs. Defended every week on The Scraps, but never on pay per views. SCRA Tag Team Titles: For tag teams. Defended about twice a month, plus pay per views.
