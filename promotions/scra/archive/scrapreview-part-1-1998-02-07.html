A ring stands in the middle of a snow filled park. Snow banks take up the sides of the ring. Over the top of one snow bank, a broadcaster's desk sits. Jon Grey begins speaking.] JG - "Hello fans and welcome to a special Saturday edition of ScraPreview as we look ahead at tomorrow night's Winter Carnival." JH - "Great name." JG - "They can't all be Rasslefest gems." JH - "Even that one's not original." JG - "Either way, the name doesn't matter. What matters is that we'll see a bunch of hungry wrestlers fighting for a title shot. But, before we get to that, we have received several phone calls at the SCRA Headquarters, inquiring on the condition of Tommy Gregory after last week's incident involving Gangsta Impasse and "Cowboy" Ken Curtis' barn." JH - "Actually, we received more phone calls from people who were worried about the animals in the barn." JG - "Yes, there were quite a few animal-lovers out there who were concerned about the welfare of the horses. That's not saying that Gregory was not on their minds." JH - "Oh, come off it, Grey! The fans were more worried about the horses than the horse's ass!" JG - "Will you shutup? Anyway, the camera crew at the barn kept the the cameras rolling, and at this time, we'd like to show you the footage of what occurred after we went off the air last week. Can we cut to the footage now?" [Cut to footage from last week...] [Street Smarts drives Gangsta Impasse's red benz through the barnyard door and hits Gregory.] GI - "Streetz, you ma man. I knew I could count an ya'll te make sure Greggie couldn't stop us." [Gangsta Impasse opens up the trunk of his car and pulls out a stereo. He turns it up to full blast as rap music blares from the speakers. Gangsta throws the rakes on Tommy Gregory.] GI - "Hey Curtis, say goo'bye te yer goats an' yer chickens. Say goo'bye te yer horses an' yer cows, cause dis i' de last time yer eva seein' em." [Gangsta, Drake, Street Smarts and Shooter McNutty begin trashing Curtis' barn, ripping down the wood and throwing the horse shit and hay all over the place. The basically finish trashing it as they all get in Gangsta's car.] GI - "An now, de nail in de coffin." [Gangsta strikes a match as it sparks on a piece of wood. The fire eventually spreads as the four take off in Gangsta's benz. As Gregory lays on the floor of the barn, the cameras continue to roll, but in a herky-jerky manner as the camera crew tries to escape the burning barn. We can hear the camera crew talking as they flee...] CM1 - "Are we just gonna leave him there?!" CM2 - "You wanna burn up tryin' to pull him out?!" [Suddenly, the crew breaks through to the outside, as smoke billows out of the barn! The crew members cough as they try to regain their composure, when suddenly, a man in jeans and sneakers, with no shirt, appears out of nowhere!] MAN - "What in God's name is going on here! The barn's on fire!" CM1 - "We know that! Gangsta set it on fire!" MAN - "Are the horses still in there?!" CM2 - "Yeah, and so is our head of security!" MAN - "Jesus Christ! What are you guys doin' standin' here?!" CM1 - "Hey man, I don't get paid for this <BEEP>!" MAN - "Christ!" [The man runs into the barn, battling the flames and smoke!] CM1 - "Hey!" CM2 - "Who is that guy?!" CM1 - "Hell if I know! Whoever he is, he's a dead man, now!" [Suddenly, through the smoke, several horses emerge from the barn! They buck and kick for a few moments, shaking off the effects of the smoke and heat! They scatter as they flee the burning barn! There's a pause, then the man appears, carrying Tommy Gregory over his shoulder! He makes it to the camera crew and dumps Gregory down on the ground.] MAN - "He's ok, he was lying on the ground, so he was underneath most of the smoke! Put that camera down and let's get him up to the house!" [Suddenly, the scene goes to snow and then black. Cut back to Grey and Hazard...] JG - "Wow. What amazing footage there." JH - "That guy Crow's a certified hero." JG - "I would tend to agree. Of course, we now know that Curtis' barn did indeed burn to the ground before the fire department could get there to put out the fire. We'll get comments from Curtis himself tomorrow night as he watches the battle royals. He is the man with the most at stake, being the current champ." JH - "And a damn fine champ. Jacks was good and will always be a fond champ in my heart, but now we've got Curtis. This guy can carry a title." JG - "We'll see his true test in the upcoming weeks. The battle royal decides who gets what depending on where people finish. So, without further ado, let's start looking ahead at tomorrow night's action!" JH - "No graphics?" JG - "No real matches. Instead, we'll go through each wrestler and I'll give my comments on what they have to do to win, or at least do well." JH - "Yee-haw. I can't believe we have to do that every week. Can't you just play a tape of last week's comments?" JG - "No. They change every week. Let's start things out with our current TV champ, Prodigy." :: Prodigy [The camera opens on to a large tv screen. The screen shows bits of past interviews from Prodigy & Kat, from the ScraPreview. The first clip shows Prodigy and Kat's first ever SCRA interview.] BK - "....but what about the SCRA? How do you think we'll be there?" P - "Kat, we got nothing to worry about. First of all with my stellar wrestling skills and the experience and gold I have proven to be a champ. So the SCRA is just another place for us to show off our wrestling abilities. And if you add my wrestling skills to your great booking and managing skills there is no way we are going to lose." [As the camera stays stuck to the screen the tape begins to be forwarded and shows highlights of Prodigy jumping Ken Curtis from behind during the Pep Rally followed by Prodigy performing the Last Ride In on the likes of Pablo Morales, Cowboy Ken Curtis, Lifetaker, and Aladdin Style. The screen once again begins to be forwarded, this time stopping at Prodigy's interview prior to Rasslefest.] P - "Back to my mission for '98. I know I'm going to have many obstacles to overcome but along with Kat and this new found strength of mine, my inner strength. I'm going to...we're going to go through all those obstacles like an F1 car going through an equestrian circuit.... (chanting) Kat, Prodigy, '98." [Once again the screen begins forwarding and stops at the Free-For-All from Rasslefest and shows Prodigy nailing the LRI on Green Knight and pinning him 1, 2, 3. The video feed now forwards to this past Sunday's ScraPreview.] BK - "So, you think your ready to bring home some new gold home this Monday night on the SCRAPS?" P - "There is not a single doubt in my mind that I will perform anything less than a steller victory." BK - "Yeah, I agree with me in your corner doing the thinking and you in the ring doing the brawling, Rock Bottom Robbie would have to be better than Superman." [Another point in the interview..] BK - "....Your gonna be walking through a park where your fears will become reality and you will be mugged. Not of your wallet, but of your integrity, pride, and most of all the SCRA TV Title." [Another point in the interview..] P - "....This Monday I'm going to have another gun pointed to my head, my first chance at gold in the SCRA and believe me when I say that my performance is going to be nothing short of spectaular. As your name says, after Monday your gonna hit "Rock Bottom" but like the Jeffersons me and Kat are gonna be movin on up." [The screen is now forwarded to this past Monday's Scraps and shows Prodigy hitting the LRI on a bloody Rock Bottom Robbie and pinning him. This is followed by Prodigy standing up and holding the gold in the air. The camera zooms in on the TV Title and stays put on it for a couple of seconds, as the screen pauses, before widening back out but the tv is gone and the title is on Prodigy's shoulder now. It seems to be glittering shinier than ever against the black turtle neck, with the sleeves rolled up and blue jeans that Prodigy is sporting today. Kat is sitting beside her man with the remote to the tv in her hand, in a pair of Adidas sweats. Prodigy sees the camera and stares into it for a moment than glances at the title on his shoulder.] BK - "SCRA, welcome to our house. What we just saw was a quick highlight reel of mine and Prodigy's accomplishments in the SCRA. I guess you could say we did exactly what we said we would do from day one, bring home some gold...." P - "....and bring it home we did. RBR, I respect you and give you credit on a great match and handing over the title like a gentleman. I know Rocky was on his way to restoring the honor, glory and tradition of the TV Title before encountering me and now insiders don't know whether I'm gonna continue that tradition or once again make the TV Title into a piece of tin on leather." [Prodigy leans forwards coming closer to the camera, holding the title on his shoulder.] P - "To all you insiders, I got one thing to say on how I'm gonna represent this title. With more guts, hard work and determination than any of it's previous holders. I'm gonna hold this title longer than anybody else in the history in the SCRA. This may not be the top dawg of titles but none the less it's gold and there's not that many people who can say they were gold in the SCRA. So, if anyone has anymore complaints about the title or just plain has a problem with me or Kat." [Prodigy points to his feet.] P - "This is the front of the complaint line, so come file a complaint. Just one thing when you come to file that complaint, make sure you have health insurance." [Prodigy gives a little smirk to the camera and leans back on the couch again.] BK - "We weren't the only new champs crowned this Monday on the Scraps though." P - "Yeah I noticed our old friend the Cowboy also captured the World Title. Kenny, if your listening, congratulations man but I have one question for you. Is it just me or are our careers from the twilight zone? We entered the SCRA on the same night, we both had our first real feud with each other, and we both wound up winning gold on the same night. Whatever, just found that ironic and thought I'd check with up." [The phone rings and Kat gets up to get it in the next room.] BK - "Be right back." P - "Sure babe and when you return can you get me some water." BK - "Yeah." P - "Back to business now. This weekend is the Battle Royal which is supposed to determine who you really are in the SCRA. I guess that means that when the smoke settles I'm gonna be the only one left standing. This will mark my second battle royal on SCRA turf. In the first one I injured the now World Champion, Kenny, and was one of the final four. This time is gonna be different though. I'm still gonna make it to the final four as well as the final three, and two and one. Than I hear that the winner of this rumble gets a shot at the World Title. Well, Prez Grey you may as well sign my name to that title shot from now cause after this weekend is over and i get my shot at the World Title, I'm gonna win it. It dosen't make a difference to me if the Cowboy's still champ or not, I'll take on whoever has the title at the time and theres gonna be no holding me down. Than I will have the World Title and the TV Title around my waist. And that's a promise." [Kat walks back in to the room with the glass of water for Prodigy and sits back down next to her man. She picks up a magazine from the coffee table and begins to read it as Prodigy drinks the water and the camera fades out.] JG - "I'd have to think that Prodigy is one of the favourites here. With his new found confidence from the TV title and Ken Curtis still lurking in his mind, a little, he may have the drive to win this thing. Plus, he's got the size and brawling skills necessary for an event like this." JH - "Hey, only half as long." JG - "But, we're doing a whole lot more." JH - "More sleep for me." JG - "Next up, we've got flashes from Gangsta Impasse." :: Gangsta Impasse [The scene opens to a desert scene. A saloon is shown as the camera comes in. Keith Spencer comes in and starts talking to the bartender.] KS - "Excuse me, barkeep. I got this address from a mystery man telling me to look for him here. He said to ask for The Cowboy Playa With The Biggest D**k Around, but I have no idea what that means." BARTENDER - "Ya wan' a Lonestar?" KS - "Is that who this mystery man is?" BARTENDER - "No, ye idiot. It's a type uv beer. Now ye wan' dat er a Scatch!" KS - "Nothing thank you. But...." BARTENDER - "HEY MA! This guy don' want anything te drink." MA - "Sew up his ass crack!" BARTENDER - "Any last words?" KS - "[gulp] Yes, I still don't know if you can help me, but I heard that this guy can fight. He's gotten rave reviews from wrestling critics from around the world and we sure could use some new talent in the SCRA." BARTENDER - "No new talent. You get ass sewn shut." [All of a sudden, a door from the back bursts open and a voice is heard saying "Hold On". The two men are now on the ground and as the camera looks at them, they look over. The music when people are about to draw starts playing as the camera shifts to the boots of the man. It starts at the black leather boots and moves up to his blue jeans and cowboy shirt. His black cowboy hat is drawn over his eyes as he starts speaking.] UNKNOWN - "I reckon ye let im go." BARTENDER - "Sss sssorry sir." [The man looks up as you can see that the man isn't a cowboy. It's Gangsta Impasse as he tears away his shirt and tight jeans, revealing a black shirt and bare legs.] GI - "I'd betta cova up. Spenca, come wit' me." KS - "What are you doing here, Mr. Impasse?" GI - "Keith...It's all fer affect. I really just wanted te show Curtis that I'm jus' as goo' a cowboy as him." [They get into a room, where there are posters of rap artists plastered on the wall.] GI - "Lemme get some pants on an' we can start de interview." KS - "Excellent. Do you want me to leave while you change?" GI - "Hell no. Don'tchoo wanna find out why they call me De Playa With The Biggest Dick Around?" [Gangsta pulls up his pants and buttons up the button, forgeting to do up his zipper. He sits down as Spencer just kind of stares at him.] GI - "Is it just me, or is it a lil' drafty in he-ah?" KS - "[signaling for him to do up his zipper] You're ah, flying low." [Gangsta zips up as Spencer starts to talk.] KS - "What are your feelings on this battle royal coming up?" GI - "Keith, I really jus wanna go in dey're do ma best an' win. I don't care who gets in ma way, whetha it's Chase Hunter or "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin. All I wan' i' anotha shot at Curtis. Te secure ma shot at IIeW." KS - "So, there's no love lost between you and Ken Curtis?" GI - "Ah hell no. From de moment he enta-d de league, I knew I could beat him. He may've gotten de title, but he ain't de real champeen of dis league. I mean, he hasn't been workin' as hard as long as me, or Eddy Jack, or Sgt. Pepper. All I's gots te say is dat Curtis should not be de champion and dats final." KS - "Does it matter whether you finish first or fifth? Do *you* really care when you get your shot at the title?" GI - "De title's got nuttin' te do wit dis. I want Curtis an' it don't matter how long it takes him te get in de ring wit' me, it's gon' be all ma anger an' frustration comin' at him at 100% effort. If Curtis wants te keep dat title until I beat him, all de better." KS - "Well, thank you Mr. Impasse, and can I use your back door to leave?" GI - "Shu-ah foo'." [The screen fades as the camera cuts back to the announcers.] JG - "This man wants to win this battle royal more than anybody. He wants Ken Curtis. Again, his size and brawling skills make him an early favourite going in. Not to be stereotypical, but Gangsta has been involved in gang wars before and knows how to take out a bunch." JH - "Not to be stereotypical, but I'll do it anyways. Way to go, Grey." JG - "I had to say it. If I didn't, what else am I going to say?" JH - "Nothing. That's what the fans want." :: Eddy "Flap" Jacks JG - "The former champ, one week removed from losing the title, seems to be a bit shaken up. We don't have any comments from him this week, but his stablemate tag team, the Praetorian Guard, are coming up later in the show." JH - "Jacks needs the gold around his waist. You'd think with all that blubber, you could keep some pants up, but you can't." JG - "He doesn't seem to have the same confidence as he did with the belt. Either way, though, Jacks is probably _the_ favourite to win this thing. He's the biggest wrestler in SCRA and one of the best brawlers. I'm sure he wants to get a shot at "his" title, too." JH - "The only drawback is everybody's gonna be gunnin' for Jacks. Being the big man and all, a whole group's gonna have to take him out." JG - "That's true. And I'm sure one person in that group will be J.W. Hardin, who comes closest to matching up size-wise with Jacks." -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
