[SCRA] The Scraps [2/4] (26 January 1998)

26 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 3 ) | | \ | | Rock Bottom Robbie vs. Sgt. Pepper / |___ | | | | Writer: AG (_________________________________________________________________________ [Jaspard enters the ring with glasses, finally able to read the cue cards. Unfortunately, the glasses have one lens knocked out and Jaspard walks around in circles for the intros.] EJ - "This matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit. Coming down the aisle first, from Liverpool, England and weighing in at 242 pounds, he is Sgt. Pepper!" [Some Beatles music begins playing over the PA system as Sgt. Pepper enters the arena accompanied by Lonely Hearts Club Band, Mal Epstein and Penelope Lane. He hits every outstretched hand he can find and when he gets to the ring, poses for the camera as fireworks go off behind him.] EJ - "His opponent, the current Scraps TV champion, he weighs in at 245 lbs. and hails from Intercourse, Pennsylvania, here comes Rock Bottom Robbie!" [Little Things blares throughout the stadium, but is soon toned down as Robbie steps into the arena. He raises the title high, eliciting a loud cheer from the fans. Robbie continues down the aisle, slapping as many outstretched hands as possible. He hops into the ring as fireworks blaze behind him and tears his shirt.] DING! DING! DING! [The two lock up with Pepper quickly sending Robbie into the ropes. Pepper bends over for a back body drop, but Robbie delivers a chop to the throat. Robbie gets to his knees and continues to throw punches at the face of Pepper. Once completely on his feet, Robbie bounces off the ropes and clotheslines Pepper. Pepper gets back to his feet only to be knocked over again by a running Robbie clothesline.] JG - "Robbie's on fire tonight." JH - "I'll set him on fire." JG - "What was that?" JH - "I have a great desire...for Robbie." [Robbie gets onto the mat and applies an armbar. Pepper to his feet, but Robbie whips Pepper into the ropes and as Robbie attempts a clothesline, Pepper grabs onto Robbie's arm and hits a crucifix.] JG - "The cover! 1.....2.....kick out by Robbie." JH - "You know Grey, I think I'm starting to like Sgt. Pepper." JG - "Really?" JH - "No, but I just wanted to say, 'I like some little peppers on my steak.'" [Robbie gets up only to be met by a spinning heel kick. Pepper gets back to his feet quickly and waits for Robbie to get up. Robbie stands up as Pepper flies at him with a cross body block. Pepper's momentum sends both men over the top ropes, Pepper landing on top of Robbie. Robbie, stays on the mat as Pepper gets back in the ring. He sees Robbie get up and begins running towards the ropes, hitting a baseball slide.] JG - "After this match, Robbie will have no teeth. Pepper's knocking the snot right out of him." JH - "Do you like men with no teeth?" JG - "I like all men." JH - "Two things. One, you're a homo. Two, I could arrange for you to have no teeth if you'd like." [Pepper slowly gets up and drags Robbie into the ring with him. He tries for the pin... 1! KICKOUT! Robbie gets on his feet after the attempted pinfall, but is grabbed by Pepper who hits an atomic drop. Robbie stays on his feet, but is sent flying through the ropes by a dropkick to the back of the head.] JH - "Robbie's been on the outside of this match more than Grey with my inside jokes." JG - "Huh?" [Robbie gets to his feet fast, a little dazed after the dropkick. He gets back into the ring and is taken down by a snap mare. This is quickly replaced by a chinlock. Robbie screams in pain as Pepper twists the hold tighter and tighter. Eventually, the brawler elbows Pepper in the stomach, loosening the hold. This allows Robbie get in another elbow, and another. Robbie gets to his feet and pushes Pepper off completely, sending him into the ropes. Robbie bends over and Pepper runs directly into a back body drop.] JH - "We should have a height competition on those things. It seems like wresetlers get higher and higher these days." JG - "It's the technology, Jack." JH - "Yes, that muscle is getting more and more technologically advanced and effective. And, don't get me started on how fat has improved." [Robbie walks over to Pepper and begins nailing Garvin stomps. He continues this attack, pushing Pepper into the turnbuckle. Pepper slouches in the corner, allowing Robbie to stand him up. Robbie than hits a knife edge chop, the slap echoing throughout the arena.] JH - "Whoo!" JG - "Jack, don't interrupt the match. Plus, I think that phrase is copyrighted." JH - "Ah, as if we don't infringe on those in these little shows." [Robbie starts throwing his usual hard punches, knocking Pepper to the mat. Robbie then moves to the opposite turnbuckle and takes a run at Pepper, diving in. Pepper moves out of the way, sliding underneath the ring. Robbie hits the ringpost hard. Pepper grabs a hold of Robbie's legs and applies a ringpost Crabalocker.] JG - "He's got the Crabalocker! And, around the ringpost!" JH - "Count him out! He's outside the ring!" [Pepper releases the hold and climbs back into the ring after the threat of countout. He picks up Robbie and delivers some hard slaps of his own. Pepper sends Robbie into the turnbuckle. Robbie bounces off hard and is met in the center of the ring by a Pepper dropkick. Robbie staggers back into the turnbuckle and Pepper quickly gets up and runs in with a lariat. Robbie stays standing in the corner, but is taken down when Pepper executes an armdrag takedown into an armbar. Pepper squeezes the hold tighter, causing Robbie to scream in agony.] JG - "Sgt. Pepper is dominating this match. He really wants that title back." JH - "(laughing) That coveted TV title. There's a thriller each week." [Robbie begins to build momentum, elbowing Pepper in the midsection and running into the ropes. Robbie comes at Pepper with an axehandle, but Pepper calmly drops Robbie with a drop toe hold. He applies a hammerlock.] JH - "What's with Pepper and this damn technical stuff. As if these two don't put on a boring match already." JG - "They are two great competitors. Every time two great competitors step in the ring, sparks fly." JH - "Or when your car breaks down." [Pepper releases the hold and then hits a standing dropkick, sending Robbie staggering back into the ropes. Robbie bounces off and is met by a kick to the midsection by Pepper. Pepper runs into the side ropes and meets Robbie in the middle with a knee lift. Pepper tries the cover... 1! 2! KICKOUT! Robbie gets up slowly, but Pepper takes him down with a hiptoss. Pepper jumps on Robbie's back and applies a camel clutch. Robbie tries to grab the ropes, but is too far. He tries to wiggle out, but can't. Eventually, with an amazing show of strength, Robbie gets onto his feet and hits a modified back suplex. He screams to the crowd. Robbie picks up Pepper and whips him into the ropes, hitting a huge lariat.] JG - "Robbie's firing back! Look at the tiger go!" JH - "Robbie's roar is about as big as a kittens. And not just any kitten, your kitten." JG - "I don't have a kitten." JH - "That's how quiet it is." [Pepper slowly gets up only to be met by a series of kicks and punches by Robbie. He hits a hard kick to the midsection, which bends over Pepper. Robbie quickly grabs Pepper's hands and flips him over with a monkey flip. Robbie runs off the ropes and hits an elbowdrop, coming down right over the neck. He goes for the pin... 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Robbie's fighting back. He almost had Pepper with that one." JH - "Almost shmallmost. Let's get onto the next matchup." JG - "Once this one finishes, we can." JH - "I'll take care of that." JG - "Get back here!" [Pepper gets up only to be taken down, again, by a snap mare. Robbie puts on a chinlock. This doesn't last long as Robbie releases the hold and lifts up Pepper, bodyslamming him in the center of the ring. Robbie goes off the ropes and goes for an elbowdrop, but Pepper moves out of the way. Pepper slides over and applies an abdominal stretch on the mat. Robbie quickly grabs a hold of the bottom ropes. Pepper lifts up his opponent and delivers a suplex. He then goes off the ropes and comes down hard on the neck of Robbie with a legdrop. Pepper goes for the cover... 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Pepper's had many near pinfalls in this match." JH - "A few too many if you ask me..." [Pepper picks up his opponent and delivers swift kicks to the midsection. He then grabs Robbie by the waist and delivers a backbreaker. Robbie falls to the mat. Pepper picks the champ back up and whips him into the ropes, bending over for a back body drop. Robbie sees this early and connects with a European uppercut, sending Pepper staggering back. Robbie grabs him and hits a spinebuster. Robbie dives onto the mat, throwing punches wildly at Pepper's face. He picks up Pepper and sends him into the turnbuckle. Robbie follows in, but Pepper lifts himself up and tries to get over Robbie, but Robbie catches him on his shoulders. Robbie runs into the middle of the ring and nails the Rock Bottom Slam. He covers... 1! 2! 3!] JG - "Another one with the Slam!" JH - "That's a real powerful move, eh Grey. Robbie's laid out so many high quality wrestlers with it." JG - "I know you were being sarcastic and I, nor the viewers, appreciate it." JH - "Sorry!" JG - "Stop it." JH - "No." JG - "Ah. That was sarcasm, right? So, that means that you're not going to be sarcastic, right?" JH - "You look into things way too much." JG - "Right now, we've got Keith Spencer standing by with Gangsta Impasse for an interview!" KS - "Ladies and gentlemen, Gangsta Impasse!" [California Love comes over the speakers as Gangsta walks into the arena, not of his usual theatrics. He simply walks to the ring, all businesss, for the interview time.] KS - "Gangsta Impasse, how do you feel coming into this match against a man who has done what no other wrestler in the SCRA has done, beat J.W. Hardin?" GI - "Hold up Spencer...all de playaz out dey're who think I can beat dat piece o' Southe'n hapat-bastard, make some noise!!" [The whole stadium erupts as Keith Spencer tries to interrupt.] KS - "So..." GI - "Hold up, buoy, I gots to answer yer question. Te me...J.W. Hardin is not the best man in de sport. If he waz, he'd be de numba one contena wouldn't he? So, in de words of a guy who I can't say cause I be sued, te be de man, ye gotta beat de man!" [The crowd pops wildly.] GI - "But, Curtis...i' you are so good, how come you haven't gotten a title shot yet? How come the only impressive vict'ry you've gott'n waz gainst Hardin? I been through all deze wretlers back stage te get te he-ah an' I ain't gon give dat up fer nothin'." [Impasse looks around which draws a huge pop.] KS - "So, now that I've heard your thoughts on Curtis and Hardin, how about some comments on that crap-filled barn." GI - "I don' have te worry bout dat, cause I gon' beat Ken Curtis." KS - "Posive attitude is always critical when you get to the top of the ranks." [As Impasse is about to speak the same hot dog vendor comes out and hits Impasse over the head with the hot-dog roller. Impasse is out cold and isn't moving. As the vendor is taken care of by Tommy Gregory, he looks back at the ring to see it's Ken Curtis again.] JH - "He did it again! What an animal!" JG - "I guess Gangsta likes hot dogs." JH - "Curtis will sure feed him a load tonight. It's gonna be great!" JG - "But, we've got the tag match right now! And, Hardin still doesn't have a partner. Any predictions?" JH - "There are only two men Hardin would team up with, but they ain't showing up." JG - "We'll have to see how this pans out. Let's go to the ring for the intros!" -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
