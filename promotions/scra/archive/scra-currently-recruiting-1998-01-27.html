SCRA: Currently recruiting

27 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Thought I'd take the time to post a little message about my little fed, Simply Canadian Rasslin' Alliance, or as it is known from here on in, SCRA. February marks the beginning of our 6 months. Almost half a year and we're still going strong. We've got some "name" stars such as "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin, Derek Mota, "Perfectionist" Nik Nyce, "Cowboy" Ken Curtis and Siberian Express as well as SCRA produced talent such as Eddy "Flap" Jacks, "Golden King" Aladdin Style and "Silverblade" Anthony Logan. The roster is small, but the competition is fierce. The reward for toppling the mountain of competitors is noticed with our 3 singles titles and lone tag title. We've got our big show every Monday night, the Scraps. Normally, there are 8 full matches on the card along with interviews, angles and storylines to keep heat going. These days, we've had about 5-6 matches per card due to the small roster. The night before, our two commentators, Jon Grey and Jack Hazard, give their in depth views on the matches along with flashes from each wrestler. With Sunday/Monday being the heavy SCRA section of the week, Wednesday provides a good calming show. Sort of. SCRA Uncut is an unedited, unscripted show where host Keith Spencer says what should've been done on the Scraps. People give their views on what's right/wrong with the league as well as wrestlers giving their opinions. Friday is our second match show with summary style matches and commentary in between. The hosts seem to change often, but they always provide a laugh. We also put out the Spinebuster Scoops, our biweekly e-zine. Each issue comes packed with news, rumours, opinions and rankings. The writers are all extremely knowledgable on SCRA and provide insightful and important features. Interested still? We've got about 10 singles slots available and 3 tag slots available. Check out the website for all the information you could possibly need on SCRA: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html And, sign up today and get in the battle royal on February 8 where you can earn an immediate title shot! Hope to see you soon! -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
