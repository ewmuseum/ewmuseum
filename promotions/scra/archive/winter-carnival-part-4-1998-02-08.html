[SCRA] Winter Carnival [4/4] (8 February 1998)

8 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[After the elimination, Hardin turns to McAllister and nails him with a Cattle Buster. He grabs Gangsta and tosses him into the turnbuckle, immediately starting an attack of punches to the head and midsection. Nyce, recovered from the figure four, moves towards the fallen McAllister and applies Standing Nycely.] JH - "That's two gigantic moves on McAllister! He's getting thrown around more than a dirty whore." JG - "Jack, you're losing your touch with those metaphors." JH - "No I'm not! I'm losing less than a...uh...ah screw it! I am losing my touch!" [Kertins moves out of the corner to help out McAllister, hitting Nyce with a dropkick to the head. Nyce breaks the hold and is quickly met by a flying headscissors by Kertins. But, Nyce is able to stop the Kertins attack and moves towards the ropes. He lifts Kertins over the top, attempting to shake him off. Kertins holds strong...until McAllister gets up and nails Nyce with a forearm to the back. Nyce bounces forward, sending Kertins over the top.] JH - "McAllister eliminated Kertins! And, Kertins helped him out!" JG - "It was inadvertant, I'm sure." JH - "Admit it! McAllister is a back stabber. He slashes people more than a toothless hockey player. How was that one?" JG - "A little weak." [With Kertins out of the question, Robbie is able to fight back against Prodigy. He pushes Prodigy out of the corner with a series of punches, bumping into Chase Hunter. This allows Edenshaw to fight back against Hunter with a rake to the eyes, then a kick upwards to the midsection. Edenshaw hits a back suplex on Prodigy to get him out of the way. This moves Robbie out of the corner and hits an elbowdrop. Robbie moves to Sgt. Pepper and points at Prodigy, the two moving into the current TV champ and attacking him together. Justice recovers from the figure four, then moves in towards McAllister and Nyce, then towards Victoria Glenn. Again, he flexes his muscles in an attempt to gain her attention. This angers McAllister who hits a furious right to Nyce, then a Thesz press on Justice.] JG - "If there's one thing you don't do to Bobby McAllister, that's bring Victoria Glenn into the picture." JH - "Yeah. Taking pictures is okay, you just have to change the heads." [McAllister tosses a series of punches to Justice, right beside Mota and Style who are still rolling around the ring with various chinlocks and just plain old punches. Watts motions to attack somebody, but stops and sits back down.] JH - "So lazy! I love this guy!" JG - "I'm just surprised nobody's going after him." JH - "Who would want to? His clothes are filthy! He probably hasn't showered in over a week. My kind of guy." [Hardin has Gangsta seated on the turnbuckle, but can't get the final push over the top. Gangsta holds onto Hardin's head tight, then converts this into a rolling DDT, sending Hardin face first in the middle of the ring. Next to Hardin, Edenshaw has Hunter in an Indian deathlock. Gangsta moves in and punches Edenshaw in the head, helping out fellow fan favourite. Sean Watts finally decides to go for somebody, moves towards the middle of the ring and pushes Gangsta. This angers Gangsta who turns towards Watts and readys for a chokeslam, but Hardin recovers from the back and hits a back suplex. Watts moves away from Gangsta, continuing his instigating with Robbie and Pepper who are still double teaming Prodigy.] JG - "The Iconoclast is going after everybody!" JH - "He doesn't care who he hits. Come to think about it, he doesn't really care about anything. Kind of like me, eh Grey?" JG - "Unfortunately, yes." [Watts pulls Robbie off the attack of Prodigy and pulls him towards the middle of the ring, suckering Robbie with a drop toe hold. This is right next to Derek Mota and Aladdin Style, forcing Robbie to get a series of punches from both men. Robbie fights back, taking the two smaller men into the corner with hard punches. Watts continues his trek around the ring towards McAllister, Nyce and Justice. He pulls Nyce away from the two and starts to throw hard punches.] JG - "Sean Watts is just trying to stir things up. He's pulling people away from their enemies and making new enemies for them." JH - "Smart cookie. Only bad thing is that he's gonna get a few people trying to kill him, too." [Watts leaps up and nails a hurricarana on Nyce, sending him into the Prodigy and Sgt. Pepper corner. Pepper grabs Nyce and continues with a series of punches to Nyce's head. Prodigy works his way up, but Watts moves in, not allowing Watts to get his balance. Hardin tosses Gangsta into the turnbuckle, following in with a lariat. Hardin then moves away from Gangsta to new blood, Chase Hunter and Gwaii Edenshaw. Hardin grabs Hunter by the head and lifts him in a claw position, then slamming him down to the mat. Gangsta moves out of the turnbuckle, a bit weary, towards Edenshaw. Edenshaw is ready and dropkicks Gangsta right back towards the corner. Opposite Edenshaw and Gangsta, Mota has Style laying on his back and Robbie on the top turnbuckle. Style gets back up from behind Mota and taps Mota on the shoulder, signalling for an alliance to dump Robbie. The two do and push Robbie over the top.] JG - "Robbie's gone! And, are Mota and Style back together?" JH - "Those crazy little guys. They do some wacky stuff, eh?" [Mota turns to Style and attempts a lariat, but Style ducks underneath and gets behind in a waistlock. Style lifts Mota across the ring with a release German suplex. This sends Mota sliding into new blood, Mike Justice. McAllister releases Justice, allowing Mota to attack Justice. McAllister moves towards Nyce and pushes Sgt. Pepper away. McAllister starts unleashing a series of punches on Nyce's head. Pepper moves away to find a new attack, this time going towards Prodigy and Sean Watts. Pepper joins in with Watts in the attack on Prodigy, pushing him in the corner, both delivering alternating punches. Watts, however, turns on Pepper and delivers a sharp knee to the midsection.] JH - "I was wondering how long it would take him to do that. I don't think Nirvana and Beethoven mix to well." JG - "They do if they're six feet underground." JH - "That was a very uncharacteristic and very rude comment, Grey. You should be ashamed of yourself." [Watts pulls Pepper in close with a series of knee jabs to the head. He moves Prodigy away from the turnbuckle, placing Pepper on top. Together with Prodigy, the two push Pepper over the top!] JG - "Watts and Prodigy have eliminated Sgt. Pepper! Pepper's gone." JH - "So, just grind some more. We should really get worried if the salt disappears. Then, we have to buy some more...oh, you were talking about Sgt. Pepper, weren't you?" [Watts quickly turns on Prodigy, hitting a kick to the midsection followed by a rocker dropper. Watts leaves Prodigy on the mat and moves towards Hardin and Hunter, joining with Hardin in delivering a series of punches to the head of Hunter. Hardin, however, doesn't like nor wants the help and turns to Watts, quickly releasing a series of punches to the head. Hunter crawls out and crouches in the corner, hiding from the rest of the wrestlers and catching his breath. Gangsta reversed the onslaught by Edenshaw and starts attacking Edenshaw with a series of punches himself.] JH - "Know what I just realized? There hasn't been one technical move this whole match." JG - "Yes there..." JH - "Don't ruin it. I don't pay attention when there is. If I see one, I just tune into another section of fights. That's what I love about these little matches, there's always a brawl going on." [Justice tosses Mota into the turnbuckle, then drives his elbow into the head of Mota. Mota drops to the mat as Justice continues the attack with kicks to the midsection. Justice moves away from Mota, looking at Victoria Glenn, again, and flexes his muscles. McAllister gets up from Nyce, looking towards Justice. He runs at Justice with a flying cross body block, but Justice ducks, pulls the rope down, sending McAllister flying over the top.] JH - "Did you see him fly? He went right over the top!" JG - "McAllister did fly..." JH - "And, he landed face first in the snow! He's gonna get up and have one giant milk moustache." [Justice, pleased with his actions, laughs at McAllister and points at Glenn, then his muscles. From behind, Nik Nyce dropkicks Justice over the top!] JG - "Justice is gone! His constant show-boating just cost him big time!" JH - "It was worth it, though. I think Glenn really likes Hot Stuff." JG - "I highly doubt that. Victoria Glenn is a one man woman." JH - "I doubt that. She's probably with as many people as are in the ring right now. That's, what, about eleven, twelve?" JG - "Uh...I think nine." [Mota moves out of the corner, looking for open spots. He sees Edenshaw and Gangsta brawling, Hardin and Watts brawling, Style and Prodigy brawling, but Hunter and Nik Nyce staying away from the attacks. Mota moves into Hunter and the two lock up, Mota slipping behind Hunter and placing him in a hammerlock. Hunter bends to get out of the hold, but Mota is able to grab Hunter's hand and execute a pumphandle slam. Nyce sees Hunter on the mat and moves in to help the attack. Mota sees Nyce coming and moves away towards Style and Prodigy. He dropkicks Prodigy from the back, then lifts Style up and hits a roundhouse kick to the midsection. Style catches the foot, but Mota hits a sweep kick down low to take down Style. Prodigy sees Mota about to hop on Style and grabs Mota in a chinlock.] JG - "I thought people would stay away if they could to get some rest, but that's not the case." JH - "Who would enter a battle royal to stand in the corner? I'm no genius, but isn't the point of this thing to fight? Now, some of our big boys can't fight that great (coughing) Grizzly! (normal), but I'm sure that we can find a different corner for them to sit in outside of the ring." [Nyce bends to pick up Hunter, but Hunter rolls between Nyce's legs and hits a dropkick to the back. Hunter moves away from Nyce towards Gangsta and Edenshaw. As Edenshaw sees him coming, he pokes Gangsta in the eyes and drops Hunter with a football tackle. Nyce recovers from the dropkick and moves towards the open Gangsta. He kicks Gangsta in the midsection, then moves on. Nyce sees Hardin on top of Watts, throwing hard rights and lefts, so Nyce hits a forearm to the back of Hardin. Hardin gets off Watts and turns towards Nyce, but is met by a hard knife edged chop to the chest. Watts gets up, too, and helps Nyce double team Hardin with a series of chops to the chest and arms. Mota begins elbowing Prodigy in the gut, releasing the chinlock. Style lunges at Mota with a lariat, but Mota ducks and Style nails Prodigy. Mota hits a mule kick from the mat to the gut of Style, then moves towards Hardin's double teamers. He pulls Watts off and tosses him in the corner. This allows Hardin to overpower Nyce's chops with his own brand of punches. This drops Nyce and Hardin moves towards Watts and Mota. Hardin sits Watts onto the top turnbuckle, then moves aside as Mota runs in with a high dropkick. Watts leans back, but doesn't fall to the floor. Hardin moves back in, grabs Watts in a gorilla press, but tosses him to the outside instead of the middle of the ring.] JG - "Sean Watts is gone! Derek Mota and J.W. Hardin have eliminated him!" JH - "You need to find somebody to help you in these things. It looks like Hardin and Mota have done that." JG - "What about Grizzly Adams? He supposedly had many friends backstage with the wrestlers." JH - "He just can't fight. A fly with a mutated eye, one wing and a pea-sized brain could escape Grizzly's wrath." JG - "If a fly had a pea-sized brain, I don't think he could fly very much." JH - "And Adams still couldn't get him!" [Mota and Hardin part ways, not looking at each other. Mota moves to Style and Prodigy, again pushing Prodigy out of the way and attacking Style. Hardin moves towards Nik Nyce who is recovering from Hardin's previous attack. Nyce, however, is ready with a face rake for Hardin. Nyce hits a European uppercut to the staggering Hardin, sending Hardin even further back. Hardin bumps into the resting Gangsta Impasse who gets up and nails Hardin with a series of elbows to the back. Nyce comes in from the front side and attempts a clothesline, but Hardin ducks, sending Nyce's clothesline to the head of Gangsta. Hardin crawls out of the way, moving towards the rolling brawl of Hunter and Edenshaw. He grabs both men by the head and tries to make them headbutt each other, but Hunter ducks out so Hardin throws Edenshaw down and Hunter jumps onto Edenshaw with a camel clutch. Hardin moves around the ring and finds Prodigy almost alone. He grabs the TV champ and tosses him into the turnbuckle, following in with a shoulderblock. Hardin holds Prodigy in place in the turnbuckle, then drives his shoulder into the midsection of Prodigy.] JG - "We've got everybody fighting. But, there's no more double teaming." JH - "It's all one on one, the way it should be. I was never fond of those group things." JG - "That's disgusting, Jack." JH - "Disgusting? I wasn't talking about that! I was talking about double teams and triple teams." JG - "You always praise those, though. You were talking about, uh, that." JH - "Hey, you know my motto: contradictions are not sins." [Hunter releases the camel clutch on Edenshaw, then lifts up the Haida Warrior. But, Edenshaw hits a fist to the midsection, pushing Hunter back. Edenshaw continues with the attack, pushing Hunter into the ropes. Edenshaw moves back, then runs at Hunter with a flying cross body. Hunter ducks, pulls the rope down and sends Edenshaw over the top!] JG - "Edenshaw's gone! The inexperience cost him!" JH - "He must have experience flying. That one was as graceful as a figure skater's little loops and twists. If only Edenshaw wore those little lacy tutus and skates, that would be really scary." JG - "Because it would've looked like a figure skater or because any guy wearing women's clothes is scary?" JH - "Both." [Hunter views the rest of the ring, seeing Hardin butting Prodigy, Gangsta on the receiving end of Standing Nycely and Mota with a chinlock on Style. Hunter moves towards Mota and kicks him in the midsection, freeing Style from the hold. Style turns behind Mota and places him in his own chinlock. Hunter quickly moves away towards Nyce. Nyce drops Gangsta's legs and looks at Hunter. The two lock up in the middle of the ring, Nyce hitting a knee to the midsection. Hunter bends over as Nyce runs into the ropes and rolls over Hunter, attempting a rolling DDT, but Hunter ducks out. Hunter looks down at Nyce and applies a figure four. Gangsta gets up, walking past the two men across the ring towards Hardin and Prodigy. Gangsta ducks under Hardin, hitting a low blow. Hardin bends over, allowing Prodigy to leap onto the turnbuckle and get a sunset flip. Prodigy releases the hold quickly, then jumps onto Hardin's shoulders, throwing punches to the head. Gangsta moves out of the way towards the two cruisers, Mota and Style. He kicks Mota in the midsection, helping Style's chinlock. Style, however, takes exception, yelling at Gangsta that he doesn't want any help. Gangsta obliges, running at Style with a lariat.] JG - "I guess that's a form of not helping Style!" JH - "Unless Style has something caught in his throat and that was some wacky way to pop it out, I don't think Gangsta was helping." [Gangsta pushes Style towards the ropes, attempting to toss him over the top...and does...but, Style gets onto the apron and slides back in the ring, underneath Gangsta's legs. Gangsta bends to grab Style, but can't as Style slides too far. But, Mota comes up from behind and runs at Style with a clothesline. Style falls into Gangsta's back, then Gangsta lifts his head and back body drops Style over the top.] JG - "Style's gone! Gangsta Impasse lifted him over like an elephant tossing a mouse!" JH - "When have you seen an elephant toss a mouse?" [Gangsta looks up to see Mota moving at him with a lariat and gets nailed. But, he doesn't go over the top. Mota continues with punches to the midsection of the grounded Gangsta. He springs Gangsta off the ropes, but Mota trips over Hunter and Nyce in the middle of the ring. Hunter gets up and moves towards Mota, angered at the break up of his move. Gangsta, no with nobody to fight, grabs Nyce by the head and pulls him to the corner. Gangsta starts driving his elbow into the head of Nyce in the corner. In the corner to the left, Hardin lifts his legs up and wraps them around the shoulder of Prodigy, driving Prodigy's shoulders into the mat. Hardin gets up and starts stomping on Prodigy.] JH - "Like squashing a bug." JG - "The weight difference isn't that big with Prodigy and Hardin. They match up, weight-wise, quite well." JH - "Like a child squashing a bug." [Gangsta grabs Nyce's head after another elbow, then tosses him violently down on the mat. Gangsta climbs to the second turnbuckle and takes off with an elbow, but Nyce rolls out of the way. Nyce continues to roll, bumping into Chase Hunter's attack on Mota. Hunter turns his attention towards Nyce and attempts to get a kick to the midsection, but Nyce grabs his foot. Hunter attempts an enzuigiri, but Nyce ducks. Still holding onto Hunter's leg, Nyce applies an ankle lock submission. Gangsta moves towards Hardin's assault on Prodigy and lifts Hardin off in a cobra clutch. Mota gets up and breaks Nyce's hold. Mota grabs Hunter and attempts to toss him over, but can't do it. Hunter springs off the ropes behind Mota and grabs him by the waist, trying to lift Mota over the top. Nyce comes in from behind, helping Hunter with the toss. The two lift Mota over!] JG - "Derek Mota's gone!" JH - "Doesn't that say something about Hunter's strength? Mota's barely over 220 lbs., but he needs Nyce to help him toss Mota over." JG - "Wouldn't that say something about Nyce, too?" [Nyce turns towards Hunter and swings with a lariat, but Hunter ducks, grabbing Nyce in an atomic drop position. He lifts Nyce up...and over the top!] JG - "Nik Nyce is gone! We are down to four people!" JH - "But, Nyce gets the World title shot, right?" JG - "Yes, Nyce is in the cage tomorrow. Four to go!" [Prodigy starts kicking Hardin in the chest as Gangsta has him in a cobra clutch. Hunter turns around, fresh off the elimination of Nyce, and walks towards the three. He starts to help Prodigy, too.] JG - "We may be seeing some teamwork in order to get Hardin over the top. The man is well over 300 lbs., so they'll need an extra boost." JH - "I just think they don't like Hardin. I don't think anybody likes Hardin, actually." [Gangsta releases the cobra clutch, then motions to the other two to lift Hardin onto the turnbuckle. The three do, attempting to push Hardin over the top. Hardin uses his weight advantage to hang on, barely. As Gangsta takes a break from pushing, Hardin swings his hand around and pokes Gangsta in the eyes. Gangsta staggers back, Prodigy looks at him and gets a kick to the head by Hardin. This leaves Hunter who simply moves away as Hardin gets down. Hardin nails Hunter with a swinging punch, sending Hunter into the corner. Hardin moves to Gangsta and Prodigy, grabs their heads and whacks them together.] JG - "Looks like Hardin's fighting back!" JH - "He ain't picking strawberries. Can you even do that in the winter?" JG - "I don't think so, Jack. Strawberries grow in the ground and with the ground frozen, I'd think that'd hamper their growth." [As Gangsta and Prodigy roll on the mat, almost blaming each other for their downfall, Hunter runs in from the corner and leaps onto Hardin's shoulders. Hardin wobbles back towards the ropes, almost able to drop Hunter right over the top. Hunter, however, is able to bring himself across Hardin's body and into a small package. He releases the hold right away as both men spring to their feet. Hardin lunges at Hunter with a clothesline, but Hunter ducks, swinging himself into a crucifix, then a continued swing into a DDT!] JG - "What a move that was! Hunter was almost eliminated twice, but he came out nailing Hardin with a DDT." JH - "Hunter claims to be all versatile and he might have a point. But, he won't be able to stay in the ring for too much longer because of his size. Plus, he's just too damn annoying." JG - "To you." JH - "I'm sure somebody in the ring doesn't like him." [Gangsta and Prodigy get up, with Gangsta running at Prodigy with an elbow. He hits Prodigy in the chest, then bounces Prodigy off the ropes and comes back with a flying elbow. Hunter gets to his feet, signalling something with Gangsta. The two pick up their respective competitors and throw them into each other, Prodigy dropping when he hits the rock body of Hardin. Hardin jumps down on Prodigy, then starts throwing lefts and rights to the head of Prodigy. Gangsta shrugs his shoulders, then runs at Hunter with a lariat.] JH - "He turned on Hunter! Gangsta Impasse is turning on the fans!" JG - "No, he's not. Gangsta just likes to fight. Plus, with a title shot on the line, he's going to do anything." JH - "Ah, we can hope, though. Gangsta is a little odd right now with that funky rap stuff, but with a little work, he can be big." [Gangsta moves off the ropes and hits an elbowdrop across the chest. He continues to do this, increasing the red on the chest.] JH - "Is that red because of the elbows or because of the cold?" JG - "I don't know, but it sure is cold out here." JH - "I think it's the cold. None of the tights have the usual bulge." [Gangsta lifts up Hunter and tosses him into the turnbuckle, following in with a lariat. He lifts Hunter onto the top turnbuckle and tries to push him over, but can't get the final push. Hardin gets off Prodigy's shoulders and moves down to the legs. He grabs both Prodigy's legs and drops his elbow across the knee. Hardin repeats the move, then grabs the legs and applies a Texas cloverleaf!] JG - "Hardin's got the cloverleaf on! He can't eliminate by submission, but this will really wear down Prodigy." JH - "It's gonna hurt Prodigy so much, he'll eliminate himself. I think Prodigy would eliminate himself anyways, though. He's not the brightest of the bunch." [Hardin looks at Gangsta trying to lift Hunter over and releases the cloverleaf. He moves over to the corner, grabs Gangsta, spins him around and drops Gangsta with a Northern lights suplex. Hardin helps Hunter off the turnbuckle, then clotheslines him over the top!] JG - "Chase Hunter's gone!" JH - "Never trust Jay Dub...who thought of that name, anyways? Pretty catchy and good for ryhmes." [Hardin moves away from the turnbuckle, looking down at the two fallen wrestlers. He just grins. As Gangsta starts to budge, Hardin moves in and starts stomping on him. Gangsta rolls under the ropes to escape the blows, not eliminating himself, and Hardin stops stomping. He turns to see Prodigy running at him with an axehandle. Hardin starts to go over the top, hanging on the top, Gangsta tries to pull the rope down, but Hardin doesn't go!] JG - "They almost got Hardin! But, the big man is still in it!" JH - "But, he's hanging on with less than a thread. The other two guys in the ring right now want to kill Hardin more than Texas wants to nab Ms. Tucker." JG - "Uh, that's a little old news, Jack." [Gangsta rolls back in the ring, then pulls Hardin's leg back down. He and Prodigy send Hardin into the ropes and meet him in the middle of the ring with a double clothesline. Gangsta runs off the ropes and nails an elbowdrop, followed by Prodigy doing the same. As Prodigy gets up, Gangsta jumps over Hardin and clotheslines Prodigy.] JG - "It really is a free for all, especially with a title shot on the line here. Gangsta would probably never hit Prodigy, but now he does." JH - "But, you never need a reason to hit Prodigy. And, you never need a reason to hit _on_ Black Kat." [Gangsta continues to hit Prodigy with hard rights, pushing him into the ropes. He sends Prodigy across the ring into the ropes, forcing Prodigy to jump over Hardin once, then again on the way back. As Prodigy lands from the second hop, Gangsta ducks his head and back body drops Prodigy over the top!] JG - "Prodigy's gone!" JH - "That's a surprise?" [As Gangsta turns back around, he sees Hardin running at him with a lariat. Gangsta gets nailed and goes over the top!] JG - "Hardin wins! Hardin wins!" JH - "I knew he would win! Jacks has the bulk, Robbie has the brawling, but Hardin's got both! What an animal!" [Gangsta pulls himself out of the snowbank and looks up the aisle only to see his nemesis: "Cowboy" Ken Curtis. Gangsta runs up the aisle and dives at Curtis, the two exchanging rapid punches in the snowy ground.] JG - "Gangsta and Curtis! They're going at it!" JH - "Gangsta's got another title shot lined up, too. Now that he's in the top five, he'll be facing Curtis, or the champ at the time, sometime in the next few weeks." JG - "And, don't forget Hardin! We could see a Curtis/Hardin rematch, this time for the title! Folks, we've got to go! Make sure you tune in tomorrow night when we'll find out who gets the shots when in a steel cage matchup! Good night!" -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
