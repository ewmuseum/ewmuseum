[SCRA] Fatal Fall, Part 5

13 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
An SCRA Production http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html JG - "Sublime could have quite some wait, there. Although, the House of Pancakes have two on one numbers, now." JH - "It is always two on one with Eddy around. He provides two bodies by himself. In fact, I hear during those toughman contests, Jacks ate somebody! There could really be two people in one for Eddy "Flap" Jacks." JG - "That sure explains the immense body mass." [Jacks walks over to the corner and helps his partner out. He spins around Knight and clotheslines him. Jacks helps Tulkas come to his senses. Tulkas soon becomes ready to fight and the two pick up Green Knight. They send him into the ropes, hitting a back body drop. Knight doesn't make it all the way to the outside.] JG - "Didn't make it quite far enough." JH - "Try and try again." [The two HoP members send Green Knight into the ropes once more. This time, as he hits the ropes, Protect Yo Neck comes on the speakers.] JG - "Gangsta Impasse! A new wrestler in the league is running full speed down the aisle." [Knight stops just before he hits the two HoP members. Gangsta slides in and the two each grab a HoP member and ddt them.] JG - "Looks like Gangsta and Green Knight have joined forces against the two House of Pancakes members." JH - "That's right, Bob! Tell him what he's won." JG - "Your sarcasm doesn't amuse me." JH - "Nothing amuses you. You are just a rock, everything just bounces right off and lands somewhere behind us in the booth. That's where all the drunk people are. They think everything is funny." [Gangsta and Green Knight go to high five each other, but Gangsta kicks Green Knight in the stomach.] JG - "Gangsta is fighting all for himself!" JH - "That's the way you have to do it. On the streets, you can't wait around for people to give you stuff, you go and get it." JG - "How would you know that?" JH - "I have been to my fair share of "hood" movies." [Gangsta nails Green Knight over the back with an axehandle. He picks up brawls with him until Green Knight goes into the ropes. Gangsta runs at Green Knight and clotheslines him over the top ropes.] JG - "Green Knight is eliminated!" JH - "Sucker. Not a very good showing on his wedding day." [Green Knight wearily gets up with help from the Activists. They give him some pointers and send him off to the second ring. In the first ring (R1), Gangsta picks up both House of Pancakes members. He forces them to headbutt each other. Gangsta picks up Tulkas and suplexes him on top of Jacks. In ring two (R2), Green Knight rolled under the ropes. Sublime started to stomp on him, not giving Green Knight a chance.] JG - "Looks like the fast and furious action has begun. I can hardly keep up with both rings." JH - "Great, we won't have to listen to you talk about pointless stuff." [Sublime continues to stomp on Green Knight, but Green Knight continues to reach his knees each time. He spins onto his back, leaving his face exposed for a kick by Sublime. Sublime nails Green Knight in the nose which immediately starts bleeding.] JH - "Green Knight is juicing! Way to go Sublime, mash his face in!" JG - "That seems to have infuriated Green Knight, much like it did to Lucifer a while back." [Green Knight grabs his nose and glares at Sublime. He gets up fast and starts throwing rights at Sublime. Sublime goes flying across the ring, landing in the corner turnbuckle. In the other ring, Gangsta ripped the turnbuckle padding off, leaving it exposed. He picked up Tulkas and whipped him into the exposed turnbuckle. Gangsta follows him in, but Tulkas ducks out of the way, leaving Gangsta running with his head into the exposed turnbuckle. Gangsta starts to bleed.] JG - "It is really starting to get nasty." [Instant Karma begins to play throughout the arena as the fans give a bewildered look towards the curtain.] JG - "Who is this?" JH - "My main man, Taskan! Finally, he gets into some good music and rids himself of that awful partner." [Taskan walks slowly to the ring. He gets in and helps up Eddy "Flap" Jacks. The two walk over and grab Gangsta. Along with Tulkas, they triple team Gangsta in the corner. In R2, Green Knight continues to dominate Sublime. He throws him into the ropes and back body drops Sublime. Sublime gets right back up and walks to Green Knight. Knight dropkicks Sublime, with the momentum carrying Sublime over the top ropes.] JG - "Sublime has been eliminated from the second ring! He is now waiting in the third ring and could be waiting for a while.] JH - "What a putz. The guy has potential, but isn't realizing it. He needs a manager, like me. We could lose all that girly acrobatic stuff and fine tune his brawling skills." JG - "Why do you get yourself into these things?" JH - "They are successful, aren't they?" [In R1, Gangsta is being pummelled in the corner. The two House of Pancakes members pick up Gangsta and set him up for Taskan. Taskan attempts an Asai moonsault, but Gangsta moves out of the way. The moonsault takes out the House of Pancakes. Gangsta hits his own Asai moonsault, raising his arms to the fans.] JG - "Now, that is how you do it!" JH - "Whatever. The House couldn't hold Gangsta because of his blood. They didn't want to get aids." [Gangsta walks around the ring, locking eyes with the Activists. He motions for one of the signs and brings it inside the ring. Baker goes under the ring and grabs another sign. Gangsta waits for Tulkas to get up and nails him with the sign, breaking the sign in half.] JG - "Tulkas is now bleeding! Get Red Cross out here, they would have a pool of donations! Green Knight is tending to what may be a broken nose in the second ring, Gangsta and Tulkas have caused each other to start bleeding from the forehead." [The German National Anthem begins playing.] JG - "Whichever Eagle this is, they better watch out." [Joseph Eagle comes down the aisle, climbing on the top rope. He hits a moonsault off the top on Taskan. Gangsta picks up Jacks and bodyslams him on top of Tulkas.] JG - "Looks like Taskan and the House of Pancakes are being out maneuvered right now. Although they have the man advantage, they aren't using it." JH - "Get more people in here! Then you will see some brawling." [Eagle moves over to help Gangsta. He climbs to the top turnbuckle. Gangsta, preoccupied with Eddy "Flap" Jacks and Tulkas, didn't see Taskan coming. Taskan rammed into the ropes, sending Eagle crashing down onto the exposed turnbuckle.] JH - "Hahahaha! No more little Germanians!" JG - "Uh, it isn't worth it." [Taskan tries to push Eagle off the turnbuckle, but the Activists use their protest signs to push Eagle towards the ring. The Activists' push overcame Taskan's and Eagle rolled off the turnbuckle, into the ring.] JG - "The Activists are really an important factor in this rumble. Thus far, they have hit people with their signs, push people back in with their signs and distracted people." JH - "Get them out of here! They are causing too much trouble." JG - "That's what activists do." [Taskan picks up Eagle and whips him into the ropes. Gangsta moves off the House of Pancakes and dives at Taskan's knees. Taskan falls to the ground and Eagle jumps over both men. Both men get up to see Eagle flying in with a cross body block. Gangsta holds Eagle's feet and spins them, helping Eagle hit a spinning headscissors takedown.] JG - "What teamwork! What a move! That might be the most amazing thing I have seen in my life." JH - "You haven't seen much, have you, Grey?" JG - "I know what you are trying to do and it isn't going to work." JH - "Come on! Where are the sources of dates coming from these days?" JG - "Stop it, Jack." JH - "Prisons? Psychiatric hospitals? Pet stores?" ["Hot" Brad Baldwin comes running down the aisle, jumping in the ring.] JH - "Where did he come from?" JG - "I didn't hear any music, did you?" JH - "Nope. Must have been cheating." JG - "It is around the time when a new competitor should come out, but why no music?" [Baldwin walks over to help Gangsta. Gangsta throws Tulkas into the opposite turnbuckle. He then points Baldwin in that direction. Baldwin doesn't want to go. Gangsta pushes him over to the corner, forcing Baldwin to attack Tulkas.] JG - "Gangsta is really taking control of the ring right now. He is single handedly crushing the House of Pancakes and controlling the traffic." JH - "That's what happens when you are an experienced street fighter who can brawl like Gangsta." JG - "So, you like him?" JH - "I don't really know. He can go either way with me." [Baldwin starts chopping at Tulkas in the corner. Gangsta also chops at Jacks. Gangsta and Baldwin meet eyes and attempt to throw Jacks and Tulkas into each other, but Jacks and Tulkas reverse. Gangsta and Baldwin meet in the center of the ring, falling back.] JH - "There's where the teamwork of the House of Pancakes show. They know how each other thinks. Beautiful." [Eagle continues his attack on Taskan. He attacks Taskan in a third corner, which allows him to utilize his aerial tactics withouth the danger. Eagle places Taskan on the second rope and dropkicks him. Taskan tumbles almost to the outside, but grasps onto the top rope.] JH - "Look at that beautiful move by Taskan. I love this guy!" JG - "He has sure changed to suit your styles, lately." JH - "The only change is that the loser Killer Compton isn't around. With that guy, Taskan was being hidden." [1979 blares through the speakers as the crowd goes nuts. Taskan realizes who is coming down as he sees Compton sprinting towards the ring. Compton rolls under the ropes. He sees Taskan hanging on and leaps over the top ropes, grabbing Taskan and executing what would seem to be a modified sunset flip.] JG - "Unbelievable! That could have been the greatest move I have seen in my entire life! Compton lept over the top rope, grabbed Taskan's back like in a sunset flip, then continued with his momentum to pull Taskan off the ropes, into a tiger suplex position and slammed Taskan into the mat." JH - "For those viewers who did not see the move, Grey described it pretty good. There is just one problem. Nobody knows what the hell any of those moves are. Therefore, once again, Grey fails." JG - "Both men are paying for that one. Taskan, obviously, got the worse end of it, ramming his back into the concrete. I think I see some blood coming out of the back of his head." JH - "Get a doctor out here! Taskan is hurt! We need a plastic surgeon, too. We can't damage his looks!" JG - "Killer Compton may also need some help. He came down hard on his legs and butt." [Both men start moving as the crowd as well as wrestlers are fixed on them. Compton gets up first and picks up Taskan. He throws Taskan in R2, motioning to Green Knight not to touch him. Taskan continues rolling, eliminating himself straight away. Compton sees this and flies over the top ropes, again, this time hitting Taskan with a swan dive.] JG - "Killer Compton is putting on one hell of a show! He is eliminating himself to get revenge on Taskan, and what a way!" JH - "It shows the stupidity, again. Why would you eliminate yourself?" [Compton throws Taskan into ring three (R3). He pushes Sublime out of the way and marches at Taskan. Taskan, once again, bails out, sending himself over the top rope. Compton follows, this time just going over the top with no attached move. The two former partners brawl down the aisle, backstage. The wrestlers who had previously been watching these incidents turn to each other and begin their own quarrels. Joseph Eagle and "Hot" Brad Baldwin attack each other. Eddy "Flap" Jacks and Tulkas "The Troll" Naugrim double team Gangsta Impasse. Thunderkiss 69 comes over the speakers as Marc Mission walks down the aisle. The big man steps over the top ropes and grabs Joseph Eagle. Mission takes Eagle and chokeslams him. He picks Eagle up and sends him flying over the top rope.] JG - "Joseph Eagle has been eliminated! Marc Mission just came right in and eliminated his enemy." [Gangsta fights his way to his fight against the double team efforts of the House of Pancakes. He floors Jacks with a powerful right and then attacks Tulkas with kicks and punches. Tulkas backs into the ropes and Gangsta runs at him, clotheslining him over the top.] JG - "Tulkas is eliminated! Eddy "Flap" Jacks is all alone and Gangsta Impasse is looking for revenge." JH - "Who cares. Eddy will floor Gangsta. I like Gangsta, but Eddy is the main man of Jack Hazard." [Gangsta picks up Jacks and starts delivering rights to the head. "Hot" Brad Baldwin sees Marc Mission turning around near the ropes. Baldwin dropkicks Mission, sending him over the top rope.] JG - "The big man, Marc Mission is now in ring two! These past few seconds have seen three top tag superstars eliminated. Ring two has more people than ring one!" [Marc Mission rolls into R2, seeing Tulkas and Eagle fighting. He joins in their fight. Green Knight is left standing alone. He walks over to the three tag stars and spins Tulkas around. Knight lands a big right, sending Tulkas staggering away from the other two. Knight and Tulkas continue fighting in the corner.] JG - "Both rings are really heating up with action. Sublime is still alone in the final ring." JH - "Look at Baldwin. I knew he was a wimp. He is just standing around." JG - "He has nobody to fight with. Gangsta Impasse and Eddy "Flap" Jacks are busy brawling. Baldwin has time to do, well, his hair." [Baldwin watches Gangsta pick Eddy up and place him on the top turnbuckle. Gangsta climbs to the second rope and starts punching Jacks as the crowd counts along. Gangsta stands on the top rope and flips Jacks over with a superplex.] JG - "An extremely high risk move there by Gangsta. Going to the top ropes in these rumbles is not recommended." JH - "Yet, the morons like Joseph Eagle continue to do so." [Eagle is seen perched upon the top rope, diving at Marc Mission with a missile dropkick. Upon hitting, the dropkick, a masked man runs into R1, attacking Baldwin.] JG - "Silverblade is here! Another newcomer to SCRA with a mysterious past. He didn't reveal much in his tryouts, but promises to be a great." JH - "He told me some stuff. He has a hidden agenda for somebody here, which is why the mask and stuff." JG - "Maybe it is a current wrestler, under a mask?" JH - "I don't think so. The voice sounded different." [Silverblade and Baldwin brawl into the corner where the exposed turnbuckle is. Baldwin nails Silverblade with a big kick to the gut, following with a European uppercut. He takes Silverblade's head and starts ramming it into the turnbuckle, ripping the mask of Silverblade. Silverblade hits a mule kick, sending Baldwin off him.] JH - "Take that Baldwin! See how many girls flock to you when your voice becomes a Grey-esque high pitched squeel!" JG - "I resent that." JH - "Grey, lower the decibels. I am glad I didn't bring Pooky out here." JG - "Pooky?" JH - "My goldfish. She wants to see some of the action, so I promised her that I would bring her to a big show. I didn't bring her this time, but she will come. Lucky too, that one would have shattered her bowl." [Silverblade tries to repair his mask so that his identity is covered. He walks over to Baldwin, passing Jacks and Gangsta on the way. Gangsta is stomping on Jacks. However, Jacks grabs Gangsta's foot and bites it!] JG - "Jacks just bit Gangsta Impasse's foot! He must be hungry." JH - "No kidding. I hear MacDonald has been starving him for weeks so that he is hungry for the gold." JG - "Good thing he didn't get a title shot." JH - "Why? Because SCRA knows he would win and doesn't want him holding the gold?" JG - "No, if he was hungry for the gold, he would eat it!"
