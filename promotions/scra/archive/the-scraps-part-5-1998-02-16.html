[SCRA] The Scraps [5/6] (16 February 1998)

16 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 6 ) | | \ | | Derek Mota vs. "Iconoclast" Sean Watts / |___ | | | | Writer: Oliver B. (_________________________________________________________________________ [The camera pans out to center ring, focusing on an unsteady-looking Ed Jaspard. Jaspard clutches the microphone protectively.] EJ - "I don't have a lot of time, folks. Our first competitor hails from Toronto, Ontario, and weighs in at 224 stones...kilograms... uh, pounds. It's Derek Mota and he bills himself as 'the human highlight reel.' Let's here it for the littlest big man in the FeWS...I mean SCRA! (to himself) That's good enough for 'em, the bastards." [Pantera's "The Great Southern Trendkill" blares over the PA as Mota, attired in little more than a shiny ring jacket and standard trunks. He enters the ring, removes the jacket and begins to loosen up for the match. Despite his rather mundane entrance, Mota is greeted by a big fan pop.] JH - "He came out first, Grey. Ain't good for the little man." JG - "Not necessarily, Jack. Mota's a lot more experienced than Mr. Watts. Let's see what Ed Jaspard has to say." [Jaspard, having recovered from a minor bout with unconsciousness, straightens himself up to his full height and resumes the introductions.] EJ - "Mr. Mota is opposed by none other than "Iconoclast" Sean Watts. Watts weighs in at 238 lbs. and designates Seattle, USA, as his hometown. Give it up, folks!" [As "Search and Destroy" becomes audible over the PA, a despondent-looking Sean Watts lumbers out of the locker room tunnel and towards the ring. Several fans wave hands and posters at Watts only to watch him shake his head in a half-hearted "no" and continue on towards the ring. Watts steps through the ropes and slumps into the corner.] JH - "Doesn't strike an intimidating presence, Grey. Still, Watts is a real animal when the bell rings." DING! DING! DING! JG - "We're about to see firsthand what happens when the 'Iconoclast''s neurosis matches up against some of Mota's considerable technical prowess." JH - "He's a madman. I wouldn't hesitate to see he's the most dangerous man in our sport." [Watts and Mota circle one another warily, neither allowing the gaze of the other man to escape. Watts attempts a locker but is shoved back by Mota, who bounces off the ropes and attempts a cross-body press only to be.... countered into a DDT by Watts. Sensing that his opponent is dazed, Watts covers Mota... 1! 2! Mota's foot is on the ropes! Huge pop! Mota gets up and unleashes a salvo of furious blows on a stunned Watts, backing him into a corner.] JG - "Explosive action early on here, Jack. Watts nailed Mota with one of his patented DDT's and nearly got the victory but..." JH - (interrupting) "The Canadian firefly proved that he's got a vicious bite." JG - "Way to close, Jack." [Sensing that his opponent is weakened, Mota whips Watts into the ropes and takes him down with a brilliant Russian legsweep. Keeping his opponent on the mat, Mota leaps over Watts and applies a reverse chinlock. Watts, however, manages to get a foot on the ropes. As the ref pushes Mota off Watts, the "Iconoclast" uses the delay to stagger his opponent with a brutal European uppercut. Wading forward, he bodyslams Mota and presses his advantage with several stomps on his prone opponent before dragging him back to his feet.] JG - "Watts making excellent uses of his size. As you can see, he's keeping Mota dazed and off the ground." JH - "...Which is something other people of Watts' size have been trying to do for years. Keep in mind that Mota rocked the world of ex-SCRA'er Aristotle Jones at Rasslefest...and that guy was close to 240 lbs." [Watts nails Mota with a standing clothesline that sends the smaller man staggering into the corner. Watts rushes in after Mota, intent on nailing his opponent with a forearm smash...but Mota rolls out of the way and the "Iconoclast" crashes into the turnbuckle! Small pop!] JG - "Excellent tactic by Mota! His ability to keep an opponent off-balance has saved him many a time in the squared circle." JH - "Watts needs to learn to control his anger...moves like that can end a rookie's career WAY quick." [Mota rebounds quickly, executing a snap-mare that flips Watts out of the corner and allows the Mota to apply an armbar.] JH - "Why's Mota slowing down the pace? That guy thrives on movement." JG - "He's not careless, Jack. Derek obviously realizes that Watts has the ability to make him pay for acrobatic miscues." [As Watts struggles to his feet, Mota trips out his legs and falls on the "Iconoclast", grabbing his heel and slapping on an ankle-lock submission... only to to be kicked into the ropes by a frantic Watts. Desperate and wearied, Watts charges into Mota, knocking him out of the ring with a brutal clothesline.] JH - "Out through the second rope...ooh, so close to the big time fall!" [Mota, dazed, stumbles into the ring steps before being hammered in the back by a brutal Watts axehandle. Seizing the advantage, Watts wraps his arms around Mota and executes a belly-to-back suplex. He yanks Mota up, slaps him and then turns him around before executing an....] JG - "Inverted DDT! Did you hear that pop, Jack?" JH - "Haven't heard a crunch that good since the Crocodile Hunters lef the league. God how I miss those guys." [Watts, satisfied that Mota is suitably wearied, heaves the Canadian into the ring and enters after him, content that his work is nearly finished. As he enters the ring, however, he's brought to his nears by a desperation forearm to the groin from a wobbling Mota. Mota staggers to his feet and bulldogs Watts, then mounts the top turnbuckle and attempts a....] JG - "Shooting star press!" JH - "He hit hard! Watts rolled out of the way!" [Watts staggers back to his feet, his body pushed to its limits, his strength nearly spent. He walks over to Mota, yanks him up and begins to pummel him. One...two...three....four....the blows rain down on Mota's head as he staggers back, unable to defend himself. Finally, realizing that Mota is helpless in the corner, Watts raises his hands skyward. He grabs Mota in a full nelson and executes a snap suplex that hurls the Canadian into middle ring.] JG - "Mota's completely laid out. He's not moving! This is it, the end, the finish!" JH - "Pretty emotional, Grey? I haven't seen you get this excited since Mike Justice's last match." [Watts puts a leg on Mota and the ref begins to count. 1! 2! Watts withdraws his leg! Huge pop!] JG - "What the....?" JH - "Why in the hell...?" [Watts lifts Mota over his shoulder and spins him around, not even flinching as he brings the smaller man's head down on his knee with an audible crunch! Watts raises his hands again, pandering to the crowd. 'I thought he could beat me,' Watts remarks as the camera zooms in on him, 'Geez...and I'm not that good!'. Mota, however, taps into some hidden reservoir of strength and sneaks up behind the celebrating Watts, downing him with an impressive backdrop. Watts, more shocked than hurt, stumbles clumsily out of the ring as Mota struggles to regain his footing.] JG - "Mota taking advantage of Watts' foolish crowd-pandering! He sure turned this thing around!" JH - "It's not over yet, Jon. Watts'll be all business when he gets back into the ring." JG - "If, Jack! Look....It's Aladdin Style!" [Watts, bracing himself on the ring steps as he regains his breath, fails to see the "Golden King" as a huge pop reverberates through the crowd. The "Iconoclast" turns just in time to watch Style plants a title into Watt's mouth.] JG - "Huh? Who's belt is that?" JH - "Who knows." [Watts falls unconscious against the ring steps. Style panders to the crowd, points at Mota, hits Watts again, then rolls him back into the ring. He departs just as a chair-wielding Tommy Gregory charges after him.] JG - "Guess somebody delivered this match to Mota...but why?" JH - "Who cares? Neither one of these guys won this match...nor did I care who won." [Back in the ring, Mota plants a foot on the KO'ed Watts. 1! 2! 3!] JG - "That match left me with a bad taste in my mouth, Jack. Both those guys wrestled so hard it was a shame that neither won." JH - (sarcastically) "Yeah...a damn shame." JG - "And what of the Aladdin Style development? Are he and Mota working together? Maybe that's why Mota didn't feel like going all-out in this one." JH - "Questions for another time, Jon, questions for another time." [As Watts lies on his back, Sengir walks down the aisle, picking up the title.] JG - "It's Sengir's belt!" [Sengir and Jezra are now standing both above Watts and Sengir is cockily smiling into the camera] JG - "Hey, what the... Jezra is taking off her trenchcoat, and... she has a referee's shirt underneath! What the hell..." JH - "Oh no! Oh God no! This is despicable! Sengir drops on top of the unconscious Watts, and Jezra drops to the mat..." [Jezra slaps her hand three times on the mat as Sengir "covers" the downed Sean Watts. Then Sengir jumps up, raises his ESWP title belt and hugs Jezra. Jezra raises his arm and grabs the house mike] JW - "Ladies and gentlemen, your winner... and STILL ESWP World Junior Heavyweight Champion... and STILL better then that joke Sean Watts... and STILL the best Cruiserweight on the face of this earth... SEEEEEEEEENGIR!!" [The crowd pops, a mixed pop paired with boos. Sengir kneels on top of Watts and rubs the belt in his face, then gets up again and takes the mike from Jezra's fingers] S - "Listen, Seanie. I remember you nailing me with your TCW Copperweight belt... well, it looks like we're even now. I am still a World champion, you are still a joke. Come on Jez, time to go, let's not disturb Seanie's sweet dreams." [Sengir raises the ESWP title belt into the air and Jezra cockily blows a kiss into the camera. "Conquest of Paradise" by Vangelis plays as the two make their way back down the aisle while the referee is trying to awaken the unconscious Sean Watts] JG - "Sengir is back! And, he wants Sean Watts." JH - "Too bad the German won't do too good. Even though Watts' work ethic is about as high as a grandma's skirt, Germans can't do anything." JG - "What's with the German bashing, Jack? I don't think you've ever told us." JH - "Do I need a reason?" JG - "Like pulling teeth. Folks, next up is another big one. This could be the main event, but we've got the World title on the line, so it isn't." JH - "It is in my books. Prodigy can't wrestle in a main event." JG - "Gangsta Impasse faces off with "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin in a classic street against country battle! Let's go to the ring for the intros!" ___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 7 ) | | \ | | "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin vs. Gangsta Impasse / |___ | | | | Writer: Trevor G. (_________________________________________________________________________ [Ed Jaspard is standing in the ring mike in hand, hair slicked back and black ray bans over his eyes.] JH - "Do we ever see Ed enter from the entrance ramp? It seems like every week he just ends up in the ring." EJ - "Foxes and Studmuffins, this matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit. Coming down the aisle first, from Detriot, Michigan and weighing in at 280 lbs, this is Gangsta Impasse!" [Led by John Drake, Gangsta Impasse walks from behind the curtains dressed in his traditional boxing robe. John climbs up the ring apron first and holds the ropes open for Gangsta. Gangsta steps through and starts shadow boxing. Climbing to the top turnbuckles, Gangsta points to his waist, signalling for the belt. He does this to all four corners, as Jaspard continues....] EJ - "And his opponent, from....." [Ed zips up his jacket] EJ - "Aw, my lip's stuck. From Dry Gulch, Texas, at 353 lbs, he is 'Outlaw' J.W. Hardin!" [Outlaw Blues echos throughout the arena as a red spotlight floods the entranceway. The giant J.W. Hardin walks slowly towards the ring, with his hat down over his eyes. Gangsta drops down from the fourth turnbuckle and takes off his robe as Hardin enters the ring. Hardin stares at Gangsta and removes his own hat and coat.] JH - "Looks like Ed's got a gusher there. Possibly a few stiches, a few less entrances?" DING! DING! DING! [Gangsta and Hardin circle each other. Gangsta's not used to facing someone that much bigger than him, but he doesn't seem intimidated at all. Hardin tries to grab Gangsta, but Gansta ducks behind Hardin and gives him a contempuous shove. Hardin turns around and glares.] JG - "Ohhh, Hardin didn't like that." JH - "I don't think he likes you. I don't think anyone likes you. I know I don't." [Again Hardin tries to grab Gangsta, and again Gangsta ducks behind the bigger man. This time Gangsta slaps him lightly in the back of the head and smirks. Hardin turns around and nails Gangsta with a slap across the face that echos through the arena. Gangsta takes two steps back and lands flat on his backside. With Gangsta rubbing his stinging cheek, both competitors glare at each other.] JH - "This is a real snoozer." JG - "Shut up. They're just trying to make a game plan." JH - "You wanna know my game plan?" JG - "Maybe." JH - "I'm gonna come at you like a hurricane of legs, fists and teeth." [Gangsta gets up, furious. The two lock up in the middle of the ring. For several moments the push and shove, neither backing down. Moments pass ... then Gangsta is pushed to the floor again! This time Hardin starts kicking Gangsta while he is down. Hardin gets four good stomps in before Gangsta rolls out of the ring.] JG - "A little regroup session between Gangsta and Drake here." JH - "Hey, you know what would be great? If we could clone a hot dog. There'd be an abundant supply of hot dogs. It'd become our currency. One hot dog would cost roughly a nickel." JG - "You've got to stop watching Saturday Night Live." [Outside the ring, Gangsta is furious. He grabs a cears from a ringside official and climbs back up on the ring apron, threatening Hardin with the cears. Hardin backs away and the referee warns Gangsta. Gangsta thinks twice, then tosses the cears to the floor and enters the ring again.] JG - "Gangsta Impasse is furious here." JH - "Yea, his bald head is starting to steam." [This time when the two lock up, Gangsta pulls Hardwin over with a headlock takedown. Hardin's shoulders are down, but before the ref can start counting, Gangsta starts pounding on Hardin's face with closed fists. The ref starts to count Gangsta fists instead of the pin. Gangsta breaks at four and then starts with kicks and stomps of his own. Hardin takes three nasty shots, then grabs Gangsta legs out from under him. Gangsta crashes to the mat and Hardin starts pounding on him. The ref starts counting, but Gangsta rolls the position over and starts landing more of his own punches. The ref starts counting again, but then Hardin rolls it over and starts nailing Gangsta again. Gangsta reverses the position one more time, but that causes both men to fall out of the ring to the floor.] JG - "A flurry of punches resulting in a clumsy action. Welcome to wrestling." [Hardin picks up Gangsta's discarded cears and takes a swing. Gangsta ducks and the cears nails the turnbuckle pole with a resounded KLANG! Gangsta throws a punch, nailing Hardin right on the bridge of the nose. Hardin drops the cears and takes a couple steps back. Gangsta catches the cears and is about to nail Hardin when the ref gets between them.] JH - "Aw come on ref, let them hit eachother with chairs until they're blue." [Gangsta tosses the cears away again and gets back into the ring. Hardin paces for a few seconds, waiting for the stars in his eyes from Gangsta's last shot to go away. Finally, at the ref's nine-count, he gets back in the ring. As he slides in under the bottom rope, he is me by Gangsta who drops an elbow on the back of the Outlaw's neck. Gangsta picks him up and whips him up against the ropes, following it up with a tilt-a-whirl suplex on the big man. The crowd pops loud, very impressed that Gangsta could hit that move against such a large opponent.] JH - "He just picked up 350 plus pounds of beef. This guy could be an olympian. Hell, he could beat all the Canadian weightlifters. What's their best? 200-230 pounds dead lift?" [Gangsta puts Hardin in the powerbomb position and singles for the end. Hardin counters it with a back body drop. Gangsta gets back up just in time to be met with a boot to the face. Gangsta goes down again. Hardin picks him up and nails a brutal side slam, followed by a leg drop. Hardin covers. 1! KICKOUT!] JG - "Looks like Gangsta Impasse was going to go for Gangsta Impact before Hardin reversed it." [Hardin picks Gangsta up and nails a headbutt, dropping Gangsta again. He starts choking out Gangsta with his boot, until the ref counts to four, then Hardin breaks. He picks Gangsta up and hits a swinging neckbreaker, followed by another legdrop. Again Hardin goes for the pin. 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JH - "Why would someone try to get a pin off a legdrop. Hardin may be big, but legdrops don't hurt." JG - "Maybe so, maybe no." [Hardin picks up Gangsta and irish whips him agains the ropes. Hardin gives him a big boot the face and Gangsta goes down again. Hardin picks him up and goes for a bodyslam, but Gangsta counters with a small package. 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "A lot of pinning going on in this match..." JH - "Hey Grey, I'm working on a new finisher. Could I try it on you?" JG - "What is it?" JH - "Well, I grab your head and smash it into the pole. Could I try it?" JG - "[sarcastically]Okay." [Gangsta rolls out of the ring to collect himself. He paces around the outside until the ref gets to the eight count, then he reenters. Hardin charges him, but Gangsta sidesteps and Hardin hits the corner stermen first. Gangsta goes behind him and tries for a german suplex. Hardin grabs the ropes so Gangsta can't take him over. Gangsta gives it another heave, but Hardin won't let go. Hardin swings with a back elbow but Gangsta ducks it. Hardin spins around from the momentum of the missed swing and Gangsta nails him with a belly-to-belly suplex. Gangsta covers. 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Gangsta's really picking the 350 pound man up with ease." [Gangsta attempts the powerbomb again. Hardin goes up, then comes crashing down hard. Gangsta starts shadowboxing, and puts on foot on Hardin's chest for the cover. 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "He hit a powerbomb on Hardin!" JH - "BUT HE DIDN'T GET THE PIN! Jeez, calm down." [Hardin rolls over onto his stomach and Gangsta uses the the opportunity to put on a camel clutch. Hardin refuses to submit. After several moments in the hold, Hardin manages to pull his knees under him. With superhuman effort, Hardin climbs to his feet and then runs backwards, sandwiching Gangsta in the corner. Both wrestlers fall, and the ref starts to count them down.] JG - "Didn't that look awkward." JH - "It was Hardin's clumsy antics which again resulted in a great wrestling maneuver." [Gangsta staggers to his feet first and sets Hardin up for the piledriver. Gangsta hits it and goes for the cover, but Hardin's too tall and puts his foot on the rope before the ref can get into position. Gangsta singles for the Gangsta impact and picks Hardin up over his shoulder. He walks towards the corner. Hardin pushes himself off of Gangsta's shoulders and levels Gangsta in the back of the head with a clothesline. Both wrestlers collapse and the ref starts counting them down again.] JG - "Hardin's fighting back." JH - "Yes he is. Quite well too." [This time it is Hardin that is up first. He singles for a powerbomb of his own and executes it perfectly. Hardin covers. 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Hardin returns the favour!" JH - "BUT AGAIN DIDN'T GET THE COVER! Jeez, calm down." [Hardin, exhausted, walks over to the opposite corner and waits for Gangsta to get up. The stunned Gangsta is slow to rise, but when he does he is met by a Hardin bulldog. Hardin covers again. 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Gangsta sure looks tired." JH - "Yea, Gangsta's usually drunk by 7 and passed out by 8." [Hardin, frustrated, picks up Gangsta by the ears again. Gangsta in desperation gouges Hardin's eyes. Hardin lets go of his opponent and is bodyslamed. Gangsta tosses Hardin over the top rope to the floor. Gangsta waits until Hardin is upright on the outside and then flies over the ropes with a suicide dive. Both wrestlers land hard on the floor. The ref slides out to check if anyone is hurt. The crowd is going wild for the plancha by the 6'8", 280 lb Gangsta Impasse.] JG - "SUICIDE DIVE BY GANGSTA! The crowd's on their feet." JH - "[silence]" [The ref sees that they are both only stunned, then climbs back into the ring to start the count. Gangsta gets up first and throws Hardin into the ring. Gangsta follows and singles for the Gangsta Impact again.] JG - "Could we see it this time? Hardin's out cold." -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
