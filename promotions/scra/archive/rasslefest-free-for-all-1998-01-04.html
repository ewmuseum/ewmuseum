[SCRA] Rasslefest Free for All!

4 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

It doesn't stay long, eventually cutting to the broadcast booth.] JG - "Hello fans and welcome to SCRA's first ever Free for All! We are here in Madison Square Gardens, New York City, just minutes away from the biggest night in SCRA history, Rasslefest!" JH - "It's gonna show these New Yorkers what sport really is. The Giants and Jets are gone. The Islanders are, well, the Islanders. The Knicks don't have the same flair without Ewing. The Rangers are playing average, but SCRA is gonna rock their world." JG - "It could pump life into New York's ailing sporting scene. We've got loads of action to get through in the Free for All, including running down the action at Rasslefest. In the big main event, we'll see Lucifer get his final shot at glory as he takes on Eddy "Flap" Jacks. That one should be great." JH - "Two big boys locking up with one thing in mind. We'd better see some crimson." JG - "I'm sure their emotions will get the better of them and we will see some wild action. Johnny Destiny and Nathan Flash do battle for the first and last time. The loser leaves town, the winner takes home the North American gold." JH - "Can't wait to see Destiny finally leave. I've been waiting for about 7 long months to see that happen." JG - "It may not. Flash may be the smarter wrestler, but he doesn't have the experience Destiny has. It should be a great match. We've also got the big dog collar match coming up! Anthony Logan and Aladdin Style will settle the score. That one should also be a real scrap." JH - "The two brothers turn on each other. I like those kinds of matches. They've got so much emotion, so much intrigue, so much... blood." JG - "The dog collar should prove to be a good weapon. In what could be the most anticipated match of the night, Ken Curtis settles the score with J.W. Hardin." JH - "This is the most anticipated. Why isn't this the damn main event?" JG - "Things are strange in bookerland." JH - "Don't I know it. I get booked with you for everything." JG - "We've also got the weight of the cruiser division sitting in the balance of Derek Mota and Reverend Aristotle Jones. This one could play a big part in deciding where the cruiserweight limit is set." JH - "We don't want no chubs like Jones in there. In fact, let's just forget about the cruisers and get more brawlers." JG - "I don't see that happening. Gangsta Impasse and Stanlee Maxx settle the score in a brutal New York Street Fight. Weapons are allowed, and will become a big factor in this matchup. I can't wait to see these two finish their feud." JH - "It's been fun, but it's time for Maxx to move on." JG - "Plus, we have the TV title on the line, the Beyonders' futures hanging in the balance and Doug Adams fighting Nemesis! What a night!" JH - "That's great. We can just skip those matches and it's okay, though." JG - "But, right now, we go to the ring where Prodigy meets Green Knight." [FART] Oh, excellent. Anyways, coming down the aisle first from Talahassee, Florida, and weighing in at 200 pounds. He is a former TV Title holder, Green Knight!" [Hero comes over the PA as Green Knight comes out from behind the curtain with Squire Kathleen. He gets to the ring and chops down the ringpost. Security stops him as Knight goes into the ring. Ed offers him a cracker, but he merely pushes his hand aside.] EJ - "And his opponent, from Toronto, Ontario and weighing in at 276 pounds, accompanied down the aisle by Black Kat, he is Prodigy!" [Prodigy and Black Kat come to the ring to Bullet with Butterfly Wings. They casually walk down to the ring, not to fast or not to slowly. Of course Black Kat leads the way since she is the manager. They greet hands with the fans who love them. They get to the ring and walk up the steps. Prodigy holds the ropes open for Black Kat to enter the ring. Prodigy then climbs the ropes as Black Kat stands in the middle of the ring and the fans go nuts over them as the music fades away. Then Prodigy once again opens the ropes so Black Kat can step out then opens up his pony tail, takes off his sunglasses handing them to Black Kat.] JH - "Wasn't that eventful." DING! DING! DING! [The two lock up with Green Knight pushing Priodigy back in the corner. Green Knight gives him a clean break, giving him a nudge on his way back. The two lock up again but Prodigy pushes him back into the corner. Prodigy gives him a series of chops before being sepatated by the ref. Prodigy rakes the eyes of Green Knight and hits a shoulderbreaker. He picks the Knight up and throws him into the ropes and hits a dropkick.] JG - "Prodigy is really fired up tonight." JH - "Yea, and so is Black Kat. Meee-owww." [Prodigy runs into the ropes and hits an elbow. Green Knight gets to his feet and recieves a back suplex. Prodigy picks Green Knight up and puts him in a waist lock. He hits a German Suplex into a bridge but Green Knight kicks out right away. Green Knight pops up and recieves a DDT. Prodigy goes over to Knight and recieves a kick to the nuts.] JG - "Green Knight is getting vicious." JH - "It's about time." [Knight gets up to his feet and picks up Prodigy. He throws him into the ropes but telegraphs a back body drop. Prodigy gets him with a tiger suplex and goes to the middle rope. He hits an elbow and picks up Green Knight. He throws him into the ropes and hits a quick belly-to-belly suplex. He springs up and picks up Green Knight. He brings him over to the turnbuckle. He smashes his head into it and places him on the top....] JG - "Superplex!" JH - "Don't get all excited, it's an easily executed move, watch, I'll give ya one." JG - "That's alright." [Prodigy gets up slow and picks up Green Knight. He hits an inverted atomic drop. He runs behind the stunned Green Knight and puts him in an abdominal stretch. Knight reaches for the ropes, but can't quite get there. As the ref checks on Green Knight, Prodigy grabs onto the ropes for more leverage. He's caught by the ref and gets his arm kicked off of the ropes. Prodigy releases the hold and throws Green Knight into the ropes. He gets him in a sleeper as Green Knight grasps wildly for the ropes.] JG - "Prodigy is really wearing down his opponent." JH - "Yea, get 'em. He deserves it. Poke 'em in the eyes." JG - "Jack, stop watching The Three Stooges!" JH - "But this is sooooo boring. Once I see a trace of blood, I'll watch." [Green Knight picks up Prodigy so he's giving him a piggyback and rams back into the turnbuckle. Prodigy stays on him though. He tries it again, but Prodigy slides off forcing him to ram himself into the turnbuckle. Prodigy delivers shots to the ribs with a fury of punches, kicks and shoulders. Prodigy again places Green Knight on top but as he goes up himself, Green Knight pushes him off. Prodigy lands on his feet bent over and staggered. Green Knight goes for a sunset flip, but Prodigy stays up and sits on him. He begins delivering punches to the skull.] JG - "Prodigy is taking out his anger on the only one in punching distance. Even the ref is keeping his distance." JH - "Aw yea. Finally, something I can watch." JG - "Jack, stop watching the Spice Channel." JH - "What else can I do? If you watch these two for too long, you're gonna fall asleep." [Prodigy picks Green Knight up and delivers a snap suplex. He gets up and picks Green Knight up with him. He throws him into the ropes and swings at him for a clothesline. Green Knight ducks it, but on his way to the ropes, Black Kat grabs his foot. Prodigy picks up Green Knight and throws him outside. Black Kat walks over him with those high heels, puncturing him in the back. Prodigy goes outside the ring and throws Green Knight into the steps. Knight sends them flying into the guardrail, sending the guardrail back a bit. Prodigy walks over to Knight and picks up the stairs. He picks them up and hits Green Knight over the back with them. The ref comes out and forces Prodigy into the ring. He goes in and holds his arms up to a loud applause. Green Knight makes his way into the ring and gets up. Prodigy gets Green Knight into a front face lock, but Green Knight throws him into the ropes. Prodigy body blocks him to the mat and runs into the ropes. He jumps over Green Knight and as he's getting back up, hits a crucifix.] JG - "Prodigy is just dominating this match so far." JH - "Yea, and Green Knight is showing us all why he shouldn't have ever made it as Television Champion." JG - "Speaking of TV Title holders, Rock Bottom Robbie defends his TV Title against the two most worthy contenders, Pablo Morales and Silver Stone tonight, only on pay per view." JH - "I should be up there as a top contender. I mean I killed those midgets a couple months back." JG - "But you can only watch it if you call your local cable company, and order Rasslefest. Kids, beg your parents. Husbands, beg your wives." [Prodigy gets up and jumps to the top turnbuckle. He attempts a guillatine leg drop, but Knight moves.] JG - "Nobody home." [Green Knight picks Prodigy up and throws him into the turnbuckle. He hits a monkey flip, followed by an elbow. He picks Prodigy up again and hits a double underhook suplex. Prodigy is slow to get to his feet, but as he does, Green Knight hits him with a belly-to-back.] JG - "Here's the cover..." [1! 2! KICKOUT!] JH - "Come on, shove it up the cazzoo." JG - "Jack, please stop yelling directions to our wrestlers." JH - "I'm not, I'm yelling at Candy." JG - "Again with the spice?" JH - "Hey, tell your brother to cancel the satellite and I'll stop watching." JG - "Then we can't broadcast worldwide." JH - "Oh, I guess it stays then." [Green Knight picks up Prodigy and throws him into the ropes. Prodigy ducks a clothesline and on his way back hits a flying crossbody. Prodigy is thrown off of Green Knight as soon as he gets on and jumps to his feet. He bodyslams Green Knight, who is just getting up. Green Knight pops back up and recieves another bodyslam. He stays down this time and recieves a big splash. Prodigy picks up Green Knight and throws him into the turnbuckle. He hits a monkey flip, followed by an elbow. He picks Green Knight up again and hits a double underhook suplex. Green Knight is slow to get to his feet, but as he does, Prodigy hits him with a belly-to-back.] JG - "Didn't we just see that?" JH - "Yea, Prodigy's returning all his moves. Haven't you ever seen anyone do that before?" JG - "Well, no." [Prodigy goes into a corner and runs at Green Knight, hitting him with a knee to the side of the head. Prodigy gets to his feet and waits for Green Knight to get up. As he gets up, Prodigy hits a football tackle. Prodigy jumps off of him and picks him up. He gives Green Knight a Canadian backbreaker as Black Kat looks on in pleasure.] JG - "Prodigy's showing a patriotic side." JH - "Yea, but he's a yank." JG - "No he's not." JH - "Jeez, don't people bring their own countries flags down to the ring anymore?" JG - "Has that ever happened?" JH - "No, but...hey, at least I'm coming up with ideas here." JG - "Of what?" [Prodigy drops Green Knight and throws him into the turnbuckle. He hits him with a shoulder to the gut and lets him fall. Prodigy picks up Green Knight and hits a running Lygerbomb.] JG - "Looks like Prodigy's been working out at the SCRA training facilities." [Prodigy picks Green Knight up and headbutts him. He sets him up and hits a piledriver into a bodyslam. He jumps on Green Knight's back and puts him in a camel clutch. Prodigy gets off of him and hits a European uppercut, followed shortly by a chokeslam. He hits Last Ride in and goes for the pin... 1! 2! 3!] JG - "Prodigy wins." JH - "Was there ever any doubt?" JG - "With all the singles talk we had, we neglected our tag teams." JH - "We have tag teams?" JG - "Yes. The Activists from Hell place their gold on the line, literally." JH - "That's right, hanging above the ring where the Guard'll climb high and grab them." JG - "Then, we have the Pros settling the score with the German Eagles and Phi Theta Kappa attempting to get a measure of revenge against Angels of Darkness." JH - "Those are all real fun matches, but where are the Siberian Express? They should be main eventing the tag scene." JG - "They're newcomers, but are up next." JH - "So, we only get to see an abbreviated free for all matchup for them?" JG - "I guess so. Let's go to the ring intros." [Alexander Gudonov enters the ring, eyes focused straight ahead. He begins taunting the fans as Dragovich enters the arena, soon followed by Bolotov. The team marches their way to the ring, getting a loud chorus of boos from the fans on their way in. The team enters the ring, awaiting their opponents.] EJ - "And their opponents, at a total combined weight of 565 lbs., here come Blizzard and Icelander, Cold Winter!" [The Soviet National Anthem fills the arena as both members of Cold Winter enter the arena. Icelander enters with his light blue ring attire, receiving a loud chorus of boos. He ignores them, unlike his partner Blizzard. Blizzard enters the arena waving a USSR flag, mocking fans and waving the flag in their face. They hit the ring, looking at the Siberian Express almost in friendship.] JG - "I wonder how the two Russian teams will fare tonight. Will they work as a team or against each other?" JH - "Is it that hard against the Malaysian Stars?" [Jaspard exits the ring, unaware that there is still one more team to enter. The Malaysian Stars run into the ring, throwing punches at the two teams already in.] DING! DING! DING! [The Siberian Express easily takes out Eric Fox, leaving Devereaux for Cold Winter. The Express pound away on Fox, then set up for the Iron Curtain. They hit the move easily, almost simultaneously with Cold Winter hitting Snowflake. The two teams pin the Stars... 1! 2! 3!] JH - "Ha! They pinned both members of the Stars in no time at all!" JG - "Things aren't looking good for that team's future." [The Express and Cold Winter both push out the Stars. Bolotov remains in the ring as does Blizzard. The two shake hands and then lock up. Bolotov moves around Blizzard and hits a bodyslam. He quickly moves off the ropes and drops an elbow, clawing across the chest of Blizzard with his elbow. Bolotov lifts up Blizzard and whips him into the ropes, nailing him with a clothesline. He moves off the ropes and drops the leg across the throat. He covers... 1! KICKOUT!] JG - "Almost had 'im. This match is going to be a match of power. Or, at least when Blizzard and Andrei Bolotov are in." JH - "That's the way it should be, especially with the Russians. I love watching two Russians knock the crap out of each other." [Bolotov lifts Blizzard to his feet and hits a powerslam. He tosses Blizzard into the Cold Winter corner, taunting Blizzard to tag in Icelander. Blizzard does, weary of the attack already done on him. Icelander runs in, but is caught by the throat. Bolotov lifts him in the air and drops him with a chokeslam. Bolotov lifts Icelander to his feet, then hoists him over his head into a gorilla press.] JG - "Look at the power Bolotov possesses. This man is an animal." JH - "That's why they call him the Russian Bear. How many bears do we have in this league now. Three?" JG - "If you are counting Bill Baer as one, than yes, three." [Bolotov twists Icelander's arm, then tags in Dragovich. Dragovich goes to the top rope and comes down across the arm with a flying axehandle. He gets on the mat beside Icelander, applying an armbar. Icelander moves his way out of the armbar, sending Dragovich into the ropes and hitting a savate kick. Dragovich drops to the mat as Icelander keeps on the attack with a flying splash. He lifts Dragovich to his feet and tags in Blizzard. Blizzard lifts Dragovich in a piledriver position and Icelander spikes the move. Blizzard goes for the cover... 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Almost had him, there. Cold Winter is coming back firing!" JH - "Too bad we didn't have the Malaysian Stars in the ring. It would've been fun for them to get some more beatings." JG - "It's no surprise they were eliminated as early as they were. They may be young and promising, but they have no work ethic." [Blizzard lifts Dragovich to his feet and nails a powerbomb. He begins choking Dragovich on the mat, but the referee steps in quickly. Blizzard gets to his feet, carrying Dragovich with him shortly after. Dragovich nails a facerake to get back in control. He kicks Blizzard in the midsection then nails a swinging neckbreaker. Dragovich moves off the ropes and comes down hard with a kneedrop. He lifts Blizzard to his feet, nails an atomic drop, sends Blizzard into the ropes and hits a hiptoss. Dragovich applies an armbar. This is short lived as Dragovich lifts Blizzard to his feet, tagging in Bolotov. Dragovich sets up for a DDT, but holds off. Bolotov moves off the ropes and drives down on the back with a legdrop, dropping Blizzard.] JG - "What a double team move!" JH - "The creativity of Alexander Gudonov is already showing. I've heard about this guy, and I think I'm gonna like 'im." JG - "You think you're going to like pretty much every newcomer we have in the league. It doesn't always turn out that way, though." JH - "Yeah, some of the wrestlers and I have had our troubles, but I'm still the most popular guy in the dressing room." [Bolotov gets to his feet, lifting up Blizzard as well. He hoists BLizzard onto his shoulders, executing a powerslam. Bolotov drops the elbow on Blizzard, following with a matted full nelson.] JH - "That move has gotta hurt. With all the force Bolotov has, I don't know if Blizzard can withstand the force." JG - "Even if he can, Blizzard will be seriously impaired as his large power base will be taken away." [Bolotov converts the move into a bow and arrow, driving his knee into the small of the back. Blizzard rolls over to his side and is able to grab hold of the bottom rope. Bolotov releases the hold, but lifts Blizzard to his feet. He kicks Blizzard in the midsection, then continues with a series of hard punches to the head. As Blizzard backs into the turnbuckle, Bolotov sends him running across the ring, following in with a lariat. Blizzard begins to fall back, but Bolotov lifts him into a fireman's carry. Bolotov drops Blizzard to the mat, then drags him to the Express' corner. He tags in Dragovich who climbs to the second rope. Bolotov hits a double underhook backbreaker and as Blizzard lies across the knee of Bolotov, Dragovich takes off and drops the elbow.] JG - "Memories of past moves rushing back." JH - "Hey, with all the creativity they have, this is wrestling. If something is done, you copy it." JG - "That is extremely true. Once something happens in one league, it seems like others follow." [Dragovich rolls Blizzard off Bolotov's knee, then continues to work. He lifts up Blizzard and attempts a kick to the midsection, but Blizzard catches it. Dragovich quickly nails an enzuigiri, knocking Blizzard down on the mat. Dragovich applies a grapevine.] JH - "This move is so musical. Wanna sing, Grey?" JG - "That's quite alright, Jack. Let's concentrate on calling the match." JH - "Just a little music. (singing) I heard it through the grapevine!" JG - "That's good. Maybe you should get a record deal." JH - "You think I could make it?" JG - "No, but keep trying." [Dragovich releases the hold and moves into the ropes, nailing a kneedrop. He quickly applies a chinlock. Blizzard is able to get out of the hold, elbowing Dragovich in the chest, then sending him into the ropes. Blizzard locks on a sleeper.] JG - "Ivan Dragovich could be in trouble." JH - "This match is putting me to sleep, too. Get some brawling, damnit!" [Dragovich moves his way around, then drops to the mat, hitting a jawbreaker on Blizzard. He quickly lifts up his fellow Russian and nails a gutwrench suplex. Dragovich moves off the ropes and hits a flying headbutt to the chest. He attempts a cover... 1! KICKOUT! Dragovich picks up Blizzard and tags in his partner. Together, the Siberian Express sends Blizzard into the ropes, each hitting a kick to the midsection. As Blizzard doubles over, Bolotov sets up and hits a powerbomb with Dragovich dropping a leg across the throat for extra force.] JG - "What a move! This Siberian Express is really working well as a team." JH - "I wonder why. Maybe because they're champs in other leagues? Maybe because Alexander Gudonov can compete with the best manager in SCRA, Marty MacDonald? Maybe because..." JG - "Okay, Jack. Let's just watch them wrestle." [Bolotov picks up Blizzard, put is caught in a small package! 1! KICKOUT! Bolotov leaps to his feet, but Blizzard hits a kick to the midsection. Bolotov bends over only to be met by an axehandle to the back. Blizzard then runs into the ropes and hits a knee lift. Bolotov wavers, but doesn't fall. Blizzard sets up for a powerbomb, but can't get Bolotov up. Bolotov hits a back body drop. Blizzard gets up quickly, but is met by a series of punches to the face by the Russian Bear. Bolotov sends Blizzard into the ropes and hits a spinning sidewalk slam. Bolotov lifts up Blizzard, but is caught in a small package! 1! KICKOUT!] JG - "Almost had him, again!" JH - "Cold Winter is barely making it through this match. Their only high points have been a few small packages." [Both men spring to their feet, but Bolotov comes up with a Thesz press, knocking Blizzard down. He picks up Blizzard and drops him with a DDT. Bolotov tags in his partner, then grabs Blizzard in a bear hug. Dragovich runs off the ropes and...] JG - "Iron Curtain!" JH - "It's over. We gonna start the real show now?" [Dragovich covers... 1! 2! 3!] JG - "And, the Siberian Express come up with a big win. They seem to be moving up in the tag team ranks." JH - "It shouldn't be too tough with our amazing tag teams." JG - "Things are looking up for the tag division, Jack. We are just one or two big signings away from a strong division. That and a bit more consistency out of a few teams." JH - "Right, wink wink, nudge nudge." JG - "Folks, we are out of time for the Free for All. Make sure you buy the pay per view coming up next. If you already have it...What? I'm getting word backstage that J.W. Hardin wants to say one final word to Ken Curtis before their match." JH - "Nothing like a Hardin quote to finish the show." JG - "Let's go backstage. After that, we'll see you at the pay per view!" [The camera focuses on a door on which is stuck a piece of white tape with "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin written on it. The door suddenly swings open and the massive cowboy fills the doorway. He wears black jeans and surprisingly is not wearing his hat.] JWH - "The talkin' is over, Curtis. If ya ain't tucked yer tail and skedaddled by now, I reckon it just proves that yer as stupid as ever'one says. They's a mighty fine line 'tween bravado and stupidity... and ya crossed it tonight. Lotta men been lookin' fer the Outlaw, Curtis. You made the mistake o' findin' me.
