SCRA: Can you compete?

4 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
That's right: Can you compete with SCRA? Think you can join the likes of "Cowboy" Ken Curtis, "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin, Gangsta Impasse and "Silverblade" Anthony Logan in catching up to the behemoth World champ, Eddy "Flap" Jacks? Think you can catch up to speedy, new North American champion, Nathan Flash? Want to etch your name in history by joining Derek Mota and "Golden King" Aladdin Style in a quest to become the first ever SCRA cruiserweight champion (weight limit 230 lbs.) Or, want to join the ranks of the new Russian blood, Siberian Express and Cold Winter, who are trying to capture the tag gold from the Praetorian Guard? If you think you can go with these wrestlers, SCRA is for you. Our big event, Rasslefest, just finished and it proved to be worth all the hype. The event itself, the roleplays leading to the event and the two preview shows for it have been posted here to give you a taste of SCRA. Read them over and see what we're all about. Interested? We've got slots open for both singles, especially cruiserweights, and tag teams. Check out the webpage at http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html or email myself at [detail withheld] for more information. Hope to see you soon! -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
