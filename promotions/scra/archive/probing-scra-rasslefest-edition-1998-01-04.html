JG - "Oy, it's going to be a fun day..." KS - "Try getting stuck with Ted Jaspard every Thursday." JH - "We've got the Jaspard blues, too. We have to listen to Ed every Monday. Then, I get stuck with Grey not once, not twice, but yes, three times a week!" RB - "You guys don't want to know the nerds that work on the Scoops." JG - "We've all got our problems, but that's not what we're here for. Tonight, SCRA's brightest minds..." JH - "And Spencer." JG - "Offer their unique insight to the night that will become on January 4! Let's start with the Free for All!" KS - "Was that an insult?" ++========================++ Prodigy vs. Green Knight ++========================++ JG - "Two singles wrestlers on opposite ends collide to kick of the major event. Any opinions?" JH - "I have an opinion. I think this match will suck." RB - "I second that." JG - "What?" RB - "Green Knight's a loser. Prodigy's a loser, but not as bad. I guess Prodigy'll crush this fading star." JH - "Agreed. Hey, want to kick Grey out and join me on Monday nights?" RB - "I wouldn't sit with you for two hours if they paid me a million bucks." JH - "That's it. You've gotten on my nerves for long enough! You're on the Jackass list!" RB - "Try making the Scoops list, then call me." JG - "Okay, Keith, any predictions?" KS - "Green Knight has been in the league for much longer than Prodigy, thus giving him more experience. So, I'd have to go with Green Knight on this one." JG - "I'm going to have to go with Prodigy." JH - "Spencer's the odd ball!" [Suddenly, Ted Jaspard storms into the studio. He dives across the desk, grabbing Hazard by the throat.] TJ - "Yousssss don't make fun of my tag tssssseam partsssnersssss!" JH - "Get this bum out of here!" TJ - "I'll leave on my own." [Jaspard leaves quietly.] JG - "As I was saying, Green Knight hasn't been close to his top form a few months back. It looks like this may be the final depression for the noble knight." JH - "No long preview crap?" JG - "Not for this match." JH - "Good. Let's move on." ++====================================================++ Cold Winter vs. Siberian Express vs. Malaysian Stars ++====================================================++ JG - "Still in the Free for All, the three newest tag teams attempt to make their mark on the tag team circuit. Any predictions here, guys?" KS - "I have one. The Siberian Express and Cold Winter have proven themselves in other leagues. But, the Malaysian Stars have yet to prove anything in SCRA. So, it's pretty much a fight to the finish between Cold Winter and the Siberian Express." JH - "Geez, you narrowed the field from three to two. How do you do it?" KS - "What?" JH - "You're supposed to pick a winner, not the two finalists." KS - "Fine. I pick whoever you pick." JH - "You can't do that! That's against the rules!" JG - "Guys, calm down. Raf?" RB - "Are the Malaysian Stars even a tag team? It's a toss-up between Cold Winter and Siberian Express, but C comes before S so I'm gonna say Cold Winter triumphs in this one." JG - "Quite the picking system you have there. It's no wonder the Scoops provides us with such accurate accounts of league rankings." RB - "Hey, take that back. I'll get Bateman out here to kill ya! The guy was a football player. Me and him go way back..." JH - "That's great. My prediction: squash! The two big Russians will beat the Stars in under 30 seconds then light Cold Winter up in another few minutes. They have to, we don't have much time on the Free for All." JG - "I see. So, we've got one for Cold Winter and one for Siberian Express. Keith, care to finally make your pick?" KS - "I don't know. All I know it's going to be a good battle." JH - "Pick the damn winner!" KS - "Fine. I pick Cold Winter because it's the opposite of your pick." JG - "I guess I'll take the Siberian Express. They seem to match up well with Cold Winter, yet are slightly larger. That could prove to be the deciding factor." KS - "See, the decision's split! It's gonna be a close match!" ++================================++ Doug "Grizzly" Adams vs. Nemesis ++================================++ JG - "The teacher against the student. This one shouldn't be too tough to call..." JH - "Adams, as much as I hate it." KS - "Adams easily." RB - "I'm not getting paid enough to make moronic picks. If Adams loses this, kick him out of the fed." JH - "How much do you Scoops'ers get paid?" RB - "Helluva lot more than you, Hazard. We don't have to spend our on cosmetic crap or speech therapy. It's all in the print for us." JH - "Yeah, well, who's funnier?" JG - "That's deba..." JH - "Don't say that! I'll prove it. If Adams loses, let's rehire Taskan and stick him in a room with Adams! That'll teach 'im." JG - "With all these walkover predictions, I'm going to take Adams as well." ++======================================++ Phi Theta Kappa vs. Angels of Darkness ++======================================++ JG - "These two tag teams will be locking up to settle the score. The party-going Phi Theta Kappers want revenge for the brutal burn a while back." JH - "Too bad they won't be gettin' any, in the ring or in the bedroom." JG - "How do you manage to get sex into every show?" RB - "On the Scoops, we can't offend the readers. We have to keep the magazine clean." KS - "Powerhouse airs too early for the "hardcore" language." JH - "Isn't that your tough sh..." JG - "No predictions, yet? I guess I'll start us off. I think Phi Theta Kappa will get this one. The team is looking for revenge and that should carry them over an otherwise fierce team. Phi Theta Kappa has been rising slowly since their entrance in the league. Despite a minor setback at the hands of Doug "Grizzly" Adams, they have maintained a winning attitude. The team of Allen Jenkins and Brad Thompson brings both strength and techincal aspects. The Angels of Darkness are quite the opposite. Both members of that team rely on strength and brawling. This has caused many problems for the young team. They seem to be guided by the Creator, who may be dragging them into a bad situation with Phi Theta Kappa." JH - "The Angels of Darkness are not fierce. I'm hoping both teams collapse on the mat, forcing a no contest. In fact, that's my prediction. Two minutes, 39 seconds into the mat, both teams collapse and can't continue." KS - "Since I've been a mamber of the SCRA family, I haven't seen the Angels of Darkness get too many wins. I think they're due for a victory. Count one on the Angels of Darkness victory train. Toot toot!" RB - "Angels of Darkness, we're on to you...wear your scary make-up and do your magic tricks. Just don't expect to win this one." JH - "What's the count there, Grey?" JG - "Two for the Kappers, one for the Angels and..." JH - "One for neither! Whoo!" KS - "Wait! If I had to pick a winner, you have to pick one!" JH - "You're just some guest, you don't make the rules." [Bullock stands up, revealing a massive frame.] RB - "But, we all abide by them." JH - "Yeah, let's just move on." ++==========================++ The Pros vs. German Eagles ++==========================++ JG - "These two have been going at it for almost three months. It should be a real brawl when they finally settle the score. Both these teams have had success in the league, yet not as much as either would like. That could perhaps be because of each other. The Pros tasted tag gold for 6 short days in October, losing it because of the German Eagles. They left the scene soon after. The German Eagles seemed to be on track to getting some gold when the Pros returned, under a new configuration. Both teams have blocked each other's chances for glory. The aerial German Eagles should match up well with the smart, technical Pros. This match could really go either way." KS - "I'm gonna have to go with the Eagles on this one. I mean Mr. Grant just hasn't been the same with Walt Jabasco. Plus, the Eagles can ambush the large Jabasco with their quickness and agility." RB - "The Eagles'll control the flow of this match, but will find a unique way to lose." JH - "I hate both teams, yet I'm going with the Pros. Anything German can't be good." JG - "I'm going to have to take the German Eagles. They have been progressing throughout the past weeks and seem ready to emerge as a new tag star." JH - "Emerge? The only thing emerging from that team is Miss Frank's chest." KS - "Miss Frank? I prefer Ocki." JH - "Yikes. Maybe you and Grey should talk after the show." JG - "Raf, any additional comments? You've been sort of quiet." RB - "I'm a man of few words. But, when I speak, people listen." JH - "Think you could teach that to Grey?" ++=======================++ Phantom vs. Sgt. Pepper ++=======================++ JG - "Both these men tasted gold yet have not been close to the same level since then. Phantom's quick thinking took the league by storm, but now it seems that everybody has adjusted. Sgt. Pepper, however, hasn't been close to the same form he was back in September, when he won the TV title. I really don't know where to call this one. It's two great technical wrestlers..." JH - "Both on the downside of their careers. I'll go with Sgt. Pepper because he's got more letters in his name. How's that picking system, Raf?" RB - "Pretty good, Jack. I don't need a system for this thriller. I hope Sgt. Pepper hammers that little nuisance Phantom into the ground." KS - "Ever since the Phantom lost the North American Title, he's kinda disappeared from the scene. Sgt. Pepper, however, has also disappeared from contention since losing the TV Title. So, since the North American Title is higher than the TV Title, I'm gonna go with The Phantom." JG - "I think I'm going to have to go with Phantom. His intelligence may prove to be too much for the fun loving Pepper." ++==================================++ Lifetaker vs. Centris - Cage Match ++==================================++ JG - "Two stars..." JH - "Again, past their prime." JG - "Face off in what will probably be their last battle. I'm hearing reports that Lifetaker will be gone after the match due to injuries sustained during his tenure as champion. Centris may also be taking a leave of absence." KS - "Well, in my opinion, these two are never weres. The only reason Lifetaker was champion was because NCW was filled with no talent. The cage is just a stipulation so the fans don't get snored out the door. These fans are hungry for blood, so that's what President Grey gave him. Anyways, Centris is wierd so look for him to find some wacky way to defeat the fading Lifetaker." JH - "I agree with ya, Spencer. Lifetaker was champ in a weak league. Centris hasn't proven a thing, just a string of fluke victories. It's about time both these guys beat the crap out of each other so both men leave. I'm glad it's a cage match. At least we'll get some value out of two low quality competitors." RB - "And it's in a cage? Oh my. Both of these guys are quitting...my kind of career match. If only we could get Phantom in one of these...Centris'll win this if he has any of his old "magic" left." JG - "I'm going to have to say Centris as well. Lifetaker's small size has proven to be way too little for the growing league. He hasn't been able to use his technical style against the league's top notch competitors. Centris has some size and strength which he should use to overpower Lifetaker in the cage. Had this match not been in a cage, Lifetaker might win." JH - "Lifetaker win? I don't think that will ever happen." RB - "Hazard make an intelligent joke? I don't think that will ever happen." JH - "Very funny, Raf. Let's just see how good Scoops' sales are doing." ++=====================================================++ Rock Bottom Robbie vs. Pablo Morales vs. Silver Stone ++=====================================================++ JG - "The TV title is on the line as these three men fight it out elimination style. Robbie has proven to be a tough competitor in the past. He has held his own with every competitor to step in a SCRA ring. His battle scars prove that. Silver Stone has also been a good competitor in the past, but seems to be fading. He isn't the fighter he once was. I look for Robbie to exploit that and use his brawling skills to eliminate Silver Stone. That leaves the former champ, Pablo Morales. Morales scored a surprising win over Green Knight to take the title, many thought he didn't deserve. It should be interesting to see how Morales' aerial skills matchup with Robbie's brawling skills." JH - "Robbie can't brawl, damnit! The guy's all hype!" JG - "He has proven otherwise in the past. I'm looking for Rock Bottom Robbie to retain his title based on his experience in big league matches." KS - "I'm gonna have to go with Robbie, too. I mean the guy's been in the title picture since his time in the NCW. He's a two time champion and look for him to keep the title against the crazy Mexican and the jackass from Nevada." RB - "RBR has beaten these guys time and time again in what has to be the league's longest-running "TV title" feud. I really don't care who wins, but there's no way Robbie jobs this one away." JH - "Looks like I'm last to pick. I'm going with Silver Stone. He needs a win to jumpstart his career again. This one could be the victory. Gold always looked good on the silver one, even though he can't compete for his North American title anymore." KS - "But, silver comes second to gold. Look for that to be the case once again." ++========================================================++ Gangsta Impasse vs. Stanlee Maxx - New York Street Fight ++========================================================++ JG - "The feud finally culminates in a big way. Gangsta takes his street tough skills into the ring against Stanlee Maxx. Both these men have had success in the league, with Gangsta having a bit more. His North American title days are only shortly behind him. I think they still linger in his mind. That could prove to be costly for Stanlee Maxx who has had his shots, but never capitalized. Gangsta is looking for some revenge, big time, and may get it in a big way. His brawling, street tough tactics are perfectly suited for this match. Stanlee Maxx has size, but he doesn't use it effectively. If Maxx can use some of his strength, he may put up a fight. Otherwise, look for Gangsta to take the win." JH - "No way, Grey. Maxx wants to crush Gangsta and his manager and get back into the title picture. He has to prove he can compete with the new breed of wrestlers. This one could be a big match for the New Yorker. The match fits his style, too. A New York Street Fight? Maxx is from New York. Look for him to get a win." RB - "Stanlee Maxx has experience on his side. That said, it's going to be all the more sweet to watch Gangsta slam his oversized head into a trash can." KS - "Exactly. Gangsta Impasse all the way. The guy's a man on a mission. Stanlee Maxx has cost him his North American Title and Gangsta is looking for revenge. He can brawl with the best of them and this is his kind of fight." JG - "Looks like once again, Jack is left out." JH - "You guys don't know what you're talking about. There's no way Maxx is going to drop this one." ++=======================================++ Derek Mota vs. Reverend Aristotle Jones ++=======================================++ JG - "These two men battle to decide if Jones can actually compete with Mota. I'm hearing that this matchup will have a big effect on the weight limit of the cruiserweight division, if we ever get enough cruiserweights. Mota burst onto the scene preaching how Jones could not compete with him. The combination of Mota's speed and aerial abilities could prove to be too much for the Reverend. That's where this whole thing started, with Mota saying Jones was not a cruiserweight. Many agree as Jones' mat style are not typical cruiserweight styles. If Jones can use his mat style to wear down Mota, he may have the match. If Mota can stay moving, he's got the win." KS - "Mota's quick, but Jones has heart. Mota has stanima, but Jones keeps his opponent on the mat. If Jones is on his game, look for him to pull off the victory, but if Mota's quickness is too much for him, look for the tough little Canadian to pull of the victory." JH - "All you did was give two conditions! That's not picking a winner." JG - "Fine, I'll take Mota." KS - "I'll take Jones." JH - "If a hot dog vendor throws a hot dog into the ring, look for Jones to get the win. If not, it's all Mota." RB - "Do you ever not contradict yourself?" JH - "Very rarely. Let's hear your pick, oh smart one." RB - "The league's most self-righteous grappler meets its most self-effacing one. Jones has the size to win this one...and will." JH - "Jones? What kind of pick is that? There's no way pigs can fly and there's no way Jones will get the win." JG - "Pigs as in hot dogs?" JH - "Exactly, Grey. I think our viewers are smart enough to get my jokes without you explaining them." KS - "You never know. Just look at the ratings Powerhouse pulls in with Jaspard at the helms." ++=======================================================================++ "Cowboy" Ken Curtis vs. "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin - Tommy Gregory is referee ++=======================================================================++ JG - "The two cowboys square off in what should settle the score. Hardin burst on the scene, immediately gunning for Curtis. He succeeded in getting Curtis riled up. But, Curtis continues his rise through the rankings. Hardin seems content with bugging Curtis, although he as well is moving up. This could be a match which features a future champ. But, this one will be decided by who brawls better. Both men rely on that tactic primarily. Hardin should have the advantage because of his size. This is where Curtis' technical skills come in handy. Curtis has to tire the big Hardin in order to have any chance of winning this match." RB - "I might take time out of my busy schedule to watch this match...if only to see the two cowboys knock the crap out of that loser Gregory. No matter who wins, the match will be a success if Gregory gets hurt." JH - "Gregory better get beat or this match is a flop. But, who do you pick?" RB - "Hardin based on pure experience." KS - "Upon his arrival in the SCRA, J.W. Hardin hasn't caused very many waves. Sure, a victory here, a victory there, but he is not in title contention just yet. Curtis, however, is already gunning for the World Title and has dominated the league. Look for Curtis to pull off the upset on the legend." JH - "Hasn't caused many waves? He crushed Pepper, Jones and a slew of jobbers, got in the face of Gregory and made Ken Curtis' life hell. Hasn't caused waves? I think not. I'm looking for Hardin in a tough battle." JG - "I've got to go with Curtis on this one. Based on his technical skills alone, Curtis has the edge. Those aspects will carry him over the top." JH - "When have technical skills done anything?" RB - "Bruising tactics are always best, but technical skills are a close second." JH - "Bullock, you're really starting to annoy me." ++=======================================================================++ "Silverblade" Anthony Logan vs "Golden King" Aladdin Style - Dog Collar ++=======================================================================++ JG - "This match is perhaps the match most filled with emotion. It's brother against brother in this one. Logan wants to eliminate Style for good while Style wants to make his comeback. I don't know if either will come true, but this will be one great match. Had Style still be an aerial wrestler, he would be in trouble. But, because Anthony Logan destroyed Style's knee forcing Style to become more mat oriented, it could be a close match. Logan's martial arts skills could come in handy with a giant chain in front of him. Style may have trouble getting Logan to the mat, too. I look for Logan to pull of the victory." KS - "Total annihilation by the up and coming Anthony Logan. I mean Aladdin Style was a high flyer and still has some of that juice in him. A high flyer won't fare very well in a dog collar match, so look for Anthony Logan to continue his reign over the House of Lords." RB - "From what I've heard, a dog collar match is right up these guys' alley. Logan has making a fool out of Style for some time. Style wants revenge, and he's going to get it." JH - "I don't know about that. Style has been getting soft, even enjoying the fans' chants. I think Logan's going to pull out the win." RB - "Who really cares about people liking the fans, good guys and bad guys? It's all how you wrestle." JH - "Of course, you would say that." RB - "What?" JG - "Stop your bickering. You two sound like you're married." JH - "Yet, you and Spencer aren't having petty arguements. Do you save that for home?" KS - "I can't believe you put up with this." JG - "That's why I'm always so stressed when I see you at night..." JH - "See!" JG - "...for the employee bowling league. Keith and I are members." JH - "That must be fun." ++=======================================================++ Activists from Hell vs. Praetorian Guard - Ladder Match ++=======================================================++ JG - "This could be the match of the night. Ladder matches always prove to be great and with twice the number of people in it, this one could put it over the top. Add that to the hatred between these two teams and you have a winning match. It's really a toss up on who the favourite is, though. Aerialists are favoured in a ladder match usually, so that gives the Activists the advantage as they possess arguably the best flyer in SCRA, Zak Rage. You also have to consider power to be an advantage because with steel ladders, you never know what could happen. That gives Bill Baer a distinct advantage. I guess the result lies on which other member will perform best, Joshua Baker or Tulkas Naugrim. This is not new for these two as they usually provide the backup offense for their partners. We'll have to wait and see how this one filters out." KS - "I have a bad feeling about this match. The Guard have tasted gold and it tasted good. The Activists have tasted gold, and haven't liked it, so they dished it off to UNICEF. The Activists don't look too hungry for SCRA gold, so look for the Guard to upset the budding Activists." RB - "I like the Praetorian Guard...they're my favorite kind of team-- silent and feisty. The Activists can talk, but they can't dance. Bear in mind, dancing is a lot like wrestling. Guard wins a quick one." JH - "I'm staying on the bandwagon and going with the Guard. Nothing can combat MacDonald's strategy." JG - "I guess I'm the oddball by going with the champs. Who'd ever thought the champions would be the underdog?" JH - "Me." KS - "Me." RB - "Me." JG - "Okay, I guess that answers my question. I don't think anything can combat the Activists need for the gold. So many organizations are counting on the Activists to win and deliver the prizes which come with winning. I think they will deliver." JH - "The Activists won't deliver squat. Perhaps a piece of silver taken from the ladder stuck in Rage's head, but that's all." RB - "How would a piece of ladder get stuck in Rage's head?" JH - "Shutup, Bullock." RB - "Good comeback. It's no wonder you do so well in the ratings." JH - "Grey, let's keep going. I can't wait to get out of this place." ++===============================++ Johnny Destiny vs. Nathan Flash ++===============================++ JG - "Another match filled with emotion. Nathan Flash stood by Destiny's side for so long, then turned on him so fast. I'm sure Destiny will have some tricks up his sleeve for Flash, as Flash has had tricks for Destiny in the past. Even if both men walk into the ring with no tricks involved, this will be a spectacular match. Both men have proven to be great aerial wrestlers, combining that with speed and technical skills. These two know each other well, so it should be great." JH - "Flash will take it in under 10 minutes. Destiny's been out of the ring for a while and that will cost him. Flash's gonna exploit every ounce of ring rust and crush Destiny." KS - "I like these two guys. I mean they both have a lot of character. Destiny has one thing on his mind...revenge. But, I think I'm gonna have to side with Nathan Flash on this one. I mean Johnny Destiny has been out of the wrestling ring for about three months and his ring rust might show through. Nathan Flash, however, has been winning match after match and tearing up the circut. Look for Flash to beat the Crazy Canuck." RB - "What? Is there any way the league's prodigal son can lose this one? This match has been booked so cleverly that Destiny could get dominated for 50 minutes, then win. No way around it...Destiny's my pick." JG - "I'm going with Destiny, too. There's something about him that seems to come through when matches are big. He always puts up the big performances. I don't think this will be any different." JH - "Split, again. How many mathces have we called 2 and 2?" JG - "I don't know, 7?" KS - "We'll have to check the tape tonight..." JH - "Aha!" KS - "After we finish bowling." JH - "Saying you were bowling doesn't disprove my theory. You could retire to the same house." JG - "Thanks, Keith." ++=============================++ Eddy "Flap" Jacks vs. Lucifer ++=============================++ JG - "These two have had the longest..." JH - "And drawn out." JG - "...feud in the league. It seems like they have been partners, then separated forever. The two big men will collide and I don't think one of them will get up. If Lucifer loses this match, I've been hearing he won't return. If he wins, Jacks will probably be crushed. The two big brawlers always seem to give their opponents the worst beatings, so this will be no different. But, Lucifer has been out of action for months. I look for Jacks to take this one with relative ease. He has been staying in shape, sort of, and is much better prepared for the match." KS - "Eddy's had the title for long enough. This is Lucifer's second shot at the world title and he wants it bad. Look for Lucifer to become the new World Champion." RB - "Is Lucifer even alive?" JH - "No, hence the name Lucifer. Finally gotcha, Bullock." RB - "That's great, Jack, but I could care less. I'm just here to call the matches. This feud died like 200 years ago...and ain't comin' back. One of my colleagues, Jimmy Slagle, reports that this is going to be Lucifer's last match. It'll be nice to see him go out with a loss." JG - "Well..." JH - "Actually, that's sort of smart. Win Fatal Fall, take a few months off and come back with a title shot. Smart thinking." JG - "Folks, there you have it.
