JH - "And after, I'm gettin' laid!" JG - "What?" JH - "You know those New York prostitues. I met one last night and I'm getting it for free!" JG - "That's great, Jack. I'm sure the entire viewing audience is very happy for you." JH - "They damn well better be. I'm the reason they watch every day, so they probably do care." JG - "Jack, we've got business to attend to. The wrestlers were hyped at the upcoming show." JH - "At least some of them." JG - "Yeah, we still had a few who would rather keep their opinions to themselves." JH - "And we thank some of them for it. But, others, what were you thinking?" JG - "Anyways, we've got to get going. We'll start out with the Free for All!" ++========================++ Prodigy vs. Green Knight ++========================++ JG - "Two people on opposite ends of their careers. Prodigy has to continue his rise, yet, Green Knight is on a quick downfall." JH - "Will he be gone soon? Maybe we can add him to the series of wrestlers who can't cut it." JG - "I don't know. We'll find out after Rasslefest when the pink slips start flying." JH - "Grey, you gettin' one?" JG - "I doubt it." JH - "There truly is no Santa." JG - "Jack! To all our younger viewers, don't listen to him. Santa is alive and well in the North Pole, preparing for next Christmas. But, we've got some comments to get to. Here's Prodigy." [The camera opens in a forest somewhere in Africa. There is no one for miles except for Prodigy. He has his hair open and is wearing nothing but a pair of shorts. He has war paint under his eyes. You can see his muscles popping as beads of sweat race down his body. He spots the camera and stops in his tracks.] P - "I'm here deep in the heart of the African jungle. I have left everything, even Kat back in Canada....until Rasslefest. The reason for me coming here to train is simple. Sure, I may not have no shiny weights here, hell I don't even have watch, or a decent pair of clothes with me. But if you look at it through my perspective you would see that I'm training like never before, I'm becoming one with nature and am going to let it fuel me for my match at Rasslefest. Over here in the jungle, I have nothing to distract me, I am more focused & pumped at this point in my life then I have ever been before. No modern electronics to bother me, no fancy crap, it's just me and the Earth." [Pauses and looks around at the great scenery] P - "Now, a lot of you are probably thinking to yourself why have I done this and I could probably get the same kind of adrenilum pumping through me via other methods. Well, 1998 is gonna be the year of The Kat & Prodigy. It's like the circle of life, if you take a moment to think about it. Right now I'm at the top of the circle, I'm co-headlining the free-for-all for Rasslefest, just stating off, a newbie. By the end of 1998, I guarantee to go full circle and go from co-headlining the free-for-all to headlining the MAIN EVENT. And if anybody thinks this is a bluff, go ahead try to play the hand dealt." [Sits down on a rock and begins, starting a fire as it is getting dark.] P - "I'll admit that 1997 has been a jobber-beating-that's-the-only-way- I-win-year like many people have said over and over again. But let's go through some of the stuff some people seem to have forgotten. I defeated Lifetaker, sure this was when he was on his downward spiral but how many people have the balls to even look eye to with that man?" [Pauses again, proving his point.] P - "I have also put up a great match against one of the best lightweights in this business, Derek Mota. And not to mention going toe to toe in a match with The Cowboy, getting up from the High Noon and many other times people had me out. I admit he gave me a beating but I kept on ticking which is a lot more than a lot of other people in this sport can say. Cowboy, show the Outlaw who the true Texan of the SCRA is at Rasslefest." [Stops to add some more wood to the fire and sits down.] P - "Back to my mission for '98. I know I'm going to have many obstacles to overcome but along with Kat and this new found strength of mine, my inner strength. I'm going to.....we're going to go through all those obstacles like an F1 car going through an equestrian circuit. Green Knight, your the first obstacle and the person who gets to see the new me in the ring. Knight, you can take your prehistoric crap and shove, cause if you don't I will this Sunday night at RASSLEFEST." [The camera begins to fade as he begins chanting "Kat, Prodigy, '98" repeatedly.] JG - "This match has to be thought of as none other than a stepping stone for the young Prodigy. He is still moving up..." JH - "And, he'll defeat yet another jobber by the name of Green Knight." JG - "We'll see what happens with that." ++====================================================++ Siberian Express vs. Cold Winter vs. Malaysian Stars ++====================================================++ JG - "The three newest tag teams attempt to make their mark in the league with this big match. We've got all three in the ring, with the winner getting immediately shot up through the rankings." JH - "Malaysian Stars are not gonna last two seconds, Grey. They're gonna be out of this match, then the league." JG - "Well, we'll see about that one. First, we had these comments from the Siberian Express." [Camera opens at night in front of what looks to be an abandoned warehouse, somewhere along the docks of Toronto's waterfront. No light is seen through any window, and no sign of life is seen whatsoever. The camera pans around to give a 360 view of the rest of the docks, where all other warehouses seem to show some form of human occupation. Again the camera focuses on the warehouse and remains focused on it until two voices are heard talking behind the camera. Then the camera shuts of and the two men begin to talk between themselves. they are the soundman and the camera man.] SOUNDMAN - "Hey Chuck, you might as well shut that thing off. You don't want to waste the battery. It's the only one we have, and if we should ever happen to find these guys, we want to make sure we have enough juice to cover their whole rant." CAMERAMAN - "I just shut it off, but I don't think it matters. I think we got screwed man. Whoever gave Hazard that tip about where these quys were holed up......he must'a been joking or something. There ain't nobody here." SOUNDMAN - "Well according to the brass, the tipster was REAL convincing about the location. Warehouse twenty three, dock three, west side. This is it, even if it's abandoned. Why would he lie about this." CAMERAMAN - "I don't know man, but we've been had. I think we should blow outta here man, I've got better things to do than stand here searching for some supposed "Super Team" that the SCRA just signed. I still haven't eaten...my old ladies mad that I ain't been spendin' enough time with the kid....Jeorpardy's gonna start in thirty minutes. I'm already mad that I had to miss Erkel!" SOUNDMAN - "Yeah...okay...let's get outta......." [Before being able to finish, the soundmans voice is interupted by a loud yell that seemed to come from somewhere inside the dark building. Immediately after, deafening silence again takes over.] CAMERAMAN - "What the hell was that!!" SOUNDMAN - "I don't know, but it came from in there (points at warehouse). That place isn't as empty as we thought!" CAMERAMAN - "This is weird! I think we better leave...and NOW!" SOUNDMAN - "No wait! I think that this is the place! I bet that that voice is connected to one of the people we're trying to find. I say we check it out." CAMERAMAN - "No way!!" SOUNDMAN - "Come on man! We have to. I mean if we don't and this is the place, then we'll get the third degree from the suits. But if we at least do a little poking around, then we at least cover our butts." CAMERAMAN - "Well.....Okay. But let's make it quick." [The two men move slowly to a small door at the front of the building. They try the knob, and upon finding it unlocked they move inside. They close the door and move down a long hallway. But instead of finding an empty blackness in front of them, a faint light can be seen at the other end. They move slowly to the end of the hallway where they are encountered with a partially open door, and on the other side of it stands the source of the light. Slowly the peek around the door to find the warehouse floor. All is empty except for a small makeshift wrestling ring. Around it stands ten or so candles which supply the faint light. Inside this ring stand two very large men. Their features are very hard to make out in this bad light, but it appears that one is bald and very large, while the other is slightly smaller and sporting some sort of crew cut. They stand beside each other staring out to another figure pacing back and forth in front of the ring.] CAMERAMAN - "What is this man?" SOUNDMAN - "I don't know. Run your camera!" CAMERAMAN - "I can't there ain't enough natural light, and I can hook up the flood light or we'll be seen. Run your tape recorder." SOUNDMAN - "Okay. Just a second." [The soundman quickly sets up his trusty tape machine. They won't be able to get a picture, but they'll at least be able to bring some sound to the brass at SCRA to prove they were in the warehouse. Both men hope that these three are the people they were sent to find.] PACING MAN - "Good good! That was a nice set of moves, but you're both a little slow on the take downs! I want you faster! You have a reputation to uphold. We have a neme to keep you know." BALD MAN - "Yes Alexander." PACING MAN - "Good. We don't want to make a bad first impression on all the sappy peons in the SCRA! We have to show them the force of our team. We have to show them why we've dominated every federation we've ever been in, are in, and ever will be in! We have to show them why we've held titles all around the world! We have to show them why we're in the best feds this sport has to offer, why we're beating the best teams around, and holding the most prestigious belts! We have to show them why we are so good!" CREW CUT - "Yes Alexander." PACING MAN - "We have to show the SCRA why we're the meanest, toughest team to grace this sport. They have to know that we're in the best shape we've ever been in, and that we're wrestling the best we've ever wrestled. They need to know that we take no prisoners and spare no weak!!!! To mess with us is like commiting suicide! To wrestle us is like fighting an army of juggernaughts, and to beat us is unthinkable!!" CREW CUT AND BALD MAN - "Yes Alexander." PACING MAN - "You've put more people out of this sport than I care to recall, and you aren't done yet. Federation after federation clammers for your presence in their leagues!! And team after team falls motionless at your feet!! You are one of the best teams around and now the SCRA will find out why!! Soon they will find out why you were rated as the second best tag team in wrestling..... why the only team "better" than you are The Prophets of Rage!" CREW CUT AND BALD MAN - "We will destroy!" PACING MAN - "Soon they will all see why you are called "THE SIBERIAN EXPRESS"!" [After hearing this tirade, the soundman shuts off the tape recorder and quickly pockets the tape from inside it.] SOUNDMAN - "Let's get out of here man!! I think we found em!" CAMERAMAN - "Yeah. And I think they've found the SCRA too!" [FADE] JG - "Certainly a big message portrayed by the Siberian Express. They are definitely going to make it tough for tag teams in this league." JH - "We're going to see which of the Russians is best. Siberian Express and Cold Winter match up size and tactics fairly well, yet we'll see which one can produce better." JG - "It comes down to the X factor." JH - "X-Factor? What the hell does that jobber have to do with this?" JG - "Not that kind of X factor. The unnamed factor: Alexander Gudonov. If he gets involved, this match may be over quickly." JH - "It'll be over quickly, anyways. We've only got a half an hour for the Free for All!" JG - "True. But, with Gudonov at ringside, the Express has a distinct advantage over their fellow Russians." ++================================++ Doug "Grizzly" Adams vs. Nemesis ++================================++ JG - "The mentor against the student." JH - "Do we really have to talk about this one? We know Adams is winning." JG - "Let's take this time to delve into the lives of myself and Jack. Jack, what did you do last night?" JH - "Shutup." JG - "OK, let's move on." ++======================================++ Phi Theta Kappa vs. Angels of Darkness ++======================================++ JH - "Please tell me these guys had comments!" JG - "Nope. Seen any good movies?" JH - "I hate talking to you, Grey." JG - "That's great. Answer one question and we can move on." JH - "Fine. I haven't seen any good movies." JG - "That's not the question." JH - "Who cares about the technicalities, we've got comments to get to!" ++==========================++ The Pros vs. German Eagles ++==========================++ JH - "Ah, I never thought I would welcome a Pros flash." JG - "I guess it's back to business. These two teams with a very storied past finally collide to settle the score. It should be a great matchup." JH - "And if not, at least this match saved me from talking to you." JG - "Jack, we're still talking." JH - "You know what I mean." JG - "Let's hear from the Pros." [The scene opens up in a gym. Two tag teams are in the ring, as the camera zooms in one team is shown to be The Pros. Walt Jabasco is in the ring against a large masked man. Grant is on the outside looking bored. Walt slingshots his opponent against the rope, leaps onto the second rope and comes back with a flying clothesline. He then pins the man. Walt then tags in Grant, then starts talking to the camera.] WJ - "Well well, here we are so close to Rasslefest. Behind me here Grant is practicing on some lesser competition." [In the background Grant executes a European Uppercut and then slams the opponent with a reverse DDT.] WJ - "Ouch! (Pauses for a second sorting his thoughts out) The German Eagles, a team that the Pros have a long past with, I may not have been wrestling when this feud started but I was there. I saw how Tavis's spirit was crushed when he saw Miss Frank with the Eagles. Because of her he lost intrest in his dream, he wanted, along with Grant, to be the best tag team in the world. They had a long way to go but The Eagles helped end this prematurely. That is part of the reason I'm here, to help Tavis save face by destroying the Eagles in the ring. And once the Eagles are out of the way the top is the limit for the Pros." [Grant walks over to the camera.] G - "I've been hearing people say the Pros aren't as good now. Or that I haven't been wrestling as good as I used to. You know what? I haven't, I've been saving myself for this fight. The Eagles are all I can think of, all other fights are second fiddle to this one. And once I get rid of the Eagles I'll be free of this great weight that's been pushing down on my chest. I will have brought respect back to the Pros and back to Tavis, wherever he is right now. And another thing Eagles, after this fight its over, I don't want to involve myself with you guys." WJ - "That's right, win or lose we don't want waste our time in a feud that goes nowhere. And after this fight the only time I want to face you in the ring is if its for the belts." G - "That's about all, see ya at Rasslefest. Eagles, be prepared for the match of your life, 'cause revenge can go a long way in a fight. Later." [The camera fades out.] JH - "Does that count as focussed for the Pros?" JG - "They don't take wrestling seriously, yet get the results." JH - "A model tag team, sort of. If only they bent the rules a bit." JG - "I don't see that happening with Walt Jabasco on the team." JH - "Tavis would've?" JG - "I don't know. I was talking about Jabasco's background in, well, can't talk about that." JH - "Great, let's move on." ++=======================++ Phantom vs. Sgt. Pepper ++=======================++ JG - "Sgt. Pepper attempts to free the league from the Beyonder, Phantom." JH - "Oh yeah, Phantom's gonna do real good without Centris. Actually, without Centris, he might actually be good." JG - "Centris has been rather lacklustre as of late. Phantom, though, hasn't been in top form. We'll see if this performance helps his ailing career." JH - "If anybody can turn around a career, it's Pepper. That guy has lost more matches to good wrestlers than anybody. Hell, I think he's lost more matches than anybody." JG - "I don't know who has that distinct honour. Here's what Phantom had to say." [The camera shows the outside of a castle. It is storming rather bad outside. It starts to pan up the side of the castle till it reaches a window at the top, where the only source of light comes from. The camera follows inside the window into a very victorian like bedroom. A huge clock sits in the room ticking a way. A beautiful looking bed is also in the room. Next to the bed is a very attractive young lady. She is dressed in a pure white dress. She paces back and forth across the room. She glances at the clock and notices that the time is 11:57 P.M. She rushes over to the telephone sitting on a little night stand by the table. She picks up the phone and dials.] C - "Come on...Come on....Answer!!" [She galnces back at the clock and sees that it is 11:58 P.M.] C - "NO!!! Not the answering machine. Hello....Sgt. Pepper...this is Christine you have got to listen to me the Phantom has something terrible planned for you..." [Suddenly the clock strikes 12:00. Lightning flashes outside the window. The funneral march can be heard in the background as the power goes off. Suddenly the door burst open and the Phantom stands.] P - "What do you think your doing? I thought you were on MY side." C - "Now Phantom....I am, but think about what you plan will do to him." [The Phantom breaks into a laughter] C - "This is NO laughing matter." P - "You are right." [With those words the Phantom grabs Christine and makes his way through the halls of the castles down to the basment, where most of his time is spent. He pushes Christine into an old broken down chair. He turns and lights a candle to shed some light into the very dark room.] P - "Sit and watch Christine. Notice how my intelligence works." [HE walks over to a t.v. and puts a tape into the vcr. This tape shows Sgt. pepper and some of his matches.] P - "Dang...Wrong tape. Sorry Christine that is the type that I have been watching to form my plan. I think I have found Sgt.'s most vulnerable weekness. But this is the tape that I wanted you as well as the rest of the wrestling world to see." [He puts another tape into the machine. This one shows all of his amazing wins, most being because of Christine. It shows how he embarrsed some of the most respected wrestlers.] C - "Notice how you win because of me." P - "SHUT-UP!!" C - "What will you do when I no longer help you. When your by yourself for this match." P - "Your starting to ANGER me CHRISTINE!! I have one more tape for you." [Once again the Phantom puts a tape into the VCR. This one is a special tape. On it it shows a Sgt. Pepper look a like... He is standing inside a ring. Blabbing his mouth off at how he is going to defeat the Phantom. Then The overture to the Phantom of the opera starts. A safe drops and hits the look like knocking him out cold. The last image before the camera blacks out is that of the safe opening and the Phantom sitting inside. Laughing a very evil laugh.] JH - "Phantom better keep Christine around! Without her, he's back on the Jackass list." JG - "You are liking Phantom, again?" JH - "I don't know. It's a toss up. Getting rid of Centris is a plus, but without Christine, he's back down." JG - "I see. I see. It'll all be on the line tomorrow night, as it seems Christine could go either way." JH - "Wait. Having Penelope Lane and Christine could prove to be a good combination. I've always been fond of that sort of stuff." JG - "Jack! Let's move on." -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
