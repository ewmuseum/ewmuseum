[SCRA] Rasslefest Preview [1/3]

4 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Clips of various matches from the past months are shown... The debut of the House of Pancakes... The Fatal Fall three ring royal rumbles... Eddy "Flap" Jacks defeating Lifetaker... Havoc 2 - Shower Wars, Food Fight, Football Brawl, Pep Rally... The Activists winning the tag team titles... "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin appearing on the scene...] VO - "It all comes to a head in 9 short days! Rasslefest is coming and only on pay per view!" [The camera goes into a studio where two men are seating. In the backdrop, a large poster bearing the words "Rasslefest: T - 9 days". As the studio lights up, Grey and Hazard can be seen flipping through pages of notes.] JG - "Hello fans. What a past couple of months it has been. So many eventful things have brought this league from mediocrity to respectability, from low budget to big budget, from boring to exciting. Yet, it will all come to a climax as Rasslefest is only 9 days away!" JH - "You know what, Grey? For once, I agree with you." JG - "That is sort of odd." JH - "And it will never happen again. We've both been involved with various wrestling aspects, but nothing has topped these past few months with SCRA." JG - "I've had a great time with you, too, Jack." JH - "That's great. Enough ass kissing, though. Let's get into the real reason for this show: recycling a bunch of clips to keep heat going while everybody takes a break." JG - "As usual, so gracefully put, Jack. We will be taking a look back at all the events from the past few months leading into the various matches signed for Rasslefest. Let's kick it all off with the Free For All matches!" !~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~! | Prodigy vs. Green Knight | |Siberian Express vs. Cold Winter vs. Malaysian Stars| !~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~! JG - "This match could be the most important in these two fine wrestlers' careers. Green Knight has not had the same success as he did in previous months. Prodigy, on the other hand, continues to impress with his hard work. This one key win at a major event could thrust him onto bigger things." JH - "Major event? It's the Free For All!" JG - "It is, but even the Free For All provides an important way for wrestlers to rack up ranking points." JH - "Gotta get everybody a match, right Grey?" JG - "I guess so, Jack. This match has little heat going in, but I'm sure these two wrestlers will put up a good show regardless. The other Free For All matchup involves three rising tag teams, all looking to make their mark in the league." JH - "The Express is looking good, Grey. Those big Russians have been eating the competition." JG - "This match is also a key point in each team's careers. A win could thrust them up in the rankings. Yet, none of the teams have had run ins with each other..." JH - "But, we have to get them matches." JG - "They will put on a great show on January 4." JH - "These matches have less heat than a jobber match!" JG - "Regardless, we have many more matches during the show, let's take a look!" !~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~! |Doug "Grizzly" Adams vs. Nemesis| !~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~! JG - "The student against the teacher. This one should be a great matchup filled with emotions. Although, I haven't seen Nemesis wrestle." JH - "If he trained Adams, he must be able to wrestle, right? Wrong. Adams can't wrestle." JG - "Let's go to the tape." [The camera fades out, into a video of the past events. A picture of the former "team" of Adams and Nemesis walk down to the ring. The voice over of Jon Grey begins.] VO - "Two former friends collide. The typical result in this dog eat dog world. Yet, this one has a different twist. Could Nemesis be considered a friend of Adams?" [Footage entitled: "December 8, Scraps" begins playing. Nemesis screams into the ring, forcing Grizzly to continue the attack on Phi Theta Kappa. Grizzly, reluctantly, continues to attack Thompson and Jenkins.] JH - "Ha! Look at him! He's humiliating Phi Theta Kappa!" JG - "This is absurd. Nemesis is forcing Grizzly to kill Phi Theta Kappa." JH - "Absurd? Let him go! Turn the Grizz loose!" [Grizzly finally has enough of the attack and lets Phi Theta Kappa scurry off to the locker room.] VO - "Pushed by a man driven by greed and power, Adams was left in a poor, poor position." [Footage entitled: "December 15, Scraps" begins playing. Nemesis grabs Grizzly by the ear, smacking him around on the way back to the ring. Phi Theta Kappa looks on in disgust.] JG - "Nemesis sure has a stranglehold over Grizzly." JH - "Because, without Nemesis, Grizz would be a nobody." [As Phi Theta Kappa look on, the voice over continues..] VO - "Yet, two fun loving characters tried to provide the saving grace Adams needed. With the overbearing Nemesis ruining the promising career of Adams, these two men had to step in." BT - "Yeah, but that's not what we're talking about today. Grizzly, we finally pinned you last week. We saw the post match confrontation between you and Nemesis and we have a proposal." AJ - "Join us." BT - "Come out here right now! We'll show you the way of partying and fun!" [Grizzly walks into the arena slowly. Nemesis rushes in after him, attempting to swerve his student. Grizzly, however, punches Nemesis!] JG - "He nailed his manager!" JH - "It's all down hill from here, Grey." [Grizzly leaves the arena.] VO - "The student must face the mentor. It will all be put on the line, humility, honour and justice." !~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~! |Phi Theta Kappa vs. Angels of Darkness| !~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~! JG - "Two young tag teams trying to make their mark in the increasingly tougher tag team division. This one could spark each teams careers and launch them into greater things." JH - "Spark? This feud is filled with sparks, Grey." JG - "True. It all happened with one spark..." [The video montage begins, first with footage labelled "December 1, Scraps". Just before the actual voices can be heard, Grey begins with his voice over comments.] VO - "It all began with one comment..." AJ - "The Angels of Darkness though are anything but Angels." JG - "What do you mean?" AJ - "They are way too weird." BT - "It is like they are on some sort of trip. Come on guys loosen up a little, let's party down!" AJ - "The Creator must have some sort of spell on them, if you believe in such a thing." BT - "I don't know. All I know is they either need to come chug a few with the PTK or get clugged a few by the PTK." VO - "Two angered men would not stand for the verbal abuse. They had to act. They had to act fast." ["We Will Rock You" and the shouts of Toga start back off, and Phi Theta Kappa make the way from ringside. Their vibrant exit, however, is cut short as the Angels of Darkness stand at the top of the aisle.] TC - "You want my children to lighten up? Children, show them what we do to "lighten up"." [A giant flame encompasses the front of the arena, catching a hold of Jenkins' toga. The Angels leave as the fire dies down. Security rushes into the arena, trying to put out the fire, which they do successfully.] VO - "Yet, partying seemed to leave the mind of Phi Theta Kappa. First, the well being of his partner, Allen Jenkins, struck Brad Thompson." [The hospital footage from December 4 begins playing.] BT - "Angels of Darkness, I don't know what you guys are for, what you care about, or what you think you are doing, but you have crossed the line. You guys do need to loosen up, it seems to me like you guys have been without lovin' for quite some time now, but hey we could have just let you be, or provided you with the means to loosen up. But you had to snap and set Allen on fire. Luckily he only came out with minor burns, but I doubt you would have cared even if it had been more serious. Allen has personally vowed to avenge this heinous act, and I plan on backing him up. Creator, we have worse plans for you though, just you wait and see." [As Thompson finishes his speech, the camera moves away from the face shot to the shot later on. Allen Jenkins' body: He has no hair left whatsoever on his body. He also has some 2nd degree burns lower on his body around his calves, where the fire originated.] VO - "But, the men were not finished yet." [As Jenkins' burns leave the screen, Carnage begins speaking. The caption appears below "December 7"] C - "Phi Theta watch and learn...learn what the underworld can do (Carnage holds out hand and fireball appears and he throws it to the ground...) Play with fire and you get burned..." [the Creator points staff again at Carnage and he stops talking and sits back down and Havoc rises to his feet] H - "Me and my Brother will not let you down father...We will do our best to hurt these men...We are only in this fed for a short time and we are already wreaking CARNAGE and HAVOC...All that cross our path will get ANNIHILATED...INFINITE SLUMBER will succumb you ALL" [After this last comment, a split screen of the two teams engulfes the screen, a line of fire dividing the two sides.] VO - "Phi Theta Kappa wants revenge. The Angels of Darkness want revenge. Only one team can get their revenge, though. Yet, both will fight like hell for it." !~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~! |The Pros vs. German Eagles| !~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~! JG - "Perhaps two tag teams have not hated each other longer than these two. Their feud has spanned across three months and two separate Pros configurations." JH - "Neither team providing any success." JG - "That's certainly debatable. The Pros were on top of the tag team scene for a brief period back in October, which caused this feud to begin." [The video montage begins with Fatal Fall shots from October 12. The German Eagles sprint to the ring. With the ref's back turned, both Eagles get in the ring, Joseph carrying a chair. Gore lets go of the hold. Joseph advances on Gore. He winds up for the chair shot, but Gore flings Tavis in front of him. The chair whacks Tavis, knocking him out.] JG - "Joseph Eagle just nailed Tavis with the chair!" JH - "Pin him!" JG - "The Eagles better get out of there before the ref turns around." [The Eagles flee the scene. Gore jumps on Tavis. Styles tells the ref to get back in the ring. The ref slides in and counts the one, two, three. Grant rolls under just after the three, trying to free his partner.] VO - "The German Eagles cost the Pros the belts. Plain and simple. The Pros wanted revenge. Not as simple. Due to various behind the scenes events dealing with Tavis, Miss Frank and Grant, the Pros soon left the SCRA scene. But, when they returned, the brutal truth came out..." [The clip from Havoc 2, the Pros return come on.] G - "I never wanted to leave, it was Tavis who dragged me away from here." JG - "Why did you leave?" G - "It was mainly Miss Franks fault. Tavis was in love with her, that's the only reason she was our manager. Tavis found out that she never loved him right after we won the tag titles, we were all hanging out in the locker room. The German Eagles were there along with some other wrestlers we had befriended. We were having a good time when all of a sudden the Eagles disappeared with Miss Frank. Tavis, who was drunk, started to worry about Miss Frank so he started searching. He found Miss Frank in the Eagles private room. From what I heard they weren't doing anything just hanging out, but Tavis went berserk and started to trash the room. The German Eagles then jumped Tavis and beat him up pretty bad. When I found this out I was furious, I didn't want Miss Frank as our manager, never did for that matter. Tavis was heartbroken he never came out of his room for training or anything. Because of him we lost a few matches and I got fed up. I told him to forget about that girl and try to get into the winning attitude he had. I said just beat the Eagles in the ring get all your anger out on them, but he didn't listen. After our last match he pulled me aside and we had a talk. He told me he wanted to go back to California and enjoy the scene down there. We parted ways after that, I haven't seen him or Miss Frank since that last match. VO - "Once again, backstage rumblings caused two teams to fight in the ring. Miss Frank is no longer the object. It is an object of respect for coworkers. The German Eagles took Miss Frank away from the Pros. They cannot deny that fact. The Pros want to get some revenge for it." !~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~! |Cage Match: Centris vs. Lifetaker| !~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~! JG - "There is one man who is looking to rid the league of the awful force known as Centris. That man is Lifetaker." JH - "These two will kill each other in this match. Neither of them are going to be returning." JG - "The irritating stench known as Centris seems to be cluttering this league. Lifetaker thinks he can stop it..." [Footage from Tennis Cage Rage begins playing. Centris picks up Lifetaker and whips him into the fence... no! Lifetaker scales the fence and...] JG - "Moonsault off the fence!" JH - "He connects! No!" JG - "Centris is gone." VO - "One man, a former champion. The other, a former champion. These two collide in what could be an upswing for both careers.... or a serious downside. As Centris burst onto the scene, disrupting all that was sacred in SCRA, nobody took offense. Finally, Lifetaker stepped up to the plate to try and stop him." [Footage entitled "December 8, The Scraps" begins playing.] L - "I had that world title because I am the best pure wrestler to ever grace the halls of SCRA towers, and I have proven that time and time again. I will tolerate no evil in my home, and you two are the personifications of evil. Centris, you arre obviously the mastermind behind all of this crap, so you and me, at the big dance, Rasslefest. The Powers that be want you go gone so badly, I agreed to wrestle you in a cage with no rules! When the blood flows from your split forehead, Centris, you will know who is the greatest, and it is not you or your pathetic Beyonders." [As Lifetaker finishes speaking, the picture shows a cage in the top half with both men facing each other in the bottom.] VO - "The eerie steel will provide the right feel for this big match. Lifetaker attempts to rid the league of the Beyonders. But, will he be enough?" !~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~! |Rock Bottom Robbie vs. Pablo Morales vs. Silver Stone| !~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~! JG - "Pablo Morales, some would say a lucky champion, attempts to recapture the title from Robbie." JH - "I don't like seeing people like Robbie hold titles, but Morales is even worse." JG - "This should be a big match, with three top contenders for the TV title squaring off." JH - "Top contenders? We've got the sliding Silver Stone and the already slid Morales." JG - "Either way, Robbie's going to be ready to fight." [Clip entitled "December 15, Scraps" begins playing.] RBR - "Yes I do. But right now is not the time. We have a great champion holding onto the belt, Gangsta Impasse. He may not be able to speak english, but he can fight. He showed me that in the four man brawl, and he is good. Mind you, I still want a belt. The world belt would be nice, but I'm gonna act out my name, start at Rock Bottom, and work my way to the top. I'm gunning for the TV Title." JG - "So you want to take on Pablo Morales?" RBR - "Not just Pablo, but everybody who has ever won the TV Title. I don't think that there has been a single effective TV title holder since the title came into the SCRA. I plan on changing that. When I win the title, I don't plan on losing it." JG - "You need to beat Morales first in..." RBR - "Beating Morales will be the easiest thing to do. That no talent bum has nothing going for him at all. He's big dumb and slow. I've fought harder opponents and won. Christ, this is gonna be an easier match for me to win. Morales, I want what you got. I challenge you to a fight at Rasslefest. However, I say we go all out, being in the spirit of Rasslefest and all. I say we have a street fight. Here are the rules... there are none. You start outside the stadium, and the first person to get back into the ring and beat out a ten count, wins. Simple, yet very effective. If you want to prove that your a real man, and not some high flying pansy ass Mexican, you'll accept this challenge. Grey, your brother better sign this one." VO - "Not a street fight, but possibly tougher. Robbie came into the ring that fateful night and challenged Morales for the next week. He came through on his promise." [Rock Bottom Robbie picks up Pablo Morales and hits the Rock Bottom Slam. 1! 2! 3!] JG - "New TV Champ, new TV champ!" VO - "So, Robbie takes on two challengers for the TV title. It should be no less a showing than any of these men's other matches." -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
