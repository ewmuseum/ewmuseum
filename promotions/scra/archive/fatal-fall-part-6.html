[SCRA] Fatal Fall, Part 6

13 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
An SCRA Production http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html [Gangsta jumps away in pain, allowing Jacks to slowly get up. Jacks meets Gangsta in the corner and kicks him. He hits a suplex. Gangsta's legs on the way down kicked Silverblade in the back. This sent Silverblade into Baldwin who bodyslammed him.] JG - "A lucky break for Baldwin. Silverblade came right in his arms." JH - "You will need more than that little piece of luck. He will need 20 more if he hopes to win this battle." [In R2, Tulkas is fighting back against Green Knight. He gets to his feet and dropkicks Knight. Tulkas puts on a figure four. Eagle is still dominating Marc Mission. He throws Mission into the ropes and hiptosses him, over the top.] JG - "Marc Mission gone! Somebody has joined Sublime in the third ring." JH - "Should be a borefest. Sublime has proved to be a failure in this league and Mission never had any promise." [We Will Rock You comes on the speakers as Harley runs to the ring. He rolls under the ropes and runs at Jacks. Jacks bends over and back body drops Harley, eliminating him.] JG - "That was fast. Harley just ran in and pretty much ran out." JH - "The guy is a no-talent, washed up bum. Jacks disposed of him as he should be disposed of." [Harley swears loudly, leading to an excited Activists from Hell who are still picketing at ringside. The congratulate Harley for letting out his feelings, but Harley doesn't like this. Harley shoves Baker. Rage trips Harley with his sign, sending Harley face first into the mat. The two Activists laugh at the Australian. Harley gets up and continues to R2. He sees Joseph Eagle waiting for him and doesn't make the same mistake. He rolls under the ring and gets up, locking up with Eagle. Eagle spins around Harley, locking his arm behind his back. In R1, Jacks and Impasse continue to fight in the corner. Baldwin is fighting with Silverblade whose mask remains ripped. Every free moment, Baldwin tries to rip it even more.] JG - "Baldwin is trying to rip the mask open. Everybody wants to know who is under the mask." JH - "Who cares. The guy is a fighter. He hates the fans, so why do they care who he is?" [In R3, Sublime grabs Mission and whips him into the ropes. Mission ducks a clothesline and comes back with a clothesline of his own. He picks up Sublime and nails Mission Impossible. Mission tosses Sublime out of the ring easily.] JG - "Sublime has been totally eliminated from the royal rumbles!" JH - "What a punk." [In R2, Tulkas and Green Knight continue to brawl in a corner, with Green Knight having the upper hand. He tries to lift Tulkas over the top, but with no avail. Tulkas slides across the ropes, landing behind Knight and hitting a back suplex.] JH - "What athleticism by Tulkas." JG - "Shouldn't I be saying that?" JH - "I like to give praise to my favs. Hey, that kind of rhymes. You aren't the only poet!" [Wish You Were Here comes on the speakers as Centris walks to the ring. He immediately attacks Silverblade, frantically trying to rip off the mask.] JG - "Centris taking great exception to the mask. He wants to rid wrestling of these gimmicks and have real men fight." [Baldwin, who was pushed aside by Centris, turns Centris around and nails him with a clothesline. Baldwin then attacks Silverblade. Centris gets up and pushes Baldwin out of the way.] JH - "Everybody wants to fight Silverblade!" JG - "That's what happens in these royal rumbles. One man becomes an enemy for all." [Baldwin comes back and pushes Centris. The two start throwing punches, moving away from Silverblade. Silverblade gets up and jumps at the two, taking them down with a cross body block. They also roll into Gangsta Impasse and Eddy "Flap" Jacks who were still fighting in the corner.] JG - "All the men in ring one are down! If somebody were to come in, they would have easy pickings." JH - "Because you can come in whenever you want, right?" [In R2, the tides have turned as Tulkas has Green Knight on the turnbuckles, trying to push him over. Eagle has Harley on edge in the corner. He lunges at Harley and sends him over the top with a big clothesline.] JG - "Harley eliminated!" JH - "That has to be a record. How long was he in there?" JG - "I don't know, but longer than the length of time in the first ring. That was only, what, 2 or three seconds." JH - "It's funny because its not me!" [Harley walks to the next ring, feeling dejected. He sees Mission and the two start fighting. Eagle sees Knight on the edge and clips Tulkas' knee. This allows Knight to get himself off the turnbuckle. The 5 men in R1 have scrambled to their feet. Jacks tries to get away from Gangsta and go after Baldwin, but Gangsta grabs him from behind.] JG - "Gangsta isn't letting Eddy away. He has some sort of obsession with Jacks." [Orion comes on the speakers as Xarax walks down the aisle. He looks focussed when he hits the ring, attacking Silverblade. Baldwin and Centris look at each other and grab Xarax. They both start attacking him. Silverblade walks away and joins in the attack of Jacks. With the help of Gangsta, they knock over Jacks. Silverblade then kicks Gangsta in the stomach and hits a swinging neckbreaker.] JG - "Silverblade pulling a "Gangsta". Gangsta Impasse turned on Green Knight earlier this evening." JH - "Everybody is turning on everybody, Grey. It is a free for all in there because everybody wants a title shot." [Marc Mission grabs Harley's throat in R3 and chokeslams him to the mat. He picks up Harley and whips him into the ropes, back body dropping the Australian to the outside.] JG - "Harley is totally eliminated!" JH - "Did you have your stopwatch? How long was that for all three rings?" JG - "I don't know. I am guessing around 2 minutes." [We Will Rock You again comes on.] JH - "Competition! Which Crocodile Hunter will get eliminated fastest? Harley set a good time, but Macdiver has always proven to be the slower of the two." [Macdiver walks over to Silverblade. They lock up. Silverblade throws Macdiver into the ropes. Macdiver ducks a clothesline, but when he looks up, he sees Jacks crouched over in a back body drop position. Macdiver is sent flying to the outside.] JH - "Macdiver setting a pretty good time in ring one. Harley's was a bit better, so Macdiver will have to make up the time in the next two." JG - "Will you cut it out?" JH - "Can't I monitor the match?" [Jacks marches forward and attacks Silverblade. He pummels him to the mat with axehandles. Gangsta walks over and, surprisingly, instead of attacking Jacks, hits an elbow on Silverblade.] JG - "After the cross by Silverblade, Gangsta Impasse seems to be focusing his attention on him rather than Jacks." JH - "Smart move. Focusing on Jacks would only lead to destruction." [In R2, Macdiver grabs Eagle as he was about to punch Tulkas. Eagle hits a mule kick, rolls over Macdiver's back and hits a back suplex.] JG - "Joseph Eagle running circles around the bigger Macdiver." JH - "This looks grim for Harley." JG - "Harley?" JH - "Yeah. Macdiver is already getting beat in the second ring. He could make up some time." [In R1, Centris grabs Xarax and puts him in a piledriver position. Baldwin helps spike the piledriver. Baldwin then dropkicks Centris. He taps his head. The crowd cheers to these antics, but Centris and Xarax both get up angered. They both run at Baldwin with a clothesline, but Baldwin ducks and the two clothesline each other. Baldwin gets up and taps his head. He turns to see Jacks running at him. Jacks rams Baldwin, sending him flying over the top ropes.] JG - "Brad Baldwin is eliminated! Two smart moves by Baldwin, but not watching his back left him helpless to Jacks' attack." JH - "Baldwin will have to put his pony tail back together and cry over to the second ring." [In R2, Eagle has Macdiver nearing elimination. Baldwin walks in and dropkicks Eagle from behind. This sends Eagle stumbling towards Macdiver with flailing arms. He knocks Macdiver with a clothesline, sending Macdiver over the top.] JH - "Get the stopwatch out! It's going to be close!" JG - "Please Jack." JH - "What? I am paying attention to the match, aren't I? Even though he may not win the time trials, Macdiver easily won the worst way to get eliminated there. That was better than Harley's running stupidity." [To Hell With Tradtion comes over the speakers. Lucky Louie Carnage walks down to the ring. He does a Harley-like run at Jacks, but Jacks hiptosses Louie to the outside.] JH - "Two in one night!" [In R3, Macdiver fights with Mission. Macdiver has the upperhand with numerous closed fist punches, splitting Mission wide open. Mission looks at the blood dripping down his face. Worried, he walks towards the ropes. Macdiver runs at him and clotheslines Mission, sending him over the top.] JG - "Marc Mission is gone, and maybe for the better of himself. He is really cut over the forehead." JH - "That may require some stiching." [In R2, Carnage walks over to Eagle and Baldwin. He spins around Eagle, who punches Carnage. He continues to hit him to the ropes. Eagle runs at Carnage, but Baldwin beats him to it, dropkicking Carnage over the top.] JG - "Both men eagerly wanting to eliminate Carnage. They have returned to fighting each other." JH - "A slap fight. They have the ultimate open fist punches, which aren't really punches, but slaps. Reminds me of gay people." JG - "Jack! Sorry to all of our viewers offended by that." JH - "It's true, isn't it? Does that mean Baldwin is just a swerve? Maybe he is really "Hot" for you, Grey." [In R1, Silverblade has Gangsta in the corner. He places him near the turnbuckle and tries to lift him over the top. He lifts up with all his strength, but cannot budge Gangsta. Jacks walks over and helps out, thrusting up with his mammoth legs.] JG - "They are trying to eliminate Gangsta Impasse! With that build, it should be tough." JH - "Look at Jacks, though. Are you saying that Jacks' body can't overcome Impasse's?" JG - "No, it is tough to eliminated all the big people." JH - "Is that what people told you when they were in battle royals? I have noticed the plumpy stature of your childhood, which for the most part carried into your adulthood." [With a big thrust, Jacks and Silverblade push Gangsta over the top. They celebrate for a moment, but this ends when Silverblade pushes Jacks into the corner. He starts throwing punches. Jacks fires back, reversing their places. Naveed comes on the stadium speakers.] JG - "Here comes Johnny Destiny! He must be fighting anyways." JH - "That isn't Destiny! It's Nathan Flash! Nathan Flash!" [Flash walks down the aisle with an evil grin.] JG - "I am getting something from the back. What! Nathan Flash has turned on Johnny Destiny! Nathan Flash has turned on Johnny Destiny! Before this rumble, Flash attacked Destiny!" JH - "Finally. Destiny was a bum who was skidding faster than a truck with a load of chickens." JG - "Chickens?" JH - "I don't know! I froze!" [Flash enters the ring, joining Centris and Xarax. He punches Xarax, but Centris spins him around. Flash clotheslines Centris. He bodyslams Xarax on top of Centris. In R3, Carnage and Macdiver are fighting, rather weakly. Macdiver runs at Carnage who ducks down. Macdiver hits the ropes and Carnage dropkicks him from behind. Macdiver falls over the top.] JH - "How long was that? Did he make it?" JG - "Please, Jack." [R2, Green Knight pushes Tulkas away in the corner and attacks Gangsta Impasse. The two exchange punches, with Green Knight surprisingly hurting Gangsta. Tulkas gets up and helps Green Knight, going after Gangsta. The two bully Gangsta into a corner, punching him in the midsection. In R1, Flash moves over to Jacks and Silverblade. He helps Jacks with Silverblade. Jacks lifts Silverblade onto the turnbuckle where both men punch Silverblade. Silverblade blocks a Jacks punch and nails Jacks with a punch of his own. He tries to hit Flash, but Flash ducks. Silverblade loses his balance, falling off the turnbuckle into Flash's arms in an inverted atomic drop postition. Flash throws Silverblade over the top.] JG - "Silverblade is gone!" JH - "I guess the number 13 wasn't lucky after all." [Flash and Jacks shake hands, waiting for who will come out next. Centris and Xarax still battle in the corner. In R2, Tulkas is backed in the corner by Green Knight and Gangsta Impasse. Baldwin again lunges at Silverblade, trying to unmask him. Joseph Eagle grabs Baldwin on his way, hitting a gutwrench suplex. A high pitched Legion of Doom-like music plays through the arena.] JG - "What a Rush! Here comes the daredevil of the league." JH - "And so we begin Rushfest, comments by Jack Hazard making fun of farm boy Rush." [Rush hits the ring as Jacks and Flash attack him. The two pummel Rush to the ground. Jacks goes off the ropes, sitting down on Rush.] JH - "Let's see you do that, Rush! If he did do it, Rush would enjoy it!" JG - "Jack! Let's see what is happening in another ring. Oh, look, Carnage is all alone." JH - "Grey, he has been alone for a while. Let's watch Rush, see what else I can say." [In R2, Tulkas fights back on his two bullies. He pokes Gangsta in the eye and gets up, taking Green Knight down with a drop toe hold. Tulkas applies an armbar, but Gangsta soon gets up and nails Tulkas with an axehandle.] JH - "Why was he going for a technical move? That's something a moron like Rush would do, not an intelligent wrestler like Tulky." JG - "Tulky?" JH - "He doesn't mind. We are old buds." [Foul Taste of Freedom comes on the speakers as Zamza walks into the building. He enters the first ring and pushes Flash out of the way and grabs Rush. He yanks Rush into a corner and starts pounding away. Flash gets up, looking angered, but doesn't stay up for long. Jacks floors him with a lariat to the back of the head.] JG - "Much like most of Jacks' alliances, that one with Nathan Flash was short lived." JH - "Let's focus on Rush and Zamza. What to say." JG - "How about nothing at all. Why are you doing this?" JH - "He thinks he can make comments about me like that? Good luck!" [Jacks picks up Flash and whips him into the turnbuckle. Flash flips upside down going into the turnbuckle, leaving him exposed. Jacks runs in and starts punching at the stomach. He lifts Flash's head so that Flash is sitting on the turnbuckle, back to the ring. Jacks gets on the second rope and pushes Flash off.] JG - "The alliance is over! Jacks eliminates Flash." JH - "That could be a tongue twister. Flap eliminates Flash. Say it ten times fast, Grey." JG - "No. Why don't you?" JH - "Flap eliminates Flash. Flap eliminates Flash. Flap eliminates Flash. Flap elipinates Flash. Flap elipimates Flash. Flash eliminates Flap. Doh!" [Tulkas and Green Knight lay side by side on the mat in R2 as Gangsta goes off the ropes. He hits a big splash on both of them. Flash enters the ring and grabs Green Knight. He throws Knight into the corner and starts punching away. Gangsta picks up Tulkas and throws him into the corner where Flash and Knight are fighting. Tulkas hits both men, sending them to the mat. Gangsta moves over to Baldwin, who just nailed both Silverblade and Joseph Eagle with a spinning heel kick. He picks up Baldwin and chokeslams Hot. In R1, Jacks stands alone, but his face lights up when the German National Anthem comes on. Chris Eagle runs down to ringside, met by Jacks' boot in the face.] JG - "I guess Jacks has plans for Chris Eagle." JH - "Nothing else for him to do, just pick on the German." [Jacks throws Eagle into the ropes and nails him with a lariat. He picks up Eagle straight away and hits a bodyslam. Jacks throws Eagle into the turnbuckle. On the way, Eagle clips the knee of Centris who was fighting with Xarax. Xarax sees Centris going down and nails a European uppercut, sending Centris staggering to the ropes. Xarax dropkicks Centris, sending him over the top.] JG - "After a long battle with Xarax, Centris is gone." JH - "What's happening with Rush? Oh look! He is hitting the bronco buster on Zamza. I bet he is used to that one." JG - "Jack!" [Zamza ducks underneath Rush when he is coming in, sending Rush through the ropes into the steel turnbuckle.] JH - "That barely affected Rush! My theory must be true!" JG - "What theory?" JH - "The Rush-factor." JG - "Please explain." JH - "It is about why Rush can take so much pain. He must have had his junior friend cut off as a boy." JG - "Jack!" JH - "No, it is true! That is the most painful experience in the world. Everything else seems like nothing. With that move barely doing anything, it proves my point." [Xarax sees Zamza ducking out and drags him to the middle of the ring. He applies a figure four. Chris Eagle, who had just floored Jacks with a dropkick, breaks this up. Rush nails Eagle with a dropkick. Jacks gets up and was about to go after Rush when Lucifer came running down to ringside.] JG - "Lucifer is coming! Jacks has hell to pay!" JH - "And that expression actually works with Lucifer!" [The Activists, who still are around, cheer as their leader enters the ring. He goes straight after Jacks and the two brawl into the corner where the still exposed turnbuckle is. They trade shots. On the other side of the ring, Rush whips Eagle with a dropkick. He grabs Zamza from Xarax and starts to throw rights at Zamza. Zamza slumps to the ground and Rush stomps at him. Xarax spins Rush around, but Rush clotheslines him. Rush picks up Zamza and throws him into the ropes, hiptossing Zamza to the outside.] JG - "Rush eliminated Zamza! They have met twice before and this time, Rush came out on top." JH - "He usually is on top, ask Alex Grey." JG - "I think you should take that comment back." JH - "Or else what? Grey will fire me? He needs me. Rush will come after me? He can't hurt me. You saw my ring prowess last night." JG - "Yes, how's your back today?" JH - "It's fine, just like it was yesterday after I jumped off the top ropes." [R2 soon becomes filled with wrestlers. Tulkas and Joseph Eagle are fighting in one corner. Green Knight and Gangsta Impasse are fighting in another. Baldwin and Silverblade are throwing punches at each other, but Flash dives in with a football tackle, taking down both men. Centris sees Zamza coming into the ring and goes after him. The two fight in the only remaining corner of R2. Louie Carnage waits alone in R3. Rush has turned on Chris Eagle and attacks him with Xarax's help.] JG - "It is a free for all everywhere! Nobody has any allies anymore, just enemies." [To Hell with Tradition comes on the speaker as Maximus Ordicus lumbers out to ringside. He tries to help Lucifer with Jacks. The two knock down Jacks. Lucifer grabs Maximus and flings him outside, though.] JG - "That was fast!" JH - "Another competitor for the Crocodile Hunters." [Ordicus runs into the next ring, going after Baldwin and Silverblade. Flash, being the free man, dropkicks Maximus from behind, sending him flying over the top ropes.] JG - "Maximus Ordicus has just been eliminated from ring two! That is probably the fastest double elimination ever!" JH - "Now look who is waiting in ring three!" [Carnage's eyes light up when he sees his partner coming into R3. The two immediately start throwing punches. The brawl into the ropes and both go over the top.] JG - "Both members of Overdrive have been eliminated! But, they are still fighting!" JH - "Gotta love the heart by those two." JG - "What heart? They are attacking each other and they are partners!"
