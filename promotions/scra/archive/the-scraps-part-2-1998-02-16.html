(_________________________________________________________________________ [Ed Jaspard comes down the ring with his brother, Ted Jaspard, both men getting a large standing ovation, as each man slaps the hands of all the outstretched arms. JH - "With no belts, no real brawlers, just technical wrestling, this is going to be the most boring match in history. I'm out of here." [Hazard gets up and walks down to the isle, and out of the arena.] JG - "JACK! JACK! Okay, well, looks, like I'll be commenting this one on my own folks." [Johnstone and Grant both circle each other and lock up. Johnstone uses his weight and strength to his advantage, and pushes Grant to the corner. Jabasco whips Zangief into the ropes, and hits a clothesline. Johnstone comes out and runs at Jabasco, and Jabasco takes him down with a clothesline, then back body drops Zangief to the outside and throws Johnstone out after him. The crowd goes nuts as Jabasco starts to raise the roof.] JG - "Unbelievable! Jabasco just cleared out the entire ring, kicking Comrades Inc. butt. I bet that if Jack were here, Jabasco probably would have kicked the crap out of him too." [Jabasco stands in the middle of the ring in a two point stance, waiting for one of the men to enter the ring. Zangief and Johnstone climb in at their corner, and Zangief tags in Johnstone. Both men circle each other, then lock up. Jabasco quickly gets the advantage with a arm lock. Jabasco works Johnstones arm, but Johnstone soon reverses the hold, but Jabasco re-reverses it with a quick back flip. Jabasco then throws Johnstone into the corner and hits a flying elbow.] JG - "Great technical wrestling shown by Jabasco here. You know who's a big fan of technical wrestling, Jack Hazard. It's the truth. He says he hates it, but thats just to make it look like he's a tough guy. Really, it's his favorite aspect of the game." [Jabasco grabs Johnstone and places his in an arm bar again, but this time, drags him over to his corner and tags in Grant. Grant continues the hold, but throws Johnstone into the ropes, and hits a drop kick. Grant then places Johnstone into a boston crab. Johnstone screams out in pain as Grant holds the move. Zangief quickly enters the ring and knocks Grant off of Johnstone to break the hold. The ref forces Zangief back to his corner.] JG - "We might have had a submission if it wasn't for Zangiefs interference. Oh look, Hazards back. Jack, how's it going?" JH - "Better than this match. I thought that it would have been over by now. What's going on?" JG - "It's turned out to be an interesting match." JH - "Liar. I've been watching back stage. This match sucks. And, no I don't like technical wrestling." JG - "Well, if you've been watching backstage, why did you come back out if you knew the match was still going." JH - "Because I couldn't stand listening to you anymore." [Grant picks up Johnstone and throws him into the ropes. Grant ducks down to early, and Johnstone sees the move ahead of time and reverses it into a swinging neckbreaker. Johnstone gets up and tags in Zangief. Zangief runs over to Grant and begins to stomp on Grants chest. Zangief then picks up Grant and hits a vertical suplex. Zangief throws Grant into the corner and goes for a springboard shoulder block, but Grant moves out of the way.] JG - "That had to hurt." JH - "If that hurt, than these guys gotta find a new line of profession. This is wrestling. A simple missed move shouldn't hurt. The only thing that should hurt is landing on your head on the cement. Otherwise, quit your crying, suck it up, and kick some ass." [Grant grabs Zangief and hits a quick body slam. Grant then hits an elbow drop, followed by a fishermans suplex. KICKOUT!] JG - "Close call on that one with an awesome drop kick by Jabasco." JH - "For once I have to agree with you Grey. That definetly was a great move by Jabasco. I mean, a top rope drop kick. You don't see them too much anymore." [Jabasco picks up Zangief and hits a backbreaker. Jabasco then grabs the big man and whips him into the ropes. Jabasco runs off the other ropes and hits a clothesline. Jabasco grabs Zangief and places him in a texas cloverleaf submission hold.] JG - "Texas Cloverleaf submission being applied by Jabasco. Those moves have caused many an oppenent the match." JH - "Nah. If Zangief submits on this weak move, I'll resign." JG - "Please submit Zangief, I'll pay you big bucks if you do." JH - "Shut up Grey." [Zangief eventually breaks the hold, and hits a snap mare. Zangief then picks up Jabasco and whips him into the ropes. Zangief goes for a back body drop, but Jabasco leap frogs over it. Zangief turns around and goes for a clothesline, but so does Jabasco, and both men get knocked out.] JH - "Hey, maybe we'll get a double count out to end this madness." JG - "You wish." JH - "I do, I really really do." [The ref begins to count, 1..2..3..4..5.., Zangief begins to move, and so does Jabasco. 6..7..8..9.., Zangief gets up, breaking the count, followed by Jabasco. JH - "You want to see some fierce attacks, watch Street Smarts fight 'fearless' Ed. Now there's some good fighting." Johnstone screams out in pain, and reaches wildly for something to grab onto.] JG - "It could be over with this move." JH - "Yeah right, nothing ever ends in a submission hold, you should know that." Remember the Cold war? Anything can happen." [Jabasco grabs Johnstone and hits a pile driver. Jabasco then drags Johnstone into his corner and tags in Grant. Johnstone grabs Grant in a double underhook position, then slams him to the mat, including the pin... 1! 2! 3!] JG - "Comrades Inc. win!" JH - "Great, now, let's get to another match, something just a little bit gooder than this one was." JG - "I don't think gooder is a word Jack." JH - "It has to be, you just said it, and your the 'oh wise one'." JG - "I said it because, well, ah screw it." JH - "Exactly. Let's move on." Maybe next time they won't be so concerned wirth getting fan mail as opposed to doing their job." KS - "I see, anyone else?" AG - "Please....don't even tempt me with remarks like that. Our presence here has increased the Tag team divisions talent level significantly. What else would you expect from the NLWP World Tag Team Champions! But enough of that. We won the right to compete for the SCRA Titles at the Spring Spectacle PPV, and now we wil come for what we truly want. That golden title that makes us the top dog. The Activists have it right now but we're coming for it at the PPV. So I must tell the activists to keep the title long enough to meet us, we'd love to take it from you." I want them to watch next week. Make sure you two watch. The Siberian Express has a nice surprise in store for you. I know you're going to love it. That's it for now."
