SCRA Showcase Card — Spots Open, Part 2

18 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SPOTS ARE OPEN FOR WRESTLERS!! SIGN UP NOW!! http://scra.home.ml.org =========== Match 3 =========== Johnny Destiny vs. Silver Stone * * for the North American Championship ============================= TF - "This matchup is scheduled for one fall and is for the SCRA North American Title! Coming down to the aisle first, your former North American champion. He is accompanied by Nathan Flash and hails from Vancouver, British Columbia. Weighing in at 254 lbs., Johnny Destiny!" [Destiny emerges as Superman's Dead comes on through the speakers. Nathan Flash soon follows as the two walk down the aisle to a huge fan reaction. They high five many fans and make their way to the ring. As Destiny walks in the ring, fireworks go off and Destiny rebounds off the ropes.] JG - "Destiny wants his gold back, bad!" JH - "Too bad that pretty boy from Vancouver isn't going to get it. Well, he will get it, but not the belt." JG - "Speaking pretty fondly of Silver Stone, Jack." TF - "And his opponent, the current North American Champion. He hails from Carson City, Nevada and weighs in at 269 lbs. Accompanied by his manager, Electra, here comes Silver Stone!" [Silver Stone comes into the aisle and stands at the top with his belt around his waist. He begins to walk down the aisle as Fight till you Die comes on the speakers. His black leather jacket looms over Electra who is wearing a Silver Doll dress. Silver Stone ignores all the fans and enters the ring, holding the ropes open for Electra.] JG - "This one promises to be a great matchup and it has started already!" JH - "Silver Stone is always the smart one, attacking his opponent before he is ready." {- As Hazard stated, Silver Stone started punching Destiny before the bell rang. Silver Stone continued to catch Destiny off guard as he hit a dropkick. Silver Stone walks over to the crowd and shows off as Destiny slowly gets up and climbs the turnbuckle. As Silver Stone turns around, he notices Destiny is no longer on the mat and looks up at the turnbuckle only to see a missile dropkick coming straight at him! Destiny then hits a lariat sending Silver Stone over the top ropes. -} JG - "Destiny is going nuts today! He really wants that gold back." JH - "Silver Stone was just a little off guard. That Destiny always attacks people from behind, just like that dropkick." JG - "Oh yes, and that Silver Stone attack wasn't from behind?" {- Destiny goes off the ropes and hits a hurricarana off the top ropes onto the outside on Silver Stone, sending Silver Stone into the ring steps. Destiny lunges at Silver Stone, but Silver Stone slouches down and grabs Destiny's tights, sending him into the guardrail. -} JH - "Ha! Did you see that! What a smart move by the champ." JG - "A smart and illegal move. Did you see how much tights he got there?" JH - "So he grabbed a guys pants. How can that be illegal?" {- Silver Stone slowly gets up and grabs Destiny and bodyslams him onto the concrete. Silver Stone climbs back in the ring and Destiny wearily rolls back in. Silver Stone kicks Destiny as he rolls in and Destiny can't stand up. Silver Stone stops the kicking assault and drags Destiny to the center of the ring. He goes off the ropes and hits an elbowdrop on Destiny. Silver Stone bends over to pick up Destiny, but Destiny hits a punch to the midsecction. -} JG - "Destiny is working his way back to his feet." JH - "What a cheap move! You don't punch a guy who is helping you get up." JG - "Oh no, I have seen you applaud that many times." JH - "Well, punching isn't the greatest way. I prefer facerakes." {- Destiny continues to work up to a vertical base. Destiny picked up Silver Stone and hit an inverted atomic drop. Destiny went off the ropes and hit a flying cross body block, sending both men to the outside. -} JG - "Here comes another brawl!" JH - "Not if Destiny is smart. Did you see how Silver Stone crushed him last time the two were on the outside?" JG - "Destiny certainly got in some nice blows." JH - "Yes, Destiny does blow well." JG - "Come on, Jack. There is no reason for that." JH - "Sorry, it will never happen again." JG - "Good." JH - "You can take my word for it and you know how good my word is!" {- Destiny rolled back in the ring first. He walked over to the ref and turned his back to the ropes. Silver Stone rolled in and clipped the leg. Silver Stone continued to keep Destiny on the mat with a cross face chicken wing. Destiny moved over to the ropes and Silver Stone broke the hold. -} JH - "Why if you touch the ropes, does the guy have to break the hold?" JG - "I don't know. It is just a rule." JH - "Stupid rule. I bet they made it up for wimps who can't tough it out. Oh, if you touch the ropes, you are free! Great rule." {- Silver Stone picked up Destiny and hit a piledriver. He went for the pin, but only got a two count. Silver Stone whipped Destiny into the turnbuckle and followed him in with a clothesline. As Destiny moved off the turnbuckle, Silver Stone came off the ropes and hit a bulldog. -} JG - "A great combo by Silver Stone." JH - "He has some great skills, eh Grey?" JG - "All wrestlers in the league are very talented." JH - "But, Silver Stone shines like his name." {- Silver Stone picked up Destiny and sent him into the ropes. He went for a back body drop but went down too early and Destiny hit a European uppercut. -} JG - "Saw that one coming a mile away." JH - "Shutup. Silver Stone just made a little miscalculation. He thought that Destiny was fast, which of course, we all know he is not." JG - "Destiny is a very quick individual. He uses his speed well." {- Destiny then went back off the ropes, but Electra grabbed his foot. Destiny turned around and Electra walloped him with her purse. Destiny fell to the mat and Silver Stone covered him. -} JG - "No! Don't make it end this way!" JH - "What a doll!" JG - "Silver Stone gets the one...two...three! He is still the North American Champion." =========== Winner =========== Silver Stone ============================ JG - "Well folks, we put on another good show tonight. Look for more announcements to come regarding the wrestlers in the league. Remember, we are looking for NEW TALENT, so any wrestlers out there, join up!" JH - "Shutup, Grey! Good night everybody!" -------------------==== Posted via Deja News ====----------------------- http://www.dejanews.com/ Search, Read, Post to Usenet
