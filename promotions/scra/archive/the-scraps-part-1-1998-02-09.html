[SCRA] The Scraps [1/5] (9 February 1998)

9 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The screen turns black and white, signalling a horrid setting. The pictures of the five wrestlers competing in the cage match, "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin, Gangsta Impasse, Chase Hunter, "Perfectionist" Nik Nyce and Prodigy, flash across the screen.] VO - "They all fight for one prize." [The screen splits into five sections, the faces still taking up a section each.] VO - "They all want one man." ["Cowboy" Ken Curtis' face comes on the screen, filling the unoccupied middle space.] VO - "When they will fight for this prize against the man is undecided. Tonight, however, it will be on...." ___________________________________________________________________________ ___ _ ___ |_ _|| |_ ___ / __> ___ _ _ ___ ___ ___ | | | . |/ ._> \__ \/ | '| '_><_> || . \<_-< |_| |_|_|\___. <___/\_|_.|_| <___|| _//__/ |_| ** LIVE ** FEBRUARY 9, 1997 ___________________________________________________________________________ [The camera moves into the outdoors, same location as the Winter Carnival the night before. It scans the park for happy visitors, finding the children throwing snow at their parents. As the camera watches the juvenille snowball fight, Jon Grey begins speaking.] JG - "If only it was as simple as a snowball fight." [The camera moves to the broadcast table where Jon Grey and Jack Hazard sit.] JG - "Welcome everyone to the Monday Night Scraps! I'm Jon Grey and with me as always is my tag team partner, Jack Hazard." JH - "It's a great night, eh? We've got snow, we've got games and we've got a main event in a steel cage where the blood will be flowing like a fat guy down a hill." JG - "I believe that would be rolling, not flowing." JH - "No, flowing. Have you seen fat guys? They're wide loads. That would carry them down pretty darn fast." JG - "Anyways, as my partner alluded to, we've got the big steel cage matchup coming up at the end of the show. The winner faces the current champion at Spring Spectacle, coming up March 15. From there, it works backwards until next week." JH - "And backwards it shall go. We're gonna see Prodigy, Hunter and Gangsta running their tails off to escape Hardin and Nyce." JG - "We've also got the return of the Activists from Hell, tonight they face the German Eagles for the tag titles." JH - "Another snoozer. Does it surprise you that when the Activists return, I instantly catch up on all my sleep?" JG - "No. We've also got the three tag finalists from last night facing off for the same rights as the singles wrestler. The winner tonight earns a title shot for his team at Spring Spectacle and it works back from there." JH - "That's a tough one. I know Jabasco is gonna be gone faster than a doughnut in a police department, but from there it's a toss up. Bill Baer and the Praetorian Guard haven't shown the same flair as when they had the belts, but that could be extra incentive to do well. It's a real toss up." JG - "As are the North American title matches. The first round gets underway tonight, with the second rounds coming next week and the following week." JH - "We're gonna see a good North American champ for once. Let's look back at our past champs: Robbie? Silver Stone? Flash? Destiny? Gangsta Impasse? Phantom?" JG - "Some would argue about that list. Some held the belt well, others not so well. Robbie has a chance at becoming a two time champ if he wins this upcoming matchup. It could be a barn-burner!" JH - "You said it Grey! We've got the man many consider to be the best brawler in the business, and he's gonna need all the violence he can muster to defeat the self-proclaimed 'tough little Canadien bastard'!" JG - "Let's go to the ring!" ___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| North American Tournament Match 1 ) | | \ | | Derek Mota vs. Rock Bottom Robbie / |___ | | | | Writer: Greg R. (_________________________________________________________________________ [The camera pans down across the crowd to the ring, where Ed Jaspard stands, mic in hand...] EJ - "Ladies and gentlemen, this next matchup is set for one fall with a 30-minute time limit. Your first competitor..." [Bush's "Little Things" blares from the arena speakers as Rock Bottom Robbie splits the entrance curtain and makes his way down the ramp. He slaps high-fives with a couple of fans wearing RBR shirts, then makes his way intently to the ring.] EJ - "... from Intercourse, Pennsylvania, weighing in at 245 lbs, he is one fo the sport's top brawlers, he is ROCK BOTTOM ROBBIE!" [The crowd pops heavy for RBR as he enters the ring and hops to the top rope, pulling his tank top apart! Suddenly, Mota slides in under the bottom rope on the opposite side of the ring and runs to the corner where RBR stands, landing a forearm to the small of RBR's back!] JG - "Whoa! Mota just came out of nowhere to attack Robbie! He's setting him up... side suplex from the middle rope! This one may be over before it begins!" JH - "You gotta love Mota! Expect the unexpected!" [Mota covers RBR, but Wellington hasn't rang the bell! Mota jumps up and gets in Wellington's face, but the ref backs him down! Wellington calls for the bell!] DING! DING! DING! JG - "Here we go!" [Mota pulls Robbie to his feet... bodyslam! Mota charges off the ropes, and springboards... moonsault! Here's a cover! 1! 2! JG - "No! Robbie kicked out forcefully!" [Mota pulls RBR to his feet and irish-whips him into the ropes! RBR rebounds and ducks a Mota clothesline! RBR off the opposite ropes, Mota leapfrogs... no! RBR caught Mota in mid-air and spins him around, slamming him to the mat with a spinebuster! Robbie straddles Mota and begins jackhammering fists to the Canadien's forehead! Wellignton steps in and pulls RBR back, RBR is fired up! RBR pulls Mota to his feet and irish-whips him into the corner, following him in with a vicious running lariat! Mota crumples but RBR picks him up and pulls him to center-ring, setting him up...] JG - "Powerbomb by Robbie! Here's a lateral press!" 1! 2! JG - "No! Mota kicked out!" JH - "It's gonna take more than that to put Mota down!" [RBR pulls Mota up and irish-whips him into the ropes! Mota rebounds, running lariat by RBR! RBR backs into the ropes and drops a knee across Mota's forehead! RBR pops up and drops a forearm to Mota's head! RBR pops up again and yells to the crowd, illiciting a pop!] JH - "You can't waste time with Mota like this!" JG - "I agree! You gotta stay on him!" [RBR pulls Mota up, but Mota lands a fist to the gut! Another! Mota is up, he slams a forearm into the side of RBR's head! Mota grabs RBR, irish-whip! RBR off the ropes, Mota picks him up and falls backward...] JG - "Hotshot! Shades of the late Eddie Gilbert, there!" JH - "Here's a cover!" 1! 2! JG - "No! Robbie gets a foot on the ropes! Wellington saw it just in time!" [Mota pulls Robbie to his feet and sets him up... Northern Lights Suplex! Mota heads up top!] JG - "What's he gonna do now!?" JH - "The crazy little bastard is capable of anything!" [Mota's up top! He leaps...] JG - "Flying elbow from the top! This one could be over!" 1! 2! JG - "No! Robbie got the shoulder up!" JH - "A lazy cover from Mota! He didn't hook the leg!" [Mota pulls RBR up and irish-whips him into the ropes. RBR rebounds, Mota catches him in an abdominal stretch!] JG - "Submission hold for Mota here!" JH - "When's the last time you saw anyone submit to an abdominal stretch, Grey?" JG - "It could happen!" JH - "Yeah, and Gangsta Impasse could start speaking English, too!" [Mota reaches out and grabs the top rope for leverage! The crowd voices it's disapproval as Wellington asks for the submission! RBR shakes it off and pulls his leg free, hiptossing Mota to the center of the ring! RBR bounds off the ropes and hurdles the prone Mota! RBR off the ropes, Mota leaps for a dropkick...] JG - "No! Robbie held the ropes and Mota hits the mat hard!" JH - "Get on him, Robbie!" [RBR leaps... flying legdrop! RBR pulls Mota to his feet and irish- whips him into the corner, following him in with a flying splash! RBR picks Mota up and places him on the top rope!] JG - "Robbie's setting him up!" JH - "You think!?" [RBR scales the rope and sets Mota up... superplex! RBR rolls over into a cover! 1! 2! JG - "No! Mota got the shoulder up!" [RBR slaps the mat in frustration, but rolls off and pulls Mota up! RBR lays a couple of forearms into Mota, backing him up against the ropes! RBR backs up and charges, but Mota falls and grabs RBR by the trunks, pulling him through the ropes to the floor!] JG - "Oh man, Robbie hit hard there!" JH - "He's playing into Mota's hands!" [Mota charges and propels himself off the opposite ropes! He leaps...] JG - "Plancha over the top rope onto Robbie! Both men hit the guardrail hard!" JH - "Unbelievable!" [Mota pulls himself up and grabs RBR, rolling him back into the ring! Mota scales the turnbuckle from the outside and leaps!] JG - "Nobody home! Mota went for the monnsault from the top, but Robbie moved at the last second!" [RBR pulls himself up and grabs Mota!] JH - "What's he doing!? He shoulda covered him!" JG - "He's going for his finisher!" [RBR picks Mota up over his shoulder and walks to the corner! Mota wriggles free and falls behind RBR, pushing him away! RBR turns and Mota throws a lariat, but RBR ducks and spins Mota around...] JG - "Release German Suplex! Cover him Robbie!" JH - "Wait a minute, it's Aladdin Style! What the heck is he doing out here!?" [Mota calls for the referee, and Wellington bends over him! RBR propels himself into the ropes, but Style hops onto the apron and nails RBR in the back of the head!] JG - "Wait a minute! What did he hit him with!?" JH - "Looks like Style has a pair of brass knucks!" [Mota gets up as Robbie staggers back, right into his arms for a...] JG - "Death Flip! Mota hit the Death Flip!" JH - "It's over!" 1! 2! 3! DING! DING! DING! JG - "Derek Mota has beaten Rock Bottom Robbie! Style's interference made it happen!" JH - "Style is exiting ringside as Mota's hand is raised!" EJ - "Here's your winner... DEREK MOTA!" JG - "And, Derek Mota moves on. He'll face the winner of the upcoming match between "Hot Stuff" Mike Justice and "Iconoclast" Sean Watts. It should be a great match between two great technicians." JH - "Technicians. What a joke. I like Justice. I like Watts. I don't like their wrestling styles." JG - "Then, what do you like about them?" JH - "Their attitude." JG - "(sigh) Let's go to Ed Jaspard for the ring intros." -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
