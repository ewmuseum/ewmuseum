[SCRA] ScraPreview [4/4] (7 February 1998)

7 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:: Doug "Grizzly" Adams JG - "Had this been two months ago, Grizzly may have had a shot due to his size and brawling. Now, he has none. Look for an early exit, to say the least." JH - "He'll be gone before the match even starts." JG - "And, before we end the singles battle royal preview, we've got that mystery man whose identity will be revealed tomorrow night." [The scene opens to an open field. A man is seen with a black t-shirt, black cowboy hat over his eyes to keep his identity a secret and blue denim jeans. He has a piece of long grass in his mouth as he approaches the camera.] UNKNOWN - "When de SCRA told me bout dis lil' rumble yous be havin' I jest knew I had te be in it. Even if Alex Grey don't lemme in...I'll make ma presence felt. I mean, ye fight in all doze rumbles as a kid fer what? So far, nothin'. But now I see an opening an' dis damn president shuts de door on dis pretty lil' face. Ma boyz are here te back me up an' if anyone messes wit' de Pony Express, dey gon' feel what a lil' southen boy can do. I don' know how I gon' be involved in dis Winter Carnival, but I will be dey're if ye like it er not." [The boy tips his cap and brushes some maneur off his shoe with a twig.] U - "Don't ye hate it when ye step in s*!t a lil' too deep? Well, Mr. President, yer steppin' in a whole pile o' horse maneur an' dey're ain't nothin' ye can do bout it, cause I gots ma boys watchin ma back. So, you can put Tommy Gregory on me, ye can make Eddy Jacks sit on me, but it ain't gon change de fact dat I gon' be at Winter Carnival '98. Will I be in de rumble? I don' know. Will I be at de tag match? Maybe. Who knows. Yer jest gon' have te watch." [From out of nowhere, a tuff red corvair pulls up beside the boy. A group of men come out, dressed real nice. One man comes right up to the boy and says:] U - "Why don't you Socs get the hell outta here." MAN - "Hey Grease. Need a haircut?" [The boy punches the other guy in the mouth as the rest of the Socs attack the boy. They hold him down and as the guy's about to punch him he says:] MAN - "Dis is fer stealin' Cherry." [Some hollers and screams are in the back as a four men run to save the boy. The Socs immediately take off as the boys attend to the boy.] UNKNOWN#2 - "Ye alright, kid?" U - "Yea, I'm alright. Dese are de boyz I talkin' 'bout. De Greasers. We're considered as de bad boys, as the hoods, but what we do is fer kicks. We ain't gon hurt anyone....unless yer in de ring. When I get to Winter Carnival, I can guarantee you dat ma presence will be felt." [The screen widens showing the whole group as the camera fades. The camera fades back in only to hear one man say, 'HEY CAN WE GO TE CARVEL?' FADE!] JG - "Now, onto the tag battle royal! First, let's hear from the team with the most riding on this event, the German Eagles." :: German Eagles OM - "What a welcome did we have in good old Germany! Thank you fans, for this. We are nothing without you. But exiting things happen again in the SCRA." CE - "The Activists from Hell are back in town! We are happy about this, because they are one of the most interesting team in the SCRA." MF - "And if they win on Winter Carnival 98..." JE - "I don't doubt that!" MF - "... they get a title shot against the Eagles." OM - "This should be a very intersting match, in which both teams have to give a lot to stand, Miss Frank. Well done." CE - "And we are looking forward to this match. These tag titles (pointing the belts) have to be competed. And we are looking for competition." JE - "We said we would be fighting champions. And we will." MF - "So, should the Activists lose..." CE - "They won't!" MF - "Should the Activists lose, we want to take another team! President Grey knows our list. He will chose the right one." JE - "And we will show, why we are the SCRA tagteam champions!" all - "For the black, red and gold!" JG - "They certainly seem ready to face oncoming competitors." JH - "Those two brothers will split with the tag titles faster than they split from their mother's womb." JG - "Perhaps. It will be quite a challenge for them as they face all the top contenders in the league." JH - "How many is that? Two? Three?" JG - "Probably. But, another team with a lot riding on this event are the Activists from Hell. If they win, they stay in the league. If not, they're gone. Let's hear from the Praetorian Guard, then the Activists themselves." :: Praetorian Guard [SCENE: Some sort of training facility. A ring, filled with two currently unknown wrestlers, stands in the middle of the room. A punching bag swings back and forth to the left, a bulky man whacking the stuffing out of a hole near the top. Other unused equipment, medicine balls, ab stretchers and skipping ropes, lay to the right of the ring. Suddenly, a man walks in front of the camera. The man is "Lumberjack" Lon Saverese.] LS - "Lots of people use this little time slot ta do various things." [The camera moves away from Lon, zooming into the punching bag. The man hitting it becomes clear: Bill Baer. His muscles flex each time his massive hands club the bag. The stuffing flies out of the top, a piece landing on the camera lens. The cameraman's hand brushes it away. As the camera stays on Baer, Lon speaks again.] LS - "Some use it ta play mind games..." [The camera pans away from the punching bag to the ring. The two wrestlers become clear: Tulkas Naugrim and HoP trainer, Tain Surefoot. Naugrim has Surefoot in the deadly Maul of Woden. Surefoot yells out to Naugrim to squeeze tighter or pull his arm down and various other improvements to Naugrim's move. Surefoot yells "Now!" and Naugrim slams him to the mat. Naugrim helps up his trainer, but Surefoot quickly drops Naugrim with a drope toe hold, waving his finger at Naugrim.] LS - "Some use it ta build confidence into a match..." [Again, the camera moves away from the ring, this time to Bill Baer who is hitting a speed bag. The bag bounces back and forth, almost at the speed a professional boxer could.] LS - "Some use it ta annoy opponents..." [Cut back to the ring, but Naugrim climbs out. Surefoot stands waiting with the medicine ball. Naugrim gets onto his back, sits up and Surefoot tosses the ball down. Naugrim goes with the ball, then sits up, throwing the ball to Surefoot on the way up. His abs flex as he lifts the ball up, almost throwing it over the head of Surefoot.] LS - "Some just do it for fun..." [The camera moves back to the face of Savarese, but the sounds still echo throughout the arena. Baer grunts as he hits the punching bag. The bag leaves a loud thud. Naugrim grunts on his way up from the sit ups. Surefoot shouts words of encouragement.] LS - "But, in da big matches, since MacDonald's gone, I believe d'old saying "A picture's worth a thousand words". Y'see, Activists, this is our little mind game. We don't say, we just do." [The flash cuts with a final loud thud from Baer.] :: Activists from Hell [The camera rises to a sunny winter day in Minnesota. During the summertime, this lakefront park is busy with children scampering about; joggers and bikers. The snow drives only the brave away as the cold winds become bitter. From the right enters "The Liberal" Zak Rage and "The Advocate" Joshua Baker. Each man is dressed in the appropriate winter garb and the view settles in as both men stand side by side. Both wear witner caps, although Rage's long dreadlocks spill freely out of his.] ZR - "[With hands shoved in pockets and a quick grin] We know what you're thinkin'...." [A quick glance around at the environment...] ZR - "[To the camera] .....You're thinking, "Where are the kids? Where are the leaflets? Where are those Unicef boxes?" [Rage shakes his head...] ZR - "Nope....not today. No gimmicks; no politics; no conspiracy theories. And....[quick grin]...I'll try to be serious, but don't count on it." [With a wink, he continues] ZR - "You see, Joshua and I just wanted to have a lil' chat with the boys named "The Guard"....just the four of us." [As Baker remains standing, Zak sits on the snowy ground] ZR - "[With a shake of his head] I bet you two are pretty gosh-darn proud of yourselves, aren't you? [nodding] Hey...you're the champs! You should be proud! You put our blades to the apron for the ol' one-two-three, _and_ you took credit for running Josh and I out of the SCRA......" [A longing slow nod] ZR - "Unfortunately for you, this is where the whole thing becomes quite problematic....._never_ in your wildest dreams did you think we'd return!" [A sly grin] ZR - "Well, my dear friends; we _are_ back and _this_ go-around, there is more of a chance that Mr. Hussein will survive than you will 'cause we are going to come atcha' full throttle, babe. Why?..." [Tilt of the head and a shrug....] ZR - "...'cause we ain't got nothin' to lose and you do." [Sniffles] ZR - "You see, that leather-and-gold means everything to you. Everything. And since you lost it to the boring boys named the Eagles, you've kinda' turned into big gobs of Jello, know what I mean? Whereas the gold doen't mean diddly to us; heck, we sold it within ten minutes of gettin' it last time and we're gonna do the same thing over again.....__BUT__" [He holds up one finger....] ZR - "...we ain't in it for the coin, either. You see, Guard...it all comes down to motives. So, when the ref has just slapped that "three" count on you on Sunday...[wink] ...think about that." [Joshua squats down besides his friend and puts a hand on Rage's shoulder] JB - "No....I'm not going to _tell_ you how badly you boys will be hurting Sunday night.....[shake of the head].....uh-uh...it's something that we can only _show_ you." [Zak nods as well] JB - "Gentlemen, this one's pretty simple; we despise you and you despise us. We're both gonna be bloody messes after Sunday but I've promised the little ones that daddy'll come home a winner, and..... [quick nod].....I don't let my children down." [Quick gesture towards Zak] JB - "I've known Zacharius for thirty years....__THIRTY YEARS__ and this is something that he wants. You two don't know Zacharius too well, but when he wants something.....[quick nod]....come hell-or-high- water, the little s<bleep> gets it." [Rage snickers and lowers his head] JB - "We're in the SCRA for good, gentlemen, and we're gonna send you home cryin'; deal with that fact 'cause it's jut the way it's gonna be." [Rage lifts his head to the camera] ZR - "Yeah, we're kinda serious 'bout this...._but_.....[winks]...we don't think we're not gonna have some fun, too." [Without warning, Rage scoops a snowball and fires it at the cameraman whom drops the camera. The scene fades as the SCRA employee shrieks in laughter while Rage and Baker pummell him with snowballs while the camera angle points into the snow-covered trees.] JH - "Oh, I wish those guys stayed out of the league. Rage's smirk, his little dreadlocks...a white boy should not try and act like a black boy. It's like an Australian woman trying to sing an Italian opera! It's just not right!" JG - "An odd way to put it, but effective nonetheless. This will be the fourth time these two teams meet and although it is not for the titles, may hold more importance than the others. The Guard want to prove once and for all that they can beat the Activists. They want to say that they moved the Activists out of SCRA. The Activists, on the other hand, are not just fighting to get back into the league, but for revenge. They have only beat the Guard once and lost another in a very, very controversial fashion. This will be their chance for revenge." JH - "Too bad they'll come up short." :: Siberian Express JG - "But, onto the actual battle royal itself. Right now, only four teams are signed up." JH - "Pathetic." JG - "We could see both Siberian Express members finishing in the top three due to their size and strength. Bolotov is an animal, so he could prove to be the final man standing. In fact, he's probably the favourite to win this thing." JH - "Yeah. The Russian Bear takes no guff from nobody. He's gonna be tossing people like there's no tomorrow." :: The Pros [The camera fades in on the familiar surrondings of the Pro's private gym. Walt Jabasco and Mr. Grant are sparring with their usual sparring partners. Tavis B. is sitting on the outside with a bottle of water in his hand.] T - "Hey Hey! Here we are once again in our private gym. Jabs and Grant are wrestling the same old wrestlers. Nothings changed, the tag ranks are weak with only five teams worth mentioning. Well that's changed, the Activists From Hell are back. Whoopeee. I personally have nothing against those fine Minnesotans but.... and this is a big but, why do they get a chance for the belts in two weeks?!" [Pauses takes a sip from the water bottle, wipes his mouth and continues to talk.] T - "I can understand the rematch with the Guard. One less team to worry about in the battle royal. But why do they get a shot if they win that match? And another thing, why are the Pros at the bottom of the rankings? Oh sure, there's Cold Winter and PTK below us but since when have they won a match? AfH does not deserve to be above us! Neither do those punks from Siberia!" [Jabasco hops off the apron. Walks over to the camera and begins to speak.] WJ - "Settle down Tavis. It doesn't matter if we are at the bottom of the rankings, come Monday we'll dominate all the other teams in the battle royal. Then we'll beat the other two teams in the matchup following that. And whoever is the champion at the time will get beat by the best team in the SCRA today." T - "Nice bragging Jabs. Hopefully you and Grant will be ready come Monday." [The camera fades out as Jabs climbs back in the ring.] JG - "Probably the sentimental favourites due to their gracious defeat the other week..." JH - "Babies." JG - "...the Pros come in with a good shot at gaining two title shots in upcoming weeks. Both have fairly good size and can hold their own in a fight." JH - "No they can't. I haven't seen one good punch from these two. I bet you could do better." [Hazard gets up.] JH - "Come on, slug me." JG - "No." JH - "Come on!" JG - "No." :: Phi Theta Kappa/Cold Winter JG - "The two final teams...with no chance. If these two last more than ten minutes, it'll be a big surprise to me and everybody else watching. I don't know why they are still around, but I suspect jobbing is a main reason. Ah well. We'll see what happens tomorrow night as Winter Carnival comes your way! For Jack Hazard...Jack?" [Suddenly, a snowball whizzes at the desk from behind, nailing Grey in the back of the head. Hazard pops back up and starts laughing uncontrollably.] JG - "This is Jon Grey." [Grey shakes the cobwebs out, gets onto the desk and takes off with a flying elbow into the snow at Hazard! The show fades out.] -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
