[SCRA] The Scraps [1/4] (26 January 1998)

Fans clamour to get their totally "unique" signs on TV. As the camera spins around the stadium, Jon Grey begins speaking.] JG - "Welcome everyone to the Scraps! Welcome to your Monday night big hit!" [The camera continues spinning, eventually setting back towards the broadcast booth. Grey and Hazard sit.] JG - "I'm Jon Grey and with me as always is my tag team partner, Jack Hazard." JH - "Nice to see you, Grey. It's always good to get out of the house on Monday nights, I guess." JG - "Uh, yeah. Folks, we've got a big lineup..." JH - "A whole five matches!" JG - "There may not be a lot of matches, but they are all quality. But, first, an update. Last night, we saw Derek Mota make a remark about Aladdin Style being a real wrestler. The prez, being the quick thinker he is, decided to change the match scheduled later on the night so that Mota and Style are in the ring together...on the same team. We picked up these comments from Aladdin Style to accept the challenge." AS - "Team up with the incomporable Derek Mota? Against that hick Texan? Mota, there's two reasons why I take your request seriously. First, because any time I can get in the ring and show Eddy Jacks that he has been ducking REAL wrestling talent, I'll take it. The only reasons he's still the champ is because he's facing brawlers. Put him in the ring against a wrestler, and he's gone. Secondly, because you're a hell of a wrestler, Mota. You're so damn good, with potential to spare, that someday you might be in my class. So, Mota, hell yeah I'll be your partner." JH - "Wow. The two tough little guys against Hardin. Hardin almost outweighs them put together!" JG - "Hardin still doesn't have a partner, but I'm sure he'll get one later on." JH - "Probably. Who knows, though." JG - "That's later on in the show! Also later on, we've got "Cowboy" Ken Curtis against Gangsta Impasse in the Barn vs. Benz matchup! The loser of the match has to clean the the other's prized possession." JH - "How original." JG - "Despite how unoriginal it may seem, the match should be thrilled with ups and downs, just like any great match between two great competitors...who hate each other." JH - "We need blood, Grey. Haven't seen the juice in a while." JG - "Also later on, we've got two titles up for grab! The Praetorian Guard defend the tags against the German Eagles and Pros. Rock Bottom Robbie puts up the TV title against Sgt. Pepper. We'll see how those two turn out." JH - "Snoozer." JG - "You want snoozer? I've got one for you, Jack. But, after the snoozer..." JH - "An extra match?" JG - "Yup. After the snoozer, we've got a winner. Bobby McAllister attempts to get revenge on Nik Nyce, but Anthony Logan is added to the mix." JH - "Just for fun. As long as he fights, it's okay." JG - "But, now, the snoozer! In an unannounced match, Phantom and... "Latexman" Art Vandilay!" JH - "Oh my gosh! What a match!" JG - "I knew you'd like it. Let's go to Ed Jaspard for the intros." ___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 1 ) | | \ | | Phantom vs. "Latexman" Art Vandilay / |___ | | | | Writer: NP (_________________________________________________________________________ EJ - "This matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 5 minute time limit. Already in the ring, "Latexman" Art Vandilay!" JH - "How do _those_ guys get in the ring without entrances? Can't we get some others to do that?" EJ - "And, his opponent, weighing in at 200 lbs. and hailing from Paris, France, Phantom!" [The Overture comes onto the PA system, but Phantom doesn't come out right away.] JH - "He's scared of Art!" JG - "Now, there he is!" [Phantom jumps in from the crowd and attacks the Latexman from behind. He pummels him with stomps to the back.] JG - "Phantom really putting the beating to the Latexman..." JH - "Who the hell comes up with these names, anyway? Latexman? I mean... really! If the jobbers are going to lose all the time, at least give them a bit of respect!" JG - "Phantom with a drop toe hold...big elbow drop...and takes Latexman right into a headlock. He's taking his time with this one, Jack." JH - "Yeah, I guess...kind of a snack raid match, in my opinion..." JG - "Hold on a second...who's that coming down the aisle? Is he new around here?" JH - "Ya got me. Toothpick sized kinda guy, isn't he? He's wearing a white t-shirt and blue jeans...wonder if he's just a lost fan. That's the tackiest red vest I've ever seen!" JG - "Phantom doesn't see him, but this young man doesn't seem interested in Phantom. In fact, he's coming right over to us, Jack." JH - "Hey!!!! Listen, someone get security. Don't you dare pick up that headset...don't you dare put them on!" JG - "Hold on, Jack, he's doing nothing wrong...what's on your mind... Mr...uhh..." CH - "Hunter...Chase Hunter." JH - "Kinda catchy...has a nice ring to it." JG - "Well, Mr. Hunter, what brings you down to us?" CH - "I'm here simply to help you commentate. Who do we have here, anyway? Ah...it's a slaughter match!! Those are always fun to watch." JH - "Who asked you?" CH - "He did, weren't you paying attention? (Chase points at Jon)" JH - "You've got some nerve coming down here..." CH - "You've got some belly down THERE..." JH - "I don't think you seem to understand who you're talking to. I am Jack Hazard! Wrestling legend extrordinaire..." [Phantom flings Latexman into the buckle and follows up with a flying knee. He then snapmares him down and puts his opponent in a front face lock.] CH - "Oh, I get it! You're one of those guys who claim to be a legend! An ICON if you will!" JH - "See, that wasn't so hard, was it?" CH - "Neither is that gut of yours...probably to make up for your thick skull...or large ego. Your pick." JH - "Who are you to talk about an ego? I've been wrestling longer than you've been alive, boy!" CH - "And you still keep talking like you're still a wrestling god! You're all the same, you legends. You've got a belly that droops down to your friggin' knees! You got for a dropkick and you end up kicking yourself in the belly!" JG - "Gentlemen, please! Phantom looks like he's setting his opponent up for a superplex...very nicely done!" CH - "Look, I came down here to simply give my two cents about professional wrestling. Lard-O here starts insulting me..." JH - "You little punk! People like you make me sick..." CH - "Funny, that's what I was going to say about your cholesterol." JG - "Maybe we should have you here more often, Chase. It would sure keep him in line." CH - "Hey, anytime. Now, about what's going on in the ring. Who did you say this guy was? Phenom? Philosophic? Philanthripist?" JH - "It's Phantom you half wit..." CH - "Thank you, half muscle. Anyway, like I was saying. Isn't this kind of stuff getting on your nerves?" JH - "You mean like you?" CH - "No no no! I mean the dark, forbodding, evil, manic depression, gothic type guys. Talk about guys who haven't been laid in a long time, huh? You'd think with the money they make in pro-wrestling, they'd afford some kind of concubine or something. I'm surprised your wife doesn't act like some night of the living dead re-run." JH - "Why you...you-you've got some nerve to make those kind of accusations!" CH - "You've got some nerve to wear that tie with that shirt. Now stop interrupting me before I force you to lose weight. You want to know the real reason more and more wrestlers do this? Because it's so easy! You just don't smile, god knows I couldn't do that for long...you walk slowly...you wear black...you barely talk! There's no fun in that...look, I'll show you how easy it is!" [Chase jumps over the commentators desk and walks closer to the ring. Phantom has Latexman in a spinning toe hold which looks like Latexman is about to submit. Chase raises his arms in front of him and starts walking around the ring, dragging one foot behind him. He rolls his eyes back and looks very much like something of the living dead. The fans are cheering and laughing to split a gut.] JH - "That little punk! Did you hear the things he was saying to me? I have every right to sue the pants off of him!" JG - "I think it's rather refreshing to see someone get the better of you. Look at Phantom! He's let go of Latexman and doesn't know what to make of Chase Hunter! Chase stops in his tracks...he turns his head towards Phantom!" JH - "That's the goofiest and most insincere smile I've ever seen! He's waving at Phantom! The fans are going nuts!" JG - "Chase now back to his zombie impression. He's shifting all the way back through the curtains. Listen to the audience! They're loving this to bits!" JH - "Phantom still looking in Chase's direction...Latexman coming from behind! Front roll up! No...wait! Phantom reversing it into his own...here's the pin... 1! 2! 3!! JH - "If it wasn't for that brat...Phantom wouldn't have had to cut it close like that." JG - "I've just gotten word that Chase Hunter, indeed, is a new member of SCRA. He's actually under contract with us!!" JH - "God help us all..." JG - "What a match that was." JH - "Yeah, gotta fill the time somehow." JG - "Next up is a doozy. We've got McAllister hell-bent on revenge, Nyce with a temper and Logan with an even bigger temper! With these three men in the ring, it should be a real barnburner." JH - "And if not a barnburner, at least a townhouse burner. Maybe a stove burner." JG - "Let's go to Ed for the intros." ___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 2 ) | | \ | | "Perfectionist" Nik Nyce vs. Bobby McAllister / |___ | | | | vs. "Silverblade" Anthony Logan / Writer: TD (__________________________________________________________________________ [Jaspard stands in the ring with the mic in hand, ready for the big intros.] EJ - "This hoe-down is set for one dance! Everybody grab your partners and YEE-HAW!" [Grey and Hazard look at each other.] EJ - "Swing your partner round and round..." [Hazard leaps to his feet, grabs Grey by the arm and starts square dancing with him.] EJ - "Punch someone and send him down!" JG - "Don't even think about it." [As Jaspard says punch someone, Jimi Hendrix's Star Spangled Banner comes onto the speakers. The "Perfectionist" Nik Nyce comes out to a booing crowd. Nyce makes his way to the ring and quickly enters the ring.] EJ - "Oh yeah! That was Nik Nyce. He weighs in at 234 lbs. and hails from Chicago, Illinois." [Before Jaspard can get the next entrance in, Walk this Way quickly interrupts him as "Silverblade" Anthony Logan comes into the arena. Logan slowly makes his way to the arena and steps into the ring, walking over to a corner and fixating his eyes on Nyce.] EJ - "That's Anthony Logan! He weighs in at 250 lbs. and hails from Fredericton, New Brunswick. Here comes..." [The Marine Hymn then comes onto the PA after Logan's music and Bobby McAllister and Victoria Glenn come out to the cheers of the crowd. Bobby holds the ropes open for Victoria and then jumps into the ring. McAllister makes his way to each corner, avoiding Logan's corner, and raises his hands above his head. Victoria then exits the ring and all three men form a triangle in the arena as the bell rings.] DING! DING! DING! JH - "Don't give Ed more time for the intros. Let's rush him, like that one." [All three men stare down each other, waiting for one another to make the first move. Logan makes the first move, punching McAllister in the face, followed by a quick elbow in Nyce's jaw. Logan then grabs McAllister and Irish whips him into Nyce, sending both men sprawling out onto the ground. Logan grabs McAllister and powerslams him into the mat. Logan then sees that Nyce is getting up, and quickly grabs McAllister and throws him towards Nyce, who meets McAllister in the face with a boot, sending McAllister back to Logan, who meets him with a belly to back suplex.] JH - "It's just like pinball! Ping! Ping! Ping! Dididididing!" JG - "That's great Jack." JH - "I like pinball. PING!" [Nyce then walks over to Logan, and both men together hit a double suplex. Logan then picks up McAllister onto his shoulders, Nyce climbs up to the top rope, and lands a flying clothesline, sending McAllister flying onto the canvass. Logan quickly covers up McAllister... 1! 2! KICKOUT! Nyce and Logan both look at each other in disbelief, and Nyce quickly grabs McAllister, and whips him into the corner, followed by a big splash by Nyce, then another big splash by Logan. McAllister falls down to the mat, completely drained. Logan grabs McAllister, and throws him into the ropes. Both Logan and Nyce go for a double clothesline, but McAllister hits them both, but still gets nailed by a clothesline. All three men go down.] JH - "It's like sardines in a can. Except, McAllister the only one who stinks." JG - "Somebody else stinks down here, Jack." JH - "Ping! Score one for Jack." [Logan is the first man up, followed by McAllister, and together they begin to brawl it out, with McAllister hitting a quick snap suplex. Nyce, however, ends McAllisters offensive moves with a quick standing drop kick. Nyce then grabs McAllister, whips him into the ropes, and back body drops him onto the canvas. Logan follows this with an elbow drop from the second rope. Logan goes for the cover and Nyce jumps on top... 1! 2! 3! McAllister is escorted out of the ring by the official as Logan goes to shake Nyce's hand.] JG - "McAllister's gone!" JH - "I knew the bum would be gone fast. When two pros like Logan and Nyce team up, it's quality. It's like the two paddles of pinball. Such a good team, but one can work without the other when the ball is on one side. You also get lots of points with those." JG - "What does points have to do with wrestling?" JH - "I like pinball." [Nyce kicks Logan in the midsection, then dives and tackles Logan, placing him into a quick armbar on the mat. Logan reverses this and whips Nyce into the ropes, nailing a clothesline. Logan then drops to the mat and begins to pound on Nyce with closed fists. The ref pulls Logan off Nyce, and starts to yell at Logan giving Nyce enough time to get up. The ref moves to the side and Nyce quickly grabs Logan and irish whips him into the corner. Logan barely moves when he hits the ring post, and simply turns around and glares at Nyce.] JH - "That's power, Grey. Logan is getting mad. We're going to see him explode, just like when you start out the pinball..." JG - "Enough about pinball! We've only got two guys left." JH - "Just like the two paddles." [Logan then walks over to Nyce as both men begin to grapple in the center of the ring. Nyce pushes Logan off of him, and Logan just comes back at Nyce. Nyce bounces off the ropes and hits a clothesline, but it doesn't move Logan. Nyce bounces off the ropes again and again hits a clothesline, which doesn't move Logan. Nyce goes for the same move again, but this time Logan stops Nyce dead in his feet with a powering chokeslam. Logan then drops an elbow onto Nyce and picks him up. Logan quickly hits a suplex, then picks up Nyce again and throws him into the ropes, hitting a dropkick. Logan goes for the quick cover... 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Almost had him! Anthony Logan is going nuts in there right now!" JH - "Like the ball in pinball." JG - "How about we talk about another childhood game?" JH - "Childhood? I've got three pinball machines at my house! It's the first thing I bought with the big paycheck I get for SCRA." [Logan places Nyce into a headlock on the mat to wear down Nyce. Nyce, however, slowly brings Logan to his feet, and repeatedly elbows Logan in the stomach, forcing Logan to break the hold. Nyce then flies off the ropes into a cross body, sending Logan to the mat. Nyce picks up Logan and hits a snap suplex. Nyce puts Logan into an armbar, and slowly brings Logan to his feet. Nyce pushes Logan up against the turnbuckles, when the ref calls for a clean break. Logan uses this to his advantage and hits Nyce with a quick kick in the midsection.] JH - "Dirty wrestling. I love that stuff. Almost as much as..." JG - "Don't even say it." JH - "I was going to say....pinball! Ha! Got ya, Grey!" JG - "That's lovely, Jack. Meanwhile, we've got a match going on." [Logan then grabs Nyce and hits a neckbreaker. Logan climbs up to the second rope and goes for an elbow drop, but Nyce moves out of the way. Nyce slowly gets up to his feet while Logan is on his knees. Nyce hits a quick piledriver, followed by the cover... 1! 2! KICKOUT! Nyce grabs Logan and hits a NycePlex, followed by a quick running Leg drop. Nyce then grabs Logan and places him into a Boston Crab.] JG - "If he can sit on the back more, Logan's in deep, deep trouble." JH - "(singing) Deep, deep trouble! Remember that Simpson's tape, Grey?" JG - "No." JH - "I have a Simpsons pinball machine. When it pings, Homer says DOH!" JG - "Jack, it's getting old. Move onto another of your childhood hobbies. Actually, maybe stay on pinball." [Logan holds on for dear life and quickly reaches for the ropes, forcing the ref to break the hold. Nyce refuses to get off, and the ref gives a four count before Nyce breaks his hold. Nyce then picks up Logan and throws him into the ropes, hitting a sleeper dead in the middle of the ring. Logan fights to break it, but eventually Nyce brings Logan down to the ground.] JG - "Logan's back in trouble!" JH - "Just like..." JG - "Don't even think about it, Jack. If you don't say something new, you may as well walk out of here." JH - "I was going to say that Logan getting up is akin to pissing on a forest fire." [The ref lifts Logan's arm once, and it drops. The second time, and it drops again. The third time...but Logan raises his arm to stop the count. Logan slowly starts to get up to his feet, and begins an onsault of elbows in the midsection, but Nyce stops it with a double axehandle across Logan's back, dropping him to his knees. Nyce then picks up Logan and hits a T-Bone Suplex, followed by a Liger Bomb. Nyce goes for the cover... 1! 2! KICKOUT! Nyce grabs Logan and throws him into the ropes, and drops down to early for a back body drop, giving Logan enough time to hit a swinging neckbreaker. Logan and Nyce are both sprawled out on the mat, when Logan begins to get up, followed slowly by Nyce. Both men begin to slug it out wearily as they get to their feet, when Nyce is thrown into turnbuckle by Logan. Logan chases after Nyce into the corner with a big splash, but Nyce moves out of the way, sending Logan flying into the ring post, and out of the ring.] JG - "He flew right over the top!" JH - "The great white ninja. He can soar like an eagle, float like a butterfly and sting like a day's worth of chili." [Nyce quickly jumps out of the ring and hits a tombstone onto Logan from the outside as the ref counts to three. Nyce then picks up Logan and guerrilla presses him onto the steel gaurdrail as the ref counts to six. Nyce then throws Logan into the ring, breaking the count. Nyce climbs up onto the canvass, and is catapulted into the ring by Logan. Logan begins to go haywire over Nyce, diving ontop of Nyce and hitting an onsault of punches into the face. Logan picks up Nyce and hits a german suplex. Logan grabs Nyce again and hits a few body chops across Nyce's chest, followed by a kick to the midsection, then a reverse neckbreaker. Logan goes for the cover... 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Almost had him!" JH - "You think the pinball's getting old? What about your little saying there?" [Logan picks up Nyce and hits a tombstone. Logan picks up Nyce again and hits an atomic drop, followed with a swinging DDT. Logan goes for the cover again... 1! 2! KICKOUT! Logan grabs Nyce, and throws him into the ropes, but Nyce grabs onto the ropes and looks at the crowd as he gloats. Logan, however, charges at Nyce with a clothesline, sending Nyce over the ropes and back outside. Logan quickly grabs a chair and makes Nyce's face a permenant part of the chair. Logan then grabs Nyce and throws him head first into the steel steps. Logan throws Nyce back into the ring and Logan climbs up to the top rope. As Nyce gets up, Logan hits a mid air frankenstiener, sending Nyce down hard. Logan then goes for the cover... 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JH - "Logan's snapped! There's no stopping him when he's like this!" JG - "And, I don't think you want to get in his way, either." [Logan picks up Nyce, and throws him into the ropes, goes for a clothesline, but Nyce ducks under it. Logan then goes for a back body drop, but Nyce leapfrogs over him, stops and hits a small package... 1! 2! KICKOUT! Both men get up and begin to grapple. Nyce gets the advantage when he hits a quick body slam. Nyce then grabs Logan and Irish whips him into the turnbuckle. Nyce then walks over to the turnbuckle and starts to punch Logan in the head from the second rope. Nyce gets off Logan, as Logan falls to the canvass. Nyce moves in, but Logan catches him with a monkey flip. Logan flips over and drops Nyce with a clothesline. As Nyce gets up, Logan grabs him and hits the Dragonstrike Heart Punch. He covers... 1! 2! 3!] JG - "He got him!" JH - "Logan comes back, gets the win. What an animal. He really knows how to fight, eh Grey?" JG - "He sure does. Anthony Logan is an experienced ring vet, measuring up with some of the other greats." JH - "I wouldn't go that far, but he holds his own. Especially when he spazzes. That's funny." JG - "I..." JH - "Speaking of funny, what about this next match?" JG - "I was going to say something else to lead in, but I guess that'll do." JH - "Was I lying?" JG - "Robbie is a good wrestler. Pepper is also coming back to his former style."
