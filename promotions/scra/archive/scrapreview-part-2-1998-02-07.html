[SCRA] ScraPreview [2/4] (7 February 1998)

7 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:: Derek Mota [Cut to Derek Mota standing in a packed mall. Thousands of people are shuffling around him, nearly all of them wandering around aimlessly. Mota just looks at them with a disgusted look and begins to speak.] DM - ""You gotta hate this place. Everyone's freakin' brainwashed by the mass advertising. Look at that!" [The camera moves over to show about a million kids pouring into the Disney Store, and about the same amount pouring out, but covered in Mickey Mouse paraphenalia, an almost zombie-like look on their faces.] DM - "Makes ya sick, don't it? Ya work ta become an individual, and then everyone wants ta be like you. Take that Aladdin guy ... yeah, he don't belong in no SCRA ring. He should be the guy workin' at the Disney Store. Fit in nicely with all the other brain dead losers in there. Style, you gotta learn one thing, boy ... never trust Derek Mota. And now you say you wanna kill me? YOU WANNA KILL ME? Try it, boy. You're just gonna learn the hard way that I don't stay down. You could take a [BLEEP]in' shotgun and shoot me in the head, and I'd still find some damn way ta stay alive. And when you've given it your all, and you barely peel yourself off the mat ta cover me for the pin, and you realize that I'm already up on my feet, you'll know what it's like ta be facin' the toughest little bastard in the world. And somehow ... I don't think you're gonna like it." [Some fat guy wearing a Yoda t-shirt with the words "There is no try" isn't looking where he's going and bumps into Mota. Derek just stops talking for a second, and gives him a Superkick to the jaw, knocking him out cold.] DM - "Damn malls. Ya gotta hate 'em. Too many people. Kinda like it's gonna be tomorrow in SCRA. Got a battle royal with the top guys makin' it ta some sorta event. I think they called it the Longest Road in some other place I used ta be once. I been there. I made it. And I'm gonna make it this time too. Hardin, you may be a prick, but you're a damn good wrestler. "Cowboy" Ken Curtis, congrats on the win, but your time is numbered. Eddy Jacks, good reign, sorry about the loss. Other guys ... Nik Nyce, Chase Hunter, you're all good wrestlers. But you're gonna learn that when you're in the ring with Derek Mota ... You gotta take it to a new level." [A number of security guards are now swarming around Mota, quickly outnumbering him and "escorting" him out of the mall. Mota just takes a quick seconds and gives a sly smile to the camera, letting himself get carried out of the building.] JH - "Disney. What a joke. I bet they make billions of dollars off stupid kids trying to get a little doll. How is that a way to make money? Oh yeah, buy the Jack Hazard doll, hitting stores today!" JG - "Good plug, Jack. Really." JH - "I try. I get a cut of the profits with that sucker." JG - "Mota's size is an instant disadvantage, but if there's one small man who may have a chance to win this thing, it's Mota. His never give up attitude may help in a match like this, allowing him to hold on for a few extra spots. If he can convince himself not to go for the high flying moves, he may have a shot at the top five, but probably not the win." JH - "Those small guys are gonna get crunched. I can't wait to see the big boys toss them like a shot put." JG - "Shot puts are very heavy." JH - "Okay, like a golf ball." :: "Silverblade" Anthony Logan JG - "Anthony Logan hasn't been the same since his brother's win back at Rasslefest, but this may be his return to glory. With his stiff martial arts style, he'll be able to maintain his focus on one competitor and beat them like a brawler would with punches." JH - "There's no way he can focus on one competitor. With a ring full of other men, he's gonna get hit some way or another." JG - "You're probably right. But, he has an outside shot at winning this thing. Perhaps look for him as a dark horse." :: "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin [Fade in on a dark and smoky room. Mexican and Caucasian men are crowded around a pit watching two gamecocks tearing at each other. The cheers of the men rise with each flurry of feathers and hands holding bills of all denominations wave around the pit. Finally, one of the birds tears into the other and a handler rushes in -- a futile attempt to rescue his fatally-wounded fighter. A roar comes from the winning gamblers, while the losers grimace and wait for the next two combatants. "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin, wearing a black shirt and his stetson, emerges from the shadows and speaks over the din of the crowd.] JWH - "There ain't no winners or losers in cockfightin'... 'cept the hombres making the bets. The birds is just tryin' to survive. Hombres flock to Juarez ev'ry weekend fer these fights. They ain't any more legal here than in the States, but they's tolerated 'cause they's a lot o' pesos involved -- a good number o' which end up in the law's pocket to keep 'em lookin' the other way. I reckon as long as they's a desire fer bloodshed and a fight to the death, it'll keep on goin'." [Hardin sticks a cigar in his mouth, but doesn't light it.] JWH - "It ain't gonna be no dif'rent in the SCRA this week, 'cept yer gonna see a whole lot more roosters in that there pit. Some big... some with big spirits. But they all better damn well come with the will to survive... or they ain't gonna make it through the night. Yeah, ya might as well call _this_ a battle royal. We're doin' the work, the fans is gettin' their thrills, and Grey is the only one getting his pockets lined with pesos. I ain't never been a big fan o' battle royals, mainly 'cause I ain't got no friends to watch my back. But with title shots on the line, I reckon there ain't gonna be many hombres with friends in that ring. 'Cept possibly a fat ol' bird name o'... Eddy Jacks." [Hardin almost grins at the mention of the name. He removes the cigar from his mouth and glances at the ring, where two more birds are being prepped for a fight.] JWH - "Jacks... yer prolly wonderin' why I cost ya the belt. And Curtis... yer prolly wonderin' why I'd help ya win it. It ain't hard to figger out. Jacks, ya stuck yer nose in my business two weeks ago. I didn't ask fer ya to drag yer fat ass out to the ring, but ya couldn't resist, could ya? Ya think I needed help 'gainst two runts like Mota and Style? Well ya don't upset the Outlaw without payin' a price... and that price was yer shiny lil' hunk o' tin." [Hardin returns the cigar to his mouth and looks at the ring, where the birds are embroiled in a vicious fight.] JWH - "As fer you, Curtis, ya must be feelin' purty good 'bout yerself... until ya hear that lil' voice inside tellin' ya that the only reason yer wearin' the strap is cause I busted Jacks' head. Now why would I do that? 'Cause I know ya can't beat me when there is gold on the line. Last time didn't mean nothin'. Next time, it'll mean ever'thing. See Curtis, I'm plannin' on winnin' that battle royal. Then I'm loadin' up and comin' after ya. I'm on yer trail..." [There is a roar behind Hardin and he looks back to see a dark-skinned man lift a bloody mass from the pit.] JWH - "...and there ain't a damn thing ya can do 'bout it. [Fade.] JG - "Another favourite for this event is J.W. Hardin. He's got the size and the brawling, but lacks one important factor: the friends. People call these things free-for-alls, but you do need somebody to watch your back just in case. Hardin doesn't have that luxury and it may cost him when five guys gang up on him." JH - "Five guys won't be able to put the Outlaw out. Especially if it's five little guys like Mota and Style. I love these two, but the little terds can't compete with the big boys." JG - "We'll find out tomorrow night. Everybody gets mixed in, small and big alike, and all fight for the title." JH - "Title shot, you mean." JG - "Right." :: Rock Bottom Robbie [Robbie is walking along in a dark alley in his home town, Intercourse, Pennsylvania. He's wearing a leather biker jacket, which is unzipped, revealing a sweatshirt that says "Rock Bottom Never Felt So Good" and Robbie holding both the NA and TV Title Belt. He has on a pair of blue jeans, as well as a pair of cowboy boots on. As he makes his way down the ally, he sees a bum, stops, and hands him a twenty, telling the bum to find some shelter from the cold. Robbie continues along the way, occasionally kicking a rock or empty beer can.] RBR - "Well, it looks like my stay as the prestigious TV Title holder is over. Well boo f*$%ing hoo. I lost to Prodigy. I can't believe that I actually lost to Prodigy. No matter, what's done is done, and it now sets me back on the course to getting what I want so bad, the North American Title." [Robbie stops in the middle of the ally, noticing the fire escape, and quickly climbs up it, motioning for the camera crew to follow him up. Robbie makes his way to the top of the building, and looks around, revealing an awestrickening view that boggles the mind. He walks over to ledge and looks out at the tiny city, overlooking the bright lights of the rest of the world.] RBR - "Up here it's peaceful, you get a chance to think for a second. Smell the roses so to speak. Down there, there's confusion, mayhem, violence, all the good stuff that people thrive upon. That's where I will be tomorrow. Down in the pit, facing every single wrestler there is in this league, vying for a spot at the title. And I will make it. I don't settle for nothing but the best, and constantly strive to reach my goals. I will get the NA Belt back. I will not be eliminated from the battle royal until there is only 6 men left. I will unload a can of whoop a$$ all over 13 wrestlers, and won't stop until there are no more wrestlers left to beat. Why? Because when you've hit rock bottom, there's no where to go but up." [Robbie looks down, kicks a few pebbles, then climbs back down the fire escape as the screen fades out.] JG - "Robbie can be considered another dark horse..." JH - "How many favourites are you picking here? I think you've said everybody so far is a favourite or dark horse." JG - "So far, everbody has a legitimate chance. Robbie does due to his amazing brawling skills. Those might allow him to eliminate a few of his peers." JH - "Robbie can't eliminate a thing. He's got more bad habits than a dirty whore." JG - "I doubt that. He does have one or two in matches, but nothing too big." :: "Golden King" Aladdin Style [cene opens with Aladdin Style standing in front of a computer-generated backdrop of a golden chess piece - the king, of course - and the Golden King logo. The rest of the backdrop is a mix of dark blue and black moving like water over sand. Aladdin Style speaks.] AS - "Cowboy Ken Curtis, congratulations. When we first met in the middle of the ring, I told you that the next time one of us would be wearing the gold. I knew there was World Title material in you... just as I know it is in me. So, after I eliminate the rest of these peices of crap tomorrow night, I'll keep my other promise. Last time we met, it was the first match of my singles career. I told you then I didn't expect to win, just to learn. The next time, I promised, the result would be drastically different. And it will be, Cowboy. I'm going to take you apart and show the world why I am truly The Golden King. I'll be the smallest, most technically gifted World Champ the SCRA has seen since the Lifetaker days! Derek Mota, you know and I know we'll be meeting in the middle of the squared circle. This won't be payback night....but it'll be the start of something deadly! I've got big plans for you, little man! Plans that will make women faint, grown men weep and give little children nightmares. Oh, Derek Mota, such sites I have to show you. Such evil tortures I have to demonstrate. Mota, you will so regret the day you betrayed The Golden King. For years I've been saying "I'm not bigger than you...I'm not badder...I'm just so goddamn much better!" This time, Derek Mota, I've got you beat on bad intentions as well. I'm going to leave you for dead, marked man!" JG - "I don't think Style has too much of a chance to win this event. His small size is an immediate disadvantage. But, his main characteristic is technical wrestling. In a match like this, you can't use technical wrestling..." JH - "And shouldn't in any match. Period." JG - "...His secondary style is aerial moves. Again, you can't do that in a match like this. He's at a severe disadvantage. But, with his speed and hard work, he could pull out an upset and last into the later stages of the match." JH - "He'll be there in the later stages. His scrawny butt will be tossing other scrawny butts out, then he'll be left with the big boys. That's when he'll get tossed." JG - "It could turn out that way. We'll have to wait and see." :: Sgt. Pepper JG - "I don't think Pepper has a chance at this thing. He showed us in his match against Robbie that his brawling skills aren't up to par with the best of the best. His technical skills are of no use in a match like this. His size isn't all that great, so perhaps an early exit for Sgt. Pepper." JH - "Perhaps. Well, yeah. Early exit sounds good." :: Bobby McAllister [Bobby McAllister is alone in a gymnasium on the campus of Penn State University.] BM - "Here it is. Here's where I really got my start. Sure, I wrestled in high school, got myself a scholarship, but I didn't really know what I was doing. This is the place where I started to learn about wrestling, started to learn some technique, instead of just bulling my way through, hoping I wasn't going to get diqualified because I didn't know the rules. (starts chuckling) Those were the days. It's a heck of a lot easier to wrestle when you're not worrying about the rules. I did all right, I reached the championship match in my weight class in the Big Ten Tournament my senior year, but I couldn't pull out a win. I lost to the best wrestler I've ever seen, Robby Colson from Iowa. Last I heard, Colson was in jail for dealing drugs. That, of course, came after he won the gold in the Olympics but lost it after failing the drug test. Nobody thought it was suspicious that Robby had gone up two weight classes since beating me that last time. I know he wasn't on the juice when he beat me - he had such incredible technique, such knowledge of the sport. I'll never forget that night in the barracks, watching that gold medal match on that little twelve-inch television with Jack and the rest. I was so happy for Colson that night - and then I, and the rest of the world, found out the truth. I remembered those days in college, when everyone was in awe of Colson, talking about all the medals he would win. He could have won gold without the steroids, but he was the only one who didn't believe it. When I wrestle, I wrestle for all the Robby Colsons-to-be, to show them that steroids aren't the answer. I go into the ring clean, and I leave it clean, win or lose." [McAllister goes into the locker room and finds his old blue locker.] BM - "Stan Linst, 190 pounds, junior from Honesdale. Coach thinks he's going to win a national championship before he graduates, brought me here to have a little talk with him, keep him from becoming the next Robby Colson. I didn't know what to say to him. I couldn't keep my buddies away from drugs in high school, how the hell am I supposed to keep Linst off them now? All I could think of was to tell him about Colson, even though he's heard the story hundreds of times already." [McAllister goes back into the gymnasium.] BM - "Sunday night, I've got the biggest match of my life. The big battle royal at Winter Festival. I've got to come up big in this one, or I've got another wait in front of me before I get a shot at gold. This is a lot to drink in right now. I never figured I'd have a chance like this so early in my career, but now I've got to fight off the entire SCRA to get my shot. When I step into that ring on Sunday night, I'm not just getting in there for me. I'm not just getting in there for Victoria Glenn, or Jack Slaton. I'm also getting in there for Stan Linst, to show him the right way to take care of business. I may not be much as talking and persuading people, but maybe this will keep Stan Linst from becoming another Robby Colson. Or another Nik Nyce, for that matter. Now, I'm not saying that Nyce is on the juice - hell, I know that's not true. Yeah, he's scum, but he's not using steroids. Nyce, I don't think it's any secret that I'm heading straight for you at Winter Festival, and if I'm reading you right, you're coming straight for me too. You get your shot to try to erase your loss to me, and I get my shot at revenge for all the things you've said about my fiancee. There's a lot of shots at gold on the line on Sunday night, but between you and me, belts have nothing to do with it. When the two of us square off, pride's the only thing on the line. And when I've got a cause to fight for, I'm that much better. I know that you step up in big situations, but I'm betting that my cause is a hell of a lot better than yours. When it comes right down to it, you're just a selfish, pretentious piece of trash who isn't fit to lie in the alleys where I grew up. And I sure won't mind throwing you a beating in the process of eliminating you. Enjoy your dinner tonight, Nyce, because I've heard that hospital food ain't much better than Marine rations - and I can tell you that thsoe weren't very tasty." [Jack Slaton enters the gym.] JS - "Mac, it's time to go. You've got to catch that flight. Victoria's already at the airport." BM - "All right, Jack. Time to take care of business." JH - "Who are those guys?" JG - "Amateurs, Jack." JH - "Amateurs. Can't make it to the big time, eh?" JG - "As in amateur wrestling. You know, Greco-Roman?" JH - "Oh, right. The _other_ wrestling." JG - "Anyways, Bobby McAllister is also one of the ones who probably can't win this thing, but may place well. He's got the drive, as we've seen in recent weeks, but lacks the brawling. We could see his speed and versatility come in handy in this, but I don't expect a big win for him." JH - "Ever again." JG - "I didn't say that." JH - "Geez, you're pretty perceptive tonight, Grey. You can actually tell when I say something." :: "Perfectionist" Nik Nyce [The screen opens up to a third rate movie studio. The lighting is very awful, the props are far from real looking, and the staff in the studio looks like it's consistening of high school drop outs. "The Perfectionist" Nik Nyce comes into the shot wearing a flashy baby blue suit. He's got a pair of shades on and his hair is slicked back. ] NN - "Well I know everyone is wondering what I'm doing at this awful move studio. No I'm not taking a cheap role in a super low budget movie. I'm here to clarify a few things. First of all Chase Hunter, nice try but your stuff doesn't work on me little boy. Sure you can play word games with wrestlers names, animate yourself in a interview segment but Chase "I watch way too many movies" Hunter, your little stunt involving me had me laughing my butt off. Your litttle thing with the fake little house, the very cheap Nik Nyce look alike, oh and to top it off the mail box with my name on it. You see Hunter what you've done a long time before you decided to make you "mark" in wrestling." [Nik Nyce laughs] NN - "You see a few months ago I had a little man by the name of Dan Kauffman thinking that I broke in his house and conducted an interview. I even went to the extremes to make it not look totally like his home but he truely thought I for some reason would break into his home. Now I had my reasons for doing this and the main reason was to show the fans that their hero's mind was unstable. Hunter you on the other hand just do your little stunts to try and get some attention. Your wrestling skills can't get the attention so you have to animate your self or try and make your own little rip off of a movie." [a 5'6 little man interupts Nik Nyce] MAN - "I hear you talking about Chase Hunter. I directed several of his segments for SCRA. The man is a total choke that doesn't even have any acting talent to make things go smooth. He was constantly begging me to help him come up with ways to get him attention in SCRA. I was mainly joking with what I said but he actually went with some of the ideas I came up with." NN - "That's even worse. Chase Hunter needs a wanna be director to help him in SCRA. Chase Hunter you want to leave a mark on SCRA? Fine, you ever come near me in real life and not fantasy land, I'll make you into a slimely little mark on SCRA canvas. I don't need little tricks like you Hunter. Nik Nyce relies all on truth. When I say Victoria Glenn is a no good whore in SCRA, I have all the proof to back it up. It's not my fault little Bobby McAllister is in total denial. Now Sunday I'm stepping into the ring for the battle royal against some men. I've won a few battle royals in my short career but I'm not perfect at them and I really don't care to be. Battle royals don't get interesting till the last four or five men in the ring and thus sometimes put me to sleep while I'm waiting for some losers to get eliminated. Now I've handled battle royals many ways. Sometimes I was totally agressive and sometimes I just watched the idiots kncok themselves out while I wound up being the winner. It doesn't matter who's really in this thing because with the amount of people in this thing, anything can happen but one thing is sure, Nik Nyce is going to continue down the path of success in SCRA. Like it or not, I'm not going to be out of this place in a few weeks. Leaving so shortly would just be too easy for all of you. I can put up with the loser fans and lackluster wrestling facility in SCRA but the SCRA won't be able to handle "The Perfectionist" Nik Nyce." [Screen Fades] JG - "Again..." JH - "Oy! Just say who can win and who can't so you don't repeat yourself." JG - "Nik Nyce could do well, but won't win. He's got the technical skills, which are great, but are of no use in this match. Unless he can come up with some sort of style to counter the match style, I don't think he can win this one. Plus, he's got Bobby McAllister gunning for him."
