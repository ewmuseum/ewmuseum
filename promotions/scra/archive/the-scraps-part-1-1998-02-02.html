[SCRA] The Scraps [1/5] (2 February 1998)

The camera flashes around the crowd, searching for innovative signs and fans. Of course, they can't find any. Instead, the camera quickly moves to the fireworks exploding off the ramp and ringposts. As the opening theatrics die down, Grey and Hazard begin their verbal wars.] JG - "Welcome everyone to the Monday Night Scraps! This is SCRA!" JH - "When did we get all those fancy fireworks?" JG - "I don't know! We've got a big night on tap! The World title is on the line as the undefeated "Cowboy" Ken Curtis takes on Eddy "Flap" Jacks. That should be a great one." JH - "Great is an understatement. We're finally gonna see some wrestling main events instead of the junk we usually toss out. In Curtis and Jacks, we've got two of the best brawlers in the biz." JG - "We had that last week with Gangsta Impasse and Ken Curtis..." JH - "When are we gonna see Gangsta shovel that crap! That's gonna be one helluva sight!" JG - "That will also be later in the show. Derek Mota takes on "Silverblade" Anthony Logan later on tonight in what should be an outstanding matchup." JH - "These guy are two of my favs, but they do too much of that fancy high flying and speed stuff to be in the category of the real favs." JG - "One week removed from the tag championships, the Praetorian Guard attempt to rebound against the Siberian Express." JH - "Another good one. The Express keeps on tooting and the Guard keeps on, well, guarding, I guess." JG - "Prodigy and Robbie duke it out for the TV title..." JH - "It had to end somewhere. We've got all quality, then this junk. I guess you can't win 'em all, unless you're a Mexican immigrant who gets out of that country. Speaking of Mexicans, what happened to Morales?" JG - "Left the promotion in a fuss. Also up tonight..." JH - "Who needs Mexicans. Let them stay in their janitorial positions." JG - "I guess we're now hiring for new janitors. Anybody interested apply within. Later on in the evening, we've got Chase Hunter making his debut against "Golden King" Aladdin Style. Style's also got some interview time set up, so we'll see what he has to say." JH - "It's gonna be fun! He's pissed off at Mota. Seeing those two little guys go at it reminded me of the childhood days. Remember getting the dogs drunk and having them attack each other?" JG - "Uh...right. "Perfectionist" Nik Nyce takes on SCRA veteran, Sgt. Pepper. Pepper seems to be surging back towards the spotlight..." JH - "The Sarge ain't surging nowhere. He'll be back on job duty within the week." JG - "But, right now, we've got Phantom against Bobby McAllister! Let's go to Ed Jaspard for the intros!" ___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 1 ) | | \ | | Phantom vs. Bobby McAllister / |___ | | | | Writer: JB (_________________________________________________________________________ EJ - "YO! Whats up! This is Eddie "E-dog" Jaspard coming to represent two of our finest Cruisers!" JH - "Does he thinks he's a gangsta?" JG - "If he is he's doing a very bad impression, he doesn't even have any of the current lingo down." JH - "What makes you an expert at ebonics?" EJ - "My first boy comes from New York, New York weighing in at 224 lbs. acompained by Victoria...." [The Marine Hymn blares over the PA system is drowned out by cheers from the fans. Victoria Glenn walks out of the entrance area with Bobby McAllister following shortly behind.] EJ - "Here is Bobby McAllister!" [They walk down to ringside slapping hands with fans and posing for a picture for one fan. Bobby jumps to the ring apron holds the ropes for Victoria and then slingshots himself into the ring. He goes to each corner and raises his hands above his head.] EJ - "And his opponent, from the city of women who don't shave their armpits, Paris France, weighing in at 200 lbs. Phantom!!" [The lights dim as Overture from the Musical "Phantom of the Opera" plays over the sound system. Christine walks down alone and stands outside the ring. After a few seconds no one else comes out and the lights rise.] JH - "Dam Phantom! Always with his suprise entrances. Don't you think they suck Grey? (Hazard looks over at Grey.) Oh no its the Phantom! He abducted Grey!" [Sitting next to Jack is the Phantom. As McAllisters back is turned Phantom jumps out of the broadcasting booth jumps onto the ropes of the ring and hits a bulldog from the top rope. The match begins.] DING! DING! DING! JH - "What did he do with Grey?" JG - "I'm right here Jack." JH - "Where did you go?" JG - "What are you talking about Jack? I've been here the whole time!" JH - "No! Phantom was sitting next to me a little while ago!" JG - "Are you ok Jack? You look pale, maybe you should go backstage and splash some water on your face." [Jack gets up and leaves. In the match after connecting with the bulldog, Phantom executes a camel clutch on Bobby. Bobby's close to the ropes though and grabs them. Phantom gets up and kicks Bobby in the kidneys. The ref motions him back which he does reluclantly. Bobby gets up and the two look like they are about to lock up when Phantom kicks him in the stomach and then executes a neckbreaker. He goes for the pin... 1! KICKOUT!] JG - "Quick kickout by Bobby, this match has been mostly Phantom though." [Pause] JG - "Wow! It's so much nicer watching a wrestling match with out Jack by my side! I should talk my brother into firing Jack!" [Bobby gets up and this time they lock up, Bobby whips Phantom against the ropes, and executes a standing dropkick. He picks Phantom up and executes a fisherman's suplex for the pin... 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Hmm... maybe I was wrong, this is boring with out Jack's keen comments." [Jack comes walking back with a bounce in his step. A fan heckles him and gives him the bird.] JH - "I heard what you said about me Grey! That really hurts!" JG - "I didn't mean it Jack, it was a rash thing to say, forgive me?" JH - "No!" JG - "Why not?" JH - "'Cause your a moron!" JG - "Same old Jack (canned laughter echoes in the background.)" JH - "Grey didn't I tell you to stop playing with the sound machine?" [Bobby aplies a figure-four leg lock. Phantom starts rocking back and forth slowly getting enough momentum to turn Bobby over, forcing Bobby to release the hold. They both rise quickly. Bobby takes the advantage by grabbing Phantoms arm and putting it in an arm bar. Phantom kicks Bobby in the stomach, then executes a piledriver. He picks up Bobby throws him in the corner and charges in after him. Phantom hops onto the ropes and comes down behind Bobby. He executes his finisher the Dreamer. He tries to drag Bobby to the middle of the ring but Bobby grabs the ropes. Using the leverage of the ropes Bobby switches the move around and crushes Phantom into the turnbuckle. Bobby gets up onto the second turnbuckle and starts punching Phantom.] JH - "He's using a closed fist! Disqualify him!" JG - "Since when do you care about that?" JH - "Since its that punk Bobby doing it." [The crowd starts counting the punches. Phantom grabs Bobby and executes an inverted atomic drop.] JG - "Looks like Bobby won't be having kids anytime soon!" JH - "Good! That means Victoria can come to a real man like me." JG - "Careful saying that or Bobby might get angry." JH - "What could he do? He's lying in the ring holding his privates!" [Phantom picks up Bobby and executes a DDT. He goes to the top rope, stops on the top to rest a bit and then takes off with a splash. As he comes down Bobby raises his legs and Phantom collides with Bobby's knee. Bobby gets up wearily and hits an elbow drop. He then picks up Phantom and executes a power bomb he runs against the ropes and comes back with a Stinger Splash. He then climbs to the top rope and waves his arm in the air as a signal.] JG - "Here come's the Stars 'n' Stripes Somersault Splash!" JH - "About time this match ended." [Bobby hits it and hooks the leg for the pin. As he looks over he sees Nyce coming down to ringside. He gets up at the 2 count and tries to rush over but the ref holds him back. Nyce looks at Bobby smiles and starts talking to Victoria. Bobby kicks the ropes in rage. He turns around only to see Phantom moving in with a small package... 1! 2! REVERSAL! 1! 2! 3!] JG - "McAllister wins!" JH - "Bobby doesn't care about the win, he cares more about his women." [Bobby rushes out of the ring but Nyce has already taken off down the ramp. He runs after him with Victoria close behind.] JG - "McAllister breaks the losing streak and comes out with a victory over Phantom. Phantom seemed to be moving up and back to his former North American self." JH - "Different times, Grey. Phantom is about as low as you go these days." JG - "He is a shell of his former self, but picking it up quite nicely." JH - "The only thing he picks up is venereal diseases from that whore Christine." JG - "Shot down again?" JH - "Yes." [Burn by the Cure blares through the speakers, setting an eerie, troubled mood. The fans cheer anyway, knowing that the popular "Golden King" Aladdin Style will soon walk through the curtains. Moments pass, then The Golden King appears through the curtains to renewed enthusiam from the crowd. Explosions of pryotechnic action follow Style's every step as he walks towards the ring. Style is dressed in black dress pants, a black turtleneck and a long, black trenchcoat. His eyes are covered by sunglasses that reflect no light. He walks slowly, his face showing no reaction to either the crowd or the explosions of noise and light around him. He steps into the squared circle and walks toward the announcer. Taking the microphone, he turns and looks carefully at the fans on all sides of the arena. He raises the microphone slowly to his face.] AS - "Cut the crap." KS - "Pardon me?" AS - "I said, Cut the crap. Turn that damn music off and tell these piece of shit fans to shut the hell up." KS - "Err....." AS - "I've got a few things to say about that dead man, Derek Mota. What the hell does he think he's doing, stabbing me in the back? ME! Doesn't he know what happens to people who call out the Golden King?" KS - "I'm sure he ..." AS - "Shut up. Just shut up. Apparently I didn't make myself clear enough when I tore apart Anthony Logan. Derek Mota, you are so bloody dead, they've already written you're obituary. You just don't jump on the Golden King. He's not a nice person. He's a cold-blooded killer. I'm a surgeon, the best technical wrestler the SCRA has ever seen. Why am I called the Golden King?" KS - "Ah, when you're brother started his campaign against you, he called you Gold's King..." AS - "And why did he do that?" KS - "He had a chess motif.." AS - "That's right! It was genius against genius, mind against mind. He found out, just like Mota's going to find out, that I'm the deadliest mind in this sport. Logan's my brother, so I didn't destroy him. Mota, you don't get the same curtosey. No mercy, Derek Mota. I'm going to leave you for dead." KS - "But why turn away from the fans?" AS - "I... I'm sorry. The fans, they were against me when I first came here. But I turned them around. I brought them over to my side. I did it by honesty and hard work. I said I was the best, and I was. I tore apart all their favorites, until the fans knew I was the real thing. I still am. But, even when they hated me, I didn't cheat. I fought hard and won. I earned their cheers. When I came back from my injury with everyone behind me ... well, it felt so good." KS - "They are still behind you." AS - "No. I don't want them behind me. I told you, they cheered me, despite my arrogance, because I was the best and backed up everything I said. I was a CLEAN wrestler, even as a heel. This time....this time I won't be clean. I'm going to destroy Derek Mota. I'm not out to beat him, like I did my brother. I'm going to destroy him. I won't be a clean wrestler anymore. As I was the most gifted technical wrestler before, now I'll be the deadliest cold-hearted surgeon. What I'm about to do doesn't deserve cheering. I don't expect them to respect me for what I'm going to do to that peice of crap. I wouldn't respect them if they did. I'm going to do evil things to him. This is fair warning to you, the SCRA, the fans, and most importantly to Derek Mota. Aladdin Style's out for Mota's blood, and he doesn't care how he gets it. Mota, you are a dead, dead man." [With that, Style throws down the microphone and leaves the ring. He walks slowly down towards the exit. There is no music and no pyrotechnics for him as he leaves. He stops just before the curtain and turns around. Again he looks at the fans. He wears no expression, but the fans close enough to see his face can see the tears flow down from under his sunglasses. Finally, he turns and walks through the curtains.] JH - "He's back, baby!" JG - "Aladdin Style seems to have stayed the same, though. He's the same as always." JH - "He went soft. You know it, I know it. But, now he's back!" JG - "And wants Derek Mota. I'm sure we'll be seeing those two collide in the near future. But, now we've got Sgt. Pepper and Nik Nyce!" JH - "A nice squash for the Perfectionist. Sgt. Pepper's gone even softer than before with his music. Beethoven? What kind of person would listen to that?" JG - "Me, for one. I'm sure many other fans listen to classical music, too." JH - "Woss wrestling fans. That's a sight." JG - "Let's go to Ed Jaspard for the intros!" -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
