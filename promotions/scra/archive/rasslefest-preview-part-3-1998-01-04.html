[SCRA] Rasslefest Preview [3/3]

4 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
!~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~! |Dog Collar Match: Anthony Logan vs. Aladdin Style| !~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~! JG - "The two brothers collide in what could be the best match of the night. Style and Logan want to kill each other." JH - "There are other good matches, the two cowboys, the tag team ladder match, but I agree with you. With the animosity between these two, it will carry this one over the edge." JG - "Two men who hate each other will be strapped in 10 feet away from each other. Sort of ironic, don't you think?" JH - "This is booking I like, Grey. Stick to mad men in a cage, two morons in a barbed wire ring, two enemies in a dog collar. That's good action, none of the Pros/German Eagles crap." JG - "This one should be great. Let's look back at how everything came to be." [The video begins rolling, starting at Fatal Fall. The Pros/House of Lords matchup plays. Gore goes back to his corner grabbing a chair. Tavis is still trying to get to Gore, but the ref is holding him back. On the inside Grant is still dominating. He whips Styles into the ropes and runs into the other side ropes, where Gore is waiting with the chair. He nails Grant with it, Styles rolls up the unconsious man. The ref gets back into the ring.] JH - "Get in there ref! Count for the new champs!" JG - "1......2.....3! House of Lords are the new tag team champs!" JH - "Finally! At long last, a good tag team champion in this league!" [The Activists from Hell run down to ringside. They whisper something to the referee who looks astonished.] JH - "What are those two idiots doing?" JG - "I don't know, but the referee wants to say something. He is getting Fetters to do it." TF - "The referee has just been informed that this is not the real Aladdin Styles!" JH - "What! You are kidding!" [Aladdin Styles comes running out, freeing himself of ropes which apparently kept him backstage. He runs at the imposter Styles who jets out of the ring. The imposter rolls under the ring as Styles gives up chase.] JG - "Who was the imposter? Why did he tie up Styles? What did the Activists from Hell have to do with it?" JH - "All these questions answered, tonight on 20/20." TF - "This match will continue with the real Aladdin Styles!" [The House of Lords seem to be exchanging heated words with the Activists, who are apparently responsible for the mix up. The Activists grin and walk back to the locker room.] JG - "I guess the Activists had something to do with it! They did want revenge against the House of Lords, but who is the imposter?" JH - "Let's get Leslie Nielson out here!" [The House of Lords still look stunned. The Pros see this and attack the team. Tavis grabs Gore and bodyslams him while Grant clotheslines Styles over the top ropes. Styles lands hard on the outside. He looks under the ring.] VO - "An imposter burst onto the scene, but with an unknown purpose. The tag team scene shuddered at the possible effects of this new threat to the champions. Yet, the House of Lords seemed confident in their new challenge, until they found out who it was." [The fateful footage of November 17, as Logan stands in the middle of the cafeteria over his fallen brother.] AS - "(coughing and weezing)Checkmate, my ass. Take off your damned mask, kickboxer! Who the hell are you?" [Silverblade removes his mask revealing a face far too similar to Style.] S - "I am the Blade. I am Anthony Logan. I am your brother..." VO - "And with those few words, two men's careers took a turn down a winding path leading to each other. Logan had to finish off Style..." [With a final thrust, Logan snaps the table in half over Style' leg. Logan acts quickly, getting on the floor and applying a figure four. Style cannot take the pain any longer and gives in.] JG - "It's over! Anthony Logan has defeated his brother!" JH - "This is unbelievable." [Logan leaves, tossing a few extra chairs at the bloodied body of Aladdin Style. The medical staff rushes in, placing the body of Style on a stretcher. They carry him off fast.] VO - "...both physically and emotionally. He accomplished the first task, sidelining Style for a month with a knee injury. Yet, the second step required more time, more effort. He had to dismantle the adopted brother, Jason Gore." [Logan's revealing interview from December 8 begins playing.] AS - "Brother, I have taken your mobility. I have caused you some of the extrodinary pain that you had visited upon me. However I am not finished with you. I will not be happy just battering your body. I will destroy your mind as well, by ending your partner's career. I am going to visit pain upon him unimaginable to you as you watch helplessly from your chair. Your adopted brother will not replace me Styles! I will not allow it! Gore! You want me, come and get me. I listened to your drivel, you gnat. The Golden Rook has pieced himself together, but I know that the mortar is still weak - the cracks still line your structure. You challeged me Rook, though you had not concept what you were getting into. Come to me Rook, come feel the Blade again. The Blade has demanded another sacrifice and I will take a piece of Styles while I destroy you. Come Gore... face your destiny...." [This quickly turns into the Logan/Gore match. Logan forces Gore to the corner and irish whips him, but Gore only makes it half-way and falls face first on the mat. Logan forces Gore to get to his feet and picks up the injured leg. Logan pulls his fist back and *WHAMMMMM!!!* Logan nails the heart punch on the knee of Jason Gore. Gore reiks in pain. The paramedics begin to run out at ringside.] JG - "They're going to stop the match, Jason Gore is in serious pain." JH - "Look at these fools with their stretchers. It was just like ten seconds ago when they were out here rescuing Ed from the leather pants." JG - "Anthony Logan is dangerous. Just look what he's doing to the knee of this man." [Jason Gore tries to say something to the referee, but Logan inteferes before anything is said. Logan picks up Gore by the hair and hits the devistating heart punch.] VO - "Seemingly, Logan's plan for elimination was complete. But, Aladdin Style recovered quickly. He challenged his brother. Two men who hate each other will be strapped together in a dog collar match!" !~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~! |Ladder Match: Activists from Hell vs. Praetorian Guard| !~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~! JG - "The two top tag teams in the sport today face off with the titles on the line, although in a strange fashion. The Activists and Guard will be in the ring, with a ladder and three titles suspended above the ring!" JH - "Three titles, eh? So, the Guard can grab all three and give one to Marty for a great job, right?" JG - "I think the third belt will have to be returned." JH - "Either way, Guard's my pick." JG - "Let's take a look at how this unfolded." [Clip from November 10, when the Guard won the titles. Naugrim sneaks in behind and rolls up Styles.] JG - "One....Two....Three! New tag team champs! We have new tag team champs!" JH - "What a surprise! The match of the night ended with a screwy rollup." [The Activists leap over the barrier, entering the ring. The stare at the fallen House of Lords, but soon turn their attention to the Praetorian Guard who hoist the belts up high. The House of Lords exit the ring, running to the locker room for the imposter. The Activists and Guard exchange a few words.] JG - "The Activists are not going after the House of Lords! They are staying with the Guard! You know what this means, don't you?" JH - "It proves that the Activists are not only wimps for not fighting the House of Lords but stupid for picking a fight with the entire House of Pancakes." JG - "No! The imposter Aladdin Styles was NOT the Activists from Hell's idea! It must be somebody else!" JH - "Why were they after the House of Lords, then?" JG - "The tag team straps! The Activists are going for the champs!" JH - "Power crazy bastards." [The Activists exit the ring after their brief showdown and being walking back to the locker room.] VO - "Two genuine individuals seemingly possessed by the need for tag team gold. Yet, they had to work their way up to the title shot. And, even then, it was a long ride." [The Pep Rally from November 17 begins playing. Kil Jaeden whips Baker into the ropes, attempting a clothesline, but Baker jumps up with a crucifix. Baker can't pull Jaeden down, but Jaeden staggers towards the ropes.] JG - "He can't get the crucifix down! Baker is getting dangerously close to being eliminated." JH - "Again!" JG - "The two are walking towards the ropes. Baker can be put over at any moment now." [Finally, Baker is able to fall to the mat, but instead of pinning Jaeden, Jaeden flies over the top, landing on the apron on the other side, as the two were so close to the ropes. Baker frees himself quickly and dropkicks Jaeden to the outside, eliminating him.] JG - "Joshua Baker wins! Joshua Baker wins!" JH - "No! They weren't supposed to be in this match! It's not fair!" VO - "Joshua Baker won the match and seemingly a title shot." [December 1, the day the Activists won the gold. Both men slide across the ring, Naugrim being urged by his partner and managerial staff, Baker being urged by screaming fans and a partner who is bouncing up and down on the ropes, itching for a tag. Naugrim is able to make the tag to Baer. Baer sports an evil grin as he walks over to Baker, ready dish out more punishment. With a final thrust, Baker dives and tags Rage. Rage, already bouncing, springboards himself to the top rope and bounces off with a missile dropkick. Baer falls hard to the mat as Rage continues running around the ring, meeting a fallen Baer in the center of the ring with a legdrop.] JG - "Rage is fired up tonight!" JH - "No kidding, Grey. But, isn't he always fired up? The guy has more highs than Rush." JG - "That's an insult, Jack. You know Zak Rage is not even close to the same class as Rush." [Rage screams to the fans, who all are standing and loving the show Zak Rage is putting on for them. Rage lifts Baer to his feet and brings him towards the turnbuckle. Rage grabs a hold of the ropes, leaps up and catches Baer in a flying headscissors. With the spinning momentum, Rage sends Baer crashing into the turnbuckle. This ends the momentum and Rage drops to the mat, able to perform a slower, modified hurricarana into a victory roll.] JG - "Here's the pin!" JH - "Come on Klondike!" JG - "One.....Two.....Three! He got him! We have new champs!" JH - "Get up, Baer!" JG - "What a move! I have never seen anything like it in my life. Throughout my many years of wrestling, I don't think anything has come close to that." VO - "The Activists won the belts and soon used their power to control the title scene. The belts did not last long around the waists of the Activists, instead, they went to UNICEF. And, the Guard took exception to this. They lost the belts to a team who did not deserve a title shot. And, the controversy continued. But, it should all be cleared up as the Activists and Guard lock up one last time in the ring, with a pair of ladders leading to the SCRA Tag Team titles!" [As the montage fades out, sounds of the various speeches from both sides begins playing.] ZR - "PG......[smirk].......that's Pretorian Guard to you civilians.... your reign will soon be over and like every good king; it's gonna be time to give up your throne." TN - "And...puff...here we are....urgh....Achilles to the Activist's Paris....urgh...aye, 'tis a fine state of affairs...urgh...to be the titan and bereft of cares....grunt...." JB - "I also mentioned our journey is lateral to the elections. First are the primaries and then the major vote. Well.....it's voting time Guard and as I sit here today and my momma is in the other room.... [he shakes his head].......it's The Activists turn to win this election." !~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~! |Nathan Flash vs. Johnny Destiny| !~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~! JG - "The two former partners collide in what should be a great matchup. Destiny wants to solidify his standing atop the league and defeat his former partner, Nathan Flash." JH - "But, Destiny is rusty. He hasn't been in the ring for a while, save his quick defeat of Gangsta Impasse. Nathan Flash will take it to Destiny quickly and painfully." JG - "Who really knows what's going to happen with these two. All I know is that it will be a high flying contest with both men giving every ounce of strength." [The eventful night of Fatal Fall begins playing. It is in the middle of the ring 1 rumble when Destiny was supposed to come out. With a big thrust, Jacks and Silverblade push Gangsta over the top. They celebrate for a moment, but this ends when Silverblade pushes Jacks into the corner. He starts throwing punches. Jacks fires back, reversing their places. Naveed comes on the stadium speakers.] JG - "Here comes Johnny Destiny! He must be fighting anyways." JH - "That isn't Destiny! It's Nathan Flash! Nathan Flash!" [Flash walks down the aisle with an evil grin.] JG - "I am getting something from the back. What! Nathan Flash has turned on Johnny Destiny! Nathan Flash has turned on Johnny Destiny! Before this rumble, Flash attacked Destiny!" JH - "Finally. Destiny was a bum who was skidding faster than a truck with a load of chickens." JG - "Chickens?" JH - "I don't know! I froze!" VO - "Some say it was insanity. Some say it was Destiny. Either way, Nathan Flash did in fact turn on his long time partner to pursue his own dreams." [Flash celebrates by himself in the ring amidst a chorus of boos. He exits the ring fast, encountering a Johnny Destiny on crutches.] JG - "What is Destiny doing? He is going to injure himself more!" JH - "Get him Nate!" JG - "No! This is horrible! Flash is attacking an injured man! This could end Destiny's career." JH - "Good, then he can manage Flash and see the pain and suffering of not being able to compete." [Flash kicks the crutch out from underneat Destiny, bending Destiny to one side. Flash hits Destiny with an axehandle to the back which floors Destiny. Flash picks up one of the crutches and breaks it over Destiny's right leg.] JG - "The horror!" JH - "Just keep it coming, Nate. End that punks career!" [Flash stops the attack when he sees security running down the aisle. Flash breaks through them all, running backstage. Destiny is carried of in a stretcher.] VO - "Flash sidelined Destiny for two months, seemingly ending the little Canadian's career. But, it did not turn out that way." [Maxx rolls Destiny on top of Impasse as Morales jumps off the apron. 1! 2! 3!] JG - "New North American Champion." [The shot goes to later on that night.] NF - "Johnny, Johnny, Johnny. What a triumphant return. You come back, you get a title shot....and you win. That's justice for you. Well, it looks like it's me and you at Rasslefest." [Johnny Destiny asks for a mike.] JD - "Revenge is oh so sweet." NF - "Johnny, I'm sick of you, I'm sick of this bullshit, and last off, I'm sick of being second in line. Johnny, I know we're not the best of friends anymore, but I have a challenge to make to you." JD - "Hey, I already have my match, what else could you possibly want?" NF - "Three simple words.... Loser.....Leaves.....Town." JG - "What?" NF - "That's right Johnny, a loser leaves town match. Now, we'll see who the number one superstar in the SCRA is. And that my friend is me." JD - "Nathan, you want it.....you got it! All I have to say is...well, you're gonna regret this decision." NF - "Not this time, my friend. I've beaten you once, I'll beat you again. Johnny, you should've stayed with those NCW jobbers." JD - "Might I remind you that you were kicked out of the league by those NCW jobbers?" NF - "If I beat you...and I lost to them....that means you're at the bottom of the pool on this one." VO - "The two former partners will collide once and for all in a loser leaves town match with the title on the line!" !~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~! |Lucifer vs. Eddy "Flap" Jacks| !~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~! JG - "The main event, hyped for over 3 months!" JH - "And lost heat about 2 months ago. This one's gonna be a squash." JG - "Jacks should put on a good performance. Lucifer has a knack for coming up big." [The first video segment begins at Onset of Fury. Two hooded men, one short and one much larger, are rushing towards the ring. One hammers Kil Jaeden with a chair, knocking the large man out. The larger thug goads Lucifer to come out of the ring, kicking the pained Kil Jaeden so as to further anger the Dark One.] JH - "This is unexpected! Who are these guys?" JG - "If we could just zoom in a little...there's an initial on their pullovers...I can't make it out, though." JH - "Looks like Jacks is getting some revenge here." JG - "What does Jacks have to do with it?" JH - "Maybe he and Lucifer were having problems. Who knows?" [Lucifer shouts something back at the two men, but is unaware of the status of Lifetaker. The little big man hits a quick hip toss, then mounts the top rope to launch himself onto Lucifer. As he is about to do so, one of the two hooded men yanks him off the turnbuckle and onto the stone floor of the arena. The larger man powerbombs him through a table, knocking him unconscious. The ref rushes to the side of the ring to shout something at the two men, who shake their heads and continue to pound Lifetaker.] JG - "What is this? Get them out of here!" JH - "The ref's powerless to do anything about this, Grey. It's perfectly legal for them to stand outside the ring. Just like it's perfectly legal for me to park in handicapped parking spaces." JG - "That's not legal." JH - "How do you figure? They sometimes park in our spaces." [Lucifer finally climbs out of the ring and charges into the two men. He seizes the initial advantage and throws the larger man over the metal fence and into the fan section. The shorter man, however, skillfully shoots out and takes Lucifer down with a brilliant wrestling move. Lucifer struggles with him on the ground but is unable to take the advantage. Meanwhile, the ref continues to shout at the combatants.] JG - "He hasn't started the count! What's going on?" [Lucifer struggles with man and succeeds in removing the thug's hood. Beneath the hood lies the face of an attractive, blonde-haired man with a long, droopy moustache. Lucifer, now completely enraged, lands a brutal blow that bloodies the short man's face. He throws him into the fence, picks up the wearied Lifetaker, then throws him back into the ring. As he is about to the enter the ring, the larger thug, now out of the fan section, charges Lucifer and slams his head into the scorekeeper's bell. He then rolls Lucifer back into the ring.] JG - "Both these guys are completely worn out! It's like they had to wrestle two matches." [As Lucifer and Lifetaker struggle to regain their feet inside the ring, the two thugs jog off to the dressing room.] VO - "The tie was almost broken, but not quite. The two hooded men were not sent by Jacks, or so it seemed." LS - "There's no bitterness here. Lucifer's free to train at our gym anytime. He just hasn't wanted to work with us. He does his own things, I guess." VO - "Then, Jacks pulled his trump card." [Eddy Jacks grabs the mike from MacDonald.] EJ - "I don't need anyone else now, eh! Everyone hear that? I'm self-made to the end. That goes for Lucifer and Kil Jaeden and whoever else thinks they can trust me. Lucifer, you're big and bad but destined to fall to me, eh." [Jacks rips off his shirt and begins to make his mammoth, sloppy pecs dance. Baer says something incomprehensible to Savarese. Surefoot smiles at the camera. MacDonald, mike in hand, continues prattling on about the HoP. Naugrim's gaze remains set directly on the camera, never flinching. This scene lasts but a moment more, with the newly-reborn HoP walking out of the ring and towards the dressing room.] VO - "Jacks brought out the House of Pancakes, his answer to the power of Lucifer. The tie was broken, forever." [Lucifer and Jacks are still throwing wild punches. Jacks is backed up in the corner, though. Lucifer lunges at him with a punch, but Jacks ducks out of the way. Lucifer falls into the exposed turnbuckle. Jacks sees this and grabs Lucifer's head, ramming it into the turnbuckle. Lucifer's head starts squirting blood. The Activists reach under the ring and grab the first aid kid. They put a swab on their sign and push it up to Lucifer's head. This stops the bleeding somewhat. However, Jacks sees the opportunity and rams Lucifer's head hard in the sign.] JG - "Jacks is splitting Lucifer wide open. The Activists can't do anything about it!" JH - "They can and did. They helped Eddy cut Lucifer even more by hoisting the sign up." [Lucifer falls down on the mat, blood squirting out of his head. Jacks jumps on Lucifer's back. He attempts to do this again, but Lucifer spins over and nails Jacks with a punch to the face. Lucifer rolls over and stands besides Jacks, hitting a sidewalk slam. Lucifer goes off the ropes and hits a legdrop.] JG - "Lucifer has really come alive as of late. As before, it looks like the blood has ignited something in him to fight harder." JH - "Because he is a freak. The blood coming out does something to his brain. Ask Rush, he is also one of those freaks who likes the pain." [The shot goes to later on in the night, when Jacks and Lucifer were almost through. Jacks had switched his moves into an STF. Lucifer was pretty much out, so Jacks let go of the big man. He picked up Lucifer and set him up for a bodyslam when Lucifer slid out of Jacks arms. Lucifer fell to the ground. Jacks looks bewildered and simply walks over to Lucifer, bending over to pick him up again. Lucifer wakes up, though and grabs Jacks arms. Lucifer executes a perfect monkey flip, sending Jacks sailing high over the top ropes.] JG - "I guess pigs can fly!" JH - "Not funny, Grey. Jacks is just a little large, that's all." [Lucifer gets up, looking unharmed and raises his arms in victory. The Activists toss their signs in the ring and join their fellow stable member. The three Pit of Damnation members celebrate in the now almost empty first ring. The three look together, but when Kil Jaeden runs down, he pushes the two Activists out of the way to get to his master. He lifts Lucifer up in victory. The Activists try and place a sign in Lucifer's hand, but Lucifer does not take it. Lucifer and Kil Jaeden exit the ring with the Activists lagging behind.] JG - "Lucifer will get his second shot at Lifetaker and the World Title at Rasslefest, providing Lifetaker still has the belt. If not, Lucifer will face whoever the champ is." VO - "Lucifer seemed to be ready for his title shot, ready for Jacks. Yet, Jacks was ready for Lucifer." [Shot cuts to October 27. Seeing the opportunity, the House of Pancakes rush to ringside. They quickly side step the two on the outside and head straight at Lucifer. The House of Pancakes set up for a monstrous move. Klondike puts on his Klondike clutch as Jacks climbs to the top. Just before Jacks leaps off, Klondike dives out of the way. Jacks lands firmly on Lucifer's back with Pancake Poison.] JG - "Oh no! Lucifer is out! Kil Jaeden is getting attacked and cannot help his mentor!" JH - "This is some pretty good fighting by House of Pancakes. That Marty MacDonald is one smart cookie." JG - "Lucifer is being left for dead in the ring, Kil Jaeden is down for the count because of the Heart Punch, this is disgusting." VO - "Jacks had injured Lucifer. He took care of his challenge for the title, and with 2 months to spare. Yet, Lucifer came back and in a big way." [Cut goes to Lucifer bursting through the ring on the Scraps and staring at Jacks.] VO - "These two mammoths will collide at Rasslefest, only at pay per view!" JG - "Folks, that's all the time we have for tonight. Make sure you buy Rasslefest on January 4! It's only available on pay per view! Good night!" -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
