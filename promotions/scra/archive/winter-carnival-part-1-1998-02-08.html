[SCRA] Winter Carnival [1/4] (8 February 1998)

8 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens up in darkness. A single spotlight shines down on the ring, highlighting the center which is filled with the ring bell. The bell rings... DING! DING! DING!] JG - "This ceremony helps purify the spirits around the games, making sure they are noble, right..." JH - "And all that jazz." [The spotlight lights up the entire ring. Except, the turnbuckles are flat on the mat instead of in their erect position. The man ringing the bell stands up and begins screaming at the top of his lungs.] JG - "This scream and the subsequent raising of the turnbuckles will prepare the ring for what's to come later." [Four men, each in the corner, start to lift the turnbuckles, each labouring for an extra amount of time for effect instead of necessity. As the turnbuckles rise, Eddy Jacks begins walking down the aisle.] JH - "What's he doing here?" JG - "You'll see." [The turnbuckles are raised. Jacks climbs through the ropes and takes his place in the middle of the ring wearing the typical sumo wrestling attire.] JH - "That's one huge butt." [Jacks claps his hands together, lifts his left leg up, then stomps down, the crowd screaming as he does. Jacks repeats the process with the other leg, again, receiving loud responses from the crowd.] JG - "The crowd urges on Jacks as he attempts to crush the spirits and make the games safe." [Suddenly, out of the top of the hills, President Grey walks out.] JH - "I thought the emperor was supposed to come down." JG - "Closest thing we've got." [As the "emperor" sits in his ringside seat, a choir begins to assemble in the middle of the ring as Jacks leaves.] JG - "The choir is going to sing Ode to Joy. It's comprised of the many different aspects of the wrestling fans." [As Grey said, a wide variety of people make up the choir. A small child sits in the front, wearing a Chase Hunter t-shirt. Beside him, a sligthly older child, probably in his teens, sits wearing a "Hardin 6:66" t-shirt. Behind the two children stand two older men. One has an unshaven face, a giant beer belly and pants pulled down an inch too low. The other adult, probably mid 20s, early 30s, stands cleanly shaved with normal clothes.] JH - "Our beloved fans. What would we do without them?" JG - "The choir's going to sing Beethoven's Ode to Joy as the parade of nations comes into the arena." [The choir, extremely aided by microphones, begins to sing an off pitch, out of synch tune as the "nations" come in. England, comprises of Sgt. Pepper and one half of the Pros in Walt Jabasco, enters the stadium, both men holding the flag together. Tulkas Naugrim enters waving the flag of Finland, its lone representative. The German Eagles enter, waving the flag of Germany proudly, possibly better than any others, and the tag belts on their waists. Icelander bears the flag of Iceland, but trips on his way in. Russia's Alexander Gudonov waves the Russian flag, followed by the Siberian Express. Blizzard tries to keep up, but the Express keeps pushing him back. "Cowboy" Ken Curtis, waving the United States of America flag, enters the arena, soon followed by the largest delegation of wrestlers. Rock Bottom Robbie stands at the front right beside the Activists from Hell, who carry flags of a different sort, Greenpeace. Grant follows behind the Activists, then Bobby McAllister and Chase Hunter. Phi Theta Kappa and Doug Adams walk in next, but soon forget why they are there and sort of trail off to the side. Gangsta Impasse enters, soon followed by "Perfectionist" Nik Nyce. "The Iconoclast" Sean Watts walks in beside Bill Baer as they discuss how hard work is overrated. "Hot Stuff" Mike Justice takes up the near to last spot, flexing his muscles on the way in. And, finally, "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin walks in, cowboy hat pulled over and not really into the festivities. Followed by the USA, the host nation, Canada enters as Eddy "Flap" Jacks waves the flag. The two united brothers, "Silverblade" Anthony Logan and "Golden King" Aladdin Style, march next. Prodigy walks behind them, along with "Haida Warrior" Gwaii Edenshaw who is trying to sneak a peak at Black Kat. Derek Mota takes up the rear for the Canadian squad.] JG - "There are all the nations!" [Suddenly, all hell breaks loose as the nations rest. Nik Nyce moves ahead to Bobby McAllister, attacking him. Icelander drops the Iceland flag and runs to Russia with his partner, only to be attacked by the Siberian Express. J.W. Hardin also deports to Canada, attacking Eddy Jacks and the flag.] JG - "No! The opening ceremonies are ruined! Somebody stop this monstrosity!" [An overhead shot of the park comes into the picture, revealing a mass brawl. The artifical snow machines work hard to keep the snow pumping over the ring. It pans around the stadium, seeing the ring surrounded by large snow banks. A few fans sit on the other side, but not many. Most walk around the grounds to various other attractions the league set up. The camera cuts into the broadcast table, inside a snow bank, nonetheless, where Grey and Hazard sit.] JG - "Hello fans and welcome to SCRA Winter Carnival! It's been a great day, with lots of fun activities for the entire family." JH - "But, it all pales in comparison for what we have upcoming. Two battle royals and the Activists getting embarassed!" JG - "That could happen. We've got the singles battle royal later on in the show with the top five receiving World title shots, when to be decided tomorrow, and the next eight being placed in the North American draw." JH - "The Longest Road." JG - "So you do know who NLWP is!" JH - "No. Who are they?" JG - "The originators of the Longest Road!" JH - "No, I thought SCRA was." JG - "(sighs) We've got the tag battle royal coming up, too. The top three face off tomorrow night for when their title shots will be." JH - "What competition that one has." JG - "But, to kick it all off, we've got the Activists returning to SCRA to take on the Praetorian Guard! Should they win, they stay in SCRA and face the German Eagles tomorrow night. Should they lose..." JH - "No more Rage and Baker! Woohoo!" JG - "We'll see what happens. Let's go to Ed Jaspard for the intros." __ __ _______________________________________________________________ | |__| | / ] Match 1 | | | |/ / Activists from Hell | | | / / vs. | ` ' \_ Praetorian Guard \ / | Writer: Jon Grey \_/\_/ \____|____________________________________________________________ [Stripper music comes over the sound system as Ed Jaspard comes out from behind the curtain, dressed in a g-string and leather knee high boots. His belly is hanging way out in front as he enters the ring. But, as he bends to go through the ropes, his g-string ripped. As Ed starts the announcements, he tries to cover it up.] EJ - "Cut the music! Um, this matchup is uh, scheduled for one fall with a 40 minute time limit. Coming down the aisle first, from Minneapolis, Minnesota, and weighing in at a total combined weight of 520lbs., they are the Activists from Hell!" [As Ed says Activists from Hell, he holds his hands high in the air, showing the world his crack. Down Rodeo comes over the PA as the Activists come out from behind the curtain, Baker with arms full of fliers and Rage with a sign saying "NO MO' ACID SNOW!" Baker starts handing the fliers out to the fans in the first and second rows. They get to the ring as Rage jumps to the top turnbuckle, holding up the sign. Jaspard is between the ropes talking to Jon Grey.] EJ - "So, could you get me a new pair of a size 52 waist g-strings?" JG - "Uh, Ed, the people can hear you." EJ - "Wonderful. Just wonderful. Well, their opponents hail from Charlotte, North Carolina and Helsinki, Finland, they weigh in at 596 pounds, they are the Praetorian Guard!" [The Dance of the Sugarplum Fairies comes over the PA but the HoP are nowhere to be found. The Guard come out from underneath the ring with chairs. Baer hits Baker while Naugrim hits Rage.] DING! DING! DING! [Baker falls outside the ring as Baer and Naugrim attack Rage. The Guard throws Rage off the ropes as they hit a double punch to the gut followed by a double bulldog. Baer then exits the ring and goes after Baker. He throws Baker into a snow bank and goes back to the apron. Naugrim tags out to Baer as the two double team Rage. They pick Rage up and hit a double suplex.] JG - "The Praetorian Guard is really showing some great double teams moves." JH - "You know what I hate? When you eat cheese and you get that little layer of crud on the roof of your mouth. I hate that." [As Naugrim exits the ring, Baer walks over to Rage and as Baer bends over, Rage gets Baer into a small package... 1! KICKOUT! Rage and Baer pop back up as Rage hits a dropkick. Rage then jumps on Baer and puts him in a chinlock. Baer makes his way up, but Rage slides in between the front of Baer's legs and ends up behind him and slaps Baer's butt.] JG - "He's slapping his butt, killing me." JH - "I don't think you'd like it if Rage slapped you in the bum bum!" [Rage waits for Baer to turn around and hits a frankensteiner. Rage starts to cartwheel around the ring and when he makes it to Baker, tags out. Baker picks Baer up and hits a powerslam. Baker then stands up and hits Baer with a legdrop. Baer gets up on his own and is met by a vicious clothesline. Baker then gets Baer in a headlock. Naugrim tries to get in the ring and save Baer, but Rage cuts him off, hitting a Lou Thesz Press.] JH - "Did you see that, Grey? That little pipsqueak just took out Tulkas." [Rage throws Naugrim out of the ring and waits for him to get up. Rage then springs over the top, hitting Naugrim with a flying crossbody. Baer makes his way up and hits a series of punches to the gut. Baer then runs off the ropes and hits a flying tackle. The two get up as Baker is met by a series of European Uppercuts. Baker is knocked back to his turnbuckle and tags out to Rage.] JG - "Baker finally tags out to Rage." [As Rage makes his way through the ropes, Baer hits him with a punch to the back, forcing him to stay bent over and hits a neckbreaker. Baer then tags out to Naugrim as the two throw Rage into the turnbuckle. Rage jumps to the second rope and hits the following Praetorian Guard with a flying crossbody. Rage picks Naugrim up and throws him into the ropes, but Naugrim reverses it. Naugrim ducks down for a back body drop, but Rage hits a sunset flip... 1! 2! KICKOUT! Naugrim pops up and hits Rage with a spinning heel kick.] JG - "Oh, he got all face on that one." JH - "Yes he did. Hey hey hey." [Naugrim gets to his feet and hits Rage with a big elbowdrop. Naugrim picks Rage up and throws him into the ropes. He hits him with a double-arm clothesline. Naugrim then goes over to tag Baer and picks Rage up. Baer climbs to the top as Naugrim sets Rage up....] JG - "Spiked Piledriver! Rage is taking an enormous amount of punishment to the neck." JH - "One move, big deal. Rage is a baby." [Naugrim exits the ring as Baer picks Rage up. He throws him into the turnbuckle and follows in with a big knee. Baer then places Rage up top and hits a belly-to-belly superplex. Baer is slow to get up and when he does, goes for the pin... 1! 2! KICKOUT! Baer gets up and makes a quick tag to Naugrim. Baer puts Rage in an abdominal stretch as Naugrim starts laying the punches on the gut of Rage. Baer releases as Naugrim goes to work, stomping on the fallen Zak Rage. Naugrim then picks Rage up and hits a kneebreaker.] JG - "Naugrim is finally using his brain and working on the knee of the high-flyer." JH - "Why is his last name Rage anyways?" JG - "Because that was his dad's last name." JH - "Okay, that makes sense." [Naugrim goes off the ropes and hits a legdrop. Naugrim gets up quick and places Rage in a figure-four. Rage makes his way over to the ropes, which happens to be the side of the Activists from Hell. Rage tags out to Baker as Baker comes in and cleans house.] JG - "Look at this! Bodyslams everywhere." JH - "Come on, get in there Lon. Your boys are getting beaten." [Eventually, Baker and Naugrim are left in the ring as Baker throws Naugrim into the ropes. Baker hits a powerslam and jumps to his feet. Baker grabs hold of Naugrim's leg and slaps on a Spinning Toe Hold. Baer breaks the hold as Naugrim gets to his feet first. He picks Baker up and as he attempts to throw Baker into the ropes, Baker reverses it. Baker attempts a clothesline, which is ducked by Naugrim, who attempts a crucifix but is reversed into a fallaway slam.] JG - "Baker's really pumped tonight." JH - "Yeah, on steroids. Doesn't the league have a policy on this kind of stuff?" [Baker picks Naugrim up and chops him into the turnbuckles. Baker then jumps to the second turnbuckle and starts delivering punches. Naugrim falls down, crouched in the corner as Baker runs from the opposite\ turnbuckle, hitting him with a knee. Baker then goes over and tags out to Rage who jumps to the top and hits a double axe handle onto the just getting up Tulkas Naugrim sending him back to the mat. Rage goes off the ropes, hitting a revised Corner Rolling Kappou Kick.] JG - "Wasn't that original." JH - "About as original as your wives' sex life, or Bill Clinton's for that matter." [Grey gives Hazard a mean look] JH - "Just a joke." [Rage picks Naugrim up and attempts to throw him into the ropes. Naugrim reverses it, throwing Rage into the ropes. Naugrim goes down for a back body drop, but Rage hits the Fishermanbuster. Rage then jumps on Naugrim and attempts to put him in a surfboard, but can't get him up. Rage gives up on it and hits Naugrim with a springboard moonsault. Rage goes for the pin... 1! 2! KICKOUT! Rage picks Naugrim up and throws him out of the ring. He goes beside Baer who does nothing and springs to the second rope, hitting Baer with a hurricarrana sending him on top of Naugrim.] JG - "Wow! This match has really picked up." JH - "Love him or hate him, Rage brings the life to the party. Although, that snow softened the blow for all of them." [Rage gets up and jumps to the top rope. He hits the Shooting Star Press to the outside, on top of the Praetorian Guard. Baker comes over and picks Baer up. He throws him into a snowbank and jumps on him, shoving his face in the snow. Rage gets up and jumps to the ring apron. He waits for Naugrim to get up and hits a springboard hurricarrana to the outside. Rage gets up and goes back into the ring. As Naugrim again attempts to get up, Rage goes to the outside of the ring apron and takes off with a springboard plancha.] JH - "[whimpering]Just let him up. He's gonna get counted out!" [Rage gets up and throws Naugrim into the ring. Rage jumps to the apron and waits for Naugrim to get up. Rage hits a springboard hurricarrana into a victory roll... 1! 2! KICKOUT! Rage then picks Naugrim up and rakes the eyes. Rage then goes off the ropes and hits the Zak Attack. Naugrim gets up, very slowly to see Zak Rage coming at him. Rage attempts a hurricarrana which is reversed into a powerbomb. Naugrim makes his way over to tag Baer as Baer goes over and hits Baker off the apron.] JG - "Oh, come on ref, he can't do that!" [Baer picks Rage up and puts him in a hammerlock. He hits Rage with a pumphandle slam and tags back out to Naugrim.] JG - "What's he doing? Naugrim is wrecked." JH - "But he wanted in." JG - "I don't know, Jack, this could cost them big time." [As Naugrim makes his way over to Rage and attempts to pick him up, Rage slides between Naugrim's legs and tags Baker. Baker scoops Naugrim up and hits a tilt-a-whirl side suplex. Rage stays in the ring as the two hit...] JG - "THE LIBERATOR!" [Rage hits Baer off the mat as Baker pins... 1! 2! 3!] JG - "The Activists win! They stay in SCRA!" JH - "No! No! No! Go away, ya bums!" JG - "That's not going to happen any time soon due to this big win by the Activists from Hell. They are back, and they face the German Eagles tomorrow night for the titles!" JH - "One day back and they are already calling the shots. That's their little justice system for ya." JG - "Let's play the clip of the new team while I calm myself." JH - "Getting a little flushed there, Grey. I didn't know you liked the Activists _that_ much." JG - "Roll the tape." -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
