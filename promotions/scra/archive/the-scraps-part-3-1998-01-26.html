___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 4 ) | | \ | | "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin vs. / |___ | | | | Derek Mota/"Golden King" Aladdin Style / Writer: JG (__________________________________________________________________________ [Ed Jaspard is seen standing in the ring chewing on some gum.] EJ - "This matchup is scheduled for one fall with a [gum falls out of his mouth] oh. [Jaspard remains sad from then on.] EJ - "A twenty minute time limit. Coming down the aisle first, from Toronto, Ontario, Canada, and weighing in at 224lbs., he is Derek Mota!" [The Great Southern Trendkill comes over the PA as Mota emerges from the curtain. The song is very heavy. The music starts instantly with heavy guitars and drums and the vocals start with a long scream. Mota runs to the ring and as he gets to the ring, springs over top of the ropes and jumps to the top turnbuckle. The Great Southern Trendkill gets cut off by Burn.] EJ - "His partner, from Fredericton, New Brunswick, and weighing in at 223lbs., he is "The Golden King" Aladdin Style!" [Style emerges from behind the curtain and walks to the ring, hitting the occasional outstretched hand. He gets to the ring and slides under the ropes. Style goes over to the opposite corner as Mota.] EJ - "Their opponent, from Dry Gulch, Texas and weighing in at 353lbs., he is, "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin!" [A red spotlight bathes the entrance as the Outlaw Blues comes over the sound system. Hardin walks slowly to the ring, wearing his usual black duster and his cowboy hat pulled low. He enters the ring, removes his hat and coat, and leans against the top turnbuckle in one corner while staring at Mota. Hardin signals for the bell, but the ref talks to him. Tommy Gregory comes out from the back and grabs a mike.] TG - "Hardin, this match permits you to have a partner. And to ensure you follow this and other rules...." [Hardin grabs the mike from him and begins talking.] JWH - "Fine, if anyone wants to just stand in the corner while I kick these lil' hobre's asses, get yer tail out here!" [Hardin throws the mike back at Gregory and attacks Mota and Style. The Dance of the Sugar Plum Fairies comes over the PA as Eddy "Flap" Jacks comes out from the curtain.] EJ - "His partner, from uh, Canada and uh....[Mota gets thrown into Jaspard which sends him through the ropes.] Ahhhhhhh." JG - "We're off to a bang here in this match." DING! DING! DING! [Hardin throws Mota out of the ring and helps Jacks throw Style out of the ring. The two start discussing words as Hardin says 'I don' need yer help' and pushes Jacks to his corner. Mota gets back into the ring and jumps on the shoulders of Hardin, spins and hits a hurricarrana. Mota picks Hardin up and twists Hardin's arm. Mota pulls Hardin over to his corner and tags out to Style. Style jumps to the top and hits a double axe handle on Hardin's arm. Hardin goes to his knees as Style applies a chinlock. Hardin gets to his feet and elbows his way out. Hardin goes off the ropes and hits a bodyblock on Style sending him to the mat. Hardin looks at Jacks, wipes his forehead and flicks his sweat at him.] JG - "A little turmoil between these two. Could Jacks could cause Hardin the match, or vise versa?" JH - "That problem is always an issue in tag team wrestling." [Hardin walks back over to Style and picked him up. Hardin throws Style into the ropes and hit a powerslam. Hardin then went off the ropes and hits a elbow. Hardin picks Style up and chops him into the corner. Hardin throws Sytle into the opposite turnbuckle and follows him up with a clothesline. As Style staggers foreward, Hardin climbs to the second turnbuckle and hits a double axe handle to the back of Style which sent him to the mat. Hardin goes for the pin but as he gets a one, Jacks breaks the pin. As Hardin yells at Jacks, Jacks signals for a tag. Hardin merely pushes Jacks back into his corner. As Hardin turns back around to pick Style up, Style gets him in a small package.] JG - "He's got him. Style's got the pin." JH - "Grey, it was only a one count, don't get so excited." [Hardin pops back up and goes for a clothesline. Style ducks it and hits a crucifix. Style picks Hardin up and throws him into the ropes. Hardin ducks a clothesline and as he approaches Jacks' side, Jacks slaps him in the back, getting a tag. Style hits Hardin with a back body drop and as Style pops back up, Eddy hits a flying lariat. Eddy picks Style up and hits a bodyslam. Eddy goes off the ropes and hits a big splash onto Style.] JH - "Splat!" [Eddy picks Style up and hits a belly to belly suplex. As Eddy attempts for another big splash Sytle moves out of the way and tags out to Mota. Mota cleans house hitting chops to the big men, sending Jacks and Hardin to the mat. Style gets up and throws Hardin out of the ring. Style and Mota team up and hit Jacks with a double suplex.] JG - "They lifted the big man!" [Style stays in the ring as the two throw Jacks into the ropes. The hit a double back body drop followed up by a double elbow drop from the top. Mota picks Jacks up as he and Style hit a double sidewalk slam. The ref warns Style as he exits the ring. Mota picks Jacks up and chops him to the ropes. Mota hits Jacks with an elbow to the forehead followed by a quick legdrop. Mota picks Jacks up again and throws him into the ropes. Jacks reverses it and Mota hits a flying cross body. Jacks rolls outside the ring as Mota springs on top of him hitting a crossbody block.] JG - "Hardin doesn't look very happy." JH - "No siree. He's grabbing a chair." [Hardin moves over to the timekeeper's table, throws the timekeeper off and grabs the chair. Hardin moves over behind Jacks and taps the chair on the ground. Jacks turns to see him and pulls Mota in front of him as Hardin hits Mota with the chair.] JG - "Hardin almost hit his partner. I don't think Hardin even cares if he wins. He just wants to dominate." JH - "It was all a part of their master plan." [Hardin turns his back and as he's about to jump to the ring apron, Jacks pulls Hardin down from the mat. Jacks argues with him as you can hear him yelling 'I came te help you an' this is the thanks I get?' Hardin climbed back in the ring as Jacks throws Mota into the ring. Jacks tags out to Hardin and holds Mota for him. Hardin delivers a punch to the chin as Jacks leaves the ring. Hardin picks Mota up and hits him with a backbreaker. Hardin looks over at Jacks who starts cheering him on. Hardin picks Mota up and hits a suplex. Hardin drags Mota over to their corner and tags Jacks. Jacks jumps up top while Hardin slides outside and holds Mota's feet. Jacks takes off with Pancake Poison... 1! 2!....] JG - "Style breaks the pin." JH - "Little bastard. Why couldn't Mota tag with someone good?" [Mota makes his way to his feet and as Jacks lifts Mota up for a running powerslam, he looks over at Hardin who is looking impressed. Jacks goes for the running powerslam but Mota slips out of it. Mota rolls him up but Hardin runs in and makes the save. Style starts to run at Hardin, but Hardin back body drops him over the ropes. Style lands on the outside of the mat though and as Hardin turns around, punches him. Hardin rakes the eyes and suplexs Style over.] JG - "Who's the legal man?" JH - "Um, him." JG - "Who?" JH - "You know, him...you know, that guy. In the ring....jeez, you're so incompitant." [Hardin and Style return to their respective corners as Jacks hits a bodyslam on Mota and tags out to Hardin. Hardin comes in and attempts a legdrop, but Mota moves out of the way. Mota gets up first and as Hardin gets up, Mota goes into the ropes and as it looks like Hardin's going to hit a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker, Mota hits a headscissors takedown. Mota then jumps to the top rope and hits a moonsault onto the fallen cowboy. Mota then runs into the ropes and hits a legdrop. Mota picks Hardin up and throws him into the turnbuckle. Hardin bounces off as Mota hits a bulldog.] JG - "Mota's bringing the underdogs back, Jack." JH - "You think you're so clever, don't you?" [Mota tags out to Style as the two pick Hardin up and hit a series of double knife-edge chop. They throw Hardin into the ropes and hit a double dropkick followed by a double elbowdrop off the top rope. Mota exits the ring as Style picks Hardin up. Style attempts a bodyslam, but can't get Hardin up. Hardin grabs Style by the hair and as he's about to hit Style with an elbow, Style ducks puting Hardin in a full nelson. Style then places his foot in front of Hardin's and trips Hardin so he lands flat on his face. Style picks Hardin up and as he's about to throw Hardin into the ropes, he reverses it. Hardin telegraphs a back body drop and Style hits a sunset flip. Hardin goes over right away, but Style only gets a one. Both men get up quickly as Style hits a double-armed clothesline. Style tags out to Mota who hits a springboard moonsault onto Hardin.] JG - "We've got a cover...1...2...Oh, Hardin's got a shoulder up." [Mota drags Hardin over to the turnbuckle as Mota jumps to the second turnbuckle. Mota takes off and hits a guillatine legdrop and tags out to Style and exits the ring. Style makes his way over to Hardin and places him in a figure four. Hardin, being right by the ropes, grabs them and forces Style to release the hold. Style picks Hardin up and throws him into the ropes. Hardin tags Jacks on his way as he recieves a running forearm from Style. As Jacks is about to grab Style, Mota runs in and hits a Lou Thesz Press, hitting a fury of punches.] JG - "Mota's really taking control in this match. He's taking out everyone out in his path." JH - "Good english." JG - "You thank." JH - "Good attempt, but....no!" [Style picks Hardin up and throws him into the ropes. Style takes off with a hurricarrana, but Hardin reverses it into a powerbomb. Hardin takes Style's legs and pushes them above Style's head putting him in a rollup... 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Almost had 'em." JH - "Damn you Style! Damn you!" [Mota jumps into the ring and dropkicks Hardin in the back of the head, allowing Style to crawl outside. Mota continues with a fast paced attack of punches and kicks, knocking Hardin to the mat. Mota begins climbing to the top.] JG - "I think he's setting up for Main Atraction!" [Mota takes off with Main Attraction...to the outside! He nails Style!] JG - "Whoa! He just nailed Aladdin Style!" JH - "Holy cow! What the hell is he doing?" JG - "Mota is unleashing a furious attack on Style! He set him up! He set him up!" JH - "How much crossing goes on in this sport?" [As Mota attacks Style, the referee rings the bell, awarding the match to Hardin/Jacks by countout. Tommy Gregory and security rush to ringside, but Hardin jumps to the outside, grabbing Gregory and nailing a Cattlebuster! Hardin calls Jacks over who climbs onto the apron and takes off with Pancake Poison.] JG - "They're crushing head of security Tommy Gregory! This is insane!" JH - "And, Mota's still attacking. Nothing's gonna pull him off Style." [Hardin and Jacks continue out of the ring area, leaving Mota with the attack. Mota eventually stops the attack and walks down the ramp, amidst a series of loud boos.] JG - "Unbelievable. I thought those two would be able to form a partnership. They are so similar." JH - "That's what you get when you trust. That's why my motto is trust nobody." JG - "Weird. But, we can't mull over this point for too long, we've got the next match to call!" JH - "Let's mull. I don't want to call this junk." JG - "It's not junk, it's the tag team titles. The Praetorian Guard puts them on the line against the Pros and German Eagles. Any predictions?" JH - "I'll fall asleep." JG - "No arguments on that one. Let's go to Ed Jaspard for the intros!" ___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 5 ) | | \ | | Praetorian Guard vs. German Eagles vs. The Pros / |___ | | | | Writer: AG (_________________________________________________________________________ [Jaspard steps into the ring sporting an Indianpolis Colts jersey.] EJ - "We won! We won!" JH - "Does Jaspard know that this isn't like 20 years ago?" EJ - "On this happy moment, I would like to bring down an SCRA tag team. Not just any tag team, the Pros!" [The Mission Impossible theme comes over the speakers as Jabasco and Grant enter the arena, soon followed by Tavis. They walk down to the ring quickly, Jabasco searching the crowd for a hidden threat. As he does, the others walk to the ring. Grant catches up.] EJ - "And, their opponent, the German Eagles!" [The German national anthem comes over the speakers as Chris and Joseph come out, for the first time in months, accompanied by Ocki the Moselman. They walk to the ring fairly quickly, acknowledging the Pros and waving their German flags for the crowd.] JH - "Isn't Jaspy forgetting something?" JG - "Hey, the Colts forgot how to win, Jaspard forgets the weights. It all works out, I guess." EJ - "And, their opponents, the Praetorian Guard!" [The Guard walks out, accompanied by their managerial staff and Eddy Jacks, belts shining around their waists. Baer and Surefoot jar it up with a group of fans as Jacks and Savarese talk. Naugrim simply stares into the ring at his upcoming challenge.] JH - "We've got more managers out here than Mike Tyson! Hey, think we can get him to show?" DING! DING! DING! [Walt Jabasco, Chris Eagle and Tulkas Naugrim start out in the ring, all sort of circling each other. Eagle and Jabasco lock arms and run at Naugrim, taking him down with a double clothesline. As Naugrim rolls back into the turnbuckle, Jabasco and Eagle hold up each other's arms in a partnership!] JG - "What a form of respect! The Pros and German Eagles are united to eliminate the Praetorian Guard!" JH - "They just know that alone, they can't be the superior abilities of the Praetorian Guard." [Naugrim gets up in the corner and charges at both men with a clothesline. Both Jabasco and Eagle duck underneath, hood their arms around Naugrim and hit a double chokeslam. Naugrim backs into the corner, again, and tags in his partner, Bill Baer.] JH - "Things are about to get rough and gruff now." JG - "Baer sizes up better and can probably use his brawling attack to keep the two men at bay. We'll see, though." [Jabasco charges at Baer, but Baer knocks him down with a big boot. Eagle runs at Baer and takes off into a flying cross body, but Baer catches him. Baer tosses Eagle onto his shoulder and drives Eagle's shoulder into a shoulderbreaker. Jabasco gets back up and runs at Baer, hitting a dropkick. Jabasco hops onto Baer and starts tossing wild punches to the head. Eagle gets up and applies a figure four to the legs of Baer, as Jabasco still tosses the punches in. Jabasco moves away from the punches and places Baer in a bow and arrow, providing the most pressure to all points of Baer's body.] JG - "They've really got Baer going now! He's in a really tough position right now!" JH - "That's nothing. You want tough? Last night after the Super Bowl party, I was..." JG - "We don't want to know about your little parties, Jack." JH - "Yet you want to know about Phi Theta Kappa's? There's a real winning team." [Naugrim hops into the ring and nails Jabasco in the back of the head, breaking the chinlock. Grant rushes into the ring to stop the attack on his partner, causing the referee to attempt to stop Grant. Naugrim uses this to stomp on Chris Eagle and break the figure four. This brings Joseph into the ring, again, causing the referee to push him back to the corner. With the referee's back turned, Naugrim continues to attack the two opponents as Baer recovers. He forces Eagle and Jabasco to headbutt each other, dropping them to the mat. Baer moves over and attempts to pin both men... 1! KICKOUT by Jabasco followed by Eagle!] JH - "Almost had him!" JG - "No he didn't! Baer never had a chance at pinning both wrestlers." JH - "Baer could beat those two bums with his eyes closed. In fact, that's a fun experiment. Let's see if he can." [Baer gets up and picks up Jabasco, hitting a vertical suplex. He quickly follows with Eagle, hitting a powerbomb onto Jabasco. Baer runs off the ropes and drops a leg across both wrestlers. He picks up Jabasco sends him into the ropes. Eagle, however, sneaks in from behind Baer and pulls his leg from underneath. As Baer drops to the mat, Jabasco leaps into the air and nails a kneedrop to the head. Eagle keeps his clutch on Baer's leg and applies an ankle lock submission as Jabasco moves towards the arms with an armbar.] JH - "Look at the double teaming!" JG - "It's perfectly legal. All three men can be in the ring at once, so it's only fair that two of them can work against the other." JH - "Then, this was booked unfairly. The Guard has all the odds stacked against them in this one. Not to mention the huge stack across Miss Frank....wait a minute. Where is she tonight?" JG - "They didn't want to stir trouble with the Pros, so Miss Frank stayed backstage." [Again, Naugrim moves into the ring to break the hold, but neither Joseph Eagle nor Grant enter the ring. The referee pushes Naugrim out of the ring as both Eagle and Jabasco break the hold and tag in their respective partners. The two teams each attempt their finishers, but with a big disagreement. Instead, they come to an agreement. Jabasco and Grant hit Pro-Pain, followed by the German Eagles' double suplex.] JG - "It's over for Baer!" JH - "Bad booking, damnit! Bad booking!" [Grant and Joseph Eagle pin Baer as Chris and Walt Jabasco attempt to keep Naugrim out of the ring. The referee slides back to the cover... 1! 2! 3!] JG - "The Praetorian Guard's eliminated! We _will_ have new champions tonight!" JH - "Next week, the new champs against the Praetorian Guard and Siberian Express! That'll teach the damn bookers." JG - "Very funny, Jack. I don't think that'll happen, though." [The Pros and German Eagles toss Baer out of the ring, ready to continue their new match. The House of Pancakes follow up with a look of disgust towards each other. In the ring, though, Grant and Joseph Eagle shake hands in the center of the ring and begin to circle each other.] JH - "Wake me when this one's done." JG - "The tag titles are on the line, Jack." JH - "What's the use of tag titles when somebody like the Pros or German Eagles hold onto them?" [The two wrestlers lock up in the middle of the ring, Grant quickly using his size advantage to push Eagle into the turnbuckle. The referee calls for a clean break...and gets it.] JG - "Now, that's respect. Although these two have had their differences in the past, they have an amazing amount of respect for each other. Enough to put aside a cheap attack for a title." JH - "That's not respect, that's stupidity." [Grant and Joseph circle each other again and lock up in the middle. Eagle moves behind Grant and applies a hammerlock. Grant gets behind Eagle and applies his own hammerlock. Eagle grabs onto Grant's head and flips himself over the top of Grant, applying a waislock to Grant. He uses this to nail a release German suplex. Grant gets up quickly, but is taken down by an Eagle dropkick. Again, Grant gets up quick, but Eagle flies in with a flying cross body. Grant catches him. Grant falls back and tosses Eagle directly over his head. As Eagle attempts to get back up, Grant takes him down with a drop toe hold. He quickly applies an ankle lock submission.] JG - "Grant's got a big submission move on the leg of Joseph Eagle. This is an extremely smart move, taking away the most important part of the German Eagles' gameplan: their high flying legs." JH - "Who cares about their little legs. The only member of the German Eagles I care about is Miss Frank. Those are some nice legs." [Eagle attempts to move towards the ropes...and makes it. Both men get up with Grant tossing Joseph Eagle into the ropes. He takes him down with a hiptoss, following up with an elbowdrop. Eagle moves out of the way and Grant misses. Both men attempt to move towards the ropes to make the tag to their respective partners.] JH - "It's time to bring back the predictions, Grey. Whoever makes the tag first...will still suck. The other guy will just suck more because they will lose the match." JG - "That's real funny, Jack. Real funny." [Joseph makes the tag, shortly followed by Grant. Instead of both men rushing into the ring, the two men circle each other and lock up in the center of the ring. Eagle hits an armdrag takedown, followed by an armbar. Jabasco begins to work his way to his feet, getting out of the hold and pushes Eagle into the ropes. Jabasco hits a clothesline. Eagle begins to get to his feet, but Jabasco hits a bulldog, then hits a leg drop coming off the ropes. He picks up Eagle and sends him into the ropes, attempting a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker, but Eagle reverses the move into a flying headscissors.] JG - "What a reversal! Chris Eagle just saved himself from a potentially devastating move and converted it into a potentially devastating move against Walt Jabasco." JH - "What kind of name is Jabasco? Reminds me of tabasco. Odd name, if you ask me." JG - "Nobody asks you, Jack." JH - "Yet, I say things anyways. Don't you just love that in a broadcast colleague?" [Eagle picks up Jabasco and hits an Irish whip into the turnbuckle, but Jabasco goes up and lands upside down, going over the top and onto the outside. Eagle moves to the outside of the apron, springs himself onto the the second rope, the top rope and into an asai moonsault to Jabasco. Both men crawl around the outside.] JG - "What a move! Chris Eagle almost killed himself with that asai moonsault, yet it worked!" JH - "It worked against him, too. Eagle pretty much popped a gut with that one." JG - "It's the life of the high flyer. They risk their careers, and lives, with every move." [Eagle gets up and is about to grab Jabasco, but Jabasco grabs Chris Eagle in an inverted atomic drop position. He drops Eagle's head across the guardrail with a hotshot. Jabasco gets in the ring and tags in Grant, who climbs back outside and tosses in Eagle. Grant applies a chinlock to Eagle, slowing down the already tired Eagles.] JH - "(giant yawn) This match almost over? I would almost go back to the opener and trade remarks with that Hunter guy instead of this junk. I've got some good ones, now." JG - "Oh, yeah? Let's hear them." JH - "Uh...uh...Hunter, you're so fat, when you sit around the house, you really sit around the house!" JG - "Hunter looks to be only around 210-220 lbs. He's a little man, compared to the beer bellies like you (giggles)." JH - "Don't start with me, Grey. It's getting late and I'm in no mood for your little jokes." He climbs back to the top and takes off with a flying headbutt, landing it across the chest of Grant. Eagle goes for the pin... 1! KICKOUT! Eagle tosses Grant into the turnbuckle and follows it up with a flying splash. He lifts Grant and sits him on the top turnbuckle. Joseph climbs up and hits a top rope frankensteiner!] JG - "What a move! He's got Grant floating now!" JH - "Floating? What kind of phrase is that?" JG - "It's an innovation phrase. We can make up meanings, we're broadcasters." [Joseph tags in his brother and they set up for the...] JG - "Double German suplex!" JH - "What? Match is over?" [Chris goes for the cover... 1! 2! 3!] JG - "New champs!" JH - "You already said we would have new champs." JG - "The German Eagles are the new champs!" [The Eagles grab the mic and begin singing the German national anthem, waving the flag proudly. They shake hands with the Pros as the good losers exit.] JH - "Make it stop! Make it stop!" JG - "Come on, Jack. This is great stuff." JH - "It's crap. I can't believe people tune into these boys singing. And, I can't believe they're champs!" JG - "That's the way the cookie crumbles. The Eagles seem to be finishing up, so we're about ready for the next match." JH - "Yee-haw. This one's a real main event." JG - "Ken Curtis and Gangsta Impasse with the added stipulation." JH - "Gangsta's gonna be shoveling some sh..."
