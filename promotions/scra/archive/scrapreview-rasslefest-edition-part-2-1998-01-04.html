[SCRA] ScraPreview [2/3] (Rasslefest edition)

4 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
++=====================++ Lifetaker vs. Centris ++=====================++ JG - "The biggest match for these two, yet it may not happen." JH - "What?" JG - "I don't know. I've been hearing conflicting reports, but I'd be surprised to see it tomorrow night." JH - "Com'on, Grey. It'd be a shame for these two to end their careers any other way than a good ol' snot kickin'." JG - "We'll see what happens. Here's what Lifetaker had to say." [in a doctor's office] L - "Well, it looks like the ring wars have finally gotten the better of me, as this left knee just cannot keep it up any longer. The doctor says I will never wrestle again once I have this surgery, so I am going to retire; but first, Centris gets his! The pain and humiliation he has caused me and the fine wrestlers at SCRA are too much for my soul to handle. So, I risk permanent injury and lameness to rid SCRA fo you forever! (gets up, and is hobbling) The fans need me, SCRA needs me, and Centris, in a sick and pathetic way, you need me! You need me to beat your ass down! When you feel the leg lock slapped on, and you beg me for mercy, you will find that the murderer of your friend has none to give....you want some, Centris, come and get some!" [walks out of the doctor's office] JH - "When did he get some injury? How come nobody informs me of these things?" JG - "I didn't think you cared." JH - "You're right. I don't. If Lifetaker's too much of a baby to show up tomorrow night for fear of injuring his knee, he'd better snap out of it." JG - "It could go deeper than that, Jack." JH - "What?" JG - "Centris may be having problems, too." JH - "Why the hell was this match signed, then?" JG - "They wanted to settle it, in the cage. Of course, who knows what's going on. Let's just move on." ++=====================================================++ Rock Bottom Robbie vs. Pablo Morales vs. Silver Stone ++=====================================================++ JG - "The TV title is on the line as these three top contenders square off for a clean winner!" JH - "When has there not been a clean winner?" JG - "Well, I guess never." JH - "Than, I suggest you rethink your opening." JG - "Uh, okay. Let's go to Robbie's flash while I think things over." [Rock Bottom Robbie is sitting in a black room with only the glow of the tv providing light. The Billiard Room Brawl is being played as Robbie watches intensly, writing down key moments in the match.] RBR - "SilverStone, do you remember this match? You should, with all the scars I gave you. I remember it quite fondly. I shattered my ankle beating you up" [Robbie lifts up his pants to reveal a scar around his right ankle] RBR - "Ahh, but it was well worth it, to watch the crimson come out of your body like water coming out of a dam. I broke my ankle about half way through that match, and I still powerslamed your a$$ through the glass window. Now, if I can beat you up on a broken ankle, what makes you so sure that you can even compete with me? I don't, neither does anyone in the SCRA except for you. Look at you, you were once one of the greats. I pushed my way through the ranks to get a shot at the gold which you were wearing, now, your no better than Sublime, who is now cooking for Mciedee's. You lost your girl, your belt, and your self respect. You are nothing in this league, and I plan to put you in with the jWo after tomorrow's fight." [Robbie watches himself powerslam SilverStone through the glass, then the tape cuts into the Robbie v. Morales matchup for the TV Title.] RBR - "Now, this, this was defiently the joke of the year. I mean, Morales, you didn't even deserve to get the belt, let alone get another shot at it for tomorrow night. You got lucky in beating Greeny, but you sure as hell got your a$$ kicked when you faced me, look at this.." [screen shows robbie hitting the Rock Bottom Slam, and getting the 1, 2, 3] RBR - "..I think I won this match in under 20 minutes. It was definetly the easiest title shot that I ever had the pleasure of winning. Morales, you are definetly not worthy of holding or owning a belt, so you might want to consider trying to job for a little while, then come back for me." [Robbie shuts off the tv and turns on the lights] RBR - "Tomorrow night, at Rasselfest, I plan on showing just why I was a NA champ, why I am a TV title champ, and why I will be a World champ by defeating two people in one shot. I've beaten them both before, I'll beat them both again at the same time. I'm the first and only champion to hold two different belts, and I plan to make it three different belts soon. Get ready SCRA, cause tomorrow is the start of the new year in wrestling, and the new Rock Bottom Robbie." [camera fades out] JG - "Three top contenders for the TV title all step in the ring to decide who is the real winner." JH - "That was okay, Grey, but not great." JG - "Seeing as we've looked at this match for the third time today, as well as the Scoops giving their insights, it's hard to come up with original openings." JH - "I feel for ya. It's hard for me to make fun of these three guys with new material." JG - "Here's Pablo Morales." [Camera scrolls in on Pablo Morales sweat all over his body. Alison comes in with a Water Bottle and gives it to him and he takes down huge gulps. Stanlee Maxx waits in the ring and calls Pablo Back in. Pablo gets in and and begins fighting with Stanlee. The big man pounds on Pablo who is absorbing the punches and begins using various technical and aerial moves to take the big man down. They continue fighting and after a while the two men shake hands and Danielle and Alison come back in with wwater and towels. Stanlee speaks to Pablo and then leaves with Danielle as Pablo finally walks over to the camera.] PM - "Wow!! What a workout. I realized after my match with Robbie that I will need to be able to absorb the punishment that these men will try to throw my way. So what better way to prepare for such a thing than to train with the premier big man and brawler that the SCRA has to offer. Stanlee is off to go finalize his gameplan for the destruction of that illiterate imbecile GANGSTA IMPASSE. Folks the SCRA will crown A new North American Champion and his name will be STANLEE MAXX! ARRIBA!" A - "Thats not the only belt thats going to change now is it? I know a certain TV Title that belongs to you that you need to get back." PM - "Of course of course the TV TITLE. I had the gold and let that punk ROBBIE take it from me. But that is just fine because You know what I hate you RObbie and So does SILVER STONE. I dont think I like your odds. Amigo I dont thinnnk If I was you I would want to be in the ring with me or him you should fear for your health and definitely for your title. SILVER STONE we have met before and I must say you are a great wrestler but you should of all people not being calling me an immigrant. The more you put me down the worse that makes you look. You might have beeen great once but now is time for new guys and I will be the Premiere Newcomer in the SCRA if the HEad Honchos will have it of course. I have gained some respect winning that title here but I still have fool like HAZARD and you SILVER STONE thinking that just because I am MEXICAN I cant compete. Well STONE I remembeer at some point BEATING YOUR F*%$ING ASS! So Know that I am coming with a plan and the end of that plan has me leaving RASSLEFEST victorious with the TV TITLE wrapped firmly around my waist once again." A - "That's right Ladies and Gentleman you are looking at the New TV champ right here and will all cheer for hm as he leaves vicorius at RASSLEFEST!" PM - "THANK YOU. COME JANUARY 4 at RASSLEFEST ROBBIE...STONE You both will go down because Iam ready willing and able to defet you both and You will both be lying flat on your backs As my hand is raised in victory and the belt is wrapped around my waist. Two new champs for RAsslefest STANLEE MAXX and PABLO MORALES with title reigns to last a liftime ARRIBA!!" JH - "But, that provided me with lots of new material! I don't know what world that crazy Mexican is in, but it's a weird one. Where does he get all these victories? Has he ever beat Silver Stone?" JG - "Not to my knowledge." JH - "And Robbie? Twice?" JG - "I don't think even once." JH - "Then, why does he say he did? And didn't he realize Gangsta Impasse lost the North American title? Or, after his crushing defeat, did he leave the arena and weep?" JG - "I don't know." JH - "Crazy Mexican. So dumb." JG - "Thanks, Jack. Let's see what Silver Stone had to say." [SilverStone sits smoking a cigar outside of a crowded park in Carson City, Nevada] SS - "SCRA, its almost here, my return to my glory days. It will all begin on January 4th when I claim a new piece of hardware, the TV title. All I have to do is go through a Mexican immigrant and my most hated rival. First of all, that mid card Mexican has beaten me before about two weeks ago, but the fact is, when I lost to him I was still taking wins for granted. He shouldn't be a problem, after all I have returned to my old training schedule. Then he have Rock Bottom Robbie. Robbie, we've had some classic matches in the past, but there has never been a clear cut winner. Unlike the past, were I've held the gold, this time you do and its my turn to capture it from you. That shouldn't be a real problem because I'm just so much better then you. I'm better looking then you, I have more skill then you and I simply out-class you. The silver lining has returned to the top of the SCRA, my reign of terror will begin again, because I'm just to SILVERSWEET to handle." JH - "This guys at least moving up, sort of. Sure, he may not be winning, but at least he's working." JG - "He is working hard. But, without status to back it up, Silver Stone is falling in the league's rankings. This match could sling him back to the top...or bury him in the bottom." JH - "I'd vote for the former." JG - "I'll stick with the latter." JH - "Cool discussion there, Grey." JG - "We can relate on a level higher than grade 3." JH - "Nah, we can't." ++================================++ Gangsta Impasse vs. Stanlee Maxx ++================================++ JG - "These two old enemies finally settle the score in the New York Street Fight. I can't really pick somebody with an advantage." JH - "Maxx." JG - "Stanlee Maxx was raised in New York, so he does have "home field" advantage. Gangsta, though, is well, a gangster." JH - "It's a tough call, but Maxx." JG - "Here's what Gangsta Impasse had to say." [The scene opens to a familiar scene, the set of the Monday Scraps. The date is unknown, Poink, The Evil Cop Clown is in a match. The screen is tinted black for affect.] GI - "Ma first appearance on de Scraps. An eventful day. I proo'd maself an got de respect from all d'other wrestlers in de league. The former Poink, dee Evil Cap Clown, felt the wrath o' ma Gangsta Impact." [The scene changes to another addition of the Scraps, Gangsta Impasse and John Drake against Poink and a mystery partner.] GI - "You threw me a cur'ball and changed yo styl'. Well, Poink, Stanlee, whateva you wanna be call'd, you had mo' changes den da Jackson fi'. An, dere's mo' to come." [The scene changes yet again to Gangsta Impasse vs.Johnny Destiny on the Scraps. Stanlee Maxx interferes, causing Gangsta the title.] GI - "An' dis put me ova' de top. Stanlee, it's time to settle de score at Rasslefest, No interference, no DQ, all de weapons you can find, oh this i' gon be all too sweet. I' you gots a surprise, I gots one too. Not only do I have John Drake watchin' ma back, but I gots the meanest New Yo'k Street Gang on the sout' side too, so Pablo, don' even think bout gettin' involved." [The screen fades to black and re-opens to Gangsta Impasse sitting in a room, black walls.] GI - "Stanlee, we had our share of bad times, and dis i' gon' be de worst o em. Stanlee, just to get-choo fired up and to gi' me a challenge, I gon make fun o' yo mamma. Yo Mamma so big, she can't wear a Malcolm X Shirt cause choppers'll start landin' on 'er. Yo Mamma has mo' chins den a Hong Knog Phone Book." [John Drake walks in and turns on the lights revealing a weight room.] JD - "Man, what-choo doin'? It's 4 o'clock in de mo'nin." GI - "Man, I wuz too hyped ova Stanlee an' me in a New Yo'k Stree' Fight, I couldn't sleep. I started liftin' an' got thinkin', I gots to flash fo' de match, so I did." JD - "But-choo gots to focus. Wit all de parties you'z be goin' to, you need some rest, especially wit de New Year comin." GI - "Man, I needs not worry bout Stanlee Maxx, he's a punk, a has been. Jeez, where've you been?" JD - "Asleep, like you." GI - "Aw man, you just woke up? Aw, now you gon' have dat nasty gas." JD - "[rubbing eyes]Shutup." [Drake passes gas] GI - "Geez, come on, I gots to talk bout Stanlee." JD - "Well, from what I he'rd, you'z already covad him." GI - "Aight, I sle'p den. But Stanlee, I gon kick yo ass come Rasslefest, hear?" [Drake and Gangsta exit the room, turning the lights out on their way leaving a black silence.] JG - "So, it's not you who has the gas in the morning!" JH - "It's a combination. Of course, between me and Drake, I could blow him away." JG - "I don't know. Drake is a big boy. Big boys..." JH - "That's great. Jack Hazard is the undisputed gas king of SCRA. Let's roll Maxx's comments." [The camera man is walking around a deserted NYC subway station. Finally, he finds Stanlee Maxx. He is dressed different. His pants are extremely baggy and hanging around his waist. He is wearing a white t-shirt with "I SHOT 2-PAC" spray painted on it.] SM - "Yo what b-up? I iz chillin hea in my home....ooops.....I mean my hood. Hea in Brooklyn Zoo. Yo, yo, yo, Gangsta what b-up now, biz-notch? You can't front on 'dis." [Stanlee uses an extensive amount of arm gestures as he speaks. His arms are flailing all over.] SM - "I goin to kiznick yo ass, b-yatch. You see what I did to yo boy 2-Pac. I filled day punk wit lead." [He makes a gun with his hand.] SM - "Ka-blow. First, I blew off his nut, den I just killed him. Hahaha. Den I gitcho boy Biggie, dat dum fat ass. You know what Gangsta, you iz next. You tink dat dis is yo hood, New York? I beez born and raised in deez streetz. I used to beez a gang banga den I became dat cop and killed bitches like you fo no reason at all. I would use whatever I got my hand on." [He suddenly becomes still. His eyes become blank. He stares dead into the camera as it zooms on his face.] SM - "Gangsta, bottom line, your ass is dead!" [Camera fades to black.] JG - "A bit of a different attitude from Stanlee Maxx. Wonder what brought that on." JH - "Mockery. Everybody knows Gangsta Impasse can't string a sentence together. It's about time fun people make of him." JG - "I guess he shouldn't learn by example." JH - "Huh? Say something you?" JG - "Are you purposely doing this?" JH - "Doing what?" JG - "Back to normal. Don't want to destroy that, so let's move on." ++=======================================++ Derek Mota vs. Reverend Aristotle Jones ++=======================================++ JG - "Much lies in the result of this matchup. Well, the weight of the cruiserweight limit lies in the balance of this matchup. If Jones wins, I'm hearing the limit will be set high as Jones will have proved that the men around that weight can compete with cruisers. If Mota wins, I'm hearing it'll be set much lower, around 220 or 230." JH - "Much lower? That's ten pounds!" JG - "Every pound counts. People have missed the mark by 1 and been held off the list. We'll have to wait and see what happens as this saga unfolds." JH - "Saga? It's a freakin' wrestling match!" JG - "Things get pretty emotional in the ring. Let's see what Derek Mota had to say." [Cut to a large dim room, a regulation size wrestling ring in the middle. Layers of smoke seem to be hovering near the top, and the camera eventually pans down, where we see Derek Mota sparring with an opponent. Mota's partner is about 240 lbs., and is trying a variety of takedowns on Derek, but each attempt is systematically blocked. Finally Mota reverses one into a Springboard Leg Drop off the top rope and gets up, his opponent groggy on the mat. Mota turns to the nearest camera.] DM - "... "Reverend" ... Heh heh. What a funny guy you are. All I did was come to the ring after your match against Curtis, make fun of the spare tire around your waist, and beat up on ya a little, and now you're offended by me! Trust me on this one, Harry Jones, this ain't the first time someone shows up at ringside ta pound on me. I've taken on three, four, hell, SIX guys at once, and your showing up at ringside sure wasn't anythin' outta the usual. Heh heh ... Jones, if there's one rule you have to go by, it's to never show that someone's getting to you. And I guess I am, aren't I? But that ain't no problem, Jones ... you may have cost me my first match in Scraps, but it showed me somethin' ... I'm winnin'. I could feel it inside of ya when we were brawlin'. You're obssessed. I betcha that when you're beatin' your wife at night, that you're picturing it ta be me, ain't ya? I hope that it ain't the same when you're doin' her, but I wouldn't doubt that either! Let's just say, Harry, that I have a problem with ya bein' in the Cruiserweight Division. Ya see, you're a 240 lb. ground based fighter. And let's just say that they created the Cruiserweight Title because the fans were bored of people like you. And now I hear that you're joinin' the Cruiser division? Don't like it, boy. I don't like it at all. And I don't like you either ... you're too weak to fit in with the heavyweights, and too slow to fit in with the cruisers ... you're a freak, Revvy boy. And the only way I know of gettin' rid of freaks is ta get in their faces and never get out. Just ask Genesis over in the IIWF ... where the hell are THEY now? You see, Jones, I never lose a feud. And when you came to ringside when I was fightin' Progidy, for me it changed from business ... to a feud. And you know what that means, don't ya? It means that I'm in your face now. There gonna be a permanent picture of me in your peripheral vision, Harry boy. You're standin' at a hot dog stand after the card, and I might just be the guy standin' behind ya. You might've been shoppin' for Christmas presents for the family, and maybe I was the guy who tripped ya. Let's just put it this way, Jones ... the man who ALWAYS gets up is on your back now. The toughest little bastard in SCRA wants some action, Reverend. And like it or not, boy, you're gonna be the first." [Mota turns back to his opponent and begins sparring once more, getting hit with a few uppercuts before tearing into the guy, taking him down with a Northern Lights Suplex. Derek Mota just looks at his fallen sparring partner and scowls... and the camera fades.] JG - "Arrogant, as always." JH - "Hey, there's a fine line between confident and arrogant. Mota is standing over that line and pissin' on it." JG - "What?" JH - "You know, destroying the line. He could care less whether people think of him as cocky or confident. He just shows what he's got in the ring." JG - "We'll see if he can hold his own with the young Aristotle Jones. It should be a great matchup." JH - "It should. We'll see about that one." -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
