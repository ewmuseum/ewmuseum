[SCRA] The Scraps [4/4]

19 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 7 ) | | \ | | "Cowboy" Ken Curtis vs. Stanlee Maxx / |___ | | | | Writer: AG (_________________________________________________________________________ [Jaspard wobbly stands in the ring, ready to go, sort of.] EJ - "This match be scheduled for one fall...uh...guy come out now... Maxx...where are you?" [Without music, Stanlee Maxx walks down to ringside, going after Jaspard. He slaps Jaspard in the head. Jaspard, however, decks him with the mic.] EJ - "Think you can hit me. Watch this intro!" [Jaspard straightens up, ready to go.] EJ - "And his opponent, weighing in at 260 lbs. and hailing from Austin, Texas, here is "Cowboy" Ken Curtis!" [Gimme Back my Bullets fills the stadium as Curtis enters the arena. He bounces to the ring in a slow jog, removing his hat along the way. As Maxx turns to check the ropes, Curtis dives in with his vest still on, attacking Maxx from behind.] DING! DING! DING! JG - "And, we're underway! Ken Curtis is nailing Stanlee Maxx right away!" JH - "Let him finish Maxx off once and for all. Gangsta Impasse did an okay job at Rasslefest, but Curtis will put the big hurt to the seven foot piece of trash." JG - "Unusually bad words for Maxx. Why the change of heart, Jack?" JH - "The guy's a quitter. He can't make it here." [Curtis places Maxx's head across the middle rope, then runs across the ring and nails a legdrop across the neck. He quickly gets up and repeats the move, keeping the leg across the head in a choke position. Curtis releases the hold, allowing Maxx to get up. Curtis grabs him from behind in a full nelson and quickly drops him with a full nelson slam. Curtis lifts up Maxx and applies a hammerlock.] JH - "All this technical stuff is making my head hurt." JG - "Are you sure it wasn't the novelty giant sized beer you had during the commercial? You know, you aren't supposed to drink that." JH - "It could've been. Why can't you drink it?" JG - "It's not really beer. The can is a souvenir, so they stick some swamp water in there." JH - "That's why the buzz I'm getting is a different feeling." [Maxx tries to get out of the hammerlock with an elbow, but Curtis ducks underneath, grabs Maxx's arm and hits an atomic drop. Maxx stays standing, but is taken down by a running lariat to the back of the head. Maxx rolls onto his knees, trying to get up, but Curtis runs in and kicks him in the midsection.] JG - "That was like a field goal! Curtis could've won the Super Bowl with that kick. Jack, who's you're prediction?" JH - "I'll tell you next week." JG - "Very funny. Just tell me now." JH - "Fine. The Argos." JG - "The Super Bowl is NFL, not CFL. Pick either the Broncos or Packers." JH - "What the hell is a Packer?" JG - "Nevermind." [Curtis climbs to the second rope and takes off with an elbowdrop. He picks up Maxx and sends him into the ropes, taking Maxx down with a drop toe hold. Curtis spins Maxx over and applies a figure four.] JG - "He's got it locked on!" JH - "What's a Bronco, too?" JG - "You don't know what a Packer or Bronco is, yet you know what an Argonaut is?" JH - "No. I just pick them because they've got Flutie." JG - "Not anymore. Doug Flutie signed with the Bills." JH - "A team's name is Bills? I bet they've got a big fan base." [Maxx pulls himself to the ropes and supposedly forces Curtis to release the hold. Unfortunately for Maxx, Curtis doesn't until the referee steps in and pulls the legs off. Curtis gets up and nails a vicious knee to the head of Maxx. He picks up Maxx and lifts him high into the air in vertical suplex position, but drives Maxx's head into the mat as a brainbuster. Curtis runs into the ropes and drops a measured elbow across the forehead of Maxx. He spins Maxx over and applies a camel clutch.] JG - "Another submission move by Curtis. He wants to make Maxx give up." JH - "Shouldn't be too tough." [Just as Maxx lifted his hand to tap out, Curtis released the hold. He gets to his feet, bringing Maxx with him and Irish whips Maxx into the turnbuckle. Curtis follows in with a running lariat. He keeps Maxx in the corner and drives his shoulder into the midsection. Curtis sends Maxx running into the opposite corner and again follows in with a lariat. Again, Curtis drives his shoulder into the chest of Maxx. Curtis takes the big man and bodyslams him back to the center of the mat. Curtis follows in with a big legdrop. He covers... 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Kickout in the first pin attempt of the match!" JH - "That's real surprising. A kickout in the first pin attempt of the match." [Curtis lifts Maxx to his feet and sends him into the ropes. He grabs Maxx around the chest and nails a spinebuster slam. Still holding onto Maxx's legs, Curtis catapults Maxx into the ropes. Maxx's neck bounces off, allowing Curtis to hit a back suplex. Curtis flips over into a cover... 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Almost had him there!" JH - "Curtis is turning it up a notch. I think he wants to get out of here...and I can't blame him." [Curtis slams the mat in disgust, then takes it out on Maxx with a rapid series of punches to the head. Maxx's nose begins to bleed from the constant attack. Curtis, however, continues his stinging blows. Curtis stops the attack for a more painful method as he lifts Maxx up. Curtis sets Maxx up into a powerbomb, but instead of dropping him to the mat, slams him into the top turnbuckle. The momentum of Maxx drops Curtis to the mat and drives Maxx's head into the mat.] JG - "What a move! Ken Curtis just lifted the 350 lbs. Stanlee Maxx well over his head and into a super version of the powerbomb." JH - "It took a lot out of Curtis, too. I think that's the first time he's touched the mat involuntarily this match." JG - "His feet are in constant contact with the mat." JH - "Very funny, Grey. You stick to the play by play, I'll offer the witty comments." [Curtis lifts up Maxx, not done with his attack. He sends Maxx running into the ropes and hits a running lariat. As Maxx's head snaps back to the mat, Curtis moves towards the ropes and follows in with a perfectly timed legdrop. Curtis picks up Maxx and whips him into the turnbuckle. He sets up and nails High Noon soon after.] JG - "He's got him!" JH - "Nobody gets up from High Noon." [Curtis goes for the cover... 1! 2! 3!] EJ - "The winner of this match, as a result of a pinfall, "Cowboy" Ken Curtis!" JH - "Nobody." JG - "I can see that. Folks, we've got a big match scheduled next. The World title is on the line as Eddy Jacks takes on Gangsta Impasse. Should be a great one." JH - "Just another win for the Jackster." JG - "It could be. Jacks has been impressive as champ, but Gangsta is also a former champ. He wants to get some more gold." JH - "So he can pawn it." JG - "We have lost our share of titles in the past. Let's get to the ring for the intros!" ___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 8 ) | | \ | | Eddy "Flap" Jacks vs. Gangsta Impasse / |___ | | | | Writer: AG (_________________________________________________________________________ Jaspard stands in the middle of the ring with the mic balanced on his nose. He begins speaking, but barely any sound comes out of the mic due to its position. Finally, Ed grabs the mic and pulls it down to his mouth.] EJ - "This matchup is scheduled for one fall with a remaining TV time limit. It is for the SCRA World Championship belt! Coming down the aisle first, the challenger..." [California Love plays over the speakers.] EJ - "He weighs in at 280 lbs. and hails from Detroit, Michigan, accompanied by John Drake, here comes Gangsta Impasse!" [Drake enters the arena, raising the roof on his way out. Gangsta soon follows, head covered by a boxing robe. He shadow boxes down the aisle, but stops half way, raising the roof and pumping the crowd with Drake. Gangsta continues shadow boxing as Drake moves to the ring. He opens up the ropes for his man and Gangsta enters, continuing his pre-match shadow boxing. Gangsta jumps on the turnbuckle and makes a motion for the belt. As he moves around the ring, the Dance of the Sugarplum Fairies takes over.] EJ - "And, his opponent, the current SCRA World champion, he weighs in at 398 lbs. and hails from Ottawa, Ontario, here comes Eddy "Flap" Jacks!" [The Praetorian Guard enters the arena, leading the way to the ring for Jacks. Jacks stands beside Savarese and the two walk down the aisle, talking over strategy. Their speedy entrance carries them into the ring fast with Jacks ready to fight.] JH - "What's that green stuff on the mic?" JG - "That's disgusting!" JH - "It was on Jaspard's nose." DING! DING! DING! [The two competitors walk to the center of the ring and quickly lock up. Jacks hits a forearm to the chest, bending over Gangsta. He quickly hits an elbow to the back which drops Gangsta. Jacks picks him up and whips Gangsta into the ropes, Jacks attempts a clothesline, but Gangsta ducks. Gangsta comes back to the center of the ring as Jacks holds up his big boot. Gangsta dives underneath the foot, clipping Jacks' other leg. The big man tumbles back as Gangsta covers... 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Almost had him there! Gangsta Impasse is really fighting with Jacks. Not many men can withstand Jacks' size, but Gangsta may be able to." JH - "I hear Jacks is putting on even more weight. He may be up to 649 lbs.!" JG - "What?" JH - "I don't know. It seems like every wrestler in this fed is either bulking up or slimming down." [Gangsta gets to his feet, lifting Jacks with him. He starts to hit Jacks with a series of hard chops to the chest, soon followed by punches to the face. Jacks absorbs the blows, grabs Gangsta by the neck and spins him around, switching positions. Jacks starts to throw a series of punches to the head. Gangsta slouches to the mat, forcing Jacks to bend over and lift him up. As he does this, though, Gangsta lifts his foot up to a big kick in the groin!] JG - "I guess Jacks' endless search for legendary status is now over with him being the only wrestler to talk like a girl." JH - "Wrestlers get low-blowed all the time. In fact, Jacks may be remembered for the wrestler not to drop and octave with his voice. His fat probably absorbed the blow." [Jacks staggers back as Gangsta gets up. He moves in and kicks Jacks again in the midsection, then drops him with a swinging neckbreaker. Gangsta quickly lifts up Jacks, whipping him into the turnbuckle. Gangsta follows in with a big splash. He grabs Jacks by the head and moves towards the next turnbuckle, ramming Jacks' head in. Gangsta continues his motion, moving to the next turnbuckle and ramming, to the next turnbuckle and finally to the original turnbuckle. Jacks drops to the mat. Gangsta climbs to the second rope and takes off, hitting a legdrop across the neck.] JG - "Gangsta is really, really attacking now. I don't think I've seen him fight like this." JH - "I don't think I've seen him fight." [Gangsta bends to lift Jacks, but Jacks gets a facerake in. As Gangsta staggers back, Jacks grabs him by the throat and drops him with a chokeslam. Jacks runs off the ropes and takes off with a big splash. He covers... 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JH - "How many people can get up from that big splash? The force coming down when Jacks executes it is unbelievable!" JG - "He sure gets his weight behind it." JH - "That thing must be stronger than the gas a day of beans gives ya!" [Jacks complains of a slow count for a second, but picks up Gangsta soon after. He sends Gangsta into the ropes and attempts a running clothesline. Gangsta ducks underneath, latching on for a crucifix. Jacks holds on, though, which leaves Gangsta in a bad placement. But, as Jacks was about to drop back with a fallaway slam, Gangsta slides out from underneath, sending Jacks crashing to the mat alone. Gangsta leaps onto Jacks' chest, throwing a series of hard right hands to the head. He gets off Jacks and sends the big man into the ropes, hitting a flying clothesline. Jacks drops to the mat as Gangsta applies a chinlock.] JG - "Finally, a rest hold!" JH - "I bet you like that sort of stuff, Grey. Why do we need a break from the action? Isn't the action what people came here to see?" JG - "I guess, but these chinlocks provide an effective way for wrestlers to catch their breath as well as wear down their opponent." JH - "That's what the technicians say." [Jacks begins to move out of the hold with elbows to the chest. Gangsta flies back after the blows, releasing the hold. Jacks gets up and takes Gangsta down with a running clothesline. He moves towards the turnbuckle and begins climbing.] JG - "Jacks going to the air?" JH - "That wacky cat. He's not afraid of anything, including heights." JG - "It's not the heights, it's the incredible speed he falls at." [As Gangsta gets up, Jacks takes off with a flying axehandle. Gangsta quickly lifts his hand and hits a punch to the midsection of Jacks. Jacks grabs his midsection and bends over, allowing Gangsta to move in with a knee lift. This sends Jacks flying back, allowing Gangsta to move off the ropes and hit an elbowdrop. As Gangsta gets up for another elbow, Jacks rolls out of the ring, attempting to catch his breath. Gangsta quickly follows to the apron, taking off with an asai moonsault!] JG - "WOW! Gangsta Impasse just nailed an asai moonsault! How can a man that big do that kind of move?" JH - "Who cares. He almost killed himself in the process and may have hurt himself more than he hurt Jacks." [Gangsta quickly gets up, lifting Jacks with him. He sends Jacks running into the ring post and as Jacks bounces off, hits a back suplex onto the concrete. Gangsta picks up Jacks and tosses him into the ring. As Gangsta climbs in, Jacks rolls away into the other corner. Gangsta follows him, but walks right into the trap as Jacks rakes the eyes. Jacks quickly gets up, or as quick as he can, and continues his attack of punches. He sends Gangsta into the ropes and drops him with an elbow. Jacks moves off the ropes and drops his leg across the neck of Gangsta.] JH - "That's one beefy piece of meat." JG - "It's sure a big chicken leg, if we're talking in food." JH - "Chicken leg? That thing's an elephant leg!" [Jacks gets up quickly, taking Gangsta with him. He whips Gangsta into the ropes and comes back with a knee to the midsection. Gangsta tumbles over, allowing Jacks to go for the pin... 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Almost had him there!" JH - "How can you finish a guy with a knee to the midsection?" JG - "I don't know. There have been some pretty lame finishers in the past. Maybe Jacks wanted to add to them." JH - "Yeah, that's something to be legendary about." [Jacks picks up Gangsta and is about to send him into the ropes, but stops himself and attempts a short clothesline. Gangsta ducks the clothesline attempt, but holds onto Jacks' arm. He kicks Jacks in the midsection, then lifts his leg over the arm and drives it into the mat. He quickly applies an armbar.] JG - "Gangsta really going to work on Jacks." JH - "He can barely wrap his hand around Jacks' arm! Look at that beast!" [Jacks begins to work his way out of the move, gets to his feet and nails Gangsta with a belly to belly suplex.] JH - "Squish!" [Jacks lifts Gangsta to his feet and drops him to the mat with a bodyslam. Jacks climbs to the second rope and takes off with a big splash, but Gangsta gets his knees up! Jacks stumbles back, but doesn't fall. Gangsta gets up and hits a kick to the midsection, but Jacks grabs the foot. Gangsta nails an enzuigiri. He quickly moves off the ropes and hits an elbowdrop. Jacks attempts to roll out of the ring, but Gangsta grabs him by the foot. Jacks uses his other foot and kicks Gangsta in the head. Jacks moves in and Irish whips Gangsta into the ropes. He nails a powerslam. Jacks runs off the ropes and drives a knee to the chest. Gangsta rolls over onto his knees only to be hit by a series of forearm shots to the back. Gangsta grabs the ropes and lifts himself up only to be met by a big knee lift. Jacks runs at him with a clothesline, but Gangsta ducks the move and kicks Jacks in the back of the leg. Jacks drops to the mat as Gangsta runs off the ropes and slides into the knee of Jacks. He quickly applies an ankle lock submission.] JG - "Gangsta's got the leg! He's got the ankle lock submission wrapped on!" JH - "Like that could make anybody submit. That thing's about as painful as watching a documentary on the history of the bikini." JG - "Which I'm sure you have taped." JH - "I've got it memorized! I've got the history shows every year from 1985 on. Not much changed in those videos from 1946-1985." JG - "Maybe that's because you can't change the past history." JH - "Past history? Geez, you're real smart, Grey." [Jacks pulls himself towards the ropes and grabs on, forcing Gangsta to release the hold. Gangsta bends to pick up Jacks, but Jacks nails a face rake. Jacks sends Gangsta running into the ropes, but Gangsta ducks a clothesline. On the way back, both men go for the clothesline and take each other down.] JG - "Both men are down!" JH - "Great observation, Grey. I really couldn't tell that from both men laying flat on their backs." [Gangsta begins to get to his feet, soon followed by Jacks. Jacks is the first to attempt an attack, but the punch is blocked by Gangsta. Gangsta then unleashes a punch of his own..another...another...another, Jacks falls back. Gangsta moves in for the cover... 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Almost had him there!" JH - "Yes, the punch is another great finisher." JG - "At least it wasn't the dreaded handshake." JH - "What? Grey, you're one odd cookie." [Gangsta picks up Jacks in an inverted atomic drop position. Instead of dropping him, though, Gangsta moves towards the ropes and nails a hotshot. As Jacks bounces back, Gangsta hits a forearm to the small of the back. Jacks' back arches, allowing Gangsta to get in with an abdominal stretch. The move doesn't last long due to the immense size of Jacks' stomach, so Gangsta releases it into a back suplex. Jacks tries to get up on his own, but Gangsta drops him with a matted DDT.] JH - "That was a weird move. How much force can you get from a 6 inch drop?" JG - "Enough to hurt Jacks, I guess." [Gangsta moves towards the ropes and attempts a baseball slide into Jacks, but Jacks moves out of the way. Gangsta slides right out of the ring into the House of Pancakes' area. The Praetorian Guard immediately grabs Gangsta and nails a double vertical suplex onto the mats outside.] JG - "Oh no! Strength in numbers is really showing right now!" JH - "And the great thing is the moronic referee can't even see past Jacks! Yet another good use for that giant frame of his." JG - "I think it's gross." JH - "The weight?" JG - "And the double teaming! Both make me sick!" JH - "What doesn't make you sick?" [The Guard goes to toss Gangsta into the ring, but Jacks motions for them not to. He slides outside himself and grabs Gangsta, tossing him into the steel post. Gangsta drops like a tonne of bricks. Jacks climbs onto the ring apron and steadies himself for a...] JH - "Big splash!" JG - "Nobody home! Gangsta just got out of the way and it saved his life!" JH - "Nothing can save that little punks life. Jacks' gonna get up and kill 'im." JG - "If they can get up. Both men are out cold and the referee is counting!" [The referee counts, but Gangsta is able to get to his feet. He lifts Jacks up which brings the Praetorian Guard running over. John Drake is able to sneak in from behind and nail both of them with a chair! Gangsta sends Jacks running into his two managers, Savarese and Surefoot. Jacks runs them over as Gangsta follows behind, hitting a lariat to the back of the head. As the entire House of Pancakes is on the mat, Gangsta takes a moment to raise the roof for the crowd. He sneaks in the ring before the referee finishes his count.] JH - "Lucky bastard. I bet he didn't even know the count was nearing 20. He probably can't count that high." JG - "Just because he speaks differently doesn't mean he's stupid. It's just his way of expressing himself." JH - "Anything different is bad. Just look at society and all the different people: Mexicans, Germans...well, that's about all the bad things I can think of." [Gangsta rolls back out, grabs Jacks' head and slams it into the guard rail. He moves towards the time keeper's table and slams it into that as well. Lifting Jacks' head back up, Gangsta motions to the crowd for one more. He runs towards the ring post and smashes Jacks head into it, then plants Jacks with a bulldog.] JG - "Oh my! Jacks' head is bouncing around more than a basketball!" JH - "That was pathetic. If you want to use either similes or metaphors, you have to make them clever. How about Jacks' head is getting planted more than a German Eagle!" JG - "That was pathetic, rude and incorrect." JH - "I try." [Gangsta rolls back in the ring, breaking the count again. He lifts up Jacks and sends him into the Praetorian Guard who are just getting up.] JG - "Wait a minute... what the...!?" [As Impasse makes his way towards Jacks, a concession worker, dressed in jeans, a white t-shirt, and a white baseball cap with "$1.50" printed on it, jumps the guardrail and brains Impasse with his hot-dog warmer!] EJ - "The winner of this match, as a result of disqualification, Gangsta Impasse!" JG - "The hot-dog man has just attacked Gangsta Impasse!" JH - "That's no hot-dog man, that's "Cowboy" Ken Curtis!" [Curtis stomps Impasse mercilessly as Impasse struggles to get to his feet! Curtis picks the hot-dog warmer up and waffles Impasse again, totally laying him out! The crowd gives a heavy heel pop to Curtis, who stands over Impasse, pointing his finger in his face and talking to him! Impasse is out cold as Curtis starts laying the boots to his ribs!] JG - "Curtis has gone absolutely ballistic!" JH - "Hey, he told Grey it was gonna get hot around here if he didn't get his title shot, I guess Impasse is the first to feel the heat!" [Jacks gets to his feet, grabs a hot dog from Curtis' stand and walks up the aisle. He eats it quickly, then moves back and grabs two more. Jacks eats those and motions for more, but Tommy Gregory and a host of security officials storm the ringside area, pulling Curtis away and up the ramp. Curtis leans down as they pull him away and grabs a hot-dog from the ground! With a wicked grin, he begins eating the hot-dog as he's being dragged away! The grin turns to a scowl as he violently pulls away from Gregory, shoving a finger in his face and yelling, pieces of hot-dog spewing from his mouth! Gregory orders him to the back and Curtis turns, giving Gregory the finger over his shoulder as he splits the curtain and disappears! With Curtis gone, Gregory turns his attention to the bloodied Impasse, who is still laid out near the ring!] JG - "Impasse is going to need medical attention! Curtis has completely snapped!" JH - "You couldn't see this coming? Curtis is a maniac!" JG - "Yeah, it takes a big man to attack a man who just wrestled a hard-fought match! From behind, no less!" JH - "The man does things his own way, Grey!" JG - "Whatever you say. Hey, we're out of time folks! We'll see you next week!" -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
