[SCRA] The Scraps [4/4] (26 January 1998)

26 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 6 ) | | \ | | "Cowboy" Ken Curtis vs. Gangsta Impasse / |___ | | | | Writer: AG (_________________________________________________________________________ [Jaspard stands in the middle of the ring with the mic on a stand, unusual for ring announcements.] EJ - "O Canada! Our home and native land!" JH - "Aren't they supposed to sing the national anthem at the start of the show?" JG - "Ed is, well, Ed. He does what he wants." EJ - "You don't like my music?" [The broadcasters look at each other with an odd look.] JG - "He can hear us?" EJ - "Ya, ya, the entrances. First, weighing in at 260 lbs. and hailing from Austin, Texas..."Cowboy" Ken Curtis!" [Gimme back my Bullets by Lynard Skynard takes over the stadium PA systems as Curtis enters the arena in a slow jog. He walks straight to the ring, all business in this one.] JH - "Curtis wants this on! Gangsta's gonna be shovelling some sh..." EJ - "And, his opponent, weighing in at 280 lbs. and hailing from Detroit, Michigan, Gangsta Impasse!" [Curtis eyes Gangsta who drives down in his Benz, John Drake in the front seat with the rap music blaring in the background. The two drive down the expanded aisle to the ring, fans trying to slap at the car. Gangsta holds a microphone and begins to speak to Curtis in the ring.] GI - "Now Curtis...dat's de second time in two weeks you done dat te me. Now eitha I have bad luck wit' weenies, which I don't think I do considering I got through Stanlee Maxx pretty damn fast..." [Gangsta looks up with an odd look on his face, very proud of himself as the crowd pops big time.] GI - "Or Curtis, yer gon' getcher ass whipped you unoriginal bastard!" [As the car pulls up beside the ring, Curtis walks outside onto the apron. He leaps off the apron and dropkicks the front windshield!] JG - "Oh no! Ken Curtis just sprayed the glass in the front windshield right into Gangsta Impasse and John Drake!" JH - "The two bums deserved it. Let's hope they got a few cuts from that little blow." JG - "I don't know if that was smart. If Curtis loses this one, that's all the more car he's going to have to clean." JH - "Wouldn't that be a smart move? _If_ he loses, he won't have to clean the windshield because it's not there. Geez, Grey. I thought you were smarter than that." [Curtis gets off the car, large imprint of his body in the hood, and moves towards Gangsta. He attempts to open up the door, but it is locked. Instead of unlocking the door, Curtis just pulls Gangsta over the door onto the outside mat. He sends Gangsta running into the ringpost with a loud thud. Curtis moves in and tosses Gangsta into the ring, the bell sounding to start the match.] DING! DING! DING! JG - "And we're underway!" [Curtis moves towards Gangsta, picks up the weary Gangsta and sends him into the ropes. Curtis attempts a clothesline, but Gangsta ducks underneath the lariat and comes back with a flying cross body block. Curtis catches Gangsta, but can't keep his balance. He stumbles back towards the ropes, with Gangsta in his arms, and eventually falls through the middle ropes. Gangsta lands on top of Curtis and starts throwing furious punches to the head of Curtis.] JG - "Gangsta is going nuts! The two fell right to the outside, but that landed Gangsta Impasse in a perfect position to stay on the offensive." JH - "Lucky bastard. Gangsta thinks his rap is "all that". He thinks that he can beat Curtis with these little punches. Let me tell you something, Grey, Ken Curtis can throw with the best of them." JG - "You know from experience? You often get into disagreements while interviewing Mr. Curtis." JH - "Disagreements? Those are friendly quarrels. Big difference." [Gangsta gets off Curtis as the ref's count reached eight and tosses him into the ring. Gangsta dives in, rolls on top of Curtis and continues with the punches. Curtis is able to get his legs up and wrap them around Gangsta's shoulders, bringing Gangsta down to the mat in a pin... 1! 2! KICKOUT! Both Gangsta and Curtis leap to their feet and lock eyes. They begin to circle each other.] JG - "Both men have already beaten each other. Yet, they are still trying to find some form of leverage with this circling to get in an even better attack." JH - "Nothing beats the punch, though. And, nobody beats Jack Hazard to the punch. Get it?" JG - "Yeah. Dual meanings, very funny." JH - "See? I got the punch line in! Nobody beats Hazard!" [Curtis and Gangsta lock up, but Curtis gets in a quick boot to the midsection. Gangsta bends over allowing Curtis to grab Gangsta by the head and toss it back into the mat. Gangsta rolls on the mat and gets up before any further Curtis attacks. Gangsta walks towards the turnbuckle, back turned to Curtis. Curtis runs in and nails a clothesline to the back of Gangsta's head, sending him tumbling into the corner turnbuckle. Curtis picks up Gangsta in an atomic drop, but Gangsta pushes off the top turnbuckle and flips over Curtis. He places Curtis into a waistlock which Curtis quickly reverses into one of his own. Gangsta re-reverses the waistlock and tosses Curtis back with a release German.] JH - "This match is going technical. And, it's not even the technical wrestling I fear every week. You know, the figure fours and armbars. This stuff is worse. This is amateur technical wrestling!" JG - "Many of these wrestlers have a background in high school or college wrestling. It's no wonder they still use some of those waistlock skills today." JH - "Those aren't skills. Those are just little grabs the all-male schools taught their gay students to provoke fear in the opposing straight schools." [Curtis lifts himself in the opposite corner as Gangsta rushes in with an elbow. Curtis ducks underneath the ropes as Gangsta's elbow hits an empty corner. Curtis, from outside, pulls Gangsta's legs from underneath him and bangs it into the ringpost. Using the weak legs of Gangsta, Curtis applies a figure four. The referee drops to the mat to check on Gangsta which gives John Drake an open opportunity to intervene in the match. With the fuzzy dice off the Benz, Drake moves towards Curtis and chokes him. The referee gets up and turns outside, but Drake slipped the dice into his pants. With a giant bulge in the midsection, Drake winks at the referee.] JH - "That's disgusting. I bet Drake was a teacher at those all-male schools." JG - "He is an outstanding teacher..." JH - "Don't even bring up the point! I don't think I can even look at those wonderful fuzzy dice anymore. It's time to look for a new car ornament in the Hazard-mobile." JG - "It was just a cover up, Jack. Drake didn't want the ref thinking he cheated." JH - "Ah! That makes it worse!" [Gangsta rolls out of the ring, but instead of going after Curtis, he moves into his car and yanks out the stereo. Still blaring the rap music, Gangsta moves back towards Curtis and puts the stereo against Curtis' ear. Curtis grabs his head, trying to mute out the sounds. Eventually, he has to lift his knee and catch Gangsta in the groin, thus dropping the stereo. Curtis rolls over and picks up the stereo. He gets to his feet, tosses the stereo down beside Gangsta's head and stomps on the stereo, thus halting the music.] JH - "Look at that! Curtis just crushed the little stereo like a bug!" JG - "Just be glad that it wasn't a full blown stereo/speaker system." JH - "Why not? Speakers would make one helluva set of weapons." [Curtis tosses the borken pieces of stereo into the crowd who eagerly attempt to hold a piece of SCRA "history". Curtis then tosses Gangsta into the ring and follows with a series of stomps to the chest. He chokes Gangsta across the throat with his big cowboy boot before the referee steps in. Curtis moves away to argue with the ref, but Gangsta moves in from behind and catches him in a rollup... 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Almost got him! Curtis has to learn to focus on the match." JH - "Hell, for once I'm focussing on the match. It's better than Drake's midsection." JG - "I thought you liked managers with bulges in their clothes." JH - "That's funny, Grey. But, I'm Jack Hazard. If a man's got a bulge, I say send him back. If a women's got a bulge, I say send her in. Then again, if the bulge is too low, I say send her back with the man." [Curtis springs up, but Gangsta stays on the mat and takes Curtis down with a drop toe hold. Holding onto the leg, Gangsta moves onto Curtis' back and applies a camel clutch.] JG - "He's got a stronger variation of the STF going." JH - "Just like Gangsta. Doesn't know how to do a real STF, so he has to make one up." [Curtis grabs the ropes, breaking the hold. Gangsta gets off Curtis and raises the roof, getting a loud cheer from the crowd. Gangsta uses these to pump himself up as he moves into Curtis. As Gangsta bends, though, Curtis gets in a face rake, turning the cheers for Gangsta to boos for Curtis. Curtis gets to his feet and drops the staggering Gangsta with a running lariat. He moves off the ropes and drops an elbow across the chest of Gangsta, holding the elbow there for added measure. Curtis releases the modified chokehold and gets to his feet, mocking Gangsta by raising the roof.] JG - "He's mocking Gangsta!" JH - "It isn't that hard. Gangsta's an easier target to hit than your mama!" JG - "I believe it's yo mama. (sighing) Some people are so out of touch with the hip lingo." [Gangsta gets to his feet, but is quickly grabbed by Curtis. Curtis nails a backbreaker, holding Gangsta across the knee for extra pressure on the lower back. Curtis grabs Gangsta by the head and lifts him straight up. He sends Gangsta running into the turnbuckle and follows in with a big lariat, sending Gangsta right over the top turnbuckle. Gangsta walks around the outside, recovering for a moment while Curtis urges him to come into the ring. Curtis again mocks Gangsta by raising the roof. Gangsta simply walks towards his Benz, climbs onto the roof and raises the roof himself! The crowd responds with a loud series of cheers.] JG - "It's a battle for the crowd response, now! Although, I think Gangsta wants the cheers and Curtis wants the boos." JH - "I'm a man of compromise. How about the crowd just shuts up." [Curtis, irate at the loud response Gangsta received, reaches over the ropes at Gangsta. Gangsta leaps onto the apron and hits a shoulderblock to the chest of Curtis. Gangsta reaches over the ropes himself and sets up Curtis for a...] JG - "Suplex onto the car!" JH - "Holy cow! Curtis just destroyed the entire front side of the car." JG - "Less to clean, right?" JH - "I don't know! I think Gangsta must be going mad for suplexing Curtis onto the car. Do you know how much body work on a car costs these days?" [Both men stay on the car for a moment, exhausted and hurt from the force of the blow. John Drake moves in and starts to help Gangsta come to, offering smelling salts which he happened to have in the car. Gangsta gets a kick from the salts, stands over the fallen body of Curtis on the car, looks to the crowd and raises the roof!] JH - "Kick him in the nuts!" JG - "Gangsta Impasse is really taking it to Curtis with his rap style, his liking for the fans, all that sort of stuff. These two are so different, they hate each other." JH - "Just like us, eh Grey? But, it's all the hate which piles up between us that provides the funny show we call the Scraps. That's why people put on good matches, too. They hate each other." JG - "What an amazing observation, Jack. I never thought that people fight because they don't like each other." [Curtis does roll over and attempt to lift his leg up for a kick to the groin, but Gangsta grabs the foot then drives it down into the hood of the car. Curtis grabs his leg which causes him to lose his balance and fall off the hood of the car. Gangsta climbs down, picks up Curtis and tosses him into the ring. Gangsta hops onto the apron and climbs to the top turnbuckle, perched and waiting for Curtis to get up. Curtis gets to his feet and Gangsta takes off, nailing an axehandle to the top of the head. Curtis drops to the mat as Gangsta hops on for the pin... 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JH - "Don't be fooled by the fall. Curtis knows how to fall hard, but that little axehandle didn't hurt him one bit. He'll be up in a second and start to club Gangsta." JG - "They know how to fall, but that one caught Curtis right on the top of the head. I don't think that was a voluntary fall." [Gangsta picks up Curtis and tosses him into the ropes attempting a kick to the midsection, but Curtis catches his foot. Gangsta thinks quickly and nails an enzuigiri. Curtis drops to the mat and rolls into the corner, attempting to lift himself up. Gangsta rushes in and nails a hurricarana!] JG - "Wow! A hurricarana by the 280 lbs. Gangsta Impasse! I don't think I've seen a big man execute one." JH - "And they shouldn't. That was sloppier than moma's meatsauce." [As Curtis bounces into the opposite corner, Gangsta rushes in and lifts him to his feet in an atomic drop position. Gangsta sits Curtis on the top turnbuckle, climbs to the second turnbuckle himself and takes off with a back suplex!] JG - "That was a super back suplex!" JH - "Not the same flair as a superplex. How about a supbackplex?" [Both men roll around the mat, but Gangsta recovers first, rolling on top of Curtis for the cover... 1! 2! KICKOUT! Gangsta lifts Curtis to his feet, then nails a tilt a whirl suplex. Moving off the ropes, Gangsta drops a leg across the chest of Curtis. He holds the leg across the chest, applying a scissorlock on Curtis. Curtis attempts to get out of the move, clawing at Gangsta's face, but to no avail. Eventually, he bites Gangsta's leg!] JH - "I'm getting a little hungry. With all these gay remarks and this, we've got two forms of biting in this match." JG - "That is one of the strangest, weirdest and grossest things you've ever said." [Gangsta releases the lock, then gets to his feet, limping on his right leg. He goes to pick up Curtis, but Curtis hits a punch to the midsection. Curtis gets to his feet with continued punches to the gut, then runs into the ropes and takes Gangsta down with a running lariat. Curtis continues to run into the ropes, forcing Gangsta to roll over in front of him. Curtis hops over Gangsta and continues running. On the way back, Gangsta leaps at Curtis with a Thesz press, but Curtis catches him and turns it into a hotshot. The Cowboy quickly hops onto Gangsta and throws a wild series of punches to the head.] JG - "Both men have gone nuts on the heads of the others..." JH - "No more of those references!" JG - "Jack, I wasn't talking about that. I think you're a little obsessed with our differently lifestyled friends." JH - "Obsessed, scared out of my mind, what's the difference, right?" [Curtis gets off Gangsta, lifting Gangsta to his feet. He sends Gangsta into the ropes and executes a spinebuster into the middle of the ring. Curtis climbs to the second rope and takes off, hitting a fistdrop to the midsection of Gangsta. Again, Curtis lifts up Gangsta, this time Irish whipping him into the turnbuckle. Curtis follows in, but Gangsta propelled himself off the turnbuckle and over top Curtis. However, Curtis is able to catch Gangsta over his shoulder. He moves into the middle of the ring and nails a powerslam. Curtis hops on the mat and covers... 1! KICKOUT!] JG - "Outstanding stamina shown by Gangsta Impasse, and for that matter, Ken Curtis. Both men have gone through one helluva beating here tonight, yet both don't want to quit." JH - "It's like I always say: You can punch 'em, you can kick 'em, but you can't pin 'em." JG - "I've never heard you say that." JH - "Oh, right. Well, consider it my new saying." [Curtis, out of pure frustration, slaps Gangsta in the side of the head. This, however, angers Gangsta who gets to his feet, shaking a finger at Curtis. Curtis attempts a punch, but Gangsta blocks it. Gangsta starts firing away with punches of his own, but is soon stopped by Curtis with a brutal face rake. As Gangsta stumbles back, Curtis hits a kick to the midsection, soon followed by a swinging neckbreaker. Gangsta slowly gets to his feet, but is met by a swift kick by Curtis. Curtis lifts up Gangsta and sets up for a vertical suplex. He hits it. Gangsta wobbles on the mat, so Curtis stays on the attack. He picks up Gangsta and sends him running into the ropes, connecting with a belly to belly suplex. Curtis runs into the ropes himself and comes down with a knee drop, but Gangsta moved.] JG - "He missed!" JH - "Fear motivated that save. Gangsta moved out of that spot faster than a cat on fire." JG - "Are we going to suffer from the childhood memories of Jack Hazard?" [Curtis rolls over on his knee, but is met by a series of stomps by Gangsta. These push Curtis to the ropes, allowing him to lift himself up. Gangsta uses this to grab Curtis and hit a snap suplex into the middle of the ring. Gangsta quickly climbs to the top and takes off with a swan dive headbutt. Curtis moves.] JG - "Twice in a matter of a few seconds!" JH - "Did you see Gangsta's head? It bounced up there faster than a..." JG - "No more memories!" JH - "I was going to say basketball. But, now that you remind me, Gangsta's head did fly faster than poop from a tree. I remember that as a young lad, climbing trees, dropping our pants and dropping the logs!" [Curtis picks up Gangsta and nails a double underhook suplex, then flips himself over Gangsta into a bridge... 1! 2! 3!] JG - "He got him!" JH - "Phew! Now when I go on those interviews, the barn will be actually fragrantly acceptable...for a few days." [Curtis gets out of the ring quickly, moving towards Gangsta's car. He starts to tear away at the seat covers.] JG - "Curtis is destroying the car. This is uncalled for." JH - "Hey, if he doesn't have to clean it, why not destroy it!" [Gangsta stirs in the ring, sees what Curtis is doing and sets himself up.] JG - "Gangsta looks mad. What's he going to do?" [With a running start, Gangsta leaps over the top ropes and hits a swan dive through the already shattered windshield onto Ken Curtis! The two start exchanging punches in the front seat!] JG - "Oh my! Folks, we're out of time! Good night!" -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
