[SCRA] The Scraps [3/5] (9 February 1998)

9 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| North American Tournament Match 4 ) | | \ | | "Golden King" Aladdin Style vs. Sgt. Pepper / |___ | | | | Writer: Mike M. (_________________________________________________________________________ [Jaspard stands in the middle of the ring, underwear on his head and shirt on his legs.] JH - "Looks like Ed not only says the announcements backwards, but gets dressed backwards." EJ - "Coming down the aisle first, weighing in at 242 lbs. and hailing from Liverpool, England, he is Sgt. Pepper!" [The crowd begins to pop as the strains of "It's getting better all the time." by the Beatles starts to play. Sgt. Pepper and his entourage make their way down the ramp, all of them with smiles on their face as they bounce their way down to the ring area. Pepper makes sure to slap every hand in sight, even going back to get some of hands he missed.] JH - "Getting better all the time. Boy Pepper's really lost it now. Things are about as good for him as they are for the Jamaican Bobsled team." JG - "But you can't deny that the fans here in SCRA love him." JH - "Hey, people love the Backstreet boys and Tofu, that tells you a little something about fan favourites doesn't it?" JG - "Well here's someone that the fans have taken a bit of a disliking to over the past few weeks." JH - "And ever since then he's watched his stock soar. Nothing says success like the loud boos coming from the ignorant masses." EJ - "And, his opponent, weighing in at 223 lbs. and hailing from Fredericton, New Brunswick, he is "Golden King" Aladdin Style!" [Aladin Style begins to walk down the ramp even before the music has stopped playing, the technicians rush to get the Cure on, Style viciously arms out of his way as the fans who have held onto their fascination with him attempt to get a touch of their favourite wrestler.] DING! DING! DING! [Pepper immediately runs at Style with a flying clothesline,which Style ducks, Pepper comes back off the ropes and Style meets him with a leg take down. Style gets off Pepper and allows Pepper to get to his knee, suddenly he delivers a huge boot right to Pepper's nose] JG - "Pepper's got to watch out for that stuff, he should have known that Style is a changed man." JH - "I think what Pepper should do is hop out of that ring and head for the showers. It'll be the first match in weeks that he hasn't been pinned." [Style delivers a perfect reverse armbar to the throat of Pepper who had just gotten back to his knees. He lifts Pepper by his hair, to his feet but Pepper alertly spins and forces Style into a reverse arm bar, he then pushes Style into the ropes, Syles springboards himself off the ropes and attempts a moonsault on Pepper, but his knee gives out and the move is botched as Style lands on his neck.] JG - "Ohhh, that has got to hurt, Style tried to revert to his old ways but it's obvious that his knee is just not ready yet." JH - "Style should have taken a lesson from Value Jet and stayed out of the skies for a while." [The crowd loves this and begins to cheer wildly as Pepper lifts Style to his feet , he waves at the crowd as he executes a nice belly to belly suplex. Pepper gets to his feet , lifts Style and offers him a trip into the ropes, then delivering a hiptoss which Style reverses into an armdrag takedown. Style quickly slaps on an armbar and pushes he knee to the throat of Pepper. The ref counts to four, Style breaks the hold by climbing to his feet and using Peppers throat as a base of support. Style slyly watches for Pepper to make it to his feet, then runs to the ropes and comes back with an excellent rolling kick.] JH - "Pepper really took that one hard..." JG - "Style goes for the pin.. 1! 2! KICKOUT!" JH - "Just denying the inevitable, that's all Peppers doing JG - "Don't underestimate this man. He needs a win badly and we all know what desperation can do for a man." JH - "You're right Grey. Pepper needs another loss about as much as I need the President telling me how great the NLWP was." JG - "It was great" JH - "Oh God please no more..." [Style helps Pepper to his feet by kindly placing his fingers in Pepper's eye sockets and picking him up like he would a bowing ball. Pepper yell in pain as Style fires him into the turnbuckle, which Pepper meets face first, Style follows up closely behind him, slides to his knees and scoops through Style legs for a pin... 1! 2! KICKOUT! Style quickly drops an elbow, but Pepper roles out of the way and allows Style to deliver some punishment ot the canvas instead of Pepper's abdomin. Pepper drops on to Syles and offers him a leg submission as a favour for all the nice things Style has done to him thus far. Style intelligently looks around him and sees that the ropes are in sight. He stretches his arm just far enough to get a piece of the bottom rope. As Pepper gentlemanly breaks the hold Style sweeps his leg and quickly pick him up by the hair...] JH - "I thought Pepper told us he was going to try to brawl his way to victory in this match, not wrestle his way to another loss." JG - "He did say he was going to try to brawl with the smaller man, however Style never makes it easy for his opponent to execute a game plan. Espescially when advancing to the North American Tournament semi-finals is at stake." JH - "Steak?" JG - "No S T A K E" JH - "MMM steak." [Pepper, as though he heard the two announcers talking, suddently remembers his strategy and delivers a kick to the midsection, Style doubles over in pain as Pepper begins to unleash fist after fist to the top of Style's head. However, Style quickly lifts the bigger man by the midsection and drops his throat onto the top rope] JH - "Peppers dreams of singing have officially been ended by Style this match, I haven't seen anyones throat take this much of a beating since that movie... what was it... Deep." JG - "Hey, boys and girls, take a look at that face paced action in the ring, both wrestlers showing their vast amount of wrestling counters." JH - "Let me count, I've watched that movie at least 20 times..." [Style, sensing that Pepper is sufficiently dazed, begins to set him up for the "Driver'. Pepper however has enough sense to realize this and monkey flips his smaller opponent. Pepper turns around to offer his fists to the fallen rulebreaker and the crowd cheers as he pops several well placed shots to the side of Style head. He lifts Style to the feet and Irish whips him into the ropes, upon returning, Style is met with a kick to the midsection. The stunned Style has little time to recover when Pepper again unleashes a flury to his head. The crowd begin to cheer as they sense the moment has finally shifted towards Pepper. He again fires Style towards the ropes and drops the smaller man with a flying shoulder block. The crowd cheers harder this time as the man from Liverpool drops a boot to the midsection of the fallen Style. He moves towards Style legs and signals for the Crabalocker and the fans go nuts. However as he begins to apply it, Style smartly gives him a boot to the rear and which sends Pepper to the ropes, Style quickly kips to his feet but he is just in time to be clotheslined by Pepper, the crowd is loving every second of this as they can sense and upset. Pepper drops for the cover... 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Whoah, that was close, we almost saw the number seven seed upset the number two seed." JH - "Ya but now Style looks extremely pissed about this whole situation." [The two men get to their feet and Pepper works Style into a headlock, he attempts to use it to take down Style, Style however, slips out of and pushes Pepper face first into the mat. He then bounces himself off the ropes and returns in order to step all over the backside of Pepper from head to toe.] JG - "That was viscious!" JH - "What a move, now that's innovation! [Style lifts Pepper to his feet and executes a sound belly to back suplex, he then moves quickly to Peppers legs which he begins to work on with an indian death lock. He breaks the hold and then splits Peppers legs wide apart, tearing at the groin muscles. He lifts Pepper to his feet and whips him into the ropes. Pepper's legs look wobbly as he returns from the ropes and Style takes his legs out from under him with a sliding leg sweep. Pepper looks dazed and Style quickly slaps on a texas clover leaf.] JG - "Oh no Peppers in trouble!" JH - "Yah, but his losing streak isn't... when a technician like Style gets you in a hold like this there ain't much you can do but hope that the ref calls for the bell before the hold snaps your legs or your back." [Pepper submits to the Texas Cloverleaf.] JG - "Style's got the win! He moves onto face Bobby McAllister two weeks from today in the second round!" JH - "A big squash win for Style coming up. He needs one of those every now and then." [The camera opens in on Prodigy & Kats' locker room. Prodigy is doing one armed push ups as Kat is counting them.] BK - "97, 98, 99, 100.... Okay, you can take a breather now as the SCRA camera are here." [Prodigy sits up and looks into the camera. He looks into the camera as he wraps a towel around his neck and has some water from the water bottle. He then passes the bottle to Kat, who sits overhead him on the bench rubbing his shoulders.] P - "Last night, I survived to the final three in the battle royal. I know that's nothing to be proud of and I'm not. But you see for a guy who was double and tripled teamed all night that's damn good. If it weren't for Rocky and The Lonely Harts Club double teaming me all night I would have made it all the way. Cause Gangsta you didn't survive much after me, so I was almost final two. Now tonight is the SCRA's, Ice Capades In A Cage or whatever it's called. I face off, with 4 others. Now this I consider to be a very easy task at that. My opponents in this Ice Capades Match are Nik Nyce, Chase Hunter, Gangsta Impasse, and The Outlaw. As far as I see Nyce and Hunter are a bunch or rookies who still have a lot to learn and will be left in my dust. My real opponents are gonna be a Gangsta and a Outlaw. I know that both these guys are top notch athletes and I will not be able to let my guard down once. But I have a advantage over these two men. My being born and raised in Canada and my experience playing hockey. See hockey and tonights match are closely related. In hockey your trapped in a rink and you have to beat the defense men who wanna crush you to get the goal. In this case, I'm gonna be trapped in a cage and the other team is the other four men in the cage and the goal in this case is the shot at the gold." BK - "Hmmm....do you feel like you have another gun pointed at you head?" [Kat smirks] P - "I think so, which means that after tonight Kenny your mine. But tonight Nyce, Hunter, Gangsta, and Outlaw you'd better come to that ring prepared for the brawl of your life cause this ain't no Smurfs on Ice." BK - "Ok, back to training now big boy." [Prodigy, gets up and heads over to the punching bag. He goes to work on it as the camera fades out.] JG - "Ice Capades in a Cage, pretty good." JH - "With him in it, it's Smurfs. Hell, it's worse than smurfs. It's Disney!" JG - "That hurts, Jack. Next up is the big tag match of the night... well, the second big one." JH - "It's odd we have two big tag matches in one night. When was the last time that happened? Ever?" JG - "This one should be good. Three tag wrestlers in the ring at the same time fighting for when they get their title shots." JH - "Two brawlers and a, well, nobody." JG - "Let's go to Ed Jaspard for the intros." ___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Tag Team Triple Threat Match ) | | \ | | Walt Jabasco vs. Andrei Bolotov vs. Bill Baer / |___ | | | | Writer: Alex G. (_________________________________________________________________________ [Jaspard comes down to the ring dressed exactly like Walt Jabasco.] EJ - "My name's Ed Jabasco, Walt's long lost brother. It's true, I've been hiding it, but it's time to come out. Jaspard, Jabasco, it's all the same." JG - "Ed, the intros." EJ - "Oh, right. This one's scheduled for one fall, elimination style by pinfall or submission. The team who wins gets a pay per view title shot and so on backwards. Coming down the aisle first, weighing in at 290 lbs. and hailing from London, England, my long lost brother, Walt Jabasco!" [Jabasco walks out to the Missiom Impossible theme, shaking his head along the way. Tavis and Grant follow behind him, but is soon motioned back by Tommy Gregory.] JG - "I guess Gregory doesn't want all the managers and other wrestlers on the outside. This one's gonna be clean." JH - "Clean is for the weak." EJ - "Coming down next, hailing from Russian and weighing in at 340 lbs., the "Russian Bear" Andrei Bolotov!" [Bolotov walks in, accompanied by Ivan Dragovich and Alexander Gudonov, but are again pushed back by Tommy Gregory. Bolotov argues, but to no avail. He continues his way down to the ring, mocking fans along the way, but not the same as when Gudonov does it.] EJ - "And, the final opponent, weighing in at 310 lbs. and hailing from Charlotte, North Carolina, "Klondike" Bill Baer!" [Baer walks out to the Dance of the Sugar Plum Fairies, once again accompanied by the House of Pancakes. Gregory almost lets it go by, annoyed at pushing everybody back, but doesn't. Baer argues, but walks down by himself. He mocks the fans in the same way as Bolotov, then hops into the ring, ready to fight.] JG - "We're going to see a clean match tonight. None of the tag partners and managers are here." JH - "It's a lot less crowded down here, too." DING! DING! DING! [The three men look at each other, trying to find an angle of attack. Eventually, Baer turns to Jabasco and starts throwing punches to the head. Bolotov sees the two attacking each other and runs at them, hitting Baer with a football tackle which also takes down Jabasco. Bolotov starts throwing alternating punches on both men, picking up Jabasco and hitting a powerbomb, into a cover... 1! Baer makes the save!] JH - "I don't think Bill Baer would ever help out Walt Jabasco, except for tonight." JG - "But, if Jabasco gets pinned, it's to Baer's advantage. He would be assured a title shot closer to the pay per view." JH - "I never said Baer was the brightest banana in the bunch." [Baer grabs Bolotov and tosses him into the ropes, grabbing Bolotov in the side and hitting a spinning sidewalk slam. He gets back up and starts to stomp on Bolotov, but Jabasco runs in from behind and hits a clothesline to the back of the head. Jabasco lifts Baer to his feet, then Irish whips him into the turnbuckle. Jabasco follows in with a flying knee. He holds Baer in the corner, then unleashes a series of kicks to the knee and midsection. Bolotov gets up and moves to the corner, spins Jabasco around and nails a kick to the midsection. As Jabasco bends over, Bolotov hooks the arms and executes a tiger driver. As Jabasco's on his way down, Baer drops his leg across the neck of Jabasco.] JG - "A pretty good double team move, despite the two bears going at it last night and never teaming up before." JH - "Pretty good, but could've been better. Baer's gotta get the leg drop timed better." [Baer looks towards Bolotov in an alliance-like manner, but Bolotov quickly ends this with a kick to the midsection. Bolotov moves towards Baer and clotheslines him over the top.] JG - "Baer's not eliminated, but this could be a chance for Bolotov to get Jabasco one on one and perhaps pin him. Or, maybe the other way around." JH - "Nice one, Grey! Usually, your meagre attempts at jokes don't have an affect on me, but this one, I think I'm going to wet my pants!" JG - "It wasn't a joke." JH - "Even funnier!" [Jabasco slides underneath Bolotov. Bolotov turns around, but sees Jabasco coming at him with a dropkick. This sends Bolotov over the top, right at the feet of Baer. Jabasco climbs to the top rope, then leaps to the outside with a flying cross body block. Baer and Bolotov catch the Pro, then hit a double backbreaker.] JH - "Jabasco's got the mentality of a 12 year old. He doesn't think before he does." JG - "But, he's got the energy of a 12 year old, too. Jabasco always goes nuts." JH - "Wouldn't you want the energy of somebody a little bit older? I don't remember that far back, but I think that the teens were my big energy years." [Baer turns to Bolotov and attempts a clothesline, but Bolotov ducks, grabs Baer's arm and hits a back suplex on the outside. Bolotov tosses Jabasco into the ring, then quickly follows in. Jabasco rolls on the apron, trying to get away from Bolotov, but can't. Bolotov drops to his knees and drives an elbow into the chest of Jabasco. Bolotov drops on top of Jabasco and starts hurling punches to the head of Jabasco. Baer gets onto the apron, then moves towards Bolotov. Bolotov gets up quickly and takes down Baer with a powerslam.] JG - "Bolotov is going nuts on everybody. He's really using his size to a big advantage." JH - "What else is he going to do? It's not like Bolotov can hit those moonsault things. He's gotta use his size or else he's just another Russian." [Jabasco starts to get up, but is Irish whipped into the turnbuckle by Bolotov. Bolotov follows in with a big splash, but Jabasco moves out of the way. As Bolotov staggers back, Jabasco moves in and hits an atomic drop. Bolotov stays up, allowing Jabasco to move back to the front side and grab Bolotov, nailing a hotshot.] JG - "Jabasco's really taking control of Andrei Bolotov." JH - "The Bear is being tamed. I just wanna know how it's Jabasco doing the taming." [Bill Baer moves in, catching Jabasco off guard with a series of knife edged chops to the chest. Jabasco starts fighting back with his own series of chops. Eventually, the two alternate hard chops to the chest. Bolotov recovers from the hotshot, then charges at the two choppers. Jabasco bends over, forcing Bolotov to take flight in an awkward position due to one arm attempting a clothesline and the other half of his body being knocked into the air by Jabasco. Baer just turns towards Jabasco and starts unleashing a series of European uppercuts, then an elbow to the head. Baer tosses Jabasco into the ropes, but can't execute his clothesline due to Bolotov hitting a drop toe hold. Bolotov starts throwing punches to the head of Jabasco, prompting Baer to come in and attempt to get his own punches in. Bolotov rakes the eyes, allowing him to continue the attack.] JG - "It's a fight for who wants to attack Jabasco more!" JH - "Let me in the ring! I'll give Jabasco a few whacks." JG - "I'm sure you would. But, could you fight off Bolotov and Baer to get the shot?" JH - "The eye rakes and punches are still there, Grey." [Bolotov gets off Jabasco and runs at the stumbling Baer, grabbing with a bear hug. Jabasco just sits and watches as Baer sits in the hold...and gives up!] JG - "Bill Baer is gone! Andrei Bolotov made him submit to the deadly bear hug!" JH - "Isn't this how the battle royal was last night? Are we gonna see a repeat?" JG - "I don't know. It could be that way, but you never know what could happen in SCRA!" JH - "Uh, that's not that accurate." [Bolotov tosses Baer to the ground, who then crawls under the ring. Jabasco moves in from behind Bolotov and hits a dropkick. He continues the attack with a series of elbowdrops to the chest of Bolotov. Jabasco stops the elbowdrops and starts stomping in the midsection of Bolotov. He lifts Bolotov to his feet and sends him into the turnbuckle, moving in and cornering Bolotov soon after. Jabasco starts to unleash a series of martial arts kicks on Bolotov, eventually knocking the Russian Bear to the mat.] JG - "Walt Jabasco is showing his martial arts skills." JH - "Where'd he pick that stuff up? I thought only good wrestlers could use martial arts." JG - "Walt knows pretty much all forms of fighting. He was a former FBI agent, you know." JH - "He knows about them, but he doesn't know how to use them." [Jabasco picks up Bolotov and whips him into the opposite turnbuckle, following in with a lariat. But, Bolotov moves out of the way, sliding underneath the lariat and rolling up Jabasco... 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Almost had him!" JH - "A surprisingly agile move from the big Russian. I didn't think he could move that fast unless there was some hairy, bulky Russian chick in the room." [Both men spring to their feet, but Bolotov knocks Jabasco down with a clothesline. Jabasco gets back up on his own power, but Bolotov scoops him onto his shoulders and executes a big running powerslam into the middle of the ring. Bolotov lifts up Jabasco and applies the bear hug for the second time on an opponent!] JG - "Bolotov's got the bear hug hooked on, again. Bill Baer already submitted to this deadly move, we'll see how long Jabasco can hold out." JH - "He'll hold out shorter than a pathetic shortstop who realizes he can't hold out for the money." JG - "It looks like he's starting to fade...fade...fade..." JH - "Jabasco's going down with this one. The bear hug will be his end." JG - "He's coming back!" [Jabasco starts to pull his hands up, trying to slide out of the hold, but can't. He brings his right hand across the neck of Bolotov, hitting hard judo chops to the neck. Bolotov loosens the hold slightly, but not enough for Jabasco to get loose. Jabasco continues with the onslaught of chops, but can't get free. He goes to the last resort, a kick to the midsection of Bolotov. This finally releases the clutch as both men drop to the mat.] JG - "Both men are out from that move. I think Jabasco's lucky he wasn't disqualified for that blatant low blow." JH - "That's what you get when the referee's don't have names." JG - "Some do! We've got Wellington and Foresmits." JH - "But, what are their first names?" [Both men slightly roll on the mat, trying to regain some power in order to make an attack. Bolotov gets to his feet first, although winded from the blow. He bends to grab Jabasco, but is met by a series of palm thrusts to the midsection. Jabasco gets to his feet and hits a sweep kick, downing Bolotov. Bolotov leaps back to his feet, but is soon met by a series of European uppercuts. This backs Bolotov into the ropes where Jabasco grabs him and sends him across the ring. Jabasco ducks for a back body drop, but Bolotov stops. Bolotov jumps onto Jabasco's neck, Jabasco's legs giving out as the weight crashes down and Jabasco hits the mat.] JG - "That was a gruesome move. Jabasco's legs looked like they just shattered." JH - "Not to mention how unsexually pleasing it looked." [Bolotov picks up Jabasco and applies the bear hug, again.] JG - "The bear hug, again! I don't know how much longer Jabasco can hold out!" JH - "He's squirming back and forth more than your brother after a meeting with a wrester!" JG - "Jabasco's arms are held in check this time. I don't think he can get out." JH - "Jabasco submits! He gave into the bear hug!" JG - "It's the Siberian Express at Spring Spectacle! What a shocker!" JH - "Shocker? They were the only real team in there." JG - "They now go on to face the champs at Spring Spectacle, which will be decided right now." JH - "Snoozefest." JG - "Let's go to Ed for the intros." -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
