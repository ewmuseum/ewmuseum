[SCRA] Rasslefest Preview [2/3]

4 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
!~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~! |NY Street Fight: Gangsta Impasse vs. Stanlee Maxx| !~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~! JG - "Perhaps no two men are more opposite than each other. And, because of this, they have had many battles." JH - "All of them run ins, Grey." JG - "They have both had a way of getting involved in each others matches. I think this match will put an end to their feelings for each other." [Footage from October 6, The Scraps, begins playing. Doink enters the ring and celebrates Poink's win. Suddenly, something comes on the big screen.] JG - "What's this?" [In front of a black screen where Gangsta Impasse and John Drake are standing. Blaring rap music in the background. The camera starts at their feet and continues rising while Gangsta Impasse begins talking.] GI - "Hello, Poink. We are the Notorious Killaz. We come to harm and do no good. We see, we like, we take. I like what I'm seeing. Haha. Poink, we're comin' to getcha." JD - "Supp Poink? Glad to see us? Do you know who we are. We are your worst nightmare. Worse than yo mama sitting right down on ya. Worse then a rotweiller on some fresh meat. We are here Poink. And we will not...ASSUME THE POSITION" [Gangsta Impasse stands at attention while making a stupid face.] GI - "Hey, who's yo friend..." [The screen fades to black and the camera looks at Poink. Poink has a confused look on his face. He turns around and sees the men that were on the screen. Impasse attacks him and John Drake attacks the Doink.] VO - "The former police officer against the street thug. These two opposite personalities are ready to collide, for the firs time." [Cut to footage of Fatal Fall, October 12. Poink sets up once again for Assume the Position.] JG - "Poink is trying it again. He has it locked on. He has Assume the Position locked on!! Oh, here comes Gangsta Impasse!!! The Notorious Killaz have hit the ring." [The bell sounds as Gangsta Impasse beats on Poink and John Drake beats on Sgt. Pepper. Gangsta Impasse introduces a new move to Poink. He hits Gangsta Impact.] JG - "Wow! Gangsta just nailed a tombstone from the second rope on the 300 plus pound Poink!" JH - "What is Gangsta doing down here? He has not business being at ringside." JG - "Poink is out cold! Gangsta is leaving the ring with John Drake. Doink has hit the ring, trying to help Poink up." [The Killaz leave the ring area as Doink helps Poink up. Poink screams at them and slowly walks back to the locker room. The footage quickly cuts to the rumbles, later that night. Klondike and Jacks have Gangsta in the corner. Poink comes out and walks down to ringside.] JG - "What does he want?" JH - "Probably wants to get some free attacks on Gangsta." JG - "Everything has its price. I don't think the president will like Poink coming down. He has no business being here." JH - "Who cares. It doesn't do anything." [Poink grabs at Gangsta's throat. He starts choking him, then chopping at the throat. The House of Pancakes move back and allow Poink to beat on Gangsta. The two Pancakers pick up Gangsta. Klondike throws him into Jacks who hits a powerslam. Jacks picks up Gangsta and throws him into Klondike who back body dropped Gangsta to the outside. Poink ran over and grabbed Gangsta, carrying him back to the locker room, but slapping him on the way back.] VO - "It seemed that all their hatred for each other was brewing upa indefinitely...until..." [The footage from November 3, the Scraps, begins playing. God of Thunder by Kiss blares over the PA as the crowd boos. A few kids are holding up "I LOVE POINK" signs. A large man standing 7' with long dirty blonde hair tall opens the curtains and strolls down the aisle. As he walks fans pelt him with garbage. He goes after some of the fans and they flinch. Before entering the ring he grabs one of the signs and slithers under the bottom rope. He starts to scream back at the audience.] JG - "Wait Sir, this interview is for Poink who are you?" [The man rips up the sign and screams into the micropone.] MAN - "POINK IS DEAD!!" [He throws the papers all over the ring. Jon Grey has a look of shock on his face.] JG - "What do you mean Poink is dead?" MAN - "You know what I have been saying about not getting respect and being poked fun at." JG - "You mean you are....." [Interrupts Grey.] MAN - "Correction, you mean was. Meet.......Stanlee Maxx!! [The camera cuts directly to later in the interview.] SM - "Now I have myself a real partner and true friend....Pablo Morales." [The two hug and wait in the ring. Gangsta and John Drake run down the aisle, ready for the tag team match.] JG - "I guess Pablo is Poink's, er, Stanlee Maxx's partner." JH - "I just think Maxx liked to bring out somebody, you know, just to show him off." JG - "Very funny. Looks like this match is ready to go!" [The four men immediately start exchanging punches. Gangsta goes after Maxx and Morales after Drake. The two brawlers, Gangsta and Maxx, throw hard lefts and rights, knocking each other, but not moving each other. The other two have considerably less force, although Drake lands a hard right hand which sends Morales stumbling back. Drake hits a dropkick which pushes Morales through the ropes.] VO - "Stanlee Maxx burst onto the scene, replacing his former appearance as Poink, but not his former vendettas. Maxx wanted Gangsta worse than ever." [Shots of Havoc 2, the Boiler Room Brawl come on. Gangsta runs down the hallway and tries to open any door he possibly can. He reaches the principal's office and finds it unlocked. Gsngsta looks to be shocked by this, but sees Pablo running after him, so he enters quickly. Gangsta closes the door behind him and locks it when, all of a sudden, CRUNCH!!!! Stanlee Maxx nails Gangsta with a running clothesline. Stanlee Maxx lifts up Gangsta and power bombs him. Pablo tries to open the door, but it is locked.] JG - "Stanlee Maxx may have caused Gangsta the match!" JH - "But, Pablo is locked out! He may have caused Pablo the match!" [Stanlee Maxx then does a splash. Stanlee Maxx climbs the principal's desk and looks to be going for a froggie splash!! All of a sudden, John Drake comes from underneath the desk and whacks Stanlee Maxx over the head with the stuffed principal's dog, Stampy. John Drake then begins to pound on Stanlee Maxx with repeated punches and kicks.] JH - "That's where Stampy went! I missed playing with him." JG - "I hope we don't get into the action!" [Gangsta finds his way to the the bell. Pablo finally breaks the door open. Pablo runs after Gangsta, but Gangsta rings the bell.] VO - "Yet, after constant tossing at night, Stanlee Maxx could not free himself from his former past. He had to get one thing out, then beat one man." [Footage from December 1, The Scraps begins. Suddenly, out comes Stanlee Maxx, holding a pile of clothes.] JG - "Oh now. What does he want?" JH - "I don't know, but it's sure gonna liven things up!" [Maxx places his clothes at ringside, then just stares at them for a second. In the ring, Flash is back on his feet. He applies an armbar on Gangsta. Maxx pulls something out of his trunks.] JG - "What is that?" JH - "I think it's a lighter." JG - "Call the fire department! There's gonna be trouble!" JH - "Ah, don't worry. A little spark never hurt anyone." [Maxx sets his pile of clothes on fire.] JG - "I think those were his police uniforms!" JH - "Poink is finally gone for good." JG - "He was gone a while ago." JH - "No. The memories were still there. Stanlee Maxx has freed himself from his horrendous past. It's back to beating times for Gangsta Impasse." [Gangsta is able to roll out of the armbar, then delivers elbows to the chest of Flash. He whips Flash into the ropes and bends over, sending Flash high over the top rope with a back body drop. Flash lands on the fire.] JH - "Now, that's extreme!" JG - "At least the fire is out." JH - "Not on Flash's pants! Look at those puppies ignite!" [Tommy Gregory comes running out, carrying a fire extinguisher. He sprays it over Flash's wrestling tights, which are now little more than the necessities to cover what needs to be covered. Stanlee Maxx, not caring that the new head of security is at ringside, jumps into the ring and gets into a wild brawl with Gangsta Impasse.] JG - "These two titans have collided again!" JH - "Stanlee Maxx is going to be sidetracked no more! Whatever guidance he received to get back to the LA-type riots with Gangsta, it was good advice!" JG - "Folks, we have to go!" [The camera fades with the shot of Gangsta and Maxx fighting in the center of the ring.] VO - "Will Gangsta Impasse be able to extinguish the fire known as Stanlee Maxx? Or, will Stanlee Maxx be able to defeat Gangsta Impasse and finally break free from his former self. It will all come out as these two behemoths lock up, in a New York Street Fight!" !~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~! |Derek Mota vs. Reverend Aristotle Jones| !~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~! JG - "These two top cruisers..." JH - "Don't call Jones a cruiser, Grey. That's what started this thing in the first place." JG - "Very true. Derek Mota burst onto the scene with great promise and immediately took exception to Reverend Jones being thought of in the same category..." [The camera goes to December 15, the Scraps. Ken Curtis quickly gets up and leaves the ring area with his clothing. As he walks down the aisle, he passes...] JG - "Derek Mota! What's he doing here?" JH - "He enjoys the good heroes like Curtis just as much as we do." [Mota climbs into the ring, grabbing a microphone and looking at the fallen Reverend.] DM - "Let me tell you a story ... I keep on hearing about this place they call SCRA, how it's the hottest fed going these days. I got "Mr." Grey on the phone, promising me the glory, kissin' between the cheeks ta get me signin' the dotted line ... And I did." [Mota bounces against the ropes and hits Jones with a Jumping Elbow Drop, keeping him on the mat. Derek Mota once again grabs the microphone and begins to speak.] DM - "Then I found out I've been bracketed. "Mota's a top cruiser", I hear. Mota's a cinch ta win that new cruiser title, people keep sayin' around the dressing rooms. And then who the hell do I have goin' up against me for this title? The "Reverend" Aristotle Jones. The man who's weighin' in at a porky 240 pounds. A man who has no right, NO RIGHT to be competin' for a cruiserweight title! Dammit, Jones, if I'm gonna put my damn name on this title, I ain't gonna be facin' you for the thing! And that's why I'm takin' you out ... right ... now!!! I'll show you what being a cruiserweight is all about!" [Derek Mota climbs to the top rope, Jones slowly beginning to stir on the mat. Mota stands upright, arms outstretched to the crowd, who pop loudly as Mota launches himself gracefully from the top rope with a ...] JH - "Shooting Star Press!!!" JG - "That move is so graceful, especially when performed by a top cruiserweight like Derek Mota. I still don't understand why he is out here, though." JH - "Do you really need an excuse to beat on Jones?" [Mota lifts Jones to his feet and starts throwing hard rights, moving Jones into the center of the ring. Mota runs against the rope, taking off for a hurricarana...] JG - "Blocked! Reverend Jones stopped the hurricarana and..." JH - "Powerbomb! Oh no! Say it ain't so, Grey!" [Jones gets up and places a figure four on Mota, attempting to take out Mota's precious vertical base. Mota is able to break out of the hold before it is locked on fully. Both men spring to their feet and start throwing furious lefts and rights. Tommy Gregory and the security staff rush down the aisle to break up the two.] JH - "Let 'em fight! Damn security staff, why'd we get 'em?" [The staff steps in between the two wrestlers, but a few workers are sent flying by a furious Derek Mota who continues to unleash punches. He walks over to Jones, diving at him and taking the Reverend down. Mota begins hurling lefts and rights on the mat as security is able to yank him off.] JG - "Derek Mota is mad!" JH - "I guess he really doesn't want Jones in the cruiserweight division, eh Grey?" [Security drags Mota to the back, literally. Jones walks peacefully down the aisle, not wanting any extra help.] VO - "The man recruited to build a cruiserweight division didn't like what he saw. Derek Mota took exception to being mentioned with talent such as Reverend Aristotle Jones. Yet, Jones showed all the capabilities of being able to compete with the wiry young cruiser. Mota, however, would not agree. We will find out which one is correct as these two clash, with the cruiserweight division hanging on the winner." !~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~! |"Cowboy" Ken Curtis vs. "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin| !~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~! JG - "This match could be called the one awaited by the most fans around the world. Sure, all the other matches have been building for months and could prove to be better than this match, but with the worldwide success of these two wrestlers, one has an easy argument." JH - "Eloquently put, Grey." JG - "Why, thank you, Jack. You know I always try my hardest in giving the fans their big lead ins." JH - "And yet, people tune out when you talk." JG - "Yes, including you." JH - "What was that?" JG - "Very funny. Let's take a look at these two wrestlers' careers over the past few months, a feud which has encompassed not just SCRA, but two other federations as well." [Footage begins with "IIWF: October 4". The famed Brody Thunder triple cross on J.W. Hardin and the Syndicate begins playing.] VO - "It all started in a land far, far away. A victim of his own teaching, J.W. Hardin fell into the hands of a wily young cowboy. Yet, he seeked revenge." [NLWP footage, November 3.] JV - "Their first night in the federation and the NLWP's newest tag team has cleaned house with the federation's two premiere stables!" TM - "I never heard of them before, Vasquez, but one thing's for sure... they're tough as nails, these two!" JV - "Hold on...." TM - "What the hell are they doing?" JV - "The Marauders are unmasking already! Not even one full night in the federation and they're revealing their identities to the NLWP!" TM - "Wait a sec ... who's that coming to the ring?" JV - "Someone else is walking to the ring! I can't tell who it is, the entrance is covered in fog!" TM - "The Marauders have their masks off, and it's ..." JV - "Oh my God..." TM - "It's Casey "Blackheart" James and J.W. Hardin! The man who was the NLWP and IIWF World Champ simultaneously and the IIWF Hall of Famer!" JV - "That could only mean the man who's coming up to the ring is..." TM - "Tiger Claw!!!" JV - "The Syndicate has made it to the NLWP! These men left the IIWF a few weeks ago and just gave up the IIWF World Tag Team Titles before becoming free agents! And now they're here! I can't believe it!" VO - "It appeared that Thunder and Hardin would meet again. But, Thunder had a friend." [Footage of Brian Lau's speech in NLWP from November 10 begins playing.] BL - "Now, I know you guys have probably figured on facing Casey and Claw tonight, and I do sympatize with you right now. Your strategies for this match are probably based on the fact that you'll be facing those two men. Well, that's just too bad, isn't it. I apologize to you, Mr. Curtis, because the only thing that you're really guilty of is having bad taste in friends. You, Thunder, on the other hand, are a marked man. You tried to make us look like fools some time back, and now it's time to repay that indiscretion a hundred-fold. This is only the beginning. Tonight, your cycle of pain turns one more degree. Tonight, I present to you the Syndicate, represented by Casey "Blackheart" James and J.W. Hardin!" VO - "Indeed, Curtis appeared to pick a bad friend in Thunder as he immediately inherited all Thunder's enemies. Hardin tracked won Curtis, coming to SCRA [The shocking entrance speech of December 8 begins playing.] VOICE - "Ain't... life... grand!" [All eyes turn to see the hulking 6'-10" figure of "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin standing midway down the aisle. Those who recognize the wrestling veteran, chomping on a cigar while dressed in his black duster and black cowboy hat, immediately give a huge heel pop which Hardin ignores. Despite the trash that flies at him, he keeps his gaze on the ring, where Curtis finally recognizes what is happening.] JG - "It's J.W. Hardin!" JH - "Speak of the devil." JWH - "Y'know Curtis, my Daddy alway said that there wasn't nothin' easier in life than trackin' a skunk. I reckon he was right. I was in Toronto havin' a beer with Casey James when I overheard some hombre talkin' 'bout Hardin being in some fed called the SCRA. Feller said this cowboy wasn't half bad. Heh... half bad." JH - "Who's Casey James?" JG - "He's a wr..." JH - "Quiet, Grey! The Outlaw is trying to enlighten this damned league!" [Hardin continues his steely gaze at the ring.] JWH - "Half bad... I knew they was talkin' 'bout you, Curtis. See, J.W. Hardin ain't _half_ anything. When I do somethin', I do it right. So I walked outta the saloon, sniffed the air, and started trackin' a skunk. The stench led me right here... to you. Daddy was right... ain't nothin' easier." [Hardin eases his 353-pound frame against the crowd barrier and pulls his hat low. A few fans reach up to pat him on the back, but he flicks his cigar at them and sends them scattering.] JWH - "I ain't here to make no name fer myself. I done that too many other places. And I ain't here to scream 'bout taking no titles. That's fer pretty boys and hombres with inferiority complexes. Naw, I'm here fer one reason and one reason only, Curtis..." [For the first time, Hardin seems to almost smile while keeping his dark eyes on Curtis.] JWH - "...I'm here to kick yer ass!" [The pop grows as the crowd begins to anticipate an unexpected bonus match between Hardin and Curtis. It slowly dies, however, as neither man makes a move.] JWH - "I ain't even sure who's the chief 'round this here place. Well somebody tell him J.W. Hardin's in town... and I got me a few days and a skunk to kill." [Hardin tosses the microphone to the ground and turns back up the aisle, leaving Curtis staring a hole in his back. Curtis calls for a mic and someone at ringside tosses him one...] JG - "Looks like we're going to see a war of words between these two cowboys." JH - "Who cares about a war of the words, I want a war of the fists! These two boys can brawl with the best!" CKC - "Well, I'll be damned, if it ain't the hall-of-famer hisself, J.W. Hardin the third, right here in the SCRA an' gunnin' for the Cowboy." [Hardin stops and turns to look over his shoulder back at the ring] CKC - "I guess I'm supposed to be shakin' in my boots up here 'bout now, but ya know what? I ain't. Ya see, that sack o' horse s**t you call a reputation don' mean a damned thang to me, ol' man. There's people in this business who look at you an' see a legend. But I look at you and ya wanna know what I see? A washed-up old-timer hangin' on to a past that shoulda been buried a long time ago." [Hardin, a wicked grin on his face, turns to face Curtis as the crowd pops for Curtis' bravado] CKC - "So all I got say is this... if you came here lookin' for a fight, you came to the right damned place, hoss. But while yer daddy was teachin' you how to track polecats an' the right way to stump-break a heifer, I hope he taught you how to take an ass-whippin'!" [The crowd pops heavy for Curtis as he drops the mic and beckons Hardin to come to the ring. Hardin continues to grin his evil grin as he shoves his cigar between his teeth and slowly turns, walks to the entrance curtains, and disappears.] VO - "These two cowboys are destined to meet, and they did. However, they met many more times before Rasslefest, running into each other behind the ring and in the ring." [Suddenly, a familiar voice booms over the public address speakers...] CKC - "Hardin!" [The crowd turns to see "Cowboy" Ken Curtis, in street clothes and with his black hat pulled down over his eyes, standing in the center of the entrance ramp, mic in hand.] CKC - "Pretty impressive showin' there against the mop-top. But when you an' I step between those ropes, you ain't gonna be hearin' no "Abbey Road". The only sound you'll be hearin' is the sound of breakin' bones an' the squeak of a stretcher wheel! You made a big mistake comin' here lookin' for the Cowboy, ol' man, an' don't you think for a minute that I ain't gonna make you pay for steppin' into my spotlight! You got a thumpin' comin', gramps, an' the Cowboy's gonna be the one dishin' it out!" [The crowd pops heavy for the Cowboy as Hardin stares hard at Curtis, a wicked grin on his face...] CKC - "As far as I'm concerned, we ain't gotta wait until Rasslefest. We can have this tussle right here, right now!" [Hardin motions to ringside for a microphone. He grabs the mic and grins at Curtis.] JWH - "Well, well. Momma Curtis musta had on a reaaaal short skirt tonight fer her lil' boy to crawl out from under it. But then, I hear a lot o' hombres spend time under yer momma's skirt." [Curtis pulls the cigarillo from his teeth, drops it to the ground, grounds it out with his boot, and breaks into a full sprint to the ring! He slides under the bottom rope and pops up, where he is met by Hardin, fists afire! The crowd roars its approval as Curtis tackles Hardin and they fall, rolling around on the mat, fists and elbows flying! Suddenly, the ring fills with security guards and referees, pulling the two cowboys apart!] JH - "Well, it looks like Gregory's guy his ducks in a row, here!" JG - "What did it take, about 1-point-2 seconds for those guards to hit that ring?" JH - "Let 'im go at it, I say!" [The referees and the guards get Hardin and Curtis pulled apart, and Curtis is pulled from the ring! Hardin breaks free and rolls under the rope, attacking Curtis again! Both men throw and land punches as the guards and referees finally get them seperated again! Curtis is pulled up the ramp, pointing his finger and screaming at Hardin! Hardin continues to eye Curtis until Curtis is pulled behind the cutains, then struggles to get loose. Hardin appears calm, then bolts for the curtain, the referees and guards in hot pursuit! He hits the curtains and disappears, followed by the guards and referees!] VO - "Tommy Gregory tried to separate them, but couldn't. It appears nobody can. Gregory can only hope he can keep order in the ring as these two infuriated wrestlers square off." [Comments from each man's past speeches fills the screen.] JWH - "Lil' lady, I been havin' some problems with Curtis in the NLWP. When someone crosses me, I don't give a damn if I gotta go to the end o' the earth to settle things. I heard the lil' skunk was hidin' out in the SCRA... so here I am." CKC - "I ain't gotta admit a damned thang, Hazard! Hardin comes in here, plants his big ass in _my_ spotlight, and you guys go all goo-goo over 'im, like he was the God-forsaken King o' Siam or somethin'. But I see the man for what he is... a pathetic ol' has-been makin' one last grab for the gusto by hitchin' his wagon to the Cowboy. You want me to say somethin' about John Wesley Hardin? I'll do ya one better, hoss... I'm gonna say somethin' _to_ John Wesley Hardin. Bring that camera in close, boy!" CKC - "Hardin, put yer hearin' aid in so you can hear me loud an' clear. You an' me, our paths were bound to cross sooner or later. You knew it an' I knew it. But when you waltzed yer big ass out last Monday an' called me out, you let yer mouth write a check that yer ass ain't gonna be able to cash. You came here to kick the Cowboy's ass? Well, you better lace 'em up tight an' bring everything you got, hotshot, 'cause there's been harder an' tougher men than you try to put the Cowboy down... an' the nursin' home's full of 'em." JWH - "Same result... and I didn't waste a bullet. Yer like them clay pigeons, Curtis. Yer headin' fer a fall. Mebbe I'll beat ya senseless... mebbe you'll beat yerself. Mebbe both will happen. But it _will_ happen. Experience says so." -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
