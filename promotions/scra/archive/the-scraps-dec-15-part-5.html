[SCRA] The Scraps, 15 December 1997, Part 5

15 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 7 ) | | \ | | Anthony Logan vs. "Lord" Jason Gore / |___ | | | | Writer: RW (_________________________________________________________________________ JG - "We're underway! But, we have two Aladdin Styles! And, I don't think Gore knows which one to fight!" JH - "He's not that dumb, Grey. Gore knows who he was just talking to and who he has to fight." [Gore and the fake Style begin brawling in the aisle, moving down to the ring area. Logan hits a crescent kick to the head, sending Gore staggering back. Gore climbs into the ring, ready to fight inside the ring. Logan follows in.] JG - "This one's going to be a real battle. Both men share a deep hate for each other." JH - "Why don't we book all the matches like this one, Grey? Do we really have to sit through Robbie and Phantom when we can experience these?" [Logan and Gore look at each other collectively. Logan tries a test of strength, but Gore backs away. They lock up in a collar and elbow tie-up. Gore rakes the eyes of Logan. Gore kicks Logan in the stomach and pushes him against the turnbuckle. He hits a knifed - edged chop. Logan quickly grabs Gore and pushes him to the turnbuckle and hits Gore with repeated knifed-edged chops of his own. *smack* *smack* the crowd yells "WHOOOOOO" every time] JH - "WHOOOOO!" JG - "Do you have to do that?" JH - "Heck, why not." JG - "I'll tell you why not." JH - "Why not?" JG - "Uh, um, uh, Logan is dominating Gore here." [Logan then kicks Gore in the chest numerous times. Logan begins to punch Gore in the chest and in the head. The referee tries to break the two men out of the corner, so he puts his own body between the two wrestlers. Gore sneaks in a punch on Logan which sends the man back.] JG - "Gore back in control." JH - "Logan falling behind." JG - "Was that absolutely necessary?" [Gore throws Logan off the ropes and executes an elbow. Logan remains standing. Gore comes off the ropes and hits a football tackle, but Logan still stands. Gore goes off the ropes again and tries a football tackle, but Logan hits his deadly heart punch while Gore was coming after him. Gore is on the mat reiking in pain and favoring his chest and upper left shoulder area.] JG - "The Dragonstrike Heart Punch! Logan could get the victory here." JH - "[British Accent]Not bloody likely.[Normal voice] He's too much of a slacker." JG - "Thanks for you're obnoxious English attempt at comedy." JH - "Any time, chap." [Before the referee could ask Gore to submit, Logan quickly picks up Gore and executes 7 crescent kicks to the chest area. Logan comes off the ropes and executes a bulldog. Logan picks up Gore and throws him to the outside.] JG - "Logan is just overpowering him here." JH - "It's like David and Goliath. A midget vs another guy, and the midget's winning." [Logan goes to the outside and begins to beat up on Gore with sabot kicks. Logan goes for a open fisted punch but Gore grabs Logan and hits him in the head. The fans start to cheer quietly.] JG - "Hmmm." JH - "Exactly." [Gore then body slams Logan. Gore then tries to hook the Texas clover leaf on Logan] JG - "He's applying a submission on the outside?" JH - "These House of Lords guys aren't too bright now, are they?" [Gore almost gets Logan over when all of a sudden Logan grabs a steel pipe from under the ring and smashes it against the leg of Gore. Logan seems to be pretty furious. Logan grabs a chair and climbs to the top turnbuckle. Logan yells loudly and jumps off the top turnbukle, with chair in hand, and executes a flying chair handle from the top turnbuckle onto the outside.] JG - "Isn't that a disqualification?" JH - "[yelling] EXTREEEEEEEEEEEME" JG - "What was that for?" JH - "I just won some money. That guy behind me just dared me." [Logan picks up Gore in a belly to back suplex position and drops him across the steel railing. Logan then grabs a chair and hits it against the leg of Gore.] JG - "Vicious." JH - "The mortality here is just brutal." JG - "Wow, you really are more than a punchline king." JH - "Ha, you admit it, I'm a king!" [Logan throws Gore into the ring and climbs the top turnbuckle. Logan dives off the top turnbuckle and executes a flying drop-kick to the leg of Gore. Gore is screaming in pain.] JG - "My, wasn't that unorthidox." JH - "[mockingly]My, oh my. Plutonium, fun word isn't it? Plutonium. How's your plutonium..." JG - "Don't mock me." [Logan pulls Gore over to the corner area and tries to apply a figure four leg lock, but he is having trouble trying to figure out how to put on the move. Logan manages to get the figure four on but grabs the ropes just as the pain begins to start in on Gore.] JG - "This match is a squash." JH - "SQUISH!" [Logan forces Gore to the corner and irish whips him, but Gore only makes it half-way and falls face first on the mat. Logan forces Gore to get to his feet and picks up the injured leg. Logan pulls his fist back and *WHAMMMMM!!!* Logan nails the heart punch on the knee of Jason Gore. Gore reiks in pain. The paramedics begin to run out at ringside.] JG - "They're going to stop the match, Jason Gore is in serious pain." JH - "Look at these fools with their stretchers. It was just like ten seconds ago when they were out here rescuing Ed from the leather pants." JG - "Anthony Logan is dangerous. Just look what he's doing to the knee of this man." [Jason Gore tries to say something to the referee, but Logan inteferes before anything is said. Logan picks up Gore by the hair and hits the devistating heart punch. Logan covers Gore. The referee counts quickly: 1! 2! 3! and forces Logan off Gore. Logan keeps hitting Gore's leg with repeated open hand punches. Security finally pulls Logan back while the paramedics slowly place Gore on the stretcher.] JG - "Ladies and gentlemen, this man is dangerous. He almost broke the knee of Jason Gore." JH - "Well, he deserves it." JG - "Why?" JH - "For hanging out with that geek Style." JG - "You used to love the House...." JH - "Shut up, I was a young juvinile." JG - "Well, on to our main event, the current North American Champion, Gangsta Impasse takes on Centris." ___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 8 ) | | \ | | Gangsta Impasse vs. Centris / |___ | | | | Writer: AG (_________________________________________________________________________ [Jaspard gets in the ring, wearing his Gangsta supporting costume. He has the sunglasses over his nose, baggy pants and a backwards shirt.] JH - "Ol' Ed is sort of out of date, eh Grey?" EJ - "This one's for you, Ahmed!" JH - "Oh no, not another Jaspard family dedication." EJ - "This matchup is scheduled for one fall with a remaining TV time limit! It is for the North American championship! Coming down the aisle first, hailing from the Land of Beyond and weighing in at 275 lbs., here comes Centris!" [Wish you Were Here plays throughout the stadium as Centris walks through, flaming staff in hand. He walks down the aisle slowly, staring around at the crowd. As he nears the ringside area, Centris places the staff underneath the ring and climbs in.] EJ - "And, his opponent, hailing from Detroit, Michigan and weighing in at 280 lbs., the current North American champion, here comes Gangsta Impasse!" [California Love cuts off Wish you Were Here as John Drake steps in, raising the roof. Gangsta soon follows, hood draped over his head and shadowboxing his way down the aisle. Drake continues to pump the crowd, leading the way for Gangsta into the ring. Drake holds open the ropes, allowing Gangsta to step in and begin shadowboxing. Fireworks blaze behind him. Gangsta ceases shadowboxing and moves towards the corner, climbing the turnbuckle and raising the North American title.] JG - "The fans certainly like Gangsta Impasse." JH - "He is a rough boy, but not in the league as the cowboys. I've always liked country better than this rap crap. And, I am not a real big fan of country, either." JG - "I guess you really don't like rap..." [Centris moves from behind and attacks Gangsta, ripping him from the turnbuckle and tossing him into the center of the ring.] DING! DING! DING! JG - "Centris wants this one. He hasn't really proved anything since dropping the TV title." JH - "Who wants that belt, anyways?" [Centris leans over to grab Gangsta, but gets caught in a rollup... 1! 2KICKOUT!] JG - "That was a close one. Gangsta Impasse almost finished this match off with a bang." JH - "And quickly. That's the one quality I look for in Centris' matches." [Centris springs up only to be taken down by a hard Gangsta clothesline. Centris, again, leaps to his feet, but Gangsta is able to put on a front face lock. Centris attempts to move his way out of it, pushing Gangsta into the ropes. Centris drops to the mat, attempting a monkey flip. However, Gangsta is able to stop, grab Centris' feet and slingshot him into the ropes. Centris' chin hits the top rope as he bounces off into Gangsta's waiting arms for a pumphandle suplex.] JH - "The most popular move in wrestling today." JG - "That's debatable, but that pumphandle has already become as used as other big phenomenoms such as the DDT and powerbomb." JH - "It's a damn shame to waste these power moves." [Gangsta goes off the ropes, coming down in the middle of the ring with an axehandle. As Centris attempts to get up, Gangsta once again drops an axehandle on the back. Gangsta stops his attack, lifting Centris up. Gangsta sends Centris into the ropes, grabbing him by the midsection into a spinning sidewalk slam.] JG - "Painful memories of that move." JH - "What, Grey?" JG - "Nothing. What a move by Gangsta." JH - "Spill your guts, Grey. What were you gonna say?" JG - "Nothing." JH - "You're not the only one who can reveal a piece of character, but cover it up right away. Hmmmmm, I remember the slams." JG - "We already knew you were a wrestler." JH - "Uh, yeah." [Gangsta goes off the ropes and drops in the middle of the ring with a knee. Centris rolls onto his stomach, then gets to his knees. This allows Gangsta to place Centris into a full nelso on the mat. Centris attempts to work his way out of the move, getting to his feet only to be stopped by a dragon suplex, into a bridge.. 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Close call! Gangsta Impasse is really taking control of this match. I don't think Centris has been in control, yet." JH - "You sound as if Centris will get in control. As I've said before, Gangsta isn't one of my favs, but Centris is just horrible." [Centris gets to his feet on his own power and is met by hard right hands by Gangsta. Gangsta pushes Centris into the turnbuckle with the hard shots, then begins to release a fury of punches in an attempt to knock the former TV champ right out. Centris slumps, but doesn't fall all the way. Gangsta lifts him back to his feet and delivers hard knife edged chops to the chest. As Centris jumps back in pain, Gangsta sends him into the opposite corner, following in with a running lariat.] JG - "He almost took Centris' head off!" JH - "That would have been quite the souvenir for the fan in the third row. That's what we need, Grey, heads." JG - "I don't think we could take any of our wrestlers' heads." JH - "Toy heads, moron. They would sell like hotcakes!" [Gangsta backs off the turnbuckle, walking right into the arms of Gangsta. This converts into an easy back suplex, slamming Centris' head into the mat. Gangsta lifts Centris to his feet, hitting a knife edged chop in the middle of the ring. Centris, again, backs into the turnbuckle. Gangsta stops him this time and lifts Centris into a tilt a whirl suplex. Gangsta moves off the ropes and comes down hard with an elbowdrop to the head. Centris rolls over, trying to get to his feet. Gangsta tries a kick to the midsection, but Centris grabs Gangsta's foot. Gangsta hits an enzuigiri, sending Gangsta out of the ring. Gangsta takes aim and leaps over the top, keeping Centris down with a swan dive.] JG - "Wow! Big swan dive by the big North American champion! I'm surprised he got up that high!" JH - "I keep saying it, Grey. These black boys can really jump. Just look at the NBA." JG - "What about all those big Croatian centers? They can get up there." JH - "The only reason those guys have a job is because they are big. They clog the hole in the middle." [Gangsta keeps Centris on the outside, hitting a snap suplex onto the mats. John Drake lifts Gangsta to his feet, as he took a lot of force. Centris gets up on his own power and attempts a kick to the midsection, but Gangsta catches the foot. Centris attempts an enzuigiri, but Gangsta ducks underneath the blow. Gangsta drops, still holding the foot, with an ankle lock submission.] JH - "He's going to get counted out, the moron!" JG - "Both men will get counted out and Gangsta will hold the belt." JH - "Oh, yeah. I guess it would be okay for Gangsta to stay outside." [Gangsta soon releases the hold, lifting Centris to his feet. Gangsta throws Centris into the ring, soon following himself. The few second delay, however, is enough for Centris to get up and hit a kick to the midsection of Gangsta. Gangsta bounces back in pain, staying on his hands and knees. Centris walks up again and hits the same kick, pushing Gangsta back further. Centris attempts the move again, but Gangsta is able to move out of the way and sweep Centris' leg.] JG - "Went to the well one too many times!" JH - "Haven't seen that happen in wrestling before." JG - "This is wrestling, Jack. If one person does it, you have to copy it." JH - "I guess so. I guess originality is pretty much out of the question, eh Grey?" [Gangsta attempts a pin.. 1! KICKOUT! Centris leaps to his feet only to be met by a hard right hand. Centris staggers back from the blow only to be met by a series of more rights and lefts. Gangsta moves Centris away from the turnbuckle and towards the ropes. He flings Centris across the ring, spinning Centris upside down and into a tombstone piledriver position. Gangsta drives the head of Centris down hard. Centris gets up under his own power, but is grabbed immediately by Gangsta and placed between his legs. Gangsta sets up and hits a hard powerbomb.] JG - "Gangsta Impasse is really going with the power moves tonight." JH - "It's always fun to see Centris get pounded around the ring. I can never get enough of this stuff. If only Gangsta could bust 'im open." JG - "I don't think that'll be happening anytime soon. Especially around Christmas. We have to be extra clean, you know." JH - "It'll be my gift. Com'on, Grey. All the Dukes of Hazard out there want their stockings filled with the juice!" [Gangsta grabs Centris' feet and slingshots him into the turnbuckle. As Centris' head bounces off, Gangsta applies a full nelson. He quickly converts this into a dragon suplex, into a bridge... 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Close call by Gangsta Impasse!" JH - "Yet another overused aspect of wrestling." JG - "What, close calls?" JH - "Yeah. I know the match isn't gonna finish until 9:59, so why bother with these close calls?" [As Centris begins to get up, Gangsta lifts him to his feet. He starts to hit hard right hands, backing Centris into the ropes. Gangsta whips Centris across the ring, attempting a clothesline. Centris is able to stop his momentum and grab Gangsta's arm, slinging himself into a crucifix. He drops to the mat, getting Gangsta into a pin... 1! KICKOUT!] JH - "Does that count as offense for Centris?" JG - "I don't know. It certainly would be a first in this matchup." JH - "This matchup? It would be the first this year!" JG - "How could he become TV champ without any offense?" JH - "That's what I'm wonderin', Grey." [Gangsta springs to his feet, taking down Centris with a hard clothesline. Centris gets right back up, only to be taken down by a Gangsta bodyslam. Again, Centris jumps up, but is taken down by an armdrag takedown, into an armbar.] JH - "A resthold this late in the match? That's odd." [Centris claws at the face of Gangsta, breaking the armbar. Centris keeps a hold of Gangsta's arm as he gets up. Once both men are on their feet, Centris twists Gangsta's arm. But, Gangsta is able to hit a lariat, taking Centris down. Gangsta goes off the ropes, hitting a legdrop to the neck area of Centris. Gangsta lifts Centris to his feet and hits a knee to the midsection, bending Centris over. Gangsta runs to the side ropes and hits a kneelift. Stanlee Maxx appears at the top of the curtains.] JG - "Not again." JH - "Maybe he'll bring some life into this matchup." [Maxx just watches the match from the top of the ramp. Gangsta points at him, signalling for something. He lifts up Centris and places him over his shoulder. Gangsta climbs to the second rope and...] JG - "Gangsta Impact!" JH - "Finally, the match can end!" [Gangsta pins Centris... 1! 2! 3!] JG - "It's over! What a title defense by Gangsta Impasse. He just demolished Centris." JH - "Was there ever any doubt?" [Centris rolls out of the ring, but Gangsta stays in. Maxx turns his back, raises his hands and...] JG - "Oh my!" JH - "The ringposts are on fire! Yet another unoriginal idea!" JG - "Why is Stanlee Maxx obsessed with fire? Folks, we are out of time! Good night!"
