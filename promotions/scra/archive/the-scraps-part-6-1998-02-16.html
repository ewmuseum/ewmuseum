[SCRA] The Scraps [6/6] (16 February 1998)

16 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Suddenly, we hear Curtis' voice booming again over the arena speakers!] CKC - "Hey, homey!" [The action in the ring stops as Impasse turns the look at the screen, seeing Curtis' face again! Impasse climbs between the ropes and starts up the ramp towards the screen, but stops halfway!] CKC - "You know I wouldn't let this happen without givin' ya one last chance t' say goodbye. I mean, you did it for me, why shouldn't I return the favor!? I'm a "southern gentleman" and all, right!?" [The camera pulls back to reveal Impasse's Benz, at the edge of Lake Ontario! The tow-truck is gone and the car sits perched on the edge of the bank!] JG - "Look at Wellington! He's not even counting Impasse out!" JH - "He wants to see what happens here, too!" [Curtis walks over to the car and leans on it...] CKC - "I don't know why yer so attached to this damned foreign piece o' crap, no way! I mean, it's just a hunk o' metal and glass. It can't keep ya company when yer out at night, roundin' up a bunch o' loose cattle! It can't eat carrots or sugar cubes outta yer hand and then nuzzle up to ya to tell ya thanks! Hell, this here's just a toy, Impasse! But, then again, I know how upset you gangbangers git when somebody takes yer favorite toy!" GI - "You a dead man, Curtis!" CKC - "Well, time t' go. I gotta match tonight, even if it is with that no-talent sumbitch Prodigy. Can't underestimate 'im, ya know! You said yer goodbyes yet? Good." [Curtis steps in behind the car and gives it a good shove, pushing it over the bank and into the lake! The crowd pops heavy for Curtis as he turns and brushes his hands together, wearing a wicked grin! Impasse yells at the screen and begins jumping around on the ramp, throwing a fit!] JG - "Good Lord! He just shoved the Benz into the water! That car is totally ruined!" JH - "Revenge is sweet, Grey!" [Suddenly, a man in jeans, a t-shirt, and a Los Angeles Dodger baseball cap hops the railing behind Impasse and rushes up behind him! Hardin sees this and begins to distract the referee!] JG - "Wait a minute... what the... who the hell is that?!" [The man grabs Impasse from behind and pulls him into a headlock! The man spins around and bulldogs Impasse onto the ramp! The man takes the baseball cap off and throws it onto the prone Impasse!] JG - "That's 'Cowboy' Ken Curtis! How in the hell...?!" JH - "It's called video, Grey! Curtis fooled Impasse! He hoodwinked him! He bamboozled him..." JG - "Ok, we get it!" [Curtis turns his attention to Hardin, and points a stern finger at him as he backs up the ramp! Wellington looks confused, then does what every referee does at a point like this, disqualifies Hardin. Curtis turns and walks up the ramp, splits the entrance curtain, and disappears!] JG - "It looks like we've seen a disqualification resulting in a win by Gangsta Impasse!" JH - "Nothing like giving a guy the match. I think Hardin's a little peeved, too." JG - "I think he's more than peeved. Curtis has been sticking that finger in Hardin's face all of a sudden and..." JH - "Yup, he's mad." JG - "Okay. I guess we'll venture into our next matchup, the World title!" JH - "A snoozer main event!" JG - "Let's go to Ed Jaspard for the intros." ___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 8 ) | | \ | | "Cowboy" Ken Curtis vs. Prodigy / |___ | | | | Writer: Alex G. (_________________________________________________________________________ [Jaspard steps into the ring, licking his finger and brushing his eye brow. Looking stylish, he continues with the intros.] EJ - "This matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 20 limit time limit! Coming down the aisle first, hailing from Toronto, Ontario and weighing in at 276 lbs., Prodigy!" [Bullet with Butterfly Wings comes on the PA with Black Kat and Prodigy walking down to the ring. They slap a few fans hands, and Prodigy gets on the mat to hold open the ropes open for Black Kat. Before he can finish entering, President Grey walks down.] AG - "Prodigy, I'm sorry to do this to you, but a requirement of holding the TV title is defending it _every_ week. You, however, have not defended it for the past two weeks. Due to this circumstance, I must strip you of the title." JG - "What! He can't do that!" JH - "Oh, Grey. Don't agree?" JG - "I think it's absurd. Prodigy wasn't booked into any title matches!" AG - "I will give the status of this vacant belt next week." [As Grey strips Prodigy of the title, Jaspard looks on cheerfully and continues with the intros.] EJ - "And, his opponent, weighing in at 260 lbs. and hailing from Austin, Texas, he is "Cowboy" Ken Curtis!" ["Cowboy" Ken Curtis enters the arena first to "Gimme back my Bullets" by Lynard Skynard. Curtis slowly jogs towards the ring, stopping to slap a few peoples hands. He hops over the top rope, and stands in the middle of the ring.] DING! DING! DING! [Curtis and Prodigy circle, then lockup collar and elbow. Prodigy gets Curtis in a side headlock, soon followed by a hiptoss. Prodigy holds Curtis on the mat with the headlock, squeezing harder and harder.] JG - "It looks like Prodigy is really attacking Curtis. Being stripped of the TV title tends to make one a little mad." JH - "Yet, he still can't fight. Instead of punching Curtis, he's going for these little headlocks and stuff!" [Curtis gets up with a series of elbows to the midsection, then moves off the ropes. Prodigy goes down for a back body drop, but Curtis leapfrogs over him. Prodigy turns around and sets up for a powerslam, but Curtis sees the move early enough and stops, nailing a swinging neckbreaker. Prodigy gets back up, but is taken down by a Curtis dropkick. Again, Prodigy hops up, but Curtis takes him down with an armdrag, quickly into an armbar.] JG - "We've seen more and more technical skills from Ken Curtis as of late. I think he wants to show that he isn't just a brawler." JH - "I think he's bored with a lack of competition and tries to give his opponents a chance by doing this junk. Let's face it, if he brawled every match, he'd be the champ, uh, a long time ago." [Curtis releases the hold, picks Prodigy up and then slams him down to the mat. Curtis goes for a few kicks to the chest, moving Prodigy over. As Prodigy turns onto his back, blocking the stomach from the stomps, Curtis leaps in the air with a standing legdrop. Curtis lifts Prodigy to his feet, then sends him running into the ropes. Curtis attempts a clothesline, but Prodigy ducks underneath, grabbing the arm and spinning around Curtis. This also spins Curtis around right into a belly to belly suplex by Prodigy. Curtis gets right back up, but is scooped high in the air by Prodigy into a gorilla press.] JG - "Prodigy showing off some of his power. He's got Curtis right on top!" JH - "That's generally where a gorilla press takes place. Then again, knowing Prodigy, it wouldn't surprise me to see him try a gorilla press on the mat." [Prodigy runs off the ropes and drops an elbow across the chest. He quickly follows this up with another elbow...and another. Prodigy lifts up Curtis and drops him with a side suplex. Prodigy quickly moves to the top rope and takes off with an elbowdrop, but Curtis moves! Prodigy rolls on the mat, slowly getting up, which allows Curtis to toss him into the turnbuckle. Curtis runs in with a lariat. Prodigy drops off the turnbuckle, landing face first on the mat. Curtis hops to the second turnbuckle, then waits for Prodigy to get up.] JG - "What's Curtis doing?" JH - "I always stress the importance of a break during hard work. That's probably what Curtis' doing right now. In fact, I think I'll take a break. Excuse me, Grey." JG - "Maybe we can get Comrade Ulanov to come back out." [As Prodigy gets to his feet, Curtis takes off with a Thesz press from the second turnbuckle. He starts to nail Prodigy in the head with a series of punches, most of them stiff shots. Curtis gets up, taking Prodigy with him in a fireman's carry. He lays Prodigy out across the top turnbuckle, then starts going at Curtis' chest with a series of elbows.] JG - "Ken Curtis with a rather obscure move, but effective nonetheless." [Curtis grabs Prodigy by the head and pulls him down to the mat, executing almost a hangman's neckbreaker. Curtis quickly follows up with a knee from the second turnbuckle...but Prodigy moves! Both men roll on the mat, trying to muster up the strength to get up. Curtis gets up first and attacks Prodigy with a right, but Prodigy blocks! Prodigy nails Curtis back with a right of his own. Curtis fires back, but Prodigy blocks again and connects with a right. Again, Curtis attempts a right, but Prodigy ducks underneath and spins Curtis around, following up with a chickenwing!] JG - "Prodigy's got the crossface on! Curtis is in trouble!" JH - "About all the trouble Curtis' in is what to wear for the after match party." [Curtis drops to the mat, attempting to wiggle his way out of the move. He reaches back, but can't get hold of Prodigy's head.] JG - "Ken Curtis is really in trouble right now. I don't know how much longer he can hold on this chickenwing." [Curtis reaches out with his foot...and gets the ropes! Prodigy breaks the hold, kicking the rope in anger. He picks up Curtis and Irish whips him across the ring, dropping Curtis with a vicious clothesline, into a cover... 1! 2! KICKOUT! Prodigy gets up, lifting Curtis with him. He hoists Curtis high over his head into a vertical suplex. Instead of dropping back, Prodigy pushes Curtis' legs forward, bouncing off the ropes and then flying back in a normal suplex.] JG - "What was that? Prodigy used the top rope to get even more speed from Curtis as he fell back." JH - "That was pretty damn fast. Normally, I can see Prodigy's pathetic attempts at wrestling. That time, it was just a pathetic blur of wrestling." [Prodigy attempts a cover, again... 1! KICKOUT! Prodigy lifts up Curtis into a tombstone piledriver position... but Curtis' momentum carries him over! Curtis has Prodigy in the tombstone position and drives him in the mat!] JG - "Curtis with the big reversal and he spikes Prodigy!" JH - "Speaking of spiked, what ever happened to Phi Theta Kappa? Somebody must've spiked their drinks at a party and got them messed real bad." [Curtis climbs to the second turnbuckle, then takes off, connecting with a kneedrop. He moves down to the leg and attempts a figure four, but Prodigy pushes Curtis off! Curtis bounces backwards into the turnbuckle as Prodigy gets up, moving in with a series of forearm shots to the head. As Curtis begins to slouch from the attack, Prodigy grabs him and hits a fireman's takedown. Prodigy runs off the ropes, but Curtis rolls on the mat, forcing Prodigy to leap over Curtis. As Curtis gets up, Prodigy comes back with a flying cross body block...Curtis catches him! Curtis drops Prodigy across his knee with almost a backbreaker to the side instead of the back. He lifts Prodigy up quickly, then nails a jumping piledriver!] JH - "He drove Prodigy's head right into the mat. That little jump before the move really got a thrust." JG - "Curtis seems to setting up Prodigy for High Noon. He's really targetting the head as he continually drives it into the mat." JH - "Hell, Curtis is planting Prodigy's head into the mat more than Elmer Fudd's mutant eggplants!" [Curtis climbs to the second turnbuckle and takes off, hitting a fistdrop to the head of Prodigy. Curtis rolls on top of Prodigy and starts attacking with a series of punches to the head. He lifts Prodigy to his feet and catches Prodigy in a waistlock. Curtis tries a German suplex, but can't get Prodigy up! Prodigy reverses the waistlock, grabbing Curtis' right hand between his legs and nailing a pumphandle slam.] JH - "Weird looking move, eh?" JG - "Nobody ever said wrestling looked pretty." [Curtis gets to his feet, but is met by a kick to the midsection. As Curtis bends over, Prodigy hooks the arms for a double underhook suplex, but Curtis shakes him off and executes a back body drop. Curtis moves off the ropes and drops the elbow across the chest. As Prodigy bends, Curtis applies a front face lock.] JG - "Curtis is still attacking the head and neck area of Prodigy. It could only be a matter of time before we see High Noon." JH - "How can you see high noon? Isn't it a time?" [Prodigy moves towards the ropes and breaks the hold. Curtis releases the hold, then lifts up Prodigy. He starts unleashing a series of hard chops to the chest of Prodigy. Prodigy reels back into the turnbuckle as Curtis continues the attack. Prodigy slumps into the mat so Curtis backs off. Curtis runs into the other side of the ring and waits for Prodigy to get back up. As Prodigy gets up, Curtis takes off across the ring with a...] JG - "Handspring elbow!" JH - "Curtis executing a cartwheel? What's next? An Eddy Jacks moonsault?" [Prodigy drops to the mat, but Curtis coninues on the attack with a series of axehandles. Curtis grabs Prodigy by the hair and lifts him to his feet. He sends Prodigy running across the ring, attempting a clothesline. But, Prodigy slides between Curtis' open legs, getting right back up and hitting a dropkick! Curtis gets up quickly, but is taken down again by a Prodigy fling cross body block, into a pin... 1! KICKOUT!] JG - "Prodigy almost had him, again! He's come so close to winning this match and becoming champ!" JH - "He's already a champ. (laughs to himself) The TV champ." [Both men get up with Prodigy ahead. As Curtis gets up, Prodigy drops him with a dropkick, sending Curtis through the ropes. Prodigy walks to the outside of the apron, waiting for Curtis to get up before taking flight with a flying knee. Prodigy gets up quickly, taking Curtis with him on his shoulder. Prodigy runs for a powerslam, but Curtis pops out of Prodigy's shoulder, pushing Prodigy into the guard rail!] JG - "Curtis narrowly escaped that one! He almost lost the match there!" [Curtis walks back to Prodigy and tosses him into the ring. Curtis climbs in after Prodigy, hitting a swift kick in the groin area. Prodigy dives back, clutching the painful area. Curtis hits another boot to the groin, moving Prodigy into the corner. As Prodigy pulls himself into seated position, Curtis moves in and begins to deliver a series of kicks to the gut. Curtis lifts Prodigy to his feet, ready for an Irish whip, but Prodigy is able to grab onto the ropes, stopping the attack. As Curtis turns to see what's the matter, Prodigy delivers an eye rake, sending Curtis staggering back. Prodigy moves in and grabs Curtis by the shoulder and leg, hoisting him onto the his shoulders. Prodigy carries Curtis around the ring, showing off his strength, then drops to the mat with a Death Valley driver. Prodigy flips Curtis onto his back and applies a camel clutch.] JH - "You can take the technicality out of a move, but you can't take the move out of a wrestler." JG - "What?" JH - "I don't know. I was trying to say that Prodigy sucks." [Prodigy abandons the camel clutch, spinning Curtis onto his back. Prodigy then grabs hold of Curtis' leg, dropping an elbow on the knee area. Prodigy gets up again, dropping the elbow again. This time, Prodigy stays on the mat, wrenching the leg. Prodigy keeps hold of the leg, but gets to his feet. This allows Prodigy to keep Curtis in control, grabbing the other leg and placing Curtis in a Boston crab.] JG - "Prodigy's in control, now. He's really attacking Ken Curtis with those technical moves to wear him down." JH - "It's wearing me down. I can't stand this junk for much longer." [Curtis begins to squirm around, trying to get out of the crab. He lifts himself into pushup position and attempts to walk over to the ropes, but Prodigy releases the hold, sending Curtis crawling right underneath the ring. The crowd gives a little laugh, seeing Curtis pretty much crawling right out of the ring. This angers Curtis who gets back on the apron and shoulderblocks Prodigy, who was trying to grab Curtis' head. Curtis flings himself over the top with a sunset flip... 1! KICKOUT! Both men spring to their feet with Curtis hitting a hard right hand to the face of Prodigy. As Prodigy is pushed back, Curtis grabs his arm and drops him with a short clothesline. Prodigy gets up slowly, but is soon grabbed by Curtis who nails a powerbomb!] JG - "It's amazing that Ken Curtis can continue to lift a 280 plus pound man this late in the match." JH - "He's been lifting cows all his life, why not Prodigy?" [Curtis lifts Prodigy's head off the mat, throwing punches to the face. Prodigy, however, is able to lift both his feet up, hitting Curtis in the head with both feet on either side of Curtis' head. Prodigy lifts his feet up again, bringing them under Curtis' shoulders. This allows Prodigy to push Curtis down to the mat into a pin... 1! 2! KICKOUT! Both men leap to their feet but Prodigy is ready first. He kicks Curtis in the midsection, bending Curtis over. This allows Prodigy to take control with a scissorkick to the back of the head.] JH - "White boys can do that move?" [Prodigy hops on top of Curtis, but is met by a rake to the eyes. As Prodigy stumbles back, Curtis hits a dropkick, sending Prodigy sailing through the top ropes to the outside of the ring as Street Smarts makes his way down.] JG - "Oh no, what's he doing here?" JH - "It's his duty to make sure Curtis holds onto the belt. This little jobber is more important than Doug Adams!" [Prodigy gets to his feet as Street Smarts walks in, hitting Prodigy with...] JG - "The Ghetto Blaster!" JH - "What? It was a bodyslam!" JG - "I never said Street Smarts had much wrestling skill. But, he comes up with some pretty good names for pretty basic moves. That was his finisher." JH - "Was that the bell? Is the match over?" JG - "I think the referee disqualified Ken Curtis!" JH - "Street Smarts cost Curtis the match! Curtis is no longer undefeated!" JG - "He also cost Prodigy the title. And, you know the one man who is responsible for this..." [As Grey says that, Gangsta Impasse emerges from the curtain, smiling at Street Smarts. Prodigy, however, is not too pleased as he rushes up the aisle towards Gangsta. Street Smarts again steps in, protecting his mentor from the attack. Prodigy deals with Street Smarts rather easily, nailing Last Ride In. But, from this distraction, Gangsta runs down to the ring and attacks Curtis!] JG - "Gangsta and Curtis!" JH - "These two end every show with a fight!" JG - "They want each other _bad_! I don't know how much longer it will be before one of them comes out with a warrant for murder! Folks, we're out of time! Good night!" -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
