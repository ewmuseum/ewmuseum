[SCRA] The Scraps [1/6] (16 February 1998)

16 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
["Cowboy" Ken Curtis, dressed in black jeans and boots, a white t-shirt, and a black leather jacket, walks amid the blackened ruins of his torched barn. His rumpled hat is pulled low over his eyes, and his ever-present cigarillo juts from his teeth. He removes the cigarillo to speak, but does not raise his head to address the camera...] CKC - "A man with a whole lotta sense once told me, 'when ya find yerself in a hole, the first thang ya gotta do is stop diggin'." [Curtis stares hard into the camera, his eyes on fire with rage...] CKC - "Impasse, the way I figger it, yer hole's about six feet deep right about now, and when I start shovellin' dirt on top of ya, yer gonna wish you'da stopped diggin'." [Curtis kicks up a cloud of ashes and embers as the scene fades to black] ___________________________________________________________________________ ___ _ ___ |_ _|| |_ ___ / __> ___ _ _ ___ ___ ___ | | | . |/ ._> \__ \/ | '| '_><_> || . \<_-< |_| |_|_|\___. <___/\_|_.|_| <___|| _//__/ |_| ** LIVE ** FEBRUARY 16, 1997 ___________________________________________________________________________ JG - "Ladies and gentlemen, my guest this evening...is a young man who has taken the SCRA by storm! He claims to be one of the most versatile..." [Jon is interupted by a huge fan pop from the jam packed audience!!] JG - "...most versatile wrestler in professional wrestling. Ladies and gentlemen...CHASE HUNTER!!" ["Push" by Moist blares through the speakers. Lasers shoot out from all sides within the arena. The fans cheer loudly as out comes...no one? [A tarzan yell cries out. We see Chase Hunter, standing on top of the big screen, beating his chest like a gorilla. He's wearing merely a leopard fur loincloth.] JH - "(Voice over) Oh good god!! Well, now I've seen everything!! I wish I kicked his butt the second he came here!!" [Chase grabs a rope beside him and begins swinging down the aisle. He lands by ringside and climbs into the ring. The women are screaming in delight, as they usually do when something like this occurs.] JG - "Uh...Mr. Hunter. Welcome..." CH - "<grunt> Me Chazan...you Jon." JG - "Could we just get on with the interview, please?" [Chase keeps smiling but seems a little more serious...despite his... quaint ensemble. He props one elbow on Jon's shoulder and leans a bit to the side.] CH - "Okay, go ahead...I m ready." JG - "Chase, there's something people are wondering. Why do you show such a lack of respect to people who, as you say, are cliche's?" CH - "It's quite simple, really. Let's take, for example...the ever popular dead guy. He starts off a match, slaughting the opponent to death...pardon the pun. Then the guy does something wrong...or gets cheated...loses it for a bit...regenerates...wins. Booooorrriiiing!! But SPEAKING of dead guys..." JG - "Chase!! No!! Don't you..." [Chase cups his hand over Jon's mouth, preventing him from speaking. Huge fan pop from this action!!] CH - "Ding dong, the Phantom s gone. Hickory dickory dock, he was CHASED out of the clock. Maybe he'll come back...as a dentist!! Or a clown!! Maybe a bald guy with an attitude!! Yeah, we don't see many bald or balding guys with an attitude around, do we!?" [Huge fan pop!!] CH - "Phantom saw the future...and the word versatility was painted all over it!!" [Another huge pop!! Chase removes his hand from Jon's mouth. Jon, surprisingly, remains quiet, regardless.] CH - "So where was I? Oh yes!! Cliches!! They're getting more and more common. I'm simply adding a little extra spice to professional wrestling. Being versatile and never the same gives the fans everything they want!!" JG - "Sounds resonable. (Jon pauses for a moment) As you know, there's one athlete that has taken an active interest in you and..." CH - "Hatian Warrior, Gumshoe Thing-a-ma-bob!!" JG - "I think he's getting upset at you for calling him that." CH - "He hasn't signed the dotted line, yet. I've got a special type of match all set up for him, too!! I'll call him Willy Wonka if I feel like it." JG - "Why do you think he's fixated on you, specifically?" CH - "He's jealous!! He's yet another cliche. The mindless, gullible, ritual filled (Chase switches to a snobby accent for a second) ancient warrior. (Back to regular voice) I wouldn't be surprised if the next one of those guys will be...Kagli-Muro, the Ethiopian Giant. The poor people over there are starving because the guy ate all the food!! Come on, you can just picture a guy like that!" JG - "But..." CH - "The guy has to stop doing this fancy smancy rituals in front of the bathroom mirror. Well, I guess I'm guilty of doing my own ritual in front of the bathroom mirror...it's called brushing my teeth." [Laughter from the audience.] CH - "Look, all he has to do is go out for a couple of beers...you know, party a little. Maybe then he won t be so boring!!" JH - "(Voice over) Whoa, wait a minute, here comes Edenshaw himself!! And I think Chase spots him!!" CH - "(Speainking in an indian chanting) Hey how are ya, hey how are ya!!" [Edenshaw climbs into the ring and stands right in Chase's face. Chase suddenly becomes very serious as they begin a very harsh staredown. Neither speak for the moment. Then, Caboto yells something and Edenshaw attacks Hunter. Grey jumps out of the ring as the two exchange punches.] JH - "Look at them go!" [Security rushes in and breaks the two up.] JG - "What a way to start out the show. Curtis comes on saying he's ready to bury Gangsta Impasse. I also hear he has a surprise in store. Chase Hunter continues his verbal attack on Gwaii Edenshaw, as well." JH - "But, that's not all!" JG - "We've got a World title defense later on tonight as "Cowboy" Ken Curtis begins his long series. Task one is against an old foe in Prodigy." JH - "A nice little match. Prodigy won't put up much of a fight so the match won't last long and the suffering for myself will be minimal. At least it will be offset by Black Kat at ringside. Did you see her last night? Hubba hubba, eh?" JG - "Quite. Also near the end of the show, Gangsta Impasse and "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin! What a match that one's set to be!" JH - "It's gonna be a brawl. But, cowboys can fight a whole lot harder than gangsters. They've been fighting since like a long time ago. These gangs are just starting to spring up." JG - "An interesting take. The North American tournament continues tonight as Derek Mota butts heads with "Iconoclast" Sean Watts. Both these men are ready to make an impact in the league and this tournament is their way how." JH - "Who wouldn't want to make an impact?" JG - "Judging from some of our past wrestlers, I don't think that's a very good question to ask, Jack." JH - "Oh, right. The Zamza syndrome." JG - "We've also got two big tag matches, the Siberian Express and Comrades Inc. defend the pride of Russia against the German Eagles and Pros, respectively. This truly is an international match as only one of the members of those four teams are born in North America!" JH - "And, he happens to be the worst of them all. Grant, you're giving us a bad name." JG - "The big cruiserweight collision takes place later in the show as four of our top cruisers rock the foundation of the soon to be formed division." JH - "A nice walk in the park for Aladdin. Those other three are a little questionable, eh?" JG - "And, we've got a Weekend Wars rematch..." JH - "Great booking." JG - "As Nik Nyce squares off with Gwaii Edenshaw. Will Chase Hunter play a factor?" JH - "We just saw him, so I'm hoping that one appearance was enough for the night." JG - "But, right now, let's go to the ring for "Hot Stuff" Mike Justice and Rock Bottom Robbie!" ___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 1 ) | | \ | | "Hot Stuff" Mike Justice vs. Rock Bottom Robbie / |___ | | | | Writer: Alex G. (_________________________________________________________________________ [Jaspard hops into the ring, ready for the big intros. Surprisingly, he acts normal.] JH - "Ha! I think Jaspard acting normal is a bigger joke than him screwing up!" EJ - "This matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. Coming down the aisle first, weighing in at 270 lbs. and hailing from Chicago, Illinois..."Hot Stuff" Mike Justice!" [Hot Stuff by Donna Summers comes over the speakers as Mike Justice walks into the arena. He makes a quick entrance, taking notice only to the fans who cheer him, the women.] EJ - "His opponent, hailing Intercourse, Pennsylvania and weighing in at 245 lbs, Rock Bottom Robbie!" ['Little Things' by Bush blasts through the speakers to a huge face pop by the fans. Rock Bottom Robbie walks through the curtains, holding his TV title belt in the air and the roar of the crowd increases. He walks quickly to the ring, slapping hands with the fans as he goes. He climbs in the ring as fire works flare in the background. Justice attacks right away.] DING! DING! DING! JG - "Oh no! Mike Justice is going right at Robbie." JH - "Dumb Robbie. He lets his little fireworks go off all the while his opponent is waiting to be him up." [But, Robbie quickly regains his composure and starts firing back with punches of his own. Justice stumbles backwards, but Robbie stays on him until Justice drops to the mat. He rolls out of the ring to avoid Robbie's attack, but Robbie slides right after him.] JH - "That's some smart thinking by Mike Justice. He's getting away from the attack quickly and harmlessly." JG - "I'm not so sure about harmlessly! Here comes Robbie!" [Robbie dives at Justice, tossing him into the ring steps on the outside. Justice bounces off as Robbie raises his arms to a loud pop from the crowd. Suddenly, Chase Hunter walks down, some sort of jar in his hand.] JG - "Oh no, what does he want?" JH - "Good question. I thought he made his appearance for the night." [Hunter walks over to Robbie, explaining something to him as Robbie dives into the ring. Hunter drops the jar's contents onto Justice's hand, leaving Justice diving around the ringside area. Hunter takes off.] JG - "It's a hot coal! Justice's hands are burning!" JH - "Hunter! No!" [Justice drops the coal, hands severely burned. Robbie bends over the ring and grabs Justice by the hair, pulling him into the ring. Dazed from the coals, Justice is easy prey as Robbie hoists him up and into the Rock Bottom Slam! The cover... 1! 2! 3!] JG - "Robbie got him!" JH - "Hey, short match for Robbie. That wasn't so bad." JG - "Robbie vowed to make short work of Justice...and he did. He must be flying high right now." JH - "He can't. Those wrestling tights don't have zippers." JG - "(sighs) Let's see what Keith Spencer is doing near the ring." KS - "Ladies and gentlemen my guest tonight for an interview, "The Perfectionist" NIK NYCE. " [Nik makes his way to the ring already in wrestling gear. He's a strong look of disgust on his face as he sees the fans of SCRA booing him. The fans don't get to him though as he makes it to the ring.] KS - "Thanks for joining me tonight Mr. Nyce." NN - "Come on, just get with the questions, I'll answer whatever your little mind can think of and then I'll beat this bum I'm facing tonight again for the second time in just a few days." KS - "Well first of all, everyone is wondering what is going on between you and Victoria Glenn." NN - "I have no interest in her. She has a lot of interest in me though. Every week I come on SCRA camera pleading and begging to have McAllister to have his little whore on a chain so she stops bothering me while I'm training for my matches but every week she keeps on bothering me. Her goal in life is for me to father her child and I just will not do it, no matter how much she disturbs my training." KS - "Well she certainly is getting a lot of attention in SCRA. Mike Justice is another wrestler in SCRA that is trying to win her over." NN - "Mike Justice can have her for all I care. Mike Justice is a funny little fellow. He thinks he's mister macho but he's really a little insecure boy that happens to have a few small little muscles. You see no matter who they all, ALL THE LADIES want Nik Nyce. I don't need to act like a Mike Justice to get the women. They flock to me. I don't have to put much work in to get the ladies." KS - "Now with Victoria Glenn is Bobby McAllister, a man that you've already fought in the ring before." NN - "Yes it's quite the little triangle isn't it. The lowly little Bobby even got a win over me but I overcame that and that is why between Bobby McAllister, Mike Justice, and I, I've risen above those two I'm on to much better things. I have a shot for the World title while both of them are stuck gunning for lesser belts. I'm showing that once again, I'm able to completly back up all my talk with great wins and great wrestling style. Even the SCRA bookers can't keep up with me, they stick me with the same opponent to wrestle tonight even though I just beat him a couple of days ago." [All of a sudden, Bobby McAllister walks out, screaming at Nyce.] JH - "This could be fun!" [McAllister jumps in the ring and dives at Nyce. The two start rolling around on the mat, throwing punches wildly at each other. Out of the back, Mike Justice emerges.] JG - "What's he doing here? I thought his hands were burned!" JH - "Nothing's too burned for a good fight." [All of a sudden, a swarm of girls leap out of the crowd and hoist Justice onto their shoulders. They start carrying him into the back.] JG - "Huh?" JH - "What?" JG - "Mike Justice is being carried away by a stream of women. What's going on here?" [Security rushes down to ringside and breaks up the Nyce and McAllister. They pull back Nyce first, escorting him to the back where Victoria Glenn stands at the top of the ramp. Nyce blows her a kiss which McAllister sees. McAllister breaks free from security and darts at Nyce, again sending the two sprawling to the ground. Security dives in, attempting to break apart the two. They yank McAllister off who is escorted by Victoria Glenn out of the arena.] JG - "I think those two are ready to kill each other." JH - "Hell, I think Nyce is ready to kill Victoria Glenn! She keeps distracting him from training..." JG - "That's a lie! Nik Nyce is making up stories to rattle McAllister." JH - "Whatever works, right? All I know is that McAllister's pants are strung so high that you can see his underwear." JG - "Lovely image, Jack. Lovely." JH - "It's not always pretty." JG - "Up next, we've got the Pros and Comrades Inc.!" JH - "Newbies against oldies. I hope the newbies win, the oldies stink." JG - "Well, let's go to the ring and find out!" -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
