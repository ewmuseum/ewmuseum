[SCRA] The Scraps, 15 December 1997, Part 3

15 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 4 ) | | \ | | Pablo Morales vs. Silver Stone / |___ | | | | Writer: GR (_________________________________________________________________________ [The camera pans down avross the crowd and to Ed Jaspard, mic in hand, standing center-ring. Some rowdy fans behind him hold up a "SCOOPS SUX" sign, while others hold up "Lifetaker Is My Dad" and "Push Prodigy" signs.] EJ - "Ladies and gentlemen, this match is for the SCRA Television title!" [Big pop!] EJ - "Introducing first, your challenger..." ["Fight Till You Die" by Pennywise screams from the public address system as Silver Stone, dressed in a black jacket and silver shades, makes his way through the curtains and to the ring. His pace is one of focus and he ignores the fans on his way to the ring.] EJ - "... from Carson City, Nevada, weighing in at 269 pounds, he is SILVER STONE!" [Pop! Stone enters the ring and begins using the ropes to stretch.] EJ - "And your SCRA Television champion..." [A strong salsa beat replaces "Fight" and the crowd pops heavy! Pablo Morales makes his way through the curtains and down the aisle towards the ring.] EJ - "... accompanied to the ring by Miss Alison, from Mexico City, Mexico, weighing in at 245 pounds, he is PABLO MORALES!" [Heavy crowd pop for the champion as he makes his way into the ring. Miss Alison holds the ropes for Morales and he slides in and begins to bounce in anticipation.] EJ - "This is a title match with a time limit of..." JG - "Whoa! Silver Stone has jumped Morales and is putting the boots to him!" JH - "Attaboy, Stone! Take it to him!" [Jaspard and Miss Alison scramble from the ring as the referee calls for the bell!] DING! DING! DING! [Stone picks Morales up and irish-whips him into the ropes... running clothesline! Morales hits the mat hard as Stone closes in! Stone leaps... elbow drop across Morales' chest!] JG - "Here's a cover!" 1! JG - "Morales kicks out with authority!" JH - "You gotta respect that! Stone went for the win pretty quick!" JG - "Wishful thinking if you ask me!" [Stone gets up and slips out of his jacket, tossing it over the top rope to the floor! He picks up Morales, who's still in his ring jacket, and gives him a knife-edge chop to the chest! Morales reels as Stone hits him with another chop! Morales backs into the ropes as Stone charges and clotheslines him over the top!] JG - "Did you see the champ's head bounce off the floor!" JH - "Look at Miss Alison! She's beside herself!" [Miss Alison makes her way over to Morales and helps him up to one knee and out of his ring jacket. She tries to get Morales to his feet as the referee counts!] 3...4...5... JH - "He's gonna get counted out!" JG - "I don't think Stone's gonna let that happen!" [Morales climbs onto the apron. Stone grabs him, but Morales delivers a shoulder-block through the ropes to Stone's gut! Another! Morales propels over the ropes...] JG - "Sunset flip! Here's a cover!" 1! 2! JG - "And Stone barely escapes!" [Both men are up, but Morales delivers a forearm to the side of Stone's head! Another! Stone is staggered, Morales irish-whips him into the ropes... Stone ducks a clothesline and propels off the opposite ropes, leapfrog by Morales, Stone off the ropes...] JG - "Powerslam by Morales! What a move by the big man!" JH - "Cover him!" [Morales picks up Stone... snap-suplex! Morales pops up quickly and drops a knee across Stone's forehead! Stone grabs his head in pain as Morales covers him!] 1! 2! JG - "Whoa! Stone just barely got the shoulder up!" JH - "I think he's playing possum!" JG - "What?" JH - "You know, playing... nevermind." [Morales picks up Stone and bodyslams him to the mat! Morales moves in and locks on a surfboard!] JH - "Snooze." JG - "This is classic wrestling, Hazard, which is real surprising given these guys' personalities!" JH - "I liked the old personalities better!" [Stone struggles to his feet as Morales tries to keep the surfboard intact! Stone reverses the surfboard and hits Morales with a boot to the gut! Another! Morales doubles over and Stone hits him with a high knee-lift! Morales hits the mat and Stone falls to straddle him, laying the punches in on Morales' head!] JH - "Now this more like it!" JG - "Get in there, ref!" [The referee counts to four before Stone stops the punches. Stone stands and pulls Morales to his feet! He picks Morales up... bodyslam! Stone propels himself across the ring and scales the ropes...] JH - "Moonsault! Here's a cover by Stone!" 1! 2! JH - "And Morales gets the shoulder up! Stone almost took the gold!" JG - "Morales is tough, he's not gonna let the strap go that easy!" [Stone pulls Morales to his feet and sets him up for a vertical suplex... no! Morales slides over the top and grabs Stone in a waistlock! Stone throws a back elbow to Morales' head! Another! Another! Stone propels himself into the ropes and rebounds, Stone ducks a clothesline and rebounds off the opposite ropes, Morales attempts a hiptoss... blocked by Stone! Stone reverese the hiptoss...] JG - "Swinging neckbreaker! Here's a cover!" 1! 2! JG - "Morales kicked out! What a neckbreaker by Stone! That wasn't the SilverSwing, though, it wasn't delivered with the usual force!" JH - "The reversal prevented that!" JG - "What? A logical call from Jack Hazard?" JH - "I have my moments." [Stone gets to his feet and propels himself into the ropes! He rebounds and leaps...] JG - "Flying splash! No! Morales got the knees up and drove them into Stone's gut! What a counter!" JH - "That's gonna leave a mark!" [Morales gets up, lays a couple of boots into Stone's head, and drops an elbow across his chest! Morales pops up heads to the corner...] JG - "He's going up top! We may see his finisher here!" [Morales climbs to the top turnbuckle and raises his arms, causing the crowd to pop heavy! Morales leaps...] JG - "Nobody home! Morales went for the flying elbow, but Stone was able to slide out of the way! Both men are down!" [The referee counts...] 2...3...4...5... [Stone crawls to the ropes and pulls himself to one knee as Morales struggles to get up! Stone is up, he lays in a couple of boots to Morales' head! He grabs Morales by the hair and picks him up... vertical suplex! Here's a cover!] 1! 2! JG - "And Morales gets the shoulder up! We almost had a new champion there!" JH - "Yeah, but almost and a quarter..." JG - "I know." [Stone is up, he picks up Morales and irish-whips him into the corner! The ring shakes as Morales hits the turnbuckle and comes staggering back out! Stone propels himself off the ropes...] JG - "Flying forearm by Stone! What a manuever! The champion is down!" JH - "Not for long!" [Stone wastes no time in picking Morales back up! He sets him up... Northern Lights suplex! Stone pops up and yells to the crowd, getting a pop! Stone steps back... elbow-drop! Stone pops up... another elbow-drop! Here's a cover!] 1! 2! JG - "Morales kicks out!" JH - "I'll give 'em this... both men are trying to win the match!" JG - "Isn't that the objective?" JH - "Not always. Sometimes, they just wanna beat each other to a bloody pulp!" JG - "Point taken!" [Stone picks up Morales and irish-whips... no! Reversed by Morales, Stone off the ropes, flying body-press... no! Morales ducks and Stone hits the canvas! Morales grabs Stone and pulls him close to the corner!] JH - "Uh, I'll have a Nacho Supreme and a large orange drink, please!" JG - "I think your order is coming right up, sir!" [Morales scales the turnbuckle, facing the crowd, and raises his arms with a yell! the crowd pops heavy for the champion as he continues his climb!] JH - "He's taking too long!" JG - "I should say so, here's comes Stone!" JH - "He was playing... nevermind." [Morales reaches the top and turns around, only to be met with a Stone fist to the gut! Another! Stone scales to the second rope...] JG - "He's setting him up for a superplex!" JH - "That could spell the end for Pablo!" [Stone sets him up... no! Blocked by Morales as he grabs the top rope! Stone pulls him again... blocked! Morales slips out of the suplex and lands a forearm to the side of Stone's head! Another! Another! Stone is staggered... and Morales pushes him to the mat!] JG - "Stone's head bounced off the mat like a basketball! We may see that top-rope legdrop after all!" JH - "Morales is up top again!" JG - "But Stone is up! What the...!" [Stone staggers towards the corner where Morales is perched, Morales leaps...] JG - "Hurracanrana! Into a victory roll! Here's the count!" 1! 2! 3! DING! DING! DING! JG - "Morales did it! He successfully defended his Television title strap with a hurracanrana into a victory roll! What an impressive move from the big man!" JH - "I gotta say, the math was a little more exciting than I thought it would be." JG - "I guess if there's no blood or dismemberment, that's about as good a compliment a bout can get from you." JH - "I guess so!" EJ - "Ladies and gentlemen, here's your winner, and STILL the SCRA Television champion... PABLO MORALES!" JG - "A surprisingly clean match from these two rulenreakers. It's nice to see a match decided on the skills of the wrestlers and not by fisticuffs." JH - "Hey, some guys' skills _are_ fisticuffs!" JG - "Point taken... I guess." JH - "What's next on our agenda, Grey? JG - "Well, we're going to get to watch the ever-popular RBR challenge fan "non-favorite" Phantom." JH - "So a guy we sort of care about challenges the worst wrestler to ever hold the North American title. Oh, forgot about Silver Stone." JG - "Phantom has looked rather lackluster in his recent matches, further evidencing his fall from grace. Once, fans "enjoyed" his many gimmicks. Now, we simply await the end of his matches." JH - "Eloquently put, Grey." JG - "Well, my speech therapy is paying off...Hazard is giving me a compliment." JH - "Whatever, Grey." JG - "(sighing) Okay, Jack. Let's go to Ed Jaspard with the intros." ___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 5 ) | | \ | | Rock Bottom Robbie vs. Phantom / |___ | | | | Writer: OB (_________________________________________________________________________ [The camera pans down to ringside, revealing a seersucker suit-clad Ed Jaspard, who is apparently complimenting his ring attire with a straw "AA" hat. Jaspard picks up the mic and, after experiencing some initial problems in mic-handling, puts it to his lips and begins to speak.] EJ - "Okay, okay...this one's going out to little Timmy, who is presently in a hospital and has been waiting for three years to watch his hero claim the Heisman....thanks, everyone, for making Charles Woodson the happiest man in college football. Mom, dad, everybody....you made this the happiest day in my life.....and especially you, coach Carr. But I want to congratulate everyone, especially the people who didn't...." [A stage manager leaps into the ring and whispers something to Jaspard, who nods his head.] EJ - "(shocked) Apparently, friends...and especially for you, Timmy....I have been informed that I am NOT Charles Woodson and I have not won the Heisman. However, I have been instructed to inform you that our first "wrestler" goes by the name of Rock Bottom Robbie and hails from Intercourse, Pennyslvania. Uh-huh...that's "believable." [Little Things blares over the PA as Rock Bottom Robbie jogs towards the ring in a nondescript jacket and trunks, taking care to slap the hands of his many fans. He enters the ring, shakes Jaspard's hands, and begins to stretch. Jaspard, still looking shocked, continues the intros.] EJ - "And his opponent, a man who was voted "Mr. Congeniality" at his high school reunion and works as a ticket-taker on the turnpike in his spare time, the man who sees London, France, and his underpants....Phantom!!!!" [Phantom, accompanied by the beautiful Christine and wearing a black singlet, begins his march to the ring as the Overture from the Phantom of the Opera begins to play over the PA. Having reached the ring, he nimbly leaps over the ropes. Chuckling at Robbie, he slumps into the turnbuckle and yawns as if tired of the pre-match spectacle. Robbie only shakes his head.] JH - "Absolutely horrible. Jaspard is the most ignorant man in the sport." JG - "Yes, but he is a "special" announcer." JH - "Is that why he doesn't get fined when he parks in the handicapped spaces at SCRA Towers?" JG - "More or less. But Alex, my ever-lovin' bro, doesn't condone our use of handicapped in lieu of "special." JH - "What??? Are you quoting the SCRA's "Guidelines for Interpersonal Relations via the Use of Creative Broacasting Terminology" again?" DING! DING! DING! [Robbie and Phantom lock up, with Phantom managing to take RBR down with a quick hiptoss. Robbie bounces right back up, though, and counters Phantom's attempted armbar with an excellent Irish whip that sends the smaller man into the turnbuckle. Displaying excellent strength, RBR grabs Phantom and powerbombs the opera fop to the mat. Clutching his back and using an opportunity provdided by Robbie's inability to capitalize on this opportunity, Phantom struggles back to his feet.] JH - "Quick start, no?" JG - "Well, Phantom's about to realize why he's not doing more in the SCRA. He's just too little to battle some of the league's bigger men on a regular basis. Robbie's a true brawler, and he has a special dislike for Phantom." JH - "Who doesn't, Grey? I mean, this guy's "marked for death" by the jWo." [Robbie continues to dominate Phantom, taking him to the mat following a fireman's carry. RBR works the smaller man on the mat, using an armbar to wear down Phantom's resistance. Robbie releases the hold, allowing Phantom to bounce back to his feet, albeit wearied and clutching his injured shoulder.] JG - "Looks like something may have given in Phantom's shoulder. He may have pulled a muscle or something." JH - "Muscle? Phantom? Aren't these terms mutually exclusive?" JG - "Actually, Jack, Phantom is quite the hunk of knotty muscle." JH - "Grey, you're forcing me to do something the GIRUCBT forbids me to do...." [Continuing his onslaught, Robbie gorilla-presses Phantom to the mat, then follows up that exceptional power move with a surfboard submission. Put on poorly, Phantom easilly breaks out of the hold.] JG - "Phantom may be ready to put on a show. We haven't seen much from him, but he is quite the aerialist." JH - "Correction there, Grey. Phantom is quite the balletist." JG - "'Balletist'? Is that a word." JH - "There is no place for balletists in wrestling." JG - "Huh?" [Robbie presses onward, taking Phantom down with a football tackle. Phantom bounces back up, though, and throws several pitiful chops that Robbie easilly blocks. Undaunted, Phantom attempts a dropkick that hits only air. Laughing off Phantom's pathetic attack, Robbie grabs Phantom from behind and hits a backbreaker that neatly KO's Phantom on the mat. Robbie, not deigning to go for a quick cover, instead mounts the top turnbuckle and nails a fistdrop that sends a shockwave rippling through Phantom's body.] JH - "Punish him! Punish him! Break that little man!" JG - "It's not like Robbie to punish an opponent...unless, of course, he holds a grudge against that opponent." JH - "He happens to be wrestling Phantom. Everyone has a grudge against that guy. And the way he's wrestling, well, we see why. It's easy to exact revenge on a pussy." [Robbie hits a textbook-perfect Rock Bottom Slam, a move he typically uses to get a pin. However, Robbie continues to pound on Phantom, delivering stomp after stomp to the midsection of his prone foe. He bounces off the ropes and delivers a legdrop, then follows it up by delivering a series of blows upon Phantom's head, drawing a crimson stream through the mask of Phantom.] JH - "The juice! The juice! The juice is loose! Finally, something interesting happens in this lackluster match!" JG - "Yes, folks, someone's bleeding! I don't know why, but blood brings out the worst in wrestling fans. Look, they're throwing stuff into the ring." [Brushing off several paper cups, Robbie yanks Phantom back up and Rock Bottom Slams him onto the mat... 1! 2! 3! And it's over. Robbie gets up, kicks Phantom out of the ring and to the tender mercies of Christine, and jogs back to the locker room.] JH - "Another lackluster drubbing. The Beyonders haven't looked good recently." JG - "Well, Robbie certainly proved he's the man to beat in the NA/TV picture. Phantom wasn't the NA champ he once was, but he remains a determined and talented competitor. Robbie's casual dismissal of this quality wrestler makes him look...." JH - "...large and in charge." JG - "Okay, we'll go with that." JH - "I got more of 'em, Grey. Wanna hear 'em?" JG - "That's quite all right, Jack." JH - "Com'on! Ask me how it's hangin'!" JG - "I think we'll just continue right on with the next match. Two up and coming stars in the league face off. Both are undefeated..." JH - "Not for long." JG - "Of course. That's what happens when you wrestle a match." JH - "What about time limit draws? Double countout? Double DQs?" JG - "We get the picture, Jack. Let's to to Ed for the intros." JH - "Again? How many of these damn things do we have to hear?" JG - "Every match." JH - "Can't we spicen things up? Get a streaker out here during the intros or something." -- ***** [name withheld] - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html ***** This article was posted from <A HREF=" http://www.slurp.net/ ">Slurp Net</A>.
