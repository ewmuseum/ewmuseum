[SCRA] The Scraps [3/6] (16 February 1998)

16 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| Match 4 ) | | \ | | "Golden King" Aladdin Style vs. Bobby McAllister vs. / |___ | | | | Chase Hunter vs. "Golden" Ponyboy Kertins / Writer: Greg R. (__________________________________________________________________________ [The camera pans down across the crowd to the ring, where Ed Jaspard stands center-ring, mic in hand.] EJ - "Ladies and gentlemen, theis next event is a four-way dance set for one fall with a thirty-minute time limit! Your first competitior..." [Suddenly, the Marine Hymn blares from the arena speakers. Bobby McAllister walks to the ring with the Marine Hymn playing in the background. He is preceded to the ring by Victoria Glenn, who walks up the steel stairs and waits on the ring apron. Both Victoria and Bobby slap hands with the fans as they walk down the aisle. Bobby jumps from the floor to the ring apron, holds the ropes for Victoria, and then slingshots himself into the ring. McAllister then goes to each corner and raises his hands above his head.] JH - "Semper fi!" JG - "When were you in the Marines?" JH - "In a previous life." JG - "Will you shutup?" EJ - "... from New York, New York, weighing in at 224 lbs, he is BOBBY MCALLISTER!" [Moderate pop from the crowd. Suddenly, "Burn" by the Cure, replaces the Marine Hymn as "Golden King" Aladdin Style splits the curtain and makes his way to the ring.] EJ - "Your second competitor, he hails from Fredericton, New Brunswick, weighing in at 223 lbs, he is "Golden King" ALADDIN STYLE!" [Big crowd pop as "Push" by Moist replaces "Burn". Lasers all over the place, from the rafters, ring, curtains...you name it, they're there. Chase walks down at a bit of a fast walk, slapping hands and poses for some people with cameras. Chase climbs the ring steps and leaps over the top rope, then does a cartwheel from the corner...cartwheels back in the same motion...and taunts Style and McAllister.] EJ - "Your third competitor, he is from Los Angeles, California, and weighs in a 220 lbs, he is CHASE HUNTER!" [Moderate crowd pop as "Hail Hail" by Pearl Jam replaces Moist. "Golden" Ponyboy Kertins slpits the entrance curtain and makes his way down the rampo, lead by the Greasers and Darry. Pony slaps the fans hands while Two-Bit plays around with them. Cherry wears a red miniskirt, while Two-Bit wears his Mickey mouse shirt.] JH - "Whatever happened to the guy that just comes to the ring and wrestles? These entrances are gettimg out of hand. Damn those Freebirds! Damn 'em!" JG - "Hey, these guys just wanna entertain the fans." JH - "Since when are the fans entertained by some guy posing and a bunch of fireworks?!" JG - "Ever heard of Hulk Hogan?" JH - "Yeah, somewhere. Isn't he dead?" JG - "No." JH - "Well, damnit, he should be!" [They get to the ring and climb the steps. Darry goes in the ring right away while Soda walks on the apron, Steve follows, Two-Bit follows him, and Pony follows him. Pony opens the ropes for Cherry. There is one Greaser at each corner as they all jump over the ropes at almost the same time. Pony flexes, Steve and Soda on either side, on one knee, flexing with one arm as Darry and Two-Bit stand cross-armed on either side of Cherry as an explosion of fireworks go off to either side. Pony removes the sweatsuit he's wearing and sits on the top turnbuckle.] JH - "Alright, get the entourages outta there and let's see some fisticuffs!" [Wellington admonishes all non-wrestlers to leave the ring, and calls for the bell!] DING! DING! DING! JG - "It looks like we're going to see McAllister and Style first." [Collar-and-elbow tieup... quick armdrag by Style puts Mac on the mat! Mac is up, they tieup again! Style forces Mac back to the corner, and the break is clean!] JH - "Bor-ing!" JG - "This is sound, clean wrestling, Hazard!" JH - "This is also sleep-inducing!" [Another collar-and-elbow, but Mac slams a knee into Style's gut! Another! Style is bent over, Mac propels himsefl into the ropes... swinging neckbreaker! Here's a cover!] 1! 2! JG - "No! Aladdin Style kicked out!" JH - "Now this is more like it!" [Mac pulls Style to his feet, irish-whip into the ropes! Style rebounds and ducks a clothesline! Style off the opposite ropes... tilt-a-whirl backbreaker by Mac! Mac pulls Style to his feet, but Style lays a forearm into Mac's gut! Another! Mac is doubled over and Style hits him with a European uppercut, staggering him back! Style gets to his feet and tags in Chase Hunter!] JG - "And here comes Hunter!" JH - "Ha! He was hiding, but now we've yanked him in!" [Hunter walks in and hits a dropkick, sending McAllister over the top rope. Hunter tags back out to Style.] JH - "Wimp!" JG - "Or, smart, on two parts. Hunter wants to stay out of this one to conserve energy for a later date and McAllister wants Style. The two will be locking up next week." [McAllister hops back in and eyes Style. The two lock up in the middle of the ring again, this time with Style moving around into a hammerlock. McAllister ducks behind Style and reverses the hold. Style re-reverses the hold. McAllister attempts a different tactic and lifts Style onto his shoulders, then drops back into a fallaway slam. McAllister runs into the ropes, but Style rolls over, forcing McAllister to jump over. As McAllister bounces off the ropes, Style hits a monkey flip. Style moves in and applies a scissorlock across McAllister's chest. Style releases the hold and applies a matted waistlock. McAllister squirms around the mat and gets to his feet, but Style takes him down with a waistlock suplex. McAllister springs to his feet and runs at Style, but Style takes him down with a armdrag, quickly followed by an armbar. Style tags out to Kertins.] JG - "Aladdin Style went to work on McAllister in that sequence. He may have the upper hand heading into their match next week." JH - "But, never discount the underdog. Then again, McAllister is so far under that a dog couldn't get there." JG - "And, we get our first glimpse at Ponyboy Kertins!" JH - "What the hell kind of name is Ponyboy?" [Kertins hops into the ring and nails an elbowdrop across McAllister's arm. On his way out, Style drops Kertins with a clothesline. McAllister uses this time to recover and moves towards Kertins, taking him down with a bodyslam. McAllister runs into the ropes, but Kertins rolls underneath again. McAllister leaps over but on the way back, Kertins attempts a hiptoss. McAllister stops the hold, then dives underneath with a drop toe hold. He quickly changes this into a figure four.] JG - "McAllister's got the move locked on! Kertins and his aerial attack are in trouble now!" JH - "An aerialist gets in trouble before the bell even rings. And, if the bell doesn't ring, he still gets in trouble." JG - "How?" JH - "I don't know. Aerialists just suck." [Kertins moves towards the ropes, trying to get out of the hold. He can't reach the ropes, but lifts his hand up, tagging in Chase Hunter!] JH - "Let's go, Lacey Chasey!" [McAllister releases the figure four and stares at Hunter, almost in mutual respect. The two move into the center of the ring and lock up, McAllister getting the advantage and whipping Hunter into the ropes, hitting a running lariat. He moves towards the top turnbuckle, waits for Hunter to get up and takes off, but Hunter ducks away from a bulldog. Hunter hops on top of McAllister and applies a grapevine submission.] JH - "McAllister's really been in trouble this match. I think everybody's had a chance to beat on him." JG - "But, he's attacked everybody else, too." [McAllister pulls himself to the ropes, releasing the hold. Hunter lays back, allowing McAllister to make the tag. McAllister does...to Aladdin Style.] JG - "He made Style get int he ring! McAllister wants Style to get some punishment going into their match next week!" [Style hops into the ring, a little weary of the task. He walks up to Hunter and attempts the facerake, but Hunter grabs Style's hand, then lifts Style onto his shoulder and drops down with a powerslam. Hunter climbs to the top rope and takes flight with a legdrop, but Style moves out of the way. Style immediately moves in, grabbing Hunter by the leg and twisting it into a Texas cloverleaf.] JG - "Aladdin Style's got the cloverleaf on! He's already defeated many with this move, some say he learned it from former partner, Jason Gore." JH - "Defeated many with this one, but also with a slew of others." [McAllister runs into the ring, breaking the hold. Hunter slides across the ring, tagging out to Kertins. Hunter smiles as he gets to the outside, able to watch two others beat each other up. Kertins runs in, but Style sidesteps him, pushing Kertins into the ropes. Style hits a hiptoss into an armbar submission. Kertins works his way over to the ropes and gets up. Style stands back and lets Kertins get up. Style runs at Kertins, but Kertins sees him. Kertins ducks and hits a back body drop. Style lands on the other side of the ring, looking around for a tag.] JG - "It seems odd that Style is trying to get out of this match. Normally, he's ready to fight." JH - "Easy rationale, Grey. When you can let two others beat each other up, then come in and finish the job and receive the same credit, do it." [Kertins moves towards Style, but recieves a thumb in the eyes. Style runs off the ropes and hits a kneelift on the bent over Kertins. Style picks Kertins up and goes for an irish whip. Kertins reverses it and goes for a clothesline. Style ducks it and hooks the arm. He hits a crucifix and keeps Kertins down for a pin... 1! KICKOUT! Kertins leaps to his feet, looking around for a tag. Hunter points towards McAllister, who easily wants to get in. McAllister and Style lock up, Style able to back McAllister into the corner. Style starts to unleash a series of chops in the corner, the sound echoing in the entire arena. But, McAllister rallies somehow and tosses Style into the turnbuckle. He starts to unleash a series of chops, only stopping to watch Style fall face first on the mat. McAllister springs onto the second rope, then takes off with a big splash on the fallen Style.] JG - "McAllister is showing that he can compete with Aladdin Style. Could this be a slight preview of what's in store next week?" JH - "Next week, we won't have these two morons sitting outside." [As if hearing Hazard, Chase Hunter turns to the broadcast booth and gives an enthusiastic wave. Meanwhile, back in the ring, McAllister lifts up Style and then goes off the ropes and hits a spinning heel kick. McAllister then follows up with a spinning toe hold. Style managed to turn over thus making McAllister lose his grip. Style is slow to get up and when he does get up he puts McAllister in a headlock. McAllister throws him off the ropes and as Style nears, McAllister attempts a clotheline, but misses. When Style came back, McAllister gets the knee up and sends Style across the ring. McAllister then attempted to pick Style up, but Style rolled up McAllister in a small package.. 1! 2! KICKOUT! As both men spring up, Style tags in Hunter.] JG - "Aladdin Style seems to be thinking. He knows that Chase Hunter would rather watch from the outside. He's forcing Hunter to get into the match." JH - "That's a first." [McAllister looks towards Hunter, being tired and all, tags in Kertins. Kertins and Hunter move towards the center of the ring and lock up. Hunter moves himself out and is able to hit a bodyslam on Kertins. Hunter picks up Kertins and throws him into the ropes. Hunter catches him and hits a spinning sidewalk slam. Kertins starts holding his back as Hunter picks him up. Hunter places Kertins' head between his legs and hits a piledriver.] JG - "This could be lights out for Ponyboy Kertins. Hunter's prime and ready for the pin." JH - "Lights out happened a few minutes ago when Kertins stepped into this match." [Suddenly, Gwaii Edenshaw walks into the arena.] JG - "Oh no." [Hunter leaves Kertins and tags in McAllister. Hunter hops off the apron and waits for Edenshaw to come down the aisle. Edenshaw does and the two start brawling.] JG - "Again!" JH - "Hunter seems to attract men." [McAllister runs off the ropes and as he goes up for a big splash but as he's in the air, he sees Kertins get his knees up, and hits them hard. Kertins rolls onto McAllister and applies an abdominal stretch. McAllister hits an elbow to the head, startling Kertins. Kertins releases the hold, allowing both himself and McAllister to get up. McAllister attempts a dropkick, but Kertins swipes him away. Kertins hops on the mat and applies a camel clutch, but McAllister is able to grab onto the ropes.] JG - "Ponyboy almost scored a surprising victory." JH - "Almost being the key word. Wait, isn't key already a word?" [Nik Nyce walks into the aisle, passing Chase Hunter mocking Gwaii Edenshaw, then stomping on his face.] JG - "Oh no, again." [Nyce passes the two, giving a glance at Hunter, but thinking something different. McAllister sees Nyce walk in and quickly tags out to Style. McAllister dives out of the ring, protecting Victoria Glenn. Nyce continues to move around the ring towards McAllister. McAllister just snaps and dives at Nyce, again the two start rolling around.] JH - "There's more fighting outside of the ring than inside!" JG - "Quite the scene!" [Kertins looks to the outside. He sees Hunter stomping a mud hole on Gwaii Edenshaw and McAllister and Nyce still brawling, to the shagrin of Glenn. He raises his arms in a "What's going on" sense, but is soon grabbed from behind by Aladdin Style into a rollup! 1! 2! 3!] JG - "Aladdin Style got the pin!" JH - "Smart cookie." [Security rushes down to ringside as Style darts out of the scene. Kertins walks away, looking angered with his Greasers. Security breaks up Hunter and Edenshaw first as Hunter backs off, mocking Edenshaw up the aisle. Nyce and McAllister, however, is another story.] JG - "These two may be going at it all night!" JH - "Let them fight!....Hey, isn't the crowd supposed to chant with me?" JG - "It looks like security is getting control. So, we're up for the next match!" JH - "Siberian Express and German Eagles! A nice Russian whipping for the Eagles." JG - "Perhaps, but the Eagles have come on strong as of late." JH - "Their time is passed." JG - "Let's go to the ring for the intros!" -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
