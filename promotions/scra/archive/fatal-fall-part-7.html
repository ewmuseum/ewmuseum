[SCRA] Fatal Fall, Part 7

13 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
An SCRA Production http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html [In R1, Rush and Xarax have Chris Eagle on the turnbuckle. They are trying to push him over and finally do with a big push by Xarax. This push forces Xarax to fall back. He sees Rush standing upright and fires a kick from a kneeling down position. This catches Rush in the chin and sends him flying over the top rope.] JG - "Rush has moved onto ring two! Xarax is proving to be a tough competitor in a ring that doesn't suit his styles." JH - "Look at poor old Rush. He has a huge gash underneath his chin!" JG - "But, he is continuing through the rings! What an animal!" [Dance of the Sugar Plum Fairies comes on the speakers as Bill "Klondike" Baer runs down the aisle, looking to help his partner, Eddy "Flap" Jacks. Xarax remains perched in the corner, trying to conceal his position. Klondike doesn't see Xarax and continues to the corner where Lucifer and Jacks are fighting. Xarax dropkicks Klondike from behind, sending him over the top.] JH - "What a dirty play by Xarax! He just attacked Baer from behind!" JG - "That happens many times in these rumbles. You revert to strange tactics." [Xarax walks over to help Lucifer, but Lucifer grabs Xarax and hoists him up in Black Vice. Xarax screams in pain as Lucifer drops him over the top ropes.] JG - "Xarax is gone in a thrilling way!" JH - "And then there were two." [Lucifer turns around to see Jacks coming at him. The two start throwing punches wildly. In R2, Klondike helps his partner eliminate Joseph Eagle. They then look around the ring for more tag team competition and see Joseph's brother, Chris. The two start after Chris and throw him into a corner. They start pounding on his chest. Klondike sets up Chris in the Klondike Clutch as Tulkas stomps at Chris' face. Chris passes out from the pain, leaving an easy toss for the Guard.] JG - "Both Eagles are in ring three! This is a repeat of what happened in the lethal lottery!" JH - "More down home fighting! I love this stuff." [Joseph Eagle helps his brother Chris into the ring. They talk for a few moments and then Chris steps over the top, wishing his brother luck.] JG - "What comradary! Chris Eagle eliminated himself! I guess he thought that he wasn't in the condition to fight his brother, after suffering the brutal Klondike Clutch, along with some Tulkas stomps." JH - "Come on! Fight damn you!" [In R2, the Guard continues to walk around the ring, looking to double team somebody. They see Gangsta Impasse attacking Green Knight. Remembering the Gangsta attacks on Jacks, the Guard attacks Gangsta. Green Knight slumps to the corner. Nathan Flash walks over and picks up Green Knight, tossing him out of the ring. The Guard starts punching Gangsta, who meagrely fights back. Gangsta slumps into the corner, falling prey to the Praetorian Guard attacks.] JG - "With both halves of the Guard in ring two, the House of Pancakes may have a good chance of winning all three rings." JH - "They already have ring one won. Hey, another tongue twister." JG - "They haven't won ring one yet. Lucifer is still in." JH - "Pretty tough to say, huh Grey?" [Lucifer and Jacks are still throwing wild punches. Jacks is backed up in the corner, though. Lucifer lunges at him with a punch, but Jacks ducks out of the way. Lucifer falls into the exposed turnbuckle. Jacks sees this and grabs Lucifer's head, ramming it into the turnbuckle. Lucifer's head starts squirting blood. The Activists reach under the ring and grab the first aid kid. They put a swab on their sign and push it up to Lucifer's head. This stops the bleeding somewhat. However, Jacks sees the opportunity and rams Lucifer's head hard in the sign.] JG - "Jacks is splitting Lucifer wide open. The Activists can't do anything about it!" JH - "They can and did. They helped Eddy cut Lucifer even more by hoisting the sign up." [Lucifer falls down on the mat, blood squirting out of his head. Jacks jumps on Lucifer's back. He attempts to do this again, but Lucifer spins over and nails Jacks with a punch to the face. Lucifer rolls over and stands besides Jacks, hitting a sidewalk slam. Lucifer goes off the ropes and hits a legdrop.] JG - "Lucifer has really come alive as of late. As before, it looks like the blood has ignited something in him to fight harder." JH - "Because he is a freak. The blood coming out does something to his brain. Ask Rush, he is also one of those freaks who likes the pain." [In R3, Joseph Eagle is dominating Green Knight. The two are having a normal singles match with Joseph controlling. He sets up Green Knight and hits a piledriver. Eagle picks up Green Knight and throws him into the ropes, hitting a gorilla press off the ropes and tossing Green Knight to the outside.] JG - "What power by Joseph Eagle! He hoisted Green Knight high in the sky and left him to dry." JH - "Why do you try and eat apple pie with that ugly tie?" JG - "Very funny, Jack." JH - "This is the night of poetry, isn't it?" JG - "It very well could be." [In R2, the Guard has Gangsta hanging on the turnbuckle. He is being lifted high over the ring, but not over the top ropes. Gangsta falls off the turnbuckle, landing on the apron. He jumps over the top ropes, hitting Klondike with a dropkick. This floors Klondike and leaves Tulkas open for a big clothesline, sending him over the top.] JG - "With so many men in ring two, one is getting eliminated almost every time we look!" JH - "Maybe we should look more often, then we could get this thing over with." [In R1, Jacks is fighting back against Lucifer. He pokes Lucifer in the eye and goes off the ropes, hitting a Vader-like punch. Lucifer hits the mat and Jacks jumps on top of him, applying an armbar.] JG - "That is the luxury they have in ring one, only two people. It is more like a singles matchup than an all out brawl." [In R2, Rush has Zamza in the corner. He runs into the corner with a baseball slide, but Zamza moves out of the way. Rush slides underneat the ropes.] JH - "Sucker! What a moron that Rush is!" JG - "He isn't eliminated! It is an over the top battle royal and as you saw, Rush went underneat!" [Zamza gets up, feeling smart, but Rush slides back in the ring. He grabs Zamza from behind, hitting a back suplex which sends Zamza over the top rope, crashing through a table.] JG - "Oh my! Zamza has to be out cold!" JH - "Or just cold. Are you feeling chilly?" JG - "A little bit." [In R2, Silverblade and Baldwin are still fighting. Baldwin nails Silverblade with a hard right, sending Silverblade staggering into the corner. Baldwin runs in, but misses as Silverblade ducked out. Silverblade rams Baldwin with a shoulderblock. He lifts Baldwin onto the turnbuckle, having Baldwin perched atop. Nathan Flash runs over and dropkicks Baldwin, sending him flying outside. Silverblade turns around and sees a grinning Flash. Flash kips up and dropkicks Silverblade over the top as well.] JG - "Nathan Flash has severely changed! The old Nathan Flash never would have gotten himself involved in another battle nor would he turn his back on somebody." JH - "Hanging around with gayboy Destiny pushed him over the edge." JG - "I don't think that is true." JH - "Come on! Destiny was losing every night. Flash had to get rid of him. Look now! Flash is going after Rush!" [In R2, Flash and Rush collide in the center of the ring with a pair of dropkicks. The two get up and circle each other, trying to grab at an arm. This happens for a while until Gangsta Impasse, who is fighting Klondike in the corner, clips Rush's knee. Flash dives on Rush and starts throwing punches. Gangsta grabs Flash by the back of the neck, picking him up and throwing him off Rush. Gangsta picks up Rush and hits a bodyslam. Flash gets back up and climbs to the top, signalling to Gangsta to hold Rush. Flash takes flight, but instead of hitting Rush, nails Gangsta with a legdrop.] JG - "Again, the deviousness shows! Flash is a changed man!" JH - "But for the better. If he would only go after Rush now, instead of beating on Gangsta, he will be over with me." [Flash pushes Gangsta out of the way and grabs Rush. An excited shreik is heard in the background. Flash pushes Rush into the corner. He measures Rush and hits a standing hurricarana, sending Rush in the center of the ring. Klondike sees the open prey and runs in, hitting a legdrop. Gangsta lunges at Klondike and the two roll on the ground, exchanging punches. Flash picks up Rush and whips him into the ropes. He hits a back body drop, eliminating Rush.] JH - "Way to go Nate! I always knew this guy was special." JG - "Please Jack. Until about a week ago, you were making comments about what Flash and Destiny did during their time off." JH - "Don't you think the sacred bond between two people who love each other is special? I still thought Flash was special, just in a different way.] [In R1, Jacks had switched his moves into an STF. Lucifer was pretty much out, so Jacks let go of the big man. He picked up Lucifer and set him up for a bodyslam when Lucifer slid out of Jacks arms. Lucifer fell to the ground. Jacks looks bewildered and simply walks over to Lucifer, bending over to pick him up again. Lucifer wakes up, though and grabs Jacks arms. Lucifer executes a perfect monkey flip, sending Jacks sailing high over the top ropes.] JG - "I guess pigs can fly!" JH - "Not funny, Grey. Jacks is just a little large, that's all." [Lucifer gets up, looking unharmed and raises his arms in victory. The Activists toss their signs in the ring and join their fellow stable member. The three Pit of Damnation members celebrate in the now almost empty first ring. The three look together, but when Kil Jaeden runs down, he pushes the two Activists out of the way to get to his master. He lifts Lucifer up in victory. The Activists try and place a sign in Lucifer's hand, but Lucifer does not take it. Lucifer and Kil Jaeden exit the ring with the Activists lagging behind.] JG - "Lucifer will get his second shot at Lifetaker and the World Title at Rasslefest, providing Lifetaker still has the belt. If not, Lucifer will face whoever the champ is." JH - "It won't be Lifetaker. He will be losing the belt soon, hopefully to somebody like Poink or Eddy." JG - "Nobody knows what will happen, but we still have lots of action going on! Look at Centris and Xarax in ring two!" [Xarax was perched on the top ropes, ready to fly down at Centris. However, Centris got up and climbed to the second turnbuckle. Xarax walked along the ropes to escape Centris, showing exceptional balance.] JG - "He must be using some magical power to stay like that!" JH - "And that magical power is called Centris. He is just using Centris for balance." [Xarax leaps off the ropes and catches Centris with a headscissors takedown. Xarax again climbs to the turnbuckle and hits a legdrop from the top. Flash throws Xarax out of the way and nails a Power Plunge on Centris.] JG - "The Power Plunge! That's Destiny's move!" JH - "Of course, Flash taught it to Destiny. Flash has been using that move for ages." [Flash picks up Centris and easily throws him over the top. He goes back towards Xarax and starts to punch him with closed fists. The two House of Pancake members in R2 are working on Gangsta in the corner. In R3, the other HoP member, Tulkas "The Troll" Naugrim, has his hands full with Joseph Eagle who is nailing Tulkas with high flying moves in a restricted area. He picks up Tulkas and hits a hurricarana, sending Tulkas flying over the top ropes.] JG - "What height on that hurricarana! Joseph Eagle really got up and whipped Tulkas out!" JH - "I have to give credit on that one, it was a big time move." [Rush and Zamza are once again battling in the corner. Zamza has Rush in a modified version of an armbar, using the ropes to help with leverage. Rush uses his free hand to scratch at Zamza.] JH - "What a girl! He is scratching." JG - "That's all he can do. But, what's this! The scratching was a distraction. Mule kick by Rush!" [Zamza keels over and Rush, in lightning like quickness, nails Zamza with a European uppercut.] JG - "What power by Rush! He just knocked Zamza all the way to Kingdom come!" [In R2, Jacks and Baer are finally pushing Gangsta over the top. With a big thrust, they push him over the top. On the other side of the ring, Flash nails Xarax with a dropkick, sending Xarax over the top. Flash continues to the outside, sliding under the ring.] JG - "What is Flash doing?" JH - "Although I don't condone making a fool out of the House of Pancakes, he is making a fool out of them! Jacks and Baer think they are the only two left!" [Baer raises his hand in defeat and graciously steps out of the ring, eliminating himself. Jacks raises his arms in victory, only to have Flash rush in and dropkick him from behind. Jacks goes flying over the top ropes, landing on top of Baer.] JH - "Flash wins with an outstanding plan!" JG - "I don't like this new Flash. Just three days ago, we saw him in action with Destiny, what gives?" JH - "He wanted to get a tune up against two people with average competition, the German Eagles. That allowed him to be ready for this event." [Flash celebrates by himself in the ring amidst a chorus of boos. He exits the ring fast, encountering a Johnny Destiny on crutches.] JG - "What is Destiny doing? He is going to injure himself more!" JH - "Get him Nate!" JG - "No! This is horrible! Flash is attacking an injured man! This could end Destiny's career." JH - "Good, then he can manage Flash and see the pain and suffering of not being able to compete." [Flash kicks the crutch out from underneat Destiny, bending Destiny to one side. Flash hits Destiny with an axehandle to the back which floors Destiny. Flash picks up one of the crutches and breaks it over Destiny's right leg.] JG - "The horror!" JH - "Just keep it coming, Nate. End that punks career!" [Flash stops the attack when he sees security running down the aisle. Flash breaks through them all, running backstage. Destiny is carried of in a stretcher. The action continued, though, in R3. The two House of Pancakes members joined in the action, targeting Centris. They double teamed Centris and threw Xarax aside. Xarax moved along to attack Rush. However, Rush was more concentrated on Joseph Eagle. Eagle and Xarax double team Rush in the corner. The pick up Rush and whip him into the turnbuckle, coming back with a double elbow. Both men pick up Rush and toss him to the outside.] JH - "See ya Rush! Eliminated by two punks like Joseph Eagle and Xarax, can't get any worse than that. Unless you are the Crocodile Hunters." JG - "Jack, what about Maximus Ordicus?" JH - "Yeah, that was pretty bad, but I actually like him. The Crocodile Hunters just get on my nerves." [Gangsta Impasse comes to the aid of Centris, nailing Klondike with a big right hand. He tosses Klondike off and attacks Jacks, bodyslamming the big man into the center of the ring. He picks up Centris and bodyslams him on top of Jacks. Then, as Klondike marched over, Gangsta picked him up and bodyslammed him on top of the other two.] JH - "That's making me hungry. A Centris sandwich with HoP bread." JG - "Very funny." JH - "No, really. I am getting hungry. Could we get some cookies out here?" JG - "Drink your water." JH - "Like water helps. Why do you think it is free? Because it doesn't do anything for you." JG - "That isn't even worth answering." [Xarax and Joseph Eagle seem to have formed a mini alliance and both marched over to attack Silverblade. This was welcomed by Baldwin who was getting stomped on in the corner. Xarax and Eagle spun Silverblade around, then, Eagle hit a spinning heel kick, Xarax a leg sweep.] JH - "Total Elimina..." JG - "What a move by those two! Where did they think of that one?" [Baldwin gets up and thanks the two who saved him. He jumps on Silverblade, applying an ankle pick. Xarax gets on the arms, putting on the armbar. Eagle, feeling left out, starts stomping away at Silverblade's face. The three stop the insane beatings and pick up Silverblade. They attempt to throw Silverblade out, but Silverblade keeps hold of Eagle's arm. This allows him to hand over the ropes. Baldwin dropkicks Eagle, sending both Joseph Eagle and Silverblade crashing to the outside.] JG - "Xarax doesn't look to happy with that one. His short-lived ally was just eliminated by somebody who they helped." JH - "Live with it buddy! This is a rumble with a title shot on the line, anything can happen." [Xarax drags Baldwin in the center of the ring. He puts on a kita jime armbar, leaving Baldwin yelping in pain. Baldwin gives up to Xarax, saying he would eliminate himself. Baldwin gets up and goes to the ropes, but turns around and runs at Xarax. Xarax bends over and hits a back body drop, sending Baldwin to the outside.] JH - "That's what you get when you can't cheat good." JG - "He wasn't cheating." JH - "Well, he wasn't honourable." JG - "Like you said before, there is an SCRA title shot on the line and people do crazy things to get it." [Xarax walks over to help attack Centris. Baer does not seem pleased and attacks Xarax instead. Centris crawls into the opposite corner, letting them sort their differences out. Xarax and Baer attack each other with hard blows, Baer easily overpowering Xarax. Baer knocks Xarax over. He picks Xarax back up and whips him into a turnbuckle. Gangsta in the other corner, whips Jacks into the same corner, sending Jacks crashing into Xarax. Gangsta runs in and hits a butt stomp on both men.] JG - "Strange, but effective. Klondike is all alone, now, with three men sitting in the corner, ready for an attack." JH - "He wouldn't go after Eddy. Those two are like brothers." [Jacks falls off, leaving Xarax slouched unconscious in the corner. Klondike runs in and tries to toss him out, but Gangsta throws Klondike away with a back suplex. He picks up Xarax and tosses him out.] JG - "Xarax eliminated and once again, Gangsta crosses paths with the House of Pancakes." JH - "So what. I don't think the House of Pancakes top priority is Gangsta Impasse." JG - "Right now it is. They are double teaming him, again!" [Klondike and Jacks have Gangsta in the corner. Poink comes out and walks down to ringside.] JG - "What does he want?" JH - "Probably wants to get some free attacks on Gangsta." JG - "Everything has its price. I don't think the president will like Poink coming down. He has no business being here." JH - "Who cares. It doesn't do anything." [Poink grabs at Gangsta's throat. He starts choking him, then chopping at the throat. The House of Pancakes move back and allow Poink to beat on Gangsta. The two Pancakers pick up Gangsta. Klondike throws him into Jacks who hits a powerslam. Jacks picks up Gangsta and throws him into Klondike who back body dropped Gangsta to the outside. Poink ran over and grabbed Gangsta, carrying him back to the locker room, but slapping him on the way back.] JH - "More beatings for Gangsta! Poink is going to hurt him so bad, they won't show it on television." JG - "I think Poink wants to use the more plentiful amount of objects backstage." JH - "Either way, Poink gets to beat the snot out of Gangsta." [The two House of Pancakes members look around the ringside area, making sure that they would be the only two left. The see Centris crouched in the corner. Centris lunges up and clotheslines both men.] JG - "Big move by Centris! You have to say the odds are easily stacked against Centris with a two on one situation." [Phantom, along with Christine in a short dress, come walking to ringside.] JG - "What are they doing here!" JH - "Who knows, but I am glad they are here! Look at that dress!" [Christine walks around the ring, catching the attention of Klondike. Klondike slides over to the side Christine is on and starts staring at Christine who is flirting with him. Jacks sees Phantom and springs to his feet, yelling at the masked man.] JG - "Phantom and Christine have caused a distraction! But, why?" [Centris dropkicks Klondike in the back, sending him over the top ropes. Christine scatters to the other side of the ring, next to Phantom. Phantom sees her running over and sprays something in Jacks' eyes.] JG - "What was that? Phantom just nailed Jacks with some sort of dust, similar to what happened to him on Monday." [Jacks stumbles back, landing near Centris who hits a back suplex, sending Jacks over the top ropes, on top of Klondike. Phantom and Christine flee the scene.] JG - "Centris wins! He is getting out of there fast, though!" JH - "No kidding. House of Pancakes were made to look stupid. They are going to want at least some measure of revenge." JG - "But when?" JH - "It will happen, oh yes, it will happen." JG - "Folks, we have had a big night, enough to make me tired. We will have a full recap on Probing SCRA with a look at the Scraps to follow this huge card! Good night!"
