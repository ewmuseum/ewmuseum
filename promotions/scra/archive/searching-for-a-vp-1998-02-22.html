[SCRA] Searching for a VP

22 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As one of our handlers so eloquently put it, "He (Alex G.) has a rough gem on his hands and it's a matter of time before the e-wrestling world sees a polished diamond." Would you like to be a part of making that diamond? The Simply Canadian Rasslin' Alliance opened its doors back in September and slowly continues to pick up steam into the new year. We've had great wrestlers grace our rings and continue to have the likes of J.W. Hardin, Ken Curtis and Derek Mota Nik Nyce take their lumps. But, in order to continue to rise in quality, we need some extra help in the form of a vice presidential position. With this help, we may be able to push SCRA into the next level. Basic functions of this position would be: ** Match writing Basically, two per week for inclusion on our big Monday show, the Scraps. These are "IIWF style" matches, each about 12 kb or 200 lines long. You have about 5 days to complete both matches for inclusion each Monday. ** Angles/booking Plan out who does what and why, including surprises, twists and heat builders. ** Miscellaneous You may be required to write things such as news captions for the webpage or an article for our e-zine every now and then. These will most likely be very rare, though. It's not much, but we need some help with matches and booking. If you are interested into taking this role in an established, quality fed, please email me, Alex G., at [detail withheld] and we can talk. Thanks, Alex G. -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
