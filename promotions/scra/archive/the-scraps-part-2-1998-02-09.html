[SCRA] The Scraps [2/5] (9 February 1998)

9 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| North American Tournament Match 2 ) | | \ | | "Hot Stuff" Mike Justice vs. "Iconoclast" Sean Watts / |___ | | | | Writer: Trevor G. (_________________________________________________________________________ EJ - "Coming to the ring, hailing from Chicago, Illinois and weighing in at 270 lbs, he is "Hot Stuff" Mike Juuuuustice!" [Mike Justice dances his way out from behind the curtain, golden jewelry glittering in the spotlight. He twirls, prances and struts his way towards the ring, stopping to flirt with the pretty girls. The men he totally ignores. Once he's in the ring, he twirls around and around, as the pyrotechnics explode around. The pyro stops, but Justice ignores Jaspard and starts doing a strip tease for the fans. Jaspard looks over at Justice, motions towards the fans, then tears his shirt off!] JH - "My eyes!" [Jaspard and Justice flex for each other.] JG - "It's no contest with this one. Ed's got the win." JH - "Nice try, Grey." EJ - "His opponent, from Seatle, Washington and tips the scales at 238 lbs, "Iconoclast" Sean Waaaaatts!" [Iggy Pop's Search and Destroy blares through the arena. Watts steps out from behind the curtain with a determined look on his face. He moves towards the ring, looking at Justice's antics with disgust. He climbs up to the ring, still glaring. Justice continues his dancing, smirking at Sean Watts.] DING! DING! DING! [Watts shoots for Justice's legs and pulls them off the mat. Justice crashes down on his back, his smirk gone. Watts hooks his legs around Justice's, forces his left forearm over Justice's throat and starts pounding with his right fist on Justice's face. The referee starts counting on Watts, but Watts won't break. Rather than disqualify Watts in the first 10 seconds of the match, the ref hauls Watts off his opponent. Justice rolls out of the ring, his face in his hands, while Watts argues with the ref.] JG - "Arguing with the ref isn't going to get you anywhere." JH - "Especially with Stubborn Sam in there." JG - "The ref's name is Sam?" JH - "I don't know. What are our referee's names?" [The ref returns Watts back to his corner, allowing Justice to come back into the ring. Justice is furious and starts pointing and hollering at Watts. This time, it is Watts that is smirking. Justice walks to the middle of the ring and calls Watts out to join him. The two lock up. There is a brief struggle, then Justice gives a mighty shove and Watts tumbles back into the corner. Watts rubs the back of his head while Justice poses in the middle of the ring. Justice calls Watts back out to try again.] JH - "Is this gonna be a Mr. Universe contest or a wrestling match?" JG - "I don't think Watts has the body to enter in the contest, let alone the outfit." [Watts comes out and the two lock up one more time. This time Watts plants his left leg behind the back of Justice's right knee. Justice gives a powerful push, but Watts has him off balance and this time Justice hits the ground. Watts follows him down and covers. 1! KICKOUT!] JG - "Watts almost had a fluky cover there." JH - "Fluky, but it almost worked." [Watts rolls off and grabs Justice's wrist as Justice stands up. Justice goes down to his knees again as Watts wrenches hard on a top wristlock. Justice rolls foward on the mat, then backwards, then spins. Justice kips up and Watts goes crashing down, caught in a straight armbar. Watts winces in pain, then does a head-stand flip, landing on his feet. A quick spin and Justice is sent down to the mat. Watts backs off, looking at his showboating opponent with respect for the first time. Justice swears, upset about being upstaged.] JH - "The wrestlers should wear microphones. Then, we can hear what they really say under their breath." JG - "Uh, I don't think we want to do that." JH - "Why not?" JG - "You were a former wrestler, you know." [The two combatants circle each other again. For the third time they lock up. Watts gets the advantage first and pulls Justice over with a headlock takedown. The ref drops to make a count, but Justice gets his shoulders up before the ref gets in position. Justice grabs Watt's tights and pulls Watts over. The ref makes the count... 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JH - "Hehe, grabbing the shorts. What a fighter." JG - "Grabbing the tights is a horrible way to get the pin." JH - "But it works, and a bit kinky. Obviously, Justice knows how to do it well." [Watts is back on top with the side headlock. Watts gets to his feet and drags Justice up with him, still maintaining the headlock. Justice hooks Watt's waist and blasts him with a side suplex, forcing Watts to release the hold. Watts clutches the back of his head and writhes on the mat. Justice gets up and starts flexing and blowing kisses to the girls in the crowd. Despite his antics, he keeps a close eye on Watts. The stunned Watts struggles to his feet but is met by a flying foremarm by Justice.] JH - "He may show off, but he watches opponents. Smart guy this Hot Stuff." JG - "You sure are praising him a lot. Are you sure I shouldn't make the assumption that you are gay?" JH - "Don't assume. It makes an ass out of u and me. Well, just you. Nobody can make an ass out of me." [Justice hauls Watts up in the air with an extended verticle suplex. The crowd pops as Justice maintains the peak for over five seconds, then sends Watts crashing down. Justice follows it up with a kneedrop on the side of Watt's head, then locks in the figure four.] JG - "Justice has the figure four hooked on! Watts is screaming in pain and I don't know how much longer he can hold on." JH - "What's he holding onto right now?" [Watts screams in pain, but refuses to submit. He neglects to keep is shoulders off the mat, and the ref starts to make the count... 1! 2! Shoulders up! The near fall seems to put fire into Watts and he starts to drag himself to the ring ropes. Finally he makes it, but he's spent nearly a minute in the figure four. Justice breaks the hold and steps back, letting Watts back to his feet. Watts cannot put weight on his left leg and has to use the ring ropes to help him stand up. Gallantly, he limps out to the center of the ring to meet his oppenent.] JG - "Sean Watts may be a slacker, but he is showing that he works in the ring. He's not going to quit." [Justice starts unloading with forearm smashes, battering Watts. Watts ducks under and goes behind Justice, cinching a waistlock, then leveling his larger oppoent with a released german suplex. Justice is down and dazed, and Watts starts hammering on his own knee in anger. Watts limps back to his feet, picks up Justice in a front face lock and nails a DDT. Immediately he picks him back up and hits a second DDT. Watts covers. 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Watts almost had him! Despite the punishment on his leg, he's fighting back against Mike Justice." [Pause.] JG - "No witty comments?" JH - "Can't think of anything." [Watts swears at the referee and then picks up Justice for a third DDT. Justice counters it with a backdrop, turns in time to catch the rising Watts in a front facelock and nails "The Iconoclast" with a DDT of his own. Justice covers. 1! 2! KICKOUT! Justice stands the dazed Watts up in the middle of the ring and rebounds off the ropes. Watts goes down from a wicked flying clothesline from the 270 lb Mike Justice. Justice grabs Watts' wounded legs and hooks in the Texas Cloverleaf. The ref asks the screaming Watts if he wants to call it a night, but Watts refuses to submit. Instead, he starts a slow, painful crawl to the ring ropes. He makes it, forcing the break.] JG - "Watts made it! He's fighting the pain in his legs and might be able to pull this one out." JH - "If he doesn't give up before. I hear this Watts is a loser, a slacker, a quitter." [Justice irish whips Watts into the ropes and nails a brutal powerslam in the middle. He covers... 1! 2! KICKOUT! Justice swears.] JG - "Good thing we don't have mics on the wrestlers. The censors would be all over our ass for this match." JH - "Just dump it all on Grey. Let him deal with it." [He picks up Watts, irish whips him into the ropes and then hooks the sleeper. Both men are in the middle of the ring and there's no way the weakened Watts can make it to the ropes this time. Watts goes down. The ref comes over and raises Watts' arm. It falls. He raises it again. Again it falls. He raises it a third time. Watts grabs a handfull of Justice's hair to keep the hand from dropping a third time.] JH - "The lucious locks of hair! They're getting destroyed!" [Still in the sleeper, Watts struggles back to his feet. Justice redoubles his pressure, and yells at the ref for not stopping the match. The ref argues, saying that Watts didn't give up, and Watts uses the opportunity to sneak in a hard shot to the groin of Justice. The sleeper is released and both combatants drop to the mat.] JG - "Watts is tired from taking the attack, Justice is tired from giving the attack." JH - "And a hard shot in the groin doesn't help out your cause when trying to get back up." [Watts is up first, walks backwards to the corner behind Justice. Justice sits up, bawling and holding his crotch. Watts runs and hit a Hennig neck snap on Justice. Watts, still limping from the figure four, climbs to his feet. He picks up Justice and nails a beautiful Russian Leg Sweep. He picks him up again and hits a backbreaker. He picks him up a third time and hits a reverse Atomic Drop. Rebounding off the ropes, he follows it all up with a brutal clothesline. Justice is down and Watts goes for the cover. 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Not going to pin after an atomic drop." JH - "But, you might be able to raise the guy's voice an octave or two." [Watts drops a knee on the side of Justice's head. Justice hollars in pain, but Watts is hurt, too. That was the knee that was damaged in the figure four and Watts is rolling around the ring clutching it. Justice uses the ring ropes to climb to his feet, then slowly climbs to the top turnbuckle. A limping Watts catches him before Justice can leap off, and Justice is tossed into the middle of the ring. Watts climbs backwards to the second rope and leaps off with a flying legdrop. He connects hard and goes for the cover... 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Watts is destroying his knee even more by going for these flying moves." JH - "I don't think he cares. He doesn't care about winning, why care about his body?" [Watts pulls Justice to his feet, but Justice pokes his smaller opponent in the eye. The ref is about to admonish "Hot Stuff", but Justice doesn't give him the chance. Instead he pulls Watts down in a small package and the ref has to make the count... 1! 2! REVERSAL! Watts turns it over, and now Justice's shoulders are down! 1! 2! REVERSAL! Justice is on top again, with Watt's shoulders down! 1! 2! 3! NO, WATTS IS IN THE ROPES! The two wrestlers were close enough to the ropes now, that Watts could reach out and grab the bottom one, breaking the count. The ref waves off the bell.] JG - "Holy cow! What close calls there!" JH - "Both these guys are beating the crap out of each other, then they roll around a bit and get closer to a pin than in the rest of the match. Weird stuff." [Both wrestlers are exhausted and are slow to climb to their feet. Justice eye gouges Watts again, and ignores the ref's complaints. He drags Watts to the middle of the ring and kicks him in the wounded leg. Watts goes down to one knee, but gets up quickly again. Justice irish whips Watts into the ropes, but Watts counters and it is "Hot Stuff" who is sent running. On the return, Watts kicks Justice in the stomach, hooks the fisherman suplex position, and nails Justice with the Seatleplex! The ref goes down to cover. 1! 2! WATTS' DAMAGED LEG GIVES OUT!] JG - "He couldn't keep the bridge! The damaged leg Justice worked on finally paid off." JH - "And, Watts still has to get back up. Who knows how much the knee is hurt if it gave way." [Justice was down, and couldn't have kicked out, but Watts couldn't maintain the bridge! Watts hollars in frustration and starts pounding on his wounded leg! The dazed Justice climbs slowly, wobbly to his feet and hauls up Watts after him. He hooks the suplex position, but in mid move Watts slips behind. Justice turns around but is met by another Seatleplex! 1! 2! 3!] JG - "He got him! He kept the leg up!" JH - "Unbelievable!" JG - "Sean Watts barely, and I mean barely, moves on. What a surprise there!" JH - "I don't know if he will be able to move on. That knee was pretty damn banged up. And, he'll have to face Mota next round. I don't know if he can keep Mota down with a bum knee." JG - "But, it provides a good matchup. Watts showed that he can get back up. Mota has shown that many, many times. Who will finally be able to keep their opponent down?" JH - "Edenshaw." JG - "Huh?" JH - "The producers kept whispering in my headset to move along. Edenshaw will keep McAllister down in the next matchup." JG - "Oh, gotcha. Let's go to the ring for the intros." ___________________________________________________________________________ (__ | |__/ |__| North American Tournament Match 3 ) | | \ | | Bobby McAllister vs. "Haida Warrior" Gwaii Edenshaw / |___ | | | | Writer: Jon B. (_________________________________________________________________________ EJ - "This match is scheduled for one fall and a twenty minute time limit. Coming down first weighing in at 280 lbs. from Queen Charlotte Islands,B.C. led by the "Haida Warrior" Gwaii Edenshaw... here's Sir John Caboto!!" JG - "As usual Eddie screws up the annoucements." JH - "Its what your brother pays him for Grey, do you think someone could be that stupid?" JG - "Have you met the Jaspard Clan?" [Over the sound system an eagle calls out several times followed by the sound of wind blowing through trees, the haunting sound of distant chanting and drum beating fades in slowly until it is at a full roar. Gwaii Edenshaw is led out by Caboto who is dressed in a pinstriped suit with an absurd looking silk neckerchief and a cane. Caboto enters the ring at a hurried pace, much farther ahead of the methodical Edenshaw, grabs the microphone from Jaspard, and starts ranting on about the glory of human savagery and athleticism in its most natural and noble form. Finally Jaspard grabs the microphone back from Caboto and continues the intros.] JH - "As much as I like that mans outfit his rantings must stop!" EJ - "And his opponent weighing in at 224 lbs, from New York, NY, led by Bobby McAllister here is the beautiful, Victoria Glenn!!" JH - "Screwed it up again." [The Marine Hymn blares over the sound system as McAllister walks down to the ring preceded by Victoria Glenn. They slap hands with some fans then Victoria climbs the steps into the ring. McAllister jumps onto the apron and holds the ropes open for Victoria. He follows her into the ring. McAllister goes to the corner to pose when Edenshaw comes up behind and elbows McAllister in the back.] DING! DING! DING! [Edenshaw turns Bobby around and starts hitting chops, finally he picks up McAllister and hits a power bomb. He goes for the cover... 1! KICKOUT! by Bobby. Gwaii and Bobby get up and start circling each other, they start to lock up but Gwaii rackes Bobby in the eyes. He throws Bobby against the ropes and lowers his head, Bobby sees this and executes a DDT.] JG - "Ouch! That DDT was well done!" JH - "Compared to DDT's done by other people?" [Bobby runs against the ropes and hits an elbowdrop. He grabs Gwaii's legs executing a figure-four leglock. Gwaii gets momentum to turn it over. Bobby lets go and again the two get up. They go to lock up again, Gwaii knees Bobby in the stomach, grabs his head, and executes a snap suplex.] JH - "Stupid marine! He fell for the same trick twice! " [Gwaii hits a a Big Leg Drop, then runs against the rope coming back with another leg drop but Bobby moves out of the way. Bobby picks him up and hits a powerbomb. He goes for the pin... 1! 2! KICKOUT!] JG - "Close kickout by Gwaii." [Bobby starts punching Gwaii, he backs Gwaii into the corner finally hitting a spin kick sending Gwaii outside. Bobby climbs to the top rope and jumps out at Gwaii hitting an elbowdrop. He throws Gwaii against the railing, then he climbs back into the ring breaking the count. Gwaii walks back into the ring but Bobby is waiting for him hitting a shoulder block.] JG - "Bobby is starting to dominate this match. What's wrong Jack, you haven't been making your snappy comments through this match." JH - "Sorry, Grey, I'm just a little tuckered out. Having to be color commentator for three straight nights gets to me after awhile." [Bobby picks up Gwaii and executes a sleeper. Gwaii tries to get up but he can't. After a half a minute Bobby lets go. Gwaii falls down and Bobby motions for his finisher. As he prepares for it he sees Nick Nyce come down to the ringside. Bobby starts yelling at him as he starts talking to Victoria.] JH - "Get her Nycley...hehehe" JG - "That was bad Jack." JH - "I know." [Gwaii comes up behind Bobby and turning him around hits a belly to belly suplex. He starts stomping on Bobby. He runs against the ropes and comes back with a elbowdrop. Hitting it he picks up McAllister executing a powerbomb. Picking up Bobby again he hits another powerbomb. Gwaii sees Mike Justice coming down to ringside and starts to climb out. Caboto yells at him to stay in the ring. He turns around and starts to run, he jumps trying to hit a splash on Bobby. Bobby rolls out of the away. Bobby sees Nick Nyce and Mike Justice outside of the ring. He runs against the opposite rope and coming back jumps over the ropes right onto Nyce and Justice.] JG - "Nice air time by Bobby!" JH - "Stupid moron, he's gonna get Nyce and Justice attacking him now." [Gwaii gets up and rolls under the ropes and starts attacking Bobby. Justice and Nyce get up and start yelling at each other. They start trading punches. Gwaii whips Bobby against the railing but Bobby reverses it. He runs at Gwaii hitting a shoulder block sending Gwaii over the railing into the crowd.] JH - "Two fights at once! This is better than sex!" JG - "Don't say that on primetime Jack!" [Nyce and Justice finally get broken up by security and are escorted back to the locker room. Bobby trys to follow them but gets held back by Gregory.] JG - "Glad Gregory is back to his old self." [Victoria yells at Bobby to get back into the ring. He dives into the ring to break the count then looks over to Gwaii who is slowly getting up on the other side of the railing. Caboto is yelling at him to get up. The ref counts... 6! 7! 8! [Gwaii jumps over the railing. Bobby sits down in the middle of the ring.] 9! 10! [Gwaii was a few inches from jumping onto the mat when the ref calls for the bell. Victoria enters the ring celebrating with Bobby. Caboto gets in the ref's face and starts yelling at him. Bobby and Victoria leave the ring. Exhausted Caboto curses at the ref then with Gwaii they walk back to the dressing room.] JG - "Justice and Nyce almost cost McAllister the win!" JH - "Too bad they didn't. McAllister can't do anything right, even lose." JG - "I think Justice was mad at his previous loss. He had to take it out on somebody, why not go after McAllister and his wife?" JH - "He wasn't mad, he just likes to fight." JG - "Bah. What about Nyce?" JH - "He likes Glenn." JG - "Well, Bobby McAllister now goes onto face the winner of this next matchup, Aladdin Style and Sgt. Pepper." JH - "That's a real tough one to call." JG - "Don't be sarcastic." JH - "Okay, it won't be a tough one to call." JG - "Let's go to Ed Jaspard for the intros." -- ***** Alex G. - President of SCRA Email: [detail withheld] Homepage: http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html *****
