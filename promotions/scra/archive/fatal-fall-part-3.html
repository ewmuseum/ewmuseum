[SCRA] Fatal Fall, Part 3

13 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
An SCRA Production http://www.bigwave.ca/~aljon/scra.html ____________________________________________________________________________ |_____ Match 3 | |___ Rock Bottom Robbie vs. Silver Stone | Writer: RW |_________________________________________________________________________ TF - "This matchup is scheduled for one fall with no time limit. It is for the North American championship! Coming down the aisle first, the champion, weighing in at 245 lbs., and hailing from Intercourse, Pennsylvania, here comes the champion, Rock Bottom Robbie!" [Rock Bottom Robbie comes through the curtain as "Little Things" blares through the amps. He takes his time as he slaps the hands of the fans as he goes along. When he gets to the ring, he hops onto the top rope and rips apart his tank top while fire works blare off in the back ground.] TF - "And his opponent, hailing from Carson City, Nevada and weighing in at 269 lbs., he is accompanied by Electra, here comes Silver Stone!" [Silver Stone enters non-shilantly, wearing a leather jacket and glasses, with Electra by his side. He fakes a high five to a fan and snickers at the poor fan. He takes off his jacket and glasses and gives them to Electra.] JG - "Both men look to be ready for battle. This matchup could go either way as both men are hungry for the title and ending this long feud." [Silver Stone runs into the ring and he and Rock Bottom Robbie begin to exchange blows. Robbie gets the upperhand.] JG - "Using his brawling skills, Robbie is overpunching Silver Stone. If this keeps up, Robbie will have the match won." JH - "But it won't. Unlike some of our other champions, examples could be Lifetaker and Sgt. Pepper, Silver Stone is a smart champion." JG - "He isn't the champion, though." JH - "Exactly." JG - "What? Oh, nevermind." [Robbie clobbers Silver Stone with repetitive axhandles over the back. Robbie goes for a suplex and begins to stomp on Silver Stone. Robbie throws Stone into the ropes and nails a clothesline. Silver Stone gets right up and superkicks Robbie.] JH - "See that? That's a fighting champion. He gets knocked down, but gets right back up and knocks his opponent down even harder." JG - "The superkick was a devestating move. Robbie looks to be out cold." [Silver Stone begins to flex his muscles and puts one finger on Robbie to pin him. As the referee counts to 2, Robbie bites Silver Stone's finger. The fans cheer.] JG - "Robbie using some strange techniques, much to the delight of the crowd!" JH - "That's disgusting! What kind of man is Robbie? Eating another person's fingers, appalling." JG - "He had to do it. Silver Stone egged him on, so Robbie reacted." JH - "Isn't there some sort of rule about biting? I believe the rule is don't. Why is Robbie getting away with that? Silver Stone will have gigantic bite marks on his finger. Those can be very disturbing!" [Robbie throws Silver Stone to the ropes and gets off a powerslam. Robbie then comes off the middle turnbuckle for a fist-drop.] JG - "Uncharacteristic aerial move by Robbie. He is pulling all the stops in making sure he holds onto his belt." JH - "It is Silver Stone's belt! He should be the champion." [Robbie gorilla presses Silver Stone, but Silver Stone wiggles out of it and applies a front-face lock and then executes a DDT on Robbie. Silver Stone celebrates. Silver Stone throws Robbie to the ropes, goes for a clothesline but Robbie ducks. Robbie comes back but Silver Stone meets Robbie with a sling shot drop on the top rope.] JG - "Silver Stone fighting back. He always seems to squeek out of those kind of situations." JH - "That's what makes him the champion." JG - "They aren't honourable ways to get out which are worthy of a champion." JH - "They don't have to be honourable, they just have to work." JG - "But kids look up to the champions. When they see Silver Stone pulling stuff like this...wait a minute! Silver Stone isn't the champion!" JH - "No kidding, Grey. That's what I have been trying to get into your thick noggin all along. He will be champion after this match ends." [Silver Stone comes off the ropes for a cross body. Zamza walks toward the ring with a notebook in his hand.] JG - "What does he want?" JH - "Take notes. Silver Stone caused Zamza a good shot at the title on Monday. Zamza probably thinks they will meet again. He has to be ready." JG - "Hopefully, we won't see another brawl like on Monday." JH - "Grey, the main event of this show is a royal rumble. How can we not see a brawl?" JG - "I meant between these two. This has to be the last match between Robbie and Silver Stone, no more screwy endings." [Silver Stone picks up Robbie into a pile driver, but he looks at Zamza. Silver Stone goes outside the ring to Electra and he begins to point and say something to Zamza. Zamza appears to be writing something down.] JG - "Get a cameraman to see what he is writing!" [Silver Stone gets back into the ring but is met a few fists from Rock Bottom Robbie. Rock Bottom Robbie suplexes Silve Stone into the ring. Robbie then does a leg drop. Robbie climbs to the top rope.] JG - "Again, going for an aerial move. Robbie doesn't usually attempt these, but on a show of this magnitued, you do everything in your power to win." JH - "He is taking too long. Silver Stone is recovering." [Robbie slowly attempts a shooting star press, but it is very poorly executed(the counter clockwise back spin was not very complete) and Silver Stone gets both of his knees up as Robbie seems to be favoring his abdomen.] JH - "That's why you don't go over your limits. Robbie just cost himself the North American title." [Silver Stone slowly gets up and executes the SILVER SWING!!] JH - "See! Now, it is definitely over." [Silver Stone then looks at Zamza and begins to complain again. Zamza, again, appears to be writing something down.] JG - "Silver Stone is being distracted by Zamza. He has to forget about Zamza and pin Robbie." JH - "How can he forget about him? Zamza is an imposing force. Then again, Silver Stone is more imposing." [Silver Stone then goes to the outside and kisses Electra. Rock Bottom Robbie appears to be out cold.] JG - "That is way too much time. Robbie is recovering." JH - "He isn't moving, Grey! How is he recovering?" JG - "That is his style. Soon, he will just sit up and be ready to go." [Silver Stone goes into the ring, picks up Rock Bottom Robbie, and goes for a pile driver. Robbie reverses it and flips Silver Stone over. Robbie then executes a swinging neckbreaker of his own. He then goes off the top ropes for a flying leg drop.] JG - "Zamza has cost Silver Stone the North American title. He had the pin, easily, but went outside to protect Electra." JH - "What heart, right Grey? Those two young loves are going to have a happy life together. Their love is stronger than any gold." JG - "Silver Stone better hope it is. Right now, that is all he has because Robbie is in control of this matchup." [Robbie throws Silver Stone off the ropes and executes the ROCK BOTTOM SLAM!!! He goes for a pin, but Electra climbs to the apron. The referee goes to stop Electra.] JG - "Robbie hit the Slam! It should be over! Get Electra out of there!" JH - "Keep her up there! I think I see some nipple!" JG - "Wrestling is cyclical. One things happens in one place, then it snowballs and happens everywhere." [Zamza nears mid way toward the ring.] JG - "Zamza looks to be getting a better look at the action." JH - "I think he wants to see the nipple." JG - "He is writing heavily in his notebook. That could be on how Silver Stone fell prey to the Slam, though." [Silver Stone grabs something out of his pants. It appears to be spiked brass knuckles!] JG - "Oh no! Spiked brass knuckles! That could kill Robbie!" JH - "They said finish the feud. This will finish it, plus a lot more." [Rock Bottom Robbie turns around, but Silver Stone nails Robbie in the groin with that foreign object.] JH - "Robbie will be talking a lot higher later on tonight." JG - "Robbie must be bleeding internally. He is coughing up blood after that attack." [Silver Stone applies a front face lock and executes the Silver Swing.] JG - "Again! Silver Stone has hit the Silver Swing twice in this match. He has to cover him now." [Silver Stone then executes another Silver Swing on Rock Bottom Robbie.] JG - "Three times! Three Silver Swings!" JH - "This will end the feud and Robbie's career. Look at Robbie's mouth. It is red." JG - "Robbie is still squirming around on the mat. Silver Stone has to pin him, but he wants to humiliate Robbie." JH - "Nothing wrong with adding another humiliation of Robbie to his resume." [Silver Stone climbs the ropes and executes a leg drop. Silver Stone executes a pile driver, follwed by a snap mare. Silver Stone throws Robbie to the ropes and executes a belly-to-belly suplex. Silver Stone picks up Robbie again and gets off the Silver Swing again. Silver Stone points to Robbie's face and says "YOUR CHAMPION HAS SPOKEN!!", and begins to slap the face of Rock Bottom Robbie.] JG - "That is uncalled for! Just pin Robbie and get it over with. He needs medical attention." JH - "Let him bleed to death." JG - "I was talking about Silver Stone. He should be placed in a mental institute. No heart, not an ounce." [Silver Stone begins to taunt Zamza, telling him "When I'm done, your next!" Rock Bottom Robbie uncanningly gets up and suprises Silver Stone(and the world) with a fisherman's suplex.] JG - "There's heart! Robbie is fighting back and strong!" JH - "You can fight all you want with heart, but if you fight like a girl, you won't go anywhere. Right Robbie?" [Electra put Silver Stone's left foot under the bottom rope and told the referee of it.] JG - "Electra has saved Silver Stone from losing, but also cost him a win when Silver Stone saved her." JH - "What a doll." [Rock Bottom Robbie begins to bicker with the referee and then graps Electra by the hair and begins to yell at her.] JG - "Robbie is going nuts!" JH - "Electra keeps turning him down for dates. He must be jealous of everything that Silver Stone has that he doesn't." JG - "Robbie has the gold, now. Silver Stone is jealous of that." [Silver Stone catches Robbie off guard with a german suplex which also brings Electra into the ring(Robbie didn't let go of Electra's hair). The referee guides Electra to the outside.] JH - "Let her stay in! Cat fight between Robbie and Electra." JG - "It wouldn't be much of a fight." JH - "Fine. Throw in Zamza on Robbie's side. That should even it up, Electra against Robbie and Zamza." [Silver Stone picks up Robbie into a fireman's carry and then into a diamond cutter! Zamza drops the notebook, comes down to the ring and grabs Electra and tries to do something to her.] JG - "Zamza has gone too far. He is going after Electra." JH - "Reminds me of the good ol' days. Men fought for their women. Let's put Electra on the line and have Zamza and Silver Stone fight for her." JG - "I don't think that will happen. We saw the heartbreak occurred when Mrs. Tyranny was stolen." JH - "But, Silver Stone will win. It doesn't matter. I would much rather fight for a woman than some stupid belt. Which is more appealling? Some gold which can't do anything or a 5'4" beautiful, big breasted women who will wrap you arou..." JG - "Back to the action!" [Silver Stone tries to grab Zamza by the hair over the top rope. Robbie rolls Silver Stone up. 1..2..three!!!] JG - "Robbie retains the gold! Robbie retains the gold!" JH - "That was cheap. Get Zamza out of here, he doesn't belong at ringside." [Silver Stone begins to argue and become angered. Silver Stone is outraged and grabs the referee. Robbie rolls out of the ring, grabbing his belt on the way. Zamza begins to walk away, laughing. Silver Stone sees this and climbs outside. He picks up the notebook and slams it to the ground. Silver Stone spins Zamza around and the two start throwing punches.] JG - "Another brawl. I guess my wishes weren't answered." JH - "It didn't happen during the match, though." JG - "Just as bad, though. What was in the notebook?" [The camera zooms down at the notebook. On the notebook, there is a sketch of Electra, naked! Hazard jumps out of his chair and grabs the book.] JH - "I'm keeping this one." JG - "I don't think that is proportional. She seems to be drawn a little larger than life." JH - "Isn't it great? Want me to make you a photocopy?" JG - "That's quite all right." JH - "Right. I bet you have plenty of these around your house. It gets lonely, doesn't it Grey." JG - "I don't have any pictures of naked ladies in my house. Especially ones drawn by myself of female valets in our league." JH - "Why not? My house is filled with them. Everybody should have some." JG - "It's that special time of night, Jack! It's main event time! Can you feel the intensity?" JH - "All I feel is that strange feeling I get whenever I know a big intro is about to take place." JG - "Jack, take it down a notch. You're way too critical of intros. They're not too long, and I find them rather stimulating." JH - "You also found Pentagon Priest stimulating. That guy was the worst NCW'er ever." JG - "For those fans who don't understand Jack's obscure reference, Pentagon Priest has the honor of having the worst gimmick in our previous league, the NCW." JH - "Thanks for that dissertation, Mr. Grey. Your wealth of knowledge is quite extensive." JG - "Sarcasm, Jack?" JH - "No, that was an outright insult. You aren't able to pick up more subtle references." JG - "Alright, Jack. I've heard enough--really." JH - "Good, 'cause it's time for Fetters to butcher the intros. I almost fear this sickening looking guy now."
