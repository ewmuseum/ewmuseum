EWC — Fan Report

EWC First Card Back...

1 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EWC House Card -- Charlotte, NC -- Charlotte Coliseum Report by [name withheld] I must say that I was a bit skeptical when I heard the EWC was beginning to put cards together again, and I was wondering if this was even the same EWC.
But I always like to give wrestling feds a chance, and, hey, I had nothing better to do that night...
So I get to the arena and find the parking lot JAMMED... Turns out the fans were waiting for the EWC to come back as much as the wrestlers were.
Grim Weibaq was already signing TONS of autographs... Death Croc was in another part of the building getting his game face on -- even though he was only scouting tonight...
I get to my seat, about 6th row. Not too bad. Near the face locker room. I get a program, and look on it... I don't recognize a lot of the names, but several of the EWC hardcores told me about the BattleBirth II card in
New York. Sounds promising, but let's see what they look like in action first...
Bell time comes -- and they're on time. First, Nick Pumpernickle comes out and welcomes us to the EWC, explaining a lot of what has happened in the last four or five months... We're all happy to see the fed back in action, and Nick tells us that he has a lot of new people and a lot of surprises to bring on the e-wrestling world...
With that, the first two wrestlers, two rookies, come out.. THE COSMIC COMMANDOS v. THE GERSHWIN BROTHERS The Gershwin Brothers came to the ring carrying sheet music and a small digital piano with them, and they treated the crowds to a passage from
"Rhapsody in Blue" (which also happened to be their ring music). The mini- concert drew polite applause from the fans.
Once the Brothers were in the ring and the piano was taken back to the locker room area, the house lights went down and Haddaway's "Life (Mission
Control Mix)" came through the P.A. system. A laser show in the ring kept the fans occupied while the music crescendoed, and soon all attention was focused on the ring entrance where more laser lights and fog effects were underway. The music hit its peak, a pyrotechnic explosion rocked the runway, and FINALLY out came the Commandos.
The duo was clad in their full space regalia, complete with space helmets, commodore-style jackets (with those huge padded shoulders), and a mini fireworks display being shot from their wristband "Communicators".
The kids loved it, most of the parents were amused, but the hardcores were hurling all sorts of jeers and obscenities at these Power Ranger clones.
Once the masked duo entered the ring and disrobed, the size discrepancy was obvious -- the Gershwins weighed close to 300lbs each, but the Commandos were billed at 436lbs combined. And the Gershwins used that advantage in the opening stages of the match. Corporal Comet was slammed and tossed around and knocked down and just plain overpowered for about 2 or 3 minutes. But the
Commandos got the tide turned when Ivan Gershwin clamped a standing side headlock on the Corporal, but Comet was able to push him back against the ropes near his own corner and get a hidden tag from Lieutenant Laser. Ivan got shoved off, caught in a leg trip takedown by Comet, and had an elbow planted on him by Laser.
That was the last time Ivan saw his own corner. The Commandos got their high-energy, high-impact double teaming going, and they were all but unstoppable. Greg Gershwin had to save his brother after the Commandos caught Ivan with a double Gourd Buster front suplex, the "Splashdown" double
Superplex, and the "Meteor Shower" double top rope fist drop. But after the third save the Liuetenant and the Corporal caught Greg from behind with a double drop kick sending him out to the floor, never to return.
Then Comet planted Ivan Gershwin in the center of the ring with a belly-to-belly suplex, tagged in Laser, and hit their "Moonshot" Rocket
Launcher for the 1-2-3. Despite the Commandos' gimmick, man fans were won over by their wrestling display.
WINNERS: Cosmic Commandos Time: 5:44 I kinda liked the Commandos, but one must wonder how much the lights and magic will work against a Savages or a Next Breed...
SCOTT WILES VS. TAKAO NOGAMI Two new guys. Nogami came out first. About 235, right at the LH limit. The main boos he got were for his manager, "The Wrestling Expert" Ed Carr. Carr came up yelling at the fans, but Nogami just ignored EVERYBODY and came into the ring with a stern glare on his face.
Then we saw Carr take the ring mike... [EC] For those of you that don't know me, I'm "The Wrestling Expert" Ed Carr. I compete in THE major league of e-wrestling, the EWF. Now, for over a year I have tried to enter a man here in the EWC. For over a year the league has attempted to drag me down, through beauracratic $^$#@! I know, the few intelligent ones out there, both of you, are sitting there saying, "Why, Ed? Why even bother with this hellhole?" Very simple:
this league needs a little bit of class, and maybe, just maybe, with my presence here, the EWC can hope to SOMEDAY be the EWF's farm system.
Now, don't get your hopes up, my EWF contract won't let me wrestle here, so instead, I give you, my main man, Takao Nogami!
(BOOS!!!) Then "No Control" came up, and Scott Wiles, with his training crew giving him a pep talk before the match, and Wiles walks to the ring, confident.
He is BIG... for 248 pounds. He took off his robe and bellowed to the sky. This started very evenly, as Nogami went to try to take Wiles down, but Wiles kept Nogami off balance with a series of blows to the midsection and a gut-wrench suplex. This drew a big pop from the crowd, and Ed
Carr went over to Nogami and tried to settle him down. Nogami got in the ring and attempted to slow the pace down with a series of takedowns, but this match soon got to be martial arts vs. UFC-style blows as Nogami fired chops and Wiles came back with a series of head butts, backfist strikes, etc.
The crowd was INTO THIS MATCH BIGTIME... Dick Kroll, who was officiating, decided to get in the middle of it and try to get control when tempers flared. He grabbed the ring mike and warned Wiles to watch the fists after a series of blows, and then backed Ed Carr back into his corner, which drew a mixed response, respective to the actions he took.
Thing was, the heat in the match was retained, as the two went at it evenly throughout the match. A leaping bell clap at about 8:35 stunned Nogami, but when Wiles tried to follow that with an underarm choke hold, Nogami blocked that and leg dragged him down. A headscissors sleeper was attempted, but blocked.
At this point, Carr pounded on the mat to get Nogami to follow up, as Kroll warned Wiles about the hold that he tried that was blocked.
The rest of the match got a bit sloppy, as both men tried to get back to the pace of the match, but they wore down a bit after the pounding each had taken. Just as well, the crowd was pretty exhausted...
The match ended in a draw, but both men got the respect that they needed to get for an opening match for each.
TIME LIMIT DRAW -- 15:00 Nogami impressed me a bit -- it sounds like Wiles, for a face, may need to watch himself should a ref like Donaldson emerge. Nogami needs to work a bit on keeping his edge in a match where a lot of blows are taken in a short time. Ed Carr will help him on that. I'm not convinced that Wiles will be able to work in the controlled environment of wrestling, but I could be wrong.
JUSTIN SHIMODO VS. THE MASKED AVENGER The Avenger came out first, and we were wondering if we were seeing a WWF gimmick starting up here, wearing yellow and red. Some of the anti-Hogan marks booed the color scheme, but most of the marks cheered. Dick Kroll was again refereeing.
Justin Shimodo came in and a lot of the fans liked what they saw, a mix of Jushin Liger and Ric Flair. Tamara, Shimodo's manager, did the honors...
[Tamara] Shut up you hack and let someone with obvious skills talk! Now I don't know much about this Masked Avenger jerk, but I do know he's in for a whole lot
of pain. So let me introduce one third of EWC's Triple Threat, from Nagasaki, Japan, he weighs in at 227 LBS... he is JUSHIN SHIMODO!!! Avenger went for the handshake, and Shimodo dropkicked him straight away. A nerve strike soon followed, and Avenger was not happy. Avenger got to his feet, and Shimodo tripped Avenger back to the mat.
This went on for about 3-4 minutes, as Shimodo basically had the upper hand with a variety of martial arts moves. Finally, Avenger got mad and started nailing Shimodo with a series of clotheslines, the last two of which were ducked and a Nelson Sleeper was put on, which got Avenger to the ropes... The second time, though, Avenger quickly went out, and Kroll called for the bell...
WINNER: Shimodo 6:22 If Avenger wants to survive in the EWC, lose the gimmick and get some experience. Especially with someone like Shimodo... Shimodo quickly established himself as not a nice guy -- some WCW people thought Sonny Ono might be coming in... Shimodo took care of business in a big way, and if this continues, maybe the Frisco Kid and others should be aware...
JOE VS. PROF DINO CRIPPLER The last match before the intermission was a contrast in styles, an ordinary Joe -- and I mean that literally, as Joe came down to
"Ordinary Average Guy". Tamara stayed down to introduce another of her "Triple Threat"... [Tamara] Well... well... well, it looks like you morons are getting your money's worth tonight. Not only do you get to see some of the best wrestlers the
EWC can provide; namely the Triple Threat, but you also get a chance to gaze upon me. But that's not why I'm here. It seems some dork named Joe got on the wrong side of a contract with me. So Joe: Three Letters and just another victum, it's time to enter the class of pain. Weighing in at
241 lbs and hailing from Princeton, NJ. He is PROFESSOR DINO CRIPPLER!!!
Crippler came down and Tamara, already happy with one dominant performance tonight, hoped for a second to establish the new members of her "Triple
Threat" as threats to faces. Crippler went for a fast drop toehold, and Joe went for an elbow drop. He missed, and Crippler clipped his knee out, and the second one nearly took the knee completely out...
At this point, Crippler was nearly perfect, as he just worked on Joe for about 6 minutes. The only offense Joe TRIED to get was when he got mad, but a couple of shots to the leg injured Joe, and the referee stopped this mismatch.
WINNER BY RSC: Prof Crippler 5:44 Joe (-3 SPD and OUT of the tournament) really needs to learn the sport before coming to the EWC. The Triple Threat has had it much too easy this week. We'll see more of them in the weeks to come...
Intermission. Gabbed with some other fans. Most were impressed by the TT, and not with their "opposition", but I said to give them time. Of course,
Joe was being carted out as we speak...
After the intermission, six of the big guys... JACK MORANDINI VS. THE HIGHWAY WORKER first... The Highway Worker is HUGE!! 6'9" 360??? The guy must've put on 50 pounds of solid muscle in the layoff. He looks good...
Jack Morandini came out with Vicki Vegas, as part of the "Vegas Mob"... Then, the referee came out... ANOTHER BIG MAN... He was known as "The Warden", and he meant business... And he was not afraid to lay into either participant should the need arise... The match started out as a slugfest, the Worker getting the upper hand. The Worker came off the ropes for a clothesline, and Morandini turned the momentum into a gigantic powerslam. At that point, Vicki Vegas got up on the apron to try to distract the Warden. Try being the operative word.
Vegas goes up and down for a chokeslam. (ouch) Morandini is slamming knucks into the Worker, so he goes up and down for a chokeslam. (double ouch)
At this point, Morandini is not a happy camper, but after a series of shots with the knucks, he's the conscious one. As the Worker was getting up, the Warden was in the face of Morandini, who cocked back to swing at him, but Worker small-packaged him, and Vegas, barely conscious, kicked at the
Worker for the save.
Problem was, the Warden caught her, and went to the outside. Bad move, as both the Warden and the Worker went to the outside to confront Vegas.
This gave Morandini all the time he needed for more work with the knucks, which he executed, knocking the Worker senseless. Of course, the crowd was booing like nuts when the Warden had no choice but to count 10 once he and
Morandini were back in the ring.
WINNER: Jack Morandini by screwjob CO. 5:45 I like the idea of the Warden, but something tells me he's going to have some other ideas about how these matches go. Morandini and Vegas work well together, and will be a force in the EWC, given time. The Worker just got caught on the wrong end of that mess. He'll be back.
ANIMAL INSTINCT VS. DAVENFORTH AND DACOSTA Our first look at the Upstarts since the layoff... (Cut to Roberto DaCosta and Professor Horatio Davenforth in DaCosta' parlor. Conspicuous by their absence are the rest of the Upstarts.) [PhD] It is a terrible shame that the rest of them had to miss this wonderful little opportunity.
[RDC] Yes, well, an Upstart is always a busy man! Belmont is training for his next North American defense, the Spectre's got his time filled with training against Light Heavyweight grapplers, and Von Hauser is back visiting Germany until the end of the week.
[PhD] I guess that means you and I will have to take care of this week's segment then, yes? [RDC] For some reason, the EWC Championship Committee has placed it upon us to prove ourselves in the tag team ranks when we have no interest in any tag team titles. Alas, we are set to face Sly Foxx's Animal Instinct at the upcoming house show.
[PhD] Worry not, Roberto, my friend! As you know, the Upstarts are always winners! We shall have no trouble defeating those odorous, obnoxious, mangy beasts in the ring!
[RDC] Yes, but I am a little worried... right afterwards, we'll each be involved in the tournament to see who gets to face Archie... now what is
THAT? One of us will end up facing him for the North American belt and along the way, we'll have to meet both Marc and our former stablemate,
Ty! [PhD] Worrying is not something an Upstart is capable of! We will also sit down and have a little talk about this matter in the days to come. For now, just remember who you are... an Upstart! And thus, a winner forever!
(Fade to black.) With that, out came PhD Horatio Davenforth and Roberto DaCosta. Both men were soundly booed by even the hardcores, as many remembered the Upstarts.
Then, out came Sly Foxx, Animal Instinct, and BARON VON HAUSER... He grabbed
Foxx, AI grabbed him, Davenforth and DaCosta grabbed AI, and the match was on up the aisle, with Mongo Christian chasing down the aisle about two minutes too late. Von Hauser got Foxx over his shoulder to carry him off when MAX
MOOSE showed up, and we had six men going at it. Von Hauser and Moose stared each other off, as Foxx was less than gently set down.
At this point, the four were still fighting on the outside, as the jump kept them on the outside. The brawl continued for about 5 minutes, and then even
Christian had no choice -- this had gone on too long.
DOUBLE COUNTOUT The brawl continued with all six men involved until security and Damage Control finally broke it up. The crowd loved it, as AI might be getting programmed into a feud with those two Upstarts. Could a six-man for
BattleBirth II be on the horizon?? Well, it was time for the main event... NON-TITLE: TY KROSS VS. EWC WORLD CHAMPION GRIM WEIBAQ (The camera opens on Ty Kross sitting on the porch of his Texas ranch house) [Cameraman] Mr. Kross. You're on. [Kross] Thanks son. Well it's been a while since ya'll last saw me in the EWC. Then again, it's been awhile since you'll have seen anyone in the EWC. But that's good. You see I've been sitting here at the Double Kross ranch just tryin' to come up with ways to get where I want to be in this here wrasslin' federation. Well it plum came to me when I was sittin'
here with my dogs Rusty and Jake. Now Jake here is a pure breed French poodle. Now you're probably askin' yourself "What in the Hell do dogs have to do with wrasslin'?" Well I'll tell ya. Jake here is all pretty to look at, but he ain't very usefull. Now Rusty here is a mutt. He's mean as a rattle snake. You see, I started in the EWC being just like
Jake. I yapped a lot, but I didn't really do much. Well now it's time to be more like Rusty. Grim Wei-baq you're the only man in this fed that can bring out the Rusty in me. So this weekend I'm gonna walk the isle and take whatever you can dish out. Go ahead and hit me with that fancy Grim's Reaper. Kick the crap out of me. Throw me around like a rag doll. You see I already know I can beat ya. You not puttin' that
EWC Heavyweight belt up is proof of that. But do you truely know that? That's the question we'll answer in the ring. So make no mistake Grim, I'm gonna do every sneaky, rotten, underhanded thing I can to beat you and I can guarentee I have more tricks up my sleeve than you do.
(fade to black) With that, out came Ty Kross. Tamara was with her, and the Triple Threat was going to try to go 3 for 3.
Then "Iron Man" came on and the biggest pop of the night for Grundig, Grim, and the League of Destruction, as they came out. Tamara complained vehemently, but the Warden would have none of it, as long as everyone stayed out of the way.
So the match began. Grim just grinned at Ty, who came after him with repeated shots to the mid-section, promptly no-sold. Ty was running like crazy after shots, using the stick and move that several other opponents had used to beat Weibaq in the past.
Weibaq just patiently waited and got him in the corner and started beating on Kross. He basically got out just as the Warden was about to lay hands on his neck. Then he went back in for some more. This drew a stiff warning from the Warden to get him out of the corner. Grim did -- into the center of the ring. Tamara was not pleased...
So it went -- Kross sticking and moving, using repeated trip slams once they started getting effective, but Weibaq cornering and pounding, until Grim had weakened Kross up enough to go for the Reaper. He went, and Kross nailed him in the head to send him off the ropes to the inside.
Kross then tried to tie up Weibaq into the Double Kross. Tamara squealed, but the Warden saw Weibaq JUST make the ropes...
At this point, Kross whipped Weibaq off to get him down for the Double Kross again, and Tamara was distracting everybody, including the Warden. Weibaq turned, threw Kross over the top, right to the feet of the LoD.
Yep -- THEY started pounding on him... At this point, Tamara was set back to the floor, and Weibaq was grinning... At that point, the Warden GRABBED
Weibaq and SLAMMED him hard, since he saw the LoD pounding on Kross.
Then he went out and grabbed Phoenix as he had just finished putting a dress on Kross... Pink with flowers...
At this point, Justin Shimodo and Prof. Crippler charged the ring on the downed Weibaq... At that, out came the Experimental Man... And at THAT came a chokeslam frenzy by the Warden!! Last count, and he was still chokeslamming, was about ten. Of course, he had no choice but to
THROW THE MATCH OUT -- NO CONTEST DUE TO MASSIVE INTERFERENCE He didn't want to -- he had no choice... Look for this to be the feud that will get the EWC going, the Triple Threat vs. Rock Solid Wrestling. Grim came out of it holding his back (-1 END -- will be OK vs. Croc) Kross his head (-1 COU). Good match, chaotic ending. All in all, I was pleasantly surprised. Some good matches, angles started, and getting ready for BattleBirth II... Fun night...
