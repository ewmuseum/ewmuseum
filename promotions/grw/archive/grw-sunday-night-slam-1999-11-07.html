GRW — Card

Sunday Night Slam

7 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<<The program opens, but instead of the usual opening credits and serene scenes of the state of Georgia that generally open up a GRW show, we are met by much the opposite as "Sunday Night Slam" hits the airwaves. A small blue Ford Ranger pickup truck pulls into the back parking lot of the arena, hurriedly coming from the driver's side is Logan Gunz. Still dressed in his wrestling attire from "Friday Night Showdown", large bags under his eyes suggest that he has yet to sleep in the last 48 hours. Quickly through the parking lot he steps, and enters through a door in the back marked simply "EMPLOYEE'S". Once inside he continues his hurried step. His arms dangling out to his sides, unable to rest against his body due to his massive size... his head is tilted slightly downward and he peers out from under his eyebrows with his steel green eyes. Sharp, focused, like lasers beaming forth from his head, his eyes lead him through the back. Several GRW ring techs and sound people dive from his path as he is so driven by his anger that he doesn't see them, or he merely doesn't care. He almost appears as if he is in delirium, "zoned out" so to speak. One has to wonder whether this has been brought on by the sleep deprivation, or the demons that Destiny's abduction have released from his soul. The camera has now caught up with him walking down the hall that houses the wrestler's lockerooms. A concerned Steve Blades steps out from his dressing room and blocks Gunz's path...>> [BLADES]: "Listen man, I saw what happened. Before you go getting all "pantherish" on somebody, let's talk..." <<Gunz says nothing, he places his hands on Blades' arms and lifts him up off the ground and sets him to the side. The scene switches to show Rick Thompson and Joe Young standing in the ring...>> [THOMPSON]: "Are we rolling? Fans, welcome to "Sunday Night Slam"! If you were watching "Friday Night Showdown", you know that Denny Langham has taken Destiny captive, and it appears that Logan Gunz has come to the arena tonight on a mission..." [YOUNG]: "Bah, I'm not interested in hearing this moron cry about his chick picking the better man. Someone tell him to toughen up and take it like a man." <<"Iron Man" By Black Sabbath explodes over the arena's loudspeakers, the thousands of GRW fans in attendance simultaneously come to the their feet... the roar of the crowd is near deafening...>> [THOMPSON]: <<screaming over the crowd noise>> "Well, Joe, here comes the Georgia State Champion...why don't you tell him yourself?" [YOUNG]: "I would...but...uh..." [THOMPSON]: "Exactly what I thought." <<Logan Gunz steps out of the wrestler's entrance, still dressed in the blue, full-length tights from Friday night. He has no shirt, and no bandanna... in fact he looks exactly the same as he did when Langham drove off with Destiny on Friday night. He stands at the top of the aisleway, the crowd now nearing bedlam in their enthusiasm. His eyes are fixed squarely on the center of the GRW ring before him. Gunz begins making his way to the ring, barely even cognizant of the fans that reach to touch him, he has other business to take care of tonight. Gunz slides under the bottom ring-rope and returns to his feet in the center of the ring. He reaches down and takes a microphone from the ring attendant, and begins to pace back and forth in the center of the ring. It's almost reminiscent of a caged animal. "Iron Man" dies out in the background, however the cheers of the fans of the state of Georgia still sound as if a tornado is ripping through the building...>> [GUNZ]: "Langham...welcome to the "Point of No Return"!!!" <<The crowd cheers, the intensity in Logan's voice goes beyond the levels he normally exhibits, in fact...it sounds more like sheer, unadulterated rage...>> [GUNZ]: "And, in case you didn't know, my name is "THE_WARRIOR"...LOGAN GUNZ, and I will be guiding you on your trip through your most terrifying experience. Your journey is going to be a non-stop, full service flight. Once you have reached your destination please be sure to check and see that you've packed everything, there is no return flight...all destinations are FINAL!! And, I'm happy to announce, that the points you earn on this trip will qualify you for one free SHOTGUN_PRESS upon arrival in...the forest...MY_FOREST!!!" <<Gunz is incensed and the fans sense it. They continue to cheer as he speaks, even more loudly when he puts an exclamation on his words...>> [GUNZ]: "Denny Langham, you made the biggest mistake of your life, not career, life when you put your hands on Destiny. You decided that you didn't have the guts, you weren't man enough to step in between those ropes and lay me down for the three count. You couldn't take the fight to the panther, Langham, you realized that within the forest myself and Destiny are too strong, so you decided to attempt to remove Destiny from the protection that the forest provides...and in doing so, you've now sealed your own fate. What you failed to realize is that Destiny and I have a bond that goes beyond distance, she is with me at all times in spirit and that is where the true strength of the panther lies. She too, is much too strong for you, Langham. Much like the panthress that she is...there is no cage you can build, no walls that you can erect that will be strong enough to capture her spirit." <<Gunz draws the camera closer to him, it focuses in on his eyes...>> [GUNZ]: "You want your re-match, "Mr. Music City"? You want another chance at the Gunz... YOU_GOT_IT. You name the date, and you name the time...because the place is already determined. Within the forest, Langham, where the panther thrives...that is where you must travel. As you stand within it's depths and you hear the creatures that surround you grow silent, you will know that the panther is near...silently drawing ever closer, and preparing to pounce. This time, though, I'm not putting you down for the 1-2-3...not this time. This time, you're going down for eternity. This time "The Warrior" will not be satisfied with mere victory, only your complete and total annihilation will pacify. You have called the panther from his lair, and you have taken the only thing that means anything in this world to him...now, the panther stalks, and he is thirsty for your blood. Destiny will be back by my side, Denny Langham, and once she is safe...it's going to be...." <<Gunz turns his head skyward with the microphone to his lips...>> [GUNZ]: "DOOM_ON_YOU!!!" <<"Iron Man" by Black Sabbath once again cranks up, and Logan slides through the ropes. The scene switches to show various shots of GRW wrestlers, and the song "Gimme Three Steps" by Lynyrd Skynyrd plays in the backgroud... then it shifts to say "Georgia Regional Wrestling proudly presents... Sunday Night Slam" with the GRW logo immediately below it, which soon fades out to show the sold out high school gymnasium. In the middle of the gym sits a simple wrestling ring. The music quiets a bit, then the scene pans out to show the ring on a monitor sitting in a studio. In front of the monitor sits a "V" shape desk, with the monitor directly above the point of the "V". On one side of the monitor sits a man dressed in a well-pressed, gray suit... GRW commentator Rick Thompson. On the other side sits a man dressed in a solid black shirt and black cowboy hat... GRW commentator "Outlaw" Joe Young...>> [THOMPSON]: "Good evening everyone... and welcome to the GRW's "Sunday Night Slam". I'm your host, Rick Thompson, and as usual joining me is my co-host "Outlaw" Joe Young!" <<Joe Young tips his hat to the camera...>> [THOMPSON]: "Fans... we saw comments that were taped earlier today by "The Warrior" Logan Gunz, who is obviously very furious and distraught over Denny Langham's kidnapping of Destiny..." [YOUNG]: "You see... this is a brilliant move my Langham..." [THOMPSON]: "BRILLIANT?!?" [YOUNG]: "Of course... this give Langham control over his rematch. It's obvious that Gunz wants Langham now... he said so... Langham is getting what he wants!" [THOMPSON]: "But... surely there will be some sort of punishment handed down from the front office, after Langham struck GRW Commissioner Bob Lane on Friday..." [YOUNG]: "Well... not if Gunz is to get Destiny back, there won't be..." [THOMPSON]: "Bob Lane has already announced that this coming Friday, on November 12th, he will come out and make an announcment regarding the situation..." [YOUNG]: "And Langham will get his way..." [THOMPSON]: "That remains to be seen. Fans, tonight we have a great program... as we will show highlights from FOUR big matches that were taped earlier today! So, without any further delay, let's go to the higlights from our first match..." <<The scene fades to match highlights...>> ******************************** FRANKIE "The Flash" CASH vs. "The Blessos Bull" ROWAN DRAPER ******************************** [THOMPSON]: "A good match featuring two new faces to the GRW. Draper used his size advantage to control the early goings of the match... but as the match went longer, Cash gained control. "The Flash" eventually nailed Draper with the "Money Maker" frog splash to gain the win..." <<The scene fades back to the program...>> [YOUNG]: "I told you Thompson... Cash is a veteran, and he will make a HUGE impact here in Georgia! This win was simply the first step..." [THOMPSON]: "I have to agree with you Joe, Cash knows his way around the ring! Let's move on to our next match-up..." <<The scene fades to match highlights...>> ************************ "Primetime" BRIAN RICH [w/h Kyle Polo] vs. "Dragonheart" JOHN ROSE [w/h Jalyn Gilbert] ************************ [THOMPSON]: "This match never happened... as Rich was in the ring awaiting his opponent, the Ty Brooks announced that John Rose has left the GRW, and therefore, Brian Rich picks up a win via forfeit... but as Rich and Kyle Polo were in the ring celebrating, the camera picked up the Hardcore Legend, who was standing at the end of the aisle, watching Rich in the ring..." <<The scene fades back to the program...>> [YOUNG]: "Brian Rich is another veteran... John Rose probably turn tailed and ran out of fear!" [THOMPSON]: "No matter what the circumstances are, John Rose is no longer on the GRW roster, and Brian Rich picks up an easy win..." [YOUNG]: "A win is a win... no matter how you get it!" <<Rich Thompson rolls his eyes...>> [YOUNG]: "And what was the Hardcore Legend doing out there?" [THOMPSON]: "He looked like he was scouting potential opponents..." [YOUNG]: "Or potential allies?!?" [THOMPSON]: "It could very well be! Only time will tell! Right now fans, we've got interesting comments that were taped earlier... let's take a look..." <<The scene fades to a taped clip showing the high school gym with the ring set up in the middle. From out of nowhere, the song "Picture This" by the Beastie Boys starts to play. The slow, sultry, jazzy tune seems to weave a spell as the lights dim slightly. Walking from the back is a pair of men. One, who is walking in front, is recognizable as Jacob Moore Jr., the son of the late legend and Foreign Talent Coordinator of the GRW. He is, as we have seen him before, a well-dressed, thin and ugly young man. His suit is of the finest black silk and of an expensive cut. The suit was likely purchased in Rome along with the sleek, leather shoes he wears. Under the three-button suit coat is a silky green shirt which clasps at the throat beneath a thin, purple tie which has a wicked sheen. In his let hand is a thick book entitled, "Japanese to English Dictionary." He looks very serious, his wide mouth a straight slit. His long, pointy nose holds up a pair of very dark, black sunglasses which hide his eyes, and his long, greasy hair is pulled back into a ponytail...>> [THOMPSON]: "Here is Jacob Moore Jr., son of the late, great "Champion of All Continents," Jacob Moore Sr..." [YOUNG]: "But who's that with him?" <<As Moore ascends the steps, he is followed closely by a Japanese man. The man is solidly built, and a little under six feet tall. He has very broad shoulders. He wears a very formal-looking gray suit which is a double breasted affair. He has a businesslike look with his white shirt and paisley tie. He scowls, his mouth in a permanent frown, and knots his heavy brow. His hair is black and short and blends in seamlessly with his well-trimmed beard and mustache. The two men enter the ring and the crowd claps to show their sympathy for Moore's loss. The music dies down. Moore wastes no time, pulling out a microphone to speak. His voice is an irksome, nasal twang with a hint of a Southern drawl...>> [MOORE]: "Earlier this week, I laid my father to rest. In his prime, no man could pin his shoulders to the mat for even three seconds. Now?" <<He seems to be overcome with emotion and lowers the microphone, his chin quivering. The Asian man clasps him on the shoulder and Moore regains his composure...>> [MOORE]: "Now--six feet of ground has him pinned for all eternity. He was a great man. He lived a great life. What is his legacy? A group of losses for his discoveries here in GRW? No. I won't let that be how YOU-- the GREEDY, FICKLE fans--remember my father!" <<The crowd doesn't know what to make of this--a few boo, but most are silent out of respect to the dead Moore Sr...>> [MOORE]: "You see, it's time for a TRUTH lesson. My father went out and found the best wrestlers there were out there in foreign lands. He found a great power-wrestler in South America named James Mossburg. What happened? GRW told him he had to wrestle submission style--so he lost. He brought in a fine technical wrestler from Mexico. GRW told him he had to be a masked luchadore--they gave him the name 'El Fantastico.' He was no high-flier, which they demanded he be--so he lost. He brought in a fantastic team of Japanese greats. The GRW demanded that they call themselves the 'Young Dragons' and wrestle totally alien styles to them--so they lost. HOW MANY OF MY FATHER'S DISCOVERIES DID YOU WANT TO RUIN, BOB LANE?" <<Several more members of the crowd boo at this point. The anger of Moore Jr. flows...>> [MOORE]: "Were you jealous because he was a TRUE champion of the WORLD, not just in B.F. Georgia?" <<Now the majority of the fans boo...>> [MOORE]: "This man next to me is 'The Phoenix' Toshio Nakemura. You KNOW he has a bad reputation for injuring people, Lane, so you made him wear a mask and call himself 'Tsuyio Dragon.' Well, the masks are off. Yours is off, his is off and mine is off. GRW, I'm unmasking ALL OF YOU!" <<He pauses, looking at Nakemura, who nods approvingly...>> [MOORE]: "Last week when my father was announced as dead, I was broken. I watched and listened carefully to EVERY SINGLE INTERVIEW given by the so-called stars of GRW last week. NOT ONE OF THEM MENTIONED THEIR CONDOLENCES! HOW DARE YOU INSULT MY FATHER??? YOU'RE NOT WORTHY TO COMPETE IN THIS SPORT--HIS SPORT!!!" <<More boos arise as Moore screams in heartbroken rage...>> [MOORE]: "And the worst is this Barry Ferguson! They announced my legendary dad's death during his match and he didn't even so much as send a card to the funeral home! Well, Ferguson: YOUR FUNERAL WILL BE NEXT!" [THOMPSON]: "Fans we're sorry about this!" [YOUNG]: "Let him speak! He has to get this off of his chest!" [MOORE]: "And Domination, Hardcore Legend, you also wrestled my father's discoveries. YOU'LL PAY TOO for making him look bad! ALL OF GRW WILL PAY: from the men in the office who FORCED LAME GIMMICKS on these INTERNATIONAL SUPERSTARS to the INGRATE WRESTLERS who wouldn't so much as MENTION my father's passing in their LOUSY INTERVIEWS!" <<Total crowd boos now, it is hard to sympathize with this raving maniac...>> [MOORE]: "And then STEVEN BLADES has the GALL to cry over his dead uncle? Hey, Blades: [BLEEP] YOUR DEAD UNCLE! [THOMPSON]: "OH GOOD LORD! I can't believe he said that!" <<The crowd is now chanting, "CRY BABY! CRY BABY!"...>> [MOORE]: "YOU'RE DAMNED RIGHT I'M CRYING! MY _DAD_ IS GONE! I'm talking my FATHER, not some drunken uncle like that pansy Blades wept over! And you call him a CHAMPION? GET REAL!!! GRW, get ready to pay for Jacob Moore Sr.'s degradation at YOUR hands! I'm going to take this man, Toshio Nakemura, 'The Phoenix,' to the TOP. This man was BANNED from Japan due to his use of the one and only ROYAL LOTUS HOLD. This hold was illegalized in Japan due to the crippling effects it had on his opponents. I know that Lane won't remove the ban on that hold in America, so we will just have to do without it. Regardless of that fact, 'The Phoenix' will show ALL OF YOU why he was actually BANNED from his homeland RIGHT HERE, NEXT WEEK. WE WANT A MATCH! And this time, we'll wrestle it OUR WAY, not YOURS, GRW! I don't care WHO you put him against, Lane: he'll KILL him. Right, Toshio?" <<The stoic Asian takes the mic...>> [NAKEMURA]: "Hai! Soo desu!" <<The mic is handed back...>> [MOORE]: "And this is a FACT: There's NOTHING that YOU..." <<He points to the crowd...>> [MOORE]: "... CAN DO ABOUT IT!" <<He drops the microphone to a chorus of boos as the song "Picture This" plays again. "The Phoenix" holds the rope as his mentor exits and both men walk up the aisle, heads held high. The scene fades back to the program...>> [YOUNG]: "Ahhhhhh... you know, Jacob Moore Jr. is going to OWN this promotion before it's all said and done! He's my kind of man!" [THOMPSON]: "Lying, cheating, selfish..." [YOUNG]: "HEY!" <<Rick Thompson chuckles...>> [THOMPSON]: "Let's move on to our next match-up..." <<The scene fades to match highlights...>> *************************** "Faithful" JUSTIN HVIZDZAK [w/h Marc LeRoux] vs. "Swift" TONY WRIGHT *************************** [THOMPSON]: "A great match-up... Justin Hvizdzak was accompanied by Marc LeRoux, who has taken the young Polish up-and-comer under his wing. Both men wrestled a great match... but late in the match Tony Wright missed the "Swiftness" superkick. Hvizdzak caught Wright's leg, and swept him to the mat with a hard takedown. The sweep obviously injured the right knee of Tony Wright, and from there on out the match belonged to Hvizdzak. "Faithful" eventually nailed Wright with the "Revolution" 360 degree frog splash to pick up the win..." <<The scene fades back to the program...>> [YOUNG]: "What a BIG win for Hvizdzak in his debut!" [THOMPSON]: "Indeed! And beyond that, we've since been told that Tony Wright's right knee is severely injured... he's going to miss some action here in the GRW! Let's just go to our next match..." <<The scene fades to match highlights...>> *************************** "The Panther" JARED MATHYS [w/h The Hardcore Legend] vs. "The Natural" KURT DREAMER *************************** [THOMPSON]: "A great match-up, with the up-and-comer Kurt Dreamer making an impressive showing. About mid-way through the match, the camera picked up a man in the crowd, watching the match intently..." [YOUNG]: "Yeah... wasn't that Aaron Darby, the former Atlanta City Champion..." [THOMPSON]: "Well... yes and no..." [YOUNG]: "Huh?!" [THOMPSON]: "It is the same man... but we've been told that he now prefers to be called "Enigma"..." [YOUNG]: "So... he's back in the GRW?" [THOMPSON]: "Yes, he'll be making his in-ring return next weekend..." [YOUNG]: "Why was he watching this match?" [THOMPSON]: "I've no idea... but another outsider made his presence felt even more. The Hardcore Legend, who accompanied Mathys to the ring, pulled the feet out from under Kurt Dreamer as he was perched on the top rope attempting a moonsault. Mathys then nailed Dreamer with a SUPER "Pantherplex", bridged up and scored the win..." <<The scene fades back to the program...>> [YOUNG]: "So Mathys picks up his SECOND win of the weekend!" [THOMPSON]: "Thanks to the Hardcore Legend..." [YOUNG]: "I'm telling you Thompson... this relationship between Legend and Mathys seems to be growing beyond Legend having Mathys as a lackey for 30 days..." [THOMPSON]: "I have to agree... and while we're on the subject, we've got an announcement to make, straight from the desk of Bob Lane..." [YOUNG]: "What is it..." [THOMPSON]: "Mr. Lane has announced that next weekend, he has signed a match to fill the vacant Atlanta City Tag Team Titles..." [YOUNG]: "Between who?!?" [THOMPSON]: "He says that as a result of the big wins this weekend... the match will pit Youth Gone Wild against the team of the Hardcore Legend and Jared Mathys!" [YOUNG]: "WOW! What a match!!" [THOMPSON]: "Absolutely! Fans... we're out of time... we'll see you this Friday! So long..." <<"Gimme Three Steps" begins to play again, as highlights from the matches begin to play. After a few moments, and a final shot of the Hardcore Legend and Jared Mathys walking to the locker room... the scene fades to black...>>
