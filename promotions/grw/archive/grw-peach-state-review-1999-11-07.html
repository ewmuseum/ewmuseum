If your's is not, it'll be returned for you to rework. If you don't know the form, check the webpage in the "Rules" section for information. If you still have questions, contact me < [detail withheld] > and I'll send you info. So... be sure to RP each week! *If anybody will be able to help out with match writing, etc... please let me know! *The webpage will be updated soon. Sorry it's not been updated in awhile... it will be updated this week (probably Tuesday evening)! You can check it out at: http://members.tripod.com/~GeorgiaWrestling/mainpage.htm *If everyone could, check your bio page on the webpage, and let me know if there's any wrong info on them... and if you don't have a pic, please try to help me find one... *If anybody knows of a handler with a strong character, who fits the GRW no-nonsense style... please send them our way! *Be sure to go to the webpage "The E-Wrestling Zone" and vote for the GRW and it's wrestlers in their rankings! There's a link on the mainpage of the GRW webpage... go there, then go to "The Rankings" section, and there will be an e-mail address to send your votes. Vote for the GRW in the feds section, as well as our wrestlers in the singles and tag teams sections! *If anyone has ANYTHING they'd like to discuss with me... be it ideas or whatever... PLEASE E-MAIL ME!
