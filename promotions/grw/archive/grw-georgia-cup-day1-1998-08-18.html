Georgia Cup Tag Team Tournament: Day 1 of 3, Part 1 of 2

18 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<<The scene fades in to show the empty "Chilli Pepper"... a tri-level dance club in downtown Atlanta, Georgia. It's about three o'clock in the morning, all the chairs are set on top of the table nicely. Pool table lights hang above, still lit. The cue ball sets in the middle of the table with a pool stick laying across. Neon "Budweiser" lights hang lifeless. But leaning against a bar table, is none other than "Die Hard" Michael Lewd. He wears a black Georgia Regional Wrestling t-shirt, Ralph Lauren jeans, and a pair of Calvin Klein combat boots. His long, sandy brown hair is tied back into a pony tail. He has a glass of Jack Daniels in his hand, his sips softly upon the cup. He rubs his face, and throws the glass with a thrust of emotion. He stares directly into the camera, and with a flare of anger says...>> [LEWD]: "What the hell are you doing here!! What the [BEEP] do you GRW people do, follow me around wherever the [BEEP] I go?? You people are SICK!!! I ought to take that camera and shove it up your mother [BEEP] ass, you stupid son of a [BEEP]. I'm sick of being the good guy, I'm sick of trying to be a fan favorite. Bob Lane, you wanted me to be a goody two shoes, but it's just not my style!! My style is to be ruthless, black hearted, not giving a rat's ass for anybody but myself!! And that's exactly what I'm going to be from this point on!!!" <<Lewd lets his hair down out of the pony tail, and punches the wall, leaving a crack in the cement block...>> [LEWD]: "Now onto other buisness. The Georgia Cup Tag Team Tournament!! Now, my tag-team partner is going to be none-other than "The Iceman" David Smith. Well let's get something straight right now Smith, I don't like you, you don't like me. That's the way it's going to be. I'm not in it to win any damn gold, I'm in it to earn some damn respect around here. So if you, or anyone else in the damn tournament get's in my way, I'll surely take them out with what I like to call an old fashioned ass whipping!!" <<Lewd reaches behind the bar table, grabbing a bottle of Jack Daniels, quickly downing the whole bottle, and tossing it at the pool table light...>> [LEWD]: "Our first opponents, whoever the hell they may be, will surely fall on their knee's in defeat. People keep questioning me about "What if you have to face Hot House??". Simple answer my amigo's, Hot House is nothing more than two men trying to act like two Billy Badass', and their's a simple solution to that. An ass whooping, Lewd style. It's like the old saying goes, when you play with fire you might get burnt. Well looks like me, myself, and I am going to have to piss on everyone's fire, and get some reputation on my own. It all starts TONIGHT!!" <<The word "TONIGHT" flashes across the screen...>> [LEWD]: "Get that crap off the screen, this is my interview not some damn promo for the Georgia Cup. Let me tell the rest of you something, if you think you're going to piss on MY fire, then you better think twice about it. Because when you [BEEP] with "Die Hard" Michael Lewd, you get [BEEP]'ed back!!! Understandable? Everyone in this tournament will get a taste of me, and hell, I might just get a taste of you!!!" <<Scene shows "TONIGHT" again over the screen...>> [LEWD]: "I TOLD YOUR STUPID ASS TO GET THAT OFF THE [BEEP] DAMN SCREEN!!! DO YOU UNDERSTAND ME!!! NEXT TIME I SEE THAT THING POP UP, I'M GOING TO TAKE THAT CORD AND WRAP IT AROUND YOUR THROAT!!! DO YOU UNDERSTAND???" <<Lewd charges the camera and suddenly the scene switches to static. The scene then switches to show a solid black screen. A low humming can be heard in the background...>> [VOICE OVER]: "Forty four wrestlers... twenty two tag teams... one team will survive, and walk away as the winner. ALL the titles are on the line... dramatic odds are at stake as the SCW Georgia Region proudly presents... ===================================== THE GEORGIA CUP TAG TEAM TOURNAMENT ===================================== <<The scene suddenly switches to show a dimly-lit, sold-out Georgia Dome in Atlanta, Georgia, as "You Wreck Me" by Tom Petty kicks in. Pyrotechnics suddenly shot off out of the each of the four ringposts... followed by a huge pop from the crowd. The scene then slowly widens... revealing the shot to be a taped clip shown on a large monitor in a studio. The studio is set up like that of a news broadcast... with a large, oak desk sitting, with various papers on top. On the front of the desk is the GRW logo, and behind the desk sits GRW broadcaster Rick Thompson. Immediately behind, and slightly to the right of Thompson is the monitor. So prepare to recieve a Floridian cruiser ass kicking." <<The scene fades to match highlights...>> [THOMPSON]: "A very heated match... as there is obviously no love lost between these four men. Several attempts to once again regain control were expertly avoided by the Diamond Dynasty, who had the obvious edge in teaming together. Eventually... Chris Rogers nailed Genesis with the "Psychedlic Splash" to gain the win. After the match, the crowd cheered Genesis and Executioner for a solid showing in their first match as a team. Genesis took the house mic, and made these comments..." <<The scene fades to a taped clip...>> [GENESIS]: "Executioner, you've wrestled with me and tried as hard as you can to win. But, we're both singles by trade. So, this will probably the last time we team together. And like I said before, you don't know who you can and can't trust. Everyone in Armageddon knows for a fact that they can trust one another. We haven't known you for very long, but we've chosen to befriend you. You won't officialy be a part of Armageddon, meaning your name won't be written next to ours, but you will be treated as if you were a member. So what do you say?" <<Executioner looks at Genesis for a moment and then nods at him, and extends his hand. Genesis grasps it, and the took shake hands as the crowd cheers wildy. The scene fades back to the program...>> [THOMPSON]: "So Chris Rogers and Phillip Starks pick up a win in a hard fought battle... and it appears that Armageddon has gained an ally in The Executioner. The camera pans over to the entrance path, only to show no movement whatsoever. The music continues to play and the fans continue to boo, but still, there is no sight of Worthington...>> [THOMPSON]: "What is going on here?" [YOUNG]: "Worthington has a flair for the dramatic, Rick. He's probably just trying to rile up this crowd of idiots that can't treat him like the respectable gentleman he is." <<Out of the back comes Iao Kiao, "The Rainbow Warrior". He is dressed in a grey business suit with sparkiling cobalt colored tie. He strides with calm purpose towards the ring, holding a cylindrical object in his right hand...>> [THOMPSON]: "Well, there's Worthington's footman, Kiao, but where is Warren? And what in the WORLD is he holding?" [YOUNG]: "Hold your water, Thompson. Good things come to those who wait. Even if they're overeager commentators." <<Iao Kiao slips through the ring ropes and approaches the referee in the middle of the ring. He gestures for a microphone and is handed one immediately. He gives the referee the microphone and can be heard to order him to hold it. Iao fixes the ref with a glare that intimates he should comply and the ref does so with no argument. Iao then pulls on the cylinder and opens it, revealing a scroll. Iao clears his throat and begins to read...>> [IAO]: <<His voice is deep and rich and his pronunciation and elocution perfect.>> "This is a missive from my employer, Warren Worthington III. <<The fans resume their boos at the mention of Worthington's name. [IAO]: <<Continuing to read off of the parchment.>> "The reasons for this are many, but I shall outline the largest, most nagging ones for you. Unfortunately, I did not prepare Iao Kiao with flash cards, so the moronic wits out in the audience need to pay particular attention." <<The fans noise rises in volume and Iao allows a quick smirk to cross his face.>> "Firstly, I do NOT tag in ANY match with a stinking American. None have proven themselves worthy of being the same caliber as a Worthington and I doubt any ever shall." <<Louder boos from the crowd>> "Secondly, I do not wrestle like Lapdog Bob Lane's puppet. Just because he wants to rule this federation with an iron fist does not mean I have to be party to it. Unfortunately my normal tag partner, Tony Wright, is not available to compete or else I would luxuriate in destroying the rest of the tag teams in this rancid and fleeting disaster of an idea that is the Georgia Cup. Thirdly, the idea of wrestling a team called the United States Bulldogs appalls me to my very core. You Yankees have that ridiculous, white headed raptor as an icon. Do NOT and I mean NEVER try and associate me with an American team who falsely uses the British icon of the bulldog. It insults me to even <<think>> there is a team allowed to compete in this region under that moniker. I shall <<not>> wrestle against them for it goes against many of my higly placed personal standards." <<Iao pauses and smiles out at the audience and the other members of the match.>> [THOMPSON]: "Oh, this is RIDICULOUS! Warren Worthington III is a coward!" [YOUNG]: "Watch yourself, Rick. He's probably tuning into this broadcast and he won't take too kindly to that type of talk coming from some mic jock. Besides, I admire the man for his beliefs." [THOMPSON]: "WHAT?!?!? Joe, the last time I checked, you were an American, pal! This is just another slap at the United States and GRW courtesy of Warren Worthington! I can't BELIEVE you!" [YOUNG]: "While I may be an American, Thompson, I also have strong patriotic feelings. In fact, I consider myself a patriot. And, as Warren is doing nothing more than expressing his personal patriotic beliefs, I applaud him for that. <<Rick says an "oh brother!" under his breath.>> You'd do good to appreciate the man for his skills and beliefs and not be so prejudiced!" You had your worthless entourage ringside for our match when they had no business being there. They are not licensed by the state or the federation and I demand a rematch from Bob Lane based on these grounds." [THOMPSON]: "OH PUHLEASE!" [IAO]: "To Volcano, my erstwhile partner in this bread and circus production of Lane's..." <<Iao looks over at Volcano and turns his body so that he can address him directly.>> "By allying with that Southern filth, Jimmy Lane, you have shown your lack of worth to me as both a wrestler and as a person. You wish to lie with dogs, Volcano, you shall smell like urine in the morning..." <<Volcano begins to charge at Iao Kiao, but the referee withstrains him...>> [IAO]: "And finally, to all the recently arrived tyros who hope to grab their ray of sunshine by competing in this farce I say thusly: live with your station and accept that you are forever to dine on muck while the Personification of World War III shall eternally sit at the head of the table.Toodles and good evening." <<Iao rolls the parchment back up and steps out of the ring, his expression deadpan and his ears seemingly deaf to the rivers of abuse flowing his way as he leaves the ring area...>> [THOMPSON]: "So Volcano faced the unbelievable task of facing a seasoned team of John and Spencer Weyrauch...
