He persued interests of becoming a professional wrestler after he left school but failed, so he decided to enter law. After recieving his law degree he started his own law firm, which became a quick success. Riding out that success, Eric stepped down as the CEO of Forbes and Meyer - his law firm, and decided to renew his interest in the wrestling industry.
After consulting with his professional wrestling friends which he developed during his time where he attempted to enter the industry, he was able to negotiate terms with Home Box Office (HBO) to put together a nationally televised segment under the banner of NAWL, North American
Wrestling League. Eric devoted considerable amounts of funds in an attempt to nationalize his federation, rather than starting on an indy basis. Currently the NAWL tours around the United States, hitting all the major cities.
As it stands, Eric's decision was well timed, and has produced a profit as of late. If current trends continue, the NAWL could be toppling the other major wrestling promotions competing with them for exposure. Of course, that depends upon interest from the fans, and quality wrestlers participating, making the atmosphere well-worked.
2: Guidelines Involvement, consistency, dedication, and general ability are all factors in the NAWL that play a part in deciding match results, storylines, and the overall heat generated by your wrestler. Below is a list and description of the things needed to succeed in the NAWL.
- Flashing. You could consider it the main way in which your character is presented to the rest of the handlers and fans. Each cycle a flashcard is produced entitled "Hype It Up" which details the flashes of all the handlers who submitt their work. If you make it a habit of writing flashes every cycle, whether you have a match or not, you could find yourself being pushed in the NAWL.
Flashes should be of appropriate length. I will assume that you all understand that. They should be ontopic, and be able to be televised to a national audience, which means that they must be suitable. Other than that, the creativity is up to you.
- Stratting. Strategies are also a deciding factor in the outcomes of matches. Those who strat for their matches find that the results are tailored to their likings the majority of the time, and that they end up with more wins in their column. If you'd like to do so, here is a listing of the important notes that should be included in your strategy: Entrance, Spots, Winning Situation, Losing Situation,
Gameplan, Offensive Strategy, Defensive Strategy, Sneak Attacks/Comments/Etc. If you make a habit of including those sections in your strategies, and write strats up for most of your matches you are gaurenteed to get a push at one time or another.
- Angles. Last, but definately not least. Storylines, or angles, develop the heat needed to push your wrestler. Whether you put out the best flashes and strats, if you don't have a storyline to follow, there's no interest, and if there's no interest, there's no air time for your wrestler. Simply put - Storylines are a must, if you don't have them, you don't have anything. If you'd like to set up an angle I suggest you contact either the Owner/Promoter, Mr. Eric Forbes
( [detail withheld] ) or another handler. 3: Telecasts Telecasts, are the events that occur each cycle during the NAWL on our television provider, HBO (Home Box Office). There is not an exact schedule for each week, but rather one for each month. Even though we cannot provide you with a schedule, here is a list of the events that take place:
NAWL Insider Report - The weekly update for the NAWL. It includes talent listings, news and rumors, notes from the president, the schedule for that particular month, and all other pertinent information pertaining to the NAWL. (Occassionally it is accompanied by a few dark matches, wrestled by new employees)
NAWL Hype It Up - It usually occurs six days following the Insider Report, this flash packet includes all the words from the wrestlers who submitt their flashes (to
[detail withheld] ). This is the main way in which your character recieves promotion. All members of the federation are encouraged to flash each cycle.
NAWL To Excess - As each promotion has a weekly event, the NAWL is no different. To Excess is our telecast program featuring all of our matches as discussed by Hype It Up.
NAWL Aftermath - Occuring only on special occassions, when it is needed, Aftermath is a basic flash show for wreslters who wish to convey their feelings about the latest event show. If you send in flashes about the current card's results the following day they will be put out on Aftermath. (NOTE: We would like to make this a permenant addition to the NAWL Telecasts, but we need participation.)
4: Noticeable Figures Eric Forbes (Promoter/Owner) - The head hancho in the NAWL, he is definately a smart one. After being a fan of the industry for many years, becoming a well-respected lawyer, and then starting his own federation, he has learned quite a bit. He knows the ins and outs, and will call you on anything he believes to go against him or his opinions. Obey Mr. Forbes and all will be all right. He can either be your best friend or worst enemy.
Scott Standford (Executive Assistant/Interviewer) - Mr. Standford was hired by Eric in an attempt to create a more close knit federation, by keeping the employees (wrestlers) informed with the upper management. You will often see Mr. Standford prowling around the backstage locker rooms, just looking to get to know the wrestlers, and inform them of news. He is an interviewer of sorts, but also Eric's
Executive Assistant. Jake Wilson (Broadcaster) - Announcing the NAWL's main show, To Excess, Mr. Wilson is a strange individual. He often has a lot to say, and the majority of it doesn't make a whole lot of sense. The reason he was hired? No one's quite sure, but they say that he adds some "color" to the show.
Roland Mills (Broadcaster) - Accompanying Jake, Mr. Mills is the more stable of the two. He has a more definate knowledge of wreslting, and has a stable personality which comes in handy when Jake goes off of the deep end.
Julietta Ozanic (Ring Announcer) - A beautiful woman with an even more beautiful voice. A former Opera singer, Miss Julietta Ozanic was persuaded by Eric to announce To
Excesses matches. Dave Fernandez (Referee) - The head official, and the only one at that. A foreigner, but a hard worker. He is somewhat of a pick-and-choose type of guy. Much like the
President, if you're on his good side - you've got a good count, but if you just don't go over with him, then you'll have a hard time in the ring.
5: Championships As it currently stands, the NAWL approves of three titles. More will be added if necessary. Such as a Skyweight Chamionship.
NAWL North American Championship - Singles Division, open weight class. Number one ranked belt in its respective division. NAWL Tag Team Championships - Tag Team Division, open weight class. Number one ranked belt in its respective division. Remains inactive until a Tag Team Division is developed.
NAWL Heritage Chamionship - Singles Division, open weight class. Number two ranked belt in its respective division.
