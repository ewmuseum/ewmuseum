A February 2001 flash-and-strategy federation staged as a lawyer-turned-promoter's HBO-televised league, recruiting through four days of daily handbook postings. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The North American Wrestling League staged its own founding as a lawyer's second act: its handbook, posted 3 February 2001, casts owner and promoter Eric Forbes as a former amateur wrestler turned attorney who stepped down as CEO of his own law firm, Forbes and Meyer, to return to the sport, using his connections to negotiate a nationally televised deal with Home Box Office rather than starting the NAWL on an independent basis. Its handbook staffed the promotion with a full on-air cast around him — Executive Assistant Scott Standford, broadcasters Jake Wilson and Roland Mills, ring announcer and former opera singer Julietta Ozanic, and head referee Dave Fernandez, described as a hard worker who called matches to his own liking.

The league's own programming ran across four cycles: a weekly Insider Report newsletter, a Hype It Up flash packet, its main televised show To Excess, and an occasional post-show flash program, Aftermath, for wrestlers who wanted the last word on a card's own results. Three championships were approved at launch — the North American Championship as its top singles title, a Tag Team Championship held in reserve until a tag division developed, and a secondary Heritage Championship — with a further Skyweight Championship floated as a future addition.

Its recruiting drive ran four straight days, 3 through 8 February 2001, reposting the same handbook and application with a new introduction each time and adding, on the fourth day, a call for introductory flash segments ahead of a planned banquet. All four posts came from its own fedhead, who by the final day was reduced to signing off with a plain “=)”

Those four posts, across less than a week, are everything that survives of the North American Wrestling League. No card, roster, or result survives to say whether its HBO ambitions were ever realized, or how the league's run ended.
