OOC STUFF First off, I would like to thank everyone who has taken interest in this event and given me the opportunity to work with you on a different level then you may be accustom too. It means a lot to me that you trust your characters in my hands and my only wish is that I could be in the thick of things competing alongside of you all.
Nevertheless, running a show such is this is something I’ve thought about for along while now and have finally decided to kick into gear. So far, things are stacking up better then I initially imagined and that is always a good sign.
For those of you who don't know me, my name is [name withheld], most simply call me Guzz as they are lazy and don't feel like typing or trying to spell my entire last name. I've been involved in e-wrestling since early 96 and have been in numerous feds and still find myself in numerous feds for some reason. Currently, I handle
Eugene Robinson, Brazen Haiden, the Walker Family Tree, and DOA when [name withheld] resurfaces from his hiding place. Anyway, enough of that... The purpose of this event is, as it should always be, to have fun. I know that thesis statement gets tossed around a lot as do the rest of you but that is our primary goal and it will remain that way.
Obviously, knowing most of you quite well, I realize we are all competitive by nature but I hope you maintain this trait in a mature and professional matter.
Now, onto business. The focal point of this project is the tournament at this stage. It is the pride and joy of the show, featuring eight tremendous handlers who I am sure will put on quite a show. I am doing my best to build several grudge/dream feuds around these to add some additional interest and product to the show so if anyone knows of people who might be interested in competing in some form or another please point them in my direction. More so, if you know of two people that would like to settle some sort of feud or just have the desire to work together, as long as they can leave the politics at the door, then feel free to point them in my direction as well.
Now, for the tournament brackets: ROUND ONE: LUKE KINSEY vs. To Be Determined/Waiting on Response EDDIE JACOBS vs. "BAD EYE" MCBAINE ADAM ROGERS vs. MADISON J. VALENTINE DEVON CASE vs. ANDREW TUCKER For those interested, the tournament was set up by agreement of both parties involved in the first round matches. Meaning, these were matches that both sides wanted and/or I proposed the idea to them and they liked it enough that they decided to sign up.
2. DEADLINES As of *this* moment, the first RP packet, fittingly entitled "The Shooting Gallery," will air October 27, 2001 which means ALL RPS ARE DUE FRIDAY, OCTOBER 26, 2001 AT MIDNIGHT PACIFIC TIME. Shows will air one week apart as long as we stay on schedule.
The show itself, a ONE NIGHT EVENT, will air November 24, 2001. Strats will be due probably the same time the THIRD packet is due so those are in and out of the way. The event will be held in the
Philadelphia Civic Center in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. 3. CLOSING I will be running a string of promos that will air either in the Shooting Gallery packets or be sent separately, pending on the structure of the piece, that will build up the event itself and possibly add some background information from an In-Character perspective as to how the event came to be.
Again, if anyone [or any fed] would like to take part in this event email me at AIM: [detail withheld] or email me at
[detail withheld] For those involved, send me an application for your character [can be copy and paste from any fed you’re in as long as it includes an adequate amount of information (stats, move list, etc)].
