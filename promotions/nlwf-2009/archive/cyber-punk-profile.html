Roster profile: Cyber Punk

c. 14 April 2009

Full Name: Cyber Punk Specialty Match: Ladder Match Stats: 6'0", 220lbs. Hometown: Washington D.C Alignment: Face Manager: None Finisher: Cyber Death Driver(A Spike Piledriver from the top or middle rope into the ground below) The End(A Pedigree)
Entrance Theme: Thousand Foot Krutch - Phenomenon Achievements: NLWF Heavyweight Champion (1x) CW Heavyweight Champion (8X) CW World Tag-Team Champion (3X) CW Extreme Champion (4X) CW Triple Crown Champion (3X) JWF Pure Wrestling Champion (1x) JWF intercontinental Champion (1x) JWF Undisputed Heavyweight Champion (1x) AWA Triple A Champion (1x) AWA West Coast Champion (1x) AWA Hardcore Champion (4x) Biography: Cyber Punk loves professional wrestling, but not because it is fun, because he loves taking everything to a new extreme! Cyber Punk loves sending the fans home thinking about his match and his match only, he's a real show stealer and that is why he brings his video camera out to all of his matches, so he can set it up and have his own personal tape of the show. Cyber Punk started in an Indy fed called Cyber Wrestling where he went by the name Shadow Deviant. Cyber Punk changed his name immediately after becoming Cyber Wrestling World Champion and the rest is history. Cyber Punk had many great matches in CW and became there greatest and best kept secret. Cyber Punk pretty much dominated CW becoming an 8 Time World Champion and headlining every PPV for an entire year as the off again on again champion. 3 Time CW Tag-Team
Champion with three different partners. 4 time Extreme Champion all in his rookie year with CW. Cyber Punk also won Icon of the Year because of his phenomenal 8 World Title, 1 Tag-Team Title year and was the first and only man entered into the Cyber Wrestling Hall of Fame when the company finally ended. Cyber Punk then tried to become a JWF superstar but was rejected by the company in March of 2007. Cyber Punk moved from wrestling to wrestling never really settling anywhere he went, Cyber Punk received and Email from the JWF when it lost all it’s wrestler and was struggling to stay alive. Cyber Punk returned to the company with the goal in mind to make them regret not signing him earlier in the year. Cyber Punk started Cyber Punk TV in the JWF and it instantly became a huge sensation pulling in the highest ratings for any segment in JWF history! Cyber Punk won the JWF Intercontinental
Championship and was one of the best V2 wrestlers to be in the JWF until the ended for a second time. Cyber Punk was finished with wrestling, signing up to only BLWA for half a second until he left because of the insults being thrown his way by there disrespectful wrestlers. Cyber Punk constantly went to JWF headquarters just to check if maybe they would ever make a return, they finally did! Cyber Punk forfeited his JWF Intercontinental Championship for a shot at the first ever JWF Undisputed Heavyweight Championship in which he won the match.
