A fantasy federation on the fwrestling.com hub that began with Direct Hit on 17 January 2009 and split into two brands, Direct Hit and Legacy, in March, with pay-per-views from New Era to Simple Survival, a Hall of Fame, fourteen titles, and a one-year show on 18 January 2010. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The No Limit Wrestling Federation's own pages describe a company of two television brands. Direct Hit, the East, opened on 17 January 2009 with a first round of a Tournament to the Title that Cyber Punk won on 1 February, only to be named number one contender while Tails walked out as the first World champion; Legacy, the West, was split off from it after the New Era pay-per-view of 1 March and a draft, and by April was run by general managers Bert Hales and C.W. Gibson, then by Justin Kash and Francesco Rossi. Ramirez Silva is the owner on the early shows and Carmine Vestieri, first a commissioner and the voice of the East, takes Direct Hit over as the spring goes on.

Thirty of its shows survive, sixteen Direct Hits, six Legacys and eight pay-per-views, from Direct Hit 1 to Simple Survival on 28 June 2009. Johnny Styles was inducted into its Hall of Fame on 22 March and retired at War Games, where Brenton Cyrus's Live or Die match ended with Alex Mark turning on his father Styles; Joe Santiago, who beat Cyber Punk there for the World title, was inducted on 23 May. Cyrus founded Salvation, won the first King of the Throne on 25 April and by the end of the month he and his followers held every East belt; he was traded to Legacy on 6 June, came back as a Direct Hit superstar on 20 June, and was still champion at Simple Survival.

Its own champions page ends on 30 June 2009 with a Universal title for each brand, a World Heavyweight title, North American, Junior Heavyweight, No Limit, two tag, Freedom, Annihilation, Proving Grounds and Women's belts. Two pages of a later run, Revolution, survive: a December 2009 news post and a one-year show on 18 January 2010 in which Jason Hawk won the Undisputed title with a pin on the champion, The Notorious K.I.D., and Ryan Apollo was named tag champion by default. Nothing later survives in this collection.
