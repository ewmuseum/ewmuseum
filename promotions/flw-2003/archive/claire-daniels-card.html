Biography

Claire has always been into some form of action. She joined a karate class at twelve and stayed in it until she turned seventeen, and after a difficult family stretch and a wrestling school in Nevada, quickly advanced and found a job with another federation, becoming one of its most successful wrestlers regardless of gender. When things didn't work out there after two years, she moved on. With her best friend, fellow wrestler, and the love of her life Matt Anderson, she came to FL, and the fate of her abilities is now in her own hands.
