House Show? Posted Monday, March 3, 2003 by Tommy Gaylord [detail withheld]
I caught up with Vice President Bookout earlier today and asked him about a house show that he had mentioned backstage and this is what he had to say.

"Forgotten-Legends Wrestling will have a house show as soon as we have 7 to 8 wrestlers. I want this to be fun for everyone and I'm sure there not having fun waiting for us to get a big roster so I have decided to put together a small house show, nothing fancy. As of right now we have the roster count of about 6, I would like to have a few more wrestlers before continuing with the house show and at the first event, I'll have an announcment to make, a BIG announcment."

I was told that the date for the house show would be released within the week and what is the big announcement, stay tuned to be kept updated.

FLW signs with RS instead of PTC? Posted Monday, March 3, 2003 by Tommy Gaylord [detail withheld]
FLW has waited and waited for PTC but we wait no longer. FLW has signed with Revolution Studios,

http://revolutionstudios.org/index2.shtml

But before FLW can get started, we need wrestlers. We need wrestlers with talent. Send in an application, if your good enough for FLW then you'll be accepted, of course. We want dedication from everyone who joins so that means if you don't have dedication then you don't have the time for FLW.

FLW signs with PTC! Posted Monday, February 24, 2003 by News Desk [detail withheld]
Forgotten-Legends Wrestling has its very own spot in the Primetime Central. www.primetimecentral.net. This is great news for FLW and it will help us make a bigger name for ourselves. PTC has lots of great talent and great federations and it just added another great federation to its community. FLW may only have the roster count of three of four but that doesn't matter. The president and vice president of FLW are working hard to get things completed and are in search of good talent. If any of you out there think your good enough to be a Legend then feel free to send us an application but only of you have talent!
