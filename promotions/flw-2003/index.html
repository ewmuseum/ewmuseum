Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Forgotten.LEGENDS — FLW to its own newsdesk — was a website federation hosted at fl.animebeta.com. Its earliest surviving post, of 24 February 2003, announces a spot in the Primetime Central network with a roster already numbering three or four, and calls for handlers to apply for an original character.

Through early March its own newsdesk tracked the roster's growth week by week — six wrestlers, then seven, then eight, adding Raven Hawk, Deleria, Snake Eyes, Luscious Lohse, Petrol, Matt Anderson, Cameron Steel and Trent Reznor by name — alongside a set of house-show rules and a plan to run its first event once it reached seven or eight wrestlers, with a promised “BIG announcment” to follow.

By 3 March 2003 its own newsdesk reported a change of course: Forgotten.LEGENDS had signed with Revolution Studios instead of Primetime Central, and was still calling for talented handlers to apply. Its last dated post, two days later, booked Trent Reznor against Cameron Steel for a card still to come.

No show, match or roleplay is known to have actually been staged in the surviving record. How Forgotten.LEGENDS' own story ended isn't in what survives.
