SWF Join Now (Thursday Night Fallout)

15 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is our current roster: Dude Love, Steve Austin,Taz ,Chris Benoit, Raven B.Hart, Sting, Kevin Nash, Hulk Hogan, Shawn Micheals, HHH Davey Boy Smith, VADER, Ric Flair, Curt Hennig, Undertaker, Ultamite Warrior. Here is what our most recent card looked like: Tonights Matchs: Sabu vs Ric Flair for SWF World Title Randy Savage vs. Vader for SWF U.S. TItle HHH vs. Taz for the SWF TV Title Davey Boy Smith vs. Dude Love Curt Hennig vs. Tommy Dreamer Chris Benoit vs. Raven Bret Hart vs. Steve Austin Sting vs. Shawn Micheals Jim Ross: Welcome to Thursday Night Fallout, i'm joined by my collegue, Bobby "the Brain" Heenan. Welcome Brain. Bobby Heenan: The pleasure is all mine (Rolls his Eyes) Ross: Lets go to our first match. Match 1: Curt Hennig vs. Tommy Dreamer Ross: This match started off slowly, both men trying to feel the other one out. Dreamer looked to make a name for himself in the SWF, Curt showing why he is one of the top stars to sign with the SWF. Dreamer showed alot of power and strength, over powering Hennig a couple times during the match. Dreamer hit Henning with a vicous clothesline...but didnt cover him, he started showboating to the crowd...the crowd cheered, but while he was doing this Hennig got up. Hennig hit Dreamer with bulldog, followed up by a suplex. Dreamer was definetly in trouble. Hennig went for a figure four, but Dreamer reversed it. Hennig screamed in pain. Dreamer released the hold, and pulled Hennig over to the corner of the ring. He flings Hennig into the other corner, following a fews seconds back, Hennig lifts his leg and kicks Dreamer right in the face!! Hennig goes to cover Dreamer, Dreamer kicks out. Dreamer rolls Hennig into a cradle...Hennig reverses, legs on the rope, he's got it 1, 2, 3......(ding, ding) Winner: Curt Hennig @ 12:32 via pinfall. Ross: What a first match Brain! Heenan: Ya, great, i knew that Perfect would pull through and beat that moron Tommy Dreamer. You know why they call him that dont you Ross. Ross: Why's that Brain? Heenan: Because he's DREAMING cuzhe thinks he's really a wrestler.!! Ross: Good grief Bobby, i dont think you better let him here you say that! Lets go to the next match! Match 2: Davey Boy Smith Vs. Dude Love Ross: Both men came to the ring with a very determined look in their eyes...Smith wanted to avenge his elimination from the Battle Royal, and Love is just psyco! Smith totally dominated this match, showing a wide arsenal of manuevers, Love really had no chance in this match. Winner: Davey Boy Smith pinned DUDE LOVE after a crucifix in 0:02:06. Heenan: Smith is a monster! This guy definetly has what it takes to go places in this organazation...Love, take some notes, um better let your mom do it , we wouldnt want you walking around with sharp objects. Ross: Your right Bobby, Smith has got to be one of the top wrestlers in the SWF, he should be getting some title shots in the future. Lets go to ringside. For the vacant SWF T.V. TITLE: Match 3: HHH vs. Taz all i can say is wow! both men wanted to take that gold belt home with them. Taz showed why he is one of the most feared wrestlers in the world, and HHH showed why many consider him the finest tacticion in wrestling today. Both men matched move for move. HHH overpowered Taz for a good 5 minutes before Taz woke up, then Taz took it to HHH like HHH has never seen before.....Taz hooked a devisting manuever to HHH that we here in the SWF Have never seen before, HHH screamed in pain, and finally submitted... Winner: Taz defeated Hunter Hearst Helmsley when the referee stopped the match in 0:10:36. (Taz won the SWF T.V. TITLE!!!!!) Heenan: What the hell was that move? Ross: I have no idea Bobby, 20 years in this business, and thats the first time i've ever seen that! Heenan: Unbelievable, thats all i can say. Match 4: Raven vs. Chris Benoit Ross: One of the best matchs tonight, both men were poised to make a jump in the ratings. Of course only one man could win. Raven dominated the first 9 minutes of this match, with ariel manuevers, and total domination. He looked to have Benoit pinned after an inverted DDT, but Benoit kicked out at a very close 2 and 3/4 count. Benoit got a burst of energy and began taking it to Raven. Raven, feeling that the match was getting out of his control, jumped out of the ring, to slow down the tempo of the match. This back fired on him. Benoit hurled himself over the top rope, slamming into a shocked Raven. Benoit continued to pummel Raven outside of the ring. Finally he threw him back in the ring......... Winner: Chris Benoit made Raven submit to the Crippler Crossface in 0:17:58. Heenan: Another fine example of the great wrestlers here in the SWF Jim, i mean, either one of those guys could be US Champ here, and speaking of Champs, check out this next match! Match 5: Bret Hart vs. Steve Austin A marquee matchup in any league, a Main Event in most. Here in the SWF it's a midcarder because of the great talent pool that the SWF has. Hatred filled the eyes of both men as they glared at each other. The bell rang and all hell broke loose. Austin, as brutal as ever, dominated this match from beginning to end. But of course when talking about Bret Hart, knowing that he is the "Excellence of Execution" and that he is the " Best their is, The best their ever was, and The best their ever will be" Then you know that it only takes one manuever for a victory. The match ended when Hart attempted to body slam Austin, Austin slipped out of Harts grasp, and Austin hit Bret with the Stone Cold Stunner. Winner: Steve Austin pinned Bret Hart with the Stone Cold Stunner in 0:22:34. Ross: That guy right their is a future world champ, bar none! Heenan: Thats if the guy stays healthy Ross, he has a bad neck. Ross: Thats true, Brain, lets go to our next match. Match 6: Sting v. Shawn Micheals It was a battle of the guy that wont saying anything, and the guy that wont shut up! Micheals looked awesome tonight, Sting looked a little rusty in the ring. The crowd was confused on who to cheer for, both men got big pops when they came to ringside. Not alot went down in this match, both men looked disinterested in the whole thing. Winner:Shawn Michaels pinned Sting after a piledriver in 0:20:10. Heenan: Both these guys are World Title caliber wrestlers, and i think their both ticked that they didnt get a shot at any of the titles. Ross: That very well could be, Bobby, but they''ll have to climb the later like everyone else. Lets go back down to ringside. For Vacant U.S. Title: Match 7: Randy Savage vs. Vader Ross: Wow, what a match! Twist and turns, Vader pounding Savage, Savage pounding Vader, it was a seesaw match from the beginning. Vader owned Savage from the beginning of the match, matching move for move Savage's attempts of defense. Savage finally tired of being beaten so badly, jumped outside of the ring. Vader followed him out. Savage took a chair a smashed it into Vaders head, Vader fell. The Ref warned Savage, Savage just laughed. Savage jumped back outside, kicked Vader in the stomach, 3 times. The 4th attempt backfired, Vader caught Savages foot, twisted him around, and dropped him on his butt on the concrete!! Savage was definatley in some pain, and trouble. Vader body slammed the former WCW/WWF world champ a couple of times on the concrete floor. Vader jumped back in to break the ref's count, then went back outside. As he was attempting to Vader bomb Savage, Tommy Dreamer came to ringside, and hit Vader with what appeared to be a bag of bricks. While this was happening, Elizabeth had the ref's attention. Vader went down hard. Dreamer, tossed Vader back into the ring, and Savage got back into the ring also. Savage climbed the top rope, and came down with the pattened Flying Elbow. Winner: Randy Savage pinned Vader after Flying Elbow drop @ 34:38. (Savage wins US Title) Ross: Vader is still in the ring, he's not moving. Heenan: What was that in the bag that Dreamer had? Ross: I dont know, looked to be a bag of bricks or something. Heenan: Here comes the strectcher, someone is gonna pay for this Ross. Ross: Thats for sure Brain, Gene Okerland is standing at ringside, Gene, whats the word? Gene Okerland: Well, i have with me, well known Dr. Micheal Zahorian, Whats the problem? Zahorian: At this time we are not sure, we will be taking exrays as soon as possible. Okerland: you heard it Jim, more later, back to you. Ross: Wow, well, its time for our Main Event...lets go to ringside. MAIN EVENT For the SWF World Title Match 8: Ric Flair vs. Sabu Ross:Sabu came to the ring where he was treated to a mixed reaction from the crowd, Then Flair came out, and got a standing ovation. This angered the World Champ, and he went crazy on Flair. Flair, jumped out of the ring, so he could slow Sabu down. Flair got back into the ring, where Sabu attacked him again. Flair got a finger to the eye Sabu, and that slowed the Champ down. Flair bounced Sabu off the ropes, and into a sleeper. Sabu was almost unconcious, when the lights went out. Heenan: What the hell, i cant see anything, i'm outta here. Ross: Someone get the lights back on. The Lights come back on. Ross: what the...both men are uncouncious.!! Wait, Sabu is getting up...he covers Flair...1...2...3!!!!! Winner: Sabu over Flair 21:42 by pinfall. Ross: What happend? Heenan: How am i supp....... Voice: That was just a taste of what is to come....The Phenom is here....you all shall bow....to the Undertaker. (Crowd goes Crazy!!!!) Ross: Oh my god!!! Heenan: That Nutcase is here, i'm outta here! Ross: Folks we;re outta time, join us this Monday Night, till then this is Jim Ross for Bobby The Brain Heenan, have a good night! all rights reserved SWF Copyright Email me if you'd like to join
