A September 1997 real-wrestler simulator SWF whose one surviving card crowns champions in three self-copyrighted title matches, one of at least six 1997 federations sharing the bare acronym SWF. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This SWF's one surviving document is a full simulated card, “Thursday Night Fallout,” dated 15 September 1997 and called by Jim Ross and Bobby Heenan, decided among a roster of real-world wrestlers: Dude Love, Steve Austin, Taz, Chris Benoit, Raven, Bret Hart, Sting, Kevin Nash, Hulk Hogan, Shawn Michaels, Triple H, Davey Boy Smith, Vader, Ric Flair, Curt Hennig, the Undertaker and the Ultimate Warrior.

Curt Hennig opened the card by pinning Tommy Dreamer; Davey Boy Smith followed by pinning Dude Love. Taz won the vacant SWF Television Title when the referee stopped his match against Triple H, and Chris Benoit made Raven submit to the Crippler Crossface. Steve Austin beat Bret Hart with the Stone Cold Stunner, and Shawn Michaels pinned Sting with a piledriver.

Randy Savage won the United States Title from Vader after Tommy Dreamer ran in and struck Vader with, in the announcers' own words, “a bag of bricks” — Vader was carried out on a stretcher, a ringside doctor unable to say more than that X-rays were coming. In the main event, for the SWF World Title, the arena lights cut out mid-match; when they came back on, Sabu had already pinned Ric Flair. The broadcast ended with a voice promising the arrival of “the Phenom… the Undertaker.”

No further document of this SWF survives. Who won the card's remaining matches, and what its next show looked like, is not recorded.
