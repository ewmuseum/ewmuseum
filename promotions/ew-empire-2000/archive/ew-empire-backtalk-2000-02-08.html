Empire Wrestling (EW) - Backtalk February 8, 2000 www.nemesis-productions.com/empirewrestling/ ========== Dean Williams ========== [It's a beautiful at the park in Atlanta, Ga. The sun is shining brightly, children are frolicking to and from, and old couples are sitting peacefully on benches, feeding breadcrumbs to ducks on the pond. The camera continues to pan from left to right, taking in all the scenes of tranquillity, until it comes face to face with a man that has been the topic of a lot of controversy lately in EW, Dean Williams. He, like everyone else here, seems to be enjoying himself. He sits by himself on a bench underneath a large oak tree, chuckling softly to himself as he reads the paper in the shade. He looks up from his reading to acknowledge the presence of the camera and stands. A slight breeze passes through, rustling some leaves and blowing
Dean's long hair back. He stares at the camera for a minute and then looks up towards the sky. He moves his hand in front of his face as if he were reading something, as he begins to speak.]
Dean: Headline: Dean Williams Wins Empire Wrestling Primary. [He moves his hand down as if he was looking at a set of lower text and continues.] Dean: Does this kid have what it takes to go to the top? Winnipeg, Manitoba,
Canada [AP]- Every once in a great while, an athlete comes along that makes those around him sit up and take notice. An athlete who possess such skill, that no matter what the odds, they can't be held back.
[People are beginning to notice the camera, and more importantly, the man who appears to be reading from thin air, and a small crowd begins to gather.]
Dean: Football had Joe Montana, hockey had Wayne Gretzky, and basketball had
Michael Jordan. And now, the sport of professional wrestling may have their answer for these men. He is the self proclaimed "Great One," Dean Williams.
[He smiles and takes off his glasses as he continues.] Dean: Employed by wrestling mogul Edward Brock, CEO of the number one wrestling promotion in the world, Empire Wrestling, Dean Williams is quickly taking advantage of Mr. Brock's forum to present himself not only as an outstanding athlete, but also as a stellar showman. "The people can't get enough of the great one," says Williams. "As long as the fans have a desire to be entertained, there will be a Dean Williams."
[The crowd is starting to increase in size. Nothing attracts a crowd like a crowd. Some people are beginning to realize who the man in the dark shades and the black shirt with red dragons emblazoned on it is and are beginning to "mark out."]
Dean: And the people definitely have a desire to be entertained. Empire
Wrestling's two weekly television shows, Showdown and Backtalk, are consistently beating out other more established shows in the ratings. When asked what he attributed EW's current trend of success to, Williams' reply was "...you're looking at it..." He just maybe right.
[A small child runs up and tries to ask Dean for his autograph, but Dean holds up a hand without looking away, and the child stops dead in it's tracks.]
Dean: Dean Williams is among the top sellers in merchandise and memorabilia at arenas nationwide and over the internet. Looking for the "Dean Williams is a Bad Man" t-shirt for you son or daughter? Good luck. They're usually among the first to sell out. Not surprisingly, other wrestlers in the federation aren't looking too kindly on the success that their co-worker is met with. "They're simply jealous," says Williams. "The people appreciate talent, something that is severely lacking in Empire Wrestling, and that's what I bring to the table each and every night. I don't need a gimmick or a snappy catch phrase to get over with the fans. I go out there week after week and give them the real me. I don't need a belt to let me know that I'm the best. I just do what I do and obviously, that's enough." Obviously, he's right. In the past 24 hours Dean Williams has fielded a gamut of challenges from every level of talent within the federation. From newcomers to former teammates to established veterans. Even the world champion has thrown his hat in the ring to get a piece of "the great one." "Curtain jerker's, j-brones, it doesn't matter. They all know that Dean Williams is the standard bearer, the high water mark in Empire Wrestling, so, naturally they want to get in the ring with me. Even Aron Rave, the newly crowned world champion, doesn't feel right about having the belt without facing me. It's sad really, but to quote a great, "to be the man, whooo, you have to beat the man."
[He holds up four fingers as he speaks his last line. By now the crowd is huge. They're whispering amongst themselves, half believing he's crazy, the others brandishing notepads and pens, waiting for their chance to approach their hero and obtain an autograph that's sure to be worth millions.]
Dean: How will these challenges be handled? That remains to be seen, but rest assured that Dean Williams will face all of them without fear in his heart. Last month at the pay per view spectacular "Self Destruct," Dean
Williams participated in what may go down as the greatest match in the history of this sport, "The Stairway to Hell," where he "single handily"
took on three challengers in a fifty foot cage. After an astounding fall from the top of the cage, a staggering fifty feet, twenty of those feet being comprised of wooden tables, Williams somehow managed to climb back into the cage and fight the good fight. Fear is certainly not a word in his vocabulary. "Am i scared of anyone in Empire Wrestling? Ha, yeah, I'm scared of what they do with each other in their free time, but when I get in the ring it's all business." Dean Williams. Surely his rise will continue and this is probably only the first of many interviews he'll be giving in the near future. "You've had enough greatness for today..."
[Dean puts his glasses back on his face as the crowd cheers his unusual interview.] Dean: Look for that article in a paper near you soon. [He bows his head and throws his arms out to the side as the crowd continues to cheer. The camera fades out to a commercial for the Dean Williams action figure with kung fu grip and patented "ass-kicking" action as he is mobbed by the crowd.]
