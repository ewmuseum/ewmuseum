Empire Wrestling (EW) - Backtalk January 14, 2000 -------------------------------- ======= Psicopa ======= [Scene fades in to a city street at night time. The streets are empty except for sporadic cards zooming past on the road. Strategically placed lights cover the street in weak and discolored light. As the camera moves along the street it becomes clear that this is an industrial area as several construction sites can be clearly seen. The camera stops outside a construction site and the camera focuses on a shady looking character. As the shadowed man raises his face, we see that it's EW personality, Psicopa - dressed in completely black street clothes for some reason.]
Psicopa [speaking quietly - almost in whispers]: Hey there folks, your friendly neighborhood luchador, Psicopa, here to entertain and amaze once again. Well, seems I've not got an upcoming match to talk about, so I'll do what a lot of people do and talk about the past - while I just wait here for a moment.
[Psicopa anxiously looks around.] Psicopa: Well, seems that for the past two weeks I've been screwed over by the Dojo. Firstly, I get that mother humpin', side-swivellin', root beer chuggin', varsity jacket wearin', hair bleachin'... mother humper CJ Swift, so beat that he don't know what direction the flying cows are migrating this summer and then just when I get that big pin on him, in comes Dojo Jojo in the ninja suit to rip me out of a win. I'm not very happy about that.
[Psicopa takes another quick look around, as if expecting something... or someone to appear.]
Psicopa: Then, as if that wasn't enough... they come in and swindle me again on Showdown. I had Wean Dilliams down and out for the count when in come the
Dojo brothers and BAM! I lose another one. I tell you what, if those yellow bellied, lilly-livered, ninja suit wearing, sushi eating, martial art kicking Dojo dopes are going to screw me out of wins and think they're going to get away with calling themselves cowboys... well, they have another thing coming.
[When did they start calling themselves cowboys.] Psicopa: Hey, don't start! Luckily, we have that killer of a match coming up, here pardner. A real DOOZY... yee haw.
[Why're you talking like that?] Psicopa: Ah'm a cowboy there, mister. Anywho... back to the match at hand. The first ever "Stairway to Hell" match. Mah chance to show the world that
AH AM the hardcore luchador... arrrr, matey.
[Oh no, not this again. His moods are just bizarre, someone help this man. As if on cue that X show's theme once again begins to play and that Muddled guy pops into screen next to Psi.]
Psicopa: Arr... mates... arrr... this match be the reason I be here. I be here to be helpin' the Empire Wrestling backstage staff... arrr... I'm out here to pick up materials... arrr.
Muddled: Could it be that the spirit of a deceased pirate has inhabited the body of this professional wrestler because it seemed better than the after life? Or could this somehow be linked to aliens... that'd be cool!
Psicopa: Arr, matey, how do ye do that? Muddled: How indeed... one of the many mysteries of man kind... [With that, Psi sneaks into an alleyway and sounds of a man changing can be heard before Psi sneaks back into view now wearing his newly aquired chicken suit, a pirate patch and a pirate hat.]
Psicopa: Arr... mates, I be wearin a suit of a chicken so thar be no one recognizing me... arr.
[With that, Psi runs into the construction site, followed closely by Muddled who's still chattering about "Unsolved Mysteries" and such. A few crashes and clangs are heard before a loud alarm starts blaring. Psi charges through the fence with several metal poles over his shoulder, followed closely by
Muddled, who doesn't seem to notice the sound at all.]
Psicopa: Arr... they're after me pot of gold! [With that, Psi runs off down the street, chased by Muddled as we fade to
Jolt... hella cool.]
================== Maniac Mike Gorman ================== [Maniac Mike Gorman is working out hard in his Las Vegas gym with him girlfriend SUNSET STRIP. Gorman is wearing a black DOKKEN t-shirt with black sweats. Gorman takes a break from his intense workout, sips a sports drink and motions over the EW cameraman]
Maniac Mike Gorman: Come over. Don't be scared. You see, most of the time, I am a very reasonable man. Intelligent. Smart. witty. Fun to be around. And then sometimes, I just SNAP. What makes Maniac Mike Gorman a maniac? Lead singers who sing out of key. Country music. Being looked down upon by an entire federation.
You see. Empire Wrestling thinks of Maniac Mike Gorman as there crazy old uncle who has come to visit for a little bit. They figure, if they ignore him long enough, maybe he will just go away.
I've got news for all you EW wrestlers. I ain't going ANYWHERE. [Gorman sets down his sports drink and starts clutching his forehead] Gorman: No. Yes. [Gorman pauses. Picks up his drink again and continues his interview.] Gorman: I've whipped two of Empire's superstars so far, and didn't even break a sweat. I've enthralled the fans with my incredible guitar playing.
But do I get any respect here. No. Now I'm a reasonable man, until it's unreasonable to use reason. And it's reached that point. Kronus. you've got something I want. No, it's not the TV title. I want to break your spirit. If I attain the TV title in doing so. Then good.
You've never been in a match with someone capable of violence like I am. I admit that you're a highly skilled wrestler. Well, so am I, and I'm willing to do ANYTHING to cause you pain.
Chew on that for awhile. For a wrestler, you're OK. For a titleholder, you're DESPICIBLE. [Gorman suddenly shoves the EW cameraman. He again grabs at his head and moans audibly. SUNSET STRIP runs to his side and helps him off camera..]

============ Blake Bryant ============ [The scene opens focused on the several bright stars that fill the night sky. Almost as a cue, the screen begins to find it's way to the geographic horizon of the present scene. Suddenly, the image of fairly small buildings comes into view and as the scan continues the viewers realize the cameraman is standing on a dark street corner in some small town. Light poles line the concrete roadway that cuts through the center of the town, giving off little to no visibility to the surrounding details. However, through this little light the on-looking fans can see a slight drizzle falling down from the heavens above. Just enough water to make the streets slick and enough noise to fill in the silent standstill.]
[Suddenly, a small commotion is heard from across the street, at which the camera is shifted quickly with a jolt. The screen comes rest upon a bundle of shadows where behind the darkness an obvious movement can be noticed.
This movement soon turns to a dark outline image, as the being steps out onto the curb and then make his way across the street. The beams of light from the street lamps give some glimpses of the man's face, but not enough for the viewers to recognize him. Within seconds, their suspicion is put to rest, as the face of Blake Bryant appears from out of the lurking shadows.
His clothing is not noticeable in detail due the remaining darkness, but the fans can make an assumption that he is wearing a light jacket. Bryant has what appears to be a small grin crossing his lips, as he shifts his eyes in all directions, peering at the surrounding environment.]
Blake Bryant: I guess I missed this. The long road trips. Well, now it's all first class plane rides. But how about the third rate hotel rooms with spring loose bed mattresses? Yea, you're right. It's the First Class Brass
Frames now. I bet with those details, the world would assume instantly that everything I used to know is gone. It's definitely not the same folks, BUT there is one thing that combines Blake Bryant of the Eighties with Blake
Bryant of the New Millenium and that's why I'm here. It's the competition.
It's Ten P.M. in the outskirts of Augusta, Maine where just around twenty four hours ago hundreds of people crowded into what would quickly become a tight squeezed arena to watch one of the grandest wrestling organizations in the world today put on a spectacular show. It just so happens, Blake Bryant was one of the lucky in attendance and what I saw last night is hard for me to explain in words. I'll say I was impressed with the talent that walked into the ring, but that's a different story for the talent that was displayed. Did I have a brain lapse or was their actually paint ball guns brought to the ring? No, things aren't exactly like they used to be, but I'm counting on myself to make competition the biggest factor of my stay in
Empire Wrestling. That's something nothing can disrupt. It's something I control from within. Competition is the attribute that will put Blake Bryant on the map once again.
[Bryant ends his sentence in a humble, yet serious tone, as he gives off a small shiver from the coldness of the drizzling rain. The EW superstar then glances down the long dark street to his left. A bright light suddenly covers his face, forcing Bryant to squint into the enlightened circle around his body. For the time being, his clothing apparel is displayed to the fans, but they only catch a quick glimpse of his black and white jump suit before the camera shifts to view an oncoming car. The scene continues as the two curious men stand on the street corner watching the automobile slowly ride passed their stationary location and back into the darkness. When the screen returns to the face of Blake Bryant he is mildly rubbing his eyes with his left hand, trying to recuperate from the blinding bright light.]
Blake Bryant: Damn, I thought that was the limo. Yea, that's what established socials like myself ride around in these days too. I know, it leaves nothing to complain about. But I didn't say I was here to complain, I said I was here to compete. I took my first step with that notion last night. Ryan Fury, I hope you don't think I came to Augusta, Maine to see a good show. That's hardly the case. I've watched, participated in and created
"good" shows. No, I came to Augusta for one reason and one reason alone and that's to see you mop the floor with Justin Clark. Was I impressed? No.
That's as blunt as I can be, but I will tell you this, I was cautioned. I saw what your potential was and I can promise you that when you and I step into the squared circle on January twenty sixth, my mindset will be simple.
It's gonna be the greatest challenge of not only my wrestling career, but of my life. And Fury, when that final bell rings there will be no question that you had just faced the greatest challenge of your life. The Skydome, what a great place for two athletes to square off in a one on one match of talent and strength. What a great site for Blake Bryant to make his much anticipated return to the wrestling ring. And above all this, it's gonna be promoted by the BEST wrestling organization known to the world today, Empire
Wrestling. Be advised Ryan Fury. I don't plan to walk away without my very first Imperial win.
[Bryant opens his mouth to obviously continue his statement, but is disturbed by another bright light shining into his eyes. This time he holds his forearm up above his eyebrows to shield his pupils from the beam of brightness. The cameraman shifts the lens of the filming device towards the roaming automobile to reveal in a short time that it is a long black limousine. When Bryant notices this detail, he turns away from the camera and shuffles over to the curb where the limo is destined to stop. However, as the transport approaches an the image of a man is revealed from one of the back windows. Bryant looks on in confusion, as the limo zooms by, the man is half way out of the window, holding what appears to be a bottle. He is dressed in a ruffled white dress shirt that has a tie wrapped loosely around the collar. The screen gets a good shot of the mans face only to reveal that his appearance would resemble a younger member of society. But, the distinctive attitude he is carrying along with the bottle, gives some comedy relief to what was until now a serious showing.]
Man: SORRY BLAKE. I'm running for mayor. Ya know we always get the nice cars and good cigars.
[The moving automobile passed by Bryant's location just as a hand from the inside reaches out to grab the collar of the mans dress shirt, pulling him back inside. But not before he gets off one more laughable statement.]
Man: The women go with the cigars. [Bryant gives a disturbed look back at the camera, as the limousine passes into the darkness and out of view. The EW superstar shakes his head in almost disbelief, before returning his full attention the camera lens only a couple feet away from his face.]
Blake Bryant: That was my limo. He's in my damn limo? Is this what it's about these days? I'm irate. Everybody so easy to sell out. Well, that's one thing Blake Bryant doesn't do. The Blake Bryant you're looking in the eyes today will be the same man you see when I walk out of this federation after my goals have been reached. And when I say that, I'm not being arrogant.
It's called confidence. Confidence in my knowledge and talent inside the squared circle. Something Ryan Fury is gonna learn a lot about come the twenty-sixth. Fury, I'm new to Empire Wrestling, but you're new to wrestling alone. Does that give me the advantage? Only if you let it. Ya see Fury, I have a particular thinking process when it comes to the odds and ends of this sport and competition in general. When it all comes down to it, it's man against man and upon any given night either of those men can be the better. That's exactly why I will take no obstacle lightly. I will look only to the next challenge in my endeavors. Doing that ensures me of one thing.
That if I do get beat, then on that night the man that beat me was better then my best. That's what you're stepping up against Fury and that's what
Empire Wrestling has to look forward to for the remainder of my time here.
And like I said, if Blake Bryant can't compete on a daily basis with the talent here at EW, then he'll walk away satisfied. Satisfied he gave his all. Sound corny? Well, time will have to heal that wound.
[Bryant glances into the camera with a ICE COLD stare, as the darkness surrounding his face seems to tighten in on the appearance. Yet again, a beam of light hits his serious expression and his attention is quickly turned to another oncoming vehicle. The screen shifts in the direction of the light as well in time to reveal a small yellow taxi cab. The cab eventually pulls up to the curb where Bryant is standing in almost disbelief. The driver who appears to be a bit overweight from the first glance, rolls down his window and speaks out at Bryant.]
Cab Driver: Hey bub. Need a lift. [Bryant processes the mans question, before turning back to the camera with a stunned expression on his face. A large breath later, Blake slowly nods his head at the waiting cab driver, as he takes several steps towards the rear door of the taxi.]
Blake Bryant: Yea, I might as well. You know where the Billiard is? Cab Driver: Sure do, mac. Just get in the damn car and put on your safety belt. [Bryant looks down off the curb at the cabby with a semi-shocked expression, before reaching down to the door handle and pulling it open. However, Bryant suddenly pauses from his actions and takes one last glance back at the screen. The Empire Wrestling Superstar speaks out with a sense of humor in his voice and a smile across his face.]
Blake Bryant: Just like old times. I guess history is gonna repeat itself. [With that said, Blake Bryant lets himself fall down into the cushioned seat of the cab. Split seconds later, the vehicle takes off without much notice, forcing Bryant to shut the door in the process of moving. The screen begins to follow the taxi cab driven by the anxious man. As the cab disappears in the darkness, the scene begins to fade to black, leaving only the faint sound of the tapping rain to control the foreground. Soon enough, that too is disconnected from the viewers.]
