Empire Wrestling: House Show Report

12 February 2000

Empire Wrestling (EW) - House Show Report February 12, 2000 www.nemesis-productions.com/empirewrestling/ Live from Tallahassee: ---------------------------- Dante Cross vs. Brinn Yakuza ---------------------------- We kick off the show with Dante Cross making his way to the sounds of bone-thugs n'harmony...he stops on the ramp to taunt a few hecklers before hopping into the ring to await his challenger. No sooner than Cross jumps onto the turnbuckle and signals to the crowd his intro is rudely interrupted by the mysterious Brinn Yakuza. As he enters the ring the men have a few words and the ref gives his final warnings. There's the bell, EW Saturday
Night is under way! Cross starts the match with a vengeance hitting Yakuza with a vicious Enziguri, followed by a standing sidekick. The little man wastes no time in wearing down Yakuza, hitting several sensational moves.
After a few minutes of toying with the obviously under-matched Yakuza, Cross hops the turnbuckle and hits the challenger with a hurricanrana he likes to call the Diamond Drop. The ref counts 3 and Dante Cross has the easy win to open the show.
----------------------------- Mark Faustus vs. Blake Bryant ----------------------------- This match was much closer than the first. The relatively new Faustus hit several strong moves, and for quite a while it looked like we might actually see the prestigious TV title change hands. But the fan favorite and ex-football star made a courageous comeback by staggering Faustus with a fireman's carry, followed by a strong shoulder block and a figure four to wear down his opponent. After a few minutes of close combat Bryant hoisted
Faustus to his shoulders and drove him hard to the mat with some sort of shoulder-breaker maneuvers. Your winner and still TV champ...Blake Bryant.
----------------------- Sidewinder vs. CJ Swift ----------------------- This match features the fan favorite Sidewinder....who got a deafening pop when introduced, against the ultra-cool CJ Swift. Swift opens the match with a bang, dominating Sidewinder with several textbook-wrestling maneuvers. But just as Swift is about to hit a huge frogsplash, Sidewinders manager Dan Thornton pulls Swifts leg from the top rope, racking him on the turnbuckle. Now Sidewinder takes control, superplexing his opponent hard to the canvas. Then Sidewinder locks on a figure four, which looks to have drained the last of swift's energy. But somehow swift makes a miraculous stretch and reaches the ropes. As the hold is broken Thornton yells something which catches Swift's attention, and provokes a rapid-fire pelvic thrust. Seeing that his opponent is distracted, Sidewinder whirls him around and drops him hard in a Michinoku driver. 'Winder then picks up his almost unconscious opponent and hoists him to the top rope to hit a devastating move he calls the Side Splitter. Ref counts three, Sidewinder and Thornton rejoices.
---------------------------- Don Corleone vs. Mike Gorman ---------------------------- Our next match pits the black Italian against the "maniac" mike Gorman. This match was fairly close at first, with both Gorman and Corleone hitting some big moves. But after a few minutes of strong offense, Gorman made a fatal error by trying to hit a missile drop kick from the top rope.
Corleone say it coming and quickly ducked out of the way. This set the pace for a Corleone offensive barrage, which included a powerbomb, followed by a sleeper. The Don then stepped back and signaled for his feared finisher, the OD.... Which effectively ended any chance for a Gorman comeback.
-------------------- Blac Haze vs. Hybrid -------------------- The match opened with Hybrid hitting Haze repeatedly with rights and lefts. This delighted the crowd, who doesn't really seem to like Haze all that much. Hybrid tried for a DVD, but Blac slipped out and wrapped Hybrid in a naked rear choke. But the mysterious Haze decided to free the fan-favorite from the move, and instead hit him with four different varieties of a DDT, this is textbook stuff, people. The fans begin to toss trash into the ring, but Blac Haze extends his arms and a thick cloud of smoke fills the ring.
When the smoke had cleared, Hybrid lay unconscious and the ref was signaling for the bell. -------------------------- Aron Rave vs. Justin Clark -------------------------- A Sweet Dreams hits the loudspeaker, the crowd erupts in boo's for "The
Nightmare" Aron Rave. The fans cheer the challenger, Justin Clark, mainly because they want to see him beat Rave's head in. But unfortunately for the fans in Tallahassee, there would be no beating of Aron Rave tonight; in fact
Clark barely laid a hand on the Canadian. Rave threw Clark into the corner and mercilessly choked him as he taunted the crowd. The Champ tossed the challenger like a rag doll before finally deciding it was time for his signature nightmare bomb. But one bomb didn't satisfy the Canadian bastard, as he hoisted the unconscious Clark once again for a second nightmare bomb.
The ref raised Clark's hand three times and signals for the bell.
