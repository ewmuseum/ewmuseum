TV Taping 11

13 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 2 —

[The camera opens with a close-up of a shrieking man, his voice too terribly high pitched. Pulling back, the owner of the voice, Raoul is precariously balanced on top of the a pink couch within the Pretty in Pink locker room, in his hand, a can of bug spray. The screeching stops as the door opens and in steps the green clad Freak E.]
Raoul: BUG!!!!! BUG!!!!! [With a smile on his face, Freak E nods his head up and down, causing his antenna to bobble in the same direction.]
Raoul: BUG!!!!! [Waving his can around, Raoul unleashes a cloud of spray that covers Freak as he comes up with a large cockroach in hand. Dropping the roach, E starts coughing as Raoul continues his screaming, pointing at the scurrying bug. The noise stops when the door bursts open and an angry Lars steps in.]
Lars: I’M FLAMING ... [Stopping in midsentence, Lars turns in time to catch Raoul who leaps into his arms for safety.]
Lars: ... mad? [Popping his head up, Freak E stops his coughing as he eyes Lars holding Raoul.] Freak E: Uh oh ... [Two heads turn in Freak’s direction before the camera moves on to find the lovely Flaxen making her way through the backstage area, passing by several fans with a wave and a smile. Stopping dead in her tracks, the leggy blonde in her civvies, gets a horrified look on her face as she is doused with liquid.
The horror passes into anger, looking at the offender, a male fan holding a Pepsi cup. Turning quickly, the man points at the Pepsi Cola table set-up and Pepsi Boy conducting a taste test. Pointing a finger, Flaxen steps closer.] Flaxen: Tonight, you get the Pepsi challenge! [Wiping the dripping liquid from her chest, Flaxen storms off as the camera moves on to find Jake Morgan pacing back and forth in his dressing room, wringing his hands together. On the coffee table in front of him sits a big bouquet of flowers. A knock on the door stops him in his tracks.]
Jake Morgan: Come in. [The door opens and Neal the intern walks in looking nervous. Jake just looks at him before asking.]
Jake Morgan: WELL? Did you give her my message? Neal: Yep, she’s right behind me. Coming this way. Jake Morgan: Man, Flaxen is going to LOVE these. [Jake smiles as he picks a cup off of the table and takes a swig while Neal gets a sick look on his face.]
Neal: Flaxen? [Neal closes his eyes and shakes his head.] Neal: Ummm ... Jake ... I thought the message was for Angel. [Spraying his drink, Jake wheels around and looks at Neal with surprise.] Jake Morgan: Why in the Hell would I give these flowers to .... [The door opens and Angel Rotten walks in. Jake looks like “a deer caught in the headlights,” but he continues speaking, with his back to Angel.]
Jake Morgan: ... anyone else but Angel. Turning towards her, he hands over the bouquet. Her eyes light up as she rushes over to take the flowers from Jake's hands. She smells them, then gives him a huge hug. Looking over Angel’s shoulder, he glares menacingly at
Neal before a clock’s ticking loudly sounds in the background. With the screen going black, the clock face of a logo hurtles through the ebony space before settling into view. With the clock face fading into the background, faces and names flash by quickly.
The Mean Green Mascot - Mexican superstar, Ieta Fujita - Ricky Starr - The pink clad team of Pretty in Pink, Lars and Billy Blaze with Raoul - Agent of
L.O.V.E., Harry Palmer - Weed - All American Buddy Lee Jr. - and finally, the Television Champion, Jake Morgan and an angry Angel Rotten. As the last of the pictures fade, the camera makes a swing through a small but packed television studio before focussing in on two men sitting at the ringside announcers’ table. The two men are a contrast in looks, one in a sports coat while the other one is in a Hawaiian shirt, leather pants and a matching hat.]
Howard Butz: Good evening, wrestling fans! Welcome once again to 30 Minute Countdown. I'm Howard Butz and the man on my left is none other than Dillon Diamond. [Diamond smiles as he gives a little salute to the fans out in TV-land.] Howard Butz: Before we get to our first match, Dillon, perhaps you'd like to make the announcement?
Dillon Diamond: It'd be my pleasure, Howie. With Halloween right around the corner, I'm happy to announce the next Double Trouble television taping ...
Faces of Fear on October 28th. Howard Butz: Faces of Fear ... double the wrestling action, double the trouble! Speaking of Halloween, do you have a costume for the occassion? Dillon Diamond: Sure do! I'm thinking of the Race Bannon of Jonny Quest fame. Howard Butz: Maybe then, I'll go as Racer X ... Dillon Diamond: No can do, compadre ... Bannon was Racer X on his days off and I've already got dibs on Bannon. Howard Butz: WHAT?!?!? Oh, give me a break, will you? They were not the same person. Dillon Diamond: Were too. Howard Butz: Were not. Dillon Diamond: Were too. Howard Butz: Why am I even arguing with you? Let’s go to Holly backstage for some late breaking news!
[Leaving the two arguing announcers, the camera moves into the back to find the very attractive Holly with microphone in hand.]
Holly: Thank you, guys ... I'm standing here in the back where just moments ago, head referee, Orly Diaz was just carried off by EMT's after being found unconscious. So far, no one's talking as to what went down back here but as soon as I find something out, I’ll let you in on it!
[Leaving the smiling woman, the camera comes back to Butz and Diamond having been joined by Raoul at the announcers’ table.]
Howard Butz: And we’ll be back after this station identification ... [The trio fades into a commercial still arguing over Racer X and Race Bannon.] *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Bally’s Gym. Hopefully, you’ve seen it. Gorgeous, slender woman in her undies kneeling on her bed. In the foreground is a camera focussed in on the bed and the woman waiting patiently for someone else, presumably her husband.
You know the one I’m talking about? I need a membership! Maybe, I can bring someone home who looks like that! Get that video camera ready! Whoohoo!
Scenes of poor children in some third world country flash by as the Christian Children’s Fund asks you to make a difference in their world for as little as 89 cents a day! Food, medicine, education all possible on 89 cents a day! Won’t you help make a difference? *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** [Returning, the camera zooms in on the odd trio sitting ringside while a bell sounds in the background.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: Our first match, between Lars and Freak E, is underway ... and joining us is Raoul ...
Raoul: Charmed, I’m sure. Sitting on the table in front of Raoul is a large can of RAID spray. Inside the ring, Lars nails the incoming Freak E with a knee to the midsection before pulling him to the center of the ring. Bending him over, Lars pulls him into a standing head scissors then drops to his knees, slamming Freak’s face into the canvas.
Howard Butz: OUCH! Lars looks a little hot under the collar tonight, Raoul. Raoul: Yes ... HOT!!!!! Pulling Freak back to his feet, Lars snaps his head back with a forearm shiver, knocking him back into the ropes. Grabbing an arm, Lars spins him off the ropes but is countered and sent across the ring. Leaping high into the air, Freak slams into the mat as Lars stops his rebound by wrapping his arms up in the ropes. Rushing forward, Lars drops a solid elbow before hooking a leg and drawing a count from Robin.
Howard Butz: Lars with a fast pin here. ONE! TWO! NO! Grabbing up his can, Lars sprays across the table causing Howard to cough and swat at the fumes.
Howard Butz: WHAT WAS THAT FOR?!?!? Raoul: Bugs. Howard Butz: There are no bugs out here. Again with a standing head scissors, Lars paddles Freak’s backside before being tossed over with a back body drop. Rushing forward, Freak hits the ropes and blasts Lars with a somersault senton. Leaning back, Freak goes for the pin.
Howard Butz: PIN! ONE! TWO! LARS THROWS HIM OFF! Back on his feet, Lars eye gouge draws a warning from the ref. Pushing Freak into the corner, Lars opens up with his limp wristed knife hands. Pulling him from the corner, Lars goes for the Irish whip but is reversed and sent into the far corner. Hitting the corner, Lars goes up into the air and as Freak E charges in right behind. Wrapping his legs around his opponent’s neck, Lars spins his body, throwing the bugman to the mat with a head scissor takedown which doesn’t go unnoticed.
Raoul: LARS! LARS! LARS! Leading the cheer, Raoul gets the fans involved in the match. Grabbing Freak’s legs, Lars grins before dropping down, planting his face into Freak’s crotch. After lingering way too long, he pushes his body forward, pinning shoulders to the mat.
Howard Butz: LARS WITH THE PIN! ONE! TWO! THREE! NO! Freak E rolled out at the last second. That was close, Dillon!
Dillon Diamond: Too close. That had to be two and fifteen sixteenths. Raoul: Quite. Grabbed by the head, Freak is thrown to the mat by his head, escaping the ring for a much needed breather. Shaking it off on the outside, he is joined by Lars via a plancha.
Howard Butz: Lars taking this one to the outside with a beautiful plancha. Raoul, with some of Lars high flying ability, has he spent any time in Mexico? Raoul: Ah yes, Mexico ... the sun, the fun, the marguaritas on those lazy beaches, the young men in their speedos ... oh, were those the days ...
With a glassy eyed look for several seconds, Raoul comes back to reality with a shriek, once again snatching up his can and letting loose with a healthy spray of RAID. Not giving Freak E a chance to recover, Lars opens up with some chops before taking a thumb to the eye. Grabbing an arm, Freak falls back, pulling
Lars and sending him crashing with his shoulder into the ring post. Rolling back to his feet, Freak sends him a second time into the post with a drop kick.
Howard Butz: Looks like the tide is starting to turn. Lars is favoring that shoulder. Dillon Diamond: Raoul, while you’re here, perhaps you can settle an argument for us. I say that Race Bannon and Racer X were one and the same while you know who says otherwise ...
Before giving an answer, Raoul bolts out of his seat, covering first his mouth then his eyes in horror as his Lars is dropped to the floor with a suplex.
Rolling into the ring to stop Robin’s count, Freak comes flying off the ring apron with a hard leg drop that seems to hurt him just as much. Slowly rising to his feet, Freak rolls Lars back into the ring. Leaning him back against the ropes, Freak dives over the top towards the ring apron, catching Lars arms with his legs then reaching down to grab his ankles. Lars cries out in pain as Freak
E pulls both his arms and legs. Dillon Diamond: LA CUCARACHA! LA CUCARACHA!

— Part 2 of 2 —

With Robin calling for a break, Freak slides off, allowing Lars to drop to the mat. With him laying facedown, Freak bends an arm to Lars’ back before sitting down across it with a leg drop.
Howard Butz: Freak E really working that arm over now. After ramming Lars into the ring post, he’s been working it over nonstop.
Dillon Diamond: You have to take advantage of every opportunity and Freak E is doing just that. A win here tonight has to vault him up in the ranks for a shot at the Young Guns title if not the Television belt.
Putting Lars in a modified camel clutch near the ropes, pulling back on the single arm, further damaging it. On the mat in front of Lars, Robin watches for the submission. Jumping up from his seat, Raoul grabs up his can with a screech and starts spraying. Close enough to the ropes to hit Freak square in the face.
Letting go of Lars, Freak E falls back clutching his eyes. Leaving the ring, Robin confronts Raoul who suddenly jumps into her arms with a frightened yell and starts spraying.
Howard Butz: Raoul sprayed Freak E in the eyes otherwise this match might be over already. Dillon Diamond: I don’t know what you’re talking about. Howard Butz: You didn’t just see what happened? Dillon Diamond: I didn’t see a thing. Howard Butz: Obviously. Whipping Freak into the far ropes, Lars catches him up in the air, burying his face in his opponent’s crotch before falling forward with a powerbomb. With
Freak E flat on his back and Lars laying facedown on top of him, Raoul jumps out of the ref’s arms, stomping something on the floor before pointing frantically towards the ring. Diving back in, Robin slaps out a three count.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: You can’t tell me that Lars won that one fair and square. Dillon Diamond: You’re right, I can’t tell you that Lars won that one fair and square. Howard Butz: So, you agree that Raoul’s cheating won him the match? Dillon Diamond: I didn’t say that. All I said was that I couldn’t tell you that Lars won that one fair and square.
Howard Butz: We’ll be right back! [With Butz shaking his head in disgust, the camera returns to the ring and a jubulant Raoul helping Lars to his feet and out of the ring.]
***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** BEER. It’s good. In fact, you should be drinking one right now. If you were, you might be able to get fine chicks like those in our commercials instead of sitting on your ass watching wrestling at one in the morning on a Saturday. So drink beer, because if you drink enough, anyone or anything can look really good. BEER. Remember, its cheaper than crack!
Taco Bell is now open later. So get your fat ass off the couch and come get fatter! Yo quiero Gordo Americanos!
***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** [Returning from the break, the camera opens on Bad Boy Corey and Kickin' Kenny of 69 Degrees talking in the backstage area.]
Bad Boy Corey: You betcha, she's one hot piece of ... [The two look over as their conversation is interrupted by Buddy Lee Jr., holding a clipboard.]
Buddy Lee Jr.: Hey guys, I need you to sign this petition. Bad Boy Corey: Petition? Kickin' Kenny: Yeah, what's it for? Buddy Lee Jr.: In search of fairness, this is get Jake Morgan out of matches as a referee. [He smiles as both Corey and Kenny sign the petition. Leaving them, the camera finds Billy Blaze hurriedly entering the building. In jeans and a 30MC t-shirt,
Billy grabs a passerby.] Billy Blaze: Have you seen Shorti? [With a shake of the head for an answer, Billy starts off down the hallway once again. Moving on, the camera returns ringside to Butz and Diamond.]
Howard Butz: It’s time for the main event ... though I don’t know really what to make of this match-up between Flaxen and Pepsi Boy.
Dillon Diamond: This one pits a battle hardened veteran in Flaxen versus the strange Pepsi Boy. All I can say, Howard, is that it’s a good thing we have
Jake Morgan refereeing this one just in case, Pepsi Boy decides to get out of hand. Howard Butz: Let’s not start that again. You know as well as I do that Jake cheats ... he did last week ...
Dillon Diamond: Cheat?!?!? How can you say that. I’m sure if he were doing ANYTHING that wasn’t on the up and up, Newton would have him marched out of that ring. Howard Butz: Whatever. There’s a high-five between the two and the bell from Morgan. We’re underway! *** Ding *** Ding *** Grabbed while backpeddling, Pepsi Boy is flipped to the seat of his pants with a snapmare. Climbing the ropes quickly, she dives over her opponent from the middle rope, snapping him forward with a flying neck snap. Lurching forward,
Pepsi Boy falls back in pain. Howard Butz: Flaxen taking it to Pepsi Boy in her first match here in 30 Minute Countdown. Dillon Diamond: Excuse me, Howie ... she won last week. Howard Butz: Give me a break, will you? Jake Morgan awarded her a win for whatever reason ...
Dillon Diamond: Because she beat Raoul. Howard Butz: No, she didn't. Moving back up the ropes, Flaxen poses for the cheering fans before leaping off and landing a corkscrew splash. After bouncing off of Pepsi Boy, she hooks a leg for a fast pin. Dropping quickly to the mat, Jake slaps out a fast count.
Howard Butz: FLAXEN WITH THE COVER! ONE! TWO! KICK OUT BY PEPSI BOY! Dillon Diamond: Absolutely breathtaking. Flaxen taking up right where she left off last week.
Stalking around the ring, Flaxen comes back in with an elbow drop that misses it's target as Pepsi Boy rolls out of the way. Following with a knee drop,
Pepsi Boy makes a cover but is tossed off on a one count. Dillon Diamond: I think it's going to take much more than that to put Flaxen away. She's a former champion afterall.
Howard Butz: Hate to tell you, Dillon. What she did elsewhere has no bearing here. Dillon Diamond: Are you saying that Pepsi Boy is going to beat her? Howard Butz: I didn't say that ... Dillon Diamond: FLAXEN WITH A COUNTER! HIP TOSS! HIP TOSS! THIS IS STARTING TO GET EXCITING! Grabbing an arm, Flaxen throws a leg across her foe before rolling her body and pulling Pepsi Boy over and putting his shoulders to the mat.
Howard Butz: There's a roll-up. ONE! TWO! CLOSE! Pepsi Boy making it to the ropes and Jake calls for a break.
Dillon Diamond: And as you notice, Flaxen breaks immediately. Can we say that Pepsi Boy would do the same? Howard Butz: I don't know. We haven't seen him have to yet. Dillon Diamond: You need to get off the Pepsi Boy bandwagon. Howard Butz: What? Dillon Diamond: You heard me. Howard Butz: Whatever. After pushing Pepsi Boy into the corner, she is reversed and slammed back. Hitting the corner hard, she is an easy target for a chop as Pepsi Boy winds up but stops short of the target, thinking twice about hitting a woman in the chest. Grabbing the ropes instead, he drives his masked face into Flaxen's stomach. Once, twice before Morgan pulls him off and offers up a harsh warning.
Stepping out from the back with a bouquet of flowers, Angel Rotten makes her way to the announcers' table, having a seat.
Dillon Diamond: I don't know what you have against Jake as a referee but in my book, he's doing a great job.
Howard Butz: What I have against him? He's not a trained referee and his counts have been a bit quick in Flaxen's favor ... and we've been joined by Angel
Rotten. Nice flowers. Angel: Thank you, Howie. It was awfully nice of Jake to get them for me. After stepping back, Pepsi Boy returns to the attack, sending Flaxen over with a monkey flip. Dropping for a cover, Pepsi Boy looks up for a referee's count only to find Morgan jawing with fans in the front row, threatening to have them tossed from the television studio to a heavy dose of booing.
Howard Butz: And what do you call this? This is fair? Dillon Diamond: Tell me about it. These fans should be ashamed of themselves. In fact, I think they need to be escorted out of here. Your thoughts on it, Angel? Angel: These are some really nice flowers. Howard Butz: Speaking of your thoughts, Angel ... how do you feel about Jake getting close to Flaxen?
Shooting Butz a look, Angel rolls her eyes before snatching up her flowers and leaving her seat. Inside the ring, Pepsi Boy argues with Morgan, until he's pulled down with an inside cradle. Morgan's quick mat slapping is abruptly stopped as Flaxen rolls off after taking a face full of flowers courtesy of
Angel. Howard Butz: Angel Rotten does not look happy! Dillon Diamond: Forget about her. Jake doesn't look happy! Holding Flaxen back, Morgan orders Angel from ringside but gets a piece of her mind instead. The cheer from the fans sends Morgan into a fury as he again orders her from ringside. Waving him off, she heads to the back after snatching up her flowers. Grabbed by the shoulder, he is spun around by a complaining
Pepsi Boy. Shaking his head, Morgan shoves Pepsi Boy back hard ... right into Flaxen. Spinning him around, Flaxen wraps him up and throws him over with a snap suplex. Howard Butz: And that's fair? Dillon Diamond: The nerve of Pepsi Boy for laying hands on a referee. How dare he. If I was the referee, I'd have tossed him out. You can't stand on that but
Jake, ever the consumate professional continues the match. Howard Butz: Oh, please. At Morgan's urging, Flaxen climbs the ropes, doing a little dance for the fans before leaping off and landing a leg drop. Bouncing back to her feet, Flaxen struts around the ring, leading the clapping fans before pointing back to the ropes.
Howard Butz: Flaxen taking her time rather than putting away Pepsi Boy. Dillon Diamond: I think she's being wary of another attack from Angel, who didn't look too happy for some reason just moments ago.
Sending Pepsi Boy into the ropes, Flaxen takes him down with a spinning heel kick. Rolling to her feet, she bounds up the ropes, coming off with a split legged moonsault from the top.
Howard Butz: She’s got the cover. NO! WHAT’S SHE DOING?!?!? Rolling Pepsi Boy over, Flaxen wraps him up in a sharpshooter, sitting back for added pressure. Fighting, Pepsi Boy desperately crawls forward.
Howard Butz: PEPSI BOY TRYING TO MAKE IT TO THE ROPES! I THINK HE’S GOING TO MAKE IT! Dillon Diamond: HE WON’T MAKE IT! Howard Butz: HE’S GOING TO MAKE IT! With Pepsi Boy getting with finger’s reach of the ropes, the bell sounds. *** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: WHAT?!?!? NO!!!!! JAKE MORGAN CALLED FOR THE BELL! IT’S OVER! HE’S AWARDED THE MATCH TO FLAXEN!!!!! [Pulling her up, a grinning Morgan raises her arm as the credits begin to roll.] *** 30 Minute Countdown© ***
