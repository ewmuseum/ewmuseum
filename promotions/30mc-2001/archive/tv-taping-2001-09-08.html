30MC — Card Archive

TV Taping

8 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 2 —

[The camera opens on Angel Rotten strolling down the hall muttering to herself.
As she rounds the corner, It’s obvious that she’s being very closely followed by “Geek Supreme”, the Agent of L.O.V.E., Harry Palmer.
With him reaching out to grab her backside, she whirls around just before he can touch her.]
Harry Palmer: Hey, baby ... [Rolling her eyes, she storms off leaving Palmer grinning. Pulling further back, the camera finds another observer to the unfolding events, just out of sight ... “Elusive” Ryan Delaney.
On the move, the camera spys Number One Challenger, Buddy Lee Jr. with Mexican luchadore of Asiatic descent, Ieata Fujita.]
Ieta Fujita: ¿Quieres que re efectué yo ra venganza ? [After waving his arms around, Fujita eyes Buddy Lee for a reply. The confused Buddy stcratches his head before answering.] Buddy Lee Jr.: English, Ieata, English. Ieata Fujita: Engrish? Buddy Lee Jr.: Yes, Ieata ... Engrish ... err ... no. I mean, yes. English. [With Fujita now confused, the camera is on the move once again. This time finding a smirking Newton inside his office, sitting behind his desk. His bodyguard, Brody, relaxes on a comfy looking chair in front of the desk.]
Brody: Why not drop the weight stip on Morgan? I think ... [His smirk melting away, Newton slowly turns his head to gaze upon Brody.] Newton: I don’t remember hiring you to think ... Brody: But boss, Morgan’s all busted up. There’s no way ... Newton: You’re right ... he can’t win. Buddy Lee Jr. can’t beat Jake Morgan ... [Newton cracks a sly grin as a clock’s ticking loudly sounds. With the screen going black, the clock face of a logo hurtles through the ebony space before settling into view. With the clock face fading into the background faces and names flash by quickly.
The Mean Green Mascot - Mexican superstar, Ieta Fujita - Ricky Starr - The pink clad team of Pretty in Pink, Lars and Billy Blaze with Raoul - Agent of
L.O.V.E., Harry Palmer - Weed - All American Buddy Lee Jr. - and finally, the Television Champion, Jake Morgan and an angry Angel Rotten. As the last of the pictures fade, the camera makes a swing through a small but packed television studio before focussing in on two men sitting at the ringside announcers’ table. The two men are a contrast in looks, one in a sports coat while the other one is in a Hawaiian shirt, leather pants and a matching hat.]
Howard Butz: Welcome wrestling fans! And a big welcome to you too, Dillon Diamond. [A surprised Diamond gives a small tip of the hat.] Dillon Diamond: Sounds like you’re happy to see me, Howie? Why the big change? Howard Butz: After last week’s intrusion by Shawn Avery ... you, my friend are a welcomed sight for sore eyes!
Dillon Diamond: I’ll take that as a compliment and second your sentiments on Mr. Avery’s departure for parts unknown. Howard Butz: With that said, Dillion ... what do we have in store for the fans this evening? Will we see the Jake Morgan/Buddy Lee Jr. confrontation or will be teased once again?
Dillon Diamond: Howard, put your money on another teaser ... Howard Butz: Just like old times. Don’t let my compliment go to your head. Dillon Diamond: The champ, Jake Morgan is in no shape to put up his title against Buddy Lee. Not only is Morgan suffering from match related injuries, his hand just being the latest of them but he has the weight stipulation to contend with ... and you know, as well as I do, Jake Morgan is not down around two thirty-five.
Howard Butz: You’d say what? Two eighty? Dillon Diamond: Easily ... Howard Butz: Looks like speculation on Jake will have to wait ... here comes 69 Degrees. [Stepping out from the back, Corey and Kenny pulled Holly out with them. Dressed in a very short near white dress and matching heels, Holly reluctantly followed into the ring.]
Bad Boy Corey: Ladies and gentlemen ... He’s Kickin’ Kenny ... Kickin’ Kenny: He’s Bad Boy Corey ... In Unison: We’re 69 Degrees! [After a little dance step which is followed by some booing, both pull Holly inbetween them.]
Bad Boy Corey: Tonight, we’re going to do a little number for you with our special friend, Holly!
[The mention of her name brings a number of cheers and whistles for the skimpily clad woman.]
Howard Butz: I don’t think they’re cheering for the dancing, Dillon. Dillon Diamond: Not now, Howie ... I’m watching Holly. [A big cheer goes up as Lars and Raoul, both dressed in matching pink thong leotards and leg warmers, step out from the back and make their way to the ring.]
Howard Butz: This is something I didn’t want to see. [The smiling Lars asks for a mic while the resentful 69ers watched on.] Lars: I’ve come to realize that all you two really need is a little direction ... a mentor. With that said and a little help from Raoul, I’m here to offer it to you! [With a wink at Raoul, the pair jump into action.] In unison: DANCE FIGHT!!! [With a selection of the Backstreet Boys music playing through the loudspeaker, Lars and Raoul give their own rendition of the 69ers dance step, adding in some hip gyrations and lewd suggestive motions of their own before coming to the finale and posing.
Finished, they look at Corey and Kenny while the fans applaud, cheer and laugh. Looking at each other, Corey and Kenny launch into action of their own, attacking Lars, bringing on the boos of the fans. Raoul cries for help as Billy and Shorti rush out from the back, hitting the ring.]
Howard Butz: Bad Boy Corey and Kickin’ Kenny working over Lars ... IT’S BILLY AND SHORTI BLAZE TO THE RESCUE! [Billy evens things up, grabbing the surprised Corey away from Lars. Grabbing him around the head, Billy slams him into the mat with a DDT.]
Dillon Diamond: THE IMPACT! [With a low blow on Kenny, Lars breaks up the beating. Bending Kenny over, Lars puts him into a standing head scissors before dropping to his knees and planting Kenny.]
Dillon Diamond: THE INTRUDER! [Because of the fighting going on around her, poor Holly is stuck in a corner unable to escape the ring. With her two dance partners down, she is free to escape ... or at least, that’s the plan until Shorti grabs her by the hair and pulls her back to the center of the ring.]
Howard Butz: WHAT’S SHORTI DOING?!?!? [Grabbing two handfuls of hair, Shorti slams Holly into the canvas. Hitting the mat, Holly lays very still, her short dress rolling up onto her midsection revealing her white cotton undies underneath.]
Dillon Diamond: INDECENT EXPOSURE! [The show breaks for a commercial with a close-up shot of Holly’s panties as the fans cheer in the background.]
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Bally’s Gym. Hopefully, you’ve seen it. Gorgeous, slender woman in her undies kneeling on her bed. In the foreground is a camera focussed in on the bed and the woman waiting patiently for someone else, presumably her husband.
You know the one I’m talking about? I need a membership! Maybe, I can bring someone home who looks like that! Get that video camera ready! Whoohoo!
Scenes of poor children in some third world country flash by as the Christian Children’s Fund asks you to make a difference in their world for as little as 89 cents a day! Food, medicine, education all possible on 89 cents a day! Won’t you help make a difference? *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** [Back from the commercial finds referee Orly Diaz calling for the bell with Ryan Delaney and Harry Palmer squaring off in the ring.] *** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: Folks, during the commercial break newcomer Ryan Delaney dragged Palmer out to the ring by his ear. Why? I don’t have a clue. Dillon Diamond: So, what else is new, Howie? Howard Butz: Alright smart guy ... why did Delaney drag him out here? Dillon Diamond: I think it’s rather obvious. He’s protecting his interests. Howard Butz: Interests? What’s that suppposed to mean? Delaney with a whip into the ropes ... Japanese arm drag.
Kipping to his feet, Delaney climbs the ropes, displaying his tongue for the fans and rolling his eyes back into his head.
Howard Butz: You know, that really is a frightful visage. Climbing down, he slowly stalks across the ring, holding his fingers to his head as if he has horns protruding before pulling Palmer to his feet and pushing him back into the corner. Tearing open Palmer’s white shirt underneath his colorful jacket, Delaney exposes skin before smashing away with several stiff reverse knife hands which loudly pop, leaving reddening bruises.
Howard Butz: Not a position I’d want to be in. Those are some of the loudest chops I’ve heard here on Thirty Minute Countdown!
Dillon Diamond: You have to credit his puro background and mix in a healthy dose of sadism here, Howie.
Climbing up onto the corner ropes, Delaney steps a leg over Palmers’ neck, locking him into the Octopus when finished. Diaz watches for a submission as
Palmer cries out in pain. Howard Butz: Delaney using the ropes for added leverage. Dillon Diamond: After seeing this guy for two weeks now, I get the feeling his only purpose here is cause his opponent pain. Lots of pain.
Seeing Delaney holding the ropes, Diaz breaks the hold, counting to four before the wrestler breaks the hold after hissing at the ref. A snapmare is followed with a knee to the back of Palmer’s head.
Howard Butz: Delaney climbing the ropes ... he’s waiting ... waiting ... Perched on the top ropes, Delaney looks like a black clad gargoyle until Palmer gets back to his feet. With Palmer turning around, the motionless attacker springs into action, throwing his prey acrss the ring with a perfectly timed head scissors takedown.
Howard Butz: Delaney with a painful arm bar. Dillon, why not just pin the man and be done with it?
Dillon Diamond: I really think something needs to be said for Delaney’s “Poetry of Pain.” Throw in his background of Japanese wrestling into his sadistic tendencies and you have a man who likes to deal out pain.
Howard Butz: A poet and didn’t even know it? Dillon Diamond: Don’t mess with this one, Howie. Howard Butz: Oh, please. Trying to toss Palmer over with a hip toss, Delaney is blocked before taking a back elbow to the head. Readjusting his arm, Palmer sends Delaney over with a hip toss of his own.
Howard Butz: Palmer with a nice counter of his own. DELANEY LANDED ON HIS FEET! Springing backwards, Delaney knocks Palmer down with a spinning leg kick. Dillon Diamond: Quick as a cat, Delaney with a counter of his own. Nice. After stomping Palmer into the mat, Delaney is up the ropes and down, landing a senton, rolling to his feet, coming up with his tongue sticking out.
Howard Butz: Ohhhhh ... we’re back to the groovy ghoulies ... With Delaney playing to the crowd, Palmer crawls across the ring for an escape to the outside. With Palmer making good on his escape, Delaney points at his opponent before leaving the ring as well, shooting himself onto Palmer with a plancha, both men hitting the floor.
Howard Butz: The match has spilled to the outside of the ring. Palmer whipped into the ring barrier! That had to hurt.
Using his boot to choke Palmer, Delaney draws a warning from the ref. Barring his tongue, Delaney rolls back into the ring to break up the count before coming back out, this time with an elbow drop off the ring apron.
Slow to get back to his feet, Delaney yanks Palmer up by the hair, pulling the glasses from his face and crushing them in his hand.
Howard Butz: That’s uncalled for. Stepping behind Palmer, Delaney throws a leg over his victims head, locking it under his chin as well as pulling back on his arm.
Howard Butz: TRIANGLE SCISSOR LOCK! Dillon Diamond: I think this one is over, Howard. Harry Palmer isn’t going to escape from this one.
Struggling to break the hold, Palmer finally taps out. Howard Butz: There it is, Palmer tapped. *** Ding *** Ding ***

— Part 2 of 2 —

[With the match over, Delaney refuses to release his grip despite warnings from
the ref. Even with Diaz trying physically pull Delaney off of Palmer, the ref isn’t able to break the hold.]
Howard Butz: DELANEY REFUSING TO BREAK THE HOLD! This is totally uncalled for, Dillon. *** Ding *** Ding *** *** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: Orly Diaz calling for help from the back. Dillon Diamond: If Delaney doesn’t break the hold, Diaz is going to take a brilliant victory away from him ...
*** Ding *** Ding *** [With security hitting the ring, the camera makes it’s way into the back to find Jake sitting on the couch in his dressing room, while a trainer wraps his knee up. His arm is already bandaged and he winces in pain as the new gauze is placed.]
Jake Morgan: Easy ... [With the door behind him opening, Morgan whips his head around.] Jake Morgan: Neal? [Standing in the doorway is a frowning Angel, not the intern. Seeing her, Morgan frowns before going back to watching his knee being wrapped.] Angel: NEAL?!?!? Do I look like that little bug? I’ve got other concerns and you’re waiting for Neal? Oh, that’s just fine ...
[Jake looks up at her quizzically.] Jake Morgan: Concerns? [Angel shakes her head.] Angel: Delaney? [A look of understanding dawns on his face.] Jake Morgan: Oh him. I loved him in Gladiator, you know the boxing one, not the Roman flick. He's also pretty cool on the Fighting Fitzgerald’s. Why? [Angel rubs the bridge of her nose, then slaps Jake on the back of the head.] Angel: Not Brian Denehey, you Putz. Delaney ... Ryan Delaney, the guy from last week. [He gives her a totally clueless look.] Jake Morgan: Sorry, I haven't got a clue who you're talking about. Maybe if you had a picture?
[Angel throws her hands up in the air.] Angel: ARRRRRGGGGGHHHHH!!!!! YOU’RE IMPOSSIBLE!!!!! [Jake quickly looks down at the trainer.] Jake Morgan: Ahhhh ... HEY! Be careful, you're supposed to stabilize the knee, not break it.
[The door suddenly opens and an out of breath Neal pokes his head in. Jumping up from his seat depsite his knee being heavily wrapped, Morgan comes around, shoves Angel out of the way and embraces the intern]
Jake Morgan: There you are. Just the one I was looking for. [Leading Neal back around to the couch, Morgan nods his head in anticipation of what Neal has to say.]
Neal: Mr. Morgan ... Jake Morgan: NEAL!!! We’re buddies, call me Jake. Neal: Okay, Jake ... I’ve got some bad news for you. It’s no go ... [Bellowing out in pain, Jake leans back against the cushions on the couch, and closes his eyes when the door behind him is slammed shut. Looking back to where
Angel was, he finds her gone.] Jake Morgan: Angel? [He looks back at the trainer.] Jake Morgan: What’s her problem? [The trainer shakes his head and begins to smile as the clueless champion shrugs. Leaving them, the camera finds it’s way into the pink patterned dressing room of Pretty in Pink. After making it’s way around the room, zooming in on posters of Fred Astaire and Ginger Rogers, Christopher Walken,
Shirley Temple, Bill Robinson and others, the camera focuses in on a distraught Raoul.] Raoul: Oh, Lars it was horrible. It backfired on you! [Pulling back, the camera finds the leader of PiP sitting with his back to his surrounding fellow PiPsters.]
Billy Blaze: I told you, Lars. You can’t trust those two. We should just whip their asses and be done with ...
[Waving his hand, Lars halts Billy before spinning around in his seat, a maniacal look on his face.]
Lars: Backfired? Can’t trust them? Oh no, troops ... that was just the first step ... [Lars giggles while the others look on in confusion at his cryptic message before breaking away for a commercial.]
***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** Local commercial. You’ve seen them before. Poorly spliced in with the more professional looking national ones. Selling cars, hawking local restaraunts, stores and/or products before disappearing with an equally poor fade into the next commercial.
BEER. It’s good. In fact, you should be drinking one right now. If you were, you might be able to get fine chicks like those in our commercials instead of sitting on your ass watching wrestling at one in the morning on a Saturday. So drink beer, because if you drink enough, anyone or anything can look really good. BEER. Remember, its cheaper than crack!
***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** [Returning from the commercial, the 30MC owner, Newton strolls to the ring followed by his bodyguard Brody, rolling out the large scale. Making his way into the ring, Newton takes a little stroll to a mixed reaction from the fans.]
Newton: For the past few weeks, we’ve been treated to the “World According To Jake”. We’ve had to listen to how he’s been cheated and screwed at every turn. We’ve heard how the Number One Contender, Buddy Lee Jr. has ducked him at every turn and despite what ailments you might have this week, how you’d take on Buddy ... or anyway with both arms bandaged and tied behind your back.
[The cheering fans brings the laughing Newton to stop momentarily.] Newton: So, what’s it going to be this week, Jake? What’s going to keep you from defending your title once again?
[As if in answer, “Nice Shot Man” starts over the PA system as the champ is followed out to the ring by Angel and Mean Green. With his arm heavily bandaged as well as his knee, Morgan steps through the ropes being held by his cohorts.]
Newton: Now, it’s the knee? [Morgan smiles as Newton closes the distance between the two, stepping right up to him.] Newton: What else, Jake. Too many people in the ring? What else is going to keep you from facing Buddy Lee this week?
Jake Morgan: I’m ready to fight ... [A livid Newton inches closer to Morgan. Now, chest to chest ... well, not exactly since Jake is taller than Newton, Newton cuts the champ off.]
Newton: Oh, you’re going to fight alright ... right here, next week ... [Still smiling, Morgan holds up an arm to keep Mean Green back.] Newton: As the Main Event match in our first ever Double Trouble television taping, you WILL be defending the 30MC Televison Title against the #1
Contender, Buddy Lee Jr. but before you do that ... you’re going to climb your fat ass up onto that scale and get weighed. If you don’t come in at two
thrty-five or under ... YOU LOSE! [Newton takes a step back, smiling.] Newton: If you come in with some stupid excuse or injury and can’t wrestle ... YOU LOSE! Do you understand me, Jake ... YOU LOSE! You WILL wrestle next week and one way or another ... I WILL be taking that title belt from you.
[Nodding his head, Morgan asks for the mic.] Jake Morgan: If I don’t come in at two hundred and thirty-five pounds or lower, I lose my title. If I’m unable to wrestle because of injury ... again,
I’ll be striped of the Television Title? Is that what you’re saying? Newton: That’s right. Now, you’re starting to see the whole picture. You’re going to lose your title and I’m going to shuffle your ass down the rankings ladder ...
Jake Morgan: Then, I guess there’s only one thing for me to do and that’s whip Buddy Lee’s ass next week.
[Sending the fans into a frenzy, Morgan smiles at Newton.] Jake Morgan: I do have a question for you, Newton. Will this stop me from keeping my title?
[Grabbing Newton’s arm, Jake pulls him close, hammering him with a short arm clothesline and spinning him to the mat. Caught by surprise, Brody moves forward to the rescue but is intercepted by Mean Green. After dispatching the feathered foe with a right hand, Brody turns into Jake and is caught around the waist. Lifted into the air, he is spun around before being dropped backfirst into the canvas with a ringshaking spinebuster.
With both men down, a gloating Morgan has a few words for the boss and his so-called bodyguard before a big cheer breaks out for Buddy Lee Jr. as he rushes out from the back.
Sliding into the ring, Buddy Lee comes up with fists ready for a fight as Morgan grabs Angel and thrusts her between himself and the number one contender before leaving the ring altogether for the safety of the outside.
The glaring Buddy Lee Jr. watches as Morgan quickly makes his way around the ring for the back after gathering Angel and Mean Green, keeping them between himself and Buddy. With the fans chanting “Buddy Lee!” the credits roll bringing the show to an end.]
