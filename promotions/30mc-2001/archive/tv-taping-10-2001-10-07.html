TV Taping 10

7 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 2 —

[The camera opens on the zebra stripe shirted pair of Jake Morgan and Robin casually speaking in the backstage area.]
Jake Morgan: So ... do you have any advice for me? [Before she can answer, they are interrupted by Ricky Starr. With a scowl on his face, he taps Morgan on the shoulder.]
Jake Morgan: Eh? Ricky Starr: You going to give me a shot at your title tonight? Jake Morgan: Can't you see I'm busy? Ricky Starr: I want a shot at your title. Jake Morgan: You and the rest of the morons in the back. I'll tell you what ... prove yourself tonight against ... [Morgan stops in midsentence as a beautiful woman with a bag over her shoulder, in jeans and a 30MC t-shirt with long, flowing blonde hair walks by. She smiles as she waves at the head turning Morgan before disappearing from view. With a stupid grin on his face, he is brought back to reality by another tap on the shoulder.]
Jake Morgan: WHAT?!?!? Oh, yeah ... you take on ... uhm ... Billy Blaze tonight and I'll think about your challange.
[Hurriedly walking off, Morgan leaves Starr scratching his head. On the move as well, the camera finds Holly looking at her watch when she is approached by Bad
Boy Corey and Kickin' Kenny of 69 Degrees.] Corey: Hey, sweet thing ... [Looking up from her watch, she frowns at the pair.] Kenny: You ready for your big night? Holly: My what? Corey: Your big night. We got you a match tonight. Holly: You did what? A match? In case you haven't guessed it yet ... I AM NOT A WRESTLER! I don't want to fight Shorti ... Kenny: Shorti? Who said anything about Shorti? [Holly smiles as the three are joined by the same blonde haired woman that passed Jake just moments ago.]
Holly: There you are. I was beginning to think you weren't going to make it. Woman: I wouldn't miss this for the world. [Both woman look surprised as Corey and Kenny whistle at the beauty.] Corey: Aren't you going to introduce us to this fine piece of cheesecake? [With an angered look on her face, Holly pokes Corey in the chest.] Holly: Nevermind who she is ... I want to know who I'm fighting if it's not Shorti. Kenny: It's all good, Holly ... this one is in the bag. Corey: Yeah, you're taking on Raoul. [With Holly slapping her forehead, the camera fades into the pink colored locker room belonging to Pretty in Pink. Looking up from a television monitor, an excited Billy Blaze looks across the room at Lars and Raoul.]
Billy Blaze: Hey, Lars ... I’ve got a match with Ricky Starr! [Looking at his young charge, Lars rolls his eyes before turning back to the business at hand. Barefoot and shirtless, Lars is wearing only a brown pair of suede pants while Raoul is adorned in a skin tight silver jumper that covers him from head to ankle. Flipping a switch on a pink boombox, Lars grabs hold of a microphone and pulls it to his lips.]
Lars: Brown Sugar!!! Raoul: How come you taste so good!?! Lars: Brown Sugar! Raoul: Just like a black boy should! [Both men are putting just a little too much emotion into their singing, sweeping their arms and really belting out the tune. The guitar solo kicks in and Lars and Raoul begin to dance around. Pulling out of the room on the dancing pair, the camera finds Ricky Starr walking by. Hearing the music, he does a little slide step just before a clock’s ticking loudly sounds in the background. With the screen going black, the clock face of a logo hurtles through the ebony space before settling into view. With the clock face fading into the background, faces and names flash by quickly.
The Mean Green Mascot - Mexican superstar, Ieta Fujita - Ricky Starr - The pink clad team of Pretty in Pink, Lars and Billy Blaze with Raoul - Agent of
L.O.V.E., Harry Palmer - Weed - All American Buddy Lee Jr. - and finally, the Television Champion, Jake Morgan and an angry Angel Rotten. As the last of the pictures fade, the camera makes a swing through a small but packed television studio before focussing in on two men sitting at the ringside announcers’ table. The two men are a contrast in looks, one in a sports coat while the other one is in a Hawaiian shirt, leather pants and a matching hat.]
Howard Butz: Are you ready for hard hitting wrestling action? I’m Howard Butz. This is Dillon Diamond ... welcome to 30 Minute Countdown! [Diamond gives a small tip of the hat.] Howard Butz: Another week and what can we expect, Dillon? Dillon Diamond: Ricky Starr ... Billy Blaze ... two of this promotion’s young talents going head to head kicking off our program. I’d say that’s a good start but it doesn’t end there. Oh no, Howie ... it doesn’t. Following on the heels of a spectacular match, we’ve got perhaps the most anticipated match in the program’s history ... Holly versus Raoul.
Howard Butz: And let’s not forget the debut of Jake Morgan as a referee. Dillon Diamond: I think that’s a given, Howie. Jake Morgan WILL be in the pinstripes tonight and that my friend is going to be a pleasure to see. The man does it all. He’s the top draw here on the Countdown, he’s the Television
Champion, and tonight, he puts referee Orly Diaz in his place. Howard Butz: Oh, please. What makes you think he’s going to even be a good referee? Dillon Diamond: Simple, Howard ... Jake Morgan is gold. Whatever he touches, he excels at ...
Howard Butz: I’d have to question Morgan’s ... [A loud round of boos erupt from the small crowd of fans in the studio.] Dillon Diamond: Looks like you can ask him that question. [The smiling Morgan, in his ref stripes, strolls down to the ring with a big grin on his face. Pushing Butz back a step, he slides in between the two announcers.]
Jake Morgan: Dillon, as always, good to see you. Howard Butz: Jake, I’ve got one question for you. What makes you think you can referee a wrestling match ... and do it better than Orly Diaz?
Jake Morgan: That’s two questions, putz but I’ll humor you. You should realize by now whatever I put my mind to, I can and will do.
[With a smile on his face, he pats the breast pocket of his shirt.] Jake Morgan: As for Diaz, well that’s an even easier one to answer. You see, I don’t cheat and you’ll see that I’m not in someone else’s pocket. [The mixed response of booing and laughter draws an angry look from Morgan. Glancing around, Morgan’s smirk returns as Newton and Brody walk out from the back. Shaking his head, Newton holds up a mic.]
Newton: Let me make this real simple for you moron. You’re not going to referee a match in this promotion tonight or any other night. Got it? Hell, you don’t even have a license ...
Jake Morgan: You’re saying because I don’t have a license, you won’t let me referee a match?
Newton: Yup. Jake Morgan: You’re a punk ... [With a smug look on his face, Newton steps up to Morgan.] Newton: Yeah ... but you still don’t have a license, Morgan. No license, no pinstripes for you.
[Stepping back, he laughs at a serious Morgan. Reaching into his shirt pocket, Morgan pulls out a folded piece of paper.] Jake Morgan: I’d say you won ... except for this ... [Morgan cracks a smile as Newton’s eyes go wide when the champ hands the paper to Butz. Unfolding the paper, Butz looks it over.]
Howard Butz: ... this hereby certifies Jake Morgan as a duly trained and licensed referee ...
[His words trail off as he reads on.] Howard Butz: Signed ... Wrestling commissioner Bull ... [With a livid Newton tearing the paper from Butz’ hands, it’s Morgan’s turn to laugh fading into a commercial.]
***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** Local commercial. You’ve seen them before. Poorly spliced in with the more professional looking national ones. Selling cars, hawking local restaraunts, stores and/or products before disappearing with an equally poor fade into the next commercial.
Good girls gone bad. You’ve seen them before ... videos of hot young chicks willing to flash their boobs. The girl next door ... on the beach, walking the street, just about anywhere ... all ready to flash some skin for the camera.
Now, you can buy these videos so you can see the parts they can’t show on television! ***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** [Returning from the commercial, Butz and Diamond have been joined by Lars and another person. Covered in a long pink robe and a towel piled high on his head with his face covered by large silver colored shades, it can only be Raoul.]
Howard Butz: We’ve been joined by Lars and is it safe to say that’s Raoul under that towel?
[Turning his head, the “turbaned” Raoul holds his hand out to Butz with the fingers hanging loosely downwards. Taking his hand, Butz shakes it limply.
Pulling it back, Raoul turns back towards the ring, his eyes focused on Billy Blaze inside the ring.] Dillon Diamond: I think he wanted you to kiss his hand. That’s what you do with royality ...
Howard Butz: Royality? You’ve got to be kidding. We’re awaiting Billy’s opponent tonight ... Ricky Starr.
[With Starr stepping out from the back, the fans cheer loudly when all of a sudden “Brown Sugar” blasts out over the PA system. Sliding into the ring,
Starr is checked over by Robin before the bell is rung.] *** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: With the start of the match, Lars, what’s on your mind in this non-title bout? Lars: As always, we’re here to support our little Billy. This should be a very entertaining affair ... anytime you have two strapping young men ...
throwing each other around ... muscular ... sweaty ... Both Lars and Raoul get a glazed over, faraway look on their faces. Inside the ring, Billy and Starr circle before locking up. Struggling for control, they push one another around until Billy forces Starr into a corner. With the ref counting, Billy steps back, breaking the hold.
Howard Butz: Lars? Raoul? Snapping back to reality, Lars looks at Butz. Again locking up, Billy gains control before snapping Starr over with a hip toss. Ready, Starr lands on his feet then grabbing Billy for one of his own with the same results. Eyeing each other, they recieve a cheer from the fans.
Lars: Is it me or did it suddenly get hot in here? Howard Butz: Collar and elbow tie-up. Billy with a go behind. Starr with a counter ... hammerlock into a side headlock. Billy with a back elbow and a reverse. Snap mare ... Starr with a leg sweep! Both men kip to their feet.
With the fans cheering, the two slap hands before circling once again. Putting Billy in a side headlock, Starr whips him across the ring, taking him over on the return with a back body drop. An elbow misses it’s target as Billy rolls out of the way. Bouncing off the ropes, Billy misses with a leg drop. Catching
Billy’s arm, Starr spins behind him, hooking the other one. Dropping to his knees and puts Billy to the mat.
Howard Butz: BACKSLIDE! ONE! TWO! Escaping, Billy rolls to his feet, catching Starr around the legs and dumping him on his back. Flipping forward, he pins Starr.
Howard Butz: BILLY WITH A COVER! ONE! TWO! Lars: This is just so exciting! Look at them go! Pulling Billy down with his legs, Starr pins his man to the mat for a two count before an escape. Whipping Billy across the ring, Starr ducks under a clothesline. Catching Billy by the neck as he passes, Starr spins him into a swinging neckbreaker. Leaping to his feet, Starr leaps up onto the middle rope before throwing his body back and spinning into a corkscrew leg drop.
Howard Butz: ROBIN WITH THE COUNT! ONE! TWO! THR ... NO! BILLY GETS THE SHOULDER UP!

— Part 2 of 2 —

Lars: OH MY! Dillon Diamond: These two young men have all the talent and energy to take them far in this business.
Howard Butz: Lars, where are you going? Leaving his seat, Lars climbs up onto the ring apron as Starr throws himself back against the ropes to continue his assault. Seeing Lars reaching through the ropes to touch him, Starr stops and has a few words for Lars and the intervening ref. Shaking his head, Starr is pulled down with an inside cradle as Robin warns Lars back to his seat.
Howard Butz: INSIDE CRADLE! ONE! TWO! KICK OUT! A drop kick puts Starr down and is followed by a senton off the middle rope. Rolling back to his feet, Billy’s backflip into a pinning predicament gets a near pinfall as the ref slaps out a two count.
Howard Butz: You’ve been very quiet, Raoul. Your thoughts on the match? Lars: Shhhhh ... Raoul is practicing his breathing. Don’t interupt him. Howard Butz: His what? His breathing? Dillon Diamond: You heard the man, Butz. Don’t mess with the man’s training regimen. Howard Butz: Oh, for the love of ... Lars: You know, Butzie ... if you weren’t so cute, I think Dillon would be the head man around here.
Raoul: Oh, Raoul, you said head. A countered Irish whip is turned into a short arm knee to the stomach and a snap mare to the mat. Climbing the ropes, Starr flips off the top turnbuckle, landing on his feet as Billy rolls out of the way at the last second. Climbing to his feet, Billy taps his head having outsmarted his opponent. Turning around, he takes a drop kick that sends him hard into the corner. Jumping up the ropes, Starr throws him over with a head scissors takedown. A warning from the rookie wrestler has Lars shrinking back in his seat.
Howard Butz: I think that’s in case you have any thoughts of getting involved. Lars: Me? We’re just here to support Billy as well as take Raoul’s mind off of his match later on against that vile creature, Holly.
Howard Butz: Hey, now ... Holly’s one of ours. Raoul: Bitch. Howard Butz: That’s uncalled for. Lars: Uncalled for. Oh, Howie please. You’ve seen what she’s done to those delightful boys ... she’s poisoned their minds against us. It’s all her fault ... and now, she wants to take out her frustrations on poor Raoul. Let me tell you, Raoul may be a fragile, delicate creature but when he’s backed into a corner ... he comes out scratching!
Raoul: MEOW! Slamming Billy back into the corner, Starr follows up with a handspring into a back elbow before opening up with chops to the chest. Spinning out of the corner, Billy delivers a few of his own before sending Starr to the outside with a solid clothesline. Climbing the ropes, he dives off, knocking Starr down with a splash. With both men out on the floor, the ref starts a count.
Howard Butz: Both men are down. They hit the floor hard! With the fans being led in a cheer by Lars, Billy is the first to his feet, sending Starr slamming into the ring barrier then rolling him back into the ring.
Lars: BILLY! BILLY! BILLY! Using the ropes, Billy flies back into the ring with a sunset flip. Pulling Starr to the mat, Billy reaches back for the ropes as Robin slaps the mat. Howard Butz: ONE! TWO! HE’S GOT THE ROPES! THREE! Coming up to her feet, Robin calls for the bell. Leaving the ring in a hurry, Billy celebrates with an ecstatic Lars while Ricky Starr complains to the ref, kicking the ropes.
*** Ding *** Ding *** [Disgusted, Starr leaves the ring but in passing PiP on his way to the back, he gets a congratulatory pat on the backside from Lars before the camera fades into a commercial break.]
***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** The American Red Cross. In the days following recent events, the ARC needs your help more than ever. Give what you can ... blood, money, donate your time if need be.
BEER. It’s good. In fact, you should be drinking one right now. If you were, you might be able to get fine chicks like those in our commercials instead of sitting on your ass watching wrestling at one in the morning on a Saturday. So drink beer, because if you drink enough, anyone or anything can look really good. BEER. Remember, its cheaper than crack!
***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** [Returning from the commercial finds Holly making her way to the ring followed by a statuesque woman with beautifully long blonde hair and a tight blue singlet with gold trim and a matching short cape. Holly is no slouch either in the looks department in her jeans and tank top.
With the girls inside the ring sauntering about to a heavy dose of cheers, Raoul stands from his ringside seat as “Hail to the Chief” starts up. Up onto the ring apron, Raoul enters the ring with Lars holding the ropes open for him.
Making his way into the corner, Raoul uses the ropes to warm up, doing deep knee bends and something that looks more suited to a ballet dancer. Limber, he turns back to his opponent before sliding out of his robe, revealing the silver jumpsuit underneath. After unraveling the towel from his head, Raoul reaches down his legs and pulls at the cuffs of his pant legs. WIth a simple snap, they tear away. Doing the same his sleeves, Raoul is left with a sleeveless singlet and matching boots.
At the center of the ring, he and Holly go eye to eye for a few words before being seperated by their seconds until they were joined by referee Jake Morgan.
Sending both parties back to their respective corners, he calls for the bell.] *** Ding *** Ding *** With the bell, both look a bit tentative as the circle, looking for an opening. As Holly moves in, Raoul slaps away at the air trying to fend her off, his eyes closed. Howard Butz: This is the match we’ve all been waiting for? Dillon Diamond: This is the feeling out process. Howard Butz: Give me a break. Raoul’s eyes are closed. Having “fended” her off, Raoul struts around the ring with an aire of superiority, his nose in the air.
Dillon Diamond: Believe it or not, Raoul comes from a long line of fighters. Howard Butz: You expect me to believe that? We’ll be lucky if we see him actually perform a move.
Moving in for the kill, Holy comes up complaining as Raoul slides his head between the ropes. He winks at Lars who has returned to the announcers’ table. With a count from Morgan, Raoul starts circling once again.
Howard Butz: Circling, circling ... are we going to see a move? As Holly darts in, Raoul again slips between the ropes. Morgan sends Holly back. Lars: Did you see that? Simply brilliant. Howard Butz: You watching the same match I am? With a warning from Morgan, Raoul again uses the ropes to escape Holly’s clutches. From the floor, Holly’s second complains to Morgan, drawing his interest. Listening to her complaint keeps the ref away from the action, allowing Holly to go to work. Grabbing Raoul by the hair, she dumps him on the mat over the ropes.
Lars: NO! NO! NO! DON’T HURT HIM! WHY DOESN’T SOMEONE STOP THIS? A reverse chinlock has Raoul flailing at the air for dear life. Tapping the mat furiously goes unnoticed as Morgan is busy talking to the woman on the outside.
Howard Butz: I think Morgan is not paying attention ... Dillon Diamond: I disagree. I think Flaxen has his undivided attention. Howard Butz: Flaxen? Dillon Diamond: You really need to brush up on your skills. Unlike you, I make it my business to know what’s going on around here.
Howard Butz: How am I expected to know who this woman is? This is her first time here. Dillon Diamond: That’s no excuse. She’s been around the indy circuit for a few years now with some matches in both Europe and Asia. I believe her first title run was with RiverBoat Wrestling ...
Howard Butz: RiverBoat Wrestling? What a stupid name ... Pushing Raoul back against the ropes, Holly sends him across the ring, knocking him down with a chop to the chest on his return. An elbow drop leaves Raoul in pain and pinned.
Howard Butz: HOLLY WITH THE PIN! ONE! TWO! THR ... KICK OUT! THAT WAS CLOSE! Jumping up from his seat, Lars looks ready to jump into the ring, stopping with a warning from Morgan.
Lars: THAT BRUTE! Whipped hard into a corner, Raoul withers under a blistering series of chops and boots to the midsection that has him sliding to the seat of his pants. From the far corner, Holly goes into a full run, jumping onto Raoul and bouncing up and down with a bronco buster.
Howard Butz: BRONCO BUSTER! Dillon Diamond: MAGNIFICENT! OH, WHAT A LUCKY MAN! Lars: Raoul isn’t built for punishment, he’s too dainty and delicate! Jumping back to her feet, Holly heads back to her corner and slaps hands with Flaxen. Pulling himself to his feet, Raoul looks ready to throw up as he waves a hand in front of his face obviously offended by an odor. Rushing to Raoul’s aid, Lars is sent scurrying back to his seat by Morgan. With Morgan busy, Holly returns to the attack only to take an eye rake. Blinded, she is easy prey for a low blow.
Howard Butz: Is that necessary? Lars: Is it necessary to bully such a delicate person? Flat on her back, Holly has her legs held in the air. After taunting her, Raoul drops down with a head butt to her crotch.
Dillon Diamond: YES! Howard Butz: Can’t you get your mind out of the gutter for just one match? Dillon Diamond: No. Popping back to his feet, Raoul is once again staggering around the ring looking sick to his stomach while an upset Lars jumps up from his seat, drawing another warning from Morgan. Climbing up onto the ring apron, he has to physically restrained from entering. Raoul continues to work, dropping an elbow before making a cover.
Howard Butz: THERE’S A COVER! WAIT A SECOND! HERE COMES 69 DEGREES! Dillon Diamond: Now, we’ll see Jake take control! Rushing out from the back, both Corey and Kenny grab one of Raoul’s legs, pulling him from the ring and free of Holly. Rushing around the ring apron, the long haired Flaxen dives off ... only to be snatched out of the air by the referee. With a smile on his face, he puts her back up on the apron, waving a finger at her.
Dillon Diamond: Taking control just like I said. With Raoul crawling back into the ring, Jake turns on the 69ers with a stern warning before leveling them both with a double clothesline.
Howard Butz: JAKE JUST TOOK OUT 69 DEGREES! AND NOW LARS IS HELPING COREY?!?! With a war now going on outside the ring, Flaxen gets into the act, using the ropes to launch herself into the air for a springboard somersault splash, wiping out Kenny. Inside the ring, Raoul is rolled up by Holly for the pin.
Howard Butz: HOLLY WITH THE COVER! THERE’S NO REFEREE! Rushing out from the back, the red, white and blue clad Pepsi Boy climbs up the ropes. Diving off, the masked man lands on top of Raoul as Holly is pulled from the ring at the last second by Flaxen. With Billy Blaze having joined the free-for-all as well as Security, Jake calls for the bell.
*** Ding *** Ding *** [With Flaxen holding Holly up, Jake runs up and lofts the confused Flaxen’s arm into the air before the show goes abruptly off the air.]
