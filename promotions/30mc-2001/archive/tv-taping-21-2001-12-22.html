TV Taping 21

22 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 2 —

[The camera opens on Jake Morgan wearing a black and white stripped shirt with
“REF” in big bold letters in the front of it, casually talking to his fellow referee, Robin when they are approached by Vic Parker. A winking Parker nods at Robin before turning towards Jake.]
Vic Parker: I’m glad you finally came around to my thinking. It’s about time the two best wrestlers in this promotion met.
[With a confused look on his face, Morgan looks from Robin to Parker.] Jake Morgan: What?!?!? Can you read? [Now confused, Parker looks to the shrugging Robin.] Vic Parker: Yeah ... Jake Morgan: What does it say on my shirt? Vic Parker: Ref? Jake Morgan: Riiiiiiiiight ... [Shaking his head, Morgan storms off, followed by the shrugging Robin, leaving Parker even more confused. On the move, the camera finds Lars and Raoul, in pink tights making their way through the back.]
Lars: We really have to do something nice for the winner of that contract thingy. Just a little something to welcome them into the fold. A cream puff basket, perhaps?
Raoul: You mean Gideon Frost or that delightful little Tommy Trevor? Lars: Of course, you big silly. Raoul: Lars, I just had an ephiany. Lars: Do tell. Raoul: What if Tommy wins? Maybe he’d be a good match for “Big Unit”? Lars: Oh Raoul, what I have in mind is just a little more ... “Unbelievable”. [As they share a giggle, a leather clad man walks up, tearing off his mirrored shades, revealing himself to be Bad Boy Corey.]
Lars: Oh, how I like that look ... Bad Boy Corey: I think you’re going to like this even better. Chris Cortez has been running his mouth about booting all of you from 30MC by himself, starting with you, Lars ... NEXT WEEK!
[With a gasp from the PiPsters, a clock’s ticking loudly sounds in the background. With the screen going black, the clock face of a logo hurdles through the ebony space before settling into view. The clock face fades into the background, giving way to faces and names that flash by quickly.
Vic Parker - One Man Boy Band, Chris Cortez - The Mean Green Mascot - Mexican superstar, Ieta Fujita - Ricky Starr - The pink clad team of Pretty in Pink,
Lars and Billy Blaze with Raoul - Agent of L.O.V.E., Harry Palmer - Weed - All American Buddy Lee Jr. - the heavenly Angel and finally, the Television Champion, Jake Morgan and an angry Angel Rotten. As the last of the pictures fade, they give way to the Snackdown! logo spinning into view, tumbling end over end before coming to a complete stop. With a smashing sound, the logo is obliterated into a million slivers, fading into the camera making a swing through a small but packed television studio then focusing in on two men sitting at the ringside announcers’ table. The two are a contrast in looks, one in a sports coat while the other one is in a Hawaiian shirt, leather pants and a matching hat.
Howard Butz: HELLO ... and welcome to the 30 Minute Countdown! I’m Howard Butz and he’s Dillon Diamond! [Diamond salutes the fans.] Howard Butz: We’ve got Christmas right around the corner but tonight, we’ve got an early present.
Dillon Diamond: We sure do, Howard. Jake Morgan puts his Television title on the line against Vic Parker! It has all the makings of a great match. Two talented men going toe-to-toe inside the squared circle.
Howard Butz: Will we see the crowning of a new champion tonight? Dillon Diamond: When you have guys of this calibre, anything can happen Howard Butz: Or we might just find that Vic bit off just a little more than he could chew by calling out Morgan.
Dillon Diamond: That’s possible but after seeing Vic earlier in the week, I’d have to say that if he’s ever been up for a match ... THIS IS THE ONE! [A chorus of boos from the audience marks the arrival of Jake from the back. Stepping out, Morgan makes his way ringside, threatening fans along the way before having a seat. Looking at Butz, Morgan points to the lettering on his shirt with a smile, pointing to the block letters “ANNOUNCER” across his chest.]
Jake Morgan: Glad to see you back after being exiled last week by Lars and company. Howard Butz: I left on my own. Jake Morgan: Sure thing. I do have to say, the show was much better for it. Great job there, Dillon. Dillon Diamond: Uh ... thanks, Jake. Howard Butz: You seem a little upbeat for a man in jeopardy of losing his title. Jake Morgan: I’m an upbeat kind of guy ... let’s roll the video tape ... Howard Butz: Huh? [The three fade from view as a clip rolls, finding Weed and Ieata Fujita talking. Walking up behind the two, Jake Morgan reaches up and grabs both men by the ears and begins to twist. In a firm grasp, their faces contort in pain as they cry out.]
Jake Morgan: Hello boys, how’s it going? [Both men twist their heads to see who has them, which causes their ears to be twisted even more.]
Weed: Dude ... THAT HURTS! Ieata Fujita: SI! SI! SI! [Twisting sharply, he releases them. Both men hop around rubbing their ears before an uncaring Jake.]
Weed: What’s the deal? Jake Morgan: SOMEONE broke into my dressing room earlier this week and stole MY food. So, when I went to get some ... it was gone. You two wouldn’t happen to know who did this, would you?
[Weed and Ieata look at each other in fear, neither one wanting to answer.] Weed: Nope, no idea at all. Anything we can do to help? [Jake gets a big smile on his face, putting his arms around the shoulders of both men. Flinching, they stay put.]
Jake Morgan: Boys, I'm glad you asked. Come with me. [Locking them into a headlock, he drags them off. Stopping in front of dressing room, he lets the go, and opens the door, finding the place trashed.]
Jake Morgan: Mean Green cleaned this room up ... Weed: He didn't do a good job. Ieata: Si, no es bueno. Jake Morgan: ... but I trashed it looking for my property. The stuff you two stole. [Both close their eyes and gulp.] Jake Morgan: So here is how you two losers are going to make it right. You’re going to go to the Janitor’s closet, grab all of the cleaning supplies you can find and clean this room up. I want it spotless and if ANYTHING I consider important is missing when I get back, you two are DEAD.
[He slaps both men on the back hard, and walks off before the camera fades. With a smooth transition, the camera returns to a grinning Jake.] Howard Butz: O ... kay. We’re ready for our first match. We’ve got Tommy Trevor already in the ring and here comes Gideon Frost. Jake Morgan: KFC. Howard Butz: What? Jake Morgan: KFC. Kicking for Christ. [With both men in the ring, referee Orly Diaz goes over last minute insructions before nearing the announcer’s table. After pointing a finger at Morgan, he calls for the bell.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: Looks like the ref isn’t going to be taking any of your abuse tonight. Jake Morgan: Abuse? From me? I’m not here to evaluate his work, tonight I’m in the official capacity to work as an announcer. Can’t you read, Howie?
Howard Butz: Whatever. Locking up in the center of the ring, Frost gains control with a quick knee to the stomach. Spinning behind Trevor, Frost takes a back elbow and is reversed.
With a hammerlock in place, Trevor is rocked with an elbow before finding himself in the same position. Rolling around Trevor, Frost locks on a side headlock.
Howard Butz: Irish whip ... SPINNING BACK KICK! Flattened by the stiff blow, a stunned, Trevor finds his legs wrapped up and Frost locking in an STF. Crying out in pain, Trevor is close enough to finger the ropes for a break.
Jake Morgan: There’s Diaz not breaking the wrestlers fast enough. Howard Butz: That was fast and orderly. Jake Morgan: To the untrained eye, maybe but I’m a professional Howard Butz: Oh you’re a pro alright. Spun around, Trevor counters an Irish whip with a knee to the stomach. A quick swinging neckbreaker later, Frost is vacating the ring for a breather. Using the ropes, Trevor propels himself to the outside with a plancha, bringing the fans to their feet.
Howard Butz: Tommy Trevor now taking the battle to the outside. After several forearms, Trevor whips Frost around but is countered and sent slamming into the ring barrier. Lifted into the air, Trevor is brought down across the metal structure stomach first. Dropping to his knees in pain, Trevor takes a drop kick to the face.
Howard Butz: You know that hurt. Jake Morgan: Ho hum ... this is a best of three matches? Howard Butz: If Frost pulls off the win, yes, they’ll face each other next week at Season’s Beatings.
Jake Morgan: You mean I might have to endure this again? Dragging Trevor across the barrier, Frost listens to the ref and rolls his opponent back into the ring. Using the ropes, he comes flying in with a slingshot leg drop. Hooking a leg, he covers Trevor.
Howard Butz: ONE! TWO! SHOULDER UP! Dillon Diamond: That was close. Jake Morgan: Slow count. Pulling Trevor off the deck, Frost lifts him into the air and holds him for a vertical suplex. Sliding free, he shoves Frost into the ropes, tagging him with a clothesline on the return. Shoving Frost into the corner, Trevor opens up with reverse knife hands until a counter. Spun around, Trevor is slammed into the turnbuckle where he’s forcefed Frost’s chops.
Howard Butz: A steady diet of knife hand chops from Frost. Speaking of diets, how’s yours coming along, Jake?
Dillon Diamond: Don’t bait him, Howard. Jake Morgan: That’s okay, Dillon. You know Butz, maybe I should have a few shirts printed up for you that say COMEDIAN across the front. Oh wait ... that means you’d have to be funny. Forget that idea.
Driven to his knees, Trevor nails Frost with an uppercut. Back on his feet, he lands several punches between Frost’s eyes until Diaz steps in with a warning for a low blow. Pulling back on his short hair, Trevor shows that Frost pulled his hair.
Jake Morgan: Yup ... this one should have been thrown out. Diaz sleeping on the job again ... oh wait, Frost would win and we’d have to see a third match ...
COME ON TREVOR! Following a snap suplex, a Trevor cover lasts for a one count. A hip toss is rolled into a La Majistral cradle.
Howard Butz: ONE! TWO! THR ... NO! Jumping to his feet, a whining Trevor shoves Diaz. Not intimidated, Diaz shoves right back. Stepping back, Trevor calls for a time out.
Howard Butz: I guess we’re not going to see Chicken Man today, are we Jake? Jake Morgan: With the way security is in this place, you just never know. Dillon Diamond: Don’t bait him, Howard. Howard Butz: Right. Arguing with Diaz, Trevor gives Frost time to clear his head. Tapping Trevor on the shoulder, Frost hits him with a massive clothesline that spins the young wrestler through the air. Pulling him back to his feet, Frost walks him into the corner before introducing his head into the turnbuckle repeatedly.
Howard Butz: Trevor staggering around the ring ... Frost wraps him up ... THERE IT IS ... THE DRAGON SLEEPER! Watching closely, Diaz looks for a submission as as Trevor’s arms go limp. Howard Butz: I don’t think Frost is going to take any chances here. He needs this victory or it’s sayanora Gideon Frost.
Jake Morgan: What kind of name is Gideon anyway? It doesn’t matter, it looks like he’s got himself a win here, just like I thought he would.
Howard Butz: What? You were cheering on Trevor just a minute ago. Jake Morgan: I did not. You know as well as I do that I’m all about winning ... and Jake Morgan is going with the winner here. Right, Dillon? Dillon Diamond: Whatever you say, Jake. Don’t bait him, Howard. Releasing his grip, Frost lifts Trevor up across his shoulders. Looking up, Trevor puts up no fight as Frost locks his arms around his helpless opponent’s waist, Dropping to his knees, Frost drives him backfirst into the mat.
Jake Morgan: That’s all she wrote. What did I say about winners? Howard Butz: Frost heading for the ropes ... There is a howl from the crowd as the masked Chicken Man rushes out from the back. Wearing a neckbrace, the man propels himself into the ring, using the ropes to hit Orly Diaz in the back with a drop kick. Using the ropes upsets
Frost’s balance, sending him dropping crotch first across them. Howard Butz: CHICKEN MAN! IT’S CHICKEN MAN! [end of part a]

— Part 2 of 2 —

Jake Morgan: LOOK AT THE SIZE OF THAT GUY! After pumping his arms in the air, the masked man stomps Diaz several times to loud booing before leaving the ring.
Howard Butz: What are you talking about, Jake? That guy’s not nearly the size as the guy who attacked Diaz twice before.
Jake Morgan: Are you kidding me? Look at the muscle ... the power of the guy. I think it’s time you got those new contacts.
Howard Butz: I don’t wear contacts ... Jake Morgan: Perhaps you should. I think THIS proves that I’M NOT CHICKEN MAN. Just like I’ve been saying all along. Right, Dillon? Dillon Diamond: I didn’t get a good look at him. It happened all too fast. With the ref and Trevor down, Frost is doubled over in pain on top of the ropes. Using the ropes, Trevor pulls himself to his feet.
Jake Morgan: My work is never done. Getting up from his seat, Morgan pulls off his shirt, revealing another one underneath - the black and white ref’s stripes. Diving into the ring, Morgan starts a count on Frost.
Howard Butz: This just stinks, Dillon! I’m betting Morgan set this all up. Dillon Diamond: Resist the urge, Howard, you can do it. Howard Butz: Knock it off. You know exactly what I mean. He’s been after Diaz for weeks now ... months even.
Dillon Diamond: We know it wasn’t, Jake because he was sitting right here with us. Howard Butz: Oh, please. Using the ropes to support himself, Trevor drags himself along before pushing Frost back onto the top turnbuckle. Climbing himself, Trevor jumps into the air, wrapping his legs around Frost’s neck, falling backwards and dragging the man over with a hurricanrana.
Howard Butz: I don’t like this one bit. Out of it himself, Trevor drags Frost closer to the ropes before dipping between them. from the apron, Trevor uses the ropes to propel himself back in, twisting in the air and coming down with a double knee drop. Rolling off,
Trevor hooks a leg. Howard Butz: Trevor with the pin ... ONE! TWO! FROST WITH A LEG ON THE ROPES! *** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: WHAT?!?!? MORGAN CALLED FOR THE BELL! HE’S AWARDING THE MATCH TO TOMMY TREVOR?!?!? OH NO! GIDEON FROST WAS SCREWED! [Leaving the ring quickly, Morgan makes his way to the back to a healthy round of boos from the fans.]
Howard Butz: I DON’T BELIEVE THIS! DILLON, I CAN’T BELIEVE THIS! LOSING IS ONE THING BUT TO LOSE OUT ON A CONTRACT THIS WAY JUST ... WELL, IT JUST SUCKS! [A yelling Butz fades from view into a commercial.] *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** The Ultimate Cheeseburger ... $1.99 of cholesteral. Football. The Carnivores. Cheerleaders. Referees. Dinosaur mascot. Blimps landing on kick returners. Funny commercial. Terrible burger. Jack in the Box. Good food ... except for their burgers.
You know it, I know it ... Dr. Pepper makes the world taste better! The only problem is the rest of the country doesn't know it. Don't believe me? Just ask for it in Milwaukee and see what they give you ... “We got root beer.”
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** [Returning from the commercial, the camera finds Newton in his office with a brand new title belt sitting on his desk. With a smile on his face, he sits back in his chair.]
Newton: Life is good ... the Young Gun belt all taken care of and NO Lost Soulers showing up. Heh ... Losers is more like it. [Newton bolts upright in his chair as the door opens and Brody walks in. A surprised look overcomes his face as he looks down at the belt.]
Brody: Is that ... I thought it was ... [Sighing, Newton shakes his head.} Newton: How many times do I have to tell you? Brody: I know, I know ... you don’t pay me to think. [Leaving the office, the camera finds a leather clad woman making her way through the hallway. With her black hair, the woman’s first appearance was by the side of LSW owner Malicea McDeath as she threw the 30MC Young Gun belt into a garbage can. Rounding a corner, the woman is stopped by a humming Chris
Cortez.] Chris Cortez: Hey baby ... look at you. [Giving her a once over, a leering Cortez nods his head.] Chris Cortez: The name’s Chris Cortez ... the One Man Boy Band. What’s your name? [Smiling, the woman shyly turns her head allowing Cortez a moment a second to whip out his Binaca spray and spritz it into his mouth. Turning, she whispers her name.]
Woman: Purity. Chris Cortez: What was that? [Giggling, she motions him to come closer. With a sly look, he moves in closer ... close enough for the woman to hit him with a head butt, dropping him to his knees. Before walking away, she flips him off.
Leaving them, the camera returns to the ring and a pacing Vic Parker.] Vic Parker: Jake, come on down and let’s get our match started! [Parker smiles as Jake steps out from the back. With a grin on his face, Morgan in his black and white stripped ref shirt rolls into the ring.]
Vic Parker: Let’s get a ref and start this up! [A cheer dies down as Morgan waves him off.] Jake Morgan: Here’s a simple question for you. Can you read? [Confused, Parker looks for support from the fans.] Vic Parker: Sure. Jake Morgan: What does the shirt say? Vic Parker: Ref? Jake Morgan: Right. Vic Parker: I thought ... Jake Morgan: You thought? I promised you a title match and that’s exactly what you’re going to get.
[A smirking Morgan nods his head.] Jake Morgan: Wait a second ... you thought it was against me? Vic Parker: Yeah ... Jake Morgan: Not a chance, Vicki ... before that happens, you have to earn it. You start that tonight with Harry Palmer. [Pointing to the back, Morgan smiles as a confused Palmer makes his way to the ring. With the fans screaming for Morgan’s head, he calls for the bell.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: He’s done it again. Jake has screwed someone out of a match twice this evening!
Dillon Diamond: Screwed? Jake said Vic Parker would be getting a title match tonight and he is.
Hesitating at first, the two wrestlers start circling as Morgan eggs them on to fight. Locking up, Palmer puts Parker into a side headlock. Pushing him off,
Parker sends the man into the far ropes. Howard Butz: Parker hitting the deck on the return ... Palmer continuing on through. Leap frog ... NO! HEAD SCISSOR TAKE DOWN!
Bouncing off the middle rope, Parker nails the seated Palmer with a drop kick to the face. Pulling him back to his feet, Parker glances at a yawning Morgan before sending Palmer across the ring. Hitting the corner, Palmer is a target for a running corner splash.
Howard Butz: Palmer staggers out of the corner ... Dillon Diamond: TIMBER! Falling flat on his face, the downed Palmer is ignored as Parker points an accusing finger at the ref. Waving it off, Morgan ignores him. Pulled to his feet, Palmer’s eye rake gives him an advantage.
Howard Butz: I suppose Morgan didn’t see that? Dillon Diamond: See what? Twisting Parker’s arm, Palmer sends him across the ring, taking him down with a clothesline. A fast leg drop leads to a cover.
Howard Butz: ONE! TWO! Dillon Diamond: That was close. Howard Butz: Not as close as that fast count. Palmer going up the ropes ... Leaping off, Palmer is hit with a standing drop kick that hits him in midair. Holding a hand to his eye, a complaining Parker gets to his feet. Howard Butz: Parker letting Jake know about that fast count. Dillon Diamond: I didn’t see a fast count. Howard Butz: Of course you didn’t. Propping Palmer up into the corner, Parker goes up the ropes. Facing the fans, he drops down onto Palmer’s lap. Grabbing him behind the head, he throws his opponent out of the corner with a monkey flip.
Howard Butz: Haven’t seen that before. Even half blinded, Vic Parker is a force to be reckoned with.
Rolling back to his feet, Parker acknowledges the fans’ chanting. Back up the ropes, Parker throws himself off, sticking a frog splash.
Howard Butz: THAT WAS PURELY UNBELIEVABLE! Hooking a leg, Parker waits for a count. Looking up, he finds Jake pulling his shirt off. Howard Butz: WHAT IS GOING ON NOW? WHAT’S HIS SHIRT SAY? Dillon Diamond: KING OF THE THREE WAY DANCE! A big boot to the face knocks Parker off of Palmer. Pulling Parker to his feet, Jake whips him across the ring, grabbing him up on the return and hoisting the body into the air before spinning around and planting the challenger into the canvas with a spinebuster. With the fans howling their displeasure, Jake tosses
Palmer through the ropes to the outside. Howard Butz: I can’t believe this. This is insane. Does he actually think he’s going to get away with this?
Dillon Diamond: You better believe. This is all Jake and he’s sending a message. Howard Butz: What message is that? Dillon Diamond: He’s the King of the Three Way Dance!. After dropping Parker on his head with a brain buster, Jake vacates the ring when Orly Diaz calls for the bell, throwing this match out due to interference.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: Thank you, Orly Diaz! [The camera zooms in on Morgan’s smirk before pulling back to show Neal the intern making his way out from the back with a mic in hand.]
Howard Butz: Here comes Neal? Neal: Not so fast, Jake! [Morgan’s smirk fades away as he turns to find Neal standing there with a video cassette in hand.]
Neal: Your days of bullying this promotion are over! No more picking on me or anyone else ... and this video tape is all the proof I need!
[Jake’s expression changes to the deer caught in the headlights look as the show comes to an end.]
