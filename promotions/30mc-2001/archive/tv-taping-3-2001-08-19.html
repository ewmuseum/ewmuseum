TV Taping 3

19 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 2 —

[The camera opens on Newton’s bodyguard, Brody, pacing in the parking lot of the 30MC studio when a brand spanking new Ford F350 comes screeching to a halt in front of him, kicking up a cloud of dust.
The young driver of the truck hops out, flipping his keys in the direction of Brody, then slapping him on the shoulder before walking off. With a confused look, Brody looks down on the keys before looking up at the grinning visage of the young driver.]
Driver: Bet you thought I forgot ya ... didn’t ya? [Making a clicking sound, the man reaches into his pocket, pulling forth a roll of cash. Stripping off a few bills, he slaps them into Brody’s hand before walking off, shouting back over his shoulder.]
Driver: Take good care of her ... I just had her detailed this morning. [Brody scratches his head before the camera leaves him. Moving elsewhere, the camera finds Neal the intern carrying an overflowing box before rounding a corner and nearly being bowled over by the Trashman.]
Trashman: Whoa ... let me help you with that. [Picking up a few of the items that spilled out, Trash puts them back in the box.] Trashman: What's all this? Neal: Just a few of the leftover props that Newton told me to get rid of. [Picking up a large paddle with the letters "SCB" along it's surface, Trash grins at the intern.]
Trashman: Mind if I keep this? [Shaking his head, Neal watches Trash walk off with his new "toy". Moving off, the camera finds Lars, Raoul and Shorti inside their dressing room before a pink flower patterned dressing screen. On the other side, a silhouete can be made out, hurriedly putting clothes on under the careful scrutiny of two pairs of hungry eyes.]
Billy Blaze: Guys? I’m not so sure about this ... Lars: Not another word. We go away for a weekend and suddenly you’re back under the influence of that ruffian, Trash ... what kind of name is Trashman, anyway?
Raoul: A dirty one, no doubt. [Stepping out from behind the screen, Billy poses in his tight one piece pink vinyl jumper with matching boots. Both Lars and Raoul lick their lips while
Shorti closes her eyes and shakes her head.] Billy Blaze: This thong is just a bit too tight ... [Shorti slaps her forehead when they fade from view as a clock’s ticking loudly sounds. With the screen going black, the clockface of a logo hurtles through the ebony space before settling into view. With the clockface fading into the background faces and names flash by quickly.
The Mean Green Mascot - Mexican superstar, Ieta Fujita - Ricky Starr - The pink clad team of Pretty in Pink, Lars and Billy Blaze with Raoul - Agent of
L.O.V.E., Harry Palmer - Weed - All American Buddy Lee Jr. - and finally, the Television Champion, Jake Morgan and an angry Angel Rotten. As the last of the pictures fade, the camera makes a swing through a small but packed television studio before focussing in on two men sitting at the ringside announcers’ table. The two men are a contrast in looks, one in a sports coat while the other one is in a Hawaiian shirt, leather pants and a matching hat.]
Howard Butz: Welcome to 30 Minute Countdown. I’m Howard Butz and the man on my right just happens to be my broadcast partner, Dillon Diamond.
[Diamond gives the camera a small tip of the hat.] Howard Butz: Tonight, we have the returning Lars taking on the Trashman in singles action ... What are you doing?
[A sheepish looking Diamond stares innocently back at Butz.] Dillon Diamond: What?!?! [Pointing at a small bottle in Diamond’s hand.] Howard Butz: That? What is that and why are you rubbing that on your arm? Dillon Diamond: This? Just a little moisterizer courtesy of Lars and Raoul. While you’ll have the alligator skin, mine will be as supple as a baby’s bottom ... Howard Butz: Oh spare us. We’ll be right back after this commercial break with Lars versus the Trashman!
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Listen to the MIX 102.9 with Jeff and Anna in the morning! What kind of crap do they play? Who knows ... I’m not up that early in the morning, anyway.
M&M’s. You know which commercial ... the green naked M&M caught without her shell on. It’s been a long time since they pushed the green M&M as an aphrodisiac angle. Five bucks says they won’t push the cancer causing red dye
M&M’s anytime soon. *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** [Back from the break finds Lars inside the ring while the rest of his entourage work the fans into a frenzy. Across the ring from him are his opponent, the
Trashman and female referee, Robin with last minute instructions.] Lars: Before this match gets underway, I’d like to take a minute and discuss NEGATIVE influences ... it seems this place is just full of them who prey on young ... impressionable ... muscular ... undulating ...
[Looking directly at Billy, Lars gets glassy-eyed for a moment giving Trash an opening. Charging in before Lars can finish, Trash knocks him to the mat with clubbing forearms.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Hammering away at Lars, Trash is finally pulled off by the ref as the action spills into the ropes. Using the precious seconds afforded them by the ref,
Billy pulls Lars from the ring to the outside for a breather. Launching himself into action, Trash takes the two pink clad men to the floor with a plancha. Coming up with fists, Trash sends Raoul off scurrying behind
Billy’s sister, Shorti. Howard Butz: Trashman taking the action back into the ring. Criss-crossing the ring, Trash slams a shoulder into an already wobbily Lars, sending him crashing to the canvas.
Howard Butz: THERE’S A COVER! ONE! TWO! KICK OUT! Dillon Diamond: I think the Trashman will find Lars made of sterner stuff ... sort of like this moisterizer with aloe. Howard Butz: Not this again? Dillon Diamond: I’m sure it covers better than you cover your bets ... Howard Butz: Will you drop my gambling problem already? Dillon Diamond: AHA! I knew it! Howard Butz: Wait ... no! FORGET IT! Trashman working the leg with a spinning toehold. After a severe leg stomping, Trash drops a leg across Lars’ knee before kipping to his feet and again attacking the leg.
Howard Butz: Trash with the leg ... HE’S GOING FOR THE FIGURE FOUR! Entwining legs, Trash falls back to the mat, leaning back trying to damage Lars’ legs. Howard Butz: The ref watching closely for a submission here. Dillon Diamond: He’s almost got it really locked in. He gets it in, this match might be over ... fast.
In pain, Lars starts to roll his body from side to side looking for an escape, reaching back for the ropes but being just too far out of reach. With encouragement from his corner and and the chanting of “P-I-P!” from the fans, Lars fights through the pain.
Howard Butz: He’s rolling ... rolling ... LARS WITH THE REVERSE! Rolling over, Lars puts the pressure on Trash’s legs but not for too long as he rolls into the ropes, within Trash’s reach.
Howard Butz: Robin calling for a break. Dillon Diamond: I’ll be in a little one-on-one action with Robin ... Howard Butz: You leave her alone! Dillon Diamond: WHAT?!?!? I’m sure the fans want to hear what our lady referee has to say. WIth both men using the ropes to pull themselves to their feet, they’re both limping. Shoving Trash into a corner, Lars comes in firing his stiff limp wristed reverse knife hands to his opponent’s chest.
Howard Butz: Lars with a handful of hair. There’s an elbow to the head. A shot to the head sents Trash heavily into the corner. Grabbing the middle ropes, Lars smacks his head repeatedly into Trash’s midsection, doubling him over with each blow.
Dragging Trash from the corner to the center of the ring, Lars locks him up in a standing head scissors where he shakes his fanny bringing a laugh from the small audience. Walking to the ropes, Lars invites his team mates up onto the ring apron, presenting Trash’s backside.
Howard Butz: Raoul up on the apron. And he’s spanking Trashman ... I’ve seen it all now. Dillon Diamond: Serves him right for hitting Raoul last week. With Robin in with a warning, Lars guides Trash back to the center of the ring where he drops to his knees, driving Trash’s face into the canvas. Rolling
Trash over, Lars scoops both of his opponent’s legs up over his shoulder, pinning him to the mat with his pumping pin.
Howard Butz: Lars with the cover ... With the ref admonishing Raoul and Shorti on the apron, no one is there to count the pinfall. With the fans starting up a count of their own, Robin turns back to her duty, dropping to the mat and slapping out a count.
Howard Butz: ONE! TWO! There’s an escape ... Getting back to his feet, Trash strikes with an eye rake, catching Lars by surprise. Whipping the blinded man across the ring, Trash nails him on the return with a drop kick to the face. Climbing the ropes, he points at Lars’ partners before leaping from the top.
Howard Butz: THERE’S A SENTON! Rolling to his feet, Trash draws boos as he heads towards Lars’ corner, pointing at Raoul then Shorti. Ready to rush the ring, Raoul is pulled back by
Billy. Waving her off, Trash climbs the ropes then dives off from the top, spinning his body and landing with a corkscrew splash that shakes the ring.
Howard Butz: WOW! This could be it for Lars. Dillon Diamond: Should be but it looks like Trash isn’t finished yet. With Billy doing his best to restrain Raoul from entering the ring and drawing the attention of the ref, Trash pulls Lars to his feet while Shorti makes her way around the ring.
Wobbling Lars with several punches to the head, he has one blocked then takes two fingers to the eyes, ala the Three Stooges. Blasting Trash to the mat with a clothesline off of a whip into the ropes, Lars regains control of the match.
Howard Butz: What does Shorti have? Liberating Trashman’s new “toy” from his corner, Shorti makes her way up onto the ring apron as Lars lifts Trash into the air. Near the ropes, the struggling Trash is able to flip behind Lars and escape. Wrapping him up around the waist, he pushes his opponent forward right into the swinging Shorti who catches Lars across the noggin before taking the brunt of the charging wrestlers, knocking her from the apron to the floor.
Howard Butz: Shorti with a hard tumble to the floor. I hope she’s alright. Dillon Diamond: Ouch. That was a pretty bad spill. Using the ropes, Trash rolls Lars up. With his tights being held, Lars struggles as his shoulders get put to the mat.
Howard Butz: THERE’S A COVER! Finally getting Raoul and Billy from the apron, Robin turns to see the pinning predicament.
Howard Butz: THE REF WITH A COUNT! ONE! TWO! THREE! *** Ding *** Ding ***

— Part 2 of 2 —

Howard Butz: The Trashman picks up the victory over Lars.
Dillon Diamond: A shot to the head and a handful of tights will do that every time. [Rolling from the ring, Trash collects his paddle and hightails it for the back followed by the booing of the audience while Raoul and Billy come to the aid of
Lars and Shorti. Leaving ringside, the camera makes it’s way into the back to find Jake Morgan sitting on a couch in his locker room, across from a 37 inch
TV, VCR and a stack of video tapes. Looking back over the couch, the TV champ scowls.] Jake Morgan: Will you hurry up? I want to watch this before my match tonight. [From off-camera Angel’s voice can be heard.] Angel: What’s the big deal? You’ve got it taped. [Rolling his eyes, Jake answers.] Jake Morgan: I know but Newton has me doing some Promotional thing after the show, so I can't watch it then and with a show like Murder in Small Town X, anything can be a clue to the murderer. I want to figure it out early. Right now my money’s on the reporter.
[A hand places a Coors Light on Jake’s shoulder. Without looking at it, he takes a swig. At the same time, Angel sits down beside him. With a grimace Jake spits the beer out and turns to Angel.]
Jake Morgan: WHAT THE HELL IS THIS? [Angel looks at him like he’s stupid.] Angel: Beer. [Shaking his head, he corrects her.] Jake Morgan: No, this isn’t beer. This is like sex in a canoe; it’s f***ing close to water. I asked for a beer not this “light” crap.
[Angel takes his rant in stride, reaching over to pat his belly.] Angel: Considering how much you've been eating lately, I thought you could use something “light.”
[Jake looks down at his belly, then back at her when the door to the dressing room opens and the Mean Green mascot steps in carrying several bags of Taco
Bell.] Mean Green: Run for the border anyone? [Angel slaps her forehead as the smiling Morgan tears the bags from his hands.] Jake Morgan: Now, this is what I like. [With a big grin on his face, the near salivating Morgan fades from view in favor of a commercial break.]
***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** BEER. It’s good. In fact, you should be drinking one right now. If you were, you might be able to get fine chicks like those in our commercials instead of sitting on your ass watching wrestling at one in the morning on a Saturday. So drink beer, because if you drink enough, anyone or anything can look really good. BEER. Remember, its cheaper than crack!
Taco Bell is now open later. So get your fat ass off the couch and come get fatter! Yo quiero Gordo Americanos!
Scenes of poor children in some third world country flash by as the Christian Children’s Fund asks you to make a difference in their world for as little as 89 cents a day! Food, medicine, education all possible on 89 cents a day! Won’t you help make a difference? ***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** [With a return from the commercial, the camera zooms in on Butz and Diamond ringside.] Howard Butz: Welcome back, fans. Next up, we’ve got ... [A loud smattering of boos announce the arrival of the Television Champion, Jake Morgan followed by Angel Rotten and the Mean Green Mascot.] Dillon Diamond: I think there’s your answer. [Diamond points up at Morgan making his way towards the announcers’ table. Stepping up, Morgan drops three large bags of Taco Bell on the table, readjusts his title belt on his shoulder then swipes the mic from Butz.]
Jake Morgan: Gimme that! [Shoving Butz down into his chair, the champ shoots both men a look before scanning the audience.]
Jake Morgan: That’s right. You just keep up your booing. Come on. You can do better than that.
[Morgan gets a surprised look on his face as their answer is a loud cheer. It’s not meant for him of course but for Buddy Lee Jr. being led to the ring by Newton, Brody and the young man in sweats from the beginning of the show.
After they file into the ring, Morgan steps up onto the ring apron.] Jake Morgan: Isn’t this just great. There’s more of ya. Our illustrious little brat of an owner. His big bad bodyguard who can’t fight his way out of a wet paper sack.
[Brody is held back as he moves forward by the smiling Newton.] Jake Morgan: Our cross-eyed soooooooperheeeeero and ... Newton’s boyfriend? [Stepping into the ring, Morgan eyes the young man in the red sweats. With a grin on his face, the man pushes his Oakleys back on top of his spiky dark hair and steps forward.]
Young man: No man, you got it all wrong. The name’s Shawn Avery ... Jake Morgan: You know what? I don’t give a rat’s ass who you are ... [Stepping forward, Newton takes the mic away from Morgan.] Newton: Oh boohoo ... let me walk a mile in the great Jake Morgan’s shoes ... [Before uttering another word, loud Mariachi music rips through the PA system, cutting him off. Looking upset, Newton stomps his foot several times before being restrained by Brody.
Stepping out from the back, Ieta Fujita determindly makes his way to the ring. Inside, he scans everyone before looking up at Morgan.] Ieta Fujita: I ... WANT ... YOU! [Snapping up the mic, Morgan steps into Fujita’s face.] Jake Morgan: You want another chance at me? Is that it? Even after I kicked your little ass all over this ring last week?
[Nodding his head, Fujita eyes Morgan’s belt.] Ieta Fujita: This time, you put something up. Jake Morgan: Oh, you want me to put something on the line this time? [Morgan rubs the faceplate of his belt when Buddy Lee Jr. steps forward.] Buddy Lee Jr.: Hold on a second. If you’re putting something on the line, Morgan ... let’s make this a threeway dance! [Both Morgan and Fujita scowl at Junior as the fans cheer loudly.] Ieta Fujita: You ... put that on the line! [Spinning around, Fujita points at the bags of Taco Bell sitting on the announcer’s table. With a grin on his face, Morgan takes a step back.]
Jake Morgan: Let’s do it. [Taking the belt off his shoulder and leveling Buddy Lee with it, Morgan sends everyone scurrying as referee Orly Diaz calls for the bell.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: We’ve got ourselves a threeway dance with Morgan’s lunch on the line? Now, I’ve really seen everything.
Throwing his belt down, Morgan misses Fujita with a clothesline. Spinning around, Morgan barely dodges a back kick then blocks several punches thrown at him. Moving forward, he rams a shoulder into Fujita, knocking him to the mat but not for long as the Asian star kips to his feet.
Grabbed up around the waist and lifted into the air, Fujita flips himself up and over Morgan, landing catlike on his feet behind the champ. Spinning around,
Morgan comes up with fists at the ready while Fujita stands there waiting in a martial pose. As the two eye each other, the fans cheer the rapidfire action.
Howard Butz: The fans starting to get into the action. Morgan and Fujita circling. With a warning for clinched fists, Morgan is hit from behind by Buddy Lee. Slammed into a corner, the champ takes several chops to the chest, counted off by the fans. Grabbed from behind, Lee is spun around and confronted by Fujita.
Both men go nose to nose, pointing at Morgan slumped in the corner. Howard Butz: We’ve got a shoving match here. DIllon Diamond: Looks like both men want a piece. I don’t blame them with this bounty of food on the line.
Howard Butz: I think Buddy Lee Jr. isn’t interested in what’s in those bags. There is a loud slap as Angel smacks Diamond’s hand as he looks into one of the bags. Back inside the ring, both men shove the other before they are once again literally nose to nose. After a few words, the two step back and make motions with their hands.
Howard Butz: What is this? Dillon Diamond: They’re settling things the old fashioned way. Howard Butz: With a game of Rock, Paper, Scissors? Dillon Diamond: I understand that’s how gentlemen solve disputes where Fujita is from. Howard Butz: You don’t even know where Fujita is from. Dillon Diamond: WIth the music and his penchant for Taco Bell, I think it’s fairly obvious.
Winning, Buddy Lee grabs Morgan out of the corner and belly-to-belly suplexes him across the ring. Stepping back, he watches as Fujita pushes him back into the corner and nails him with a jump spin kick to the head.
Howard Butz: There’s a double whip into the ropes. Fujita on the run ... HURRICANRANA! Morgan hits the canvas before Buddy Lee drops down to make the cover. Diaz slaps out a two count when Fujita pulls him off of Morgan.
Howard Butz: We’re back to arguing. With their attention diverted, Morgan pulls himself to his feet. Whistling at his opponents, he charges them, sending them flying with a double clothesline.
Stomping Buddy Lee, Morgan works out some frustration before pulling him up and suplexing him.
Howard Butz: Fujita whipped into the corner ... Morgan with the corner splash. Now, he’s picking up Buddy Lee. There’s a whip into Fujita ... Dillon Diamond: Ouch. Did you hear the impact? That had to smart. Howard Butz: The champion with a shortarm clothesline. Climbing the ropes. Morgan’s perched up the top turnbuckle ... waiting ... waiting ... Waiting for Fujita to turn, Morgan dives off the top and slams into him with a stiff clothesline.
Dillon Diamond: I don’t think I’d want to be either one of those two men in the ring with Jake. You know one of these days he’s going to snap.
Pulling Futita up by the hair, Morgan pushes him into the air and drops him throatfirst across the ropes. Picking up Buddy Lee, Morgan pounds him into the mat with several suplexes and a sidewalk slam.
Howard Butz: Buddy Lee Jr. is taking a beating! The ref is pushed aside as Morgan tries to pull Junior to his feet. Seeing movement out of the corner of his eye, Morgan grabs Diaz quickly and holds him in front as a shield. A superkick catches the ref from behind.
Reaching into the ring, Mean Green trips up Fujita, sending him facefirst into the canvas. Pushing Fujita up into the air, Morgan sits down into a powerbomb and holds him for the pin.
Howard Butz: THERE’S NO ONE TO MAKE THE COUNT! Disgustedly, Morgan stomps Fujita several times before slapping Diaz’ face. Grabbed from behind, Morgan drops the ref and is sent flying with a release German suplex by Buddy Lee. Holding his head, Morgan escapes the ring, right into the arms of his compatriots.
Howard Butz: Buddy Lee with some words for Morgan. Dropping down, Junior covers an unmoving Fujita as Diaz slaps out a weak count. Howard Butz: Diaz with the count. ONE! TWO! THREE! *** Ding *** Ding *** Dillon Diamond: Jake Morgan was robbed. He does all the dirty work and Buddy Lee Jr. comes away with the prize. Nice work. Do you think he’ll share? Howard Butz: Will you stop? Folks, we are out of time. For my broadcast partner, I’m Howard Butz ... good night!
[The credits roll with Jake Morgan being led to the back.] *** 30 Minute Countdown© ***
