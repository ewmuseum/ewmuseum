30MC — Card Archive

TV Taping 2

11 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 2 —

[The camera opens on a pacing Young Gun Champ, Billy Blaze inside his locker room. Fidgeting with the belt over his shoulder, Billy looks up as the door opens and in steps his sister, Shorti.]
Billy Blaze: Well? Shorti: According to Neal, Lars and Raoul had an emergency. Something about a big sale? [Slamming a fist into his other hand, Billy squints his eyes.] Billy Blaze: Then, I’ll just have to take care of Trash by myself! Shorti: Drop it, Billy, he’s not worth it ... [Brushing past his sister, Billy leaves the room talking back over his shoulder.] Billy Blaze: Sorry, sis ... no one touches my sister and gets away with it! [With the door closing behind him, Shorti rolls her eyes before the camera is on the move, focussing in on Neal the intern, his mind elsewhere and a silly smile on his face.]
Voice: Neal ... [With no reaction, save for the goofy look on his face, the other person in the room shouts.]
Voice: NEAL!!! [With the intern snapping to attention, the camera pulls back to reveal the office of 30 Minute Countdown’s owner, Newton.]
Newton: Once again ... get rid of that. [Throwing a thumb behind him, Newton points Neal’s attention towards the large scale in the corner.]
Neal: Sure thing, boss. Newton: Any idea why it was kept in here? Neal: Uhm, yeah ... that’s for Jake Morgan’s weigh-in in his title fight. I’ll go get a dolly to get it out of here. Newton: On second thought, leave it right there. [The boss starts rubbing his chin when they fade from view as a clock’s ticking loudly sounds. With the screen going black, the clockface of a logo hurtles through the ebony space before settling into view. With the clockface fading into the background faces and names flash by quickly.
The Mean Green Mascot - Mexican superstar, Ieta Fujita - Ricky Starr - The pink clad team of Pretty in Pink, Lars and Billy Blaze with Raoul - Agent of
L.O.V.E., Harry Palmer - Weed - All American Buddy Lee Jr. - and finally, the Television Champion, Jake Morgan and an angry Angel Rotten. As the last of the pictures fade, the camera makes a swing through a small but packed television studio before focussing in on two men sitting at the ringside announcers’ table. The two men are a contrast in looks, one in a sports coat while the other one is in a Hawaiian short along with leather pants and a matching hat.]
Howard Butz: Howard Butz here and to my right, my broadcast partner, Dillon Diamond. [Diamond gives the camera a small tip of the hat.] Howard Butz: We’ve got alot of action starting off with a match between the Rookie, Ricky Starr and the strange Freak E but before we go to the ring, we’ve got Holly backstage with Harry Palmer ...
[The scene melts into the backstage area and an attractive young woman standing in front of the 30MC banner with the goofy looking Harry Palmer.]
Holly: Thanks guys. I’m standing here with Harry Palmer ... [Palmer makes a clicking noise before pushing his taped black framed galsses back on his nose.]
Harry Palmer: Hey .... chick ... [Holly can only roll her eyes as Palmer adjusts his ill fitting glasses, Looking from Palmer back into the camera, Holly’s yelp follows a shot that lands on her backside. After stiffening under the grinning Palmer’s shot, she slaps him across the face before storming off.
Returning from the back, the camera focuses in on the head shaking Butz and a grinning Diamond.]
Howard Butz: I can’t believe what I just saw. Dillon Diamond: Harry Palmer is all class. I bet the Agent of L.O.V.E. doesn’t wash that hand for at least a week. Howard Butz: Harry Palmer’s hygiene aside, we have to break for a commercial before our opening match. Don’t go away!
***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** BEER. It’s good. In fact, you should be drinking one right now. If you were, you might be able to get fine chicks like those in our commercials instead of sitting on your ass watching wrestling at one in the morning on a Saturday. So drink beer, because if you drink enough, anyone or anything can look really good. BEER. Remember, its cheaper than crack!
Taco Bell is now open later. So get your fat ass off the couch and come get fatter! Yo quiero Gordo Americanos!
Scenes of poor children in some third world country flash by as the Christian Children’s Fund asks you to make a difference in their world for as little as 89 cents a day! Food, medicine, education all possible on 89 cents a day! Won’t you help make a difference? ***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** [Back from the commercial, the camera zooms in on the ring and referee calling for the bell of the first match of the evening.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: Ricky Starr, Freak E and the bell means we’re underway, Moving in to lock up, Starr comes up empty handed as Freak moves in and darts away at the last second. Shaking his head as the strange Freak E lives up to his name, buzzing around the ring, drawing boos from the fans.
Howard Butz: Starr motioning for his opponent and Freak E takes the bait. There’s a collar and ... NO! Starr with a reverse knife hand. That sounded painful. Dillon Diamond: It sure did. Popping hands off of his opponent’s chest, Starr backs him up into the far ropes before spinning off with an arm and sending him across the ring. On the return, Starr ducks his head setting up for a back body drop but Freak with a little surprise dives over the top, spinning his body and pulling Starr to the mat with a sunset flip.
Howard Butz: THERE’S A PIN ATTEMPT! ONE! TWO! STARR WITH THE KICKOUT! Circling each other, Starr closes the distance and knees Freak E in the midsection. Grabbing an arm and sending him off, Starr flattens him on the return with a shoulder block. Throwing himself back against the ropes, the rookie shoots himself forward somersaulting into a leg drop.
Howard Butz: Ricky Starr with a cover. Referee Orly Diaz with a one count. You have to hook the leg ...
Dillon Diamond: There’s a reason they call him the rookie. He’ll learn. After locking up in the center of the ring, Freak E is able to get Starr across his shoulders in a fireman’s carry. Starting to spin, E loses his grip and
Starr slides off behind him. Driving a knee into his back, Starr grabs Freak E’s head and pulls him backwards, flattening him with an inverted DDT. Howard Butz: Beautiful escape. Dillon Diamond: I’ve been in the business a long time, Howie ... I can see this kid’s got all the tools. He just needs a little seasoning.
Howard Butz: Starr scrambling up the ropes. He’s quieting the fans. Dillon, you can hear a pin drop in here.
Dillon Diamond: Yeah, that and your mouth working overtime. Howard Butz: Very funny. Leaping off the top, Starr lands a senton and rolls to his feet, taking a bow and breaking the silence as the fans roar their approval.
Howard Butz: You gotta love that move, Dillon. Dillon Diamond: He’s wasting just a little too much time in my book. Against a guy a little higher up, Starr might be on the losing end of this one. Lucky for him, this guy isn’t on the same page as say a Jake Morgan.
Pulling Freak to his feet by the antennae poking out of his head, Starr throws him roughly into the corner, climbing the ropes and raising a fist. With the roar of approval, Starr hammers away as the fans count each blow.
Dropping down from the ropes, Starr steps back and allows Freak E to stagger out of the corner and fall face first into the mat. Flipping the “big bug” over, Starr grabs his legs and falls backwards to the mat, slingshotting E into the top turnbuckle.
Howard Butz: Freak E with a face full of turnbuckle. Staggering back, Freak E is grabbed from behind. Lifted into the air, he is dropped with an old fashioned back suplex. With his opponent out on the canvas,
Starr points at the corner before starting to climb. Howard Butz: Ricky Starr back up the ropes. Spinning around towards the fans, he throws his body back, slicing through the air and landing on top of Freak E with a flawless moonsault. Hooking a leg,
Starr’s head bobs up and down on the ref’s count. Howard Butz: ORLY DIAZ WITH THE COUNT! ONE! TWO! THREE! *** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: A fastpaced match and Ricky Starr picks up a big victory. Dillon Diamond: Perhaps not the best opponent but in this business, a win is a win is a win and Ricky Starr got one of those tonight.
Howard Butz: He sure did, Dillon but now we have to move on to this station identification!
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Kids love them! Adults love them! You love them. Everyone loves them! Those candy coated shells, Those marvelous peanuts, rice krispies, almonds and peanut butter they’re now stuffing them with. Even the plain chocolate ones! Don’t forget those terrific, wonderfully colored candy coated shells and you have the perfect candy treat! M&M’s can’t be beat! Buy some today!
Judge Judy wasn’t enough. They couldn’t stop with the People’s Court. Oh no, in this day and age of “reality television”, there’s even more! Catch
Power of Attorney every Monday night at 7 pm! *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** [Dipping into the back, the camera winds it’s way through the hallways, stopping at the painful moan coming from a dark corner. Moving in slowly, the camera finds a body laying amongst the contents of a crushed garbage can.
Inching foward, the bloody face of the Trashman comes into view. Snapping away, the camera returns ringside to Howard Butz and Dillon Diamond.]
Howard Butz: The Trashman busted open? If I was a betting man, even money says that Young Gun Champion, Billy Blaze had something to do with it.
Dillon Diamond: There we go with that gambling problem again. You need to break the cycle. Howard Butz: Not that again? Dillon Diamond: I’m just here to help. Howard Butz: You could help by paying attention to these words from the Television Champion, Jake Morgan!

— Part 2 of 2 —

[As they fade from view, Diamond can be heard mumbling the words “support group” to the eye rolling Butz. With a smooth transition, the camera shows a small table with several books upon it. Passing over the titles - Harry Potter:
The Sorcerer’s Stone, Cinderella and Jack and the Beanstalk, just to name a few, the camera spins around to behind a high backed leather chair for a moment before spinning around to it’s front and revealing it’s occupant ... the
30MC Television Champion, Jake Morgan, wearing blue jeans, cowboy boots and a stuffy, red, smoking jacket
With a book in his hand and a pipe dangling from his mouth, he smiles as he closes the cover to his “Wrestling Fairy Tales” tome. Looking up at the camera and pulling the pipe from his mouth to place it on the table next to him, Jake speaks in a slow, soothing tone.]
Jake Morgan: Welcome ... come in, have a seat and I’ll tell you the newest fairy tale in my collection.
[He takes a deep breath, then begins.] Now, I know you have all heard the story of the Arrogant Ass, a.k.a. Jason Flint. And the story of the Extreme Clown, Offspring, who couldn’t share the spotlight with anyone, so, he tried to tear the Circus down. I know those are your favorites but today I have a new one. Okay?
[pause] Okay. This one takes place in the magical land of 30 Minute Countdown. This Land, much like the other Lands we have talked about, was ruled by the benevolent King Morgan. At the beginning of our tale, the EVIL mage, Newton tries to steal the King's crown but is thwarted by one of his own men. A man who wouldn't take what was given to him, instead wanting to earn it.
[He smiles at the camera, then continues.] Now the king, was touched by this show of honor and told all that would hear, just how NOBLE the old, fat, bald, knight Buddy Lee Jr. was but before the praise could be sung by the king's heralds, Buddy Lee showed his true colors.
He began to spread lies. He began to tell everyone that would hear that he was the rightful king, and that good King Morgan entered into his kingdom and stole the crown. So, he challenged the king to a duel, to retake what he claims was stolen. The King refused, by saying that to fight him would only give Buddy Lee's lies attention and the King wouldn't do tha ...
[The smile disappears and the smoking jacket is ripped of, ending up on the floor with an angry Jake Morgan standing in front of the camera.]
To Hell with this. Buddy Lee Jr., you claim that I came and took your belt from you? What were your exact words?
[He looks back on the table and pulls off a piece of paper. After looking at it for a few seconds, he looks back to the camera.]
Oh yes, here it is, “you bullied your way into the ring and just took it.” I hate to tell you, Buddy Lee but I was already in the ring. I didn't interfere in your business and take your belt. No, that's a bold faced lie.
You entered MY ring, attacked ME and in the process, got your ass handed to you. So, you can tell that “FAIRY TALE” to all that will listen but Mr.
Good Guy, you and I both know the truth. I am the rightful champion, you said it yourself. You want to fight me to strip me of the Championship.
What more proof do you need? [He shakes his head.] You think that you can take the Television Title from me? Now, you ARE living in a fantasy land.
[Finished, the taped message fades back to Butz and Diamond sitting ringside.] Howard Butz: We’ve heard comments from both Buddy Lee Jr. and the champion this week, what’s your take on the situation, Dillon?
Dillon Diamond: What? No more of Morgan’s story? I was enjoying that. Howard Butz: Can’t you be serious for a minute? Dillon Diamond: I was. I wonder if Jake will let me borrow his book ... Howard Butz: Oh please. Let’s just go to the ring for our next match ... [Switching to the ring, the voices of the arguing Butz and Diamond can be heard before being drowned out by the booing of the fans as Jake Morgan and Angel
Rotten emerge from the back and make their way to the ring. The champion, with his belt over his shoulder, takes the time to get to know his front row fans, arguing with them before climbing into the ring .
After circling the ring, Morgan shows Fujita his title belt before handing it off to Angel. With last minute minute instructions from Orly Diaz, the two men are directed back to their corners before the bell is sounded.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Starting to circle, Morgan catches Fujita offguard, slamming a clubbing forearm across the shoulder, knocking the man to a knee. Several times, Morgan’s heavy forearm comes crashing down on his opponent, keeping him in place.
Howard Butz: Pulling Fujita to his feet, Morgan with a short arm clothesline ... NO! Fujita ducking under the blow. Spinning back kick that misses. Morgan with the clothesline and Fujita ducks that one.
Spinning around after the missed blow, Morgan comes up with raised fists to find Fujita in a martial arts stance ready for action. Taking a step back, the two men start circling each other.
Howard Butz: Both men sizing the other one up, looking for an opening. Dillon Diamond: I didn’t know they taught martial arts in Mexico City. Morgan charges in and is put down with a drop toe hold. Moving down, Fujita traps Morgan’s leg with his own, pushing the appendage forward and causing alot of pain.
Howard Butz: Orly Diaz asking the champion if he wants to submit. Dillon Diamond: Jake Morgan might be alot of things but he’s no quitter. Working the leg, Fujita has Morgan in trouble until moving forward slightly and coming down with a chop to the middle of his back. Reaching foward, Fujita grabs hold of Morgan’s chin and pulls it up while still having the leg.
Howard Butz: Painful looking rear chinlock and ankle submission hold. I don’t know if Jake can hold out on this one. How much pain can one man take?
Dillon Diamond: If anyone can take it, it’s this guy. He’s like a Timex ... takes a licking and keeps on ticking. Letting up on the hold, Fujita stomps the leg several times bringing a grimace from the champ. Pulling Morgan to his feet, the Mexico City madman, throws a leg over Jake’s neck, pushing down with his modified abdominal stretch, the octopus.
Howard Butz: The ref watching closely for a submission. Jake is being twisted into knots by Fujita ...
Dillon Diamond: With his Mexican heitage, I believe it’s pronounced FA-JITA. Long A, silent J. Howard Butz: Oh please. Dillon Diamond: I heard that he’s the heir to the Fajita fortune. He’s worth billions. Howard Butz: Stop that. Falling backwards, Morgan is able to escape the hold and uses the opportunity to escape the ring. Sliding to the outside, he is not given much of a break as
Fujita hits the outside as well with a well placed baseball slide. Howard Butz: There’s a series of chops to Morgan’s head and neck. Stepping away, Fujita pistons his arms forward then back before striking with a blow to the throat. Doubling Morgan over, Fujita hammers him in the back with an axe kick, dropping him to the floor.
Howard Butz: Diaz counting both men out of the ring and Fujita rolls Jake back into the ring. It’s been almost all Ieata Fujita from the very beginning.
Dillon Diamond: FA-jita. Howard Butz: Stop that. Fujita whipping Jake across the ring. SPINNING BACK KICK! FUJITA WITH THE COVER! ONE! TWO! NEAR FALL! Dillon Diamond: That was close but as we’ve seen in the past, the champ is one resiliant mofo.
Howard Butz: You can say that again. Dillon Diamond: Okay ... the champ is one resiliant mofo. With Morgan crawling away, Fujita follows him, kicking at the leg. Allowing Jake to pull himself to his feet, Fujita launches a series of stiff kicks at the knee and thigh, doing his best to injure the leg.
Pulling himself along the ropes into the corner, Morgan takes more punishment from his opponent’s hands and feet.
Howard Butz: Ieata Fujita a whirlwind of punches and kicks. Dillon Diamond: The ref is keeping a close eye on Jake who looks ready to go at any moment.
Taking blow after blow, Jake lunges foward and tags Fujita with a powerful clothesline, spinning him off of his feet.
Howard Butz: JAKE OUT OF NOWHERE WITH A BIG CLOTHESLINE! Dillon Diamond: Yeah but does he have enough to finish Fujita off? Sliding to the outside, Jake’s victory is short lived as Fujita comes flying to the outside with a plancha With Morgan down, Fujita is quickly to his feet and popping him with his “Feets of Fury”.
Howard Butz: Orly Diaz with a warning to take the match back inside the ring. JAKE WITH A LOW BLOW! Dillon Diamond: ouch. Howard Butz: That my friend is an understatement. Grabbing Fujita, Morgan pulls forward from his seated position and drives his opponent’s face into the ring barrier. Using it to stand, he limps badly before whipping Fujita into the ring steps.
Howard Butz: Fujita’s kicks are really starting to tell on Jake. Morgan leading Fujita around the ring ... AND HERE THEY COME!
With both announcers scrambling from their position, Morgan bashes Fujita’s head into the table, sending papers and drinks scattering. Grabbing Fujita by the ear, Morgan pushes him back into the ring. Not ready to join his opponent on the inside just yet, Jake steals a chair and slides it back into the ring.
Howard Butz: Morgan arguing with Diaz from the ring apron. Dillon Diamond: Don’t argue. Get into the ring and finish your opponent. Howard Butz: Fujita with a shoulder to Jake’s midsection. Well said Dillon. His focus on the ref and Ieata Fujita gets right back into the match. More shoulders to Jake’s stomach ...
Dillon Diamond: I think there’s quite a bit of extra padding there with all that Taco Bell recently.
Howard Butz: Which is exactly what started this all off as last week, Fujita took some of Jake’s lunch.
Dillon Diamond: He made a run for the border. Howard Butz: Jake’s making the run now. Forearm to the back stops Fujita. Climbing into the ring, Jake whips Fujita around and waits for him to return with a big boot to the face. The blow nearly sends him falling over as well because of the pain that registers on his face.
Limping to the corner, Jake climbs the ropes before coming off with an elbow drop. Hooking a leg, Jake makes the cover.
Howard Butz: ONE! TWO! THR ... NO! FUJITA WITH A SHOULDER UP! Arguing over a slow count, Jake picks Fujita up and puts him down with a body slam, following up with a fist drop. Again with a cover, Morgan is escaped after the near pin.
Again arguing with the ref, Jake wraps Fujita up with a belly-to-belly. Suplexed overhead, Fujita somehow lands on his feet. Spinning around, Morgan grabs an arm and pulls Fujita forward, nearly taking his head off with the violent shortarm clothesline.
Howard Butz: NEAR DECAPITATION BY JAKE MORGAN! Dillon Diamond: Jake’s big equalizer. Pulling Fujita to his feet, Jake wraps him up in a cobra clutch. With Morgan holding on for dear life, Fujita’s arms flail away before going limp.
Howard Butz: THERE’S NO ESCAPING THAT HOLD! THE REF IS CALLING FOR THE BELL! *** Ding *** Ding *** [With the match over, Morgan throws Fujita down to the mat. Stalking across the ring, he grabs up the chair and slams it across the helpless Fujita’s back, ignoring the warnings from the ref.]
Howard Butz: Where’s security? [Charging out from the back in answer, Buddy Lee Jr. hits the ring. Seeing reinforcements coming, Morgan slides to the outside with the fans chanting for
Buddy Lee Jr.] Howard Butz: WE ARE OUT OF TIME! SEE YOU NEXT WEEK! The credits roll with Buddy Lee and Jake Morgan glaring at each other.] *** 30 Minute Countdown© ***
