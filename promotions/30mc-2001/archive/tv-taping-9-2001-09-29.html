TV Taping 9

29 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 2 —

[The camera opens on Neal the intern hurriedly making his way through the backstage area with a large wrapped box in his hands. Rounding a corner, he nearly knocks over Angel Rotten.]
Angel: What's your problem? Neal: There you are! [With a meek smile on his face, he thrusts the box towards a surprised Angel. Taking the box, she shakes it then smiles as she opens it. Looking inside, she grins back at Neal before muttering under her breath.]
Angel: Jake ... you shouldn't have. [Neal shrugs as the camera fades away before finding Raoul walking with Shorti Blaze, both carrying handled shopping bags.] Shorti: Why doesn't Lars let Billy take care of 69 Degrees? [With a sigh, Raoul launches into a high-pitched rebuttal.] Raoul: Oh, Shorti ... I think Corey and Kenny are just headed down the wrong path and it's going to take Lars to set them straight ...
[He giggles at the comment, looking at a confused Shorti.] Shorti: The wrong path? Are you kidding? Those guys have attacked you guys several times ...
Raoul: Now, now. Everything will be alright in the end. [Again he giggles at his comment.] Shorti: You guys take care of them and I'll take care of Holly. [Whirling around, Raoul tears off his oversized pink sunglasses to look directly at Shorti.]
Raoul: HOLLY! THAT'S IT! [Grabbing her by the arm, Raoul leads her away. Leaving them, the camera finds a grinning Jake Morgan. Seated on a couch, Morgan looks at a closed box on a table in front of him. Liftng the lid slightly to peer inside, Jake’s smile disappears as the door suddenly bursts open as Ricky Starr steps inside.
Slamming the boxtop closed then jumping to his feet, Morgan looks the young wrestler over.]
Jake Morgan: WHAT DO YOU WANT?!?!? [Not taken aback, Starr steps closer.] Ricky Starr: One thing ... I want a crack at your title. [Morgan’s steely gaze softens into a smile before he rubs his chin.] Jake Morgan: Hmmm ... tell you what I’ll do. I’ll take it under advisement ... [Morgan looks down at the box on the table as a clock’s ticking loudly sounds in the background. With the screen going black, the clock face of a logo hurtles through the ebony space before settling into view. With the clock face fading into the background, faces and names flash by quickly.
The Mean Green Mascot - Mexican superstar, Ieta Fujita - Ricky Starr - The pink clad team of Pretty in Pink, Lars and Billy Blaze with Raoul - Agent of
L.O.V.E., Harry Palmer - Weed - All American Buddy Lee Jr. - and finally, the Television Champion, Jake Morgan and an angry Angel Rotten. As the last of the pictures fade, the camera makes a swing through a small but packed television studio before focussing in on two men sitting at the ringside announcers’ table. The two men are a contrast in looks, one in a sports coat while the other one is in a Hawaiian shirt, leather pants and a matching hat.]
Howard Butz: Howard Butz here along with Dillon Diamond ... welcome to 30 Minute Countdown! [Diamond gives a small tip of the hat.] Howard Butz: Dillon, it’s been a relatively quiet week here following the surprising recapture of the Television Title by Jake Morgan. Actually, the only one who’s really been running off at the mouth has been Morgan, himself.
Dillon Diamond: I think “running off at the mouth” is a bit harsh. Here’s a man with the odds stacked against and from out of nowhere, he pulls what has to be the upset against two men in Buddy Lee Jr. and Drake Russell ... in a virtual handicap match.
Howard Butz: Handicap match? Dillon Diamond: I just call them as I see them, Howard. Howard Butz: How much did he pay you to say that? [Stopping, Diamond looks up and starts counting off on his fingers.] Dillon Diamond: Pay me? Are you doubting my journalistic integrity? Howard Butz: Integrity? Oh, please. Let’s go to Holly and her interview taped earlier with the Television Champion, Jake Morgan ...
[Butz and Diamond fade from view as the pre-recorded segment rolls with the camera opening on Holly in the back of the studio next to the Television
Champion, Jake Morgan. He is wearing a black and red Man, Myth, Morgan T-shirt, black trunks, and of course his Television Title. She smiles at the camera, turns to Jake, and begins to ask him a question.]
Holly: Jake, after you lost your Title at Double Trouble, several of the wrestlers here had a lot to say about you. But this week, after you recaptured your gold, they were silent. Do you know why?
[Jake gives her a contemptuous look.] Jake Morgan: It’s pretty simple, really ... they’re afraid. [She gives him a blank look. Jake shakes his head, and begins to "speak with his hands" as he explains it to her.]
Jake Morgan: When they thought I was defeated and easy prey, they circled like vultures, hoping to pick my corpse clean. When I sprung to life and regained my title they ran away like the cowards they are.
[She looks up at him, as if he's said something that offended her.] Holly: I don't know about Delaney, but I sincerely doubt that Drake Russell is a coward. [Jake turns and positions himself so that he towers over her.] Jake Morgan: Really? Then where the hell is he? Why was he silent this week? [Stammering, Holly tries to answer.] Holly: I ... I ... I ... [Jake shakes his head.] Jake Morgan: That's what I thought, Holly. You had better be careful, the last interviewer that fell for Drake, got put through a table, courtesy of me.
Holly: I haven't fallen for hi ... [Jake glares at her. She gulps and steps back as a smiling Jake walks off. With the clip over, the camera returns Butz and Diamond ringside.]
Howard Butz: Scared? Does Jake Morgan actually believe they are scared of him? And was he threatening Holly? Was that what that was? Dillon Diamond: Jake does have a point. Two weeks ago ... last week ... Ryan Delaney, Drake Russell, Buddy Lee Jr. all have something to say. Jake takes back his belt ... not a peep. I think he’s got something there. Where’s
Drake Russell? He’s not even in the building. For all we know, he’s left the area. As for Holly, I think it’s more concern on Jake’s part rather than a threat. I’m sure he’d hate to see her get hurt ...
Howard Butz: Give it a rest will you? What are you? His PR man? We’ll be right back following these words from our sponsers ...
[The camera fades from a disgusted Howard But into a commercial.] *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Bally’s Gym. Hopefully, you’ve seen it. Gorgeous, slender woman in her undies kneeling on her bed. In the foreground is a camera focussed in on the bed and the woman waiting patiently for someone else, presumably her husband.
You know the one I’m talking about? I need a membership! Maybe, I can bring someone home who looks like that! Get that video camera ready! Whoohoo!
Scenes of poor children in some third world country flash by as the Christian Children’s Fund asks you to make a difference in their world for as little as 89 cents a day! Food, medicine, education all possible on 89 cents a day! Won’t you help make a difference? *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** [Returning from the commercial, the camera finds Butz and Diamond having been joined ringside by Holly.]
Howard Butz: Welcome back. We’ve been joined by the ever lovely Holly ... Dillon Diamond: That’s the first thing you’ve said tonight I agree with Butzie. Howard Butz: Why don’t you go play in the traffic or something, Dillon? [Before being able to answer, “It’s Raining Men” starts to play through the PA system bringing a cheer from the fans as Raoul is followed out to the ring by Shorti Blaze. Inside the ring, Raoul asks for a mic as Shorti climbs the corner ropes and plays to the crowd. Walking around the ring and acknowledging the fans wit his limp wristed waving, Raoul brings the fans to their feet.]
Raoul: HELLLLLLLLLLLOOOOOOOOOO!!! [Bending at the waist, Raoul blows kisses to the adoring fans as Shorti climbs down from the corner.]
Raoul: I love you all! [Kisses to the fans are followed by more for the announcers.] Raoul: The last few weeks have been a whirlwind of action for us Pretty in Pinksters. With our Young Gun Champion, Billy busting his tushy defending his title, with Shorti joining the team and Lars and I doing everything in our power to make this a better place for all of us ... there have been a few persons out there who have constantly been doing their hardest to thwart our big plans ... first, there is that horrid Trashman ... why even his name is horrid.
[Raoul turns away from the mic, making a face until being comforted by Shorti.] Raoul: That horrid little man has gone out of his way to be a big meanie but as we all saw, dear little Billy took care of him last week, so we’re hopefully finished with him ... and that allows us to move on to our next project.
[Giggling, Raoul looks at Shorti before turning back to the fans.] Raoul: That brings us to 69 Degrees. Lars has an interest in their ... “talents” and has taken it upon himself to do to them what he did to Billy. With Lars behind them, plugging away ... they are bound to come around to our way of thinking.
[Raoul giggles at the disgusted groans and mixed in laughter from the audience.] Raoul: Only ... there seems to be a little problem. [Putting a finger to his lips, Raoul looks around the ring and the audience before focusing in on the announcers’ table. Wagging his finger, he points out a shocked Holly.]
Raoul: You missy, are the problem. Instead of wanting them to grow and discover their true selves ... you want to keep it buried. You won’t let them come out
... [Cutting him off, Holly screeches into a mic from ringside.] Holly: WHAT?!?!? I WANT NOTHING TO DO WITH THOSE TWO!!!!! [Leaving the ring, Shorti chases Holly away from Butz and Diamond, stalking her around the ring. With the fans chanting “Catfight!” in the background,
Holly escapes by entering the ring, only to be cut-off by Raoul. With a finger pointed at the woman in high heels and a minidress, Raoul steps forward forcing
Holly to back away until she bumps into Shorti.] Dillon Diamond: This is starting to get interesting, Howard! [With nowhere else to run to, Holly is forced to stop until Raoul let’s her have a limp-wristed slap that surprises more than hurts.]
Dillon Diamond: Yeah, this is getting real good! Howard Butz: Calm yourself, Dillon. Shouldn’t we be doing something about this? Dillon Diamond: I am! I’m all eyes! [Shocked, Holly lashes back with one of her own but is pulled back by her hair. Looping an arm around Holly’s head, Shorti pulls her backwards before slapping down with her free arm and planting her into the canvas with an inverted DDT. With her dress riding up high on her thighs and her legs going out in every direction, Holly is out on the mat with Shorti standing above her and taunting her.]
Howard Butz: The cavalry has arrived ... HERE IS 69 DEGREES! [Rushing out to the ring, both Corey and Kenny come in ready for a fight, threatening a backpeddling Raoul. As Corey grabs Raoul by the hair, they are joined by a frantic Lars.]
Dillon Diamond: And Lars to the rescue. [Stepping inbetween both parties, Lars does his best to ease the tension. Pushing an angered Shorti back, Lars averts any disasterous situation from happening.] Howard Butz: I think Lars has ended any situations between the two teams. Dillon Diamond: I wouldn’t say that ... HERE COMES BILLY!

— Part 2 of 2 —

[Racing out from the back, the Young Gun Champion hits the ring looking for a little action while the fans start chanting his name. Moving in quickly, Lars keeps him from mixing it up as well. Explaining himself to Billy, Lars leaves
Shorti who talks the opportunity to taunt Corey and Kenny.] Howard Butz: IT’S ABOUT TO HIT THE FAN! HERE COMES THE TRASHMAN AND HE’S GOT A CHAIR! [Coming in swinging, he barely misses a ducking Lars, taking out instead, a surprised Shorti, clobbering her with a loud bang.
Howard Butz: HE HIT SHORTI!!!!! THE TRASHMAN NAILED SHORTI WITH THE CHAIR!!!!! [Using the opening to leave, Corey and Kenny collect Holly and make their way to the back, leaving a situation in the ring. With Shorti down, the rest of the
Pipsters stand shocked, all waiting for someone to act ... that someone is Raoul. Slipping in unnoticed behind Trash, Raoul drops to his knees before swinging in an uppercut between Trash’s legs. Doubling over in pain, Trash drops his weapon.]
Dillon Diamond: Raoul in his favorite position ... Howard Butz: WHAT?!?!? Dillon Diamond: I said ... Howard Butz: Nevermind. I heard exactly what you said. Do you mind keeping those comments to yourself?
Dillon Diamond: What did I do? All I said was ... Howard Butz: We heard exactly what you said. [Kicking the chair out of the way, Billy gives Lars a nod before the each grab an arm and send Trash crashing to the mat with a double hip toss. Throwing themselves back against the ropes, Billy and Lars shoot forward, dropping with a pair of splashes.]
Howard Butz: All we need is a referee and a bell and we’ll have a match. Dillon Diamond: Well, there’s your referee! [Running out from the back, referee Robin hits the ring and comes up calling for the bell.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: AND THERE’S THE BELL! While Raoul pulls a groggy Shorti from the ring, Lars and Billy are busy putting Trash up on the ropes, sitting him on the middle one and facing out towards the crowd. Stepping back to the opposite side of the ring, Lars takes a running start before jumping up and slamming into Trash’s back. Holding on for dear life, he pistons his hips furiously before dropping off. Without Lars to hold him in place, Trash falls back, his legs still entangled in the ropes, leaving his face exposed.
Howard Butz: Trash in a vulnerable position here on the ropes. BASEBALL SLIDE! BASEBALL SLIDE! Booted in the face, Trash falls from the ropes landing face down on the mat. Dragging him unceremoniously to the middle of the ring, they roll him to his back, each grabbing a leg. With a double nod, the two men fall to the canvas with a leg splitter. In pain, Trash shoots upright.
Howard Butz: They seems to targeting a certain area. I am glad NOT to be the Trashman right now. Rolling to his feet, Billy slams back against the ropes, shooting forward to drive both boots into Trash’s face.
Howard Butz: They are really working over Trashman here, Dillon. It’s hard to believe that these two guys used to be friends once upon a time.
Dillon Diamond: You can say that again but with Trash attacking Lars and Raoul on several occassions, it’s pretty easy to see where this is all growing from. I mean, you have to go with who got you to this point and since coming in under Lars’ wing, Billy has blossomed ...
Howard Butz: Lars and Raoul? What about Trash hitting Shorti? Dillon Diamond: Uhm ... yeah, that, too. Howard Butz: Lars lifting Trashman into the air ... and Billy’s going up the ropes. This does not look good for Trashman. MISSILE DROP KICK! BILLY AND LARS
WITH A MISSILE DROP KICK BACK SUPLEX COMBINATION! Rolling Trash out of the way, Lars engages the referee, pulling her close and going cheek to cheek and stepping across the ring in a Tango. Trying to push away from Lars, Robin is spun right into Billy’s arms. Pulling her in tight,
Billy starts up a little Salsa before dipping her. Not to be left out, Lars cuts in, grabbing Billy and pulls him in close before pushing him away then snapping him back into a dip of his own. With the combined cheering from the woman in the small audience and the laughter, Lars and Billy take a bow.
Howard Butz: What is this? Dillon Diamond: Just a little fancy footwork, Howard. Howard Butz: This is a wrestling ring ... not a dance floor. Dillon Diamond: I think you’re jealous because you have two left feet. Howard Butz: Oh, brother. Dillon Diamond: While I, on the other hand, have been known to cut a rug in my past. Howard Butz: Cut a rug? Wasn’t that when you worked summers with your brother, the housing contractor?
Dillon Diamond: No, I was laying some pipe ... Howard Butz: Let’s watch the match. Working Trash into a corner, Lars opens up with his loud popping limp-wristed chops to the chest. With his opponent slumping in the corner, Lars climbs to the middle rope, his crotch just mere inches from Trash’s face. After doing some hip gyrations, he is tapped on the back by Billy, who wants to cut in.
Leaving his spot, Lars opens up for Billy who climbs the ropes. Punching a fist into the air, he brings it down on top of Trash’s noggin with each blow being counted out by the fans. Jumping down, he allows Trash to stagger out of the corner into Lars’ arms. Bending Trash over, Lars’ pelvic thrustings are followed by his pump handled slam.
Howard Butz: There’s that awful move. Pointing at the ropes, Lars starts to climb while down below, Billy grabs up Trash’s legs into the air, exposing him to an open shot. With a lusty grin on his face, Lars dives from the top, falling with his head into Trash’s crotch and holding in place for the pin.
Howard Butz: Diving headbutt. THERE’S THE COVER! ONE! TWO! THREE! *** Ding *** Ding *** [Rolling from the ring, Lars and Billy Blaze celebrate with Shorti and Raoul as they make their way to the back to a loud cheer while the ref helps a glaring
Trashman to his feet before disappearing into the back to find Jake Morgan inside his dressing room. Dressed in jeans and a gray 30MC shirt, the champ paces several times back and forth when the door opens and the voice of Angel
Rotten is heard.] Angel: Oh, Jake ... you shouldn’t have. [Spinning around his features twist in disbelief as he sees Angel dressed in a tight black leather dress with a zippered front pulled way down and finished off in matching black spike heels. Her black lipsticked smile disappears at
Jake’s displeasure.] Jake Morgan: What the Hell are you wearing?!?!? [Storming out of the room, Jake leaves a pouting Angel as the camera goes to a commercial.]
***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** Local commercial. You’ve seen them before. Poorly spliced in with the more professional looking national ones. Selling cars, hawking local restaraunts, stores and/or products before disappearing with an equally poor fade into the next commercial.
Good girls gone bad. You’ve seen them before ... videos of hot young chicks willing to flash their boobs. The girl next door ... on the beach, walking the street, just about anywhere ... all ready to flash some skin for the camera.
Now, you can buy these videos so you can see the parts they can’t show on television! ***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** [Returning from the commercial, the camera follows Jake Morgan walking out to the ring to a healthy dose of booing. Largly ignoring the taunts of the fans,
Jake waves them off as he steps into the ring before calling for a mic. Flipping his hair back, Jake adjusts his Television Title on his shoulder before addressing the crowd.]
Jake Morgan: It’s good to be here as YOUR Television Champion. [Morgan waits for the rising volume to fall before continuing.] Jake Morgan: In my short stay here, I have seen every underhanded tactic and trick to keep me away from this ...
[With a smile, Morgan taps the plate of his belt.] Jake Morgan: And tonight, I want to address just one little aspect of those trying to cheat me ... I want to talk about the officiating here and more importantly, Orly Diaz.
[Stepping back, Morgan waits for Diaz to step out from the back. Angrily making his way to the ring, Diaz is held back by fellow official Robin. Climbing into the ring, Diaz paces with Robin between he and Jake.]
Jake Morgan: This week, I made a few comments about you Diaz and your officiating of my matches. I’ve said some things that surely made your blood boil ... frankly, I attacked your integrity and I threatened your livelihood.
[Diaz glares at Morgan, looking ready to attack if it weren’t for Robin.] Jake Morgan: And though you’re not the only referee in this promotion, I singled you out ...
[Pacing a bit himself, Morgan stops in front of Diaz before sliding his belt off his shoulder and handing it to Robin.]
Jake Morgan: Maybe it was all said in the heat of the action ... but now, that I’ve had time to cool down and think about, I thought to myself that perhaps I was being unfair. Afterall, I haven’t walked a mile in YOUR shoes ... [With a grin on his face, Morgan thrusts out his hand and motions for Diaz to take it. Reluctantly, the ref takes it and the two shake. Looking around, Jake nods his head before pulling Diaz in close and nearly decapitating him with a shortarm clothesline. Pulling a stunned Diaz to his feet, Morgan pushes him up into the air before dropping him onto the canvas with a brainbuster.]
Howard Butz: OH, MY GOD!!!!! JAKE WITH A BRAINBUSTER!!!!! HE SUCKERED ORLY DIAZ!!!!! CAN WE HAVE SECURITY!!!!! [With the fans booing, Jake lays in with the boots before picking up his mic.] Jake Morgan: Maybe it’s time, I walked a mile in YOUR shoes, Orly Diaz. [Throwing down the mic with a dull thud, Morgan strips off his shirt, revealing a black and white stripped one underneath. Throwing down his shirt, Morgan smirks as the show comes to an end.]
