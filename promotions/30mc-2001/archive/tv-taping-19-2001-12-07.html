TV Taping 19

7 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 2 —

[The camera opens on a smiling Vic Parker signing away for several PYT’s - that’s Pretty Young Things to the uninitiated, when he is grabbed by the shoulder and spun around. Surprised at first, Parker’s cocky smile is quick to return at the sight of an angry Trashman.]
Trashman: Don’t know who I am? Just a simple garbageman, am I? I’ll tell you what ... tonight’s match will be a little indoctorination into what I’m all about. Let’s make it a hardcore match!
[Shrugging his shoulders, Parker nods his head.] Vic Parker: That’s fine by me. [Turning back towards his admiring fans, Parker rolls his eyes before the camera leaves him for Newton’s office. Behind his desk, Newton looks up as his door opens and Brody steps inside.]
Newton: Well? Brody: No one’s seen her. Newton: GREAT! My Young Gun champion has a title match tonight and she’s busy with that two-bit gothic trash promotion ...
[Newton smiles as the door opens only to see it melt away as Lars and Raoul step inside.] Lars: Hello Newty ... you, too Brodzilla. [The Pipsters share a giggle.] Lars: We were wondering if you’ve seen Neal? We haven’t seen him and we’re still waiting on that information he has for me. You know ... the big secret.
[Shaking his head, Newton shrugs.] Lars: Oh well, I’m sure he’ll show if he knows what’s good for him. Back to work now, boys. Raoul and I have more to do for tonight’s Cloud 9.
Toodles. [Giving Brody a lusty onceover, Lars spins on his heels and exits, followed by Raoul when a clock’s ticking loudly sounds in the background. With the screen going black, the clock face of a logo hurdles through the ebony space before settling into view. The clock face fades into the background, giving way to faces and names that flash by quickly.
The Mean Green Mascot - Mexican superstar, Ieta Fujita - Ricky Starr - The pink clad team of Pretty in Pink, Lars and Billy Blaze with Raoul - Agent of
L.O.V.E., Harry Palmer - Weed - All American Buddy Lee Jr. - the heavenly Angel and finally, the Television Champion, Jake Morgan and an angry Angel Rotten.
As the last of the pictures fade, the camera makes a swing through a small but packed television studio before focussing in on Howard Butz inside the ring, standing with a dark haired man wearing a pair of sunglasses. Standing confidently, the man takes the glasses off before folding them into his shirt pocket.]
Howard Butz: Welcome wrestling fans Howard Butz here along with young Tommy Trevor. Tommy, just last week, you were here ready, willing and able to fight for a contract in the 30 Minute Countdown. This week, however, it’s a different story ...
Tommy Trevor: That’s right, Howard. Tommy Trevor is here to wrestle anyone and everyone. You want to step between the ropes with me, I’m more than happy to give him ... and the fans the best of Tommy Trevor.
[Trevor smiles at the cheer from the fans.] Howard Butz: What a difference a week makes. Your opponent heads for the hills. 69 Degrees’ member Kickin’ Kenny steps in against you and winds up in the hospital and best of all, Newton is ready to offer you a contract ...
[A grinning Trevor nods his head when the duo is joined by the "One Man Boy Band" Chris Cortez. Climbing into the ring, Cortez approaches Butz and steps between him and Trevor, muscling the young wrestler out of the way.]
Chris Cortez: It’s great to be back! Howard Butz: Do you mind? Chris Cortez: Not at all. Were you saying something? [Looking first to Trevor then back to Butz, Cortez waves a hand.] Chris Cortez: Oh, well ... it doesn’t matter. As I was saying, I’ve shed some baggage, the One man Boy Band has more time for his matches ... his music
... and his muchachas! [Listening to the jeers, Cortez’ head bobs up and down. As the jeering gives way to all out booing, Cortez rips his sunglasses from his face before realizing they’re not for him but for the Television champ, Jake Morgan making his way into the ring. With his belt over his shoulder, Morgan tears away the mic from Butz. Strolling around the ring, Morgan steps right up into
Cortez’ face.]
Jake Morgan: I’M A FIGHTING CHAMPION! [With a sudden turn, Morgan gets in Trevor’s face.] Jake Morgan: Did you hear what I said? I’M A FIGHTING CHAMPION AND THIS BELT DOES MORE THAN SIT AROUND MY WAIST! DID YOU HEAR ME? MAYBE IF YOU WEREN’T HIDING IN THE SHADOWS, YOU’D HEAR ME! [With the booing growing louder, Morgan paces the ring while the rest inside look confused. Wheeling around, Morgan smashes Trevor in the head with his belt, putting him down like a ton of bricks. Spinning quickly, he does the same to Cortez. With the two men down, Morgan turns his attention to a scared Butz.]
Jake Morgan: Or maybe it was you being the weasel that you really are. [Stepping forward Morgan is stopped by the lovely referee, Robin. Listening to reason, Morgan leaves the ring for the back. The camera returns to the ring and a somewhat frightened Butz.]
Howard Butz: We’ll be right back after these words from our sponsers! *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** The Ultimate Cheeseburger ... $1.99 of cholesteral. Football. The Carnivores. Cheerleaders. Referees. Dinosaur mascot. Blimps landing on kick returners. Funny commercial. Terrible burger. Jack in the Box. Good food ... except for their burgers.
You know it, I know it ... Dr. Pepper makes the world taste better! The only problem is the rest of the country doesn't know it. Don't believe me? Just ask for it in Milwaukee and see what they give you ... “We got root beer.”
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** [Back from the break, the camera makes a sweep of the ring and Robin talking to Vic Parker in his corner before making its way to the announcers’ table, finding Butz and Dillon Diamond flanked by Jake Morgan and Lars.]
Howard Butz: We’re ready for our first match between Vic Parker and the Trashman and as you can see, we’ve been joined by Jake Morgan, who I hope has calmed down some from hitting Tommy Trevor and Chris Cortez, and not to be forgotten, Lars.
Lars: I just love hardcore. Jake Morgan: As the reigning King of HardKore, you’re lucky to have me around to bail you out, Butz.
Howard Butz: Right. Anyway ... here comes the Trashman. [Swinging around, the camera zooms in on the gas mask wearing Trashman dragging a garbage can full of weapons out to the ring. With the fans cheering on in anticipation of the action, Trash raises his arms from the outside of the ring.
Not one to waste an opportunity, Parker slides to the outside, ramming his boots into Trash’s back, knocking him into the ring barrier.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Hammering away, Parker opens up on Trash, slinging him around to go smashing into the ring barrier and finishing him with a snap suplex.
Jake Morgan: Hey, Dillon ... coffee ... black ... now! Dillon Diamond: Sure thing, Jake. Get you anything else? Lars: Big banana smoothy for me! Diamond rushes off to the back while the ref tries to get the action back into the ring. After throwing the garbage can into the ring, Parker slides Trash back in as well.
Howard Butz: Was that necessary? Jake Morgan: What? I would have sent you instead but you probably would have put something into my coffee. At least, I can trust Dillon.
Howard Butz: Right. Parker dumping the weapons on the mat and his opponent. Jake and Lars in unison: That’s not hardcore. Lars: JINX! Pulling himself to his feet using the ropes, Trash takes a crutch across his back, splintering it as it hits.
Jake Morgan: You gotta keep the man down. In pain, Trash is whipped across the ring and on the return blasted by a spinning heel kick. Jumping to his feet, Parker hits the corner, coming off the middle rope and landing with an elbow drop.
Lars: Where’s the paddles? No whips? No chains? No leather? You know, Raoul likes leather. Pulling Trash to his feet, Parker cinches him up to throw him over with a suplex but is blocked. Countering with a head butt, Trash sends Parker back into the ropes then whipping off his gas mask. Throwing it down, he retrieves a cookie sheet off the mat, slamming it over Parker’s head. Slumping against the ropes in pain, he takes several rights and lefts to the midsection.
Howard Butz: Trash taking control of the match. He’s got part of the splintered crutch ... OH NO!!!
Pulling Parker in close, he tries to gouge his opponent with the sharp splinter. With it nearly to his head, Parker fights for all he’s worth, keeping it just inches from it’s target. A back elbow catches Trash, stunning him long enough for Parker to escape the hold. Jumping up onto the ropes,
Parker comes off with a springboard drop kick, sending Trash over the ropes, spilling out onto the floor.
Jake Morgan: We almost had something there but pretty boy luckily escaped. Grabbing the garbage can, Parker waits until Trash gets back to his feet before throwing it. With a loud bang, it slams into Trash’s head, knocking him to the floor. Sliding to the outside, Parker drops the can on top of Trash before climbing back onto the apron. With a running start, he dives off, flipping his body and landing on top of both, bringing the fans to their feet.
Howard Butz: SUICIDE DIVE! Slamming Trash into the barrier, Parker pushes the can into his seated opponent’s face. Moving back, Parker takes a running start, coming off his feet and slamming them into the can.
Howard Butz: Parker with the drop kick right into the garbage can! With the can falling away, a now bleeding Trash slumps over. With a warning from the ref, Parker lifts Trash to his feet before falling back to the floor with a suplex. Sliding into the ring, he helps Trash back to his feet.
Howard Butz: Vic Parker taking the match back into the ring ... Grabbing Parker by the head, Trash jumps off the apron to the floor, pulling his opponent’s throat across the top rope. Snapping back, Parker hits the mat clutching his throat. With blood running from a wound on his forehead, Trash taps his head before telling the fans to shut up.
Howard Butz: Trashman not the most popular man today. Jake Morgan : What do you expect? He looks like Carrot Top. Lars: I’m thinking a day at the salon, some peroxide and highlights, he could look quite dashing. Perhaps a little bang ... show the more mysterious side?
Howard Butz: Right. Jake, you and the Trashman were partners once upon a time ... Jake Morgan: Yeah, so? Howard Butz: I just thought ... Jake Morgan: You thought nothing. I taught this guy everything he knows and what? This match is still going on. Looks to me, he didn’t pay much attention.
Tossing the can back into the ring, Trash comes back in using the ropes to slingshot his body into a leg drop. Putting the garbage can over Parker’s head, Trash picks up a broom and starts jumping around the ring as if it’s a guitar, swinging his arm around wildly. Finished, he brings it down across the can and splintering it with a loud bang.
Howard Butz: Looks to me as if he’s taking care of business. Jake Morgan: To you it would. Lars: I think Billy would have won already. Jake Morgan: BILLY! He wasn’t cutting any promos in the dark was he? Lars: Dear, sweet Billy? I should say not! That face? That hair? With that look ... would you hide in the shadows? Simply scrumptious. Lars stares off into the distance while Trash drags Parker’s face across the ring ropes into the corner. Pulling Parker up by the hair, Trash takes a back elbow. Wobbled, he’s an easy target as Parker slams him into the turnbuckle.
Clearing the middle of the ring, Parker sets up the flattened garbage can before returning with a cookie sheet. Bringing it down, he bends it around
Trash’s head. Tossing it aside with a cocky grin, he points back at the can before jumping up into the air and landing on top of Trash’s thighs. Throwing his body back, he sends Trash over onto the garbage can with a monkey flip.
Bolting up in pain, he takes a drop kick to the back of his head. Hooking a leg, he makes the cover.
Howard Butz: ONE! TWO! THR ... NO!

— Part 2 of 2 —

Charging the ropes, Parker hits the middle rope and flies back, sticking a moonsault, again covering for the pin only to be tossed off on a two count.
Howard Butz: A resiliant Trashman getting the shoulder up. Dillon Diamond: HERE WE ARE! Hearing the voice, Morgan bolts from his seat, coming up swinging and knocking the drinks from Diamond’s hands. Regaining his composure, Mrogan returns to his seat. Picking the cups off the floor, Diamond puts them down on the table before silently taking his seat.
Howard Butz: What’s the matter, Jake? You seem a little jumpy today. Voices in the dark ...
Snapping around, Morgan points a finger in Butz’ face. Jake Morgan: Don’t push me, Butz. You wouldn’t like to see me angry. Picking up his drink, Lars dips a finger into his froothy concoction. Swirling it around, he holds it up before sliding it into his mouth until it disappears.
Looking at Diamond with a smile on his face, he pulls it back out with a wet smacking sound.
Lars: AH! Thick and frothy with a head just the way I like it! Grabbing up a piece of the broken broom handle, Parker wraps it around Trash’s throat, choking him with it. Flailing with his arms at first, the moves become less animated. Lifting one of Trash’s arms, the ref lets it drop before counting one. Lifting a second time, it drops just as quickly. A third time, it doesn’t drop as Trash strikes with an elbow. Slamming his head back, he catches Parker in the face, staggering him.
Howard Butz: Trashman with the escape! Bounding off the ropes, Trash shoots for the troubled Parker only to run right into his boot.
Howard Butz: SUPERKICK! PARKER WITH THE SUPERKICK! No longer paying attention, Morgan’s looking all around. A stare from Butz draws a harsher one in return.
Jake Morgan: Not a word, Butz. Struggling to push Trash up the ropes, Parker gets him seated on the top before climbing. Lars: OOPS! Sorry boys ... gotta run. Time to get into costume, you know! TOODLES! Howard Butz: There goes Lars ... anyway, Vic Parker climbing up the ropes ... TORNADO DDT! TRASHMAN WITH THE DDT! With Parker readying him for a suplex off the top, Trash, lying in wait was able to sneak in the devastating move, sending both men into the mat ... Parker taking the worst of the bump. Pulling himself to his feet, Trashman makes his way to the ropes nearest the esteemed announcers.
Howard Butz: Trashman coming over now. What’s he doing? With a maniacal look on his face, peeking out from under the blood, he points down at the angered Morgan. Nodding his head, he motions around his waist.
Howard Butz: I think he wants a shot at your belt, Jake. Jake Morgan: I know exactly what he wants, Dillon Diamond: Don’t bait him, Howard. Howard Butz: Oh, please. Somewhat dazed, Parker moves to his knees, seeing Trashman with his back to him. Moving in quickly, Parker grabs his opponent and pulls him to the mat with an inside cradle.
Howard Butz: PARKER WITH THE INSIDE CRADLE! ONE! TWO! THREE! Despite struggling, Trashman can’t break free as Robin calls for the bell. *** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: Vic Parker with an impressive first victory ... Jake Morgan: I’d hardly call that impressive. Howard Butz: Right. Don’t go away ... we’ll be right back with Cloud 9. [Leaving the ring, the camera find Newton quickly moving through the backstage area. Moving in and out of 30MC personnel, he is grabbed from behind and spun around. A solidly built muscular man with a shock of white hair peeking out from a pair of sunglasses smiles at the boss.]
Man: Name’s Frost ... Gideon Frost. [Letting go of Newton, the man pulls the gallses from his head, releasing a length of hair that drops from the top and falls to just below his nose. With a sweep of his hand, he pushes it out of his face.]
Gideon Frost: I’d like a shot at that contract. [Hurriedly looking down at his watch, Newton looks back up to the larger man.] Newton: Sure thing ... whatever. [Pulling away, Newton scurries off leaving Frost grinning. The camera switches showing a pile of white beanbag chairs each marked with the number “9”, lying on the entrance platform. A single spotlight shines brightly onto the area as if from the heavens. The audience begins to cheer loudly as “It’s
Raining Men” begins to play over the loudspeakers. The camera angle switches again to a backstage view showing Lars and Raoul, dressed in skimpy angel costumes, flitting around ready to make their grand entrance. Without warning, Newton rushes in, shouting.]
Newton: STOP! Don’t even think about it. I thought I made it perfectly clear that there would be no more Cloud 9’s! What part of that did you two not understand?
Lars: It’s not our fault. Angel brought it up! I think she wanted us to do it for her show!
Newton: What!?! I know for a fact that she wouldn’t want you two on her spotlight program. Lars: Well, I can’t just stand around and allow her to sully the good name of my trademarked program Cloud 9!
Raoul: Yeah! It’s a re-BUTT-al! [Both men wiggle their posteriors and smile.] Newton: I don’t care what your excuse is, I’m not going allow it. [Out of nowhere, Raoul leaps onto Newton’s back screeching the entire time. Newton looks more annoyed than worried.) Raoul: Run Lars! I’ll hold him off until you’re done! [Lars rushes through the curtains just as the camera switches to inside the studio, plops onto the pillows and strokes himself sensuously. The crowd is a mixture of applause and laughter.)
Lars: OOOHH!!! Welcome to a special edition of “Cloud 9”! Trademarked by Backdoor Productions let’s not forget! This special show is dedicated to the “STAR” ... [Lars rolls his eyes and makes the quotation marks in the air with his hands.] Lars: ... of tonight’s show ... Angel. I have to admit that I don’t know if she truly has been “touched by and angel”, but I’m positive that the poor girl is
“touched” in the head! [An angry Newton busts through the curtain, toting Raoul on his back, followed by 2 security guards. Raoul struggles with all his "might".]
Raoul: You’re not going anywhere! Newton: Oh shut up! Get him off my set, boys! [Security grabs Lars under each arm and begins to carry him away.] Lars: Noooooo! [Newton is all smiles, pointing over his shoulder at Raoul, he laughs.) Newton: Don’t worry boys, I got this one. [His expression changes as Raoul slaps him on the backside, spurring him on before the camera returns to Butz and Diamond.]
Howard Butz: Just when you thought you’ve seen it all ... next up, Angel defending her title against Harry Palmer ...
Dillon Diamond: With Orly Diaz and Palmer making it into the ring, this should be a good one, Howard.
Howard Butz: I bet Miss Angel is just a little bit steamed over the comments made by Palmer last week.
Dillon Diamond: Tell me you didn’t put money down! Howard Butz: Will you stop? Dillon Diamond: It’s a sucker’s bet ... you know as well as I do, Angel wasn’t mad at Palmer because she’s attracted to him. You could see it in her eye. He’s suave and sophisticated ...
Howard Butz: There seems to be a discussion going on inside the ring between Palmer and Diaz. Dillon Diamond: He’s probably getting some tips from Palmer ... afterall, he is the Agent of L.O.V.E.!
Howard Butz: Diaz is calling for the bell?!?!? *** Ding *** Ding *** Raising his arm into the air, Diaz starts counting. Howard Butz: What’s going on?!?!? Diaz has started counting? This is insane. Dillon Diamond: Maybe Jake’s right about Orly? Howard Butz: Will you give me a break? Diaz continuing with his count ... eight ... nine ... ten ... there’s the bell. *** Ding *** Ding *** [Moving to the corner, Diaz holds Palmer’s arm in the air. Declared the winner, Palmer starts jumping up and down, having to stop to adjust his black rimmed glasses. realizing what just happened, the fans start booing.]
Dillon Diamond: Howard, do you know what that means? WE HAVE A NEW YOUNG GUN CHAMPION! Howard Butz: THAT’S NOT ALL WE HAVE! LOOK! [Rushing out from the back, the man in the chicken mask dives into the ring. Coming up quickly, he spears Diaz, pounding him into the mat with a heavy thud. Not wanying any part of that action, Palmer vacates the ring in a hurry. Back to his feet, the masked man shakes an angry fist at the booing fans.]
Howard Butz: You tell me that’s not Jake Morgan! Dillon Diamond: Okay ... that’s not Jake Morgan. Howard Butz: You know just as well as I do, Jake is under that mask ... [A sudden loud boom through the studio sends the man in the mask rushing off like ... well, a chicken without his head. Flickering lights gives way to the studio being bathed in inky blackness.]
Howard Butz: What is this all about? CAN WE HAVE SOME LIGHTS PLEASE?!?!? [A sudden spotlight rests on the entrance from the back when a thick blasts outward. The entire studio sits in utter silence, watching.]
Dillon Diamond: Is that a fire? Howard Butz: Will you stop? That is not a fire! [First, there is movement within which quickly gives way to a figure. Seconds pass as the figure begins to take form, making its way out.]
Dillon Diamond: SOMEONE’S COMING OUT! [Stepping out into the single spotlight, the red headed, black clad Malicea McDeath turns her gaze upon the silent, captive audience. Following her out, another black leather clad woman with equally dark locks joins the LSW matron, holding something in her hands.]
Howard Butz: What is this about? [Holding out her hand, McDeath takes what the other woman was holding. Running her hand along it’s length, stroking it, she suddenly hefts it over her head
... the 30MC Young Gun belt. The other woman moves quickly, fetching a nearby garbage can, turning it over and emptying it’s contents.]
Howard Butz: What is going on? [Dragging the can back, she puts it in front of McDeath before stepping back. Still holding the belt overhead, McDeath suddenly throws it down with a loud clank from inside the can. Throwing her arms out from her side, McDeath takes a small step back before there is a loud “whooshing” sound and flames shoot upwards from the can. With the lights out and her face peering through the flames, McDeath eerily laughs as the credits roll.
