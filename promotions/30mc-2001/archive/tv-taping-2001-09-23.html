TV Taping

23 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 2 —

[The camera opens on Shorti Blaze, sister of Young Gun Champion, Billy Blaze, making her way through the backstage area when she is suddenly grabbed by the arm and pulled sharply around to be looking in the eye of an angered Trashman.]
Trashman: Well ... look who we have here. [Shorti's eyes go wide with fright at the sudden confrontation as the Trashman grins at her discomfort.]
Trashman: I got a message for your brother ... you tell him I'm not only going to kick his ass but I'm taking his precious title from him, too.
[As Neal the intern rushes into the scene, the Trashman shoves her roughly into his arms.] Trashman: And I got something for Lars if he pokes his nose in where it doesn't belong! [Turning, he leaves the two arm-in-arm before the camera cuts away to Ryan Delaney sitting Indian style on a table. His black purse covered with all sorts of pins is sitting in front of him, a pack of gum and cigarettes lay open. His head is hung down, his hair covering his face as his voice creeps out.]
RYAN DELANEY: Sadist ... a name many are tossing around and it’s seemingly landed on me. But then again, that _is_ what this world does. You see a man dressed in black, he's a gothic. Someone wearing Old Navy is a prep, and a person on the football team is naturally a jock. That is how you deal with people. You stick us in our little groups, and leave us alone because you _all_ are afraid. Afraid of change, of knowledge, and of being different.
[He lifts his head up, and smoke comes out. He takes a long puff on the cigarette and blows it out his nose, then continues. The anger in his voice is gone.]
RYAN DELANEY: And that is really a shame too ... for I am nothing like anything you people have seen before.
[A sigh of grief.] RYAN DELANEY: {BLEEP}ing ecstasy cigarette ... Do jack shit ... [Focused on the burning butt in his hand, Delaney flicks it away as a voice shouts out from down the hallway.]
Voice: Hey man, try some of this sh{BLEEP}! [Turning his head slowly in the direction of the voice, he sees a toking Weed making his way over before fading into Lars and Raoul together in the “Pink
Cave”.] Lars: Oh, that hideous belt. I can’t believe he actually asked me to wear it last week. Can you believe it.
Raoul: Now, now Lars ... We must remember that Billy is young and impressionable. You know as well as I do that he meant well ... and I do have to say that I am so proud of you for handling it so ... diplomatically.
Lars: Well ... Raoul, you know me ... [With a wink from Lars, Raoul’s hand shoots to his mouth stifling a giggle.] Raoul: Yes, I do ... [Together, they share a giggle when the door bursts open, startling Raoul who jumps into Lars’ arms with a fright. A concerned Billy Blaze pokes his head in.]
Billy Blaze: GUYS! Have you seen Shorti? [Looking at each other, both men shake their heads.] Billy Blaze: Someone said that Trashman got her! [With a look of concern. they fade from view as a clock’s ticking loudly sounds. With the screen going black, the clock face of a logo hurtles through the ebony space before settling into view. With the clock face fading into the background faces and names flash by quickly.
The Mean Green Mascot - Mexican superstar, Ieta Fujita - Ricky Starr - The pink clad team of Pretty in Pink, Lars and Billy Blaze with Raoul - Agent of
L.O.V.E., Harry Palmer - Weed - Jake Morgan and an angry Angel Rotten - and finally, the Television Champion, All American Buddy Lee Jr..
As the last of the pictures fade, the camera makes a swing through a small but packed television studio before focussing in on two men sitting at the ringside announcers’ table. The two men are a contrast in looks, one in a sports coat while the other one is in a Hawaiian shirt, leather pants and a matching hat.]
Howard Butz: One week removed from Double Trouble and we are back right here for more wrestling action. Ladies and gentlemen, I’m Howard Butz and the man sitting next to me is Dillon Diamond. Welcome to Thirty Minute Countdown!
[Diamond gives a small tip of the hat.] Howard Butz: Dillon, last week’s show had everything a wrestling fan could ask for ... what can we expect this week?
Dillon Diamond: I think it’s pretty safe to say that we will get more of the same as some issues just weren’t finished at Double Trouble. We may have a new Television Champion but I think we haven’t seen the end of things between
Buddy Lee Jr. and Jake Morgan ... [A cheer goes up interrupting Diamond as Drake Russell steps out from the back. With a big smile on his face, Russell makes his way to the ring.] Howard Butz: Looks like we’re about to be joined by a man, many have said had a big say in last week’s crowning of a Television Champ. Welcome Drake
Russell ... [Russell smiles and waves at the fans before shaking hands with both Diamond and Butz.] Drake Russell: Thanks guys, it’s good to be here. Howard Butz: You’ve heard the talk and the accusations laid out this week. Any responses? Drake Russell: Howard, all I can say is that some things never change ... [Russell is cut off by the loud booing as Jake Morgan steps out from the back and angrily makes his way towards the ring. Dressed for battle in black shorts, boots and his Man, Myth, Morgan T-shirt with his hands taped up in black,
Morgan steps up and eyes the smiling Russell.] Jake Morgan: I've been hearing a lot of talk about me in the past week. Ryan Delaney thinks he's so great that his sh{BLEEP} doesn't stink, well I’ve got news for you, Ryan, it smells almost as bad as your promos.
[He grins as the fans boo loudly.] Jake Morgan: Orly Diaz is talking as if he is innocent of robbing me of matches. He acts as if he is being persecuted by me, well Orly, if you feel bad now, just wait until later.
[He stops for a second, running a hand over his mouth.] Jake Morgan: Drake Russell ... [The fans cheer loudly at the mention of the name bringing an angry look from Morgan.] Jake Morgan: Drake ... you talk as if you were the mastermind behind that act of thievery, but anyone who knows you, knows the truth. You are an idiot who can't think his way out of a wet paper bag. Talk the big talk Drake, but when you enter the ring with me again, you will learn that talk is cheap. Let that be a lesson to you!
[After poking Russell in the chest sharply, he takes a quick step back.] Jake Morgan: And finally, Buddy Lee Jr. thinks he’s a fighting champion but he’s a joke. Buddy, that belt was stolen from me and one day real soon, I will come and take back my property.
[There is a huge cheer as the TV Champ bursts out from the back and makes a beeline for Morgan. With his belt over his shoulder, he steps up to Morgan.]
Buddy Lee Jr.: You know, Jake ... you’ve got a big mouth and one of these days that mouth is going to get you into a load of trouble. You want to come out here and run everyone down for YOUR loss ... you want to do that, that’s your business ... but when you badmouth me ... then it’s my business. Well,
Jake ... guess what? Tonight is your lucky night ... you’ve got yourself a rematch! [A surprised Morgan looks around at the loud and rowdy audience. With a sly grin settling on his lips, he turns back towards the champ.]
Jake Morgan: Tonight ... you and I in a rematch but this time without all the stupid stipulations. No weigh-in, no Orly Diaz just that belt ... my belt on the line.
[Morgan smiles as Buddy nods his head up and down.] Jake Morgan: Just the two of us ... IN A LADDER MATCH! [Jabbing Jake in the chest, Buddy steps closer.] Buddy Lee Jr.: FAIR ENOUGH! [As the two go nose to nose, Russell snatches away the mic from Morgan.] Drake Russell: Hold on a second, guys. Buddy ... you’re right ... Jake has a big mouth and one of these days, it is going to catch up with him ... I say let it be tonight ... let’s make this a three-way dance! And Jake ... LET THAT BE
A LESSON TO YOU! [With Jake’s eyes going wide, Buddy nods his head again as the fans start cheering in the background before the camera switches to Howard Butz.]
Howard Butz: This just keeps on getting more and more interesting ... DO NOT GO AWAY! ***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** The American Red Cross. In the days following recent events, the ARC needs your help more than ever. Give what you can ... blood, money, donate your time if need be.
BEER. It’s good. In fact, you should be drinking one right now. If you were, you might be able to get fine chicks like those in our commercials instead of sitting on your ass watching wrestling at one in the morning on a Saturday. So drink beer, because if you drink enough, anyone or anything can look really good. BEER. Remember, its cheaper than crack!
***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** [Back from the commercial finds Billy Blaze being led to the ring by Lars and the waiting Trashman being held back by ref Orly Diaz. Sliding into the ring,
Billy is ready for a fight barely missing Diaz as he goes on the attack.] *** Ding *** Ding *** Toe-to-toe, Billy and Trash exchange a barrage of punches, neither man withering under the pressure while on the outside of the ring, a worried Lars shouts encouragement.
Howard Butz: They’re wasting no time in this one. Dillon Diamond: You can say that again, Howie. They might have been friends once upon a time but now after Trash’s manhandling of Billy’s sister, there is certainly no love lost.
Blocking a punch, Billy catches Trash with a knee to the midsection, knocking him back against the ropes. Throwing Trash into the corner, Billy’s sudden monkey flip sends his opponent landing on his butt in the middle of the ring.
Howard Butz: Lars seems to be enjoying this. Dillon Diamond: As long as his man’s on top, I would assume so. Howard Butz: What is that supposed to mean? Wait ... I don’t want to know. There’s a knee to the back of Trash’s head ... and a reverse chinlock. Fighting the hold, Trash works himself back to his knees then finally standing. A back elbow catches Billy and breaks the hold but a forearm to the back of the head has Trash reeling in pain. Working his magic, Lars has the audience clapping for the Young Gun champ.
Howard Butz: Billy firmly in control now with a body slam and a leg drop. Pulling Trash off the mat, Billy lifts him vertically into the air and holds him until an escape as the challenger wiggles free. Grabbing Billy from behind,
Trash bulldogs him into the canvas. Howard Butz: A sudden turn here ... Dillon Diamond: Billy went to the well once too often. With Billy out on the mat, Lars is up on the ring apron protesting, drawing the attention of the referee. Diaz tries to order Lars to the floor but has little success as Trash taunts Billy’s mentor.
Howard Butz: Trashman has things in order here but is wasting his time taunting Lars. Dillon Diamond: There’s a belt on the line ... take the pin then taunt away. Running out from the back, a red, white and blue clad masked figure hits the ring, dashing up the corner ropes. As Trash turns around, the masked figure leaps from their perch, crashing into Trash with a missile drop kick. With
Trash laid out flat, the mysterious person darts for the back. Howard Butz: WHAT THE HELL!?!?!?! Dillon Diamond: Odds are, that’s Pepsi Boy. Wobbily but aware of his surroundings, Billy drops on top of Trash for the cover. As Lars leaves the ring apron, Orly Diaz turns to find Billy draped across Trashman.
Howard Butz: THERE’S THE COVER! ONE! TWO! THREE! BILLY BLAZE DEFENDS HIS TITLE WITH SOME HELP FROM A MASKED FRIEND! *** Ding *** Ding ***

— Part 2 of 2 —

Howard Butz: I don’t know what that was about, Dillon but I don’t think
we’ve seen the last of this and I for one am curious why this Pepsi Boy would attack the Trashman like this?
Dillon Diamond: Who knows. You gotta like it though, things are starting to get crazy around here and means good things for the fans here and those watching ay home.
Howard Butz: You have a point there, Dillon. Well folks, we’ve got Holly standing by with referee Orly Diaz. Holly?
[Switching to the backstage area, the lovely Holly comes into view standing next to Orly Diaz.]
Holly: Yes, Howard ... I am here with the senior referee, Orly Diaz. Orly, there’s been alot of talk from Jake Morgan about biased officiating. Your thoughts?
Orly Diaz: Not much I can say. Jake loses his belt and wants to blame it on everyone but himself, that’s what he’s going to do. I have a job to do and that’s exactly what I do ...
[Both Holly and Diaz stop as Raoul walks into view.] Raoul: Have either of you seen Shorti? Holly: I’m trying to do an interview here. [Turning his nose up, a disgusted Raoul walks off.] Raoul: Hmph ... Hussy. [Leaving the back, the camera returns to ringside on Butz and Diamond.] Howard Butz: What was that about? Dillon Diamond: You’re asking me? Why don’t you just tell us what’s coming up next? Howard Butz: Well, to be honest ... I don’t know what to expect. Now, this is interesting ...
[Stepping out from the back, Weed walks out to the ring with an arm around the face painted Ryan Delaney.]
Dillon Diamond: I’m not so sure I’d trust Delaney to be that close. Howard Butz: One word comes to mind ... FREAK. [Stepping into the ring together, Delaney circles the ring with his fingers sticking up as little horns on his head while Weed heads up the ropes to celebrate with the fans. With a running start from across the ring, Delaney nails the climbing Weed with a spinning heel kick to the back, knocking him from his perch.
Tangled up in the ropes, Weed hangs over them, dangling on the ring apron.] Howard Butz: You can’t trust this freak. [Pointing at Weed, he charges forward, nailing the man with a baseball slide and knocking him out of the ring onto the floor. With his opponent down,
Delaney uses the ropes to propel himself to the outside, landing an asai moonsault. Jumping to his feet, Delaney slithers his tongue out of his mouth causing fans to recoil in fear.]
Howard Butz: DID YOU SEE THAT? HIS TONGUE ... IT’S FORKED! WHAT LEVELS OF PERVERSITY WILL THIS MAN GO TO? DIllon Diamond: It’s hard to say with a man who seems to take pleasure in pain. [After rolling Weed back into the ring, they are joined by referee Orly Diaz who calls for the bell.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Whipping Weed across the ring, Delaney catches him on the rebound, taking him to the mat with a fujiwara arm bar. Torturing the arm, Delaney laughs at the pain caused to Weed who cries out.
With the ref asking for a submission, Delaney pulls Weed to his feet. A whip into the ropes and a drop toe hold leads into a single crab. Throwing his body back, Delaney puts more pressure on the leg while grabbing Weed’s head for an inverted bow and arrow.
Howard Butz: WEED CAN’T TAKE MUCH MORE OF THIS! LOOK AT THAT LEG! Dillon Diamond: He has to submit. That much pressure will snap tendons, destroy ligaments and even break a bone. The ref has to get involved here.
Looking closely at the leg, a worried Diaz gets ready to call for the bell when Delaney lets go. Back to his feet, a drop kick to Weed’s leg puts him back down for a following knee drop from the second rope. Rolling back to his feet,
Delaney rolls his eyes back in his head as he snakes his tongue back out of his mouth. Howard Butz: This guy is sick. Someone needs to stop this. This isn’t wrestling. Ryan Delaney’s intent here is to injure his opponent.
Dragging Weed to the center of the ring, Delaney holds the leg up before dropping a knee down into his thigh several times. Falling backwards, he shoots his man up into the corner turnbuckle.
Howard Butz: The fans are letting him hear what’s on their minds. Delaney grins at the Weed chants from the fans, going so far as to clap along. Face down on the top turnbuckle, Weed takes stiff kick to the back. Grabbing a handful of hair, he pulls Weed’s head back, locking on a dragon sleeper.
Howard Butz: Weed’s arms are flailing away. This does not look good. Dillon Diamond: It hasn’t looked good for Weed before the opening bell. With Weed’s arms going limp, Delaney lifts his body into the air. Pushing forward, he drops Weed’s body across the top rope before falling back with a slingshot dragon suplex. Rolling over Weed, Delaney takes the pin.
Howard Butz: There’s a cover. ONE! TWO! WHAT’S HE DOING?!?!? HE LET HIM UP! Dillon Diamond: I don’t think he’s finished destroying Weed. Stepping away to taunt the fans, Delaney heads to his corner and his black purse while Diaz helps a beaten Weed to his feet. Leaning back against the ropes, Weed wants no more.
Howard Butz: That’s it, Weed is calling it quits. *** Ding *** Ding *** Dillon Diamond: That’s the smart thing to do in this case. Howard Butz: Wait a second ... what’s Delaney up to? OH, MY GOD!!!!! [Spinning around, Delaney releases a fireball that lights up in Weed’s face, sending the wrestler to the mat. With the fans booing, Delaney struts around the ring when Buddy Lee Jr., Ieata Fujita and Drake Russell rush out from the back. Outnumbered, he leaves the ring as the fans boo loudly.]
***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** Local commercial. You’ve seen them before. Poorly spliced in with the more professional looking national ones. Selling cars, hawking local restaraunts, stores and/or products before disappearing with an equally poor fade into the next commercial.
M&M’s. You know which commercial ... the green naked M&M caught without her shell on. It’s been a long time since they pushed the green M&M as an aphrodisiac angle. Five bucks says they won’t push the cancer causing red dye
M&M’s anytime soon. ***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** [Returning to ringside the camera finds an upset Butz.] Howard Butz: If you’re just joining us, you just missed a terrifying moment as Ryan Delaney threw fire into Weed’s face. We’re not going to show it because of it’s upsetting violence.
Dillon Diamond: One good thing, Weed gave a thumb’s up as the EMT’s carted him off. Howard Butz: Let’s just hope that he is going to be alright. [The fans cheer as Buddy Lee Jr. and Drake Russell make their way to the ring. Thise cheers turns to boos as Jake Morgan steps out from the back with a ladder of his own followed by Angel.]
Jake Morgan: No Buddy, we’re going to use my ladder for this one! [Morgan slides his ladder into the ring as referee Robin holds back both of his opponents.]
Howard Butz: NO WAY! Dillon, that ladder is covered in barbed wire. What is Jake thinking? Dillon Diamond: It looks like payback time. [A chant of “YOU FUCKED UP!” is heard in the background and grows in volume as Jake enters the ring and picks up his ladder. Looking up at the title belt dangling from the ceiling, Buddy can only smile ... that is until, Jake smacks the ladder into him. Caught by surprise, Robin calls for the bell.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Hit by Russell from behind, Morgan is knocked to his knees. Hammering away with forearms, Russell pushes Morgan up against the ropes before nailing him with a solid clothesline that carries both men to the outside of the ring.
Howard Butz: Both men on the outside of the ring and Drake Russell opens up on Morgan. Dillon Diamond: Inside the ring, Buddy Lee is setting up Jake’s ladder. Howard Butz: They forgot all about the champion. Buddy Lee is going to make this a very short title defense.
Dillon Diamond: I don’t think so ... Setting up the ladder near the ropes, Buddy climbs to the top before leaping off and crashing into both Russell and Morgan on the outside.
Howard Butz: ALL THREE MEN ARE DOWN! The ref starts a count before Russell is up on a seven count. Pulling Jake up, Russell slams his head into the ring apron before taking a back elbow from the former champ. Punching away at Russell, Morgan rolls him back into the ring.
Howard Butz: Jake setting up his ladder in the corner. Irish whip ... NO! RUSSELL WITH THE REVERSE! Morgan cries out in pain as he slams backfirst into the barb wire covered ladder. Russell comes in throwing punches before taking a thumb to the eye which draws a warning from the ref. Kicking the other ladder to the center of the ring, Jake body slams Drake on top of it before dropping an elbow. Grabbing his ladder, he lifts it overhead then drops it down on top of the prone
Russell.
Howard Butz: OH, MY GOD! With a clatter, the second ladder lands on top of the first one as Drake rolls out of the way. Pulled to his feet, Drake catches Morgan with a punch, stunning him. Rolling to Jake’s back, Russell lifts him with a waistlock, falling back and suplexing Jake onto the two ladders.
Howard Butz: That looked bad. Dillon Diamond: It sure did, Howie but you have to remember, Jake can take alot of punishment.
Howard Butz: That’s the truth but it looks like Drake Russell hit his head on the ladder. Several stomps to the head by Morgan draws a warning from the ref who quickly backs away from Morgan’s hard stare. A shot from behind knocks Jake into the corner.
Howard Butz: Buddy Lee now back in the match and is taking it to Jake. Spinning him around, Buddy’s chops land with precision, turning Jake’s chest red with each blow. A kick to the midsection leaves Jake slumped in the corner as Buddy pulls his ladder out from the bottom and props it up in the corner.
Howard Butz: What’s Buddy got in mind here? Dillon Diamond: I don’t know but i can tell you, Jake’s not going to like it. Pulling Jake into the air, Buddy suplexes him onto the propped up ladder, crushing it with his adversary’s weight. Grabbing up the other ladder, Drake slams it down onto Jake’s head.
Howard Butz: Drake Russell with an Irish whip. He catches Buddy Lee on the return ... THAT HAD TO HURT!
Pushed into the air, Buddy Lee comes down on top of the ladder, smashing it into Jake’s face.
Dillon Diamond: I don’t know who that hurt more ... Jake or Buddy Lee? Howard Butz: There’s your answer! JAKE IS BUSTED OPEN! Pulling Jake up, Russell pummels away as Buddy sets up the ladder and starts to climb. Howard Butz: I think Drake is content to beat Jake up and allow Buddy Lee to win the match.
Dillon Diamond: It certainly looks that way. There must be alot of bad blood between these two.
Howard Butz: We’re winding down on time bit don’t worry, we’re not going anywhere until this one is over!
Looking dazed with his face covered in blood, Jake catches Drake with an uppercut to the family jewels. A fast DDT plants Drake into the mat.
Howard Butz: NO ONE SAW THAT COMING! BUDDY LEE NEAR THE TOP! With Buddy reaching up for the belt, Jake kicks the ladder, tipping it over and sending Buddy Lee crashing to the outside of the ring, taking a terribly bad bump.
Howard Butz: OH, MY GOD! OH, MY GOD! With the crowd in stunned silence and both opponents down, Jake sets up the ladder and begins his climb to the top. Reaching the top, he pulls the belt free when the program goes abruptly off the air.
