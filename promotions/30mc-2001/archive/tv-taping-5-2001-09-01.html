TV Taping 5

1 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 2 —

[The camera opens on Angel Rotten and Mean Green huddled around a seated Jake
Morgan while another person bandages his arm. With his arm being wrapped in gauze, Morgan cries out in pain to the apologetic wrapper.]
Jake Morgan: Not so rough ... Angel: Stop being such a big baby. [Taking greater care, the person wrapping the arm looks up as Neal, the intern enters the room.]
Angel: What are you doing in here you little worm? Jake Morgan: Calm down, Angel. I told him he could hang here if he wanted. Angel: You did? Oookkkaaayyy. Could you excuse us for a moment? I need to talk to Jake ... ALONE!
Jake Morgan: Calm down. I’ve got it all figured out ... and you ... keep wrapping! Man: I don't think a hangnail needs this much ... Jake Morgan: No one's paying you to think. KEEP WRAPPING! [Turning, the smiling Morgan looks at the nervous intern.] Jake Morgan: Well? Neal: Well ... uhm, I haven't been able to get near the ... [A pained moan escapes Morgan as he reaches over to a table loaded with an assortment of fast food, cutting Neal off before the camera cuts away to
Newton’s office where he paces away before Shawn Avery and Brody.] Newton: How about a handicap match? You think that’d teach Larsy a lesson? Shawn Avery: Oh yeah ... maybe against the little hip-hoppers ... what’s there name? Newton: You mean 69 Degrees? [The boss rolls his eyes as the door opens and Buddy Lee Jr. steps in.] Buddy Lee Jr.: Newton, I think we need to drop the weight stipulation in my match against Jake.
Newton: You know how to knock? Forget it. The stipulation stays. Buddy Lee Jr.: But ... he’ll never make weight and the only way I’ll get to take him on is if he makes weight ... otherwise, he gets stripped ...
Newton: Riiiiiiiiight ... now you’re starting to see the bigger picture ... [Newton and company laugh at the frowning Junior before the camera breaks away to focus in on Lars, wrapped in a pink feather boa, with a hand on Billy
Blaze’s shoulder. Standing off to the side, a concerned Raoul watches.] Lars: Billy ... we need to talk. It’s about your sister. Now, I want you to know ... Raoul and I don’t hold her responsible for the incidents of the past few weeks.
Raoul: That’s right, Billy. Lars: And of course, we don’t hold you responsible, either. Raoul: Oh, of course not. Not you, Billy. Lars: We know Shorti’s trying to fit in. To be a part of the group but Billy, it’s just messing up the chemistry. Raoul and I are here for a bigger purpose and we can’t let a little bad chemistry mess up our calling ...
Billy Blaze: Come on, Lars. Give her another chance. Shorti’s ... [Billy stops as the door opens and his sister walks in holding a tray full of drinks.] Shorti: DId I just hear my name? [Stepping into the room, Shorti trips and stumbles, dumping the contents of the tray all over Lars.]
Shorti: Oops ... [A fuming Lars glares at Billy as a clock’s ticking loudly sounds. With the screen going black, the clockface of a logo hurtles through the ebony space before settling into view. With the clockface fading into the background faces and names flash by quickly.
The Mean Green Mascot - Mexican superstar, Ieta Fujita - Ricky Starr - The pink clad team of Pretty in Pink, Lars and Billy Blaze with Raoul - Agent of
L.O.V.E., Harry Palmer - Weed - All American Buddy Lee Jr. - and finally, the Television Champion, Jake Morgan and an angry Angel Rotten. As the last of the pictures fade, the camera makes a swing through a small but packed television studio before focussing in on two men sitting at the ringside announcers’ table. The two men are a contrast in looks, one in a sports coat while the other one is in a Hawaiian shirt, leather pants and a matching hat.]
Howard Butz: Welcome to 30 Minute Countdown! I’m Howard Butz and the man on my right as you all know is Dillon Diamond.
[Diamond gives a small tip of the hat.] Howard Butz: What do we have in store for tonight? Will we FINALLY get to see Jake Morgan taking on Buddy Lee Jr. for the Television Title? And ... what kind of punishment does Newton have in mind for Lars of Pretty in Pink?
Dillon Diamond: A betting man, as you well know, will get the answers to those and other questions when we come back from this commercial!
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** M&M’s. You know which commercial ... the green naked M&M caught without her shell on. It’s been a long time since they pushed the green M&M as an aphrodisiac angle. Five bucks says they won’t push the cancer causing red dye
M&M’s anytime soon. Scenes of poor children in some third world country flash by as the Christian Children’s Fund asks you to make a difference in their world for as little as 89 cents a day! Food, medicine, education all possible on 89 cents a day! Won’t you help make a difference? *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** [Back from the break, the camera finds the lovely Holly standing in the back with both members of 69 Degrees on either side of her.]
Holly: Corey ... Kenny ... your thoughts on tonight’s debut? Bad Boy Corey: Holly, while we’re looking forward to getting into the ring, we’d really like to do this number for you ... hit it, Kenny!
[In unison, the pair start humming as they do a little dance number for her. Rolling her eyes, she tells the camera to cut with a little hand motion across her throat. Returning to ringside, the camera focuses in on a laughing Howard
Butz.] Howard Butz: You wanted song and dance last week, Dillon. I think you’re going to get your wish tonight.
Dillon Diamond: What are the odds on that, Howie? [Before an answer, “It’s Raining Men” plays over the PA system as an angry Lars leads the rest of Pretty in Pink to the ring. With Raoul dabbing at his wet face with a pink hanky, Lars strips the feather boa from his neck and wrings it out wetting the canvas.]
Lars: I’m fighting mad and I’m not going to take it anymore! [After putting his foot down, Lars shoos Raoul away.] Lars: Last week, the Trashman’s interference took away from YOUR F-U-N. He had come in and ruin it for all of YOU. That’s right ... Y-O-U! And that makes me fighting mad!
[A chant of Lars’ name brings a hesitation in his speech.] Lars: I’m not the only one ... so is Newton. He’s fighting mad and he’s not going to take it anymore! NEWTON ... we all say do your worst ... make the
Trashman PAY! [With the fans cheering, the members of 69 Degrees hit the ring, coming up with ahold of Lars’ arms. With the surprised PiPsters watching, they send him across the ring for a ride. On the return, they blast him with a double drop kick. Hitting the ring, referee Orly Diaz calls for the bell.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Pulling the confused Lars to his feet, Kenny and Corey send him over with a double hip toss before following with a pair of leg drops. Getting in the middle of it, Diaz leads Kenny from the ring.
Howard Butz: Looks like we have our punishment for Lars ... a handicap match against the team of 69 Degrees.
Dillon Diamond: By the look on his face, I’d say Lars is taken completely by surprise over this.
Sent into the corner, Lars hits the turnbuckle, flipping up and over the ropes to the ring apron. Making a tag, Corey gets into the match, nailing Lars with a clothesline, sending him to the outside of the ring.
Howard Butz: The boys taking it to the outside early in the match. Holding onto the ropes, Corey drives his head into Lars’ midsection before diving over the top and grabbing him in a sunset flip and slamming him down into the hard floor.
Howard Butz: OH MY GOD POWERBOMB! Dillon, did you see that? Dillon Diamond: Sure did, Howard. That looked bad. Chants of “HOLY SHIT!” are heard in the background as Corey slowly pulls Lars to his feet before whipping him into the ring barrier. Hitting hard, Lars slumps to the floor while Corey is joined on the outside by his partner where they start to dance for the booing fans.
Howard Butz: Looks like they’re more interested in dancing for the fans than their opponent.
Diaz’ count is broken up by the concerned PiPsters who need to be held back as Raoul and Billy look ready to kill. After some words between both parties,
Corey and Kenny roll back into the ring before rolling back outside. Howard Butz: 69 Degrees not quite finished with Lars on the outside. Pulling Lars off the floor, they hammer him several times before rolling him back into the ring. Still reeling from the earlier move, Lars slides along the ring ropes looking for his corner and an escape. Falling into his corner, he looks for support only to find his compatriots being warned off by the referee.
A despondant Raoul cries on Billy’s shoulder. Howard Butz: Kenny, now hammering away at Lars ... OH! Head into the turnbuckle. This looks like it’s going to be a short night’s work for Lars.
Dillon Diamond: I guess there’s something to be said for crossing the boss. Don’t. With Lars on his knees, Kenny grabs up the feather boa still inside the ring. Looping it around Lars neck from behind, the 69er pulls back, strangling his opponent. On top of the situation, the ref is in quickly with a warning.
Howard Butz: 69 Degrees isn’t even interested in going for the pin. What are they doing? Dillon Diamond: Howard, that’s called dancing! With Corey coming into the ring, both boys start their little dance routine which draws boos from the fans while an angry Billy Blaze paces the outside of the ring. Having seen enough, he darts into the ring but is immediately stopped and led back outside.
Howard Butz: Is this really necessary? Do they have to destroy Lars’ boa? Having a tug-of-war in the middle of the ring, they pull in opposite directions, stretching it to it’s full capacity until snapping.
Howard Butz: This is terrible. They’ve beaten the man, they’ve destroyed his property ... now pin him and be done with it.
Dillon Diamond: Howard, I have a feeling they’re sending a message to the rest of the locker room.
After more dancing, Diaz pushes Kenny from the ring. Pulling Lars to his knees, Corey rears back with a fist only to be doubled over from a low blow. With no one to stop him, Lars rolls to the safety of the outside. Charging into the ring, Kenny argues with the ref before dashing across the ring and up the ropes. Jumping from the top at the easy target, Kenny slams facefirst into the ring barrier as in prima ballerina form, Lars pirouettes out of the way at the last second.
Howard Butz: Now, that’s dancing! Lars heading back into the ring. There’s a whip into the corner!
Putting Corey up on the ropes, Lars charges across the ring and jumps on his opponent’s back, hanging on him and pumping his weight into him. Dropping off of Corey, Lars allows him to fall back, held in place by his legs intertwined in the middle ropes. Climbing back up, Lars stands on Corey’s testicles and bounces up and down to the fans’ cheering.
Howard Butz: I think there’s a message being sent, Dillon. Dillon Diamond: I can’t argue that one with you. Pulling Corey away from the ropes and sitting him in the corner, Lars smoothers him with his crotch in an obscene bronco buster until a warning from the ref pulls him off.
Howard Butz: Kenny coming in to make a save ... Lars with a front kick. Standing head scissor and a lift. What’s Lars setting up here? Dillon Diamond: Look at the power. To hold him up that long ... Holding Kenny in the air with his face buried in the man’s crotch, Lars finally sits down into a powerbomb, shaking the ring. Snatching up both halves of his boa and wrapping it around his neck, Lars drags both men to the center of the ring. Laying head to feet, Lars drops down making the cover on both ...
his head in Corey’s crotch and his own in Kenny’s face. Howard Butz: HE’S COVERING THEM BOTH! ONE! TWO! THREE! Dillon Diamond: It’s the Daisy Chain! *** Ding *** Ding ***

— Part 2 of 2 —

[With the help of the rest of PiP, an exhausted Lars is led to the back
followed by the cheers of the fans. Stopping, he turns to the fans abd blows a kiss at them before disappearing into the back. Following him into the back, they are stopped by Holly with a mic in hand.]
Holly: Lars, your thoughts on the attack by 69 Degrees? [Exhausted and out of breath, he is held up by Billy.] Lars: I think it’s rather obvious that this whole attack was masterminded by the very jealous Trashman in order to escape his punishment. Well Trash, you will get what’s coming to you!
[Glaring into the camera, his angry face fades from view heading into a commercial break.] ***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** BEER. It’s good. In fact, you should be drinking one right now. If you were, you might be able to get fine chicks like those in our commercials instead of sitting on your ass watching wrestling at one in the morning on a Saturday. So drink beer, because if you drink enough, anyone or anything can look really good. BEER. Remember, its cheaper than crack!
Taco Bell is now open later. So get your fat ass off the couch and come get fatter! Yo quiero Gordo Americanos!
***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** [Returning to the program, the camera zooms in on the announcers from their ringside table.]
Howard Butz: Welcome back. Dillon, we’re heading into our main event match ... will we see the Television Champion put his title on the line against Buddy Lee Jr.? Dillon Diamond: There’s no telling. To be honest, it seems like both men are ducking each other ... stipulation here, different match there. Both men are saying they want to settle it in the ring but do they really?
Howard Butz: Hard to believe that two of this promotion’s very best would be ducking each other, Dillon. They both talk the talk ...
Dillon Diamond: Right but so far, no one’s walking the walk and until that happens, that’s all it is, alot of talk.
Howard Butz: We’ve got ... Shawn Avery?!? [Stepping from the back, the smiling manager makes his way towards the announcers’ table and has a seat.]
Shawn Avery: It’s your pleasure, guys Howard Butz: What can we do for you, Shawn? Shawn Avery: I’m just here to deliver a message from the boss. He wants to see Dillon here ... and if I were him, I’d make it fast.
Dillon Diamond: WHAT?!? He wants to see me? Shawn Avery: Yeah, like yesterday. [Tearing off his headphones, Diamond darts for the back while Avery settles in.] Howard Butz: Thanks for the message ... you can go now as we get ready for our main event match.
Shawn Avery: Naw, I think I’ll just stick around and help out here. Show you how a professional does it.
Howard Butz: Riiiiiiiiight ... anyway, there’s the Televsion Champion now. Jake Morgan coming to the ring with Angel and Mean Green. What’s with the wrapped arm? [Making his way to the ring to a heavy chorus of boos, Jake holds his heavily bandaged arm, a grimace of pain on his face.]
Shawn Avery: If you did your homework, I think you’d find Jake reinjured his arm in last week’s match.
Howard Butz: Looks like another excuse to me ... [Quieting the crowd, Morgan strolls to the ring.] Jake Morgan: Buddy Lee ... no more hiding, no more ducking me ... no more excuses ... get your ass out here and let me put an end to this little game of yours!
[Finishing his sentence, Morgan grimaces in pain and leans heavily on a concerned Angel Rotten. Stepping out from the back, Buddy Lee Jr. heads into the ring. Looking over Morgan, a head shaking Junior asks for the mike.]
Buddy Lee Jr.: How can you say you’re ready to defend your title? LOOK ATCHA! You’re overweight. Your arm is completely bandaged. How in the heck can you say you’re ready to defend your title? How can you?
[Stepping forward. Morgan grabs the mic back, grimacing in the process.] Jake Morgan: WHAT?!? I’m the Television Champion ... I could beat the likes of you with both arms heavily bandaged. Get your little scale out here and let’s do this!
[Shaking his head, Junior points to Morgan’s arm.] Jake Morgan: WHAT?!? You’re not going to fight me again? Are you chicken? Is there a yellow streak running down your back? Is that it?
[With Buddy Lee stepping forward, a grimacing Morgan pushes Angel Rotten in front of him. Stopping in his tracks, Junior glares at the champ.]
Howard Butz: Another week, another disappointment ... Shawn Avery: Looks that way. Another week and the challenger doesn’t want to fight for the title.
Howard Butz: WHAT?!? Shawn Avery: You heard Morgan, he said he’s ready to go with both arms bandaged ... the mark of a champion. Buddy Lee’s the coward here.
Howard Butz: Give me a break. Wait, we’ve got heated words inside the ring and the fans are getting restless. We may get our match afterall!
[With the fans booing, Morgan jabs Junior in the chest several times until the challenger steps forward again, stopping with Angel between him and Morgan.
Shoving Angel into Buddy Lee, Morgan leaves the ring in a hurry.] Howard Butz: What were you saying about being a coward? [With a few words Morgan on the outside, Junior ignores Angel until she slaps his face, nearly spinning him around from the unexpected force. Surprised, he stares at her until she slaps him a second time just as hard. Rearing back for a third blow, her arm is caught by the expectant Lee.]
Shawn Avery: What’s he going to do slap a woman around? That’s real sporting. [Waiting for something to happen, the fans rise to their feet as a leather clad person races from the back and hits the ring. With long black hair and a white painted face, the newcomer grabs Buddy Lee by the shoulder and spins him around and bears his teeth.]
Howard Butz: Who the hell is this? Shawn Avery: If you knew anything about the business, you’d know exactly who this is. Howard Butz: And you know? Shawn Avery: Of course, I know. As “the Manager Extraordinaire”, I make it my business to know ... that’s Elusive Ryan Delaney ...
Howard Butz: Okay ... if you say so. [Caught by surprise, Buddy Lee lets go of Angel before taking a couple of rights to the head.]
Howard Butz: Are those fangs? [Bearing his teeth at the ref, Delaney gets the bell wrung.] *** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: We’ve got a match underway? Pushing Junior back against the ropes, Delaney opens up with some serious chops that turns his opponent’s chest bright red. A whip into the ropes and shot to the throat sends Buddy to the mat in pain.
Howard Butz: This Delaney works quick. Buddy Lee is down and in pain. Grabbing an arm, the leather clad man wraps a leg around Buddy’s neck. Dropping to the mat, he pulls back on the arm with one hand while cutting off the blood supply with his leg.
Howard Butz: Some kind of leg sleeper ... Shawn Avery: Triangle scissor lock ... Using the ropes with his free hand for added leverage, Delaney is forced to break the hold by the ref. On his feet, Delaney snakes his tongue out at the retreating Diaz. Pulling the choking Buddy to his feet and slamming him back into the corner, Delaney strikes with more reverse knife hands before snap maring him over.
Howard Butz: Delaney climbing the ropes ... there’s a knee to the back. Buddy Lee in all kinds of trouble. There’s a cover ... ONE! TWO! KICK OUT! Pushing Lee to his stomach, Delaney drops a knee to his back before pulling back on his head with a reverse chinlock. Holding a struggling Buddy in place,
Delaney rolls his eyes back in his head as his tongue darts out again. Shawn Avery So much for your Number One contender. Howard Butz: This one isn’t over yet. Struggling, Buddy is able to crawl to the ropes and get a break. releasing his grip, Delaney departs with a shot to the back before drawing a warning from the ref. Pulled to his feet, Junior is spun around but reverses the hold to send
Delaney off with an Irish whip but is reversed himself and sent off across the ring. Howard Butz: There’s a reverse ... Here comes Buddy Lee! Shawn Avery: Spoke too soon, Delaney with a reverse of his own. On the return, Lee ducks under Delaney’s thrown punch, wrapping his arms around his attacker’s waist, Lee lifts Delaney into the air to throw him.
Howard Butz: Buddy Lee with a belly-to- Thrown overhead, Delaney lands on his feet. After nailing him with a forearm to the head, Delaney’s eye gouge has Buddy reeling.
Shawn Avery: Spoke a little too soon there, Howie. How do you keep your job? Howard Butz: Never mind me. We’ve got an abdominal stretch ... no, it’s ... Shawn Avery: That’s an octopus. I think this one is as good as over. Continuing to work Buddy’s neck, Delaney struggles to keep his balance as Buddy fights to break the hold. Throwing himself back, Buddy slams Delaney into the corner, freeing himself. Holding the back of his neck, Lee unloads chops of his own before setting up his opponent for a suplex.
Howard Butz: I think you’re right. This one is about to be over! EXPLODER SUPLEX! Throwing himself back against the ropes, Buddy is tripped up and pulled to the outside by Morgan. Angered, Lee rears back and threatens the begging champ.
Letting go, he hits Angel who is pulled into the way by the champ. Shawn Avery: What were you saying about sporting? Hitting a defenseless woman. Howard Butz: Oh please. With the fans booing, the surprised Lee is spun around and takes a face full of green mist. Dropping to the floor with his face covered and in pain, the ref calls for the bell.
*** Ding *** Ding *** [Zooming in on his face, Ryan Delaney rolls his eyes back in his head while sticking his tongue out as green liquid drips from his mouth before the credits roll.]
