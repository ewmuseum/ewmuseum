TV Taping 16

18 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 2 —

[The camera opens with a close-up on a small television monitor showing the
30MC Young Gun champ, Angel addressing her fans at a recent Lost Soul Wrestling event. With one belt around her waist and another resting comfortably on her shoulder, the blonde haired beauty is caught in mid-dialogue.]
Angel: I would also like to send my love to a very special man in my life. Newton, I thank you for all that you have done for me and I’m glad to be working alongside of you ...
[A hand comes into view turning down the sound as the camera pulls back to show a grinning Newton sitting at his desk. Looking back, a grimace of pain on his face for a second from the beating he took just two weeks ago, he nods his head at his bodyguard, Brody standing there behind the boss.]
Newton: I knew it ... the babe’s warm for my form. [Rolling his eyes, Brody shakes his head before interjecting.] Brody: I don’t think that's what she ... [Throwing up a hand, Newton cuts the bigger man off.] Newton: How many times do I have to tell you, I don’t pay you to think? Why don’t you go and guard my body or something?
[Shaking his head, Newton sits back in his chair with a big grin on his face as a disgruntled Brody storms off.]
Newton: And don’t slam the door! [With a slam, the camera is on the move, making a sweep of a trashed locker room, furniture everywhere. Pulling back, a shirtless Jake Morgan, Television champion, is seen posing in front of a mirror with a big grin on his face.
Flexing his biceps, Morgan nods his head.] Jake Morgan: Man ... myth ... masterpiece. [Letting his breath out, Morgan pushes his belly out, grabbing it with both hands and shaking the bulbous leftovers of his binge eating.]
Jake Morgan: Lovely ... huh? [The sound of a door closing behind him catches Morgan’s attention. Releasing the grip on his stomach, Morgan turns his head to find a man with short dark hair and his eyes covered by a pair of wraparound shades. Tearing off the shades, the young man extends a hand to Morgan who just looks down at it.]
Man: Just wanted to introduce myself. The name’s Tommy Trevor ... it’s going to be a pleasure working with you once I win myself a contract.
[Shaking his head, Morgan glares at the intruder.] Jake Morgan: Anyone around here know how to knock? [The camera leaves Morgan looking at the grinning intruder to find an arguing Billy Blaze and the Trashman in the backstage area.] Trashman: We’re not finished yet. Billy Blaze: Yeah, yeah ... whatever. Trashman: You think you're hot because of teaming up with Lars but I got your number. Billy Blaze: Like the last time we met in the ring and I did what? Kicked your ass? [No rebuttle as Buddy Lee Jr. and Ricky Starr step between the two young men.] Buddy Lee Jr.: Nice, nice. Can’t we all just get along? [A scowling Starr looks from Blaze to Trash while Buddy awaits an answer, getting none.] Buddy Lee Jr.: I don’t care what your problems are but I want you to shake hands and make-up.
[Taking a step back, Trashman shakes his head.] Trashman: You on crack? That’s not going to happen. Buddy Lee Jr.: O .... kay. I see this is going to be a problem. Then let me make this as easy as possible.
[Spinning on his heels, Buddy Lee Jr. steps up to the standing Trashman, waving a finger in his face.]
Buddy Lee Jr.: I better not see you around our tag match. I see you, we’re going to have problems.
Ricky Starr: Yeah, what he said. [As they stalk off, the camera fades to black as a clock’s ticking loudly sounds in the background. With the screen going black, the clock face of a logo hurtles through the ebony space before settling into view. The clock face fades into the background, giving way to faces and names that flash by quickly.
The Mean Green Mascot - Mexican superstar, Ieta Fujita - Ricky Starr - The pink clad team of Pretty in Pink, Lars and Billy Blaze with Raoul - Agent of
L.O.V.E., Harry Palmer - Weed - All American Buddy Lee Jr. - the heavenly Angel and finally, the Television Champion, Jake Morgan and an angry Angel Rotten.
As the last of the pictures fade, the camera makes a swing through a small but packed television studio before focusing in on two men sitting at the ringside announcers’ table. The two men are a contrast in looks, one in a sports coat while the other one is in a Hawaiian shirt, leather pants and a matching hat.]
Howard Butz: Welcome to 30 Minute Countdown. I’m your host, Howard Butz and the man on my right is none other than Dillon Diamond.
[With a little salute, Diamond turns towards Butz.] Dillon Diamond: Glad to be here, Howie. Howard Butz: Big night tonight. We’ve got the newly crowned Young Gun champ, Angel with her first title defense ... Dillon Diamond: This is one match, I’m really looking forward to! Howard Butz: What? Last week, you were screaming foul as Angel defeated the former champion, Flaxen.
Dillon Diamond: Gotta change with the times. That was last week, this is a new day. Howard Butz: Whatever. Also up in our main event, we have four men squaring off that promises to be a barnburner.
Dillon Diamond: That it is, Howard. On one side, you have the current number one contender in Buddy Lee Jr. and his partner, the high flying Ricky Starr taking on the dynamic duo of Billy Blaze and Lars of Pretty in Pink. Four men all vying for the top spot here in the Countdown.
Howard Butz: That they are, Dillon but right now, we have to break away for a commercial ... you know the drill ... DON’T GO AWAY! WE’LL BE RIGHT BACK!
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Kids love them! Adults love them! You love them. Everyone loves them! Those candy coated shells, Those marvelous peanuts, rice krispies, almonds and peanut butter they’re now stuffing them with. Even the plain chocolate ones! Don’t forget those terrific, wonderfully colored candy coated shells and you have the perfect candy treat! M&M’s can’t be beat! Buy some today!
You know it, I know it ... Dr. Pepper makes the world taste better! The only problem is the rest of the country doesn't know it. Don't believe me? Just ask for it in Milwaukee and see what they give you ... “We got root beer.”
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** [A return from the commercial finds Angel in her “fighting whites” slidng into the ring before bouncing to her feet. Grabbing up two title belts, the young woman jumps up onto the corner ropes to celebrate with the cheering fans.]
Howard Butz: The fans just spreading a little of that love around for the new Young Gun champ. Dillon Diamond: You bet, Howard. I wouldn’t mind spreading in a little of that love as well ...
Howard Butz: Do you mind? Dillon Diamond: Not at all, Howard, not at all. [Jumping down, Angel heads over to the announcers’ table, asking for a mic.] Angel: WHOOOOOO! It’s great to be here with so many wonderful fans. HE loves you! [Punching her belts into the air, the smiling Angel turns when she is joined inside the ring by Holly. Walking up to Angel, she takes the mic away.]
Holly: I don’t know who you think you’re fooling with all this holier than thou ... [Her eyes narrowing, Angel drops the belts to the mat before snatching up the mic.] Angel: In just a few short weeks here, I see that I have my work cut out for me. I must set things right. There is time for all who stray from HIS flock.
Angel Rotten has questioned HIS authority and for that, next week is HER judgement day ... [Closing her eyes, she crosses herself.] Angel: ... but I have much to do before then. With Flaxen gone, I can now put you back on the straight and narrow road to your salvation.
[Shocked at first, Holly gets an indignant look on her face as she steps forward with a few choice words. Pointing a finger in Angel’s face for a second, the two are pried apart by referee Robin. With Robin seperating everyone, Holly gets a chance to sneak in a slap to Angel’s face.]
Angel: HE said, “Turn the other cheek.” [Taking a step forward, Holly slaps the other side of her face. Looking proud of herself, Holly struts around the ring as the jeers and whistles.]
Howard Butz: I don’t think Holly should have done that. [Dropping the mic to the mat, Angel grabs Holly from behind by her long hair, pulling her to the center of the ring. With a look of surprise, Holly pleads for help from the fans. Lifted into the air, Holly is put down with a back suplex. Jumping to her feet, Angel snatches up her belts and drops them in her corner. Signaling to the ref, the bell is rung.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Dillon Diamond: This is going to be good, Howard! Howard Butz: What was Holly thinking by getting herself into this? Dillon Diamond: A whole lot of trouble! Yanking Holly to her feet by the hair, Angel stops long enough for Holly to plead her case. A sudden right hand is blocked by the champ as Holly shows here true colors.
Howard Butz: Angel with a block. Spinning her around ... BACK SUPLEX! Slamming back against the ropes, Angel shoots forward for a senton, rolling to her feet. In a fluid motion, she hits the middle ropes, throwing her body backwards and gracefully knifes through the air, sticking a moonsault. Hooking a leg, she makes the cover.
Howard Butz: ONE! TWO! THREE! NO! HOLLY WITH THE SHOULDER UP AT THE LAST POSSIBLE INSTANCE! Dillon Diamond: That was so close, that really could have gone either way. Howard Butz: In this case, it means more pain for Holly. Angel cinching Holly up. SNAP SUPLEX!
Pulling Holly to her feet, Angel slams her back into the corner. Reading her the riot act, Angel smacks home a reverse knife hand chop that has the fans whooing loudly. Stepping back to put her all into another, Angel leaves herself open to an eye gouge.
Howard Butz: That smarts! Holly with a handful of hair. There’s a snapmare. Blinded, Angel tries crawling for the ropes to pull herself up, only to take kicks to the midsection. Followed across the ring by her kicking opponent,
Angel takes several more before getting to the ropes. Howard Butz: Robin calling for a break as Angel uses the ropes to pull herself to her feet.
Dillon Diamond: Not good, Howard, Angel can’t see a thing and Holly isn’t going to give her a clean break.
With the ref moving in to seperate the two, she gets into an argument with Holly that leads to a shove. Howard Butz: HEY! You’re not supposed to put your hands on an official. Dillon Diamond: You know that wouldn’t happen with Jake in there. Howard Butz: Not this again? Grabbing Holly from behind, Angel stops for a second, punching her arm into the air before taking her to the mat with an inverted DDT. Getting back to her feet, the champ kicks Holly’s arms across her body before turning away.
Seemingly deep in thought, Angel slowly walks from the downed woman towards the nearest corner, taking small steps.
Howard Butz: What’s she doing? Dillon Diamond: I’m not sure but it looks like she’s counting off steps. Climbing up onto the corner turnbuckle, Angel balances herself before leaping high into the air. Spinning her body, she corkscrews through the air, shaking the ring with a splash. Landing on top of an unmoving Holly, she hooks a leg.
Howard Butz: CORKSCREW SPLASH! CORKSCREW SPLASH! THERE’S THE COVER! ONE! TWO! THREE! *** Ding *** Ding ***

— Part 2 of 2 —

[With Angel being helped out of the ring by the ref, the camera pans the
cheering crowd, focusing in on a lone woman. With long red hair, a stylishly dressed beauty all in black with matching heels stands from her frontrow seat.
Following her until she makes it to the aisle, the camera breaks to the back to find the two members of 69 Degrees in a discussion.]
Bad Boy Corey: I don’t know, Kenny, maybe we should take up Lars’ offer and become Pretty in Pinksters.
Kickin’ Kenny: Don’t tell me you’re getting cold feet. Bad Boy Corey: It’s not that ... it’s ... look what he’s done for Billy. The stylish hair ... and all the chicks ... [Thinking about it, Kenny nods his head in agreement when both heads whirl around at the sound of a voice.]
Voice: THERE YOU ARE! [Spinning around, the camera catches a handsome young man with an open light colored baseball jersey. The unknown man flashes his pearly whites as he throws an arm around the shoulders of the two confused wrestlers. Moving to follow the trio as they walk off, the camera zooms in on the lettering across his
shoulderblades: CHICKMAGNET.] Young man: This is your lucky day! [With them moving off into the distance down the hallway, the camera fades into a commercial.]
***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** BEER. It’s good. In fact, you should be drinking one right now. If you were, you might be able to get fine chicks like those in our commercials instead of sitting on your ass watching wrestling at on a Saturday night. So drink beer, because if you drink enough, anyone or anything can look really good. BEER.
Remember, its cheaper than crack! Mach 3 razors. Ever seen the handsome, shirtless guy walk into his bathroom ready to shave off the three minutes of facial growth? Lather up and suddenly start flicking the Mach 3 across his face making wide swaths through the shaving cream, then making these sideways motions across his chin. Right. If these things actually had blades in them, the guy's face would be a bloody mess. Take it from someone with facial hair unlike these guys.
***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** [Back to the show, the camera pans the ring where Buddy Lee Jr. and Ricky Starr loosen up awaiting the arrival of their opponents for tonight’s match.
Getting last second instructions from referee Orly Diaz, they don’t have to wait long as Pretty in Pink arrives. Stepping out from the back, Lars and
Billy, dressed in identical pink leather chaps over the tiniest pink trunks, turn towards each other and slap out a high five. Following through, their hands continue on their downward motion, slapping their partner’s buttcheek.
Lars giggles before getting a second swat on the ass, this time from Raoul, in a pink bellbottomed jumpsuit and white platform shoes. Rearing back for another blow, Raoul stops as Billy turns, waving a finger in his face.]
Howard Butz: We’ve got the grand entrance, now all we need is the bell to kick this one off.
[Nearing the ring, PiP stops as Starr comes shooting forward to get started. Grabbed from behind by Buddy to give them a fair entrance, Starr complains until both men are pulled to the mat and outside the ring.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Caught by surprise, Lee and Starr are hammered before both being sent crashing into the ring barrier. With their opponents down, Lars and Billy strip off their chaps, handing them to Raoul before resuming.
Howard Butz: DOUBLE SUPLEXES ON THE FLOOR! Heading back into the ring, Billy dashes from the far side forward, using the ropes to propel himself to the outside, knocking Lee and Starr to the ground a second time. After a warning from the ref, Lars gets a finger wagging.
Howard Butz: Diaz trying to get the match into the ring ... WHAT THE?!?! Ladies and gentlemen, we’ve just been joined by Jake Morgan. Have a seat ...
Jake Morgan: Sure thing ... Howard Butz: And what do we owe the ... Jake Morgan: Knock off the brown nosing, Butz. I’m here to scout the officiating in this match. I could care less about either of these teams.
Looking at Diaz, Lars sucks Diaz’ finger into his mouth sending the ref running off in disgust. With help from Billy, they roll Buddy into the ring.
Jake Morgan: There’s one. Howard Butz: One what? Dillon Diamond: Howard, will you let the man do his job? Howard Butz: His job? What are you talking about. Jake Morgan: At least you understand, Dillon. A whip into the ropes and the return has Buddy Lee wrapped up in Lars’ grasp from behind. Throwing a leg around Buddy’s, Lars hooks an arm for an abdominal stretch. Struggling not to be caught, Buddy has Lars hopping on one foot across the ring to his corner and a waiting Ricky Starr.
Howard Butz: There’s a tag to Starr. Vaulting into the ring, Starr catches Lars with a drop kick to the chops. Hitting the ropes, Starr crisscrosses the ring, coming back to the middle of the ring and landing a running knee drop. Rolling to his feet, Starr comes up with a roar. Grabbing Lars’ legs and opening them, he lets out a cry before dropping a leg to the jewels. Lars bolts upright in pain while Raoul shrieks in terror on the outside. Billy attempts to enter the ring but is sent back outside.
Howard Butz: Starr making a tag. Dillon Diamond: Quick tags keeps both men fresh. Dragging a pleading Lars back to his corner, Buddy Lee slams him into the turnbuckles before throwing him with a belly-to-belly suplex. Dropping for a cover, he is thrown off on a two count. Crawling to his corner, Lars sends the women in the audience into a frenzy as Billy reaches in for the tag, showing his backside.
Howard Butz: Buddy Lee is not going to let Lars get away that easily. Dillon Diamond: The key to a successful tag team is fresh members and making sure you work over opponent as much as possible.
Howard Butz: What do you think, Jake? Jake Morgan: I think Orly Diaz is doing a terrible job. Look right there, a man not in the match up on the ring apron. That’s two.
With Raoul in a panic and on the apron, Diaz moves in to stop him. With the coast clear, Starr runs in for a double team but is stopped by Buddy Lee.
Arguing with Buddy, Starr is left open for a drop kick from Billy Blaze, who takes out both men. With Starr rolling to the outside, Billy sends Lee across the ring, hammering him to the mat with a spinning heel kick.
Howard Butz: Buddy Lee rolling to the outside of the ring. Leaning on Starr for support, the two men are left wide open for Billy’s tope con hilo, all three men collapsing on the floor. Helped by Lars into the ring,
Billy pulls himself to his feet, holding his arm to a loud cheer. Making his way onto the apron, Buddy Lee is pulled back into the ring by both men. Leaving for his corner, Lars winks at Diaz before blowing him a kiss.
Howard Butz: WHERE’S THE TAG? Jake Morgan: What tag? Howard Butz: Lars ... Billy? That’s my point! Sending Lee across the ring, Billy flips him up into the air with a back body drop. A tag brings Lars into the ring with a knee drop off the ropes. Pulled to his feet, Buddy Lee is sent into the turnbuckles. Spinning Lee around, Lars swivels his hips before launching his limp wristed reverse knife hands.
Counting on Lars for having his opponent in the corner, Diaz is distracted by Raoul. Howard Butz: HERE COMES RICKY STARR! Coming through the ropes, Starr is sent to the floor by a running Billy Blaze knee to the head. Sliding out of the ring, Billy lands forearms while Lars works over Lee.
Jake Morgan: That’s three. Howard Butz: JAKE!?!? WHERE ARE YOU GOING?!?!? Leaving his seat, Morgan has a few words for Diaz. Arguing with both Morgan and Raoul, Diaz is grabbed by the ankles and pulled to the outside of the ring. Yanking him to his feet, Morgan is animated before shovng Diaz away and pulling him back into a short arm clothesline.
Howard Butz: WHAT IS THIS?!?!? MORGAN TAKING OUT ORLY DIAZ!!! THAT’S HIS JOB? With Lars putting the boots to a sinking Lee in the corner, the PiPster doesn’t see Pepsi Boy hit the ring with a chair in hand. Rearing back, he waffles Lars over the head.
Howard Butz: PEPSI BOY WITH A CHAIRSHOT! Dillon Diamond: I don’t envy him right now. HERE COMES BILLY AND HE’S LIVID! Spinning Pepsi Boy around, Billy sends the masked man off for the far ropes, dropping him with a diving clothesline. Pulling him up and snapping vertically in the air, Billy holds the smaller Pepsi Boy ... that is until he’s joined in the ring.
Howard Butz: THIS IS GETTING OUT OF HAND, DILLON! IT’S 69 DEGREES! Dillon Diamond: And someone else ... Entering the ring behind Billy, they pull Pepsi Boy free. Spinning around, a confused Billy is grabbed by the arms and sent for a ride across the ring.
Bouncing back off the ropes, Billy runs right into the stranger’s boot. Howard Butz: SUPER KICK! SUPER KICK! WHO IS THAT, DILLON? Dillon Diamond: I don’t know, Howard but he nearly took Billy’s head off! Beside himself, Raoul rushes the ring only to be grabbed and lifted into the air by Corey and Kenny. With his feet still kicking, Raoul is dropped in front of the grinning third 69er. With a front kick, he doubles Raoul over before hooking his arms. One double underhook face driver later and Raoul is out of the picture.
Howard Butz: All three Pipsters are down and out ... listen to that, Dillon, the fans don’t like it one little bit.
Dillon Diamond: They sure don’t. With a chant of “BULLSHIT!” going on in the background and under the instruction of the third man who cracks Buddy Lee over the head with the chair,
Corey and Kenny drag Buddy’s body and drape it across Billy. Satisfied with their work, they leave the ring before Jake dives in.
Howard Butz: THIS IS GETTING WORSE BY THE SECOND! JAKE WITH A COUNT! THIS STINKS, DILLON! ONE! TWO! THR ... WHAT’S HE DOING? HE STOPPED HIS COUNT?!?!? NOW, HE’S CALLING FOR THE BELL? I JUST DON’T KNOW WHAT TO MAKE OF THIS, DILLON! *** Ding *** Ding *** [Rising to his feet, Morgan disgustedly waves his arm at the two men lying prone on the canvas. Sliding between the ropes, he makes his way for the back amid a rousing chorus of boos.
Leaving Morgan, the camera zooms in on the smiling faces of 69 Degrees and their accomplice.]
Young man: It’s just the beginning, baby! [The smiles disappear as a thump is heard over the PA system. The camera moves back to the ring and an angry Lars with a mic in hand over the downed Raoul.]
Lars: IT’S TIME FOR A SPANKSGIVING!!! [A loud cheer for the battered Lars fades into a commercial as the show ends.] *** 30 Minute Countdown© ***
