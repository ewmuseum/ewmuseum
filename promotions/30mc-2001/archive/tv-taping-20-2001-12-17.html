TV Taping 20

17 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 2 —

[The camera opens inside the office of 30MC boss, Newton. Seated at his desk,
Newt looks over to his bodyguard, Brody.] Newton: Who’s Gideon Frost? [Shrugging his shoulders, Brody follows Newton’s lead when the door to the office opens and in steps former Young Gun champion, Angel.]
Newton: There she is! [A bit confused, Angel smiles at Brody standing silently to the side.] Angel: You wanted to see me? Newton: Sure did. I’ll get right to the point. In this business, I have to do everything in my power to see that the fans are satisfied ... my fans. The ones out there in the audience as well as those sitting at home watching us. NOT the ones watching you at that crappy little Lost Soul promotion. Lost Soul?
What’s that all about? More like “Waste of Time” if you ask me. [Waving a hand, Newton cuts Angel off.] Newton: Anyway, I can’t risk my investments ... you, my dear, are an investment. So, from here on out, I forbid you from competing for “them”.
Angel: WHAT?!?!? You know I can’t do that ... HIS work isn’t ... Newton: That’s all. End of story. [Leaving a ready to argue Angel, the camera is on the prowl, finding newcomer, Gideon Frost storming through the backstage area. Stopping, he holds up a pink t-shirt, shaking his head as reads off the slogan on the front of it.]
Gideon Frost: Sacrificing ALL to bring you good fashion?!?!? [Closing his eyes, Frost lowers his head.] Gideon Frost: I AM NOT GOING TO WEAR THIS. [Grabbed from behind, Frost’s eyes snap open, ready for action as he’s spun around. Staring back at him is a heavily muscled, shirtless man with a crazy look in his eye.]
Man: YOU KNOW WHERE BRODY IS? [Pulling away with a laugh, an unimpressed Frost looks the man over.] Gideon Frost: Nope. [Stepping forward, just inches from Frost’s face, the man speaks.] Man: YOU SEE HIM ... TELL HIM KILLJOY’S LOOKING FOR HIM. Gideon Frost: Riiiiiiiight ... [A loud slapping sound causes Killjoy to straighten up.] Killjoy: HUH?!?!? [Rubbing his backside as he spins around, he finds a smiling chap wearing Lars looking him over.]
Lars: Hellooooooo ... Tarzan! Some highlights and conditioner would do wonders for that hair!
[With a wink at the angered man, Lars looks around him towards Frost.] Lars: And you, Frosty better get ready. Big match tonight and I’m the referee! Toodles! [With the camera following Lars as he saunters off, a loud roar can be heard in the background as Killjoy lets off some steam. On the move once again, the camera finds Jake stepping into his still demolished dressing room and finding the bandaged Mean Green picking it up, tossing boxes of Twinkies, Hostess and
Little Debbies cakes along with bags of fast food into a big green garbage bag.] Jake Morgan: WHAT ARE YOU DOING?!?!? Mean Green: Cleaning up? Jake Morgan: No ... not the Twinkies. Leave the Yodels, too. Wait, not those ... [Bending over, Morgan lifts up a McDonald’s bag and hands it to his “Fowl Friend”.] Jake Morgan: Here. Throw this away. [Looking at the bag, to Morgan then back to the bag, Mean Green turns it over and shakes it.]
Mean Green: It’s empty. Jake Morgan: THAT’S RIGHT. GO THROW IT AWAY! [Shrugging his shoulders, Green walks off, leaving Morgan to roll his eyes as a clock’s ticking loudly sounds in the background. With the screen going black, the clock face of a logo hurdles through the ebony space before settling into view. The clock face fades into the background, giving way to faces and names that flash by quickly.
Vic Parker - One Man Boy Band, Chris Cortez - The Mean Green Mascot - Mexican superstar, Ieta Fujita - Ricky Starr - The pink clad team of Pretty in Pink,
Lars and Billy Blaze with Raoul - Agent of L.O.V.E., Harry Palmer - Weed - All American Buddy Lee Jr. - the heavenly Angel and finally, the Television Champion, Jake Morgan and an angry Angel Rotten. As the last of the pictures fade, they give way to the Man-to-Man logo hurtling into view, tumbling end over end before coming to a complete stop. With a smashing sound, the logo is obliterated into a million slivers, fading into the camera making a swing through a darkened studio.
The lights are low enough to only see four silhouettes inside the ring when spotlights begin to zoom around showing off several pink frosted christmas trees around the ring and the ring ropes wrapped in silver and gold garland.
“It’s Raining Men” begins to play as lights suddenly burst to life. With a quick flash of the announcers finds Howard Butz and Dillon Diamond in matching pink leather jackets and Santa caps. Butz is visibly upset, while
Diamond is all smiles, even dancing in his seat before the camera moves back to the ring and it’s occupants.
Standing in the center are four members of Pretty in Pink dressed as the Village People. Lars as the cowboy, Raoul as the indian, Billy Blaze as the cop and finally, a large overweight individual in the biker’s outfit. Without warning, the group leaps into their rendition of “YMCA” complete with dance number while Lars and Raoul belt out the tune for the cheering crowd.
Switching to a view of the announcers’ table shows a tale of two people. One, Howard Butz sits with his head buried in his hands while the other, Dillon Diamond is standing and trying to follow along to the dance steps of the men in the ring. The crowd buzzes with excitement and laughter.
The short version draws to a close as Lars approaches the front of the ring, waiting for the noise to die down to address the crowd. “The Pastel
Avenger” is ALL smiles.] Lars: Welcome to what will no doubt turn out to be the most spectacular show in the history of 30 Minute Countdown, because tonight ... I’M IN CHARGE!!!
[Lars does a little spin move.] Lars: TONIGHT ... we have dazzling new decorations ... stunning new outfits ... singing ... dancing ... and if you’re good ... maybe even a little wrestling! THIS IS MAN-TO-MAN! JUST THE WAY I LIKE IT! [The crowd cheers.] Lars: Tonight, you’ll get to see the battle for my phone number. [Lars puts his hand to his mouth, with a surprised look on his face.] Lars: Kidding! The dashing youngster Tommy Trevor ... [Raoul jumps up and down in the background, clapping.] Lars: and that maniacal freak Gideon Frost battle it out for a contract! [Raoul cringes.] Lars: And of course, there will be plenty of other surprises. What you won’t be seeing is Angel, Angel Rotten, Malicea McDeath or anybody like that. This is a show starring the boys, by the boys and for the boys! So sit back and get ready to quiver from all the fun and excitement! Kisses!
[With Lars sending everyone from the ring, the camera returns to Butz and Diamond.] Howard Butz: I can’t believe this. Can you? What a second, why am I asking you? Dillon Diamond: Lars certainly knows how to throw a bash, doesn’t he? And look, we’re being joined by Raoul.
Howard Butz: Oh great. Raoul: Well, Mr. Party Pooper, if you’re not having fun, why don’t you take it to the back?
[Taking off his headset, Butz shakes his head before vacating for the back.] Raoul: Oh, well. Looks like it’s just the two of us, Dill. Dillon Diamond: How did you do that? Raoul: Just that old Raoul charm. I leave them speechless. Now, what do we do? Dillon Diamond: Do? We go to Holly in the back with Vic Parker! [Leaving the ring, the camera moves to the back to find Holly with a smiling Vic Parker.] Holly: Thanks guys, I’m here with Vic Parker. Vic, despite having only a debut victory over the Trashman, you seem intent on drawing the Television champion, Jake Morgan out into a confrontation. Do you think that’s a wise thing ...
Vic Parker: Holly, to some, my words are nothing more than bravado. To others, it’s a signing of my own death warrant but let me fill you in on a little something. There’s a reason why they call me “Unbelievable” ...
[Parker stops as an Tommy Trevor walks through. Wearing a powder blue schoolboy outfit, Trevor looks just a tad upset, drawing a chuckle from both holly and
Parker.] Holly: Excuse me? Do you mind? You’re upsetting my shot. Tommy Trevor: Upset? [Looking at Holly, he grabs the microphone and pulls it to his lips, while she keeps her grip on it, looking scared.]
Tommy Trevor: YOU’RE DAMN RIGHT, I AM UPSET!!! I can’t believe that in this day and age, ANYONE can waltz in and steal the contract of somebody who EARNED it.
Holly: Um ... Gideon Frost? Tommy Trevor: That’s right, Gideon Frost. He practically steals my contract with this organization, then he has the jack to say that he’s a follower of the Lord. Pshaw. His soul may belong to the Lord, but once he steps in that ring, his {BLEEP!} is mine.
[Releasing the mic, Trevor storms off. The camera leaves the back and a bewildered Holly and Parker laughing hysterically.]
Raoul: I think he’s mad but in that Lars Original outfit, I’m sure he’ll get over it. Oh, well ... let’s wrestle! Not the two of us, you big silly!
Those big muscular ... sweaty ... half naked ... brutes ... [Raoul’s eyes glaze over momentarily until snapped back to reality.] Dillon Diamond: Raoul? Raoul? [Inside the ring, Lars instructs Frost back to his corner before checking over Trevor. Forcing the young wrestler to turn around, Lars takes pleasure in patting him down until Trevor gets wise to him and chases him off. Returning to the center of the ring, Lars tears away his chaps, revealing his ultra short, pink shorts before calling for the bell.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** With the bell, the two start circling until Lars steps in, pointing a finger in Frost’s face. Frost shrugs off the tongue lashing, trying to go around him until Lars warns him back, threatening to end the match.
Dillon Diamond: I wonder what’s being said? Raoul: I KNOW! I KNOW! Dillon Diamond: Raoul, you don’t have to raise your hand. Raoul: Frosty isn’t wearing the shirt Lars especially whipped up for him. I have to say, Lars is showing wonderful restraint by allowing this match to continue, despite the slight by Frosty.
Stepping aside, Lars allows them to continue when Trevor shoots for Frost’s legs, dumping him onto the canvas. Into a top mount, he gets ahold of an arm, twisting it. A quick shot to the head allows Frost to pull his opponent over, wrapping his legs around Tommy’s head.
Raoul: Arm twisty ... FROSTY WITH A LEG THINGY! Watching closely, Lars asks for the submission as Trevor fights to break free. Doing his best to get loose, Trevor fights his way to the ropes. Just inches away, his arm falls short. Applying more pressure, Frost has Trevor crying out in pain.
Raoul: Oh, mama ... wrap those legs around me! FACE IN THE CROTCH! FACE IN THE CROTCH! Making it to his knees, Trevor forces Frost to his back for a one count before being able to pull his head free. Back on his feet, both men start circling before locking up in the center of the ring. Out of a collar and elbow tie-up,
Frost works Trevor into a side headlock. Pushing Frost off, Trevor sends him across the ring. A return shoulder butt floors Trevor.
Raoul: That’s one of those move things. Dillon Diamond: Shoulder butt Raoul: You said butt.

— Part 2 of 2 —

Pulling Trevor back to his feet, Frost goes back to the side headlock, wrenching it in tight. After taking an elbow to the stomach, Frost spins beind his opponent, wrapping him up around the waist before slamming him into the mat with a belly-to-back suplex. Bridging, he gets a two count from the ref before an escape.
Charging forward, Frost misses with a clothesline as Trevor ducks it. A sidestep later, Frost eats canvas from a drop toe hold. Working down the body, the young man has Frost’s ankle. Bellowing out in pain, Frost won’t submit.
Raoul: That doesn’t look like fun. You know, Dillon, speaking of fun, wasn’t Lars’ introduction just fun? The outfits, the music and let’s not forget the “Big Unit” making his debut. Wasn’t he fabulous?
Crawling forward, Frost makes the ropes for an escape. A tap on the tush from Lars makes Trevor break the hold. Coming up complaining, Trevor draws a warning from the ref.
Bouncing off the ropes, Trevor rushes forward to deliver a nasty drop kick to the seated Frost’s face. A cover has Lars down on the mat.
Raoul: LARS WITH THE COUNT! ONE! TWO! Oh, and Frosty gets up. Oh well, maybe next time. Doesn’t Lars look simply professional out there?
Dillon Diamond: I have to say, he is doing a good job. Anytime, the ref has little to do, you have a great match.
Raoul: Oh. I was referring to his outfit. Stepping out from the back, Jake Morgan carrying a clipboard makes his way to the ring. His white t-shit has the word “EVALUATOR” across the front.
Pulling a pencil from behind his ear, Morgan watches. Hammering away with forearms, Trevor pulls Frost to his feet before putting him right back down with a dragon leg screw. Working the knee with a spinning toe hold, Trevor celebrates early.
Shaking his head, Morgan writes on the clipboard. Raoul: What do you think Jacob’s here for? Dillon Diamond: It’s hard to say if he’s watching the match or evaluating the referee. Raoul: If it’s Lars, I’m sure Jacob will give him a very high score. Following a heavy stomping, Trevor hits the far ropes, throwing himself back off the middle one to land a moonsault, landing hard across Frost’s pulled up knees. Into a fetal position, Trevor rolls around the mat in pain.
Raoul: I don’t think he meant to do that with his flippity thing. Getting back to his feet, a limping Frost pulls Trevor to his feet, unloading with a barrage of reverse knife hands. Faltering along the ropes, a running knee lift sends Trevor through them and out onto the apron. Using the time to shake out his knee, Frost nails his target with several punches before bringing him back into the ring with a vertical suplex. A cover nets him a two count from Lars.
Whipped into the ropes, Trevor returns to where he started, only this time hosited into the air and dropped with a sidewalk slam. Landing heavily, Frost covers his man.
Raoul: ONE! TWO! THREE! NO ... LARS IS TELLING US HE WAS JUST KIDDING! TOMMY TREVOR KICKED OUT! Pulled to his knees, Trevor lashes out with a low blow that catches Frost square in the family jewels. Swinging his arm back, he takes Frost off his feet with a shot to the back of his knee. Stumbling to his feet, he rolls Frost to his back, grabbing a foot, Trevor jumps into the air, slamming it down into the mat and causing Frost all kinds of pain.
Calling out to the fans, the “schoolboy” gets a swift pat on the backside and a warning from the ref. After slamming the knee into the canvas several more times, he drops down with a leg across the back of Frost’s leg before coming up ready to argue with the ref.
Dillon Diamond: Tommy Trevor and Lars are going at it. They’re nose to nose and chest to chest.
Raoul: DON’T GET LARS MAD! Shoving Lars back, Trevor has a few more words before pulling his shirt open, tearing it off and throwing it down on the mat.
Dillon Diamond: I think he’s complaining about his outfit, Raoul. Raoul: Maybe it was a little too tight but to say those things to Lars. How insensitive. Lars worked so hard on that schoolboy outfit.
His eyes going wide, Lars step forward, striking out at the young wrestler, grabbing for his chest.
RAOUL: TITTY TWISTER! LARS WITH THE TITTY TWISTER! Letting go, Lars storms off across the ring while Trevor cries out in pain. Grabbed from behind, he’s wrapped up in a cobra sleeper. With Trevor flailing away in the center of the ring trapped in Frost’s grasp, Lars heads for the near ropes and some consoling from Raoul.
Dillon Diamond: Tommy Trevor is in all kinds of trouble! Raoul: How can you talk about the match when it’s obvious that Lars has grievously injured. His feelings have been shattered.
Caught in the center of the ring, Trevor’s flailings become less apparent as he slips off into deep slumber from Frost’s hold. A few last arm movements all but disappear under his opponent’s grip and the match apparently over except for one little problem, Lars is on the other side of the ring, crying into the turnbuckle.
Dropping Trevor like a ton of bricks, Frost stalks across the ring, grabbing Lars by the shoulder and spinning him around. An angry look on his face, the newcomer points a finger at the ref until Lars attempts to lick it. Jumping back with a disgusted look on his face, he waves off the winking Lars before heading back to Tommy Trevor, who is trying to pull himself to his feet.
Grabbing him from behind, Frost pushes him forward into the ropes with a forearm to the back. Bouncing back off the ropes, he’s open to a running bulldog. Picked up and lifted into the air, a near lifeless Trevor hits the canvas with a brainbuster suplex.
Picking Trevor up off the canvas, Frost swings him up into the air and onto his shoulders. With Trevor look up into the lights, Frost crosses himself before locking both arms around Trevor’s torso. With a sudden lurch, he drops to his knees, slamming his helpless opponent into the mat.
Raoul: A pick him up into the air, over your shoulder, drop to your knees and slam your opponent into the mat painful one of those whatchamacallits!
Dillon Diamond: Right. On the outside of the ring, Morgan continues his pacing, occassionally writing on his clipboard.
Returning to his feet, Frost limps around the ring several times, shaking out his knee. Returning to the still downed Trevor, he kicks his opponent’s arms to his side before Lars pulls him away.
Dillon Diamond: Frost and Lars now having some words. What’s this all about? Raoul, you don’t have to raise your hand! Raoul: I’ve seen that look in his eye. I think Lars is ready to play doctor. He wants to revive poor little Tommy with some MOUTH-TO-MOUTH! Shoving Lars away, Gideon Frost drops to his knees, then hooking a leg for the pin. Dillon Diamond: THERE’S THE COVER! ONE! TWO! WHAT?!?!? HE STOPPED THE COUNT? WHERE’S LARS GOING? Raoul: OH NO! NOT THE NAIL! Dillon Diamond: The nail? What are you talking about? Raoul: It’s from Lars’ total vanquishing of Pepsi Boy some weeks back when he injured his hand. The hang nail? Don’t you remember, you big silly?
Dillon Diamond: When he left before the match was over? Raoul: Exactly. All that hard pounding on the mat and not giving the nail proper time to heal ... it’s terrible. Horrible. Awful.
Dillon Diamond: I think we get the picture. Raoul: How could you? A man in Lars’ position, where his look is everything ... his reputuation ... his life ... a single horrifiying moment like this could ruin his entire life. Do you hear me, Dillon ... HIS ENTIRE LIFE!
Dillon Diamond: I don’t think a hangnail ... Raoul: Not just a hangnail, Dillon ... LARS’ HANGNAIL! Jumping back to his feet, Frost grabs Lars by the collar. Ready to throttle him, Frost is stopped short by a double axe handle to the back. Spun around, he takes a front kick to the ribs followed by a swinging neck breaker.
Dillon Diamond: Saved by Tommy Trevor. Raoul: Ah, how sweet. Having taken a beating, Trevor comes up holding the back of his head. Throwing himself back off the ropes, he drops Frost with a drop kick to the knee, taking his feet out from under him.
Pulling Frost to his feet, Trevor wraps him up from behind. Pushing him forward into the ropes, Trevor tries to fall backwards by Frost holds on for dear life.
Not letting go of the ring ropes, Frost can’t be pulled off. Stepping in to help, Lars grabs Trevor from behind and starts to pull as well, his pelvis pistoning back and forth, drawing a laugh from the crowd. Letting go, Lars starts counting on Frost, who is looking for an opening to strike, each time ready to throw a back elbow.
With a high kick that would put any Rockette to shame, Lars knocks Frost’s arms free of the ropes. Pulling back, Trevor rolls Frost to the mat. Grabbing a handful of tights, he holds on for dear life.
Dillon Diamond: TREVOR WITH THE PIN! ONE! TWO! THREE! HE HAD THE TIGHTS BUT LARS MISSED IT! Raoul: I think little Tommy was just pinching him. *** Ding *** Ding *** [Making a run to leave the ring, Trevor is cut off by Jake Morgan up on the ring apron. Holding up a hand, Morgan hands the clipboard to Trevor before pulling off his t-shirt, revealing another one underneath. This one in black has white lettering with CHAMPION across the front.
Taking the clipboard back, he motions Trevor closer. Confused, Trevor inches closer ... close enough for Morgan to blast him with his clipboard. Leaving
Trevor laid out on the canvas, Morgan strolls to the back to a round of booing.] Raoul: Well, that wasn’t very nice. [After being chased around the ring by a mad Gideon Frost, Lars calls for security who rush out to restrain him. Dragging him off and a wobbled Tommy
Trevor, a relieved Lars asks for a mic.] Lars: That’s all for our show tonight. I do so hope you enjoyed it! [Taking a bow, Lars finds Billy Blaze dragging Neal the intern out by the ear, followed by the “Big Unit”, still in their Village People gear.]
Billy: Not quite, Lars. We still have a little business left. [Pushing Neal into the ring, they join Lars.] Billy: WE HAVE A LITTLE SPANKING TO DO! [Looking terrified, Neal tries to find an escape route, finding his path blocked by the PiPsters.]
Lars: You got away the first time ... THIS TIME THERE’S NO ESCAPE! Neal: WAIT! I promised you some information in exchange, you wouldn’t spank me ... Lars: With your pants pulled down in front of all these people! So, this had better be good.
[Dashing out from the back, the masked Pepsi Boy slides into the ring for the save with fists raised. Billy holds back the much bigger “Big Unit”.]
Neal: No, no escape. A while back, Lars ... you were preparing the new set for your Cloud 9 show. You were going to spare no expense for these fans. You went all out to make it spectacular.
[Billy and Unit stops Pepsi Boy from inching forward with threats.] Neal: A Lars Event is what it would have been ... only, someone went out of their way to take that all away.I KNOW WHO THAT SOMEONE IS! I know who would do such a cruel thing and destroy your life’s work. I KNOW! And with a promise from you to never spank me again, I will only be more than happy to turn over that tape showing the mastermind and his accomplice at work.
[With a devious look on his face, Neal looks to Lars for an answer.] Dillon Dimaond: This is getting better by the moment. Raoul: SHHHHHH!!!!! I can’t hear. Neal: And as a show of good will on my part, I’m willing to finger his accomplice right now ...
[Looking to his PiPsters for support, Lars nods his head.] Neal: His accomplice ... IS PEPSI BOY! [Lars is shocked as Neal points out the confused, head shaking masked man while Billy and Unit slowly move in closer before the show goes off the air.] *** 30 Minute Countdown© ***
