TV Taping 12

21 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 2 —

[The camera opens on Jake Morgan standing in front of Angel, talking quietly with her in the backstage area. Sliding in closer, the camera allows us to eavesdrop on their conversation.]
Jake Morgan: What now? Angel: Who were those flowers really for? [Jake looks uncomfortable.] Jake Morgan: You got them, didn’t you? [Angel shakes her head, but still seems unconvinced.] Angel: Yeah well, I overheard Neal and Flaxen talking, and Neal said those flowers were for her.
[She looks hurt and upset. A flash of anger passes over Jake’s face at the mention of Neal and Flaxen.]
Jake Morgan: You actually listened to Neal? I thought you hated him? [Grabbing her by the shoulders, he turns her to face him.] Listen, Neal is a moron, who would tell anyone, anything if he thought it would be beneficial to him. Trust me. Now, I have a match to go announce.
[Angel grabs Jake and gives him a big hug. Rolling his eyes, Jake gives her a pat on the back before moving away. Shaking his head, he can be heard muttering.]
Jake Morgan: I'm going to f{BLEEP}ing kill him. [Moving off, the camera finds Lars traipsing around his pink decorated locker room when the door snaps open, startling him. Looking over the large man's shoulder, the camera looks in as the man hands over what appears to be a sign.
Looking down at it then to the hidden man in front of him.] Man: I need your help ... I’ll do anything. [With a sly smile crossing his lips, again Lars is startled as the door again flies open and in steps an angered Ricky Starr. Looking at the man in front of
Lars, Starr brushes past him before stepping up to the “Pastel Avenger”. Jabbing a finger into Lars’ chest, Starr gets his message across.] Ricky Starr: I WANT YOU!!!!! Lars: Oh my ... [With a glassy eyed look, Lars drops his sign to the floor. As Starr storms off, the camera zooms in on the sign ... “JAKE MORGAN WEIGHT LOSS CENTER”.
Leaving them, the camera finds a limping Shorti Blaze with her brother, Billy Blaze walking through the backstage area.] Billy Blaze: What's the matter? Shorti Blaze: It's nothing. I'll be ready for whatever happens tonight ... [Grimacing in pain, she stops when the blue and gold clad Flaxen saunters into view, a big smile on her face as she makes eye contact with Billy, drawing his gaze. Cocking her head, Flaxen steps up to Billy with an overly suggestive walk. Smiling, she leans in close, causing the Young Gun champ to blush as she whispers into his ear. A deep shade of red crosses his face as she steps back.]
Billy Blaze: Meet you in the ring? Tonight? You bet!!! [Walking off, Flaxen stops in her tracks, turning back to Billy and the eye rolling Shorti. With a slight bend, she blows a kiss at Billy before walking off. Shaking her head, Shorti limps off when a clock’s ticking loudly sounds in the background. With the screen going black, the clock face of a logo hurtles through the ebony space before settling into view. With the clock face fading into the background, faces and names flash by quickly.
The Mean Green Mascot - Mexican superstar, Ieta Fujita - Ricky Starr - The pink clad team of Pretty in Pink, Lars and Billy Blaze with Raoul - Agent of
L.O.V.E., Harry Palmer - Weed - All American Buddy Lee Jr. - and finally, the Television Champion, Jake Morgan and an angry Angel Rotten. As the last of the pictures fade, the camera makes a swing through a small but packed television studio before focussing in on two men sitting at the ringside announcers’ table. The two men are a contrast in looks, one in a sports coat while the other one is in a Hawaiian shirt, leather pants and a matching hat.]
Howard Butz: We've got alot to get to this week, Dillon, so let’s get straight to business. Next week ...
Dillon Diamond: Oh, yeah ... next week. Halloween just around the corner and 30 Minute Countdown puts on it’s second Double Trouble taping in Faces of Fear. The first taping was full of surprises, what this one will bring is anyone’s guess but I’ll go on record saying this will be just as good if not better.
And ... you better believe that Jake Morgan will be a big part of it. Howard Butz: Here we go again. Listen folks, don’t go away. WE’LL BE RIGHT BACK! *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Commercials, commercials, commercials - all in the name of “free television”. We endure this crap just so we don’t have to pay for our programming but is it really free? Not really because when you think about it, every hour long block of television is spliced with twenty to nearly twenty five minutes of commercials. And to make matters worse, for our “free television”, we’re dishing out anywhere from thirty to seventy-five bucks a month to the cable companies just to have a few worthwhile channels on top of tons of crap channels that no one watches.
On a positive note ... drink Dr. Pepper! It makes the world taste better! Nothing like being hooked! *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** [Returning from the break finds Butz and Diamond arguing.] Dillon Diamond: If I were you, I’d be careful what I said about Jake Morgan. You know, he told me that you were near the top of his list ... Howard Butz: He did not. There’s no list ... Dillon Diamond: You heard what he said this past week. I’d think it’s a safe bet that he put you as well as a few others on notice.
Howard Butz: On notice? Oh, please. Dillon Diamond: I’m just telling you what I heard. Howard Butz: What did he tell you? Dillon Diamond: Oh, now you want to know ... all I have to say is that I’d really watch what I say about Jake Morgan ...
[Butz rolls his eyes as a loud round of booing sounds from the small studio audience crowd.]
Howard Butz: What’s Jake going to do? Beat me up? [Diamond smirks as the target of the booing steps up behind Butz, towering over him ... the Television champ, Jake Morgan.]
Dillon Diamond: Maybe you should ask him? [Seeing the shadow cast from behind him, Butz gulps deeply as a pair of hands rest on his shoulder.]
Jake Morgan: Want to ask me something, Butz? Howard Butz: Nope, sure don’t Jake. [Shaking his head, Morgan sits down between the pair.] Dillon Diamond: Great to see you as always, Jake. Howard Butz: To what do we owe this honor of your presence? Jake Morgan: It’s important for everyone to see THEIR champion ... that’s your champion, too, Butz. And don’t you forget it.
Howard Butz: Don’t you worry about that, Jake ... we know all too well. Maybe a little too well.
Jake Morgan: You’re not getting smart with me, are you Butz? Howard Butz: Perish the thought. Looks like we'll have to take up this interesting conversation at a later date, Jake. We’ve got a match to get to
... and here comes Ricky Starr ... [Stepping out from the back, Starr makes it halfway to the ring to a loud round of cheering when “Brown Sugar” starts to blare over the PA system. Looking upset, Starr enters the ring with a flip over the ropes.]
Howard Butz: Speaking of Ricky, rumor has it that you won’t give him a title shot. Jake Morgan: Easy question, even easier answer. This kid is good but there’s no way that he deserves a shot at a veteran like myself. You want to get to me, there’s a long bumpy road you have to go through to get there. Am I right,
Dillon? Dillon Diamond: Oh, absolutely, Jake. Absolutely. You don't want the kid to get hurt. Jake Morgan: Don't get me wrong, he’s good but he couldn’t even beat Billy Blaze and if you can't beat Blaze, you’re certainly don’t belong in the ring with me. Howard Butz: Speaking of Billy, here comes his partner, Lars accompanied to the ring with his faithful companion, Raoul.
[Entering the ring, Lars waves to Starr across from him. A disgusted wave off is all he gets in return. After being checked over by Robin, Lars and Raoul share a hug before the bell is rung.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** With Starr coming across the ring, Lars blows a kiss towards his adversary. With a look of disgust, Starr picks up the pace, charging in after him, missing a double axehandle as Lars dips out of the way. With Starr’s back to him,
Lars gives him a swift pat on the backside and a little wave as Starr complains to the referee.
Jake Morgan: And this is a guy you want me to face? Please. Howard Butz: All I’m asking is why you’re ducking ... Jake Morgan: Ducking? Whenever Ricky Starr makes his way to the Number One contender spot, I’ll be more than happy to face him.
Howard Butz: You’ve faced others that weren't in that spot. What gives? Jake Morgan: Sometimes, you need to make an example of people, Butz ... and I’m starting to feel one of those times coming on. Doing a dance across the ring from Starr, Lars shakes his booty, further incensing Starr. Grabbing the retreating Lars, Starr sends him across the ring.
Bouncing off the ropes, Lars is smashed into the canvas with a drop toe hold. Pulling Lars to his feet, Starr hops up onto the ropes. Walking across the top rope, he dives off, throwing Lars over with a diving arm drag.
Howard Butz: Starr with a leg. He’s got a spinning toe hold, trying to ground Lars. With Lars crying out in pain, Starr spins several times, wrapping the leg around his own. Unentangling himself, Starr sits down into a leg drop. Going into a quick handstand, Starr drops down with a knee to the thigh.
Jake Morgan: Smart wrestling here by Starr but just not good enough. Bouncing to his feet, Starr heads to the near ropes, eyeballing the seated Morgan, who waves him off. Jake Morgan: This is exactly my point. Starr’s more worried about me than focusing on the task at hand and that’s the guy in the ring with him. Beat that guy first, take care of business, then come looking for the next guy.
Dillon Diamond: I have to agree with you there, Jake. Returning to Lars, Starr rolls him over before grabbing his leg by the ankle. Lifting it high into the air, he slams the knee down into the mat bringing a pained cry from his foe.
Howard Butz: Of course, you do. Dillon Diamond: Hey, he’s the champion. With Lars limping away, Starr takes him down with a drop kick to the injured leg. Dragging Lars to the center of the ring, Starr pulls him up by the hair, threatening him with a closed fist. With Lars pleading, Robin steps in with a count for the closed fist.
Howard Butz: Ricky Starr continues to work over Lars’ leg. Jake Morgan: That wouldn’t be happening if I was the official in there ... Howard Butz: What should she be doing? Jake Morgan: You have to come right out and establish control. You can’t let either man take advantage of the situation.
Paying attention to the ref, Starr is open for a low blow. With his opponent doubled over in pain, Lars draws a warning from Robin.

— Part 2 of 2 —

Howard Butz: I suppose you’d do something about that?
Jake Morgan: About what? Dillon Diamond: Why don’t you stop picking on Jake, Howard. Howard Butz: I’m not picking on him, just asking questions. After a bodyslam, Lars dances before dropping an elbow and covering Starr for a two count. Pushing Starr back into the corner, Lars opens up with his reverse
“limphands” before turning to an admiring Raoul on the outside and waving. Not paying attention, Lars hits air as Starr sidesteps the blow out of the corner. Grabbing Lars by the head and pushing him into the corner, Starr let’s loose with shots to the chest of his own. With each blow, Lars let’s out a loud gasp.
Jake Morgan: I still don’t see why you’re in such a hurry to see me destroy this kid. Whipping Lars across the ring, Starr takes him down with a high landing spinning heel kick. Kicking the flattened Lars’ arms to his side, Starr hits the middle rope and comes flying off with a moonsault. Hooking a leg, he makes the cover but is pushed off just before the three count. Frustrated, Starr pushes Lars into a corner.
Howard Butz: Ricky Starr going up the ropes. There’s a shoulder mount ... HURRICANRANA ... NO, LARS WITH THE POWERBOMB! In pain, Lars crawls away towards Raoul for support, leaving Starr out on his back. Jake Morgan: See? High risk moves are just that. You wear your opponent down first, then you bring it.
Dillon Diamond: That’s exactly what I was going to say, Jake. Howard Butz: Stereo morons ... Dillon Diamond and Jake Morgan: WHAT?!?!? Howard Butz: Nothing. Robin starting a count on both wrestlers. With support from Raoul and cheers from the fans, Lars pulls himself up to his feet using the ropes. Leaning back against them, he throws the back of his hand to his forehead, looking ready to faint.
Howard Butz: Raoul has been joined by Shorti in Lars’ corner. Looks like she’s trying to pull Raoul away from the ring?
With Lars staggering towards Starr, Shorti frantically tugs at Raoul, trying to bring him with her.
Howard Butz: Lars with the cover. ONE! TWO! THR ... NO! STARR GOT THE SHOULDER UP! Pulling Starr to his feet, Lars sends him across the ring, sending him flying over with a back body drop on the return. Landing hard on his back, Starr is in for more as Lars sits back, dropping down for a face smothering pin.
Jake Morgan: I think this one is over. There’s a number one contender for you. Dropping to the mat, Robin slaps out a count until Starr barely gets a shoulder up. Breathing hard, Lars pulls Starr back up before lifting him onto his shoulders. Hearing a screeching Raoul, Lars puts Starr down.
Howard Butz: What’s going on here? Jake Morgan: Just another case of a wrestler not taking care of business first. I don’t care who it is, I finish the match then bother with anything else. Being dragged away from the ring by Shorti, Raoul cries out for Lars. With pleading eyes, Raoul draws Lars to the ropes which is as far as he gets when he is pulled to the mat by Starr with a simple schoolboy rollup. Lars leg kick through the air trying to break free as Robin slaps out a count.
Howard Butz: STARR WITH THE PIN! ONE! TWO! THREE! RICKY STARR HAS THE WIN! RICKY STARR PULLS OUT THE VICTORY! *** Ding *** Ding *** Jake Morgan: And ... I’m supposed to be impressed? I think this match answered all your questions about Ricky Starr getting his title shot.
Howard Butz: And that answer is what? Jake Morgan: You just keep on pushing, don’t you Butz? Howard Butz: Not me, Jake, but right now, we have to break for a commercial! *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Kids love them! Adults love them! You love them. Everyone loves them! Those candy coated shells, Those marvelous peanuts, rice krispies, almonds and peanut butter they’re now stuffing them with. Even the plain chocolate ones! Don’t forget those terrific, wonderfully colored candy coated shells and you have the perfect candy treat! M&M’s can’t be beat! Buy some today!
BEER. It’s good. In fact, you should be drinking one right now. If you were, you might be able to get fine chicks like those in our commercials instead of sitting on your ass watching wrestling at one in the morning on a Saturday. So drink beer, because if you drink enough, anyone or anything can look really good. BEER. Remember, its cheaper than crack!
***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** [Returning from the break, the camera finds a disinterested Newton sitting at his desk stiffling a yawn before shooting a look to his nearby bodyguard,
Brody. Swinging around, the camera focuses in on Buddy Lee Jr. with a clipboard in hand, flanked by Freak E, Weed, Harry Palmer and Orly Diaz, with a neck brace.]
Buddy Lee Jr.: I don’t think you understand ... I have a signed petition here from most of the guys and girls in the back ... JAKE MORGAN MUST GO!!!!!
[Sitting up in his chair, Newton picks up a pencil and taps on it his desk blotter before looking at the men in front of him.]
Newton: Let me get this straight ... You want me to relieve Morgan of his duties as a referee?
[With confirming nods, Newton drops the pencil, sitting back in his chair. Glancing to his right, Newton looks at Brody.] Brody: Boss, I think ... [Shaking his head, Newton holds up a finger, cutting Brody off.] Newton: Brody, I don’t pay you to think ... and as for the rest of you, Morgan stays right where he is ... [Grinning from ear to ear, Newton sits back in his chair as the rest in the room grumble before moving on to find Bad Boy Corey and Kickin’ Kenny practice a little dancestep in the hallway.]
Bad Boy Corey: We need to find Flaxen and work some dancin’ magic with her. [Singing together off-key, the pair stop as the Trashman walks in. Holding a Singapore cane, he levels it at the pair.] Trashman: A couple of weeks back, you left me alone in the ring to get doubleteamed by Lars and Billy Blaze after I bailed you two out. Well, guess what, girls ... next week at Faces of Fear, YOUR ASSES ARE MINE!!!!!
[With eyes wide with surprise, both 69’ers gulp hard before the camera fades back into ringside and Howard Butz and Dillon Diamond.]
Howard Butz: Looks like a challenge has been issued for next week’s Double Trouble taping ... Faces of Fear. The Trashman looking for a little payback on both members of 69 Degrees. Do you think he’ll be going it alone, Dillon?
Dillon Diamond: I’d say by the look in his eye ... either alone or with a partner is just fine by Trash ... just as long as he gets Corey and Kenny in the ring.
Howard Butz: I’d have to agree with you there ... it looks like were ready for our next ... I really don’t know what to call it, Dillon. Flaxen asked
Billy Blaze to join here in the ring earlier tonight but it didn’t look like it was for a match.
Dillon Diamond: Beats me, too but we have Jake in the ring as well ... [With Flaxen sauntering around the ring to a healthy cheer, Jake in jeans and a buttoned down watches from a corner until she swings around to him. Running her hand down his chest, she suddenly tears the shirt away, revealing a sleeveless referee’s shirt underneath. With a smile on his face, Jake flexes a bicep when a loud cheer brings him back to reality as the Young Gun Champion, Billy
Blaze steps out from the back alone.] Howard Butz: I guess we are going to have a match? [With his title belt across his shoulder, Billy circles the ring slapping hands before entering the ring with a big grin on his face. With a few whistles, he waves at the audience while Flaxen asks for a mic. Handed one by Diamond, she takes a stroll before stopping several feet from Billy.]
Flaxen: In the few short weeks I’ve been here ... I’ve noticed something. I’ve noticed something these fans have known about for quite awhile now. Billy ... I’ve noticed you. [Pointing a finger at Billy, she slides closer to him before putting a digit to his chest.]
Flaxen: I’ve noticed your ruggedly handsome boyish looks. I’ve noticed your youth and enthusiasm inside the ring ...
[With a smile, she looks back at the whistling audience before turning back to a blushing Billy.]
Flaxen: All that talent with your high speed aerial assault ... you are a complete package ... you make this kitty purr ...
[She stops as the whistles and catcalls turn into a chant of Billy’s name, leaving a grinning Billy laughing.]
Flaxen: Throw that complete package together ... the body ... the looks ... the talent ... it adds up to one thing ...
Howard Butz: Where is this going? Dillon Diamond: Who cares, Howard ... just shut up. I can’t hear Flaxen! [Stepping closer, she runs her hand up his chest and across the faceplate of the belt resting comfortably on his shoulder.]
Flaxen: You’ve got this ... [Billy nods his head up and down with a big grin on his face when the bell rings.] *** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: WHAT IS THIS?!?!? Looking confused, Billy turns around to Jake looking for an answer. With Jake shrugging his shoulders, Billy is hit from behind by a scissor kick that knocks him to his knees.
Howard Butz: IT’S A SET UP! BILLY BLAZE HAS BEEN SET UP! Pulling himself to his feet, Billy holds the back of his head from the blow. Looking for his attacker, he finds her waiting for him on the top turnbuckle. Spinning away from him, she launches herself from the ropes, slamming into Billy with a moonsault body press. Knocking him to the mat, she hooks a leg. Howard Butz: I CAN’T BELIEVE THIS! Dropping quickly, Morgan slaps out a count fast and furiously. Dillon Diamond: ONE! TWO! THREE! WE HAVE A NEW YOUNG GUN CHAMPION! Calling for the bell, Morgan makes a beeline for the belt. *** Ding *** Ding *** [Amid a chorus of boos, Morgan hands her the belt when Corey and Kenny of 69 Degrees rush out to the ring. With them entering, Flaxen quickly leaves. Hot on her heels and ready to celebrate, both men are taken off their feet by a Morgan double clothesline.
Leaving them flat on their backs, Morgan finds Lars, Raoul and Shorti making their way to the ring. Leaving himself, Morgan leads Flaxen towards the back.]
Howard Butz: I JUST CAN’T BELIEVE THIS! [With a confused look on his face, Lars cradles Billy’s head in his lap while looking back at the retreating Morgan and Flaxen while Raoul and Shorti nervously look on as the credits roll, bringing the show to an end.]
