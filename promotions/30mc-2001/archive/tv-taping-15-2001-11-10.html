TV Taping 15

10 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 2 —

[The camera opens on 30MC newcomer Angel making her way through the backstage area. Rounding a corner, she suddenly stops in her tracks when confronted by the bigger Brody, bodyguard of the president, Newton. After last week’s boot to the face by Brody, Angel takes a step back. Seeing the concern etched upon her face, Brody puts his hands up.]
Brody: Wait a second ... I only wanted to apologize to you for what happened last weekend. That kick wasn’t meant for you ...
[Rubbing her cheek, Angel nods her head.] Angel: I know. Brody: ... and if there’s any way I can make it up to you, just ask. I’m really, really sorry ...
[Brody suddenly turns around at the female laughter behind him to find Flaxen with the Trashman.]
Flaxen: Now, isn’t this special? Look at the two lovebirds kissing and making up. [Flaxen and Trash share a laugh.] Flaxen: Once you’re all finished, maybe the two of you will join the two of us inside the ring? You and I have a little unfinished business to take care of, Angel.
[With his arms crossed, Trash nods his head in agreement as Flaxen points at Angel before the camera is on the move. Moving on, the camera settles in on a pink clad Lars in a one piece jumpsuit and matching headband. Working up a sweat with calisthenics that would make Jack Lalane envious, Lars stops.]
Lars: That's it ... kick those legs ... kick ... kick. Oh, you'd make a wonderful chorus girl. Okay, now touch your toes. One ... two ... touch your knees ... three ... four ... touch the floor. And again ...
[Waving a finger, Lars counts along silently.] Lars: Again ... [Turning away, Lars looks to companion Raoul, dressed in an identical fashion, only not sweaty.]
Lars: Oh, Raoul ... just imagine what we could have done with Jacob! Raoul: I know ... [Startled by the door opening, Raoul lets out a shreik as a 30MC employee sticks his head into the room.]
Employee: Excuse me, Lars ... Newton wanted me to drop this off with you. He wants you to work the main event tonight.
[Tossing what looks like a dark shirt to Lars, he ducks out of the room. Opening it up with his back to the camera, Lars shakes his head.] Lars: Quickly, Raoul ... we must fix this! [Leaving them, the camera finds Shorti Blaze making her way through the back carrying a small duffel back. With a slight limp, she quickly moves through until hearing the sound of a familiar voice.]
Voice: SHORTI! SHORTI! WAIT UP! [Turning around, she is approached by brother, Billy.] Billy: Wait up. Where are you going? We’re back here. [With Billy grabbing for her bag, she pulls it away from him.] Shorti: Yeah, yeah ... I'll be right along. I've got some ... stuff ... to do. [Scratching his head, Billy gets a confused look on his face.] Billy: What kind of stuff? Shorti: Stuff. Just stuff ... uhm, womanly stuff. [With a look of disgust, Billy backs away.] Billy: Uhm ... sure. See you later. [Shaking her head, she starts once again on her way when she is startled.] Voice: Psssst ... psssst! [Looking for the owner of the voice, she sees Neal the intern poke his head out of a closet.]
Shorti: NEAL! [Shorti’s eyes light up as a nervous Neal looks to see if the coast is clear.] Neal: Shhhhh ... not so loud, your brother might be looking for me. [With Neal nervously looking around, the camera fades to black as a clock’s ticking loudly sounds in the background. With the screen going black, the clock face of a logo hurtles through the ebony space before settling into view. The clock face fades into the background, giving way to faces and names that flash by quickly.
The Mean Green Mascot - Mexican superstar, Ieta Fujita - Ricky Starr - The pink clad team of Pretty in Pink, Lars and Billy Blaze with Raoul - Agent of
L.O.V.E., Harry Palmer - Weed - All American Buddy Lee Jr. - the heavenly Angel and finally, the Television Champion, Jake Morgan and an angry Angel Rotten.
As the last of the pictures fade, the camera makes a swing through a small but packed television studio before focusing in on two men sitting at the ringside announcers’ table. The two men are a contrast in looks, one in a sports coat while the other one is in a Hawaiian shirt, leather pants and a matching hat.]
Dillon Diamond: WAIT! Allow me ... Howard Butz: Okay. Dillon Diamond: WELCOME TO 30 MINUTE COUNTDOWN! Howard Butz: Why the excitement? Dillon Diamond: You have to ask? We’ve got an intergender tag match with Young Gun champion, Flaxen with the Trashman taking on Angel and a partner, she’s not naming.
Howard Butz: Okay. Dillon Diamond: Then, in the main event, you have Jake Morgan putting his title on the line against Pepsi Boy. We haven’t seen too much of Jake in the ring over the past few weeks so this should be a real treat.
Howard Butz: You know, DIllon, I was thinking just the opposite. We’ve seen too much of Jake lately but before we go any further, we have a few bills to pay, so, don’t go away!
[Butz and Diamond fade from view arguing into a commercial.] ***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** The Ultimate Cheeseburger ... $1.99 of cholesteral. Football. The Carnivores. Cheerleaders. Referees. Dinosaur mascot. Blimps landing on kick returners. Funny commercial. Terrible burger. Jack in the Box. Good food ... except for their burgers.
Mach 3 razors. Ever seen the handsome, shirtless guy walk into his bathroom ready to shave off the three minutes of facial growth? Lather up and suddenly start flicking his Mach 3 across face making wide swaths through the shaving cream, then making these sideways motions across his chin. Right. If these things actually had blades in them, the guy's face would be a bloody mess. Take it from someone with facial hair unlike these guys.
***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** [Returning from the break, the camera finds Angel in all white, jeans, t-shirt with a golden halo and white shoes, climbing the ropes. After closing her eyes and looking skyward, she jumps down from her perch, heading to Brody standing in the center of the ring. Taking a mic from him, she turns back towards the small but boisterous crowd.]
Angel: All you good people ... you ... [Leaning against the ropes, she points out to fans on the other side of the protective barricade.]
Angel: ... and you ... and you ... all of you. You all deserve better and tonight, with the help of my big, strong guardian angel ...
[With a quick smile and a nod of the head, she points to Brody.] Angel: ... we are going to make this place better for all of us. [After tossing the mic to Butz, she turns towards the back, awaiting their opponents. A big cheer goes up when Billy Blaze of PiP steps out and not
Flaxen. Making his way around the ring, Billy slaps hands with the fans before sliding in under the ropes. Picking up the mic from Butz, he approaches Brody and Angel.]
Billy Blaze: I’m not here to crash anyone’s party but I’d really like you to reconsider your choice tonight and take me on as your partner. You see,
I’ve got a little more interest in both of the people you’re fighting tonight ... and well, I might not be as big as him but I have a bigger bone to pick with Flaxen and the Trashman.
[Waiting for an answer, Billy grins as the fans chant his name in the background.] Howard Butz: What a big decision to have to make. Do you take Billy Blaze or do you go with the bigger Brody?
Dillon Diamond: She’s new here and already the guys are fighting to team with her. Howard Butz: I think Billy’s interest is deeper than that and for Brody, I think he’s trying to make up for hitting her last week ...
Dillon Diamond: SHE BETTER DECIDE QUICKLY BECAUSE HERE COMES FLAXEN AND THE TRASHMAN! [Charging out from the back, Trash hits the ring and comes up swinging his wooden paddle, slamming it over Brody’s head and knocking him to the canvas.
Grinning over his handiwork, Trash turns right into a stiff clothesline that carries him and Billy to the outside of the ring.]
Howard Butz: BRODY’S DOWN AND BILLY’S TAKING IT TO TRASH! Dillon Diamond: And inside the ring, we have the ladies in a heated discussion. I wonder what they’re talking about. Howard Butz: You better believe it’s better homes and gardens. [In the middle of the ring, the ladies badmouth each other with things heating up as Flaxen jabs a finger into Angel’s chest to get her point across.
Laughing, she points down at Angel’s partner Brody on the canvas, trying to get it together following the sneak attack.
Taking a step back, a smiling Angel points to the belt around Flaxen’s waist. With Angel continuing to talk, Flaxen’s face lights up in surprise as she points to her belt. Reluctant at first, Flaxen nods her head as they are joined by referee Robin inside the ring.]
Howard Butz: I don’t know what was said but Angel seems awfully happy. [Turning away to help Brody to his feet, Angel is yanked by her hair to the mat. With the start of the action, Robin calls for the bell.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Taking her title belt off, Flaxen waits for Angel to get back to her feet before slamming the metallic plate into her opponent’s face. With a stern warning from the ref, Flaxen drops it into her corner.
Howard Butz: Flaxen taunting the downed Angel ... OH! KICK TO THE RIBS! Kick her while she’s down, why don’t you?
Dillon Diamond: And on the outside, Billy letting Trashman get up close and personal with the ring barrier.
Moving away because of the ref, Flaxen boots Brody one for good measure. Strutting seductivly across the ring, she pulls Angel back to her feet by the hair. WIth an Irish whip, she smacks a near helpless Angel into the corner turnbuckle.
Howard Butz: This does not look good for Angel right from the get go. Dillon Diamond: I was thinking the very same thing but I guess Flaxen’s the Young Guns champion for a reason and she’s making the most of her opportunity. Speaking of opportunities, Billy with a legdrop off of the ring apron.
Charging across the ring, Flaxen builds up a good head of steam before launching herself into the air for a corner splash. Coming down, she lands exactly where Angel WAS. With no Angel to break her fall, Flaxen slams hard into the turnbuckle chestfirst.
Howard Butz: That had to hurt. Angel opening up with chops on the champion and the fans are counting right along.
One, two, three ... each blow lands, the last blow hitting harder and popping more than the previous one. Grabbing Flaxen’s arm, Angel rips her out of the corner, driving a knee into her midsection. Doubled over in pain, Flaxen has a leg wrapped around her shoulder and is rolled to the mat.
Howard Butz: ANGEL WITH THE PIN! ONE! TWO! THR ... NO! Back on her feet, Angel sends Flax across the ring, into the far ropes. On the return, Flaxen goes right under a leap frog. Hitting the ropes once again,
Flaxen shoots right back for a deep arm drag. Howard Butz: Angel with the arm bar pulling a dazed Flaxen to her feet. What was that about making the most of an opportunity?
Dillon Diamond: OUCH! I was talking about opportunities like that ... Trashman meet or should I say eat Brody’s boot?
Twisting on the arm, Angel kicks out Flaxen’s legs putting the leggy blonde on her backside. Not willing stay down, Flaxen kips to her feet. Grabbed by the arm, she is sent across the ring again, this time returning to a leapfrogging
Angel just as before, only this time, the Heavensent one doesn’t get high enough, instead landing on Flaxen’s shoulders. Spinning around to face the opposite direction, Angel is sent into the ropes and out onto the ring apron by
Flaxen’s momentum. Howard Butz: Lucky for Angel, she caught the ropes or that could have been a very bad bump.
Dillon Diamond: You can say that again, Howard. If you want to see bad, you only have to look on the outside of the ring as Billy and Brody work over
Flaxen’s partner. Holding onto the ropes and kicking her feet back, Angel shoots off of the apron and back into the ring, catching a winded Flaxen around the head with her legs.
Pushing off from the ropes which she used to break her fall, Angel spins her body away towards the center of the ring, taking Flax with her.
Howard Butz: SPINNING HEAD SCISSORS! Slamming down onto the mat, Flaxen wobbles as she pulls herself back to her feet. Howard Butz: Flaxen looks dazed. That really seemed to take the wind out of the champion’s sails. Angel climbing the ropes ...
Dillon Diamond: LOOK OUT FLAXEN! Howard Butz: FLYING SOMERSAULT NECK BREAKER! ANGEL HOOKS THE LEG! ONE! TWO! THREE! ANGEL WINS! ANGEL WINS! *** Ding *** Ding ***

— Part 2 of 2 —

[Jumping to her feet, an exhuberant Angel hits the corner ropes to celebrate
with the cheering fans. Coming back down, she has her arm raised by Robin before being handed the Young Gun belt. Her face lighting up, Angel thrusts the belt into the air.]
Howard Butz: Referee Robin awarding the title belt to the victorious Angel? What is going on here? Dillon Diamond: That must have been what they were talking about before the match. Angel wanted a shot at the belt and Flaxen granted it to her.
Howard Butz: Don’t go away fans, we’ll be right back! [Leaving the ring, the camera makes its way into the back to find a pacing Ricky Starr and Buddy Lee Jr.] Ricky Starr: I don’t understand this ... [Grabbing Starr by the shoulders, Buddy stops his pacing.] Ricky Starr: I can’t get a match and you ... you’re the number one contender ... Buddy Lee Jr.: Don’t worry, Ricky. Everything will work out. That’s just the nature of the business ...
[He stops as Television champion, Jake Morgan walks by. Stopping, Morgan chuckles as he points at the pair.]
Jake Morgan: Mr. Fairplay and the Wannabe ... [Grabbing Starr, Buddy Lee stops him from attacking Morgan.] Buddy Lee Jr.: You’re time’s coming, Morgan. Jake Morgan: Yeah, yeah ... been there, done that. [Wheeling around, Morgan’s smile narrows into a glare as Angel Rotten walks into view.] Jake Morgan: I might have know you’d be here with these two. [Storming off, he leaves a confused Rotten wondering what she did wrong.] *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Commercials, commercials, commercials - all in the name of “free television”. We endure this crap just so we don’t have to pay for our programming but is it really free? Not really because when you think about it, every hour long block of television is spliced with twenty to nearly twenty five minutes of commercials. And to make matters worse, for our “free television”, we’re dishing out anywhere from thirty to seventy-five bucks a month to the cable companies just to have a few worthwhile channels on top of tons of crap channels that no one watches.
You know it, I know it ... Dr. Pepper makes the world taste better! The only problem is the rest of the country doesn't know it. Don't believe me? Just ask for it in Milwaukee and see what they give you ... “We got root beer.”
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** [Returning from the break, the camera zooms in on Butz and Diamond.] Dillon Diamond: AH HA! I KNEW IT! Angel Rotten IS conspiring against Jake. I should have known.
Howard Butz: What are you talking about? It’s purely coincidence. Alot of people walk through the backstage area.
Dillon Diamond: Butz, you are so gullible. Howard Butz: Oh, please. Look, saved by Jake Morgan ... [Leaving them, the camera focuses in on Morgan stepping into the ring with his title belt over his shoulder aned a mic in hand.]
Jake Morgan: Let’s make this short and sweet ... bring me Pepsi Boy! [The fans start up as Newton steps out from the back followed by Brody, nursing a head injury with a bag of ice at the back of his head. Seeing the boss,
Morgan rolls his eyes.] Newton: I’ll keep this equally short. It’s been awhile since you’ve defended your title on a weekly basis, so I’d like to make this special for you. Tonight, I’m going to double the viewing pleasure as well as double your fun. You will be defending your title against Pepsi Boy ... as well as Brody!
[With a look of surprise, Brody spins Newton around and starts pointing to his head before snatching away the mic.]
Brody: No way, I just took a shot to the head in the last match. I’m not up for it, why not get Buddy Lee Jr. or Ricky Starr or ...
Jake Morgan: Looks like your BOY isn’t up for it, Newt. That’s too bad, I’d love kicking his ass AGAIN but if a little lovetap is going to keep him out of action ...
[Brody pushes Newton aside before stepping forward. Taking back the mic, Newton laughs.] Newton: And to keep it all fair and such, Lars is going to be your referee! [The fans start howling as Lars in ultra tight pink hot pants and boots with a referee’s shirt tied high on his stomach bounces out of the back followed by
Raoul. With Lars slithering into the ring, Raoul has a seat ringside.] *** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: We’re underway despite missing one of the combatants and for the second week in a row, we’ve been joined by Raoul. Once again, it’s a pleasure ...
Raoul: Quite. Stepping inbetween the two men, Lars is sandwiched as they go nose to nose. Wiggling just a little too much, Lars goes glassy-eyed for a moment before they heed his call and step back.
Circling, the pair ball their fists looking for the right opening to strike. Taking control, Lars slaps at their hands, warning them of punching. Locking up, Morgan wins control, twisting the arm into an overhead wristlock. Pulling down sharply, Jake tries to dislocate the arm.
Howard Butz: Jake putting pressure on the wrist ... Brody with the ropes and the ref is calling for a break.
Raoul: HEAD referee. Say it. Howard Butz: HEAD referee? Raoul: Again. Dillon Diamond: HEAD referee. Raoul: I love that. Howard Butz: Can we get back to the match now? Dillon Diamond: He said ... Howard Butz: I don’t care what he said. Jake pushing Brody into the corner. Reverse knife hands. The ref calling for a break. Raoul: HEAD REFEREE. Glaring at Lars and sending him off with a squeal, Jake takes a right to the head. Backed up, Jake takes several more before being grabbed. With a counter,
Jake grabs an arm and pulls Brody into a short arm clothesline, putting him down hard. Howard Butz: Brody hit his head hard. Raoul: You said hard. Using the ropes to pull himself up, Brody takes several shots to the back of his head along the ropes before being grabbed by the hair and having his face dragged along the top rope.
Howard Butz: Morgan going for the eyes. There’s a body slam and an elbow drop. Jake with a nonchalant cover. ONE! Brody with a shoulder up.
Pulling Brody to his feet, Morgan sends him across the ring. Dipping down, Morgan waits for the return instead taking a kick to the head that straightens him up. Rushing forward, Brody lands a flying shoulder, knocking both men down.
Howard Butz: HERE COMES PEPSI BOY! With Morgan flat on his back, Brody struggles to a knee, his eyes still bothering him. Slipping between the ropes, Pepsi Boy runs in but is cut off by
Lars. Lifting the masked man off his feet, Lars easily deposits him over the ropes in his corner.
Dillon Diamond: WOW! That’s power. Did you see how easily Lars returned Pepsi Boy to his corner? Without giving anything away, can you give us any insight into Lars’ training regimen?
Raoul: There’s alot of hard ... work. Long hours of grueling, strenuous, sweaty ... Raoul stops, getting that faroff look in his eye. Even Diamond waving a hand in his face doesn’t snap him out of it.
Dillon Diamond: Raoul? Raoul? Raoul: But don’t let those tight gluts fool you. Lars likes to cheat every once in awhile. I’ve caught him snacking on a big, hot, juicy Johnson ...
Howard Butz: WHAT?!?!? WAIT! DON’T ANSWER THAT! Raoul: What? All I was going to say that Lars likes a big Johnson ... Howard Butz: I know what you’re going to say. Inside the ring, Brody makes a tag to Pepsi Boy who comes in with a slingshot leg drop. Rolling back to his feet, he hops on then off of Jake’s chest.
Raoul: You don’t like a big Johnson ... Howard Butz: NO!!! Raoul: I thought everyone likes Johnsonville sausage. I guess I was wrong. Howard Butz: Oh. Pulling Jake to his feet, Pepsi Boy whips him across the ring, sticking him with a drop kick that knocks him back into the enemy corner. Lars warns Brody following a forearm to the back. Making a tag, Brody comes in with a big boot to Jake’s face, knocking him down.
Raoul: Lars’ absolute fave is the cheese filled sausage. There’s nothing like taking a bite out of a big cheese filled sausage ... one bite and it drips down on your chin. YUM! Lick all that gooey filling up. OOH ... I’m getting all tingly!
Howard Butz: Right ... Brody with a tag and Pepsi Boy with a cover. ONE! WHAT? LARS IS SPANKING ... I MEAN, SLAPPING OUT THE COUNT ON PEPSI BOY’S BACKSIDE! Raoul: *GIGGLE* Arguing with Lars does no good as the masked wrestler has to contend with a hand in his face. Pushing Lars out of the way, Jake lifts Pepsi Boy off his feet before falling backwards and throwing his opponent overhead. Rushing into the ring, Brody hammers Morgan with forearms to the back.
Howard Butz: Spinning Jake around ... EYE RAKE! MORGAN ATTACKING THOSE EYES! Wagging a finger in Morgan’s face, Lars is pushes aside. Not to be ignored, Lars slaps him on the backside as he walks by. Furious, Jake turns to find Lars with his backside sticking out looking for one in return. With the fans laughing, Jake waves him off, returning to Brody.
Howard Butz: Jake’s not biting. Raoul: He bites? OH! Hammering away, Jake pulls Brody to his feet before slamming him into the mat with a DDT. Rolling back to his feet, Morgan listens to the boos before pointing down at Pepsi Boy.
Dillon Diamond: This certainly does not look good for Pepsi Boy and Brody. Howard Butz: You said a mouthful. Yanking Pepsi Boy to his feet, Jake sends him across the ring, catching him up on the return and spinning into a ring shaking spinebuster.
Howard Butz: SPINEBUSTER! SPINEBUSTER! Rolling Pepsi Boy up, Morgan holds him down while Lars slaps out his three count on Pepsi’s rear.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Raoul: LARS! LARS! LARS! [Lars blows a kiss towards his adoring fans before lifting Morgan’s arm into the air. Pulling away, Morgan stares down at the two unmoving bodies with a gleam in his eyes as the show goes off the air.
