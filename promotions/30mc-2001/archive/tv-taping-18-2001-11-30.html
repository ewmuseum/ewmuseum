30MC — Card Archive

TV Taping 18

30 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 2 —

[The camera opens on the 30MC banner with a perturbed Holly standing in front of it. With mic in hand, she shifts from foot to foot, looking down at her watch. Shaking her head as she looks off-camera, she shrugs her shoulders before walking off.
Moving on, the camera finds two members of 69 Degrees talking in the hallway.] Bad Boy Corey: Did you see I’m number nine on the weekly rankings? Kickin’ Kenny: Yeah and Chris is number ten. This is great. Bad Boy Corey: Great? Chris is here for less than two weeks and he’s in tonight’s main event. We should be there. We’ve been here from the start.
We’re 69 Degrees. This is our show, man ... not that lip syncing ... [Looking at Kenny, Corey suddenly lurches forward. Turning around, Corey looks at Chris Cortez, who just slapped him in the back of the head. As both his teammates look down at the shorter Cortez, he slaps them both upside the head.]
Kenny and Corey: OW!!! Chris Cortez: Big night tonight, guys. Chris Cortez makes his mark tonight ... Bad Boy Corey: What about us? Chris Cortez: What about you? After I sing tonight ... Bad Boy Corey: You’re singing? What about us? Chris Cortez: Next time, guys. Tonight, it’s the One Man Boy Band ... [Rolling his eyes, Corey shakes his head as Television champion, Jake Morgan walks on by.] Chris Cortez: JAKE! Just the man I was looking for. [Turning around, Jake yawns as he looks at all three men.] Chris Cortez: I’m going to need a few minutes before tonight’s match to sing my big hit and I need you to make sure that no one interrupts.
[Pulling a small red spiral notebook from his back pocket, Morgan thumbs through it before writing something down. Finished, he nods his head as he tucks it back into his pocket. Looking unamused, Morgan sweeps a glance at all three men before moving on, shaking his head as he goes.
After pumping his fist, Cortez throws his arms around the other two men before leading them off.]
Chris Cortez: After tonight, boys ... everyone is going to know that sixty-nine is more than a number!
[Cortez and company disappear down the hall as a clock’s ticking loudly sounds in the background. With the screen going black, the clock face of a logo hurdles through the ebony space before settling into view. The clock face fades into the background, giving way to faces and names that flash by quickly.
69 Degrees - The Mean Green Mascot - Mexican superstar, Ieta Fujita - Ricky Starr - The pink clad team of Pretty in Pink, Lars and Billy Blaze with Raoul - Agent of L.O.V.E., Harry Palmer - Weed - All American Buddy Lee Jr. - the heavenly Angel and finally, the Television Champion, Jake Morgan and an angry
Angel Rotten. As the last of the pictures fade, they give way to the 69 Is More Than a Number logo hurtling into view, tumbling end over end before coming to a complete stop. With a smashing sound, the logo is obliterated into a million slivers, fading into the camera making a swing through a small but packed television studio then focusing in on two men sitting at the ringside announcers’ table.
The two are a contrast in looks, one in a sports coat while the other one is in a Hawaiian shirt, leather pants and a matching hat.
Howard Butz: If you’re looking for wrestling action ... you’ve come to the right place ... WELCOME TO 30 MINUTE COUNTDOWN! I’m Howard Butz and the man on my right is none other than Dillon Diamond!
[Diamond gives a little salute to the camera.] Howard Butz: Well, Dillon ... what’s up for tonight? Dillon Diamond: In a nutshell, we have on hand perhaps the best new boy band in the business ...
Howard Butz: Best new boy band? In the business? Isn’t that an oxymoron? Dillon, in case you haven’t noticed, our business is WRESTLING not music. DIllon Diamond: I don’t know about morons, Howie but I can say 69 Degrees with Chris Cortez is special. Don’t hate them because they’re good.
Howard Butz: Moving on ... we were supposed to have an interview with the Young Gun champion, Angel over this week’s happenings at the last Lost Soul Wrestling show. From what I hear, Newton and Brody were in attendence which led to the boss being somewhat manhandled.
Dillon Diamond: I heard that rumor but as far as I know, that’s just a rumor. Howard Butz: What about today? Dillon Diamond: Today, we have the first match-up between two young men looking to win themselves a contract here in the Countdown.
Howard Butz: I understand you were able to spend some time with both men this week. You’re thoughts?
Dillon Diamond: Only one, Howie. I’m going to go out on a limb here with my pick to win the contract. Tommy Trevor has to be one of the nicest kids I’ve ever met and watching him work out was something. Pure motion, non-stop action.
If HardKore is half the man, this should be a good series. Howard Butz: Speaking of HardKore, we have this from Jake Morgan on the subject ... [The camera fades into Jake Morgan standing in the back with Holly. Giving her a mean glance, he swipes the mic from her hand before pushing her out of the picture with a smile on his face.]
Jake Morgan: HardKore ... [Jake chuckles for a few seconds before looking back at the camera.] Jake Morgan: Catchy name. Only there’s one little problem ... that title belongs to me. You see, I’m the RECOGNIZED King of HardKore in these parts
... and as such, Mr. HardKore ... [Jake runs a hand through his hair as he pauses for a second.] Jake Morgan: Make that, the wrestler formerly known as HardKore ... you can’t legally use that name in the same promotion as me.. Now, being the resonable man that I am, I went to my lawyers to come up with an easy solution to what potentially could be a VERY big problem ...
[Jake’s grin spreads across his face.] Jake Morgan: They’re solution ... a new name. Maybe Jake Morgan Jr. or any variation on the theme ... approved by me, of course. Maybe SoftKore, would describe you better ... or my favorite ... John Jacob Morganheimer Schmidt.
[The smiling Morgan fades from view as the camera fades into a commercial.] *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Commercials, commercials, commercials - all in the name of “free television”. We endure this crap just so we don’t have to pay for our programming but is it really free? Not really because when you think about it, every hour long block of television is spliced with twenty to nearly twenty five minutes of commercials. And to make matters worse, for our “free television”, we’re dishing out anywhere from thirty to seventy-five bucks a month to the cable companies just to have a few worthwhile channels on top of tons of crap channels that no one watches.
AOL. The No.1 Internet service provider in the WORLD! Your friends have it, your aunt has it, hell, your dog even has it! Why don’t you have it? What is wrong with you? You could be having just as much fun as them! Doing such things as contacting people with Instant Messaging, E-Mail and Chatrooms! AOL, it’s so addictive, it’s no wonder why its only 23.99 a month for unlimited access!
Yes, that’s right, UNLIMITED ACCESS! Giving you just enough time, to kill off whatever life you still have, by being online 24/7! AOL, why don’t YOU have it yet?
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** [Back from the break, the camera finds a young man warming up in the corner, bouncing off the ropes awaiting his opponent.]
Howard Butz: We’ve got young Tommy Trevor warming up in his corner. All we need is one HardKore to join us.
Dillon Diamond: You heard what Jake said ... John Jacob Morganheimer Schmidt. Howard Butz: I’m NOT going to call him that ... Dillon Diamond: I’m sure Jake wouldn’t have a problem with just JJMS for short. Howard Butz: Oh, please ... wait a second, Dillon, looks like Trevor is going to be joined inside the ring by Corey and Kenny of 69 Degrees.
[Stepping out from the back, the two are booed as they make their way into the ring. With a mic in hand, Kenny waves off the jeering crowd as Tommy Trevor talks with referee Orly Diaz.]
Kickin’ Kenny: Hold on ... He’s Bad Boy Corey ... I’m Kickin’ Kenny ... and along with Chris Cortez ... we’re ... [Before being able to finish, Kenny has the mic snatched from his hand by Trevor, bringing a cheer from the fans.] Tommy Trevor: No one cares who you are ... all they want right now is for HardKore to get out here so I can kick his ass! [With a yawn, Kenny snatches the mic back.] Kickin’ Kenny: Hate to disappoint you but HardKore isn’t here ... Dillon Diamond: JJMS. Howard Butz: WIll you knock that off? Dillon Diamond: That’s what Jake wants. Howard Butz: Oh, please. [In the background, the small but vocal crowd starts chanting “69 Sucks!” bringing Kenny to cover his ears. In the far corner, Corey can only shake his head. Grabbing back the mic, Trevor points a finger in Kenny’s face.]
Tommy Trevor: If HardKore’s not here ... then you’ll do! [Following a loud thump of the mic being smacked into Kenny’s head, there is a cheer from the fans and the ringing of the bell.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: THERE’S THE BELL! WE’VE GOT A MATCH, DILLON! Grabbing Kenny’s arm, Trevor sends him off for the far ropes, missing with a clothesline on his ducking opponent. Bouncing off the ropes again, Kenny comes back in applying a side headlock. Cranking it in, he works over Trevor’s neck who can’t seem to escape.
Howard Butz: Tommy Trevor trying to escape but Kenny seems to have it locked in well. Dillon Diamond: I’m thinking that Tommy Trevor bit off more than he can chew by attacking Kickin’ Kenny.
Howard Butz: More than he can chew? Dillon Diamond: This IS they’re show afterall. Trying to push Kenny off for the ropes, Trevor has no luck as the 69er moves his opponent around the ring.
Dillon Diamond: And let’s not forget Bad Boy Corey waiting in the wings. He could prove to be Kenny’s “Ace in the Hole”.
Kenny’s smile disappears as Trevor stops fighting, lifting Kenny off his feet and falling back for a suplex. Pulling Kenny off the deck, Trevor sends him across the ring, nailing a front kick on the return.
Howard Butz: Kenny doubled over ... SWINGING NECK BREAKER! Dipping quickly between the ropes for the ring apron, Trevor comes back in with a slingshot leg drop allowing him to make a cover. Diaz slaps out a two count before Trevor is thrown off.
Dillon Diamond: Tommy Trevor is not going to get a win here that easily. Complaining about his hair being pulled, Kenny is spun around and lifted into the air before being brought down on Trevor’s knee.
Howard Butz: INVERTED ATOMIC DROP! What were you saying about it not being easy? With the fans laughing, Kenny hops around the ring in pain while Trevor jumps up onto the top rope, balancing himself from his perch. Turning around, Kenny walks right into a face full of missile drop kick that sends him sprawling.
Howard Butz: Or that one? Dillon Diamond: This match is far from over, Howard. Let’s not forget about Bad Boy Corey on the outside of the ring. Whipping Kenny hard into the corner, Trevor follows in with a clothesline. Stepping back, he allows Kenny to fall flat on his face. Using the ropes to pull himself to his feet, Kenny is grabbed by the wrist and spun around right into a knee to the gut. Doubled over in pain, he his snapped up into the air and bounced on the canvas with a Northern Lights suplex.
Howard Butz: Tommy Trevor is really working Kenny over in this one, Dillon. Dillon Diamond: Well ... I did say he was good. Crawling across the ring for an escape, Kenny heads towards his corner, reaching out a hand to his partner.
Howard Butz: I don’t think this is a tag team match. Rather than complain about it, Trevor steps back motioning for Bad Boy Corey to come and get some. Looking at Trevor then to Kenny, Corey drops from the ring apron. With a disgusted wave of his hand, he walks off for the back.
Howard Butz: And there goes your wildcard, Corey. Dillon Diamond: WHAT? WHERE’S HE GOING? Howard Butz: I think he’s had enough.

— Part 2 of 2 —

Pulling himself to his feet, Kenny’s surprised look is interrupted as Trevor grabs him by the pants and throws him through the ropes to the outside. Making his way to the apron, Trevor turns his back on his opponent, watching over his shoulder. With Kenny slowly rising to his feet, Trevor hits the middle rope and comes flying off, spinning his body through the air and sticking a perfect Asai moonsault.
Howard Butz: ASAI MOONSAULT! ASAI MOONSAULT! With the fans cheering, Trevor bounces back to his feet then sending a dazed Kenny slamming into the ring barrier. Under directions of the ref, Trevor rolls Kenny back into the ring. Howard Butz: Tommy Trevor wasting no time in taking the fight back into the ring. On the ring apron, waiting for the wobbly Kenny to return to his feet, Kenny is caught by a surprise eye rake. Grabbing him by the head, Kenny spins around and drops to the mat, bringing Tommy’s throat across the ropes. Using a snapmare,
Kenny brings Trevor back into the ring. Howard Butz: Kenny with the reverse chinlock. Pulling Trevor to his feet, Kenny flattens him with a bulldog before rolling him over for the cover.
Howard Butz: THERE’S THE PIN! ONE! TWO! THR ... NO! TOMMY TREVOR GOT THE SHOULDER UP IN TIME! Kenny now arguing with the ref. The count was too slow? Oh, come on! Dillon Diamond: You know, we haven’t mention JJMS one time in this match. Howard Butz: What? DIllon Diamond: JJMS. Howard Butz: Oh, you mean HardKore? Dillon Diamond: No, JJMS. Howard Butz: Well, he’s not here and if you haven’t noticed, we have a match going on here in the ring .. and a pretty good one at that. Kenny working over Tommy Trevor in the corner. Orly Diaz warning Kenny about the closed fist.
Pulling Trevor out of the corner, Kenny pushes him back against the ropes before sending him across the ring. Hitting the far side, Trevor shoots back like a missile. In one fluid motion, the newcomer goes airborne at the last second, wrapping his legs around his target’s neck. Spinning his body, Trevor throws himself back, taking Kenny with him and throwing him over onto the mat.
Howard Butz: HURRICANRANA!!! Dillon Diamond: WOW! That came out of nowhere! Gathering his wits on one knee, Trevor pulls himself together, stepping to the outside of the ring. Grabbing the ropes, he uses them to propel himself back in. In midair, he twists his body around to be looking in the opposite direction before crashing down with a double knee drop to Kenny’s midsection.
Howard Butz: WHAT THE?!?!? With Kenny bolting upright in pain, he is dragged by the head to the center of the ring where he is flipped over.
Howard Butz: Tommy Trevor with a step over toe hold ... there’s a cross face ... STF! STF! Dillon Diamond: Kenny’s in the center of the ring!!! He’s got nowhere to go! Howard Butz: HE TAPPED! KENNY TAPPED! IT’S OVER! TOMMY TREVOR, WELCOME TO 30 MINUTE COUNTDOWN! *** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: WOW! [From his knees, Tommy Trevor has his arm raised by referee Orly Diaz as the fans cheer their approval before the camera makes it’s way into the back to find a head shaking Bad Boy Corey. Rounding a corner, he literally runs into fellow 69er, Chris Cortez ... an angry Chris Cortez.]
Chris Cortez: What are you and Kenny thinking by spoiling my spotlight? Bad Boy Corey: What? Chris Cortez: Tonight’s MY big night. I’m going to sing, kick some ass then spends some time wit da ladies ...
Bad Boy Corey: What about Kenny and me? Chris Cortez: You can spends the evening together ... [Grabbed by the shoulder, Cortez is spun around by a grinning Harry Palmer, Agent of L.O.V.E.. Fixing his multicolor jacket, Palmer clears his throat before addressing Cortez.]
Harry Palmer: Just the man I was looking for. You think you’re a chick magnet? I hate to say it but you’ve just met your match ...
[All three heads pivot as Young Guns champ, Angel rushes by.] Harry Palmer: Watch and learn ... [Pulling out some binaca, he spritzes.] Harry Palmer: HEY ANGEL! [Hearing her name, she turns to find Palmer approaching.] Harry Palmer: What say you and I get together after tonight’s show for a little ... [Leaning in close, he whispers in her ear. Her serene look changes quickly to shock then disgust as she steps back. With a big wind up, she slaps Palmer across the face while in the background Cortez laughs and the head shaking
Corey rolls his eyes. Moving on, the camera finds a smiling Vic Parker in the back signing autographs for a couple of young fans when he is grabbed by the shoulder from behind and spun around. His look of surprise melts into a grin as he looks back at the Trashman.]
Trashman: What say I give you the welcome you deserve? Next week, you and me. [After jabbing a finger into Parker’s chest, Trash waits for an answer. Stroking his chin, Parker answers right back with a jab of his own.] Vic Parker: You're on. [Leaving the back, the camera scans the ring, focusing in on a bored looking Jake Morgan in his referee gear leaning heavily in the corner before sweeping the cheering fans then settling in on Howard Butz, Dillon Diamond and Lars.]
Howard Butz: We’re ready for our main event match and some help here from Lars. As always, it’s a pleasure ... Lars: Pleasure is my middle name. Howard Butz: Right. Anyway, we’ve got Weed facing the self proclaimed leader of 69 Degrees, Chris Cortez.
Lars: What a horrid little man. Just another distraction and bad influence for those darling young boys, Kenny and Corey.
Howard Butz: Any thoughts on the last match? Lars: Had Kenny been under me ... [Lars gets a faroff look in his eye.] Howard Butz: Lars? LARS! [Lars snaps back to reality.] Lars: Sorry, I was lost in thought for a second there. Sweaty, undulating young bodies and all ...
Howard Butz: Right. We’ve got Weed in the ring and here comes Chris Cortez. Lars: BOO! HISS! Howard Butz: Dillon? Dillon Diamond: Should be a good match-up. It more or less depends on Weed’s condition. Howard Butz: Looks like Cortez has something to say. Lars: BOO! HISS! [Moving to the ring, Cortez quiets the crowd as he pulls his headset into place to address them.]
Chris Cortez: Tonight, I’m going to hit you with my soon to be released single. Music please.
[Posing in the center of the ring as the music starts, Cortez does a little dance step before belting out his tune.]
Chris Cortez: A! E! I! O! U! A! E! I! O! U! [Looking at his watch, Morgan moves to the center of the ring and calls for the bell as the music comes to an abrupt halt.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Dillon Diamond: What gives? Howard Butz: I think Jake heard enough. Thank goodness. Lars: Chris Cortez ... bad. Jacob ... good though he looks a little soft around the middle.
[With the fans laughing, Morgan raises Weed’s hand in victory.] Howard Butz: What’s this? Morgan disqualified Cortez. Lars: Cortez ... bad. Dillon Diamond: He can’t do that. Can he? Howard Butz: You’re always telling me how he’s the head ref. Lars: You said head. Say it again! [Leaving the ring, Morgan is stopped by Cortez. After a Cortez complaint, Morgan nonchalantly pulls his little red notebook from his back pocket.] Jake Morgan: Rule #35 states ... “any deviation from the scheduled programing leads to a DQ.” Weed wins by disqualification.
[Shoving past Cortez, Morgan leaves for the back. With Weed up on the ropes celebrating and Cortez pouting, they are joined by Orly Diaz, who rushes out from the back.]
Howard Butz: Orly Diaz talking to both men. I’m not sure what’s going on in there ... Dillon Diamond: Are you ever? [Stepping back, Diaz calls for the bell.] *** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: Looks like Diaz is calling for a match between Cortez and Weed. Lars: Cortez ... bad. Locking up, the smaller Cortez is pushed up against the ropes where he takes a knee to the gut. Weed opens up with knife hands which give way to punches before Diaz breaks up the action in the corner. A whip across the ring by Weed ends with a running knee that flips Cortez through the air, putting him down.
Howard Butz: I think Weed seriously dislikes Cortez’ music. Dillon Diamond: I don’t think he’d know good music if it bit him on the backside. Following a suplex, Weed starts to climb the ropes. Howard Butz: Weed going to the top ... Jumping off, he corkscrews his body, landing with a splash and hooking a leg. Dropping to the mat, Diaz slaps out a count. Howard Butz: ONE! TWO! WHO IS THIS?!?!? Darting out from the back, a big man wearing a chicken mask pulls Diaz to his feet. Whipped across the ring, Diaz is lifted into the air and planted with a ring shaking spinebuster. With the deed done, the masked assailant dashes off for the back.
Howard Butz: One guess who that is. Dillon Diamond: Chicken Man? Howard Butz: Get real, Dillon. You know as well as I do that was ... Getting up from his seat, Lars grabs up his folding chair. Lars: Cortez ... bad. Howard Butz: LARS! WHERE ARE YOU GOING?!?!? Sliding into the ring with his chair, Lars sets it up before pulling Cortez into a standing head scissors. Flipping him up into the air, Lars holds him up high, his face buried in Cortez’ crotch for way too long before falling forward and crushing the chair.
Howard Butz: OH MY GOD! LARS JUST PLANTED CORTEZ INTO THE CHAIR! With Diaz down and Weed watching the unmoving pair of Cortez and Lars with his face still buried in the 69er’s crotch, they are joined by Jake Morgan.
Diving into the ring, Morgan slaps out a count. Howard Butz: MORGAN’S BACK! HERE’S A COUNT! ONE! TWO! THREE! Dillon Diamond: Of course, he is. He’s the referee! *** Ding *** Ding *** [Jake pulls the euphoric Lars to his feet, raising his arm into the air as he looks down at the unmoving Orly Diaz.]
Howard Butz: Look at the smug look on Morgan’s face ... he takes out Diaz hiding under a mask then has the nerve to come back?
Dillon Diamond: That was Chicken Man! [Focusing in on the laughing Morgan’s sly grin, the show goes abruptly off the air.] *** 30 Minute Countdown© ***
