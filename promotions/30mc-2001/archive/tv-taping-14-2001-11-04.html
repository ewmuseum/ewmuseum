TV Taping 14

4 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 2 —

[The camera opens on a large desk, moving along it's paper filled surface before pulling back to reveal the owner of 30MC, Newton in his chair. Looking worse for wear, Newton sits there with a brace on his neck and various bruises to go along with a blackened eye. With a grimace of pain, Newton sits back.]
Newton: SOMEONE'S GOING TO PAY! [Leaving him yelling to no one in particular, the camera moves on to find Lars and Raoul making their way through the backstage area.]
Lars: What is Newton thinking? First, we have to put up with a couple of them, now, there's more of them? Flaxen? Angel? Isn't one bad enough?
[Nodding his head up and down, Raoul agrees with his partner.] Raoul: I hear you, Larsy. And to make matters worse, this Angel is using Cloud 9? Our trademarked Cloud 9? [Looking at each other, the two wave a finger in the air as their heads shake in unison.] In Unison: UH UH! [They stifle a giggle before pointing a finger at the other.] In Unison: JINX! [They giggle again as the camera is once again on the move, this time finding an arguing pair of Angels in the hallway.]
Angel: I used you?!?!? I show you around ... I introduce you to everyone ... I put you on the winning team ... and all I ask is for you to do one little thing
... which you can’t even do correctly ? On top of that ... YOU HIT JAKE! How can you think I'm using you?
[Ready to reply, Angel stops when Flaxen, the 30MC Young Gun champion, steps into view.] Flaxen: Well, well, well ... what do we have here? Two peas in a pod ... [Holding up here hand, Angel cuts off the blonde haired beauty.] Angel: Angel ... God still loves you and wants you to turn from your wicked ways and for you, Flaxen, we have some unfinished business ...
[All three women turn their heads as Lars and Raoul walk by. Stopping, Raoul sniffs at the air before grabbing Lars' arm.]
Raoul: Lars, do you smell tuna? [Turning his head slowly towards the three growling women, Raoul smirks as a clock’s ticking loudly sounds in the background. With the screen going black, the clock face of a logo hurtles through the ebony space before settling into view. The clock face fades into the background, giving way to faces and names that flash by quickly.
The Mean Green Mascot - Mexican superstar, Ieta Fujita - Ricky Starr - The pink clad team of Pretty in Pink, Lars and Billy Blaze with Raoul - Agent of
L.O.V.E., Harry Palmer - Weed - All American Buddy Lee Jr. - the blonde bombshell, Flaxen and finally, the Television Champion, Jake Morgan and an angry Angel Rotten.
As the last of the pictures fade, the camera makes a swing through a small but packed television studio before focussing in on two men sitting at the ringside announcers’ table. The two men are a contrast in looks, one in a sports coat while the other one is in a Hawaiian shirt, leather pants and a matching hat.]
Howard Butz: We’re back for another week of wrestling action, people. Thank you for joining us. Dillon, it’s a big week off a big show with last week’s
Faces of Fear television taping. [Nodding his head, Diamond whistles loudly.] Dillon Diamond: We’ve got girl troubles ... big isn’t the word for it, Howard. It was bigger than big. First, we have a split in the “City of Angels” as Angel Rotten and the newcomer Angel have some words over last week’s turn of events. Add that to the challenge by this upstart newcomer to
Flaxen, we’re all set for some big fireworks ... by the way, did you get any good candy this week?
Howard Butz: Candy? Oh, please. Anyway, we’ve got Holly in the back with Jake Morgan. Dillon Diamond: Let’s not forget, Television champion, Jake Morgan. Howard Butz: Let’s go to Holly ... [The camera fades from Butz and Diamond to zoom in on Holly standing in the back with Jake Morgan in his referee’s stripes ... a very pissed off Jake
Morgan.] Holly: Thank you, Dillon ... I’m here with a man who needs no introduction ... Jake Morgan. [Morgan looks off, rubbing his chin.] Holly: Jake ... Jake? [Turning quickly, he stares her down before stepping back with a big grin on her face.] Jake Morgan: Let me make this short and sweet for you, Holly because as you know, I AM A BUSY MAN. Seems to me that some people around here are just a bit ungrateful. In fact, they're quite a bit ungrateful.
Holly: Are you referring to Newton? [Jake grins as he looks away before turning back to her.] Jake Morgan: Holly, all I have to say is that some people are in positions ONLY because of me. BECAUSE OF ME.
Holly: Who are you talking about? Jake Morgan: Those who don’t remember how they got to where they are ... often get a little reminder ... [Nodding his head, Jake storms off, leaving Holly alone, shrugging her shoulders.] Holly: Back to you guys ... [With a smooth transition, Butz and Diamond reappear.] Howard Butz: Thank you, Holly. Dillon, who do you think he's talking about? [Diamond starts whistling as he looks off.] Howard Butz: Dillon? Dillon Diamond: I'm not going to say but if I were you, I wouldn’t look too far for an answer to your question.
Howard Butz: WHAT?!?!? Me? What are you talking about? What has Jake Morgan done for me except bully me around?
Dillon Diamond: Jake Morgan ... champion wrestler ... referee ... can being an award winning announcer be far away?
Howard Butz: Oh, please ... I’m getting the signal for a commercial break. DON’T GO AWAY! *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Commercials, commercials, commercials - all in the name of “free television”. We endure this crap just so we don’t have to pay for our programming but is it really free? Not really because when you think about it, every hour long block of television is spliced with twenty to nearly twenty five minutes of commercials. And to make matters worse, for our “free television”, we’re dishing out anywhere from thirty to seventy-five bucks a month to the cable companies just to have a few worthwhile channels on top of tons of crap channels that no one watches.
You know it, I know it ... Dr. Pepper makes the world taste better! The only problem is the rest of the country doesn't know it. Don't believe me? Just ask for it in Milwaukee and see what they give you ... "We got root beer."
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Howard Butz: We’re back and we’ve got Holly in the ring with the Television champion, Flaxen ...
[As the camera moves off to the ring, Butz and Diamond can be heard arguing in the background. Inside the ring, a smiling Flaxen saunters around the ring with her title belt firmly over her shoulder. Stepping up to Holly, she takes away the mic.]
Flaxen: I’d like to welcome you all tonight. Since you’re all here to see wrestling, I’ll get right to the point. Several persons here have a problem with me ... you know exactly who you are ... Angel and Angel Rotten. If you have a problem, I have a simple solution ... I’m waiting for you right here
... [She starts pacing the ring with a smile on her face as small studio audience howls for the leggy blonde.]
Flaxen: That’s right ... it’s hard being this good all the time ... [Holly and Flaxen spin around as “It’s Raining Men” starts up over the PA system. Stepping out from the back, dressed in pink shorts with chaps and white boots, Lars waves a pink cowboy hat around while bouncing up and down on a stick figure horse. Stepping out after him is his “Tonto”, Raoul dressed like a native American, completed by a small headdress with feathers. Slapping
Lars on the ass, Raoul sends him on his way to the ring. Riding around the ring with his music going on in the backlground, Lars slaps hands with the fans before handing over his trusty steed to Raoul and sending him off to the announcers’ table with a playful slap on the tush.
Climbing into the ring, Lars walks around, eyeing Flaxen before pulling a mic tucked into his chaps.]
Lars: Did you say it’s good being this hard all of the time? [Lars giggles as he looks towards Raoul sitting with Butz and Diamond outside the ring. Before Flaxen can correct him, Lars cuts her off, putting a hand up quickly.]
Lars: Honey, talk to the hand. Now, let me tell you burns my chaps ... [Licking a finger, he touches it to his backside, making a searing sound as he does so.] Lars: Sssssssssss ... [Turning back towards Flaxen, he continues.] Lars: Things around here just are not right. They’ve been going down faster than Jim J Bullock on a sinking submarine ever since YOU ladies showed up ...
and honey, I use the term loosely. While you’re out here flapping your gums, we could be watching any number of well tanned, muscular, oiled down ...
[Lars gets that dreamy far-off look in his eye for a couple of seconds before snapping back to reality.]
Lars: ... wrestlers in the back. MALE wrestlers ... face it ... they’re stronger, more athletic, better wrestlers ... blah blah blah ... and as you can tell by PiP, we’re sexier and possess greater fashion sense. Face it sister, you’re nothing more than three day old tuna left out in the sun ...
[Rushing out to the ring, Jake Morgan slides into the ring and steps between Lars and Flaxen before the Young Gun champion can ring his neck.] Lars: To prove my point and to show you just what Fun, Athletic Guys we really are ... I challenge you and the Angels to a handicap match next week ...
[Nodding her head in agreement, Flaxen and Lars are joined in the ring by a Singapore cane swinging Angel Rotten. Ducking under the blow, Flaxen slides in behind Morgan for protection when the ring becomes even more crowded as the
“heavenly” Angel slides under the ropes.] Howard Butz: Looks like the fireworks are just about to begin. We’ve got a challenge by Lars against the three ladies in the ring for next week but it doesn’t look like they want to wait until then.
Dillon Diamond: Sure doesn’t, Howard ... AND HERE COMES SHORTI BLAZE! [Diving into the ring, Shorti goes right for Holly trying to escape the ring before things get brutal.. In a short skirt and heels, Holly isn’t in any shape for a fight. Grabbed by the hair, she is dragged back in. A fast inverted
DDT puts her down hard. Seeing the action starting, Morgan calls for the bell.]

— Part 2 of 2 —

*** Ding *** Ding ***
Howard Butz: AND WE’RE UNDERWAY!
With the sounding of the bell, all Hell breaks loose as the combatants pair off ... Shorti puts a stomping on Holly, Angel Rotten takes several swings at her namesake, Angel while Flaxen and Lars circle each other.
Howard Butz: I’m not sure what this is. Is this a battle royale or what? If you’re just tuning in, we’ve been joined ringside by Raoul. Raoul, thanks for joining us.
Raoul: The pleasure is all yours. Howard Butz: Right ... Pulling Holly to her feet, Shorti body slams her quickly before sitting down with a leg drop. Across the ring, Angel ducks one of Rotten’s blow and moves in, striking with a elbow, causing Rotten to drop her weapon. A knee to the stomach is followed by a hip toss. Throwing her self back against the ropes,
Angel dashes across the ring, making a beeline for Flaxen but is cut off by the ref. Moving in to lock up, Lars is taken down with a drop toe hold, smashing his face into the canvas.
Raoul: NOT THE FACE! NOT THE FACE! Howard Butz: Angel arguing with Morgan who has her by the wrist. Seems that he’s already protecting Flaxen ...
Dillon Diamond: Protecting Flaxen? How can you say that? Jake is just establishing control quickly. WIth this many wrestlers ... things can get a bit out of hand.
Raoul: Hussy. Trying to break free, Angel is hit from behind by Rotten and knocked to her knees. Dropping forearms, Rotten turns her attention towards Morgan and takes up Angel’s place arguing with the TV champ.
Howard Butz: Hotly contested argument going on between Jake and Angel Rotten right now. Dillon Diamond: And while you’re focusing on that, Flaxen’s got Lars in a bad way. Rolling him over to his back, she graly jewels, bringing a pleading Lars to bolt upright in pain.
Raoul: NOT THERE! GOD NO, NOT THERE! Grabbed by her flowing locks, Flaxen is pulled back to her feet by Shorti. Several fast jabs send Flaxen back against the ropes in trouble as the second PiPster in the ring comes to Lars’ rescue. After landing several blows, Shorti is blocked before taking a shot between the eyes, Spinning Shorti around, Flaxen grabs her by the hair and bulldogs her, driving her face into
Lars crotch, getting two birds with one stone. Howard Butz: OUCH! Raoul: NOT THERE! GOD NO, NOT THERE! The fans let out a howl of pain as Lars sits upright a second time in pain. With Lars and Shorti down, Flaxen rolls back to her feet only to be put down by an Angel drop kick. Seeing Flaxen go down, Morgan shoves Angel Rotten aside to come to the champ’s aid.
Grabbed from behind, Angel is lifted into the air for a vertical suplex by Rotten. Fighting her way free, Angel falls in behind Rotten, pushing her forward into the ropes. Hitting them, she pulls backwards, rolling Rotten up in a pinning predicament. Morgan pushes them over from the hold.
Howard Butz: What’s Jake doing? Dillon Diamond: I think he’s explaining things right now. Howard Butz: Looks like he’s telling them that they have to go over the top rope to be disqualified ...
Dillon Diamond: Sure looks that way. Howard Butz: Angel Rotten and Flaxen now going at it in the corner. Rotten opening up with punches. She’s got the champ in trouble. HERE COMES JAKE!
Raoul: Floozy. Grabbing Rotten, Jake pulls her away from the slumping Flaxen. Pulling herself back to her feet, Shorti yanks Holly back up by the hair. Fighting to break free, she is able to slap Shorti across the face. Angered, Shorti wheels her around, sending her across the ring with an Irish whip ... heading directly for the far corner and Flaxen.
Howard Butz: Holly turned into a projectile and SHE’S HEADING RIGHT FOR FLAXEN! Dipping her shoulder, Flaxen sends Holly up and over the top rope to the ring apron where the lovely young woman rolls off to the floor.
Howard Butz: THERE’S THE FIRST ELIMINATION! HOW HOLLY GOT INTO THIS MATCH IS BEYOND ME! Dillon Diamond: One down and four more to go before we have a winner. Raoul: Whore. A drop kick by Angel sends Shorti smashing into the corner turnbuckle. Climbing the ropes, Angel walks along them before diving off and landing an elbow to the top of her opponent’s head.
Howard Butz: That’s something you just don’t want to do. Someone hits those ropes, even by accident and you’re eliminated.
Dillon Diamond: I don’t know if that’s inexperience by Angel or just not knowing what the rules in this match are.
Howard Butz: Good point, Dillon. I’d bet ... Dillon Diamond: Oh, no ... I’m not giving any odds in this match. You have to fight the urge. You can beat this ...
Grabbing Flaxen from behind, Lars spins her around before snatching her up into the air. With her inverted for a piledriver, Lars makes a horrible face as if he’s smelled something bad. Unable to complete his move, Lars is knocked to the canvas as Angel Rotten kicks his legs from behind. Holding Flaxen in the air, he loses his balance and falls, with her landing on top of him. Throwing himself down to the mat, Morgan slaps out a count.
Howard Butz: WHAT IS THIS?!?!? Jake making a count on the pinned Lars? Is he making the rules up as he goes along? What gives?
Raoul: LARS! LARS! LARS! Jumping up from his seat, Raoul leads a cheer for the struggling Lars. After sending him for a double whip into the corner, Flaxen handsprings into a back elbow that land solidly.
Across the ring, Morgan is embroiled in an argument with head referee Orly Diaz who has also hit the ring. With them going at it, Angel sends Angel Rotten across the ring before laying her out with a cross body block. Pulled up quickly, Rotten is flattened with a snap suplex before being pushed into a corner and hammered by the combined might of Angel and Shorti.
On the other side of the ring, Flaxen taunts Lars, using a boot to choke him, using the ropes for added leverage, sending Raoul into a screaming frenzy.
Howard Butz: Jake and Orly now in a shoving match! Things are starting to get out of hand. Dillon Diamond: Angel and Shorti with Angel Rotten nearly out of the ring! With Rotten holding on to the ropes for dear life, Angel and Shorti work in unison to push her out of the ring. After shoving Diaz, Morgan smirks until
Diaz comes back with a shove of his own, sending the TV champ right into the three women in the corner. The added impetus from Morgan being just enough to send Angel Rotten to the outside. Landing on apron, a bewildered Rotten pulls herself to her feet ... just in time to take a double drop kick that sends her to the floor.
Howard Butz: TWO DOWN! ANGEL ROTTEN HAS JUST BEEN ELIMINATED THANKS TO JAKE MORGAN! Dillon Diamond: Jake? When did that happen? I didn’t see that. With another one down, Shorti turns her attention across the ring, moving in on Flaxen, nailing her with a low blow from behind. Taunting the downed Flaxen, Shorti is spun around and whipped across the ring. On the return, she is caught around the head and taken down with a spinning neckbreaker by Angel.
Howard Butz: Angel climbing the ropes again. This could be dangerous! From the top turnbuckle, Angel waits for Shorti to stand before leaping into action. Jumping from the top, she lands on Shorti’s shoulders. Spinning her body, she dumps the PiPster out onto the ring apron before crashing to the mat herself.
Howard Butz: THERE GOES SHORTI! Dillon Diamond: Not yet, Howie. Not until she hits the floor. Moving across the ring, Angel pulls Flaxen to her feet only to seperated from her by Morgan. With the two women trading barbs, they both look to Lars sitting in the corner. With a pleading look in his eyes, Lars is pulled to his feet before being sent over with a double suplex.
Howard Butz: The girls are working in unison here. They seem to have put aside their differences for the moment to try to eliminate a common enemy in Lars.
Raoul: Of course. Who is the better wrestler here who has been dominating this match? Pulling him back to his feet, they send him across the ring ... directly at Shorti, who is pulling herself to her feet using the ropes. Hitting the far side, Lars topples over the top ropes that has been pulled down by Shorti as she uses it to support her weight. With nothing to catch him, Lars tumbles over, taking Shorti with him to the floor.
Howard Butz: LARS AND SHORTI ARE ELIMINATED! Raoul’s piercing cry cuts through the air as both of the Pipsters tumble to the floor in a heap. Jumping up from his seat, he rushes to their aid.
After crossing herself, Angel turns right into a clothesline. Knocked to the canvas, Angel takes a stomping before Flaxen takes a moment to saunter around the ring.
Howard Butz: Here comes 69 Degrees! The fans boo as Corey and Kenny make their way to the ring, shouting encouragement to Flaxen. Bouncing off the ropes, she drops a knee to Angel’s midsection. Looking down at Angel, Flaxen kicks her before pulling her up by the hair.
Howard Butz: Flaxen with an Irish whip! Slamming Angel into the far corner, Flaxen works her over with kicks and chops. Howard Butz: Things are starting to turn against Angel here. WHAT?!?!?! A COMMERCIAL? NOW? ***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** The Ultimate Cheeseburger ... $1.99 of cholestoral. Football. The Carnivores. Cheerleaders. Referees. Dinosaur mascot. Blimps landing on kick returners. Funny commercial. Terrible burger. Jack in the Box. Good food ... except for their burgers.
Mach 3 razors. Ever seen the handsome, shirtless guy walk into his bathroom ready to shave off the three minutes of facial growth? Lather up and suddenly start flicking his Mach 3 across face making wide swaths through the shaving cream, then making these sideways motions across his chin. Right. If these things actually had blades in them, the guy's face would be a bloody mess. Take it from someone with facial hair unlike these guys.
***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** Howard Butz: Welcome back folks. Flaxen has been working over Angel in the corner ... WHAT’S THIS?
Marching out from the back, Buddy Lee Jr. leads several of the wrestlers carrying signs against Morgan make their way to the ring. Drawing Morgan’s attention, Jake ignores the action inside the ring to watch Diaz and Buddy
Lee’s group. Howard Butz: Angel taking a beating in the corner ... DOUBLE EAR SLAP! FLAXEN IS STAGGERED! Climbing the ropes, Angel flies off with a shooting star press. Howard Butz: SHOOTING STAR PRESS! Pulling Flaxen to her feet, Angel tosses her over the top rope right into 69 Degrees. Howard Butz: SHE’S DONE IT! ANGEL WINS! WAIT A SECOND! 69 DEGREES ROLLS FLAXEN BACK INTO THE RING! Busy jawing with the guys on the outside of the ring, Jake never sees Flaxen being eliminated. Shaking out the cobwebs, Flaxen hammers a turning Angel with a spinning heel kick.
On the outside of the ring, 69 Degrees harass the PiP members bringing on a pushing match with Raoul until Billy Blaze rushes out from the back, touching off a brawl as Buddy Lee’s group moves in to break things up.
Turning back to the match, Morgan is there to see a Flaxen release German suplex on the ragdoll named Angel.
Howard Butz: IT’S PANDEMONIUM HERE! Pushing Angel against the ropes, Flaxen opens up with chops to the chest while Morgan leans back against the ropes watching. Slapping her arm, Flaxen moves in for a clothesline but comes up with air as Angel ducks underneath. Spinning around, Angel its a desperation drop kick that sends Flaxen into the ropes ...
into the ropes if they weren’t being held down by Morgan’s weight as he leans against them. With nothing to catch her, Flaxen tumbles to the outside, ending the match.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: ANGEL WINS! ANGEL WINS! [Looking down at Flaxen, Morgan shrugs his shoulders before grabbing Angel’s arm. Holding Angel’s arm in the air, Morgan doesn’t see Brody hit the ring until the last second. With a big boot coming for him, Morgan ducks out of the way leaving Angel open for the shot. With her going down in a heap, an apologetic
Brody is open for a ring-shaking spinebuster. With fighting going on all around, Morgan comes up grinning as the credits roll.] *** 30 Minute Countdown© ***
