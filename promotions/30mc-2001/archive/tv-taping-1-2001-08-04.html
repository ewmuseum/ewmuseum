30MC — Card Archive

TV Taping 1

4 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 2 —

[The camera opens on Backyard Billy and a young woman quickly strolling through a hallway when they are stopped by the Trashman. With a smile on his face,
Billy adjusts his half of the HKW tag team titles on his shoulder.] Trashman: Got a minute, Billy? Billy: Sure. You’ve met my sister, haven’t you, Trash? Trashman: Yeah. Hey Shorti, long time no see. Anyway, I’ve been thinking. Maybe, it’s time for the Trashman and Backyard Billy take this place over ... [Waving a hand, Billy cuts Trash off.] Billy: “Backyard” Billy’s no longer here. From now on, it’s Billy Blaze and though you and I go way back, Trash ... I’m a “PiPper, now. Come on,
Shorti. [Brushing past Trashman, Billy walks off proudly tapping his title belt while Shorti shrugs her shoulders before following. Leaving a confused Trashman, the camera goes on the prowl, focussing in on several large Taco Bell bags. Pulling back, the camera finds Jake Morgan scarfing down in delight as a perturbed
Angel Rotten looks on in disgust as the food disappears into his vacuum of a mouth.] Angel: Don’t we have to get readu for some ring time? [Looking up at her, Morgan mouth’s something unintelligible, losing several bits of food as he does so.]
Angel: That’s just gross. If you keep eating like this, you’re never going to make weight for your match against Buddy Lee Jr.
[Continuing his eating assault, Morgan turns as an Asian man in red and green tights steps up.]
Asian man: Yo queiro Taco Bell! [Snatching up a wrapped taco, the man darts off.] Jake Morgan HEY!!! [With Morgan in hot pursuit, they fade from view as a clock’s ticking loudly sounds. With the screen going black, the clockface of a logo hurtles through the ebony space before settling into view. With the clockface fading into the background faces and names flash by quickly.
The Mean Green Mascot - Mexican superstar, Ieta Fujita - Ricky Starr - The pink clad team of Pretty in Pink, Lars and Billy Blaze with Raoul - Agent of
L.O.V.E., Harry Palmer - Weed - All American Buddy Lee Jr. - and finally, Jake Morgan and an angry Angel Rotten. As the last of the pictures fade, the camera makes a swing through a small but packed television studio before focussing in on two men sitting at the ringside announcers’ table. The two men are a contrast in looks, one in a sports coat while the other one is in a Hawaiian short along with leather pants and a matching hat.]
Howard Butz: I’m Howard Butz and the man next to me is my broadcast partner, Dillon Diamond. If you’re here looking for Hard Knocks Wrestling, well ... the rumors have come to fruition and it no longer exists ... Dillon Diamond: Fruition? Look Butz, put away that mail-in college diploma and just spell it out for the people at home. Most of the morons at home aren’t going to understand anyway.
Howard Butz: You understand me ... Dillon Diamond: Of course, I can. I’m edjimacated. It’s simple. Newton came along, plunked down some change, bought Hard Knocks Wrestling and here we are with 30 Minute Countdown ... that’s you’re cue ...
Howard Butz: Riiiiiight ... Dillon Diamond: That’s where you say ... Thirty minutes of fast, hard hitting wrestling action ... the way it was meant to be. It’s pretty simple really.
Howard Butz: What about the Wrestling Union? What about the Commissioner? Dillon Diamond: What about it? Howard Butz: I’m sure the fans at home want to know what’s going on. Which wrestlers are here? What about the title belts?
Dillon Diamond: Need to know basis. Gannon’s sitting by the pool and I’m sure YOUR new boss, Newton will clue you in when HE wants to.
[A disgusted Butz shakes his head.] Howard Butz: He’s your boss, too. Dillon Diamond: We’re like this. [Holding his hand up, Diamond crosses his fingers.] Howard Butz: Great ... looks like I’m saved by the bell. Here comes Jake Morgan and Angel Rotten ... [Pulling back, the camera follows Morgan and Rotten to the ring as Filter’s “Nice shot, man” plays in the background. After some words for the booing fans, Morgan instructs Rotten to hold the ring ropes open for him.]
Jake Morgan: Nice, real nice. Another week, another venue. And here I am looking around and I see some of the same toothless lowlives that I saw last week. No matter where we go, some things never change.
[Rotten laughs along with Morgan while the disgruntled fans yell obscenities and other deragatory remarks at the HKW Champion. Shooting Angel a look, Morgan stops her laughing before going on.]
Jake Morgan: I don’t care who’s running the show ... whether it’s Knox or that little crybaby Newton. As long as you send people after these ...
[Thrusting his two title belts into the air, Morgan pauses for the boisterious crowd to get a good look at the HKW Outlaw title and his X Junior Heavyweight belt]
Jake Morgan: I’ll keep knocking them down ... [The boos give way as the new owner, Newton strolls out from the back followed by his bodyguard, Brody, carrying a black case. With a smirk on his face,
Newton smiles as the big bodyguard steps between he and Morgan in case of trouble.] Newton: I’m glad to hear that, Jake but you see there’s a little problem. You see, unlike you, I wasn’t blowing hot air when I said that this was war. And unlike you and some of your locker room buddies who were expecting something long and drawn out, I took a different route. I bought the stinking
HKW and sent Knox and most of your wrestling friends packing. No need for big messes when quick and silent will work just as well. Now, you might be asking yourself if I would send the State Club Boyz, Ghost, Jason
Flint, Dean McQueen and your beloved Bull Gannon packing ... why didn’t I just fire your *&@ as well?
Why didn’t I? [Newton smiles at the silent Morgan.] Newton: It’s pretty simple. I wanted to see your face as I stripped your title belts from you.
[Newton’s grin spreads at Morgan’s surprise.] Newton: With the X and Hard Knocks Wrestling effectively merged then dissolved in favor of 30 Minute Countdown, your belts are useless. You, Jake ... ARE NO
LONGER CHAMPION! [Newton laughs as chants of “YOU GOT SCREWED!” echo through the television studio. Taking a step forward, Morgan stops and smiles as Brody intercepts him.]
Jake Morgan: Let me get this straight. Because you changed the name of the promotion and got rid of a few useless wrestlers ... you’re stripping me of the title?
Newton: That’s right ... there are no champions here! [The crowd comes alive as Queen’s “We Are the Champions” blares announcing the entrance of Pretty in Pink led by an angry Billy Blaze.
Following behind, Raoul has ahold of Billy’s arm trying to hold him back while having ahold of one of Lars’ arms and pulling the reluctant “Head
PiPer” to the ring. The bizarre train stops when they enter the ring.] Billy Blaze: NO CHAMPIONS? WE ARE THE CHAMPIONS! LARS AND I! [Shaking his head, Newton nods to Brody who holds up the case.] Newton: There have been some changes, Billy and while the old belts and titles may have been something that you cherished ... there needs to be a change for the better. The old belts just won’t do ...
[After yawning, Lars smiles as he snatches Billy’s belt away and gladly hands them both over to Newton. Lunging for his belt, Billy is held back by Raoul who shoots Lars an evil look.]
Newton: Don’t get me wrong, there will be a Young Guns champion ... [Reaching into the case, Newton pulls forth a single silver plated belt and holds it up.] Newton: There just won’t be two of them. Just one but never let it be said I didn’t appreciate hard work. As the former Young Guns champions, the two of you will get the chance to wear this.
[Holding up his hands, Lars shakes his head declining the invitation, drawing a slap from Raoul.]
Newton: Tonight ... you and Lars ... one on one with the winner being the NEW Young Gun Champion. [All three PiPsters look upset before the fans start chanting “P-I-P!” over and over again. Turning from the three arguing men, Newton wheels around to
Morgan.] Newton: You, however, don’t have it as easy. Seeing as how you took it upon yourself to steal your belt away from it’s rightful owner, I take great pleasure in doing the same to you.
[Reaching into the case, Newton pulls out a second belt, holding it into the air.] Newton: No, Morgan ... this beauty goes to it’s rightful owner, Buddy Lee Jr.! [Ready to kill someone, Morgan is stopped by Angel and the interceding Brody. Marching out from the back, Buddy Lee Jr. brings a loud cheer as he makes a beeline for the ring. Stepping into the ring. Buddy Lee Jr. looks at Newton then Morgan.
Holding the belt out, Newton hands it to Buddy Lee. Again looking from Newton to Morgan, Lee’s eyes drop to the strap in his hands. Shaking his head, he looks back up at Newton.]
Newton: Our first 30 Minute Countdown Television Champion! Buddy Lee Jr.: Thank you but I can’t accept this. In the case of fair play, I have to follow through with the wishes of former Commissioner Gannon.
{Turning, he hands the belt to a surprised Morgan while a livid Newton has to be restrained by Brody.]
Buddy Lee Jr.: Jake, you have until the end of August to defend your title. As dictated by Commissioner Gannon, you have to make weight or the belt returns to me.
[Morgan smiles as Buddy Lee holds out his hand before the cmaera pulls back to the announcers’ table and a stunned Howard Butz.]
Howard Butz: Buddy Lee Jr. just hands the belt back to Jake Morgan? We have to break for a commercial but don’t go away ... we’ve got partners Billy Blaze taking on Lars with the Young Guns strap on the line!
***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** BEER. It’s good. In fact, you should be drinking one right now. If you were, you might be able to get fine chicks like those in our commercials instead of sitting on your ass watching wrestling at one in the morning on a Saturday. So drink beer, because if you drink enough, anyone or anything can look really good. BEER. Remember, its cheaper than crack!
Taco Bell is now open later. So get your fat ass off the couch and come get fatter! Yo quiero Gordo Americanos!
Scenes of poor children in some third world country flash by as the Christian Children’s Fund asks you to make a difference in their world for as little as 89 cents a day! Food, medicine, education all possible on 89 cents a day! Won’t you help make a difference? ***COMMERCIAL BREAK***

— Part 2 of 2 —

[Back from the commercial, the camera finds a nervous Neal stalking the back
hallways, his head darting from side to side. Rounding a corner, he stops dead in his tracks as he bumps into Billy Blaze’s sister, Shorti.]
Shorti: Uhm ... I’m kind of lost. I’m supposed to be with my brother for his match. Can you point me in the direction of the ring?
Neal: Uhm ... uhm ... uhm ... [The young woman smiles at the stammering intern.] Shorti: Just point the direction.. [Not able to say a word, Neal points in the direction behind him. With a laugh, the young woman heads off.]
Neal: Uhm ... uhm ... uhm ... [Leaving the intern, the camera continues through the backstage area, this time finding two men ... one under a thick cloud of smoke as he deeply inhales on a hand rolled cigarette while the other one, in a green bug outfit, complete with antennae, buzzes to himself.
After taking another hit, the smoking man passes it on to the “bugman”. Taking a hit and finally blowing out some smoke, both men are covered by a thick cloud. Passing, Ricky Starr waves his hand trying to clear away the smoke, wrinkling his nose from the smell. With the “bugman” handing it to him, the rookie declines with a wave of the hand. Shrugging his shoulders, he hands it back to the owner before taking off, flapping his arms and buzzing loudly.]
Ricky Starr: What’s his problem? [WIth a laugh, the other man takes a long hit.] Weed: He’s a freak, man. [Shaking his head, Starr walks off as the man goes back to his smoking. Leaving the back, the camera returns to ringside and the announcers.]
Howard Butz: We’re almost ready for tonight’s title match-up as the Pretty in Pink partners, Backyard Billy ...
Dillon Diamond: Billy Blaze! Howard Butz: As Billy Blaze and Lars square off against one another for the Young Gun title. This should be a good one! [It’s Raining Men” plays loudly over the PA announcing the arrival of Lars and Raoul. Looking like he’d rather be somewhere else, Lars finally enters the ring at the urging of Raoul. A cheer rips through the studio when Lars’ music fades into “We Are the Champions” as Billy and his sister emerge from the back. Making his way to the ring, Billy circles, celebrating with the fans before flipping over the top rope into the ring.
Meeting in the center of the ring, both Lars and Raoul recieve last second instructions from the referee, Orly Diaz. They embrace, giving one another little pecks on the check before stepping back to their corner.]
Dillon Diamond: WHAT!?! You’ve never seen two men embrace? It’s an old European custom. Howard Butz: I didn’t say anything. Anyway, we’re ready for the match to begin! *** Ding *** Ding *** CIrcling the ring, Lars tries to leave only to be blocked by Raoul and ordered back into the match.
Howard Butz: Lars a little tentative tonight ... Dillon Diamond: Do you blame him? Billy’s been on the move upwards over the past few weeks. He’s not one YOU would want to face inside thew squared circle. I’d bet, YOU wouldn’t do too well against Raoul.
Again with the circling, Lars tries to bail from the ring but is pulled back in by Billy. Whipped into the far ropes, Lars crisscrosses the ring with Billy leapfrogging over the top then dropping to the mat to allow his opponent to pass. Grabbing the ropes, Lars slides to the outside, waving a hand before heading towards the back.
Howard Butz: I think Lars is done for the night. WHAT? We have to go to a comercial now? We’ll continue with the action!
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Commercials, commercials, commercials - all in the name of “free television”. We endure this crap just so we don’t have to pay for our programming but is it really free? Not really because when you think about it, every hour long block of television is spliced with twenty to nearly twenty five minutes of commercials. And to make matters worse, for our “free television”, we’re dishing out anywhere from thirty to seventy-five bucks a month to the cable companies just to have a few worthwhile channels on top of tons of crap channels that no one watches.
On a positive note ... drink Dr. Pepper! It makes the world taste better! Nothing like being hooked! *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** On the outside of the ring, Lars and Raoul argue while Billy mounts the ropes. Climbing to the top, he waits until Raoul pushes Lars back towards the ring. With his partner turning, Billy leaps from the top, taking him down with a flying head scissors takedown.
Howard Butz: Beautiful head scissors. Dillon Diamond: I told you this kid could wrestle. You stick with me, Butz and I’ll make sure you turn that little gambling losing streak around. Howard Butz: WHAT!?! I don’t gamble ... Dillon Diamond: That’s what all addicts say ... Howard Butz: Anyway ... Billy taking the match back into the ring. Whip into the ropes and Lars goes down from a drop kick.
Rolling back to his feet, a surprised Lars rushes back to his corner and the consoling arms of Raoul. Again the two argue as Lars points to the back.
Grabbed from behind, Lars is spun around and sent across the ring into the far corner. Howard Butz: RUNNING CORNER SPLASH! Dillon Diamond: This kid can sure wrestle. Too bad, you can call a match as well as he can wrestle.
With Lars hitting the mat facefirst, Billy darts across the ring, leaping up onto the middle ropes. Throwing his body back, he slices through the air, landing a moonsault. Bouncing to his feet, Billy circles the ring gesturing across his waist to the fans cheering.
Howard Butz: Billy taking it to Lars and getting the fans into this match. Dillon Diamond: Yeah, he walks them up after you succeed in putting them to sleep. After high fiving Shorti, Billy crosses the ring and does the same with Raoul. Returning to the action, Billy pulls Lars to his feet. Grabbing an arm, Billy is reversed into an armbar then a hammerlock. Led around the ring, Billy gets an earful before being released. Whipping his hands off, Lars steps between the ropes for the outside of the ring, again trying to leave.
On the outside of the ring, Lars becomes embroiled in a shouting match with some front row fans who want him to go back in and fight. Waving them off, he turns towards the ring just in time to see a flying Billy coming right at him.
Howard Butz: BILLY WITH A PLANCHA! Dillon Diamond: You want to keep it down over there, I’m scoping out Billy’s sister. Howard Butz: Keep your mind on the match, Dillon. Pushing Lars back into the ring, Billy flies over him from the ring apron with a sunset flip. Trying to pull Lars over, Billy loses his grip, allowing Lars to drop to his knees, smothering Billy with his crotch.
Howard Butz: LARS WITH A PIN ATTEMPT! ONE! TWO! NO! With Diaz counting, Lars rolls off of Billy at the last second realizing the count. Backpeddling from the ref, Lars pleads his case for wanting to leave.
Trying to exit the ring, he is pulled back in by the ref and given a stern warning. Howard Butz: Orly Diaz with a warning for Lars. Dillon Diamond: What’s he going to do? Throw it out? With a surprised look, Lars peers back at his corner and the wide-eyed Raoul who is pleading with him to get into the match. With resignation on his face,
Lars returns to battle, circling the ring with Billy before locking up. Howard Butz: Collar and elbow tie-up. Lars in control with an arm wringer. Leading Billy into the corner, Lars works the arm until Billy climbs the ropes and throws his body over then tossing Lars to the mat with a modified arm drag.
Howard Butz: BILLY WITH THE COVER! ONE! LARS ISN’T FIGHTING! TWO! DIAZ WITH A WARNING FOR LARS! KICK OUT! A confused Billy argues with the ref as Lars escapes to his corner and the glaring Raoul. Howard Butz: Lars doesn’t seem to want to be in the match which I can understand having to face his partner ...
Dillon Diamond: I think it’s rather obvious, Butz. Diaz obviously told Lars, he’s going to disqualify them both if he didn’t get his act together. No belt for EITHER man.
Howard Butz: Now, Raoul’s arguing with Lars, telling him to get back in the match. This is just too weird ...
Grabbed from behind, Lars is spun around but counters, sending Billy off for the far corner. Charging across the ring, Lars slams into the turnbuckle as a feigning Billy steps aside. Jumping up onto the ropes, Billy lands on Lars shoulders, spinning his body, looking in the opposite direction as Lars.
Throwing himself over, Billys sends Lars to the canvas with a hurricanrana. pulling Lars to his feet, Billy again spins him around only to be countered and sent heavily into the far corner.
Howard Butz: Lars gearing up for another big corner splash. Not learning from the first time, Lars charges the corner and just like before, Billy sidesteps him at the last second allowing Lars to smash into the corner. Dillon Diamond: It’s like the old saying ... you can lead a horse to water but you can’t make him ...
Howard Butz: You can’t teach an old dog new tricks ... Dillon Diamond: I can ... Howard Butz: Oh, never mind. Billy going up the ropes again. SHOULDER SPIN HURRICANRANA! NO! LARS WITH A SITDOWN POWERBOMB! Dillon Diamond: I told you, you can’t make him drink. Howard Butz: WHAT!?! Nevermind. Lars pulling Billy to his feet. Setting him up for the pump handle slam. There’s that disgusting hip gyration ...
WIth Billy ready to be put away, Lars releases his grip after spying the action in Billy’s corner between Shorti and the Trashman. With him reading her the riot act, it’s Raoul to the rescue with a sharp slap to the face.
Howard Butz: We’ve got a situation brewing in Billy’s corner ... Dillon Diamond: Looks like the Trashman is asking Billy’s sister for a date. She’s a pretty hot piece of ... Howard Butz: That’s enough of that. Pushing her aside, Trash grabs Raoul by the throat but stops and shoves him aside as Lars has come to the near ropes, his attention on the outside of the ring.
Howard Butz: Lars and Trashman now having some words. Dillon Diamond: Maybe Lars wants to double with Trash and Billy’s sister? Howard Butz: I don’t think that’s what’s being discussed. Shaking the cobwebs from his head, Billy slides in behind Lars. Dropping to his knees and shooting an arm through his opponent’s legs, Billy pulls him to the mat with an inside cradle..
Howard Butz: BILLY WITH THE SCHOOL BOY! ONE! TWO! THREE! WE’VE GOT A NEW YOUNG GUN CHAMPION! BILLY BLAZE IS THE NEW YOUNG GUN CHAMP! *** Ding *** Ding *** [After jumping up and down and celebrating, Lars brings him back down to Earth by pointing out Trashman on the outside along with the downed Raoul and Shorti.
The credits roll with Billy glaring at the Trashman.] *** 30 Minute Countdown© ***
