TV Taping 4

25 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 2 —

[The camera opens in a modestly furnished office. Moving through, it focuses on a scale standing in the corner. Zooming closer, a handwritten sign dangling from the scale becomes clearer. It’s simple message underneath crossed out
numbers: 0 DAYS UNTIL NEW CHAMPION. Moving on, the camera finds 30MC newcomer Shawn Avery inside his office, sitting at his desk with his feet propped up and the phone to his ear when the door to the office opens and in steps two young men in matching silver shirts and tan slacks. Looking at thetwo before him, Avery speaks into the phone.]
Shawn Avery: Hold on a second. [Looking at the two men, Avery gives them a confused look.] Man #1: We don’t want to bother you but we wanted to introduce ourselves ... he’s Kickin’ Kenny ... Man#2: He’s Bad Boy Corey ... In unison: And we’re 69 Degrees! [The two men do a little dance step in front of a shocked Avery who waves them to stop.] Shawn Avery: Guys ... this is a very important phone call ... [Shooing them both from his office, Avery shakes his head, returning to his conversation.] Shawn Avery: Sorry about that ... I need a large pepperoni pizza with extra cheese ... [Leaving Avery to order his food, the camera is once again on the move, this time finding Shorti Blaze talking to a nervous Neal, the intern.]
Shorti: Stop that! [Playfully, she swats Neal's hand adjusting his collar as he nervously looks around.] Neal: I’m sorry, it's just that I have to be on the lookout for Lars and your brother. They're not too happy with me lately.
Shorti: Don't you worry about my brother ... [Giving her a sheepish smile, his eyes open wide in terror as a familiar voice shouts out down the hallway.]
Voice: THERE YOU ARE! [Turning around, they see the advancing Billy Blaze. With Neal hiding behind her, Shorti rolls her eyes.]
Shorti: Knock it off. Billy Blaze: Oh no, he’s not getting away this time. [Reaching for Neal, Billy abruptly stops and steps back. With a look of relief on his face, Neal suddenly goes white as he is grabbed from behind. Pulling him by the collar is Television Champ, Jake Morgan, a box of Krispie Kremes under his free arm.]
Jake Morgan: There a problem here? [With Billy about to protest, Morgan narrows his eyes, glaring at the Young Gun Champ.] Jake Morgan: No? THEN BEAT IT! [Grabbing Shorti by the arm, Billy Blaze hightails it out of there. With Morgan letting go, Neal breathes a sigh of relief.]
Neal: Thanks, I owe you one ... [Morgan flashes a wide grin at the intern as the camera fades off into Raoul, dressed in a short, pink toga and matching sandals, pushing a dolly loaded with several boxes toward the studio doors. Two large men wearing “30 Minute
Countdown” security tees, look up at the approaching Raoul shaking their heads and chuckle.]
Raoul: Hi boys! Security#1: What can we do for ya? Raoul: These are the outfits that you two are supposed to hand out tonight for all the fans. Dont worry, there are a coupla extra for you guys so that you don’t stick out like a sore thumb.
[Security man #2 reaches into the top box and pulls out an exact replica of Raoul’s toga.] Security#2: I ain’t wearin this! Raoul: (annoyed) The big boy himself wants everyone in these. They’ve already been delivered to ALL the dressing rooms.
Security#1: Newton wants us in these? Raoul: Newton? What's he g ... Oh yeah! I dont think Newton would be very happy if you two didn’t wear the ensemble provided.
Security#2: Damnit! We're gonna look like fools. Security#1: No way I'm losing my job over this though. Security#2: I guess the boss gets what the boss wants. [Both guards unhappily snatch up a toga and head off, mumbling all the way.) Raoul: The bow goes in back! [Raoul grins as a clock’s ticking loudly sounds. With the screen going black, the clockface of a logo hurtles through the ebony space before settling into view. With the clockface fading into the background faces and names flash by quickly.
The Mean Green Mascot - Mexican superstar, Ieta Fujita - Ricky Starr - The pink clad team of Pretty in Pink, Lars and Billy Blaze with Raoul - Agent of
L.O.V.E., Harry Palmer - Weed - All American Buddy Lee Jr. - and finally, the Television Champion, Jake Morgan and an angry Angel Rotten. As the last of the pictures fade, the camera makes a swing through a small but packed television studio before focussing in on two men sitting at the ringside announcers’ table. The two men are a contrast in looks, one in a sports coat while the other one is in a Hawaiian shirt, leather pants and a matching hat.]
Howard Butz: This is 30 Minute Countdown ... I’m Howard Butz and the man on my right just happens to be, Dillon Diamond.
[Diamond gives a small tip of the hat.] Howard Butz: Dillon, tonight’s the big night. This is the one we’ve all been waiting for as Jake Morgan puts his title on the line against the Number
One contender, Buddy Lee Jr.! Dillon Diamond: That’s right, Howie. The bad blood’s been brewing for the past month and a half between these two and finally, we’re going to see them once and for all going one on one.
Howard Butz: Dillon, hold that thought ... we’re going to a commercial! [Leaving the smiling announcers, the camera moves to the back where Lars, Raoul and Billy stand ready to enter the studio area as crowd noise filters in from the background. Dressed in pink togas, Lars and Billy each have hold of a giant box, while Raoul flitters in front of them carrying a bottle of bubbles.]
Lars: Get ready guys, this is gonna be great! Raoul: I hope I dont mess up with the bubbles. Lars: If anyone can handle all that blowing, its you! [They giggle as the camera breaks away for a commercial.] *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Kids love them! Adults love them! You love them. Everyone loves them! Those candy coated shells, Those marvelous peanuts, rice krispies, almonds and peanut butter they’re now stuffing them with. Even the plain chocolate ones! Don’t forget those terrific, wonderfully colored candy coated shells and you have the perfect candy treat! M&M’s can’t be beat! Buy some today!
Judge Judy wasn’t enough. They couldn’t stop with the People’s Court. Oh no, in this day and age of “reality television”, there’s even more! Catch
Power of Attorney every Monday night at 7 pm! *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** [Back from the commercial, the camera focuses in on promotion owner Newton. Without noticing, he walks past the two guards dressed in the pink togas. Stopping dead in his tracks after several steps, he cringes. Spinning around, he stares at the guards who just smile.]
Security#1: Hey boss! Newton: What in the hell are you two idiots wearing? Security#2: What do ya mean boss? The little guy that hangs out with Lars told us you wanted us to wear them and pass them out to all the fans as they came in.
Security#1: Yeah, something about a big show. They just walked by a coupla seconds ago. Newton: <BLEEEEEEPP> LAAAAAAARRRRRRSSSSS!!!! [Newton runs off down the hallway as the scene changes back to Lars and crew just as they enter the studio. Raoul skips in front of the other two, blowing bubbles into the crowd while Lars and Billy toss pink pillows into the audience. Billy throws several more into the ring before all three enter.
As Billy climbs up the ropes, Lars quickly unsnaps Billy's toga without his knowledge. The ladies in the audience go nuts as Billy barely catches it before it falls off.]
Lars: Uh oh! Looks like Billy might not be wearing anything under that toga! [The ladies howl and a drooling Raoul hops up and down clapping.] Lars: Its great to see everybody! Especially for this special edition of Cloud 9! Who else, besides Pretty in Pink, could bring you this much fun? Billy: Jake Morgan? Lars: I think NOT! Billy: Angel? Lars: Not even if he shaved his facial hair! Billy: Trashman? Raoul: Not on his best day! ALL 3 in unison: PILLOWFIGHT!!! [The crowd joins in using the pillows provided as Pretty in Pink have their fun in the ring. After a couple of whacks, the pillows, destined to bust, do just that, sending feathers everywhere. The crowd roars with excitement.]
Howard Butz: I don’t know what to make of this, Dillon. My info says Cloud 9 was cancelled ...
Dillon Diamond: I guess no one told the Pipsters about it though it sure looks like fun! [Diamond smacks Butz over the head with a pillow as the camera shifts back to the ring.] Billy: YEAH! Who wants ice cream? Cuz here it comes. [Billy points to the back as the fans chant “P-I-P!” but no one comes out.) Lars: What the!?! [The chanting turns to booing as the identically clad 69 Degrees duo step out from the back. Making their way to the ring, Corey and Kenny do their best to quiet the now booing fans. Inside, Corey takes the mic away from Billy.]
Bad Boy Corey: No, no, no ... you guys have been shut down. There is no more Cloud 9. The three of you are intruding on our time ... these fans’ time. It’s time for Bad Boy Corey and Kickin’ Kenny ... it’s 69 Degrees! [Both men start dancing but stop at the abrupt booing.] Lars: Are you saying the fans want to see this ... when they can have this? [Nodding his head at Billy Blaze, Lars’ eyes go wide as Billy pulls down his togo covering his chest. Snapping his head back, Lars turns just in time to catch a near faint Raoul.
By the cheering of the small audience, Lars has his answer.] Lars: I think you’re intruding on our time! [Grabbing up pillows, the Pipsters let 69 Degrees have it over the heads, touching off a brawl. Not taking the “playtime” lightly, Corey and Kenny come up throwing punches.]
Howard Butz: No referee but we have a fight in progress. Dillon Diamond: Looks like 69 Degrees doesn’t like the truth though I’d have to say, I’d like to see them dance a bit ...
Howard Butz: Oh please! There goes Raoul to the outside with a little help! Lars and Billy to the rescue with double drop kicks. [Pulling Kenny to his feet, Lars whips him across the ring into the far ropes. On the return, Billy’s leapfrog turns into a hurricanrana, sending his foe to the outside. Corey’s double axe handle trips up Lars who knocks over Billy from behind, landing on top of Billy with his face buried in his backside.]
Howard Butz: That’s not a pleasant sight. Corey with a double noggin knocker and Kenny pulling Billy to the outside.
[With Kenny and Billy duking it out, Lars and Corey are left to themselves. Pulled to his feet, Lars is sent into the far ropes. Charging back, he barely dukes under a clothesline and making his way to the opposite ropes. Firing back towards the center of the ring, Lars nails Corey with a high cross body block.]
Howard Butz: TILT-A-WHIRL BACKBREAKER! [Flat on his back, Corey is subjected to a face smoothering as Lars drops to his knees and buries his crotch into his opponent’s face.]
Howard Butz: Billy Blaze returning to the ring with Kenny. Looks like the Pipsters are setting somehting up here, Dillon. Dillon Diamond: Sure does, Howard. [From opposite corners, both members of 69 Degrees are sent careening into each other. Smacking off the other, they fall flat on their backs. Under Lars’ guidance, Billy sets up Kenny in the corner.]
Howard Butz: Lars is signalling for the “Love Train”! I don’t think I want to watch this. [Raoul joins Lars and the two move around the ring very closely. Giving Raoul a hug and a pat on the rump before sending him from the ring, Lars runs in place, getting up a head of steam before charging across the ring and landing on the seated Kenny in the corner.
Rather than the usual bouncing bronco buster, Lars’ version is much slower and suggestive.]
Howard Butz: I knew I didn’t want to see this! [A mixed reaction from the fans follows Lars as he rubs away while on the other side of the ring, Billy is knocked over the head from behind by the ambushing
Trashman and his trusty paddle.] Howard Butz: IT’S THE TRASHMAN!

— Part 2 of 2 —

[A hysterical Raoul gets Lars attention while Billy’s sister Shorti gets
Trashman’s, grabbing the paddle away before another blow can fall.]
Howard Butz: Shorti to the rescue of her fallen brother. Dillon Diamond: I think Trash has more to worry about than Billy’s sister. LOOK! Howard Butz: Lars is hopping mad! [Spinning Trashman away from Shorti, Lars threatens Billy’s attacker with a raised fist. With the paddle, Shorti rears back for a mighty swing. Catching her grunt, Trash ducks at the last second and is luckily missed. Lucky for him that is but not so lucky for Lars as Shorti’s blow catches him between the eyes and knocks him off his feet.]
Howard Butz: I don’t think that was supposed to happen. Dillon Diamond: Maybe not but it did. You know that Lars is going to have one killer of a headache.
[A clothesline takes Shorti off her feet, allowing Trashman to gather up his weapon and leave the ring to a chorus of boos.]
Howard Butz: OH COME ON! THAT WASN’T NECESSARY! [Trash disappears into the back followed by the two members of 69 Degrees before the camera swings back to the ring where both Billy Blaze and Lars are laid out in a pile of feathers trying to be revived by Shorti and Raoul.
Leaving ringside, the camera makes it’s way into the back finding Jake Morgan in front of a blank wall, dressed for battle with his title belt draped over his shoulder. Without waiting for the camera to get set, Jake begins to speak before the operator steadies it.]
Jake Morgan: The wait is over Buddy Lee Jr. It's time. [He rubs the belt.] Time for me to weigh in. Time for us to enter the ring. Time for me to defend MY TITLE. Time for you to learn why I've been a champion in every promotion, I've ever been in. Time for you to learn to fear me. [Pause] More importantly, it's time for me to put an end to you. Once and for all. [He gets up real close to the camera lens.] If I was you, I'd make the most of what little time I had left and ... [He pauses as he takes a step back and looks around then back at the camera.] ... PRAY! [Looking away, Jake smiles real big as he reaches for something out of view. His head snaps around at the sound of Angel’s shouting voice.] Angel: LOOK ATCHA! Jake: WHAT?!? [Pulling back, the camera shows Jake reaching for a box of Krispie Kremes. Caught with his hand in the cookie jar.] Angel: How are you going to make weight if you keep stuffing your face? Jake: Got it all figured out. [Jake walks off, bumping the camera as he passes followed by the eye rolling Angel.] ***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** BEER. It’s good. In fact, you should be drinking one right now. If you were, you might be able to get fine chicks like those in our commercials instead of sitting on your ass watching wrestling at one in the morning on a Saturday. So drink beer, because if you drink enough, anyone or anything can look really good. BEER. Remember, its cheaper than crack!
Scenes of poor children in some third world country flash by as the Christian Children’s Fund asks you to make a difference in their world for as little as 89 cents a day! Food, medicine, education all possible on 89 cents a day! Won’t you help make a difference? ***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** [Back from the break finds Newton and Brody inside the ring with the scale while the champ and his troops circle the outside. A taunting boss has Morgan upset.]
Howard Butz: This is it folks! A weigh -in as stipulated weeks ago by then commissioner ...
Dillon Diamond: I’ve got a question. Does Buddy Lee have to weigh in as well? Howard Butz: Excuse me! As I was saying before being so rudely interrupted, Morgan must make weight at two thirty-five or forfeit his title. Dillon, to be honest, I think Jake’s gained weight.
Dillon Diamond: Nonsense, he’s in tip-top fighting shape. You know how television makes you look like you’ve gained ten pounds. It’s “phantom” weight.
Howard Butz: I’m seeing him live ... if I had to bet, I’d say Jake’s easily put on twenty pounds. “Phantom” weight, sheesh.
Dillon Diamond: Got some money down on Jake not making weight, do you? Howard Butz: That’s not what I meant! Here comes Buddy Lee Jr.! [The challenger comes out to a big cheer. With a stern look on his face, he waves them to silence as he enters the ring.]
Buddy Lee: Hold on. We can’t do this. [The fans start up again but this time booing instead of cheering while Newton has to be restrained by Brody.]
Buddy Lee: Jake ... this match has to go on. It’s what these fans want. It’s what’s right but you know ... I know ... the fans all sitting here and those at home watching know ... there’s just no way you’re going to make weight.
Howard Butz: What’s Buddy Lee saying? [A perked up Morgan has a grin on his face as the fans jeer him.] Buddy Lee: This week, you’ve been given a reprieve but remember this ... the clock is ticking down on YOUR fifteen minutes of fame, not mine!
Howard Butz: Am I understanding this correctly? There is no title match today? And look at Newton, he’s fit to be tied! Dillon Diamond: It’s the sporting thing to do. [With Morgan entering the ring, Junior circles him after having Brody take Newton away.] Buddy Lee: Don’t think you’re getting off easily. You see, last week, someone got involved where they didn’t belong and robbed someone of pinning you ... this week, we’re going to right that wrong!
[Not waiting to hear more, Jake slams his belt into Junior’s head. With Jake putting the boots to Buddy Lee, he is joined inside the ring by referee Orly
Diaz.] *** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: It looks like we have a match but I’m not sure what Buddy Lee was talking about. Wait a second, here comes Ieta Fujita!
Diving into the ring, Fujita comes up with fists and feet, sending Morgan scurrying for the safety of the outside. Once there, Fujita eyes the trio being booed and points to Mean Green then motioning for him to come into the ring.
Howard Butz: I got it. We’ve got a tag match against Morgan and the mascot because of Mean Green’s interference last week that kept Fujita from pinning
Morgan. Dillon Diamond: Took you that long? Howard Butz: Like you knew. Oh, please. The mascot keeps Fujita occupied as Morgan slides back into the ring for a sneak attack but takes a back kick instead.
Howard Butz: Fujita waiting for Morgan. Maybe it’s all that “phantom” weight ... Fujita opening up with stiff kicks. Morgan into the corner.
There’s a spinning back kick ... NO! MORGAN WITH A CLOTHESLINE! Heading back to his corner, Morgan tags in Mean Green and escapes to the outside. Pulling Fujita to his feet, the mascot sends him over with a hip toss.
Bridging to his feet, Fujita backflips, putting the big bird into an arm bar. Howard Butz: Fujita climbing the ropes. LOOK AT THIS! FUJITA WALKING THE ROPES LIKE A CAT! Dillon Diamond: That takes agility and balance. Diving off the top, Fujita twists his body and throws the mascot over with an arm drag as he hits the mat. A tag brings Junior in with a knee drop to the bird’s kisser. Morgan is interceoted by the ref as he charges into the ring.
Howard Butz: A belly-to-belly suplex and a fast tag. The team of Buddy Lee Jr. and Ieata Fujita using fast tags to keep Morgan and his partner off balance. Climbing the corner ropes, Fujita’s somersault leg drop turns into a pinning predicament that is broken up by a Morgan stomp.
Howard Butz: Morgan with the save. Lucky for Mean Green because I don’t think he’s recovered from the suplex.
Dillon Diamond: I have to agree with you there. He’s seeing stars right now and unless he clears his head, this one is going to be over fast.
A whip into the ropes is followed by a jump spin kick that lands square on the button. With a tag, Junior is back in with an airplane spin before dumping his opponent facefirst onto the mat.
Howard Butz: Mean Green trying to crawl to his corner ... cut off by Buddy. Reverse chinlock ... Dillon Diamond: MG has to make a tag if he and Morgan have any chance of winning this one because it’s been all Buddy Lee and Fujita from the get-go.
Reaching to make a tag, the bird is just too far. Having seen enough, Morgan charges the ring and nails Buddy Lee in the face with a boot. Knocking him off of the mascot brings Fujita in as well.
Howard Butz: We’ve got ourselves a regular pier six brawl going on. Morgan hammering away on Buddy Lee ... FUJITA WITH A KICK TO THE BACK!
Stumbling into the ropes, Morgan is sent across the ring. Slamming into the corner, he is an easy target for Fujita’s running head scissors takedown.
Rolling to the outside, Morgan escapes into Angel’s arms for comfort. Howard Butz: Fujita heading for the ring apron ... ASAI MOONSAULT! OH MY GOD! MORGAN PUSHED ANGEL INTO THE ONCOMING FUJITA! Dillon Diamond: From this angle, it looked like Angel made the sacrifice to save Jake. What a gal!
WIth Fujita and Angel down, Morgan slides back into the ring following Buddy Lee’s belly-to-back suplex and bridge. Howard Butz: THERE’S A PIN! ONE! TWO! THR ... NO! JAKE WITH THE SAVE! He should be disqualified.
Dillon Diamond: I think he’s the legal man in and he’s explaining that right now to the ref.
Shoving the ref aside, Buddy Lee fires away with punches at Jake, knocking him back into the ropes After going to the boots, Junior takes a thumb to the eye stopping him. A follow-up short arm clothesline spins Buddy off his feet.
Howard Butz: Jake ignoring the ref ... SPINEBUSTER! Dillon Diamond: That shook the ring. I wouldn’t want to be on the recieving end of that! With a smug look on his face, Jake circles the downed Buddy despite the protests of the ref. Using the ropes, Fujita catches Morgan with a missile drop kick, sending him from the ring once again.
Howard Butz: There’s a tag from Buddy Lee. Fujita climbing to the top. CORKSCREW SPLASH! CORKSCREW SPLASH! Morgan desperately trying to get back into the ring takes a shot from Buddy Lee as Fujita hooks Mean Green’s leg.
Howard Butz: HOOK THE LEG! ONE! TWO! THREE! FUJITA WITH THE PIN! *** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: Buddy Lee Jr. and Ieata Fujita with the clean win! Dillon Diamond: What about Buddy Lee inside the ring? Howard Butz: That’s enough from you. Folks, we are out of time. We’ll see you next week! [Turning from the arguing announcers, the camera heads back to the ring where Morgan glares at the celebrating winners before the credits roll.] *** 30 Minute Countdown© ***
