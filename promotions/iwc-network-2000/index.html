Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The IWC-Network announced itself on 30 April 2000 not as a federation of its own but as a contract system layered over three other e-feds it named, EWC, AWC, and SHW, meant to let wrestlers move between them the way real contracts and jumps between promotions worked.

Under the system a wrestler signed with one of the three for a minimum of two weeks, with options to extend or leave for another of them, and any of the three federations’ owners could inquire about buying out a contract. A second post, two weeks later, spelled out the same rules again and sought further small federations willing to serve as its independent tier, feeding talent up to the three it already had.

No further document survives. Whether any wrestler actually moved between the network's three named federations under its own contract system, or whether more federations joined it, is not recorded.
