[HWA] Calling all Handlers

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fellas, This is to any free agent or any handler already involved in an e-fed looking for a possible 2nd (or 3rd, etc;) home. Just about any character is welcome as we have a pretty diverse roster of talent. One thing I would mention tho' - we could use some true babyfaces. So if you're interested - take a look. Who runs this thing? Phil D. Payne. He has been the HWA Promoter since it began. Former Office Member of the Extremely Violent Championship Wrestling, Phil branched off to create his own Wrestling Federation. Thus, the HWA was born. In it's first tenure, the HWA showcased some of the youngest talent in the World out of New York City. Now Phil is back to re-open the HWA after almost two full years of inactivity. This time around though, a lot has changed. Phil is operating under his birth name - Bill Parker and also looking to bring the HWA back to it's origins in Queens, New York. If the HWA doesn't become the greatest Federation on the planet, at least we all know with Phil Payne running it, it will be fun if nothing else. 2.) Why should I bother to join? Because you enjoy playing this game. Yeah, this game. Keep at that, fellas. It's a game we play thru writing. We create stories and that's how we compete. The people who write the best stories, obviously, have the most success. I can't tell you that this Fed will run forever or that we'll be the best. I just want to have fun. Plain and simple.============================================================ Contents: I. Introduction i. History of the HWA ii. Role-playing iii. Strats iv. Angles v. Booking II. Application ============================================================ I. Introduction i. A History of the HWA ii. Roleplaying Roleplaying is essential in the HWA, we all know that. There are a few loose rules on the Roleplaying. The minimum for Roleplays during the Week is 1, but 2 is the maximum. On the HWA Report, you are allowed 1 Small Backstage Type Mic Time BUT pieces will only be added as they come in. So, more or less -- first come, first serve. On Hardcore Tendencies, In-Rings are allowed also. SWEARING - I don't care too much. The way the HWA is going to be run - we are pretty much our own bosses. It's an Indy Fed at heart and so, I'm going to run it like such. You guys know how early ECW operated. There you go. Drop the F bombs if you'd like. But just keep in mind - when you swear like that, it's a double edge sword. Sure, it can emphasize a point really well - but if you do it too much, it just gets repetitive. So, just watch it. ATTACKS - Don't write up useless attacks on Announcers, Reporters, and Wrestlers. Unless it is OK'd by the Office, don't do it. We all know these types of rules, I feel stupid writing it but hey, some people still don't know. FORMAT - No real "set" set-up. But there is a basic format most use. Still, do things your own way. Here is just a rough out-line of what is 'normal.' [SCENE: Enter Scene here] WRESTLER: Talk here [SCENE: End it] That's pretty much it. LIMITS/MAXIMUMS - 2 Roleplays is ideal but 1 is perfectly fine. For Hardcore Tendencies, you have to write atleast 1 Roleplay and if you want, a Small Pre-Match bit. If you don't have a Match, it is still good to send out atleast 1 RP. It keeps your name out there so you're not overlooked. Just something to keep in mind. iii. Strats Strats are not a requirement but they are very much appreciated by the Writers. It allows your Wrestler to be better represented so when it comes time for readers to depict how your Character is, strats can help a lot. Is he an agitating heel who begs off and cheap shots? Does he like to cut out all the BS and go straight after his opponent? Strats help you out in that department. So, don't bother or hesitate to write one. iv. Angles Definetly the gas that will power HWA. I think it's vital to keep everyone involved. Interest is lost quickly when someone sits out a week or two with no direction. Never hesitate to propose an Angle to the Office. We'll always try our best to make sure it works out and to make sure collaboration amongst the Handlers is met. v.. Booking HWA Booking is pretty easy to understand. Roleplays will be the deciding factor in Judging Matches. Also involved in the Judging, each having a slight amount of power in it - Are Angles [Certain wrestlers might have a lot of heat or an Angle where it is a must situation for a Win or a Loss.], Strats will not necessarily help in a regular Match but if it is very close, it will be added in. Strats also add a lot to a Match so please -- don't be discouraged to write them up. Also, the current situation will help decide Matches in certain situations. We look at what is best for the Wrestlers. The best Roleplaying will not always win. If you did out Roleplay your opponent and lost, it is far from personal. The HWA wants to add in a sense of great unpredictability to add to the entire picture. ============================================================ IV. Profiles of the Alliance i. Owners Michael Emery is the Founder and Owner of the Hardcore Wrestling Alliance. However, in August of 1998, he turned the Promotion over to his Vice President Phil D. Payne when Emery ventured off into a new Area of Pro-Wrestling. Alongside Chuck Poleschuk, Emery aided in running a stint of Promotions from 1998 to 2001. Emery is back, however, this time he is doing little to nothing. Phil D. Payne - From the historied Family of Wrestlers and Presidents. Such Paynes are Ed. E. Phil is no different as he ventured into the Wrestling World and quickly established himself as a Top Promoter in the Northeast. Payne is back on more time to see if he can find it in himself to actually gut it out and make things work. ii. Commentators JJ Herman, HWA Icon! Herman is the Manager of the Infamous Mafia. Although sometimes embarassingly funny, where only he laughs at his jokes, JJ has a strong head on his shoulders when it comes to Wrestling. Very spuratic and emotional. When watching HWA TV, it is definetly a Show in itself watching JJ Herman behind the mic. At the current moment, JJ Herman is the sole HWA Commentator but it is almost a guarantee that by the first show comes rolling around, he will have a sidekick for better (or worse.) iii. Referees * For use in Strats (Optional) HEAD REFEREE - Fujinama Goto, a man with a vast knowledge of Wrestling. Goto has seen it all when it comes inside the Ring. He's called Matches such as Flyguy Ragnarok vs. Deathwarrant and Gripper vs. Dr. Death. Goto is the man to call on when you need a Match called down the line in spectacular fashion. Some people take for granted a Referee's presence in the Ring, but Goto reassures them they have a role and he does it better then anyone. SECOND REFEREE - David Rodriques is the second Referee of the HWA. For the majority of his Career as a Referee, he has been in Japan's SJWP. Rodriques works for free in the HWA and holds a side job unbeknownst to anyone. He is just happy he can return to the States under the clause that he has a stable job. THIRD REFEREE - Jimmy Lane is the final Referee of the HWA. He isn't exactley a Pro at it but he is far from a newbie. Lane has a hard time calling Matches because he is intimidated by the larger Competitors but also is subconcious of the fact he needs to make everything fair. So his timid nature constantly clashes with his good natured mind. Always a site to see. iv. Reporters * For Roleplays/In-Rings Tim Nielsen is the head of the HWA Media Department. He'll always be bringing in the news everyone wants to know. With a degree in Journalism, Nielsen reports nothing but the truth. He despises of speculation and especially Reporters who make things up just to knab some attention. Tim embodies the "A1, Perfect Reporter." Chris Patrick is a new addition to the HWA. He has been around the Wrestling Scene for quite some time and off Camera feels he should be the Head. However, Patrick does his work and does it well. Patrick is the stereotypical Reporter. He's an Ex-Wrestler who ruptured disc in his back and can no longer Wrestle. Sometimes, in those rare occaisons a Wrestler says a little too much and gets Patrick to respond, physicall. In the end, it always ends up hurting Patrick, in more ways then one. Richard Freedman is the Third Reporter. He is always on the scene. Most of the time when a Backstage Scuffle breaks out or a Wrestler request some quick time, Freedman is there. Dick, as some of the more brash Wrestlers like to call him, asks nothing but the tough questions. Sometimes he aggravates Wrestlers with his implications which leads to some pretty interesting things. v. Ring Announcer The Voice of the HWA is but one man. His name is Keith Smith. Smith doesn't want anything to do with the Matches. "Let the Wrestlers Wrestle, let the Announcer announce." The woman took a strong liking to Smith the last time around and many feel he'll try and use that as leverage this time around. Smith is still one of the most respected men in the HWA Locker Room due to his willingness to take a "bump" or someone or just go with the flow, regardless of how much he really hates it. ============================================================ III. Titles i. King of New York This is our World Title. The HWA is operating out of Queens, NY. We're not going to go anywhere. We're not a global promotion. So why have a "World" title? Let's keep it real simple. This is for the one guy who is, if you will, mo` preme. This is for the one HWA Wrestler who is just the top dog. Only one thing can be said about this Title... 9 times out of 10, the journey will be much more satisfying than the end. ii. New York Tag-Team Champions Originally known as the World HWA Tag-Team Championship, the New York Tag-Team Championship is for the one duo who can become the best of the best. As of press time, there isn't a Tag-Team division but these Titles remain nonetheless. iii. Brass Knux Champion Previously known as the HWA Television Title, the Queens Championship, and the North American Title - the Brass Knux Champion is for the best of the best but in a different way. The King of New York is for the best of the Promotion. The top guys in the company. But that still leaves us with new guys and talent who is good but not exactly "top draw" quality yet. This title caters to them. This title is for the best of the rising talents. Easily a catapult to bigger and better things. ============================================================ III. Scheduling i. Hardcore Tendencies Hardcore Tendencies is the bi-monthly show the HWA puts on. It is almost always held in the HWA Arena, "The house that Payne built," in Queens, NY. Most of the time there will be 4-6 matches. You guys all know the drill with shows. I really don't even know why I'm writing this. ii. Eye of The Storm The Flash Packet of the HWA. This will be the Ticket to getting your voice heard in the HWA. Every Wrestler booked is required to write up at least One Flash for the Packet. Eye of The Storm has no set Day to be released but will usually come before Hardcore Tendencies. iii. HWA Report The Mid-week Report of the HWA. In this could be small Interviews, short clips, and a review of the Week. Any Flashes that were kept off Eye of The Storm for whatever reason will also go here. The HWA Report will be hosted by JJ Herman and Sam TDK. Not sure if this is going to be a main thing but who knows? v. Supercards Every so often, we'll have a supercard to either 1.) restart things or 2.) to blow off some major feuds. They aren't really billed as big deals but you'll see how big of a deal it is when it comes down to writing the shows. v. The Schedule The weekly schedule of the HWA is as follows: [Keep in mind, this is a rough outline. The Cards have no set day to be released but will always be sent a day during the week.] Sundays - Hardcore Tendencies, Update, and Line-Up Mondays - Roleplay/Strat/In-Rings Sent In Tuesdays - Roleplay/Strat/In-Rings Sent In Wednesdays - HWA Report, Roleplay/Strat/In-Rings Sent In Thursdays - Roleplay/Strat/In-Rings Sent In Fridays - Roleplay/Strat/In-Rings Sent In Deadline Saturday - HWA Eye of The Storm ============================================================ V. We could always use an extra Report or such thing. But that is all really needed. I don't want to say anything and shoot myself in the foot by cutting some people off for the future. =) Below are the HWA Apps. There are two.
