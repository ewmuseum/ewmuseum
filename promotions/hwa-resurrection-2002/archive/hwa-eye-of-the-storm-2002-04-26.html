[HWA] Eye of The Storm 4.27.02

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Terry "Gunner" Vaughn 2.) H2O 3.) "Sadistic Scholar" Sebastian Montague 4.) "Homicidal Maniac" James Calscione 5.) Ethan Van Ryan --*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--* TERRY VAUGHN [Up.] [We open up to what appears to be a very low class apartment on the "other" side of town. We come upon a man sitting at a table. His head is lowered into his hands. He then looks up and runs his hands threw his hair and lets out a sigh. To his left are a large stack of bills. He turns his head and looks at them, shaking it in disgust. He turns to his right which sits a small stack of money. Right then he drops his head into the table as you hear a huge thump.] [We move away from his as we come upon a smaller cooler. He has a few beers in there, can't tell what kind because it is covered with ice. We move a little bit over as we come upon an unplugged refrigerator, we are assuming it's being taken out. We move back to the man, he still has his head pressed against the table. In the middle of the small stack of money and the large stack of bills sits a small cup of soup with the words "Add Water" on the front.] [The camera moves away from the soup and comes back to the man. He appears to be Terry "Gunner" Vaughn, the big 6'10, 285 pounder. For a big man he looks very stressed out, disenchanted, upset, you pick the poison. He lifts his head and peers into the camera. His expression turns from upset to very angry.] Terry "Gunner" Vaughn: "Five long years. Five long hard years of blood, sweat and tears. Busting my ass, night after night, battling the so-called "toughest" the street had to offer. And what did it get me?" [Vaughn lifts his arms and motions the things around him.] TGV: "Not a god damn thing." [Vaughn slams his fist into the table.] TGV: "I've done everything I could do to keep my head above water. To make sure that I had the necessities to survive. But, i'm done with all that. It's time Terry Vaughn moves up in the world. And that is where the HWA comes into play." [Pause.] TGV: "I heard about this little upstart promotion and it's going to make me a star. Even if they don't know it yet. This is my time to shine. Not only will the HWA make me a star, but every one of you in it will too. There is only one simple question to be answered." [Gunner rises to his feet.] TGV: "Are you willing to help me build the HWA into a multimillion dollar promotion, riding around in the limo's, sold out arenas, huge bi-rates for the PPV? Or, do I have to drag every one of you fuckers on my back? 'Cause either way this promotion is goin' to the top." [Quick, yet dramatic pause.] TGV: "And i'll be the first in line." [Vaughn laughs...very sadistically.] TGV: "H2O will be the first man I destroy. But, definitly won't be the last. Once I get done mowing his ass down, i'll take it to the rest of you. One by one, I will take you all to the top. And I dare say, HWA, you will thank me in the end for my charity." [Vaughn begins to laugh as the camera fades to darkness.] --*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--* H2O The camera fades into a gym where H2O has been seen working out many times in the past. H2O is standing in front of a worn old looking punching back that looks like H2O has taken his fist to it one too many times. H2O pulls his fist back, takes a few deeps breaths and then turns away from the back. H2O: Its been a long, winding road getting here. It has taken more than any man would ever have thought possible. I have given what many people would have called a superhuman effort and now I stand here before you. I stand here in front of a group of cold uncaring world. No other man employed by this federation has so much as bothered to come out here and say a word. No one gives a damn. The people look at Mike Emery and they laugh at him. But not me, instead I see the most sadistic man on the planet. The one man who will let me do whatever the hell I want to whatever the hell I want. That is the man who appreciates all that I have done in my career. (H2O pauses) H2O: If some others don't see it my way, I guess that I am going ot have to make it my business to teach them some respect. The voices in my head have come back, they have been begging me to spill more blood, to feed their thirst. This week they are going to get exactly what they want. Terry 'Gunner' Vaughn the voices don't like you. I don't know who the hell you are, I mean you haven't even bothered to conduct a single interview. So I am going to decide that I hate you, don't feel too bad about that becuase I hate just about everyone. I mean even if you did come out here and run your mouth I would proberably hate you anyway. There is nothing which you can can do to prevent me from hating you, from being out for your blood. You are going to be getting into the ring with the most dangerous man in this sport and you are going to learn that I have earned that title. (H2O takes a breath.) H2O: This sport is dying, anyone can see that. I have given my heart and soul to this sport for more years than I care to remember and right now it is starting to wind down. Well I am going to give it my all, or at least all that I have left until the sport last day. Gunner in a few months people will be safe from the menace known as H2O. No one will ever have to look over his shoulder for fear that H2O is going to break him in half and make beat him to within an inch of his life. In a few months the world will be safe from my brutaility but Gunner that time won't come soon enough for you. You might just be one of the last men to suffer innumerable tortures at my hands. Terry 'Gunner' Vaughn PREPARE TO BE ERADICATED. (The camera fades out.) --*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--* SEBASTIAN MONTAGUE [Fade in] [The camera slowly fades in to a dimly lit class room. Neat rows of desks fill the room which has a large brown desk at the far end with an empty chalk board behind it. Sitting in the middle of the golden tint cast on the room from the sun is a rather familiar face. Long, golden locks hang over his neatly shaven face where two bright, cobalt blue eyes shine like diamonds. Attired in a plain white button down and red suspenders is none other than that man you thought you'd never see again. That's right. It's Sebastian Montague. School is back in session. Here comes that raspy, Canadian tone that just seems to be carried by sarcasm and arrogance.] "SADISTIC SCHOLAR" SEBASTIAN MONTAGUE: I know what you're asking yourselves. Is it true!? Could it be!? Well, the answer is yes and yes. Two weeks ago I received a phone call from Phil Payne. I knew what he wanted. Payne is the type of man who only calls you when he needs something. He's almost a bigger waste of space than the so-called "talent" of this Federation. Ahh yes, the "talent" of this federation, ... this shithole. The HWA, the Hardcore Wrestling Alliance. Things look quite different this time around. I don't see that filthy, foul, and foolish drunk Rhett Garland. I don't see my lackey Warren Worthington. And I don't see any of the supposed 'big names' like Terrence Steele or Brian Floyd. Only one man remains from the original Hardcore Wrestling Alliance. Only one man... and boy do I have a bone to pick with him. [Montague slowly sits up from the chair and walks over to his chalk board. Drawing a piece of chalk to the board, he quickly begins to write something down. As he finishes, Montague steps aside and the board clearly reads... "E-V-R-." Montague grins and places the chalk down. Walking to his seat, Sebastian shakes his head in, apparently, disgust.] "SS" SM: It's quite funny in a weird way. Everything, I suppose, really does come full circle. Imagine this if you will. It's November of 2000. The Hardcore Wrestling Alliance is in full effect. Amazing popularity for a lowly Independent Promotion out of New York; a hotbed for indy wrestling. Mike Emery was awarded a Top 10 finish for best President. Our weekly show, Hardcore Tendencies was a Top 10 finish. And a few of our wrestlers were on the Top 200 of 2000. We were gearing up for our first 'big' show: Fall Fracas. Every last wrestler, or excuse for such, was preparing to go all out. Myself included as I had a big match-up against the aforementioned scumbag Rhett Garland. At the tip top of the card were six names. Six names 'till this day I absolutely hate and disgust. Six names of the poorest excuses for "wrestlers" this country has ever seen. Six names that I've constantly thought about for almost two full years... sitting, waiting, and hoping for retribution. I'm like a torn angel waiting for my chance at redemption. And now, finally my chance has come. [Montague smiles from ear-to-ear.] One of those six names has been called. And this name is the one I hate the most. This is the name of a man who represents every last thing wrong with Pro-Wrestling. This is the name of a man who doesn't even deserve my attention, let alone the chance ... the opportunity to step into the ring with me! This man's name is represented in three simple letters... E ... [Pause.] V... [Longer pause.] R... [Montague's eyes light up with a fiery rage that just seems to fittingly represent his deep rooted hatred.] Ethan Van Ryan. Lord, that name just irritates me to no end. Ethan, do you remember me? That 'forever mid-carder' in the previous HWA? Maybe you do and maybe you don't. Let's say you don't. Good because I hope you don't. I hope on May 4th, I come out of nowhere and show you just what type of talent laid low before when you were making money and Top 200's by throwing out whimpy chair shots against nobodies like Terrence Steele. [Montague stands up, his shoulders pumping up and down as he's overcome with pure, passionate rage.] Where is Mr. Trauma now? Nowhere to be found! He got the phone calls from Payne. He knows what's going on in Queens again but this time he knows about something else. He knows Sebastian Montague is here and that he means business. So Steele showed those chair shots didn't do too much damage because he made a wise decision to stay home. But you, Ethan, no - you had to come. You had to get that spotlight one more time. Well too bad, it's my time. I'm thirty-two and it's my time to shine. My time to be top dog. I am waiting, Ethan... I am waiting. The time has come for you to be taught a lesson, Van Ryan. There will be no cutting of my class. Oh no. Everyone has to show up sometime or another. Win, lose, or draw you will learn something in my class. And that's one simple lesson you will never forget. [Montague flashes a broad, sadistic smirk.] Don't fight me to win ... fight me to survive. [Fade to black.] --*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--* JAMES CALSCIONE "The Homicidal Maniac" James Calscione Sage: Journal Entry #1 [ The hushed whispers, incoherent words uttered as the once illuminate sun shrivels away, reverting downwards as it displays no such restraint, allowing darkness to prevail. The former hue of light blue and foggy white slowly disappears, replaced by a sin black. Blurred and taint images. If one scrutinizes for an extended period of time, they are apt to discover some figure beyond the fog. Though, does it matter in actuality? A figure, what does he represent? Another motherfucker striving to step into the spotlight desired by so many others? Perhaps he is one who wishes not to have his face on camera, not to be recognized by the nuances who would ridicule and adore him in the very same instance. Raped of his dignity, pillaged of his pride. Is this a suitable sequence for this particular individual? An opportunity to be praised by his peers as he stands alone, inept to defend himself against the criticism of these deemed superior individuals. Thus, he chooses otherwise. His face remains covert. His identity lingers amid the unknown. Continuing to abide beyond the fog. A path chosen by those who wish for apathy to prevail. ] Education; A system by which the learned pass their knowledge down to others so that they may then indoctrinate others seeking intelligence. Purpose? To create a utopia of educated youth adoloscents and adults alike to share wisdom with one another, thus advancing society. [ Anticipate the emergence of a well-known figure, you shall be disappointed. The image on your screen becomes slightly clearer. What one may have had difficulty discerning, gradually grows visible. As previously recognized, a figure does exist. His identity remains unknown, though. Do not feel disenchanted, for he shall become ocular when he feels it necessary. Minor degrees of light shine through the vacancies between tree branches blanketed by leaves, clinging to trees and those which have fallen upon the soil. Vast patches of grass surrounded by numerous plants, ultimately surrounded by a few large trees towering towards the sky. An area, perhaps 40 feet in diamter, which one would hardly consider spacious. Nearly defeaning silence overcast by the chirping of natures finest. ] Nearly two-hundred and forty years ago, a revolution occurred in what was then referred to as "the new world," as an outcry for democracy stretched from the bitter-cold colony of Maine, reaching the plantations of South Carolina. Regions occupied by those who had sought a desirable and better world, waged war on perhaps the most potent nation of all during the time period, Great Britain. An excruciating struggle for the opportunity to be deemed free and govern oneself. As many united to battle against tyranny and injustice, counsels were held to discourse on a constitution for this soon to be nation, as the battlefields were engulfed with indignation and terror. Those held in such high regard for their exceptional minds and vast knowledge established a document which exists to this very day. Men who are now considered paramount figures of their times and extraordinary mortals were titled outlaws by those who were granted unlimited authority over all the masses. The latter are men who throughout their lives may have had many worldly possesions and owned a large quantity of material entities, yet, when are any of these names dropped in a positive sense, or, dropped at all, for that matter? The outlaws, now considered men of good character and revolutionaries with just cause, were condemned as villains and scoundrels. An act of treason against the government bloomed into easily the most prosperous nation in today's society. [ A figure stands, confined in this pre-described area. Perhaps this represents a refuge of sorts. A safekeep from the rest of the world, where any fragments of rays are granted rites of passage. Hardly exposed to the world, the figure seems secure and rather comfortable in his dome. Something which is irregular for a person of this occupation, one whose mind is generally consumed with thoughts of malice and how to plot the demise of his adversary. Though, in this particular moment, when one is apt to focus on what is significant by virtue of the environment, serenity prevails. A figure stands roughly 6 feet tall, a good, toned figure, but hardly abnormally muscular. Shaggy black hair and an unkempt facial look, eyes of sin are overspread by scruffy, half shaven facial hair. Attired only in baggy sweat pants and an unbuttoned white dress shirt, the figure neglects to wear shoes. His expressionless face is only noticeable due to his now closed eyes. ] What knowledge one can acquire from this? Those who are derided by their peers, ridiculed and recognized as representatives of that which is evil and criminal, may one day be considered favorable by the masses. Today, I am disdained and berated by my peers. Those who were granted rites of passage into my mind were appalled, and fled at the first opportunity to do so. These are people who have left footprints, distant memories of what it is to be "normal." This is something which I contemn, for it speaks boldly of that which is counterfeit. A false sincerity, a mask to protect oneself. Terrified of the world they live in, disregarding any proposed notion for change. What differs me from these individuals? My dress attire? No. My hair style? No. My bodily organs? I would hope not. Yet, I am considered different. What I hold dear to me is spit upon by these people who claim to be righteous. My thought structure frightens these people. My heart breeds hope through malice. I stand alone. This is what I prefer. This is why I reside in such promotions as HWA. A roster of few, which excludes stables seeking glory, depending upon one another. Battling to service the conscience of oneself, no need to please others. HWA can honestly claim to be different in that respect. We are separate from other promotions in the sense that the dick riders are prohibited, impelling these bitches to remain elsewhere. This is what I hope never changes. My vocation may be clear to you already. Hopefully to Taylor Threat. Grasp my nature, spit that verbal fire which you desire to so very much. Attempt to understand the complexity of the mind of this sage. My claims are few, my demeanor is obvious and evident to all who are able to comprehend even the pettiest of sentences. I'm real, I state my opinion and stand by it. Though, why philosophize, I will speak what I consider truth, and truth is all that is apt to devour the benighted opposition. Alter my words, misinterpret what I have said, and you will have schemed your own demise. Atleast in that sense, you will die knowing that it is not I who have conquered you, but it is you who have conquered yourself. [ Fade Out ] --*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--* ETHAN VAN RYAN [Fade in, to an image of a lone man walking down a dark, poorly lit street, slowly coming towards the camera. Almost as if on cue, a street light behind him blinks, then slowly fizzles out. The figure stops, looks up at the light, then continues towards the camera. He reaches the camera, and is that a point far enough away that he can be heard.] MAN: [Singing.] New York! New York! [He is now at the point where he can be seen, but not much of his face is visable aside from him medium length, dirty blond hair.] MAN: What a shithole. [A hand wipes some of the hair away from his face, and the man lets loose a shit-eating grin, a grin that only someone like Ethan Van Ryan could have.] EVR: What, you think I forgot about HWA? [Another grin.] EVR: [Laughing, almost evilly.] How could I forget about the Hardcore Wrestling Alliance, A place where I shed blood, sweat and tears,.. A place I helped put on the map! But, enough about me. The HWA is back, thus, I am back. It's been along time coming, and I didn't even believe the rumors when I first heard them,.. They were true though. The only thing that upsets me, is the fact that Terrence Steele didn't sign up as quick I did. Is he still hanging his head in shame? I can't see why, I _did_ give me a run for my money? Truely though,.. It doesn't matter. Only one thing matters tonight. Not Terrence Steele, not me. All that matters is the HWA, and next week, Sebastion and I are going to put on a _show_ like no other. Next week, the rebirth of the HWA. We show _you_ what it's all about! [Fade.] --*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*--*
