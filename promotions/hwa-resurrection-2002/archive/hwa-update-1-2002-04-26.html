Closing Comments ~ =~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~ ============================================================ I. From The Desk I'll spare you guys the fancy introduction. We all know why we are here. To have some god damn fun playing this game we call e-wrestling. That is all I want. To have fun. Fortunately, this is the type of enviroment I think we all need to truly have fun. Granted, things are very small and I'm not sure any of you really talk to one another - hopefully that'll change as time goes on. Things might be a little rough in the early going. But just bare with me. I'm trying to find a good niche for things. Testing things out. Some might go off real well. Others might just blow up in my face. But hey - that's part of life, trying new things. So here we are. Let's try something new. Let's all try to actually play this game the way it's meant to be played and enjoy it. Let's see if we can at least do that, fellas. I know I'm willing to see if I can do it. I hope you all are too. II. Roster 1.) H2O (6'4", 245/Neutral) [0-0-0] 2.) "Homicidal Maniac" James Calscione (6'0", 215/Heel) [0-0-0] 3.) Taylor ThreaT (5'10", 181/Heel) [0-0-0] 4.) Ethan Van Ryan (6'1", 236/Heel) [0-0-0] 5.) "Sadistic Scholar" Sebastian Montague (6'4", 237/Face) [0-0-0] 6.) Terry 'Gunner' Vaughn (6'10", 280/Face) [0-0-0] III. News & Rumors - As the Hardcore Wrestling Alliance re-opens its' doors, there is heavy speculation around the New York Indy leagues that Terrence Steele has caught wind of the Hardcore Resurrection. If that is true, let this be a fair warning to all of the HWA Wrestlers. Especially EVR. - Already much has been made out of the signing of notorious street-fighter, Terry Vaughn. A lot of people are wondering why he is here and if the HWA is going to sanction his matches. For those unaware, "Gunner" as he's called, is known to be one of the deadliest shoot-fighters ever. And now he's bringing that to the HWA. - The first edition of Hardcore Tendencies is slated for May 4th. The show is subject to either a cancellation/post-ponement tho' due to the availability, or lack thereof, of the HWA Arena in Queens, NY. IV. Hardcore Tendencies Line-Up The Resurrection of Hardcore begins on May 4th, 2002! LIVE From the HWA Arena in Queens, New York. *OPENING MATCH* Taylor ThreaT vs. "The Homicidal Maniac" James Calscione As the Hardcore Wrestling Alliance plans to kick things off, it does so in a big way. When the first bell of the night rings, the HWA fanatics are going to be treated to a special opening bout of high-flying, martial arts. TAYLOR THREAT, one of the countries top Independent wrestlers takes on a virtual unknown in "THE HOMICIDAL MANIAC" JAMES CALSCIONE. Who knows what could happen when these two collide. *SECOND MATCH* H2O vs. Terry 'Gunner' Vaughn In the second match, there is no doubting if there will be bloodshed. The only question is how much. Two of the sickest individuals in the HWA will be hell bent on destroying one another when H2O, the unbalanced, psychotic warrior, takes on the infamous Street Fighter, TERRY 'GUNNER' VAUGHN. *MAIN EVENT* Ethan Van Ryan vs. "The Sadistic Scholar" Sebastian Montague In a clash of the 'veterans,' the final contest of the night will pit two HWA veterans against one another. In the past, altho' both men have been top tier HWA Stars, they've never met in the ring before. Well, all that ends May 4th when EEE-VEE-ARE takes on SEBASTIAN MONTAGUE, the HWA's resident Sadistic Scholar. Someone's going to be taught a lesson in Hardcore May 4th. DEADLINES: In-Rings/Strats are due SATURDAY, APRIL 27TH. #1st Eye of The Storm Packet release: FRIDAY, APRIL 26TH #2nd Eye of The Storm Packet release: FRIDAY, MAY 3RD. V. Closing Comments There it is, fellas. Let's get the ball rolling now. If any of you have questions, don't hesitate to ask. That's all from me. Hope you enjoy the show. I'm looking forward to it. Oh yeah, and if anyone wants to write a Match - feel free to volunteer. And of course, any angle ideas for the show any of you have - let me know. Take it easy guys, - Mike
