PX's Gift To FMW

29 September 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PX — 29 September 2012 Hello one and all, I have an announcement to make. I thought I'd never be back here again, but here I am, with a Gift for FMW. I'm sure the older members, at least those who remember me, will appreciate it.
FMW is going through its usual drama, so I thought I'd give you all a reminder of better times. I finally retrieved all the old show files, or most of them at least, off of the old hard drive. From what I can see, I have most shows from the 2.0 cycle right up to the 6.0 cycle: which I think is from Death Row to Ultimatum. I also appear to have every NEW show, for those who want to see the origins of PX, Celt, Matt Dunn, Cactus Sam and Skyler Striker among others.
I'll upload these shows sporadically, I can't give you a time frame for them. Along with the shows I have a lot of the promoes that go with them, so if you wrote something awesome in that time that you don't have saved, I can take a look for it. I plan on uploading at least some of those promos, such as the promo's for the original Circus Maximus.
I hope you all enjoy these vintage shows, there's some great stories in here to read. This is as good a way to mark my 1400th post as any, I think
Nicholas Gray — 29 September 2012 What I am about to type I mean 100%. You get NEW 3.3 (that was the Savant/Skyler dual debut WHERE I MADE YOU WHO YOU ARE etc etc yeah?) up, I will suck your dick.
PX — 30 September 2012 Can do, I'll throw it up now, since you asked so nicely EDIT: I'm posting the NEW shows in "Other", but if someone made a NEW section for them, that'd be great
Edible14 — 1 October 2012 PX, I just wanted to note that you are gentleman and a scholar. Much appreciated. PX — 1 October 2012 After posting Nick's debut, today I uploaded the oldest show in my catalogue from all the way back in 2006; the NEW Holiday Massacre. This show has to be one of my all time favourites, I implore you all to read it. It was the debut for everyone on that show, I think, the names that stand out on that show are myself, Celt and Guiomar. The rest of those guys faded away. Nevertheless, it was a hilarious show to read. Check it out.
