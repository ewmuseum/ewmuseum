I'm a champion. I'm your champion. Antonio stops at a table where a picture sits alone under a bright light. The camera zooms in on a snap of Alexis Machine, Antonio's tag partner at the upcoming show.
Then, not only to you insult me - a man whose only aim to to bring a little life, a little happiness to this godforsaken place - by tagging me up with an upstart. A loud mouth. The antithisis of what I stand for.
The Elephant Gun scruntches up the picture of his tag partner and frowns as he throws it to the ground.
You couldn't motivate your employees through increasing the quality of their opponents, so you try and incite action by partnering them with their opposites? Novel idea.
I am going to beat Kuruk again. Beat Sharpedo again. Then I will crush Alexis. Trying to cling on to what I have achieved? What I have fought for? No.
I have lost my motivation. I have lost my direction. But I will not lose this match. FMW...breath life into me. Breath life into your company. FMW just breath.
