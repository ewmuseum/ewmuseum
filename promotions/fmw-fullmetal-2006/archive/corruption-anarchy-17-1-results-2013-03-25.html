Abel Steele — 25 March 2013 We cut to a sold out Oracle Arena in Oakland, California. The crowd is hyped and as the camera pans to the ring suddenly the transmission cuts out.
As the screen comes back up we cut to a sombre looking P Thurston Devereaux, sitting behind the FMW General Manager’s Desk at the Ammunition Head Office.
Devereaux: Good evening. Fans across the world tonight I address you, not in my normal role as General Manager for the Ammunition Brand, but as a representative of the full board of Full Metal Wrestling.
He pauses a moment to let the importance of that statement settle in Devereaux: Tonight the news I have to deliver is perhaps the most dire in the history of Full Metal Wrestling, with consequences that will be felt for the rest of this company’s existence.
As you all know the broadcast of the Corruption & Anarchy event taped weeks ago at the Oracle Arena in Oakland has been delayed beyond what any of us imagined was possible.
Tonight I, regrettably, must inform you all that the broadcast of the show from Corruption & Anarchy 17.1 has been cancelled, pending the resolution of a stalemate in the Full Metal Boardroom over broadcast Royalties.
Sir Nick Bryson in his position as owner of Anarchy and with his control over much of the talent across all three brands of the Full Metal universe, has demanded that the revenue generated from the Corruption and Anarchy shows be re-negotiated immediately. The nature of those negotiations is confidential and a matter for the Board of Full Metal Wrestling however the impacts are not.
The Board has requested that the next show for the Ammunition Brand continue whilst the negotiations take place, however Sir Bryson has enforced his contractual rights to ensure that no other Full Metal Wrestling events, or talent may be booked until the matter currently before the Board is resolved.
The Board considers Sir Nick’s position to be untenable and as such we regrettably must inform you all, the fans, that there will be no Full Metal Wresting event booked for the foreseeable future.
Devereaux pauses here to take a sip of water, clearly emotional at the announcement he has just made. After taking a moment to compose himself he continues to read from pre-written speech.
Devereaux: Further, the Board of Full Metal Wrestling considers that the delay between shows has become such that they must act in some way to allow the fans a chance to see the superstars not contracted to Sir Nick Bryson in action.
It is with a heavy heart, but with the blessing of the Full Metal Board of Directors, that I announce that we have found a solution that does not breach our contractual position whilst allowing the Superstars to do what they do best.
Devereaux pauses over the next line, almost chocking on the words as they come out. Devereaux: A generous offer to host any and every member of the Full Metal Wrestling roster has been received by an independent third party. This company has generously offered to host any and all Full Metal superstars seeking a safe place to perform, free from the risk of legal prosecution. This company has no formal ties to either Sir Nick Bryson or to Full Metal Wrestling. It will be a safe haven while the impasse at Board level is resolved.
Tonight I announce our next “unofficial” Full Metal Wrestling Event proudly brought to you by our partners…
GOLD STANDARD WRESLING presents, live from The Bridgestone Arena in Nashville, Tennessee. "SANCTUARY" Devereaux: I urge you all to support this company as they bring you what will be an epic event featuring the best FMW can offer from all three of its brands, for one time only.
