Vendetta — 21 June 2012 Okay, so in an attempt to resurrect the forums, this is a game I used to run at an old fed. Rules are simple:
- I name some FMW characters, past and present. - You vote for what the result would be if they were in a match together. - After the first five votes/day/whatever I'll announce the winner based on votes. - Continue. If you're down, join in! If anybody has any problems with it, feel free to say. On his day he, frankly, easily edges out both Dunn and Anwyl and that's why he'd win.
