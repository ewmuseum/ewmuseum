Anwyl — 24 January 2011 Alrighty, after a bit of brainstorming by myself I have expanded on the idea of the FMW Video Game (recently posted a box art) I was thinking of using the MDickie game Wrestling Mpire 2008 (freely available through his site ) and modifying the textures and spritz to create an actual real life FMW Video Game!!!
Modifying the textures in MDickies games is completely legal (as seen at the Mpire Mall who modify everything to create a whole new wrestling experience for the MDickie titles). Before I was going to start on any textures and what not I want to know who should be in the roster (former characters, current roster etc.) including either who their character is and/or a base picture with their face in full view.
Divisions & Roster Ammunition: Coruption: Distortion: Anarchy: Alchemy: Anxiety: New Era Wrestling: Legends: Deceased: I need some help working out the rosters please!!! I would also like to know FMW Alumini from the Anarchy, Alchemy, Anxiety and Distortion!!!
