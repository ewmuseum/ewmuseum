[name withheld] — 22 February 2013 Hey guys! So the reason the show, and our moving forward, has stalled a little here is because I got a much larger response from people than I had anticipated.
With talking to these people, including a lot of surprises coming back, I decided to revamp the show entirely and take it in a different direction, because of all the talent!
Also, I'm really pleased to announce a few changes to the staff structure. I've had more than a few people take new commitments to staff, and were looking at seven new staff members. I'm also please to say that were introducing a scouting and recruiting team, helmed by Levi! Were looking at some creative ways of spreading the word and finding new talent outside of traditional e-fed channels, which will continue our trend of outside the box thinking.
Also, Abel Steele is taking over as Head Writer for the blue brand, and Braxton has agreed to support him in the understudy position! We also have an expanded senior staff, including a new creative team, with the designated purpose of grooming not just people with more experience, and responsibility, but to try and explore new ideas and creative streams.
A big thank you to the people who have stuck through and been patient. People like Loins, Eastwood, David, Bob, Marky Mark, and others. Also, another huge thanks to the people who have been willing to stand up when we reached out to them, for chartering territory that's is unknown for them, like Loins, Levi, David, and all of the other staffers coming in. A special thanks to Abel Steele, who is a living embodiment of honor.
I will be finishing things up and looking to post on Monday/Tuesday (finally!!) and I sincerely look forward to moving along and accomplishing great things with the rest of you again!
Thank you so much, everyone.
