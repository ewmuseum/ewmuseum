Full Metal Wrestling lived on a Forumotion board from 2006 to 2014 as a roleplay federation of numbered cycles. Its brands were Ammunition, run by the general manager P Thurston Devreaux, and Corruption and Anarchy, and for each cycle, 16.2, 16.3, 17.1, a voting and promo thread opened, then a trash-talk thread, then a results post narrated as a broadcast with commentators; the pay-per-views were Catalyst, staged in the fiction before a sold-out crowd in Toronto, and Sanctuary. Between shows the board ran games, Survivor, a wrestling fantasy league, Fact or Fiction and Clash of the Titans.

A post of 26 September 2012 asked members who had signed up to 'the new FMW' to announce their arrival, and one of 27 March 2013 says the response had been larger than expected and that the show and its staff would be revamped. The board's last thread, opened on 15 October 2013, begins 'I miss this place. RIP FMW.' and runs on in replies to 7 March 2014.
