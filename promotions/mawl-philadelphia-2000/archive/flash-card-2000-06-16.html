MAWL (Philadelphia, 2000) — Card Archive

Flash Card

16 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@ THE MIDDLE ATLANTIC WRESTLING LEAGUE presents... FLASH CARD - Friday, June 16th, 2000 Live from the MAWL Arena - Philadelphia, Pennsylvania http://mawl.virtualave.net Containing live interviews from: Hypnosis, The Kreibs & “Kid Ego” Tucker Warren @@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@ [The lights go out and the crowd rises to their feet and boo. Different color lights flash through out the crowd. "Dragula" begins to play over the
PA System. The lyric pick up and a spotlight goes to the top of the
Tuck-Tron. The Kreibs and Breanna walk out side by side. The Kreibs wearing his flashy shirt, khaki pants, North Carolina hat, and the MAWL Vicious
Title on his shoulder. Breanna is wearing a Tommy Hilfiger top and pants.
The Kreibs gives the crowd the bird. He grabs a microphone and begins to talk.] The Kreibs: I swear to god each week you morons get dumber and dumber! [The crowd begins to chant "ASSHOLE!".] The Kreibs: You realize that I don't care if you guys cheer or boo me. You guys are so retarded that no one cares any more. But what I care about my
Vicious Title. See I am trying to defend my title as much as I can. I have already challenged Evenflow to a match for my title. See I am all about opportunity or trying to make the biggest winning streak ever. See this
Saturday at Breaking Point I ant to defend my Vicious Title against
Evenflow. Evenflow is a young man who deserves to get a decent spot on TV. I have seen his matches and he is pretty good. I mean when The Kreibs challenges you, you have to accept. I am the future of MAWL some day I will be headlining pay per views, wait! I already am! But anyways. Evenflow I need someone to beat to get some momentum for this Sunday. So at Breaking
Point in a Vicious Title match me and you! What do ya' say? Now I have to address my opponent this Sunday... Chris Register.
[The Kreibs and Breanna walk down to ringside. Breanna gets in the ring. The
Kreibs digs under the ring and finds a ladder. He slides it in the ring and he follows. He sets up the ladder.]
The Kreibs: Chris this Sunday I will beat you from pillar to post in this very ring. Only there will be a cage around us and it will be a ladder match as well. See Chris I have to say that you are jealous of me. You brought me here to MAWL and then I became better than you! It's that simple! We had high hopes and we set goals. I have made mine and you haven’t quite have gotten there. I mean I am a superstar and you are a nobody! You are a tag team partner with Hypnosis! Come on, that's horrible. Hypnosis has to be the worst wrestler ever. If he walks away with the Mid Atlantic Title this
Sunday, our ratings will drop! The only way we could save them is by having
The Kreibs VS Hypnosis for a main event! Good god. I was listening to Hypnosis talk and I just about fell asleep! Hypnosis I will get you. Watch out. Now back to Chris Register. She Chris I have climbed the ladder of success in MAWL.
[The Kreibs climbs to the top of the ladder.] The Kreibs: This Sunday will be no different. When I make you bleed and then capture my MAWL Vicious Title. So Chris what are you gonna do? Now hit my music!
["Dragula" hits and the camera pans across tho the announce table as The
Kreibs and his valet leave the ring area.]
Michael Reed: Welcome to the MAWL Arena in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, the last flash card prior to our big pay per view event in Quake of Fire. I’m
Michael Reed and joining me tonight is new announce team member Chris
Coleman filling in for Ben Tuckwell who is finalizing stuff for Sunday
Night. Good evening Chris.
Chris Coleman: Hey Michael and fans, how are things? Some of you at home might know me from back in Ring Rage Wrestling but if you don’t, my name is
Chris Coleman and I’ll be bringing you a new segment on MAWL television called In-Depth Interviews to be held twice a month on Wednesday Afternoons.
Those not familiar with the format, it’s basically a one-on-one interview with one of the MAWL’s top wrestlers. Should be excellent and I’m looking forward to hosting it.
Michael Reed: Anyway, what you just saw was The Kreibs speaking about his upcoming future in the MAWL and his match against Chris Register but one thing that surprises me is he had the fan support and decided to abuse them here tonight. Maybe it’s just nerves but that’s not a good way to go into a pay per view.
Chris Coleman: Upon demand from both wrestlers, Chris Register and Hypnosis will be fighting here tonight for the rights to both of the tag team titles and to choose a new partner. Let’s go to the ring and await this big contest.
********************************** SINGLES MATCH FOR TAG TEAM TITLES Chris Register vs. Hypnosis ********************************** Hypnosis managed to contain an explosive Chris Register early, which led to a victory for the MAWL Vice President. Register looked home following a big powerbomb but Hypnosis managed to escape the Critical Condition hold and lock Register in his own hold, the Hypnotiser for the submission victory.
WINNER: Hypnosis ********************************** [Trent Lyon stands beside the ring awaiting Hypnosis to exit the ring. His music dies down and an out of breath Hypnosis accepts the interview with Lyon.]
Trent Lyon: What have you got to say about the match? Hypnosis: The match. well it was a good one I gotta admit, but in the end, the worst wrestler got his ass kicked, and the best in the business is standing here with both of the tag title belts. The best newcomer is another notch in the win column for me... now i move on to MAWL's Impact player. The
East Coast Champion. The Middle Atlantic Champion, and the Commissioner of
MAWL. Dark Wolf. Tomorrow night i will claim the Middle Atlantic title as well, proving i am the greatest entertainer to enter the squared circle. The best singles wrestler. The best tag wrestler. The best.
Trent Lyon: Now the question that everyone wants to ask. Who is your new tag partner. Hypnosis: That, Mullins, is something even I don't know. Trent Lyon: So you mean you haven't even asked anyone yet. Hypnosis: Do I really need to ask anyone. I'll be in my locker room. MAWL wrestlers. You know where to find me.
[With that Hypnosis' music starts up again and Hypnosis walks off with one tag belt over his shoulder, and one round his waist.]
Michael Reed: A good win for Hypnosis and the question still remains… who will hold the tag team title belts with him going into Quake of Fire on
Sunday. Got anything to say about this Chris?
Chris Coleman: Nah. I’ve got something else though. Ben Tuckwell mentioned to me something about announcing the new MAWL signings so I’ll begin with that right away. This week, Damien Mullins in his recruiting efforts has managed to pick up Ric Beauty and Studd Muffin, two men the ladies will definitely enjoy to watch is all I have to say about them. Continuing with the interviews, I think we have one from a young 17 year old who has a big future ahead of him according to MAWL officials… Tucker Warren!
[Fans are packed to the brim in the MAWL arena in Philly, PA. The fans are stoked for the upcoming PPV and the 5000 strong fans are frothing at the mouths for some more MAWL action. After some wonderment of who’s next, 'It's
All About Me' by Sisqo and Mya kicks in over the PA system. Foreign to this music, the fans don’t really know what is going to happen next. Then the crowd erupts as 'Kid Ego' Tucker Warren strolls out through the curtains.
Kid Ego, decked out in faded carpenter jean shorts, a neon green t-shirt that reads 'It's All About Ego' in black lettering, and a pair of blue and gray Asics cross trainers, slaps hands with his fans who are stretching over the guard rail to try and get a touch of the newest MAWL superstar. Da Kid runs his fingers through his bleach blond hair as he steps onto the ring apron. Smiling that grin that only he can smile he turns to the crowd and thrusts his arms into the air to which the crowd reacts with an average pop.
Warren grins some more, leap frogs the top rope, calls for a mic and gets it.] Warren: "Ya know with a reaction like this..." [Stops to grin at all his fans.] Warren: "It's obvious to me that the all the AWESOME fans in MAWL realize that..." [The crowd gives a decent pop as Kid Ego pauses for dramatic suspense.] Warren: "IT'S ALL ABOUT EGO!!!!" [Some of the crowd cheers the young man while others sit in silence, waiting to see what else he has to say.]
Warren: "Well I am glad to see all my Ego Maniacs showed up in full force to see their hero, moi, prepare for his big matches at the upcoming Pay Per
View. Well so far all the others in the tourney have been pretty quiet so it appears that Da Kid will have to do all of their talking for them."
[Flashes that million dollar smile.] Warren: "The man I face in the first round, Griff, is a guy who I'm sure doesn’t realize what kinda mess he has been placed in. Griff, in all honesty, there probably isn't a WORSE place for you to be than in Buffalo this Sunday. Because when you step into that ring you are going to be put into a wrestling clinic courtesy of 'Kid Ego' Tucker Warren because I am going to show the world what kind of class talent that MAWL has signed.
Because come this Sunday Griff-o the book is going to be throw at you, chapter by STINKING CHAPTER. The world of wrestling is going to see a TRUE high flyer, no one is better than Da Kid, show you how its done. Then, at will might I add, I am going to turn you into a pretzel Griff when I show the world submission display like they have never seen."
[Shakes his head as if to say to all of his Ego Maniac’s 'poor guy'.] Warren: "Like I said Griff, I almost feel bad for you because I'm sure that in the contract that you signed for MAWL, no where did you see the clause about getting your rear end handed to you on a Pay Per View. The moral of the story Griff-o? Read the fine print!"
[Da Kid once again flashes his grin.] Warren: "And then when I dispatch Griff I am going to have a few more challengers trying to get what I already consider MY cruiserweight title. To the other challengers, whoever they may end up being, you guys aren't going to be any better off than my buddy Griff was. You see you guys may end up being in AN EVEN WORSE spot because a title is at stake. And when it’s all said
done in the business people are ALWAYS going to remember the titles that you won. And with titles comes respect and those are the two things that Da Kid is looking for here in this great sport of Pro Wrestling. So with that in mind, knowing how BADLY that Griff was embarrassed, well, after the three way elimination match..."
[Grins as the fans once again.] Warren: "You fellas are going think Griff GOT OFF EASY!!!! So to all the players in this tournament to give 'Kid Ego' Tucker Warren his cruiserweight belt I say good luck..."
[Shakes his head and smiles.] Warren: "CAUSE YOUR ALL GOING TO NEED IT!!!" [Da Kid begins to step out of the ring. He then pauses, steps back in and says one last thing..]
Warren: "Oh yeah, Dark Wolf or Hypnosis, thanks for keeping MY BELT warm, cause it's only going to be a LITTLE BIT!!!"
[ Da Kid hops over the top rope and lands on his feet on the arena floor. He goes through the crowd as the fans mob him to chat and hopefully get an autograph.]
Michael Reed: He’ll be a fan favorite one day, that I can say for sure after being signed to a long term contract by the MAWL. He’s got the appeal needed and it’s only a matter of time and good luck to him. The Tuck-Tron in this building is starting to light up so let’s see what’s about to happen.]
[The Tuck-Tron comes to life and there is a camera located outside Hypnosis' door. As the camera approaches the door voices are heard inside. The camera pokes in the door but Hypnosis is obstructing the view of the mystery person.]
Mystery: Hypnosis, I think I am the man for you to choose. Hypnosis: Why. You haven't proved anything. Mystery: True, but if I [BLEEP!] up you can just split up the tag team like that. But I won't [BLEEP!] up. I've even got a great gimmick for us.
Hypnosis: Hold on a second. [Hypnosis turns around to see the camera poking through the door. He grabs the camera and throws it to the floor and the screen turns to static.]
Chris Coleman: That was from just after Hypnosis’ victory. That’s all we’ve got tonight I’m looking to catch up with the Middle Atlantic Champion following Quake of Fire on Wednesday Afternoon, whoever it may be. Goodnight on behalf of myself and Michael Reed.
Michael Reed: Hypnosis hasn’t named his partner yet and Ben Tuckwell has the right to strip him of the titles if necessary.
[The lights go out in the arena and the arena is silent.] Chris Coleman: Who is this. We have no more scheduled interviews. I hate it when people mess with the schedule!!!
[A blue glow suddenly engulfs the arena and "Murder Ink" by Dr. Dre plays on the PA system.]
Michael Reed: To my knowledge, no-one in the fed has that music, or has requested a change in music.
[As white pyro continuously streams up from either side of the entrance, two silhouettes are seen. The figure on the left lifts a microphone to his mouth.]
Hypnosis: It's me MAWL. Didn't ya miss me. I got the announcement to make. Chris Coleman: Damn that Hypnosis. Hypnosis: I introduce you to your new Tag Team Champions. MURDER INC. [The Tuck-Tron responds with the Murder Inc. logo being shown.] Hypnosis: And now introducing to you, the second member of Murder Inc. [A white spotlight starts at the bottom of the ramp and moves slowly up the ramp. The other figure steps forward with one of the tag straps held high in the air. As the spotlight reaches the figure Hypnosis introduces his new partner.]
Hypnosis: G.L.C. !!! [MAWL cameras fade out with a picture of G.L.C holding the title above his head with Murder Ink still playing in the background.]
