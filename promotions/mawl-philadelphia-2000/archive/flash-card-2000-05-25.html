MAWL (Philadelphia, 2000) — Card Archive

Flash Card

25 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@ THE MIDDLE ATLANTIC WRESTLING LEAGUE presents... FLASH CARD - Thursday, May 25th, 2000 Live from the MAWL Arena - Philadelphia, Pennsylvania Website: http://www.crosswinds.net/~mawl @@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@ [No Leaf Clover” by Metallica begins play in the background, becoming louder and louder with every second of music. The new yellow MAWL logo flies in from the back of the screen until it is situated just above the center of the screen. As the electric guitar kicks in, the Flash Card image stamps itself onto the screen in blood red writing, just below the MAWL logo. We then fade into the newly built MAWL arena in Philadelphia where 5000 fans are jammed into the relatively small complex. We then pan to the announce table containing Michael Reed and he is joined by MAWL President Ben
Tuckwell.] Ben Tuckwell: Welcome to the second edition of the Middle Atlantic Wrestling League presentation of Flash Card! I'm disappointed to say that both of tonight's matches have been cancelled with "The Franchise" Ron Jolla and
"Superstar" Shawn Saxon being released from their MAWL duties. Michael Reed: Yes. Mr. Tuckwell announced earlier today that he will be making some big cuts after this week's Breaking Point and he's started tonight by firing Saxon and Jolla, who haven't been at their full potential lately. There are a few others under the pump, reportedly including Billy
Heal, Coiler and a couple of others. Ben Tuckwell: Deservedly so. I don't want any half-assed performances in the Middle Atlantic Wrestling League towards my goal of world domination of the wrestling industry. Even if I've only got ten wrestlers, if they're the real deal and are wanting to succeed, I guarantee that they will.
Michael Reed: So who has put their hand up for interviews tonight Ben? Ben Tuckwell: We've got two former DU members in The Kreibs and Chris Register speaking out about the split plus The Brothers Grimm are to take part. Just an announcement though about The Brothers Grimm... the referees committee have decided that they are to be banned from using masks in their matches.
Michael Reed: That's a good decision Ben. It must've been hell for the referees out there on Saturday night and they need to be completely focused for the big tag team title match at Breaking Point.
Ben Tuckwell: Hey, I didn't make the decision. I love The Brothers Grimm and I can't believe I swooped onto talent that good. We've got a whole crop of great new talent and I just hope all of the youngsters can establish themselves as stars of the future. I'm behind every one of them 100 percent.
Michael Reed: Talking about new signings, I hear you've signed two relative wrestling veterans. Any more info on this?
Ben Tuckwell: Yeah, I've signed "The Perfectionist" Stefan Andrews and Impetigo, both are 30 years of age and big stars of the business. I'm hoping that experience mixed with young blood will provide the combination needed for success.
Michael Reed: Good luck to both of those guys. I think it's time we began with tonight's interviews so bring 'em out.
("Dragula" by Rob Zombie blasts over the MAWL PA System. The crowd rises and cheers. The Tuck-Tron goes to the back of a limo. The Kreibs and Breanna are sitting in the back and ready to speak.)
The Kreibs: Well, Well, Well, looks like I am the man! I am still undefeated here in MAWL! I beat The Cleamson-Heal Connection single handedly, and I beat Shawn Saxon. I will stay undefeated here in MAWL this Saturday as I take on Ice for The Vicious Title.
(The crowd roars.) The Kreibs: Well now I wanna address Chris Register, that Kreibs wanna-be. See Register it's nothing personal, but I just wanna get my hands on some gold. We have know each other for a long time. But, we have never, ever met in the ring. None The Soul Retreiver, Dark Carnival, we never have faced each other. I can tell you why too. I am better! It's True, It's True.
Thanks [name withheld]. Register I am told that I have to face you in a Vicious Title match next Saturday at Breaking Point. Well I am all cool with that! Since,
I am the soon to be Champion. I get to choose what kind of match we have! A Cage Match! That's right Chris a cage match! You have never ever won a cage match in your life and never will! So Chris how bad and cocky will you be next Saturday! We will find out. I am awesome!
(The Kreibs and Breanna smile at each other.) The Kreibs: I have got tons of e-mails and letters saying, "Why Kreibs, Why? Why break away from DU?" Well it's real simple. I thought geez we have had fun for so long. Then I thought is this getting me anywhere? What about me?
Then I broke away from DU and reunited with Breanna. That is the answer to you question. No get outta here!
(The Kreibs shoves the camera man out of the limo. The camera fades.) Michael Reed: That was The Kreibs keeping it short and sweet and what a match that could be. Register and Kreibs for the Vicious Title at Breaking
Point in just over a week's time inside of a steel cage. Ben Tuckwell: Hmm. I'm thinking about how to improve that match a little but let me get back to you on it. There's so many options for a cage match it's just not funny.
Michael Reed: Whatever it is, I'll have a great view and it should be a joy to watch. (The house lights dim and the loudspeakers come to life. "Ladies and Gentlemen, the Mid Atlantic Wrestling League, in association with the Mirage Resort in beautiful Las Vegas, proudly present to you the masters of mystery and mayhem: THE BROTHERS GRIMM!" Double flashpots explode on the entrance ramp and Jim and Tim dance their way through the smoke as the beginning notes of "Hot Lava" fill the air. The song, however, is almost drown out by the chorus of boos coming from the sold-out arena. The Brothers seem not to notice, and continue dancing and smiling down the aisle. Jim looks pretty comfortable in his shorts, sandals, and Las Vegas Thunder hockey jersey.
Tim's long hair bounces around wildly, complementing his black leather boots, pants, and Harley Davidson Cafe muscle tee. The ugliness from the crowd continues as TBG enter the ring. A few crumpled cups come flying at them, thanks to the front row of fans. Tim sees one coming and manages to duck, but the cup continues on its way and hits Jim full in the face, soaking him pretty good. The audience likes it a lot. Jim looks momentarily upset, but Tim pats him on the back and raises a microphone to his mouth.)
Tim: This is NOT the kind of reception I would have expected from the City of Brotherly Love.
Jim: Yeah, don't you idiots know that you're not going to find better brothers than us? Tim: Philadelphia, we won't take up a lot of your time... (Huge pop from the crowd.) Tim: But we wanted to make you aware of two things. Jim: And since most of you can't walk and fart at the same time, we'll go over this really sloooooooooowly for you.
Tim: First and formost, we're up here to do our part for our newly found home, MAWL: Tickets went on sale for Quake of Fire PPV this morning, and it's not sold out yet!
Jim: What's wrong with you people? Tim: Don't you know that The Brothers Grimm are going to be on the card? Jim: We are? Tim: Well, the SECOND thing we came out here to make all you fine people aware of is the fact that today, this very day, is our two week anniversary in this wonderful federation.
Jim: And we're getting a chance at the tag team belts on Saturday! Hell, we'll most likely be champs at the PPV!
Tim: So there you have it: The Brothers Grimm at Quake of Fire on June 18. Mark it on your calendar. Jim: And buy those tickets! Tim: Thanks. Jim: See ya! (Litter and boos greet The Brothers Grimm as they exit the ring, and accompany them all the way back down the aisle.)
Ben Tuckwell: I don't like missiles projected from the crowd but thankfully all glass bottles are banned from the MAWL arena because those things could be lethal in the wrong hands.
Michael Reed: Marine Midland Arena is the place of MAWL's second pay per view entitled Quake of Fire and it should be a great card. Remember, tickets are still on sale for this upcoming event so head over to Buffalo, New York and pick up your tickets from the nearest outlet.
Ben Tuckwell: I KNOW! If The Kreibs manages to beat Ice for the Vicious Title at Breaking Point, it shall be Chris Register and Kreibs inside of a steel cage with a ladder situated in the middle of the ring, the title above it. That definitely will be vicious and all the fans should be cheering on
Kreibs so they can get this match to happen. Michael Reed: Excellent. I can see a lot of blood being shed during a match like that with the personal conflict between the two. Next week shall be
SPECTACULAR! Ben Tuckwell: Oh no, I'm not booking this for Breaking Point... you've got to be kidding me Michael Reed? This is the first match booked for Quake of
Fire on June 18th and it should be one of the highlights of that card. Michael Reed: And two weeks after that, MAWL vs. NAWC: Collision Course is to be held but this card is still in doubt with the NAWC's recent break. I'm prayin' it goes ahead because it could be the opportunity of a lifetime for those wrestlers and could be a chance to prove the MAWL as a top league.
Ben Tuckwell: Even if it doesn't go ahead at the planned date, it will take place soon. Another announcement I need to make is that we've signed a partnership detail with the IEA and a supercard between us and them is to be held in the latter part of this year.
Michael Reed: You're watching MAWL, the league of the future and we've got one more interview to go for tonight's festivities if you wish to call them that.
(The lights in the MAWL arena slowly start to go dim until darkness engulfs the entire crowd. The sold out crowd starts to scream, but is suddenly stopped as an explosion roars throughout the arena. Huge orange flames come shooting from the ring entrance and in big bold neon green letters the word
"REGISTER" flashes across the Tuck-Tron, followed by a huge neon green money symbol. "Nobody's Real" begins to play over the PA system as the lights switch back on. Standing in the ring entrance with his arms outstretched is the one, the only, CHRIS REGISTER! The crowd instantly starts to boo. Chris looks over the crowd and then raises a microphone to his mouth.)
Register: Thank you ladies and gentlemen. I'm glad to have such a warm welcome because I'm feeling so darn good today. You see, I was in the back when I ran into one of The Kreibs' best friends in the whole world!
(By this time Chris has made his way halfway down the ramp and the crowd has quieted down, listening to what Chris has to say.)
Register: We had never met before, so I told her who I was. I tell you, The Kreibs sure picks his friends wisely. This girl, she's got to be one of the best! (Chris walks up the ring steps and enters the ring. He smiles and turns towards the Tuck-Tron.)
Register: In fact, I think she needs a round of applause. She knows who she is...guys, please escort her out here!
(From behind the curtain two guys walk through the ring entrance, and with them they have one of The Kreibs' best friends...a blow-up doll. Everyone in the crowd begins to laugh, but then they realize what they are laughing about and the laughing turns to boos directed towards Chris.)
Register: Look at her. She's got the best plastic skin a girl could have. Look at that mouth, all puckered up like that. Isn't she adorable? (The crowd starts to boo louder. Chris looks around and grins.) Register: Alright, I've seen enough of her. Get The Kreibs' girlfriend out of here. Lets everybody have a round of applause for her.
(Chris starts clapping for her, and is the only one in the whole arena doing so.) Register: Now that I've had my comic relief, onto business. It looks like by coming out here on Tuesday paid off. Not only did I piss of The Kreibs, but
I got my well deserved match with him. The only thing left to figure out is...what kind of match will it be? Now, I could beat The Kreibs in just about any way possible, you name it and I'll beat him. Hell, his rubber girlfriend would put up a better fight. But over the years, I've developed a tendency. Out of all the matches I've been in, out of all the matches I've won, the only one I can't seem to conquer is...the cage match. Kreibs knows this, he knows this very well. And after thinking this over, I realized that
I have a very golden opportunity here. You see, I can make my very first cage match win special by not only winning, but by beating my FORMER friend
The Kreibs. Wouldn't that be a treat? (Chris pauses for a moment, and then continues.) Register: But enough about The Kreibs, lets talk about W. Life. The first question I have is, who the hell is that? The second question I have is, why the hell am I wrestling him? Two very well put questions. Now, if anybody can tell me, I will be amazed. Is there anybody in the crowd that knows the answer?
(Everybody in the crowd is silent.) Register: It looks like all you buffons are as stumped as me. So here's what I'm going to do. I'm just going to show up this Saturday night, I'm going to find this W. Life character, I'm going to ask him these very questions, and then I'm going to knock his teeth so far down his throat that he'll have to stick his toothbrush up his ass to brush them.
(The crowd starts to boo as Chris grins.) Register: Thank you, and have a nice evening. ("Nobody's Real" starts to play over the PA system as Chris goes to the second rope to pose. The crowd boos louder, Chris exits the ring and makes his way up the ramp blowing kisses as he goes before he leaves.)
Ben Tuckwell: Chris Register sure knows how to put on a great show when it comes to interviews and he's one to back up his words as well, making The
Kreibs and Register match even more unpredictable. Michael Reed: That's all the time we've got tonight but next week's flash cards will contain matches that will go ahead after Ben Tuckwell cuts the contracts of several MAWL wrestlers. Goodnight everyone.
