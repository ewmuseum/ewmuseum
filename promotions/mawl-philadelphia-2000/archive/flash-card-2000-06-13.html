MAWL (Philadelphia, 2000) — Card Archive

Flash Card

13 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@ THE MIDDLE ATLANTIC WRESTLING LEAGUE presents... FLASH CARD - Friday, June 9th, 2000 Live from the MAWL Arena - Philadelphia, Pennsylvania http://mawl.virtualave.net Containing live interviews from: GLC, Hypnosis & The Kreibs @@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@ [Fireworks go off and the fans cheer. The official MAWL logo appears on the center of the television screen and then five seconds later, the official
MAWL logo fades away. All of a sudden, the lights dim, the curtains spread open, and the spotlight shines on the top of the ramp way, and the fans rise to their feet and begin to wonder what MAWL superstar will come out next.
"Electric Head part 2" by White Zombie and G.L.C. and Mini G.L.C. walk from out of the curtains and into the spotlight as they both look exactly the same, short dark brown hair with short bangs hanging in the front, dark brown eyebrows, dark brown eyes, dark brown eyelashes, a light tan, and somewhat of muscular bodies and are both seen wearing a black bandana, black tanktop t-shirt, black Khakis, and black boots with white lacings. They both stop at the top of the ramp way and they continue walk down the aisle as the fans in the aisle way pad them both on the back and shoulders and they shrug it off. They approach the black steel steps and they climb the black steel steps, they step onto the ring apron, and enter the ring as "Electric Head part 2" by White Zombie fades away. And they stand in the center of the ring and G.L.C. pulls a microphone from out of his back pocket and holds the microphone up to his chin.]
G.L.C.: Hello Middle Atlantic Wrestling League! [The fans give a mix reaction of boos and cheers.] G.L.C.: It's me, Gabriel Luis Coronel but you all moronic fans of mine can call me ... G.L.C.!
[The fans boo.] G.L.C.: And this little man right is ... [G.L.C. bends down and holds the microphone up to Mini G.L.C.'s chin.] Mini G.L.C.: .... [G.L.C. stands up and holds the microphone up to his chin.] G.L.C.: That's right folks, Mini G.L.C. is his name! We are now here in the
MAWL after signing ourselves a contract for a couple of years. So you'll be seeing this pretty face for years to come ... And ...
[G.L.C. sighs and looks down at Mini G.L.C. and then continues to speak.] G.L.C.: And this pretty face too! I know all of you dip-(no audio) will be enjoying our company.
[The fans boo.] G.L.C.: What? Why are you booing us? Is it the part about you all being moronic dip-(no audio)?
[The fans boo.] G.L.C.: Ah! I see! Anyway, onto business ... We're here to capture ourselves some gold and some victories, isn't that right Mini Me ... Oh (no audio) ...
I mean, Mini Myself. Mini G.L.C.: ... G.L.C.: That's right! So, I would like to put out a challenge to anyone in this promotion at the next upcoming event. So, I want an answer in twenty-four hours. The cock ... Oh (no audio) ... I mean, the clock is ticking, tick tock, tick tock, tick tock, tick tock! Would you like to say anything before we leave Mini Myself?
Mini G.L.C.: ... G.L.C.: No? I guess not! So M-A-W-L prepare for me ... G.L.C. and Mini
G.L.C.! We're here and we're ready! Wait are we ... Oh yeah ... We are!
Good-bye for now my loyal trustful dip-(no audio) stupid (no audio) fans! [The fans boo. G.L.C. tosses the microphone down on the ring mat and G.L.C. and Mini G.L.C. pose for cameras and flashes are seen through-out the arena. They both leave the ring and slowly walk to the back locker room flipping off the fans while smiling and they go through the curtains and the curtains close. We pan across to the announce table seating Michael Reed and Ben
Tuckwell.]
Michael Reed: Welcome to the first of two flash cards prior to Quake of Fire on Sunday night. You just witnessed new signing G.L.C. accompanied by his mini counterpart conduct their first MAWL interview and I'm pretty impressed with it all. I'm Michael Reed and joining me as always in none other than the owner and operator himself... Ben Tuckwell. Good evening Ben and I'm sure you've got more big announcements to make as usual, don't you?
Ben Tuckwell: Yeah, I suppose you could say that. A couple new signings as usual thanks to the good work of Damien Mullins and they are none other than
Kodiak and Erik Rave, the latter formerly of the promotion Ring Rage
Wrestling, the company from which we signed our latest announce member in
Chris Coleman who will be hosting In-Depth Interviews, a new 15 minute show to be held when scheduled.
Michael Reed: That's great. Unlike past flash cards, we do not have any matches tonight so wrestlers can concentrate on their matches at the upcoming pay per view event. This card will be made up entirely of interviews from the top superstars of the MAWL plus a couple of rookies as well. Let's get underway as quickly as possible with more monologues from our wrestlers.
(The lights go out and the crowd rises to their feet and chant, "Kreibs." Flashing lights go all around the MAWL Arena. The lights stop. "Dragula" begins to play on the MAWL PA System. The lyrics begin to play as a spotlight goes to the top of the MAWL Tron. The Kreibs and Breanna walk out side by side. The Kreibs is wearing a flashy shirt, khaki pants, a North
Carolina hat, and The MAWL Vicious Title around his waist. Breanna is wearing her usual Tommy Hilfiger top and pants. The Kreibs smiles at his fans. The Kreibs and Breanna walk down to the ring side by side. The Kreibs gets up on thew apron and holds the middle rope open for Breanna. Breanna gets in and The Kreibs follows. The Kreibs grabs a mic.)
The Kreibs: Well my undefeated streak is still alive! Yes I am truly the best wrestler in MAWL today! See I didn't beat Chris Register, but I didn't loose to him. All in all Chris gave me a run for my money. When I almost had him beat he grabbed a chair and tried to hit me with it. Then when that didn't keep me down, he brought out the biggest piece of shit in the world,
Hypnosis! It took the best Tag Team in MAWL to take out me! Chris I know that I could beat you any day that I want! It is real simple, This Sunday at
Quake Of Fire, I guarantee there will be blood shed and it won't be mine!
And lets say if Hypnosis wants to climb in our cage then I will have someone there to make sure that he doesn't help Mr. Register. If he does however, I can personally guarantee that he won't walk away with the Mid Atlantic
Championship! Today I was thinking up strategy in the back, then I wandered by the Quake Of Fire Card! Chris is you are not too winded by defending you
Tag Team Title, then I guess you will give me a challenge. Oh yeah,
Hypnosis. What a big piece f shit! My god. He leaves and comes back. The he says that I am scared and I hide behind things. Hypnosis, who attacks people from behind? Who attacks a man when he is about to win a match! You obviously. Hypnosis I am gonna get you in a match some time soon. I know it will be a good match. I mean anything with me in it is a good match! So
Hypnosis you better watch your back this Sunday and maybe Saturday night, also.
(The crowd roars. The Kreibs smiles.) The Kreibs: Which brings me to this Saturday Night. I know Evenflow beat
Chris Cain. That means he gets a Vicious Title shot. Right! So Evenflow, I am challenging you to a Vicious Title match this Saturday at Breaking Point!
Evenflow is just some young punk that I will beat and then go on to defeat the biggest piece of scum in the world, Chris Register. Now I want to address all you morons! Yes I know you love me, cheer for me, and worship me. But, the fact is you are all a bunch of fat morons! Hey I only speak the truth!
(The crowd boos and starts to throw things.) The Kreibs: You are also very rude and unattractive as I can see. You are all a bunch of pig vomits! You look like pigs and you make me wanna vomit!
AHAHAHAHAHAH....! (The crowd boos and chants, "asshole".) The Kreibs: I see you guys have a potty mouth too. So Evenflow, Chris Register, and Hypnosis watch out for The Kreibs, because he is that damn good! Hit the music.
("Dragula" blasts over the PA System. Kreibs and Brenna mock the crowd as they leave the ringside area.)
Michael Reed: The Kreibs has first say before his big match at Quake of Fire and he seems pretty confident of winning, already challenging Evenflow to a
Vicious Title match at the event following it which is Saturday Night
Breaking Point. Ben Tuckwell: You're right Michael and I bet some of those men he talked about will have some comments to reply to this interview, especially Chris
Register. Register isn't in the house tonight from my knowledge but then again, I've been out here since the start and basically anyone could've turned up.
Michael Reed: Alright Ben. We've got one more interview scheduled for the night and that is from the challenger at this week's pay per view in
Hypnosis. His opponent, the Middle Atlantic and East Coast Champion is Dark
Wolf and you obviously weren't happy with Dark Wolf's announcement at
Breaking Point, were you Ben? Ben Tuckwell: Absolutely not. For one, he got Moxlow released from the company and although I may not like him, he was quite a good asset to the
MAWL and it's a shame to see him go. Not only that, but he announced himself as MAWL Commissioner and proceeded to change the rules to allow him to hold both of the big titles at the same time. Not to fear though, he may prove me wrong and end up a really good commish, but I guess only time will tell eh?
Michael Reed: Yeah. Our final interview tonight is Hypnosis so let's go to him now. **Hypnosis steps out to the crowd of deafening boos as "Come With ME" by Puff Daddy plays. Hypnosis walks up to one of the crowd and goes to slap there hand then as they swing he moves out the way and laughs. He continues to taunt the crowd until he runs down the ramp and jumps onto the apron. He looks at the crowd as the red pyro follows him up the aisle. The crowd start to throw rubbish at him, but he easily completes the somersault over the top rope. He stands in the ring with his arms outstretched and an H is formed by pyrotechnics behind him. The music dies down and eventually so does the crowd. Hypnosis puts the microphone up to his mouth and before he has the chance to say anything an "Ass-hole" chant starts.**
Hypnosis: Shut up, or I'll give you something to cram up your own one. And it'll be my foot.
**The crowd start booing him loudly again.** Hypnosis: I've got a few bitches to talk about today. Register. Dark Wolf, but right now. Kreibs.
**The crowd start to cheer the Vicious champion.** Hypnosis: Yeah, whatever. Cheer the loser. Now I haven't got much to say to you, punk. You came out here and talked smack to me, and what do you get from it. An ass kicking from The Hypnotic One. You've got to admit it. Me and Chris beat you so bad that you couldn't even stand up. What kind of
Vicious Champion gets carried out flat on his back. As I said before I don't want your title, I don't need your title, quite simply. **bleep** your title.
Daniel Mullins: Surely you've got to admit he's going over the line now
Ben!!! Ben Tuckwell: It was bleeped wasn't it. I knew he was gonna say it. I got the tech crew to bleep whenever I press this button. Check it out.
Hypnosis: I just **bleep**. I said I wanna **bleep**. Who's doing that. **By this point Ben Tuckwell is in hysterics over his actions, as are most of the crowd. Hypnosis goes down to the prez and stares at him. He just looks at Hypnosis and waves his finger at him in a "No you won't" manner.
Hypnosis grabs the president's button and throws it to the floor, crushing it under his foot. Hypnosis climbs back into the ring.**
Ben Tuckwell: Damn you Hypnosis. That equipment cost a friggen' fortune! Hypnosis: So Tuckwell. you think you're smart, and Dark Wolf you think you're smart by making yourself Commissioner. Well I've got something to top both of you.
**Hypnosis calls to someone at ringside who brings in the MAWL rulebook. Hypnosis flicks through the pages and then stops.** Hypnosis: Here we go. Sit back and prepare to be beaten, intellectually. As Dark Wolf correctly read before "If the MAWL has only one owner, a commissioner can be named. The man wishing for that position, can claim it for himself." Dark Wolf, well done for doing that, however if you had read on you would have realized you made one huge mistake. It continues with, "If the MAWL has only two owners then the vacant spot can be claimed by any person under MAWL contract." Well Dark Wolf, you may be the Commissioner, but I claim the last vacant spot. Vice President.
That's right. **The crowd boo wildly at Hypnosis' actions.** Ben Tuckwell: Stupid Hypnosis. This is becoming a damn circus and I don't care who wrote that rulebook, it needs to be changed immediately.
Hypnosis: So As your acting Vice President I have two orders as of now. The first is that at Quake of Fire, at the biggest match in MAWL history, Dark
Wolf versus the greatest entertainer to enter the squared circle. Me. Not only will the winner get the Middle Atlantic title, and the loser gain the
East Coast title. but both of out "titles" will be on the line. The winner will be both Vice President and Commissioner.
**The crowd boo as Hypnosis paces the ring before his next announcement.** Hypnosis: And my final announcement. Chris Register. You are a bitch. No, that's not it. Christopher Register, damn I'd have hated to be you growing up, you will fight me. NOW. This match will be for both Tag titles, and at the next Flash Card, on Friday the winner's partner will have to be named by the end of the night or the champion will be stripped of the title.
**The crowd cheer at the thought of either Hypnosis or Register being stripped of the title.** Hypnosis: Enough Corporate crap, Register, bring your ass out here. It's time for the Critical Condition vs. The Hypnotiser. Let's find out just how long it will take me to win.
**Hypnosis throws the mic. out of the ring as "Nobody's Real" starts on the PA system and the neon green searchlights start scanning the crowd. Register charges out from behind the curtain and makes his quick journey to the ring.**
Michael Reed: REGISTER IS HERE IN THE BUILDING AND HERE HE COMES! Chris rolls into the ring and the tag team champions are exchanging punches and this is unbelievable. Security is running out to stop the chaos and that's all the time we have for you tonight viewers. Tune in on Friday for the second flash card and if you have yet to do so, order Quake of Fire by calling 1800-BUY-MAWL as soon as possible. Goodnight.
Ben Tuckwell: SEE-YA! [Fade out.]
