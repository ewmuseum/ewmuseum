MAWL (Philadelphia, 2000) — Card Archive

Flash Card

6 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@ THE MIDDLE ATLANTIC WRESTLING LEAGUE presents... FLASH CARD - Tuesday, June 6th, 2000 Live from the MAWL Arena - Philadelphia, Pennsylvania http://mawl.virtualave.net @@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@ (The lights go completetly black and the crowd goes nuts. "Another One Bites
The Dust" by Queen plays over the MAWL PA System. The crowd gives a weird look. The lights go back on and there is the MAWL Vicious Champion The
Kreibs. He is wearing khaki pants, a Hawaiian shirt, North Carolina hat, and of course the Vicious Title on his shoulder. He has a mic. in his hand.)
The Kreibs: Sorry to worry all my fans about the awesome music I have. There is a reason for it though. I am undefeated in MAWL! I am 5-0! The reason I am 5-0, is because I made the pin in the six man tag, therefore I have
Hypnosis and Chris Registers victories. That makes me 5 wins and 0 loses! I am truly the greatest MAWL wrestler ever to grace through that ring.
(The Kreibs points at the ring. The crowd starts to chant Breanna...Breanna.) The Kreibs: Breanna is here just hold on! Now Ice I give you all the credit in the world. Good match and a good man won! I walked out with the Vicious
Title on my shoulder! Now I suppose I have to defend my title. Well my first title defence will be against Chris Register! Now a couple week back I said we were gonna duke it out in a cage match. I am changing that up again. Make it a ladder, cage match! Yes, at the next PPV The Kreibs versus Chris
Register in a Cage/Ladder match for The Vicious Title! Speaking of Chris
Register, nice try with the blow up doll. I have Breanna though now hit the music.
("No Body's Real" blasts over the PA System and Breann comes out with 2 retards wearing Chris Register shirts and sweat pants. The crowd laughs and points.)
The Kreibs: I would like to introduce you all to Chris Register's biggest fans, Rodney and Warren!
Warren: Have you seen my baseball? The Kreibs: What exactly do you guys do? Rodney: Well I wash w..w..windows at Hardees and Warren asks people if they have seen his baseball. We also belong to the Chris Register fan club! I am the president.
The Kreibs: How many "Registerholics" are in this fan club? Warren: Have you seen my baseball? (Rodney pushes Warren aside.) Rodney: He asked me! Right now there are 2. The Kreibs: Really? (The Kreibs hits the Breakdown on Rodney. Then walks over to Warren and hits him with the Vicious Title.)
The Kreibs: Hit my music! ("Dragula" blasts over the PA System. The Kreibs and Breanna walk backstage and we fade to a black screen. “No Leaf Clover” by Metallica begins play in the background, becoming louder and louder with every second of music. The new yellow MAWL logo flies in from the back of the screen until it is situated just above the center of the screen. As the electric guitar kicks in, the Flash Card image stamps itself onto the screen in blood red writing, just below the MAWL logo. We then fade into the newly built MAWL arena in
Philadelphia where 5000 fans are jammed into the relatively small complex.
We then pan to the announce table containing Michael Reed and he is joined by MAWL President Ben Tuckwell.)
Michael Reed: Welcome to the Middle Atlantic Wrestling League arena and you just saw The Kreibs attempt to make a fool of Chris Register. They'll meet in a preparation match on Saturday but we're less than two weeks away from
Quake of Fire, the second pay per view of the MAWL calendar where Chris
Register and The Kreibs will meet inside of a cage. Anyway, welcome once again to Ben Tuckwell who apparently has a few things he'd like to say.
Ben Tuckwell: That's right Michael. Rumors are flying around before Quake of
Fire and I'd just thought I'd take care of some of them. The first one is about some wrestlers making up an alliance and from what I know, The
Brothers Grimm may be involved with it. The second one is an interfed supercard... Michael Reed: The North American Wrestling Council has backed out of the deal scheduled for July 2nd but they've guaranteed that Collision Course will go ahead at a later date which is yet unknown.
Ben Tuckwell: No, no that dumbass. They, the fans, already knew about that deal being pushed back but in light of that, a tentative date has been set for an interfed supercard against The International E-Wrestling Alliance on
July 9th. The IEA contains names such as Gnome and David Dumont and will be a huge show for all involved.
Michael Reed: Excellent! Any other news we should know about? Ben Tuckwell: Today Damien Mullins signed up two new recruits in the forms of Marcus "Flash" Roberts, a cousin of our former Middle Atlantic Champion
Dark Derecho plus GLC, a 14 year veteran of the sport, both will make great additions to the MAWL.
Michael Reed: We've got another interview scheduled for you right now but I don't know who's about to do it so if we wait a second, I'm sure we'll find out soon.
[The sound of rumbling and the scratching of a microphone is heard. The scene is pitch black. ]
LOUD VOICE: GAWD DAMMIT! TURN IT ON... [Suddenly, a lens cap is removed. A large man, approximately 6’5” and 240 pounds stands in front of the camera wide-eyed, like a deer in the headlights, somewhat shocked by the camera. The man wears a loose gray tank-top, and what looks to be a pair of blue and white wrestling tights reading “THE COLT” in large white letters. The scene is a not very well lit, average living room. The man shakes his head erratically for a second, getting himself out of the trance. ]
MAN: Turn on the music quick! [The man behind the camera scrambles for something and the camcorder is hit by a flailing limb. The camera teeters for a moment. A ‘click’ is heard, followed by a less then CD Quality version of “Da Rockwilder” by Method Man and Redman.]
[ With all the cheesiness of a public access program, the man starts to pose. Flexing with all of his might, a fierce grimace comes over his face.
He flexes his biceps, his triceps, his chest and more. After a few minutes of intense flexin’ the man stops. Somewhat self-consciously he asks...]
MAN: Was that good? [A faint “Yeah, the camera is still going!”, is heard. The man gets intimidated by the camera again, but forces himself to continue. He struggles out a mean sneer as he stares into the camera with his green eyes.
He speaks as if he’s said these words a million times.] MAN: MAWL... The time is now. _NOW_ “The Colt” Lycon Holt has made his presence known. This news, is bad news for all you Middle Atlantic Wrestling
Leaugue stars. A human wrecking ball, in the form of me, has just torn down the wall and as the MAWL “superstars” look on in fear as I tower above... No mercy will reign supreme.
[Holt smiles to someone behind the camera.] You see, I’ve been waiting. Waiting and thinking, long and hard about my debut in MAWL. I couldn't think of a better place then here, my humble abode in the rainy city of Seattle. My hometown. The place where “The Colt” calls home. I know you fans are itching to get a look at my stomping grounds, but that comes in time.
“The Colt” has more important things to attend to. And that is this JUNE THE
18TH!, on MAWL's PPV spectacular QUAKE OF FIRE... [A sudden realization rushes over Lycon and he says to himself.] My first match... a PPV!
[He thinks about that thought, and then continues speaking.] Lycon Holt will be making his very first appearance on MAWL PPV! And what a better way to kick things off "Colt" style. I'll be teaming with a man by the name of
Steven "Lustful" Belar... A very familiar name, but I just can't place it.
Anyhow, me and you Belar, all the way to the gold! With our collective talent, the Brothers Grimm stands no chance. The UWF stands no chance! WE
WILL BE UNSTOPPABLE! Teamwork is key Belar, and I'm a team player. [ Lycon smiles politely.]
And you fans will get an up close and personal look at the future of MAWL. Not just the future, but the best up and coming talent you've seen in a long time. Because for the past few months, yours truly has been in the equivalent of MAWL's “training grounds,” provin’ my grit, and getting ready for this Monday... [An awkward pause.] My time has come!
[A slight rumbling is heard as Holt looks to someone behind the camera. Lycon nods his head, as the camera gets taken off its tripod into a handheld state.] So, without any further adieu, I’d like to give you all the royal tour of
“The Colt”’s apartme-... er, palace.
[Lycon steps back and gestures around his living room. A small TV sits on what looks to be a dresser, a glass table being held up by a plastic polar bear sits in the middle of the room, a bouquet of flowers sits in the center of the table. An average size red velvet couch, the nicest looking thing in the room, sits to the right of the camera, opposite the TV. A window on the opposite wall reveals downtown Seattle. The hustling and bustling streets lay below. ]
“THE COLT” LYCON HOLT: This... This is the main room of The Colt’s Paradise. As you can see, I surround myself with only the best of items for my personal use. Like this oak dresser drawer, this extravagant polar bear table, this plush velvet coast, and this baby over here. [Points to his TV.]
This IS where the Colt gets his entertainment. Does it get any better then this? And over here... [The Colt shifts to the right, where the kitchen is located. Someone, a few inches shorter and much smaller then Lycon is seen eating a sandwich. The guy sees the camera and suddenly ducks behind the counter.]
LH: Ha, ha. [Trying to save face.] Another one of my many ... servants. Ha... How ‘bout that... But anyhow, this is where The Colt feeds his mighty appetite for food. With such delicacies as _”MANWHICH”_, _”BIRDS EYE”_ and _
“HUNGRY MAN”_ .
And over here... [The camera follows Holt into a small hallway. Lycon looks into the camera as he opens a door. ]
LH: This where the magic takes place. Where "The Colt" lays his head to sleep, and where "The Colt" chisels out more muscles in the rock that is his body...
[The door is now open and the camera looks inside the room. Its a disaster area. Strewn all over the floor is dirty underwear, old clothes, a few plates, food wrappers, and more. A fold up, unmade bed is in the center of the room and in the corner, the most expensive thing in the entire house, is a deluxe excerise machine. Lycon, still looking into the camera and smiling, moves his gaze to the room. The gaze suddenly disappears and Lycon puts his hands over the camera. You can hear “I thought you cleaned in here!” .
Another rumbling and the hand is taken off of the camera. We’re now back in the living room. Lycon looks a little frustrated as he begins to speak.]
LH: Jackasses... Anyhow, that's enough for today. MAWL, take notice, “The
Colt” rides runs wild in Middle Atlantic Wrestling League!
[The scene fades as Lycon mumbles some vulgar words to the guy behind the camera.] Ben Tuckwell: Lycon Holt's first time in front of the camera but from what we just saw, this kid's got a hell of a lot of potential and he's only 21 years old with a bright future in front of him.
Michael Reed: I was entertained as well, although the flexing of muscles in the early parts of the interview were things that he needs to learn not to do.
Ben Tuckwell: I thought you'd like that sorta thing Reed! We've got matches that need to be started so let's get right on with the show with "The
Perfectionist" Stefan Andrews and Impetigo, two guys who are at the top of their careers and will show if they have something to offer the MAWL.
Michael Reed: I'm looking forward to this one, Impetigo is the smallest guy in this league at only 198 pounds but his high flying skills are reportedly amazing and it should be interesting to see if he can match up against
Stefan Andrews, a technical wrestling that is 50 pounds heavier.
Ben Tuckwell: Let's cross to it now! ####################################### IMPETIGO vs. "THE PERFECTIONIST" STEFAN ANDREWS ####################################### Impetigo didn't get to show off his high flying skills and was dominated the whole match by a bigger and stronger Stefan Andrews. "The Perfectionist"
lived up to his nickname, pulling off devastating suplexes and great submission holds before finally picking up the victory with the
Perfectionist Combo, consisting of a double powerbomb followed up by an elevated Boston Crab to make Impetigo tap the mat in pain.
WINNER: "THE PERFECTIONIST" STEFAN ANDREWS ####################################### Michael Reed: Nice display by Stefan Andrews and he'll be in the title scene before you know it. I was thoroughly impressed by that, although I was terribly disappointed with Impetigo's performance. With all the hype leading up to this match, it was lopsided and Andrews came out with a submission win in no time at all.
Ben Tuckwell: Impetigo just needs a while to find his feet and what better opportunity to show off those skills than at Quake of Fire as part of the lightweight tournament, also participating is "Crippling" Curt Denton,
Marcus "Flash" Roberts and Evenflow, the first two yet to make their debut and they have a great opportunity to leave their mark with only two matches.
Michael Reed: Yep. Four man mini-tournament, the winner only having to beat two men on the night for victory. The pay per view lineup is basically complete, with Belar and Holt taking on the Brothers Grimm for the tag titles. Chris Register and The Kreibs in a cage ladder match for the Vicious
Title, Jeremy and Moxlow for the East Coast Title then the match that is on everyone's lips right now. Hypnosis and Dark Wolf, the first two signings in
MAWL history squaring off for the league's biggest prize, the Middle
Atlantic Title. Ben Tuckwell: I've been told by my executive committee that a very important wrestler has arrived and is demanding some interview time right now, so I guess we better give it to him.
Michael Reed: I wonder who it is? Looks like we're about to find out eh Ben? Ben Tuckwell: Uh. Yeah. (Derecho is seen entering the MAWL building. The fans are shocked to see him here after his mysterious absence from the federation. All of a sudden, we cut to a scene in the arena right before the first match on the Flash Card can take place. "Busted" by Matchbox 20 hits and the fans cheer and boo him, not knowing how to react. Derecho isn't dressed in wrestling gear, but rather just normal street clothes. He enters the ring and asks for a microphone. One is handed to him from ring announcer Trent Lyon.)
Dark Derecho: Thanks Trent. I need some quiet as I have a very important announcement to make concerning my future here with MAWL.
(The crowd still makes noise as they usually tend to do) Dark Derecho: I SAID SHUT UP! (The crowd boos frantically at him.) Dark Derecho: I don't even know why I am making this announcement in front of all of you inbred morons who can't show a little respect towards a former
MAWL champion for just a few moments so I can address everyone. I feel as if
I am wasting my time out here talking to you people.
(The crowd boos, and some chants of "ASSHOLE" can be heard in various sections of the audience.)
Dark Derecho: Now.. as all of you may know.. I haven't been around MAWL for a little bit. I lost the MAWL title because I wasn't aware that I was defending it. I was called down at the last moment and said since I hadn't been participating, that I would drop the title to John Porter.. so without argument I went out there and I did just that. I still gave it my all because I am not a sore loser. Then, I am asked to tape an interview to send it in for a match against Hypnosis. I admit, that it usually isn't my style, but then again, I couldn't do it either since a camera crew never arrived at my door. Still, no hard feelings. The point I am trying to make here is that
I am going to be heading out of MAWL.
(The fans cheer like crazy.) Dark Derecho: I know it pains you to see me go. I know you want me to stay badly. (The crowd starts chanting "Get Out Asshole.. Get Out" followed by five claps.) Dark Derecho: The reason I am leaving is NOT due to losing the MAWL title.. it's NOT because they forgot to send a camera crew, but it's because I've been offered a better job elsewhere. You see, I will be signing a contract with the ever-so-popular Tornado Wrestling in the very near future, and as you know.. I can't be in two places at once, but don't you worry yourselves one bit for you see.. MAWL would be losing it's spark without a Roberts here. MAWL just wouldn't be the same, so I have decided to get on the horn to my cousin Marcus. He literally almost had an orgasm when I told him that
I would like for him to fill my spot in MAWL. He was excited about getting his big break. I had some discussions with the owner here at MAWL and he said that Marcus was welcome.. so without further adeux.. here is my replacement while I head off to TW... Marcus "Flash" Roberts!
("I Disappear" by Metallica plays over the speakers as Marcus walks out with a big grin on his face. He steps into the ring and hugs Derecho. He takes the microphone away.)
Marcus Roberts: Man.. now that's what I call a great intro, cous, but did you have to go and tell them the whole.. you know.. orgasm bit? I mean, that was a little too much, but then again, we all know whata card you can be sometimes!
(Marcus laughs as Derecho just shrugs and grins.) Marcus: Man, it is very nice of you to give me this opportunity.. you are such a nice guy.. really you are.. you deserve massive kudos for this. Some props would be nice, too.
(The fans boo.) Marcus: I don't know why these people hate you.. I guess they must be stupid or something.. I don't know.. I'm not going to stand here and try to figure it out. I have more matters to attend to. Like the owner instantly putting me in this tournament for the Cruiserweight championship or something? Wow..
I mean.. I dreamed of being in the big leauges and now here I am, already in a tournament where I will be fighting for a shot at a piece of gold.. man you really know how to talk sweet deals. I should have had you back in the mWo.
(The people still continue to boo him.) Marcus: Oh well.. what's done is done and now I must turn to the future.. MY future for you see, I am getting my big break, and I will make this future of mine a nice one.. a profitable one.. a desireable one. Therefore, I will reluctantly step into anyone's path and cause a disruption. Derecho is all serious in his work.. I am more laid back, but that doesn't mean I can come around and kick some tail every now and then. I may be a rookie in this sport, but I do know my way around a wrestling ring. All I ask is that you come and try me.. ladies, that goes for you, too.
(Marcus grins as Derecho shakes his head as if Marcus just embarrassed himself.) Marcus: Well.. I really can't think of anything else to say.. It must be all of this excitement
(Derecho takes the microphone back.) Dark Derecho: it could also be these fans.. their combined stupidity alone is enough to make you forget what you are saying. Trust me.. if you want to cut a good interview in front of people like these, you need intense mental preparation.
(The boo starting chanting "You Suck" at Derecho.) Dark Derecho: Come on, Marc.. let me show you around, give you the ten cent tour of the place.. trust me.. you'll thank me later.
("Busted" by Matchobox 20 hits the PA as they both walk off the stage and into the back. The people cheer once they seem them leave.)
Ben Tuckwell: Oh my... that's come out of the blue and he's lucky that short contract we issued him with has already finished because I could've fined him for some of those comments. Damn Derecho. At least he's supplemented the hole in the roster with another member of his family and like I said earlier, Marcus is a huge investment for this company and is a star of the future, like most of these youngsters who are trying to get a break somewhere.
Michael Reed: Most definitely and it'lll be interesting to see where some of these guys are in five years!
Ben Tuckwell: What are you talking about? Most of them have signed long term contracts with the league and if they do perform well, they won't be looking for a bigger job like Dark Derecho, they'll remain loyal to their roots in the MAWL. Anyway, I'm looking to make the league into one of the worldwide powerhouses in a few years so watch out to the big leagues out there.
Michael Reed: Well said Ben. Our main event tonight is Max Douglas and
Hypnosis, a grudge match formed after last week's incidents at Breaking
Point. Hypnosis was beating down The Kreibs in his Vicious Title match when
Max Douglas showed his face in the MAWL and saved The Kreibs and proceeded to inflict pain on Hypnosis. The grudge is here to be settled and this'll be the biggest match of MAWL Flash Card history which has spawned only 3 events.
Ben Tuckwell: Should be a great match! Something you don't want to miss so put down the remote control and glue your eyes onto the television because
Hypnosis and Max Douglas is coming right at you in just a moment.
**"Come With Me" by Puff Daddy blares on the PA system and there is a mixed reaction from the crowd. The newly crowned number one contender heads down to the ring as red pyro all along the aisleway shoots to the ceiling. As
Hypnosis jumps onto the apron and somersaults into the ring the crowd are heard booing louder than ever in Hypnosis' MAWL history. He holds his outstretches his arms as an "H" is formed by red and white pyro behind him.
The music finishes and the crowd dies down.** Hypnosis: Now that's the kind of welcome I expect for the number one contender. **All the fans in the arena boo.** Hypnosis: I don't want you all to love me, hell if you all loved me I'd kill myself, I've seen some of you. Now that's ugly.
**Hypnosis points to a member of the crowd, the camera focuses on him and he doesn't realise it as he eats his Hot Dog, spilling mustard on his already stained wife-beater. He looks up at the camera and then starts hurling abuse and spitting bits of food out. Some of the crowd laugh at this while the rest of them boo at being called ugly.**
Hypnosis: What I wanted was to get the respect I deserve for being a high flyer, for being a mat technician, but mostly for being the greatest entertainer to enter the squared circle. Now as you all saw on Breaking
Point, possibly the most famous breaking point, being viewed around the world, and ratings being taken in, that only was the ratings at its peak whenever they spoke my name, but that The Hypnotic One beat Dark Derecho to become the number one contender, and to get my title shot at Quake of Fire,
MAWL's Pay-Per-View. Now I understand that down here in Philadelphia,
Pennsylvania, most of you people are too poor to pay for it, and that you'll all chip in for one 6 inch TV and huddle around it to watch my greatness.
For a start the MAWL arena is probably the best thing you have going for you, and that houses 3,000 of you. And I mean houses. I saw most of you bums sleeping in here this morning, cant even afford your own cardboard boxes.
**The crowd drown out Hypnosis for a bit, who just stands back laughing.** Hypnosis: I got to give respect to Chris Register, breaking up DU was the best for both of us. Admitting that him and the Kreibs were holding me back.
It's all true. Register may have talent, but Kreibs was nothing but a whining bitch. Michael Reed: Can he say that on TV? Ben Tuckwell: He just did and it's not that bad anyway so get over it Reed you f'n idiot. Michael Reed: Woh. Hypnosis: I know that the new "Vicious" champion will be real happy with his achievement, and give all those other "Vicious" fighters a chance to get what they all want. A crack at the "gold". What kind of acclaim is that.
Vicious champion. For a start using the word vicious around Kreibs is enough to make me laugh. He's nothing more than a wimp, who hides behind things, weapons, people. You name it, he's hidden behind it. Kreibs, screw you and your title, I don't want nothing to do with it, what I do want, and I'm gonna get is the Middle Atlantic Heavyweight Title belt. I mentioned
Heavyweight there just to clear things up with the people in the back. I'm
210 pounds and I can "pound".
**Hypnosis chuckles at the terrible pun as the crowd boo.** Hypnosis: Any one of you. At Quake of Fire I will prove this by taking my spot at the top of the mountain. Dark Wolf, we were the first two members of
MAWL, and you have been here the whole time, working your ass off to get to the top. The only problem is I turned up again, and now it's my time to shine. I've always been an ally, initially asked to be part of Ability
Unmatched, but I turned that down cuz, lets say their Ability is Unmatched cuz its so bad. Then I was part of Digital Underground, well that didn't last long, and what did I get from it? NOTHING. From now on I'm on my own.
The only way I would join another faction is I was sure I would gain from it, and I will lead the MAWL into a new era. MAWL has never been as strong as it is now with Hypnosis soon to take the helm. But now onto tonight. Max
Douglas. What the hell do you think you are doing boy. I was doing the world a favour by kicking the Kreibs' ass, and you come out and ruin it. We've met before Max, and we'll meet again, the only problem is I am greater then you ever will be. I am the embodiment of MAWL. Working on Breaking Point, and then both Flash cards, and the finishing it off with Quake of Fire. If you were in this situation then you would've collapsed by the time you got to the ring. You are a great representative of the people. Fat, slow, dumb, with a great passion for junk food.
Max, there's so much I could say about you, and so little time, so I'm just gonna call you out here, and then me and you are gonna battle, and just as before, you are gonna be left on the floor, unconscious. And Douglas.
IF YOU WAKE UP. YOU WILL HAVE LOST TO ME!!! **Hypnosis shoots his arms out to the side and the pyro in the aisle shoots back up, "Come With Me" by Puff Daddy begins again and the lights turn off leaving just a path of red. A white spotlight fixes on Hypnosis who throws the mic. out of the ring.**
####################################### HYPNOSIS vs. MAX DOUGLAS ####################################### "Rock Superstar" by Cypress Hill began to play immediately after Hypnosis threw down the microphone and Max Douglas came charging out like a bull in a
Spanish bullfight. Douglas began well, using his whole array of moves to down the number one contender but like a true champion always does, Hypnosis fought back and after a chair shot on Max Douglas, he locked on the
Hypnotiser on the unconscious opponent and the bell was called for as the show went off air.
WINNER: Hypnosis #######################################
