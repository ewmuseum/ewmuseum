Annihilation 2000, Part One

7 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Middle Atlantic Wrestling League ANNIHILATION 2000 - Live from the Pittsburgh Civic Center Sunday, May 7, 2000 http://www.crosswinds.net/~mawl [The screen fades to black once again and we hear the sound of a crackling fire. Flames engulf the screen and the Annihilation 2000 logo rises up from the bottom until it hits the center of the picture. The MAWL logo bounces into the top right hand corner before we cross back over to the arena, this time to the announce table where Michael Reed and Damien Mullins take their place.]
Michael Reed: Welcome to the big one! This is ANNIHILATION 2000! I'm Michael
Reed and joining me tonight in Pittsburgh is none other than Damien Mullins.
Good evening Damien. Damien Mullins: Hey Reed. We saw at the start of the show AU pulling into the parking lot but I ask this question again, where is Dark Wolf this week?
He wasn't with them last week either! Michael Reed: I'm sure we'll find out sooner or later. What a big card we have in store for all you fans watching at home. To begin with, we have the
Clemson-Heal Connection taking on Ability Unmatched for the Tag Team Titles and we're hoping that this match ends the feud, with the winners of the battle almost certainly declared winners of the war.
Damien Mullins: That match is going to be close. Nobody has a clear cut advantage and a lot of power wrestling should be displayed during the tag team title match. The CHC have the slight size advantage but I don't think it will count for much. All I know is that the battle will be very personal and should be entertaining to watch.
Michael Reed: Following it will be a four way elimination match for the number one contendership. Wait, I'm being told that Dark Wolf has been found and we've got the camera there.
[The camera cuts to the interior of a limo. Dark Wolf and Wild Animal are relaxed in their seats having drinks of expensive wine.]
Dark Wolf: Hey driver, how long till we get to the arena? The show is starting now. Driver [in a thick southern accent]: About five minutes. This traffic is terrible. Everybody's rushing to the arena to see you stomp those boys.
Dark Wolf: I know. I'm the star of the show. Just get us there fast! [He leans back into his seat as the Tuck-Tron fades to black.] Michael Reed: Hmm. I wonder what will happen if Dark Wolf doesn't arrive in time! What were we talking about again Damien?
Damien Mullins: The number one contendership match. Dark Wolf, Billy Heal,
Tommy Clemson and Evenflow all fighting with the last man left standing declared the winner and he will go on to face either John Porter or Dark
Derecho next week at Breaking Point. I think that one of the CHC would have to be favorites, not because I like 'em but because I can hardly see
Evenflow and the Wolf on the same side after Dark Wolf defeated Evenflow a couple of weeks ago to pinch the Vicious Title.
Michael Reed: Speaking of the Vicious Title, that is match three on the card and Dark Wolf defends it against Ice. Dark Wolf defeated Ice back when he was known as Greg Davis but now he has a lot meaner attitude, which suits the hardcore arena. I think Ice may come out victorious seeing as that this will be the third consecutive match that Dark Wolf has to compete in. Mr.
Tuckwell has put an awful load on his shoulders tonight at Annihilation 2000. Damien Mullins: Sure has. Then it'll be time for the two big matches to conclude the MAWL championship round robin. First off, "Five Star" John E.
Moxlow and Coiler battle it out for the East Coast Championship Title while
Dark Derecho and the "Powerhouse" John Porter will be both going for the
Middle Atlantic Title. What a night this could be for Ability Unmatched if they could hold all the titles by the end of it.
Michael Reed: That would be amazing. Moxlow beat Coiler earlier in the
Championship Round Robin and he'll be looking to make it two straight against the former Vicious Champion but Coiler will be doing his best to stop him. Then it's time for the two titans of the Middle Atlantic Wrestling
League to do battle. Both men stand at four wins and no losses and the winner takes out the coveted Middle Atlantic Title, plus remains undefeated.
Damien Mullins: Everybody has been waiting for this match-up and it could go either way. Porter has Ability Unmatched in his corner and that could be the deciding factor in tonight's main event. I don't think Dark Wolf is here yet and the match is about to begin.
Michael Reed: We've got some more live footage Damien so take a look at this. [We cut back to inside the limo. The car shudders to a stop. Dark Wolf and
Wild Animal are thrown forward a little.]
Dark Wolf: What the [BLEEP]! This isn't the Civic Center! Driver: I know. Dark Wolf: What do you mean you know?! [The driver ignores him. He locks the doors except for the driver's side door. He steps out of the limo and shuts the door, locking it. The camera focuses back in on Dark Wolf and Wild Animal.]
Dark Wolf: This is bull-[BLEEP]! [The door by Wild Animal is quickly unlocked from the door. She is pulled from the car. Dark Wolf tries to get out as well but has the door slammed in his face.]
Dark Wolf: [BLEEP]!! You let me out of here you sons of bitches!! [Through the tainted windows, we see a group of masked figures pummeling
Wild Animal. We cut to outside the car as Dark Wolf begins to pound the bullet proof windows. Wild Animal is being choked on a railing that looks over a quarry below. After some more shots to the gut and face, the men pick
Wild Animal up. Kicking and screaming, they hoist her over the edge.]
Wild Animal: Let me go!! Let.. me.. go!!! [They dump her over. The camera watches as Wild Animal falls into the quarry. Her body becomes limp on impact. Cut back to inside the car. Dark
Wolf stopped pounding the windows and sits there in shock. The door behind him opens this time. The camera pans around to find a hand reaching in and grabbing a hold of Dark Wolf. The hand pulls him outside the car.]
Voice of Masked Man: She never liked you Dark Wolf. She wanted me. Not you. Dark Wolf: What the [BLEEP]? Crazy Horse?! Is that you?!! Why are you doing this man?! We used to be the best of friends!
Man: You figured me out. [He removes his mask, revealing a tough looking face, swollen eyes and jet black hair reaching to his shoulders.]
Crazy Horse: You deserted me Dark Wolf. You and Wild Animal deserted me. We were the best in the business. Then you decided to desert me.
Dark Wolf: What are you talking about?! I never deserted you! You chose not to come with me!
[Crazy Horse nails Dark Wolf with a stiff shot to the face. Dark Wolf lunges at him but the other men hold him back. Crazy Horse removes a black glove from his hand, revealing a set of brass knuckles.]
Crazy Horse: Now it's time, for you to suffer for your mistakes. [He hits a shot to the ribs and another to the face. Dark Wolf is busted wide open. A few more shots to various spots and Dark Wolf is nearly limp.
The men carry him to the edge of the quarry. Crazy Horse walks to him as his blood soaked face is held up.]
Crazy Horse: Now, for your end. Throw him over. [The men throw Dark Wolf over the edge. He plummets down and lands not far from Wild Animal. Thunder is heard from above as rain begins to fall. The scene cuts back to the arena.]
Damien Mullins: Holy Christ! Did you see that? This throws the complete card into havoc and Trent Lyon is standing in the ring, waiting to introduce the tag team title match.
Michael Reed: Things don't look good! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- MAWL Tag Team Title Match The Clemson-Heal Connection vs. "Powerhouse" John Porter Written by [name withheld] ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- Trent Lyon: The following contest is for the Middle Atlantic Wrestling
League TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIPS! It is scheduled for the one fall. Introducing first, weighing in at a combined weight of 609 pounds. THE CLEMSON-HEAL
CONNECTION! [The music plays and "The Real Deal" Billy Heal and "Tough" Tommy Clemson walk out, accompanied by Heal's manager, Justin T. Harris. They slap the front row's hands before entering the ring.]
Trent Lyon: Their opponent, fighting the battle all by himself, weighing in at 306 pounds, he is from Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. "POWERHOUSE" JOHN
PORTER! [The entrance ramp explodes in a shower of green and gold confetti as "Brutality" by Urban Voodoo begins to blast through the arena. The Powerhouse walks through the curtain and the fans saranade him with a chorus of boos. John takes in the reaction in, loving every second of it as he walks to the ring and slides in.]
Damien Mullins: John Porter is going to take on both Billy Heal and Tommy
Clemson but I think he's got something up his sleeve here to help Ability
Unmatched come away with the tag team titles.
Michael Reed: It wouldn't surprise me one bit if Coiler came running out halfway through and joined the AU team. Billy Heal is opting to start this match against Porter while Clemson is trying to get the crowd going from the apron.
[DING, DING, DING!] Damien Mullins: That's the bell and two of the biggest wrestlers in the MAWL circle each other and they lock up. Neither man can get the advantage and the tie-up is broken. The Real Deal charges at Porter who ducks the lariat attempt. Both men turn around but the Powerhouse is first to react by catching his opponent and dropping him with a belly-to-belly suplex.
Michael Reed: Both these men are over 300 pounds and I think a lot of power wrestling will be shown in this match with Clemson not far behind, weighing in at 289. Billy arises and Porter takes him by the hand and crashes him to the mat with a short-arm clothesline. Can John Porter keep this up though?
Damien Mullins: Of course he can, his ability is unmatched! Michael Reed: You used that shocking phrase last week as well, you know? Porter allows Heal to get to his feet and Billy gets the Powerhouse in a side headlock but Porter counters with a kick to the shin and gets behind
The Real Deal and hits a reverse DDT.
Damien Mullins: Man! John Porter has done all the attacking thus far and it doesn't look like stopping unless Heal makes the tag but Porter stops a crawling Billy Heal with a kick to the ribs. He drags Heal to the center of the ring and flexes his biceps for the unappreciative crowd. How dare you people? He's putting on a show!
Michael Reed: Not that good of a show though because Billy Heal has managed to make the tag to Tommy Clemson. Porter notices and stomps the ground in disbelief before running at Clemson who knocks Porter down with a big right hand to the head. Porter gets up and receives another shot to the head.
Damien Mullins: Damn it. 2 on 1 just isn't fair! Clemson pushes Porter into the corner and spits on his right hand. How disgusting!
Michael Reed: He spat on it because he's going to chop Porter's chest with an open hand. Yes. Open hand chop to his bare chest and did you hear that echo through the Pittsburgh Civic Center? My god that was loud! HE DOES IT
AGAIN! Another chop to Porter's chest and the crowd are going nuts.
Damien Mullins: That's because they are nuts. They're as bad as Tommy Clemson. Look at this! He's smiling as he places John Porter on the top turnbuckle. Clemson also climbs to the top rope and he hooks Porter's head and executes a superplex onto the canvas. He covers.
[ONE!] [TWO!] [KICKOUT!] Michael Reed: Kickout at two by John Porter and Tommy Clemson gets down on the ground and locks him in an Abdominal Stretch. Porter won't submit but it has the possibility of taking a lot out of him which is the major goal.
Damien Mullins: The odds are unfair, you have to admit Michael! Clemson is smiling again and now he's pointing at the Tuck-Tron. What's going on here?
[The Tuck-Tron lights up and we see Coiler in the AU dressing room, pounding at the door and trying to kick it down. He continues to pound on it as the camera changes to outside the door where large boxes are stacked against the door. Ron Jolla walks into the scene and dusts off his hands before walking off and the Tuck-Tron fading to black.]
Damien Mullins: I KNEW IT! Coiler should be down here fighting right now but
Ron Jolla has locked him in the Ability Unmatched locker room. John Porter will have to fight his own battle now as he continues to fight the pain of this Abdominal Stretch by Tommy Clemson.
Michael Reed: He's been locked in it for a while but Porter is almost at the ropes now while Clemson is trying to pull him back. Porter grabs the ropes and Clemson releases the hold. Porter looks to be feeling the effects of that Abdominal Stretch as Clemson pulls him up and nails him with a single arm DDT.
Damien Mullins: This is a crucial stage of this matchup because if John
Porter is to win, he needs to fight back right now. Clemson tags out to Heal but stays in the ring as the pair work on a double team with Clemson slamming Porter's head into Billy's awaiting boot.
Michael Reed: Heal steps over Porter and waits in the center of the ring for
John to get to his feet. John is struggling to get to his feet and that
Abdominal Stretch took its toll on him, which was the aim of Clemson. He manages to get to his feet but is met head on with a spear from The Real
Deal which takes the wind out of him once more. That was a devastating maneuver from Billy Heal.
Damien Mullins: He pulls up Porter immediately and puts him in a side headlock then drapes the arm over. Is he going to deliver the Real Deal? He attempts it but Porter blocks it then suplexes Billy over himself. Both men are down and the referee is starting to count.
Michael Reed: They are both starting to move and Heal is the first one to his feet. He whips Porter into the ropes but it's reversed and Porter catches Billy Heal with a Samoan Drop and the match isn't over yet.
Damien Mullins: Not by a long way! Porter pulls Billy up and hooks his leg with his own leg. Side Russian Legsweep time is it? No, it's a Front Russian
Legsweep and Billy goes face first into the mat. Porter makes the cover.
[ONE!] [TWO!] [BROKEN UP BY CLEMSON!] Michael Reed: Tommy Clemson stopped the count and Danny Graham is telling him to get out of the ring. He does as Porter goes back to work on Billy
Heal by sending him into the ropes and nailing him with a Double Arm
Backbreaker. Damien Mullins: If my eyes serve me correctly, it seems like John Porter is winning, right Michael? Porter whips Billy Heal into the ropes once more but he makes the unfortunate mistake of dropping his head which allows Billy to hit the swinging neckbreaker and regain the momentum.
[The Tuck-Tron lights out to show Coiler kicking down the door of the AU dressing room. He pushes away the boxes and starts running towards the ring area.]
Michael Reed: Coiler has broken out and this match is about to become even for the first time! Wait a minute, what the?
[John E. Moxlow comes out of his locker room to see Coiler running towards him. He ducks out of sight and as Coiler comes past, he uses a Drop Toe Hold onto the concrete. Coiler is busted open as the Tuck-Tron fades away.]
Michael Reed: Maybe not. Billy Heal hooks Porter in the side headlock... THIS TIME HE HITS THE REAL DEAL! Clemson comes in... CLEMSON CRUSH! Billy makes the cover and this is all!
[ONE!] [TWO!] [THREE!] Michael Reed: WE HAVE OUR TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS! Damien Mullins: Damn it! Trent Lyon: The winners of this match and NEW TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS... THE CLEMSON-HEAL CONNECTION! Michael Reed: Tommy Clemson and Billy Heal have done it and they are leaving happy men. Porter is out cold in the ring while Heal and Clemson have left the ring area. What is this? Here comes one of the new MAWL signings, Chris
Register.
Damien Mullins: He's in the ring and stomping away at John Porter. Porter is out and Register is adding insult to injury here. He's calling someone from the back, who could it be?
Michael Reed: Someone's coming out but I've never seen him before. Who is it
Damien? Damien Mullins: That's The Kreibs. He's a good friend of Register's apparently. They are both stomping at John Porter and now they're calling someone else out. Who this time?
Michael Reed: IT'S HYPNOSIS! HE'S MAKING HIS MAWL RETURN! He joins in the attack before they roll John Porter out of the ring for paramedics to take care of.
[Register takes the microphone and begins to speak.] Chris Register: WE ARE DIGITAL UNDERGROUND AND WE WANT THE TAG TEAM TITLES! CLEMSON AND HEAL... WATCH OUT! [He drops the microphone and all three man raise their arms in unison. They cross back over to the announce table.]
Damien Mullins: Wow! Digital Underground has been formed consisting of two newcomers in Chris Register and The Kreibs plus one of MAWL's first wrestlers... Hypnosis.
Michael Reed: They may be taking over from Ability Unmatched, who haven't had the best of nights. Dark Wolf is lying in a quarry somewhere in
Pittsburgh, Porter got his butt kicked that match by 5 different men and
Coiler is out cold backstage after running into his opponent for tonight,
John E. Moxlow!
Damien Mullins: Things don't look good for AU, especially Porter who has to recover fast if he is to beat Dark Derecho. Derecho is pulling into the arena now I'm being told.
[A nice black BMW pulls up into the parking lot. The car engine shuts down. The driver and passenger door opens. The trunk pops open as well. Derecho steps out of the driver's side and Samantha comes out of the passenger's side. Samantha is in short jean shorts, white tennis shoes, and a black t-shirt tucked into her shorts. Derecho is wearing brown boots, long light blue jeans, a white t-shirt and a pair of shades. The people in the audience boo when they see him come out of the car. Derecho pulls out his gear from the trunk and walks towards the arena entrance. As he enters the building,
Trent Lyon approaches him with a microphone.]
Trent Lyon: Excuse me, Derecho, but I couldn't help notice that you have arrived here at the arena and I was hoping that we can get some words from you about you big match tonight where you face John Porter for the Middle
Atlantic championship title.
Dark Derecho: Gee mister.. it's awful nice of you to notice my arrival here. Just like you noticed my arrival, all those reject rednecks out in that audience, from the ringside seats to the nosebleed section will notice me kick John Porter's ass up and down the ring, up the aisle and all the way back to wherever the hell he's from, then, we'll stop, realize we need to pin each other in the ring, turn around, and I'll kick his ass all the way back here to the arena where I will put on the Vortex Powerbomb and become the new and 1st Middle Atlantic champion here in MAWL. It is my destiny, it is my goal, and it's my soul purpose here in MAWL. To stand on top of the mountain, look down at all the peasants below and make them realize that I am the best damn wrestler that ever set foot into a MAWL wrestling ring.
It's the truth and tonight, when I face John Porter, I will show you exactly why it is the truth. Now I've gone and wasted too much time on you and your stupid interview, as far as I am concerned you can take that to the presses.
Trent Lyon: Just one more question, though. Dark Derecho: I've got a question for you. Ever play the game of H-Bed? Trent Lyon: H-Bed? I'm not sure if I am familiar with the game. Dark Derecho: Let me explain it to you, then. I take that microphone, shove it up your ass, and you spend the next day laying in a Hospital bed, having the entire ward listen to all the lovely sounds a body can make. It's fun.
Want to play? Trent Lyon: Uhm.. no thanks. Dark Derecho: That's what I thought. [Derecho walks off camera and Samantha playfully smiles at Trent Lyon and bounces off screen.]
Damien Mullins: Boy! I would hate to have Trent Lyon's job of interviewing the wrestlers. H-Bed? Haha!
Michael Reed: It's not all that funny, you know that? Trent Lyon should be back here to introduce the next match shortly, which has become a three way elimination match after Dark Wolf's withdrawl. Well Trent is jogging down the ramp holding the microphone and we should be starting any second.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- Three Way Elimination Match - Number One Contendership Evenflow vs. "Tough" Tommy Clemson vs. "The Real Deal" Billy Heal Written by [name withheld] ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- Trent Lyon: The following contest is a three way elimination match. It has a twenty minute time limit. Introducing first, standing at 5 feet 11 inches and weighing in at 216 pounds... EVENFLOW!
["Faith" by Limp Bizkit hits and the crowd boo Evenflow as he makes his way out and down to the ring.]
Trent Lyon: His opponent, hailing from Demoines, Iowa, weighing in at 289, he is half of the tag team champions, "TOUGH" TOMMY CLEMSON!
["Kashmir" hits, and the crowd lets out a huge pop. Tommy Clemson, wearing his usual attire walks out, and raises one fist in the air. He looks into the stands quickly, and then walks down the ring, not slapping hands, and then rolls under the bottom rope. He stands up, and then pumps the single fist again, as his music fades away.]
Trent Lyon: Their opponent, weighing in at 320 pounds, he is the other half of the MAWL tag team champions, please welcome "THE REAL DEAL" BILLY HEAL!
[The lights dim in the arena except for a single spotlight on the entrance curtain. The song "Tom Sawyer" begins to play and Heal walks out from behind the curtain and pyro goes off and Heal sprints to the ring and slides under the ropes and leaps to his feet and bows to the crowd as a loud explosion goes off behind him.]
Michael Reed: We have three men in the ring and one of them will walk out the number one contender. One thing I can say is that Evenflow will need to bust his gut out there if he is to win against the tag team champions. Will this be a repeat of the last match we saw?
Damien Mullins: I definitely hope not Michael. I couldn't stand watching that again. [DING, DING, DING!] Damien Mullins: That's the bell and the CHC look at Evenflow then walk towards him, trying to intimidate the former Vicious Champion. Evenflow doesn't like it but he has no choice as they whip him into the ropes and knock him down with a double shoulderblock. Simply from seeing that, you can tell that Evenflow is going to be targeted until eliminated.
Michael Reed: It looks that way as Billy Heal picks the 216 pound Evenflow up like a rag doll and tosses him down in a move known as a bench press slam. Evenflow is giving up 70 pounds to Clemson and over 100 to the Real
Deal so teamwork may have nothing to do with it in the end, it may just be the size advantage.
Damien Mullins: You're kidding! If Heal and Clemson fought, they would annihilate each other and Evenflow would preserve his energy until it was required. But that ain't happening as they knock down Evenflow with a double clothesline, and the crowd are actually cheering this rubbish?
Michael Reed: They're showing some respect to the tag team champions which is fair enough. Billy Heal kicks Evenflow in the midsection and pulls him up into a piledriver position while Clemson goes to the second rope, leaping off and pushing down the legs in a bone crunching spike piledriver.
Damien Mullins: They may as well pin him now so we can get some decent action happening. Billy Heal and Tommy Clemson leaving the ring now and they're talkin' with the fans at ringside while Evenflow remains down in the ring. Can you believe this?
Michael Reed: Evenflow is getting up and the Clemson-Heal Connection don't see him as he launches himself over the top rope and nails them both with a big suicide dive. The small man isn't giving up but he's got a big fight ahead of him if he is to go on and face the Middle Atlantic Champion next week at Breaking Point.
Damien Mullins: This is a good start to that as he lays the stomps to both
Clemson and Heal. He enters the ring and awaits for the other two competitors to do so and Evenflow is taking this opportunity to catch his breath before the fighting begins again.
Michael Reed: Billy Heal is first in the ring and Evenflow tries for the
Irish whip but The Real Deal reverses it and sends Evenflow into the ropes.
BAM! Spinebuster Slam and boy did that sound and look painful from where I'm sitting. Damien Mullins: Evenflow just isn't big enough to compete against the heavyweights of this promotion. I'm sure he'll be glad when they introduce a cruiserweight division to the MAWL.
Michael Reed: I hear there are plans of introducing some sort of lightweight division in the near future so stay tuned. Clemson goes for a legdrop but
Evenflow rolls out of the way. Tommy gets up and Evenflow hits him with a right hand before dropkicking him in the face, sending him flying over the top rope.
Damien Mullins: Way to go 'Flow! Billy Heal, who has been watching on during the past little sequence goes for a lariat but Evenflow ducks it and retalliates with a kick to the gut. THE FLOW! HE GOT THE FLOW! Hook of the leg.
[ONE!] [TWO!] [THREE!] Damien Mullins: YES! Evenflow and Tommy Clemson are about to go one-on-one after Evenflow just eliminated "The Real Deal" Billy Heal. Clemson rolls into the ring and the little man stomps him and repeatedly does it. He allows Tommy to stand up and pulls himself up onto Tommy Clemson's shoulders and takes him down with a hurricarana. There is still hope for Evenflow!
Michael Reed: He quickly gets up to the top rope and executes a moonsault onto Tommy Clemson. He's taunting the crowd now and Evenflow stands over
Tommy Clemson who quickly gets up and rolls him up.
[ONE!] [TWO!] [KICKOUT!] Damien Mullins: No! Evenflow kicks out at two but Clemson doesn't stop attacking as he pulls Evenflow to his feet and kicks him in the gut. He raises both of his arms and picks up Evenflow and connects with a running powerbomb. Clemson doesn't stop, lifting up Evenflow immediately and taking him out with an inverted powerslam.
Michael Reed: He could pin Evenflow now in my opinion but he's not doing that and this could come back to haunt him in the end. He lifts Evenflow to his feet and knocks him down again with a right hand. Clemson is celebrating and it's safe to say that he is in complete control of this match.
Damien Mullins: Don't speak too soon. Clemson whips Evenflow into the ropes and pulls him up onto the shoulders for a Samoan Drop but Evenflow counters it with a Crucifix.
[ONE!] [TWO!] [KICKOUT!] Damien Mullins: Almost and if Evenflow won there, I could've laughed at you

[ONE!] [TWO!] [THREE! NO! SHOULDER UP!] Damien Mullins: That was very close. Evenflow onto the apron and he's going to attempt a springboard move. Frankensteiner? Clemson holds onto the legs!

Michael Reed: What a victory and I'm being told Ben Tuckwell is backstage with an announcement on the Vicious Title. Let's cross over now.
[The Tuck-Tron lights up and we see Ben Tuckwell seated in his office wearing a black MAWL long sleeved dress shirt. He sees the camera and smiles.]
Ben Tuckwell: As you know, Dark Wolf will be out of action for a couple of months and therefore, the Vicious Championship has been vacated. Ice, I'm not just going to give the title to you, but instead a Vicious Rumble will be held next week at Breaking Point to determine the new Vicious Champion.
Whoever wants to compete can compete and good luck to all. [Fade to black.] Michael Reed: Woh. That's a big announcement! Ladies and gentlemen, we need to take a break and following these advertisements, John E. Moxlow and
Coiler meet for the East Coast Championship. Stay tuned!
