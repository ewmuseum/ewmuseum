Flash Card

9 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@ THE MIDDLE ATLANTIC WRESTLING LEAGUE presents... FLASH CARD - Friday, June 9th, 2000 Live from the MAWL Arena - Philadelphia, Pennsylvania http://mawl.virtualave.net Containing interviews from: Dark Wolf, Evenflow, Hypnosis & The Kreibs @@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@ [The scene opens up inside the arena somewhere dark. Probably a boiler room or somewhere of that sort. He's sitting in the rafters with a baseball bat, like the old Evenflow. He's wearing traditional skeleton face paint and black body suit with built in pads. He's giving that cocky laugh he always has too, assuring he'll get a victory.]
It's time for the acid rain to flood the arena once again. As I, the imperial knight of darkness, Evenflow will reign supreme as I face Chris
Cain, for #1 contenders spot for Vicious Title. Chris Cain, there is something you should no...
I'm not an ordinary man. I AM IMMORTAL, I CANNOT DIE! YOU WILL NOT WIN! You fail to realize that this is not a world you can survive Chris, trust me
I know, I know the world better than every man put together, I AM THE
[BLEEP!]ING WORLD! Woah... a new catch phrase and a win all in one night, killer. Well anyways, the Vicious Title will soon be mine again, and I WILL reign supreme, you remember that. Good night...because this will be the last night you'll live asshole...
I'm sure you are wondering why I hate you so much, because you are a poser, a wannabe hardcoreist, a wish I was EVENFLOW, and you will die like that too...
[The camera fades to black as “No Leaf Clover” by Metallica begins play in the background, becoming louder and louder with every second of music. The yellow MAWL logo flies in from the back of the screen until it is situated just above the center of the screen. As the electric guitar kicks in, the
Flash Card image stamps itself onto the screen in blood red writing, just below the MAWL logo. We then fade into the MAWL arena in Philadelphia where
5000 fans are jammed into the relatively small complex. We then pan to the announce table containing Michael Reed and he is joined by MAWL President
Ben Tuckwell.]
Michael Reed: Welcome to another edition of MAWL's Flash Card, as you notice, we've changed the days Flash Cards are held from Thursday Nights to
Friday Nights but one thing we can assure you of is that Flash Cards will be continuing in the MAWL following very high ratings for our targeted area.
Ben Tuckwell: Yes, a decision has been made and a couple of wrestlers have asked for their releases so more about those will be revealed at Breaking
Point. The MAWL has also signed the services of a promising youngster in
"Kid Ego" Tucker Warren. At only 17 years of age, he's an athletic boy and may be a late replacement for Quake of Fire's lightweight tournament but I can't confirm this yet either.
Michael Reed: All depends on performances I suppose Ben. A few guys are wrestling for their positions over the next two weeks because like you said
Ben, major cuts are made after every MAWL pay per view.
Ben Tuckwell: That's correct Michael. I've got another big announcement to make but I'll save that for later in the show at the peak of the ratings, probably just before the main event which is a tag team title match pitting champions Brothers Grimm up against Chris Register and Hypnosis. The
Brothers Grimm have been ordered to wear seperate tights in the quest to tell them apart and I think the problem has been solved.
Michael Reed: Excellent Ben, we've also got Steven "Lustful" Belar taking on
Coiler. The results to the MAWL mid-year awards were also released earlier today, with Chris Register taking out the best rookie category and Dark
Derecho winning both best wrestler and impact player, the latter he tied for with John Porter.
Ben Tuckwell: That reminds me about something! I've got TWO _HUGE_ announcements to make throughout the course of the show, both significant in the future of the Middle Atlantic Wrestling League.
Michael Reed: Great, let's begin with the action, Steven Belar and Coiler coming to you next!
========================= SINGLES MATCH Steven "Lustful" Belar vs. Coiler ========================= Coiler came out looking like he had just woken up with a hangover and Belar capitalized on this, totally annihilating Coiler with his mix of crisp moves and painful submissions. Following Belar's win via a Texas Cloverleaf,
Coiler looked to have suffered an injury in his upper leg and was helped out of the arena by officials.
WINNER: Steven "Lustful" Belar ========================= Michael Reed: Belar dominated Coiler and continues his good run of easy victories. Coiler looks to have torn his hamstring muscle in the course of that match and he'll be out from anything from 3 weeks to 3 months from the looks of things.
Ben Tuckwell: That's not good considering the position we're in currently but we'll keep plugging at it, just like the Japanese Attitude in World War
II. Fight to the death they say and that's what the MAWL will do!
Michael Reed: That's nice to know as well Ben. Should we reveal one of the big announcements at this time?
Ben Tuckwell: Yeah okay, what the hell! July 9th has been penciled in for the date of an interfed supercard as the Middle Atlantic Wrestling League takes on the International E-Wrestling Alliance in a show titled "Interfed
Warfare".
Michael Reed: We revealed that last week. Is it finalized then Ben? Ben Tuckwell: Yep. Five matches have been booked thus far containing the title holders of both leagues facing off against each other. More will be announced as the date approaches but at the moment we're concentrating on
Quake of Fire and that alone.
(The lights go out and the crowd screams. Lights of different colors go through out the crowd. The lights stop. "Dragula" begins to play and a lime green spotlight goes to the top of the MAWL Entrance way. The lyrics start to talk and The Kreibs walks out wearing a wife-beeter, North Carolina hat, kahaki pants, and The Vicious Title around his waist. Breanna walks out and stands next to him. She is wearing her Tommy Hilfiger top and Tommy pants.
The Kreibs gets a mic. and begins to speak.) Kreibs: Well I see that Hypnosis has come back from hiding. (The fans boo. The Kreibs cracks up.) Kreibs: I know exactley why he left. He was scared that I would beat his ass! It's that simple.
(The crowd roars with approval.) Kreibs: Well he came back, then he bad mouthed me, then he said he didn't even want me in a match! Well Hypnosis you are wrong my pal! You just don't come back and challange The Kreibs! Come on. Then you actually think I hide?
No! I don't hide behind anything. Hypnosis is jealous of me! It's True, It's

(The crowd boos. The Kreibs and Breanna walk to the ring and get in.) Kreibs: Now Chris me and you go back along way. We have been life long buddies. Out of all the years me and you have been doing this. We will meet for the first time this Saturday. Now I know that you have your hopes set his to beat The Kreibs this Saturday in a non-title match. Then to beat me again at the PPV! Well Christopher it is not gonna happen. Now I am gonna have a match this evening. I don't like to let my fans down. So Mitchell
Reed come in the ring take my mic and call the match. Now hit his music.
("Nobody's Real" blasts over the PA System. A midget wearing a Chris Register mask, a flash shirt and black leather pants. He gets in the ring under the bottom rope and puts his dukes up.)
Michael Reed: Come on! He's a midget! Kreibs: That's it! I have heard enough outta you, Mitchell. Breanna please take my microphone!
(Breanna grabs the mic and Michael sits back down at the announcer's table. The Kreibs holds the midget back with his arm and the midget trys to him but he can't.) Breanna: Kreibs, as uasual has the commanding lead. (The Kreibs hits the Breakdown.) Breanna: OUCH! The Breakdown is executed on Chris Register. (The Kreibs throws the midget out of the ring ang grabs the mic. from Breanna.) Kreibs: See Register it will be like that this Saturday! Then at the PPV I will clmib the ladder and take my Vicious Title and put it around my waist!
So hit my music! ("Dragula" blasts over the PA System as we pan back over to the announce table once again.)
Michael Reed: The Kreibs and Chris Register war is getting more and more personal every single second and they'll get their first opportunity to lay hands on each other this week at Breaking Point to relieve some of the heat but believe me, when Quake of Fire comes around and we have the Vicious
Title match, it'll be one of the most memorable matches in MAWL history.
Ben Tuckwell: No doubt! I guess I'll reveal the second big announcement now and it has something to do with working with another fed yet again. The MAWL is working a "farm fed" deal with the North American Wrestling Council. What this means is that the NAWC will send their new recruits our way as to strengthen our roster and allow them to prove themselves and when both organizations feel the time is right, we release them to head off into the big world of the NAWC.
Michael Reed: Sounds like a good deal for both companies. The MAWL gets the wrestlers early in their career and send 'em off for some more exciting youngsters. This'll give a lot of wrestlers the chance they deserve to use the MAWL as a stepping stone to greater things.
Ben Tuckwell: That's not all Michael, if the NAWC has some experienced wrestlers not going at their full potential, they'll send 'em down here to find their feet once again. It's an excellent system and I look forward to it, with it starting immediately and I think we'll be receiving a couple of wrestlers in the next week or so.
Michael Reed: Nice work Ben, you're doing wonders with the MAWL since you took over as full owner and I apologize for doubting you earlier in your reign. Let's continue with the interviews and Dark Wolf, MAWL Middle
Atlantic Champion apparently has something lined up for us tonight. [The scene fades in with the shot of the hospital we've seen in Dark Wolf's latest interviews. A caption appears...]
LUTHERAN GENERAL HOSPITAL OUTPATIENT CENTER JUNE 7, 2000 5:29 PM [Cut to just inside the building. Dark Wolf stands at a desk with a duffle bag in hand. He scribbles his name on a sheet of paper and then straightens up. A nurse behind the desk shuffles a handful of papers before handing him the top sheet.]
Nurse: Here you are. Remember, the doctor says to go easy on those ankles. Stay out of the ring. Dark Wolf [taking the sheet]: Of course. [He folds the sheet and stuffs it into a back pocket. He is decked out in street clothes, a black leather jacket, flannel shirt underneath, black jeans, and Nike Air shoes. He turns from the desk and walks through the doors to the blazing light of the sun outside. A black Jeep Wrangler sits at the curb, a young woman leaning against the passenger's side door. She is lean but strong. She wears a pair of loose denim shorts, a white blouse, and white Reeboks. A black baseball cap covers her eyes slightly from view.]
Dark Wolf: Ready to go Shannon? Shannon: You bet. [The woman, who we now realize is "Sharpshooter" Shannon Rourke, walks around the front of the topless Jeep and steps in the driver's side. Dark
Wolf walks to the curb and tosses his duffle bag in the back. He then climbs into the Jeep and Rourke starts it up.]
[Cut to inside the Jeep. Rourke is still at the wheel. Dark Wolf sits relaxed in the passenger's seat. The Jeep is in motion.]
Dark Wolf: I was very pleased with your assistance at Breaking Point. There is no doubt that you have the qualities of a great manager.
[Rourke looks at him briefly and smiles.] SSR: Thanks. Dark Wolf: So the trial run was very much a success I think. Although Porter did tear some tendons in my right ankle, he didn't injure me enough that I can't wrestle. The doctors want me to stay out of the ring but what do those quacks know? You're hired Shannon. Congratulations.
[Again Rourke smiles.] Dark Wolf: This Saturday we've got to be at the South Shore Auditorium in
Brooklyn for Breaking Point. I'm scheduled for a non-title match against
John E. Moxlow. Moxlow has had four wins and two losses in his career thus far in the MAWL. In case he doesn't know his math, that's six matches total. I also have had six matches here Moxlow. Unlike you though, I have only one loss. So do the math if you can. The result for Saturday's Breaking
Point is pretty predictable. You see, I'm a hot spot in the MAWL. I always have, and always will. You will add another one to that growing loss column of yours, and I... I will add one more to the win column.
SSR: That's right. Aside from last Breaking Point, Wild Animal helped Dark
Wolf get where he is today.
Dark Wolf: Even though I didn't need it. SSR: Well, yeah,... but a little help never hurt anybody. Dark Wolf: True. SSR: So as I was saying, she helped him get to the heights he's at now. I'll continue to help him maintain that status. I give you my word as a former
United States Special Forces Lieutenent, that with my managerial skills, and his incredible wrestling ability, he'll roll right through Mr. Moxlow.
Dark Wolf: Enough said about him. If this wasn't a non-title match, I'd take that East Coast title right out from under his nose too. I'd be the first man to hold two titles in MAWL history. Who can beat that?
SSR: Nobody that I can think of. Dark Wolf: Now, there's this Hall of Fame voting going on now. Myself,
Porter, and Dark Derencho are the candidates for induction. Now...
[He rubs his left index finger across his lips.] Dark Wolf: Let me see. Porter beat Derencho. So that means Derencho shouldn't be inducted. And don't forget I beat Porter. So that means he shouldn't be inducted. Who else?... Oh! That leaves only one candidate! Me!
[Cocky smile.] Dark Wolf: So there. I've cleared that up for you all now. You should know now who to vote for.
SSR: Don't get carried away with that though. You've got Moxlow on Saturday and then Hypnosis at Quake of Fire for the title. You've got a lot of fighting ahead of you. And some very formidable opponents too.
Dark Wolf: Formidable opponents? No. We have Moxlow, a paper champion. He's never faced me. We have Hypnosis, a man who joined the MAWL the same time I did. Ability Unmatched offered him a spot among us but he declined it.
Therefore, he is nothing. Though I will admit, AU is nothing great anymore. Porter, AU's founder is no longer a powerhouse in the MAWL. So that means Coiler and myself are all that are truly unmatched in our abilities. I know Coiler's is injured at the moment, but when he returns, I want him as my tag team partner.
SSR: Really? What would you call yourselves? Dark Wolf: Well I haven't talked with him about it yet, but the name Deadly
Force comes to mind.
SSR: Deadly Force... I like that. Dark Wolf: Yeah. It's the name I've used when winning past tag titles. Together, we will win the MAWL tag straps too. SSR: Now as your manager, I want you to focus on Hypnosis too. You've mentioned everything else under the sun but Hypnosis. What do you plan for him?
Dark Wolf: I plan to leave him unable to prepare for me. I plan to dominate him left, right, and center. I plan on retaining my Middle Atlantic title and sending him to the bottom of the ladder where he belongs. When Darkness falls Hypnosis, the Fangs of Fear will be upon you. It is inevitable. Are you ready?
[Cut to outside the Jeep as it speeds off down a highway. Fade back to announce table.] Ben Tuckwell: Simply superb. Dark Wolf definitely has championship qualities within him and he's one brave mother, battling it out with two stuffed ankles.
Michael Reed: I'm surprised at your decision to let him wrestle though Ben. I'd of thought you'd give him a week off to prepare for Quake of Fire and hopefully get back to 100 percent fitness.
Ben Tuckwell: Dark Wolf said he was ready to compete and if he says that,
I'll go with him. Let's get on with the show shall we? Stop this inane blabbering of your's Michael Reed!

Chris REGISTER!!! **Hypnosis waits in the ring, and "Nobodies' Real" begins again and Register makes his way down to ringside, having bottles and paper thrown at him in the aisleway. ========================= TAG TEAM TITLE MATCH Chris Register/Hypnosis vs. The Brothers Grimm [c] ========================= Chris Register had a bit to say, most of which is inaudible to the television audience. The Brothers Grimm charged down the ramp with different tights to tell them apart and the new tag team of Hypnosis and Register managed to take them apart with little fightback at all. The rule change disadvantaged The Brothers Grimm and cost them big time as they proceeded to lose the tag team titles as Hypnosis locked Jim Grimm in the Hypnotiser while Register applied the Critical Condition on Tim Grimm for a double submission victory. Both men took their titles and had a staredown as the
Flash Card went off air.
