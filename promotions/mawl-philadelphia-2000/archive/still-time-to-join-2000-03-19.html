There Is Still Time to Join

There are only 7 spots left for the MAWL Championship Round Robin so check out the webpage at
http://hammer.prohosting.com/~mawl and add it to your bookmarks. The Middle Atlantic Wrestling League is a booked regional fed located in the three states of Pennsylvania, New Jersey and New York.
Roleplaying is done through a message board plus there is an interactive out-of-character board and we are run by a three man executive committee.
The MAWL is scheduled to start on March 21st with a house show so if you want to wait until we have a card, stay tuned for that. We are not claiming to be the best e-fed on the internet but we are planning to have a lot of fun so check us out and I hope that a few of you join.
