Saturday Night Breaking Point, Hour Two

8 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MAWL Saturday Night Breaking Point - Hour Two - April 8, 2000 Live from the Nassau Veterans Memorial Coliseum - Uniondale, NY http://hammer.prohosting.com/~mawl Michael Reed: Welcome back to MAWL Saturday Night Breaking Point. This is our second hour and on the way we have the much talked about showdown between 2 Damn Hard and Dark Derecho plus Prophecy against Billy Heal and a rematch of the giants when Seth takes on "Tough" Tommy Clemson for a second time in MAWL history.
Rich Delaney: Let's just hope that it is not as boring as the last match that they had! Anyway, we better get on with the show 'cause I'm sure that
Fox Sports will be pissed off if we exceed the time put aside for the MAWL.
Michael Reed: We've got plenty of time Rich. We can just talk like this for half an hour if we wanted to.
Rich Delaney: You're an idiot, you know that? Shut your mouth and let's get on with some action instead of all the bullcrap you are talking about! Trent
Lyon, do your job!
Trent Lyon: The following match is a division 3 contest. It is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, coming out of Wheeling, Virginia, led to the ring by Justin T. Russell, weighing in at 320 pounds, he is known to the fans as the REAL DEAL... HE IS BILLY HEAL!
[The lights dim in the arena except for a single spotlight on the entrance curtain. The song "Tom Sawyer" begins to play and Heal walks out from behind the curtain and pyro goes off and Heal sprints to the ring and slides under the ropes and leaps to his feet and bows to the crowd as a loud explosion goes off behind him.]
Trent Lyon: His opponent, accompanied by Angel, weighing in at 295 pounds... PROPHECY! [The lights in the arena flicker and dim, and fade. The words "It has been written," spoken by a distorted demonic voice, echo through the arena.
Death's Head by Slayer blasts through the arena as Prophecy and Angel step out from behind the curtain. They stand at the top of the ramp for a brief moment. Prophecy's eyes scan the arena, and they make their way calmly to the ring, as "Death's Head" by Slayer blasts through the arena..."Death's pointed at your head..." The music is heavy and fast... Prophecy climbs into the ring, and through the ropes. Angel follows him, into the ring. He goes directly to a corner, and waits for his prey to step into the ring. Angel blesses him, and steps out of the ring.]
Rich Delaney: What an introduction from Prophecy. I know him from way back when he was David Rage but he's a lot darker now and I can swear that he won't be relenting on Billy Heal. His girl is pretty good like last time as well.
Michael Reed: She is quite a nice looking girl, yeah. Rich Delaney: Another thing I've been meaning to point out is the amount of big guys here in the MAWL. Everybody is either a giant or a little guy, weird huh?
Michael Reed: Not really. I mean both guys are evenly matched up in style and size and it should be a good contest.
[DING, DING, DING] Michael Reed: They tie up and Prophecy goes for a short arm clothesline but
The Real Deal ducks it and nails Prophecy with a swinging neckbreaker. Not a bad counter there from the rookie.
Rich Delaney: Heal puts Prophecy in a Full Nelson and Prophecy easily makes the ropes for the hold to be broken. Armdrag takedown by Billy Heal on
Prophecy and he goes for yet another swinging neckbreaker but this time
Prophecy knows what to do as he reverses it into a side suplex.
Michael Reed: Gutwrench powerbomb by Prophecy into an automatic pinfall attempt. [ONE!] [KICKOUT!] Rich Delaney: Prophecy gets straight back up from that and runs into the ropes and knocks Heal down with a running clothesline. Prophecy sends Billy into the corner and hits him square in the face with a reverse elbowsmash.
He takes a couple of steps back and man, what a bone crunching move. Charging clothesline into the corner and that definitely took the wind out of Billy Heal. Michael Reed: Billy Heal reverses an Irish Whip and sends Prophecy into the ropes. Heal misses with a clothesline but with the momentum, he continues off the other ropes and then knocks Prophecy down with a clothesline. Billy puts Prophecy in a Boston Crab and Heal lets go after a short time. Prophecy into the ropes... spinebuster slam from The Real Deal. Cover now.
[ONE!] [KICKOUT!] Michael Reed: Kicks out at one! Armdrag takedown from Heal once again and he goes up top and nails Prophecy with a diving shoulderblock. Not bad for a guy his size. Piledriver from Billy Heal and he makes the cover once again.
[ONE!] [TWO!] [KICKOUT!] Rich Delaney: Close but Prophecy is too damn good to be pinned this early. Prophecy dodges a right hand from Billy Heal and sends him into the ropes and then knocks him down with a big boot kick to the face. Into the ropes once more and a powerslam there from Prophecy.
Michael Reed: Heal gets up and Prophecy charges at him for a clothesline but
The Real Deal ducks it and pulls the 295 pound Prophecy up into a Gorilla
Press as if he's only a cruiserweight. What power from Heal as he slams
Prophecy down to the mat. Prophecy rolls under the bottom rope and Billy
Heal follows him out and knocks Prophecy into the ringsteps.
Rich Delaney: The ref. is at five for the countout now and Billy Heal goes for a piledriver but Prophecy backdrops him onto the ringside mats. Prophecy hits a fallaway slam on Billy and the count is now at eight. Prophecy rolls
Heal into the ring and what's he doing? He's taking out a table! Yes, get hardcore baby!
Michael Reed: Not more tables! Prophecy is setting the table up at ringside while Billy Heal is on his feet. Prophecy slides into the ring to be met with stomps by Heal. The Real Deal places Prophecy on the top turnbuckle and climbs up to the second rope but Prophecy was ready as he pushes Heal back down to the mat and awaits him to get back into a standing position.
Rich Delaney: Heal is on his feet and Prophecy leaps off and nails him with a flying clothesline. They are back out of the ring once more and Prophecy knocks down Heal with a short-arm clothesline. Gorilla Press slam on the outside and the referee is at six.
Michael Reed: Prophecy grabs Billy by the hair and pulls him over to where the table is set up. Prophecy pulls Billy's head between the legs and signals to the crowd. Heal is up and the 320 pounder goes crashing through the table with extreme force. Billy is out cold and Prophecy grabs the fallen Heal and rolls him back into the ring.
[DING, DING, DING] Rich Delaney: What in the hell is the bell ringing for? Nobody made a pin! Trent Lyon: The winner of this match by countout, "THE REAL DEAL" BILLY HEAL! Rich Delaney: Oh my! What a bad decision from Danny Graham! Prophecy should have been the winner by default damn it!
Michael Reed: Well Prophecy realized what had happened and he's now in the ring laying the boots to the unconscious Heal. Prophecy picks him up and drills him with the Prophecy finishing move once more. Here comes the manager Justin T. Russell and he's arguing with Prophecy, getting right in his face.
Rich Delaney: Prophecy is laughing at the little guy and he pushes him aside but Russell gets back up and into Prophecy's face once more. Prophecy nails him with a hard right hand and peels him off the mat. He signals to the crowd and delivers Justin T. Russell with the Prophecy! I love this game!
Michael Reed: Now who's using other company phrases? Even though Prophecy lost the match, he's left quite a large amount of devastation on both Billy
Heal and his manager Justin T. Russell, both lying unconscious in the ring.
That has knocked Prophecy out of contendership for the Middle Atlantic Title though which is disappointing not only for him but the MAWL fans as well who were expecting the big man to be there.
Rich Delaney: After our ring crew and paramedics have gotten rid of the mess at ringside and the two bodies in the ring, it'll be time for the match that
I've been waiting for all night. 2 Damn Hard against the former RRW champion
Dark Derecho who just signed a contract with the MAWL. Both are equal in size with 2 Damn Hard only an inch taller and Derecho only five pounds heavier and I guarantee that it'll be up there with the Hypnosis, John
Porter match.
Michael Reed: It's time for Dark Derecho to take on 2 Damn Hard and I'm picking 2 Damn Hard to come away with victory. He was terribly unlucky not to win the Vicious Title last week and he'll make up for it here!
Trent Lyon: The following match is another singles bout scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, hailing out of Charlotte, North Carolina and weighing in at 229 pounds, 2 DAMN HARD!
["Southtown" by P.O.D. hits the loudspeakers, and the crowd begins an immediate pop. 2 Damn Hard walks out of the entry way wearing his ring attire and a smile on his face. He slaps hands with fans at ringside, makes his way around the ring, and slides underneath the ropes.]
Trent Lyon: His opponent, led to the ring by Samantha, he is a former RRW
World Champion and is out of Stuart, Florida. Weighing in at 234 pounds,
DARK DERECHO! [As "Busted" by Matchbox 20 comes on, the arena lights darken, but the entrance light give off a blue glow. Derecho walks out from behind the curtain with Samantha by his side. From here, Derecho just walks normally to the ring and climbs in using the steel steps.]
[DING, DING, DING] Rich Delaney: Derecho going to work right away, whipping 2 Damn Hard into the ropes and using a drop toe hold to send him face first into the mat.
Derecho sends 2 Damn Hard into the turnbuckle and he bounces off right into a powerbomb from Derecho. Samantha is cheering him on as he makes an early cover.
[ONE!] [TWO!] [KICKOUT!] Michael Reed: Too early but Derecho has shone so far in what he has done! Derecho goes for another Irish Whip but it is reversed this time but Derecho knocks 2 Damn Hard down with a clothesline and covers once more.
[ONE!] [TWO!] [THR... KICKOUT!] Rich Delaney: 2 Damn Hard just kicked out there and I'm starting to really doubt his value to the company.
Michael Reed: Get off it Rich, he just needs to warm up! Russian Legsweep by
Dark Derecho and there is a dragon suplex. Both wrestlers' shoulders are on the mat and this is a double pinfall!
[ONE!] [TWO!] [BROKEN UP!] Rich Delaney: Derecho goes for the whip into the turnbuckle but it is reversed and Derecho is in the corner. 2 Damn Hard charges at him but
Derecho moved out of the way and 2 Damn Hard runs into steel. Dark Derecho executes a Samoan Bulldog on 2 Damn Hard and follows it up with a hurricanrana. Another cover from the Dark one.
[ONE!] [TWO!] [KICKOUT!] Michael Reed: Close but no cigar. Another Russian Legsweep from Dark Derecho and he is starting to get under the crowd's skin with boos coming from everywhere. He laughs at them while pickng 2 Damn Hard up from the mat and sending him into the ropes. 2 Damn Hard knocks Derecho down with a clothesline and then lifts him to his feet for a devastating DDT.
Rich Delaney: 2 Damn Hard goes for a headbutt but it is blocked and Derecho powerbombs 2 Damn Hard back down to earth and his short reign of attack has come to an end. Derecho lifts 2 Damn Hard to his feet and goes for a punch but it is blocked and 2DH bodyslams Dark Derecho.
Michael Reed: 2 Damn Hard whips Derecho into the ropes and Derecho sticks out the arm and down goes 2 Damn Hard. Dark Derecho misses with a superkick and 2 Damn Hard takes advantage with a smashing sidewalk slam.
Rich Delaney: Will you quit doing that? Michael Reed: Doing what? Rich Delaney: Using an adjective starting with the same letter as the move name. The crowd is right behind the sucklidy suck 2 Damn Hard as he throws
Derecho out of the ring and goes through the ropes himself. They are brawling in the ringside area and Derecho gains the upper hand and sends 2
Damn Hard crashing into the guardrail. They re-enter the ring and Dark
Derecho whips 2 Damn Hard into the turnbuckle and charges him with a shoulder.
Michael Reed: Derecho is now setting 2 Damn Hard up for another powerbomb but this time 2 Damn Hard reverses it with a hell raisin' hurricanrana.
Rich Delaney: Shut your god damn mouth bitch! Bodyslam by 2 Damn Hard and now he goes for a side slam but it is countered with a DDT by Derecho.
Derecho goes up to the top rope and jumps off with a twisting flying elbowdrop. Derecho is really stomping away viciously at 2 Damn Hard who I have to say is almost out of this match. 2 Damn Hard is lifted to his feet.
VORTEX POWERBOMB BY DARK DERECHO! 2 DAMN HARD IS OUT OF IT NOW! [ONE!] [TWO!] [THREE! NO, KICKOUT!] Rich Delaney: Holy crap, he kicked out of the Vortex Powerbomb. That's almost supernatural. Derecho is in a state of shock as he lifts up 2 Damn
Hard and drops him with a hanging vertical suplex into a cover.
[ONE!] [TWO!] [KICKOUT!] Michael Reed: This is great effort from 2 Damn Hard, kicking out of almost everything thrown at him. Derecho rolls 2 Damn Hard out of the ring and follows him and slams 2 Damn Hard's head into the steel ring steps.
Rich Delaney: Back in the ring now and Derecho uses a Dragon Suplex into a bridge. [ONE!] [TWO!] [THREE! WHAT? SHOULDER UP!] Rich Delaney: This is starting to get really annoying, not only on my behalf but on Derecho's behalf as well! Vortex Powerbomb for a second time on 2
Damn Hard and tell me this is all!
[ONE!] [TWO!] [THREE! WAIT! NO!] Rich Delaney: [BLEEP!] Michael Reed: Calm down! 2 Damn Hard is almost unconscious but he still kicks out. Urinage Suplex by Dark Derecho and this has to be it.
[ONE!] [TWO!] [THR... SHOULDER UP!] Rich Delaney: Ah damn it! Just lie down and be pinned for the bitch's sake! 2 Damn Hard gets up and punches Derecho and then hits a diving clothesline on Dark Derecho and the crowd are on their feet, cheering him on. He whips
Derecho into the ropes and executes the Sideswipe... I think I know what's coming and it's not good news for Derecho!
Michael Reed: Yes, 2 Damn Hard is going up top now... there it is, the
Hardliner right across the throat of Derecho... is this it?
[ONE!] [TWO!] [THR... KICKOUT!] Rich Delaney: No it's not! Both men are out of it and the first to roll over should get the win. Derecho is stirring, he drapes the arm across 2 Damn
Hard's chest.
[ONE!] [TWO!] [SHOULDER UP!] Michael Reed: Man, what does a man have to do to pick up a win around here? Derecho is up and he hits 2 Damn Hard with a running knee lift. VORTEX POWERBOMB FOR A THIRD TIME! THIS HAS TO BE IT! [ONE!] [TWO!] [THREE!] Michael Reed: Derecho has won after three Vortex Powerbomb's and 2 Damn Hard kicked out a fraction too late. That certainly goes down in my books as a five star match and it exceeds the quality shown in the Hypnosis versus John
Porter match.
Trent Lyon: The winner of this match by pinfall... DARK DERECHO! Rich Delaney: The pinfalls did get annoying during the middle but I too have to say that I was on the edge of my seat the whole time and I do give credit to both men who both put up gallant fights and Derecho came out on top eventually and that is a hard earned paycheck! What's this? Coiler is walking down the ramp holding what looks to be a piece of paper. Coiler hops into the ring and hands the note to 2 Damn Hard, what does it say?
Michael Reed: I wouldn't have a clue Rich. Anyway we are running short on time so let's get on with our final match of the night, Seth versus Tommy
Clemson.
Trent Lyon: This match is the fnal match of the night. It is scheduled for one fall and its time limit is the remaining television time. Introducing first, he is the MAWL's biggest superheavyweight weighing in at 450 pounds... SETH!
[The lights go out in the sold out arena as "Metamorphosis" by Methods of
Mayhem comes over the speakers. The giant of a man appears at the top of the ramp as fire surrounds him. When it clears Seth slowly walks down to the ring, he slides in lets out a mighty yell.]
Trent Lyon: His opponent, standing at 6 feet 11 inches and weighing in at
289 pounds, from Demoines, Iowa, "TOUGH" TOMMY CLEMSON!
["Kashmir" hits, and the crowd lets out a huge pop. Tommy Clemson, wearing his usual attire walks out, and raises one fist in the air. He looks into the stands quickly, and then walks down the ring, not slapping hands, and then rolls under the bottom rope. He stands up, and then pumps the single fist again, as his music fades away.]
Rich Delaney: God damn, Seth is a big fat fool. Both men are staring each other down, just waiting for the bell.
[DING, DING, DING] Rich Delaney: They lock up and Clemson breaks the tieup and chops Seth's chest and we heard that from down here. Seth stumbles back but Clemson isn't laying off and he's hitting him with hard right hands now.
Michael Reed: Clemson steps back and tries to clothesline Seth over the top rope but Seth ducks it and elevates Clemson over the top rope and onto the floor below. Seth climbs onto the apron and jumps off with a double axhandle smash. That hurt Clemson and now Seth is delivering hard kicks to the ribs of Tommy. The referee is telling them to get back in the ring and Seth obeys him and rolls the tough one into the ring and then slides in himself.
Rich Delaney: Seth goes for a big legdrop but Clemson moves out of the way. Clemson pulls Seth to his feet and drops him with a single arm DDT. Cover now. [ONE!] [TWO!] [KICKOUT!] Michael Reed: Seth kicked out of it and here comes Coiler holding some sort of box. Seth ducks the clothesline attempt and kicks Clemson in the gut. He lifts Tommy Clemson above his head and throws him to the outside with a
Gorilla Press Slam to where Coiler is waiting. Coiler opens the box...
what's inside? Rich Delaney: He's pouring the contents of the box over Tommy Clemson, what are they? They're moving... SCORPIONS! COILER JUST PUT A BUNCH OF LIVE
SCORPIONS ON TOMMY CLEMSON. THE BELL RINGS AND CLEMSON WINS IT BY DQ.
Michael Reed: What a night we've had. Six winners, six losers. That's all the time we've got for you tonight, see you next Saturday when the MAWL brings Breaking Point to you live. On behalf of Rich Delaney, I'm Michael
Reed... goodnight!
