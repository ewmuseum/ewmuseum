Saturday Night Breaking Point, Hour Two

15 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MAWL Saturday Night Breaking Point - Hour Two - April 15th, 2000 Live from Madison Square Garden - New York City, NY http://hammer.prohosting.com/~mawl Michael Reed: Welcome to hour TWO of Saturday Night Breaking Point. This is the business end of the show where Coiler and Tommy Clemson meet in a cage to hopefully settle their differences followed by a big Vicious Title match between Dark Wolf and Evenflow.
Rich Delaney: What about Michael Lake and John Porter? Michael Reed: Didn't you get the memo this morning? Lake has pulled out of the MAWL for a while to get his life back on track so Porter has won his division by default now seeing as that both Hypnosis and Set are injured.
Rich Delaney: Yeah! Congratulations John Porter! Who will be that division's
East Coast Title representative?
Michael Reed: That will be decided sometime later on. Maybe John Porter will get a double chance!
Rich Delaney: That would just be too good to be true. Let's get on with the cage match! The cage is already down and we are awaiting our two competitors!
Trent Lyon: The following contest is a Cage Grudge match!! [The crowd cheers.] Trent Lyon: Making his way to the ring first, from Detroit, Michigan, he weighs 240 pounds, COILER!!!!!
["Open Your Eyes" by Guano Apes hits the arena. Coiler walks out and jaws with a fan. He is heard sniffing often as he continues on his way to the cage. He steps through the door and goes through the ropes. He then stands in one corner awaiting his opponent.]
Trent Lyon: And his opponent, Demoines, Iowa, he weighs in at 289 pounds, "TOUGH" TOMMY CLEMSON!!!!! ["Kashmir" hits, and the crowd lets out a huge pop. Tommy Clemson, wearing his usual attire walks out, and raises one fist in the air. He looks into the stands quickly, and then walks down the ring, not slapping hands, and then rolls under the bottom rope. He stands up, and then pumps the single fist again, as his music fades away.]
[DING, DING, DING] Michael Reed: Here we go! These two lock up in the center of the ring. Clemson backs Coiler into the ropes. A whip across. Coiler comes off! Ducks a clothesline! Off the other side! Tommy lowers his head for a back body drop. But Coiler stops himself! Coiler with a boot to the face!
Rich Delaney: Will you shut up and give me a chance to talk?! Coiler with a go behind into a waist lock! And takes Tommy over with a German suplex!
Coiler's back up now and he's stomping away on Tommy! Coiler is climbing up the side of the cage! He's half way up! Tommy is back to his feet! Coiler jumps off! Dropkick! No!! Tommy steped to the side!! Coiler landed hard!
Michael Reed: Tommy pulls him up now! Onto his shoulders! And hits a samoan drop!! Rich Delaney: Damnit! I told you to shut up! This is my show! Michael Reed: Tough Tommy is back up now and he helps Coiler up! Tommy backs him into the ropes! Coiler with a low blow! That got Tommy down!
Rich Delaney: Alright, that's it!! [Rich drops his headset and stands up, about to pound Michael Reed.] Michael Reed: Knock it off Rich. You don't scare me! [Rich sits back down and puts his headset back on.] Rich Delaney: Just stay quiet. Oh!! Coiler connects with a big swinging neckbreaker!! He's up on the top turnbuckle now! Jumps off! And drops a fist to the gut!! He covers! One... two...
Michael Reed: Wait! There's no pinfalls in this match! Rich Delaney: I knew that! I was just repeating what the referee was saying! Michael Reed: Sure. Rich Delaney: Just shut up! I don't need your crap! Michael Reed: Watch that mouth young man! Tommy's informing the referee that there are no pinfalls. Coiler is right back at him! Merciless!! Coiler backs him into the corner!! Lands a left to the jaw! A right to the jaw! And a big uppercut that sends Tommy reeling!
Rich Delaney: Coiler bounces off the ropes now! And connects with a big clothesline!! He literally dove at Tommy with that one!! Coiler is back up and he's going up the turnbuckles now! He's on top, waiting for Tommy to get up! Clemson's up! Coiler jumps off into a plancha!! But Tommy moves away!!
<SSSSSSSMMMMMMAAAAAAAAAAACCCCCCCCKKKKKKK!!!!!!!!!!!!> Michael Reed: Coiler landed real hard on the canvas there!! He's in some serious pain!! Rich Delaney: Tommy's going up the cage wall now! He seems to be having trouble with his footing! He's half way up! Three quarters of the way up!
Coiler's on his feet! He's lunging up the wall after Clemson!! Tough Tommy's at the top!! Coiler's not far behind!! Tommy's going over!! Coiler has him!!
Coiler's on top with him!! They're exchanging shots up there!! Michael Reed: This is insane!! Coiler with a shot to the gut of Tommy now!! <BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOMMM!!!!!!!!!!!!> Michael Reed: DDT!!!!! DDT!!!!! COILER DDTED TOMMY CLEMSON OFF THE TOP OF THE CAGE TO THE CANVAS!!!! Rich Delaney: That nearly broke the canvas!! Both men are down and out! Neither man is stirring! [Huge heel pop.] Michael Reed: What's this?!! Why are the fans booing? Wait a minute! What the?!! That's "Powerhouse" John Porter at the entranceway!! What's he doing out here?!!
Rich Delaney: And what's in that bag of his?!! Michael Reed: I don't know!! Porter's coming down to ringside though!! He looks like he has something planned!!
Rich Delaney: He just pulled a set of keys out of his pocket! He's unlocking the cage door!!
Michael Reed: Uh oh! I don't like the look of this! For either Coiler or
Tommy Clemson! [Porter kneels down beside Coiler and appears to be jawing at him. He then stands up and walks to Tommy Clemson. He unties the bag and pulls out a huge snake.]
Michael Reed: What the..?!!! It's a snake!! Good God!!! Get security down here and stop this psychopath!!!
[Porter puts the snake on Clemson who has come to a little. He sees the snake and starts squirming.]
Rich Delaney: HA HA HA!!!! THIS IS GREAT!! ["Tom Sawyer" by Rush hits the arena. The crowd now starts cheering.] Michael Reed: Now what?!! [Billy Heal and Justin T. Harris come out to ringside.] Rich Delaney: It's Billy Heal!! He's going into the cage with Justin's cell phone!! Porter doesn't see him! Coiler is getting up!!
<CCCCCCRRRRRRRRAAAAAAAACCCCCCCCKKKKKKKKK!!!!!!!!!!!> Rich Delaney: And Heal just clobbered Coiler with that cell phone!!! Coiler's out cold!!! Michael Reed: Powerhouse sees it now!! He swings at Heal with a hard right! Heal ducks it!! Heal with a swing of that cell phone!! Porter ducks!! [Ron Jolla jumps the guardrail with a baseball bat and nails Justin Harris with it.] Rich Delaney: It's Ron Jolla!!! And boy did he nail Harris with that bat!! Michael Reed: This is ludicrous!! DING!! DING!! DING!! Rich Delaney: What?!! No!!! No!! Michael Reed: It's about time!! The referee has called for the bell! He's throwing this match out!!
Trent Lyon: Ladies and gentlemen, the referee has declared this match, A NO CONTEST!! Michael Reed: Well there you have it! But meantime, we still have this brawl going on in the cage! Porter and Heal are going at it! Coiler and Clemson are out cold on the canvas!! And Justin Harris is still getting pounded with that baseball bat courtesy of Ron Jolla!! We'll try and get this all cleared up before the next match!
Trent Lyon: The following contest is for the Middle Atlantic Wrestling
League Vicious Championship. The match is to be completed under Wolf Rules.
Introducing first, the challenger. Representing Ability Unmatched, he is led to the ring by Wild Animal and he weighs in at 278 pounds... DARK WOLF!
[The lighrs dim down to almost pure darkness. The letters "AU" appear on the screen as a gold spotlight shines down on the entrance way. A howling wolf is heard and then "Prizefighter" by Bush hits the arena and rockets blow off from either side of the entrance way. Dark Wolf steps onto the ramp and taunts the crowd as pyros blow off all around him. He then makes his way down to the ring, taunting fans as he goes. He steps into the ring and an errupion of gold pyros go off forming a loud barrage overhead.]
Trent Lyon: His opponent, the current MAWL Vicious Champion. From Toronto,
Ontario, Canada and weighing in at 216 pounds, EVENFLOW!
[The lights go out and "Faith" by Limp Bizkit begins to play. Different colored lights flash everywhere and Evenflow steps out with the Vicious
Title draped over his right shoulder. He walks down the ramp and into the ring where he charges at Dark Wolf with the belt.]
Rich Delaney: Evenflow charged at Dark Wolf but Dark Wolf ducked the belt shot and delivers a knee to Evenflow's gut. Evenflow doubles over and Dark
Wolf drops him with a DDT. Great work from my fellow AU member!
Michael Reed: Since when were you a member of Ability Unmatched? Evenflow is up and Dark Wolf uses a single arm DDT this time.
Rich Delaney: I was talking with Ports in the back and he said that I am their newest member.
Michael Reed [nodding sarcastically]: I'm sure he did. Dark Wolf hooks both arms of Evenflow and this time it's a Double Arm DDT that brings Evenflow down. DDT frenzy from Dark Wolf today!
Rich Delaney: Definitely is a DDT frenzy as Dark Wolf puls Evenflow to his feet and hits a jumping DDT. Dark Wolf must have a point to prove with the repetition of all these moves because I know that he has a much better move repetoire than he's showing at this current stage.
Michael Reed: Evenflow fighting back with right hands but Dark Wolf blocks one and uses an excellent fall forward reverse DDT. Another DDT but that's probably the best one we've seen over the course of the night.
Rich Delaney: HAHA! Dark Wolf is taunting Evenflow! Did you hear that Michael? He said 'How do you like that you damn wannabe!' Classic! Haha! Michael Reed: Dark Wolf makes the cover and maybe he wasted too much time taunting the fallen one.
[ONE!] [TWO!] [KICKOUT!] Michael Reed: Near fall and the showboating did cost him a three count in my opinion. Rich Delaney: Eck. Dark Wolf didn't want to win it yet! He wants to inflict some pain like he did to Greg Davis last week! Dark Wolf with a side headlock on Evenflow and he goes for a vertical suplex but Evenflow blocked it and hits Dark Wolf with a vertical suplex of his own.
Michael Reed: Evenflow is leaving the ring now and is searching underneath it for something. He's pulled out a basketball. What the hell?
Rich Delaney: This is the home of the Knicks you idiot! Evenflow bounces the basketball once and rolls in the ring and throws it at Dark Wolf and it nails him square in the forehead knocking him back down. Not such a bad weapon afterall, eh Reed?
Michael Reed: Evenflow is underneath the ring once again and he's taking out a table this time. The table is in the ring and Evenflow is setting it up now. Remember fans in Pittsburgh, Annihilation 2000 is coming your way on
May 7 and tickets are still available.
Rich Delaney: All titles will be decided on that night. In the ring now
Evenflow has Dark Wolf set up for a powerbomb. He lifts him but can't complete it due to Dark Wolf's size. Dark Wolf backdrops Evenflow onto the mat and raises his hand! Yeah Wolf!
Michael Reed: Dark Wolf pulls Evenflow off the mat and he grabs hold of him. BELLY-TO-BELLY OVERHEAD SUPLEX RIGHT THROUGH THE TABLE! THE TABLE IS SHATTERED INTO SEVERAL PIECES AND EVENFLOW IS IN THE MIDDLE OF THE MESS! Rich Delaney: Dark Wolf is now stomping away at Evenflow's right knee and is not relenting. Take a look at the referee. He just stands back and counts to three when necessary. I could do that!
Michael Reed: Tell one of the board members. You could get to be a referee which would be great.
Rich Delaney: You think so? Excellent! Michael Reed: Yeah! Referees get more money that us too. Muhaha. Rich Delaney: What was that laugh for? Michael Reed: Oh nothing. Dark Wolf has locked Evenflow in a kneebar submission hold but Evenflow is fighting the pain and is making his way to the ropes. He stretches out and makes the ropes! Dark Wolf is forced to release the hold.
Rich Delaney: Dark Wolf drags Evenflow over towards the turnbuckle and rolls out of the ring. He grabs Evenflow's legs and slams Evenflow's right knee across the steel ring post. Dark Wolf is out to injure Evenflow and he's doing a good job of it so far.
Michael Reed: Dark Wolf is now applying a figure four leglock around that ring post and is applying as much pressure as possible. Evenflow must have some torn ligaments in there somewhere.
Rich Delaney: Dark Wolf breaks the hold and Evenflow gets up and is limping. Dark Wolf slides into the ring and goes for a dropkick to the knee but
Evenflow sidesteps it and Dark Wolf hits air.
Michael Reed: Evenflow limps toward the ropes and climbs to the top. Dark
Wolf is shaking off the daze and Evenflow hits him with a flying cross body block. Evenflow kicks Dark Wolf in the midsection and he grabs hold of the tights. THE FLOW! THE FLOW! EVENFLOW JUST HIT HIS FINISHER AND HE COVERS
DARK WOLF! [ONE!] [TWO!] [THREE! NO! SHOULDER UP!] Rich Delaney: The pinning attempt wasn't strong enough. Dark Wolf easily raised his shoulder up from the mat to escape defeat. Evenflow lifts Dark
Wolf up and gets him in a side headlock but Dark Wolf kicks Evenflow hard in the knee sending him down to the mat. That knee is definitely getting weak from the targeting by the Wolf.
Michael Reed: The tides have turned once again. Dark Wolf has been dominating the majority of this match but Evenflow too has had his good moments. Dark Wolf delivers one hard stomp to the knee and is going to the top rope for some acrobatics.
Rich Delaney: Not acrobatics. A simple flying legdrop across Evenflow's throat and DW is now stomping away at the throat area. This is perfectly legal in this type of matchup and Dark Wolf is blatantly choking Evenflow but the ref. is powerless to do anything and he could kill him if he wanted to but Dark Wolf lets go of the choke hold.
Michael Reed: Dark Wolf is now intimidating the referee and Evenflow uses the opportunity and delivers a low blow to Dark Wolf's groin region.
Rich Delaney: Don't you mean his knob... sorry Fox Sports, I mean't groin region. Michael Reed: You're cutting a very thin line Rich. Rich Delaney: I'll be out of this job soon to take up refereeing hardcore matches like this one and you'll miss me more than you miss your momsy wumsy. Evenflow takes Dark Wolf down with a hurricanrana and he is mounting another fightback against the odds.
Michael Reed: Dark Wolf rolls out of the ring to catch his breath and Evenflow bounces off the ropes and leaps onto him with a suicide dive. Dark
Wolf is down and I think Evenflow might have landed awkwardly on that injured right knee. Time will tell though.
Rich Delaney: Evenflow is up on his feet but is unsteady as he lifts Dark
Wolf up and pulls him to the top of the ramp. Evenflow nails Dark Wolf with the Flow DDT move for the second time in this match and it may be over as he hooks the leg.
[ONE!] [TWO!] [BROKEN UP!] Rich Delaney: John Porter and Coiler have rushed through the curtain together and Coiler is sporting an AU t-shirt. Porter knocks Evenflow down and both him and Coiler begin stomping away at Evenflow.
Michael Reed: Coiler scoops Evenflow up and hits him with the Coiler Cripple. Porter lifts Evenflow to his feet and places him upon his shoulders... PORTER PLUNGE! THIS IS MADNESS! DO SOMETHING REFEREE!
Rich Delaney: Interference is legal remember. No DQ? Dark Wolf is up and now all three men are stomping away at the unconscious Evenflow. Dark Wolf applies the Fangs of Fear and Danny Graham checks Evenflow and calls for the bell. We have a new Vicious Champion!
Trent Lyon: The winner of this match by involuntary submission and NEW VICIOUS CHAMPION... DARK WOLF! [After a short celebration of Dark Wolf winning the Vicious Title, AU slides into the ring and Porter calls for a mic.]
John Porter: You see this? [Points to AU] This is the most dominating force in professional wrestling today. There is not a single wrestler or even a team that can defeat us. I hope all you poor bastards in the back are taking a good look at us because there isn't a thing any of you can do to stop us.
Earlier tonight you witnessed the Powerhouse rip apart Michael Lake and our newest member, Coiler, absolutely annihilate Tommy Clemson, and you just saw
Dark Wolf bring Ability Unmatched it's first title belt. In a few short weeks I will bring home the Middle Atlantic Title and Coiler will also be holding a belt. You know why? Because our Ability is Unmatched, no matter who you are, no matter where you came from, no matter what you do, you can't stop us. We are AU and we are the best.
[Porter tosses the mic to Dark Wolf and flips off the crowd as the ravenously boo. Dark Wolf adjusts the belt over his shoulder and begins to speak.]
Dark Wolf: John was right. This is the best thing to hit the MAWL! The forming of Ability Unmatched, has been something we've planned for a while now. Tonight, it all comes together. Tonight, we become your worst nightmare!!
[The crowd boos.] Dark Wolf: Tonight, and every night following it, IS OUR NIGHT!! We will say what we want, and we will DO [cocky smile] whatever we want!
[The other members of AU nod in agreement.] Dark Wolf: Evenflow, wannabe, you will never be! Hope that hospital food is terrible! As for the rest of you losers in the back, prepare. AU is here,
AND AU IS HERE TO STAY!!! The MAWL is OURS!! And there is nothing you can do about it!
[The crowd is getting very rowdy now and showering the ring with garbage. Dark Wolf hands the mic to Coiler and he begins to talk.] Coiler: Its very simple the reason as to why I decided to join up here with the AU as Jack Nicholson put it, I was looking for a few good men. If you look at these two you will quickly see that they are just not good they are great, and now the MAWL will have to deal with the fact that we will damn well do anything we want, and for guys like Evenflow, Tommy Clemson, it is just the beginning of there nightmare, we are here to inflict pain on people whenever the hell we want too.
[ Everyone in the ring smiles as they look down at all the garbage that has filled up the ring ]
Coiler: Tommy, this is just the start of our love hate relationship, you see cause I love to see you in pain and I just simply hate your guts. I am warning you of more pain to come and more pain from all three of these men, so be very careful on how you conduct your business in the MAWL.
