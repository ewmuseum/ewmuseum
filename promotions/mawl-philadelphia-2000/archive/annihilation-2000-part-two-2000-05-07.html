Middle Atlantic Wrestling League ANNIHILATION 2000 - Live from the Pittsburgh Civic Center Sunday, May 7, 2000 http://www.crosswinds.net/~mawl Michael Reed: Welcome back to hour two of the Middle Atlantic Wrestling
League's first ever pay per view event. Up now is the East Coast Title match between John E. Moxlow and Coiler. This should be a great contest so let's cross over to Ternt Lyon who can introduce this match for us!
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- MAWL East Coast Title Match Coiler vs. "Five Star" John E. Moxlow Written by [name withheld] ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- Trent Lyon: This match is for the vacant MAWL East Coast Championship Title. It is scheduled for the one fall. Introducing first, from Philadelphia,
Pennsylvania, standing at 6 foot 6 and weighing in at 296 pounds... "FIVE
STAR" JOHN E. MOXLOW! [Everything goes black. The beat of the song kicks up with blue lasers and lights flashing to the bass. Once the vocals start Moxlow walks bobbing his head and stretching out his arms. He then makes his way towards the ring and slides in under the ropes. He walks around the ring then steps up on the ropes in a corner and just raises his hands. He then jumps down and does a bit of shadow boxing as he waits for his opponent.]
Trent Lyon: His opponent, from Detroit, Michigan, stands at 6 feet 2 inches and is weighing in at 240 pounds. Reprsenting Ability Unmatched... COILER!
["Open Your Eyes" by Guano Apes hits and Coiler walks out and the music cuts off.] Michael Reed: There's Coiler and Moxlow doesn't want to waste any time in this contest and he leaves the ring and begins running at Coiler. Coiler continues to walk down, ignoring Moxlow but he can't ignore him anymore, as he tackles the AU member down to the steel ramp.
[DING, DING, DING!] Damien Mullins: This bout is going to begin on the outside as the bell sounds. Moxlow and Coiler exchanging punches in the middle of the ramp and they're pushing back towards the ring. Coiler attempts to suplex Moxlow but
John E. blocks it and then suplexes Coiler onto the ringside mat. Looks like they're trying to make up for the missed Vicious Title match. Heh.
Michael Reed: Both men are in the ring and Moxlow with a kick to the midsection. Puts in the headlock and executes a textbook Northern Lights
Suplex on Coiler. Moxlow is happy about connected with that move as he taunts a few more fans at ringside.
Damien Mullins: Classic! All of these fans are going to end up getting a spray by the end of the night! Coiler is up now and Moxlow sees this and hits him with a T-Bone Suplex. Man, Moxlow has a large array of wrestling moves in his repetoire. I mean, he has four finishers!
Michael Reed: That's right and most of them would finish off the opponent. Damien Mullins: Most of them? You mean all of them! 5150 submission hold, the Dropzone which is shows amazing athleticism for a 296 pound man, the
Blackout which is my personal favorite and the Stardom which is the one he does through tables or onto chairs. Magnificent!
Michael Reed: It is quite impressive but all wrestlers in the MAWL are! Moxlow with a kick to the midsection and he tries for a powerbomb but Coiler flips over the back and then hooks Moxlow's arm between the legs and executes a pumphandle slam. That's a decent move Damien!
Damien Mullins: S'pose it is. Coiler pulls Moxlow to his feet and whips him into the ropes. He lifts him up then steps out of the way and Moxlow hits the mat face first after the flap jack from Coiler. It's like going back to wrestling school with the display being shown here by Coiler and John E.
Moxlow. Simply superb I reckon. Michael Reed: Coier goes up to the top rope and awaits a standing Moxlow. He gets what he wants and connects with a Flying Cross Bodyblock. Coiler remains on top for the pinfall attempt.
[ONE!] [TWO!] [SHOULDER UP!] Damien Mullins: Nope. Moxlow gets the shoulder up and Coiler isn't happy with the count. Five Star sneaks up on Coiler and grabs him around the waist, throwing him overhead for a release German suplex. Moxlow now leaving the ring and he's looking to spice up the match a little as he takes a chair from ringside and folds it up before sliding back into the ring.
Michael Reed: Moxlow is taunting Coiler as he gets up, holding the chair high above his head. Coiler up and he turns around...
< < C R A C K > > Damien Mullins: He goes down and of course, there won't be any disqualifications because the title holders need to be decided tonight here at Annihilation 2000. Coiler gets up again, clutching at his skull.
< < S M A C K > > Michael Reed: Another hard chair shot from John E. Moxlow and Coiler could receive brain damage if he keeps smacking him over the head like that! Phew.
He's getting rid of the chair and he lifts Coiler up from the mat and whips him into the ropes. Coiler ducks a clothesline but keeps going with the momentum and John E. Moxlow helps him along, tossing him over the top rope.
Damien Mullins: Moxlow is following him out and he's leading Coiler up the ramp. Moxlow puts Coiler in a side headlock and raises his right arm before
DDTing Coiler into the hard steel ramp. Blood has been drawn and it's coming from the face of Coiler.
Michael Reed: It's the same wound inflicted earlier in the night and Coiler is looking awfully flat footed in this match-up. Moxlow rolls Coiler into the ring and places him on the turnbuckle in the tree of woe. He isn't kneeing the midsection though, he's pulling back Coiler's neck and Moxlow looks like a man possessed and it seems he wants to break it.
Damien Mullins: Not a bad tactic. Moxlow takes a couple of steps back and goes for a diving shoulderblock but the referee was in the way, trying to free up Coiler fromt he corner and he's gone down. Wait a second! There's a fan jumping the guardrail and he's coming into the ring! SECURITY!
Michael Reed: That's not a fan, it's Blake "The Hurricane" Storm. He must've just signed with the MAWL but he's looking to leave his impact in this match as he hits Moxlow with his finisher then picks up Coiler and delivers it to him too. Not only are both competitors out cold after this attack but so is the referee and it could go either way from here.
Damien Mullins: This so called Hurricane is waking up the referee and he's starting the 10 count.
ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!! FOUR!!! FIVE!!! SIX!!! SEVEN!!! Michael Reed: Moxlow is starting to crawl over towards Coiler and can he pin him here? EIGHT!!! NINE!!! Damien Mullins: He drapes the arm across the chest of Coiler and Rich Delaney begins the count. [ONE!] [TWO!] [NO! RIGHT SHOULDER UP!] Damien Mullins: Coiler got the shoulder up and Moxlow can't believe it. Moxlow is first up and he goes for a running lariat but Coiler ducks. He kicks Moxlow in the midsection and drops him with a DDT. Coiler uses the adrenalin rushing through his blood to go up to the top rope and as Moxlow turns around, Coiler nails him with a flying dropkick.
Michael Reed: Coiler is pleased with that as he goes up top one more time. He twirls around his hand a little and goes for the moonsault but Moxlow gets the knees up and you think that if he tried that a split second earlier, this match could be over.
Damien Mullins: I'm sure Coiler would realize his mistake. He's feeling the pain of it right now as you can see! Moxlow hops up to his feet and pulls
Coiler up as well. He tucks the head between the legs and pulls up Coiler into a powerbomb position and he strolls over to the corner and powerbombs him into the steel turnbuckle.
Michael Reed: Only his left knee hit the steel turnbuckle and it must be hurting now as Coiler is grabbing at it. Moxlow stomps away the hand then begins stomping hard at the injured left knee.
Damien Mullins: Moxlow rolls out of the ring and pulls Coiler's legs towards the corner and wraps the left knee against the ring post. He pulls the leg back and you can hear the crack of the knee go against the ring post. Moxlow is one brutal wrestler and this could lead to great success in the MAWL.
Michael Reed: Coiler can hardly stand up now and is limping heavily on the left knee. Moxlow should finish him off right here and he puts Coiler on the apron and then gets on it himself. He's setting him up for what looks to be the Stardom? THE STARDOM RIGHT THROUGH OUR TABLE! COILER IS OUT COLD!
Damien Mullins: Damn it! They spilled by bottle of water! Anyway, Moxlow rolls Coiler into the ring and he makes the cover and this should be it!
[ONE!] [TWO!] [THREE!] Damien Mullins: Yep, that's all folks! Trent Lyon: The winner of this match by pinfall and NEW EAST COAST CHAMPION, "FIVE STAR" JOHN E. MOXLOW! Michael Reed: Moxlow is the first ever MAWL East Coast Champion and he has something to say!
John E. Moxlow: You know Coiler? [catching his breath] I told you the one thing. I would have is this belt. Did you listen to me? Nope. Did you take any advice that I gave you? Nope. Did you win? Heh, well seeing how you look now , that is fat nope. Yet seeing you laying there and trying to remember what actually happend to you, why don't I just show you?
[Moxlow picks up Coiler and hits him with the Blackout. He picks up the microphone again.] John E. Moxlow: Now, I am hoping that refreshed your memory. Now onto what matters. [He flings the belt over his shoulder and points up the ramp.]
Tuckwell, I told you already that the cold ride will come after I got this belt. ICE told you mainly the same thing. Now how we are going to do this...hmmm...You know what? I ain't going to tell you, you just gotta watch your back son because you never know where we will be.
[Moxlow drops the mic, hops over the top rope and down to the floor and heads off towards the back, cursing at the fans on the way.]
Damien Mullins: Congratulations to Moxlow but the most important match of the night is up next and you can hear the fans warming up their voices, ready for it to begin. Can you hear the crowd noise increasing Michael?
Michael Reed: I sure can and I can't wait to see who ends up the first ever
Middle Atlantic Champion! Just wait until Trent Lyon begins with the introductions, the screaming will blow the roof off!
Damien Mullins: Derecho won four round robin matches while Porter only needed to two to advance to the finals of it. Good luck to both men and may the best man win!
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- MAWL Middle Atlantic Title Match Dark Derecho vs. "Powerhouse" John Porter Written by [name withheld] ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- Trent Lyon: This match is FOR THE MIDDLE ATLANTIC CHAMPIONSHIP TITLE! [Most of the crowd stand up and begin cheering and clapping at the announcement that the match is finally here.]
Trent Lyon: Introducing first, weighing in at 306 pounds, he is from Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. Representing ABILITY UNMATCHED..."POWERHOUSE" JOHN PORTER! [The entrance ramp explodes in a shower of green and gold confetti as "Brutality" by Urban Voodoo begins to blast through the arena. The Powerhouse walks through the curtain and the fans saranade him with a chorus of boos. John takes in the reaction in, loving every second of it as he walks to the ring and slides in.]
Trent Lyon: His opponent, standing at 6 foot 1 and weighing in at 234, he is accompanied to the ring by Samantha, DARK... DERECHO!
["Busted" by Matchbox 20 comes over the loud speakers and Dark Derecho walks out to a mixed reaction. He walks down the ramp and slides under the bottom rope.]
Michael Reed: Woh! Derecho just hopped into the ring and clotheslined an unexpecting John Porter down to the mat and the plan to get the upper hand early has worked for Derecho.
[DING, DING, DING!] Damien Mullins: It did as Porter gets to his feet and Derecho uses a head scissors takeover to floor the big man once again. Derecho is giving up over
70 pounds and so far he's looking the better man but let's not forget that
John Porter is not at full strength after the tag team title match earlier.
Michael Reed: Also congratulations to Dark Derecho who won his round contest in the Master of the Squared Circle tournament earlier in the week during tapings. The MOTSC was aired earlier today and our very own president Ben
Tuckwell helped the MAWL go 2-0 after his win over the Incisor.
Damien Mullins: Alright! Good luck to the other guys in it as Porter uses his strength to overpower Dark Derecho in the tie-up and nails him with a belly-to-belly suplex. On the MOTSC tournament, both these men are in it meaning that the MAWL has a very good representation and being over different sides of the draw, they may very well meet in the finals.
Michael Reed: That would be great! Let's not draw our concentration too far away from this match. John Porter uses a short-arm clothesline on Dark
Derecho and I'm sure a few more momentum swings will be coming our way before the end of the night.
Damien Mullins: I think we're seeing one right now as Porter attempts for a release German suplex and Derecho shows his athleticism by flipping over to his feet. Kick to the midsection and the Dark One drops Porter face first with a stunning DDT.
Michael Reed: Derecho rapidly climbs to the top rope, looking to enforce some impact while the big man is still down. He hits John Porter with a flying twisting elbow drop and that's an admirable move from Dark Derecho.
He whips John Porter into the ropes and jumps up for a dropkick but Porter holds onto the ropes and Derecho hits air and goes down.
Damien Mullins: Porter pulls Derecho up to his feet and clocks him with a reverse DDT. The Powerhouse makes the cover.
[ONE!] [TWO!] [KICKOUT!] Michael Reed: Has John Porter got enough left in him to keep attacking? At the moment it seems so as he helps Derecho off his knees to his feet and puts him in the headlock. What this time? A ravishing brainbuster from the
Powerhouse John Porter and he's going for the cover but Samantha is up on the apron distracting the big man.
Damien Mullins: Hey, he's Derecho's girl. Dark Derecho is the big man! Porter is arguing with Samantha but he looks like he doesn't want to hit a girl and that goes against him because Dark Derecho is crawling over towards
Mr. Porter and he hits him in the jelly bag with a low blow and Porter goes down.
Michael Reed: A cheap shot from Dark Derecho and it looks like Samantha wants to put herself into this match as a factor in the end result. Porter is up after the low blow and Derecho gets him with a side Russian legsweep.
Damien Mullins: Derecho waits for Porter to get up again and he knocks him back with a running knee lift. Just remember, this is the setup to his finisher! Porter stumbles forward, right into Dark Derecho's waiting hand.
He pulls him down into a standing headscissors and lifts him.. CONNECTED! VORTEX POWERBOMB! Cover and maybe a new Middle Atlantic Champion! [ONE!] [TWO!] [THREE! NO! TWO AND NINE TENTHS!] Damien Mullins: Oh my! Porter had enough energy stored to kick out of the
Vortex Powerbomb at the last possible moment and Derecho can't believe his luck. Derecho tosses Porter over the top rope and now he's having a friendly little chat with Danny Graham as Samantha heads over to the Powerhouse.
Michael Reed: Yes. Derecho is arguing with Graham while Samantha is kicking
John Porter as hard as she can and the Powerhouse is definitely feeling it in the abdominals after Clemson's submission hold in tonight's first match.
Damien Mullins: Well Samantha feels the beating is thorough enough as she steps out of the way and Derecho let's go of Danny Graham who tells off
Samantha for getting too close to the action but he didn't see the attack so it worked out well. Derecho is leaving the ring and we're about to see some action on the outside now and we like that, that's where all the weapons are!
Michael Reed: They're coming our way as well and Derecho slams Porter head first into our announce table. Dark goes for an Irish Whip into the steel steps but Porter reverses it and Derecho goes flying over the top of the steel stairs.
Damien Mullins: Decent reversal and Porter is fighting back after looking so down and out less than 30 seconds ago. Porter places Derecho on the top turnbuckle now and he goes up himself. Side headlock and he hits him with a swinging DDT.
Michael Reed: Derecho rolls out of the ring to take a breather and Porter follows him and once again the brawl spills to the outside. Porter tosses
Derecho over the ringside barricade and now we're into the crowd as both men are exchaning right hands. Derecho seems to have the advantage and he tosses
Porter back over the barricade to ringside.
Damien Mullins: Derecho rolls the Powerhouse into the ring and whips him into the ropes. Porter bounces back off and Derecho uses a drop toe hold.
Immediately Derecho lifts Porter to his feet and executes a Urinage Suplex. Derecho blatantly chokes Porter in front of the referee and Samantha distracts Danny once more as Derecho uses his boot now, standing on the throat of John Porter.
Michael Reed: He's going to leave a pink boot mark on Porter's throat if he continues to apply this pressure. Porter can hardly breathe right now and
Samantha finally hops off the apron when Derecho releases the choke. What's
Samantha doing now? She's heading our way but she's gone past us and over to the timekeeper area. OH NO!
Damien Mullins: OH YEAH! Samantha has the ring bell and she rolls in into the ring to Dark Derecho. Danny Graham is warning him with disqualification but he won't get disqualified in a Middle Atlantic Title match, especially the first one! Porter is getting up... DERECHO CLOCKS HIM WITH THE RING
BELL! THIS IS IT! [ONE!] [TWO!] [THREE!] Michael Reed: DERECHO WINS! PORTER IS NOT HAPPY! Trent Lyon: The winner of this match and NEW MIDDLE ATLANTIC CHAMPION... DARK DERECHO! Damien Mullins: Porter is arguing with the referee about a fast count while
Derecho is celebrating. John Porter sees him and charges but Derecho sees the bottled up emotion and ducks out of the way. He kicks Porter in the gut.
VORTEX POWERBOMB FOR A SECOND TIME! UN-DAMN-BELIEVABLE! Michael Reed: Derecho kicks Porter out of the ring to a waiting Samantha who has a lead pipe. She is repeatedly whacking John Porter in those sore ribs and he's going to be injured after this, you can tell. Let's see what Dark
Derecho does to celebrate, shall we?

Dark Derecho: This is quite possibly the most important day in my life. I want to thank the fans for being there. Although all of your stupid asses were so ignorant not to recognize my talent, all live in the same trailer park, have kids that go out and sell their drugs and their bodies so you can eat at night, and all that other stuff.. you still managed to put away your third world living conditions and come see your hero win the MAWL title.
Bless your wretched hearts! You've all been great.. really, you have. [Derecho wipes a tear from his face. As the fans are upset with him, booing and tossing garbage into the ring.]
Dark Derecho: Now onto business. Since I am the MAWL champion, I'm going to have people gunning for me left and right. All I have to say is bring it on.
As I proved here in this round robin, I am the best, I am that good, I cannot be defeated, and that 5-0 record goes and proves it and as I said earlier... it's true.. I am the best and I just proved it, and I will prove it again to anyone who thinks they have the balls to come and step up. So if you think you've got something to prove to me.. I'm right here.. come and get it.
[A moment of silence.] Dark Derecho: That's what I thought. [Cross over to announcers.] Michael Reed: It's disappointing that this night has to come to an end but it definitely was a good show and kudos to all of the MAWL wrestlers who participated. Recapping results... Clemson-Heal Connection are tag team champions, Tommy Clemson won the number one contenders match, John E. Moxlow is the East Coast Champion and Dark Derecho is the Middle Atlantic Champion.
Damien Mullins: And guess what? Ability Unmatched has walked out of tonight empty handed and with some serious injuries to worry about. Next week at
Breaking Point, we have a Vicious Rumble for the Vicious Title of course so we'll see you then. Goodnight all!
