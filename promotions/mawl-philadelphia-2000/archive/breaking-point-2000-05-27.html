MAWL (Philadelphia, 2000) — Card Archive

Saturday Night Breaking Point, Part I

27 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@ The Middle Atlantic Wrestling League presents… SATURDAY NIGHT BREAKING POINT Saturday, 27 May, 2000, 7PM EST Live from The Fire Hall: West Newton, PA Web: http://www.crosswinds.net/~mawl @@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@ [“Demons” by Offspring begins to play and the previously black screen engulfs in flames. The flames slowly die down until a hazy gray smoke covers the screen. The smoke slowly dissolves to reveal the red MAWL logo behind it. The logo flies off the screen and we fade into The Fire Hall in West
Newton, Pennsylvania, which is packed with fans. The Breaking Point logo spins around in the foreground as we look at some of the signs in the crowd including: “DARK WOLF IS THE TRUE CHAMPION”, “I WANT TO BE HYPNOTISED” and
“SHOW ME THE MONEY CHRIS REGISTER!” We then pan over to the announce table where Michael Reed is joined by his regular Co-host, Damien Mullins.]
Michael Reed: Good evening and welcome to Breaking Point. My name is Michael
Reed and I’m joined by Indy fed legend Damien Mullins. How are you Damien?
Damien Mullins: I’m great Michael. I’ve had a nice week touring the states looking for the best free agents in the wrestling business and I’ve been talking with some big names, including Nytemare, Gravis Knight and Puah “the
Hawaiian Love Machine”. There are a few other names that I got in contact with and I’ll let you know about them later Michael, but all of them would be great recruits for the MAWL to sign. They may help replace our soon to be depleted roster if Ben Tuckwell fires half of them as he says he might.
Michael Reed: While you were away on your recruiting missions, I hosted the new MAWL flash card shows which turned out to be a great success, both receiving a rating just over 2.0 in our telecast area. Tuckwell revealed plans of expanding the MAWL worldwide, which would be experience, all of its own.
Damien Mullins: It definitely would, but let’s move right along and preview tonight’s matches and you could even mistake this line-up for a pay per view card, that’s how good it is.
Michael Reed: We start with “The System” Chris Cain and Steven Belar, two relatively newcomers to the MAWL with each only having wrestled one match,
Cain with a loss and Belar with a draw. Both will be wanting to put a win on the board to get up the confidence levels.
Damien Mullins: Steven Belar is my choice by far. He’s got so many moves in his arsenal that he could wrestle a whole month worth of matches and not use one move twice. Hopefully he puts on a display for the fans and wrestling enthusiasts can learn something from this guy.
Michael Reed: Second match of the night is to be contested between Chris
Register and W. Life, both with terrific athleticism and reasonable mat skills, it should be a joy to watch. This match could go either way in my opinion.
Damien Mullins: I’m not gonna pick a winner for that match right now, I’ll make my prediction a bit later on when I see how they look after they come out to wrestle. Match three is the big number one contenders match between former champion Dark Derecho and returning superstar Hypnosis. This is a match-up we’ve been waiting to see for a long time and whoever wins will have a tough task ahead of them, having to face either John Porter or Dark
Wolf at Quake of Fire on June 18th from Buffalo, New York.
Michael Reed: Titles are on the line in our second hour, beginning with The
Kreibs and Ice for the Vicious Title. Kreibs has been in top form, disposing of Shawn Saxon comprehensively while Ice hasn’t done much lately. Kreibs for me in that match.
Damien Mullins: I’m going with The Kreibs as well, making him a warm favorite to pick up his first MAWL title, the Vicious Title. Tag Team Title action is on the blocks following it, with the Clemson-Heal Connection making their first defense of the titles since Annihilation 2000 when they take on newcomers The Brothers Grimm, who caused havoc last week on Breaking
Point, showing up looking exactly the same.
Michael Reed: The MAWL has eradicated that problem, barring them from wearing masks in any of their future matches. The Brothers Grimm are however the MAWL’s only specialist tag team and will be a tough challenge for the
CHC, but I’m sticking with the sentimental favorites and tipping Tommy
Clemson and Billy Heal to retain.
Damien Mullins: Nah… Tim and Jim Grimm must have more tricks stored up their sleeves and I think they’ll be WAY too good for The Clemson-Heal Connection, who haven’t even proved themselves yet. They won the tag team titles in a handicap match and lost a six man tag match to Digital Underground so I wouldn’t exactly say they are on a hot streak right now.
Michael Reed: Our main event sees two former Ability Unmatched members meeting up for the MIDDLE ATLANTIC CHAMPIONSHIP! Dark Wolf and “Powerhouse”
John Porter will square off and the champion Porter will have to be favoured to win the match with Dark Wolf returning from a serious injury months before he is supposed to. I wouldn’t be surprised if Dark Wolf is taken out of here in a stretcher with two broken ankles tonight.
Damien Mullins: How can you say that? John Porter is Dark Wolf’s buddy and I doubt that he’ll go out there with the intention to cause injury. I admit that the Powerhouse is unpredictable at times, but to go that far, I don’t think so.
Michael Reed: Porter has shown that he’ll go to extraordinary lengths for the title, punching out Samantha last week. I don’t think it’s beyond him to target Dark Wolf’s ankles if it means him retaining the title for at least another three weeks until Quake of Fire. We need to get proceedings underway and Trent Lyon is in the ring, ready to introduce our first match.
@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@ SINGLES MATCH “The System” Chris Cain vs. Steven “Lustful” Belar Written by [name withheld] @@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@ Trent Lyon: The following contest is tonight's opening match and it is scheduled for the one fall. Introducing first, from Friendship, Maine, weighing in at 240 pounds... "THE SYSTEM" CHRIS CAIN!
[The crowd sits patiently in the arena. Chattering and slight talk is heard through the stands. All of a sudden the sounds of "El Phantasmo" starts up.
Cameras focus in on the entrance as out walks Chris Cain, wearing his standard wrestling tights. Red and black covered, with the words "The
System" on the back in dark red scar style writing. Cain looks over the crowd, and begins his walk down to the ring slightly giving looks to those in the aisle seats. Proceeding to walk up the steps, and onto the ring apron, taking one final look over the audience, Cain step through the ropes, and into the ring.]
Trent Lyon: His opponent, from Miami, Florida, at a weight of 245 pounds... STEVEN "LUSTFUL" BELAR! ["I'm Too Sexy" by Right Said Fred blares over the public announcing system, it continues for a few moments, the fans begin to wonder who will emerge from the back suddenly the curtains part down the middle and Steven Belar comes out clad in his wrestling attire as he jogs down the ring though taking enough time to flirt with the ringside women. Finally, after doddling long enough Belar slides gracefully into the ring.]
Damien Mullins: Boy, Belar’s entrance is a joy to watch and did you see that blonde in the front row with the big you know what’s?
Michael Reed: Yeah Damo, I saw her and she looks like your type. [DING, DING, DING!] Michael Reed: Belar and Cain circle each other and they tie-up. Belar gets underneath Cain and scoops him up and slams him onto the mat. Belar locks in the surfboard hold and The System makes it to the ropes after a brief moment of pain.
Damien Mullins: Chris Cain into the ropes now.. drop toe hold! Belar picks up Cain from the mat and goes for another whip but it's reversed and Chris
Cain connects with a kick to the gut followed up by a DDT.
Michael Reed: Cain pulls up Steven Belar and puts him in a front facelock. He lifts him up into a suplex position and this is his famous hanging version of the move. He's holding up Belar for what seems an eternity before sending the mat specialist crashing back down to earth.
Damien Mullins: Chris Cain sends Belar into the ropes and ducks his head which allows Belar to catch him in a Sunset Flip. Cain is trying not to be pulled down but Belar gets a handful of tights to pull down The System for the pinfall attempt.
[ONE!] [TWO!] [KICKOUT!] Michael Reed: It's a bit too early and Chris Cain managed to escape by kicking Belar in the face with both feet. Both men up and Belar attempts a kick to the midsection but Cain managed to hold onto the right leg and now he's doin' some talking to Belar who is in a nowhere position.. ENZUGIRI! He didn't see that coming and now Belar is the one gloating.
Damien Mullins: And he has the right to do that if he feels inclined. Belar goes up to the top rope very quickly... TOUGH LOVE! He connected and this could be over after only around two minutes of action. Wait! Belar isn't making the cover and Chris Cain sits up.
Michael Reed: Belar dropkicks him back down to the mat and he's locked in the Texas Cloverleaf... CAIN IS TAPPING! IT'S OVER ALREADY!
[DING, DING, DING!] Trent Lyon: The winner of this match by submission... STEVEN "LUSTFUL" BELAR! Michael Reed: Chris Cain is one of the men in danger of losing their contracts and this performance will do him no good whatsoever, losing is under 3 minutes. It was simply shocking in my opinion, and I'm one of the fairer critic.
Damien Mullins: For once I'll have to agree with you. Chris Cain never looked like winning as soon as the bell rang and he definitely will need to pick up his act if he is to keep his contract, with Ben Tuckwell looking to improve his roster for the possible interfed pay per view against the North
American Wrestling Council.
Michael Reed: We're hoping the NAWC doesn't back out because that would give the MAWL a lot of exposure, with the pay per view being telecast all over the world. MAWL stars such as John Porter, Dark Derecho and Hypnosis could be taking on the best in the world with names such as Rick Stevens and Jason
Blake.
Damien Mullins: Well Chris Cain is walking up the ramp now and the crowd are booing a man who they used to cheer for and The System doesn't look to pleased. He can't blame the crowd for the result though, it was all his wrong doing and the only to blame is himself.
Michael Reed: In our next match, we have two promising youngsters battling it out but first I have a memo to read out from the desk of President Ben
Tuckwell. At MAWL Quake of Fire on June 18th, a mini-tournament is to be held to determine the Middle Atlantic Wrestling League's first lightweight champion. This is a new belt that is to be introduced at Quake of Fire and we've got plenty of great high flyers who fit the bill perfectly for this type of thing.
Damien Mullins: Some superstars that may be participating will include new signing Impetigo plus one of the wrestlers from our following match in W.
Life may also take this opportunity. All those under the weight of 235 pounds have the option to be included.
Michael Reed: The pay per view is really starting to take shape now and by the end of tonight, we shall know who will be facing off for the Middle
Atlantic Title at Quake of Fire. Anyway, we’ll get on with the card and
Chris Register shall take on W. Life.
Damien Mullins: This is probably the toughest match of the night to a pick a winner for. Life looked very impressive last week, with Life picking up a win against Ron Jolla who has been released from his contract while Chris
Register has had a lot of talking to do and seems to be in top form and confident about his match. I won’t be taking sides but if I had to pick one,
I think Register would come out on top in a very, very close match. Good luck to both men though!
@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@ SINGLES MATCH Chris Register vs. W. Life Written by [name withheld] @@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@ Trent Lyon: This match is a singles match scheduled for one fall only. Introducing first, from Toronto, Ontario, Canada, weighing in at 240 pounds… W. LIFE! [“Hotel California” by the Eagles starts to play and W. Life emerges from behind the curtain. He struts down the ramp and slides under the bottom rope and signals for his music to cut off, which it does.]
Damien Mullins: That is one song I would hate to have as my theme music. It’ s one of those songs you would hear being played at a mother’s meeting and that’s the one thing that will cost W. Life this match… his music.
Michael Reed: Are you serious? I hope not because ability is a lot more important than a ring entrance.
Damien Mullins: Speaking of ring entrances, watch this one by Chris Register! It’ll be superb like usual! Trent Lyon: His opponent, from Madison, Wisconsin, weighing in at 230 pounds… CHRIS REGISTER!
[The lights in the arena begin to dim as search lights flash throughout the crowd. Then all the lights stop and the arena is silent and dark. Suddenly a huge explosion goes off startling the crowd and shooting flames high into the air. "Nobody's Real" begins to play over the PA system as the lights turn back on. Standing in the ring entrance is Chris Register with his arms stretched out in the air. He blows kisses and flexes his muscles as he makes his way down the ramp and into the ring.]
Damien Mullins: Our president Ben Tuckwell is from Wisconsin and it must please him to see another guy from his home state in the Middle Atlantic
Wrestling League. Anyway, that entrance is one of the best I’ve seen in the
MAWL, it had the lot. Lights going out, explosions, flames and most importantly, great music.
Michael Reed: Danny Graham is ready for this match to begin so ring the bell and let’s see who out of these two is better, Chris Register or W. Life?
Damien Mullins: I’d have to say Chris Register. [DING, DING, DING!] Michael Reed: Similar start to our first match as they lock up in battle. Register with the go-behind and he has W. Life in a back grapple and puts him on the canvas with a backdrop. Chris Register quickly into the ropes and connects with a quick legdrop across the throat of Life, sucking the air right out of him.
Damien Mullins: Register picks up the hippie from the mat and sends him into the ropes and catches him on the shoulders. He’s holding him there and it looks like W. Life will be the victim of the Samoan Drop… wait, W. Life is using ALL of his energy to counter this move. Register on the mat in a crucifix but he kicked out before our referee could even begin the count.
That definitely proves Chris Register is one tough customer. Michael Reed: Both men are up on their feet and are circling each other. Chris Register offers a test of strength to Life and W. Life is accepting it which is a bad idea as Register takes advantage and kicks Life in the midsection and he doubles over in pain. Register with a side headlock… DDT!
Chris Register nailed W. Life with a DDT and Register is sitting atop the turnbuckle, having a breather while W. Life gets back up.
Damien Mullins: Life is up and he turns around and heads toward the turnbuckle on which Chris Register is seated. Life lunges at Register but
Chris kicks him in the face knocking him back down to the mat. Register gets up into a standing position on top of the turnbuckle and as W. Life gets on his feet, Register leaps off and knocks him down with a spinning heel kick from the top rope. Not a bad move, eh?
Michael Reed: Quite a good move actually Damien. Chris Register is going to attempt to make W. Life tap here as he crosses the legs over and turns him onto his stomach, locking in the sharpshooter, applying pressure as he goes.
Will W. Life tap out like Chris Cain did earlier tonight, only time will tell I guess but I think W. Life has a higher pain tolerance than that of
Chris Cain.
Damien Mullins: W. Life is certainly giving it all he’s got in his quest to reach the ropes. Chris Register’s had enough and he breaks the hold with
Life only inches away from the ropes. Some angry stomps from Register and he doesn’t want W. Life mounting any sort of attack during this match from the looks of things.
Michael Reed: Register bounces into the ropes and he goes for an elbow drop but W. Life was ready and he rolled out of the way to buy himself some time.
Life stands up and helps Register up as well from his previous position on the mat. Whip into the ropes from Life and he gets Chris Register in the face with a dropkick. Cover from Life.
[ONE!] [TWO!] [KICKOUT!] Damien Mullins: Kick out at two by Chris Register and there is no way that he’s going to lose after a dropkick. W. Life will need to annihilate him by executing The Dew on several different occasions if he is to have any chance of winning.
Michael Reed: By the sounds of things, you’re making out that Chris Register is super human… I think if W. Life hit The Dew once, a three count would almost be assured. Chris Register goes for a clothesline after being whipped into the ropes by Life by he misses with it and turns around to be scooped up and nailed by a Michinoku Driver at the hands of W. Life. Life thinks about the cover but instead goes up to the second rope. Could this be a moonsault?
Damien Mullins: He attempts it but Register smartly rolled out of the way to avoid the impact and both men are down. Life is the first to get up, followed soonly after by Chris Register. Life with a kick to the gut… DAMN
IT! HE HIT THE DEW ON REGISTER! Hooks the leg…
[ONE!] [TWO!] [THREE! NO!] Michael Reed: Register escaped that count in the nick of time and W. Life will need to put in a little more effort to beat Chris Register with a three count.
Damien Mullins: See Michael, I have all the answers right here and I knew that one hit wouldn’t knock down Chris Register. It’s gonna take three strikes to get Chris Register out and W. Life places Register in a standing leglock. Register kicks him up into the air and amazingly, W. Life lands neck first on the top rope and that was perfectly executed on Chris Register
’s behalf. I bet he didn’t even feel the pain of The Dew.
Michael Reed: I think you should ask him that Damien, because as you can see, Chris Register is stumbling around a little and The Dew has probably caused him to become dizzy. He sees W. Life though and taps him on the shoulder. Life turns around and is met with a kick to the midsection and
Register places him in a standing headscissors, Powerbomb from Chris
Register! Could this be all? Damien Mullins: We all know what time it is, it’s time for the Critical
Condition! He grabs the legs of Life and he can feel that they’re limp, so he lifts them up and rolls W. Life over onto his front and Danny Graham is calling for the bell and Chris Register wins it.
Trent Lyon: The winner of this match by submission… CHRIS REGISTER! Michael Reed: Great match by both men and kudos to Chris Register on an impressive victory. We need to take a break fans but after it is over, we’ll see tonight’s big matches including John Porter and Dark Wolf for the Middle
Atlantic Title. But first we’ll see who will have the rights to face the winner of that match, it either being Dark Derecho or Hypnosis. Be right back, stay tuned!
