Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

American Allstar Wrestling's own surviving record opens sideways: a 3 October 1999 classified-ad post promoting a message-board service for other e-feds — help wanted, webmasters, graphics designers, wrestlers looking to sign — closing with its own credit line, “Presented by American Allstar Wrestling,” and a link to its own Angelfire-hosted site.

A second post the next day, from its own vice president, recruits directly: an “interfed PPV” against a fed styled TTOSWF is coming, with every one of its titles on the line, and a large roster is about to be cut down for members who will not roleplay. “Please only sign up if you intend to role-play,” it warns, pointing applicants to the same site.

No founding statement, roster, card or result of any kind survives — including for the interfederation pay-per-view the second post announces.
