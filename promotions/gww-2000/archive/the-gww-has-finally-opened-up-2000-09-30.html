The GWW Has Finally Opened Up!

30 September 2000

Today, history has been made as the GWW opened up. Right now, we're a small fed, with a small staff with an average web design but we hope to expand very shortly. We have a very experienced staff that knows what they are doing with angles, storylines and such and they know how to do it well!
Things will expand over time, not many links are up now but we are working on them. We wanted to get the rules and application up so people could join.
The url is http://members.home.net/gww2000 We're looking for staff members to help us out. - 1 or 2 match writers. - 1 or 2 logo designers. - 3 or 4 columnists. - Hotline Reporter - 1 or 2 Rp Judges If any of these jobs interest you, contact Rob Black at [detail withheld] That's it for now, we hope you check out the site and help us out.
