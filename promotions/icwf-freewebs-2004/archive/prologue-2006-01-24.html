IN THE BEGINING I. THE HITMAN RETURNS December 2005 Bret Hart's long awaited return to the ring took place in the thriving X-Treme Championship Wrestling in the winter of 2005. Bret immiediatly became a top contender and was adored by the fans once again. However, there was one man that didn't share this admiration for the Hitman. XCW's Owner and proven promoting genius, Chad Costello. Despite Bret Hart's ascension to the top of the ranks, and his outstanding battles at the top of XCW's cards, it quickly became clear the Chad Costello hated Bret with every fiber of his being. Going to such lengths as bringing the Hart family out to publicaly berate Bret. Constant interferences in his matches and generally being a thorn in the side of Bret lead to a backstage blowout of epic porportions. Bret was respected backstage but all the respect in the world couldn't help what came next. His last match occured on XCW's flagship show, Slaughter. In his final epic match, Bret squared off against good friend Sting for the XCW World Title. Thanks to Costello, Bret Hart's own nephew, Teddy Hart cost his uncle the match by brutally attacking him with brass knuckles. Chad, feeling victorious, finally fired Bret shortly after embarrassing him on a national stage. II. THIS MEANS WAR Late January 2006 Of course, Bret would not take this lying down. Returning back to Calgary that night, Bret began preparing to unleash an idea he's had in his mind a long time... A promotion that thrives on that excellence of it's workers, a federation FOR THE FANS. With low capital, and a small roster, Bret looks to take it to Costello the only way he can. ICWF doesn't have the corporate backing that XCW and EWA have, so Hart got on the horn with small venues in attempts to book his shows. Quickly, the ICWF was the talk of the internet wrestling community. Finally an edgier alternative, a gritty, underground RAW federation seperate from the glitz and glamour of XCW.
