An Introduction ¨°²~·-.¸.¸,¸.·'`·..·´'·.¸,¸.¸.-·~²°¨ ICWF... A name that has lingered in the name of one wrestling fan for nearly all his life... ICWF is a federation that originated from it's belligerent and hard headed creator, Lance. Avery has been a wrestler for nearly ten hard years and a wrestling fan all of his life. At age 26, about two years ago, he was going after the only thing that alluded him his entire career. A world Championship. In the federation known as CIW, Lance was a star. Having conquered the tag ranks with good friend Orlando Robinson, dominationg the US Title scene and running rampant with the North American title... He started the journey. The long hard quest to finally acheive what every wrestler dreams of becoming, a World Champion. Only three weeks away from his scheduled title shot, Lance sustained a _serious_ neck injury after being thrown off a balcony, through a stack of tables. Lance was sidelined for over 5 months, missing his beloved title shot. Doctor's told him that he should and could no longer compete in the squared circle. Devastated by the unjust news, he went into a serious depression, lasting nearly 10 long months. His long time girlfriend, Ashley Parks, was there for him. Trying to coax him to getting his life back together. Until one day, she struck a chord. The idea of running his own wrestling promotion was one that he pondered for nearly a year before taking true action. He saved up as much money as he possibily could, before setting out. It took him a few months but he finally found a company that could support his idea. The World Sports Network took Avery's word and gave him most of the money he'd need to get things started. They told him that if ICWF got off to a good enough begining, he`d see more money roll in. With all the dough he was given, Lance began on a one man quest. For 10 months he searched, wandered and looked everywhere for the whole shebang. He found rings, belts, staff and he even purchased an official ICWF Arena in his hometown of Seattle, Washington. The man worked twenty four hours a day, seven days a week, five weeks a month. With alot of blood, sweat and tears poured into the operation, Avery is ready to present ICWF to the public... ¸.-·~²°¨'·.¸,¸..·´`·..¸,¸.·'¨°²~·-.¸ 2.
