Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This UeWA introduced itself on 31 August 1997 as “a new league,” its founder assuring readers he was not “some kid who has no idea of what he's doing.” It planned to use both real and invented wrestlers, one card a week (“undecided”), and asked for two roleplays a week from every handler along with a sample roleplay to join.

Four championships were named at the outset: a World Title, an International Title, a Cruiser Title and a Tag Team Title — matching, title for title, a second promotion of the same name that had opened only two days before.

No card, roster, or further document of this UeWA survives beyond its founding post. What became of it, or whether it ever ran a card, is not recorded.
