Greetings people, Myself and a friend [Chad Dupree] are starting up a brand spankin' new e-fed called New Breed Wrestling [NBW]. We're looking to do this thing the way it's supposed to be done and are looking for quality roleplayers for the league. It's run on TNM 6.2 PLUS, but is semi-booked, so if you don't flash you'll get jobbed. It will be run mainly through e-mails and the web page [when it's published] will be for basic information on the league and have a "flash" button on it. You are allowed to have 3 wrestlers, but you can have less. One wrestler for singles and the other two will be for tag team. The titles are as follows: NBW World Heavyweight Title NBW TV Title NBW World Jr. Heavyweight Title [for those wrestlers 235 pounds or less] NBW World Tag Team Titles NBW TV Tag Team Titles All World titles will be defended monthly and TV titles will be defended on a weekly basis. There will be two weekly shows, taking place on Monday's and Friday's and a PPV will occur once every two months. That's the basic info...if you have any questions feel free to e-mail me or my co-president [ [detail withheld] ]. The application is below...thanks for your time.
