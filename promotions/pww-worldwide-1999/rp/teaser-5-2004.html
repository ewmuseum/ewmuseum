What this is One of the federation’s own countdown teasers to the reopening: Blaise McClancy reads the invitation that brings him to the PWW, and with it the federation’s own account of who ran it before and who runs it now.

Rebirth?

The wide expanse known as the Upper Mojave Desert.

A small place, located about an hour north of a small town. You’ve probably heard about it… it’s called Las Vegas. A place for gamblers, sinners and people who are hard up on their luck to come and finally feel some sense of acceptance.

People come here to make their fortunes; they come to make their lives better. Or at least have the chance to do so.

Las Vegas grows and stretches its fingers everywhere here.

And if you think it’s bad trying to come here and get away, imagine having been born here.

But not born in Las Vegas itself. Born in a small little place known as the Valley of Fire. A holy land to the original inhabitants many years ago. Before Bugsy Segal came and built the flamingo. Before the railroad and work camps for the damn just a bit south, down at Boulder City. Before the mobs, before the corporations, before anything else was here.

The Paiute were here. This was their land. Now reduced to their little reservations… but they’re some of the richest people here. At least the ones in charge of them are. They’ve opened their own casinos, smoke shops that sell killing tobacco for cheap prices with no taxes, and fireworks that would normally be illegal off the extraterritorial land that is the Paiute reservation.

It’s no wonder that I’ve wanted to leave the place so many times. But I keep coming back.

It’s home.

Last time I came back, I have only Rebecca with me. We could have left at any time after my knee healed. But now with both Rhiannon and Breanna here as well, it makes it hard. The money runs thinner each day. I still can’t believe I’ve not been able to make my return.

One year ago I learned my lesson in humility at mine own cousin’s hands. 1 year ago I lost my grace, fell like so many others. Cast aside by the fans. Why? Because I wasn’t there. Because I had to go have the doctors put half a pound of steel and plastic into my knee. Because my head had swollen to the point where my own family saw it right to set me back down to reality.

Then, what was it? 6 months ago, I tried to return for a tourney. But I wasn’t ready. No. Wasn’t ready. Again, no one knew me. I’m not remembered for being on the top of the game.

I was at the top. The top of a federation that coined itself as Superior to others for 5 months. And while I was on top, they were truly that in a little place known as Revolution Studios. But how things change. Rev Studios has now become something else, and that federation is no more.

They lost me, and then lost the other McClancy’s and their ilk, and it all went down hill from there.

Nothing left of my old history but dust…

I guess my latest claim to fame was successfully getting my cousin, Fey, ready for her performance at the CWMC a few months ago.

Things needed to change soon though. Fortunately for me, even when I was making in the line of 5 million dollars a month, I was living frugally. But then again, growing up in a place like this kinda teaches you the value of money. But with my two new daughters, I need to do something.

Fey and Ian have both offered me a place in their federation, but I feel that some would think that the cousin of the President and former student of the CEO would get special treatment. I don’t need that. I’ve proven myself in other places.

But what?

Ah, yes, finally the drive is over.

 

The man known as Blaise McClancy pulls his smoke gray Chevy Avalanche up to the driveway of his house. A small oasis hiding only a few hundred yards from the lines that separate the state of Nevada, county of Clark from the world of the natives from which his mother comes from.

Dust rises as he comes to a stop. The yard isn’t sand like some would think out here. He’s had it rocked over with a mixture of colors. They form geometric patterns around many plants that he’s taken to cultivating. From native cacti to even his bonsai and dwarf trees. A hobby he picked up when learning his art in Japan.

Something that many see as a strange hobby, seeing as how he’s not Japanese.

He’s of Native blood. But not like any other native many have ever seen. His mother came from the Paiute, from a line descending from many holy men and women.

But his father, the great Conner McClancy, was an Irish immigrant who had been outcast from his family over some very controversial things, including the want to marry the woman he loved over in the states.

As the truck comes to a stop, it rocks a little as he slams on the parking brake, taking it out of gear. The engine’s roar comes to a stop and the door opens.

Out steps a man who looks nothing like you’d advertise as a Native Wrestler. But it’s obvious his two different heritages.

Standing there, pulling his bags from the back storage area on the vehicle, is a man not much over six feet in height. His face and skin tone scream that he’s where he belongs, on the reservation. In fact, if not for the other traits, he’d look not unlike Jay Phoenix’s own family.

But something sets him apart: The emerald green eyes, and fiery red hair. Currently sitting on his head with all of its weight pulling it down to the full extent of it’s length, just down to his shoulder blades. He squints in the sun, but doesn’t bother putting on any shades, only a twenty or so foot walk to the front door.

The hair flies from his face as he walks. The very color of it hasn’t changed since he was born. It’s what prompted his father to give him his first name, Blaise, a Gaelic name meaning ‘firebrand’.

He gets to the door, and opens it fully. The hinges squeak a bit as the dry environment doesn’t take much to start to take a toll.

"I’ll have to do something about that soon", he thinks to himself.

"Shh!!", a gentle voice responds to his entrance. He looks over and sees his girlfriend. The woman he loves, sitting in a chair. She looks tired, but still looks as perfect as ever.

She has just two months ago given birth to twin girls: The McClancy curse. Conner and Raven had avoided it somehow, but it had hit him. Every McClancy family had been twins for some time. In fact, Duncan, his uncle had two sets of twins.

But both girls were beautiful, Rhiannon and Breanna. His father had requested the names, to keep them Gaelic in nature. Becca had been receptive enough.

Yeah, unlike most people who seem to be in this field of work, Blaise got along rather ‘smashingly’ with his father and mother. He’d never thought about killing them, or anything. And his first teacher for wrestling had been his father in his own backyard.

Blaise looked upon the woman that he lived with, and had mothered his children. She had started out in wrestling while he was in school. She had a history of being mostly unknown. Formerly a manager of a tag team in GIWF, she had come under Childress’ wing when his friends had ‘won’ her off that other team.

Then she had managed a few other tag teams, even in such feds as the PWW. It was when she was acting as Ian Childress’ secretary in the fed he was in that they met and continued from there. First she was his valet, then his date, now the mother of his children. But not his wife.

Not yet. He had to get things straight in his life first.

He looked at her, and then. Two little red haired girls laying asleep on the couch in their little pallets. They’d fallen asleep while drinking from their bottles, surrounded by pillows. The dogs must be outside.

Some peace and quiet for them and her.

"Don’t worry", he whispered to her. He then tossed his backpack into another chair. It made a noise with the metal leg weights he used for his running hitting each other. A sour look from her let him know that he was treading on thin ice now.

Before he closes the door, he reaches out and grabs the mail from the box next to it.

The door then shuts with a small ‘click’.

Becca starts to talk softly, "How was it?"

"It sucked. Sometimes I wonder what some of these promoters are thinking. I mean, pay me a couple of grand to come in and basically not do anything. I don’t mind helping the guys in the feds, but damn, the guy counting the money is the one making out like a bandit. Not them.

The poor guy I fought was scared shitless knowing just who I was when I got there. Sometimes this whole forget who "Hazard" was hurts them. They hear my real name, and think that it’s some pushover, and then the guy has to Take the Risk. I swear this poor guy almost pissed his pants in the ring when I took him up onto my shoulders." He was going to get into trouble if he kept talking. But he had to get it out.

"Tell you what, tell me about it later when we’re cuddling. I don’t want to wake them right now to move them into the nursery." She replied to him, moving him to talk about something else.

She knew that he was on the edge. He needed to get back into the sport fully. But with the loss of his previous home, only one place had made him an offer. The fed owned by his own family was not a place he wanted to go though. It was bad enough when everyone in that other place thought that he got everything handed to him so many times. But he held their highest accolade for 5 months straight and had defeated all that had come for him.

But one… The one that had broken his knee.

Sean still called, so did Fey. But Blaise wouldn’t sign with them. He was afraid of the backlash, afraid that the fans would not see him for what he truly was.

"What’s in the mail today, babe?" Again, she tried to change the subject.

He started to flip through the massive amounts of envelopes that had been there. He didn’t get fan mail anymore. But between offers from the Indy feds that thought he’d make a few appearances and the amounting bills, he got enough.

"Hmm, Indy fed, Indy fed…. Nevada Power… Indy Fed…. New Registration for your car… Something from Vanilla Networks, whomever they are… Trash bill… Tribal Counsel Newsletter, still don’t know why they bother sending that to me. I’ll never friggin’ join…", he rattled them off, a look of distaste on his face for the last one.

"Might have something to do with that huge windfall you gave them a year ago. You are a holy woman’s son. And you turned around and bought them a new playground and rebuilt part of that ratty ass school. You still love the people here. Don’t give me that crap.", she said with a smile on her face.

He smirked, "Yeah, but not like I’d ever get into the political stuff. "

"Wait, what was that thick one again?", suddenly she looked like something had hit her.

"What? This one? Vanilla Networks? Probably some sort of Adult Cable Channel. Probably want me to come on one of their talk shows.", he said as he studied the envelope some.

Nothing but a company logo and address in the upper left hand corner, prepaid postage, but the entire thing was a bit over an inch thick.

"What does the logo look like?", she asked him.

In response he flung the envelope across the room to her. He then bent down and started to unbuckle the knee brace he still work some times. Especially when driving from the McCarran International Airport to home, or back again. Long drive with the leg in the same position.

He watched as her face got a huge smile on it. She suddenly started to open it.

"You’re not going to believe this." She added as she tore the envelope open.

"What? Is it some of the back money owed to me by…" He didn’t get a chance to finish his question.

"Dear Mr. McClancy, Allow me to introduce myself, my name is Sam Gordon, the General Manager of Professional World Wrestling and director of Vanilla Networks.

I’m a direct person, so before I explain things, allow me to fill you in on this. This is an invitation to come and join us at the offices of the PWW in Flagstaff, Arizona, to see if you would be the type of wrestler needed for Professional World Wrestling.

…. Blah…blah…. Just call… cell phone number…. Blah…blah… and tickets will be readied for you to fly here…. Blah… blah…. We’ll make our initial offers…. Blah… blah… saw you in SCWF… blah…blah… blah…

Signed Sam Gordon…. General Manager of Professional World Wrestling for Jaymes Phoenix, Owner and CEO of Vanilla Networks and the PWW." She trailed off on most of it. But her face was showing her dumbfounded state.

"What? Another damn indy fed wants me to join. I’m not going to do that. Especially something that sounds like some fly by night… wait? Jaymes Phoenix?"

He stopped. It had hit him like a hammer.

"Do you know what this is Blaise? This is an offer to join the PWW when it reopens." She had to fill him in. He didn’t know much about her time in the PWW. She had managed a tag team there known as Future Shock years ago.

"But the PWW closed. It was ruined by that guy, what was his name?"

"Sinclair. Joseph Maurice Sinclair. I don’t think he’s been around for some time. Other then his law suits against Ian that have been stopped. But this is the PWW. Looks like Sam Gordon has somehow got back into control of it. Well other then it says that she’s now an employee of Mr. Phoenix."

"Is that the same Jaymes Phoenix I’m thinking of, as in released from Ian’s fed after he had complications that led to the release of more then one person?"

Blaise was leaning forward in his seat. She could see he was intrigued.

"Well it looks like somehow the PWW is back, and it’s got new owners, and it’s making a run for it. Vanilla Networks canceled their contract with the UWF a while back now. And with it went Sam. So she’s back to helping the PWW out. I think this may be what you’ve been waiting for."

She had him, he was very interested.

But he was fully thinking what this could be. After a year out of the main stream of the industry, could it finally be time to come back?

"There’s only one problem." He filled in.

"And what would that be?" She replied.

 

"If this is heading to a TV deal with Vanilla Networks providing the production crew, I’m going to have to change the way I do thing."

"Why?"

"C’mon, if it wasn’t for that Lafayette based fed being a little rat hole when I got to it, I’d never have been able to do things the way I did there. One of these problems is the fact that my style does not agree with these U.S. based sports entertainment federations. There isn’t anyone out there in this country in the real sports entertainment that will take it like they have to. I’ll hear them all complaining again.

Hell, I remember when I had people coming out of the woodwork, and they all wanted to try to stop me from doing what I do best. Of course my usual answer was to go eat shit. But I don’t know."

His enthusiasm visibly died down a bit. He remembered all he had gone through to get the original contract he had. And then what he did to keep it. And then when it was all gone, what he had gone through to try to make the name he had.

But he was reluctant to jump through those hoops again.

Very reluctant…

"Look, what can hurt. They’re offering to fly you out, wine you and dine you and basically make you and offer. You keep talking about having to pay for your travel and that to go to these indy appearances. Besides, wouldn’t be nice to give it a try again? As for having to change, there are worse things they could ask you to do.

It can’t hurt. You can at least admit that.". She finally had him.

"You’re right. It can’t. Hell, it’s Jay Phoenix. I can already guess why I got the letter."

"Why?"

"Because I WON’T sign with Ian. Jay and I talked for a bit while he was in SCWF. We were going to team up for a bit, but then he left when they refused to pay him his full contract. Even though Ian wanted him to run that so bad, they refused to pay him as much as they were paying me.

I got the letter because for once we have someone running a federation that will do something. He also knows my wrestling style already…"

He was beginning to trail off. He’d forgotten to unbuckle the last strap on the knee brace. He stood up, and it gave a minor racket as metal buckles hit the two sidebars that kept everything the way it was supposed to be. Becca gave him a look that showed he’d wake the babies up. But at this time Blaise didn’t care. He walked into the kitchen, clanging away on the monster brace the whole time.

Opening the refrigerator he grabs a bottle of something. We’ll just say that it’s logo is a lizard. He opens it and downs a good half the bottle before making his way back into the living room.

"Will you tighten that thing down or take it off?" She was showing irritation now.

"Oh, sorry. I don’t know. I can’t look to be too anxious. For all I know, Jay would just be wanting to make me into a toy due to the fact that I was trained by the likes of Ian, Sean and Ace. He’s never gotten along with them. Even when on friendly terms." He was getting doubtful again.

"Jay and Sean have never had a real problem. And Jay’s only problem with Ian actually is the little Johnny Blaze situation. You had nothing to do with that. I think he knows just what you’re capable of and that’s what he’s after. Plus you’re a fresh face. You’re not some gothed up freak, you’re not some powerhouse or mask wearing idiot. You’re Blaise McClancy, a guy who once wrestled under the name of Hazard. You’re a man not afraid to do what it takes in the ring to make the crowd cheer, and get the job done.

Hell, your money you were earning in the that other fed, the cheap bastards weren’t paying you that much. Most of what you were making was from merchandising sales since we had secured a 30% for you.

The crowd likes you, and that’s what Jay probably wants with this.

Tell you what, take a few days, give them a call. But think about it. But if you ask me, it can’t hurt to go to a dinner with Ms. Gordon and maybe Jay and see what they have to offer. It’s that simple."

She had him on that. Finally, she had him. Opportunity had come knocking and it was time for him to finally take his options and run with them.

"Alright. Now, you want help moving them into the nursery?" It was time for a ‘peacemaker’ on the day.

"Yes, that would be very nice. I think they’ll sleep for some time."

Blaise reached down and scoops up one of the red-haired children. Becca gets up and grabs the other one. Both carry the little McClancy’s to the back and we fade out for a small time….

Only to fade back in again…

Late night, two lovers laying in their bed, one that folds up and can become a recliner. Two red-haired people. One the color of fire, the other the color of auburn.

It’s Becca and Blaise again. That night. Becca is laying there with the covers covering up to her waist. A small gossamer nightie top covers her torso. Blaze is shirtless as he lays slightly under the covers also. His legs covered by a pair of sweats.

Blaise has the remote control in his hands, switching channels.

And he comes by CBS… a regular local station for him, KLAS TV 8…. David Letterman….

From the TV comes the following:

David Letterman can be heard saying, "Well one night we talk about M&Ms and then we get M&Ms delivered to the studio, and tonight we just talked about wrestling and women in jello and guess what?" as he sits at his little desk.
Paul Shaffer responds, " Wow … you mean we got some babes and jello arriving?" "Not quite, but we do have our next guest ... the former World Champion wrestler, JAY PHOENIX!" Letterman retorts as the band starts to play.
"Day by day, watching you disappear Wishing that you were still here beside me On my own, swimming against the tide There's nobody on my side but your memory"

Becca looks on as on the TV, Jaymes Phoenix comes out onto the set and moves to shake hands with Mr. Letterman.

It catches her a bit off guard when she feels movement on the bed.

She turns just in time to see Blaise leaning over to the night stand and picking up the letter and his cell phone.

"I guess we’ll give them a test. I’m going to guess that she’ll have her voice mail on."

He dials the number and puts the phone to his ear.

And then he’s taken by a little surprise.

"Hello? Yes, is this Sam Gordon? I’m sorry if I woke you. Oh, you’re not asleep. You’re waiting for people to call about Jay’s appearance. Oh, you know how some wrestlers are huh. Okay.

Well, my name is….. Oh, you know my voice? Okay. I got your letter today also."

He then went silent for a bit.

Becca could see the corners of his mouth rising into a smile.

"Yes, I’ve been cleared medically for full competition. I’ve been working indy dates to keep the ring rust off. "

He trailed off again as the murmer of a voice from the woman on the other side could be heard.

"Well, two days from now? That would be perfect. Flagstaff. I could drive there if you need me too. It’s only a few hours from here.

You’d prefer me to fly? Okay. Bring the wife and kids? Uh…"

Becca knew that most considered her his wife. But he’d never asked her. By common law they were spouses in Nevada, but it was never made official. But maybe that would change soon.

"I’ll make sure we have everything packed" She said under her breath, just audible enough for him to hear.

He turned and smiled at her. "Yeah, we’ll be there. You can call this number to get me at anytime. Just let me know the flight details. Thank you Ms. Gordon."

He hung the phone up and set it down.

"I guess you heard that…?"

"Yes, I did. I’d be happy to join you for this. Who knows, maybe the old Kitsune will be some good luck for you?"

"Who knows, who knows", he says as he turns off the TV.

With that, he reaches over and turns out the simple lamp on the nightstand.

The End… or is this a new beginning?
