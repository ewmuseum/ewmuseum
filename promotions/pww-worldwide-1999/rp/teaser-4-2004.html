PWW T-Minus [4]

February 2004

What this is One of the federation’s own countdown teasers to the reopening: Marvellous Mike Wade’s signing party at Wade Manor, announcing his contract with the returning PWW.

¦ PWW : T-Minus ... [4] ¦

The camera shoots up and we are taken to what appears to be the back yard of a house. In the backyard there is a marquee and there is a stage set up. It looks as if a big party is taken place and a huge crowd is in attendance. There are members of the wrestling press up near the front of the stage ready for whatever is about to happen with their cameras and notepads.

Eventually a rather tall very thin man wearing a butlers outfit and a pair of shades along with a pair of sneakers makes his way out on the stage. He has a microphone in hand and begins to speak in a voice very like that of legendary announcer Michael Buffer, a voice that doesn’t suit his scrawny appearance at all. The man’s name is of course, Feeble Monty.

Monty: Ladies and Gentlemen of the wrestling press. Fans, friends and well wishers, you are all gathered here today at Wade Manor for a reason. That reason is to celebrate Mike Wades entry into the PWW. So without further adieu let me introduce to you, the magnificent, mind-blowing, Marvellous Mike Wade!!!

Mikes “fans and well wishers” begin to applaud as he makes his way up onto the stage accompanied by his ever present, 2 foot 5 inch leprechaun manager, Mickey Moore. Mike takes the mic from Monty and addresses his public.

Mike: Yes today is a very special day for me. Today is the day which I announce my official signing with the returning PWW. A legendary fed run by a legendary man, Jay Phoenix. Jay and I go back a long ways. Back to the days of Ground Zero Wrestling. Me and him had many the intense battle in GZW. Many times we clashed and many times we fought. Jay was a great opponent and I’m sure he’ll make a great boss. 

But enough of the small talk. I’ve been asked to give this speech to all you friends and well wishers, and this is also going out across PWW television. I’ve been asked to tell you all a little bit about myself, why I’m here in PWW and what I hope to achieve in PWW. 

Well first things first I suppose. I am Mike Wade, the most Marvellous competitor you will have ever seen. A 5 star wrestler and a 5 star man. I am unstoppable, the best all round wrestler you will have ever seen in your life at a slim, trim, 201 and ¾ pounds baby!! This man standing next to me is my heterosexual life partner Mickey Moore. Now Mickey may be 2 foot 5 and he may believe he is a living breathing leprechaun but don’t count him short. Like me he is handsome, he’s sexy and he’s Irish. Of course without the handsome and sexy parts they’re just for me. Mickey’s just Irish like me. He is a part of the humourous band of characters that follow me around called the Wade Parade, but don’t worry you’ll learn more about those guys in the weeks to come!! 

Now why and how I am in PWW. Well I’m sitting here in my 3 bedroomed humble abode known to the outside world as Wade Manor, watching TV one night and all of a sudden the phone rings. Who’s there? Why it’s my old “pal” Jay “Tatanka” Phoenix. What did he want you ask? Well of course he wanted the most electrifying man in LIFE!! He wanted Mike Wade for PWW and who could really blame him? If you were restarting a legendary fed like PWW who would you want on your team? Mike f’n Wade of course. 

Now why am I here? Well that’s very simple and you should all know it. I’m here to be the best. Not number two not number 3, number 1. I am here to be PWW. I am here to demand the respect of those around me and to beat you all down into bloody pathetic pulps. Soon you will all learn that I’m every bit as good as I say I am and I am every bit better then you are!! I have an open challenge right here right now for any PWW “superstar” to step up. It’s been used as a cliché too much over the last few years in wrestling “Who’s Next?” but I want to know WHO THE FUCK IS FIRST????!!!! So PWW look out because Mike Wade is in town, and he’s huntin for gold baby!! And remember if you wanna get laid, join the Parade!!
