PWW T-Minus [3]

February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is One of the federation’s own countdown teasers to the reopening: Nemesis in a London corridor, taking a friend with him to the new federation he is joining.

¦ PWW : T-Minus ... [3] ¦

London isn't paved with gold. 

London is a slum. 

London is full of crime, homeless people, and millions of bloody tourists. 

The homeless people come with high hopes, only to get ripped off by some cruel bed and breakfast while looking for a job, and then forced to retreat to doorways, to get ignored and mocked. 

The same goes for any major city. 

For example - Hollywood. People think they can go there and mingle with the stars. 

They've got more chance of jumping up and catching the sun.

Back to London. 

Back to the UK. 

The country where everyone outside of which, especially America, is led to believe it is full of posh snobs with crooked teeth. 

You couldn't be further away from the truth.

We open the scene in a corridor.

'F***ING GRASS!' a deep, macho voice shouts. 

A baseball bat connects with head.

Twice.

'I think he's dead Grant..' says a calmer tone. 

The bat is brought down again. 

Whoever decided that green and brown look good on walls, must have been colorblind, or out of good paint.

We are in a corridor, a run down corridor. 

A corridor with big chunks missing in the walls. 

A corridor that has a floor with white tiled, every few of which are cracked or gone all together. 

A corridor which has a roof that is basically a large pipe.

Standing in this green and brown walled corridor, are two men (three if you count the dead one) which are different in more ways than one. 

First off there's the color. 

The man who has just been beating a man to death with a baseball bat is indeed black, while the innocent watcher is white. 

The man armed with a baseball bat wears nothing but dark blue ripped jeans, with his muscular chest on show - while the other man is dressed in a 'dusty' tuxedo, and wears a top hat. 

The man with the bat has more piercing on his face than he has teeth, while the other man doesn't have a single one ... piercing that is, he has plenty of teeth.

All in all, there is many differences.

'I think it is safe to say,' says the watcher 'that you won the skirmish. Sure, you had a baseball bat, and yes, you did deliver the first blow to the back of his head, but nevertheless, you are indeed the victor.'

The black man shoves his bat underneath his belt, and then grabs one of his victim's arms. 

After a few seconds of pause, the white man does the same.

'What did he do to deserve that washout then?' says the white man, half smiling as he lifts the arm.

They both start to drag the man along the corridor by his arms.

'That mother****er went an' got the f***in' pigs on me club' the deep voiced pierced man replies, frowning as he does so.

The other man listens, while looking back at the trail of blood being left behind them.

'I'm sure he's guilt ridden. I'm also sure that he now feels a lot of remorse to what he did. What did this guy have on you Grant?'

The man we know as Grant turns to the man who asked.

'All we did is stick to the f***in' code. He put a bet on a fight, he lost the bet, he never payed up ... we vowed to trash him in one way or another, and then the f***er goes and gets the ol' bill on us! So the joint is ruined, I have a handful of money seekin' badasses after me, and no income. If you got here an hour later, I'd be on f***in' concord by now.'

After a moment of abrupt silence, the other man replies.

'Concord's been taken out of service Grant.' says the White Man. Grant sighs, rolling his big heavy eyes up towards his temples.

'It was a term of speech Nemmy.' was the quick reply.

'Nemesis..' exclaims Nemesis, taking a breath in the process as he slowly speaks his alias with glory.

Grant cups his hand and raises it up slightly, before shrugging.

'Whatever. It beats the other names.. and it suits you to. Really good name for a sick crowbar obsessed freak. I'm surprised you never killed anyone with your antics.'

A small smile surfaces on the shadowed face of Nemesis. He replies, slowly.

'I thought you didn't watch wrestling? You always told me it was for puffs ... so doesn't that make you one?' says Nemesis, anxious for a reply.

Luckily for him, the baseball bat armed man by his side took the remark for what it was. 

A joke.

'I heard a measly tale in me local ... it's surprising how many sick freaks you have rooting for you to rip that metal into someone's eye, it's put a whole new front on the sport. I'm surprised you're not in some jail cell by now.' Grant replies.

'You know what..,' says Nemesis, looking down at the body too 'the feeling is neutral. Unfortunately, I can only imagine your future being six feet under from what you've told me recently. I suggest you get out of here while you're still breathing.'

Grant laughs, despite not being amused.

'Yeah! Where to? Don't worry about me you softy, all I need to do is lie low until the heats off me.' the sentence got quieter as the end approached.

Nemesis smiles 'I don't have many friends in the states ... sure, I have a few, but nobody really understands me. Besides, I don't know anyone in the new federation I'm joining.. '

Grant slowly closes his mouth, moving his lips upwards as he thinks about the proposal that Nemesis may have in mind.

'You should come with me ... you'd make a good sidekick,' a few moments pass. ' ...joke...'

Grant drops the arm of the carcass he has dragged so far.

'Hmm.. I heard you were joinin' PWW for a change of scenery, but I never believed it because I believed you were going to retire soon - Ok.. fine.. I will come then. Only because you want me to though, I mean.. I can stick up for ma' self here you know!'

An evil, cocky smile emerges on Nemesis's face.

'We'll make an excellent team.'
