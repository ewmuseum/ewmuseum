What this is One of the federation’s own countdown teasers to the reopening: how Exceptional Dave Hurst and a plastic duck got to the PWW.

¦ PWW : T-Minus ... [6] ¦

I suppose you want to know how Hurst got to PWW… What? Who is this ‘Hurst’, you ask? He’s the greatest technical wrestler in the Universe you goon! Now listen up and you might learn something… well probably not, but at least you might get an insight into who this mug is and why the hell PWW hired him.

It was the height of winter. The soft but chilling snow lied on the ground as a carpet. The wind was howling and swirling like there’s no tomorrow, the snow was still in the air and was swept in the wind and flying in all directions at a tremendous speed… you get the picture. It was freaking cold, alright?!

Ahem. Yeah anyway, with everyone already home and tucked away in the slender warmth of their own bed, the sound of the howling reduced by the double glazing to a relaxing murmur. Who’s stupid enough to still be outside on a night like that?

A mysterious man, who’s not really mysterious, but I thought I’d mention it so you’d feel he’s more important than just a bloke passing in the street. This man is the kind of person that would be out on a night like that, because he was. You see it’s quite obvious if you think about it, isn’t it?

Yes, yes it is. This mysterious man is no longer a mystery to you and me as I can reveal to you that it was Ex-Old School Wrestling star Exceptional Dave Hurst.

Things weren’t faring to well for this man, as you can tell by the very sad atmosphere that I’ve provided you with. The problem he faced with when signing to Old School Wrestling was that a lot of the amenities that he took for granted such as the apartment, the car, the credit card, all of those were provided by the company. He couldn’t access his bank account and his savings that day. It was only that day that he was told that he had to leave the apartment and was slung out of the house. Somebody wasn’t reading their mail!

With Hurst out on the streets, with only the loose change that he had in his wallet to suffice, he had to do something to get himself out of the cold. What better way to do that than to walk across the snowy street and look for the late bus to somewhere very hot.

Waiting by the bus stop, some bastard had wrecked the place and there was now an illegible timetable there. Hurst just sat there on the seat with his back pressing against the bus shelter and waited for the next bus that came along.

Time went by and no sign of a bus. It looked bleak for Hurst and increasingly like he’d missed the last bus. He would have to sleep outside tonight and not even in the warm comfort of a run down, badly heated bus.

But then something hit him. No not an idea, something literally hit him! On the head! In the swirling wind a bright yellow object had been swept up, blown around and had smacked The Exceptional One right in the head!

“OW! What the fuck was that?!” Hurst cried out, rubbing the sore area of his head.

He looked around. On the floor, looking up at him was a small plastic yellow duck.

“Where the bloody hell did you come from?!” Hurst yelled at the inanimate object. The recent blow to head mixed with the extreme weather had obviously sent him insane.

The small duck, unsurprisingly, only looked up at EDH expressionlessly. Hurst saw it as an insult for some unknown reason. He got up from where he was sitting, dragged a leg back and sent it swinging forward giving the duck a clear kick into the middle of the road.

As he did so, in the distance, the bus that he had been waiting so long to catch suddenly appeared in the distance. As the bus came closer to him he read the word on the top of the bus.

Arizona.

“Wow! That place is a fucking desert, it MUST be hot there!” Hurst spoke to himself. He signaled to the bus driver that he’s ready to get on and the bus stopped. As he paid to get on the bus, he glanced across the bus. Low and behold, sitting there on one of the bus seats towards the back of the bus was a small yellow object. On closer observation, it was the plastic duck from earlier…

This really puzzled Hurst and he looked across the bus driver and through his window to see if he could see the duck he kicked clean across the road, but there was no trace… Did the duck move…?

This was very bizarre and quite spooky too. Hurst paid his fare and walked across the bus, sitting down on the seat beside the duck.

“Now… how the bloody hell did you get on this bus, Duck?” Hurst asked the duck. The duck might be made of plastic, it may not move, but Hurst has a funny feeling that the duck is hearing everything that he says.

“Maybe you’re some kind of good luck charm or something.” Hurst continued, “I tell you what, I’m going to keep you. I’ll call you ducky…”

And with that Hurst went on his journey with the plastic duck by his side. Maybe this duck is a lucky charm. The duck appeared and then the bus showed up after all. Surely that wasn’t just coincidence.

The duck may have an innocent exterior. It may be bringing Hurst good luck, but nobody knows of the deep dark secret that comes with this duck. Nobody knows where this duck came from. Well… I do, but you get what I mean. I could tell you about the history of this insignificant duck, but we’ll save that for another time.

In the meantime, I can tell you what happened when the bus arrived at Arizona.

Well, the bus arrived, Hurst got off. By that I don’t mean he got a bit frisky, he stepped off the bus. Unfortunately for him though, it was dark again. The distance he had traveled left him back at night again. At least it wasn’t too cold this time and wasn’t snowing. Hurst and his newly found duck decided to find a decent spot in the sand to lay down and go to sleep. It would be a poor decision on his part.

What you readers are unaware of, is the extent to which Exceptional Dave Hurst rolls in his sleep. It’s a sight to behold, it really is. Hurst had rolled, in one direction, quite regularly all night. It got to morning, he got up, brushed himself off. And found that he was in the middle of nowhere.

Well, it wasn’t really ‘nowhere’. It was the middle of the Arizona desert, but you get what I mean. As the puzzled expression reached Hurst’s face as he began to awaken and stretched his arms out, you can’t help but think… what a fucking idiot!

He has managed to get himself lost. Luckily for him, the plastic yellow duck he had befriended just happened to have been swept with the breeze and rolled alongside him as he slept. Well, saying it was luck is not really doing it justice. The miles that Hurst managed to roll and it so happened that exactly the same direction and distance the duck was blown. That really is quite an achievement.

Hurst walked over to where the duck lied buried under a blanket of sand and picked it up, brushing off any excess grains.

“Blimey Ducky…! How the hell did we get here?!” He said to the duck, a stupid question to give to a duck that doesn’t speak, but there you go. I guess it was one of those rhetorical things that idiot pet owners do all the time.

Anyway, Hurst and Ducky begin their travels to try and find where the hell they came from. They picked a random direction, and walked. The sun beat down on them like a very hot thing. Hurst’s feet smelt like fried bacon. For two reasons: 1) It’s very hot and his feet were burning. 2) He hadn’t washed them for a couple of days!

The life had been sucked out of Hurst’s body. He couldn’t carry Ducky any longer. He stopped, dropped and rolled, because he was on fire. But just after that he lied down on the sand, motionless, unable to move a muscle. He’d only been moving for 10 minutes, what a wimp!

“I can’t go on Ducky!” Hurst exclaimed, “I have no more energy… please… go on without me! Don’t worry about me!!”

Then it hit him… yes the duck had been caught in the breeze once again. But Hurst looked up from where the duck had hit him and noticed an object in the background…

It was a cactus… there are plenty of them in the desert ya know!

BUT! Just next to the cactus was another object, one that he couldn’t quite put a finger on. You know, because of the distances involved. It could be another cactus, but for some reason cacti ‘branches’ don’t seem to wave about slowly with the wind that much. But this one was…

Hurst clawed his way across the desert to find out what this object was, could it be a mirage… maybe a strange Indian fellow that is doing some strange form of meditation waving his arms about and stuff like that. I’ll give you 3/1 odds on the mirage, any takers??

Well, it was the Indian. And damn you all for not taking up the bet! This Indian was not any ordinary Indian, but Professional World Wrestling owner Jay Phoenix. He was doing his early morning meditation and glanced to one side noticing Hurst desperately clawing his way across the desert.

He placed his hand in between a few of the cactus’s needles and ripped at it, tearing some of the cactus off and turning it over revealing a very damp interior. He passed it to Hurst, who wasn’t ready to take it and the needles stabbed him in the hand.

“AHHH…! YOU BASTARD!” He shouted, to which Jay replied, “You were supposed to mind the needles you idiot!!”

“You never told me that!!” Hurst said, and Jay could only show him the obvious, “Well, it is a cactus,” he said. Hurst just grumbled before removing the needles from his hand and taking a sip of the cactus juice.

From there Hurst got talking to the wise Indian dude. Jay found out that Hurst was a ex-prestigious wrestler, and Hurst learned that Jay was an Indian dude who was also in charge of a wrestling federation and was hiring.

If you people at home now wants to put 2 and 2 together…

No! They didn’t have a ‘relationship’, you pervs! Hurst was hired and they all lived happily ever after…NOT WITH EACH OTHER!

This isn’t going so well… anyway, Hurst was hired, got a place to stay which wasn’t Jay Phoenix’s bed, because Hurst is not a ‘bloody poof’ and ducky was brought along for the ride.

And that’s how Hurst got to PWW…
