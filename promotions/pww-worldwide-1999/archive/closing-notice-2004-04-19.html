PWW — the reopening abandoned

19 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The federation’s own front page after the reopening failed: the owner’s signed notice that the PWW would stay closed, and what the federation had meant to him. The people he thanks are named by first name only.
Monday 19th April 2004

Well it seems that the old adage of 'best laid plans' and all that is remarkably apt indeed.

In 1997 the PWW originally opened, and not too long after that I joined myself and got to know ... via this wonderful medium called the internet ... some very good people.

Pat, Gren, Chris, RJ & Kimi, Sonny, Aaron to name a few.

... and then in 2001/2 it closed.

Thanks to the PWW though I was introduced to the World of efedding, and caught the 'bug' that has never really left me since, allowing me to combine a love of wrestling, with a love of writing, with a love of creating ... and thanks to the PWW I got to know literally hundreds of other talented people, some of whom I can now have the pleasure of calling friends.

You all know who you are, so don't expect a list too! :o)

Anyway, recently I came up with the idea of re-opening the PWW and bringing it back to the World so that others could have the same level of enjoyment that I had all those years ago.

... now we get back to the best laid plans part ...

The Gods of Efedding thought otherwise though, because since announcing the reopening and gathering a VERY talented group of people together I have had physical problems (I blame the stairs!), PC problems (on my second laptop), and host problems (losing a site once I will forgive, twice though is just wrong!), and now on top of that I have the dreaded, fatal, no escaping problem.

Real Life~!

Yes, it seems that it has caught up with me finally, and while I 'almost' have the time do continue with efedding, I don't have the time to do it all justice.

Most especially not the PWW itself.

If it cannot be done 110% then it shouldn't be done at all in my book.

So, while it was a wonderful attempt to bring it back, and while I am stunned and grateful for the amount of support and encouragement I received throughout from the handlers who had come on board, from the VP Phil, from Pat and RJ, and from everyone else, it is finally time to realise that this is not going to happen.

... yet ...

GRIN ... yeah I will never give up totally, but I am facing reality here and the PWW for the moment is going to stay closed, though perhaps not as closed as it was this time last year ... there is a ghost of a chance that it may appear some time, some day.

Just not today.

Jay
