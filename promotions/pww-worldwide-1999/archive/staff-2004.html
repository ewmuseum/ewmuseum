PWW Staff, 2004

February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The federation’s own staff page at the reopening — its on-screen front office and commentary team, with each figure’s own history in the federation, and beneath them the two people running it out of character. One real name and four contact details are removed under the privacy rule; the memorial name standing in the same block is not.

Staff (on-screen / in-character)

Jay Phoenix - Owner/CEO
Owner and CEO of both the PWW and Vanilla Networks, the media company that produces the PWW shows for television.
A former PWW superstar, Phoenix is the only man to ever hold ALL the PWW titles (World, Intercontinental, European, CWTV, Tag) as well as winning both the Master of the Mat annual tournament and the Chris Williams Memorial Cup.
Well known in the wrestling business as a hardworking man of ethics, Phoenix has decided to 'resurrect' the federation that he started with, the place that he has always called home.
Can he achieve the same level of success running the PWW as he did as a member of the roster?
Only time will tell.

Sam Gordon - General Manager
The last president of the original PWW and former managing-director of Vanilla Networks, Samantha Gordon was both the best president of the PWW ... bringing it to unparalleled glories, and the worst ... abusing her power and hand picking her favorites to hold the titles in her guise of Madame Syn.
Now chosen by Jay Phoenix to help with the day to day running of his federation, and the production of the PWW's flagship television show ... Monday Wildfire, Sam Gordon has promised that she has turned over a new leaf and only has the best wishes of the PWW at heart.

Michael Burke - Play by play A veteran of the PWW, Michael Burke was the first voice ever heard on a PWW show in 1997, and was part of the team right up until the last Friday Fury on New Year's Eve 2001 as 2002 dawned and the federation closed it's doors.  Now heading up the media department as well as fulfilling his old role of on air play by play man at all television events, Michael is happy to be 'home'.

Thomas Johnson - Color Commentary
As night is to day, so is Thomas Johnson ... TJ to those that know and loathe him ... to Michael Burke.
Where Michael is professionalism and objectivity personified, TJ is fun, games and whatever crosses his mind at that time
However, part of the success of the PWW was always the dynamics of the pairing on commentary, and Jay Phoenix has realized that in order to capture and build upon the legacy of the past he needs both men at the announce table ... and God help everyone!

Kerrie Douglas - Ring announcer
The voice of the PWW, and as a former Ms USA, often the face of it to, adorning many posters and issues of PWW magazines.
Kerrie Douglas' main role is that of the PWW ring announcer, but as a long term member of the staff he knowledge of wrestling is extensive and she is invaluable in compiling the PWW website and archives, also working on the DVD and promotional packages.
