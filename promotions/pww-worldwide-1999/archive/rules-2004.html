PWW Rules, 2004

February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The federation’s own rulebook at the reopening: its four title divisions, its weekly show and pay-per-view calendar, its roleplay limits and deadlines, and the annual tournament and memorial cup it staged. Two mail addresses are removed under the privacy rule.

The following rules must be obeyed as closely as possible, a first violation of the rules and you will receive a written warning, any other violations and your contract will be terminated, The President also has the right to terminate a contract at his own discretion, you will be informed if your contract is terminated, and you will be removed from the roster and mailing list straight away.

I know that it may seem that there are a lot of rules and that it may make you think that this place is too strict, but it is simply to try to head of arguments before they begin, and so far that has worked.

The last rule is the most important after all!

1. Application Forms

The Application form must be filled in as much as possible, application forms with what seems not enough information will be deemed void, the sample roleplay section MUST be filled in and be no less than 20 lines, the president has the right to the change the application form whenever necessary. The PWW has the right to reject an application, also there is a limit of 30 active wrestlers in the federation at one time, if we are full we will keep your application on file and you will be put into a waiting list, should an opening arrive in the PWW you will be informed. The Application Form should be emailed to [detail withheld] if the online form is not working.

2. Contact list

The contact list is kept online purely for the benefit of the members of the PWW in order for them to contact each other.  It is NOT to be used for unsolicited emails (SPAM), recruitment, advertising or any other non-PWW related material.
Any breaches of this should be passed on to me right away, and this will be passed onto the relevant ISP for them to deal with accordingly.

3. Number Of Wrestlers:

There is a limit of 30 active wrestlers at one time in the PWW, therefore you are only allowed to have one active single wrestler, and one tag-team, per email address, anyone found using more than one email address in order to control more than this limit will be kicked out of the federation. 

4. Title Divisions

4.1. Nightmare Division (hardcore / extreme)

The 'Nightmare Title' division is specifically for the sort of matches that have become known as hardcore or extreme ... table matches, falls count anywhere, no DQ/countout matches, etc.

4.2. Shooting Star Division (television)

This is the 'starting' division, open to all wrestlers, and could be considered to be similar to a 'Television' title in other federations.  It may be the first title on the road to the World title, but that doesn't make it any less important.

4.3 Gemini Division (tag)

There is a tag division in the PWW and there are two ways to compete in this.  1/ You can join up with another existing singles wrestler in the fed and work together in the tag division, or 2/ you can control a tag team yourself.  If you decide to control a tag team yourself, please fill in the application form for the tag team or mail it to [detail withheld].   

4.4. Eternal division (world)

This is the main title of the federation, the pinnacle of a wrestler's achievement.  Anyone reaching this height can legitimately claim to be the best in the PWW ... at that time at least. 

4.5. Other titles / divisions

At this time there are no plans to bring in any other titles or divisions (ie Woman's, Cruiserweight, etc) but if roster size and demand dictate otherwise, these will be brought into the PWW.
Once a year, there will also be a single elimination style tournament, open to the whole roster, the winner of which will be able to claim the title of 'Master of the Mat'.  This is not an official belt, but is rather an accolade, and will not be defended.

5. Cards

The PWW currently runs one weekly televised wrestling show called WildFire, which normally consists of five matches and one main event and airs on a Monday.
PWW also hosts a Pay Per View event every eight-twelve weeks.
Scheduled PPVs are - WrestleFest (main PVV), Winter Warzone, Master of the Mat (elimination tournament), Release the Pressure.
The PWW also hosts the annual Chris William's Memorial Cup.
The reason for this timing is so as to not rush angles and story lines and have the develop at an acceptable pace and it also makes the PWW more exciting as well as a higher quality.
A second weekly show, Sabotage may be brought in ... focusing more on the newer wrestlers, interviews and storyline progression ... sometime in the future.

7. Wrestling Rules

The same ring rules apply in the PWW as those that are used in most major organisations (i.e. World Wrestling Entertainment.)

One fall, count outs, DQ's for what takes place inside and outside the ring.

Matches also have specific time limits, which are as follows:

Low card match: 10-15 mins.

Mid card match: 20-30 mins.

Main event and all title matches: 60 mins.

(Except Shooting Star title matches which have a time limit of 15-30 mins)

Championship belts can only change hands on pitfall or submission, you cannot win the belt via DQ or count out (unless a contract stipulation is in effect)

Special Matches and Hardcore matches may take place and rules for those matches will be described when the match is announced.

PWW refs can range from strict to lenient, meaning some refs may allow weapons into the ring while others will call for an immediate DQ.

8. Champions

The championship belts are as follows and must be defended in the designated time or the champion will be stripped of the title, Wrestlers are only allowed to have one belt (apart from the tag titles), if you should end up with two belts you will be forced to drop the lesser belt (unless for the purposes of an angle or unification of titles), wrestlers will usually (but not always) defend the belts on every PPV and on the occasional weekly show, a title may be defended on a house show at the discretion of the president with the agreement of the current champion:

PWW Eternal Champion - Once a month

PWW  Shooting Star Champion - Every week

PWW Nightmare Champion - Anytime, anyplace.

PWW Gemini Champions - Every two weeks

These are the MINIMUM times for title defenses, but they can be defended more often.

9. Stables

Stables may be formed in this federation and there is NO limit has to how many people you have in your stable, however this could change during the course of the federation. Please keep the president informed of all stable plans.

10. Roleplay

This is what makes or breaks your PWW wrestling career, the more consistently well that you roleplay the more chance you will have of success. While size is not the most important factor, roleplays should aim to be at least 30 lines ... or 500 words... long as a minimum.

You are expected to roleplay at least ONCE every two weeks ... and at least ONCE for every booked match ... to keep your PWW contract active, if you fail to roleplay your contract will be terminated, if you are going on vacation, doing exams and would rather not roleplay for that period of time you will be granted leave from the PWW and your contract will not be terminated. IF YOU FAIL TO DO THIS YOUR CONTRACT WILL BE TERMINATED IN ORDER TO ALLOW PEOPLE ON THE WAITING LIST TO JOIN.

As we are looking for QUALITY and not quantity, your roleplay could be 1000 lines long but if it is repetitive and mind boggingly boring you are more than likely going to lose to someone who does 30 line roleplays that get straight to the point, interesting and relevant. You may only roleplay for yourself and no one else. If you are a tag team or in a stable you can include the other wrestler(s) in your roleplays however they can only make small comments and nothing special or important.

Please remember that there is no age limit for acceptance into the PWW, and while there is no rule against mature content or language, try to keep in mind the feelings of others, and DO NOT make personal attacks against handlers of the wrestlers.

10.1. Roleplay limits

These are the limits for RPs for each sort of card/match:

Wildfire matches

Regular matches - there is a three (3) RP limit.
Main event matches - there is a five (5) RP limit.
Title matches - there is a five (5) RP limit.  

PPV matches

Regular matches - there is a five (5) RP limit. 
Title matches - there is a seven (7) RP limit. 
Main event- there is a seven (7) RP limit.

No more than 1 (one) roleplay can be posted on any single calendar day.

10.2  In-Ring / on-card interviews

As well as doing a roleplay for the week you could try and get more attention for your wrestler by doing an in-ring or on-card interview. Working in the same manner as a roleplay but instead it would be seen on one of the PWW's Premier Wrestling shows. Say what you want and get what you need to get off your chest with the members paying their full attention and also with the world watching.    

All in-ring / on-card interviews MUST be fully written out in the same style and format of the car in which it will appear.

11. Sneak Attacks

For the good of the federation and to have as much member input as possible you may use sneak attacks on a card, sneak attacks are basically if you have a grudge against someone and want to get revenge or cost them a match, you can come down the ring, bash them over with a head and cost them a match, beat them up in the locker room, the possibilities are endless, however sneak attacks must be for a reason and I need a paragraph to explain your motives, you may only use one sneak attack per card and it is possible for sneak attacks to go against you if you are using them to much.  Remember that sneak attacks do not take the place of good roleplaying.

All sneak attacks MUST be fully written out in the same style and format of the car in which it will appear.

12. Upsets (via Sneak Attacks)

For the good of the federation, or for the good of angles, there is a distinct possibility that an "upset" could happen to you, for example even if you roleplay better than your opponent you may still lose the match, but ONLY if someone has used good sneak attacks against you, it happens in real wrestling, someone gets hit over the head with a chair and the other person gets a win. You may be required to do "The Job" but it has been known that these people are usually highly rewarded in the near future. If someone has used sneak attacks against you but you absolutely whipped your opponent in roleplaying you are more than likely going to recover and still win the match.

Please remember that PWW is a fully BOOKED fed, in that the president/staff decides who wins a match, and this is not solely limited to RPing alone.

13. Deadlines

Deadlines are very important and must be met for your roleplays and sneak attacks to count towards a match. Take into account Monday Night WildFire is on a Monday (obviously) so sending in a sneak attack Monday morning is not a good idea, as the majority of the card will be written and it may be to late to the change the card. Roleplays must also be sent before the relevant deadline in order  to count towards your match should you have one, missing the deadline by a few minutes can be disastrous to your chances of victory so make sure you get it all done on time. Also remember the president of the fed does this out the kindness of his heart, so there may be times when he also is unable to post a card on the correct date and meet his own deadlines, should this happen you will be made aware of it through email and possibly be given more time to send roleplays and sneak attacks. 

Delays happen, we are all human.  PWW will try to keep to card deadlines, but please be patient if there is a problem!

14. Forum

The PWW forum is for the use of PWW Members and fans to interact.
There are many different categories to choose from, so please try to keep to the right one and keep things on topic and relevant.
Attacks against other posters, or posts of a discriminatory or overtly offensive nature are not allowed ... neither is deliberate flaming!

15. Chat Room

The PWW Chat Room is for the use of PWW Members and fans to interact.
Please try to keep things on topic and relevant.
Attacks against other posters, or posts of a discriminatory or overtly offensive nature are not allowed ... neither is deliberate flaming!

16. No Asking

While suggesting possible matches, or even asking for specific matches to promote your angle or feud is very welcome, asking to win matches (or moaning about the result of a match) will result in you being jobbed in your next match, no matter the standard of roleplay or ongoing angle. The best way to get a match against someone specific is to challenge them in a roleplay  or on-card interview (or even after a sneak attack).

17. Angles

These are the things that are going to make or break your character.  It is one thing to have a good character, and be able to write interesting roleplays, but without a REASON in your wrestler’s life, career or feud it is all pretty pointless.  The president can make angles for you, and book you into feuds, but interaction is always preferred, so if you have an idea for your wrestler, let the president know it and it can be worked into the ongoing plot of the PWW.  

These angles ... or ongoing storyline if you prefer that term ... are the backbone of the PWW, and are what separate it from other feds.

PWW is not a place to show that you are a better writer ... it is not the place to show that you can sneak attack or trash talk week after week ... PWW is a place where you combine everything that is good in e-fedding into one package!

We don't claim to be an angle fed ... we don't claim to be a roleplay fed ... we are just the PWW, the best of both worlds.

18. Have fun

Probably the most important aspect, even after reading all these rules and regulations, the main thing is to enjoy yourself and have fun!
