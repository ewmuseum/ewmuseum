A federation that ran from July 1997 to New Year's Eve 2001 on four titles, a weekly Wildfire and a Master of the Mat tournament, named its television championship for an executive killed in 1999, and signed a full roster for a reopening in 2004 that never staged a show. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

PWW's earliest surviving broadcast, Wednesday Night Wildfire, posted 21 January 1999, already presents an established federation running out of the Manchester Evening News Arena under President Alan Miller and Commissioner Robert DeSoto. It opens on the aftermath of an attack: a wrestler named Congo is reported to have taken out three opponents and several arena staff the week before, among them the promotion's own World Champion, Johnny Blaze.

Blaze, shown recovering in a hospital bed, vowed on camera to defend his title against Congo anyway at the promotion's own upcoming Warzone event in Norway, framing the rematch as revenge for two separate hospital stays Congo had already put him through.

The same broadcast staged a recruitment match for the promotion's own stable, AMW, led by Northern Touch: a newcomer, El Magnifico Hernandez, submitted a wrestler called Army of One to earn membership. A second angle showed President Miller manipulating a preview match between the Assassin and Ian “Obscurity” Squire, ordering the special guest referee to fast-count Squire to the win, then huddling backstage with Squire and Jefferey “BrYd” Johns over an unspecified plan the broadcast never explains.

The promotion's own record continues into November 1999, when its pay-per-view built toward a tournament it called the Master of the Mat, staged, by its own account, as the second annual, from the Sky Dome in Toronto. No result from that night, and no closing statement after it, survives.

Chris Williams Memorial Cup

Crossover booking with PWW (1997)
PWW's own rulebook names the Cup an annual fixture on its own calendar, and the Cup's own roll of past champions opens there, undated: Jay Phoenix, its first-ever champion, of what the roll calls the Chris Williams Television Title, followed in order by three further champions the record leaves undated.
