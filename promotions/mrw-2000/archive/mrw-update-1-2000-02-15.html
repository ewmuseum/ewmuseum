Hey Yo. Just thought I'd post the first MRW Update. As you might or might not know, MRW is one of the regions under the new Alliance of Champions. Our roster as it stands right now is 12 members strong. There are still 8 spots left to be claimed. Take a look at the Update, and let me know what you think of it. If it sounds interesting, e-mail me and I'll send you the MRW FAQ and Application. Thanks.

In this update, we'll have the roster as it looks so far, the preview for the first week's card, and how this whole 'Alliance of Champs' is going to work from here on in. There is a little information you might want to know, so I advise you take a look!
++ THE ALLIANCE SETS DATE FOR FIRST 'ACTV' ++ Yes, you're not seeing a mirage, the Alliance Board of Directors (ABOD) has discussed it, and have scheduled the first Alliance Championship
Television (ACTV) for February 21st, 2000! The first card will feature matches from the MRW, the DWF, and the ACeWF, along with the inaugural
Alliance World Champion being crowned. The MRW will not be represented in this match, something that has angered both CEO Setzer Van Strife and
COO Jason Wnuk. Van Strife has _guaranteed_ that the MRW will be represented, whether the AoC likes it or not. What he means, we'll have to wait and find out!
++ MRW EXECUTIVES TALK ABOUT POSSIBLE NEW TITLE ++ After about a week of deliberations, the MRW Executive Board has decided that another title might be in order. The demand for MRW is _very_ high right now, with many of wrestling's top names desiring to see what Jason
Wnuk is capable of. And, seeing as many of the heavyweights have shown a great displeasure in only one title for them, when there are more of them than juniorweights. The MRW Executive Board is looking into it right now, and an announcement on the new title will be made at this week's ACTV, from none other than COO Jason Wnuk.
++ MAJOR SIGNING RUMORS SURFACE ++ The buzz around the executive board is that the MRW is in talks with some MAJOR players in the wrestling world. There is talk that a rather
'devious' wrestler has already signed his contract. Also rumored to be talking to CEO Setzer Van Strife is one that could be 'simply the best'
in this sport. Who signs their name on the dotted lines? I guess we'll have to wait and see! But for now, here is the roster of the twelve men that will participate in the MRW's inaugural card!

Illinois. We'll be coming to you LIVE from the United Center, and we're hoping to fill the lower section! This week will be a gear up towards the big show the next week, where the MRW Midwest Champion, MRW
Juniorweight Champion, and MRW Midwest Tag Team Champions will be crowned! The CEO, Setzer Van Strife and the COO Jason Wnuk will be there, and they're going to have a big announcement about the titles and how they will be awarded at the next show!
This week will be a warm-up for next week's _huge_ show. This week, 4 matches will take place, as CEO Setzer Van Strife will randomly pick three names at a time. The three chosen will then face each other in a triple threat match, under ELIMINATION RULES. There will be _one_ winner, and he will have to endure two others to become the true winner! In the first bout, MATTY DEE, KIDD KHAOS, and 'TOO COOL' CHRIS
HOPPER will lock horns. Will Dee prove that he is the "Superman" of the
MRW? Will the newcomer Khaos show that he's too good for the other two?
Or will MRW Midwest Title favorite Hopper show everyone up by living up to his nickname and calmly taking down both Dee and Khaos?
In the second bout, 'DISGUSTING' RICHARD DORKUS, 'TRENDY' BRYCE JORDAN, and DARKY DEVIL will take it to the ring and see who comes out the victor. Will the massive Dorkus show that strength and power are the keys to winning? Or will Jordan thwart Dorkus and Devil's plans with some amazing aerial ability? Could Darky Devil be the surprise of all three of these men, pulling off a "Cinderella" win over the other duo?
In the third bout, ALPHA WILDE, 'SIMPLY GOLDEN' JEFF KEENAN, and TOMMY
'LORD' LIONHEART will go at it in what should be an AMAZING aerial bout.
Wilde seems to be the newcomer to this group, and has a lot to prove against the other two high flyers. Can he show that he has what it takes to pull of a victory in the MRW? Will the newly-returned Keenan shake off the remaining ring rust and take out the other two? Or will
Lionheart, star of other federations, show what a true seasoned ring veteran is all about? This is one to watch.
And, in our main event, 'SICK & TWISTED' ZACK SHARP, 'PURE ATHLETE' TONY
VIRTUE, and DEREK DOUGLAS will take it to the limits, and show what being in the main event is all about. Will Sharp, the maniacal menace, teach the other two a lesson in what "extreme" is all about? Will Virtue come in and show what being a pure athlete is about? Or will Derek
Douglas take his large EGO into the ring and squash the other two? This show should be nothing short of amazing.
