RWF (Juno, 2001): Big Trouble at the Iron Grill

21 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Some background: Iron Chef started with the Flying Octopus Balls Clan, whose gimmick was to take off bad Asian martial arts films. After that group split up, he joined the Penthouse, RWF's corporate stable, who stay in the Penthouse Suite of the Juno Hyatt. His new superstar lifestyle got the better of him, and even after two trips to rehab and a spiritual retreat back to Japan, he decided the only solution was to go back to his first calling--gourmet chef. This RP takes place in his restaurant, about three years after leaving RWF. Make it a habit to post updates on old characters every few years.
Iron Chef's roster page: http://sayn02g0d.hypermart.net/mitsukoshi.html
"Big Trouble at the Iron Grill" Posted by Iron Chef on May 21, 2001, 2:25 pm Saturday night at The Iron Grill in downtown Juno, Montana. The sporting press has gathered to cover King of the Forest IX, descending like drunken locusts as they do each year.
The main dining floor holds nearly 100 people, seated at and around the Benihana-style teppanyaki grill tables. The side wall is comprised of a giant sushi bar, which the Iron Chef Mitsukoshi Teppanyaki himself mans, the kitchen behind him. A window next to the sushi bar opens into the bar, so waitresses can walk up and get their drink orders.
The Iron Chef serves up various sushi dishes, placing them on the conveyor belt as fast as it passes by him, a big goofy smile on his face. His former manager, Fuikui-san winds around the dining room, refilling wine glasses in his new job as head waiter.
--- At a table near the back, two cocky reporters are getting particularly drunk. As Fuikui-san approaches, the shorter one says to the other, in a mock Vietnamese voice, "Hey, babykiller, you want refill?"
"Seriously," the other one says, "I'm not a babykiller. You paying for this round?" "Sure, sure," the short one says, "Me love you long time." The other reporter stands up, shaking his head. "I gotta go piss," he says, "be right back." --- Fuikui-san walks up to the table as the other reporter leaves. "Who spill soy sauce," he says in terrible English, pointing at the stain on the tablecloth.
"He did," the short reporter says, pointing in the direction of the other reporter. Back at the sushi grill, a tray full of drinks from the bar is slid up into the window. Mitsukoshi continues what he's doing, but his eyes are on the drinks until a waitress comes and takes them away.
--- Back at the table, the other reporter comes back from the restroom. "You won't believe this crazy shit. There was a Jap in the restroom wearing a black robe and holding a ninja sword. He pointed back at you, then made a,"
he gestures with his hand, "slicing motion across his throat." "Yeah right," the short one says. He downs the rest of his drink. "Jesus Christ, I feel like I'm in fucking Disneyland with all these gooks around. It's like China Land in Epcot." "Hey, seriously," the other one says, acting like he's getting mad, "I like Chinese. They only come up to your knees, and they're ready to please." "Hey, what are you lookin' at," the shorter one says all of a sudden. "Oh, nevermind, I thought that Jap cook was looking at me, but he's just slant-eyed." The other reporter starts laughing his ass off.
--- Back at the sushi bar, Mitsukoshi is talking back to the kitchen in Japanese as he stocks behind the counter. "I got those papers ready for your family,"
he says. "Just take it easy on the salt from now on." Fuikui-san comes rushing up, saying, "Teppanyaki-sama, we have troubles." He points back to the two reporters, who are still making racist comments, drawing stares from the tables full of Japanese tourists who they've been oblivious to this whole time. "Customers complaining," he says in English.
"So," Mitsukoshi says, "tell them to pay and go home. We don't need their business." --- Fuikui-san meekly walks up to them, hiding his huge, puffy slant-eyes. He sits the bill on the table and says, in such a bad accent that it's barely understandable, "Sir, we appreciate you business. We are so sorry you must go."
"The fuck," the shorter one says, "Do you speak English?" "Sorry?" Fuikui-san says. "English! Do you speak it," he says, imitating Samuel L. Jackson. "Hey," the other one says, "I think they want us to go." He hands the shorter one the bill.
As the reporter looks over the bill, Fuikui-san nervously looks back towards the sushi bar for help. Mitsukoshi doesn't seem to notice, he's still filling the plates and chatting with the kitchen.
Then, the Iron Chef looks up suddenly as the short reporter throws a plate full of rice down on the floor.
"This fried rice stinks," he yells. "We're not paying!" Fuikui-san kneels down on the floor, cleaning up the rice. The short reporter starts laughing, but the other one starts to get money out of his wallet.
"Hey," the short one says, "the fuck you think you're doing? We're not paying for this shit."
As Fuikui-san stands up, he says, "So sorry," and turns back towards the kitchen. Then, the short reporter kicks him in the ass.
--- When Fuikui-san gets back to the sushi bar, he's sobbing. Mitsukoshi is staring right at the short reporter, steaming. The Iron Chef slaps
Fuikui-san across the forehead, making him drop the rice. "What was that," Mitsukoshi says. "Be taking some pride in you self!" Mitsukoshi takes a fresh plate off the counter and kneels down, scooping the dirty rice into it, staring at the short reporter the whole time in an
intimidation-type fashion. "Wait. What you doing?" Fuikui-san says, grabbing the Iron Chef by his arm. Mitsukoshi says, in an impassioned speech, "I tell them that they are such stupid that their little brain don't gave them a little bit of space for cultural things and that's why they don't respect different cultures, they are in their little world thinking they are the best, but who are they in other countries? Just another son of a bitch."
"Don't say so, it could be dangerous," Fuikui-san pleads. "Hey! I won't gonna be hearing that shit without telling anything, right? And all that people who hurt you, deserve to be hurt too! Who are they anyway? Their brain is like shit, what do shit deserves? Well... just shit!"
The Iron Chef grabs a knife off the sushi bar counter and continues on to the dining floor, as the kitchen staff cheers.
--- Back at the table, the two reporters sit there cockily with their arms folded across their chests, meeting the Iron Chef's stare. He walks up and sits down across from them. "You got a problem with the rice?" he says.
The short reporter smirks. "Rice mean nothing to you, but to us, it is like mother and father. Why you hurt my family?" The Iron Chef covers his eyes and acts like he's crying. "I feel so sorry with my rice," he says.
Then, he looks up and slams the plate of rice down in front of the short reporter, a crazy spark in his eyes. The short reporter looks to his friend, but the other guy just scoots back from the table.
"Apologize to the rice right now!" "Get a load of this guy," the short reporter says, with a nervous laugh. Then, the Iron Chef's knife flashes through the air in front of the reporter's face. "Please! I'm asking you," he says. "Eat the fucking rice!"
The entire restaurant is silent. Everyone is watching the short reporter to see his next move. It's a Japanese stand-off, until the Iron Chef cuts his own forehead with the knife, a crazy intimidating grin on his face, and he takes a handful of the rice, sticking it in his mouth and letting it fall out as he chews, rice and blood dripping down on the table in front of him.
The short reporter then bows his face into the plate of rice like a dog and starts eating it. The other reporter opens his wallet and starts counting bills, but he just lays it all on the table, and runs out.
"That's good," Mitsukoshi says, patting the short reporter on the back of the head. "Fried rice is good for you."
