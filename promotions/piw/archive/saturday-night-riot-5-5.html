Saturday Night Riot 5/5

Mar 9, 1997, 12:00:00 AM

*********************************************************** I>- WORLD TITLE - The Royal Champion vs Hardcore Harris -<I *********************************************************** Mark Walters: Here we go! This is the match we've all been waiting for! We are setting up the ring now for our main event matchup, a barbed wire cage match between The Royal Champion and "Hardcore" Matt Harris for the PIW World Title...Jim, this should be a good one. Jim F'N Johnson: That's right, Mark. This match has been building up for a while now. The Royal Champion, the great competitor that he is, is getting his shot at the World Title finally...these two men are two of the best PIW has to offer. Mark: And both of them are coming off injuries...RC was laid out on Wednesday by Slade, and set on fire! Harris has just been in and out of therapy all week, and as I hear it just went this afternoon to get checked out! Jim: Well, both men know this is for the title...I think either one of them would wrestle with a bullet wound if they had to... Mark: Adrenaline is a strange thing, Jim. Jim: You said it, Mark. But this match should prove to be full of extreme, bloody, entertaining action! [Johnson stands up and turns to the fans.] You want extreme action?!?! Crowd: YEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEAH! PIW! PIW! PIW! PIW! PIW! PIW! Mark: These people are fired up, let's get to Ricky Wix for the ring announcements. Ricky Wix: This following contest is scheduled for one fall, and is for the PIW World Championship! Crowd: YEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEAH! [Mr.' Perfect's theme starts up.] Crowd: BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! Ricky: Introducing first, from the Royal Castle, weighing in at 575 pounds, and led to the ring by the Royal Lawyer and the Royal Advisor...the challenger...THE ROYAL CHAMPION! (the house lights dim as Mr.Perfect's music blasts through the arena. The Royal Lawyer comes out in a shoffer driven gold golf cart holing his Royal Briefcase in his right hand. He is yelling at fans from his golfcart telling them to "shut the hell up!". After that The Royal Adviser walks out yelling at fans stealing PIW merchandise and ripping it up and taking food from little kids and then throwing it back at them. The Royal Champion then comes out. The Royal Slaves are carrying The Royal Thone he is sitting on to the ring as The Royal Champion yells at the crowd. He has his WEW World Title over his right shoulder and is pointing at it with his left hand as he yells at the fans. One he reaches the ring he gets out of his throne and steps over the top rope into the ring where The Royal Lawyer and Adviser are waiting. The Royal Champion holds his WEW World Title up as the place EXPLODES WITH BOOS! Fans are throwing trash inthe ring and security are holding ! wild fans back from jumping in the ring and attack them. The Royal Lawyer, Adviser and Champion simply laugh as The Royal Champion hands The Royal Lawyer the title and he puts it in his briefcase. The Royal Adviser then hands The Royal champion the house mic) The Royal Champion-Thank you for that warm welcome and now you are going to see why I am the reigning WEW World Champion AND MY OPPONENT ISN'T! (he laughs and throws the mic as the ring announcer and walks off yelling at some fans still) Jim: Not a crowd favorite, never was, but this guy can kick anyone's ass! Mark: Looks are deceiving, and the Royal Champion has proven that time and time again here in the PIW...he is definitely worthy of top contender status. Jim: And he doesn't look that bad after what happened to him on Wednesday...is that a new title? Mark: He probably just paid to get it repaired. Jim: Yeah...he would do that. Ricky: And his opponent... ["Control" by Juno Reactor blares over the PA.] Crowd: YEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEAH! HARRIS! HARRIS! Ricky: Hailing from New York City, New York, and weighing in tonight at 250 pounds...led to the ring by "Sinister" Sydney Smythe...he is THE CURRENT PIW WORLD CHAMPION..."HARDCORE" MATT HARRIS! [The lights go down and "Control" by Juno Reactor plays over the loud speakers. A spotlight shines on the curtain. Sydney Smythe walks out with a black blazer with the letters SSS on the back, black halter top, and black shorts. She has long black hair, green eyes, and an athletic figure. She walks halfway down the aisle and waits. Another spotlight hits the curtain. Then Matt Harris strides out with an I Love NY leather jacket over his wrestling attire of a black shirt with the letters HC on it, gray vest, black jean shorts, black gloves with the fingers cut out, combat boots, and sunglasses. He immediately riles the crowd up into a frenzy, nearly jumping into them in a Chris Jericho-like fashion. After they are sufficiently crazy, he runs over and yells a few pre-match sayings into the camera. He runs past Syd to the ring. She follows and Matt holds the ropes open for her. He takes off his jacket and gives it to her, then lets her out of the ring. He gives his opponent a thumbs down.] Jim: Harris just gave RC the thumbs down, and the Royal Champ is pissed. Harris is pointing at his title and laughing. The Lawyer and Advisor are talking to RC right now...this match will be underway any second. Mark: Yep, and...hey! Who's that coming down to ringside? Jim: That's Jeremy Stevens...he just grabbed the mike from Wix and is trying to get the crowd's attention. What's he doing here? [Crowd boos as Jeremy Stevens grabs a open mic from ringside and stands on the outside of the ring. Delaying the start of the match.] JEREMY: Please can everyone just settle down, I got something important to say. [Crowd stops booing] JEREMY: As we all know last Saturday Night the wrestling was dealt another big setback. One of the brightest and most talented wrestlers in years Silver Casey Stranglehold or something like that, died at the hands of the DEA. [Crowd Cheers!!!] JEREMY: Now I didn't know Casey, but I did admire him...Casey you will be missed, you were one of the greats. Nobody combine the wrestling skills of Jim Duggan and the charisma of Lazertron better than you. CROWD: CASEY SUCKS!!! CASEY SUCKS!!! JEREMY: Now before this clash of titans gets started I just would like to say one thing. JEREMY: Matt, there is no doubt about it you are Hardcore. This crowd loves you as you come out here week in and week out busting your ass on a table or busting your face with a chair... CROWD: HARRIS!!! HARRIS!! JEREMY: Matt, I am concerned...That is a big boy over there. Matt I am here to let you know that you have nothing to worry about. [Crowd chants "Shut the fuck up!"] JEREMY: If you were to died tonight here in Club Expo not only would you get the biggest pop of your career but _I_ Jeremy Stevens will do everything in my power to take good care of Syd for you. So now you can go out tonight give everyone their money's worth, cause now you know Syd is in good hands. [Crowd boos as Jeremy Stevens winks at Syd Smythe and walks back to the locker room] Mark: Harris is pissed! He starts out of the cage...Oh, no! Avalanche from behind by the Royal Champion and this one is underway! Jeremy Stevens is laughing his way to the back...Sydney Smythe is disgusted, and Matt Harris is getting the boots out to him in the ring by the Royal Champion. <DING DING DING> Jim: RC picks Matt up off the mat...over his head...he just tosses him into the side off the cage! It's a good thing Harris wears all those layers of clothing or else his back would be shredded wheat! RC pulls him off the cage and tosses him to the mat. Harris slowly getting up...RC in with a big boot. Harris blocks and sweeps the Royal Champion's other leg...that'll buy him some time... Mark: RC up to his feet rather quick...and here comes Harris! Huge right to the Royal Champion's head! And another! Harris kicks RC in the stomach...no effect! RC punches Harris in the chest, hard! He's staggered! The Royal Champ in with a clothesline! Harris ducks! Dropkick to the back of the Royal Champion and he goes flying into the corner... Jim: Harris in there like a bolt, and he's on the back of the Royal Champion, punching him in the head over and over again! That isn't smart...RC just falls backward! Crowd: OOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOH! Mark: Harris pancake! Jim: RC gets up, and picks up Harris, who is holding his ribs...he picks him up...all the way....vertical suplex....he's keeping him up there for a long time. Harris's head must be swimming! And down! Harris just bounced a good foot and a half in the air! Mark: The Royal Champion with a lateral press! 1! 2! NO! It's not that easy to put away Harris. The Royal Champion picks up Harris and tosses him into a corner. Harris hits hard and here comes the Royal Champion. Harris dives to the side...and RC nails the turnbuckle! He staggered...Harris quickly to the top...Spinning DDT! Jim: He just spiked the Royal Champion! Harris turns to the crowd and throws his hands in the air! Crowd: YEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEAH! Mark: He should have covered the Royal Champ there instead of being a glory hog. That could come back to haunt him later... Jim: Maybe...Harris picks up the Royal Champion off the mat...swinging neckbreaker! He goes off the ropes...huge legdrop! The cover! 1! 2! Power Kickout by the Royal Champion! Mark: He sits up, and Harris right back over! RC just shoved him away! Harris flies into a corner! I can't believe the power of the Royal Champion! He just threw Harris halfway across the ring, and Harris was still airborne! Jim: That's power! RC to his feet and over to Harris, who kicks RC in the knee! RC fires back with a shot to the head! Harris with a shot of his own! The Royal Champ returns! Harris with a headbutt! Another! He shoves the Royal Champion backwards, and jumps to the second turnbuckle! Missile dropkick! No! Royal Champion catches Harris in a powerbomb! He holds it for a pin! 1! 2! 3! No! Mark: Harris gets the shoulder up in time! RC picks him up and puts him in the corner, facing the cage! He runs and drives a knee into Harris' lower back! Ow! Harris was just at the chiropractor a few hours ago! And he got driven into the cage. Harris slumps to his knees! The Royal Champion grabs him by his hair...up and down! Like a ragdoll! Jim: Harris is getting his ass kicked! That's something you don't see too often! RC stomps once and climbs to the second turnbuckle...big splash coming up! No! Harris lifts both boots into RC's face! The Royal Champ falls into the corner. Harris slides away backwards on his elbows, cut up and holding his back. His arm got sliced on that cage. Mark: RC shaking the cobwebs out and he runs at Harris! Harris scrambles to his feet and sidesteps the Royal Champion...he pushes him into the side of the cage! The champion gets caught up there! Harris walks over to the other side of the ring. What's going on now? The Advisor slipping brass knuckles on the Royal Champion...no! Sydney Smythe over and she...oh, my GOD! Crowd: YEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEAH! PIW! PIW! PIW! PIW! Jim: She maced the Advisor! She grabs the knucks and tosses them into the crowd! Oh, no! The Lawyer nails her with the briefcase! What a despicable act! The Royal Lawyer just laid out Sydney Smythe! The Advisor is blinded! Matt Harris didn't see it, but he runs at RC...huge knee lift! Harris jumps up...inverted DDT! The cover! 1! 2! No! Mark: Harris getting frustrated...he picks up the Royal Champion. Some huge rights to the head. He backs him up into a corner, and pounds him some more! He's got a shoulder in him, and no way...YES! He picks RC up to the top rope! Harris holding his back now...he's hurt! Now he's climbing the cage! What the hell is he doing? He tosses some kicks into RC for good measure! Jim: Harris on top of the cage...he points to the crowd...he gives the thumbs down! Yes! Yes! Harris off! He lands on the Champion's back and...YEEEEEEEEEEEEAH! Crowd: OOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOH! PIW! PIW! PIW! PIW! PIW! PIW Mark: INVERTED HARDCORE DRIVER OFF THE TOP ROPE! Harris stumbles across the ring and lands in the opposite corner. What the hell is wrong with him? He isn't covering... Jim: He's covering something. He nailed his groin on the back of RC's head delivering that move...he is in some real pain, but the Royal Champ isn't moving. Harris crawling across the ring...he drapes an arm over the Royal Champion. 1! 2! 3! Yes! NO! The ref holding up two fingers. Mark: Harris is so frustrated! He rolls off and slowly gets to his feet. The Lawyer slides the briefcase under the cage and RC blindly grabs it! Wait, Syd is back on her feet! She taps the Lawyer on the shoulder... and he turns around...huge right hand by Sydney! The Lawyer is down! The crowd loves it! Jim: Harris goes to splash the Royal Champ, and gets a face full of briefcase! He slumps to the mat, and now RC is on his feet...he drags him to the corner...he puts the briefcase down...He's dragging Harris to the top...we may see the Royal Drop! Mark: Syd runs over and puts the mace in Matt's hand! RC has Harris up...he gets maced and just drops Harris! Harris nails his head on the briefcase and he's out. RC just struggling to keep his balance up there! He's gonna fall! Harris slowly coming to...he realizes what's going to happen...he's trying to get out of the way! Jim: Matt gets the briefcase up! Crowd: OOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOH! Mark: RC falls and gets nailed with the briefcase! Harris can barely move...he makes it to the corner with the briefcase, and he's going up top again...what the hell is he doing now...he puts the briefcase behind his leg and...OH MY GOD! Jim: Arabian face buster! He covers the Royal Champion! 1! 2! 3! <DING DING DING> Wix: Here is your winner, at nineteen minutes...thirteen seconds...AND STILL PIW WORLD CHAMPION, "HARDCORE" MATT HARRIS! Jim: What a match! What a match! Mark: Wait! Here comes Cashpot and Gameshow...they're in the cage! They pull Harris off of the Royal Champion and start pounding on him! They are doing a number on RC! Harris gets to his feet and he has no clue what's going on! Jim: Johnny Extreme jumps out of the stands, and he's climbing the cage...he's up top with the Extremist belt, and down across the face of Harris! This is carnage... Walters: Here comes some help from the back.. There breaking this up.. Folks we will be RIGHT BACK!!! (Commerical... You see a few of em and then you come back.. The Ring is empty. The cage is down..) Johnson: Fans.. We finally got this under control.. We are out of time now.. This has bean.. OAAAAAAAAAUHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!! (Someone Hit's Johnson with a CHAIR) Walters: What the?!?! a Fan just hit johnson with a chair.. NO WAIT A SECOND!!! WHAT ARE YOU DOING HERE!?!?! ITS MAGUS FROM EWA?!?! He is getting into the ring.. [Magus is handed the mic, and he folds out the chair, and takes a seat in it.] Crowd: BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!! EWA-SUCKS!! EWA-SUCKS!! EWA-SUCKS!! EWA-SUCKS!! EWA-SUCKS!! EWA-SUCKS!! [Magus smiles, and pats his EWA NA Title, which is now drapped around his shoulder.] Magus: Last night .. some of your wrestlers: Hardcore Harris, Johnny Extreme, the Eden Brothers, and the Royal Family made a little stop by at EWA's WAR card. Did you all see what happened? [Crowd yells out, "YEAH!" Some of them say a few explicit 'lyric's' and throw trash into the ring.] Magus: Then I guess you all saw those wreslters that I just listed, _including_ Jim Johnson. [points over towards the laid out Johnson] What I just did was very un-like myself. But desperate, and unusual actions come as a result of _WAR_! Yep, WAR .. not against Cordell Crane, or any other _EWA_ wrestler, but against [pointing out to the crowd] the PIW! One 'fan' of your federation said that the EWA is nothing but entertainers, and the PIW has athletes. [Magus stands up and smiles a litle bit.] Do you call a bunch of _thugs_ jumping two men who just fought a long, brutal match _ATHLETES_? Crowd: YEAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!! Magus: [chuckles] Well, we'll just see who the 'athlete' is when I face _your_ represitive in the Summit Cup -- SLADE! [Crowd cheers, and some start to chant "SLADE! SLADE! SLADE!"] He was one of your lead _thugs_, against myself and Cordell Crane. Slade, you think that I am inferior to ya, eh? You think that I can't handle your 'extremity?' We'll see Slade, we'll see what happens when I step into the ring with you, April 7th, I believe is the slated date for our match up. April 7th, you [points to the crowd] and the rest of the PIW will realize .. just how 'athletic' you are, or how 'extreme' you are. Speaking of Slade .... _WHERE IS HE_? [Magus pauses for about 5 seconds .. and there is silence in the crowd, awaiting Slade.] Walters: [over Magus] I think he isn't in the arena. Magus: Looks like the pansy isn't here, eh? He talks the talk, comes down to WAR, and his thugs beat on me, but when I come down to his [smirks] hell-hole, he isn't here! Slade .. COME OUT COME OUT WHEREVER YOU ARE! [30 seconds pass .. still, nothing. Magus taps on his wrist, as if seeing what time it is.] Magus: Is he afraid of facing up to a _REAL_ man? I.E., a member of the Extreme Wrestling Association? Slade -- I hope you are spending this valuable time preparing for a match, because 1 month isn't nearly enough time to prepare for me. I guess that this 'athlete' isn't gonna come out ... afraid, JUST LIKE ALL OF THOSE PIW THUGS! [Suddenly Matt Harris, and the Royal Family come out, along with some other wreslters, such as Johnny Extreme. They try to get to the ring, but officials, possibly anticipating this block them off. Magus points at them.] Magus: So .. boys, how ya doing tonight? [smiles, and readjusts the position of his NA Title.] It seems like your figure-head, Slade ain't here. Well .. he will _have_ to be here next week, or the week after that, or after that. Because Slade, you're gonna get yours, you can BANK on that one! [The wreslters that are being blocked by the officials try and bust through, but they can't do it.] Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling .. you want a _WAR_? Eh? You've got one! Slade ... [Magus suddenly get a very, very series look on his face. You can see the fire raging in his eyes.] _THIS_ ... is .. _WAR_! Superstar Summit: PIW will find out that they are not quite so extreme. Summit Cup: Slade will realize .. just what it means ... to be _DESTROYED_! LIGHTS OUT! [credits roll as the camera fades to black.] [static]
