CWO SHOWCASE 2/23/97

23 February 1997

CWO SHOWCASE 2/23/97 Producer: Jim Longo ( [detail withheld] ) Executive Producer: Brent G. Alles ( [detail withheld] ) (The opening music begins to play as we see highlights from the Focus-Benjamin Blake Bentley match, with Focus pinning Benjamin Blake Bentley after the Focal Point powerbomb. Finally, the CWO SHOWCASE logo fades in on a darkened screen, then we see a packed arena as the camera pans to Kevin Rinaldi & Fabulous Feenix.) Kevin: Hello, everyone, and welcome to CWO SHOWCASE!!! I'm Kevin Rinaldi, and with me is the one, and thankfully the only, Fabulous Feenix!!!! Feenix: WHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!! Hello, America!!!!! Be fabulous!!!! Kevin: As you saw in the opening credits, Benjamin Blake Bentley repeated his previous victory against the newcomer Focus after 16 minutes of fierce action!! In our opening televised match, Tommy Lightning joins the CWO, and his first opponent will be Sin!!! Feenix: Gad, the kid isn't old enough to drink yet, and he's wrestling professionally? Don't you people have any child labor laws or something? Kevin: The Panther takes on one half of the North American Tag Team Champions, Ron "The Rude Guy" Johnson! Feenix: The Rude Guy's been assigned a lot of singles matches lately--is Assistant Director Jim Longo trying to wear him down? Kevin: David "The Destroyer" Davis battles the ever-popular Johnny Reb!!! Feenix: And Davis is going to continue his winning ways!!! He almost single-handedly won the Survivor match for his team last week!!! Kevin: Jason Varga takes on Talon!!!! And, in our main event, CWO Light Heavyweight Champion Tiger Rogers defends his title against the former champion, Moses!!! We'll be back after this!!!! COMMERCIAL BREAK Kevin: And we're back with our first look at Tommy Lightning!!! Let's go to the ring!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. ("The Kid is Hot Tonight" by Loverboy plays as the fans cheer) Coming down the aisle, weighing 235 pounds, from Vancouver, British Columbia. Canada, Tommy Lightning! ("Creature of the Wheel" by White Zombie plays as the fans boo) And his opponent, weighing 240 pounds, from the Mexican deserts, Sin! Kevin: The end of one road, and the beginning of another. Tommy Lightning makes his debut against Sin, who has declared that this will be his final match in the CWO! And Tommy goes for a fireman's carry takedown, but Sin kicks him right in the face!!! Feenix: Now THERE's an auspicious start!!! Put this baby back in diapers! Kevin: Sin drags Tommy to his feet by his hair...and throws him through the ropes and out of the ring!!! Sin going after the youngster!!! The Mexican grinding the boy from Vancouver's face into the floor!!! Feenix: So much for North American Free Trade, right? Kevin: Tommy managing to roll back in the ring....and catches Sin with a kick on the way in!!! Feenix: Well, actually, that's not true.....they're trading punches, kicks, and they seem to be pretty free with them...... Kevin: Tommy sends Sin into the corner....misses the shoulder!!!! And Sin making his disgust known to the crowd......and they're giving it right back!!!! Feenix: Tasteless barbarians. Half the people out there shouldn't even be allowed to vote. Kevin: Sin arguing with a woman in the front row.....Tommy's on his feet! Tommy taps Sin on the shoulder.....and nails him with a kick to the gut! Tommy sending Sin into the other corner...but AGAIN misses with the shoulder!!! Feenix: Says something about the schools in Vancouver, doesn't it? Kevin: Sin with a quick cover!!!! 1....Tommy gets the shoulder up!!! Feenix: "The Kid is Hot Tonight"--yeah, right!!! He's about as hot as Kellen and Tamara put together!!!! Kevin: Now wait a minute!!! Both of those women are extraordinarily beautiful!!! Feenix: First off, I'M more beautiful than they are!!! Second, Lust puts us all to shame!!! Kevin: No accounting for taste.....Sin sends Tommy over the ropes this time, but the end result is the same!!!! Sin going after Tommy, but Tommy's ready for him this time!!! Both men brawling outside the ring....Sin catches Tommy with a quick jab that stuns the youngster!! Feenix: This is unbelievable....if he's having this much trouble with Sin, how's he going to take on people like Bane and the Master of Darkness??? Kevin: Hopefully, he'll never have to...Tommy with a good punch there! Tommy up top......flying sunset flip!!! Cover!!! 1....2....Sin kicks out!!! Tommy sends Sin into the ropes.....nails him with the Lightning Strike!!!!! Feenix: What Lightning Strike? It's a dropkick!!!! Kevin: Cover!!!! 1....2.....Sin kicks out!!! Feenix: Then again, if Sin's having this much trouble with a wet-behind- the-nads greenie, maybe he SHOULD retire!!!! Kevin: Maybe!!! Tommy with a chop to Sin's chest.....knocks him down with a boot to the head....going for a kneedrop....but Sin moves out of the way!!! But Sin continuing to argue with that woman in the front row!!! Feenix: Hey, Sin, the match is this way!!!! Kevin: Tommy with another kick to Sin's head!!! And this time Tommy sends SIN out of the ring!!! Tommy climbing outside the ring....and nails Sin with an axhandle off the ring apron! Tommy climbing back into the ring...Sin rolling under the bottom rope. Tommy's on the top buckle....Sin shakes the ropes, and Tommy lands hard!!! Feenix: It's okay, he didn't damage anything vital...he only landed on his head!!! Kevin: And Sin slapping the rookie in the face!!!! Sin sends Tommy into the ropes.....reverse knee clip!!!! Cover!!!! Sin's got his feet on the ropes for leverage!!!! 1.....2......NO!!! Tommy managed to get HIS foot on the ropes, and the referee breaks up the cover!!! Feenix: Sin finally concentrating on the match, instead of that bimbo in the first row!! Kevin: Sin stomping away on Tommy's head and chest!!! Into the ropes......and Sin drops the leg!!!! And AGAIN Sin slaps Tommy's face!!! Feenix: That might have been a mistake.... Kevin: Tommy Lightning has gotten new life!!!! Tommy nails Sin with a left hand that sends the heel across the ring!!!! Tommy up on the buckle...flying sunset flip!!!! Cover!!!! 1.....2....3!!!!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner, in 4 minutes and 40 seconds, in his CWO debut, TOMMYYYYYY LIGHTNING!!!!!!! Kevin: Tommy Lightning gets the win in his first CWO match! We'll be back after this... wait a minute... who's this... it's the Eternal Wanderers! The Eternal Wanderers attacking Tommy Lightning and Exodus just clipped Lightning in the knee! Exile now has his new finisher on him, the X-Treme, and Tommy Lightning is getti ng a very painful introduction to the CWO... we have to go to commercial... we'll be right back.... COMMERCIAL BREAK Kevin: We're back with more CWO wrestling action!!! Let's go to the ring!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. ("Taps" plays as the fans cheer) Coming down the aisle, weighing 210 pounds, from Detroit, Michigan, Panther! (The Bad Guys' theme music plays as the fans boo) And his opponent, weighing 238 pounds, from Attica, New York and accompanied by Herbie Versmells, Ron 'The Rude Guy' Johnson! Kevin: And Panther daring Johnson to charge him!!! And Johnson stupid enough to take him up on it!!! Panther with an armbar takedown!!! Panther going for a spinning toe hold, but Johnson kicks him off!!! Johnson with a devastating right hand!!! Johnson sends Panther into the ropes....dropkick!!!! Johnson heading up top.....flying double stomp!!!! Flying headscissors by Johnson!!!! Another.....NO!!! He missed!!! And Panther laughing at him!!! Feenix: That could be a mistake, the Bad Guys are not people you want mad at you!!! Kevin: Panther with a textbook fireman's carry--he's made a lot of enemies by leaving Blood Incorporated, hasn't he? Feenix: That he did, but a man's gotta do what a man's gotta do. Wrestling is an individual sport, and Panther did what he thought was best for his career!!! Kevin: Panther with a spinning toe hold.......he releases it....flip elbow drop!!! And Panther swatting the side of Johnson's head!!! Feenix: Johnson didn't like that!!! Kevin: Johnson on his feet!!!! Double axehandle!!!! And a head scissors roll!! Feenix: But Panther's fighting back!!! Kevin: Panther with a kick to Johnson's gut!!! And a huge chop!!! But Johnson comes right back with fists!!! Panther is down!!! Johnson up top....flying double stomp!!! Johnson sends Panther into the ropes...and nails him with a reverse neckbreaker!!!! Johnson stomping away on the prone Panther!!! Going up top again......but this time Panther avoids the double stomp!!! Feenix: Johnson got greedy, and this time it cost him!!! Kevin: Panther with a camel clutch!!!! Johnson in agony!!!! Panther releases the hold....gives Johnson a few stomps.....going up top....and nails the elbow!!!! Panther applying an armbar....working the shoulder of Johnson......releases the hold.....going up top....but this time it's Johnson who avoids the aerial attack!!!! Feenix: That's why I never used to go to the turnbuckles!!! Too many things could go wrong!!! Kevin: Johnson sends Panther out of the ring!!! Johnson's going after him, but Panther's ready for him!!! OH, MY!!!!! Johnson sent Panther headfirst into the concrete floor, and Panther comes up bloody!!! Feenix: That could decide the match!!!! Kevin: Both men climbing back into the ring.....Johnson sends Panther into the ropes......but Panther catches him with a flying headscissors!!! Panther with a quick camel clutch!!!!! He releases the hold.....going up top...but Johnson catches him in the throat on the way down!!!! Johnson with a headscissors!!! Feenix: These two are almost perfectly matched!!! We haven't had a single cover!!!! Kevin: Johnson sends Panther into the ropes.....Panther nails him with a dropkick on the rebound!!! Panther with a wild swing that's ducked by Johnson!!!! Johnson with a forearm to Panther's head!!! Panther stuns Johnson with a chop!!! Panther going up top....flying head scissors!!!! Panther slaps on another camel clutch, applying more pressure to the Rude Guy's back!!! Panther releases the hold....going up top again.....but MISSES the moonsault! Feenix: This is fantastic action!!! Kevin: Johnson sends Panther into the ropes....but Panther nails him with a flying headscissors!!! Panther with a flip elbow drop!!!! Fistdrop by Panther!!!! Cover!!! 1...2....thr...no, Johnson's foot is on the ropes....Johnson's foot is on the ropes!!! Feenix: Twelve minutes into the match and this is the first cover!! What a match!!! Kevin: Panther with a quick headscissors!!! He's going for the Eclipse....he got it!!! Panther cutting off the blood to Johnson's brain!!!! Johnson's almost out!!!! Panther lets him out....going up to the buckles.....flip elbow drop!!! And Panther reapplies the Eclipse.......and Johnson is out!!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner, in 13 minutes and 20 seconds......PANTHER!!!!!!!!!!!! Kevin: Panther chalks up a win against the North American tag champion!!! Feenix: If he had a tag partner, that should get him a title shot!!! Kevin: We'll be back!!!! COMMERCIAL BREAK Kevin: And we're back!!!! Johnny Reb and David Davis getting set to hit the mat!!! Let's go to the ring!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. ("Sweet Home Alabama" by Lynyrd Skynyrd plays as the fans cheer) Coming down the aisle...weighing 237 pounds, from Mobile, Alabama and accompanied by General Lee, Johnny Reb! ("Livin' on the Edge" by Aerosmith plays as the fans boo) And his opponent, weighing 235 pounds, from Los Angeles, California and accompanied by Jimbo Jones, David 'The Destroyer' Davis! Kevin: And Davis goes after Reb immediately....sends him into the ropes....crescent kick!!! Davis with a backflip bodypress!!!! Cover!!!!! 1...2....Reb gets the shoulder up!!! Feenix: David and Goliath have split up as a tag team, and Davis is really putting everything he has into his solo career!!! The champs better watch their belts!!! Kevin: Davis up on the top buckle....elbow drop!!!! 1....2....again Reb gets the shoulder up!!! And Jimbo Jones was holding Reb's legs!!! Feenix: Another reason Davis is going to get a championship.....he's got a great manager!!! Kevin: Davis with a headlock......Reb sends Davis into the ropes....Davis with a shoulderblock that sends Reb to the canvas.....Davis into the ropes again...Reb ducking under the leapfrogging Davis.....both men into the ropes again....and Reb nails Davis with a Frankensteiner!!!! Davis caught completely by surprise!! Reb sends him into the ropes.....clothesline!!!! Reb going up top...dropkick!!! Davis was knocked through the ropes by that one!!! Reb going outside...he's got a chair!!!! And Reb breaks the chair over Davis's head!!! Feenix: This is a hero????? Kevin: Reb has Davis by the hair......he's going to slam Davis's head into the floor.....but Davis reverses!!!! Reb's head is split open!!! Feenix: Hey, this is wrestling, not golf!!!! Kevin: Both men climbing back into the ring.....Reb bleeding badly! Davis up top.....flying elbow!!! Cover...and Davis has his feet on the ropes!!! 1..... 2....Reb gets the shoulder in the air!!! Feenix: That cut's gonna get worse before this is over, and it's going to affect Reb's effectiveness!! General Lee should throw in the towel now!!! Kevin: Well, Davis is the one doing the throwing.....as in throwing Johnny Reb over the top rope!!! Davis nails Reb with a fistdrive to the floor!!! Davis rolls Reb back into the ring....General Lee poking Davis, but the Destroyer ignoring him! Davis has Reb up........POWERBOMB!!!! Cover!!! 1.....Reb gets the foot on the ropes!!!! Feenix: Johnny Reb hasn't mounted any kind of offense since he hit the floor!!! This match has been all David Davis!!! Kevin: Davis climbing up top.....but Reb is on his feet!!! Reb grabbing Davis off the buckle....and superplexes him off the buckle!!! How's that for offense??? Feenix: Not bad... Kevin: Reb running into the ropes.....but he missed the elbow drop!!!! Davis nails him with the headbutt!!!! And Davis slaps Johnny Reb across the face...and Reb responds by PUNCHING Davis in his!!!!! Feenix: Can't fault him there.... Kevin: Reb nails Davis with the Southern Cross!!!! Davis is down!!!! Reb turning him over.....surfboard!!!! The ref asking Davis if he's ready to give it up, but Davis screaming no!!!! Feenix: Screaming is right!!!! He's louder than Drew Barrymore!!!! Kevin: Reb releasing the hold, heading up top....but he misses the dropkick by a mile!!!! Feenix: He can't see!!!! The blood's in his eyes!!! Kevin: Davis sends Reb into the ropes....spinning heel kick!!!! Reb knocked out of the ring!!!!!!!!! Davis arguing with the referee...wait a minute!!!! What's Gold Kard doing here????? Feenix: Nothing good for Reb, I can tell you!!!! Kevin: Gold Kard has a metal pipe......and he nails Reb across the back of the head with it!!!! Reb is out!!!!! The referee didn't see it.....he's continuing the count.....8......9.....10!!!! That's it!!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner as a result of a countout in 8 minutes and 39 seconds.......DAVID "THE DESTROYER" DAVISSSSSSS!!!!! Kevin: And Johnny Reb is still unconscious!!!!! They're bringing the EMS technicians for him!!!! And Gold Kard is nowhere to be seen!!! We've got to go to a commercial, we'll try to get more information!!!! COMMERCIAL BREAK Kevin: And we're back!!!! The paramedics have taken Johnny Reb to the hospital for tests, but the general feel is that he'll be fine in a couple of days. We don't know yet what action, if any, will be taken against Gold Kard for his cowardly attack, but even Feenix thinks he should be suspended!!! Feenix: That's right!!! Yeah, this is wrestling, you do what you have to do to win, but a sneak attack like that? That just says you've got no stones!!!! Kevin: Let's go to the ring for our next match!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. ("My Jekyll Doesn't Hide" by Ozzy Osbourne plays as the fans boo) Coming down the aisle, weighing 235 pounds, from parts unknown and accompanied by Sandman, Talon! ("Fanfare for the Common Man" by Aaron Copland plays as the fans cheer) And his opponent, weighing 235 pounds, from Jordan, New York and accompanied by Jim Peer, Jason 'Flash' Varga! Kevin: And Flash attacks Talon at superspeed!!!! Feenix: YOU should be hit with a metal pipe for that!!! Kevin: Sorry...Varga with a big chop........drives Talon back into the corner.... and a forearm to the jaw!!! Feenix: That loosened some teeth!!!! Kevin: Varga heading up top....but Talon catches him on the way down with a fist to the midsection!!! Talon sends Varga into the corner....flying splash!!! Sends him into the ropes....big jump kick!!!! Setting him up for another....but this time Varga ducks!!! Varga kicks Talon's legs out from under him!!! Scoops him up.....and dumps him outside the ring!!!!! Feenix: And Talon landed hard!!! He's not moving all that fast!!! Kevin: Varga drags Talon back into the ring....slaps on a headlock....working the head and neck....raises a fist......looks to the crowd for encouragement!!! Feenix: BIG mistake!!!! The fans don't matter!!!!! He's gotta concentrate on the match!!!! Kevin: Varga nails Talon with the fist!!!! Talon hits the deck!!! Varga scoots up top.....ASAI MOONSAULT!!!! And Talon rolling under the ropes to escape the fury of the Flash!!!! Feenix: Varga's not letting him get away, though!!! Kevin: Varga nails Talon with a plancha off the ring apron!!!! Talon is dazed!!! Varga drags him back into the ring again......applies an armbar....look at the stretch on Talon's arm and shoulder!!!! Nothing human should be able to bend like that!!! Feenix: Talon's better than human, Kevin!!! Kevin: WHOA!!!!!! Talon nailed Varga with his free forearm!!!! Talon with a jump kick!!! Cover!!!! 1.....2.....thr...NO!!! Varga's foot is on the ropes!!!! Feenix: Varga lucky he was so close.....Talon caught him right on the button with that one!!!! Kevin: Talon sends Varga into the corner......flying splash!!!! Talon with a jumping spin kick!!! Cover!!!!! 1......2.......NO!!! Varga kicks out!!! Feenix: This match has turned completely around!!! Talon has Varga reeling now, and he's not the kind of guy you can let get that kind of advantage over you!! Kevin: Talon with a reverse thrust kick!!! Roundhouse kick!!!! Another one!!!! Talon is absolutely deadly with his legs!!!! Sends Varga into the ropes....Varga ducks the roundhouse kick!!!! Varga with a chop!!!! Varga slapping on the abdominal stretch...NO! Talon executes a textbook hiptoss!! Feenix: Why so surprised??? Most of the so-called bad guys know how to wrestle! Kevin: Like Baby Waldo??? Feenix: Okay, he's an exception..... Kevin: Talon with a jump kick!!!! Cover!!! 1.....Varga gets the shoulder up!!!! Talon going for another jump kick.....HE MISSED!!!!!! Varga with a huge right hand!!! Cover!!!! 1...2.....thr....NO!!! Talon just got his foot around the bottom rope!!!! Feenix: Varga's getting his second wind, now.....if he gets on a roll, Talon could be out!!! Kevin: Varga with a jumping neck snap!!! That move can break a man's neck!!!! Talon on the mat.....and Varga turns him over in a Boston crab!!!! Talon refuses to submit!!! Varga releases the hold.....setting Talon up..........FLASH FLOOD!!!!!! Cover!!!!! 1...2.....3!!!!!! Feenix: I hate to say it, but a great victory for the Flash!!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner, in 9 minutes and 45 seconds..... JASON "FLASH" VARGAAAAAAAAAA!!!!!! Kevin: The Flash pulls another one out!!!! We'll be back after this!!! COMMERCIAL BREAK Kevin: And we are back with our main event!!!! Moses tries to take back his Light Heavyweight title from the champion, Tiger Rogers!!! Feenix: He should go back to parting the Red Sea, because it ain't gonna happen!!! Kevin: Let's go to the ring!!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit, for the CWO Light Heavyweight Title!!!!! (Psalm 47 plays as the fans cheer) Coming down the aisle, the challenger...weighing 228 pounds and accompanied by the Evangelist, from Bethlehem, Israel, Moses!!!! ("Unforgiven" by Metallica plays as the fans boo) And his opponent, weighing 240 pounds, from Warner Robbins, Georgia and accompanied by Jimbo Jones, the CWO Light Heavyweight Champion....Tiger Rogers!!!!! Kevin: And Moses attacks Rogers as he steps through the ropes!!!! Dropkick!!!! Moses up top...springboard moonsault!!! Cover!!! 1...Rogers kicks out!!! Feenix: Moses is fired up....he wants that title back!!! Kevin: Moses sends Rogers into the ropes...Frankensteiner!!!! Moses going for another moonsault.....he got it!!!!! Cover!!!! 1..2....Rogers kicks out again!!! Feenix: Jones is beside himself!!!! And Rogers doesn't know where he is!!! Kevin: Moses sends Rogers through the ropes!!!! He's going after the champion!!! Moses has a chair!!!! And he shatters it over Rogers's head!!!!! Moses up on the ring apron......clothesline!!!!!! Rogers is dazed!!!! Moses rolls him back into the ring....and applies the Texas Cloverleaf!!!! Rogers in agony!!!! Moses wrenching the hold back and forth, trying to get the champion to give it up!!!! Feenix: Rogers hanging tough, though!!!! He's not going to give up the title so easy!!! Kevin: Moses releasing the Cloverleaf....and slapping on an octopus!!! Rogers screaming in pain!!!! He's going to need his chiropractor tomorrow!!! Feenix: If he doesn't start mounting some kind of offense, he's going to need a coroner!!! Kevin: Moses releases the hold.....going up top.......but he MISSES the Asai Moonsault!!!! Rogers dropping the knee to the head!!!! Cover!!!! 1....2...thr... NO!!! Moses gets his foot on the ropes!!! Feenix: Moses making an amateur mistake.....he should have brought Rogers out to center ring before covering him!!! Kevin: Rogers with a crescent kick!!! Moses knocked out of the ring!!! Rogers going after him!!! Rogers has a chair.....and he nails Moses in the back with it!!! Rogers sending Moses into the ringpost.....NO!!! Moses reverses!!! Moses rolling Rogers back into the ring!!! Moses up top....springboard moonsault!!! Cover!!!! Feenix: Not if Jimbo Jones can help it!!!! Kevin: Jimbo Jones on the ring apron!!!! Moses attacking Jimbo Jones...but Rogers getting to his feet!!!! Rogers charging.....but Moses moves out of the way and Rogers levels his own manager!!!! Feenix: That's going to come out of the champion's paycheck, I can tell you that!!! Kevin: Moses nails Rogers with a plancha!!!! Cover!!!! 1..2....thr...NO!!! Rogers just managed to kick out!!!! Feenix: Moses looks unbeatable......he may just win his title back!!!! Kevin: Moses going for another octopus......Rogers resisting!!! Rogers kicking Moses off!!! Rogers all over Moses with fists and elbows!!! Rogers sending Moses into the ropes.....kneelift!!!! Cover!!!! 1....2...thr....NO!!!! Moses somehow found the energy to kick out!!!! Feenix: He's almost out of gas, though, look at how he's carrying his arms!!! Kevin: Moses nails Rogers with a quick chop!!!! Moses sends Rogers into the ropes......and meets him with a boot to the face!!!! Cover!!!! 1.....2...no!!! Rogers gets the foot on the ropes!!! Moses going up top.......Jimbo Jones pushes him off the buckle!!!!! Feenix: And THAT's going to come out of Rogers's paycheck, too!!!! Jones shouldn't have had to save his butt!!!! Kevin: Rogers has Moses up........trying to set him up for a powerbomb.....but Moses Frankensteiners him!!!!! Moses has him up for a Tiger Bomb...he got it!!! Cover!!!! 1..2...Rogers kicks out!!!! Feenix: Look!!!! It's Inferno!!!! Kevin: Rogers shoves Moses out of the ring.....now he's arguing with the referee!!! Good Lord!!!! Inferno suckerpunched Moses with a roll of quarters!!!!! Moses is out cold!!!! Inferno rolling Moses into the ring......Rogers has him up.... Inverted Powerbomb!!!!! Cover!!!!! 1...2...3!!!!!!!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, after 13 minutes and 52 seconds, your winner and STILL CWO Light Heavyweight Champion, TIGER ROGERS!!!!!!!!!! Kevin: Inferno saves his partner once again!!!!! And we're out of time!!! For the Fabulous Feenix, I'm Kevin Rinaldi, saying, good night everybody!!!!
