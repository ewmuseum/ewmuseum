CWO TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER 11/11/97 - HOUR #2 "LIVE" FROM ONONDAGA WAR MEMORIAL, SYRACUSE, NY RATING: TV-14 (V, L) Executive Producer: Brent G. Alles ( [detail withheld] ) (A spinning globe is shown, as the CWO logo is slowly superimposed over it.) VOICE-OVER: The CWO... providing wrestling excellence the world over.... (Montage of CWO action is shown as Aerosmith's "Fever" plays in the background. The CWO TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER explodes on to the screen, and then we go to a roaring crowd in the Syracuse Onondaga War Memorial, as pyrotechnics go off to signal the start of hour #2. Johnny Starr is in the ring.) JOHNNY STARR (standing in the ring): Welcome back, everybody, to hour #2 of this week's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER. I'm Johnny Starr, and I'm meant to be interviewing someone right now, but I've no idea who he or they are.... (Suddenly from backstage, a collection of people emerge. The lead pair both wear identical kilts, white shirts, and are playing their bagpipes at an almost intolerable level; behind them come two more kilted individuals, one carrying the cross of St. Andrew, the other the Caledonian flag. In the middle walks a young woman, also kilted, who carries a scroll in her hand.) STARR: What the....? (The entourage gets into the ring, and Starr approaches the woman.) STARR: Um... young lady, may I ask you who you are, and what you are doing here? WOMAN (unfolds the scroll): My name is Rhiannon McCloud of the Clan McCloud. My Clan is one of tha' oldest and most well respected in our land, and our name is synonymous with honor. It has come to our attention tha' several individuals in this organization are takin' our name in vain, an' not givin' tha' Clan name the respect we have fought for an' won over countless centuries. Well, now all of this stops. I am authorized by my Clan leaders to issue every single man an' woman in this federation with a formal declaration of war. Before, we did not commit to the rules of warfare because, somewhat foolishly, we presumed that everyone was here for honorable combat - an' we now see we were wrong. From now on, it's war, an' all tha' trappings that go with it. If ANY of ye decide ta cross tha' Clan McCloud, or sully our fine name, you have made yeselves targets in a conflict where we intend ta take no prisoners. To carry out tha' orders of tha' Clan Leaders, we entrust tha' individuals ye know as the Highlanders as our foot soldiers, ta serve up tha' revenge we desire for tha' wrongdoings our Clan has suffered. This is ye first and final warning, CWO; Clan McCloud is now at war. (With that, Rhiannon McCloud rolls up the scroll, stares intently at Starr for a moment, and then the whole entourage leaves the ring, bagpipes blaring and flags hoisted proudly in the air. We go to our announcing team of Kyle Melinn, Christine Ferrara, and David "The Truth" Thomas. The crowd starts to chant, "Wendy's! Wendy's! Wendy's!", much to the annoyance of Thomas.)
