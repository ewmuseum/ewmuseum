CWO EYE ON THE STORM: ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II

15 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWO EYE ON THE STORM: ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II "LIVE" FROM COW PALACE, SAN FRANCISCO, CA 2/14/97, 7:30 PM (EYE ON THE STORM opening comes up as we go to Jerry Covenant and Mike Postema inside a packed Cow Palace, as the crowd roars, fireworks going off.) [JC] - Here we are ladies and gentlemen, live from San Francisco, California. We are mere moments away from St. Valentine's Massacre II! Tonight, all of the belts from the CWO will be on the line, and a lot of feuds will be decided. [MP] - That's right, we've got the Creatures of the Night, facing off against perhaps the best team in the CWO, the Highlanders! Gold Kard vs. the man that turned his back on him, Miguel Sanchez. [JC] - You can rest assured both men will pull out all the stops in that match. Gold Kard appalled by being betrayed, and Sanchez just sick of Kard. [MP] - The Big Cool taking a crack at the Bad Guys for the North American tag team belts, and in a match I'll be looking forward to the most, Tiger Rogers takes on Moses. [JC] - That promises to be a highflying match to be certain, on the flip side of the coin from that match, in the "Monsters of the Ring" match, Sandman will be taking on Big Rich Clark. [MP] - A lot of sneak attacks have gone down as of late, a lot of brawls with four or five men in the ring, those two were always in it. [JC] - In what will be the most vicious match of the night, Johnny Reb is taking on "Master of Darkness" Damion. The ropes have been lined with barbed wire, and these two will get to run wild. [MP] - Easily one of the most intense feuds ever in the CWO, and it's had a lot of influence over the world title picture, which could change tonight as well. [JC] - Wayne Schillo facing, for the first time anywhere, former CWO World Champion Hile Troy. His first World Title shot in a long time, but many feel that he is just to over the hill to pull it off. [MP] - Schillo has been dominant as a champion, turning away all of his challengers, but if anyone can sneak a win by him, Troy would be the man to do it. [JC] - All of this and more, so let's get to the ring with announcer Tony the K for our opening match of Eye On the Storm! [TK] - Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall, with a twenty minute time limit. Weighing in at 265 pounds, from Crown Heights, New York, accompanied by Rabbi Solomon, here is Masher Maccabee! ("Hava Negila" set to a rap beat plays) [JC] - The fans trying to boo Maccabee out of the arena now, but he simply swats his hands and covers his ears at their disapproval. [MP] - Well, that's what he's got Rabbi Solomon at ringside for, so that he can keep him in focus. [TK] - And his opponent... Weighing 319 pounds, from Bethlahem, Israel, accompanied by the Evangelist, here is Savior! [JC] - This is more like the kind of match you'd expect to see taking place on the Golon Heights or on the West Bank, not in the middle of the ring in CWO! [MP] - Well, we've had crusades of sorts going on for a long time. I'm still waiting for Sin to just scorch them all with a fireball and end it. [JC] - Savor starting the match off right away with a kick to the chest of Maccabee, knocks him down with it and starts stomping away, going for the pin! One.. two.. kickout! [MP] - He should know better than that, there's no way he's going to get a pinfall in the first thirty seconds. [JC] - Savior with a side headlock now on Maccabee, turns it into a fireman's carry takedown! Again going for the pin, and this time Maccabee gets his foot on the ropes. [MP] - Well, maybe after his pinfall fails for the tenth time, he'll get the idea. [JC] - Savior picks Maccabee up and fires him to the outside of the ring. Savior with a steel chair, lining it up to nail him with it, but Maccabee moves out of the way and Savior hits the ringpost! [MP] - That's gotta hurt! The idea is to beat your wrestlers IN the ring, not beat the ring! [JC] - Maccabee grabs Savior by the hair and slams his head into the mat and rolls him back into the ring. Maccabee hovering over Savior, and Savior hits him with an elbow shot, Maccabee returns it with a forearm! [MP] - A LOT of force behind that forearm, knocking Savior off his feet and down. [JC] - Maccabee off the ropes, somersault legdrop, he hits! Masher going for the pin... one... two... no, kickout! Maccabee goes up to the top, leaps off, flying elbowdrop lands! Maccabee going for a figure four, Savior reverses it! One... two... kickout! [MP] - That's a definite way to wake up your opponent, catch him in a pinning predicament out of nowhere. [JC] - Maccabee puts Savior in the corner, Maccabee with a hard chop, a nice double axehandle knocks Savior back down. Maccabee going up top once again, flying elbow drop... misses! [MP] - That is what we call going to the well once to often. [JC] - Savior picking up Maccabee, launches him into the ropes, lands a clothesline! Savior this time throws Maccabee OVER the ropes instead of at them, Savior tumbling down to the outside of the ring. [MP] - These guys don't approve much of the scientific aspect do they? [JC] - Savior on the apron, runs... flying elbowdrop down on Maccabee! What a move! Savior fires him back into the ring, Savior in the ring, plays to the crowd. [MP] - He should be trying to put the final touches on Maccabee, not sitting their playing to the idiot fans. [JC] - They pay your salary, I might add. Savior in the corner, he leaps out and goes for a superkick, Masher blocks it! Maccabee throws a punch, Savior comes back with a chop, Masher returns with a low blow! [MP] - That's a quick way to end a punchout. [JC] - Maccabee with a somersault leg drop, he scrapes Savior off the mat, piledriver position, slams his down in the Jerusalem Jackknife! He's going up top now, Homentaschen Hangover legdrop, he hits! [MP] - Going for the pinfall... one... two... kickout! How the HELL did he kickout from that! Savior must be running on total instincts! [JC] - Maccabee picking Savior up, Savior with a punch, but Maccabee knocks him back down. Maccabee picking Savior up a second time, power bomb! Going back up to the top rope... [MP] - If he lands another legdrop, the match will be over. [JC] - Maccabee leaps off the top rope, somersault legdrop, landed! One... two... three! It's over, all over! [TK] - Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the match in a time of 9:39, Masher Maccabee! [MP] - Next we'll see the Deadly Sins going up against Mason Dixon, but first, that goon Covenant will attempt an interview. Let's join him now in the ring. [JC] - Ladies and gentlemen, if you'll please welcome the man who will be the challenger tonight for the world title, former champ, Hile Troy! ("2112 Overture" by Rush plays) Troy makes his way to the ring slowly, slapping hands with the fans as he moves toward the ring. Troy seems to be having more fun on his mind than pressure as he walks then enters the ring [JC] - Well Troy, tonight is your shot that you've been waiting for, for quite some time. Your shot at the world title, against one of the few men that you've never wrestled against before, Wayne Schillo. [HT] - I've never wrestled him before, but I don't NEED the experience, Schillo, your a hell of a champion, but you are NOT the wrestler of the year, I AM. Who is it that saved the CWO from total annihilation? ME! You might have beaten Barry Hart, but you've still never faced the REAL challenges in this league! Where were you, when I was out there taking on both Sandman AND Bane? Back at your frat house, playing shoots and ladders for pretzels with a bunch of drunken jocks! I might have never faced you, but you've never faced ANYONE like me before. People say that I'm over the hill, past my prime... maybe I am. Truth is, I've gotta be running close to the end of my career, but I guarantee you tonight, I'll show the people out there, that I've got one good match left in me! Later tonight, you'll find out yourselves! [JC] - Good luck to you, Mr. Troy, let's get set for the next match! [TK] - Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit. Making their way to the ring, at a combined weight of 761 pounds, here are Pride and Sloth, the Deadly Sins! ("Sweet Dreams" by Marilyn Manson plays) [JC] - The Deadly Sins, unpopular wherever they go, and definitely not happy that they aren't in a title matchup tonight. [MP] - Well, they're a good tag team, but this is the CWO, fierce and vicious competition, it's social Darwinism! [JC] - Let's avoid your social science discussions, if we may please? I don't care what you mastered in, that subject is pathetic! We take you now to pre-recorded comments from both teams. (Stonewall Mason and Beauregard Dixon are in the interview area) DIXON: Y'all saw it Tuesday naht! The baddest men in the game can fall to the Suthun Spike. Deadly Sins, if Bane leaves any of ya fo' us, y'all are gonna get the same! MASON: Y'all heard us raht! This is a new Mason-Dixon. And this pair is gunnin' for a shot at the gold. So this Friday at the Massacuh, after we stand tall aftuh our victory, we head out aftuh what was once ours and what will be ours again...The World Tag Team Titles. DIXON: We've been down fo' too long. Friday naht, we prove once and fo' all that we are back, and that we mean business. MASON: THE SOUTH WILL RAHS AGAIN! (Deadly Sins are in the interview area.) PRIDE: First off, everybody wants to know what's going on with Lust. Why did she do what she did, and where is she now. Well, it's none of your damned business! So we're gonna talk about things we WANT to talk about now, things like Doomsday SLOTH: I can hear Maximum Overdrive now "We've beaten you before, we'll do it again, blah blah blah"... Well, look who we were with! Sandman Jr! And Miguel Sanchez! No wonder we lost! However, we don't give a rat's ass what you do, but Sin is our buddy, and we'll protect him. Don't send your boys after him, we don't come after you! PRIDE: As for Mason-Dixon, at the Massacre, you boys are going to get destroyed! They put us on the Eye to prevent us from making the regular PPV to violent. Well, we're STILL gonna be violent, and you hicks are going to fall, just like the South! SLOTH: That's South, not Sloth! Finally, Hood Rats, Ganglord... wow, did YOU guys pick the wrong ones to mess with! For a heel tag team, all the other heels sure are jealous of us... especially you ghetto trash boys... soon... very soon... you will face Repention as well! (Back to the ring.) [TK] - And their opponents... at a combined weight of 629 pounds, led to the ring by General Lee, here are Stonewall Mason, Beauregard Dixon, the team of Mason Dixon! ("A Country Boy Can Survive" by Hank Williams Jr plays) [JC] - Fans giving nothing short of thundering ovation for this team, and Mason Dixon are charging the ring right off the bat! [MP] - Referee ducking into a corner, and we've already got the bell! [JC] - Dixon in the ring with Pride, he hammers Pride with a double axe handle, follows it up quickly with an eye gouge. Dixon makes the tag to Mason... Mason in the ring, nice running shoulderblock! [MP] - Mason Dixon starting off well with quick tags, they know that the pressure is on, the cameras are running, and the world is watching. [JC] - Mason off the ropes, he goes for another shoulderblock, and Pride reverses it with a Frankensteiner! He picks Mason up, fires him into the ropes, Mason reverses it, powerslam off the ropes! [MP] - These guys aren't sparing ANY time in bringing out all of the high impact moves that they have in their arsenal. [JC] - Mason fires Pride hard into the ropes, Pride back off the other end and he pops Mason with a clothesline! Kneelift to the forehead, lands a chop to the chest. [MP] - At the pace that these guys are going, this match can't last long. [JC] - Mason with a low blow, he picks Pride up and slams him down to the mat. Mason grabbing Pride's legs, he turns him over into a Boston crab, but Pride is right near the ropes and the hold is broken. [MP] - Ask the guy you talked to, Hile Troy. Speed, power, ability, these things don't always matter as long as you can keep track of where you are in the ring. [JC] - Mason stuffs Pride into the corner and lays in a hard chop. Mason winding up for a big punch, but Pride dodges it! Pride with a waistlock from behind into a German suplex! [MP] - Awesome counter right out of the middle of nowhere, countering on Mason's fury behind that punch. [JC] - Pride up on his feet right away, he lands an elbowdrop. I'm surprised he hasn't gone for a pin yet. Pride now with his back turned to the action, mocking the crowd with laughter. [MP] - Mason coming up from behind! Mason hits with a kick to the chest, grabs Pride by the throat and drops him in a chokeslam! [JC] - It's MY job to call the action! Chokeslam! Mason makes the tag to Dixon, Dixon stomping away on Pride. Pride back his feet, he comes up from behind with an eye rake, lifts Dixon up into a gorilla press! Pride locking on a spinning toe hold, but Dixon easily makes it to the ropes. [MP] - Like I said earlier, presence of mind, presence in the ring. [JC] - Pride whipping Dixon into the ropes, but it's reversed! Dixon catches Pride with a back body drop, lifts him up off the mat and drops him down in a slingshot suplex. [MP] - The Deadly Sins failing to work as a team here, Pride is good, but he can't fight off both Mason and Dixon for the whole match, they need tags! [JC] - Pride slowly making it to his feet, Dixon lining him up for something, guns of the south knockout punch! He hits it! One... two... kickout! [MP] - One of the most basic, if not the most basic finishers you'll ever see, but still fairly effective, just not enough to end the match [JC] - Dixon whips Pride into the ropes, smashes him with a clothesline! Dixon with a standing side headlock, but Pride easily reaches the ropes once again. Dixon fires Pride into the corner and unloads with a punch, another, oh hell about ten of them! [MP] - These boys certainly like to just dump all their aggression out into punches. Whatever works for you, I guess. [JC] - Dixon lands a heart punch! Going for the pin, Pride is out flat! Cover, 1... 2... and Sloth enters the ring and attacks Dixon! Mason enters the ring but Sloth tosses him out! Pride with Dixon over in the corner now... [MP] - Their setting him up for their finisher! [JC] - Pride with Dixon up on the top rope, Sloth climbing the ropes in the opposite corner! Pride off with a superplex, Sloth off with a high flying moonsault... the Repention and it's hit! [MP] - Stick a fork in Dixon, he's done, and Mason is out the outside. [JC] - And now General Lee distracting the referee, I guess he felt he had been dormant for long enough! Sloth going to attack General Lee, and Dixon comes up from behind and lands a belly to back suplex! [MP] - He thinks he's going to get a pin fall, but he isn't pinning the legal man! [JC] - Pride off the second rope with a legdrop! He rolls Dixon over, one... two... three! It's over! [TK] - Ladies and gentlemen, the winners of the match, in a time of 14:22, the Deadly Sins! [JC] - That's all for Eye of the Storm, folks! If you haven't done so already, call your local cable company and tell them you want the CWO's Saint Valentine's Day Massacre!
