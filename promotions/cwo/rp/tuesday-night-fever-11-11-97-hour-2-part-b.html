TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER 11/11/97 - HOUR #2 - PART B

12 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(When we come back, Tony the K is in the ring along with head referee John Myering.) TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall with a 45 minute time limit. It is a non-title affair. Introducing first, coming down the aisle.... ("Hero" by Ministry plays as the fans cheer the entrance of "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum and especially Dallas.) TONY THE K: Weighing 214 pounds, from Miami, FL, accompanied to the ring by Dallas, and representing the Riders on the Storm, "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum! (Fulcrum comes down slapping hands with the fans, as does Dallas, and gets in the ring.) TONY THE K: And his opponent.... ("Sweet Home Alabama" by Lynyrd Skynyrd plays as mixed reaction is heard at the entrance of Johnny Reb and his entourage.) TONY THE K: Weighing 237 pounds, from Mobile, AL, accompanied to the ring by General Lee and former CWO World Champion Rebel Yell, he is the current CWO World Champion, Johnny Reb! (Johnny Reb comes down slapping hands with those fans wearing his merchandise, as Rebel Yell carries the World Championship belt on his shoulder. General Lee follows behind, waving the Confederate flag proudly.) MELINN: Well, whether you like him or not, he is the World Champion! THOMAS: Damn straight! FERRARA: "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum made the challenge last week, as you saw earlier, based on what Rebel Yell did to his friend Diamondback Chris Myers at TERROR NIGHT '97. THOMAS: Oh what? You gonna cry? You gonna cry, little baby? Please. MELINN: Fulcrum a real high flyer, while Johnny Reb tends to stay on the mat, although he can come off the top rope like nobody's business as well. All right, bell has rang. (Johnny Reb plays to the crowd, getting a mixed reaction.) FERRARA: One thing you have to say, Johnny Reb has become very sure of himself here in 1997. THOMAS: That's how you win matches! Screw the fans! Not literally, judging by the appearance of most of them. MELINN: Knock it off, Thomas. Johnny Reb now on the attack, taking it right to "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum. Reb has Fulcrum up... running powerslam! FERRARA: Now that's unusual, Reb taking a power attack approach from the start. Usually he starts off slow, feeling his opponent out. THOMAS: He knows he can beat this loser whenever he wants, he might as well have some fun. MELINN: Reb stomping away on Fulcrum, and Dallas is up on the ring apron! THOMAS: Get her out of there! FERRARA: Oh, and I suppose the Mobile Mafia down with Reb is just fine? THOMAS: They're moral support! MELINN: Whatever... Fulcrum now back on his feet, he hits Reb with a forearm smash, but Reb comes back with a chop, and applies a chicken wing. FERRARA: And this is the Reb we're more used to seeing, very technical and methodical. MELINN: Reb lets go of the chicken wing, trying to switch into an arm wringer but Fulcrum counters into one of his own! Fulcrum going for an armbar but Johnny Reb counters it and goes right back to the arm wringer. THOMAS: Don't try to hang with the big boys, "Airborne Disease," you just can't cut it! MELINN: Good exchange there by both men a few minutes ago, and now Reb lets go of the arm wringer and whips Fulcrum into the ropes... Reb goes for a kick to the midsection but "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum grabs his foot... and Johnny Reb with a lightning fast enzuigiri! THOMAS: Ha ha! FERRARA: And you could hear the crack of Johnny Reb's foot meeting the skull of Mark Fulcrum right there. MELINN: Reb with an elbow drop, and now look at him showboating to the crowd! (Crowd goes nuts as Sandman Jr. makes his entrance, cane in one hand, Coke in the other, as CWO officials try to hold him back.) THOMAS: Uh oh... here comes trouble.... MELINN: Sandman Jr. coming down to confront Johnny Reb at ringside, and CWO officials are trying to hold him back! THOMAS: Hit them with the cane! (Johnny Reb motions for Sandman Jr. to "come on down." Sandman Jr. gets up on the ring apron. Johnny Reb grabs the mike.) JOHNNY REB: Listen, you piece of trash "garbage" wrestler, normally I'd be pissed having someone come down and disrupt my match, but I can beat this other piece of trash.... (He points to Fulcrum.) REB: Any time. November 23, 1997, prepare to see your "hard-core" wrestling go down the tubes when for the first time in your life you actually have to WRESTLE! Of course, I don't think you have it in you! What do you have to say to that, "little Sandman?" (Sandman Jr. grins, a weird look in his eyes. He shotguns the Coke, and then spits it into the face of Johnny Reb as the crowd cheers!) MELINN: Oh my! (Reb, furious, wipes the Coke off his face and slaps Sandman Jr. right in the face!) THOMAS: Uh... that's not good.... (Sandman Jr. hauls off and hits Reb with the Singapore cane! The CWO officials immediately drag him off the ring apron, trying to hold him back.) SANDMAN JR.: COME ON! COME ON! FERRARA: Johnny Reb has just been nailed by that Singapore cane! THOMAS: Why hasn't the ref called this match? MELINN: Ref letting this match continue, perhaps he should have disqualified "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum for Sandman Jr.'s interference, but he's letting it go. (Tom Krepps runs down to ringside.) TOM KREPPS (to Sandman Jr.): All right, get the hell out of here! Get the hell out of here, or I'll pull you out of the PPV! THOMAS: Do it! (Sandman Jr. hesitates, then begins to back off, holding the cane high as the crowd cheers.) MELINN: And "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum is taking advantage of the confusion, he's stomping away on Johnny Reb! THOMAS: This is ridiculous! The ref should have called this match! FERRARA: Not really fair to Fulcrum to end this match on a disqualification, it wasn't his fault that Sandman Jr. is out of control! THOMAS: Doesn't matter! Live by the rules, die by the rules. MELINN: Well, it's immaterial now, as Fulcrum hits Reb with a standing moonsault! Rebel Yell and General Lee screaming at the ref, John Myering ignoring them. (Fulcrum taunts Johnny Reb.) MELINN: Fulcrum advancing on Johnny Reb, and Johnny Reb rolls him up for the pin! THOMAS: Yes! MELINN: 1... 2... thr... no, Fulcrum reverses it! 1... kickout. FERRARA: Johnny Reb either playing possum or acting out of desperation, and he almost got Fulcrum right there. (Fulcrum has the legs of Johnny Reb split apart. He looks towards the crowd, who give their approval.) MELINN: Fulcrum... drives his head down into the midsection of Johnny Reb! THOMAS: Another disqualification! MELINN: Referee momentarily distracted by Dallas, that didn't help Johnny Reb's cause... and another standing moonsault by Fulcrum! Stomping away, another standing moonsault! Another one... and this time Johnny Reb gets his knees up! THOMAS: Ha ha! Fool me once, shame on me, fool me twice, shame on you! MELINN: Fulcrum doubled over in pain, but Johnny Reb is still trying to recover from being hit by Sandman Jr.'s Singapore cane moments earlier, and I'm now being told that CWO officials are trying to get Sandman Jr. out of the building, but he won't leave! THOMAS: What a jerk! FERRARA: Maybe Kellen can calm him down, because he certainly needs to do that if he wants to stay the #1 contender! MELINN: Johnny Reb going for an arm wringer but Fulcrum counters into one of his own. "AIRBORNE" MARK FULCRUM (to crowd): I'VE GOT HIM NOW! MELINN: Johnny Reb with a chop, stuns Fulcrum and gets out of the arm wringer. Going up top... Southern Cross... no! "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum moves out of the way! FERRARA: Fulcrum just too fast for Johnny Reb, he's been facing big men as of late, it has to be different for him going against a light heavyweight high flyer like "Airborne" Mark. MELINN: And now Fulcrum snaps on an armbar, he may be going for the submission win. THOMAS: If he gets a submission, I'll eat my T-shirt. MELINN: Boy would I love to see that.... Fulcrum now letting go of the hold.... FULCRUM (to Reb): COME ON! MELINN: Reb nailing Fulcrum with a double axhandle, and a Frankensteiner! THOMAS: Yes! MELINN: And now Johnny Reb is giving the signal, he's going for the Texas Cloverleaf! (Mixed reaction by the crowd.) MELINN: Johnny Reb going for the Cloverleaf... but Fulcrum quickly goes to the ropes. FERRARA: "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum saw that coming, he knew what trouble he was going to be in if Johnny Reb got that hold put on. MELINN: Johnny Reb waiting for Fulcrum to get back to his feet... and another Frankensteiner! And now he's going up top... Jackson's Revenge flying headbutt... no! Fulcrum moves out of the way! THOMAS: Not good, definitely not good. MELINN: "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum getting back to his feet, as is Johnny Reb, Fulcrum with a dropkick! And now he's going up top! "AIRBORNE" MARK FULCRUM: AIR FULCRUM COMING UP! THOMAS: Doesn't this idiot ever shut up? MELINN: Fulcrum... oh my goodness! 450( splash from the top, and he's going for the pin... oh no, General Lee distracting the referee! Come on! FERRARA: This has been a wild match so far, John Myering having a hard time keeping his mind on the match. THOMAS: Probably thinking of that vodka martini he's slipping into after tonight's event. MELINN: And Fulcrum has had enough, he's going after General Lee! Lee quickly getting off the ring apron... look out for Johnny Reb... and Fulcrum spins around and nails him right in the face! (Crowd pop as Fulcrum taunts Reb again.) MELINN: Johnny Reb back on his feet, still trying to fight back, elbow smash, kick to the midsection again caught by "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum... Reb going for the enzuigiri but this time Fulcrum ducks it! THOMAS: Wow, he learned something! MELINN: Fulcrum picks Reb up from the canvas, and nails him with a chop! FERRARA: Listen to the sound of that! MELINN: Reb is down, Fulcrum with a standing moonsault! And now he's going for an armbar but Johnny Reb counters it. Reb going up top... he jumps off... and Fulcrum with a jump kick! Wow! FERRARA: What timing! THOMAS: Come on, Reb! MELINN: Both men down on the mat after that one, both getting back to their feet... Fulcrum runs into the ropes... and Reb with a back body drop! Reb picks Fulcrum up... hits him with a kick... Fulcrum comes back with a kick of his own, a chop... Johnny Reb with a forearm smash... "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum with an elbow smash and Johnny Reb goes down. Cover, 1... kickout. FULCRUM (to Johnny Reb): COME ON! MELINN: Reb kicks Fulcrum in the midsection, goes up top... Southern Cross connects! FERRARA: But he didn't go for the cover, that's a mistake. MELINN: Reb measuring Fulcrum for an elbowdrop... and Fulcrum moves out of the way! THOMAS: Reb waited too long on that one, you can't give people opportunities like that. MELINN: And Fulcrum with the armbar, he's trying to break Johnny Reb's arm right off! FERRARA: Do you like ketchup or mustard on your T-shirt, "Truth?" THOMAS: Shut up! JOHNNY REB: AHHH! HE'S BREAKING MY ARM, REF, HE'S BREAKING MY ARM! REFEREE JOHN MYERING: Johnny Reb, do you want to give up? JOHNNY REB: AHHH! NO! SON OF A.... MELINN: Johnny Reb in a lot of pain, he's been in that armbar for over a minute now, but still he will not give up. THOMAS: Say what you want about him, he's not a quitter! MELINN: And Fulcrum now letting go of the hold, Reb holding his arm limply on the mat, Fulcrum going up top... Air Fulcrum! Forget about it! Mark Fulcrum with an opportunity to get a win over the World Champion, 1... 2... thr... no! Johnny Reb kicks out! He kicks out! THOMAS: How does he do it? FERRARA: Great resilience on the part of Johnny Reb, he has been Air Fulcrumed twice now, and still he will not give up.... MELINN: Wait a minute... Johnny Reb rolling out of the ring, still holding his arm. JOHNNY REB (to Rebel Yell and General Lee): All right, boys, that's it, we're out of here.... (Johnny Reb, Rebel Yell, and General Lee begin to leave.) MELINN: What the... that coward! THOMAS: Hey, that idiot Fulcrum almost broke his arm, the goof! Johnny Reb has to fight Sandman Jr. in Wembley Stadium in just a little more than a week! He can't take any chances! MELINN: Still, this is not the Johnny Reb we one knew... wait a minute... he's back! THOMAS: Oh, for crying out loud.... MELINN: Sandman Jr. is back, and he is not going to let Johnny Reb escape, but it's too late, the referee has reached a ten count! TONY THE K: Ladies and the gentlemen, the winner of this contest as a result of a countout in 10:55, "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum! (Decent pop from the crowd.) MELINN: Well, a hollow victory for Fulcrum, but that's not the story, as Sandman Jr. is going wild! He hits General Lee with the cane! He hits Rebel Yell with it! And now Johnny Reb... he gets hit by that Singapore cane again! FERRARA: Johnny Reb must feel like that kid in Singapore who was caught doing graffiti a couple of years ago. THOMAS: That is a dangerous weapon, and it should be impounded so that madman can't use it! (The lights go out as the crowd reacts.) MELINN: Oh no.... (Eerie sounds of the night followed by that intense eerie music plays, and the lights go on to reveal the Dark Side surrounding a confused Team Johnny Reb and Sandman Jr....) FERRARA: This may be an ambush! (The Dark Side attack Team Johnny Reb and Sandman Jr.!) MELINN: And The Dark Side are attacking both Johnny Reb and his entourage, AND Sandman Jr.! THOMAS: Get me out of here! MELINN: You stay put, Thomas... Kellen is on the scene now... wait a minute... Kellen took one look at Master of Darkness Damion, and she has collapsed to her knees! FERRARA: What the...? (Master of Darkness Damion walks up to Kellen, who is on her knees, and strokes her hair before Sandman Jr. nails him from behind.) MELINN: Master of Darkness Damion... I'm not sure what that was all about... but we have got a brawl here, as "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum has joined the fray! FERRARA: This is something, Johnny Reb and the man he just fought, along with the man he's going to fight at NOVEMBER PAIN '97, are now all fighting the Dark Side! MELINN: Wrestling certainly breeds strange bedfellows... and now security is on the scene, trying to separate these entities... folks, we're out of time, I'm Kyle Melinn, for Johnny Starr, Christine Ferrara, and David "The Truth" Thomas, we'll see you next week!
