CWO SHOWCASE SPECIAL: SPRING BREAK '97 3/16/97

25 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWO SHOWCASE SPECIAL: SPRING BREAK '97 "LIVE" FROM DAYTONA BEACH, FL 3/16/97 (CWO SHOWCASE opening comes up.) Kevin: Hello everybody, and welcome to CWO SHOWCASE: SPRING BREAK '97!!!! I'm Kevin Rinaldi, and with me is All-American Skip Vaughn!!! Skip: Hello, CWO!!! Kevin: And the action is hotter than the temperature outside!!! In our opener, Mason-Dixon knocks heads with Destroyer Davis and Inferno!!! Skip: An old established team against two men who've never worked together before!! Any bets? Kevin: Battlecat battles Bloodbath!!!! Skip: Alliteration is the last refuge of the feebleminded, Kevin..... Kevin: "Lonestar" Mike Williams tries to melt Steel down!!! Skip: The Williamses are tremendous assets to this league!!! Wonder if they have any more coming? Kevin: And in our final matchup, Da Hood Rats meet Mister Extreme and Brad The Man!!!! We'll be back!!! COMMMERCIAL BREAK Kevin: And we're back with our opener!!! Let's go to the ring!!! Tony the K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following tag team matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit!!! ("Livin' on the Edge" by Aerosmith plays as the fans boo) Tony the K: Coming down the aisle, at a combined weight of 540 pounds and accompanied by Jimbo Jones, David "The Destroyer" Davis and Inferno!!! ("Country Boy Can Survive" by Hank Williams, Jr. plays as the fans cheer) Tony the K: And their opponents, at a combined weight of 629 pounds and accompanied by General Lee, Stonewall Mason and Beauregard Dixon, the team of Mason-Dixon!!! Kevin: And Mason nails Davis with a series of heart punches!!! Davis is reeling! Mason snaps on a Boston crab!!! Davis diving for the ropes.....he makes it!! Mason into the ropes....but Davis nails him with a crescent kick!!! Davis climbing up top.....flying elbow!!!! Cover!!! 1..2..Mason gets the shoulder up!!! Skip: That's the best part of the CWO....the incredible parity and competition!!!! Kevin: Davis sends Mason into the ropes....dropkick!!!! Davis going for the STF...but Mason powers out of it!!!! Mason sends Davis into the ropes....and meets him with a boot to the face!!!! Skip: Not that it could make that face any worse!!! Kevin: Mason with a heart punch!!! Chop!!!! Mason going for a leg twist....but Davis counters out!!! Davis with a dropkick!!!! Another one!!!! Davis climbing up top...and nails the moonsault!!!! Cover!!! 1...2...thr....NO!!!! Mason gets the shoulder up!!! Skip: Dixon and Inferno are BOTH itching to get into this match!!! It's not fair for Mason and Davis to hog the action!!! Kevin: Davis has Mason up.....and sends him out of the ring!!! Davis going after him...and both Dixon and Inferno joining in!!!! Mason slams Davis into the ring apron!!! Mason climbs back into the ring.....Davis following him...and Mason nails him with a double axehandle!!! Mason tags Dixon!!!! They send Davis into the ropes.....going for a double backdrop.....but Davis nails them with a double noggin knocker!!!! Inferno coming into the ring!!!! Wild brawl!!!!! Mason and Dixon slam Davis and Inferno into each other!!! Skip: Yee-ha!!!! Kevin: Sorry, Skip, you can't play Southern!!! Davis tagging Inferno into the match....and Dixon dropkicks him right out of the ring!!! Dixon going after him!! Dixon has him up....vertical suplex onto the concrete!!!! Dixon rolling Inferno back into the ring....cover!!!! Jimbo Jones is on the ring apron!!! Dixon going after him....Inferno on his feet!! Inferno nails Dixon with a fisherman's suplex!!! Cover!!! 1...2..Dixon kicks out!!! Skip: Jimbo Jones is one of the most loathsome individuals in the CWO!!! Something really nasty should happen to him!!! Kevin: Inferno drops the elbow...no!!!! Dixon rolls out of the way!!! Dixon twisting Inferno's left leg, trying to get him to give......moving into a grapevine...but Inferno powers out!!! Inferno with a half nelson....and he's pulling the tights!!!! 1...2..th...NO! Dixon just barely kicked out of that one!!!! Skip: Inferno using any trick in the book to win this match!!! Kevin: Inferno has Dixon up.....power bomb!!!! Brings him up again.....standing bulldog!!!! Cover!!! 1....Dixon kicks out again!!! Skip: How much more can he take? He's got to get out of there!!! Kevin: Inferno going for the elbow drop....but he's tripped by General Lee!! Dixon tags Mason!!! They've got Inferno up....double face slam!!!! Mason straddling the prone Inferno, and he's raining down the punches!!!! Mason brings him up.....brainbuster!!!!! Mason brings him up.....sends him into the ropes....but Inferno catches him with a clothesline!!! Inferno drops the elbow!!! Skip: Dixon's entering the ring!!! Kevin: Dixon nails Inferno from behind!!! Blatant doubleteaming!!! They have Inferno up....Southern Spike!!!!! Cover!!! But Jimbo Jones distracts the ref!!! Mason goes after him!!!! Inferno slams Mason from behind!!! Cover!!! But Dixon runs in and pulls Inferno off of Mason!!! Skip: I hate to say it, but this will be a tainted victory for Mason-Dixon if they win. There was no reason for what just happened! Kevin: Inferno has Mason up.....he slams him into the corner!!! But Mason catches him on the way in!!! Mason has him in a bearhug......belly to belly suplex!!!! Inferno rolling out of the ring!!!! Mason tagging Dixon, and Inferno tagging in Davis!!! Dixon clotheslines Davis!!! Cover!!!! 1..2...thr... NO! Davis got the shoulder up!!! Skip: Davis landed on his head, he's hurt!!! Kevin: Dixon has him up.....HUGE bodyslam!!! Dixon has the legs....going for a Boston crab....but Davis powers out and sends Dixon headfirst into the buckle!!! Davis slams Dixon's head into the buckle again!!! Dixon is down!!! Cover!!! 1....2...thr...no, his foot's on the ropes, Dixon's foot is on the ropes!!! Skip: Davis should have brought him to center ring, he probably would've gotten the fall... Kevin: Davis tagging Inferno....Inferno going up top.....diving at Dixon headfirst...but Dixon moves!!!! Inferno crashes face first into the canvas!!!! Dixon tags in Mason!!! They bring Inferno up......double clothesline!!!! Mason stomps away on Inferno!!! He tags Dixon back in!!! They have Inferno up....double back suplex!!!! Dixon slaps on a chinlock, working Inferno's neck and back.....and Dixon tags in Mason!!! Skip: Classic tag team strategy....frequent tags to keep the guy off balance, and their own energy up!!! Kevin: Here comes Davis!!! But Mason and Dixon are ready for them!!! Mason-Dixon working over their opponents in the corners....and Inferno and Davis collide in center ring!!!! Mason tosses Davis out of the ring, then tags Dixon back in....Dixon with a Boston crab, exerting tremendous pressure on Inferno's lower back!!! Inferno reaching for the ropes......he made it! Dixon tags in Mason....they have Inferno up...double face slam!!! Mason tags Dixon....Dixon sends Inferno into the ropes...and hits the Guns of the South!!!! Cover!!!! 1...2....3!!!! It's over!!!! Tony the K: Ladies and gentlemen, your winners, in 22 minutes and 28 seconds, the team of MASON-DIXONNNNNNNNNNN!!!!!!! Kevin: The Southerners get the duke!!! And we'll be back after this!!! COMMERCIAL BREAK (Battlecat is shown in the interview area.) Battlecat: Bloodbath, you maybe the Phenomenon of the CWO but I am not going to roll over and play dead for you. I have the utmost respect for you but now you are taking on the Lord of the jungle and I will hunt you down and move up the rankings to the US title. I came here to the CWO to do one thing and that is to represent my country by winning the CWO North American Title and you my friend are in the way. SO get ready because I am going to do whatever it takes to beat you and advance in the ladder to the title. I have trained for years in the international rings and I am ready to prove to the CWO that I am good enough to beat anybody in this federation so Bloodbath you may be the Caretaker of the dead but when I am done with you it will be you that rests in peace. All I have to say to my fans out there is VIVA LUCH LIBRE CWO y VIVA MEXICO! Kevin: And we're back with Battlecat and Bloodbath!!!! Let's go to the ring!!! Tony the K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit!! (Battlecat's theme music plays as the fans boo) Tony the K: Coming down the aisle, weighing 247 pounds, from Hong Kong, Battlecat!!! ("Graveyard Symphony" plays as the fans cheer) Tony the K: And his opponent, weighing 328 pounds, from Death Valley, California, Bloodbath!! Kevin: Battlecat giving up tremendous weight to the man from Death Valley!!! But Battlecat takes the fight to him with a huge chop!!! Bloodbath responds with a HUGE double axehandle that sends Battlecat to the canvas!!! Skip: A good big man will always beat a good little man, Kevin!!! Kevin: Battlecat climbs back to his feet....and nails Bloodbath with a right hand! Battlecat dodges Bloodbath's answering swing!! Battlecat sends Bloodbath into the ropes.....wheel kick sends Bloodbath out of the ring!!! Battlecat going after him with a clothesline off the ring apron!!!! Battlecat rolls back into the ring...and Bloodbath rolling in after him....cover!!!! 1...2...thr...NO!! Bloodbath gets the shoulder up!!! Skip: Battlecat putting up a tremendous fight, but Bloodbath will win out in the end!!! Kevin: Battlecat climbing up top....going for a flying clothesline!!! But Bloodbath nails him in mid-air!!!! Bloodbath has him up....side suplex!!!! Cover!!! 1....Battlecat gets the shoulder up!!! Skip: Here we go!!! Kevin: Bloodbath drops the elbow!!!!! He's got a leg.......spinning toe hold....into a figure four!!!! Battlecat trying to turn over........he reversed it!!! Battlecat untangling himself.......going for a Spinning Wheel Kick....but Bloodbath dodges!!! Bloodbath slaps on a headlock.....Battlecat driving the forearm into Bloodbath's kidneys!!! Battlecat climbing up top.....flying elbow!!! Cover!!! 1...2...Bloodbath kicks out!!! Skip: He's hurt, though!!!! Those kidney shots took something out of him!! Kevin: Battlecat climbing up top....but misses the elbow!!!! Bloodbath has him up... and tosses him out of the ring!!!! Bloodbath going after him!! He's got a chair!!! But Battlecat grabs it away and nails him with it!!!! Battlecat rolling the bigger man into the ring.....cover!!!! 1...2...thr....Bloodbath kicks out!!! Skip: Battlecat pulling out all the stops on this one!!! He wants this win!!! Kevin: Battlecat up on top.....double stomp!!!! The Cat going up top....but this time Bloodbath catches him with a fist to the gut!!! Bloodbath going up top...but Battlecat grabs him and slams him to the mat!!! Skip: You'd think these two would stop with the turnbuckles already.... Kevin: Battlecat sends Bloodbath into the corner!!!!! And sends him across to the other corner!!!!! Battlecat nails Bloodbath with a clothesline!!! Sends him into the corner....and nails Bloodbath with an elbow that sends Bloodbath to the floor!!!! Battlecat going after him.....but Bloodbath already climbing back into the ring....Battlecat catches him with a forearm on the way in!!! Battlecat sends him into the ropes......elbowsmash!!!! Going for a Spinning Wheel Kick....he got it!!! Cover!!!! 1....2...thr.....NO!!! Bloodbath just managed to get the shoulder up!!! Skip: Battlecat on a roll.....Bloodbath not managing much of an offense!!! Kevin: Battlecat with an elbow!!!! Battlecat has him up....Arabian face buster!!!! Cover!!! 1...2...thr...Bloodbath kicks out!!!! Skip: I take back what I said.......the little man is kicking the stuffing out of the big man!!!! Kevin: Battlecat climbs up top.....flying clothesline!!! He brings Bloodbath up.....Spinning Wheel Kick!!! Cover!!!! 1...2....3!!!!!!!!!!! Tony the K: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner, in 16 minutes and 59 seconds, BATTLECAT!!!! Kevin: And the fans not happy with the result, but Battlecat was just the better wrestler!!! We'll be back!!!!! COMMERCIAL BREAK Kevin: And we're back with Mike Williams and Steel!!! Let's go to the ring!! Tony the K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit!!! ("Kalifornia Love" by 2Pac and Dr. Dre plays as the fans boo) Tony the K: Coming down the aisle, weighing 265 pounds, from Compton, California and accompanied by Da Boss, Steel!!!! ("Walk Like a Camel" by Southern Culture on the Skids plays as the fans cheer) Tony the K: And his opponent, weighing 234 pounds, from New Braunfels, Texas, "Lonestar" Mike Williams!!!! Kevin: And Williams goes on the attack!!!! Inside cradle.....1...2...Steel gets the shoulder up!!!! Skip: Mike Williams is so technically solid, he's one of the most dangerous men in the CWO!!! Kevin: Williams going for a sleeperhold....but Steel powers out!!! Steel sends Williams into the ropes....flying clothesline!!! Steel stomping away on Williams!!! Williams fighting back!!! Williams sends Steel into the corner.....but Steel avoids the follow-up splash!!! Steel sets Williams up on the buckle.....superplex!!!! But Steel's not going for the cover!!! Skip: That's a mistake!!!! The competition in this league is so fierce, you never know when someone'll reverse you!!! Go for the win!!! Kevin: Steel climbing up top....flying bodypress!!!! Cover!!! 1....Williams kicks out!!! Skip: Well, like I said, go for the win!!!! Kevin: Steel sends Williams into the ropes....shoulder tackle!!! Steel snapping on The Gank...but Williams not giving in!!! Steel releases the hold....climbs up top...but misses the headbutt!!!!! Williams with an inside cradle!!! 1.....Steel kicks out!!! Skip: Go, Lonestar!!! Kevin: Williams with a chop!!!! He has Steel up.....pump-handle powerbomb!!! Williams going for the Lonestar Press....he got it!!!! Cover!!! 1....2...thr... NO! Steel just got the shoulder off the mat!!! Skip: Lonestar's on a roll now!!! Kevin: Williams with a full nelson....but Steel nails him with an elbowsmash!!! Steel with a shoulder tackle!!! Another one!!! Steel into the ropes...legdrop!!! Steel climbing up top.....going for a headbutt!!!! But Williams brings his foot up!!!!!! Steel took that one right on the jaw!!!! Williams covers!!! 1...2... thr.....Steel gets the shoulder up!!!! Skip: Williams showing tremendous presence of mind!!! Steel got greedy, and it cost him!!! Kevin: Williams with a sleeperhold!!!! Steel trying to fight free, but Williams has it on tight!!!! The ref raising Steel's arm, trying to see if he's out!!! Steel lunges for the ropes....and just barely makes it!!!! Williams sends him into the ropes....dropkick!!!! Williams climbs up top....flying clothesline!!! And Steel takes to the hills!!! Skip: Williams not going after him, though!!! Kevin: The referee counting....8.....Steel climbing back into the ring....and Williams stomping on him as he gets back in!!!! Steel fighting back with a headlock!!!! Steel sends Williams into the ropes....cross body block!!!! Steel drops the elbow!!!! And Steel stomping on Williams' knee!!! Steel brings him up.....fireman's carry!!!! Cover!!!! 1...2...thr... NO!!! Williams just managed to kick out of that one!!! Skip: A miracle, Kevin!!! Kevin: Steel going for a half nelson...but Williams twists out of it!!! Williams nails Steel with a right hand!!! Williams brings Steel up.....inside cradle!!!! 1...2....3!!!! He got him!!!!! Tony the K: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner in 14 minutes and 42 seconds, LONESTAR MIKE WILLIAMS!!!!!!! Kevin: Lonestar pulls out the victory!!!! We'll be back!!! COMMERCIAL BREAK (The Masters of Hardcore come into their locker room. They don't look too pleased, but still have an air of contentment about them.) "Mr. Extreme" Chris Ohlhoff: Well, we worked our asses off out there, plus we got to hurt some people. The outcome of the match doesn't really matter. As long as we're satisfied with the beatings that we gave those f**kers. Brad "The Man" Couchman: Yeah, that's right. But, there's been some problems that we've had as of late. Care to explain them, Brian? "Beautiful" Brian Clark: Sure, why the hell not. Last Tuesday, there was an incident on FEVER. After the Highlanders got a cheap win over us, these mystic freaks known as the Eternal Wanderers came down and wanted to help us lay beatings on the Highlanders. Now, as far as we know, you guys don't like the Highlanders. Well, my boys here don't like ANYBODY. And further more, they, as well as I, get EXTREMELY pissed off when people stick their noses in our business. If you don't like the Highlanders, fine, but try to be more considerate of others. Now, you have my boys ALL riled up over this. "Mr. Extreme": That's right. Exile, Exodus and Lord Guru, you little b*stards are not ready to handle a team of our nature. You may be good in the ring, but as soon as the match goes to the floor, it's f**king ours. There is no escape when we're on the floor. Now, don't think for a second that we can't handle it in the ring. Both Brad and I have a repertoire of over 60 maneuvers. WE know exactly what we're doing both inside that ring and out. We just enjoy the outside part a whole lot better. "The Man": That's right. We are here to stay, whether you like it or not. Now, all that's left is for us to get some gold. "Beautiful": Don't worry about that one. Leave it all up to me. I'll be able to get you one of those soon enough. All in due time. "Mr. Extreme": See, we know where we're going. But, does anyone else have a clue what we're all about. Let's just wait and see. (They literally throw the cameraman out of the room, and slam the door shut behind him.) Kevin: And we're back with our final match of the night!!!! Da Hood Rats take on Ohlhoff and Couchman!!! Let's go to the ring!!!! Tony the K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following tag team matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit. ("Bow Down" by Westside Connection plays as the fans boo) Tony the K: Coming down the aisle, at a combined weight of 679 pounds and accompanied by T.A.S., Warren and Nate, Da Hood Rats!!! ("Ball Tongue" by KORN plays as the fans keep on booing) Tony the K: And their opponents, at a combined weight of 486 pounds and accompanied by Beautiful Brian Clark, "Mister Extreme" Chris Ohlhoff and Brad "The Man" Couchman!!!! Kevin: Ohlhoff and Nate starting the match.....they circle.....they tie up!! Ohlhoff backing Nate into the ropes....the ref calling for the break...but Ohlhoff chops Nate across the chest!!! Nate responds with a right hand!!! Nate sends Ohlhoff into the corner....but misses the avalanche!!!! And Ohlhoff slaps Nate across the face!!! Skip: Sportsmanship isn't something these guys care much about, that's for sure!!! Kevin: Ohlhoff sends Nate into the corner.....but runs right into an elbow!!! Nate tags in Warren.....Warren sends Ohlhoff into the corner....but misses the leapfrog!!!! Ohlhoff snaps on a scorpion.....but they're too close to the ropes, Warren reaches them easily. Ohlhoff goes for a figure four sleeper...but Warren reverses!!!!! And this time it's OHLOFF reaching for the ropes!!! Skip: These guys need to brush up their ring generalship....they're making rookie mistakes all over the place!!! Kevin: Ohlhoff tags in Couchman....Couchman with a figure four sleeper!!!! Warren desperately trying to reach the ropes....the referee lifting Warren's arm, seeing if he's out.....but Warren makes one final lunge to force the break!!! Fans: Boring, boring, boooring!!!!! Kevin: The fans not liking the action so far....Couchman dragging Warren to his team's corner....Couchman tags in Ohlhoff....they have Warren up....double faceslam!!!! Nate coming to his partner's rescue, but Couchman going after him!! Couchman and Ohlhoff slam Da Hood Rats into each other!!!!! The referee getting Couchman and Nate out of the ring.....Ohlhoff has Warren in a figure four leglock!! Warren reaching the ropes....but Ohlhoff nails him with a Dragon Suplex!!! Cover!!!! Wait....T.A.S. is on the ring apron....the ref trying to get rid of him instead of counting the pin!!!! Skip: Managers should be banned from ringside!!! The interference is just getting out of hand!!! Kevin: Ohlhoff has Warren up....and tosses him out of the ring!!! Ohlhoff going after him!!!! Ohlhoff has a chair....but Warren grabs it and shatters it over Ohlhoff's head!!!!! Warren rolls Ohlhoff into the ring....cover!!! 1...2...thr... NO! Ohlhoff just manages to kick out!!! Fans: YEAH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Kevin: The fans getting into the match!!! Warren tags in Nate....Nate nails Ohlhoff with a chop right to the throat!!! Ohlhoff in pain!!!! Nate brings him up....and stoops to a low kick!!!! Skip: These guys are animals!!!! Worse than animals, not even a rabid pig would behave like that!!! Kevin: Double axehandle by Nate!!! Elbowsmash!!! Nate has Ohlhoff by the hair...and sends him to the floor!!!! Nate tagging in Warren....Warren going after Ohlhoff... but Ohlhoff nails Warren with a chair!!! Ohlhoff going for a vertical suplex....but Warren reverses it!!!!!!! Warren rolling back into the ring....and tags his partner!!! Skip: That was a pretty pointless shift for Warren...he was in for barely any time at all!!!! Kevin: Ohlhoff climbing back into the ring....Nate slams him into Da Hood Rats' corner!!!! Warren and Nate working over Ohlhoff in the corner!!!! Nate going for the Gangsta Drop....he got it!!!! Cover!!!! Wait....Beautiful Brian Clark is on the apron!!! The referee trying to get rid of the manager....but meanwhile, the match goes on!!! Nate sending Ohlhoff into the corner....avalanche!!!!! Nate stomping away on Ohlhoff!!!! Nate going for another Gangsta Press...he got it!!! Cover!!! Couchman trying to make the save, but Warren's right there....1...2.. Ohlhoff kicks out!!! Skip: Lack of trust by Couchman there...Ohlhoff obviously had enough to get out himself!! Kevin: Nate tagging in Warren....Warren misses the dropkick!!! Ohlhoff tagging in Couchman!!!! They have Warren up......double faceslam!!!! Couchman going up top......but Warren shakes the ropes, and Couchman lands hard!!!! Warren tags in Nate....but Couchman nails Nate with a clothesline!!! Couchman tags in Ohlhoff....they send Nate into the ropes....double clothesline!!! Warren coming in to help his partner....four-way brawl!!!!! Skip: And the referee doesn't know where to look!!! Kevin: Nate and Warren pounding on Ohlhoff and Couchman....and they ram their heads together!!!!! The referee driving Nate and Ohlhoff out of the ring....Warren applying a sharpshooter!!!! Couchman trying to escape, but Warren has it on tight!!!! Warren releasing the hold....and tosses Couchman out of the ring!!! Warren climbing out onto the apron.....but misses with the flying clothesline!!! Skip: I've never understood why guys go outside....you can't win the match out there!!! Kevin: Both men climbing back into the ring.....Warren tagging in Nate....and they nail Couchman with a double bodyslam!!!! Nate tagging Warren back in...and Warren playing to the crowd instead of finishing Couchman!!!! Skip: That could be a mistake!!!! Kevin: Couchman is slowly climbing to his feet.....he nails Warren in the back!!! Couchman has him up.....piledriver!!!! Cover...and Couchman's using the ropes for leverage!!!! Nate trying to save his partner, but Ohlhoff nails him with a huge right!!! 1....2....3!!!!!!! Tony the K: Ladies and gentlemen, your winners, in 20 minutes and 10 seconds, "MISTER EXTREME" CHRIS OHLOFF AND BRAD "THE MAN" COUCHMAN!!!!!!!! Kevin: That's all for tonight, folks, we hope you enjoyed CWO SHOWCASE: SPRING BREAK '97, we'll see you next time! (Closing creds and music as we fade out.)
