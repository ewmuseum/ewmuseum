SUNDAY SHOWCASE 9/21/97 - HOUR #2

21 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWO SUNDAY SHOWCASE - HOUR #2 9/21/97 CWO Arena Producer: Steve White ( [detail withheld] ) Executive Producer: Brent G. Alles ( [detail withheld] ) (Fireworks light up the arena as the camera pans from the sea of CWO fans to the announcers' booth.) MIKE WILIAMS: Hello, ladies and gentlemen! This is Mike Williams, along with my colleague 'Dr. Doom' Josh Miller of the AAWA, welcoming you to CWO Sunday Showcase! We've got one heck of a card lined up tonight. We should let you know, though, that during the commercial break between hour #1 and hour #2, there was an altercation in the locker room between The Focus, Diamondback Chris Myers, and Sandman Jr. with Benjamin Blake Bentley, the Reverend, and his dark minion Sandman. That only serves to heat up the 6-man match that you will see next week on TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER. Josh, what do you think of this crowd, huh? JOSH MILLER (looking disgustedly at the fans): Well, Mike... it's as good as you can expect from *this* league... MW: League affiliation aside, we've got two great lightweights in action in the form of Sandman Jr. versus Carlos Maia, The Pyro-Technics take on the Coldfront, and the light heavyweight title is up for grabs when Jason 'Flash' Varga defends his strap against Wildfire! Once again, we thank the CWO for allowing us to man the helm of Showcase this week. JM (still looking nervously at the fans): Ya know what Mike... I'm transferring out of *any* job opps you sign me up for. This is too much. *Carlos Maia vs. Sandman Jr.* CHAD JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. Coming down the aisle... ('You Give Love a Bad Name' by Bon Jovi plays as the fans boo.) CHAD JOHNSON: Weighing 235 pounds, from Rio de Janeiro. Brazil, 'The Brazilian Lover' Carlos Maia! (Maia comes to the ring egging on guys and trying his damnedest to flirt with *any* women he can before leaping into the ring and playing to the crowd. He finally settles on a very explosive black woman and plants a big kiss on her.) MW: The man is narcissism incarnate. We got comments with "The Brazilian Lover" before the match, let's go to them now. (The scene opens in aroom, with a dim light and a bed. Over the bed are three beautiful women surrounding "The Brazilian Lover" Carlos Maia.) "THE BRAZILIAN LOVER" CARLOSMAIA: Well, well, tonight I face this small version of Sandman... I should ask, why does Sandman still permit that this inferior version keeps walking in this fed? If I was him, I should put him in a wheelchair for stealing my name... but anyways, Sandman, my present to you: a beating that Sandman JR. will remeber for a long time... IF he recovers from it! HAHAHAHA! (He pauses and kiss one of the women.) MAIA: Oh, and Tuesday I will face Amish Farmer... what is it, a country fed? After our match, Mr. Farmer you will return to your rightful place... THE MOOD! (The women caress Maia's chest as the camera fades. Back to the ring.) CHAD JOHNSON: And his opponent... ('Enter Sandman' by Mighty Mighty Bosstones plays as the fans cheer.) CHAD JOHNSON: Weighing 235 pounds, from Philadelphia. PA, accompanied by Kellen, Sandman Jr.! (Sandman Jr. Comes out to a huge pop as he struts maniacally down the aisle chugging a Coke.) MW: The fans love this man! JM: Simple minds, simple pleasures. MW: What exactly is that supposed to mean? JM: Figure it out. (Bell rings as Maia dropkicks Sandman to the mat and hooks the leg.) MW: Maia getting right down to business here! 1! .. .. .. 2! .. .. .. Shoulder up! JM: And these are two of the most impressive guys they have around here? Hah. MW: Maia with a whip to the near ropes...and a big spinning leg lariat lays out Junior! Cover by Maia! 1! .. .. .. 2! .. .. .. 3! Shoulder up! (Maia gets to his feet and flicks sweat from his brow on the beaten down Junior.) MW: Maia up top... and a dazed Sandman Junior catches a flying bodypress squarely in the chest! (Maia hooks the leg as Kellen jumps up on the apron distracting the ref, and causing Maia to release the cover and try to get rid of her.) JM: Get that mutt down! See Mike!? No woman can be trusted around this man! MW: A sure win by Maia has been averted thanks to the actions of Kellen, and Carlos is furious! (Maia berates Kellen near the ropes as Sandman charges in from behind.) MW: Sandman with a head of steam... and Maia whips around with a right hook! JM: Nice catch! He should be known as the 'Deion of Pro-Wrestling'. MW: Will you stop. JM: Hey. Speaking of football. Did ya hear about the big trade? William 'The Refrigerator' Perry has been traded for Emmett Smith. The deal's been titled 'A Fridge for a Coke machine'. (JM laughs maniacally) MW: You're horrible... (Maia taunts a groggy Sandman before whipping him to the ropes.) MW: Maia with a whip...and a beautiful rana! You don't see that every day! (Maia goes to the top and waits for Sandman to get to his feet.) MW: Maia with a flying double axhandle on Sandman, and a series of stomps keeps the little guy down! Maia from the second rope...flying kneedrop! NO! Sandman rolls out of the way! (Sandman Jr. Climbs to the top and yells to the crowd) SANDMAN JR.: YOOOOOOOOOO! JM: Nooo! MW: PERMANENT DREAM! 1! .. .. .. 2! .. .. .. 3! Shoulder up! Amazingly enough, Maia able to recover from that devastating guillotine legdrop and get his shoulder up at the very last second. JM: Maia's probably one of the very few men you can't underestimate in the CWO, Mike. MW: Forearm smash by Maia and a big backdrop! Failed swinging punch by Sandman Jr. And a Hurricanrana lays him out! (Sandman gets up from the blow only to be taken down by a backslide.) MW: 1! .. .. Kickout! Pickup by the Brazilian Lover...whip into a kneelift, and a frogsplash ends it all! JM: I'm really starting to take a shining to this Maia kid. MW: Maia up top! Somersault bodyblock! JM: Wow! (Maia signals for another bodyblock, but Kellen jumps on the apron and once again grabs his attention.) JM: There's that mutt again.. MW: Kellen back in the mix, and Sandman nails the 'Lover' with an elbow to the back of the cranium! Sandman with a legclip to a dizzies Maia... and applies a Boston Crab! (Sandman rocks back and forth wrenching on the ankles of Maia and grinding his weight into his back before releasing the hold and setting in an STF.) MW: Sandman with a quick series of submissions, and Maia's just as quick to reach the ropes. Release by Sandman and an elbow to the back! Headscissors by Junior, and another quick escape by Maia! (Maia kips up to his feet and raises his fists triumphantly.) JM: That's it, Carlos! Show that brat who's boss! MW: Sandman catching Maia in mid-celebration with an Irish Whip... Frankensteiner! Whip by Sandman... another Frankensteiner, and Maia's up and over the ropes! (Sandman plays to the crowd before launching himself with a slingshot plancha to the outside.) MW: Sandman with a suicidal dive onto Maia! And now the wild one's going for the time-keeper's chair! JM: Man. That job's gotta suck. Every fed I go to, he's the one that always ends up standing. (Sandman Junior charges in to Maia with the chair, but Maia darts out of the way and slams Sandman's head into the railing.) MW: Both men just tearing each other up out on the tarmac... and the ref's throwing this one out! CHAD JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen...due to out-of-ring time.the referee has declared this match a DOUBLE DISQUALIFICATION! (Crowd boos as Maia clips the leg of Sandman Jr.) MW: A less than great end to a fantastic match here tonight. JM: Aw c'mon Mike! I think seeing that brain dead skater-schmuck getting put out to pasture is *more* than great! MW: Wait a minute, that woman that Maia kissed before the match... she's up against the guardrail... she just handed him something! JM: I think it's brass knuckles! MW: And Maia lays out Sandman Jr. with those brass knuckles! (Maia grabs the black woman in his arms and heads towards the back.) MW: What is the meaning of this?!? Folks, we have to go to a commercial, stay tuned, we'll try to keep you posted! (Scene fades to commercial as we see Sandman Jr. being assisted backstage by Kellen.) *The Pyro-Technics vs. The Coldfront* CHAD JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the following tag team matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit. Coming down the aisle... (TNT by AC/DC plays as the fans boo.) CHAD JOHNSON: At a combined weight of 655 pounds, accompanied by Holly Rockets, Detonator, C-4, Pyro-Technics! MW: The Pyro-Technics are relative newcomers to the CWO, and they have a chance to make a mark here by defeating the veterans... The ColdFront. MW: Is 'veteran' a nice way of saying 'mid-card loser'? CHAD JOHNSON: And their opponents... ('More Human Than Human' By White Zombie plays as the fans boo White and cheer Gerstung.) CHAD JOHNSON: At a combined weight of 407 pounds, Kurt Gerstung, Steve White, Coldfront! MW: We've seen these men in action before Josh. They've got all the talent it takes, but that doesn't seem to stop them from losing. JM: Maybe because...they were born losers? (Bell rings as Kurt Gerstung stands atop the top turnbuckle and leaps off.) MW: Gerstung with a Flying leg lariat off the bat! Another, and a handspring moonsault! JM: Just look at that little sucker fly! (Gerstung rolls over to White and makes the tag.) MW: Tag to White... and a Scorpion deathlock by the founder of the AAWA keeps C-4 off of his feet and on the mat. Not a place you want to be with him. (C-4 extends his legs, countering the hold, and tags to Detonator.) MW: C-4 with a slick reversal and a tag to Detonator! Double bodyslam by the Pyro-Technics! JM: Wow! What power! I never knew a head could bounce like that! (Detonator plays to the crowd as C-4 gets back to his corner.) MW: Detonator making his way over to White, who may be out cold... (White rolls Detonator up and grabs a fistful of tights.) MW: Rollup by White! 1! .. .. .. 2! .. .. ..Thr. No. The ref breaking the count after seeing the pull of the tights. JM: I see corrupt ref's are not a rare thing in this sport. MW: White with a whip to the corner.and Detonator bounces back with a hiptoss! Forearm smash by White! Whip by White... and spinning headscissors take the big man off his feet! (White plays to the booing crowd and kicks Detonator in the gut.) MW: Kick to the midsection by White! Double leg cradle! 1! .. .. .. 2! .. .. .. Thr. No. It seems that White, again, had a handful of tights. (White argues irately with the ref and finally gives up fuming.) MW: White with the dazed Detonator..and a whip to the corn..no..a self-reversal by White, and Detonator slams into the ref! (White stands there playing to the crowd as if he's shocked at first, but begins to grin.) JM: Hahahaha! I love it! MW: White with a blatant attack on the ref. JM: Prove it! MW: And a whip to the corner! (White gets atop the buckle, wraps his leg around Detonator's head from the rear, and leaps off.) JM: DEEP FREEZE! MW: That devastating top-rope legdrop-facebuster variation finds it's mark here, but it won't do too much good with the ref out! (White tags to Gerstung who lands a double-stomp to the knee from the second buckle.) MW: Gerstung up top once more... Moonsault! AND A BIG MISS! Tag to C-4 by Detonator! (The ref slowly gets to his feet as Gerstung reverses a single-leg takedown.) MW: C-4 with a whip to the ropes... and Gerstung comes back with an Icepick DDT! JM: That's the move that he used to put away countless victims in his earlier days. (Gerstung leaps to the top buckle and lands a Frankensteiner from the top.) MW: Beautiful Frankensteiner, and a tag to White! Cover! 1! .. .. .. Kickout with authority! JM: So far these Pyro-Technics have fizzled. MW: White with a standing arm-scissors, and an elbow to the face breaks the hold. White retaliates with a kick to the gut and a whip.reversal! (White bounces violently from the corner as C-4 plows through him with a football tackle.) MW: Good God! C-4 literally exploding into White, and following up with a cover! (Gerstung flies over the ropes to break the pin as The Coldfront becomes overpowered by The Pyro-Technics.) MW: Gerstung flying into the ring to make the save, and flies back out via Detonator Express! JM: Hah. The man does love to fly. MW: Clawhold applied by C-4...Tag to Detonator, and the two big men are just beating the life out of White! Whip by Detonator! (Detonator tries to land a palm blow as White slings off the ropes, misses and White lands a crucifix, but Detonator gets out before the 1 count.) MW: Detonator on his feet... and a big chop sends White to the mat! Whip to the corner by the big man. Avalanche! JM: That's a whole lotta flyin' hurtin', Mike. DETONATOR (to crowd): IT'S OVER! MW: Cover by Detonator. 1! .. .. .. 2! .. .. .. 3! NO! Shoulder barely up! JM: Amazing! (Detonator frustratingly picks up White and unceremoniously dumps him over the ropes.) MW: Detonator obviously frustrated with White.just stomping away on him as he attempts to crawl back in the ring. (Detonator signals to C-4 who climbs into the ring as Detonator climbs to the top.) MW: C-4 with White in a backbreaker position... and Detonator takes off with a flying double-stomp into the chest! JM: THE EXPLOSION! THIS ONE'S OVER! (As White crashes to the mat, his leg catches on the ropes.) MW: Cover by C-4! 1! .. .. .. 2! .. .. .. The ref sees White's foot on the rope! JM: I'll be. MW: C-4 with a Backward bridge reversal by White and a tag to Gerstung! (As Gerstung gets in the ring, Detonator enters and both teams brawl.) MW: C-4 and Detonator with both members of the Coldfront...And they whip em into each other! JM: Am I the only one that heard that satisfying coconut sound?! MW: Headscissors by C-4.escape by Gerstung. C-4 with a tag to Detonator and a double back suplex to Gerstung! (Detonator plays it up as Gerstung clambers to his feet behind Detonator.) MW: Gerstung on the second buckle... flying leg lariat and Detonator's still up! Elbow smash to the chest of the bigger man and another leg lariat! Tag to White! JM: Hasn't this schmuck been beaten down enough?! (White whips Detonator to the corner, and attempts it again.) MW: Whip reversed by Detonator, and Detonator charges in! Leapfrog by White and a rollup! 1! .. .. .. 2! .. .. .. Thr! Shoulder up and a whip to the ropes...tossup into a stun gun! JM: That'll sting tomorrow. MW: Cover! 1! .. .. .. 2! .. .. .. 3! And just like that, The Coldfront takes home a victory here tonight, on Sunday Showcase, right out of the jaws of near defeat. CHAD JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen... your winners in 12:03... Steve White... Kurt Gerstung... THE... COOOOOOLDFROOOOOONT! ('Hey Man Nice Shot' by Filter plays to a mixed reaction. Bell rings.) MW: White's refusing to let off of C-4! White up top... elbowdrop! Another! White with the Indian Deathlock applied on a prone C-4 and Detonator meets Gerstung with a king-size clothesline! JM: Now *this* is entertainment! (Bell rings again as the scene fades to commercial.) *Jason 'Flash' Varga vs. Wildfire* CHAD JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. It is for the CWO Light Heavyweight Champion title. Coming down the aisle... ('My Sherona' by the Knack plays as the fans cheer.) CHAD JOHNSON: Weighing 232 pounds, from Venice Beach. CA, Wildfire! MW: Wildfire's definitely in the top-draw as far as Lightweights go in this sport, but you kind of have to question just how much of a chance he stands against the more than impressive Varga. JM: The wild fruit and the flashy fruit. They're fightin' over a title! Man. I'm gonna hate workin' here. CHAD JOHNSON: And his opponent... ('Fanfare for the Common Man' by Aaron Copland plays as the fans cheer.) CHAD JOHNSON: Weighing 235 pounds, from Jordan. NY, accompanied by Jim Peer, the CWO Light Heavyweight Champion, Jason 'Flash' Varga! (Varga runs to the ring slapping hands. Once in the ring he does a little hip-gyration dance to the crowd.) JM: (Chanting to Varga) FROOOOOTCAAAAAKE! FROOOOOOOTCAAAAAKE! MW: Will you stop? (Bell rings.) MW: Varga starting things off with an armbar... escape by Wildfire and a whip. Scoop slam and Varga goes to the outside to collect his thoughts. JM: Yeah. All three of em. (Wildfire goes to the outside, opposite of Varga, picks up the chair used against Maia earlier that night and swings.) MW: Wildfire with the steel chair! And Varga with a receiving forehead! JM: Oh my God! Is the chair all right?! (Wildfire rolls back in the ring, and stomps away on Varga when he attempts to get in.) MW: Wildfire showing quite a mean streak in there. Wildfire with a whip... clothesline by Varga and Wildfire's down! JM: He musta spun a good 180 there, Mike! MW: Varga up top! (Wildfire staggers over to the corner, shakes the ropes, causing Varga to land in the nether- regions.) MW: Varga obviously in serious pain from that spill. JM: Awww c'mon! The man's fakin'! How can ya hurt what ya can't see? MW: Wildfire with a right hook and a whip to the corner.handspring elbow! Great move! JM: Flashy, but is it effective? I doubt it. MW: Headlock applied, and a quick escape by Varga, followed up by a chop. (Crowd 'WHOOS!' with each following chop.) MW: Varga up top... flying crossbody press! (Varga slaps Wildfire around while he lies prone on the mat before applying a double reverse chinlock.) MW: Varga with a release and a stomp to Wildfire... Varga making his way up top... (Miss Amber distracts Varga, allowing Wildfire to nail him in the gut.) MW: Outside interference galore here tonight folks, and Wildfire takes Varga for the ride! (Wildfire unleashes a devastating Superplex on Varga.) MW: SUPERPLEX BY THE CHALLENGER! JM: I never knew so little weight could make the ring rattle like that! (At the end of the Superplex, Varga keeps his momentum and ends up rolling onto Wildfire for the cover!) MW: Inadvertent cover by Varga! 1! .. .. .. 2! .. .. .. Thr! Shoulder up! JM: I think Wildfire knocked the crap outta himself with that one! (Varga applies an armbar, only to release it quickly and climb up to the top.) MW: Varga back up top... and again Wildfire shakes the ropes, this time making Varga land squarely in the ring! (Wildfire taunts Varga, who retaliates with a headlock into an Irish Whip.) MW: Whip by the champ... and a spinning elbow finds it's mark! Flying cross body press! Varga back up top... elbowdrop! NO! Wildfire to quick, and Varga eats the mat! JM: Hah. Splat. MW: Varga back to his feet. Kick to the midsection. Faceslam! JM: Thank God for rhinoplasti. MW: Varga with a Figure-4 and another fast release... pickup. Small package by Varga! 1! .. .. ..2! .. .. .. 3! NO! JM: These little guys sure can take a lickin', eh Mike? MW: Indeed... Wildfire with a whip to the corner and Varga bounces back with a spinning elbow! Varga up top! FLASH FLOOD! JM: Why the hell does he shake his butt like that?! MW: 1! .. .. .. 2! .. .. .. Thr! Shoulder up! Amazing! JM: Flying fruit. Who'da thunk it. (Varga applies a headlock to the winded Wildfire and keeps it applied.) MW: A simple yet effective weardown move being used by Varga. JM: I'll give him credit. The fans like flying fruity flips and all, but it takes something more to win. (Varga releases the hold and whips Wildfire to the ropes.) MW: Irish Whip by the champion... double dropkick! (The referee begins to count to 10 as both men struggle to get up.) MW: Varga back to his feet... and a stomp to the chest of the ascending Wildfire keeps his opponent down! Figure-4 applied by the champ, and the crowd here loves it! (Varga taunts Wildfire as he wrenches away on the lock. Wildfire slowly begins to rock side to side in order to reverse the hold.) MW: Wildfire flipping Varga over for the reversal, and a release by the champ sets Wildfire loose! (Wildfire starts to beat the literal life out of Varga. An Irish whip sends the champ to the corner with a thunderous impact as Wildfire flies in with a splash, followed up by repeated headslams and punches.) JM: The man's going psychotic! MW: Pardon the pun, but Wildfire's on fire here tonight folks! Wildfire with both arms hooked, and an underhook piledriver! Pickup..another! NO! Backdrop by Varga! (Varga staggers to stay on his feet and starts to stomp away on Wildfire.) MW: Wildfire from the second buckle... flying kneedrop! Headlock applied... and a counter by Wildfire... Superkick attempt by Wildfire, and Varga lands one of his own! JM: That's how it's done.. MW: Wildfire back up top! Flash Flood! (Wildfire moves out of the way, and shoves Varga towards the mat in mid-flight... causing him to paint the canvas with his face.) MW: HUGE FACESLAM STYLE MOVE BY WILDFIRE! THAT MAY AS ELL HAVE SEALED THE FATE OF VARGA! JM: I LOVE IT! (Varga gets whipped into the corner as Wildfire follows up with another vicious series of stomps, punches, and headslams.) MW: Whip by Wildfire, and the ref meets Varga up close and personal! JM: Zebra season here tonight! (Wildfire repeatedly beats down on Varga, until the TV-Time bell rings.) MW: Wildfire's insane! Asai Moonsault! (Bell rings again.) CHAD JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen..due to television time... this match has been declared a DRAW! (Crowd boos as Wildfire looks around furiously.) MW: Apparently we've run out of time for this bout, and these two will just have to sort out their differences some other time! (Cut to Wildfire *still* taking out his anger on Varga.) JM: The man had it won! I see no point in ending a match early, just so some old fart can watch 'Golden Girls' reruns... MW: That may be so, but as it stands, the time has expired, and we have to give up our position... saying goodnight for Josh Miller, this is Mike Williams. (Scene fades out to officials coming to the ring to split up Wildfire and Varga.)
