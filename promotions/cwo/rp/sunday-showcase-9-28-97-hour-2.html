SUNDAY SHOWCASE 9/28/97 - HOUR #2

28 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWO SUNDAY SHOWCASE - HOUR #1 9/28/97 Producer: Steve White ( [detail withheld] ) Executive Producer: Brent G. Alles ( [detail withheld] ) (Fade into a spinning globe, as the CWO logo is slowly superimposed on it.) VOICE-OVER: The CWO... providing wrestling excellence the world over.... (Fireworks light up the arena as the camera pans from the sea of CWO fans to the announcers' booth.) MW: Hello fans, and welcome once again to another jam-packed hour of great action here on Sunday Showcase! JM: (muttering) I had a toilet that was jam-packed once..took 3 hours to fix and wasn't that great. MW: Tonight we're glad to bring you SJPW action when Kenji Ishida takes on "Lightning" Lanther, Brian Williams takes on the mammoth Painmaster, and the Mercenaries aim to eliminate Attitude Dance in tag action! *Painmaster vs. Brian 'The Hammer' Williams* CHAD JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. Coming down the aisle... ('Wherever I May Roam' by Metallica plays as the fans boo) CHAD JOHNSON: Weighing 332 pounds, from Oakland. CA, Painmaster! (The lights dim as the mammoth Painmaster strolls to the ring.) MW: Painmaster set for action against Fergus McTavish later tonight at September Slam II. I'm thinking he bit off more than he can chew with two matches of this caliber. JM: Yer crackin' me up Mike. You place *way* too much confidence in bony punks like Williams, and burly fruitcakes like McTavish. CHAD JOHNSON: And his opponent... ('You Shook Me All Night Long' by AC/DC plays as the fans cheer) CHAD JOHNSON: Weighing 190 pounds, from Syracuse. NY, Brian 'The Hammer' Williams! JM: Look at this bony schmuck will ya? MW: Brian Williams not the biggest man in this sport, Miller, but definitely a contender in any class. (Bell rings as Williams socks Painmaster in the jaw and proceeds to stomp away) MW: Williams with an uncharacteristic show of aggression here! JM: C'mon ref! Closed fists! MW: KEUKA SMACK (boxes the ears, then uppercuts)! 1!.2!..Thr! Shoulder up! JM: God! If that was me, I'd be DQ'd by now! MW: Williams with a kick to the gut of Painmaster and a forearm from the big man levels Williams! Cover! 1!.Two! Foot on the ropes! (Williams slowly gets to his feet, only to get slapped back down by another massive forearm from Painmaster.) MW: Painmaster with another crushing blow to the back of Williams! Whip to the ropes... missed clothesline by Painmaster, and Williams comes back with a Cobra Clutch on the rebound! (Painmaster simply shrugs off Williams and receives a chop to the back of the neck.) JM: Look at that dolt trying to power Painmaster to the mat MW: Williams with a stinging chop to a rather sensitive area, and another chop to the throat! (Williams attempts a dropkick, only to get shoved to the mat in mid-air.) MW: Williams back to his feet and fires off a right! Another! And a kick to the knee brings the big man down! ('The Hammer' plays to the crowd before applying a headlock to the downed giant.) MW: Headlock applied. Apparently Williams trying to take Painmaster to the mat and keep him off his balance.. JM: Lotta good it'll do. The man's arms are bigger than 'The Hummer'. (Williams releases the headlock and immediately dropkicks Painmaster in the lower back.) MW: Williams just rifling a beautiful kick to the kidney region of the big man, and a series of stomps makes sure he stays on the mat! Kneedrop! JM: That had about the same effect as dropping a sock on him! MW: Painmaster just shrugging off the kneedrop from Williams, and unloading with a roundhouse right! Another! JM: Whoah! With hands like that, it's just like getting beaned with a frozen turkey! (Williams ducks under the third right and kicks Painmaster in the armpit.) MW: Williams retaliating with a kick to the ribs! Forearm! (Williams yells to the crowd and does a little jig.) JM: What the Hell is that?! MW: That's the signal for the (crowd oohs) MW: KEUKA SMACK! (Painmaster stiffens up and topples over like a tree.) MW: 1!2!.3! JM: I'll be damned. CHAD JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner in 5:10..BRIAN..'THE HAMMER'..WILLLLLLLLIAAAAAMS! ('You shook me all Night Long' plays as the fans cheer and Williams plays it up in the ring.) MW: Just like that, The Hammer can take even the most monstrous men down for the 3-count and OH MY GOODNESS! (Painmaster charges the showboating Williams from behind with a big lariat.) JM: WOW! MW: Painmaster obviously upset about that rather quick loss, and taking out on Williams! POWERBOMB! JM: There. Now he feels better MW: I don't think Williams is feeling very well after that power... LEGDROP! JM: Right across the throat! Nice and crisp! I'm impressed! MW: Painmaster with Williams... PILEDRIVER! (crowd oohs at the violent impact.) JM: Keuka-Fruit ain't movin'! MW: Painmaster with another pickup! (Painmaster signals for another piledriver, but before he can execute it, Fergus McTavish comes running to the ring and slides in under the ropes.) MW: MCTAVISH WITH A ROUNDHOUSE RIGHT! ANOTHER! McTAVISH: OATMEAL! (Painmaster gets dumped in between the ropes and slowly makes his way backwards to the lockers as McTavish taunts him from the ring) MW: McTavish setting things straight in the ring, and calling Painmaster out! JM: McTavish? Straight? Cowboy Mike, the man hangs out with a 10 year old boy. He has scoutmaster written all over him. MW: Back to the case in point, McTavish and Painmaster will tangle in the ring tomorrow night at September Slam II! (Scene fades to commercial at a panning view of the arena. Williams is still lying in the ring, unconscious, and being checked on by McTavish. When we come back, Johnny Starr is in the ring.) JOHNNY STARR: It is my pleasure to bring to you people the team that will face "The Law" in a Thunderdome Cage match on SEPTEMBER SLAM II, I GIVE YOU MONDO GECKO, EL GRANDE PISTOLERRO, COL. TAGGART, AND RANGER TAURUS, CPE! (Crowd roars as the four men come down wearing combat fatigues and CPE 4 EVER T-shirts as they get into the ring. Tamara Wilcox comes down as well but she is wearing a skin tight leather jump suit and high heels and is carrying a whip) STARR: Gecko, it started at TEAM CHALLENGE SERIES when a supposed friend, Lincoln Kennedy caused you to get eliminated and then the Law attacked you. Now as I can see you have gotten these two... MONDO GECKO: Starr just hand over the microphone because I am in no mood to answer questions right now! (Mixed reaction from the crowd) GECKO: As you people can see the Law wanted to make a war so that they could climb up the title ladder and advance to a title shot. MUSTAFA RAHIM YOU ARE NOTHING BUT A GHETTO WANNABE LOSER! He says how the CWO forced him into the stupid gimmick and he is freeing himself. Bull****, you were a horse**** wrestler who couldn't wrestle a clean match to save your life so you get the gorilla twins to help you out and that Oakland Raider revolving door tackle! As far as I am concerned, I one upped you again just like I did in our matches because Taggart and Taurus are bigger, stronger, smarter and meaner than Jerome and Jermain. SEPTEMBER SLAM in THUNDERDOME CAGE MATCH, I AM GOING TO SHOW EVERY BODY WHAT MONDO EXTREME PAIN IS ALL ABOUT!! (Gecko tosses the mike to Taggart who stares at the crowd and Starr) TAGGART: When I came here to the CWO they told me that I would be handed top notch competition and that I would receive a fair shot. SO far it seems that unless you are Joe Eagle, Lord Troy, Masher Maccabee, or The Law you get shafted. Law, you guys are going to be EXTERMINATED WHEN WE GET OUR HANDS ON YOU AND THERE IS NOTHING YOU CAN DO ABOUT IT. SEPTEMBER SLAM II WILL MARK THE DAY WHEN CPE BECAME THE ULTIMATE GROUP IN THE CWO!! (Tosses the mike to Taurus who gesture to the crowd who cheer as he pumps his fist) TAURUS: First off I am going to guarantee you fans out there the there is going to be a lot of violence and gore so anybody who doesn't like DON'T WATCH IT. Second off, Law you guys are nothing but gangsta wannabes and in da Great State of Texas, we lynch vermin like you up by your scrawny necks. SEPTEMBER SLAM II, CPE is going to make short work of you boys and go on to bigger and better things. (Taurus hands the mike to Pistolerro who makes a NWO like gesture to the crowd who roar in approval.) PISTOLERRO: ARRIBA LA RASA!! Mustafa Rahim, you little punta perro, you think you and your goons can screw me out of the North American Title and not pay? Estupido gringos, I am goin to take this whip and whip you chicos like the dogs you are. CPE is the way of life and with my compadres watching my back and any weapon in that cage, I am going to do some bodily harm to you four losers. As my hermano Konnan once said, IT WILL BE A 187 AND A HIGHWAY TO HELL, ARRIBA LA RASA Y CPE PARA VIVA!!) (Pistolerro hands the mike back to Starr who is stunned by the attitude of CPE) STARR: Tamara, you are the reason behind this group, what is going on here with these guys? WILCOX: It is really simple Johnny and I am surprised you haven't figured it out. The Law wanted to make an example out of my boyfriend Gecko and Pistolerro. So I brought in to big bad monsters who know how to beat people up and take care of problems! Law, I hope you guys bring all the surviving Black Panthers you can find because my men are going to destroy you gangsta rejects once and for all. Also at SEPTEMBER SLAM II you might even see some surprises as CPE might as we say expand! Johnny that is all for now (CPE leaves as they high five the crowd and exit through the runway. Fade to commercial. When we come back, Chad Johnson is in the ring.) *SJPW Exhibition Match* *Kenji Ishida vs. Masashi "Lightning" Lanther* MW: We're privileged to present next folks, a match featuring two stars from the SJPW wrestling family. CHAD JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. Coming down the aisle... ("Painkiller" plays to a good pop) CHAD JOHNSON: Weighing 250 pounds, from Tokyo, Kenji Ishida! (Ishida comes down to the ring slapping hands with the fans) MW: The powerful Ishida making his way to U.S. soil this week to showcase just what he can do. CHAD JOHNSON: And his opponent... ("Shock Me" plays as the fans cheer) CHAD JOHNSON: Weighing 233 pounds, from Nagasaki, Masashi "Lightning" Lanther! (The colorful Lanther runs to the ring slapping hands with the fans) JM: Good God. Look at this guy. Where does he shop? The 5 and Dime Lucha-aisle? (bell rings as both men shake hands) MW: Lanther with a fireman's carry, into a rolling cradle into a bridge! 1!. . . . 2!..THR! WOW! JM: WOW's right! The man moves like a monkey! (Lanther approaches for a facerake, but Ishida avoids it and whips him to the corner.) MW: Lanther going for the ride and bouncing off the corner! And Ishida's waiting with an octopus hold! JM: An oldie but goodie! (As Ishida works the hold in, Lanther reaches the ropes, causing a break) MW: Ishida with a powerbomb! Backdrop by Lanther! (Ishida gets dumped over the ropes and Lanther rolls out right behind him.) MW: Lanther slamming Ishida to the floor and making his way back to the ring. JM: Good tactic. When you're obviously overpowered by a man that's basically your size. Hit and run or fight dirty. (Lanther waits in the ring and tries to stomp on the approaching Ishida, but catches a fist to the jaw) MW: Ishida in control with a whip to the corner! And a running splash! Whip to the opposite cor... reversal by Lanther (Lanther catches Ishida on the rebound and attempts a DDT, but Ishida hooks his leg and flips back.) MW: Fisherman's suplex! 1!2!.Kickout! JM: Great move. MW: Ishida with Lanther choking him against the ropes, and breaking off at the 3-count. (Ishida goes for a forearm to the back, but Lanther darts out of the way and whips Ishida to the ropes.) MW: Lanther with a whip to the ropes... backspin DDT! NO! Reversal into an armbar takedown! JM: These guys are impressin' the Heck outta me! No fancy flyin! No showboatin! Just good ol' wrestling *talent*! Something the CWO obviously has a habit of shelving. MW: Ishida with an elbow to the temple and a small package! 1!2! Thr! (Lanther kips up to his feet and yells to the crowd to a good pop) MW: Lanther obviously worked up here tonight folks! Lanther with a kick to the thigh... Lanther up top! FRANKENSTEINER! JM: Perfect follow-through! MW: Lanther with a facerake! Lanther back up top! (Lanther takes off attempting a flying headscissors, but misses.) MW: Lanther taking a risk perhaps one too many times, and paying the price as we speak... Gutwrench Suplex by Ishida! JM: Ishida's great Mike! Reminds me of a young Chono! (Lanther rolls out of the ring in pain as Ishida takes off from the apron with a double axhandle.) MW: Flying axhandle to Lanther! (Both men tear into each other, smashing each other's heads into the railing and knocking each other to the concrete before Ishida rolls back in.) MW: Ishida stomping away on Lanther, who's trying to return in-ring.but Lanther fires off a kick to the ribs! Slingshot Sunset flip! Sit-down by Ishida! 1!.2!..Kickout! JM: There's usin' yer head in a tight situation. I'm really warming up to Ishida. (Lanther whips Ishida to the corner and attempts a Victory Roll, but Ishida moves out of the way.) MW: Ishida with a spinning heel kick! Duckunder by Lanther, who fires off one of his own! Springboard Splash! (Just before Lanther connects, Ishida jerks his knees up, catching Lanther in the gut to a chorus of groans from the audience.) JM: That's gonna sting in the mornin'. MW: Ishida raising the knees and sending Lanther to the fetal position! 1!2!Shoulder up! JM: Bah. MW: Ishida dumping Lanther over the ropes! (Lanther grabs onto them on the way down, kips up, and slingshots himself to Ishida) MW: Lanther with a great deal of force from the ropes... SPINEBUSTER BY ISHIDA! JM: NOW *THAT'S* TALENT! LANTHER NEARLY WENT THROUGH THE MAT! MW: 1!.2!.3! Ishida takes the pin! JM: Can I call em, or can I call em Mike!? CHAD JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen.your winner in 8:10, KENNNNJIIIIIII SHIIIIIDAAAAAAA! ('Painkiller' plays as the fans cheer the exit of Ishida.) MW: SJPW manages to garner more and more fans each and every week here folks, and that's something I'm sure Ishida doesn't mind. JM: That man will go far. (fade to commercial. During the fade, video footage of Ishida catching Lanther from a springboard into a Spinebuster is shown in slo-mo from different angles) *The Mercenaries vs. Attitude Dance* CHAD JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the following tag team matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit. Coming down the aisle... ('Iron Man' by Black Sabbath plays as the fans boo) CHAD JOHNSON: At a combined weight of 555 pounds, Kaptain Krunch, Korperal Punishment, Mercenaries! MW: The Mercenaries face a challenge here today with the expectation to take on Attitude Dance today, and The Highlanders as well as The Bad Guys tomorrow at September Slam II. JM: Now here are two guys that obviously took G. I. JOE too seriously as kids. MW: The Mercenaries may be a little militaristic, but they can more than back up any claims they make in the ring. JM: 'Little militaristic'? Good God Mike. They must've been beaten with a rubber hose daily to get the way they are. CHAD JOHNSON: And their opponents... ('MotownPhilly' by BoyzIIMen plays as the fans explode) CHAD JOHNSON: At a combined weight of 555 pounds, accompanied by Russell Hill, Jim Peer, Joe Eagle, Jason 'Flash' Varga, Attitude Dance! (The fans go nuts as Varga and Eagle slap hands on the way top the ring.) MW: The former World champ Eagle, and the current Light heavyweight Champ make their way to the ring in style! Just look at these fans! JM: Big deal. I'll bet if you tossed something shiny to that sea of simps, they'd cheer just as loud if not louder. (bell rings as Varga starts out against Punishment) MW: This one should be huge folks! (Varga plays to the fans as Punishment rails him from behind with an elbow.) MW: Big elbow to the back of Varga! Chop! Another and the lightweight's sent to the mat! (Punishment stomps twice quickly on Varga before dropping into an STF) MW: Punishment with the STF applied and Varga tangles himself in the ropes. Tag to Krunch and a double back suplex lays Varga out cold! JM: Now *that's* teamwork. The two fruity schmucks are getting a true lesson from the two fatigue-wearin'-M.R.E. eatin'-sign shootin schmucks. (Krunch lays a thunderous bodyslam and covers) MW: 1!.2!.kickout! Krunch with a whip to the ropes and Punishment nails him in the back with an axhandle from the outside! Eye gouge by Krunch! (Krunch struts around arrogantly as the ref berates him) MW: Irish Whip... big backdrop by Krunch! Double underhook suplex and a tag to Punishment! JM: Man Mike. I shoulda been in this bush league. If a schmoe like Varga or Eagle is worthy of a belt, I'd be the Ric Flair of the CWO. (Punishment tries to apply a Figure-4, but Varga gets loose and tags Eagle as the fans explode) MW: Eagle with a right to Punishment! Another! And a clothesline takes Krunch off the apron! (As Eagle plays to the fans, Punishment comes from behind with a backbrain chokeslam.) JM: WHAT POWER! MW: Punishment with a Scorpion Deathlock in the Mercenaries' corner and a tag to Krunch! JM: Not a place he wants to be. MW: Punishment with the lock still applied and Krunch lands a big legdrop! Another! (Punishment releases the hold and takes his place outside the ring as Krunch re-applies the hold.) MW: Eagle still in that devastating hold..he reaches the ropes! JM: Dang. MW: Krunch with an axhandle to the prone Eagle, and a tag to Punishment! Punishment with Eagle on his shoulders... and Krunch from the top with the FLYING CLOTHESLINE! (Punishment rolls with Eagle into a pin with the tights pulled, but Russell Hill distracts the ref.) JM: Someone get that fat slob off of there! MW: Krunch releasing the cover, and landing a forearm... STF applied, no, counter by Eagle! Tag to Varga! (crowd pop) MW: Varga with a spinning heel kick! Texas Cloverleaf by Varga, and Punishment makes sure to hit the ropes! JM: How in the world can that twiggy goon dish out any hurtin'?! MW: Varga off of the ropes... flying clothesline! Kneedrop! Varga from the top... FLYING HEADBUTT! (Varga tags to Eagle who covers Punishment) MW: 1!2!..Shoulder up! Eagle with a whip, and a clothesline sends The Merc out of the ring! (The fans cheer deafeningly as Eagle slams Punishment's head into the guardrail twice, until Krunch nails him from behind with a chair.) JM: There's that blue chair again! That thing's won more matches than anyone else I know! MW: Krunch with the chair again! And a whip to the stairs by Punishment! (Varga runs into the action, but Krunch clothesline him directly with the chair as Punishment throws Eagle into the steps again.) MW: Punishment with Eagle into the steps! Again! NO! Reversal, and Punishment gets a taste of his own medicine! (Varga grabs the leg of Krunch and trips him as Eagle works over Punishment.) MW: Eagle from the apron with an elbowsmash! BIG MISS! (the fans go nuts as Eagle flies through the Mexican announcer's table) MW: And Eagle ends up in the lap of Jaime Delgado of the Spanish announcing team! JM: Why's it always those guys?! (Korperal Punishment rolls back into the ring followed by the dogged Eagle.) MW: Both men in the ring finally on the 9 count... and a backbreaker finds it's mark on Eagle! JM: HERE COMES JOHNNY REB! MW: The man that Eagle will face tomorrow night for the World Title making his way ringside here tonight, and chances are his intentions aren't good! (Deafening boos fill the arena as Reb saunters over to ringside with Rebel Yell by his side.) MW: Kneedrop by Punishment, and Eagle rolls to the outside! (Krunch approaches Varga and backdrops him onto the tarmac as Reb approaches Eagle) JM: THAT MORON! (As Joe Eagle blindly fumbles for Varga, Reb grabs him by the shoulder and DDT's him onto the cement. Root verbally assaults the unconscious Eagle afterwards.) MW: DDT BY REB! JM: THAT'S CEMENT MIKE! THIS ONE'S OVER! MW: Eagle apparently searching for consolation from Varga, and getting a DDT into the floor as his reward! (Reb rolls Eagle into the ring and leaves laughing maniacally) MW: Krunch up top, and Punishment trying to get Eagle on his shoulders. (Punishment struggles with the dead weight of the limp Eagle before eventually getting him up on his shoulders. Eagle is so out of it, he kind of leans back instead of sitting straight up.) MW: KRUNCH FROM THE TOP! (crowd boos loudly as Krunch attempts a clothesline but his arm comes down instead of across the throat of Eagle.) JM: WHAT WAS THAT!? SOME SORT OF FLYING INVERTED BULLDOG! MW: MORE LIKELY A BOTCHED CLOTHESLINE THAT TURNED OUT IN FAVOR OF THE MERCENARIES! COVER BY PUNISHMENT! 1!2.!.3! CHAD JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen... your winners in 14:07, THE... MERRRRCENNNNARRRRIEEEEES! (The crowd boos extremely loud as Eagle lies lifelessly in the ring and the Mercenaries taunt the fans.) MW: Well fans! There you have it! Johnny Reb plants Eagle! Eagle loses! They meet tonight on their own terms at September Slam II! JM: I wanna be there when he comes to! As far as he knows, Varga put him under! This is gonna be great! Ahahah! MW: This is Mike Williams folks, signing off for 'Dr. Doom' Josh Miller and say 'See ya next week on Sunday Showcase, and tune into September Slam II: Revenge is the name of the game'! (Scene fades out to a panning shot of the arena. Eagle is still out cold, the Mercs are making their way backstage, and Varga is finally getting back into the ring to tend to Eagle.)
