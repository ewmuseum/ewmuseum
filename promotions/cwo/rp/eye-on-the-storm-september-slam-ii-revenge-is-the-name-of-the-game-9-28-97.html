WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: Joe Eagle (w/Russell Hill) vs. Johnny Reb (champion, w/General Lee and Rebel Yell) STARR: Of course the main event tonight pits Joe Eagle against CWO World Champion Johnny Reb. At one time, these two men were the best of friends. However, at TEAM CHALLENGE SERIES that all ended, as Johnny Reb, at first refusing to take a tag from Joe Eagle to get a chance at pinning Gold Kard to win the title, then came off the top rope on to Eagle while the referee was distracted, then rolled Eagle off of Gold Kard and made the cover himself, winning the title. After that, any remnants of friendship between the two men were quickly destroyed, as Johnny Reb started making a nuisance of himself in many of Joe Eagle's affairs. That was met by Joe Eagle's refusal to allow himself or even any of his associates, like CWO Light Heavyweight Champion Jason "Flash" Varga to come to Reb's aid when he was being attacked by the likes of Gold Kard International. The friendship is gone, now all that remains is Joe Eagle's apparently all-consuming desire to hold the CWO World Championship for a second time, and Johnny Reb's equal desire to not let that happen. WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (TRIANGLE MATCH): Mercenaries vs. Highlanders vs. Bad Guys (champions, w/Herbie Versmells) STARR: Gavin McCloud has felt the pain the Bad Guys can dish out, he was put on the disabled list for a while after a vicious attack by those two ex-cons. The Mercenaries and the Highlanders have had a long-standing rivalry. The Mercenaries and the Bad Guys co-existed, and even teamed up during the TEAM CHALLENGE SERIES against The Regulators, Conor McCloud, and Fergus McTavish, the Whirling Scottish Devil. However, before that match took place, the Bad Guys interference cost the Mercenaries the North American titles they held. So now you have a situation where each of the three teams in this triangle match is looking for revenge against the other two, as well as the World Tag Team Titles being on the line, and you have the recipe for a truly explosive confrontation. NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP: "Monster" Rich Clark vs. Masher Maccabee (champion) STARR: Who is the toughest man in the CWO? Is it 7'4", 446 pound "Monster" Rich Clark, former World and North American Champion? Or is it Masher Maccabee, who although earned the title not by beating anybody, but by having it handed to him literally on a silver platter by Gold Kard, has since then proven to be a very able champion and a tough man to beat. Maccabee shows no fear against the monster of CWO, but with the wild Scotsman Fergus McTavish still on his tail, Maccabee may be burning the candle at both ends and may end up losing the North American Championship to Clark tonight. NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Bloodbath and Wildfire vs. The Big Cool (champions, w/Russell Hill) STARR: Once considered one of the strongest pairings here in the CWO, the Wild Side, Bloodbath and Wildfire, are currently seized with many internal problems. First of all are the mysterious attacks of weakness that Bloodbath has been suffering. Many believe that these are somehow related to the mysterious Reverend. Also, some inner turmoil grips the soul of Wildfire as well, driving the once flamboyant popular wrestler into a vow of silence. The Big Cool, a strong team in their own right, may have the upper hand here at SEPTEMBER SLAM II, unless the Wild Side can find a way to conquer their inner demons, at least for one very important night. TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (loser leaves CWO): Benjamin Blake Bentley vs. The Focus (champion) STARR: Not much else has to be said about this feud, it has been going on for months. These two men are very evenly matched, except in attitude, Bentley being cocky and arrogant, The Focus being determined and relentless. Tonight, their careers are on the line, because while the winner will have the increasingly important Television title in their grasp, the loser will be forced to leave the CWO... forever. LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP (no DQ, there must be a winner): Moses vs. Jason "Flash" Varga (champion, w/Jim Peer) STARR: The man who says he "owns" the Light Heavyweight Championship... he's held it three times, more than any other champion... tries to get it for a fourth time against the man who defeated him for it, Jason "Flash" Varga. It is an interesting contrast in styles, the devil-may-care brawling of Varga against the technical proficient high flyer in Moses. Moses is still a member of Gold Kard International, and Varga will have to be aware of their presence here tonight. THUNDERDOME (all 8 men in a cage, weapons allowed): The Law (Mustafa Rahim, ???, Brother Justice) vs. CPE (Mondo Gecko, El Grande Pistolerro, CPE Enforcers) STARR: Mondo Gecko thought he could trust Lincoln Kennedy, the Oakland Raider. He thought wrong. At TEAM CHALLENGE SERIES, he was viciously betrayed by Kennedy, who turned out to be a secret member of Mustafa Rahim's "gang," the Law. Mondo Gecko, along with his best friend Pistolerro, needed two men to team with against the Law, since the Regulators lost a loser leaves CWO match to the Mercenaries. Col. Taggart and Ranger Taurus stepped up to the plate, and now the CPE hopes to gain revenge against the Law tonight in the Thunderdome cage, where weapons are allowed, and the fight continues until one man can no longer continue. However, Mustafa Rahim, frustrated with the "disrespect" the CWO has shown him in the past, has sent Steel on a mission... to find the member of The Law who can bring down the CPE, once and for all. RESPECT MATCH: Brent "The Ace" Alles (w/Gordy Alles) vs. Lord Hile Troy STARR: So much has been revealed in this match even before the ink dried on the contract. Alles, who we now know to be the father of Amanda Bloom's still unborn child, revealed to the world that Lord Hile Troy had indeed been his trainer before entering the sport, along with his father Gordy Alles. Lord Hile Troy, once a respected man, now swollen with pride and greed as a member of Gold Kard International, haughtily rebuked his former student and even went so far as to air Alles' "dirty laundry." Alles wanted a scientific match with his former mentor, to try and gain his respect. Troy quite literally spit in his face. Now, after all that has gone down, what was hoped to be a display of skill between two of the best in our sport now has devolved into a very heated situation. Alles wants Troy's respect, but now instead of wanting it in a scientific manner, Alles has vowed to beat it out of the cocky former World Champion. Troy, of course, is not impressed. 6-MAN ACTION: "Gigolo" Jimmy Heat and the Coldfront (w/Tommy Bomber) vs. Deadly Sins Wrath, Pride, and Sloth (w/Lust) STARR: Heat teamed with the Deadly Sins at TEAM CHALLENGE SERIES. Big mistake. Because he hit on the manager of the Deadly Sins, Lust, he quickly was subject to the attack by the jealous Pride and Sloth. Does Lust harbor secret feelings for Heat? We may never know, but Heat's new alliance with the Coldfront (along with their bodyguard and wrestler in training Tommy Bomber) is going to try to end the supremacy of the Deadly Sins tonight. BOUNTY MATCH: Vegas High Roller (w/Joe Root) vs. Wayne "College Guy" Schillo (If Schillo wins, he gets 5 minutes in the ring with Joe Root.) STARR: It has not been an easy year for Wayne "College Guy" Schillo. First there was the out of control feud with Brent "The Ace" Alles. Then, everybody thought that Schillo had betrayed the CWO as Ninja Waynitsu, a former role played by Schillo, made a reappearance in the camp of Gold Kard International, even going so far as to cost Joe Eagle the title against Gold Kard. Joe Eagle then proceeded to berate his former best friend and slap him in the face. Schillo could not defend himself, as he was being blackmailed by Gold Kard International. However, at the end of TEAM CHALLENGE SERIES, Wayne Schillo finally found the courage to stand up to the extortion tactics of Gold Kard, and came down to the ring to reveal that Ninja Waynitsu had in fact been Joe Root, former CWO Announcer (who "quit" earlier this year, saying he couldn't "take" the antics of Gold Kard International). Going through a period of self disclosure himself, Wayne Schillo now wants revenge against the Vegas High Roller, and more importantly against the man behind the mask, Joe Root. Vegas High Roller simply wants the money, based on a long standing bounty put on Schillo's head by Gold Kard, to the tune of $2,000,000. Vegas High Roller will not attempt to beat Schillo tonight, he will attempt to end his career. However, if Wayne Schillo wins, he then gets 5 minutes in the ring with the impish Joe Root, and that may be 5 minutes of sweet revenge indeed. 8-MAN SUBMIT OR SURRENDER MATCH: The Unit (Sean Holiday, Skyhawk, Newton Bros., w/Toni Newton) vs. The Cabin (Exodus, Judgment, Ghost Riders, w/Lord Guru) STARR: All the Unit were trying to do was make their presence known here in the CWO. They couldn't believe that they were being hassled, considering their past success in other wrestling federations. However, the Cabin was not impressed at all, and even went so far as to attack Johnny B. Goode after a Newton Bros. match. Johnny B. Goode, as many Unit fans know, was in a car accident a few years ago that almost left him paralyzed for life. By sheer force of will, Goode was able to regain the ability to walk, although his active wrestling career was finished forever. Now, after the attack, Goode's ambulatory status is again at an uncertain point. Dedicating this match to their fallen manager, the Unit is going to make the Cabin pay the ultimate price by having one of them utter the words, "I Quit." NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP #1 CONTENDER MATCH: Painmaster vs. Fergus McTavish, the Whirling Scottish Devil STARR: The powerful newcomer Painmaster quickly caught the eye of many powerful wrestling personalities, including former World Champion Gold Kard, who had a special assignment for the newcomer: destroy Fergus McTavish, the Whirling Scottish Devil. Even though the major threat of Gold Kard International has ended, Fergus still feels, in his own warped way, that it's his duty to get Gold Kard International out of the CWO forever. Being very busy in other affairs right now, Gold Kard needed a "perfect" hitman, and found one in Painmaster. Will he be able to put down the wild Scotsman? We'll have to see, but keep this in mind: the winner gets a shot at the North American title at TERROR NIGHT '97, that certainly is going to make it more interesting. TEXAS TWISTER MATCH: Reverend and Sandman vs. Diamondback Chris Myers and Sandman Jr. (All men fighting at the same time, falls count anywhere in the building.) STARR: Not much is known yet about the mysterious Reverend, except his uncanny control over his "dark minion" Sandman, and his apparent debilitating effect on Bloodbath. In other matters, popular CWO star Diamondback Chris Myers was his first target. Sandman Jr. was also attacked by Sandman, who claimed that since Sandman Jr. had stole his name, he would steal Sandman Jr.'s "soul." The feud has escalated, and now all four men meet in a first ever "Texas Twister" match, in which all four men fight at the same time, and falls count anywhere in the building. Gold Kard vs. Holocaust STARR: Gold Kard is not easily defeated. Even though Johnny Reb beat him for the World title at TEAM CHALLENGE SERIES, you can be sure Gold Kard was hatching a plan only seconds after his defeat. That plan was probably derailed when the mysterious masked Holocaust, along with the former Light Heavyweight Champion Red Raven, made it their business to disrupt Gold Kard's business. Holocaust says he hates Gold Kard, nobody is really sure why, although it is thought that there is some past connection betwe en the two men that nobody dares speak of. X-Factor OR Marc Mission vs. Southern Dynamite STARR: A last minute change, the unorthodox duo of X-Factor and Marc Mission will have to decide which one of them gets to go against rising young star Southern Dynamite. "The Brazilian Lover" Carlos Maia vs. "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher STARR: At TEAM CHALLENGE SERIES, Deutscher eliminated Maia in under a minute after a Stun Gun. Maia then retaliated with a series of attacks against the loner of the CWO. Now, these two meet tonight, and keeping with the theme, revenge will certainly be the name of the game.
