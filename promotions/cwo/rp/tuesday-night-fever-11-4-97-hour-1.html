CWO TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER 11/4/97 - HOUR #1 "TAPED" IN SAGINAW, MI (10/28/97) RATING: TV-14 (L, V) Producer: [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] ) Executive Producer: Brent G. Alles ( [detail withheld] ) (A spinning globe is shown, as the CWO logo is slowly superimposed over it.) VOICE-OVER: The CWO... providing wrestling excellence the world over.... (Montage of CWO action is shown as Aerosmith's "Fever" plays in the background. We go to a roaring crowd in Saginaw, MI, as fireworks go off in the background, signaling the start of hour #1 of TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER. Johnny Starr is standing in the ring.) STARR: Ladies and gentlemen, my guests at this time went into last Sunday's TERROR NIGHT looking to settle some scores. Please join me in welcoming my friends, Johnny B. Goode and the UNIT! (Crowd gives megapop as Def Leppard's "Gods Of War" blasts over the P.A. system. Blue lasers light up the arena as pyrotechnics explode at the ring entrance. When the explosions die down, Johnny B. Goode slowly makes his way to the ring, followed by "Assassin 64" Sean Holiday, Steve and Howard Newton, and Skyhawk. All five men are wearing jean shorts, "UNIT" T- shirts, and work boots, although Goode's left leg is still encased in plaster. Holiday sports a nice shiner over his right eye where he ran into a CWO videocam during the Saturday Night Special, and Skyhawk wears a small bandage on his forehead, hiding the stitches he needed after being given a Tommy Knocker on a steel chair at Eye On The Storm. In addition, Steve and Howard proudly carry their new CWO World Tag-Team title belts slung over their shoulders. When the five hit the ring, the four wrestlers climb opposite turnbuckles and play to the crowd as overhead pyrotechnics sparkle down on them.) STARR: Oh my, what an entrance! GOODE: Johnny Starr! It's good to be alive, pal! (Crowd roars in approval.) GOODE: If I may just paraphrase NRBQ, "What a wild weekend!" Okay, we'll settle down and admit it. It didn't go totally by our plans. Not in the least. But for what we got done, it was a party! Tell 'em, Sean! HOLIDAY: You know, "Ultimate Heel" Union Caine, you pinned me at Saturday Night Special. Okay, I grant you that. I could be nice and say that it could happen to anyone. But we all know better, now, don't we? Ever since we came out here and said we'd start kicking butts and taking names, the boys upstairs in the CWO front office have been nervous. Do you all really think it was an accident that a CWO cameraman just happened to be in the way during a match? Caine, you know as well as I do that you didn't win that match. Tom Krepps did. (Crowd gives a very vocal mixed reaction.) HOLIDAY: By putting me down and out, he managed to give us a loss, hoping we'd shut up. No dice, Krepps. All you did was make us madder. Bottom line, Caine, is this: are you up for a rematch? Just you and me, and keep the damn cameraman off the ring apron. I know you're watching this thing, so don't keep us in suspense. We'll be here all night long, so when you're done playing in the garbage out back, give me a yell. GOODE: You know, Johnny Starr, I gotta tell you something. Right now, Tom Krepps isn't the only person we're upset with. There's another little scumbucket running around, who's cost us two matches so far. That's right, I'm talking about one Mr. "Gigolo" Jimmy Heat. You cost us our first match here in the CWO, kid, and you cost this man... (Johnny B. Goode motions to Skyhawk, who flexes to cheers from the fans.) GOODE: ...revenge upon your idiot friend, Tommy Boy. Now, the war isn't over yet between Skyhawk and that moron, but neither is the war between us and you. SKYHAWK: Tommy Bomber, you've got a lot going for you. But the more you keep messing in our affairs, like your bud Jimmy Heat, the more it's going to cost you! This... (Skyhawk motions to the bandage on his forehead, causing the fans to start chanting "Stitches! Stitches!") SKYHAWK: ...is one of the more painful injuries I've received during my wrestling career. Not because I got bloody. That's happened before. But because I was dumb enough to turn my attention away from you long enough for you to take advantage of it! Yeah, like Steve admitted to doing last FEVER, I made a rookie mistake. And as a result, I got Knockered onto a steel chair! (Crowd boos at this memory.) SKYHAWK: So now, Tommy Boy, you've caused me two defeats. Both times with steel chairs. And I'm getting to the point where I believe that the gloves have to come off. So, Tommy Boy, listen up and get someone to explain the big words to you: I want a rematch with you, right here on FEVER, next week! Let's make sure there's no more cheap shots, shall we? I say, let's go for a Death Match, because I sure as hell feel like killing you just now! (Pandemonium reigns as the crowd reacts to this statement with a major pop.) STARR: This is incredible, Johnny B. Goode! I've never seen your guys so pumped up before! GOODE: You ain't seen nothing yet, Johnny Starr, because I still got to do one more thing. May I? (Johnny Starr gives Johnny B. Goode his microphone.) GOODE: Ladies and gentlemen, last Sunday, at TERROR NIGHT, we came, we partied, we conquered! THE GOLD IS OURS!
