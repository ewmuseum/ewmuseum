CWO EYE ON THE STORM: SPRING BREAK '97

22 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWO EYE ON THE STORM: SPRING BREAK '97 "LIVE" FROM CLUB LA VELA, PANAMA CITY, FL 3-7-97 (EYE ON THE STORM opening comes up as we go to a roaring crowd of maddened college students and others at Club La Vela. David "The Truth" Thomas, dressed in an atrocious Hawaiian shirt and [God help us] shorts that show off his incredibly white legs, is next to the better-dressed Kyle Melinn, who is in a stylish polo shirt with khaki shorts.) KYLE MELINN: Good evening, everybody, and welcome to Club La Vela in Panama City, FL, where we are ready for SPRING BREAK '97! I'm Kyle Melinn, and with me as always, the man who has had the ASPCA on alert for the past few days with beached whale reports, David "The Truth" Thomas! DAVID "THE TRUTH" THOMAS: Hey, watch it, Melinn! You know that all these lovely ladies are here for one thing only tonight, to handle the Truth! MELINN: Certainly a lot of lovely ladies in the audience, and everybody is ready for intense CWO action, let's go to the ring for our first match! (Tony the K is in the ring along with referee Gary Alles.) TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, our opening contest is scheduled for one fall. Coming down the aisle.... ("Cantaloop" by US3 plays as the fans cheer the entrance of the Big Cool.) TONY THE K: At a combined weight of 765 pounds, accompanied by their manager Russell Hill, Jim "Jazzman" Taylor and Jumbo Jenkins, the Big Cool! (The Big Cool come down slapping hands and get in the ring.) TONY THE K: And their opponents.... ("Civil War" by Guns 'N' Roses plays as the fans cheer the entrance of the Regulators.) TONY THE K: At a combined weight of 651 pounds, accompanied by their manager Tamara Wilcox, Regulator Fire and Regulator Ice, the Regulators! (Regulators come down slapping hands and get into the ring as the males in attendance at Club La Vela hoot appreciatively at Tamara. A split screen is shown of the Regulators in the interview area with comments.) REGULATOR FIRE: Big Cool, you guys are tough, rugged, and honest, and for that we respect you two but understand one thing. We are on a mission to win the World title belts and we are going to maul over anyone who gets in our way. So Big Cool, you two guys are great wrestlers who will be in the wrong place at the wrong time. We are here to win titles and to regulate the CWO and nothing will stand in our way. Tell 'em, Ice! REGULATOR ICE: Well, it goes like this: we are hungry for titles and respect, and you guys are next on our feeding frenzy. We are on a Road Kill Tour and it goes straight to the Spring Break PPV. Already the Eternal Wanderers and the Deadly Sins have felt the Power of the Regulators and you guys will be next. Big Cool, you guys are two tremendous wrestlers and we respect that but we are going to maul you two into the ground and go on to the world tag team titles. We will win the belts and regulate the CWO for JUSTICE WILL BE SERVED! (Back to the ring.) MELINN: Two of the best tag teams in the CWO colliding to start us off here tonight, a beautiful night in downtown Panama City, FL. All right, Jazzman starting against Regulator Fire, and Fire starts off quickly with a chop. Russell Hill still on the canvas, and Fire shoves him off! THOMAS: Way to go! MELINN: The Big Cool not too happy about that, and Jazzman comes back with a forearm smash, but Fire responds with a punch, goes for a dropkick... it's sidestepped by the Jazzman. Jazzman with a kick to the midsection, and now a wristlock applied... referee asking Fire if he wants to submit, you can forget about that. THOMAS: They'd rather eat their own mother. MELINN: Jazzman tags to Jumbo Jenkins, Jenkins goes for an inside cradle but Fire is out before a 1 count. Fire with a quick blow that stuns Jumbo, he goes up top... flying clothesline! Cover, 1... shoulder up. Legdrop, and now Fire going for a powerslam but it's blocked. Jumbo tries to show some power of his own with a gorilla press but Fire slips out. Fire with a karate chop that floors Jumbo! THOMAS: Jumbo, at 450 pounds, is going to be hard to slam around, which might be a detriment to the Regulators, with their heavy power attack. MELINN: Fire up top again... flying elbow drop! Up top a second time... another one! Fire runs into the ropes... and Jumbo comes back with a football tackle! He speared him right in the midsection, and now Jumbo with a cover, 1... 2... thr... kickout. Jumbo whips Fire into the corner... Fire bounces back... belly-to-belly suplex! Jumbo up top... flying clothesline! THOMAS: I can't believe the ring ropes are still standing! MELINN: Jumbo with a bodyslam! Gorilla Press! And there's the Jumbo Bomb! Jumbo tries to whip Fire into the corner but it's reversed... Fire charges in but Jumbo moves out of the way! Jenkins with a bodyslam, followed by a falling headbutt. Now Jazzman is in the ring, Jazzman with a backbreaker, Jumbo up top... big splash... no! Fire moves out of the way! Regulator Ice is in, he knocks Jazzman out of the ring, those two men brawling outside the ring now. THOMAS: The Big Cool going for the Star Flash perhaps a little too early, and that could be the break that the Regulators need. MELINN: Fire now with a figure four applied... but Jumbo manages to reverse it! Fire reaches the ropes, and Jumbo breaks the hold. Jazzman back in the ring now, he nails Fire with a backbreaker, Jumbo up top again... big splash! This time they hit with the Star Flash and that could be it, 1... 2... thr... no! Regulator Fire kicking out with a lot of authority, and he is back on his feet! THOMAS: Look at Jumbo! He can't believe it! MELINN: We've talked a lot about the resiliency of the Regulators, and now Fire with an elbow smash, but Jenkins comes back with a forearm smash. He whips Fire into the corner... Ice trying to come in, but the referee holding him back, and now the Big Cool is doubleteaming Fire in the corner. THOMAS: The Big Cool used to be rulebreakers, and you can see that those tactics still exist in the minds of these men. MELINN: Jenkins goes for a forearm smash but it's ducked by Fire, Fire with a quick blow, he goes up top... flying clothesline! And now... he makes the tag to Regulator Ice! Here comes the Iceman with a chop, an elbow smash, but Jumbo comes back with a left hook! THOMAS: He floored him with that one! Evander Holyfield couldn't do better! MELINN: Jumbo with a cover, 1... 2... shoulder up. Another cover, 1... 2... thr... kickout. Jumbo with an elbow smash, but Ice comes back with a chop. Ice tags to Fire, Regulators with a double back suplex on Jumbo! THOMAS: Now that's power! MELINN: Fire goes for a karate thrust but it's ducked by Jumbo, Jumbo with a hard right hand, now stomping away on Regulator Fire. JUMBO (screaming to the crowd): YEAH, BABY! MELINN: Jumbo letting his concentration slip just a bit, and that gives Fire enough time to tag to Ice. Regulators with a double dropkick! Ice with the cover, 1... shoulder up. Ice with a kick to the midsection, and Jumbo rolls outside the ring. THOMAS: Here we go! MELINN: Ice outside now, he's got a chair but Jumbo kicks him in the midsection, causing him to drop it. Jumbo now with the chair, he nails Ice with it! Ice rolled back in the ring by Jumbo, who tags to the Jazzman. Jazzman stomping away on Ice, cover, 1... 2... shoulder up. Jazzman goes for a dropkick but it's sidestepped by Ice. Ice kicks the Jazzman in the midsection, and now he's picking him up by the throat! THOMAS: Almost like a mother cat picks up a kitten! MELINN: Ice with a Choke Slam! That could be it, cover, 1... 2... thr... no, Jazzman kicked out. Ice with a legdrop, a fist drop, a kneedrop, using a low-risk aerial attack to wear down the Jazzman. Ice going for another legdrop but Jazzman rolls out of the way! THOMAS: Not low-risk enough! MELINN: Jazzman with a cover, 1... 2... thr... shoulder up. Jazzman tags to Jumbo, the Big Cool now doubleteaming Ice, they nail him with a double faceslam. Jumbo with a cover, 1... 2... thr... shoulder up. Jumbo with another cover, 1... 2... shoulder up. Jumbo complaining to referee Gary Alles about a slow count, and frustration on the part of the Big Cool is beginning to show. THOMAS: Well, let's face it, they've dished out everything in their arsenal, and still the Regulators are coming back! MELINN: Jumbo with a kick to the midsection, followed by a forearm smash, gorilla press! Ice rolls outside the ring, Jumbo following him, he slams Ice into the ring apron! Jumbo rolls back in the ring now, followed by Ice. Jazzman in the ring, he nails Ice with a backbreaker, Jumbo up on the top rope... and oh no! Fire from behind, he pushed Jumbo off the turnbuckle, and Jumbo falls to the canvas! THOMAS: All's fair in love and war! MELINN: Fire in the ring now, he clotheslines Jazzman out of the ring! Fire with a legdrop on Jumbo, big DDT! And now Fire... he's going to... oh my goodness... he's going to Tombstone Jumbo! Can he get him up? Yes! He did it! Tombstone! That could be it, 1... 2... 3! TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the winners of the match in 14:59, the team of Regulator Fire and Regulator Ice, the Regulators! (Crowd cheers as "Civil War" plays.) MELINN: Great tag team match starting us off tonight here on EYE ON THE STORM, this match only an indication of what is to come on the PPV broadcast ahead. (Cut to promo as a slow black and white image montage of Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo is shown.) VOICE-OVER: He's on top of the world... but now.... (Bane is shown in darkness, chuckling evilly. "Sympathy for the Devil" by the Rolling Stones plays as a montage of Bane in action is shown.) VOICE-OVER: He must face a man who not only has no soul... but is out to steal the souls of those he faces.... (Images of both men are shown in a split-screen.) VOICE-OVER: Schillo... Bane.... (SPRING BREAK '97 logo crashes through the screen.) VOICE-OVER: CWO SPRING BREAK '97: FEAR THE FUTURE. (Back to Kyle and David.) MELINN: Well, what else can we say about a confrontation between two men that many in the CWO have been waiting for a long time. Bane and Schillo, that is the main event tonight, but we are ready for our next matchup, a heated rivalry that we've watched developed over the last couple of months. Tony the K, take it away! (Tony the K is in the ring along with referee Tom Krepps.) TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall. It a special grudge match. Introducing first, coming down the aisle.... ("Milque Toast" by Helmet plays as the Focus makes his entrance.) TONY THE K: Weighing 225 pounds, from parts unknown, the Focus! (The Focus makes his way down to the ring. A split-screen comes up with him in the interview area with comments.) THE FOCUS: Ron Johnson, I beat you just like I said I would. Nobody can get up after the Focal Point, and I proved to the world once again that I can compete here in the CWO. Now to EYE ON THE STORM... Benjamin Blake Bentley, I will defeat you again. Rest assured that not only will I beat you, but I will hurt you. Maybe a special Focal Point will be in order, just to show you and the CWO how serious I am. Do you want to screw around with me Benjamin, do you really want to feel the wrath of the Focus? Well, I am positive that you don't want to see me angry, but you will... at EYE ON THE STORM. Prepare. (Back to the ring.) TONY THE K: And his opponent.... (Cheesy harpsichord music plays as Benjamin Blake Bentley makes his entrance to the boos of the crowd, an attractive young model on his arm as usual.) TONY THE K: Weighing 234 pounds, from Greenwich, CT, Benjamin Blake Bentley! (Bentley makes his way down to the ring arrogantly and gets in as his valet holds the ropes for him. Bentley curtsies to the crowd as the continue to boo him.) MELINN: Benjamin Blake Bentley stirring up the crowd here at Club La Vela tonight. These two men have really developed a heated rivalry in a short time, with the Focus somewhat irritated at his perceived inability to get a "fair shake" from the officials here in the CWO. THOMAS: Well, he should stop whining and start winning, as far as I'm concerned! MELINN: All right, bell has rung, we're underway. Bentley starting off quickly with a chop, followed by a brainbuster and a cover, he's got the tights! 1... 2... thr... shoulder up! Now see, right there is what the Focus is talking about. THOMAS: So what? Be dirtier than your opponent, that's what I always say! MELINN: Bentley whips the Focus into the ropes... goes for a European uppercut but the Focus ducks underneath and gets Bentley with a backslide! 1... 2... thr... and Bentley barely gets his shoulder up, he was not ready for that. THOMAS: I'll say this, the Focus definitely knows how to wrestle, but so does Bentley. MELINN: Focus going for a cover again, 1... and Bentley is in the ropes. Focus whips Bentley into the corner... and now look at this, Bentley begging for mercy, that's pathetic... the Focus moving in... and Bentley meets him with a rake to the eyes! THOMAS: Ha ha ha! MELINN: Bentley whips the Focus into the ropes... double clothesline! Both men down, but the Focus is back to his feet first. Focus with a reverse neckbreaker, followed by an inside cradle, 1... 2... thr... shoulder up. Focus goes for another inside cradle but Bentley is out of it before a 1 count. Bentley with a forearm smash, Focus with a chop, Bentley with a double axhandle, Focus is down, Bentley with a cover, 1... 2... kickout. Another cover, 1... 2... thr... and another nearfall for Bentley. Bentley and Focus now trading blows, Focus runs into the ropes... nails Bentley with a double axhandle. And now the Focus may be ready for the Focal Point, that devastating top rope powerbomb of his! THOMAS: If he hits that, it'll be all over! MELINN: Wait a minute, Bentley's valet up on the ring apron, do you know her name, Truth? THOMAS: I think it's Charysse, but whatever it is, she is definitely a looker! MELINN: You can't even blame somebody named the Focus for being distracted by this lady, and she has enabled Bentley to get back to his feet, Bentley trying to come from behind, but the Focus nails him with a clothesline! THOMAS: This guy is getting good at not being distracted! MELINN: Getting good at knowing Bentley's tricks, at least. Focus with a side headlock but Bentley counters with a back suplex. Bentley up top now... flying clothesline... the Focus nails him in the gut! Focus now up top... flying cross body press... but Bentley moves out of the way! THOMAS: That wasn't good, Focus hit the mat hard, it may be Nobility time, Melinn! MELINN: Bentley whips Focus into the ropes... and another double clothesline! Referee Tom Krepps making his ten count, the Focus again struggling to his feet, he snaps an armbar on Bentley... Bentley reaches the ropes. Focus with a side headlock on now... referee asking Bentley if he wants to give it up, Bentley saying no. Bentley manages to reach the ropes, and the Focus nails him with a punch. Focus with another punch, he goes up top... flying cross body press! THOMAS: Focus not going for the pin, though, and that could be a mistake. MELINN: Focus with an inside cradle but Bentley out of it before a 1 count. Focus with a hard right hand, and now he has Bentley in the corner, Focus and Bentley both up on the ropes... there's the Focal Point! Top rope powerbomb and a cover... wait a minute... Focus' feet are on the ropes but Bentley's valet is distracting the ref, come on! THOMAS: Hey, considering what Krepps has to go home too, I'd be distracted by that nubile young thing too if I were him. (Bentley's valet screams as the Focus charges towards her.) MELINN: Focus is enraged, he's going after the valet, who gets off the canvas in a hurry, Bentley coming from behind, but the Focus spins around and nails him with a series of chops! Bentley comes back with a hard right hand, though, and the Focus rolls outside the ring. Bentley following him outside, he whips him into the guardrail! Focus rolled back in the ring now by Bentley, Bentley with a headlock applied... Focus reaches the ropes. Bentley with a rollup, 1... kickout. Bentley with an elbow drop and a cover, 1... kickout. THOMAS: Come on, Bentley, give him the Nobility! MELINN: Focus runs into the ropes... Bentley goes for a chop but it's ducked by the Focus, Focus with a backslide... but Bentley reverses it! 1... 2... thr... and the Focus just barely gets his shoulder up. THOMAS: Bentley still with enough presence of mind to be able to counter that backslide. MELINN: And now Bentley whips Focus into the ropes... goes for a throat punch but it's avoided by Focus, both men crisscrossing the ropes, Bentley with an elbow smash, but Focus responds with a forearm smash! Focus stomping away on Bentley, and now he's got him in the corner again... a second Focal Point! That could be it, 1... 2... thr... no, Bentley kicked out! He kicked out! I don't believe it! THOMAS: I do! There's no beating the man from Connecticut! MELINN: Focus goes for a DDT... but Bentley counters with a backdrop! This has been a close contest, both men have to be exhausted by now. Bentley whips the Focus into the ropes... and look at that! Backhand slap by Bentley, that has to be humiliating! Bentley with an eye rake, and now a headlock applied... Focus reaches the ropes. THOMAS: Notice that Bentley hasn't been playing to the crowd or even to his valet, he is, you'll pardon the pun, focused on this match. MELINN: Bentley stomping away on Focus, who rolls outside the ring, Bentley now outside the ring, he's got a chair, he nails the Focus with it! Bentley slams Focus into a ringside table! Bentley with a chair again, but this time the Focus grabs it away and hits Bentley with it! I believe the Focus has been opened up, a cut in his forehead from Bentley's attack, but the Focus is back on the offense. Focus back in the ring now, followed by Bentley. FOCUS (to Bentley): COME ON! MELINN: Bentley meets the Focus with a kick to the midsection, now both men trading blows, Bentley with a double axhandle knocks the Focus to the ground, and now Bentley with an elbow drop, and he's setting up the Focus for the Nobility! THOMAS: This is it, Melinn! MELINN: But the Focus slumping to the ground, Bentley not able to secure the double underhook properly, the Focus may be unconscious. THOMAS: Come on, Benny! MELINN: Bentley picks Focus up, Focus with a forearm smash, he may be fighting mostly on instinct now, both men trading blows again, Bentley with an elbow smash and the Focus rolls outside the ring. Bentley follows, he slams Focus into the ringpost! Focus' forehead covered in blood, he may have lost a lot of it so far! Focus rolled back in the ring now by Bentley, Bentley goes for a throat punch but it's avoided by Focus, Focus with an elbow smash, but Bentley comes back with a punch and a cover, 1... 2... kickout. THOMAS: This is intense! MELINN: And now Bentley with a sleeperhold, Focus may go down to this... no, he manages to reach the ropes. And now Bentley has Focus in the Nobility... he nails it! Inverted piledriver and a beauty, wait a minute... Bentley's got his feet on the ropes, 1... 2... 3! Travesty of justice again for the Focus! TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the match in 13:19, Benjamin Blake Bentley! (Bentley curtsies before collapsing to the mat.) MELINN: Well, those of you on the PREVUE CHANNEL, we thank you for joining us, if you haven't ordered CWO SPRING BREAK '97 yet, do so now, those of you watching the pre-game show, stay with us... wait a minute... referee Gary Alles is coming down to the ring. THOMAS: What does he want? MELINN: Alles conferring with Krepps, and now Krepps is speaking to Tony the K. TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, referee Tom Krepps has just announced to me that as a result of the testimony of referee Gary Alles, that he has reversed his decision. Therefore, your winner, as a result of a disqualification, the Focus! (The crowd erupts as Bentley gets right in Krepps face, angrily berating him.) MELINN (laughing): I love it! THOMAS: That's not fair, Melinn! There's no instant replay rule in the CWO! MELINN: Wait a minute... from behind! The Focus has brought a table into the ring, what's he going to... oh my! Focus with a Focal Point top rope powerbomb through the table! THOMAS: Get him out of there! ("Milque Toast" by Helmet plays as the crowd cheers the Focus.) MELINN: The crowd may not always appreciate the Focus, but they appreciate that! We've got to go, we thank you for joining us on CWO EYE ON THE STORM: SPRING BREAK '97, and we hope to see you in a few minutes! This is Kyle Melinn, for David "The Truth" Thomas, so long!
