SUNDAY SHOWCASE 10/26/97 - HOUR #2

26 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SUNDAY SHOWCASE HOUR #2 10/26/1997 CWO Arena Producer: Steve White ( [detail withheld] ) Executive Producer: Brent G. Alles ( [detail withheld] ) (The CWO logo in metallic letters slowly fades into view against a dark screen. As the roar of the fans slowly builds up, the letters explode, revealing a packed CWO arena. A slow panning shot of the arena catches the 2nd hour fireworks and ends up stopping at the Showcase announcer's booth.) Mike Williams: Welcome ladies and gentlemen! You are on hand to what's sure to be another great hour of CWO entertainment here today! And let's not forget that just hours away, 'Terror Night 97' will make history with what's billed as the single most e xciting card in PPV history! JM: Exciting my rear end! The only guys with any talent in this fed don't even get top-billing! Instead we've got that moron Miguel Sanchez, who's been wasted on talentless hacks like Sandman Jr., prancing around in a garbage match! And that's one of the better bouts! Don't even get me started on the rest of the card! MW: (mutters) I can already tell you're going to be a hoot to work with today... JM: And I know you're gonna be loving every minute of it. MW: Back on the subject of Showcase fans, tonight we've got the vile Lord Hile Troy taking on the Focus in a 2 out of 3 falls match, Masher Maccabee and Moses will take on the Highlanders, and the Light Heavyweight title is at stake when Southern Dynamit e steps in the ring with Carlos Maia! -TWO OUT OF THREE FALLS MATCH- *Lord Hile Troy vs. The Focus* CHAD JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for two out of three falls with no time limit. Coming down the aisle... ('Milque Toast' by Helmet plays as the fans cheer) CHAD JOHNSON: Weighing 225 pounds, from parts unknown, The Focus! MW: Here's a man that's made his mark in rings all across the world fans... both competing in the SJPW and AAWA, The Focus has made it known that he's right up there with the likes of Malenko, Benoit, you name it. JM: Speaking of AAWA... MW: Miller... CHAD JOHNSON: And his opponent... ('Ride of the Valkyries' by Wagner plays as the fans boo.) CHAD JOHNSON: Weighing 240 pounds, from Blackpool. England, accompanied by Gold Kard, Lord Hile Troy! MW: Here comes one of the dirtiest players in the game fans! Troy's already gotten a win over The Focus, albeit tainted. JM: You betcha, but a win's a win. (The bell rings as Troy and Focus size each other up) MW: Troy with a chop to the throat! And a series of stomps! JM: The man knows what it takes to take someone out! That's for damn sure! MW: Troy hooking the leg! 1!..2!..Thr! Kickout! (Troy gets to his feet and jerks the Focus up by his hair.) MW: Troy with a fistful of hair JM: I think you mean stubble. MW: And a DRAGON SUPLEX! JM: WOW! He got that move right off the bat! Smart man! (Troy stomps away on The Focus while verbally abusing him) MW: Troy just pounding the Focus into the mat..surfboard applied! And the Focus gets in the ropes! (Troy whips The Focus into the corner, and follows up with a massive lariat.) JM: God! Did you see his head snap! MW: Troy with a devastating clothesline to the former TV Champion, and following it right up with a Bow And Arrow! (The Focus tries to keep his composure before howling in pain as Troy gets an evil smirk across his face.) JM: The man loves his job. MW: Troy with a release and momentum from the ropes...BULLDOG! 1!...2!...Thr! Kickout! (As the Focus gets to his feet, Troy gets a disgusted look on his face and lands a stiff low blow as the ref warns him and the crowd groans.) JM: This man's technical ability is something else! Eh Mike?! MW: After that cheap shot by Troy the Focus is in one of the worst positions that I've ever seen him in.. JM: Aw! C'mon! He's in the perfect position for a man like himself! The fetal position! (laughs) (As Troy approaches the doubled over Focus, he lands a forearm to the back.) MW: Troy with a crushing forearm to the back of The Focus... and The Focus fires off one of his own! Kick to the midsection! DDT! (Crowd pop) JM: What?! MW: That's exactly the type of stamina it's going to take for The Focus to come out on top in that big Triple Cage match at Terror Night 97 folks! JM: Stamina my Baltimorian Butt! He wouldn't have gotten up if he was a *real* man! MW: Focus with a Full-Nelson applied..and Troy gets in the ropes... Irish whip by The Focus... and a big back body drop sends the Lord flying! Focus with a small package! (Gold Kard gets on the apron and begins to yell at The Focus, distracting the ref.) MW: Kard breaking the count... and Troy coming from behind with a rollup of his own! (Kard jumps off the apron.) MW: 1!..2!..3! Focus is pinned! JM: (laughs) (The ref sends both men to their respective corners as the bell rings again.) MW: And Troy 1-ups The Focus early on.. JM: What? And your surprised? (Troy whips The Focus into the corner and charges in) MW: Troy with a head full of steam and a raised knee...and The Focus greets him with a forearm to the face! Irish whip... and a spinebuster lays Troy out! JM: What the Hell?! MW: Focus with a kick to the midsection... and a big DDT! (Lord Hile Troy rolls to the outside, only to be caught by The Focus, and have his head driven into the ringside steps.) MW: And Troy eats steel! Both men in the ring..and The Focus lays on a Texas Cloverleaf! (Troy scrambles into the ropes to break the hold.) MW: Troy breaking the hold...and going for a ride to the corner! Focus up top! JM: NO! (Big pop.) MW: YES! FOCAL POINT! 1!..2!....3! (As Troy lays prone in his corner and slowly gets to his feet, the bell rings for a third time.) MW: Both men have certainly shown what they're capable of here tonight, but it seems that The Focus has quite an advantage now over the clearly shaken Lord Hile Tr... inside leg cradle by Focus! 1!...2! Kickout! JM: Wishful thinking Mike! (Troy whips The Focus to the corner and catches him on the rebound with a side suplex.) MW: Great continuity by Troy..and a series of stomps keeps his man down. Whip to the corner.. (Troy gets atop the turnbuckle and repeatedly punches the Focus in the face to a series of boos.) MW: Troy with those closed fists..and a second rope DDT! JM: Goodnight! MW: Troy hooking the leg..1!..2!..Shoulder up! (The Focus gets to his feet as Troy pounds away on him. After one particularly low blow, he folds like a deck of cards.) MW: Troy with a cheap shot on Focus and the ref is not impressed! Gold Kard up on the apron gets the ref's attention yet again! (While the ref's distracted, Troy pulls The Focus up off the mat by his ear and lands another low blow.) MW: Troy with another low blow! JM: I think The Focus is out cold! This is simply too great! MW: Troy with The Focus...DRAGON SUPLEX! 1!..2!..3! This one's over! CHAD JOHNSON: Ladies and Gentlemen... your winner in 22:38... LORD... HILE... TROOOOYYYYYYYY! ('Ride of the Valkryies' by Wagner plays to massive boos.) MW: There you have it folks. Troy goes into Terror Night with a tainted win... somehow I don't think he'll be able to cheat his way past one Brent Alles in that big stretcher match at Terror Night 97. JM: Speaking of Alles, isn't he the guy that *snapped* your neck in two? (Smirks) MW: (ignoring Miller completely.) We'll be right back after a word from our sponsor. *commercial break* *Masher Maccabee/Moses vs. The Highlanders* CHAD JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the following tag team matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 45 minute time limit. Coming down the aisle... ('Hava Negila' set to a rap beat plays as the fans boo.) CHAD JOHNSON: At a combined weight of 493 pounds, Masher Maccabee and Moses! JM: One of the few reasons to watch this show folks... Yer seein' em here. I have nothing against GKI, but that says nothing. The only men that haven't been Light Heavyweight champions in this fed is Kurt Gerstung and Bob, the janitor with the gimpy leg. CHAD JOHNSON: And their opponents... ('Scotland the Brave' plays as the fans explode) CHAD JOHNSON: At a combined weight of 525 pounds, the CWO World Tag Team Champions, Gavin McCloud, Conor McCloud, Highlanders! MW: And the CWO World Tag Champs make their presence known in style! JM: Just look at those drunken skirt-flashin' doofs. The only good thing that ever came out of Scotland is Wilbur from Mr. Ed. MW: I don't think you'll find many people that agree with you there. Thus far we've seen both the Highlanders and Fergus McTavish, who was injured at the hands of Maccabee come from the great land of Scots. (The bell sounds as Maccabee and Conor lock up.) MW: Conor with a headlock takedown and a release... kick to the ribs, and Maccabee fires off a right! Another! Conor with a kick to the midsection and a stiff uppercut to the chin! (Both men just break into a wild brawl.) MW: Maccabee with a neckbreaker attempt, and Conor comes out with a dropkick! (Maccabee stumbles into Moses for the tag.) MW: Moses with an armbar takeover! 1! Kickout! Moses with a whip... big backdrop! (Conor flies over the ropes into the railing.) JM: WOW! (Moses taunts Gavin as Conor slowly makes his way back in the ring.) MW: Moses up top... and a Moonsault catches Conor as he gets through the ropes! JM: Alllllll skilllllllllll! MW: Moses tagging to Maccabee, and I must say I'm duly impressed by these two men tonight. JM: Mike. I hate to break it to ya, but you'd be impressed by a shiny piece of tinfoil. MW: Whip to the ropes by Maccabee... and there's a tag by Conor! Both Highlanders are just rifling off blows to the dome of GKI's current gold holder! (Maccabee catches the heads of the Highlanders and smashes them together.) MW: Maccabee's certainly taking a risk in a match of this caliber, especially when you consider the fact that he'll have to defend his gold against an as of yet unnamed man at 'Terror Night 97'! JM: Great job plugging Mike. Really subtle. (Maccabee tags to Moses who kicks Gavin in no-man's land. Crowd boos and groans in unison.) MW: Moses with a cheap shot to Gavin McCloud, and a bodyslam lays him on the mat! Moses from the top... THE COMMANDMENT! 1!..2!...3! NO! Shoulder up! (Gavin gets up and roars furiously to the crowd who cheer in response.) MW: Moses landing that devastating Moonsault seems to have done absolutely nothing for him! (Gavin runs off the ropes.) MW: Gavin off the far side...Flying clothesline! Both men get dumped over the ropes! (Maccabee runs into the ring and nails Gavin through the ropes with a baseball slide.) JM: Oh my God! I've never seen someone so scrawny take a hit like that and live! MW: Moses with a steel chair...and brings it down across the back of Gavin! And again! Here comes Conor! (Conor flies over the ropes with a plancha and takes out both GKI members as the ref signals for the bell.) MW: This one's thrown out folks! CHAD JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen... the referee has decided this match... A DOUBLE... COUNTOUT. (Massive boos from fans who suddenly pop as Gavin leaps off the apron with a dropkick to the mug of Moses.) *commercial break* -CWO LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION TITLE MATCH- CHAD JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. It is for the CWO Light Heavyweight Champion title. Coming down the aisle... ('Somebody Slap Me' by John Anderson plays as the fans cheer.) CHAD JOHNSON: Weighing 185 pounds, from Henderson. TN, accompanied by Melanie Jackson, Southern Dynamite! JM: Who said 'Deliverance'?! MW: One of the newest sensations of the CWO making his way to the ring, in hopes of obtaining some gold and respect here tonight. JM: Well, he's got the gold in all 15 of his teeth, but the respect he'll have to work for. He is, after all, your 3rd cousin twice removed, thrice married in, and your dad...Isn't he? CHAD JOHNSON: And his opponent... ('You Give Love a Bad Name' by Bon Jovi plays as the fans boo.) CHAD JOHNSON: Weighing 235 pounds, from Rio de Janeiro. Brazil, accompanied by Joe Root, the CWO Light Heavyweight Champion, 'The Brazilian Lover' Carlos Maia! (The crowd starts to boo as they notice the CWO Light Heavyweight champion, "The Brazilian Lover" Carlos Maia in the aisle entrance. He has the belt around his waist, and Joe Root is at his side.) MAIA: Well, well, well... Pistolerro, Gerstung, and now Dynamite. What a week! And I still have the dumbest of the dumbest, Wayne "I Am A College Gal" Schillo!! (Crowd boos.) MAIA: SHUT UP! Joe, what do you think of my... (grin) "challenge" today? ROOT: Southern Dynamite... I bet you're gonna explode him! (Both laugh.) MAIA: This man is a genius, isn't he? (More boos.) MAIA: Oh, forget about it, you guys are too dumb to see his wisdom! (Insane boos, the crowd starts to throw things on them.) MAIA: Hey, stop it! I'm your damn Light Heavyweight champion! I deserve some respect from your ugly faces!! Ah, screw it! Dynamite, get ready to be DYNAMITED! Schillo, prepare to return to school... better thinking to KINDERGARTEN! (Root raises Maia's arm as the crowd keeps booing and throwing things on them. Maia makes a smooching gesture towards the woman who gives him a blurred-out hand gesture.) MW: (laughing) I see I'm not the only one fed up with Maia's attempts at making passes to the fans. (Bell rings) MW: Dynamite with a forearm smash and a pretzel hold! Reversal by Maia and a whip to the corner! Whip to the ropes by Maia... and a failed Mississippi Burning Necksnap by Dynamite! Frankensteiner by Maia, and Dynamite rolls out of the ring! JM: Now *THIS* is a match Mike! (Maia goes to the outside and begins to brawl with Dynamite) MW: You'd think these two have hated each other since the day they were born! JM: Yeah! YEAH! Me likey! If Maia beats that fruit Schillo into the mat like this, I'll sure as Hell shell out the dough so my nephew can tape it offa PPV! (Dynamite lands a bodyslam on Maia, rolls back in the ring, and stomps away on Maia upon his return.) MW: Dynamite from the top...Flying headbutt! Figure-4 sleeper applied, but Maia's too slippery...Maia from the second buckle.. Flying Axhandle! NO! DYNAMITE HITS WITH A DDT! (Big pop as Dynamite plays to the crowd.) MW: Full Nelson by Dynamite, and a release into a chop to the lower back! Double stomp and a cover! 1!..2!...Kickout! (Dynamite tries to whip Maia to the corner, but it's reversed.) MW: Maia with a head full of steam...MISSISSIPPI BURNING! JM: No! No! NO! (Southern Dynamite plays to the crowd before coming off the 2nd buckle with a flying legdrop.) MW: 1!..2!..3! KICKOUT! That one was too close for Maia's comfort, and that's a guarantee. (Southern Dynamite motions for a double-underhook piledriver, but it's blocked by Maia and turned into a small package.) MW: Small package by Maia! 1! Kickout! Dropkick by Maia, and an Asai Moonsault catches the Southern Boy off guard! JM: Big surprise. Bright lights catch that toad off guard. MW: Maia with a Fisherman's suplex... reversal by Dynamite! 1!...2!..Shoulder up! (Maia gets up flustered only to be greeted by another Mississippi Burning neck snap.) MW: Dynamite opting for an Irish whip instead of a cover...and paying for it through a kneelift by Maia! 1! 2! Kickout! Maia from the top! FALLING IN LOVE! (As Maia lands the Frankensteiner, Dynamite reverses it into a powerbomb and leaves Maia laying dazed on the mat to a huge pop.) MW: ABSOLUTELY AMAZING! JM: MIKE! THIS ISN'T FUNNY! (Wayne Schillo cockily makes his way to the ring) MW: Schillo's here and the fans are on their feet! (Maia slowly gets up and starts to taunt Schillo.) MW: Maia sees Schillo, and he's not pleased one bit! Superkick from behind by Dynamite! REBEL EDGE! 1!...2!..3! (Huge pop.) JM: This is BULL! CHAD JOHNSON: Ladies and Gentlemen... your winner in 10:49... and NEW CWO WORLD LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION! SOUTHERN... DYNAAAAAMIIIIIIITE! ('Somebody Slap Me' by John Anderson plays to huge cheers as Dynamite makes his way to the locker rooms. Maia is left on his back out cold.) MW: Maia, reaping what he's sewn here tonight folks, and losing the title just hours before Terror Night 97! JM: This is BULL Mike! If Maia doesn't get a rematch, SOON, I don't know what's gonna happen, but it's not gonna be nice! (Fadeout.)
