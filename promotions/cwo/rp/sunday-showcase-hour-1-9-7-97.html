SUNDAY SHOWCASE - HOUR #1 - 9/7/97

9 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWO SUNDAY SHOWCASE - HOUR #1 9/7/97 CWO Arena NOTE: This Showcase was taped before last week's Friday Night Fightin', in which Matsuhiro Torunaka wrestled his retirement match. (The letters CWO come up on the screen, and lightning flashes and thunder rumbles.) VOICE-OVER: CWO...the proud force in sports entertainment. ("Numbered Days" by the Mighty Mighty Bosstones plays as a montage of shots fill the screen... the Mercenaries with the Flying Clothesline on Regulator Ice...Sandman Jr. and Kellen give a heated interview, with Sandman swinging the cane for emphasis... Fergus McTavish hits the Highland Fling on Benjamin Blake Bentley... Brian 'The Hammer' Williams nails the Keuka Smack on Chris Myers... The Focus with a Focal Point on Wildfire... and Johnny Reb holding the CWO World Heavyweight Title high in the air.) O'BRIEN: THIS IS SUNDAY SHOWCASE! (The camera cuts to a shot of a jam-packed CWO arena. The fans are waving signs such as GKI IST VINISHED!, FOCUSED ON THE GOLD, and REVEREND AND SANDMAN...THE UNHOLY FUTURE. The camera then goes to the announcers booth, where Chris O'Brien and Jerry Covenant sit. Chris is wearing a Drew Bledsoe jersey and blue jeans, while Jerry is in his trademark sweater and turtleneck!) COVENANT: Welcome, fans, to CWO Sunday Showcase! O'BRIEN: It's great to be here, following a great day of football that ALMOST got us pre-empted. But we pulled some strings and we're here to give you fans some action! COVENANT: We haven't been on in a while due to the pre-season football and the tragedy involving Princess Diana Spencer, but now we're ready for some wrestling....wait...OK, it appears Fergus McTavish is in the ring, and he's demanding a mike. O'BRIEN: This should be good... (The camera cuts to Fergus McTavish, the Whirling Scottish Devil, standing in the ring. Wee Billy is with him, skateboard in hand, and holding a rolled up poster of some sort.) FERGUS: Right then, lissen up ya buncha steamin' piles of yak puke! GKI, ya walk around CWO tinkin' ya own da place an da wrassles. Well, mebbe fer some ya do. But ya doan own me! (Fans cheer) COVENANT: Fergus getting a favorable response tonight. O'BRIEN: He's nailing GKI, Jerry! Hell, you could get cheered if you did that! COVENANT: Thanks, Chris....what? O'BRIEN: I couldn't resist. FERGUS: Now a smart fella knows not ta <bleep> in da wind. Well, GKI, ya've gone an <bleep>ed in da wind of the Whirling Scottish Devil! Ya wants a piece of me? Well, 'ere's what I tink of ya! (Billy proceeds to unroll the poster, revealing it to be a GKI poster. Fergus turns his back to the camera and lifts the front of his kilt. Water hitting the mat can be heard.) COVENANT: Uh, fans....Fergus McTavish appears to be....uh, relieving himself on that GKI poster. O'BRIEN: Relieving, hell! He's <bleep>ing on it! COVENANT: Well, our content rating just went through the roof. O'BRIEN: Hey, this is no worse then NYPD Blue! FERGUS: So GKI, are ya ready ta tangle wif the Whirling Scottish Devil? 'Cause I sure am! Oh, and one last ting... (Fergus turns his back to the crowd and lifts up his kilt, mooning the crowd.) FERGUS: (Over his shoulder) Gets ready for the Whirling Scottish Devil! IT'S NOT OATMEAL! (Billy falls to the ground laughing as the cleanup crew gets the mess out of the ring.) COVENANT: Well, that was certainly interesting. O'BRIEN: GKI falling apart, and now everyone wants to jump in. COVENANT: Let's go down to Chad Johnson for the intros of our opening match here on SUNDAY SHOWCASE! JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is set for one fall with a twenty minute time limit! Currently in the ring, weighing 295 pounds, from Glasgow, Scotland, accompanied to the ring by wee Billy, Fergus McTavish, the Whirling Scottish Devil! O'BRIEN: McTavish in a freshly cleaned ring after that little incident...though by the response of some of the ladies, it wasn't so little, if you know what I mean. COVENANT: No, I don't, Chris. JOHNSON: And his opponent... ('Judgment Day' by Van Halen plays as the fans boo.) JOHNSON: ...weighing 468 pounds, accompanied by Lord Guru, from parts unknown, JUDGEMENT! O'BRIEN: A member of the Cabin set to go up against the strangest man here in CWO as the bell rings and McTavish comes right out with a chop on the big man! And another one! Judgment comes back with a kick, and now a forearm smash, but McTavish comes right back with another chop! And Judgment decides he's had enough and rolls outside the ring. COVENANT: This is going to be good old fashioned smashmouth wrestling tonight, fans, as Fergus McTavish follows Judgment to the floor! Judgment was waiting, though, and catches McTavish in the apron! O'BRIEN: Judgment wailing away...and now he's up on the apron....flying axehandle! 486 pounds behind that move as Judgment rolls back in the ring. COVENANT: And McTavish gets back up and follows him back in! The Whirling Scottish Devil is mad as hell, and he does a little dance in the ring before bounding off the ropes....Judgment catches him with a DDT! O'BRIEN: Judgment going for the pi! Cover, 1...2...thr...shoulder up! McTavish so close to losing early as Judgment stomp away on the shoulder of Fergus McTavish. And now a hammerlock applied by Lord Guru's man. COVENANT: Working on the shoulder ensures The Devil is going to have a hard time hitting that top rope Highland Fling! O'BRIEN: Fergus is bucking down under Judgment's hammerlock....he tries to grab the ropes, and Judgment responds by surprising McTavish with a side suplex! COVENANT: Cover! 1...2...thr...McTavish manages to get his shoulder up! Judgment has ruled this match so far as he pulls McTavish up...wicked chop! And now Judgment is setting the Devil up for the Judgment Call inverted suplex! Judgment has him.....he hits! McTavish slams down with authority as Judgment with a lateral press! 1....2...thr....NO! McTavish manages to get his shoulder up! Billy slamming the side of the ring as Judgment hits another chop! JUDGEMENT (to Fergus): WILLIAM WALLACE WAS A PUSSY, PUNK! O'BRIEN: What, he's trying to make Fergus mad? Whatever, as Judgment puts McTavish to the mat with a double axehandle, and now he reaches down....Fergus with a small package! Cover, 1...2...thr...shoulder up! Fergus McTavish was playing possum, and now he's up and goes for a short clothesline! COVENANT: Judgment steps out of the way, and now he goes for a side suplex, but it's blocked! Fergus with a forearm smash, and Judgment is reeling! Another one! And now a chop! But now Judgment simply pushes McTavish to the ground! O'BRIEN: Judgment stomps a mudhole in Fergus, and now another pin attempt! Cover, but only a one count as Judgment applies a full nelson on McTavish! COVENANT: That's a move you don't see anymore. McTavish jumping around, trying to get to the ropes or knock the big man over, and he does the former! O'BRIEN: Judgment releases the hold, and McTavish, with some spark still left, tries to grab him and whip him into the corner, but Judgment reverses and sends the Whirling Scottish Devil into the corner! And now Judgment goes for a gut punch, but McTavish hits him first! And now Judgment is down! McTavish kicks away....and now a leg hammerlock! COVENANT: McTavish twisting that leg around, and if he can keep the big man off his feet, he's going to walk away with a win, Highland Fling or no Highland Fling. Judgment grunting as McTavish works that leg around. O'BRIEN: McTavish lets go now, and throws Judgment out of the ring! And now Billy hands McTavish a chair! McTavish going for a chair shot, but it's blocked by Judgment! Nice block! COVENANT: The brawling getting more desperate as Judgment throws McTavish in the ring and stomps away. (Judgment plays to the crowd.) O'BRIEN: Judgment wasting time as McTavish gets up and nails Judgment from behind with a double axehandle! And now he spins Judgment around...kick to the gut! And now a DDT! And McTavish is on fire as he pulls Judgment back up by his hair...another kick! And now another DDT....Judgment blocks! COVENANT: McTavish doesn't waste any time, and he kicks Judgment again! European Uppercut sends Judgment outside....and McTavish follows him out again! Judgment has a chair now, and he crushes McTavish with it! O'BRIEN: It's wee Billy from behind....OH NO! He slams that skateboard into the crotch of Judgment! COVENANT: The ref is calling for the bell, as this match has gone out of control. JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, at a time of 10:35, the ref has declared this match a double disqualification! O'BRIEN: McTavish and Billy stomping away on Judgment as we go to our first break! See you in a bit! (Fade to commercial, and then after the break, back to the show.) O'BRIEN: We're back with our next matchup! CWO meets SJW in an interfed match! Let's not waste time as we go down to Chad Johnson! JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the following tag team matchup is set for one fall! Coming down the aisle... (Theme music plays as the fans boo.) JOHNSON: ...at a combined 500 pounds, Australian Assassin and Vegas Villain! O'BRIEN: Two newcomers set to go up against two of Superstar Japan Wrestling. COVENANT: This should be nothing but a high flying extravaganza! Assassin and Villain are simply outclassed. JOHNSON: And their opponents... (Theme music plays as the fans cheer) JOHSON: ...representing SJW, weighing a combined 460 pounds, here are Matsuhiro Torunaka and Hideko Hayabusa! COVENANT: Two of SJW's best show off their skills here tonight! O'BRIEN: I'll be honest, Jerry, I've never watched SJW, so I am very interested in this matchup. Vegas Villain starting against Matsuhiro Torunaka, and Villain comes right out with a punch! But Torunaka comes back with an elbow smash, and now he goes for a surfboard! COVENANT: Torunaka starting off fast as Villain manages to reach the ropes! He's laid out from that surfboard as Matsuhiro goes up top....FLYING SOMERSAULT HEADBUTT! O'BRIEN: MY GOD! That was incredible! COVENANT: Torunaka isn't done yet! He's back up as Villain is groggy...flying senton! O'BRIEN: And he still is going to the well! Torunaka drags Villain to the center of the ring, and goes back up....he misses with a swan dive headbutt as Villain rolls out of the way! And now Vegas Villain goes for a DDT, but Torunaka gets out of that easily. COVENANT: And Villain gets whipped to the corner for his troubles! He comes out....Torunaka going for an abdominal stretch, but Villain avoids that, and now it's his turn to whips Torunaka! Vegas Villain goes for a kick, but Torunaka stops that with a double axehandle! And now Torunaka up top yet again....flying bodypress! Cover, one, two, kick out! O'BRIEN: Torunaka really impressing me as he hurls Vegas Villain out of the ring, and now....HE GOES OVER THE ROPES AND HITS A FLYING CLOTHESLINE! WOW! COVENANT: Torunaka rolling Villain back into the ring, and Villain hits Torunaka with an elbowsmash! And now he tags out to Australian Assassin, who double team Torunaka with a double body slam! O'BRIEN: Assassin whips Matsuhiro Torunaka into a corner, and follows up with a brutal double axehandle. ASSASSIN (to Torunaka): AIN'T SO TOUGH NOW, ARE YA? COVENANT: Torunaka's been in the ring for this whole matchup so far, and needs to make a tag, and Assassin goes into the corner....Torunaka responds with a hard chop! And now a dropkick! A tag to Hideko Hayabusa now, who goes up top.....FROG SPLASH! But Assassin gets his knees up! O'BRIEN: An eye gouge now, and a tag back to Vegas Villain, who comes in with an elbowdrop! And now another one...Hayabusa rolls out of the way! BACK BRAIN KICK! Out of nowhere! And a cover, one, two....Hayabusa has his feet on the ropes...THREE! SJW wins! JOHSON: At a time of 5:20, your winners....MATSUHIRO TORUNAKA AND HIDEKO HAYABUSA! COVENANT: A superb display of athleticism by SJW, with Hayabusa getting a measure of revenge by cheating to get the win! O'BRIEN: A win is a win, Jerry. We're on a break now, but coming up next, El Grande Pistolerro takes on Diamondback Chris Myers! Don't miss it! (When we come back from commercial, Chad Johnson is standing in the ring.) JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is set for one fall with a 20 minute time limit! Coming down the aisle... (Theme music plays as the fans cheer) JOHNSON: ...weighing 240 pounds, from Mexico City, accompanied by Tamara Wilcox, El Grande Pistolerro! O'BRIEN: The great light heavyweight El Grande Pistolerro makes his way to the ring in what is sure to be a great match up! JOHNSON: And his opponent... ('Shoots and Ladders' by Korn plays as the fans cheer) JOHNSON: weighing 275 pounds, from Phoenix, AZ, accompanied by Dallas, Diamondback Chris Myers! O'BRIEN: Myers carrying that hockey stick with him that he plans to use on the Reverend at SEPTEMBER SLAM! Remember, fans, don't forget to bring your own weapon! COVENANT: Just as I feared....CWO is going the extreme route. O'BRIEN: ARE NOT! Myers just has a temper and made a rash statement. I'm sure Commissioner Krepps had a nice talk with Chris. COVENANT: Myers gives the stick to Dallas as the bell rings! Collar and elbow lockup....Myers going for an armbar, but Pistolerro counters nicely by whipping Myers to the ropes....Myers with a Frankensteiner! O'BRIEN: And now Myers whips Pistolerro into the corner with velocity! Pistolerro bounces out, and Myers with another Frankensteiner! And Pistolerro is up....dropkick by Chris Myers! COVENANT: Myers leading on points early on...and Myers tries to go back to that armbar, but Pistolerro counters with another whip...CLOTHESLINE! And Myers staggers...he's over the ropes! O'BRIEN: Pistolerro on the apron....he goes for a flying axehandle, but Myers moves, and Pistolerro hits the railing! And now....Myers has a chair? COVENANT: A little practice for September 28th, and Myers swings, but Pistolerro has a chair of his own! Both men hit chairs, and now both men roll back into the ring. O'BRIEN: Even in his matchups with Serpentmaster and Bentley, I've never seen Chris Myers go for a chair. The Reverend must have really messed up his brain. COVENANT: Myers is wearing brown and green wraparound shades on doctor's orders as Pistolerro with a shoulder block! And now a karate kick! O'BRIEN: EL Grande Pistolerro with a rollup attempt, but Myers reverses! 1...Pistolerro reverses! 1...Myers re-reverses! 1...kick out by Pistolerro. And now Myers goes for ANOTHER armbar, but Pistolerro simply pushes Myers down to the mat! COVENANT: Pistolerro going up top now...flying clothesline on Myers! Pistolerro covers! One...two...thr...Myers kicks out! So close, and now Myers goes for a dropkick that sends Pistolerro flying! O'BRIEN: Hubba hubba! Tamara Wilcox on the apron, and Myers is distracted momentarily as Pistolerro nails him with a karate kick! And now Pistolerro has Myers on the ground...and Chris rolls out of the way of an elbowdrop! And Myers tries that armbar once again, but Pistolerro whips Myers...no, it's reversed, and Myers sends Pistolerro into the corner! And now Myers goes for an avalanche, but a chop stops him cold! And now a headlock by Pistolerro, and Myers reaches the ropes. COVENANT: Myers sends Pistolerro bounding off, but Pistolerro comes back with a karate thrust! And a superkick takes Myers off his feet! And Pistolerro climbs up top....FLYING ELBOW! O'BRIEN: And now Dallas distracts Pistolerro....Myers with a small package from behind! 1...2...shoulder up! MYERS (to Pistolerro): VIVA LA RAZA THIS! COVENANT: Myers with some what of an attitude tonight as he whips El Grande into the ropes....dropkick sends Pistolerro into the referee! The ref is down as Pistolerro is wobbly.....RATTLER! Chris Myers hits that devastating sideways DDT and covers....no ref! The ref is still down, and Myers is slapping the mat! (The lights suddenly flicker!) O'BRIEN: Oh crud....THE SANDMAN IS COMING UP THROUGH THE RING! Myers doesn't see him...Sandman with a belly-to-back suplex! And Myers comes down HARD! Oh no....Sandman has Myers on the top turnbuckle....HE DELIVERS A SANDBLAST TO CHRIS MYERS! The Sandman is using Myers to send a message to Sandman Jr.! COVENANT: LOOK! It's Sandman Jr. hitting the ring! He's got that cane, and Sandman retreats into the ring! Pistolerro covers an unconscious Myers, and the ref is just conscious enough to count! One, two, three! JOHNSON: The winner of this match, at 8:51, EL GRANDE PISTOLERRO! O'BRIEN: Sandman, Jr. and Dallas trying to help Myers up, who's lucky is wasn't the Reverend with another flash to the face! COVENANT: Fans, stick around for hour #2! END HOUR #1
