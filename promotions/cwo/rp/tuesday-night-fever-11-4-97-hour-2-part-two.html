TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER 11/4/97 - HOUR #2 - PART TWO

4 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(When we come back from commercial, Johnny Starr is standing by the entranceway, microphone in hand.) STARR: Ladies and gentlemen, would you please put your hands together and help me welcome two members of the Riders on the Storm, here are Dallas and "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum! (The sound of jets flying overhead gives way to "Hero" by Ministry as Dallas walks out, wearing black jeans and a CWO denim shirt. Next, out steps Mark Fulcrum, wearing the trademark leather bomber jacket over a Pantera T-shirt. Both Riders step up to Starr as fireworks go off behind them.) MELINN: And the Riders are in the house! FERRARA: Notable by his absence, however, is the leader of the Riders, one Diamondback Chris Myers, who was brutally attacked at TERROR NIGHT. THOMAS: No he wasn't! FERRARA: Rebel Yell cracked him with a chair! THOMAS: No he didn't! Yell was getting ready to unfold the chair to sit down and that idiot Myers ran into it! FERRARA: Why do I bother Kyle? MELINN: I don't know. And for that matter, where are the Wild Cards? One would guess they would be here too. STARR: OK, Dallas, "Airborne", I guess the big story is what happened this past Sunday at TERROR NIGHT over in Detroit. As everyone can see, Chris Myers isn't here tonight, because of what happened at TERROR NIGHT. If we could roll the footage... (Footage comes up on the screen from the World Title match between Myers and Johnny Reb. Myers was on the receiving end of a Reb Frankensteiner, and rolled out of the ring, when Rebel Yell picked up a steel chair and clocked Chris with it.) STARR: As we can see right here, the trainer of Johnny Reb, former CWO World Heavyweight champ Rebel Yell, nailed Chris with a steel chair. And then, Yell rolled him back in the ring, and Reb applied the Texas Cloverleaf to Chris, who was unconscious from that chair strike. (Reb is shown holding on the Cloverleaf on an obviously unconscious Chris Myers, with Dallas looking on, her face twisted with worry. Rebel Yell is shown approaching her, white towel in hand.) YELL: Honey, he's going to break that leg! (Rebel Yell hands Dallas the towel) YELL: You better throw that towel in, honey, or else your man is out of this sport forever! (Dallas looks at the towel, and throws it in. Yell and General Lee get in the ring to celebrate with Johnny as the crowd throws objects in and Dallas looks over Chris.) STARR: You obviously had no choice, and did the only thing you could do, Dallas. The question is... where is Chris Myers tonight? DALLAS: Johnny, what a lot of people didn't see was Chris getting loaded into an ambulance after that match. That strike with the chair gave Chris knocked him out, and the Cloverleaf strained his right leg. What a lot of people also don't know is Chris has torn tendons in his right leg before, and the fact he couldn't fight that Texas Cloverleaf just put more pressure on that leg. STARR: So Chris re-tore those tendons? DALLAS: Oh, no! Chris tore them when he was 13 and just starting to wrestle in school. The leg has been fine the past 10 years, but what Reb did didn't exactly tickle, if you know what I mean. To answer your original question, Johnny, Chris is up in Detroit General Hospital right now. The doctors looked at his leg and made sure he didn't have a concussion. His head is fine, and so is his leg. He'll be out tomorrow morning and ready to wrestle. And for that matter, Ace and Deck are there with him also. They wanted to be here tonight, Johnny, but I insisted they stay with Chris. Wild Cards, Chris, if you're watching we'll see you soon. STARR: OK, Dallas, now if I may turn to this young man... (Mark Fulcrum steps up) STARR: ...the newest member of the Riders, Mark Fulcrum. Now, Mark, you've been in CWO for little over a month now, coming in right after SEPTEMBER SLAM. In that time... well, you don't exactly have the best record, if I may be so honest. FULCRUM: You may. STARR: You've suffered losses to Moses, Skyhawk, Red Raven...and even earlier this evening, you lost to Wrath from the Deadly Sins. Now, you talked about being a big player in the light heavyweight division here in CWO. But right now, you're not exactly the force to be reckoned with. What are your thoughts on that? MELINN: And Starr going right after Mark with that question! FULCRUM: Well, Mr. Starr, I'd be lying if I said I was proud so far with what I've done so far here in this fine federation. I've been losing left and right, and for the most part, I've been proving I can put my foot in my mouth. THOMAS: Hey, Ferrara, can you do that? FERRARA: What? No! THOMAS: Damn, that would be a neat trick to see... MELINN: And once again, we apologize for the Truth's comments. FULCRUM: And earlier tonight, with the world watching and my good friend in the hospital, I came out here and got my ass kicked by Wrath. Well, Wrath, you beat me. What can I say? The Cards have something they want to say about the sins in a second, but what I want to talk about for a second is Johnny Reb, that lowdown, stinking, loudmouth, rebel jackass of a World champ. Reb... when was the last time you took a good, long look in the mirror? Throughout the match this past Sunday, all I kept hearing was "the old Reb would never do that" or "and Reb cheats, something we haven't seen before". So, you're champ. Oh, goody, you've worked your butt off for a long time here, and you finally get the gold. But how did you get it? By turning on your best friend and stabbing him in the back. And how have you defended that belt? By cheating and having that goon Rebel Yell back you up. How can you stand to look at yourself? Myers threw everything including the kitchen sink at you last night. Reb, you should have been pinned one, two, three, and my friend should be walking around with a World championship around his waist. But you won, and you kept it. Does that belt mean so much to you, Reb, that you were willing to turn your back on your morals, your beliefs, your friends, and your fans? Does it mean that much? God, I hope not. Reb, that belt is all you have. And when you lose it... that's it. You won't have anything left. But before that happens, I want you to take a good look at everything. And you're going to do that. By facing me. (The crowd goes nuts as Starr looks shocked.) STARR: What? Are you challenging Johnny Reb? FULCRUM: Damn straight. Reb, I don't care about that belt. Hell, I don't even want to TOUCH that thing after you've had your hands all over it. But I do want you. Non-title, of course. You see, Johnny, when you put Chris in the hospital, you attacked a brother. The Riders are family, Reb. And I'm not going to sit around and watch you just walk around smirking. So I'm going to get you in this ring, and I'm going to have the Cards and Dallas behind me so Lee and Yell can't interfere, and I'm going to fly rings around you. They don't call me "Airborne" for nothing. Reb, next week, on FEVER, I want you in that ring, non-title, man to man. And we'll see how much of a man you really are. (Mark throws his hand in the air and gives the Riders sign as "Hero" starts up again.) STARR: There you have it, ladies and gentlemen, hopefully Tom Krepps can confirm this match for us later tonight! Don't go away, Television Championship coming up! (Logo and music come up as we fade to commercial.) Check out the latest e-mail wrestling league and Join for FREE! URL is http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Field/3523/wwa.html you will receive your wrestler's results e-mailed to you and the complete rankings! Join today! (Back to the show, as Tony the K is in the ring along with head referee John Myering.) TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall with television time remaining. It is for the CWO Television Championship. Introducing first, coming down the aisle.... ("Gods of War" by Def Leppard plays as the fans cheer the entrance of Skyhawk.) TONY THE K: Weighing 290 pounds, from Philadelphia, PA, accompanied to the ring by Johnny B. Goode, Skyhawk! (Skyhawk and Johnny B. Goode make their way down to the ring. Goode wears a T-shirt that says, "I'M BACK!" on it.) MELINN: Nice to see Johnny B. Goode back here in the CWO, after that vicious attack perpetrated by the Cabin only a few months ago. THOMAS: I'm just on cloud nine. TONY THE K: And his opponents.... ("Graveyard Symphony" plays as the lights go off, the crowd booing.) TONY THE K: Weighing 328 pounds, from Death Valley, CA, accompanied by the Reverend, Bloodbath! (A single spotlight illuminates Bloodbath and the mysterious robed Reverend as they make their way down to the ring.) FERRARA: The Reverend leads his newest dark minion Bloodbath down to the ring, and Bloodbath and the Reverend's Church were very successful in their attempt to take down members of Mondo Gecko's team, The Focus had to be hospitalized following the event, and Wildfire has not been seen since then. THOMAS: The Reverend is doing a service, just like Jack Kevorkian! MELINN: What?!? Anyway, the Unit has to be pleased to finally have some CWO gold in their grasp, as the Newton Bros. beat the World Tag Team Champions Highlanders for the titles at TERROR NIGHT '97. THOMAS: Yeah, and now the Highlanders are nothing but a couple of paranoid freaks! Pathetic! FERRARA: And the Unit would definitely be glad for Skyhawk to get the TV title, and add some more gold to their group. MELINN: And here we go, the two big men going toe to toe! Skyhawk has Bloodbath in position... oh my goodness! A jack-knife power bomb! A jack-knife power bomb to the Television champion! THOMAS: Holy cow! MELINN: Skyhawk with a legdrop! And now Skyhawk goes for a falling splash... but Bloodbath gets his knees up! (Bloodbath slowly rises to his feet.) FERRARA: Skyhawk definitely well conditioned, as are all the Unit members, Johnny B. Goode and Toni Newton see to that, and Skyhawk is back on the attack. MELINN: Skyhawk with a hard right hand, and now he's got Bloodbath up in the horizontal backbreaker! THOMAS: I don't believe it! MELINN: Skyhawk certainly showing his power early on in this match, but he can't keep the lanky Bloodbath up there for long. Skyhawk now whips Bloodbath into the corner... he moves in but Bloodbath nails him with a chop! (Bloodbath raises his arms to the crowd, as they boo him.) FERRARA: Bloodbath not getting any crowd approval since turning to the dark side, and becoming a dark minion of the Reverend. MELINN: Skyhawk is still unrelentless, though, he hits Bloodbath with a double axhandle, and gets him up in the horizontal backbreaker again! THOMAS: He's going to hurt his back doing that! MELINN: Again, Skyhawk not able to keep Bloodbath up in the air very long in that hold, he lets it go and nails Bloodbath with an elbow smash. Bloodbath runs into the ropes... and a double clothesline! FERRARA: One thing about Bloodbath, he may have turned to the dark side, but that same evil force that seems to make Sandman nigh invulnerable seems to be affecting him now as well. THOMAS: That's not good news for anybody. MELINN: Both men getting back to their feet before referee John Myering can reach a 10 count... and Skyhawk with a gutwrench suplex! Cover, 1... 2... thr... and Bloodbath kicks out with authority! (Bloodbath gets right back to his feet.) FERRARA: And even Skyhawk is a bit taken aback at Bloodbath's presence, it has to be an eerie experience facing this guy. THOMAS: I'll say. MELINN: A very intense power match so far, as Bloodbath whips Skyhawk into the corner... Bloodbath moves in but Skyhawk is up and over with a sunset flip! 1... 2... shoulder up! A backflip cradle, 1... 2... kickout! FERRARA: Amazing agility on the part of the big man, he is definitely going places here in the CWO. MELINN: Skyhawk goes for another sunset flip... and Bloodbath counters with a sit-down! THOMAS: Hello! MELINN: 328 pounds of Bloodbath crashing down on Skyhawk, 1... 2... and Skyhawk brings his legs up and take Bloodbath down to the mat! 1... 2... and Bloodbath is in the ropes. FERRARA: Great presence of mind there by Skyhawk, even after being crushed by Bloodbath. MELINN: Skyhawk going for a Samoan Drop but Bloodbath gets out of it and applies a headlock. THOMAS: Bloodbath likes to wrestle this kind of match, slow and deliberate. MELINN: Referee asking Skyhawk if he wants to give it up, I don't think you're going to be seeing that tonight. Bloodbath lets go of the hold, rollup, 1... 2... kickout. (Reverend gets in the face of Skyhawk.) REVEREND: JOIN US! SET YOURSELF FREE! MELINN: Skyhawk being verbally assaulted by the Reverend, and now Bloodbath is going up top... flying fist to the head! (Bloodbath raises his arms to the crowd, as they boo.) MELINN: Skyhawk still getting back to his feet, amazing endurance on his part, he hits Bloodbath with an elbow smash, he's got him in position... power bomb! THOMAS: Again, unbelievable! FERRARA: Both of these men have been throwing each other around all over the place! MELINN: Skyhawk goes for a legdrop... and Bloodbath rolls out of the way! (The Reverend is shown laughing.) MELINN: What a truly evil character. THOMAS: You can say that again! He gives me the heebie-jeebies! MELINN: Bloodbath goes for a headlock, Skyhawk counters with an elbow to the ribs... sunset flip... he's trying to get Bloodbath over... Bloodbath tries to punch him in the face... Skyhawk moves out of the way! (Bloodbath holds his hand in pain, although his face shows emotion.) THOMAS: Man, doesn't this guy feel anything? MELINN: Skyhawk going for a bodyslam but Bloodbath counters with a clubbing forearm to the back. And now Bloodbath has Skyhawk in position... and a piledriver! FERRARA: Awesome power on the part of Bloodbath, and you can actually see that the flamboyant Johnny B. Goode, manager of the Unit, is actually concerned for his man now. THOMAS: He should be! MELINN: Bloodbath going for the figure four... Skyhawk kicks him in the face. Skyhawk back on his feet... sunset flip... again he's trying to get him over... Bloodbath tries to come down with a sit-down, but again Skyhawk moves out of the way! FERRARA: Skyhawk proving to be one step ahead of the game several times tonight, he is ready to win that Television title! MELINN: Skyhawk goes for the pin, 1... shoulder up. Now he picks Bloodbath up from the canvas, going for a DDT... Bloodbath blocks it... and Bloodbath with a back body drop! THOMAS: That didn't work! (Bloodbath raises his arms to the crowd, again getting more boos.) MELINN: But look at Skyhawk, still fighting his heart out, he nails Bloodbath with a chop, sunset flip... and Bloodbath with another sit-down! THOMAS: Hey, quit using that move, idiot! MELINN: Cover by Bloodbath, 1... 2... and Skyhawk brings his legs up and takes Bloodbath down! 1... 2... kickout. FERRARA: Skyhawk actually using a great deal of mat wrestling to confound the Television Champion, and it may be working so far. MELINN: Skyhawk with a vertical suplex! Cover, 1... 2... thr... no, so close, but Bloodbath gets his shoulder up. Skyhawk with another cover, 1... and Bloodbath puts his foot on the ropes. FERRARA: Skyhawk must be getting a little frustrated here, he's done everything in his power to put Bloodbath down, and still the dark minion keeps coming back. MELINN: Skyhawk going for the horizontal backbreaker again... he gets Bloodbath up in it again! FERRARA: Skyhawk really going after the back of Bloodbath, but again the frame of Bloodbath proves too much to keep up in the horizontal backbreaker for long. THOMAS: What is this, the World's Strongest Man competition? Wrestle him! MELINN: Skyhawk whips Bloodbath into the ropes... and another double clothesline! FERRARA: This match slowly settling into a pattern, as both men dish out the punishment, but neither one can gain a clear advantage. MELINN: Both men again getting to their feet, what a match this has been so far... Skyhawk with a hard right hand... he tries to whip Bloodbath into the ropes but it's reversed... Bloodbath with a clothesline! THOMAS: But Skyhawk's still up! Look at his eyes! MELINN: The blaze of determination in the eyes of Skyhawk, and he whips Bloodbath into the ropes... legsweep! FERRARA: And Bloodbath is back on his feet! MELINN: Whip into the ropes again by Skyhawk... and a powerslam! FERRARA: And again Bloodbath gets right back to his feet! MELINN: A look of concern on the face of Skyhawk, he indeed has to be wondering what he has to do next. And now Skyhawk runs into the ropes... and Bloodbath with the DDT! THOMAS: He planted him with that one! MELINN: Bloodbath with a rollup, but Skyhawk is out of it before a 1 count. FERRARA: Bloodbath really hesitated there before going for the pin, sometimes it seems that he's in an extreme inner turmoil, like he may be trying to fight the control of the Reverend. THOMAS: Man, this is getting too Sci-fi Channel for me. MELINN: Skyhawk back to his feet, he whips Bloodbath into the corner... oh no! Bloodbath collides with John Myering, and Myering goes down! THOMAS: Oh, great! FERRARA: This might not be good for Skyhawk, the other members of the Unit hopefully are still in the building, in case any of the other members of the Church decide to attack! MELINN: Bloodbath runs into the ropes... Skyhawk with a hard right hand! And he picks the big man up... bodyslam! And Bloodbath rolls out of the ring. THOMAS: It's like two mastodons colliding in there! MELINN: Skyhawk waiting in the ring for Bloodbath, he's not going to take any chances for any tricks by the Reverend or his Church, and now Bloodbath rolls back in the ring. Skyhawk with a clubbing double axhandle, and a cover. (Skyhawk slaps 1... 2... 3.) FERRARA: Well, Skyhawk may have counted it, but John Myering is still writhing around on the canvas after colliding with Bloodbath a few moments ago. THOMAS: This is ridiculous! MELINN: Skhyawk picks Bloodbath up... and a tremendous kneelift! And now Skyhawk is going up top! FERRARA: We could see the Skydive here! MELINN: Skyhawk... backflip splash! The Skydive indeed! He's got the cover, referee John Myering just has to count the pinfall.... (Crowd reacts.) MELINN: Wait a minute... oh my god! It's Wildfire coming up through the ring! THOMAS: Ah! FERRARA: Wildfire is coming up through the ring, he had not been seen after Terror Night, and his face is a mask of crimson and white! THOMAS: Get me out of here! MELINN: Referee John Myering counting 1... 2.... (Wildfire hits Skyhawk with the baseball bat he carries.) MELINN: What?!? FERRARA: Oh, no, not Wildfire.... THOMAS: The Reverend said that he was going to capture more souls, and Wildfire might be one of them! MELINN: Bloodbath is not moving, not after that backflip splash by Skyhawk, and now Skyhawk is out cold, after being hit by a baseball bat. (Wildfire rolls out of the ring.) JOHNNY B. GOODE (getting in Wildfire's face): WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU DOING?!? (Wildfire shoves him aside with the bat. He then goes after the Reverend as the crowd goes wild.) MELINN: And the Reverend doesn't want any part of Wildfire, especially when he's got that baseball bat! (The Reverend heads back to the dressing room. Wildfire grabs the Television Championship belt away from its position at ringside, and puts it around his waist.) THOMAS: Thief! Thief! (Wildfire walks back to the dressing room, the Television belt around his waist.) TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of this contest in 11:39 as a result of a disqualification, Skyhawk. Bloodbath keeps the Television Championship. THOMAS: He may have kept it, but Wildfire's got it, that criminal! MELINN: Ladies and gentlemen, we have just witnessed an incredible turn of events... I'm not sure what exactly is going on, but hopefully we can get to the bottom of it, but we are not done yet, the World Champion speaks when we come back! (Logo and music come up as we fade to commercial. When we come back, Johnny Starr stands alone in the ring.) JOHNNY STARR: Fans, in what has become an all too common occurrence in the CWO lately, Johnny Reb was able to retain his CWO World Heavyweight Championship with a little help from his friends. Ladies and gentleman, allow me to introduce at this time, the CWO Heavyweight Champion of the World, JOHNNY REB! ("Sweet Home Alabama" by Lynyrd Skynyrd plays and a mixed pop, leaning more towards boos is heard as Johnny Reb makes his way to the ring. He's accompanied by his manager, General Lee, his trainer, former CWO World Heavyweight Champion, Rebel Yell, and Mason-Dixon.) STARR: You must be very proud of yourself Johnny, very proud indeed with the manner that you were able to pull out that victory at Terror Night over Chris Myers. Your thoughts, on that match. JOHNNY REB: Like y'all said Mistuh Starr, ah am VERY proud indeed with mah victory. Ah made Chris Myers submit to the best damn finisher in the sport. (Chorus of boos is heard.) STARR: Oh come on!! Rebel Yell waffled him with a chair! REB: Now let there be NO doubt about who the Undisputed Champion of the CWO is. Ah've taken on all comers. Not that there have been many lately. For the second week in a row, no yella bellied coward wants to challenge me for mah belt! Well, ah have he ard the whispers going round... oh, they say 'Reb's a papuh Champion. He ain't fought nobody.' Well to that ah say, who else do ah have to beat? Every top name on the roster has fallen to me at some tahm. The list goes on. Rich Clark, Sandman, Gold K ard, Joe Eagle, and now you too, Chris Myers. Y'all have fallen to Johnny Reb. Nobody is gonna take this belt from me. Y'all wanna try, then step on up and sign the line. Johnny Reb ducks NOBODY! LEE: That's Raht! Mah man has been the champion of this here federation longuh than any othuh! He is Undisputed, he is the greatest of all tahm. So now he goes to cement himself as a true legend in this here sport. Bring anyone to the ring comissionuh , and they too shall fall lahk the rest. STARR: Do you honestly think you have it in you to back up this talk, Johnny Reb? What about Foolkiller? What about someone like Mondo Gecko? Do you think you've proved everything you have to prove? REB: Mistuh Starr, whether y'all hate me, or whether y'all love me, y'all bettuh recognize that ah am the best thing goin' in the CWO! (Crowd reacts as Tom Krepps comes down to the ring and gets in.) STARR: Ladies and gentlemen, the Commissioner of the CWO, Tom Krepps. TOM KREPPS: First of all, let me just say that I think it's a disgrace that our World Champion, the highest symbol of our organization, behaves in a manner like you do, Johnny Reb. (Crowd cheers.) REB: Well, let me tell ya somethin', old man, whether you like it, or you don't like it, this is the new and improved Johnny Reb. I toiled for too long, always being a bridesmaid, and now ah'm the World Champion, and there ain't nothin' anybody can do about it. KREPPS: Well, let me tell you, I've made a few decisions that I'd like to inform everybody about before we leave tonight. First of all, you want to play rough with Diamondback Chris Myers, that's fine. Because next week, you're going to take on his best friend "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum, and even though it's a non-title match, I'm pretty sure that he's gonna kick your ass! (Crowd cheers.) KREPPS: Now, as for the matter of who's going to be your next challenger for the title, the #1 contender that will take you on at NOVEMBER PAIN '97 on November 23, 1997, in Wembley Stadium in London, England. And I just heard you rattle off a bunch of names of people that you've beaten, people you don't think are "worthy" enough to take you on for the title. Well, Mr. Reb, I have a name for you: the #1 contender to your title, who will take you on at NOVEMBER PAIN '97... the CWO "hard-core" icon... Sandman Jr.! (Crowd goes absolutely nuts as "Enter Sandman" by the Mighty Mighty Bosstones begins to play.) THOMAS: What?!? MELINN: How about that! (Sandman Jr. bursts through the curtain along with Kellen. She hands him a Coke, which he shotguns and then slams it against his head, opening already established cuts from TERROR NIGHT '97. He raises his cane in the air as the crowd roars.) REB: Well, well, well. So ah get to take on the sickest freak in the federation. Let me tell you something, "junior," this man standing beside me, Rebel Yell, represents tradition here in the CWO. Something you wouldn't know a hell of a lot about. Ah am the World Champion because ah am the greatest WRESTLER in this federation. Ah don't mess around with barbed wire, ah don't fight people 20 feet above the ring, ah beat their asses right here in the squared circle. And when November 23 rolls around, pal, you're just the next carcass that ah'm gonna leave in the ring. (Sandman Jr. has got a mike.) SANDMAN JR.: Shut your fried chicken eatin', sister-sleepin' with, pickup-drivin' mouth! (Crowd cheers.) SANDMAN JR.: Johnny Reb... I've only got one thing to say... prepare for the World Championship to go... HARD-CORE! (Crowd cheers again as Sandman Jr. thrusts his cane in the air, Reb acting in disgust.) MELINN: Ladies and gentlemen, there you have it, Sandman Jr. vs. Johnny Reb, NOVEMBER PAIN '97, we have to go, we'll see you next week! (Closing creds come up as we fade out.)
