CWO EYE ON THE STORM: CWO CELEBRATION

15 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWO EYE ON THE STORM: CWO CELEBRATION "LIVE" FROM THE VAN ANDEL ARENA, GRAND RAPIDS, MI 12/15/96 (EYE ON THE STORM graphic and music comes up as we go to a roaring crowd in the Van Andel Arena as a pyrotechnic display goes off. We go to "Killer" Kyle Melinn and David "The Truth" Thomas.) "KILLER" KYLE MELINN: Hello, everybody, and welcome to EYE ON THE STORM: CWO CELEBRATION! We are a half an hour away from the one that may prove to be our last, or the first of many more! I am "Killer" Kyle Melinn, and with me as always, David "The Trut h" Thomas! DAVID "THE TRUTH" THOMAS: The only one of us who WILL have a job after tonight! (Laughs.) MELINN: That remains to be seen, however, we are ready for our first match. Let's go to the ring. (Tony the K is in the ring, along with referee John Myering.) TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following tag team matchup is scheduled for one fall. Coming down the aisle... ("Slam" by Onyx plays as the fans boo the entrance of D. E. A. T. H.) TONY THE K: At a combined weight of 585 pounds, accompanied by their manager Da Boss, Benton and Green, D. E. A. T. H.! (D. E. A. T. H. comes down to the ring antagonizing the fans.) TONY THE K: And their opponents... ("Walking in LA" by Missing Persons plays as the fans cheer the entrance of Hollywood Muscle.) TONY THE K: At a combined weight of 480 pounds, Steve Keller and Wayne Carpenter, Hollywood Muscle! (Hollywood Muscle comes down to the ring slapping hands.) THOMAS (dryly): I'm surprised they could take time out from their fan mail for this match. MELINN: Blood Inc. and the Four Aces slowly developing a rivalry, this match here on EYE ON THE STORM a latest in the series. We are ready to go, Benton starting against Wayne Carpenter, and Carpenter attacks right off the bat with a body slam! Cover, 1 ... 2... thr... and Benton just gets his foot on the ropes! THOMAS: I have to give him credit, Carpenter had Benton off-guard there. However, that won't last. The Hollywood Muscle refers to the one in their head blocking off the brain membrane. MELINN: Carpenter goes for a vertical suplex but it's blocked. Benton now with an armbar applied... Carpenter gets out of it by punching Benton in the face, whips him into the corner... Carpenter slams Benton's head into the turnbuckle! Benton is down, cover by Carpenter, 1... 2... shoulder up. Whip into the ropes... double clothesline! Both wrestlers are down! THOMAS: Desperation move there by Benton, and it worked, Carpenter felt the same impact. MELINN: But Carpenter is up first, he nails Benton with a legdrive. Figure four applied... Benton reaches the ropes. Tag to Steve Keller by Carpenter, Keller with a forearm smash, stomping away on Benton, cover, 1... kickout. Headscissors applied... Be nton rolls out of it, both men on their feet, Benton whips Keller into the corner... nails him with a forearm to the head. Tag to Green, double body slam by D. E. A. T. H. on Keller. THOMAS: That's what they've got to do, use those gang-bangin' tactics on the pretty boys, they'll crumble. MELINN: Green with a chinlock applied... Keller gets out of it, whips Green into the ropes... cross body block! Elbow smash, and now a sleeper applied... Benton gets out if with a jawbreaker! THOMAS: Where's Isaac Yankem when you need him? MELINN: Green up top... flying clothesline! Boston crab applied... Keller reaches the ropes. Green whips Keller into the ropes... big boot to the face! Keller rolls outside the ring. THOMAS: That's the best move he's made all match! Now head back to the dressing room and it will be even better! MELINN: Both teams brawling outside the ring, Keller is back inside the ring now, trying to stomp on Green, but Green's got his leg! Oh no! Green just slammed Keller's leg into the steel ringpost! He could have hobbled him there! THOMAS: Definitely. And smart tactics there, hobble the faster duo, both of them light heavyweights, like I've said before, there's nothing beating street smarts. MELINN: Green tags to Benton, D. E. A. T. H. whips Keller into the ropes, going for a double back body drop... but Keller comes back with a double noggin knocker! Crowd responding well to that one! THOMAS: Well, D. E. A. T. H. telegraphed that doubleteam a little there, honest mistake. MELINN: Green rolls outside the ring, Keller with an abdominal stretch on Benton... Benton counters with a hiptoss. Whip into the ropes... spinning roundhouse kick! Fireman's carry takedown and a cover, 1... kickout. Benton up top... flying clothesline ! Cover, 1... and Keller is in the ropes. THOMAS: Luck. MELINN: Benton up top again, though, flying clothesline! A rake to the eyes, and back up top a third time... another flying clothesline! A rake of the eyes, and a tag to Green. THOMAS: Those series of flying clotheslines really debilitating to Steve Keller, you can see him trying to regain his senses. MELINN: Green goes for a neckbreaker, but it's reversed by Keller! And Keller crawling to make the tag to Carpenter... he does! (Crowd pop.) MELINN: Carpenter in, cleaning house! Body slam, headscissors applied... Green reaches the ropes. Chop by Carpenter, followed by a backdrop! Tag back to Keller, and Keller now with a figure four applied. THOMAS: The Muscle has been working well in this match, I'm surprised. I'm sure they'll screw it up any minute now. MELINN: Green reaches the ropes, Keller with a hard right hand, he tags to Carpenter, Carpenter whips Green into the corner... oh no! Green colliding with the referee, John Myering, he is down! THOMAS: John Myering should quit drinking all that beer and get in shape, then maybe he could get out of the way once in a while! MELINN: And with the referee out, perhaps the advantage is in D. E. A. T. H.'s corner, with their rulebreaking tendencies... but Wayne Carpenter with an elbowsmash, headscissors applied... Green escapes the hold and tags to Benton. D. E. A. T. H. whips C arpenter into the ropes... double clothesline! Benton stomping away on Carpenter now. (Benton plays to the crowd, who boo him.) MELINN: Crowd not reacting too well to the antics of Benton. THOMAS: Bunch of morons. MELINN: Benton going for a kneedrop... Carpenter rolls out of the way! Benton clutching his knee, he could've hurt it there, Carpenter with a Boston crab applied now. THOMAS: Why not a kneelock submission? Wouldn't that make more sense? MELINN: Benton reaches the ropes, John Myering has sufficiently recovered from his collision with Green a few moments ago, he makes Wayne Carpenter break the hold. Carpenter with a hard chop, he tags to Keller, Keller with an abdominal stretch applied... Benton reaches the ropes. Tag to Carpenter, he whips Benton into the corner... big kneelift! Wayne throws Benton out of the ring, tag to Steve Keller, look at this! Double dropkick off the turnbuckles on to the ring floor! Wow! (Crowd pop.) THOMAS: Come on, D. E. A. T. H.! Kick it in high gear! MELINN: Keller rolls Benton back in the ring, hits him with a legdrive. Boston crab applied... Benton escapes the hold. Tag to Green, Benton and Green trying to doubleteam Steve Keller, but he fights back! Benton is out of the ring, Keller with an elbow smash, setting Green up for the piledriver... he nails him! Cover, 1... 2... and Benton makes the save. Keller goes for a shoulderbreaker but Green blocks it with a poke to the eyes. THOMAS: Good Greco-Roman move there, stunned the former stuntman. MELINN: Green tags to Benton, double dropkick by D. E. A. T. H., followed by a splash by Benton! Benton stomping away on Keller, and this time he connects with a kneedrop! Stomping away some more, he makes the cover, 1... 2... thr... no, a kickout, so c lose. THOMAS: You're telling me! MELINN: Benton with a fireman's carry takedown, cover, 1... kickout. Tag to Green, whip into the ropes... flying shoulderblock! Keller is knocked out of the ring by that one! THOMAS: All right! MELINN: And now both teams brawling outside the ring, Benton in the ring, stomping on Keller before he gets in, I'm not sure there was a tag there.... THOMAS: Ah, who cares? MELINN: Benton goes for a figure four, but Keller kicks him in the face. Keller back on his feet, he nails Benton with a double axhandle, tags to Wayne Carpenter. Right hand by Carpenter, Boston crab applied... Benton escapes the hold and tags to Green. (Green taunts Carpenter.) MELINN: Green trying to get Carpenter's goat, Carpenter charges in, he's met by a kick to the midsection that stuns him. Green goes up top... another flying clothesline! THOMAS: That move has worked very well for the team tonight. MELINN: Green tags to Benton, fireman's carry takedown, cover, 1... 2... kickout. Rake to the eyes, he tags to Green, there's the Powerslam! Green's signature move, that could be it, 1... 2... thr... no, he got his shoulder up! THOMAS: I don't believe it! MELINN: And neither does Green, he tags to Benton, Benton now choking Wayne Carpenter, referee John Myering making the five count, Benton breaks before the five. Benton up top... flying clothesline, he tags to Green, Benton nails Keller, Benton and Kelle r brawling outside the ring, referee John Myering trying to break that fight up, look out! Da Boss just handed Green a cane, and he nails Carpenter with it! Cover, oh no, 1... 2... 3! TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the winners of the match in 19:53, Benton and Green, D. E. A. T. H.! (Crowd boos.) MELINN: Travesty of justice, but Benton and Green pull it out. We'll be right back. **PROMO** (CWO logo slowly comes into focus.) VOICE-OVER: One year ago... an organization was formed to bring the best professional e-wrestling excitement to Cyberspace... it's been a hell of a year.... (Montage of CWO action is shown as intense music plays. CWO logo comes crashing through one of the montage shots.) VOICE-OVER: CWO CELEBRATION. Tonight, only on Play Per View. **PROMO** (Back to the ring, where Tony the K and referee Tom Krepps are standing.) TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall. Coming down the aisle... ("YMCA" by the Village People plays as the fans boo the entrance of Cake Boy.) TONY THE K: Weighing 289 pounds, from San Francisco, CA, accompanied by Toodles the Chimp, Cake Boy! (Cake Boy comes down eating cake and licking his lips, much to the disgust of the crowd. He also give some cake to Toodles, and then spanks him playfully.) MELINN: Boy, this guy's something, huh? THOMAS: He's flamboyant! MELINN: You can say that again. TONY THE K: And his opponent.... ("Enter Sandman" by the Mighty Mighty Bosstones plays as the fans cheer the entrance of Sandman Jr., with Kellen receiving her own form of cheers, as usual.) TONY THE K: Weighing 235 pounds, from Philadelphia, PA, accompanied by the lovely Kellen, Sandman Jr.! (Sandman Jr. comes down slapping hands, then gets it the ring after going through his usual pre-match Coke and cigarette routine.) THOMAS: I can't believe I ever thought Kellen was attractive. Was I beer-goggling? MELINN: I think you're in the minority with those thoughts. Sandman Jr. ready to go up against Cake Boy, and Cake Boy rubbing his chest suggestively at Sandman Jr., you can see him straining to keep his "reformation" going against this man. THOMAS: This was the guy who bashed Ricky Kidd, after all. MELINN: True enough, and here we go, Sandman Jr. starting off fast with a kick, he whips Cake Boy into the ropes... dropkick! Whip into the corner... charges in with a running knee, but Cake Boy nails him with a kick to the midsection! THOMAS: See, Sandman Jr.'s letting his emotions get the better of him, Cake Boy is a master of mindgames, that's how he's going to beat him. Of course, if you add up Sandman Jr.'s and Kellen's brain power together, I think you get a lima bean. MELINN: Cake Boy with a hard right hand that floors Sandman Jr. Scorpion deathlock applied... Sandman Jr. reaches the ropes. Whip into the corner... Cake Boy moves in but this time Sandman Jr. meets him with a kick! And now Sandman Jr. choking Cake Boy , referee Tom Krepps making the five count, Sandman Jr. breaks before the five. THOMAS: See again, Sandman Jr. fighting a lot of anger, "reformation," ha! MELINN: Whip into the ropes... Cake Boy comes back with a clothesline! Cake Boy picks Sandman Jr. up, Sandman Jr. with a forearm smash but Cake Boy comes back with a punch. Dropkick by Cake Boy, oh my goodness! Precision and power behind that move! Co ver, 1... 2... thr... and Sandman Jr. just barely gets his shoulder up. THOMAS: That was an unbelievable dropkick! Especially from an over 280-pounder! MELINN: Figure four applied by Cake Boy... Sandman Jr. reaches the ropes. (Cake Boy plays to the crowd, rubbing his chest and licking his lips, to much boos.) THOMAS: You know, I've heard Cake Boy is an excellent cook. MELINN: Well, I won't be eating his cookies anytime soon. Whip into the corner by Cake Boy... he moves in, nailing Sandman Jr. with repeated blows to the head, Sandman Jr. is down, elbow drop by Cake Boy, cover, he's got the ropes for leverage, 1... 2... thr... and again, so close. Cake Boy with another dropkick, followed by an elbow smash, he goes for a legdrop... but Sandman Jr. rolls out of the way! THOMAS: I guess you can't always have your cake and eat it too. MELINN: And Sandman Jr. with an STF applied... he lets it go. (Sandman Jr. plays to the crowd, who cheer him.) MELINN: Sandman Jr. taking his concentration off Cake Boy, and he's paying for it now, Cake Boy whipping him into the corner, again punching him in the head. Scorpion deathlock applied... Sandman Jr. in a lot of pain, struggling for the ropes... he makes it. Kick to the midsection by Cake Boy, followed by an elbow drop, cover by Cake Boy, again he's using the ropes for leverage, this time Kellen is up on the ring apron complaining about it.... THOMAS: Get that ugly tramp off of there! MELINN: Wait a minute... Cake Boy has got a hold of Kellen, oh no! He just backhand slapped her! THOMAS: They're fighting over Sandman Jr.! MELINN: Sandman Jr. coming from behind, but he's met by Cake Boy. Sandman Jr. with a chop, but Cake Boy comes back with an elbow smash. Cake Boy tries to whip Sandman Jr. into the ropes, it's reversed... Cake Boy comes back with a clothesline! THOMAS: He leveled him with that one! (Cake Boy plays to the crowd again.) MELINN: Cake Boy whips Sandman Jr. into the ropes again... another hard clothesline! Hard right hand, stomping away on Sandman Jr., cover, 1... 2... shoulder up. Cake Boy, oh my goodness! Another dropkick, catching Sandman Jr. right in the face! THOMAS: He might have broken his nose with that one! MELINN: Sandman Jr. could be out, cover by Cake Boy, 1... 2... 3! TONY THE K: The winner of the match in 8:59, Cake Boy! (Crowd boos.) MELINN: And Cake Boy is not finished, he's got a piece of cake, he's smearing it all over Sandman Jr.'s body, and now he's licking it off! How disgusting! THOMAS: Maybe he's hungry? MELINN: Look out! Sandman Jr. shoving Cake Boy off of him, and now he's got his Singapore cane! He's spanking Cake Boy with the cane, and Cake Boy is hightailing it out of the arena! (Crowd cheers.) MELINN: That's all the time we have for EYE ON THE STORM, if you haven't ordered CWO CELEBRATION yet, do so now! For David "The Truth" Thomas, this is "Killer" Kyle Melinn, we hope to see you in a couple of minutes! (Fade out to countdown.) 10... 9... 8... 7... 6... 5... 4... 3... 2...
