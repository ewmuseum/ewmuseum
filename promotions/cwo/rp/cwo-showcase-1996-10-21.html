CWO SHOWCASE 10/20/96

21 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWO SHOWCASE 10/20/96 Producer: [name withheld] Executiver Producer: Brent G. Alles (the opening music begins to play, but, instead of the normal mini-screens featuring past matches flashing across a black background, we see Johnny Reb setting up and applying the Texas Cloverleaf on Hellhound, and the referee waving for the bell. "CWO SHOWCASE" fades in on the screen, as the screen fades to black. The camera pans to Kevin Rinaldi and an older, heavyset man with a disapproving look on his face) Kevin (KR): Hello, everyone and welcome to another edition of CWO Showcase! I'm Kevin Rinaldi, and I am deeply honored and privileged to have with me tonight, the legendary ringmaster, Angelo Corleone. Welcome, Angelo! Angelo (AC, a Bruno-Sammartino type): Hello, Kevin. I wish I could say it was great to be here. KR (deciding not to respond to that): As you saw in our opening montage, in a match held before the show was taped, in what could be a preview of Terror Night, Johnny Reb did indeed defeat the Hellhound by way of his patented Texas Cloverleaf. The time, for you stats fans out there, was 7:41. Johnny Reb and Hellhound are on opposite sides of that FOUR CAGE CASKET ELIMINATION MATCH, and there is a very good chance that they could face each other again! But the rest of the card is hot, hot, HOT!!!!!! In our opener, Sandman Junior takes on the man called Talon! AC: The Mad Doctor's Ghost--what kind of name is that? Anyway, he takes on Killa of the Ultimate Warriors. KR: The merciless men of the Mercenaries lock up with the Deadly Sins! AC: And in the main event, Sandman battles Gold Kard. Big whoop. KR: Big whoop indeed, Angelo! And we'll be back after this message from our sponsors! **COMMERICAL BREAK** (the camera fades in again as Kevin and Angelo are obviously arguing. They realize that the camera is one them and quickly regain their composure.) KR: And we're back with our first match! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is scheduled for one fall with a twenty minute time limit. ("My Jekyll Doesn't Hide" by Ozzy Osbourne begins to play as the fans mostly boo, but there are a few cheers here and there) Introducing first, weighing in at 235 pounds, from parts unknown and accompanied by the Sandman, Talon!!!!!! ("Enter Sandman" by the Mighty Mighty Bosstones plays as the fans get down to some serious booing, jeering, and throwing things) And his opponent, also at 235 pounds, from Philadelphia, Pennsylvania and accompanied by Kellen, Sandman Junior! KR: And Sandman looks like he wouldn't mind a match against Junior! AC: You mean these guys are on opposite sides? Back in my day, you knew who your friends were, and if you had to have a gimmick, you each used a different one. KR: True, Angelo, but back in your day, television hadn't been invented yet! And Sandman Junior catches Talon with a forearm smash! Sandman Junior looking to the crowd for approval, and he ain't getting it! AC: I've been reading CWO Magazine--only Bane is more hated than Junior. That must be why some fans were cheering for this Talon creep! KR: Sandman Junior with the STF on Talon.....holding it on......now releases it. And Sandman Junior hits with a somersault splash! Talon is hurt! AC: High risk maneuver by Sandman Junior, but he's got the match under control in the early stages. Now, in my day, we waited until the middle or end of the match to do that stuff... KR: Boston crab by Sandman Junior....he released it almost as soon as he applied it! And Sandman Junior stomping on Talon's back! Taunting him! Sandman Junior picks Talon up...and tosses him out of the ring! AC: This guy's a real loser! He doesn't know how to do anything but cheat! KR: Actually, Sandman Junior's had a hard week, losing matches to Maximum Overdrive and Flyboy. AC: Sandman Junior...Maximum Overdrive...Flyboy...doesn't anybody use a real name anymore? It's like walking into a comic book!!!! KR: Things change, Angelo, and we have to change with them....OH MY!!!! Sandman Junior was going to nail Talon with a chair, but Talon beat him to it!!!! And Talon clotheslines Sandman Junior outside the ring!!! AC: Referee's counting, boys!!!!! KR: Talon first into the ring....missed with the jump kick as Sandman Junior was re-entering the ring! And the two men are brawling away! Elbowsmash by talon sends Sandman Junior to the mat! Cover! 1.....2.....thr...NO! Kickout by Sandman Junior! AC: Talon showing his inexperience....you have to hook the leg to get a pinfall! KR: Jumping spin kick by Talon sends Sandman Junior outside the ring! And Talon's going after him! Talon sends Sandman Junior into the metal ringpost! Talon climbing back into the ring, and Sandman Junior tries to shake the cobwebs! Talon nails him as he climbs back into the ring!!! Into the ropes....somersault splash!!!! AC: What's that idiot doing? KR: Talon playing to the crowd! He doesn't see Sandman Junior getting up! Sandman Junior nails him from behind--NO!! Talon dodged! Talon with a jump kick!!!! Roundhouse kick!!!! Two!!!! Sandman Junior into the ropes....SPIN KICK BY TALON!!!!! Sandman Junior running for cover! Talon goes after him and the two men are brawling outside the ring again!!!!! AC: These guys had better learn that you win the match inside the ropes, not outside it!!!! KR: Both men back in the ring......elbow by Talon....BIG splash!!!!! Talon has Sandman Junior up.....he goes into the ropes.....NO!!!!! Kellen tripped Talon as he hit the ropes! Talon landed hard on his shoulder....he's hurt! AC: And if Sandman Junior doesn't take advantage of that, he's a fool. KR: Double axehandle by Sandman Junior!!!!! Going up top.....HE HITS WITH THE PERMANENT DREAM!!!!!! Cover! 1...2......3!!!!!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner, at 9 minutes and 19 seconds, SANDMAN JUNIOR!!!!!!!!!!!! KR: That was disgusting!!!!! Kellen should be barred from ringside during Sandman Junior's matches! Sandman Senior and security helping Talon from the ring...some of the fans cheering his efforts. AC: And well they should, Kevin! Talon had this match until Sandman Junior's flunky tripped him up! KR: And we'll be back after this!!!! COMMERCIAL BREAK KR: And we're back with our next matchup! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, our next contest is scheduled for one fall with a twenty minute time limit! ("I'm a Dead Guy - What's Your Excuse?" begins to play as the fans cheer) Now coming to the ring area, weighing 575 pounds, from parts unknown, the Mad Doctor's Ghost! ("Hell's Bells" by AC/DC plays as the fans keep cheering) And his opponent, weighing 190 pounds, from Erie, Pennsylvania, he is one half of the Ultimate Warriors, here is Killa!!!! KR: Killa giving up nearly four hundred pounds in weight to the Mad Doctor's Ghost! AC: In my day, if a match like this happened, it would have been put right before the midgets. KR: Well, it isn't your day, anymore, Angelo, as Killa charges in and starts hammering on the Ghost! AC: He's looking to take him out early...if the Ghost gets his weight behind something, Killa is finished. KR: Killa with a headlock.......and lands the forearm to the Ghost's chest! But the Ghost is still standing! Killa sends the Ghost into the ropes.... clothesline!!!! Killa going for a running kneelift.....the Ghost moves out of the way! Mad Doctor's Ghost with an elbowsmash! Forearm smash by Mad Doctor's Ghost! Killa is down, and the Ghost is sitting on him!!!! AC: What did I tell you? KR: Killa managing to make the ropes! The referee is bringing both men to their feet...and Killa standing toe to toe with the Ghost, exchanging forearms and elbows! AC: Is he out of his mind? KR: Killa grabbed the arm when Mad Doctor's Ghost went for a chop, and sends him into the ropes! Clothesline by Killa! The Ghost is down!!!! Cover! 1.....2.....thr.....shoulder up!!!!! AC: Killa's pulling out all the stops, dictating the pace of the match. Mad Doctor's Ghost needs to slow things down, and use his bulk to his advantage. KR: Both men on their feet....Killa with a beautiful dropkick! Forearm smash!!!! Killa goes for the DDT....HE GOT IT!!!!! AC: Go for the pin now, Killa!!!! KR: Killa not following up.....Mad Doctor's Ghost is back on his feet! Elbow to Killa's jaw! Killa is down! Mad Doctor's Ghost with the Sudden Death!!!!! He goes for the pin! 1.....2....Killa gets the shoulder up! AC: Killa should have gone right for the cover! You always try to end the match as quickly as possible, get the win! KR: Mad Doctor's Ghost sends Killa into the corner!!!! He's trapped him there with his bulk!!! And the Ghost boxes Killa's ears!!! Killa in trouble!!! Elbow by the Ghost!!!!! He sends Killa into the opposite corner!!!! Charging in with the shoulder....NOBODY HOME!!!!! AC: Killa displaying terrific agility! KR: Killa slaps the arm wrencher on the Ghost! The Ghost reaches the ropes and catches Killa with a forearm on the break! And the Ghost has a chokehold on Killa! Get in there, ref!!!! AC: Dirty wrestling, there! I thought both these guys were supposed to be fan favorites! KR: The referee making the Ghost break the choke, but the damage has already been done.....the Ghost has Killa up.....HUGE powerslam!!!!! He's going for the Sudden Death....HE HITS IT!!!!! Cover! 1.....2.......3!!!!!!!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the match, at 15 minutes and 33 seconds.....THE MAD DOCTOR'S GHOST!!!!! KR: And Killa was just giving away too many pounds!!! AC: That's right....once the Ghost was able to use his size advantage, Killa was history. KR: And we'll be back after this!!! ***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** KR: And we're back! Angelo, since Showcase is eventually going to focus on the Light Heavyweight Division, what can you tell our viewers about the tournament? AC: Well, Kevin, I'm going to go with Wayne Schillo as my pick to win the tournament, since he's the only guy with a real name!!!! KR: You keep harping on that, don't you? AC: Yes, I do!!!!! When I was wrestling, yeah, we used gimmicks, but we only used them to enhance the overall show! Now it seems like they ARE the show! Between McMahon, Turner, and all these little feds running around, it's more like a circus tent than a collection of athletes! KR: Well, why are you staying in wrestling, then? AC: First off, because I can always use the money. I've got grandchildren in college, and I want to make sure they get the best damn education they can. Second, you don't devote over thirty years of your life to something and then just walk away. And last, I want people to know that I remember a different era of professional wrestling, and, I think, a better one. We were men, then. I don't have any respect for any of the clowns we saw tonight, or any of the other so-called wrestlers in this zoo! KR (he's stunned....almost speechless): We're going to go to a commercial... ***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** (the camera fades in on Kevin Rinaldi--Angelo Corleone is not with him) KR: And we're back with our next contest. Angelo Corleone is conferring with the CWO administration, he may or may not be back. Let's go to the ring! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the following tag team matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit! ("Iron Man" by Black Sabbath begins to play as the fans cheer halfheartedly) Coming down the aisle, accompanied by their fellow member of Doomsday Maximum Overdrive, at a combined weight of 555 pounds, here are Kaptain Krunch, Korperal Punishment, the team know as the Mercenaries! ("Sweet Dreams" by Marilyn Manson begins to play as the fans boo for all they're worth) And their opponents, led to the ring by Lust, at a combined weight of 761 pounds, Pride and Sloth, the Deadly Sins!!!!! KR: The Mercenaries set to take on the Deadly Sins, the most hated team in the CWO! The Mercenaries aren't exactly choirboys, but the fans seem to be cheering for them over the Sins! There's the bell! Kaptain Krunch immediately all over Pride!!!! Krunch sends him into the ropes... backdrop!!!! Elbow smashes into the bridge of Pride's nose!!!! Krunch bringing the Sinner over to his team's corner!!! He lifts the huge Pride onto his shoulders!!!! Korperal Punishment on the buckle!!!! THEY HIT THE FLYING CLOTHESLINE!!!! Krunch with the cover!!!! 1....2.... NO! Pride manages to kickout! (he confers with someone in tones too low to understand) Ladies and gentlemen, Angelo Corleone is still with CWO management, we.... or I guess I should say, I will keep you posted. Krunch with a double underhand suplex on Pride!!!! And Krunch taunting the big man!!! Going for the scorpion deathlock....Pride powers out and sends Krunch headfirst into the buckle!!!! Pride getting to his feet!!! Sends Krunch into the ropes....OH MY GOD!!!!! How does a man weighing nearly 400 pounds hit a Frankensteiner!!!!! Pride has the code red on Kaptain Krunch! Krunch reaches the ropes!!!!! Pride nails him on the break with a huge fist!! Sends Krunch into the corner.....follows up with a shoulder block!!! Krunch is in trouble!! Pride tagging in Sloth now!!!!!! They have him up.....double suplex!!! Sloth going for the cover!! 1......Krunch kicks out!!! Sloth sends Krunch into the corner......jumping avalanche!!!! Krunch fighting bakc with chops, staggering Sloth!!!! OH MY GOD!!!!!! Krunch, somehow, found the energy for a chokeslam!!!! Krunch drops the elbow!!! Going for the STF... Sloth powers out of it!!!!! Sloth on his feet and sends Kaptain Krunch into the corner! Going for another avalanche.....he missed!!!!!!! Krunch pulling him out of the corner, and hits with the clothesline!!!!! Sloth trying desperately to get to his corner!!! OUCH!!!!!! Sloth nailed Krunch below the belt with that kick!!!! Krunch is down in agony! Sloth going for the big butt splash!!!!!! HE HITS IT!!!! Cover!!!! 1...2.....thr...NO! Kaptain Krunch will not be defeated!!! (again confers with someone else) Ladies and gentlemen, I have just been informed that Angelo Corleone WILL be allowed back on the air for our final matchup. Right now, though, Sloth has tagged Pride back into the match. They double team Kaptain Krunch mercilessly!!!! Pride going for the moonsault! He's up....NO ONE HOME!!! And Pride hit the mat hard!!! Krunch crawling over to cover him!!! 1....2....Pride gets the shoulder up!!!!! Krunch tagging in Korperal Punishment!!!!! They send Pride into the ropes-- double clothesline!!!!!! Punishment with a double axehandle!!!! Krunch back in the ring....double body slam by the Mercenaries!!! Krunch has Pride up.....Punishment going up top for the Flying Clotheline....NO!!! Lust shoved Punishment off the buckle!!!! The referee is calling for the bell!!!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, as a result of outside interference, the referee has disqualified the Deadly Sins. Your winners, at 9 minutes and 21 seconds, the Mercenaries!!!!!!!! KR: And the two teams still brawling all the way back to the dressing room! We'll have to get security back there!!!! ANd we'll be back!!!!! ***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** (the camera comes up on Angelo Corleone. His face is calm, but his eyes are furious--this man is eating crow and he hates it) AC: Ladies and gentlemen, I made several unwarranted and untrue remarks about the wrestlers and management of the CWO. For this, I...humbly... apologize. The CWO is the premier wrestling federation in the country. My comments were completely uncalled for, and I openly and officially retract them. (the camera fades out, then fades back in on Kevin and Angelo. Angelo doesn't look any happier, but Kevin seems to be focusing on the business at hand) KR: And we're back with our main event of the evening!!! Gold Kard versus the Sandman!!! AC: And these guys don't like each other much, I can tell you, Kevin! KR: That's right! In fact, the two are scheduled to meet again in tag team action at Terror Night, just one week away!!!!! Sandman and his longtime partner and friend Lord Foul the Despiser will tangle with Gold Kard and his ally Brent "The Ace" Alles, with a special stipulation for that matchup! If Sandman and Foul win, Foul gets Gold Kard's North American title! But if Kard and Alles win, Alles becomes the new TV champion! AC: I can't say that I agree with that stipulation Kevin. A tag team match is not the same as a one-on-one situation. We could end up with Foul being pinned quickly and costing Sandman his title, even though Sandman never got into the match!!! KR: Be that as it may, those are th conditions for the their matchup! Let's go to the ring!!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall with a twenty minute time limit!!! It is a Non-Title versus Non-Title Matchup! ("Ride of the Valkyries" by Richard Wagner begins to play as the fans boo their butts off) Now coming down the aisle with German Choklat, weighing 245 pounds, he is the CWO North American champion, from Berlin, Germany, here is Gold Kard!!!!!!! ("Enter Sandman" by Metallica begins to play as the fans cheer) And his opponent, weighing in at 355 pounds, from parts unknown, the CWO Television champion, HE......IS....SANDMAN!!!!!! KR: And Gold Kard attacks Sandman as he's climbing through the ropes, whaling on him with punches and kicks!!! Sets him up.... flying dropkick!!!!! Sandman is down! Gold Kard going for the Boston crab...Sandman powers out and sends Gold Kard flying!!! Sandman climbing to his feet--double axehandle catches Gold Kard in the chest! Sandman has Gold Kard by the hair.....tosses him through the ropes!!! Sandman into the ropes....MISSES WITH THE DANGEROUS DIVE!!!!! AC: Sandman shouldn't have done that. KR: Real colorful commentary, there, Angelo...Gold Kard has a chair! Sandman takes it away from him!!! Sandman nails him with the chair! Sandman climbs back into the ring....Gold Kard trying to follow, but Sandman shoves the referee out of the way and introduces Gold Kard to his boot!!!!! AC: And this guy's supposed to be a role model? Why would the fans cheer someone like this? KR: That's the way wrestling is today, Angelo! Sandman going for a sleeper!!!! He's got it!!! Gold Kard waving his arms wildly, trying to break the hold!!! Sandman keeping the pressure on!!!! And he finally releases it!!!! Gold Kard is groggy!! Sandman sends him into the corner.....AVALANCHE!!!!!!!! And Sandman kicks him on the way down!!! Playing to the crowd....and they love it!!! AC: I can't believe this. KR: Sandman with a reverse chinlock on Gold Kard....moving into another sleeper!!!! Keeping the pressure on Gold Kard's carotid artery! Gold Kard elbows Sandman in the gut!!!! Another one!!! Sandman breaks the hold!! Gold Kard into the ropes, and catches Sandman with a headscissors roll!!!! Sandman back on his feet... dropkick by Gold Kard!!! Cover! 1....2....shoulder up!! AC: Gold Kard mounting a comeback. KR: Another dropkick by Gold Kard! And Gold Kard slaps on the Boston crab!!! Sandman in agony, but he won't give in!!! Gold Kard breaks the hold, and drives the head into the small of Sandman's back! Going for the cover!!! 1....Sandman kicks out!!! AC: This is wrong...... KR: Huh? Gold Kard all over Sandman!!! Another dropkick!!!! Forearm smash to Sandman's chest! And ANOTHER dropkick!!!! Cover! 1....2....Sandman kicks out!!!! AC: This is really wrong..... KR: What are you talking about? Gold Kard hits with the headscissors roll! Going up top.....DIE FLIEDER KROSS!!!!!! Cover.....and Gold Kard's got the tights!!!!! 1.....2....3!!!!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner, in 8 minutes and 23 seconds, GOLD KARRRRRRD!!!!!!!! KR: And that brings another great night of CWO wrestling to a close, right, Angelo? AC: Great. Right. This is ridiculous. I'm not going to do this anymore. Things were better in the old days!!!!! (he gets up and leaves. The camera fades out on a stunned and dumbfounded Kevin Rinaldi, as the copyright info appears on the bottom of the screen)
