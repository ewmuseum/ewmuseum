SUNDAY SHOWCASE 9/21/97 - HOUR #1

21 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWO SUNDAY SHOWCASE - HOUR #1 9/21/97 Producer: Chris O'Brien ( [detail withheld] ) Executive Producer: Brent G. Alles ( [detail withheld] ) (Fade into a spinning globe, as the CWO logo is slowly superimposed on it.) VOICE-OVER: The CWO... providing wrestling excellence the world over.... ('Virtuality' by Rush plays as a montage of CWO superstars in action... the Newton Brothers with a Powerline on Jumbo Jenkins... Mustafa Rahim locks the Eclipse on the Focus... Jason Varga gets the crowd going... Southern Dynamite nails the Rebel Edge on Vegas High Roller... Joe Eagle lands the Eagle Shock on Gold Kard... and Johnny Reb holds the CWO World Heavyweight Belt high in the air.) O'BRIEN: THIS IS SUNDAY SHOWCASE! (The camera switches to a filled to the rafters CWO Arena. Fans everywhere are waving signs such as UNITIC FOR LIFE!, VEGAS HIGH ROLLER: WANTED DEAD OR ALIVE, and THERE CAN BE ONLY ONE: GO HIGHLANDERS!) O'BRIEN: Man, oh, man, is this going to be something! (The announcer's booth is shown, and Chris O'Brien and Jerry Covenant are sitting at the table. Jerry, as always, is in his trademark white turtleneck and CWO sweater, while Chris is wearing a University of Pittsburgh football jersey) O'BRIEN: Hello fans, and welcome to another edition of CWO SHOWCASE! COVENANT: As you can see, my broadcast partner has fully recovered from last week's little bender. O'BRIEN: Yes, I have! And we're counting down the days, folks, at SEPTEMBER SLAM II: REVENGE IS THE NAME OF THE GAME, is only a week away! We're going to see Joe Eagle and Johnny Reb go at it for the World Heavyweight title, which both men richly deserve! Brent 'The Ace' Alles and Lord Hile Troy in a respect match! Diamondback Chris Myers and Sandman Jr. will go at it with the Reverend and the Sandman in a Texas Twister match! Highlanders, Mercenaries, and the Bad Guys in a three-way dance for the World Tag belts! Wayne 'College Guy' Schillo and Vegas High Roller, with a $2,000,000 million dollar bounty at stake! For the TV belt, the Focus meets Benjamin Blake Bentley, and the loser has to leave CWO forever! And so much more! COVENANT: Now that was a heck of a sales pitch! Now, fans, let's put aside all this talk and get right to the action, here on SUNDAY SHOWCASE! (The camera cuts to Chad Johnson standing in the middle of the ring, in a tuxedo.) JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, this first match on SUNDAY SHOWCASE is set for one fall! ('Judgment Day' by Van Halen plays as the fans boo.) JOHNSON: Coming down the aisle, accompanied by Lord Guru, from parts unknown, weighing 468 pounds, he represents the Cabin and he is JUDGEMENT! O'BRIEN: And we all saw the Cabin save Judgment from Skyhawk last week on SHOWCASE, and I wonder if the Unit is backstage, willing to return the favor. COVENANT: The Cabin and the Unit locking up at SEPTEMBER SLAM II in a four-man "Surrender or Submit" match, and both teams can't wait to tear into each other. JOHNSON: And his opponent... ('Ride of the Valkyries' by Wagner plays as the fans boo HARD.) JOHNSON: From Blackpool, England, weighing 240 pounds, accompanied by Gold Kard, here is Lord Hile Troy! O'BRIEN: Icon of CWO, my ass. COVENANT: My colleague here not familiar with the history of CWO, but we're not here to talk about the past! The bell rings, and Troy charges right in and NAILS Judgment with a hard right hand! Judgment comes back with an elbow smash, but Troy with another punch! O'BRIEN: And now Hile Troy with a low shot! Judgment right back with a forearm smash to the head! Troy responds with a kick, and now a headlock, but Judgment powers out easily, and lays a double axehandle to Hile Troy! COVENANT: The big man grabs Troy and whips him to the ropes, and he sticks that big boot right in the face of the icon! O'BRIEN: Troy down HARD, and Judgment picks him up with ease, and now Troy goes flying into the corner! COVENANT: Troy bounces out, and Judgment with a lariat! And a cover, 1... 2... thr... NO! Gold Kard puts Troy's foot on the ropes! O'BRIEN: No surprise there, Jerry, but Troy was THIS close to getting put away! COVENANT: And as always, GKI saving each other. Judgment swipes Troy's foot off the ropes, and now a nice side suplex! O'BRIEN: And now Judgment with a hammerlock attempt....Troy counters with a whip, and now it's Judgment who's hitting the corner! And Troy flies in with a body check! COVENANT: Judgment down, and Troy pulls him up by the hair...Judgment catches Troy off guard, and now Hile Troy is in the corner again! Judgment not wasting any time...a series of gut punches! O'BRIEN: Pin! And Judgment with his feet on the ropes for leverage! 1... 2... thr... and Troy kicks out! Judgment not daunted here, to his credit. COVENANT: He's in with one of the craftiest players around, and Troy blocks a side suplex attempt, and manages to get a crossface chicken wing on the big man! O'BRIEN: Yeah, but Judgment with those long arms of his able to reach the ropes easily! And another whip to the corner, and Judgment works over the abdominal area of Troy! COVENANT: You didn't say gut! We might make a real announcer out of you yet, Chris! O'BRIEN: Sure, whatever, as Judgment whips Troy to the opposite corner and gets a head of steam...running forearm smash, and Troy goes flying out of the ring! COVENANT: Judgment not wasting any time, and he follows Troy out! He grabs Troy from behind....measured right hand! And now Troy is whipped into the guardrail! O'BRIEN: Gold Kard from behind with a chair! He slams it on Judgment! The big man staggers as Troy and Kard roll him back in the ring. And Troy tries to go for the crossface, but Judgment still attentive and powers out easily. COVENANT: Judgment now whips Hile into the corner, but the referee gets clipped! O'BRIEN: The ref is down, and Judgment from behind....inside cradle! Troy rolls through and reverses it! The ref can't count! 1... 2... 3... 4... 5.... 6... damn. COVENANT: Judgment got lucky as Troy lets go and kicks Judgment down low. And now a whip to the ropes... TROY WITH A SPINEBUSTER! O'BRIEN: I DON'T BELIEVE IT! Lord Hile Troy just did a spinebuster on Judgment! (Lord Hile Troy plays to the crowd) COVENANT: And now Troy follows up with a series of low blows and two chops! Judgment manages to come back with a nice forearm smash! The referee is still down as Troy comes back with a forearm smash of his own! And now the two men trade kicks! Judgment gets Hile down, and he covers! O'BRIEN: Tough luck, as the ref is still down! Lord Guru tries to awaken him as... oh, man, Judgment's going to the top turnbuckle? COVENANT: Troy is up and groggy... flying lariat! That was incredible! And now Judgment going for a side suplex, and he hits it! And now Judgment goes for a DDT... no! Troy squeezes out! O'BRIEN: Troy whips Judgment into the corner, showing amazing resiliency... Troy flies in with a low blow! (Lord Hile Troy plays to the crowd) O'BRIEN: Judgment from behind with a forearm smash to the back of the head! And now an elbow smash! And both men trade right hands in the middle of the ring! COVENANT: Judgment comes out better... and another side suplex! O'BRIEN: Does this guy know anything else? COVENANT: He knows the inverted suplex, Chris. Judgment Call, he calls it, as now Judgment with a chop! And Troy goes outside, to be comforted by Gold Kard. O'BRIEN: How sweet. COVENANT: Judgment rolls out, too, and Lord Guru hands him a chair! SMACK! I heard that all the way back here! And Judgment nails Gold Kard for good measure! O'BRIEN: Judgment now back in the ring, and Troy manages to stagger back in... Judgment tries to whip Troy to the ropes, but it's reversed! Belly-to-belly suplex by Troy! TROY (to Judgment): COME ON, YOU CAN DO BETTER THEN THAT! COVENANT: And Troy going for that crossface, but Judgment powers out! O'BRIEN: Judgment is on one knee as Troy comes off the ropes... Judgment was playing possum, and just misses a DDT! COVENANT: Troy not missing a beat as he whips Judgment into the corner... reversal! Troy flies in, and Judgment right in there, nailing Troy in the stomach! O'BRIEN: Judgment now... full nelson attempt, but Troy counters! Judgment stops a comeback with a left hand, and now a forearm smash! Troy comes back with one of his own, and that took Judgment off his feet! Hile Troy now stomping away on Judgment. COVENANT: Hile measures... kneedrop! TROY (to Judgment): I GUESS YOU CAN'T DO ANY BETTER THEN THAT! COVENANT: And Troy FINALLY with that crossface applied! The ref asking if Judgment wants to submit... nope, didn't think so. O'BRIEN: Judgment gets to the ropes... the ref counts five and Troy releases. LOW BLOW! And now two hard right hands! COVENANT: Troy now whipping Judgment into the corner... repeated punches! And now Troy going for an STF, but Judgment counters! Double axehandle by Judgment knocks Hile Troy off his feet! Legdrop by the big man! O'BRIEN: I smell a comeback... COVENANT: Judgment grabs Troy... he's going for the Judgment Call! He lifts Troy up... TROY BLOCKS! And now Judgment goes over as Troy reverses it into a backdrop! O'BRIEN: And now Troy snaps back on the crossface... OK, so I was wrong. Judgment isn't going to submit, though... COVENANT: Troy putting pressure on, but Judgment just waiting it out... Troy releases in disgust and goes for a low blow... Judgment blocks! And now he hits a low blow of his own on Troy! O'BRIEN: Judgment now whipping Troy to the ropes... he goes for a backdrop... WHOA! Troy stops short, picks up Judgment... PILEDRIVER! He just piledrove Judgment! COVENANT: And now a headlock by Troy, he's got to be getting frustrated. O'BRIEN: Ah, he can cope! Judgment drives an elbow into the stomach of Troy, and Troy releases! And now a kick by Judgment! Cover, 1... 2... kickout! (The bell rings.) COVENANT: What, was that a pin? O'BRIEN: Ah... nope. COVENANT: Then what was it? JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the 20 minute time limit has expired. This match is a draw! O'BRIEN: I KNEW IT! I swear, Troy is like Steven Regal. They always get draws! COVENANT: A great matchup to kick things off, fans! Don't go away, we'll be right back! (We come back from commercial, and the camera shows O'Brien and Covenant at the table.) O'BRIEN: Before we go into our next match, fans, we have a couple of announcements concerning next week. As you all know, next week is the big PPV, SEPTEMBER SLAM II! It's being held down in San Antonio, Texas, a good 2000 miles away from here. So, to ensure we don't miss a thing, next week's SUNDAY SHOWCASE will not be from the CWO Arena. COVENANT: That's right, usually we come to you each and every Sunday afternoon from here in the CWO arena. But next week, we'll be taping SUNDAY SHOWCASE Saturday night, September 27th, in Austin, Texas, at the Far Side nightclub! O'BRIEN: FIGHTIN' FRIDAY NIGHT may be gone, but we'll be coming to you, one night only, as CWO's finest go at it in front of 1000 clubgoers! Plus, you get to see me try to make Jerry drink! COVENANT: Ahem. With that said, let's go to our next matchup! JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is set for one fall, with a 20 minute time limit. ('Thunderkiss 69' by White Zombie plays as the fans boo.) JOHNSON: Coming down the aisle, accompanied by Marc Mission, from Vanceburg, KY, weighing 290 pounds, X-Factor! O'BRIEN: We all saw Marc Mission last week get a win here on SUNDAY SHOWCASE, and now his tag team partner tries to get a win over the same person. JOHNSON: And his opponent... ('Shoots and Ladders' by Korn plays as the fans cheer.) JOHNSON: Weighing 275 pounds, accompanied to the ring by the lovely Dallas, from Phoenix, AZ, here is Diamondback Chris Myers! COVENANT: Myers losing last week as a result of a sneak attack by the Sandman, and now he's going up against the tag team partner of the man who beat him, so I'm wondering...will the Reverend show up? O'BRIEN: I wouldn't be surprised. In the short time I've been here, the hottest feuds I've seen are Eagle/Reb, Sandman/Sandman Jr., and Myers/Reverend. COVENANT: The bell rings, and we're off! X-FACTOR (to Myers): HEY, COME HERE, SISSY BOY! O'BRIEN: Myers walks right up and gets in the face of X-Factor... OH! Factor slaps Chris right across the face! X-FACTOR (to Myers): I CAN'T BELIEVE YOU FELL FOR THAT! COVENANT: Myers rubbing his face... and he comes back and NAILS Factor! And a small package attempt! But X-Factor gets out before the 1 count. O'BRIEN: X-Factor going for a vertical suplex, but Myers blocks with another small package attempt! And again, Factor out before one! COVENANT: Factor leaps up top, trying to catch Myers off guard, but Diamondback shakes the ropes, and Factor falls off! (Diamondback Chris Myers makes the universal 'make some noise' motion with his arms) O'BRIEN: Chris playing to the crowd as he hits a chop on X-Factor, who comes back with a forearm smash! And now Myers hits one of his own! And now Factor, and he takes Myers off his feet! Cover by X-Factor, 1... 2... no! Myers gets his shoulder up! X-FACTOR (to Myers): BOY, DO YOU SUCK! COVENANT: Myers being stomped on by Factor now... and a legdrop! Chris Myers rolls out of the ring to clear his head... MARC MISSION FROM BEHIND! O'BRIEN: DAMN! Myers got NAILED over the head by a chair swung by Mission! And look at all that blood! Mission rolls Myers back into the ring, where X-Factor is jumping on the ropes. X-FACTOR (to crowd): WHERE DO I EAT? CROWD: THE GARBAGE DUMP! COVENANT: I still do not get that...Myers from behind with a shot to the back of the head! And now a dropkick! And another one! Cover, 1... 2... kick out by X-Factor! O'BRIEN: Myers is busted open here, and he misses a dropkick! X-Factor now with a neckbreaker, but Myers manages to get away! COVENANT: X-Factor to the ropes now, measuring... Myers with a spinning kick! His martial arts training kicked in there, but he's too groggy to cover! O'BRIEN: X-Factor back up, and now HE kicks Chris Myers! And now X-Factor going for a press slam... NO! He throws Chris Myers out of the ring and onto the floor! COVENANT: Myers hits the ground hard, and here comes Mission again... he grabs Chris and puts him on the French announcer's table! O'BRIEN: Our friends in Quebec are about to get a close look as X-Factor is on the top turnbuckle... OH MY GOD! X-Factor just DROVE Diamondback through that table! COVENANT: Dallas is noticeably worried as her man is rolled back in the ring, and he's bleeding from a gash on his forehead AND his cheek... what kind of shape is Myers going to be in for the six man tag on FEVER AND the match next Sunday! O'BRIEN: I think right now, Chris is trying to focus on X-Factor, who goes for a pin! 1... kick out by Myers! X-FACTOR (to Myers): HOW YOU FEELING, PRETTY BOY? COVENANT: And a low blow attempt... Myers steps out of the way! And he drives an elbow down onto X-Factor! And now an armbar attempt, but X-Factor ends that with a rake to the eyes. O'BRIEN: And now X-Factor drives his thumb right into the huge gash on Chris' forehead! COVENANT: Myers on one knee... X-FACTOR IS CALLING FOR THE X-FILE! Myers hears the call and pulls himself up! He whips X-Factor to the corner and follows him in! Avalanche... X-Factor catches Chris with a double axehandle! O'BRIEN: And now another eye rake, and Myers' face is soaked in red! COVENANT: And X-Factor with two low blows, and Myers is reeling, but he manages to get in a kick attempt. O'BRIEN: X-Factor blocks that weak kick and now whips Myers into a corner. Avalanche, but Myers hits a double axehandle of his own! And now a small package! 1... 2... X-Factor reverses! 1... 2... thr... NO! Myers breaks! And now he tries the small package again! 1... 2... thr... X-Factor grabs the tights and reverses... 1... 2... no! The ref sees it! X-Factor argues with the ref as Myers is standing on the ropes, trying to catch his breath. COVENANT: Myers rolls out of the ring to collect his thoughts... IT'S THE REVEREND! The Reverend from underneath the ring, and he's nailing Myers! Myers fights back, and now both men are brawling up the aisle! O'BRIEN: The ref is counting! Myers is being counted out! 1... Myers with kick to the Reverend... 2... another one! 3... Reverend with a scoop slam! 4... he's kicking Chris.... 5... Myers trips the Reverend and they're brawling on the floor... 6... 7... 8... 9... 10! JOHNSON: The winner of this contest, as a result of a countout, at a time of 8:42, X-FACTOR! O'BRIEN: Security trying to break up this mess, but blood is flying everywhere as Chris Myers and the Reverend are tearing into each other! COVENANT: Fans, we have to take a break, but if anything happens, we'll let you know! (We come back from commercial, where the crew has finished cleaning up as much of Chris Myers' blood as possible.) O'BRIEN: Fans, during the break, security managed to break apart Myers and the Reverend, and now both men are back in their dressing rooms. COVENANT: I think Chris is just happy the Reverend didn't try fry him with another fireball. OK, one more match and we can call hour #1 done! JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is set for one fall with a 20 minute time limit! ('Enter Sandman' by Metallica plays as the fans boo.) JOHNSON: Coming down the aisle, from parts unknown, weighing 355 pounds, here is the Sandman! O'BRIEN: Well, maybe Sandman Jr.'s going to rain on this match. COVENANT: I don't think he's here tonight, Chris. JOHNSON: And his opponent... ('Six Foot Foam Skull' by Shallow, North Dakota plays as the fans cheer.) JOHNSON: weighing 243 pounds, from Pittsburgh, PA, Miguel Sanchez! O'BRIEN: Two men Sandman Jr. really hates, I think he's going to show up... COVENANT: The bell rings, and Sanchez comes right out with a face rake! Sandman not fazed as he nails Miguel with a chop! And now Sandman whips Miguel to the corner! Sanchez bounces out, and Sandman whips him into the opposite corner! O'BRIEN: Sandman following up with a punch to the midsection, and now Sandman tries AGAIN to whip Sanchez, but Sanchez manages to reverse, and it's Sandman who hits hard! And now Sanchez goes for a Victory Roll! He can't quite hook, but Sandman hits the canvas hard! And Miguel Sanchez, from the great city of Pittsburgh, stomps away on the black eyed freak. COVENANT: A little bias from the man wearing the Pitt Panthers jersey, while Sanchez with a standing splash, and now an elbowdrop! O'BRIEN: COVER... 1... 2... thr... no! Foot on the ropes! COVENANT: Sandman gets lucky as Miguel Sanchez pulls him up... legsweep attempt is avoided, and Sandman now with a headlock! Applying pressure to that head and neck area. O'BRIEN: He releases, but quickly lays a double axehandle across the back to keep Sanchez down. Sanchez tries to lay an elbow into the stomach of Sandman, but a kick by Sandman ends that quick. COVENANT: A quick match so far as Sandman picks up Sanchez... BODY SLAM! Sanchez tries to roll outside, but Sandman catches him on the apron, and puts the boots to Miguel! O'BRIEN: And now Sandman pulls Sanchez back in... body slam! And a sleeper... no! Miguel counters, and now a reverse thrust kick is avoided by Sandman! COVENANT: Sandman tries to catch Sanchez now with a kneelift, but that's avoided, but the chop afterwards isn't! And now a forearm smash across the face... POWERSLAM! In one fluid motion, Sandman picks up Sanchez and brings him back down WITH AUTHORITY! O'BRIEN: And Sanchez is down... no, he's pulled up and whipped to the corner, no! Reversal by Sanchez... FRANKENSTEINER! Not many men get up that fast from a Sandman powerslam, and now Sanchez is up top... MOONSAULT! Cover, 1... IT'S THE REVEREND AGAIN! HE CATCHES SANCHEZ AND PULLS HIM OFF! (The bell rings) JOHNSON: At a time of 5:59, the referee has disqualified Sandman and awarded this match to Miguel Sanchez! O'BRIEN: All well and good, but the Reverend and the Sandman have Miguel Sanchez in their mercy! COVENANT: The Reverend is putting Sanchez into the Agony! He's locked in that reverse surfboard as Sandman wails away on the stomach of Sanchez! Fans, we HAVE to end this hour, we're sorry, we'll show you footage of anything else that happens! END HOUR #1
