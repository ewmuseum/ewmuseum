CWO EYE ON THE STORM: CWO TERROR NIGHT '96: TALES FROM THE CRYPT

27 October 1996

CWO EYE ON THE STORM: TERROR NIGHT '96 "LIVE" FROM JOE LOUIS ARENA, DETROIT, MI 10/27/96 DARK MATCHES: Payne and Hate battled to a double countout with the Apocalypse (Plague and Pestilence) in 1:00. Sin pinned Tub-O-Blub after a forearm drop in 6:20. Flame Spirits (Griffen and Wolf) defeated Ultimate Warriors (Cyrax and Killa) in 23:39 when Griffen pinned Killa after a legdrop. Clark Gray defeated Mad Doctor's Ghost by disqualification in 4:08 when Lafayette attacked Clark Gray. (EYE ON THE STORM opening comes up. We go to Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello everybody, we are 30 minutes away from CWO PPV excitement, CWO TERROR NIGHT '96: TALES FROM THE CRYPT. 8 men versus 8 men, four steel cages, 12 coffins, a terrifying match with terrifying implications. However, our big news story is the absence of Lord Foul the Despiser. We take you to footage just recently obtained of his whereabouts. (We join Dave Schutton's pursuit of Lord Foul.) DAVE SCHUTTON (into airplane phone): Yeah, YEAH, you heard me right... LONDON... No, not London, Massachusetts... London, ENGLAND! No... he doesn't know I'm following him. VOICE HEARD ON THE PHONE: Well, you tell him to get his a** back across the border into the states! If we don't have him here tonight for that match we're going to lose SEATS, and then we'll lose MONEY, and then I'll lose my JOB! SCHUTTON: You want ME to tell the former World Champion that he has to ignore going back to his hometown so he can face his two worst enemies? VOICE ON PHONE: You're a reporter! You want that Pulitzer? Think of something! SCHUTTON (sighing): Okay... I'll get back to you... (The reporter looks around slowly trying to come up with an idea. He sees a male flight attendant walking down the aisle, and his eyes light up wide with brilliance. He gets up and walks to the bathroom.) SCHUTTON (as if trying to get someone's attention): Stu... stupid door! The door is ALWAYS stuck! (The flight attendant comes over.) FLIGHT ATTENDANT: May I help you? SCHUTTON (motioning attendant to door): Yeah... this door... it's stuck. FLIGHT ATTENDANT (opening door with ease): What do you mean it's stu...HEY! (Schutton quickly shoves the attendant into the bathroom and follows him inside. After a small thump, nothing is heard, until Schutton leaves the bathroom wearing the attendant's job outfit.) SCHUTTON (pushing a cart uncertainly down the aisle): Ummm... does anyone need anything? LITTLE RED-HAIRED BRAT: I want some ice cream, and a zinger, and triple chocolate ice cream, and a burrito, and some pizza, and some... MOM: Oh no you don't, Ferguson! He'll take a glass of water, thank you. SCHUTTON (fumbling around with the glass): OK... here you are son. (Schutton pushes the cart up to the first class section, where he spots Lord Foul the Despiser.) SCHUTTON (clearing his throat first): Can I get you anything sir? LORD FOUL (chewing on peanuts and covered in empty bags): Yeah, more honey roasted cashews! And I know I'm not in England yet, so you don't have any real tea, so how about some Snapple? SCHUTTON (nervously): OK, here are your peanuts... I'll go try to find you a Snapple. (Foul chomps down on some more cashews, and Schutton returns a few minutes later with a box full of tea.) SCHUTTON: I've got Snapple, and a ton of other kinds of tea for you, so, what brings you going to England? LORD FOUL: I'm going home... SCHUTTON: So you're just dropping all of your ties in the good ol' states and shipping off to Jolly ol' England? LORD FOUL: I'm going home... I can carry my ties on from England you know, these modern days of modems and cellular phones. SCHUTTON: What is your profession? LORD FOUL: I umm... I'm a... ahh... reporter, for a pro wrestling magazine. SCHUTTON: You're kidding me, aren't you? I KNOW your n... never going to leave America... with that type of job! LORD FOUL: Oh, I am leaving, there are always replacements out there to help with everything, and they can maintain my part while I'm gone... you better take your seat, we're getting ready to land. SCHUTTON: Oh yeah... I've got a uhh... phone call to make. (Foul continues to wolf down on the peanuts while Schutton runs to the back of the jet to make a call on his cellular phone.) SCHUTTON (while the phone rings): C'mon... C'mon... pick up pick up! VOICE ON PHONE: Well, what did he say? SCHUTTON: He said he wanted Snapple and honey roasted cashews, that's what he said! I am NOT going to tell him myself to come home! If you want to try to call him when he gets to his house, you can do that! Because I'm not going to! VOICE ON PHONE: Schutton, you listen to me, you lackey! You WILL get him on this phone NOW and he will come home NOW and tell him if he doesn't, that we WILL get our legal team together and.... (Schutton hangs up the phone.) SCHUTTON: Blah blah blah... I better get out of these clothes, I've got quite a trip ahead of me.... (Fade out. Back to Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: So, based on this, Lord Foul the Despiser will not be in the Tag Team Elimination match tonight against Gold Kard and Brent "The Ace" Alles. Taking his place will be the Mad Doctor's Ghost. Here, then, is the final lineup for CWO TERROR NI GHT '96: TALES FROM THE CRYPT. THE CRYPT (4 cages, elimination casket match): Slocum's Soldiers ("Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum, Johnny Reb, Jackass Joel, Phantom Freightrain, Mason-Dixon, Hoss and Little Joe) vs. The Dark Side (Master of Darkness Damion, Hellhound, Hangman, Rooster, Mercenaries, Creatures of the Night) WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (Lumberjack match): World Champion Barry "Master of Pain" Hart (w/Gold Kard, Brent "The Ace" Alles, and the Pit Bosses) vs. Vincent "The Lip" Manocelli (w/Joe Eagle, Russell Hill, and the Big Cool) TAG TEAM ELIMINATION MATCH: CWO Television Champion Sandman and Mad Doctor's Ghost vs. CWO North American Champion Gold Kard and Brent "The Ace" Alles (If Sandman and Mad Doctor's Ghost win, Mad Doctor's Ghost gets Gold Kard's North American title, if Gold Kard and Brent "The Ace" Alles win, Brent "The Ace" Alles gets Sandman's TV title.) NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (Dog Collar match): CWO North American Champions Pit Bosses vs. Gods of Thunder (AC and DC) FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE: Marc "Compton" Daniels vs. Bane STRETCHER MATCH: Miguel Sanchez vs. Sandman Jr. SUBMISSION MATCH: Inferno vs. Dark Raven Deadly Sins vs. Samoans Maximum Overdrive vs. Akillies "Big" Rich Clark vs. Baby Waldo Judgment vs. Sorrow Hollywood Muscle vs. Kamikaze and Mysteria JOHNNY STARR: We are ready for our EYE ON THE STORM matchup, the final in the CWO Light Heavyweight Championship Tournament. Let's take a look at how the tournament went down. (Montage of tournament action is shown with music. Graphic of "1ST ROUND - GROUP A" comes up.) JOHNNY STARR VOICE-OVER: 16 of the top light heavyweights, 240 pounds and under, came to the CWO Light Heavyweight Championship Tournament looking for one thing: the belt. In the first round, held on the October 1st Tuesday Night Fever, Killa defeated My steria after Mysteria missed with a moonsault. Lord Foul the Despiser beat his protégé with a small package. Tiger Rogers defeated Cyrax after a big boot. Dark Raven defeated Steve Keller of Hollywood Muscle after the Death from Above Flying Dropkick. (Graphic of "1ST ROUND - GROUP B" comes up.) STARR V-O: In the second half of the first round, held on the October 8th Tuesday Night Fever, Flyboy upset CWO World Champion "Master of Pain" Barry Hart, pinning him after turning over a flying cross body press. Talon defeated Wayne Carpenter of Hollyw ood Muscle after a jumping spin kick. Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo defeated Johnny Reb, shockingly using a chair in the heat of battle to defeat the Southern wonder by countout. Sandman Jr. Quickly defeated David "Destroyer" Davis after whomping him with his Singapore cane. (Graphic of "QUARTERFINALS" comes up.) STARR V-O: In the quarterfinals round, held on the October 15th Tuesday Night Fever, Lord Foul the Despiser pinned Killa quickly with the Pedigree. Dark Raven narrowly defeated Tiger Rogers by disqualification. Flyboy pinned Talon with the Flying Bodypr ess. Wayne "Keuka Guy" pinned. Sandman Jr. After a splash. (Graphic of "SEMIFINALS" comes up.) STARR V-O: In the semifinals, held on the October 22nd Tuesday Night Fever, Lord Foul the Despiser was shockingly upset by the Dark Raven after Master of Darkness Damion interfered. In the other match, Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo got past Flyboy. This set s up our match tonight... (Split screen of Schillo and Raven is shown.) STARR V-O: Two men... they've clawed their way to the top through 14 other light heavyweight superstars... not the step must be taken to gain the ultimate prize. (Camera opens on MOD Damion and, in the background, Hellhound and Dark Raven seem to be toying with something.) MOD DAMION: As many of you in the CWO already know there is a new addition to The Dark Side. Now with my guidance the great Dark Raven will win the Light Heavyweight title. Look at him... (Points to where Hellhound and Dark Raven are ripping apart what looks like some kind of animal or meat.) MOD DAMION: Is he ready or what? I have been working on bringing out the killer instinct in him. After my match with him I knew it was in there, he just could not reach it, that is why he lost, but now... tonight... he will show you all. LONG LIVE THE NIGHT! (We go to "Killer" Kyle Melinn and David "The Truth" Thomas, graphics saying their names appear below them. David "The Truth" Thomas is dressed as Superman and "Killer" Kyle Melinn is dressed as a vampire.) "KILLER" KYLE MELINN: Good evening, everybody, and welcome to EYE ON THE STORM, as Johnny has been detailing for you, we are ready for the final in the CWO Light Heavyweight Championship Tournament. I'm "Killer" Kyle Melinn, and we are ready for the scar iest night of CWO action ever. Joining me as always, making Dean Cain look like Adonis himself, David "The Truth" Thomas. DAVID "THE TRUTH" THOMAS: Hey, watch your mouth, Melinn! Did I ever tell you about the time Teri Hatcher hit on me? MELINN: I'll bet. THOMAS: Hey, I don't lie! I'm the Truth! MELINN: Tony the K, take it away! (CWO LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP TOURNAMENT - FINAL graphic comes up. Tony the K is in the ring.) TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall. It is the final in the CWO Light Heavyweight Championship Tournament! (The crowd cheers.) TONY THE K: Coming down the aisle... (The theme from "Halloween" plays as the fans boo. Dark Raven, accompanied by Master of Darkness Damion, enters.) TONY THE K: Weighing 231 pounds, from London, England, accompanied by Master of Darkness Damion, Dark Raven! MELINN: And how appropriate that we start tonight off with the theme from "Halloween," Wayne Schillo definitely has a fearsome challenge ahead of him. THOMAS: Yeah, but he better be ready for a trick, not a treat, because there's no way he's going to beat the Raven on his night, Terror Night! TONY THE K: And his opponent... ("You Shook Me All Night Long" plays as the crowd cheers. (Schillo screams to the crowd, "5 TO 1, BABY!") THOMAS: Yeah, those were the odds against you winning, college boy. MELINN: Schillo throws Raven out of the ring, he follows him outside, look out! Dark Raven with a chair! He nailed Schillo with it, he's back in the ring now, Schillo following, Dark Raven with a dropkick. Raven with a kick to the midsection, he goes f or an armbar but Schillo counters it. Schillo with a double axhandle, Raven is down, Schillo up top... flying elbow drop! Cover, he's got the ropes for leverage... 1... 2... thr... and Schillo just barely gets his shoulder up. Dark Raven tries t o whip Schillo into the ropes, it's reversed, Schillo goes for a kick to the head, it's avoided, Raven with another back body drop! THOMAS: Raven using his speed to great advantage, keeping Schillo off balance at all times. MELINN: Raven whips Schillo into the ropes... Schillo comes back with a kick to the head! Almost the Keuka Crack, but not quite. THOMAS: He better use that as soon as he can. In fact, he'd just better go get a gun and shoot Dark Raven, that's the only way he's going to beat him. MELINN: Schillo whips Dark Raven into the ropes... Dark Raven comes back with a dropkick! Criss-cross action here in this matchup! Cover by Raven, 1... shoulder up. Raven with a face claw, he goes for a power elbow drive, and again he misses! Schillo able to avoid the speed and impact of that elbow drive so far. THOMAS: Yeah, but he can only evade so long. MELINN: MOD surprisingly non-active in this match so far, although I'm sure that will change after long, Schillo with a dropkick, Dark Raven struggling to his feet, here we go... Keuka Crack! That's it! THOMAS: No! MELINN: Nailed him with the kick, 1... 2... thr... no! Dark Raven just barely kicking out from that devastating kick right to the chin. Schillo whips Dark Raven into the ropes, Dark Raven comes back with a Frankensteiner! THOMAS: See, Raven always knows where he is in the ring, even though Schillo has been battering him, he still has presence of mind to know how to counteract every maneuver Schillo thinks of, and that's not many.
