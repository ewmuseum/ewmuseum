SUNDAY SHOWCASE 10/12/97 HOUR #2

12 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWO SUNDAY SHOWCASE - HOUR #2 10/12/97 Producer: Steve White ( [detail withheld] ) Executive Producer: Brent G. Alles ( [detail withheld] ) (Fade into a spinning globe, as the CWO logo is slowly superimposed on it.) VOICE-OVER: The CWO... providing wrestling excellence the world over.... (Fireworks and pyrotechnic fountains explode to massive cheers and light up the arena as a long- panning shot of the arena slowly cranes its way down to the announcer's booth where Josh Miller and Mike Williams sit in their usual spots.) MIKE WILLIAMS: Welcome once again to another EXPLOSIVE hour of non-stop CWO action here on Sunday Showcase! Tonight we've got Gold Kard and Wildfire in action... JOSH MILLER: Gold Kard....Now there's a man with goals. MW: Indeed. We also have The Harvesters of Sorrow up against a total opposite, attitude-wise the Nation of Denomination... JM: And let's not forget one of the CWO's best, Carlos Maia, is going to literally DESTROY that goof Varga for the Light Heavyweight Title! MW: That remains to be seen, but we know for a fact that you won't want to miss out on all the action coming your way! Don't touch that dial! (Commercial break. When we come back, Chad Johnson is in the ring along with referee Gary Alles.) -*Gold Kard vs. Wildfire*- CHAD JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. Coming down the aisle... ('My Sherona' by the Knack plays as the fans cheer) CHAD JOHNSON: Weighing 232 pounds, from Venice Beach. CA, Wildfire! (Wildfire comes down to the ring silently.) JM: Oh yeah. He stands a *good* chance against the multiple time former-World Champ Gold Kard. *laughs* MW: Just a few weeks ago fans, we saw Wildfire come with a split-second of claiming the Light-Heavyweight title as his own, and word is, he hasn't given up on that road to gold yet... JM: Shows his intelligence. A born loser that comes back for more. CHAD JOHNSON: And his opponent... ('Ride of the Valkryies' by Wagner plays as the fans boo) CHAD JOHNSON: Weighing 245 pounds, from Berlin. Germany, accompanied by Lord Hile Troy, Gold Kard! MW: Here's the man that set the CWO on it's ear, by forming a 'stable' of sorts... and gaining near-total control of the CWO. Hence, trying his best to dismantle this great fed.... JM: Truly a great, great man. (Bell rings.) MW: Gold Kard starting things off with a stiff right to the jaw! Big uppercut and Wildfire's down! JM: This man impresses me on *SO* many levels Mike! MW: Gold Kard from up top....flying headbutt! Kard back up on the buckle... flying bodypre... NO! Wildfire stops him dead in the air with a stiff right of his own! (Wildfire raises his arms to the crowd and slaps on a Figure-4!) MW: Wildfire trying to break Kard early on in this matchup! JM: And did you see what he did before he put it on? He raised his arms just like Bloodbath used to do, before the Reverend took over his mind! MW: Those two set to collide at TERROR NIGHT '97 in the TRIPLE CAGE OF TERROR, but for now Gold Kard is trying to reverse the figure four! (Gold Kard rocks Wildfire over into a painful reversal.) MW: And he reverses it! JM: I love it! MW: Wildfire still eerily silent, even though he must be in great pain... wait a minute... another reversal by Wildfire! JM: What?! MW: Wildfire with the Figure-4 still applied.....and a release. WILDFIRE (to Gold Kard): C'MON! JM: Whoa! He said a word! MW: Wildfire taking his time, taunting Kard... such intensity on the part of this young man as of late, and now he may have been driven over the brink by the betrayal of his best friend Bloodbath... and Kard fires off a left to the chin! JM: That'll teach 'em! MW: Big elbow by Kard! Another! Forearm smash and a dropkick! (Gold Kard climbs up top.) MW: Gold Kard from the top! MISSILE DROPKICK! Cover and a fistful of tights! 1!....kickout! (Gold Kard taunts the crowd as Wildfire sneaks up from behind with an elbow to the back.) JM: That l'il cheat! MW: Elbow to the back by Wildfire and a whip....Frankensteiner! Cover, and Gold Kard gets out before the 1 count! JM: After a move like that, his man should be down! This just reassures the fact that Wildfire is a 2-bit HACK! MW: Gold Kard with a dropkick, and Wildfire goes over the ropes! (Gold Kard roughs up Wildfire outside of the ring before rolling him back in.) MW: Cover! 1!....2!.....Thr! Kickout by Wildfire! (Holocaust slowly makes his way ringside to boos.) MW: It's Holocaust! JM: What's that moron doing here?! MW: Holocaust the twin brother of Gold Kard... but it's not a good family relationship... and Gold Kard is definitely distracted by him! (Gold Kard goes up to the buckle and signals for Die Fleider Kross, but Holocaust gets up on the apron, catching his attention.) MW: Holocaust up on the apron....and Gold Kard unleashes with Die Flieder Kross! BIG MISS! JM: NO! That idiot Holocaust distracted him! MW: Gold Kard back to his feet.....right cross and a cross body-block! 1!....2!.....kickout! (Holocaust watches on sternly as Gold Kard misses with a flying dropkick.) MW: Wildfire with a whip to the ropes.....clothesline by the prez of GKI! Cover! 1!.....2!.....Thr! (Gold Kard complains about a slow count, but Wildfire charges in from behind with a clothesline of his own.) MW: Oh my! Kard over the top.....and here comes Holocaust! (Holocaust crashes a steel chair across the head of Gold Kard and rolls him back in the ring.) JM: Aww! What the HELL! MW: Gold Kard may very well be out cold after that shot...and Wildfire with the...YES! The WILD THING! (Gold Kard lies unconscious in the hold as the ref signals for the bell.) CHAD JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen... your winner in 9:18... WIIIIIILDFIIIIIIIIIIIRE! MW: And Wildfire isn't going to break the hold! JM: Do something, Troy! (Lord Hile Troy comes in the ring and hits Wildfire in the back of the neck. This causes Wildfire to release the hold, but he turns around slowly and extends his arms upward as the crowd roars, causing even Lord Hile Troy to take a step back.) MW: Listen to this crowd! JM: He's even got Lord Hile Troy a little bit intimidated! ('My Sherona' by the Knack plays as Wildfire rolls out of the ring and backs down the aisle, again not slapping hands with the fans.) JM: All I have to say is... Holocaust will pay... dearly. I know GKI. And they *WON'T* take this lightly. MW: And the tortured soul of Wildfire still knows no peace... maybe it will after TERROR NIGHT '97 when he gets a chance to avenge the ultimate betrayal of Bloodbath. (Commercial break. When we come back, Chad Johnson is in the ring along with referee Kendall Dolemeyer.) -*Harvesters of Sorrow vs. Nation of Denomination*- CHAD JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the following tag team matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit. Coming down the aisle... ('War Machine' by KISS plays as the fans boo.) CHAD JOHNSON: At a combined weight of 680 pounds, accompanied by Phantom Lord, Pain and Suffering, the Harvesters of Sorrow! JM: No cutesy gimmicks. No butt kissing. Just hurting. A couple of guys *I* can relate too! MW: These two men apparently have a past relationship with the Reverend, they are the two latest additions to his cult or "church," if you will, and they will also be in the TRIPLE CAGE OF TERROR at TERROR NIGHT '97. CHAD JOHNSON: And their opponents... ('Amish Paradise' by Weird Al Yankovic plays as the fans cheer.) CHAD JOHNSON: At a combined weight of 556 pounds, Amish Farmer, Jehovah's Witness, the Nation of Denomination! JM: There's a couple of guys that are down to *your* level Mike. Nothing to worry about. Just how many cows there are left to milk, and trying to go steady with yer cousin/grandma/mom. (The bell rings.) MW: Suffering attacking before the bell with a big lariat, and Amish Farmer's on the cement! JM: Hah. MW: Suffering with a scoop slam and a cover! 1!....2!.....kickout! (Suffering tags to Pain as Amish Farmer gets up to his feet.) MW: Pain with Farmer.....big Bran Buster! Pain with a powerbomb, and Suffering with a buttdrop! JM: THE CHAIR OF PAIN! MW: Reverse DDT by Pain! JM: They're just whippin' out those great moves! Aren't they, Mike?! MW: Pain with a powerslam attempt.....tag to Jehovah's Witness! Sleeper! (Pain escapes the sleeperhold with a limp jawbreaker sit-down.) MW: Pain with a leaping double axhandle! 1!.....2!.....Shoulder up! Irish Whip....trip to Witness....and another reverse DDT! JM: I'm lovin' this! MW: Pain with a tag to Suffering....big splash! 1!....2!.....Shoulder up! Pickup and a neck snap by Suffering! (Jehovah's Witness rolls to the outside.) MW: Witness on the outside.....and Pain comes around the corner with a big clothesline! (Suffering leaps on Witness from the apron as Farmer takes down Pain with a dropkick.) MW: This match has just turned into a Donny-Brook! JM: And you're complaining?! (Suffering rolls back in the ring as Pain tosses Witness in and brawls with Farmer.) MW: Witness coming off the ropes.....float over into a sleeper! (Jehovah's Witness keeps the hold on until Amish Farmer gets onto the apron and signals for a tag.) MW: Tag to Farmer! Reverse neckbreaker, and Pain could be out for the count after that! 1!....2!.....foot on the ropes! JM: Never count these guys out for the count Mike! MW: Whip to the ropes by Farmer...and Pain makes the tag to Suffering! (Suffering walks calmly up to Farmer and slaps him across the face.) JM: Woohoo! MW: Suffering with a rather demeaning blow to Farmer....and a kick to the midsection! 1!....Kickout! (Suffering whips Farmer to the ropes, Farmer ducks a clothesline and applies an abdominal stretch.) MW: Stretch applied....and there's the tag to Witness! (Farmer keeps Suffering in the hold as Witness lands a few jabs to the gut.) MW: Excellent teamwork on the part of the Denomination... JM: Excellent cheating's more like it... MW: Suffering with a jab to the eye of Witness, and forcing a tag to Farmer! JM: Great strategy! Keep the fresh man OUT of the game! MW: Suffering with a big bulldog and a tag to Pain! Reverse DDT unto the Farmer! Bodyslam! Legdrop...and a big miss and a tag to Witness! JM: The moron! The fresh man was IN! MW: Witness charging in...and catching a double axhandle between the eyes! THE ANGRY CHAIR! 1!....2!....THR! AMAZING! JM: LUCK! MW: Tag to Suffering....and both of the Harvesters just stomping away on the big country boy from Pennsylvania! JM: Isn't wrestling one of the Amish' sins that sends ya straight to Hell? MW: I really don't know.. JM: Must be.....their career's already there! Heheh. MW: Double bodyslam by The Harvesters, and Pain's back in! Cover! 1!....2!....Shoulder up! Pain from the top! Flying headbutt! Cover! 1!....2!....Thr! Amish Farmer breaks the count with a flying lariat! (As Pain throws a few lefts to Farmer, Witness climbs up top.) MW: Farmer with a big bodyslam! And Witness from the top with the Watchtower! That's it! (Bloodbath and Sandman storm the ring as Witness makes the cover.) JM: Woohoo! The *true* way has arrived! MW: Sandman and Bloodbath have no reason to be here tonight! Oh my God! Bloodbath with a tombstone to Farmer! And a big clothesline shoves Witness to the outside! (Sandman hoists Farmer up top and drops him with a powerslam.) JM: SANDBLAST! THIS IS ABSOLUTELY GREAT! (Sandman spits at the audience.) MW: FANS WE HAVE TO APOLOGIZE FOR THE ACTIONS OF THE DUO OF SANDMAN AND BLOODBATH, BUT AT THIS POINT, WE SEE LITTLE THAT CAN STOP THEM! (The carnage continues as Sandman, Bloodbath, and the Harvesters of Sorrow continue to destroy the Nation of Denomination, until suddenly the crowd erupts!) MW: AND WILDFIRE HAS RETURNED! AND HE'S GOT THE CPE WITH HIM! JM: SECURITY! (Wildfire and the CPE [Mondo Gecko, El Grande Pistolerro, and the CPE Enforcers Taggart and Taurus] brawl with the Reverend's men.) MW: Ladies and gentlemen, we have to go to commercial, tape machines are rolling, if anything happens we'll let you know when we get back! (Commercial break. When we come back, Chad Johnson is in the ring along with head referee John Myering.) CWO LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION TITLE MATCH -*'The Brazilian Lover' Carlos Maia vs. Jason 'Flash' Varga*- CHAD JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. It is for the CWO Light Heavyweight Champion title. Coming down the aisle... ('You Give Love a Bad Name' by Bon Jovi plays as the fans boo.) CHAD JOHNSON: Weighing 235 pounds, from Rio de Janeiro. Brazil, accompanied to the ring by Joe Root, 'The Brazilian Lover' Carlos Maia! (Maia comes to ringside getting boos from a great majority of the women he tries so desperately to impress.) MAIA: (to camera) AM I NOT BEST LOOKING MAN IN CWO?! I AM NO FAT PIG LIKE VARGA, AND I AM NO A STUPID FAT PIG LIKE SANCHEZ! I AM SIMPLY (poses) TOO GOOD FOR THEM! ROOT: Ha ha ha! You're the man, Carlos! MW: Maia's foreign roots showing through, with his attempts at a well formed sentence... JM: Like to see you try and speak Portuguese. Besides, with a smart guy like Joe Root with him, he doesn't need to speak English! MW: I'm not so sure of Root's ability to speak it either.... CHAD JOHNSON: And his opponent... ('Fanfare for the Common Man' by Aaron Copland plays as the fans cheer.) CHAD JOHNSON: Weighing 235 pounds, from Jordan. NY, accompanied by Jim Peer, the CWO Light Heavyweight Champion, Jason 'Flash' Varga! JM: Did someone say fruit? MW: Will you stop? What is your problem with him? JM: Plenty. That's what. (The bell rings as Maia comes off the ropes with an elbow to the back.) MW: Elbow from Maia, and Varga comes back with one of his own! (Varga stomps away on the downed Maia before failing to land an elbowdrop.) MW: Maia with a whip to the far side.....and a superkick takes the champ off his feet! Flying bodypress from the second rope! Pickup... DDT! JM: WOW! Is that new champion I smell!? MW: Varga slowly up to his feet....and fires off a chop! Another! And a bodyslam courtesy of Maia lays him back down! Legdrop! JM: Haha! Pressed fruit! (Jim Peer grabs the ankle of Maia, catching his attention.) JM: Look at that little weasel.... MW: Peer getting involved here....allowing Varga to land a body-press! And Joe Root isn't going to get involved, even against a bodyguard, you saw what happened to him at SEPTEMBER SLAM II when he tried to compete against the likes of Wayne "College Guy" Schillo! (Maia rolls over with the press, but Varga gets out before the 1 count.) MW: Great counter by Maia, followed up by a whip to the near side... sunset flip by The Brazilian! Varga puts the brakes on... and misses with a sit-down cover! (Maia leaps to the top buckle, gyrates his hips, and lands a flying Frankensteiner on Varga.) JM: FALLING IN LOVE! MW: That brutal Frankensteiner from the top definitely found it's mark on the champ.... Cover and a fistful of tights! 1!....2!..... (The ref sees the tights and breaks the count.) MW: Maia complaining to the ref....and Varga comes from behind with a small package! 1!.....Reversal! 1!.....2!....Kickout! (Varga comes off the ropes with a chop, but Maia knocks him to the mat with a shove and chops away on him.) MW: The mean streak of Maia showing through here in this matchup, and a falling forearm puts the champ on his belly! Maia from the top... flying axhandle! (Varga catches him on the way down with a fist to the gut.) MW: Fist to the midsection puts an end to Maia's antics for the time being! Varga unloading with a series of punches and closing with a kick to the chest! Varga from the second buckle... ASAI MOONSAULT! (Varga picks up a flattened Maia and whips him to the ropes.) MW: Maia going for the ride....and hitting the brakes! Superkick! NO! Varga with a duckunder the leg! Bodypress! Rollover by Maia! 1!.....kickout! JM: This guy's too much! (As Varga kips up to his feet, Maia absolutely clocks him with a vicious Superkick.) JM: Haha! Like to see him duck that one! MW: Maia with a whip to the near ropes.......and a rana takes the champ to the mat! (Maia argues with the ref as suddenly, the Reverend appears from under the ring!) MW: Oh my god, it's the Reverend! JM: Ahhh! Get me out of here! (The Reverend releases a flash of light into the eyes of Varga.) MW: We've seen that trick before, ask Diamondback Chris Myers, and now Jason "Flash" Varga has fallen prey to it as well! (The Reverend leaves through the crowd, as they give the mysterious man a wide berth.) JM: A flash to the "Flash," you've gotta love the irony! MW: Referee didn't see it, he was arguing with Maia, who's now going up top! MAIA (to crowd): THIS WILL BE PROVING WHO IS REAL CHAMPION, CWO! JM: FALLING IN LOVE! ALL OVER AGAIN! AHAHA! MW: Maia with a cover and a handful of tights! 1!......2!......3! JM: HE DID IT! HE DID IT! CHAD JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen... your winner in 10:28... and NEW CWO Light Heavyweight champion...'THE BRAZILIAN LOVER'... CARRRRLOS... MAAAAAIIIIIIIIAAAAAAAA! ('You Give Love A Bad Name' plays to massive boos as Maia poses, on the second buckle, to the crowd, the belt around his waist as Joe Root celebrates with him.) MW: There you have it fans. The NEW CWO Light HHHHHHeavyweight champion... I have to give credit where it's due folks, Maia did manage to defeat one of the toughest, fightingest champions this sport has ever seen, but he did it obviously with a lot of help from the Reverend!! JM: Fruitiest... flashiest... feminine.... Hey, maybe Diamondback Chris Myers can recommend a good optometrist for Varga! Or at least a good seeing-eye dog company! (Laughs maniacally.) MW: Shut up, you sick freak! "Big" Jim Peer tending to his friend Varga, who is clutching his eyes, he's going to need medical attention, god help us if it's as bad or worse as when the Reverend did it to Diamondback Chris Myers. Folks, we've gotta go, this is Mike Williams signing off for Dr. Doom Josh Miller, and inviting you to join in next week, for more CWO Sunday Showcase! (Scene fades out to a panning view of the arena, where Maia is cockily strutting down the aisle with his newly won belt around his waist, Joe Root patting him on the back, as both get pelted with garbage.)
