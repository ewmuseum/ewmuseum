CWO EYE ON THE STORM: TRIPLE THREAT

25 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWO EYE ON THE STORM: TRIPLE THREAT 1/25/97 "LIVE" FROM CHICAGO, IL (ROSEMONT HORIZON) DARK MATCH: Revolution (Cake Boy and Lafayette) won a triangle match. Revolution defeated Hoss and Little Joe in 23:52 when Lafayette pinned Hoss after the Bayonette (running thrust to the throat). Revolution defeated David and Goliath in 24:23 when Lafayette pinned David "The Destroyer" Davis with the Bayonette. (EYE ON THE STORM opening comes up as we go to "Killer" Kyle Melinn and David "The Truth" Thomas as the crowd roars, a pyrotechnic display going off in the arena.) "KILLER" KYLE MELINN: Good evening, everyone, and welcome to EYE ON THE STORM: TRIPLE THREAT here on the PREVUE channel! I'm "Killer" Kyle Melinn, and with me as always is my broadcasting partner, David "The Truth" Thomas! DAVID "THE TRUTH" THOMAS: That's 1996 ANNOUNCER OF THE YEAR David "The Truth" Thomas to you, Melinn! MELINN: We have two great matches here on EYE ON THE STORM to give you a taste of what the actual event will be like. Also, we will be going to the CWO Control Center with Johnny Starr to get a complete run-down of the card and also comments from two of the three participants in the mega-main event tonight, for the World Championship, the champion Master of Darkness Damion, the former champion Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo, and the man who would be champion, Johnny Reb. THOMAS: Yeah, but as the Master has promised, Reb will never hold gold in the CWO while the Master is in the league! MELINN: That remains to be seen, but for now let's go to the ring! (Tony the K is in the ring, along with referee Gary Alles.) TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is a triangle match. Introducing first, coming down the aisle... (Battlecat's theme music plays as the fans cheer his entrance. Battlecat is wearing a "Wild Ones" T-shirt.) TONY THE K: Weighing 247 pounds, from Mexico City, Mexico, Battlecat! (Battlecat comes down slapping hands.) TONY THE K: His opponent... ("Carmina Burana" by Karl Orff plays as the fans cheer the entrance of Clark Gray.) TONY THE K: Weighing 260 pounds, from Minneapolis, MN, Clark Gray! (Clark Gray comes down to the ring, very somberly.) MELINN: And a man who perhaps should not be here tonight, given his current mental state over the tragedy of Wayne Carpenter. THOMAS: And he wasn't at the funeral, what's up with that? MELINN: Well, as I've said before, I guess people handle grief in different ways. THOMAS: Yeah, but missing your partner's funeral? That's strange. TONY THE K: And finally... (The lights shut off as mixed reaction is heard as "Graveyard Symphony" plays, signifying the entrance of Bloodbath, who is bathed in a red spotlight as he makes his quiet yet fearsome walk down to the ring.) TONY THE K: Weighing 328 pounds, from Death Valley, CA, Bloodbath! (Bloodbath makes his way down to the ring.) THOMAS (sarcastically): Here's my vote for "Most Original Gimmick" of the year. Now all this guy needs is a manger named Mort Ishin. Sheesh. MELINN: The lights are back on, the bell has rung, and we are underway. Clark Gray against Battlecat, both men trading blows right off the bat, Battlecat getting the better of Gray momentarily, he goes up top... flying clothesline! Battlecat goes up top again... flying kneedrop... and Clark Gray rolls out of the way! THOMAS: Battlecat a long-time wrestler in the lucha libre tradition down in Mexico, you can see it in the risks he takes. However, as I've said before, the more risks you take, the more mistakes you make. MELINN: Gray stomping away on Battlecat, he goes for the pin, 1... shoulder up. Gray goes for the shoulderbreaker, but Battlecat slips out, Battlecat with a Russian legsweep! THOMAS: Planted him with that one! Battlecat has great speed, he's a little bit larger than your average luchador, but he's as slippery as a snake. MELINN: Battlecat whips Gray into the ropes... big clothesline. Forearm smash, whip into the ropes again... another clothesline. Battlecat up top... flying elbow drop! Gray staggering to his feet... Spinning Wheel Kick! That could be it, 1... 2... thr... no, Gray just barely gets his shoulder up. THOMAS: Gray's in real trouble here right off the bat. This guy should not be in the ring, he does not have his full mental capacity, not that he ever did. MELINN: And now Battlecat has got that Wild Ones T-shirt, he's choking Gray with it! THOMAS: Well, in all of these Triple Threat matches, there are no disqualifications, no count-outs, everything's truly out in the open, and it's good to see Battlecat bending the rules a bit. MELINN: You always say that! Battlecat whips Gray into the ropes... another clothesline. Up top again... flying elbow drop! Gray rolls outside the ring, trying to regroup, but Battlecat follows him outside and smashes him into the ringside steps! Battlecat rolls back in the ring, followed by Gray. Battlecat with a cover, 1... 2... shoulder up. Battlecat with a brainbuster! THOMAS: Textbook brainbuster, crashing Gray down on his head, a very well performed move. MELINN: Battlecat trying to apply a Boston crab, but Gray using his impressive musculature to resist it. THOMAS: Former Mr. Minneapolis, this guy does have an impressive build, with a little training, he could be a good power wrestler. MELINN: Who's going to train him? You? THOMAS: I just might! MELINN: Gray tags to Bloodbath, and we get our first look at this big monster of a man. THOMAS: Yeah, he's willing to "undertake" any task. MELINN: Bloodbath whips Battlecat into the corner... working on Battlecat with a series of hard blows. Battlecat is down, and now Bloodbath with a figure four applied... he lets it go, nails Battlecat with a legdrop. Back on the figure four now, Battlecat screaming in pain, and again, Bloodbath lets it go. THOMAS: Both times he really hasn't had the move cinched in that well, Bloodbath a very power-orientated wrestler, it's admirable to see him trying some moves, but stick to what works. MELINN: Battlecat back on his feet and... oh my... Bloodbath has got him up... Tombstone Powerbomb! Forget about it! Cover, 1... 2... 3! TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, Battlecat has been eliminated in 5:52! MELINN: And as we take you to our Split Screen, you can see this devastating move finishing Battlecat. THOMAS: At least that's original... sort of. MELINN: Meanwhile, Clark Gray is in the ring right away, he whips Bloodbath into the ropes... big back body drop! Gray going for a figure four... he's got it hooked... but Bloodbath turns it over! THOMAS: Well, Gray should have figured that if Bloodbath knows how to put it on, he probably knows how to counter it. MELINN: Gray reaches the ropes, Bloodbath picks him back up and whips him into the ropes... both men with a clothesline! Both men are down! Referee not making a count, as we've said before, there are no disqualifications or countouts in any of these matches tonight, however, a ref can stop a match if he so desires. THOMAS: Don't stop it now, these guys aren't finished! MELINN: Both men getting to their feet, Bloodbath a little quicker, he's up on the top rope... flying fist to the head! Double axhandle by the big man, he whips Gray into the ropes... but Gray is quicker, he backdrops Bloodbath! Gray now with a headscissors applied. THOMAS: Good desperation move there by Gray, he needs to avoid that Tombstone Powerbomb, we saw how easily it finished off Battlecat. MELINN: Gray lets the headscissors go, he picks Bloodbath up, he goes for a punch... look at this! Bloodbath caught the hand of Gray, he's twisting Gray's arm around, Gray yelling in pain... the crowd reacting... Bloodbath whips Gray into the ropes... big lariat! THOMAS: Gray may have had a good strategy a few moments ago, keep the big man on the mat, immobilize him with holds, keep away from a neutral base. MELINN: Bloodbath whips Gray into the corner... working him over now... Bloodbath with a chokehold, but Gray counteracts with an ear ringer. Gray goes for a backbreaker but it's blocked by Bloodbath. Bloodbath throws Clark Gray out of the ring! THOMAS: Bloodbath perhaps a bit stunned by that ear ringer, it may have affected the execution of that backbreaker. MELINN: Both men brawling outside the ring, Gray now back in the ring, stomping on Bloodbath before he gets in, Gray going for the Abdominal Stretch... no, Bloodbath hiptosses him out of it. THOMAS: Bloodbath a hard man to get an Abdominal Stretch on properly, being so tall. MELINN: Bloodbath now with a figure four leglock applied... he lets it go and begins to stomp on Gray. THOMAS: Again, he doesn't keep those holds on very long, may be a mistake, take advantage of those submission holds to wear your opponent down, I say. MELINN: Bloodbath with a forearm drop, back on the figure four leglock, he lets it go, Gray with a poke to the eyes. THOMAS: Now there's a move we don't see any of the Aces using a lot! MELINN: Gray going for a powerslam, but Bloodbath with an inside cradle! 1... 2... thr... and Gray kicks out with authority. Gray on his feet, trying to get some energy, Bloodbath hammering on Gray, but the blows seem to be having no effect. THOMAS: Have to give Bloodbath credit, that inside cradle out of nowhere was a good move. MELINN: Gray with a DDT! That could have done it! Crowd reacting to that move, cover, 1... 2... kickout. THOMAS: Should have hooked the leg, that could have been a major mistake. MELINN: Gray with an inside cradle... no, wait a minute... it's reversed! 1... 2... 3! TONY THE K: The winner of the match in 11:58, Bloodbath! ("Graveyard Symphony" plays as mixed reaction is heard as Bloodbath slowly leaves.) MELINN: And as we go back to the replay, we can see here, Gray with a devastating DDT, didn't get the pin, goes for an inside cradle, but the reversal by Bloodbath wins the match. And you can see Gray is very upset, chastising the referee, but the match goes down as a win in the record books for Bloodbath. We now take you to Johnny Starr in the control center. (Johnny Starr is in the control center.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, we hope you're enjoying EYE ON THE STORM on the PREVUE channel. If you have not done so already, I urge you to call your local cable operator and ask for TRIPLE THREAT, because you can't even imagine the excitement that's going to be happening tonight. Let's look at the complete lineup of matches for tonight's card. Highlanders vs. Coldfront vs. Mercenaries STARR: Three of the top teams in the CWO collide in the opening contest for TRIPLE THREAT, should be a great one. Vincent "The Lip" Manocelli vs. "Big" Rich Clark vs. Baby Waldo STARR: "Big" Rich Clark and Baby Waldo have feuded over the past months here in the CWO, Manocelli may be caught in the crossfire of two giants of the ring. Joe Eagle vs. Jackass Joel vs. Panther STARR: Breaking his alliance with Blood Inc., the newly hopeful fan favorite Panther goes after two veterans of the ring, former CM2WF World Champion Joe Eagle and former CM2WF North American champion Jackass Joel. Big Cool vs. D. E. A. T. H. vs. Regulators STARR: Three more top teams in the CWO collide in this match, another great tag team contest. Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Gold Kard vs. Brian "The Hammer" Williams STARR: Alles and Gold Kard formerly "partners" in the attempt to take over the CWO and make it the CM2WF once again. That plot ultimately failed when Gold Kard was defeated by Hile Troy at CWO CELEBRATION. However, before that even occurred, Gold Kard attacked Alles when Alles questioned Gold Kard's authority on the December 10th TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER. Subsequently, Alles prevented Gold Kard from walking out on his match with Troy, throwing him back in the ring and enabling Troy to get the victory. Alth ough they both claim that this match is "business, not personal," you can be sure that a lot of heat will be felt between those two. Thrown into the mix is rookie friend of Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo, Brian "The Hammer" Williams, and both Gold Kard and Alles have told him not so politely to "stay the hell out of the way." TV CHAMPIONSHIP: Maximum Overdrive (champion) vs. Miguel Sanchez vs. Sin STARR: Overdrive and Sanchez have been feuding since September, and we've seen many a wild match between these two. Throw in the unorthodox Sin, who has also made the 1996 Rookie of the Year Sanchez his target, and you have the makings of an intense brawl, with the Television championship at stake. NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Eternal Wanderers (champions) vs. Pit Bosses vs. Bad Guys STARR: All three of these teams have held the North American titles during the past few months, so a real triangle situation already exists. Three top brawling teams collide, and only one will come out with the titles still intact. NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP: Hile Troy (champion) vs. Bane vs. Sandman STARR: The 1996 Feud of the Year spills into 1997, as Bane and Sandman both gun for "marked man" Hile Troy, the current holder of the CWO North American championship. Sandman, who has vowed to "remake" the Chaotic Dynasty, this time by his designs, has made it clear that he bears no allegiance to his former partner with a vicious attack on this week's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER. This one may be the most intense match of the night! WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Creatures of the Night (champions) vs. Biblical Powers vs. Deadly Sins STARR: Frustrated at CWO CELEBRATION, the Biblical Powers gun for the titles again in a match with not only their arch enemies the Deadly Sins, but also the dominant World Tag Team Champions the Creatures of the Night. WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: Master of Darkness Damion (champion) vs. Johnny Reb vs. Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo STARR: The big one, the main event, with the champion, the former champion, and the man who would be champion all involved. MOD Damion vs. Johnny Reb was a close runner-up to the Bane vs. Sandman feud for Feud of the Year, and it started back last September, when Damion first made his vow that Reb would never hold a title in the CWO as long as Damion was there. Reb was also frustrated at CWO CELEBRATION, when his own trainer and mentor, former CWO champion Rebel Yell, turned against him and joined the " Dark Side." Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo was thrown into the mix when he was defeated by Damion for the title on the December 3rd TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER. However, any possible thought of an alliance between Reb and Schillo was shattered this week on TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER when Johnny Reb, disorientated by an attack by Damion while the lights were off, put Schillo in the Texas Cloverleaf, thinking that Schillo had been responsible for the attack. Although CWO officials were able to pull Reb off of Schillo, you can be sure that Schillo now has a healthy fear of being put in that move again. We now take you to comments from all three men. ("Sweet Home Alabama" by Lynyrd Skynyrd plays as footage from various Johnny Reb matches is shown with various voice-overs included. Footage of Johnny Reb vs. Sandman is shown.) JOHNNY REB: The baddest men in the business have felt the pain. They all know what it's lahk to hear the vertebrae crack... (Footage from Reb and Beauregard Dixon vs. the Coldfront is shown.) REB: And there can be but one end for all of them: submission... (Footage from Reb vs. Wayne Schillo is shown.) REB: one mistake is all it takes. Schillo, y'all once made a mistake...then ah made one of mah own. Well, not ta'naht; not evuh again. (Footage from Reb vs. Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Miguel Sanchez is shown.) REB: People can yak all naht about how bad this move is or they can cripple someone lahk that...hogwash. I've proven tahm an' tahm again; nobody beats the Texas Clovuhleaf! Ta'naht, the Dark Side shall see the laht... (Footage of Reb vs. Master of Darkness Damion is shown.) REB: Ta'naht, Johnny Reb lives the dream. (Camera opens on MOD Damion who is seen standing in the center of a ring with no lights on in the arena except for a single spot light which shines down on him.) MOD DAMION: Johnny Reb, you have been running your mouth off a lot this last week about how you will beat me and Schillo this Saturday. Well, "mistah," I know you can't beat me and for that matter I don't think you can even beat Wayne Schillo, he was a champ once and you, you are nothing, and you were never anything. Last time we met you thought you had everything you needed to beat me but true to my word I proved you did not. I told you, you idiot, that you would never hold any gold in the CWO and that I would do anything to see that it stays that way. Our last meeting, like I said, you thought you had it all but your hero, your mentor saw the power of the Dark Side and like so many gave in to its power and joined with me against you. Now you have nothing, you can't even count on Schillo for help after you attacked him from behind in the dark. Perhaps he shall join with me too and see that you do not survive for very long in our match. I look forward to seeing both Wayne Schillo and you, Johnny Reb, in my DARKNESS one more time. I will enjoy ripping your soul away from your lifeless carcass after our match. See you both in this ring, my world, my "FOREVER NIGHT". LONG LIVE THE NIGHT! (Camera zooms in on MOD as he raises his hands above his head and the spotlight disappears and within seconds all the lights in the arena come on but MOD Damion is nowhere in sight, as the camera fades out. We go to Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo in the interview area.) WAYNE "KEUKA GUY" SCHILLO: The time for talking is over! Master of Darkness Damion, you have beaten me once, you took the 2nd most precious thing to me away, and now I get another shot. Johnny Reb, I've said my piece about you, you know where I stand. Looking at the CWO Title History, it seems like the CWO World Championship changes ownership every month. Well, Damion, look for history to repeat itself tonight, and look for Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo to become the first two-time CWO World Champion! EIGHT TO ONE, BABY! (Back to Johnny as music plays.) STARR: You can feel the intensity as we get closer and closer to this big event. Don't miss out, call your cable operator right now. CWO TRIPLE THREAT, it's triple the excitement! (Back to Tony the K in the ring along with referee Tom Krepps.) TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is a triangle match. Introducing first, coming down the aisle... ("Danger Zone" by Kenny Loggins plays as the fans cheer the entrance of Flyboy.) TONY THE K: Weighing 240 pounds, from Annapolis, MD, Flyboy! (Flyboy comes down slapping hands before getting into the ring.) MELINN: And again, it seems like another of the Aces are dealing with the tragedy by getting into the ring. THOMAS: All of them are morons. TONY THE K: His opponent... ("Kalifornia Love" by 2Pac and Dr. Dre plays as the fans boo the entrance of Steel and Da Boss.) TONY THE K: Weighing 265 pounds, from Compton, CA, accompanied by his manager Da Boss, Steel! MELINN: The de facto leader of Blood, Inc., now that Panther has apparently defected. THOMAS: Panther has made the biggest mistake in his life. He could have had it all, with the strong backing of Blood Inc. behind him, but now he's a marked man himself. TONY THE K: And finally... ("Down" by 311 plays as the fans boo the entrance of Inferno and Jimbo Jones.) TONY THE K: Weighing 305 pounds, from parts unknown, accompanied by his manager Jimbo Jones, Inferno! MELINN: We haven't seen this guy in a while, but he looks ready to go. THOMAS: A former Ultimate Extreme Fighting champion, this guy can take it to you. MELINN: All right, bell has rung, we're underway, Inferno starting against Steel, and he starts out strong! Power bomb! Legdrop! Cover, 1... 2... kickout. Inferno goes for a falling headbutt... but Steel rolls out of the way. THOMAS: Head, meet mat! Play nice! MELINN: Steel going to work now, stomping on Inferno, elbow drop, stomping away some more, headlock applied... he lets it go... he goes up top... flying cross body block! Steel picks up Inferno, pokes him in the eyes, spinning roundhouse kick! THOMAS: Don't let this "gangsta" image fool you, Steel a student of the martial arts, as you saw by that kick right there. MELINN: Steel now choking Inferno, he goes up top again... flying headbutt! Headlock applied... he lets it go and whips Inferno into the ropes... dropkick! Steel with an elbow smash, followed by a shoulder tackle. Steel goes for a powerslam... wait a minute... Inferno cinching in a half nelson... half nelson suplex! Oh my! THOMAS: He could have broken his neck with that one! MELINN: Out of nowhere, and a cover, 1... 2... 3! TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, Steel has been eliminated in 4:41. MELINN: Inferno celebrating, playing to the crowd... look out! Flyboy off the top turnbuckle with a flying bodypress! THOMAS: Inferno needs to pay attention, Flyboy is a dangerous high flyer, he doesn't make very many mistakes. MELINN: Flyboy with a bodyslam, followed by a splash, Flyboy goes up top again... going for the Flying Bodypress... and look at that! Jimbo Jones shaking the ropes, and Flyboy falls down on the top turnbuckle! THOMAS: He'll be a flying nun, now! MELINN: Flyboy falls back in the ring, writhing in pain, Inferno whips him into the ropes... back body drop! Whip into the corner... Inferno nails Flyboy with an elbow smash, then a chop. Inferno with a power bomb! Goes for an elbow drop... Flyboy rolls out of the way! THOMAS: Flyboy a very speedy light heavyweight, the power and wrestling attack is what Inferno should use, it's worked well so far. MELINN: Flyboy stomping away on Inferno, he goes up top again... Flying Bodypress... and this time he hits! That could be it... wait a minute... Jimbo Jones up on the ring apron, distracting the referee, and Flyboy nails him! The crowd loving that... look out! Inferno from behind, clipping the knee of Flyboy. THOMAS: Smart move, as I always suggest, you have to hobble these high flyers. MELINN: Inferno with a power bomb, followed by a legdrop! Cover, 1... kickout. Inferno goes for an inside cradle but Flyboy is out before a 1 count. Flyboy quickly up on the top turnbuckle... flying bodypress... and Inferno catches him in mid-air! THOMAS: Uh oh! MELINN: Inferno turning Flyboy's attempted bodypress into a fisherman's suplex! Impressive maneuver, 1... 2... and Flyboy is in the ropes. THOMAS: Lucky. MELINN: Inferno now setting up Flyboy for a piledriver... he nails it! Driving Flyboy's head into the mat, forget about it! Cover, 1... 2... 3! TONY THE K: The winner of the match in 8:46, Inferno! ("Down" by 311 plays as the fans boo Inferno and Jimbo Jones, as they leave.) MELINN: And Flyboy still trying to recover from that devastating piledriver, Inferno with an impressive win tonight... wait a minute... Clark Gray has returned... he's coming down to ringside... those of you with us on the PREVUE channel... we hope you can join us on TRIPLE THREAT... good night from Chicago! (Creds come up for the benefit of the PREVUE channel.) THOMAS: Look at the look on Gray's face, Melinn, he is not happy. CLARK GRAY (grabbing the mike): Another loss! How much do we have to lose, Flyboy, huh? How much can we lose? You know, in these times of low morale, we "aces" look for a leader, and we have to look to you. You remember that night, Flyboy, that night where I wanted to get the hell out of the locker room, but you were still signing autographs? Huh? You remember that night? "Just a few more autographs, Clark, then we can get out of here." If we would have left when I wanted to, then... then Wayne would still be alive. MELINN: Now, this is unfortunate, you can't blame... THOMAS: Shut up, Melinn, I want to hear this! FLYBOY (taking the mike): Listen, Clark, I know... I know you're upset... this is probably the darkest time that the Aces have ever went through, and I'm having just as hard of a time with it as you are. I know Wayne was your cousin, but he was my friend too, dammit. Now, I know that you missed the funeral, and I can't say I'm not disappointed, but we all have our own roads to take when it comes to this tragedy, so I understand. But Clark, I really think you need some help, man. There's no reason why any of us have to face this alone. We have to pull together, or we're all lost, so come on man... (As Flyboy tries to put his arm around Clark's shoulder, Clark snaps and nails Flyboy in the face.) MELINN: Oh no, oh no... THOMAS: I love it! MELINN: Shut up, you sick... (Gray has Flyboy on the mat, and he wails on Flyboy. Tears stream from Clark's eyes.) MELINN: And a tragedy turns more tragic if you can believe it... (Steve Keller comes down to the ring as Clark Gray leaves, covering his face with his arm as the fans boo.) MELINN: And now Clark is leaving, you can see the tears of rage coming down his face... the fans booing, Steve Keller attending to Flyboy, this is really... well, folks, we have to go, we hope you can join us for TRIPLE THREAT, this is Kyle Melinn, David "The Truth" Thomas, we... well, we'll see you in a few minutes. 10... 9... 8... 7... 6... 5... 4... 3... 2...
