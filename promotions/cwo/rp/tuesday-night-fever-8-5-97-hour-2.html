THE OUTRAGEOUS WRESTLING FEDERATION ==================================================== INTRODUCTION This is not your usual e-wrestling federation like you are in now. This fed is a joint fed that I giving the opportunity to as many feds as I can. By putting together a select group of great wrestlers, some great cards can be assembled! But there is no role playing, it's more of a professional type situation where only the best gimmicks and wrestlers make it, not the best role players. So basically all you have to do is fill out the form below for only one of your wrestlers, or for one of your tag teams. I am only allowing either a singles wrestler or tag team for each person so that the number of wrestlers I have to choose from is not so huge! So send me your best wrestler(s) and see if your fantasy wrestler can make it with the best of the best! CARDS You'll see two big cards a week. Tuesday Night Thunder and Friday Night Inferno will be the two big cards for the week. There will be a highlight show on Sunday called Sunday replay, but there will be no new matches on that card. ASSOCIATES I need associates! If you would like to be in my 11 man crew of voters, let me know. What you'd be is my assistants. When I have an important decision to make that I really can't, I will ask all 11 to vote and I expect an answer before the deadline. I will be selecting some back ups in case you will be gone for some time, but I need to be informed. Your e-mail addresses WILL only be kept by me so no one can e-mail and try and sweet talk you. And you will not be allowed to vote on an issue involving your wrestler if you currently have one. You do no have to have a wrestler currently active in the OWF to be my associate either. SELECTIONS I plan on selecting 50 wrestlers to get started. These will be picked on criteria determined by me. The same goes for my 11 associates and back ups. Those not selected will be put on a waiting list and just like any fed, if a current wrestler is not making it, he will be fired. The person fired will be allowed to create another gimmick, can be the same wrestler with a different gimmick, but that wrestler will then be put on the waiting list. To replace the void, I will hire a new wrestler off the waiting list that fits the criteria. PAY PER VIEWS There will be PPV's also. The OWF will have cards a lot like other e-feds, but the big difference is that you don't have to role play. You have to have the right stuff to make it in the OWF, a lot like real federations. There will be one pay per view a month. INPUT I don't mind hearing input from active wrestlers and waiting list wrestlers. Input makes things better, but I don't want to hear complaining about how your wrestler is doing. I will always give you one warning for things like complaints and stuff. But after that, I assume the right to fire you and NOT allow you another wrestler. So don't get carried away in what you say to me. CONCLUSION You have to do this! It's a blast! I will only require you to speak to me when I ask. You don't have to role play since you are busy role playing in many feds already. I will try my hardest to get everyone there chance in the OWF, whether long or short, but I will try in due time. I really want to get this started soon, so get the applications in soon as I plan on starting my selections very soon! It's fun though, and see if your wrestler really has what it takes to be the best in the e-fed world!
