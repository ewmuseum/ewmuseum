Hopefully this is the last time you're going to see a card in this form. After next week, FIGHTIN' FRIDAY NIGHT will be cancelled. I'm happy with just having two quality shows a week, FEVER and SHOWCASE, rather than bordering on overkill status. Because the matches for next week have already been run, I will produce a card for next week, but after that we will just be concentrating on the two shows, as well as the PPV's, of course. Thanks for your patience in the matter. Brent G.
