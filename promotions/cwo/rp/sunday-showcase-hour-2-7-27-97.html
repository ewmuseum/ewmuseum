THAT COULD BE ALL. 1...2...KICKOUT BY REB!! RUSSELL: Reb showing his resilience and Lone Wolf tries to hit him with those brass knuckles but Reb ducks and knife edge chop and a forearm smash. TONY THE K: This match is scheduled for one fall with a twenty time limit. Introducing first.... ("Sweet Dreams" by Marilyn Manson plays as crowd boos.) TONY THE K: Weighing in at a combined weight of 761 accompanied by Lust and Wrath, Pride and Sloth, The Deadly Sins! (The Deadly Sins make their way down to the ring.) TONY THE K: And their opponents ("Questioning My Sanity" by L7 plays as crowd cheers.) TONY THE K: Weighing in at a combined weight of 691 pounds, "Monster" Rich Clark & Miguel Sanchez!
