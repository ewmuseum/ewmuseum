CWO SHOWCASE 1/12/97

14 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWO SHOWCASE 1/12/97 Producer: Jim Longo ( [detail withheld] ) Executive Producer: Brent G. Alles ( [detail withheld] ) (The opening music begins to play as we see flashes of the wrestlers to appear tonight: Sin, Ron Johnson, Talon, Mysteria, Dark Raven, Wayne Carpenter, the Flame Spirits, Hoss and Little Joe, Benjamin Blake Bentley, Kamikaze, Wayne Schillo, Tiger Rogers, Barry Hart, and finally, holding his championship belt aloft, Hile Troy. The CWO SHOWCASE logo fades in as the music ends, and the camera pans to Kevin Rinaldi and the lovely Tamara.) KR: Hello, everyone, and welcome to CWO SHOWCASE!!! Happy New Year! TW: Should old acquaintance be forgot.... KR: Not now, Tamara, we have a card hot enough to melt the polar icecaps! In our opener, Ron Johnson finds out if he has to pay for his Sin! TW: Groan!!!! Talon gets his claws into Mysteria! KR: And you thought MINE was bad? Dark Raven battles Wayne Carpenter! TW: The Flame Spirits try to burn Hoss and Little Joe! Hey, Kevin, you know what Mrs. Cartwright used to say to Lorne Greene? KR: No, what? TW (sings to the "Bonanza" theme): "Get it up, get it in, get it out, don't mess my hair up!!!" KR (grinning, apparently he and Tamara have mended their fences) I'm not sure you can say something like that on TV.....Benjamin Blake Bentley knocks heads with Kamikaze! TW: He of the fabulous butt, Wayne Schillo, tries to cage Tiger Rogers! KR: And in our main event, former World Champion Barry Hart takes on the current North American champion Hile Troy!!!! We'll be back after this message from our sponsors!!! ****COMMERCIAL BREAK**** KR: And we're ready for our first match!!! Let's go to the ring!! TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. ("Welcome to Planet M/F" by White Zombie plays as the fans boo) TONY THE K: Coming down the aisle, weighing 240 pounds, from Petoskey, Michigan, Sin!!!! (Theme music plays as the fans keep booing) TONY THE K: And his opponent, weighing 238 pounds, from Attica, NY.... (There is a tremendous explosion from the dressing room area) TW: What was that? (People are screaming, running around...things are getting seriously chaotic) KR: There's been some kind of disturbance, coming from the locker rooms! Ron Johnson and his manager had just reached the ring area...both he and Sin look confused; they don't know if they're going to have the match or not!! TW: They're not!!! Security's escorting them from the ring...and everyone's heading for the exits!!!!! KR: We'll take a break, but we will be back after we find out what's going on! ****COMMERCIAL BREAK**** (The camera fades in on Kevin and a visibly shaken Tamara--they are outside the arena) KR: We're back!!! CWO Security did a commendable job getting the fans and the wrestlers out of the arena--this is what we know so far. An explosive device was detonated in the locker room area customarily reserved for fan favorites, specifically in an area used by the Four Aces. As you may know, Wayne Carpenter was scheduled to wrestle Dark Raven on tonight's card. We don't know if he or any of his teammates were in the area at the time of the explosion, but we're trying to find out. The local polic e and fire departments are on the scene, and... damn, I can see at least three ambulances. We can only hope that any injuries are minor. We'll try to get a statement from fire chief David Szbornik....Chief, can you tell us what happened? CHIEF DAVID SZBORNIK: We're still trying to get at the epicenter of the explosion, but we have questioned several of the wrestlers, and we believe that all four of the Four Aces were in the locker room area when this occurred. TW: How are they??? Are they all right??? CDS: At this moment, we have no information on their conditions. Excuse me. (He leaves the camera's view) KR: This is terrible.....we'll be back after this. ****COMMERCIAL BREAK**** KR: We're back.......several stretchers have been conveyed from the arena to the ambulances, but we can't get close enough to see who was on them. There's still no word on the condition of any of the Four Aces, but all other wrestlers known to be in the arena have been accounted for--the only injuries were minor cuts and bruises, suffered by Wayne Schillo. If the Aces manage to get out of this in one piece, we can all count ourselves extremely lucky. Obviously, we will not be able to bring you tonight 's card......we apologize for the circumstances.....we'll be switching you to local programming, but we will cut in as other developments become known. For Tamara Whitley, this is Kevin Rinaldi saying......say a prayer. (The camera fades out) LATER THAT NIGHT.... (A booming voice interrupts the inane sitcom. The graphic "SPECIAL REPORT" fills the screen) Voice: This is a SPECIAL REPORT from CWO News. We take you to our reporter, Kevin Rinaldi. (The camera comes up on a haggard-looking Kevin Rinaldi) KR: Good evening, everyone. We have more information on the explosion that rocked the CWO arena earlier tonight. The police and fire departments have been over the scene, and, while no official word has come out, a source from within one of the departm ents has told us that the explosive was indeed a pipe bomb. Moreover, the bomb may have been planted by one of the wrestlers in the CWO. The Four Aces were taken to a local hospital--officials are withholding the name of the hospital to avoid mobs of fa ns descending on the wrestlers. Flyboy and Steve Keller have been listed in critical but stable condition, but Clark Gray and Wayne Carpenter are still in surgery. Wayne Schillo has been treated for his injuries and released; he should be out of action less than a week. The Aces, however, do appear to have been the primary targets of the bombing. We'll be returning to local programming, but we will try to bring you more information as we receive it. Again, the authorities have determined that it was, indeed, a bomb that sent the Four Aces and Wayne Schillo to the hospital, and two of the Aces, Clark Gray and Wayne Carpenter, remain in surgery. Reporting from the CWO ar ena, this is Kevin Rinaldi. (The "SPECIAL REPORT" graphic comes up, then the sitcom returns)
