CWO TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER - HOUR #1 "LIVE" FROM SAN FRANCISCO, CA 9/2/97 Producer: [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] ) Executive Producer: Brent G. Alles ( [detail withheld] ) (Fade in to a spinning globe, as the CWO logo is slowly superimposed over it.) VOICE-OVER: The CWO... providing wrestling excellence the world over.... (Fast montage of CWO action erupts on to the screen, as Aerosmith's "Fever" plays in the background. The CWO logo then explodes on to the screen, and we go to a roaring crowd in the Cow Palace in San Francisco, CA, as pyrotechnics go off to signal the start of hour #1 of CWO TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER. Johnny Starr is in the ring.) STARR: Good evening, everybody, and welcome to TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER! (Crowd cheers, as a chant of "J Starr! J Starr!" goes up.) STARR: Thank you very much. Ladies and gentlemen, please help me welcome the former CWO World Champion, JOE EAGLE! ("Motownphilly" plays as the fans go nuts over the entrance of Joe Eagle, who comes to the ring followed closely by Jason "Flash" Varga and Brian "The Hammer" Williams, manager Russell Hill, bodyguard "Big Guy" Jim Peer, and the North American Tag Team Champions The Big Cool, Jim "Jazzman" Taylor, and Jumbo Jenkins. The seven men are headed straight to the ring, without slapping hands as they usually do.) STARR: Well, Joe, you asked for some time tonight. The floor is yours. JOE EAGLE: Thanks, my man. Fans of the CWO, I have to tell you., this has been a trying time for the Eagle-man. Twice I had the World Championship in my hands, and twice I was thwarted by folks I thought were my friends. Johnny Reb... I have nothing to say to you. If you thought that LAST FEVER was bad, you just try to make it to the ring tonight. I swear to God, if I see you come down the aisle tonight, it will take every security guard the CWO has ever CONSIDERED hiring to keep me off of you. When you let me back in the ring at the team challenge series, I thought you were solidifying the friendship we'd created in the past few months. Well, "mah friend", you can GO TO HELL! STARR: Joe, what about Wayne Schillo? You guys had quite an exchange a few weeks ago here on Fever.... EAGLE: We'll get to Schillo in good time. You see, Jerry, I've renewed my promise to the fans. You guys are number one in my book. I'm out here for nobody but YOU! Unfortunately, this isn't like the old days of the CWO, or even the CM2WF. These days, you can't count on the people who say you can trust them. I am sick and tired of being lied to, deceived, cheated...well, bottom line is, there's only 6 people in the CWO that I trust anymore, and they're all in the ring with me right now. Jazzman, you and Jumbo have been with me from the beginning. Flash, you've given me your word, and I can respect you for what you've done here. Big Guy, Russell, you guys take your lives into your hands each time we come out here. And, of course, The Hammer. (The crowd cheers for Brian Williams.) EAGLE: Brian, we've known each other a long time. Longer than I've known Schillo, as a matter of fact. Brian, you are welcome into the family of the Tower of Power anytime you want! There's 2 faces missing from this family portrait right now, though. The first is coming to the ring right now. Ladies and gentlemen, my new manager, the first CWO World Champion...Vincent "The Lip" Manocelli! (Manocelli comes to the ring to the cheers of the crowd. He is 6'4, and weighs about 200 pounds. He walks with a pronounced limp because of an injury suffered at the hands of Rich Clark, which put Manocelli out of wrestling.) EAGLE: We've missed you, Vince. You belong here. There's one more, and I don't know what he'll do. Wayne, if you're listening, listen harder, because I'm only gonna say this once. We all make mistakes, and I make more than most folks. But, I admit when I'm wrong. I was wrong to hit you, but you left me no choice. Man, I don't know what to say... the family is not the same without you. Can you forgive me? (The crowd reacts as Wayne "College Guy" Schillo comes out of the entrance and makes his way down to the ring. His face is grim, and he does not slap hands with the fans like he usually does. He climbs in the ring, as the Tower of Power looks at him expectantly.) STARR: Wayne "College Guy" Schillo, thank you for joining us, you've heard Joe Eagle, what is your response? (Schillo looks down at his feet for a moment as the crowd bubbles with anticipation. Finally, he lifts his head, looking right into the eyes of Joe Eagle.) WAYNE "COLLEGE GUY" SCHILLO: You know, everybody knows the College Guy has been doing a lot of thinking as of late, thinking about such things I thought I knew about, like friendship, trust, and honor. You know, Joe, you talk about not being able to count on people, about being deceived, well when Joe Root and that idiot Vegas High Roller were pulling that farce, I couldn't tell everybody that I was innocent, because Gold Kard International was blackmailing me. But still, it really let me know who my tr ue friends were, because even though I couldn't say anything, everybody was ready to point the finger at me. (Joe Eagle is shown in close-up, a sad frown upon his face.) SCHILLO: Now, like I said before, I'm not going to paint my face all white and go hide out for the next 12 months. I've got better things to do with my time. First and foremost (he looks towards the camera) Vegas High Roller, Joe Root, fun and games are one thing, I love 'em myself, but like I said, you used to be after my hide for a "bounty," now the tables are turned, and I'm going to collect! (Crowd cheers.) SCHILLO: But as for you, Joe, and the rest of your "posse," let me say this. I hear you talking about Johnny, about how he betrayed you, and I see, Joe, that you still don't have any trust for anybody. SEPTEMBER SLAM II, revenge may be the name of the game, but Joe, what's going to be left when it's all over? What's the price for getting back that precious gold belt? (Shot of Joe Eagle is shown again, as he rubs his face.) I was so close to the edge that I may have fallen off forever, but the love of this man (he points to Gordy) and this woman (he hugs Amanda, who smiles) brought me back from the edge. I did things this year that I still have nightmares about, injured young wrestlers like myself because I was afraid of them overshadowing me. There's probably no way that I can ever apologize to those men, but if there's one thing I can do, is to try to live my life better than I was living it before.
