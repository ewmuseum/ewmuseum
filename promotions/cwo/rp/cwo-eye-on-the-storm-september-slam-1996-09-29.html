CWO EYE ON THE STORM: SEPTEMBER SLAM

29 September 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWO EYE ON THE STORM: SEPTEMBER SLAM "LIVE" FROM THE CYBERSPACE ARENA IN GRAND RAPIDS, MI 9/29/96 (EYE ON THE STORM graphic and music comes up as we go to the control room with Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, CWO fans, and welcome to EYE ON THE STORM: SEPTEMBER SLAM. We are only moments away from the first Play Per View event held by the revitalized and rejuvenated CWO. I'll be back in a few moments to give you the complete rundown of th e card, but we've got a great matchup to go to first. Kyle, Randy, and Dave, take it away! (Go to the ring as fireworks shoot off, the crowd roaring.) "KILLER" KYLE MELINN: Good evening, everyone, and welcome to the Cyberspace Arena here in Grand Rapids, MI! I'm "Killer" Kyle Melinn, and with me as always is the ubiquitous David "The Truth" Thomas, and he's going to be on his best behavior tonight, rig ht? DAVID "THE TRUTH" THOMAS (muttering): Yes... (other stuff under his breath) MELINN: Also joining us is the "Texas Rose," Randy Johnson! "TEXAS ROSE" RANDY JOHNSON: Well, thank you very much, Kyle Melinn, ah am so glad to be heah at the premiere event of the CWO. Lotta things on the lahn tonight, and a lot of clubberin'! MELINN: All right, let's go to the ring for our EYE ON THE STORM matchup! TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. Coming down the aisle... ("Kiddie Grinder (remix)" by Marilyn Manson plays as the fans boo) from parts unknown, weighing 324 pounds, Sorrow! MELINN: Very imposing figure in Sorrow, he's had a couple of impressive battles so far here in the CWO. TONY THE K: And his opponent... ("Six Foot Foam Skull" by Shallow, North Dakota plays as the fan cheer) weighing 243 pounds, from Pittsburgh, Philadelphia, Miguel Sanchez! (Sanchez comes down to the ring slapping hands, then rolls in and nails Sorrow with a dropkick!) MELINN: How about that! Sanchez wasting no time in this match. JOHNSON: Well, he cain't afford to, Kyle Melinn, he's got a big man in there, and he's got to take advantage of the situation. MELINN: Sanchez with another dropkick, and Sorrow is knocked down. Cover, 1... 2... kickout. Sanchez just battering Sorrow with dropkicks, he's stunning the big man. Sanchez playing to the crowd, that might have been a mistake... Sorrow clotheslines hi m out of the ring! THOMAS: Sanchez looking for that tequila sunrise... and it cost him! MELINN: Sorrow outside the ring now, he's got a chair... Sanchez kicks him in the midsection... Sanchez with a chair now... he nails Sorrow with it! Again with the chair! THOMAS: Come on, ref! MELINN: Anything goes outside the ring, you know that, Truth, and Sorrow knew that! Sanchez rolls back in the ring, followed by Sorrow. Sanchez stomping away on Sorrow, he picks him up and applies a headlock, Sorrow counters with a punch, breaking the h old. Sorrow with a knee-clip, Sanchez is knocked down. Sorrow stomping away on Sanchez now, Sorrow with a camel clutch! JOHNSON: He's got it applied properly there, sittin' back on the back, Sorrow a big man, he's causin' a big hurtin' on Miguel right now. MELINN: Sorrow releasing the hold, he goes for the pin, he's got the tights, 1... 2... shoulder up! Sorrow with a kick to the midsection, he throws Sanchez out of the ring. Sorrow playing to the crowd, they're booing him. Sanchez back in the ring, Sorr ow with a brainbuster! THOMAS: Look at the power behind that! MELINN: Sorrow whips Miguel into the ropes... and Miguel comes back with a sleeperhold, jumping on the shoulders of the huge Sorrow! Sorrow not going down, Sanchez releases the hold. Whip into the corner by Sanchez... Victory Roll! Wow! What agility o n the part of Sanchez, referee counting, 1... 2... shoulder up. Sanchez with a dropkick, Sorrow is down, Sanchez up top, Sorrow struggling to his feet, flying dropkick, he nails him! Cover, 1... 2... 3! (The crowd cheers.) TONY THE K: The winner of the match, in 9:19, Miguel Sanchez! MELINN: And as we watch the replay, Miguel with an awesome Victory Roll, that almost got him the win, and then that precision flying dropkick off the top rope, nails him right in the mush, cover and a win for Miguel Sanchez! JOHNSON: What a way to kick off September Slam! MELINN: Let's go back to Johnny Starr. JOHNNY STARR: All right, thanks, guys, we are ready to give you the complete rundown for this great event. Gods of Thunder (AC and DC) vs. Mercenaries (Korperal Punishment and Kaptain Krunch) STARR: Tag team powers collide as the rough and tumble Australian team Gods of Thunder take on the powerful Mercenaries. Bane vs. Flyboy STARR: Newcomer Bane going up against one of the premiere high flyers of the CWO, Flyboy. Flyboy has to look out for that Merciful Death sleeperhold of Bane's. David and Goliath (w/Jimbo Jones) vs. The Big Cool (Jumbo Jenkins/Jazzman Taylor, w/Russell Hill) STARR: When the Big Cool lost the #1 contenders match on this weekend's Showcase, they were then slated to take on Jimbo Jones' team of David and Goliath. You can be sure the Big Cool will be looking to take out their frustration on someone, and David an d Goliath are looking hungrily at the Big Cool as a stepping stone for greater things to come. Lord Foul the Despiser vs. "Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum (w/Sondra) STARR: Two of the CWO's finest collide in one of the more interesting matches on the card. Will Slocum's power-based attack overwhelm the former world champion, or will the savvy veteran be able to outsmart the Texan? We'll have to see. Ultimate Warriors (Cyrax and Killa) vs. Deadly Sins (Pride and Sloth, w/Lust) STARR: Deadly Sins looking to prove themselves to the hierarchy of the CWO, they'll need a win over the tough light heavyweight tag team of the Warriors. TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP: Sandman vs. Phantom Freightrain (w/Dapper Dan, champion) STARR: Phantom Freightrain having possibly his toughest title defense as his first title defense. Two behemoths of the ring colliding for the gold. NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Hoss and Little Joe (w/Dapper Dan) vs. Samoans (Smog and Grog, w/Holly, champions) STARR: More big men collide as the "Bonanza team" goes against the huge, and we do mean huge, Samoans. NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (two out of three falls): Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo vs. Gold Kard (w/German Choklat) STARR: They met twice in the recent CWO Tourney, Gold Kard scoring two tough victories. Now Gold Kard wants to put the college boy behind him, and "Keuka Guy" is looking to taste his first gold here in the CWO. WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Mason-Dixon (w/General Lee) vs. Creatures of the Night (Night Crawler and Night Slinger, champions) STARR: Mason-Dixon winning the recent #1 contenders match on SHOWCASE, and this match has intensified due to the war between Southern Pride and the Dark Side. An intense matchup made even hotter, this one could explode! WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: Marc "Compton" Daniels (w/the A/K) vs. Vincent "The Lip" Manocelli (w/Russell Hill, champion) STARR: "The Lip" making another tough title defense against the goofy but wily veteran Marc "Compton" Daniels. All of that action makes for an explosive card, we hope you can join us for September Slam, it's coming up in just a few short moments. For Ky le Melinn, David Thomas, and Randy Johnson, I'm Johnny Starr saying thanks for joining us on EYE ON THE STORM, and hopefully we'll see you in a few minutes! THE COUNTDOWN BEGINS...
