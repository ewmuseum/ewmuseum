CWO SUNDAY SHOWCASE 12/7/97 - HOUR #1 CWO ARENA, GRAND RAPIDS, MICHIGAN RATING: TV-14 (V, L) PRODUCER: Chris O'Brien ( [detail withheld] ) EXECUTIVE PRODUCER: Brent G. Alles ( [detail withheld] ) (Fade in on a spinning globe, as the letters CWO are slowly superimposed over) VOICE-OVER: Cyberspace Wrestling Organization...providing wrestling excellence the world over.... ("Song 2" by Blur plays as a montage of CWO superstars in action fill the screen...Sandman Jr. hits the Permanent Dream on Hellhound ..."Airborne" Mark Fulcrum with Air Fulcrum on Masashi Lanther... the Jade Dragons with a Dragon Strike on Jermain Justice...the Four Aces celebrate their return to action...Tommy Bomber screams at the camera while Jimmy Heat looks on in amusement..."The Ultimate Heel" Union Caine with the Cainedriver on the Amish Farmer onto the concrete...the Deadly Sins with the Repention on the CPE Enforcers...Wildfire holds aloft the CWO Television title...and a split screen shot of Mondo Gecko, Lord Hile Troy, and Johnny Reb, who holds aloft the CWO World title) COVENANT: THIS IS SUNDAY SHOWCASE! (The camera switches to a shot of the jam-packed CWO Arena in Grand Rapids, Michigan. Fireworks go off as the fans go absolutely nuts. Everywhere, signs are waved: REPENT, CARDS, FOR JUDGEMENT IS AT HAND!; MCTAVISH 3:16, I JUST FLUNG YOUR ASS!; WELCOME BACK ACES!; and GECKO/TROY/REB: LET'S SETTLE THIS!) O'BRIEN: Back, and in full color to boot! (We go to the announcer's booth, where Jerry Covenant and "The Original" Chris O'Brien sit. Jerry is, as always, in his trademark CWO sweater and turtleneck, while Chris has on a number #19 Steve Yzerman Team Canada jersey and black combat boots) O'BRIEN: It's SUNDAY SHOWCASE, boys and girls, so grab the popcorn, pop open the beer, and let's go to work! COVENANT: Setting a positive example for America's youth once again, I see. O'BRIEN: NO! I am shocked and appalled at that accusation, Jerry. COVENANT: Well, you said... O'BRIEN: Don't you read the memos? COVENANT: What? O'BRIEN: We're on TSN now in Canada! So, I'm corrupting the youth of TWO nations! And if we get that deal with the Sky network, we'll be in Europe! I'll be corrupting an entire CONTINENT, baby! COVENANT: Sigh. Along with the local degenerate Chris O'Brien, I'm Jerry Covenant, and we're only a week away from CWO CELEBRATION! Three years in the making, Chris! O'BRIEN: You know it, and we'll finally settle once and for all if Johnny Reb is a real champion, when he goes up against Lord Hile Troy and Mondo Gecko, chosen in a fan vote to take on the champion. COVENANT: The second triangle World title match this year in CWO. Chris, I'll be impressed if you can name the other one. O'BRIEN: Uh....OK, MAY-HEM, Bane/Goliath/Rich Clark! COVENANT: None who are still with us here in CWO, but I'm impressed, Chris! O'BRIEN: I know my history somewhat. Fans, in this first hour, you'll see Hile Troy and Mondo Gecko in action, along with tag team action! COVENANT: Speaking of tag teams, I have to ask...what did you think of Tom Krepps decision to give the North American Tag belts over to Mark Fulcrum and Chris Myers on FEVER? O'BRIEN: As a longtime Rider's fan, Jerry, I think what Tom did was only fair. We had that huge 32 team tournament in England at NOVEMBER PAIN, and giving the belts to the winners is the only way to go! Heck, he is even letting the Empire, who lost to the Riders in the finals, get first crack! You know, for all the bitching people do around here, Tom Krepps is doing what he thinks is fair, and I am not going to argue that point! COVENANT: You're sticking up for the establishment? O'BRIEN: Hey, they're paying all my bar tabs, aren't they? COVENANT: They what? How did you swing that? O'BRIEN: I have my own car, so the money they save on rental fees goes over to the tabs! COVENANT: I can't get that kind of offer... O'BRIEN: I got skills, Jerry! I'll negotiate your next contract, if you want. COVENANT: No thanks! Now, let's dispense with the chit-chat and go down to Chad Johnson for the intros in our opening match here on SUNDAY SHOWCASE! (We go to Chad Johnson, standing in the ring, tuxedo on and mike in hand) JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to the opening match on CWO SUNDAY SHOWCASE! (The crowd cheers) JOHNSON: The opening matchup is set for one fall, with a 20 minute time limit! ('Sweet Dreams' by Marilyn Manson plays as the fans boo, except for a couple hard-core fans who cheer) JOHNSON: ...from parts unknown, and representing the Deadly Sins, he is accompanied by his manager Lust, weighing 288 pounds, WRATH! COVENANT: Former CWO TV champion, and he will be taking on the Focus next Sunday in a submission match! What do you think, Chris? Chris? CHRIS! O'BRIEN: AH! What? COVENANT: You were looking at Lust again, weren't you? I swear, you are a sick puppy. O'BRIEN: And? Your point? JOHNSON: And his opponent.... ('Ride of the Valkyries' by Wagner plays as the fans boo) JOHNSON: ...from Blackpool, England, weighing 240 pounds, here is Lord Hile Troy! O'BRIEN: Icon....well, maybe. COVENANT: I am shocked at that! I thought you hated him! O'BRIEN: Hey, if the fans voted him in for CELEBRATION...I should give the guy a chance, right? COVENANT: A refreshing change from Michael Postema you are, Chris. O'BRIEN: HEY! Don't EVER mention me and that jerk in the same sentence! (The bell rings as Lord Hile Troy plays to the crowd) COVENANT: And Wrath from behind with a hard right hand! And now a vicious chop that sends Troy down! Cover! 1...2...thr...and Hile Troy narrowly avoids a major upset by getting his shoulder up! O'BRIEN: Troy needs to look out, Wrath feels he's been slighted by the championship committee, and would love to pull the win here. COVENANT: Wrath now tries to whip Hile Troy to the ropes....and a reverse neckbreaker catches Troy! O'BRIEN: Troy got rocked! Wrath pulls up Troy...and a kick to the stomach by Troy, and a small package! COVENANT: Wrath manages to get out before the one count, and now both men trade punches and chops! Wrath with a chop! (Crowd WOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOS!) O'BRIEN: Troy comes back with a right hand, and Wrath with one! And now Troy with a kick that drops Wrath! And now Troy with the crossface chickenwing! COVENANT: Troy can brawl, and he can outwrestle you, too. That's why he's been a champion before, back in the days of Lord Foul the Despiser. Wrath now trying to get to his feet... O'BRIEN: Troy hanging on....and Wrath is two his feet....jawbreaker! Wrath made Troy let go with that move! COVENANT: Wrath now wasting no time...he's going for the Repentance Rack! O'BRIEN: Troy is up! And Wrath's got him locked in! Wrath with that horizontal backbreaker, and Troy may be in trouble! COVENANT: The ref asking if Hile wants to submit...Troy won't! Wrath went for the hold too early in the match, and Troy isn't worn down enough yet! O'BRIEN: If Wrath had gotten a quick win, it sure would have opened some eyes around here! COVENANT: Wrath lets go, and now a back rake! Troy is in major trouble early...and Wrath with a forearm smash! Troy staggers back...and now Wrath goes for a DDT, but Troy somehow manages to get out of it! O'BRIEN: And now Troy with a small package! 1...2...and the ref sees that Troy has a handful of Wrath's trunks! COVENANT: Troy desperate, and now both men on their feet again...Wrath with an elbow smash, and now another back rake! O'BRIEN: Wrath going to work on that lower back, a smart move...oh, man, I sound like Gordy Alles! COVENANT: Gordy Alles is a respected man, Chris. O'BRIEN: I know, but I don't want to sound so...dry. COVENANT: I'll let him and his son know you said that... O'BRIEN: NO! MOMMY, I DON'T WANT TO BE ALLESBROKEN! COVENANT: Mommy? O'BRIEN: I have issues, what can I say? COVENANT: Back to the match, Wrath is going for a powerbomb....and he's got Troy up....but Troy fights for dear life, nailing Wrath in the head...and Wrath goes down! And Hile on top, grabbing a leg! 1...2...thr...and Wrath barely kicks out! O'BRIEN: Troy saving himself right there, smart move by the pro! TROY (to WRATH): COME ON, I THOUGHT YOU COULD WRESTLE! O'BRIEN: And Troy punctuates that with a low blow! And now a small package! 1...2...shoulder up! COVENANT: Not annoyed by all those repeated small package attempts, Chris? O'BRIEN: Not anymore, he isn't going for them in bunches of four or five in a row. COVENANT: And Troy now back to that crossface chickenwing, and Wrath isn't going to give up! Practice for the submission match next Sunday. O'BRIEN: Troy now lets go of that hold, and now he whips Wrath to the ropes....and Wrath comes off with a clothesline that catches Hile off guard! (The fans start to cheer as the Focus comes down the aisle) COVENANT: What's he doing here? O'BRIEN: Well, duh! He's scouting, Jerry! COVENANT: Wrath is going for the Repentance Rack...he sees the Focus! WRATH (to the Focus): WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU DOING HERE? O'BRIEN: The Focus just staring at Wrath as Lust comes over, yelling at the ref. LUST: Get him out of here, ref! REF: Nope, he's not interfering, Lust. LUST: Would it help if I said please? O'BRIEN: Oh, my. COVENANT: Wrath ignoring the situation, and going back to a fallen Troy, and now a Fujiwara armbar applied. O'BRIEN: The Focus just looking on, arms crossed, as Wrath locks in that armbar. Troy trying to counter, but Wrath is that much more intense, with his longtime rival at ringside. COVENANT: Wrath trying to hold on...but Troy slips a hand in...and he counters into a standing armbar! Wrath tries to get to the ropes...Troy releases, and now he turns it into a small package! 1...2...and Lust is on the apron! LUST: Get him backstage, r.... O'BRIEN: AND TROY WITH A DROPKICK, KNOCKING LUST TO THE FLOOR! That bastard! COVENANT: Troy taking matters into his own hands...and Wrath from behind! Troy staggers back...and a atomic drop by Wrath! O'BRIEN: Troy trying to hang on as Wrath knocks him down now...and a half Boston applied by Wrath. COVENANT: Wrath going for a lot of submission moves in this match up, but his eyes keep wandering over to the Focus...who hasn't moved this whole time. O'BRIEN: The man is cool, Jerry. Troy now reaches the ropes, and Wrath releases. Troy to his feet...and Wrath whips him to the ropes, and catches him with a powerslam! WRATH (to Troy): YOU AIN'T SO TOUGH, YOU FAIRY! O'BRIEN: Wrath now with a hair pull...and Troy turns that into an inside cradle! 1...and that's all he gets! COVENANT: Troy now follows up with a kick! And now a vertical suplex by Lord Hile Troy! Troy is on fire! Off the ropes...fistdrop! And Wrath rolls outside the ring. O'BRIEN: Wrath trying to catch his breath....THE FOCUS FROM BEHIND! He has Wrath...OH MY! The Focus just did a backbreaker, but he used the railing instead of his knee! COVENANT: Wrath now rolled back in, and Troy saw the whole thing! Cover by Lord Hile Troy! 1...2...3! JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of this match, by pinfall, in a time of 10:07, Lord Hile Troy! COVENANT: Oh my...Troy not one to look a gift horse in the mouth, and he took full advantage of the Focus' interference! O'BRIEN: Troy walking backstage, walking right past the Focus, who doesn't even look at him. His eyes are focused on the ring, where Wrath is being attended to by Lust. COVENANT: Fans, we're going to commercial, when we come back, tag team action, as Brother Justice takes on Mason-Dixon! (Fade to commercial. When we come back, Chad Johnson is standing in the ring) JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is set for one fall, with a 30 minute time limit! ('There Goes the Neighborhood) by Body Count plays as the fans boo) JOHNSON: At a combined weight of 635 pounds, here are Jermain Justice...Jerome Justice...they represent the Law, BROTHER JUSTICE! O'BRIEN: A good tag team running in neutral right now. They could use a win here to get back in the thick of the whole tag team mess. COVENANT: The same could be said about the next team too, Chris. JOHNSON: And their opponents... ('Country Boy Can Survive' by Hank Williams Jr. plays as the fans cheer) JOHNSON: At a combined weight of 629 pounds, accompanied by General Lee, here are Stonewall Mason and Beauregard Dixon...MASON-DIXON! O'BRIEN: Another team standing in place. Over the past two weeks, the tag team situation here in CWO got a whole lot messier. We've got new World champs in the Deadly Sins, new #1 contenders in the Wild Cards, new North American tag champs in Diamondback Chris Myers and "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum, new #1 contenders for those belts in Foolkiller and Union Caine, the Highlanders and the Jade Dragons want to kill each other, the Mercenaries and the Big Cool, pun intended, are at each other's throats, and the best tag team around, the Newton Brothers, just got suspended for 90 days! At this rate, a smart tag team could go all the way to the top! COVENANT: And great insight from my broadcast partner, as we start out with Jermain Justice and Beauregard Dixon. Both men lock up...and Dixon with a side headlock, but Justice to the ropes, bounces Dixon off... and a hard right hand by Jermain! O'BRIEN: Dixon not staggering, he comes back with a right of his own! And now he whips Jermain Justice to the ropes...heart punch! A heart punch, and Jermain Justice rolls out of the ring! COVENANT: And Beauregard Dixon rolls right outside after him! He whips Jermain to the ring apron! And now he slams his head off it...no! Jermain blocks, and instead drives Dixon's head to the canvas! O'BRIEN: Justice saved himself, and now he rolls Dixon back in the ring, and nails him with a double axehandle! COVENANT: And now a tag to Jerome Justice, and both men pound away on Beauregard Dixon, nailing him in the corner. And now Jerome puts Dixon on the ropes...Irish whip...Dixon ducks a clothesline, and comes back with a cross body block! O'BRIEN: Both teams showing offense early on, as now a tag to Stonewall Mason by Dixon. Mason comes in, and applies headscissors to Jerome Justice. COVENANT: Mason-Dixon former 6-man tag champs, along with Johnny Reb, and longtime CWO mainstays...Justice to the ropes, and Mason releases. Justice and Mason lock up...and Jerome forces Mason to the Brother Justice corner, and a tag to Jermain Justice. O'BRIEN: Brother Justice long time tag team partners, and yes, as the name implies, they are brothers, while Mason-Dixon are cousins. Brother Justice sends Stonewall Mason to the ropes...double clothesline! COVENANT: And now Jerome Justice tagged back in, quick tags between both members of Brother Justice early on. And a double powerslam on Stonewall Mason...and now here comes Beauregard Dixon! And we've got a brawl here, folks! O'BRIEN: Mason-Dixon and Brother Justice just tearing into one another...and Brother Justice with the upper hand, just beating down Mason-Dixon in the center of the ring. COVENANT: Jerome Justice and Stonewall Mason the legal men as the ref clears the ring...and Jerome off the ropes...legdrop! And now he measures up...elbowdrop! O'BRIEN: Jerome tags back in Jermain...there's a joke in there, but I can't figure it out...and Jermain Justice goes for a body slam, but it's blocked! He tries again...and Mason blocks again! And now Mason...vertical suplex! COVENANT: Mason with great ring presence, catching Jermain Justice off guard. And now a tag to Dixon by Mason, and Mason-Dixon sends Jermain to the ropes...double clothesline! Mason leaves the ring as his cousin stomps a mudhole in Jermain. O'BRIEN: Dixon now picks up Jermain...and Jermain with a punch! And now an inverted atomic drop! Dixon is down, and Jermain covers! 1...2...and a kickout by Dixon, and Jermain seizes the opportunity and makes a tag. COVENANT: Jerome comes in, and he catches Dixon off guard! HUGE clothesline, and Beauregard Dixon is on the floor! O'BRIEN: Jerome Justice now...baseball slide! And he wastes no time hitting the floor...and he slams Dixon into the ring post! COVENANT: Jerome now rolls him back in the ring...and a tag to Jermain Justice now. Brother Justice showing some improvement so far in this matchup. O'BRIEN: Jermain lines up...and Dixon dodges a kick! Dixon now...belly-to-back suplex! Jermain caught off guard, and now he rolls out of the ring! COVENANT: And it's Dixon turn to go after him! Dixon and Jermain brawling outside the ring! O'BRIEN: General Lee egging his man on....OH NO! Jerome Justice from behind with a steel chair on Dixon! COVENANT: Mason coming over to help...and Jerome clocks him too! O'BRIEN: Brother Justice now rolls Dixon back in the ring... and they're calling for the Final Justice! COVENANT: Jermain has Dixon up top...and Jerome is on the opposite turnbuckle...POWERBOMB BY JERMAIN, AND A FLYING SOMERSAULT SPLASH BY JEROME! They hit the Final Justice, and Jermain covers! 1...2...3! JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the winners of this contest, in a time of 8:39, by pinfall, BROTHER JUSTICE! O'BRIEN: Brother Justice with a win here today, and it's back to the proverbial drawing board for Mason-Dixon. COVENANT: Fans, when we come back, Mondo Gecko takes on Masher Maccabee! It'll be a classic, don't miss it! (Fade to commercial. When we come back, we are at the announcer's booth with Jerry and Chris) O'BRIEN: Welcome back to SHOWCASE! Our next matchup is sure to be a doozy, as one of the participants in the three-way dance at CWO CELEBRATION, Mondo Gecko, goes up against former North American champion Masher Maccabee! COVENANT: Maccabee going up against new North American champ Fergus McTavish at CELEBRATION, in a scaffold match, and he's looking to let off a LOT of steam here today! (We go to Chad Johnson, standing in the ring, mike in hand) JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the next matchup is set for one fall, with a 20 minute time limit! ('Hava Negila' set to a rap beat plays as the fans boo) JOHNSON: Introducing first, weight 265 pounds, from Crown Heights, New York, here is MASHER MACCABEE! O'BRIEN: Masher going right to the ring, no nonsense, he knows the wild man McTavish is looking on. He needs to set a good tone going into the 14th. JOHNSON: And his opponent... ('Hip Hop Hooray' by Naughty by Nature plays as the fans cheer) JOHNSON: ...from Oakland, California! Weighing 222 pounds, accompanied by Tamara Wilcox, MONDO GECKO! COVENANT: Gecko a long time superstar here in CWO, and he may finally get his chance to break through to the upper tier of this sport on December 14h! O'BRIEN: Trained by Randy Savage himself, Jerry, as the bell sounds! Both men lock up....and Masher with a double axehandle! Now Masher whips Mondo to the ropes...Mondo ducks another axehandle! And now off the ropes comes Gecko...and Masher moves away from a cross body block! COVENANT: Maccabee wasting no time, as he pulls Gecko up...elbow smash, right to the forehead! Mondo comes back with a vicious chop! (Crowd WOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOS!) O'BRIEN: And now Mondo Gecko...standing dropkick sends Masher down! Mondo now...he's going up top, high risk....and a missile dropkick, right to the kisser of Masher Maccabee! COVENANT: Mondo setting a tone here as now a side headlock by the high-flyer. O'BRIEN: Gecko applying pressure as Tamara Wilcox gets the crowd going on the outside of the ring. COVENANT: No comments about Miss Wilcox? O'BRIEN: Hey, that lady could kick my butt! COVENANT: Maccabee manages to reach the ropes now, and Mondo releases the headlock, only to be thanked by a Masher axehandle! O'BRIEN: And Masher from behind...German suplex! Bridge! 1...2...and Mondo kicks out! COVENANT: Masher now...he's going up top, he likes it up there...and Mondo rolls away from a flying elbow drop! And Mondo now with a cradle! 1...2...shoulder up! O'BRIEN: Both men putting on a botched aerial display so far, as Mondo now with a headlock, standing side. Masher pushes him to the ropes, whips him off...and a dropkick by Masher Maccabee! COVENANT: Masher now with an elbow smash, and now proceeding to stomp away on Mondo Gecko...and he's calling for the Hangover! O'BRIEN: Masher up top, if he hits the Homentaschen Hangover, it's all over....AND MONDO ROLLS AWAY! Mondo Gecko rolls away from that somersault legdrop! COVENANT: He got awfully lucky, Chris, and now Mondo's turn to go up top...SWAN DIVE! Beautiful swan dive, and a cover! 1...2...and Maccabee kicks out! O'BRIEN: Shades of Chris Benoit as Mondo now with an Irish whip...Masher reverses, and a HUGE lariat sends Gecko crashing to the mat! MASHER (to Mondo): COME ON AND WRESTLE ME, PUNK! O'BRIEN: And Masher with a double axehandle! Now Masher up top once again...Mondo gets up, and he catches him! PRESS SLAM! Masher goes flying across the ring, and Mondo up top! MISSILE DROPKICK! He caught Masher, and he's not done yet! Across the ring...FLYING LEGDROP! COVENANT: Masher is done, and he's holding his head, and his back, as Mondo up top again...another swan dive! O'BRIEN: Cover him! Mondo Gecko has this won, but he's not covering, instead stomping on Masher Maccabee! COVENANT: Youthful exuberance, I guess, but Mondo calling for the Gecko Spike! O'BRIEN: Masher is put up top, he doesn't know where he is right now! Mondo up top too...FRANKENSTEINER! Top rope Frankensteiner! 1...2...thr...AND MASHER KICKS OUT! COVENANT: INCREDIBLE! Masher kicked out of the Gecko Spike! O'BRIEN: Mondo now going right back to work, a double axehandle sends Masher down...now Mondo with a kick to the back of Masher, and a press cover! 1...2...and Masher manages to kick out again! COVENANT: That's pure instinct Masher is going on right now as Mondo with a hammerlock now. Masher trying to get to the ropes, but Mondo not letting up! O'BRIEN: Masher to the ropes, and Mondo now holds the lock for the five count before letting go. COVENANT: And now Mondo....he goes for a gator breaker....but Masher steps away! Mondo not daunted, he comes back in...AND MASHER NAILS HIM! Masher catches him off guard... O'BRIEN: Wait! Masher has something in his hand! Masher with a handful of tights as the ref counts 1...2...3! JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of this match, by pinfall, in a time of 9:32, MASHER MACCABEE! O'BRIEN: We see on the replay, Mondo misses the gator breaker...and right here! Masher reaches down his tights, and he nails Mondo Gecko right in the head with it. COVENANT: Whatever it was, Masher pulls out a win, here on the final match of hour #1 of SUNDAY SHOWCASE! For Chris O'Brien, I'm Jerry Covenant, we'll see you next week, stay tuned for hour #2!
