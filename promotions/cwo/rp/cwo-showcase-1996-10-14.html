CWO SHOWCASE - 10/13/96

14 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The opening music begins to play as scenes from previous matches involving Tiger Rodgers, Dark Raven, Sandman Junior, Steve Keller, the Creatures of the Night, the Deadly Sins, Brent Alles, and Lord Foul travel across the screen. the camera pans to Kevin Rinaldi and the Fabulous Feenix) Kevin (KR): Hello everyone and welcome to CWO Showcase!!!!!!! I'm Kevin Rinaldi, and with me is... Feenix (FF): Don't strain yourself, Kevin, you couldn't do me justice!! With him, ladies and gentlemen, is the best, the brightest, the baddest man on the planet, the one, the only, the FABULOUS FEENIX!!!!!! KR: The "baddest man on the planet," huh? FF: That's right, Kevin. You step in the ring with me, you better have your insurance paid up! KR: Really. And the last time you stepped in the ring was when? FF: Er....ah....that's not why we're here, Kevin! Shame on you! We still haven't announced tonight's card! KR: Right. Well, in our opening match, Dark Raven takes on Tiger Rodgers! FF: Sandman Junior locks up with Steve Keller! KR: The Deadly Sins butt heads with the Creatures of the Night! FF: Brent Alles does his business with Lord Foul the Despiser! KR: "Does his business????????" FF: Shut up! KR: And in our main event, Gold Kard International battle Doomsday!!! And we'll be back after this! **COMMERCIAL BREAK** Kevin: And let's go to the ring for our first match!!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall with a twenty minute time limit. ("Unforgiven" by Metallica begins to play as the fans boo) Coming down the aisle, weighing in an 240 pounds, from Warner Robbins, Georgia and accompanied by Jimbo Jones, Tiger Rodgers! (the theme from "Halloween" plays as the fans keep on booing) And his opponent, weighing 231 pounds, from London, England, Dark Raven!!!!! KR: And both men's mouth's going a mile a minute!!! And we can't broadcast what they're saying over the air! FF: If my daddy had heard ME talking like that, I'd have gotten a mouthful of soap!!!!! KR: And Rodgers charges the Raven!!!! Backdrop by the Raven!!!! He sends Rodgers into the ropes....FRANKENSTEINER!!!! Cover! 1....2.....thr...no! Rodgers kicks out! FF: These guys are pulling out all the cylinders! One of them might not walk out of here! KR: Raven misses with a flying dropkick...Rodgers on the attack! Brings Raven to his feet...kneelift!!!! Rodgers into the ropes.....flying clothes- line!!! Raven is down!! Rodgers goes for the pin!!! 1....2....Raven kicks out! FF: Great action here, the kind of excellence you'd expect from the CWO!! Almost as good as me!!!!!! KR: What-EVER! Rodgers sends Raven into the ropes...big boot to the face! And Raven goes through the ropes....Rodgers is going after him! Both men brawling outside the ring! Rodgers sends Raven's head into the steps...NO! Raven reversed it!! Both men brawl their way back into the ring! Dark Raven with a huge savate kick!!! Rodgers is down! Raven with the cover! 1.....kick out by Rodgers! FF: I said it before and I'll say it again, these guys are going to kill each other! KR: Both men trading chops and axehandles...standing dropkick by Raven!!! He's going for Death From Above!!!!!! Wait!!!! Jimbo Jones is distracting him! And Rodgers is back on the attack! Armbar! Double axehandle! Rodgers going for the pin! 1...2...kick out by the Raven! FF: That Jimbo Jones is looking out for his man, just the way a manager should. If Dark Raven doesn't know enough to have a manager, that's his problem! KR: Like you would know....Rodgers with a series of chops and kicks...sends Raven into the ropes....RAVEN with a dropkick!!!!! Another one!!!! Raven going for Death from Above once again! NO!! Jimbo Jones trips him up! The referee is calling for the bell! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the referee has awarded this match, as a result of a disqualification for outside interference, to Dark Raven!!!!! KR: And the referee is placing himself between Rodgers and the injured Raven....Rodgers is livid, but he's leaving the ring area. FF: He's not stupid enough to risk a fine for attacking the ref after the match! KR: Security helping Dark Raven back to the dressing room, and we'll go to a commercial! **COMMERICAL BREAK** KR: And we're back with our next contest! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall with a twenty minute time limit. ("Enter Sandman" by Mighty Mighty Bosstones plays as the fans boo) Coming down the aisle, weighing 235 pounds, from Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, accompanied by Kellen, here is Sandman, Junior!!! ("Walking in LA" by Missing Persons plays as the fans cheer) And his opponent, weighing 240 pounds, one half of the team of Hollywood Muscle, from Hollywood, California, Steve Keller!!!!!! KR: Sandman Junior waiting patiently for the bell...and he explodes once it rings!!!! Chop across Keller's chest! Elbow smash!!!! Sends Keller into the ropes...FRANKENSTEINER!!! Cover! 1...2....3!!!!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the match....Sandman, Junior!!!!!! FF: What a ripoff! Keller doesn't deserve to be in this league!!!! KR: Keller unable to mount any kind of offense in this matchup, and we'll go to another commerical. **COMMERICAL BREAK** KR: And we're back!!! Here's a flash from our next match! (fade in on the Creatures of the Night) Night Slinger: Deadly Sins...we have taken this match to prove to everyone that the two of you got lucky when you put that blemish on our record with the loss you dealt us. We know it, and you know it. We just want to prove it to the Master. When we lost, we embarrassed him and the DARK SIDE but tonight, we will correct that wrong! Night Crawler: Deadly Sins, you will pay tonight with your bodies. We are going to drag your sorry carcasses all around the ring. We will not settle for a victory; we are out to hurt you! Night Slinger: That's right! Like he said, you will pay with your bodies. After tonight, you will need a long time off to recover and lick your wounds! As creatures of the night, we do know how to fight and we do knwo how to cause some serious damage! Both: LONG LIVE THE NIGHT!!!!!! AND LONG LIVE THE DARK SIDE!!!!! (back to Kevin and Feenix) FF: These two need some serious psychiatric help!!! KR: That's as may be, but if they have their way, it'll be the Deadly Sins who need the help! Let's go to the ring!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the following tag team match is scheduled for one fall with a thirty minute time limit. (eerie sounds of the night play as the fans boo) Coming down the aisle, at a combined weight of 549 pounds, Night Slinger and Night Crawler, the Creatures of the Night!!!! ("Sweet Dreams" by Marilyn Manson plays as the fans keep booing) And their opponents, at a combined weight of 761 pounds and accompanied by Lust, Pride and Sloth, the Deadly Sins! KR: And the Creatures not even letting the Sins get into the ring!!! Night Slinger sends Sloth and Lust out of the ring! Night Crawler with a DDT on Pride! Elbow drop!!! Night Crawler sends Pride into the buckle headfirst!!! Kneelift by Night Crawler, and Pride is knocked out of the ring!!! Night Crawler going after him!!!! Wild brawl outside!!!! FF: Watch out for Lust!!! KR: Lust just nailed Night Crawler with a chair across the back!!! The ref didn't see it!!! Pride slams Night Crawler into the ring steps! Both men slowly climbing back into the ring...Night Crawler catches Pride with an elbow! Cover!!!! 1....2.....thr...NO! Pride manages to kick out! FF: The Creatures have something to prove, and the Sins can't get started! Wonder what Lust is doing after the match!!! KR: Something better than being with you, that's for sure!!! Night Crawler tags Night Slinger....Pride clotheslines Night Slinger!!! He drags Night Slinger over to the corner!!!! Pride going for a superplex!!!! He tags Sloth!!! Pride hits the superplex, then scurries out of the way...MOONSAULT by Sloth! Cover!!!! 1....2.....3!!!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, your winners, the Deadly Sins!!!! KR: And the Creatures of the Night are not taking defeat gracefully!! They're still attacking the Sins!!!! Crawler has a chair!!! But the Sins are responding in kind!!!! We've got to get Security in here!!! And we'll go to a commercial, but I have a feeling that this feud has NOT been settled!!!!! **COMMERCIAL BREAK** KR: And we're back!!! Security was finally able to separate the two teams, but things have not settled down in either camp! We hope to get their reactions at a later date! But let's go to our next match! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall with a twenty minute time limit. ("Who Made Who" by AC/DC begins to play as the fans boo) Coming down the aisle, weighing in at 280 pounds and accompanied by Gold Kard, from Grandville, Michigan, Brent "The Ace" Alles!!!!!! ("Thus Spake Zarathustra" by Strauss begins to play as the fans cheer) And his opponent, weighing 240 pounds, from Blackpool, England, Lord Foul the Despiser!!!!! KR: Referee giving both men their instructions, but neither is listening... they're just staring each other down. Who among us could stand before those gazes? FF: I could and I did! During my long, illustrious career.... KR: Wait a minute! Have you ever actually wrestled a match???? FF: Well, er.... KR: You haven't!!!!! So shut your mouth!!!!! You just made us miss the start of the match! Alles is in the corner....Foul charging in....misses with the clothesline! Foul out of the ring!!!! He's climbing back up.... the Ace moving in....Foul with a shoulder to the gut! Foul has Alles up... GOOD LORD!!!!! HE JUST SUPLEXED ALLES OFF THE RING APRON ONTO THE CONCRETE FLOOR!!!! FF: I don't believe it!!!!!! KR: Gold Kard moving in....he just kicked Lord Foul!!!! Alles back in the ring, Foul going after him....headlock by Lord Foul...Alles grabs the ropes. Foul reapplies the hold....this time Alles powers out, but Foul catches him with a small package! 1.....2...shoulder up by Alles! FF: Alles was able to escape the headlock, but he left himself wide open, and Foul capitalized on it! KR: Alles catches Foul with a fist to the gut as both men climb to their feet....standing dropkick by Alles!!! Alles going for a reverse chinlock, but Foul keeping him from locking his hands! And Foul goes south!!! Alles clutching himself in pain! Foul with the cover!!!! 1....Alles gets the shoulder up! FF: For a supposed fan favorite, Foul uses a lot of foul tactics!!!! KR: You were up all night coming up with that one, weren't you.....Foul has Alles up.....spinebuster!!! Up again....DDT! Foul going for the cover! 1....2...thr...shoulder up!!!!! FF: What is wrong with Brent Alles? KR: Good question!!! Alles with an armbar....Foul breaks the hold! Alles sends Foul into the ropes....both men collide and go down!!!! Foul is up first! He goes for the pin, but Gold Kard distracts the referee!!! Foul continuing to go to town on Alles!!! Going for the cover again....1.... 2....3!!!!!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the match...Lord Foul!!!!!!! KR: A very unusual outing for Brent Alles! FF: The suplex off the top rope took most of the fight out of him!!!! KR: And we're going to a commercial!!! **COMMERCIAL BREAK** KR: And we're back with our final match of the evening!!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the following special tag team contest is scheduled for one fall with a 45 minute time limit. ("Ride of the Valkyries" by Wagner plays as the fans boo) Coming down the aisle, at a combined weight of 770 pounds, accompanied by German Choklat, here are Gold Kard and the Pit Bosses...together, they are Gold Kard International!!!!!!! ("Back in Black" by AC/DC plays as the fans cheer) And their opponents, at a combined weight of 834 pounds, here are Maximum Overdrive, Korporal Punishment, and Kaptain Krunch, the team of Doomsday!!!!!!! KR: And GKI refuses to enter the ring! FF: They have to make sure that Gold Kard is ready! KR: The Pit Bosses are on the ring apron, and they're telling the referee to move Doomsday back! The faces oblige, and the Pit Bosses spread the ropes to allow Gold Kard to enter! FF: And Doomsday wants to start the match already!!!!! KR: Bell ringing.....Pit Boss #2 and Kaptain Krunch starting things off. Clothesline by Pit Boss #2!!!! Krunch is down! Boss #2 brings him up to his feet...but Krunch trips him up and applies the figure four before the Boss can counter! Krunch applying the pressure....Boss #2 in pain.... Krunch releases the hold! FF: Well, what did you expect? He's not going to get a submission from the Pit Boss this early! Think about it! KR: Krunch going for a flying shoulderblock....missed!!! Pit Boss #2 has him up....sends him into the ropes....clothesline!!!! Pit Boss #2 has him up again...and tags in Gold Kard!!!!! The two men pound on Kaptain Krunch, knocking him down! Elbow drop by Gold Kard! Flying knee drop! Gold Kard has him up....snap suplex!!!!!! Gold Kard going for a Boston crab.....NO! Kaptain Krunch straightens his legs and sends Gold Kard flying!!!! Krunch on his feet.....flying double axe handle!!! Krunch has Gold Kard up.....CHOKESLAM!!!!!!!!!! And he tags Korporal Punishment!!! they're going for the Mercenaries' Flying Clothesline! Krunch has Gold Kard up...Punishment going to the top rope...NO! German Choklat distracted Krunch, allowing Gold Kard to escape!!!! Punishment hits the mat, hard! The referee forcing Kaptain Krunch to leave the ring! FF: He tagged out, he has to go!!! KR: Gold Kard with a dropkick on Korporal Punishment! Has him up...German suplex!!!! Going for the Die Flieder Kross...HE GOT IT!!!!! Cover! 1... 2.....thr...NO! Punishment kicked out! FF: Punishment has got to get out of there!!!!! KR: Gold Kard going for the headlock.....Punishment throws the elbow into Gold Kard's gut!!! And Punishment dives for the corner!!! He tags Maximum Overdrive!!! Double axehandle by Overdrive!!!!! He slaps on the STF!!!! Gold Kard writhing in pain!!!!! He's reaching for the ropes...made it!!! Referee bringing both men up.....Overdrive with a running clothesline on Gold Kard!!!! Kard is down!!!! Overdrive going for the cover!!!! 1....2... Gold Kard gets the foot on the ropes! FF: Maximum Overdrive is almost as fabulous as I am!!!!!! KR: Yeah, sure......DDT by Maximum Overdrive...he sends Gold Kard into the corner....charges in with the elbow....Gold Kard dodges!!!!! And Gold Kard give Overdrive a thumb to the eye!!!! That should be an automatic disqual- ification!!!!! Chop by Gold Kard! Flying elbow drop!!! Here's the cover! 1....2.....thr....Maximum Overdrive kicks out!!! FF: Maximum Overdrive got lucky there!!!! KR: That he did...Gold Kard going for a flying head butt.....Overdrive moves out of the way!!!! He tags Kaptain Krunch!!! They have Gold Kard up... double body slam!!!! Krunch stomping away!!!! He tags in Overdrive!!! Double Russian Leg Sweep!!!!! Overdrive with the cover!!! 1.....2...kick out by Gold Kard! FF: Time to tag, Gold Kard!! KR: I don't think he can hear you, not with his head wrapped in Overdrive's arms! Kard pushes Overdrive into the ropes.....flying shoulderblock by Maximum Overdrive! He tags in Kaptain Krunch! Double back suplex!!!! German Choklat is on the ring apron! Kaptain Krunch going to argue with her! Gold Kard nails him in the back with a drop kick!!!! Krunch is down! Gold Kard going for Die Flieder Kross! He hita it!! Cover....1...Krunch kicks out! FF: Gold Kard getting his revenge!!! KR: Clotheline by Gold Kard!!!! Elbow drop!!!! Another DFK! Cover....and Gold Kard's got the tights!!!! FF: Say no to crack, Kaptain Krunch!!!!! KR: 1...2....thr...NO!!!! Krunch kicks out!!!! Gold Kard going for another splash....no, he hesitated.....Krunch nails him with a boot! Krunch with a flying double axe handle! He's stomping away!!!! Krunch tags in Korporal Punishment! Punishment rakes Gold Kard's eyes! Forearm smash! Kard is down! And Punishment introducing his boot the the side of Kard's head! Punishment has him up.....going for a bodyslam....no! Kard dropped behind him and hit with a spinning neckbreaker!!!!! Kard with the cover!!! 1....Punishment kicks out!!!! FF: Gold Kard might even be MORE fabulous than I am!!!!! KR: No comment....Punishment catches Kard with a fist to the jaw....Kard is down....Punishment with the STF!!!! But Gold Kard escapes the hold!!!! Punishment sends Kard out of the ring...going after him...Punishment slams Kard into the floor!!!! Both men back in the ring.....Punishment with a standing wrist lock......moving into a crossface chickenwing....applying the pressure to Gold Kard's shoulder....Punishment tags Kaptain Krunch! Double back suplex by the Mercenaries! Krunch with a forearm smash! Tags Korporal Punishment...double body slam!!!!! Korporal Punishment has Gold Kard up...small package...no! Gold Kard reverses it!!! 1... Punishment kicks out!!! FF: Gold Kard's gotta get out of there! KR: Kard sending him into the corner!!! Monkey flip! Kard kicking the prone Korporal! Flying knee drop by Gold Kard! Gold Kard with Die Flieder Kross! Cover--Gold Kard's got his foot on the ropes!!! 1...2... Punishment gets the shoulder up! FF: Gold Kard's got his second wind! KR: Flying head scissors by Gold Kard!!!!! Flying knee drop! Elbow drop! Flying cross body press! Cover! 1...2.....thr.....NO!!!!! Punishment kicks out!!!!! FF: Go, Gold Kard!!!!! KR: Flying dropkick by Gold Kard!!!! UP to the top.....DFK!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Cover....Gold Kard's got the tights! 1...2...thr...Punishment still alive! FF: Not for long, though! KR: Gold Kard going for the dropkick... Punishment moves!!!! Punishment misses with a flying double axe handle!!! Kard tags Pit Boss #2!!!! Pit Boss #2 choking Punishment on the ropes!!! Pit Boss #2 into the ropes! He leaps...Punishment moves out of the way! FF (in falsetto): Hello, my name is Pit Boss #2!!!!! KR: Punishment tags Maximum Overdrive!!!! Double Russian Leg Sweep! Maximum Overdrive has Pit Boss #2 on his feet......and walks right into an elbow!!!!! Pit Boss #2 tags Gold Kard! Kard with a splash!!!! Going for the pin...he's got the tights again! Punishment trying to make a save... nailed by Pit Boss #1, who hasn't even been in the match at all! 1....2.... 3!!!!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, your winners, Gold Kard International!!!!! KR: And that's all the time we have! For the Fabulous Feenix, I'm Kevin Rinaldi! Good night!
