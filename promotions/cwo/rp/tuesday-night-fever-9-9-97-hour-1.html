CWO TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER 9/9/97 "LIVE" FROM RICHMOND COLISEUM, RICHMOND, VA Producer: [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] ) Executive Producer: Brent G. Alles ( [detail withheld] ) (A spinning globe is shown, as the CWO logo is slowly superimposed over it.) VOICE-OVER: The CWO... providing wrestling excellence the world over.... (Montage of CWO action is shown as Aerosmith's "Fever" plays in the background. The CWO logo explodes on to the screen, and then we go to a roaring crowd in Richmond, VA, as pyrotechnics go off to signal the start of hour #1 of Tuesday Night Fever.) JERRY COVENANT: You're here, live from Richmond, Virginia, at the Richmond Coliseum for this week's Tuesday Night Fever! MICHAEL POSTEMA: Yeah, sure. Just give me my check and then I'll be happy. JERRY COVENANT: Such a pleasant attitude. Well last week we heard some odd comments from Wayne Schillo as it related to Joe Eagle and the rest of his, perhaps former, friends. Schillo obviously upset at the fact no one believed him as it stood with GKI. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Just what we need, another Brent Alles running around with the isolationist attitude, being an island unto himself. JERRY COVENANT: Speaking of him, we also saw the recovering Alles out last week to confront the man he called his teacher, Hile Troy. Alles insisted that he wanted a 'scientific match' with Lord Hile Troy at the upcoming September Slam. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Well, it _could_ be nice to actually see some _wrestling_ for once instead of chairs and glamour and glints. Show the young punks and the guys coming from other leagues just how we do things here. (Arena lights dim as "Firestarter" by Prodigy begins to play.) JERRY COVENANT: Well, it appears as though our first match is starting up. But, what _is_ this drivel pouring out of the speakers. MICHAEL POSTEMA: This is techno Covenant, get with it, we don't deal with guitar anymore! TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, making his way to the ring, weighing in at 231 pounds, from London, England, here is the Red Raven! JERRY COVENANT: Fans booing the man who has been pearl harboring Gold Kard left and right in his matches these days. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Which makes absolutely no sense! JERRY COVENANT: And neither does Red Raven really. But he's going to have his hands full to say the least. However, we managed to catch up with Holocaust and Red Raven earlier, here are comments from them. (Holocaust and Red Raven are in the interview area.) HOLOCAUST: We're back from the depths of hell. We're looking to burn our names into the history of this league and we plan on starting with you, Gold Kard. You sold out your people and your cause for fame and fortune. Well, now I plan on making you pa y dearly for all you have done. As for the Red Raven, his vow of silence has been broken. (Raven, standing behind Holocaust, pushes him to the side.) RED RAVEN: UH HUH! (Camera fades to black. Back to the ring.) JERRY COVENANT: Well, Holocaust certainly making his threat known to Gold Kard, although we still don't know why Holocaust is after him! MICHAEL POSTEMA: What was that stuff about selling out your people, anyway? And who does Red Raven think he is, Puff Daddy? ("Shoots and Ladders" by Korn begins to play.) TONY THE K: And his opponent, weighing in at 275 pounds, from Phoenix, Arizona, accompanied by Dallas, here is Diamondback Chris Myers! JERRY COVENANT: And a _deafening_ roar from the crowd as Myers and the lovely Dallas make their way down to the ring. Myers of course has a big tag match coming up against Sandman and the Reverend at September Slam. MICHAEL POSTEMA: I know Myers is brave, and he's got the action to back up the bravery, but I just don't know how wise it was to take a match like that. JERRY COVENANT: The bell has rung and we're underway. Red Raven trying to quickly circle around Chris Myers, who backs up into the corner to keep Raven in front of him. Both men lock up, Raven with an amateur takeover. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Good basic wrestling, but Raven _has_ to watch out for the Rattler. Sandman may have taken near three of them, but Red Raven is NOT Sandman. JERRY COVENANT: Myers back up on his feet and he catches Raven with a kneelift, he fires Raven into the ropes, Raven reverses it and catches Myers with a spinning leg lariat. Raven with a legdrop, goes for the pin! One... two... kickout! MICHAEL POSTEMA: Red Raven is just so quick, and that will give Myers a bit of a problem. Myers can brawl, and he can wrestle, but he can't fly. JERRY COVENANT: Raven bounding off of the ropes, clothesline, Myers ducks it, Raven back off the other end. Myers catches him in a tilt-a-whirl slam and plants him into the mat. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Now that cut down all of that speed. Someone better get Raven a sick bag after that move! JERRY COVENANT: Myers goes for a German suplex, Raven reverses it into a suplex of his own and just _folds_ Myers like an accordion into the mat. Raven up to the top rope, he comes off with a flying bulldog headlock! MICHAEL POSTEMA: This is kind of surprising to me, Red Raven is just dominating the first minutes of this match. JERRY COVENANT: Raven twisting Myers up for a figure four, Myers kicks him off and Raven bounds back into the corner. Raven charges out with a dropkick on the already fallen Myers! MICHAEL POSTEMA: Ouch... now that _has_ to hurt. JERRY COVENANT: Raven going for the pin, but Myers kicks out before a one count. Raven with an Irish whip shoots Myers into the corner, follows him in, flying splash. Myers crumbles out of the corner, and Raven just stalking over him. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Picking his shots, but he's taking too long, he needs to be going for the pin. JERRY COVENANT: Raven measuring Myers up for something, Dallas is up on the apron! She pulled do... she pulled down! MICHAEL POSTEMA: What! She can't... or... she can... she did! JERRY COVENANT: Myers up from behind, Rattler, he hit the Rattler! One... two... three! MICHAEL POSTEMA: Replay! I want a replay! JERRY COVENANT: Not on your life! TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the match, in a time of 5:02, "Diamondback" Chris Myers! JERRY COVENANT: And "Diamondback" Chris Myers with a big win over Red Raven, he will go on with Sandman Jr. to go against the mysterious Reverend and his dark minion Sandman in that Texas Twister match, we'll be talking about that in the future as well. We'll be right back after this commercial break, ladies and gentlemen. (Camera breaks away to a commercial.) Political scientists and White House Staff are baffled as to the cause... however, the TV Guide says we'll see the same happen again _this_ Friday. VOICE-OVER: CWO Friday Night Fightin'... don't blame it on us, we just broadcast it. (Camera back to Covenant and Postema.) JERRY COVENANT: As the weeks go by, Friday Night Fighting _is_ gaining in popularity, attendance, and quality. Just one more ingredient to making the CWO the best there is. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Let's just get to this next match. I'm just waiting for the Mercenaries to come out here, then all hell can break loose! ("Iron Man" by Black Sabbath crunches over speakers.) TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following tag team matchup is scheduled for one fall, with a forty-five minute time limit. Making their way to the ring, at a combined weight of 555 pounds, here are Kaptain Krunch, and Korperal Punishment, the Mercenaries! JERRY COVENANT: The fans booing two of the men who we saw battle to a double DQ last week against Bloodbath and Wildfire. They've been on a tear as of late, trying to rip their way back to the belts. MICHAEL POSTEMA: And as far as I'm concerned, they'll do it. No team can match their work in that ring. TONY THE K: And their opponents, at a combined weight of 519 pounds, accompanied by Herbie Versmells, here are the CWO World Tag Team Champions, Joe "The Mean Guy Rucker", Ron "The Rude Guy" Johnson, here are The Bad Guys! JERRY COVENANT: The top of the crop in the CWO. We're going to see _all_ stops pulled in this match. Neither of these teams care what the fans think, neither of these teams care what they have to do to win a match. All they do is fight, and win. MICHAEL POSTEMA: And they are probably the two best teams we've seen in the CWO, in a very long time. JERRY COVENANT: Korperal Punishment starting off against Ron Johnson. Punishment with a kick, the follows it with a double underhook suplex! Action right off from the start of this one. Punishment whips Johnson into the ropes, back body suplex! MICHAEL POSTEMA: I'll give you good odds right now if you want to take the champs. Fifty bucks, what do you say? JERRY COVENANT: I say Punishment is going for the pin, and he's got the tights! The referee sees it and makes him break the pin. Both men back to their feet, Punishment with a standing wrist lock, he makes the tag to Kaptain Krunch. Both Mercenaries in the ring, double body slam. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Maybe I should place a call into Vegas right now. JERRY COVENANT: Kaptain Krunch with an STF now down on the mat, wrenching around on Johnson. The Rude Guy squirming around, he makes it to the ropes and the hold is broken. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Just as well, you aren't going to get a submission out of either one of those guys, especially not this early in a match. JERRY COVENANT: Krunch with an elbow smash, Johnson rolls to the outside of the ring and Krunch follows him. Krunch grabs Johnson by the arm and fires one half of the tag champs into the guard rail. MICHAEL POSTEMA: We're moving furniture, he's got the chair now! JERRY COVENANT: Johnson rolling back into the ring before Kaptain Krunch can use the chair. Johnson catches Rude on his way back in, suplexes him over the rope. Rude off the ropes, he catches a recovering Johnson with a flying clothesline!! Johnson going for the pin, one... two... Krunch gets his foot on the ropes. MICHAEL POSTEMA: More important than the pin attempt is the fact that Johnson now has the advantage. JERRY COVENANT: Johnson makes the tag to Rucker. Rucker in the ring with a rollup on Krunch, he's pulling the tights. One... two... kickout! Krunch makes the tag to Punishment, and now both of the Mercenaries are pounding down on Rucker, while Johnson is to tired to intervene. MICHAEL POSTEMA: To tired. He needs to get off his lazy ass and get in there. He's a _tag_ champion. Help your partner! JERRY COVENANT: Punishment trying to get a scorpion deathlock on, but Rucker pushes up out of that. Punishment goes for a hiptoss, but Rucker reverses it into an abdominal stretch. Punishment with an eye rake and he escapes the hold. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Well, why bother letting your opponent use the ropes all while you waste energy to try to break the hold? What he did there was a bit easier, and a lot smarter. JERRY COVENANT: Punishment whips Rucker into the ropes, double clothesline and both men are down. Referee exercising his count, very slowly however. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Well, no one wants to see a double countout in a match of this magnitude. JERRY COVENANT: Punishment back to his feet first, trying to apply a figure four leglock but Rucker powers out of it. Rucker back on his feet, he lands a forearm, an atomic drop puts Rucker down on the mat. Goes for a kneedrop, but Punishment rolls out of the way. MICHAEL POSTEMA: That's going to cost him a tag opportunity right there. JERRY COVENANT: Punishment with a tag to Kaptain Krunch. Both Mercenaries in the ring, and they land a double clothesline on Rucker. Johnson has had enough and he's in the ring! All four men brawling, the champs pounding on the Mercenaries in opposite corners. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Gee... I wonder what's coming up here. JERRY COVENANT: Double Irish whips and Krunch and Punishment collide against one another. Rucker climbing to the top rope, he comes off on Krunch with a moonsault! Going for the pin... one... two... no! MICHAEL POSTEMA: That was close, you almost could have made five dollars! JERRY COVENANT: Rucker and Krunch the legal men. Punishment up from behind, DDT on Rucker! He rolls Krunch over on top. One... two... no! Rucker getting back to his feet. He charges at Krunch, who meets him with a body drop that puts both men over the ropes. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Spilling to the outside of the ring paid for the Mercenaries the last time. Rucker better get himself in the ring if he knows what's good for him. JERRY COVENANT: Krunch with a chair and he wraps it right over the forehead of Johnson, and we've got all four men down on the floor brawling again! MICHAEL POSTEMA: What a fun match this must be for the referee. JERRY COVENANT: Krunch once again with that chair down over the forehead of Johnson, this is to much damage for any one man to take in such a short period of time MICHAEL POSTEMA: He's been busted wide open, and you can't wrestle a match very long when you can't even see what it is you're after. JERRY COVENANT: Rucker and Krunch back in the ring now. Rucker with a reverse neckbreaker right in the middle of the ring, nicely executed. Krunch rolls back to the outside, and Herbie Versmells is right there to throw him back in the ring! MICHAEL POSTEMA: In a title match, a manager has to go above and beyond my friend. JERRY COVENANT: Rucker makes the tag to Johnson. Johnson in the ring via the second rope, he comes off with a double axehandle that knocks Krunch down, and you can now see that this match is taking its toll on all men in the ring. MICHAEL POSTEMA: It's not that we've been running so long, it's that the pace of the match has been so brutal. JERRY COVENANT: Johnson going for an elbowdrop, and Krunch rolls out of the way. Krunch with an elbow drop of his own and he's right on target. Krunch makes the tag to Punishment, Punishment in the ring and Johnson meets him with a sharp uppercut. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Hello! Welcome to the ring! JERRY COVENANT: Johnson catches Punishment in the gut with a knee, lifts him up and _drops_ him down in a powerbomb! Brutal! Johnson making a tag to Rucker, and they together are pounding down on Punishment in their corner. Krunch in the ring, classic tag team mistake! MICHAEL POSTEMA: No! He's just plowing right through the referee over to the other corner. JERRY COVENANT: Punishment taking it to both Rucker and Johnson. Krunch sitting down in the corner. Herbie Versmells just blasted Krunch with a roll of quarters from the outside of the ring! The Referee didn't see a thing! MICHAEL POSTEMA: Well, there is the above and beyond once again. JERRY COVENANT: Johnson throws Punishment outside of the ring. Rucker setting Punishment up into a piledriver position. Johnson going up to the second rope, spike piledriver, they nail it! One... two... three! MICHAEL POSTEMA: See, you could have made some money off of me, Covenant! TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the winners of the match, in a time of 15:02, and _still_ CWO World Tag Team Champions, The Bad Guys! JERRY COVENANT: We'll be right back folks after this last commercial break of hour one, stay tuned! (Camera fades to black.) VOICE-OVER: Order your very own CWO playing card deck. All of your favorite stars plastered down on well laminated and sturdy playing cards. Order yours now! (Camera back to Mustafa Rahim, who stands in the darkness with his head facing down to the ground, an emotionless tone in his voice.) MUSTAFA RAHIM: I am sure it has come to many of your attentions that you have not heard much from "The Law" recently. I am here now to clear your shallow minds of any collapse of me and my brothers. We felt that sometimes a man should let his actions do the talking and not the open mouth that is so often used by many in the CWO. During this time I came to a revelation that "The Law" needed a cleansing. This cleansing could not be achieved with the current members of "The Law" so I sent my brother Steel on a search far and wide for the savior of this great faction. Steel returned to me and he spoke and I quote "I have seen the future of CWO Mustafa, and I have seen the man that will lead us to the top, we have but to bring him here and follow him to the top of the mountain." Knowing that my brother would not say such things unless they were true, I sent for that man, and he did respond. (A tape begins to play, and a slow deep voice begins to speak softly.) VOICE: Time is but of fleeting seconds past, and the future but a glimpse of what has become but, not yet allowed us to see. I shall come forward to the past and deliver the future. Listen not to the words I speak, but rather to the message that I hear. I have said what is to be said, and you have listened just the same. No more words will say what actions will tell. MUSTAFA RAHIM: It has been often said that men fear most what that don't know. If that is so. Fear is here and no more must be said. (The lights go dark and the camera fades out on the faint shadow of Mustafa Rahim. Back to Jerry and Mike.) JERRY COVENANT: Well, Mustafa Rahim with some very mysterious comments concerning his upcoming match with Mondo Gecko and the CPE in Thunderdome. We will be talking about that very unique match that will happen on September 28 in San Antonio, TX, at SEPTEMBER SLAM II: REVENGE IS THE NAME OF THE GAME in the weeks to come. However, we now want to take you back to last week's FEVER, in which Gold Kard International member and current CWO North American champion Masher Maccabee certainly got a rude awakening.... (Footage is shown from last week's FEVER, as The Focus is fighting Masher Maccabee, who is accompanied by Moses.) MELINN: And The Focus has had enough of Moses' interference, he's taking it to him outside the ring! FERRARA: Wait a minute... somebody's coming down to ringside.... MELINN: It's Fergus McTavish! THOMAS: What's he doing here? MELINN: I don't know, but he's got a haggis in his hands.... THOMAS: Ugh. I can smell it from here. MELINN: Masher Maccabee getting back on his feet as the referee tries to separate Moses and The Focus outside the ring. And now Fergus McTavish is up on the ring apron, holding the haggis. THOMAS: Get that psycho out of here! MASHER MACCABEE (to Fergus): Hey, you skirt-wearin' freak... why don't you get the hell out of here? What are you going to do, hit with me that? (He laughs, as Fergus smiles broadly, and then hauls off and hits Masher with the haggis square in the nose!) MELINN: Oh my goodness! THOMAS: Disqualify The Focus! He's in cahoots with McTavish! MELINN: Masher's nose has been busted open... I didn't think a haggis could... wait a minute... McTavish is removing a brick from the haggis! FERRARA (laughing): Can you believe this! THOMAS: That... that unmitigated... jerk! (Jump cut to later on.) COVENANT VOICE-OVER: Masher Maccabee was then easy pickings for the Television champion The Focus. However, if that wasn't enough, watch what happened after the match.... (Fergus is shown coming over to the announcer's table.) FERGUS MCTAVISH (right in Thomas' face, who is enraged): Lissen up, you slimy goat turd! I'm puttin' ya and da rest a yer wussy pals in GKI on notice! I'll rip ya all a new arse, one by one, or all at once I dinna care! Gets yerself ready ta face me tartan wrath! Oh yeah, one more ting.... (Fergus drops the mike and moons Masher, who is still holding his broken bleeding nose.) MCTAVISH: Gets ready fer da Whirling Scottish Devil! IT'S NOT OATMEAL! (Fans start to chant, 'OATMEAL! OATMEAL! OATMEAL!') THOMAS: You son of a bitch.... (Thomas leaps over the announcing table, attacking Fergus, who quickly takes Thomas down to the mat outside the ring and starts wailing on him. Back to this week's show and the ring.) TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit, and it is for the CWO Television Title. Coming down the aisle, weighing in at 295 pounds, from Glasgow, Scotland, here is Fergus "Whirling Scottish Devil" McTavish! (Fast playing bagpipe version of "Saturday Night" plays.) JERRY COVENANT: The wildest man in the CWO has his shot tonight. It's not often McTavish is in the spotlight, but I wouldn't think it will affect him in much way. MICHAEL POSTEMA: You're right, he's already hopeless, especially picking a fight with Gold Kard International. TONY THE K: And his opponent, from parts unknown, the CWO Television Champion, here is The Focus! ("Milque Toast" by Helmet plays as fans boo.) JERRY COVENANT: Once again, we see a match where neither man care about the fans' opinions, and it once again is for a title, and it's hard to say what either of one of these men _won't_ do when you get right down to it. MICHAEL POSTEMA: As insane as McTavish is, there is just that eerie quality around Focus. JERRY COVENANT: McTavish runs across the ring with an elbow smash. Focus back up to his feet, nails McTavish with the double axehandle. Focus lifts McTavish up, tiger driver! Focus picks him up again, and we see a _second_ tiger driver. Focus now stomping away with reckless abandon. MICHAEL POSTEMA: McTavish must have figured if he got the quick start that he'd be able to wrap the match up, but it isn't working that way at _all_ as of right now. JERRY COVENANT: Focus with a reverse neckbreaker. He puts McTavish in a full nelson, but Fergus breaks out of it and nails a punch. McTavish with a chop, the champ with a double axehandle, follows it with an eye gouge. Fergus swinging around blindly and he lands one that knocks Focus down. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Blinding him was like caging a dangerous animal, it just made him frantic and even more dangerous. JERRY COVENANT: McTavish fires Focus into the ropes, catches him with a powerslam! One... two... kickout! Fergus brings Focus to his feet, and Focus comes up with a kneelift, forearm smash. Fergus shrugs it off and puts on a bearhug. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Well, he should know that he has the strength advantage in this match, and if he wants to have any chance of winning, he _has_ to use it. JERRY COVENANT: Focus biting McTavish in order to get out of that one. Focus with a kick, a chop, clothesline McTavish but that has no effect. Focus off the ropes for a second one, McTavish ducks it and comes back with a spinebuster! FERGUS MCTAVISH (to crowd): It's _not_ oatmeal! MICHAEL POSTEMA: No buddy, it isn't. It also isn't wise to sit there and waste time when you could be winning a match. JERRY COVENANT: Focus getting back up to his feet, he catches McTavish with a low blow. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Ouch! JERRY COVENANT: The referee admonishing The Focus for that deliberate low blow... wait a minute... from the crowd... it's Masher Maccabee! MICHAEL POSTEMA: This isn't good for Fergus! JERRY COVENANT: Masher's got that North American title belt in his hand... and he nails Fergus with it! MICHAEL POSTEMA: And the ref didn't see it! JERRY COVENANT: Fergus just got absolutely clocked by that, obvious retaliation for Fergus' actions last week on FEVER. However, now The Focus is back on the attack, as he follows Masher's attack with a headbutt. Focus with a snap suplex, goes for the pin, one... two... kickout! MICHAEL POSTEMA: Now maybe Fergus will wake up and realize that the next time he gets a decent advantage he needs to make use of it. JERRY COVENANT: Focus picks McTavish up, German suplex... he holds onto the waistlock, hits him with another one and goes for the pin! One... two... three! JERRY COVENANT: With a little help from the North American Champion, Masher Maccabee. Well, it's been as great as usual folks, but it is time for us to leave you in the talented hands of the second hour broadcast team. When we come back, you will join th em, don't go away!
