TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER 9/30/97 - HOUR #1

5 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWO TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER - HOUR #2 9/30/97 "LIVE" FROM SANTA FE, NM Producer: [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] ) Executive Producer: Brent G. Alles ( [detail withheld] ) RATED: TV-14 (V) (A spinning globe is shown, as the CWO logo is slowly superimposed over it.) VOICE-OVER: The CWO... providing wrestling excellence the world over.... (Montage of CWO action is shown as Aerosmith's "Fever" plays in the background. We go to a roaring crowd in Santa Fe, NM, as pyrotechnics go off to signal the start of the show. We join our announcing team of Covenant, Johnson, and Postema.) JERRY COVENANT: Welcome fans to the number one, action packed wrestling excitement you'll find on Television _anywhere_! This is Tuesday Night Fever! We had it all last night at September Slam II, surprises, upsets, and all round excitement! ERIC JOHNSON: Title changes and title retainments, pure wrestling, brawling, cheating, fire, weapons, anarchy, blood. Those cage matches were unbelievable. JERRY COVENANT: Yes indeed. I'm being told that we may have still footage for you from the trucks out back, but due to the graphic material in the Thunderdome match, we aren't allowed to show portions of that. MICHAEL POSTEMA: That's why we need to get rid of the stupid "family" label that has been slapped on us. THEN we can show stuff like that! That's what the people want to see, you heard the cheers last night! JERRY COVENANT: So much has gone down, and yet so much remains to occur. Let's get to the ring with Tony the K for our first match. TONY THE K: The following contest is scheduled for one fall, with a twenty minute time limit. Making his way down to the ring, weighing in at 332 pounds, from Oakland, California, here is the Painmaster! ("Wherever I May Roam" by Metallica plays) JERRY COVENANT: Although he did earn the respect from many fans last night at September Slam II, Painmaster was unable to accomplish what Gold Kard wanted. Fergus McTavish has survived. MICHAEL POSTEMA: That was the past, and this guy has a bright future as far as I'm concerned. ERIC JOHNSON: And he has been signed to take on one of the best in the league, Brian "The Hammer" Willams, at Terror Night, 1997. MICHAEL POSTEMA: He'll have a fun time with that, tossing around Brian. ERIC JOHNSON: You should know by now never to under estimate him. TONY THE K: and his opponent, weighing in at 320 pounds, from Chicago, Illinois, here is "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher! ("Hit em' High" plays as the fans cheer) JERRY COVENANT: And "Lone Wolf" is wasting no time, sprinting to the ring! MICHAEL POSTEMA: Well, there's a brilliant strategy... JERRY COVENANT: Deutscher explodes under the bottom rope, but Painmaster is right there to meet him, stomping him down. Deutscher back up on his feet, Painmaster with a belly to belly suplex! MICHAEL POSTEMA: Fricken idiot, runs right at a guy like Painmaster who has been taking on the best in the CWO and dominating. JERRY COVENANT: Lone Wolf back up on his feet, Painmaster hits him with a double axehandle and Deutscher rolls to the outside of the ring. Painmaster following him out, and both men beating the hell out of each other! MICHAEL POSTEMA: Just some of the action that makes Fever the number one show. That, and the fact that we _don't_ have that idiot O'Brien on the show! That staggering drunk can't even call a match without wanting to run down to the ring. JERRY COVENANT: I assume you're refering to the Lone Wolf's match against Lord Hile Troy on the last episode of CWO SHOWCASE? MICHAEL POSTEMA: Got that right. This idiot drank so many bottles of beer, he fit right in with those redneck Texans! Those people spend more time polishing _their_ belts than our champions do! ERIC JOHNSON: A lot of great wrestlers came out of Texas you know, Mike. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Who? "Outlaw" Ron Bass? JERRY COVENANT: Back to the match at hand, both men in the ring. Painmaster rocks Deutscher with a hard right hand, Irish whip into the corner, it's reversed by Deutscher and he comes in with a clothesline. ERIC JOHNSON: These two are moving around fairly quick when you consider the size of both men. JERRY COVENANT: Painmaster with a knee, a bit below the belt. He's got Ryan set up for the "Ultimate Pain," front face suplex! He hits it! One... two... kickout! MICHAEL POSTEMA: Now that's surprising! JERRY COVENANT: Painmaster with a surprised look on his face after that one. He puts Deutscher in a piledriver position, but the Lone Wolf flips him over. Painmaster getting back up to his feet, and Deutscher greets him with a chokeslam! MICHAEL POSTEMA: Hello! JERRY COVENANT: Lone Wolf putting Painmaster in a Boston crab, and now we'll see just how much he has mastered his pain. Painmaster gritting his teeth from the hold, and he pushes up right out of it! MICHAEL POSTEMA: How's _that_ for mastering it? He avoided it all together! JERRY COVENANT: Lone Wolf firing Painmaster into the ropes, goes for a clothesline but Painmaster ducks it! Lone Wolf coming back off the other end, and he gets caught in a belly to belly suplex! ERIC JOHNSON: Painmaster is very proficient with the suplexes and slams in general. Deutscher will have to stay away from those if he wants to last much longer in this match. JERRY COVENANT: Painmaster picks up Deutscher, and snaps him over in a belly to back suplex! Deutscher quickly becoming rocked by all of these. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Now if I were O'Brien, I'd be complaining and ranting and raving that Painmaster just used two suplexes in a row. Who is he to criticize Hile Troy's wrestling style, or my announcing? JERRY COVENANT: Painmaster going for the pin... one... kickout. He fires Deutscher into the ropes, going for a fireman's carry but Lone Wolf turns it into a crucifix! One... two... thr... no! Close, so close there. ERIC JOHNSON: I must say I'm impressed by this match. I truly thought that it would just be a lot of punching and kicking. JERRY COVENANT: Deutscher going up to the second rope, he comes off with a flying bulldog headlock! Nice move there, and he jarred Painmaster's neck with it. ERIC JOHNSON: Setting him up for his stun gun finisher. JERRY COVENANT: Deutscher putting him in a piledriver position. Lifts him up and _smears_ him down in a powerbomb! He's giving the signal for the stun gun! ERIC JOHNSON: Well, Painmaster's neck has been weakened from those two. If Deutscher hits this, Painmaster will be in serious trouble! JERRY COVENANT: And he does hit it! Referee Alles right there to make the count! One... two... three! It's over! TONY THE K: The winner of this match, in a time of 8:42, "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher! JERRY COVENANT: We'll be right back after the commercials... wait a minute... CWO Commissioner Tom Krepps is coming down to the ring.... (Tom Krepps gets in the ring as Deutscher looks at him coldly.) TOM KREPPS: All right, "Lone Wolf," I've got something to say to you. Last Sunday, at SEPTEMBER SLAM II: REVENGE IS THE NAME OF THE GAME, you lost your match to "The Brazilian Lover" Carlos Maia. That's a fact. However, after the match, you then proceeded to give a Stun Gun to a CWO official. Now, as you know, you have been fined heavily for that, and I expect a written apology from you to go to that official. However, I want to talk to you about a pattern I see in you. "LONE WOLF" RYAN DEUTSCHER: Oh yeah, what's that? TOM KREPPS: I don't think it's any big secret that you're try to emulate one "Stone Cold" Steve Austin. Now, having another wrestler as your hero is fine, but let me tell you, Deutscher, don't take it so far that you actually start doing the stupid stuff that he does as well. "LONE WOLF" RYAN DEUTSCHER: First of all, yes, I consider myself a fan of "Stone Cold" Steve Austin. However, I'm not trying to directly copy him. In fact, I'm going to do him one better.... (He kicks Krepps in the midsection and delivers a Stun Gun!) "LONE WOLF" RYAN DEUTSCHER: I'M GONNA START AT THE TOP! (Deutscher leaves, getting a pop from the hardcore fans, and getting booed by the marks.) COVENANT: What the... Deutscher is out of his mind! POSTEMA: He really thinks he's "Stone Cold" Steve Austin! JOHNSON: Somebody had better get some medical attention for Tom Krepps! COVENANT: Ladies and gentlemen, we have to go to commercial, we'll keep you updated on this situation! What a lunatic! (Logo and music come up as we fade to commercial.) VOICE-OVER: If you missed _the_ world title defense of the year, if you missed the mystery, the surprise, the adrenaline that was September Slam II... you can still see it! Available this Wednesday, Replay of Revenge! Order now! (Back to FEVER.) JERRY COVENANT: We saw many things go down last night, and one of them was the "respect match" between Brent "The Ace" Alles and Lord Hile Troy. (still shots of match shown) MICHAEL POSTEMA: That's right, and if you thought this was going to be a nice and simple scientific match, boy, have you got another thing coming! JERRY COVENANT: Alles controlled the first half of the match, Troy came back in the second half, then both men went to the outside. Alles broke Troy through a table on the first trip outside. On the second... he tried it again, but the tabled didn't break. MICHAEL POSTEMA: No, but Alles and Troy both did! JERRY COVENANT: The footage from the result of that is to graphic to show. Alles was taken to the hospital and released we understand earlier this morning with some bruised ribs and a shoulder injury, he should return to action shortly. Hile Troy on the other hand had internal injuries, explaining him coughing up blood. He underwent many exams by the doctors, but is still in the hospital for examination. However, we have him via phone, I believe. Hile Troy, are you there? LORD HILE TROY: (via phone) Yeah Jerry, I'm here. JERRY COVENANT: What can you tell us of your injuries, and when do you expect to be back in action? LORD HILE TROY: The doctors have said I have a bruised kidney, some cracked ribs, and lower back injuries to my spine. As far as when I'll be back into the ring, as soon as they can get me a contract to fight that fat drunk again. JERRY COVENANT: So you're telling me that after all of this, Brent Alles _still_ does not have your respect? LORD HILE TROY: Why should I respect him? How much grappling did he use? He drove me through a table twice. Let me tell you, when I do get back in there with him, I'll kick his ass. I've got other plans for his chic's ass though, if you know what I mean. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Ha! Give em hell, Troy! JERRY COVENANT: Would you care to comment on any of the other results from the card last Sunday? LORD HILE TROY: You saw all the same stuff that I did. You saw GKI go out there, especially Masher, and show the CWO why we still are the best at what we do, hands down. ERIC JOHNSON: Do you care to comment on the return of your once hated rival, and the man who defeated you for a world title, Foolkiller? (Dial Tone is Heard) JERRY COVENANT: I guess he didn't have anything to say about that one. ERIC JOHNSON: Well, after I heard the name I went back into the files and did some searching, and Foolkiller in his short career was one of the most dominate wrestlers ever. He defeated Lord Hile Troy _and_ Rebel Yell, two CWO greats in a triangle match, then defeated Sandman in a title vs. title match. JERRY COVENANT: That being one of the greatest matches in the CWO history as well. Many are wondering if Foolkiller is the "thing that has returned", which Reverend and his minions keep speaking of. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Just what we'd need, Reverend, Bloodbath, Sandman, and Foolkiller all working together. JERRY COVENANT: Well, none the less, let everyone in the CWO take heed that Foolkiller has returned. But now, let's get up to the ring for our next match. TONY THE K: The following matchup is tag action, with a 30 minute time limit. Making their way to the ring, at a combined weight of 454 pounds, here are Red Raven and Holocaust! ("Firestarter" by Prodigy begins to play) MICHAEL POSTEMA: I'll tell ya, what I loved about last night was when all the people in the arena, including Melinn thought that Joe Eagle had the match won _for sure_, and then Reb pulled it off. JERRY COVENANT: Reb stating that he had "no one" left in the CWO to defeat, although I'm sure the championship committee can find him some suitable competition. ERIC JOHNSON: Easily, the list would go on and on. Brent Alles is moving up in line after his victory last night. Chris Myers is also another one Reb would have to look out for. Now Foolkiller. Fergus McTavish, Moses, Masher, the list goes on and on. TONY THE K: and their opponents... at a combined weight of 525 pounds, they are the CWO WORLD Tag Team CHAMPIONS. Gavin and Conor McCloud, The Highlanders! ("Scotland the Brave" plays as fans cheer) JERRY COVENANT: That's right, last night the Highlanders pulled off what many people said they could never again do, they won the tag team belts. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Yeah, they won them, but how long can they hold _on_ to them is the big question. Coming up at TERROR NIGHT 97', they've got to defend against the Newton Brothers. JERRY COVENANT: Holocaust starting off against Conor McCloud, he trips him up and lands a fistdrop. Holocaust with a reverse chinlock, but Conor is able to reverse it. Conor with a double axehandle. Holocaust makes the tag to Red Raven. ERIC JOHNSON: I guess he had enough of that. JERRY COVENANT: Red Raven in the ring in a big way, savate kick! He locks on a claw hold, forcing Conor down trying to get the pin from it. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Pinning one half of the world tag team champions with your single hand, good idea. JERRY COVENANT: Conor McCloud dragging himself over to the ropes, and he makes it, and makes the tag to Gavin. Gavin in the ring, a hard chop across the chest of Raven, and he fires him to the outside. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Anything is legal on the outside, I don't know how wise it was to do that with a high flyer like Red Raven. ERIC JOHNSON: For once, I agree with you Postema. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Stick with me kid, you'll go places. Otherwise, you'll end up on Showcase with that hoser O'Brien. JERRY COVENANT: All four men on the outside of the ring. Gavin with a chair, hovering over Raven, and Raven spews his fire mist, but it's blocked by the chair! MICHAEL POSTEMA: That chair is a collector's item now. JERRY COVENANT: Holocaust and Conor McCloud throwing punches back and forth on the outside of the ring, Conor whips Holocaust into the steps! Gavin and Raven back in the ring. Raven off the ropes, goes for a flying body block, and Gavin turns it into a powerslam! One... two... no! MICHAEL POSTEMA: Get in there Holocaust! JERRY COVENANT: The champions looking in prime form tonight. Gavin with a headbutt, he throws Raven into the ropes and pastes him with a clothesline. Elbow drop, missed! Raven up on his feet, and he catches Gavin off guard with a dropkick. ERIC JOHNSON: Raven is the quickest man in this match, and if he wants a victory he'll have to utilize that. JERRY COVENANT: Raven with a karate chop, Raven with a backbreaker! He bounds off the ropes, springboard moonsault! One... two... kickout! MICHAEL POSTEMA: Nicely done, you won't see the Highlanders doing a move like that! JERRY COVENANT: They haven't needed to thus far. Raven ramming the head of Gavin into the turnbuckle, and that has no effect, Gavin returns the favor. Gavin running Raven into the opposite corner, Raven's head hit the connecting bolt! ERIC JOHNSON: Well, he's living up to his name "Red" now. JERRY COVENANT: Raven is busted open. Gavin with a double axehandle, and he makes the tag to Conor. Fans cheering in homage to the new champs. Conor picks Raven up, going for a slam of some type. Red Raven floats over top, spinwheel kick! MICHAEL POSTEMA: Brutal! Raven should just focus on singles competition the way he's carried the effort thus far. ERIC JOHNSON: Well, until he makes a tag, he has no choice but to carry the effort! JERRY COVENANT: Raven makes the tag to Holocaust. Holocaust on the second rope, flying lariat connects! He goes up to the top, spinning leg lariat... he landed it! One... two... kickout! ERIC JOHNSON: Give Conor credit there for being able to kick out of that, even if he was fresh in the ring. JERRY COVENANT: Holocaust with a backdrop, tags Red Raven back into the ring. Red Raven on the top rope, he goes for a frog splash but Conor gets his knees up! Conor makes the tag to Gavin. Gavin with an atomic drop. MICHAEL POSTEMA: They tried to go to the top to many times, that's all that there is to it. JERRY COVENANT: Gavin off the ropes for a clothesline, but Red Raven turns it into a belly to belly suplex! Give him credit here, Red Raven is showing us a lot of fight. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Or a nice reaction to some uppers. JERRY COVENANT: Raven going for the pin... one, two... no! Raven wiping the blood away from his eyes to see. MICHAEL POSTEMA: WHY did Holocaust tag him back in? JERRY COVENANT: I couldn't tell you, Mike. I was wondering the same thing. Raven on the second rope, he comes off with a flying bulldog and takes one half of the tag champs down. He goes for the pin once again, and once again he fails. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Well, if he can keep up the high impact moves, he may just win yet. JERRY COVENANT: Anything is possible. Raven trying to go up top once again, but Conor shakes the ropes and Raven takes a tumble! Gavin makes the tag to Conor, who is going up top! ERIC JOHNSON: It looks like we're going to see the quickening! JERRY COVENANT: Gavin with the superplex, Conor with a splash! Holocaust is making no attempt to even enter the ring! One... two... three! It's over! TONY THE K: The winners of the match, and _still_ CWO World Tag Team Champions... The Highlanders! JERRY COVENANT: All right, we'll be right back with an update on the great action from September Slam II, right after this commercial. (Logo and music come up as we fade to commercial. JERRY COVENANT: All right folks, one of the more shocking things that went down last night, besides all of the great action, was the discovery that Holocaust is indeed the brother of former CWO autocrat, Gold Kard. MICHAEL POSTEMA: And we all saw the look on Gold Kard's face when he found out, he was a pale white. Why, remains to be known. ERIC JOHNSON: Well, even with my research I couldn't unfold the type of relationship that Gold Kard shared with his brother. Will they tag? Will they feud? Only time will tell. JERRY COVENANT: But next we've got a North American Title match for you folks, a member of GKI, going up against GKI's #1 target, let's get to the ring. TONY THE K: The following match, is scheduled for one fall, and is for the CWO NORTH AMERICAN TITLE. Making his way to the ring the challenger, weighing in at 295 pounds, from Glasgow, Scotland, here is Fergus McTavish! (Bastardized version of "Saturday Night" begins to play) ERIC JOHNSON: And McTavish can afford to get disqualified if he wants to have fun tonight, because he has the same title shot at TERROR NIGHT, available only on PPV. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Yes, but where a GKI champion goes, GKI goes. JERRY COVENANT: Exactly, as we saw at September Slam in his title match against Rich Clark. TONY THE K: And his opponent... weighing 265 pounds, from Crown Heights, New York, accompanied by Moses, here is the CWO NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION, Masher Maccabee! ("Hava Negila" set to a rap beat plays.) JERRY COVENANT: He may have had that belt literally handed to him, but now Masher Maccabee is a well proven, and well tested champion who has proven he is the real deal. MICHAEL POSTEMA: I had been telling you that all along. JERRY COVENANT: All right, both men eyeing each other now. Lockup, and Fergus with a quick rollup! One... two... kickout! ERIC JOHNSON: I highly doubt that Maccabee was expecting that so early in the contest, especially from Fergus. JERRY COVENANT: McTavish tries to fire the champ into the corner, it's reversed, and Maccabee comes in with a nice jumping knee, a forearm, but Fergus steps right back out with a headbutt. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Stay on him, Masher! JERRY COVENANT: McTavish going for a DDT, but Maccabee lifts him up and slams him down in an atomic drop, a forearm smash and Fergus goes down. Going for the pin... one... two... kickout. ERIC JOHNSON: If you're Gold Kard, you have to wonder what it's going to take. He tried to have Painmaster take him out, that didn't work, he's attacked him, that didn't work, and now he _has_ to defend against Fergus at a major PPV. JERRY COVENANT: Masher with a chop, a thumb to the eye, he lifts Fergus up, tiger driver! A brief display of strength from a wrestler who usually takes to the air. ERIC JOHNSON: Much like his leader, Gold Kard, Maccabee posses skill in many abilities. JERRY COVENANT: Maccabee now playing to the crowd, and Fergus is right back up on his feet! He catches the champ from behind with a double axehandle. Maccabee comes back with a kick, a total brawl! MICHAEL POSTEMA: So much for your waistlocks and armbars. JERRY COVENANT: Both men simply trying to pummel the other, Fergus with a kick, clothesline! McTavish going for the Highland Fling, Maccabee blocks it, DDT! MICHAEL POSTEMA: YES! YES!... er, nice counter wrestling. JERRY COVENANT: The champ picking Fergus back up, goes for a front face piledriver, Fergus blocks that, and comes back with a DDT of his own! ERIC JOHNSON: No excitement now, Postema? MICHAEL POSTEMA: I'm telling you, I'll have you put on Showcase just.. like.. _that_! JERRY COVENANT: Fergus fires Masher into the ropes, powerslam! Going for the pin... one... two... kickout. Fergus lands with a kneedrop, going for the pin once again, and another kickout. ERIC JOHNSON: Fergus has to get on a rally, start using his power moves, then he can get a pinfall. JERRY COVENANT: Fergus with a reverse chinlock, slowing the pace of the match down. Masher reaching up, he grabs a handful of hair, referee Gary Alles not letting that by. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Alles just has it in for any and all GKI wrestlers, that's what it comes down to. ERIC JOHNSON: Well, is there some reason that he should have prejudice against the wrestlers of GKI, after what they have done and attempted to do to the Alles family? JERRY COVENANT: Moses up on the apron now, Fergus walking over toward him, Maccabee coming up from behind, and Fergus meets him with a right hand! He gives the left hand to Moses! He's giving the signal for the highland fling! MICHAEL POSTEMA: No way, not gonna happen! JERRY COVENANT: Fergus loading Masher up on the top rope, and he lands the piledriver! Moses in the ring, he attacks Fergus! Referee calling for the bell. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Fergus is going to get it now! After him Moses! JERRY COVENANT: Fergus hammering Moses down in the corner, and here come Gold Kard and Vegas High Roller, the rest of GKI has hit the ring, Gold Kard and Roller beating Fergus down. Kard grabs Gary Alles by the collar! GOLD KARD: Give this one to your nephew, old man! JERRY COVENANT: Kard setting the official up, piledriver right in the center of the ring, and now it's 4-on-1 in the center of the ring. ERIC JOHNSON: Here come the Highlanders! JERRY COVENANT: Highlanders running down to even out the fight, and GKI has bailed out like the cowards that they are! ERIC JOHNSON: That shouldn't surprise anyone. JERRY COVENANT: Certainly not, but folks, that's all the time we have for this week, so until next time, this is the hour one staff, signing off! Stay tuned, though, for Hour #2! END HOUR #1
