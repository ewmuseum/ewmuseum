CWO SUNDAY SHOWCASE - HOUR #1 9/28/97 Producer: Chris O'Brien ( [detail withheld] ) Executive Producer: Brent G. Alles ( [detail withheld] ) (Fade into a spinning globe, as the CWO logo is slowly superimposed on it.) VOICE-OVER: The CWO... providing wrestling excellence the world over.... ('Stigmata' by Ministry plays as a montage of CWO superstars fill the screen....the Highlanders put the Quickening on Brother Justice...Brent 'The Ace' Alles nails an Allesbreaker on Joe Eagle...Skyhawk with a Skydive onto Chris Myers...Sandman roars as he puts a clothesline to Sandman Jr... the Focus with a Focal Point on Jason Varga....and Johnny Reb holds the CWO World Heavyweight title high in the air) O'BRIEN: WELCOME TO SUNDAY SHOWCASE! (The camera fades in on a jam-packed nightclub. A wrestling ring sits in the middle of the dance floor, while all around it are 1200 screaming CWO fans. On the table is numerous papers and two bottles of Killian's Red. Chris takes a swig from one as Jerry talk) COVENANT: Fans, welcome to a great edition of CWO SUNDAY SHOWCASE! As you can see behind me, we are definitely not in Grand Rapids, and this isn't Kansas, this is the Far Side nightclub in Austin, Texas! This is SHOWCASE, boys and girls, and we are just hours away from SEPTEMBER SLAM II: REVENGE IS THE NAME OF THE GAME! COVENANT: That's right, Chris. In just a couple of hours, from the Alamodome in San Antonio, Texas, we will have one of the most anticipated PPV's in the history of CWO! Their friendship dissolved at TEAM CHALLENGE, and now Joe Eagle, the Comeback Kid, is gunning for Johnny Reb and the CWO Heavyweight title! O'BRIEN: In another big match, a three-way dance for the World Tag Belts have champs the Bad Guys taking on the Mercenaries AND the Highlanders! The Focus and Benjamin Blake Bentley for the TV belt, and the loser LEAVES CWO forever! Goode on the disable list for life! COVENANT: So much and more, as EVERY CWO title will be on the line later on tonight! Masher Maccabee against Rich Clark for the North American title. O'BRIEN: Damn, that's good beer. So now Gold Kard with a Greco-Roman thumb to the eye of Vengeance, who's staggering....and a nice headscissors roll takeover! Vengeance not daunted, and now he pick up Kard.....DDT! Again! O'BRIEN: Man, these fans are LOVING this, Covenant! Vengeance with another whip, and Kard hits....Vengeance follows up with a big boot to the face! Kard could be in real trouble, Jerry! Kard going to the air a lot tonight. O'BRIEN: Maybe he's trying to get back into form for the match against Holocaust. COVENANT: Could be, but Kard is arguing with the referee, and Vengeance is back up..... O'BRIEN: Vengeance is going UP TOP? And Kard turns around....FLYING SHOULDERBLOCK! Man, I felt the ring shake up here! COVENANT: And Kard bounces into the ropes and comes off after that impact....Vengeance misses a DDT attempt, and Kard comes around for a sleeper attempt! O'BRIEN: I can't believe Kard did that! He's cinching it in, and Vengeance is trying for the ropes.....well, the ref asking for a submission, but I don't think Vengeance is going to quit.
