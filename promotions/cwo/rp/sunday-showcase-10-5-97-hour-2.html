SUNDAY SHOWCASE 10/5/97 - HOUR #2

5 October 1997

CWO SUNDAY SHOWCASE - HOUR #1 10/5/97 Producer: Steve White ( [detail withheld] ) Executive Producer: Brent G. Alles ( [detail withheld] ) (Fade into a spinning globe, as the CWO logo is slowly superimposed on it.) VOICE-OVER: The CWO... providing wrestling excellence the world over.... (Fireworks light up the arena as the CWO logo appears in the lower right-hand of the screen. A long panning shot of the arena ends in the announcer's booth where we see Mike Williams and Josh Miller in their usual positions.) MW: Welcome once again to Sunday Showcase folks! In this hour, you'll see Chris 'Diamondback' Myers take on Carlos Maia! The Bad Guys. JM: All right! MW: taking on The Mercenaries, as well as a very special Light Heavyweight title match between Jason 'Flash' Varga and SJPW's Masashi 'Lightning' Lanther! *Chris 'Diamondback' Myers vs. 'The Brazilian Lover' Carlos Maia* CHAD JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. Coming down the aisle... ('You Give Love a Bad Name' by Bon Jovi plays as the fans boo) CHAD JOHNSON: Weighing 235 pounds, from Rio de Janeiro. Brazil, 'The Brazilian Lover' Carlos Maia! (Maia comes to the ring arrogantly strutting and making advancements towards several women.) JM: The ladies love this guy, but what if that ebony Chyna chick he's been seen with catches this? MW: The ever-egotistical Maia had better get focused, because soon enough he's got to take on the former CWO Television Champ and one of the consummate athletes of the CWO... Chris Myers! JM: You never know, Myers could go for that. Wouldn't surprise me. CHAD JOHNSON: And his opponent... ('Shoots and Ladders' by KORN plays as the fans explode) CHAD JOHNSON: Weighing 275 pounds, from Phoenix. AZ, accompanied by Dallas, Diamondback Chris Myers! (Myers comes down slapping hands with the hysterical fans before sliding under the ropes and giving the sign for 'The Rattler' to a huge pop) MW: On of the best-loved men in this sport! Chris Myers! JM: Just look at that desert-baked goon.I think all those buzzard-pies he gets off I-95 are getting to him. (Bell rings. Chris Myers dives down into a small package.) MW: Small package by Diamondback! 1! 2! Kickout! Armbar applied by Myers... release. (Myers dropkicks Maia as he gets to his knees.) JM: Just look at that! Where's the sportsmanship this guy touts?! MW: Dropkick by Myers, spinning elbowdrop! Another! Myers with Maia in a neckbend, swinging into THE RATTLER! 1! 2! And Maia gets a foot on the ropes! JM: Just goes to show ya Mike, no matter how much your opponent *cheats*, cool heads will prevail. (Myers goes for a dropkick but Maia darts out of the way and delivers one to a kneeling Myers.) MW: Beautiful dropkick by Maia! Somersault splash! Maia with a fistful of tights! 1!.shoulder up! Maia with a forearm to the face of Myers and a head full of steam off the ropes! Sunset flip and another handful of tights! 1! 2! Thr...! The ref sees the extra leverage of Maia's! (Maia lands another dropkick on Myers and heads to the top.) JM: He does love those dropkicks MW: Maia up top! We may be seeing that bodypress of his! JM: Good! MW: Myers running to the buckle (Myers runs to the corner, causing Maia to fall on the ringpost) MW: AND RINGS THE BELLS OF MARY! (Crowd groans/pops) MW: Whip to the corner by Maia... big run-in DDT! (Maia lifts up the head of Myers by the hair.) Maia: (to camera) SCHILLO! I SHOW YOU WHO IS SEXIEST MAN, TOUGHEST MAN, AND BEST MAN IN CWO! THIS IS YOU SCHILLO! A FAT WRECK! MW: Broken English insults aimed at one Wayne Schillo, who The Lover will have to face at Terror Night '97. JM: Who cares if it's broken English. The point gets across, and he speaks the truth. (Myers rolls outside the ring to collect his thoughts) MW: Myers on the apron, attempting to get back in, and Maia meets him with a fist to the face! Anoth... NO! Slingshot shoulderblock by Diamondback! Small package! 1!..2!..Reversal! 1! 2! Kickout! JM: Maia is, simply put, great Mike. MW: Maia with a standing wristlock applied... and an open-hand slap across the face keeps Myers on his toes! Another! (Myers begins to look genuinely pissed) MW: Myers with a legsweep escape and a whip to the ropes... FRANKENSTEINER! (Myers dives down for an armbar, but Maia is quick to escape.) MW: Whip to the corner by Maia... Rana! JM: Ya don't see those every day! MW: 1! 2! Kickout with authority! (Myers roars to the crowd, who roars back in appreciation.) MW: Maia running to the corner, Asai Moonsault! BIG MISS! Spinning elbowdrop by Myers! Another! (Myers stomps away on Maia who rolls away from the furious Myers to the outside of the ring.) JM: That's the way to play nice and cool... let that over-worked idiot Myers drain himself crying about his bonehead mistakes in the ring, all the while you can catch your breath. MW: Maia sliding under the ropes and Myers begins stomping away yet again! Spinning elbowdrop! Nothing but mat! Maia from up top! Flying axhandle! (Myers rolls to the outside.) JM: Someone tell that pansy that you can't win from the outside. MW: Maia from the opposite ropes, flying bodypress! (Crowd pop) MW: Maia with a steel chair! And he catches the guardrail! (Maia and Myers trade blows for a while outside the ring before Maia clips the knee of Myers and smashes a chair brutally across his back.) JM: There's that chair again! MW: Maia with a crushing blow to the back of Myers and making his way in to beat the count! (Maia tries to stomp on Myers as he gets back in, but Myers grabs the leg of Maia and wraps it around the ringpost.) JM: Cheater! MW: Myers just made a human pretzel out of the Brazilian Lover's right leg... and a slingshot legdrop brings him in with style! Small package! 1!.2!.3! (Crowd explodes.) Chad Johnson: Ladies and gentlemen..your winner in 9:15... CHRIS... 'DIAMONDBACK... MYYYYYYYYERRRRRS! ('Shoots and Ladders' by Korn plays to a huge ovation as Myers makes his way back to the locker room.) MW: Chris Myers, one step closer to attaining his dream of winning the World Heavyweight Championship at Terror Night '97 from Johnny Reb! JM: Yah, just that, a dream. He'll wake up soon enough and realize that he's a Safeway bagger in wrestler's clothing. (commercial break.) *The Bad Guys vs. The Mercenaries* Chad Johnson: Ladies and gentlemen, the following tag team matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit. Coming down the aisle... ('Iron Man' by Black Sabbath plays as the fans boo) Chad Johnson: At a combined weight of 555 pounds, Kaptain Krunch, Korperal Punishment, Mercenaries! JM: Oh joy. The neo-gestapo MW: The Mercenaries coming off of a loss from September Slam, and my guess is that they're looking to make an example of their opponents here tonight, and get right back on the championship ladder. Chad Johnson: And their opponents... (Theme music plays as the fans boo) Chad Johnson: At a combined weight of 519 pounds, accompanied by Herbie Versmells, Joe 'The Mean Guy' Rucker, Ron 'The Rude Guy' Johnson, Bad Guys! MW: It's a sure bet that both of these teams are in a peachy mood after being beaten out by the Highlanders just last Sunday for the World Tag-Titles. JM: Those little sheep-lovers will get theirs. Don't worry. (Bell rings as Punishment runs into the ropes and Johnson lays into him with a shoulder tackle.) MW: Johnson with a shoulder tackle, and a series of stomps to the prone Punishment! JM: Is that the peachy attitude you were mentioning? (Johnson lands a splash and covers, but Punishment gets out after the 1 count.) MW: Tag to Rucker... and Rucker continues the stomping of Punishment! JM: This is great! MW: Johnson distracting the ref (Rucker pulls something from his tights and nails Punishment across the forehead with it.) MW: Bad Guy's Bash! 1!..2!3! KICKOUT! Kneedrop by Rucker followed up by a rake to the back and another series of stomps! Kneedrop! (Rucker laughs maniacally while stomping away on Punishment.) JM: The guy obviously *loves* his job! MW: Headlock by Rucker... reversal into a whip by Punishment... reversal by Rucker... and a clothesline levels the militant one! (Punishment rolls over to Krunch who begins to clean house with a series of rights.) MW: Krunch's on fire! Right hook sends Johnson to the outside! Punishment and Krunch with Rucker... double faceslam! JM: Oooh, that's gotta just plain SUCK! MW: Rucker with a tag to Johnson! (On the way out of the ring, Rucker clips the knee of Krunch, taking him to the mat.) MW: Johnson from the top! FLYING DOUBLE STOMP! JM: GREAT TEAMWORK! Johnson: (To Krunch) GET UP YOU F****** P****! MW: Some rather rough comments directed towards Krunch, and taking up time that may be used to get the win I might add. JM: Hey. He's just savoring the moment. MW: Johnson with a whip to the ropes... reversal by Krunch... big backdrop! Scorpion deathlock! (Krunch rocks back wrenching the hold in as Johnson grabs the ropes.) MW: Release by Krunch, and a forearm makes sure Johnson stays down... Krunch up top! FLYING LEGDROP! (Krunch tags to Punishment.) MW: The Mercenaries with a whip to Johnson... big double clothesline! Punishment with the leg of Johnson... figure-4! JM: Can it be?! A wrestling move in this 'wrestling' league?! The end is nigh Mike.. MW: Johnson in the ropes now and a release by Punishment... Krunch storms the ring! Pickup by Krunch... FLYING LARIAT BY PUNISHMENT! JM: That's the move that *destroyed* Joe Eagle last week! No one does it like the Mercs! (As Johnson hits the mat, he reaches out and tags Rucker.) MW: Tag to Rucker! Punishment with a small package... 1!.. kickout! Kick to the gut by Rucker! Rocker dropper! Rucker up top! Moonsault! Splash! JM: Great fluidity! These guys may not be technical on a usual basis, but they can string together moves in a pinch! MW: Rucker reaching in his trunks JM: I hate it when they bunch up like that. MW: And whipping out a pair of brass knucks! (Rucker nails Punishment across the chin as he falls back like a ton of bricks.) MW: BAD GUY'S BASH! JM: That's the second time he's eaten those! Ahahah! (The ref signals for the bell. Krunch leaps in the ring and begins swinging away.) Chad Johnson: Ladies and gentlemen, your winners in 7:03, THE... MERRRRRCENARIEEEEEES! ('Iron Man' by Black Sabbath plays as the fans boo the unconscious Punishment and the furious Krunch. Commercial break. When we come back, Masashi "Lightning" Lanther is seen backstage, waiting to go out and face Jason Varga.) Masashi Lanther: Though my last appearance in CWO was lackluster, tonight, I have the opportunity to wrestle Jason "Flash" Varga for the Light Heavyweight championship. This is an opportunity for me to once again showcase my skills, and possibly win another title. I will not insult Mr. Varga, for I am sure he is an excellent competitor. This shall hopefully be a confrontation that will test us both to our limits, and allow the fans tonight to see a match of the highest caliber. Tonight Jason Varga, I hope to take you to the limit, and I expect the same from you. Win or lose, you will know how dangerous an opponent I can be after tonight. (Lanther leaves the dressing room, and prepares to head to the ring.) CWO LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION TITLE MATCH *Jason 'Flash' Varga vs. Masashi 'Lightning' Lanther* Chad Johnson: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. It is for the CWO Light Heavyweight Champion title. Coming down the aisle... ("Shock Me" plays as the fans cheer) Chad Johnson: Weighing 233 pounds, from Nagasaki, representing the SJPW, Masashi "Lightning" Lanther! (The colorful Lanther runs to the ring slapping hands with the fans.) JM: Oh great. The Salvation Army Lucha-tight wearing Lanther is back in action. Whoopee. MW: My colleague making reference to the flashy garb of Lanther JM: Flashy?! Hell! It's downright goofy! Chad Johnson: And his opponent... ('Fanfare for the Common Man' by Aaron Copland plays as the fans cheer) Chad Johnson: Weighing 235 pounds, from Jordan. NY, accompanied by Jim Peer, the CWO Light Heavyweight Champion, Jason 'Flash' Varga! (Varga makes his way to the ring slapping ands with eager fans.) JM: A flashy bum and a fruitcake. This is gonna be a good one. (Bell rings.) MW: Lanther with a rolling reverse cradle! 1! 2! Thr! Shoulder up! Lanther off the ropes... crucifix! Reversal by Varga! Samoan Drop! 1! 2! Shoulder up! JM: Does this make sense to you Mike? Lanther, a bum, has one match in the CWO. Which he loses. The next week he gets a title shot. (Varga plays to the crowd to a good pop) MW: Varga with an elbow to the chest of Lanther, hooks the leg, small package! 1! 2! Kickout! (Varga gets to his knees and argues with the ref about a slow count.) MW: Varga having some differences with the ref here tonight, and a chop to the back from Lanther ends his little sidebar! Release German suplex! JM: Wow! I never knew a head could bounce like that! MW: Lanther with a stunning suplex on Varga, and here's a bridging belly-to-back! 1! 2! Thr! Shoulder up! JM: I think the fruitcake's still rattled from that big German he took! MW: Lanther with a whip to the ropes, and a palm blow sends Varga to the outside! Lanther off the opposite ropes, baseball slide! (Varga moves out of the way and jerks Lanther to the cement before dropping an elbow and getting back in the ring. Varga gets atop a ringpost and plays to the crowd as Lanther gets back in the ring.) MW: Varga wasting time once again, and coming off the ropes with a flying sunset flip! Lanther out before the 1-count coming back with an Irish Whip to the corner, monkey flip sends Varga over the ropes! JM: For a touted lightweight with great agility, yadda yadda, he sure is coming off looking like a hammered bum. MW: Indeed Varga appears be off of his usual game today... uncharacteristic... OH MY GOD! Lanther with a springboard dropkick to the outside! JM: Look at the li'l' sucker fly! MW: Lanther with a whip and the ringpost is the end of the line for Varga! Whip to the apron! (Lanther rolls Varga back in the ring and plays to the crowd. Lanther whips Varga to the corner and charges in.) MW: Whip to the corner by Lanther... and Varga leaping off the buckle with a flying forearm! JM: Viva El Tito Santana! Ariba! MW: Varga from the second buckle... Asai Moonsault! Kneedrop and a reverse chinlock! JM: Well. That was an anti-climatic ending if I do say so myself. MW: Lanther with a hair pull and an escape from the lock, whip to the ropes and a leaping clothesline dumps Varga outside once again! (Lanther gets on top the post and performs a flying dropkick to the outside, which pegs Varga directly in the back.) MW: Missile dropkick and Varga eats the railing! (Lanther slinks back into the ring as Varga gets up to his feet holding his back.) MW: Both men back in and Varga fires off a right! Whip to the ropes, legsweep takes the contender off his feet! Varga up top! Flash Flood! NOBODY'S HOME! JM: Hah! (Lanther drops the leg across the throat of the prone Varga and leaps to the top buckle.) MW: Lanther from up top, flying elbow! (Varga jerks his leg up and clips Lanther in the jaw to a chorus of cheers.) MW: Varga berating Lanther, who comes up with a right hook across the chin! JM: That'll teach that li'l' fruit to mouth off! MW: Armdrag takedown... reversal by Varga and another stiff fist to the face of Varga! JM: Varga's gotta be one of those S&M freaks. He's just gotta. MW: Varga goes to for a rid e to the corner, and a Lanther charges in with a chop! Forearm by Varga! (Both men trade blows for a good minute or so until Lanther whips Varga to the corner.) MW: Varga to the near corner... bouncing off and coming out with a slap! Duckunder by Lanther! Big Release German Suplex! JM: I love that move! MW: 1! 2! Thr! Kickout! Lanther looking less than impressed with his opposition heading to the second buckle, Asai and a bridging back-suplex! (Varga gets out before the 1-count and takes down Lanther with a legsweep.) MW: Varga up top, flying body press! JM: And there's nothing to greet him but mat! This is great! The most impressive man in this championship 'match' is a mid-carder from another fed! MW: Lanther with an armdrag and Varga puts the brakes on, and comes out with one of his own! Varga with a series of stomps and crushing axhandles to the back of Lanther! JM: I think the little fruitcake's had enough! I've never seen him act like this! MW: Varga from the top... flying axhandle! (Lanther leaps to his feet, jabs Varga in the gut, and proceeds to dump him out of the ring.) MW: Lanther off the ropes! Baseball slide finds it's mark! (Crowd pop) JM: (to Varga) HOW'S THAT METAL TASTE, PRETTY BOY?! (Both men brawl outside the ring. Lanther relies on chops to the chest, but Varga puts a halt to him with a whip to the steel steps.) MW: And once again Varga feels the unforgiving steel! (Both men slowly get in the ring.) MW: Lanther charges in with a forearm to the face of Varga! Spinning headscissors cradle! 1! Shoulder up! Lanther with a bow-and-arrow applied! (Varga howls in pain) JM: Varga sounds ready to sing backup for the 'Village People'! MW: Lanther with a release and a dropkick to the lower back of Varga! Lanther from the top! Moonsault! (Lanther tries to apply a chinlock deathlock, but Varga reaches the ropes as he applies it.) MW: Varga apparently still knows where he is in the ring JM: There's a first. (Jim Peer gets on the apron and taunts Lanther who turns his head.) MW: Peer getting involved with the match, and Varga comes off the top with The Flash Flood! (Crowd pop) MW: Rollup by Varga! 1! Shoulder up! JM: That's pretty impressive Mike! Especially considering he took that move the way Varga likes it. From behind. MW: Enough with the insults... Lanther with a whip to the corner and a big bridging back suplex! 1! Shoulder up! Varga with a closed fist! Another! And Lanther decks him with kick to the gut! Anoth... no! Varga with the leg of Lanther, Dragonscrew takedown! Varga from up top! Flying bodypress! (Lanther gets to his feet and raises a foot that catches Varga in the ribs to a chorus of boos) MW: Lanther taking exception to the boos form the crowd and taking his eyes off of Varga... punch to the small of the back! JM: Just like that little punk. MW: Varga up top... flying forearm! Figure-4 to the downed Lanther, but the international superstar's just too much at this point and time in the bout! (Lanther whips Varga to the corner and rushes in.) MW: Lanther with a monkey flip attempt... and an elbow from Varga ends that mighty fast! Another flying forearm from the champ! Whip by Varga, DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE! (The ref begins to count both men out until Varga gets to his feet.) MW: Varga with an axhandle to the back of Lanther... and Lanther connects with a brutal upwards kick! JM: Did ya hear that crack!? MW: 1! 2! Thr! Shoulder up! Lanther with a whip to the ropes and charges in with a kick that catches Varga in the head on the rebound! (The ref begins to count as Varga lays outside on the tarmac unconscious.) Ref: 7.8..9 (Bell rings as Lanther victoriously raises both hands in the air to some cheers.) MW: It appears that Lanther has indeed pulled an upset here tonight folks! Keep in mind that a title can *not* change hands through a countout. Chad Johnson: Ladies and gentlemen... this match has gone past the 20 minute time-limit... therefore it is considered a... DRAW! (Chorus of boos from crowd. Lanther looks around in shock.) MW: Correction folks! There was *no* countout! There was *no* victory for the SJPW here tonight! (Lanther looks angrily at the crowd who continue to boo.) JM: I think Lanther doesn't know what's going on! He thinks they're booing *him*! MW: That may be the case, and he doesn't look happy at all about it! This isn't the way we expected this bout to end, and it sure as Heck ain't the way that Lanther WANTS it to end. JM: No joke! I wanted to see Varga get a few more stiff ones in the mug! MW: Folks, this is Mike Williams signing off for Dr. Doom Josh Miller and telling you to join us next week on 'CWO Sunday Showcase'! (Scene fades out to a panning view of the arena as the CWO logo appears on screen.)
