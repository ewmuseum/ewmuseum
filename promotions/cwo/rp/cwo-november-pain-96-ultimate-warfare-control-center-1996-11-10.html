CWO NOVEMBER PAIN '96: ULTIMATE WARFARE CONTROL CENTER

10 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO NOVEMBER PAIN '96: ULTIMATE WARFARE graphic comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the second CONTROL CENTER for CWO NOVEMBER PAIN '96: ULTIMATE WARFARE. Teams of 4 competing in WARGAMES matches, the winners go on to a final two-ring battle royal, the winner of that getting a shot at the World champion at CWO CELEBRATION on December 15, 1996. Let's look at the almost complete line-up. First, on EYE ON THE STORM: NOVEMBER PAIN '96 - ULTIMATE WARFARE, we have this match: EYE ON THE STORM: Bad Guys, Sin, ??? vs. Gods of Thunder, Highlanders STARR: No news on who the fourth member will be for the Bad Guys and Sin. The Bad Guys recently attacked the Gods of Thunder at a house show, you can be certain those crazy Australians will be looking for revenge. Also, we see the return of the Highlanders, a popular CWO and CM2WF team, to a PPV event. Battle Cat, Mondo Gecko, Regulators vs. Ganglord, Sorrow, Eternal Wanderers STARR: Another showcase of newcomers to the CWO, with one wily veteran, Sorrow, thrown in there for good measure. Sandman Jr. Miguel Sanchez, "Big" Rich Clark, ??? vs. Maximum Overdrive, Baby Waldo, Mercenaries STARR: We've all seen what has transpired to lead up to this. Keep in mind that Sandman Jr. and Miguel Sanchez fought in a brutal stretcher match only weeks ago at TERROR NIGHT. Still, the attacks from Doomsday have been enough to drive these men to an alliance. However, who will their fourth man be? The Biblical Powers and Deadly Sins will be facing each other, so you can rule them out. We will have to wait and see what happens. Samoans, Biblical Powers vs. Deadly Sins, Unholy Duo STARR: Samoans and Deadly Sins have a big history, the Sins have been trying to destroy them ever since TERROR NIGHT. The Deadly Sins and Unholy Duo have a natural emnity with the Biblical Powers, go figure. Vincent "The Lip" Manocelli, Joe Eagle, The Big Cool vs. Gold Kard, Brent "The Ace" Alles, Pit Bosses STARR: They have said that they want to take the CWO down organization by organization, they finished off the Chaotic Dynasty, and now they are going after the Tower of Power, Gold Kard International looking for a win by them and a win by "Master of Pain" Barry Hart later in the night to "protect the corporate interests." Jackass Joel, Phantom Freightrain, Hoss and Little Joe vs. Steel, Panther, D. E. A. T. H. STARR: The men of Blood Inc. taking on the four men who recently formed the Confederate Associates with Johnny Reb, Mason-Dixon, and Ben Slocum. Inferno, Tiger Rogers, David and Goliath vs. Dark Raven, Hellhound, Creatures of the Night STARR: Inferno's long-running feud with Dark Raven became his long-running feud with the Dark Side once the latter joined that organization. Now, it is the Alliance vs. the Dark Side, and you can expect this one to be bloody. Flyboy, Clark Gray, Hollywood Muscle vs. Tub-O-Blub, Akillies, Kamikaze and Mysteria STARR: This will probably be the "friendliest" match of the night. Both teams have a healthy respect for each other, but you can be sure with the World Championship shot on the horizon, that friendship and respect will fly out the window. Sandman, Talon, Flame Spirits vs. Bane, ???, Apocalypse STARR: Who is the most intense force in the CWO, Sandman or Bane? That argument has been raging through the past months. However, now Sandman leads his team of hand-picked men against Bane's team of hand-picked men. We do not know who the fourth man on Bane's team will be, although Bane has hinted that Sandman and his partners should fear him even more than they do him. Hangman, Rooster, Mad Doctor's Ghost, Lafayette vs. Johnny Reb, Judgment, Southern Pride STARR: The "Gold Kard International Auxiliary Team" is going after the Confederate Associates, plus Judgment. Mad Doctor's Ghost is, of course, a card-carrying member of GKI, while Hangman, Rooster, and Lafayette are associates. Hangman and Rooster have both had their run-ins before with Johhny Reb, in face, Hangman hung Reb from a scaffolding once! Should be intense. So, there you have it, 10 WARGAMES matches, 40 men will advance into that final two-ring over the top rope battle royal, the winner to receive a shot at the World Championship at CWO CELEBRATION. However, there are two more matches at CWO NOVEMBER PAIN that might just overshadow all of the ULTIMATE WARFARE carnage, if you can believe that. TEXAS DEATH MATCH (winner gets a shot at the World title on the 12/3/96 TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER) Master of Darkness Damion vs. "Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum STARR: No need to do a big recap of the history between these two men. In May of this year, Damion and the Creatures of the Night dropped Sondra Slocum, Ben's sister and manager, from the rafters in a steel cage. Thankfully, they were just trying to scare her, and the cage just barely avoided hitting the ground. Slocum was not able to gain an amount of revenge at first, since Damion and the Creatures took off to the Orient, but when Damion and Slocum both returned to the revitalized CWO, the fire was lit again. Now, they will meet in a Texas Death match, where you have to pin your opponent, and then he has to meet a ten-count. The winner will receive a shot at the World Championship on the December 3rd TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER. And finally, the granddaddy of them all.... WORLD SERIES OF WRESTLING (4 out of 7 FALLS): Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo (champion) vs. "Master of Pain" Barry Hart STARR: Just imagine... 4 out of 7 falls. If you lose 3 in a row, you've got to win 3 in a row to even it up. A supreme test of endurance, and you're not going to see a rip-off here, there will be no disqualifications, no countouts, and no time limit. Also, Gold Kard, Hart's manager, and Brian Williams, Schillo's trainer, have been banned from ringside. The best man will truly win. Both men are skilled competitors, one embraces the fans, one turned his back on them. Truly a main event of epic proportions, and a show of epic proportions. You can't afford to miss CWO NOVEMBER PAIN '96: ULTIMATE WARFARE, so call your cable operator right now! This has been Johnny Starr with the CWO NOVEMBER PAIN CONTROL CENTER.
