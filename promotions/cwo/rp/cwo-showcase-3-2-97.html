CWO SHOWCASE 3/2/97

3 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWO SHOWCASE 3/2/97 Producer: Jim Longo ( [detail withheld] ) Executive Producer: Brent G. Alles ( [detail withheld] ) DARK MATCHES Bloodbath/Wildfire defeated Eternal Wanderers when Wildfire pinned Exile after the Firebawl. Time of match: 12:32. Highlanders defeated Sandman and Talon when Gavin McCloud pinned Talon after the Quickening. Time of match: 11:18. Pit Bosses defeated Chris Greco/Tommy Lightning when Pit Boss #1 pinned Chris Greco after the Spike Piledriver. Time of match: 13:34. (Montage of CWO action is shown as the theme for SHOWCASE plays. We go to a roaring crowd and our announcers, Kevin Rinaldi and Skip Vaughn.) Kevin Rinaldi: Hello, everybody, and welcome to CWO SHOWCASE!!! I'm Kevin Rinaldi, and with me former AAU champion Skip Vaughn! Skip Vaughn (Joe All-American. Perfect face, perfect smile, perfect body, perfect moron): Thank you, Kevin! Gosh, it's swell to be here!!! Kevin: I didn't think anyone still used gosh, but we've got to go to the ring for our next match--Johnny Reb and Sandman Junior!!! Let's take it to the ring!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with a twenty minute time limit!!! ("Sweet Home Alabama" by Lynyrd Skynyrd plays as the fans cheer) Coming down the aisle, weighing 237 pounds, from Mobile, Alabama and accompanied by General Lee, Johnny Reb!!!! ("Enter Sandman" by the Mighty Mighty Bosstones plays as the fans keep cheering) And his opponent, weighing 237 pounds, from Philadelphia, Pennsylvania and accompanied by Kellen, Sandman Junior!! Kevin: And Johnny Reb nails Sandman Junior with an enzuigiri right off the bat!!! Sandman Junior is dazed!!!! Reb stomping away on Sandman Junior!!! Going for a figure four....but Sandman Junior kicks him off!!! Sandman Junior with a dropkick!! Brings him to his feet.....but Reb nails him with an elbow!!! Reb scoots up top..... Alabama Jam!!! Cover!!! 1......kickout!!!! Skip: Gosh, this isn't like any wrestling I've ever seen! Kevin: You're just now figuring that out??? Reb sends Sandman Junior into the ropes......DDT!!! Brings him up.....sends him again....but this time Sandman Junior catches him with the elbow!!! SJ with a clothesline!!! Reb is down!!! SJ into the ropes.....and drops the elbow!!!! Sandman Junior snaps on the STF.....but Reb is reversing it!!!! Reb's got him!!!! Reb releases the hold....goes up top.....but MISSES the dropkick!!! Skip: Well, he should have stayed on his man, these jumping things aren't real wrestling, and they don't work half the time anyway!!! Kevin: Whatever.....SJ sends Reb into the ropes.....dropkick!!! Brings him up.... sends him into the ropes again....flying kneelift!!!! Cover!!! 1...2....Reb gets the shoulder up!!! Skip: I have to admit, though, the action is rather exciting! Kevin: SJ sends Reb into the ropes.....and connects with a big right hand!!! SJ with a flying headscissors!!! SJ's keeping the hold on....but Reb escapes!!! Reb with a huge chop!!! Scoops SJ up.......running powerslam!!! Cover!!! 1....Sandman Junior kicks out!!! Skip: Golly.....what kind of condition are these two in to survive something like that??? Kevin: The CWO's wrestlers are the best conditioned in the world, Skip!!! Reb nails SJ with a headbutt!!! Sends SJ into the ropes....double axe handle!!! Reb executes a beautiful German suplex!!!! And the crowd loving it!!! Skip: Shouldn't he be wrestling right now, instead of waving to the fans? The other guy's getting up..... Kevin: Sandman Junior indeed on his feet!!! Sandman Junior taps Johnny Reb on the shoulder....and drives the elbow into the face!!!! Another elbow sends Reb to the canvas!!! Sandman Junior running into the ropes.....Permanent Dream!!! Cover!!! 1....2....kickout by Reb!!! Skip: And this Sandman Junior doesn't look too pleased!!! Kevin: Reb catches SJ with a right hand!!! Sends him into the ropes....clothesline!!! Reb snaps on the Texas Cloverleaf!!!!! SJ trying to reach the ropes.....his face is contorted in agony!!! The crowd screaming at him to escape....he's nodding his head....and the ref's calling for the bell!!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, after 13 minutes and 30 seconds of action, your winner by submission is.....JOHNNY REB!!!!!!!! Kevin: And Sandman Junior insisting that he didn't submit!!! He must have been responding to the fans!!! But it goes in the record books as a win for Johnny Reb! The two men shaking hands...and we'll be back after this!!! COMMERCIAL BREAK Kevin: And we're back with our next match!!! The Rude Guy and the Focus!!! Let's go to the ring!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. ("Milque Toast" by Helmet plays as the fans boo) Coming down the aisle, weighing 225 pounds and hailing from parts unknown, the Focus!! (the Bad Guys' theme music plays as the fans keep booing) And his opponent, weighing in at 238 pounds, from Attica, New York and accompanied by Herbie Versmells, he is one half of the North American Tag Team Champions, Ron "The Rude Guy" Johnson!!! Kevin: From two men who exemplify the best of the CWO to two who represent the lowest! The Focus nails Johnson with a forearm!!!! The Focus goes for a small package...but Johnson reverses!!! 1....the Focus kicks out!!!! Skip: Actually, not bad wrestling from either of these men! Kevin: Give it time!!! Johnson with a figure four......Focus reaches the ropes!!! Johnson going up top.....but MISSES the double stomp!!! Full nelson by Focus!!! Johnson trying to power out.........but Focus ducks behind him for an inside cradle!! Cover!!! 1....2.....Johnson kicks out!!! Skip: If Johnson loses, does the other guy get to wrestle for the tag team title? Kevin: No, but it would make a nice scenario, wouldn't it? Focus nails Johnson with a dropkick.....going for a half Boston....but Johnson nails him with a boot to the gut!!! Johnson with a flying headscissors!!! Going for another one......no dice!!! Focus with a backslide!!!! 1....2....Johnson gets the shoulder up!!! Skip: I still haven't seen any dirty stuff from either of these two! Kevin: They may be having an off night!!! Focus rolls Johnson into a small package! Skip: Is he allowed to hold onto Johnson's trunks like that? Kevin: No!!! 1...Johnson kicks out!!!! Skip: He cheated?????? Kevin: Welcome to the CWO, Skip!!! Focus sends Johnson into the ropes...backdrop!!! Brings him up....sends him into the ropes again........reverse neckbreaker!!!! Cover!! Skip: He's pulling the shorts again!!!! Kevin: 1.....kick out!!!! Skip: I don't believe this!!! Why doesn't the referee do something? Kevin: Go up and ask him!!! Johnson with a flying headscissors!!!! Going up top.... flying double stomp!!! And Johnson snaps on the figure four!!!! The Focus trying to escape the hold, but Johnson's got it on good and tight!!! Johnson releases the hold... going up top....another flying stomp!!! Brings the Focus up....sends him into the corner.....handspring avalanche!!! Going back.....but this time the Focus moves!!! The Focus nails Johnson with an elbow!!!! Dropkick by the Focus!!!! Skip: What's that little guy doing? Kevin: Herbie Versmells is on the ring apron!!! The Focus leaving Johnson on the mat and going after the manager!!! Focus has Versmells by the collar!!! Johnson getting on his feet!!! He charges.....but the Focus moves and Johnson runs into his own manager!!! Skip: These guys ARE cheating!!! Kevin: Finally, he wakes up.....Focus sets Johnson up for a powerbomb....he got it!!! Johnson is hurt!!!! The Focus brings him up top......and nails him with the Focal Point!!! Cover!!!!! 1...2....3!!!!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner, in 14 minutes and 22 seconds, the FOCUS!!! Kevin: The newcomer takes out the veteran!!! And we'll be back!!! COMMERCIAL BREAK Kevin: And we're back with our final matchup, for the CWO Light Heavyweight Championship! Let's go to the ring!!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the next contest is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit! It is for the CWO Light Heavyweight Championship!!! ("Hip Hop Hooray" by Naughty by Nature plays as the fans cheer) Coming down the aisle, the challenger, weighing 222 pounds, from Oakland, California, Mondo Gecko!!!! ("Unforgiven" by Metallica plays as the fans boo) And his opponent, weighing 240 pounds, hailing from Warner Robbins, Georgia and accompanied by Jimbo Jones, he is the CWO Light Heavyweight Champion.....Tiger Rogers!!!!! Kevin: And Gecko attacks Rogers as he steps through the ropes!!!! Gecko with a flying headscissors!!!! Gecko scooting up top....flying elbow drop!!!! Cover!!! 1......Rogers kicks out!!!! Skip: He wants that belt, Kevin!!! Go Gecko!!! Kevin: What happened to "gosh golly, this isn't the way I wrestle?" Skip: It's the CWO, Kevin!!! It's infectious!!!! Kevin: Gecko sends Rogers into the ropes....he runs the other way.....but Jimbo Jones trips him!!! And of course, the ref didn't see it!!! Rogers stomping on Gecko!!!! Brings him up... sends him into the ropes...double axe handle! Cover!! 1...2...Gecko gets the shoulder up!!!! Rogers swings a left, but Gecko ducks!!! Gecko sends Rogers into the ropes.....and nails him with a chop across the throat! Cover!!!! 1..2...Rogers gets HIS shoulder up!!! Skip: This is the best action in the world!!!! The UFC has nothing on us!!! Kevin: Not too loud, Skip, we might get sued!!! Gecko with a cross body block!! Going up top....flying legdrop!!! Cover!!!! 1....2...Rogers kicks out!!! Gecko going up top again.....swan dive!!!! Skip: Hey, who's that? Kevin: It's Masher Maccabee!!! What's he doing here? Gecko with another swan dive!!! Cover!!!!! 1....2...Rogers again manages to avoid the pin!!! Skip: What's he carrying? Kevin: It's the CWO Television Title belt.....Maccabee's the current champion! Gecko with a hard elbow to Rogers' chest!!!! And another!!!!! Jimbo Jones is on the ring apron!!! Gecko attacks Jones....but Rogers nails Gecko from behind! Kneelift by Rogers!!! Cover...and Jones is holding the challenger's legs!!! 1.....the ref catches him!!! Skip: He should banish that slimeball from ringside!! Kevin: No argument here!!!! Rogers sends Gecko into the ropes....Tiger Spin Kick!!!! Rogers drops the elbow!!! Going up top...moonsault!!!! Rogers brings Gecko up....and sends him out of the ring!!!! Skip: What's that Masher guy doing????? Kevin: Masher Maccabee nails Gecko with the TV title belt!!! Gecko is out cold!!! The ref was arguing with Rogers and Jones, and didn't see it!!! Maccabee rolls Gecko back into the ring!!!! Cover!!! 1......2.....3!!!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner, in 10 minutes and 57 seconds, the CWO Light Heavyweight Champion, TIGER ROGERSSSSSSSSSS!!!!! Kevin: And Tiger Rogers pulls another one out from under a rock!!! And we're out of time!!! For Skip Vaughn, I'm Kevin Rinaldi saying, good night, everybody!!!!! (The closing credits begin to roll as we see highlights from the night's matches.)
