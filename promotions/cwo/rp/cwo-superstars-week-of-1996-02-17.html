CWO Superstars (week of 2/14/96-2/21/96)

17 February 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWO SUPERSTARS (week of 2/14/96 - 2/21/96) WINCE MCMAMM: Good afternoon, everybody, and welcome to CWO Superstars. We've got a couple of matches for you today, as well as information about next week's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER. Unfortunately, joining me as always is my broadcast partner, Ricky "Heartbreaker" Kidd. RICKY KIDD: Hey, watch your mouth, McMamm! If it wasn't for me you'd lose half you viewership! That is, of course, the ladies! WINCE MCMAMM: Well, let's get to our first match. TONY KWIATKOWSKI (ring announcer): Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall. Currently in the ring, from Nutbush, TN, weighing in at 228 lbs., Mark Worker! (Some boos as Mark holds his hands up high.) TONY K: And his opponent... (Rebel Yell's theme music starts up) Weighing in at 265 lbs., and accompanied by his manager Ruben Zuffelt, the current CWO World Champion, Rebel Yell! (Huge pop by the crowd.) WINCE MCMAMM: All right! The NEW CWO World Champion joining us this week on superstars. RICKY KIDD: Yeah, but when he faces Sandman at next week's Tuesday Night Fever in a rematch for the title, he won't be so lucky. WINCE MCMAMM: That remains to be seen. Anyway, Rebel Yell and Mark Worker lock up. RICKY KIDD: I haven't heard a lot about this idiot Mark Worker, have you McMamm? WINCE MCMAMM: Well, he's a young man just starting out in the sport, some good athletic skills... and, oh my goodness! Rebel Yell just kicked him in the head! Now he's locking on a chinlock deathlock submission! Worker screaming in pain! Worker manages to get to the ropes after a few seconds... Rebel Yell kicks him in the thigh! Worker crumples to the mat. Here we go... Rebel Roll Up! 1... 2... 3! RICKY KIDD: Solid athletic skills, eh, McMamm? WINCE MCMAMM: Well, he is in there with the World Champion, and Rebel Yell making short work of Mark Worker here on Superstars this week. TONY K: The winner of the match, in 42 seconds, the CWO World Champion, Rebel Yell! (Crowd cheers.) WINCE MCMAMM: All right, let's go to Johnny Starr in the ring. JOHNNY STARR: All right, Wince, I'm here with a man who has to be at the happiest point in his life right now, Rebel Yell. How are you doing, champ? REBEL YELL: AH FEEL GRRRRREAT! (Fans cheer.) You know, Johnny, all this time that ah've been in the CWO, ah've been telling everyone that I would be World Champion, and nobody believed me, but now ah've got the belt, and things are sweet as a summer day in Georgia! JOHNNY STARR: We're all happy for you, champ. But, as you know, Lord Foul and Sandman, the leaders of the Chaotic Dynasty, have pinpointed you for destruction. RUBEN ZUFFELT (clearing his throat): Well, I'm currently trying to make a deal with those gentlemen... REBEL YELL: Ruben, what the hell are you talkin' about? We ain't makin' no deals with them scalywags. Let me tell you, Johnny, ol' Sandy's never bothered me before, and he's not gonna bother me again. And Lord Foul, didn't ah beat that big dumb Brownsville Bomber of yours enough to convince you not to mess with me? Shucks. JOHNNY STARR: Well, anyway, what are your thoughts about the rematch for the title against Sandman at next week's Tuesday Night Fever? REBEL YELL: Well, ah cain't say ah'm too happy about it. Commissioner Santana, what is this, open week on Rebel Yell? But ah'm a fighting champion, Johnny. I'll take on all of those durn Chaotic Dynastys m'self, if I have too. Now, ah respect Sandman, he's a tough S. O. B., but I've come too far and worked too hard to see this belt fade away. JOHNNY STARR: All right, thank you very much, champ. REBEL YELL: Thank you, Johnny. (Fans cheer as Johnny walks away.) ** COMMERCIAL BREAK ** WINCE MCMAMM: All right, a very good interview with the CWO World Champ, Rebel Yell. RICKY KIDD: Yeah, but did you see how Ruben and him are STILL not getting along? There's still trouble in the Confederate's Camp. WINCE MCMAMM: Well, that remains to be seen, and in fact, we've got two more of the Confederate Associates coming up! TONY K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following tag-team matchup is scheduled for one fall. Currently in the ring, at a combined weigh of 510 lbs., the Ho Hos! (Boos by the fans at the rather rotund jobbers.) TONY K: And their opponents, (Confederate Associates music starts up), at a combined weight of 601 lbs., and accompanied by their manager, Ruben Zuffelt, they are the CWO World Tag Team Champions, the team of Col. Braxton Morris and Fu Man Chu, the Southern Wonders. WINCE MCMAMM: All right, the World Champs taking on the Ho Hos. Oh, look at that! Col. Braxton Morris attacking Ho Ho #1 before the bell! They're all business in the ring! Flying cross body press by the Colonol... 1... 2... kickout. Morris goes for a jumping elbow thrust, but Ho Ho #1 ducks. RICKY KIDD: Don't underestimate the Ho Hos, McMamm. Here comes #2 in the ring now, double Hotshot by the Ho-Hos! A double front-layout suplex by the Ho-Hos! I would laugh so hard, McMamm, if the Southern Blunders got beat by these doofs. WINCE MCMAMM: Not much chance of that, but Ho Ho #1 with a DDT and a splash, 1... 2... kickout. Ho Ho #1 goes for an inverted atomic drop... but Morris blocks it. Chu enters the ring now... he kicks Ho Ho #1 in the head... inverted power bomb by Morris! Beautiful move! Now they whip Ho Ho #1 into the ropes... double fist to midsection. Morris with a spinning leg lariat... and now the Double Frontbreaker! Forget about it! Morris makes the pin... 1... 2... 3! TONY K: The winners of the match, at 1:33, the Southern Wonders! WINCE MCMAMM: Southern Wonders showing their stuff, but with the immense tag team competition in the CWO, you have to wonder how long they can keep the belts. ** COMMERCIAL BREAK ** TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER CONTROL CENTER JOHNNY STARR: Hi folks, Johnny Starr here with your TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER control center. We have eleven exciting matches coming up on this weeks TNF, and here's the rundown. WOMEN'S 6-PERSON ACTION: Li'l Cheetah, "Bodacious" Janet Muniz, and Sweet Sue (w/Tall Troll) vs. Vegas Showgirl, Lady of Rage, and Southside Sherona (w/Gold Kard AND Ruben Zuffelt... sheesh) The Hunter vs. Cross-Cut Soldiers of Darkness (Hellbound/Griffen) vs. Zeus & Ric Flair Marc "Compton" Daniels vs. Johnny Reb (w/General Lee) 6-MAN ACTION: Midnight Rockers (Colt, Flight, Thunder) vs. Creatures of the Night (Night Slinger/Night Crawler) and Master of Darkness Damion Lord Foul the Despiser vs. Sting Jade Dragon Force (Hasa Osaki & Kyoto Kasanori w/Jim Cornette) vs. Too Cold Scorpio and Jushin "Thunder" Liger Harley Davidson vs. Bob Backlund CWO TV TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Homophobia (Basher & Slasher, champs, w/Ruben Zuffelt) vs. The Freezing Point (Brad "Ice" Cold and Johnny "Deep" Freeze w/Leo "Hollywood" Caron) CWO TV CHAMPIONSHIP: Entombed Crusher (champ) vs. Gold Kard (w/Vegas Showgirl) CWO WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (rematch from St. Valentine's Day Massacre): Rebel Yell (champ, w/Ruben Zuffelt) vs. Sandman (w/Lord Foul) JOHNNY STARR: All right, key attention should be paid to that Harley Davidson-Bob Backlund matchup. Mr. Backlund has gone on record by saying that he doesn't have a lot of liking for Mr. Davidson. BOB BACKLUND CLIP: Harley Davidson, I am flabbergasted and appalled by your lack of genteelry. You wear dirty and masticated clothes to the ring, and I do not have the tolerance for it any more. I will show you the proper way to behave in the ring! JOHNNY STARR: Also at the Fever, the match between the Japanese sensations Jade Dragon Force vs. a sensation in his own right, Jushin Thunder Liger and his partner Too Cold Scorpio! Manager Jim Cornette, of course, has some things to say about this. JIM CORNETTE (Jade Dragon Force are silent in the background wearing eerie masks): Too Old Scorpio and Pushin' Blunder Liger, you may wonder why mah boys have been scoutin' you out. Well, it's because they're studiers of the craft, master torturers, and they need to find a way to convince Chico Santana that they deserve a shot at one of the tag championships. So, Liger and Scorpio, you're gonna be our steppin' stones. How does that feel? Ha ha ha. JOHNNY STARR: All right, and also, that big rematch between Sandman and Rebel Yell! SANDMAN CLIP (w/LORD FOUL): Rebel Yell! You piece of Southern Trash! Consider yourself lucky, boy, cuz you had your one night! As you might say while sitting on your porch picking fleas off your neighbor, "The sun even shines on a dog's @$$ one fine day!" But come this Tuesday night, I will regain the belt and become the 3-time CWO World Champion! JOHNNY STARR: That's it for now, folks, we'll see you Tuesday night!
