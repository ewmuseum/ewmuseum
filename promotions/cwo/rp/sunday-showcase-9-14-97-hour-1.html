SUNDAY SHOWCASE 9/14/97 - HOUR #1

CWO SUNDAY SHOWCASE - HOUR #1 9/14/97 Producer: Chris O'Brien ( [detail withheld] ) Executive Producer: Brent G. Alles ( [detail withheld] ) (Fade into a spinning globe, as the CWO logo is slowly superimposed on it.) VOICE-OVER: The CWO... providing wrestling excellence the world over.... ('Halls of Illusion' by Insane Clown Posse plays as a montage of shots fill the screen... Rich Clark with a chokeslam on Joe Eagle... Brent Alles hits an Allesbreaker on Wayne Schillo... the Newton Brothers with a pair of Newton Bombs on Pyrotechnics... the Reverend locks the Agony on Southern Dynamite... Chris Myers lands the Rattler on Gold Kard... and Johnny Reb holds the CWO Heavyweight title high in the air.) COVENANT: THIS IS SUNDAY SHOWCASE! (The camera switches to a shot of a jam packed CWO Arena. Fans everywhere are waving signs such as VARGA IS EAGLE'S BITCH!, USE MY SIGN, JR.!, and HILE TROY, MEET THE ALLESBREAKER. We go to the announcer's booth, where Chris O'Brien and Jerry Covenant. Chris is wearing a Los Angeles Dodgers baseball jersey and silver sunglasses, and his hair is rumpled. Jerry, as always, is in his white turtleneck and CWO sweater.) COVENANT: Welcome, fans, to CWO SUNDAY SHOWCASE! O'BRIEN: ACK! Don't yell, Jerry. COVENANT: Why not? You deserve it! O'BRIEN: Look.... COVENANT: Fans, I have to apologize for my broadcast partner. It seems he got extremely drunk last night, and ALMOST caused us to be preempted... again.... O'BRIEN: Look, Covenant, with all due respect, keep your voice down, and let me explain.... COVENANT: While Chris tries to talk his way out of a reprimand, let's go to Chad Johnson for the intros for our first match here on Sunday Showcase! JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the first matchup is set for one fall, with a 20 minute time limit. Coming down the aisle... ('Wigglestick' plays as the fans boo.) JOHNSON: ...from Nashville, TN, weighing 210 pounds, here is 'Gigolo' Jimmy Heat! O'BRIEN: AGH! Do they have to play it so loud? COVENANT: Heat making his way to the ring here, one of CWO's light heavyweights, and at least SOME of the ladies here are swooning over him. O'BRIEN: Who is he facing? I kind of forget. Seven gin martinis tend to do that to you. Let me see here... oh, no! COVENANT: What? Chris covering his ears... JOHNSON: And his opponent.... ('American Nightmare' by White Zombie BLARES over the PA as the fans cheer loudly.) JOHNSON: From Grandville, MI, weighing 289 pounds, here is Brent 'The Ace' Alles! (Alles makes his way down to the ring.) O'BRIEN: Why do they have to play that so fraggin' LOUD! It figures... the one time I get trashed in this town is the one time Syracuse University's favorite son has to be wrestling! COVENANT: Wrestling standout at Syracuse... so, why, Chris did you get drunk last night? O'BRIEN: I'll tell you during the next matchup, Jerry. It ties in. Anyway, the bell rings, and as it reverbs in my head, a collar and elbow lockup, and Alles with a headlock applied. COVENANT: I can't wait to hear that story, as Heat counters by pushing Brent Alles to the ropes, and now Alles goes bounding across the ring, and HEAT CATCHES HIM WITH A SLINGSHOT FACEBUSTER! O'BRIEN: MY HEAD! Why must you yell, Jerry? COVENANT: It was a nice move, Chris. And now Jimmy Heat picks up Brent Alles, but Alles responds with a loud chop, and down goes Heat! O'BRIEN: Alles stomping away now, and I swear, these stomps seem really loud today. COVENANT: Your imagination, Chris. Alles off the ropes now... he goes for an elbowdrop, but Heat rolls out of the way! Alles back up, and he counters an attempted armdrag takedown with an arm wrencher! O'BRIEN: Alles saved me from hearing 210 pounds slamming into the mat, and now Heat reaches the ropes, and now 'The Ace' with a slingshot suplex, and Heat comes down with authority. Thanks, Brent. COVENANT: Brent Alles is not wrestling to save you from a pounding headache, Chris. O'BRIEN: I can wish, can't I? Alles follows Heat outside, and he grabs 'Gigolo' from behind...oh, please don't.....AGHHH! Alles just whipped Jimmy Heat into the ringside steps, and it's ringing in my head! COVENANT: Chris and Heat holding their heads as Alles rolls his opponent back in the ring...Alles goes for an elbowdrop, but Heat moves! And now he wastes time by swiveling his hips at Alles. HEAT (To Alles): I SWEAR, WHAT DOES AMANDA SEE IN YOU? O'BRIEN: That was not a good thing to say, as Alles swoops in with a double axehandle on Jimmy Heat! And now a headlock attempt, but Heat blocks with a punch. COVENANT: Maybe Lord Hile Troy paid Jimmy Heat to taunt Alles, get him mad. And now Jimmy Heat with a kick to the knee, and Alles is down! Heat now going up top... FLYING SPINNING LEG LARIAT! O'BRIEN: Covenant.... COVENANT: HEAT GOES FOR A PIN! 1... 2... thr... no, the ref sees Heat using the ropes for leverage! O'BRIEN: If you won't stop screaming, at least scream only at the cool stuff? COVEANT: Heat now with a dropkick on Brent Alles! Cover, and Heat pulls the tights! 1... 2... the ref sees it again! O'BRIEN: We've got a good ref in there tonight. And now Heat pulls Alles up after jawing with the referee... backspin DDT! Now that was something you could of screamed about, Covenant. COVENANT: Heat up top again, he's a high flying lightweight... somersault headbutt is missed as Brent Alles rolls out of the way! O'BRIEN: And Alles snaps on an arm wrencher! Heat grimaces under the pain as one of this fed's top notch technicians works that shoulder over.... COVENANT: Alles pushed to the ropes by 'Gigolo', and Alles is Irish whipped across the ring! Heat with a clothesline! O'BRIEN: Jimmy Heat going up top again...he misses an Asai moonsault! Alles is countering every attempt by Jimmy Heat to go airborne! And now Alles goes for the pin! 1... 2... shoulder up! COVENANT: And both men back up... 'The Ace' dodges a dropkick, and now an elbowsmash! Heat comes back with a pair of his own! And Alles goes outside... HEAT WITH A PLANCHA OVER THE TOP ROPE, AND DOWN GOES ALLES! O'BRIEN: I'm not going to say anything, but Heat is clearing off the timekeeper's table. and he's putting Brent Alles on it! Heat on the apron... ALLES MOVES, AND HEAT GOES THROUGH THE TABLE! COVENANT: I guess Chris' headache is gone... Heat is rolled back into the ring by Alles, and now he's going in... oh, wait, a fan has jumped the railing... IT'S HILE TROY! LORD HILE TROY FROM BEHIND! O'BRIEN: Alles is jumped, and Troy with a hold... BELLY-TO-BACK SUPLEX! The ref is checking on Jimmy Heat, and doesn't see Troy! And now what... Troy's got a whiskey flask, and now he's pouring whiskey all over Alles! COVENANT: Troy stooping to a new low, and now he throws Alles in the ring... Jimmy Heat grabs him and puts him on the top turnbuckle! We all know what this is... JIMMYPLEX! Top rope Fisherman suplex with a Slop Drop landing, and this one is over! 1... 2... 3. JOHNSON: The winner of the contest, by pinfall, at 7:05, 'Gigolo' Jimmy Heat! O'BRIEN: Hile Troy now pounding away on Alles, and Heat jumps into! Both men are not letting up, and Hile Troy with a Boston Crab! And Heat is up top....legdrop onto Alles' head! COVENANT: Fans, we're going to take a break and spare you of this, we'll be back... wait a minute... here comes... the Deadly Sins?!? (The Deadly Sins Wrath, Pride, and Sloth hit the ring as the fans react.) O'BRIEN: I don't believe this, they're helping Alles? COVENANT: I don't know if they're helping Alles so much as they're trying to get to "Gigolo" Jimmy, Heat and the Coldfront set to meet the Sins at SEPTEMBER SLAM II, but Heat and Troy are now quickly beating a fast retreat. We do have to go to a commercial, we'll be right back. (Fade to commercial. When we come back, Chad Johnson is standing in the ring.) JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the next matchup is a tag team match set for one fall! Coming to the ring.... ('Thunderkiss 69' by White Zombie plays as the fans boo.) JOHNSON: ...at a combined weight of 625 pounds, here is the tag team of X-Factor and Marc Mission! COVENANT: The highly unorthodox tag team taking on one of CWO's oldest teams. Fans, during the break, security escorted Jimmy Heat and Hile Troy from the building, preventing any further attack by the Deadly Sins. O'BRIEN: Alles walked out under his own power, and you know he is TICKED for the 28th. JOHNSON: And their opponents... ('Sweet Dreams' by Marilyn Manson plays as the fans boo.) JOHNSON: ...weighing a combined 761 pounds, accompanied by Lust, here are Pride and Sloth, they are THE DEADLY SINS! COVENANT: So, Chris, what's the story behind you being hungover this morning? O'BRIEN: Well, I was cruising around last night, trying to find a nice place to drink, when I run into Sloth out in a bar. So, we talked, and we kind of had a contest.... COVENANT: YOU tried to outdrink Sloth? O'BRIEN: Stupid, I know. COVENANT: What was the prize? O'BRIEN: 2 hours with Lust. COVENANT: And if he won? O'BRIEN: No, I just got 2 hours with Lust. Hell, Jerry, the reason I was late getting here wasn't because I was drunk, I just couldn't get out of the damn handcuffs! COVENANT: And some information I did NOT want to know... X-Factor starting off against Sloth... Sloth turns for some last minute advice from his partner, and X-Factor from behind with a double axehandle! And now a legdrop! X-FACTOR (To Sloth): COME ON, FAT BOY! O'BRIEN: Lust looking good today.... COVENANT: I do not want to hear any stories from you, Chris. O'BRIEN: Hell, all I can remember, Jerry, is that I want more... Sloth nails X-Factor with an elbow smash, and a tag to Pride, who comes in with a double axehandle of his own! And X-Factor goes outside the ring! COVENANT: Here comes Lust! And she NAILS him with her whip! And now Lust rolls X-Factor back in the ring, where Pride applies a half Boston. O'BRIEN: X-Factor under pressure as Pride cinches in that half Boston....he manages to reach the ropes, and Pride releases after a five count from the ref. A tag to Sloth, who comes in and goes for the pin! 1... 2... thr... shoulder up! And now a tag to Pride, and both men proceed to beat the hell out of X-Factor. COVENANT: X-Factor needs to make a tag here... O'BRIEN: Well, DUH! COVENANT: ..as Pride goes for a Code Red, but X-Factor manages to avoid it, and now he's on his feet, and he slaps Pride. X-FACTOR: (To Pride): YOU AIN'T THAT GOOD! O'BRIEN: And now X-Factor ducks under Pride and tags in Marc Mission, who whips Pride into a corner! Pride bounces out....DDT! And now Pride rolls outside the ring, and Marc Mission follows him! Wild brawl between the two men! Mission tries to whip Pride into the guardrail, but Pride reverses, and Mission goes slamming into the rail! COVENANT: You seem to be back to normal, Chris, as Pride goes for a cover! One... two... shoulder up! Tag to Sloth, who does a double whip with his partner... Marc Mission ducks a double clothesline, and comes off the ropes with one of his own! And now he picks up Sloth and goes for a whip to the corner, Sloth reverses, and Mission goes into the corner! O'BRIEN: Mission comes out, and Sloth comes off the ropes... X-FACTOR WITH A CHOP TO THE BACK OF SLOTH, AND MISSION WITH A VERTICAL SUPLEX! He just took over the big man! Pin, 1... kick out! Sloth is up, and now he nails Mission with a double axehandle! Mission comes back with a dropkick, and Sloth goes down! COVENANT: Mission with a knee to the head! (Marc Mission plays to the crowd.) COVENANT: And now Marc Mission off the ropes... legdrop! (Marc Mission plays to the crowd.) O'BRIEN: Wasting time... Sloth with a punch from behind! And now a set of forearm smashes! Mission comes back with a flurry of punches! Sloth is reeling... he retaliates with a kick to the gut of Marc Mission! And a tag to Pride, who goes for a tumble weed.... COVENANT: ...Mission rolls out of the way! And now he's got the legs of Pride... FIGURE FOUR! Figure four leglock, and Pride is screaming! O'BRIEN: Mission's got it in, but I don't think Pride's going to submit. COVENANT: No joke, Pride's pride won't let him give... he's reaching for the ropes... Lust grabs his hands, and he is going for a reversal... Lust and Pride are almost there... THEY REVERSE! Mission lets go, and he's in pain! A tag to Sloth, and Pride's got Mission by the hair! O'BRIEN: Pride's putting Mission on the turnbuckle, and he's going to be paying for his sins.... COVENANT: PRIDE WITH A SUPERPLEX, AND SLOTH FROM THE OTHER SIDE WITH A MOONSAULT! REPENTION! Cover by Sloth, one, two, three! JOHNSON: At a time of 8:21, your winners, by pinfall... the Deadly Sins! O'BRIEN: Sins with a win, and I'm sure they're going to celebrate, and I am NOT going to be a part of it tonight! COVENANT: Skyhawk vs. Sandman is up next, don't go away! (Fade to commercial. When we come back, Chad Johnson is standing in the ring.) JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the next contest is set for one fall, with a 20 minute time limit! ('Gods of War' by Def Leppard plays as the fans cheer.) JOHNSON: Coming down the aisle, weighing 290 pounds, from Philadelphia, PA, here is Skyhawk! COVENANT: Proud member of the Unit coming to the ring, and he's out to make a name of himself here in CWO, like in so many other feds around the world.... O'BRIEN: OH MY GOD! Sandman from behind! He NAILS Skyhawk with an axehandle, and now he hurls Skyhawk into the ring! COVENANT: Sandman going for a quick win, as the bell rings with Sandman whipping Skyhawk to the ropes, and going for a backdrop... sunset flip! Sandman kicks out after a one count, and now Skyhawk bodyslams Sandman! Cover, 1... 2... thr... shoulder up! O'BRIEN: Skyhawk bouncing back after that sneak attack by Sandman, and now Sandman bounces up with that amazing resiliency and comes off the ropes, but Skyhawk with a legsweep, and down goes the big man! COVENANT: Skyhawk follows up with a legdrop! Cover again, 1... 2... thr... shoulder up! And now a DDT attempt by the Unit member, but Sandman avoids it, and now he goes for a sleeper! O'BRIEN: And this time Skyhawk avoids easily, and NAILS Sandman with a punch right to the lower abdominal area! COVENANT: Sandman outside the ring now, and Skyhawk goes to kick him on the apron....Sandman grabs his leg and SLAMS it into the ring post! Nice reaction, and now Sandman back in the ring, wailing on Skyhawk. O'BRIEN: Skyhawk could really use Johnny B. Goode right now, who is still in the hospital after that vicious attack two weeks ago. Sandman now coming off the ropes... SKYHAWK CATCHES HIM WITH A POWERSLAM! COVENANT: The Unit knows their wrestling, and Skyhawk is no exception. He pulls Sandman up, and he Irish whips Sandman... no, reversal... DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE! Both men catch each other! O'BRIEN: The two men are down, and Skyhawk is on his feet first, going for a reverse chinlock, but Sandman counters with a Greco-Roman punch to the face, and now he goes for a reverse chinlock of his own, but Skyhawk counters! COVENANT: A wrestling match here, move and countermove, hold and counterhold, as Skyhawk is slammed into the corner by Sandman, and now an Avalanche! And now Sandman trying to end it with a Sandblast! He's got Skyhawk up top... HE NAILS THE SANDBLAST! O'BRIEN: Those black hourglass eyes freak me out, Jerry! Cover, 1, 2, thr... NO! Skyhawk kicks out of the Sandblast! I don't believe it! COVENANT: Sandman not showing any concern, as he hits an atomic drop! And now he whips Skyhawk into a corner... a chop! Skyhawk comes back with a double axehandle, and now a sunset flip from the corner! 1... 2... kick out! O'BRIEN: Skyhawk with a bodyslam... check that, Sandman blocks, and now HE picks up Skyhawk... POWERSLAM! COVENANT: Skyhawk gets back up... he nails Sandman in the face, and now another attempted Irish whip, and AGAIN, Sandman reverses, and Sandman charges in... SKYHAWK WITH A FOREARM SMASH! And now Skyhawk up top... he tries to connect with a flying knee, but Sandman moves! O'BRIEN: Both men trade blows now, a flurry of punches... Skyhawk with the upper hand, and he kicks Sandman in the midsection! He goes up top now... going for the Skydive.....HE HITS! One, two... Sandman now kicks out! Both men have kicked out of each other's specialty moves! COVENANT: Skyhawk now FINALLY whips Sandman into the corner... Sandman bounces out, and Skyhawk with a good old fashioned headbutt! O'BRIEN: Don't see that anymore, and now Skyhawk with a backbreaker! He's strong for his size, and now Skyhawk off the ropes... Sandman with a clothesline! He had enough presence to do that, and now Sandman whips Skyhawk to the ropes... SKYHAWK BLOCKS A BACK DROP AND JACK-KNIFES SANDMAN! Cover, one, two, kickout! COVENANT: Skyhawk... ASAI MOONSAULT! Sandman is down! Somersault splash now, and Skyhawk covers! O'BRIEN: What the... IT'S THE CABIN! IT'S JUDGEMENT, EXODUS, AND THE GHOST RIDERS! AND THEY'RE BEATING ON SKYHAWK! COVENANT: There's the bell.... JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner, via DQ, at 11:21... SKYHAWK! COVENANT: The Cabin laying into Skyhawk... THEY SCATTER! SEAN HOLIDAY AND THE NEWTON BROTHERS COME FLYING TO THE RING! O'BRIEN: Sandman leaving with the Cabin... if he's with the Cabin, is the Reverend too? COVENANT: We may be jumping to conclusions as the Unit dares the Cabin to step in the ring... Skyhawk is up and around, fortunately... fans, that's it for hour #1, stay with us for more action in hour #2! END HOUR #1
