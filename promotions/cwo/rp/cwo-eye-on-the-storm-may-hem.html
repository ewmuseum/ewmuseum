CWO EYE ON THE STORM: MAY-HEM

4 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EYE ON THE STORM: MAY-HEM 5/4/97 "LIVE" FROM THE MOLSON CENTRE, MONTREAL, QUEBEC, CANADA (EYE ON THE STORM opening comes up as we go to a roaring crowd in Montreal, joining our announcing team Kyle Melinn, Joe Root, and David "The Truth" Thomas as fireworks go off in the background.) MELINN: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the EYE ON THE STORM for MAY-HEM, I'm Kyle Melinn, and we are almost ready to begin what has quickly become one of the most talked about PPV's in a long time here in the CWO. With me are my broadcasting partners Joe Root and David "The Truth" Thomas. DAVID "THE TRUTH" THOMAS: Oh, boy, does Gold Kard International have a surprise for everybody tonight! MELINN: What surprise is that? THOMAS: Like I'm going to tell you! JOE ROOT: Besides the big 60-man battle royal, of course all eyes are on the World championship match between the three monsters of the ring, 7'4" "Monster" Rich Clark, 7' Goliath, and of course the heartless, merciless, soulless Bane. MELINN: That only one of the great 5 title defenses we have on MAY-HEM tonight, but we are ready for our matchup here on this program, it is a lottery tag team match, two randomly drawn teams of two fighting for the numbers 59 and 60 slots in MAY-HEM, the losers of course getting slots 1 and 2. Let's go to the ring! (Tony the K is in the ring along with referee Gary Alles.) TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, our opening contest is a lottery tag team match scheduled for one fall with no time limit. Introducing first, coming down the aisle.... ("Bow Down" by Westside Connection plays as the fans boo the entrance of Nate.) TONY THE K: Weighing 400 pounds, accompanied by T. A. S., from Da Hood Rats, Nate! (Nate and T. A. S. antagonize the crowd on their way to the ring.) TONY THE K: And his partner.... ("Limelight" by Rush plays as the fans cheer the entrance of Arlon Flaym.) TONY THE K: Weighing 240 pounds, from Springhill, TN, Arlon Flaym! (Arlon Flaym comes down slapping hands with the fans and gets in the ring, regarding Nate warily.) MELINN: This definitely an odd combination, but they could be facing an even odder combination, no doubt. TONY THE K: Their opponents.... ("Graveyard Symphony" plays as the lights go off as the fans cheer the entrance of Bloodbath.) TONY THE K: Weighing 328 pounds, from Death Valley, Bloodbath! (Bloodbath makes his way down to the ring and raises his arms as the lights go back on to the cheers of the crowd.) TONY THE K: And his partner.... (A fast-playing bagpipe version of the Bay City Rollers' "Saturday Night" plays as the fans boo the entrance of Fergus McTavish, the Whirling Scottish Devil.) TONY THE K: Weighing 295 pounds, from Glasgow, Scotland, Fergus McTavish, the Whirling Scottish Devil! (Fergus runs to the ring and immediately attacks Nate.) MELINN: And here we go, they're not wasting any time! Fergus McTavish wailing away on Nate, and Nate quickly tries to escape out of the ring, but he's followed by the Whirling Scottish Devil, who has a chair... and he nails Nate with it! Faceslam into t he floor! Fergus back in the ring now, followed by Nate, Fergus stomping away, and a tag to Bloodbath. MELINN: Bloodbath pounding on Nate now, knocking him to the ground. (Bloodbath raises his arms to the crowd, who cheer.) ROOT: Bloodbath of course having a history with Nate through his association with Ganglord, many CWO fans will remember the wild brawl that Bloodbath and Ganglord had earlier this year. MELINN: Bloodbath going for a figure four leglock but he's kicked in the face. And Nate makes the tag to Arlon Flaym. Flaym with a small package but Bloodbath is out of it before a 1 count. Bloodbath throws Flaym out of the ring! THOMAS: Well, this is what this lottery match is all about, strange combinations trying to co-exist. Flaym following him, and again all four men brawling outside the ring. Both men back in the ring, and Bloodbath makes a tag to Fergus McTavish. THOMAS: Look at the look on Fergus' face, he doesn't like working with Bloodbath, that's for sure! ROOT: But with that plush number 59 or 60 spot, this match will make even the worst of enemies the best of friends! Flaym with a cradle... it's reversed by Fergus! 1... 2... thr... and Flaym barely gets out of that cradle. Fergus with a tag to Bloodbath, Bloodbath with an inside cradle, 1... shoulder up. And Bloodbath throws Arlon Flaym out of the ring again. ROOT: A lot of action has taken place out there already, both of these teams, all four of these men are willing to do whatever it takes to win those two final slots in MAY-HEM. MELINN: Bloodbath setting Flaym on a table, what's he going to... oh my! Bloodbath with an elbow drop off the ring apron, and he drove Flaym through a table, can you believe it?!? THOMAS: That Flaym ain't eternal! MELINN: Bloodbath now trying to slam Arlon Flaym into the ringpost... Flaym slips out and slams Bloodbath into it instead! All four men again brawling outside the ring, now Flaym and Bloodbath are back in the ring, Flaym with a tag to Nate. THOMAS: I'm sure Nate wishes Warren was down with him right now instead of that goofball Flaym. MELINN: Nate trying to stomp on Bloodbath but he's met with a shoulderblock. Bloodbath now with a tag to the Whirling Scottish Devil. Fergus with a jawbreaker, he's got Nate in the corner, trying to go for the Highland Fling... no, Nate counters it with a rollup! 1... 2... shoulder up. And... oh my, Fergus with a kick to the groin and Nate goes down! ROOT: Very obvious low blow, and I'm surprised the referee didn't disqualify Fergus right there. THOMAS: Did you see what McTavish did to the referee the other night? I wouldn't say anything at all to him! FERGUS (screaming to the crowd): IT'S NOT OATMEAL! MELINN: Uh... well, Fergus whipping Nate into the corner... again playing to the crowd... now charging in with an elbow smash. FERGUS (to Nate): C'mon, ya bugger, put up a fight! Nate responding with a kick to the groin of his own, and he makes the tag to Arlon Flaym. ROOT: I guess Nate figured turnabout was fair play. THOMAS: At least he had the balls to do it! MELINN: Nate and Arlon Flaym with a double faceslam on McTavish, a surprising doubleteam maneuver from two men who to our knowledge have never worked together before. Flaym now setting Fergus up on the turnbuckle... going for a superplex but it's blocked by Fergus. Flaym falling to the mat... McTavish with a flying clothesline! And he makes the tag to Bloodbath. ROOT: Lucky break there for the combination of Fergus and Bloodbath, if Flaym would have connected with that superplex, that could have wrapped up the 59th and 60th spots for them right there. (Bloodbath raises his arms to the crowd, who cheer.) MELINN: Flaym nailing Bloodbath with a punch, a kick, Bloodbath fights back with a hard chop and Flaym is down. Bloodbath with the figure four... he's got it on! THOMAS: Give it up, Flaym, that 59th position ain't worth a broken leg! MELINN: Flaym in a lot of pain here, but he's trying to reverse the hold, can he do it... yes he can! Pain reversed on to Bloodbath, although Bloodbath doesn't show a lot of any kind of emotion at all... as he reaches the ropes. Flaym with a tag to Nate, who throws Bloodbath out of the ring. ROOT: Figure four a great submission hold to use, however, you have to be careful to avoid the reversal, as just happened to Bloodbath. MELINN: Nate stomping on Bloodbath before he gets back in the ring, and he tags to Arlon Flaym. Nate and Flaym pounding on Bloodbath, knocking him down. THOMAS: Notice that Nate and Flaym are actually co-operating in this match, Fergus is too pig-headed to even do that with the World title shot at stake! MELINN: Flaym goes for a leg scissors but Bloodbath counters it. (Bloodbath raises his arms to the crowd.) THOMAS: What is he doing, checking for deodorant? MELINN: Bloodbath with a tag to Fergus, who comes in with a double axhandle. Going for the cobra clutch but Flaym counters out of it, nails Fergus with a chop, but McTavish comes back with a double axhandle and a chop of his own, and Flaym is down. Fergus puts his feet on the ropes, cover, 1... 2... and Flaym kicks out. Whip into the ropes... Fergus goes for the short clothesline, but it's avoided by Flaym, who makes the tag to Nate. Nate whips Fergus into the corner... nails him with the kick, now going for the Gangsta Drop but it's blocked. ROOT: The Gangsta Drop that awesome press slam of Nate's, but McTavish is a very big guy to try that on, he's nearly 300 pounds. MELINN: Fergus with a DDT! And a tag to Bloodbath. Nate back to his feet, he nails Bloodbath with an elbow smash, another one, now stomping away on the man from Death Valley, he throws him out of the ring. Nate now trying to stomp on Bloodbath outside the ring, but Bloodbath grabs his leg! Bloodbath dragging Nate over to the corner... and he slams his leg into the ringside post! THOMAS: That's not good, because those legs of Nate have to hold up 400 pounds of his flab most of the time, I'm sure they're already weak. MELINN: Bloodbath back in the ring now, going for the figure four leglock but Nate counters with a rollup! 1... kickout. Nate back on his feet... bodyslam! And a tag to Arlon Flaym. Flaym has Bloodbath in position... piledriver! ROOT: Now that was impressive, Arlon Flaym with a piledriver on the 328 pound Bloodbath. MELINN: Flaym stomping away, he goes for a figure four but Bloodbath kicks him in the face. Flaym fights back though, with a double axhandle, forearm smash, goes for a clothesline but it's ducked by Bloodbath, Bloodbath with a quick tag to Fergus, and Fergus just rocks Flaym with a short clothesline! THOMAS: Now that was the first actual attempt at a doubleteam we've seen from those two all night! MELINN: Fergus with a cover, he's got the tights, 1... 2... thr... shoulder up. Fergus now choking Flaym. REFEREE GARY ALLES (counting): 1.... MELINN: And Fergus lets go. Fergus now going for the Boston crab but Arlon kicks him in the face. Arlon with a double axhandle, a forearm smash, and Fergus is down. Tag to Nate, who makes the cover, 1... 2... thr... shoulder up. Nate stomping away, he goes for the reverse chinlock but Fergus powers out of it. Fergus with a punch and a tag to Bloodbath. ROOT: This has been a pretty even match so far, both of these teams actually able to work together better than we thought they would. MELINN: Bloodbath with a figure four leglock... Nate reaches the ropes. Bloodbath drags Nate back to the center of the ring, going for the figure four again but Nate kicks Bloodbath in the midsection, doubling the big man over. Tag to Flaym... Flaym wit h a big clothesline! Bloodbath still up... Hurricanrana into a Cradle! 1... 2... thr... no, a kickout by Bloodbath, so close there. ROOT: Again, give Flaym a lot of credit, that's an awesome move to do to a big man like that. MELINN: Flaym with another clothesline, a double axhandle, and Bloodbath rolls out of the ring. Flaym trying to stomp on Bloodbath outside the ring, but he's met with a fist to the face! (Bloodbath raises his arms to the crowd.) MELINN: Flaym with a chop, he goes for a clothesline but it's ducked, Bloodbath with a rollup! 1... shoulder up. Bloodbath with a rollup but Arlon Flaym reverses it, 1... 2... thr... no, he almost had him but Bloodbath kicked out! S. trying to get the 400 pound Nate back in the ring... and look at this! Bloodbath has Fergus by the throat... and a chokeslam off the top rope! Wow! ROOT: Unbelievable! MELINN: And referee Gary Alles still making his count... he gets to 10! Nate has been counted out! TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the winners of this match in 18:00 as a result of a countout, and therefore receiving the entry numbers 59 and 60 in the MAY-HEM battle royal, Bloodbath and Fergus McTavish, the Whirling Scottish Devil! ("Graveyard Symphony" plays as the fans cheer.) MELINN: And Fergus McTavish and Bloodbath are not finished yet! Both of those men brawling back to the dressing room, and Arlon Flaym may need medical attention after that devastating top rope piledriver that McTavish calls the Highland Fling. Let's go to Johnny Starr in the control center. (Johnny Starr is in the control center as MAY-HEM graphic comes up.) JOHNNY STARR: All right, welcome fans to the control center portion of this EYE ON THE STORM broadcast, I'm Johnny Starr, we hope you enjoyed our opening matchup between Arlon Flaym and Nate against Bloodbath and Fergus McTavish, the Whirling Scottish Devil. As a result of that match, we now know that Arlon Flaym and Nate will be the first two men out for MAY-HEM, and that Bloodbath and Fergus McTavish will be the last men out, numbers 59 and 60. However, the rest of the entry numbers are of course up for grabs, and this is what makes MAY-HEM truly unique. (Starr reads along as the graphic for the rules of MAY-HEM comes up.) MAY-HEM BATTLE ROYAL 1. 60 MEN 2. TWO RINGS 3. 2 MEN START OUT IN EACH RING 4. 1 MAN IS ADDED TO EACH RING EVERY TWO MINUTE 5. IN ORDER TO ELIMINATE SOMEBODY, THEY MUST GO OVER THE TOP ROPE AND TOUCH THE FLOOR. THROWING SOMEBODY INTO THE OTHER RING DOES NOT ELIMINATE THEM, HOWEVER, ONCE A PERSON HAS BEEN THROWN INTO ANOTHER RING, HE MUST STAY IN THAT RING. STARR: Of course the winner of the MAY-HEM battle royal will get the World title shot against whoever is the champion at the CWO's June PPV, SUMMER HEAT. That World title will be decided tonight in what many are guessing will be the final confrontation between the "Monsters of the Ring," 7'4" "Monster" Rich Clark, 7' Goliath, and the man known only as Bane. WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: TWO RINGS, ONE SURROUNDED BY A STEEL CAGE TWO MEN START OUT IN THE CAGE, ONE ESCAPES TO FIGHT THE OTHER MAN FOR THE TITLE Bane vs. "Monster" Rich Clark vs. Goliath STARR: Let's take you back to the April 15th TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER from Tokyo, Japan, where Bane won the World title for a second time from "Monster" Rich Clark. (Footage from 4/15/97 TNF is shown, as "Monster" Rich Clark comes down to the ring and enters, striding over the ropes, only to be attacked by Bane with the shovel!) MELINN: And we knew this was going to happen, why somebody didn't take that thing away from Bane is beyond me! THOMAS: Are you going to be the one who takes it away from him, Melinn, huh? ROOT: "Monster" Rich Clark really being battered by that gold shovel of Bane's, what an insult, to beat the World champion with the very belt he possesses. MELINN: Bane now choking Clark on the mat with the shovel, and now look at this! Bane using the sharp end of the shovel, slamming Clark with it, and Clark is bleeding, come on, get that thing out of his hand! (Crowd reacts as Randy Johnson makes his entrance.) MELINN: And here comes CWO Commissioner Randy Johnson! The Texas Rose coming down, hopefully to restore order to this match. THOMAS: Yeah, right. MELINN: Johnson in the ring, he's got a mike... ROOT: "Monster" Rich Clark has some nasty gashes from that shovel attack, we may not have a World championship match after all. RANDY JOHNSON (with Japanese translation right behind him): Bane, as the Commissioner of this league, I order you to give me that shovel or I will throw out this match! You will have no chance of regaining your world championship, unless you give me that shovel! THOMAS: Give it to him, all right! MELINN: Well, he better not, if he wants another chance at that belt, "Monster" Rich Clark trying to get back to his feet after that vicious attack with the shovel, and it looks like Bane is indeed handing that shovel to Commissioner Johnson. ROOT: Very brave move by Commissioner Johnson, of course, during his long and fabled wrestling career as the "Texas Rose," this man would back down from no one. MELINN: All right, and... oh my god! Bane looked like he was going to hand the shovel to Commissioner Johnson, and then he must have changed his mind, as he just swung it at Johnson and hit him right in the head! THOMAS: Grand slam! Johnson is a retired wrestler, he's in his fifties, for crying out loud and... oh no... Bane's got that special trophy now, especially designed by the Super Japan promotion for this match... and he just broke it over Johnson! He just broke that trophy right over our Commissioner! ROOT: Bane is out of control! THOMAS: And he likes it! MELINN: Bane using a piece of the trophy to slam "Monster" Rich Clark with, and now Bane is going outside the ring, he's threatening the timekeeper, leave him alone! BANE (growling at the timekeeper): RING THE BELL, YOU STUPID JAPANESE ****, RING THE BELL OR ELSE! (The timekeeper meekly rings the bell. Back to Johnny.) STARR: A savage match then ensued, with Clark valiantly trying to retain the title, but the pre-match attack by Bane had done its job. MELINN: Bane bleeding as well, not as bad as Clark, but some, and now Bane is stalking Clark, we've seen this before... Merciful Death! He's got it on in the center of the ring! THOMAS: It's over! ROOT: Clark has just lost too much blood, 7'4" "Monster" Rich Clark, it takes a hell of a lot to put him down, but that unwarranted shovel attack at the beginning of the match definitely swung the advantage Bane's way. Bane has reclaimed the World championship! ("Sympathy for the Devil" plays as the fans boo. Back to Johnny.) STARR: However, due to the circumstances behind the match, and also Bane's reprehensible attack on CWO Commissioner Randy Johnson, the CWO Championship Committee decided to hold the title up, and schedule this match between the three top contenders: North American champion Goliath who holds a victory over "Monster" Rich Clark from CWO SPRING BREAK '97, the former World champion Clark, and also the former World champion Bane. Two men will start out in the steel cage, the object will be to beat the other opponent into a state where he can be handcuffed to the cage. Then, the man who escapes the cage will have to face the other with the World title on the line. This will probably be one for the ages, you can't afford to miss it, call your cable company right now. WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Highlanders vs. Pit Bosses (champions) STARR: The World Tag Team Championships are on the line, as the Highlanders go for the gold once again. Some may they might have a chance of winning it, since there has been some apparent tension between the Pit Bosses as of late. However, the Pit Bosses have assured everyone that everything is fine, and Friday night in Buffalo, NY, they were able to win a non-title match against the Highlanders, so perhaps they are back on track. Regardless of the "behind the scenes" stuff, this match should be truly exciting. NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP: Tiger Rogers vs. Jason "Flash" Varga (champion) STARR: Originally supposed to have been a title vs. title situation, Tiger Rogers lost his Light Heavyweight championship to Moses this week at the house show in Erie, PA. However, Tiger Rogers still has a chance to win the North American championship from the man who took it from Rogers' Alliance friend and partner, Goliath. You can be sure the Alliance is going to be looking for revenge in this one, and Jason "Flash" Varga is definitely going to have a tough time of it against the man who went to the finals in the very first CWO Championship Tourney. NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Mercenaries vs. Bad Guys (champions) STARR: The Bad Guys have been under fire recently from a lot of top teams, but their attention will have to be focused on the red-hot Mercenaries tonight. TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP: Johnny Reb vs. Inferno (champion) STARR: Originally supposed to have been Johnny Reb against Masher Maccabee, this was changed when Inferno won the belt from Maccabee at a house show and then successfully defended it not only in a rematch but also in a match against former Television champion Maximum Overdrive. Now, the hungry Johnny Reb may have his chance at winning his first major title here in the CWO, but again, the Alliance will probably play a large part in this matchup, so Johnny Reb will have to be ready for them. All of that, plus of course the super 60-man, two ring MAY-HEM battle royal, you can't afford to miss it. Last year's MAY-HEM was of course won by wrestling legend Sabin Rene Figaro, and he went on to win what was then known as the CM2WF World title. Could history repeat itself this year, will this year's MAY-HEM winner win the title at SUMMER HEAT? You'll have to tune in tonight to find out, as the CWO brings you great PPV action in MAY-HEM!
