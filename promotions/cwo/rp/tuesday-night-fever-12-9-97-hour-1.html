TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER 12/9/97 - HOUR #1

9 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWO TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER 12/9/97 - HOUR #1 "LIVE" FROM THE ROSEMONT HORIZON, CHICAGO, IL RATING: TV-14 (L, V) PRODUCER: [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] ) EXECUTIVE PRODUCER: Brent G. Alles ( [detail withheld] ) (Fade into a spinning globe, as the CWO logo is slowly superimposed over it.) VOICE-OVER: The CWO... providing wrestling excellence the world over.... (Montage of CWO action is shown, as Aerosmith's "Fever" plays in the background. The CWO TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER logo explodes on to the screen, and we go to a roaring crowd in Chicago, IL, as pyrotechnics go off to signal the start of hour #1. We join our announcing team of Jerry Covenant, Eric Johnson, and Michael Postema.) JERRY COVENANT: You're looking at it, the excitement, the glamour, the heat that IS Tuesday Night Fever! Live from the Rosemont Horizon in Chicago, Illinois, this is the CWO ready to bring you folks not one, but TWO of the hottest hours on TV. ERIC JOHNSON: The North American tag titles will be on the line, and we'll have great singles action as well as we move into our next great pay per view, CWO CELEBRATION. MICHAEL POSTEMA: That's right, and it looks like Johnny Reb may have bragged his way into a corner at this one, where he'll have to take on not one, but two opponents. JERRY COVENANT: Johnny Reb is a three way dance against both Mondo Gecko and the former multi-time world champion Hile Troy. Also, the Wild Cards will be granted a hard earned title shot against the Deadly Sins, the Highlanders will take on the Jade Dragons, and in what will be one of the most anticipated matches of the year, Fergus McTavish and Masher Maccabee will once again collide. ERIC JOHNSON: It promises to be nothing but the best, emanating live from downtown Grand Rapids at the Van Andel arena, the very heart of the CWO. JERRY COVENANT: Let's get down to the ring to Johnny Starr. (Johnny Starr is standing in the center of the ring) STARR: Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome... Painmaster! (There is silence for a few seconds. Everyone is wondering what is going on. Suddenly, the music of one Stone Cold Steve Austin starts up and Painmaster, dressed in the gear of SCSA, walks to the ring. The die-hard Painmaster fans are going nuts with this. He gets to the ring, and climbs a turnbuckle, throwing his hands up into the air. He then descends the turnbuckle, and walks over to Starr, staring at him.) STARR: Painmaster, just what in the heck to do you think you're doing? PAINMASTER: Why don't you just shut up for a second, son, before I kick your ass! (The die-hards are cheering like mad. One fan can be seen in the crown with a sign that reads "THE ORIGINAL Painmaster.") PAINMASTER: You come out here wanting to talk to me about my match with that Lone Loser? You expect me talk to you about him and glorify him? You remind me of a jackass! (More cheering.) STARR: And just what is the meaning of you copying one of the great stars of another organization? PAINMASTER: Because I can son, and that's the bottom line, because Painmaster said so! (More cheering ensues.) PAINMASTER: But you want to know the real reason I've done this? It's real simple. I want to show all these people out here just how easy it is to copy a gimmick and a wrestler that has already been done. You see Lone Puppydog, you can't think of a good gimmick on your own, so you have to copy someone else's. Well, "son", that's just one thing I cannot stand. You are looking at The Original! One who does not have to copy gimmicks to get him over! I just come in, do my business in the squared circle, and get out. And I take great pride in ridding federations of two classes of idiots. One class is lame, cartoonish gimmicks, and the other are people who copy someone else's gimmick! (Crowd is still cheering.) PAINMASTER: When I was in another federation, there was this wrestler that was known as the Grand Pubba. A lame gimmick that he got from an old cartoon. I took the liberty of beating his punk caveman ass, and throwing him out of that damn federation! Now, the second class of people that I despise has shown its head here in the CWO! First, Joe Eagle copies Mark Mero, and now, Lone Donkey copies Stone Cold Steve Austin! Eagle, I'm not finished with you, and I'll deal with you in due time. But you, Lone Ass, at CWO CELEBRATION, your ass will be out the door, if I have anything to say about it! This federation will not copy gimmicks, and it will not have people running around in cartoonish costumes! And that, as Stone Cold would say, is the bottom line! ("Wherever I May Roam" from Metallica starts up as the arena has started to cheer Painmaster.) JERRY COVENANT: Well, there you have it, and when we come back, Painmaster is going to be in action! Don't go away! (Logo and music come up as we fade to commercial. When we come back, Tony the K is in the ring along with referee Gary Alles.) TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. Making his way down the ring, weighing 231 pounds, from Corpus Christi, Texas, here is Tommy Bomber! ("Of Wolf and Man" by Metallica plays as Tommy Bomber makes his way down to the ring.) JERRY COVENANT: Fans taking exception to Tommy Bomber, who in a world filled with "between wrestlers", still managers to get a mouth full of jeers on his way down to the ring. MICHAEL POSTEMA: That doesn't mean squat when it comes to the record books. TONY THE K: And his opponent, weighing in at 332 pounds, from Oakland, California, here is the Painmaster! ("Wherever I May Roam" by Metallica plays as the fans boo the entrance of the Painmaster.) MICHAEL POSTEMA: What _is_ it with all of this damned Metallica? ERIC JOHNSON: They're a popular band, Postema. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Bah... give me Megadeth or Dream Theater any day. JERRY COVENANT: Both of these men at a crossroads in their careers, both of them badly need a win, and a win here on Tuesday Night Fever in front of the world is the best kind they could get. MICHAEL POSTEMA: That's right, this isn't some house show held in a barn or a high school gymnasium, this is the lights and the life! JERRY COVENANT: Both men moving to the middle of the ring, lockup, and Tommy Bomber gets shoved back into the corner by the larger Painmaster. MICHAEL POSTEMA: He shouldn't have even bothered with a tie-up, this guy has him outweighed by over 100 pounds, he can't match strength. JERRY COVENANT: They lock up once more, Painmaster with an armbar, Bomber reverses it, chop to the chest, no effect... bounds off the ropes, and Painmaster _rips_ him down by the hair! ERIC JOHNSON: Referee warning Painmaster for those kinds of actions, although with what we've seen as of late in the CWO, I don't know how stern I'd apply that warning. JERRY COVENANT: Painmaster picking Bomber back up, _clubs_ him over the back with a forearm, throws him into the ropes... clothesline, ducked... Bomber with a spinning back kick! Bomber off the ropes, cross body block! Going for the pin... one... two... no! MICHAEL POSTEMA: He simply doesn't have enough weight to keep a man as large as Painmaster down this early in the match. JERRY COVENANT: Bomber with a short arm scissors, but Painmaster is right near the corner, gets the rope. Both men back up, Painmaster with a side headlock, Bomber tries to shove him off... BULLDOG HEADLOCK but Painmaster, nice quick reversal! ERIC JOHNSON: Not only that, but when you've got a man the size of Painmaster running with your head, and you're caught off guard, it's going to twist your neck terribly. JERRY COVENANT: Painmaster now going for a pin of his own... one... two... no! Painmaster failing to hook either leg there, and he paid for it. Picks Bomber back up, belly to belly! MICHAEL POSTEMA: Hey! Those light heavyweights really DO fly! JERRY COVENANT: Painmaster now stalking Bomber, Tommy Bomber in the corner, Painmaster with a hard right, another, an elbow to the side of the head... he charges in, Bomber moves! Tommy Bomber now unloading! A chop, a second one, knee to the chest... Irish whip to the other corner, Tommy Bomber.... handspring... HANDSPRING AVALANCHE! ERIC JOHNSON: Oh yeah! That brought the crowd up to its feet! JERRY COVENANT: Bomber with a straight right hand, he's throwing punches as fast as he can... Painmaster throws a wild one, BOMBER DUCKS IT and comes back with MORE punches, Bomber now grabbing Painmaster by the head, bulldogging headlock of his own! MICHAEL POSTEMA: He's putting up a hell of a fight for a little guy! ERIC JOHNSON: No one who weighs over 200 pounds is a little guy. JERRY COVENANT: Tommy Bomber off the ropes, legdrop... going for the pin, one... two... no! Picks Painmaster back up, Bomber off the ropes, cross body block... PAINMASTER CAUGHT HIM! MICHAEL POSTEMA: Going DOWN? JERRY COVENANT: Painmaster with a backbreaker... lifts him up, ANOTHER backbreaker! Locking him in... lifts him for a power bomb! Bomber trying to break his grip... Bomber with a _Frankensteiner_ reversal! ERIC JOHNSON: What action! JERRY COVENANT: Bomber scraping Painmaster up off the mat, synching his arms behind his back... TOMMY KNOCKER Double Arm DDT! One... two... no! Bomber going up to the top rope... whatever he hits, he could very well win with right here... he leaps... DOUBLE AXEHANDLE.... NO! CHOKE SLAM REVERSAL! MICHAEL POSTEMA: What strength! Say goodnight to little Tommy! JERRY COVENANT: Painmaster blocking Bomber's double axehandle attempt with a choke hold... into a _choke slam_! That was an _awesome_ display of strength without a doubt! MICHAEL POSTEMA: And now he's giving us an amazing display of STUPIDITY by not going for the pinfall. JERRY COVENANT: Painmaster picking Bomber up, has him in a power slam position... running, NO! Bomber hops off and shoves Painmaster into the turnbuckle, then dropkicks him outside of the ring! ERIC JOHNSON: Well, that if nothing else will give Tommy Bomber a well needed rest after that choke slam. MICHAEL POSTEMA: It's Deutscher! JERRY COVENANT: "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher just came from under the ring! He taps Painmaster on the back, STUN GUN! Rolls Painmaster back to the inside of the ring! MICHAEL POSTEMA: This is going to be easy pickings for Tommy Bomber now. JERRY COVENANT: Bomber picking up Painmaster... _brainbuster!_ Forget about it, that's it! One... two... three! TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the match in a time of 12:04, Tommy Bomber! JERRY COVENANT: Ladies and gentlemen, we'll be right back after this commercial break! (Camera cuts away) VOICE OVER: It's a tradition... three years in the making. (images of past CWO and CM2WF wrestlers are shown) VOICE OVER: This year, a champion who feels he is without challengers. JOHNNY REB: Ah have beaten tha best y'all have ta offah me! VOICE OVER: Must take on not one... but two, of the top contenders in the world. MONDO GECKO: Nothing could be sweeter, than a little GOLD this holiday. HILE TROY: You know what they say... FOURTH time is a charm! VOICE OVER: Once again, there are THREE sides to every story. One ring, three destinies... CWO CELEBRATION 97! (Camera back to FEVER. Johnny Starr is in the ring.) STARR: Ladies and gentleman, at CWO CELEBRATION '97, this man will finally achieve his dream as he gets ready to face Johnny Reb and Lord Hile Troy in a three way dance for the World Title! Will you please give it up for MONDO GECKO! ("Hip Hop Hooray" plays as the fans go nuts as Gecko walks down the aisle and slaps the fans' hands!) STARR: Gecko, congratulations on being named the co-number 1 contender for the belt and now the dream is almost a reality. You, Reb, and Lord Troy for the CWO World tile at CWO CELEBRATION '97! GECKO: You know Johnny, ever since I was a little kid they had said I couldn't do it. When I went to Long Beach State and made the team as a walk-on wide receiver, they said I would never play, I did. When I entered the wrestling profession they said I would never make it in the business, I did. When I got to the CWO they said I would be eaten up alive and sent back home, I won three titles and I am part of the future of the CWO! But most importantly, numerous people back home said that I would never last in the rough streets of Oakland, I DID! (Crowd roars as Starr claps his applause as well.) STARR: However, Mondo, in your way to your ultimate dream stands two "giants" of the CWO who you know very well. Your former close friend and tag partner, Johnny Reb, and a former GKI nemesis Lord Hile Troy. How will you beat them? GECKO: Reb, I was one of the first people to congratulate you when you won the belt and I even offered to help you out! But when I came to you for help against The Dark Side and The Law, you told me to "Stick It"! It seems when you got to the top you forgot about your friends and that is a cardinal mistake. The last time you beat me I was tired, battered and in the middle of a feud with Masher Maccabee! This time, however, I got some new moves, some new techniques and you have gotten old! Reb, as you find out at CWO CELEBRATION, payback's a bitch! Lord Hile Troy, you are considered the man who represents the CWO and as much as I still harbor ill-will, I respect you! But Troy, I got something you don't and that is two trainers who know everything about you and Johnny Reb. For the past two weeks I have been training with Gordy and Brent "The Ace" Alles! And I am going to say right now, Brent "The Ace" Alles, it would be an honor for me if you would accompany me to the ring with Tamara Wilcox. Hopefully, this time it will be the three of us, no Mason-Dixon or Rebel Yell or even GKI to bail anybody out. WE WILL SEE WHO IS THE BEST AND I GUARANTEE YOU THAT MONDO GECKO WILL BE THE NEW WORLD CHAMPION! CPE 4 LIFE! STARR: All right, there you have it, ladies and gentlemen, Mondo Gecko! (Crowd cheers.) STARR: We'll be right back! (Logo and music come up as we fade to commercial. When we come back, Tony the K is in the ring along with referee Kendall Dolemeyer.) TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall, with a 30 minute time limit. Making their way to the ring, at a combined weight of 525 pounds, here are, the PIT BOSSES! ("Money Talks" by AC/DC plays as the fans cheer the entrance of the Pit Bosses.) JERRY COVENANT: Shocking turn of events that the Pit Bosses reunited, especially in the wake of the destruction of Gold Kard International. ERIC JOHNSON: Are the Pit Bosses still aligned with Gold Kard? We don't know where they stand as far as the whole Damion situation is concerned. JERRY COVENANT: Hard to say, it's one of many questions that needs to be answered concerning the whole Gold Kard/Damion affair. TONY THE K: And their opponents... at a combined weight of 489 pounds, accompanied by Dallas, the CWO NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS... "Diamondback" Chris Myers, "Airborne" Marc Fulcrum, the Riders On the Storm! ("Shoots and Ladders" by Korn plays as the fans cheer the entrance of the Riders, who come down slapping hands with the fans.) JERRY COVENANT: They may have been "handed" those tag titles, but by winning the tournament, I'd say they MORE than earned them. MICHAEL POSTEMA: You'll get even my agreement on that one, Covenant. That tournament had more teams than a normal tournament would for the _world_ titles. ERIC JOHNSON: Well, they'll get their opportunity to prove their critics wrong tonight, right now. JERRY COVENANT: Pit Boss #2 starting off against Marc Fulcrum. #2 with a double axehandle, a hard right hand that puts Fulcrum on his knees, he drapes him over the top rope, now trying to choke him out! MICHAEL POSTEMA: In their day, these guys were known for being one of the quickest working tag teams in the league. JERRY COVENANT: #2 fires Marc into the ropes... catches him an spinning elbow... going for the pin, one... two... no! Picks Fulcrum up by the hair, shoots him into the corner, follows in with a clothesline! Makes the tag to #1. ERIC JOHNSON: Already showing it with a tag in the first minute of the match. JERRY COVENANT: #1 in the ring, kneelift to the chest, Fires Fulcrum into the ropes... _reversal_... Fulcrum off the ropes with a flying forearm! Knocking #1 down, goes for a pinfall of his own. One... two... kickout! MICHAEL POSTEMA: That's okay, just a minor delay in their great game plan. JERRY COVENANT: Fulcrum picks him up, _swinging neckbreaker_! Makes a somersault across the ring, tag to Myers! Myers in the ring with a body slam on #1... #2 in the ring, Fulcrum with an eye gouge! ERIC JOHNSON: Chris Myers using some brawling tactics to stem the flow of former North American Tag Champions. JERRY COVENANT: Good CWO education you have, Johnson. Myers kick to the chest... _RATTLER!_ He hit the Rattler... going for the pin... Referee shaking his head no! Myers is trying to pin the wrong man! MICHAEL POSTEMA: Ha! What a dolt! What a clod! What a maroon! JERRY COVENANT: You've been reading MAD magazine again, haven't you? MICHAEL POSTEMA: Who can resist spy vs. spy? JERRY COVENANT: Pit Boss #1 with an elbow drop to the back of Myers head! Going for a pin of his own... one... two... no! ERIC JOHNSON: The referee having a difficult time trying to sort out these four men, especially since the Pit Bosses are so interchangeable. JERRY COVENANT: #1 picking Myers up, full nelson clamped on, swinging Myers around like a rag doll. He picks him up and _drops_ Myers down onto the canvas! MICHAEL POSTEMA: Oh no, don't drop him on his butt, real effective move. JERRY COVENANT: #1 makes the tag to #2, this might as well BE spy vs. spy. #2 in the ring, big splash... missed! Myers using the ropes to climb back to his feet, #2 back to his feet as well... double axehandle! ERIC JOHNSON: NO! JERRY COVENANT: Myers buries a hard fist into the gut of #2, picks him up, side suplex, and that may be all the break that Myers needs to make the tag. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Fine, let him tag, that's just fresh meat as far as the Pit Bosses are concerned. JERRY COVENANT: Myers reaching out... reaching... and he _makes it_! Fulcrum in the ring with a spin kick on #2... back body drops #1 over his head! Fulcrum fires #2 in for the ride... ducks down, and #2 leaps over him tackling #1 out of the ring! ERIC JOHNSON: Now is the time for Myers and Fulcrum to get in the double teaming! JERRY COVENANT: Myers back in the ring, both men... _double suplex_ and Pit Boss #2 is in a world of trouble on the inside of the ring! Myers with a body slam... Fulcrum off the ropes, leg drop! One... two... no! MICHAEL POSTEMA: On the outside! He's got brass knuckles! JERRY COVENANT: Pit Boss #1 up on the apron, he swings and catches Myers in the head with those knuckles! Fulcrum dropkicks him off the mat and the knuckles go flying! ERIC JOHNSON: Unfortunate... Fulcrum could have showed the referee those brass knuckles and gotten as easy disqualification victory. JERRY COVENANT: That's probably exactly why he _didn't_ try to do that, he doesn't want the easy victory. Fulcrum turning around, and Pit Boss #2 is there to greet him! Low blow... belly to belly suplex! MICHAEL POSTEMA: See, you have to be careful with these guys, it's almost as if they know what one-another are thinking all of the time. JERRY COVENANT: #2 going for the pin... he's got the tights, and the referee sees it! The Pit Bosses won't be able to get that easy of a win either it seems. ERIC JOHNSON: Whether or not they're aligned with GKI, once a rulebreaker, always a rulebreaker. JERRY COVENANT: Okay... all four men back in the match, with #1 and a groggy Chris Myers now in their corners. #2 with a wristlock, Marc Fulcrum reverses it... #2 with a drop toe hold, impressive! MICHAEL POSTEMA: These guys may be one of the more under ranked tag teams in the CWO. For their size, they have the skills. Don't forget they were in the GKI camp with such greats as Brent Alles, Hile Troy, and Gold Kard himself... that's a lot of technical influence. JERRY COVENANT: #2 picking Fulcrum up above his head, press slams him into the turnbuckle! I couldn't see Fulcrum keeping up a good pace in this match for much longer at this rate. ERIC JOHNSON: That's the thing about these two though, once you think you've got them beat, they come back twice as hard. JERRY COVENANT: #2 tags out, or not... both Pit Bosses now in the ring, they fire Fulcrum into the ropes... double clothesline, _ducked it_!. Back off the other end, Myers with a knee to the back of number two! MICHAEL POSTEMA: I guess after those brass knuckles he wanted to do some cheating of his own? JERRY COVENANT: Marc Fulcrum with a kick to the gut, he sets #1 up... piledriver, and now he's giving the signal for his Air Fulcrum 450! MICHAEL POSTEMA: 4:20?! JERRY COVENANT: No! _450_... off the top, he _NAILS IT_! Referee there to make the pin, #2 tied up with Chris Myers... One... two... THREE! ERIC JOHNSON: A successful and well-fought title defense. TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the winners in a time of 15:59... and _still_ CWO NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS... Marc Fulcrum and Chris Myers! (Fulcrum and Myers celebrate in the ring.) JERRY COVENANT: We'll be back with our final match of hour one right after this, so stay tuned! (Camera fades away) VOICE-OVER: Now in toy stores just in time for Christmas, it's CWO vs. The WORLD! All of your favorite CWO stars are here! Gold Kard, Chris Myers, Fergus McTavish, The Highlanders, MOD Damion, Moses, Johnny Reb, Sandman Jr. and more! Real 3d motion for _maximum_ game play! Buy now! (Camera returns to FEVER, as El Grande Pistolerro is warming up in the locker room, getting ready for his match by doing some last minute stretching! As he looks up, he faces the camera!) PISTOLERRO: Maia, mi estupido gringo, you never learn do you? First you ran your mouth with me an insulted me until you got spanked! Now you attacked my friend, Southern Dynamite and thought it would go unanswered.? Maia, you are nothing but a pretty boy punk who needs to be taught some respect and I am just the man to do it! I have proven myself time and time again and just like that bastardo Hellhound will find out, Pistolerro always gets his man! Ariba la raza y CPE 4 LIFE! WILCOX: Joe Root, if you think for a moment that you are going to lay your greasy palms on Pistol over here, you got another thing coming! Touch him and I will stick my high heels so far up your keister you will be picking plastic out of your cheeks. CPE rules here and Maia, if you think about kissing me, I will slap the taste out of your mouth! (Back to the ring, where Tony the K is with referee John Myering.) TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall, with a 20 minute time limit. Making his way to the ring, weighing 240 pounds, from Mexico City, Mexico, accompanied by Tamara Wilcox, El Grande Pistolerro! (CPE's theme music plays as the fans cheer the entrance of Pistolerro, who comes down slapping hands with the fans.) MICHAEL POSTEMA: Someone needs to tell this guy to spring the 10$ to get some _real_ music played for his entrance, my god! ERIC JOHNSON: Perhaps he prefers to do his talking in the ring? MICHAEL POSTEMA: And just _perhaps_, NAFTA wasn't a good as we all thought? TONY THE K: and his opponent, weighing 235 pounds, from Rio De Janeiro, Brazil, here is "The Brazilian Lover" Carlos Maia! ("You Give Love a Bad Name" plays) MICHAEL POSTEMA: Now _this_ is music! ERIC JOHNSON: Get out of the 80's, Postema! MICHAEL POSTEMA: No way! I ain't looking for nothing but a _good time!_ JERRY COVENANT: Yeah, great, great... this match should let us see some of the high-flying excitement that's taking over America. MICHAEL POSTEMA: No... we _aren't_ one of those other leagues where the fans have to tolerate little masked goofs running around doing _flippy kicks_ for 30 minutes each show! JERRY COVENANT: Postema! They are great, talented athletes! MICHAEL POSTEMA: Who are dressed like circus midgets. JERRY COVENANT: All right... Both men lock up in the middle of the ring, Maia with a headlock. Pistolerro shoves him off to the ropes, Pistolerro with a leapfrog, back off the other end, hip toss... reversal! Maia goes for an elbow drop and misses, and both men are back to square one. MICHAEL POSTEMA: All _that_ for just ONE move? JERRY COVENANT: That's right. They lock up again, waistlock by Maia, reversal by Pistolerro into a GERMAN SUPLEX! Pinning combination! One... two... no! Referee was way out of position there. ERIC JOHNSON: With guys that fly like this, anywhere can be out of position, because the men can end up six feet away from you in a matter of a second. JERRY COVENANT: Maia up on his feet first, knee to the midsection, DDT! He _planted_ Pistolerro into the mat, who is a long ways away from his home in Mexico City. MICHAEL POSTEMA: This stuff's made in NEW YORK CITY. ERIC JOHNSON: New York City? JERRY COVENANT: MEXICO CITY! Maia for the pin... one... no! Rather early kickout there. Maia picking Pistolerro up off the mat, fires him into the ropes, back br... NO! Pistolerro with a change into a flying headscissors takedown! MICHAEL POSTEMA: More little midgets jumping around on trampolines more like it. JERRY COVENANT: Always the cynic. Pistolerro with a hard chop, spinning back kick! Maia knocked down to the mat, Pistolerro going for the pinfall... one... two... kickout. ERIC JOHNSON: Way too early to get a pinfall. JERRY COVENANT: You'd be surprised what can happen. Pistolerro off the ropes, flying shoulderblock knocks Maia back down. Pistolerro seems uncertain as to what he wants to do. MICHAEL POSTEMA: A lot of good having that bimbo Wilcox on the outside of the ring does. JERRY COVENANT: Pistolerro with a front face lock, lifting Maia up for a suplex, Maia floats over the top... ROLLUP! One... two... no! That was a close as any pinfall we've seen tonight. ERIC JOHNSON: Referee lucky enough to be close to make that pinfall. JERRY COVENANT: Maia catches Pistolerro with a sharp European uppercut, a headbutt... Maia off the ropes, bulldog headlock! Maia off the ropes again... kneedrop right to the chest. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Wilcox is up on the apron, she thinks its a strip bar runway! JERRY COVENANT: Maia moving over to Wilcox, he throws a slap at Wilcox who jumps off of the mat. MICHAEL POSTEMA: Almost got what she deserved. JERRY COVENANT: Pistolerro up from behind, he buries a fist into the back of Maia, front facelock... slingshots him off the ropes... OUCH! Slingshot _brainbuster_! ERIC JOHNSON: Jarring his neck right into the mat, that had to hurt. JERRY COVENANT: Pistolerro going up top, all the way up top... he leaps off... FLYING HEADBUTT... MISSED! He just cracked his head onto the mat, Wilcox is beside herself! ERIC JOHNSON: All Maia has to do is roll over and get the win! JERRY COVENANT: Maia drapes an arm over the knocked out Pistolerro... one... two... three! It's done! Maia didn't even have to earn that win! TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the match in a time of 7:03... "The Brazilian Lover" Carlos Maia! JERRY COVENANT: Wilcox can't be happy at that, Pistolerro most less costing himself what could have been a victory after that vicious brainbuster move... but, our time has come... we're ready to roll into hour two... when we come back Kyle Melinn and David "The Truth" Thomas will take us away, so don't you go away! (Logo and music come up as we fade to commercial.) END HOUR #1
