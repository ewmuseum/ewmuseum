CWO SHOWCASE--12/8/96

9 December 1996

CWO SHOWCASE 12/8/96 Producer: [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] ) Executive Producer: Brent G. Alles ( [detail withheld] ) (The opening music begins to play as we see shots of flash across a dark screen. As the music ends, the words "CWO SHOWCASE" fade into the screen in gold lettering. The titles fade out in favor of a packed arena as the camera pans to Kevin Rinaldi and Tamara.) Kevin Rinaldi: Hello, everyone, and welcome to CWO SHOWCASE!!! I'm Kevin Rinaldi, and with me is the one, and thankfully the only, Tamara! Tamara: Hi, guys!!!!! KR: We have a fantastic card for you tonight! In our opener, Tiger Rogers locks up with Killer Steve Keller! T: I hope he's a lady killer, because I think he's to die for! KR (rolls his eyes, "here we go again"): Wildfire takes it to Destroyer Davis! T: Wildfire....now there's a guy who's really hot! KR: In tag team action, the new team of Flyboy and Clark Gray, the Pair of Aces, take on Blood, Inc., Panther and Steel! T: Ooooo, Steel--tell me something, how hard is he? KR (shoots her a look): Hile Troy meets Sandman Junior! T: Two more absolute hunks in the CWO!!!! KR: And in our main event, CWO Light Heavyweight Champion Dark Raven defends his title against Moses!!! T: Now there's a man who'd put me on my knees! KR (sputtering): And let's go to a commercial! (as the video begins to fade) Listen, you psychotic nympho, you....... ****COMMERCIAL BREAK**** (the camera fades in on Kevin and a visibly-sulking Tamara) KR: And we're back with our first match! Let's go to the ring! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. ("Walking in LA" by Missing Persons plays as the fans cheer) Coming down the aisle, weighing 240 pounds, from Hollywood. CA, Steve Keller! ("Unforgiven" by Metallica plays as the fans boo) And his opponent...weighing 240 pounds, from Warner Robbins. GA, accompanied by Jimbo Jones, Tiger Rogers! KR: And KELLER attacks Rogers before the bell! Keller has him up! SHOULDERBREAKER! And an early cover! 1...2...Rogers gets the shoulder up! T: Hope Keller isn't that quick in everything he does! KR: Keller snaps on a headscissors, working the Tiger's neck back and forth. Keller has him up...and tosses him through the ropes and outside the ring! But Keller does not go after him! T: I might be distracting him, Kevin! KR: I bet....Keller catches Rogers as the man from Georgia reenters the ring...sends him into the ropes...huge clothesline!! Keller brings Rogers up....vertical suplex!! Keller rolls Rogers onto his stomach..going for a Boston crab...NO! Rogers powers out and sends Keller headfirst into the buckle! Keller is hurt! Rogers going up top.... MOONSAULT!!! Cover! 1...2...Keller powers out! T: He's bleeding!! KR: Keller must have caught his head on some exposed metal, because he is indeed bleeding. Rogers sends him into the ropes...dropkick sends Keller out of the ring! And Rogers is going after him! Rogers has Keller by the hair, heading for the ringpost...Keller blocks it!!!! And Keller sends Rogers into the post instead! The two men climbing back into the ring....Rogers catches Keller across the eyes! Rogers throws a left...Keller blocked it!!! He's got Rogers in the air...POWERSLAM! Cover! 1...Rogers gets the shoulder up! T: WOW, this is exciting!! KR: Keller hits with the vertical suplex! Keller goes for the cover!!! Wait! Jimbo Jones is on the ring apron!!! Keller on his feet...and he nails Jones with a right! But Rogers is up, too! T: Look out, Stevie!!!! KR: Rogers from behind!!!! Keller is down, and Rogers is stomping away! Rogers snaps on a leg scissors!!!! Keller in agony! Rogers brings him up, sends him into the ropes.....BIG dropkick! Rogers up top...MOONSAULT! Cover!!! 1...2...3!!!!!! T: Oh, POOT!!!!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner, in 7 minutes and 34 seconds, Tiger Rogers!!!!!!!!! ("Unforgiven" begins to play as the fans boo some more) KR: A very even match, but Jimbo Jones was the difference! And we're going to a commercial ****COMMERCIAL BREAK**** KR: And we're back with our next match! The red hot Wildfire meets the Destroyer! Let's go to the ring!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. ("My Sherona" by the Knack plays as the fans cheer) Coming down the aisle...weighing 232 pounds, from Venice Beach. CA, Wildfire! ("Livin' on the Edge" by Aerosmith plays as the fans boo) And his opponent...weighing 235 pounds, from Los Angeles. CA, accompanied by Jimbo Jones, David 'The Destroyer' Davis! KR: And Wildfire leaps to the attack, sending Davis into the ropes! But it's Davis who catches him with a dropkick!!!! And Davis plays to the crowd! T: And they're not buying!!!! Well, good, I'LL take him, if they won't KR: Well, he's got a previous engagement, because Wildfire's got him! Wildfire sends him into the corner....follows him in with a handspring elbow! And Wildfire sends Davis out of the ring!!!! T: Hi, Dave!!!!!!! KR: Wildfire waiting in the ring for Davis to return.....catches with a chop on his way in!!! Wildfire gouges the eyes!!!! And Davis sent to the concrete floor again!!! Jimbo Jones is beside himself!!! T: Where? I only see one of him!!!!! KR: Wildfire again catches Davis on his way back into the ring....sends him into the ropes.....Jimbo Jones trips Wildfire from behind!!!! How'd the ref miss that!!! T: Maybe he had his eyes on Davey...I can't stop watching him!!!! KR: Davis slaps on the STF!!!! Wildfire straining...reaching....and manages to get the ropes! The ref brings both men up....Davis sends Wildfire into corner...going for the Slingshot......AND MEETS WILDFIRE'S ELBOW INSTEAD!!!! And the crowd goes nuts!!!! Wildfire going to town on Davis, raining blows on his head!!!!! Davis scampers out of the ring, but Wildfire's going after him!!! Wildfire sends Davis into a table!!!! Wildfire has a chair...NO!!! Davis grabbed and whacked Wildfire with it!!! T: Not over here, guys, this is a new outfit!!!!! KR: Davis slams Wildfire onto the concrete floor!!! He grabs a chair and beats Wildfire with it!!!! Davis rolls back into the ring and Wildfire staggers after him! T: Hi, Dave!!! Here I am!!! KR: Davis playing to the crowd, but Wildfire coming up behind....Wildfire with the shoulder into the kidney!!!! Davis is hurt!!! Wildfire sends him into the corner! Handspring elbow!!!! Wildfire rakes the eyes, but Davis fights back with a low kick!!! T: That must have hurt!!! KR: Davis applies the STF!!!!! Wildfire writhing in pain!!!! Davis releases the hold, going up top......DESTROYER DROPPER!!!!!!!! Cover!!! 1...2....thr....WOW!!!! Wildfire somehow summoned the strength to get the shoulder up!!! T: Who's that? KR: That's Ganglord!!! What's he doing here?? Davis hits with a clothesline, knocking Wildfire out of the ring!!! And Davis arguing with the referee!! Ganglord nailing Wildfire from behind!!!!! Ganglord rolls him back in the ring!!!! Davis going up top.....MOONSAULT!!!!! Cover!!! 1... 2... 3! Davis steals the win!!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner, at 6 minutes and 29 seconds, David "The Destroyer" Davis!!!!!! KR: He should say David Davis and Ganglord, because Ganglord was as responsible for the win as anything Davis did!!!! We'll be back after this!!!!! ****COMMERCIAL BREAK**** KR: And we're back with our tag matchup!!! The Pair of Aces battle Blood, Incorporated! Let's go to the ring!!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the following tag team matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit. ("Danger Zone" by Kenny Loggins plays as the fans cheer) Coming down the aisle, at a combined weight of 500 pounds and accompanied by their partners Hollywood Muscle, Flyboy, Clark Gray, the Pair of Aces! ("Kalifornia Love" by 2Pac and Dr. Dre plays as the fans boo) And their opponents...at a combined weight of 475 pounds, accompanied by Da Boss, Steel, Panther, Blood Inc.! KR: And Clark Gray and Panther begin the match. The two men circling....they lock up...Panther with a side headlock....Gray sends him into the corner!!! And Gray rams Panther's head into the buckle!!!! Gray slaps on a bearhug......GOOD LORD!!!! Panther powers out!!!! But Gray catches him with an elbow across the jaw! Panther is down!!! Gray going for a spinning toehold--and clamps on the figure four!!! Panther trying for the ropes.....he's got them!! Great action!! T: And great butts!!!! KR: Gray sends Panther into the ropes.....but Panther hits with a legsweep!!! Gray crashes to the mat!!! Panther drops the elbow!!! And Panther stomping away on the prone Ace!!! T: Isn't Gray awfully close to the corner? KR: Yes, he is, and he tags Flyboy!!! Poor ring awareness on Panther's part! Flyboy slams the fist into Panther's jaw!! Sends him into the ropes.....FRANKENSTEINER BY PANTHER!!!! Panther with a camel clutch, but Flyboy immediately grabs the ropes! Flyboy with an inside cradle!!! Da Boss is on the ring apron!!! Flyboy attacks the Boss, but Panther gets him from behind!!!! Panther with a bridge pincover!!! 1...2... thr...Flyboy kicks out!!!!! T: Nice view!!!!! KR: Will you stop??? Panther with a standing headlock.....Flyboy sends him into the ropes.....big elbow by Flyboy!!! Flyboy sends Panther into the corner....hits with the shoulder block!!!! Cover!!!! 1...2...thr....Panther kicks out!!!!! T: And an even better view!!!!! KR: Give me strength...Flyboy with a spinning toehold...Panther shoves him off as he spins around! Panther going for an elbow....he missed!!! Flyboy has him up.....dropkick!!!!! Cover!!! 1....2....thr....almost!!!!!! T: Isn't Steel getting bored? KR: Steel arguing with Steve Keller and Wayne Carpenter, but Panther should try to make a tag sometime soon....Panther with a flying headscissors on Flyboy....he snaps on the camel clutch!!! Flyboy trying to break the hold....HE DOES!!!!!! Flyboy with a huge elbowsmash!!!! Panther is down!!!! Flyboy with a figure four!!! Panther trying to get to the ropes.....he gets there!!!! The referee brings both men up....and Flyboy nails Panther with a shoulder!!! Flyboy up top....FLYING BODYPRESS!!!!!! Cover!!! 1... 2... 3! T: Oooh, baby!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, your winners, in 10 minutes and 47 seconds, Flyboy and Clark Gray, the Pair of Aces!!!! KR: And Hollywood Muscle in the ring to help their partners celebrate the win....and Da Boss and Blood, Inc. don't take too kindly to that!!!! All seven men brawling in the ring!!!!! Flyboy nails Panther with a dropkick, sending him out of the ring!!!! Clark Gray powerslamming Da Boss!!!! Wayne Carpenter and Steve Keller double teaming Steel, and they toss him out!!!! Security reaching ringside and separating the two sides, but Blood, Inc. are livid!!!!!!! Da Boss: This ain't over!!!!! You hear me? This ain't (bleep)in' over!!!!!! You punks are gonna die!!!!! KR: And the Four Aces heading back to the dressing room, and we'll go to a commercial! ****COMMERCIAL BREAK**** KR: And we're back!!!! Blood, Incorporated refused to talk to reporters after their match, but from the sounds coming from the locker room, they were not happy!!! Let's go to the ring for our next matchup! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. ("Enter Sandman" by the Mighty Mighty Bosstones plays as the fans cheer) Coming down the aisle, weighing 235 pounds, from Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, accompanied by Kellen, Sandman Jr.! ("2112 Overture" by Rush plays as the fans cheer) And his opponent, weighing 240 pounds, from Blackpool, England, Hile Troy! KR: And Troy resorting to some gamesmanship!!!! He won't enter the ring until Sandman Junior's been checked for foreign objects! The referee obliging, Troy entering the ring......and Sandman Junior nails him as he steps through the ropes!!! Sandman Junior whips Troy into the ropes... FRANKENSTEINER!!!!!! Cover!!! 1...2...thr...Troy puts his foot on the ropes! T: Lucky for him!!!! KR: And Sandman Junior making Troy pay for his gamesmanship earlier!!! Big dropkick by Sandman Junior!!! Sandman Junior going up top.....somersault splash!!!! Here's another cover!!! 1...2...Troy just manages to kick out! T: This guys is really mad!!!! I think it's kind of cute!!!! KR: You would...Sandman Junior tosses Troy to the concrete floor!!!! He's going after him!!! Sandman Junior has Troy by the head, sending him to the ringpost....NO! Troy reverses it!!!! Troy rolls Sandman Junior back into the ring! Troy with a clothesline!!!! Sandman Junior answers with an elbowsmash!!! Troy with a forearm!!!! Sandman Junior with a chop!!! And the two men go toe-to-toe!!!!! T: This is fun!!!!! KR: Troy sends Sandman Junior into the ropes...knees him in the midsection!!! Another trip into the ropes.....clothesline!!!! And Troy stomping away on the junior Sandman! The crowd loving it!!! Troy with a legbar!!!! Sandman twisting and turning, trying to escape!!! Troy releases the hold, going for the Dragon Suplex....Kellen's on the ring apron!!!! Troy backhands her off, but Sandman Junior is right behind him!!! T: That (bleep)!!!! Someone oughta kick her butt!!!! KR: Sandman Junior nails Troy from behind!!!! Troy is hurt!!! Sandman Junior whips him into the corner!!! Going for a running knee....Troy dodges!!!! Troy with a small package!!!! 1...2...Sandman Junior gets the shoulder up!!! T: I'm still mad about that (bleep) Kellen!!!!! KR: Troy with a crossface chickenwing!!!! Sandman Junior in trouble!!! Troy brings his man up, sends him into the corner....belly to belly suplex!!!!! Troy goes for a kneedrop.....Sandman Junior moves!!!! Sandman Junior kicks Troy right in the head!!!! Sandman Junior snaps on the STF!!!! Troy in agony! Sandman Junior releases the hold, brings Troy to his feet......sends him into the ropes.....TROY GETS HIM WITH THE CLOTHESLINE!!!! Troy with the Indian deathlock! Troy going for a small package! T: She's doing it again!!! KR: Kellen on the ring apron! Troy has her by the blouse!!! Wait!!! Tamara's at ringside!!! She's gesturing to Troy!!! Troy ducks.....and Sandman Junior nails Kellen instead of Troy!!!! T (returning): That'll teach her!!!! KR: Troy with a bulldog on Sandman Junior!!! Brings him up...DRAGON SUPLEX!!!! Cover!!!! 1....2.....3!!!!!! This one's done!!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner, in 11 minutes and 42 seconds, Hile Troy!!!!!!! KR: And Sandman Junior and Kellen are not happy with the result or each other!!! And Kellen looking at us!!! T: Maybe next time, (bleep)! KR: And we'd better go to a commercial before things get ugly! ****COMMERCIAL BREAK**** KR: And we're back with our Light Heavyweight Title matchup!!! Let's go to the ring!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 60 minute time limit. It is for the CWO Light Heavyweight title! (music plays as the fans cheer) Now coming down the aisle, weighing 228 pounds, from Bethlehem, Israel and accompanied by The Evangelist, the challenger, Moses!!!!! (the theme from "Halloween" plays as the fans boo) And his opponent, weighing 231 pounds, from London, England, the CWO Light Heavyweight champion, Dark Raven!!!!! KR: And Moses and Dark Raven taking it to each other from the get-go!!! Moses with the armbar takeover! Dark Raven escapes before the ref begins to count! Raven with a dropkick....missed!!! Moses brings him up...sends him into the corner!! Moses with a kick to the midsection! Raven is down! Cover! 1.... Raven kicks out! T: Holy cover!!! KR: Raven sends Moses into the corner....going for a monkey flip...Moses shoves him off!!! Moses with the octopus!!! Raven in serious pain!!!! Moses drops him, and the crowd goes nuts!!!! Moses brings Raven up....sends him into the ropes.... CLOTHESLINE!!!!! Cover! 1...2...the champ gets the shoulder up! T: Holy nearfall!!! KR: If you weren't a woman.....Moses with another octopus.....Moses releases the hold, but he's off-balance...Raven nails him with a dropkick! Raven clawing at his man's face!!!! Moses shoves him off!!! Raven charges.....Moses nails him with the armbar takeover!!!! Cover!! 1...2....Raven's shoulder is up!!! T: Holy takedown!!! KR: Raven with a dropkick....and Raven stomping away on Moses!!! Raven with the power elbow drive!!! And Raven slapping Moses across the face! WHOA!! Moses slaps back!!! Raven is down!!! Moses with an Asai moonsault!!! Cover!!! 1...2... Raven gets his foot on the ropes!!!! T: Holy feet! KR: You're going to do that the entire match, aren't you? T: Holy yes!!!! KR: Moses sending Raven out of the ring....and Moses nails the champ with a chair! Moses rolls Raven back into the ring...going up top....HE MISSED WITH THE MOONSAULT! Raven with a chinlock, cranking the head back and forth!!! Moses reaching for the ropes....he got them!!! Both men on their feet.....Raven sends Moses into the ropes... collision!!!! Both men are down!!!! Referee counting.....both men climbing to their feet....Raven misses with the dropkick! Moses acknowledging the cheers of the crowd! Moses going for a power bomb.....Raven boxes his ears with his feet!!! Raven going for a savate kick.....Moses ducks!!! Another armbar takeover by Moses!!!! Cover!!! 1...2...thr...Raven with the kick out! T: Holy repetition!!!! KR: Moses with a Frankensteiner!!!! Cover!!! 1....Raven's shoulder is up!!! (silence) KR: Tamara seemingly having problems with her mike (chuckle)! Moses with another octopus!!! Moses working the hold, putting tremendous pressure on Raven's back!!! Moses sends Raven into the ropes....HE MISSED THE FRANKENSTEINER!!!! Raven with a kick!!! Raven sends Moses into the ropes, and he HITS with the Frankensteiner!!! Raven slaps on the chinlock.....Moses breaks the hold!!! Raven brings him up.... dropkick!!!! Raven sends Moses into the corner!!! Monkey flip by Dark Raven!!! Raven drops the elbow onto the chest!!! Armbar by Raven, working the shoulder....he releases the hold...brings Moses up.....ANOTHER FRANKENSTEINER!!!! Moses has to be almost out!!! Raven drops the elbow!!!! Going up top.....he's going to go for Death From Above.....the Evangelist distracts him!!!! T: Holy interference! And nice try, Kevin, but I found the on switch on my microphone! KR: Too bad! Moses on his feet!!! Moses with a tiger bomb!!! Raven is down!!! Moses going up top....going for The Commandment!!!!!! HE GOT IT!!!!!! Cover! 1.....2....thr....NO! Raven somehow manages to kick out!!!! T: Holy stamina!!! KR: Moses with a Texas cloverleaf!!! Raven reaches for the ropes!! Moses tries to keep him away from them!!!! Raven reaching.....reaching.....he got them!!! Moses brings him up....sends him into the ropes....FRANKENSTEINER!!!!!! Cover!!!! 1....2......3!!!!!! New champion!!!!!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, in 14 minutes and 22 seconds, your winner, and NEW CWO Light Heavyweight champion, MOSES!!!!!!!!!!!!! KR: Moses wins the CWO Light Heavyweight Title!!!!! For Tamara, I'm Kevin Rinaldi saying, good night, everybody!!!! (the closing credits roll as the music plays)
