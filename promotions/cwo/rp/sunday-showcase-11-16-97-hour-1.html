SUNDAY SHOWCASE 11/16/97 - HOUR #1

17 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWO SUNDAY SHOWCASE - HOUR #1 11/16/97 CWO ARENA, GRAND RAPIDS, MICHIGAN RATED TV-14 (V, L) PRODUCER: Chris O'Brien ( [detail withheld] ) EXECUTIVE PRODUCER: Brent G. Alles ( [detail withheld] ) (Open to a shot of a spinning globe, as the letters CWO are slowly superimposed over it) VOICE-OVER: CWO...providing wrestling excellence the world over... (Camera opens to a shot of the CWO announcer's booth. Jerry Covenant and "The Original" Chris O'Brien are shown sitting there, as makeup personnel apply whatever is needed. The seats behind them are full, as a last couple of stragglers reach their seats before the show opens) COVENANT: So, who do you think is going to win the Carpenter Cup, Chris? O'BRIEN: Good question. A better one might be, who isn't going to win the Cup? Look at all the talent involved! You got the Mercenaries, the Wild Cards, the Bad Guys, the Legion of Doom, the Syndicate... COVENANT: Who? O'BRIEN: A team from another fed, but that's not important right now. Would you believe I'm not going to....a little more on the nose, please...bet on this one? COVENANT: No? I'm shocked, coming from a guy who bets on traffic jams. O'BRIEN: Ever try driving I-90 through Chicago? NOT recommended. COVENANT: So who isn't going to win, in your own opinion? O'BRIEN: I believe, you can eliminate every tag team that isn't a tag team. Guys like Myers/Fulcrum, Schillo/Williams... COVENANT: Don't count out Brian Williams, Chris. The man did win... O'BRIEN: Oh, enough about the damn March Madness tournament! The man hasn't done a single thing since then! I don't hype on Joe Eagle winning MAY-HEM, do I? COVENANT: No... O'BRIEN: When "the Hammer" does something to impress me, I'll give him credit. Until then, my pretend money is on... (A loud cheer from the crowd drowns out the name of the team Chris mentions) COVENANT: Really? Why them? O'BRIEN: Oh, call it a hunch. OFF-CAMERA VOICE: 15 seconds to intro, guys... ("Sabotage" by the Beastie Boys plays as the camera switches to a montage of CWO superstars in action...Skyhawk with a Skydive onto a helpless Tommy Bomber...Wildfire clocks Bloodbath with his baseball bat...Union Caine with a Cainedriver on Painmaster...Brent "The Ace" Alles challenges Lord Hile Troy... the Jade Dragons with the Dragon Strike on Conor McCloud... "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum nails Air Fulcrum on Johnny Reb... Reb and Sandman Jr. brawl in the aisle at FEVER...and a shot of the huge brawl involving the Dark Side that ended FEVER) COVENANT: Welcome to SUNDAY SHOWCASE! (The camera goes to a jam packed CWO Arena in Grand Rapids, Michigan. Fireworks go off as fans cheer wildly and wave their signs: MERCENARIES GLASS RECYCLING CLUB, FERGUS AUTO BODY REPAIR, LONG LIVE THE EMPIRE, REB'S A COWARD, and 32 TEAMS: 1 WINNER: 1,000,000 BUCKS: CAN I JOIN?) O'BRIEN: We could team up, Jerry! COVENANT: No thanks! (To the announcer's booth we go, where "The Original" Chris O'Brien and Jerry Covenant sit. Jerry is wearing the trademark CWO sweater and turtleneck, while Chris has his feet up on the desk, wearing a New York Rangers hockey jersey and black steel toed boots) COVENANT: Along with my relaxed colleague, Chris O'Brien, I'm Jerry Covenant, bringing you yet another edition of SHOWCASE! O'BRIEN: And why didn't we get asked to go to New York, Jerry? The stars of CWO are going to be in the Garden tonight, and I'm stuck here in fraggin Grand Rapids! What the hell is so grand about this place anyway? COVENANT: It's where we are today, Chris! We've got a jam packed... O'BRIEN: Yeah, avoid the question, Jerry. COVENANT: ...ahem. A jam packed show today, folks, and let's not waste any time, let's go down to the ring! (The camera goes to Chad Johnson, mike in hand, in the middle of the ring) JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to CWO SUNDAY SHOWCASE! (The crowd roars its approval) JOHNSON: The opening matchup is set for one fall, with a 20 minute time limit! ('You Give Love a Bad Name' by Bon Jovi plays as the fans boo) JOHNSON: Coming down the aisle, weighing 235 pounds, from Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, here is 'The Brazilian Lover' Carlos Maia! O'BRIEN: Maia the former Light Heavyweight champion here in CWO, and he's ditched Joe Root as a manager. Smart move. JOHNSON: And his opponent... ('Soul Driver' by Bruce Springsteen plays as the fans cheer) JOHNSON: ...from Grandville, Michigan, weighing 289 pounds, here is Brent "The Ace" Alles! COVENANT: The new father of a baby boy, and the local boy here tonight, Brent's set to go up against Lord Hile Troy in a British Rules Cage match at NOVEMBER PAIN, to settle their feud once and for all! O'BRIEN: and British Rules are.... COVENANT: I have no clue. The bell rings as Maia poses for the crowd, and Alles wastes no time, grabbing Maia and locking in an arm wrencher! O'BRIEN: Maia got caught looking away, you can't do that with a guy like Brent Alles. COVENANT: Maia reaches the ropes, which were nearby, lucky for him. And Brent releases ('The Brazilian Lover' Carlos Maia blows a kiss to the crowd, which boos back) O'BRIEN: Nice crowd tonight, as Brent and Carlos lock up...Carlos comes out with an Irish Whip, and Brent ducks under a right hand and comes off... a clothesline from Maia connects! COVENANT: Brent now pulled up and sent to the ropes again by Maia, who now hits a Frankensteiner! O'BRIEN: Alles rolls out of the ring, Maia off to a quick start today...but not anymore, as Brent moves out of the way from a pescado! COVENANT: You knew what that move was? I'm impressed, Chris, you're learning! O'BRIEN: Yes, folks, I can be taught! COVENANT: Brent rolls back in the ring now, as Maia slowly gets up. Even hitting that padding can knock a man silly. Maia tries to get back in the ring, and he's met with a boot to the gut by Alles! And an inside cradle! 1...and Maia kicks out. O'BRIEN: Both men up now, and Alles advances. Maia catches him with a rollup and a handful of tights! 1...2...and Alles kicks out! COVENANT: Alles caught off guard there, and now Maia goes for a dropkick, and misses! Now Alles goes for a scoop slam, but Maia turns it into a small package! 1...2...kick out! O'BRIEN: Both men just catching the other off guard, but Maia's going airborne, while Alles is trying to keep it on the mat. COVENANT: Maia now leaps up top...and he tries a flying body press, but Alles rolls it over! Alles....gets a 1 count only. And now Alles with a standing headlock on Carlos Maia. O'BRIEN: You have to wonder...Hile Troy is playing mind games with Brent Alles, and is Troy in the building tonight? COVENANT: Who knows, Chris? Alles applies pressure, and Maia is reaching for the ropes....Alles with a standing switch, into an armbar, and he yanks Maia away from those ropes! O'BRIEN: Alles twisting the arm of Maia, I don't know why. He should be working on the legs, one would think. COVENANT: Would you tell Brent Alles how to wrestle? O'BRIEN: Good point. MAIA: AH! REF: Do you want to give, Carlos? MAIA: NO! NO! O'BRIEN: And Alles replies by twisting even further, and now Maia is double over...and Maia with a thumb to the eye, and that breaks the hold! COVENANT: A double axehandle now by Maia, and he's going up top as Alles staggers...and Alles catches Maia in mid air with an elbow! It was Alles's turn to play possum there as Maia gets smacked in the jaw! ('The Brazilian Lover' Carlos Maia rolls outside the ring) O'BRIEN: And the crowd behind Alles, who is just watching Maia, who is pondering strategy ringside. COVENANT: He's wondering probably how to beat Brent Alles. O'BRIEN: Well, he ain't thinking about stock prices, Jerry! COVENANT: That was a ridiculous comment, wasn't it? O'BRIEN: Yeah, but I forgive you your one, as opposed to Postema's mistake ridden rants. COVENANT: Back to the match, if we may? Maia back in the ring, and he comes off the ropes, only to have "The Ace" catch him in a back body drop! And now back to the armbar...no, Maia counters with a low blow! O'BRIEN: And Alles is doubled over as Maia goes up top...flying axehandle right to the back of the head of Brent Alles! ('The Brazilian Lover' Carlos Maia wiggles his hips) O'BRIEN: Yeah, yeah, and now Maia whips Brent to the ropes, no, it's reversed! Flying forearm! Alles now stomping away on Carlos Maia! COVENANT: The momentum changed right quick...and now Alles is setting Carlos Maia up....ALLESBREAKER! O'BRIEN: It's over! Brent covers... (Lord Hile Troy appears in the aisleway, mike in hand) TROY: HEY, BRENT! O'BRIEN: What's he doing here? TROY: Brent, I've been thinking...and you did not pick the right name for your kid. COVENANT: Brent stands up and is looking at Troy. Maia isn't moving at all, that Allesbreaker was right on the mark. TROY: Let's look at what you could have named him...how about Bud? (Crowd boos) TROY: Or Samuel Adams? Samuel Adams Alles. Or you could have gone the American Indian route. Rolling Rock Alles has a nice ring to it... O'BRIEN: Alles is trying not to lose control here. But Troy's pushing all the right buttons. TROY: Or...I've got it! You could have named him Brass Monkey! COVENANT: Huh? TROY: It's not a good sounding name, but it's cheap, like his mom... O'BRIEN: AND BRENT ALLES IS OFF AND RUNNING! Hile Troy drops the mike and runs backstage as Alles is charging after him. The ref looks confused...and he starts to count! COVENANT: I don't think Brent Alles is coming back. O'BRIEN: And the ref reaches ten! JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of this match, by result of a countout, 'The Brazilian Lover' Carlos Maia! COVENANT: And Maia isn't moving at all. The winner of this match can't even get up, folks! O'BRIEN: When Alles and Troy get together, it's going to be war! Don't go away, up next, we've got Wayne Schillo and Brian Williams taking on the Harvesters of Sorrow! (Fade to commercial. When we come back from commercial, we go to Jerry and Chris in the broadcast booth.) COVENANT: Fans, as you all know, one of the biggest tournaments in the history of wrestling will begin this week, as 32 teams from around the world will collide for $1,000,000 and the Carpenter Cup trophy! O'BRIEN: And up now, we've got two of the tag teams that will compete in this tournament! Let's send it down to Chad Johnson! JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is set for one fall with a 30 minute time limit! Coming down the aisle... ('War Machine' by KISS play as the fans boo) JOHNSON: At a combined weight of 680 pounds, accompanied by Phantom Lord, Suffering and Pain, the Harvesters of Sorrow! COVENANT: Two huge men, who have come into CWO with a vengeance, and are a part of what was once the Reverend's Church...though who runs it now is quite a mystery. JOHNSON: And their opponents... ('You Shook Me All Night Long' by AC/DC plays as the fans go absolutely NUTS!) JOHNSON: ...at a combined weight of 380 pounds, here is the team of Wayne 'College Guy' Schillo and Brian 'The Hammer' Williams! O'BRIEN: WHAT? 380 pounds? Sloth weighs more then that! COVENANT: But both of these men are longtime CWO icons, Chris, and they've got more... O'BRIEN: Say they got heart, and I'll slap you, Jerry. COVENANT: Well, what else can I say? These men have proven themselves, in spite of their small size. O'BRIEN: I know, I know, I'm just sick of 'heart' this and 'heart' that. COVENANT: The bell sounds, and Pain comes over and clocks Brian Williams with an elbow smash as the ref was checking him! Cover by Pain! 1...2...thr...and Williams manages to kick out! O'BRIEN: He had his bell rung there, as Pain now tries for a Scorpion Death Drop, but 'The Hammer' nails him with a double axehandle! And now a dropkick sends Pain down! And Williams covers! 1...2...kick out! COVENANT: Brian Williams wasting no time, coming in with two forearm smashes! Pain tries to hit one of his own, but Williams stops him cold with a double axehandle! And now Williams up top...moonsault! O'BRIEN: A very surprising move there from 'The Hammer' and now Pain pulls himself up, and Williams tries another axehandle, but Pain with a shoulderblock! And now a bearhug! COVENANT: Pain just so much bigger then Brian Williams, and he doesn't want to get caught by the big man! Williams manages to reach behind him and grab the ropes, and Pain releases. O'BRIEN: Williams with a smart tag to Wayne Schillo, who is mounting a comeback after the smear campaign by GKI this past summer. Both men close in, and Schillo with a kick! And now he stomps on a fallen Pain, and a cover! 1...and Pain kicks out! But Schillo tries again! 1...and Pain kicks out! COVENANT: It will take more then that to put down Pain, and Schillo now signaling for the Boston Crab! Schillo reaches for the legs, but Pain gets them away, and Schillo just picks him up and sends him to the 'College Guy's corner! O'BRIEN: Pain getting schooled by these two men as Brian Williams and Wayne Schillo doubleteam Pain. Odd to see these two break the rules, even this slightly. COVENANT: Everyone wants that cup, Chris! Pain goes down, and Wayne covers! 1...2...thr...and Pain manages to get his foot on the ropes! O'BRIEN: Phantom Lord looking on in concern as Schillo tags back in Brian Williams! And Williams dropkicks Pain, who was just standing back up! And Williams off the ropes...Pain manages to roll away from a elbowdrop! And he wastes no time in getting over to tag his partner. Suffering comes in, and he and Pain with a double faceslam on 'The Hammer'! SUFFERING (to Williams): COME ON, YOU PUNK! COVENANT: And Williams responds to that by tripping Suffering with a legsweep, and now he's chopping a fallen Suffering! O'BRIEN: I love it! Look at the emotion on William's face, he's just pounding away! COVENANT: A tag to Schillo now by 'The Hammer', who comes in and hits a dropkick! Pin! 1...2...and a kickout by Suffering, and now Schillo tags back in Brian Williams. O'BRIEN: Both men trading quick tags, they look ready for this week. COVENANT: Williams now yanks up Suffering and applies a headlock! Applying pressure now, trying to wear the big man down. O'BRIEN: No surprise, for his size, Suffering doesn't have much stamina. Williams is taking a gamble here, though, because he's trying to stand toe-to-toe with the big man, in a sense. COVENANT: That almost was a smart statement, Chris, you are improving. Suffering now reaching out....and he manages to grasp the ropes now. Williams releasing right away, and Suffering repays the kindness with a double axehandle! O'BRIEN: Williams tries to play nice, and Suffering covers! 1...2...and Williams kicks out! Pain tagged in and Williams stands up...and ducks a dropkick from Pain! COVENANT: Pain sent to the ropes now by Brian Williams...and Pain blocks a reverse neckbreaker attempt! And Pain now hoists up Williams! He presses him over his head...AND HURLS HIM OVER THE TOP ROPE ONTO THE FLOOR! O'BRIEN: Williams just got delivered FedEx style! And Pain wastes no time, and goes outside the ring! He picks up Williams and tries to slam him into the ringpost, but 'The Hammer' reverses and sends Pain to the steel! COVENANT: A back and forth match tonight as now Pain is rolled back in the ring... O'BRIEN: At great strain by 'The Hammer'. COVENANT: ...and covered by Brian Williams! 1...2...kick out! (Brian Williams plays to the crowd, who respond with a loud cheer) COVENANT: And Pain now takes advantage and catches Williams with an elbow! O'BRIEN: Pain with a forearm, and a cover! 1...and a shoulder up! COVENANT: In comes Suffering, giving his partner a rest, but not before a double faceslam! And Suffering now sends Williams to the ropes...and a spinning heel kick by the big man! O'BRIEN: Nice move! COVENANT: Williams hauled up now, and Suffering with a right hand! But now 'The Hammer' with an elbow smash! And a double axehandle! O'BRIEN: And a rake to the eyes ends that offense! Suffering now tags in Pain, and a double clothesline sends Williams down to the canvas! COVENANT: Pain covers! 1...2...kick out! (Wayne Schillo starts clapping, trying to get the fans behind Brian Williams) O'BRIEN: And the Arena is jumping as the Harvesters are calling for their finisher, the Angry Chair! COVENANT: What is an Angry Chair? O'BRIEN: A messed up Alice in Chains song, Jerry! But Suffering is up top....Pain powerbombs Williams, and Suffering with a flying buttdrop I wouldn't wish on my worst enemy! Suffering covers, 1...2...three! NO! 'THE HAMMER' GETS HIS FOOT ON THE ROPES! COVENANT: The Harvesters are beside themselves as Suffering pulls up Williams...and Williams with a double axehandle! AND HE DIVES ACROSS THE RING AND HITS SCHILLO! O'BRIEN: Here comes the 'College Guy'! Schillo with a dropkick that sends Suffering down! And off the ropes...splash by Schillo! Cover! 1....2...shoulder up! Schillo not frustrated, he hits a kick to the gut of Suffering. COVENANT: And now a whip to the ropes...and Suffering manages to grab hold, and Schillo misses a dropkick! A tag to Pain, and Pain stands over the fallen Schillo. PAIN (to Schillo): IT'S TIME FOR A LESSON IN PAIN, SCHILLO! O'BRIEN: And Pain pulls up Schillo...and Schillo clocks Pain with a forearm! And now Schillo with a DDT! COVENANT: Schillo on fire, and he tags in Brian Williams! Here comes Suffering, but he goes down courtesy of a double clothesline! O'BRIEN: Williams lines up Pain...KEUKA SMACK! It's illegal, but I love that combo! Williams covers, 1...2...3! JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the winners of this contest, at a time of 12:17, the team of Brian Williams and Wayne Schillo! O'BRIEN: And Williams/Schillo with a huge amount of momentum going into the Carpenter Cup! Fans, don't go away, we've got our last match coming up right after this! (Fade to commercial. We see a shot of a group of young kids playing in the street. A dark blue minivan pulls up, and the window rolls down) VOICE-OVER: Remember, kids, beware of strangers. (Dave "The Truth" Thomas leans out the window) THOMAS: Hey, kids, which way to the nearest Arby's? KID #1: STRANGER! KID #2: DON'T TALK TO HIM! KID #3: LET'S GET HIM! (In a rush memorable of "Night Of The Living Dead", the mob of kids surround the minivan, and we see Dave Thomas being pulled out) THOMAS: HEY! What are you kids doing? KID #1: GET THE ROCKS! (Poor Dave is pelted with rocks as the screen fades to a red logo) VOICE-OVER: Brought to you by Cyberspace Wrestling Organization. (We come back to the booth, where Chris and Jerry are trying their hardest not to laugh) O'BRIEN: Remember kids....oh....don't do.... COVENANT: We're....my God...being...serious... (Both men lose it and begin to crack up. They're still laughing when we go to Chad Johnson) JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is set for one fall with a 20 minute time limit! Coming down the aisle... ('Wherever I May Roam' plays as the fans boo) JOHNSON: ...from Oakland, California, weighing 332 pounds, here is Painmaster! (Instead of hearing comments, we still have Chris and Jerry busting at the seams) O'BRIEN: OH MAN THAT'S THE FUNNIEST! JOHNSON: And his opponent... (Eerie sounds of the night play as the fans boo) JOHNSON: from parts unknown, weighing 312 pounds, here is the Master of Darkness, Damion! COVENANT: OK...hehe...we have to call the match. O'BRIEN: I don't know if I can...OK, Damion comes out with an elbow smash, and Painmaster right back with a chop! (Crowd WOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOS!) O'BRIEN: OK, that WOO cleared my head. COVENANT: Painmaster covers! 1...kick out! Painmaster taking on the leader of the Dark Side, and the possible new leader of the Church, but right now, Damion is on the receiving end of a belly-to-back suplex! (Painmaster screams to the crowd, who boo in response) O'BRIEN: And Painmaster now throwing Damion outside the ring! Both men are big, and this is going to be a fight. COVENANT: Painmaster grabs Damion and slams his head into the ring apron! And now he sends the Master of Darkness into the steel guardrail! O'BRIEN: Damion not focused right now as Painmaster gets a chair...and he clocks the leader of Dark Side with it! COVENANT: This match has started out with a bang as now Painmaster rolls back in the ring. Damion with a small cut from that chair, and he's just looking at Painmaster, who is daring him to get back in the ring. O'BRIEN: Damion moves very slowly up to the ring apron, and Painmaster comes over, only to be hit with a nasty facerake! And now Damion to the top...flying clothesline takes Painmaster off his feet! COVENANT: Damion making his return to CWO, and he's been on a roll lately, and he's going back up again...and Painmaster catches him up top! And a MASSIVE body slam brings the Master of Darkness down! O'BRIEN: Painmaster flexes for the crowd, but he should be concerned with the man on the ground. COVENANT: Painmaster reaches for Damion...and Damion with a rollup! 1...2...thr...and Painmaster kicks out! Damion almost pulled off a major surprise on Painmaster, and now Damion follows up with a vertical suplex! O'BRIEN: Damion quick for his size, and now a lightning fast backbreaker! And now Painmaster up top again...flying elbow drop! Cover, and Damion with his feet on the ropes! 1...2...thr...and the ref sees it and denies a win for the Master of Darkness. COVENANT: Damion now grabs Painmaster and just hurls him to the corner! And he follows up with a dropkick right to the back of the head! And a forearm smash! O'BRIEN: Painmaster just having absolutely NO offense since that early flurry, it's been all Damion. COVENANT: Damion up top again, and a flying clothesline right to the throat! O'BRIEN: Damion now setting Painmaster up....DARKNESS FALLS! Tombstone Piledriver, and a cover! 1...2...3! JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, in a time of 4:54... COVENANT: AND JOHNSON INTERRUPTED BY RED RAVEN AND HOLOCAUST! Both men hit the ring and start wailing away on Damion! O'BRIEN: Red Raven accused of turning his back on Damion in the past, and I guess Raven's making a preemptive strike tonight! COVENANT: Fans, we've got to go....HERE COMES THE DARK SIDE! Hellhound and the Creatures of the Night hit the ring, and out go Red Raven and his partner! O'BRIEN: Yeah, but they just sent a message to the Dark Side, that they're not afraid! Fans, we are out of time, for Jerry Covenant, I'm Chris O'Brien, and we will see you this week at the Carpenter Cup! END HOUR #1
