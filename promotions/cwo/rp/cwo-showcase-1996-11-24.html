The music comes to an end as the CWO SHOWCASE logo fades in... and then the camera pans to Kevin Rinaldi and, unfortunately, the Fabulous Feenix.) KEVIN RINALDI (KR): Hello, everybody, and welcome to CWO Showcase, shortened this week so that we can bring you the Rupaul Thanksgiving special!!! FABULOUS FEENIX (FF): You better work!!!!!! KR: In our dark first match, Tiger Rogers completely dominated and defeated a listless Wayne Carpenter in 6 minutes and 56 seconds. Carpenter was unable to mount any kind of offensive against Rogers. FF: I can't stand any of those Four Aces pansies, but I especially hate Wayne Carpenter! The Ace of Hearts got broke tonight!!!! KR: This card was originally supposed to include a light heavyweight title match between Talon and Barry Hart, but this was canceled when Talon lost the title to Dark Raven. Dark Raven will defend the title next week against an opponent to be determined. But let's go to the ring for our first match! TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. ("2112 Overture" by Rush begins to play as the fans cheer.) TONY THE K: Coming down the aisle, weighing 225 pounds, from Los Angeles. CA, Kamikaze! ("Taps" plays as the fans boo.) TONY THE K: And his opponent, weighing 210 pounds, from Detroit. MI, accompanied by Da Boss, Panther! KR: And Kamikaze leads off with a huge dropkick!!!! Panther hits with the flying headscissors! Panther with a quick cover! 1...2...thr...kick out by Kamikaze! Panther with a headlock, working Kamikaze's neck. He releases the hold and plays to the cro wd--but they aren't buying!! FF: Who was it who said that no one ever went broke underestimating the intelligence of the American people? He'd prove it here! KR: Kamikaze coming up behind him....dropkick!!! And Kamikaze starts whaling on Panther!! Going for the senton....he missed!!!!! Panther stomps on the prone Kamikaze!!! Panther with a camel clutch!!!! Kamikaze in agony!!! Panther releases the hold and stomps away on Kamikaze!!! Going for a flying elbow... nobody home!!!! FF: Kamikaze, like the coward he is, moves out of the way instead of taking his punishment like a man! KR: And I suppose YOU would have let Panther hit you? FF: Well....ah.... KR: Got it....Kamikaze slaps on a figure four leglock!!!!! Panther reaching for the ropes, but he can't reach them! Kamikaze releases the hold, and goes for the plancha! He's up....HE MISSED!!! Panther quickly slaps on the Eclipse! Kamikaze going... . going........gone!!! FF: Good night and good riddance! TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner in 6 minutes and 56 seconds, Panther!!!!!! KR: Panther gets the win over Kamikaze, and he "graciously" wakes the defeated grappler! And we're going to a commercial! ****COMMERCIAL BREAK**** KR: And we're back with our tag matchup of the evening! TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following tag team matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit. ("Country Boy Can Survive" by Hank Williams Junior begins to play as the fans cheer.) TONY THE K: Coming down the aisle, at a combined weight of 629 pounds, accompanied by General Lee, Stonewall Mason, Beauregard Dixon, Mason-Dixon! ("Cantaloop" by US3 plays as the fans boo.) TONY THE K: And their opponents, at a combined weight of 765 pounds, accompanied by Russell Hill, Jim 'Jazzman' Taylor, Jumbo Jenkins, The Big Cool! KR: Mason and Taylor starting the match. Whoa! Taylor just shoved Mason! Mason responds with a chop to Taylor's chest! And the Jazzman tags in Jenkins! Jenkins with a clothesline!!!! Jenkins has him by the hair...and he tosses Stonewall through the ropes and outside the ring!!! Wild brawl outside!!!! Mason the first one back, and he tags Dixon in as Jenkins slithers back into the ring!!! Mason-Dixon send Jenkins into the ropes...double clothesline!!! NO!!! Jenkins clotheslines both of them!!!! And the crowd giving him a piece of their minds! FF: Can they spare it?????? KR: Beauregard Dixon whips Jumbo Jenkins into the corner.....following him in....AVALANCHE! Dixon slaps on a side headlock, and works the big man's neck!!! Double axehandle by Dixon! Has him up....inverted atomic drop!!! And Dixon goes for the Texas Cloverleaf....but Jenkins powers out!!! What a display of strength by Jumbo Jenkins! FF: The Big Cool are two of the finest conditioned athletes in professional sports! The only strength Mason and Dixon have is in their breath!!!! KR: Dixon sends Jenkins into the ropes...clothesline!!! Jenkins is down!!! Dixon rolls him onto his stomach...going for a Boston crab...and again Jenkins powers out!!! And once again, the crowd tells him where to go and what to do when he gets there! FF: And since they're experts at it, they could probably give him pointers, if he ever stooped to their level!! KR: Dixon sends Jenkins into the ropes again.....going for another clothesline....whoops!!! He and Jenkins BOTH go down!!!! And Jenkins is on his feet first! Jenkins with a kick to Dixon's gut!!! Cover! 1 ... kick out by Dixon!!!!! FF: Pure luck! KR: Jenkins back on his feet...falling headbutt!!!!! Cover!! Wait!!! General Lee on the ring apron!!!! The ref tries to get him back to the floor!!! Jenkins has Lee by the shirt! And Beauregard Dixon nails Jumbo Jenkins from behind! FF: Another coward!!! How many yellow bellied scumsuckers are there in the CWO? KR: I can only count one, and I'm looking at him!!! FF: Why you..... KR: Dixon with a grapevine, but he's too close to the ropes...Jenkins grabs them easily. The referee brings both men to their feet. Dixon has Jenkins' wrist... sends him into the ropes...DROPKICK! And Dixon whaling on Jenkins with fists and feet!!!!! Has him back up... clothesline!!!!! And up again... DDT! And AGAIN.....CLOTHESLINE!!!!! And Jenkins heads for the hills!!! FF: Jenkins wisely taking a breather... he's got ten seconds before he's counted out! KR: Dixon going after him....both men's partners and managers getting into it!!! Six-way brawl outside the ring!!!!! The referee calling for the bell! TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, at 7 minutes and 6 seconds, the referee has declared this match a double disqualification! KR: And that's not stopping the fight!!! Security trying to break these guys up!!! And we're going to a commercial! ****COMMERCIAL BREAK**** KR: And we're back! Security did manage to separate the two teams, but there is bad blood there, I can tell you? Let's go to the ring for our last match! TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. ("Sweet Home Alabama" by Lynyrd Skynyrd plays as the fans cheer) TONY THE K: Coming down the aisle, weighing 237 pounds, from Mobile, Alabama, accompanied by General Lee, Johnny Reb! ("Livin' on the Edge" by Aerosmith plays as the fans boo.) TONY THE K: And his opponent, weighing 235 pounds, from Los Angeles, CA, accompanied by Jimbo Jones, David 'The Destroyer' Davis! KR: And Reb attacks Davis before the bell! Reb sending Davis into the ropes... but Davis nails him with a flying clothesline!!!! Reb through the ropes to the floor, and Davis going after him! Davis nails Reb with a chair!!!! Davis going for another s hot....NO! Reb grabs the chair and hits HIM with it!!! Both men continuing to brawl back and forth!!! WAIT! Jimbo Jones nailed Reb from behind!!! How could the ref not have seen that? FF: Seen what? KR: Both men back in the ring, but Reb still reeling from the vicious assault by Jimbo Jones. Davis with the cover! 1...2...Reb gets the shoulder up! FF: Davis is still going to win! He's too strong, too fast, too good, and Reb is just too ugly!!! KR: Davis sends Reb into the ropes....spinning heel kick! Davis gouging the eyes of Johnny Reb! Davis up top....MOONSAULT! Davis has Reb by the hair....and sends him out of the ring!! Once again, Davis going after him!!! Davis slams Reb's head into the ringside steps!!! Davis nails Reb with a chair!!!!! Reb in real trouble!!! Davis rolls him into the ring...and slaps on the STF!!! Reb in agony!!! Davis releases the hold and goes for the pin!!! 1...2...NO! Reb somehow managing to kick out FF: Only delaying the inevitable!! KR: Davis with an elbow...going for another one....REB MOVED OUT OF THE WAY!!!! Where's he getting the strength to do that? Reb stomping away on the prone Davis! Reb going for Jackson's Revenge....HE HITS IT!!!!! Cover! 1...2...thr...NO! Davis gets the shoulder up! FF: Reb using illegal tactics to get that cover! KR: What illegal tactics?????? KR: Have a nice trip back to planet Earth, you freak....Reb has Davis in the air.....he's carrying him to the corner.....seats him on the top buckle....he's going for a superplex!!!! NO!
