SATURDAY NIGHT SPECIAL 12/13/97

15 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWO SATURDAY NIGHT SPECIAL: CWO CELEBRATION '97 12/13/97 "LIVE" FROM THE ORBIT ROOM, GRAND RAPIDS, MI Producer: Ryan Deutscher ( [detail withheld] ) Executive Producer: Brent G. Alles ( [detail withheld] ) (Show begins with Sandman in a dark interview area.) SANDMAN: You now know what happens when you mess with the darkness, Alles. You felt my power when I knocked you out Tuesday night. That was only a preview of our match Saturday night at the orbit room. Falls count anywhere, you're going through the stage, hell I may even bring you outside and onto the East Beltline, or into the mall. Where ever the darkness tells me to take you. Let's go for a falls count ANYWHERE match? Alles, you may have been a match for an old man like Hile Troy, but in this type of match, Darkness will prevail, and believe me, if you survive this match to wrestle another day you will be a changed man, say, a man who only sees darkness. It's gonna feel good to Sandblast you into eternal darkness. LIGHTS OUT! (Johnny Starr is in the ring.) STARR: Ladies and gentleman I am proud to produce one of the stars of this three way dance at CWO CELEBRATION 97. The man who has been dubbed the leader for the New Generation and Cold Pain Enterprises, MONDO GECKO! ("Hip Hop Hooray" by Naughty by Nature plays as the crowd cheers for Gecko as he, Gordy Alles and Tamara Wilcox come to the ring.) STARR: Gecko, you announced to everybody last week about Brent Alles joining CPE and you delivered! Now, with CWO Celebration coming around the corner, you will take on Lord Hile Troy and Johnny Reb in a three-way dance for the world title. We saw on Tuesday what happened as Troy attacked you and then you attacked Reb and Troy while they were brawling, what is your take on this match now? GECKO: For the past three years I have busted my tail off to win titles and represent my fans, my girlfriend, my family, and my mentors to the best of my abilities. Now in the Three Way World Title Match, I get to off the "icons" of the CWO. Bottom line here is that it is time to usher a new generation into the CWO and the CPE is ready to deliver! (Crowd roars as Gecko plays to the crowd.) GECKO: Recently I went over to the CCWA, where Southern Dynamite runs that fed and I captured the WWW Cruiserweight title and the WWW Tag team titles with Col. Taggart. So now I go for the ultimate prize, which is the CWO World title and solidify CPE's p osition here in the CWO. STARR: Gecko, you got to remember that both Lord Hile Troy and Johnny Reb are experienced champions and much older than you! Are you ready for this task? GECKO: Mr. Starr, I have been busting my tail off with Col. Taggart, Brent Alles, and Gordy Alles. Lord Troy, as I said before, you overgrown fossil, you WERE great but I am younger, stronger, and quicker than you! The reason you were gold this past year is because of your smarts and GKI and they are no longer around! Johnny Reb, you were once my bes t friend and I used to respect you! But it seems that Rebel Yell and General Lee have turned you into a coward! Bottom line, you two, is that I have prepared for either of your moves and I will be the next CWO World champion. GET READY FOR MONDO EXTREME ACTION AS CPE IS IN THE HOUSE! GORDY ALLES: Mondo, I was in charge of your original training when you came to the CWO. I have told Lance Russell and I will tell you this, you got what it takes to be a champion, all you need to do is go out there and do it! I am proud to be training you and I can't wait to see you and my son leading the CWO once again! (Gecko and Alles celebrate in the ring as the fans go crazy! Grinding metal music is heard as a montage of hard-core CWO action is shown. The "CWO Saturday Night Special Logo" comes up, and we go to a cheering crowd in Grand Rapids, MI. We join our broadcast team of "Double K" Krash Kelly and Le Ann Stevenson.) KELLY: Hello everyone! I'm "Double K" Krash Kelly and this is Le Ann Stevenson! Welcome to CWO Saturday Night Special! STEVENSON: On the eve of CWO Celebration, we are at the sold out Orbit Room in Grand Rapids, MI! KELLY: Tomorrow night, only on Pay Per View, CWO Celebration will be brought to you from where it all started, the Van Andel Arena, just about ten minutes away from us tonight! STEVENSON: Don't get caught at the last minute, call your local cable company and purchase this tonight as you all know it is sold out! KELLY: On the show tonight there will be a four way tag team match between the Bad Guys, The Creatures of the Night, The Dogs of War, and last but not least, The Pit Bosses! And the really interesting twist to this is the winner will receive a shot at the World Tag Team Titles sometime in January! STEVENSON: In one of the most dangerous matches of wrestling the Sandman will face Brent "The Ace" Alles in a falls count anywhere match! This one could end up anywhere! KELLY: From there on, as a warm up to tomorrow nights main event, there will be a three-way-dance between Joe Eagle, Tsutomu Kobayashi, and Gold Kard! STEVENSON: We will also be having a contest to win two front row tickets and back stage passes to tomorrow night's event! KELLY: We've got to take a break, we will be back with the four way tag team match so stay tuned! (Fade to commercial. When we come back Tony the K is standing in the ring.) TONY THE K: Ladies and gentleman, this opening contest is a four way tag team match. The winners will receive a World Tag Team title shot sometime in January! And now, team number one.... (Bad Guys' theme music plays as the fans boo.) TONY THE K: Coming down the aisle, accompanied by Herbie Versmells, at a total combined weight of 519 pounds, The Bad Guys! (Bad Guys come down to the ring antagonizing the crowd. We go to a scene where the camera opens on the Creatures of the Night standing alone in the DARK with one dim spot light shining on them.) NIGHT CRAWLER: At this Saturday Night Special the Creatures are in a four way dance off to see who gets the title shot at the tag champs. To all you other teams involved we've beaten you all. Pit Bosses, you've come back with the same goal as we have to get the gold and show these pathetic so called tag teams in the CWO what it really means to be the best. You guys had better watch every move you make. For if you make the same mistake as one Brent "the Ace" Alles did and get in the way of the Dark Side you will suffer and you will pay with your souls. Just ask the Sandman what he has in store for Mr. Alles. (Methodical, scary laughter is heard in the distance.) NIGHT SLINGER: Man, I can't wait to see that. Like my partner said, we will come out of this match the winners one way or another and then the Creatures of the Night can take our rightful place at the top of the CWO where we are the longest reigning champions this fed has ever had. Tour number two will be even longer. The master has demanded nothing but gold and new souls. Well tonight he gets 4 maybe 6 new souls to torture and torment and add to his brilliant collection. NIGHT CRAWLER: Bad Guys, Dogs of War, you all are in for a world of pain. The ring is our FOREVER NIGHT. Once inside you will never leave under your own power. BOTH MEN: LONG LIVE THE NIGHT! (Camera fades away as we hear the laughter again then silence pure eerie silence. Back to the Orbit Room, where the lights are now off as the dark music of the Dark Side begins to play as the fans boo their entrance, as they are illuminated by a spotlight.) TONY THE K: And team number two, accompanied by "Master of Darkness" Damion, at a total combined weight of 549 pounds, The Creatures of the Night! (Creatures of the Night make their way down to the ring. The lights go up and the "Imperial March" plays as the fans boo.) TONY THE K: And team number three, accompanied by "Mastermind" Vince DaVinci, at a total combined weight of 476 pounds, The Dogs of War! (Dogs of War make their way down to the ring. "Money Talks" by AC/DC plays as the fans cheer the entrance of the Pit Bosses.) TONY THE K: And the final team, at a total combined weight of 525 pounds, The Pit Bosses! KELLY: And all four teams are in the ring and we are under way here on Saturday Night Special. STEVENSON: Now the Pit Bosses seem to be the only team getting any support from the fans! KELLY: Yes but they are the only team without a manager! Logan Fett of the Dogs of War will start things off with Pit Boss #2! Fett goes for a double ax handle but Pit Boss #2 nails him with a chop... and now a drop kick, Pit Boss #2 with the cover, 1... 2 ... kick out! STEVENSON: And Pit Boss #2 with a near fall as he tags in Eddie Fantastic! KELLY: Logan Fett's partner has been tagged in and they will have to go at it. Now the rules are you have to make some sort of contact before you can tag out. Fantastic bounces off the ropes and runs into his partner, now they tag in the night Crawlers! STEVENSON: There might just be four tag teams breaking up after tonight! KELLY: And the Night Crawlers make contact and Night Crawler makes the tag to Eddie Fantastic. Fantastic jumps into the ring and gets clotheslined down. Night Slinger whips Fantastic into the corner and charges in! STEVENSON: Night Slinger has just connected with lights out and Fantastic slumps over in the corner. Slinger with the cover 1 ... 2 ... 3! TONY THE K: The Dogs of War have been eliminated! KELLY: We're down to three teams as Ron Johnson comes into the ring and nails Night Slinger with a hard right. Now with the head lock applied! STEVENSON: Both Bad Guys had yet to be in the ring so they are pretty rested! KELLY: Slinger back to his feet, sticks a thumb into Johnson's eye. Slinger makes the tag to Pit Boss #1! STEVENSON: Pit Boss #1 with a single leg take down and applies the figure four but Joe Rucker in to make the save! KELLY: Johnson back to his feet nails Pit Boss #1 with a drop kick.. bounces off the ropes and drops a leg across Pit Boss #1's face! STEVENSON: Johnson tags his partner in and they double team Pit Boss #1! Pit Boss #2 get into the ring... and now the Creatures of the Night join the action, all three teams are going at it! KELLY: The ref finally being able to get some order in this match as Pit Boss #1 makes the tag to Night Crawler! Rucker not wasting any time and takes Night Crawler down with a powerful clothesline! Rucker stomps away on the head of Night Crawler! STEVENSON: Rucker just dropkicked Night Crawler into his partner, Night Crawler is all alone! Ron Johnson coming in the ring and they attack the Pit Bosses! Now the Bad Guys have Night Crawler set up, Bad Guys Bash, and Rucker with the cover, 1 ... 2 ... 3! TONY THE K: The Creatures of the Night have been eliminated! KELLY: MOD Damion now has to wait for their chance at the tag team gold as his team has been eliminated and we are down to two teams! STEVENSON: Pit Boss #1 comes in and takes control of the match! KELLY: Pit Boss #1 sends Rucker into the ropes... flying forearm! Pit Boss #1 with the cover 1 ... 2 ... kick out! STEVENSON: And Rucker just getting his shoulder up, Pit Boss #1 applies a headlock. KELLY: Rucker back to his feet and Pit Boss #1 makes the tag to Ron Johnson! STEVENSON: Now the bad Guys have to face each other! KELLY: Johnson pushes Rucker! And Rucker pushes Johnson. STEVENSON: The Bad Guys are not tagging out, there actually taking it to each other. KELLY: The Bad Guys are a crafty team, I bet they are just setting up the Pit Bosses. Now Johnson tags in Pit Boss #1 and superkicks Pit Boss #2! STEVENSON: The Bad Guys are double teaming Pit Boss 1! Now they have him set up... Bad Guys Bash, folks, it's all over, 1 ... 2 ... 3! TONY THE K: Ladies and gentleman, the Pit Bosses have been eliminated, the winners of this contest, The Bad Guys! KELLY: And the Bad Guys come away with this four way tag team match and win the right to face the World Tag Team Champions sometime in the next month, now lets take you to Johnny Starr with these comments from Brent Alles. (Johnny Starr is standing in the back stage area with Brent "The Ace" Alles.) STARR: Thank you Krash, I am with Brent "The Ace" Alles as he prepares for his falls count anywhere match with the Sandman. Brent, what are your thoughts? ALLES: I know this is a falls count anywhere match, and I know what kind of a man the Sandman is, but I don't care, I'm gonna wipe up the Orbit Room with his sorry ass! And then tomorrow night I will be at ringside when Mondo Gecko wins the CWO World Title! CPE 4 LIFE! (Fade to commercial. When we come back, Sandman is in the interview area.) SANDMAN: Alles, today we have a match, falls count anywhere in the orbit room. We've been going at it for the past couple of weeks. I say we end this feud here today. Let's make this match, falls count ANYWHERE, not just in the orbit room. I could pin you in the middle of East Beltline, or in the mall even, it don't matter. But I can tell you this Alles, after this match, after you have seen the darkness, you will be a changed man, there will be a dark side to you. Once you see the darkness, a man can never go back. You will eternally have the darkness in your mind. Today Alles, Grand Rapids, Michigan, your final resting place. LIGHTS OUT! (Tony the K is in the ring along with referee Kendall Dolemeyer.) TONY THE K: Ladies and gentleman, this next contest is scheduled for one fall. It will be falls count anywhere! ("Enter Sandman" by Metallica plays as the lights go off, the fans booing.) TONY THE K: From parts unknown, weighing 355 pounds, The Sandman! (Sandman makes his entrance, illuminated by a spotlight.) STEVENSON: The Sandman walking down the aisle and Brent Alles charges down and nails him with a chair! KELLY: Alles not waiting for his entrance, with a chair shot on Sandman, may have knocked him out! STEVENSON: This match is falls count anywhere in Grand Rapids, MI, so this could go anywhere! KELLY: Alles has Sandman up... and drops him across the steel barricade! Alles now pounding away! STEVENSON: Sandman is swinging but he's not connecting! KELLY: But Alles is connecting with a clothesline! Now the two are in the backstage area. We had a special camera set up to follow all the action! STEVENSON: Alles slams Sandman onto a table... Now Alles climbs on top of a latter and leaps... but Sandman rolls out of the way! KELLY: Alles just went crashing through that table and I think he's busted open! STEVENSON: Sandman has Alles and is dragging him down the aisle... Sandman slams Alles into the ring steps! KELLY: Now Sandman sends Alles flying into the guard rail and Alles flips over it! STEVENSON: Sandman and Alles are fighting through the crowd! Now they are in the front of the arena! KELLY: Alles with a low blow, he grabs a kettle.. Oh my god, Alles just dumped hot pop corn grease all over the Sandman, as he is screaming in pain! STEVENSON: Alles with a drop kick and Sandman goes flying head first through the glass doors of the Orbit room! KELLY: They have battled out side to the streets of Grand Rapids! Alles whips the Sandman but its reversed and Alles is sent flying into a parking meter! STEVENSON: This match has gotten out of hand as they brawl out side! KELLY: Wait a minute.. Sandman has just taken Alles into the mall. They are fighting in the mall next door! STEVENSON: Hey come on guys, now is not the time for Christmas shopping! KELLY: They are in the food court in the mall and Sandman has just nailed the sandblast on a table! This match has got to be over! 1 ... 2 ... thr... (A nearby woman picks up a chair and breaks it over the Sandman's back.) KELLY: And a fan breaks up the pin and the Sandman is outraged! STEVENSON: He had the match won and a fan broke it up! KELLY: Now the Sandman is chasing after the woman! STEVENSON: Alles has gotten up.. KELLY: He nails Sandman in the stomach... Alles Breaker! 1 ... 2 ... 3! (Back in the Orbit Room, Tony The K announces the winner) TONY THE K: Ladies and gentleman the winner of this contest, Brent "The Ace" Alles! KELLY: Alles gets the win in a wild contest that ended up in the mall next door! STEVENSON: I hope no one was hurt but that's the price you have to pay if you get involved in the CWO action. KELLY: Now we move onto our main event. It will be a three way dance that is sort of a warm up to tomorrow nights main event but don't get me wrong, this match is a main event in its own right! We have to take a break but when we return, the main event.... (Fade to commercial.) JOHNNY STARR VOICE OVER: Tomorrow night, live on Pay-Per-View, the event that will bring 1997 to a close, CWO Celebration.... (CWO World Championship Graphic comes up) STARR V-O: For the CWO World Championship, a three way dance between champion Johnny Reb, Mondo Gecko, and Lord Hile Troy! (CWO World Tag Team Championship Graphic comes up.) STARR V-O: For the CWO World Tag Team Championship, the Wild Cards take on the Deadly Sins! (CWO North American Championship Graphic comes up.) STARR V-O: For the CWO North American Championship, Masher Maccabee takes on Fergus McTavish, the Whirling Scottish Devil! (North American Tag Team Championship Graphic comes up.) STARR V-O: For the CWO North American Tag Team Championship, Foolkiller and "Ultimate Heel" Union Caine take on "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum and "Diamondback" Chris Myers! (CWO World Television Championship Graphic comes up.) STARR V-O: For the CWO World Television Championship, in a casket match, Bloodbath takes on Wildfire! (CWO Light Heavyweight Championship Graphic comes up.) STARR V-O: For the CWO Light Heavyweight Championship, SJPW's Masashi "Lightening" Lanther takes on Jason "Flash" Varga! And other great action, so don't wait until the eleventh hour, order CWO CELEBRATION today! (When we come back, Tony The K is in the ring along with referee John Myering.) TONY THE K: Ladies and gentleman, the following contest is the three way dance! ("Ride of the Valkryies" by Wagner plays as the fans boo.) TONY THE K: Coming down the aisle, accompanied by "Master of Darkness" Damion, from Berlin, Germany, weighing 245 pounds, Gold Kard! (Gold Kard and Master of Darkness Damion make their way down to the ring. SJPW's theme music plays as the fans cheer.) TONY THE K: And second, from Nagoya, Japan, weighing 235 pounds, representing the SJPW, Tsutomu Kobayashi! (Kobayashi makes his way down to the ring. "MotownPhilly" by BoyzIIMen plays as the fans cheer.) TONY THE K: And finally, accompanied by Russell Hill, from Philadelphia, PA, weighing 320 pounds, Joe Eagle! KELLY: Now we are to our main event as Joe Eagle wastes no time and starts in on Gold Kard! STEVENSON: No love loss between them two as Kobayashi nails Eagle with a drop kick, and now he's pounding away on Gold Kard. Gold Kard not getting off to a good start here in this contest. KELLY: Kobayashi whips Gold Kard into the ropes... high back body drop! STEVENSON: And now Eagle nails Kobayashi with a drop kick! Eagle's putting the boots to Kobayashi! KELLY: Gold Kard comes from behind and nails Eagle with a double axhandle! Now Gold Kard and Kobayashi with the double team on Eagle! STEVENSON: They whip Eagle into the ropes... and Eagle clotheslines both men! Eagle has erupted! KELLY: Eagle just wailing away on both men! STEVENSON: Eagle nails Kobayashi with a drop kick and he falls out of the ring! KELLY: Eagle and Gold Kard taking it to each other now! Kobayashi going up top, nails Eagle with a flying drop kick! STEVENSON: Eagle is down and now Gold Kard is going up top... Die Flieder Kross! Gold Kard with the cover, 1 ... 2 ... 3! TONY THE K: Joe Eagle has been eliminated from this contest! KELLY: Gold Kard is celebrating but wait a minute, Kobayashi is going up behind Gold Kard, he has it on! The Jumbo Sleeper and Gold Kard is going out! The ref is calling for the bell, Gold Kard is out cold! TONY THE K: Ladies and gentleman the winner of this contest Tsutomu Kobayashi! KELLY: And Tsutomu Kobayashi taking advantage of Gold Kard having his back turned and won the match! STEVENSON: Now tonight has truly been an action packed night! KELLY: It sure has but that's all the time we have, so for Le Ann Stevenson, I'm "Double K" Krash Kelly, and we will see you tomorrow night at CWO Celebration! (Closing creds come up as we fade out.)
