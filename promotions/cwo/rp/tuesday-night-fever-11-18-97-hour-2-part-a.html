CWO TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER 11/18/97 TAPED IN GLASGOW, SCOTLAND (5 HOUR TIME DIFFERENCE) RATING: TV-14 (L, V) EXECUTIVE PRODUCER: Brent G. Alles ( [detail withheld] ) (Fade into a spinning globe, as the CWO logo is slowly superimposed over it.) VOICE-OVER: The CWO... providing wrestling excellence the world over.... (Camera shot opens on Fergus McTavish still on crutches standing on the coastal shoreline with his wee pal Billy standing beside him.) Soon enough laddie, soon enough. But first we gots some business ta take care of, doan we? BILLY (smiles): Yeah, we sure do. MCTAVISH: Oy, Musher Macaroon! Been takin' da bus around town 'ave we? Wonder why? BILLY: Here, maybe this is the reason. (Billy hands Fergus a crumpled and bent piece of metal.) When an where ya ain't gonna know, but one way or another, I'll nail yer sorry arse I will. Oh, an' one more ting..... (Fergus turns his back to the camera and lifts up his kilt, mooning the camera.)
