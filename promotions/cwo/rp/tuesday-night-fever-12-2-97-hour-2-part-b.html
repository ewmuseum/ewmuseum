TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER 12/2/97 - HOUR #2 - PART B

3 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Logo and music come up as we fade to commercial. When we come back, Johnny Starr is in the ring.) JOHNNY STARR: Ladies and gentlemen, at NOVEMBER PAIN, the World Title again found itself shrouded in controversy with Johnny Reb narrowly escaping the onslaught of Sandman Jr. Ladies and Gentlemen, please join me in welcoming to the ring the reigning CWO World Heavyweight Champion. JOHNNY REB! ("Sweet Home Alabama" by Lynyrd Skynyrd plays as a mixed crowd reaction is heard. Accompanying Johnny Reb to the ring are his manager General Lee, his trainer, former CWO World Heavyweight Champion Rebel Yell, and his cousins, Stonewall Mason and Beauregard Dixon, the team of Mason-Dixon. Johnny Reb has a bandage on his forhead covering the 9 stitches he received as a result of a chair shot by Sandman Jr. in their match at NOVEMBER PAIN.) STARR: Well, Johnny Reb, it seems that your grasp on that World Title Belt survived yet another night. But your victory was not without controversy yet again. Would you like to comment on the involvement of Master of Darkness Damion in your match? JOHNNY REB: What am ah supposed ta say Mistuh Starr??? How long have y'all been with this league? Ah know it's longuh than ah have. And y'all know that ah hate no one more than Damion. But ah am not in a position to tell the man how and when ta come. If he's got a thing for someone's lady, that's his business. (Chorus of boos heard from the crowd) REB: Now there are a couple of othuh things that Ah'd like ta address here. First of all, ah would like to congratulate the Deadly Sins for bringing the CWO World Tag Team Titles back around the waists of a CWO team. (Mixed reaction heard from the crowd, with an audible "UNIT" chant heard in the distance) REB: And ah also would like to say something ta Sandman Jr. Y'all gave me a hell of a match. Y'all gave me all that ah wanted. But ah proved once and for all, that ah am the best WRESTLER in the CWO. Junior, y'all earned mah respect as a fighter, but y'all still ain't good enough as a wrestler. (Boos heard from the crowd) REBEL YELL: YEEEE-HAAAWWW. Mistuh Starr, is there any doubt now that mah man is the greatest World Champion that the CWO has evuh seen? He has turned away all comers. He has defended the belt all around the world. He is the embodiment of all that is good about wrestling. He is a true champion through and through. KYLE MELINN: I hope Johnny Starr has his hip waders with him, because it's getting pretty deep in there. DAVE THOMAS: Shut up, Melinn. That's our champion in the ring!! STARR: With all of that aside Mr. Yell, Johnny Reb now moves on to CWO Celebration, where he will face an as of yet unknown opponent. YELL: That makes no difference to us Mistuh Starr. We have taken on all comers. We have taken out all that the CWO has thrown at us. At CWO CELEBRATION, nothin's gonna change. This man is the greatest wrestler in the history of the CWO, and at the next Pay Per View, he will be the UNDISPUTED greatest of all time. STARR: How can you say that when you don't even know who you're man is going to face? We still haven't received word from Commissioner Tom Krepps as to who the #1 Contender is. REB: Well there's no bettuh tahm than the present to find that out. Krepps, y'all haul your carcass out here now and tell me who I get ta kick the hell out of at the Pay Per View! (Tom Krepps enters.) THOMAS: It's been a rough night for ol' Kreppsie! He'd better watch out, or Team Johnny Reb will knock him silly too! MELINN: I doubt it, after seeing what Krepps did to the Unit earlier tonight. He truly stands for law and order. THOMAS: Yeah, right. He's as crooked as they come! (Krepps enters the ring.) STARR: Well, Commissioner Krepps, everybody here in the CWO, all the fans, are awaiting the results of the balloting. Who will face Johnny Reb at CWO CELEBRATION? KREPPS: Well, first of all, Johnny, let me just say thank you to everybody that voted. We received many votes, but in fact something quite amazing happened. There was a tie. STARR: A tie? KREPPS: That's right, a tie. REB (grabbing the mike): Fahn by me. Have those two boys fight and the winner can take me on. KREPPS: You know, we thought about doing that. But let me tell you something, "Johnny." You have a little problem in the respect department. Seems like every title defense you've had has been mired in controversy. So, we're going to give you a chance to get some respect. REB: What the hell do you mean? KREPPS: I mean this: you're going to face both of the men who tied, three-way dance! (Crowd cheers as Johnny Reb reacts angrily.) REB: No way! KREPPS: Yes way, because if you don't, I'll strip you of the title! (Crowd cheers again.) THOMAS: This guy has delusions of grandeur! REB: All right, ya got me over a barrel. So who the hell am ah gonna face? KREPPS: Well, one of the men will make the fans really happy. He's a fine young man who many have said deserved a shot for a long time. Ladies and gentlemen, one of the men who will face Johnny Reb for the World title in the three-way dance... Mondo Gecko! (Crowd cheers wildly.) MELINN: How about that! Mondo Gecko finally gets his shot! THOMAS: Big deal. He's still a loser! REB: All right, no big deal, ah've beaten that punk before, ah'll beat him again. Who's the other one? KREPPS: The other man you're going to face might not make everybody happy, but truly, if there is one man who has been a symbol for this federation, it is this man: ladies and gentlemen, the second man who will face Johnny Reb in the three-way dance for t he title at CWO CELEBRATION... Lord Hile Troy! (Crowd cheers wildly.) REB: What?!? No way! No way in hell! KREPPS: Read 'em and weep, pal. We'll see you at CWO CELEBRATION! (Krepps leaves the ring as the crowd continues to cheer, Johnny Reb still stomping angrily in the ring.) MELINN: Well, there you have it, folks, Mondo Gecko... Lord Hile Troy... Johnny Reb... three-way dance, the World Championship on the line, all at CWO CELEBRATION '97, which now may be the biggest event of the year! Folks, we're out of time, we'll see yo u next week!
