We are a member of FPWO LAST WEEK ON FEVER… 1. Wildfire was shown arguing with Commissioner David “The Truth” Thomas. Thomas tells him in order to get a World title shot, he has to face Richard
Gracie that night.
1. Allen Carter had words of wisdom for the crowd, declaring himself not the Television Champion, but the “Internet” Champion only to be rebuked by World
Champion Damion, who told Carter he would put the world title on the line that night.
1. Matushiro Torunaka defeated Philly Flyer. 1. Pit Bosses had comments about their match with the Magic Group. 1. Pit Bosses defeated the Magic Group. They Spike Piledrove both Wizard and Necromancer, AND their manager Avitar! They even threw the dwarven
Apprentice into the crowd! All four men were sent to the hospital.
1. The Pit Bosses reveled in their newly revealed “heeldom”, claiming that what they did to the Magic Group was “strictly business.” 1. Wildfire defeated “The Ultimate Fighting Machine” Richard Gracie to get a title shot. This came after interference by Christopher “Spot” Light. Light also attacked Wildfire after the match, as partial revenge for what
Wildfire did to him at SUMMER HEAT 2000.
1. Light was then back in Kent Alles’ office, yukking it up with the CFO of the CWO. Wildfire tried to attack him, only to encounter the Pit Bosses, who beat him up and threw him through a plate glass window.
1. Thomas had an angry confrontation with Kent Alles. It was apparent that neither man totally trusted the other. 1. World Tag Team Champions The Beauregard Boys defeated Gold Kard and Brian Derks by disqualification after Gold Kard hit the Beauregards with the title belt. Gold Kard and Derks beat up the Boys after the match.
1. Foolkiller (who returned on the 7/25/00 edition of FEVER) had an interview, only to be interrupted by the arrogant Christopher Light. Light angered Foolkiller enough to be choke slammed, and then Foolkiller called for a ref, to “show this punk up right.” Out came a grinning Kent Alles with a referee shirt on, only to be stopped by Commissioner Thomas, who said that since Kent didn’t have a referee’s license, he couldn’t be one. Kent stayed at ringside, though, and eventually pulled out the actual referee and laid him out after Foolkiller delivered the Thunderfunk to Light and had a sure win. Foolkiller chokeslammed Kent after the match, but the Pit Bosses then attacked Foolkiller. The surprisingly returned Magic Group (Wizard and
Necromancer only, coming back in the ambulance that took Wildfire away) made the save!
1. World Champion Damion defeated Television (“Internet”) Champion Allen Carter in a well-fought match. Damion eventually secured the Darkness Falls for the win. (CWO opening comes up… a montage of CWO action with intense music in the background. The CWO logo is displayed.)
