CWO REPORT 4/2/97

3 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control room.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Wednesday, April 1, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night was another explosive TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER for the CWO, let's look at what happened in our FEVER FLASHBACK! _______________________________________________________________________________ FEVER FLASHBACK (4/1/97 MATCH ONE: Jason "Flash" Varga pinned Centurion with a rollup in 14:27. After the match an enraged Centurion delivered the devastating Arakkis Slingshot Brainbuster to Varga. In-ring interview with Wayne Schillo in which he enigmatically discusses some of his recent personal problems and states that he will not be in the ROCK that weekend because he wants to take some time off. Doomsday then entered, and Maximum Overdrive confronted Schillo about this, saying that Doomsday had been watching his backs for month, and that he basically owed them. Schillo then agreed to fight with Doomsday against the Unholy Alliance and Deadly Sins in THE ROCK at APRIL ARMAGEDDON. MATCH TWO: Masters of Hardcore (Ohlhoff and Couchman) defeated Bloodbath and Wildfire in 11:41 when Ohlhoff made Wildfire submit to a figure four sleeper after Wildfire was struck by steel chairs by the North American Tag Team Champions the Bad Guys. Johnny Reb had some comments about his match with Gold Kard at APRIL ARMAGEDDON before his match tonight on FEVER. MATCH THREE: Johnny Reb and Masher Maccabee wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. David "The Truth" Thomas accompanied Maccabee down to the ring, and the pair argued with the referee for more time after the time limit expired, giving Reb the chance to knock their heads together and clothesline them with the Confederate flagpole. Segment with Mondo Gecko in training for his match against Masher Maccabee this Sunday at APRIL ARMAGEDDON. MATCH FOUR: The Focus defeated CWO Television Champion Mondo Gecko by disqualification when Masher Maccabee attacked the Focus. Thomas then joined Maccabee in an attack on Mondo until Johnny Reb stormed the ring, driving them off, though not before Tamara Wilcox was able to spin kick, yes spin kick, David "The Truth" Thomas. MATCH FIVE: The Highlanders defeated "Lonestar" Mike Williams and Kurt "Thrill from the Chill" Gerstung in 13:40 when Gavin pinned Gerstung after a "rocket launcher" by his partner Conor. The Masters of Hardcore were standing on the runway during this match, scouting, and were attacked by the Eternal Wanderers after the match. The Highlanders joined the fray, and a three-way brawl was had back to the dressing room. Comments from Brian "The Hammer" Williams about his match for the World title tonight against Bane. MATCH SIX: A World title match between Brian "The Hammer" Williams and champion Bane never took place, as Brent "The Ace" Alles attacked Williams before the match started. Bane, who had vowed to punish Alles is he ever interfered in his matches again, then attacked Alles, but was drawn off in a fight against "Monster" Rich Clark. Alles then dragged Williams to the ring and was going to Allesbreaker him on to a steel chair (the action that injured Williams for several weeks before) except that Brian's girlfriend and valet, Amanda Bloom, ran down to the ring and shielded Brian's body with her own. Alles then had some derogatory comments for the pair before slamming the microphone on Williams and walking away. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That FEVER of course beginning the countdown to this Sunday, when APRIL ARMAGEDDON hits PPV. Before that, though, the CWO has some great house shows lined up, starting with Roanoke, VA, tonight! Let's look at the lineup for this great card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW 4/2/97 Roanoke, VA Mercenaries vs. Creatures of the Night The Focus vs. Chris Greco Arlon Flaym vs. El Grande Pistolerro Mason-Dixon vs. Eternal Wanderers Wrath vs. Wildfire WORLD 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Wild Bunch (Mondo Gecko and the Regulators) vs. Doomsday (Baby Waldo and the Mercenaries, champions) WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): David "The Destroyer" Davis/Inferno vs. Pit Bosses (champions) TRIANGLE MATCH (no titles on the line): "Monster" Rich Clark vs. North American champion Goliath vs. World champion Bane _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: We now turn to comments from CWO superstars. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Sandman Jr. is in the interview area, looking really, REALLY insane. Kellen is nowhere to be found). SANDMAN JR.: TIGER ROGERS! I had you running, but Jones had to get all up in our business! Son, next time, I am gonna hurt you so bad you won't even remember your name! But first, Vlad the Impaler... hey Vlad, since you're hanging out with that freak Serpentmaster, what exactly are you impaling, anyway? Mr. Salami? I'm gonna crush you, and then it's on to April Armageddon, where AGAIN I AM NOT ON THE PAY PER VIEW, and I get to face the Focus. Well, I'm only gonna be 'focused' on one thing: KICKING YOUR @$$! 'Cause I am Sandman Jr., the master, and the ruler, of the WORLD! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Sloth, Pride, and Wrath are in the interview area, looking over what's on the agenda for this week.) WRATH: I see I am the first to go this week, as I bring havoc and pain to Wildfire. Wildfire is beloved by the fans, but not by the Sins. And the Sins are the only ones whose opinions matter. Wildfire, your fire will be doused, and you will get the Rack! PRIDE: You tell him, Wrath. Looks like later on, we get to totally destroy those losers Greco and Lightning. You two aren't even worth our time, so we're going on to other things. SLOTH: S'right! Seems Wrath will be pulling double duty come April Armageddon! He'll be with us, but he'll also be on Showcase. WRATH: Arlon Flaym, another one of the fire. His fire too will be extinguished. 'Cause I am Wrath, he is nothing. SLOTH: Very poetic. Now, THE ROCK. Me and Pride won this last year, with some good teammates, and some not-so-good ones. Looks like more of the same this year. PRIDE: Me, you, Wrath, Vlad, Serpentmaster (he better not touch me), Dark Raven, Hellhound... can you ask for better? But look at the other schmucks with us: Panther, Mason and Dixon, and that guy who REALLY needs an ass-kicking, Moses. SLOTH: But that's OK, you see, because that moron Maximum Overdrive's team is on the other side. M. O., Waldo, and Mercs, be prepared to get destroyed, and we'll be running in the money. WOO-HOO! _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Wild Bunch is in the interview area.) EL GRANDE PISTOLLERO: Arlon Flaym, you are a top notch opponent, mi amigo, and I am honored to get the chance to face you. But be prepared, for I am making it known that I am ready to pursue my dream, which is to become the next Light Heavyweight champion. I am ready to unleash my arsenal on all those who get in my way and I am ready to take to the air and win the title for my fans and my family. Arlon Flaym, you're a tough competitor but nothing is going to stop EL GRANDE PISTOLERRO, VIVA CWO! MONDO GECKO: Baby Waldo and the Mercenaries, you better polish those belts up real well because we are ready to become the new Six Man Champions. The Wild Bunch needs some more gold in our trophy case and the fans of the CWO are tired of you bums as champs. You guys have escaped through the skin of your teeth and we feel it is time of a changing of the guard. REGULATOR FIRE: Doomsday, you guys have cheated and suckerpunched everybody in the CWO and we are gonna put a stop to it when we get you guys in the ring. Last time you guys used every cheap trick in the book and we were not ready for it. This time, however, we got the TV champion teaming with us and we are ready to become champions. You three punks are in for a world of hurt because we are ready to take the gold. Tell 'em, Ice. REGULATOR ICE: WELL, it goes a lot like this, Doomsday. Mercenaries, we have beaten you guys twice and we will destroy you two thugs again. Mondo here can beat up Crybaby Waldo any day of the week and will do so when we take you punks on. It is going to be a long day for you three losers because we will destroy you and take the belts back to the CWO fans. GET READY FOR REGULATION! _______________________________________________________________________________ (THE MASTERS OF HARDCORE sit in their locker room, pouring over the nights events in their heads. They think about once again breaking a table in their match, and then, by all means, letting the Bad Guys interfere. They are thinking about Sunday, and their triangle match against The Eternal Wanderers, and The Highlanders. They sit, quietly, until "Mr. Exreme" opens his mouth.) "MR. EXTREME" CHRIS OHLHOFF: All right, there seems to be a problem with comprehension on the part of certain teams in the CWO. By saying this, I am referring DIRECTLY to the so-called Bad Guys. Look, "Bad Guys", last time someone tried to interfere in one of our matches, we put them through a table, right, Wanderers? WE DON'T LIKE IT WHEN PEOPLE STICK THEIR NOSES IN OUR BUSINESS. It's as simple as that. Now you see, you will be paid back for that one. That I guarantee. BRAD "THE MAN" COUCHMAN: That's right, Bad Guys. We ain't here to play games. We are here to win the gold, and we can't do that if @$$holes like yourselves continue to interfere in our matches. There is just one thing about it that bothers us, really. We have no problem with a gang beating situation, no problem with two guys beating up two other guys that they don't like, no. Our problem, which the Wanderers found out, is that they COST us the match. If not for them, we'd be 4-0 now, instead of 3-1. That's our problem. "BEAUTIFUL" BRIAN CLARK: Bad Guys, The Masters want you. They want you in the ring, as soon as possible! BRAD "THE MAN" COUCHMAN: That's right. You see, we've already proved our skill, but for you, there is no escape. Wanderers, Highlanders, prepare for the beatings of your lifetimes, because we are the ones who'll give them to ya. We've beaten the Highlanders to bloody pulps before in our debut match on FIGHTIN', but on Sunday, the whole is watching, and we are ready to show everyone, just why we are THE MASTERS OF HARDCORE! (They grab their stuff, shove the cameraman out of the way, and take off. Fade to black.) _______________________________________________________________________________ ("Monster" Rich Clark is standing in the rain, out in the middle of a forest. The rain beads up on his bald head, and then drips down off his face. He is wearing a long, leather trench coat, which by now is soaking wet. Beside him is the mysterious masked man, who stands just over 6 feet tall, but is dwarfed by the 7'4" Clark. He is also dressed all in black. They stand there, doing nothing, until Rich breaks the silence.) "MONSTER" RICH CLARK: Bane, Goliath, tonight you two and I face off in a triangle match. Although one man will possibly get his hand raised in victory, there can be no winner of such a match. My hatred for both of you consumes my every waking moment, my every thought, and every dream. Bane, on Sunday you and I will face off for the title. That match has already been decided. Goliath, on March 7th, I took you all the way to the beach in our match, and Bane's interference cost me a win, as well as my North American Title. No more talking, no more listening, just pain. That's all that's left in this for all three of us. There is no possible way out for either of you, because I am a machine, programmed to win the CWO World Heavyweight Title. I used to have only one dream, and it was to win that title. Now I have two, one for the title, and the other is to see to it that neither of you ever walk again, that is, IF I chose to let you live. ("Monster" Rich Clark whispers something to the masked man, and the two of them begin to walk off to the background. Fade to black.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Moses is inside the synagouge, reciting some bible verses in ancient Hebrew. He sees the camera, and pauses for a second. He then gets up, and goes outside, motioning for the camera to follow him. Once outside, he begins to tell his story.) MOSES: All right. I have been here in the CWO since last year in October. In that time, my brother Savior and I lost only four times in tag team competition, and I have only lost four times in singles competition. Now, I am involved in the ROCK, in which I am forced to team with such heathens as The Unholy Alliance, and The Deadly Sins. Yesterday, I saw Wayne Schillo whining about having to team up with people whom he doesn't trust. Look at my team, and see what I have to deal with. No matter, the prestige of winning will help me on my quest for the CWO World Heavyweight Title. Now, on to more pressing matters. There is a man who calls himself Gold Kard. Now, I have put up with a lot of crap in my life for being Jewish, but never have I ever been simply called Jew. Gold Kard, you are an anti-Semitic b******, and a NAZI to boot. You see, I wish to issue you a challenge. You and me, in the ring, fighting one on one. Scientific test of skill. That way, neither of us can complain about the other winning. I've beaten you once, Gold Kard, and I will do it again. Prepare to be brought down, because I will be victorious. There is no one who can compare to me when it comes to pure ability. Few have even come close, but they were proven frauds. This is the end of it all, Gold Kard. Are you ready to accept? (Moses gets into his car, a black Cadillac, and drives off. Fade to black.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Eternal Wanderers are in a remote section in the Andes Mountains. Exile and Exodus are standing by.) EXODUS (a smirk on his face): So... Masters of Hardcore, did you like that? Did you like what happened? You don't realize what you've done, do you? You have sealed your fate... you have doomed your life. Eternal (smiles wickedly) Wanderers. We are the former North American Tag Team title holders... and we are going to be World Champions once again. EXILE (a dark sinister look on his face): Masters of Hardcore, the door between sanity and insanity is but an inch thick... (lifts his fingers, motioning an inch between them) and the door you have opened has let out these monsters! (A wild gleeful laugher escapes his lips and he suddenly turns livid.) EXILE: Come... walk the aisle... (a wild laughter escapes his lips) take a walk... (laughs hysterically) on the dark side! (Exodus has a concerned look on his face for Exile, who falls on his knees, a wild look in his eyes. Exodus pats him on the back, calming him.) EXODUS: Masters Of Hardcore... (a cold, deathlike look on his face) your Darkness comes. Hardcore... yes... (a thin lip smile) we will see. And Highlanders... we have not forgotten you. (His eyes ablaze as Exile starts to bang his head on the floor.) EXODUS: Your Quickening is upon you... and this time, there will only be one.. and that is the Eternal Wanderers! (Holding back Exile, Exodus starts to walk back to the fire... as the scene fades.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today. We'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT!
