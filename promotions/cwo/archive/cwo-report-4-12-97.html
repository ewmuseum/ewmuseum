CWO REPORT 4/12/97

12 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control center.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Saturday, April 12, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the CWO's Orient Express tour continued with a stop-over in Taipei, Taiwan. Let's look at what happened at last night's card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 4/11/97 Taipei. Taiwan Brian 'The Hammer' Williams pinned Brent 'The Ace' Alles after the Keuka Smack (ear ringer and an uppercut). During the match Maximum Overdrive came to ringside and stole Amanda Bloom away, which was a major distraction to Alles. Bloom was later found unharmed in the locker room. Time of match: 16:28. Johnny Reb pinned Dark Raven after administering the Texas Cloverleaf. Dark Raven would not submit, so Johnny Reb turned him over for the pin. Time of match: 15:06. Mason-Dixon and Tiger Rogers/David 'The Destroyer' Davis wrestled to a 30 minute time limit draw. Wrath made El Grande Pistolerro submit to the Repentence Rack. Time of match: 13:51. Brother Justice defeated Da Hood Rats when Jerome Justice pinned Warren after the dropkick. Time of match: 8:11. LIGHTS OUT MATCH: Inferno defeated Panther after pinning him with the Inferno's Blaze. Panther was then unable to meet a ten count. Time of match: 30:52. The Big Cool defeated Highlanders when Jumbo Jenkins pinned Conor McCloud after the Star Flash (backbreaker by Jazzman, flying splash by Jumbo). Time of match: 7:19. CWO NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION TITLE MATCH: Jason 'Flash' Varga defeated champion Goliath by disqualification when Tiger Rogers attacked. Time of match: 14:29. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Tonight is the big Tokyo Dome show, let's look at the lineup for that great card. _______________________________________________________________________________ SATURDAY, APRIL 12, 1997 TOKYO, JAPAN Deadly Sins vs. David "The Destroyer" Davis and Inferno Steel vs. Sandman Jr. Goliath & Tiger Rogers vs. Mason-Dixon Gold Kard vs. Johnny Reb Vlad the Impaler and Serpentmaster vs. Big Cool NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): The Bad Guys vs. Wild Ones (Mondo Gecko and El Grande Pistolerro) STEEL CAGE: Bishop vs. Master of Darkness Damion WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Pit Bosses (champions) vs. Highlanders WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: Brian "The Hammer" Williams vs. "Monster" Rich Clark (champion) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We now turn to comments from CWO superstars, starting with The Focus. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Focus is in the interview area.) THE FOCUS: Sandman Jr... the Extreme Icon, heh? Well, the fact of the matter is that I gave you a hell of a hard time in our match. I deserve another title shot, but it's not going to be for a little while. In the meantime, do you want to get hardcore with me? I'll bring some chairs, you bring a table, and I'll kick your @$$ until you cry. Never run your mouth to a man, Junior. Mr. Prez, what do you say? Sign the match. Me and him, in any special stipulation match you can dream of. After I beat you, Junior, my string of wins are going to start again, and then I'll beat Tiger Rogers, and Masher, because I'm drinking milk... oops. Simply put, that's the bottom line. Junior, you've been invited... bring me a gift, I'd love to see you try. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We haven't heard from them in a while, but now Ganglord and Da Hood Rats break the silence. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera fades into Ganglord and Da Hood Rats, who are not looking very pleased.) GANGLORD: This is getting to be a lot of bull, guys, we haven't won in a long time here, seems like forever, so from now on I got a new plan: we are gonna take matters into our own hands. If I see things startin' to go bad for ya's I'll be right down there to make sure things get better. WARREN: And we'll be there to do the same for you, G, you better believe that! NATE: Yeah, preach on, brother Warren! T. A. S.: YES! This is the type of togetherness I like to see. For that reason I want to publicly issue a challenge to the 6-man tag champs. My boys want a shot at your titles and they will take them and as for anyone else that wants a piece of any one of my guys, you better realize that you want one, you get the others. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: They get the shot at the North American Tag Team titles at MAY-HEM, now we hear comments from the Mercenaries. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on the Mercenaries. The other members of DOOMSDAY are nowhere to be seen.) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Well, what happened in the match, Regulators? I thought you were going to send us to hell or something. You know, I'm getting really tired of seeing your ugly faces every time I turn around. Why don't you just admit that the Mercenaries have your number and move on with your lives? KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Well, first you have to HAVE a life to start with. I guess that the Regulators don't have anything else better to do than have us beat the crap out of them week in and week out. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: As for you, Bad Girls, we've brought it to the attention of the CWO hierarchy that your finisher is illegal and should be banned. Using two foreign objects to get the win is anything but sportsmenlike. The referee should have disqualified you two morons and given us those North American titles on general principle. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Very soon you two will find out, just like the Regurgitating Retards did, that the Mercenaries are THE TEAM THAT RULES THE WORLD! We are close to getting those titles. So close you don't realize it yet..... _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today, we'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT!
