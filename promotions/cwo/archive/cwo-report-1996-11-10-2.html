CWO REPORT 11/9/96

10 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Talon is in the interview area, grinning.) TALON: Barry Hart, I don't know much about you 'cept the fact that I will beat you in the match that only a fool would sign against myself. A steel cage? I mean, come on. Only one man's gonna come out of this match, Hart, and that man will be Talon. You made a mistake challenging me for this match. LIGHTS OUT! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on The Creatures of the Night, Night Crawler and Night Slinger, and their partner, Hellhound. All three men are wearing their 6-man tag titles.) NIGHT SLINGER: Well, guys, it seems like that anyone who thinks that they have what it takes to get in the ring and take away these here gold belts of ours has the right to, according to the CWO. Tell you what, this is just fine with the Dark Side because ain't nobody in this federation that got what it takes to get these belts away from us. HELLHOUND: Do we get to beat up on some more losers or what? I want to get in the ring now and rip some boy limb from limb. I want to steal his soul and feast on the lifeless carcass. NIGHT CRAWLER: Yes, Hellhound, tonight we get another team that is wet behind the ears. Panther and D.E.A.T.H., you boys think you are the ones to take these belts from the Dark Side. I don't think so. What you are ready for is the biggest a** kicking you have yet been treated to. We don't play games here and tonight when you walk from your peaceful light into our chaotic NIGHT, you will truly know the meaning of fear. NIGHT SLINGER: Boys, tonight, watch and learn from the best as we drag your sorry carcasses all over the ring. All that will be left of you is a bloody mess. Come, welcome to our "Forever Night"! ALL THREE: LONG LIVE THE NIGHT! (As the camera fades out, it shows Hellhound licking his gold belt.) HELLHOUND (muttering): I will never leave you, my pet, for you are always mine and shall always be in my posesion and control. _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO Report opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Saturday, November 9. Last night, the superstars of the CWO were in Sacramento, CA, let's look at the results from that great card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW REPORT FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 8, 1996 SACRAMENTO, CA LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP (STEEL CAGE, NON-TITLE): "Master of Pain" Barry Hart vs. Talon (champion) Barry Hart pinned Talon in 8:08 using the ropes for leverage after the Cardiac Arrest (shooting star press). STEEL CAGE: Deadly Sins vs. Mercenaries Mercenaries defeated the Deadly Sins in 12:15 when Korperal Punishment pinned Pride after the Mercenaries' Flying Clothesline (Kaptain Krunch lifted Pride up, Korperal Punishment came off the top rope with a flying clothesline). NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Gold Kard vs. Vincent "The Lip" Manocelli Gold Kard pinned Vincent "The Lip" Manocelli with Die Flieder Kross (flying somersault splash) in 7:44. After the match Gold Kard was attacked by the Tower of Power, bringing Gold Kard International out for the save. The two teams brawled back to the dressing room. TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Inferno vs. Brent "The Ace" Alles (champion) Inferno pinned Alles with an inside cradle in 12:41. After the match, MOD Damion attacked Inferno, delivering the Darkness Falls tombstone piledriver to him before the Alliance arrived to drive him off. 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Blood Inc. (Panther and D. E. A. T. H.) vs. The Dark Side (Hellhound and Creatures of the Night) (champions) Blood Inc. defeated the Dark Side when Panther made Night Slinger submit to the Eclipse (Oriental thumb and a sleeper) in 3:47. Before the match the Dark Side was attacked by the Alliance, who gave them a severe beating, obviously affecting the outcome of the match. SUBMISSION MATCH: Rooster vs. Flyboy Rooster made Flyboy submit to the Roosterlock in 14:53. After the match, Rooster refused to let the Roosterlock go, prompting the other members of the Four Aces (Clark Gray and Hollywood Muscle) to make the save. TEXAS TORNADO MATCH: Gods of Thunder vs. The Big Cool Gods of Thunder defeated the Big Cool in 9:23 when DC pinned Jim "Jazzman" Taylor after the Lightning Spike (double Tombstone piledriver on a chair). After the match, the Gods of Thunder were attacked by the Bad Guys. Sandman vs. Mad Doctor's Ghost Mad Doctor's Ghost defeated Sandman by disqualification when Talon and the Flame Spirits attacked him in 16:55. Moses vs. Tiger Rogers Moses pinned Tiger Rogers with the Commandment springboard moonsault in 8:47. Hollywood Muscle vs. Bad Guys Bad Guys defeated Hollywood Muscle in 9:21 when Joe "The Mean Guy" Rucker pinned Wayne Carpenter after Herbie Versmells struck him with a cellular phone. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Tonight we're in Portland, OR, let's look at the lineup for that card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW SATURDAY, NOVEMBER 9, 1996 PORTLAND, OR LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Dark Raven vs. Talon (champion) NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Pit Bosses (champions) vs. Bad Guys TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Joe Eagle MOD Damion vs. Inferno Apocalypse vs. The Big Cool Tiger Rogers vs. "Master of Pain" Barry Hart WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): The Creatures of The Night vs. David & Goliath Bane vs. Sandman Lafayette vs. David "The Destroyer" Davis GangLord vs. Steel Flyboy vs. Dark Raven _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We now go to comments from CWO superstars. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on a rather happy MOD Damion, he is laughing out loud, sees the camera, and tries to compose himself.) MOD DAMION: Inferno, do you see what happens when you try to get in the way of one of the Dark Side? You tried to get at Dark Raven and at the same time you and your so called friends jumped me. You see, it does not pay to be an enemy of the Dark Side. We are all powerful and control everything that happens in the CWO, and when I want something, I get it. I wanted a chance to get my hands on you in the ring and now Saturday, I have my chance. Inferno, you will not walk out of the ring under your own power, I will take control of your soul after you have entered my domain and I render you completely helpless. There is nothing you can do. Like the many better men to try before you, you will go down to the Darkness Falls. LONG LIVE THE NIGHT!!! (Camera fades as Damion walks into the shadows and disappears.) _______________________________________________________________________________ ("Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum is in the interview area with his sister Sondra.) "MODERN DAY WARRIOR" BEN SLOCUM: It seems to me as if I still have one more date with death before it's finished. Well, Master of Darkness Damion, I'm calling your bluff and at the November Pain PPV we'll see just how much guts you really got when we square off in that Texas Death Match. I'm guessing you don't have enough to put my shoulders down and knock me out. You have shown that you indeed do have a big mouth and a bunch of illiterate henchmen who follow you around, but in the end you'll end up just as embarrassed as you must have felt after the last PPV, and your humiliating loss there. SONDRA: I had hopes that would have been the last we saw or heard of Mr. Darkness and his goons, but maybe he didn't learn his lesson good enough the last time? Anyway, after the Texas Death Match I'm pretty sure that not even the dark side can come up with any more excuses and therefore that will be where the final chapter in this story is written. BEN SLOCUM: With the rest of the Confederate Associates backing me up, I really don't see how I can lose. With all the constant sneak attacks and other crap going on I've realized the importance of having an alliance to help you out whenever you're in trouble. This time around I'll be prepared for whatever anyone throws at me, and that's a promise. SONDRA: Perhaps this organization will be all it takes to finally clean up this great fed from all the sewer rats who've polluted the air for much too long. You should all look at it as a whole new beginning for the CWO, and a bright one it will be, at least for some of us. BEN SLOCUM: To prepare for the big match I do need one or perhaps a few matches leading up to it. Right now I don't really have anyone I hold a grudge against so therefore I'm issuing an open contract to fight anyone that wants a shot at the Modern Day Warrior, and who wants to get a taste how the Modern Day Bomb feels. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The scene opens outside the locker room area where the Hangman and his manager "Notorious" Nicole Lane are standing.) "NOTORIOUS" NICOLE LANE: Soon, November is over, but before that happens you will once again get a chance to see the madman that is and always have been the "number one mercenary" in the wrestling world. At the November Pain PPV it's time for Wargames, and I can assure you that this man has no intentions of giving up, so Johnny Reb and his little band of girl scouts had better get ready for another long hard night of pain and suffering before they eventually end up on the losing side. HANGMAN: I've lived with the pain my entire life, and that's why I welcome the feeling of it with open arms. Quite frankly, I don't think that too many of you sissies have what it takes to withstand the punishment that I can dish out, but I guess that's something we'll just have to find out. Anyway, my hunting season has started and you're all my prey now. LANE: It's time for the fedheads to stop ignoring and mistreating this man and time for them to finally give him some matches, and we want matches with no DQ rules so the fights are on equal terms. Knowing what a bunch of threelegged cowards that signs the matches, they will probably continue to protect their fragile fossils from any real competition, though. Just remember that no matter where you hide or for how long we will find you in the end, and the experience of having your body dismembered limb by limb will be one you never forget. HANGMAN: Talon! If you want more punishment, all you have to do is ask, and if your buddy Sandman wants to poke his nose in where it doesn't belong he might just get some too. I will take on anyone with the guts to accept my challenges, and I challenge anyone to a barbed wire Death Match. I and no one else will lead you down those final steps you take on death row. That's just the way it is. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for the Report today.
