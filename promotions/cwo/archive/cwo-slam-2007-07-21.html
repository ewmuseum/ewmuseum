Stacey-X is sitting in her chair with her clipboard on her lap.* STACEY: "Hey CWO fans, welcome to Saturday SLAM! I'm your host, former CWO Amazon Stacey-X! Starting this week, SLAM! is back to its regular programming of exclusive shows, and recaps for the week! We've got an amazing show for you this week. As well as the recap for FEVER and EXTREME, we have singles action aplenty, as newcomers Afro Punk and "Canadian Outlaw" James Bauer fight it out. Later, it's Amazon action as Sahara takes on former Amazon Champion April Raines, and finally, we have the monster Titan taking on CWO veteran Adam Smoke! Let's go to Jackson & Max." ----- NEVILS: "Hey CWO fans, and welcome to Saturday SLAM! I'm your commentator Jackson Nevils..." SEARY: "...And I'm Max Seary. We're here in the American Bank Center, in Corpus Christi, Texas, and we're about to have two newcomers battle it out to try and impress the crowd, as Afro Punk takes on "Canadian Outlaw" James Bauer." (Mike Alexander is in the ring.) ALEXANDER: This contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first... Hailing from New York City, NY... Weighing in at 221 pounds... Afro Punk ("Renegades Of Funk" by Rage Against The Machine plays over the PA system as Afro Punk enters the arena and makes his way down to the ring.) NEVILS: "This young NYC native made his debut last week on Fever, impressing many with a determined attempt on the FEVER Title." His opponent... Hailing from Victoria, British Columbia, Canada... Weighing in at 203 pounds... "Canadian Outlaw" James Bauer ("You Know My Name" by Chris Cornell plays over the PA system as James Bauer enters the arena and makes his way down to the ring.) SEARY: "And here we have new signing "Canadian Outlaw" James Bauer, looking psyched for this, his debut match. They lock up. Afro Punk hits Bauer with a Snapmare. Punk whips Bauer into the ropes... Punk misses with a shoulderblock... Bauer comes back and hits a Hurracanrana on Afro Punk. He follows it up into a pin! Spencer Dolloff counts: One, Kickout. NEVILS: "Afro Punk with the early advantage, but here comes James Bauer." SEARY: "We nearly had it just then Jackson. Afro Punk is going to have to stop resting on his laurels if he wants to win this match." Bauer goes for a Spinebuster Slam, but Afro Punk counters it with a Kneelift, which Punk follows with a Jawbreaker. Afro Punk whips James Bauer into the ropes... Punk hits Bauer with a shoulderblock. Afro Punk hits James Bauer with a Fisherman Suplex, and he's holding it for the pin. Spencer Dolloff counts: One, Two, Kickout. NEVILS: "Afro Punk's turn with the pin attempt there. James Bauer able to kick out after the two-count however." Punk goes for a Snapmare, but James Bauer reverses it into a Backdrop. James Bauer runs into the ropes... Bauer hits Afro Punk with an elbow. James Bauer whips Afro Punk into the ropes... Afro Punk hits Bauer with a shoulderblock. Punk runs into the ropes... he hits James Bauer with a high leg clothesline. Afro Punk is going for the pin. Spencer Dolloff counts: One, Two, Thr... kickout. SEARY: "Afro Punk nearly had it with that high leg clothesline right there. James Bauer has to realize that this is the CWO here, and he needs to up his game." Punk executes a Hammerlock on Bauer. Afro Punk is getting a good reaction from the crowd, as he goes for an Atomic Drop, but James Bauer reverses it in the air, and hits Afro Punk with a Bulldog! Bauer runs into the ropes... Punk hits Bauer with an elbow. Punk executes a chop on James Bauer, and follows it up by locking Bauer in a Front Facelock... Bauer tries to escape the hold... Bauer reaches the ropes after 8 seconds. Afro Punk hits a Gutwrench Suplex on Bauer. James Bauer bumps into Spencer Dolloff, knocking him down. Punk hits a dropkick on James Bauer. He follows that up with an elbowdrop. Punk takes James Bauer down with a Dragon Screw. Afro Punk is getting a good reaction from the crowd as he covers James Bauer. There is no referee to count. NEVILS: "Dolloff is still down, after Afro Punk accidentally threw James Bauer into him." SEARY: "It's Punk's own fault that there's no-one to count the pin. Punk throws Bauer into the turnbuckle. Punk climbs the turnbuckle, and hits Bauer with a series of punches as the crowd counts. The crowd is starting to get behind Afro Punk. Punk takes Bauer down with a series of stomps in the corner. Punk executes a Hiptoss on Bauer, into the turnbuckle. Punk charges in with a dropkick to the face. Spencer Dolloff is sporting a dazed look but is back on the job. NEVILS: "Spencer's back on the job, maybe Afro can finish this now. He's taking James Bauer to school out here." Afro Punk whips James Bauer into the ropes... Bauer and Punk take each other out with a double clothesline... Both men are down! SEARY: "Both men are down. Wait, I think James Bauer may be stirring..." Bauer climbs to his feet, slowly followed by Punk... Bauer hits an Armdrag on Afro Punk. Bauer kicks Afro Punk on the floor. The crowd is starting to get behind James Bauer. Bauer takes Afro Punk down with a Vertical Suplex. James Bauer punches Afro Punk. Afro Punk kicks James Bauer. The crowd is starting to get behind Afro Punk. James Bauer kicks Afro Punk. There are lots of chants for James Bauer. NEVILS: "The crowd can't seem to decide who they want to win this match Max." SEARY: "That seems to be the case Jackson. Of those who are interested, half seem to be rooting for Bauer, the other half for Afro Punk. Talk about your fickle crowds." Afro Punk kicks Bauer. Bauer replies with a punch. The crowd is going crazy. Bauer hits a DDT on Afro Punk. James Bauer has the crowd going wild. Bauer goes for a Small Package, but Afro Punk reverses it. Spencer Dolloff counts: One, Two, Thr... kickout. NEVILS: "That's the second near 3-count for Afro Punk. He's gotta be wondering what it's going to take to keep Bauer down. Afro Punk goes for a Backbreaker, but Bauer wriggles out of it, and hits Punk with a Fallaway Slam. The crowd is going crazy. Bauer runs into the ropes... Bauer hits Punk with an elbow. James Bauer hits a Sidewalk Slam on Afro Punk. James Bauer goes for a Manhattan Drop, but Afro Punk counters it with a Sunset Flip. Spencer Dolloff counts: One, Two, Shoulder up. SEARY: "Afro Punk with a Sunset Flip... so close!" Afro Punk hits a Back Suplex on James Bauer. There are lots of chants for Afro Punk. Punk goes for a Piledriver, but Bauer reverses it into a High Angle Spinebuster Slam. Spencer Dolloff counts: One, Two, Kickout. James Bauer complains about a slow count. NEVILS: "Bauer with a pinning attempt. He seems to feel that Spencer wasn't quite counting to speed." Bauer kicks Afro Punk. James Bauer has the crowd going wild. Bauer kicks Afro Punk. The crowd is going crazy. Punk punches James Bauer, followed by a kick to the midsection. There are lots of chants for Afro Punk. Afro Punk runs into the ropes... Bauer hits Afro Punk with a roundhouse kick! Bauer runs into the ropes. He goes for a clothesline, but Afro Punk ducks it... Afro Punk executes the Jumble Jaw Jive on James Bauer! There are lots of chants for Afro Punk, as Afro Punk goes for the pin. NEVILS: "It's the Jumble Jaw Jive!" SEARY: "That Superkick straight to the jaw of James Bauer! I think he may be out." Spencer Dolloff counts: One, Two, Three. There are lots of chants for Afro Punk. ALEXANDER: The winner is Afro Punk. Time of match: 0:08:51 NEVILS: "Wow, Afro Punk just getting victory with the Jumble Jaw Jive, but not without a good fight from James Bauer." SEARY: "What a match. This shows the quality of talent that the CWO is signing these days. That's it for us here in the American Bank Center. Back to Stacey in the studio." STACEY: "Now that was an entertaining match. I doubt we've heard the last from either of those young men. After the break folks, we'll recap the action for last week's Fever, as GM Lawrence Dawson continues his scheming..." ----- Commercial Break ----- FEVER RECAP Highlights from FEVER 7/16/07 are shown - - - - - *News Flash* Backstage at the most recent CWO houseshows, there have been sightings and rumors. "I have recently been told by several eye witnesses that they swear they saw THe Heavy Metal Machine backstage at recent CWO events." We can only wonder as to when and where this man will appear next. "That man has gone missing, and CWO fans everywhere are asking the same thing, is he gone for good or will he return," says Kyle as he sighs in disbelief, "I dont know weither I can miss the guy or if I'm happy he's gone, but I for one want to know where he is." ----- Commercial Break ----- *The camera comes up on a pre-recorded promo. GENESIS: "Maybe you should take Amy with you just in case." SAHARA: "If I'm in trouble, send the Strike Force in but believe me babe, the only person who's gunna need back up is April. Once I'm done with her she's gunna wish she never stood in my way." *Sahara throws the bottled water to the ground and makes her way to the ring while Genesis looks on in a concerned manner.* *The camera cross-fades back to the studio.* STACEY: "That Sahara sure looks like one hell of a rough customer. Hailing from Calgary, Alberta, Canada... Weighing in at 140 pounds... Sahara ("Not Enough" by Our Lady Of Peace hits the speakers as "Sahara: A New Revolution appears on the CWOtron. 3 seconds later a puff of smoke evaporates into the air, and through the smoke appears an angry Sahara to a rapturous reception of boos by the CWO fans. April Raines (April's theme music plays over the PA system as she comes down to the ring and gets in.) NEVILS: "Two of the most talented amazons squaring off tonight." SEARY: "Don't you mean the hottest, look at Sahara in the tight lycra- clad suit, some one call the fire brigade, whoo." The bell rings and the match begins. April Raines goes on the attack. SEARY: "April showing she's all business going straight for that Top Rope Frankensteiner. Amazing. Sahara couldn't have expected that." April hits Sahara with a kick to the midsection. GILFARGE: "Sahara looks dazed, maybe April should give her mouth to mouth just to be sure." April Raines works the crowd. April hits Sahara with a Bodyslam. April goes for a forearm smash, but Sahara blocks it and executes a Cradle Suplex on April, followed by a Monkey Flip. Sahara goes for a Vertical Suplex, but April blocks it, before hitting Sahara with a Vertical Suplex of her own. April runs into the ropes... she hits Sahara with a clothesline. Sahara reeling from this assault from April Raines." April hits Sahara with a forearm smash, following it up with a Schoolgirl Pin... John Myering counts: One, Kickout. April Raines works the crowd as she climbs to the top turnbuckle, but Sahara throws her to the mat. NEVILS: "I suspect April may have been going for the Rainestorm there. NEVILS: "Can you stick to the match please, you pervert." Sahara hits a jumping neck snap on April. A wave of boos is going through the crowd as Sahara goes for the cover. John Myering counts: One, Kickout. SEARY: "Sahara trying to end this quick. But April has the benefit of experience here." Sahara goes for a splash, but April Raines gets her knees up. Sahara begs off, but April hits her with a chop. April goes for a Northern Lights Suplex, but Sahara blocks it, instead hitting April with a Vertical Suplex. A wave of boos is going through the crowd. Sahara slaps April. April responds with a kick, then a punch, then a dropkick. You can hear fans cheering for April Raines. Sahara springs to her feet, and whips April Raines into the ropes... Sahara hits April with an elbow, followed by a forearm smash. She quickly climbs the turnbuckle, and hits a Frog Splash on April Raines. John Myering counts: One, Two, Kickout. SEARY: "Ooooo, so close, so so close." NEVILS: "Sahara nearly had the pinfall right there." Sahara whips April into the ropes... April misses with a shoulderblock... Sahara misses with a clothesline... Sahara hits April with a Spinning Headscissors Takedown! John Myering counts: One, Two, Kickout. SEARY: "Another close pin from Sahara. Can she put April away though?" Sahara hits a Vertical Suplex on April. Sahara executes a Monkey Flip on April Raines. Sahara is going for the pin. John Myering counts: One, Two, Shoulder up. Sahara trash talks the crowd. The crowd is booing Sahara. Sahara sends April into the turnbuckle. Sahara runs shoulder-first into the corner, but April dodges aside, letting Sahara collide with the turnbuckle. April hits a Hiptoss on Sahara, followed by a Hurrancanrana. *Amy Savage comes running down the ramp, she starts to scream obscenities at April Raines.* NEVILS: "What's Amy doing here?" SEARY: "Special guest referee?" Amy distracts April by climbing on the apron. April and Amy are having a slanging match on the apron... Sahara slowly gets back upto her feet... Sahara goes for a Flying Dropkick off the top rope to her, but April quickly darts to the side, and Sahara hits Amy! Amy goes flying to the floor. NEVILS: "Oh my!! April went for the Rainestorm, her Flying Somersault Sitdown Splash, but Sahara dodged it, and hit April with the Reverse DDT she calls the Oasis! I think April may be unconscious." John Myering counts: One, two, three. McCREARY: The winner is Sahara. Time of match: 0:06:57. Quite a few boos are audible. SEARY: "Wow, there's a fair few people here unhappy with that result. Adam executes a superkick on Titan. Smoke executes a roundhouse kick to the head on Titan. Adam executes an avalanche on Titan. Adam finally takes Titan down a second Avalanche.
