REPORT 2/13/99

February 16, 1999

CWO REPORT 2/13/99 AEN (ALL ENTERTAINMENT NETWORK) 10:00 AM EST RATING: TV-PG PRODUCER: Ryan Deutscher ( [detail withheld] ) EXECUTIVE PRODUCER: Brent G. Alles ( [detail withheld] ) http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/5403/ ("Dateline News" theme plays as the "CWO REPORT" logo comes up. We fade to
Krash Kelly at the CWO Report Desk.)
KELLY: Hello everyone and welcome to the return of the CWO Report. I am your host, "Double K" Krash Kelly, and we have a lot of wrestling action to cover as we close in on CWO St. Valentines Day Massacre, tomorrow night.
(CWO St. Valentines Day Massacre only on PPV graphic comes up.) KELLY: We well get to the line up a little later in the show, now lets move on to the action. Let’s travel back in time...
____________________________________________________________________________ ____ CWO AFTER DARK 1/30/99 The show began with "Diamondback" Chris Meyers in the cemetery in front of the grave site of his mother and father.
Johnny Reb defeated "Dragonfire" Rich Johnson in 2:35 by disqualification when the Renegades interfered.
MoR Wildfire and Union Caine defeated The Lunatic Fringe and The Dogs of War in 14:57, when Union Caine pinned Logan Fett after a flying headbutt.
CWO LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP Tommy Lighting defeated Secret Agent Super Dragon in 7:30 to become the new
CWO Light Heavyweight Champion, when Geoff Bondavarsky hit SASD with a reverse DDT. After the match the Dark Side came to the ring and brutally beat Tommy Lighting, injuring his ankle.
Sandman Jr, Col. Taggart, and "The Avenger" Jesse Ewiak defeated Mondo
Gecko, Ranger Taurus, and Snake Martin in 10:51 when Jessie Ewiak pinned Snake
Martin with the Ewiakbomb.
FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE MATCH Sean Holiday defeated Wishbone in 12:32 after a powerbomb. The True Americans defeated Vladimir Popalov and "Dark Shogun" Akueri Hakujin in 6:44 by disqualification when Super Bad Ass Sweet Daddy Jones and Secret
Agent Super Dragon interfered.
CWO WORLD TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP Chris Marcus defeated Simon St. Severe in 7:28, to retain the CWO World
Television Championship after Snake Martin hit Severe with a chair.
CWO NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP The Newton Brothers defeated Fallen and Warhawk to become the new CWO North
American Tag Team Champions after Steve Newton pinned Fallen with a rollup.
Footage from CWO November Pain '98: Hollywood Haskins turns on Masher Maccabee STRAP MATCH Masher Maccabee defeated Hollywood Haskins in 5:56 after touching all four turnbuckles in succession. After the match Haskins and a mystery man beat up Maccabee.
CWO WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP Creatures of Darkness defeated the Deadly Sins in 9:52 to retain the CWO
World Tag Team Championships, when Night Crawler pinned Pride after a flying elbow drop. Referee Bob Koster came to the ring and made the pin after Wrath had
Pride in the repentance rack. After the match Wrath left with the Creatures of Darkness, and was greeted by Kent Alles and Commissioner Krepps, and given a large sum of money.
Sandman defeated Lord Hile Troy in 9:59 after the sandblast. After the match the Dark Side made their way to the ring. But, the lights went out, and when they came back on Gold Kard was helping Troy out of the ring. This
went on to set up Warhawk/Fallen vs Gold Kard/Troy on the next Fever. Footage from CWO Summer Heat '98: Diamondback Chris Meyers defeats Foolkiller to become the new CWO World Champion. Footage from CWO Celebration '98: Diamondback Chris Meyers eliminates Brent
Alles from the battle royal to receive the first PPV shot at the CWO World
Championship.
CWO WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP "Diamondback" Chris Meyers defeated Foolkiller in 8:44 after the rattler. Sandman came to the ring and brutally beat Foolkiller with a barb-wire baseball bat. After the match the Dark Side came on to the runway to toast their new member. Myers just stood in the ring in disbelief.
____________________________________________________________________________ ___ KELLY: You know, in my tenure in the CWO, I have witnessed a lot of things. But after CWO After Dark, after what Chris Myers did. After he stabbed all of his fans in the back. Well anyway, let’s move on to CWO Fever...
____________________________________________________________________________ ____ CWO FEVER 2/1/99 HOUR #1: The show started off with the Outlaw Riders making comments about their hatred for the Darkness and Gordy Alles.
Brian 'The King' Derks defeated Marc 'Compton' Daniels in 4:40 after some sort of head crushing maneuver.
Snake Martin and Ranger Taurus defeated Warhawk and Sandman by disqualification when the Dark Side interfered. Gordy Alles was so enraged that he said Chris Marcus would defend the TV title later tonight.
Lester Sheets comes to the ring and along with Secret Agent Super Dragon,
Super Bad Ass Sweet Daddy Jones, Akueri Hakujin, and Vladimir Popalov, formed the Sheets Foundation.
CWO WORLD TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP "Dark Shogun" Akueri Hakujin defeated Chris Marcus after blowing him in the face with a fire ball and then hitting the Triad Bomb to become the new CWO
World Television Champion. After the match Allen Carter came onto the runway and was joined by the rest of the True Americans. He then berated
Hakujin
for winning the TV title. Lester Sheets fired back by making an offer to
George Washington to join the Sheets Foundation. Then, Gordy Alles came to the ring and striped Tommy Lighting of the CWO Light Heavyweight
Championship, and set up a match at CWO St. Valentines Day Massacre, between
Secret Agent Super Dragon and George Washington.
Wildfire and "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum ended in a no contest when the Outlaw
Riders interfered. After the match the Dark Side Security.
Steve Keller defeats MoR Union Caine and Joe Eagle when Russel Hill threw in the towel. Caine was so irate, that he challenged Eagle and anyone to face himself and Wildfire at CWO St. Valentines Day Massacre.
Just before the end of hour 1, we cut to The True Americans beating up the
Sheets Foundation. As well as Jesse "The Avenger" Ewiak also being beaten.
HOUR 2 At the start of hour 2, Jesse "The Avenger" Ewiak was laying beaten on the ground as Diamondback Chris Meyers came in the arena and was about to tend to the fallen Ewiak, when Col. Taggart came in and accused him of beating
Ewiak. Myers was then beat up by Taggart and Sandman Jr.
The Lunatic Fringe are seen accepting an offer from the Darkness. The Renegades defeated The Lunatic Fringe in 10:21 when Kamikaze tapped out after the Technicality.
Comments from Hollywood Haskins and Iceman X. Iceman X and Masher Maccabee ended in a double count out. During the break
Iceman ran back stage looking for Masher, but was met by the DSF.
Comments from "Smilin" Doc Watson and Fergus McTavish "The Whirling Scottish" Devil. Comments from Geoff Bondavarsky. Flashback to last weeks Fever when Gold Kard and Lord Hile Troy reunite. Warhawk and Fallen defeated Gold Kard and Lord Hile Troy in 8:10 after
Fallen
nailed Gold Kard with the Darkness Falls tombstone piledriver. After the match the Dark Side came and brutally beat Gold Kard, possibly breaking his neck. The Outlaw Riders came to the ring and brawled to the back with the
Dark
Side. ____________________________________________________________________________ __ KELLY: That was truly one of the most disturbing sites I have ever seen. My condolences go out to the family of Gold Kard. Well, now let’s move on to
CWO St. Valentines Day Massacre...
(Main event graphic comes up.) KELLY: In the main event "Diamondback" Chris Meyers defends the CWO World
Championship against Col. Taggart! Now let’s run down the rest of the card...
