CWO REPORT 3/29/97

29 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Saturday, March 29, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the CWO MARCH MADNESS TOURNAMENT entered into the quarterfinals round in St. Louis, MO, let's see what went down! _______________________________________________________________________________ CWO MARCH MADNESS TOURNAMENT QUARTERFINALS ST. LOUIS, MO 3/28/97 Masher Maccabee received a bye. Johnny Reb pinned Exile after the Jackson's Revenge (flying headbutt). Time of match: 5:19. Night Slinger upset 'Monster' Rich Clark, pinning him after the Lights Out avalanche and outside interference by Bane. Time of match: 14:05. The Focus pinned Gold Kard after the Focal Point. Time of match: 9:29. CWO Light Heavyweight champion Tiger Rogers pinned David 'The Destroyer' Davis after the Inverted Powerbomb. Time of match: 17:58. Brian 'The Hammer' Williams pinned Jason 'Flash' Varga after the Keuka Smack. Time of match: 8:21. Brad 'The Man' Couchman pinned Chris Greco after the Piledriver. Time of match: 9:31. Joe Eagle defeated Clark Gray by countout when Brent 'The Ace' Alles attacked Clark Gray, the two of them brawling back to the dressing room. Time of match: 7:57. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) These two have never met before in the CWO, and their confrontation should be electric! Also, tomorrow is SHOWCASE, with more great matches of CWO action! (Montage of CWO action is shown as a SHOWCASE promo. Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: The Focus has really made a name for himself quickly here in the CWO, first with an intense feud with Benjamin Blake Bentley, now advancing to the Finals stage of the CWO MARCH MADNESS TOURNAMENT, and next Tuesday on FEVER, he will receive a shot at the Television title, currently held by this man, Mondo Gecko. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Mondo Gecko is in the interview area.) MONDO GECKO: Ya know, all of these matches this week have been a breath of fresh air. First there was Panther and now The Focus gets his shot at the TV title, both men are REAL MEN who don't need A MANAGER TO WIN MATCHES. Focus, anybody who beats the snot out of BBB is an ally of mine and I am glad to give you a shot. But baby, if you think you're gonna beat me for the belt than you have been hit in the head one too many times. You see, I am ready to give the fans what they want, a high impact match, and giving Masher & The Truth a good old fashioned beating. Ever since I have arrived in the CWO I promised the fans action and excitement. And as the label says, SATISFACTION GUARANTEED! So Focus, you are a tough individual and if you ever need my help all you have to do is call on me for help. But next Tuesday you are gonna face a ball of fire when I fly the skies and soar all over you. PREPARE TO GET MONDO EXTREME! Masher Maccabee, I am gonna be watching you, so if you try to pull any crap against Johhny Reb, you can bet your @$$ that I will come down and take you out. SEE YA! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: The countdown has begun, we are a little more than a week away from the World Championship match between "Monster" Rich Clark and the man who holds the title, Bane. This rivalry is heated up exponentially, and we take you now to comments from the "Monster," Rich Clark. _______________________________________________________________________________ ("Monster" Rich Clark sits in a room, watching the CWO report. As soon as he sees Goliath on the television screen, he goes absolutely ballistic. Another man, wearing a mask, enters the room, and calms him down.) MASKED MAN: Rich, you've got to be calm. You seem to be losing touch with reality, again. "MONSTER" RICH CLARK: Reality is something that I've never been totally able to grasp. Goliath, you think you deserve a shot at Bane, well too f*cking bad, 'cause I've got it. You see, if you had pinned me by yourself, instead of having that freak Bane help you by clocking me with a shovel, maybe you'd get your match, but you didn't. This thing may not be over between us. You don't know how hard I've worked to get this title shot. Now, I've finally made it, and next Sunday, that belt will be mine. Live with it. MASKED MAN: Rich, what about me, man? "MONSTER" RICH CLARK: Don't worry, little buddy. Soon, you'll get your chance. Just wait, and maybe you'll be forgiven. MASKED MAN: But, I shouldn't even be here. "MONSTER" RICH CLARK: Look, I got you a second chance, what more do you want? It's about time that you came back. I couldn't believe when they fell for it the first time. MASKED MAN: Look, how much longer do I have to wear this stupid thing? (pointing to the mask) "MONSTER" RICH CLARK: As long as it takes to get you back in full time. This should be good enough for now, shouldn't it? MASKED MAN: Fine. It better not take to long, because I hate the way blood clots up in these things. "MONSTER" RICH CLARK: Quit complaining. Now, we must take leave. MASKED MAN: OK. Let's get the f*ck out of here. (They grabs the van keys, and leave the house. Fade to black.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Also on the PPV, a tag team triangle match pitting the Highlanders and the Masters of Hardcore, Ohlhoff and Couchman, against these gentlemen, the Eternal Wanderers. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The scene opens. We see a very active Exodus punching two dummy bags. He is working extensively as he hits two sacks full of metal beads. On the side, Exile is meditating and there is a hooded man by him. It is Guru Crush Storm. He is passively standing by, looking attentively at Exodus. Suddenly, Exodus falls on his knees as the bags blow and out comes a hot fiery liquid. It oozes onto his wounded arm where he feels the pain entering within.) GURU CRUSH STORM: Patience, Exodus... you will have your moment. Push too hard and what you percieve to be an easy victory will slip through your hand. (a cold look) What has our Lord taught us? EXODUS (a pained look but an understanding one): Our Lord has taught us... (sighs) do not underestimate an opponent. Think of him as an equal or more and then... humble them as I would. (a cold look in his eyes) You are correct to remind me, Storm. (looks at the silent and meditating Exile) Is he doing well? GURU CRUSH STORM: There are arts which not all are privy to. (looks at Exodus) You understand he fights within... but the charm that Moon Knight gave him will help. EXODUS (an irritated look): Moon Knight? (humphs) I don't trust the Knights of the Moon. Why does our Lord allow him... (A hand appears on his shoulder.) LORD GURU: I allow him... (his eyes blazing) for it suits my purpose. What do you know what my mind should say, Exodus? (Lord Guru gives him a squeeze on the wound that makes him wince.) EXODUS: Forgive me, my Lord. I await the confrontation against the fools the Highlanders and the SO called "hardcore extreme team:... (a heated expression) Exile must also be ready! LORD GURU: And he will, he will... (smile) do not worry much, my son... for his time will come to shine as you have. Get ready for a war, child... for it is coming. (With that, the scene fades.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today, we'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT!
