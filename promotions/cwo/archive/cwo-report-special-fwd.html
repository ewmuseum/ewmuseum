CWO REPORT SPECIAL (fwd)

25 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens up on slow motion shots of several CWO superstars in action. The letters CWO zoom by on the screen and fade in to Mitch E. Washey.) MITCH E. WASHEY: Welcome everybody to a special edition of the CWO REPORT. We have several things to cover in this REPORT, so let's get things started by announcing the tentative match schedule for the next CWO PPV, the CWO's Rockin' Independance Day. CWO ROCKIN' INDEPENDENCE DAY 7/6/97 TENTATIVE LINEUP - CARD SUBJECT TO CHANGE WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: Gold Kard vs. Joe Eagle (champion) WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Bad Guys vs. Highlanders (champion) NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP: Masher Maccabee vs. Tiger Rogers (champion) NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Mercenaries vs. Regulators (champions) TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP: Panther vs. Benjamin Blake Bentley (champion) WORLD 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Brent "The Ace" Alles, Vegas High Roller, Matsuhiro Torunaka vs. Jason "Flash" Varga, Wayne "College Guy" Schillo, Brian "The Hammer" Williams (champions) LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Mondo Gecko vs. Moses (champion) GRUDGE MATCH: Johnny Reb vs. Lord Hile Troy SINGAPORE CANE MATCH: Sandman Jr. vs. The Focus BURIED ALIVE MATCH: Fergus McTavish vs. Bloodbath "Monster" Rich Clark vs. GKI's New Man Diamondback Chris Myers vs. Wrath Ghost Riders and ?? vs. Goliath, Inferno, David "The Destroyer" Davis El Grande Pistolerro vs. "The Brazilian Lover" Carlos Maia New Men vs. X-Factor and Marc Mission Bishop and Dark Raven vs. Bloody Fangs EYE ON THE STORM: ROCKIN' INDEPENDENCE DAY The Big Cool vs. Deadly Sins vs. Coldfront Steel vs. Wildfire Mason-Dixon vs. Brother Justice "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher vs. Matthew "The Man" White WASHEY: It promises to shape up to be an exciting and explosive night for the CWO. Now let's get to comments made by CWO superstars. We start things off with the Scottsman, Fergis McTavish. ____________________________________________________________________________ ______ (Camera shot opens on Fergus McTavish, The Whirling Scottish Devil, talking on the telephone. His voice is mired with a thick Scottish accent.) Fergus: But Ma, I tolds ya already, me match wif WRATH has been delayed a week! (Fergus appears to be listening to his Ma's response.) Fergus: Nay, Ma! I dinna quit! I likes it here! An I gots me a new friend. Aye, the wee laddie. Reminds me a meself when I was his age. (Fergus is listening again.) Fergus: Ma, I keeps tellin' ya, its not me fault! Da Director's been workin' hard da last couple a weeks an so tings get behinds. Jus keeps watchin' yer telly an I'll be on soon......I hopes. Ya know Ma, I miss yer haggis. Kin ya send me somes? Dis crap dey serve at da CWO cafeteria ain't enough ta feeds a hungry Scotsman likes meself. An da lunch-ladies? Argh, are dey ugly! So how much do I want? Aye, two boxes will be enough. What's dat? Jus a sec..... (Fergus calls the camera-guy over.) Eh, boyo, I wants ta say sumtin' ta me Ma in private so gimme a sec, OK? Camera-Guy: Sure thing, Mr. McTavish. (Camera-guy leaves, but still has the camera pointed at Fergus.) Fergus: Alrights Ma, what's dat again? Och, alrights! I luvs ya too! G'Bye. (Fergus hangs up the phone.) (The camera-guy giggles at this and Fergus hears him.) Fergus: Right laddie! Yer dead! Ya hears me! Yer dead! (The camera-guy turns and runs like hell, but his camera is still aimed in the other direction, pointed at Fergus.) Fergus: Dat's right ya wussy! Runs all ya wants but sooner or later, The Whirling Scottish Devil will gets ya! So gets yerself ready fer a beatin'! I'll tear ya a new one I will! Oh, and one last ting.... (Fergus turns his back to the still running camera-guy and lifts up his kilt, mooning the camera) Fergus: (over his shoulder) Gets ready for The Whirling Scottish Devil! ITS NOT OATMEAL!!!! (Camera shot gets blocked as the camera-guy runs around a corner but much laughing and hooting can still be heard) __________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: At least nobody can say that Mr. McTavish's interviews aren't entertaining. We now go to comments from one of the fastest rising stars of the CWO. Diamondback Chris Myers is standing by. __________________________________________________________________________ (Diamondback Chris Myers is shown in a ring, sparring with another wrestler. Dallas is on the outside, coaching) Dallas: Ok, grab him.... (The other wrestler grabs Chris and puts him on his shoulders, as if going for a torture rack. Chris, however, managers to swing around, and in doing so, gets his arms around the wrestler's neck and puts a Rattler on him) Myers: OH YEAH! Dallas: Nice! How's he doing? Myers: He's fine. Just a little sore, right? (The wrestler manages a smile as he holds his neck. As Chris and Dallas turn away, it's obvious he's in pain. He leaves to the locker room) Myers: Do you think I'm ready for the Roller, Dallas? Dallas: Almost. Just a few more tapes to go over... (Chris rolls his eyes as a cell phone rings) Dallas: Oops, that's mine....Hello? Yes....yes...what? Tuesday? Ok, thanks! Myers: What was that all about? Dallas: It's on for THIS Tuesday, Chris! Which means you have that much more time to train! Myers: I have the best finisher on the planet, Dallas! Dallas: And the High Roller has GKI behind him, remember? Myers: Hell, I could beat them all... Dallas: One at a time! Now, let's not get your head in the clouds, Chris! Who's next after Roller? Myers: You DID send that request into CWO HQ, right? Dallas: Yeah, why? Myers: Well, I made a little challange... Dallas: CHRIS! Come on, you can't go on making challanges left and right! High Roller's going to be tough enough. You CAN beat him, but you have to focus! Myers: Calm down, it's for next week! Dallas: Who? Myers: Actually, more like what! I asked for a shot at the TV title. Dallas: WHAT! Chris, don't you think it's too soon? You only just got here! Myers: It's been two months, Dallas! People are calling me the future of CWO, and I'm not going to sit around fighting losers! I want some gold to prove myself! Besides, this Bentley guy's going to be a piece of cake! Dallas: Uh, Chris... Myers: I mean, who does he think he is, Hunter Hearst Helmsley? Dallas: Chris... Myers: I walk in, Rattle him, and walk out with a belt! Dallas: DIAMONDBACK! He's probably NOT going to be champ in a week! He's fighting Fergus McTavish this week? Myers: Really? That insane Scotsman? The guy who runs around screaming something about oatmeal? Dallas: Yeah, the one with that little kid? Myers: Hmm.........guess I never thought about fighting him....he'll be tough... Dallas: One thing at a time, Chris! Let's go watch those tapes on Vegas High Roller, AND then back to training....come on, 'Future of CWO'... Myers: Don't get sarcastic on me, little lady! I AM beating Roller and I WILL beat Bentley/McTavish, and that TV belt will be around MY waist! Dallas: I didn't say otherwise, Chris! I'm just saying FOCUS, ok? Myers: That's why you're the manager! Let's go figure out how to beat this Roller guy! What was it called, the Vegas Swinger... (Myers and Dallas go into the office as the camera fades out) ___________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: GKI is still making it's presence felt in the CWO, and we now go to one of it's members, Masher Maccabee. ___________________________________________________________________________ Masher is in the GKI offices, "Well, I'm back in the spotlight. I punked the Wild Bunch, and me and Brother Moses punked Mason-Dixon. But you know what, I feel like I might be overshadowed in GKI, being a part of the greatest group of wrestlers in the world. Solution: GOLD. Johnny Reb, I want my title back. I challenge you to a lights-out match on FEVER for the TV strap. No DQ, No CO, all out war, with two special stipulations. 1. None of your cronies, or any of mine can be involved, and 2., that whomever wins, must use his finisher. Your wimpy cloverleaf to the nastiest move on the planet, the Homentaschen Hangover. What do you think? A yes might help, being that we own your scrawny ass anyway! HA HA HA! GKI is just tooooooooo sweet!" ____________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Another GKI member, who surprised the whole federation a week ago, is back with more comments. Here's the one and only, Lord Hile Troy. ____________________________________________________________________________ Outside of a CWO arena, fans gather in line, awaiting that night's action, when a glorious and sleek black limo which bears the GKI logo pulls up. The fans at first try to peer through the heavily tinted glass, until the door opens and a man steps out wearing a sharp armani suit and royal blue sunglasses, a man recognized by the fans as Lord Hile Troy Young Boy: Y... you cheated Johnny Reb out of his television title last week wi... with that briefcase! You're a BAD MAN! BAD! Lord Hile Troy: (while litting a cigarette) You're right kid, I am! And look where it's gotten me now! You know... I _used_ to stick up for people like you, hell last year I FOUGHT for people like you, and where did THAT get me? Stuck behind two fraternity boys? I don't think so, no one steps on me. (Troy flicks his cigarette at the kid's feet) Lord Hile Troy: Let me ask you son, who is your FAVORITE CWO superstar? Young Boy: Brian "The Hammer" Williams! He's the bestest there is! Lord Hile Troy: Didn't you ever call Dave Thomas's Hotline? Don't you know that he is a heroine addict? Do you even know what heroine IS? (Little Boy shakes his head "no") Lord Hile Troy: It's a _drug_, now I know they've taught you in school that drugs are BAD. Brian Williams is a drug dealer, bad man, very bad! Young Boy: N... no he isn't! You're making that up! You're a liar! Lord Hile Troy: Oh am I? (Troy speaks over his shoulder) What do YOU think? GKI Limo Driver: Oh, heroine addict all the way! He sells all kinds of drugs. I heard he tried to kidnap a little boy once too! (The young boy drops a 'Brian Williams' poster and runs crying) Lord Hile Troy: OK! Who wants the newly printed Gold Kard International Tshirt, with MY picture on it as well? Teenage Fans: We Do! GKI... GKI... GKI! Lord Hile Troy: Alright, okay, one for each and everyone of you. This way, after we finish cleaning out this federation, and CWO shirts are a thing of the past, you'll already be in _style_! Dave Schutton: Troy! Hile Troy! Dave Schutton, associated world of sports, wrestling division, mind if I ask you a few questions? Lord Hile Troy: Fire away! Dave Schutton: Okay, first of all, WHY GKI? Lord Hile Troy: (rolling eyes) And I thought these would be DIFFICULT questions! Well, I defeated Gold Kard, I won the North American Title and I SAVED the CWO. Then what? Sandman cheats me OUT OF that title, and the CWO offices _won't_ give me a rematch!... THEN, the fans stick it to me and vote Wayne Schillo as the best wrestler there is?... So I'm back in Blackpool England, and I get a phone call from the Golden One. I'll admit, at first I was about ready to throw some choice words at him, but the more I listened, the more it made _sense_ to join him! Between he and I, NO ONE in this league has more experience, more intelligence, or more ability! Not only that, but we've got guys like Masher and Moses, the cream of the crop as far as the new generation. Hell, were unbeatable! Dave Schutton: How many zeros did it take exactly to get you into GKI? Lord Hile Troy: Enough to buy your mom off for the next 10 years... Dave Schutton: Umm... alright... we all witnessed you crashing down on former Television Champion Johnny Rebel with that steel briefcase. Sources say that he's hot to find you, sense the two of you already have a HUGE past. What are your feelings on that? Lord Hile Troy: Exactly, we have a huge past. It goes all the way back to the original CWO. That redneck dosen't deserve to have that title. He should go back to eating soup out of a can in his trailer park at home, with a warm can of beer, watching old "Dukes of Hazzard" reruns on the Nashville Network! Gold Kard was _generous_ enough to let me take care of him. If he comes looking for a match, he'll get one! (Fans around start chants of "GKI" and "Troy") Dave Schutton: Alright, big match for you next week in your ring return against Pride, of the Deadly Sins, any comment? Lord Hile Troy: Well, first off, this _isn't_ my ring return. I've been tagging and tagging WELL I might add, with Vegas Highroller. We ALSO cleaned up at the last pay per view. So now, they give me a TAG wrestler for my first singles match? Oh what is the championship committee of the CWO thinking? That's okay though, I won't under estimate this match, I'll just win this match like all the rest. And don't think, anyone ELSE in the CWO, that we aren't seeing your comments, regulators, Wild Things, oh... we hear you loud and clear, and oh yes, you too, will fall __________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Finally today, we hear from a team that has been very vocal as of late, and who claim to be the reigning CWO North American Tag Team Champions. Of course, I'm talking about none other than the Mercenaries. __________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on the Mercenaries, who are relaxing on a sunny beach somewhere in the Caribbean. Their CWO NA Tag Team titles are not far from their reach. Kaptain Krunch is sitting in a lawn chair wearing dark shades, and Korperal Punishment is relaxing with one of those tropical drinks with the little paper umbrellas sticking out of it.) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Well CWO, once again, it's time for the REAL North American Tag Team Champions to enlighten the dimmed minds of the CWO. Like the Regulators, who still think that they are the champions. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Haven't they got it figured out that we beat them for these titles? We destroyed them. Now on the CWO upcoming Pay Per View, we have a match scheduled with them for the titles. What's wrong with this picture? KAPTAIN KRUNCH: For starters, we say who we defend these titles against. We're the champions after all. Besides, we've already beaten the Regulators. What's left to prove? That the Regulators can take a bigger beating than they already have at our hands? KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: If the CWO wants us to defend these titles, they can kiss our asses. If they try and take them from us, we'll beat their asses. We are the end all, beat all in the tag team ranks. We don't play by anybody's rules but our own. Hell, we've hardly heard a peep out of the Regulators. I guess that they know that they're as good as beaten. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: IF we're in a good mood, we just might put the titles up. But to be completely honest with everybody, there isn't one damn team in the CWO that's good enough to get in the ring with us. That goes for the Highlanders, too. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: So to just simplify everything for everybody, you spell tag team success M-E-R-C-E-N-A-R-I-E-S. Why? Because we're THE TEAM THAT RULES THE WORLD, and you're not. ________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Well, that's all the time we have for today. So long everybody. (fade to closing credits)
