Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SCORCH 11/21/99 (taped 11/18/99) Washington, DC (MCI Center) (SCORCH opening plays.) ANNOUNCER 1: Fans, I understand that we had a crew in the locker room of Ron and Shirley Beauregard during last week's Wayne Carpenter Memorial
Semi-Finals match between the Yakuza and the True Americans. Unfortunately, due to technical difficulties, we were unable to show it to you live...
ANNOUNCER 2: You mean they were aslepp in the truck again? ANNOUNCER 1: I don't think so. Anything is possible I guess. At any rate, we bring you the pre-recorded comments from the Hardest Working Tag Team in
Wrestling, the Beauregards.
(We now cut to the aforementioned footage. The Beauregards are sitting in a couple of folding chairs watching the TV monitor in the back. The Big Cat is up pacing behind the pair. Suddenly, the Yakuza win the match.)
RON: BOSS! ITS THE YAKUZA! BIG CAT: GOOD GOLLY DAY! Lester Sheets actually worked those boys into the finals huh. SHIRLEY: If they can beat anything Alles throws at them, can we beat em? BIG CAT: WELL IF THIEVES DIDN'T STEAL THEY WOULDN'T BE THEIVES! Of course we can beat them Shirley. All we have to do is study.
RON: STUDY? But.....but......you mean.......but......STUDY? What in tarnation do we have to study for Boss.
BIG CAT: If there's one thing I ever teach you two it will be the value of preparation. SHIRLEY: It sells for about five bucks. BIG CAT: WHAT? SHIRLEY: Five bucks. I had to go shopping for Grandma Beauregard the last time I was in Alabama. Preperation H is selling for about five bucks at the
Big Lots in Hoover.
BIG CAT: NOT PREPARATION H YOU TWIT! PREPARATION! RON: You mean.......STUDYING? BIG CAT: If you boys had passed auto-body we'da been all right. I mean preparing. Like.....like.....remember in high school...when you boys carried
Hoover High to the Class AA State semi-finals.
SHIRLEY: We lost that game. BIG CAT: That's right! Because you didn't prepare. You didn't know that
Blountvegas High liked to run the ball. Your coach didn't tell you to get ready for the run...you played the pass and you got drilled.
RON: Yeah, and if we'da known, we WOULD'VE bailed out old Charlie Carter from jail the night before.
SHIRLEY: He was the best defense fella on the team. RON: Even though he did have trouble with stealin cars. BIG CAT: What we have to do boys, is study the Yakuza. Find out what makes the Yakuza tick. We have to.......become the Yakuza.
RON: But Boss, aren't all foriegners the same. SHIRLEY: Yeah. You said that them foriegners were here just to take our titles and our women.
BIG CAT: DOH! I TOLD YOU NEVER TO SAY THAT ON THE AIR! SHIRLEY: Sorry....I forgot. BIG CAT: Edit. 3....2.......That's what we have to do fellas. We aren't the
Hardest Working Tag Team in Wrestling for nothing. We're going to work our tails off in the next couple of weeks. As they say in the biz, "The Titles are on the Line." And you know what titles mean boys.
SHIRLEY: FREE BEER LIKE IN THE COMMERCIALS! BIG CAT: Nope. RON: FREE TRIPS TO SIX FLAGS! BIG CAT: Wrong again. SHIRLEY: A NEW NISSAN SENTRA! BIG CAT: Not even close. SHIRLEY: Well...what then? BIG CAT: 2 for 1 VIPs at the Elite Show club and the Mouse's Ear! RON: ROCK! (With that, we cut back to the announcers) ANNOUNCER 2: Editors aren't what they used to be. ANNOUNCER: It appears that the Big Cat has the Beauregards focused on becoming the North American Tag Team Champions. We'll be right back.

He's a failure, Big Gord. Plain and simple. It's all a failure...this entire federation, from my opponent Iceman X tonight to the entire frigging Cruiserweight division...and, of course, the Alles'. Mary has some sense to get the hell away from THIS family.
