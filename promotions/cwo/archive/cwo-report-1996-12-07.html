CWO REPORT

7 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(On the screen appears the CNN logo, accompanied by its music. Wolf Blitzer is seen standing in front of the camera. Behind him fire crews are desperately trying to put out a huge building swallowed by fire.) BLITZER (He seems a little shaken): This is Wolf Blitzer reporting live from Graceland in Memphis, Tennessee. A shocking and horrible event has just occurred here at the once home of Elvis Presley. The building behind me was once the proud tourist attraction known as Graceland. Now it is an out of control burning ruin. From what I have been told, police expect this to be caused by an explosion. Nothing is for certain, however, until the Federal Investigators can examine the remains. Nothing is known at this time what caused this explosion, but investigators suspect it to have been a bomb. Police have no clues as of yet to who could have done this. The only information left is writing left on the cement that reads: There is only one king. It appears to have been written in red paint. (Just then a person walks up to Wolf and whispers something in his ear.) BLITZER: I have just been informed that the police have some correlation to the case. It would appear, just before dawn in an obscure small town in Tennessee, a gun store was broken into. The owner was just opening the privately owned shop when he was assailed by an unknown attacker. The attacker broke in, beat him mercilessly, and stole a variety of supplies. Among the items stolen were supplies that police authorities say could have been fashioned into a homemade bomb. Also, stolen was a can of red paint. The owner of the store has then taken to the nearest hospital were he appears to be in stable condition. (Wolf pauses as the same man approaches him and informs him of further details.) BLITZER (his head drops for a second, he resumes saddened): I was just informed that the owner of the store that was broken into has died in the hospital of a heart attack. It is unknown if it was caused by the beating he took or his old age. Police did manage to get a brief description of the attacker. The old man claimed it was a giant wearing a mask. Police now believe that the man who committed both of these crimes was the professional wrestler known as Bane, who is wanted for numerous charges. So far he has been able to dodge the authorities, but they are now setting up road blocks at all the exits of the state in hopes of apprehending him. For those of you just tuning in, Graceland has been destroyed by an explosion which officers believe to have been caused by a bomb. More information will be brought to you in near future. For CNN, I'm Wolf Blitzer. (CNN logo comes up.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Saturday, December 7, 1996. As you can see, the Bane situation has elevated to such a high degree, that many are wondering how the CWO can sanction an event involving him and Sandman. For insight into this, we now go to CWO Commissioner Randy Johnson, live via satellite. Randy, welcome to the REPORT. (Randy Johnson is shown in his CWO office.) RANDY JOHNSON: Thank you very much, Johnny. STARR: Mr. Johnson, as I just stated, the big question is: how can the CWO sanction such a match? JOHNSON: Well, Johnny, the fact is, we're not. This is an unsanctioned event, a locked steel cage affair. However, we are giving Sandman and Bane this forum because both requested it, and quite a few CWO fans want to see it. STARR: But what about Bane's criminal actions? JOHNSON: Well, hold on there, Johnny. First of all, we do not know if it is indeed Bane who has been committing these crimes. In this country, let's remember, you're innocent until proven guilty. So, at CWO CELEBRATION, a locked steel cage, an open invitation to both men, Sandman and Bane, and the match will go until one of the man cannot continue. STARR: How will that be judged, Mr. Johnson? Will a referee be in the cage as well? I wouldn't want to be that referee! JOHNSON (smiling): No, ah wouldn't want to be in there with those two mad lions either. However, as Commissioner of this great wrestling organization, ah have taken it upon myself to be the man who will judge when the match should stop. And don't worry, ah've been through enough bloody battles that ah can take it. Ah've got a strong stomach. And if one of those two guys won't stop when ah demand it, well, let me just say that the Texas Rose still has a lot of fight left in him, and ah can take either of those two if ah have to. STARR: All right, thank you very much, Mr. Johnson. Moving onto other matters, last night, the superstars of the CWO were in Pierre, SD, let's look at how that card went down. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW REPORT FRIDAY, DECEMBER 6, 1996 PIERRE, SD STREET FIGHT: Bad Guys vs. Lafayette and Rooster Bad Guys defeated Lafayette and Rooster in 16:20 when Johnson pinned Lafayette after the Bad Guys' Bash (striking him with illegal objects). Steve Keller (substituting for the injured Mad Doctor's Ghost) vs. "Big" Rich Clark "Big" Rich Clark pinned Steve Keller in 7:03 after an elbowdrop. Mason-Dixon vs. Mondo Gecko and Battlecat Mason-Dixon defeated Mondo Gecko and Battlecat in 23:08 when Stonewall Mason pinned Battlecat after the Southern Spike Piledriver. After the match, Mason-Dixon were attacked by the Creatures of the Night. Stonewall Mason's right knee was injured by them. Sandman Jr. vs. Rooster Sandman Jr. defeated Rooster in 14:05 after the Permanent Dream flying legdrop. Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Hile Troy Hile Troy pinned Brent "The Ace" Alles in 6:09 after the Dragon Suplex. Flame Spirits vs. Team Blitzkrieg Team Blitzkreig defeated the Flame Spirits in 7:22 when Mysteria pinned Wolf after the Revolution top rope forearm. LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Talon vs. Dark Raven (champion) Talon and Dark Raven wrestled to a double disqualification in 4:42. TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (Spin the Wheel, falls count anywhere, non-title): Miguel Sanchez (champion) vs. Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo pinned Miguel Sanchez in 9:28 in the stands after hitting him with a chair. NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Sandman vs. Gold Kard Gold Kard pinned Sandman in 20:59 after Die Flieder Kross (flying somersault splash). _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: As you saw in the report, a brutal attack by the Creatures of the Night on Mason-Dixon, obviously trying to send a message to Johnny Reb. Early reports indicate that Stonewall Mason may be out for up to seven weeks. We'll keep you posted. Tonight, though, we are in Calgary, Alberta, Canada, let's look at the card there. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW SATURDAY, DECEMBER 7, 1996 CALGARY, ALBERTA, CANADA Deadly Sins vs. Mondo Gecko and Battlecat STRAP MATCH: Hellhound vs. Johnny Reb Joe Eagle vs. Brent "The Ace" Alles Vince Manocelli vs. Mad Doctor's Ghost Big Cool vs. Highlanders Ganglord vs. Wayne Carpenter Gold Kard vs. Hile Troy NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Pit Bosses vs. Mercenaries WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Creatures of the Night vs. Flame Spirits WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Master of Darkness Damion (champion) vs. "Master of Pain" Barry Hart _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Again, Mad Doctor's Ghost injury at the hands of Brent "The Ace" Alles will probably prevent him from wrestling tonight, look for somebody to take his place. We now take you to comments from CWO superstars, after this commercial break. _______________________________________________________________________________ **COMMERCIAL BREAK** Hi, you don't know me, but I'm publicizing the NGK (Next Generation Kombat). You make Mortal Kombat Kharacters (heh heh heh) and, well, fight. Enter the fed and join Bane's Court (go to this address and see what I mean!) http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Field/3459/ There are 6 guys already. You can have man or woman. 10 more and a tournament starts... HURRY! **COMMERCIAL BREAK** _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on MOD Damion who is watching the taped comments from the previous nights' CWO report. He finishes and shuts off the TV, turns to the camera, and begins.) MOD DAMION: Looks like everyone is just a little pissed off at The Dark Side, well tough s@#$. Look, we rule the CWO ,we hold most off the gold and soon shall hold all the gold. Johnny Reb, on Sunday, December 15, you do not have a prayer on Sunday. I will destroy you, I will not hold nothing back this time. Johnny Reb, on that night I take more than just your soul, I want your very existence, I want your confidence and yes, I want your life. Reb, you will not leave the ring with this belt and you will not leave under your own power if you leave at all. Rebel Yell, you too will have more than just the match to worry about because I will be bringing my Creatures to the ring with me, just to even the odds. Johnny Reb, prepare all you want but it will not help. I have all my forces after you and the first to get their hands on you is my big pet Hellhound. See you at CELEBRATION if you make it there. As for "Big" Rich Clark, consider yourself lucky you beat me once. If there is a next time, you will not be so lucky. And Pit Bosses, my Creatures can handle you any time they want. When the belts are on the line, they are a completely different team. No one can beat them. Oh yes, Pit Bosses, watch for a new tag team to be formed sometime soon from The Dark Side and you two will be their targets as long as you wear those NA tag belts. The DARK SIDE RULES YOU ALL, and the time is rapidly coming when the time will come for all of you to pay due to us all. You all shall bow before the Dark Side. LONG LIVE THE NIGHT! (Camera fades as MOD walks away.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (In a lost long cave... somewhere in the mountains of the Andes. Lord Guru and the Eternal Wanderers, Exile and Exodus, are there.) LORD GURU: My children... I bring you back here for a reason... (His eyes peer out at them from his hood.) LORD GURU: This is no place for a home... but you will come back here and be locked up if you do not succeed! (Both Exile and Exodus cower... and bow to him.) EXODUS: My lord... I have been given the job of finding our home... I claim the CWO... and we have done well, my LORD! Don't you see, we have done well! LORD GURU: Not WELL enough! (whispers) For the end is near... and you will be stuck here. EXILE: My lord... I stand to please you... (Exile laughs maniacally.) EXILE: We will Prove it to you... first I will dismantle the young Sandman... (Exile laughs insanely, hits the wall with his head, blood starts to seep down over the already dried up blood on his head.) EXILE: Don't you see... this man will fall... and fall he will. (Exile looks into the darkness... hands outstretched.) EXILE: I Want a home.... (laughs out loud) I Want a home... and the CWO is it. (Exodus grabs a rope and ties up the insane Exile.) EXODUS: My Lord... he loses his mind every day... first the NIWF, Sean Torrington... then the Deranged Ones in the WHCW... it is time for our home to SOLIDIFY! First, Sandman Jr., prepare for a rude awakening when you meet this lunatic. (Sighs) But once we're through with you... FIRST YOU, FLAME SPIRITS... and then YOU, BIBLICAL TOWERS! You faced The Cabin in the WWAW and LOST TO THE ANGELS OF GURU... and NOW... YOU FACE THE CABIN HERE! HERE, THE ETERNAL WANDERERS WILL BECOME THE EXTREME SURVIVORS! LORD GURU: Not yet, Exodus... until you prove that to me! Until you prove it to me! Come my children... (A portal of light appears at the end of the Tunnel.) LORD GURU: We must prepare.... (They all walk into the light... as the scene fades out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Hile Troy sits in the recently moved offices at the headquarters of the CWO in downtown Grand Rapids, Michigan. It is obvious he is in an executive's room.) HILE TROY: Like the chair? It belongs to Randy Johnson himself, or as he prefers me to call him, "RJ". Hey, dumpkoff, see this big emblem behind my head, it says "Cyberspace Wrestling Organization", not the "Cyberspace Mark Two Whatever the hell it was called." Everything goes through cycles, economics, musical preference, everything, and your domination in the league is no exception. Not since MY Chaotic Dynasty has one man had so much influence in the league. (One of Johnson's secretaries enters the room and brings Troy a large silver tray of chilled shrimp.) HILE TROY: How can I stay in the light heavyweight division with this bounty? Alles, I guess you found out who you can trust and who you can't, and now your dream has blown up in your face at the hands of the wonderpunk. You know, it JUST occurred to me that if I beat him, I walk away the North American champion! This match isn't the "North American Title Match" at the pay per view, it's the "CWO vs. CM2WF" match, and men out there like Schillo, Eagle, Ganglord, Bane, Gecko... Gacko... whatever the hell your name is, you may not like me, but your support has to be fully behind me, or maybe I should just get disqualified? (Troy's eyes gaze down onto his desk, where he reads a headline on a newspaper: "Mystery Man Still on the loose.") HILE TROY (murmuring): Mystery man my sweet a... er, yeah, I'm a fan favorite again, aren't I? Sometimes I tend to forget. Bane, don't think that the defense of this federation has made me forget about your FOUL self. My little pupil, Bane, I do hope you are having fun, galavanting around, attacking paramedics and cops and drunken hobo's the way you are, because when you and I step into the ring, no matter what the conditions are, you're going to meet your MAKER! You will suffer your first convincing loss in the league, well, that is figuring Sandy doesn't tear you apart in that cage, which he is more than likely to do. Bane, you are near the top of Hile Troy's hit list... with the annoying Gold Kard, Damion, I'm watching you son... Schillo, little boy, good luck, you'll need it. Ganglord, yes, I can see you even from WAY up here in the clouds of success, and Wildfire, if you need help, I'll serve you as a better ally than anyone else you could find. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today, we'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT.
