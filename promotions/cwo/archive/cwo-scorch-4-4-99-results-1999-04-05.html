SCORCH 4/4/99 - Results

April 5, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SCORCH 4/4/99 LIVE FROM: Indianapolis, IN (Market Square Arena) HOUR #1 Cris Marcus and Kent Alles opened the show. Marcus discussed why he betrayed the Outlaw Riders. Then, Kent Alles called out Lord Hile Troy. He told the CWO legend that the Alleses had one other little “favor” to ask of him, to take Snake Martin out tonight. If Troy didn’t comply, then he wouldn’t have a chance at that #59 or #60 slot in the MAY-HEM battle royal
(not to mention the fact that he still had to take Brent “The Ace” Alles out either before or during the APRIL ARMAGEDDON event as well!). To make matters worse, Cris Marcus was going to accompany Troy down to ringside, to make sure things went all right.
MATCH ONE: Australian Assassin pinned Kamikaze after the DDT, using the ropes for leverage. Time of match: 0:54.
After the match was over, Masher Maccabee came down to indirectly thank the
Assassin for his save the other night, and to offer to “train” him for the
“big time”. The long-time CWO veteran accepted.
MATCH TWO: 'Mr. Technical' Cooper Young and “Dragonfire” Rich Johnson (who apparently is back to calling himself “Calvin” again, even going so far as to carry a stuffed tiger to ringside with him) wrestled to a double countout. Time of match: 15:00.
The Sheets Foundation had an interview in the ring, but it ended badly as red, white, and blue confetti exploded over top of them, courtesy of the
True Americans.
MATCH THREE: In a match to determine the #1 contenders to the North American
Tag Team Titles, Bad Company (Ewiak and Taggart) defeated Warhawk and the
Fallen when 'The Avenger' Jesse Ewiak pinned Fallen after the superplex.
Time of match: 12:20. New True American Union Caine and his fellow True Americans (Sandman Jr,
Allen “Mr. America” Carter and Geoff “The Attitude Adjuster” Bondavarsky)
attacked Mike Sexton in the locker room before Caine and Sexton’s matchup, probably to soften him up for Union Caine.
MATCH FOUR: Union Caine pinned Mike 'Money' Sexton after the Cainedriver. K ent Alles prevented the other Outlaws from accompanying Sexton down to the ring (only his manager Trent Charles was allowed). Time of match: 10:08.
HOUR #2: MATCH FIVE: Masher Maccabee defeated Secret Agent Super Dragon by forfeit. (The True Americans, dressed as cowboys, hog tied and gagged SASD, along with the rest of the Sheets Foundation, before the match.)
Wrath and Sandman had an interview, discussing who Pride’s mystery partner might be tonight.
A new tag team coming in to the CWO, Hardcore Sensation, was shown having a press conference.
Pride was shown, getting ready for his match with Wrath and Sandman. MATCH SIX: The original Deadly Sins reunited, as Pride and Sloth (the mystery partner) defeated Wrath and Sandman when Pride pinned Sandman after the superplex. The match saw the ring break after a forceful whip into the corner of Sloth. Time of match: 9:58. After the match Sandman still attacked the Deadly Sins, and even Sandblasted the 450 pound Sloth off the top turnbuckle!
“Playboy” Wayne Schillo had an interview, along with his own “Schillo Girls,” and not one, not two, but three Playmates! (How does this guy rate?) MATCH SEVEN: Reb and Fantastic defeated Hollywood Haskins/Iceman X in a wild
“Iceman Rules” match (meaning there were none) when Masher Maccabee and
Australian Assassin attacked Iceman X and Hollywood Haskins, setting the
Iceman up for the pin. Lots of “plunder” was used before that, including a keyboard, guitar, shovel, you name it! Time of match: 8:16.
The Creatures of Darkness discussed their upcoming match with Reb and Fantastic at APRIL ARMAGEDDON ’99. MATCH EIGHT: Snake Martin defeated Lord Hile Troy by disqualification when
Cris Marcus attacked. Time of match: 9:30. The other Outlaws made the save as the show came to a close.
