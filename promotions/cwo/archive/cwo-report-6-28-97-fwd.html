CWO REPORT 6/28/97 (fwd)

29 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on several CWO wrestlers in action. CWO REPORT flashes by and fade in to REPORT host, Mitch E. Washey.) WASHEY: Hello everybody and welcome to another edition of the CWO REPORT. We are coming to you from Cincinatti where the CWO House Show just wrapped up. It was an exciting night, so let's get right to the results. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 6/28/97 CINCINATTI, OH Steel made Matthew "The Man" White submit to The Gank (Texas Cloverleaf). Time of match: 12:59. Vlad the Impaler pinned 'Mr. Niceguy' Steve White after the Long Kiss Goodnight (bite to the neck). Time of match: 11:29. Wildfire was scheduled to meet the masked man who had been hanging around 'Monster' Rich Clark as of late. Instead of the masked man, Miguel Sanchez appeared, saying that since there was anarchy in the CWO that he didn't have to abide by the rule that he could not compete anymore when he lost to 'Monster' Rich Clark earlier this year. Wildfire then went on to pin Miguel Sanchez after the Firebawl (backflip moonsault). Time of match: 12:06. Moses was scheduled to take on Brent 'The Ace' Alles but Gold Kard and Alles' father Gordy told Moses to cease and desist as an obviously inebriated Alles was not fit to wrestle and besides, Alles was a GKI member whether he liked it or not. Moses won th e match by default. Diamondback Chris Myers pinned Wrath after the spinning elbowdrop. Time of match: 7:55. Panther made 'The Brazilian Lover' Carlos Maia submit to the Eclipse (Oriental thumb and sleeperhold). Time of match: 4:52. Wayne 'College Guy' Schillo pinned El Grande Pistolerro after the forearm smash when El Grande Pistolerro charged at him in the corner. Time of match: 11:40. Schillo's feet were accidently on the ropes when he pinned Pistolerro, and Schillo tried to exp lain that this was unintentional but a ticked off Pistolerro would not accept his explanation and accused him of being another "GKI sellout" before leaving in a huff. Benjamin Blake Bentley/Lord Hile Troy defeated the currently beltless North American Tag Team Champions the Regulators when Benjamin Blake Bentley pinned Regulator Ice after Lord Hile Troy struck Regulator Ice with a cane when the referee was not looking. Time of match: 9:43. WASHEY: The CWO will be in Dayton, OH for a special Sunday afternoon card. Here's the lineup for tomorrow afternoon. SUNDAY, JUNE 29, 1997 DAYTON, OH Bloody Fangs vs. Benjamin Blake Bentley and Lord Hile Troy Wrath vs. Inferno Deadly Sins vs. Bishop/Dark Raven Sandman Jr. vs. Vegas High Roller Wild Ones vs. Gold Kard & Matsuhiro Torunaka El Grande Pistolerro vs. Carlos "The Brazilian Lover" Maia NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Regulators (champions) vs. Mercenaries NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (two out of three falls): Brian "The Hammer" Williams vs. Tiger Rogers (champion) WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (no DQ): Bad Guys vs. Highlanders (champions) WASHEY: Bell time is 1:00 pm. There are a few tickets remaining, so don't miss out on what's sure to be a great card. It's now time for the interview portion of the REPORT, and today we start with a tag team that is scheduled to appear tomorrow in Dayton, the Mercenaries. ___________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on the Mercenaries, who are holding the CWO NA Tag Team titles over their shoulders.) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: You know, we've come out here for the past two weeks, telling everybody that we are the undisputed NA Tag Team champions. But in the meantime, everybody else has been saying that the Regulators are still the champions. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: We're getting damn sick of it, too. In fact, we haven't heard one single word from the Regulators since we beat them for the titles. Now that tells us one of two things. That they're either letting their wrestling do the talking for them, which isn't saying a hell of a lot, or they're a couple of scared, gutless punks, who know that we are the rightful champions of the belts that they formerly held and are too damn scared to speak. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: And it doesn't take an Einstein to figure out which one we think they are. The Regulators used to talk a good game, at least they talked better than they fought, but now you don't hear a peep out of them. Why? Probably for the same reason Brother Justice decided to keep their big mouths shut after they got the Mercenaries attention. They've got a yellow streak bigger than a six lane highway running straight down the middle of their backs. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: For two weeks, we've come out here and told everybody like it is. Tomorrow, the Regulators step in the ring with us for the first time since we kicked their asses from one end of the ring to the other. Now what do you think they'll have to say about that? My guess is that they'll say something to an official, trying to worm their way out of the match. After the beating they got from us last time, it wouldn't surprise me. Regulators, we're calling you two pieces of scum out. Tomorrow, if you think you are the rightful owners of these titles, drag your scroungy asses down to the ring and prove it. But you'd just better remember that you're stepping in the ring with THE TEAM THAT RULES THE WORLD, and we have your number, you cowards. ____________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Some extremely tough talk from the self proclaimed "Team that rules the world". Next on the REPORT, we hear comments from one of the hottest young stars in the CWO, Diamondback Chris Myers. ____________________________________________________________________________ (Diamondback Chris Myers and his lovely manager Dallas are standing outside the Civic Arena in Downtown Pittsburgh. The sign outside the box office states "CWO FEVER, 7/1/97, SOLD OUT!!!" Dallas is wearing a Steelers jersey, and Myers is in jeans and an Arizona Diamondbacks baseball jersey) Myers: Tuesday, a lovely young lady comes home! Dallas: That's right! I was born and raised about 20 minutes from where me and Chris are standing, and this is the first time I've come back to Pittsburgh ever since I became a manager! Myers: And this is my adopted hometown too! I'm from Phoenix, as you all can tell from the shirt, but I've trained in Oakland, 5 minutes away, ever since I began wrestling! And this crowd is going to see Diamondback Chris Myers win his first title in CWO! Dallas: Benjamin Blake Bentley, TV champ. Otherwise known as BBB. Which is also the sound you make when you give someone a raspberry, and Tuesday, SIR Bentley is going to get one hell of a raspberry, right? Myers: You said it. Bentley, everyone is pointing to me and saying 'There's the future of CWO.' Well, in order to prove that statement true, I need gold! And since you're the most pathetic excuse for a champion I've ever seen...hell, Benjamin, the only reason you've got that belt around your waist is an assist from Lord Hile Troy! In my manager's hometown, in my home for the past 4 years, do you really think GKI's going to bail you out? I don't think so. Now, onto the next person. Wrath, you point to me and say "Here's a Rocky Miavia type, who ain't gonna last in CWO." This is my third CWO PPV, and some fans are getting ready to annoint me CWO's savior, which by the way, is completely false. As I said before, CWO needs to pull together, and one man ain't going to save it by himself. But I digress... Wrath, you'll be getting a title shot at R.I.D., and you'll probably walk in expecting a cakewalk. Well, take a good long look at me, Wrath, because the next time you see me, I'll be carrying that TV belt and painted up for war, baby! ' Dallas: And Miss Lust, I don't suggest trying anything, because my man will be so focused, you can't distract him. Now, if you turn those attentions towards me...well, that's another story! Myers: And last, Ryan Deustcher. "Lone Wolf", we've done this dance before, and I came out on top. I am not prading around, saying how great I am. I'm letting my actions talk for me. If you want to go another round, Ryan, fine. After R.I.D., you can fight me, for the TV belt which will be around my waist, come hell or high water. BBB, sorry to end your reign so fast, but I'm striking a blow against GKI, and you just happen to be on the receiving end of that punch. See you in Pittsburgh, blue blood, and see you in Philly, Wrath. ______________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Finally today, we will hear comments from the CWO Television Champion, Benjamin Blake Bentley. Let's go to him now. ______________________________________________________________________________ (Scene opens with BBB over-seeing one of the many household helpers from stately Bentley Manor polishing his belt.) BBB: Careful you fool! Don't you know how to polish a simple buckle you fool?! Look at that! (holds the man's head and jerks belt toward it) Look I say! You see that!? You left some polish in the crack of my nameplate. You may begin packing. Man: Yes sir. (Man leaves the room looking down at his feet like a scolded child) BBB: My God man.....I need this belt in prime condition since I'm going to be the holder for a good long time. (places belt on it's mount by the fireplace) Yes. (looks at camera) You heard me Meyers. You won't get my belt this Tuesday. I have bigger fish to fry, so I won't even give you a snowball's chance to get a second glimpse at it. (smirks) And after you it's that......wrestler...Panther. What impact has he ever had on this sport? I think the CWO booking board needs some ventilation in their offices. Panther. I have one thing to say to you. It will by my pleasure humiliating you. which brings me to my old friend. The Jokus. (laughs) You know you have not a chance with me. You brag about your wins over me, but compare them to my wins over you and you're bush-leagues boy. I will, however give you a chance to try and fight for my belt. Just not in the near future. I'm booked solid....So...come down on Fever Tuesday if you want one last look at the belt before it goes away on a long trip away from you. (Scene fades out with BBB chuckling to himself. Before complete blackout you hear him mutter 'Ha. Focus. Proof God has a sense of humor.') ______________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: That's all the time we have for the REPORT today. We'll see everybody tomorrow! (fade to closing credits)
