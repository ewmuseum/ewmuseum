House Show Results 6/12/99

June 13, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HOUSE SHOW 6/12/99 Ft. Lauderdale, FL (National Car Rental Center) 'Assassin 64' Sean Holiday (subbing for Simon St. Severe) pinned Brian 'The
King' Derks after the Permanent Vacation. Time of match: 9:35.
Omar Martinez pinned Vladimir Popalov after the Seeing Stars (swinging neckbreaker). Time of match: 4:20.
Mondo Gecko and North American Champion Black Jack Hollister defeated "The
Ultimate Athlete" Cris Marcus and Smilin' Doc Watson when Black Jack
Hollister pinned Smilin' Doc Watson after the Texas Stampede. Time of match: 15:28. Deliverance made 'The Avenger' Jesse Ewiak submit to the Squealer. Time of match: 11:19. Diamondback Chris Myers/'Airborne' Mark Fulcrum defeated Creatures of Darkness when Diamondback Chris Myers pinned Night Crawler after the Rattler. Time of match: 9:53. World Tag Team Champion Johnny Reb made Southern Dynamite submit to the
Texas Cloverleaf. Time of match: 9:09. Johnny Reb was attacked by
Deliverance after the match! North American Tag Team Champions the Hardcore Sensation defeated Strictly
Business in a non-title match when 'Radical' Kirk Madness pinned Mr. Eagle after the Sensational Bomb (Madness holds opponent up on his shoulders,
Insanity catches them with an Asai leg lariat off the top rope). Time of
match: 4:22. Brent 'The Ace' Alles defeated Darkness by disqualification when the Creatures of Darkness attacked him when he had Darkness in the Alles Clutch. Time of match: 29:23. Surprisingly, Mondo Gecko and Black Jack Hollister made the save!
