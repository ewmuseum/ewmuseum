What this means is that any arena the CWO holds a show in basically becomes in essence an "extreme zone," where anything goes. Backstage is no longer necessarily a safe place for these wrestlers, it is indeed an area where the fights that shape their lives can continue. Division, where "anything goes."

We have camera crews not only around the ring to film the action going on there, but also roaming backstage to capture the goings-on back there as well. In addition, hidden cameras are placed to capture more footage for us to present. Every wrestler and personality in and around that ring now, as part of their contract, knows that anything and everything that goes on in the CWO EXTREME ZONE… will probably be caught on tape for the viewing public to witness.
