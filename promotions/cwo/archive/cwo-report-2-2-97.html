CWO REPORT 2/2/97

2 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Footage taken from the 2/1/97 House Show in Concord, NH, is shown, with the camera opening on the CWO 6-man chapionship belt. It then zooms out to show Hellhound stroking his belt like a child would a small kitten. The Creatures of the Night are with him.) NIGHT SLINGER: Coldfront, you guys can't get the job done alone and take the world titles from us, so you enlist the help of the loser "Lonestar" Mike Williams and think that you will take our 6-man titles with his help. I don't think so. Hellhound would have no trouble defeating you, Mike Williams, in a singles match. NIGHT CRAWLER: That's right, and paired with us, he is even better. You boys are welcome to enter into this world of DARKNESS, but you shall never leave. It has been a while since we brought a new soul for the master and now is the perfect time to start again. HELLHOUND: Yes, yes, master will have three new souls to play with after we beat the Coldfront and the Lonestar, he will be so happy with us. NIGHT SLINGER: Coldfront, there has been no one able to stop us so far and there will never be anyone able to stop us including the three of you. ALL THREE: LONG LIVE THE NIGHT! (Camera fades to black.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Sunday, February 2, 1997. Last night the CWO was in Concord, NH, let's look at the results from that great card! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 2-1-97 NEW HAMPSHIRE The Big Cool defeated Deadly Sins when Jumbo Jenkins pinned Pride after the Star Flash (backbreaker by Taylor, flying big splash by Jumbo). Time of match: 18:15. Johnny Reb pinned Ganglord after the leg drop. Time of match: 4:05. Mason-Dixon defeated Revolution when Stonewall Mason pinned Lafayette after the Southern Spike Piledriver. Time of match: 19:21. Brian 'The Hammer' Williams pinned Brent 'The Ace' Alles after the Keuka Smack (boxing of the ears, then an uppercut). Time of match: 10:41. Eternal Wanderers defeated Pit Bosses when Exile pinned Pit Boss #1 after the End of the World (Exile with a Boston crab, Exodus with a moonsault). Time of match: 10:39. Gold Kard pinned Miguel Sanchez after the Die Flieder Kross, using the ropes for leverage. Time of match: 2:56. Bane made Maximum Overdrive submit to the Merciful Death. Time of match: 12:10. WORLD 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Lonestar and the Coldfront defeated The Dark Side (Hellhound and the Creatures of the Night) when 'Mr. Niceguy' Steve White pinned Night Slinger after the Coldsnap. Time of match: 2:06. 'Lonestar' Mike Williams and the Coldfront won the CWO 6-Man Tag Team Championship. Before the match the Dark Side was attacked by Johnny Reb and Mason-Dixon. WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Benjamin Blake Bentley pinned Wayne 'Keuka Guy' Schillo after the splash and outside interference from 'Monster' Rich Clark (chokeslamming Schillo outside the ring). Time of match: 9:03. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Don't forget about the great CWO SHOWCASE coming out later today, here is the complete lineup for the show. _______________________________________________________________________________ CWO SHOWCASE 2/2/97 Ron "The Rude Guy" Johnson vs. Steve Keller Mondo Gecko vs. Tiger Rogers Benjamin Blake Bentley vs. Flyboy David "The Destroyer" Davis vs. "Lonestar" Mike Williams The Big Cool (Jumbo and Jazzman) vs. Revolution (Lafayette and Black Death) Dark Raven vs. Panther Sin vs. Johnny Reb Brian "The Hammer" Williams vs. Sandman Jr. LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Talon vs. Moses (champion) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: The big news is that we have new World 6-Man Tag Team Champions! Congratulations to "Lonestar" Mike Williams and the Coldfront, however, as you saw, the victory was not totally on the up and up. The Dark Side was attacked by Southern Pride before the match, and it is very clear now that Johnny Reb has totally declared war on the Dark Side. With attacks coming from all sides, are the CWO World Tag Team Champions, the Creatures of the Night, overextended? Tomorrow, the final lineup for ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II will come out, but already we have told you some of the great matches that will appear on that PPV. One of those involves the World Tag Team Championship, a match between the Creatures of the Night and the quickly rising Highlanders. Master of Darkness Damion doesn't think too highly of this team, but the Creatures of the Night should be careful not to overlook the Scottish brawlers, or they may lose their second championship in the month of February. We take you now to comments from the Highlanders. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Scene opens in a CWO studio; in it are Gavin and Conor McCloud, the Highlanders.) CONOR MCCLOUD: What's that you're sayin', Damion? Stand up, man, yer voice is muffled! Hahahaha! Drunken fools we mae be, but we're gonna be the drunken tag team champions aftae St. Valentine's Day massacre! We beat yer little friends once, and we're gonna do it again! GAVIN MCCLOUD: So we made a little mistake - it happens to tha' best o' us all. And your big mistake will be accepting that title bid, Creatures of the Night. Oh, let me guess, we're gonna feel tha' power the 'Dark Side', right? Whoooo, I've soiled me kilt over that one. Give me a break... and here's a warnin' to the rest of tha' Dark Side; keep yer nose out of this match if you want to keep them. We've got balls bigger than King Kong, and if you try ta interfere with our match, you'll be on the end of a good ol' fashioned kicking. Just remember..... CONOR MCCLOUD: THERE CAN BE ONLY ONE! Cheers! Hahahahaha..... (Scene fades to black.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: We now have comments from the other warring party against the Dark Side, Johnny Reb. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Johnny Reb and General Lee appear in the interview area.) LEE: Well, Damion, it all will come to a head at St. Valentines Day Massacuh II! Friday naht, February the 14th, Johnny Reb will get his revenge. On that Friday naht, Johnny Reb will end it. End your career. End your existence. REB: Damion, ah have been through the ring wars. Ah've spilled mah blood all over the CWO. Through it all, General Lee has been beside me. Through it all, General Lee has seen the good, the bad, and the ugly. Son, it will be ugly on that Friday naht, mark mah words. But the only way mah towel is gonna hit the mat is if y'all throw it in there yourself! LEE: That's raht! Nobody in this league can take what mah man has taken in the past. At St. Valentines Day Massacuh, Damion, Johnny Reb is gonna run you down, wear you out, and bury you alive. REB: As for you Panther, ah was breakin' backs with the Texas Clovuhleaf while you were still hangin' out on the corner bumming quarters. Y'all just stick ta what y'all know best...them three hussies. You don't want any of this. LEE: Tahm is runnin' out on you Damion. Good day, suh. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: We haven't heard from him in a while, but Ganglord wants to make sure he is not forgotten here in the CWO, especially to one individual in particular, Bloodbath. Let's hear from the man from Sacramento. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera fades in on Ganglord, who is not in a good mood.) GANGLORD: Bloodbath, I'm getting very sick to my stomach watching you wrestle, you want to know why? Well, I'll tell you, I want to get in that ring with you so bad it's not even funny. So we can get this crap over with and I may move on to bigger and bettr things, so you better be watching your back, Bloodbath! (Camera fades out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Well, that's all the time we have for today... (Gold Kard enters the set.) STARR: Wait a minute... what are you doing here? GOLD KARD: Shut up, dumbkopf, I'm rich, I can do what I want! Sanchez! By now you have seen the knowledge and the skill I possess. Last night in Concord, NH, I showed you the legacy that I can pass down to you, the legacy of Gold Kard International. Don't be a fool, Sanchez. Accept the offer. I await your response at this week's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER. Do not disappoint me, Herr Sanchez. (Gold Kard leaves.) STARR (annoyed): We'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT.
