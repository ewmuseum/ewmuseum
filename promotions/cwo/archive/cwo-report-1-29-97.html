CWO REPORT 1/29/97

29 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Wednesday, January 29, 1997. Last night was an explosive TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER in St. Louis, MO, let's look at what went down! _______________________________________________________________________________ TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER RECAP 1/28/97 ST. LOUIS, MO DARK MATCH: Sandman Jr. and "Monster" Rich Clark wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. DARK MATCH: Highlanders defeated David and Goliath by countout in 1:34. (Tower of Power had a press conference, announcing the retirement of Vince Manocelli due to injuries, and introducing Jason "Flash" Varga, who would be Vince's replacement in a match later on that night. Intro by Mike Postema and Jerry Covenant, then an interview with the Deadly Sins before Bane and Sin's match. Comments from Sin about Maximum Overdrive before the match begins as well.) Bane and Sin wrestled to a double countout after outside interference by the Deadly Sins Pride, Sloth, and Wrath. (Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo and Brian "The Hammer" Williams had an interview.) Dark Raven defeated Phantom Freightrain in 2:01 after the Death From Above flying dropkick. (Comments from Talon about how he is Sandman's "hitman" now against Hile Troy.) Bloodbath defeated Talon as a result of a reversed decision/disqualification in 7:05 when Hile Troy revealed that Sandman had broken an empty champagne bottle over Bloodbath's head. Coldfront defeated the returning Mason-Dixon by disqualification when the pairing of Hell's Pets (Hellhound and Dark Raven) attacked the Coldfront. The Creatures of the Night also came down, and the match degenerated into an 8-man brawl. Flyboy defeated Steel in 12:56 with the Flying Bodypress. (Comments from the Wild Ones, Mondo Gecko and Battlecat, about how they are ready for tonight's match with Eagle and his new partner. When we come back from commercial, Joe Eagle introduces Varga to the crowd.) Attitude Dance (Joe Eagle and Jason "Flash" Varga) defeat the Wild Ones in 4:31 when Jason "Flash" Varga pinned Battlecat after the Flash Flood (swivel of the hips, belly bump, then clothesline). Brian "The Hammer" Williams defeated Maximum Overdrive in 14:34 to win the Television Championship. (In a humorous interview, the Highlanders, forgetting that the World Tag Team champions are the Creatures of the Night, not the Deadly Sins, made a challenge to them, calling them the Sins through the whole thing.) Pit Bosses defeated the Eternal Wanderers by disqualification when Lord Guru shoved Pit Boss #2 off the top turnbuckle, preventing the Spike Piledriver attempt. Afterwards, both teams brawled back to the dressing room. (Comments from Damion towards his match with Hile Troy for the #1 contenders spot. Commercial, and then comments from Sandman, who makes a rather unusual challenge to MOD on the next FEVER, a blindfold match.) Hile Troy defeated Master of Darkness Damion in 10:17 to earn the title shot against Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo (or whomever the World Champion is) at ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II. Troy was unconscious on the mat after a Darkness Falls tombstone piledriver by Damion, and a victory for him seemed imminent, when suddenly, Johnny Reb attacked with a chair, DDT'ing the former World Champion on to it. Reb rolled Troy on top of Damion for the cover and the win. Because the match was a no-disqualification, no-countout affair, the ref could not disqualify Troy for Reb's outside interference. Reb then got on the mike and said that because Damion had vowed that Reb would never hold gold in the CWO while Damion was around, that the tables were turned, and now Damion would not hold gold while Reb was around! Then, more confusion was added to the outcome, as "Monster" Rich Clark stormed the ring, choke slammed the still groggy Hile Troy, then delivered a speech to the crowd about how he was a renewed threat to the CWO. Then, the lights went out, except for a spotlight over the ring, as Sandman's voice could be heard over the loudspeaker, telling Clark that he (Sandman) was the real monster, and that Clark would find that out. The show ended as the lights came back on, and Clark called out for Sandman. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: A lot of twists and turns last night, our congratulations to Brian "The Hammer" Williams for winning the CWO Television title, he remains undefeated here in the league. So, we are now left with two things from last night's finale. At ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II, Friday, February 14, in San Francisco, CA, Hile Troy, former CWO World champion during the previous incarnation, will have his first major shot at the title, going up against Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo or whomever holds the title at that point. Also, the CWO Championship Committee was quick to book a match for the North American title, and in the "Monsters of the Ring" match, you will see "Monster" Rich Clark, all 7'4", 446 pounds of him, take on the North American champion, 6'6", 355 pounds of Sandman. These two matches alone should make it a night to remember, and there will be more great matches announced soon. However, the CWO has just begun its New England tour, let's look at tonight's card in Portland, ME, at the Portland Civic Center! _______________________________________________________________________________ WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 29, 1997 PORTLAND, ME Cake Boy vs. David "The Destroyer" Davis Black Death vs. Bloodbath Baby Waldo vs. "Lonestar" Mike Willams TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Brian "The Hammer" Williams vs. Brent "The Ace" Alles Highlanders vs. The Eternal Wanderers Panther vs. "Master of Darkness" Damion LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Dark Raven vs. Moses (champion) NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): The Bad Guys (champions) vs. The Big Cool WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title, cage-escape): Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo vs. "Monster" Rich Clark _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: All eyes will be on that Schillo-Clark match, even though it is a non-title affair. If Clark were to beat Schillo in a match that many consider to be Schillo's specialty, then the Championship Committee would again be taking a much closer look at this newly aggressive attitude from Rich Clark. We now take you to comments from CWO superstars, starting with the former North American tag team champions, the Eternal Wanderers. They may have lost the belts to the Bad Guys, but they are still ready to take it to any team in the league. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Far away at the Mountain of Khitomer.) EXILE (looking at the fire as Exodus walks into the cave): Bad Guys... (a devoid look in his eyes) we have been cheated of our belts. (Voice raises as he reaches out into the fire) But then again... sanity was gained for a price. (starts to laugh hysterically as his hands burn) Bad Guys... the monster is unleashed! (As his screams and laughter start to grow, Exodus runs out of the cave. Noticing what is happening, he runs over, pulling Exile out of the fire, and throwing snow on him. Exodus' eyes are full of pity.) EXODUS: You Fool! You were cured... you were cured! EXILE (a mad look on his face): Highlanders, Mercernaries and Pit Bosses.. (laughs even louder) do do do do do do... OM.. DOOM is approaching, Doom is approaching! (Exile laughs louder and Exodus has no choice but to clobber him and use the nerve pinch to calm him down. A figure appears from the shadows.) FIGURE: Our Lord does ask for a price. (Exodus stares at the shadowed form in front of him, understanding and acceptance filling his eyes.) EXODUS: Storm... (sighs) I know, I know. Why are you here? GURU CRUSH STORM: Our Lord has a message. Train, and train hard. For your next Challange is upon you. Not just here with your next three matches against the Highlanders, Mercenaries and Pit Bosses but your upcoming Matches in the WHCW and TWD. EXODUS (staring at the camera): Highlanders... for all it is worth, I pity you. Mercenaries... pray tell that the president is paying you enough... for it may be enough for the hospital bills, but... (a look of hatred in his eyes) Pit Bosses... we will face you again, and this time... be prepared to Face what your foolishness unleased! (Exodus looks over at his partner and smiles.) EXODUS: For if you thought it was hard the first time, prepare for a second and harder coming! Bad Guys... (a serious expression on his face) next time... you will face a pain greater than any other. Pity yourself... and lose those titles before we catch up with you... because it won't be pretty! (Picking up Exile, he walks into a portal of light and departs with the man known as GURU Crush Storm.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We now have a response from the Pit Bosses. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Pit Bosses are waving their fingers in front of the camera.) PIT BOSSES: OOGA BOOGA, ABRACADABRA! (They laugh humorously.) PIT BOSS #1: You see, Eternal Wanderers, we could really give a care less about all that David Copperfield stuff you guys pull, we've seen the magicians here in Vegas, and they don't impress us either. PIT BOSS #2: Wake up, Wanderers, and live in the real world. The real world where one hard fact awaits you: you have attracted our attention. We'll admit it, we're stand-up guys, and we didn't think much of you until after TRIPLE THREAT. PIT BOSS #1: And now what we do think of you isn't pretty, boys. And at ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II, the name is appropriate, because we are going to kick your sorry Doug Hennig-loving butts out of here. Your marker's up, gentlemen, and it's time to cash out. PIT BOSS #2: TAKE IT TO THE BANK! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: As you saw already, CWO World Champion Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo's best friend, Brian "The Hammer" Williams, now has a title belt around his waist as well, the Television Championship. We take you now to comments from these two champions. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Brian "The Hammer" Williams and Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo are standing in the interview area together. Wayne has his belt over his shoulder, and Brian is trying to put his around his waist.) BRIAN "THE HAMMER" WILLIAMS: Wow, I never thought I would have a title so fast! This is great! WAYNE "KEUKA GUY" SCHILLO: But now you're a champion, a role model. Use your title reign as an opportunity to beat up people all the time! WILLIAMS: Broken bones, scars, asbestos poisoning, Norplant injuries, if you are involved in an accident, you need to call the tough smart... SCHILLO: Brian, we're wrestlers, not injury lawyers! WILLIAMS: Um... sorry about that. Anyway, how do we wear these belts? They don't fit through beltloops! SCHILLO: Put it over your shoulder like this. Chicks really dig it. Wear it when you go out this weekend! It'll do wonders for your love life! WILLIAMS: But what about Amanda? SCHILLO: One word for you, mister TV Title Holder Man: THREESOME. WILLIAMS: Wayne, you truly are a wise and wonderful world champion. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Wayne and Brian obviously enjoying their respective title reigns, but they are under fire now respectively from Hile Troy and Brent "The Ace" Alles. Now, the man who lost that Television title last night, Maximum Overdrive, also had some comments for us, we now go to Doomsday. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens in DOOMSDAY's locker room following CWO Fever. It's not a happy area.) MAXIMUM OVERDRIVE: You know, it makes me laugh sometimes at how fate can play a role in things. I destroyed any and all competition that was placed in front of me. I beat Sanchez, I beat Sin, I beat Joe Eagle last week, Bane was on my list, and out of nowhere comes this buddy of Keuka's to ambush me and take my title. (Baby Waldo and the Mercenaries are silent.) OVERDRIVE: I thought we had an agreement between our two teams. I thought we had things understood. Well, you're not going to see me pout and cry and complain how things turned out. I'm going to pick myself up, dust myself off, and take back what is rightfully mine. (The Mercenaries start to nod.) OVERDRIVE (to the group): You've seen what can be accomplished. I was the first member of this group to hold gold in the CWO, and I won't be the last. All of you made fine showings at the PPV. I know what you're capable of. Now it's time to let the rest of this league know what we're capable of. And you know what must be done. (Waldo and the Mercenaries leave the locker room.) OVERDRIVE (turning to camera): Sin, I saw your comments prior to last night's card. Face it, idiot. I beat your a$$ 1... 2... 3, in the middle of the ring. You're nothing but a worthless piece of garbage that has to rely on cheap magic tricks to get a point across. If you want another butt-kicking, sign the line and I'll be more than happy to oblige. And that goes for anybody that I have to go through to get my TV title back in the hands of the only person that was worthy of defending it... and that man is me. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: All right, before we sign off for today, we do happen to have a special guest with us, and it is former North American champion Gold Kard. (Gold Kard is shown as German Choklat massages his neck.) GOLD KARD: Thank you, dumbkopf. That's enough, German Choklat. And now, I merely want to take up a short moment of you stupid Amerikan dumbkopf's time with a request to a man I've been watching very closely here in the CWO: Miguel Sanchez. The 1996 Rookie of the Year. But, Herr Sanchez, you now have to avoid something that is known as the Sophomore Slump. Only I can bring you through this, for am I not the greatest wrestler this federation has ever known? STARR: Well... GOLD KARD: SHUT UP! Anyway, Herr Sanchez, Gold Kard International is currently, how should I put this, depleted, what with the departure of Herr Hart. It is just myself and the Pit Bosses. Of course, us three alone provides the symbol of excellence to this pathetic league, but we are always looking into areas of expansion, in the corporate world and in the business world. Herr Sanchez, I know you are looking for a friend right now, what with that idiot Clark betraying you. Well, perhaps I cannot be your friend, Sanchez, but who better than me to be your mentor? So, Sanchez, you have heard my offer, do not let me down. STARR: Thank you, Gold Kard. GOLD KARD: Shut up, dumbkopf. If I would have not been cheated in that match with that dumbkopf Troy, you would have been fired first. (Gold Kard leaves as Johnny glares at him.) STARR: Well, that's all the time we have for today, we'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT!
