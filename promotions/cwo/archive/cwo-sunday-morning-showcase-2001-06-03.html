CWO Sunday Morning Showcase 6/3/01

CWO SUNDAY MORNING SHOWCASE 6/3/01 (taped 5/30/01) http://www.angelfire.com/wrestling2/cwo2k [detail withheld] SEGMENT ONE CWO LOGO OPENING SHOW OPENING We join our hosts Johnny Starr and T. J. Marks in the studio as they do a recap of MAY-HEM 2K1. (They also serve as the commentators during the match.)
COMMERCIALS SEGMENT TWO BACkYArD vs. BRIAN “THE PIPER” MCTAVISH Tony the K is in the ring along with referee Jeremy Lang. TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. Introducing first, coming down the aisle...
'Dragula' by Rob Zombie plays as the fans cheer. TONY THE K: Weighing 225 pounds, from Trenton, NJ, BacKyaRd! BacKyaRd comes down to the ring and gets in. TONY THE K: And his opponent... Bagpipe music plays as the fans boo. TONY THE K: Weighing 225 pounds, from Glasgow, Scotland, Brian McTavish! McTavish comes down to the ring and gets in. The bell rings. McTavish hits with a punch, then a kick, then another kick. He rakes BacKyaRd in the eyes. He hits him with a punch, but BacKyaRd comes back with one of his own! BacKyaRd goes for a punch but it’s blocked. McTavish goes for a punch but it’s blocked. BacKyaRd finally connects with a punch.
BacKyaRd climbs to the top turnbuckle. BacKyaRd hits with a flying bodypress! McTavish rolls outside the ring. BacKyaRd nails McTavish with a flying axhandle off the ring apron! McTavish is rolled back in the ring by
BacKyaRd. BacKyaRd goes for the pin! 1... 2… kick out! BacKyaRd goes for an elbowdrop but McTavish rolls out of the way! McTavish hits with a punch.
BacKyaRd hits with a punch. BacKyaRd whips McTavish into the corner. McTavish bounces back from the corner... BacKyaRd hits with a “Stun Gun”
(neck drop clothesline on the top rope)!
BacKyaRd climbs to the top turnbuckle. BacKyaRd hits with a flying axhandle! BacKyaRd tries to whip McTavish into the corner but it's reversed. McTavish plays to the crowd, getting booed. McTavish chokes
BacKyaRd with his boot. The referee makes his count... 1 ... 2 ... 3 ...
4 ... 5 ... the referee warns McTavish. McTavish lets go. McTavish hits with an eyerake.
McTavish hits with a kick. BacKyaRd hits with a kick. McTavish nails
BacKyaRd with a punch, then a chop. BacKyaRd hits with a punch, then another one. McTavish hits with a punch. BacKyaRd hits with a punch.
McTavish hits with a punch. BacKyaRd hits with a punch. McTavish hits with a punch. BacKyaRd hits with a punch, then another one. McTavish hits with a punch, then another one. McTavish hits with a bodyslam! McTavish hits with a splash! McTavish plays to the crowd.
McTavish hits with a thumb to the eye. McTavish hits with a chokehold. The referee makes his count... 1 ... 2 ... 3 ... 4 ... 5 ... the referee warns McTavish. McTavish lets go. McTavish applies a headlock. McTavish releases the hold. McTavish hits with a bodyslam! BacKyaRd rolls outside the ring.
McTavish goes outside the ring. McTavish and BacKyaRd brawl outside the ring. They brawl up the runway. They brawl back towards the ring.
McTavish hits with a kick. McTavish tries to hit BacKyaRd with a chair but
BacKyaRd avoids it, grabs the chair and nails McTavish with it! BacKyaRd tosses McTavish back in the ring.
BacKyaRd climbs to the top turnbuckle and hits with a flying fistdrop! BacKyaRd climbs to the top turnbuckle again… he goes for the Flying Butt
Splash... he hits! BacKyaRd goes for the pin! 1... 2... 3! McTavish is pinned!
MATCH STATS BacKyaRd pinned Brian “The Piper” McTavish after the Flying Butt Splash in 6:20. COMMERCIALS SEGMENT THREE Back to the studio for a recap of EXTREME 5/30/01. COMMERCIALS SEGMENT FOUR WOMEN’S DIVISION GERMAN CHOKLAT vs. BIG MAMA WOO Tony the K is in the ring along with referee Chris Connor. TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. Introducing first, coming down the aisle...
“Ride of the Valkyries” plays as the fans cheer a little. TONY THE K: Weighing 134 pounds, from Berlin, Germany, German Choklat! Choklat comes down to the ring and gets in. TONY THE K: And her opponent... “Lady Marmalade” by Li’l Kim, Mya, Pink, and Christina Aguilera plays as the fans boo. TONY THE K: Weighing 276 pounds, from Wyoming, MI, Big Mama Woo! Woo comes down to the ring and gets in, playing to the crowd. The bell rings. Woo argues with the referee. Choklat hits with an eye rake.
Choklat hits with a DDT! Choklat stomps away on Woo. Choklat goes for the pin! 1... Woo kicks out! Choklat stomps away on Woo.
Choklat climbs to the top turnbuckle and goes for a flying splash but Woo rolls out of the way! Woo throws Choklat out of the ring. Woo goes outside the ring. Woo and Choklat brawl outside the ring. Woo avalanches Choklat against the ringpost outside! Woo rolls Choklat back in the ring. Woo whips Choklat into the corner. Choklat bounces back... Woo hits with a clothesline!
Woo goes for the pin! 1... 2… Choklat gets her shoulder up! Woo applies a camel clutch. Woo releases the hold, then brings her weight down on
Choklat! Woo goes for the pin! 1 ... 2 ... thr... no! Choklat gets her shoulder up! Woo hits with a koppo (rolling) kick.
Woo hits with a bodyslam! Woo climbs to the top turnbuckle. Woo goes for the Big Mama Splash (flying splash from the top turnbuckle)... she hits!
Woo goes for the pin! 1... 2... 3! Choklat is pinned! MATCH STATS Big Mama Woo pinned German Choklat after the Big Mama Splash (flying splash from the top turnbuckle) in 3:17. COMMERCIALS SEGMENT FIVE Back to the studio for a preview of the upcoming EXTREME (6/6/01), notification of ticket on-sale dates, etc.
COMMERCIALS SEGMENT SIX CWO WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP SJPW SUPERSTARS (Fujinami and Torunaka) vs. RENEGADES OF PHUNK (Charlie Phreak and Matty Rude, champions) Tony the K is in the ring along with referee John Myering. TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following tag team contest is scheduled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit. It is for the CWO World
Tag Team Championship! Introducing first, coming down the aisle...
Japanese synth music plays as the fans give a mixed reaction. TONY THE K: At a combined weight of 445 pounds, from the Superstar Japan
Wrestling Federation, Torunaka and Fujinami!
Torunaka and Fujinami come down to the ring and get in, playing to the fans. TONY THE K: And their opponents... 'Renegades of Funk' by Rage Against the Machine plays as the fans cheer. TONY THE K: At a combined weight of 440 pounds, the CWO World Tag Team
Champions, Charlie Phreak and Matty Rude, the Renegades of Phunk!
Fujinami starts against Phreak. The bell rings. They lock up. Fujinami applies an abdominal stretch. Phreak counters the hold with a hiptoss!
Fujinami runs into the ropes... Phreak hits with a clothesline! Phreak climbs to the top turnbuckle… Phreak hits with a flying spinning clothesline!
Phreak climbs to the top turnbuckle… Phreak misses with a flying move as
Fujinami kicks him in the stomach!
Fujinami goes for the Dragon Sleeper… he hits! Rude makes the save. Rude is made to leave the ring. Fujinami tags to Torunaka. Torunaka applies a standing wristlock. Phreak reaches the ropes. Torunaka goes for a backslide but Phreak gets out of it before the 1 count. Phreak runs into the ropes... Torunaka hits with a jumping elbow thrust!
Torunaka whips Phreak into the ropes… Phreak comes back with a leg lariat! Phreak tags to Rude. Phreak and Rude nail Torunaka with a double dropkick! Rude climbs to the top turnbuckle… going for a flying legdrop but Torunaka rolls out of the way! Torunaka climbs to the top turnbuckle… and hits with a flying somersault headbutt! Rude rolls outside the ring. Torunaka nails
Rude with a flying dropkick off the ring apron!
Wild brawl! Both teams continue brawling outside the ring. Torunaka rolls back in the ring, followed by Rude. Torunaka whips Rude into the ropes…
Rude comes back with a flying forearm! Rude whips Torunaka into the corner…
Rude charges in with an avalanche! Rude whips Torunaka into the ropes...
Torunaka hits with a leg lariat! Torunaka tags to Fujinami. Fujinami goes for a rana but Rude counters with a power bomb! Rude whips Fujinami into the ropes... Rude hits with a rana!
Rude tags to Phreak. Torunaka comes in but Rude and Phreak nail him with a double dropkick! Torunaka rolls out of the ring.
Phreak climbs to the top turnbuckle. Phreak hits Fujinami with the Phreak
Out (flying spinning leg lariat)! Phreak goes for the pin! 1... 2... 3!
Fujinami is pinned! MATCH STATS CWO World Tag Team Champions the Renegades of Phunk defeated SJPW Superstars (Torunaka and Fujinami) to retain the titles when Phreak pinned Fujinami after the Phreak Out (flying spinning leg lariat) in 4:28.
