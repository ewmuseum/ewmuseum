CWO REPORT 5/11/97

11 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Show opens with Brian "The Hammer" Williams, CWO North American Champion Jason "Flash" Varga, and Wayne "College Guy" Schillo, all playing Sega.) BRIAN "THE HAMMER" WILLIAMS: Did you see that crap? The friggin' Wild Bunch sayin' all this crap about "blah, blah, titles, blah...." What gives? JASON "FLASH" VARGA: Man, if I see one more flash about titles from those guys... wait, flash? I'M FLASH! I AM THE MAN!! WAYNE "COLLEGE GUY" SCHILLO: Anyway, the 6-man titles might not be recognized as one of the 'upper echelon' of titles, but they're all we've got right now, and, more importantly, we three won them TOGETHER! WILLIAMS: I'M 30-1, and I KICK ASS! If the Wild Bunch thinks I'm gonna roll over and let them just TAKE the titles, they're WRONG! DEAD WRONG! Hey, Varga, where'd you put Tecmo Bowl? VARGA: Leave me alone, let's keep playing 'In Your House!' SCHILLO: Um... guys... what about the rest of our flash? VARGA: FLASH?!? THAT'S ME! WILLIAMS: My turn! _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control center.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Sunday, May 11, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the CWO finished up our stay in the Southwest with a stop at Salt Lake City, UT, let's look at what happened there! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 5/10/97 Salt Lake City, UT Mercenaries defeated Ghost Riders when Korperal Punishment pinned Blaze after the Mercenaries' Flying Clothesline. Time of match: 17:17. Baby Waldo made Centurion submit to the Cradle Rocker (Waldo pulls on opponent's head, trying to separate it from the shoulders). Time of match: 7:10. Serpentmaster pinned Diamondback Chris Myers with the reversed small package. Time of match: 12:08. Vegas High Roller pinned Bloodbath after the back body drop, pulling the tights. Time of match: 17:22. Gold Kard pinned CWO Light Heavyweight Champion Moses after Die Flieder Kross (flying somersault splash). Time of match: 7:29. Goliath and CWO Television Champion Johnny Reb wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. WORLD 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Champions the College Guys (Jason 'Flash' Varga, Wayne 'College Guy' Schillo, Brian 'The Hammer' Williams) defeated Wild Bunch (Mondo Gecko and the Regulators) when Wayne 'College Guy' Schillo pinned Mondo Gecko after the kneedrop. Time of match: 22:15. Inferno defeated CWO World Champion 'Monster' Rich Clark by disqualification when Clark's masked friend attacked Inferno. Time of match: 9:57. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Don't forget about the great SUNDAY SHOWCASE, coming out later today, let's take a look at the line-up! _______________________________________________________________________________ SHOWCASE 5/11/97 WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Tiger Rogers and Goliath vs. Highlanders (champions) Fergus McTavish, the Whirling Scottish Devil vs. Brent "The Ace" Alles The Focus vs. Benjamin Blake Bentley LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Sandman Jr. vs. Moses (champion) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We now turn to comments from CWO superstars, starting with Diamondback Chris Myers. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Diamondback Chris Myers is shown doing roadwork somewhere in Northern Pennsylvania. Dallas is off to the side, holding a stopwatch and watching her man run.) DALLAS: Ok, Chris, now, once more around the way, and you're done! (Myers show mock horror, but smiles again and runs off camera.) DALLAS: So you want to know about Chris? CAMERAMAN: You're the one who invited us up here, Dallas. You tell us what you want the CWO to hear. DALLAS: OK. Well, Chris is happy to be here, and he's estatic over winning his first match, that triangle one. Now, he's training for his match against Serpentmaster somewhere down the line. CAMERAMAN: What about his match this week, against "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deustcher? DALLAS: Chris is focused on that match, too. Maybe I misspoke earlier. I should have said Chris is training for his match against "Lone Wolf", but he's also training for the contest against the Serpentmaster. We're looking at this match as a training for the big one. CAMERAMAN: Any concerns about interference from the Serpentmaster's partner, Vlad the Impailer? DALLAS: None whatsoever! I may not look like much, but I can hold my own, and if Vlad comes to the ring and tries to interfere with Myers, I'll stop him. Plain and simple. CAMERAMAN: Do you plan to be here in CWO for a while? DALLAS: Of course! We don't plan to be some rookies who blow in here, make a lot of noise, then just walk away. Chris has his eye on gold, and I plan to help lead him to it. CAMERMAN: One more question, and we're done. DALLAS: You don't HAVE to leave if you don't want to.... CAMERAMAN: Really? Uh... thanks. You mentioned in a previous interview that Chris Myers has some stablemates in another fed. Any chance of them joining the CWO? DALLAS: You mean the Wild Cards? Oh, no! There's already the Wild Bunch and the Wild Ones and the Wild Side in this fed, who needs one more? Honestly, though, Ace and Deck are busy in another federation, so I can't say for sure right now. Someday, but not anytime soon. Chris is planning to stand on his own two feet. CAMERAMAN: Ok, thanks. DALLAS: No problem, sweetie. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: On this week's special THURSDAY NIGHT FEVER, Maximum Overdrive and Doomsday perpetrated a vicious attack on Brent "The Ace" Alles, and most people believe that it was indeed a "hit" put on Alles by Wayne "College Guy" Schillo and Brian "The Hammer" Williams. Now, however, Alles is looking ahead to a no holds barred stretcher match at SUMMER HEAT with Maximum Overdrive, let's hear from him now. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on Maximum Overdrive, who is winding up the last Brent "The Ace" Alles interview.) MAXIMUM OVERDRIVE: What a bunch of pathetic whining. Alles, you don't even know which side of your mouth to talk out of. "Oh, I do things by myself. I don't need anybody else to get the job done." That is a complete and total load of BS. I guess that you didn't need your bimbo to spray mace into the eyes of Brian Williams all those times, huh? You talk about straight ahead wrestling? How many times have I sat through a CWO Report and seen your interferance costing somebody a match? (Overdrive flips off the television.) OVERDRIVE: You want to talk desperation? I not only used the one move that's put everybody out of action on you, but I had you carted off on a stretcher. Now I guess that that's it. I'm finished with YOU! But you always have to have the last word. That's fine with me. You want a repeat of FEVER? You want a repeat of the last stretcher match? Great. Instead of injuring you, I'm going to put you out of action for good. You want to threaten my life? I'll TAKE yours. (Overdrive jumps up, picks up the television and throws it to the floor.) OVERDRIVE: NOBODY THREATENS MY LIFE! THAT'S THE MISTAKE YOU MADE, YOU GUTLESS PUNK! I DON'T NEED ANYBODY ELSE TO HELP ME DO IT! NOBODY HELPED ME WIN THAT STRETCHER MATCH! NOBODY HELPED ME PUT THE ALLESBREAKER ON YOU! AND I WILL WIN AT THE PPV BY MYSELF AND TEACH YOU THAT YOU DON'T [censored] WITH MAXIMUM OVERDRIVE! (Overdrive starts to pace the room, trying to calm down.) OVERDRIVE: You're always out here, talking about mind games. To hell with your "mind games". You want to play? Then play in reality. Play my Pain Game. Having your neck almost broken on FEVER wasn't a hallucination. Getting taken out on a stretcher wasn't a hallucination, it was complete and total reality. (Overdrive walks right up to the camera.) OVERDRIVE: I guess that you're right on one count. You do reap what you sow. At the PPV, when you're carted back to the ambulance, and when the doctor pronounces you unfit to compete in this sport ever again, just remember that you brought it all on yourself. "Reap the whirlwind"? Boy, I AM the whirlwind. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: He betrayed his best friend and long-time partner at MAY-HEM, and now the man formerly known as Pit Boss #1 seeks to make an impact in singles competition, as the Vegas High Roller. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Shots of Las Vegas are shown, as the Brian Seitzer Orchestra's "When Lady Luck Walks Into the Room" plays in the background. The Vegas High Roller is shown walking into the casino, his hair slicked back, wearing dark glasses, and a white shirt with a gaudy bright white leisure suit jacket over it. The white shirt is unbuttoned, revealing his thick chest hair with a gold medallion hanging down into it. He walks with a cocky swagger, pointing his finger at various people and saying, "Hey!" and "Who loves ya, baby?" and other such cool sayings. He gets to a roulette table as a Vegas moll sidles up to him.) VEGAS HIGH ROLLER: Hey, baby, major market talent here talkin' to ya, the Vegas High Roller. I used to be hangin' with that punk of a partner, but now I've seen the light, the golden light if you will. I was sick of bein' a loser, but now I did my part, I did my part. And the future's nothin' but bright for the Vegas High Roller. ROULETTE SPINNER: Sir, your bet? VEGAS HIGH ROLLER: $1000 on #14, my lucky number. (He smiles a pearly white smile at the Vegas moll. The roulette spinner drops the ball....) ROULETTE SPINNER: 14! You win $35,000, sir! VEGAS HIGH ROLLER: You see, it's just that easy, baby, and it's just that easy to beat anybody and everybody in the CWO. I'm goin' all the way to the top, and now that I'm not part of that loser tag team anymore, I never crap out. (Vegas High Roller leers at the Vegas moll.) VEGAS HIGH ROLLER: C'mon, baby, let's see what $35,000 can do in this town! VEGAS MOLL: Oh, Vegas High Roller, you're the man! VEGAS HIGH ROLLER: Don't I know it! (They stroll off as the music swells again as we fade out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today, we'll see you next time on the REPORT!
