CWO REPORT 4/24/97

24 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Show opens up with Mondo Gecko in the interview area.) MONDO GECKO: Gold Kard, I finally get to take on the CWO's neo-Nazi and end your miserable career once and for all. I am tired of having to watch as people like you, Brent "Ace" Alles, and the rest of GKI spread lies and decit as normal people suffer for it. Well, where I come from, we let our actions do the talking and Gold Kard, when I get my hands on you, there is going to be nothing left except a worthless piece of trash lying helplessly in the ring. I am tired of the CWO doing nothing to punish the rulebreakers there, that is why I am going to destroy you and be at ringside for the Reb-Maccabee match. GKI, THE WILD BUNCH DECLARES WAR ON YOU GUYS. GET READY FOR MONDO EXTREME ACTION, DIG IT, *****! _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Thursday, April 24, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the CWO was in St. Louis, MO, in the Kiel Auditorium, let's see what went down. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 4/23/97 St. Louis, MO (Kiel Auditorium) FINISHER VS. FINISHER: Joe Eagle pinned Benjamin Blake Bentley after the Eagle Shock (tiger driver). Time of match: 26:28. Brent 'The Ace' Alles pinned 'Lonestar' Mike Williams after the Allesbreaker. Time of match: 35:16. After the match, Alles delivered the Allesbreaker to Williams on a steel chair. Williams suffered an injured neck and will be out of wrestling for an indeterminate time. Mondo Gecko pinned Gold Kard after the cradle. Time of match: 9:45. Brian 'The Hammer' Williams defeated 'Monster' Rich Clark by disqualification when Brent 'The Ace' Alles attacked. Time of match: 13:54. Kurt 'Thrill from the Chill' Gerstung pinned Light Heavyweight champion Tiger Rogers in a non-title match after the Icepick (DDT). Time of match: 11:40. Vlad the Impaler/Serpentmaster defeated Creatures of the Night when Vlad the Impaler pinned Night Crawler after the Long Kiss Goodnight (bite to the neck). Time of match: 12:07. CWO TELEVISION CHAMPION TITLE MATCH: Inferno pinned Arlon Flaym after the leg drop. Time of match: 25:09. Inferno defended the CWO Television Champion title. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: As you can see from last night's house show, Brent "The Ace" Alles is totally out of control, and he has been heavily fined for his actions against "Lonestar" Mike Williams. We got some comments from "The Ace." _______________________________________________________________________________ (Brent "The Ace" Alles is in the interview area, his chin rests on his fist, as Amanda Bloom clutches around him.) BRENT "THE ACE" ALLES: Well, Mondo Gecko, Maximum Overdrive, Wayne Schillo, and oh yes, my good friend Brian Williams, did you see what happened to your young friend last night? You know, call me crazy, but when I heard his neck snap like a chicken's, all I could picture in my mind's eye were all of your faces. Gentlemen, MAY-HEM approaches, the maelstrom beckons, and there is no hope for any of you. This is my sincere promise. I will go through 59 of the other losers in this organization and I will go on to that World title shot. Nothing's going to stop me. Nothing. There is no depth I will not sink to get what I want. Of course, I guess there are some levels I won't sink to, right, Wayne? CO1 - EOT. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Of course, if you were watching the REPORT yesterday, Brent "The Ace" Alles produced that audio tape which apparently is a recording of an unwanted advance by Wayne Schillo on Amanda Bloom, who was his best friend Brian "The Hammer" Williams' girlfriend at one time. However, we cannot confirm that it was indeed Schillo on the tape, that part was largely inaudible. Our audio technicians are working around the clock to try and amplify it so we can get some answers. Until then, Wayne Schillo has announced that he will be at next week's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER for an interview, maybe he will answer some of the charges then. All right, enough of that, let's talk about tonight's great house show in Kansas City, MO, let's look at the lineup! _______________________________________________________________________________ THURSDAY, APRIL 24, 1997 KANSAS CITY, MO Dark Raven vs. Joe Eagle El Grande Pistolerro vs. Chris Greco The Focus vs. Arlon Flaym Moses vs. Sloth Johnny Reb vs. Masher Maccabee The Masters of Hardcore vs. Mason-Dixon NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): The Bad Guys (champions) vs. Wild Side TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title, steel cage): Wayne 'College Guy' Schillo vs. Inferno (champion) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We now turn to comments from CWO superstars, starting off with El Grande Pistolerro, who has a match against Chris Greco in Kansas City tonight. _______________________________________________________________________________ (El Grande Pistolerro is in the interview area.) EL GRANDE PISTOLERRO: Chris Greco, you are a tough opponent and I respect you tremendously. However, I have come here to become the Light Heavyweight champion and I plan on doing that no matter who I have to go over to get it. I am ready to do what ever it takes and do any extreme move to win the match. The Wild Bunch is involved in a War with GKI and Masher/ Thomas, you come anywhere at ringside during Gecko's matches, I will come down and hurt you so badly that your dog will bleed. The Wild Bunch has big plans and cleaning up the CWO is one of them. GRECO, GET READY FOR THE FIGHT OF YOUR LIFE. VIVA LA WILD BUNCH Y EL CWO! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: One of the more unusual newcomers to the CWO is Fergus McTavish, the Whirling Scottish Devil. We are going to get our first look at him in action next week on FEVER, but for now, let's go to a taped segment with this wild Scotsman. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on a large, red-headed Scotsmen engaged in a heated debate in a diner. Its Fergus McTavish, The Whirling Scottish Devil. His voice is mired with a thick Scottish accent.) FERGUS MCTAVISH, THE WHIRLING SCOTTISH DEVIL: Haggis! RALPH THE WAITER: Sorry. FERGUS: No haggis?!? RALPH: No. Now do you want a hamburger? FERGUS: I said, "HAGGIS!" (And with that, he smashes a plate over Ralph The Waiter's head, busting him wide open. Ralph The Waiter's eyes roll back in his head and he sprawls out on the floor.) FERGUS (wiping blood from his hand): Och, ya bugger, I said, "HAGGIS!" (Fergus looks around the diner at the horrified patrons.) FERGUS: 'Ere, turn yer heads and keep stuffin' yer pie holes! (The patrons do as ordered.) FERGUS (to the camera): Oooh, now I gots ta wait ta order breakfast. I still wants haggis! Ya know, I mades my debut last week inna "Dark Match." Dunno why it was called "Dark." I could see the whole time. But no matter. (He picks up the menu.) FERGUS: Aye, this 'ere menu reminds me of da CWO. Lots and lots of nothin'. Sure, dere's nice names an all, likes "Vlad the Impaler" an' "Masher Maccabee" an' "Moses" an' even "Maximum Overdrive." But you knows, its all flash with nay substance. Oh where oh where does CWO find dese guys? An' I still wants haggis! (Fergus looks around for another waiter, not noticing that the entire staff is hiding in the kitchen) FERGUS: I dunno where CWO finds dese guys. But I do know one thing. I gots me a match next week wif "El Grande Pistolerro." HAR! So ya thinks ya gots a big gun, do ya? You ain't see the guns of The Whirling Scottish Devil, me boy! When I'm done wif ya, dey'll be calling ya a Cap-Pistolerro! Ya gots dat right. (Fergus looks around the diner once more before getting up.) FERGUS: Well, looks like I'll be getting me haggis elsewheres. Oh, and one last thing.... (Fergus jumps up on the table, turns his back to the camera and lifts up his kilt, mooning the cameraman.) FERGUS (over his shoulder): Gets ready for The Whirling Scottish Devil! IT'S NOT OATMEAL! MADGE THE MANAGER (running out from the kitchen): Hey, you nut! You can't do that! Put your pants back on! (Camera fades, much laughing and hooting can still be heard.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny, with his eyebrows raised.) STARR: Um... yeah. Anyway, we'll be back tomorrow with another REPORT, until then, so long for now!
