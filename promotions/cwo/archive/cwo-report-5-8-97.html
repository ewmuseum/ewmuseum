CWO REPORT 5/8/97

8 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Show opens up with Mondo Gecko and Tamara Wilcox in the interview area.) MONDO GECKO: Maximum Overdrive, you are proably the most toughest man to figure out in the CWO. You wrestle as a rulebreaker, yet you and I share a similar distaste in that loser, Brent "Ace" Alles. Oh well, no matter. After I am done flying all over you and beating you, me an' Pistolerro are going out that night to town and party 'til dawn. Hey, Pistolerro is one party animal and we are ready to have some great times. You see, Maximum Overdrive, I am preparing myself for the right time when I will capture championship gold but first I got to take care of the geeks from Gold Kard International. Maximum Overdrive, you are a big man and I have respect for your toughness but it is GOING TO BE MONDO EXTREME ACTION FOR YOU, DIG IT! TAMARA WILCOX: Mondo has picked it up a notch and now the Wild Bunch is stronger than ever. I would like to take an opportunity to ask the CWO championship committee to let the Wild Ones in the Battle Royal because they are one of the top three teams in the CWO and everybody knows that. Second of all, I want to say that I have trained in the ring for a while and I am going to offer a challenge to "Truth" Thomas or that slut Amanda Bloom. I challenge you, any of you two, to a match on the Eye Of The Storm in a wrestling match. Thomas, I already knocked you on your fat butt and Amanda, after what you have pulled in the CWO, you deserve to be beaten and tied up like a dog. The Wild Bunch is the best around and as my boyfriend Mondo says, it is time FOR MONDO EXTREME ACTION! _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control center.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Thursday, May 8, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the superstars of the CWO were in Mexico City, MX, for a huge show there, let's look at what went down! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 5/7/97 Mexico City, MX El Grande Pistolerro pinned Lizmark Jr. in 10:00 after the Springboard Frankensteiner after a special CWO - AAA exhibition match. Mondo Gecko pinned Maximum Overdrive after the Top Rope Frankensteiner. Time of match: 5:06. Joe Eagle and Inferno wrestled to a double disqualification. Time of match: 5:46. Mercenaries defeated Mason-Dixon when Korperal Punishment pinned Beauregard Dixon after the Mercenaries' Flying Clothesline. Time of match: 7:07. CWO LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION TITLE MATCH: Champion Moses made Dark Raven submit to the octopus hold. Time of match: 13:06. CWO Television Champion Johnny Reb pinned Serpentmaster after the German suplex. Time of match: 15:29. CWO NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS TITLE MATCH: Champions the Bad Guys defeated The Big Cool when Ron 'The Rude Guy' Johnson pinned Jim 'Jazzman' Taylor after the headscissors roll. Time of match: 23:57. CWO North American Champion Jason 'Flash' Varga defeated Goliath by disqualification when the Alliance attacked. Time of match: 22:16. CWO World Tag Team Champions the Highlanders defeated Coldfront when Conor McCloud pinned 'Mr. Niceguy' Steve White after the Quickening. Time of match: 17:05. CWO WORLD CHAMPION TITLE MATCH: 'Monster' Rich Clark pinned Wayne 'College Guy' Schillo after the small package, using the ropes for leverage. Time of match: 17:33. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Tonight we are in Santa Fe, NM, for a one hour special THURSDAY NIGHT FEVER show, then followed by some more great action, let's look at the lineup for tonight's show in New Mexico! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW THURSDAY, MAY 8, 1997 SANTA FE, NM THURSDAY NIGHT FEVER: DARK MATCH: Centurion vs. Steel NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (street fight): Wild Side vs. Bad Guys (champions) TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP: Masher Maccabee vs. Johnny Reb (champion) TAG TEAM BATTLE ROYAL (winner gets title shot at SUMMER HEAT): Masters of Hardcore The Big Cool Ghost Riders Regulators Mercenaries New Men (Akillies and Spartacus) Vlad the Impaler and Serpentmaster AND ONE "WILD CARD" TEAM FROM THE FOLLOWING TEAMS: Deadly Sins Brother Justice Mason-Dixon Coldfront Wild Ones (Mondo Gecko and El Grande Pistolerro) Attitude Dance (Joe Eagle and Jason "Flash" Varga) David "The Destroyer" Davis and Inferno Wayne Schillo and Brian "The Hammer" Williams Maximum Overdrive and Baby Waldo NON-TELEVISED MATCHES: Brother Justice vs. Attitude Dance Sandman Jr. vs. Tiger Rogers El Grande Pistolerro vs. Centurion (substituting for Chris Greco) New Men vs. Maximum Overdrive and Baby Waldo Mondo Gecko vs. Gold Kard Brian "The Hammer" Williams vs. Goliath I QUIT MATCH: Benjamin Blake Bentley vs. The Focus STRAP MATCH: Matthew "The Man" White vs. Brent "The Ace" Alles NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Bad Guys (champions) vs. Regulators _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We now turn to comments from CWO superstars, starting with a man who has a lot to be happy about these days, Joe Eagle. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Joe Eagle and the Tower of Power are celebrating Eagle's win at May-hem. The party has gone into the wee hours of the night, and everyone is quite tipsy at this point.) JOE EAGLE: I told ya... I told EVERYONE that I'd be there! Soon, boys, soon... JIM "JAZZMAN" TAYLOR: What's soon, Joe? EAGLE: Well, you see Jimmy, I've got a plan for us. JUMBO JENKINS: Does it involve mass quantities of cheese, or a cheese product? EAGLE: NO! Dammit, stop thinking about your stomach for a minute, and listen! You see, the "Comeback kid" is back, and I'm ready to roll! I just wish that jerk "Sandman" was here to see it. 6 Sandblasts is nothing compared to the hell I've been through in the last 8 months. I've gone from being at the pinnacle of our sport - the World Champion - to being an also-ran, on the undercard at most events. There's NOBODY in the CWO who could take what I have and still be back. Not Schillo, not Rich Clark... nobody! Sure, I thought about leaving. Wouldn't anyone? I had to deal with the fans turning their back on me. But now, I'm back. The story of the year... Joe Eagle wins the title, 1 year after his first reign began! RUSSELL HILL: And don't forget who brought you here, Joe! EAGLE: Well, as much as I hate to admit it, you're not a bad manager after all. But none of that matters now. It's all up to me, and don't think for a minute that I'll give in. Don't matter who's wearin' the belt: even my buddy Brian Williams. Hammer, you saw how focused I am on getting this belt. Anyone gets in my way, you get steamrolled. That's the way it's gonna be, because I am the once and future king of the CWO! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Also finding a reason to celebrate after MAY-HEM were the Highlanders, as they won the World Tag Team Championships from the new broken up Pit Bosses. Let's hear from the Scottish wildmen! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Scene opens in a darkly lit pub; from the rafters hang strips of tartans, and the people are wild and jubilant. In the middle of it all are Conor and Gavin McCloud, wielding their World Tag Team belts.) GAVIN MCCLOUD(slightly slurred: Och, we told ye we'd do it, an' now look where tha' World Titles have come... SCOOOOTLAAAAAND! (Huge cheer from the crowd.) GAVIN: Aye, it was a good scrap we had with tha' Pit Bosses, but good ol'-fashioned Scottish pride and thick-headedness saw through... now we're continuing the tradition of fine Scottish world champs, like wee Steven Hendry and Jocky Wilson - jus' you try an' come and take these belts from us, an' you'll see how a Celt defends his most prized possessions.... CONOR (even more slurred): Aye, and we'd like to raise our glasses and say a big CHEEEEERRRRS to all our American fans who cheer us... even though they speak a bit funny, thanks for keepin' faith wi' us... now we've got tha' belts, we'll nae be letting them go without a fight. Whoever wins tha' wee battle royal, ye better get ready for the premier Tag-Team in tha' world today... US! Cheeeeers! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We now turn, however, to a man who didn't have much to celebrate after MAY-HEM, because he only lasted 6 seconds in the big battle royal. However, it wasn't Bloodbath's fault, as he was viciously attacked by Fergus McTavish, the Whirling Scottish Devil, before the match. However, the Wild Side has another shot at the North American Tag Team Championships tonight on THURSDAY NIGHT FEVER, let's hear from them. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Bloodbath, with his forehead bandaged up, is standing with Wildfire. Wildfire is wearing a black cloak, and is kneeling down on the side of Bloodbath.) BLOODBATH (very dark and angry): You stupid Scottish twit! You have meddled with powers that you can't handle! You have stepped into the dark side and there is no way of escape! You may have beat me, but you didn't destroy me, and that is the one fatal mistake that will cost you your life! You want me in a strap match, well, you don't tell me what to do! I tell you what to do! I want you in a casket match! But I'm not gonna stop at that! After I stuff your @$$ in the casket, I'm gonna drag it, with your rotting carcass, and stuff you into a hole for all eternity! (Just then Bloodbath vanishes into the shadows as a white light shines on Wildfire. He then emerges from the black cloak.) WILDFIRE (intense): Listen up! We got our rematch with the Bag Guys! We're finally gonna get what is rightfully ours! And we have a little surprise just in case the Little Boys want to try anything funny! Let's just say that we have someone to watch our backs! Now there ain't nothing stopping us from completing our quest! (There is thunder and lighting and Bloodbath appears again.) BLOODBATH (yelling): WE WILL BE CHAMPIONS! THE BAG GUYS WILL BE NO MORE! (The camera fades out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That should be a great match tonight on FEVER, it's a street fight, no time limit, no disqualifications, no countouts. However, both Fergus McTavish and Bloodbath's requests have been granted, as they will face each other in a first of its kind Strap Casket match at SUMMER HEAT, the object of course being to drag your opponent around the ring, touching all four corners, and then stuff him into a casket to finish it! Should be intense. Another match signed for SUMMER HEAT is Benjamin Blake Bentley vs. The Focus in an "I Quit" match. Let's hear from the Focus. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Focus is in the interview area.) THE FOCUS: HA! I was wondering when you were going to start running your mouth. Of course I have had my foot shoved down it for the better part of a few months. I accept your challenge, no problem. I am the Focus, and I can do it all in the CWO, period. I will make you scream 'I Quit' just like the little girl that you are. Soon you will know why you shouldn't have crossed the line, Benjamin. You sure as hell better be ready for me. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Finally today, he made his mark in MAY-HEM, and now he's hoping to continue his winning ways here in the CWO, let's hear from newcomer Diamondback Chris Myers. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Diamondback Chris Myers is shown in his gym in Pittsburgh, working over a punching bag. Dallas is reading over the latest batch of CWO notices.) DALLAS: Guess what? DIAMONDBACK CHRIS MYERS: What? (Myers delivers a spin kick to the bag.) DALLAS: You've got your first match coming up! MYERS: Really? Who? (Myers with some lefts and rights into the bag.) DALLAS: Some guy called the Serpentmaster. MYERS: Serpentmaster? (Myers delivers a HARD right and turns to his lovely manager.) MYERS: That's the guy who eliminated me at MAY-HEM, isn't it? DALLAS: Yep, last time I checked. MYERS: Interesting.... (Myers notices the camera pointing at him.) MYERS: You guys have this knack for showing up at the right time, I swear. DALLAS: Another interview? CAMERAMAN: No, Dallas. Just a few comments. MYERS: You caught me at the right time! I just found out who I'm going up against for my first singles match in CWO! Serpentmaster, you drew the target on yourself at MAY-HEM when you threw me out of the ring. I don't want to sit up here and spew out useless words, so let me make my point. You are the first victim of the Rattler in the CWO. DALLAS: That's no threat, that's a promise! MYERS: The snake reference is purely coincidental. Ever seen the Rattler, Serpentmaster? Simple, but unique, and voted the best finishing move in TWO feds! May 10, you're going to feel it on you, and it's lights out. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for the REPORT today, we hope to see you at THURSDAY NIGHT FEVER tonight in Santa Fe, NM, and we'll definitely see you tomorrow on the REPORT!
