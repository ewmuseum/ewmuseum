3. Geoff Bondavarsky had an interview. 4. Vegas Villain wanted to join the Pit Bosses. The Bosses told him if he took out Necromancer, he could join. 5. Necromancer defeated Vegas Villain. 6. Necromancer had a replacement for Wizard, but Commissioner Thomas told him that it was Foolkiller’s decision. (Why? Who knows?) Foolkiller assured Necro that it would be worth it.
7. Christopher “Spot” Light and Iceman X defeated Geoff “The Attitude Adjuster” Bondavarsky and “The Ultimate Fighting Machine” Richard Gracie. 8. German Choklat came out to trash talk, but the Hooters’ Employee of the Month nailed her with a guitar from behind! 9. Brent “The Ace” Alles’ qualifying opponent turned out to be his former trainer and long time adversary, Lord Hile Troy, but Alles was able to prevail over Troy to advance to the BATTLEGROUND match on Sunday.
10. World Tag Team Champions the Beauregard Boys defeated the Creatures of the Night by disqualification when Gold Kard International attacked. 11. Foolkiller and Wildfire defeated the Pit Bosses. After the match, Foolkiller was attacked by Bad Boy Sports Entertainment (Kent Alles’ stable), but Brent “The Ace” Alles surprisingly made the save!
12. Television Champion Allen “Mr. America” Carter’s qualifying opponent turned out to be Masher Maccabee! Carter was also victorious tonight over the former World Champion (his belt was on the line as well), advancing to
BATTLEGROUND. 13. World Champion Damion defeated Quake when the Pit Bosses attacked. Foolkiller and Necromancer made the save to end the show! BATTLEGROUND PPV RECAP 1. The show started, and then showed how Damion and the Creatures of the Night were attacked earlier in the day. 2. Australian Assassin and Vegas Villain defeated Rising Sun (Torunaka and Dragon) when the Villain pinned Torunaka. 3. Johnny Starr had an interview with Damion, who said he would still keep his World title match with Wildfire that night. 4. Foolkiller still needed a fourth man for BATTLEGROUND that night, but unfortunately Union Caine couldn’t make it. Foolkiller’s former manager,
“Mastermind” Vince DaVinci told him to call… someone.
5. Geoff “The Attitude Adjuster” Bondavarsky defeated Iceman X in a wild match to become the CWO Hardcore Champion. 6. Wildfire talked about his match with Damion tonight, saying that he was sorry what happened to Damion, but it didn’t mean there would be any excuses. 7. Creatures of the Night (Crawler and Slinger) came down to the ring, not happy, wanting to know who attacked them earlier in the day. The culprits were revealed, as the Darknight Express (Demolition and Dimento) returned to the CWO! A match ensued, with the Creatures winning after Crawler pinned
Demolition, but the DKE got the last laugh (for now), attacking them after the match was over.
8. German Choklat won the Evening Gown match over Hooters’ Employee of the Month, after hitting her with a guitar. Afterwards, Choklat and Sengir were attacked by the other seven Hooters’ Employees of the Month for the year
2000! German Choklat’s weave was torn right off her head!
9. “The Ultimate Fighting Machine” Richard Gracie had an interview, saying he was ready for Christopher “Spot” Light in their Two out of Three Falls match for the Cruiserweight title. Light and manager Kent Alles were ready as well.
10. Light defeated Gracie in that match to win the Cruiserweight title. The first fall was won by Light, as he nailed his Lights Out 450 splash to gain victory. The second fall was won by Gracie, making Light tap out to the No
Way Out. The final fall was won by Light, after Kent Alles knocked Gracie out with chloroform or something similar outside the ring. Light mockingly put Gracie in a submission hold, and the ref stopped the match. Eddie
Fantastic then made his return to the CWO, attacking Light after the match!
11. True Americans (Television Champion Allen “Mr. America” Carter, new Hardcore Champion Geoff “The Attitude Adjuster” Bondavarsky, and Juli Ashton) were shown in footage taped earlier in the day, with them arriving at the airport. 12. World Tag Team Champions the Beauregard Boys (Ron and Shirley) defeated Gold Kard International (Gold Kard and “Baron” Brian Derks) when Shirley
Beauregard defeated Brian Derks.
13. Brent “The Ace” Alles, Foolkiller, Necromancer, and the mystery fourth man, revealed to be the returning Omar Martinez, defeated Allen “Mr. America” Carter, Quake, and the Pit Bosses in the Battleground match. Pit
Boss #2 and Necromancer started the match out. Allen Carter came out next, to make it two-on-one. Foolkiller then came out to even the score. Next was Pit Boss #1, to make it three-on-two. Brent “The Ace” Alles then came down to make it even again. Quake was the last man down for his side, and then Omar Martinez made his surprising return as the fourth man on
Foolkiller’s team! At the end, Necromancer had Pit Boss #2 in a crossface submission, and Alles had the same move on Pit Boss #1 in the same move outside the ring! Foolkiller, meanwhile, had been handcuffed to the cage by
Carter, Bondavarsky, and Martinez, who wasted no time in turning on the man who called him to be the fourth! Carter proceeded to jab Foolkiller with a shockstick, trying to make him give up. It ended up being Pit Boss #2 who submitted first, although it was questionable. Alles thought he had won the match, and bitterly put Necromancer in the Alles Clutch after the match.
Foolkiller was saved by Eddie Fantastic and Richard Gracie, as the reunited
True Americans scattered.
14. Wildfire defeated World Champion Damion to win the title. Wildfire wrestled a sound strategy, working on the injured knee of Damion, and in the end, when Damion tried for his Darkness Falls Tombstone piledriver, his knee buckled, and Wildfire fell on top of him, hooking the legs for the pinfall and the victory.
