CWO EYE ON THE STORM: INFERNO 2007

Jun 27, 2007, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWO - Championship Wrestling Organization http://www.cwowrestling.net EYE ON THE STORM: INFERNO 2007 (Pre-show shown on the PPV preview channel before the show.) - - - - - *The New Icon is sitting In a chair. We can see a fresh bandage on his forehead. The International title sitting on his lap.* This coming PPV. The Icon will once again show the world Why I am Just that. The Icon. I will not only beat Gold Kard. But I will also be crowned the extreme champion. Being a duo champion. Icon: Some will say what am I thinking. What are you doing being in a randomized match. Two against one. You being the top dog in the match. Well this is true. top dog I am. I am the Flavor of the month. more like the Flavor of the Decade. Icon: Can anyone stop my steam rolling further to the top. Not a damn person. See you guys at the PPV. *The scene fades to black.* - - - - - Starr is found backstage with B-Quikk. Q-Quikk has some things on his mind, he called Starr over to him for a special interview. STARR: We are here with B-Quikk. About the up coming Pay Per View, how do you think you will fare with Gold Kard and The Icon? If I am not mistaken The Icon has been on a roll and he has fought his way up to become the Champion. How do you think you will do? You are the underdog going into this match... B-QUIKK: He may have won the IC belt, but soon he will lose his belt to none other then the B-Quikk. About that underdog thing, they can say what they want. I come out every week and beat the @#%$ out of anyone in my way. The match at Inferno is no different. Also, this just came into my head and I laughed a little. Didn't The Icon lose to Justin Paine at a CWO Live event? Now that's funny. Oh Gold Kard and Icon, if the TLC match is picked, you guys will enter my world of pain. You will never be the same again. - - - - - (The scene shows the CWO logo and says promotion paid by the Monarch and the CWO and the fans go wild.) VOICE: "HE IS A FORMER EXTREME CHAMPION" (The Prince is shown in his great extreme matches with the Extreme title) VOICE: "HE IS A FORMER FEVER CHAMPION" (The Prince is shown with the Fever Title) VOICE: "HE IS A FORMER TWO-TIME INTERNATIONAL CHAMPION" (The Prince is shown when he won the international title the first and second time.) VOICE: "AND NOW......." ("Come with Me" by P Diddy plays as scenes from The Princes great match show, vs. DMT, vs. Nightrain, vs. Eddie Flash, and as the scenes are being shown the voice is heard...) VOICE: "NOW THE TIME AS COME FOR THE PRINCE TO TAKE HIS PLACE AS THE WORLD CHAMPION." THE PRINCE'S VOICE: "THIS IS MY DESTINY" VOICE: "TONIGHT HE TAKES ON FIVE OTHER MEN FOR HIS DREAM TO BE ACCOMPLISHED...WILL IT BE ACCOMPLISHED!!!!! THE PRINCE'S VOICE: "I WILL BE WORLD CHAMPION!!!!" VOICE: "WATCH INFERNO LIVE FROM HOUSTON, TEXAS ONLY ON PAY PER VIEW." P. DIDDY'S VOICE AND THE PRINCE'S VOICE: "COME WITH ME" (The music stops and the Screen goes black and Pyro explodes from the roof above the ring.) - - - - - (Johnny Starr is shown in the EYE ON THE STORM control center.) STARR: Good evening, CWO fans... we are less than 30 minutes away from the hottest CWO PPV of the year, INFERNO! We will be going over the complete line-up, but for now let's hear from a man who will defend his EXTREME title at the same time as he goes for Christopher John's International title... B-Quikk! - - - - - (B-Quikk is shown in the ring.) *CLICK, CLICK, BOOM..... B-Quikk flies from the ground while fire works are going off and the top of the ramp he holds his belt up high for all to see, he slowly makes his way down to the ring letting fans touch the belt, he slides into the ring. B-Quikk looks around and then he gets a microphone and begins to talk* B-Quikk: Wow, a lot of people here tonight. *Pauses* Well you guys probably know why I am here. I am here to talk about this upcoming match. The Two Fall, Two Titles, Triple Threat match. The Extreme Champion me, The IC champion The Icon and of course the no champion Gold Kard. *The People start to chant Gold Kard's name* B-Quikk: Yea, yea, yea. Chant his name all you want. That fact of the matter is that after this match is over. After blood is shed. After us three put everything on the line. There will be one winner, one winner to hold the belts up high, or in this matter maybe two winners. I am telling you all here tonight that one of those belts will be mine. *The Crowd boos* B-Quikk: Both of these men hate me a lot and I am not very fond of them. Also I know they are not fond of each other so this should be a pretty good match. I will say this in front of everyone here tonight. Tonight I will come out on top, I will make Gold Kard and The Icon bleed, because after the match is over, after the medics come down to the ring. There will be one man standing, one man holding the belts up high. That man will be me. *B-Quikk throws his microphone down and taunts the crowd* - - - - - (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Also involved in that match, as stated, will be Gold Kard and the International Champion Christopher John. Let's hear from him. - - - - - (this will be played out at the PPV now. As Jason drake will soon be out of the picture.) *Camera cuts to the a The Face of Titan. His new mask. His hair pulled back. You can see a smile on his face under the mask.* Titan: (Deep Voice) Boo. *Camera zooms out. The Icon is Standing there in a new black suit. the International title around his waist. Titan is seen with a shoulder brace on.* Icon: Looky who reunited. Since everyone has back up nowadays. I have awoke the Monster. Allowing him to be himself. Titan: The next world champion. Icon: Whoa there tiger. This is my promo no not yours. So yeah the rumors are true. We set that whole match up sorta. We been talking for weeks. The Match was of course real. Drake though was played. Those "guards" of yours. Paid off. By titan here. *The new Icon pulls out some blood capsules.* Icon: Then The big Finish. The Icon Effect off the top rope to the outside. Notice that the padding was extra padded. Granted Titan did further an injury already there. But we know the Titan. He's a Machine like Drake has said. Icon: So Titan is going into this match hurt. Big disadvantage right. Wrong. You see. What doesn't kill you only make you stronger. Titan here will do what he has to. Icon: this brings me to the "Triangle Match" Both titles on the line. Two fall. Wow guess the Icon will be a dual champion. I mean Quikk. What the hell have you really done. Oh you and Tony played tag with that belt. Icon: Gold Kard dude last time I saw you. You were feeling the Iconic effect. As I was walking out of the ring with this *Slaps the International title a couple times.* Around my waist. *Titan laughs at this.* Icon: So Keep it simple and quick when it comes to jobbers. I'm begging the Pres. Make this a dark match. Why. *He starts to chuckle.* You two aren't Pay Per View Material. Sorry it's true. - - - - - *Cameras cut to Justin with Starr. The INFERNO Backdrop can be seen in the background* STARR: I am now with Justin Paine, who will compete tonight against JSL. Justin, you made a very bold claim by saying that you were screwed out of a chance to compete in tonight's INFERNAL Match, care to comment further about that? PAINE: You know STARR I could make further comment about that, but right now that's not what I'm worried about, I know that in the end I will be the one smiling, may not be today, or tomorrow but rest assured I do have something planned. STARR: Care to elaborate for us as to what that "plan" might be? PAINE: You know what Johnny, some things are just better kept secrets until the time is right, and I feel that this one of those secrets that's better left off, just know that everyone will know sooner than later. STARR: Right...Are you really prepared for JSL Tonight? PAINE: You're kidding me right? (Paine looks the camera dead on and takes his sunglasses off) PAINE: I promise you all that I will walk out of here tonight with my hand raised in the air as the winner tonight. STARR: Don't you think that that is a bit of a bold statement? I mean you did promise a win over Welfare on FEVER and that didn't work out to your advantage so much. PAINE: Excuse me? STARR: Well I'm just saying that you kn... (Justin grabs the arm that Starr is holding the mic in and brings it up to his face) PAINE: Don't you ever question me when I say I'm going to win or I will snap this arm right off. Got it? Good. I'm through talking with you Starr, I've got a match to get to - - - - - (An INFERNO commercial ad is shown, showcasing B-Quikk, Gold Kard, and Christopher John.) VOICEOVER: Three men, One ring, Two Belts, Two Falls, This match is for the International Champion and the Extreme Championship. These men are going to everything on the line tonight when The Extreme Champion B-Quikk, The International Champion Christopher John and the new Extreme Manager Gold Kard meet up in one ring. Who will end up with the belts tonight? Watch and find out. - - - - - STARR: Lots of great action tonight... let's hear more from B-Quikk... - - - - - B-Quikk is found in is locker room, Starr enters the locker room and looks at B-Quikk. B-Quikk stares right back and smirks. B-Quikk: So Starr I guess your here for a interview, or a few words of enlightenment, or maybe you want to be my rag doll to warm up with? So which is it. Starr: Ah, I would like to interview you. B-Quikk: Aw man, I could use a punching bag. Oh well. So Starr what's the first question? Starr: Ah okay well, the first question is how are you feeling right now? B-Quikk: How am I feeling? What a stupid ass question. Anyways I am feeling fine. Not scared at all. I just want to enter that ring with the Extreme Championship and exit with the Extreme Championship and hopefully another belt as well. Starr: So you are battling Chris and Gold Kard tonight. You only have versed Gold Kard before not Chris. How did you prepare for this match? B-Quikk: I did what I normally do. I get up, go to the bathroom, Shower, drive here, then your ass came in. Besides that I didn't have to prepare for anything because I know I will be coming out of that match with a belt. Now go before I am tempted to use you as a punching bag. Starr: Ah well, okay. - - - - - STARR: And more from The New Icon - - - - - *The camera crew walks in on The New Icon. He is working on a heavy bag. He turns around.* Icon: Whoa there. What do you think this is. Walk in on the Icon. Trying to get a peek at the Icon in his underwear. Well you're a little too late. *The Icon picks up a battle of water and dumps water on his hair. Then drinks some.* Icon: Getting warmed up for the match. See I'm not really worried about winning the Extreme Title. Why it's a glorified Paper Weight. Icon: Look am I worried. Of course not. Why. I can beat both of these men in a one on one match. I've proven that time and time again. Especially with Gold Kard. *He picks up a towel he wipes his face off with it. He then tosses it aside.* Icon: This is how it's going to go down. I work on one man. That's who I go after. Gold Kard. Why. Again. I've faced this man on Four different matches. He has YET to beat me. Now that said. He gets taken out first. Then All I got to do is beat What ever his name is. *Camera man says something.* Icon: What was that? Camera Man: B-Quikk. Icon: Yeah that's his name. I keep forgetting his name. I mean he really hasn't done much to impress me. He carries a paper weight around with him and calls himself champion. Well tonight. I will show him what a Champion is. Icon: You see it's Time for the Rise of the ICON. Week after week match after match I pave my legacy. A Legacy of Gold. A legacy that all will remember all will one day go you remember the match win the Icon beat those Jobbers. Icon: Yes yes, Gold Kard I'm well aware that you're a hall a famer. That's the real big problem and why I call your a jobber. Your career is over with. In the mud. It was buried a long time ago. Your Legacy. The reason why you're being inducted into the Hall of fame. You taint it. Dishonoring what you have done in the past. Thus why you're nothing anymore. Thus why you will not be at the heights of your career once more. Its over and you best Retire. *the Icon signals for the camera crew to leave the locker room.* - - - - - STARR: That match only one of the big matches on the show tonight... of course the one everybody is talking about is the INFERNAL MACHINE... FEVER Champion Brent "The Ace" Alles... Surge... Prince David Tita... Ryan Welfare... the 7'3", 450# Titan... and the 2007 MAY-HEM winner Rt Savage will all compete inside the INFERNAL MACHINE to determine a new World Champion. Who will it be?
