Fever 4/1/99 - Results

April 3, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FEVER 4/1/99 Charlotte, NC (Charlotte Coliseum) Tribute to Ike Newton, father of CWO superstars the Newton Bros., was shown. Ike tragically died at age 56 of a stroke. The ring bell was sounded 5 times in his memory.
Darkness and the Dark Side were shown in the luxury suite, along with the
Alleses. Stills from March Madness were shown. Interview with Foolkilller, revealing that last year when he signed a contract to reenter the CWO, it also guaranteed him a title shot at APRIL
ARMAGEDDON '99, no matter what, and in a cage no less.
Johnny Reb and Eddie Fantastic (who were in the ring with Foolkiller, as well as Vince DaVinci) then stated that they were going to get a rematch with the Creatures of Darkness at APRIL ARMAGEDDON, with the Fallen suspended above the ring in a cage!
Masher Maccabee then came out to say that he also had a contract to face
Darkness that very night! (The legal department must have been very busy!)
Through all this, Darkness and the Dark Side were shown reacting, not too happy, but Gordy Alles said they would think of something.
MATCH ONE: Brian 'The King' Derks defeated the Fallen by disqualification when the Dark Side attacked. Time of match: 4:42.
Warhawk was shown, out of character, stating his appreciation for all that
Ike Newton (his trainer) had done for him.
Simon St. Severe had an interview. At the conclusion, Kent Alles interrupted, and was choked by Severe momentarily but
MATCH TWO: Hollywood Haskins pinned Simon St. Severe after the Hollywood
Bomb and interference by Iceman X. Time of match: 6:30.
The Unit came out to do an interview, as they tried to be as upbeat as they could despite the tragedy in their lives. However, the Alleses (Gordy and
Kent) then came down to ruin it, saying that since the Newtons left a contractual match, that they would be fined. Holiday was then fined, just because he was associated with them! But the worse would come for Johnny B.
Goode, who was fined $100,000 and then fired! Goode responded by socking
Gordy in the jaw! Security quickly entered the ring to drag the Unit off.
The Alleses still had business to discuss. Kent Alles stated that Reb and
Fantastic couldn't get another title shot at the champs that easy, they would have to go through the Mercenaries that night. Gordy then called Lord
Hile Troy down to the ring. Gordy made Troy an offer that if he could take out his own son permanently before or during APRIL ARMAGEDDON, that he would get the prized #59 or #60 slot at MAY-HEM this year (the winner of course getting a shot at the World champion at SUMMER HEAT). Troy surprisingly accepted immediately, and said that he would start tonight!
MATCH THREE: Reb and Fantastic defeated the Mercenaries in a "#1 Contender Match" when
'Irresistible Assassin' Eddie Fantastic pinned Korperal Punishment after the inside cradle. Time of match: 17:31.
Brent "The Ace" Alles was shown backstage with a guest when both were attacked by Troy! Troy told Alles to get out of the CWO or he wouldn't be responsible for what he did.
MATCH FOUR: Sandman pinned 'Assassin 64' Sean Holiday after the Sandblast and outside interference by Warhawk. Time of match: 9:57.
The Sheets Foundation arrived at the arena and went to their locker room, only to find a prank and a note left for them there by Allen Carter and the
True Americans.
HOUR #2: The True Americans came out for an interview, dressed as... cowboys, and
Geoff "The Attitude Adjuster" Bondavarsky... recited poetry. Never a dull moment on FEVER!
MATCH FIVE: CWO NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP - Joe Eagle/Flash Varga defeated Renegades when Flash Varga pinned Cooper Young after the Flash
Flood. Time of match: 10:19. Joe Eagle/Flash Varga won the CWO North
American Tag Team Champions titles. After the match Johnson attacked Young, nailing him with a Hobbes' Revenge (jumping neck snap he used when he was known as Calvin). Southern Dynamite came down to make the save, but got a
Hobbes' Revenge for his trouble as well. The association of the Renegades might be over.
The Outlaw Riders had an interview, discussing March Madness. Ranger Taurus was first, stating that he now wanted to be known as Black Jack Hollister, rather than be forced to perform under the gimmick the CWO wanted him to.
However, another shock on a night of shocks then occurred... as Mondo Gecko said he couldn't trust ANY of the Riders, and apparently left the group!
MATCH SIX: CWO NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP - Fergus 'Whirling Scottish
Devil' McTavish pinned Black Jack Hollister after the Highland Fling. Time of match: 8:20. Fergus 'Whirling Scottish Devil' McTavish won the CWO North
American Champion title.
Darkness came down for an interview, issuing an affirmative reply to Foolkiller's challenge. Sandman Jr. and Geoff Bondavarsky were shown before there match discussing how Bondavarsky had paid Sandman Jr. to wreck Fergus' date.
MATCH SEVEN: True Americans (Carter, Bondavarsky, and Sandman Jr.) defeated the Sheets Foundation (Hakujin, Dragon, and Popalov) by disqualification when Fergus attacked Sandman Jr. in the ring. Fergus had attacked
Bondavarsky prior to this. Time of match: 11:22. The two teams brawled in the ring, and then Union Caine (late of the Men of Respect) came down, wearing a MoR T-shirt. He then attacked the Foundation! Caine wripped off his MoR T-shirt to reveal a True Americans T-shirt beneath. He left laughing with the other TA.
MATCH EIGHT: CWO WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP - Darkness defeated Masher Maccabee by disqualification when he refused to release the Blackout (dragon sleeper).
Time of match: 6:32. Darkness retained the CWO World Champion title. Darkness still refused to release it when the Australian Assassin (yes, that's right) came down to make the save. However, Darkness then delivered a THUNDERFUNK to the hapless Australian, seemingly a warning to Foolkiller of things to come.
After the cameras stopped rolling, however, Foolkiller stormed down to the ring and an impromptu match occurred! Both men battled back and forth, but eventually Foolkiller scored a Thunderfunk! He slapped the mat three times as the crowd went nuts. The Dark Side then came out to make the save, but
Foolkiller rolled out to escape, as the show ended on Foolkiller staring down the World Champion.
