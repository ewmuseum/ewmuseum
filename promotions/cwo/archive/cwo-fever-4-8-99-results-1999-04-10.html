FEVER 4/8/99 - Results

April 10, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FEVER 4/8/99 Des Moines, IA (Veterans Memorial Coliseum) DARK MATCH: Southern Dynamite/'Mr. Technical' Cooper Young defeated Simon St. Severe/'Smilin'' Doc Watson when 'Mr. Technical' Cooper Young made 'Smilin'' Doc Watson submit to the Technicality. Time of match: 13:57. Severe attacks Watson after the match!
HOUR #1: The Unit had an interview, discussing recent events in their life and asking for a match that night with three of the Dark Side, which they received.
"Assassin 64" Sean Holiday also asked for a scaffold match with Warhawk at APRIL ARMAGEDDON '99! Vladimir Popalov was shown entering the building, obviously angry with something. MATCH ONE (TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP): Fallen defeated Champion Vladimir
Popalov by disqualification when Foolkiller attacked the Fallen, delivering a Thunderfunk in the process. Time of match: 6:31.
Foolkiller dragged the Fallen back, wanting to make an example of him. He put him on a forklift and Vince DaVinci lifted him into the air.
The Dark Side were shown dealing with the Fallen situation. Darkness said that he didn't want Foolkiller touched, that he wanted him "intact" for
ARMAGEDDON. Lord Hile Troy had an interview, where he actually didn't say that much, promising an explanation for his actions of late next week on FEVER.
The Hardcore Sensation had a taped piece, showing them playing strip poker with Stunning Dora. (Thank goodness for her presence.)
The North American Tag Team Champions, Joe Eagle and Flash Varga, had a quick segment discussing their lack of respect for the current state of affairs in the tag team division. They were then delivered a mysterious note that said the Rat Pack would be reforming next week on FEVER! Varga protested his innocence to Eagle, who didn't seem so sure...
MATCH TWO: Col. Taggart defeated Brent 'The Ace' Alles by disqualification when Lord Hile Troy attacked Taggart while he had Alles in the Annihilator.
Time of match: 8:41. Troy then put Alles into the figure four. Ewiak came out and attacked Troy for what he did to Taggart. Alles and Troy then brawled through the crowd to the back as Ewiak tended to Taggart. Gordy and
Kent Alles then came out to inform Ewiak and Taggart that they would be getting a North American Tag Team title match at APRIL ARMAGEDDON '99.
However, it was not the joyous announcement that it should have been, because Ewiak and Taggart were told that they would have to break up for good if they didn't win the match. Taggart would eventually threaten Kent
Alles, and that brought the Hardcore Sensation (Kurt Insanity and Kirk
Madness) down to ringside to beat on Bad Company. Beat on them they did, as they spike piledrivered Taggart through a table and delivered their signature move, the Sensational Bomb (where Insanity delivers an Asai leg lariat off the top rope to an opponent that is on the shoulders of Madness)
to Ewiak. Taggart had to be taken away on a stretcher, and his medical condition is not known at this time.
Fergus McTavish winning the North American title last week on FEVER was shown. MATCH THREE: Fergus 'Whirling Scottish Devil' McTavish defeated Man Mountain
Marko by disqualification when Sandman Jr. attacked. Time of match: 7:33.
Fergus 'Whirling Scottish Devil' McTavish retained the CWO North American
Champion title. Wayne Schillo also got involved during the match, pretending to be a kid in a wheelchair wearing a Fergus match. This gave him the opportunity to beat on Marko momentarily, but Fergus then clotheslined him! However, the lights then went out and when they went back on, Sandman Jr. was in the ring and hit Fergus with the Singapore cane repeatedly. The Sheets Foundation made the save.
A taped piece involving George Washington down in Mexico was shown. In it, the True American discussed his desire to regain his original name, Omar
Martinez, but didn't know how to go about it.
HOUR #2: Footage was shown of Union Caine joining the True Americans from last week. MATCH FOUR: Allen 'Mr. America' Carter and Geoff 'The Attitude Adjuster' Bondavarsky defeated Secret Agent Super Dragon and 'Dark Shogun' Akueri
Hakujin when Geoff 'The Attitude Adjuster' Bondavarsky pinned Secret Agent
Super Dragon after the Rhino Charge and outside interference by George
Washington! Time of match: 10:21. Washington left as quickly as he came, adding intrigue to the match.
The Outlaw Riders had a flash, discussing recent matters and their matches coming up that very night. They also asked for Gecko to come back, that he could trust the rest of them.
MATCH FIVE: CWO WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS: Hollywood Haskins and Iceman X defeated the World Tag Team Champions Creatures of Darkness by disqualification, which was a surprise considering the amount of weapons used at the beginning of the match. Time of match: 4:52. Creatures of
Darkness retained the CWO World Tag Team Champions title. The disqualification came because of an attack by the Australian Assassin and
Masher Maccabee. The Assassin even went so far as to put Haskins' ankle in a chair, and then stomp on the chair! Early reports have it that Haskins suffered a broken ankle, which wouldn't be too surprising.
Black Jack Hollister defeated Cris Marcus after pinning him with a Texas
Stampede. Marcus (accompanied by VP Kent Alles) tried to get away from the assault of his former Outlaw Riders teammate, but Mondo Gecko came out to make sure he got back in the ring, decking Kent Alles in the process! Time of match: 3:22. There was no reunion between Gecko and Hollister, though, as the former leader of the Riders left as soon as he came.
Dark Side (Darkness, Warhawk, Sandman) defeated the Unit (Holiday and the
Newton Bros.) after Warhawk pinned Holiday with the War Bomb after a Samoan drop and Sindy Sinn throwing black powder in her former charge's eyes. Time of match: 17:51. After the match the Dark Side continued to beat on the
Unit. Foolkiller came out to make the save, but it was an ambush!
(Obviously, Darkness' earlier order to leave Foolkiller alone was a ruse.) The Fallen, avenging his humiliation by Foolkiller earlier on in the show, delivered a Tombstone piledriver to the former World Champion on the steel runway! The Fallen, Creatures of Darkness, and Wrath then carried
Foolkiller to the ring and gave him to Darkness, who delivered a Thunderfunk to add to the humiliation. He then covered Foolkiller as Wrath made an of course unofficial three count. The show ended with Darkness momentarily triumphant over Foolkiller.
After the cameras stopped rolling, though, Johnny Reb and "Irresistible
Assassin" Eddie Fantastic, along with Brent "The Ace" Alles came out to make the save, and along with the Unit drove the Dark Side away. An angry
Foolkiller then said that if Darkness thought he was going to destroy him, that he had another think coming and promised that somebody at ARMAGEDDON was going to BURN IN HELL.
