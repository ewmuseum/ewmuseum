CWO REPORT 12/4/96

4 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The CNN News screen appears and it's theme music plays. On screen comes Wolf Blitzer, the famous Gulf War news correspondent. He is standing out in the middle of a street. Police and amublances can be seen every where down the road. It looks as if fire trucks are trying to put the whole block out while police keep people at bay.) WOLF BLITZER (in his professional voice): This is Wolf Blitzer reporting live from Miami, Florida. A strange and vicious crime has happened to this dominantly African-American neighborhood where crime takes place daily. From eyewitness accounts, a huge caucasian figure wearing a mask came into the neighborhood at about 1:00 AM. Residents approached the figure and a fight proceeded. The man single-handedly took out all eight of them. Neighbors heard the ruckus, and came out to investigate. The masked stranger then attacked them, crippling two residents. But that is not the end to this destruction. After that the hulk went to a parked car and brought out some kind of containers. From there he started to set houses on fire. As you can see behind me, the whole neighborhood went up in flames. Fire fighters are just now bringing the fires under control. Let me take you to one of the eye witnesses. (Wolf motions for the witness to approach. A teenage youth stands next to Blitzer.) BLITZER: Can you tell us what you saw here tonight? WITNESS (voice shaking): Yeah, I can tell ya. This big dude, comes walking up in our crib like he owns da place. So a couple of my homeboys walks over to da man and aks da man whas he doin' here. He then starts hittin' my boyz, and layin em out. I ain't ever seen someone like him. For da first time in my life I waz scared white. BLITZER: Thank you for your interview. (With that the boy is taken away by police for further questioning.) BLITZER: The havoc that came down upon this hostile environment is unbelieveable. It is frighting to know that one man did this, and he is still walking free. (A voice from CNN headquarters asks Wolf a question. It is the voice of James Bernard.) BERNARD: Wolf, are there any leads to who committed this crime? And are there any casualties? BLITZER: Right now there is no evidence to who or what did this. However, the reports of a huge masked figure lead to a suspect who is a wrestler in the CWO known as Bane. A week ago, there was a Pay Per View event held here in Tampa, Florida. There, Bane was thrown off a balcony and thought to have been taken to the hospital. Instead, the ambulance was found parked a few blocks down the road. The driver and paramedic were found severly beaten and unconscious inside the vehicle. The man known as Bane has not been seen or heard of since then. Police have put out an APB on him hoping to subdue him, and bring him in for questioning. He is considered extremely dangerous, and if you see a person like him DO NOT, I repeat DO NOT try to approach him. Call the police, and wait indoors. As far as any casualties are concerned it is unknown at this present time. The police are skepticule that everyone got out of their homes in time. What I do know is that four people were aparently crippled when they attacked the big man. There is no word yet, on how their condition has progressed. (The voice of Bernard is heard again.) BERNARD: Wolf, what is the situation like down there? Are things starting to get under control? BLITZER: The fires are starting to be under control, but there is a more dangerous condition starting to unfold. It seems that the local people are angry that a white man came in here and did this. Police are trying to calm them down in hopes that no riots will take place. (Just then screaming can be heard as well a shattering glass. Police are running towards a huge crowd that has started rioting. A brick flies past Wolf's head.) BLITZER: As you can see it has gotten out of hand here, and the police are telling us to get out of the area as quickly as possible. With that, I am Wolf Blitzer, reporting live from CNN. (The CNN logo comes up as we fade out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello everybody, welcome back to the REPORT, it's good to be back with you after the holiday. As you saw by the CNN footage at the top of our show, the big story in the CWO right now, well, one of the big stories at least, is Bane. We will keep you posted on this situation as we receive information, Bane is currently scheduled to face Sandman in a Locked Steel Cage match at CWO CELEBRATION, no word yet on how that match will be affected. However, last night, we witnessed a strange attack by the Apocalypse on the Deadly Sins, in which they kidnapped their manager, Lust. We take you back to last night's FEVER. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Footage from last night's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER is shown, Johnny Starr is in the locker room area with the Deadly Sins and Lust.) JOHNNY STARR: All right, I am with the Deadly Sins, Pride and Sloth, along with their manager Lust, and gentlemen, you have the shot against the Pit Bosses at November Pain. PRIDE: That's right, Johnny, and we... (Suddenly Bane and the Apocalypse attack.) STARR: What the...? (Bane and the Apocalypse batter the Sins and Starr, and Bane leaves with the screaming Lust over his shoulder.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: All right, it should be noted that we thought the man with the Deadly Sins was Bane, however, that man was caught later in the evening, and was found to be an impostor, making this situation even more strange. The impostor has been held for questioning, however, Lust, the manager of the Deadly Sins, who are scheduled to take on the Pit Bosses for the North American Tag Team Championship at CWO CELEBRATION, was not found. Well, we're not trying to make light of this situation, but in the CWO, the show must go on, and we have several shows scheduled this week. Let's take a look at tonight's show, scheduled for Olympia, WA. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 4, 1996 OLYMPIA, WA Coldfront vs. Gods of Thunder Mike "Lonestar" Williams vs. Lafayette Sin vs. Johnny Reb Highlanders vs. Deadly Sins Black Death vs. Bane Cake Boy vs. Sandman Jr. LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Dark Raven vs. Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Steel vs. Miguel Sanchez (champion) WORLD 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (steel cage): Blood Inc. (Panther and D. E. A. T. H.) vs. The Dark Side (Hellhound and Creatures of the Night) (champions) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: As you can see, Bane was scheduled to take on newcomer Black Death on this card, however, with the recent incident, you can be sure that if Bane even comes near the Olympia Auditorium, he will be apprehended by the authorities. So, a replacement for Bane against Black Death will probably be found. The Olympia card will also mark the CWO debut of a strange newcomer known only as Cake Boy. We have comments from him. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Cake Boy is in the interview area, dressed in a pink leotard and holding a piece of cake.) CAKE BOY: Ssay there, boyss. There'ss a new wressssstler in town. (He takes a bite of cake.) CAKE BOY: And my name'ss Cake Boy. I'm looking to get my handss on a few gooood men. I'm sstarting with you, Ssandman Jr. I wanna put you to ssleep. Mr Ssandman, bring me a dream. (Cake boy makes a large kissing sound). CAKE BOY: Asss for all of you other wresstlerss out there. Watch out, causse Cake Boy isss here. I'll be sseeing you, in your dreamss. (Cake Boy licks frosting off cake leaving his mouth covered.) CAKE BOY: Yummmy. (Cake Boy smiles seductively as the camera fades.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Uh... yeah. Cake Boy's opponent on the card will be Sandman Jr., we have comments with him, these have not been seen before, taken after his team's loss at NOVEMBER PAIN in the WARGAMES match against Doomsday. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Sandman Jr., Kellen, and ECW's Sandman in the locker room.) SANDMAN JR.: Dammit, it's my fault we lost... we shoulda beat those bastards... DAMMIT!!! ECW SANDMAN: YOOOO! Junior! Don't be so down! Let me tell ya, having the fans on your side now is worth the loss! Nothing wrong being a bad guy for awhile, but sometimes yas just need the fans! Forget Doomsday, ya got friends you can count on now, not like the Sins, and now you can start focusing on titles! Trust me, it's all worth it! And cut out the cigarettes kid, I appreciate you being like me, but the stuff is bad! SANDMAN JR.: You're right! Dammit all, you're right! There's a reborn Sandman Jr now folks, and it's time to do some housecleaning here in the CWO! Prepare for the second coming of SANDMAN JUNIOR!!! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Also on the card will be Sin, and Sin's apparently not too happy with the CWO these days. We go to comments from him. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Sin is inside an occult room. Burning torches and skulls nailed to the dark, dank walls surround him. Sin is standing on a Red Pentagram 7 foot in diameter.) SIN: Hello there, boys and girls. It seems the people who run the music made a little mistake at Eye On The Storm. They forget that I am not of the Holy stature. They have been notified and taken care of. I am not angry at the fact my team lost at Eye On The Storm... it's not my fault they sucked. Although. I have been pondering the thought of maybe making a 3-man team with the Deadly Sins. Although I have not contacted them, I thought maybe every once in a while we could partner up and jointly take over in a 3-man competition. So I am asking you, DEADLY SINS, how's about it? Oh and soon there will be a new tag team intering the CWO. Also with whom I am allies. So, CWO, watch out... because Sin is about to intervine. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Sin's opponent on the card will be Johnny Reb, and based on last night's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, Johnny Reb will have to be careful not to overlook Sin on his way to his World title match at CWO CELEBRATION against Master of Darkness Damion. We take you now to comments from Reb and crew. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Johnny Reb, Rebel Yell, and General Lee are in a dimly lit interview area.) JOHNNY REB: It figures that it had ta happen this way. As a mattuh of fact, it's the only way ah would want it. The man who cost me gold on three diffrent occasions has to face his destiny. Look me in mah eyes Damion! You cain't. You know it will end at CWO CELEBRATION. You cain't beat Johnny Reb. Y'all have tried...and y'all have failed. Three times ah've had a hand in puttin' you away Damion. Welcome ta mah world son. Welcome ta the fate that has befallen you. GENERAL LEE: Damion, how does it feel to realize that this tahm, y'all are the prey? How does it feel, knowin' that this man has and will again put you down? Y'all are lookin' at a real short reign boy. JOHNNY REB: Y'all say that you bring the naht? Ah've been through yo' naht. Ah've seen the darkness...you didn't finish the job when you had the chance. Next Sunday, yo' chickens come home ta roost. REBEL YELL: As for chickens comin' home ta roost... let's talk about the CM2WF... ah've been there, ah've done that. But ol' Gold Kard wants ta' live in the past. Well Gold Kard, ah am your past. You could nevuh get the best of Rebel Yell. Well the man standin' beside me maht as well be mah clone. All summuh, ah trained this man at mah gym in Chattanooga. And now he is ready; he is ready to reclaim the spot destined fo' the Confederate Associates. This man is ready ta' double down. He will be the CWO North American champion, and he will be the CWO World Heavyweight Champion. JOHNNY REB: The Confederate Associates are greatuh than the sum of their parts. Yeah, it hurts losin' Ben Slocum, but we will get by. Ben had to get his house in order, but one thing is certain, he will always be an Associate. We've had the General and Dappuh Dan scoutin' a replacement, but that's fo' later. Fo' now...we get busy bringin' the gold home. GENERAL LEE: Y'all will pay for what you have done Damion. Good Day, suh. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: The other man factored in that triangle is the now-former World champion Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo, we have comments from him. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo is in the Dorm Room Discussion area.) WAYNE "KEUKA GUY" SCHILLO: This week, I don't need any guests... I can say what I need to say myself. Listen up, GKI, you've gotten yourself into a situation I don't think you can handle. You took away the thing that meant more to me than anything else, and now, you're gonna pay. You let the kids down, you let the ladies down, you let all the fans down, and they all stand behind me. The fans will not stand for things like this happening in the CWO.... Watch out! Damion, joining GKI was the biggest mistake of your life. See guys, I don't give a damn if every other wrestler in the CWO joins the CM2WF and they all come after me... I'm not letting you get to me, and I have a feeling I'm not the only man who feels this way. You can cheat me out of my title, but you can't cheat the fans. They know who the real champ of the CWO is, and you GKI deviants are pathetic... I WILL get my title back... I WILL beat every member of GKI, one by one, in a steel cage, starting with that fat tool the Mad Doctor's Ghost, and ending with you, Damion. Watch your back. I've been calm up to now, BUT I'M NOT GONNA BE SO CALM WHEN I TEAR EVERY SINGLE ONE OF YOU THE PIECES! FIVE TO ONE! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: And finally, we hear from the now World champion, Master of Darkness Damion. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on MOD Damion who is holding is new World title over his head with the rest of the Dark Side at his feet.) MOD DAMION: Who is the most powerful force in the CWO? CREATURES OF THE NIGHT AND HELLHOUND (Dark Raven is mute): You are, master. You are the greatest force in the CWO. MOD DAMION: Yes, I am. I also control the greatest stable in the CWO. The Dark Side. We are the rulers of this CWO, no one can stop us from doing what we want. Look at all the gold worn by these beasts. (The camera zooms out to show the creatures of the night standing on either side of Damion with Hellhound and Dark raven at the outer ends of the line.) MOD DAMION: These two Creatures of the Night are the World Tag Team champs and with my pet Hellhound are also the world 6-man tag champs, and the beast at the end, Dark Raven, he is the two time current lightweight champion. Of course, I am the NEW WORLD CHAMPION of the CWO. I rule these beasts, they rule all of you in the CWO, and now I rule them and you. I rule the CWO. I have this belt I have this team and soon we shall have the last four belts not currently in my possession. That's right, we will get the NA tag titles, the NA singles title, the TV title, and the new Hardcore title. Then you will see that The Dark Side is truly the greatest powerhouse team in the CWO. There ain't nothing anyone in the CWO can do to stop my from successfully completing my quest. Ben Slocum thought he could and now look at him. He has run away with his tail between his legs. Johnny Reb thinks he can but so far has been unable to, and come Dec. 15, I will rid the CWO of him too. As for the rest of you, get in my way and you all will suffer the same consequence as Ben and Johnny have and will. It will be slow, it will be painful, and yes it will hurt, I promise you. THE DARK SIDE: LONG LIVE THE NIGHT! (Camera fades to black as the champs all pose with their gold, then turn and leave.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for the REPORT today. We'll see you tomorrow!
