CWO REPORT 12/8/96

8 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on the Dark Side . MOD Damion stands between the Creatures of the Night while Hellhound sits at his master's feet.) MOD DAMION: Johnny Reb, are you ready? I told you the Dark Side was coming after you and tonight Hellhound has the first chance. I have had him on a chain for most of his existence and now you will be at the other end of that rope and in the strap match tonight you will suffer for all that you have done wrong to the Dark Side. Tonight I have a former champ to toss around the ring and prove to everyone that I am the rightful champion and ruler of the CWO. Barry Hart, you will be my sacrificial lamb tonight. I will destroy you right in the middle of the ring. HELLHOUND: Johnny Reb, I will drag you around the ring while I beat you senseless like the master has told me. You see, you are not to make it to CWO Celebration, but if you do, you will be so weak that the Master will be able to destroy you with no problem, not that he would have any trouble at all anyway. MOD DAMION: Yes, my pet, you will destroy that scum and come next Sunday, Johnny, I will be ready, will you? Tonight, my Creatures are also in action, they have a young team to beat but a team that could be great if they could stay away from teams the like of my creatures. NIGHT SLINGER: Flame Spirits, the master has picked you to be our next challenge and it is up to us to prove to him that we are still able and this we shall do. You boys are in a world of trouble. NIGHT CRAWLER: You see, boys, we are the WORLD tag team champs, and you are nothing. We have beat every team in this fed that has dared to enter the ring with us and now you two shall see what fears and DARKNESS they all talk about. Your fears shall fuel us to victory, your souls will power the master in his quest for supremacy. MOD DAMION: Tonight is a time for the Dark Side to come out and play. We all will be at the arena and that can only mean trouble for the rest of you. ALL FOUR: LONG LIVE THE NIGHT! _______________________________________________________________________________ (CNN logo comes up as we go to the ever faithful Wolf Blitzer. Blitzer looks worn out, as if he has been traveling a lot.) BLITZER: Good evening, America. I'm Wolf Blitzer, reporting live from St. Louis, Missouri. Behind me is the Budweiser Brewing Company. It stands engulfed in flames. (Scenes of the plant are shown as fire fighters are struggling to subdue the ever growing flames.) Wolf: A fire has been started here at the brewing plant by an unknown assailant. Everyone who was working inside got out in time, although some had to be helped out by others. It appears that someone broke in, and set fire to the instillation from the inside. Three security guards were attacked from behind and knocked unconscious. They did not get a look at their attacker, and the surveilance cameras were destroyed in the fire. It is unknown who did this atrocity, but one clue was left behind. A message was written in what appears to be blood in the parking lot leading to the building. The blood has been determined to have come from an animal. The inscription read: Do not seek to find for whom the bell tolls... it tolls for thee... Sandman. This is the only clue as to who has committed this crime. Federal investigators on the scene have linked the message back to the wanted criminal known as Bane. Sandman is another wrestler who threw Bane off a balcony some time ago before these crimes started. Police are on their way to question Sandman now. As far as the villain known as Bane is concerned, he has still managed to outsmart the police. There is now a nation-wide manhunt for this criminal. Police are doing the best they can, but the slippery Bane eludes them at every turn. It seems that he is constantly on the move, and no one can predict where he will strike again. As of now, he is leaving in his wake what the police are calling a "Trail of Terror." The search continues for this man. He is considered extremely dangerous. If you see him, do not approach him. Instead, call the police right away. That is all from St. Louis, Missouri. This is Wolf Blitzer reporting live for CNN. _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Sunday, December 8, 1996. As you can see, the Bane situation continues to escalate, still, CWO Commissioner has claimed that he will not back down, the Locked Steel Cage match between Sandman and Bane will still go down one week from now at CWO CELEBRATION. As always, we will keep you posted. Last night, however, the superstars of the CWO were in Calgary, Alberta, Canada, let's look at the results from that great card from the Great White North. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW REPORT SATURDAY, DECEMBER 7, 1996 CALGARY, ALBERTA, CANADA Deadly Sins vs. Mondo Gecko and Battlecat Deadly Sins defeated Mondo Gecko and Battlecat in 3:27 when Pride pinned Battlecat. STRAP MATCH: Hellhound vs. Johnny Reb Hellhound defeated Johnny Reb in 16:56, dragging him around the ring, after delivering the Day to Night (gorilla press followed by a splash). After the match, Hellhound refused to let up, prompting Beauregard Dixon and Rebel Yell to make the save. Joe Eagle vs. Brent "The Ace" Alles Brent "The Ace" Alles pinned Joe Eagle in 10:40 after the Allesbreaker. After the match, Brent "The Ace" Alles was attacked by the Pit Bosses. Vince Manocelli vs. Brian "The Hammer" Williams (substituting for Mad Doctor's Ghost) Akillies was supposed to have been the substitution for Mad Doctor's Ghost, but newcomer Brian "The Hammer" Williams came down to ringside and asked Akillies to step aside, as he needed "to see what kind of man Manocelli is." Akillies agreed, and the match was on. A hard-fought battle was waged, and Brian "The Hammer" Williams came out on top, pinning Manocelli after the Keuka Smack (boxing of the opponent's ears, then an uppercut) in 16:21. After the match, Manocelli shook Williams' hand, and said that maybe they should team up sometime in the future. Big Cool vs. Highlanders Big Cool and Highlanders wrestled to a double disqualification in 10:29. Ganglord vs. Wayne Carpenter Carpenter and Ganglord wrestled to a double countout in 2:29. Gold Kard vs. Hile Troy Gold Kard pinned Hile Troy in 8:33 after Die Flieder Kross (flying somersault splash), pulling the tights. NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Pit Bosses vs. Mercenaries Pit Bosses defeated the Mercenaries by countout in 3:56. WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Creatures of the Night vs. Flame Spirits Flame Spirits defeated the Creatures of the Night in 12:44 when Wolf pinned Night Crawler after the Eternal Flame (Wolf lifts up opponent, Griffen delivers a running lariat). WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Master of Darkness Damion (champion) vs. "Master of Pain" Barry Hart "Master of Pain" Barry Hart pinned Master of Darkness Damion after the Cardiac Arrest (shooting star press) in 10:58. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Don't forget about Showcase, coming out later today, here's the lineup! _______________________________________________________________________________ CWO SHOWCASE SUNDAY, DECEMBER 8 Steve Keller vs. Tiger Rogers Wildfire vs. David "Destroyer" Davis Pair of Aces (Flyboy and Clark Gray) vs. Blood, Inc. (Steel and Panther) Sandman Jr. vs. Hile Troy LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Dark Raven (champion) vs. Moses _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We now take you to comments from CWO superstars. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Exile is in the interview area. A crazed look is in his eye... blood is covering his face.) EXILE: Sandman Jr., (chuckles) do you think that I don't know where home is? (smiles) I DO, I DO! It's here! CWO... CM2WF... or whatever... (laughs maniacally) They don't tell me... but I was exiled... (ponders... then looks puzzled) So long ago... I don't remember... I know the NIWF exiled me... (laughs insanely) and the WHCW tries... but they can't throw us out... (laughs) (All of of a sudden, Exodus comes from behind... and puts Exile in a bear hug. While Exodus holds the screaming and kicking Exile, Lord Guru appears. Lord Guru looks into Exile's eyes, Exile calms down.) LORD GURU (cold voice): Put him in the cage. Make sure he is ready for Tuesday... and... (looking hard at Exodus who suddenly Cringes) make sure that he is ready... for if he isn't, YOU will suffer his fate. (smiles) (Looking determined, Exodus walks away with Exile in a bearhug.) LORD GURU (looking at the camera): It is time... (whispers) for your end... Sandman Jr., prepare to meet the man who really puts you to sleep.... (Camera fades out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Bad Guys are in the interview area with Herbie Versmells.) JOE "THE MEAN GUY" RUCKER: HAHAHA! Tower of Power, you think you're out of your contract with Russell Hill?!? Not so fast! If I remember correctly, the contract said that it shall be null and void if all members of the stable agree to leave it... Ron and I are in the stable.... RON "THE RUDE GUY" JOHNSON: AND WE'RE NOT SO SURE WE WANT OUT! RUCKER: I mean, we couldn't care less about your pathetic stable, but if you want out, what's in it for us? What are you willing to pay? JOHNSON: We're waiting for your answer, lard butts. HERBIE VERSMELLS: They got you by the keg, ToP! Whatcha gonna do? _______________________________________________________________________________ (Dorm Room Discussion opens up as Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo is with Doomsday: Baby Waldo, Maximum Overdrive, and the Mercenaries. Waldo's wearing a "5 to 1" t-shirt that's a few sizes too small.) WAYNE "KEUKA GUY" SCHILLO: Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to another fantastic edition of the Dorm Room Discussion! Today, let's welcome our guests, Baby Waldo, Maximum Overdrive, and the Mercenaries! So, what brings you to the DRD today? MAXIMUM OVERDRIVE: Well, to tell you the truth, we thought we'd pay a visit to the uncrowned CWO World Champion. Oops, sorry about that couch. Waldo! Where are your manners? SCHILLO: Don't worry about the couch... the CWO pays for that... Well, what do you think of the recent events in the CWO? OVERDRIVE: A lot is going on in the CWO. A lot of it I don't like. You have the Creatures of the Night posing as the CWO World Tag Team Champions, you have GKI trying to run this fed into the ground, and it seems that we have a common enemy in the person who is wearing the CWO World Title. SCHILLO: Yeah, I'm a little miffed about that myself. The CWO President has done nothing about it, and at the same time, GKI is falling apart! Waldo, I see you're wearing a "5 to 1" t-shirt... can you explain the significance of that? WALDO: Uhhhh, AAAARRRRGGGG! BBBWWWAAAAHHHH! SCHILLO (laughing): Overdrive, do you care to translate? OVERDRIVE: I'd be happy to. You see KG, I've noticed that several individuals in this federation seem to have you singled out. On more than one occasion, we have aided you when nobody else would as you were being attacked by these individuals. I will cut to the chase. The members of Doomsday are offering you membership in our ranks. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: We have beer! KAPTAIN KRUNCH: And chicks! We have got chicks, too! SCHILLO: That's an enticing offer... I've never been one to turn down chicks! With Brian entering the fed, what I need now is some sort of alliance. You help me out, and I'll help you out. Together, we can get something accomplished... what do you say? OVERDRIVE: I think that Doomsday would be more than happy to accept. Together, we can clean up this federation of the scum that have been residing here for too long. PUNISHMENT: Where's the keg around here? SCHILLO: The keg's behind the couch. Help yourself! PUNISHMENT: Thanks! SCHILLO: We've gotta show these CM2WF guys that the CWO will not just sit around and watch itself be overrun! We've got to stand up for our fed! OVERDRIVE: I will have to warn you, KG, that things aren't going to get pretty. SCHILLO: I can take it. We've got to do this for our fans! OVERDRIVE: There's going to be blood spilled, maybe some careers ended. SCHILLO: That's a chance we have to take. I hope it doesn't have to come to that, that we can get GKI to come around. OVERDRIVE: Can you guarantee this? SCHILLO: I can guarantee that wherever MOD Damion or GKI want to stick their noses, they'll have to deal with all six of us! OVERDRIVE: So we have a deal then? SCHILLO: You screw with one of us, you screw with all of us! It's a deal! OVERDRIVE: Excellent. Punishment, beer for everybody!! SCHILLO: And with that, we're out of time for today. Until next time, here's wishing you and yours plenty of chicks! PUNISHMENT: What kind of beer you got in this? ALL: FIVE TO ONE! _______________________________________________________________________________ (WILDFIRE siting at a table at his home in Venice Beach, California.) WILDFIRE: Ganglord, you keep saying that you have already beaten me! You keep saying that you are the best! Well, I will warn you one last time, DO NOT UNDERESTIMATE ME! (Just then Amber bursts through the door.) AMBER: TAS, you low life excuse of a manager! If my man wins, then I want you in the ring for 5 minutes! That's right, 5 minutes! If you think you're so f@#$%^g good then except my challenge! WILDFIRE: Just wait, calm down Amb... AMBER: I'm warning you TAS, if you even try to get involved at CWO CELEBRATION, I will have no problem kicking your @$$! _______________________________________________________________________________ (The future North American champion and CWO ambassador to the world, Hile Troy, stands before the camera.) HILE TROY: World ambassador of the CWO. NOT the world champion, the man that owns the night. NOT the German wunderpunk who thinks... THINKS, that he owns the league, monopoly money doesn't buy things, Adolf, and when you made a bet against me like that, it was your worst move, and definitely, CERTAINLY not the supposed commitor of several crimes across the country, Bane. (Hile looks at a note which he holds in his hand.) HILE TROY: So Bane, you may be above the law, since they cannot seem to catch you. But you are NOT above pain, Bane, and if the FBI or the CIA can't find you and lock you up, you can be certain that I'll meet you in the ring, that's figuring anything is even LEFT of you after Sandman gets his hands on you! The world saw what I did to Brent "The Ace" Alles, one of the finest technical wrestlers the CWO has to offer, I beat him AGAIN with the Dragon Suplex! Gold Kard, that Dragon Suplex is going to defeat you next! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Gold Kard International is in the interview area with German Choklat.) GOLD KARD: My, my, my, a lot of things stirred up again in the CWO. Schillo, you a fool to join those dumbkopfs known as Doomsday, what have they done in this league? NOTHING! Just like you, college boy dumbkopf! Everybody now knows that your world title reign was nothing but a fluke, and myself and Herr Damion proved that! However, Herr Damion, I would warn you, do not betray us. There is a place for you and your men in the CM2WF, but if you cross me, then the wrath of GKI shall be yours to deal with. You keep your "pets" on a leash and keep them away from the Pit Bosses, or I will not be responsible for what happens to them. PIT BOSS #1: That's right, but not overlooking CWO CELEBRATION, Deadly Sins, like we said earlier, you'd better wake up. PIT BOSS #2: No, wait a minute, don't. Being the holiday season, we need to defeat you guys nice and quick to get back to our holiday shopping. BARRY "MASTER OF PAIN" HART: Hey, everybody, look at my butt! GOLD KARD (grabbing Hart): SHUT UP, DUMBKOPF! YOUR VAINGLORIOUS STATEMENTS ABOUT YOUR POSTERIOR TRY MY PATIENCE! (He lets him go.) Herr Hart, I make this statement to you: at CWO CELEBRATION, you take on that dumbkopf Brent "The Ace" Alles, the man who didn't know his place. Now I'm telling you, you'd better know your place in this organization. Already, we have seen you suffer a humiliating loss to the college boy dumbkopf at NOVEMBER PAIN. Now, I make this vow to you, and I am a man of my word, that if you do not beat Alles at CWO CELEBRATION, you are fired! Do you understand me?!? HART (glaring): I understand. GOLD KARD: Good. And what you dumbkopf CWO fans should understand is that in one week, ONE WEEK, all of your dreams and fantasies come crashing down around you, for when I beat that washed-up relic Troy, who I ALREADY BEAT THIS WEEK, in case you didn't notice, the CWO banner shall fall, crashing to the ground, crumbling to dust, and from those ashes shall arise a new banner, the CM2WF, and from then on, it will be wrestling the way I want it! (Gold Kard laughs manically as we fade out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Well, that's all the time we have for today. We'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT.
