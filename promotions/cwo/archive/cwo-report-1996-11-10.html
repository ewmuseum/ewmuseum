CWO REPORT 11/10/96

10 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for November 10, 1996. Last night the superstars of the CWO were in Portland, OR, let's look at the results of that great card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW REPORT SATURDAY, NOVEMBER 9, 1996 PORTLAND, OR LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Dark Raven vs. Talon (champion) Dark Raven and Talon wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Pit Bosses (champions) vs. Bad Guys Bad Guys defeated Pit Bosses in 11:32 when Joe "the Mean Guy" Rucker pinned Pit Boss #1 after striking him with illegal objects (Bad Guys' Bash). Herbie Versmells also held the leg of Pit Boss #1 to aid in the pinfall victory. TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Joe Eagle Brent "The Ace" Alles pinned Joe Eagle with a rollup in 7:21. MOD Damion vs. Inferno Master of Darkness Damion pinned Inferno in 5:40 when Inferno missed with a flying elbow drop. Apocalypse vs. The Big Cool The Big Cool defeated Apocalypse in 22:22 when Jim "Jazzman" Taylor pinned Pestilence after the Star Flash (backbreaker by Taylor, big splash by Jumbo Jenkins). Tiger Rogers vs. "Master of Pain" Barry Hart Barry Hart pinned Tiger Rogers after the Cardiac Arrest (shooting star press) in 9:59. WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): The Creatures of The Night vs. David & Goliath Creatures of the Night defeated David & Goliath in 24:41 when Night Crawler pinned Goliath after a big splash. Bane vs. Sandman Bane and Sandman wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. After the match Bane put the Merciful Death on Sandman. Talon and the Flame Spirits came down to the ring, and so did the Apocalypse, and both teams brawled all over the arena. Lafayette vs. David "The Destroyer" Davis David "The Destroyer" Davis pinned Lafayette after the Destroyer Dropper (flip off top rope) in 5:58. GangLord vs. Steel Steel made Ganglord submit to the Gank (Texas cloverleaf) in 9:07. Flyboy vs. Dark Raven Dark Raven pinned Flyboy in 6:34 after the Death From Above flying dropkick, using the ropes for leverage. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We now unfortunately have comments from CWO North American champion Gold Kard. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Gold Kard is in the interview area with the fetching German Choklat.) GOLD KARD: As one of your Amerikan singers once put it, it's hard to be humble when you're perfect in every way. Make that GOLDEN in every way. Let's look at the record books, shall we? The only man to still have his title from the start is yours truly, Gold Kard, the master wrestler. I've taken on dumbkopfs in cages, and I triumphed. I've taken on dumbkopfs in back alleys, and I triumphed again. I have surrounded myself with the top wrestlers in the sport, and we all have gold, either in our past, present, or future. And, when we are done with the Tower of Flour at NOVEMBER PAIN, we will move on to our next step of domination of this league! Come after this belt if you want, but to you dumbkopfs, it will be fool's gold. Gold Kard International is taking over, and there's nothing you can do about it, dumbkopfs! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today. Stay tuned for the CWO NOVEMBER PAIN CONTROL CENTER, coming out later today. And we'll see you Tuesday night on the FEVER! Bye, folks.
