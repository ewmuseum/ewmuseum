CWO REPORT 4/13/97

13 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control center.) STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Sunday, April 13, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the CWO held the first day of a big two-day show at the Toyko Dome in Japan. Tonight, of course, is the big TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER taping, but for now let's see what happened at last night's event! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 4/12/97 Tokyo, Japan David 'The Destroyer' Davis/Inferno defeated Deadly Sins by countout. Time of match: 18:35. Steel made Sandman Jr. submit to The Gank (Texas Cloverleaf). Time of match: 11:28. Goliath/Tiger Rogers and Mason-Dixon wrestled to a double disqualification. Time of match: 26:52. Johnny Reb made Gold Kard submit to the Texas Cloverleaf. Time of match: 5:46. Vlad the Impaler/Serpentmaster defeated The Big Cool when Serpentmaster made Jumbo Jenkins submit to the Snake Bite (modified cobra clutch). Time of match: 8:07. Wild Ones defeated Bad Guys by disqualification. Time of match: 2:46. The Mercenaries came down to ringside and videotaped this match. STEEL CAGE: Bishop pinned 'Master of Darkness' Damion after the Hell's Inferno (fireball). Time of match: 6:40. CWO World Tag Team Champions the Pit Bosses and Highlanders wrestled to a double disqualification. Time of match: 7:58. CWO WORLD CHAMPION TITLE MATCH: Champion 'Monster' Rich Clark pinned Brian 'The Hammer' Williams after the Choke Slam. Time of match: 23:32. Williams was attacked by Brent 'The Ace' Alles early in the match, but Alles was escorted away by security and the match continued. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We now turn to comments from CWO superstars, starting with CWO World Champion, "Monster" Rich Clark. _______________________________________________________________________________ ("MONSTER" RICH CLARK is sitting in a dark room, all alone. There is nothing around, just RICH, and some walls. RICH sits on the ground, cross legged, like a child would, thinking about what will happen next. It is raining outside, and soon, it will be morning.) RICH: Bane, no more talking. No more words will be spent on you, no more attention payed to your feeble attempts at frightening me, no more of any of that s***. This is personal, and when I get my hands on you, I will NOT be responsible for any actions that I may take. You have something that is dear to me, something that I have worked my whole life for. You came and just took it. And now, the Dark Side has turned on me. I no longer needed the Dark Side. I have to make it on my own. This is my life, and I'll do things however I want to. You tried to control me. You tried to involve me in things that I was no part of. You attempted to make me war against the Unholy Alliance with you. Well, things have gone awry. This is the end for us, Dark Side. Soon, you will no longer be in the CWO, and that is a guarantee. It is time for me to attain my final goals. Ridding the CWO of those with no morals whatsoever, and attempting to regain control of my own life. This is the first time in my wrestling career that I have been alone. I have been managed by father in the past, but he now manages the Masters of Hardcore. I was helped along the way by Miguel and The Dark Side. Now I am on my own, trying to restore order. Soon enough, things will work out, and when they do I will still be ON TOP OF THE WORLD! (Lightning crashes in the background, then all of a sudden, the rain stops, and the sun begins to come up. The camera pans down from the window, and RICH is gone without a trace. FADE TO BLACK.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Next, we see comments from the former two-time Television champion, Mondo Gecko. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Mondo Gecko is seen walking in the streets of downtown San Francisco, occasionally shaking hansd with local gang members and basketball players in the street courts. He comes upon the Cow Palace and gazes upon the home of the San Jose Sharks.) MONDO GECKO: It seems that the CWO is overrun with problem brats here, from racist Gold Kard to punk brat Brent Alles to the thug Masher Maccabee. Brent Alles, you were lucky to go to high school and be able to wrestle. I remember comming here every day having to do janitorial work here to help support the family. I remember being told by every teacher that I wasn't working hard enough, even though I had a learning disability and nobody knew it. Brent "Ace" Alles, you are really starting to annoy me and so is the whole Gold Kard International. That is why I am going to declare war on Gold Kard International and the entire lot of thugs here. I am asking Moses, Johnny Reb, & Brian Williams to join me in ridding the CWO of Gold Kard International and the Ace! Masher Maccabee, for a big tough man you always need interference and I am sick and tired of it. First it was Rabbi Solomon, then Dave Thomas and now you have got Gold Kard, that neo-Nazi scum, to watch your back. What's the matter, tough guy, are you afraid of a light heavyweight and a female kicking your tail in? Bottom line here is Gold Kard, you're a friggin' loser who needs to be pimp-slapped upside the head and I am going to do so. Moses, Johnny Reb, & Brian Williams, I am asking you to join me so we can get rid of the trash. DIG IT, BABY! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Finally today, before we sign off, we take you live via satellite to the home of Brent "The Ace" Alles' family, who finally have comments after the vicious attack perpetrated by Brent "The Ace" Alles upon his own father last Tuesday on FEVER. Alles family, welcome to CWO REPORT. (Family is shown in the living room of Gordy's house. They include Brent's stepmother Debbie Alles, Brent's sister Mary Alles, his brother Kent Alles, and his stepsisters Jamie and Kim Dreyer.) MRS. DEBBIE ALLES (BRENT'S STEPMOTHER): Thanks, Johnny. STARR: Mrs. Alles, how is the family holding up? MRS. ALLES: Well, things are all right. Gordy's resting comfortably, he's got a brace on, thank goodness it was only a strain and nothing more serious. STARR: Kent, you are perhaps the closest with your brother Brent, what do you think is going on in his mind right now? KENT ALLES: I don't know, Johnny, we've all been doing a lot of thinking these past few days. We've tried contacting Brent, but to no avail. We saw yesterday on the REPORT that he was in Byron Center sometime recently, but we weren't able to track him down. Brent, if you're listening to this, please come home, we've got to talk about this, man. STARR: All right, thank you very much, Alles family, and we wish you well in the days ahead and a speedy recovery to Gordy. All right, that's all the time we have for today, we'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT!
