CWO REPORT 4/23/97

23 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control center.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Wednesday, April 23, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr.... (Brent "The Ace" Alles bursts into the control center.) STARR: Uh, Brent "The Ace" Alles... this is a surprise... um, we're trying to do the CWO REPORT here.... BRENT "THE ACE" ALLES: I know. And I've got an exclusive for you. (He slams an audio tape down on the counter.) ALLES: I want you to play this. STARR: Well, listen, we've got the whole show to do.... ALLES: Play it! STARR: Um... all right, we'll get it back to our technicians.... ALLES: I'll be waiting. I've got time. (Alles leaves.) STARR: O-K, well, last night was another shocking TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, let's take you back to what happened in our FEVER FLASHBACK! _______________________________________________________________________________ TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER 4/22/97 "LIVE" FROM BATON ROUGE, LA MATCH ONE: Spartacus defeated Arlon Flaym in 12:47, pinning him after the Spartan Spin Slam (airplane spin into a flying body slam). Comments from the Regulators and the Big Cool regarding their match tonight on FEVER as well as other comments about thing in general in the CWO. MATCH TWO: Regulators defeated the Big Cool in 14:40 when Regulator Fire pinned Jumbo Jenkins after the Total Regulation (spin kick by Regulator Ice, legsweep by Fire). Comments from Johnny Reb about his upcoming Television championship match at MAY-HEM, now apparently against Inferno. MATCH THREE: Johnny Reb made Steel submit to the Texas Cloverleaf in 11:31. In-ring interview with Brian "The Hammer" Wililams. Brian states that Alles' psychological warfare is not working, and also his disgust at not being included in the main event at MAY-HEM. Brent "The Ace" Alles then came down to the ring and taunted Williams, and then Amanda Bloom dropped the bombshell, saying that Wayne "College Guy" Schillo, Brian's best friend, had propositioned her while Brian was in the hospital recuperating from his injury at the hands of Brent "The Ace" Alles. Williams then attacked Alles, leading Bloom to spray mace in his eyes. Alles and Bloom were escorted out of the building, as Williams was taken to the local hospital to have his eyes flushed. Comments from Da Hood Rats and Ganglord about Brother Justice. MATCH FOUR: Wild Side (Bloodbath and Wildfire) defeated Da Hood Rats in 14:42, when Wildfire pinned Nate after the flying legdrop. Before the Television Championship match was started, Gold Kard stated that the Alliance had betrayed him, and that they should forfeit the Television title back to him as part of their "agreement." (Masher Maccabee was joined by Gold Kard, David "The Truth" Thomas, and a mysterious masked man.) The Alliance came down to the ring, and when Gold Kard again demanded they forfeit the title, Inferno clocked Maccabee with it, starting the match, as the members of GKI and the Alliance brawling outside the ring. MATCH FIVE: In an impromptu lumberjack match, Inferno defended the Television Championship against Masher Maccabee in 10:58, pinning him after the Inferno's Blaze power bomb and outside interference by Mondo Gecko, who smashed the Television belt over Maccabee's head. Mondo Gecko was able to do this due to the fact that the members of GKI and the Alliance were brawling outside the ring. Locker room scene with Masher Maccabee angrily confronting Gold Kard and David "The Truth" Thomas about the lack of protection in tonight's and last week's matchups with Inferno. Gold Kard manages to calm Maccabee down, saying that in MAY-HEM, they would look out for each other, and that one of them would be getting the World title shot in June. Replay of last week's footage in which the Masters of Hardcore attack the Highlanders and then deliver the Hardcore Bomb to their own manager, "Beautiful" Brian Clark. The Masters of Hardcore then make a challenge to the Bad Guys for a non-title match in a cage. MATCH SIX: The Bad Guys, accepting the challenge made last week, defeated the Masters of Hardcore in a steel cage match in 14:04, when Ron "The Rude Guy" Johnson pinned "Mr. Extreme" Chris Ohlhoff after the Bad Guys' Bash (double strike with steel chairs). The Bad Guys got the chairs from the Highlanders, who threw them over the cage and into the ring. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR (off-camera): Is it ready yet? Well, let me know. (He turns towards the camera.) All right, another great FEVER, and tonight CWO action continues in St. Louis, MO, at the Kiel Auditorium, let's look at the lineup for that card. _______________________________________________________________________________ WEDNESDAY, APRIL 23, 1997 ST. LOUIS, MO FINISHER (NOBILITY) VS. FINISHER (EAGLE SHOCK): Benjamin Blake Bentley vs. Joe Eagle "Lonestar" Mike Williams vs. Brent Alles Mondo Gecko vs. Gold Kard Brian 'The Hammer' Williams vs. 'Monster' Rich Clark, LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Kurt "Thrill from the Chill" Gerstung vs. Tiger Rogers (champion) Serpentmaster/Vlad the Impaler vs. Creatures of the Night TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP: Arlon Flaym vs. Inferno (champion) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We now turn to comments from CWO superstars. The new North American champion here in the CWO is Jason "Flash" Varga, and we have comments from him regarding his title vs. title matchup with Tiger Rogers at MAY-HEM. Let's hear from the "Flash." _______________________________________________________________________________ ("Flash" Varga is in a dark room, drinking a beer and staring at his North American title belt. His bodyguard, Jim Peer, is also there.) JASON "FLASH" VARGA: Man, what a night. I've been waiting for months to get here, and now I finally did it! Peer, who's the man? JIM PEER: Uhh... you are, aren't you? VARGA: Of course I am! I AM THE MAN! Ahh, what a sound. And what a BELT! This thing is great! You know, I was looking at the rankings a little while ago, and I noticed something... well, something rather intriguing. PEER: What's that, my man? VARGA: Well... since there's no World champ, who do you think is el numero uno in the CWO? PEER: Err... I don't know. That "Monster" guy? VARGA: NO! It's me, you nitwit! LOOK! (Varga points emphatically to the fact that his name is the first one on the rankings list, since there is no world champ listed.) PEER: Oh, I get it. Congrats! VARGA: Well, it's tough being the best in town, but I do what I can. Fans of the CWO, I told you back in January that I was the man, and some of you scoffed at me. I told you that I would take over the CWO, and there were a few other wrestlers who laughed. "Nobody as out of shape as that punk will EVER win in the CWO!" Well, look who's got the belt now! LOOK AT ME, CWO! LOOK AT ME! I AM THE MAN! Fans, I promised you the World, and now we've got it! PEER: Come on, man, there's nobody here but me! You don't need to yell! VARGA: Oh yeah... sorry. There's only one little problem in my mind right now. You see, this MAY-HEM pay per view thing is... well, I understand that things are all set in motion before I "officially" became the MAN in the CWO, but... well... why in the hell is Goliath in this triangle match? I mean... it's gonna be great to have 2 belts after my demolition of Tiger Rogers but still.... PEER: Guess you'll just have to be happy with what you've got! VARGA: Don't count on it. CWO... I will not rest until the World title is around my belly! I AM THE MAN! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: As we saw in the FEVER FLASHBACK, the Masters of Hardcore suffered a defeat to the North American Tag Team Champions the Bad Guys in the steel cage due to the fact that the Highlanders threw in steel chairs for the Bad Guys to use. Here are now are the Masters of Hardcore with a reaction to last night's match. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Masters of Hardcore are in their dressing room, cleaning their wounds from the match on FEVER. They have the TV set up, watching a tape of their match with the Bad Guys, and at the end of the match, they get extremely pissed off when they see The Highlanders coming out with the chairs, and throwing them into the ring.) "MR. EXTREME" CHRIS OHLHOFF: Highlanders, right there you just made your THIRD mistake. By interfering in one of our matches and COSTING us a victory over the Bad Guys, you have sealed your own fate. Just you wait now, because what we have in store for you will be nothing compared to what you've seen so far. In care you don't know, your first mistake was coming to the CWO in the first place. It's about time that Brad and I remedied that. Your second mistake was downtalking us in your interviews for the last month. Your THIRD and final was costing us that match earlier tonight. It's time for you to pay for your mistakes, and trust me, it ain't gonna be pretty. BRAD "THE MAN" COUCHMAN: That's right, you kilt-wearing fruits. There is nothing that you can do that will keep us from beating you, nay, eliminating you from the CWO. Eventually, you will find out the power of the MASTERS, and when you do, it will be time for you to run away, with your ***** between your legs, like the @$$ pirates that you are. Or, to use a phase that Scotsmen like yourselves would understand, "Float away, ya wee girls." (The MASTERS OF HARDCORE grab what they can and take off, pushing the cameraman on their way out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Also on last night's FEVER, we saw Television champion Inferno successfully defend his title against the former two-time champion Masher Maccabee, though not without help from his friends in the Alliance and also the interference by Mondo Gecko. Let's hear from the Alliance. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Alliance is standing in a back CWO locker room just after Inferno's TV title defense, all look very pissed off. Inferno has an ice pack on his shoulder, Tiger Rogers appears to have a broken nose, and David "The Destroyer" Davis is bleeding from the head.) JIMBO JONES: Well, well, well... GKI... so you've decided you want a war... well, hold on a second... what's this show rated? (Jones looks up at the upper left hand corner of the screen and a TV-14 is up there.) JONES: Ahhh, that's right... I can finally get this off my chest. ***damn it, Masher Maccabee & GKI, we've got a little something for you... (Everyone gives a double bird to the camera, Goliath has one of the biggest middle fingers I've ever seen.) JONES: Now that that's off my chest... we've got a little business to take care of in GKI. And I've got a challenge to you, Gold Kard, so you want a piece of the Alliance, we're giving you a match with our PERSONAL hitman... (David "The Destroyer" Davis steps forward.) DAVID "THE DESTROYER" DAVIS: So... you claim we didn't do your job? Well, I'll prove the Alliance does what it wants, when it wants. Let's go, me and you. We'll keep it clean, no interferences, hell, we'll throw this SOB. in a cage... you want to have a piece of Jones? He can be suspended in his own cage above the ring, if you beat me... you get him in the ring for 5 minutes! JONES: That's right, the Alliance fears no one, and that includes me! And as for you, Rich Clark & Bane, you've got a BIG something coming at MAY-HEM... Rich Clark, specifically you, you're holding my boys belt and he wants it back! GOLIATH: RICH CLARK! I kicked your @$$ back in Florida, and come MAY-HEM, I'll do it again. If I've got to launch your @$$ through the steel cage, I've got no problem! I'll just beat the hell out of.... (Goliath begins going insane, beating the camera, and the walls... all four men jump on him to restrain him, Inferno manages to put him in a straight jacket.) TIGER ROGERS: I'll admit, I've taken a few losses here and there, but I've still got the belt, and that's all that counts. The Alliance has a new attitude, and if any of you @$$-wipes have a problem with that, you can answer to the 4 most dangerous men in professional wrestling! INFERNO: Cuz, we've got the gold, and we're about to have it all! After Goliath finishes off Rich, and Tiger & I defend our gold, we'll be on top of the world, and NOBODY, and I MEAN NOBODY, that includes you, GKI, can stop us! Never mess with fire, my friend... you'll always get burnt! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: The Focus is not taking his CWO career lightly, he's going up against the toughest competition he can find, including at this point not only Sandman Jr. but former two-time CWO World Champion Wayne "College Guy" Schillo as well. Let's hear from The Focus. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Focus is in the interview area.) THE FOCUS: OK, listen up. Sandman Jr., you want to run your mouth? Fine. Next week, I'm going to get extreme on your @$$. You want to swing that cane at me, go right ahead. Wayne Schillo, you gave me a hell of a wrestling match. Now, I want to test your fighting skills. If you want in on this match, just say so. Mr. President, sign the match! I can go any night of the week, but Tuesday is looking mighty nice to me right now. I am a rising force in the CWO. Soon, I will have a title, I can guarantee it. Inferno, Tiger Rogers... start polishing those belts, and get one last picture taken with them, because one of them will be mine soon, and you can bank on it. Sandman Jr., Wayne Schillo... PREPARE! You will never be rid of me. I will never go away. Just get ready for the fight of your lives, because I will keep getting up, and coming on stronger than before. You're going to have to kill me to keep me down. Period. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Last night on FEVER the Wild Side scored an impressive victory over Da Hood Rats, and they continue their focus on winning the North American Tag Team titles from the Bad Guys sometime in the near future, avenging their loss at APRIL ARMAGEDDON. Let's hear from the Wild Side. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Wild Side is in the locker room after Tuesday Night Fever.) WILDFIRE (intense): Ohhhh Yeaaaah! Another BIG win for the Wild Side! And fans, this is probably the first and only time you'll ever hear me say this, but I have to give Da Hood Rats credit, they fought us all the way! Now on to bigger and better things! We, the uncrowned North American Tag Team champions, need the help of the fans of the CWO! We need you to make your posters, wear your t-shirts, and make your noise to convince the CWO championship comittee that we deserve another shot at those titles! And we have a special suprise for Kyle Melinn on Tuesday Night Fever next week! We also have a little message to deliver to David "The Truth" Thomas! (The lights go out and all that is seen is the figure of Bloodbath and the shadow of Wildfire.) BLOODBATH (mysterious): Thomas, you got something to say to us, you'll get your chance next week on Tuesday Night Fever! (Bloodbath's voice gets even darker.) BLOODBATH (mysterious): The time for talking is done, Bad Guys, you are the quest we must accomplish! We will put you down and capture what is rightfully ours, those tag belts! You won't even acknowledge that we exist! But next week on Tuesday Night Fever you will get the message.... (The camera fades out as the mysterious bells toll.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Earlier we talked about Mondo Gecko's involvement with last night's Television championship match, as he struck Masher Maccabee with the Television Championship belt. One might say that this is strangely uncharacteristic of the popular Gecko, but his feud with Maccabee has reached such epic proportions, that it definitely has driven him to the edge. Let's hear from Mondo Gecko and his friend, El Grande Pistolerro. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Wild Bunch is sitting in the locker room watching the tapes of the Tuesday Night Fever on the 64 inch screen TV. They rewind it to the part when Mondo Gecko bashes Masher Maccabee in the head.) MONDO GECKO: DDDDDAAAAHHHHHH HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAH! AHHAHAHAHAHAHAHA! Hey, Masher, how's your head feeling, you need any relief like Rolaids or something? (He starts cracking up and rolling around in laughter.) MONDO GECKO: Oh god, you were like a beached whale out there, laid out unconscious.... (Gecko then gets real serious.) MONDO GECKO: You know, Masher, this never had to happen. I only came here in the CWO to face the best competition and live my childhood dream. YOU ARE THE ONE WHO STARTED THIS LIVING HELL! HOW DOES IT FEEL TO HAVE YOUR HOPES DASHED AND YOUR DREAMS STOLEN?!? You know, I never can pay you back and show how terrible it was to see my GIRLFRIEND, Tamara, in the hospital, helpless, because you beat her up. You started all these problems. People like you, Gold Kard, Bane, and Brent Alles are the reason why the CWO has problems and I am tired of this. (He pauses for a second.) MONDO GECKO: I remember going home after I lost the TV title to you at April Armageddon and talking to my older brother, who is now 30. And he told me something very important, he said sometimes you must do things that you don't like to do to teach other people a lesson. So now, I declared war on GKI & Brent Alles, and I am going to make everybody who crossed me very unhappy. Because you see, I can be as mean and as nasty as the next guy around here. You see, I know what rule breaking is and what hardcore is. I ran the streets of San Francisco and Compton for my entire life! Try having to be in street fights 2-3 times a day because you have to survive and stick up for your family and your heritage. As Kevin Nash said, "Where I come from, you don' buy respect or get respect... YOU GOTTA BEAT IT OUT OF ME!" That is what you guys have to do! Brent Alles, you play all these stupid mind games, you need your tramp to hide behind and yet you still suck as a wrestler! No wonder your father is ashamed off you, YOU AREN'T EVEN A REAL MAN! Gold Kard, you are a class A GEEK! WW II ended and the Nazis lost, you stupid neo-Nazi punk! I am going to hurt you when I get my hands on you because this is what I do to people who hurt my people. EL GRANDE PISTOLERRO: Mondo, you have to relax and control yourself, mi amigo. This is not the way to do things, we will get our revenge on MAY-HEM when we get all the crap in the ring in a 60 man battle royal! MONDO GECKO: GKI & Alles, your time for punishment is near and soon you will realize that you made the mistake of pissing me off. You see, when you hurt my family... (he points to Tamara & the Wild Bunch) you brought out a dark side in me you won't forget. GET READY FOR MONDO EXTREME ACTION... AND PAIN... DIG IT! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Earlier, we heard from the Focus, who laid down a challenge to Sandman Jr. and the Focus. We now hear from Sandman Jr. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Sandman Jr. is in the interview area.) SANDMAN JR.: Focus, I can not believe this, but you actually said something that made sense... you said you want me OUT of the ring... you want a gimmick match, I got one for you, FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE! You man enough? Bentley, I haven't forgotten you either, I'm gonna kick your @$$ at my earliest convenience too. Finally, Gold Kard, you've done a lot of bad things here in the CWO, and you're gonna get caned for it on Friday night. Be prepared to be taken TO THE EXTREME! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: The Deadly Sins will be in action all this week, and they have some comments about their upcoming matches. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Deadly Sins are in the interview area.) SLOTH: So, another full plate... what the hell is this... hey Moses, you couldn't beat Pride one-on-one, and you won't do it to me... get over it, fight someone that you can WIN against. PRIDE: Then, me and Sloth gotta take on those two morons Max Overdrive and Baby "I want to be Mad Doctor's Ghost" Waldo... you two morons we STILL owe, so be prepared to face REPENTION! SLOTH: And Eternal Wanderers, we haven't forgotten we gotta fight you, so be prepared to get SQUASHED! WRATH: Brad Couchman, you have a stupid name. "The Man" is a stupid nickname. I'm going to crush you, maim you, desecrate your body in ways you didn't know were possible. Be prepared to face WRATH! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Well, that's all the time we have for today.... (Alles comes back into the control center, furious.) ALLES: Wait a minute, dammit, I brought that tape in for you to play and I want you to play it! STARR: Well, I, uh... Steve, do we have that ready? Um... I guess we do... folks... an audio tape brought to us from Brent "The Ace" Alles.... _______________________________________________________________________________ (Audio tape is cued. Some barely audible speech can be heard, and then Amanda Bloom's response.) AMANDA BLOOM: Wayne, stop it! This isn't the time! Brian's in the hospital! (More barely audible speech is heard.) BLOOM: What?!? Wayne, you can't mean that! (More barely audible speech.) BLOOM: Wayne, I told you to stop it... Wayne, get off me... GET OFF ME! (The tape stops.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny and "The Ace," who smiles triumphantly.) STARR: What... what is the meaning of this? ALLES: I think it speaks for itself. Just doing my job in the name of journalism. (Alles leaves.) STARR (pauses, then): Folks... we'll... we'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT....
