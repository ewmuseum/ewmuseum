CWO REPORT 4/17/97

17 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control center.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Thursday, April 17, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the CWO went up to the Great White North for some action in Calgary, Alberta, Canada, let's see what went down! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 4/16/97 Calgary, Alberta, Canada Panther made Maximum Overdrive submit to the Eclipse after outside interference by Brent 'The Ace' Alles. Time of match: 4:53. Baby Waldo pinned Ganglord after the splash from the top rope. Time of match: 5:23. The match scheduled between Brad "The Man" Couchman and Conor McCloud never took place, as the Highlanders attacked the Masters of Hardcore before the match and both teams brawled back to the dressing room. Pride and Moses wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. Wild Side (Bloodbath and Wildfire) defeated David 'The Destroyer' Davis/Inferno when Wildfire pinned Inferno after the inverted DDT. Time of match: 22:55. Dark Raven pinned Light Heavyweight champion Tiger Rogers in a non-title match after the reversed inside cradle. Time of match: 17:09. Vlad the Impaler/Serpentmaster defeated World Tag Team Champions the Pit Bosses in a non-title match when Serpentmaster made Pit Boss #2 submit to the Snake Bite (modified cobra clutch). Time of match: 23:30. STEEL CAGE (BISHOP'S ARENA): Bishop pinned Johnny Reb after the Hell's Inferno (fireball). Time of match: 13:08. After the match Bishop was attacked by the Dark Side ('Master of Darkness' Damion and the Creatures of the Night), who slammed his arm in the cage door, apparently breaking it. The rest of the Unholy Alliance then arrived to drive the Dark Side off. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: We do have an update on Bishop's condition after last night's vicious attack by the Dark Side. His arm was indeed broken, and he will be on the disabled list for 6 weeks. The Unholy Alliance - Dark Side war shows no signs of letting up, and you can be sure the Unholy Alliance will be looking for revenge now that their leader has been put on the shelf temporarily. All right, tonight we spend another night in our Northern neighbor, in Vancouver, let's look at what we have planned for our fans there! _______________________________________________________________________________ THURSDAY, APRIL 17, 1997 VANCOUVER, BRITISH COLUMBIA, CANADA Big Cool vs. Creatures of the Night Wild Bunch (Mondo Gecko and El Grande Pistolerro) vs. Chris Greco/Tommy Lightning Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Maximum Overdrive Mercenaries vs. Vlad the Impaler/Serpentmaster 'Master Of Darkness' Damion vs. Benjamin Blake Bently Regulators vs. Deadly Sins Joe Eagle vs. Johnny Reb NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title, STRAP MATCH): Goliath (champion) vs. Bane _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: We now turn to comments from CWO superstars, starting with the Wild Bunch. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Wild Bunch is in the interview area.) EL GRANDE PISTOLERRO: Chris Greco/Tommy Lightning, you two are worthy and admirable opponents and we have a great deal of respect for you two. However, we are on a quest to win the tag team titles and the road to the gold seems to go through you two. I have traveled many miles and have trained too many years to be denied the tag team gold around my waist. It seems that everybody in the CWO has their own agenda and wants to see the demise of the Wild Bunch. Well, I hate to tell Tiger Rogers and everybody else this, but we are rested and ready to win the whole enchilada. Lightning & Greco, you guys are gonna have to be on your best because we are coming after you with everything we have got. VIVA LA WILD BUNCH! MONDO GECKO: Lightning & Greco, you guys are cool with us and we are looking forward to a tough, clean match where we don't have to worry about outside interference. Bottom line here, though, is the Wild Ones are ready to take the tag team gold, whether it is from the Bad Boys or from the Pit Bosses. We don't care. All we know is it has been a while since we have held some gold and title belts would look real good around our waists. Lightning & Greco, get ready, baby, FOR MONDO EXTREME ACTION, DIG IT! REGULATOR FIRE: Deadly Sins, you guys are going to be the first victims of our Roadkill Tour and we are ready to destroy you two once and for all. It seems that everybody here does whatever they want and won't worry about the consequences. We are here to prove that you don't want to cross us for we are not the type of guys you fool around with. We have our eyes set on the tag team titles and we are ready to win the titles. Deadly Sins, get ready for a funeral because we are going to bury you two forever. Tell 'em, Ice! REGULATOR ICE: WELL! It seems that everybody wants to play rough, well that is fine with us because we are ready to do everything in our power to win. Bottom line here is that we are ready to do everything in our power to win those titles and if it means tearing you guys limb from limb that is fine with us. OOOHHHH WWHHAATT A RRUUSSHH! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Not only does the Focus have Wayne Schillo on the horizon, but now he faces a match against the deadly Bane on next week's SHOWCASE. However, one other person in particular has the Focus in his sights, and that is Sandman Jr. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Sandman Jr. is in the interview area.) SANDMAN JR.: YOOOOOOO! Focus, I kicked your ass, I'll do it again if you want! I've lost ONE specialty match EVER, and I WON'T lose another one to the likes of you... as for the stipulation... I don't know... how about a Lumberjack match? Well, do YOU have any better ideas? As for YOU, Triple-B... that was sure cheesy the way you beat me... I'm gonna have to whip your sorry butt too... I want this to be a cane vs. cane match, cause no one EVER EVER EVER hits me with my cane and gets away with it, you hear me, Bentley? I AM THE KING OF EXTREME! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: They're still around making trouble for everybody in the CWO, let's hear from the Deadly Sins. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Deadly Sins are in the interview area.) PRIDE: Moses, I owe you for leaving me for dead during THE ROCK... here's a fact: evil will always beat good. ALWAYS! And Pride is VERY VERY EVIL! So bring it on, Holy Man, and I'll show you that be it singles or in a tag team, NO ONE BEATS THE DEADLY SINS! SLOTH: Regulators, the only thing YOU'LL be regulating is the bottom of the mat while me and Pride grind your faces into it. You boys are messing with the wrong dudes. PRIDE: Oh hell, I gotta wrestle TWICE as singles? No matter, Panther, you'll be as dead as a doornail when I kick your sorry jobbing butt from here to next week. Have a fun time LOSING! WRATH: This week on Showcase, I take on the one called Centurion... he fancies himself some kind of god or something? Well, fact is, gods are susceptible to DEADLY SINS, and once you face ME, you will wish you stayed on Olympus, or wherever you think you're from. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Always a source of intrigue here in the CWO, we now hear from the mysterious element known as the Lord's Cabin, led of course by the current manager of the Eternal Wanderers, Lord Guru. _______________________________________________________________________________ (We find Exodus and Exile, both are standing and are carrying two dolls. Both the dolls are being hanged and both are dressed as The Masters of Hardcore. Grabbing gasoline fluid, Exile puts it on the dolls. Exodus, looking back, sees Lord Guru standing, and he nods. Grabbing a stone, Exodus walks up to the first one. Bowing before the Couchman Effigy, he casts the stone down. Suddenly, fire erupts, covering the doll of Couchman, burning it. Looking at Exile, Exile tosses a white skull within and it starts to burn. Both stand back quietly, awaiting the moment. Suddenly, the fire reaches its highest, and bursting apart, in a blazing inferno, stands a man, dressed in a brown trenchcoat, a steel chain in his hand, two more draped across his chest, wearing sunglesses. His face, painted white, like a white skull, is smoking a cigarette. He steps out, the biker chain in his left hand, the white skull mask in his right hand. He looks at the Eternal Wanderers and smiles. Walking over to Exodus, he shakes his hand and then, walking over to Exile, he hugs him. Then, noticing Lord Guru, he bows before him.) THE NEW MAN: My Lord... (smiles) I have heeded your summons. What may I do for you? LORD GURU (a smile on his cragly face): Blaze... I understand that you and Vengeance are getting ready for the match at London Explosion. And... I understand that you're ready to make your moment known in the WHCW. However... (smiles) the Cabin needs help here in the CWO. Yes... I know that the Eternal Wanderers are capable. However... I think it is time for some new blood and well... (a cold laughter) your cold blood, all right. BLAZE (chuckling lowly): Exodus... (smiles) are you well? EXODUS (rubbing his arm): I'm coping with the pain. (He looks serious.) But... I can't seem to get the things done correctly. I need time off and well, Exile does too. (A cold look.) I hate admitting it, brother... but wear and tear in the other leagues have cost me my edge. CWO is our home... but we are not capable recently. We desire a much needed break. BLAZE (eyeing him): I understand, Exodus... the Eternal Wanderers must wander once more. For now... (He smiles, his skull-like visage a terrible sight. He then looks at Lord Guru.) BLAZE: And we are to come here to the CWO? LORD GURU: Yes, Blaze. You and the man who wreak havoc, here in the CWO. The Angels are busy... the Hearttakers are training... but... the Ghost Riders... BLAZE: Will ride... not just here, but WIWA and WHCW. (A chuckle of amusement.) Yes... we will do it. May I ask... the Interrogating Cripplers? LORD GURU: Almost. They will be done... soon. Where is your counterpart, Blaze? (Smiling, Blaze puts on his skull mask. Suddenly, it lights up, the white skull surrounded by flames. Looking at the effigy doll put up of Ohlhoff, he walks up to it, and touching it, turns it aflame. Waiting, and waiting, and suddenly, a loud roar. A loud ugly sound that even makes Exodus cringe but Lord Guru smiles.) VOICE (deep baritone and hoarse sounding): VENGEANCE... WILL... BE... served! (Lord Guru smiles as a hand comes out of the fire as Exodus stares in awe.) LORD GURU: Excellent.... (Fade out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr, who is silent for a moment.) STARR: Well... we now turn to the Alliance, who have been making a strong showing as of late here in the CWO, and they have a chance to hold all the singles titles in the CWO if luck is with them. Let's hear from the Alliance. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Alliance is standing in a back CWO locker room, this is just after the news was announced about Goliath getting the World title shot.) JIMBO JONES: Ladies and gentlemen... I present to you the stable that is merely matches away from holding every SINGLE SINGLES title in the CWO. First, ladies and gentleman... the CWO Light Heavyweight Champion of the World... the man who's been holding his title longer that any current CWO champion... TIGER ROGERS! TIGER ROGERS: That's right... I'm the top dawg in the CWO right now. I'm unstoppable! You've seen me pin wrestler after wrestler with my dreaded Inverted Powerbomb, and there isn't a superstar who can stop me! JIMBO JONES: And the Alliance's high-flying henchman... David "The Destroyer" Davis... DAVID "THE DESTROYER" DAVIS: I am the man responsible for assisting the Alliance in getting this gold. When a member of the Alliance wins a belt, he doesn't win it... we all do. So give a little credit where credit is due! JIMBO JONES: And the next CWO Television Champion... here is INFERNO! INFERNO: Ladies and gentleman... in less that one week, Masher Maccabee will no longer be the CWO Television Champion because he will have been embarassed in front of thousands of fans as he is pinned by Inferno. Masher... didn't you mommy ever tell you? Never play with fire... you'll ALWAYS get burnt! JIMBO JONES: AND... the current CWO North American champion.. the man who will meet Bane and "Monster" Rick Clark in a WORLD Title match like no other... I present to you... GOLIATH! GOLIATH: BANE! RICH CLARK! MAY-HEM! HAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHAAAAAAAA! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Speaking of the CWO Television Champion, let's hear from Masher Maccabee. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Masher is at Gold Kard's mansion, sipping a glass of fine Maneschewitz wine along with David "The Truth" Thomas and Gold Kard.) MASHER MACCABEE: OK, let's review here. I am the TWO-TIME Television Champion, and a charter member of the NEW GKI. Why then are the only names mentioned as contenders for the World Title Window Pane, Girliath, and the Munster? Why not the baddest finish in the CWO, the Homentaschen Hangover? I challenge the winner of this MAY-HEM match to a ladder match for the title any time, anywhere! Whether you like it or not, CWO, I am the best, and sometimes the TRUTH hurts... tell 'em TRUTH! DAVID "THE TRUTH" THOMAS: You da man, Masher, you da man! GOLD KARD: Didn't I tell you, Herr Maccabee? Didn't I tell you how you would live like a king? When you are with GKI, you are with the cream of the crop, the best there ever will be! (All three men clink glasses and laugh.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: And now for a lighter moment.... _______________________________________________________________________________ (Jason "Flash" Varga is sitting in Wayne Schillo's house, talking to the camera. Wayne "College Guy" Schillo and Brian "The Hammer" Williams are in the background, playing Nintendo.) JASON "FLASH" VARGA: All right, CWO, this is the Man! I am coming to you live from the home of "College Guy" Schillo, who is busy behind me on the video game kick. I have a more pressing issue to discuss, however... WAYNE "COLLEGE GUY" SCHILLO: You suck, Williams! College rules, Keuka Drools! VARGA: HEY! I'm tryin' to talk over here! SCHILLO: Sorry... here, have a brew! VARGA: Oh, yeah! Goliath, you're gonna wish you never showed up when you feel the Flash Flood! And, there's a more pressing matter to discuss... since I will, of course, be the North American Champion after Saturday, there's a little problem with your "title match" at MAY-HEM. Since I'll be the number 1 contender, shouldn't I get the title shot? SCHILLO: HEY! I should get the shot! WILLIAMS: Both of you shut up! I should get the shot! VARGA: Well, just remember, fans of the CWO... I am THE Man, no matter WHAT the championship committee thinks! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny, who is shaking his head.) STARR: Anyway, we now turn to the Wild Side, as Bloodbath and Wildfire have some comments for the Bad Guys. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Wild Side and Miss Amber are in the locker room, after Tuesday Night Fever.) WILDFIRE: Ohhhh yeahhhh! We've been silent for a while and it's good to finally break that silence! We got a lot to say! BLOODBATH (dark and mysterious): As we were dealt a grave injustice at April Armageddon, we have decided to call ourselves the uncrowned North American Tag Team Champions! WILDFIRE: Bad Guys, you're running scared, you won't respond to anything because you're afraid to admit to the fact that Wild Side is the rightful owners of those belts! MISS AMBER: Well, since you won't give my guys a rematch, well, we're gonna start kicking a little @$$ here in the CWO and then you won't have a choice, you'll have to face us! WILDFIRE: Rematch... yeah right, you two punks aren't getting off that easy! We want you to step in the danger zone! In a fifteen foot high steel cage! BLOODBATH (dark and mysterious): I will be preparing your graves right in the center of the ring! REST IN PIECE! WILDFIRE: I've got one more message! This one's to Wayne Schillo! Listen up, man, you and I gotta get together, I have a proposition for you! MISS AMBER: What ya gonna do when the Wild Side, the UNCROWNED NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS, run wild on you! (The camera fades out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Finally today, our cameras were fortunate enough to catch up with the CWO World Tag Team Champions the Pit Bosses in an uncomfortably intimate moment. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Pit Bosses are in the locker room, after being defeated by Vlad the Impaler and Serpentmaster in a non-title match. Pit Boss #1 has his head in his hands, while Pit Boss #2's head is up against the locker, a dejected look on his face.) PIT BOSS #1: Well? PIT BOSS #2: Well, what? PIT BOSS #1: Well, what was that out there tonight? Huh? You submitted to that Snake Bite move! PIT BOSS #2: Hey, I was unconscious, what do you want me to do? PIT BOSS #1 (standing up, furious, he slams a locker): We looked like jokes out there tonight! It's a damn good thing all these matches have been non-title, or we'd be back at the bottom of the heap right now! PIT BOSS #2: Hey, what do you want me to do? There's a lot of tough teams.... PIT BOSS #1: We're supposed to be a tough team! But we still look like jokes if we get beat by people all the time! They're going to think we're paper champions! Now damn it, at MAY-HEM, we take on the Highlanders, we know how tough these guys are, they've beaten us before, and if we don't get our act together, we're gonna go down again. PIT BOSS #2: Look, don't be getting this arrogant "I carry the team" attitude, you and I have been together since our days in the Las Vegas Wrestling Federation, we were just a couple of jobbers before we got together, and now we are the best damn tag team in the sport. PIT BOSS #1: Well, we don't look like it! And if we don't get our act together, then those two Scottish guys are going to take these titles from us! You'd better be focused, that's all I have to say. (Pit Boss #1 leaves in a huff. After a moment, Pit Boss #2 gets up and kicks a locker, hard.) PIT BOSS #2: Son of a *****. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today, we'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT.
